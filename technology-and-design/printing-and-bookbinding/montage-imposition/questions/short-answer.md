# Montage (imposition) — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Say in one sentence what this lesson teaches.
**Answer:** The rules and kinds of imposition (work-and-turn "τούμπα γωνία/δόντια"), drawing up the imposition and assembling the films.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name one material, tool or step this lesson works with.
**Answer:** Any one named in the lesson text — for example, the first step of sub lesson 1: Montage (imposition).
