# From writing to printing — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Say in one sentence what this lesson teaches.
**Answer:** Early scripts (cuneiform, hieroglyphic, Cretan-Minoan, the alphabet), reproduction of texts in the East, copying in Europe, and Gutenberg — movable type, the press and inks.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name one material, tool or step this lesson works with.
**Answer:** Any one named in the lesson text — for example, the first step of sub lesson 1: From writing to printing.
