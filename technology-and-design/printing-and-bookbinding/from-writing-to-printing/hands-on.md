# Hands-on — From writing to printing

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: early scripts (cuneiform, hieroglyphic, Cretan-Minoan, the alphabet), reproduction of texts in the East, copying in Europe, and Gutenberg — movable type, the press and inks. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
