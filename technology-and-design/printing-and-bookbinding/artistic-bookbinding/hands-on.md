# Hands-on — Artistic bookbinding

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: the stages — removing the old cover, trimming, pressing, sewing, spine gluing, rounding, cloth, headbands, marbled papers, building and covering the case. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
