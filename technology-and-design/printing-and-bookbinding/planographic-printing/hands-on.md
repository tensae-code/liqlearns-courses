# Hands-on — Planographic printing

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: lithography's invention and development, direct lithography and indirect (offset) photolithography — printing stages, transferring the image to the plate, kinds and parts of offset machines, dry offset. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
