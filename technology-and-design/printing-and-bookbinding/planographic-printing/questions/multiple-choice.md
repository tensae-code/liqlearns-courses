# Planographic printing — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson teach?
a) Lithography's invention and development, direct lithography and indirect (offset) photolithography — printing stages, transferring the image to the plate, kinds and parts of offset machines, dry offset b) Only how to draw pictures c) Nothing practical d) A song to memorise
**Answer: a) Lithography's invention and development, direct lithography and indirect (offset) photolithography — printing stages, transferring the image to the plate, kinds and parts of offset machines, dry offset**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Sub lesson 1 is about…
a) Planographic printing b) Cooking lunch c) A sports match d) The weather
**Answer: a) Planographic printing**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Sub lesson 2 asks the learner to…
a) Complete the making in full and check it b) Stop before starting c) Copy a partner's work d) Throw the materials away
**Answer: a) Complete the making in full and check it**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Greece teaches this topic inside which school book?
a) Printing Tech b) Only mathematics c) Only history d) It is not taught at school
**Answer: a) Printing Tech**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** What should the learner do first in this lesson?
a) Try the Do it first task in their own way b) Skip to the last page c) Wait to be given the answer d) Nothing at all
**Answer: a) Try the Do it first task in their own way**
