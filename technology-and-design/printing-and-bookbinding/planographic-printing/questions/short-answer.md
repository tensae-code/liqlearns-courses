# Planographic printing — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Say in one sentence what this lesson teaches.
**Answer:** Lithography's invention and development, direct lithography and indirect (offset) photolithography — printing stages, transferring the image to the plate, kinds and parts of offset machines, dry offset.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name one material, tool or step this lesson works with.
**Answer:** Any one named in the lesson text — for example, the first step of sub lesson 1: Planographic printing.
