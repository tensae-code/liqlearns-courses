# Engraving and printing a matrix

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Moldova's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Cutting the matrix — xylography and linocut
Grade 6 engraving (gravarea) makes the printing block: an image is cut into wood — xylography — or into linoleum — linocut — with knives and small chisels. What is cut away stays white; what stands prints. The finished block is the matrix.

## Sub lesson 2 — Printing from the matrix, by the sheet
The matrix is then printed on paper — one block, many impressions. The work follows set working procedures, recorded on a technological sheet: the maker's ordered instructions, from materials to the finished print.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: engraving (gravarea) cuts an image into wood or linoleum to make a matrix — xylography in wood, linocut in linoleum — and the matrix prints the image on paper, by procedures written on a technological sheet. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
