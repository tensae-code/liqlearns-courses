# Lesson plan — Engraving and printing a matrix

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Printing and Bookbinding · **Lesson:** Engraving and printing a matrix
**Place in the curriculum:** Printing and Bookbinding (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Moldova expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** engraving (gravarea) cuts an image into wood or linoleum to make a matrix — xylography in wood, linocut in linoleum — and the matrix prints the image on paper, by procedures written on a technological sheet.
**This lesson's own level:** stocked from Moldova's *Educație tehnologică* (Technological Education) textbooks — see lesson/sources.md. Moldova counts in the acceptance count (student books, not evidence-only).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **Cutting the matrix — xylography and linocut** — Grade 6 engraving (gravarea) makes the printing block: an image is cut into wood — xylography — or into linoleum — linocut — with knives and small chisels.
2. **Printing from the matrix, by the sheet** — The matrix is then printed on paper — one block, many impressions.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of Printing and Bookbinding. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Explain what a matrix is and how it prints.
2. Follow the engraving procedures from a technological sheet.
3. Explain how Moldova's book teaches it.
## What you need
Linoleum or soft wood block, engraving knives and small chisels, ink and paper.
