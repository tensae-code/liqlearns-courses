# Engraving and printing a matrix — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is cut away in engraving prints as the image's dark part.
**Answer: False** — What is cut away stays white; what stands prints.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** One matrix can print the image many times on paper.
**Answer: True** — The matrix exists to be printed from, impression after impression.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The technological sheet orders the work from materials to print.
**Answer: True** — Procedures are recorded on the technological sheet.
