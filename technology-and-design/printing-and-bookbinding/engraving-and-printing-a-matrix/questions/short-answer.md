# Engraving and printing a matrix — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is a matrix?
**Answer:** The engraved block — wood or linoleum — from which the image is printed on paper.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is a technological sheet?
**Answer:** The maker's written, ordered procedures for the work, from materials to the finished print.
