# Engraving and printing a matrix — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is gravarea?
a) Engraving — cutting an image to make a printing matrix b) Weaving c) Firing clay d) Forging
**Answer: a) Engraving — cutting an image to make a printing matrix**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is xylography?
a) Engraving in linoleum b) Engraving in wood c) Printing on cloth d) A kind of ink
**Answer: b) Engraving in wood**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** What is linocut?
a) Engraving in linoleum b) Engraving in wood c) A paper cut d) A metal die
**Answer: a) Engraving in linoleum**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Which tools cut the matrix?
a) Knives and small chisels b) Brushes c) Needles d) Planes
**Answer: a) Knives and small chisels**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** What records the working procedures?
a) A poster b) A receipt c) A technological sheet d) Nothing — they are memorised
**Answer: c) A technological sheet**
