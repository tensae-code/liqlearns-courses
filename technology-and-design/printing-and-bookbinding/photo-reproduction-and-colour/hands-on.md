# Hands-on — Photo-reproduction and colour

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: the visible spectrum, additive and subtractive colour methods, the reproduction studio (the halftone screen/ράστερ, traditional and modern studio equipment). Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
