# Hands-on — Screen printing

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: the method, basic equipment, making the printing screen/plate (direct/artistic and indirect), kinds of screen-printing machines. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
