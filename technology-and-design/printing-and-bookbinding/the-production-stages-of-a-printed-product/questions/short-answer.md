# The production stages of a printed product — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Say in one sentence what this lesson teaches.
**Answer:** The client, organisation and editing, the creative stage (graphic designer, layout, page make-up, typesetting, corrections, the final dummy), prepress (films, stripping, proofs, montage), printing, and finishing.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name one material, tool or step this lesson works with.
**Answer:** Any one named in the lesson text — for example, the first step of sub lesson 1: The production stages of a printed product.
