# Hands-on — The production stages of a printed product

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: the client, organisation and editing, the creative stage (graphic designer, layout, page make-up, typesetting, corrections, the final dummy), prepress (films, stripping, proofs, montage), printing, and finishing. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
