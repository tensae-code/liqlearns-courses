# Hands-on — Box-making and packaging

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: paper boxes (surface preparation, cardboards, box construction and types) and metal packaging (raw material, the printing process, kinds of metal boxes); lab — designing and making a box for a child's toy. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
