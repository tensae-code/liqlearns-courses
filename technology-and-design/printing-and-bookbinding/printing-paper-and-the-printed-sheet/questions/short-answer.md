# Printing paper and the printed sheet — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Say in one sentence what this lesson teaches.
**Answer:** Paper standardisation, standardisation of machine printing areas, folding the paper, the printed sheet and its page, folding and gathering printed sheets.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name one material, tool or step this lesson works with.
**Answer:** Any one named in the lesson text — for example, the first step of sub lesson 1: Printing paper and the printed sheet.
