# Hands-on — Printing paper and the printed sheet

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: paper standardisation, standardisation of machine printing areas, folding the paper, the printed sheet and its page, folding and gathering printed sheets. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
