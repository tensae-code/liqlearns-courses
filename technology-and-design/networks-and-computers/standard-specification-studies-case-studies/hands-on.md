# Hands-on — Standard specification studies (case studies)

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: specifying a computing system for a civil/architect engineer, networking a small business, installing and choosing office-automation software, choosing high-performance computer hardware. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
