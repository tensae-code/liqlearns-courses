# Hands-on — Conduct guide

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: the role of the sales technician: the professional role, what customers want beyond technical knowledge (positive/willing attitude, listening, reliability, politeness, responsibility, honesty, knowledge of legislation, professionalism), categories of "difficult" customers, corporate customers and customer expectations. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
