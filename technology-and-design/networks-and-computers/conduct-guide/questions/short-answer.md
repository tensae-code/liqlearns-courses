# Conduct guide — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Say in one sentence what this lesson teaches.
**Answer:** The role of the sales technician: the professional role, what customers want beyond technical knowledge (positive/willing attitude, listening, reliability, politeness, responsibility, honesty, knowledge of legislation, professionalism), categories of "difficult" customers, corporate customers and customer expectations.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name one material, tool or step this lesson works with.
**Answer:** Any one named in the lesson text — for example, the first step of sub lesson 1: Conduct guide.
