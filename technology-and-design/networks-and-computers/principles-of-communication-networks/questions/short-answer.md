# Principles of communication networks — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Say in one sentence what this lesson teaches.
**Answer:** What networks serve, convergence of telecoms and computing, network services, switching (circuit vs packet, datagram vs virtual circuit) and multiplexing, protocols and network architecture, the OSI seven-layer model, LAN vs WAN, standards and standards bodies, integrated-services networks.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name one material, tool or step this lesson works with.
**Answer:** Any one named in the lesson text — for example, the first step of sub lesson 1: Principles of communication networks.
