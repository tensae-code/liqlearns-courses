# Hands-on — Principles of communication networks

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: what networks serve, convergence of telecoms and computing, network services, switching (circuit vs packet, datagram vs virtual circuit) and multiplexing, protocols and network architecture, the OSI seven-layer model, LAN vs WAN, standards and standards bodies, integrated-services networks. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
