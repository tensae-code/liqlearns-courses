# The IT market and selling — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Say in one sentence what this lesson teaches.
**Answer:** The concept of a sale and forms of trade (retail–wholesale), the product life cycle, the marketing mix, innovation for entrepreneurship in IT; categories of personal and mobile computing devices (desktop PC, laptop, tablet, smartphone).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name one material, tool or step this lesson works with.
**Answer:** Any one named in the lesson text — for example, the first step of sub lesson 1: The IT market and selling.
