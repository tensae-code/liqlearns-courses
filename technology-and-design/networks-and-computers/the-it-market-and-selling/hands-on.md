# Hands-on — The IT market and selling

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: the concept of a sale and forms of trade (retail–wholesale), the product life cycle, the marketing mix, innovation for entrepreneurship in IT; categories of personal and mobile computing devices (desktop PC, laptop, tablet, smartphone). Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
