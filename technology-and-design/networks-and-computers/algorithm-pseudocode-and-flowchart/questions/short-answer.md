# Algorithm, pseudocode and flowchart — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In your own words, what is an algorithm?
**Answer:** A step-by-step plan for solving a problem.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In your own words, what is a flowchart?
**Answer:** The plan drawn with symbols — start/end, process, input/output, flow.
