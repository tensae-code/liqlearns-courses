# Lesson plan — Algorithm, pseudocode and flowchart

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Networks and Computers · **Lesson:** Algorithm, pseudocode and flowchart
**Place in the curriculum:** Networks and Computers (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Malaysia expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** an algorithm is the plan, pseudocode writes it in words, and a flowchart draws it in symbols — all checked and improved before use.
**This lesson's own level:** stocked from Malaysia's *Reka Bentuk dan Teknologi* (RBT — Design and Technology) textbooks — see lesson/sources.md. Malaysia counts in the acceptance count (student books, not evidence-only).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **Three ways to plan a program** — An algorithm is the step-by-step plan; pseudocode writes the plan in plain ordered words; a flowchart draws it with symbols — start/end, process, input/output, and the flow arrows joining them.
2. **Checking, improving, applying** — Plans are checked and improved before they are coded, and the same tools break down practical problems — the book uses pseudocode and a flowchart to plan measuring and marking a board.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of Networks and Computers. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Define algorithm, pseudocode and flowchart, and name the flowchart symbols.
2. Write and improve a pseudocode and flowchart for a real situation.
3. Explain how Malaysia's book teaches it.
## What you need
