# Hands-on — The personal computer

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: its basic units and architecture, the central unit, and the motherboard (processor and memory sockets, motherboard slots and other parts, motherboard form-factor standards) and the processor. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
