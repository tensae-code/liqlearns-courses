# Hands-on — Software categories and specifications

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: operating systems for PCs, open-source operating systems, operating systems for tablets and smartphones — advantages and disadvantages by use (and the further software categories). Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
