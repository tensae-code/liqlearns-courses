# Programming design basics

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Malaysia's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Programs live inside appliances
Programming design begins at home: the smart TV, the digital clock, the remote control and the air conditioner all carry computer programs inside them. The appliances around us are computers at work.

## Sub lesson 2 — What a program is
A program is a coded instruction sequence that lets data be processed by a computer: instructions, written in code, telling the machine what to do with what it senses and holds. Designing a program is designing behaviour.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: a program is a coded instruction sequence that lets a computer process data — and programs live inside everyday appliances. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
