# Hands-on — The Internet and the Web

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: what the Internet is, ways of connecting, server categories, IP addresses and DNS, Internet governance, the WWW, browser settings, search engines and organising useful addresses/portals. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
