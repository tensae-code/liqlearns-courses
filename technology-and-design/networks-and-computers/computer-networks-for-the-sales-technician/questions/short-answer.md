# Computer networks for the sales technician — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Say in one sentence what this lesson teaches.
**Answer:** Network types, transmission media and cables, LAN topologies and interconnection technologies, LAN devices (network cards wired/wireless, modems, hubs, switches, routers, wireless access points), Ethernet cables/connectors and wiring, LAN settings and troubleshooting (network-level faults, the network technician's tools, connecting UTP twisted-pair cables).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name one material, tool or step this lesson works with.
**Answer:** Any one named in the lesson text — for example, the first step of sub lesson 1: Computer networks for the sales technician.
