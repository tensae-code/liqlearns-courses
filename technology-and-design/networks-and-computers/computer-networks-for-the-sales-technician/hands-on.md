# Hands-on — Computer networks for the sales technician

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: network types, transmission media and cables, LAN topologies and interconnection technologies, LAN devices (network cards wired/wireless, modems, hubs, switches, routers, wireless access points), Ethernet cables/connectors and wiring, LAN settings and troubleshooting (network-level faults, the network technician's tools, connecting UTP twisted-pair cables). Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
