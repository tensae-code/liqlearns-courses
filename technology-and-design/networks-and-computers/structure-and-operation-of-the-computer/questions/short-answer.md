# Structure and operation of the computer — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Say in one sentence what this lesson teaches.
**Answer:** Computer architecture, the CPU (registers, architecture), memory, the bus, peripheral units, decoding circuits, data-transfer techniques (interrupts, DMA), handshaking, and serial communication (the serial port, the UART, modem communication, the RS-232C standard, its signals/pins and connecting terminal devices).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name one material, tool or step this lesson works with.
**Answer:** Any one named in the lesson text — for example, the first step of sub lesson 1: Structure and operation of the computer.
