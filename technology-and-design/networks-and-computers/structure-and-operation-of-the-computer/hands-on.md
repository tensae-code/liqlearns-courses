# Hands-on — Structure and operation of the computer

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: computer architecture, the CPU (registers, architecture), memory, the bus, peripheral units, decoding circuits, data-transfer techniques (interrupts, DMA), handshaking, and serial communication (the serial port, the UART, modem communication, the RS-232C standard, its signals/pins and connecting terminal devices). Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
