# Hands-on — Peripheral devices and their specifications

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: (printers, scanners and the input/output devices), TV tuners, UPS units, game controllers, sound cards, speakers and microphones, MIDI devices, USB flash drives, card readers, CD–DVD–Blu-ray. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
