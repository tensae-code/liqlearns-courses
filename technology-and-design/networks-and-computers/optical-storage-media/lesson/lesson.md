# Optical storage media

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Greece's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Optical storage media
Greece's book teaches it this way: the CD-ROM and its disc, reflection/refraction/diffusion of light, the laser light source, the CD reading mechanism, CD-R and DVD (*Computers & Peripherals*, printed p. 247). The learner works through the named materials and steps in order, saying what each step does before doing the next one.

## Sub lesson 2 — Optical storage media — doing it yourself
Now the learner does the whole task unaided: the CD-ROM and its disc, reflection/refraction/diffusion of light, the laser light source, the CD reading mechanism, CD-R and DVD. Working from the first sub lesson's sequence, the learner plans the make, works through it step by step, and checks the finished piece against what Greece's book shows.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: The CD-ROM and its disc, reflection/refraction/diffusion of light, the laser light source, the CD reading mechanism, CD-R and DVD. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.

## Do It Yourself
1. Do the Do it first task again, carefully this time, using everything the lesson taught.
