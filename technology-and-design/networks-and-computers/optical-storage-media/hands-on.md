# Hands-on — Optical storage media

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: the CD-ROM and its disc, reflection/refraction/diffusion of light, the laser light source, the CD reading mechanism, CD-R and DVD. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
