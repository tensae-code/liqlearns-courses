# Hands-on — Introductory concepts

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: the computing system, the history of computing-system technology, categories of computing systems, and the applications of informatics (in business, widespread software applications, the Internet, the electronics technician's work, daily life). Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
