# Hands-on — Hardware specifications and choosing for the customer's needs

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: processor characteristics and how to choose a CPU, memory technologies/specifications/performance and choosing the right memory, graphics cards (integrated vs dedicated, outputs, choosing), power-supply sizing, internal and external hard disks and choosing a disk; laptop and tablet/smartphone categories by use (incl. 2-in-1 devices). Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
