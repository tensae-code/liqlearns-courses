# Hardware specifications and choosing for the customer's needs

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Greece's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Hardware specifications and choosing for the customer's needs
Greece's book teaches it this way: processor characteristics and how to choose a CPU, memory technologies/specifications/performance and choosing the right memory, graphics cards (integrated vs dedicated, outputs, choosing), power-supply sizing, internal and external hard disks and choosing a disk; laptop and tablet/smartphone categories by use (incl. 2-in-1 devices) (*Sales Topics & HW/SW Specs*, printed p. 29). The learner works through the named materials and steps in order, saying what each step does before doing the next one.

## Sub lesson 2 — Hardware specifications and choosing for the customer's needs — doing it yourself
Now the learner does the whole task unaided: processor characteristics and how to choose a CPU, memory technologies/specifications/performance and choosing the right memory, graphics cards (integrated vs dedicated, outputs, choosing), power-supply sizing, internal and external hard disks and choosing a disk; laptop and tablet/smartphone categories by use (incl. 2-in-1 devices). Working from the first sub lesson's sequence, the learner plans the make, works through it step by step, and checks the finished piece against what Greece's book shows.
