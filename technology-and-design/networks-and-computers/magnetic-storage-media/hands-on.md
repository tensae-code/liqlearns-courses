# Hands-on — Magnetic storage media

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: characteristics of storage media, magnetic materials, the floppy disk and its drive and data organisation, the hard disk and its organisation and connection, LS-120, Zip and Jaz drives. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
