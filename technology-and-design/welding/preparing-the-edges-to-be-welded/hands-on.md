# Hands-on — Preparing the edges to be welded

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: groove (butt) joints, fillet joints, edge/parallel joints, lap joints, shaped and special joints, criteria for choosing the joint, and welding symbols. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
