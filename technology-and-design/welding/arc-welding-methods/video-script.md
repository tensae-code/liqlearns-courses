# Video script — Arc welding methods

A short demonstration video, made later, in a super-simple voice.

Arc welding methods.
Principles of arc welding, filler addition, shielding from the atmosphere, MMA (coated electrodes), MIG/MAG, FCAW, TIG, submerged-arc (SAW) and plasma (PAW); current types and when each is used; welding machines and connections; the welder's hazards and eye/face protection with the mask.
Watch the steps. Then do them yourself.
