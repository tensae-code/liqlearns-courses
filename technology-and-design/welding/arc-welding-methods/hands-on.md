# Hands-on — Arc welding methods

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: principles of arc welding, filler addition, shielding from the atmosphere, MMA (coated electrodes), MIG/MAG, FCAW, TIG, submerged-arc (SAW) and plasma (PAW); current types and when each is used; welding machines and connections; the welder's hazards and eye/face protection with the mask. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
