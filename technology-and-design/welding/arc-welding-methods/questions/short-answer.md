# Arc welding methods — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Say in one sentence what this lesson teaches.
**Answer:** Principles of arc welding, filler addition, shielding from the atmosphere, MMA (coated electrodes), MIG/MAG, FCAW, TIG, submerged-arc (SAW) and plasma (PAW); current types and when each is used; welding machines and connections; the welder's hazards and eye/face protection with the mask.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name one material, tool or step this lesson works with.
**Answer:** Any one named in the lesson text — for example, the first step of sub lesson 1: Arc welding methods.
