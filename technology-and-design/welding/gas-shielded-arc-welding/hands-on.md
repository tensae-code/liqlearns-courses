# Hands-on — Gas-shielded arc welding

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: shielding gases and the effect of CO2/oxygen, wires and rods and their AWS/ISO/EN standardisation, MIG/MAG parameters and metal transfer (incl. pulsed current), TIG technique and non-consumable electrodes, FCAW, robot welding machines. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
