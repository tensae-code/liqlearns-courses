# Gas-shielded arc welding — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Say in one sentence what this lesson teaches.
**Answer:** Shielding gases and the effect of CO2/oxygen, wires and rods and their AWS/ISO/EN standardisation, MIG/MAG parameters and metal transfer (incl. pulsed current), TIG technique and non-consumable electrodes, FCAW, robot welding machines.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name one material, tool or step this lesson works with.
**Answer:** Any one named in the lesson text — for example, the first step of sub lesson 1: Gas-shielded arc welding.
