# Hands-on — MMA welding in practice

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: welding positions, cutting and preparing edges, coated electrodes — their characteristics, standardisation (ISO 2560 A/B) and choosing the right electrode, cellulosic/rutile/basic electrodes, welding stainless steels, aluminium and cast iron, storing electrodes. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
