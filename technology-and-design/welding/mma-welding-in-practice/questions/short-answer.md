# MMA welding in practice — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Say in one sentence what this lesson teaches.
**Answer:** Welding positions, cutting and preparing edges, coated electrodes — their characteristics, standardisation (ISO 2560 A/B) and choosing the right electrode, cellulosic/rutile/basic electrodes, welding stainless steels, aluminium and cast iron, storing electrodes.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name one material, tool or step this lesson works with.
**Answer:** Any one named in the lesson text — for example, the first step of sub lesson 1: MMA welding in practice.
