# MMA welding in practice

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Greece's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — MMA welding in practice
Greece's book teaches it this way: welding positions, cutting and preparing edges, coated electrodes — their characteristics, standardisation (ISO 2560 A/B) and choosing the right electrode, cellulosic/rutile/basic electrodes, welding stainless steels, aluminium and cast iron, storing electrodes (*Welding*, printed p. 248). The learner works through the named materials and steps in order, saying what each step does before doing the next one.

## Sub lesson 2 — MMA welding in practice — doing it yourself
Now the learner does the whole task unaided: welding positions, cutting and preparing edges, coated electrodes — their characteristics, standardisation (ISO 2560 A/B) and choosing the right electrode, cellulosic/rutile/basic electrodes, welding stainless steels, aluminium and cast iron, storing electrodes. Working from the first sub lesson's sequence, the learner plans the make, works through it step by step, and checks the finished piece against what Greece's book shows.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: Welding positions, cutting and preparing edges, coated electrodes — their characteristics, standardisation (ISO 2560 A/B) and choosing the right electrode, cellulosic/rutile/basic electrodes, welding stainless steels, aluminium and cast iron, storing electrodes. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.

## Do It Yourself
1. Do the Do it first task again, carefully this time, using everything the lesson taught.
2. Check your work against the lesson's own checks — fix one thing you find.
3. Keep your work: it is practice evidence, and the next lesson builds on it.
