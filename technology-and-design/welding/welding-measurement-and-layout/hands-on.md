# Hands-on — Welding measurement and layout

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: Measurement; Print reading; Measurement units and drawing instruments; Dimensioning, Layout/fit-up Techniques; also: Measurement accuracy; Application of measurement in Print reading; in layout or fit-up techniques. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
