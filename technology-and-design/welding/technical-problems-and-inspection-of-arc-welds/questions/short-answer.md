# Technical problems and inspection of arc welds — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Say in one sentence what this lesson teaches.
**Answer:** Edge preparation, arc striking and holding, welding parameters, arc blow, thermal distortion, cracking (and hydrogen's role), porosity and other defects, preheating, quality control and acceptance, visual inspection, non-destructive and destructive testing, the quality-control sheet.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name one material, tool or step this lesson works with.
**Answer:** Any one named in the lesson text — for example, the first step of sub lesson 1: Technical problems and inspection of arc welds.
