# Hands-on — Technical problems and inspection of arc welds

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: edge preparation, arc striking and holding, welding parameters, arc blow, thermal distortion, cracking (and hydrogen's role), porosity and other defects, preheating, quality control and acceptance, visual inspection, non-destructive and destructive testing, the quality-control sheet. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
