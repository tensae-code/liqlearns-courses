# Resistance welding and other methods

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Greece's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Resistance welding and other methods
Greece's book teaches it this way: spot, seam and butt resistance welding; lab — spot-welding sheet metal and car body panels (*Welding*, printed p. 384). The learner works through the named materials and steps in order, saying what each step does before doing the next one.

## Sub lesson 2 — Resistance welding and other methods — doing it yourself
Now the learner does the whole task unaided: spot, seam and butt resistance welding; lab — spot-welding sheet metal and car body panels. Working from the first sub lesson's sequence, the learner plans the make, works through it step by step, and checks the finished piece against what Greece's book shows.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: Spot, seam and butt resistance welding; lab — spot-welding sheet metal and car body panels. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
