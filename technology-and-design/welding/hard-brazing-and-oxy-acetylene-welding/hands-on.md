# Hands-on — Hard brazing and oxy-acetylene welding

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: the oxy-acetylene outfit and the welder's tools/equipment, lighting and shutting down the outfit, flame fusion lines, hard-solder coating, butt and T fillet joints, bronze welding, oxy-fuel cutting and its defects. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
