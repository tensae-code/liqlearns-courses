# Hands-on — Welding safety

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: Safe Handling Techniques for Tools; Safe Maintenance and Safe Procedures; Mechanical Fasteners used in Welding; also: Risk assessment; Risk management; Safe operational procedure of machines; also: Material Handling; Safe Handling Procedures for Tools and Equipment. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
