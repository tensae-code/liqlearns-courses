# Hands-on \u2014 The 3R rule \u2014 repair, reuse, recycle

Real practice \u2014 this subject is learned by doing.

## End-of-life tribunal
Bring three discarded or worn objects. For each, argue the verdict in order: reduce (could this waste have been avoided?), reuse, repair, or recycle. Record the verdict and the reason.

## Criteria scorecard
Choose a product and score it on the five life-cycle criteria (materials, making cost, energy, repairability, recyclability). Write one design change that would improve its worst score.
