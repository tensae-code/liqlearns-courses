Scope: Gabon's secured books are the combined *Super Efficace* Sciences teacher guides (Biologie / Sciences physiques / Technologie / TIC in one volume). Only the Technologie strand is stocked in this course; the Biologie and Sciences physiques strands belong to the Science course and the TIC strand to the ICT course.

Gabon's own way: Gabon's 3R is reduce, reuse, recycle \u2014 taught in Grade 5 with the order defended (recycling costs energy: sorting, collecting, re-making), joined to Grade 4's repair-reuse-recycle reflection and Grade 5's life-cycle criteria for judging designs. The canonical title's \u201crepair\u201d is Gabon's Grade 4 strand; the reduce-first order is Gabon's Grade 5 strand, and both are kept in the provenance rows.
