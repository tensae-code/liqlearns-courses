# The 3R rule \u2014 repair, reuse, recycle \u2014 multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the correct 3R order?
a) Recycle, reuse, reduce b) Reduce, reuse, recycle c) Reuse, recycle, reduce d) Reduce, recycle, reuse
**Answer: b) Reduce, reuse, recycle**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Why does recycling come last?
a) Recycling is useless b) Recycling costs energy \u2014 sorting, collecting, re-making c) Recycling is forbidden d) Recycling is the fastest
**Answer: b) Recycling costs energy \u2014 sorting, collecting, re-making**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A repairable broken object should first be\u2026
a) Thrown away b) Recycled at once c) Repaired, so it lives on d) Buried
**Answer: c) Repaired, so it lives on**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Which is a life-cycle criterion for judging a design?
a) The designer's age b) Possibility of repair c) The shop's name d) The object's colour only
**Answer: b) Possibility of repair**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** The five criteria are materials, making cost, energy needed, and\u2026
a) Repair and recycling possibilities b) Weight and price only c) Brand and look d) Size and shape
**Answer: a) Repair and recycling possibilities**
