# The 3R rule \u2014 repair, reuse, recycle \u2014 true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Recycling costs no energy.
**Answer: False** \u2014 Sorting, collecting and re-making all cost energy \u2014 that is why recycling comes last in the order.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Repairing an object saves raw material and energy.
**Answer: True** \u2014 Each kept object is material and energy not spent on a replacement.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Life-cycle criteria can only be applied after an object is thrown away.
**Answer: False** \u2014 They are for designing and judging objects \u2014 applied best before and while the object is made.
