# The 3R rule \u2014 repair, reuse, recycle \u2014 short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the three end-of-life choices before throwing away.
**Answer:** Repair it, reuse it, or recycle it \u2014 in that order of asking.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Why judge a design by life-cycle criteria before making it?
**Answer:** Because materials, cost, energy, repairability and recyclability are cheapest to fix at the design stage; a well-judged design is easier on the world at every stage.
