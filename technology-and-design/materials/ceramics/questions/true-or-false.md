# Ceramics — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The ceramics room is part of the technology, not just a place.
**Answer: True** — Its norms — light, ventilation, drying shelves, kiln — are taught with the craft.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Firing happens on the drying shelves.
**Answer: False** — Ware dries on the shelves; the kiln does the firing.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Each potter's tool exists for a technique.
**Answer: True** — Materials, kit and technique are taught as one system.
