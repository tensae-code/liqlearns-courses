# Ceramics — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What three things does the ceramics lesson join?
**Answer:** The potter's materials, the tools, and the work techniques — as one system.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the room features the norms name.
**Answer:** Light, ventilation, drying shelves, and the kiln for firing.
