# Ceramics — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the ceramics lesson gather?
a) The potter's materials and tools, and the work techniques b) Only prices c) Only history d) Only painting
**Answer: a) The potter's materials and tools, and the work techniques**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** How are materials, kit and technique related?
a) They are unrelated b) As one system — each tool serves a technique, each technique answers the material c) Only the material matters d) Only the room matters
**Answer: b) As one system — each tool serves a technique, each technique answers the material**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What rests on the drying shelves?
a) The ware, while it dries b) The tools c) The fuel d) The learners' bags
**Answer: a) The ware, while it dries**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the kiln do?
a) Fires the ware b) Dries the room c) Mixes the clay d) Stores tools
**Answer: a) Fires the ware**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Which norms keep the ceramics room?
a) Silence only b) No norms c) Hygiene and work-protection norms — light, ventilation, shelves, kiln d) Kitchen norms
**Answer: c) Hygiene and work-protection norms — light, ventilation, shelves, kiln**
