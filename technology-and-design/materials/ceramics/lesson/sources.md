**Acceptance count: taught in 2 countries (Moldova, Greece).**

Moldova's own way: Moldova teaches ceramics in Grade 6 as a complete workshop system — the potter's materials, tools and techniques inside a normed ceramics room (light, ventilation, drying shelves, kiln) — before the wheel lesson that follows it.

Greece's own way: Greece teaches this inside its Greek-language Gymnasio and EPAL technology books (Ministry of Education / ITYE Diophantos) — raw materials, processing them and making clay, shaping, drying and firing, glazes and decoration, ancient ceramics. The Greek classroom's own examples named in the provenance rows above are part of the teaching, not decoration.
