# Ceramics

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Moldova's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — The potter's materials, tools and techniques
Grade 6 ceramics gathers the craft whole: the potter's materials and tools, and the work techniques that shape clay into ware. Materials, kit and technique are taught as one system — each tool exists for a technique, each technique answers the material.

## Sub lesson 2 — The ceramics room and its norms
The craft has its own room, kept by hygiene and work-protection norms: light to see the work, ventilation for the room's air, drying shelves where ware rests, and the kiln where firing finishes it. The room is part of the technology.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: ceramics is a room as much as a material: the potter's materials and tools, the work techniques, and a ceramics room kept by norms — light, ventilation, drying shelves, and the kiln for firing. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.

## Do It Yourself
1. Do the Do it first task again, carefully this time, using everything the lesson taught.
2. Check your work against the lesson's own checks — fix one thing you find.
3. Keep your work: it is practice evidence, and the next lesson builds on it.
