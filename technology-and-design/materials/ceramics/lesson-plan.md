# Lesson plan — Ceramics

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Materials · **Lesson:** Ceramics
**Place in the curriculum:** Materials (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Moldova expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** ceramics is a room as much as a material: the potter's materials and tools, the work techniques, and a ceramics room kept by norms — light, ventilation, drying shelves, and the kiln for firing.
**This lesson's own level:** stocked from Moldova's *Educație tehnologică* (Technological Education) textbooks — see lesson/sources.md. Moldova counts in the acceptance count (student books, not evidence-only).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **The potter's materials, tools and techniques** — Grade 6 ceramics gathers the craft whole: the potter's materials and tools, and the work techniques that shape clay into ware.
2. **The ceramics room and its norms** — The craft has its own room, kept by hygiene and work-protection norms: light to see the work, ventilation for the room's air, drying shelves where ware rests, and the kiln where firing finishes it.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of Materials. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name the potter's materials, tools and work techniques.
2. Describe the ceramics room and its hygiene and protection norms.
3. Explain how Moldova's book teaches it.
## What you need
Clay and potter's tools, and a ceramics-room plan to study.
