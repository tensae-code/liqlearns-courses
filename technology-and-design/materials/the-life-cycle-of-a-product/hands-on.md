# Hands-on \u2014 The life cycle of a product

Real practice \u2014 this subject is learned by doing.

## Life story
Choose one object and write its full life story: each stage, and the question that stage raises. End with two possible endings \u2014 thrown away, or sorted and recycled \u2014 and what each ending costs.

## Sorting practice
Collect a bag of clean household discards. Sort them into kinds as your local practice does (like Libreville's bottle collection). Record what you sorted, where each kind goes, and what it saves.
