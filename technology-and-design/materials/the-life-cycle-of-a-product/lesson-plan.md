# Lesson plan \u2014 The life cycle of a product

**Course:** Technology and Design \u00b7 **Title:** Materials \u00b7 **Lesson:** The life cycle of a product
**Place in the curriculum:** Materials (CURRICULUM.md) \u2014 stocked in the Gabon expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** every technical object has a life: designed, made, sold, used, and at the end of life sorted, recycled or thrown \u2014 and each stage raises its own questions.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on teacher-guide evidence only \u2014 Gabon's *Super Efficace* Sciences teacher guides (EDIG / \u00c9dicef, Institut P\u00e9dagogique National, 2024) are the books in hand for this topic (see lesson/sources.md). Teacher guides are evidence, counted nowhere in the acceptance count.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on \u2014 an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of \u2026" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **The stages of an object's life** \u2014 From design to end of life, stage by stage, with the question each stage raises.
2. **Sorting and recycling** \u2014 Sorting (tri) and recycling keep materials in use and cut waste.
3. **What sorting saves** \u2014 Recycling saves raw materials and energy and reduces pollution.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson \u2014 no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of Materials. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. List the stages of a technical object's life in order.
2. Explain sorting and the 3R order.
3. Name three things recycling saves or reduces.
## What you need
An everyday object to trace, and examples of sorted materials if available.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** \u2014 the opening doing task in lesson/lesson.md.
