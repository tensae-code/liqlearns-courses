# The life cycle of a product \u2014 short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the stages of a technical object's life.
**Answer:** Design, making, arrival at the point of sale, use, and end of life.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Name the raw materials recycling helps save.
**Answer:** Oil, ores and wood \u2014 they stay un-extracted when recovered material is used instead.
