# The life cycle of a product \u2014 true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** An object's life ends when it is bought.
**Answer: False** \u2014 Being bought is the middle of its life; use and end of life follow.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Recycling is the first choice in the 3R rule.
**Answer: False** \u2014 The order is reduce first, then reuse, then recycle.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Sorting decides whether a material can be recycled.
**Answer: True** \u2014 Separated into kinds, materials can be recycled; mixed together, they are usually lost.
