# The life cycle of a product \u2014 multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which order follows an object's life?
a) Use, design, make, sale, end of life b) Design, make, sale, use, end of life c) Sale, design, use, make, end of life d) Make, end of life, design, use, sale
**Answer: b) Design, make, sale, use, end of life**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What question belongs to the end-of-life stage?
a) What colour is it? b) What becomes of it? c) Who designed it? d) How is it advertised?
**Answer: b) What becomes of it?**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Sorting (tri) means\u2026
a) Throwing everything together b) Separating waste and materials into kinds so they can be recycled c) Burning waste d) Buying new materials
**Answer: b) Separating waste and materials into kinds so they can be recycled**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The 3R rule, in order, is\u2026
a) Recycle, reuse, reduce b) Reduce, reuse, recycle c) Reuse, reduce, recycle d) Remove, redo, recycle
**Answer: b) Reduce, reuse, recycle**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Recycling saves\u2026
a) Only money b) Raw materials and energy, and reduces pollution c) Nothing d) Only time
**Answer: b) Raw materials and energy, and reduces pollution**
