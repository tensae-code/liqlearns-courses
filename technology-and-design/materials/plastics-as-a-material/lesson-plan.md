# Lesson plan — Plastics as a material

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Materials · **Lesson:** Plastics as a material
**Place in the curriculum:** Materials (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Cuba expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** plastics are a material family with an origin, a behaviour under heat, and advantages worth knowing — and recovered plastic is a working material: traced with a template, cut, perforated and made into articles.
**This lesson's own level:** stocked from Cuba's Educación Laboral programmes and methodological orientations (teacher programme documents) — see lesson/sources.md. Cuba is evidence only for this lesson, counted nowhere in the acceptance count.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **What plastics are** — Cuba's courses introduce plastics by their origin and their classification by behaviour under heat — some soften and can be reshaped, others do not. Their advantages (lightness, resistance to water and rot) explain why they are everywhere.
2. **Working recovered plastic** — Recovered plastic is stocked as a real material: articles are traced on it with a template, cut, and perforated on the sufridera (punching block), then joined into finished articles. What others throw away, the workshop uses.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
