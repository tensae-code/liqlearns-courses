# Plastics as a material

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Cuba's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — What plastics are
Cuba's courses introduce plastics by their origin and their classification by behaviour under heat — some soften and can be reshaped, others do not. Their advantages (lightness, resistance to water and rot) explain why they are everywhere.

## Sub lesson 2 — Working recovered plastic
Recovered plastic is stocked as a real material: articles are traced on it with a template, cut, and perforated on the sufridera (punching block), then joined into finished articles. What others throw away, the workshop uses.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: plastics are a material family with an origin, a behaviour under heat, and advantages worth knowing — and recovered plastic is a working material: traced with a template, cut, perforated and made into articles. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
