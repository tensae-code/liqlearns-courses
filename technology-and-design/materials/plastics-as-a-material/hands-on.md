# Hands-on — Plastics as a material

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## Sort by heat behaviour
From pictured or sample plastics, sort what you know: which soften with heat and which do not. Say why the difference matters to a maker.

## Trace, cut, perforate — on paper first
Draw a simple article for recovered plastic. Mark where the template is traced, where cuts fall, and where perforations go on the sufridera.
