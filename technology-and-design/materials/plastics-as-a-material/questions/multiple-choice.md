# Plastics as a material — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is recovered plastic?
a) plastic saved after use and worked again into articles b) Only a price c) Only decoration d) Nothing to do with this lesson
**Answer: a) plastic saved after use and worked again into articles**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is punching block (sufridera)?
a) Only history b) the block that supports material while it is perforated c) Only storage d) A kind of glue
**Answer: b) the block that supports material while it is perforated**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is template (plantilla)?
a) Only old tools b) Only painting c) a pattern used to trace a shape repeatedly and exactly d) Only a price
**Answer: c) a pattern used to trace a shape repeatedly and exactly**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson teach, in one line?
a) Only decoration b) Nothing to do with this lesson c) Only history d) plastics are a material family with an origin, a behaviour under heat, and advantages worth knowing — and recovered plastic is a working material: traced with a template, cut, perforated and made into articles.
**Answer: d) plastics are a material family with an origin, a behaviour under heat, and advantages worth knowing — and recovered plastic is a working material: traced with a template, cut, perforated and made into articles.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** How does Cuba's book teach this?
a) As theory only, never practised b) Only in a glossary c) Only as an exam d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Spanish
**Answer: d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Spanish**
