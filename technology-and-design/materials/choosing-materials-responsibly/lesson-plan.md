# Lesson plan — Choosing materials responsibly

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Materials · **Lesson:** Choosing materials responsibly
**Place in the curriculum:** Materials (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Finland expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** a responsible material choice is checked before you buy or use it — it is safe, it carries trusted labels and certificates, it fits circular-economy thinking, it is used without waste, and it comes from a reliable supplier.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on a student learning material — Finland's *Katse tulevaan* (TuleVa project learning material, published via aoe.fi) (see lesson/sources.md). It counts in the acceptance count (learning material for craft teaching, not evidence-only).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **Safe first — hazard and chemical checks** — The first check is safety.
2. **Labels and certificates you can check** — Trusted marks help you check a claim instead of just believing it.
3. **Circular use, no waste, reliable suppliers** — Responsible choosing also looks past the purchase.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of Materials. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Explain safe first — hazard and chemical checks in your own words.
2. Explain labels and certificates you can check in your own words.
3. Explain circular use, no waste, reliable suppliers in your own words.
## What you need
No materials are needed; if available, one packaged craft material with labels to practise reading.
