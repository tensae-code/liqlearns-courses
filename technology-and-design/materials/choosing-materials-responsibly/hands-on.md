# Hands-on — Choosing materials responsibly

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## Audit one real material
Take one craft material at home or school. Check it against the lesson's list: safety information or safety data sheet, EU eco-label or other eco mark, ISO or Oeko-Tex certificate where relevant, and who supplied it. Write one line for each check — found, not found, or not relevant.

## Plan a waste-free use
For the same material, write how you would use it resource-efficiently: measure before cutting, plan cuts to leave usable leftovers, and say what the leftovers could become.
