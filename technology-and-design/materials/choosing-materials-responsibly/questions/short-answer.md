# Choosing materials responsibly — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 3)** Name two things resource-efficient use means in practice.
**Answer:** Using only what the product needs — for example measuring and planning cuts so little is wasted — and keeping leftovers usable (accept equivalent wording).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why is a label only useful if you know what it stands for?
**Answer:** Because the mark is a claim tested against a named rule; without knowing the rule you cannot tell what has actually been checked.
