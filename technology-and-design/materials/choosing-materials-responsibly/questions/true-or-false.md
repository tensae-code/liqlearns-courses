# Choosing materials responsibly — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** An unknown chemical in a material is a reason to stop and find out more.
**Answer: True** — Chemical criteria are part of the safety check.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A responsible choice ignores where the material came from.
**Answer: False** — Suppliers' reliability and responsibility are part of the check.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Circular-economy thinking includes reusing and recycling material.
**Answer: True** — Reuse and recycling are central to circular thinking.
