# Choosing materials responsibly — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the first check when choosing a material responsibly?
a) That it is safe and non-hazardous b) That it is the cheapest c) That its colour is bright d) That it is the newest
**Answer: a) That it is safe and non-hazardous**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Where do you check a product's chemical safety information?
a) Its safety data sheet b) The price tag only c) Nowhere — chemicals do not matter d) A friend's guess
**Answer: a) Its safety data sheet**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the EU eco-label tell you?
a) The product meets European environmental criteria b) The product was made in one named town c) The product never wears out d) The product is free
**Answer: a) The product meets European environmental criteria**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which of these is a certificate or standard named in the lesson?
a) Oeko-Tex (and ISO criteria) b) A shop's own sticker with no rule behind it c) A coloured ribbon d) No mark at all
**Answer: a) Oeko-Tex (and ISO criteria)**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** What does circular-economy thinking ask about a material?
a) Whether it can be reused, repaired into use or recycled at the end b) Whether it is the heaviest c) Whether it comes in a big packet d) Whether it is shiny
**Answer: a) Whether it can be reused, repaired into use or recycled at the end**
