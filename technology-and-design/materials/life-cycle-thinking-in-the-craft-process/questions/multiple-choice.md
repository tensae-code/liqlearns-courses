# Life-cycle thinking in the craft process — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the first stage on the Finnish craft process map?
a) Material — judged on responsibility, economy and durability b) Selling c) Recycling d) Throwing away
**Answer: a) Material — judged on responsibility, economy and durability**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What happens in the design stage of the map?
a) Sketches, experiments and prototypes, and asking whether the product is necessary b) Only buying tools c) Sorting waste d) Cleaning the finished product
**Answer: a) Sketches, experiments and prototypes, and asking whether the product is necessary**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Which of these belongs to care and repair?
a) Cleaning the product, replacing worn parts and repairing damage b) Choosing the material c) Sketching the design d) Sorting waste
**Answer: a) Cleaning the product, replacing worn parts and repairing damage**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** What happens at the recycling stage?
a) Waste is sorted, material is reused and leftover material is used for something new b) The product is made c) The tools are chosen d) The design is sketched
**Answer: a) Waste is sorted, material is reused and leftover material is used for something new**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** When does life-cycle thinking happen?
a) At every stage — each choice affects the stages after it b) Only at the very end c) Only when buying material d) Never
**Answer: a) At every stage — each choice affects the stages after it**
