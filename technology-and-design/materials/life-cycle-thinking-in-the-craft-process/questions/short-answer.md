# Life-cycle thinking in the craft process — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 3)** Name the six stages of a craft product's life in order.
**Answer:** Material, technique, design, making, care & repair, and recycling.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why does a product that can be repaired live longer than one that cannot?
**Answer:** Damage and worn parts can be fixed instead of ending the product's use, so it stays in service instead of being thrown away.
