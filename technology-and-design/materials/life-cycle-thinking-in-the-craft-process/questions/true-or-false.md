# Life-cycle thinking in the craft process — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The design stage asks whether the product is really necessary.
**Answer: True** — Asking about necessity is part of the design stage on the map.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A product's life ends as soon as the making is finished.
**Answer: False** — After making come care & repair, and then recycling.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Leftover material should always be thrown away at once.
**Answer: False** — The recycling stage uses leftover material for something new.
