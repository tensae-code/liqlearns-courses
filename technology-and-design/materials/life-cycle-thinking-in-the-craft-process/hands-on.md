# Hands-on — Life-cycle thinking in the craft process

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## Draw the life-cycle map
Draw a circle with six stages: material, technique, design, making, care & repair, recycling. Pick a simple product — a wooden spoon or a cloth bag — and write one real sentence for each stage of that product's life.

## Repair before you replace
Find one item at home that is damaged or worn. Write which care-and-repair step could give it a longer life — cleaning, replacing a part, or repairing it — and what recycling step would come if repair truly failed.
