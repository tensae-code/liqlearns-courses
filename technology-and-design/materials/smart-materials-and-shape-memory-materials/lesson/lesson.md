# Smart materials and shape-memory materials

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Iran's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Materials that respond
An ordinary material just bears its conditions; a smart material reacts to them. Some change colour with temperature or light, some produce a voltage when squeezed, some stiffen or soften on command of a magnetic or electric field. The response is built into the material itself, with no motor or computer added.

## Sub lesson 2 — Remembering a shape
A shape-memory alloy can be bent out of shape and then, on heating, returns exactly to the shape it was trained to remember. The book's example is nitinol, an alloy of nickel and titanium: deformed while cool, it recovers its remembered shape when warmed, because its internal crystal structure changes back. Such materials work in medical devices, couplings and actuators that must move without motors.
