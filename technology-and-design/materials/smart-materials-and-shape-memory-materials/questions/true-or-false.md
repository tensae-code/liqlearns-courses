# Smart materials and shape-memory materials — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Smart material is a material that senses a change in its conditions and responds to it.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Shape-memory alloy has nothing to do with this lesson.
**Answer: False** — It is part of the lesson: an alloy that returns to a remembered shape when heated.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Nitinol is the nickel–titanium shape-memory alloy.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.
