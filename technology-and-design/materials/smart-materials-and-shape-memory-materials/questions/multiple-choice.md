# Smart materials and shape-memory materials — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is smart material?
a) a material that senses a change in its conditions and responds to it b) Only a price c) Only decoration d) Nothing to do with this lesson
**Answer: a) a material that senses a change in its conditions and responds to it**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is shape-memory alloy?
a) Only history b) an alloy that returns to a remembered shape when heated c) Only storage d) A kind of glue
**Answer: b) an alloy that returns to a remembered shape when heated**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is nitinol?
a) Only old tools b) Only painting c) the nickel–titanium shape-memory alloy d) Only a price
**Answer: c) the nickel–titanium shape-memory alloy**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson teach, in one line?
a) Only decoration b) Nothing to do with this lesson c) Only history d) smart materials sense a change — heat, light, stress, a field — and respond to it; shape-memory materials go further and return to a shape they remember.
**Answer: d) smart materials sense a change — heat, light, stress, a field — and respond to it; shape-memory materials go further and return to a shape they remember.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** How does Iran's book teach this?
a) As theory only, never practised b) Only in a glossary c) Only as an exam d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Persian
**Answer: d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Persian**
