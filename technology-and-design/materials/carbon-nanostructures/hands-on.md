# Hands-on — Carbon nanostructures

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## Draw the cage
Draw a ball pattern of pentagons and hexagons and label it C60. Under it, write two jobs a hollow carbon cage can do.

## Roll the sheet
Roll a sheet of paper into tubes of two different diameters. Explain, in the lesson's terms, why a carbon tube's properties depend on how it is rolled.
