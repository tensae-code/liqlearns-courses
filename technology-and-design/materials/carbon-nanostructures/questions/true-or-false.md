# Carbon nanostructures — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Fullerene is a hollow cage molecule of carbon atoms, such as C60.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** C60 has nothing to do with this lesson.
**Answer: False** — It is part of the lesson: the fullerene of 60 carbon atoms arranged like a football.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Carbon nanotube is a one-atom-thick carbon sheet rolled into a tube.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.
