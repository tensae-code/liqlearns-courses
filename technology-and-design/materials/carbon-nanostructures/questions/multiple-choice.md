# Carbon nanostructures — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is fullerene?
a) a hollow cage molecule of carbon atoms, such as C60 b) Only a price c) Only decoration d) Nothing to do with this lesson
**Answer: a) a hollow cage molecule of carbon atoms, such as C60**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is C60?
a) Only history b) the fullerene of 60 carbon atoms arranged like a football c) Only storage d) A kind of glue
**Answer: b) the fullerene of 60 carbon atoms arranged like a football**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is carbon nanotube?
a) Only old tools b) Only painting c) a one-atom-thick carbon sheet rolled into a tube d) Only a price
**Answer: c) a one-atom-thick carbon sheet rolled into a tube**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson teach, in one line?
a) Only decoration b) Nothing to do with this lesson c) Only history d) carbon atoms alone can build nano structures — fullerenes, hollow balls of carbon such as C60 and C70, and carbon nanotubes, rolled sheets of carbon with remarkable strength and conductivity.
**Answer: d) carbon atoms alone can build nano structures — fullerenes, hollow balls of carbon such as C60 and C70, and carbon nanotubes, rolled sheets of carbon with remarkable strength and conductivity.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** How does Iran's book teach this?
a) As theory only, never practised b) Only in a glossary c) Only as an exam d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Persian
**Answer: d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Persian**
