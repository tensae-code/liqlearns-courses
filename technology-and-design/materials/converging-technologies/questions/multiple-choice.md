# Converging technologies — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is convergence?
a) different technology families uniting and working together b) Only a price c) Only decoration d) Nothing to do with this lesson
**Answer: a) different technology families uniting and working together**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is nanotechnology?
a) Only history b) technology at the scale of 1–100 nanometres c) Only storage d) A kind of glue
**Answer: b) technology at the scale of 1–100 nanometres**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is cognitive technology?
a) Only old tools b) Only painting c) technology concerned with thinking, learning and decision-making d) Only a price
**Answer: c) technology concerned with thinking, learning and decision-making**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson teach, in one line?
a) Only decoration b) Nothing to do with this lesson c) Only history d) four great technology families — nano, bio, info and cognitive technologies — are uniting, and their meeting points create new powers none of them has alone.
**Answer: d) four great technology families — nano, bio, info and cognitive technologies — are uniting, and their meeting points create new powers none of them has alone.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** How does Iran's book teach this?
a) As theory only, never practised b) Only in a glossary c) Only as an exam d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Persian
**Answer: d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Persian**
