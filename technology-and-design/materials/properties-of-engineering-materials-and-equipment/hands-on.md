# Hands-on — Properties of engineering materials and equipment

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: Types of Engineering Material and their Properties; Types of Engineering Equipment; Methods of Using Material and Equipment in Engineering; Process Equipment Used in Various Engineering. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
