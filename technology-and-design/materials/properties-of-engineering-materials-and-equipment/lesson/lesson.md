# Properties of engineering materials and equipment

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Papua New Guinea's guide teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Properties of engineering materials and equipment
Papua New Guinea's guides teach it this way (the teacher guide — evidence, printed p. 222): Types of Engineering Material and their Properties; Types of Engineering Equipment; Methods of Using Material and Equipment in Engineering; Process Equipment Used in Various Engineering. The learner works through the named materials and steps in order, saying what each step does before doing the next one.

## Sub lesson 2 — Properties of engineering materials and equipment — doing it yourself
Now the learner does the whole task unaided: Types of Engineering Material and their Properties; Types of Engineering Equipment; Methods of Using Material and Equipment in Engineering; Process Equipment Used in Various Engineering. Working from the first sub lesson's sequence, the learner plans the make, works through it step by step, and checks the finished piece against what the PNG guide shows.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: Properties of engineering materials and equipment — Types of Engineering Material and their Properties; Types of Engineering Equipment; Methods of Using Material and Equipment in Engineering; Process Equipment Used in Various Engineering. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
