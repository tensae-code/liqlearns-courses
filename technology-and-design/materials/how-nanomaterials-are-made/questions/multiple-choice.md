# How nanomaterials are made — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is top-down method?
a) making nanomaterials by breaking bulk material down to nano size b) Only a price c) Only decoration d) Nothing to do with this lesson
**Answer: a) making nanomaterials by breaking bulk material down to nano size**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is bottom-up method?
a) Only history b) making nanomaterials by assembling atoms and molecules c) Only storage d) A kind of glue
**Answer: b) making nanomaterials by assembling atoms and molecules**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is photocatalyst?
a) Only old tools b) Only painting c) a material that speeds a chemical reaction when light falls on it d) Only a price
**Answer: c) a material that speeds a chemical reaction when light falls on it**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson teach, in one line?
a) Only decoration b) Nothing to do with this lesson c) Only history d) nanomaterials are made two ways — top-down, breaking bulk material down, and bottom-up, building up from atoms and molecules — and each route suits different products.
**Answer: d) nanomaterials are made two ways — top-down, breaking bulk material down, and bottom-up, building up from atoms and molecules — and each route suits different products.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** How does Iran's book teach this?
a) As theory only, never practised b) Only in a glossary c) Only as an exam d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Persian
**Answer: d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Persian**
