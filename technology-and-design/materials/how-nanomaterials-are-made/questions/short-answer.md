# How nanomaterials are made — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In your own words, what is top-down method?
**Answer:** Making nanomaterials by breaking bulk material down to nano size.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In your own words, what is photocatalyst?
**Answer:** A material that speeds a chemical reaction when light falls on it.
