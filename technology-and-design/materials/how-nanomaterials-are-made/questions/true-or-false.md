# How nanomaterials are made — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Top-down method is making nanomaterials by breaking bulk material down to nano size.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Bottom-up method has nothing to do with this lesson.
**Answer: False** — It is part of the lesson: making nanomaterials by assembling atoms and molecules.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Photocatalyst is a material that speeds a chemical reaction when light falls on it.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.
