# Sustainable waste management — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the first step of Rwanda's waste unit?
a) Burning everything b) Classification of wastes c) Hiding waste d) Buying more
**Answer: b) Classification of wastes**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** By what are wastes grouped?
a) By colour only b) By kind c) By size only d) By owner
**Answer: b) By kind**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is the second half of the classification?
a) The price b) The sources of the wastes c) The smell d) The age
**Answer: b) The sources of the wastes**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Why know a waste's source?
a) To blame someone b) To know what waste to expect before it appears c) For decoration d) It is useless
**Answer: b) To know what waste to expect before it appears**

**5. (Sub lesson 1)** Why must different wastes be classified separately?
a) They behave and harm differently and must be handled differently b) For fun c) To make lists longer d) No reason
**Answer: a) They behave and harm differently and must be handled differently**
