# Sustainable waste management — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Classification of wastes opens Rwanda's sustainable waste management unit.
**Answer: True** — The Primary 6 unit begins with classification of wastes and their sources.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** All wastes come from one single source.
**Answer: False** — Wastes arise from many sources — homes, schools, workshops, farms — and the source is part of the classification.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Knowing the source helps you expect waste before it appears.
**Answer: True** — Source knowledge is the beginning of managing waste rather than suffering it.
