# Sustainable waste management

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Rwanda's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Classification of wastes
Rwanda's Primary 6 book gives waste its own applied unit: the first step is classification — wastes are grouped by kind, because different wastes behave differently, harm differently, and must be handled differently.

## Sub lesson 2 — Where wastes come from — their sources
The second half of the classification is the source: every waste comes from somewhere — a home, a school, a workshop, a farm. Knowing the source tells you what waste to expect before it appears, which is the beginning of managing it rather than suffering it.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: waste is first known by its kind and its source — classify what is thrown away and where it came from, and handling it sustainably becomes possible. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
