# Classification of materials

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Rwanda's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Metals and non-metals
Rwanda's Primary 5 materials unit begins by grouping everyday materials as metals and non-metals. Classification is the first move of materials thinking: before you choose a material for a job, you know which family it belongs to.

## Sub lesson 2 — The general characteristics of each family
Each family carries general characteristics — the shared behaviour that lets you predict how an unfamiliar piece will act. Learn the family's character and a new material stops being a surprise: you can place it, name its family, and expect its ways.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: everyday materials sort into two great families — metals and non-metals — and each family has its own general character that tells you what it is good for. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
