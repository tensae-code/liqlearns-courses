# Classification of materials — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Everyday materials can be grouped as metals and non-metals.
**Answer: True** — That two-family grouping opens Rwanda's Primary 5 materials unit.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A material's family tells you nothing about how it behaves.
**Answer: False** — Each family has general characteristics that let you predict a member's behaviour.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Classification comes before choosing a material for a job.
**Answer: True** — Knowing the family is the first step of materials thinking.
