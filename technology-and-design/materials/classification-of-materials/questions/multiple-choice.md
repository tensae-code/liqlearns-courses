# Classification of materials — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Into which two families does Rwanda group everyday materials?
a) Big and small b) Metals and non-metals c) Old and new d) Hard and soft only
**Answer: b) Metals and non-metals**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is classification the first move of?
a) Shopping b) Materials thinking — knowing the family before choosing c) Drawing d) Cleaning
**Answer: b) Materials thinking — knowing the family before choosing**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What does each family carry?
a) A price b) General characteristics shared by its members c) A colour d) An owner
**Answer: b) General characteristics shared by its members**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** What can you do once you know a family's character?
a) Nothing b) Predict how an unfamiliar piece of that family will behave c) Sell it d) Hide it
**Answer: b) Predict how an unfamiliar piece of that family will behave**

**5. (Sub lesson 1)** When do you classify a material?
a) After throwing it away b) Before choosing it for a job c) Never d) Only in exams
**Answer: b) Before choosing it for a job**
