# Hands-on — Recycling

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## Audit one article's afterlife
Choose a used article (a lamp, a bottle, a notebook). Write what can be recovered from it, what is hazardous, and where each part should go.

## Plan recovery into a build
Take a build you know. Add a recovery stage to its plan: what offcuts and leftovers arise, and what each becomes next.
