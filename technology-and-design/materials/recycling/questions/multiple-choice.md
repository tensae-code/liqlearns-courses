# Recycling — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is recycling (reciclaje)?
a) recovering used material and returning it to production b) Only a price c) Only decoration d) Nothing to do with this lesson
**Answer: a) recovering used material and returning it to production**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is recovery?
a) Only history b) taking a valuable or hazardous material back out of a used article c) Only storage d) A kind of glue
**Answer: b) taking a valuable or hazardous material back out of a used article**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is environmental policy?
a) Only old tools b) Only painting c) the state's rules and aims for protecting the environment d) Only a price
**Answer: c) the state's rules and aims for protecting the environment**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson teach, in one line?
a) Only decoration b) Nothing to do with this lesson c) Only history d) recycling is part of the work process, not an afterthought: know why it matters, what the state policy asks, and how materials — even the mercury in fluorescent lamps — are recovered and returned to use, paper included.
**Answer: d) recycling is part of the work process, not an afterthought: know why it matters, what the state policy asks, and how materials — even the mercury in fluorescent lamps — are recovered and returned to use, paper included.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** How does Cuba's book teach this?
a) As theory only, never practised b) Only in a glossary c) Only as an exam d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Spanish
**Answer: d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Spanish**
