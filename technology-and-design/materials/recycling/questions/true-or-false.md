# Recycling — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Recycling (reciclaje) is recovering used material and returning it to production.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Recovery has nothing to do with this lesson.
**Answer: False** — It is part of the lesson: taking a valuable or hazardous material back out of a used article.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Environmental policy is the state's rules and aims for protecting the environment.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.
