# Recycling

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Cuba's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Why recycling matters
Cuba's 2023 Grade 7 book gives recycling its own section (El reciclaje): recovering materials saves resources and protects the environment, and state environmental policy makes it a duty of the workplace, not a private hobby.

## Sub lesson 2 — Recovering, in practice
The book's examples are concrete: recovering mercury from fluorescent lamps — a hazardous recovery done properly — and recycling paper. Recycling sits inside the work process itself: the maker plans recovery as one of the stages of working.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: recycling is part of the work process, not an afterthought: know why it matters, what the state policy asks, and how materials — even the mercury in fluorescent lamps — are recovered and returned to use, paper included. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
