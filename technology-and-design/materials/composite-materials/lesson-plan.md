# Lesson plan — Composite materials

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Materials · **Lesson:** Composite materials
**Place in the curriculum:** Materials (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Iran expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** a composite joins two different materials — a matrix that holds the shape and a reinforcement that gives strength — so the mixture does what neither part can do alone.
**This lesson's own level:** stocked from Iran's *Use of New Technologies, Grade 11* (student book) — see lesson/sources.md. Iran counts in the acceptance count (student books, not evidence-only).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **Matrix and reinforcement** — Every composite has two parts.
2. **How composites are made** — Iran's book surveys the making routes: powder metallurgy, casting, hot pressing, sintering, extrusion and injection, and hand lay-up of fibre sheets.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of Materials. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Explain composite materials and its main parts, in their own words.
2. Carry out the lesson's central task unaided.
3. Explain how Iran's book teaches it.
