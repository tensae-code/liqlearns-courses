# Nanotechnology — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Nanometre is one billionth of a metre; the nano scale runs 1–100 nm.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Surface effect has nothing to do with this lesson.
**Answer: False** — It is part of the lesson: behaviour dominated by a material's surface, which grows as particles shrink.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Quantum effect is changed behaviour of electrons at very small scales, altering a material's properties.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.
