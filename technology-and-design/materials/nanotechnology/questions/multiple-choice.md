# Nanotechnology — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is nanometre?
a) one billionth of a metre; the nano scale runs 1–100 nm b) Only a price c) Only decoration d) Nothing to do with this lesson
**Answer: a) one billionth of a metre; the nano scale runs 1–100 nm**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is surface effect?
a) Only history b) behaviour dominated by a material's surface, which grows as particles shrink c) Only storage d) A kind of glue
**Answer: b) behaviour dominated by a material's surface, which grows as particles shrink**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is quantum effect?
a) Only old tools b) Only painting c) changed behaviour of electrons at very small scales, altering a material's properties d) Only a price
**Answer: c) changed behaviour of electrons at very small scales, altering a material's properties**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson teach, in one line?
a) Only decoration b) Nothing to do with this lesson c) Only history d) at the nano scale — 1 to 100 nanometres — materials behave differently: surface effects and quantum effects change their properties, and that change is a tool.
**Answer: d) at the nano scale — 1 to 100 nanometres — materials behave differently: surface effects and quantum effects change their properties, and that change is a tool.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** How does Iran's book teach this?
a) As theory only, never practised b) Only in a glossary c) Only as an exam d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Persian
**Answer: d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Persian**
