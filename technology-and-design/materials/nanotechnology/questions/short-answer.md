# Nanotechnology — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In your own words, what is nanometre?
**Answer:** One billionth of a metre; the nano scale runs 1–100 nm.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In your own words, what is quantum effect?
**Answer:** Changed behaviour of electrons at very small scales, altering a material's properties.
