# Nanotechnology

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Iran's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — The nano scale
A nanometre is a billionth of a metre. Nanotechnology works with structures from 1 to 100 nanometres across. Iran's book reaches the scale through investigations with dissolving and size: as a lump is divided smaller and smaller, the total surface grows enormously compared with the volume, until surface behaviour dominates what the material does.

## Sub lesson 2 — Why properties change
Two effects change properties at the nano scale. Surface effects: with so much of the material at its surface, reactions, sticking and dissolving all speed up. Quantum effects: at this smallness, electrons behave in ways that change colour, conductivity and strength. A material that is dull and inert in bulk can be active, coloured or strong as a nanomaterial — the substance is the same, the scale is new.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: at the nano scale — 1 to 100 nanometres — materials behave differently: surface effects and quantum effects change their properties, and that change is a tool. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
