# Hands-on — Nanotechnology

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## Shrink the cube
Take a 1 cm cube. Work out its total surface area. Now imagine it cut into cubes of 1 mm, then smaller. Record how the total surface grows as the pieces shrink.

## Same substance, new behaviour
Name one material and one property that changes when it is made nano-sized. Explain the change using surface or quantum effects.
