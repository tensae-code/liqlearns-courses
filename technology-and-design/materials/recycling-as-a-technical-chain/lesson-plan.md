# Lesson plan — Recycling as a technical chain

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Materials · **Lesson:** Recycling as a technical chain
**Place in the curriculum:** Materials (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Morocco expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** recycling is a technical chain — collection, sorting, treatment and remaking — and the glass bottle walks the whole loop: used bottle in, new bottle out.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on teacher-guide evidence only — Morocco's Technologie teacher guides (Ministère de l'Éducation Nationale, du Préscolaire et des Sports, Royaume du Maroc) are the books in hand for this topic (see lesson/sources.md). ## Sub lessons
1. **The chain — collection, sorting, treatment** — Recycling begins with collection: used products are gathered instead of thrown away. Sorting separates them by material — glass from metal from plastic — because each material is treated differently. Treatment cleans and prepares the material (glass is washed and crushed into cullet) so it can serve again.
2. **Remaking — the glass-bottle loop** — Remaking closes the loop: treated material becomes a new product. Glass shows the loop at its cleanest — a bottle is collected, sorted by colour, washed, crushed, melted and blown into a new bottle, and glass can repeat the loop without losing its quality.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name the links of the recycling chain in order.
2. Say why sorting by material matters.
3. Trace the glass-bottle loop from used bottle to new bottle.
## What you need
A glass bottle (or picture of one) and examples of sorted materials.
