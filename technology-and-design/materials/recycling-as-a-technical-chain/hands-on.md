# Hands-on — Recycling as a technical chain

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## Chain audit
Pick one material your household throws away — glass, plastic, metal, paper. Write its recycling chain link by link. Mark the link most likely to break where you live, and say why.

## Sort it right
Lay out pictures or names of twelve waste items. Sort them into material piles as a sorting link would. For the glass pile, write the treatment steps (wash, crush) and the remade product.
