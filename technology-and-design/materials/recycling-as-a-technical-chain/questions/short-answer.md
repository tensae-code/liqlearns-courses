# Recycling as a technical chain — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Trace the glass-bottle loop in order.
**Answer:** Used bottle → collection → sorting by colour → washing and crushing (cullet) → melting → new bottle → use → collection again.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why is recycling taught as a chain rather than one act?
**Answer:** Because no single act completes it: collection without sorting, or sorting without treatment and remaking, leaves the material unused — every link must do its function.
