# Recycling as a technical chain — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The recycling chain runs in this order:
a) collection, sorting, treatment, remaking b) remaking, collection, sorting, burial c) sorting, burning, collection, use d) collection only — there are no other links
**Answer: a) collection, sorting, treatment, remaking**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Sorting separates waste…
a) by material, because each material is treated differently b) by size only c) by colour of the bin only d) not at all — all waste is mixed
**Answer: a) by material, because each material is treated differently**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Cullet is…
a) washed, crushed glass ready for remaking b) a new kind of plastic c) the sorting machine's name d) burnt glass ash
**Answer: a) washed, crushed glass ready for remaking**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The glass-bottle loop shows that glass…
a) can be melted and remade into new bottles repeatedly b) can be recycled only once c) cannot be recycled d) becomes plastic after melting
**Answer: a) can be melted and remade into new bottles repeatedly**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Putting a bottle in the right bin is…
a) only the first link of the chain b) the whole recycling chain c) not part of recycling d) the treatment link
**Answer: a) only the first link of the chain**
