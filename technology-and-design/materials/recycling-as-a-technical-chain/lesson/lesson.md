# Recycling as a technical chain

## Do it first
Follow a glass bottle you finished today. Where does it go tonight, next week, next month? Name each hand it passes through. That journey, organised, is the recycling chain.

## Sub lesson 1 — The chain — collection, sorting, treatment
Each link of the chain has its own function, like the functions of a technical system. Collection answers 'gather'; sorting answers 'separate by material'; treatment answers 'make the material usable again'. A chain breaks at its weakest link: unsorted waste cannot be treated well, and uncollected waste never enters the chain at all.

## Sub lesson 2 — Remaking — the glass-bottle loop
The glass-bottle loop is Morocco's closing example for the chain: bottle → collection → sorting → washing and crushing → melting → new bottle → use → collection again. Because the loop closes, recycling is taught as a chain, not as a single act: throwing a bottle in the right bin is only the first link, and the chain only works if every link does its job.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: recycling is a technical chain — collection, sorting, treatment and remaking — and the glass bottle walks the whole loop: used bottle in, new bottle out. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
