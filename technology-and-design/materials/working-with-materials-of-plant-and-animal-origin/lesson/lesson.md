# Working with materials of plant and animal origin

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Cuba's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — The materials and their character
Cuba's Grade 9 book opens a chapter to trabajos con materiales variados — work with varied materials of plant and animal origin. Each material brings its own strength, texture and limit, and the maker learns them by handling, not by list.

## Sub lesson 2 — Combining them in one article
The chapter's second movement is combination: plant and animal materials joined with each other and with other materials in a single article. The join must respect both sides — a fibre is not glued like a shell, nor a shell drilled like wood.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: materials of plant and animal origin are worked with their own techniques — fibres, seeds, shells, wood and more — and they combine with other materials in one article, each kept true to what it is. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.

3. Keep your work: it is practice evidence, and the next lesson builds on it.
