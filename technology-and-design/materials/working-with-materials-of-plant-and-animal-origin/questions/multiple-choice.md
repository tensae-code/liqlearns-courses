# Working with materials of plant and animal origin — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is material of plant origin?
a) a working material that comes from plants — fibre, seed, wood, leaf b) Only a price c) Only decoration d) Nothing to do with this lesson
**Answer: a) a working material that comes from plants — fibre, seed, wood, leaf**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is material of animal origin?
a) Only history b) a working material that comes from animals — shell, horn, leather, wool c) Only storage d) A kind of glue
**Answer: b) a working material that comes from animals — shell, horn, leather, wool**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is combination?
a) Only old tools b) Only painting c) joining different materials into one article d) Only a price
**Answer: c) joining different materials into one article**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson teach, in one line?
a) Only decoration b) Nothing to do with this lesson c) Only history d) materials of plant and animal origin are worked with their own techniques — fibres, seeds, shells, wood and more — and they combine with other materials in one article, each kept true to what it is.
**Answer: d) materials of plant and animal origin are worked with their own techniques — fibres, seeds, shells, wood and more — and they combine with other materials in one article, each kept true to what it is.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** How does Cuba's book teach this?
a) As theory only, never practised b) Only in a glossary c) Only as an exam d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Spanish
**Answer: d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Spanish**
