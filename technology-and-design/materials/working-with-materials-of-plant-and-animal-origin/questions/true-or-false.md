# Working with materials of plant and animal origin — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Material of plant origin is a working material that comes from plants — fibre, seed, wood, leaf.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Material of animal origin has nothing to do with this lesson.
**Answer: False** — It is part of the lesson: a working material that comes from animals — shell, horn, leather, wool.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Combination is joining different materials into one article.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.
