# Hands-on — Rubber technology

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## Work the topic
Carry out the topic's core task on a fresh example: what rubber is, its history (from Haiti to the world's plantations), the rubber tree Hevea brasiliensis and latex, and what rubber is used for. Work in order, naming each part or step as you go.

## Check it
Check your finished work against the lesson's own rules. Find one thing that is wrong or weak, fix it, and say what you changed and why.
