# Hands-on — Drawing an object with a changed form

Real drawing practice with pencil and paper — this subject is learned by doing.

## Change and redraw
Choose a part drawing, change one feature, and execute the changed drawing fully — no old dimension of the changed feature may remain.

## Workability check
List what your change affects: fit, strength and use. Mark each as still workable or needing another change, with a reason.
