# Hands-on — Constructing regular polygons inscribed in a circle

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: Regular polygons inscribed in a circle: dividing the circle into 3, 4 and 6 equal parts with compasses and set square to build the triangle, square and hexagon. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
