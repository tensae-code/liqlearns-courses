# Video script — Arranging Dimensions — the Two Methods

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Three steps on a bar. Two ways to write their places.

Chain: each step measured from the last. Compact — but errors add up, link by link.

Parallel: every step measured from one datum face. No error can travel.

Choose by what matters — the step sizes, or the exact positions.

And give no size twice. A size the maker can already work out needs no figure.

Dimension the same bar both ways. Feel the difference.
