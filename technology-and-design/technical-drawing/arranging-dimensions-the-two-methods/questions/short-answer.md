# Arranging Dimensions — the Two Methods — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Three steps are each made 0.2 too long in a chained part. How far has the last shoulder drifted from its intended place?
**Answer: 0.6 — the three small errors have added up along the chain.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Holes along a plate must each sit at an exact distance from the plate's left edge. Which arrangement do you choose, and why?
**Answer: parallel arrangement from the left edge as datum, because each hole is then located independently and no error accumulates.**
