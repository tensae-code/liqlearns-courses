# Graphic design — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Form, space and colour are the language of graphic design.
**Answer: True** — The book names them as its language.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Searching for ideas comes after the design is finished.
**Answer: False** — Idea searching is the designer's first work, before committing to a design.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Print (tipar) is part of the introduction to graphic design.
**Answer: True** — The part introduces print alongside materials, tools and idea searching.
