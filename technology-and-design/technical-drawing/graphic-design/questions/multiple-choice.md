# Graphic design — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What opens the graphic-design part?
a) Its materials and tools b) Its exam c) Its history of printing only d) Its prices
**Answer: a) Its materials and tools**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the language of graphic design?
a) Wood, metal, clay b) Form, space and colour c) Price, speed, size d) Ink only
**Answer: b) Form, space and colour**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** How many words does that language rest on here?
a) Three — form, space, colour b) One c) Ten d) None
**Answer: a) Three — form, space, colour**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** What is tipar in this lesson?
a) Print — where designs are multiplied b) A drawing tool c) A colour d) A paper size
**Answer: a) Print — where designs are multiplied**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** What is the designer's first work?
a) Buying frames b) Signing the work c) Searching for ideas d) Copying exactly
**Answer: c) Searching for ideas**
