# Lesson plan — Graphic design

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Technical Drawing · **Lesson:** Graphic design
**Place in the curriculum:** Technical Drawing (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Moldova expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** graphic design speaks in form, space and colour, with its own materials and tools — and it begins by searching for ideas and meeting print.
**This lesson's own level:** stocked from Moldova's *Educație tehnologică* (Technological Education) textbooks — see lesson/sources.md. Moldova counts in the acceptance count (student books, not evidence-only).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **Materials, tools and the language of graphic design** — Moldova's Grade 9 book opens graphic design with its kit and its language.
2. **Print, and searching for ideas** — The introduction closes with print (tipar) — where graphic designs end up multiplied — and with searching for ideas, the designer's first work: gathering, comparing and choosing directions before committing to one design..

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of Technical Drawing. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name the materials and tools of graphic design.
2. Use its language — form, space and colour — and search for ideas before designing.
3. Explain how Moldova's book teaches it.
## What you need
Drawing and lettering tools, coloured media, and printed examples to study.
