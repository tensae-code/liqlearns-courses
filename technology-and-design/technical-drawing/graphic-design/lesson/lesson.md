# Graphic design

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Moldova's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Materials, tools and the language of graphic design
Moldova's Grade 9 book opens graphic design with its kit and its language. The kit is the materials and tools the graphic designer works with; the language has three words the whole part speaks: form, space and colour. Every later graphic task is judged in that language.

## Sub lesson 2 — Print, and searching for ideas
The introduction closes with print (tipar) — where graphic designs end up multiplied — and with searching for ideas, the designer's first work: gathering, comparing and choosing directions before committing to one design.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: graphic design speaks in form, space and colour, with its own materials and tools — and it begins by searching for ideas and meeting print. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.

## Do It Yourself
1. Do the Do it first task again, carefully this time, using everything the lesson taught.
2. Check your work against the lesson's own checks — fix one thing you find.
3. Keep your work: it is practice evidence, and the next lesson builds on it.
