# Drawing scales and material representation symbols in architectural drawing — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** A room is 4.5 m long. How long is it on a 1:100 plan, and on a 1:10 detail?
**Answer:** Answer: 45 mm at 1:100; 450 mm at 1:10.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Why do plans use symbols instead of drawing materials realistically?
**Answer:** Answer: The symbol is read at a glance, identically by every builder; a realistic drawing would be slow, unclear at small scale, and different from every hand.
