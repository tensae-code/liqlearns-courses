# Drawing scales and material representation symbols in architectural drawing — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A dashed line on a drawing usually shows…
a) A hidden edge b) A visible edge c) A centre line d) A dimension
**Answer: a) A hidden edge**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Drawing a line in the wrong type is dangerous because…
a) The reader trusts the code and builds what the line says b) It looks untidy only c) The paper tears d) Nothing changes
**Answer: a) The reader trusts the code and builds what the line says**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** At 1:100, a 6 m wall is drawn…
a) 6 cm long b) 6 m long c) 60 cm long d) 6 mm long
**Answer: a) 6 cm long**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A 45 mm line at 1:10 represents…
a) 450 mm in reality b) 45 mm in reality c) 4.5 m in reality d) 10 mm in reality
**Answer: a) 450 mm in reality**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Materials in a plan section are shown by…
a) Agreed standard symbols and hatchings b) Realistic drawings of the material c) Colour photographs d) Leaving the space blank
**Answer: a) Agreed standard symbols and hatchings**
