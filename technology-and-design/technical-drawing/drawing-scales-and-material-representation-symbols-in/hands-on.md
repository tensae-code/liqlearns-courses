# Hands-on — Drawing scales and material representation symbols in architectural drawing

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## Convert both ways
Take three real lengths (a door, a room, a book). Convert each to paper length at 1:100 and at 1:10. Then take two paper lengths and convert them back to reality at both scales.

## Symbol legend
Draw a small legend for a plan: invent no symbols — use standard material patterns for concrete, timber and earth as shown in Samoa's book (pp. 5-6) and label each. Then draw one wall section using two of them.
