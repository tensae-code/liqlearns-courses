# Hands-on — Character design for animation

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: building a character from circles and ovals, head-count proportions (a small child 1/3, a tall figure 1/5–1/6 of the body), and drawing movement along the line of action (the running-rabbit cycle). Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
