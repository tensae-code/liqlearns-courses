# Character design for animation — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Say in one sentence what this lesson teaches.
**Answer:** Character design for animation — building a character from circles and ovals, head-count proportions (a small child 1/3, a tall figure 1/5–1/6 of the body), and drawing movement along the line of action (the running-rabbit cycle).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name one material, tool or step this lesson works with.
**Answer:** Any one named in the lesson text — for example, the first step of sub lesson 1: Character design for animation.
