# Hands-on — Symbolising map data in QGIS

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: visualising point information with symbols scaled to the data (settlements sized by census population 1991/2001/2011), the rule that bigger qualitative difference needs bigger visual separation. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
