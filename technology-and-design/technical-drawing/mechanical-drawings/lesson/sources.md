| mechanical drawings: the detail drawing (bản vẽ chi tiết) — its content is the representations, the dimensions, the technical requirements and the title block; the representations express the shape of each part of the machine part; the technical requirements include the tolerance symbols and the surface-roughness values of the part's surfaces, and instructions on machining and surface treatment; the title block holds the drawing-management contents and product management; the information box explains surface roughness (độ nhám bề mặt, symbol Ra or Rz — after machining a surface is never perfectly flat; the smaller the roughness value the smoother the surface) and dimensional tolerance (dung sai kích thước — the difference between the largest and smallest limit sizes; e.g. 56⁺⁰·¹ means the largest limit size is 56.1 and the smallest 55.9, a tolerance of 0.2; a machined part meets the technical requirement if its measured size lies between 56.1 and 55.9); reading a detail drawing means fully and exactly understanding its contents — the part's name, use, shape, structure, dimensions and material, and its technical requirements (practice: read the bearing-support detail drawing) (original string `mechanical drawings`) | Vietnam, *Công nghệ 10 — Thiết kế và công nghệ*, printed p. 80 |
