# Hands-on — Drawings of joined parts

Real drawing practice with pencil and paper — this subject is learned by doing.

## Sort the joints
Find and draw four joints — two detachable, two permanent — labelling family, kind and the parts joined in each.

## Show the joint
Draw one detachable joint (a bolted pair of plates) and one permanent joint (a riveted or welded pair) as they are shown on drawings, with designations where they belong.
