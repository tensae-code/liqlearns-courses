# Drawings of joined parts — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A detachable joint is one that…
a) Must be cut apart b) Can be taken apart without damage c) Is always welded d) Has no fastening
**Answer: b) Can be taken apart without damage**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which is a permanent joint?
a) A bolted joint b) A welded joint c) A screwed joint d) A pinned removable joint
**Answer: b) A welded joint**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Why does a drawing show a joint by an agreed representation?
a) For decoration b) So the joint can be made (and, if detachable, taken apart) from the drawing c) To hide the fastening d) To save ink only
**Answer: b) So the joint can be made (and, if detachable, taken apart) from the drawing**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** The first step in reading a joint on a drawing is to…
a) Measure the sheet b) Name its family — detachable or permanent c) Count the parts d) Copy the title block
**Answer: b) Name its family — detachable or permanent**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Riveted and soldered joints belong to the…
a) Detachable family b) Permanent family c) Threaded family d) Loose family
**Answer: b) Permanent family**
