# Drawings of joined parts — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** A riveted joint is detachable.
**Answer: False** — Riveting is permanent; separation destroys the joint.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A drawing of a detachable joint should let it be taken apart from the drawing.
**Answer: True** — Showing the fastening and parts serves making and later separation.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** All joints are shown the same way on drawings.
**Answer: False** — Each kind has its agreed representation and designation.
