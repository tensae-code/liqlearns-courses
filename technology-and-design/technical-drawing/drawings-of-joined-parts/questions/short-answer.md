# Drawings of joined parts — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, or by doing the drawing step named.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the two families of joints and one example of each.
**Answer:** Detachable (e.g. threaded/bolted) and permanent (e.g. welded, soldered, riveted).

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the order family → kind → representation tell a reader?
**Answer:** How the thing was assembled and whether it can ever come apart.
