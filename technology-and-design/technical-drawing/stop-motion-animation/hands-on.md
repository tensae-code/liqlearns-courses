# Hands-on — Stop-motion animation

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: its kinds (sand, cut-out, puppet, claymation, object and strata-cut), the puppet's wire armature, storyboards, and how Aardman's films are shot. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
