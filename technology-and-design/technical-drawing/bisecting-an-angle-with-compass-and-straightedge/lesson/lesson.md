## Do it first

## Sub lesson 1 — Bisecting an angle with compass and straightedge
Mongolia's book teaches it this way (*Design Technology 6 (Mongolia)*, printed p. 23): Bisecting an angle step by step (a 90° angle, arc R = 30 mm), and dividing a circle into 8 or 16 parts by the same method. The learner works through the named materials and steps in order, saying what each step does before doing the next one.

Working from the first sub lesson's sequence, the learner plans the make, works through it step by step, and checks the finished piece against what Mongolia's book shows.
