# Hands-on — Bisecting an angle with compass and straightedge

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: Bisecting an angle step by step (a 90° angle, arc R = 30 mm), and dividing a circle into 8 or 16 parts by the same method. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
