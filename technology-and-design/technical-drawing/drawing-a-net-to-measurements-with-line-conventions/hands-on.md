# Hands-on — Drawing a net to measurements with line conventions

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: dashed line = fold, solid line = cut, black = glue tab; a character box net 10 cm wide with 1 cm tabs. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
