# Advertising design — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the worked example for the poster in Moldova's book?
a) A poster for a school event b) A shop window c) A book cover d) A road sign
**Answer: a) A poster for a school event**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What comes first in making the poster?
a) Printing b) Study c) Hanging d) Framing
**Answer: b) Study**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the study settle?
a) What the poster must say, to whom, and where it will be seen b) The price of paper c) The maker's marks d) The weather
**Answer: a) What the poster must say, to whom, and where it will be seen**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** What follows the study?
a) Elaborating the production project for the advertising material b) Throwing the study away c) Copying another poster d) Guessing the layout
**Answer: a) Elaborating the production project for the advertising material**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the production project plan?
a) Only the colours b) Only the frame c) Layout, lettering and making — as a project to carry out d) Only the date
**Answer: c) Layout, lettering and making — as a project to carry out**
