# Lesson plan — Advertising design

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Technical Drawing · **Lesson:** Advertising design
**Place in the curriculum:** Technical Drawing (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Moldova expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** an advertising poster is made in stages — first study, then the production project for the advertising material — so the poster is designed before it is produced.
**This lesson's own level:** stocked from Moldova's *Educație tehnologică* (Technological Education) textbooks — see lesson/sources.md. Moldova counts in the acceptance count (student books, not evidence-only).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **Study first** — Moldova's Grade 9 graphic-design part begins poster work with study: what the poster must say, to whom, and where it will be seen.
2. **The production project** — Study becomes a production project for the advertising material: the poster's layout, lettering and making are planned as a project to be carried out, not improvised at the drawing board.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of Technical Drawing. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the stages of making an advertising poster.
2. Carry a poster from study to a production project.
3. Explain how Moldova's book teaches it.
## What you need
Paper, drawing tools, and a real school event to advertise.
