# Advertising design

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Moldova's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Study first
Moldova's Grade 9 graphic-design part begins poster work with study: what the poster must say, to whom, and where it will be seen. The school event is the worked example — a real message, a real audience, a real wall.

## Sub lesson 2 — The production project
Study becomes a production project for the advertising material: the poster's layout, lettering and making are planned as a project to be carried out, not improvised at the drawing board. Designing the advertisement is graphic design put to work.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: an advertising poster is made in stages — first study, then the production project for the advertising material — so the poster is designed before it is produced. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.

## Do It Yourself
1. Do the Do it first task again, carefully this time, using everything the lesson taught.
2. Check your work against the lesson's own checks — fix one thing you find.
3. Keep your work: it is practice evidence, and the next lesson builds on it.
