Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: Types of CAD Program or Free CAD; Using CAD Program; Methods of Downloading and Installing CAD Program; Methods of Sketching Domestic Building — Gable Roof; also: AUTOCAD Program in Engineering Fields; Types of AUTOCAD Program; Significance of AUTOCAD Program; Methods of Downloading AUTOCAD Program; also: Purpose of AutoCAD Program; Types of AutoCAD program; Methods of Downloading CAD/Autodesk Software; Designing Domestic Building Using CAD Program. ## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
