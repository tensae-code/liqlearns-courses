# Lesson plan — Photographic composition

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Technical Drawing · **Lesson:** Photographic composition
**Place in the curriculum:** Technical Drawing (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Mongolia expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Photographic composition — the rule of thirds (the 90-10, 80-20, 70-30, 60-40 balances), the golden section and the Fibonacci golden spiral (0, 1, 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13, 21…), light and shadow, colour, form and balance.
**This lesson's own level:** stocked from Mongolia's *Visual Art & Technology (Дүрслэх урлаг, технологи)* student books (Ministry of Education, Culture, Science and Sports of Mongolia) — see lesson/sources.md. It counts in the acceptance count (student book, not evidence-only). Mongolia's books are image-only scans read by a VISUAL-READ method, as filed in lesson/sources.md.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
