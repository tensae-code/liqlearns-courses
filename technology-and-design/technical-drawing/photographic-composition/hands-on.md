# Hands-on — Photographic composition

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: the rule of thirds (the 90-10, 80-20, 70-30, 60-40 balances), the golden section and the Fibonacci golden spiral (0, 1, 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13, 21…), light and shadow, colour, form and balance. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
