# Photographic composition — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Say in one sentence what this lesson teaches.
**Answer:** Photographic composition — the rule of thirds (the 90-10, 80-20, 70-30, 60-40 balances), the golden section and the Fibonacci golden spiral (0, 1, 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13, 21…), light and shadow, colour, form and balance.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name one material, tool or step this lesson works with.
**Answer:** Any one named in the lesson text — for example, the first step of sub lesson 1: Photographic composition.
