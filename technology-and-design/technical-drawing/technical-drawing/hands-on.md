# Hands-on — Technical drawing

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## Coin at 2:1
Measure a coin's diameter with a ruler. Draw its circle at scale 2:1 (double). Measure your drawing and divide by 2 to check you recover the real diameter.

## Which drawing?
For three jobs — show a visitor what a machine looks like; show a fitter how its parts assemble; give a machinist one part to make — name the right drawing type for each (cavalier perspective, assembly drawing, detail drawing) and justify each choice.
