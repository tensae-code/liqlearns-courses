# Technical drawing — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does each drawing type show: cavalier perspective and assembly drawing?
**Answer:** Cavalier perspective shows the object's shape in one view (front true, depth angled and shortened). The assembly drawing shows the whole mechanism assembled, parts in place and numbered.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A coin's real diameter is 25 mm. How big is it drawn at 2:1, and how do you check?
**Answer:** 50 mm on the drawing; check by dividing the drawn length by the scale: 50 ÷ 2 = 25 mm, the real size.
