# Drawing instruments, materials and the drawing workplace — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why does each drawing instrument matter?
a) Each is made for one kind of line, curve or job b) They are all interchangeable c) Only for decoration d) To fill the desk
**Answer: a) Each is made for one kind of line, curve or job**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Where are additional materials for independent work listed?
a) On the title block only b) In the course's appendices c) They are never listed d) On the sheet margin
**Answer: b) In the course's appendices**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What is done first at the drawing workplace?
a) The drawing is signed b) The sheet is fixed square and instruments are set in reach c) The eraser is thrown away d) The light is switched off
**Answer: b) The sheet is fixed square and instruments are set in reach**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** A finished drawing sheet should be kept…
a) Folded into a pocket b) Flat and clean c) Under the instruments d) Rolled with the drawing inside a tube of rubbish
**Answer: b) Flat and clean**

**5. (Sub lesson 1)** How have drawings been executed through history?
a) Always by computer b) In different ways, while hand instruments stay recognisable c) Only with a pen d) Never on paper
**Answer: b) In different ways, while hand instruments stay recognisable**
