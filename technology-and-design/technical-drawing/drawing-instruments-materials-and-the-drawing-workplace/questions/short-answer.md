# Drawing instruments, materials and the drawing workplace — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, or by doing the drawing step named.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name three drawing instruments and one job for each.
**Answer:** For example: pencil — lines and lettering; compasses — circles and arcs; ruler — straight measured lines.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Give two rules of a good drawing workplace.
**Answer:** Sheet fixed square, instruments complete and within reach, good light, finished sheets kept flat and clean (any two).
