# Hands-on — Drawing instruments, materials and the drawing workplace

Real drawing practice with pencil and paper — this subject is learned by doing.

## Set the workplace
Set up a complete drawing workplace from a list, without help. Check: sheet square and fixed, instruments complete, light good, spare paper for trial lines.

## Instruments through history
Draw or list three ways drawings have been executed at different times, and name one instrument or material that belongs to each way.
