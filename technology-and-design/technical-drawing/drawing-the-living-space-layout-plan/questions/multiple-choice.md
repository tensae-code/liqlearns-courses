# Drawing the living-space layout plan — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Where does the arrangement plan start?
a) From the beneficiary's individual wishes and needs b) From the furniture shop c) From a copied plan d) From the walls alone
**Answer: a) From the beneficiary's individual wishes and needs**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Who is the beneficiary?
a) The builder only b) The person the space is designed for c) The teacher d) Any passer-by
**Answer: b) The person the space is designed for**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** When is the plan drawn?
a) On paper first, before anything is moved or built b) After the furniture is placed c) Never — it stays an idea d) Only for marking
**Answer: a) On paper first, before anything is moved or built**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The plan values the space in which two ways?
a) Functionally and aesthetically b) Cheaply and quickly c) Big and small d) Old and new
**Answer: a) Functionally and aesthetically**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** What happens to a plan that works but is unpleasant to be in?
a) It passes b) It is built anyway c) It is sent back — it fails the aesthetic test d) It wins a prize
**Answer: c) It is sent back — it fails the aesthetic test**
