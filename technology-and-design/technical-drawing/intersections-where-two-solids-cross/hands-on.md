# Hands-on — Intersections — Where Two Solids Cross

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## The pencil-and-box test, drawn
Repeat the opening task slowly: push the pencil through the box at two different angles and trace each intersection line on the box with a marker. Compare the two curves — the steeper angle stretches the curve — then say why a drawing must find the curve for the exact angle, not a near one.

## Fit is the proof
From your Do It Yourself development, cut and roll the cylinder and offer it up to the prism. Mark every place the edge gaps or overlaps, then trace each fault back to its piercing point on the drawing. A fault in the fit is a point plotted wrong — find it on paper, not at the bench.
