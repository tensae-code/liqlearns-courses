# Intersections — Where Two Solids Cross — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The line of intersection is —
a) the outline of the bigger solid  b) the line where the surfaces of two solids cross, lying on both surfaces at once  c) a centre line
**Answer: b) the line where the surfaces of two solids cross, lying on both surfaces at once**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A piercing point is —
a) the point where an edge or generator of one solid passes through the surface of the other  b) the apex of a cone  c) the centre of a circle
**Answer: a) the point where an edge or generator of one solid passes through the surface of the other**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Piercing points on a cylinder are conveniently found by —
a) guessing  b) dividing its surface into generators and reading where each pierces the other solid's face  c) folding the drawing
**Answer: b) dividing its surface into generators and reading where each pierces the other solid's face**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** An auxiliary cutting plane finds a point of the intersection where —
a) its cut across the first surface crosses its cut across the second surface  b) the plane leaves the sheet  c) two centre lines cross
**Answer: a) its cut across the first surface crosses its cut across the second surface**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Parts of the intersection curve are drawn dashed when they —
a) are the longest  b) pass behind or inside the solids, on surfaces turned away from the viewer  c) were found by cutting planes
**Answer: b) pass behind or inside the solids, on surfaces turned away from the viewer**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** A piercing point is carried into a development —
a) anywhere it fits  b) on its own generator or face, at its true distance along it  c) only in the title block
**Answer: b) on its own generator or face, at its true distance along it**
