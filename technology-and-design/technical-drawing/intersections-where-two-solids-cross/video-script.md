# Video script — Intersections — Where Two Solids Cross

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Push one solid through another. Where their surfaces cross, a line is born.

That line belongs to both surfaces. Find it point by point — piercing point by piercing point.

Work from generators where a face shows edge-on. Work through cutting planes where none does.

Sort the curve: visible where the surfaces face you, hidden where they turn away.

Then carry every point into the development, on its own generator, at its true distance.

Cut to that curve, and the two solids fit. That is the whole unit, working.
