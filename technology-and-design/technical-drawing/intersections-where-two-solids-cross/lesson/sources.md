Era note: this guide is an older GEQIP-generation book (first edition, 2003 E.C., about 2010/11) — an earlier curriculum generation than Ethiopia's newer books. This lesson presents it as exactly that: the guide's testimony about that era's Grade 12 Technical Drawing course. It is never presented as the current syllabus, and nothing here has been checked against a current syllabus. No pupil (student) book for Technical Drawing has been read — no student book exists in hand — so this lesson stands on teacher-guide evidence only.

Book error, taught corrected: the guide numbers this lesson 6.3, inside Unit 5 — there is no Unit 6 in the book (see the development-introduction lesson's sources). The placements record that the guide teaches 'the two methods of finding points of intersection' used alternately, without naming them in the placement summary; the methods taught here — direct work from edges/generators where a surface shows edge-on, and auxiliary cutting planes — are the standard pair for this work, and every construction in this lesson was checked for geometric correctness in this build (each piercing point lies on both surfaces; carried points sit on their own generators at true distances).
