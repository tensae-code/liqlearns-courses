# Hands-on — Sectioning — words and common practice

Real drawing practice with pencil and paper — this subject is learned by doing.

## Cut a fruit, draw it
Cut an orange or onion in half. Draw the cut face and hatch — on paper — only the solid flesh the knife passed through.

## First section
Draw the front and section views of an open box with a ball inside it. The ball lies beyond the cut: outline only, no hatching on the ball.
