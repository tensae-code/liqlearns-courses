# Sectioning — words and common practice — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** A hole the cutting plane passes through is hatched.
**Answer: False** — Empty space was not cut; voids stay unhatched.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The section view is the view from the direction of the cutting-plane arrows.
**Answer: True** — The arrows show the side you look from.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Parts lying beyond the cutting plane are drawn in outline without hatching.
**Answer: True** — Only material the plane itself cuts is lined.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** When drawing a section, you first choose where the cut reveals most.
**Answer: True** — The cut is placed to reveal the interior features that matter.
