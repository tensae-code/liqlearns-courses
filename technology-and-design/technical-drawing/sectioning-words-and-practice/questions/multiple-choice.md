# Sectioning — words and common practice — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A section view is made by…
a) really sawing the object in the classroom b) imagining the object cut and the nearer part removed c) photographing the object d) shading the outside
**Answer: b) imagining the object cut and the nearer part removed**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The cutting-plane line shows…
a) the colour of the object b) where the object was cut, with arrows for the direction of sight c) the price of the part d) the outside edges only
**Answer: b) where the object was cut, with arrows for the direction of sight**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Section lining (hatching) is drawn on…
a) every face in the view b) only the faces the cutting plane cut through solid material c) holes and empty spaces d) the title block
**Answer: b) only the faces the cutting plane cut through solid material**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Hatching lines are usually drawn…
a) at random angles b) thin, evenly spaced, usually at 45 degrees, one direction per part c) as thick black bands d) only vertically
**Answer: b) thin, evenly spaced, usually at 45 degrees, one direction per part**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** In an assembly section, adjacent parts are hatched…
a) identically, so they merge b) in different directions or spacings, so each part stays distinct c) not at all d) in colour only
**Answer: b) in different directions or spacings, so each part stays distinct**
