# Sectioning — words and common practice — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, or by doing the drawing step named.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the line that shows where the object was cut and what its arrows mean.
**Answer:** The cutting-plane line; its arrows show the direction of sight for the section view.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** State the hatching rule in one sentence.
**Answer:** Hatch only solid material the cutting plane passed through; leave voids and uncut parts unhatched.
