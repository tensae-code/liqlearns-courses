# Hands-on — Electronic design of cutting patterns (CAD)

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: CAD garment-design programs — their use and operation, pattern grading on computer (skirt, bodice, sleeve, trousers), preparing the pattern for cutting, creating and constructing the marker (τοποθέτηση), and printing/plotting. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
