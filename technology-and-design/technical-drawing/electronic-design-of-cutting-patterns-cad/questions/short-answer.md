# Electronic design of cutting patterns (CAD) — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Say in one sentence what this lesson teaches.
**Answer:** CAD garment-design programs — their use and operation, pattern grading on computer (skirt, bodice, sleeve, trousers), preparing the pattern for cutting, creating and constructing the marker (τοποθέτηση), and printing/plotting.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name one material, tool or step this lesson works with.
**Answer:** Any one named in the lesson text — for example, the first step of sub lesson 1: Electronic design of cutting patterns (CAD).
