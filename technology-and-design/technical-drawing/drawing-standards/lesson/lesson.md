# Drawing standards

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Cuba's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — What a norm is
Cuba's 2023 Grade 7 book opens its standards teaching (§1.2) with the idea itself: a norm is an agreed, written rule. Drawings follow one so that any reader, anywhere, reads the same drawing the same way.

## Sub lesson 2 — The Cuban norm, from ISO
The Norma Cubana for drawing adapts the international ISO norms (NC-ISO) to Cuban practice. The Grade 8 programme builds on it throughout its drawing unit — formats, lettering, dimensioning and scales as the NC-ISO norms prescribe.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: a norm (Norma Cubana, NC-ISO) is the agreed rule that makes drawings readable by everyone: what a norm is, why drawings follow one, and how the Cuban norm adapts the ISO standard. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
