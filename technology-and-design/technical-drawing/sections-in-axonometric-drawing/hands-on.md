# Hands-on — Sections in axonometric drawing

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: cutting a quarter away to show the inside, and drawing the ellipse that represents a circle in isometric (major axis 1.22d, minor 0.71d, built by the 8-point method). Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
