# Hands-on — Developing Solids — Parallel Line, Radial Line, Triangulation

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Roll the cylinder, fold the prism
Make both developments from the parallel-line sub lesson in card: roll the cylinder's rectangle and tape its seam — measure the tube's diameter and compare it with the drawing's. Fold the prism and check its edges meet without forcing.

## The cone that must meet itself
Cut the cone sector from your Do It Yourself work, roll it, and set it on its drawn base circle. If the edges overlap or gap, re-measure the arc against the circumference and find where the pattern went wrong.
