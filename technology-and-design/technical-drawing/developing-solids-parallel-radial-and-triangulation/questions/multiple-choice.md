# Developing Solids — Parallel Line, Radial Line, Triangulation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Parallel-line development is used for —
a) cones and pyramids  b) prisms and cylinders  c) spheres
**Answer: b) prisms and cylinders**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The stretch-out line for a prism equals —
a) its height  b) the perimeter of its base  c) one face's width
**Answer: b) the perimeter of its base**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The development of a cylinder of diameter 50 is a rectangle whose long side is about —
a) 50  b) 100  c) 157
**Answer: c) 157 (pi times the diameter)**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Radial-line development is used for solids whose edges —
a) are all parallel  b) all meet at one apex — pyramids and cones  c) are all hidden
**Answer: b) all meet at one apex — pyramids and cones**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The radius of a cone's development sector is —
a) the base radius  b) the vertical height  c) the slant height
**Answer: c) the slant height**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** A cone has base radius 30 and slant height 90. Its sector angle is —
a) 90 degrees  b) 120 degrees  c) 180 degrees
**Answer: b) 120 degrees (360 x 30/90)**

**7. (Sub lesson 3)** In the true-length construction, the hypotenuse of the right triangle gives —
a) the plan length  b) the true length of the inclined edge  c) the height difference only
**Answer: b) the true length of the inclined edge**

**8. (Sub lesson 3)** Triangulation develops a surface by —
a) rolling it  b) dividing it into triangles and building each at its true lengths, sharing edges in order  c) copying the elevation
**Answer: b) dividing it into triangles and building each at its true lengths, sharing edges in order**
