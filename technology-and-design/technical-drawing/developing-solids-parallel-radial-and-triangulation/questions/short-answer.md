# Developing Solids — Parallel Line, Radial Line, Triangulation — short answer

Answer in a short sentence or a small calculation.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why is a cylinder's pattern rolled, not folded?
**Answer: because its curved surface unrolls smoothly — the rectangle bends round the generators; there are no fold edges.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A pyramid's slant edge leans in both views. What must be done before the radial development can be swung?
**Answer: its true length must be found (by the true-length rule), because the development arc is swung with the true slant length as radius.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** An edge measures 40 in plan and its far end stands 30 higher than its near end. What is its true length?
**Answer: 50 — the hypotenuse of the right triangle with base 40 and upright 30.**
