# Video script — Developing Solids — Parallel Line, Radial Line, Triangulation

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Three solids, three constructions. Choose by the shape of the edges.

Edges parallel? Parallel line. Stretch out the perimeter — or the circumference — and raise the faces on it.

Edges meeting at an apex? Radial line. Swing the true slant length, step the base along the arc.

A cone becomes a sector. Its arc must equal the base circumference. Check it before you cut.

Edges leaning every way? Triangulation. Make every length true with a right triangle.

Then build the surface triangle by triangle, sharing edges, until it lies flat.

Develop all three. Card first. Check, then cut.
