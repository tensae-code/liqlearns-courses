# Topographic drawing

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Cuba's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — First notions of drawing the land
Cuba's Grade 9 book places dibujo topográfico inside its agriculture chapter (§1.2): before the garden is worked, the land is drawn. The learner takes the first notions — what of the land must appear on paper for the drawing to guide real work.

## Sub lesson 2 — The land drawing at work
A topographic drawing serves the production around it: beds, nurseries, plantings and paths are placed on the drawing before they are placed on the ground. It is drawing in the service of agriculture, filed by the book between soil preparation and the garden tools.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: topographic drawing (dibujo topográfico) draws the land itself: the first notions of representing ground — its shape and features — so that work on the land can be planned on paper. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
