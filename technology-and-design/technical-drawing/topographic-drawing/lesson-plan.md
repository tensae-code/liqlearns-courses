# Lesson plan — Topographic drawing

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Technical Drawing · **Lesson:** Topographic drawing
**Place in the curriculum:** Technical Drawing (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Cuba expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** topographic drawing (dibujo topográfico) draws the land itself: the first notions of representing ground — its shape and features — so that work on the land can be planned on paper.
**This lesson's own level:** stocked from Cuba's *Educación Laboral — 9no grado* and the Cuba books in lesson/sources.md — see lesson/sources.md. Cuba counts in the acceptance count for its student-book placements; its programme and methodological-orientations placements are evidence only, counted nowhere.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **First notions of drawing the land** — Cuba's Grade 9 book places dibujo topográfico inside its agriculture chapter (§1.2): before the garden is worked, the land is drawn. The learner takes the first notions — what of the land must appear on paper for the drawing to guide real work.
2. **The land drawing at work** — A topographic drawing serves the production around it: beds, nurseries, plantings and paths are placed on the drawing before they are placed on the ground. It is drawing in the service of agriculture, filed by the book between soil preparation and the garden tools.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
