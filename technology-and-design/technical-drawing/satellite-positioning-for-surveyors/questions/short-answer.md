# Satellite positioning for surveyors — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Say in one sentence what this lesson teaches.
**Answer:** What GPS is (the Global Positioning System, position in X, Y, Z), the older ways of fixing position (stars, compass, sextant, radio-wave trilateration from transmitters of known coordinates), the satellite constellations (Navstar GPS, GLONASS, BeiDou, Galileo/GNSS), how a GPS fix works (triangulation between spheres, satellite timing/synchronisation, distance measurement), receiver types and accuracy (simple receivers 3–5 m, dual-frequency GPS for higher accuracy).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name one material, tool or step this lesson works with.
**Answer:** Any one named in the lesson text — for example, the first step of sub lesson 1: Satellite positioning for surveyors.
