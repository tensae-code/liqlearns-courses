# Video script — Satellite positioning for surveyors

A short demonstration video, made later, in a super-simple voice.

Satellite positioning for surveyors.
What GPS is (the Global Positioning System, position in X, Y, Z), the older ways of fixing position (stars, compass, sextant, radio-wave trilateration from transmitters of known coordinates), the satellite constellations (Navstar GPS, GLONASS, BeiDou, Galileo/GNSS), how a GPS fix works (triangulation between spheres, satellite timing/synchronisation, distance measurement), receiver types and accuracy (simple receivers 3–5 m, dual-frequency GPS for higher accuracy).
Watch the steps. Then do them yourself.
