# Hands-on — Satellite positioning for surveyors

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: what GPS is (the Global Positioning System, position in X, Y, Z), the older ways of fixing position (stars, compass, sextant, radio-wave trilateration from transmitters of known coordinates), the satellite constellations (Navstar GPS, GLONASS, BeiDou, Galileo/GNSS), how a GPS fix works (triangulation between spheres, satellite timing/synchronisation, distance measurement), receiver types and accuracy (simple receivers 3–5 m, dual-frequency GPS for higher accuracy). Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
