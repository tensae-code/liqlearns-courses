# Drawing circles with compasses

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Mongolia's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Drawing circles with compasses
Mongolia's book teaches it this way (*Design Technology 6 (Mongolia)*, printed p. 13): The parts of the compasses, how to set and check them, drawing a circle, radius R and diameter, the 360° circle; exercises with R40 and a 60 mm diameter. The learner works through the named materials and steps in order, saying what each step does before doing the next one.

## Sub lesson 2 — Drawing circles with compasses — doing it yourself
Now the learner does the whole task unaided: The parts of the compasses, how to set and check them, drawing a circle, radius R and diameter, the 360° circle; exercises with R40 and a 60 mm diameter. Working from the first sub lesson's sequence, the learner plans the make, works through it step by step, and checks the finished piece against what Mongolia's book shows.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: Drawing circles with compasses — The parts of the compasses, how to set and check them, drawing a circle, radius R and diameter, the 360° circle; exercises with R40 and a 60 mm diameter. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.

## Do It Yourself
1. Do the Do it first task again, carefully this time, using everything the lesson taught.
2. Check your work against the lesson's own checks — fix one thing you find.
3. Keep your work: it is practice evidence, and the next lesson builds on it.
