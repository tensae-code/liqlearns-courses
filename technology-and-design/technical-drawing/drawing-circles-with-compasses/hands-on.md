# Hands-on — Drawing circles with compasses

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: The parts of the compasses, how to set and check them, drawing a circle, radius R and diameter, the 360° circle; exercises with R40 and a 60 mm diameter. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
