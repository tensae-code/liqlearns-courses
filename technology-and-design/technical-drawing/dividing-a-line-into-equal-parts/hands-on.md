# Hands-on — Dividing a line into equal parts

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: Dividing a straight line into equal parts (seven shown) and building string-art curves by joining the division points of two lines. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
