# Georeferencing a scanned map in QGIS — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Say in one sentence what this lesson teaches.
**Answer:** Ground control points (GCP table), matching analogue-map coordinates to real coordinates, judging the residuals and the RMS error (excluding the worst control point and repeating until the error is within the limit for the map's scale).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name one material, tool or step this lesson works with.
**Answer:** Any one named in the lesson text — for example, the first step of sub lesson 1: Georeferencing a scanned map in QGIS.
