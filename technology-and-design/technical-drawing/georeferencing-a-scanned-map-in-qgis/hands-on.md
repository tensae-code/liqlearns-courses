# Hands-on — Georeferencing a scanned map in QGIS

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: ground control points (GCP table), matching analogue-map coordinates to real coordinates, judging the residuals and the RMS error (excluding the worst control point and repeating until the error is within the limit for the map's scale). Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
