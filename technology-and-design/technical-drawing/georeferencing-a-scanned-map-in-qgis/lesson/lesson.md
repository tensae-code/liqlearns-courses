# Georeferencing a scanned map in QGIS

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Greece's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Georeferencing a scanned map in QGIS
Greece's book teaches it this way: ground control points (GCP table), matching analogue-map coordinates to real coordinates, judging the residuals and the RMS error (excluding the worst control point and repeating until the error is within the limit for the map's scale) (*Geoinformatics in Technical Works*, printed p. 32). The learner works through the named materials and steps in order, saying what each step does before doing the next one.

## Sub lesson 2 — Georeferencing a scanned map in QGIS — doing it yourself
Now the learner does the whole task unaided: ground control points (GCP table), matching analogue-map coordinates to real coordinates, judging the residuals and the RMS error (excluding the worst control point and repeating until the error is within the limit for the map's scale). Working from the first sub lesson's sequence, the learner plans the make, works through it step by step, and checks the finished piece against what Greece's book shows.
