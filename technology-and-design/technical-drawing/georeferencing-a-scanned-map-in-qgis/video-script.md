# Video script — Georeferencing a scanned map in QGIS

A short demonstration video, made later, in a super-simple voice.

---

Georeferencing a scanned map in QGIS.
Ground control points (GCP table), matching analogue-map coordinates to real coordinates, judging the residuals and the RMS error (excluding the worst control point and repeating until the error is within the limit for the map's scale).
Watch the steps. Then do them yourself.
