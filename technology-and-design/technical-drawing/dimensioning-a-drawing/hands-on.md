# Hands-on — Dimensioning a drawing

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## Dimension a curved article
Draw a simple article with one curved face. Dimension it: add the radius or diameter dimension, the symmetry axis, and one hidden-profile line, each in its proper style.

## Read the lines
On a pictured dimensioned drawing, point to a hidden-profile line and a symmetry axis, and say what each tells the maker.
