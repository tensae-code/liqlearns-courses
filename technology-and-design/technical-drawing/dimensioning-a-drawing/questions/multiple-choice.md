# Dimensioning a drawing — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is dimensioning (acotado)?
a) stating a drawing's sizes with dimension lines and figures b) Only a price c) Only decoration d) Nothing to do with this lesson
**Answer: a) stating a drawing's sizes with dimension lines and figures**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is symmetry axis?
a) Only history b) the line about which a shape is mirror-equal c) Only storage d) A kind of glue
**Answer: b) the line about which a shape is mirror-equal**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is radius?
a) Only old tools b) Only painting c) the distance from a curve's centre to its rim, half the diameter d) Only a price
**Answer: c) the distance from a curve's centre to its rim, half the diameter**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson teach, in one line?
a) Only decoration b) Nothing to do with this lesson c) Only history d) dimensioning (acotado) states a drawing's sizes so the article can be made: Cuba's Grade 6 programme adds the lines for hidden profiles and symmetry axes, and dimensions curved surfaces by radius and diameter.
**Answer: d) dimensioning (acotado) states a drawing's sizes so the article can be made: Cuba's Grade 6 programme adds the lines for hidden profiles and symmetry axes, and dimensions curved surfaces by radius and diameter.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** How does Cuba's book teach this?
a) As theory only, never practised b) Only in a glossary c) Only as an exam d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Spanish
**Answer: d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Spanish**
