# Dimensioning a drawing — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Dimensioning (acotado) is stating a drawing's sizes with dimension lines and figures.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Symmetry axis has nothing to do with this lesson.
**Answer: False** — It is part of the lesson: the line about which a shape is mirror-equal.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Radius is the distance from a curve's centre to its rim, half the diameter.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.
