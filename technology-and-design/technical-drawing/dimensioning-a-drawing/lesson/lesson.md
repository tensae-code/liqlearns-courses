# Dimensioning a drawing

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Cuba's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Dimensioning with the full line alphabet
A dimensioned drawing speaks through its lines: beside the dimension lines stand the lines for hidden profiles (perfiles ocultos) and the symmetry-axis lines (ejes de simetría). The Grade 6 programme teaches dimensioning (§2.2) with both line kinds named.

## Sub lesson 2 — Curved surfaces: radius and diameter
Curves are dimensioned by their radius and diameter (radio y diámetro): the dimension states the curve's true size from its centre. The learner dimensions a curved surface so a maker could reproduce the curve exactly.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: dimensioning (acotado) states a drawing's sizes so the article can be made: Cuba's Grade 6 programme adds the lines for hidden profiles and symmetry axes, and dimensions curved surfaces by radius and diameter. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
