# Lesson plan — Dimensioning a drawing

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Technical Drawing · **Lesson:** Dimensioning a drawing
**Place in the curriculum:** Technical Drawing (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Cuba expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** dimensioning (acotado) states a drawing's sizes so the article can be made: Cuba's Grade 6 programme adds the lines for hidden profiles and symmetry axes, and dimensions curved surfaces by radius and diameter.
**This lesson's own level:** stocked from Cuba's Educación Laboral programmes and methodological orientations (teacher programme documents) — see lesson/sources.md. Cuba is evidence only for this lesson, counted nowhere in the acceptance count.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **Dimensioning with the full line alphabet** — A dimensioned drawing speaks through its lines: beside the dimension lines stand the lines for hidden profiles (perfiles ocultos) and the symmetry-axis lines (ejes de simetría). The Grade 6 programme teaches dimensioning (§2.2) with both line kinds named.
2. **Curved surfaces: radius and diameter** — Curves are dimensioned by their radius and diameter (radio y diámetro): the dimension states the curve's true size from its centre. The learner dimensions a curved surface so a maker could reproduce the curve exactly.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
