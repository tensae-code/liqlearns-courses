# Hands-on — Attribute data in GIS

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: the attribute table of a vector layer (shapefile), opening it three ways, and creating/shaping descriptive (attribute) data for geographic entities. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
