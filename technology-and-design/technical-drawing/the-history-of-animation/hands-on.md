# Hands-on — The history of animation

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: from the thaumatrope, phenakistoscope and zoetrope through Reynaud's Théâtre Optique, Blackton and McCay, to Disney (Mickey Mouse 1928, Snow White 1937) and Mongolian animation. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
