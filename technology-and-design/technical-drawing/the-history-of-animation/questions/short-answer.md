# The history of animation — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Say in one sentence what this lesson teaches.
**Answer:** The history of animation — from the thaumatrope, phenakistoscope and zoetrope through Reynaud's Théâtre Optique, Blackton and McCay, to Disney (Mickey Mouse 1928, Snow White 1937) and Mongolian animation.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name one material, tool or step this lesson works with.
**Answer:** Any one named in the lesson text — for example, the first step of sub lesson 1: The history of animation.
