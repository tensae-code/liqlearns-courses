Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson teach?
a) The history of animation — from the thaumatrope, phenakistoscope and zoetrope through Reynaud's Théâtre Optique, Blackton and McCay, to Disney (Mickey Mouse 1928, Snow White 1937) and Mongolian animation b) Only how to draw pictures c) Nothing practical d) A song to memorise
