# Hands-on — Drawing lettering and the title block

Real drawing practice with pencil and paper — this subject is learned by doing.

## Layout a sheet
Take a fresh sheet: draw the frame, place a title block in its standard place, and plan where a front view, top view and dimensions would sit, in light lines.

## Letter to standard
Letter the alphabet and figures 0–9 in drawing lettering at one set height, using faint guide lines. Check every letter is the same height and spacing.
