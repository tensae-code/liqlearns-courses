# Drawing lettering and the title block — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, or by doing the drawing step named.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Name three things the title block records.
**Answer:** For example: the object's name, who drew/checked it, the scale, the sheet number, the material (any three).

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Why must lettering be standard rather than personal handwriting?
**Answer:** So the drawing reads to standard — the same for every reader, whoever drew it.
