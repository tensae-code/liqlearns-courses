# Geometrical constructions — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, or by doing the drawing step named.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Why is a constructed triangle exact, while a drawn-by-eye one is not?
**Answer:** Its sides are carried by the compasses at the given lengths and its corner is fixed where the arcs cross — nothing depends on guessing.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** How do you find the centre angle of any regular polygon?
**Answer:** Divide 360° by the number of sides.
