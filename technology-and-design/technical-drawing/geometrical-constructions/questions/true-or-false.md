# Geometrical constructions — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In a construction you may measure a side by eye if it looks right.
**Answer: False** — Sizes come from the instruments, never from guessing.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** A regular pentagon has five equal sides.
**Answer: True** — Pentagon = five sides, all equal in a regular pentagon.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The arcs in a triangle construction cross at the third corner.
**Answer: True** — Each arc is swung at one given side length from a base end; their crossing is the corner.
