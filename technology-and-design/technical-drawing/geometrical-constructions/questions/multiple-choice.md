# Geometrical constructions — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In a construction, a length is carried from place to place by…
a) The ruler's edge b) The compasses c) The protractor d) Your eye
**Answer: b) The compasses**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Where is the third corner of a constructed triangle?
a) Halfway along the base b) Where the two compass arcs cross c) At the protractor's centre d) Anywhere it looks right
**Answer: b) Where the two compass arcs cross**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** How many sides does a regular octagon have?
a) Six b) Seven c) Eight d) Five
**Answer: c) Eight**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** The centre angle of a regular hexagon is…
a) 360° ÷ 6 = 60° b) 360° ÷ 5 = 72° c) 180° ÷ 6 = 30° d) 90°
**Answer: a) 360° ÷ 6 = 60°**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** A regular polygon has…
a) Sides of different lengths b) All sides equal and all angles equal c) Only right angles d) Curved sides
**Answer: b) All sides equal and all angles equal**
