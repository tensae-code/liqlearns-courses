# Hands-on — Geometrical constructions

Real drawing practice with pencil and paper — this subject is learned by doing.

## Construct a triangle
Rule a base. With compasses set to two given lengths, swing arcs from each end and join the crossing point. Measure the sides to prove they match the given lengths.

## Construct a hexagon and an octagon
For each: draw a circle, find the centre angle (360° ÷ sides), step the points out and join them in order. Check all sides with compasses — they must be equal.
