# The camera and how photographs are made

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Mongolia's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — The camera and how photographs are made
Mongolia's book teaches it this way (*Design Drawing & Technology 12 (Mongolia)*, printed p. 49): camera types from box camera to SLR and smartphone (Leica 1932, Polaroid 1975, Canon digital 2000), the parts of a digital camera, and its five systems. The learner works through the named materials and steps in order, saying what each step does before doing the next one.

## Sub lesson 2 — The camera and how photographs are made — doing it yourself
Now the learner does the whole task unaided: camera types from box camera to SLR and smartphone (Leica 1932, Polaroid 1975, Canon digital 2000), the parts of a digital camera, and its five systems. Working from the first sub lesson's sequence, the learner plans the make, works through it step by step, and checks the finished piece against what Mongolia's book shows.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: The camera and how photographs are made — camera types from box camera to SLR and smartphone (Leica 1932, Polaroid 1975, Canon digital 2000), the parts of a digital camera, and its five systems. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.

## Do It Yourself
1. Do the Do it first task again, carefully this time, using everything the lesson taught.
2. Check your work against the lesson's own checks — fix one thing you find.
3. Keep your work: it is practice evidence, and the next lesson builds on it.
