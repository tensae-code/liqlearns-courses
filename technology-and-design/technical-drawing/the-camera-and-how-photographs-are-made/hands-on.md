# Hands-on — The camera and how photographs are made

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: camera types from box camera to SLR and smartphone (Leica 1932, Polaroid 1975, Canon digital 2000), the parts of a digital camera, and its five systems. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
