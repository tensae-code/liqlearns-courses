# Size and Location Dimensions — the Theory — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Give two examples of size dimensions on a stepped shaft.
**Answer: any two of — the diameters of the steps, the lengths of the steps, the overall length (each measures a feature itself).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A slot must be milled in a bar. Which two questions must its dimensions answer?
**Answer: how big it is (size dimensions) and where it sits (location dimensions).**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Why must a figure never be copied from the measured length of a drawn line?
**Answer: because the drawing may be at a scale — the figure states the object's true size, the line only the scaled size.**
