# Architectural and construction drawings — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, or by doing the drawing step named.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the three chief drawings of a building and what each shows.
**Answer:** Plan — layout looked down on; facade — outside face; section — the building cut through, showing construction.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Why must a reader learn construction symbols separately from machine-drawing conventions?
**Answer:** Building drawings follow construction's own conventions and symbol set; machine-drawing habits alone will misread them.
