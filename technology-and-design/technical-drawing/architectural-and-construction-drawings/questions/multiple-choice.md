# Architectural and construction drawings — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** A building plan shows the building…
a) From the side only b) Looked down on, cut at a set level c) In perspective only d) As a photograph
**Answer: b) Looked down on, cut at a set level**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A facade shows…
a) The inside of a wall b) An outside face of the building c) Only the roof plan d) A machine part
**Answer: b) An outside face of the building**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** How do building drawings differ from machine drawings?
a) They use no lines b) In scale, content and construction conventions c) They have no dimensions d) They are always coloured
**Answer: b) In scale, content and construction conventions**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Doors and windows on construction drawings are shown by…
a) Full pictorial pictures b) Conventional images and symbols c) Photographs pasted in d) Leaving gaps with no mark
**Answer: b) Conventional images and symbols**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** A section of a building shows…
a) Only the front door b) The building cut through, revealing its construction c) The site from the air only d) The builder's tools
**Answer: b) The building cut through, revealing its construction**
