# Architectural and construction drawings — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** A plan and a facade show the same thing.
**Answer: False** — A plan looks down on the layout; a facade shows an outside face.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Windows on construction drawings are drawn as full pictorial pictures.
**Answer: False** — They are shown by conventional images and symbols.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Building drawings are a kind of technical drawing.
**Answer: True** — They are technical drawing in construction's dialect, with its own conventions.
