# Hands-on — Technical lettering

Real drawing practice with pencil and paper — this subject is learned by doing.

## Print the alphabet
On grid paper, print the full alphabet and the numbers 0–9, one character per square. Check: are all letters the same height? Is the spacing even? Redo the three worst letters.

## Name plate
Print your name and class on a grid, as the workbook does. Show it to a partner — they must read it instantly, with no guessing.
