# Technical lettering — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, or by doing the drawing step named.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why must lettering on a drawing be printed evenly instead of written quickly?
**Answer:** So anyone can read the words and figures at a glance, with no chance of misreading a letter or number.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name two things that keep printed letters the same height.
**Answer:** Guidelines and a grid (printing between the lines, one character per square).
