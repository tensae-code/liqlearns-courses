# Technical lettering — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is technical lettering?
a) Joined-up handwriting on a drawing b) Printing letters and numbers by hand, evenly and clearly c) Typing labels on a computer d) Colouring the title of a drawing
**Answer: b) Printing letters and numbers by hand, evenly and clearly**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What do guidelines do in lettering?
a) Decorate the page b) Keep letters level and all one height c) Show where to fold the paper d) Mark the centre of the sheet
**Answer: b) Keep letters level and all one height**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** In vertical lettering, the letters…
a) Lean to the right b) Stand straight up, evenly spaced c) Get smaller along the line d) Join to each other
**Answer: b) Stand straight up, evenly spaced**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Which sign does the workbook include with the numbers?
a) The percent sign % b) The diameter sign Ø c) The degree sign only d) The plus sign
**Answer: b) The diameter sign Ø**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** What is the workbook's final lettering task?
a) Copying a paragraph b) Printing your name and class on a grid c) Drawing a border d) Writing a story
**Answer: b) Printing your name and class on a grid**
