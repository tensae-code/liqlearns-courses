# Hands-on — CAD in construction and production

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: what a computer-aided design system does beyond drawing (storing design alternatives, materials and stress analysis, cost estimates). Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
