# Hands-on — Types of section — choosing and drawing the right cut

Real drawing practice with pencil and paper — this subject is learned by doing.

## Four cuts, one block
Draw one hollow block as a full, a half and an offset section. Label each type and one feature each reveals best.

## Choose and defend
For three objects described by a partner — symmetrical cup, offset-holed plate, bar with changing profile — name the section type you would use and why.
