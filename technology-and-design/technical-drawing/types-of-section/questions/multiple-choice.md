# Types of section — choosing and drawing the right cut — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A half section is best for…
a) a symmetrical object — one half in section, one half outside view b) an object with no inside c) a very long bar only d) a flat plate
**Answer: a) a symmetrical object — one half in section, one half outside view**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** An offset section is used when…
a) features do not lie in one straight line, so the cutting plane bends b) the object is round c) you want no hatching d) the object is symmetrical
**Answer: a) features do not lie in one straight line, so the cutting plane bends**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** One small hidden detail, with the rest of the view worth keeping, calls for…
a) a full section b) a broken-out section c) no section d) a revolved section
**Answer: b) a broken-out section**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The bends of an offset cutting plane appear in the section view as lines.
a) True — always draw them b) False — they are shown on the cutting-plane line, not in the section view c) Only for round objects d) Only in pencil
**Answer: b) False — they are shown on the cutting-plane line, not in the section view**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Where a section shows a feature plainly, hidden-detail lines for it should be…
a) kept and darkened b) left out c) hatched d) doubled
**Answer: b) left out**
