# Types of section — choosing and drawing the right cut — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, or by doing the drawing step named.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Contrast a revolved and a removed section.
**Answer:** Both show a part's cut profile; a revolved section turns it in place inside the view, a removed section draws it outside the view where there is room.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** State the test for choosing a section type.
**Answer:** Does this type reveal the interior features the drawing must describe, with the least loss of other information?

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Give the drawing order for a section in the chosen type.
**Answer:** Lay the cutting-plane line through the features; project the section from the arrows' side; draw and hatch the cut faces; add visible edges beyond the plane in outline; check that voids are unhatched and plainly shown features carry no hidden-detail lines.
