# Types of section — choosing and drawing the right cut — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A revolved section draws the cut profile turned in place inside the view.
**Answer: True** — A removed section draws it outside the view instead.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The right section type is chosen by habit — always the full section.
**Answer: False** — Choose by the object and what must be revealed, with the least loss.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A full section removes the nearer half of the object in the drawing.
**Answer: True** — That is the full-section bargain: full interior, outside half lost.
