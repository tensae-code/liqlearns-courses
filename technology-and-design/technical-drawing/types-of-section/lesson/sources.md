Era note: this guide is an older GEQIP-generation book (first edition 2003 E.C., funded through the General Education Quality Improvement Project, GEQIP) — a different curriculum generation from the current syllabus. These lessons present it as such, as the guide's testimony about the course of its era, never as the current syllabus unchecked. No student book for Technical Drawing exists in hand, so nothing here could be checked against a pupil text.

Honesty: The placements state that the guide identifies and compares the types of section but do not enumerate the types one by one; this lesson teaches the standard family (full, half, offset, broken-out, revolved, removed) that those comparisons concern. The book also mis-numbers this unit's lessons — two different lessons are both numbered 3.2; this is the first of them (Types of Sections, 8 periods). No narrower pages than printed pp. 42–44 are given, so none are claimed.

Belarus's own way: Belarus teaches разрэзы (sectional views) as the step beyond сячэнні — the cut shows the object behind the cutting plane as well — and then combines view and section in one image, including the half-view/half-section of a symmetric part.
