# Secondary auxiliary views — projecting a projection — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** An oblique surface shows an edge view in the top principal view.
**Answer: False** — It is tilted to every principal plane, so no principal view gives an edge view.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** An error in the primary view is carried into the secondary view.
**Answer: True** — That is why step one is checked before projecting again.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Point labels may change between the two projections.
**Answer: False** — Keep the same labels through both steps.
