# Recognising geometric solids — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, or by doing the drawing step named.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How do you tell a cube from a cuboid?
**Answer:** A cube's faces are all equal squares; a cuboid's faces are rectangles, with length, width and height generally different.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Why learn to see the solids inside a combined object?
**Answer:** Because once you can name its simple parts, you can draw the whole object part by part.
