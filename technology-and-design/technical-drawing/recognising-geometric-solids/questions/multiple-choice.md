# Recognising geometric solids — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A cube has…
a) Six equal square faces b) Six rectangular faces c) One square face d) A circular base
**Answer: a) Six equal square faces**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A square pyramid stands on…
a) A triangular base b) A square base, with triangular faces meeting at an apex c) A circular base d) No base
**Answer: b) A square base, with triangular faces meeting at an apex**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A hemisphere is…
a) A whole sphere b) Exactly half a sphere c) Two spheres joined d) A flat circle
**Answer: b) Exactly half a sphere**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A cylinder has…
a) One circular end b) Two equal circular ends joined by a curved surface c) A point at the top d) Six faces
**Answer: b) Two equal circular ends joined by a curved surface**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** A combined cylinder is…
a) A cylinder cut in half b) Two cylinders joined into one object c) A cone on a sphere d) A cuboid
**Answer: b) Two cylinders joined into one object**
