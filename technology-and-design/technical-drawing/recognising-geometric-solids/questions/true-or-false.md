# Recognising geometric solids — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** A hemisphere has one flat circular face and one dome.
**Answer: True** — It is half a sphere, so it has exactly those two surfaces.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A cone and a square pyramid are the same solid.
**Answer: False** — A cone has a circular base and curved surface; a square pyramid has a square base and flat triangular faces.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Colouring and shading help a flat drawing read as a solid.
**Answer: True** — The workbook shades faces by direction so the solid form shows.
