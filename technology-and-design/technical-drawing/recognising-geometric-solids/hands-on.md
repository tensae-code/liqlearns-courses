# Hands-on — Recognising geometric solids

Real drawing practice with pencil and paper — this subject is learned by doing.

## Name and shade
Draw the seven solids. Label each, then shade the faces or surfaces so each drawing reads as a solid, as the workbook does.

## Find the solids inside
Take a combined solid (two cylinders joined). Draw it, draw a line where the two parts meet, and label each part.
