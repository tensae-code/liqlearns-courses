# Recognising geometric solids

## Do it first
Draw the seven solids. Label each, then shade the faces or surfaces so each drawing reads as a solid, as the workbook does.

## Sub lesson 1 — Solids with flat faces: cube, cuboid, square pyramid
A cube has six equal square faces. A cuboid (a box shape) has six rectangular faces, its length, width and height all different in general. A square pyramid stands on a square base with four triangular faces meeting at one apex point. Malta's workbook has you name each solid, then colour and shade it — shading the faces by their direction is what makes a flat drawing read as a solid.

## Sub lesson 2 — Round solids: cone, sphere, cylinder, hemisphere
The cone has a circular base and one curved surface rising to a point. The sphere is perfectly round in every direction, like a ball. The cylinder has two equal circular ends joined by a curved surface, like a tin. The hemisphere is exactly half a sphere — one flat circular face and one dome. Naming these four on sight, from any angle, is the workbook's second set.

## Sub lesson 3 — Combined solids
Real objects are often two solids joined: the workbook's combined cylinders are cylinders of different sizes stacked or joined into one object. The skill is to stop seeing 'a strange shape' and start seeing the solids inside it — name each part, and the whole object becomes drawable. Recognising the solids inside a combined object is the first step of drawing it.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: the common geometric solids can be recognised and named on sight — cube, cuboid, square pyramid, cone, sphere, cylinder and hemisphere — and told apart even when two are combined into one object. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.

## Do It Yourself
1. Do the Do it first task again, carefully this time, using everything the lesson taught.
2. Check your work against the lesson's own checks — fix one thing you find.
3. Keep the sheet: it is practice evidence, and the next lesson builds on it.
