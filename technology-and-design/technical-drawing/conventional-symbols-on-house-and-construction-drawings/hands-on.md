# Hands-on — Conventional symbols on house and construction drawings

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: materials (brick, concrete, sand, gravel, stone, wood, grass, water) and the symbols for windows, doors, stairs and ramps. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
