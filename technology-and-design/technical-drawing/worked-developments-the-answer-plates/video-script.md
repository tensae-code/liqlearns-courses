# Video script — Worked Developments — the Answer Plates

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The last pages of the unit are answers. Plates A to J, drawn in full.

They answer the development exercises — Checkpoint 5.11, Exercise 1. Draw yours first. Then open the plate.

Solution B: a hexagonal prism opened flat. Six panels, fold lines dashed, stations numbered. Count along them.

Solution C: a transition piece. A grid on the elevation, projected across — and below, a divided circle giving the true lengths.

Solution G: a cone carrying a second solid. The curve where they cross is swung with concentric arcs, then carried into the flat sector.

Solutions H and I: ducts, with diamond-shaped holes that must land in exactly the right place in the pattern.

Check station by station. The first place your drawing leaves the plate is where the mistake is.
