# Hands-on — Worked Developments — the Answer Plates

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Mark your own work like a plate
Take your card prism from the developing-solids lesson. Number its faces 1 to 6 in the order they stand in its development, dash its fold lines if you drew them solid, and check each panel's width against the prism itself with dividers. Any panel that fails its measurement gets redrawn — that is the plates' job done by hand.

## The cutout that must land right
On a flat card rectangle, mark a diamond-shaped cutout where a second piece would pass through, fold the card into a simple duct, and look through the diamond: if the opening sits crooked or off its station, the flat marking was wrong — flatten, correct the station, and fold again.
