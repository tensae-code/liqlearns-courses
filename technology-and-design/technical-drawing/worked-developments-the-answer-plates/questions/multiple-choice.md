# Worked Developments — the Answer Plates — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The solution plates, lettered A–J, are —
a) extra exercises with no answers  b) the worked answers to Checkpoint 5.11 / Exercise 1  c) the course contents pages
**Answer: b) the worked answers to Checkpoint 5.11 / Exercise 1**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** An answer plate is used in which order?
a) copy the plate, then attempt the exercise  b) attempt the exercise first, then check against the plate  c) memorise the plate before drawing anything
**Answer: b) attempt the exercise first, then check against the plate**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** In solution B, the numbered marks along the hexagonal prism's development are —
a) its stations, so any point can be followed from the solid into the flat pattern  b) page numbers  c) fold angles
**Answer: a) its stations, so any point can be followed from the solid into the flat pattern**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In solution C, the divided circle below the drawing supplies —
a) the fold lines  b) the true lengths the projected grid alone cannot give  c) the lettering
**Answer: b) the true lengths the projected grid alone cannot give**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** In solution D, the development of the pointed solid is built from —
a) the numbered division points around its plan circle, carried into the pattern in order  b) a single centre line  c) the elevation alone
**Answer: a) the numbered division points around its plan circle, carried into the pattern in order**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** In solution G, the intersection curve is carried into the cone's development by —
a) free-hand sketching  b) concentric arcs swung about the apex  c) a divided straight line
**Answer: b) concentric arcs swung about the apex**

**7. (Sub lesson 3)** Solutions H and I are duct developments whose flat patterns carry —
a) hexagonal end faces  b) diamond-shaped cutouts  c) section lining
**Answer: b) diamond-shaped cutouts**
