# Worked Developments — the Answer Plates — short answer

Answer in a short sentence or a small calculation.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the exercises the plates A–J answer.
**Answer: Checkpoint 5.11 / Exercise 1 — the development exercises of Unit 5.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In solution C, the grid projected from the elevation and the divided circle do different jobs. State each job in a few words.
**Answer: the grid says where each point goes in the development; the divided circle gives the true lengths.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Why must the intersection curve appear in the cone's flat sector in solution G, and not only in the views?
**Answer: because the cone is made from the flat pattern — without the curve in the sector, the rolled cone has no opening where the intersecting solid must enter.**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** What is the right order for a cutout in your own duct development: cut, or place first?
**Answer: place it first — mark the cutout in the flat pattern, check its station against the solid, and only then cut.**
