Era note: this guide is an older GEQIP-generation book (first edition, 2003 E.C., about 2010/11) — an earlier curriculum generation than Ethiopia's newer books. This lesson presents it as exactly that: the guide's testimony about that era's Grade 12 Technical Drawing course. It is never presented as the current syllabus, and nothing here has been checked against a current syllabus. No pupil (student) book for Technical Drawing has been read — no student book exists in hand — so this lesson stands on teacher-guide evidence only.

Honesty note: the solution plates are figure pages with almost no text (printed pp. 101–104 carry under 100 characters of text layer per page); their content stands in the placements as read visually from the rendered plates and is taught here at exactly that depth — solutions B, C, D, G, H and I as the placements describe them, and the remaining lettered solutions named only as standing in the same family, because the placements describe no further plate individually. The two pictures in this lesson are drawn renders in the manner of solutions B and C, made for this build; they are not reproductions of the guide's plates. This lesson is the stocked home of the plates: the "accuracy bar" rows in the sources of the developing-solids and intersections lessons cite the same pages as the standard those lessons are drawn to, and stand unchanged.
