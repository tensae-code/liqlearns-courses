# Hands-on — Buffer zones in GIS

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: creating a buffer around features (800 m around the roads of Andros, dissolving the results) and reading the before/after data. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
