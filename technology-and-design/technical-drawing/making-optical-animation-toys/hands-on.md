# Hands-on — Making optical animation toys

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: a phenakistoscope (a Ø250 mm disc with 8 or more slots 30×5 mm), a zoetrope (a cylinder with 12–24 slots) and a flip book, following the given sequences. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
