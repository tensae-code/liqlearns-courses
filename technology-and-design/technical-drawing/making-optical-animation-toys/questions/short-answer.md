# Making optical animation toys — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Say in one sentence what this lesson teaches.
**Answer:** Making optical animation toys — a phenakistoscope (a Ø250 mm disc with 8 or more slots 30×5 mm), a zoetrope (a cylinder with 12–24 slots) and a flip book, following the given sequences.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name one material, tool or step this lesson works with.
**Answer:** Any one named in the lesson text — for example, the first step of sub lesson 1: Making optical animation toys.
