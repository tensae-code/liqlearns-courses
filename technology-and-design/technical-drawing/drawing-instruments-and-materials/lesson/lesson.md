# Drawing instruments and materials

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Iran's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — The drawing board kit
The drawing board gives a flat, true surface; on it work the T-square or straightedge for horizontal lines, set squares for angles, compasses and dividers for circles and measuring, pencils of graded hardness, and the eraser and brush that keep the sheet clean. Each instrument has one job, and knowing the kit is the first drawing skill.

## Sub lesson 2 — Papers and the A-series
Drawings are made on papers chosen for the work — plain, grid and millimetre papers, the millimetre grid letting measurements be read straight from the sheet. Standard sheets form the A-series: A0 is the base sheet of one square metre, and each next size halves the one before — A1, A2, A3, A4 — so drawings scale between sizes without waste. Iran's Grade 7 book opens its drawing module with exactly this kit and these sizes.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: technical drawing (نقشه‌کشی) begins from its kit — board, instruments and papers — and from the A-series paper sizes every drawing is planned on. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
