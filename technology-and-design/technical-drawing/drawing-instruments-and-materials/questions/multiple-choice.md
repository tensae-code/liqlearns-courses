# Drawing instruments and materials — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is drawing board?
a) the flat board that gives technical drawing its true working surface b) Only a price c) Only decoration d) Nothing to do with this lesson
**Answer: a) the flat board that gives technical drawing its true working surface**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is millimetre paper?
a) Only history b) paper ruled in millimetre grids so measurements read straight from the sheet c) Only storage d) A kind of glue
**Answer: b) paper ruled in millimetre grids so measurements read straight from the sheet**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is A-series?
a) Only old tools b) Only painting c) the standard paper sizes, each halving the one before, from A0 down d) Only a price
**Answer: c) the standard paper sizes, each halving the one before, from A0 down**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson teach, in one line?
a) Only decoration b) Nothing to do with this lesson c) Only history d) technical drawing (نقشه‌کشی) begins from its kit — board, instruments and papers — and from the A-series paper sizes every drawing is planned on.
**Answer: d) technical drawing (نقشه‌کشی) begins from its kit — board, instruments and papers — and from the A-series paper sizes every drawing is planned on.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** How does Iran's book teach this?
a) As theory only, never practised b) Only in a glossary c) Only as an exam d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Persian
**Answer: d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Persian**
