# History of technical drawing — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is pictograph?
a) a picture-sign that carries meaning, among drawing's first records b) Only a price c) Only decoration d) Nothing to do with this lesson
**Answer: a) a picture-sign that carries meaning, among drawing's first records**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is Gudea?
a) Only history b) the ancient ruler whose statue holds one of the earliest known plans c) Only storage d) A kind of glue
**Answer: b) the ancient ruler whose statue holds one of the earliest known plans**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is universal language?
a) Only old tools b) Only painting c) the book's name for drawing: readable by everyone, everywhere d) Only a price
**Answer: c) the book's name for drawing: readable by everyone, everywhere**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson teach, in one line?
a) Only decoration b) Nothing to do with this lesson c) Only history d) technical drawing has a history: from cave pictographs — Cuba's own among them — and the statue of Gudea to Leonardo, drawing grew into the universal language makers share.
**Answer: d) technical drawing has a history: from cave pictographs — Cuba's own among them — and the statue of Gudea to Leonardo, drawing grew into the universal language makers share.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** How does Cuba's book teach this?
a) As theory only, never practised b) Only in a glossary c) Only as an exam d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Spanish
**Answer: d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Spanish**
