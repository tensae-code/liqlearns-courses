# History of technical drawing

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Cuba's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — The first drawings
Cuba's 2023 Grade 7 book opens Dibujo Básico with history (§1.1): cave pictographs, including Cuban pictographs, are drawing's first records — marks that carried meaning across people and time before writing did.

## Sub lesson 2 — Gudea, Leonardo, and the universal language
The book's line runs from Gudea — whose statue holds an early plan — to Leonardo, whose drawings join art and engineering in one hand. The lesson's close is the book's claim: drawing is a universal language, readable beyond any spoken tongue.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: technical drawing has a history: from cave pictographs — Cuba's own among them — and the statue of Gudea to Leonardo, drawing grew into the universal language makers share. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
