# Drawing and reading architectural floor plans and elevations — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A floor plan is…
a) The building viewed from above, cut through the walls b) A photograph of the front c) A view from underneath d) A list of materials
**Answer: a) The building viewed from above, cut through the walls**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Door swings are drawn on a plan so that…
a) Clashes with furniture and walls can be checked b) The door looks decorative c) The plan fills the page d) The builder can skip the door
**Answer: a) Clashes with furniture and walls can be checked**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** An elevation's widths come from…
a) The plan, projected upward b) The roof pitch c) The builder's guess d) The window sizes only
**Answer: a) The plan, projected upward**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Framing is shown on the elevation because…
a) The builder counts and places members from it b) It decorates the drawing c) Elevations are pictures d) Framing is invisible in building
**Answer: a) The builder counts and places members from it**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** A truss drawing is decided by…
a) Its span and its pitch b) Its colour c) The door position d) The plan's scale only
**Answer: a) Its span and its pitch**
