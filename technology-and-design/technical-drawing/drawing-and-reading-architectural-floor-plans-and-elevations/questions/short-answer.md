# Drawing and reading architectural floor plans and elevations — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What data do you need before drawing a floor plan?
**Answer:** Answer: Overall sizes, wall positions, and where windows and doors fall (with door swings).

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** A truss spans 5 m. What else must be given before you can draw it, and why?
**Answer:** Answer: The pitch — span alone gives only the base; pitch fixes the slope and therefore the height and member lengths.
