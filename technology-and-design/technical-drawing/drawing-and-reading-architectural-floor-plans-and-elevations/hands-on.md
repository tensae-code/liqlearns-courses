# Hands-on — Drawing and reading architectural floor plans and elevations

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## Plan from data
Given data: a room 4 m × 3 m, one door 0.9 m wide in the long wall, one window 1.2 m wide opposite. Draw the floor plan at 1:50 with door swing shown.

## Truss from span and pitch
For a span of 6 m and a pitch of 30°, draw the truss triangle to scale, mark span and pitch, and measure the rise your drawing gives.
