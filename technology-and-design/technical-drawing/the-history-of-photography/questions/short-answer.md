# The history of photography — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Say in one sentence what this lesson teaches.
**Answer:** The history of photography — the camera obscura, Ibn al-Haytham's optics, Niépce's first photograph (1826), Daguerre, and the arrival of photography in Mongolia.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name one material, tool or step this lesson works with.
**Answer:** Any one named in the lesson text — for example, the first step of sub lesson 1: The history of photography.
