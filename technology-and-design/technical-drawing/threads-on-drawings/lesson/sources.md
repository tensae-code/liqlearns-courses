| the conventional representation of threads (TCVN 5907:1995): for a VISIBLE thread — the thread crest line (đường đỉnh ren) is drawn in thick continuous line, the thread limit line (đường giới hạn ren) in thick continuous line, the thread root line (đường chân ren) in thin continuous line; in the end view (looking along the axis) the crest circle is drawn in thick continuous line and the root circle is drawn about 3/4 of a circle in thin continuous line; for a HIDDEN thread, the crest, root and limit lines are all drawn in thin dashed lines; the thread symbols and designations (TCVN 0204:1993) — thread kinds by profile: metric thread (ren hệ mét) symbol M, square thread (ren vuông) symbol Sq, trapezoidal thread (ren thang) symbol Tr; a designation gives the profile symbol, the thread diameter d in mm, the pitch p in mm, and the helix direction (LH for left-hand; right-hand is not written) — e.g. M10 × 1 (M: metric; 10: diameter 10 mm; 1: pitch 1 mm) and Tr 20 × 2 LH (Tr: trapezoidal; 20: diameter; 2: pitch; LH: left-hand helix); and representing a threaded JOINT: an external and an internal thread screw together when their elements match (profile form, thread diameter, pitch, helix direction); at the place where the two threads engage, by convention only the external thread is drawn and the internal thread is not — the external thread's part is taken to cover the internal thread's part (original string `the conventional representation of threads (TCVN 5907`) | Vietnam, *Công nghệ 10 — Thiết kế và công nghệ*, printed p. 76 |
