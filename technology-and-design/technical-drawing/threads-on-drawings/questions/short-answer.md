# Threads on drawings — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, or by doing the drawing step named.

**1. (Sub lesson 3)** What two things together describe a thread on a drawing?
**Answer:** The conventional representation (where it is and how far it runs) and the designation (exactly which thread).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Where does a thread representation show the thread ending?
**Answer:** The convention marks how far the thread runs and where it ends on the part.
