# Threads on drawings — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why are threads not drawn turn by turn?
a) Threads are invisible b) The helix would bury the drawing; convention shows it clearly c) Drafters cannot draw curves d) Paper is too small
**Answer: b) The helix would bury the drawing; convention shows it clearly**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** An external thread is found on…
a) The inside of a nut only b) A bolt or rod c) A plain hole d) A sheet edge
**Answer: b) A bolt or rod**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** An internal thread is found in…
a) A bolt head b) A nut or tapped hole c) A solid rod d) A centre line
**Answer: b) A nut or tapped hole**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the thread designation state?
a) The maker's name b) The thread's kind and size (and pitch/length where needed) c) The drawing's scale only d) The material colour
**Answer: b) The thread's kind and size (and pitch/length where needed)**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** How does a reader tell external from internal thread on a drawing?
a) By counting turns b) By the sheet size c) By the conventional representation for each case d) By the title block alone
**Answer: c) By the conventional representation for each case**
