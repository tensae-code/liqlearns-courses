# Preparing a project's working drawings — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** At scale 1:1, a length on the paper is…
a) Half the true length b) The true length c) Twice the true length d) Unrelated to the true length
**Answer: b) The true length**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The plan and front views in the workbook's set are drawn at…
a) 1:1 b) 1:2 c) 1:10 d) No scale
**Answer: b) 1:2**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** At 1:2, the dimension figures on the drawing state…
a) Half sizes b) The true sizes c) Double sizes d) No sizes
**Answer: b) The true sizes**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Which two circuit drawings belong in the set?
a) A poster and a photo b) The schematic and the circuit layout c) A map and a chart d) A list and a label
**Answer: b) The schematic and the circuit layout**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** The workbook's worked example uses…
a) A 9 V supply, LEDs, 330 Ω resistors and a switch b) A motor and a gearbox only c) Mains electricity d) No components
**Answer: a) A 9 V supply, LEDs, 330 Ω resistors and a switch**
