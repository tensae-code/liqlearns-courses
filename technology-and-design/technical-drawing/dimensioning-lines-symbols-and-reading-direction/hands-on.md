# Hands-on — Dimensioning — Lines, Symbols and Reading Direction

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Measure and dimension a real thing
Measure a book, a box or a phone with a ruler. Draw its front view and dimension its length and width fully — extension lines, dimension line, arrowheads, figures — in the aligned system.

## The same thing, the other system
Redraw the same view and dimension it unidirectionally: every figure horizontal, every dimension line broken in the middle for its figure. Compare the two sheets — which is quicker to read, and why?
