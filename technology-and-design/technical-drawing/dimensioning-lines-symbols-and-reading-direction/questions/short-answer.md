# Dimensioning — Lines, Symbols and Reading Direction — short answer

Answer in a short sentence or a small drawing.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the four parts that make up an ordinary dimension between two faces of an object.
**Answer: extension lines, a dimension line, arrowheads, and the figure.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A drawing's figures all stay horizontal even on sloping dimension lines. Which system is it, and from where is every figure read?
**Answer: the unidirectional system; every figure is read from the bottom of the sheet.**
