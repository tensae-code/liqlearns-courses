# Dimensioning — Lines, Symbols and Reading Direction — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The thin lines drawn out from the object that mark the two places a size is measured between are —
a) dimension lines  b) extension lines  c) leader lines
**Answer: b) extension lines**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The arrowheads of a dimension touch —
a) the centre of the object  b) the extension lines, at the exact limits of the size  c) the title block
**Answer: b) the extension lines, at the exact limits of the size**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** A leader line is used to —
a) point to a small feature and carry its figure or note on a shoulder  b) cut the object in section  c) fold a development flat
**Answer: a) point to a small feature and carry its figure or note on a shoulder**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In the aligned system, a figure on a vertical dimension line is read from —
a) the top  b) the left  c) the right-hand side
**Answer: c) the right-hand side**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** In the unidirectional system, every figure is written —
a) along its line, in every direction  b) horizontally, and read from the bottom of the sheet  c) upside down
**Answer: b) horizontally, and read from the bottom of the sheet**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** The fault to avoid on one drawing is —
a) using arrowheads  b) mixing the aligned and unidirectional systems  c) lettering figures clearly
**Answer: b) mixing the aligned and unidirectional systems**
