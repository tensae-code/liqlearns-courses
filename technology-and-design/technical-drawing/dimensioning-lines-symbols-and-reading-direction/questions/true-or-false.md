# Dimensioning — Lines, Symbols and Reading Direction — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A dimension is only a number; the lines around it carry no meaning. — **False** (the dimension line, extension lines, arrowheads and figure each have a job)

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Arrowheads on one sheet should be neat, closed and the same size throughout. — **True**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** In the aligned system, figures are read from the bottom or from the right-hand side. — **True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In the unidirectional system, the sheet must be turned to read vertical dimensions. — **False** (every figure stays horizontal and is read from the bottom)
