# Video script — Dimensioning — Lines, Symbols and Reading Direction

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A drawing shows a shape. A shape alone builds nothing.

A maker needs the sizes. The sizes are the dimensions.

Every dimension has parts. Extension lines mark where the size runs.

The dimension line runs between them. Arrowheads touch the limits.

The figure is the number. It states the true size.

Figures are read two ways. Aligned: the figure follows its line — read from the bottom, or from the right.

Unidirectional: every figure stays flat and level — read from the bottom only.

Choose one system. Keep it everywhere. Now dimension your own drawing.
