# Video script — Placing Dimensions — Diameters, Radii, Holes and Notes

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Round features have their own rules. Learn them once, use them always.

A diameter carries its symbol before the number. A radius carries R.

A cylinder is dimensioned where it looks like a rectangle — diameter and length in one clear view.

An arc's centre is a small cross. The radius is measured from that cross.

Counter-bored, spot-faced, countersunk — the note gives diameter and depth.

A blind hole's depth is the depth of the full diameter, from the surface. The drill point does not count.

Write the note so the maker asks nothing.
