# Hands-on — Placing Dimensions — Diameters, Radii, Holes and Notes

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Measure a real hole's finish
Find a real counter-bored or countersunk hole — on a hinge, a bracket, a machine part, a door fitting. Measure the hole diameter, the enlargement diameter and its depth, and write the proper note for it.

## The blind-hole depth, with a drill in mind
In soft wood or thick card, drill (or push a pencil point) to make a blind hole. Measure the full-diameter depth from the surface with a thin stick, ignoring the pointed bottom, and write the note. Compare your depth with the stick's total reach — the difference is the drill point the rule excludes.
