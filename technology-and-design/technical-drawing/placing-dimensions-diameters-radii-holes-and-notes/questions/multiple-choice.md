# Placing Dimensions — Diameters, Radii, Holes and Notes — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A diameter value on a drawing is preceded by —
a) the letter R  b) the diameter symbol  c) no symbol at all
**Answer: b) the diameter symbol**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** R15 on a drawing means —
a) a diameter of 15  b) a radius of 15  c) 15 holes
**Answer: b) a radius of 15**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** A cylinder should be dimensioned, where possible, in —
a) the circular view only  b) the rectangular view, where diameter and length both show  c) no view — in the title block
**Answer: b) the rectangular view, where diameter and length both show**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** When a dimension is given to the centre of an arc, the centre is shown by —
a) a small cross  b) a shaded square  c) nothing
**Answer: a) a small cross**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The diameter and depth of a counter-bored hole are specified —
a) inside the circle  b) in a note  c) in the scale
**Answer: b) in a note**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** A countersink is shaped to let —
a) a flat-head screw sit flush  b) a drill pass through  c) a shaft spin freely
**Answer: a) a flat-head screw sit flush**

**7. (Sub lesson 2)** The depth of a blind hole, as specified, is —
a) the total reach of the drill including its point  b) the depth of the full diameter from the surface of the object  c) half the hole's diameter
**Answer: b) the depth of the full diameter from the surface of the object**
