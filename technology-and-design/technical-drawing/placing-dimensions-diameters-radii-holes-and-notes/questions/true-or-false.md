# Placing Dimensions — Diameters, Radii, Holes and Notes — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The diameter symbol may be left off when the view is a clear circle. — **False** (a diameter value is preceded by its symbol)

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A radius dimension line runs from the centre to the arc, its arrowhead touching the arc. — **True**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A spot-face is a deep conical enlargement. — **False** (it is a shallow flat seating; the conical enlargement is the countersink)

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The conical point a drill leaves at the bottom of a blind hole is counted in the specified depth. — **False** (only the full-diameter depth from the surface is specified)
