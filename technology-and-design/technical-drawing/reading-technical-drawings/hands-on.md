# Hands-on — Reading technical drawings

Real drawing practice with pencil and paper — this subject is learned by doing.

## Read in order
Read a part drawing strictly in order — title block, views, dimensions, technical requirements — and write a one-line summary after each step.

## Describe the part
From a drawing alone, describe a part fully to a partner: shape, main sizes and one requirement. Your partner checks your description against the drawing.
