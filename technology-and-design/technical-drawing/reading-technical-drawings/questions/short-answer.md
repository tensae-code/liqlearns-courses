# Reading technical drawings — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, or by doing the drawing step named.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Give the order of reading a part's drawing.
**Answer:** Title block first, then the views, then the dimensions and the technical requirements, until the shape is fully understood.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What does a finished drawing tell you about an object?
**Answer:** What it is, its shape in the views, its true sizes, and the requirements it must meet.
