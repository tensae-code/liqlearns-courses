# Reading technical drawings — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** What is the first thing to read on a part's drawing?
a) The largest dimension b) The title block c) The hatching d) The border
**Answer: b) The title block**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the title block give the reader?
a) The part's colour only b) The part's name, and such facts as scale and material c) The maker's opinion d) Nothing useful
**Answer: b) The part's name, and such facts as scale and material**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** After the title block, what builds the shape in your mind?
a) The views b) The sheet frame c) The lettering height d) The paper size
**Answer: a) The views**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** When is reading a drawing finished?
a) When every line has been looked at once b) When the part's shape is fully understood c) After one minute d) When the title block is copied
**Answer: b) When the part's shape is fully understood**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** What do dimensions add after the views?
a) The true sizes of the part b) The drawing's age c) The sheet number only d) Decoration
**Answer: a) The true sizes of the part**
