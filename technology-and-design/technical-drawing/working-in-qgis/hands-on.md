# Hands-on — Working in QGIS

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: coordinate reference systems (transforming data from WGS 84 / EPSG:4326 to the Greek grid GGRS87 / EPSG:2100, on-the-fly CRS transformation) and organising work as QGIS projects (.qgs files, data references/paths). Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
