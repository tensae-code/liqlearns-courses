# Intersection of a surface with a plane — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Say in one sentence what this lesson teaches.
**Answer:** Intersection of a surface with a plane — constructing the intersection line point by point for pyramid, prism, cone (the conic curves — ellipse, parabola, hyperbola) and cylinder, using auxiliary planes.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name one material, tool or step this lesson works with.
**Answer:** Any one named in the lesson text — for example, the first step of sub lesson 1: Intersection of a surface with a plane.
