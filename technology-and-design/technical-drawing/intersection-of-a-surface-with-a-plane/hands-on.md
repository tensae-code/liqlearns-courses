# Hands-on — Intersection of a surface with a plane

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: constructing the intersection line point by point for pyramid, prism, cone (the conic curves — ellipse, parabola, hyperbola) and cylinder, using auxiliary planes. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
