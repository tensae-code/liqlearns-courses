# Intersection of a surface with a plane

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Mongolia's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Intersection of a surface with a plane
Mongolia's book teaches it this way (*Design Drawing & Technology 12 (Mongolia)*, printed p. 14): constructing the intersection line point by point for pyramid, prism, cone (the conic curves — ellipse, parabola, hyperbola) and cylinder, using auxiliary planes. The learner works through the named materials and steps in order, saying what each step does before doing the next one.

## Sub lesson 2 — Intersection of a surface with a plane — doing it yourself
Now the learner does the whole task unaided: constructing the intersection line point by point for pyramid, prism, cone (the conic curves — ellipse, parabola, hyperbola) and cylinder, using auxiliary planes. Working from the first sub lesson's sequence, the learner plans the make, works through it step by step, and checks the finished piece against what Mongolia's book shows.
