# Hands-on — Graphic information and the kinds of drawings

Real drawing practice with pencil and paper — this subject is learned by doing.

## Three ways for one object
Choose a tool or a piece of furniture. Write a short word description, draw it pictorially, then draw a labelled diagram of it. Compare the three with a partner.

## Sort the images
Collect or draw six images — a photograph, a sign, a chart, a scheme, a pictorial picture and a technical drawing. Sort them into pictorial images and graphic images proper, and say why each belongs where you put it.
