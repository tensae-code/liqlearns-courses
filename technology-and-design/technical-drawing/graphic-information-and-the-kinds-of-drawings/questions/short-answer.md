# Graphic information and the kinds of drawings — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, or by doing the drawing step named.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name three kinds of graphic images proper.
**Answer:** Drawings, diagrams, charts, schemes and technical drawings (any three).

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** What can a photograph not do that a technical drawing can?
**Answer:** It cannot give the exact shape and size, to rules, that a maker needs to make the object.
