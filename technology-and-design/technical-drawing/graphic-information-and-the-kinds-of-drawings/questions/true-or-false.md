# Graphic information and the kinds of drawings — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A word description is a kind of graphic image.
**Answer: False** — Graphic information is carried by images; words alone are not graphic images.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A scheme shows how parts connect.
**Answer: True** — Showing connections is the scheme's job.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Every image tells everything about an object.
**Answer: False** — Each kind tells only what its kind can tell; choose the image for the job.
