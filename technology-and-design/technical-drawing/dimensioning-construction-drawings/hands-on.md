# Hands-on — Dimensioning construction drawings

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: level marks (±0.000, +2.35, −1.15), the module system, axis marks in 6–12 mm circles, and drawing a floor plan with wall thicknesses and room areas in m². Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
