# Orthographic projection — points, lines, planes and surfaces — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In orthographic projection, the lines of sight are…
a) diverging from one eye point b) parallel and at right angles to the picture plane c) always diagonal d) curved
**Answer: b) parallel and at right angles to the picture plane**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** How many principal views are there?
a) Three b) Four c) Six d) Eight
**Answer: c) Six**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** An inclined surface is…
a) parallel to a principal plane b) perpendicular to one principal plane but tilted to the others c) tilted to every principal plane d) always curved
**Answer: b) perpendicular to one principal plane but tilted to the others**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** An oblique surface shows its true shape in…
a) the front view b) the top view c) no principal view d) every view
**Answer: c) no principal view**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** An auxiliary view is projected onto a plane…
a) parallel to the inclined surface b) behind the object c) at random d) inside the object
**Answer: a) parallel to the inclined surface**
