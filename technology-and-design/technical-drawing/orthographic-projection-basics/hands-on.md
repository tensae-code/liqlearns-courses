# Hands-on — Orthographic projection — points, lines, planes and surfaces

Real drawing practice with pencil and paper — this subject is learned by doing.

## Tilt test
Hold a card parallel to your face, then tilt it, then turn it obliquely. Sketch what you see each time and label the class of surface.

## Project a point
Draw front and top views of a box and project one corner point between the views with a light projector line.
