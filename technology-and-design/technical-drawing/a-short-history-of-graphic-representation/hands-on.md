# Hands-on — A short history of graphic representation

Real drawing practice with pencil and paper — this subject is learned by doing.

## A timeline in drawings
Make a three-panel timeline of graphic representation — early depiction, ruled plan, modern technical drawing — drawing an example of each and writing one line under each on what it adds.

## Why the rules came
Pick one modern drawing rule (a line type, a dimension, a standard view) and write how an earlier image without that rule could mislead a maker.
