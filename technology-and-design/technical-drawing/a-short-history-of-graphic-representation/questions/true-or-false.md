# A short history of graphic representation — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Early depictions already gave exact construction sizes.
**Answer: False** — They showed and told, but did not give exact size or construction.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Graphic representation became a language with conventions.
**Answer: True** — Agreed rules let strangers read and build from a drawing.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The history of drawing makes its modern rules unnecessary.
**Answer: False** — The history explains why each rule exists.
