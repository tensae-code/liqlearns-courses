# A short history of graphic representation — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, or by doing the drawing step named.

**1. (Sub lesson 3)** Name one problem earlier images could not solve that the technical drawing solves.
**Answer:** A maker who had never seen the object could not build it exactly from a likeness alone; the technical drawing gives views, sizes and rules to build from.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In what order did graphic representation develop here?
**Answer:** Early depictions, then images/drawings made to rules, then the modern standard technical drawing.
