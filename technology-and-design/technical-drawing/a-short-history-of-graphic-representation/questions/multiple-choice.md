# A short history of graphic representation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What did early depictions do well?
a) Give exact construction sizes b) Record and show what a thing looked like c) Follow modern standards d) Replace all words
**Answer: b) Record and show what a thing looked like**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What could early depictions not yet give?
a) Any likeness b) Exact size and construction c) A record of animals d) A story
**Answer: b) Exact size and construction**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Why did drawings come to be made to rules?
a) To make them prettier b) So a thing could be made by someone who had never seen it c) To use more paper d) To hide information
**Answer: b) So a thing could be made by someone who had never seen it**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** What completes the development in the modern technical drawing?
a) Colour and shading only b) Standard views, dimensions, lines and lettering to standards c) Photographs d) Free handwriting
**Answer: b) Standard views, dimensions, lines and lettering to standards**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Knowing the history of graphic representation helps you…
a) Skip learning the rules b) Understand why the modern rules exist c) Draw without measures d) Date every sheet
**Answer: b) Understand why the modern rules exist**
