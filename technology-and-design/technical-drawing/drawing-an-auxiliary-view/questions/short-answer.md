# Drawing an auxiliary view — the true shape of an inclined surface — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, or by doing the drawing step named.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Describe the two moves that place one point in the auxiliary view.
**Answer:** Draw its projector at right angles from the edge view, then lay off along it the same distance from the folding line as the point sits from the reference line in the neighbouring view.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Name the three checks of a finished auxiliary view.
**Answer:** Projectors at right angles; transferred distances equal; same corners, holes and arrangement as the real surface.
