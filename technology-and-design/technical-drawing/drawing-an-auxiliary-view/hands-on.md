# Hands-on — Drawing an auxiliary view — the true shape of an inclined surface

Real drawing practice with pencil and paper — this subject is learned by doing.

## One slanted face
Draw front and top views of a wedge. Build its auxiliary view and cut the true shape from scrap paper to compare with the wedge's face.

## Distance drill
On a ready-made edge view, transfer five marked points into an auxiliary view and verify every distance twice.
