# The drawing board and its accessories — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, or by doing the step named.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** State the heart of this topic in your own words.
**Answer:** T-square (left- and right-handed), drawing-board clips, drawing pins / thumb tacks, cellotape for fixing the sheet.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** What do you do when your check finds something wrong?
**Answer:** Fix one thing you find, and say what you changed and why.
