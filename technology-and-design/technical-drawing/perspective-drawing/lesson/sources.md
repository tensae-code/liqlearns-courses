| perspective projection (hình chiếu phối cảnh): a representation built by central projection (phép chiếu xuyên tâm) whose centre of projection is the eye point (điểm nhìn) and whose picture plane (mặt phẳng tầm nhìn / mặt tranh) is an upright plane; its basic feature is that it gives the viewer a feeling of near and far distance like real observation; it is often used to represent large works (houses, dams, bridges…); by the number of vanishing points it divides into one-point perspective (the picture plane parallel to one face of the object — e.g. a room interior, the vanishing point F' on the horizon line tt), two-point perspective (the picture plane not parallel to any face — e.g. a house, with two vanishing points F' and G'), and three-point perspective; the steps for sketching a one-point perspective (draw the horizon line tt; choose a vanishing point F' on tt; draw the object's front view; draw the perspective projection of the lines perpendicular to the picture plane by joining the front view's points to F'; put the depth into the perspective — take a point I' on A'F' for the object's width and draw lines parallel to the front view's edges; finish by erasing the intermediate lines and darkening the visible edges) and for a two-point perspective (horizon line and two vanishing points F', G'; draw an initial reference line A'B' vertical so the angles F'A'G' and B'F'G' are not less than 120°; draw the perspective of the base; put the height in from a point D'; draw the object's edges by joining the determined points to the two vanishing points; finish) (original string `perspective projection (hình chiếu phối cảnh)`) | Vietnam, *Công nghệ 10 — Thiết kế và công nghệ*, printed p. 71 |
