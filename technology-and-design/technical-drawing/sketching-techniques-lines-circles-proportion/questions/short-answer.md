# Sketching techniques — lines, circles and proportion — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, or by doing the drawing step named.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Give the order of steps in the box method of sketching a circle.
**Answer:** Light enclosing square; centre lines and diagonals; mark where the circle crosses them; join the marks with a smooth curve.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** How can you measure proportion with a pencil?
**Answer:** Hold it at arm's length and mark lengths with your thumb, then compare the lengths.
