# Sketching techniques — lines, circles and proportion — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Looking at the pencil tip helps a long line stay straight.
**Answer: False** — Look at the end point you are heading for.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A multi-view sketch shows a 3D object in flat views.
**Answer: True** — Front, top and side, each seen looking straight at that face.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Detail should be drawn before the light proportional layout.
**Answer: False** — Lay out the whole sketch lightly in proportion first.
