# Sketching techniques — lines, circles and proportion — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How do you sketch a long straight line that stays straight?
a) With short finger strokes b) With one steady movement of the whole arm, eyes on the end point c) By pressing very hard d) By drawing it in dots
**Answer: b) With one steady movement of the whole arm, eyes on the end point**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** How can you check a midpoint you marked by eye?
a) Guess again b) Compare the two halves, e.g. by folding the paper c) Erase the line d) Ask the pencil
**Answer: b) Compare the two halves, e.g. by folding the paper**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** In the box method, what is drawn first?
a) The finished circle, dark b) A light enclosing square with centre lines and diagonals c) The shading d) The title
**Answer: b) A light enclosing square with centre lines and diagonals**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Before sketching an arc, what must you find first?
a) Its colour b) Its centre c) Its price d) Its name
**Answer: b) Its centre**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** What does proportion in a sketch mean?
a) Every part keeps its true relative size b) The sketch fills the page c) All lines are the same length d) The sketch is dark
**Answer: a) Every part keeps its true relative size**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** In a multi-view sketch, the front, top and side views must…
a) be drawn at random places b) stay in line so widths and heights agree c) overlap each other d) all show the same face
**Answer: b) stay in line so widths and heights agree**
