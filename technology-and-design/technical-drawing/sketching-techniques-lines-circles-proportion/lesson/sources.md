Era note: this guide is an older GEQIP-generation book (first edition 2003 E.C., funded through the General Education Quality Improvement Project, GEQIP) — a different curriculum generation from the current syllabus. These lessons present it as such, as the guide's testimony about the course of its era, never as the current syllabus unchecked. No student book for Technical Drawing exists in hand, so nothing here could be checked against a pupil text.

Honesty: The placements state that the guide teaches four methods of drawing a circle but do not enumerate the four individually; this lesson teaches the enclosing-square (box) method in full — the method the placements name for the GIF — and does not invent names for the other three. No narrower pages than printed pp. 4–7 are given in the placements, so none are claimed.

Malaysia's own way: Malaysia names two straight-line methods — the short-line method and the continuous two-point line method — and builds circles, ellipses, cylinders and cones inside isometric boxes, step by step.
