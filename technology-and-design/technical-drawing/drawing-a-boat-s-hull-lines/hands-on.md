# Hands-on — Drawing a boat's hull lines

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: the three orthogonal projections of the hull (side, half-breadth and body plan) on a numbered grid, choosing the widest section, and building the hull from sheet or carved from a block in clamps (2×5 cm battens). Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
