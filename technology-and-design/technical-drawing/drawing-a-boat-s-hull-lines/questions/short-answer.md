# Drawing a boat's hull lines — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Say in one sentence what this lesson teaches.
**Answer:** Drawing a boat's hull lines — the three orthogonal projections of the hull (side, half-breadth and body plan) on a numbered grid, choosing the widest section, and building the hull from sheet or carved from a block in clamps (2×5 cm battens).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name one material, tool or step this lesson works with.
**Answer:** Any one named in the lesson text — for example, the first step of sub lesson 1: Drawing a boat's hull lines.
