# Video script — Drawing a boat's hull lines

A short demonstration video, made later, in a super-simple voice.

Drawing a boat's hull lines.
Drawing a boat's hull lines — the three orthogonal projections of the hull (side, half-breadth and body plan) on a numbered grid, choosing the widest section, and building the hull from sheet or carved from a block in clamps (2×5 cm battens).
Watch the steps. Then do them yourself.
