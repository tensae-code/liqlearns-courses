# Drawing a labelled schematic of a made device — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, or by doing the step named.

**1. (Sub lesson 2) A label is correct but written across the connection lines so it cannot be read. Does it meet Jamaica's standard?**
**Answer:** No — the standard is correctly and neatly; an unreadable label fails the neatness half and makes the drawing unusable.

**2. (Sub lesson 3) Why does the maker draw the device they just made, instead of only building it?**
**Answer:** The drawings are the record another maker can build and check from — and drawing them proves the maker understands the connections and layout, not only the assembly.
