# Drawing a labelled schematic of a made device — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Reproducing the schematic means drawing the circuit…**
a) As you wish it were b) As it truly is in the finished device c) From imagination d) Without connections
**Answer: b) As it truly is in the finished device**

**2. (Sub lesson 2) The guide's labelling standard is…**
a) Some components labelled b) All components, correctly and neatly c) Labels in any place d) No labels needed
**Answer: b) All components, correctly and neatly**

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Neat lettering matters because…**
a) It decorates the page b) It makes the drawing readable and usable by others c) It hides faults d) It fills space
**Answer: b) It makes the drawing readable and usable by others**

**4. (Sub lesson 3) A layout diagram shows…**
a) The connections only b) Where the parts sit — the arrangement c) The price list d) The labels only
**Answer: b) Where the parts sit — the arrangement**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) A schematic missing one connection is…**
a) Still complete b) A wrong instruction to the next maker c) Neater d) A layout diagram
**Answer: b) A wrong instruction to the next maker**
