# Hands-on — Drawing a labelled schematic of a made device

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## The motor schematic
Reproduce the schematic of the electromagnetic motor (or another made device) with all components correctly and neatly labelled. Check it against the real device connection by connection.

## Layout companion
Draw the layout diagram of the same device, showing where each labelled part sits. Lay the two drawings side by side and confirm every label appears in both.
