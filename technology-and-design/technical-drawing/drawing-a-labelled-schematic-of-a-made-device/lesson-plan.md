# Lesson plan — Drawing a labelled schematic of a made device

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Technical Drawing · **Lesson:** Drawing a labelled schematic of a made device
**Place in the curriculum:** Technical Drawing (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Jamaica expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** a made device is recorded by reproducing its schematic diagram with every component correctly and neatly labelled — a drawing another maker could build and check from.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Jamaica's *National Standards Curriculum Guide — Grades 4–6 — Resource & Technology* (Ministry of Education, Youth and Information, Kingston, Jamaica, © 2018), a curriculum guide with full unit teaching content, read COMPLETE (see lesson/sources.md). It counts in the acceptance count (curriculum guide with full teaching content, not evidence-only).

Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **Reproducing the schematic** — The schematic of the device you made is drawn again, cleanly: the same connections, in the standard layout, complete.
2. **Labelling every component, correctly and neatly** — Each component on the diagram is labelled — correctly, so the label names the true part; neatly, so the drawing can be read at a glance.
3. **Diagrams that illustrate the layout** — Beyond the schematic, simple diagrams illustrate the device's layout — where the parts sit — completing the record of the make.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of Technical Drawing. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Reproduce the schematic of a made device completely and truly.
2. Label every component correctly and neatly.
3. Draw a simple layout diagram of a device to accompany its schematic.
## What you need
Paper, a pencil, a ruler, and the made device (motor or torch) to draw from.
