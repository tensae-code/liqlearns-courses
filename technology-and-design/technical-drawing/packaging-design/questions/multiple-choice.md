# Packaging design — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is traditional packaging?
a) packaging from plant materials such as coconut and banana leaves b) Only a price c) Only decoration d) Nothing to do with this lesson
**Answer: a) packaging from plant materials such as coconut and banana leaves**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is eco-friendly material?
a) Only decoration b) material that does not burden the environment c) Only a price d) A kind of glue
**Answer: b) material that does not burden the environment**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is complete label information?
a) Only history b) Only painting c) a label carrying everything the user needs to know d) Only storage
**Answer: c) a label carrying everything the user needs to know**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson teach, in one line?
a) good packaging is eco-friendly, easy to store, fully labelled, user-friendly, and keeps the product’s quality. b) Only prices c) Only old tools d) Nothing practical
**Answer: a) good packaging is eco-friendly, easy to store, fully labelled, user-friendly, and keeps the product’s quality.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** How does Malaysia's book teach this?
a) As theory only, never practised b) Only in the glossary c) Only as an exam d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Malay (RBT)
**Answer: d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Malay (RBT)**
