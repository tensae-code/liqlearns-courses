Indonesia's own way: Indonesia stocks this lesson from its Prakarya teacher guides (teacher-guide evidence only, counted nowhere) — the topic is worked the Prakarya way: observe and explore, then design (merancang), then produce, then present, building from local potential (potensi lokal).

Malaysia's own way: Malaysia grounds packaging design in plant-leaf tradition — coconut and banana leaves — before its five characteristics of good packaging.

Mongolia's own way: Mongolia teaches this inside Design Technology (Дизайн технологи) — designing a sweet box — researching boxes, and drawing the nets of a prism, pyramid, cone and cylinder (cone net centre angle = 360° × r/l; cylinder net length = πD), marking cut lines bold and fold lines dashed, using a morphological matrix to combine form, size, material and decoration. The Mongolian classroom's own examples named in the provenance rows above are part of the teaching, not decoration.
