# Packaging design

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Malaysia's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Packaging around us
Packaging wraps food and non-food products alike, and Malaysia begins with the traditional craft: packaging from plant materials — coconut and banana leaves — still living beside factory packaging.

## Sub lesson 2 — The characteristics of good packaging
Good packaging is judged by named characteristics: eco-friendly material, easy to store, complete label information, user-friendly handling, and keeping the product’s quality. A package is a design, judged like any design.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: good packaging is eco-friendly, easy to store, fully labelled, user-friendly, and keeps the product’s quality. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
