**Acceptance count: taught in 3 countries (Cuba, Fiji, Iran).**

Cuba's own way: Cuba teaches CAD on LibreCAD by name: the Grade 8 programme prescribes CAD tools for orthogonal and isometric drawing, the Grade 8 student book works LibreCAD for isometric drawing with layers and dialogues, and Grade 9 closes the course with the LibreCAD environment, constraints and editing.

Fiji's own way: Fiji teaches computer-aided drawing in Year 12 on AutoCAD — its icons, commands, object snaps, and zoom and pan.

Iran's own way: Iran introduces CAD in Grade 9 by its working purpose first — what CAD drawing is used for in jobs and projects — and only then the drawing-software environment, before any shape is drawn.
