# Hands-on — Line types on drawings

Real drawing practice with pencil and paper — this subject is learned by doing.

## Copy the four lines
Rule four lines across a page: a faint construction line, a firm black outline, a centre line of long and short dashes, and a fold line as the workbook draws it. Label each one.

## Use them together
Draw a circle: set it out with faint construction lines, finish the outline in black, and cross it with centre lines. Check the centre lines stick out just beyond the circle.
