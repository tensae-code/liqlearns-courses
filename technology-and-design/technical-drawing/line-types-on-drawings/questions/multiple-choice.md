# Line types on drawings — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is a construction line for?
a) The finished edge of the object b) Setting out the drawing — it stays faint c) Showing where to fold d) Marking the centre
**Answer: b) Setting out the drawing — it stays faint**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the black outline show?
a) A hidden edge b) The finished edge of the object c) The middle of a part d) A line to erase
**Answer: b) The finished edge of the object**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A centre line is drawn…
a) As one thick black line b) As long and short dashes through the middle c) As a row of dots only d) Around the outside of a shape
**Answer: b) As long and short dashes through the middle**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** What does a fold line tell the maker?
a) Cut here b) The sheet bends along this line c) This edge is hidden d) Paint this face
**Answer: b) The sheet bends along this line**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Where do centre lines cross?
a) At a corner b) At the exact centre of a circle or symmetrical part c) They never cross d) At the edge of the sheet
**Answer: b) At the exact centre of a circle or symmetrical part**
