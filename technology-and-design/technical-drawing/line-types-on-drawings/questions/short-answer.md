# Line types on drawings — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, or by doing the drawing step named.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why are construction lines drawn faint?
**Answer:** They are only guides for building the drawing; the finished outline must stand out black, so the guides stay light.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What two things does a centre line find for you?
**Answer:** The middle of a part, and — where two cross — the exact centre point.
