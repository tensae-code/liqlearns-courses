# Line types on drawings — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Construction lines should be the blackest lines on the drawing.
**Answer: False** — They stay faint; the outline is the black line.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Centre lines stick out a little beyond the shape they centre.
**Answer: True** — They extend just past the shape on both sides.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A fold line and an outline mean the same thing.
**Answer: False** — An outline is a finished edge; a fold line is where the sheet bends.
