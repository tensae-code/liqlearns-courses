# Lesson plan — Line types on drawings

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Technical Drawing · **Lesson:** Line types on drawings
**Place in the curriculum:** Technical Drawing (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Malta expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** each kind of line on a technical drawing has one job — a faint construction line to build with, a black outline for the finished edge, a centre line through the middle, and a fold line where sheet material bends.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on a student workbook — Malta's *Design and Technology — Graphical Communication Component — Workbook 1 ("Communicate Graphically")*, Directorate for Quality and Standards in Education, Malta, 2015 (see lesson/sources.md). It counts in the acceptance count (student workbook, not evidence-only).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **Construction lines and outlines** — A drawing is built on construction lines: faint, light lines you draw first to set out shapes, centres and sizes.
2. **Centre lines** — A centre line marks the middle of a round or symmetrical part.
3. **Fold lines** — A fold line shows where a flat sheet will be folded — on a net or development, it is the edge where one face bends up against the next.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of Technical Drawing. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Draw a faint construction line and a firm black outline, and say what each is for.
2. Draw a centre line through a circle or a symmetrical shape.
3. Show a fold line on a net and say what happens along it.
## What you need
A pencil, a ruler and plain paper.
