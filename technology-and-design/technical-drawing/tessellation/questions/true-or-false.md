# Tessellation — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A tessellation may have small gaps if the pattern looks nice.
**Answer: False** — No gaps and no overlaps — that is the rule.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Triangles and squares can tessellate together.
**Answer: True** — They are one of the workbook's repeating pairs.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A 60° rhombus appears in the workbook's tessellations.
**Answer: True** — It is one of the paired patterns practised.
