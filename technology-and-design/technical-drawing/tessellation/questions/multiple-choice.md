# Tessellation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A tessellation is…
a) Any pattern with shapes b) Shapes fitting perfectly together with no gaps and no overlaps c) Shapes drawn in a row d) A pattern of circles only
**Answer: b) Shapes fitting perfectly together with no gaps and no overlaps**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which of these tessellates alone?
a) Regular pentagon b) Hexagon c) Circle d) Star
**Answer: b) Hexagon**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Why does a regular pentagon fail alone?
a) It is too big b) Its corner angle does not divide 360°, so gaps are left c) It has too few sides to draw d) It only works in colour
**Answer: b) Its corner angle does not divide 360°, so gaps are left**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** In the octagon tessellation, the gaps are filled by…
a) Triangles b) Squares c) Hexagons d) Circles
**Answer: b) Squares**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Which pair does the workbook repeat?
a) Pentagons and circles b) Triangles and hexagons c) Squares and stars d) Rectangles and ovals
**Answer: b) Triangles and hexagons**
