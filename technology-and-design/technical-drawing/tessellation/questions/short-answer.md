# Tessellation — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, or by doing the drawing step named.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the four shapes the workbook tessellates alone.
**Answer:** Squares, rectangles, triangles and hexagons.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the strict test of a tessellation?
**Answer:** The shapes cover the surface with no gaps and no overlaps, and the pattern can repeat.
