# Hands-on — Tessellation

Real drawing practice with pencil and paper — this subject is learned by doing.

## Tessellate one shape
Cover half a page with one shape — squares, triangles or hexagons — with no gaps and no overlaps. Check the corners: they must meet exactly.

## Tessellate a pair
Draw the octagon-and-square tessellation: octagons in a row, squares in the gaps, repeated. Then try triangles with hexagons.
