# Hands-on — Pictorial sketching — isometric sketching that someone else could build from

Real drawing practice with pencil and paper — this subject is learned by doing.

## Build-from-my-sketch test
Sketch an object with dimensions and material. A partner lists what they could and could not make from it. Repair the gaps.

## Grid-paper practice
On grid paper, sketch the same block three times, checking that parallel edges stay parallel.
