# Pictorial sketching — isometric sketching that someone else could build from — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, or by doing the drawing step named.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Name three things a detailed technical sketch carries that a rough one may lack.
**Answer:** Features on every side, basic dimensions, and a basic material description.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** List the four marking items with their points.
**Answer:** Principles 50; accuracy 25; line weight, technique and neatness 20; lettering 5.
