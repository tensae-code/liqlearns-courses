# Video script — Surface Development — Flat Sheets That Become Things

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Unfold a box. Its whole outside lies flat on the table.

That flat layout is a development — the solid's surface, true size, on one sheet.

Boxes, cans, funnels, pipes, ducts: all begin as flat patterns like this.

Sheet-metal workers work from developments. An error in the pattern is an error in the thing.

Edges are hemmed — folded back for strength and safety.

Meeting edges are joined. The pattern allows for both.

Now unfold, draw and label a development of your own.
