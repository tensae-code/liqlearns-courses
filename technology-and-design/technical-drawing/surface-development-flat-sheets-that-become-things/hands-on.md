# Hands-on — Surface Development — Flat Sheets That Become Things

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Develop a real can's label surface
Wrap a strip of paper tightly around a tin can, mark where it overlaps, and unroll it: you have measured the development of the curved surface. Check its length against the can's circumference — they should agree.

## Card first, always
Before cutting any real material, make the full development in card, fold it up and check it closes. The guide's way is demonstrate, then practise — the card model is where mistakes are cheap.
