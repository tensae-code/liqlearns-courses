# Surface Development — Flat Sheets That Become Things — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name four everyday things made from developments.
**Answer: any four of — cardboard or metal boxes, tin cans, funnels, cake pans, furnace pipes, elbows, ducts, roof gutters.**

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Why must a hem's width appear in the flat pattern and not be taken from the finished size?
**Answer: because the hem consumes sheet material — if the allowance is not added in the pattern, the finished edge comes out short.**
