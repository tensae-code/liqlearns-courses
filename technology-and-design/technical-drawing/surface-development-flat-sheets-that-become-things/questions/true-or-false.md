# Surface Development — Flat Sheets That Become Things — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Furnace pipes, elbows and ducts are made from flat sheet by development. — **True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A prism's development contains curved surfaces. — **False** (a prism is all flat faces — rectangles and its two ends)

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The solid must be identified and its surfaces listed before its pattern is begun. — **True**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Hem and joint allowances are guessed at the bench after cutting. — **False** (they are drawn into the flat pattern before cutting)
