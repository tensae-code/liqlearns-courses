# Surface Development — Flat Sheets That Become Things — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A development is —
a) a photograph of a solid  b) the solid's entire surface laid out flat, at true size, in one connected pattern  c) a section through a solid
**Answer: b) the solid's entire surface laid out flat, at true size, in one connected pattern**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which of these is made from a flat development?
a) A cast-iron wheel  b) A roof gutter  c) A carved beam
**Answer: b) A roof gutter**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Sheet-metal workers work from developments because —
a) the development is their working drawing, and an error in it cannot be corrected after cutting  b) developments are decorative  c) sheets cannot be measured
**Answer: a) the development is their working drawing, and an error in it cannot be corrected after cutting**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The development of a cylinder's curved surface is —
a) a triangle  b) a sector  c) a rectangle whose length is the circumference
**Answer: c) a rectangle whose length is the circumference**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** A cone's curved surface develops into —
a) a sector of a circle  b) a rectangle  c) a square
**Answer: a) a sector of a circle**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** A hem is —
a) a type of rivet  b) an edge folded back on itself for strength, stiffness and safety  c) a hole in the sheet
**Answer: b) an edge folded back on itself for strength, stiffness and safety**
