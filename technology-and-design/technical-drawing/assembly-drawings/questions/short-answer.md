# Assembly drawings — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, or by doing the drawing step named.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Name two things an assembly drawing must show.
**Answer:** Parts in place/identified in the needed views, and the dimensions governing the assembly (overall, joining, checking) — any two.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** How does detailing differ from reading an assembly drawing?
**Answer:** Reading recovers each part's shape from the whole; detailing turns that into a separate working drawing for each part, complete enough to make it.
