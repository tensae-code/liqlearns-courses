# Assembly drawings — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is an assembly drawing's purpose?
a) To detail one part fully b) To show a whole unit assembled, parts in place and fitting together c) To list materials only d) To replace all part drawings
**Answer: b) To show a whole unit assembled, parts in place and fitting together**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which dimensions belong on an assembly drawing?
a) Every making dimension of every part b) Overall, joining and checking dimensions c) No dimensions d) Only thread pitches
**Answer: b) Overall, joining and checking dimensions**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Where does a part's full making detail belong?
a) On the assembly drawing b) On its own part (detail) drawing c) In the title block d) Nowhere
**Answer: b) On its own part (detail) drawing**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Detailing (дэталіраванне) means…
a) Adding decoration b) Working from the assembly drawing to the separate part drawings c) Copying the assembly larger d) Erasing part numbers
**Answer: b) Working from the assembly drawing to the separate part drawings**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Reading an assembly drawing means…
a) Reading only the title b) Recovering each part's shape and place from the whole c) Counting sheets d) Checking the scale only
**Answer: b) Recovering each part's shape and place from the whole**
