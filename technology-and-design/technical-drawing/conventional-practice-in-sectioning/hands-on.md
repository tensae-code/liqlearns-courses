# Hands-on — Conventional practice in sectioning — the agreed exceptions

Real drawing practice with pencil and paper — this subject is learned by doing.

## Correct the wheel
Take a section of a spoked wheel drawn with hatched spokes. Redraw it conventionally and write one line on why the spokes stay plain.

## Along or across?
List six parts — rib, shaft, bolt, web, spoke, block wall — and for a lengthwise and a crosswise cut each, write hatch or no hatch.
