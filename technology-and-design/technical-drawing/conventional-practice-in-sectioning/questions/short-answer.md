# Conventional practice in sectioning — the agreed exceptions — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, or by doing the drawing step named.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** State the central conventional rule.
**Answer:** Ribs, webs, spokes and solid parts like shafts and bolts are not section-lined when the plane passes along their length or axis; they are hatched when cut across.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** How is this unit's work assessed?
**Answer:** Continuously — the teacher follows and assesses each student's work over the whole activity of each lesson against the described minimum level, with extra help for those below it.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** When you finish a section, what question do you ask about each part before hatching it?
**Answer:** Was it cut across, or along its length or axis? Hatch it only if it was cut across (and it is not a conventionally uncut part cut along its axis).
