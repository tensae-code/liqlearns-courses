# Conventional practice in sectioning — the agreed exceptions — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Conventions are carelessness that good drafters avoid.
**Answer: False** — They are the agreed rules of the language; breaking them makes a drawing say something false.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A shaft sectioned along its axis is hatched to show it is solid.
**Answer: False** — By convention it is drawn uncut and unhatched along its axis.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Thin gaskets may be drawn solid black instead of hatched.
**Answer: True** — A standard conventional treatment for very thin material.
