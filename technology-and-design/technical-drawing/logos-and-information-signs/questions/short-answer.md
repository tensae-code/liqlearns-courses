# Logos and information signs — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Say in one sentence what this lesson teaches.
**Answer:** Logos and information signs — what a logo is (Toyota, Coca-Cola 1886–2009), designing a logo from a letter, and information signs (parking, hospital, airport, warnings).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name one material, tool or step this lesson works with.
**Answer:** Any one named in the lesson text — for example, the first step of sub lesson 1: Logos and information signs.
