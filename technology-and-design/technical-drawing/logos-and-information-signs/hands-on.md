# Hands-on — Logos and information signs

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: what a logo is (Toyota, Coca-Cola 1886–2009), designing a logo from a letter, and information signs (parking, hospital, airport, warnings). Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
