# Building sections and facades

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Mongolia's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Building sections and facades
Mongolia's book teaches it this way (*Design Drawing & Technology 11 (Mongolia)*, printed p. 65): the vertical section showing wall build-up (brick, insulation, plaster layers with levels 2500/2200/±0.000/−800), and naming facades (main, side, courtyard). The learner works through the named materials and steps in order, saying what each step does before doing the next one.

## Sub lesson 2 — Building sections and facades — doing it yourself
Now the learner does the whole task unaided: the vertical section showing wall build-up (brick, insulation, plaster layers with levels 2500/2200/±0.000/−800), and naming facades (main, side, courtyard). Working from the first sub lesson's sequence, the learner plans the make, works through it step by step, and checks the finished piece against what Mongolia's book shows.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: Building sections and facades — the vertical section showing wall build-up (brick, insulation, plaster layers with levels 2500/2200/±0.000/−800), and naming facades (main, side, courtyard). Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
