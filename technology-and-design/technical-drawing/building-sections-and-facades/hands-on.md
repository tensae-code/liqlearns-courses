# Hands-on — Building sections and facades

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: the vertical section showing wall build-up (brick, insulation, plaster layers with levels 2500/2200/±0.000/−800), and naming facades (main, side, courtyard). Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
