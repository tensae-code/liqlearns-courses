# Lesson plan — Drawing on a grid — copying and enlarging

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Technical Drawing · **Lesson:** Drawing on a grid — copying and enlarging
**Place in the curriculum:** Technical Drawing (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Cuba expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the grid (cuadrícula) copies and rescales a drawing square by square: draw the article on a grid to enlarge or reduce it, and take the template from the rescaled drawing.
**This lesson's own level:** stocked from Cuba's *Orientaciones metodológicas: Educación Laboral — 5to grado* and the Cuba books in lesson/sources.md — see lesson/sources.md. Cuba counts in the acceptance count for its student-book placements; its programme and methodological-orientations placements are evidence only, counted nowhere.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **Representing on a grid** — Cuba's Grade 5 guidance teaches representing an article mediante el método de cuadrículas — by the grid method — before making wooden articles: the original is squared, and each square's content is redrawn into the new grid, larger or smaller.
2. **From grid drawing to template** — The 2023 Grade 7 book uses the same method to copy and enlarge a drawing (§1.7 closing). The rescaled drawing then gives the plantilla — the template the maker traces onto the material — so grid, drawing and template form one chain.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
