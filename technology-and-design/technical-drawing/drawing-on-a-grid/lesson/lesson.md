# Drawing on a grid — copying and enlarging

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Cuba's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Representing on a grid
Cuba's Grade 5 guidance teaches representing an article mediante el método de cuadrículas — by the grid method — before making wooden articles: the original is squared, and each square's content is redrawn into the new grid, larger or smaller.

## Sub lesson 2 — From grid drawing to template
The 2023 Grade 7 book uses the same method to copy and enlarge a drawing (§1.7 closing). The rescaled drawing then gives the plantilla — the template the maker traces onto the material — so grid, drawing and template form one chain.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: the grid (cuadrícula) copies and rescales a drawing square by square: draw the article on a grid to enlarge or reduce it, and take the template from the rescaled drawing. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
