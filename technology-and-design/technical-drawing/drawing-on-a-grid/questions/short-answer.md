# Drawing on a grid — copying and enlarging — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In your own words, what is grid (cuadrícula)?
**Answer:** A network of equal squares laid over a drawing for copying or rescaling.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In your own words, what is template (plantilla)?
**Answer:** The full-size pattern traced onto material before cutting.
