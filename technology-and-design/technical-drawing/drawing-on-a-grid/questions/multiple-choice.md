# Drawing on a grid — copying and enlarging — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is grid (cuadrícula)?
a) a network of equal squares laid over a drawing for copying or rescaling b) Only a price c) Only decoration d) Nothing to do with this lesson
**Answer: a) a network of equal squares laid over a drawing for copying or rescaling**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is enlarge/reduce?
a) Only history b) redrawing at a bigger or smaller size by the grid method c) Only storage d) A kind of glue
**Answer: b) redrawing at a bigger or smaller size by the grid method**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is template (plantilla)?
a) Only old tools b) Only painting c) the full-size pattern traced onto material before cutting d) Only a price
**Answer: c) the full-size pattern traced onto material before cutting**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson teach, in one line?
a) Only decoration b) Nothing to do with this lesson c) Only history d) the grid (cuadrícula) copies and rescales a drawing square by square: draw the article on a grid to enlarge or reduce it, and take the template from the rescaled drawing.
**Answer: d) the grid (cuadrícula) copies and rescales a drawing square by square: draw the article on a grid to enlarge or reduce it, and take the template from the rescaled drawing.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** How does Cuba's book teach this?
a) As theory only, never practised b) Only in a glossary c) Only as an exam d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Spanish
**Answer: d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Spanish**
