# Hands-on — Reading an assembly drawing

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: title block, parts list, then each part's form, size and connections — worked on a valve and a lathe centre (GOST screw М8). Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
