Papua New Guinea's own way: Papua New Guinea teaches this inside Technology and Industrial Arts (TIA), Standards-Based — Architectural 3D Sketching; 3D Free-Hand Sketching — sketching as an engineering problem-solving tool. The PNG classroom's own examples named in the provenance rows above are part of the teaching, not decoration.

Kazakhstan's own way: Kazakhstan teaches this inside Художественный труд / Көркем еңбек (Artistic Labour) and Трудовое обучение / Еңбекке баулу (Labour Training) — §8 — sketches and drawings (эскизы, чертежи) of architectural buildings and structures: reading and making the architectural drawing. The Kazakh classroom's own examples, materials and national crafts named in the Kazakhstan provenance rows above are part of the teaching, not decoration.

Cuba's own way: Cuba teaches freehand drawing (dibujo a mano alzada) in Grade 7 in both editions — straight lines, curved lines, flat figures and the ellipse, and the two-view freehand drawing (frontal and lateral) of the article to be built; the croquis is its named product.

Indonesia's own way: Indonesia stocks this lesson from its Prakarya Kerajinan teacher guides (teacher-guide evidence only, counted nowhere) — sketching is taught inside designing a craft product: the design draft (rancangan) and design sketch (sketsa rancangan) come before making, and carry the product's display, packaging and presentation with them.

Fiji's own way: Fiji teaches free-hand sketching inside Basic Technology geometry — Year 9 takes sketching with rendering and shading, Year 10 returns to sketching in its geometry opening, and Year 13 Technical Drawing closes the set with freehand sketching and tone.

Jamaica's own way: Jamaica teaches sketching inside designing — learners produce concept drawings of the model car they are making, present them for peer critique, and modify the drawings in response; the drawing is the design's visual representation, revised in public before making begins.

Belarus's own way: Belarus teaches the sketch (эскіз) as a working drawing of a part in its own right — with its own purpose, sequence and rules — placed in the course after the complete projection work, not only at the start of designing.
