# Free-hand sketching — principles and place in design — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, or by doing the drawing step named.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the four jobs a free-hand sketch does.
**Answer:** It organises thoughts, generates ideas, records dimensions and transfers information.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Name two materials you need for free-hand sketching.
**Answer:** A soft pencil and plain paper (a notebook to keep ideas in).
