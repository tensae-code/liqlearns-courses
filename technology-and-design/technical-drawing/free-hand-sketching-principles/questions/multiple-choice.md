# Free-hand sketching — principles and place in design — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is free-hand sketching?
a) Drawing only with instruments at exact scale b) A fast drawing made with hand and eye only c) Copying a photograph d) Colouring a finished drawing
**Answer: b) A fast drawing made with hand and eye only**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which is NOT one of the four jobs of a sketch?
a) Organising thoughts b) Generating ideas c) Replacing all written instructions forever d) Transferring information
**Answer: c) Replacing all written instructions forever**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Where does sketching belong in the design process?
a) At the very end, after making b) In the beginning phase, fixing the design's parameters c) Only during selling d) It has no place in design
**Answer: b) In the beginning phase, fixing the design's parameters**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** How does instrument drawing differ from sketching?
a) It uses rulers and compasses for an exact scaled drawing b) It is always faster c) It needs no paper d) It is done without looking
**Answer: a) It uses rulers and compasses for an exact scaled drawing**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Where should you keep your sketch ideas?
a) In your memory only b) In a sketch notebook c) On loose scraps you throw away d) Only on a computer
**Answer: b) In a sketch notebook**
