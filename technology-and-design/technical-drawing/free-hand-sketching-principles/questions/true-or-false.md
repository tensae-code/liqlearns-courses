# Free-hand sketching — principles and place in design — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Sketching needs artistic talent before it can be useful.
**Answer: False** — Once the basic techniques are known, the limit is thinking through the details, not artistic ability.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A sketch can record dimensions beside the shape.
**Answer: True** — Recording dimensions is one of its four jobs.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** You should stop and erase every faint wrong line at once.
**Answer: False** — Sketch over light lines; use the eraser sparingly.
