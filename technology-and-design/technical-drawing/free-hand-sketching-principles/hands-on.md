# Hands-on — Free-hand sketching — principles and place in design

Real drawing practice with pencil and paper — this subject is learned by doing.

## Sketch and show
Sketch a tool from your home in two minutes, free-hand. Show it to a partner. They must name the tool and guess its length. If they cannot, sketch it again, clearer.

## Start the notebook
Rule the first page of a sketch notebook: date, object, one-line idea. Fill it with three quick sketches today.
