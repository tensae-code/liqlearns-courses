# Hands-on — School garden work

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## Work one bed end to end
In a school or home bed: prepare it, sow or transplant, water, and note the weeding it will need and when.

## Draw the garden plan
Draw a map (croquis) of a real or imagined school garden: beds, paths, water point, and which crop goes in each bed this season and next.
