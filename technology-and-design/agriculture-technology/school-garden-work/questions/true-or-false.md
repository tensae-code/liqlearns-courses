# School garden work — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Bed (cantero) is a prepared raised strip of soil where crops are sown in rows.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Crop rotation has nothing to do with this lesson.
**Answer: False** — It is part of the lesson: changing which crop grows in a bed each season to keep the soil healthy.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Polyculture is growing different crops together in the same ground.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.
