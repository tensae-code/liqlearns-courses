# School garden work — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is bed (cantero)?
a) a prepared raised strip of soil where crops are sown in rows b) Only a price c) Only decoration d) Nothing to do with this lesson
**Answer: a) a prepared raised strip of soil where crops are sown in rows**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is crop rotation?
a) Only history b) changing which crop grows in a bed each season to keep the soil healthy c) Only storage d) A kind of glue
**Answer: b) changing which crop grows in a bed each season to keep the soil healthy**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is polyculture?
a) Only old tools b) Only painting c) growing different crops together in the same ground d) Only a price
**Answer: c) growing different crops together in the same ground**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson teach, in one line?
a) Only decoration b) Nothing to do with this lesson c) Only history d) the school garden is worked as a whole craft: sow and transplant, water, weed and harvest — and, as the learner grows, prepare beds, feed the soil organically, plan the garden on a map, and rotate the crops.
**Answer: d) the school garden is worked as a whole craft: sow and transplant, water, weed and harvest — and, as the learner grows, prepare beds, feed the soil organically, plan the garden on a map, and rotate the crops.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** How does Cuba's book teach this?
a) As theory only, never practised b) Only in a glossary c) Only as an exam d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Spanish
**Answer: d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Spanish**
