# Running a fertigation system — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is stock solutions A and B?
a) the two concentrated fertiliser solutions mixed to feed the crop b) Only a price c) Only decoration d) Nothing to do with this lesson
**Answer: a) the two concentrated fertiliser solutions mixed to feed the crop**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is an EC meter?
a) Only decoration b) a meter reading the solution’s strength against a target value c) Only a price d) A kind of glue
**Answer: b) a meter reading the solution’s strength against a target value**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is coco peat?
a) Only history b) Only painting c) a growing medium made from coconut husk fibre d) Only storage
**Answer: c) a growing medium made from coconut husk fibre**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson teach, in one line?
a) running fertigation means mixing solutions A and B, reading EC and pH against targets, choosing media and seed, and managing site, water and cost. b) Only prices c) Only old tools d) Nothing practical
**Answer: a) running fertigation means mixing solutions A and B, reading EC and pH against targets, choosing media and seed, and managing site, water and cost.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** How does Malaysia's book teach this?
a) As theory only, never practised b) Only in the glossary c) Only as an exam d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Malay (RBT)
**Answer: d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Malay (RBT)**
