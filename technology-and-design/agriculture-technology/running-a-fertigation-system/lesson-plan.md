# Lesson plan — Running a fertigation system

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Agriculture Technology · **Lesson:** Running a fertigation system
**Place in the curriculum:** Agriculture Technology (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Malaysia expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** running fertigation means mixing solutions A and B, reading EC and pH against targets, choosing media and seed, and managing site, water and cost.
**This lesson's own level:** stocked from Malaysia's *Reka Bentuk dan Teknologi* (RBT — Design and Technology) textbooks — see lesson/sources.md. Malaysia counts in the acceptance count (student books, not evidence-only).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **Solutions, meters and media** — Running the system is chemistry and care: stock fertiliser solutions A and B, mixed as directed; EC and pH meters read against target values; growing media chosen — coco peat, rice husk or peat moss — and seed and varieties selected for the system.
2. **Site, equipment and irrigation types** — The site must be level with a clean water source, and the start-up cost faced honestly.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of Agriculture Technology. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Explain solutions A and B and the EC/pH checks.
2. Choose media, seed and irrigation type for a given crop.
3. Explain how Malaysia's book teaches it.
## What you need
Meter pictures; samples or pictures of the three media.
