# Running a fertigation system

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Malaysia's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Solutions, meters and media
Running the system is chemistry and care: stock fertiliser solutions A and B, mixed as directed; EC and pH meters read against target values; growing media chosen — coco peat, rice husk or peat moss — and seed and varieties selected for the system.

## Sub lesson 2 — Site, equipment and irrigation types
The site must be level with a clean water source, and the start-up cost faced honestly. Irrigation itself comes in types — sprinkler, mist and drip — each suiting a different crop and system.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: running fertigation means mixing solutions A and B, reading EC and pH against targets, choosing media and seed, and managing site, water and cost. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
