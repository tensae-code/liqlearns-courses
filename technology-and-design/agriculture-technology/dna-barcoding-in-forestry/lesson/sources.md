| DNA barcoding in forestry: a DNA barcode is a short, characteristic DNA sequence in the genome of a species, used to identify and distinguish it from other species (as products in a supermarket are identified by their barcode, organisms can be identified by the differences in their DNA barcode); its applications — identifying forest-tree species (accurately identifying forest-tree species, especially rare ones, matters greatly for conservation and propagation, but many species have very similar morphology making identification difficult, time-consuming and costly; with DNA barcodes scientists can identify forest-tree species quickly and accurately — barcodes have been built in Vietnam and worldwide for many rare forest trees such as hoàng đàn and sưa đỏ, some eucalyptus varieties and some ginseng species), authenticating forest products (many forest products such as precious timber, medicinal plants and wild animals, once processed into finished products, are very hard to identify accurately, causing difficulty for inspection and control by the forest-protection force and for preventing illegal exploitation, trade, transport and storage of forest products; DNA barcoding allows fast, accurate determination of the origin of forest products, helping prevent illegal activities in time and protect rare forest flora and fauna — customs today also applies DNA barcoding to determine the origin of products of wood, flora or fauna origin to stop the illegal cross-border transport of rare plants and animals and their products), and controlling harmful agents for forest trees (many insects are intermediate hosts transmitting disease to forest trees and are outwardly very similar to other insects, making them hard to identify for prevention; DNA barcoding identifies the disease-causing species precisely even at the latent (larval) stage, supporting programmes controlling pests and diseases of forest trees and raising the effectiveness of pest prevention and environmental protection) — the identification method noted: first extract and collect the DNA from the sample to be identified, amplify the specific DNA sequence (used as the barcode) by PCR, sequence it to create the barcode, and enter it into the database, an online system helping researchers collect, manage and analyse DNA barcodes (original string `DNA barcoding in forestry`) | Vietnam, *Chuyên đề học tập Công nghệ 12 — Lâm nghiệp – Thuỷ sản*, printed p. 16 |
