| biotechnology in forestry — the concept and the achievement survey: biotechnology in forestry applies biotechnology to create new forest-tree varieties, raise production efficiency, protect and develop forest resources towards sustainable forestry and ecological-environment protection; its main directions are selecting, creating and multiplying forest-tree varieties, conserving and developing the gene resources of rare wild animals and plants, genetic-diversity analysis of forest trees, protecting rare wild flora and fauna, and producing microbial preparations serving sustainable forestry — with the achievements: in selecting and creating varieties, gene technology and cell technology shortened the time to create varieties with outstanding yield, quality and resistance that traditional selection cannot reach (the triploid hybrid acacia lines X101, X102, X201, X205 — fast growth, straight stems, few branches, resistance to pests, disease and adverse conditions, good wood quality; the white eucalyptus line resistant to leaf-spot disease created using molecular markers (GA20, GA21); the transgenic acacia (xoan ta) line growing far beyond the non-transgenic variety; the transgenic eucalyptus lines UU28, UU78 and UU89 — fast growth, long wood fibres); in multiplying, tissue culture produces healthy, uniform, disease-free plants for large-scale afforestation programmes (acacia, eucalyptus, pine, agarwood (trầm hương), Ngọc Linh ginseng (sâm Ngọc Linh), … multiplied by cell tissue culture); in fertiliser production, microbial preparations and organic fertilisers restore and stabilise soil pH and raise the water-holding capacity of forestry land, reducing forest-fire risk, with the microbial preparations for pine (MF1) and for eucalyptus (MF2) named; and in DNA barcoding, barcodes have been built for rare forest species (Ngọc Linh ginsberg (sâm Ngọc Linh), sâm Vũ Diệp, tam thất hoang, …) and rare wild animals (hoa mai leopard, sun bear, moon bear, …) (original string `biotechnology in forestry`) | Vietnam, *Chuyên đề học tập Công nghệ 12 — Lâm nghiệp – Thuỷ sản*, printed p. 5 |
