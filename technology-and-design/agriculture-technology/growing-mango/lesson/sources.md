| growing mango (xoài): planting technique — spring crop (February–April) and autumn crop (August–October); spacing from 3 × 4 m to 8 × 8 m by variety and intensity; prepare the hole 80–90 cm in diameter and 40–50 cm deep with 20–30 kg organic fertiliser and 1 kg phosphorus per hole mixed with the soil and refilled (in low-lying Mekong land, dig drainage ditches in strips or mound up (đắp ụ) to avoid waterlogging and root rot); plant as for citrus, mounding the soil around the base 20–25 cm above the garden surface; care — weeding/earthing 2–3 times a year; top-dressing by annual tables (architecture period organic 15–30, business 50–70 kg/tree/year…), architecture-period applications 4–5 times a year at new-flush stages (February–October), business-period in four applications (after harvest: 75% organic + 60% P + 50% K, restoring growth; at flowering: 50% P + 30% K; three weeks after fruit set: 25% organic + 20% N + 15% K; one month after the third: 20% N + 15% K, accumulating substances in the fruit), the first in a trench 20–30 cm wide and 15–20 cm deep around the canopy projection; watering — architecture period every 2–3 days, about 10 litres/tree; business period: restrict water at flower-bud differentiation (December–January) and before harvest, otherwise every 2–3 days 40–50 litres/tree; pruning — pinch at 1.0–1.2 m height, keep a main trunk 0.6–0.8 m, keep 2–3 strong shoots as level-1 branches spread in all directions, pinch level-1 branches at 1.0–1.2 m to make level-2 and likewise level-3; in the business period thin and shorten branch tips annually to control canopy size and remove pest/diseased and shaded branches; controlling flowering — stimulate flower buds about 45 days after harvest with Paclobutrazol 10% at 10–20 g per metre of canopy diameter diluted in 20–30 litres of water and watered around the canopy; stimulate flowering: 40–60 days after Paclobutrazol, spray KNO₃ 3% (first spray), then seven days later KNO₃ 1.5% (second spray), wetting both leaf surfaces evenly; increase fruit set with GA₄ at 0.002–0.004% or H₃BO₃ at 0.01% before flowering (30% of flowers open) and at fruit set, and after fruit is two weeks old spray anti-drop preparations (α-NAA 0.002%, GA₃ 0.001%), repeated after 7–10 days; fruit bagging with special bags against pests and disease (original string `growing mango (xoài)`) | Vietnam, *Công nghệ 9 — Mô đun Trồng cây ăn quả*, printed p. 37 |
