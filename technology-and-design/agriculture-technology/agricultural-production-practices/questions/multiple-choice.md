# Agricultural production practices — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is compost?
a) rotted organic matter worked into the soil to feed it b) Only a price c) Only decoration d) Nothing to do with this lesson
**Answer: a) rotted organic matter worked into the soil to feed it**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is sowing calendar?
a) Only history b) the plan of what is sown in each season of the year c) Only storage d) A kind of glue
**Answer: b) the plan of what is sown in each season of the year**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is biological control?
a) Only old tools b) Only painting c) using living enemies of a pest to keep the pest down d) Only a price
**Answer: c) using living enemies of a pest to keep the pest down**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson teach, in one line?
a) Only decoration b) Nothing to do with this lesson c) Only history d) agricultural production begins with the ground: prepare the soil, feed it with organic fertilisers and compost, sow to a calendar, care for the crop after sowing, and guard its health — in urban, suburban and family agriculture alike.
**Answer: d) agricultural production begins with the ground: prepare the soil, feed it with organic fertilisers and compost, sow to a calendar, care for the crop after sowing, and guard its health — in urban, suburban and family agriculture alike.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** How does Cuba's book teach this?
a) As theory only, never practised b) Only in a glossary c) Only as an exam d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Spanish
**Answer: d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Spanish**
