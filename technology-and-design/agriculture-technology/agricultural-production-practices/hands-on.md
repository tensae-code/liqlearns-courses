# Hands-on — Agricultural production practices

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## Make a compost plan
List the organic materials available at home or school. Order them into a compost plan: what goes in first, what is added, and when the compost is ready to use.

## Draw the year's sowing calendar
For your place, draw a sowing calendar for four vegetables: sowing month, main cares after sowing, and harvest month.
