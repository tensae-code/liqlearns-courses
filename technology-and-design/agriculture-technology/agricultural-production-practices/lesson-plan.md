# Lesson plan — Agricultural production practices

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Agriculture Technology · **Lesson:** Agricultural production practices
**Place in the curriculum:** Agriculture Technology (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Cuba expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** agricultural production begins with the ground: prepare the soil, feed it with organic fertilisers and compost, sow to a calendar, care for the crop after sowing, and guard its health — in urban, suburban and family agriculture alike.
**This lesson's own level:** stocked from Cuba's *Educación Laboral — 9no grado* and the Cuba books in lesson/sources.md — see lesson/sources.md. Cuba counts in the acceptance count for its student-book placements; its programme and methodological-orientations placements are evidence only, counted nowhere.

Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **Preparing and feeding the ground** — Production starts before any seed: the soil is prepared, and organic fertilisers and compost — including worm humus (humus de lombriz) — are made and applied so the ground can feed plants. Cuba's Grade 9 book teaches this inside urban, suburban and family agriculture, the forms of growing closest to the learner's own home.
2. **Sowing, care and garden health** — Sowing follows a calendar; after sowing come the cultural cares, including the different types of irrigation. Garden health is its own part: recognising pests and diseases, using biological controls and plant tinctures (tinturas) instead of reaching first for chemicals.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
