# Agricultural production practices

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Cuba's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Preparing and feeding the ground
Production starts before any seed: the soil is prepared, and organic fertilisers and compost — including worm humus (humus de lombriz) — are made and applied so the ground can feed plants. Cuba's Grade 9 book teaches this inside urban, suburban and family agriculture, the forms of growing closest to the learner's own home.

## Sub lesson 2 — Sowing, care and garden health
Sowing follows a calendar; after sowing come the cultural cares, including the different types of irrigation. Garden health is its own part: recognising pests and diseases, using biological controls and plant tinctures (tinturas) instead of reaching first for chemicals.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: agricultural production begins with the ground: prepare the soil, feed it with organic fertilisers and compost, sow to a calendar, care for the crop after sowing, and guard its health — in urban, suburban and family agriculture alike. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
