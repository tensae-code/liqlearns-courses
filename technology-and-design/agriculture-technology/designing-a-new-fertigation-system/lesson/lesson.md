# Designing a new fertigation system

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Malaysia's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Designing the system
A new fertigation system is designed like any product: the new design is sketched, the design elements in it are analysed, and the sketch is evaluated before anything is built.

## Sub lesson 2 — The mock-up proves it
The design is then built as a mock-up or model of the new fertigation structure — the agricultural system passes through the same design cycle as a chair or a car.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: a new fertigation system is designed by the cycle: sketch, analyse the elements, evaluate, and build a mock-up. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
