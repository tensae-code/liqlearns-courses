| farming aquatic animals to the VietGAP standard: the concept (VietGAP — Vietnamese Good Aquaculture Practices — is the practice code applied in aquaculture ensuring food safety, reducing disease and environmental pollution, ensuring social responsibility and traceability of product origin, contributing to sustainable aquaculture), the benefits (for producers: lower production costs, stable quality, good relations with workers and the surrounding community; for workers: a safe working environment and labour skills raised through VietGAP training; for consumers and society: clear product origin and safe food; for processors: guaranteed raw material, lower inspection costs and more export opportunities), and the farming process (prepare the farming site — the site must lie in a planned aquaculture zone with no food-safety, labour-safety or environmental-sanitation risks; renovate and clean the site, removing pathogens in ponds, cages, canals and tidal flats by dredging mud, clearing bushes, liming, drying the pond bottom; supply enough clean water fitting each species, with a filter grid at the intake to block rubbish and wild fish; check and adjust the environmental indicators to the VietGAP standard; prepare the full farming equipment such as pumps, paddle wheels, boats and environmental measuring instruments (pH, temperature, turbidity, NH₃, H₂S, …); identify and prevent hazards to food safety, labour safety and the environment; select and stock seed of clear origin from quality-controlled seed farms, of balanced body, uniform size and quick reaction with no disease signs, quarantined to regulation, transported in specialised equipment, caught and transported gently to avoid abrasion, disinfected before stocking, at the density and season of the process; manage feed and feeding — feed of clear origin and assured quality, correctly packaged, of size and quality fitting each species and growth stage, its quality checked before use, never expired or substandard feed, and no hormones or growth stimulants; feed in the early morning or cool afternoon, in the right amount and way for each species; manage the environment — regularly inspect the environmental factors for timely treatment, periodically use microbial preparations to clean the pond environment, use paddle wheels to raise dissolved oxygen especially at night, on overcast days and in the last months of the crop, and observe the animals' activity daily to detect abnormalities early; manage disease — plan disease prevention for the farmed animals, use comprehensive prevention from the seed stage and prevention during farming, regularly monitor health with a prevention and treatment process fitting each species, and keep a disease log recording the disease situation, causes, medicines and treatments used; harvest with suitable tools and methods and specialised vehicles, recording the quantity, date and time, and the addresses of dispatch and destination for products leaving the farm; collect and treat waste — dead animals collected and buried away from the farming area with disinfectant, pond waste water treated to the environmental indicators before discharge, solid waste such as medicine bottles and chemical packaging collected into containers and destroyed to regulation, and all waste collection and treatment recorded; keep records and traceability — each farm household keeps a farming diary recording all technical information through the process (date of stocking, quantity and origin of seed, feed given, quantities, origin, method, medicines and chemicals used, environmental changes, disease, harvest quantity, …); and internal inspection — the farm owner self-inspects at least once a year on the basic contents of the VietGAP farming process; personnel requirements — managers must have aquaculture knowledge and VietGAP training certificates, workers must be trained in VietGAP and in labour safety for their exact jobs) (original string `farming aquatic animals to the VietGAP standard`) | Vietnam, *Công nghệ 12 — Lâm nghiệp – Thuỷ sản*, printed p. 101 |
