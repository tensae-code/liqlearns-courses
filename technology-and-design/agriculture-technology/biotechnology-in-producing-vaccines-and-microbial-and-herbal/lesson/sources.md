| biotechnology in producing vaccines and microbial and herbal preparations for aquatic disease prevention and treatment: DNA vaccine production (inactivated vaccines are widely used but costly to produce and give short protection, often needing boosters; DNA vaccines are more stable, cheaper to produce than inactivated vaccines and contain no pathogen, so they are safer than traditional vaccines; the production process drawn: isolate the pathogen → separate the gene encoding the antigen → insert the antigen-encoding gene into a plasmid and into the bacterial genome → grow the bacteria carrying the plasmid with the inserted antigen gene → purify the plasmid containing the antigen-encoding gene → add stabilising substances and bottle → inject the vaccine into the fish), microbial preparation production in four steps (step 1: isolate and select microorganism strains able to prevent and treat aquatic diseases; step 2: culture and multiply the biomass of the selected strains in a suitable medium and conditions; step 3: mix the microorganism biomass with a suitable carrier to make the preparation; step 4: package, preserve and use — worked for Bacillus sp.: isolate and select the Bacillus strain; culture and multiply it in Nutrient Broth medium at pH 7–7.5 and 30–35 °C; mix the Bacillus biomass with a suitable carrier (maltodextrin sugar, lactose, corn starch, …); package, preserve and use — the beneficial bacteria compete with or produce substances inhibiting the pathogens, or stimulate the animals' immunity), and herbal preparations (many herbs — garlic, Artemisia annua (thanh hao hoa vàng), white basil (hương nhu trắng), ngũ bội tử, cà gai leo, xuyên tâm liên, hoàng thảo, trầu không, thanh táo, … — are researched and applied in preventing and treating aquatic diseases; they contain many active substances of high antibacterial activity (allicin, polyphenols, alkaloids, quinones, terpenoids, steroids, …) and can raise the animals' immunity, and herbal preparations can be used for both prevention and treatment, are safe for humans and environmentally friendly — the worked preparation of fermented garlic rich in allicin: grind fresh garlic finely; mix with molasses or sugar syrup, vinegar and clean water evenly in the ratio 10:1:1:16; put the mixture into a container and ferment about 10–15 days in summer or 25–30 days in winter; the resulting fermented garlic liquid rich in allicin is packaged, preserved and used — dosed at 2 litres of preparation mixed into 1 tonne of fish feed, fed 1–2 times a month for disease prevention) (original string `biotechnology in producing vaccines and microbial and herbal preparations for aquatic disease prevention and treatment`) | Vietnam, *Công nghệ 12 — Lâm nghiệp – Thuỷ sản*, printed p. 130 |
