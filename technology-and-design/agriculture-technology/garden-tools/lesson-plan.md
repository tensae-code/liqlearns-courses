# Lesson plan — Garden tools

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Agriculture Technology · **Lesson:** Garden tools
**Place in the curriculum:** Agriculture Technology (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Cuba expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** every garden tool has its work, its safe way of being used, and its care: a tool that is used right, cleaned and stored lasts and does not hurt its user.
**This lesson's own level:** stocked from Cuba's *Educación Laboral — 9no grado* and the Cuba books in lesson/sources.md — see lesson/sources.md. Cuba counts in the acceptance count for its student-book placements; its programme and methodological-orientations placements are evidence only, counted nowhere.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **The garden tools and their uses** — The learner meets the agricultural work tools one by one — what each is for, how it is held and worked, and which job it must never be forced into. Cuba's Grade 9 book gives the tools their own section inside the agriculture chapter, before the growing work begins.
2. **Care and maintenance of tools** — After use comes care: cleaning off soil, drying, checking edges and handles, and storing tools in their place. Maintenance is taught as part of using the tool, not as an extra — the tool you care for is ready and safe next time.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
