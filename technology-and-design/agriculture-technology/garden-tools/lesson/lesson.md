# Garden tools

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Cuba's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — The garden tools and their uses
The learner meets the agricultural work tools one by one — what each is for, how it is held and worked, and which job it must never be forced into. Cuba's Grade 9 book gives the tools their own section inside the agriculture chapter, before the growing work begins.

## Sub lesson 2 — Care and maintenance of tools
After use comes care: cleaning off soil, drying, checking edges and handles, and storing tools in their place. Maintenance is taught as part of using the tool, not as an extra — the tool you care for is ready and safe next time.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: every garden tool has its work, its safe way of being used, and its care: a tool that is used right, cleaned and stored lasts and does not hurt its user. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.

## Do It Yourself
1. Do the Do it first task again, carefully this time, using everything the lesson taught.
2. Check your work against the lesson's own checks — fix one thing you find.
3. Keep your work: it is practice evidence, and the next lesson builds on it.
