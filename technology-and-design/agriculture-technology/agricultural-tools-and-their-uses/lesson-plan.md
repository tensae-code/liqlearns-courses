# Lesson plan — Agricultural tools and their uses

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Agriculture Technology · **Lesson:** Agricultural tools and their uses
**Place in the curriculum:** Agriculture Technology (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Rwanda expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the farm has its own toolkit: Rwanda opens its technology course by identifying the agricultural tools, grouping them by their uses, and learning the work each one does in farming.
**This lesson's own level:** stocked from Rwanda's *Science and Elementary Technology* pupil's books (Rwanda Education Board) — see lesson/sources.md. Rwanda counts in the acceptance count (student books, not evidence-only). The lesson also carries teacher-guide evidence, named in sources and counted nowhere.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **Identifying agricultural tools and grouping them by uses** — The Primary 4 book's first technology lesson identifies the agricultural tools and groups them by their uses — tools that dig together, tools that cut together, tools that carry together.
2. **Uses of agricultural tools in farming work** — The second lesson walks the groups into the field: what each tool actually does in farming work.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of Agriculture Technology. Recommended, never forced.
