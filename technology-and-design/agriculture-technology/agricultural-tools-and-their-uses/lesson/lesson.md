# Agricultural tools and their uses

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Rwanda's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Identifying agricultural tools and grouping them by uses
The Primary 4 book's first technology lesson identifies the agricultural tools and groups them by their uses — tools that dig together, tools that cut together, tools that carry together. Grouping by use is the learning: the group tells you the work before you ever pick the tool up.

## Sub lesson 2 — Uses of agricultural tools in farming work
The second lesson walks the groups into the field: what each tool actually does in farming work. The guide's lesson sequence teaches the same Unit 1 identification to the teacher, so the pupil's grouping and the guide's sequence are two views of one lesson.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: the farm has its own toolkit: Rwanda opens its technology course by identifying the agricultural tools, grouping them by their uses, and learning the work each one does in farming. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
