# Agricultural tools and their uses — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Agricultural tools open Rwanda's Primary 4 technology course.
**Answer: True** — Unit 1 of the Primary 4 book is the agricultural tools unit.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A tool's use-group is learned before its field work is walked through.
**Answer: True** — Identification and grouping come first; uses in farming work follow.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Grouping tools by uses means the group tells you the work.
**Answer: True** — That is the point of the grouping — diggers with diggers, cutters with cutters.
