# Agricultural tools and their uses — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How does Rwanda group the agricultural tools?
a) By price b) By their uses c) By colour d) By owner
**Answer: b) By their uses**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What does a tool's group tell you?
a) Its price b) The work it does c) Its age d) Its shop
**Answer: b) The work it does**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the second lesson teach?
a) Tool prices b) The uses of the tools in farming work c) Tool history d) Tool songs
**Answer: b) The uses of the tools in farming work**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** How do the pupil's book and the teacher's guide relate here?
a) They teach different units b) The guide's sequence teaches the same Unit 1 identification c) The guide replaces the tools d) They disagree
**Answer: b) The guide's sequence teaches the same Unit 1 identification**

**5. (Sub lesson 1)** Why group by use rather than by name?
a) Names are secret b) Grouping by use teaches the work each tool does c) It is shorter d) Alphabet was banned
**Answer: b) Grouping by use teaches the work each tool does**
