| gene technology in creating aquatic varieties: gene technology is applied mainly through the gene-transfer technique on fertilised eggs or early-stage embryos; the basic steps of gene transfer into aquatic animals in three steps (step 1: select and collect the gene to be transferred and prepare the recipient cells; step 2: put the gene to be transferred into the recipient cell (embryo) by a suitable technique; step 3: incubate the eggs and create the transgenic stock) — with the worked diagram of transferring the growth-hormone (GH) gene into Atlantic salmon (the broodstock Atlantic salmon's fertilised eggs receive the construct built from the regulatory gene of the ocean pout (cá pout đại dương) and the growth-hormone gene of the Chinook salmon, producing the transgenic Atlantic salmon stock), and the result after 18 months of farming: the GH-transgenic Atlantic salmon is 61 cm long and weighs 3.0 kg against the non-transgenic fish at 33 cm and 1.3 kg; and creating triploid aquatic varieties (thanks to biotechnology, triploid (3n) aquatic varieties grow far beyond diploid (2n) ones, bringing great benefit to farmers — triploid varieties have been created such as oysters, clams (nghêu), mussels (trai), scallops (sò điệp), cá hồi vân, salmon, …; the steps drawn: the egg (single set) is fertilised by sperm, the fertilised egg's second polar body is retained by intervention (heat shock, high pressure, chemicals) so the zygote carries three chromosome sets and develops into the triploid animal) (original string `gene technology in creating aquatic varieties`) | Vietnam, *Chuyên đề học tập Công nghệ 12 — Lâm nghiệp – Thuỷ sản*, printed p. 25 |
