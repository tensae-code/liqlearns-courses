| the technique of nursing fish seed in two stages: stage 1, nursing from fry (cá bột) to fingerling (cá hương) — prepare the pond (suitable site, area 500–2,000 m², depth 1.2–1.5 m, renovated to standard; water taken into the pond 5–7 days before stocking), select and stock the fry (fry that have completely absorbed the yolk sac and can feed externally, body length 1–10 mm by species; stocking seasons — the North has two main crops, February–May and July–September, the South farms all year, mainly February–May; stock in the early morning or late afternoon, balancing the temperature inside the transport bag against the pond water so the fish are not heat-shocked; nursing density from 100 to 250 fry/m² by species), care and manage (choose the feed and feeding regime to the species' standard nursing process; monitor the environment and the fry's health regularly and treat problems promptly; use microbial preparations periodically to the maker's instructions to improve the pond environment), and harvest (after about 25 days of nursing, stop feeding 1–2 days before harvest; harvest in the early morning or cool afternoon with a fine-mesh net or drag net, handling gently to avoid injuring the fish, then keep the fingerlings in a cage or tank at least 4–5 hours to acclimatise them to the stocking density before putting them into the transport equipment); stage 2, nursing from fingerling to juvenile seed (cá giống) — prepare the pond (rectangular, 1.2–1.5 m deep, 1,000–2,000 m²), select and stock (fingerlings 0.7–7 cm — tilapia fingerlings 0.71–2.5 cm, pangasius 3–7 cm; stock early morning or cool afternoon at 10–20 fish/m² by species), care and manage (industrial feed of 30–40% protein by the species' nutritional needs; the daily feed amount calculated from the total fish weight in the pond, at first 10–15% of body weight per day, later reduced to 7–10%), and harvest (after 30–60 days, when the seed reaches the standard sizes in the table — tilapia over 2.5 cm and 1.0 g, carp 7–10 cm and 15–20 g, grass carp 12–15 cm and 40–45 g, bighead-type carp 5–6 cm, pangasius seed small grade 7–15 cm and 3–10 g, large grade 15–20 cm and 10–30 g, per QCVN 02-33 standards) (original string `the technique of nursing fish seed in two stages`) | Vietnam, *Công nghệ 12 — Lâm nghiệp – Thuỷ sản*, printed p. 72 |
