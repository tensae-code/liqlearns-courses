# Gardens and ornamental plants

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Cuba's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — The garden and its care
The school garden and the family garden are the teaching ground: watering, hilling (aporque), weeding and pruning are the recurring cares, each with its moment and its reason. Cuba's Grade 7 programme teaches them inside its agriculture unit, with the garden tools named alongside.

## Sub lesson 2 — Reproducing ornamental plants
New plants come from old ones: cuttings (estacas), layering (acodo) and division multiply ornamental plants without seed. Transplanting and sowing in pots complete the set — the learner can start a plant, move it, and keep it growing.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: a garden is planned care: know the school or family garden, keep it watered, hilled and weeded, prune at the right time, and multiply ornamental plants by cuttings, layering and division. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
