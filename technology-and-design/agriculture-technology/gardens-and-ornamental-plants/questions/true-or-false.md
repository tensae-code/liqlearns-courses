# Gardens and ornamental plants — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Cutting (estaca) is a piece of stem planted to grow into a new plant.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Layering (acodo) has nothing to do with this lesson.
**Answer: False** — It is part of the lesson: rooting a stem while it is still joined to the parent plant.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Hilling (aporque) is drawing soil up around a plant's stem for support and rooting.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.
