# Gardens and ornamental plants — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In your own words, what is cutting (estaca)?
**Answer:** A piece of stem planted to grow into a new plant.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In your own words, what is hilling (aporque)?
**Answer:** Drawing soil up around a plant's stem for support and rooting.
