# Gardens and ornamental plants — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is cutting (estaca)?
a) a piece of stem planted to grow into a new plant b) Only a price c) Only decoration d) Nothing to do with this lesson
**Answer: a) a piece of stem planted to grow into a new plant**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is layering (acodo)?
a) Only history b) rooting a stem while it is still joined to the parent plant c) Only storage d) A kind of glue
**Answer: b) rooting a stem while it is still joined to the parent plant**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is hilling (aporque)?
a) Only old tools b) Only painting c) drawing soil up around a plant's stem for support and rooting d) Only a price
**Answer: c) drawing soil up around a plant's stem for support and rooting**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson teach, in one line?
a) Only decoration b) Nothing to do with this lesson c) Only history d) a garden is planned care: know the school or family garden, keep it watered, hilled and weeded, prune at the right time, and multiply ornamental plants by cuttings, layering and division.
**Answer: d) a garden is planned care: know the school or family garden, keep it watered, hilled and weeded, prune at the right time, and multiply ornamental plants by cuttings, layering and division.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** How does Cuba's book teach this?
a) As theory only, never practised b) Only in a glossary c) Only as an exam d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Spanish
**Answer: d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Spanish**
