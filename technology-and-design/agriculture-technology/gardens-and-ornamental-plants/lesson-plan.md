# Lesson plan — Gardens and ornamental plants

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Agriculture Technology · **Lesson:** Gardens and ornamental plants
**Place in the curriculum:** Agriculture Technology (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Cuba expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** a garden is planned care: know the school or family garden, keep it watered, hilled and weeded, prune at the right time, and multiply ornamental plants by cuttings, layering and division.
**This lesson's own level:** stocked from Cuba's Educación Laboral programmes and methodological orientations (teacher programme documents) — see lesson/sources.md. Cuba is evidence only for this lesson, counted nowhere in the acceptance count.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **The garden and its care** — The school garden and the family garden are the teaching ground: watering, hilling (aporque), weeding and pruning are the recurring cares, each with its moment and its reason. Cuba's Grade 7 programme teaches them inside its agriculture unit, with the garden tools named alongside.
2. **Reproducing ornamental plants** — New plants come from old ones: cuttings (estacas), layering (acodo) and division multiply ornamental plants without seed. Transplanting and sowing in pots complete the set — the learner can start a plant, move it, and keep it growing.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
