| biological bedding (đệm lót sinh học) for livestock houses: what it is (a mixture lining the house floor that reduces smell, cuts environmental pollution and suppresses and destroys harmful microbes), its two components — the bedding materials (dry, water-absorbent, crumbly materials: rice husks, straw, sawdust, peanut shells, maize cobs, cane bagasse) and the microbial preparation (Bacillus subtilis and Bacillus licheniformis producing enzymes that decompose cellulose, starch and protein; Streptomyces sp. producing antibiotics and enzymes and reducing rotten smells; Lactobacillus plantarum and L. acidophilus producing lactic acid and bacteriocin and deodorising strongly; Saccharomyces cerevisiae fermenting to a pleasant smell and stimulating the other microbes), its advantages and disadvantages (the microbes' activity can raise the house temperature, so houses must be airy and cooled in summer), and making it in five steps (spread the first bedding layer 20–30 cm thick and water it evenly; sprinkle part of the microbial preparation; spread the second layer 20–30 cm; sprinkle the rest; cover the whole surface with tarpaulin or nylon, and stock the animals after 2–3 days) (original string `biological bedding (đệm lót sinh học) for livestock houses`) | Vietnam, *Công nghệ 11 — Chuyên đề học tập: Công nghệ chăn nuôi*, printed p. 22 |
