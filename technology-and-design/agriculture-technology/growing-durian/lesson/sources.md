| growing durian (sầu riêng): planting technique — usually at the start of the rainy season in the South (late April–early May) to save establishment watering; spacing 6–8 m between trees and rows, a density of 125–277 trees/ha; prepare the hole — in high land such as the Central Highlands, round holes 80 cm in diameter or square holes 70–80 cm across and 50–60 cm deep; in low-lying Mekong Delta land, dig ditches in strips (lên liếp) or build mounds (ụ) 70–100 cm across the top and 100–150 cm across the base to prevent waterlogging, widening the mound yearly with the tree's growth rate; basal dressing 20–30 kg organic fertiliser + 0.7–1.0 kg superphosphate + 0.5 kg lime powder per hole, mixed with the topsoil and refilled into the hole or mixed with the mound soil; plant as for citrus and shade the young plant, not blocking more than 50% of the light; care — weeding/earthing 2–3 times a year, legume intercrops allowed; top-dressing by annual tables (architecture organic 10–30, business 50–70 kg/tree/year with N, P, K), architecture applications 4–5 times a year before and after new flushes, business applications in four (after harvest: 100% organic + 40% N + 30% P + 25% K; at flowering: 20% N + 40% P + 25% K; two weeks after fruit set: 20% N + 15% P + 20% K; one month after the third: 20% N + 15% P + 30% K), applied dissolved for watering or broadcast around the base, the post-harvest application in a trench 20–30 cm wide and 15–20 cm deep by canopy projection; watering — full water all year in the architecture period; in the business period much water after harvest, at flush growth, flowering and fruit growth, but water restricted in the period before flowering to create the conditions for flower-bud differentiation; pruning — from the first years, remove wrongly-placed level-1 branches after each flush to make even canopy tiers, the first tier about 1.0 m above the ground, each tier of 3–4 level-1 branches, tiers 0.4–0.6 m apart; in the business period prune mainly after harvest (old weak, pest/diseased, upright or downward-hanging branches, thinning for an airy canopy); controlling flowering — create dryness: 30–40 days after the second fertiliser application, spray Paclobutrazol at 0.001–0.0015% on the undersides of the leaves on both faces; when the flower buds reach 0.5 cm (30–40 days after stimulating the buds), water once every two days to help the flowers develop; increase fruit set with Ca-, Bo- and K-containing foliar fertilisers as recommended and/or supplementary hand pollination of the flower clusters mid-canopy, and thin out excess flower clusters and young fruit so the fruit set is just enough (original string `growing durian (sầu riêng)`) | Vietnam, *Công nghệ 9 — Mô đun Trồng cây ăn quả*, printed p. 45 |
