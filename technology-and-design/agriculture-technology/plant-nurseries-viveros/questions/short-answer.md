# Plant nurseries (viveros) — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In your own words, what is nursery (vivero)?
**Answer:** The place where young plants are raised before transplanting.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In your own words, what is seedling bag?
**Answer:** A small bag of soil in which a young plant is raised and moved.
