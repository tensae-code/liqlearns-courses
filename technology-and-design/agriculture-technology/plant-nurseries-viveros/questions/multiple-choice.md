# Plant nurseries (viveros) — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is nursery (vivero)?
a) the place where young plants are raised before transplanting b) Only a price c) Only decoration d) Nothing to do with this lesson
**Answer: a) the place where young plants are raised before transplanting**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is grafting (injerto)?
a) Only history b) joining a shoot to a rooted stem so they grow as one plant c) Only storage d) A kind of glue
**Answer: b) joining a shoot to a rooted stem so they grow as one plant**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is seedling bag?
a) Only old tools b) Only painting c) a small bag of soil in which a young plant is raised and moved d) Only a price
**Answer: c) a small bag of soil in which a young plant is raised and moved**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson teach, in one line?
a) Only decoration b) Nothing to do with this lesson c) Only history d) a nursery (vivero) raises young plants before their real life begins: seedlings in bags, grafted plants — including the English graft — and ornamental plants started under care.
**Answer: d) a nursery (vivero) raises young plants before their real life begins: seedlings in bags, grafted plants — including the English graft — and ornamental plants started under care.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** How does Cuba's book teach this?
a) As theory only, never practised b) Only in a glossary c) Only as an exam d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Spanish
**Answer: d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Spanish**
