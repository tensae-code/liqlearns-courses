# Plant nurseries (viveros) — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Nursery (vivero) is the place where young plants are raised before transplanting.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Grafting (injerto) has nothing to do with this lesson.
**Answer: False** — It is part of the lesson: joining a shoot to a rooted stem so they grow as one plant.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Seedling bag is a small bag of soil in which a young plant is raised and moved.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.
