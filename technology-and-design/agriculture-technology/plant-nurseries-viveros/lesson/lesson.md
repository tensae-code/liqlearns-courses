# Plant nurseries (viveros)

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Cuba's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — The nursery and its seedling bags
In the vivero, plants start small and protected: seedlings are raised in bags (bolsas) that hold their soil and roots together until transplanting. Cuba's Grade 9 book gives nurseries their own sections inside the agriculture chapter.

## Sub lesson 2 — Grafting and ornamental plants
Grafting joins a chosen shoot to a rooted stem so the new plant carries the chosen one's qualities; the book teaches grafts including the English graft (injerto inglés). Ornamental plants are raised in the nursery too, by the same careful starting methods.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: a nursery (vivero) raises young plants before their real life begins: seedlings in bags, grafted plants — including the English graft — and ornamental plants started under care. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
