# Lesson plan — Plant nurseries (viveros)

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Agriculture Technology · **Lesson:** Plant nurseries (viveros)
**Place in the curriculum:** Agriculture Technology (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Cuba expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** a nursery (vivero) raises young plants before their real life begins: seedlings in bags, grafted plants — including the English graft — and ornamental plants started under care.
**This lesson's own level:** stocked from Cuba's *Educación Laboral — 9no grado* and the Cuba books in lesson/sources.md — see lesson/sources.md. Cuba counts in the acceptance count for its student-book placements; its programme and methodological-orientations placements are evidence only, counted nowhere.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **The nursery and its seedling bags** — In the vivero, plants start small and protected: seedlings are raised in bags (bolsas) that hold their soil and roots together until transplanting. Cuba's Grade 9 book gives nurseries their own sections inside the agriculture chapter.
2. **Grafting and ornamental plants** — Grafting joins a chosen shoot to a rooted stem so the new plant carries the chosen one's qualities; the book teaches grafts including the English graft (injerto inglés). Ornamental plants are raised in the nursery too, by the same careful starting methods.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
