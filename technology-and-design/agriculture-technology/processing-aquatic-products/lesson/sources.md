| processing aquatic products: traditional fish sauce from fish in four steps (step 1: prepare the materials — fresh fish and clean salt, wash the fish and drain (the fish used can be freshwater fish such as tilapia and cá linh or marine fish, anchovies (cá cơm) being the most common); step 2: ferment (ủ chượp) — mix fish and salt in suitable ratio into the fermenting vessel (wooden barrel or glazed earthenware jar), cover the top with a thick layer of salt, press down, cover tightly and ferment from 6 to 12 months; step 3: draw and filter the sauce — when the sauce is fragrant and a rich reddish-brown, it can be drawn; draw it through the tap at the bottom of the barrel and filter to remove sediment so the sauce is clear; step 4: bottle, label, preserve and take to consumption — the flow drawn as fish + salt → ferment 6–12 months → draw and filter → bottle), pickled shrimp (tôm chua) in three steps (step 1: prepare and pre-process the materials — wash fresh shrimp, cut the whiskers and remove the heads, drain, soak the shrimp in white rice wine for about an hour, take out and drain really dry; step 2: prepare the spices — the main spices are galangal (riềng), chilli, salt and some others by region and taste, and process the spices; step 3: soak the shrimp with the spices — put the shrimp into a clean glass jar or bottle, pour the spice mixture in, mix well, cover tightly, keep the jar at room temperature and after 5–7 days it can be used), frozen fish fillet processing (the flow drawn: select and pre-process the materials — weigh the fish, wash and cut off the head to remove all the blood in the body; fillet — separate the flesh of the two body sides, removing head and viscera; wash and trim the pieces — wash in clean water and trim away fat, skin scraps, bones, …; grade — sort by the fillet pieces' weight, colour and sensory quality; freeze — blast-freeze the fillets until the product's centre temperature reaches ≤ −18 °C; package and carton — pack the product in sealed PE bags, label and carton to requirement; preserve in a cold store at ≤ −20 °C ± 2 °C), canned aquatic products (the flow drawn: select materials, wash and classify; mechanical processing — filleting, skinning, salting, vinegar-soaking, smoking, …; preliminary heat processing — blanching, steaming, frying, drying; fill the cans and add auxiliary materials (sauce, oil); exhaust air and seal the lids — expel the air and seal with a seaming machine, by steam spraying or vacuum; sterilise with steam at temperatures above 100 °C; incubate and preserve — store in a warehouse at room temperature for 14 days; label and carton to current regulations; preserve and use — storage warehouse at 35 °C maximum), surimi production from oceanic squid using biotechnology (the flow drawn: whole oceanic squid → pre-process (remove viscera, preliminary treatment) → wash 1 in salt-water solution → wash 2 in neutral solution → dewater → gel formation (grind finely with enzyme, sorbitol, salt, …; gel incubation under cold conditions) → surimi → freeze; in this process catalyst enzymes are used to form cross-links, strengthening the gel-forming ability during surimi production), and the preservation and processing practicals (preserving aquatic products by sun-drying/drying in four steps: wash the products (fish, shrimp, squid, …) and clean the gut; arrange them on a sieve, tray or drying line; sun-dry or machine-dry, the time depending on the product type or the user's preference; package and store in the refrigerator's cool or freezer compartment — and making fish paste (chả cá) in five steps: pre-process the materials — wash the fresh fish, remove head and viscera, take only the flesh and cut it into small pieces; freeze the flesh in the freezer compartment for about 2 hours; grind finely — grind the fish flesh until the mixture is fine and sticky, adding spices as suitable such as pepper, fish sauce, onion, garlic and dill; shape the paste into flat or round balls as preferred; fry for use or preserve in the freezer for gradual use) (original string `processing aquatic products`) | Vietnam, *Công nghệ 12 — Lâm nghiệp – Thuỷ sản*, printed p. 111 |
