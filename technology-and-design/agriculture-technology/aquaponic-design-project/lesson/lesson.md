# Aquaponic design project

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Malaysia's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — What aquaponics is
Aquaponics joins fish and plants in one system: the fish feed the plants, the plants clean the water, and the water returns. Malaysia teaches what the system is, how it is designed, its advantages, and its components by name.

## Sub lesson 2 — The design project
The system is then designed properly: an informative sketch of the aquaponic system, the sketch analysed, a model of the system built and evaluated, and a more efficient design proposed. Aquaponics passes through the same design cycle as every RBT product.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: aquaponics joins fish and plants in one water system — designed by sketch, model and evaluation, like any RBT product. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.

3. Keep your work: it is practice evidence, and the next lesson builds on it.
