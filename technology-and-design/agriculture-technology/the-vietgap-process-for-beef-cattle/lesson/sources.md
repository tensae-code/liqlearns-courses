| the VietGAP process for beef cattle: choose the site (away from main traffic roads, homes, industry, schools, processing plants, markets and hospitals); design the houses and equipment (fenced farm divided into zones — growing cattle, fattening cattle, feed processing, waste treatment, grazing; disinfection pits at the gates; house area of 4–5 m² per beef animal, with feed troughs, water troughs and a feed-mixing corridor; feed choppers and mixers and transport vehicles, cleaned after every use); prepare the breeding stock (clear origin with quarantine certificates and health records; buy from certified disease-safe farms; isolate new cattle at least 21 days watching for abnormal signs; place them into pens by age and sex, with full records); feed (concentrates and supplements of clear origin, quality-checked by batch, stored separately and dry — never mouldy; green forage grown clean without insecticide or weedkiller residues, washed before feeding; clean safe drinking water, with water quality tested periodically; feeding by stage, including the TMR — total mixed ration — method of feeding all feed types mixed in one ration); veterinary hygiene (clean houses, troughs daily; rest and disinfect the whole area at least 7 days after each batch out; the three principles — prevention better than treatment, correct diagnosis before treatment, veterinary medicines only as the manufacturer directs and under the vet's supervision; sick cattle isolated and treated by protocol, dead cattle never slaughtered but disposed of by regulation); manage waste (collect and treat promptly, using advanced processes such as biological bedding); keep records (feed and water logs, medicine, vaccine and herd-health logs, weight-gain and feed-consumption logs — stored as computer files and hard copies in a herd-management system with region codes, farm codes and individual numbers so origin can be traced and the farm can be certified); and internal inspection at least once a year (original string `the VietGAP process for beef cattle`) | Vietnam, *Công nghệ 11 — Chuyên đề học tập: Công nghệ chăn nuôi*, printed p. 54 |
