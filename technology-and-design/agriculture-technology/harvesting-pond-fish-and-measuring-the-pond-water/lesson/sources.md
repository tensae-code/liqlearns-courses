| harvesting pond fish and measuring the pond water: two harvest forms — partial harvest (netting out fish that have reached commercial size, lowering the density, small fish kept on) and total harvest (pumping out part of the water, netting two or three times at cool times of day, then draining and catching all); measuring water temperature with a thermometer — fish grow best at 25–28 °C — by immersing the thermometer's head, holding 5–10 minutes and reading the red mark; measuring water transparency with a Secchi disc (suitable 20–30 cm) — lower the disc until its black-white boundary disappears, raise until it reappears, and average the two depths; how to make a Secchi disc from a thin round metal plate 20–30 cm across painted black and white (original string `harvesting pond fish and measuring the pond water`) | Vietnam, *Công nghệ 7*, printed p. 76 |
