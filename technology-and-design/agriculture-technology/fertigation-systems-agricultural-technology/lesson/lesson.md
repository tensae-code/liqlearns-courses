# Fertigation systems (agricultural technology)

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Malaysia's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — What fertigation is
Fertigation feeds plants through their water: fertiliser is delivered dissolved in the irrigation, so feeding and watering become one system. Malaysia grows it with and without a rain-shelter structure.

## Sub lesson 2 — Fertigation against conventional planting
The book compares the two ways head-on — crop type, irrigation, water, capital, operating cost, disease risk and uniformity — so the new system must win on evidence, not fashion.

## Say it to your companion
