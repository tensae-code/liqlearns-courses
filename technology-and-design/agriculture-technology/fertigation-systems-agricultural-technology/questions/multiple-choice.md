# Fertigation systems (agricultural technology) — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is fertigation?
a) delivering fertiliser dissolved in the irrigation water b) Only a price c) Only decoration d) Nothing to do with this lesson
**Answer: a) delivering fertiliser dissolved in the irrigation water**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is a rain-shelter structure?
a) Only decoration b) a cover that shelters the crop while it grows c) Only a price d) A kind of glue

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is uniformity?

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson teach, in one line?
