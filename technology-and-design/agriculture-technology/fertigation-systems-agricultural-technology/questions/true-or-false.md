# Fertigation systems (agricultural technology) — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Fertigation is delivering fertiliser dissolved in the irrigation water.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A rain-shelter structure has nothing to do with this lesson.
**Answer: False** — It is part of the lesson: a cover that shelters the crop while it grows.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Uniformity is how even the crop grows across the whole planting.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.
