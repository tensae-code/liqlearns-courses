# Lesson plan — Fertigation systems (agricultural technology)

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Agriculture Technology · **Lesson:** Fertigation systems (agricultural technology)
**Place in the curriculum:** Agriculture Technology (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Malaysia expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** fertigation delivers fertiliser through the irrigation water — and Malaysia compares it with conventional planting on cost, water, disease risk and uniformity.
**This lesson's own level:** stocked from Malaysia's *Reka Bentuk dan Teknologi* (RBT — Design and Technology) textbooks — see lesson/sources.md. Malaysia counts in the acceptance count (student books, not evidence-only).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **What fertigation is** — Fertigation feeds plants through their water: fertiliser is delivered dissolved in the irrigation, so feeding and watering become one system.
2. **Fertigation against conventional planting** — The book compares the two ways head-on — crop type, irrigation, water, capital, operating cost, disease risk and uniformity — so the new system must win on evidence, not fashion.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of Agriculture Technology. Recommended, never forced.

2. Compare fertigation with conventional planting on the book’s seven points.
3. Explain how Malaysia's book teaches it.
