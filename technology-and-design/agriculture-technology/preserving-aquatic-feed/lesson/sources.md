| preserving aquatic feed: mixed (manufactured) feed (usually floating pellets for fish or sinking pellets for shrimp, with balanced nutrition and low water content, moisture from 10% to 12%, so it keeps for a long time, from 2 to 3 months; it must be carefully packaged, stored on racks 10–15 cm above the floor in a dry, cool, airy store), supplements (very low moisture, from 5% to 7%; carefully packaged and labelled by type and stored in a dry, airy place, some supplements keeping up to 2 years; some need careful storage away from direct light to limit oxidation, or to the maker's recommendation), fresh live feed (worms, trash fish, earthworms (giun quế), … — high water content so it cannot keep long; store in cold conditions such as a cold store, refrigerator or freezer to keep the feed fresh and reduce spoilage — at a cool 4–8 °C fresh feed keeps about 3–5 days, and in a freezer up to 1 month; some live feeds such as small fish, worms and algae can be kept alive in tanks or ponds to supply the farmed animals), and raw materials (by group: protein-supplying materials such as fish meal, meat meal and blood meal in powder form absorb moisture easily so do not keep long; high-protein materials mould easily during preservation, so wrap in nylon bags or dry well before storing; energy-supplying materials such as cereals and cassava keep longer as whole grains or pieces than as flour; additives of low moisture but high moisture-absorption must be stored in sealed bags to keep long) (original string `preserving aquatic feed`) | Vietnam, *Công nghệ 12 — Lâm nghiệp – Thuỷ sản*, printed p. 85 |
