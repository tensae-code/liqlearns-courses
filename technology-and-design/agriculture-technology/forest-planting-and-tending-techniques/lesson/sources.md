| forest planting and tending techniques: the planting seasons by region (North — spring or summer-autumn, February to July; Central — the rainy season, September to December; South — the rainy season, May to November, all in cool, humid weather), planting by direct seeding (prepare the sowing ground completely or in patches — in rows or in clusters; prepare seed of better quality than nursery seed and treat it before sowing to raise germination; broadcast sowing spreads seed evenly over the whole area, patch sowing sows in rows (a furrow along the row, seed along the row continuously or spaced) or in clusters (3–5 seeds per hole at fixed spacing)), planting with seedlings (dig the planting hole to a size fitting the species, usually 30 × 30 × 30 cm or 40 × 40 × 40 cm, and apply basal fertiliser — organic or inorganic — then cover with surface soil; prepare healthy seedlings of standard age, height and root-collar diameter; for bare-root seedlings make a hole in the pit, set the seedling, fill, firm the soil and mound the base; for container (soil-ball) seedlings slit and peel off the bag, set the ball in, fill and firm twice, taking care not to break the ball), and tending (weeding and hoeing carefully for three consecutive years after planting, best just before top-dressing, by complete or patch methods, in strips or around holes on slopes; top-dressing fertiliser; watering; pruning and thinning — prune with shears or a sharp knife the branches below one third of the canopy height, at the start of the dry season on dry days, and thin to keep the right stand density, removing the thinned seedlings for gap-filling or the nursery; and gap-filling (trồng dặm) — check 20–30 days after planting: at 85% survival or above, replant only where trees died in clusters; below 85%, replant wherever trees died, with the same technique as the main planting, and after one year continue with container seedlings of the previous year if survival is still under 85%) (original string `forest planting and tending techniques`) | Vietnam, *Công nghệ 12 — Lâm nghiệp – Thuỷ sản*, printed p. 26 |
