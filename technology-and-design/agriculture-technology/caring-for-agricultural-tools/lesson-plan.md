# Lesson plan — Caring for agricultural tools — maintenance, storage and dangers

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Agriculture Technology · **Lesson:** Caring for agricultural tools — maintenance, storage and dangers
**Place in the curriculum:** Agriculture Technology (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Rwanda expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** farm tools are kept in good condition, stored in a safe place after use, and handled knowing their dangers — sharp edges cut, and agrochemical mist harms — with precautions to match.
**This lesson's own level:** stocked from Rwanda's *Science and Elementary Technology* pupil's books (Rwanda Education Board) — see lesson/sources.md. Rwanda counts in the acceptance count (student books, not evidence-only).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **Maintenance and storage of agricultural tools** — The Primary 4 unit continues: maintenance keeps the tools in good condition, and after use the tools are stored in a safe place.
2. **Dangers of misusing agricultural tools, and precautions** — The book names the dangers plainly: cuts from sharp tools, and agrochemical mist among them.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
