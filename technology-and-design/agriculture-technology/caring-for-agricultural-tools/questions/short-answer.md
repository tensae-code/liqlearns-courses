# Caring for agricultural tools — maintenance, storage and dangers — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the two keeping habits for agricultural tools.
**Answer:** Maintenance — keeping them in good condition — and storage in a safe place after use.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the two dangers the book names.
**Answer:** Cuts from sharp tools, and agrochemical mist.
