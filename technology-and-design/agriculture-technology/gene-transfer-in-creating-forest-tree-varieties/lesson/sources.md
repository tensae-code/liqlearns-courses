| gene transfer in creating forest-tree varieties: gene-transfer technique moves a DNA segment from a donor cell to a recipient cell and is commonly applied to create forest-tree varieties superior in yield, quality and resistance to pests, disease and adverse conditions to ordinary varieties; the process of gene transfer into forest trees using a plasmid as the vector in five basic steps (step 1: collect the DNA carrying the gene to be transferred from the donor cell and collect plasmids from bacteria by a suitable method; step 2: cut and collect the gene to be transferred and join it into the plasmid with suitable enzymes to create recombinant DNA; step 3: transfer the recombinant DNA (carrying the gene) into the recipient cell by a suitable technique; step 4: select the cell line carrying the transferred gene on a selective medium; step 5: culture in a suitable medium so the cells carrying the transferred gene develop into complete plants) — drawn in detail for creating fast-growing transgenic acacia (the growth gene, the regulatory gene, the plasmid opened by the cutting enzyme, the joining enzyme, the recombinant DNA transferred into the recipient cell, selection on the selective medium, and the complete transgenic plant) (original string `gene transfer in creating forest-tree varieties`) | Vietnam, *Chuyên đề học tập Công nghệ 12 — Lâm nghiệp – Thuỷ sản*, printed p. 10 |
