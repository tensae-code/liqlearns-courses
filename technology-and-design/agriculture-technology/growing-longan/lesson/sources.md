| growing longan (nhãn): planting technique — rainy-season planting (Mekong Delta and Southeast June–July; Central Coast and Central Highlands June–September; the North in the spring crop February–April and autumn crop August–October); spacing 6 × 7 m between trees and rows, about 280 trees/ha; prepare the hole by soil type (alluvial/plain soil: 80 cm wide, 40–60 cm deep, 20–30 kg organic + 0.5–0.7 kg phosphorus + 0.5 kg lime per hole; infertile hill soil: 100 cm wide, 60–80 cm deep, 30–50 kg organic + 0.7–1.0 kg phosphorus + 0.5 kg lime), the basal fertiliser mixed with the excavated soil and refilled; plant as for citrus (small central hole, bag slit, fill 2–3 cm above the bag, firm, mound, stake, mulch, water); care — weeding/earthing 2–3 times a year, possibly intercropping legumes; top-dressing by annual tables (architecture period organic 10–30, business 30–50 kg/tree/year with N, P, K per the tables), in the business period split into four applications (after harvest: 100% organic + 30% N + 70% P + 30% K, to restore growth and push new flushes; at flowering: 30% N + 30% P + 20% K; at fruit set: 30% N + 30% K; one month after the third: 10% N + 20% K), the first application in a trench 20–30 cm wide and 15–20 cm deep around the canopy projection with the organic fertiliser spread first, then the inorganic, covered and watered, later applications dissolved for watering or broadcast by canopy projection (about 50 cm from the trunk) and watered in; watering — architecture period weekly 10–30 litres/tree; business period by stage (only when the soil is prolonged dry at flower-bud differentiation (Nov–Dec), 25–40 litres; at fruit development and ripening (late May to harvest) 25–40 litres; otherwise every 15 days 50–80 litres), water-saving techniques preferred; pruning — pinch at 0.8–1.0 m height to make level-1 branches, pinch level-1 branches at 0.5–0.7 m to make level-2, and after harvest remove spent fruit stalks, pest/diseased and shaded branches; controlling flowering — mechanical girdling (khoanh vỏ): in December, with a special tool ring a closed circle on level-1 or level-2 branches 0.5–1.5 m above the ground, the ring cut 0.3–0.5 cm wide, healthy vigorous trees possibly re-ringed after 15–20 days with a 0.1–0.2 cm cut; root pruning (chặn rễ) as the less-used alternative; chemical induction: water KClO₃ at the mature-flush stage to stimulate flower-bud differentiation, then water continuously for 5–7 days; increasing fruit set by supplementary macro- and micronutrient fertiliser (Bo, Mn, Mo, Cu, Zn…) and growth regulators (α-NAA, GA₃…) at flowering and fruit set (original string `growing longan (nhãn)`) | Vietnam, *Công nghệ 9 — Mô đun Trồng cây ăn quả*, printed p. 28 |
