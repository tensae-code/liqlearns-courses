| growing banana (chuối): planting technique — in the South from the start to the middle of the rainy season (May–August) so the plants grow in wet conditions; in the North the spring crop (February–April) and autumn crop (August–October); spacing — dwarf banana (chuối tiêu) 2 m between plants, 2–2.5 m between rows, about 2000–2500 plants/ha; tall banana (chuối tây) 2 m between plants, 2–2.8 m between rows, about 1800–2000 plants/ha; prepare the hole 40 × 40 × 40 cm with about 15 kg organic fertiliser and 380–410 g superphosphate per hole mixed with the excavated soil and refilled; plant in a small central hole, slit the bag, set the plant, fill and firm by hand to about 5 cm above the bag surface, with nylon sheeting allowed around the base against weeds and for moisture; care — weeding and earthing up around the base; top-dressing by crop year (year-1 bananas: no organic, N 0.5–0.6, P 0.4–0.5, K 0.8–0.9 kg/plant/year; year-2: organic 10–15, N 0.4–0.5, P 0.5–0.8, K 0.8–0.9), year 1 split into seven applications (the first one month after planting, the rest 1.5 months apart), year 2 into five (the first after the year-1 harvest, then monthly), applied 20–30 cm from the base of young plants and 30–60 cm for large plants — the first two applications in a shallow trench, later ones broadcast on the surface and watered in (on slopes a shallow trench must be dug uphill of the plant first); watering by stage (first month: every two days, 4–5 litres/plant; one month to flowering: every seven days, 5–10 litres; flowering, bunch formation and fruit development: 20–25 litres every three days; the 30 days before harvest: restrict watering), water-saving techniques preferred; other care — prune leaves (cut old, diseased leaves whose photosynthetic area is under 50%, early and regularly, gathering and removing diseased leaves from the garden to limit spread; keep old or mechanically damaged leaves as soil cover); prop against lodging (bananas fall easily: detect and earth up leaning plants early; when the plant bunches, use one or two stakes against the bunch's neck; use nylon rope, one end tied at the pseudostem neck, the other tied to a base in the opposite row against the bunch's direction, to keep the plant upright); controlling flowering and fruiting — for a high yield of good fruit the plant must reach the right size (planting 10–12 months); when the plant is about to flower, apply supplementary NPK (18-10-14) at 250–300 kg/ha; when the bunch has emerged, use cytokinin at a suitable dose to stimulate large fruit and even size and quality along the bunch (original string `growing banana (chuối)`) | Vietnam, *Công nghệ 9 — Mô đun Trồng cây ăn quả*, printed p. 54 |
