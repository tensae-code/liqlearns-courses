# Livestock production

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Cuba's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Animals in food production
Cuba's Grade 9 book closes its agriculture chapter with livestock production (producción pecuaria): raising animals and other species as part of producing food. The learner first places animal raising inside the whole production picture, beside the crops.

## Sub lesson 2 — Sustainable raising
The aim named by the book is sustainable food production: animals are kept so the system can continue — feeding, housing and care are planned, and what the animals give returns to the farm, as the manure that feeds the compost and the soil.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: raising animals is production too: keep animals and other species so that food production is sustainable — fed, housed and cared for as a system, not as an afterthought. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
