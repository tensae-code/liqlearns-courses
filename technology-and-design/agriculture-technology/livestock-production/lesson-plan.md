# Lesson plan — Livestock production

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Agriculture Technology · **Lesson:** Livestock production
**Place in the curriculum:** Agriculture Technology (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Cuba expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** raising animals is production too: keep animals and other species so that food production is sustainable — fed, housed and cared for as a system, not as an afterthought.
**This lesson's own level:** stocked from Cuba's *Educación Laboral — 9no grado* and the Cuba books in lesson/sources.md — see lesson/sources.md. Cuba counts in the acceptance count for its student-book placements; its programme and methodological-orientations placements are evidence only, counted nowhere.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **Animals in food production** — Cuba's Grade 9 book closes its agriculture chapter with livestock production (producción pecuaria): raising animals and other species as part of producing food. The learner first places animal raising inside the whole production picture, beside the crops.
2. **Sustainable raising** — The aim named by the book is sustainable food production: animals are kept so the system can continue — feeding, housing and care are planned, and what the animals give returns to the farm, as the manure that feeds the compost and the soil.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
