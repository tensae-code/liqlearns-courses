# Livestock production — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is livestock production (producción pecuaria)?
a) raising animals for food production b) Only a price c) Only decoration d) Nothing to do with this lesson
**Answer: a) raising animals for food production**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is sustainable?
a) Only history b) able to keep going without using up what it depends on c) Only storage d) A kind of glue
**Answer: b) able to keep going without using up what it depends on**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is species?
a) Only old tools b) Only painting c) a kind of living thing, such as chickens or rabbits d) Only a price
**Answer: c) a kind of living thing, such as chickens or rabbits**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson teach, in one line?
a) Only decoration b) Nothing to do with this lesson c) Only history d) raising animals is production too: keep animals and other species so that food production is sustainable — fed, housed and cared for as a system, not as an afterthought.
**Answer: d) raising animals is production too: keep animals and other species so that food production is sustainable — fed, housed and cared for as a system, not as an afterthought.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** How does Cuba's book teach this?
a) As theory only, never practised b) Only in a glossary c) Only as an exam d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Spanish
**Answer: d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Spanish**
