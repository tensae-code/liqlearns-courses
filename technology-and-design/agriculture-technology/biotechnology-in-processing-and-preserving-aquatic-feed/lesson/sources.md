| biotechnology in processing and preserving aquatic feed: making lysine-rich feed from pangasius by-products (about 60% of the fish's body cannot be used as human food — head, skin, fat, viscera and bones — and these by-products contain much protein of types suited to making lysine-rich feed; the process in five steps: treat the raw material — clean the by-products and remove impurities and high-lipid parts; dry and grind the material into powder, possibly mixing in other flours such as corn flour and rice bran; hydrolyse — mix the powder with water and enzyme, add clean water and incubate for a suitable time so the enzyme hydrolyses the protein in the material into lysine; pellet and dry the hydrolysed product into pellets of various sizes, drying in suitable conditions to keep the product quality; package and preserve or consume — check the product quality, package, store in a warehouse or take to consumption), fermenting soybean meal for aquatic feed (plant proteins such as soybean, peanut and cottonseed are much used to replace fishmeal protein to cut cost and reduce pressure on wild fish, but they contain anti-nutritional substances and toxins and are unbalanced in amino acids with low lysine and methionine; high-soybean diets can reduce disease resistance and stress tolerance, so soybean meal is fermented — fermentation raises the protein content over the original, removes the anti-nutritional substances and toxins, and makes it easier to absorb; the process in five steps: multiply the biomass of beneficial microorganisms in a nutrient medium under suitable conditions to obtain starter biomass; mix the soybean meal with the microorganism biomass and fermentation medium in suitable proportions to make the fermentation mixture; ferment the mixture under suitable conditions and time to create the product; evaluate the product's quality — bacterial density, enzyme activity, ability to inhibit pathogens; dry and package — dry the product at 40 °C until the moisture reaches 9–11%, package, preserve and use), and biotechnology in preservation (aquatic feed is usually high in protein and easily damaged by harmful microorganisms, so enzymes and microbial preparations able to inhibit mould and bacteria are added to extend the preservation time) (original string `biotechnology in processing and preserving aquatic feed`) | Vietnam, *Công nghệ 12 — Lâm nghiệp – Thuỷ sản*, printed p. 89 |
