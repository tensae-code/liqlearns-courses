| the VietGAP process for meat pigs in nine parts: choose the site (away from main roads, homes, industry, schools, processing plants, markets and hospitals, with clean water; separate zones — clean farming area, isolation area, feed processing and stores, waste treatment); design the houses and equipment (microclimate fitted to each age — minimum area 0.8–1 m² per pig for pigs over 3 months; feeders, drinkers, feed mixers and transport vehicles, hygienic and disinfected after every use); prepare the breeding stock ("clean" stock is the key input — clear origin, quarantine certificate, health file and vaccination certificate; new pigs isolated at least 21 days; stock pens by age group in full rows, applying all-in-all-out and the prescribed densities, with full records); feed (complete mixed feed or farm-mixed raw materials of clear origin, clean and safe, never mixed with banned chemicals or veterinary drugs; feed stores dry and raised 0.3–0.5 m off the ground against damp, with walls and mesh against rats and birds; drinking water clean and safe, its quality and the supply system checked periodically); veterinary hygiene (disinfect people and vehicles entering; protective clothing; clean houses, feeders and drinkers daily; spray disinfection around the farming area twice a week; after each batch out, rest the house at least 7 days for cleaning and disinfection; a disease-prevention plan with vaccination and deworming; sick pigs isolated and treated by regulation, and after recovery isolated at least 1 more month before sale); manage waste (collected and treated promptly; wastewater treated and quality-checked periodically); manage transport (separate lanes for bringing piglets in and pigs out; suitable vehicles, disinfected before and after transport); keep records (feed, water, medicines, vaccines, disease and herd-health logs, stored as computer files and hard copies, in a herd-management system with codes and ear tags for traceability and certification); and internal inspection at least once a year — with the practical of calculating the feed needed for a pig farm by stage (weaned pigs 7–30 kg, growing pigs 31–100 kg, in kg of feed per kg of weight gain) (original string `the VietGAP process for meat pigs in nine parts`) | Vietnam, *Công nghệ 11 — Chuyên đề học tập: Công nghệ chăn nuôi*, printed p. 58 |
