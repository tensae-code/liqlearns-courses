# Budikdamber — fish and vegetables in a bucket — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson teach?
a) Budikdamber — fish and vegetables in a bucket b) Cooking rice c) Map reading d) Letter writing
**Answer: a) Budikdamber — fish and vegetables in a bucket**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In the Indonesian book, where is this topic worked?
a) In a sports unit b) At Unit II Kegiatan 1 (exploring the Budikdamber method) c) Only in an exam d) It is not in the book
**Answer: b) At Unit II Kegiatan 1 (exploring the Budikdamber method)**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What comes first in the Prakarya sequence?
a) Selling the product b) Throwing materials away c) Observing, exploring and planning before making d) Marking the work
**Answer: c) Observing, exploring and planning before making**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Which detail belongs to this topic?
a) The detail named in the book: a bucket aquaculture system growing freshwater fish and vegetables together, and using its waste b) Parts of a bicycle pump only c) Parts of a television only d) No details are named
**Answer: a) The detail named in the book: a bucket aquaculture system growing freshwater fish and vegetables together, and using its waste**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** What does "local potential" (potensi lokal) mean in Prakarya?
a) Importing every material b) Building from the materials and traditions of the learner's own region c) Working only from photographs d) Skipping the planning stage
**Answer: b) Building from the materials and traditions of the learner's own region**
