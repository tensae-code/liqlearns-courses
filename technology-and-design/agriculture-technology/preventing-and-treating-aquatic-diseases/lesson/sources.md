| preventing and treating aquatic diseases — the general measures drawn from the four common diseases (for the bacterial diseases of tilapia and pangasius: comprehensive prevention — disinfect and sterilise the pond and the farming water source before and during farming, on hot sunny days give the fish a suitable feeding regime and add microbial preparations and vitamin C to raise resistance; treatment — disinfect the pond water combined with mixing bacteria-killing preparations (beta glucan, allicin, polyphenol, garlic extract, …) into the feed for 5–7 days, adding vitamin C and resistance boosters, and after treatment add microbial preparations to the feed and the environment to restore the beneficial microflora; the disinfectant dose is calculated — for example, to disinfect a pangasius pond of 1,000 m² area and 1.5 m depth, that is 2,000 m³ of water... the worked example uses BKC solution at 1 litre per 2,000 m³; for viral nervous necrosis of marine fish there is no effective treatment, so comprehensive prevention is the suitable measure — select healthy seed free of pathogens, ensure a suitable living environment and avoid shocking the fish during farming, treat fresh feed to kill pathogens before feeding, add vitamin C and resistance boosters, especially in the disease season, and use vaccine to prevent the disease in fish, as in the marine cage models (vaccination against VNN pictured); for white-spot disease of shrimp, caused by a virus with no specific cure, prevention is the main measure — comprehensive prevention (choose seed from reputable farms, healthy and disease-free with quarantine certificates; strictly control the farming water source — settle, filter, disinfect and treat the source water before putting it into the pond; block intermediate hosts from entering the pond; when using fresh feed ensure it is not infected; ensure hygiene and disinfect tools and vehicles entering and leaving the farming area to limit pathogen entry) and handling when disease occurs (absolutely do not discharge the diseased pond's water outside before disinfecting it; apply destruction measures to dead shrimp; do not move shrimp out of the farming area, to limit the spread of disease)) (original string `preventing and treating aquatic diseases`) | Vietnam, *Công nghệ 12 — Lâm nghiệp – Thuỷ sản*, printed p. 122 |
