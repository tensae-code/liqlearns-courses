| biotechnology in producing microbial preparations serving forestry — three production processes on one pattern: the preparation that rapidly decomposes the flammable material under the pine canopy (the litter under a pine canopy — dry branches, leaves and cones — is flammable material and a cause of forest fires; scientists produced a microbial preparation able to decompose (break down cellulose in) this material into organic matter, raising the material's moisture and the soil's nutrient and humus content, limiting forest fires, fighting erosion and leaching and improving the soil's physical and chemical properties; the production process in four steps: isolate and select microorganism strains able to degrade cellulose; multiply the biomass of the selected strains; mix the microorganism biomass with a suitable carrier to make the preparation; package, preserve and use), the MF1 preparation for pine and other forest trees (a combined microbial preparation including microorganisms that dissolve insoluble phosphate and microorganisms that inhibit or kill the fungus causing root rot of pine and some other forest trees; applied to pine and other trees in nurseries and plantations to raise survival rate and yield and reduce disease; production in the same four steps), and the MF2 preparation for eucalyptus, acacia and other trees (a combined preparation including phosphate-dissolving microorganisms, microorganisms inhibiting or killing the fungi causing leaf spot and tip dieback of eucalyptus, and some carriers and micronutrients; applied to eucalyptus, acacia and other trees in nurseries and plantations to raise survival and yield and reduce disease; production in the same four steps) — the phosphate-dissolving microorganism strains commonly belong to the genera Bacillus, Pseudomonas, Mycorhiza, Candida, Micrococcus and Flavobacterium, and the strains antagonistic to the pine root-rot fungus commonly belong to Bacillus, Pseudomonas, Streptomyces, Burkholderia, Trichoderma, Chaetomium, Penicillium and Aspergillus (original string `biotechnology in producing microbial preparations serving forestry`) | Vietnam, *Chuyên đề học tập Công nghệ 12 — Lâm nghiệp – Thuỷ sản*, printed p. 13 |
