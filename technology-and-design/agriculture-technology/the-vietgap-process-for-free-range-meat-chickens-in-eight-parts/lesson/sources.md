| the VietGAP process for free-range meat chickens in eight parts: choose the site (away from main roads and crowded places, fenced, with room for houses and service areas); design the houses and equipment (naturally ventilated houses keeping the microclimate, the service area separated from the farming area, and the ranging garden — flat or on a hillside, fenced so chickens cannot fly or stray out, with shade trees and full equipment: feeders, drinkers, brooders, veterinary stores); prepare the breeding stock (full origin documents and veterinary quarantine papers; breed-standard appearance and health; if the house already holds chickens, isolate the new stock separately for at least 2 weeks); feed (hygienic mixed feed, unspoiled and in date, from reputable producers; drinking water clean, free of E. coli and Coliform bacteria and toxic heavy metals, stored in covered tanks and piped to the houses); veterinary hygiene (protective clothing for everyone entering, a disinfection pit at the gate, scheduled spraying around the farming area, disinfection of houses and equipment before, during and after each batch, full vaccination — Marek, Newcastle, Gumboro, infectious bronchitis, avian influenza — to schedule); manage waste (a separate manure store at the end of the farming area, other wastes collected to the prescribed place for destruction, house-washing water drained to a holding tank, never straight into the environment); keep records (four notebook types — daily farming diary, vaccination and treatment log, feed and supplies in-out log, product sales log); and internal inspection at least once a year — with the practical of calculating the feed needed for a chicken farm by growth stage (chick feed for days 0–14, grower feed for days 15–75, finisher feed for days 76–120) (original string `the VietGAP process for free-range meat chickens in eight parts`) | Vietnam, *Công nghệ 11 — Chuyên đề học tập: Công nghệ chăn nuôi*, printed p. 51 |
