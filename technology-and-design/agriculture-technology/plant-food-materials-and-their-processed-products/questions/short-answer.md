# Plant food materials and their processed products — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** State in one sentence what this lesson teaches.
**Answer:** Plant food materials (fruit, vegetables, cereals, seeds/grains, tubers) and their processed products, incl. traditional products (dodol, jenang, wajit, bika ambon).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the stages the Indonesian books work through for this topic.
**Answer:** Observe and explore, then plan or design (merancang), then produce, then present the finished work and reflect on it.
