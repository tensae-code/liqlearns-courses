# Parts of a commercial fertigation system

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Malaysia's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — The parts of the system
A commercial fertigation system is named part by part: nutrient tank, water pump, dripper set with micro-tubes and nipples, filter, timer, and the field pipes — main, secondary and tertiary HDPE poly pipes with their connectors: sockets, 90° elbows, tees and valves.

## Sub lesson 2 — Source, medium, outlet
Under the parts sit three basic components every system must have: a source, a medium that carries, and an outlet that delivers. Any fertigation design can be checked against the three.
