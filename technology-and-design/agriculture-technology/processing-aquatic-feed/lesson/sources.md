| processing aquatic feed: manual processing (at small scale or household level — the feed is processed by cutting, slicing, grinding, … suited to the mouth size of the farmed species and raising its ability to catch prey; trash fish ground into a paste to feed newly feeding softshell-turtle seed; manually processed feed usually has a short preservation time), industrial processing in five steps (step 1: select suitable raw materials; step 2: pre-process the materials by sun-drying or drying, chopping, crushing, grinding, …; step 3: mix the materials and add minerals and additives in suitable proportions; step 4: mix the feed evenly with a binder and transfer it to the pelletiser; step 5: dry, package and preserve — industrial processing is usually at large scale and the feed keeps longer), and the small-scale practicals (processing and preserving feed for grass carp at the seed stage: cut the grass (elephant grass, Guinea grass (Ghine Mombasa), Paspalum, Ruzi, …) into 5–10 cm segments with a suitable cutting tool, put the cut grass into a nylon bag and tie the mouth tightly, store the bag in a cool airy place and feed the fish gradually, then clean up the tools; and processing minced trash fish as feed for eels or softshell turtles: wash the trash fish (500 g), cut it small with knife or scissors, grind it finely in a blender, put the ground fish into a plastic box, cover and store in the refrigerator's cool compartment for the turtle or eel to eat gradually, then clean up) (original string `processing aquatic feed`) | Vietnam, *Công nghệ 12 — Lâm nghiệp – Thuỷ sản*, printed p. 86 |
