# Linear measurement in surveying — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is this lesson's topic?
a) Linear measurement in surveying b) An unrelated workshop rule c) A different subject's chapter d) None of these
**Answer: a) Linear measurement in surveying**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The heart of the topic is…
a) Units, instruments and methods for measuring length, basic equipment for straight-line distance, and the errors that occur in ordinary chain measurement b) Skipping the steps c) Guessing the parts d) Copying without checking
**Answer: a) Units, instruments and methods for measuring length, basic equipment for straight-line distance, and the errors that occur in ordinary chain measurement**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** When learning a new part of this topic, you should learn…
a) Only its colour b) Its name, what it does, and how it connects to the parts around it c) Nothing — parts do not matter d) Only the last step
**Answer: b) Its name, what it does, and how it connects to the parts around it**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** If the topic is a process, the order of the steps…
a) Does not matter b) Is part of the teaching — doing steps out of order changes the result c) Should be reversed d) Is decided by guessing
**Answer: b) Is part of the teaching — doing steps out of order changes the result**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Checking your work is…
a) Optional and never needed b) The last step of the work itself c) Done only by the teacher d) A different lesson
**Answer: b) The last step of the work itself**
