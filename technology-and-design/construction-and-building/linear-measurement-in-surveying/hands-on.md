# Hands-on — Linear measurement in surveying

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## Work the topic
Carry out the topic's core task on a fresh example: units, instruments and methods for measuring length, basic equipment for straight-line distance, and the errors that occur in ordinary chain measurement. Work in order, naming each part or step as you go.

## Check it
Check your finished work against the lesson's own rules. Find one thing that is wrong or weak, fix it, and say what you changed and why.
