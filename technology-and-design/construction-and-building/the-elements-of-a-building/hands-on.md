# Hands-on — The elements of a building

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: foundations, plinth, walls, floors, roof, stairs, windows and doors — the 17 parts of a house section, and what each does. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
