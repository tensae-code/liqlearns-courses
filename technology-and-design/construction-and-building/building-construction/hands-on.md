# Hands-on — Building construction

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## Trace the path
At a real deck or timber floor, list the members in order from the surface you stand on to the ground. Sketch the path with arrows.

## Name the members
From the lesson's cutaway description, draw and label: bearers, joists, ledger, decking, fascia, battens, plates. Under each, write what it carries.
