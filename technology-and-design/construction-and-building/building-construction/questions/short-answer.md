# Building construction — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Trace the floor's load path from decking to ground.
**Answer:** Answer: Decking → joists → bearers → posts → post footings → ground.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain foundation vs footings in one sentence.
**Answer:** Answer: The footings are the individual load-spreading parts in the ground; the foundation is the whole supporting system they form together.
