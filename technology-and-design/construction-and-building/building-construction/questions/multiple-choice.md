# Building construction — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A footing is…
a) The part that spreads a load into the ground b) The whole foundation system c) A wall plate d) A roof member
**Answer: a) The part that spreads a load into the ground**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The foundation is…
a) The whole supporting system the footings belong to b) One post's base only c) The decking d) The fascia
**Answer: a) The whole supporting system the footings belong to**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Joists rest on…
a) Bearers b) Decking c) Battens d) Fascia
**Answer: a) Bearers**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A ledger's job is to…
a) Fix a new structure to an existing one b) Carry the roof slope c) Finish the roof edge d) Carry roofing sheets
**Answer: a) Fix a new structure to an existing one**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Joist hangers and Trip-L-Grips exist because…
a) Formed metal connectors grip joints against lifting and slipping better than nails alone b) Nails are banned c) They decorate the frame d) Timber cannot be nailed
**Answer: a) Formed metal connectors grip joints against lifting and slipping better than nails alone**
