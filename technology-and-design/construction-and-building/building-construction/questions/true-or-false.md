# Building construction — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A member's name tells its place in the load path.
**Answer: True** — Bearers bear joists; joists carry the deck — the names are jobs.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Roof pitch is best guessed while standing on the roof.
**Answer: False** — Pitch is measured and set at the drawing stage.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A frame out of plumb makes later sheeting and doors fight.
**Answer: True** — Every later part inherits the frame's error.
