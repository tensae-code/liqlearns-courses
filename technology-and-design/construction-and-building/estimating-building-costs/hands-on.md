# Hands-on — Estimating building costs and materials

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: Use of Price Catalogues; Materials List and Quotations; Estimating Costs; also: Building Costs Estimation; Methods of Estimating Building Costs; Method of Preparing Material List and Quotation; The Role of Building Estimator; also: Building Applied Maths; Methods of Calculating Substructure Members; Methods of Calculation Superstructure. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
