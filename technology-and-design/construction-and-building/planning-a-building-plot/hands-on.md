# Hands-on — Planning a building plot

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: the norms (playground 0.7 m² per child, rooms 5 m apart), drawing the site plan of a 0.7 ha plot at 1:5000 from measurements (2800×2500 cm), and using Google Earth. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
