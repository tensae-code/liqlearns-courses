# Planning a building plot — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Say in one sentence what this lesson teaches.
**Answer:** Planning a building plot — the norms (playground 0.7 m² per child, rooms 5 m apart), drawing the site plan of a 0.7 ha plot at 1:5000 from measurements (2800×2500 cm), and using Google Earth.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name one material, tool or step this lesson works with.
**Answer:** Any one named in the lesson text — for example, the first step of sub lesson 1: Planning a building plot.
