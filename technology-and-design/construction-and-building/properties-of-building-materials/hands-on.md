# Hands-on — Properties of building materials

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: density, specific weight, absorption/permeability/water permeability, frost resistance, expansion–contraction, thermal conductivity and resistance, fire resistance (physical properties and the rest of the property set). Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
