# Properties of building materials — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Say in one sentence what this lesson teaches.
**Answer:** Density, specific weight, absorption/permeability/water permeability, frost resistance, expansion–contraction, thermal conductivity and resistance, fire resistance (physical properties and the rest of the property set).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name one material, tool or step this lesson works with.
**Answer:** Any one named in the lesson text — for example, the first step of sub lesson 1: Properties of building materials.
