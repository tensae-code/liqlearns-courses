# Hands-on — Installation of plumbing fittings and accessories

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: Method of Analysing Plumbing Fitting and Accessories to Specific Task; Methods of Installation and Connection of Water Distribution System; of Drain, Waste, Vent System to Existing Plumbing System. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
