# Hands-on — Plumbing tools, equipment and materials

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: Plumbing tools and equipment; Manual tools; Power tools and uses; Types of plumbing materials and Specifications; also: Types of manual plumbing tools; Types of power plumbing tools; Categories of plumbing materials. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
