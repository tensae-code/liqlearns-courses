# The civil-engineering field — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is civil engineering?
a) the field that builds structures and transport for society b) Only a price c) Only decoration d) Nothing to do with this lesson
**Answer: a) the field that builds structures and transport for society**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is kiln?
a) Only history b) the oven in which bricks are fired hard c) Only storage d) A kind of glue
**Answer: b) the oven in which bricks are fired hard**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is brick?
a) Only old tools b) Only painting c) a clay block fired in the kiln into a durable building stone d) Only a price
**Answer: c) a clay block fired in the kiln into a durable building stone**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson teach, in one line?
a) Only decoration b) Nothing to do with this lesson c) Only history d) civil engineering builds the structures and transport a society stands on — and the field is entered through its first material: the brick, and how it is fired in the kiln.
**Answer: d) civil engineering builds the structures and transport a society stands on — and the field is entered through its first material: the brick, and how it is fired in the kiln.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** How does Iran's book teach this?
a) As theory only, never practised b) Only in a glossary c) Only as an exam d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Persian
**Answer: d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Persian**
