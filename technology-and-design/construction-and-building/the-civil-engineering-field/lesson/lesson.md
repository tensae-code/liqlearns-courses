# The civil-engineering field

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Iran's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Structures and transport
The civil-engineering field covers what is built for everyone: structures — buildings, bridges, dams — and transport — roads and the works that carry movement. Iran's Grade 9 Civil Design and Build module opens the field as a field: its two great branches named before any single skill, so the learner sees where the module's bricklaying belongs in the world's work.

## Sub lesson 2 — What a brick is, and its firing
The brick is the field's first material: clay formed into a block and fired in the kiln until it hardens into a building stone that weather cannot soften. The firing transforms the material — soft clay in, durable brick out — and the module teaches the brick's making before its laying, so the wall lessons that follow rest on a known material.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: civil engineering builds the structures and transport a society stands on — and the field is entered through its first material: the brick, and how it is fired in the kiln. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
