# Bricklaying — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In your own words, what is trowel?
**Answer:** The bricklayer's flat tool for spreading mortar and bedding bricks.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In your own words, what is stretcher bond?
**Answer:** The bond where every brick shows its long face, courses offset by half a brick.
