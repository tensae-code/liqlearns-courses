# Bricklaying — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Trowel is the bricklayer's flat tool for spreading mortar and bedding bricks.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Spirit level has nothing to do with this lesson.
**Answer: False** — It is part of the lesson: the tool that proves a course horizontal and a face vertical.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Stretcher bond is the bond where every brick shows its long face, courses offset by half a brick.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.
