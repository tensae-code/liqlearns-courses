# Bricklaying

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Iran's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Tools of the trade
Three tools govern laying. The trowel spreads mortar and beds each brick. The spirit level proves every course horizontal and every face vertical. The straightedge checks the wall's flatness across many bricks at once. Iran's Grade 9 module names the tools with the job — bricklaying is taught as governed work, not piling.

## Sub lesson 2 — The half-brick wall in stretcher bond
In stretcher bond every brick shows its long face, and each course is offset half a brick so the vertical joints never line up — the offset is the wall's strength. The module builds a half-brick wall in stretcher bond following a work flowchart: the steps written first, the wall built to them, course by checked course.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: bricklaying builds a true wall course by course: trowel, spirit level and straightedge govern the work, and the half-brick wall rises in stretcher bond following a work flowchart. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
