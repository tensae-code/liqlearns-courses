# Lesson plan — Bricklaying

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Construction and Building · **Lesson:** Bricklaying
**Place in the curriculum:** Construction and Building (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Iran expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** bricklaying builds a true wall course by course: trowel, spirit level and straightedge govern the work, and the half-brick wall rises in stretcher bond following a work flowchart.
**This lesson's own level:** stocked from Iran's *Work & Technology, Grade 9* (student book) — see lesson/sources.md. Iran counts in the acceptance count (student books, not evidence-only).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **Tools of the trade** — Three tools govern laying.
2. **The half-brick wall in stretcher bond** — In stretcher bond every brick shows its long face, and each course is offset half a brick so the vertical joints never line up — the offset is the wall's strength.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
