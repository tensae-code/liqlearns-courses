# Hands-on — Modern position-fixing

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## Work the topic
Carry out the topic's core task on a fresh example: astronomical observations, GPS (Global Positioning Systems) and national coordinate grids (Sri Lanka's grid and trigonometrical stations). Work in order, naming each part or step as you go.

## Check it
Check your finished work against the lesson's own rules. Find one thing that is wrong or weak, fix it, and say what you changed and why.
