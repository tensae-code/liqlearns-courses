# Hands-on — OHS on-site in construction

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: Types of OHS On-Site; Hazard Communication On-Site; OHS on Setting Scaffolding; Personal Protective Equipment Use On-Site; Material Handling Safety. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
