# Hands-on — Plumbing fundamentals

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: Introduction to plumbing; Plumbing Fundamentals; Career pathways in Plumbing Technology; also: Fundamental Concept of Plumbing System; Plumbing System; Types of Plumbing System; Water Distribution System; Drain, Waste, Vent System. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
