# Plumbing fundamentals

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Papua New Guinea's guide teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Plumbing fundamentals
Papua New Guinea's guides teach it this way (the teacher guide — evidence, printed p. 141-146): Introduction to plumbing; Plumbing Fundamentals; Career pathways in Plumbing Technology; also: Fundamental Concept of Plumbing System; Plumbing System; Types of Plumbing System; Water Distribution System; Drain, Waste, Vent System. The learner works through the named materials and steps in order, saying what each step does before doing the next one.

## Sub lesson 2 — Plumbing fundamentals — doing it yourself
Now the learner does the whole task unaided: Introduction to plumbing; Plumbing Fundamentals; Career pathways in Plumbing Technology; also: Fundamental Concept of Plumbing System; Plumbing System; Types of Plumbing System; Water Distribution System; Drain, Waste, Vent System. Working from the first sub lesson's sequence, the learner plans the make, works through it step by step, and checks the finished piece against what the PNG guide shows.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: Plumbing fundamentals — Introduction to plumbing; Plumbing Fundamentals; Career pathways in Plumbing Technology; also: Fundamental Concept of Plumbing System; Plumbing System; Types of Plumbing System; Water Distribution System; Drain, Waste, Vent System. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.

## Do It Yourself
1. Do the Do it first task again, carefully this time, using everything the lesson taught.
2. Check your work against the lesson's own checks — fix one thing you find.
3. Keep your work: it is practice evidence, and the next lesson builds on it.
