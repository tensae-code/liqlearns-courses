# Hands-on — Processes and procedures in construction

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: Analysing Building Processes and Procedures; Method of Designing Gable Roof Using AutoCAD Program; Estimating the Cost of Material; Building Setting Out and Construction Process; Furniture Installation and Furnishing the Building. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
