# Building walls in other bonds — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Header bond is the bond where every brick shows its end face, tying across the wall.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Header-and-stretcher bond has nothing to do with this lesson.
**Answer: False** — It is part of the lesson: the bond alternating header and stretcher courses.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Bond is the pattern in which bricks are laid so the wall holds together.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.
