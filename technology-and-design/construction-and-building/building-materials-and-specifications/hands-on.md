# Hands-on — Building materials and specifications

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: Types of Building Materials; Timber Building and Specifications; Methods of Processing Timber; Types of Timber Used; Brick wall Building and Specifications; Types of Bricks Used; Concrete Building and Specification; Methods of Mixing Concrete; Steel Building Structure and Specification. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
