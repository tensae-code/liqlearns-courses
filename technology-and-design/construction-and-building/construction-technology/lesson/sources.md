Haiti programme note: Haiti's ETAP programmes for the 3e cycle (MENFP, Direction Enseignement Fondamental, version définitive 28 juillet 2024) are curriculum/framework documents, recorded RECORD-ONLY in the placements file and not stocked; the teaching stocked here comes from the Projet Nectar pedagogical sequences that deliver the programme. The LibreOffice Impress (PAO) Nectar sequence in the same placements file is likewise RECORD-ONLY — pure ICT content, stocked in the ICT course, not here.

Haiti's own way: Haiti teaches construction as organised team fabrication: the greenhouse site is prepared with assigned responsibilities and tooling, and the structure is then built following the project's own cahier des charges.

Samoa's own way: Samoa teaches construction technology as two complete worked builds — a timber deck built in sequence (posts, bearers, joists, decking, finish) and a shelter framed in full (notched and bolted posts, temporary bracing and levelling, ledger fixing for the attached shelter, rafters marked and cut, collar ties and connectors, gable roof) — the whole construction, not a stage of it.
