# Lesson plan — Construction technology

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Construction and Building · **Lesson:** Construction technology
**Place in the curriculum:** Construction and Building (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Haiti expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** construction technology turns a specification into a standing structure: the site is prepared with assigned team responsibilities and the right tooling, then the structure is built following the cahier des charges.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on teacher-guide evidence only — Haiti's ITAP Projet Nectar pedagogical sequences (MENFP) are the books in hand for this topic (see lesson/sources.md). Teacher guides are evidence, counted nowhere in the acceptance count.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **Preparing the site** — The build starts before the first part is raised.
2. **Team responsibilities and tooling** — Each team member holds an assigned part of the build.
3. **Building to the specification** — The greenhouse rises following its cahier des charges.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of Construction and Building. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say what site preparation includes and why it comes first.
2. Assign team responsibilities and tooling for a build.
3. Build and check a structure against its specification.
## What you need
A build site or model site, the project's cahier des charges, tools for the build, and a team responsibility chart.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — the opening doing task in lesson/lesson.md.
2. **The explanation** — the sub lessons in order (lesson/lesson.md).
   - ADD IMAGE: a picture of the greenhouse site prepared and marked out, the team at their assigned stations, the cahier des charges posted at the site edge — the student matches each worker to the responsibility chart.
   - ADD GIF: a GIF of a bare site becoming a building site — marked, tooled, staffed — then the greenhouse frame rising stage by stage — the student names each preparation step before the first frame appears.
   - ADD AUDIO: a voice reading the build order — prepare the site, assign responsibilities and tooling, build to the specification aloud — the student repeats them.
   - ADD VIDEO: a short video of the lesson demonstration — see video-script.md.
3. **Say it to your companion** — the learner explains the lesson's key rule aloud.
4. **Questions** — the questions/ files, spread over the sub lessons.
5. **Hands-on** — hands-on.md.
6. **Do It Yourself** — the closing doing block in lesson/lesson.md.
