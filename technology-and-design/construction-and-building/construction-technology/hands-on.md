# Hands-on — Construction technology

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## Responsibility chart
For a class build, write the responsibility chart: tasks, owners, tools. After the build, mark any task that was done twice or left undone and trace it to the chart.

## Specification check
During a build, check three stages against the cahier des charges and record each result. For any stage that fails, write what must be redone and why the check caught it.
