# Construction technology — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Site preparation comes…
a) After fabrication b) Before fabrication begins c) Only if time remains d) Never — sites prepare themselves
**Answer: b) Before fabrication begins**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A build on an unprepared site…
a) Is faster b) Inherits the problems preparation would have removed c) Is always stronger d) Needs no specification
**Answer: b) Inherits the problems preparation would have removed**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Team responsibilities in the build are…
a) Decided afterwards b) Assigned before the work starts c) Held by one person d) Unimportant
**Answer: b) Assigned before the work starts**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Tooling is assigned…
a) Randomly b) Together with the tasks c) After the build d) Only to the teacher
**Answer: b) Together with the tasks**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** During fabrication, the structure is checked against…
a) The weather b) Its cahier des charges c) Other teams' buildings d) Nothing
**Answer: b) Its cahier des charges**
