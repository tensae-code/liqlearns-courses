# Construction technology — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Site preparation is a delay before the real construction work.
**Answer: False** — Preparation is construction work — the build's quality is set before the first part is raised.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Assigned responsibilities prevent work being done twice or left undone.
**Answer: True** — Naming who prepares, measures, assembles and checks before the start is what organises the build.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Building to specification is the same as improvising carefully.
**Answer: False** — Fabrication follows the cahier des charges and each stage is compared with it — that is what separates construction from improvisation.
