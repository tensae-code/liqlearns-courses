# Construction technology — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why does the sequence give site preparation its own stage?
**Answer:** Because a structure built on an unprepared site inherits every problem preparation would have removed; the build's quality is set before fabrication begins.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the four responsibilities the lesson assigns as an example.
**Answer:** Preparing, measuring, assembling and checking — each named and owned before the work starts.
