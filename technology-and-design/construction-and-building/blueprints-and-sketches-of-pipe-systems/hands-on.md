# Hands-on — Blueprints and sketches of pipe systems

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: Blue Print and Pipe System; Method of Manual Sketching Blueprint and Pipe System; Methods of Sketching Blueprint and Pipe System using CAD Program; Methods of Downloading CAD Program. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
