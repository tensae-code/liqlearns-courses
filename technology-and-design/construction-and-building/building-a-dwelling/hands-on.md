# Hands-on — Building a dwelling

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: ancient dwellings; the kiiz ui (yurt) as the people's traditional dwelling; an ancient dwelling (tipi) constructed from sticks and fabric, with the fabric pattern/net diagrams shown. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
