# Hands-on — Plumbing tubing, fixtures and faucets

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: Types of Corrugated Stainless Steel Tubing; Types of Faucets and Fittings; Methods of Stainless Steel Tubing; of Faucets and Fitting. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
