# Hands-on — Hazard communication in the plumbing trade

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: Hazard Communication; Types of Hazard Communication; Common Hazards in Workplace; Methods of Minimising Hazard in Workplace. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
