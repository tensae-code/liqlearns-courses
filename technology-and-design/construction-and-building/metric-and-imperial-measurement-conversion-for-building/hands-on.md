# Hands-on — Metric and imperial measurement conversion for building

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## Convert and check back
Convert 450 mm, 1200 mm and 2400 mm to inches (divide by 25.4). Then convert each answer back. Any that does not return to its start hides an arithmetic slip — find it.

## Dual tape audit
Measure three objects with a tape (or from given metric sizes) and record each in both systems side by side, marking which figure you would order materials by.
