# Metric and imperial measurement conversion for building — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Converting back is a valid check on a conversion.
**Answer: True** — Returning to the starting figure proves the arithmetic.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** About 300 mm to the foot is exact enough for a final cut.
**Answer: False** — It is a rule of thumb for estimates; cuts use exact conversion.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A 25 mm conversion error can stop a door fitting.
**Answer: True** — Frames and doors work to millimetres.
