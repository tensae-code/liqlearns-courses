# Metric and imperial measurement conversion for building — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** One inch is exactly…
a) 25.4 mm b) 25 mm c) 10 mm d) 30 mm
**Answer: a) 25.4 mm**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Both systems meet on site because…
a) Drawings may be metric while materials and tools are imperial b) Imperial is banned c) Metric tapes do not exist d) Builders prefer confusion
**Answer: a) Drawings may be metric while materials and tools are imperial**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** To convert millimetres to inches you…
a) Divide by 25.4 b) Multiply by 25.4 c) Add 25.4 d) Divide by 10
**Answer: a) Divide by 25.4**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A conversion table's purpose is to…
a) Kill arithmetic slips by reading across b) Replace measuring c) Decorate the book d) Convert colours
**Answer: a) Kill arithmetic slips by reading across**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** A rule of thumb is fit for…
a) Quick estimates, not final cuts b) Ordering doors exactly c) Setting out a house d) Replacing the table always
**Answer: a) Quick estimates, not final cuts**
