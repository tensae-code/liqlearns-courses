# Metric and imperial measurement conversion for building — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Convert 600 mm to inches, showing the working.
**Answer:** Answer: 600 ÷ 25.4 ≈ 23.6 inches.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Why do builders keep both the table and the rules of thumb?
**Answer:** Answer: Rules of thumb estimate fast in conversation and planning; the table (or exact factor) gives the verified figure a cut or order needs.
