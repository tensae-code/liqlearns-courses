# Hands-on — Building materials in general

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: their historical development in constructions, specifications and standards, quality/quality control/quality assurance, regulations, how to choose building materials, and how their production has evolved. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
