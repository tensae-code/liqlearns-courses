# Building materials in general — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson teach?
a) Their historical development in constructions, specifications and standards, quality/quality control/quality assurance, regulations, how to choose building materials, and how their production has evolved b) Only how to draw pictures c) Nothing practical d) A song to memorise
**Answer: a) Their historical development in constructions, specifications and standards, quality/quality control/quality assurance, regulations, how to choose building materials, and how their production has evolved**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Sub lesson 1 is about…
a) Building materials in general b) Cooking lunch c) A sports match d) The weather
**Answer: a) Building materials in general**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Sub lesson 2 asks the learner to…
a) Complete the making in full and check it b) Stop before starting c) Copy a partner's work d) Throw the materials away
**Answer: a) Complete the making in full and check it**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Greece teaches this topic inside which school book?
a) Building Materials I b) Only mathematics c) Only history d) It is not taught at school
**Answer: a) Building Materials I**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** What should the learner do first in this lesson?
a) Try the Do it first task in their own way b) Skip to the last page c) Wait to be given the answer d) Nothing at all
**Answer: a) Try the Do it first task in their own way**
