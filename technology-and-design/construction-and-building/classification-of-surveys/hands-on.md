# Hands-on — Classification of surveys

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## Work the topic
Carry out the topic's core task on a fresh example: by nature of the field (land surveys — topographic, cadastral, city; hydrographic surveys with graduated pole/rope and echo sounder), by instrument, by purpose, and the basic plane/geodetic division. Work in order, naming each part or step as you go.

## Check it
Check your finished work against the lesson's own rules. Find one thing that is wrong or weak, fix it, and say what you changed and why.
