# Video script — Classification of surveys

A short demonstration video, made later, in a super-simple voice. The demonstrator works through the topic while the voice speaks.

---

This lesson is about classification of surveys.
By nature of the field (land surveys — topographic, cadastral, city; hydrographic surveys with graduated pole/rope and echo sounder), by instrument, by purpose, and the basic plane/geodetic division.
Work step by step. Check your work as you go.
