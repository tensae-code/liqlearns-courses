# Factors that act on building materials — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Say in one sentence what this lesson teaches.
**Answer:** Physical (heat), chemical (air, water–soil, direct contact with chemicals), mechanical forces (wind, sound, radioactivity, external and internal forces), and organic factors.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name one material, tool or step this lesson works with.
**Answer:** Any one named in the lesson text — for example, the first step of sub lesson 1: Factors that act on building materials.
