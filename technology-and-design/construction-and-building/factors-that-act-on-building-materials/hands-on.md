# Hands-on — Factors that act on building materials

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: physical (heat), chemical (air, water–soil, direct contact with chemicals), mechanical forces (wind, sound, radioactivity, external and internal forces), and organic factors. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
