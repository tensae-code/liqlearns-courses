# Plaster work — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is mould?
a) the hollow form that gives a cast piece its shape b) Only a price c) Only decoration d) Nothing to do with this lesson
**Answer: a) the hollow form that gives a cast piece its shape**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is casting?
a) Only history b) pouring liquid material into a mould and letting it set c) Only storage d) A kind of glue
**Answer: b) pouring liquid material into a mould and letting it set**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is carbon paper?
a) Only old tools b) Only painting c) the coated paper that transfers a traced design onto a surface d) Only a price
**Answer: c) the coated paper that transfers a traced design onto a surface**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson teach, in one line?
a) Only decoration b) Nothing to do with this lesson c) Only history d) plaster work makes a cast piece: prepare the mould, cast the plaster, then transfer a design onto the set piece with carbon paper and carve it.
**Answer: d) plaster work makes a cast piece: prepare the mould, cast the plaster, then transfer a design onto the set piece with carbon paper and carve it.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** How does Iran's book teach this?
a) As theory only, never practised b) Only in a glossary c) Only as an exam d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Persian
**Answer: d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Persian**
