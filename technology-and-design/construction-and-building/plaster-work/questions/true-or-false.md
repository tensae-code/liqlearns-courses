# Plaster work — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Mould is the hollow form that gives a cast piece its shape.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Casting has nothing to do with this lesson.
**Answer: False** — It is part of the lesson: pouring liquid material into a mould and letting it set.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Carbon paper is the coated paper that transfers a traced design onto a surface.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.
