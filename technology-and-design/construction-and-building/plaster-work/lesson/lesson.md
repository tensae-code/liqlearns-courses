# Plaster work

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Iran's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Mould and cast
The piece begins with its mould — the hollow form that gives the plaster its shape. Plaster is mixed to a pouring consistency, cast into the mould, and left to set; the mould is then opened to release the piece. Mix, pour and patience decide the casting's soundness: too wet weakens it, too hurried traps bubbles.

## Sub lesson 2 — Transfer and carve
The design reaches the piece by transfer: the drawing is laid over carbon paper on the plaster and traced, leaving its lines on the surface. Carving follows the transferred lines, cutting the design into the set plaster. Iran's Grade 9 module runs the whole craft in one project — mould, cast, transfer with carbon paper, carve — a finished plaster piece from raw powder.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: plaster work makes a cast piece: prepare the mould, cast the plaster, then transfer a design onto the set piece with carbon paper and carve it. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
