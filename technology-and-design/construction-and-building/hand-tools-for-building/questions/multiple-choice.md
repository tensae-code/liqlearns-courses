# Hand tools for building — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A string line is used to…
a) Carry a straight line across a site for setting out b) Measure small objects c) Cut timber d) Level a photograph
**Answer: a) Carry a straight line across a site for setting out**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A spirit level proves a surface level when…
a) Its bubble sits centred b) It is laid diagonally c) It is the longest tool d) The surface is wet
**Answer: a) Its bubble sits centred**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A crosscut saw is matched to…
a) Cutting across the grain b) Cutting sheet panels only c) Paring joints d) Copying angles
**Answer: a) Cutting across the grain**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A sliding bevel's job is to…
a) Copy and set an angle unchanged b) Measure length c) Scribe parallel lines d) Drive chisels
**Answer: a) Copy and set an angle unchanged**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** A dull tool is dangerous because…
a) It must be forced, and force slips b) It cuts too fast c) It is too light d) It makes no noise
**Answer: a) It must be forced, and force slips**
