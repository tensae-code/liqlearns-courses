# Hand tools for building — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Equal diagonals prove a set-out rectangle's corners are square.
**Answer: True** — That is the standard site check.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A chisel may be driven towards the body if the cut is small.
**Answer: False** — Chisels are always driven away from the body.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The marking gauge scribes a line parallel to an edge.
**Answer: True** — That is its single, precise job.
