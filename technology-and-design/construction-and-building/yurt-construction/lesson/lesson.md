# Yurt construction

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Kazakhstan's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Yurt construction
Kazakhstan's books teach it this way (the student book, printed pp. 52–53): The kiiz ui's parts (kerege lattice, uyk poles, shanyrak crown, sykyrlauyk door) and how the felt house is raised (Lesson 18). The learner works through the named materials and steps in order, saying what each step does before doing the next one.

## Sub lesson 2 — Yurt construction — doing it yourself
Now the learner does the whole task unaided: the kiiz ui's parts (kerege lattice, uyk poles, shanyrak crown, sykyrlauyk door) and how the felt house is raised (Lesson 18). Working from the first sub lesson's sequence, the learner plans the make, works through it step by step, and checks the finished piece against what the Kazakh book shows.
