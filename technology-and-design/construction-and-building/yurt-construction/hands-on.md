# Hands-on — Yurt construction

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: the kiiz ui's parts (kerege lattice, uyk poles, shanyrak crown, sykyrlauyk door) and how the felt house is raised (Lesson 18). Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
