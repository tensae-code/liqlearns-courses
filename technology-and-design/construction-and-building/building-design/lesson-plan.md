# Lesson plan — Building design

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Construction and Building · **Lesson:** Building design
**Place in the curriculum:** Construction and Building (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Moldova expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** a building is designed twice over: its siting (amplasarea) — where it stands — and its constructive composition (alcătuirea constructivă) — what it contains and how its entrances and circulation join the rooms, worked on the school.
**This lesson's own level:** stocked from Moldova's *Educație tehnologică* (Technological Education) textbooks — see lesson/sources.md. Moldova counts in the acceptance count (student books, not evidence-only).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **Siting and composition — amplasarea și alcătuirea constructivă** — Grade 9 building design names a building's two design questions: amplasarea — its siting, where and how it stands on its place — and alcătuirea constructivă — its constructive composition, what the building is made of as an organised whole.
2. **Worked on the school** — The worked example is the school itself: the rooms a school must contain, and how their entrances and circulation are designed — how people arrive, enter and move between rooms without collision or waste.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of Construction and Building. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Explain siting and constructive composition.
2. Design a school's rooms, entrances and circulation.
3. Explain how Moldova's book teaches it.
## What you need
Squared paper, and a school (your own) to study.
