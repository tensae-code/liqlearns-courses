# Building design

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Moldova's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Siting and composition — amplasarea și alcătuirea constructivă
Grade 9 building design names a building's two design questions: amplasarea — its siting, where and how it stands on its place — and alcătuirea constructivă — its constructive composition, what the building is made of as an organised whole. Both are answered together, on paper, before building.

## Sub lesson 2 — Worked on the school
The worked example is the school itself: the rooms a school must contain, and how their entrances and circulation are designed — how people arrive, enter and move between rooms without collision or waste. The learner designs the building they sit in.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: a building is designed twice over: its siting (amplasarea) — where it stands — and its constructive composition (alcătuirea constructivă) — what it contains and how its entrances and circulation join the rooms, worked on the school. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
