# Building design — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Siting and composition are one building's two design questions.
**Answer: True** — Amplasarea and alcătuirea constructivă are answered together.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Entrances and circulation are decorations added after design.
**Answer: False** — They are designed with the rooms — how people arrive, enter and move.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The school is designed as the worked example.
**Answer: True** — The rooms a school must contain, and their entrances and circulation, carry the lesson.
