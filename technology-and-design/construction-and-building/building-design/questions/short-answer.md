# Building design — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the two design questions of a building.
**Answer:** Its siting (amplasarea) and its constructive composition (alcătuirea constructivă).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the school example design?
**Answer:** The rooms a school must contain, and how their entrances and circulation are designed.
