# Building design — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is amplasarea?
a) The building's siting — where and how it stands b) Its paint c) Its price d) Its furniture
**Answer: a) The building's siting — where and how it stands**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is alcătuirea constructivă?
a) Its roof colour b) Its constructive composition — what it contains as an organised whole c) Its age d) Its name
**Answer: b) Its constructive composition — what it contains as an organised whole**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** When are siting and composition answered?
a) Together, on paper, before building b) After building c) Never d) One at a time, years apart
**Answer: a) Together, on paper, before building**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** What is the worked example?
a) The school — its rooms, entrances and circulation b) A house c) A bridge d) A shop
**Answer: a) The school — its rooms, entrances and circulation**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** What does circulation design govern?
a) The heating b) The timetable c) How people move between rooms without collision or waste d) The uniform
**Answer: c) How people move between rooms without collision or waste**
