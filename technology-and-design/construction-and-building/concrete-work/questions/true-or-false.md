# Concrete work — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Wet concrete pushes hard against its boxing.
**Answer: True** — Formwork must be built level and strong.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The water-cement ratio decides the hardened strength.
**Answer: True** — Water beyond the ratio is bought with strength.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Grooves are cut so shrinkage cracks come neatly in chosen lines.
**Answer: True** — Control grooving guides the cracking.
