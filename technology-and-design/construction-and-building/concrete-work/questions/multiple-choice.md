# Concrete work — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In the 3-4-5 method, the diagonal must measure…
a) 5 units when the sides are 3 and 4 b) 3 units c) 4 units d) Any length
**Answer: a) 5 units when the sides are 3 and 4**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Reinforcement is placed in a slab because…
a) Steel carries the tension concrete cannot b) It decorates the surface c) It colours the concrete d) It replaces formwork
**Answer: a) Steel carries the tension concrete cannot**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Slab volume is calculated as…
a) Length × width × thickness, totalled over the bays b) Length × width only c) Thickness alone d) Whatever the truck brings
**Answer: a) Length × width × thickness, totalled over the bays**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Extra water in the mix…
a) Makes placing easier and the concrete permanently weaker b) Makes stronger concrete c) Has no effect d) Saves cement safely
**Answer: a) Makes placing easier and the concrete permanently weaker**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Screeding levels the surface by…
a) Drawing a straightedge along the formwork b) Trowelling in circles first c) Flooding with water d) Waiting for gravity alone
**Answer: a) Drawing a straightedge along the formwork**
