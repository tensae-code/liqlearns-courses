# Concrete work — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Your 3-4-5 diagonal reads short. What does that mean and what do you move?
**Answer:** Answer: The corner is not square (it is less than a right angle); adjust the side lines until the diagonal reads exactly 5.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why calculate volumes before the pour day?
**Answer:** Answer: Concrete must be ordered and placed continuously; running short mid-pour leaves a weak joint, and the calculation (with waste margin) prevents it.
