# Hands-on — Concrete work

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## Prove 3-4-5
With string and tape on flat ground, set out a 3-4-5 triangle (use 1.5 m, 2 m, 2.5 m if space is short). Verify the diagonal and write what a diagonal of 2.4 m instead would tell you.

## Volume and order
A floor is 5 m × 4 m × 0.1 m thick, in two bays. Calculate the concrete volume, add 10% waste, and write the order quantity. Then state what happens to strength if the mixer adds extra water for ease.
