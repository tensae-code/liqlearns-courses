# Lesson plan — Concrete work

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Construction and Building · **Lesson:** Concrete work
**Place in the curriculum:** Construction and Building (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Samoa expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** a concrete floor is laid in order: set out with the 3-4-5 method, box formwork in bays with reinforcement, calculate and order the material volumes, then mix, place, screed and finish — with the water-cement ratio deciding the strength.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on student book evidence — Samoa's MESC textbooks (see lesson/sources.md). It counts in the acceptance count (student book, not evidence-only).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **Setting out 3-4-5, formwork and reinforcement** — The 3-4-5 triangle makes a true right angle on site; boxing (formwork) shapes the slab in bays; steel reinforcement carries the tension concrete cannot.
2. **Calculating, ordering and mixing** — Volumes are calculated from the bays' sizes before ordering; the water-cement ratio is measured, because water beyond the ratio buys ease with strength.
3. **Placing, screeding and finishing** — Concrete is placed bay by bay, screeded level off the boxing, then edged, grooved and finished — foam finishing among the book's methods.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of Construction and Building. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Set out a right angle by 3-4-5.
2. Calculate a slab's volume and state the water-cement rule.
3. Order the placing, screeding and finishing steps.
## What you need
Tape measure and string for the 3-4-5 practice; a calculator for volumes.
