# Hands-on — History and theory of buildings

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: Introduction to Building; Theory of Buildings; Types of Buildings (residential, educational, institutional, assembly, business, mercantile, industrial, storage, hazardous); Importance of building and career paths. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
