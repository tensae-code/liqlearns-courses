# Hands-on — Components of building and woodwork

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: Building Components; Woodwork; Types of Furniture; Types of Joinery; Methods of cutting joints and fittings. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
