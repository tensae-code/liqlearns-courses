# Hands-on — Plumbing trade maths

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: Applied Math in Plumbing; Method of Calculating Water Distribution System, DWV system; Methods of Preparing Plumbing Material List; The Role of the Estimator. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
