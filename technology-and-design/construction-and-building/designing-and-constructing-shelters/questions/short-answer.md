# Designing and constructing shelters — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Span stays the same, spacing widens. What happens to the required timber size, and why?
**Answer:** Answer: It grows — each timber now carries a wider strip of roof or floor, so a stronger (bigger) section is needed.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name two wind provisions in a shelter design.
**Answer:** Answer: Any two of: bracing, tie-downs/strapping of roof to frame to footings, adequate fixings, designing to the maximum design wind speed.
