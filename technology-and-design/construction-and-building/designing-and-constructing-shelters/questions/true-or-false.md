# Designing and constructing shelters — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Designing by span table is designing by accumulated evidence.
**Answer: True** — The table distils what stands and what sags.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A roof strong enough for its own weight is automatically safe in wind.
**Answer: False** — Wind lift is a separate force the fixings and tie-downs must resist.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Approval can catch a wrong span while it is still a drawing.
**Answer: True** — That is the point of approving before building.
