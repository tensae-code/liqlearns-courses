# Designing and constructing shelters — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A span table is read by…
a) Span and spacing together, giving the timber size b) Colour of timber c) Price only d) The builder's habit
**Answer: a) Span and spacing together, giving the timber size**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Wider spacing at the same span demands…
a) Bigger timber b) Smaller timber c) The same timber always d) No timber
**Answer: a) Bigger timber**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Wind loading includes…
a) Lift that tries to tear the roof off b) Only downward weight c) Rain weight only d) Snow only in Samoa
**Answer: a) Lift that tries to tear the roof off**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The design wind figure used is…
a) The maximum design wind speed for the place b) The average breeze c) Yesterday's weather d) Zero, for calm sites
**Answer: a) The maximum design wind speed for the place**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Drawings are approved before building because…
a) Errors cost pencil strokes before building and structures after b) Approval is a celebration c) Builders like stamps d) Regulations require a party
**Answer: a) Errors cost pencil strokes before building and structures after**
