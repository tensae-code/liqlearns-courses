# Lesson plan — Designing and constructing shelters

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Construction and Building · **Lesson:** Designing and constructing shelters
**Place in the curriculum:** Construction and Building (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Samoa expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** a shelter is designed before it is built: span tables give timber sizes, spacings and spans; wind loading sets the forces the design must resist; regulations govern it; and the working drawings go for approval before building starts.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on student book evidence — Samoa's MESC textbooks (see lesson/sources.md). It counts in the acceptance count (student book, not evidence-only).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **Span tables — size from the table** — A span table answers: how far can this timber, at this spacing, safely span? Size, spacing and span are read together.
2. **Wind loading** — Wind pushes and lifts: the design must resist the maximum design wind speed for the place.
3. **Regulations and drawing approval** — Local building regulations set the rules, and the working drawings are submitted for approval before building.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of Construction and Building. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Read a span table by span and spacing.
2. Explain wind loading and why lift matters.
3. Say why drawings are approved before building.
## What you need
A sample span table if available (or the lesson's description); paper for the design exercise.
