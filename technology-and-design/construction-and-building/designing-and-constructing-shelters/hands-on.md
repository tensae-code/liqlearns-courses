# Hands-on — Designing and constructing shelters

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## Use the table
Given a span table (teacher-provided or from Samoa's book, pp. 58-60), find the timber size for two cases: a 3 m span at 600 mm spacing, and the same span at 900 mm spacing. Record how the size changed and why.

## Design check
Write a one-page design note for a small shelter: span and spacing chosen, wind provisions (bracing, tie-downs), and the approval step — in the order a builder would meet them.
