# Brick parts and wall thicknesses

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Iran's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — The parts of a brick
A brick is a small block with a vocabulary: its large faces, its long sides, its ends each carry names the bricklayer uses to say exactly how a brick sits in the wall. Iran's Grade 9 module teaches the parts before the laying, because every later instruction — stretcher, header, bond — is spoken in these terms.

## Sub lesson 2 — Wall thicknesses
Walls are named by the brick's length: a half-brick wall is one stretcher's width thick; a one-brick wall takes the brick's full length as its thickness; thicker walls add more. Thickness is chosen by the wall's job — load, height, weather and separation — and the module teaches the standard thicknesses as the wall-types the learner will build.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: a brick has named parts — faces and edges with their own terms — and walls are built to named thicknesses: half-brick, one-brick and thicker, each thickness a different strength and use. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
