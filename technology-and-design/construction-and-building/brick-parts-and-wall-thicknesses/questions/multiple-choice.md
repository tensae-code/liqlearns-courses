# Brick parts and wall thicknesses — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is stretcher?
a) a brick laid with its long face showing, or that long face itself b) Only a price c) Only decoration d) Nothing to do with this lesson
**Answer: a) a brick laid with its long face showing, or that long face itself**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is header?
a) Only history b) a brick laid with its end face showing, or that end face itself c) Only storage d) A kind of glue
**Answer: b) a brick laid with its end face showing, or that end face itself**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is half-brick wall?
a) Only old tools b) Only painting c) a wall one stretcher's width thick d) Only a price
**Answer: c) a wall one stretcher's width thick**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson teach, in one line?
a) Only decoration b) Nothing to do with this lesson c) Only history d) a brick has named parts — faces and edges with their own terms — and walls are built to named thicknesses: half-brick, one-brick and thicker, each thickness a different strength and use.
**Answer: d) a brick has named parts — faces and edges with their own terms — and walls are built to named thicknesses: half-brick, one-brick and thicker, each thickness a different strength and use.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** How does Iran's book teach this?
a) As theory only, never practised b) Only in a glossary c) Only as an exam d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Persian
**Answer: d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Persian**
