# Brick parts and wall thicknesses — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Stretcher is a brick laid with its long face showing, or that long face itself.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Header has nothing to do with this lesson.
**Answer: False** — It is part of the lesson: a brick laid with its end face showing, or that end face itself.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Half-brick wall is a wall one stretcher's width thick.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.
