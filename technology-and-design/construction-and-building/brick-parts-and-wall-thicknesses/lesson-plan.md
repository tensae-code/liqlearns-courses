# Lesson plan — Brick parts and wall thicknesses

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Construction and Building · **Lesson:** Brick parts and wall thicknesses
**Place in the curriculum:** Construction and Building (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Iran expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** a brick has named parts — faces and edges with their own terms — and walls are built to named thicknesses: half-brick, one-brick and thicker, each thickness a different strength and use.
**This lesson's own level:** stocked from Iran's *Work & Technology, Grade 9* (student book) — see lesson/sources.md. Iran counts in the acceptance count (student books, not evidence-only).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **The parts of a brick** — A brick is a small block with a vocabulary: its large faces, its long sides, its ends each carry names the bricklayer uses to say exactly how a brick sits in the wall.
2. **Wall thicknesses** — Walls are named by the brick's length: a half-brick wall is one stretcher's width thick; a one-brick wall takes the brick's full length as its thickness; thicker walls add more.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
