# Basic home improvement — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, or by doing the step named.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** State the heart of this topic in your own words.
**Answer:** Maintaining a building over its lifetime: painting, tiling (laying tiles, grouting, sealing), checking electrical wiring and lighting, inspecting plumbing for leaks, sewer connections, exterior walls, pavement, driveway and roof — part of basic home improvement.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** What do you do when your check finds something wrong?
**Answer:** Fix one thing you find, and say what you changed and why.
