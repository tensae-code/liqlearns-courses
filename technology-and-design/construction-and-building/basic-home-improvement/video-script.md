# Video script — Basic home improvement

A short demonstration video, made later, in a super-simple voice. The demonstrator works through the topic while the voice speaks.

---

This lesson is about basic home improvement.
Maintaining a building over its lifetime: painting, tiling (laying tiles, grouting, sealing), checking electrical wiring and lighting, inspecting plumbing for leaks, sewer connections, exterior walls, pavement, driveway and roof.
Work step by step. Check your work as you go.
