# Hands-on — Boatbuilding construction sequence

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## Order the build
On cards, write the parts: keel, stem, transom, frames, shores, timbers. Arrange them in build order and write one line for each saying what it does. Swap one pair and explain what breaks.

## Timber choice
For the keel and for a frame, choose between oak and cedar as the annex presents them as prepared boat timbers, and write what property your choice relies on.
