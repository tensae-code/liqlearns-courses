# Boatbuilding construction sequence — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The construction sequence can be shuffled without consequence.
**Answer: False** — The order is fixed: the keel is laid first because every other part takes its position from it.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The transom is the flat board that closes the stern.
**Answer: True** — Stem at the bow, transom closing the stern, frames between them give the hull its shape.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Material preparation is separate from construction and does not affect the build.
**Answer: False** — Oak and cedar are prepared as boat timbers as part of the technology; a part is only as good as its prepared timber.
