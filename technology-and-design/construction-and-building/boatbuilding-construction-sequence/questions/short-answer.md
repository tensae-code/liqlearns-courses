# Boatbuilding construction sequence — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why must the keel be laid before the frames?
**Answer:** Because the keel is the backbone from which every other part takes its position; without it there is nothing true to measure or fix the frames to.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the stem angle control, and why does that make it a design decision?
**Answer:** It sets the boat's speed through the water, so choosing the angle is choosing performance, not just shape.
