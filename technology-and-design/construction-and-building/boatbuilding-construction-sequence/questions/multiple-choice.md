# Boatbuilding construction sequence — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The first piece laid in the canot build is the…
a) Transom b) Keel, on supports c) Roof d) Sail
**Answer: b) Keel, on supports**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The keel serves as the boat's…
a) Decoration b) Backbone — everything takes position from it c) Anchor d) Rudder
**Answer: b) Backbone — everything takes position from it**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The stem angle sets the boat's…
a) Colour b) Speed c) Crew size d) Price
**Answer: b) Speed**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Frames are held during building by…
a) Ropes only b) Shores (props) c) The crew's hands d) Nothing
**Answer: b) Shores (props)**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** The timbers named as prepared boat timbers are…
a) Pine and bamboo b) Oak and cedar c) Teak and mahogany only d) Plastic and fibreglass
**Answer: b) Oak and cedar**
