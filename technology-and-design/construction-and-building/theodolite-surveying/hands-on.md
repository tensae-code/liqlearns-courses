# Hands-on — Theodolite surveying

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## Work the topic
Carry out the topic's core task on a fresh example: parts of the theodolite, setting it up, measuring angles with it, its adjustments and tests, errors, and the full theodolite survey method (incl. where chain surveying alone cannot be used). Work in order, naming each part or step as you go.

## Check it
Check your finished work against the lesson's own rules. Find one thing that is wrong or weak, fix it, and say what you changed and why.
