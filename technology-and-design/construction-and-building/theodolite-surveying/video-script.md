# Video script — Theodolite surveying

A short demonstration video, made later, in a super-simple voice. The demonstrator works through the topic while the voice speaks.

---

This lesson is about theodolite surveying.
Parts of the theodolite, setting it up, measuring angles with it, its adjustments and tests, errors, and the full theodolite survey method (incl. where chain surveying alone cannot be used).
Work step by step. Check your work as you go.
