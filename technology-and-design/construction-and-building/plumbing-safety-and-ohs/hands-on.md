# Hands-on — Plumbing safety and OHS

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: Occupational Health Safety Regulations and standards; Application of OHS in Plumbing; also: Personal Safety; Safe Handling of Plumbing Tools and Equipment; also: OHS On-Site; Types of Hazards on site; Type of Personal Protective Equipment used On-Site; The Role of OHS officer On-Site; Methods of controlling Hazard on site and workplace. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
