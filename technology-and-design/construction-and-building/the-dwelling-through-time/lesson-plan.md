# Lesson plan — The dwelling through time

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Construction and Building · **Lesson:** The dwelling through time
**Place in the curriculum:** Construction and Building (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Moldova expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the dwelling has a history (evoluția locuinței): from the peasant house with porch and one room to later house forms — the construction culture that stands behind interior design.
**This lesson's own level:** stocked from Moldova's *Educație tehnologică* (Technological Education) textbooks — see lesson/sources.md. Moldova counts in the acceptance count (student books, not evidence-only).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **From the peasant house onward** — Moldova's Grade 9 book teaches evoluția locuinței — the evolution of the dwelling: it begins with the peasant house with porch and one room, the traditional Moldovan house in its simplest complete form, and follows the house forms that came later.
2. **The culture behind interior design** — The history is placed as foundation: the construction culture behind interior design.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of Construction and Building. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Trace the dwelling's evolution from the peasant house onward.
2. Explain why this history stands behind interior design.
3. Explain how Moldova's book teaches it.
## What you need
Pictures of house forms across time, including the peasant house.
