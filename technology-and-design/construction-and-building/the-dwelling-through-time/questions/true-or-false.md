# The dwelling through time — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The peasant house with porch and one room opens the dwelling's evolution.
**Answer: True** — It is the starting form of evoluția locuinței.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Interior design has no need of the dwelling's history.
**Answer: False** — The history is the construction culture behind interior design.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Later house forms simply repeat the first form unchanged.
**Answer: False** — The evolution follows the forms that came later — each answering its time.
