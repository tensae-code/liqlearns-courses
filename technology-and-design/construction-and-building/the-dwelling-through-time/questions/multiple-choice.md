# The dwelling through time — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is evoluția locuinței?
a) The evolution of the dwelling b) A house price list c) A building law d) A furniture style
**Answer: a) The evolution of the dwelling**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Where does the evolution begin?
a) With apartment blocks b) With the peasant house with porch and one room c) With palaces d) With tents only
**Answer: b) With the peasant house with porch and one room**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What does each house form answer?
a) Its time's life, with its time's building b) Nothing c) Only fashion d) Only cost
**Answer: a) Its time's life, with its time's building**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Why is the history taught in this book?
a) It is the construction culture behind interior design b) For dates only c) To fill pages d) It is unrelated to design
**Answer: a) It is the construction culture behind interior design**

**5. (Sub lesson 1)** The peasant house's two named parts are…
a) Tower and hall b) Garage and loft c) Porch and one room d) Kitchen and bath
**Answer: c) Porch and one room**
