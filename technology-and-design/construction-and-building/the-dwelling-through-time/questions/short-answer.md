# The dwelling through time — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** From what does the dwelling's evolution start?
**Answer:** From the peasant house with porch and one room, following the house forms that came later.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why does interior design study this history?
**Answer:** Because it is the construction culture behind interior design — design works with the tradition, not against it.
