# The dwelling through time

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Moldova's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — From the peasant house onward
Moldova's Grade 9 book teaches evoluția locuinței — the evolution of the dwelling: it begins with the peasant house with porch and one room, the traditional Moldovan house in its simplest complete form, and follows the house forms that came later. Each form answers its time's life with its time's building.

## Sub lesson 2 — The culture behind interior design
The history is placed as foundation: the construction culture behind interior design. The learner who will arrange interiors first learns what a dwelling has been — porch, room, and everything later houses added — so design works with the tradition, not against it.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: the dwelling has a history (evoluția locuinței): from the peasant house with porch and one room to later house forms — the construction culture that stands behind interior design. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
