# Hands-on — Architecture and building to a project

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: what architecture is; inventing buildings (Gaudí) and building a paper house to an architect's project; a collective architecture project assembled from each child's building. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
