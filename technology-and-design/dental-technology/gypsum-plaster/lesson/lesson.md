# Gypsum (plaster)

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Greece's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Gypsum (plaster)
Greece's book teaches it this way: its preparation and kinds (impression, Paris, hard, extra-hard), setting — working/setting time, setting expansion and what affects them — mechanical properties, mixing and making casts (εκμαγεία) (*Dental Materials*, printed p. 63). The learner works through the named materials and steps in order, saying what each step does before doing the next one.

## Sub lesson 2 — Gypsum (plaster) — doing it yourself
Now the learner does the whole task unaided: its preparation and kinds (impression, Paris, hard, extra-hard), setting — working/setting time, setting expansion and what affects them — mechanical properties, mixing and making casts (εκμαγεία). Working from the first sub lesson's sequence, the learner plans the make, works through it step by step, and checks the finished piece against what Greece's book shows.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: Its preparation and kinds (impression, Paris, hard, extra-hard), setting — working/setting time, setting expansion and what affects them — mechanical properties, mixing and making casts (εκμαγεία). Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
