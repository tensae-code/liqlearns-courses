# Hands-on — Gypsum (plaster)

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: its preparation and kinds (impression, Paris, hard, extra-hard), setting — working/setting time, setting expansion and what affects them — mechanical properties, mixing and making casts (εκμαγεία). Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
