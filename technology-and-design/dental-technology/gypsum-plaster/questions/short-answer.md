# Gypsum (plaster) — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Say in one sentence what this lesson teaches.
**Answer:** Its preparation and kinds (impression, Paris, hard, extra-hard), setting — working/setting time, setting expansion and what affects them — mechanical properties, mixing and making casts (εκμαγεία).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name one material, tool or step this lesson works with.
**Answer:** Any one named in the lesson text — for example, the first step of sub lesson 1: Gypsum (plaster).
