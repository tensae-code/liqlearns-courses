# Hands-on — Resins

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: polymerisation (addition, condensation, copolymerisation), synthetic and acrylic resins, heat-cured acrylics for denture bases and veneer crowns/bridges, cold-cured acrylics. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
