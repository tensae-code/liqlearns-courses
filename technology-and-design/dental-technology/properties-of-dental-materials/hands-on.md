# Hands-on — Properties of dental materials

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: states of matter, mechanical properties (stress–strain, elastic limit, modulus, yield, fracture, ductility/brittleness, hardness, fatigue, creep) and rheological properties (viscosity, viscoelasticity). Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
