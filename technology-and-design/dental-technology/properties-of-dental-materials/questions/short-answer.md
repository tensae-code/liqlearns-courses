# Properties of dental materials — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Say in one sentence what this lesson teaches.
**Answer:** States of matter, mechanical properties (stress–strain, elastic limit, modulus, yield, fracture, ductility/brittleness, hardness, fatigue, creep) and rheological properties (viscosity, viscoelasticity).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name one material, tool or step this lesson works with.
**Answer:** Any one named in the lesson text — for example, the first step of sub lesson 1: Properties of dental materials.
