# Hands-on — Dental alloys

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: gold and palladium alloy systems, base-metal alloys (Ni-Cr, Co-Cr), alloys for partial-denture frameworks, and soldering/joining (incl. laser welding, fluxes). Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
