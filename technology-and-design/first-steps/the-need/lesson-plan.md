# Lesson plan — The need

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** First Steps · **Lesson:** The need
**Place in the curriculum:** First Steps (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Morocco expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** every product exists because of a need: someone — a client or user — needs something done, and the product is the answer; a need must be expressed and validated before anything is designed.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on teacher-guide evidence only — Morocco's Technologie teacher guides (Ministère de l'Éducation Nationale, du Préscolaire et des Sports, Royaume du Maroc) are the books in hand for this topic (see lesson/sources.md). Teacher guides are evidence, counted nowhere in the acceptance count. The pupil manuals themselves are not in hand; the placements are filed from the guides' answer elements (Part 5) as guide evidence.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **The need behind a product** — A product is an answer to a need: before the object exists, someone lacks something or wants a job done. Expressing the need means saying who has it, what they want to be able to do, and in what situation — without yet naming the object that will answer it.
2. **Who the product serves — client and user** — The person a product serves may be an internal client (inside the firm that makes it) or an external client (outside it), and the user who handles the product every day is not always the person who chose it. Naming the client and the user tells the designer whose need counts.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of First Steps. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say what a need is and how it differs from the product that answers it.
2. Name the client and the user of a familiar product, and say whether each is internal or external.
3. Express a need in one clear sentence that names no object.
## What you need
A familiar product to examine, and paper for writing needs.
