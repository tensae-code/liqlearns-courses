# The need — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A need and the product that answers it are the same thing.
**Answer: False** — The need comes first and is stated without naming an object; the product is one possible answer.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The client and the user of a product are always the same person.
**Answer: False** — A client asks for or buys the product; a different person may use it daily.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A product can serve an internal client inside the enterprise that makes it.
**Answer: True** — Morocco's course distinguishes internal clients from external clients.
