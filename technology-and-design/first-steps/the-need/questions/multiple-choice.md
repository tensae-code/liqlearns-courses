# The need — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A need is…
a) what someone wants to be able to do, before any product is named b) a finished product on a shelf c) a price tag d) a factory machine
**Answer: a) what someone wants to be able to do, before any product is named**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Expressing a need well means naming…
a) who has it, what they want to do, and in what situation b) the exact product to build c) the cheapest material d) the factory's name
**Answer: a) who has it, what they want to do, and in what situation**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The user of a product is…
a) the person who uses it every day b) always the person who paid for it c) the factory worker only d) never a real person
**Answer: a) the person who uses it every day**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** An internal client is…
a) a client inside the same enterprise b) a client in another country c) a client who never pays d) the product itself
**Answer: a) a client inside the same enterprise**

**5. (Sub lesson 1)** Why is a need validated before designing?
a) To check the need is real before building an answer to it b) To choose the colour first c) To skip the design stage d) To raise the price
**Answer: a) To check the need is real before building an answer to it**
