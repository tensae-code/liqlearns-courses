# The need — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Write one need sentence for a school bag without naming the bag.
**Answer:** A learner needs to carry books and tools to school each day, keeping them dry and easy to reach.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** For a classroom lamp, name a client and a user.
**Answer:** Client: the school that buys it (external to the maker). User: the pupils and teacher who work under its light.
