# Design careers — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is a graphic designer?
a) a designer who shapes images and print b) Only a price c) Only decoration d) Nothing to do with this lesson
**Answer: a) a designer who shapes images and print**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is an animator?
a) Only decoration b) a designer who designs moving pictures c) Only a price d) A kind of glue
**Answer: b) a designer who designs moving pictures**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is a design career path?
a) Only history b) Only painting c) a chosen route into one of the design careers d) Only storage
**Answer: c) a chosen route into one of the design careers**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson teach, in one line?
a) design is a family of careers — graphic designer, chef, fashion designer, animator, architect — and a path is chosen by what you love to design. b) Only prices c) Only old tools d) Nothing practical
**Answer: a) design is a family of careers — graphic designer, chef, fashion designer, animator, architect — and a path is chosen by what you love to design.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** How does Malaysia's book teach this?
a) As theory only, never practised b) Only in the glossary c) Only as an exam d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Malay (RBT)
**Answer: d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Malay (RBT)**
