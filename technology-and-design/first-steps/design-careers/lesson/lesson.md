# Design careers

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Malaysia's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Five design careers
Design is work people do: the graphic designer shapes images and print, the chef designs food, the fashion designer designs clothing, the animator designs moving pictures, and the architect designs buildings.

## Sub lesson 2 — Choosing a design career path
Each career designs a different thing for different people. Choosing a path starts from what you love designing — pictures, food, clothes, movement or buildings — and grows with skill.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: design is a family of careers — graphic designer, chef, fashion designer, animator, architect — and a path is chosen by what you love to design. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
