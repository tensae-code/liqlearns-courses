# Representing a technical object in its environment, with a written description — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Representing a tool in its environment shows…
a) Only decoration b) Scale, the ground it works and what it acts on c) The maker's signature d) Nothing new
**Answer: b) Scale, the ground it works and what it acts on**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A tool drawn alone on a blank page hides…
a) Its shape b) Its work c) Its outline d) Its name
**Answer: b) Its work**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The descriptif carries what a picture cannot, such as…
a) Colour b) Names, purposes and order of operations c) Paper size d) The date
**Answer: b) Names, purposes and order of operations**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The descriptif is best described as…
a) A caption b) The second half of the representation c) An optional extra d) A price tag
**Answer: b) The second half of the representation**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** The pair is checked by asking whether a new reader could understand…
a) The artist's feelings b) What the tool is, where it works and what it does c) Only the tool's colour d) The team's names
**Answer: b) What the tool is, where it works and what it does**
