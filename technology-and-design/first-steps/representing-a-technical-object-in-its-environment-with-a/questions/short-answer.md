# Representing a technical object in its environment, with a written description — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name two things an environment shows that a lone-object drawing hides.
**Answer:** Any two of: the tool's scale, the ground or material it works on, what it acts upon, and who or what works it.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why is the descriptif not just a caption?
**Answer:** Because it carries the names, purposes and order of operations the picture cannot show — it is half of the representation, not a label under it.
