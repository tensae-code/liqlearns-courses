# Hands-on — Representing a technical object in its environment, with a written description

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## Represent and describe
In a team, represent one mechanised tool in its environment and write its descriptif. Exchange with another team: from their pair alone, answer what the tool is, where it works and what it does. Note any question you could not answer.

## Environment audit
Take a lone-object drawing from a book or your notes. List everything its missing environment would have told you, then redraw it in context.
