# Parts of an object and the function of each part (ballpoint pen) \u2014 short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why reassemble the pen after dismantling it?
**Answer:** Reassembling proves every part was found and understood \u2014 a missing or spare part shows at once.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Give the function of the tip support.
**Answer:** It holds the pen's point \u2014 the ball and its housing \u2014 in place at the writing end.
