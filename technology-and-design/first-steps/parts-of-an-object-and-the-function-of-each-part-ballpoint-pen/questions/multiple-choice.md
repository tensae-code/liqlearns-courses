# Parts of an object and the function of each part (ballpoint pen) \u2014 multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How are a pen's parts found?
a) By guessing b) By dismantling and reassembling it c) By reading its price tag d) By weighing it
**Answer: b) By dismantling and reassembling it**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which list names pen elements?
a) Body, ink cartridge, tip support, ball, cap, end plug b) Wheel, chain, brake, bell c) Mesh, frame, handle d) Needle, thread, cloth
**Answer: a) Body, ink cartridge, tip support, ball, cap, end plug**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the ink cartridge do?
a) It rolls the ink out b) It stores the ink c) It protects the point d) It closes the body
**Answer: b) It stores the ink**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which part gives the ballpoint pen its name?
a) The cap b) The end plug c) The ball at the point d) The body
**Answer: c) The ball at the point**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the cap do?
a) Stores ink b) Protects the point and stops ink drying or marking c) Rolls on the paper d) Holds the point
**Answer: b) Protects the point and stops ink drying or marking**
