# Making an object

## Do it first
Fold a sheet of paper back and forth like a concertina, in even folds. Open it out. You have done the heart of this lesson's make: even folding turns a flat sheet into a fan's body.

## Sub lesson 1 \u2014 The steps of fabricating an object
Fabricating a technical object means carrying out a set of steps in order. First you gather the materials and tools. Then you prepare each part \u2014 measuring, marking and cutting. Then you join the parts, and finally you finish and check the object: does it work the way it should? Skipping a step or changing the order usually shows in the finished thing.

## Sub lesson 2 \u2014 Making a fan (\u00e9ventail)
The fan (\u00e9ventail) is made from paper sheets, ice-cream sticks, rulers and glue. The paper is folded evenly \u2014 the ruler helps keep the folds straight and the same width. The sticks form the fan's frame, one at each side, glued to the folded paper. When the glue holds, the fan opens into a half-circle and folds shut again: a flat sheet has become a working object.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: an object is made step by step: for the paper fan, the steps run from flat sheets and sticks to a finished fan that opens and closes. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
