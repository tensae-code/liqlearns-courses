# Hands-on \u2014 Making an object

Real practice \u2014 this subject is learned by doing.

## Make the fan
Fold a paper sheet in even concertina folds, using the ruler to keep them equal. Glue an ice-cream stick to each outer edge. Let it dry, then open and close your fan five times.

## Step cards
Write the five steps of your make on five cards (gather, fold, glue the sticks, dry, check). Shuffle them, then put them back in order and explain why the order matters.
