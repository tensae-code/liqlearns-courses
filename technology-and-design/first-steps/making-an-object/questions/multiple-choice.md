# Making an object \u2014 multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the first step of fabricating an object?
a) Finish and check b) Gather the materials and tools c) Join the parts d) Throw the scraps away
**Answer: b) Gather the materials and tools**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Why does the order of the steps matter?
a) It does not matter at all b) Each step prepares the next; skipping shows in the finished object c) Only the last step counts d) Steps are only for teachers
**Answer: b) Each step prepares the next; skipping shows in the finished object**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is the fan in this lesson made from?
a) Metal and glass b) Paper sheets, ice-cream sticks, rulers and glue c) Clay and string d) Wood planks and nails
**Answer: b) Paper sheets, ice-cream sticks, rulers and glue**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** What do the ice-cream sticks do in the fan?
a) They colour the paper b) They form the frame at each side c) They cut the paper d) They replace the glue
**Answer: b) They form the frame at each side**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** How do you check the finished fan?
a) Weigh it b) Open it into a half-circle and fold it shut again c) Hide it d) Paint it first
**Answer: b) Open it into a half-circle and fold it shut again**
