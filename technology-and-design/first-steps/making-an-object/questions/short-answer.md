# Making an object \u2014 short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the steps of fabricating an object in order.
**Answer:** Gather, prepare, join, finish \u2014 then check that the object works.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why is the paper folded back and forth evenly?
**Answer:** Even concertina folds let the fan spread open evenly into a half-circle and fold shut again.
