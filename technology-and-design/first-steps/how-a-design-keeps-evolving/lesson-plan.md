# Lesson plan — How a design keeps evolving

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** First Steps · **Lesson:** How a design keeps evolving
**Place in the curriculum:** First Steps (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Haiti expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** a design keeps evolving through its wear parts, its materials and its safety elements — the modern mouldboard plough is the same lineage answer, redesigned where it wears, what it is made of, and how it protects its user.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on teacher-guide evidence only — Haiti's ITAP Projet Nectar pedagogical sequences (MENFP) are the books in hand for this topic (see lesson/sources.md). Teacher guides are evidence, counted nowhere in the acceptance count.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **Designs do not stand still** — A finished design is a stage, not an end.
2. **Wear parts lead the change** — The parts that wear are redesigned first and most.
3. **Materials and safety in modern plough design** — New materials and safety elements carry the evolution forward.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of First Steps. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name the three drivers of continuing design evolution.
2. Say why wear parts lead the change.
3. Apply the three-driver check to another object.
## What you need
Pictures of an older and a modern mouldboard plough, and any familiar tool for the closing transfer task.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — the opening doing task in lesson/lesson.md.
2. **The explanation** — the sub lessons in order (lesson/lesson.md).
   - ADD IMAGE: a picture of an old and a modern mouldboard plough side by side, with the share, the mouldboard material and a safety element marked — the student finds the three drivers — wear part, material, safety — on the modern plough.
