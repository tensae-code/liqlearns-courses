# How a design keeps evolving — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why does the lesson use the mouldboard plough to teach continuing evolution?
**Answer:** Because it is a mature, centuries-old design that still changes — proving evolution does not stop when a design works.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** State the general rule this lesson gives for predicting how a design will evolve.
**Answer:** Examine the object's wear parts, its materials and its safety elements — the next changes will come from those three places.
