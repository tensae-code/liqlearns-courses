# How a design keeps evolving — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Evolution of a design ends once the design works well.
**Answer: False** — The synthesis teaches the opposite: a mature design like the mouldboard plough keeps evolving.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Safety elements are one of the drivers of modern plough design.
**Answer: True** — Wear parts, materials and safety elements are the three drivers named.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Materials have nothing to do with how a wear part evolves.
**Answer: False** — Better materials make wear parts last, so materials and wear drive the evolution together.
