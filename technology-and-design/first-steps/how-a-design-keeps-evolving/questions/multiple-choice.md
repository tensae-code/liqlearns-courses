# How a design keeps evolving — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A design that works is…
a) Finished forever b) A stage that keeps evolving c) Forbidden to change d) Only copied
**Answer: b) A stage that keeps evolving**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The mouldboard plough's long history shows that evolution…
a) Stopped centuries ago b) Continues in a mature design c) Only happens for new inventions d) Is random
**Answer: b) Continues in a mature design**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Wear parts lead redesign because…
a) They are decorative b) Performance is lost where the object wears c) They are cheapest d) They never wear
**Answer: b) Performance is lost where the object wears**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** On a plough, the wear parts meet…
a) The air b) The soil on every pass c) The shed wall d) Nothing
**Answer: b) The soil on every pass**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** The three drivers named in the synthesis are…
a) Price, colour, fashion b) Wear parts, materials and safety c) Size, weight, speed d) Maker, seller, buyer
**Answer: b) Wear parts, materials and safety**
