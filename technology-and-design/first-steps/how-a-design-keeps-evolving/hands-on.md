# Hands-on — How a design keeps evolving

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## Three-driver audit
Take a familiar object (bicycle, hoe, kettle). Write its wear parts, its materials, and its safety elements. For each, write one change its next design generation might make.

## Plough comparison
From pictures of an older and a modern mouldboard plough, list every difference you can find and sort each under wear, materials or safety.
