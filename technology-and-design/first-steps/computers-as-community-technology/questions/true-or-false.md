# Computers as community technology — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A computer stores information as one of its four steps.
**Answer: True** — Storing is the third step: in, process, store, display.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A mainframe and a personal computer are exactly the same size and serve the same number of people.
**Answer: False** — A mainframe serves many people and a whole organisation; a personal computer serves one person.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** IT managers organise the whole computer system.
**Answer: True** — That organising job is what IT managers do.
