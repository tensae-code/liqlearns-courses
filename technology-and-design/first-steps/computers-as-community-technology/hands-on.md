# Hands-on — Computers as community technology

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## Four steps in real life
Use any computer task — typing a sentence, taking a photo, playing a song. Write down the four steps for that task: what went in, what was processed, what was stored, what was displayed.

## Job match
A program needs designing, a screen is broken, and a school's whole system needs organising. Say which worker handles each job: software engineer, technician or IT manager. Explain your choices to a partner.
