# Lesson plan — Evolution of technical objects

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** First Steps · **Lesson:** Evolution of technical objects
**Place in the curriculum:** First Steps (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Haiti expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** technical objects evolve in a lignée — a lineage of objects sharing one main function — and the lineage is read by comparing date, materials, form, energy used and the productivity need each step answered.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on teacher-guide evidence only — Haiti's ITAP Projet Nectar pedagogical sequences (MENFP) are the books in hand for this topic (see lesson/sources.md). Teacher guides are evidence, counted nowhere in the acceptance count.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **A lignée — one function, many objects** — Objects that do the same main job form a lineage across time.
2. **Comparing across the lineage** — Five comparison points make the evolution readable.
3. **From hand tools to machines** — The ploughing lineage climbs from muscle power to mechanisation.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of First Steps. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Define a lignée by its shared main function.
2. Compare two lineage members on the five points.
3. Trace the ploughing lineage from manual to mechanised.
## What you need
Pictures or examples of manual and mechanised ploughing tools, and a blank five-point comparison table.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — the opening doing task in lesson/lesson.md.
2. **The explanation** — the sub lessons in order (lesson/lesson.md).
   - ADD IMAGE: a picture of a lineage wall — digging stick, spade, hoe, plough and motorised cultivator in a row under one heading: turning the soil — the student reads the shared main function, then names what changed at each step.
   - ADD GIF: a GIF of the lineage row lighting up step by step, with energy changing from muscle to animal to engine — the student names the energy used at each step as it lights.
   - ADD AUDIO: a voice reading the five comparison points — date, materials, form, energy used, productivity need aloud — the student repeats them.
   - ADD VIDEO: a short video of the lesson demonstration — see video-script.md.
3. **Say it to your companion** — the learner explains the lesson's key rule aloud.
4. **Questions** — the questions/ files, spread over the sub lessons.
5. **Hands-on** — hands-on.md.
6. **Do It Yourself** — the closing doing block in lesson/lesson.md.
