# Hands-on — Evolution of technical objects

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## Build a lineage
Choose a function (carrying water, lighting a room, cutting wood). List at least four objects in its lineage and order them by date. For each, note materials and energy used.

## Five-point comparison
Take two neighbouring members of the ploughing lineage and complete the five-point comparison. Write one sentence: what need did the newer step answer?
