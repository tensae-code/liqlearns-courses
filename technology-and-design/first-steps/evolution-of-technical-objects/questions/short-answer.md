# Evolution of technical objects — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the five points used to compare members of a lineage.
**Answer:** Date, materials of manufacture, form, energy used, and the productivity need the step answered.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** How does the energy used change along the ploughing lineage?
**Answer:** It climbs from human muscle power for the manual tools to animal and then engine power for the mechanised tools.
