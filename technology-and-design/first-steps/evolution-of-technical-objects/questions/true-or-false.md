# Evolution of technical objects — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Objects in a lineage can have completely different main functions.
**Answer: False** — A lineage is defined by one shared main function; form, materials and energy change around it.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Fixed comparison points let you say exactly what changed at each step.
**Answer: True** — Date, materials, form, energy and productivity need are applied to every member the same way.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In the lineage, 'newer' automatically means the step answered no particular need.
**Answer: False** — Each step answers a productivity need — more ground worked, less effort — which the comparison makes visible.
