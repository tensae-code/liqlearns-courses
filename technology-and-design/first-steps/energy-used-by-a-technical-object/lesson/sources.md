Scope: Gabon's secured books are the combined *Super Efficace* Sciences teacher guides (Biologie / Sciences physiques / Technologie / TIC in one volume). Only the Technologie strand is stocked in this course; the Biologie and Sciences physiques strands belong to the Science course and the TIC strand to the ICT course.

Gabon's own way: Gabon returns to energy twice: Grade 3 teaches sources and transformation (the bicycle, sailboat and vehicle as the three worked examples), and Grade 5 re-teaches the forms of energy through daily-life examples such as solar panels making electricity \u2014 the spiral is Gabon's own way and is kept in this lesson's two provenance rows.
