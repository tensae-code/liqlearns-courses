# Hands-on \u2014 Energy used by a technical object

Real practice \u2014 this subject is learned by doing.

## Energy labels
Walk through your home or school and label five working objects with the form of energy each one uses. Bring the list back and sort it by form.

## Source sorting
Write eight sources on cards: oil, gas, coal, wood, wind, sunlight, moving water, and one of your own. Sort them into renewable and non-renewable piles and defend each choice to a partner.
