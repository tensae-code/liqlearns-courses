# Energy used by a technical object \u2014 multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which source is renewable?
a) Coal b) Oil c) Wind d) Gas
**Answer: c) Wind**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which list is all non-renewable?
a) Sunlight and moving water b) Oil, gas and coal c) Wind and sunlight d) Moving water and wind
**Answer: b) Oil, gas and coal**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A vehicle burning fuel and moving is changing\u2026
a) Light into sound b) Chemical fuel energy into movement c) Movement into coal d) Heat into paper
**Answer: b) Chemical fuel energy into movement**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A sailboat is moved by\u2026
a) Muscular energy b) Electrical energy c) Wind energy d) Coal
**Answer: c) Wind energy**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Solar panels turn light energy into\u2026
a) Coal b) Electricity c) Wood d) Movement of water
**Answer: b) Electricity**
