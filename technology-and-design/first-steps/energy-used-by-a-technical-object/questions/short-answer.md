# Energy used by a technical object \u2014 short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name two renewable and two non-renewable energy sources.
**Answer:** Renewable: wind and sunlight (or moving water). Non-renewable: oil and gas (or coal, wood).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A lamp is switched on. What energy change happens?
**Answer:** Electrical energy changes into light (and heat) \u2014 electricity is the energy the lamp uses to do its work.
