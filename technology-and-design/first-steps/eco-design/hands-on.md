# Hands-on \u2014 Eco-design

Real practice \u2014 this subject is learned by doing.

## Three-question audit
Choose three objects. For each, write the three eco-design answers: the need it answers, its materials, its likely end of life. Mark the object whose answers worry you most, and say why.

## Prototype plan
For one simple object, sketch a prototype plan: what the first version will be made of, what you will test, and what impact question it must answer (repairable? recoverable materials?).
