# Eco-design \u2014 multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** When does eco-design think about impacts?
a) Only after the object breaks b) From the very start of designing c) Only at the shop d) Never
**Answer: b) From the very start of designing**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which is an eco-design question?
a) What colour is fashionable? b) What need does it truly answer, and what will become of its materials? c) Who is the richest buyer? d) How fast can it be sold?
**Answer: b) What need does it truly answer, and what will become of its materials?**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is a prototype?
a) The final object for sale b) A first object built to test the idea c) A drawing frame d) A kind of glue
**Answer: b) A first object built to test the idea**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Besides working, what does an eco-design prototype test?
a) The price tag b) Whether it can be repaired and its materials recovered c) The maker's speed d) The shop's size
**Answer: b) Whether it can be repaired and its materials recovered**

**5. (Sub lesson 1)** Why make impact choices before cutting material?
a) Choices made early do less harm at every stage b) Cutting is forbidden c) Impacts do not matter d) Paper is stronger than wood
**Answer: a) Choices made early do less harm at every stage**
