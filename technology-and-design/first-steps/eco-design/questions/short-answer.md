# Eco-design \u2014 short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the three questions eco-design asks from the start.
**Answer:** What need does the object answer? What materials will it take? What happens at the end of its life?

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does a prototype save?
**Answer:** Material and mistakes \u2014 it tests the idea, including repairability and material recovery, at small cost before the real make.
