# Evolution of technical systems — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name a family and its function of use.
**Answer:** Example: the lamp family — its function of use is to light a space; generations run from oil lamp to filament bulb to LED.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** For the electric vehicle, name one thing that stayed the same and two that changed.
**Answer:** Stayed: carrying people by road. Changed: electrical energy stored in a battery instead of fuel; an electric motor and electronic control instead of a combustion engine.
