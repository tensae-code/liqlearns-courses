# Evolution of technical systems — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Objects belong to one family when they share…
a) the same function of use b) the same colour c) the same factory d) the same price
**Answer: a) the same function of use**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Across generations of a family, what stays the same?
a) The function of use b) The energy source, always c) The materials, always d) Nothing at all
**Answer: a) The function of use**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** In the electric vehicle, the petrol engine is replaced by…
a) an electric motor fed by a battery b) a bigger fuel tank c) a horse d) a steam boiler only
**Answer: a) an electric motor fed by a battery**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Evolution of a system is driven by…
a) needs, available technology and constraints such as cost and pollution b) fashion alone c) the maker's mood d) nothing — systems never change
**Answer: a) needs, available technology and constraints such as cost and pollution**

**5. (Sub lesson 1)** A family is defined by its…
a) function, not its shape b) shape, not its function c) age only d) country of sale only
**Answer: a) function, not its shape**
