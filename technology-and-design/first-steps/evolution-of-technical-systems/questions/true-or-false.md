# Evolution of technical systems — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Two objects that look completely different can belong to the same family.
**Answer: True** — A family is defined by the shared function of use, not by appearance.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The electric vehicle has a different function of use from the petrol car.
**Answer: False** — Both carry people and goods by road; the principles and energy chain changed, the function stayed.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Each generation in a family must keep every part of the previous one.
**Answer: False** — Generations keep the function and improve principles, energy, materials or control.
