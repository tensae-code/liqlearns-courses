# Lesson plan — Evolution of technical systems

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** First Steps · **Lesson:** Evolution of technical systems
**Place in the curriculum:** First Steps (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Morocco expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** technical systems evolve in families: objects that share the same function of use form a family, and each generation answers the same need better — the electric vehicle is the running case.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on teacher-guide evidence only — Morocco's Technologie teacher guides (Ministère de l'Éducation Nationale, du Préscolaire et des Sports, Royaume du Maroc) are the books in hand for this topic (see lesson/sources.md). Teacher guides are evidence, counted nowhere in the acceptance count. The pupil manuals themselves are not in hand; the placements are filed from the guides' answer elements (Part 5) as guide evidence.

Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **Families of objects sharing a function of use** — Objects that render the same service — the same function of use — belong to one family, however different they look. Inside a family, generations succeed each other: each keeps the function and improves the principles, the energy, the materials or the control.
2. **The electric vehicle as the running case** — Morocco's Grade 9 course follows one family in full: road vehicles, ending with the electric vehicle — its motor, its battery and its charging — to show how a system evolves when its energy source and its control change while its function stays.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of First Steps. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say what makes objects one family.
2. Order the generations of a familiar family and name what improved.
3. Explain, on the electric vehicle, what stayed the same and what changed.
## What you need
Pictures of one object family across time (lamps, phones or vehicles), and paper.
