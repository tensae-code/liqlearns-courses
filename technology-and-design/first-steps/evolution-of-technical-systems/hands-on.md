# Hands-on — Evolution of technical systems

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## Family timeline
Choose a family — lighting, telephones, washing clothes. Draw four generations in order. Under each, write what improved. Above all four, write the shared function of use.

## Same or changed?
For the electric vehicle versus the petrol car, make two lists: 'stayed the same' (function, roads, passengers) and 'changed' (energy stored, motor, control, emissions). Present your lists to a partner.
