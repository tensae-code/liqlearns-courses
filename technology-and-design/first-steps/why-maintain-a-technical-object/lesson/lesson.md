# Why maintain a technical object

## Do it first
Think of two similar objects you know: one cared for, one neglected \u2014 two bicycles, two tools, two bags. Which still works well? The difference between them is this lesson's subject.

## Sub lesson 1 \u2014 What maintenance does
A technical object does not stay new by itself. Maintenance is the regular care that keeps it in working order: cleaning, checking, adjusting, replacing small worn parts before they fail. A maintained object keeps doing its job; a neglected one fades, loosens and stops. Maintenance also lengthens the object's life and keeps its appearance \u2014 a cared-for thing serves more years and looks like it.

## Sub lesson 2 \u2014 Maintenance is safety
For some objects, maintenance is not only about the object. A bicycle with worn brakes or a loose chain is a danger to its rider. There, maintenance keeps the user safe: the check you make at home prevents the failure on the road. Whenever an object's failure could hurt someone, maintenance is a safety duty, not a chore.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: maintenance keeps an object working, makes it last longer and keeps its appearance \u2014 and for a bicycle, it keeps the rider safe. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
