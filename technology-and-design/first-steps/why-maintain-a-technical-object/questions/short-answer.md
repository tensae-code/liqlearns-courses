# Why maintain a technical object \u2014 short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name three things maintenance does for an object.
**Answer:** It keeps it in working order, lengthens its life, and keeps its appearance.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Tell maintenance from repair in one sentence.
**Answer:** Maintenance is regular care before failure; repair fixes an object after something has broken.
