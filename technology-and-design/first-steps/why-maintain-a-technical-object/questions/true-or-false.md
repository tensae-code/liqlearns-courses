# Why maintain a technical object \u2014 true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Maintenance lengthens an object's life.
**Answer: True** \u2014 Kept objects serve more years \u2014 one of the gains the guide names.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Appearance has nothing to do with maintenance.
**Answer: False** \u2014 Keeping its appearance is one of the three gains named in the guide.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A bicycle's brakes can be left unchecked if the bicycle still moves.
**Answer: False** \u2014 Worn brakes are a danger to the rider; checking them is a safety duty.
