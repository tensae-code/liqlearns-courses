# What design is — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Reka bentuk (design) is the pattern and form of a product that is designed.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A designed product has nothing to do with this lesson.
**Answer: False** — It is part of the lesson: a product given its pattern and form on purpose.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Why design matters is it meets different needs and tastes and makes daily life easier.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.
