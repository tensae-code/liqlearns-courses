# What design is — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is reka bentuk (design)?
a) the pattern and form of a product that is designed b) Only a price c) Only decoration d) Nothing to do with this lesson
**Answer: a) the pattern and form of a product that is designed**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is a designed product?
a) Only decoration b) a product given its pattern and form on purpose c) Only a price d) A kind of glue
**Answer: b) a product given its pattern and form on purpose**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is why design matters?
a) Only history b) Only painting c) it meets different needs and tastes and makes daily life easier d) Only storage
**Answer: c) it meets different needs and tastes and makes daily life easier**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson teach, in one line?
a) design (reka bentuk) is the pattern and form of a product — it matters because it meets different needs and makes daily life easier. b) Only prices c) Only old tools d) Nothing practical
**Answer: a) design (reka bentuk) is the pattern and form of a product — it matters because it meets different needs and makes daily life easier.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** How does Malaysia's book teach this?
a) As theory only, never practised b) Only in the glossary c) Only as an exam d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Malay (RBT)
**Answer: d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Malay (RBT)**
