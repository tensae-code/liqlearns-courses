# What design is

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Malaysia's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Design is the pattern and form of a product
Design — reka bentuk — is the pattern and form given to a product that is designed. Malaysia shows it everywhere: in clothing, in glasses, in cakes and in everyday products, each shaped on purpose.

## Sub lesson 2 — Why design matters
Design matters because people differ: a good design meets different needs and tastes, and makes work and daily life easier. The same product, designed differently, serves a different life.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: design (reka bentuk) is the pattern and form of a product — it matters because it meets different needs and makes daily life easier. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
