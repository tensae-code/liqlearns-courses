# Careers in design and technology — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** An industrial designer is a designer of manufactured products.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A service designer has nothing to do with this lesson.
**Answer: False** — It is part of the lesson: a designer of services and how they are experienced.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A career’s object is the thing a given design career designs — vehicles, lamps, interiors, services.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.
