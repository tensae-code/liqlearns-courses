# Careers in design and technology — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is an industrial designer?
a) a designer of manufactured products b) Only a price c) Only decoration d) Nothing to do with this lesson
**Answer: a) a designer of manufactured products**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is a service designer?
a) Only decoration b) a designer of services and how they are experienced c) Only a price d) A kind of glue
**Answer: b) a designer of services and how they are experienced**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is a career’s object?
a) Only history b) Only painting c) the thing a given design career designs — vehicles, lamps, interiors, services d) Only storage
**Answer: c) the thing a given design career designs — vehicles, lamps, interiors, services**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson teach, in one line?
a) design and technology open fourteen named careers, each defined by what it designs. b) Only prices c) Only old tools d) Nothing practical
**Answer: a) design and technology open fourteen named careers, each defined by what it designs.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** How does Malaysia's book teach this?
a) As theory only, never practised b) Only in the glossary c) Only as an exam d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Malay (RBT)
**Answer: d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Malay (RBT)**
