# Careers in design and technology

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Malaysia's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Fourteen careers
Tingkatan 1 names fourteen design-and-technology careers — architect, automotive designer, engineering designer, graphic designer, fashion designer, game designer, food presentation designer, industrial designer, instructional designer, interior designer, landscape architecture, lamp designer, product designer and service designer — and what each does.

## Sub lesson 2 — What each career designs
Each career is defined by its object: vehicles, games, food presentation, lamps, interiors, landscapes, services. The list teaches that designing is one skill aimed at many targets.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: design and technology open fourteen named careers, each defined by what it designs. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
