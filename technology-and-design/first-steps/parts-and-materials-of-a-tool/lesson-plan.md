# Lesson plan \u2014 Parts and materials of a tool

**Course:** Technology and Design \u00b7 **Title:** First Steps \u00b7 **Lesson:** Parts and materials of a tool
**Place in the curriculum:** First Steps (CURRICULUM.md) \u2014 stocked in the Gabon expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** a tool has parts \u2014 a handle for grip and a working edge that does the job \u2014 and each part's material is chosen for a reason, like metal for strength.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on teacher-guide evidence only \u2014 Gabon's *Super Efficace* Sciences teacher guides (EDIG / \u00c9dicef, Institut P\u00e9dagogique National, 2024) are the books in hand for this topic (see lesson/sources.md). Teacher guides are evidence, counted nowhere in the acceptance count.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on \u2014 an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of \u2026" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **The parts of a tool** \u2014 Handle and working edge: one part you hold, one part that works.
2. **Why materials are chosen** \u2014 A hammer's blade must be metal for strength; handles come in wood, plastic or metal.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson \u2014 no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of First Steps. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name a tool's parts: handle and working edge.
2. Say why a hammer's working part must be metal.
3. Ask of any part: what material, and why?
## What you need
A hammer or similar hand tool to examine.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** \u2014 the opening doing task in lesson/lesson.md.
2. **The explanation** \u2014 the sub lessons in order (lesson/lesson.md).
   - ADD IMAGE: a picture of a picture of a hammer with its two parts labelled \u2014 handle and head \u2014 and a small card under each naming its material \u2014 the student reads the cards and says which part does the work.
   - ADD GIF: a GIF of a GIF of a wooden hammer head striking a nail and cracking, then a metal head striking cleanly \u2014 the student says why the second one works.
   - ADD AUDIO: a voice reading the two parts aloud \u2014 handle for grip, working edge for the job \u2014 the student repeats them on a real tool.

6. **Do It Yourself** \u2014 the closing doing block in lesson/lesson.md.
