# Hands-on \u2014 Parts and materials of a tool

Real practice \u2014 this subject is learned by doing.

## Part survey
Examine three tools. For each, draw the tool, label its parts, and write the material of each part. Add one sentence: why is that part made of that material?

## Material test talk
Hold a hammer. Explain to a partner why the head is metal and not wood, and why the handle could be wood, plastic or metal. Swap tools and repeat.
