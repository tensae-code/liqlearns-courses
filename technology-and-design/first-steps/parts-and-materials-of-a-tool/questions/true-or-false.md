# Parts and materials of a tool \u2014 true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Every part of a tool does the same job.
**Answer: False** \u2014 The handle is for grip and the working edge does the work \u2014 the parts have different jobs.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Materials in a tool are chosen by chance.
**Answer: False** \u2014 Materials are chosen for reasons \u2014 metal where strength and resistance are needed.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A handle can be made of plastic.
**Answer: True** \u2014 Handles come in wood, plastic or metal.
