# Parts and materials of a tool \u2014 multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the handle of a tool for?
a) Cutting b) Grip \u2014 the part you hold c) Decoration only d) Measuring
**Answer: b) Grip \u2014 the part you hold**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the working edge?
a) The part that meets the material and does the job b) The part you hold c) The tool's box d) The tool's name
**Answer: a) The part that meets the material and does the job**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Why must a hammer's blade be metal?
a) Metal is cheaper b) For strength and resistance c) Metal looks better d) Wood is too light to hold
**Answer: b) For strength and resistance**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Handles can be made of\u2026
a) Only glass b) Wood, plastic or metal c) Only paper d) Only stone
**Answer: b) Wood, plastic or metal**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** When judging a tool, what do you ask about each part?
a) Who sold it? b) What material is it, and why that one? c) How old is it? d) What colour is it?
**Answer: b) What material is it, and why that one?**
