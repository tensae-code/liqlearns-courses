# Parts and materials of a tool \u2014 short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the two main parts of a tool and the job of each.
**Answer:** The handle \u2014 for grip, the part you hold; the working edge \u2014 the part that meets the material and does the job.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why would a wooden hammer head fail?
**Answer:** The working part needs strength and resistance; wood would crack or fail at the blow, so the head is made of metal.
