**Acceptance count: taught in 3 countries (Iran, Malaysia, Mongolia).**

Malaysia's own way: Malaysia teaches invention through national examples — the pendrive of Datuk Pua Khein Seng, the wau bulan logo, Putra Mosque geometry.

Iran's own way: Iran teaches invention from nature first — inventions inspired by creation, burdock leading to hook-and-loop fastening — beside Iranian inventors, with forced-relationship and brainstorming as its creativity techniques (Grade 7, Module 1).

Mongolia's own way: Mongolia teaches this inside Design Technology (Дизайн технологи) — how Velcro, the sticky note and the headphones were invented, and the work of the Wright brothers, Edison, Bell and Mongolia's З.Түмэн-Өлзий. The Mongolian classroom's own examples named in the provenance rows above are part of the teaching, not decoration.
