# Inventors and inventions

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Malaysia's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Invention and innovation defined
Invention creates what did not exist; innovation improves and renews what exists, in design. Malaysia defines both, then applies their principles inside design careers.

## Sub lesson 2 — Malaysian examples
The book proves the ideas at home: the Malaysia Airlines wau bulan logo innovation, the Putra Mosque’s geometric design, and the pendrive invented by Datuk Pua Khein Seng — a Malaysian invention the whole world uses.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: invention creates the new; innovation renews the existing — and Malaysia teaches both through Malaysian examples, including the pendrive. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
