# Inventors and inventions — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is invention?
a) creating what did not exist before b) Only a price c) Only decoration d) Nothing to do with this lesson
**Answer: a) creating what did not exist before**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is innovation?
a) Only decoration b) improving and renewing what already exists c) Only a price d) A kind of glue
**Answer: b) improving and renewing what already exists**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is the pendrive?
a) Only history b) Only painting c) the portable storage device invented by Datuk Pua Khein Seng d) Only storage
**Answer: c) the portable storage device invented by Datuk Pua Khein Seng**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson teach, in one line?
a) invention creates the new; innovation renews the existing — and Malaysia teaches both through Malaysian examples, including the pendrive. b) Only prices c) Only old tools d) Nothing practical
**Answer: a) invention creates the new; innovation renews the existing — and Malaysia teaches both through Malaysian examples, including the pendrive.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** How does Malaysia's book teach this?
a) As theory only, never practised b) Only in the glossary c) Only as an exam d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Malay (RBT)
**Answer: d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Malay (RBT)**
