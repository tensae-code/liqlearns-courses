# Giving an object a second life (making from pallets)

## Do it first
Look at something thrown away or stored unused \u2014 a crate, a pallet, an old board. Say what new object you can see in it. That seeing is the start of this lesson.

## Sub lesson 1 \u2014 Reuse as a making project
Recycling and reuse stop being slogans when they become a project. A wooden pallet has finished its first life carrying goods; its wood is still sound. The maker who sees a bench in a pallet is practising reuse with hands, not words: material saved, waste avoided, and a new object gained at small cost.

## Sub lesson 2 \u2014 Building a bench from pallets
The bench is built from reclaimed pallets with the ordinary tools of making: saw to cut the pallet wood to size, hammer and nails, screws and screwdriver for the joinings that must hold weight. The making run still applies \u2014 gather (the pallets), prepare (clean, check for bad boards), make, finish \u2014 and the finished bench is tested the honest way: by sitting on it.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: reuse becomes a real make: reclaimed pallets, worked with saw, hammer, nails, screws and screwdriver, become a bench \u2014 an object given a second life. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.

## Do It Yourself
1. Do the Do it first task again, carefully this time, using everything the lesson taught.
2. Check your work against the lesson's own checks \u2014 fix one thing you find.
3. Keep your work: it is practice evidence, and the next lesson builds on it.
