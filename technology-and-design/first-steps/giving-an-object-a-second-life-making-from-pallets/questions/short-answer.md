# Giving an object a second life (making from pallets) \u2014 short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What two things are gained when a pallet becomes a bench?
**Answer:** Material is saved and waste avoided \u2014 and a new, useful object is gained at small cost.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the making stages as they apply to the bench.
**Answer:** Gather the pallets, prepare (clean and check the boards), make the bench, finish it \u2014 then test it by sitting.
