# Giving an object a second life (making from pallets) \u2014 multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is a pallet's second life in this lesson?
a) Firewood b) A bench c) A boat d) A bookshelf of glass
**Answer: b) A bench**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Reuse as a making project means\u2026
a) Talking about waste only b) Building a new object from old material c) Buying new wood d) Hiding old material
**Answer: b) Building a new object from old material**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Which tool list fits the bench build?
a) Saw, hammer, nails, screws, screwdriver b) Needle and thread c) Brush and paint only d) Ruler alone
**Answer: a) Saw, hammer, nails, screws, screwdriver**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** What is the honest test of the finished bench?
a) Its colour b) Sitting on it c) Its price d) Its weight
**Answer: b) Sitting on it**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** In the making run, gathered material here is\u2026
a) New timber from a shop b) The reclaimed pallets c) Plastic bottles d) Cork pieces
**Answer: b) The reclaimed pallets**
