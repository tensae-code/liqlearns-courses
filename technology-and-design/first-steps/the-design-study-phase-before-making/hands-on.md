# Hands-on \u2014 The design/study phase before making

Real practice \u2014 this subject is learned by doing.

## Study sheet
Choose a small object to make. Write its study sheet: need, function, dimensions, materials, and the making steps in order. Add one question each step raises.

## Swap and check
Exchange study sheets with a partner. Check: is the need stated? Are dimensions fixed? Could you start making from this sheet alone? Report one gap you find.
