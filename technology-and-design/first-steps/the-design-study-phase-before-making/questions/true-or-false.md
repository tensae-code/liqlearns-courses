# The design/study phase before making \u2014 true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The study phase happens after the object is made.
**Answer: False** \u2014 The study phase comes before making \u2014 it settles need, dimensions, materials and steps.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Changing your mind costs nothing during the study phase.
**Answer: True** \u2014 On paper, a change is an eraser stroke; at the bench, it is wasted material.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Materials are chosen during the making, by chance.
**Answer: False** \u2014 Materials are chosen in the study phase, part by part, on purpose.
