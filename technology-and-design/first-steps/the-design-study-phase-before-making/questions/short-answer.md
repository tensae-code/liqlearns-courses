# The design/study phase before making \u2014 short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the five things the study phase settles.
**Answer:** The need, the function, the dimensions, the materials, and the making steps in order.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why fix dimensions on paper instead of at the bench?
**Answer:** So every part is decided before work begins and the object does not surprise the maker at the bench.
