# The design/study phase before making \u2014 multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What comes first, the study phase or the making?
a) The making b) The study phase c) They never meet d) Selling
**Answer: b) The study phase**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What do you state first in the study phase?
a) The price b) The need and the function c) The colour only d) The maker's name
**Answer: b) The need and the function**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Fixing dimensions means\u2026
a) Guessing sizes at the bench b) Deciding how long, wide and thick before work begins c) Making it as big as possible d) Skipping measurements
**Answer: b) Deciding how long, wide and thick before work begins**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** When do you list the making steps?
a) After finishing b) In the study phase, in order c) Never d) During selling
**Answer: b) In the study phase, in order**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Why ask each step's questions during the study phase?
a) Questions are cheaper before you cut b) Questions slow the work c) Steps have no questions d) Only teachers ask questions
**Answer: a) Questions are cheaper before you cut**
