# The design/study phase before making

## Do it first
Think of a simple object you could make \u2014 a small box, a sieve, a toy. Without touching any material, answer aloud: What need does it answer? How big will it be? You have begun the study phase.

## Sub lesson 1 \u2014 State the need and fix the size
Making starts before making. In the design or study phase, you state the need the object answers and the function it must perform. Then you fix its dimensions \u2014 how long, how wide, how thick \u2014 so every part is decided before work begins. An object whose size is fixed on paper does not surprise you at the bench.

## Sub lesson 2 \u2014 Choose materials and list the steps
Still in the study phase, you choose the materials, part by part, and you list the making steps in order. Each step raises questions \u2014 what tool? what could go wrong? \u2014 and the study phase is where you ask them, while changing your mind still costs nothing. Only when need, dimensions, materials and steps are settled does the making begin.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: before making comes the study: state the need and the function, fix the dimensions, choose the materials, and list the making steps \u2014 with the questions each step raises. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.

## Do It Yourself
1. Do the Do it first task again, carefully this time, using everything the lesson taught.
2. Check your work against the lesson's own checks \u2014 fix one thing you find.
3. Keep your work: it is practice evidence, and the next lesson builds on it.
