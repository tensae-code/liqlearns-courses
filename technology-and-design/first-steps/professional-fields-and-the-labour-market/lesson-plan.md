# Lesson plan — Professional fields and the labour market

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** First Steps · **Lesson:** Professional fields and the labour market
**Place in the curriculum:** First Steps (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Moldova expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** there are over 6,000 known professions, grouped into professional fields, and the labour market keeps evolving — choosing a field means reading those trends.
**This lesson's own level:** stocked from Moldova's *Educație tehnologică* (Technological Education) textbooks — see lesson/sources.md. Moldova counts in the acceptance count (student books, not evidence-only).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **The diversity of professions and their fields** — Moldova's Grade 9 book opens career orientation with scale: over 6,000 professions are known.
2. **Trends in the labour market** — The book then reads the labour market itself: how it evolves, which work grows and which fades, and what trends mean for a learner choosing now.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of First Steps. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Describe the diversity of professions and how they group into fields.
2. Read a labour-market trend and say what it means for choosing a profession.
3. Explain how Moldova's book teaches it.
## What you need
Paper for a fields map, and access to examples of local jobs.
