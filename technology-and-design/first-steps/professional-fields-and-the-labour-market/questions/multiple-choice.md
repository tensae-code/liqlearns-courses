# Professional fields and the labour market — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How many professions does the book say are known?
a) Over 6,000 b) About 60 c) About 600 d) Exactly 100
**Answer: a) Over 6,000**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** How are professions organised for choosing?
a) By salary only b) Into professional fields — families of related work c) By alphabet only d) By town
**Answer: b) Into professional fields — families of related work**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** In what order is the choice made?
a) Field first, profession second b) Profession first, field never c) At random d) By family tradition only
**Answer: a) Field first, profession second**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the book study about the labour market?
a) Its evolution and trends — which work grows and which fades b) Only its history c) Only foreign markets d) Nothing — markets never change
**Answer: a) Its evolution and trends — which work grows and which fades**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Where does career orientation sit in Moldova's course?
a) In a separate school b) Only after graduation c) Inside the technology subject d) Nowhere
**Answer: c) Inside the technology subject**
