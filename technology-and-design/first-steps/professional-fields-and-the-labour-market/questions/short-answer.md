# Professional fields and the labour market — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why group professions into fields?
**Answer:** So the choice can be made field first and profession second, instead of comparing thousands of single professions.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why study labour-market trends when choosing?
**Answer:** Because the market evolves — growing work and fading work point the choice in different directions.
