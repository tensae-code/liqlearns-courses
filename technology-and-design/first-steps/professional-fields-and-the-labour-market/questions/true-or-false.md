# Professional fields and the labour market — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** There are over 6,000 known professions.
**Answer: True** — The book opens career orientation with that scale.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A learner must compare all 6,000 professions one by one to choose.
**Answer: False** — Professions group into fields; the choice is field first, profession second.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The labour market stays still, so trends do not matter.
**Answer: False** — The book studies the market's evolution and trends precisely because it changes.
