# Professional fields and the labour market

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Moldova's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — The diversity of professions and their fields
Moldova's Grade 9 book opens career orientation with scale: over 6,000 professions are known. No one chooses among 6,000 one by one — professions group into professional fields, families of work that share tools, materials and settings, and the choice is made field first, profession second.

## Sub lesson 2 — Trends in the labour market
The book then reads the labour market itself: how it evolves, which work grows and which fades, and what trends mean for a learner choosing now. Career orientation sits inside the technology subject because technology is where the fields are met with the hands.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: there are over 6,000 known professions, grouped into professional fields, and the labour market keeps evolving — choosing a field means reading those trends. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
