# What technology is

## Do it first
Look around the room you are in. Point to three things that help people do a job — something that heats, something that carries messages, something that helps you learn. For each one, say the job it does. Those helpers are technology.

## Sub lesson 1 — What technology means
Technology is using science to do practical tasks or solve problems. It often works through machines or tools, but the heart of it is the solving: someone has a job to do or a problem to fix, and knowledge is put to work to do it. A snowmobile, a stove, a computer and a sewing machine are all technology, because each one puts knowledge to work on a real task.

## Sub lesson 2 — Where technology is used and what it helps us do
Technology is used in the places of everyday community life: at home, at school, at the store and at the health centre. It helps us find information, communicate with each other, create things, travel, heat our homes, stay healthy and make clothing. When you spot a machine or a tool doing one of those jobs, you are looking at technology at work.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: technology is using science to do practical tasks or solve problems, often with machines or tools — and it is at work all around the community, at home, at school, at the store and at the health centre. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
