# Hands-on — What technology is

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## Technology hunt
Walk around your home or classroom with a partner. Find five examples of technology. For each one, say: what it is, and what job or problem it handles. Count how many different helping jobs your five examples cover.

## Draw your community
Draw four places — home, school, store, health centre. In each place, draw one piece of technology and label the job it does.
