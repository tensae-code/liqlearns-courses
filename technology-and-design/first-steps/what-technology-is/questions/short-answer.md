# What technology is — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Say in one sentence what technology is.
**Answer:** Technology is using science to do practical tasks or solve problems, often with machines or tools.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name three things technology helps people do.
**Answer:** Any three of: find information, communicate, create, travel, heat homes, stay healthy, make clothing.
