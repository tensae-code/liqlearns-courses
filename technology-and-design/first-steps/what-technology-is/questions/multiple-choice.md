# What technology is — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is technology?
a) Using science to do practical tasks or solve problems b) Only computers c) Anything old d) A kind of weather
**Answer: a) Using science to do practical tasks or solve problems**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Technology often works through what?
a) Machines or tools b) Dreams c) Songs only d) Nothing at all
**Answer: a) Machines or tools**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Which is a place where technology is used?
a) The health centre b) Nowhere c) Only in stories d) Under the sea only
**Answer: a) The health centre**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which job can technology help with?
a) Heating homes b) Making people forget c) Stopping the sun d) Hiding information forever
**Answer: a) Heating homes**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** A snowmobile that carries a hunter across the snow is an example of technology helping people to…
a) travel b) sleep c) forget d) hide
**Answer: a) travel**
