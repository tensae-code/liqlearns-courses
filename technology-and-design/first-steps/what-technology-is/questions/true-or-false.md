# What technology is — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Technology is only computers.
**Answer: False** — Technology is any use of science to do practical tasks or solve problems — stoves, snowmobiles and sewing machines count too.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Technology helps people stay healthy, for example at the health centre.
**Answer: True** — Staying healthy is one of the jobs technology helps with.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Technology is used only at school.
**Answer: False** — It is used at home, at school, at the store and at the health centre.
