# Lesson plan — What technology is

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** First Steps · **Lesson:** What technology is
**Place in the curriculum:** First Steps (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Canada expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** technology is using science to do practical tasks or solve problems, often with machines or tools — and it is at work all around the community, at home, at school, at the store and at the health centre.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on a student reader — Canada's *Technology in the Community* (Tulliniliara Reading Series, Level 14), Government of Nunavut / Inhabit Education (see lesson/sources.md). It counts in the acceptance count (student book, not evidence-only).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **What technology means** — Technology is using science to do practical tasks or solve problems.
2. **Where technology is used and what it helps us do** — Technology is used in the places of everyday community life: at home, at school, at the store and at the health centre.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of First Steps. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say what technology is in your own words.
2. Name places in the community where technology is used.
3. Name things technology helps people do.
## What you need
Nothing special — eyes to look around the room and the community, and a partner to talk with.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — the opening doing task in lesson/lesson.md.
2. **The explanation** — the sub lessons in order (lesson/lesson.md).
   - ADD IMAGE: a picture of a picture of a small community scene — a home, a school, a store and a health centre — with one helpful machine or tool shining in each place — the student points to each place and names the job its technology does.
