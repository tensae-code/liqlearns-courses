# Hands-on \u2014 Joining and assembly in making

Real practice \u2014 this subject is learned by doing.

## Bracket check
Examine a framed object. Find how its corners are held. Draw one corner, label the fixing, and write whether it holds a right angle.

## Fix the mesh (talk-through or make)
Explain \u2014 or do \u2014 the fixing of a mesh to a frame: where do the screws or nails go, how many, and why there? Check by sifting or pressing gently.
