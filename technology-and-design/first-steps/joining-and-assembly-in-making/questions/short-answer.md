# Joining and assembly in making \u2014 short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the bracket's two jobs.
**Answer:** It keeps two parts at a right angle, and it strengthens the whole object.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why must a fixing be long enough to grip both parts?
**Answer:** So the two parts are held as one; a fixing that grips only one part cannot hold the joining under strain.
