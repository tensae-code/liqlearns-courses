# Joining and assembly in making \u2014 multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does an assembly bracket (\u00e9querre) keep?
a) The colour b) The right angle c) The price down d) The mesh loose
**Answer: b) The right angle**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Besides holding the angle, a bracket also\u2026
a) Weakens the object b) Strengthens the whole c) Cuts the wood d) Measures the parts
**Answer: b) Strengthens the whole**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** A right angle is\u2026
a) 45 degrees b) 90 degrees \u2014 square and true c) 180 degrees d) Any angle
**Answer: b) 90 degrees \u2014 square and true**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In the sieve, screws and nails fix\u2026
a) The mesh to the frame b) The frame to the wall c) The sand to the mesh d) Nothing
**Answer: a) The mesh to the frame**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** An object is only as strong as\u2026
a) Its paint b) Its joinings c) Its name d) Its box
**Answer: b) Its joinings**
