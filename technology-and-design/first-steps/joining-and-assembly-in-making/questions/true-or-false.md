# Joining and assembly in making \u2014 true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** An unbraced frame corner can fold or twist out of square.
**Answer: True** \u2014 That is why the bracket is added \u2014 it braces the corner.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Brackets are only for decoration.
**Answer: False** \u2014 Brackets work: they keep the right angle and strengthen the whole.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Any fixing, placed anywhere, holds equally well.
**Answer: False** \u2014 A fixing must suit its job and sit where the strain falls.
