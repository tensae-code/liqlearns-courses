# Biotechnology — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is biotechnology?
a) using living systems — cells, microbes, enzymes — as technology b) Only a price c) Only decoration d) Nothing to do with this lesson
**Answer: a) using living systems — cells, microbes, enzymes — as technology**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is cell?
a) Only history b) the smallest unit of living systems c) Only storage d) A kind of glue
**Answer: b) the smallest unit of living systems**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is biofuel?
a) Only old tools b) Only painting c) fuel made from waste or plant matter by living processes d) Only a price
**Answer: c) fuel made from waste or plant matter by living processes**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson teach, in one line?
a) Only decoration b) Nothing to do with this lesson c) Only history d) biotechnology uses living systems — cells, microbes and enzymes — as technology: to make fuels and products, to clean the environment, and to work the sea.
**Answer: d) biotechnology uses living systems — cells, microbes and enzymes — as technology: to make fuels and products, to clean the environment, and to work the sea.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** How does Iran's book teach this?
a) As theory only, never practised b) Only in a glossary c) Only as an exam d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Persian
