# Biotechnology

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Iran's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Living systems as technology
Biotechnology is technology whose working parts are alive. Its smallest unit is the cell: every living system is built of cells, and biotechnology directs what cells and microbes already do — ferment, digest, grow, transform — toward human purposes. Because the workers are alive, they copy themselves and run on simple feedstocks, which no machine tool can do.

## Sub lesson 2 — Clean energy and the sea
Iran's book works two fields in full. For clean energy: biofuels are made from waste and plant matter by living processes, set against fossil fuels that only deplete and pollute. For the sea: marine biotechnology pursues food, medicines and materials from sea organisms, and the protection of the sea itself. In both fields the living system is the factory.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: biotechnology uses living systems — cells, microbes and enzymes — as technology: to make fuels and products, to clean the environment, and to work the sea. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
