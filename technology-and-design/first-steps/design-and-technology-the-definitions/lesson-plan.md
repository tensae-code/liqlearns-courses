# Lesson plan — Design and technology — the definitions

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** First Steps · **Lesson:** Design and technology — the definitions
**Place in the curriculum:** First Steps (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Malaysia expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** design is the planned arrangement of materials for a product that affects life; technology applies science and mathematics with natural resources to ease human work.
**This lesson's own level:** stocked from Malaysia's *Reka Bentuk dan Teknologi* (RBT — Design and Technology) textbooks — see lesson/sources.md. Malaysia counts in the acceptance count (student books, not evidence-only).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **The two definitions** — Malaysia defines its subject at Tingkatan 1: design (reka bentuk) is the planned arrangement of materials to produce a product that affects life; technology is applying science and mathematics with natural resources to make tools, energy or systems that ease human work.
2. **Concrete and abstract fields** — The fields divide in two: concrete fields make things — manufacturing, mechanical, electrical, electronic, mechatronic, food, agriculture/fertigation, textiles/fashion — while abstract fields serve — services and communication.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of First Steps. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State both definitions in the book’s terms.
2. Sort fields into concrete and abstract, with examples.
3. Explain how Malaysia's book teaches it.
