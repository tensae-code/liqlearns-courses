# Design and technology — the definitions — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is design (reka bentuk)?
a) the planned arrangement of materials to produce a product that affects life b) Only a price c) Only decoration d) Nothing to do with this lesson
**Answer: a) the planned arrangement of materials to produce a product that affects life**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is technology?
a) Only decoration b) applying science and mathematics with natural resources to make tools, energy or systems that ease human work c) Only a price d) A kind of glue
**Answer: b) applying science and mathematics with natural resources to make tools, energy or systems that ease human work**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is a concrete field?
a) Only history b) Only painting c) a design-and-technology field that makes things — manufacturing, mechanical, food, fashion d) Only storage
**Answer: c) a design-and-technology field that makes things — manufacturing, mechanical, food, fashion**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson teach, in one line?
a) design is the planned arrangement of materials for a product that affects life; technology applies science and mathematics with natural resources to ease human work. b) Only prices c) Only old tools d) Nothing practical
**Answer: a) design is the planned arrangement of materials for a product that affects life; technology applies science and mathematics with natural resources to ease human work.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** How does Malaysia's book teach this?
a) As theory only, never practised b) Only in the glossary c) Only as an exam d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Malay (RBT)
**Answer: d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Malay (RBT)**
