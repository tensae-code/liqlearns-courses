# Design and technology — the definitions — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In your own words, what is design (reka bentuk)?
**Answer:** The planned arrangement of materials to produce a product that affects life.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In your own words, what is a concrete field?
**Answer:** A design-and-technology field that makes things — manufacturing, mechanical, food, fashion.
