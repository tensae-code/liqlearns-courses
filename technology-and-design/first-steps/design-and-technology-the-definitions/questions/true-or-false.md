# Design and technology — the definitions — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Design (reka bentuk) is the planned arrangement of materials to produce a product that affects life.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Technology has nothing to do with this lesson.
**Answer: False** — It is part of the lesson: applying science and mathematics with natural resources to make tools, energy or systems that ease human work.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A concrete field is a design-and-technology field that makes things — manufacturing, mechanical, food, fashion.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.
