# Design and technology — the definitions

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Malaysia's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — The two definitions
Malaysia defines its subject at Tingkatan 1: design (reka bentuk) is the planned arrangement of materials to produce a product that affects life; technology is applying science and mathematics with natural resources to make tools, energy or systems that ease human work.

## Sub lesson 2 — Concrete and abstract fields
The fields divide in two: concrete fields make things — manufacturing, mechanical, electrical, electronic, mechatronic, food, agriculture/fertigation, textiles/fashion — while abstract fields serve — services and communication. The definitions organise the whole subject.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: design is the planned arrangement of materials for a product that affects life; technology applies science and mathematics with natural resources to ease human work. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
