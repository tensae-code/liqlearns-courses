# Lesson plan — Technology as a system

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** First Steps · **Lesson:** Technology as a system
**Place in the curriculum:** First Steps (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Morocco expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** a technical system is described, not just looked at: functional and behavioural analysis says what it does and how it behaves, from several points of view, across its life cycle — from the expression of need to the functional specification (Cdcf).
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on teacher-guide evidence only — Morocco's Technologie teacher guides (Ministère de l'Éducation Nationale, du Préscolaire et des Sports, Royaume du Maroc) are the books in hand for this topic (see lesson/sources.md). Teacher guides are evidence, counted nowhere in the acceptance count. The pupil manuals themselves are not in hand; the placements are filed from the guides' answer elements (Part 5) as guide evidence.

Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **Describing a system — functional and behavioural analysis** — Functional analysis asks what a system does: its main function and the services it renders. Behavioural analysis asks how it behaves: what enters, what happens inside, what leaves, and how it reacts when conditions change. Together they describe a system completely without opening it.
2. **Points of view and the life cycle — from need to the Cdcf** — A system is seen differently by its user, its maker and its maintainer — each point of view reveals different functions. Across its life cycle the system is needed, specified, made, used, maintained and retired; the functional specification — the cahier des charges fonctionnel (Cdcf) — fixes what it must do before it is made.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of First Steps. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Describe a familiar system by its function and its behaviour (inputs, transformation, outputs).
2. Say how the user, maker and maintainer see the same system differently.
3. Put the life-cycle phases in order and say what the Cdcf fixes.
## What you need
A familiar technical object to describe, and paper for the description.
