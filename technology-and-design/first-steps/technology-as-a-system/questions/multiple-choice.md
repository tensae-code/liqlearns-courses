# Technology as a system — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Functional analysis of a system asks…
a) what the system does — its functions and services b) what colour it is painted c) who sold it d) how heavy the box is
**Answer: a) what the system does — its functions and services**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Behavioural analysis follows…
a) inputs, what happens inside, and outputs b) only the price c) only the outside shape d) the maker's holidays
**Answer: a) inputs, what happens inside, and outputs**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The Cdcf (functional specification) is written…
a) before making, fixing what the system must do b) after the system is thrown away c) only by the user, never checked d) instead of any need
**Answer: a) before making, fixing what the system must do**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which is a life-cycle phase of a system?
a) Maintenance during its use b) Dreaming about it c) Forgetting it exists d) Selling the specification only
**Answer: a) Maintenance during its use**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The maintainer's point of view stresses…
a) that the system stays repairable b) that it is never opened or checked c) the user's wishes only d) the colour of the manual
**Answer: a) that the system stays repairable**
