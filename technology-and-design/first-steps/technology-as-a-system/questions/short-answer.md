# Technology as a system — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Describe a kettle as a system in three short parts.
**Answer:** Function: heat water. Inputs: electrical energy and cold water. Behaviour/output: the water is heated and hot water leaves for use.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the life-cycle phases in order.
**Answer:** Expression of need, specification (Cdcf), design and making, use, maintenance, end of life.
