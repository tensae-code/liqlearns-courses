**Acceptance count: taught in 2 countries (Iran, Greece) — plus teacher-guide evidence only (Morocco), named here, counted nowhere.**

Morocco's own way: Morocco teaches Technologie in collège as a systems course — a technical object is read through its functions (power supply, detection, timing, switching, signalling, transmission), modelled with SysML against a functional specification (Cdcf), and the course closes with enterprise creation and a full design-and-build project. That systems-first, function-by-function way is carried in this lesson exactly as the guides teach it.

Iran's own way: Iran teaches systems in Grade 7 by analysing the electric fan as a system and comparing the systems of the human body, distinguishing natural from artificial technology products (Work & Technology G7, Module 1).

Greece's own way: Greece teaches this inside its Greek-language Gymnasio and EPAL technology books (Ministry of Education / ITYE Diophantos) — technology in the modern work environment, interrelation of systems, production and the economy, production and the environment, technology and society. The Greek classroom's own examples named in the provenance rows above are part of the teaching, not decoration.
