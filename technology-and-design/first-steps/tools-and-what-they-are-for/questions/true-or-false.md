# Tools and what they are for \u2014 true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A tool's shape follows the job it does.
**Answer: True** \u2014 Each trade's tools are shaped by its work \u2014 that is why the use is the way to learn them.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Tools exist only in workshops.
**Answer: False** \u2014 Daily life is full of tools \u2014 in kitchens, gardens and classrooms.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Guessing is a good way to choose a tool.
**Answer: False** \u2014 Choose a tool by its job: name what it is for, then pick it up.
