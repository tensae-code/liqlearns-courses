# Tools and what they are for \u2014 multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How is a tool best learned?
a) By its colour b) By what it is used for c) By its price d) By its weight alone
**Answer: b) By what it is used for**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which trade works with stone?
a) Tiler b) Leatherworker c) Stonemason d) Tailor
**Answer: c) Stonemason**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Which trade cuts, pierces and stitches leather?
a) Leatherworker b) Stonemason c) Tiler d) Mason
**Answer: a) Leatherworker**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Where else do you find tools besides workshops?
a) Nowhere b) In daily life \u2014 kitchens, gardens, classrooms c) Only in museums d) Only in pictures
**Answer: b) In daily life \u2014 kitchens, gardens, classrooms**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** A tool named with its job is a tool you can\u2026
a) Hide b) Choose on purpose c) Forget d) Break
**Answer: b) Choose on purpose**
