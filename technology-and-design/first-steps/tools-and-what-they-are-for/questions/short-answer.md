# Tools and what they are for \u2014 short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the three trades in this lesson and one job of each.
**Answer:** Stonemason \u2014 shapes and lays stone; tiler \u2014 cuts and sets tiles; leatherworker \u2014 cuts, pierces and stitches leather.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name two tools from daily life and what each is for.
**Answer:** Any two everyday tools named with their uses, e.g. a kitchen knife for cutting food and a garden hoe for working soil.
