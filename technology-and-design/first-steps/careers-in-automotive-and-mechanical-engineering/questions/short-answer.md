# Careers in automotive and mechanical engineering — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, or by doing the step named.

**1. (Sub lesson 3) Name two skills from the toy-car project and the full-scale job each one grows into.**
**Answer:** E.g. designing to a need → vehicle designer; understanding mechanisms/energy and working to specification → mechanical/automotive engineer (any two correct matches).

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Why does the guide teach safety inventors inside a careers lesson?**
**Answer:** Because safety is one of the industry's real callings: protecting drivers and passengers is design work, done through the same design process as any other product.
