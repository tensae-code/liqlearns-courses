# Careers in automotive and mechanical engineering — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Design and engineering jobs exist only for cars.**
**Answer: False — The guide names aircraft and boat designers and engineers as well.**

**2. (Sub lesson 3) Trimming an axle to specification is the same kind of skill engineers use at full scale.**
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Making vehicles safer is not part of the industry's work.**
**Answer: False — Safety-device inventors are named as key people in it.**
