# Careers in automotive and mechanical engineering — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Which jobs does the guide name for motorised transport?**
a) Only car salespeople b) Designers and engineers for motor vehicles, aircraft and boats c) Only drivers d) Only mechanics
**Answer: b) Designers and engineers for motor vehicles, aircraft and boats**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) A designer's main work is to…**
a) Sell the vehicle b) Shape the vehicle — turn the need into a design c) Drive the vehicle d) Fuel the vehicle
**Answer: b) Shape the vehicle — turn the need into a design**

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Inventors of safety devices are singled out because…**
a) Their work protects people — vehicles must be made safer, not only faster b) They are the best paid c) They work alone d) They draw the best
**Answer: a) Their work protects people — vehicles must be made safer, not only faster**

**4. (Sub lesson 3) The skills the automotive industry requires are…**
a) Unrelated to school projects b) The project's own skills: design, mechanisms, accuracy, safety c) Only computer skills d) Only strength
**Answer: b) The project's own skills: design, mechanisms, accuracy, safety**

**5. (Sub lesson 2) A safety device begins as…**
a) A law b) A design answer to a real problem, tested and evaluated c) An accident d) A decoration
**Answer: b) A design answer to a real problem, tested and evaluated**
