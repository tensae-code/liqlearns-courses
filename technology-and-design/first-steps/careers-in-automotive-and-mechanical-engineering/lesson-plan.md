# Lesson plan — Careers in automotive and mechanical engineering

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** First Steps · **Lesson:** Careers in automotive and mechanical engineering
**Place in the curriculum:** First Steps (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Jamaica expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** behind every vehicle are designers, engineers and inventors — for motor vehicles, aircraft and boats — and the automotive industry asks for design skill, mechanical understanding and safety thinking.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Jamaica's *National Standards Curriculum Guide — Grades 4–6 — Resource & Technology* (Ministry of Education, Youth and Information, Kingston, Jamaica, © 2018), a curriculum guide with full unit teaching content, read COMPLETE (see lesson/sources.md). It counts in the acceptance count (curriculum guide with full teaching content, not evidence-only).

## Sub lessons
1. **The people who design vehicles** — Motor vehicle, aircraft and boat designers and engineers turn needs into machines that move people safely.
2. **Inventors of safety devices** — Some of the industry's most important people invent safety: the devices that protect drivers, passengers and others on the road.
3. **The skills the industry requires** — Design skill, mechanical understanding, accuracy and safety thinking — the project's own skills — are what the automotive industry asks for.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of First Steps. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name design and engineering jobs in vehicle, aircraft and boat manufacturing.
2. Explain the place of safety-device inventors in the industry.
3. Name the skills the automotive industry requires and match them to project skills.
## What you need
Pictures of a car, an aircraft and a boat, paper and a pencil.
