# Hands-on — GPS technology

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## Three-part sketch
Draw the GPS system: the Earth, at least three satellites sending signals, one ground station, and a person holding a device. Label each of the three parts and its job.

## Who needs it and why
Pick two GPS users from the lesson — for example a hunter and a coast guard crew. For each, write one sentence: where they travel, and how GPS keeps them safe.
