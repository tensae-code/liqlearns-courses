# GPS technology — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does GPS stand for?
a) Global Positioning System b) Great Paper Sheets c) Ground Plane Service d) General Pilot School
**Answer: a) Global Positioning System**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What do the orbiting satellites do?
a) Send signals b) Carry passengers c) Heat the Earth d) Make maps on paper
**Answer: a) Send signals**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Why does a GPS device listen to several satellites?
a) Comparing several signals fixes its position b) One satellite is always broken c) More signals make it louder d) It does not need to
**Answer: a) Comparing several signals fixes its position**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Which of these people uses GPS?
a) A boater b) Nobody outdoors c) Only office workers d) Only children
**Answer: a) A boater**

**5. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the job of ground stations?
a) Tracking the satellites b) Selling devices c) Flying planes d) Catching fish
**Answer: a) Tracking the satellites**
