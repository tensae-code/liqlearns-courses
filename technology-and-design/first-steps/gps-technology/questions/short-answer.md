# GPS technology — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the three parts of the GPS system.
**Answer:** Orbiting satellites sending signals, ground stations tracking them, and portable GPS devices receiving the signals.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Name three kinds of workers who use GPS.
**Answer:** Any three of: hunters, boaters, pilots, fishers, prospectors, coast guard.
