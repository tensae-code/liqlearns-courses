# GPS technology — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** A GPS device can fix its position from a single satellite signal alone.
**Answer: False** — It needs signals from several satellites and compares them.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Ground stations track the satellites to keep the system accurate.
**Answer: True** — Tracking the satellites is the ground stations' job.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Pilots and fishers can both use GPS.
**Answer: True** — Both are named among GPS users.
