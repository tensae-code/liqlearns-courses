# Making a technical object \u2014 multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the correct order of the making stages?
a) Finish, gather, make, prepare b) Gather, prepare, make, finish c) Make, sell, gather, finish d) Prepare, finish, gather, make
**Answer: b) Gather, prepare, make, finish**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What happens in the finishing stage?
a) Materials are bought b) Edges are smoothed, joints checked, the object tested c) The need is stated d) The workplace is chosen
**Answer: b) Edges are smoothed, joints checked, the object tested**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A sieve is made of\u2026
a) Mesh and a frame b) Glass and wire c) Paper and glue only d) Cork and cardboard
**Answer: a) Mesh and a frame**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which is a use of a sieve?
a) Hammering nails b) Grading sand in masonry c) Cutting wood d) Lighting a room
**Answer: b) Grading sand in masonry**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** What are the checkerboard's pieces cut from?
a) Metal b) Glass c) Cork d) Stone
**Answer: c) Cork**
