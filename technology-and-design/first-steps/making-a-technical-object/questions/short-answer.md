# Making a technical object \u2014 short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the four making stages in order.
**Answer:** Gather materials and tools, prepare the workplace, make the object, finish it.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** What must come before gathering, in the checkerboard make?
**Answer:** The study phase \u2014 need, function, dimensions, materials and steps settled first.
