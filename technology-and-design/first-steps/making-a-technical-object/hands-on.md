# Hands-on \u2014 Making a technical object

Real practice \u2014 this subject is learned by doing.

## Make a sieve
With a frame and mesh, make a small sieve: join the frame true, fix the mesh with screws or nails, finish the edges. Test it by sifting sand or flour and report what passes and what stays.

## Make a checkerboard
Write the study sheet first (need, dimensions, materials, steps). Mark the board with ruler and set square, colour alternate squares, cut cork pieces, and play one game on your board.
