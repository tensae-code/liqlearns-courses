# Organising work in the workshop — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Work station (puesto de trabajo) is the arranged place where one worker performs their part of the process.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Work means has nothing to do with this lesson.
**Answer: False** — It is part of the lesson: the tools, instruments and equipment used in the work.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Work object is the material or article the work is performed on.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.
