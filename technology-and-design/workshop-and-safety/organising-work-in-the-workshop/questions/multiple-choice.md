# Organising work in the workshop — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is work station (puesto de trabajo)?
a) the arranged place where one worker performs their part of the process b) Only a price c) Only decoration d) Nothing to do with this lesson
**Answer: a) the arranged place where one worker performs their part of the process**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is work means?
a) Only history b) the tools, instruments and equipment used in the work c) Only storage d) A kind of glue
**Answer: b) the tools, instruments and equipment used in the work**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is work object?
a) Only old tools b) Only painting c) the material or article the work is performed on d) Only a price
**Answer: c) the material or article the work is performed on**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson teach, in one line?
a) Only decoration b) Nothing to do with this lesson c) Only history d) work is organised before it is done: every work process has its components — work objects, work means and human work — and the work station is arranged, with protection norms, so the components meet without waste or danger.
**Answer: d) work is organised before it is done: every work process has its components — work objects, work means and human work — and the work station is arranged, with protection norms, so the components meet without waste or danger.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** How does Cuba's book teach this?
a) As theory only, never practised b) Only in a glossary c) Only as an exam d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Spanish
**Answer: d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Spanish**
