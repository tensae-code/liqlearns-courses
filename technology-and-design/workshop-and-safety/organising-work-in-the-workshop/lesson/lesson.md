# Organising work in the workshop

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Cuba's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Components of the work process
Cuba's Grade 7 programme opens with the components (§§1.1–1.4): the work objects (what is worked on), the work means (tools and equipment), and human work itself. The 2023 book adds the characteristics of the school workshop as the place where the three meet.

## Sub lesson 2 — Organising the work station
The puesto de trabajo is arranged so the process flows: objects and means within reach, in order of use, and the protection norms (normas de protección) governing the station. Organisation is taught as content, examined like content.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: work is organised before it is done: every work process has its components — work objects, work means and human work — and the work station is arranged, with protection norms, so the components meet without waste or danger. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.

## Do It Yourself
1. Do the Do it first task again, carefully this time, using everything the lesson taught.
2. Check your work against the lesson's own checks — fix one thing you find.
3. Keep your work: it is practice evidence, and the next lesson builds on it.
