# Lesson plan — Organising work in the workshop

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Workshop and Safety · **Lesson:** Organising work in the workshop
**Place in the curriculum:** Workshop and Safety (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Cuba expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** work is organised before it is done: every work process has its components — work objects, work means and human work — and the work station is arranged, with protection norms, so the components meet without waste or danger.
**This lesson's own level:** stocked from Cuba's *Programa: Educación Laboral — 7mo grado* and the Cuba books in lesson/sources.md — see lesson/sources.md. Cuba counts in the acceptance count for its student-book placements; its programme and methodological-orientations placements are evidence only, counted nowhere.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **Components of the work process** — Cuba's Grade 7 programme opens with the components (§§1.1–1.4): the work objects (what is worked on), the work means (tools and equipment), and human work itself. The 2023 book adds the characteristics of the school workshop as the place where the three meet.
2. **Organising the work station** — The puesto de trabajo is arranged so the process flows: objects and means within reach, in order of use, and the protection norms (normas de protección) governing the station. Organisation is taught as content, examined like content.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
