# Keeping the workshop safe and tools maintained — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is storing tools?
a) putting every tool in its place after use b) Only a price c) Only decoration d) Nothing to do with this lesson
**Answer: a) putting every tool in its place after use**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is oiling scissors?
a) Only decoration b) cleaning and oiling scissors so they keep cutting truly c) Only a price d) A kind of glue
**Answer: b) cleaning and oiling scissors so they keep cutting truly**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is tool maintenance?
a) Only history b) Only painting c) small regular care that prevents failure and injury d) Only storage
**Answer: c) small regular care that prevents failure and injury**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson teach, in one line?
a) a kept workshop is a safe workshop: organise, clean, store — and maintain each tool with small regular care. b) Only prices c) Only old tools d) Nothing practical
**Answer: a) a kept workshop is a safe workshop: organise, clean, store — and maintain each tool with small regular care.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** How does Malaysia's book teach this?
a) As theory only, never practised b) Only in the glossary c) Only as an exam d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Malay (RBT)
**Answer: d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Malay (RBT)**
