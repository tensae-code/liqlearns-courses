# Keeping the workshop safe and tools maintained

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Malaysia's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Organising, cleaning and storing
A safe workshop is a kept workshop: work is organised, benches and floors are cleaned, and every tool is stored in its place so the next learner finds order, not hazards.

## Sub lesson 2 — Maintaining the tools
Tools are maintained, not just used: the book shows cleaning and oiling scissors so they keep cutting truly, and cleaning a keyboard so it keeps working. Small regular care prevents failure and injury.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: a kept workshop is a safe workshop: organise, clean, store — and maintain each tool with small regular care. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.

## Do It Yourself
1. Do the Do it first task again, carefully this time, using everything the lesson taught.
2. Check your work against the lesson's own checks — fix one thing you find.
3. Keep your work: it is practice evidence, and the next lesson builds on it.
