# Lesson plan — Keeping the workshop safe and tools maintained

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Workshop and Safety · **Lesson:** Keeping the workshop safe and tools maintained
**Place in the curriculum:** Workshop and Safety (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Malaysia expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** a kept workshop is a safe workshop: organise, clean, store — and maintain each tool with small regular care.
**This lesson's own level:** stocked from Malaysia's *Reka Bentuk dan Teknologi* (RBT — Design and Technology) textbooks — see lesson/sources.md. Malaysia counts in the acceptance count (student books, not evidence-only).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **Organising, cleaning and storing** — A safe workshop is a kept workshop: work is organised, benches and floors are cleaned, and every tool is stored in its place so the next learner finds order, not hazards.
2. **Maintaining the tools** — Tools are maintained, not just used: the book shows cleaning and oiling scissors so they keep cutting truly, and cleaning a keyboard so it keeps working.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of Workshop and Safety. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Describe how the workshop is organised, cleaned and stored.
2. Carry out the book maintenance examples and say why each matters.
3. Explain how Malaysia's book teaches it.
## What you need
Scissors, oil and a cloth; a keyboard to clean.
