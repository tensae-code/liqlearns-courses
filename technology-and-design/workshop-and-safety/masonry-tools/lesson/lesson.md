# Masonry tools — use, care and dangers

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Rwanda's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Examples of masonry tools
Straight after carpentry, Rwanda's Primary 5 book names the masonry tools: the builder's own kit, each tool with the work it does on a building site. Naming the examples is the entry — a learner who can point to the tool and name its job is ready to use it.

## Sub lesson 2 — Usage, maintenance and dangers
The book then teaches the usage and maintenance of the masonry tools together with their dangers, in one lesson — the same pattern as carpentry: a tool kept well and used rightly is the safe tool, and the dangers are named so they can be avoided.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: the masonry tools — the builder's kit for stone, brick and mortar — are learned the same way as the carpenter's: name them, use them, maintain them, and know their dangers. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
