# Masonry tools — use, care and dangers — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In what order does Rwanda teach the masonry tools?
**Answer:** First examples of the tools, then their usage and maintenance together with their dangers.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What pattern do carpentry and masonry share in the book?
**Answer:** Name the tools, teach their use, teach their maintenance, and name their dangers.
