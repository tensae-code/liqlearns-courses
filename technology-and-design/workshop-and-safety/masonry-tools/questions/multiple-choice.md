# Masonry tools — use, care and dangers — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** When does Rwanda teach masonry tools?
a) Before carpentry b) Straight after the carpentry tools, in the same Primary 5 tools sequence c) In Primary 2 d) Never
**Answer: b) Straight after the carpentry tools, in the same Primary 5 tools sequence**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the first step with the masonry tools?
a) Pricing them b) Naming examples of them and their work c) Painting them d) Selling them
**Answer: b) Naming examples of them and their work**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is taught together in the second masonry lesson?
a) Songs and stories b) Usage and maintenance, and the dangers of masonry tools c) Only prices d) Only history
**Answer: b) Usage and maintenance, and the dangers of masonry tools**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** How does the masonry pattern compare with carpentry?
a) It is the opposite b) The same: name, use, maintain, know the dangers c) There is no pattern d) Masonry has no dangers
**Answer: b) The same: name, use, maintain, know the dangers**

**5. (Sub lesson 1)** Why name a tool's job when you name the tool?
a) A tool is learned as name, shape and job together b) For decoration c) Jobs do not matter d) To make the list longer
**Answer: a) A tool is learned as name, shape and job together**
