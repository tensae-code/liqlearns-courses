# Masonry tools — use, care and dangers — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Masonry tools are the builder's kit for building work.
**Answer: True** — Rwanda names the masonry tools as the examples of the builder's trade, straight after carpentry.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Masonry tools have no dangers worth naming.
**Answer: False** — The book teaches the dangers of masonry tools together with their usage and maintenance.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Usage, maintenance and dangers are taught in one combined lesson.
**Answer: True** — The Primary 5 book's second masonry lesson covers usage and maintenance and the dangers together.
