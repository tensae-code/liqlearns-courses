# Measuring and marking out — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** You should start measuring from the very edge of the ruler.
**Answer: False** — Measure from the zero mark, lined up with the end of the thing.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The workbook practises measuring on everyday things like a key and a phone.
**Answer: True** — Pencil, key, screwdriver and mobile phone are its practice objects.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Once lines are marked out, there is no need to check them.
**Answer: False** — Checking the marking out is the last cheap chance to fix a mistake.
