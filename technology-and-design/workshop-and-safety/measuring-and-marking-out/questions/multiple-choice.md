# Measuring and marking out — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In the workshop, lengths are measured in…
a) Centimetres only b) Millimetres c) Hand spans d) Steps
**Answer: b) Millimetres**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Where must the ruler start when you measure?
a) At the ruler's edge b) At the zero mark, exactly at the end of the thing c) Anywhere along the ruler d) At the 10 mark always
**Answer: b) At the zero mark, exactly at the end of the thing**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Which tool gives a true right angle when marking out?
a) Compasses b) Set square c) Protractor only d) Free hand
**Answer: b) Set square**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** What are compasses used for in marking out?
a) Straight edges b) Circles and arcs c) Measuring weight d) Right angles
**Answer: b) Circles and arcs**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Why check the marking out before cutting?
a) A line can be fixed; a cut cannot be uncut b) To make the work slower c) The teacher requires it only d) Checking is never needed
**Answer: a) A line can be fixed; a cut cannot be uncut**
