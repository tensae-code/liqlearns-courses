# Measuring and marking out — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, or by doing the drawing step named.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why do you put your eye directly above the mark when reading a ruler?
**Answer:** So the reading is true — looking from the side shifts the mark you read and gives a wrong length.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does marking out do before making begins?
**Answer:** It puts the design's true lines, at the measured sizes, onto the material or sheet, so cutting and making follow checked lines.
