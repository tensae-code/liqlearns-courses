# Lesson plan — Measuring and marking out

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Workshop and Safety · **Lesson:** Measuring and marking out
**Place in the curriculum:** Workshop and Safety (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Malta expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** measure in millimetres with a ruler, reading from the zero mark — then mark out a design accurately with ruler, set squares and compasses before any making begins.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on a student workbook — Malta's *Design and Technology — Graphical Communication Component — Workbook 1 ("Communicate Graphically")*, Directorate for Quality and Standards in Education, Malta, 2015 (see lesson/sources.md). It counts in the acceptance count (student workbook, not evidence-only).

## Sub lessons
1. **Measuring in millimetres** — Lengths in the workshop are measured in millimetres, because making needs finer steps than centimetres.
2. **Marking out a design** — Marking out is drawing the design's true lines on the material (or the drawing sheet) before cutting or making: the sizes you measured become lines in the right places.
3. **Checking before you cut** — Marking out is the last cheap moment to be wrong: a line can be rubbed out, a cut cannot be uncut.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of Workshop and Safety. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Measure everyday objects in millimetres, reading from the zero mark.
2. Mark out a given design with ruler, set squares and compasses.
3. Check marked-out lines against the design before cutting.
## What you need
A ruler marked in millimetres, a pencil, set squares, compasses and paper or card.
