# Hands-on — Measuring and marking out

Real drawing practice with pencil and paper — this subject is learned by doing.

## Measure four things
Measure a pencil, a key, a screwdriver and a phone in millimetres, as the workbook does. Write each length. Measure each one again — the two readings must agree.

## Mark out the jeep
Mark out the workbook's jeep design (or a simple vehicle outline of your own) with ruler, set squares and compasses. Check every corner with the set square before you finish.
