# Workshop organisation and safety — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In your own words, what is tool (herramienta)?
**Answer:** A hand-held means of work, such as a hammer or scissors.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In your own words, what is protection and hygiene norms?
**Answer:** The workshop rules that guard the worker's body and health.
