# Workshop organisation and safety — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Tool (herramienta) is a hand-held means of work, such as a hammer or scissors.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Device (dispositivo) has nothing to do with this lesson.
**Answer: False** — It is part of the lesson: a fitted arrangement that holds, guides or aids the work.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Protection and hygiene norms is the workshop rules that guard the worker's body and health.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.
