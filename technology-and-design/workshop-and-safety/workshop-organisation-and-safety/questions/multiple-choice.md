# Workshop organisation and safety — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is tool (herramienta)?
a) a hand-held means of work, such as a hammer or scissors b) Only a price c) Only decoration d) Nothing to do with this lesson
**Answer: a) a hand-held means of work, such as a hammer or scissors**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is device (dispositivo)?
a) Only history b) a fitted arrangement that holds, guides or aids the work c) Only storage d) A kind of glue
**Answer: b) a fitted arrangement that holds, guides or aids the work**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is protection and hygiene norms?
a) Only old tools b) Only painting c) the workshop rules that guard the worker's body and health d) Only a price
**Answer: c) the workshop rules that guard the worker's body and health**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson teach, in one line?
a) Only decoration b) Nothing to do with this lesson c) Only history d) the school workshop runs on organisation and safety: know the work station, the tool, the instrument and the device as basic concepts — and keep the general norms of protection and hygiene in every process, manual, mechanical or automated.
**Answer: d) the school workshop runs on organisation and safety: know the work station, the tool, the instrument and the device as basic concepts — and keep the general norms of protection and hygiene in every process, manual, mechanical or automated.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** How does Cuba's book teach this?
a) As theory only, never practised b) Only in a glossary c) Only as an exam d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Spanish
**Answer: d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Spanish**
