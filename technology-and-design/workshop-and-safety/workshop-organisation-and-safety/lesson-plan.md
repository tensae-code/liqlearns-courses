# Lesson plan — Workshop organisation and safety

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Workshop and Safety · **Lesson:** Workshop organisation and safety
**Place in the curriculum:** Workshop and Safety (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Cuba expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the school workshop runs on organisation and safety: know the work station, the tool, the instrument and the device as basic concepts — and keep the general norms of protection and hygiene in every process, manual, mechanical or automated.
**This lesson's own level:** stocked from Cuba's *Programa: Educación Laboral — 2do grado* and the Cuba books in lesson/sources.md — see lesson/sources.md. Cuba counts in the acceptance count for its student-book placements; its programme and methodological-orientations placements are evidence only, counted nowhere.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **The basic concepts** — Cuba's Grade 2 programme starts the whole subject here: pupils distinguish puesto de trabajo (work station), herramienta (tool), instrumento (instrument) and dispositivo (device) — the four words every later lesson will use.
2. **Protection and hygiene norms** — The 1989 Grade 7 book states the general norms of protection and hygiene in the school workshop, and sets them over every kind of work process — manual, mechanical and automated. Safety is not a lesson among lessons; it is the workshop's constitution.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
