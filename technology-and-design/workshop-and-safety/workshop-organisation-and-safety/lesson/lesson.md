# Workshop organisation and safety

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Cuba's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — The basic concepts
Cuba's Grade 2 programme starts the whole subject here: pupils distinguish puesto de trabajo (work station), herramienta (tool), instrumento (instrument) and dispositivo (device) — the four words every later lesson will use.

## Sub lesson 2 — Protection and hygiene norms
The 1989 Grade 7 book states the general norms of protection and hygiene in the school workshop, and sets them over every kind of work process — manual, mechanical and automated. Safety is not a lesson among lessons; it is the workshop's constitution.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: the school workshop runs on organisation and safety: know the work station, the tool, the instrument and the device as basic concepts — and keep the general norms of protection and hygiene in every process, manual, mechanical or automated. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
