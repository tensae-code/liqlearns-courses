# Hands-on — Carpentry tools — use, care and dangers

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## Tool roll call
Lay out or draw six carpentry tools. Name each, say its job, and say one care action it needs.

## Blade check
Examine a saw blade (or a clear picture of one). Decide: sharp or dull? Clean or dirty? Write the maintenance you would do and one safety measure for using it.
