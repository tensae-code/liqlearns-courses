# Carpentry tools — use, care and dangers — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the three saws in Rwanda's saw family.
**Answer:** The bow/frame saw, the table saw and the wood/hand saw.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What three maintenance ideas does the book teach for carpentry tools?
**Answer:** Check sharpness and sharpen blades, clean the tools, and oil metal parts against rust.
