# Carpentry tools — use, care and dangers — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which tools belong to the saw family in Rwanda's book?
a) Bow/frame saw, table saw, wood/hand saw b) Hammer, nail, screw c) T-square, ruler, pencil d) Brush, pot, cloth
**Answer: a) Bow/frame saw, table saw, wood/hand saw**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the T-square do in carpentry?
a) Cuts wood b) Draws straight lines on wood c) Holds nails d) Sands edges
**Answer: b) Draws straight lines on wood**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What maintenance does a saw need?
a) Painting b) Checking blade sharpness, sharpening and cleaning c) Soaking in water d) Nothing
**Answer: b) Checking blade sharpness, sharpening and cleaning**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Why are the T-square's metal parts oiled?
a) To make them shiny b) Against rust c) To make them heavier d) For luck
**Answer: b) Against rust**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** How does the book end the carpentry unit?
a) With a song b) With the dangers of carpentry tools and health and safety measures c) With prices d) With a story
**Answer: b) With the dangers of carpentry tools and health and safety measures**
