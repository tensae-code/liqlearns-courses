# Carpentry tools — use, care and dangers — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The T-square is a cutting tool.
**Answer: False** — The T-square draws straight lines on wood; the saws do the cutting.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Oiling metal parts protects them against rust.
**Answer: True** — The book teaches oiling the T-square's metal parts against rust.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A sharp, well-kept saw is safer to respect and use than a dull, dirty one left uncared for.
**Answer: True** — Maintenance — sharpness checked, blade sharpened, tools cleaned — is taught together with the dangers and safety measures.
