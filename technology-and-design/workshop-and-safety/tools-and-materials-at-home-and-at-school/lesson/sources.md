The Primary 2 pupil's book also exists in a parallel Kinyarwanda edition (© 2019), read once and placed once under the English edition.

Rwanda's own way: Rwanda teaches the first technology lesson in the learner's own home and classroom: Science and Elementary Technology opens by walking the home room by room and naming each tool by its job, in English and in the parallel Kinyarwanda edition alike.
