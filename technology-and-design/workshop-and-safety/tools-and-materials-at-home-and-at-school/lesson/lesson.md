# Tools and materials at home and at school

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Rwanda's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Tools and materials at home, room by room
Rwanda's Primary 2 book walks the home room by room — kitchen, bedroom, living room — naming the tools and materials found in each and what each is used for: the hoe for digging is the book's own example of a tool named by its work. Learning a tool starts with two questions: where does it live, and what job does it do?

## Sub lesson 2 — School materials and their uses
At school the same habit applies: each school material has its use — the coloured marker writes on the board and on papers. Naming the material and its use together is the first step of technology: nothing in the room is just 'a thing', everything is a tool for a task.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: every room at home and every corner of the classroom has its own tools and materials, and each one has a job — a hoe for digging, a coloured marker for the board and papers. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
