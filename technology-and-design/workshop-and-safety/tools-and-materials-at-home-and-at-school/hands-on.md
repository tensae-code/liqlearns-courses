# Hands-on — Tools and materials at home and at school

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## Room-by-room survey
Walk one room at home and one corner of your classroom. List every tool or material you find, the room it lives in, and the job it does.

## Name the use
Pick five school materials. For each one, say its name and its use in one sentence, the way the book does with the coloured marker.
