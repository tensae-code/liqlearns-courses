# Tools and materials at home and at school — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How does Rwanda's Primary 2 book organise the tools at home?
a) By price b) Room by room — kitchen, bedroom, living room c) By colour d) Alphabetically
**Answer: b) Room by room — kitchen, bedroom, living room**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the book's example of a tool named by its job?
a) A marker for the board b) A hoe for digging c) A broom for the yard d) A cup for water
**Answer: b) A hoe for digging**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is a coloured marker used for at school?
a) Digging b) Writing on the board and on papers c) Cooking d) Sweeping
**Answer: b) Writing on the board and on papers**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** What two things do you name when you learn a tool?
a) Its price and its shop b) Where it lives and what job it does c) Its colour and weight d) Its owner and age
**Answer: b) Where it lives and what job it does**

**5. (Sub lesson 1)** Why name a material together with its use?
a) Because a tool is understood by the task it does b) Because names are hard c) Because rooms are big d) Because school says so
**Answer: a) Because a tool is understood by the task it does**
