# Tools and materials at home and at school — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Every room at home has its own tools and materials.
**Answer: True** — Rwanda's book teaches the home room by room — kitchen, bedroom, living room — each with its own tools.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A coloured marker is a digging tool.
**Answer: False** — The coloured marker is a school material for writing on the board and papers.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Learning a tool means naming where it lives and what job it does.
**Answer: True** — That pair — place and use — is how the book introduces every tool.
