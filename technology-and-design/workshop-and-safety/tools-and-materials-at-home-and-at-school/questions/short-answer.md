# Tools and materials at home and at school — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the three rooms Rwanda's book walks through at home.
**Answer:** The kitchen, the bedroom and the living room.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How does the book teach a school material?
**Answer:** By naming the material together with its use — for example, the coloured marker for the board and papers.
