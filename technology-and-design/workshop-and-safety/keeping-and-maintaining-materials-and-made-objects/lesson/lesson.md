# Keeping and maintaining materials and made objects

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Rwanda's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Keeping home and school materials properly
Rwanda's Primary 2 book gives keeping its own lessons, at home and at school: materials are washed, wiped, cleaned, greased and ironed, each in the way that suits it — school materials are even washed. Care is not an extra; it is part of using a thing well.

## Sub lesson 2 — Maintaining the objects you have made
From Primary 4 to Primary 6 the same rule follows the maker's own work: the utilities and learning objects you have made — baskets, mats, paper shapes, toys — must be maintained too. A made object that is not kept is soon not an object at all.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: tools, materials and the objects you make last only if they are kept properly — washed, wiped, cleaned, greased and ironed — and the things you make need the same care as the things you were given. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.

## Do It Yourself
1. Do the Do it first task again, carefully this time, using everything the lesson taught.
2. Check your work against the lesson's own checks — fix one thing you find.
3. Keep your work: it is practice evidence, and the next lesson builds on it.
