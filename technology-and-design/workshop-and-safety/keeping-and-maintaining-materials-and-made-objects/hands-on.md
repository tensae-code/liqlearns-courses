# Hands-on — Keeping and maintaining materials and made objects

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## Care rota
Choose three materials at home or school. For each, write how it is kept properly — washed, wiped, cleaned, greased or ironed — and how often.

## Maintain your make
Take one object you have made. Clean it, repair one small fault, and store it the way that keeps it best. Note what you did.
