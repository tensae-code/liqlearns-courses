# Keeping and maintaining materials and made objects — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Greasing is one of the ways the book keeps a material properly.
**Answer: True** — The Primary 2 list is washing, wiping, cleaning, greasing and ironing.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Objects you make yourself need no maintenance.
**Answer: False** — The Primary 4, 5 and 6 books each close their making work with maintaining the utilities and learning objects made.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Care is part of using a thing well, not an extra.
**Answer: True** — Rwanda gives keeping its own lessons at home and at school from Primary 2.
