# Keeping and maintaining materials and made objects — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the five keeping actions in Rwanda's Primary 2 book.
**Answer:** Washing, wiping, cleaning, greasing and ironing.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which objects does the maintenance lesson cover in the making years?
**Answer:** The utilities and learning objects the learners have made themselves.
