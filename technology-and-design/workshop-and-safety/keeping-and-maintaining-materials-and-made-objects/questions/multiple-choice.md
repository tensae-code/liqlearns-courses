# Keeping and maintaining materials and made objects — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which list names ways Rwanda's book keeps materials properly?
a) Throwing, burning, hiding b) Washing, wiping, cleaning, greasing, ironing c) Painting, selling, lending d) Breaking, soaking, freezing
**Answer: b) Washing, wiping, cleaning, greasing, ironing**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Does the book's care cover school materials too?
a) No, only home materials b) Yes — school materials are kept properly too, including washing them c) Only books d) Only tools
**Answer: b) Yes — school materials are kept properly too, including washing them**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What must be maintained besides given materials?
a) Nothing b) The utilities and learning objects you have made yourself c) Only the teacher's things d) Only new things
**Answer: b) The utilities and learning objects you have made yourself**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** What happens to a made object that is not kept?
a) It improves b) It is soon not an object at all c) It multiplies d) Nothing ever
**Answer: b) It is soon not an object at all**

**5. (Sub lesson 1)** Where does Rwanda teach keeping?
a) Only in Primary 6 b) From Primary 2 (home and school materials) through the making years (made objects) c) Only at home d) Nowhere
**Answer: b) From Primary 2 (home and school materials) through the making years (made objects)**
