# Lesson plan — Hand tools

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Workshop and Safety · **Lesson:** Hand tools
**Place in the curriculum:** Workshop and Safety (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Jamaica expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** hand tools are chosen by the job: each tool belongs to a class by its use and purpose — cutting, boring, striking, driving, measuring — and a good maker lists the right tools and materials for every component before starting.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Jamaica's *National Standards Curriculum Guide — Grades 4–6 — Resource & Technology* (Ministry of Education, Youth and Information, Kingston, Jamaica, © 2018), a curriculum guide with full unit teaching content, read COMPLETE (see lesson/sources.md). It counts in the acceptance count (curriculum guide with full teaching content, not evidence-only).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **Classes of hand tools** — Sort tools by what they do: snips cut, a gimlet bores, a mallet strikes, a screwdriver drives, rulers measure.
2. **Choosing the tool for the component** — Each component of the thing you make names its own tools — choose per part, not per habit.
3. **The material and tool list** — Before making starts, the plan becomes a list: every material and every tool, ready to check off.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of Workshop and Safety. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Classify common hand tools by use and purpose.
2. Select the right tools and materials for a given component.
3. Prepare a material and tool list for a small project.
## What you need
Five common hand tools or pictures of them, paper and a pencil.
