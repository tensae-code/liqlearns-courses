Fiji's own way: Fiji classifies hand tools in Year 9 Basic Technology and teaches them with the bench appliances — bench hook, marking knife and gauge — through saws, planes, chisels and files to spanners, mallets and pliers.

Jamaica's own way: Jamaica teaches hand tools inside the making — tools are classified by use and purpose, then immediately put to work selecting tools and materials component by component for the class's toy car, with a written material and tool list before making begins.
