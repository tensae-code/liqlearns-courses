# Hand tools — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) A gimlet belongs to which class?**
a) Cutting b) Boring c) Striking d) Measuring
**Answer: b) Boring**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Snips and nippers are used for…**
a) Boring holes b) Driving screws c) Cutting sheet and wire d) Measuring lengths
**Answer: c) Cutting sheet and wire**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Why is a mallet's head softer than a hammer's?**
a) It is cheaper b) It strikes without damaging the work c) It is lighter to carry d) It cannot strike
**Answer: b) It strikes without damaging the work**

**4. (Sub lesson 2) Who or what decides which tool to use?**
a) The maker's favourite b) The component and its job c) The tool's colour d) Chance
**Answer: b) The component and its job**

**5. (Sub lesson 3) When is the material and tool list prepared?**
a) After making finishes b) Halfway through c) Before cutting begins d) Never
**Answer: c) Before cutting begins**
