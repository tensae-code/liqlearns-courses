# Hand tools — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, or by doing the step named.

**1. (Sub lesson 2) A component needs a hole for an axle and then a screw to fix its wheel. Name the tools, in order.**
**Answer:** Gimlet (boring tool) for the hole, then the screwdriver that fits the screw (driving tool).

**2. (Sub lesson 3) Why write the material and tool list before making?**
**Answer:** So nothing is missing halfway, no time is lost hunting for tools, and each component's needs are thought through while changes are still free.
