# The school technology laboratory — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson teach?
a) How the workshop is laid out and equipped (benches, balances, compression/tension test machines, wind tunnels, measuring instruments, electronics, robots and computer-guided automation modules) b) Only how to draw pictures c) Nothing practical d) A song to memorise
**Answer: a) How the workshop is laid out and equipped (benches, balances, compression/tension test machines, wind tunnels, measuring instruments, electronics, robots and computer-guided automation modules)**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Sub lesson 1 is about…
a) The school technology laboratory b) Cooking lunch c) A sports match d) The weather
**Answer: a) The school technology laboratory**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Sub lesson 2 asks the learner to…
a) Complete the making in full and check it b) Stop before starting c) Copy a partner's work d) Throw the materials away
**Answer: a) Complete the making in full and check it**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Greece teaches this topic inside which school book?
a) EPAL Research Work b) Only mathematics c) Only history d) It is not taught at school
**Answer: a) EPAL Research Work**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** What should the learner do first in this lesson?
a) Try the Do it first task in their own way b) Skip to the last page c) Wait to be given the answer d) Nothing at all
**Answer: a) Try the Do it first task in their own way**
