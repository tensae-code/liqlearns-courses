# Hands-on — The school technology laboratory

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: how the workshop is laid out and equipped (benches, balances, compression/tension test machines, wind tunnels, measuring instruments, electronics, robots and computer-guided automation modules). Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
