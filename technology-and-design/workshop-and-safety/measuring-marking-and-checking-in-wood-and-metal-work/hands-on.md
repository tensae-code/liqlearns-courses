# Hands-on — Measuring, marking and checking in wood and metal work

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## Read three instruments
From real instruments or clear pictures, read a length on a folding rule, a thickness on a caliper, and an angle on a false square. Record each reading.

## Mark a board on paper
Draw a board and mark it for a cut: the measure, the gramil line, a compass arc and the check you would make before sawing.
