# Measuring, marking and checking in wood and metal work — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is caliper (pie de rey)?
a) an instrument that measures thickness and diameter precisely between its jaws b) Only a price c) Only decoration d) Nothing to do with this lesson
**Answer: a) an instrument that measures thickness and diameter precisely between its jaws**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is marking gauge (gramil)?
a) Only history b) a tool that scores a line parallel to an edge at a set distance c) Only storage d) A kind of glue
**Answer: b) a tool that scores a line parallel to an edge at a set distance**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is false square?
a) Only old tools b) Only painting c) an adjustable square that carries any angle from one piece to another d) Only a price
**Answer: c) an adjustable square that carries any angle from one piece to another**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson teach, in one line?
a) Only decoration b) Nothing to do with this lesson c) Only history d) before cutting comes measuring: the folding rule, tape measure, caliper (pie de rey), marking gauge (gramil), compasses and false square measure, mark and check the work in wood and metal.
**Answer: d) before cutting comes measuring: the folding rule, tape measure, caliper (pie de rey), marking gauge (gramil), compasses and false square measure, mark and check the work in wood and metal.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** How does Cuba's book teach this?
a) As theory only, never practised b) Only in a glossary c) Only as an exam d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Spanish
**Answer: d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Spanish**
