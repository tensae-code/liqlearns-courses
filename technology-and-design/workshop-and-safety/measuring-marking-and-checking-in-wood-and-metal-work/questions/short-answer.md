# Measuring, marking and checking in wood and metal work — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In your own words, what is caliper (pie de rey)?
**Answer:** An instrument that measures thickness and diameter precisely between its jaws.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In your own words, what is false square?
**Answer:** An adjustable square that carries any angle from one piece to another.
