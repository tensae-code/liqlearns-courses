# Lesson plan — Workshop safety

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Workshop and Safety · **Lesson:** Workshop safety
**Place in the curriculum:** Workshop and Safety (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Malaysia expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** workshop safety comes first: follow the workshop rules, dress safely, and respect the separate rules for materials and for tools.
**This lesson's own level:** stocked from Malaysia's *Reka Bentuk dan Teknologi* (RBT — Design and Technology) textbooks — see lesson/sources.md. Malaysia counts in the acceptance count (student books, not evidence-only).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **The rules that keep the workshop safe** — Malaysia opens RBT with workshop safety: workshop rules, personal safety — apron on, hair tied, no loose items — and separate safety rules for materials and for tools.
2. **Personal, materials and tool safety** — Personal safety protects the learner, materials safety protects against what is handled, and tool safety governs how each tool is held, used and put away.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of Workshop and Safety. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the workshop rules and the personal-safety rules.
2. Tell materials-safety rules from tool-safety rules.
3. Explain how Malaysia's book teaches it.
## What you need
An apron and the workshop rule chart to study.
