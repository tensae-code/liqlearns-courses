# Workshop safety

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Malaysia's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — The rules that keep the workshop safe
Malaysia opens RBT with workshop safety: workshop rules, personal safety — apron on, hair tied, no loose items — and separate safety rules for materials and for tools. Safety is the first lesson of the subject, before any making.

## Sub lesson 2 — Personal, materials and tool safety
Personal safety protects the learner, materials safety protects against what is handled, and tool safety governs how each tool is held, used and put away. The three rule sets work together every lesson.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: workshop safety comes first: follow the workshop rules, dress safely, and respect the separate rules for materials and for tools. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.

## Do It Yourself
1. Do the Do it first task again, carefully this time, using everything the lesson taught.
2. Check your work against the lesson's own checks — fix one thing you find.
3. Keep your work: it is practice evidence, and the next lesson builds on it.
