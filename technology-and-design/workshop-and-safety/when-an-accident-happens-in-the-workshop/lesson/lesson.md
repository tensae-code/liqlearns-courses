# When an accident happens in the workshop

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Malaysia's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — First responses: scald, burn and electric shock
When an accident happens, the first minutes matter: a scald or burn is treated at once, and an electric shock is answered without touching the casualty while the current still flows.

## Sub lesson 2 — Escape plan and fire extinguisher
Every workshop has an emergency-escape plan — the route out, known before it is needed — and a fire extinguisher whose use is learned before any fire. Knowing the plan and the extinguisher is part of the craft.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: when an accident happens: treat the injury, follow the escape plan, and know the fire extinguisher before you need it. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.

## Do It Yourself
1. Do the Do it first task again, carefully this time, using everything the lesson taught.
2. Check your work against the lesson's own checks — fix one thing you find.
3. Keep your work: it is practice evidence, and the next lesson builds on it.
