# When an accident happens in the workshop — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A scald or burn is an injury treated at once in the first minutes.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** An electric shock response has nothing to do with this lesson.
**Answer: False** — It is part of the lesson: helping without touching the casualty while current flows.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The emergency-escape plan is the known route out of the workshop in an emergency.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.
