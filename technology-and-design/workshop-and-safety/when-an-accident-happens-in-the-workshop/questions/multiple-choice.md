# When an accident happens in the workshop — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is a scald or burn?
a) an injury treated at once in the first minutes b) Only a price c) Only decoration d) Nothing to do with this lesson
**Answer: a) an injury treated at once in the first minutes**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is an electric shock response?
a) Only decoration b) helping without touching the casualty while current flows c) Only a price d) A kind of glue
**Answer: b) helping without touching the casualty while current flows**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is the emergency-escape plan?
a) Only history b) Only painting c) the known route out of the workshop in an emergency d) Only storage
**Answer: c) the known route out of the workshop in an emergency**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson teach, in one line?
a) when an accident happens: treat the injury, follow the escape plan, and know the fire extinguisher before you need it. b) Only prices c) Only old tools d) Nothing practical
**Answer: a) when an accident happens: treat the injury, follow the escape plan, and know the fire extinguisher before you need it.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** How does Malaysia's book teach this?
a) As theory only, never practised b) Only in the glossary c) Only as an exam d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Malay (RBT)
**Answer: d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Malay (RBT)**
