# Lesson plan — When an accident happens in the workshop

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Workshop and Safety · **Lesson:** When an accident happens in the workshop
**Place in the curriculum:** Workshop and Safety (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Malaysia expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** when an accident happens: treat the injury, follow the escape plan, and know the fire extinguisher before you need it.
**This lesson's own level:** stocked from Malaysia's *Reka Bentuk dan Teknologi* (RBT — Design and Technology) textbooks — see lesson/sources.md. Malaysia counts in the acceptance count (student books, not evidence-only).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **First responses: scald, burn and electric shock** — When an accident happens, the first minutes matter: a scald or burn is treated at once, and an electric shock is answered without touching the casualty while the current still flows.
2. **Escape plan and fire extinguisher** — Every workshop has an emergency-escape plan — the route out, known before it is needed — and a fire extinguisher whose use is learned before any fire.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of Workshop and Safety. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say how a scald/burn is treated and how to respond to an electric shock.
2. Describe the escape plan and the extinguisher steps.
3. Explain how Malaysia's book teaches it.
## What you need
A workshop escape plan and an extinguisher picture to study.
