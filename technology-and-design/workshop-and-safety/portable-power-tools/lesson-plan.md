# Lesson plan — Portable power tools

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Workshop and Safety · **Lesson:** Portable power tools
**Place in the curriculum:** Workshop and Safety (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Samoa expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** portable power tools — circular saw, planer, drill and jigsaw — cut, shape and bore at machine speed: each has its specification, its step-by-step safe use, and its protections: eye and ear protection, clamped work, and drill size matched to speed.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on student book evidence — Samoa's MESC textbooks (see lesson/sources.md). It counts in the acceptance count (student book, not evidence-only).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **The four tools and their work** — The circular saw cuts straight at speed; the planer shaves surfaces true; the drill bores holes; the jigsaw cuts curves.
2. **Safe use, step by step** — Machine safety is a sequence: check the tool, protect eyes and ears, clamp the work, cut, switch off, wait for the stop.
3. **Size and speed — the drill rule** — On a drill, bit size and speed belong together: large bits turn slowly, small bits may turn fast.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of Workshop and Safety. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Match the four tools to their cuts.
2. Recite the safe-use sequence.
3. State the drill size-speed rule.
## What you need
No live tools needed for the theory; hands-on is inspection and planning under supervision only.
