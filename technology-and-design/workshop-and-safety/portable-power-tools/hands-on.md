# Hands-on — Portable power tools

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## Sequence drill (no tools live)
Write the safe-use sequence for the circular saw as a checklist of at least six steps, in order. Swap with a partner and hunt for a missing step in theirs.

## Size-speed table
Make a table: bit sizes small, medium, large against drill speeds fast, medium, slow. Fill it by the size-speed rule and write the reason under the table.
