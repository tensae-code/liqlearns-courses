# Portable power tools — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Give the safe-use sequence in order.
**Answer:** Answer: Check the tool, put on eye and ear protection, clamp the work, make the cut, switch off, wait for the complete stop.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Why does a large bit at high speed endanger the wrist?
**Answer:** Answer: It can grab the work and twist suddenly; its size gives the motor leverage against the holder, and it overheats — slow speed keeps it cutting, not grabbing.
