# Portable power tools — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The jigsaw's special cut is…
a) Curves b) Long straight rips only c) Holes only d) Planing flat
**Answer: a) Curves**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The planer's job is to…
a) Shave surfaces true and flat b) Bore holes c) Cut curves d) Drive screws only
**Answer: a) Shave surfaces true and flat**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Work for a power tool must be…
a) Clamped, never held loose by hand near the cut b) Held by a partner's fingers c) Balanced on a knee d) Left free to spin
**Answer: a) Clamped, never held loose by hand near the cut**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** After switching off, you…
a) Wait for the tool to stop before setting it down or reaching in b) Grab the blade to stop it faster c) Walk away at once d) Unplug it by the cord mid-spin
**Answer: a) Wait for the tool to stop before setting it down or reaching in**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** A large drill bit should turn…
a) Slowly b) As fast as the drill goes c) At any speed — size does not matter d) Backwards
**Answer: a) Slowly**
