# Safety signs and symbols in the workshop — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A prohibition sign is a sign that forbids an action.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A warning sign has nothing to do with this lesson.
**Answer: False** — It is part of the lesson: a sign that alerts you to a danger.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A mandatory sign is a sign that orders a protective action.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.
