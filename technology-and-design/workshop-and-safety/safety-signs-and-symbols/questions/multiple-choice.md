# Safety signs and symbols in the workshop — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is a prohibition sign?
a) a sign that forbids an action b) Only a price c) Only decoration d) Nothing to do with this lesson
**Answer: a) a sign that forbids an action**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is a warning sign?
a) Only decoration b) a sign that alerts you to a danger c) Only a price d) A kind of glue
**Answer: b) a sign that alerts you to a danger**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is a mandatory sign?
a) Only history b) Only painting c) a sign that orders a protective action d) Only storage
**Answer: c) a sign that orders a protective action**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson teach, in one line?
a) a safety sign is read by its colour: prohibition, warning, mandatory, or safe condition. b) Only prices c) Only old tools d) Nothing practical
**Answer: a) a safety sign is read by its colour: prohibition, warning, mandatory, or safe condition.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** How does Malaysia's book teach this?
a) As theory only, never practised b) Only in the glossary c) Only as an exam d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Malay (RBT)
**Answer: d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Malay (RBT)**
