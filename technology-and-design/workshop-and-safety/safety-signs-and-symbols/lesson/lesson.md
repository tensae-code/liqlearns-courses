# Safety signs and symbols in the workshop

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Malaysia's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Reading a safety sign by its colour
A safety sign speaks by colour before words: each colour family carries one kind of message — prohibition, warning, mandatory action, or safe condition — so a learner can read the workshop at a glance.

## Sub lesson 2 — The four colour messages
Prohibition signs forbid, warning signs alert to danger, mandatory signs order a protective action, and safe-condition signs point to safety. Malaysia heads the strand "Utamakan Keselamatan" — put safety first.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: a safety sign is read by its colour: prohibition, warning, mandatory, or safe condition. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
