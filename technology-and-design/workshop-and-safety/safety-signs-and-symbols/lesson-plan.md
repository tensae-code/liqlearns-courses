# Lesson plan — Safety signs and symbols in the workshop

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Workshop and Safety · **Lesson:** Safety signs and symbols in the workshop
**Place in the curriculum:** Workshop and Safety (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Malaysia expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** a safety sign is read by its colour: prohibition, warning, mandatory, or safe condition.
**This lesson's own level:** stocked from Malaysia's *Reka Bentuk dan Teknologi* (RBT — Design and Technology) textbooks — see lesson/sources.md. Malaysia counts in the acceptance count (student books, not evidence-only).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **Reading a safety sign by its colour** — A safety sign speaks by colour before words: each colour family carries one kind of message — prohibition, warning, mandatory action, or safe condition — so a learner can read the workshop at a glance.
2. **The four colour messages** — Prohibition signs forbid, warning signs alert to danger, mandatory signs order a protective action, and safe-condition signs point to safety.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of Workshop and Safety. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Read a safety sign and name its family from its colour.
2. Explain what each of the four colour messages orders or tells.
3. Explain how Malaysia's book teaches it.
## What you need
Printed safety signs or a sign chart.
