# Lesson plan — Maintenance and repair

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Workshop and Safety · **Lesson:** Maintenance and repair
**Place in the curriculum:** Workshop and Safety (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Cuba expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** maintenance keeps a school alive: know what deteriorates it, survey the defects (defectación), maintain furniture, labs, workshops, books, gardens and sports areas — and repair, from a water tap to a bound book, while saving water and energy by plan.
**This lesson's own level:** stocked from Cuba's *Programa: Educación Laboral — 8vo grado* and the Cuba books in lesson/sources.md — see lesson/sources.md. Cuba counts in the acceptance count for its student-book placements; its programme and methodological-orientations placements are evidence only, counted nowhere.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **Deterioration, survey and the methodology** — Cuba's Grade 8 unit opens with the factors that deteriorate school property and the maintenance methodology, whose first act is defectación — the defect survey that lists what is failing before any repair is planned. The Grade 8 student book adds the objectives and characteristics of school maintenance.
2. **Maintaining and repairing, in practice** — The areas are named: furniture, laboratories, workshops, books, gardens, sports areas. The repairs are concrete — repairing a water tap is the unit's worked example — and bookbinding (encuadernación) repairs the library. Water and energy saving plans run through it all.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
