# Maintenance and repair

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Cuba's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Deterioration, survey and the methodology
Cuba's Grade 8 unit opens with the factors that deteriorate school property and the maintenance methodology, whose first act is defectación — the defect survey that lists what is failing before any repair is planned. The Grade 8 student book adds the objectives and characteristics of school maintenance.

## Sub lesson 2 — Maintaining and repairing, in practice
The areas are named: furniture, laboratories, workshops, books, gardens, sports areas. The repairs are concrete — repairing a water tap is the unit's worked example — and bookbinding (encuadernación) repairs the library. Water and energy saving plans run through it all.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: maintenance keeps a school alive: know what deteriorates it, survey the defects (defectación), maintain furniture, labs, workshops, books, gardens and sports areas — and repair, from a water tap to a bound book, while saving water and energy by plan. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.

3. Keep your work: it is practice evidence, and the next lesson builds on it.
