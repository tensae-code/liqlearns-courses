# Maintenance and repair — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In your own words, what is defect survey (defectación)?
**Answer:** The inspection that lists an object's defects before repair is planned.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In your own words, what is bookbinding (encuadernación)?
**Answer:** Repairing and rebinding books so they return to use.
