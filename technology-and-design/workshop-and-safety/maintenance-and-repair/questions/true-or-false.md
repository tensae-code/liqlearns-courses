# Maintenance and repair — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Defect survey (defectación) is the inspection that lists an object's defects before repair is planned.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Maintenance has nothing to do with this lesson.
**Answer: False** — It is part of the lesson: the regular care that keeps property working and slows deterioration.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Bookbinding (encuadernación) is repairing and rebinding books so they return to use.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.
