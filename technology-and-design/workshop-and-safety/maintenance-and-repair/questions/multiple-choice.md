# Maintenance and repair — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is defect survey (defectación)?
a) the inspection that lists an object's defects before repair is planned b) Only a price c) Only decoration d) Nothing to do with this lesson
**Answer: a) the inspection that lists an object's defects before repair is planned**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is maintenance?
a) Only history b) the regular care that keeps property working and slows deterioration c) Only storage d) A kind of glue
**Answer: b) the regular care that keeps property working and slows deterioration**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is bookbinding (encuadernación)?
a) Only old tools b) Only painting c) repairing and rebinding books so they return to use d) Only a price
**Answer: c) repairing and rebinding books so they return to use**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson teach, in one line?
a) Only decoration b) Nothing to do with this lesson c) Only history d) maintenance keeps a school alive: know what deteriorates it, survey the defects (defectación), maintain furniture, labs, workshops, books, gardens and sports areas — and repair, from a water tap to a bound book, while saving water and energy by plan.
**Answer: d) maintenance keeps a school alive: know what deteriorates it, survey the defects (defectación), maintain furniture, labs, workshops, books, gardens and sports areas — and repair, from a water tap to a bound book, while saving water and energy by plan.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** How does Cuba's book teach this?
a) As theory only, never practised b) Only in a glossary c) Only as an exam d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Spanish
**Answer: d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Spanish**
