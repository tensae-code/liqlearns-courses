# Hands-on — Maintenance and repair

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## Survey like a defectación
Survey one area (a classroom corner, the garden tools). List every defect you find, ordered by urgency, with the repair each needs.

## Plan a tap repair
Write the steps to repair a dripping water tap: tools, parts, the water shut-off, the check afterwards — and the saving the repair makes.
