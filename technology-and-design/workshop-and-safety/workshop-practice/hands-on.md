# Hands-on — Workshop practice

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## Classify the workshop stock
List the wood and metal stock of a real or imagined workshop. Classify each piece: usable as-is, usable cut down, or for recycling.

## Design for a real need
Name one real need in your school or home. Sketch a socially-useful article that answers it, to be built from the classified stock.
