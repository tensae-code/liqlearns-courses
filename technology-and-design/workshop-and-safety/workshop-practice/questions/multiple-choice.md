# Workshop practice — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is workshop (taller)?
a) the room and organisation where making work is done b) Only a price c) Only decoration d) Nothing to do with this lesson
**Answer: a) the room and organisation where making work is done**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is socially-useful article?
a) Only history b) an article made to serve a real need of the school or community c) Only storage d) A kind of glue
**Answer: b) an article made to serve a real need of the school or community**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is creative design?
a) Only old tools b) Only painting c) designing with invention, not only copying a model d) Only a price
**Answer: c) designing with invention, not only copying a model**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson teach, in one line?
a) Only decoration b) Nothing to do with this lesson c) Only history d) workshop practice is real work under real conditions: mind the environmental and working conditions of the workshop, collect and classify wood and metals (recycling at the bench), design creatively, and build articles that are socially useful.
**Answer: d) workshop practice is real work under real conditions: mind the environmental and working conditions of the workshop, collect and classify wood and metals (recycling at the bench), design creatively, and build articles that are socially useful.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** How does Cuba's book teach this?
a) As theory only, never practised b) Only in a glossary c) Only as an exam d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Spanish
**Answer: d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Spanish**
