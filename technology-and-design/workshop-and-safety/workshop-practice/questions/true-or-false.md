# Workshop practice — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Workshop (taller) is the room and organisation where making work is done.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Socially-useful article has nothing to do with this lesson.
**Answer: False** — It is part of the lesson: an article made to serve a real need of the school or community.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Creative design is designing with invention, not only copying a model.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.
