# Workshop practice

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Cuba's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Conditions and materials of the workshop
Cuba's Grade 9 workshop chapter (Cap. 6, Trabajos de taller) opens with the environmental and working conditions of the taller, and with collecting and classifying wood and metals — the workshop's own recycling, feeding its stock.

## Sub lesson 2 — Creative design, useful articles
The chapter's aim is named: building socially-useful articles (artículos de utilidad social), with creative design (diseño creativo) as the method. Practice is not exercise — the workshop produces for real needs.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: workshop practice is real work under real conditions: mind the environmental and working conditions of the workshop, collect and classify wood and metals (recycling at the bench), design creatively, and build articles that are socially useful. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.

## Do It Yourself
1. Do the Do it first task again, carefully this time, using everything the lesson taught.
2. Check your work against the lesson's own checks — fix one thing you find.
3. Keep your work: it is practice evidence, and the next lesson builds on it.
