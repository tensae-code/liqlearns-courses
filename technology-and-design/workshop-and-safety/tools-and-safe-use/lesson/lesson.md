# Tools and safe use

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Kazakhstan's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Tools and safe use
Kazakhstan's books teach it this way (the student book, printed pp. 6–7): Glue for joining, scissors for cutting paper/fabric; safety rules for scissors, wire-cutters and wire (pass closed, rings forward; never hold wire at eye level); general workshop safety rules and organising the workplace. The learner works through the named materials and steps in order, saying what each step does before doing the next one.

## Sub lesson 2 — Tools and safe use — doing it yourself
Now the learner does the whole task unaided: glue for joining, scissors for cutting paper/fabric; safety rules for scissors, wire-cutters and wire (pass closed, rings forward; never hold wire at eye level); general workshop safety rules and organising the workplace. Working from the first sub lesson's sequence, the learner plans the make, works through it step by step, and checks the finished piece against what the Kazakh book shows.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: Tools and safe use — glue for joining, scissors for cutting paper/fabric; safety rules for scissors, wire-cutters and wire (pass closed, rings forward; never hold wire at eye level); general workshop safety rules and organising the workplace. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.

## Do It Yourself
1. Do the Do it first task again, carefully this time, using everything the lesson taught.
2. Check your work against the lesson's own checks — fix one thing you find.
3. Keep your work: it is practice evidence, and the next lesson builds on it.
