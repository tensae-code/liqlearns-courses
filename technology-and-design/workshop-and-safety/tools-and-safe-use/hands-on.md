# Hands-on — Tools and safe use

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: glue for joining, scissors for cutting paper/fabric; safety rules for scissors, wire-cutters and wire (pass closed, rings forward; never hold wire at eye level); general workshop safety rules and organising the workplace. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
