# Tools and safe use — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Say in one sentence what this lesson teaches.
**Answer:** Tools and safe use — glue for joining, scissors for cutting paper/fabric; safety rules for scissors, wire-cutters and wire (pass closed, rings forward; never hold wire at eye level); general workshop safety rules and organising the workplace.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name one material, tool or step this lesson works with.
**Answer:** Any one named in the lesson text — for example, the first step of sub lesson 1: Tools and safe use.
