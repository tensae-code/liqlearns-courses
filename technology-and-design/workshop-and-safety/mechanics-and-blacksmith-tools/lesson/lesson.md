# Mechanics and blacksmith tools

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Rwanda's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Mechanics tools of the garage
Rwanda's Primary 6 book opens its tools unit at the garage: spanners, hack saw, pliers, bench vice, screwdriver and the mechanics hammer — each identified, its uses named, and how to use and maintain it taught, down to where a mechanic stores tools. The guide's key competence says it in one line: use and maintain mechanics and blacksmith tools safely.

## Sub lesson 2 — Blacksmith tools of the forge
The second trade is the blacksmith's: the forge tools and their work — the blacksmith hammer and blacksmith's pliers named in the guide's tool kit among them. Forge work is hot work: the tools are shaped for gripping, holding and striking metal at the fire, and their care is part of the craft.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: the garage and the forge each have their own tools — spanners, pliers and the bench vice at the bench; the forge tools at the fire — and both trades keep their tools used rightly, maintained, and stored where they belong. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
