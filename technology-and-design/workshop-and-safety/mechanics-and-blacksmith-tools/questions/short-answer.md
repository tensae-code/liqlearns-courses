# Mechanics and blacksmith tools — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the six mechanics tools in Rwanda's Primary 6 book.
**Answer:** Spanners, hack saw, pliers, bench vice, screwdriver and mechanics hammer.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is the unit's key competence, in the guide's words?
**Answer:** To be able to use and maintain mechanics and blacksmith tools safely.
