# Mechanics and blacksmith tools — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which list names Rwanda's garage tools?
a) Spanners, hack saw, pliers, bench vice, screwdriver, mechanics hammer b) Needle, thread, scissors c) Hoe, rake, watering can d) T-square, saw, plane
**Answer: a) Spanners, hack saw, pliers, bench vice, screwdriver, mechanics hammer**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What else does the mechanics lesson teach besides identifying tools?
a) Prices b) Their uses, how to use and maintain them, and where a mechanic stores tools c) Their colours d) Their inventors
**Answer: b) Their uses, how to use and maintain them, and where a mechanic stores tools**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Where does the blacksmith work?
a) At the bench vice b) At the forge c) At the drawing board d) In the garden
**Answer: b) At the forge**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which tools does the guide's kit name for the blacksmith?
a) Blacksmith hammer and blacksmith's pliers b) Needle and thread c) Spanner and vice d) Saw and T-square
**Answer: a) Blacksmith hammer and blacksmith's pliers**

**5. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the guide's key competence for this unit?
a) To sell tools b) To be able to use and maintain mechanics and blacksmith tools safely c) To draw tools d) To count tools
**Answer: b) To be able to use and maintain mechanics and blacksmith tools safely**
