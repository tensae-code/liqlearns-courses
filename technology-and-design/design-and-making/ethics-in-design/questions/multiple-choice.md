# Ethics in design — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is originality?
a) designing your own work — no plagiarism b) Only a price c) Only decoration d) Nothing to do with this lesson
**Answer: a) designing your own work — no plagiarism**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is a patent?
a) Only decoration b) a protection for an invention c) Only a price d) A kind of glue
**Answer: b) a protection for an invention**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is copyright?
a) Only history b) Only painting c) a protection for original creative work d) Only storage
**Answer: c) a protection for original creative work**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson teach, in one line?
a) ethical design is original, honestly priced, useful, environmentally friendly and safe — and respects patent, trademark, copyright and industrial design. b) Only prices c) Only old tools d) Nothing practical
**Answer: a) ethical design is original, honestly priced, useful, environmentally friendly and safe — and respects patent, trademark, copyright and industrial design.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** How does Malaysia's book teach this?
a) As theory only, never practised b) Only in the glossary c) Only as an exam d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Malay (RBT)
**Answer: d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Malay (RBT)**
