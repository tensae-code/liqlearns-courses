# Ethics in design

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Malaysia's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — The designer’s duties
Ethics in design is a list of duties: originality — no plagiarism; honesty in pricing; usefulness; being environmentally friendly, with recycled materials; and safety in use. A design can be clever and still be wrong.

## Sub lesson 2 — Intellectual property in Malaysia
Other people’s designs are protected: patent, trademark, copyright and industrial design are Malaysia’s named protections. Respecting them is part of designing, not an extra.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: ethical design is original, honestly priced, useful, environmentally friendly and safe — and respects patent, trademark, copyright and industrial design. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
