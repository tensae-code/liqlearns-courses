# Hands-on — Setting up an enterprise

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## Mini market study
Choose a product for your school or neighbourhood. Survey five people: would you buy it, at what price, how often? List two rivals and their prices. Write your verdict: go, change, or stop — with the evidence.

## Price it like the fan maker
Cost your product per unit: materials + energy + work + distribution. Add a margin of your choice. Show the full calculation, then check your price against the rivals from your study.
