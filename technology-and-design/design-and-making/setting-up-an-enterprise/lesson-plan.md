# Lesson plan — Setting up an enterprise

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Design and Making · **Lesson:** Setting up an enterprise
**Place in the curriculum:** Design and Making (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Morocco expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** setting up an enterprise runs in order: find the idea, study the market, choose a name and logo, fix the selling price from costs and margin, and write the commercial plan that will sell the product.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on teacher-guide evidence only — Morocco's Technologie teacher guides (Ministère de l'Éducation Nationale, du Préscolaire et des Sports, Royaume du Maroc) are the books in hand for this topic (see lesson/sources.md). Teacher guides are evidence, counted nowhere in the acceptance count. The pupil manuals themselves are not in hand; the placements are filed from the guides' answer elements (Part 5) as guide evidence.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **Finding the idea and studying the market** — An enterprise starts from an idea for a product that answers a real need. The market study tests the idea: who are the clients, what do they want, who else sells it, at what price? Morocco works the study on real cases — a survey for a solar-energy product, and a product study of an aerovoltaic panel.
2. **Name and logo, price from costs, and the commercial plan** — The enterprise needs an identity — a name and a logo clients remember (the course compares Moroccan company logos) — and a price fixed from arithmetic: unit cost plus margin. The commercial plan then organises selling: advertising (the course analyses posters), packaging levels, and modern selling channels.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of Design and Making. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Carry out a small market study: clients, wants, rivals, prices.
2. Fix a selling price from unit costs plus margin, showing the arithmetic.
3. Describe a commercial plan: name and logo, advertising, packaging, channels.
## What you need
Paper, a calculator, and a product idea of the learner's own.
