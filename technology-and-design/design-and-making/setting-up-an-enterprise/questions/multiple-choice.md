# Setting up an enterprise — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A market study tests an idea by asking…
a) who the clients are, what they want, who the rivals are and their prices b) only the maker's friends c) whether the logo is pretty d) nothing — ideas need no testing
**Answer: a) who the clients are, what they want, who the rivals are and their prices**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Morocco's course surveys future clients for which product?
a) A solar-energy product b) A fan only c) A school bag d) A bicycle
**Answer: a) A solar-energy product**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The selling price is fixed as…
a) unit cost plus margin b) the rival's price copied blindly c) the highest price imaginable d) cost minus margin
**Answer: a) unit cost plus margin**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Packaging levels include…
a) the product's own pack, a grouping pack and a transport pack b) only a gift ribbon c) one level only, ever d) no packaging at all
**Answer: a) the product's own pack, a grouping pack and a transport pack**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** A logo's job in the commercial plan is…
a) to give the enterprise an identity clients remember b) to replace the product c) to fix the price d) to pay the workers
**Answer: a) to give the enterprise an identity clients remember**
