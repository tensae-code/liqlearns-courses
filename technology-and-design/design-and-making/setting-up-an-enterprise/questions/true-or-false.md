# Setting up an enterprise — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** If a market study shows no clients, the honest move is to change the idea.
**Answer: True** — The study exists to test the idea; its verdict governs whether to go, change or stop.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The selling price should be set by wishing for the most money possible.
**Answer: False** — Price is calculated from unit costs plus a margin, then checked against the market.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Advertising and packaging belong to the commercial plan.
**Answer: True** — Morocco's course places poster analysis, packaging levels and selling channels inside the commercial plan.
