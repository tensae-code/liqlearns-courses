# Setting up an enterprise — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** List the steps of setting up an enterprise in order.
**Answer:** Find the idea; study the market; choose name and logo; fix the selling price from costs and margin; write and run the commercial plan.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A fan costs 40 to make per unit and the margin is 10. What is the selling price, and how was it fixed?
**Answer:** 50 — selling price = unit cost (40) + margin (10).
