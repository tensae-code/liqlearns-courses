# Hands-on — Fabrication sheet / fiche de fabrication

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## Sheet for a real make
During your next practical make, keep the fabrication sheet as you work: materials, tools, operations in order. Afterwards, hand the sheet to another group and note any question they must ask you — each question marks a gap in the sheet.

## Improvement pass
Take a finished sheet and mark the one step that wasted the most material or time. Write the changed step for the next make and say what it should save.
