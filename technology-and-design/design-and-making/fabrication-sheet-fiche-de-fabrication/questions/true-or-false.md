# Fabrication sheet / fiche de fabrication — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A make without its fabrication sheet can still be checked fully against its specification.
**Answer: False** — Without the record of with what and how it was made, the make cannot be checked or repeated.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The sheet should be written from the real work, including changes made during fabrication.
**Answer: True** — Guessed or copied steps corrupt the record; the sheet follows the fabrication as it happened.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The fabrication sheet is an optional extra after the real deliverable.
**Answer: False** — In the Haiti sequence the sheet is part of the deliverable each group produces.
