# Fabrication sheet / fiche de fabrication — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Why must the sheet record changes made during making, not just the plan?
**Answer:** Because the sheet is evidence of the real fabrication; a reader repeating the make needs what was actually done, and checking against the specification compares the real process, not the intention.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** How does a fabrication sheet help a group improve its next make?
**Answer:** The wasteful step — in material or time — is visible on the sheet, so the group can change that exact step next time instead of relying on memory.
