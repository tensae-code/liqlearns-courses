# Fabrication sheet / fiche de fabrication — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A fabrication sheet records…
a) Only the final price b) With what and how a thing was made c) The maker's opinions d) Nothing after delivery
**Answer: b) With what and how a thing was made**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Haiti's fishing sequence, the fabrication sheet is produced by…
a) The teacher alone b) Each group, for its own boat maquette c) Nobody — it is optional d) The boat's buyer
**Answer: b) Each group, for its own boat maquette**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** If a step changed during making, the sheet records…
a) The original plan only b) The change — what was actually done c) Nothing about steps d) A guess
**Answer: b) The change — what was actually done**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A good sheet lets a reader…
a) Rebuild the same object from the sheet alone b) Skip the making c) Avoid all tools d) Sell the object
**Answer: a) Rebuild the same object from the sheet alone**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Improvements to the next make are planned from…
a) Memory alone b) The fabrication sheet, where wasteful steps are visible c) The colour of the model d) Chance
**Answer: b) The fabrication sheet, where wasteful steps are visible**
