# Organising small-series production — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** State the two questions that separate prototype work from series work.
**Answer:** Prototype: 'Can we make it work?' Series: 'Can we make it repeatedly, to the same standard?'

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why is each unit checked against the prototype rather than against the previous unit?
**Answer:** Because checking against the previous unit lets errors accumulate and the standard drift; the tested prototype is the fixed standard for the whole run.
