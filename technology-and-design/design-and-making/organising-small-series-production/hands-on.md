# Hands-on — Organising small-series production

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## Organise a run
Plan a small series of a simple device (your tested filter, or a model). Write the roles, the operation order, the materials for the full run, and the per-unit check. Run it for at least four units.

## Drift audit
Compare the first and last units of a run side by side against the prototype standard. List every difference and trace each to its cause in the organisation.
