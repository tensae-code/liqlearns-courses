# Costing and pricing a made product — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A price can be set well without knowing the unit cost.
**Answer: False** — The price is built from the cost price and production cost; without them it is a wish.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Margin is what the makers add for the enterprise to live and grow.
**Answer: True** — Margin stands between the costs and the selling price, beside taxes.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Copying a competitor's price guarantees a sound price.
**Answer: False** — A copied price blind to your own costs may sell at a loss; build from costs, then position against the market.
