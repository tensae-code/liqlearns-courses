# Costing and pricing a made product — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Pricing starts from…
a) The competitor's price b) The cost price and production cost of the device c) A round number d) The buyer's face
**Answer: b) The cost price and production cost of the device**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Unit costs are counted from…
a) Imagination b) The real production run c) Last year's prices d) Nothing
**Answer: b) The real production run**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The selling price is constructed as…
a) Costs alone b) Costs + margin + taxes c) Taxes alone d) Margin minus costs
**Answer: b) Costs + margin + taxes**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Leaving taxes out of the price…
a) Is wise b) Invents profit that is not there c) Lowers costs d) Is required
**Answer: b) Invents profit that is not there**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Positioning against competition means…
a) Copying their price blindly b) Setting the built price with the market's prices in view c) Ignoring the market d) Always undercutting
**Answer: b) Setting the built price with the market's prices in view**
