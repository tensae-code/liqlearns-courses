# Costing and pricing a made product — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Write the selling-price construction in order.
**Answer:** Selling price = cost price / production cost + margin + taxes.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Why must a maker use 'both eyes' — costs and competition — when pricing?
**Answer:** Costs alone may produce a price no buyer will meet; competition alone may produce a price below cost. Building from costs and positioning against the market avoids both failures.
