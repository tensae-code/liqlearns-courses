# Combining materials in one article — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Recovered material is material saved from use or waste and used again.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Offcut has nothing to do with this lesson.
**Answer: False** — It is part of the lesson: a leftover piece, used here for testing joins.
