# Hands-on — The maker's materials and tools

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: the materials the art-labour lesson needs and "the secrets of little masters" (Term 1, Lesson 1). Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
