# The maker's materials and tools — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson teach?
a) The maker's materials and tools — the materials the art-labour lesson needs and "the secrets of little masters" (Term 1, Lesson 1) b) Only how to draw pictures c) Nothing practical d) A song to memorise
**Answer: a) The maker's materials and tools — the materials the art-labour lesson needs and "the secrets of little masters" (Term 1, Lesson 1)**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Sub lesson 1 is about…
a) The maker's materials and tools b) Cooking lunch c) A sports match d) The weather
**Answer: a) The maker's materials and tools**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Sub lesson 2 asks the learner to…
a) Complete the making in full and check it b) Stop before starting c) Copy a partner's work d) Throw the materials away
**Answer: a) Complete the making in full and check it**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Kazakhstan teaches this topic inside which school subjects?
a) Artistic Labour and Labour Training b) Only mathematics c) Only history d) It is not taught at school
**Answer: a) Artistic Labour and Labour Training**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** What should the learner do first in this lesson?
