# Hands-on — Diffusing a product

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## Design the brochure
Design the advertising brochure for your class device: what it is, the need it answers, its price, how to get it. Test it on a reader outside the class and record what they understood and missed.

## Close the arc
Write the project's whole arc in five lines — need surveyed, specification, prototype, production and price, diffusion — and under it the sequence's definition of entrepreneurship in your own words.
