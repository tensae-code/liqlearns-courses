# Diffusing a product — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Diffusion means…
a) Destroying the product b) Making the product known to its users c) Storing it silently d) Raising its price
**Answer: b) Making the product known to its users**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A device unknown to its users is…
a) Fully successful b) Worth nothing to the people who never hear of it c) Cheaper to make d) Finished
**Answer: b) Worth nothing to the people who never hear of it**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The communication support designed in the sequence is…
a) A radio station b) An advertising brochure for the water-filter device c) A billboard law d) A price tag only
**Answer: b) An advertising brochure for the water-filter device**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A brochure must say…
a) Only the maker's name b) What the device is, the need it answers, its price and how to get it c) Nothing about price d) Only praise
**Answer: b) What the device is, the need it answers, its price and how to get it**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** The sequence defines entrepreneurship as…
a) Selling anything at any price b) Answering a societal need with a good or service c) Avoiding society d) Making one prototype
**Answer: b) Answering a societal need with a good or service**
