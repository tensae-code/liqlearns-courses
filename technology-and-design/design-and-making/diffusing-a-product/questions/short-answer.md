# Diffusing a product — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the four things the brochure must tell its reader.
**Answer:** What the device is, what need it answers, what it costs, and how to get it.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** How did the class's own project embody the definition of entrepreneurship?
**Answer:** It began from a surveyed societal need (drinking-water access) and answered it with a good — a specified, prototyped, produced, priced and diffused water-filter device.
