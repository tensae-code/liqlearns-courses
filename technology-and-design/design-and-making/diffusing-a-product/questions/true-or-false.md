# Diffusing a product — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The technology project ends when production ends.
**Answer: False** — Diffusion — making the product known — is the closing part of the project, taught after production and pricing.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A brochure is itself a designed product.
**Answer: True** — It is designed for a reader who must be persuaded, using the course's design skills.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The class's entrepreneurship definition was only read, never lived.
**Answer: False** — The class lived it: surveyed need, specification, prototype, production, price and diffusion of a real device.
