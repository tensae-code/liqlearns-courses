# Analysing the project plan and building the work schedule

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Malaysia's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Breaking the work down
A schedule is built, not guessed: identify the activities — the work breakdown structure — then order them as they must truly happen.

## Sub lesson 2 — Estimating and building
Estimate the resources each activity needs and the time it takes, build the schedule from those estimates, then estimate cost and form the budget. Each estimate feeds the next step.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: build a schedule in order: break down the work, order it, estimate resources and time, build the schedule, then cost and budget it. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.

## Do It Yourself
1. Do the Do it first task again, carefully this time, using everything the lesson taught.
2. Check your work against the lesson's own checks — fix one thing you find.
3. Keep your work: it is practice evidence, and the next lesson builds on it.
