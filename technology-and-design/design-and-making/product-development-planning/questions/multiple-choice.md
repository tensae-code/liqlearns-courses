# Product development, planning — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the agreed concept?
a) the design concept the team has settled on b) Only a price c) Only decoration d) Nothing to do with this lesson
**Answer: a) the design concept the team has settled on**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is planning in sketches?
a) Only decoration b) working out elements and principles as drawings c) Only a price d) A kind of glue

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is the product sketch?
a) Only history b) Only painting c) the agreed design drawn whole, ready for the build d) Only storage
**Answer: c) the agreed design drawn whole, ready for the build**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson teach, in one line?

**Answer: a) development planning turns the agreed concept into sketches of elements and principles, ending in the product sketch.**
