# Product development, planning — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The agreed concept is the design concept the team has settled on.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Planning in sketches has nothing to do with this lesson.
**Answer: False** — It is part of the lesson: working out elements and principles as drawings.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The product sketch is the agreed design drawn whole, ready for the build.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.
