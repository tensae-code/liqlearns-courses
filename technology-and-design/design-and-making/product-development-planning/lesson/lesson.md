# Product development, planning

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Malaysia's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Planning the agreed concept
Development planning fixes the agreed concept: the design elements and principles are planned as sketches, so the concept is argued in drawings before it is argued in materials.

## Sub lesson 2 — Producing the product sketch
The planning ends in the product sketch — the agreed design, drawn whole — which the build that follows will be organised around and judged against.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: development planning turns the agreed concept into sketches of elements and principles, ending in the product sketch. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
