# Making a model / maquette from drawings to answer a specification — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why do the drawings come before the cutting?
**Answer:** Because the drawings fix the model's shape and sizes from the specification, so every part is made to a checked plan instead of a guess.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Name two things a finished maquette cannot prove.
**Answer:** It cannot prove the real thing's strength or durability (and, for a boat, its seaworthiness) — only the real object can answer those.
