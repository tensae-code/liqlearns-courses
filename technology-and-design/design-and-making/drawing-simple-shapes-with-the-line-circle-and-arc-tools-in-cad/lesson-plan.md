# Lesson plan — Drawing simple shapes with the line, circle and arc tools in CAD software

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Design and Making · **Lesson:** Drawing simple shapes with the line, circle and arc tools in CAD software
**Place in the curriculum:** Design and Making (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Iran expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the first CAD drawing skill is three tools — line, circle and arc — from which every simple shape is built.
**This lesson's own level:** stocked from Iran's *Work & Technology, Grade 9* (student book) — see lesson/sources.md. Iran counts in the acceptance count (student books, not evidence-only).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **Line and circle** — The line tool draws a straight segment between two points the user fixes; joined lines build any straight-edged shape.
2. **The arc and simple shapes** — The arc tool draws part of a circle — fixed by points, centre or angles according to the software.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
