# Drawing simple shapes with the line, circle and arc tools in CAD software — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In your own words, what is line tool?
**Answer:** The CAD tool that draws a straight segment between two fixed points.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In your own words, what is arc tool?
**Answer:** The CAD tool that draws part of a circle.
