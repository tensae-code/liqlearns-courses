# Drawing simple shapes with the line, circle and arc tools in CAD software

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Iran's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Line and circle
The line tool draws a straight segment between two points the user fixes; joined lines build any straight-edged shape. The circle tool fixes a centre and a radius (or diameter) and draws a true circle no hand could match. Iran's Grade 9 Computer Drawing module teaches these two first, as the alphabet of CAD.

## Sub lesson 2 — The arc and simple shapes
The arc tool draws part of a circle — fixed by points, centre or angles according to the software. Lines, circles and arcs together draw the simple shapes of real parts: plates with rounded corners, holes, slots and handles. The skill is choosing which tool makes each edge of the shape in front of you.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: the first CAD drawing skill is three tools — line, circle and arc — from which every simple shape is built. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
