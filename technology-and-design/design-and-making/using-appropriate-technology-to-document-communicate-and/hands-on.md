# Hands-on — Using appropriate technology to document, communicate and present design and project work

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: Unit 2 Topic 4 (benchmark 10.1.2.4, the only Unit 2 topic elaborated): appropriate technology for textile projects; functions of textile tools (measuring, pattern, marking, cutting tools). Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
