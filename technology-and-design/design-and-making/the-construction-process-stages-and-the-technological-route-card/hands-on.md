# Hands-on — The construction process stages and the technological route card

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## Write the school card
For an article you will make or have made, write the carta tecnológica escolar: stages, operations, materials and tools.

## Compare card and article
Lay the finished (or pictured) article beside its card. Find one stage the card described well and one it missed. Improve the card.
