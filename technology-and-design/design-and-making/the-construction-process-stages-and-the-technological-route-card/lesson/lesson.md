# The construction process stages and the technological route card

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Cuba's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Stages, written as a school card
Cuba's 2023 Grade 7 book teaches the construction process stages through the carta tecnológica escolar: the learner writes the stages of their own article's making, in order, before making starts.

## Sub lesson 2 — From card to article
The card is then the build's companion: work follows it stage by stage, and the finished article is compared against it. The habit transfers — any new article can be planned the same way, because the stages do not change, only the article does.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: the school technological card (carta tecnológica escolar) turns the construction process into a school habit: stages in order, operations named, means listed — the same discipline the route card teaches, written for the learner's own build. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
