# The construction process stages and the technological route card — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is school technological card (carta tecnológica escolar)?
a) the learner's written plan of the construction stages for their article b) Only a price c) Only decoration d) Nothing to do with this lesson
**Answer: a) the learner's written plan of the construction stages for their article**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is construction process?
a) Only history b) the whole sequence of stages by which an article is made c) Only storage d) A kind of glue
**Answer: b) the whole sequence of stages by which an article is made**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is operation?
a) Only old tools b) Only painting c) one named step inside a stage d) Only a price
**Answer: c) one named step inside a stage**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson teach, in one line?
a) Only decoration b) Nothing to do with this lesson c) Only history d) the school technological card (carta tecnológica escolar) turns the construction process into a school habit: stages in order, operations named, means listed — the same discipline the route card teaches, written for the learner's own build.
**Answer: d) the school technological card (carta tecnológica escolar) turns the construction process into a school habit: stages in order, operations named, means listed — the same discipline the route card teaches, written for the learner's own build.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** How does Cuba's book teach this?
a) As theory only, never practised b) Only in a glossary c) Only as an exam d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Spanish
**Answer: d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Spanish**
