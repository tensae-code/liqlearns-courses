# The construction process stages and the technological route card — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In your own words, what is school technological card (carta tecnológica escolar)?
**Answer:** The learner's written plan of the construction stages for their article.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In your own words, what is operation?
**Answer:** One named step inside a stage.
