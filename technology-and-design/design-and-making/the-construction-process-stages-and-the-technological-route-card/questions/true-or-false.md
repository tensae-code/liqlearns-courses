# The construction process stages and the technological route card — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** School technological card (carta tecnológica escolar) is the learner's written plan of the construction stages for their article.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Construction process has nothing to do with this lesson.
**Answer: False** — It is part of the lesson: the whole sequence of stages by which an article is made.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Operation is one named step inside a stage.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.
