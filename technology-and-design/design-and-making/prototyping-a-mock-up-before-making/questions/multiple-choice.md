# Prototyping — a mock-up before making — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) A mock-up is made from…**
a) The final expensive material b) Cheap material like cardboard, at full size c) Metal only d) Nothing — it is imagined
**Answer: b) Cheap material like cardboard, at full size**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) The main reason to mock up first is…**
a) To practise cutting b) To find faults while fixing them is cheap c) To use up cardboard d) To delay making
**Answer: b) To find faults while fixing them is cheap**

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Jamaica's car-body mock-up is checked for compatibility with…**
a) The paint b) The chassis c) Another class's car d) The toolbox
**Answer: b) The chassis**

**4. (Sub lesson 2) A body that looks well but does not fit its chassis is…**
a) A success b) A failed body c) Finished d) A finish
**Answer: b) A failed body**

**5. (Sub lesson 3) A project is finished when…**
a) The making stops b) The testing for accuracy and workability passes c) The bell rings d) The paint dries
**Answer: b) The testing for accuracy and workability passes**
