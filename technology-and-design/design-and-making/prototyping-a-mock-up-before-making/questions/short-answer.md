# Prototyping — a mock-up before making — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, or by doing the step named.

**1. (Sub lesson 2) Your mock-up body sits crooked on the chassis and one wheel arch is too small. What do you do, and why is this good news?**
**Answer:** Trim and remake the cardboard until it fits — the faults were found in cheap card, before the real material was cut.

**2. (Sub lesson 3) Name the two final tests of a finished project.**
**Answer:** Accuracy (matches the design/dimensions) and workability (actually runs/works as intended).
