# Hands-on — Prototyping — a mock-up before making

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## Mock-up and check
Mock up one part of a design in cardboard at full size. Check it against everything it must fit, list every fault found, and fix the card — not a drawing — until it fits.

## Final test
Test any finished project you have for accuracy (against its plan) and workability (does it do its job?). Write the two verdicts separately.
