# Hands-on — Engineering material specification

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: Types of Engineering Materials in Specific Field; Methods of Categorising Engineering Material; Engineering Implication to Development; Method of Problem-Solving on Engineering Effect. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
