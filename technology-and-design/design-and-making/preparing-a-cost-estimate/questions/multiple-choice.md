# Preparing a cost estimate — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the unit/quantity method?
a) costing by unit price multiplied by quantity b) Only a price c) Only decoration d) Nothing to do with this lesson
**Answer: a) costing by unit price multiplied by quantity**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is SMART?
a) Only decoration b) Specify, Measurable, Attainable, Realistic, Time-Bound — the test of a good target c) Only a price d) A kind of glue
**Answer: b) Specify, Measurable, Attainable, Realistic, Time-Bound — the test of a good target**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is dividing tasks?
a) Only history b) Only painting c) giving each team member named work and roles d) Only storage
**Answer: c) giving each team member named work and roles**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson teach, in one line?
a) a cost estimate multiplies unit price by quantity (in RM), sets SMART targets, and ends by forming the team and dividing tasks. b) Only prices c) Only old tools d) Nothing practical
**Answer: a) a cost estimate multiplies unit price by quantity (in RM), sets SMART targets, and ends by forming the team and dividing tasks.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** How does Malaysia's book teach this?
a) As theory only, never practised b) Only in the glossary c) Only as an exam d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Malay (RBT)
**Answer: d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Malay (RBT)**
