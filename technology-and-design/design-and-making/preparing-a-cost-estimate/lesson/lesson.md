# Preparing a cost estimate

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Malaysia's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — The unit/quantity method
A cost estimate is calculated, not felt: the unit/quantity method prices each item by its unit price times its quantity, worked in the book as an RM example, ringgit by ringgit.

## Sub lesson 2 — SMART, and the team
Targets are set SMART — Specify, Measurable, Attainable, Realistic, Time-Bound — and the estimate closes with people: forming the work team and dividing tasks and roles so the numbers have owners.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: a cost estimate multiplies unit price by quantity (in RM), sets SMART targets, and ends by forming the team and dividing tasks. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
