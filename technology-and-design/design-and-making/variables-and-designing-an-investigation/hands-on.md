# Hands-on — Variables and designing an investigation

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: relations between variables, theory/research/scientific method, survey vs descriptive vs experimental research, reliability and threats to it, generalising results. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
