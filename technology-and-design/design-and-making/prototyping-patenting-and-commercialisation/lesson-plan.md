# Lesson plan — Prototyping, patenting and commercialisation

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Design and Making · **Lesson:** Prototyping, patenting and commercialisation
**Place in the curriculum:** Design and Making (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Iran expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** an idea becomes a product by being prototyped and improved, protected by a patent if it is truly new, and commercialised — produced and marketed by a route the maker chooses.
**This lesson's own level:** stocked from Iran's *Use of New Technologies, Grade 11* (student book) — see lesson/sources.md. Iran counts in the acceptance count (student books, not evidence-only).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **Prototype, then improve** — A prototype (pilot) is the first working form of the product, built to be tested.
2. **Patent and the routes to market** — A patent protects an invention that meets three conditions: it is novel (new), useful, and non-obvious.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of Design and Making. Recommended, never forced.
