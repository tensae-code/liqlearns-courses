# Prototyping, patenting and commercialisation — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Prototype is the first working form of a product, built for testing and improvement.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Patent has nothing to do with this lesson.
**Answer: False** — It is part of the lesson: legal protection for an invention that is novel, useful and non-obvious.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Commercialisation is turning a product into market sales by a chosen production and marketing route.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.
