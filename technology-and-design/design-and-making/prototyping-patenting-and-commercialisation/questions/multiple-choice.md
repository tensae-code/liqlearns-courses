# Prototyping, patenting and commercialisation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is prototype?
a) the first working form of a product, built for testing and improvement b) Only a price c) Only decoration d) Nothing to do with this lesson
**Answer: a) the first working form of a product, built for testing and improvement**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is patent?
a) Only history b) legal protection for an invention that is novel, useful and non-obvious c) Only storage d) A kind of glue
**Answer: b) legal protection for an invention that is novel, useful and non-obvious**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is commercialisation?
a) Only old tools b) Only painting c) turning a product into market sales by a chosen production and marketing route d) Only a price
**Answer: c) turning a product into market sales by a chosen production and marketing route**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson teach, in one line?
a) Only decoration b) Nothing to do with this lesson c) Only history d) an idea becomes a product by being prototyped and improved, protected by a patent if it is truly new, and commercialised — produced and marketed by a route the maker chooses.
**Answer: d) an idea becomes a product by being prototyped and improved, protected by a patent if it is truly new, and commercialised — produced and marketed by a route the maker chooses.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** How does Iran's book teach this?
a) As theory only, never practised b) Only in a glossary c) Only as an exam d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Persian
**Answer: d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Persian**
