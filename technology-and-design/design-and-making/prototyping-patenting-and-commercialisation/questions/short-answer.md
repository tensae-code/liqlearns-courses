# Prototyping, patenting and commercialisation — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In your own words, what is prototype?
**Answer:** The first working form of a product, built for testing and improvement.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In your own words, what is commercialisation?
**Answer:** Turning a product into market sales by a chosen production and marketing route.
