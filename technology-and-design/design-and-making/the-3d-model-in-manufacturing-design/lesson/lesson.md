# The 3D model in manufacturing design

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Malaysia's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Sketch, mould, make
Manufacturing design works through a 3D model: an informative 3D model sketch is made and analysed, the mould (acuan) for the model is made, and the model itself is produced by the work process, step by step.

## Sub lesson 2 — Finishing, evaluating, improving
The model is finished using electrostatic and electroplating concepts, then evaluated — and the evaluation generates ideas for a more efficient product design. The model is a manufacturing argument, not a toy.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: the 3D model runs sketch → mould (acuan) → make → finish (electrostatic, electroplating) → evaluate → ideas for a more efficient design. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
