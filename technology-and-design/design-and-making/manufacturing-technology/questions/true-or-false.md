# Manufacturing technology — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Manufacturing technology is the technology of making products at production scale.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Conventional manufacturing has nothing to do with this lesson.
**Answer: False** — It is part of the lesson: production by older, simpler methods.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Modern manufacturing is production by current machine and system methods.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.
