# Manufacturing technology — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is manufacturing technology?
a) the technology of making products at production scale b) Only a price c) Only decoration d) Nothing to do with this lesson
**Answer: a) the technology of making products at production scale**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is conventional manufacturing?
a) Only decoration b) production by older, simpler methods c) Only a price d) A kind of glue
**Answer: b) production by older, simpler methods**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is modern manufacturing?
a) Only history b) Only painting c) production by current machine and system methods d) Only storage
**Answer: c) production by current machine and system methods**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson teach, in one line?
a) manufacturing technology makes products at scale — and conventional and modern manufacturing are compared process by process. b) Only prices c) Only old tools d) Nothing practical
**Answer: a) manufacturing technology makes products at scale — and conventional and modern manufacturing are compared process by process.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** How does Malaysia's book teach this?
a) As theory only, never practised b) Only in the glossary c) Only as an exam d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Malay (RBT)
**Answer: d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Malay (RBT)**
