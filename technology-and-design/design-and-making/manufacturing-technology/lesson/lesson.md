# Manufacturing technology

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Malaysia's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Identifying manufacturing technology
Manufacturing technology is the technology of making products at production scale. Malaysia teaches learners to identify it in the product-production process around them.

## Sub lesson 2 — Conventional against modern manufacturing
The comparison runs again at factory scale: conventional manufacturing against modern manufacturing, process by process, so learners see what modern methods change — and what they cost.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: manufacturing technology makes products at scale — and conventional and modern manufacturing are compared process by process. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
