# Engineering development — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson teach?
a) Engineering development — Methods on Engineering Development in Specific Fields; Types of Engineering Fields; Methods of Process Material from Raw to Finished Product; Types of Equipment used in Engineering b) Only how to draw pictures c) Nothing practical d) A song to memorise
**Answer: a) Engineering development — Methods on Engineering Development in Specific Fields; Types of Engineering Fields; Methods of Process Material from Raw to Finished Product; Types of Equipment used in Engineering**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Sub lesson 1 is about…
a) Engineering development b) Cooking lunch c) A sports match d) The weather
**Answer: a) Engineering development**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Sub lesson 2 asks the learner to…
a) Complete the making in full and check it b) Stop before starting c) Copy a partner's work d) Throw the materials away
**Answer: a) Complete the making in full and check it**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Papua New Guinea teaches this topic inside which school course?
a) Technology and Industrial Arts (TIA) b) Only mathematics c) Only history d) It is not taught at school
**Answer: a) Technology and Industrial Arts (TIA)**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** What should the learner do first in this lesson?
a) Try the Do it first task in their own way b) Skip to the last page c) Wait to be given the answer d) Nothing at all
**Answer: a) Try the Do it first task in their own way**
