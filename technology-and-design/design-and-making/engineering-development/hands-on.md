# Hands-on — Engineering development

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: Methods on Engineering Development in Specific Fields; Types of Engineering Fields; Methods of Process Material from Raw to Finished Product; Types of Equipment used in Engineering. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
