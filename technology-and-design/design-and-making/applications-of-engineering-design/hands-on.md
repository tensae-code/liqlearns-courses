# Hands-on — Applications of engineering design

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: Methods of Engineering Designs Structures in Various Field; Types of Design Specification and Symbols used in Engineering; The Common Significant Design Specifications Used and Applied in Engineering. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
