# Product development, starting the product — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is a customer-needs study?
a) finding out what customers need before stating the product b) Only a price c) Only decoration d) Nothing to do with this lesson
**Answer: a) finding out what customers need before stating the product**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the project brief?
a) Only decoration b) the statement of the product to be made c) Only a price d) A kind of glue
**Answer: b) the statement of the product to be made**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is technology bases?
a) Only history b) Only painting c) the technologies that fit a product’s design d) Only storage
**Answer: c) the technologies that fit a product’s design**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson teach, in one line?
a) product development starts by stating the product from a customer-needs study and the brief, then identifying the technology bases that fit it. b) Only prices c) Only old tools d) Nothing practical
**Answer: a) product development starts by stating the product from a customer-needs study and the brief, then identifying the technology bases that fit it.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** How does Malaysia's book teach this?
a) As theory only, never practised b) Only in the glossary c) Only as an exam d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Malay (RBT)
**Answer: d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Malay (RBT)**
