# Product development, starting the product

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Malaysia's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Starting from the customer
Product development starts outside the workshop: a customer-needs study and the project brief state the product to be made. Nothing is developed for nobody.

## Sub lesson 2 — The technology bases
The product’s technology bases are then identified — the technologies that fit this product’s design — so development begins standing on named, suitable ground.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: product development starts by stating the product from a customer-needs study and the brief, then identifying the technology bases that fit it. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
