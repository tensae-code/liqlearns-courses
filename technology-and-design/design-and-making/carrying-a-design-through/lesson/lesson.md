# Carrying a design through

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Vietnam's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Carrying a design through
Vietnam's book teaches it this way: Carrying a design through (*Công nghệ 5*, printed p. 18). The learner works through the named materials and steps in order, saying what each step does before doing the next one.

## Sub lesson 2 — Carrying a design through — doing it yourself
Now the learner does the whole task unaided: Carrying a design through. Working from the first sub lesson's sequence, the learner plans the make, works through it step by step, and checks the finished piece against what Vietnam's book shows.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: Carrying a design through. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
