# Mechanical components

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Malaysia's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Gears in their types
Mechanical components are identified in families — gears first, in their types: bevel, spur, helical, herringbone, rack and pinion, miter, screw, internal, spiral bevel and worm — beside the other machine elements the chapter names.

## Sub lesson 2 — How a mechanical system works
Components become a system: Malaysia takes a chosen product and reads its mechanical system whole — which components, meshing how, doing what work — so identification ends in understanding.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: mechanical components — gears in ten named types and the other machine elements — are read as one working system in a chosen product. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
