# Mechanical components — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A spur gear is one of the named gear types.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A worm gear has nothing to do with this lesson.
**Answer: False** — It is part of the lesson: one of the named gear types, driving at a right angle.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A mechanical system is components working together in a product to do mechanical work.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.
