| the technical design process (quy trình thiết kế kĩ thuật), a sequence in which some activities repeat until the wanted result is reached: step 1 define the problem (the first work; by its end the questions must be answered clearly — what problem or need needs solving, and why that problem or need needs solving; problems and needs are identified through observing the natural world and people's living environment, reading documents, surveying users' needs, exchanging views to discover unsolved existences and shortcomings or needs for improvement and human wishes in each specific context); step 2 research the overview (the main work is researching existing knowledge and solutions and preparing fully the basis for the following problem-solving activities; a good overview inherits others' experience, avoids repeating others' design errors, clarifies who will use the product, what solutions already exist in practice and what science and technology will be used to solve the problem; overview information comes from researching documents, scientific works and related technology, reading technical documents about existing products, and talking directly with users and experts); step 3 determine the requirements (propose the requirements and detailed criteria the product must achieve; one way to build criteria is analysing existing solutions or products; the product's requirements and criteria must be stated clearly and expressed through (1) the functions and performance standards of each function, (2) the limits on physical quantities such as mass and size, (3) the issues to care about — finance, environmental protection, safety, aesthetics); step 4 propose, evaluate and choose a solution (proposing solutions: there are always many solutions to a problem, so propose as many as possible, sticking to the requirements and criteria set; evaluating and choosing: from the proposed solutions, examine and evaluate comprehensively the fit with the product's requirements and criteria, and from there choose the best solution that just meets the product's requirements and criteria and fits the financial, technological, equipment and human resources available; to ensure the chosen solution is optimal, review and answer the questions — what is the solution's biggest advantage, what limitations remain, is there a way to overcome the limitations); step 5 build the prototype for the solution (the chosen and completed solution must be expressed as a detail design drawing, complete enough to build the prototype from; the prototype is the working version of the chosen solution, usually made from materials unlike the final product's; this sample will be examined, evaluated and tested for the level of fit with the product's requirements and criteria; the materials, tools, technology and functional modules chosen must ensure the prototype is made quickly and cheaply); step 6 test and verify the solution (the final step ensuring the output quality of the designed product; the prototype is experimented on to evaluate the level of fit with the requirements and criteria set; from the experiment results, the solution and prototype are completed; when the prototype meets only part of, or cannot meet, the requirements and criteria set, the solution and prototype must be adjusted and re-experimented; this process can repeat many times until the requirements are met); step 7 make the technical file (ending the design process, a solution/product solving the problem is created; the technical file of the designed product fully reflects the product's shape, structure, technical parameters and the technological process for making it; this is also the time the author can publish the results or register the invention copyright if the result is new and creative; the file's main content just needs to clarify the problem to be solved, the overview research, the research method, the results and discussion, the direction of further research, and the conclusion) (original string `the technical design process (quy trình thiết kế kĩ thuật), a sequence in which some activities repeat until the wanted result is reached`) | Vietnam, *Công nghệ 10 — Thiết kế và công nghệ*, printed p. 105 |
