# Why design and technology matter

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Malaysia's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Importance and history
Design and technology matter because they drive modern technology and the development of society, science and business; they meet needs, ease work, foster invention and creativity, and open careers. The book grounds the claim in the subject’s history.

## Sub lesson 2 — Roles and fields served
The subject serves four parties — the individual, society, producers and the nation — across fields from industry and engineering to health and safety, information and communication, home, sport, transport, applied science and the military.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: design and technology matter because they meet needs, ease work and foster invention — serving individual, society, producers and nation across many fields. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
