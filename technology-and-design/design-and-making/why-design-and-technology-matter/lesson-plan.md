# Lesson plan — Why design and technology matter

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Design and Making · **Lesson:** Why design and technology matter
**Place in the curriculum:** Design and Making (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Malaysia expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** design and technology matter because they meet needs, ease work and foster invention — serving individual, society, producers and nation across many fields.
**This lesson's own level:** stocked from Malaysia's *Reka Bentuk dan Teknologi* (RBT — Design and Technology) textbooks — see lesson/sources.md. Malaysia counts in the acceptance count (student books, not evidence-only).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **Importance and history** — Design and technology matter because they drive modern technology and the development of society, science and business; they meet needs, ease work, foster invention and creativity, and open careers.
2. **Roles and fields served** — The subject serves four parties — the individual, society, producers and the nation — across fields from industry and engineering to health and safety, information and communication, home, sport, transport, applied science and the military.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of Design and Making. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Explain the importance of design and technology, with its history.
2. Name the roles for individual, society, producers and nation, and the fields served.
3. Explain how Malaysia's book teaches it.
## What you need
Examples of technology serving each field.
