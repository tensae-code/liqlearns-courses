# Why design and technology matter — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is importance?
a) driving development, meeting needs, easing work, fostering invention and careers b) Only a price c) Only decoration d) Nothing to do with this lesson
**Answer: a) driving development, meeting needs, easing work, fostering invention and careers**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is a role for society?
a) Only decoration b) what design and technology does for the community as a whole c) Only a price d) A kind of glue
**Answer: b) what design and technology does for the community as a whole**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is a field served?
a) Only history b) Only painting c) an area the subject works in — industry, health, transport, home, sport and others d) Only storage
**Answer: c) an area the subject works in — industry, health, transport, home, sport and others**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson teach, in one line?
a) design and technology matter because they meet needs, ease work and foster invention — serving individual, society, producers and nation across many fields. b) Only prices c) Only old tools d) Nothing practical
**Answer: a) design and technology matter because they meet needs, ease work and foster invention — serving individual, society, producers and nation across many fields.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** How does Malaysia's book teach this?
a) As theory only, never practised b) Only in the glossary c) Only as an exam d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Malay (RBT)
**Answer: d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Malay (RBT)**
