# Lesson plan — Enterprise foundations inside the technology course

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Design and Making · **Lesson:** Enterprise foundations inside the technology course
**Place in the curriculum:** Design and Making (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Morocco expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** inside the technology course, an enterprise is studied as a system that answers a need: product and need, good and service, markets and price formation, the firm, its distribution channels and its economic sectors.

## Sub lessons
1. **Product, need and market** — A product answers a need. A good is a product you can hold and store; a service is work done for you that cannot be stored. Products meet needs on a market, where buyers and sellers meet and prices form from supply and demand — the enterprise exists to put the right product on that market.
2. **The enterprise, distribution and sectors** — An enterprise organises people, machines and money to make and sell products. Between the maker and the user sit distribution channels — direct sale, wholesalers, retailers. The economy's sectors group enterprises: primary (raw materials), secondary (making), tertiary (services).

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Tell a good from a service and give an example of each.
2. Explain in simple terms how a price forms on a market.
3. Name an enterprise's distribution channel and its economic sector.
## What you need
Local examples: shop receipts, adverts or market prices learners know.
