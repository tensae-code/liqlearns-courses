# Hands-on — Enterprise foundations inside the technology course

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## Good or service?
List ten things families pay for. Mark each G (good) or S (service). For two of them, write the need each answers.

## Channel and sector map
Pick one local enterprise. Write its product, its distribution channel (direct, wholesaler, retailer), and its sector — primary, secondary or tertiary — with one sentence justifying each.
