# Enterprise foundations inside the technology course — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A good differs from a service because a good…
a) can be held and stored b) is always free c) cannot be sold d) is work done for you
**Answer: a) can be held and stored**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A market is…
a) any meeting of supply and demand for a product b) only a building with stalls c) only a website d) a place where nothing is priced
**Answer: a) any meeting of supply and demand for a product**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** When demand rises and supply stays fixed, prices tend to…
a) rise b) fall to zero c) never change d) be banned
**Answer: a) rise**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A bakery belongs to which sector?
a) Secondary — it makes products b) Primary — it mines c) Tertiary — it only serves d) No sector at all
**Answer: a) Secondary — it makes products**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** A distribution channel is…
a) the route a product travels from maker to user b) a television station c) a discount coupon only d) the factory's chimney
**Answer: a) the route a product travels from maker to user**
