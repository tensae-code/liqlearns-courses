# Enterprise foundations inside the technology course — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Give one good and one service, and the need each answers.
**Answer:** Example: bread (good) answers the need to eat; a bus ride (service) answers the need to travel.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Trace a loaf from maker to eater through a long channel.
**Answer:** Baker → wholesaler → retailer (shop) → eater; each step is a distribution stage adding reach and cost.
