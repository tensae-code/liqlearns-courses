# Enterprise foundations inside the technology course

## Do it first
Name one thing your family bought this week and one service it paid for. The bought thing is a good; the paid help is a service. Both were answers to a need, sold on a market. That is the enterprise's world.

## Sub lesson 1 — Product, need and market
Morocco's Module 4 starts technology's enterprise strand from the product itself: product versus need first, then good versus service — a loaf is a good, the baker's delivery is a service. A market is any meeting of supply and demand for a product; when demand rises against a fixed supply, prices tend to rise, and the reverse when supply grows. Price formation is read, not guessed.

## Sub lesson 2 — The enterprise, distribution and sectors
The enterprise is itself a system: inputs of material, energy and work; transformation; products out to a market. Distribution channels decide how the product travels — a short channel sells direct, a long channel passes through intermediaries, each adding cost and reach. Naming an enterprise's sector says where it stands in the economy: a farm is primary, a bakery secondary, a repair shop tertiary.
