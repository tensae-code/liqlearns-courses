# Setting up a small business

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Iran's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — The entrepreneur and the business
Entrepreneurs share traits the book names and trains: seeing needs, taking considered risks, persisting. The business itself may be making, trading or services — including internet business, which sells through networks rather than a shopfront. Choosing the kind of business follows the need found and the maker's skills.

## Sub lesson 2 — Money, marketing and the plan
A small business plans its money before it spends: what must be bought, at what price — buying well is a skill of comparing quality and price — and what income is expected. Marketing and advertising bring the buyers: the product must be known to be sold. All of it is written into a simple business plan, step by step, ending in a profit calculation: income minus all costs. Iran's Grade 7 book closes the module with a school-bazaar project — a needs survey, advertising, and selling the class's own products.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: a small business starts from the entrepreneur's traits and a kind of business, and stands on money planning, buying well, marketing — and a simple written business plan whose profit is calculated. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
