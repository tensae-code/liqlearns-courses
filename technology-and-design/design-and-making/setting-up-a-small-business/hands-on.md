# Hands-on — Setting up a small business

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## The school bazaar
Plan one product for a school bazaar: survey five people's needs, price the product, and write the advertising line that would sell it.

## Does it profit?
For your bazaar product: list every cost, set a price, estimate sales, and calculate the profit. If it makes a loss, change one number and say why.
