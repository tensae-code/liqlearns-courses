# Setting up a small business — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Entrepreneur is a person who starts and runs a business, taking its risks.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Business plan has nothing to do with this lesson.
**Answer: False** — It is part of the lesson: a simple written plan for a business: what, for whom, at what cost, for what profit.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Profit is income minus all costs.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.
