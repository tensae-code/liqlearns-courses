# Setting up a small business — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is entrepreneur?
a) a person who starts and runs a business, taking its risks b) Only a price c) Only decoration d) Nothing to do with this lesson
**Answer: a) a person who starts and runs a business, taking its risks**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is business plan?
a) Only history b) a simple written plan for a business: what, for whom, at what cost, for what profit c) Only storage d) A kind of glue
**Answer: b) a simple written plan for a business: what, for whom, at what cost, for what profit**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is profit?
a) Only old tools b) Only painting c) income minus all costs d) Only a price
**Answer: c) income minus all costs**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson teach, in one line?
a) Only decoration b) Nothing to do with this lesson c) Only history d) a small business starts from the entrepreneur's traits and a kind of business, and stands on money planning, buying well, marketing — and a simple written business plan whose profit is calculated.
**Answer: d) a small business starts from the entrepreneur's traits and a kind of business, and stands on money planning, buying well, marketing — and a simple written business plan whose profit is calculated.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** How does Iran's book teach this?
a) As theory only, never practised b) Only in a glossary c) Only as an exam d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Persian
**Answer: d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Persian**
