# Planning a design project — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is an organisation chart?
a) a drawing of the project team and its posts b) Only a price c) Only decoration d) Nothing to do with this lesson
**Answer: a) a drawing of the project team and its posts**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the project manager?
a) Only decoration b) the post carrying main determination, sub-determination, activity management and the team matrix c) Only a price d) A kind of glue
**Answer: b) the post carrying main determination, sub-determination, activity management and the team matrix**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is scheduling?
a) Only history b) Only painting c) ordering the work in time to avoid loss, control labour and manage materials d) Only storage
**Answer: c) ordering the work in time to avoid loss, control labour and manage materials**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson teach, in one line?
a) planning a design project means a charted team, a manager with named tasks, and a schedule that prevents waste of time, cost, labour and materials. b) Only prices c) Only old tools d) Nothing practical
**Answer: a) planning a design project means a charted team, a manager with named tasks, and a schedule that prevents waste of time, cost, labour and materials.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** How does Malaysia's book teach this?
a) As theory only, never practised b) Only in the glossary c) Only as an exam d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Malay (RBT)
**Answer: d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Malay (RBT)**
