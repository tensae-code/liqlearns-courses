# Planning a design project

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Malaysia's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — The team and its chart
A planned project has a team with named posts, drawn on an organisation chart: in industry, executive manager, project manager, production, engineering, accounting and R&D; at school — manager, secretary, treasurer, designer, and mock-up/model builder.

## Sub lesson 2 — The manager’s tasks and scheduling
The project manager carries main determination, sub-determination, activity management and the team matrix. Scheduling exists for named reasons: to avoid time and cost loss and waste, control labour, and manage raw materials.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: planning a design project means a charted team, a manager with named tasks, and a schedule that prevents waste of time, cost, labour and materials. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
