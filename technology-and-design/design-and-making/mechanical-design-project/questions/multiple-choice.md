# Mechanical design project — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is component suitability?
a) whether a chosen component fits its job in the gadget b) Only a price c) Only decoration d) Nothing to do with this lesson
**Answer: a) whether a chosen component fits its job in the gadget**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is a working gadget?
a) Only decoration b) a built mechanical product that actually works c) Only a price d) A kind of glue
**Answer: b) a built mechanical product that actually works**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is proposing improvements?
a) Only history b) Only painting c) naming changes that would make the system better d) Only storage
**Answer: c) naming changes that would make the system better**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson teach, in one line?
a) the mechanical project 3D-sketches a gadget from chosen components, builds it working, summarises its system’s strengths and weaknesses, and proposes improvements. b) Only prices c) Only old tools d) Nothing practical
**Answer: a) the mechanical project 3D-sketches a gadget from chosen components, builds it working, summarises its system’s strengths and weaknesses, and proposes improvements.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** How does Malaysia's book teach this?
a) As theory only, never practised b) Only in the glossary c) Only as an exam d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Malay (RBT)
**Answer: d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Malay (RBT)**
