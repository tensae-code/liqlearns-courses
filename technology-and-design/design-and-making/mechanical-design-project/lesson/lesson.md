# Mechanical design project

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Malaysia's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Designing the gadget
The mechanical project designs a gadget: a 3D sketch using the chosen mechanical components, with each component’s suitability analysed before building — the right gear for the right job, argued in advance.

## Sub lesson 2 — Build, judge, improve
The working gadget is built, its mechanical system’s strengths and weaknesses are summarised honestly, and improvements are proposed. The project grades the system, not just the maker.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: the mechanical project 3D-sketches a gadget from chosen components, builds it working, summarises its system’s strengths and weaknesses, and proposes improvements. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.

## Do It Yourself
1. Do the Do it first task again, carefully this time, using everything the lesson taught.
2. Check your work against the lesson's own checks — fix one thing you find.
3. Keep your work: it is practice evidence, and the next lesson builds on it.
