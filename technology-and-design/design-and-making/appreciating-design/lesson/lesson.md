# Appreciating design

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Malaysia's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Comparing products by function
Appreciating design means judging it: Malaysia compares three water bottles by function — which is easy to carry, which is easy to use, which is easy to close — and lets the comparison teach what good design is.

## Sub lesson 2 — Judging the designs around us
The same functional eye turns on every product around us: does it do its job well, for the person using it? Appreciation is a skill of looking with criteria, not taste alone.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: appreciating design means comparing products by function — easy to carry, easy to use, easy to close. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
