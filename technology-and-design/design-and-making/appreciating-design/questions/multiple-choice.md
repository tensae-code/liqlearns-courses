# Appreciating design — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is appreciating design?
a) judging design by how well a product does its job b) Only a price c) Only decoration d) Nothing to do with this lesson
**Answer: a) judging design by how well a product does its job**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is function?
a) Only decoration b) what a product does and how well it does it c) Only a price d) A kind of glue
**Answer: b) what a product does and how well it does it**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is a functional criterion?
a) Only history b) Only painting c) a named test such as easy to carry, easy to use, easy to close d) Only storage
**Answer: c) a named test such as easy to carry, easy to use, easy to close**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson teach, in one line?
a) appreciating design means comparing products by function — easy to carry, easy to use, easy to close. b) Only prices c) Only old tools d) Nothing practical
**Answer: a) appreciating design means comparing products by function — easy to carry, easy to use, easy to close.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** How does Malaysia's book teach this?
a) As theory only, never practised b) Only in the glossary c) Only as an exam d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Malay (RBT)
**Answer: d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Malay (RBT)**
