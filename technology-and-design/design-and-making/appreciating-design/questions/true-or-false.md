# Appreciating design — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Appreciating design is judging design by how well a product does its job.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Function has nothing to do with this lesson.
**Answer: False** — It is part of the lesson: what a product does and how well it does it.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A functional criterion is a named test such as easy to carry, easy to use, easy to close.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.
