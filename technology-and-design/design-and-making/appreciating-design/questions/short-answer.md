# Appreciating design — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In your own words, what is appreciating design?
**Answer:** Judging design by how well a product does its job.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In your own words, what is a functional criterion?
**Answer:** A named test such as easy to carry, easy to use, easy to close.
