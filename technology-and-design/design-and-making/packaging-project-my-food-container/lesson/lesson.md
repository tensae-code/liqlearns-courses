# Packaging project (my food container)

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Malaysia's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Designing the container as a net
The food-container project designs packaging the maker’s way: ideas generated, then the container sketched as a flat net — a development — with measurements, so the flat sheet’s future folds are designed before any cutting.

## Sub lesson 2 — Cost, make, present
The improved sketch is costed, then made in the maker’s order — measure and mark, cut, join, decorate — and presented. Packaging stops being bought wrapping and becomes a designed product.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: the packaging project designs a food container as a measured flat net, costs it, makes it — measure, cut, join, decorate — and presents it. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
