# Basic product sketches in 2D and 3D; choosing the best product idea using the design elements; sketching the chosen product; improving the chosen sketch — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is a 2D sketch?
a) a flat sketch showing a product’s faces b) Only a price c) Only decoration d) Nothing to do with this lesson
**Answer: a) a flat sketch showing a product’s faces**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is a 3D sketch?
a) Only decoration b) a sketch showing the product’s whole form c) Only a price d) A kind of glue
**Answer: b) a sketch showing the product’s whole form**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is choosing by the elements?
a) Only history b) Only painting c) selecting the best idea using the design elements as tests d) Only storage
**Answer: c) selecting the best idea using the design elements as tests**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson teach, in one line?
a) sketch product ideas in 2D and 3D, choose the best by the design elements, then sketch and improve the chosen product. b) Only prices c) Only old tools d) Nothing practical
**Answer: a) sketch product ideas in 2D and 3D, choose the best by the design elements, then sketch and improve the chosen product.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** How does Malaysia's book teach this?
a) As theory only, never practised b) Only in the glossary c) Only as an exam d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Malay (RBT)
**Answer: d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Malay (RBT)**
