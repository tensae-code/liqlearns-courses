# Basic product sketches in 2D and 3D; choosing the best product idea using the design elements; sketching the chosen product; improving the chosen sketch

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Malaysia's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Sketching products in 2D and 3D
Product ideas are sketched both flat and solid: basic product sketches in 2D show the faces, and in 3D show the whole form, so ideas can be compared fairly before one is chosen.

## Sub lesson 2 — Choosing and improving by the elements
The best idea is chosen using the design elements as the tests; the chosen product is then sketched properly and improved — the sketch is a decision tool, not a drawing exercise.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: sketch product ideas in 2D and 3D, choose the best by the design elements, then sketch and improve the chosen product. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.

2. Check your work against the lesson's own checks — fix one thing you find.
3. Keep your work: it is practice evidence, and the next lesson builds on it.
