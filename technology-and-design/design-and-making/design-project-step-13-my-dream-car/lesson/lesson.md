# Design project, step 1–3 (my dream car)

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Malaysia's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Steps 1–3: idea, sketch, improve
The dream-car project runs the cycle’s first half: generate car ideas, choose and sketch the design, then analyse, evaluate and improve the sketch. Improvement is judged against the design elements — shape, form, size and space.

## Sub lesson 2 — Evaluating against the elements
A sketch improves by criteria, not by mood: shape checked against shape, form against form, size and space each questioned in turn. What survives evaluation is a design worth building.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: project steps 1–3: generate ideas, sketch the chosen design, then analyse, evaluate and improve it against the design elements. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
