# Systems modelling with SysML — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the three SysML diagram families taught and the question each answers.
**Answer:** Requirements — what must it satisfy? Behaviour — what does it do? Structure — what is it made of?

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How do you check one Cdcf requirement in a SysML model?
**Answer:** Find it in the requirements diagram, follow it to the behaviour that fulfils it, then to the blocks carrying that behaviour; if a link is missing, the design has a fault.
