# Systems modelling with SysML — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** SysML is…
a) a modelling language for systems, drawn in diagrams b) a programming language for games only c) a brand of motor d) a drawing style for posters
**Answer: a) a modelling language for systems, drawn in diagrams**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The requirements diagram records…
a) what the system must satisfy b) the colour of the casing c) the price list only d) the maker's address
**Answer: a) what the system must satisfy**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** A use-case diagram shows…
a) the actors around the system and the services it renders them b) the screws inside the system c) only the system's weight d) the factory plan
**Answer: a) the actors around the system and the services it renders them**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A requirement with no block serving it is…
a) a design fault found by tracing the model b) normal and expected c) a sign the model is finished d) impossible to check
**Answer: a) a design fault found by tracing the model**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Morocco works SysML against the Cdcf of…
a) an automated gate b) a bicycle bell c) a school bag d) a kitchen knife
**Answer: a) an automated gate**
