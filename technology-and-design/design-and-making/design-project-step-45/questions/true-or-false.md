# Design project, step 4–5 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A cutting mat is the mat that protects the table and guides safe cutting.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A glue gun has nothing to do with this lesson.
**Answer: False** — It is part of the lesson: the tool that joins cardboard with hot glue.
