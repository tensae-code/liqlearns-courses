# Design project, step 4–5

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Malaysia's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Step 4: building the model
The improved sketch becomes a car model in cardboard, made with named tools and materials — cutting mat, ruler, scissors, craft knife, glue gun — following the making steps safely and in order.

## Sub lesson 2 — Step 5: decorate and present
The model is decorated with suitable recycled materials, then presented: the maker shows the product and tells its story from idea to finished model. Making ends in showing.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: project steps 4–5: build the model safely with the named tools, decorate it, and present the product. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.

## Do It Yourself
1. Do the Do it first task again, carefully this time, using everything the lesson taught.
2. Check your work against the lesson's own checks — fix one thing you find.
3. Keep your work: it is practice evidence, and the next lesson builds on it.
