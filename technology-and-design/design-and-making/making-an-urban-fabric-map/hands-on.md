# Hands-on — Making an urban-fabric map

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: building a map of a settlement's buildings over the National Cadastre (Κτηματολόγιο ΑΕ) basemap, categorising the building layer and highlighting the buildings that matter to the settlement (town hall, school). Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
