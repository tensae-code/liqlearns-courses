# Making an urban-fabric map — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Say in one sentence what this lesson teaches.
**Answer:** Building a map of a settlement's buildings over the National Cadastre (Κτηματολόγιο ΑΕ) basemap, categorising the building layer and highlighting the buildings that matter to the settlement (town hall, school).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name one material, tool or step this lesson works with.
**Answer:** Any one named in the lesson text — for example, the first step of sub lesson 1: Making an urban-fabric map.
