# Applying the project brief as a table and sketches; building a mock-up or model from the brief; evaluating the finished mock-up or model — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In your own words, what is applying the brief?
**Answer:** Turning the brief into a table and sketches that can be checked.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In your own words, what is evaluating against the brief?
**Answer:** Judging the finished model by the requirements it came from.
