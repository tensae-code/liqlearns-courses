# Lesson plan — Applying the project brief as a table and sketches; building a mock-up or model from the brief; evaluating the finished mock-up or model

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Design and Making · **Lesson:** Applying the project brief as a table and sketches; building a mock-up or model from the brief; evaluating the finished mock-up or model
**Place in the curriculum:** Design and Making (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Malaysia expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** apply the brief as a table and sketches, build the mock-up or model from it, and evaluate the result against the brief.
**This lesson's own level:** stocked from Malaysia's *Reka Bentuk dan Teknologi* (RBT — Design and Technology) textbooks — see lesson/sources.md. Malaysia counts in the acceptance count (student books, not evidence-only).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **The brief applied** — A brief is applied, not just written: Malaysia turns it into a table and sketches — the requirements laid out where they can be checked — before any material is cut.
2. **Mock-up, model, evaluation** — From the brief a mock-up or model is built, and the finished mock-up or model is then evaluated against the brief it came from.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of Design and Making. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Turn a brief into a table and sketches.
2. Build a mock-up from a brief and evaluate it against the brief.
3. Explain how Malaysia's book teaches it.
## What you need
