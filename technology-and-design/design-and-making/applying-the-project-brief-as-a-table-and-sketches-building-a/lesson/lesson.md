# Applying the project brief as a table and sketches; building a mock-up or model from the brief; evaluating the finished mock-up or model

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Malaysia's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — The brief applied
A brief is applied, not just written: Malaysia turns it into a table and sketches — the requirements laid out where they can be checked — before any material is cut.

## Sub lesson 2 — Mock-up, model, evaluation
From the brief a mock-up or model is built, and the finished mock-up or model is then evaluated against the brief it came from. The brief judges its own product.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: apply the brief as a table and sketches, build the mock-up or model from it, and evaluate the result against the brief. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
