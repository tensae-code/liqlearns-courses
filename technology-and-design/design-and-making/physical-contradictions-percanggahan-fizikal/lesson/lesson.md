# Physical contradictions (percanggahan fizikal)

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Malaysia's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Finding the contradiction
A physical contradiction — percanggahan fizikal — is one part needing two opposite things at once. Malaysia teaches learners to identify and analyse the contradiction before touching the design.

## Sub lesson 2 — The inventive principle answers
For the contradiction an inventive principle (prinsip inventif — the TRIZ-style principles in Malay dress) is chosen; the design change it implies is justified, and the product is sketched focused on the part carrying the inventive problem.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: a physical contradiction is answered by choosing an inventive principle, justifying the change it implies, and sketching the part that carries the problem. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
