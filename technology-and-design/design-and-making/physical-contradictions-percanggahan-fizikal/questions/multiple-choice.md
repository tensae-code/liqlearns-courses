# Physical contradictions (percanggahan fizikal) — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is percanggahan fizikal?
a) a physical contradiction — one part needing two opposite things at once b) Only a price c) Only decoration d) Nothing to do with this lesson
**Answer: a) a physical contradiction — one part needing two opposite things at once**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is prinsip inventif?
a) Only decoration b) an inventive principle chosen to answer a contradiction c) Only a price d) A kind of glue
**Answer: b) an inventive principle chosen to answer a contradiction**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is justifying the change?
a) Only history b) Only painting c) explaining why the principle’s implied design change is right d) Only storage
**Answer: c) explaining why the principle’s implied design change is right**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson teach, in one line?
a) a physical contradiction is answered by choosing an inventive principle, justifying the change it implies, and sketching the part that carries the problem. b) Only prices c) Only old tools d) Nothing practical
**Answer: a) a physical contradiction is answered by choosing an inventive principle, justifying the change it implies, and sketching the part that carries the problem.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** How does Malaysia's book teach this?
a) As theory only, never practised b) Only in the glossary c) Only as an exam d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Malay (RBT)
**Answer: d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Malay (RBT)**
