# Physical contradictions (percanggahan fizikal) — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In your own words, what is percanggahan fizikal?
**Answer:** A physical contradiction — one part needing two opposite things at once.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In your own words, what is justifying the change?
**Answer:** Explaining why the principle’s implied design change is right.
