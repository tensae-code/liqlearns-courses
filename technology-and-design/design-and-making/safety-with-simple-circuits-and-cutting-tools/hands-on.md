# Hands-on — Safety with simple circuits and cutting tools

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## Stage safety card
Write a safety card for the flashlight project: one precaution per stage, in your own words. Keep it at the bench while building and tick each stage as you observe it.

## Fault spot
Look at a set-up bench (or a described one) with three planted faults — wet hands near the circuit, a bare wire across the battery, an open blade left at the edge. Find and fix all three on paper.
