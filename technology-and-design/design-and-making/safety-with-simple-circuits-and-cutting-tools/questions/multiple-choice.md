# Safety with simple circuits and cutting tools — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Joining a battery's two sides directly together makes…**
a) A brighter bulb b) A short circuit c) A switch d) A shade
**Answer: b) A short circuit**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Circuit work is done with…**
a) Wet hands b) Dry hands c) Gloves full of water d) No checks
**Answer: b) Dry hands**

**3. (Sub lesson 2) When cutting, the blade is directed…**
a) Toward the body b) Away from the body and holding hand c) At a classmate d) Upward while walking
**Answer: b) Away from the body and holding hand**

**4. (Sub lesson 2) When a cut ends, the tool is…**
a) Left open at the bench edge b) Closed, covered or laid safely at once c) Carried open d) Hidden in the work
**Answer: b) Closed, covered or laid safely at once**

**5. (Sub lesson 3) Safety precautions in Jamaica's project are placed…**
a) Only in the first lesson b) At each stage of the project c) Only in the examination d) Nowhere
**Answer: b) At each stage of the project**
