# Safety with simple circuits and cutting tools — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, or by doing the step named.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) List three precautions for making a simple circuit.**
**Answer:** Dry hands; sound insulation except at stripped contacts; no direct battery-to-battery connection (no short circuit) — and check the layout against the schematic before connecting (any three).

**2. (Sub lesson 3) Why does the guide repeat safety at each stage instead of once at the start?**
**Answer:** Each stage has different hazards — circuit stages and cutting stages endanger differently — so the precaution must be met where the hazard is.
