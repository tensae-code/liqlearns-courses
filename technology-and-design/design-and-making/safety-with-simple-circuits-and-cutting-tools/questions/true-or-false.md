# Safety with simple circuits and cutting tools — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) A short circuit can heat the wire and ruin the battery.**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Material being cut should be held so it cannot slip into the cut.**
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3) Battery circuits are so gentle that no precautions are needed.**
**Answer: False — The habits learned on them are the habits of all electrical work, and the guide names precautions for them.**
