# Lesson plan — Safety with simple circuits and cutting tools

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Design and Making · **Lesson:** Safety with simple circuits and cutting tools
**Place in the curriculum:** Design and Making (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Jamaica expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** electrical making has its own safety rules: precautions are observed when making simple circuits and at each stage of the project, and cutting tools are used safely throughout.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Jamaica's *National Standards Curriculum Guide — Grades 4–6 — Resource & Technology* (Ministry of Education, Youth and Information, Kingston, Jamaica, © 2018), a curriculum guide with full unit teaching content, read COMPLETE (see lesson/sources.md). It counts in the acceptance count (curriculum guide with full teaching content, not evidence-only).

Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **Precautions with simple circuits** — Even a small battery circuit is made carefully: dry hands, sound wires, no short circuits, and checks before connecting.
2. **Cutting tools, used safely** — Every cut in the project — housing card, wire, shade — follows the cutting rules: cut away from the body, control the material, put the tool down safely.
3. **Safety at each stage** — The project is checked stage by stage: the precautions belong to the stage — circuit stages, cutting stages, assembly stages each have theirs.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of Design and Making. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the precautions for making simple circuits.
2. Use cutting tools safely and state the cutting rules.
3. Write a stage-by-stage safety check for a small electrical project.

The flashlight project's tools and materials, paper and a pencil.
