# Lesson plan — The principles of design

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Design and Making · **Lesson:** The principles of design
**Place in the curriculum:** Design and Making (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Malaysia expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the principles of design are the rules for arranging the elements — and principles (how) must not be confused with elements (what).
**This lesson's own level:** stocked from Malaysia's *Reka Bentuk dan Teknologi* (RBT — Design and Technology) textbooks — see lesson/sources.md. Malaysia counts in the acceptance count (student books, not evidence-only).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **Principles: how the elements are used** — If the elements are what a design is made of, the principles are how they are arranged and used.
2. **Principles against elements** — The book closes by comparing principles against elements side by side — what a thing is made of versus how it is organised — so the learner never confuses the two lists again.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of Design and Making. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Apply the principles to designing a basic object.
2. Compare principles against elements without confusing them.
3. Explain how Malaysia's book teaches it.
## What you need
A basic object to design; the elements list for comparison.
