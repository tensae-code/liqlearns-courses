# The principles of design

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Malaysia's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Principles: how the elements are used
If the elements are what a design is made of, the principles are how they are arranged and used. Malaysia teaches the principles as working rules, applied at once to designing a basic object.

## Sub lesson 2 — Principles against elements
The book closes by comparing principles against elements side by side — what a thing is made of versus how it is organised — so the learner never confuses the two lists again.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: the principles of design are the rules for arranging the elements — and principles (how) must not be confused with elements (what). Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
