# Implementing and presenting a design project

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Moldova's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Implementing — carrying the project through making
Moldova's Grade 9 clothing-design part does not stop at the sketch: the project is implemented, carried through making until the designed thing exists in material. Implementation is where the design meets its tests — materials, fit, time — and is adjusted honestly.

## Sub lesson 2 — Presenting the project
The cycle closes with presentation of the project: the made thing is shown and the project explained — what was designed, how it was made, and how it answers its brief. In the book's project cycle, presentation belongs to evaluate and valorise: the work is judged and its worth stated.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: a design project is finished only when it is implemented — carried through making — and presented: the project ends in a made thing, shown and explained. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.

## Do It Yourself
1. Do the Do it first task again, carefully this time, using everything the lesson taught.
2. Check your work against the lesson's own checks — fix one thing you find.
3. Keep your work: it is practice evidence, and the next lesson builds on it.
