# Lesson plan — Implementing and presenting a design project

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Design and Making · **Lesson:** Implementing and presenting a design project
**Place in the curriculum:** Design and Making (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Moldova expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** a design project is finished only when it is implemented — carried through making — and presented: the project ends in a made thing, shown and explained.
**This lesson's own level:** stocked from Moldova's *Educație tehnologică* (Technological Education) textbooks — see lesson/sources.md. Moldova counts in the acceptance count (student books, not evidence-only).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **Implementing — carrying the project through making** — Moldova's Grade 9 clothing-design part does not stop at the sketch: the project is implemented, carried through making until the designed thing exists in material.
2. **Presenting the project** — The cycle closes with presentation of the project: the made thing is shown and the project explained — what was designed, how it was made, and how it answers its brief.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of Design and Making. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Carry a design project through making to completion.
2. Present the finished project — the thing and the reasoning together.
3. Explain how Moldova's book teaches it.
## What you need
A design project ready to make, its materials, and a place to present.
