# Implementing and presenting a design project — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does implementing a design project mean?
a) Carrying it through making until the thing exists b) Drawing it again c) Filing the sketch d) Describing it to a friend
**Answer: a) Carrying it through making until the thing exists**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What tests does the design meet in making?
a) Only the teacher's taste b) Materials, fit and time c) Only the price d) Nothing — making changes nothing
**Answer: b) Materials, fit and time**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** What may honestly happen during implementation?
a) The design is adjusted b) The design is hidden c) The project is abandoned d) The brief is burned
**Answer: a) The design is adjusted**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** What closes the project?
a) Presentation of the project b) Storage c) Silence d) A new brief at once
**Answer: a) Presentation of the project**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** What is shown in the presentation?
a) Only the sketch b) Only the receipts c) The made thing, with the project explained d) Nothing — presentation is silent
**Answer: c) The made thing, with the project explained**
