# Implementing and presenting a design project — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A project is not finished when the sketch is finished.
**Answer: True** — It must be implemented — carried through making.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Presentation is separate from judging the work.
**Answer: False** — In the book's cycle, presentation belongs to evaluate and valorise — the work is judged and its worth stated.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Adjustments during making are an honest part of implementation.
**Answer: True** — Materials, fit and time test the design, and it is adjusted honestly.
