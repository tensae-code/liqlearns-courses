# Conventional vs modern tool technology

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Malaysia's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Conventional: simple hand-tool technology
Conventional technology makes products with simple hand tools — knife, hammer, hand saw — and household standbys like the stone mortar and the hand ice-shaver. The hand supplies the power and the judgement.

## Sub lesson 2 — Modern: complex machine technology
Modern technology works through machines — drill, circular saw, blender, vacuum cleaner, sewing machine — where the machine supplies power and speed. Malaysia sets the two side by side so learners see what a machine changes and what it does not.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: conventional technology works by hand tools; modern technology works by machines — the machine changes power and speed, not purpose. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
