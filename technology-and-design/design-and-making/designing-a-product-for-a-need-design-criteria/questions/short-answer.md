# Designing a product for a need (design criteria) — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, or by doing the step named.

**1. (Sub lesson 3) Two designs tie on brightness; one is much easier for a child to switch on in the dark. Which wins for Jamaica's need, and why?**
**Answer:** The easy-switch design — the need is a light for children (in hurricane season, in the dark), and ease of using the switch is one of the named criteria.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Write a stated need for a light for an elderly grandparent, in one sentence.**
**Answer:** E.g. 'A simple, steady light an elderly person with stiff hands can switch on easily during a power cut.' (Any plainly stated user + situation.)
