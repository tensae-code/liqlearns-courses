# Designing a product for a need (design criteria) — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Jamaica's example need is…**
a) A light for a factory b) A simple light for children, for hurricane season c) A light for a theatre d) No need is stated
**Answer: b) A simple light for children, for hurricane season**

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Which is one of the six flashlight criteria?**
a) Its colour b) Brightness of beam c) Its brand d) Its price tag design
**Answer: b) Brightness of beam**

**3. (Sub lesson 2) 'Durability of the battery/wire housing' asks…**
a) Will it survive use? b) Is it shiny? c) Is it heavy? d) Is it new?
**Answer: a) Will it survive use?**

**4. (Sub lesson 3) Bigger batteries brighten the beam but grow the size — this is…**
a) A mistake b) A design trade-off the criteria must settle c) Forbidden d) Irrelevant
**Answer: b) A design trade-off the criteria must settle**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Designing in Jamaica's project happens…**
a) Alone, with no need stated b) In teams, for a stated user need c) After making d) Without criteria
**Answer: b) In teams, for a stated user need**
