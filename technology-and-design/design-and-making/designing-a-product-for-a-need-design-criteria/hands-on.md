# Hands-on — Designing a product for a need (design criteria)

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## Judge a real light
Take a real flashlight. Score it out of 5 on each of Jamaica's six criteria for the need 'a light for a child in hurricane season'. Write the weakest criterion and one design change that would fix it.

## Team design brief
In a pair, write the need and criteria for a simple light for a user you choose. Sketch two designs and mark each against the criteria; keep the winner and its scores.
