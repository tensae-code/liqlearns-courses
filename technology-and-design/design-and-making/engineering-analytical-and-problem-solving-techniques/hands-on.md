# Hands-on — Engineering analytical and problem-solving techniques

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: Engineering Skills and Techniques; Types of Skills and Techniques used in Engineering Field; Methods of Decision Making in Graphs; Types of Graphs used in Engineering. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
