# Inventive problem solving

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Malaysia's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Two kinds of problem
Problems divide: a non-inventive problem yields to known methods, while an inventive problem has no ready solution and demands a new idea. Malaysia teaches learners to tell them apart first.

## Sub lesson 2 — A process for each kind
Each kind has its own problem-solving process — the routine route for the routine problem, and the inventive route, worked step by step, for the problem nobody has solved yet. Choosing the right process is the first solution.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: tell non-inventive from inventive problems first — each kind has its own problem-solving process. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
