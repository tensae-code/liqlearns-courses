# Why packaging design matters

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Malaysia's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Three jobs of packaging
Packaging design matters because a package does three jobs at once: it promotes the product, it protects the product’s safety and physical quality, and it delivers complete product information to the user.

## Sub lesson 2 — Promotion, protection, information
Remove any one job and the design fails: unpromoted, the product is unseen; unprotected, it spoils; uninformed, the user cannot choose or use it safely. The three jobs are the criteria of the strand.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: packaging design matters for three jobs: promotion, protection, and complete information. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
