# Why packaging design matters — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Promotion is the package job of making the product seen and wanted.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Protection has nothing to do with this lesson.
**Answer: False** — It is part of the lesson: the package job of keeping product safety and physical quality.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Information is the package job of delivering complete product information.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.
