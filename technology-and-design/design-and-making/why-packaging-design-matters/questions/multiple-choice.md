# Why packaging design matters — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is promotion?
a) the package job of making the product seen and wanted b) Only a price c) Only decoration d) Nothing to do with this lesson
**Answer: a) the package job of making the product seen and wanted**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is protection?
a) Only decoration b) the package job of keeping product safety and physical quality c) Only a price d) A kind of glue
**Answer: b) the package job of keeping product safety and physical quality**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is information?
a) Only history b) Only painting c) the package job of delivering complete product information d) Only storage
**Answer: c) the package job of delivering complete product information**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson teach, in one line?
a) packaging design matters for three jobs: promotion, protection, and complete information. b) Only prices c) Only old tools d) Nothing practical
**Answer: a) packaging design matters for three jobs: promotion, protection, and complete information.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** How does Malaysia's book teach this?
a) As theory only, never practised b) Only in the glossary c) Only as an exam d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Malay (RBT)
**Answer: d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Malay (RBT)**
