# Design project management

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Malaysia's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — What project management is
A design project is managed, not just made: project management plans and steers the work from a problem to a finished product. It begins by generating design ideas to solve the problem.

## Sub lesson 2 — The implementation steps
Management runs in steps — the project implementation steps carry the chosen idea through planning, making and finishing in order, so time, people and materials arrive when they are needed.

## Say it to your companion
