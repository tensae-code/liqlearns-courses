# Design in business — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is marketing?
a) the work of bringing a product to its buyers b) Only a price c) Only decoration d) Nothing to do with this lesson
**Answer: a) the work of bringing a product to its buyers**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is design’s marketing role?
a) Only decoration b) design as the product’s face in the market — a marketing instrument c) Only a price d) A kind of glue
**Answer: b) design as the product’s face in the market — a marketing instrument**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is advertising design methods?
a) Only history b) Only painting c) the named, comparable ways of designing advertising d) Only storage
**Answer: c) the named, comparable ways of designing advertising**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson teach, in one line?
a) design in business is design as a marketing instrument — with named, comparable methods of advertising design. b) Only prices c) Only old tools d) Nothing practical
**Answer: a) design in business is design as a marketing instrument — with named, comparable methods of advertising design.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** How does Malaysia's book teach this?
a) As theory only, never practised b) Only in the glossary c) Only as an exam d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Malay (RBT)
**Answer: d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Malay (RBT)**
