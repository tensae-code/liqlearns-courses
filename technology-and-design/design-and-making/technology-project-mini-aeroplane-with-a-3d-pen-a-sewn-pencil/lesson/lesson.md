# Technology project (mini aeroplane with a 3D pen + a sewn pencil case)

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Malaysia's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Two products, one full cycle
The technology project runs the whole cycle twice in one unit: a mini aeroplane built with a 3D pen and a sewn pencil case — ideas generated for a product competition, both products sketched with measurements, and each sketch evaluated and improved element by element.

## Sub lesson 2 — Costing, making, presenting
Before making, the materials are costed in a cost table — design meets arithmetic. Then the products are made step by step and presented, closing the cycle the book opened with.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: the technology project runs the full cycle on two products — sketched with measurements, costed in a table, made and presented. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.

## Do It Yourself
1. Do the Do it first task again, carefully this time, using everything the lesson taught.
2. Check your work against the lesson's own checks — fix one thing you find.
3. Keep your work: it is practice evidence, and the next lesson builds on it.
