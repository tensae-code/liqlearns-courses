Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is a 3D pen?
a) a pen that draws solid plastic lines to build a product b) Only a price c) Only decoration d) Nothing to do with this lesson
**Answer: a) a pen that draws solid plastic lines to build a product**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is a cost table?
a) Only decoration b) a table calculating the material costs of a product c) Only a price d) A kind of glue
**Answer: b) a table calculating the material costs of a product**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is a measured sketch?
a) Only history b) Only painting c) a design sketch carrying the product’s measurements d) Only storage
**Answer: c) a design sketch carrying the product’s measurements**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson teach, in one line?
a) the technology project runs the full cycle on two products — sketched with measurements, costed in a table, made and presented. b) Only prices c) Only old tools d) Nothing practical
**Answer: a) the technology project runs the full cycle on two products — sketched with measurements, costed in a table, made and presented.**
