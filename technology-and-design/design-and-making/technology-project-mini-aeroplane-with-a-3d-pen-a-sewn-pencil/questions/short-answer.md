# Technology project (mini aeroplane with a 3D pen + a sewn pencil case) — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In your own words, what is a 3D pen?
**Answer:** A pen that draws solid plastic lines to build a product.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In your own words, what is a measured sketch?
**Answer:** A design sketch carrying the product’s measurements.
