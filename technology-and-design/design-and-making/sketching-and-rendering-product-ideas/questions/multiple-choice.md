# Sketching and rendering product ideas — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is lakaran?
a) a sketch b) Only a price c) Only decoration d) Nothing to do with this lesson
**Answer: a) a sketch**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is beralun?
a) Only decoration b) a wavy line c) Only a price d) A kind of glue
**Answer: b) a wavy line**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is rendering?
a) Only history b) Only painting c) making a sketch look real with colour, texture and light-and-shadow d) Only storage
**Answer: c) making a sketch look real with colour, texture and light-and-shadow**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson teach, in one line?
a) a sketch speaks in kinds of line, and rendering — colour, texture and light-and-shadow — makes it look real. b) Only prices c) Only old tools d) Nothing practical
**Answer: a) a sketch speaks in kinds of line, and rendering — colour, texture and light-and-shadow — makes it look real.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** How does Malaysia's book teach this?
a) As theory only, never practised b) Only in the glossary c) Only as an exam d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Malay (RBT)
**Answer: d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Malay (RBT)**
