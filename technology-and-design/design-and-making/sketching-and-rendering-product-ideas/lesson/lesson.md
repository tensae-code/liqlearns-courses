# Sketching and rendering product ideas

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Malaysia's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — The kinds of line in a sketch
A sketch (lakaran) speaks in lines: thin, thick, wavy (beralun), dashed (putus-putus), zig-zag and spiral. Each line does a job — showing form, shading, tone, pattern or movement — and the sketcher practises them deliberately.

## Sub lesson 2 — Rendering: making a sketch look real
Rendering gives a sketch reality: colour, texture, line and form worked with coloured pencils, marker pens, highlighters or chalk pastels, and light-and-shadow effects that lift the drawing off the page.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: a sketch speaks in kinds of line, and rendering — colour, texture and light-and-shadow — makes it look real. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
