# Editing tools in CAD

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Iran's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Copying with order: mirror and array
Mirror flips a selected part across a line, giving the symmetric half for free. Array repeats a part in rows, columns or a circle at exact spacings. Both tools multiply careful work instead of repeating it by hand — the drawing stays exact because the computer does the copying.

## Sub lesson 2 — Reshaping: rotate, trim, extend, scale — with snap
Rotate turns a part around a point; trim cuts a line back to a boundary; extend lengthens a line to meet one; scale enlarges or reduces by a factor. Through all editing, snap aids pull the cursor to exact points — ends, midpoints, centres — so edits land precisely. Iran's Grade 9 module teaches the set together, as one editing toolbox used with snap for precise drawing.

## Say it to your companion
