| the principles of technical design, part 2 — the sustainable-development principles: the resource-saving principle (nguyên tắc tiết kiệm tài nguyên — every material in designed products originates from natural resources; analysing the product's life cycle shows that at every step from design through production and use to disposal of the product, energy and resources are needed to perform it; the resource-saving principle in technical design helps reduce the exhaustion of natural resources toward sustainable development; natural resources exist in many forms — some are being exhausted (petroleum, natural gas, water resources…) and some are almost endless (solar energy, wind energy) whose exploitation and use are being encouraged; in the design process, the need to use the least material while still ensuring the product is durable enough and meets the requirements must be calculated, and design solutions must also save fuel and energy when producing and using the product); the environment-protection principle (nguyên tắc bảo vệ môi trường — every designed technology product is made from one or many different materials, recyclable or not; when a large quantity of non-recyclable material is used, technical design will negatively affect the environment; using non-economical materials in technical design will lead to exhaustion of natural resources and rising product prices; in design, smart materials must be chosen and used, and recyclable, reusable materials; most of the production energy is taken from burning non-renewable fuels such as oil and coal, whose combustion products and gases pollute the environment, and waste from discarded products also pollutes; technical design must strictly follow the environment-protection issue, ensuring sustainable development; this principle is performed and managed strictly in every step of the design process, in production and product use, technological solutions at both ends, materials used, and energy consumed; meeting this principle, smart environmentally friendly materials and renewable energy are often used in the design process; the guidance for designing toward environment protection — by material (use materials that can be recycled and are reusable, non-toxic materials, design to prevent pollution generation, materials not yet processed causing little pollution to the environment; if toxic materials must be used, careful design is needed to prevent harm to the environment), by production (use the cleanest production technology possible, limit waste treatment, use energy-saving components), by use (design an automatic switch-off when not in use, design energy-consumption displays via fuel labels, design to ensure long use time and high technical and aesthetic durability, design products that can be repaired, upgraded and have parts replaced), by recycling (design for easy disassembly, joints and connections that are easy to disassemble by hand or with simple tools, separate different materials for convenient recycling); with the 6R rule engineers follow in design (Recycle — use recyclable materials; Reduce — save materials and energy in the design process, production waste; Rethink — think again and consider environmentally friendly solutions; Repair — design for easy repair, extending the product's life; Reuse — reuse parts of existing products; Refuse — refuse, not accept solutions that are not best for the environment) (original string `the principles of technical design, part 2`) | Vietnam, *Công nghệ 10 — Thiết kế và công nghệ*, printed p. 116 |
