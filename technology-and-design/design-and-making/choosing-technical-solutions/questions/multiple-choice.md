# Choosing technical solutions — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A technical solution is best described as…
a) Found ready-made b) A choice among workable solutions c) Always the cheapest d) Always the largest
**Answer: b) A choice among workable solutions**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Choosing well means…
a) Hiding the trades b) Naming what is being traded c) Choosing in silence d) Copying another group
**Answer: b) Naming what is being traded**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The trade-off triangle's three factors are…
a) Colour, shape, shine b) Size, materials and budget c) Time, luck, hope d) Maker, user, seller
**Answer: b) Size, materials and budget**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** If the budget is cut, the balance responds by…
a) Nothing yielding b) Size or materials yielding c) Magic d) Ignoring the budget
**Answer: b) Size or materials yielding**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** A greenhouse's dimensions follow…
a) A number picked first b) Available space, contents and budget c) The teacher's guess d) Last year's greenhouse
**Answer: b) Available space, contents and budget**
