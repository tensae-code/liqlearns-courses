# Choosing technical solutions — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Why must a chosen solution be stated as a balance rather than announced as 'the answer'?
**Answer:** Because several solutions were workable; stating the balance among size, materials and budget lets the choice be defended, changed and learned from.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** State the dimensions rule from the synthesis.
**Answer:** A structure's dimensions follow the available space, what it must hold (its contents), and the budget — the dimensions are the result of the three.
