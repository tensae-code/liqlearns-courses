# Choosing technical solutions — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** One factor alone — say budget — should fix a structure's dimensions.
**Answer: False** — Dimensions follow available space, contents and budget together.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A stated balance among size, materials and budget can be defended and learned from.
**Answer: True** — Naming the trade is what makes the choice examinable; a silent choice cannot be.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Enlarging a structure's size leaves its budget unaffected.
**Answer: False** — Size, materials and budget pull against each other; enlarging size strains the budget.
