# Hands-on — Choosing technical solutions

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## Defend a choice
Take two workable solutions for a greenhouse (or a shelter, or a stall). Choose one and write its defence as a balance: what it favours, what it yields, and why that trade suits the need.

## Size by the rule
For a structure you know, measure the available space, list its contents, and state a budget. Derive its dimensions from the three and write the derivation in order.
