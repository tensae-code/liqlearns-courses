# Hands-on — Designing an automatic product

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: classifying robots (wheeled, walking, tracked, combined) and designing a walking robot's structure by morphological study of legs (2, 4, many). Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
