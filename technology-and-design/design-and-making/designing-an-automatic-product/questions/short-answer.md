# Designing an automatic product — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Say in one sentence what this lesson teaches.
**Answer:** Designing an automatic product — classifying robots (wheeled, walking, tracked, combined) and designing a walking robot's structure by morphological study of legs (2, 4, many).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name one material, tool or step this lesson works with.
**Answer:** Any one named in the lesson text — for example, the first step of sub lesson 1: Designing an automatic product.
