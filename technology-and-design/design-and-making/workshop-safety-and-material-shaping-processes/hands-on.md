# Hands-on — Workshop safety and material-shaping processes

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## Safety card
Write a safety card for one tool: how it is held, where the free hand stays, where the cut travels, and how it is put down. Test it by talking a partner through a practice cut on scrap.

## Three moves
Take one offcut through all three moves — trace a shape, cut it out, shape its edges smooth. Note which move felt most dangerous and what instruction controls it.
