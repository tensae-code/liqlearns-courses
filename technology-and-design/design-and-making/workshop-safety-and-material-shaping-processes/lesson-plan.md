# Lesson plan — Workshop safety and material-shaping processes

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Design and Making · **Lesson:** Workshop safety and material-shaping processes
**Place in the curriculum:** Design and Making (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Haiti expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** safe making rests on three shaping moves — tracing, cutting and shaping — each done under safety instructions for handling the tools that do it.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on teacher-guide evidence only — Haiti's ITAP Projet Nectar pedagogical sequences (MENFP) are the books in hand for this topic (see lesson/sources.md). Teacher guides are evidence, counted nowhere in the acceptance count.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **Safety before the tool moves** — Instructions are learned and followed before any material is touched.
2. **Tracing, cutting, shaping — the three shaping moves** — Materials take form through a named sequence of processes.
3. **Assembling the model safely** — Assembly is shaping work too, and the same rules hold to the end.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of Design and Making. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the safety-first rule for tool work.
2. Name tracing, cutting and shaping and what each does.
3. Carry the same safety discipline through assembly.
## What you need
Tracing tools, cutting tools and shaping tools for the class make, the safety instructions for each, and material offcuts for practice.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — the opening doing task in lesson/lesson.md.
2. **The explanation** — the sub lessons in order (lesson/lesson.md).
   - ADD IMAGE: a picture of a workbench laid out for tracing, cutting and shaping, with the safety instructions posted above it — the student names the tool used for each of the three moves.
