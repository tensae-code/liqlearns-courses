# Workshop safety and material-shaping processes — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Safety is a pause in the making process, separate from the work itself.
**Answer: False** — Safety is part of the process — the instructions govern handling tools through the whole fabrication.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Cutting and shaping are the same move with the same dangers.
**Answer: False** — Tracing, cutting and shaping are distinct processes, each with its own tools and dangers.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A part should be held securely during assembly.
**Answer: True** — Parts are held securely and sharp edges controlled from tracing to the finished assembly.
