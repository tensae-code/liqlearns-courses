# Workshop safety and material-shaping processes — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why are safety instructions given before the making starts, not after?
**Answer:** Because the dangers live in the handling of the tools themselves; the safe grip, hand positions and cut direction must be known before the first move, not learned from an accident.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Why does the lesson insist assembly follows the same rules as cutting?
**Answer:** Because assembly still uses tools and sharp parts; a workshop careless at the last stage is not safe, so the discipline covers the whole fabrication.
