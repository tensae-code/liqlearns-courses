# Workshop safety and material-shaping processes — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Safety instructions for tools are given…
a) After the work b) Before the work begins c) Only after an accident d) Never in writing
**Answer: b) Before the work begins**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The free hand during a cut must…
a) Hold the offcut in the cut's path b) Stay in a known safe position c) Rest on the blade d) Hold the tool too
**Answer: b) Stay in a known safe position**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Tracing (traçage) means…
a) Cutting the part out b) Transferring the drawing's lines onto the material c) Painting the finished model d) Throwing offcuts away
**Answer: b) Transferring the drawing's lines onto the material**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Façonnage (shaping) gives a part…
a) Its colour b) Its final form c) Its price d) Its name
**Answer: b) Its final form**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Assembly work needs…
a) No rules — the danger is past b) The same safety discipline as cutting c) Faster work d) Loose tool storage
**Answer: b) The same safety discipline as cutting**
