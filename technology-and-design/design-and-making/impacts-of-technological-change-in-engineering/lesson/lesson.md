## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Papua New Guinea's guide teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 2 — Impacts of technological change in engineering — doing it yourself

Working from the first sub lesson's sequence, the learner plans the make, works through it step by step, and checks the finished piece against what the PNG guide shows.
