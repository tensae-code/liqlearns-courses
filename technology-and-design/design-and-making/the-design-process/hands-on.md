# Hands-on — The design process

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## Run the whole process on paper
Take the problem from Do it first. Write one line for each of the seven steps: the problem, your ideas, your choice and its reasons, how you would test it, how you would make it, how you would judge it, and how you would present it.

## Evaluate a finished thing
Pick any made object at home. Judge it against the problem it was made to solve: does it solve it fully, partly, or not at all? Write the verdict and one improvement.
