Kazakhstan's own way: Kazakhstan teaches this inside Художественный труд / Көркем еңбек (Artistic Labour) and Трудовое обучение / Еңбекке баулу (Labour Training) — The organiser — plan the make first (draw the scheme, choose materials, recall the scissors-safety rules), then build an organiser two ways (paper "lion" organiser; organiser from three carton tubes) (Topic 11). The Kazakh classroom's own examples, materials and national crafts named in the Kazakhstan provenance rows above are part of the teaching, not decoration.

Fiji's own way: Fiji runs the design process as a cycle — investigate, design, make, evaluate — and requires a design folio documenting it: Year 9 works the cycle through to a finished product, and Year 10 names the stages again (investigating ideas and information, developing, refining and testing) with the folio as the record; Year 11 teaches the same cycle as the design cycle and problem solving, with the golden mean, symmetry and composition inside it.

Jamaica's own way: Jamaica teaches designing through one named method — the 'E' Design Process — used in every Resource & Technology project, with its five aims stated in the guide's appendix. That named, repeated process is carried here exactly as Jamaica frames it, and the same steps organise Jamaica's toy car, flashlight and motor projects elsewhere in this course.

Haiti's own way: Haiti opens every ITAP design project the same way — first the need is interpreted and written into a cahier des charges (main function and constraint functions), then existing technical solutions are researched and compared before designing: Haitian fishing craft, artisanal against industrial, for the boat; working greenhouse professionals visited for the greenhouse; a field questionnaire on drinking-water access for the water station, its specification written from the survey results. The two opening moves stocked here are Haiti's repeated project opening across all three sequences.

Haiti programme note: Haiti's ETAP programmes for the 3e cycle (MENFP, Direction Enseignement Fondamental, version définitive 28 juillet 2024) are curriculum/framework documents, recorded RECORD-ONLY in the placements file and not stocked; the teaching stocked here comes from the Projet Nectar pedagogical sequences that deliver the programme. The LibreOffice Impress (PAO) Nectar sequence in the same placements file is likewise RECORD-ONLY — pure ICT content, stocked in the ICT course, not here.

Samoa's own way: Samoa runs the design process as one complete building project — the Unit 6 deck: investigate and critique existing structures first, let concept sketches lead to working drawings, build as a team, then evaluate the finished product against the objectives.

Moldova's own way: Moldova's Grade 9 *Educație tehnologică* is built as eleven parts, and every part runs the same project cycle — design (elaborare/proiectare), make (realizare), then evaluate and valorise (evaluare și valorificare) — with success criteria stated up front, so the design process is the book's working method, not a single chapter.

Iran's own way: Iran runs the design process at three grades: Grade 11 traces the full development path — idea, concept, pilot, patent, company, production, market — with the wheel as the worked example of a need becoming a product; Grade 7 works the making process on a handmade bag, tested for load strength and improved; Grade 9 prescribes the whole project — define the need, plan the work stages, research existing products, design, build, test — on a motion-mechanism crane.
