| The design process as a whole (original string `the design process`) | Jamaica, *National Standards Curriculum Guide — Grades 4–6 — Resource & Technology*, printed p. 31 — Jamaica's 'E' Design Process used in every project: define the problem in its context, generate ideas, select a solution (resources, efficiency, cost), test the solution, produce it (safety, accuracy, efficiency), evaluate against the original problem/design, present results; the appendix's five aims of the Design Process (identify needs/problems, plan work to criteria and deadlines, use resources incl. the natural environment for alternative proposals, produce a product/system that solves the need, evaluate the solution and the process). **Acceptance count: taught in 1 country (Jamaica).**

Jamaica's own way: Jamaica teaches designing through one named method — the 'E' Design Process — used in every Resource & Technology project, with its five aims stated in the guide's appendix. That named, repeated process is carried here exactly as Jamaica frames it, and the same steps organise Jamaica's toy car, flashlight and motor projects elsewhere in this course.
