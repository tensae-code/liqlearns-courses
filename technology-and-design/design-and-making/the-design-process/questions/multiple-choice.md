# The design process — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is the first step of Jamaica's 'E' Design Process?**
a) Produce the product b) Define the problem in its context c) Present the results d) Buy materials
**Answer: b) Define the problem in its context**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) When selecting a solution, which three things does the guide compare?**
a) Colour, size and shine b) Resources, efficiency and cost c) Speed, noise and weight only d) Price, brand and packaging
**Answer: b) Resources, efficiency and cost**

**3. (Sub lesson 2) When is a solution tested?**
a) Never b) Only after selling it c) Before full production, while faults are cheap to fix d) Only by the teacher
**Answer: c) Before full production, while faults are cheap to fix**

**4. (Sub lesson 2) Producing the solution must be done with…**
a) Safety, accuracy and efficiency b) Speed only c) No checking d) The most expensive tools
**Answer: a) Safety, accuracy and efficiency**

**5. (Sub lesson 3) Against what is the finished product evaluated?**
a) Against a classmate's product b) Against the original problem and design c) Against its colour d) It is never evaluated
**Answer: b) Against the original problem and design**
