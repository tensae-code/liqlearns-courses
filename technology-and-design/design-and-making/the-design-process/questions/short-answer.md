# The design process — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, or by doing the step named.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Why must a problem be defined in its context, not just named?**
**Answer:** Because who needs it, where and why decide what will count as a good solution — the same object can solve the problem in one place and fail in another.

**2. (Sub lesson 3) Name the five aims of the Design Process from the guide's appendix.**
**Answer:** Identify needs/problems; plan work to criteria and deadlines; use resources including the natural environment for alternative proposals; produce a product/system that solves the need; evaluate the solution and the process.
