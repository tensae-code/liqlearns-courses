# Hands-on — The construction process stages and the technological route card (carta de ruta tecnológica); the sketch (croquis)

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## Route-card a familiar build
Take a build you know (a stool, a kite, a box). Write its route card: stages in order, one operation and one check per stage.

## Croquis before card
Draw the croquis of the same article first, then check: does your route card build exactly the article your croquis shows? Fix whichever is wrong.
