# The construction process stages and the technological route card (carta de ruta tecnológica); the sketch (croquis) — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is route card (carta de ruta tecnológica)?
a) the card that records the stages and operations of a build in order b) Only a price c) Only decoration d) Nothing to do with this lesson
**Answer: a) the card that records the stages and operations of a build in order**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is sketch (croquis)?
a) Only history b) a quick freehand drawing that fixes an article's shape c) Only storage d) A kind of glue
**Answer: b) a quick freehand drawing that fixes an article's shape**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is stage?
a) Only old tools b) Only painting c) one main phase of the construction process d) Only a price
**Answer: c) one main phase of the construction process**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson teach, in one line?
a) Only decoration b) Nothing to do with this lesson c) Only history d) making follows stages, and the stages are written down: the technological route card (carta de ruta tecnológica) carries an article from stage to stage, and the sketch (croquis) carries its shape.
**Answer: d) making follows stages, and the stages are written down: the technological route card (carta de ruta tecnológica) carries an article from stage to stage, and the sketch (croquis) carries its shape.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** How does Cuba's book teach this?
a) As theory only, never practised b) Only in a glossary c) Only as an exam d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Spanish
**Answer: d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Spanish**
