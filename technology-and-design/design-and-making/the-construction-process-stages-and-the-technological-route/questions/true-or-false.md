# The construction process stages and the technological route card (carta de ruta tecnológica); the sketch (croquis) — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Route card (carta de ruta tecnológica) is the card that records the stages and operations of a build in order.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Sketch (croquis) has nothing to do with this lesson.
**Answer: False** — It is part of the lesson: a quick freehand drawing that fixes an article's shape.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Stage is one main phase of the construction process.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.
