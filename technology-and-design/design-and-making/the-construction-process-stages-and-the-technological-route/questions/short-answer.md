# The construction process stages and the technological route card (carta de ruta tecnológica); the sketch (croquis) — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In your own words, what is route card (carta de ruta tecnológica)?
**Answer:** The card that records the stages and operations of a build in order.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In your own words, what is stage?
**Answer:** One main phase of the construction process.
