# Lesson plan — The construction process stages and the technological route card (carta de ruta tecnológica); the sketch (croquis)

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Design and Making · **Lesson:** The construction process stages and the technological route card (carta de ruta tecnológica); the sketch (croquis)
**Place in the curriculum:** Design and Making (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Cuba expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** making follows stages, and the stages are written down: the technological route card (carta de ruta tecnológica) carries an article from stage to stage, and the sketch (croquis) carries its shape.
**This lesson's own level:** stocked from Cuba's Educación Laboral programmes and methodological orientations (teacher programme documents) — see lesson/sources.md. Cuba is evidence only for this lesson, counted nowhere in the acceptance count.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **The stages of the construction process** — Every build passes through the same stages — preparing, shaping the parts, joining, finishing, checking. Cuba's Grade 7 programme names the construction process stages as a thing to learn in themselves, in the unit that opens the course.
2. **The route card and the sketch** — The carta de ruta tecnológica records the route: which stage, which operation, which means, in which order. Beside it stands the croquis — the quick sketch that fixes the article's shape before the stages begin. Card and sketch together are the build's papers.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
