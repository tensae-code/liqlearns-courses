# The construction process stages and the technological route card (carta de ruta tecnológica); the sketch (croquis)

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Cuba's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — The stages of the construction process
Every build passes through the same stages — preparing, shaping the parts, joining, finishing, checking. Cuba's Grade 7 programme names the construction process stages as a thing to learn in themselves, in the unit that opens the course.

## Sub lesson 2 — The route card and the sketch
The carta de ruta tecnológica records the route: which stage, which operation, which means, in which order. Beside it stands the croquis — the quick sketch that fixes the article's shape before the stages begin. Card and sketch together are the build's papers.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: making follows stages, and the stages are written down: the technological route card (carta de ruta tecnológica) carries an article from stage to stage, and the sketch (croquis) carries its shape. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
