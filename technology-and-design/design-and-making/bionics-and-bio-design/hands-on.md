# Hands-on — Bionics and bio-design

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: learning design from nature — honeycomb structure, burdock hooks and Velcro, the dragonfly and the helicopter, the firefly and LED light, animal shapes in car design. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
