# How a modern production unit is organised — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson teach?
a) Profit, the role of new technology, workers' knowledge and skills, business and the environment, and the company organigram b) Only how to draw pictures c) Nothing practical d) A song to memorise
**Answer: a) Profit, the role of new technology, workers' knowledge and skills, business and the environment, and the company organigram**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Sub lesson 1 is about…
a) How a modern production unit is organised b) Cooking lunch c) A sports match d) The weather
**Answer: a) How a modern production unit is organised**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Sub lesson 2 asks the learner to…
a) Complete the making in full and check it b) Stop before starting c) Copy a partner's work d) Throw the materials away
**Answer: a) Complete the making in full and check it**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Greece teaches this topic inside which school book?
a) Technologia B Gym b) Only mathematics c) Only history d) It is not taught at school
**Answer: a) Technologia B Gym**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** What should the learner do first in this lesson?
a) Try the Do it first task in their own way b) Skip to the last page c) Wait to be given the answer d) Nothing at all
**Answer: a) Try the Do it first task in their own way**
