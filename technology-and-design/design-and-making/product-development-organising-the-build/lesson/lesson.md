# Product development, organising the build

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Malaysia's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Schedule and lists
Organising the build prepares two documents before making: the work schedule, and the list of materials and equipment the product needs. The build is provisioned, not improvised.

## Sub lesson 2 — The product budget
The lists are priced into the product budget — development’s money plan — so the build that follows spends inside a number the team already agreed.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: organising the build means preparing the work schedule, the materials and equipment list, and the product budget before making. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
