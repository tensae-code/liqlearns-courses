# Product development, organising the build — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the work schedule?
a) the timed plan of the build b) Only a price c) Only decoration d) Nothing to do with this lesson
**Answer: a) the timed plan of the build**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the materials and equipment list?
a) Only decoration b) everything the product needs, listed before making c) Only a price d) A kind of glue
**Answer: b) everything the product needs, listed before making**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is the product budget?
a) Only history b) Only painting c) the agreed money plan for the build d) Only storage
**Answer: c) the agreed money plan for the build**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson teach, in one line?
a) organising the build means preparing the work schedule, the materials and equipment list, and the product budget before making. b) Only prices c) Only old tools d) Nothing practical
**Answer: a) organising the build means preparing the work schedule, the materials and equipment list, and the product budget before making.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** How does Malaysia's book teach this?
a) As theory only, never practised b) Only in the glossary c) Only as an exam d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Malay (RBT)
**Answer: d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Malay (RBT)**
