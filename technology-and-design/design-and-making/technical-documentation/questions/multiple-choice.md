# Technical documentation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A notice d'utilisation is written for…
a) The builders' memories b) The person who will use the product without its builders c) The examiner only d) No reader
**Answer: b) The person who will use the product without its builders**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The manual's reader will have…
a) The builders beside them b) Only the document c) A second manual d) Nothing to do
**Answer: b) Only the document**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The Haiti groups include in the manual…
a) Only a drawing b) A work plan and the production steps c) The price list d) Their names only
**Answer: b) A work plan and the production steps**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Knowing how the product was made helps the user with…
a) Nothing b) Maintenance, repair and rebuilding c) Selling it faster d) Hiding faults
**Answer: b) Maintenance, repair and rebuilding**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** A manual is judged by…
a) Its length b) Its reader's success in using the product c) Its handwriting d) Its cover
**Answer: b) Its reader's success in using the product**
