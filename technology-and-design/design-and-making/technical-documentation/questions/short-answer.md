# Technical documentation — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why must everything the user needs be inside the document?
**Answer:** Because the user will not have the builders beside them — the document is all they will have, so anything left unwritten is lost to them.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** What is the group's test of a finished manual?
**Answer:** Hand it to someone who did not build the product and watch whether they can use and care for it from the document alone; every hesitation marks a fault to revise.
