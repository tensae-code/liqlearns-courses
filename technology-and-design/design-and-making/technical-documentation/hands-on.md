# Hands-on — Technical documentation

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## Write the manual
For a product your group has made, write its notice d'utilisation including the work plan and production steps. Keep every step in the order a user meets it.

## Stranger test
Give your manual to a reader who did not build the product. Note every question they ask; each question is a revision. Revise and record what changed.
