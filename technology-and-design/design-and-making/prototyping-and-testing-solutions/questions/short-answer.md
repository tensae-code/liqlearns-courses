# Prototyping and testing solutions — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why is a prototype built cheaply and honestly rather than as a polished final product?
**Answer:** Because its purpose is evidence — whether the solution works and where it fails — and its failures are the information the next version needs.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** What decides whether the prototype solution passes?
**Answer:** The test against the need the specification stated: water goes through the device and the result is examined against that need.
