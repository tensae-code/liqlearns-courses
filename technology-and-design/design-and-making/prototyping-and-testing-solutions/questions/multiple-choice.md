# Prototyping and testing solutions — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A prototype's job is to give…
a) A final product b) Evidence — whether the solution works and where it fails c) A decoration d) A price
**Answer: b) Evidence — whether the solution works and where it fails**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A prototype should be judged as…
a) A finished product b) A first working version built to be tried c) A failure if imperfect d) Worthless
**Answer: b) A first working version built to be tried**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The bottle filter's layers include…
a) Only cotton b) Gravel and clay balls, fine sand, activated charcoal, cotton c) Glass and metal d) Paper only
**Answer: b) Gravel and clay balls, fine sand, activated charcoal, cotton**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Activated charcoal's work in the filter is to…
a) Hold the bed b) Take up what water carries dissolved and coloured c) Cut the bottle d) Add dirt
**Answer: b) Take up what water carries dissolved and coloured**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** The prototype build is governed by safety instructions for…
a) Painting b) Cutting and sharp tools c) Singing d) Waiting
**Answer: b) Cutting and sharp tools**
