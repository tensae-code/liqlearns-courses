# Prototyping and testing solutions — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A prototype that fails a test has wasted the team's work.
**Answer: False** — Its failures are the information the next version needs — that is the prototype's purpose.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Each layer of the bottle filter has its own work to do.
**Answer: True** — Coarse layers stop coarse dirt, sand filters finer, charcoal takes up dissolved and coloured matter, cotton holds the bed at the outlet.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Safety instructions are unnecessary because a prototype is only a model.
**Answer: False** — The build involves cutting and sharp tools, and the sequence governs it with safety instructions.
