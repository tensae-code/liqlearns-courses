# Lesson plan — Prototyping and testing solutions

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Design and Making · **Lesson:** Prototyping and testing solutions
**Place in the curriculum:** Design and Making (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Haiti expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** a prototype is a first working version built to be tried: Haiti's teams build a bottle water-filter — gravel and clay balls, fine sand, activated charcoal, cotton — and test whether their solution actually cleans water.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on teacher-guide evidence only — Haiti's ITAP Projet Nectar pedagogical sequences (MENFP) are the books in hand for this topic (see lesson/sources.md). Teacher guides are evidence, counted nowhere in the acceptance count.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **Prototype — the first working try** — Built to answer a question, not to be the final product.
2. **Building the bottle filter** — Layers in a bottle: gravel and clay balls, fine sand, activated charcoal, cotton.
3. **Trying it — and working safely** — The test decides, under safety instructions for cutting and sharp tools.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of Design and Making. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say what a prototype is for.
2. Name the bottle filter's layers in order.
3. Test a prototype against its specification, safely.
## What you need
A plastic bottle, gravel and clay balls, fine sand, activated charcoal, cotton, cutting tools under the safety instructions, and dirty water to test with.
