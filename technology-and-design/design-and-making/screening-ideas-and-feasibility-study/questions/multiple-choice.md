# Screening ideas and feasibility study — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is screening?
a) comparing generated ideas and choosing which to carry forward b) Only a price c) Only decoration d) Nothing to do with this lesson
**Answer: a) comparing generated ideas and choosing which to carry forward**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is feasibility study?
a) Only history b) a structured test of whether an idea can succeed before it is built c) Only storage d) A kind of glue
**Answer: b) a structured test of whether an idea can succeed before it is built**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is market feasibility?
a) Only old tools b) Only painting c) whether a product can sell: buyers, price, competition, marketing d) Only a price
**Answer: c) whether a product can sell: buyers, price, competition, marketing**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson teach, in one line?
a) Only decoration b) Nothing to do with this lesson c) Only history d) before building, ideas are screened and the survivor is tested by a feasibility study — can it sell (market feasibility) and can it be made (technical feasibility)?
**Answer: d) before building, ideas are screened and the survivor is tested by a feasibility study — can it sell (market feasibility) and can it be made (technical feasibility)?**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** How does Iran's book teach this?
a) As theory only, never practised b) Only in a glossary c) Only as an exam d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Persian
**Answer: d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Persian**
