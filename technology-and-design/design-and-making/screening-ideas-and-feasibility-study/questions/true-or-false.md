# Screening ideas and feasibility study — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Screening is comparing generated ideas and choosing which to carry forward.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Feasibility study has nothing to do with this lesson.
**Answer: False** — It is part of the lesson: a structured test of whether an idea can succeed before it is built.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Market feasibility is whether a product can sell: buyers, price, competition, marketing.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.
