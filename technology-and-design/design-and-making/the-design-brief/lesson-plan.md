# Lesson plan — The design brief

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Design and Making · **Lesson:** The design brief
**Place in the curriculum:** Design and Making (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Samoa expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** a design brief states what is to be made and what it must do: Samoa works it twice — constructing a garment to a brief, and identifying the key features a garment needs for its purpose before deciding style and design.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on student book evidence — Samoa's MESC textbooks (see lesson/sources.md). It counts in the acceptance count (student book, not evidence-only).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **What a brief is** — The brief is the design's orders: the product, its purpose, and the features it must have.
2. **Working a brief — the garment project** — Samoa's Year 9 brief is carried through making: matching notches, pinning and tacking, machine seams, a hand-hem finish, then evaluating the product.
3. **Briefing a new garment — the school-social top** — Year 11 briefs a top for a school social: identify the key features for the purpose, revise fibre properties, then decide style and printed design before making.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of Design and Making. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say what a design brief contains.
2. Carry a garment brief through its construction steps in order.
3. Explain purpose → fibre properties → style as the brief's decision order.
## What you need
Paper for writing a brief; a garment to evaluate against a sample brief.

1. **Do it first** — the opening doing task in lesson/lesson.md.
2. **The explanation** — the sub lessons in order (lesson/lesson.md).
   - ADD IMAGE: a picture of a written brief page — product, purpose, key features — beside the garment it produced, with arrows from three features to the places they appear on the garment — the student matches each brief feature to its place on the garment.
