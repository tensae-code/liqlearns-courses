# Hands-on — The design brief

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## Write the brief
Write a design brief for a top for a named occasion: product, wearer, purpose, and at least four key features it must have. Beside each feature, note the fibre property or design choice that will deliver it.

## Evaluate against a brief
Take the brief you wrote and a real garment. Evaluate the garment feature by feature against the brief and write the verdict: met, partly met, or missed — with evidence.
