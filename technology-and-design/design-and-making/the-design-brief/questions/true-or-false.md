# The design brief — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Later decisions are judged against the brief.
**Answer: True** — The brief is the standard the product is evaluated by.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Tacking before machining is wasted effort.
**Answer: False** — Pinning and tacking hold the layers so the machine seam lands truly.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Deciding style first and purpose later serves the purpose just as well.
**Answer: False** — Purpose-first ordering makes the design answer the need; style-first relies on luck.
