# The design brief — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Write the three things every garment brief should name.
**Answer:** Answer: The product, its purpose (wearer and occasion), and the key features it must have.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Why does the Year 11 brief revise fibre properties before choosing style and print?
**Answer:** Answer: The key features depend on material behaviour; knowing what fibres can do lets the style and print be chosen to deliver the features the purpose demands.
