# The design brief — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A design brief states…
a) What is to be made and what it must do b) Only the maker's name c) The price of cloth in town d) Nothing written
**Answer: a) What is to be made and what it must do**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Key features in a brief are…
a) The non-negotiable things the product must have or do b) Decorations added at the end c) The maker's favourite colours d) Steps that can be skipped
**Answer: a) The non-negotiable things the product must have or do**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Notches are matched so that…
a) Pattern pieces meet truly when joined b) The cloth looks patterned c) The seams can be skipped d) The hem is hidden
**Answer: a) Pattern pieces meet truly when joined**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The construction order in the Year 9 project is…
a) Match notches, pin and tack, machine the seams, hand-finish the hem, evaluate b) Evaluate, cut nothing, sell c) Hem first, then cut, then design d) Machine everything, never evaluate
**Answer: a) Match notches, pin and tack, machine the seams, hand-finish the hem, evaluate**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** For the school-social top, style is decided…
a) After purpose and fibre properties b) Before the purpose is known c) Never d) By copying another top
**Answer: a) After purpose and fibre properties**
