# Hands-on — Composition, form and proportion in design

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: the laws of composition (unity, balance, rhythm, emphasis) and the golden ratio 1:1.618 with the Fibonacci sequence 0, 1, 1, 2, 3, 5, 8…, constructed geometrically and found in nature and products. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
