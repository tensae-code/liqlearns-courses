# Hands-on — Evaluating a made product

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## Evaluate the flashlight (or any product)
Write a five-item checklist for a made product, score each item on a 1–5 scale, and finish with the need verdict in one sentence.

## Two judges
Evaluate the same product independently with a partner using the same checklist. Compare scores; where you differ by more than one point, find the evidence that settles it.
