# Evaluating a made product — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) A prescribed checklist is used so that…**
a) The judge can skip items b) No side of the work is forgotten c) The product looks neat d) Marks are higher
**Answer: b) No side of the work is forgotten**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) A rating scale records…**
a) Only pass or fail b) How well each item scores, by degrees c) The maker's name d) The date only
**Answer: b) How well each item scores, by degrees**

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Functionality asks…**
a) Is it pretty? b) Does it do its job? c) Was it expensive? d) Is it new?
**Answer: b) Does it do its job?**

**4. (Sub lesson 2) Accuracy compares the product against…**
a) Another student's work b) Its design and dimensions c) The checklist's length d) Its colour
**Answer: b) Its design and dimensions**

**5. (Sub lesson 3) The final evaluation question is whether the product…**
a) Took a long time b) Meets the original need c) Used all the material d) Pleased the class
**Answer: b) Meets the original need**
