# Evaluating a made product — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, or by doing the step named.

**1. (Sub lesson 3) A motor is neat and accurate but will not turn. Give its verdict in evaluation language.**
**Answer:** Neatness and accuracy score well, functionality fails — and since the need was a working motor, the product does not yet meet its original need; fault-finding and rework follow.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Why prescribe the checklist instead of letting each judge choose what to look at?**
**Answer:** So every judge examines the same sides of the work and verdicts can be compared — evaluation becomes evidence, not taste.
