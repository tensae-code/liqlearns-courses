# Lesson plan — The integrated smart-building project (design side)

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Design and Making · **Lesson:** The integrated smart-building project (design side)
**Place in the curriculum:** Design and Making (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Kuwait expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the book's closing project designs a smart building as one whole: its parts and its IoT devices are modelled, their print settings are tested, and everything is prepared virtually in UltiMaker Cura with geometric precision before anything is executed, alongside the network plan.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on a student book — Kuwait's *الذكاء الرقمي 8* (*Digital Intelligence, Grade 8, Term 2*), Ministry of Education, State of Kuwait (see lesson/sources.md). It counts in the acceptance count (student book, not evidence-only).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **One project, designed as a whole** — The integrated project joins two kinds of design in one building: the physical parts that will be modelled and printed, and the IoT devices that will sense and act inside it.
2. **Modelling the parts and devices in Cura** — Each part of the building and each device housing is modelled and brought into UltiMaker Cura, where its print settings are tested on screen.
3. **Preparing virtually before execution** — Only when the virtual build is complete and checked does execution begin.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of Design and Making. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say what the integrated project designs as one whole.
2. Say what is modelled and tested in Cura, and why precision matters there.
3. Put the project order — design, test virtually, execute — in order and defend it.
## What you need
A computer with UltiMaker Cura if available; if not, follow the project steps on paper with a sketch of the building and its devices.
