# The integrated smart-building project (design side)

## Do it first
Sketch a small smart building: walls, a door, and two devices that sense or act — a light that answers darkness, a fan that answers heat. Your project must design the building and its devices together. That joined-up design is this lesson's work.

## Sub lesson 1 — One project, designed as a whole
The integrated project joins two kinds of design in one building: the physical parts that will be modelled and printed, and the IoT devices that will sense and act inside it. Neither side is designed alone — the parts must hold and fit the devices, and the devices' places are fixed while the building is designed, alongside the network plan that will connect them.

## Sub lesson 2 — Modelling the parts and devices in Cura
Each part of the building and each device housing is modelled and brought into UltiMaker Cura, where its print settings are tested on screen. Size and fit are checked with geometric precision in the virtual build: parts that must meet must match, and a device must sit exactly where the design puts it. Errors found here cost nothing but a correction.

## Sub lesson 3 — Preparing virtually before execution
Only when the virtual build is complete and checked does execution begin. Preparing virtually means every part has been modelled, sliced, previewed and approved before any material is used — so the real build follows a tested plan instead of discovering its mistakes in plastic. That order — design, test virtually, then execute — is the project's core discipline.

## Say it to your companion

Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
