# Hands-on — The integrated smart-building project (design side)

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## Design the whole
On paper, design one room of a smart building: draw its parts, place two IoT devices, and draw the lines of the network plan joining them. Label what each part must fit.

## Virtual check list
Write the checklist you would complete in Cura before executing this project: every part modelled, every setting tested, every fit checked. Tick each item only when you could prove it.
