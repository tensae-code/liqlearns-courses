# The integrated smart-building project (design side) — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Say in one sentence what the integrated project joins together.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Say the project order in one sentence.
**Answer:** Design the whole, model and test it virtually in Cura, prepare everything, and only then execute.
