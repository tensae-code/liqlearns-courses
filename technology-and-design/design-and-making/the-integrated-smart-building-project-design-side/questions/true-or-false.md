# The integrated smart-building project (design side) — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In the integrated project, the building's parts must hold and fit its IoT devices.
**Answer: True** — Neither side is designed alone; the parts must fit the devices.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Errors found in the virtual build cost material to fix.
**Answer: False** — Errors found on screen cost nothing but a correction.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Execution begins only when the virtual build is complete and checked.
**Answer: True** — Every part is modelled, sliced, previewed and approved first.
