# The integrated smart-building project (design side) — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the integrated project design as one whole?
a) A smart building's parts and its IoT devices together b) Only the network cables c) Only a poster about buildings d) Two unrelated projects
**Answer: a) A smart building's parts and its IoT devices together**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Alongside what plan is the design done?
a) The network plan that will connect the devices b) A shopping list c) A weather forecast d) No plan at all
**Answer: a) The network plan that will connect the devices**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What happens to parts and devices in Cura?
a) They are modelled and their print settings are tested on screen b) They are printed without checking c) They are deleted d) They are painted
**Answer: a) They are modelled and their print settings are tested on screen**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Why is geometric precision checked in the virtual build?
a) Parts that must meet must match, and devices must sit exactly where designed b) Precision is only for decoration c) It makes the screen brighter d) It is never checked
**Answer: a) Parts that must meet must match, and devices must sit exactly where designed**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** What is the project's core order?
a) Design, test virtually, then execute b) Execute first, design later c) Print everything, then plan d) Skip the virtual build
**Answer: a) Design, test virtually, then execute**
