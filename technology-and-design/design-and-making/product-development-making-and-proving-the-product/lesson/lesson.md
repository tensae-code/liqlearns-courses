# Product development, making and proving the product

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Malaysia's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Making and proving
The product is produced — functioning, or partly functioning and honestly labelled so — then tested and evaluated on its function and suitability. Development does not end at made; it ends at proved.

## Sub lesson 2 — Improvements and documentation
Improvements are proposed from the test-report results, not from opinion, and the product documentation is prepared — the development’s written record, closing the longest project in the Malaysian RBT course.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: making ends in proving: test and evaluate the product, improve from the test report, and document the product. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
