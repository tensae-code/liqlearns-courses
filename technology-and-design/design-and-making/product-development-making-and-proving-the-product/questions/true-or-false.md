# Product development, making and proving the product — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A functioning product is a produced product that works as designed.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The test report has nothing to do with this lesson.
**Answer: False** — It is part of the lesson: the written results of testing and evaluating the product.
