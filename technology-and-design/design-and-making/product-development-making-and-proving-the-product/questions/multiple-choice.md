# Product development, making and proving the product — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is a functioning product?
a) a produced product that works as designed b) Only a price c) Only decoration d) Nothing to do with this lesson
**Answer: a) a produced product that works as designed**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is the test report?
a) Only decoration b) the written results of testing and evaluating the product c) Only a price d) A kind of glue
**Answer: b) the written results of testing and evaluating the product**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is product documentation?
a) Only history b) Only painting c) the prepared written record of the product’s development d) Only storage
**Answer: c) the prepared written record of the product’s development**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson teach, in one line?
a) making ends in proving: test and evaluate the product, improve from the test report, and document the product. b) Only prices c) Only old tools d) Nothing practical
**Answer: a) making ends in proving: test and evaluate the product, improve from the test report, and document the product.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** How does Malaysia's book teach this?
a) As theory only, never practised b) Only in the glossary c) Only as an exam d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Malay (RBT)
**Answer: d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Malay (RBT)**
