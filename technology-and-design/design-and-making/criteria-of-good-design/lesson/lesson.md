# Criteria of good design

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Malaysia's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Criteria explored from real products
Good design is not a feeling: Malaysia explores the criteria of good design from real products, holding each product against the criteria and letting the evidence teach the standard.

## Sub lesson 2 — Judging with criteria
With criteria in hand, any product can be judged fairly — the same tests, applied the same way, to bottles and buildings alike. Criteria turn taste into judgement.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: good design is judged by criteria, explored from real products — the same tests applied to every product. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.

## Do It Yourself
1. Do the Do it first task again, carefully this time, using everything the lesson taught.
2. Check your work against the lesson's own checks — fix one thing you find.
3. Keep your work: it is practice evidence, and the next lesson builds on it.
