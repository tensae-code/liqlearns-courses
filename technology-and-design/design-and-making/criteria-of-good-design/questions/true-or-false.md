# Criteria of good design — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A criterion is a named test a product is judged against.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Exploring from real products has nothing to do with this lesson.
**Answer: False** — It is part of the lesson: deriving the criteria by examining actual products.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Fair judgement is applying the same criteria the same way to every product.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.
