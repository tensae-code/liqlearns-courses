# The elements of design — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Garisan (line) is the element that divides areas and shows direction, flow, connections, tension and stability.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Rupa (shape) has nothing to do with this lesson.
**Answer: False** — It is part of the lesson: the flat outline of a thing — organic or geometric.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Nilai (value) is the lightness or darkness element of a design.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.
