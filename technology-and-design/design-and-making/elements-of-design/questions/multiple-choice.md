# The elements of design — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is garisan (line)?
a) the element that divides areas and shows direction, flow, connections, tension and stability b) Only a price c) Only decoration d) Nothing to do with this lesson
**Answer: a) the element that divides areas and shows direction, flow, connections, tension and stability**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is rupa (shape)?
a) Only decoration b) the flat outline of a thing — organic or geometric c) Only a price d) A kind of glue
**Answer: b) the flat outline of a thing — organic or geometric**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is nilai (value)?
a) Only history b) Only painting c) the lightness or darkness element of a design d) Only storage
**Answer: c) the lightness or darkness element of a design**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson teach, in one line?
a) the elements of design — line, shape, form, texture, size, colour, space and value — are what every product is built from and read by. b) Only prices c) Only old tools d) Nothing practical
**Answer: a) the elements of design — line, shape, form, texture, size, colour, space and value — are what every product is built from and read by.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** How does Malaysia's book teach this?
a) As theory only, never practised b) Only in the glossary c) Only as an exam d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Malay (RBT)
**Answer: d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Malay (RBT)**
