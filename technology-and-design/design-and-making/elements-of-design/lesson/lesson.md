# The elements of design

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Malaysia's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — The eight elements
Every design is built from elements: line (garisan), shape (rupa — organic and geometric), form (bentuk), texture (tekstur), size (saiz), colour (warna), space (ruang) and value (nilai). Malaysia shows each element on real products and buildings.

## Sub lesson 2 — Reading the elements in a chosen product
At Tingkatan 1 the elements are identified from a chosen product — line first, with its jobs: dividing areas, showing direction and flow, showing connections, tension and stability. The designer reads a product element by element before changing any of them.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: the elements of design — line, shape, form, texture, size, colour, space and value — are what every product is built from and read by. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
