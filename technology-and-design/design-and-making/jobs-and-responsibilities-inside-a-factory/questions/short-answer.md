# Jobs and responsibilities inside a factory — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Say in one sentence what this lesson teaches.
**Answer:** General director, production director and production line planning/optimisation, quality-control director and quality standards/certification bodies, and the other company roles pupils take on.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name one material, tool or step this lesson works with.
**Answer:** Any one named in the lesson text — for example, the first step of sub lesson 1: Jobs and responsibilities inside a factory.
