# Hands-on — Jobs and responsibilities inside a factory

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: general director, production director and production line planning/optimisation, quality-control director and quality standards/certification bodies, and the other company roles pupils take on. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
