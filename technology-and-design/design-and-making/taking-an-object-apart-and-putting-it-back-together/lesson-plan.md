# Lesson plan \u2014 Taking an object apart and putting it back together

**Course:** Technology and Design \u00b7 **Title:** Design and Making \u00b7 **Lesson:** Taking an object apart and putting it back together
**Place in the curriculum:** Design and Making (CURRICULUM.md) \u2014 stocked in the Gabon expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** dismantling is analysis: take a torch apart and reassemble it and you learn its components, its energy, and the difference between assemblies you can undo and those you cannot.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on teacher-guide evidence only \u2014 Gabon's *Super Efficace* Sciences teacher guides (EDIG / \u00c9dicef, Institut P\u00e9dagogique National, 2024) are the books in hand for this topic (see lesson/sources.md). Teacher guides are evidence, counted nowhere in the acceptance count.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on \u2014 an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of \u2026" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **Taking the torch apart** \u2014 A torch (lampe de poche) separates into its components; assembly and disassembly show how it works.
2. **Demountable and non-demountable assembly** \u2014 Some joinings undo for repair; others are made never to open.
3. **Why dismantling matters** \u2014 Broken or worn parts can be changed only if the object can come apart.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson \u2014 no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of Design and Making. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name a torch's components and its energy source.
2. Tell demountable from non-demountable assembly.
3. Say why dismantling matters for repair.
## What you need
A torch (lampe de poche) that can be opened, with its cell or battery.

1. **Do it first** \u2014 the opening doing task in lesson/lesson.md.
2. **The explanation** \u2014 the sub lessons in order (lesson/lesson.md).
