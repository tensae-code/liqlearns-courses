# Hands-on \u2014 Taking an object apart and putting it back together

Real practice \u2014 this subject is learned by doing.

## Torch analysis
Dismantle a torch. Draw and label its components, name its energy source, and write its use function. Reassemble and test. Note one part that could wear out and how you would change it.

## Assembly survey
Find four assembled objects. For each, decide: demountable or non-demountable? Write the evidence (screws, clips, glue, welds) that decided you.
