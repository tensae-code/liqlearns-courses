# Taking an object apart and putting it back together \u2014 short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What four things does dismantling a torch reveal?
**Answer:** Its use function, its look (user expectation), its working principles and its technical functions.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** What happens to a non-demountable object when one inner part fails?
**Answer:** It often cannot be opened without damage, so one failed part can kill the whole object instead of being changed.
