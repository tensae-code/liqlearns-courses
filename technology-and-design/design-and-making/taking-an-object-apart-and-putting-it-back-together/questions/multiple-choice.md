# Taking an object apart and putting it back together \u2014 multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is a torch's energy source?
a) Wind b) A cell or battery c) Muscular energy d) Sunlight only
**Answer: b) A cell or battery**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Taking an object apart and reassembling it is a way to\u2026
a) Waste time b) Analyse how the object works c) Hide its parts d) Make it heavier
**Answer: b) Analyse how the object works**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A demountable assembly is one that\u2026
a) Can never be opened b) Can be undone and redone c) Is made of glass d) Needs no tools ever
**Answer: b) Can be undone and redone**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which is a non-demountable joining?
a) A screw thread b) A clip c) Glue or welding d) A battery cover
**Answer: c) Glue or welding**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Why does dismantling matter?
a) So broken or worn parts can be changed b) So objects look older c) So parts can be lost d) It does not matter
**Answer: a) So broken or worn parts can be changed**
