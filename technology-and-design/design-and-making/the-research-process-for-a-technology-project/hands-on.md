# Hands-on — The research process for a technology project

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: title, problem, purpose, social needs served, hypothesis, parameters held out, limits, procedure, definitions, conclusions, proposals for further research, bibliography. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
