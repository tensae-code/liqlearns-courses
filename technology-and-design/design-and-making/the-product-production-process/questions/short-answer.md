# The product production process — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In your own words, what is the trial model (mock-up)?
**Answer:** A first made version that proves the design before the real product.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In your own words, what is presenting the product?
**Answer:** Showing and explaining the finished product at the end of the cycle.
