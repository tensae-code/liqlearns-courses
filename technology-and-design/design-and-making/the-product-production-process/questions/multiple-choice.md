# The product production process — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the trial model (mock-up)?
a) a first made version that proves the design before the real product b) Only a price c) Only decoration d) Nothing to do with this lesson
**Answer: a) a first made version that proves the design before the real product**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is analysing the sketch?
a) Only decoration b) checking and improving the design drawing before materials are spent c) Only a price d) A kind of glue
**Answer: b) checking and improving the design drawing before materials are spent**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is presenting the product?
a) Only history b) Only painting c) showing and explaining the finished product at the end of the cycle d) Only storage
**Answer: c) showing and explaining the finished product at the end of the cycle**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson teach, in one line?
a) the product production process is Malaysia's cycle: idea → sketch → analyse and improve → trial model → make → present. b) Only prices c) Only old tools d) Nothing practical
**Answer: a) the product production process is Malaysia's cycle: idea → sketch → analyse and improve → trial model → make → present.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** How does Malaysia's book teach this?
a) As theory only, never practised b) Only in the glossary c) Only as an exam d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Malay (RBT)
**Answer: d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Malay (RBT)**
