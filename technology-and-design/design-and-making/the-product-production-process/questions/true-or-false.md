# The product production process — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The trial model (mock-up) is a first made version that proves the design before the real product.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Analysing the sketch has nothing to do with this lesson.
**Answer: False** — It is part of the lesson: checking and improving the design drawing before materials are spent.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Presenting the product is showing and explaining the finished product at the end of the cycle.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.
