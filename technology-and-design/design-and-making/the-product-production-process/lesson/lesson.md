# The product production process

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Malaysia's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Malaysia's own design-process cycle
Malaysia runs every product through one cycle: generate the idea, sketch the design, analyse and improve the sketch, make the trial model or mock-up, make the product with the chosen technology, and present the product. Nothing is made before it is sketched and improved.

## Sub lesson 2 — The cycle at work
The cycle is a loop of judgement: the sketch is analysed before materials are spent, and the trial model proves the design before the real product is made. Presentation closes the cycle — the product is shown and explained.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: the product production process is Malaysia's cycle: idea → sketch → analyse and improve → trial model → make → present. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
