# Hands-on — Redesigning a thing by changing its component parts

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: drawing 3–5 new designs for a cup by adding or changing handles, lids and bases. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
