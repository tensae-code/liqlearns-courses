| the principles of technical design, part 1 — the optimisation principles: the iteration principle (nguyên tắc lặp đi lặp lại — the process of finding a solution to a problem in technical design includes different steps performed in sequence; each step must be performed fully, in the right order and by the right method, and is often repeated to ensure an optimal solution is obtained; the repeated action aims to review, adjust, improve and control the quality of intermediate products in the design process as well as in the whole design process — proposing solutions, building the prototype for the solution and verifying the solution are the highly iterative stages because of the multi-solution character of technical-design solutions; this principle is very important and is considered the essence of technical design); the simplification principle (nguyên tắc đơn giản hoá — for a problem or need, there are many different solutions in idea and technology that can be used, towards the optimal solutions; when examining and before choosing a solution, always ask: is there a simpler solution?; the simplification principle also makes the solution easy to approach, easy to understand and easy to apply in practice; however, the proposed solutions are an important basis for performing the simplification principle in technical design; for a product, simplification is expressed through its form, structure, function, and operations of installation, use, maintenance and repair of the product); the optimal-solution principle (nguyên tắc giải pháp tối ưu — in technical design there are many different solutions to a given problem; having a perfectly complete solution is hard to achieve, so the optimal solution is the real goal of technical design; the optimal solution is proposed on the basis of fully considering constraints such as time, cost, technology and implementation resources and effects on the environment, especially user and customer needs; the constraint on the balance between technology, materials, function and form is the balanced choice for the designed product); the minimal-finance principle (nguyên tắc tối thiểu tài chính — expressing the goal of designing a quality product with minimal cost; this principle has great significance for solving many societal problems, meeting many human needs with a limited financial resource; among relatively equal solutions in the level of meeting the set requirements, examine and choose the solution of lowest cost; this principle must also be followed in the design process and the product-production process) (original string `the principles of technical design, part 1`) | Vietnam, *Công nghệ 10 — Thiết kế và công nghệ*, printed p. 114 |
