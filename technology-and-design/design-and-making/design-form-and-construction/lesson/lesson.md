# Design — form and construction

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Kazakhstan's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Design
Kazakhstan's books teach it this way (the student book, printed p. 67): Form and construction — the designer's work: sketching and building a flower vase of your own design (Lesson 14). The learner works through the named materials and steps in order, saying what each step does before doing the next one.

## Sub lesson 2 — Design
The Kazakh treatment continues and completes the topic: What design is (from disegno: intention, plan, drawing, pattern); designing a product (§11). The learner finishes the make, checks it against the book's own sequence, and fixes one thing before presenting the work.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: Design — form and construction — form and construction — the designer's work: sketching and building a flower vase of your own design (Lesson 14). Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
