# Hands-on — Studying a technical object

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## Three-question study
Choose one manual and one mechanised tool from documentation. Complete a study sheet for each: form, materials part by part, function of use. Underline one answer where the machine's study differs most from the hand tool's.

## Documentation reading
From a photograph alone, write what you can and cannot answer of the three questions. List what extra evidence you would need for the gaps.
