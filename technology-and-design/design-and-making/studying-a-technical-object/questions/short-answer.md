# Studying a technical object — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Write the three questions of the study in order.
**Answer:** What is its form? What materials is it made of, part by part? What is its function of use — what does the user do with it?

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** What does a fixed study shape reveal that changing questions would hide?
**Answer:** It reveals the evolution between objects exactly, because both were asked identical questions, so every difference in the answers is a real difference in the objects.
