## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Greece's book teaches it, and compare.

Greece's book teaches it this way: the product-design team and design engineers' research across materials (ceramics, petrochemicals, plastics) and electrical/electronic systems (*EPAL Research Work*, printed p. 252). ## Sub lesson 2 — Technical design in production — doing it yourself
Now the learner does the whole task unaided: the product-design team and design engineers' research across materials (ceramics, petrochemicals, plastics) and electrical/electronic systems. Working from the first sub lesson's sequence, the learner plans the make, works through it step by step, and checks the finished piece against what Greece's book shows.
