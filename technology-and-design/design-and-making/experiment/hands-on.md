# Hands-on — Experiment

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: making yoghurt (тараг) — 1 litre of milk heated to 40–45°C, 50 g of starter stirred in, fermented at 35–40°C for 2–4 hours; the technology card with quantities, temperatures and times, and judging the result. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
