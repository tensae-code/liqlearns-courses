# Lesson plan — The design project — carrying a project through

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Design and Making · **Lesson:** The design project — carrying a project through
**Place in the curriculum:** Design and Making (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Morocco expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** a design project is carried through in full: analyse the context, write the specification, model the system, make and assemble the parts, then test that it works — Morocco's worked project designs and builds an autonomous wind turbine.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on teacher-guide evidence only — Morocco's Technologie teacher guides (Ministère de l'Éducation Nationale, du Préscolaire et des Sports, Royaume du Maroc) are the books in hand for this topic (see lesson/sources.md). Teacher guides are evidence, counted nowhere in the acceptance count. The pupil manuals themselves are not in hand; the placements are filed from the guides' answer elements (Part 5) as guide evidence.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **From context and specification to a buildable model** — A project opens with context analysis — social, technological, economic, ecological — then a specification, then modelling: SysML diagrams and the functional chains (information chain, energy chain) of the future product. Only then is a buildable model proposed: in Morocco's project, learners measure the local wind (anemometer, weathervane), research the alternator, and propose a wind-turbine model they can actually make.
2. **Making, assembling and testing** — The project's second half is the workshop: make the parts, assemble them into the turbine, and test that it works — turning wind into electricity autonomously. Testing is against the specification: if the turbine does not produce as specified, the project loops back to the model, not to excuses.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of Design and Making. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. List the stages of carrying a project through, in order.
2. Say what is measured and researched before a buildable model is proposed.
3. Describe how a finished project is tested against its specification.
## What you need
Paper for the project file; for a class build: rotor material, a small alternator or motor, a multimeter, an anemometer if available.
