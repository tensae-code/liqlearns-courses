# The design project — carrying a project through — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A design project opens with…
a) context analysis and a specification b) buying parts at random c) painting the finished product d) writing the advert first
**Answer: a) context analysis and a specification**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Before proposing a buildable wind-turbine model, learners measure…
a) the local wind, with an anemometer and weathervane b) the teacher's height c) the price of paint d) nothing — wind is assumed
**Answer: a) the local wind, with an anemometer and weathervane**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Morocco's rule: a building permit is needed for masts of…
a) 12 m or more b) 2 m or more c) any height at all d) 100 m exactly
**Answer: a) 12 m or more**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Making and assembling come…
a) after the model answers the specification on paper b) before any specification c) instead of testing d) only if time remains
**Answer: a) after the model answers the specification on paper**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** A project is finished when…
a) the made thing passes its own specification in testing b) it looks finished c) the parts are bought d) the report title is written
**Answer: a) the made thing passes its own specification in testing**
