# Hands-on — The design project — carrying a project through

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## Project file for a wind turbine
Write a one-page project file: context (who needs the power, what constraints), specification (what the turbine must do), the two functional chains in words, what you would measure (wind) and research (alternator) before proposing your model.

## Test plan
Write the test your finished turbine must pass: what you will measure, with what instrument, and what result counts as success against your specification. Add the rule check: would your mast need a building permit (12 m or more)?
