# Working safely with tools and materials — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, or by doing the step named.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Why does Jamaica attach safety to every task instead of teaching it once?**
**Answer:** Because each stage has its own hazards — cutting, boring and finishing are dangerous in different ways — so the safe habit must be learned inside each skill.

**2. (Sub lesson 3) A classmate says 'my car looks best, so my work is best,' but they cut toward their hand throughout. Answer them.**
**Answer:** Looks are not the whole judgement: observing safety is part of the work's quality, so unsafe making is not well-made work however fine the product looks.
