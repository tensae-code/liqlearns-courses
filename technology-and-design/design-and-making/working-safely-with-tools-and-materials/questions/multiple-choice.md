# Working safely with tools and materials — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Jamaica's rule places safety considerations…**
a) In one talk at the year's start b) In every practical task c) Only for sharp tools d) Only for examinations
**Answer: b) In every practical task**

**2. (Sub lesson 2) A safe workspace is…**
a) Crowded and dim b) Clear, lit and ordered c) Anywhere at all d) Locked
**Answer: b) Clear, lit and ordered**

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Tools should be used…**
a) For any job that comes up b) Only for their purpose, and stored safely c) Until they break d) By whoever grabs them first
**Answer: b) Only for their purpose, and stored safely**

**4. (Sub lesson 3) A finely made project produced unsafely is…**
a) Well made b) Not well made — safety is part of the work's quality c) The teacher's problem d) Acceptable if no one saw
**Answer: b) Not well made — safety is part of the work's quality**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Finishing work (paints, wrappings) has safety considerations about…**
a) Nothing b) Ventilation and care with the finishing materials c) Speed d) Colour choice only
**Answer: b) Ventilation and care with the finishing materials**
