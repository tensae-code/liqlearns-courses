# Working safely with tools and materials

## Do it first
Look at your own workspace right now. Find three safety faults — a tool where it could fall, a blocked working space, a material stored badly — and fix all three before you do anything else.

## Sub lesson 1 — Safety in every task
There is no stage of making without a safety side. Cutting has its safe grip and direction; boring has its clamped work and controlled twist; finishing has its ventilation and care with paints. Jamaica's guide attaches safety considerations to every practical task in the project, so safety is learned as part of each skill — not as a list to recite once and forget.

## Sub lesson 2 — Safe use of workspace, tools and materials
A safe workspace is clear, lit and ordered, with room to work and no trailing hazards. Tools are used only for their purpose, carried and stored safely, and put down where they cannot fall or be grabbed blindly. Materials are handled for what they are — sharp edges, splinters, dust and finishes each respected. Safe and healthy use of all three is the habit this lesson builds.

## Sub lesson 3 — Safety is a making skill
In Jamaica's projects, observing safety considerations is one of the things the work is assessed on, alongside accuracy and finish. That is the professional truth as well: the maker's skill includes coming through the job unhurt, with the workspace, tools and materials respected. Safe habits learned on a toy car scale up to every workshop the learner will ever enter.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: safety is part of every practical task, not a separate lesson: the safe and healthy use of the workspace, the tools and the materials is observed at every stage of making. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.

## Do It Yourself
1. Do the Do it first task again, carefully this time, using everything the lesson taught.
2. Check your work against the lesson's own checks — fix one thing you find.
3. Keep the work: it is practice evidence, and the next lesson builds on it.
