# Lesson plan — Working safely with tools and materials

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Design and Making · **Lesson:** Working safely with tools and materials
**Place in the curriculum:** Design and Making (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Jamaica expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** safety is part of every practical task, not a separate lesson: the safe and healthy use of the workspace, the tools and the materials is observed at every stage of making.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Jamaica's *National Standards Curriculum Guide — Grades 4–6 — Resource & Technology* (Ministry of Education, Youth and Information, Kingston, Jamaica, © 2018), a curriculum guide with full unit teaching content, read COMPLETE (see lesson/sources.md). It counts in the acceptance count (curriculum guide with full teaching content, not evidence-only).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **Safety in every task** — Jamaica's rule is 'in every practical task': each stage of a project carries its own safety considerations, observed while the work happens.
2. **Safe use of workspace, tools and materials** — Three things are kept safe together: where you work, what you work with, and what you work on.
3. **Safety is a making skill** — Working safely is judged like any other part of the work: a project made unsafely is not well made, however fine it looks.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of Design and Making. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State Jamaica's rule that safety considerations belong to every practical task.
2. Describe the safe and healthy use of workspace, tools and materials.
3. Explain why safe working is judged as part of the quality of a project.
## What you need
A real workspace to inspect, and the tools and materials of a current project.
