# Hands-on — Nature of engineering in various fields

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: The Nature of Engineering in Various Fields; Significance of Engineering Field to Society; The Roles and Responsibilities of Engineering Fields; Methods of Controlling Pollution in Engineering Fields. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
