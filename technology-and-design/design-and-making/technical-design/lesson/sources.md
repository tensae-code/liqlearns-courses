| technical design (thiết kế kĩ thuật): the engineers' distinctive creative activity of discovering and solving problems and practical needs by applying mathematics, natural science and current technology; its result is a solution, a technical process, a technology product; the activity's main steps (define the problem; propose, choose and concretise the solution; experiment, evaluate and adjust the solution); the design process must consider resources, environment, economics and humanities, and it trains the designer's overview vision and sharp, multi-dimensional thinking; its two roles — developing products (design activity is usually tied to product development: wholly new products created for the first time, or products developed from the previous version of the same product; designed products are very diverse, from simple to complex, reflecting every aspect of social life, promoting social development and making life ever more convenient) and developing technology (design activity turns understandings of science and engineering into practical technology solutions — production, governed and influenced by economics, politics and society and by artistic and humanistic factors; through design, technologies are improved and develop continuously, the new technology always better with more advantages than the same kind before it); design-related occupations and their character — the occupations demand knowledge of many fields, mainly mathematics, science, technology and art; design problems are very diverse, depending on the social life context; one design problem's product is also very diverse, reflecting the multi-faceted character of design activity; the designer needs spatial thinking and good logic, creative capacity, aesthetic capacity, and skilful use of graphics software supporting design activity; the occupations presented — construction architect, landscape architect, interior designer and decorator, product designer (industrial fine-art designer), fashion designer — each with its main work and workplaces (original string `technical design (thiết kế kĩ thuật)`) | Vietnam, *Công nghệ 10 — Thiết kế và công nghệ*, printed p. 101 |
