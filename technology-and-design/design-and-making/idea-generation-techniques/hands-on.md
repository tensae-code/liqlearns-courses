# Hands-on — Idea generation techniques

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## Run the rules
Brainstorm solutions to one real class problem for five minutes, obeying all four rules. Count the ideas; only then judge them.

## Dig five deep
Take a fault you know — a dripping tap, a dying plant, a late bus. Ask why five times, in writing, until you reach a fixable root cause.
