# Idea generation techniques

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Iran's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Brainstorming under rules
Brainstorming works because of its rules, not in spite of them: say every idea aloud, judge nothing while ideas are flowing, aim for quantity, and build on others' ideas. Criticism during generation kills the unusual ideas — and the unusual idea is often the one that works. Only when the flow stops does the group turn to judging.

## Sub lesson 2 — The five whys
The five-whys technique finds the root cause of a problem. State the problem, ask why it happened, then ask why of that answer, and so on — about five times — until the answer is a cause you can actually fix. Fixing a surface symptom lets the problem return; fixing the root cause ends it. Iran's book teaches both techniques with class activities inside the idea-discovery section of its product-development module.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: ideas are produced by method, not luck: brainstorming gathers many ideas under strict rules, and the five-whys technique digs from a problem down to its root cause. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
