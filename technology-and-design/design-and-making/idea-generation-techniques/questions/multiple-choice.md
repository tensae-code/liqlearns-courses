# Idea generation techniques — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is brainstorming?
a) generating many ideas in a group under rules that ban judgement while ideas flow b) Only a price c) Only decoration d) Nothing to do with this lesson
**Answer: a) generating many ideas in a group under rules that ban judgement while ideas flow**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is five whys?
a) Only history b) asking why about five times to dig from a problem to its root cause c) Only storage d) A kind of glue
**Answer: b) asking why about five times to dig from a problem to its root cause**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is root cause?
a) Only old tools b) Only painting c) the deepest cause of a problem, the one whose fixing ends the problem d) Only a price
**Answer: c) the deepest cause of a problem, the one whose fixing ends the problem**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson teach, in one line?
a) Only decoration b) Nothing to do with this lesson c) Only history d) ideas are produced by method, not luck: brainstorming gathers many ideas under strict rules, and the five-whys technique digs from a problem down to its root cause.
**Answer: d) ideas are produced by method, not luck: brainstorming gathers many ideas under strict rules, and the five-whys technique digs from a problem down to its root cause.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** How does Iran's book teach this?
a) As theory only, never practised b) Only in a glossary c) Only as an exam d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Persian
**Answer: d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Persian**
