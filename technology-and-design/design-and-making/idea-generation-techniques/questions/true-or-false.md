# Idea generation techniques — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Brainstorming is generating many ideas in a group under rules that ban judgement while ideas flow.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Five whys has nothing to do with this lesson.
**Answer: False** — It is part of the lesson: asking why about five times to dig from a problem to its root cause.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Root cause is the deepest cause of a problem, the one whose fixing ends the problem.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.
