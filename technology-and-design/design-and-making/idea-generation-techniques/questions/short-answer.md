# Idea generation techniques — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In your own words, what is brainstorming?
**Answer:** Generating many ideas in a group under rules that ban judgement while ideas flow.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In your own words, what is root cause?
**Answer:** The deepest cause of a problem, the one whose fixing ends the problem.
