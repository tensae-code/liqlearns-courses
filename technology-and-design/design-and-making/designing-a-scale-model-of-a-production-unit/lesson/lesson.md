# Designing a scale model of a production unit

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Greece's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Designing a scale model of a production unit
Greece's book teaches it this way: technical design and model-making skills, choosing tools and materials, solving technological problems while building (*Technologia B Gym*, printed p. 75). The learner works through the named materials and steps in order, saying what each step does before doing the next one.

## Sub lesson 2 — Designing a scale model of a production unit — doing it yourself
Now the learner does the whole task unaided: technical design and model-making skills, choosing tools and materials, solving technological problems while building. Working from the first sub lesson's sequence, the learner plans the make, works through it step by step, and checks the finished piece against what Greece's book shows.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: Technical design and model-making skills, choosing tools and materials, solving technological problems while building. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.

## Do It Yourself
1. Do the Do it first task again, carefully this time, using everything the lesson taught.
2. Check your work against the lesson's own checks — fix one thing you find.
3. Keep your work: it is practice evidence, and the next lesson builds on it.
