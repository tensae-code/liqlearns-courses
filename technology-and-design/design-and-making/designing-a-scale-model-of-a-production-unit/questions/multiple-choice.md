# Designing a scale model of a production unit — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Sub lesson 1 is about…
a) Designing a scale model of a production unit b) Cooking lunch c) A sports match d) The weather

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Sub lesson 2 asks the learner to…

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Greece teaches this topic inside which school book?
