# Planning the work

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Cuba's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — The technological card
Cuba's 1989 Grade 7 book gives planning its own chapter: the carta tecnológica for making an article — the operations in order, the materials and tools each operation needs, and the checks along the way. The card is written before the first cut.

## Sub lesson 2 — Working from the plan
With the card in hand, making becomes following: each operation is done, checked against the card, and ticked off. When something must change, the card is changed first — the plan stays the true record of how the article is made.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: before making comes planning: the technological card (carta tecnológica) sets down what will be made, in what order, with what means — so the work itself can simply follow the plan. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.

## Do It Yourself
1. Do the Do it first task again, carefully this time, using everything the lesson taught.
2. Check your work against the lesson's own checks — fix one thing you find.
3. Keep your work: it is practice evidence, and the next lesson builds on it.
