# Lesson plan — Planning the work

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Design and Making · **Lesson:** Planning the work
**Place in the curriculum:** Design and Making (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Cuba expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** before making comes planning: the technological card (carta tecnológica) sets down what will be made, in what order, with what means — so the work itself can simply follow the plan.
**This lesson's own level:** stocked from Cuba's *Educación Laboral — Trabajos de Taller y Dibujo Básico — 7mo grado (1989 edition)* and the Cuba books in lesson/sources.md — see lesson/sources.md. Cuba counts in the acceptance count for its student-book placements; its programme and methodological-orientations placements are evidence only, counted nowhere.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **The technological card** — Cuba's 1989 Grade 7 book gives planning its own chapter: the carta tecnológica for making an article — the operations in order, the materials and tools each operation needs, and the checks along the way. The card is written before the first cut.
2. **Working from the plan** — With the card in hand, making becomes following: each operation is done, checked against the card, and ticked off. When something must change, the card is changed first — the plan stays the true record of how the article is made.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of Design and Making. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Explain planning the work and its main parts, in their own words.
2. Carry out the lesson's central task unaided.
3. Explain how Cuba's book teaches it.
## What you need
Paper for the card; a simple article to plan; its materials and tools (or pictures).
