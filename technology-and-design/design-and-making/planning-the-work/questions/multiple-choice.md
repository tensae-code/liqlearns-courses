# Planning the work — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is technological card (carta tecnológica)?
a) the written plan of operations for making an article b) Only a price c) Only decoration d) Nothing to do with this lesson
**Answer: a) the written plan of operations for making an article**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is operation?
a) Only history b) one step of work in the making sequence c) Only storage d) A kind of glue
**Answer: b) one step of work in the making sequence**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is sequence?
a) Only old tools b) Only painting c) the order in which operations are done d) Only a price
**Answer: c) the order in which operations are done**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson teach, in one line?
a) Only decoration b) Nothing to do with this lesson c) Only history d) before making comes planning: the technological card (carta tecnológica) sets down what will be made, in what order, with what means — so the work itself can simply follow the plan.
**Answer: d) before making comes planning: the technological card (carta tecnológica) sets down what will be made, in what order, with what means — so the work itself can simply follow the plan.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** How does Cuba's book teach this?
a) As theory only, never practised b) Only in a glossary c) Only as an exam d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Spanish
**Answer: d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Spanish**
