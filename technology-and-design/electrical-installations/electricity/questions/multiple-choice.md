# Electricity — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is conductor?
a) a material that lets electric current pass b) Only a price c) Only decoration d) Nothing to do with this lesson
**Answer: a) a material that lets electric current pass**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is insulator?
a) Only history b) a material that blocks electric current c) Only storage d) A kind of glue
**Answer: b) a material that blocks electric current**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is magnetic effect?
a) Only old tools b) Only painting c) the current's power to make magnetism, as in an electromagnet d) Only a price
**Answer: c) the current's power to make magnetism, as in an electromagnet**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson teach, in one line?
a) Only decoration b) Nothing to do with this lesson c) Only history d) electricity begins with where the current comes from and what it does: generated chemically and magnetically, the current shows itself in heat, light and magnetism — and matter either lets it pass (conductors) or stops it (insulators).
**Answer: d) electricity begins with where the current comes from and what it does: generated chemically and magnetically, the current shows itself in heat, light and magnetism — and matter either lets it pass (conductors) or stops it (insulators).**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** How does Cuba's book teach this?
a) As theory only, never practised b) Only in a glossary c) Only as an exam d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Spanish
**Answer: d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Spanish**
