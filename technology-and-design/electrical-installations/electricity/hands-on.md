# Hands-on — Electricity

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## Sort the materials
Take ten household materials (unplugged, dry). Sort them into 'would conduct' and 'would insulate', and say what each is used for electrically.

## Find the three effects
In your home or school, find one device for each effect of the current: heat, light, magnetism. Name the device and its effect.
