# Choosing electrical appliances

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Kazakhstan's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Choosing electrical goods
Kazakhstan's books teach it this way (the student book, printed p. 62): Choice and evaluation of electrical goods and household техника (§20). The learner works through the named materials and steps in order, saying what each step does before doing the next one.

## Sub lesson 2 — Choosing electrical appliances — doing it yourself
Now the learner does the whole task unaided: choice and evaluation of electrical goods and household техника (§20). Working from the first sub lesson's sequence, the learner plans the make, works through it step by step, and checks the finished piece against what the Kazakh book shows.

## Say it to your companion
