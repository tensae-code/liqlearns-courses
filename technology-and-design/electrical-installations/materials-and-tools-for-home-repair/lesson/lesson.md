# Materials and tools for home repair

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Kazakhstan's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Materials and tools for home repair
Kazakhstan's books teach it this way (the student book, printed p. 64): Modern materials, equipment and tools for repairing a room, with practical work (§21). The learner works through the named materials and steps in order, saying what each step does before doing the next one.

## Sub lesson 2 — Materials and tools for home repair — doing it yourself
Now the learner does the whole task unaided: modern materials, equipment and tools for repairing a room, with practical work (§21). Working from the first sub lesson's sequence, the learner plans the make, works through it step by step, and checks the finished piece against what the Kazakh book shows.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: Materials and tools for home repair — modern materials, equipment and tools for repairing a room, with practical work (§21). Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.

1. Do the Do it first task again, carefully this time, using everything the lesson taught.
