# Making a simple electrical article — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the part's simple made article?
a) Two bulbs coupled in parallel b) A motor c) A relay d) A heater
**Answer: a) Two bulbs coupled in parallel**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is recalled from Grade 8?
a) Soldering b) The series connection c) The electromagnet d) Nothing
**Answer: b) The series connection**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** In parallel, each bulb sits…
a) On its own branch b) On one shared path c) Outside the circuit d) In series
**Answer: a) On its own branch**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** What document orders the making?
a) A technological sheet b) A poster c) A receipt d) A song
**Answer: a) A technological sheet**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Under what rules is the article made?
a) Kitchen rules b) No rules c) The work-protection rules for electrical installation work d) Road rules
**Answer: c) The work-protection rules for electrical installation work**
