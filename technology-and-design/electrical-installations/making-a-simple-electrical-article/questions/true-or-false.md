# Making a simple electrical article — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In a series connection both bulbs share one path.
**Answer: True** — Series is the single-path connection recalled from Grade 8.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Parallel coupling gives each bulb its own branch.
**Answer: True** — That branch structure is the parallel lesson's point.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The technological sheet may be skipped if you feel confident.
**Answer: False** — The article is made by the sheet, under the protection rules.
