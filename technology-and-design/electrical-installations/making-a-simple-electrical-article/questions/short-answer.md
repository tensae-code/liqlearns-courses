# Making a simple electrical article — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How do series and parallel differ for two bulbs?
**Answer:** Series puts both bulbs on one path; parallel gives each bulb its own branch.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What two disciplines govern the making?
**Answer:** The technological sheet (the ordered procedure) and the work-protection rules for installation work.
