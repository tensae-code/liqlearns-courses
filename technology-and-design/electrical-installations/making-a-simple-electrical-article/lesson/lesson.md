# Making a simple electrical article

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Moldova's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Series recalled, parallel made
The make recalls Grade 8's series connection — one path through both bulbs — and builds its contrast: two bulbs coupled in parallel, each on its own branch. The article is simple so the connection itself is the lesson: same bulbs, two architectures, different behaviour.

## Sub lesson 2 — By the technological sheet, under protection rules
The coupling is made from a technological sheet — the ordered written procedure — and under the work-protection rules for electrical installation work. Sheet and rules together make the article a piece of electromontaj, not a experiment of chance.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: a simple electrical article is made by the sheet: two bulbs coupled in parallel — with the series connection recalled from Grade 8 — under the work-protection rules for installation work. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.

## Do It Yourself
1. Do the Do it first task again, carefully this time, using everything the lesson taught.
2. Check your work against the lesson's own checks — fix one thing you find.
3. Keep your work: it is practice evidence, and the next lesson builds on it.
