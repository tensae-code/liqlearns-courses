# Hands-on — Electrical licensing and wiring rules

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: Role of statutory/regulatory body, National Energy Authority Electrical Licensing; Electrical Standards; Wiring Rules (AS/NZS 3000). Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
