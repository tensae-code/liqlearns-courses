# Gas installation safety

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Iran's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — The rules that prevent accidents
Gas installation safety is a rule system, learned whole. Connections must be sound and tested; hoses and fittings are checked and replaced on their cycle; rooms with gas appliances must be ventilated, because gas and its burning both poison the air in a closed space. Iran's Grade 9 module states the rules as the direct prevention of gas accidents in the home.

## Sub lesson 2 — When gas is suspected
The smell of gas is an alarm with its own procedure: no flames, no switches — a spark can ignite the mixture; shut the supply if it can be done safely; open the air paths; leave and raise the alarm. The rules' order matters as much as their content, and the module teaches them as conduct, not commentary.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: gas accidents at home are prevented by rules: sound connections, ventilation, respect for the smell of gas, and knowing exactly what to do — and never do — when gas is suspected. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
