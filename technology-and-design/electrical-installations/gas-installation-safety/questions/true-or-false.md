# Gas installation safety — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Ventilation is the movement of fresh air through a room, required where gas appliances work.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Gas supply shut-off has nothing to do with this lesson.
**Answer: False** — It is part of the lesson: the valve that stops gas entering the home's installation.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Leak is gas escaping from a connection, hose or fitting.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.
