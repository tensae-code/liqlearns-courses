# Gas installation safety — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is ventilation?
a) the movement of fresh air through a room, required where gas appliances work b) Only a price c) Only decoration d) Nothing to do with this lesson
**Answer: a) the movement of fresh air through a room, required where gas appliances work**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is gas supply shut-off?
a) Only history b) the valve that stops gas entering the home's installation c) Only storage d) A kind of glue
**Answer: b) the valve that stops gas entering the home's installation**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is leak?
a) Only old tools b) Only painting c) gas escaping from a connection, hose or fitting d) Only a price
**Answer: c) gas escaping from a connection, hose or fitting**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson teach, in one line?
a) Only decoration b) Nothing to do with this lesson c) Only history d) gas accidents at home are prevented by rules: sound connections, ventilation, respect for the smell of gas, and knowing exactly what to do — and never do — when gas is suspected.
**Answer: d) gas accidents at home are prevented by rules: sound connections, ventilation, respect for the smell of gas, and knowing exactly what to do — and never do — when gas is suspected.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** How does Iran's book teach this?
a) As theory only, never practised b) Only in a glossary c) Only as an exam d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Persian
**Answer: d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Persian**
