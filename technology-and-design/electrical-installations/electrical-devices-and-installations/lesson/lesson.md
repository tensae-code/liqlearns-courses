# Electrical devices and installations

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Cuba's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — The devices
Cuba's 1989 Grade 7 book teaches the installation through its devices: switches, lamp holders (portalámparas), receptacles and plugs, incandescent lamps and fuses — what each does in the circuit and where each belongs.

## Sub lesson 2 — Joining conductors and the electrician's tools
Between the devices run the conductors, and their joins decide whether the installation works and is safe. The electrician's tools for the work are pliers and the knife; splices (empalmes) join conductors so the join carries the current and can be insulated.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: an electrical installation is devices joined correctly: switches, lamp holders, receptacles and plugs, lamps and fuses — and the conductor joins between them, made with the electrician's tools. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
