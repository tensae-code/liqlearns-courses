# Electrical devices and installations — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Lamp holder (portalámpara) is the socket that holds a lamp and connects it to the circuit.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Fuse has nothing to do with this lesson.
**Answer: False** — It is part of the lesson: a safety device that breaks the circuit when current runs too high.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Splice (empalme) is a join between two conductors.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.
