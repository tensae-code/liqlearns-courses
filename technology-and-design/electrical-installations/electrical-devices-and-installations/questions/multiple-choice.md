# Electrical devices and installations — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is lamp holder (portalámpara)?
a) the socket that holds a lamp and connects it to the circuit b) Only a price c) Only decoration d) Nothing to do with this lesson
**Answer: a) the socket that holds a lamp and connects it to the circuit**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is fuse?
a) Only history b) a safety device that breaks the circuit when current runs too high c) Only storage d) A kind of glue
**Answer: b) a safety device that breaks the circuit when current runs too high**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is splice (empalme)?
a) Only old tools b) Only painting c) a join between two conductors d) Only a price
**Answer: c) a join between two conductors**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson teach, in one line?
a) Only decoration b) Nothing to do with this lesson c) Only history d) an electrical installation is devices joined correctly: switches, lamp holders, receptacles and plugs, lamps and fuses — and the conductor joins between them, made with the electrician's tools.
**Answer: d) an electrical installation is devices joined correctly: switches, lamp holders, receptacles and plugs, lamps and fuses — and the conductor joins between them, made with the electrician's tools.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** How does Cuba's book teach this?
a) As theory only, never practised b) Only in a glossary c) Only as an exam d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Spanish
**Answer: d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Spanish**
