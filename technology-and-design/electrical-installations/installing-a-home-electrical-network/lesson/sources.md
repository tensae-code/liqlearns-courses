| installing a home electrical network: the five-step process — (1) study the schematic and determine the relations between the devices and appliances; (2) draw the installation diagram (fix the devices' actual positions; draw the installation diagram); (3) prepare the materials, tools and devices for installation (from the installation diagram, reserve conductors, insulating conduit, devices to install and safety devices); (4) install (run the conductors; install the devices); (5) inspect and test the network's operation (check the firmness of conductor joints and devices; check the insulation at each joint and device so energising is not dangerous; use suitable tools to check for breaks or short circuits; check every device works correctly and safely) — assessed on correct order, correct firm and safe connections, correct function, and safe tidy work; the panel-circuit build (mạch bảng điện) in the same five steps: study the schematic (fuse in series with the socket branch and with the switch branch to the lamp) → draw the installation diagram → prepare (13 × 18 cm plastic board, fuse, switch, socket, lamp and holder, plug, 10 m soft conductor, screws, screwdriver, stripper, insulated pliers) → install (mark the elements' positions on the board and the holes for threading conductors from behind; cut conductors to length for each position and strip the ends; fit the devices on the board and connect the conductors into them) → inspect and test (install and wire to the diagram, firm well-insulated joints, neat arrangement, connect the source and test the circuit with a test pen, trial-run it) (original string `installing a home electrical network`) | Vietnam, *Công nghệ 9 — Mô đun Lắp đặt mạng điện trong nhà*, printed p. 29 |
