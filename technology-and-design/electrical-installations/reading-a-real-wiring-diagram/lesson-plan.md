# Lesson plan — Reading a real wiring diagram

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Electrical Installations and the Home · **Lesson:** Reading a real wiring diagram
**Place in the curriculum:** Electrical Installations and the Home (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Iran expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** a wiring diagram is the circuit's map: it shows how every wire and part connects, and reading it truly — tracing each run — comes before touching a wire.
**This lesson's own level:** stocked from Iran's *Work & Technology, Grade 9* (student book) — see lesson/sources.md. Iran counts in the acceptance count (student books, not evidence-only).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **What the diagram shows** — A real wiring diagram shows the whole installation as connected: the supply, every switch, socket and lamp, and the wire runs joining them.
2. **From diagram to board** — The diagram decides the work before the work begins: which parts are needed, where each sits, and which wire joins what.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of Electrical Installations and the Home. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Explain reading a real wiring diagram and its main parts, in their own words.
2. Carry out the lesson's central task unaided.
3. Explain how Iran's book teaches it.
## What you need
A wiring diagram of a simple lamp circuit; pencil for tracing.
