| the related knowledge for the buzzer project — the four-pin push button and debouncing: the button's structure (a four-pin switch, the pins forming two pairs lengthwise, the two pins of a pair connected to each other; pressing the button connects all the pins together) and its ideal behaviour in a pull-down circuit (pressed, the output signal changes from low to high voltage; released, from high to low), contact bounce (in reality, because the mechanical parts making the button are not ideal, the output signal oscillates continuously between high and low levels for a short time after pressing or releasing — this phenomenon is called bounce noise (bouncing), and if not removed it causes errors in determining the moment the player pressed the button), and the two remedies (in hardware, electronic circuits remove the bounce in the signal — an RC filter circuit, or a flip-flop circuit; in software, add a sufficiently large time delay (about 10–100 ms, larger than the bounce duration) after detecting the signal's state change into the algorithm) (original string `the related knowledge for the buzzer project`) | Vietnam, *Chuyên đề học tập Công nghệ 12 — Công nghệ điện – điện tử*, printed p. 30 |
