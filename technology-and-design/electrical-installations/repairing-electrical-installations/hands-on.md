# Hands-on — Repairing electrical installations

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## Fault-hunt on paper
A lamp does not light. Write the checks in order — lamp, fuse, plug, cable continuity, switch — and say what each check rules out.

## Safety gate
List the safety norms that must be obeyed before any plug is opened. If one norm cannot be met, the repair does not start — say why.
