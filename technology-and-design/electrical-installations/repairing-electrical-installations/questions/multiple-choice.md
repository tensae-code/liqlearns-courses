# Repairing electrical installations — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is continuity?
a) whether a path for the current is unbroken from end to end b) Only a price c) Only decoration d) Nothing to do with this lesson
**Answer: a) whether a path for the current is unbroken from end to end**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is receptacle (toma)?
a) Only history b) the fixed socket into which a plug is inserted c) Only storage d) A kind of glue
**Answer: b) the fixed socket into which a plug is inserted**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is fault?
a) Only old tools b) Only painting c) the break or defect that stops a circuit working d) Only a price
**Answer: c) the break or defect that stops a circuit working**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson teach, in one line?
a) Only decoration b) Nothing to do with this lesson c) Only history d) small electrical repairs are a taught craft: check continuity to find the fault, then repair plugs, receptacles and switches — always inside the safety norms, and never on a live circuit.
**Answer: d) small electrical repairs are a taught craft: check continuity to find the fault, then repair plugs, receptacles and switches — always inside the safety norms, and never on a live circuit.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** How does Cuba's book teach this?
a) As theory only, never practised b) Only in a glossary c) Only as an exam d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Spanish
**Answer: d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Spanish**
