# Lesson plan — Repairing electrical installations

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Electrical Installations and the Home · **Lesson:** Repairing electrical installations
**Place in the curriculum:** Electrical Installations and the Home (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Cuba expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** small electrical repairs are a taught craft: check continuity to find the fault, then repair plugs, receptacles and switches — always inside the safety norms, and never on a live circuit.
**This lesson's own level:** stocked from Cuba's *Programa: Educación Laboral — 8vo grado* and the Cuba books in lesson/sources.md — see lesson/sources.md. Cuba counts in the acceptance count for its student-book placements; its programme and methodological-orientations placements are evidence only, counted nowhere.

## Sub lessons
1. **Finding the fault: continuity** — Repair begins with diagnosis: a continuity check shows whether a cable, a resistance or a fuse still carries the current or is broken inside. Cuba's books teach the check first — on cables, resistances and fuses — before any part is replaced.
2. **Repairing plugs, receptacles and switches** — The repairs themselves are the small ones of home and school: plugs, receptacles (tomas) and switches, opened, checked, reconnected or replaced. The safety norms govern every step, and the 1989 book extends the craft to appliances — the electric iron, lighter and cooker, and fluorescent-lamp faults.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
