# Electricity at home and school

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Cuba's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Sources, the bill and saving
The Grade 8 course opens electricity with where electrical energy comes from, how consumption is read on the notice, and energy-saving plans (planes de ahorro) for home and school. Saving is taught as a plan, not a wish.

## Sub lesson 2 — Conductors, cables and the electrician's tools
Wire and cable are not the same: a cable gathers conductors, each insulated. The electrician's tools are named as a set — pliers, knife, screwdriver, neon tester (probador de neón) and soldering iron — each with its job in installation and repair.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: electricity at home and school is a managed thing: know its sources, read the consumption notice, save energy by plan — and know conductors, cables and insulators, and the electrician's tools, by name. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
