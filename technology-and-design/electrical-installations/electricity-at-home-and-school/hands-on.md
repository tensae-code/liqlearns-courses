# Hands-on — Electricity at home and school

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## Read the notice
Study a real or pictured consumption notice. Find the consumption figure and say what it measures, then list three plan measures that would lower it.

## Wire or cable?
Examine pictured or sample (unplugged) conductors. Sort them: bare wire, insulated wire, cable. For each, say where it is used.
