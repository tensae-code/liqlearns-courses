# Electricity at home and school — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is cable?
a) conductors gathered together, each insulated, in one covering b) Only a price c) Only decoration d) Nothing to do with this lesson
**Answer: a) conductors gathered together, each insulated, in one covering**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is neon tester?
a) Only history b) a small tester whose lamp glows when voltage is present c) Only storage d) A kind of glue
**Answer: b) a small tester whose lamp glows when voltage is present**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is energy-saving plan?
a) Only old tools b) Only painting c) a written set of measures to reduce electricity consumption d) Only a price
**Answer: c) a written set of measures to reduce electricity consumption**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson teach, in one line?
a) Only decoration b) Nothing to do with this lesson c) Only history d) electricity at home and school is a managed thing: know its sources, read the consumption notice, save energy by plan — and know conductors, cables and insulators, and the electrician's tools, by name.
**Answer: d) electricity at home and school is a managed thing: know its sources, read the consumption notice, save energy by plan — and know conductors, cables and insulators, and the electrician's tools, by name.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** How does Cuba's book teach this?
a) As theory only, never practised b) Only in a glossary c) Only as an exam d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Spanish
