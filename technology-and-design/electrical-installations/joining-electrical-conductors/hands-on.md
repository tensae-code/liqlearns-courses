# Hands-on — Joining electrical conductors — splices

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## Choose the splice
For three situations — joining two ends in a box, extending a line that will be pulled, branching to a new lamp — name the right splice and say why.

## Judge a splice
Study pictured splices (or practise on dead, unplugged wire with supervision). For each: does it hold? does it carry? is it insulated? Say what you would redo.
