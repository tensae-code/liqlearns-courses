# Joining electrical conductors — splices — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In your own words, what is pigtail splice (rabo de cochino)?
**Answer:** A splice made by twisting two conductor ends together.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In your own words, what is T-derivation?
**Answer:** A branch splice that joins a new conductor onto a running one.
