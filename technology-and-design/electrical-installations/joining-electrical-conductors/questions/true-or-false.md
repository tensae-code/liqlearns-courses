# Joining electrical conductors — splices — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Pigtail splice (rabo de cochino) is a splice made by twisting two conductor ends together.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Western union splice has nothing to do with this lesson.
**Answer: False** — It is part of the lesson: an in-line splice wound for strength where the wire may be pulled.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** T-derivation is a branch splice that joins a new conductor onto a running one.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.
