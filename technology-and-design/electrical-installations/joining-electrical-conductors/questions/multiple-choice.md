# Joining electrical conductors — splices — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is pigtail splice (rabo de cochino)?
a) a splice made by twisting two conductor ends together b) Only a price c) Only decoration d) Nothing to do with this lesson
**Answer: a) a splice made by twisting two conductor ends together**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is Western Union splice?
a) Only history b) an in-line splice wound for strength where the wire may be pulled c) Only storage d) A kind of glue
**Answer: b) an in-line splice wound for strength where the wire may be pulled**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is T-derivation?
a) Only old tools b) Only painting c) a branch splice that joins a new conductor onto a running one d) Only a price
**Answer: c) a branch splice that joins a new conductor onto a running one**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson teach, in one line?
a) Only decoration b) Nothing to do with this lesson c) Only history d) a splice is a joint that must act like unbroken wire: the pigtail (rabo de cochino), the Western Union and the T-derivation each join conductors for a different situation — and every splice is finished by insulating it.
**Answer: d) a splice is a joint that must act like unbroken wire: the pigtail (rabo de cochino), the Western Union and the T-derivation each join conductors for a different situation — and every splice is finished by insulating it.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** How does Cuba's book teach this?
a) As theory only, never practised b) Only in a glossary c) Only as an exam d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Spanish
**Answer: d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Spanish**
