# Circuit testing and verification

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Papua New Guinea's guide teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Circuit testing and verification
Papua New Guinea's guides teach it this way (the teacher guide — evidence, printed p. 114): Testing Safety; Testing Principles; Testing Devices; Verification and Compliance; Fault Finding and Performance Testing. The learner works through the named materials and steps in order, saying what each step does before doing the next one.

## Sub lesson 2 — Circuit testing and verification — doing it yourself
Now the learner does the whole task unaided: Testing Safety; Testing Principles; Testing Devices; Verification and Compliance; Fault Finding and Performance Testing. Working from the first sub lesson's sequence, the learner plans the make, works through it step by step, and checks the finished piece against what the PNG guide shows.

## Say it to your companion
