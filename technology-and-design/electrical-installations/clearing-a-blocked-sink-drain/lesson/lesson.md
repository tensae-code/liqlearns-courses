# Clearing a blocked sink drain

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Iran's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Clearing the blockage
A sink blocks where grease, food and hair gather — usually in the trap under the sink. Clearing proceeds in order: remove what can be reached, work the trap (catching its water in a bowl first), and clear the run beyond. Iran's Grade 9 module teaches the job as a home repair in its own right, inside the Mechanical Installations sequence.

## Sub lesson 2 — Chemical safety
Drain chemicals are strong enough to dissolve blockages — and to burn skin, eyes and lungs. Their rules are part of the lesson: read the label, protect hands and eyes, ventilate, never mix chemicals, and keep them from children. A clear drain bought with a chemical injury is a failed job: safety is a step of the method, not a warning beside it.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: a blocked sink drain is cleared by working through the blockage in order — mechanical methods first — with chemical safety governing every chemical used. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
