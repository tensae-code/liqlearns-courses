# Clearing a blocked sink drain — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is trap?
a) the bent pipe under a sink that holds water to seal the drain and catches blockages b) Only a price c) Only decoration d) Nothing to do with this lesson
**Answer: a) the bent pipe under a sink that holds water to seal the drain and catches blockages**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is blockage?
a) Only history b) the gathered material stopping a drain's flow c) Only storage d) A kind of glue
**Answer: b) the gathered material stopping a drain's flow**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is chemical safety?
a) Only old tools b) Only painting c) the rules for using strong cleaning chemicals without injury d) Only a price
**Answer: c) the rules for using strong cleaning chemicals without injury**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson teach, in one line?
a) Only decoration b) Nothing to do with this lesson c) Only history d) a blocked sink drain is cleared by working through the blockage in order — mechanical methods first — with chemical safety governing every chemical used.
**Answer: d) a blocked sink drain is cleared by working through the blockage in order — mechanical methods first — with chemical safety governing every chemical used.**
