| choosing the devices and tools for installation: switching and taking devices are chosen by rated current and rated voltage — a switching device's rated current is computed from the load's rated current, not smaller than the circuit's calculated current, usually 130% of it to cover starting currents (e.g. calculated 10 A → choose a 13 A aptomat, or the next standard rating above and nearest to 13 A); taking devices per TCVN 9206:2012 (5 A for lighting appliances and fans; 40 A for high-power appliances; 20 A for sockets; sockets and plugs of rated 6.3 A or 10 A can serve loads of small current), with plug and socket compatible in shape, size and safety standard; the tools — measuring tools (electricity meter for consumption, clamp meter for checking current, multimeter for checking voltage and for finding breaks and shorts in resistance mode with the circuit fully disconnected) and installation tools, which must ensure electrical safety (insulated pliers, screwdriver, test pen, wire stripper — e.g. an insulated-handle wrench for tightening bolts so the hand never touches live metal; a stripper that removes insulation easily without damaging the conductor core) (original string `choosing the devices and tools for installation`) | Vietnam, *Công nghệ 9 — Mô đun Lắp đặt mạng điện trong nhà*, printed p. 20 |
