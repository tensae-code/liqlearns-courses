# Practical wiring — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Two-way switch is one of a pair of switches commanding one lamp from two places.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Traveller wires has nothing to do with this lesson.
**Answer: False** — It is part of the lesson: the wires joining a two-way switch pair.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Terminal is the prepared end where a wire connects to a part.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.
