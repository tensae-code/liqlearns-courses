# Practical wiring — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is two-way switch?
a) one of a pair of switches commanding one lamp from two places b) Only a price c) Only decoration d) Nothing to do with this lesson
**Answer: a) one of a pair of switches commanding one lamp from two places**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is traveller wires?
a) Only history b) the wires joining a two-way switch pair c) Only storage d) A kind of glue
**Answer: b) the wires joining a two-way switch pair**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is terminal?
a) Only old tools b) Only painting c) the prepared end where a wire connects to a part d) Only a price
**Answer: c) the prepared end where a wire connects to a part**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson teach, in one line?
a) Only decoration b) Nothing to do with this lesson c) Only history d) practical wiring proves the procedure on a full board: a two-way (staircase) switch, a socket and a lamp — wired to the national building rules for connections, terminals and wire sizes.
**Answer: d) practical wiring proves the procedure on a full board: a two-way (staircase) switch, a socket and a lamp — wired to the national building rules for connections, terminals and wire sizes.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** How does Iran's book teach this?
a) As theory only, never practised b) Only in a glossary c) Only as an exam d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Persian
**Answer: d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Persian**
