# Practical wiring

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Iran's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — The two-way staircase circuit
A two-way switch circuit commands one lamp from two places — the classic staircase case, a switch at each end of the stairs. Two switches, joined by traveller wires, share control: either switch changes the lamp's state. It is the one-way circuit grown up, and the class work of Iran's Grade 9 Electricity module.

## Sub lesson 2 — Socket, lamp and the national rules
The practice board also carries a socket and a lamp, so the board holds a small real installation. Over the work stand the national building rules for connections: how terminals are made and which wire sizes each circuit demands. Practical wiring is where craft meets regulation — the rules are part of the skill, not paperwork after it.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: practical wiring proves the procedure on a full board: a two-way (staircase) switch, a socket and a lamp — wired to the national building rules for connections, terminals and wire sizes. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
