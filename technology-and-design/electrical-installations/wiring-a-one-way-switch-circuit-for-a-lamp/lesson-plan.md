# Lesson plan — Wiring a one-way switch circuit for a lamp

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Electrical Installations and the Home · **Lesson:** Wiring a one-way switch circuit for a lamp
**Place in the curriculum:** Electrical Installations and the Home (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Iran expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the one-way switch circuit is wiring's first complete job: supply, one switch, one lamp — connected so the switch, and only the switch, commands the light.
**This lesson's own level:** stocked from Iran's *Work & Technology, Grade 9* (student book) — see lesson/sources.md. Iran counts in the acceptance count (student books, not evidence-only).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **The circuit and its parts** — The circuit joins three things: the electrical supply, a one-way switch, and a lamp.
2. **Wiring it** — Iran's Grade 9 Electricity module wires this circuit as a set practical: parts laid out from the diagram, fixed, and connected in order, then tested.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of Electrical Installations and the Home. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Explain wiring a one-way switch circuit for a lamp and its main parts, in their own words.
2. Carry out the lesson's central task unaided.
3. Explain how Iran's book teaches it.
## What you need
Practice board, one-way switch, lamp holder and lamp, wire and tools — work only on the class practice supply, with the teacher.
