# Wiring a one-way switch circuit for a lamp

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Iran's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — The circuit and its parts
The circuit joins three things: the electrical supply, a one-way switch, and a lamp. The switch sits in the live run so that opening it truly cuts the lamp's power — a lamp wired so its switch interrupts the wrong conductor can stay live while dark, which is a danger, not a detail.

## Sub lesson 2 — Wiring it
Iran's Grade 9 Electricity module wires this circuit as a set practical: parts laid out from the diagram, fixed, and connected in order, then tested. One switch, one lamp, complete control — the pattern every larger installation is built from, including the two-way staircase circuit of the next lessons.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: the one-way switch circuit is wiring's first complete job: supply, one switch, one lamp — connected so the switch, and only the switch, commands the light. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
