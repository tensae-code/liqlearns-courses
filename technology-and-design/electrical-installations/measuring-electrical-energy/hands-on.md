# Hands-on — Measuring electrical energy — the meter and the bill

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## Read a meter
From a real meter (looked at, not touched) or a clear picture, read the consumption figure and write it down with today's date. Read it again later and find the difference.

## Compare two bills
With two pictured or real bills from different months, find: which month used more, by how much, and one likely reason.
