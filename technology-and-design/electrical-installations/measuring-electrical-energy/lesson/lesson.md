# Measuring electrical energy — the meter and the bill

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Cuba's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — The meter
Cuba's Grade 8 book gives measuring its own section (§3.3): the meter (contador) records the electrical energy a home or school uses, accumulating the count that everything else is billed from.

## Sub lesson 2 — The consumption bill
The bill turns the meter's count into money and information: what period, what consumption, what price. Reading it well — comparing one bill with the last — is how a household sees whether its saving plan is working.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: electrical energy is measured, and the measurement is the bill: the meter counts the consumption, and reading the meter and the consumption bill tells a household what its electricity costs and where it goes. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
