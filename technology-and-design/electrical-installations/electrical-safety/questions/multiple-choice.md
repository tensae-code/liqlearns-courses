# Electrical safety — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Where does the dangers lesson stand in Rwanda's unit?
a) First b) After tools, materials and the circuit c) In another book d) It is not taught
**Answer: b) After tools, materials and the circuit**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Why do the earlier cares exist — right tool, insulation, correct circuit?
a) For marks b) Because of the dangers of electricity c) For speed d) For beauty
**Answer: b) Because of the dangers of electricity**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** How should you treat a conductor?
a) As dead until it sparks b) As live until you know it is not c) As a toy d) As insulation
**Answer: b) As live until you know it is not**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** What does staying safe include?
a) Guessing quickly b) Respecting insulation and never working guessing c) Touching bare wires d) Ignoring rules
**Answer: b) Respecting insulation and never working guessing**

**5. (Sub lesson 1)** Where does this safety knowledge apply?
a) Only in the exam b) At school and at home, in every later use of electricity c) Nowhere d) Only for teachers
**Answer: b) At school and at home, in every later use of electricity**
