# Electrical safety — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The dangers lesson explains why the unit's earlier cares exist.
**Answer: True** — Right tool, insulation and correct circuit are all answers to electricity's dangers.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A conductor can be assumed dead if it looks quiet.
**Answer: False** — Treat every conductor as live until you know it is not.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Electrical safety is only for the classroom.
**Answer: False** — It is knowledge carried into every later use of electricity, at school and at home.
