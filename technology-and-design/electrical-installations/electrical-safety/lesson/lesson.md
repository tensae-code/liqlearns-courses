# Electrical safety

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Rwanda's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — The dangers of electricity
After the tools, the materials and the circuit itself, Rwanda's Primary 5 unit names the dangers of electricity. The dangers are not decoration on the lesson — they are why every earlier care (right tool, insulation, correct circuit) exists.

## Sub lesson 2 — Staying safe with electricity
Staying safe is the matching half: respect insulation, never work guessing, keep the circuit's rules, and treat every conductor as live until you know it is not. Safety is taught as knowledge you carry into every later use of electricity, at school and at home.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: electricity is a servant that punishes carelessness: Rwanda's unit ends by naming its dangers plainly and teaching how to stay safe with it. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
