# Lesson plan — Technological machines and household appliances

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Electrical Installations and the Home · **Lesson:** Technological machines and household appliances
**Place in the curriculum:** Electrical Installations and the Home (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Azerbaijan expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The home is full of technological machines — бытовая техника — each one doing a job: washing, cooling, cleaning, cooking or lighting.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on a student textbook — Azerbaijan's *Технология (Technology), Grade 2*, Russian edition as held (see lesson/sources.md). It counts in the acceptance count (student book, not evidence-only).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **Machines that do work in the home** — Technological machines and household appliances (бытовая техника) are the devices that do work in the home: they wash clothes and dishes, keep food cold, clean floors, cook food and light rooms.
2. **What each appliance does** — The learner matches appliance to job: the washing machine washes, the refrigerator cools and preserves food, the vacuum cleaner cleans, the stove cooks.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
