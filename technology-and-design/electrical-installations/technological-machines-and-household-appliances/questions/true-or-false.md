# Technological machines and household appliances — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Household appliances do no real work.
**Answer: False** — They wash, cool, clean, cook and light — real household work.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A stove's job is cooking food.
**Answer: True** — Cooking is the stove's household job.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Бытовая техника means paper folding.
**Answer: False** — It is the book's term for technological machines and household appliances.
