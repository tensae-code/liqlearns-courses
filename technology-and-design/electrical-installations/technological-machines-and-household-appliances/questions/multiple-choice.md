# Technological machines and household appliances — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What are household appliances?
a) Technological machines that do work in the home b) Garden plants c) Paper toys d) Sewing stitches
**Answer: a) Technological machines that do work in the home**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the Russian term used in the book for this technology?
a) Бытовая техника b) Оригами c) Мозаика d) Лепка
**Answer: a) Бытовая техника**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What does a refrigerator do?
a) Cools and preserves food b) Washes clothes c) Cooks soup d) Cleans floors
**Answer: a) Cools and preserves food**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which appliance washes clothes?
a) The washing machine b) The stove c) The lamp d) The vacuum cleaner
**Answer: a) The washing machine**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** How is each appliance learned in this lesson?
a) By the job it does in the home b) By its colour c) By its price d) By its size only
**Answer: a) By the job it does in the home**
