# Technological machines and household appliances — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name three household appliances and the job of each.
**Answer:** Any three of: washing machine — washes clothes; refrigerator — cools and preserves food; vacuum cleaner — cleans; stove — cooks; lamp — lights the room.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why does the lesson teach appliances by their jobs?
**Answer:** Because each technological machine was made to take over one household job — knowing the job is knowing the machine.
