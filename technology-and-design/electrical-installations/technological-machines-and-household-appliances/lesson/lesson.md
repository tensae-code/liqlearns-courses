# Technological machines and household appliances

## Do it first
Walk through a home in your mind. Count the machines working there — in the kitchen, the laundry, the living room. Then learn what this book calls them and what each one does.

## Sub lesson 1 — Machines that do work in the home
Technological machines and household appliances (бытовая техника) are the devices that do work in the home: they wash clothes and dishes, keep food cold, clean floors, cook food and light rooms. Each appliance was made to take over one household job.

## Sub lesson 2 — What each appliance does
The learner matches appliance to job: the washing machine washes, the refrigerator cools and preserves food, the vacuum cleaner cleans, the stove cooks. Naming the job each machine does — and using each one safely, with adults — is the lesson's test.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: household appliances are technological machines, each made to do one job in the home — washing, cooling, cleaning, cooking or lighting. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
