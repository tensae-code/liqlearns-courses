# Mechanical installations in the home — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is mechanical installations?
a) the home's water, drainage, heating and cooling systems b) Only a price c) Only decoration d) Nothing to do with this lesson
**Answer: a) the home's water, drainage, heating and cooling systems**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is water supply?
a) Only history b) the system bringing clean water under pressure to the taps c) Only storage d) A kind of glue
**Answer: b) the system bringing clean water under pressure to the taps**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is drainage?
a) Only old tools b) Only painting c) the system carrying used water away from the home d) Only a price
**Answer: c) the system carrying used water away from the home**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson teach, in one line?
a) Only decoration b) Nothing to do with this lesson c) Only history d) a home's mechanical installations — water supply, drainage, heating and cooling — are systems to maintain and repair, and the module opens by knowing their tools and materials.
**Answer: d) a home's mechanical installations — water supply, drainage, heating and cooling — are systems to maintain and repair, and the module opens by knowing their tools and materials.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** How does Iran's book teach this?
a) As theory only, never practised b) Only in a glossary c) Only as an exam d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Persian
**Answer: d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Persian**
