# Mechanical installations in the home

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Iran's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — The home's systems
Four systems serve the house. Water supply brings clean water under pressure to every tap. Drainage carries used water away, sealed against smells and leaks. Heating warms the rooms; cooling — in Iran, characteristically the evaporative cooler — cools them. Iran's Grade 9 Mechanical Installations module opens with this survey: the systems named as systems, before any single repair.

## Sub lesson 2 — Tools and materials of maintenance
Each system has its tools and materials: wrenches and sealants for water, rods and chemicals for drains, the service parts for heating and cooling. The module teaches the kit with the systems, because maintenance begins by knowing what the work needs — the lessons that follow (taps, drains, flush tanks, the cooler, gas safety) each draw on this opening.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: a home's mechanical installations — water supply, drainage, heating and cooling — are systems to maintain and repair, and the module opens by knowing their tools and materials. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
