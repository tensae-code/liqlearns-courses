| Lesson part | Where it comes from |
|---|---|
| designing the home network's diagrams: the schematic in two steps — (1) define the design task (the electrical appliances' characteristics; the network's scope: number of rooms and areas to supply; estimate the total load power from the appliances and devices, hence the number and kind of sockets needed); (2) determine the devices, appliances and the relations between them (the quantity of each device; draw the devices and the connecting lines expressing the electrical relations; conventions — the supply circuit is usually drawn horizontal, switching/protective/taking devices are placed on the left of the appliance, switches are drawn in the open state); the installation diagram in three steps — (1) study the schematic (describe the quantity and connection of the devices); (2) fix the actual installation positions (draw the device symbols at their true positions; place the main board, branch boards, power sockets and switches in each area for convenience and appearance; the connection system and conductors from the distribution board to the sockets and devices); (3) draw the installation diagram (draw the source conductors; draw the conductors connecting the devices and appliances following the schematic and the chosen wiring method; result: the drawing with the devices' true positions); worked example — from a schematic to an installation drawing with the source 3 m above the ground, the switch 1.5 m above the floor (original string `designing the home network's diagrams`) | Vietnam, *Công nghệ 9 — Mô đun Lắp đặt mạng điện trong nhà*, printed p. 15 |
| drawing the home system's diagrams: the schematic diagram (showing how the devices work and connect, without exact positions or distances) drawn in three steps — fix the purpose and requirements and identify the elements and their symbols; analyse the connections between the elements; draw the schematic — worked through for a one-room home system (220 V supply through phase and neutral to the outdoor board, through the main breaker and meter, then branch breakers feeding each room's lights, air conditioner and sockets); and the installation diagram (showing the positions for construction, fault-finding and repair) drawn in three steps — from the schematic and the devices' positions, list the numbers of switching, protection and supply elements and their symbols; fix the installation positions of the main board, branch boards and loads and the positions of panels, switches and control devices; draw the source lines and the connecting runs neatly and legibly — worked through for a living room (original string `drawing the home system's diagrams`) | Vietnam, *Công nghệ 12 — Công nghệ điện – điện tử*, printed p. 41 |
