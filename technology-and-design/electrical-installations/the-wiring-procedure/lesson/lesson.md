# The wiring procedure

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Iran's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Lay out, fix, connect
The procedure has three stages in fixed order. First, lay out the parts from the diagram — every part in its place, checked against the drawing. Second, fix them in place, firmly and finally. Third, connect them, wire by wire, following the diagram. Iran's Grade 9 module fixes this order because each stage protects the next: layout errors are free, fixing errors are awkward, connection errors are dangerous.

## Sub lesson 2 — Colours that trace faults
Wires are connected in different colours by rule, each colour keeping one job through the whole installation. When a fault comes — and faults come — the colours let the fault be traced: follow the colour, find the break. Colour discipline at wiring time is fault-finding done in advance.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: all wiring follows one procedure: lay out the parts from the diagram, fix them in place, then connect them — with different wire colours so faults are easy to trace. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
