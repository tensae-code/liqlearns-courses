# The wiring procedure — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In your own words, what is wiring procedure?
**Answer:** The fixed order of wiring: lay out the parts, fix them, then connect them.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In your own words, what is fault tracing?
**Answer:** Finding a wiring fault by following the runs and colours.
