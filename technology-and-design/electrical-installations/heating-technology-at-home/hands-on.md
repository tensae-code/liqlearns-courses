# Hands-on — Heating technology at home

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## Trace the circle
Draw a house with a boiler, pipes, two radiators and a thermostat. Draw arrows showing the hot water's full circle, out hot and back cool, and label the thermostat's job in one sentence.

## Heating helpers
Match the problem to the worker: a leaking pipe, a furnace that will not start, a room that is always too cold. Say whether a plumber or a furnace technician handles each, and why.
