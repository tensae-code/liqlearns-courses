# Heating technology at home — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Radiators give the hot water's heat to the room's air.
**Answer: True** — That giving-off of heat is the radiator's job.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A thermostat burns fuel to make heat.
**Answer: False** — The furnace burns the fuel; the thermostat only controls how much heated water is pumped.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Plumbers and furnace technicians keep heating systems running.
**Answer: True** — Both are named as the workers who keep home heating running.
