# Heating technology at home — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Tell the heat's journey from furnace to room in one or two sentences.
**Answer:** The boiler furnace burns fuel to heat water; the hot water is pumped through pipes to radiators around the rooms, which give the heat to the rooms' air.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the thermostat control, and why does that matter?
**Answer:** It controls how much heated water is pumped, so the home holds a steady comfortable temperature.
