# Heating technology at home — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does a boiler furnace burn fuel to heat?
a) Water b) Air only c) Snow d) Clothing
**Answer: a) Water**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** How does the hot water reach the rooms?
a) It is pumped through pipes to radiators b) It flies c) People carry it in buckets d) It does not travel
**Answer: a) It is pumped through pipes to radiators**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What does a thermostat do?
a) Controls how much heated water is pumped b) Burns the fuel c) Makes the water d) Paints the radiators
**Answer: a) Controls how much heated water is pumped**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** How do a Coleman stove and a qulliq heat?
a) By burning fuel, the same basic way b) With electricity only c) They do not heat d) With cold air
**Answer: a) By burning fuel, the same basic way**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Who looks after the furnace itself?
a) A furnace technician b) A software engineer c) A pilot d) Nobody
**Answer: a) A furnace technician**
