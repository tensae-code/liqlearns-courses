# Lesson plan — Servicing an evaporative (water) cooler

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Electrical Installations and the Home · **Lesson:** Servicing an evaporative (water) cooler
**Place in the curriculum:** Electrical Installations and the Home (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Iran expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the evaporative (water) cooler cools by passing air through wet pads — and its periodic service, done with electrical safety, keeps it cooling season after season.
**This lesson's own level:** stocked from Iran's *Work & Technology, Grade 9* (student book) — see lesson/sources.md. Iran counts in the acceptance count (student books, not evidence-only).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **Parts of the cooler** — The cooler's parts work one chain: a reservoir and pump lift water to the pads; the pads hold the water across the air's path; a fan draws outside air through the wet pads, where evaporation cools it, and drives the cooled air into the house.
2. **The periodic service** — Service is periodic — done on a cycle, before failure: clean the reservoir and pads of scale and dirt, check the pump and water distribution, check belt, bearings and motor, and test the whole run.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
