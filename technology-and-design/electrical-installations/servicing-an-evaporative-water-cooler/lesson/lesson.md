# Servicing an evaporative (water) cooler

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Iran's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Parts of the cooler
The cooler's parts work one chain: a reservoir and pump lift water to the pads; the pads hold the water across the air's path; a fan draws outside air through the wet pads, where evaporation cools it, and drives the cooled air into the house. Iran's Grade 9 module names the parts first — service without part-knowledge is guesswork.

## Sub lesson 2 — The periodic service
Service is periodic — done on a cycle, before failure: clean the reservoir and pads of scale and dirt, check the pump and water distribution, check belt, bearings and motor, and test the whole run. Because water and electricity meet in this machine, electrical safety governs the service: isolate before opening, keep connections dry, restore covers before testing. The module teaches the service steps and the safety as one job.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: the evaporative (water) cooler cools by passing air through wet pads — and its periodic service, done with electrical safety, keeps it cooling season after season. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
