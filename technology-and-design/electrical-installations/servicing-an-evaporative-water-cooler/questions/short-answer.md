# Servicing an evaporative (water) cooler — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In your own words, what is evaporative cooler?
**Answer:** The cooler that cools air by passing it through wet pads.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In your own words, what is periodic service?
**Answer:** Maintenance done on a regular cycle, before failure.
