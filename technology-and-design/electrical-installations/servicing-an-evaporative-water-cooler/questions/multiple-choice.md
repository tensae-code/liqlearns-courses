# Servicing an evaporative (water) cooler — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is evaporative cooler?
a) the cooler that cools air by passing it through wet pads b) Only a price c) Only decoration d) Nothing to do with this lesson
**Answer: a) the cooler that cools air by passing it through wet pads**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is pads?
a) Only history b) the water-holding mats across the air's path where evaporation cools it c) Only storage d) A kind of glue
**Answer: b) the water-holding mats across the air's path where evaporation cools it**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is periodic service?
a) Only old tools b) Only painting c) maintenance done on a regular cycle, before failure d) Only a price
**Answer: c) maintenance done on a regular cycle, before failure**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson teach, in one line?
a) Only decoration b) Nothing to do with this lesson c) Only history d) the evaporative (water) cooler cools by passing air through wet pads — and its periodic service, done with electrical safety, keeps it cooling season after season.
**Answer: d) the evaporative (water) cooler cools by passing air through wet pads — and its periodic service, done with electrical safety, keeps it cooling season after season.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** How does Iran's book teach this?
a) As theory only, never practised b) Only in a glossary c) Only as an exam d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Persian
**Answer: d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Persian**
