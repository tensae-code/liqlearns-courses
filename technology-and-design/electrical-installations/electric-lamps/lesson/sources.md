| the incandescent lamp: structure — glass bulb, filament (sợi đốt) and base (đuôi đèn, screw or pin type); when it works, the current in the filament heats it to a very high temperature and it glows; typical specifications (220 V – 25/60/100 W); Edison's first practical bulb, January 1879 (original string `the incandescent lamp`) | Vietnam, *Công nghệ 6*, printed p. 61 |
| fluorescent and compact lamps: the fluorescent tube's structure — a glass tube coated inside with phosphor powder and two electrodes; in operation, the discharge between the electrodes acts on the phosphor layer and makes it emit light; the compact lamp is a low-power fluorescent lamp whose tube is U-shaped or spiral, working on the same principle (original string `fluorescent and compact lamps`) | Vietnam, *Công nghệ 6*, printed p. 61 |
| the LED lamp: structure — outer shell, LED circuit board (bảng mạch LED) and base; the LED board emits the light and the shell spreads it evenly; LED stands for Light Emitting Diode, an electronic component that emits light when current passes through it; light output is measured in lumens (lm); comparing lamp kinds at equal brightness — 220 lm needs 25 W incandescent, 6 W compact or 3 W LED, and LED lamps save the most electricity (original string `the LED lamp`) | Vietnam, *Công nghệ 6*, printed p. 62 |
