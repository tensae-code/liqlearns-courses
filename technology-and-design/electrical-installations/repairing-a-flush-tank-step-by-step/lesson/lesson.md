# Repairing a flush tank, step by step

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Iran's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — How the tank works
The flush tank stores water and releases it in one rush. A float valve fills it and stops at the level; the flush mechanism — handle, lift and seal — releases the water and seals again. Most faults live in these two systems: a tank that runs forever has a sealing or float fault; a tank that will not fill has a supply or valve fault. The symptom names the system to open.

## Sub lesson 2 — The repair sequence
Iran's Grade 9 module teaches the repair as steps: isolate the water, empty and open the tank, inspect float and seal, replace the worn part or adjust the level, refill and test several flushes. Step-by-step is the craft's protection — no step skipped, no part forced, the tank proven before the tools are put away.

## Say it to your companion
