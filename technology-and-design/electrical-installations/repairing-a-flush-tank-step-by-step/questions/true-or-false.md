# Repairing a flush tank, step by step — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Flush tank is the tank that stores water and releases it in one rush to flush.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Float valve has nothing to do with this lesson.
**Answer: False** — It is part of the lesson: the valve that fills the tank and stops the water at its level.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Seal is the part that closes the flush outlet so the tank holds its water.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.
