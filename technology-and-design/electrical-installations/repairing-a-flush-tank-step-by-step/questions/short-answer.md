# Repairing a flush tank, step by step — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In your own words, what is flush tank?
**Answer:** The tank that stores water and releases it in one rush to flush.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In your own words, what is seal?
**Answer:** The part that closes the flush outlet so the tank holds its water.
