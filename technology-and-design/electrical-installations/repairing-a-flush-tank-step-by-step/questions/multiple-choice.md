# Repairing a flush tank, step by step — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is flush tank?
a) the tank that stores water and releases it in one rush to flush b) Only a price c) Only decoration d) Nothing to do with this lesson
**Answer: a) the tank that stores water and releases it in one rush to flush**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is float valve?
a) Only history b) the valve that fills the tank and stops the water at its level c) Only storage d) A kind of glue
**Answer: b) the valve that fills the tank and stops the water at its level**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is seal?

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson teach, in one line?
