# Electrical installation work (electromontaj) — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is electromontaj?
a) Electrical installation work b) Plumbing c) Carpentry d) Painting
**Answer: a) Electrical installation work**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which components are studied first?
a) Bulbs only b) The electromagnet, the electric motor and starter relays c) Switches only d) None — straight to wiring
**Answer: b) The electromagnet, the electric motor and starter relays**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Why study how each component works?
a) Because installation and fault-finding stand on that understanding b) For an exam only c) It is not needed d) To delay work
**Answer: a) Because installation and fault-finding stand on that understanding**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** What work does the lesson aim at?
a) Designing and executing installations, and finding and fixing simple faults b) Only selling appliances c) Only drawing d) Only cleaning
**Answer: a) Designing and executing installations, and finding and fixing simple faults**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Where are simple faults fixed?
a) In furniture b) In books c) In appliances and networks d) Nowhere — faults are ignored
**Answer: c) In appliances and networks**
