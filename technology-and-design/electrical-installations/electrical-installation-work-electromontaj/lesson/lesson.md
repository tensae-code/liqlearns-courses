# Electrical installation work (electromontaj)

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Moldova's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — The components — electromagnet, motor, relays
Grade 9 electromontaj begins inside the devices: the electromagnet, the electric motor, and starter relays — what each is and how each works. Installation craft is not wire-joining alone; it stands on understanding the components the installation connects.

## Sub lesson 2 — Designing, executing, and fixing faults
The components serve the whole trade: designing and executing electrical installation work, and finding and fixing simple faults in appliances and networks. A fault is found by someone who knows how the parts should work — diagnosis is component knowledge at work.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: electromontaj — electrical installation work — is built on components: study the electromagnet, the electric motor and starter relays and how each works, and you can design and execute installations and find and fix simple faults. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
