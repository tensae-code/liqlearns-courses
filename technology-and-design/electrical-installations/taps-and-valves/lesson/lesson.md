# Taps and valves

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Iran's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Stop valve and draw-off tap
Two fittings govern the water. The stop valve (شیر فلکه) sits on the line and shuts off the supply to a whole section — the first thing to find before any repair. The draw-off tap is the outlet fitting where water is drawn. Knowing which fitting does which job is the start of every water repair in the module.

## Sub lesson 2 — Fixing a dripping tap
A drip is a worn seal wasting water drop by drop. The fix proceeds step by step: shut the stop valve, open the tap to drain, dismantle in order, replace the worn washer or seal, reassemble and test. Iran's module teaches the sequence for the ordinary tap and for the lever mixer tap alike — the modern fitting fixed by the same ordered method.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: water is commanded by taps and valves — the stop valve (شیر فلکه) that shuts a whole line and the draw-off tap at the outlet — and a dripping tap, even a lever mixer, is fixed step by step. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
