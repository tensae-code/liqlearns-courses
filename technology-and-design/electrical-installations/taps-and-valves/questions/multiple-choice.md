# Taps and valves — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is stop valve (شیر فلکه)?
a) the valve that shuts off water to a whole line or section b) Only a price c) Only decoration d) Nothing to do with this lesson
**Answer: a) the valve that shuts off water to a whole line or section**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is draw-off tap?
a) Only history b) the outlet tap where water is drawn c) Only storage d) A kind of glue
**Answer: b) the outlet tap where water is drawn**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is washer?
a) Only old tools b) Only painting c) the small seal inside a tap whose wearing causes dripping d) Only a price
**Answer: c) the small seal inside a tap whose wearing causes dripping**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson teach, in one line?
a) Only decoration b) Nothing to do with this lesson c) Only history d) water is commanded by taps and valves — the stop valve (شیر فلکه) that shuts a whole line and the draw-off tap at the outlet — and a dripping tap, even a lever mixer, is fixed step by step.
**Answer: d) water is commanded by taps and valves — the stop valve (شیر فلکه) that shuts a whole line and the draw-off tap at the outlet — and a dripping tap, even a lever mixer, is fixed step by step.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** How does Iran's book teach this?
a) As theory only, never practised b) Only in a glossary c) Only as an exam d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Persian
**Answer: d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Persian**
