# Taps and valves — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In your own words, what is stop valve (شیر فلکه)?
**Answer:** The valve that shuts off water to a whole line or section.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In your own words, what is washer?
**Answer:** The small seal inside a tap whose wearing causes dripping.
