# Taps and valves — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Stop valve (شیر فلکه) is the valve that shuts off water to a whole line or section.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Draw-off tap has nothing to do with this lesson.
**Answer: False** — It is part of the lesson: the outlet tap where water is drawn.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Washer is the small seal inside a tap whose wearing causes dripping.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.
