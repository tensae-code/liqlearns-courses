# Taps and valves — matching

Match each item on the left with the one on the right.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** stop valve (شیر فلکه) — the valve that shuts off water to a whole line or section

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** draw-off tap — the outlet tap where water is drawn

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** washer — the small seal inside a tap whose wearing causes dripping
