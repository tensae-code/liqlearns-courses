# Silk — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Say in one sentence what this lesson teaches.
**Answer:** Silk — how silk thread is made (700–1500 m from one cocoon), natural vs artificial silk compared (properties and care — ironing at 150–160°C vs 100–150°C), and an experiment to identify silk materials.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name one material, tool or step this lesson works with.
**Answer:** Any one named in the lesson text — for example, the first step of sub lesson 1: Silk.
