# Hands-on — Silk

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: how silk thread is made (700–1500 m from one cocoon), natural vs artificial silk compared (properties and care — ironing at 150–160°C vs 100–150°C), and an experiment to identify silk materials. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
