# Hands-on — Reuse in practice

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## Plan a jeans product on paper
Choose a new product that recycled jeans could become — a bag, a cushion cover, an apron or a pencil case. Draw the jeans and mark which part supplies each piece of your product, including one ready-made part (a pocket, a seam or loops) used as it is.

## Waste audit for your plan
For your paper plan, list every offcut it would leave. Beside each, write its use — a smaller part, a patch, stuffing, or a kept leftover — until no offcut is simply 'bin'. That is minimising the waste your own work produces.
