# Reuse in practice — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why is denim from old jeans a good material for a new product?
a) It is strong and proven, with useful ready-made parts like pockets and seams b) It is brand new c) It has never been worn d) It is always free of wear everywhere
**Answer: a) It is strong and proven, with useful ready-made parts like pockets and seams**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What does kierrätysfarkut mean in the Finnish material?
a) Recycled jeans b) A new pair of jeans c) A denim shop d) Jeans dye
**Answer: a) Recycled jeans**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Who was the Finnish teaching pilot for?
a) Children aged 7–9 b) Only teachers c) Only factory workers d) Adults over 60
**Answer: a) Children aged 7–9**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** What is one ready-made part of jeans that can serve a new product as it is?
a) A pocket (also: strong seams, belt loops) b) The price tag c) The shop bag d) Nothing on jeans is reusable
**Answer: a) A pocket (also: strong seams, belt loops)**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** What is the best way to deal with waste from your own work?
a) Minimise it — plan and cut so as little waste as possible is produced b) Produce more of it c) Hide it d) Burn it
**Answer: a) Minimise it — plan and cut so as little waste as possible is produced**
