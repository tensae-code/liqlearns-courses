# Reuse in practice — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A worn pair of jeans can still be a valuable material.
**Answer: True** — Its denim is strong and its pockets, seams and loops are ready-made.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Reuse means buying new material that looks old.
**Answer: False** — Reuse works with the actual old material — here, recycled jeans.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Waste you never produce needs no sorting or recycling.
**Answer: True** — Minimising waste at the source comes before sorting and recycling.
