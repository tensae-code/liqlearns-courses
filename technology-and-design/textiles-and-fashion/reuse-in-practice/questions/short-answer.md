# Reuse in practice — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** List the pilot's making steps in order.
**Answer:** Choose the new product, plan which parts of the jeans it needs, cut economically, make it, and finish it so it will be used.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Name two uses for denim offcuts.
**Answer:** Smaller parts, patches, straps or stuffing — any two of these (accept other sensible uses).
