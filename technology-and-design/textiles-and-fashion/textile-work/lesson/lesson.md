# Textile work

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Cuba's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Fabrics: types, fibres, structure
From Grade 2's first tejidos — their characteristics, properties and uses, traced with a template (plantilla) and cut — to Grade 9's full account: natural and artificial fabrics, their classification by nature and environmental impact, fabric structure (plain weave, knitted, felt) and sewing thread. The Grade 7 programme adds fibres and their obtaining, and the human story of textile genres.

## Sub lesson 2 — The sewing means and joining stitches
The means are named as a set: scissors, thimble (dedal), tape measure, needle, pins. With them, the joining stitches — hilván, pespunte, punto atrás, bastilla — and the fixing of buttons. Grade 5 adds fabric care (wash, dry, iron), the zip repair, the herringbone hem and darning (zurcido).

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: textile work begins with the cloth itself: fabric types and their properties, natural against artificial fibres, the structure of fabric — and the sewing means and stitches that work it. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.

## Do It Yourself
1. Do the Do it first task again, carefully this time, using everything the lesson taught.
2. Check your work against the lesson's own checks — fix one thing you find.
3. Keep your work: it is practice evidence, and the next lesson builds on it.
