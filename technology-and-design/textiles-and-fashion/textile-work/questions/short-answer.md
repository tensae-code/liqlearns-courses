# Textile work — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In your own words, what is fabric (tejido)?
**Answer:** Cloth made from fibres, by weaving, knitting or felting.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In your own words, what is thimble (dedal)?
**Answer:** A cap worn on the finger to push the needle safely.
