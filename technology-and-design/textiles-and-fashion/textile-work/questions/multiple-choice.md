# Textile work — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is fabric (tejido)?
a) cloth made from fibres, by weaving, knitting or felting b) Only a price c) Only decoration d) Nothing to do with this lesson
**Answer: a) cloth made from fibres, by weaving, knitting or felting**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is fibre?
a) Only history b) the fine thread-like unit from which yarn and fabric are made c) Only storage d) A kind of glue
**Answer: b) the fine thread-like unit from which yarn and fabric are made**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is thimble (dedal)?
a) Only old tools b) Only painting c) a cap worn on the finger to push the needle safely d) Only a price
**Answer: c) a cap worn on the finger to push the needle safely**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson teach, in one line?
a) Only decoration b) Nothing to do with this lesson c) Only history d) textile work begins with the cloth itself: fabric types and their properties, natural against artificial fibres, the structure of fabric — and the sewing means and stitches that work it.
**Answer: d) textile work begins with the cloth itself: fabric types and their properties, natural against artificial fibres, the structure of fabric — and the sewing means and stitches that work it.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** How does Cuba's book teach this?
a) As theory only, never practised b) Only in a glossary c) Only as an exam d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Spanish
**Answer: d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Spanish**
