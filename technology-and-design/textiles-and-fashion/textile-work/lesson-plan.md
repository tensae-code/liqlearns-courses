# Lesson plan — Textile work

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Textiles and Fashion · **Lesson:** Textile work
**Place in the curriculum:** Textiles and Fashion (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Cuba expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** textile work begins with the cloth itself: fabric types and their properties, natural against artificial fibres, the structure of fabric — and the sewing means and stitches that work it.
**This lesson's own level:** stocked from Cuba's *Programa: Educación Laboral — 2do grado* and the Cuba books in lesson/sources.md — see lesson/sources.md. Cuba counts in the acceptance count for its student-book placements; its programme and methodological-orientations placements are evidence only, counted nowhere.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **Fabrics: types, fibres, structure** — From Grade 2's first tejidos — their characteristics, properties and uses, traced with a template (plantilla) and cut — to Grade 9's full account: natural and artificial fabrics, their classification by nature and environmental impact, fabric structure (plain weave, knitted, felt) and sewing thread. The Grade 7 programme adds fibres and their obtaining, and the human story of textile genres.
2. **The sewing means and joining stitches** — The means are named as a set: scissors, thimble (dedal), tape measure, needle, pins. With them, the joining stitches — hilván, pespunte, punto atrás, bastilla — and the fixing of buttons. Grade 5 adds fabric care (wash, dry, iron), the zip repair, the herringbone hem and darning (zurcido).

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
