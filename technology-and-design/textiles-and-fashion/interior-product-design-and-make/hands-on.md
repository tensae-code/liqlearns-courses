# Hands-on — Interior product design-and-make

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: decorative products for the interior: sketch and preparation (§19), creating a decorative 2D/3D interior product (§20), decorating and presenting it (§21). Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
