# Knitting a useful article — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the knitting module's making project?
a) House slippers (papucei de casă) b) A sampler square c) A wall hanging d) A doll's scarf
**Answer: a) House slippers (papucei de casă)**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Why a useful article?
a) Slippers are fashionable b) Usefulness is the test — it must fit, hold and be worn c) Samples are forbidden everywhere d) No reason is given
**Answer: b) Usefulness is the test — it must fit, hold and be worn**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the project spend?
a) Everything the module taught — casting on, knit and purl, edges, casting off b) Only casting on c) Only sewing d) Bought parts
**Answer: a) Everything the module taught — casting on, knit and purl, edges, casting off**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** How is the finished article judged?
a) By wearing — fit, hold and finish b) By weight c) By price d) It is not judged
**Answer: a) By wearing — fit, hold and finish**

**5. (Sub lesson 1)** What are papucei de casă?
a) Gloves b) Hats c) House slippers d) Socks for outdoors
**Answer: c) House slippers**
