# Knitting a useful article — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The module ends in a useful article, not a sampler.
**Answer: True** — The making project is house slippers.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Any knitted shape passes, worn or not.
**Answer: False** — The article must fit, hold together and be worn — usefulness is the test.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The slippers use the module's whole technique.
**Answer: True** — Casting on, knit and purl, edges and casting off are all spent on the pair.
