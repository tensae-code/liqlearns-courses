# Hands-on — Knitting a useful article

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## Plan the pair
Plan the slippers as a project: sizes, yarn, stitch pattern, and the making order — before casting on.

## Knit and judge
Knit one slipper (or a scaled test). Judge it as a useful article: fit, hold, finish — and write the fix for its weakest point.
