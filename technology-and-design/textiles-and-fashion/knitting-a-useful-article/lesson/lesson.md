# Knitting a useful article

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Moldova's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — The project idea — useful, not a sample
Grade 4 knitting does not end in a sampler: the module's making project is a useful article — house slippers (papucei de casă). Usefulness is the test: the article must fit, hold together and be worn.

## Sub lesson 2 — Knitting the slippers
The slippers spend everything the module taught — casting on, knit and purl, edges, casting off — on a shaped, finished pair. The project is where technique becomes an article, judged by wearing.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: knitting proves itself on a useful article: the Grade 4 module's making project is house slippers (papucei de casă) — every stitch learned, spent on something worn. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.

## Do It Yourself
1. Do the Do it first task again, carefully this time, using everything the lesson taught.
2. Check your work against the lesson's own checks — fix one thing you find.
3. Keep your work: it is practice evidence, and the next lesson builds on it.
