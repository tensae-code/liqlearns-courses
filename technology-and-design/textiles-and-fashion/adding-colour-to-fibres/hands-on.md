# Hands-on — Adding colour to fibres

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## Rub a pattern
Lay paper over a textured surface (a carved board, a coin, a leaf vein) and rub colour across it. Compare the rubbed pattern with a freehand copy of the same pattern: what does each method do better?

## Dye evaluation
Examine two dyed samples (natural and synthetic if identifiable). Score each for colour evenness and wash-fastness evidence, and write which you would choose for a garment worn weekly, with reasons.
