# Adding colour to fibres — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain how the upeti board creates a pattern without paint being drawn on.
**Answer:** Answer: The cloth is laid over the board's carved pattern and colour is rubbed across it; the raised carving takes more colour, so the pattern rises through the rubbing.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Name three things you check when evaluating a dye.
**Answer:** Answer: Any three of: the colour it gives on this fibre, wash- and light-fastness, safety, availability, cost.
