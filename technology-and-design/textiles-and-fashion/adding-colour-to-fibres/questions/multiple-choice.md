# Adding colour to fibres — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Fui adds colour by…
a) Immersion — soaking the cloth b) Rubbing over a board c) Freehand painting d) Weaving
**Answer: a) Immersion — soaking the cloth**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Elei adds colour by…
a) Rubbing over the carved upeti board b) Soaking for a day c) Burning the pattern in d) Stitching the pattern
**Answer: a) Rubbing over the carved upeti board**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The upeti is…
a) A board carved with the pattern b) A dye plant c) A type of bark d) A painting brush
**Answer: a) A board carved with the pattern**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Tutusi/mamanu is…
a) Freehand painting with no carved guide b) Immersion dyeing c) Machine printing d) Folding the cloth
**Answer: a) Freehand painting with no carved guide**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** A dye is evaluated by…
a) Colour on the fibre, fastness, safety and availability b) Its price only c) Its smell only d) Its bottle shape
**Answer: a) Colour on the fibre, fastness, safety and availability**
