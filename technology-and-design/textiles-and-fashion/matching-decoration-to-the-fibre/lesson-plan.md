# Lesson plan — Matching decoration to the fibre

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Textiles and Fashion · **Lesson:** Matching decoration to the fibre
**Place in the curriculum:** Textiles and Fashion (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Samoa expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** decoration must match the fibre: fibres differ in absorbency, so a dye, print or texture that sings on one fibre fails on another — the decorator tests absorbency first.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on student book evidence — Samoa's MESC textbooks (see lesson/sources.md). It counts in the acceptance count (student book, not evidence-only).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **Fibres drink differently** — Absorbency — how readily a fibre takes up liquid — differs from fibre to fibre.
2. **Which decoration suits which fibre** — Match the method to the material: absorbent fibres take dyes and prints kindly; resistant fibres need other routes.
3. **Test before you decorate** — A small trial on the actual fibre answers what theory cannot.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of Textiles and Fashion. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Explain absorbency and how it differs between fibres.
2. Match decoration methods to fibre behaviour.
3. Say why a trial scrap comes first.
## What you need
Cotton and synthetic cloth scraps and a little water for the drop test.
