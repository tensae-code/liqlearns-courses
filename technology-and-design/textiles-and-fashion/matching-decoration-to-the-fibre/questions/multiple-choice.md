# Matching decoration to the fibre — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Absorbency is…
a) How readily a fibre takes up liquid b) How shiny a fibre is c) How long a fibre is d) How expensive a fibre is
**Answer: a) How readily a fibre takes up liquid**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A water drop beading on a cloth tells you the fibre…
a) Resists taking up liquid b) Is very absorbent c) Is cotton d) Cannot be decorated at all
**Answer: a) Resists taking up liquid**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** An absorbent fibre usually takes immersion dyeing…
a) Evenly and kindly b) Not at all c) Only in spots by nature d) Worse than a resistant fibre
**Answer: a) Evenly and kindly**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** If a fibre resists water-based dye, the decorator…
a) Chooses another dye class or a surface/mechanical method b) Gives up decorating forever c) Uses more water d) Blames the cloth
**Answer: a) Chooses another dye class or a surface/mechanical method**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** The trial scrap is decorated first to…
a) Check colour strength, evenness and fastness on the actual fibre b) Waste time c) Use up spare dye d) Practise cutting
**Answer: a) Check colour strength, evenness and fastness on the actual fibre**
