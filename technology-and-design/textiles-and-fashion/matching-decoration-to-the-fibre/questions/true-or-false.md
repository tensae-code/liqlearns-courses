# Matching decoration to the fibre — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** All fibres absorb liquids equally.
**Answer: False** — Absorbency differs from fibre to fibre — the drop test shows it.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The fibre's behaviour sets the menu of decoration methods.
**Answer: True** — Method follows material, not the decorator's wish.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Skipping the trial can cost the whole piece.
**Answer: True** — A failed decoration on the real cloth may be unfixable.
