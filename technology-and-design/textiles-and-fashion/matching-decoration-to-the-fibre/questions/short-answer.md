# Matching decoration to the fibre — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Describe the drop test and what it tells you.
**Answer:** Answer: A drop of water is placed on the fabric; if it sinks in, the fibre is absorbent and will take dye/print kindly; if it beads, the fibre resists liquid and needs another route.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A fibre will not take your dye evenly. Name two things you can change.
**Answer:** Answer: Any two of: the dye class, the method (surface print instead of immersion), the decoration type (mechanical texture instead), or the fabric itself.
