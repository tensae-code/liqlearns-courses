# Hands-on — Matching decoration to the fibre

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## Drop test survey
Drop-test four fabrics and rank them by absorbency. Predict which will dye most evenly, then check your prediction against any dyed examples available.

## Trial scrap
Decorate a small scrap (print or dye, as available, with supervision) and judge the result: strength, evenness, fastness after a rinse. Write the verdict and what you would change for the real piece.
