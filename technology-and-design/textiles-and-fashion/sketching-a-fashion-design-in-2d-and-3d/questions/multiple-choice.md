# Sketching a fashion design in 2D and 3D — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is a labelled fashion sketch?
a) a 2D/3D fashion drawing naming elements, materials and joining techniques b) Only a price c) Only decoration d) Nothing to do with this lesson
**Answer: a) a 2D/3D fashion drawing naming elements, materials and joining techniques**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is analysing the elements?
a) Only decoration b) reading the design elements at work in the fashion design c) Only a price d) A kind of glue
**Answer: b) reading the design elements at work in the fashion design**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is producing the product?
a) Only history b) Only painting c) making the fashion product from the labelled sketch d) Only storage
**Answer: c) making the fashion product from the labelled sketch**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson teach, in one line?
a) a fashion design is sketched in 2D and 3D with elements, materials and joining techniques labelled — analysed, then produced from the sketch. b) Only prices c) Only old tools d) Nothing practical
**Answer: a) a fashion design is sketched in 2D and 3D with elements, materials and joining techniques labelled — analysed, then produced from the sketch.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** How does Malaysia's book teach this?
a) As theory only, never practised b) Only in the glossary c) Only as an exam d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Malay (RBT)
**Answer: d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Malay (RBT)**
