# Sketching a fashion design in 2D and 3D — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A labelled fashion sketch is a 2D/3D fashion drawing naming elements, materials and joining techniques.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Analysing the elements has nothing to do with this lesson.
**Answer: False** — It is part of the lesson: reading the design elements at work in the fashion design.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Producing the product is making the fashion product from the labelled sketch.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.
