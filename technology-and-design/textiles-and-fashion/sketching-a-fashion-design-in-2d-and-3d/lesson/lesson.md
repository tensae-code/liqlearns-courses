# Sketching a fashion design in 2D and 3D

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Malaysia's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Sketching fashion, labelled
A fashion design is sketched in 2D and 3D and labelled — elements, materials and joining techniques named on the drawing, with trousers and a shoe as the book’s worked examples — so the sketch carries the whole making plan.

## Sub lesson 2 — From sketch to product
The elements in the fashion design are analysed, the materials of fashion design are chosen, and the fashion product is produced from the labelled sketch. The drawing is the contract the making fulfils.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: a fashion design is sketched in 2D and 3D with elements, materials and joining techniques labelled — analysed, then produced from the sketch. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.

## Do It Yourself
1. Do the Do it first task again, carefully this time, using everything the lesson taught.
2. Check your work against the lesson's own checks — fix one thing you find.
3. Keep your work: it is practice evidence, and the next lesson builds on it.
