# Lesson plan — Sketching a fashion design in 2D and 3D

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Textiles and Fashion · **Lesson:** Sketching a fashion design in 2D and 3D
**Place in the curriculum:** Textiles and Fashion (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Malaysia expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** a fashion design is sketched in 2D and 3D with elements, materials and joining techniques labelled — analysed, then produced from the sketch.
**This lesson's own level:** stocked from Malaysia's *Reka Bentuk dan Teknologi* (RBT — Design and Technology) textbooks — see lesson/sources.md. Malaysia counts in the acceptance count (student books, not evidence-only).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **Sketching fashion, labelled** — A fashion design is sketched in 2D and 3D and labelled — elements, materials and joining techniques named on the drawing, with trousers and a shoe as the book’s worked examples — so the sketch carries the whole making plan.
2. **From sketch to product** — The elements in the fashion design are analysed, the materials of fashion design are chosen, and the fashion product is produced from the labelled sketch.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of Textiles and Fashion. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Sketch a fashion design in 2D and 3D with full labels.
2. Analyse its elements, choose materials and outline production.
3. Explain how Malaysia's book teaches it.
## What you need
Sketch paper; a garment to design.
