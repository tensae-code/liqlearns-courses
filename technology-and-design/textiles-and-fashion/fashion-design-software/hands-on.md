# Hands-on — Fashion design software

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: Fashion CAD and My Label 3D — entering body measurements, choosing a style, printing the pattern and cutting. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
