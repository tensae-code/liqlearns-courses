# Hands-on — Fabric printing techniques

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## Design a repeating motif
On paper, design one small motif that could repeat across a fabric — a leaf, a star, a simple animal. Draw it four times in a row, evenly spaced, as it would look printed. Mark where a stencil's openings would be.

## Stencil test on paper
Cut a simple stencil from stiff paper (with an adult's help if needed) and dab colour through it onto scrap paper or scrap fabric with pigment or paint. Write what made the print clean or smudged, and what you would change on the second print.
