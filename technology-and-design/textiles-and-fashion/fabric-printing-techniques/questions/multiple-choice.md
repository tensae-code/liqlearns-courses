# Fabric printing techniques — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In fabric printing, where does the colour go?
a) Onto the surface of the textile, in the pattern's shapes, using pigments b) Through every fibre of the whole cloth, always c) Only into the water d) Nowhere — printing uses no colour
**Answer: a) Onto the surface of the textile, in the pattern's shapes, using pigments**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** How is printing different from dyeing?
a) Printing applies a pattern to the surface; dyeing colours the textile through b) They are identical c) Printing never uses colour d) Dyeing only works on paper
**Answer: a) Printing applies a pattern to the surface; dyeing colours the textile through**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** How does stencil printing make its pattern?
a) Colour is applied through openings cut in a stencil b) The fabric is folded c) The pattern grows on the fabric d) Heat alone makes it
**Answer: a) Colour is applied through openings cut in a stencil**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** What can stencil and screen-style printing do well?
a) Repeat the same pattern evenly, piece after piece b) Print without any colour c) Print a different random pattern each time with no control d) Work on water
**Answer: a) Repeat the same pattern evenly, piece after piece**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** What should you do before printing on the real fabric?
a) Test on a scrap first b) Stack wet prints at once c) Move the fabric while printing d) Skip drying
**Answer: a) Test on a scrap first**
