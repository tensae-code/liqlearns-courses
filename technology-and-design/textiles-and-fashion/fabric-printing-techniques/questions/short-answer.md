# Fabric printing techniques — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 3)** List the clean-print steps in order.
**Answer:** Lay the fabric flat and still, place the stencil or screen exactly, apply pigment evenly, lift carefully, and let the print dry.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why do printers use a stencil or screen instead of painting freehand each time?
**Answer:** So the same pattern can be repeated evenly and accurately on piece after piece.
