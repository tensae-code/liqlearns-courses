# Hands-on — Knotting

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: the mounting knot, flat knot, diagonal and spiral knots worked from diagrams; a key fob finished with a bead; calculating cord length (cord = 8 × finished length). Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
