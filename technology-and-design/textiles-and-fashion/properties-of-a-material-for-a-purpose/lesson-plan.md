# Lesson plan — Properties of a material for a purpose

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Textiles and Fashion · **Lesson:** Properties of a material for a purpose
**Place in the curriculum:** Textiles and Fashion (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Samoa expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** a material is chosen for a purpose by its properties: cotton suits clothing and bed linen in Samoa's climate because of how it behaves in heat, moisture and washing.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on student book evidence — Samoa's MESC textbooks (see lesson/sources.md). It counts in the acceptance count (student book, not evidence-only).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **Properties decide the purpose** — Every material has properties — absorbency, softness, strength, coolness — and a purpose needs some of them and not others.
2. **Cotton in a hot climate** — In Samoa's climate, clothing and bed linen must handle heat and sweat every day.
3. **Testing a choice** — A choice is proved by investigating the material, not by guessing.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of Textiles and Fashion. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Explain choosing a material as matching properties to a purpose.
2. Give cotton's properties that suit clothing and bed linen.
3. Say how an investigation proves a choice.
## What you need
A cotton item and, if possible, a synthetic item to compare.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — the opening doing task in lesson/lesson.md.
2. **The explanation** — the sub lessons in order (lesson/lesson.md).
   - ADD IMAGE: a picture of cotton clothing and bed linen in a hot, sunny room, with small labels naming absorbency, softness and washability — the student matches each label to the job the item does.
   - ADD GIF: a GIF of a drop of water soaking into cotton cloth while it beads on a synthetic cloth — the student says which cloth absorbed and why that matters in heat.

   - ADD VIDEO: a short video of the lesson demonstration — see video-script.md.
