# Properties of a material for a purpose — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A purpose needs some properties and not others.
**Answer: True** — Matching starts from what the job demands.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Cotton leaves sweat sitting on the skin.
**Answer: False** — Cotton absorbs moisture, which is why it suits hot climates.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** An investigation can overturn a first guess about a material.
**Answer: True** — Testing, not guessing, justifies the choice.
