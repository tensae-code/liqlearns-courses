# Properties of a material for a purpose — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Choosing a material for a purpose means…
a) Matching the material's properties to what the job demands b) Always choosing the cheapest c) Choosing the brightest colour d) Copying another product
**Answer: a) Matching the material's properties to what the job demands**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which cotton property helps most in a hot climate?
a) It absorbs sweat and lets air move b) It melts in heat c) It repels all water d) It never needs washing
**Answer: a) It absorbs sweat and lets air move**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Cotton suits bed linen partly because…
a) It washes well and often b) It cannot be washed c) It is waterproof d) It is stiff
**Answer: a) It washes well and often**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** A material choice is best proved by…
a) Investigating and testing the material b) Reading its label only c) Guessing d) Asking the price
**Answer: a) Investigating and testing the material**

**5. (Sub lesson 1)** Which is a property of a material?
a) Absorbency b) Its shop c) Its advertisement d) Its box
**Answer: a) Absorbency**
