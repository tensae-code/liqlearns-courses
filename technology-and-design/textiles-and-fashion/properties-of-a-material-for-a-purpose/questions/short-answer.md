# Properties of a material for a purpose — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name two properties that make cotton suit clothing in Samoa.
**Answer:** Answer: It absorbs sweat and stays comfortable in heat (accept also: softness, washability, breathability).

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Why is handling and wetting a material better than trusting its label?
**Answer:** Answer: The investigation shows what the material actually does, so the choice rests on evidence, not claims.
