# Hands-on — Properties of a material for a purpose

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## Absorbency test
Drop the same amount of water on cotton and on a synthetic fabric. Time how long each takes to soak in. Record the result and what it means for clothing.

## Purpose match
Choose three purposes (school shirt, bed sheet, rain cover). For each, name the two properties that matter most and a material that has them.
