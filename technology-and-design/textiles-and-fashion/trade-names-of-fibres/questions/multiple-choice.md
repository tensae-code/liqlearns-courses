# Trade names of fibres — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson teach?
a) The commercial names under which fibres are sold b) Only how to draw pictures c) Nothing practical d) A song to memorise
**Answer: a) The commercial names under which fibres are sold**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Sub lesson 1 is about…
a) Trade names of fibres b) Cooking lunch c) A sports match d) The weather
**Answer: a) Trade names of fibres**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Sub lesson 2 asks the learner to…
a) Complete the making in full and check it b) Stop before starting c) Copy a partner's work d) Throw the materials away
**Answer: a) Complete the making in full and check it**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Greece teaches this topic inside which school book?
a) Fabric Technology b) Only mathematics c) Only history d) It is not taught at school
**Answer: a) Fabric Technology**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** What should the learner do first in this lesson?
