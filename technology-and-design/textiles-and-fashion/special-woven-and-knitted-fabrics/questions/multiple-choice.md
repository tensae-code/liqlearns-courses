# Special woven and knitted fabrics — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson teach?
a) Terry fabrics, velvets, piqué, lancé and jacquard wovens; special knits (support-yarn knits, terry and velvet knits, fibre-tuft knits, piqué knits, fillet/azuré knits, warp-knitted lace) b) Only how to draw pictures c) Nothing practical d) A song to memorise
**Answer: a) Terry fabrics, velvets, piqué, lancé and jacquard wovens; special knits (support-yarn knits, terry and velvet knits, fibre-tuft knits, piqué knits, fillet/azuré knits, warp-knitted lace)**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Sub lesson 1 is about…
a) Special woven and knitted fabrics b) Cooking lunch c) A sports match d) The weather

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Sub lesson 2 asks the learner to…
