# Hands-on — Sewing the dress to a technology plan

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: the material and equipment plans, the 24-step sewing sequence for the sample dress, and lace (гипюр) — its kinds, properties, uses and care (iron at no more than 150°C). Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
