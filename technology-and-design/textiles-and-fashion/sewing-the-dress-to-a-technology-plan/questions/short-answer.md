# Sewing the dress to a technology plan — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Say in one sentence what this lesson teaches.
**Answer:** Sewing the dress to a technology plan — the material and equipment plans, the 24-step sewing sequence for the sample dress, and lace (гипюр) — its kinds, properties, uses and care (iron at no more than 150°C).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name one material, tool or step this lesson works with.
**Answer:** Any one named in the lesson text — for example, the first step of sub lesson 1: Sewing the dress to a technology plan.
