# Fashion and fabric usability — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Say in one sentence what this lesson teaches.
**Answer:** What fashion is and how it is created, keeping up with fashion (magazines, fabric exhibitions, suppliers, internet, travel), fashion's role in building a collection, usability of fabric/garment incl. technical garments (protective and high-visibility clothing).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name one material, tool or step this lesson works with.
**Answer:** Any one named in the lesson text — for example, the first step of sub lesson 1: Fashion and fabric usability.
