# Fashion and fabric usability — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson teach?
a) What fashion is and how it is created, keeping up with fashion (magazines, fabric exhibitions, suppliers, internet, travel), fashion's role in building a collection, usability of fabric/garment incl. technical garments (protective and high-visibility clothing) b) Only how to draw pictures c) Nothing practical d) A song to memorise

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Sub lesson 1 is about…
