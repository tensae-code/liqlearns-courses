# Hands-on — Fashion and fabric usability

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: what fashion is and how it is created, keeping up with fashion (magazines, fabric exhibitions, suppliers, internet, travel), fashion's role in building a collection, usability of fabric/garment incl. technical garments (protective and high-visibility clothing). Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
