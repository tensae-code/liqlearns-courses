# Hands-on — Fabrics for occasion dresses

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: the four requirement groups (appearance, use, health, technology), the property table for cotton, linen, silk, wool, viscose and polyester, and the ways of laying patterns on fabric (lengthwise, folded, mixed). Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
