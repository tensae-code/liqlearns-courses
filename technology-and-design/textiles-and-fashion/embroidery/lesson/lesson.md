# Embroidery

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Moldova's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — The materials — canvas and mouliné
Grade 4 needlework opens with materials: the ground is home-woven canvas of natural fibres — hemp, cotton, linen and wool — and the stitching thread is mouliné. Fibre first: what the canvas is woven from decides how the embroidery sits and lasts.

## Sub lesson 2 — The tools — needle, scissors, thimble, hoop
The tools are four: the needle that carries the thread, the scissors that cut true, the thimble that protects the pushing finger, and the embroidery hoop that holds the canvas taut. Each tool answers a need the hands meet in the first hour of stitching.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: embroidery begins with its kit: home-woven canvas of natural fibres — hemp, cotton, linen, wool — mouliné threads, and the tools: needle, scissors, thimble and embroidery hoop. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.

## Do It Yourself
1. Do the Do it first task again, carefully this time, using everything the lesson taught.
2. Check your work against the lesson's own checks — fix one thing you find.
3. Keep your work: it is practice evidence, and the next lesson builds on it.
