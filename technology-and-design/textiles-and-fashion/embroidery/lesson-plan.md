# Lesson plan — Embroidery

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Textiles and Fashion · **Lesson:** Embroidery
**Place in the curriculum:** Textiles and Fashion (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Moldova expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** embroidery begins with its kit: home-woven canvas of natural fibres — hemp, cotton, linen, wool — mouliné threads, and the tools: needle, scissors, thimble and embroidery hoop.
**This lesson's own level:** stocked from Moldova's *Educație tehnologică* (Technological Education) textbooks — see lesson/sources.md. Moldova counts in the acceptance count (student books, not evidence-only).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **The materials — canvas and mouliné** — Grade 4 needlework opens with materials: the ground is home-woven canvas of natural fibres — hemp, cotton, linen and wool — and the stitching thread is mouliné.
2. **The tools — needle, scissors, thimble, hoop** — The tools are four: the needle that carries the thread, the scissors that cut true, the thimble that protects the pushing finger, and the embroidery hoop that holds the canvas taut.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of Textiles and Fashion. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name the embroidery materials — canvas fibres and threads.
2. Name and handle the embroidery tools.
3. Explain how Moldova's book teaches it.
## What you need
Canvas, mouliné threads, needle, scissors, thimble and an embroidery hoop.
