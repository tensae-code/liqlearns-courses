# Embroidery — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the canvas fibres.
**Answer:** Hemp, cotton, linen and wool — natural fibres, home-woven into canvas.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the four tools and one job each.
**Answer:** Needle (carries the thread), scissors (cut), thimble (protects the finger), hoop (holds the canvas taut).
