# Embroidery — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the embroidery ground?
a) Home-woven canvas of natural fibres b) Paper c) Plastic sheet d) Leather
**Answer: a) Home-woven canvas of natural fibres**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which fibres are named for the canvas?
a) Silk and nylon only b) Hemp, cotton, linen and wool c) Only wool d) No fibres are named
**Answer: b) Hemp, cotton, linen and wool**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** What thread is used?
a) Mouliné threads b) Wire c) String d) Ribbon only
**Answer: a) Mouliné threads**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the thimble do?
a) Protects the pushing finger b) Cuts the thread c) Holds the canvas d) Measures the cloth
**Answer: a) Protects the pushing finger**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the hoop do?
a) Stores needles b) Dyes the thread c) Holds the canvas taut d) Irons the work
**Answer: c) Holds the canvas taut**
