# Embroidery — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The canvas fibres named are natural fibres.
**Answer: True** — Hemp, cotton, linen and wool are all natural fibres.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The thimble is a cutting tool.
**Answer: False** — The scissors cut; the thimble protects the finger that pushes the needle.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The hoop holds the canvas taut for stitching.
**Answer: True** — That is the embroidery hoop's job.
