# Hands-on — Introduction to pattern-making

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: the garment, the general characteristics of the human body's outward appearance and the basic morphological indices; the usefulness of the garment pattern, the basic lines and basic elements of pattern construction, preparing a pattern for production. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
