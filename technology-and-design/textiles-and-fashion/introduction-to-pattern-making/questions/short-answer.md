# Introduction to pattern-making — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Say in one sentence what this lesson teaches.
**Answer:** The garment, the general characteristics of the human body's outward appearance and the basic morphological indices; the usefulness of the garment pattern, the basic lines and basic elements of pattern construction, preparing a pattern for production.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name one material, tool or step this lesson works with.
**Answer:** Any one named in the lesson text — for example, the first step of sub lesson 1: Introduction to pattern-making.
