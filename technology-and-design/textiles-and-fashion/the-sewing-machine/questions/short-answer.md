# The sewing machine — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain in one or two sentences how a machine stitch is made.
**Answer:** The needle carries the top thread down through the material to meet a second thread from below; the two threads lock together and hold the stitch tight.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Name one strength of hand sewing that the machine does not have.
**Answer:** It needs no power and can be done anywhere (accept also: it is the older way, done with needle and sinew).
