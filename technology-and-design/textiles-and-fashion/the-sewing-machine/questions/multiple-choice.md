# The sewing machine — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How many threads make a machine stitch?
a) Two — one from above and one from below b) One c) Four d) None
**Answer: a) Two — one from above and one from below**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What carries the top thread down through the material?
a) The needle b) The motor alone c) The sewer's finger d) The cloth itself
**Answer: a) The needle**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the electric motor do?
a) Moves the needle up and down and feeds the material through b) Chooses the cloth c) Threads itself with sinew d) Nothing
**Answer: a) Moves the needle up and down and feeds the material through**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** What is the older hand-sewing way in Nunavut?
a) Needle and sinew, sewn by hand b) Only machines c) Glue d) Staples
**Answer: a) Needle and sinew, sewn by hand**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Why do machine stitches land in an even line?
a) The machine feeds the material through a little at a time as each stitch forms b) Luck c) The cloth moves by itself d) They do not
**Answer: a) The machine feeds the material through a little at a time as each stitch forms**
