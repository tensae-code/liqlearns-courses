# Lesson plan — The sewing machine

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Textiles and Fashion · **Lesson:** The sewing machine
**Place in the curriculum:** Textiles and Fashion (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Canada expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** a sewing machine makes a stitch by carrying the top thread down on its needle to meet a second thread from below, locking the two together — while its motor moves the needle and feeds the material through.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on a student reader — Canada's *Technology in the Community* (Tulliniliara Reading Series, Level 14), Government of Nunavut / Inhabit Education (see lesson/sources.md). It counts in the acceptance count (student book, not evidence-only).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **How the stitch is made — two threads meet** — A machine stitch is made from two threads, not one.
2. **The motor moves the needle and feeds the material** — An electric motor does the machine's hard work.
3. **Hand sewing — the older way** — Before and beside the machine stands hand sewing: one needle, one thread, pushed through by hand.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of Textiles and Fashion. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Explain how a machine stitch locks two threads together.
2. Say what the electric motor does in a sewing machine.
3. Describe hand sewing with needle and sinew as the older way.
## What you need
A piece of sewn clothing to examine; a needle and thread for the hands-on only, used carefully and with an adult nearby if the learner is young.

## The run of the lesson (in order)

2. **The explanation** — the sub lessons in order (lesson/lesson.md).
   - ADD IMAGE: a picture of a picture of a sewing-machine needle halfway down through cloth, its top thread meeting the second thread below the cloth, with the two threads locking — the student points to the top thread, the lower thread and the lock.
