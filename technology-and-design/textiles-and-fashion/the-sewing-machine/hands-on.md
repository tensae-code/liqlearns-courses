# Hands-on — The sewing machine

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## Read a real seam
Take a sewn item and find the top thread and the lower thread on a seam. Gently pull a loose end if one exists and watch which thread moves. Write two sentences on how the two threads hold the material together.

## Hand-sew a short line
With needle and thread, sew five running stitches through two small pieces of cloth. Compare your line with a machine seam: which is more even, which was faster to make, and which could be done anywhere with no power?
