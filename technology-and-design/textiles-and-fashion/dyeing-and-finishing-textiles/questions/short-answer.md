# Dyeing and finishing textiles — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 3)** What is the difference between dyeing and bleaching?
**Answer:** Dyeing adds colour to a textile; bleaching removes colour, lightening or whitening it.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Why does fibre knowledge come before dyeing?
**Answer:** The right dye, additive and mordant depend on the fibre being dyed, so the fibre must be known first.
