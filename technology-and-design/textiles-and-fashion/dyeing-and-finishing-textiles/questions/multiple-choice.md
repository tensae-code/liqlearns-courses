# Dyeing and finishing textiles — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does a mordant (puretus) do in dyeing?
a) Helps the dye bind to the fibre so the colour holds b) Removes all colour c) Makes the fabric stiffer only d) Cleans the workshop
**Answer: a) Helps the dye bind to the fibre so the colour holds**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which dyes does this lesson's dyeing use?
a) Synthetic dyes b) Only plant colours c) Chalk d) No dye at all
**Answer: a) Synthetic dyes**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What are the two families of bleaching agents named in the material?
a) Oxidising and reducing agents b) Hot and cold water c) Acids only d) Soaps only
**Answer: a) Oxidising and reducing agents**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** What does bleaching do to a textile?
a) Removes colour — lightening or whitening it b) Adds a pattern c) Sews pieces together d) Makes it waterproof in every case
**Answer: a) Removes colour — lightening or whitening it**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** How can finishing improve a fabric?
a) By mechanical treatment or by chemical treatment b) Only by painting it c) Only by cutting it d) It cannot improve it
**Answer: a) By mechanical treatment or by chemical treatment**
