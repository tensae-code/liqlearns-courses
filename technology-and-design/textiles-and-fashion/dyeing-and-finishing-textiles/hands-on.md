# Hands-on — Dyeing and finishing textiles

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## Process detective
Take three textiles at home. For each, write which of dyeing, bleaching or finishing it most likely went through, and the evidence: strong even colour suggests dyeing, whiteness suggests bleaching, a special surface feel suggests finishing.

## Plan a dye job on paper
Choose a fibre (for example wool or a synthetic). Write a plan: which dye family the lesson names, why a mordant may be needed, and the safety checks you would make before any real dyeing. Paper plan only — no chemicals.
