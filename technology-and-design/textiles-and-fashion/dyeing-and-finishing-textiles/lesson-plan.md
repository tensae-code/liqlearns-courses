# Lesson plan — Dyeing and finishing textiles

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Textiles and Fashion · **Lesson:** Dyeing and finishing textiles
**Place in the curriculum:** Textiles and Fashion (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Finland expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** dyeing gives a textile its colour and finishing improves what the fabric can do — dyeing uses dyes with additives and mordants, bleaching uses oxidising or reducing agents, and finishing improves fabric properties by mechanical or chemical means.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on a student learning material — Finland's *Katse tulevaan* (TuleVa project learning material, published via aoe.fi) (see lesson/sources.md). It counts in the acceptance count (learning material for craft teaching, not evidence-only).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **Dyeing with synthetic dyes, additives and mordants** — Dyeing colours a textile through and through, not just on its surface.
2. **Bleaching — oxidising and reducing agents** — Bleaching removes colour instead of adding it.
3. **Finishing — improving fabric properties** — Finishing improves a fabric's properties after it is made.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of Textiles and Fashion. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Explain dyeing with synthetic dyes, additives and mordants in your own words.
2. Explain bleaching — oxidising and reducing agents in your own words.
3. Explain finishing — improving fabric properties in your own words.
## What you need
No dyeing is done in this lesson; paper and pencil only. Any real dye work needs adult supervision and safety instructions.

1. **Do it first** — the opening doing task in lesson/lesson.md.

   - ADD IMAGE: a picture of a picture of three fabric pieces in a row — one being dyed in a dye bath, one white piece being bleached, and one finished fabric with a smooth improved surface — the student names the process at each piece.
   - ADD GIF: a GIF of a white fabric piece lowered into a dye bath and lifted out coloured, the colour holding as the fabric is rinsed — the student watches for the moment the colour is in the fabric, not just on it.
