# Decorating fabric — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A stamp print is made by…
a) Pressing a colour-loaded stamp onto the cloth b) Soaking the whole cloth c) Weaving the pattern in d) Cutting the cloth
**Answer: a) Pressing a colour-loaded stamp onto the cloth**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which of these is a Samoan stamp material in the book?
a) Banana stem b) A plastic ruler c) A metal coin press d) A sewing machine
**Answer: a) Banana stem**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Dyeing differs from printing because…
a) The dye soaks in and colours the fibre itself b) It uses no colour c) It only works on paper d) It never lasts
**Answer: a) The dye soaks in and colours the fibre itself**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A print usually shows on the back of the cloth…
a) Fainter than on the front b) Stronger than on the front c) Not at all, ever d) In a different pattern
**Answer: a) Fainter than on the front**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Before using a dye you should…
a) Read its instructions and protect your skin b) Taste it to check it c) Mix it with food colouring d) Skip protection if in a hurry
**Answer: a) Read its instructions and protect your skin**
