# Decorating fabric — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name three natural stamp materials from Samoa's book.
**Answer:** Answer: Any three of: potato, banana stem, taro, taamu, pawpaw, leaves.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Give two dye-safety rules.
**Answer:** Answer: Any two of: read the instructions first, protect skin, work in fresh air, label containers, keep dyes away from food and drink, wash hands and tools afterwards.
