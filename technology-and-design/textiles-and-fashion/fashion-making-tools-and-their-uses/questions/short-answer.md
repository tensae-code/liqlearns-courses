# Fashion-making tools and their uses — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In your own words, what is drafting tools?
**Answer:** French curve and l-square/set square — the tools that draw the pattern.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In your own words, what is the sharp-tool rule?
**Answer:** Store sharp tools in a special container.
