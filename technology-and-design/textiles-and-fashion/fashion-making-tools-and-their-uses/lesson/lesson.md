# Fashion-making tools and their uses

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Malaysia's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — The tool families
Fashion tools come in families by job: measuring (ruler, measuring tape), drafting (French curve, L-square/set square), cutting (paper scissors, fabric scissors, snips, seam ripper), marking (tailor’s pencils, tailor’s chalk, tracing wheel and carbon paper, pins), and sewing (thimble, hand needles, sewing machine, embroidery machine) — plus tweezers, hot-glue gun, stapler and tape.

## Sub lesson 2 — Sharp tools, safe storage
The families end in a safety rule the book states plainly: store sharp tools in a special container. Tool knowledge includes where tools live when they are not in the hand.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: fashion tools are learned in five families — measuring, drafting, cutting, marking, sewing — and sharp tools are stored in a special container. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.

2. Check your work against the lesson's own checks — fix one thing you find.
3. Keep your work: it is practice evidence, and the next lesson builds on it.
