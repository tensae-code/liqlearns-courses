# Garment transformation technology

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Kazakhstan's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Garment transformation technology
Kazakhstan's books teach it this way (the student book, printed p. 32): Transforming and decorating sewn products (clothing, accessories): changing a garment's form into another (§8). The learner works through the named materials and steps in order, saying what each step does before doing the next one.

## Sub lesson 2 — Garment transformation, teacher-guide evidence
The Kazakh treatment continues and completes the topic: The guide's Section 3 lesson plans for transforming and re-making garments, incl. the micro-drawing lesson's place in the course (printed p. 7) and the transformation section (printed p. 45) (teacher-guide evidence for Book 28). The girls' guide's evidence for Book 33: the tymak transformation and the bag project, with Hooke/Leeuwenhoek microscope history for the micro-art lessons (teacher-guide evidence). The learner finishes the make, checks it against the book's own sequence, and fixes one thing before presenting the work.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: Garment transformation technology — transforming and decorating sewn products (clothing, accessories): changing a garment's form into another (§8). Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
