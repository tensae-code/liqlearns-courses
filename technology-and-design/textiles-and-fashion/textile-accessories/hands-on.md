# Hands-on — Textile accessories

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: designing and making accessories from textile materials; decorating sewn products with a monogram; the bag sewn and shown at a defile (presentation ceremony). Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
