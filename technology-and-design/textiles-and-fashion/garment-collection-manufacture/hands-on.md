# Hands-on — Garment collection manufacture

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: from idea to fashion: creating your own image and developing ideas (§8); creating the individual product (§9); creating a collection of clothing and accessories for your image (§10); organising the work (§11). Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
