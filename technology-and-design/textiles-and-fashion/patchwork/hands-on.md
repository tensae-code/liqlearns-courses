# Hands-on — Patchwork

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: designing blocks on squared paper (an 18×18 cm block), drafting templates with seam allowances, the sewing sequence for joining blocks, and using patchwork for dusters, bags and panels. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
