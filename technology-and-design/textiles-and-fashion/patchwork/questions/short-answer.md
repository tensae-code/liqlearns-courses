# Patchwork — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Say in one sentence what this lesson teaches.
**Answer:** Patchwork — designing blocks on squared paper (an 18×18 cm block), drafting templates with seam allowances, the sewing sequence for joining blocks, and using patchwork for dusters, bags and panels.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name one material, tool or step this lesson works with.
**Answer:** Any one named in the lesson text — for example, the first step of sub lesson 1: Patchwork.
