# Hands-on — Garment design on the folk cut

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: designing clothing on the basis of the folk cut (§12); developing the sketch and preparing materials, tools and equipment (§13); creating the product on the folk-cut basis (§14). Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
