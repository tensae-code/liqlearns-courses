# Constructing the women's bodice — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Say in one sentence what this lesson teaches.
**Answer:** The basic elements, the bodice base for knitted and for woven fabrics, the classic jacket, the dress base, the vest, the kimono, bodices with darts and dart transfers (shoulder-dart transfer, redingote and princess bodices).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name one material, tool or step this lesson works with.
**Answer:** Any one named in the lesson text — for example, the first step of sub lesson 1: Constructing the women's bodice.
