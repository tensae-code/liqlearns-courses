Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: Regulation in the Textile Industry; Assessment criteria and technical work. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
