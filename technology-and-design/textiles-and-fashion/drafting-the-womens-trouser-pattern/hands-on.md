# Hands-on — Drafting the women's trouser pattern

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: the basic front/back frame, flared (καμπάνα) styles by two methods with master and copy patterns, sailor trousers, tube trousers, bermuda and shorts, grading from the basic pattern. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
