# Hands-on — "Fast cloth" garment construction

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: decorative tasks from simple geometric forms (poncho, cape, tunic): drafting from geometric shapes, facing and seam processing (§12). Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
