# Sewing along a straight line — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** What makes a line of sewing good in this lesson?
**Answer:** The stitches stay on the marked line and are even — the same length from stitch to stitch.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is ready before the first stitch is made?
**Answer:** A threaded needle and a straight line marked on the material to follow.
