# Sewing along a straight line — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Fast sewing is the goal of the first lesson.
**Answer: False** — The goal is stitches on the line and even — worked slowly and safely.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The stitches follow a marked line.
**Answer: True** — The marked straight line guides every stitch.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Stitches of any length are fine as long as they are quick.
**Answer: False** — Stitches should be even, of the same length.
