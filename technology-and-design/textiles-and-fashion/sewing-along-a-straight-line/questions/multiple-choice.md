# Sewing along a straight line — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What guides the first sewing?
a) A marked straight line b) A curved picture c) Nothing — you guess d) The edge of the table
**Answer: a) A marked straight line**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** How is the needle pointed while sewing?
a) Away from you, working slowly b) Towards your face c) Any way at all d) It is thrown across
**Answer: a) Away from you, working slowly**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** How do the stitches go along the line?
a) In and out, even stitches of the same length b) One giant stitch c) Random lengths d) Only on the ends
**Answer: a) In and out, even stitches of the same length**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** What do you check as you sew?
a) That stitches stay on the line and stay even b) How fast you finish c) The colour only d) Nothing
**Answer: a) That stitches stay on the line and stay even**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Why is straight-line sewing learned first?
a) It is the foundation every later stitch builds on b) It is the hardest sewing c) It needs no line d) It is only decoration
**Answer: a) It is the foundation every later stitch builds on**
