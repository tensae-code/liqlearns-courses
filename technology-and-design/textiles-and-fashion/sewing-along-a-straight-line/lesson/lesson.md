# Sewing along a straight line

## Do it first
Draw a straight line on a piece of cloth or card. Imagine stitching exactly along it, stitch after stitch, none wandering off. Then learn first sewing properly.

## Sub lesson 1 — First sewing — needle, thread and the marked line
The first sewing lesson starts safely: a needle threaded, the line to follow marked straight on the material, and the rule of working slowly with the needle always pointed away. The line is the guide — every stitch is placed on it.

## Sub lesson 2 — Stitching along the line
The needle passes in and out along the line, making even stitches of the same length. The learner sews the whole line, checking that stitches stay on the line and stay even — straight sewing is the foundation every later stitch builds on.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: first sewing follows a marked straight line with even stitches, needle in and out, slowly and safely. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.

## Do It Yourself
1. Do the Do it first task again, carefully this time, using everything the lesson taught.
2. Check your work against the lesson's own checks — fix one thing you find.
3. Keep your work: it is practice evidence, and the next lesson builds on it.
