# Braiding and felt sewing

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Kazakhstan's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Braiding and felt sewing
Kazakhstan's books teach it this way (the student book, printed pp. 60–66): A four-strand round kamshy whip braided; a torsyk sewn from felt (Lessons 19–21). The learner works through the named materials and steps in order, saying what each step does before doing the next one.

## Sub lesson 2 — Braiding and felt sewing — doing it yourself
Now the learner does the whole task unaided: a four-strand round kamshy whip braided; a torsyk sewn from felt (Lessons 19–21). Working from the first sub lesson's sequence, the learner plans the make, works through it step by step, and checks the finished piece against what the Kazakh book shows.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: Braiding and felt sewing — a four-strand round kamshy whip braided; a torsyk sewn from felt (Lessons 19–21). Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.

## Do It Yourself
1. Do the Do it first task again, carefully this time, using everything the lesson taught.
2. Check your work against the lesson's own checks — fix one thing you find.
3. Keep your work: it is practice evidence, and the next lesson builds on it.
