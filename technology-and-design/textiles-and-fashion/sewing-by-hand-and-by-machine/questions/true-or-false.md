# Sewing by hand and by machine — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Hand sewing (costura a mano) is joining cloth with needle and thread by hand.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Machine sewing (costura a máquina) has nothing to do with this lesson.
**Answer: False** — It is part of the lesson: joining cloth with the sewing machine.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Seam is the line where two cloth pieces are joined.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.
