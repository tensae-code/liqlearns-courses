# Sewing by hand and by machine — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is hand sewing (costura a mano)?
a) joining cloth with needle and thread by hand b) Only a price c) Only decoration d) Nothing to do with this lesson
**Answer: a) joining cloth with needle and thread by hand**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is machine sewing (costura a máquina)?
a) Only history b) joining cloth with the sewing machine c) Only storage d) A kind of glue
**Answer: b) joining cloth with the sewing machine**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is seam?
a) Only old tools b) Only painting c) the line where two cloth pieces are joined d) Only a price
**Answer: c) the line where two cloth pieces are joined**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson teach, in one line?
a) Only decoration b) Nothing to do with this lesson c) Only history d) sewing joins cloth two ways: by hand, stitch by stitch under the fingers' control, and by machine, the stitches formed at speed — and the sewer knows both, and when each is right.
**Answer: d) sewing joins cloth two ways: by hand, stitch by stitch under the fingers' control, and by machine, the stitches formed at speed — and the sewer knows both, and when each is right.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** How does Cuba's book teach this?
a) As theory only, never practised b) Only in a glossary c) Only as an exam d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Spanish
**Answer: d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Spanish**
