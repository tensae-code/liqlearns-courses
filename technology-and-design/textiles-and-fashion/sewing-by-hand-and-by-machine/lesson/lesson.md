# Sewing by hand and by machine

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Cuba's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Hand-sewing techniques
Cuba's Grade 9 textiles chapter (Cap. 2) teaches costura a mano as its own technique set: the hand stitches that join, hem and finish, worked with needle control that no machine replaces for small and fine work.

## Sub lesson 2 — Sewing with the machine
Costura a máquina follows: the sewing machine forms the same joins at speed and evenness. The learner compares the two ways — control against speed — and chooses per job, as the book's sequence teaches both in one chapter.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: sewing joins cloth two ways: by hand, stitch by stitch under the fingers' control, and by machine, the stitches formed at speed — and the sewer knows both, and when each is right. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.

## Do It Yourself
1. Do the Do it first task again, carefully this time, using everything the lesson taught.
2. Check your work against the lesson's own checks — fix one thing you find.
3. Keep your work: it is practice evidence, and the next lesson builds on it.
