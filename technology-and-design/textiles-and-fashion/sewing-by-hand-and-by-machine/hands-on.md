# Hands-on — Sewing by hand and by machine

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## Same join, two ways
Plan one simple join (two cloth squares). Write how you would sew it by hand and by machine: stitches, order, checks for each.

## Choose the way
For four jobs — a button, a long straight seam, a delicate hem, a repair on thick cloth — choose hand or machine and defend the choice.
