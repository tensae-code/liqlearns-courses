# Hands-on — Seam and stitch reference tables

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: the appendix tables: seam kinds drawn and described (петельный, обмёточная, козлик, крестообразный, потайной…) with what each is used for; decorative stitches (шов «вперёд иголку», тамбурный); ribbon-embroidery and bead-embroidery stitch charts (Appendix Tables 3–6, viewed by me). Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
