# Seam and stitch reference tables

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Kazakhstan's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Seam and stitch reference tables
Kazakhstan's books teach it this way (the student book, printed pp. 93–94): The appendix tables: seam kinds drawn and described (петельный, обмёточная, козлик, крестообразный, потайной…) with what each is used for; decorative stitches (шов «вперёд иголку», тамбурный); ribbon-embroidery and bead-embroidery stitch charts (Appendix Tables 3–6, viewed by me). The learner works through the named materials and steps in order, saying what each step does before doing the next one.

## Sub lesson 2 — Seam and stitch reference tables — doing it yourself
Now the learner does the whole task unaided: the appendix tables: seam kinds drawn and described (петельный, обмёточная, козлик, крестообразный, потайной…) with what each is used for; decorative stitches (шов «вперёд иголку», тамбурный); ribbon-embroidery and bead-embroidery stitch charts (Appendix Tables 3–6, viewed by me). Working from the first sub lesson's sequence, the learner plans the make, works through it step by step, and checks the finished piece against what the Kazakh book shows.
