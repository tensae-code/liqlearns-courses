# Hands-on — Analysing the variety of Nusantara craft (ragam kerajinan Nusantara)

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## Plan it first
Write the plan before you touch any material: what you will make or do for analysing the variety of nusantara craft (ragam kerajinan nusantara), the materials and tools you need, and the order of steps — the design stage (perancangan) the Indonesian books put before every production stage.

## Do the work and show it
Carry out the plan step by step, safely. Present the finished work with its packaging or label where the book asks for one, and say what you would improve next time.
