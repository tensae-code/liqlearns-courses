# Textile fibres — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What are regenerated fibres made from?
a) Cellulose — plant material dissolved and formed again into fibre b) Only animal hair c) Metal wire d) Nothing — they grow as fibres
**Answer: a) Cellulose — plant material dissolved and formed again into fibre**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** How do synthetic fibres differ from regenerated fibres?
a) Synthetic fibres are built from chemical building blocks; regenerated fibres start from cellulose b) They are exactly the same c) Synthetic fibres come from sheep d) Regenerated fibres are mined
**Answer: a) Synthetic fibres are built from chemical building blocks; regenerated fibres start from cellulose**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Which of these is an animal fibre?
a) Sheep's wool b) A synthetic fibre c) A regenerated cellulose fibre d) Glass fibre
**Answer: a) Sheep's wool**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which animal fibres are named beside sheep's wool in the Finnish cards?
a) Angora, mohair and cashmere b) Only silk thread c) Cotton and linen d) No others
**Answer: a) Angora, mohair and cashmere**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Which three questions do the Finnish material cards ask of every fibre?
a) Its properties, its life cycle, and its handling and care b) Its price, its colour and its shop c) Its weight only d) Its brand only
**Answer: a) Its properties, its life cycle, and its handling and care**
