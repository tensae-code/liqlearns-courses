# Textile fibres — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 3)** Why does knowing the fibre help you care for a fabric?
**Answer:** Each fibre family has its own handling and care needs, so the fibre tells you how to wash, dry and store the fabric so it lasts.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name three animal fibres from the lesson.
**Answer:** Sheep's wool, angora, mohair and cashmere — any three of these.
