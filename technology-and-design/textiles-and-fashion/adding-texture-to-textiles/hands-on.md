# Hands-on — Adding texture to textiles

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## Texture survey
Collect or examine four textiles. For each, record the texture in words and guess how it was made (woven in, threaded in, looped, printed). Check your guesses against the cloth's back.

## Add a texture band
On a small woven or paper-weave sample, thread soft material (yarn, strips, feathers if available) into one band. Describe how the band changed the piece's feel and look.
