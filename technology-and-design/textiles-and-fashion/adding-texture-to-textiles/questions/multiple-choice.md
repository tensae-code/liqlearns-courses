# Adding texture to textiles — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Texture in a textile is…
a) How the surface feels and catches light b) Only the colour c) The garment's size d) The price
**Answer: a) How the surface feels and catches light**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A raised, looped or laid-on surface changes…
a) Both the hand-feel and the look b) Nothing but the weight c) Only the label d) The fibre type
**Answer: a) Both the hand-feel and the look**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Feathers are added to a textile by…
a) Threading and weaving them in, secured in the planned direction b) Gluing them loosely on top c) Painting feather shapes d) Sewing the whole bird in
**Answer: a) Threading and weaving them in, secured in the planned direction**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A feather band gives a surface that…
a) No dye can imitate — real depth and movement b) Looks flat like print c) Fades in one wash by design d) Cannot be felt
**Answer: a) No dye can imitate — real depth and movement**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Fine-mat texture comes from…
a) Narrow strips woven very close b) Thick cords c) Paint d) Loose loops
**Answer: a) Narrow strips woven very close**
