# Adding texture to textiles — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Give two ways to add texture to a textile.
**Answer:** Answer: Any two of: threading/weaving feathers in, fine close weaving (fine mats), sinnet cord work laid in patterns (accept also loops/terry from the weaving lesson).

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** How does a fine mat's texture differ from sinnet texture?
**Answer:** Answer: The fine mat's texture is fineness itself — narrow strips woven close and even; sinnet texture is raised cord lines standing in relief above the surface.
