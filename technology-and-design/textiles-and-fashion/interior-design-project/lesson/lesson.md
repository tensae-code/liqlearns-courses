# Interior design project

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Kazakhstan's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Interior design project
Kazakhstan's books teach it this way (the student book, printed Section 1): Cultural identity and interior design: Kazakh craft, the yurt interior, felting/shi/kinusaiga experiments as design research. The learner works through the named materials and steps in order, saying what each step does before doing the next one.

## Sub lesson 2 — Interior design project — doing it yourself
Now the learner does the whole task unaided: cultural identity and interior design: Kazakh craft, the yurt interior, felting/shi/kinusaiga experiments as design research. Working from the first sub lesson's sequence, the learner plans the make, works through it step by step, and checks the finished piece against what the Kazakh book shows.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: Interior design project — cultural identity and interior design: Kazakh craft, the yurt interior, felting/shi/kinusaiga experiments as design research. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.

## Do It Yourself
1. Do the Do it first task again, carefully this time, using everything the lesson taught.
2. Check your work against the lesson's own checks — fix one thing you find.
3. Keep your work: it is practice evidence, and the next lesson builds on it.
