# Hands-on — Sewing tools and equipment

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## Measure truly
Measure the same cloth width three times, carefully. If the readings differ, find what moved — tape twisted, stretched, or read at an angle — and get three equal readings.

## Kit audit
Check a real sewing kit against the basic list. Note anything missing, damaged or stored unsafely, and fix the storage.
