# Lesson plan — Sewing tools and equipment

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Textiles and Fashion · **Lesson:** Sewing tools and equipment
**Place in the curriculum:** Textiles and Fashion (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Samoa expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** sewing begins with a small kit used well: scissors chosen for cloth and cared for, a tape measure for true sizes, and basic equipment kept clean, sharp and safe.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on student book evidence — Samoa's MESC textbooks (see lesson/sources.md). It counts in the acceptance count (student book, not evidence-only).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **Scissors — the cutting tool** — Dressmaker's scissors cut cloth; small scissors snip threads — and cloth scissors never cut paper.
2. **The tape measure** — Measuring is deciding: the tape measure gives the sizes every later step obeys.
3. **The basic kit and its care** — Needles, pins, thread and marking tools, each stored safely and kept in working order.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of Textiles and Fashion. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say which scissors cut cloth and how to care for them.
2. Use a tape measure correctly.
3. Name the basic kit and its storage rules.
## What you need
Scissors, a tape measure and a basic sewing kit to handle (sharps with care).
