# Sewing tools and equipment — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Give two care rules for cloth scissors.
**Answer:** Answer: Any two of: cut cloth only (never paper), wipe clean, store closed and dry, carry point-down or cased.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** List three things checked when measuring with a tape.
**Answer:** Answer: The tape is flat, straight and not stretched (and read at eye level).
