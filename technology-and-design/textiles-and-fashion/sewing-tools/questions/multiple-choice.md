# Sewing tools and equipment — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Cloth scissors should never cut paper because…
a) Paper blunts the blades meant for fabric b) Paper is too soft c) It is bad luck d) The scissors would change colour
**Answer: a) Paper blunts the blades meant for fabric**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** How should scissors be carried?
a) Closed, point down or in a case b) Open, points up c) Swinging by one handle d) In a pocket with pins
**Answer: a) Closed, point down or in a case**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A tape measure must be laid…
a) Flat and straight, not stretched b) In a loose coil c) Across a fold only d) Under the cloth
**Answer: a) Flat and straight, not stretched**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A measuring error matters because…
a) It is sewn into everything that follows b) It disappears later c) The tape corrects itself d) Sizes do not matter
**Answer: a) It is sewn into everything that follows**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Rusty pins are a problem because…
a) They can stain the cloth b) They sew faster c) They are stronger d) They measure better
**Answer: a) They can stain the cloth**
