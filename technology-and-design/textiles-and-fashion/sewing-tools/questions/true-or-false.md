# Sewing tools and equipment — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** One pair of scissors can serve for both cloth and paper with no harm.
**Answer: False** — Paper blunts cloth blades; keep a cloth-only pair.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The tape should be read at eye level.
**Answer: True** — Reading at eye level avoids a slanted, wrong reading.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Sharps should be put back in their place after use.
**Answer: True** — Lost needles and pins endanger people, especially feet.
