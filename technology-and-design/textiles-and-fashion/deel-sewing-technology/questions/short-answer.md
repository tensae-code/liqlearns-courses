# Deel sewing technology — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Say in one sentence what this lesson teaches.
**Answer:** Deel sewing technology — the cutting methods (straight, shaped, mixed), the sequence of making, frog fastenings (сампин) knotted step by step, and sewing on the loop-and-button with 0.5–0.6 cm stitches.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name one material, tool or step this lesson works with.
**Answer:** Any one named in the lesson text — for example, the first step of sub lesson 1: Deel sewing technology.
