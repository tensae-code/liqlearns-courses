# Hands-on — Deel sewing technology

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: the cutting methods (straight, shaped, mixed), the sequence of making, frog fastenings (сампин) knotted step by step, and sewing on the loop-and-button with 0.5–0.6 cm stitches. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
