# Hands-on — Cutting layouts and trim for the deel

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: calculating fabric length from the width (140 cm → 2.6–3 m; 70 cm → 5–6 m), the kinds of trim (эмжээр) and their width calculations, and the full pattern-piece layout with a numbered piece list. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
