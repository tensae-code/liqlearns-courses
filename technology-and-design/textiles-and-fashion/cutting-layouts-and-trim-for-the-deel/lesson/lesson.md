# Cutting layouts and trim for the deel

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Mongolia's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Cutting layouts and trim for the deel
Mongolia's book teaches it this way (*Design Drawing & Technology 11 (Mongolia)*, printed p. 108): calculating fabric length from the width (140 cm → 2.6–3 m; 70 cm → 5–6 m), the kinds of trim (эмжээр) and their width calculations, and the full pattern-piece layout with a numbered piece list. The learner works through the named materials and steps in order, saying what each step does before doing the next one.

## Sub lesson 2 — Cutting layouts and trim for the deel — doing it yourself
Now the learner does the whole task unaided: calculating fabric length from the width (140 cm → 2.6–3 m; 70 cm → 5–6 m), the kinds of trim (эмжээр) and their width calculations, and the full pattern-piece layout with a numbered piece list. Working from the first sub lesson's sequence, the learner plans the make, works through it step by step, and checks the finished piece against what Mongolia's book shows.
