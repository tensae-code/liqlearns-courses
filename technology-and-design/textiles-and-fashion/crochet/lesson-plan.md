# Lesson plan — Crochet

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Textiles and Fashion · **Lesson:** Crochet
**Place in the curriculum:** Textiles and Fashion (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Moldova expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** crochet is read as well as worked: materials and tools, the basic elements, and graphic schemes that are read like a score — then techniques worked in rows and ornamentally, for lace traditional and modern.
**This lesson's own level:** stocked from Moldova's *Educație tehnologică* (Technological Education) textbooks — see lesson/sources.md. Moldova counts in the acceptance count (student books, not evidence-only).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **Materials, basic elements and reading schemes** — Grade 4 crochet opens with materials and tools, the basic crochet elements — the stitches the hook makes — and reading graphic schemes: the drawn notation of crochet, read symbol by symbol.
2. **Techniques in rows and ornamentally — lace** — Grades 5–6 work the techniques out: crochet worked in rows and worked ornamentally, toward lace articles traditional and modern.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of Textiles and Fashion. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Work the basic crochet elements.
2. Read a graphic scheme and work crochet in rows and ornamentally.
3. Explain how Moldova's book teaches it.
## What you need
Crochet hooks, yarn, and printed graphic schemes.
