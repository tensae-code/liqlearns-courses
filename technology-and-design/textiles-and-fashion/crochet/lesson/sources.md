Cuba's own way: Cuba gives crochet (tejido a croché) a full Grade 9 chapter (Cap. 4): fibres, materials and tools, basic and decorative points, chart symbols, flat and round work, and patterns.

Moldova's own way: Moldova teaches crochet as a literate craft — graphic schemes read like notation from Grade 4 (with tablecloth border/frieze variants), then row and ornamental techniques toward traditional and modern lace.

Mongolia's own way: Mongolia teaches this inside Design Technology (Дизайн технологи) — the parts of the hook, choosing hook and yarn, the chain stitch and basic stitches, and the chart symbols (○ chain, × double crochet, † treble).
