# Crochet

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Moldova's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Materials, basic elements and reading schemes
Grade 4 crochet opens with materials and tools, the basic crochet elements — the stitches the hook makes — and reading graphic schemes: the drawn notation of crochet, read symbol by symbol. The tablecloth's border and frieze variants show why schemes matter: one scheme, many borders.

## Sub lesson 2 — Techniques in rows and ornamentally — lace
Grades 5–6 work the techniques out: crochet worked in rows and worked ornamentally, toward lace articles traditional and modern. The row builds the fabric; the ornamental work builds the lace.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: crochet is read as well as worked: materials and tools, the basic elements, and graphic schemes that are read like a score — then techniques worked in rows and ornamentally, for lace traditional and modern. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.

## Do It Yourself
1. Do the Do it first task again, carefully this time, using everything the lesson taught.
2. Check your work against the lesson's own checks — fix one thing you find.
3. Keep your work: it is practice evidence, and the next lesson builds on it.
