# Crochet — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What opens Grade 4 crochet?
a) Materials and tools, and the basic elements b) Lace history only c) Selling d) Knitting
**Answer: a) Materials and tools, and the basic elements**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is a graphic scheme in crochet?
a) A photograph b) The drawn notation of the work, read symbol by symbol c) A price list d) A song
**Answer: b) The drawn notation of the work, read symbol by symbol**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** What do the tablecloth variants show?
a) Border and frieze variants from schemes — one scheme, many borders b) That schemes are useless c) That borders are bought d) Nothing
**Answer: a) Border and frieze variants from schemes — one scheme, many borders**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** How are techniques worked in Grades 5–6?
a) In rows and ornamentally b) Only in circles c) Only by machine d) They are not worked
**Answer: a) In rows and ornamentally**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** What do the techniques build toward?
a) Only blankets b) Only toys c) Lace articles, traditional and modern d) Nothing wearable
**Answer: c) Lace articles, traditional and modern**
