# Crochet — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Crochet schemes are read symbol by symbol.
**Answer: True** — Graphic schemes are crochet's drawn notation.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Ornamental crochet and row crochet are the same work.
**Answer: False** — Techniques are worked in rows and ornamentally — two modes, fabric and lace.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Lace articles are both traditional and modern in the book.
**Answer: True** — The Grades 5–6 lesson surveys traditional and modern lace articles.
