# Crochet — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What are the basic elements and schemes for?
**Answer:** The elements are the hook's stitches; the graphic schemes are their drawn notation, read symbol by symbol to work the piece.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What two modes of technique does Grades 5–6 teach?
**Answer:** Crochet worked in rows, and crochet worked ornamentally — toward traditional and modern lace.
