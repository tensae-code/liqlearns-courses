# Hands-on — Designing Nusantara craft from local materials

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## Plan it first
Write the plan before you touch any material: what you will make or do for tools, materials and techniques for designing nusantara craft from local regional potential, ending in a product prototype, the materials and tools you need, and the order of steps — the design stage (perancangan) the Indonesian books put before every production stage.

## Do the work and show it
Carry out the plan step by step, safely. Present the finished work with its packaging or label where the book asks for one, and say what you would improve next time.
