# Felt making

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Kazakhstan's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Felt working
Kazakhstan's books teach it this way (the student book, printed Term 3 lessons): The tekemet (felt carpet) and felt work (Term 3 felt lessons). The learner works through the named materials and steps in order, saying what each step does before doing the next one.

## Sub lesson 2 — Felt-making technology
The Kazakh treatment continues and completes the topic: Kazakh felt and shi craft: текемет/сырмақ/сырдақ/түскиіз; dry and wet felting; felted beads; preparing materials and tools (§§1–4). The learner finishes the make, checks it against the book's own sequence, and fixes one thing before presenting the work.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: Felt making — the tekemet (felt carpet) and felt work (Term 3 felt lessons). Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.

## Do It Yourself
1. Do the Do it first task again, carefully this time, using everything the lesson taught.
2. Check your work against the lesson's own checks — fix one thing you find.
3. Keep your work: it is practice evidence, and the next lesson builds on it.
