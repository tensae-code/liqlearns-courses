# Flat weaving — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Warp is the threads held stretched and still in weaving.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Weft has nothing to do with this lesson.
**Answer: False** — It is part of the lesson: the thread carried over and under across the warp.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Shirazeh (شیرازه) is the binding worked round a weaving's edge to protect and finish it.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.
