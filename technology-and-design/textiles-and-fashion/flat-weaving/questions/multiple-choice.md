# Flat weaving — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is warp?
a) the threads held stretched and still in weaving b) Only a price c) Only decoration d) Nothing to do with this lesson
**Answer: a) the threads held stretched and still in weaving**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is weft?
a) Only history b) the thread carried over and under across the warp c) Only storage d) A kind of glue
**Answer: b) the thread carried over and under across the warp**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is shirazeh (شیرازه)?
a) Only old tools b) Only painting c) the binding worked round a weaving's edge to protect and finish it d) Only a price
**Answer: c) the binding worked round a weaving's edge to protect and finish it**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson teach, in one line?
a) Only decoration b) Nothing to do with this lesson c) Only history d) flat weaving interlaces warp and weft on straw: over, under, row by row — until a woven bag stands finished with two-colour work, hidden thread ends, a bound edge (شیرازه) and a strap.
**Answer: d) flat weaving interlaces warp and weft on straw: over, under, row by row — until a woven bag stands finished with two-colour work, hidden thread ends, a bound edge (شیرازه) and a strap.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** How does Iran's book teach this?
a) As theory only, never practised b) Only in a glossary c) Only as an exam d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Persian
**Answer: d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Persian**
