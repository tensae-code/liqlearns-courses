# Flat weaving — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In your own words, what is warp?
**Answer:** The threads held stretched and still in weaving.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In your own words, what is shirazeh (شیرازه)?
**Answer:** The binding worked round a weaving's edge to protect and finish it.
