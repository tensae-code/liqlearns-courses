# Flat weaving

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Iran's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Warp and weft on straw
Every weaving stands on two thread sets: the warp, held stretched and still, and the weft, carried over and under across it. Iran's Grade 8 handicrafts module weaves on straw: the flat woven base grows row by row, and two-colour work patterns it by changing the weft's colour to a planned design.

## Sub lesson 2 — Finishing the bag
A weaving is finished by its edges: thread ends are hidden inside the work so nothing unravels or shows, the edge is bound with شیرازه (shirazeh) binding to protect it, and a strap is added to carry the bag. The full make sequence — weave, colour, hide, bind, strap — turns a flat woven piece into a usable bag.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: flat weaving interlaces warp and weft on straw: over, under, row by row — until a woven bag stands finished with two-colour work, hidden thread ends, a bound edge (شیرازه) and a strap. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
