# Lesson plan — Flat weaving

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Textiles and Fashion · **Lesson:** Flat weaving
**Place in the curriculum:** Textiles and Fashion (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Iran expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** flat weaving interlaces warp and weft on straw: over, under, row by row — until a woven bag stands finished with two-colour work, hidden thread ends, a bound edge (شیرازه) and a strap.
**This lesson's own level:** stocked from Iran's *Work & Technology, Grade 8* (student book) — see lesson/sources.md. Iran counts in the acceptance count (student books, not evidence-only).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **Warp and weft on straw** — Every weaving stands on two thread sets: the warp, held stretched and still, and the weft, carried over and under across it.
2. **Finishing the bag** — A weaving is finished by its edges: thread ends are hidden inside the work so nothing unravels or shows, the edge is bound with شیرازه (shirazeh) binding to protect it, and a strap is added to carry the bag.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
