# Hands-on — Textile mosaic (kurak) technology

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: designing a sewing product in the textile-mosaic technique: choosing and preparing the material, the sequence of the work (§17). Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
