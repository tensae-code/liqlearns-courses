# Factors influencing consumer selection of textiles and garments

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Papua New Guinea's guide teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Factors influencing consumer selection of textiles and garments
Papua New Guinea's guides teach it this way (the teacher guide — evidence, printed p. 46): Social and economic factors; Cultural and environmental factors; Technological factors; also: Climatic and Occasional Factors; Age and Profession Factors. The learner works through the named materials and steps in order, saying what each step does before doing the next one.

## Sub lesson 2 — Factors influencing consumer selection of textiles and garments — doing it yourself
Now the learner does the whole task unaided: Social and economic factors; Cultural and environmental factors; Technological factors; also: Climatic and Occasional Factors; Age and Profession Factors. Working from the first sub lesson's sequence, the learner plans the make, works through it step by step, and checks the finished piece against what the PNG guide shows.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: Factors influencing consumer selection of textiles and garments — Social and economic factors; Cultural and environmental factors; Technological factors; also: Climatic and Occasional Factors; Age and Profession Factors. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
