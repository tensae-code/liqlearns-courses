# Lesson plan — Colour theory for textile design

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Textiles and Fashion · **Lesson:** Colour theory for textile design
**Place in the curriculum:** Textiles and Fashion (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Samoa expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** colour choice in textiles is designed, not guessed: the colour wheel organises hues into schemes, and colours carry psychological and cultural effects and optical illusions that drive clothing and textile decisions.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on student book evidence — Samoa's MESC textbooks (see lesson/sources.md). It counts in the acceptance count (student book, not evidence-only).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **The colour wheel and schemes** — The wheel arranges colours by relationship; schemes are tested combinations picked from it.
2. **What colours do to people** — Colours carry psychological and cultural meanings that change how a garment is read.
3. **Optical illusions of colour** — Colours change each other: size, distance and even shape seem to shift with the colours around them.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of Textiles and Fashion. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Use the colour wheel to build a scheme.
2. Describe psychological and cultural effects of colour.
3. Give one optical illusion colours create.
## What you need
Coloured papers or cloths and a colour wheel picture if available.
