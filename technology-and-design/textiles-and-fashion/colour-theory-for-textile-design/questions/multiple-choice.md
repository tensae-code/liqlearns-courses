# Colour theory for textile design — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The colour wheel organises colours by…
a) Their relationships — neighbours and opposites b) Their price c) Their weight d) Alphabetical order
**Answer: a) Their relationships — neighbours and opposites**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Colours opposite on the wheel give a scheme that is…
a) Bold and striking b) Always calm c) Invisible d) Forbidden
**Answer: a) Bold and striking**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A colour's meaning can change with…
a) Culture and occasion b) Nothing ever c) The time of day only d) The fabric's weight
**Answer: a) Culture and occasion**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Designers choose colours for clothing in order to…
a) Use their psychological and cultural effects on purpose b) Avoid all effects c) Copy other garments blindly d) Save dye only
**Answer: a) Use their psychological and cultural effects on purpose**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** The same grey looks lighter on a dark background because of…
a) An optical illusion of colour contrast b) A change in the paper c) Dim light only d) A printing error
**Answer: a) An optical illusion of colour contrast**
