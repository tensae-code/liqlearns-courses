# Colour theory for textile design — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Build a calm scheme and a bold scheme from the wheel, in words.
**Answer:** Answer: Calm — neighbouring hues (e.g. blue with blue-green and green); bold — opposites (e.g. blue with orange).

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Give one way a designer uses a colour illusion on clothing.
**Answer:** Answer: For example, vertical darker side panels slim the figure, or a light colour on dark ground is made to advance and read at a distance.
