# Colour theory for textile design — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A colour scheme is a deliberate combination picked from the wheel.
**Answer: True** — Schemes are planned, not guessed.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A colour means the same thing in every culture and occasion.
**Answer: False** — Colour readings differ by culture and occasion.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Stripes can make a figure look wider or slimmer.
**Answer: True** — Colour and pattern create optical illusions designers use.
