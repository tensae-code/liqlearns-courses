# Hands-on — Colour theory for textile design

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## Two schemes, one pattern
Draw one simple pattern twice. Colour one in neighbouring hues, the other in opposites. Write one line on how each version would suit a different occasion.

## Illusion test
Place the same mid-grey paper strip on black and on white. Describe the difference you see, then explain it as an optical illusion of colour.
