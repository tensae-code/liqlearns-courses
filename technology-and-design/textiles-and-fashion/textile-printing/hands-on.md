# Hands-on — Textile printing

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## Paper stencil print
Design a simple motif, transfer it to card, cut it (with supervision) and print a row of repeats on paper or scrap cloth. Judge the cleanest print and say which step decided it.

## Choose the method
Write a one-paragraph design brief for printing a school-cloth pattern: name the method (stencil, screen or roller) and defend the choice by colours, repeats and run size.
