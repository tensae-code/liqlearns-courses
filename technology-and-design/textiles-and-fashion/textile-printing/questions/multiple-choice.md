# Textile printing — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Stencil printing's steps in order are…
a) Design, transfer to plastic or card, cut, print b) Cut, guess, print, design c) Print, cut, transfer, design d) Design, print, cut, transfer
**Answer: a) Design, transfer to plastic or card, cut, print**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In a stencil, colour reaches the cloth…
a) Only where the mask is cut away b) Everywhere under the sheet c) Where the card is thickest d) After the card is removed and washed
**Answer: a) Only where the mask is cut away**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Screen printing pushes colour through…
a) A prepared mesh, one colour per screen b) A solid sheet c) A carved roller only d) The cloth's own holes
**Answer: a) A prepared mesh, one colour per screen**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Roller printing suits…
a) Factories and long runs, printing continuously b) One single T-shirt c) Hand painting d) Cutting stencils
**Answer: a) Factories and long runs, printing continuously**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** A printing design brief must name…
a) The product, the design and the chosen method, with reasons b) Only the colour c) Only the price d) The printer's name only
**Answer: a) The product, the design and the chosen method, with reasons**
