# Textile printing — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why must the design come before the cutting?
**Answer:** Answer: The stencil only passes what the design decides; cutting follows the transferred design, so a design fault is cut permanently into the mask.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** A run of 500 identical cloths is needed. Which method and why?
**Answer:** Answer: Roller printing (factory) or screen printing for a workshop run — both repeat exactly at speed; a hand-cut stencil suits short runs and teaching, not hundreds of repeats.
