# Threading the sewing machine — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Say in one sentence what this lesson teaches.
**Answer:** Threading the sewing machine — winding the bobbin, threading the lower thread and the upper thread through guides and tension discs, and adjusting stitch length.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name one material, tool or step this lesson works with.
**Answer:** Any one named in the lesson text — for example, the first step of sub lesson 1: Threading the sewing machine.
