# Hands-on — Threading the sewing machine

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: winding the bobbin, threading the lower thread and the upper thread through guides and tension discs, and adjusting stitch length. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
