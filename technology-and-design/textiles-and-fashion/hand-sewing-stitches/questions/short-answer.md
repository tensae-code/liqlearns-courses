# Hand sewing stitches — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Describe how to hem a straight edge.
**Answer:** Answer: Fold the raw edge under, pin or press it flat, then take small slanting stitches catching a thread or two of the garment and a bite of the fold, keeping them close and even.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Why does a curved hem pucker if sewn flat like a straight one?
**Answer:** Answer: The fold carries more cloth than the edge it joins; without easing, the extra fullness bunches into puckers.
