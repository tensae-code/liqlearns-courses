# Hand sewing stitches — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A knot starts a line of hand sewing and holds the thread's end.
**Answer: True** — The knot anchors the first stitch.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Good hemming shows big stitches on the right side.
**Answer: False** — Good hemming is nearly invisible from the right side.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** If a curved hem is not eased it will pucker and twist.
**Answer: True** — The extra fullness has nowhere to go.
