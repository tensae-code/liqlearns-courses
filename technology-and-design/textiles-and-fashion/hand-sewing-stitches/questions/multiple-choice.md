# Hand sewing stitches — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A running stitch goes…
a) In and out of the cloth in an even row b) Only on the surface c) Around the edge only d) Through one layer only
**Answer: a) In and out of the cloth in an even row**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Evenness in a running stitch means…
a) Stitches of equal length and spacing b) The longest stitches possible c) Stitches of any length d) One stitch only
**Answer: a) Stitches of equal length and spacing**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A hem's job is to…
a) Fold the raw edge under and hold it so it cannot fray b) Decorate the middle of the cloth c) Join two separate garments d) Cut the edge neatly
**Answer: a) Fold the raw edge under and hold it so it cannot fray**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In close hemming the stitches are…
a) Small, close and slanting, catching very little of the outer cloth b) Large and loose c) On the right side, proudly showing d) Made with a machine
**Answer: a) Small, close and slanting, catching very little of the outer cloth**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** A curved hem must be eased because…
a) The folded edge is fuller than the line it joins b) Curves are always longer garments c) The thread is curved d) Hems cannot be pinned
**Answer: a) The folded edge is fuller than the line it joins**
