# Hands-on — Hand sewing stitches

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## Even row
Sew a 10 cm running-stitch line on scrap cloth. Measure your stitches: aim for equal lengths. Unpick and redo until the row is even.

## Hem a curve
Cut a curved scrap edge, fold a narrow hem, ease the fullness and hem it. Check the hem lies flat with no pucker.
