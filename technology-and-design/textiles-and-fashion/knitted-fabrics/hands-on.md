# Hands-on — Knitted fabrics

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## Stretch and record
Measure the width of a knitted and a woven sample, stretch each gently, measure again, then release and measure a third time. Record how each behaved.

## Jersey faces
Turn a single-jersey garment inside out. Describe both faces and decide which is single and which would be double jersey by feel against a thicker knitted item.
