# Knitted fabrics — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Knitted cloth is built from…
a) Loops of yarn pulled through loops b) Interlaced warp and weft c) Glued fibres d) Pressed sheets
**Answer: a) Loops of yarn pulled through loops**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Knitted cloth stretches because…
a) Its loops change shape under pull b) It has no yarn c) It is wet d) It is woven tightly
**Answer: a) Its loops change shape under pull**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** In weft knitting, the loops form…
a) Across the fabric from one travelling yarn b) Only lengthwise c) In the warp beam d) Not at all
**Answer: a) Across the fabric from one travelling yarn**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Double jersey is…
a) Two interlocked knitted layers, firmer than single jersey b) A woven cloth c) A single thread d) An unknitted yarn
**Answer: a) Two interlocked knitted layers, firmer than single jersey**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** A cut woven edge tends to…
a) Fray b) Ladder loop by loop c) Melt d) Stretch double
**Answer: a) Fray**
