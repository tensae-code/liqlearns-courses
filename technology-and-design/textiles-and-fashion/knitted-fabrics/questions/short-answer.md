# Knitted fabrics — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Tell weft knitting from warp knitting.
**Answer:** Answer: Weft knitting forms loops across the fabric from a travelling yarn; warp knitting forms loops along the length from many yarns at once.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** A sports shirt should stretch with the body. Woven or knit — and why?
**Answer:** Answer: Knit — its loops change shape under pull and spring back, so it moves with the wearer.
