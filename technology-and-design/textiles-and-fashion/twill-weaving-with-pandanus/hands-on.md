# Hands-on — Twill weaving with pandanus

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## Work it out first
Plan a small woven square (say 20 cm). Write down the finished size, then work out and record the anchor-cord lengths you would need, with your allowances stated.

## Paper twill
Weave a small paper-strip sample in twill pattern between two string anchor cords. Mark where the stepping went wrong, if it did, and what it did to the diagonal.
