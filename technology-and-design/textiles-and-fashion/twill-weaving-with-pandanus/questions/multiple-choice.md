# Twill weaving with pandanus — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Pandanus strips must be prepared so they…
a) Lie flat and even b) Stay curled c) Keep their thorns d) Dry mid-weaving
**Answer: a) Lie flat and even**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Uneven strips produce…
a) Uneven weaving b) Stronger cloth c) Faster work d) No effect
**Answer: a) Uneven weaving**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The anchor cords' lengths are worked out from…
a) The finished size of the piece b) The weaver's height c) The leaf's length only d) Guesswork
**Answer: a) The finished size of the piece**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Planning size-first, materials-second is…
a) The habit this lesson teaches b) A waste of time c) Only for factories d) Forbidden in weaving
**Answer: a) The habit this lesson teaches**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Twill's diagonal appears when…
a) Every row steps the same regular way b) Rows step randomly c) Only two strips are used d) The cords are removed
**Answer: a) Every row steps the same regular way**
