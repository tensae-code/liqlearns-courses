# Twill weaving with pandanus — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Why are cord lengths calculated instead of cut by eye?
**Answer:** Answer: The cords must fit the finished size with working allowance; a cord cut short cannot be lengthened once weaving starts, so the size decides the lengths in advance.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Your twill diagonal wobbles halfway up. What went wrong and how do you fix the next piece?
**Answer:** Answer: A row stepped irregularly; unpick to the fault or restart the pattern count, keeping each row's step identical and checking against the previous row.
