# Twill weaving with pandanus — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Weaving can begin before the finished size is known, with no loss.
**Answer: False** — The anchor cords cannot be cut rightly until the size is set.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Material preparation is half the weaving.
**Answer: True** — Even strips are a precondition of even cloth.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** One irregular row leaves the twill diagonal unharmed.
**Answer: False** — The diagonal depends on every row stepping alike.
