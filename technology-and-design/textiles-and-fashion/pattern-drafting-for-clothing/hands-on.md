# Hands-on — Pattern drafting for clothing

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: what a pattern (выкройка) is: tracing it onto felt, cutting, joining the shoulders — a kamizol made to a drawn pattern (Lesson 11). Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
