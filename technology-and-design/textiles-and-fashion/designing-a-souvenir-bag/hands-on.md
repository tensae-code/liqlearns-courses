# Hands-on — Designing a souvenir bag

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: the dimensioned pattern (33.5 cm wide, R15 curve), checking the draft with tangent-blend geometry, and planning cost and decoration with Mongolian ornament. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
