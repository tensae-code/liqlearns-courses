# Hands-on — Modelling a sewn product with a set-in sleeve

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: the features of modelling a sewn product with a втачной рукав (one-piece/set-in sleeve drafting) (§15). Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
