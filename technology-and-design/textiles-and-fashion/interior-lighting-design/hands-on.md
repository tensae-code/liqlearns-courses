# Hands-on — Interior lighting design

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: lighting in the interior, natural and artificial (§19); developing a lighting project for an interior (§20); zoning and planning an interior (§21). Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
