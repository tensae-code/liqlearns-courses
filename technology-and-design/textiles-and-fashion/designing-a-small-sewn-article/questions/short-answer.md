# Designing a small sewn article — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Say in one sentence what this lesson teaches.
**Answer:** Designing a small sewn article — a key-ring fob designed through a step table — draw 3–5 variants, make the pattern, cut the parts, sew in order, add a 12 cm × 1 cm strap.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name one material, tool or step this lesson works with.
**Answer:** Any one named in the lesson text — for example, the first step of sub lesson 1: Designing a small sewn article.
