# Hands-on — Designing a small sewn article

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: a key-ring fob designed through a step table — draw 3–5 variants, make the pattern, cut the parts, sew in order, add a 12 cm × 1 cm strap. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
