# Sewing on a button — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Say in one sentence what this lesson teaches.
**Answer:** Sewing on a button — marking the place, knotting, sewing two-hole and four-hole buttons (parallel, cross and square patterns) and shank buttons.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name one material, tool or step this lesson works with.
**Answer:** Any one named in the lesson text — for example, the first step of sub lesson 1: Sewing on a button.
