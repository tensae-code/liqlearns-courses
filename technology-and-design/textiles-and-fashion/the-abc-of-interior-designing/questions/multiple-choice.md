# The ABC of interior designing — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What are the ABC's three basics?
a) The interior plan, the measured survey (releveu) and the design concept b) Paint, paper, glue c) Price, shop, date d) Nothing is listed
**Answer: a) The interior plan, the measured survey (releveu) and the design concept**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is a releveu?
a) A furniture list b) The measured survey of the existing space c) A colour chart d) A receipt
**Answer: b) The measured survey of the existing space**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Why survey before designing?
a) So the design starts from the space as it truly is, not from guesses b) To delay work c) For the archive only d) It is optional
**Answer: a) So the design starts from the space as it truly is, not from guesses**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** How are spaces arranged?
a) Organised on their functionalities b) By size only c) By colour only d) At random
**Answer: a) Organised on their functionalities**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the design concept do?
a) Buys the furniture b) Paints the walls c) Organises the new interior — the idea the plan follows d) Nothing
**Answer: c) Organises the new interior — the idea the plan follows**
