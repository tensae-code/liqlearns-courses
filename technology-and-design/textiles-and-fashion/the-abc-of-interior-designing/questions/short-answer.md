# The ABC of interior designing — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the three basics of the ABC.
**Answer:** The interior plan, the measured survey (releveu) of the existing space, and the design concept.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How are spaces arranged in the ABC?
**Answer:** By organising them on their functionalities — each space placed and shaped by what it is for.
