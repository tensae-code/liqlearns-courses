# The ABC of interior designing — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The releveu measures the existing space truly.
**Answer: True** — It is the measured survey — the design's honest start.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Spaces are arranged by functionality — what each space is for.
**Answer: True** — Organising spaces on their functionalities is the ABC's arranging rule.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A design concept is drawn after the furniture is bought.
**Answer: False** — Plan and concept follow the survey and govern what is bought and placed.
