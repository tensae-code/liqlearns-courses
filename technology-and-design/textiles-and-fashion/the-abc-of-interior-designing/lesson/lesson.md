# The ABC of interior designing

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Moldova's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — The measured survey — releveu
The ABC begins with what exists: the measured survey (releveu) of the existing space — the room measured truly and drawn as it is. No interior is designed from guesses; the releveu is the design's honest starting document.

## Sub lesson 2 — Plan, concept, functionalities
From the survey come the interior plan and the design concept — the idea that organises the new interior. Arranging is done by organising spaces on their functionalities: each space is placed and shaped by what it is for.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: the ABC of interior designing is three basics: the interior plan, the measured survey (releveu) of the existing space, and the design concept — arranging by organising spaces on their functionalities. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
