# Taking body measurements for a skirt — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Say in one sentence what this lesson teaches.
**Answer:** Taking body measurements for a skirt — waist, hip and seat height, recording them with letter codes, and setting up the pattern grid at scale M1:4.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name one material, tool or step this lesson works with.
**Answer:** Any one named in the lesson text — for example, the first step of sub lesson 1: Taking body measurements for a skirt.
