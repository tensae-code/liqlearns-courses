# Hands-on — Hand-stitch reference charts

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: the appendix tables of hand stitches and their uses (basting, hemming, ribbon and bead embroidery stitches), every stitch drawn (Appendix, Tables 3–6). Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
