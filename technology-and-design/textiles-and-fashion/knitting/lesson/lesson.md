# Knitting

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Moldova's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Materials, tools and casting on
The knitting modules open with materials and tools, then the basic elements: casting on — putting the first stitches on the needle — and the first stitches themselves. Grades 5–6 add knitting patterns and models: the written and pictured forms a knitted article is worked from.

## Sub lesson 2 — Knit, purl, edges and casting off
Grade 4 works the stitches step by step: knit stitch and purl stitch — the pair from which knitted fabric is built — the edge stitches that keep the sides true, and casting off, the finishing that frees the work from the needles without unravelling it.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: knitting is two stitches and their edges: cast on, knit and purl worked step by step, edge stitches kept, and cast off to finish — with materials, tools and patterns/models to read. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.

## Do It Yourself
1. Do the Do it first task again, carefully this time, using everything the lesson taught.
2. Check your work against the lesson's own checks — fix one thing you find.
3. Keep your work: it is practice evidence, and the next lesson builds on it.
