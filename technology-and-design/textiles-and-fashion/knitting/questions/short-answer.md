# Knitting — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the knitting sequence from start to finish.
**Answer:** Cast on, work knit and purl stitches with edge stitches kept, and cast off.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What are knitting patterns/models?
**Answer:** The written and pictured forms from which a knitted article is worked.
