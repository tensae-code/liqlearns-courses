# Knitting — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What puts the first stitches on the needle?
a) Casting on b) Casting off c) Purling d) Sewing
**Answer: a) Casting on**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which two stitches build knitted fabric?
a) Tighel and ramurică b) Knit and purl c) Chain and slip d) Cut and glue
**Answer: b) Knit and purl**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** What do edge stitches do?
a) Keep the sides of the work true b) Decorate the middle c) Join pieces d) Dye the yarn
**Answer: a) Keep the sides of the work true**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** What finishes the work?
a) Casting off b) Casting on again c) Cutting the yarn only d) Nothing — it stays on the needles
**Answer: a) Casting off**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** What do patterns/models give the knitter?
a) The yarn's price b) The shop's address c) The written and pictured forms the article is worked from d) Nothing useful
**Answer: c) The written and pictured forms the article is worked from**
