# Knitting — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Knit and purl are the stitch pair knitting is built from.
**Answer: True** — Grade 4 works them step by step.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Casting off is how knitting begins.
**Answer: False** — Casting on begins; casting off finishes and frees the work.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Edge stitches are decorative extras that may be dropped.
**Answer: False** — Edge stitches keep the sides of the work true.
