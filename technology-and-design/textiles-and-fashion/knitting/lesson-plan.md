# Lesson plan — Knitting

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Textiles and Fashion · **Lesson:** Knitting
**Place in the curriculum:** Textiles and Fashion (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Moldova expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** knitting is two stitches and their edges: cast on, knit and purl worked step by step, edge stitches kept, and cast off to finish — with materials, tools and patterns/models to read.
**This lesson's own level:** stocked from Moldova's *Educație tehnologică* (Technological Education) textbooks — see lesson/sources.md. Moldova counts in the acceptance count (student books, not evidence-only).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **Materials, tools and casting on** — The knitting modules open with materials and tools, then the basic elements: casting on — putting the first stitches on the needle — and the first stitches themselves.
2. **Knit, purl, edges and casting off** — Grade 4 works the stitches step by step: knit stitch and purl stitch — the pair from which knitted fabric is built — the edge stitches that keep the sides true, and casting off, the finishing that frees the work from the needles without unravelling it..

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of Textiles and Fashion. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Cast on, work knit and purl stitches, keep edge stitches, and cast off.
2. Read knitting patterns/models.
3. Explain how Moldova's book teaches it.
## What you need
Knitting needles, yarn, and a patterns/models page to read.
