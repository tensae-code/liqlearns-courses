# Properties of fibres, yarns and fabrics — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Define tensile strength in one sentence.
**Answer:** Answer: Tensile strength is how much pulling force a material can stand before it breaks.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** A fibre is strong but very uneven. Why might a manufacturer reject it?
**Answer:** Answer: It would fail in making — spinning and weaving unevenly and breaking in the machine — even though the finished material might have been strong.
