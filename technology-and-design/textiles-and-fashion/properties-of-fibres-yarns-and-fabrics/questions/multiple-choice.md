# Properties of fibres, yarns and fabrics — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is a yarn?
a) Fibres twisted together b) A single fibre c) A finished garment d) A weaving machine
**Answer: a) Fibres twisted together**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is a fabric made from?
a) Yarns woven or knitted together b) Loose seeds c) Raw bolls only d) Dye
**Answer: a) Yarns woven or knitted together**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Tensile strength means…
a) How much pulling a material stands before breaking b) How soft a material feels c) How bright its colour is d) How fast it dries
**Answer: a) How much pulling a material stands before breaking**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** How is tensile strength tested?
a) By pulling the material until it breaks b) By washing it c) By ironing it d) By folding it
**Answer: a) By pulling the material until it breaks**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Besides strength, a manufacturer also checks…
a) Evenness, length and fineness of the fibre b) Only the price of the label c) The colour of the shop d) Nothing else
**Answer: a) Evenness, length and fineness of the fibre**
