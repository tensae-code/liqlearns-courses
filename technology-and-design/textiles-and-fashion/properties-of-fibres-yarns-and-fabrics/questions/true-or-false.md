# Properties of fibres, yarns and fabrics — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A fabric's strength can come from its fibres, its yarn twist, or its structure.
**Answer: True** — The three levels multiply together.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The strongest fibre is always the best choice for every product.
**Answer: False** — The whole set of qualities must match the product.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Tensile strength is measured by pulling.
**Answer: True** — The pull test is the test of tensile strength.
