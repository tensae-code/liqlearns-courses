# Machine sewing

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Iran's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Safety and first stitching
The sewing machine's needle moves faster than any hand can react, so its safety rules are strict: fingers clear of the needle's path, attention on the seam, machine stopped before adjustments. Iran's Grade 7 module runs the sequence in order — safety with the machine, ironing the fabric ready, then simple machine stitching on practice pieces before any real garment.

## Sub lesson 2 — The pillowcase with French seams
The module's project is a pillowcase. A French seam encloses the raw edges inside the seam itself: sew wrong sides together, trim, turn, and sew again — the edge is finished and cannot fray. The pillowcase is small enough to finish and complete enough to teach measuring, cutting, machine stitching, seaming and pressing in one make.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: machine sewing joins the machine's speed to the sewer's control: safety with the machine first, then ironing, simple machine stitching, and a complete make — a pillowcase with French seams. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.

## Do It Yourself
1. Do the Do it first task again, carefully this time, using everything the lesson taught.
2. Check your work against the lesson's own checks — fix one thing you find.
3. Keep your work: it is practice evidence, and the next lesson builds on it.
