# Hands-on — From material to product

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## Read two yarns
Take two yarns or threads from different items. For each, write: its likely material family, how its spinning shows in thickness and surface, and one product it would suit. Say which question — material or spinning — explains the biggest difference between them.

## Draft to save material
On paper, draw a rectangle as your fabric. Draft pieces for a simple product — a small bag: two rectangles and a strap — and arrange them inside the fabric rectangle to leave the smallest leftover. Measure and write how much of your 'fabric' is used and how much is left.
