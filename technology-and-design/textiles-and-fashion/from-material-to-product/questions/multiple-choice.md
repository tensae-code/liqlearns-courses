# From material to product — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What decides much of a yarn's behaviour?
a) Its material — the fibre it is made from b) Only its colour name c) The shop it was bought in d) Nothing
**Answer: a) Its material — the fibre it is made from**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Can two yarns of the same material be different?
a) Yes — the spinning method changes thickness, smoothness, strength and surface b) No — material is the only thing that matters c) Only if they are different colours d) Never
**Answer: a) Yes — the spinning method changes thickness, smoothness, strength and surface**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What two questions do you ask when reading a yarn?
a) What is it made of, and how was it spun? b) How much does it cost, and who bought it? c) Is it new, and is it shiny? d) Where is it stored, and when?
**Answer: a) What is it made of, and how was it spun?**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** What is kaavoitus?
a) Pattern drafting — drawing the product's pieces carefully before cutting b) A kind of yarn c) A dyeing method d) A sewing stitch
**Answer: a) Pattern drafting — drawing the product's pieces carefully before cutting**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Why draft a pattern carefully before cutting?
a) So pieces fit the material economically, with little waste b) To use more material c) To avoid measuring d) It is not needed
**Answer: a) So pieces fit the material economically, with little waste**
