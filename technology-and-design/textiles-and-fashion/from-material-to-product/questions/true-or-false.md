# From material to product — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A yarn is fibres spun together into a thread.
**Answer: True** — That is what a yarn is.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Spinning method makes no difference if the material is the same.
**Answer: False** — Spinning changes a yarn's thickness, smoothness, strength and surface.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Careful pattern drafting can reduce cutting waste.
**Answer: True** — A checked draft laid out closely wastes little material.
