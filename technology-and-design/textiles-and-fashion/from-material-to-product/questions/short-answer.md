# From material to product — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 3)** How does economical cutting help the product's whole life cycle?
**Answer:** It saves material and money at the making stage and leaves less waste, lowering the cost counted across the craft process.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Give one way a yarn's material shows in the finished product.
**Answer:** Its feel, stretch, dye-taking or wearing behaviour carries into the product (accept any one property named in the lesson).
