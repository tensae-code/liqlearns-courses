# Hands-on — Knitting technology

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: knitting with needles: kinds of loops; knitted products made (scarf, stand, phone case) (§§15–16). Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
