# Hands-on — Textile design-and-make sequence

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: developing a product from textile materials (author's doll, soft toy): idea and sketch; investigating textile kinds, properties and colour; choosing and preparing materials, tools and equipment (§§17–19). Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
