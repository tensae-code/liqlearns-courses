# Hands-on — Identifying fibres by experiment

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: comparing wool, silk, cotton and flax samples, and the burn test — how each fibre burns, smells and what ash it leaves. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
