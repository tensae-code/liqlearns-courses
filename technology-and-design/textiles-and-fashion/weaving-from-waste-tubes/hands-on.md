# Hands-on — Weaving from waste tubes

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: a basket woven from newspaper tubes; a trivet woven from paper tubes; a bicycle built from paper tubes (Section 6–7 lessons). Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
