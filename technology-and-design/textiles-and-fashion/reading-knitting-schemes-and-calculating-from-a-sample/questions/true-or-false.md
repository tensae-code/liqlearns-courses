# Reading knitting schemes and calculating from a sample — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Stitch counts for an article are calculated from a measured sample.
**Answer: True** — Sample, tape measure, calculation — in that order.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A gauge sample is a waste of yarn and time.
**Answer: False** — It carries the measure the whole article's calculation stands on.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Circular knitted objects are among the module's techniques.
**Answer: True** — The lesson joins schemes and calculation to knitting techniques and circular objects.
