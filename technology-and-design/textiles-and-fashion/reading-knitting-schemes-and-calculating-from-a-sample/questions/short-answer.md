# Reading knitting schemes and calculating from a sample — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Give the calculating order.
**Answer:** Knit a gauge sample, measure its width with the tape, then calculate the stitches for the article's size.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why not guess the stitch count?
**Answer:** Because the sample's measure — your hands, yarn and needles — is what makes the article come out its intended size.
