# Reading knitting schemes and calculating from a sample — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is knitted before the article?
a) A gauge sample b) The article itself, twice c) A scarf d) Nothing
**Answer: a) A gauge sample**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** How is the sample measured?
a) By weight b) Its width, with the tape c) By eye only d) By colour
**Answer: b) Its width, with the tape**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the sample tell?
a) How many stitches make a given width in your hands, yarn and needles b) The price c) The fashion d) Nothing
**Answer: a) How many stitches make a given width in your hands, yarn and needles**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** What is calculated from the sample?
a) The stitches for the article's size b) The knitting time only c) The yarn colour d) The button count
**Answer: a) The stitches for the article's size**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** What are scheme tehnologice?
a) Advertisements b) Songs c) Technological schemes — the knitting's written working documents d) Price lists
**Answer: c) Technological schemes — the knitting's written working documents**
