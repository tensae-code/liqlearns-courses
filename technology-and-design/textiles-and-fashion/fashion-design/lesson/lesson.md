# Fashion design

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Malaysia's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — What fashion design is
Fashion design is the design of clothing and how it is worn — a design field with its own definition, taught inside RBT as a full strand, not a hobby corner.

## Sub lesson 2 — The types of fashion design
The field divides into types, each designing for a different wearer, occasion and way of making. Naming the types lets a learner place any garment — and any fashion ambition — on the map.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: fashion design is a defined design field with named types — each type designs for a different wearer and occasion. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
