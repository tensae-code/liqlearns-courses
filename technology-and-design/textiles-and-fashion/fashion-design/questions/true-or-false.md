# Fashion design — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Fashion design is the design of clothing and how it is worn.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A type of fashion design has nothing to do with this lesson.
**Answer: False** — It is part of the lesson: one division of the field, designing for a different wearer or occasion.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Placing a garment is assigning a garment to its fashion type with reasons.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.
