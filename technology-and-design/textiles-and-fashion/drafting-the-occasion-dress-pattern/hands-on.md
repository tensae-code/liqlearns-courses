# Hands-on — Drafting the occasion-dress pattern

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: the sleeveless fitted dress from the base pattern, flaring the skirt by the quarter-circle method ((90+60)/2 = 75 cm radius; waist 72 ÷ 6.2 ≈ 11.6), and modelling a one-shoulder asymmetric dress. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
