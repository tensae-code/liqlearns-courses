# Embroidery stitches — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How is a stitch executed well?
**Answer:** Step by step in its sequence, observing the hygiene and safety norms at every stage.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is the ramurică?
**Answer:** The stem stitch formed of overlapping stitches, taught in Grade 6.
