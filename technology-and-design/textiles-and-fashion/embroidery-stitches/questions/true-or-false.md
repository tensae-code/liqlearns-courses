# Embroidery stitches — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Safety norms apply at every stage of stitching, not only at the start.
**Answer: True** — The Grade 4 lesson observes them at every stage.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The ramurică is built of separate, spaced stitches.
**Answer: False** — It is a stem stitch of overlapping stitches.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The năfrămiță is embroidered to prove the advanced techniques.
**Answer: True** — The small napkin carries the Grade 6 stitch work.
