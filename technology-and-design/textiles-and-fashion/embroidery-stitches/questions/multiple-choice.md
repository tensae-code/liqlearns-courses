# Embroidery stitches — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How are stitches taught in Grade 4?
a) Step by step, under hygiene and safety norms at every stage b) All at once c) Without norms d) By watching only
**Answer: a) Step by step, under hygiene and safety norms at every stage**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which stitch is Grade 4's worked example?
a) The ramurică b) The tighel stitch c) Chain stitch only d) No stitch is named
**Answer: b) The tighel stitch**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is the ramurică?
a) The stem stitch of overlapping stitches b) A knot c) A hem d) A buttonhole
**Answer: a) The stem stitch of overlapping stitches**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** What article proves the advanced stitches?
a) A small napkin (năfrămiță) b) A shirt c) A curtain d) A bag
**Answer: a) A small napkin (năfrămiță)**

**5. (Sub lesson 1)** What makes a stitch repeat evenly?
a) Speed b) Luck c) Following its steps in order d) Thicker thread
**Answer: c) Following its steps in order**
