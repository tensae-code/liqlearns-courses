# Hands-on — Embroidery stitches

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## Tighel sampler
Work a row of tighel stitches on canvas. Photograph or sketch the row and mark the step where evenness is won or lost.

## Stem and napkin
Work a ramurică stem line of overlapping stitches, then plan a năfrămiță: which stitches, where, in what order.
