# Embroidery stitches

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Moldova's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — First stitches — the tighel, step by step
Grade 4 executes embroidery stitches step by step — the tighel stitch is the worked example — observing the hygiene and safety norms at every stage. A stitch is a sequence, not a gesture: order the steps and the stitch repeats evenly.

## Sub lesson 2 — Advanced stitches — ramurică and the năfrămiță
Grade 6 advances the technique: sewing and embroidery techniques including the ramurică — the stem stitch built of overlapping stitches — carried out by embroidering a small napkin (năfrămiță), where the stitches must serve a finished article.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: embroidery stitches are executed step by step under the safety norms — the tighel stitch in Grade 4, then advanced work in Grade 6: the ramurică (stem) stitch of overlapping stitches, proved on a small napkin (năfrămiță). Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.

## Do It Yourself
1. Do the Do it first task again, carefully this time, using everything the lesson taught.
2. Check your work against the lesson's own checks — fix one thing you find.
3. Keep your work: it is practice evidence, and the next lesson builds on it.
