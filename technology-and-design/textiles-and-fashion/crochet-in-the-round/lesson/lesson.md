# Crochet in the round

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Moldova's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — The circular technique — lace edge and corner
Grade 4 turns crochet circular: the technique for a napkin's lace edge, worked in the round, and the forming of the lace corner — the place where a round edging must turn without breaking its pattern. The make is a napkin trimmed with narrow lace.

## Sub lesson 2 — In the round in Grade 6 — crocheted details
Grade 6 returns to the round after surveying traditional and modern uses of crocheted details: crocheting worked in the round as a technique of its own, the centre-out construction behind doilies, motifs and details.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: crochet in the round grows from a centre outward: the circular technique edges a napkin in lace and forms the lace corner — and in Grade 6 the round is worked for its own sake, for crocheted details traditional and modern. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
