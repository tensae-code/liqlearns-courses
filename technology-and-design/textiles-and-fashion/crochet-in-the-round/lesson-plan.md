# Lesson plan — Crochet in the round

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Textiles and Fashion · **Lesson:** Crochet in the round
**Place in the curriculum:** Textiles and Fashion (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Moldova expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** crochet in the round grows from a centre outward: the circular technique edges a napkin in lace and forms the lace corner — and in Grade 6 the round is worked for its own sake, for crocheted details traditional and modern.
**This lesson's own level:** stocked from Moldova's *Educație tehnologică* (Technological Education) textbooks — see lesson/sources.md. Moldova counts in the acceptance count (student books, not evidence-only).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **The circular technique — lace edge and corner** — Grade 4 turns crochet circular: the technique for a napkin's lace edge, worked in the round, and the forming of the lace corner — the place where a round edging must turn without breaking its pattern.
2. **In the round in Grade 6 — crocheted details** — Grade 6 returns to the round after surveying traditional and modern uses of crocheted details: crocheting worked in the round as a technique of its own, the centre-out construction behind doilies, motifs and details..

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of Textiles and Fashion. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Work circular crochet for a lace edge and form a lace corner.
2. Make a napkin trimmed with narrow lace.
3. Explain how Moldova's book teaches it.
## What you need
Crochet hook, fine yarn or thread, and a napkin to edge.
