# Crochet in the round — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How does crochet in the round grow?
a) From a centre outward b) Edge to edge in rows c) From a corner only d) It does not grow
**Answer: a) From a centre outward**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the circular technique make in Grade 4?
a) A sweater b) A napkin's lace edge c) A rope d) A tablecloth centre
**Answer: b) A napkin's lace edge**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** What special forming does the edging need?
a) Forming the lace corner b) Cutting c) Dyeing d) Ironing only
**Answer: a) Forming the lace corner**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the Grade 4 make?
a) A napkin trimmed with narrow lace b) A wide curtain c) A blanket d) A hat
**Answer: a) A napkin trimmed with narrow lace**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** What does Grade 6 survey before the round?
a) Knitting patterns b) Weaving looms c) Traditional and modern uses of crocheted details d) Nothing
**Answer: c) Traditional and modern uses of crocheted details**
