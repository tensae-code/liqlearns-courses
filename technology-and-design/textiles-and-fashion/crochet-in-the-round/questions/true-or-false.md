# Crochet in the round — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The lace corner must be formed so the pattern survives the turn.
**Answer: True** — Forming the lace corner is named in the lesson.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Crochet in the round is worked in straight rows.
**Answer: False** — It is worked circularly, from a centre outward.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Crocheted details have both traditional and modern uses.
**Answer: True** — Grade 6 surveys both before working the round.
