# Sewing tools, safety and hand stitches — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Pincushion is the cushion that stores pins safely and keeps them findable.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Buttonhole opener has nothing to do with this lesson.
**Answer: False** — It is part of the lesson: the sharp tool that cuts a buttonhole slit.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Buttonhole stitch is the stitch worked round a buttonhole to stop fraying — the mouse-tooth stitch.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.
