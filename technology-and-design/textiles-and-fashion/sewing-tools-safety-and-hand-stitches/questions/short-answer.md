# Sewing tools, safety and hand stitches — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In your own words, what is pincushion?
**Answer:** The cushion that stores pins safely and keeps them findable.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In your own words, what is buttonhole stitch?
**Answer:** The stitch worked round a buttonhole to stop fraying — the mouse-tooth stitch.
