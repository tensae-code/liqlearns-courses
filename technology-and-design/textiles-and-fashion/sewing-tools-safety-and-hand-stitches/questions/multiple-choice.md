# Sewing tools, safety and hand stitches — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is pincushion?
a) the cushion that stores pins safely and keeps them findable b) Only a price c) Only decoration d) Nothing to do with this lesson
**Answer: a) the cushion that stores pins safely and keeps them findable**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is buttonhole opener?
a) Only history b) the sharp tool that cuts a buttonhole slit c) Only storage d) A kind of glue
**Answer: b) the sharp tool that cuts a buttonhole slit**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is buttonhole stitch?
a) Only old tools b) Only painting c) the stitch worked round a buttonhole to stop fraying — the mouse-tooth stitch d) Only a price
**Answer: c) the stitch worked round a buttonhole to stop fraying — the mouse-tooth stitch**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson teach, in one line?
a) Only decoration b) Nothing to do with this lesson c) Only history d) hand sewing begins with its tools used safely — from buttonhole opener to pincushion — and its first stitches: sewing on buttons and hooks, and the buttonhole (mouse-tooth) stitch.
**Answer: d) hand sewing begins with its tools used safely — from buttonhole opener to pincushion — and its first stitches: sewing on buttons and hooks, and the buttonhole (mouse-tooth) stitch.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** How does Iran's book teach this?
a) As theory only, never practised b) Only in a glossary c) Only as an exam d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Persian
**Answer: d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Persian**
