# Hands-on — Sewing tools, safety and hand stitches

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## Safe kit
Arrange or draw the sewing kit. For each sharp tool, write its safety rule and its storage place.

## Sew a fastening
Sew on a button with a thread shank, or work buttonhole stitch round a short slit. Check: firm, neat, no fraying.
