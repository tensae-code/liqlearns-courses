# Sewing production sequence

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Kazakhstan's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Sewing production sequence
Kazakhstan's books teach it this way (the student book, printed pp. 60–76): Control of materials and equipment (§14); creating the product: cutting the details, assembly and finishing of details (§15); decorating the product (§16); discussion, evaluation and presentation (§17). The learner works through the named materials and steps in order, saying what each step does before doing the next one.

## Sub lesson 2 — Sewing production sequence — doing it yourself
Now the learner does the whole task unaided: control of materials and equipment (§14); creating the product: cutting the details, assembly and finishing of details (§15); decorating the product (§16); discussion, evaluation and presentation (§17). Working from the first sub lesson's sequence, the learner plans the make, works through it step by step, and checks the finished piece against what the Kazakh book shows.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: Sewing production sequence — control of materials and equipment (§14); creating the product: cutting the details, assembly and finishing of details (§15); decorating the product (§16); discussion, evaluation and presentation (§17). Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.

## Do It Yourself
1. Do the Do it first task again, carefully this time, using everything the lesson taught.
2. Check your work against the lesson's own checks — fix one thing you find.
3. Keep your work: it is practice evidence, and the next lesson builds on it.
