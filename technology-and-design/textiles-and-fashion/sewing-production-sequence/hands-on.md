# Hands-on — Sewing production sequence

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: control of materials and equipment (§14); creating the product: cutting the details, assembly and finishing of details (§15); decorating the product (§16); discussion, evaluation and presentation (§17). Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
