# Hands-on — Felt floor-covering techniques

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: tekemet (pattern inlaid while felting) and syrmak (two-colour appliqué felt), and the patterned shy reed mat: how each is made. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
