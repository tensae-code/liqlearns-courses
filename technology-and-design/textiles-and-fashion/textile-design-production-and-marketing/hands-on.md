# Hands-on — Textile design, production and marketing

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: Process of fashion design in textile and clothing; Production processes of textile and clothing; Marketing processes of textile and clothing products. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
