# Hand embroidery (bordado manual) — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is embroidery (bordado)?
a) decorating cloth with stitched thread designs b) Only a price c) Only decoration d) Nothing to do with this lesson
**Answer: a) decorating cloth with stitched thread designs**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is graph paper reproduction?
a) Only history b) redrawing a design square by square to change its size c) Only storage d) A kind of glue
**Answer: b) redrawing a design square by square to change its size**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is stitch type?
a) Only old tools b) Only painting c) a named embroidery stitch, chosen for the part it fills d) Only a price
**Answer: c) a named embroidery stitch, chosen for the part it fills**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson teach, in one line?
a) Only decoration b) Nothing to do with this lesson c) Only history d) hand embroidery (bordado manual) decorates cloth with thread: a prepared work area, the right materials and tools, basic stitches and their types — and designs copied, enlarged and reproduced on graph paper.
**Answer: d) hand embroidery (bordado manual) decorates cloth with thread: a prepared work area, the right materials and tools, basic stitches and their types — and designs copied, enlarged and reproduced on graph paper.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** How does Cuba's book teach this?
a) As theory only, never practised b) Only in a glossary c) Only as an exam d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Spanish
**Answer: d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Spanish**
