# Hand embroidery (bordado manual)

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Cuba's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Area, materials and first stitches
Cuba's Grade 9 book gives embroidery a full chapter (Cap. 3): the work area and its safety norms, the materials and tools, then the basic stitches (puntadas básicas) from which all embroidery is built.

## Sub lesson 2 — Designs: copy, enlarge, reproduce
The embroiderer works from a design: copying it, enlarging it, and reproducing it on graph paper (papel cuadriculado) so it can be transferred to cloth at the wanted size. Stitch types are chosen per part of the design.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: hand embroidery (bordado manual) decorates cloth with thread: a prepared work area, the right materials and tools, basic stitches and their types — and designs copied, enlarged and reproduced on graph paper. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.

## Do It Yourself
1. Do the Do it first task again, carefully this time, using everything the lesson taught.
2. Check your work against the lesson's own checks — fix one thing you find.
3. Keep your work: it is practice evidence, and the next lesson builds on it.
