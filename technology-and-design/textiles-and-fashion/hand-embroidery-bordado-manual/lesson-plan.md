# Lesson plan — Hand embroidery (bordado manual)

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Textiles and Fashion · **Lesson:** Hand embroidery (bordado manual)
**Place in the curriculum:** Textiles and Fashion (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Cuba expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** hand embroidery (bordado manual) decorates cloth with thread: a prepared work area, the right materials and tools, basic stitches and their types — and designs copied, enlarged and reproduced on graph paper.
**This lesson's own level:** stocked from Cuba's *Educación Laboral — 9no grado* and the Cuba books in lesson/sources.md — see lesson/sources.md. Cuba counts in the acceptance count for its student-book placements; its programme and methodological-orientations placements are evidence only, counted nowhere.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **Area, materials and first stitches** — Cuba's Grade 9 book gives embroidery a full chapter (Cap. 3): the work area and its safety norms, the materials and tools, then the basic stitches (puntadas básicas) from which all embroidery is built.
2. **Designs: copy, enlarge, reproduce** — The embroiderer works from a design: copying it, enlarging it, and reproducing it on graph paper (papel cuadriculado) so it can be transferred to cloth at the wanted size. Stitch types are chosen per part of the design.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
