# Hands-on — Leather work

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: making leather products; the leather-worker's tools (cobbler's knife with slanted blade, wide-bladed scissors, the punch — a steel tube with a sharpened edge) (§10). Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
