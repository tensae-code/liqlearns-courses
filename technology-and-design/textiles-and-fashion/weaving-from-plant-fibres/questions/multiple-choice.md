# Weaving from plant fibres — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which fibres does the book name?
a) Straw, corn husks, willow and bulrush b) Cotton and wool c) Silk only d) Plastic strips
**Answer: a) Straw, corn husks, willow and bulrush**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What are pănuși?
a) Willow rods b) Corn husks c) Straw d) Bulrush leaves
**Answer: b) Corn husks**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** How are collected fibres stored?
a) Dry, after sorting b) Wet c) Buried d) Frozen
**Answer: a) Dry, after sorting**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** What is Grade 5's worked make?
a) Articles from corn husks b) Willow baskets only c) Straw hats only d) Nothing is made
**Answer: a) Articles from corn husks**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** What does Grade 6 add?
a) Dyeing b) Selling c) Weaving techniques and models, and the design stages of a woven article d) Knitting
**Answer: c) Weaving techniques and models, and the design stages of a woven article**
