# Weaving from plant fibres — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Plant fibres are collected and sorted before storing.
**Answer: True** — The order is collecting, sorting, storing dry, then processing.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Fibres keep best stored wet.
**Answer: False** — They are stored dry.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A woven article has design stages — it is designed, not improvised.
**Answer: True** — Grade 6 teaches the design stages of a woven article.
