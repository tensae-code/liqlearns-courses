# Weaving from plant fibres — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the four plant fibres with their Romanian names where given.
**Answer:** Straw, corn husks (pănuși), willow (lozie) and bulrush (papură).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Give the fibre preparation order.
**Answer:** Collecting, sorting, storing dry — then processing ready for work.
