# Weaving from plant fibres

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Moldova's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — The fibres — collecting, sorting, storing, processing
Grade 5 names the plant fibres: straw, corn husks (pănuși), willow (lozie) and bulrush (papură). Craft begins outdoors — collecting the fibres, sorting them, and storing them dry — then processing them ready for work. The book's worked make is articles from corn husks.

## Sub lesson 2 — Techniques, models and the design stages
Grade 6 weaves: techniques and models for weaving articles from the different plant fibres, and the design stages of a woven article — the woven thing is designed, not improvised, from fibre choice to finished form.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: plant fibres are a harvest before they are a craft: straw, corn husks (pănuși), willow (lozie) and bulrush (papură) are collected, sorted and stored dry — then processed, woven by taught techniques and models, through the design stages of a woven article. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.

## Do It Yourself
1. Do the Do it first task again, carefully this time, using everything the lesson taught.
2. Check your work against the lesson's own checks — fix one thing you find.
3. Keep your work: it is practice evidence, and the next lesson builds on it.
