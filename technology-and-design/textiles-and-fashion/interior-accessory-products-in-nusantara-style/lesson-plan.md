# Lesson plan — Interior accessory products in Nusantara style

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Textiles and Fashion · **Lesson:** Interior accessory products in Nusantara style
**Place in the curriculum:** Textiles and Fashion (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Indonesia expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** interior accessory products in Nusantara style: project planning, product design and making (incl. anyaman weaving).
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on teacher-guide evidence only — Indonesia's Prakarya teacher guides (Kemendikbudristek, Pusat Perbukuan, Kurikulum Merdeka editions) are the books in hand for this topic (see lesson/sources.md). ## Sub lessons
1. **Interior accessory products in Nusantara style** — Interior accessory products in Nusantara style: project planning, product design and making (incl. anyaman weaving).
2. **How it is done — the book's own sequence** — The topic is worked in the book at Unit 2 Produk Aksesoris Interior Nusantara (*Prakarya Kerajinan G10 TG*, p. 101).
3. **The Indonesian way — local potential, safety and care** — Prakarya builds from local materials and local potential (potensi lokal), plans before making, and puts safety first.
