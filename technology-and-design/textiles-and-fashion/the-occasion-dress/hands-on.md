# Hands-on — The occasion dress

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: its styles (classic, cocktail, evening), the ensemble (dress with hat, bag, shoes and jewellery in one style), and describing a design (silhouette, length, neckline, decoration). Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
