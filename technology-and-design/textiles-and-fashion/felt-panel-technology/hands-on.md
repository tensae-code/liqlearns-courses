Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: felt wall panels (панно) made as an independent research-and-make project (§7). Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
