# Lesson plan — Beadwork

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Textiles and Fashion · **Lesson:** Beadwork
**Place in the curriculum:** Textiles and Fashion (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Moldova expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** beadwork (mărgele) makes clothing accessories with meaning: ornaments and jewellery in folk dress carry meanings first — then techniques, the making of an accessory, and the evaluation of the finished article.
**This lesson's own level:** stocked from Moldova's *Educație tehnologică* (Technological Education) textbooks — see lesson/sources.md. Moldova counts in the acceptance count (student books, not evidence-only).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **Meanings in folk dress** — Grade 9 beadwork opens with meaning: the ornaments and jewellery of folk dress are not decoration without sense — they carry meanings in the tradition.
2. **Techniques, making and evaluating** — Then the technology: the making techniques of beadwork, the making of a clothing accessory from beads (mărgele), and — in the book's project cycle — the evaluation of the finished article, judged as a made thing with a meaning to carry..

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of Textiles and Fashion. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Explain the meanings of ornaments and jewellery in folk dress.
2. Make a bead accessory and evaluate the finished article.
3. Explain how Moldova's book teaches it.
## What you need
Beads (mărgele), thread or wire, and pictures of folk-dress ornaments.
