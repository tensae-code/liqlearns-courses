# Hands-on — Beadwork

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## Read an ornament
Study a folk-dress ornament or jewel (real or pictured). Write what it is, where it is worn, and what meaning the lesson says such ornaments carry.

## Make and evaluate
Make a small bead accessory. Evaluate it in three lines: technique, finish, and whether it carries its meaning well.
