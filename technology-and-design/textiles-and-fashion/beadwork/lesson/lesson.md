# Beadwork

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Moldova's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Meanings in folk dress
Grade 9 beadwork opens with meaning: the ornaments and jewellery of folk dress are not decoration without sense — they carry meanings in the tradition. The beadworker learns what the ornament says before learning how it is made.

## Sub lesson 2 — Techniques, making and evaluating
Then the technology: the making techniques of beadwork, the making of a clothing accessory from beads (mărgele), and — in the book's project cycle — the evaluation of the finished article, judged as a made thing with a meaning to carry.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: beadwork (mărgele) makes clothing accessories with meaning: ornaments and jewellery in folk dress carry meanings first — then techniques, the making of an accessory, and the evaluation of the finished article. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.

## Do It Yourself
1. Do the Do it first task again, carefully this time, using everything the lesson taught.
2. Check your work against the lesson's own checks — fix one thing you find.
3. Keep your work: it is practice evidence, and the next lesson builds on it.
