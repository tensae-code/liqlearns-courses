# Beadwork — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What are mărgele?
a) Beads b) Buttons c) Threads d) Stones only
**Answer: a) Beads**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What opens the beadwork part?
a) Prices b) The meanings of ornaments and jewellery in folk dress c) Shop addresses d) Bead sizes only
**Answer: b) The meanings of ornaments and jewellery in folk dress**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is made?
a) A clothing accessory from beads b) A garment c) A shoe d) A bag of beads
**Answer: a) A clothing accessory from beads**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** What closes the work?
a) Evaluating the finished article b) Selling it at once c) Hiding it d) Nothing
**Answer: a) Evaluating the finished article**

**5. (Sub lesson 1)** Folk ornaments are…
a) Random b) Meaningless c) Meaning-carrying, not senseless decoration d) Modern inventions
**Answer: c) Meaning-carrying, not senseless decoration**
