# Beadwork — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Ornaments in folk dress carry meanings.
**Answer: True** — The part opens with those meanings.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Evaluation of the finished article is part of the beadwork technology.
**Answer: True** — Making is followed by evaluating, in the book's project cycle.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Technique is taught before any meaning is mentioned.
**Answer: False** — Meanings open the part; techniques follow.
