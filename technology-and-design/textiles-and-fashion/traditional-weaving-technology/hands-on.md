# Hands-on — Traditional weaving technology

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## Weave a sample panel
With paper strips or any leaf strips available, weave a small flat panel in plain weave and bind its edges with string (standing in for sinnet). Test it as a screen against a lamp's light.

## Judge the technology
Write three sentences: the job the polasisi does, the materials it uses, and how it is repaired or remade. Compare with a factory-made blind on the same three points.
