# Traditional weaving technology — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the two materials of a polasisi and their sources.
**Answer:** Answer: Coconut leaves (the weaving strips) and sinnet, cord rolled from coconut husk fibre.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Give two reasons the polasisi is good technology for its place.
**Answer:** Answer: Any two of: materials are local and renewable, it shades while ventilating, it can be raised or lowered, it is repairable and remade from the same materials.
