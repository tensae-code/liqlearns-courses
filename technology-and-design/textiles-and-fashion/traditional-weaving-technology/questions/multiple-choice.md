# Traditional weaving technology — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A polasisi is made from…
a) Coconut leaves and sinnet b) Cotton and polyester c) Bamboo and wire d) Paper and glue
**Answer: a) Coconut leaves and sinnet**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Sinnet is…
a) Cord rolled from coconut husk fibre b) A type of leaf c) A woven pattern d) A dye
**Answer: a) Cord rolled from coconut husk fibre**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A polasisi serves in a fale as…
a) A blind that shades and screens while letting air move b) A roof beam c) A floor covering only d) A door lock
**Answer: a) A blind that shades and screens while letting air move**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The weaving of a polasisi follows…
a) The same warp-and-weft logic as cloth, in leaf at house scale b) Knitting loops c) Machine stitching d) Gluing
**Answer: a) The same warp-and-weft logic as cloth, in leaf at house scale**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Traditional weaving counts as technology because…
a) It does a job by a repeatable method with chosen materials b) It is very old only c) It is decoration d) It uses no materials
**Answer: a) It does a job by a repeatable method with chosen materials**
