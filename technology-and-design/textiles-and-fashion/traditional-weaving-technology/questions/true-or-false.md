# Traditional weaving technology — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The materials for a polasisi are local and renewable.
**Answer: True** — Coconut leaves and husk fibre grow on the islands.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A polasisi blocks all air from the fale.
**Answer: False** — It screens sun while still letting air move.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A polasisi can be repaired and remade from the same materials.
**Answer: True** — Repairability is part of its design.
