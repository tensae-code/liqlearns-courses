# Working with patterns — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The fabric's width decides…
a) How the pattern pieces can be laid out b) The colour of the thread c) The stitch type d) Nothing
**Answer: a) How the pattern pieces can be laid out**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The book lays out the puletasi top on…
a) 90 cm and 115 cm fabric b) Only 200 cm fabric c) Paper only d) No fabric
**Answer: a) 90 cm and 115 cm fabric**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The grain line on a pattern piece must lie…
a) Parallel to the selvedge, along the lengthwise threads b) Across the cloth c) At any angle d) On the fold always
**Answer: a) Parallel to the selvedge, along the lengthwise threads**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Cutting on the fold produces…
a) A whole, symmetrical piece when opened b) Two separate pieces c) A narrower piece d) A twisted piece
**Answer: a) A whole, symmetrical piece when opened**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Largest pieces are placed first because…
a) They have the fewest possible homes; small pieces fill the gaps b) They are the prettiest c) They are cut fastest d) The book says so for no reason
**Answer: a) They have the fewest possible homes; small pieces fill the gaps**
