# Working with patterns — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** How do you check a piece is on the grain?
**Answer:** Answer: Lay its grain line parallel to the selvedge and measure the distance at both ends of the line — equal distances mean it is straight.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Why plan the layout on both widths before buying fabric?
**Answer:** Answer: The same pieces fit differently on 90 cm and 115 cm cloth; the layout shows how much fabric the garment needs at each width, so you buy enough and waste little.
