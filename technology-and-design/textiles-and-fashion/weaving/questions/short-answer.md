# Weaving — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain warp and weft in one sentence each.
**Answer:** Answer: The warp is the set of threads stretched lengthwise; the weft is the thread that travels across, over and under the warp.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Why is plain weave a good choice for a firm work cloth?
**Answer:** Answer: Its strict over-one-under-one interlacing locks every thread often, making a firm, stable cloth.
