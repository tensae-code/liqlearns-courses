# Weaving — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Every woven cloth interlaces two thread sets at right angles.
**Answer: True** — Warp and weft crossing is the definition of weaving.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Satin weave goes strictly over one, under one.
**Answer: False** — That is plain weave; satin floats over several warps.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The weave pattern affects strength and absorbency, not just looks.
**Answer: True** — Structure decides what the cloth can do.
