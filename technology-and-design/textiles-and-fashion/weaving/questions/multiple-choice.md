# Weaving — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The warp threads…
a) Run lengthwise and are held in place b) Travel across last c) Are always coloured d) Are cut first
**Answer: a) Run lengthwise and are held in place**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The weft thread…
a) Travels across, over and under the warp b) Runs lengthwise c) Holds the loom d) Is the finished cloth
**Answer: a) Travels across, over and under the warp**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Terry weave is recognised by…
a) Loops standing up from the surface b) A shiny flat face c) Diagonal lines only d) No weft
**Answer: a) Loops standing up from the surface**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Twill weave shows…
a) Diagonal lines from its stepped pattern b) Loops c) A mirror shine d) Holes only
**Answer: a) Diagonal lines from its stepped pattern**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** A towel uses terry weave because…
a) Its loops absorb water b) It is the cheapest c) It needs no washing d) It is shiny
**Answer: a) Its loops absorb water**
