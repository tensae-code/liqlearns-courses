# Weaving kinds and macramé

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Kazakhstan's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Weaving kinds and macramé
Kazakhstan's books teach it this way (the student book, printed pp. 36–40): Macramé, ribbon, bead, newspaper-tube, straw and plant-stem weaving; reading a macramé symbol chart; weaving from a plastic bottle (cut a 1–1.5 cm spiral ribbon, stretch over a box template, set the shape with warm air) (§§11–12). The learner works through the named materials and steps in order, saying what each step does before doing the next one.

## Sub lesson 2 — Weaving kinds and macramé — doing it yourself
Now the learner does the whole task unaided: macramé, ribbon, bead, newspaper-tube, straw and plant-stem weaving; reading a macramé symbol chart; weaving from a plastic bottle (cut a 1–1.5 cm spiral ribbon, stretch over a box template, set the shape with warm air) (§§11–12). Working from the first sub lesson's sequence, the learner plans the make, works through it step by step, and checks the finished piece against what the Kazakh book shows.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: Weaving kinds and macramé — macramé, ribbon, bead, newspaper-tube, straw and plant-stem weaving; reading a macramé symbol chart; weaving from a plastic bottle (cut a 1–1.5 cm spiral ribbon, stretch over a box template, set the shape with warm air) (§§11–12). Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.

## Do It Yourself
1. Do the Do it first task again, carefully this time, using everything the lesson taught.
2. Check your work against the lesson's own checks — fix one thing you find.
3. Keep your work: it is practice evidence, and the next lesson builds on it.
