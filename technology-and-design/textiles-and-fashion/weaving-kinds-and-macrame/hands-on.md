# Hands-on — Weaving kinds and macramé

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: macramé, ribbon, bead, newspaper-tube, straw and plant-stem weaving; reading a macramé symbol chart; weaving from a plastic bottle (cut a 1–1.5 cm spiral ribbon, stretch over a box template, set the shape with warm air) (§§11–12). Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
