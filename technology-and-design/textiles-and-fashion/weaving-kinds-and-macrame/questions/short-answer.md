# Weaving kinds and macramé — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Say in one sentence what this lesson teaches.
**Answer:** Weaving kinds and macramé — macramé, ribbon, bead, newspaper-tube, straw and plant-stem weaving; reading a macramé symbol chart; weaving from a plastic bottle (cut a 1–1.5 cm spiral ribbon, stretch over a box template, set the shape with warm air) (§§11–12).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name one material, tool or step this lesson works with.
**Answer:** Any one named in the lesson text — for example, the first step of sub lesson 1: Weaving kinds and macramé.
