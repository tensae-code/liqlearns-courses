# Advanced deel pattern drafting

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Mongolia's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Advanced deel pattern drafting
Mongolia's book teaches it this way (*Design Drawing & Technology 11 (Mongolia)*, printed p. 95): collar forms (round and square standing collars, R = neck/3 + 1), sleeve drafting, and modifying the base pattern for men's and women's deels. The learner works through the named materials and steps in order, saying what each step does before doing the next one.

## Sub lesson 2 — Advanced deel pattern drafting — doing it yourself
Now the learner does the whole task unaided: collar forms (round and square standing collars, R = neck/3 + 1), sleeve drafting, and modifying the base pattern for men's and women's deels. Working from the first sub lesson's sequence, the learner plans the make, works through it step by step, and checks the finished piece against what Mongolia's book shows.
