# Hands-on — Advanced deel pattern drafting

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: collar forms (round and square standing collars, R = neck/3 + 1), sleeve drafting, and modifying the base pattern for men's and women's deels. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
