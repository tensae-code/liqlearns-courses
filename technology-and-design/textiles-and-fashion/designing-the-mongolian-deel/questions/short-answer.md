# Designing the Mongolian deel — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Say in one sentence what this lesson teaches.
**Answer:** Designing the Mongolian deel — the parts and styles of national dress, taking the measurements (neck, chest, sleeve, length, wrist) and drafting the deel pattern with pattern-drawing symbols.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name one material, tool or step this lesson works with.
**Answer:** Any one named in the lesson text — for example, the first step of sub lesson 1: Designing the Mongolian deel.
