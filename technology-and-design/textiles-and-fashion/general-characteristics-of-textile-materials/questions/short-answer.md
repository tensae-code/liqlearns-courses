# General characteristics of textile materials — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Say in one sentence what this lesson teaches.
**Answer:** Classification of textile materials (fibres, yarns, fabrics), the general characteristics of fibres and fabrics, and the general methods of identifying fibres (preliminary examination, microscopic examination, burning test, solubility test, staining test, melting point, specific gravity).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name one material, tool or step this lesson works with.
**Answer:** Any one named in the lesson text — for example, the first step of sub lesson 1: General characteristics of textile materials.
