# Hands-on — General characteristics of textile materials

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: classification of textile materials (fibres, yarns, fabrics), the general characteristics of fibres and fabrics, and the general methods of identifying fibres (preliminary examination, microscopic examination, burning test, solubility test, staining test, melting point, specific gravity). Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
