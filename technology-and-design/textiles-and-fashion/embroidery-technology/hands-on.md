# Hands-on — Embroidery technology

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: kinds of artistic embroidery (satin stitch, contour, cross-stitch, richelieu cutwork, tapestry, silk ribbons); counted vs free seams; the tools and materials (§9). Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
