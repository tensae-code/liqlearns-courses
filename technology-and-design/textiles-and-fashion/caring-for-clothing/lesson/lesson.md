# Caring for clothing

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Cuba's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Selecting, folding, organising
Cuba's Grade 6 programme teaches the care of the wardrobe (§1.1): selecting garments, folding (doblado), and distributing them in escaparates (wardrobes) and valijas (suitcases) so they keep their shape and are found at once.

## Sub lesson 2 — Removing stains
Stain removal (eliminar manchas) closes the lesson: a stain is treated by what it is and what the fabric is. The programme files the skill inside simple sewing work — care of clothing is part of textile competence, not housework apart.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: clothing is cared for as a craft: select and organise garments, fold them properly, store them in wardrobe and suitcase in order — and remove stains from fabrics without ruining them. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.

## Do It Yourself
1. Do the Do it first task again, carefully this time, using everything the lesson taught.
2. Check your work against the lesson's own checks — fix one thing you find.
3. Keep your work: it is practice evidence, and the next lesson builds on it.
