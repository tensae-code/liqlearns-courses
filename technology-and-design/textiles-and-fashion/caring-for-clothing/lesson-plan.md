# Lesson plan — Caring for clothing

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Textiles and Fashion · **Lesson:** Caring for clothing
**Place in the curriculum:** Textiles and Fashion (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Cuba expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** clothing is cared for as a craft: select and organise garments, fold them properly, store them in wardrobe and suitcase in order — and remove stains from fabrics without ruining them.
**This lesson's own level:** stocked from Cuba's Educación Laboral programmes and methodological orientations (teacher programme documents) — see lesson/sources.md. Cuba is evidence only for this lesson, counted nowhere in the acceptance count.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **Selecting, folding, organising** — Cuba's Grade 6 programme teaches the care of the wardrobe (§1.1): selecting garments, folding (doblado), and distributing them in escaparates (wardrobes) and valijas (suitcases) so they keep their shape and are found at once.
2. **Removing stains** — Stain removal (eliminar manchas) closes the lesson: a stain is treated by what it is and what the fabric is. The programme files the skill inside simple sewing work — care of clothing is part of textile competence, not housework apart.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
