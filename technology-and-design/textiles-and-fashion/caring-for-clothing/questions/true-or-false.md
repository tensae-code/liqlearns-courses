# Caring for clothing — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Folding (doblado) is folding a garment so it keeps its shape in storage.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Stain removal has nothing to do with this lesson.
**Answer: False** — It is part of the lesson: treating a mark on fabric by its kind and the fabric's kind.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Wardrobe organisation is distributing garments in wardrobe and suitcase in a planned order.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.
