# Caring for clothing — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In your own words, what is folding (doblado)?
**Answer:** Folding a garment so it keeps its shape in storage.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In your own words, what is wardrobe organisation?
**Answer:** Distributing garments in wardrobe and suitcase in a planned order.
