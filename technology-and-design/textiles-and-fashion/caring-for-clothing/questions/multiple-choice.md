# Caring for clothing — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is folding (doblado)?
a) folding a garment so it keeps its shape in storage b) Only a price c) Only decoration d) Nothing to do with this lesson
**Answer: a) folding a garment so it keeps its shape in storage**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is stain removal?
a) Only history b) treating a mark on fabric by its kind and the fabric's kind c) Only storage d) A kind of glue
**Answer: b) treating a mark on fabric by its kind and the fabric's kind**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is wardrobe organisation?
a) Only old tools b) Only painting c) distributing garments in wardrobe and suitcase in a planned order d) Only a price
**Answer: c) distributing garments in wardrobe and suitcase in a planned order**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson teach, in one line?
a) Only decoration b) Nothing to do with this lesson c) Only history d) clothing is cared for as a craft: select and organise garments, fold them properly, store them in wardrobe and suitcase in order — and remove stains from fabrics without ruining them.
**Answer: d) clothing is cared for as a craft: select and organise garments, fold them properly, store them in wardrobe and suitcase in order — and remove stains from fabrics without ruining them.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** How does Cuba's book teach this?
a) As theory only, never practised b) Only in a glossary c) Only as an exam d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Spanish
**Answer: d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Spanish**
