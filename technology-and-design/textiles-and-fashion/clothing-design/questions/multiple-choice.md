# Clothing design — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is design vestimentar?
a) Fashion / clothing design b) Shoe repair c) Weaving d) Knitting
**Answer: a) Fashion / clothing design**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Where does the creation process begin?
a) In the shop b) In theoretical study and new ideas c) In sewing at once d) In selling
**Answer: b) In theoretical study and new ideas**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Where does it end?
a) In implementation in clothing b) In a sketch only c) In a test d) It never ends
**Answer: a) In implementation in clothing**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** What governs every stage?
a) The principles of clothing design b) Fashion shows only c) Prices d) Nothing
**Answer: a) The principles of clothing design**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** What makes the process design rather than copying?
a) Expensive cloth b) Speed c) Its principles, applied at every choice d) Secrecy
**Answer: c) Its principles, applied at every choice**
