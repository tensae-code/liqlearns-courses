# Clothing design

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Moldova's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — The staged creation process
Grade 9 fashion design (design vestimentar) defines design as a staged creation process: it begins in theoretical study and new ideas — learning, looking, inventing — and ends in implementation in clothing, the idea made wearable. No stage is skipped: study feeds ideas, ideas feed the making.

## Sub lesson 2 — The principles of clothing design
The stages are governed by principles: the principles of clothing design that judge every choice from first sketch to finished garment. Principles are what make the process design, rather than copying.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: clothing design (design vestimentar) is a staged creation: theoretical study and new ideas first, implementation in clothing last — governed throughout by the principles of clothing design. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
