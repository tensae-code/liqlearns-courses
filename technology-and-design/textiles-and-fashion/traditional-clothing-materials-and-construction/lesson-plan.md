# Lesson plan — Traditional clothing materials and construction

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Textiles and Fashion · **Lesson:** Traditional clothing materials and construction
**Place in the curriculum:** Textiles and Fashion (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Samoa expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** early Samoan clothing was made from what the islands give: malo, titi, lavalava and siapo from bark, ti leaves and fau fibre, blackened with mud and smoke.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on student book evidence — Samoa's MESC textbooks (see lesson/sources.md). It counts in the acceptance count (student book, not evidence-only).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **Clothing from bark, leaf and fibre** — The malo, titi and lavalava were made from bark, ti leaves and fau fibre.
2. **Siapo — bark cloth** — Siapo is bark cloth: beaten bark made into a sheet that can be worn and decorated.
3. **Blackening with mud and smoke** — Mud and smoke were used to blacken and finish the materials.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of Textiles and Fashion. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name the traditional garments and their materials.
2. Describe how siapo is made.
3. Say how mud and smoke were used.
## What you need
No special materials; pictures of traditional garments if available.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — the opening doing task in lesson/lesson.md.
2. **The explanation** — the sub lessons in order (lesson/lesson.md).
   - ADD IMAGE: a picture of a malo, a titi of ti leaves, a lavalava and a sheet of siapo bark cloth laid out with the bark, leaves and fau fibre each came from — the student matches each garment to its material.
   - ADD GIF: a GIF of bark being beaten with a mallet, spreading wider and thinner into a continuous sheet of siapo — the student watches the sheet grow and says what beating does to the fibres.
   - ADD AUDIO: a voice reading the lesson's key rule aloud — bark, ti leaves and fau fibre — clothing made from the islands — the student repeats it.
   - ADD VIDEO: a short video of the lesson demonstration — see video-script.md.

4. **Questions** — the questions/ files, spread over the sub lessons.
