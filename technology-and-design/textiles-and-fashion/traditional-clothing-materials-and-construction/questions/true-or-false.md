# Traditional clothing materials and construction — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Traditional Samoan garments were manufactured from plant materials.
**Answer: True** — Bark, ti leaves and fau fibre were selected, prepared and assembled.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Siapo is woven on a loom.
**Answer: False** — Siapo is beaten bark cloth, not woven.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Blackening with mud and smoke was a deliberate finishing process.
**Answer: True** — It changed the material's colour and durability on purpose.
