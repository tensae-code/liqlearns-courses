# Traditional clothing materials and construction — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The titi was traditionally made from…
a) Ti leaves b) Cotton yarn c) Beaten bark only d) Animal skin
**Answer: a) Ti leaves**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Siapo is…
a) Bark cloth beaten from inner bark b) A woven cotton sheet c) A knitted garment d) A type of dye plant
**Answer: a) Bark cloth beaten from inner bark**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Beating the bark does what?
a) Spreads the fibres into a continuous sheet b) Cuts it into threads c) Dyes it black d) Makes it waterproof forever
**Answer: a) Spreads the fibres into a continuous sheet**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Fau fibre was used as…
a) A wearable clothing material b) Fuel only c) A food d) A building beam
**Answer: a) A wearable clothing material**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Mud and smoke were used to…
a) Blacken and finish the materials b) Wash the garments c) Soften cotton d) Make needles
**Answer: a) Blacken and finish the materials**
