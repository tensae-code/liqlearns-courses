# Traditional clothing materials and construction — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name three traditional garments and one material each is made from.
**Answer:** Answer: Malo, titi (ti leaves), lavalava and siapo (bark) — fau fibre also served as a clothing material.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Put siapo-making in order: strip, soak, beat. What does the beating achieve?
**Answer:** Answer: The beating spreads and felts the bark fibres together until separate strips become one continuous sheet.
