# Hands-on — Traditional clothing materials and construction

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## Material chain
For one garment (malo, titi, lavalava or siapo), write its chain: material → preparation → forming → finishing. Compare your chain with how a factory T-shirt is made.

## Test a fibre
If plant fibre is available (a leaf strip or bark), try softening it by beating or soaking under supervision. Record what changed and what stayed the same.
