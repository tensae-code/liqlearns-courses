# Felt bag construction

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Kazakhstan's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Felt bag construction
Kazakhstan's books teach it this way (the student book, printed pp. 46–47): Making an ayakkap (felt bag for dishes): sketch, symmetric ornament cut from folded felt, appliqué, fringe and strap (steps viewed by me). The learner works through the named materials and steps in order, saying what each step does before doing the next one.

## Sub lesson 2 — Felt bag construction — doing it yourself
Now the learner does the whole task unaided: making an ayakkap (felt bag for dishes): sketch, symmetric ornament cut from folded felt, appliqué, fringe and strap (steps viewed by me). Working from the first sub lesson's sequence, the learner plans the make, works through it step by step, and checks the finished piece against what the Kazakh book shows.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: Felt bag construction — making an ayakkap (felt bag for dishes): sketch, symmetric ornament cut from folded felt, appliqué, fringe and strap (steps viewed by me). Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
