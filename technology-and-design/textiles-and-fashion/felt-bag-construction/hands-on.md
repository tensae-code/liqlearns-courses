# Hands-on — Felt bag construction

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: making an ayakkap (felt bag for dishes): sketch, symmetric ornament cut from folded felt, appliqué, fringe and strap (steps viewed by me). Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
