# Crochet technology — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Say in one sentence what this lesson teaches.
**Answer:** Crochet technology — the art of crochet: kinds and techniques of crochet; developing a product sketch, choosing materials and tools; crocheting a product (napkin, bag, phone case) (§§12–14).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name one material, tool or step this lesson works with.
**Answer:** Any one named in the lesson text — for example, the first step of sub lesson 1: Crochet technology.
