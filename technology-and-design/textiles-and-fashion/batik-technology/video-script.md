A short demonstration video, made later, in a super-simple voice.

Batik technology.
Batik technology — hand-painting silk/cotton/synthetic cloth with a resist fixing the drawing's contours: sketch (нобай) drawn, transferred to tracing paper at product size, fabric chosen and prepared, dye applied inside the resist contours in steps, the product finished and decorated by stamp-printing.
Watch the steps. Then do them yourself.
