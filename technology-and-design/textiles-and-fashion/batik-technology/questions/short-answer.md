# Batik technology — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Say in one sentence what this lesson teaches.
**Answer:** Batik technology — hand-painting silk/cotton/synthetic cloth with a resist fixing the drawing's contours: sketch (нобай) drawn, transferred to tracing paper at product size, fabric chosen and prepared, dye applied inside the resist contours in steps, the product finished and decorated by stamp-printing.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name one material, tool or step this lesson works with.
**Answer:** Any one named in the lesson text — for example, the first step of sub lesson 1: Batik as a textile technology.
