# Hands-on — Batik technology

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: hand-painting silk/cotton/synthetic cloth with a resist fixing the drawing's contours: sketch (нобай) drawn, transferred to tracing paper at product size, fabric chosen and prepared, dye applied inside the resist contours in steps, the product finished and decorated by stamp-printing. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
