Kazakhstan's own way: Kazakhstan teaches this inside Художественный труд / Көркем еңбек (Artistic Labour) and Трудовое обучение / Еңбекке баулу (Labour Training), where making is learned by making — Hand-painting silk/cotton/synthetic cloth with a resist fixing the drawing's contours: sketch (нобай) drawn, transferred to tracing paper at product size, fabric chosen and prepared, dye applied inside the resist contours in steps, the product finished and decorated by stamp-printing.
