# Batik technology

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Kazakhstan's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Batik as a textile technology
Kazakhstan's books teach it this way (the student book, printed batik lessons): Hand-painting silk/cotton/synthetic cloth with a resist fixing the drawing's contours: sketch (нобай) drawn, transferred to tracing paper at product size, fabric chosen and prepared, dye applied inside the resist contours in steps, the product finished and decorated by stamp-printing. The learner works through the named materials and steps in order, saying what each step does before doing the next one.

## Sub lesson 2 — Batik technology, 2025 form
The Kazakh treatment continues and completes the topic: The resist-dyeing sequence carried from Book 28 with Kazakhstani batik artists as the worked examples. The learner finishes the make, checks it against the book's own sequence, and fixes one thing before presenting the work.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: Batik technology — hand-painting silk/cotton/synthetic cloth with a resist fixing the drawing's contours: sketch (нобай) drawn, transferred to tracing paper at product size, fabric chosen and prepared, dye applied inside the resist contours in steps, the product finished and decorated by stamp-printing. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.

## Do It Yourself
1. Do the Do it first task again, carefully this time, using everything the lesson taught.
2. Check your work against the lesson's own checks — fix one thing you find.
3. Keep your work: it is practice evidence, and the next lesson builds on it.
