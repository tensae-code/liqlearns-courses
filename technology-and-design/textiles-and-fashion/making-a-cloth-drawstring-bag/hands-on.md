# Hands-on — Making a cloth drawstring bag

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: choosing the size, cutting the pattern (30 cm × 12 cm shown), sewing the seams 15 mm from the edge, threading the cord and decorating; checking your own work. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
