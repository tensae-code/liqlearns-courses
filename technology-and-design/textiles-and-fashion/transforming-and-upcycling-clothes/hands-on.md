# Hands-on — Transforming and upcycling clothes

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: changing a garment's form into another form; recycling: re-using textile materials; ideas for transformation. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
