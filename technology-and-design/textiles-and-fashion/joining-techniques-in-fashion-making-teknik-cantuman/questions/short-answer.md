# Joining techniques in fashion making (teknik cantuman) — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In your own words, what is teknik cantuman?
**Answer:** Joining techniques in fashion making.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In your own words, what is tampalan?
