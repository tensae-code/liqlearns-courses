# Joining techniques in fashion making (teknik cantuman) — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is teknik cantuman?
a) joining techniques in fashion making b) Only a price c) Only decoration d) Nothing to do with this lesson
**Answer: a) joining techniques in fashion making**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is sulaman?
a) Only decoration b) embroidery — bead, appliqué, thread and ribbon joining that decorates c) Only a price d) A kind of glue
**Answer: b) embroidery — bead, appliqué, thread and ribbon joining that decorates**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is tampalan?
a) Only history b) Only painting c) patching/appliqué joined with glues or wax d) Only storage
**Answer: c) patching/appliqué joined with glues or wax**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson teach, in one line?
a) fashion joining (teknik cantuman) spans sewing and embroidery, glued patching, and temporary mock-up joins — chosen by whether the join must last. b) Only prices c) Only old tools d) Nothing practical
**Answer: a) fashion joining (teknik cantuman) spans sewing and embroidery, glued patching, and temporary mock-up joins — chosen by whether the join must last.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** How does Malaysia's book teach this?
a) As theory only, never practised b) Only in the glossary c) Only as an exam d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Malay (RBT)
**Answer: d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Malay (RBT)**
