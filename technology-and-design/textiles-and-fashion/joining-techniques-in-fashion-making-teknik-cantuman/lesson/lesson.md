# Joining techniques in fashion making (teknik cantuman)

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Malaysia's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Permanent joins: sewing and embroidery
Fashion is joined — teknik cantuman — above all by sewing, by hand and by machine, and by embroidery (sulaman): bead, appliqué, thread and ribbon work that joins while it decorates.

## Sub lesson 2 — Patching and temporary joins
Patching/appliqué (tampalan) joins with fabric glue, hot glue, wax or all-purpose glue; and mock-ups are joined temporarily — stapler, cellulose tape, string ties for accessories — so a design can be tried before it is committed.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: fashion joining (teknik cantuman) spans sewing and embroidery, glued patching, and temporary mock-up joins — chosen by whether the join must last. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
