# Lesson plan — Joining techniques in fashion making (teknik cantuman)

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Textiles and Fashion · **Lesson:** Joining techniques in fashion making (teknik cantuman)
**Place in the curriculum:** Textiles and Fashion (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Malaysia expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** fashion joining (teknik cantuman) spans sewing and embroidery, glued patching, and temporary mock-up joins — chosen by whether the join must last.
**This lesson's own level:** stocked from Malaysia's *Reka Bentuk dan Teknologi* (RBT — Design and Technology) textbooks — see lesson/sources.md. Malaysia counts in the acceptance count (student books, not evidence-only).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **Permanent joins: sewing and embroidery** — Fashion is joined — teknik cantuman — above all by sewing, by hand and by machine, and by embroidery (sulaman): bead, appliqué, thread and ribbon work that joins while it decorates.
2. **Patching and temporary joins** — Patching/appliqué (tampalan) joins with fabric glue, hot glue, wax or all-purpose glue; and mock-ups are joined temporarily — stapler, cellulose tape, string ties for accessories — so a design can be tried before it is committed.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of Textiles and Fashion. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name the joining techniques and what each joins.
2. Choose lasting versus temporary joins for a purpose.
3. Explain how Malaysia's book teaches it.
## What you need
Fabric scraps, needles, glues, stapler and tape for trials.
