# Hands-on — Fabrics

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## Follow one thread
Unpick a few centimetres of stitching from an old cotton garment. Roll the thread between your fingers and look for the twist. Write one sentence on what the twist does.

## Order the making
Write the four steps on four slips of paper, shuffle them, and put them back in order. For each step write what would go wrong if it were skipped.
