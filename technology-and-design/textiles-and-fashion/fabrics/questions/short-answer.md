# Fabrics — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the four steps from boll to thread, in order.
**Answer:** Answer: Harvesting, ginning, grading, spinning.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain why the steps must happen in order.
**Answer:** Answer: Each step removes a problem the next step cannot fix — seeds must leave before spinning, and fibres must be sorted before they are twisted, or the yarn is weak and lumpy.
