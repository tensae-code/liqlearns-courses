# Fabrics — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Spinning twists fibres together into a continuous thread.
**Answer: True** — Twisting is what turns loose fibre into yarn.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Grading can be skipped with no effect on the yarn.
**Answer: False** — Ungraded, mixed fibre spins unevenly and weakly.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Two cotton threads can behave differently because their making differed.
**Answer: True** — Picking, cleaning, sorting and twisting choices change the thread.
