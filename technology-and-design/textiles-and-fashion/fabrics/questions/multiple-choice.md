# Fabrics — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is ginning?
a) Separating the cotton fibre from the seeds b) Dyeing the fibre c) Weaving the thread d) Cutting the cloth
**Answer: a) Separating the cotton fibre from the seeds**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which step comes immediately before spinning?
a) Grading b) Harvesting c) Ginning d) Sewing
**Answer: a) Grading**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Why must grading come before spinning?
a) Mixed long and short fibres spin into a weak, lumpy yarn b) Grading makes the fibre white c) Spinning cannot start in the morning d) Grading removes the colour
**Answer: a) Mixed long and short fibres spin into a weak, lumpy yarn**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** What happens if seeds are left in the fibre?
a) They break the thread during spinning b) They make the thread stronger c) They dye the thread d) Nothing
**Answer: a) They break the thread during spinning**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Why is thread called a made material?
a) Human decisions in picking, cleaning, sorting and twisting are built into it b) It grows ready-made on the plant c) It is made of plastic d) It is never touched by people
**Answer: a) Human decisions in picking, cleaning, sorting and twisting are built into it**
