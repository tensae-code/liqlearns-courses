# Hands-on — Standards in the fashion industry

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: Quality Control and Quality Assurance; What makes these two concepts so important in raising standards in fashion industry. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
