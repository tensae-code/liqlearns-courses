# Lesson plan — Hats and bags

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Textiles and Fashion · **Lesson:** Hats and bags
**Place in the curriculum:** Textiles and Fashion (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Papua New Guinea expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Hats and bags — Textiles Technology unit: Hats and Bags.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on teacher-guide evidence only — Papua New Guinea's *Technology and Industrial Arts (TIA)* teacher guides (Department of Education, Papua New Guinea) are the books in hand for this topic (see lesson/sources.md). It is counted nowhere in the acceptance count.

Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.
