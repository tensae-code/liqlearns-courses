# Interior design

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Moldova's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Arrangement for the way rooms are used
Grade 9 interior design starts from use: an interior is arranged for the way its rooms are actually used — who is in them, doing what, when. Arrangement follows life, not the furniture catalogue.

## Sub lesson 2 — Ambient comfort
The arrangement's aim is ambient comfort: the room feels right to be in — its light, air, space and order serving the people in it. Comfort is the design's verdict, checked in the room itself.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: interior design arranges for life: arrangement and ambient comfort, designing for the way rooms are actually used. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
