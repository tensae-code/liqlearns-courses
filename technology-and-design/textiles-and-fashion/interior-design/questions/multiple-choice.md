# Interior design — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does interior arrangement follow?
a) The way rooms are actually used b) The catalogue c) The alphabet d) Chance
**Answer: a) The way rooms are actually used**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What must be known first?
a) The paint price b) Who uses the room, for what, and when c) The shop hours d) Nothing
**Answer: b) Who uses the room, for what, and when**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is the arrangement's aim?
a) Ambient comfort b) Maximum furniture c) Maximum cost d) Emptiness
**Answer: a) Ambient comfort**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Where is comfort checked?
a) In the room itself — light, air, space and order b) In a catalogue c) In a test d) Nowhere
**Answer: a) In the room itself — light, air, space and order**

**5. (Sub lesson 1)** A room arranged against its use is…
a) Well designed b) Cheaper c) Badly designed, however it looks d) Traditional
**Answer: c) Badly designed, however it looks**
