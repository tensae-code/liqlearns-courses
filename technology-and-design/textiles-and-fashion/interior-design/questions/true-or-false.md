# Interior design — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Interior design designs for the way rooms are used.
**Answer: True** — Use — who, what, when — opens the design.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Ambient comfort is only about temperature.
**Answer: False** — Comfort is the room's whole feel — light, air, space and order serving its people.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Comfort is checked in the room itself.
**Answer: True** — The room is where the design's verdict is read.
