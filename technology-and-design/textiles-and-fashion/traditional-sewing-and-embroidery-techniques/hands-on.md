# Hands-on — Traditional sewing and embroidery techniques

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## Read an ornament
Study a folk garment or ritual object (real or pictured). Sketch its ornament and note its colours and where the embroidery sits.

## Work the tradition
Embroider a small motif in traditional ornament and colour, using ornamental stitches. Label the stitches you used.
