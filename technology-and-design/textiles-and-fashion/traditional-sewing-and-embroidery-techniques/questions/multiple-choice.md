# Traditional sewing and embroidery techniques — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What carries traditional embroidery?
a) Folk garments, and household and ritual objects b) Only pictures c) Only school books d) Only bags
**Answer: a) Folk garments, and household and ritual objects**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The tradition's language has which two parts?
a) Price and size b) Ornament and colour c) Speed and noise d) Ink and paper
**Answer: b) Ornament and colour**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What works the ornament?
a) Sewing and embroidery techniques and ornamental stitches b) Printing c) Weaving only d) Painting only
**Answer: a) Sewing and embroidery techniques and ornamental stitches**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** What is read first?
a) The ornament — its forms and colours b) The price c) The shop d) The date
**Answer: a) The ornament — its forms and colours**

**5. (Sub lesson 1)** Ritual objects are…
a) Excluded from embroidery b) Made of metal c) Among the embroidered things of the folk world d) Never decorated
**Answer: c) Among the embroidered things of the folk world**
