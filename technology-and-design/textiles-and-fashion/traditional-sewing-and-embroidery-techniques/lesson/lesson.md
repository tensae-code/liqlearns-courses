# Traditional sewing and embroidery techniques

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Moldova's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Where the embroidery lives
Grades 5–6 open embroidery from the tradition: folk garments decorated with embroidery, and household and ritual objects decorated the same way. Embroidery is not a hobby corner — it is how the folk world marks its clothes and its sacred and household things.

## Sub lesson 2 — Traditional ornament, colour and stitches
The tradition has its language: traditional ornament and colour, worked with sewing and embroidery techniques and ornamental stitches. The learner reads the ornament first — its forms and colours — then works it with the stitches that carry it.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: traditional embroidery decorates the folk world: garments and household and ritual objects carry traditional ornament and colour, worked with the tradition's sewing and embroidery techniques and ornamental stitches. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
