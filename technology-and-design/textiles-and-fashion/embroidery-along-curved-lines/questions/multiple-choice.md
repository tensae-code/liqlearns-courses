# Embroidery along curved lines — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why are stitches shorter on a curve?
a) The line bends a little at every step b) Thread is expensive c) Short stitches are always faster d) Curves need no stitches
**Answer: a) The line bends a little at every step**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What happens if you sew a curve with long straight stitches?
a) The stitching cuts across the bend instead of following it b) It follows the curve better c) Nothing changes d) The curve straightens itself
**Answer: a) The stitching cuts across the bend instead of following it**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** How is the work handled on a curve?
a) Turned gradually as you sew b) Never turned c) Folded in half d) Held upside down only
**Answer: a) Turned gradually as you sew**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** What is embroidered in this lesson?
a) A flower b) A fence c) A truck d) A bowl
**Answer: a) A flower**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Which parts of the flower are followed stitch by stitch?
a) Its curved lines — petals and stem b) Only its centre dot c) The cloth's edge d) Nothing — it is glued
**Answer: a) Its curved lines — petals and stem**
