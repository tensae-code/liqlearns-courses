# Embroidery along curved lines — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How do you keep stitching smooth around a bend?
**Answer:** Take short, even stitches and turn the work gradually, so the line of stitches follows the curve.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What skill from the earlier lesson does embroidery on curves build on?
**Answer:** Sewing even stitches along a line — first learned on a straight line, now carried around bends.
