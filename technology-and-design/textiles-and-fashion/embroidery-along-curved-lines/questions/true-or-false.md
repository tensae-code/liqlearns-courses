# Embroidery along curved lines — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Long stitches follow a curve best.
**Answer: False** — Long straight stitches cut across the bend; short stitches follow it.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The flower's petals are embroidered along their curved lines.
**Answer: True** — Petals and stem are stitched stitch by stitch along their bends.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The work is never turned while embroidering a curve.
**Answer: False** — The work is turned gradually so the stitching can follow the bend.
