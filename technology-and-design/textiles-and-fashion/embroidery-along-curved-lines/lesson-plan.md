# Lesson plan — Embroidery along curved lines

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Textiles and Fashion · **Lesson:** Embroidery along curved lines
**Place in the curriculum:** Textiles and Fashion (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Azerbaijan expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Embroidery along curved lines carries the straight-line stitch around bends: short, even stitches follow the curve — here embroidering a flower.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on a student textbook — Azerbaijan's *Технология (Technology), Grade 2*, Russian edition as held (see lesson/sources.md). It counts in the acceptance count (student book, not evidence-only).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **From straight stitching to curves** — A curve cannot be sewn with long straight stitches: the line bends a little at every step.
2. **Embroidering a flower** — The flower's curved lines — petals and stem — are embroidered stitch by stitch along their bends.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
