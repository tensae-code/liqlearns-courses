# Hands-on — Designing textile products for the home

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: an apron/potholder design: pattern and sketch; choosing and preparing the material; the sequence of work; cutting, processing the details, assembly, finishing and decorating (§§23–28). Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
