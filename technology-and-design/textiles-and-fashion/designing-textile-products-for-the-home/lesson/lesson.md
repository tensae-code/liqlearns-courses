# Designing textile products for the home

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Kazakhstan's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Designing textile products for the home
Kazakhstan's books teach it this way (the student book, printed pp. 64–78): An apron/potholder design: pattern and sketch; choosing and preparing the material; the sequence of work; cutting, processing the details, assembly, finishing and decorating (§§23–28). The learner works through the named materials and steps in order, saying what each step does before doing the next one.

## Sub lesson 2 — Designing textile products for the home — doing it yourself
Now the learner does the whole task unaided: an apron/potholder design: pattern and sketch; choosing and preparing the material; the sequence of work; cutting, processing the details, assembly, finishing and decorating (§§23–28). Working from the first sub lesson's sequence, the learner plans the make, works through it step by step, and checks the finished piece against what the Kazakh book shows.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: Designing textile products for the home — an apron/potholder design: pattern and sketch; choosing and preparing the material; the sequence of work; cutting, processing the details, assembly, finishing and decorating (§§23–28). Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.

## Do It Yourself
1. Do the Do it first task again, carefully this time, using everything the lesson taught.
