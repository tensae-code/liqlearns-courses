# Ethnostyle garment design

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Kazakhstan's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Ethnostyle garment design
Kazakhstan's books teach it this way (the student book, printed pp. 24–30): Kazakh national clothing in ethnostyle: designing a product in ethnostyle (headdress, waistcoat) and creating it (§§7–9). The learner works through the named materials and steps in order, saying what each step does before doing the next one.

## Sub lesson 2 — Ethnostyle garment design — doing it yourself
Now the learner does the whole task unaided: Kazakh national clothing in ethnostyle: designing a product in ethnostyle (headdress, waistcoat) and creating it (§§7–9). Working from the first sub lesson's sequence, the learner plans the make, works through it step by step, and checks the finished piece against what the Kazakh book shows.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: Ethnostyle garment design — Kazakh national clothing in ethnostyle: designing a product in ethnostyle (headdress, waistcoat) and creating it (§§7–9). Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
