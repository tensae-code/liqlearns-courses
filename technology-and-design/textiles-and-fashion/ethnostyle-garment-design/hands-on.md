# Hands-on — Ethnostyle garment design

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: Kazakh national clothing in ethnostyle: designing a product in ethnostyle (headdress, waistcoat) and creating it (§§7–9). Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
