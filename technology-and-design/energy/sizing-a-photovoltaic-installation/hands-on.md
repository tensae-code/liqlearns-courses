# Hands-on — Sizing a photovoltaic installation

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## Size the Kay peyi house
From the lesson's load list, sum the appliance powers to choose the inverter, then calculate the battery capacity in Ah from the stated energy need and regulator voltage. Show every step.

## Size another house
Invent a three-room house with five appliances. Build its load list, size its inverter and its battery, and write one sentence on what changed most from the Kay peyi sizing and why.
