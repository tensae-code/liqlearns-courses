# Sizing a photovoltaic installation — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Why is the inverter sized from a sum rather than from the largest single appliance?
**Answer:** Because several appliances may run at once; the inverter must supply their combined power, not just one appliance's.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** State the battery calculation and its unit.
**Answer:** Battery capacity (in amp-hours, Ah) = the energy the house needs from storage ÷ the regulator voltage.
