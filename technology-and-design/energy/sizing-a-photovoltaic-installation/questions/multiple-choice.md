# Sizing a photovoltaic installation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Sizing begins with…
a) Buying the biggest panel b) The load list — appliances and their watts c) Painting the roof d) Choosing a battery colour
**Answer: b) The load list — appliances and their watts**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Appliance powers are given in…
a) Amp-hours b) Watts c) Volts only d) Litres
**Answer: b) Watts**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The inverter is sized by…
a) Guessing b) Summing the powers of the appliances that may run at once c) The house's colour d) The panel's size alone
**Answer: b) Summing the powers of the appliances that may run at once**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** An undersized inverter means…
a) Free electricity b) The house's appliances defeat the installation c) Better storage d) Nothing changes
**Answer: b) The house's appliances defeat the installation**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Battery capacity in amp-hours is calculated as…
a) Energy × voltage b) Energy ÷ regulator voltage c) Power + time in days d) Voltage ÷ energy
**Answer: b) Energy ÷ regulator voltage**
