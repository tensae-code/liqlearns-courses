# Lesson plan — Sizing a photovoltaic installation

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Energy · **Lesson:** Sizing a photovoltaic installation
**Place in the curriculum:** Energy (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Haiti expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** sizing (dimensionnement) starts from the load list in watts: the inverter is chosen by summing the appliances' powers, and the battery's capacity in amp-hours comes from the energy needed divided by the regulator voltage.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on teacher-guide evidence only — Haiti's ITAP Projet Nectar pedagogical sequences (MENFP) are the books in hand for this topic (see lesson/sources.md). Teacher guides are evidence, counted nowhere in the acceptance count.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **List the loads first** — Every appliance the house will run, with its power in watts.
2. **The inverter — sum the powers** — The inverter must carry the appliances together.
3. **The battery — amp-hours from energy ÷ voltage** — Storage sized by a division, not by the biggest battery available.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of Energy. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Build a load list with powers in watts.
2. Size an inverter by summing appliance powers.
3. Calculate battery capacity in Ah from energy ÷ voltage.
## What you need
The Kay peyi load list (lamps, television, fan, radio with powers), a calculator, and the sizing formulae from the lesson.
