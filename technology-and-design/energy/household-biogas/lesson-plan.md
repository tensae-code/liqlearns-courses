# Lesson plan — Household biogas

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Energy · **Lesson:** Household biogas
**Place in the curriculum:** Energy (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Indonesia expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** household biogas: digester design, anaerobic fermentation of waste, methane gas for the home.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on teacher-guide evidence only — Indonesia's Prakarya teacher guides (Kemendikbudristek, Pusat Perbukuan, Kurikulum Merdeka editions) are the books in hand for this topic (see lesson/sources.md). ## Sub lessons

3. **The Indonesian way — local potential, safety and care** — Prakarya builds from local materials and local potential (potensi lokal), plans before making, and puts safety first.
