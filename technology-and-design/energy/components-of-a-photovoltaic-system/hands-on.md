# Hands-on — Components of a photovoltaic system

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## Chain diagram
Draw the energy chain of a photovoltaic system with all four components and their jobs. Under it, write what stops if each component is removed — four short answers.

## Component hunt
From photographs of a real installation, identify the four components and the order energy passes through them. Note any component you cannot find and what evidence would settle it.
