# Lesson plan — Components of a photovoltaic system

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Energy · **Lesson:** Components of a photovoltaic system
**Place in the curriculum:** Energy (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Haiti expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** a photovoltaic system has four main components in one energy chain — panel, regulator, battery, inverter — and the inverter's role in the chain is to change direct current into alternating current.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on teacher-guide evidence only — Haiti's ITAP Projet Nectar pedagogical sequences (MENFP) are the books in hand for this topic (see lesson/sources.md). Teacher guides are evidence, counted nowhere in the acceptance count.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **The four main components** — Panel, regulator, battery, inverter — each with one job.
2. **The energy chain** — Energy flows in one order from sunlight to appliance.
3. **The inverter's job — DC to AC** — Why the chain needs a converter at its end.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of Energy. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name the four main components and each job.
2. Give the energy chain in order.
3. Explain why the inverter changes DC to AC.
## What you need
Component pictures or the real components, the energy-chain diagram, and the Kay peyi installation schema to connect the two.
