# Components of a photovoltaic system — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The four main components are…
a) Panel, regulator, battery, inverter b) Panel, wire, lamp, switch c) Battery, motor, wheel, frame d) Regulator, roof, wall, door
**Answer: a) Panel, regulator, battery, inverter**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The regulator's job is to…
a) Make sunlight b) Control charging and protect the battery c) Change DC to AC d) Store electricity
**Answer: b) Control charging and protect the battery**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The correct energy-chain order is…
a) Battery → panel → inverter → regulator b) Panel → regulator → battery → inverter → loads c) Inverter → battery → panel → loads d) Loads → inverter → panel → battery
**Answer: b) Panel → regulator → battery → inverter → loads**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Each connection in the schema exists because…
a) It looks neat b) Energy passes that way in the chain c) The wire was spare d) Rules demand decoration
**Answer: b) Energy passes that way in the chain**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** The inverter changes…
a) AC into DC b) DC into AC c) Light into heat d) Watts into volts
**Answer: b) DC into AC**
