# Components of a photovoltaic system — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Write the energy chain of a photovoltaic system in order.
**Answer:** Sunlight → panel (DC produced) → regulator (charging controlled) → battery (energy stored) → inverter (DC changed to AC) → the house's appliances.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Why must the inverter stand between the battery and the house's appliances?
**Answer:** Because the battery supplies direct current while the appliances run on alternating current; the inverter converts DC to AC so the stored electricity can run them.
