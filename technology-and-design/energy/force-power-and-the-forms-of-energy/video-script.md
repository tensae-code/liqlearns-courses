# Video script — Force, power and the forms of energy

A short demonstration video, made later, in a super-simple voice. The demonstrator works through the topic while the voice speaks.

---

This lesson is about force, power and the forms of energy.
Mechanical, heat, electrical, sound and magnetic energy, each with its everyday machine example (vehicle engine, torch, electric fan, radio, magnet).
Work step by step. Check your work as you go.
