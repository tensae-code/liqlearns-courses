# Lesson plan — Generating electrical energy

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Energy · **Lesson:** Generating electrical energy
**Place in the curriculum:** Energy (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Moldova expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** electrical energy is obtained by technologies suited to the geographic zone — and it spreads furthest of all energies because it travels through conductors, transforms, and converts easily into other forms.
**This lesson's own level:** stocked from Moldova's *Educație tehnologică* (Technological Education) textbooks — see lesson/sources.md. Moldova counts in the acceptance count (student books, not evidence-only).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **Technologies suited to the zone** — Grade 9 energy work begins with place: the technologies for obtaining electrical energy are chosen suited to the geographic zone — the generation a region's land, water, wind and sun can actually feed.
2. **Why electricity spreads furthest** — The lesson then explains electricity's reach: electrical energy spreads furthest because it is transported through conductors, it can be transformed — raised and lowered — for the journey, and it converts easily into other forms at the point of use: light, heat, motion..

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of Energy. Recommended, never forced.

1. Match generating technologies to a geographic zone.
2. Explain why electrical energy spreads furthest.
3. Explain how Moldova's book teaches it.
