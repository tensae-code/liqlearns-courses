# Generating electrical energy — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** One generating technology suits every geographic zone equally.
**Answer: False** — Technologies are chosen suited to the zone.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Transformation helps electricity travel far.
**Answer: True** — It is raised and lowered for the journey through conductors.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Easy conversion into other forms is one of electricity's spread-advantages.
**Answer: True** — Light, heat and motion at the point of use complete the chain.
