# Generating electrical energy — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How are generating technologies chosen?
a) Suited to the geographic zone b) The same everywhere c) By price only d) By alphabet
**Answer: a) Suited to the geographic zone**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What feeds a zone's choice?
a) Fashion b) Its land, water, wind and sun — what the region can actually feed c) Imports only d) Nothing
**Answer: b) Its land, water, wind and sun — what the region can actually feed**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** How is electrical energy transported?
a) Through conductors b) In trucks c) In pipes only d) It cannot travel
**Answer: a) Through conductors**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** What can be done to electricity for its journey?
a) It can be transformed — raised and lowered b) It can be frozen c) It can be painted d) Nothing
**Answer: a) It can be transformed — raised and lowered**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** At the point of use, electricity…
a) Disappears b) Only heats c) Converts easily into other forms — light, heat, motion d) Only lights
**Answer: c) Converts easily into other forms — light, heat, motion**
