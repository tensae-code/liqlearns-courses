# Generating electrical energy — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why must generation suit the zone?
**Answer:** Because each region can only feed generation from what it has — its land, water, wind and sun.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Give the three reasons electricity spreads furthest.
**Answer:** It is transported through conductors, it can be transformed for the journey, and it converts easily into other forms.
