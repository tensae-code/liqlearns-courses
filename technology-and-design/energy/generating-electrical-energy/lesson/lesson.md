# Generating electrical energy

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Moldova's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Technologies suited to the zone
Grade 9 energy work begins with place: the technologies for obtaining electrical energy are chosen suited to the geographic zone — the generation a region's land, water, wind and sun can actually feed. There is no universal generator; there is the right technology for the zone.

## Sub lesson 2 — Why electricity spreads furthest
The lesson then explains electricity's reach: electrical energy spreads furthest because it is transported through conductors, it can be transformed — raised and lowered — for the journey, and it converts easily into other forms at the point of use: light, heat, motion.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: electrical energy is obtained by technologies suited to the geographic zone — and it spreads furthest of all energies because it travels through conductors, transforms, and converts easily into other forms. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
