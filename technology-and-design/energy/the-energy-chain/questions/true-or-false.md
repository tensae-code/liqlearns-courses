# The energy chain — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Distribute decides which parts receive energy, and how much.
**Answer: True** — Feed supplies the whole system; distribution routes it under control — the hair-dryer's settings are distribution choices.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Transmission and transformation of movement are the same thing.
**Answer: False** — Transmission carries a movement (rotation stays rotation); transformation changes its kind (rotation into translation, for example).

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A weak link anywhere in the chain caps the action everywhere.
**Answer: True** — The actuator can only spend what feed, distribution, conversion and transmission deliver.
