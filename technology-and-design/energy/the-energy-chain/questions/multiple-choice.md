# The energy chain — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The energy chain runs…
a) feed, distribute, convert, transmit, act b) act, feed, convert, store, sell c) feed only — one function d) sense, decide, display
**Answer: a) feed, distribute, convert, transmit, act**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The feed function must state…
a) the nature of the current, the voltage and the power b) the colour of the cables c) the user's name d) the room temperature
**Answer: a) the nature of the current, the voltage and the power**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** In a DC motor, the rotor is…
a) the turning part; the stator stands fixed b) the fixed part c) the battery d) the switch
**Answer: a) the turning part; the stator stands fixed**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A dynamo is…
a) the motor run in reverse: rotation converted into electricity b) a kind of battery c) a broken motor d) a pneumatic cylinder
**Answer: a) the motor run in reverse: rotation converted into electricity**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Actuators are found…
a) at the end of the chain, spending energy on the task b) at the feed, storing energy c) outside the chain d) only in diagrams
**Answer: a) at the end of the chain, spending energy on the task**
