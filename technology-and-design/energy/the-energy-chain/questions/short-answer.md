# The energy chain — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the five functions in order, French and English.
**Answer:** Alimenter/feed, distribuer/distribute, convertir/convert, transmettre/transmit, agir/act.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Read a wind turbine as an energy chain in one sentence per function.
**Answer:** Feed: the wind delivers mechanical energy. Distribute: the yaw/pitch controls orient and limit it. Convert: the alternator turns rotation into electricity. Transmit: cables and converter carry it to the network. Act: the energy is spent in the loads it serves (in this reading, delivery is the action end).
