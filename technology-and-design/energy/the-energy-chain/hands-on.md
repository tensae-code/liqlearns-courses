# Hands-on — The energy chain

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## Chain the hair-dryer
Write the hair-dryer's full energy chain: name the carrier of each of the five functions, and for feed state the supply requirements (current nature, voltage, power). Add one distribution choice the user makes with its controls.

## Motor and dynamo
Draw a DC motor labelled stator/rotor and write its conversion in one sentence. Then write the dynamo sentence (same machine, reversed conversion) and one place each direction is used.
