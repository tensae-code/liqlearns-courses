Morocco's own way: Morocco teaches renewable energy technology inside the systems course — sources first, then a hydroelectric plant's functioning and energy-flow diagram, wind-turbine parts, solar electric set against solar thermal, and the energy-efficiency class — with Morocco's own installed-capacity figures worked as an exercise.
