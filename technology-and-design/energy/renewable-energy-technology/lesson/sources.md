Haiti programme note: Haiti's ETAP programmes for the 3e cycle (MENFP, Direction Enseignement Fondamental, version définitive 28 juillet 2024) are curriculum/framework documents, recorded RECORD-ONLY in the placements file and not stocked; the teaching stocked here comes from the Projet Nectar pedagogical sequences that deliver the programme. The LibreOffice Impress (PAO) Nectar sequence in the same placements file is likewise RECORD-ONLY — pure ICT content, stocked in the ICT course, not here.

Haiti's own way: Haiti teaches renewable energy through one house: the maison « Kay peyi » and its lamps, television, fan and radio are the standing example for the sources, the chain and every later calculation in the sequence.

Morocco's own way: Morocco teaches renewable energy technology inside the systems course — sources first, then a hydroelectric plant's functioning and energy-flow diagram, wind-turbine parts, solar electric set against solar thermal, and the energy-efficiency class — with Morocco's own installed-capacity figures worked as an exercise. That national-systems framing is carried here exactly as the guide teaches it.

Iran's own way: Iran teaches renewable energy technology source by source in Grade 11 — solar (solar thermal and photovoltaic systems, with a build-it-yourself box solar cooker activity), wind (turbine parts named: rotor, tower, power transmission, generator, control), geothermal (direct and power-plant use), biomass and biogas (the plant's three parts: inlet, digester tank, outlet), and hydro with pumped storage for peak hours, tidal and ocean-wave (Pelamis) power.
