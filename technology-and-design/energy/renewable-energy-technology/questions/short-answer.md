# Renewable energy technology — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name three renewable sources the sequence identifies.
**Answer:** Any three of: sunlight, wind, flowing water, and biomass (plant and animal matter).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why does the installation need a battery if the panel already makes electricity?
**Answer:** Because the house needs power when the sun is not shining; the battery stores the panel's electricity for those hours.
