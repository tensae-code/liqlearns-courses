# Renewable energy technology — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A fuel that is used up is a renewable source.
**Answer: False** — Renewable sources are replaced by nature; a fuel consumed once is not renewed.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The inverter changes stored direct current into alternating current for the house.
**Answer: True** — That conversion is the inverter's one job in the chain.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The photovoltaic chain works the same with any link removed.
**Answer: False** — Each link has one job; removing storage, for example, leaves power only while the sun shines.
