# Renewable energy technology — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A renewable source is one that…
a) Is used up once b) Nature replaces c) Must be imported d) Never gives energy
**Answer: b) Nature replaces**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which of these is renewable?
a) Kerosene b) Sunlight c) A disposable battery d) Charcoal from a felled forest used once
**Answer: b) Sunlight**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The panel's job in the chain is to…
a) Store electricity b) Capture sunlight and produce direct current c) Change DC to AC d) Protect the battery
**Answer: b) Capture sunlight and produce direct current**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Without storage, a photovoltaic system gives power…
a) All night b) Only while the sun shines c) Never d) For a week
**Answer: b) Only while the sun shines**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** The Kay peyi house loads named in the sequence are…
a) Lamps, television, fan and radio b) A factory line c) Street lighting d) A water pump station
**Answer: a) Lamps, television, fan and radio**
