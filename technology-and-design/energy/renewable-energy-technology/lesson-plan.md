# Lesson plan — Renewable energy technology

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Energy · **Lesson:** Renewable energy technology
**Place in the curriculum:** Energy (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Haiti expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** renewable energy technology harvests sources that renew themselves — sun, wind, water, biomass — and a photovoltaic system turns sunlight into electricity a house can use.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on teacher-guide evidence only — Haiti's ITAP Projet Nectar pedagogical sequences (MENFP) are the books in hand for this topic (see lesson/sources.md). Teacher guides are evidence, counted nowhere in the acceptance count.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **Renewable sources around the house** — Sources that renew themselves, identified where the learner lives.
2. **How a photovoltaic installation works** — Sunlight becomes electricity, stored and converted for the house.
3. **The Kay peyi house as the example** — The whole system is taught on one Haitian two-room house.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of Energy. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Identify renewable sources in a local place.
2. Explain the photovoltaic chain link by link.
3. Name the Kay peyi house loads the system serves.
## What you need
Pictures of renewable sources and of a domestic PV installation, and the component chain diagram from the lesson.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — the opening doing task in lesson/lesson.md.
2. **The explanation** — the sub lessons in order (lesson/lesson.md).
   - ADD IMAGE: a picture of a Kay peyi house with its photovoltaic installation — panel on the roof, battery and inverter inside, lamps, television, fan and radio as the loads — the student traces the chain from sunlight to one working appliance.
   - ADD GIF: a GIF of sunlight striking a panel, current flowing to a battery, then through an inverter to a lit lamp — the student names each link's job as the current passes it.
   - ADD AUDIO: a voice reading the photovoltaic chain — panel, regulator, battery, inverter, house aloud — the student repeats them.
   - ADD VIDEO: a short video of the lesson demonstration — see video-script.md.
3. **Say it to your companion** — the learner explains the lesson's key rule aloud.
4. **Questions** — the questions/ files, spread over the sub lessons.
5. **Hands-on** — hands-on.md.
6. **Do It Yourself** — the closing doing block in lesson/lesson.md.
