# Hands-on — Solvents and solutions in conservation

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: kinds of solution, solubility and concentration, solvents used in conserving antiquities and artworks, their dangers and uses. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
