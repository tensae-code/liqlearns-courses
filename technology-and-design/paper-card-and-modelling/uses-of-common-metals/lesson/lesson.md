# Uses of common metals

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Rwanda's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — The common metals and their uses
The middle metals lesson is a survey of use: the common metals, each with the work it does in the world the learner lives in. A metal's uses are its characteristics at work — the survey quietly proves the classification that came before it.

## Sub lesson 2 — Use follows character
No metal is used at random: each use fits the family's general characteristics learned in the classification lesson. Reading uses back to characteristics is the skill — given a new job, the learner can now argue which metal family member belongs in it.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: between classification and maintenance stands use: Rwanda's materials unit names what the common metals are actually used for, so the family is known by its work in daily life. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
