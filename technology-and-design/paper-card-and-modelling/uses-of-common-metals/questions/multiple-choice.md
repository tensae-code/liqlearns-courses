# Uses of common metals — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Where does the uses lesson stand?
a) Before classification b) Between classification and maintenance c) After maintenance d) In another unit
**Answer: b) Between classification and maintenance**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the uses survey show?
a) Prices b) What each common metal is used for in daily life c) Colours d) Inventors
**Answer: b) What each common metal is used for in daily life**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A metal's uses are…
a) Random b) Its characteristics at work c) Chosen by shops d) Secret
**Answer: b) Its characteristics at work**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** What skill does the lesson build?
a) Memorising prices b) Arguing which metal belongs in a new job, from characteristics c) Drawing metals d) Selling metals
**Answer: b) Arguing which metal belongs in a new job, from characteristics**

**5. (Sub lesson 1)** The uses lesson quietly proves which earlier lesson?
a) The waste lesson b) The classification of materials c) The circuit lesson d) The levers lesson
**Answer: b) The classification of materials**
