# Uses of common metals — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Uses of common metals is the first metals lesson.
**Answer: False** — Classification comes first; uses is the middle lesson, maintenance the last.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Metals are used at random, without regard to characteristics.
**Answer: False** — Each use fits the family's general characteristics.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Given a new job, the learner can argue for a metal from its characteristics.
**Answer: True** — Reading uses back to characteristics is the lesson's skill.
