# Paints as materials — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Say in one sentence what this lesson teaches.
**Answer:** Paints as materials — kinds of paint (watercolour, gouache, acrylic) and painting stones; a ladybird built from a painted stone (Lesson 4).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name one material, tool or step this lesson works with.
**Answer:** Any one named in the lesson text — for example, the first step of sub lesson 1: Paints as materials.
