# Hands-on — Paints as materials

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: kinds of paint (watercolour, gouache, acrylic) and painting stones; a ladybird built from a painted stone (Lesson 4). Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
