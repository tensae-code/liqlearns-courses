# Plastic recycling codes — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Say in one sentence what this lesson teaches.
**Answer:** Plastic recycling codes — the marks 1–7 (PET, HDPE, PVC, LDPE, PP, PS, other) — what each plastic is used for and whether it can be recycled or heated.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name one material, tool or step this lesson works with.
**Answer:** Any one named in the lesson text — for example, the first step of sub lesson 1: Plastic recycling codes.
