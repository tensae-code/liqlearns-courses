# Hands-on — Plastic recycling codes

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: the marks 1–7 (PET, HDPE, PVC, LDPE, PP, PS, other) — what each plastic is used for and whether it can be recycled or heated. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
