# Hands-on — Board-game design project

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: board games and their history (§12); developing the board game (§13); making the game's details (§14); graphic documentation (§15); testing the game (§16); analysis and presenting the work (§§17–18) — Togyzkumalak as the worked example. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
