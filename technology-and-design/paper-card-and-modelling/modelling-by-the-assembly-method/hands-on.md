# Hands-on — Modelling by the assembly (joining) method

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: kneading, rolling, flattening plasticine/clay; zoo animals made from separately made parts then joined; a paper fence built (Lesson 18). Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
