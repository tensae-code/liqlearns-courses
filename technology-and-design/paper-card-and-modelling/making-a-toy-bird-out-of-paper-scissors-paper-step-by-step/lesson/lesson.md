# Making a toy bird out of paper

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Rwanda's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — The materials: scissors and paper
Rwanda's Primary 2 making begins with the smallest kit: scissors and paper. Naming the materials first is the discipline — a make starts by knowing exactly what it needs, nothing more.

## Sub lesson 2 — The steps of the making
The bird is made step by step: each step changes the paper a little, in order, until the toy stands as a bird. Following steps in order — and seeing the toy arrive at the end of them — is the whole first lesson of production.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: with only scissors and paper, step by step, a flat sheet becomes a toy bird — Rwanda's first making lesson teaches that making is a sequence anyone can follow. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.

## Do It Yourself
1. Do the Do it first task again, carefully this time, using everything the lesson taught.
2. Check your work against the lesson's own checks — fix one thing you find.
3. Keep your work: it is practice evidence, and the next lesson builds on it.
