# Making a toy bird out of paper — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The toy bird is made from clay.
**Answer: False** — The bird is made from paper, with scissors — clay belongs to the modelling lessons.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Steps must be followed in order for the toy to arrive.
**Answer: True** — Making is a sequence: each step changes the paper a little, in order.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** A make starts by naming its materials.
**Answer: True** — Scissors and paper are named first — knowing exactly what a make needs is the discipline.
