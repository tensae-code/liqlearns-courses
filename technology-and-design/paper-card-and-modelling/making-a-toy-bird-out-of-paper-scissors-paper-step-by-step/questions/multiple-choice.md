# Making a toy bird out of paper — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What materials does the toy bird need?
a) Clay and wire b) Scissors and paper c) Wood and nails d) Thread and cloth
**Answer: b) Scissors and paper**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Why are the materials named first?
a) A make starts by knowing exactly what it needs b) For decoration c) Materials do not matter d) To lengthen the lesson
**Answer: a) A make starts by knowing exactly what it needs**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** How is the bird made?
a) All at once b) Step by step, in order c) By guessing d) By buying it
**Answer: b) Step by step, in order**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** What does each step do?
a) Nothing b) Changes the paper a little, toward the bird c) Ruins the paper d) Adds materials
**Answer: b) Changes the paper a little, toward the bird**

**5. (Sub lesson 1)** Where does this make stand in Rwanda's course?
a) The last lesson b) The first making lesson, in Primary 2 c) In Primary 6 d) It is not taught
**Answer: b) The first making lesson, in Primary 2**
