# Making solids from paper

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Rwanda's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — From flat shapes to solids
Every earlier paper make was flat: shapes, polygons, toys. The solids lesson turns the sheet upward — the flat net becomes a form that stands, holds space and casts a shadow. It is the paper climb's last step: 2D mastered, 3D attempted.

## Sub lesson 2 — Making the solids
The solids are made from paper by the learners' own hands, in the same Objects Production unit that began with a paper bird in Primary 2. The material never changed; the demand did — that is the course's quiet argument about depth.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: the paper sequence ends in three dimensions: Primary 6 learners make solids from paper — flat sheets becoming standing, space-holding forms. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
