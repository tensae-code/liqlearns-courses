# Making solids from paper — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is made in this lesson?
a) Flat shapes b) Solids from paper c) Wire toys d) Clay animals
**Answer: b) Solids from paper**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** How do solids differ from the earlier paper makes?
a) They are coloured b) They stand in three dimensions — forms that hold space c) They are bigger only d) They use glue only
**Answer: b) They stand in three dimensions — forms that hold space**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Where does this lesson stand in the paper sequence?
a) First b) The last step — 2D mastered, 3D attempted c) Outside it d) In Primary 2
**Answer: b) The last step — 2D mastered, 3D attempted**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** What stayed the same across the paper years?
a) The demand b) The material — paper; the demand rose c) The shapes d) Nothing
**Answer: b) The material — paper; the demand rose**

**5. (Sub lesson 1)** The first paper make, back in Primary 2, was…
a) A solid b) A toy bird with scissors and paper c) A polygon d) A filter
**Answer: b) A toy bird with scissors and paper**
