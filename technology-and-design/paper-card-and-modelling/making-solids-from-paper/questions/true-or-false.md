# Making solids from paper — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Solids from paper are flat shapes.
**Answer: False** — Solids stand in three dimensions — the flat sheet becomes a space-holding form.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The paper material stays the same while the demand rises across the years.
**Answer: True** — Bird (Primary 2) to solids (Primary 6) — one material, rising demand.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** This is the paper sequence's closing lesson.
**Answer: True** — Flat shapes and polygons come first; solids complete the climb.
