# Lesson plan — Toys, various materials and teaching aids

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Paper, Card and Modelling · **Lesson:** Toys, various materials and teaching aids
**Place in the curriculum:** Paper, Card and Modelling (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Rwanda expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the Kinyarwanda Primary 2 guide plans Unit 2 as toys, various materials and teaching aids — making toys, modelling them in clay, with scissors and paper among the lesson materials.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on teacher-guide evidence only — Rwanda's *Science and Elementary Technology* teacher guides are the books in hand for this topic (see lesson/sources.md). Teacher guides are evidence, counted nowhere in the acceptance count.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **The guide's plan for Unit 2** — This lesson stands on the Kinyarwanda teacher guide alone: Unit 2 (Ibikinisho — toys) is planned as toys, various materials and teaching aids, with the lesson materials listed — umukasi (scissors), urupapuro (paper) among them.
2. **Modelling toys in clay** — Inside the unit the guide names the clay work: Kubumba ibikinisho — modelling toys in clay.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
