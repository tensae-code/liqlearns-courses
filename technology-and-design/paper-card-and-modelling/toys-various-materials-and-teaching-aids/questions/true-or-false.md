# Toys, various materials and teaching aids — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson is stocked from a pupil's book.
**Answer: False** — It stands on the Kinyarwanda teacher guide — evidence only, counted nowhere.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The guide names clay modelling of toys inside Unit 2.
**Answer: True** — Kubumba ibikinisho — modelling toys in clay.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Scissors and paper are among the unit's lesson materials.
**Answer: True** — The guide lists umukasi (scissors) and urupapuro (paper).
