# Toys, various materials and teaching aids — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Whose plan is this lesson built from?
a) The English pupil's book b) The Kinyarwanda Primary 2 teacher guide c) The Primary 6 guide d) No book
**Answer: b) The Kinyarwanda Primary 2 teacher guide**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** How does the guide frame Unit 2?
a) As drawing b) As toys, various materials and teaching aids c) As electricity d) As waste
**Answer: b) As toys, various materials and teaching aids**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Which lesson materials does the guide list?
a) Wire and saws b) Scissors (umukasi) and paper (urupapuro) among them c) Only clay d) Only sticks
**Answer: b) Scissors (umukasi) and paper (urupapuro) among them**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** What clay work does the guide name?
a) Pots b) Kubumba ibikinisho — modelling toys in clay c) Bricks d) None
**Answer: b) Kubumba ibikinisho — modelling toys in clay**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The made toys are also called…
a) Rubbish b) Teaching aids — the class learns with what it makes c) Decorations only d) Products for sale
**Answer: b) Teaching aids — the class learns with what it makes**
