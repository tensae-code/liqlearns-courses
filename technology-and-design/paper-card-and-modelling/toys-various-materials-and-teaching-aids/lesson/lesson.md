# Toys, various materials and teaching aids

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Rwanda's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — The guide's plan for Unit 2
This lesson stands on the Kinyarwanda teacher guide alone: Unit 2 (Ibikinisho — toys) is planned as toys, various materials and teaching aids, with the lesson materials listed — umukasi (scissors), urupapuro (paper) among them. It is the guide's own framing of the pupil book's toys unit.

## Sub lesson 2 — Modelling toys in clay
Inside the unit the guide names the clay work: Kubumba ibikinisho — modelling toys in clay. The guide thus pairs the pupil book's paper and stick makes with clay modelling, and calls the products teaching aids as well as toys: what the class makes, the class learns with.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: the Kinyarwanda Primary 2 guide plans Unit 2 as toys, various materials and teaching aids — making toys, modelling them in clay, with scissors and paper among the lesson materials. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
