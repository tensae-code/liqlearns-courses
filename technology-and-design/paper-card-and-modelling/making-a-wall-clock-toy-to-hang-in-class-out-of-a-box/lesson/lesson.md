# Making a wall clock toy to hang in class out of a box

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Rwanda's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — The material: a box
The clock begins as a box: a flat-faced, ready-shaped object, like the bottle before it a rescued everyday thing. Choosing the box is choosing the clock's face and body in one move.

## Sub lesson 2 — Making and hanging the clock toy
The box becomes a clock — face, hands, the marks of the hours — and the finished toy is hung in class. A make that ends on the wall teaches the full arc of production: material, making, and a real place of use.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: out of a box, the class makes a wall clock toy to hang in class — a make whose finished place is the classroom wall itself. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
