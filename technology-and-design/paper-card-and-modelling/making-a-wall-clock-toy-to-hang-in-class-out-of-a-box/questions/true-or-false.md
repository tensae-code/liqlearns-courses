# Making a wall clock toy to hang in class out of a box — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The clock toy is made from an empty bottle.
**Answer: False** — The bottle makes the airplane; the clock is made out of a box.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The finished clock toy is hung in class.
**Answer: True** — Its place of use is the classroom wall — the make ends in real use.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Choosing the box chooses the clock's face and body together.
**Answer: True** — The box is flat-faced and ready-shaped for the purpose.
