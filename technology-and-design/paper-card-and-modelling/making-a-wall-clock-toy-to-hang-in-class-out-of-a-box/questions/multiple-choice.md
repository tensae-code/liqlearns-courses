# Making a wall clock toy to hang in class out of a box — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the wall clock toy made out of?
a) A bottle b) A box c) Sticks d) Clay
**Answer: b) A box**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Why does the box suit the make?
a) It is flat-faced and ready-shaped — face and body in one move b) It is round c) It is metal d) It tells time already
**Answer: a) It is flat-faced and ready-shaped — face and body in one move**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the box become?
a) A car b) A clock — face, hands and hour marks c) A bird d) A house
**Answer: b) A clock — face, hands and hour marks**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Where does the finished toy go?
a) Home b) Hung in class c) Thrown away d) In a cupboard
**Answer: b) Hung in class**

**5. (Sub lesson 1)** What arc does this make teach?
a) Buying and selling b) Material, making, and a real place of use c) Drawing only d) None
**Answer: b) Material, making, and a real place of use**
