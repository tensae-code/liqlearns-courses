# Hands-on — Working plastics

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: bending polystyrene by heating, cutting PVC foam with a hot wire, welding polyethylene film, and cutting phenolic sheet — with the safety signs for each machine. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
