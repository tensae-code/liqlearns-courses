# Making toys using sorghum straws and sticks

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Rwanda's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — A toy house from sticks and sorghum
The first Primary 5 sorghum make is a toy house from sticks and sorghum: the stalk material now carries walls and a roof — a structure, not only a vehicle. Building a house in miniature teaches what sticks can bear and how they join at corners.

## Sub lesson 2 — A toy bicycle from sorghum
The second make is a toy bicycle in the same materials — the wire bicycle of Primary 4 re-attempted in straws and sticks, so the learner feels what the new material gives and what it refuses.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: sorghum returns in Primary 5, bigger: from straws and sticks the learners make a toy house and a toy bicycle — the Primary 2 car grown into structures. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.

## Do It Yourself
1. Do the Do it first task again, carefully this time, using everything the lesson taught.
2. Check your work against the lesson's own checks — fix one thing you find.
3. Keep your work: it is practice evidence, and the next lesson builds on it.
