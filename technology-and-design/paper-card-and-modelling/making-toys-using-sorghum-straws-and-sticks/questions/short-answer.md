# Making toys using sorghum straws and sticks — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the two Primary 5 sorghum toys.
**Answer:** A toy house and a toy bicycle.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does repeating the bicycle in straw teach?
**Answer:** What the new material gives and what it refuses — material behaviour learned by comparison.
