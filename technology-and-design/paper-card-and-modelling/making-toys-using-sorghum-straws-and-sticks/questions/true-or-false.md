# Making toys using sorghum straws and sticks — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The toy house is made from wire.
**Answer: False** — The house is made from sticks and sorghum.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The Primary 5 bicycle is the wire bicycle re-attempted in straw and sticks.
**Answer: True** — Same toy, new material — the comparison is the teaching.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Sorghum is used in both Primary 2 and Primary 5 makes.
**Answer: True** — The car (Primary 2), then the house and bicycle (Primary 5) — a spiral of materials.
