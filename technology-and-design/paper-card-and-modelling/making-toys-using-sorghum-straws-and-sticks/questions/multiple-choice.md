# Making toys using sorghum straws and sticks — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which two toys are made from sorghum in Primary 5?
a) A car and a plane b) A toy house and a toy bicycle c) A doll and a cat d) A clock and a mat
**Answer: b) A toy house and a toy bicycle**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is new about the house as a make?
a) It is drawn b) It is a structure — walls, corners and a roof in stalk material c) It is bought d) It is clay
**Answer: b) It is a structure — walls, corners and a roof in stalk material**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The sorghum bicycle repeats which earlier make?
a) The clay doll b) The wire toy bicycle of Primary 4, re-attempted in straw c) The paper bird d) The box clock
**Answer: b) The wire toy bicycle of Primary 4, re-attempted in straw**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Why repeat a toy in a new material?
a) For fun only b) So the learner feels what the new material gives and refuses c) Materials do not matter d) To use up straw
**Answer: b) So the learner feels what the new material gives and refuses**

**5. (Sub lesson 1)** Where did sorghum first appear?
a) Primary 5 b) Primary 2 — the toy car from dry sorghum sticks c) Primary 6 d) In the guide only
**Answer: b) Primary 2 — the toy car from dry sorghum sticks**
