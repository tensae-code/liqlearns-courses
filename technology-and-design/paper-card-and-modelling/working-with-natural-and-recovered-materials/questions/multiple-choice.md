# Working with natural and recovered materials — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is recovered material?
a) material saved after use — containers, lids, boxes, offcuts — and used again b) Only a price c) Only decoration d) Nothing to do with this lesson
**Answer: a) material saved after use — containers, lids, boxes, offcuts — and used again**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is threading (ensarte)?
a) Only history b) passing a thread or cord through punched materials to join them c) Only storage d) A kind of glue
**Answer: b) passing a thread or cord through punched materials to join them**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is awl (punzón)?
a) Only old tools b) Only painting c) a pointed tool for perforating, worked over the sufridera block d) Only a price
**Answer: c) a pointed tool for perforating, worked over the sufridera block**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson teach, in one line?
a) Only decoration b) Nothing to do with this lesson c) Only history d) natural and recovered materials are a real stock for making: know what they are, collect and classify them — natural by origin, recovered by source — and make articles, with punching and threading (ensarte) and gluing among the techniques.
**Answer: d) natural and recovered materials are a real stock for making: know what they are, collect and classify them — natural by origin, recovered by source — and make articles, with punching and threading (ensarte) and gluing among the techniques.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** How does Cuba's book teach this?
a) As theory only, never practised b) Only in a glossary c) Only as an exam d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Spanish
**Answer: d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Spanish**
