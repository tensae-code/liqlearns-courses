# Working with natural and recovered materials — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Recovered material is material saved after use — containers, lids, boxes, offcuts — and used again.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Threading (ensarte) has nothing to do with this lesson.
**Answer: False** — It is part of the lesson: passing a thread or cord through punched materials to join them.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Awl (punzón) is a pointed tool for perforating, worked over the sufridera block.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.
