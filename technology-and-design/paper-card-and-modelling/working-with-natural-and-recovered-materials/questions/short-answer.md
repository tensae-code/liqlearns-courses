# Working with natural and recovered materials — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In your own words, what is recovered material?
**Answer:** Material saved after use — containers, lids, boxes, offcuts — and used again.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In your own words, what is awl (punzón)?
**Answer:** A pointed tool for perforating, worked over the sufridera block.
