# Lesson plan — Working with natural and recovered materials

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Paper, Card and Modelling · **Lesson:** Working with natural and recovered materials
**Place in the curriculum:** Paper, Card and Modelling (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Cuba expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** natural and recovered materials are a real stock for making: know what they are, collect and classify them — natural by origin, recovered by source — and make articles, with punching and threading (ensarte) and gluing among the techniques.
**This lesson's own level:** stocked from Cuba's Educación Laboral programmes and methodological orientations (teacher programme documents) — see lesson/sources.md. Cuba is evidence only for this lesson, counted nowhere in the acceptance count.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **Collecting and classifying** — Natural materials are classed by origin — vegetal, animal, mineral. Recovered materials are what use has left: containers, lids, boxes, production offcuts. Cuba's primary programmes teach collecting and classifying as the first operation, before any article is designed.
2. **Making with them** — The techniques follow the materials: punching seeds and shells with awl (punzón) on the sufridera and threading them (ensarte) on thread or cord, gluing (engomado), cutting with round-point scissors. In Grade 7 the lesson widens: classifying paper, card, glass and plastics, and recycling and its economic importance.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
