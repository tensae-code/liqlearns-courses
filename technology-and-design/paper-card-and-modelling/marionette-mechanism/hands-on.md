# Hands-on — Marionette mechanism

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: puppets moved by strings or a rod; a flat camel marionette made; a volumetric horse marionette from waste (cups for head/body, paper-spring legs, strings to a control stick); the orteke dancing-goat puppet worked by the dombra player's fingers (Lessons 19–20). Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
