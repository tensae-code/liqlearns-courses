# Marionette mechanism — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Say in one sentence what this lesson teaches.
**Answer:** Marionette mechanism — puppets moved by strings or a rod; a flat camel marionette made; a volumetric horse marionette from waste (cups for head/body, paper-spring legs, strings to a control stick); the orteke dancing-goat puppet worked by the dombra player's fingers (Lessons 19–20).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name one material, tool or step this lesson works with.
**Answer:** Any one named in the lesson text — for example, the first step of sub lesson 1: Marionette mechanism.
