# Making utility objects in wood — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The spoon in this lesson is made of banana fibre.
**Answer: False** — The spoon is made in wood; banana fibre made the mat and dustbin.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A hoe handle is judged in the hand and in the field.
**Answer: True** — Grip, length and strength are its measures.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Both wood makes are utility objects for daily use.
**Answer: True** — A spoon for the kitchen and a handle for the hoe — the home's own objects.
