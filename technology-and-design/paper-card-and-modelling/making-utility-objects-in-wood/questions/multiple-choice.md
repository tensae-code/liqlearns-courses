# Making utility objects in wood — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which two objects are made in wood?
a) A doll and a bicycle b) A spoon and a hoe handle c) A mat and a dustbin d) A clock and a bird
**Answer: b) A spoon and a hoe handle**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What must a wooden spoon be?
a) Rough and square b) Smooth, shaped to the mouth and the pot c) Painted only d) Hollow metal
**Answer: b) Smooth, shaped to the mouth and the pot**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** How is a hoe handle judged?
a) By colour b) By grip, length and strength c) By price d) By weight alone
**Answer: b) By grip, length and strength**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which earlier unit does the hoe handle tie back to?
a) Electricity b) The agricultural tools of Primary 4 c) Waste d) Paper shapes
**Answer: b) The agricultural tools of Primary 4**

**5. (Sub lesson 1)** Carving the spoon teaches…
a) Nothing b) Wood's grain and patience c) Electricity d) Weaving
**Answer: b) Wood's grain and patience**
