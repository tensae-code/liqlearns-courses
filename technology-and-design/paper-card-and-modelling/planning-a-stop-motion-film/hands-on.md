# Hands-on — Planning a stop-motion film

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: 24 frames per second in cinema but 12 changes per second in animation (a 90-minute film needs 64,800 drawings), the equipment (camera, computer, tablet, sound, lighting) and free programs (qStopMotion, Stopgo, Toonloop, JellyCam, MonkeyJam, Purple Elephant). Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
