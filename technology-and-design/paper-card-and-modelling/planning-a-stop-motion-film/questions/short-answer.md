# Planning a stop-motion film — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Say in one sentence what this lesson teaches.
**Answer:** Planning a stop-motion film — 24 frames per second in cinema but 12 changes per second in animation (a 90-minute film needs 64,800 drawings), the equipment (camera, computer, tablet, sound, lighting) and free programs (qStopMotion, Stopgo, Toonloop, JellyCam, MonkeyJam, Purple Elephant).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name one material, tool or step this lesson works with.
**Answer:** Any one named in the lesson text — for example, the first step of sub lesson 1: Planning a stop-motion film.
