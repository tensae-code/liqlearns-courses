# Hands-on — Traditional embroidery crafts

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: needle-painting (зүү ороох) a wallet, appliqué (зээгт наамал), and coin-purse weaving (зоос ширээний сүлжмэл) — weaving on 8 numbered threads in the right order. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
