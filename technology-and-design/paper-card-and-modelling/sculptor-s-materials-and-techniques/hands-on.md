# Hands-on — Sculptor's materials and techniques

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: soft materials built up (clay, wax, gypsum), hard ones cut away (stone, wood); modelling, carving and casting; stacks, loops and the sculptor's stand (§7). Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
