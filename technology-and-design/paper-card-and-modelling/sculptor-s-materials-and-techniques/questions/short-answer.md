# Sculptor's materials and techniques — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Say in one sentence what this lesson teaches.
**Answer:** Sculptor's materials and techniques — soft materials built up (clay, wax, gypsum), hard ones cut away (stone, wood); modelling, carving and casting; stacks, loops and the sculptor's stand (§7).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name one material, tool or step this lesson works with.
**Answer:** Any one named in the lesson text — for example, the first step of sub lesson 1: Sculptor's materials and techniques.
