# Modelling animal sculptures in clay — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the modelling mixture?
a) Clay alone b) Clay, fine sand and water c) Sand and cement d) Paper and glue
**Answer: b) Clay, fine sand and water**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Why does the mixture matter?
a) It decides whether the sculpture stands or slumps b) It changes the colour only c) It does not matter d) It makes the clay heavier only
**Answer: a) It decides whether the sculpture stands or slumps**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Which two animals are modelled?
a) Cow and dog b) A goat and a bird c) Fish and frog d) Cat and hen only
**Answer: b) A goat and a bird**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** What does modelling mean here?
a) Drawing the animal b) Building the animal's form by hand until the clay reads as the animal c) Buying a model d) Tracing
**Answer: b) Building the animal's form by hand until the clay reads as the animal**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Two animals teach that…
a) Animals are the same b) The method is one, the forms are many c) Clay is alive d) Birds are easier always
**Answer: b) The method is one, the forms are many**
