# Modelling animal sculptures in clay — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The clay is used dry and unmixed.
**Answer: False** — The material is a prepared mixture of clay, fine sand and water.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The goat is modelled before the bird.
**Answer: True** — The book's order is the goat sculpture, then the bird.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Preparing the mixture is part of the make.
**Answer: True** — The proportions decide whether the sculpture stands — the mix is a skill, not a detail.
