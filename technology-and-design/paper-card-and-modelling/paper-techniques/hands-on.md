# Hands-on — Paper techniques

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: quilling and crumpling — quilling defined (thin paper strips coiled and glued to a base) and the crumpling (қабыстыру) technique for volume applique (Topic 12). Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
