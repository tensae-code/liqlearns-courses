# Working with paper and thin card

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Cuba's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Measuring and tracing
Grade 3's paper unit adds measurement: regla and cartabón (rule and set square) lay out work in centimetres, and the plantilla (template) repeats a shape exactly. Tearing, folding and cutting continue, now inside measured work.

## Sub lesson 2 — Joining with strips and tabs; covering
Pieces are joined by fileteado — edging strips — and by pestañas, tabs folded and glued into the join. The article is finished by forrado, covering it cleanly. Each joining method leaves a different edge, and the maker chooses by the article's use.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: thin card raises the standard: measure and trace in centimetres with rule and set square, use the template, and join pieces with fileteado strips and tabs (pestañas) — then cover (forrado) the finished article. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.

## Do It Yourself
1. Do the Do it first task again, carefully this time, using everything the lesson taught.
2. Check your work against the lesson's own checks — fix one thing you find.
3. Keep your work: it is practice evidence, and the next lesson builds on it.
