# Lesson plan — Working with paper and thin card

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Paper, Card and Modelling · **Lesson:** Working with paper and thin card
**Place in the curriculum:** Paper, Card and Modelling (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Cuba expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** thin card raises the standard: measure and trace in centimetres with rule and set square, use the template, and join pieces with fileteado strips and tabs (pestañas) — then cover (forrado) the finished article.
**This lesson's own level:** stocked from Cuba's Educación Laboral programmes and methodological orientations (teacher programme documents) — see lesson/sources.md. Cuba is evidence only for this lesson, counted nowhere in the acceptance count.

Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **Measuring and tracing** — Grade 3's paper unit adds measurement: regla and cartabón (rule and set square) lay out work in centimetres, and the plantilla (template) repeats a shape exactly. Tearing, folding and cutting continue, now inside measured work.
2. **Joining with strips and tabs; covering** — Pieces are joined by fileteado — edging strips — and by pestañas, tabs folded and glued into the join. The article is finished by forrado, covering it cleanly. Each joining method leaves a different edge, and the maker chooses by the article's use.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
