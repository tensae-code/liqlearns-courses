# Working with paper and thin card — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Tab (pestaña) is a small flap folded from one piece and glued to another to join them.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Edging strip (fileteado) has nothing to do with this lesson.
**Answer: False** — It is part of the lesson: a strip glued along an edge to join or strengthen pieces.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Covering (forrado) is applying a clean outer layer over a finished article.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.
