# Working with paper and thin card — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is tab (pestaña)?
a) a small flap folded from one piece and glued to another to join them b) Only a price c) Only decoration d) Nothing to do with this lesson
**Answer: a) a small flap folded from one piece and glued to another to join them**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is edging strip (fileteado)?
a) Only history b) a strip glued along an edge to join or strengthen pieces c) Only storage d) A kind of glue
**Answer: b) a strip glued along an edge to join or strengthen pieces**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is covering (forrado)?
a) Only old tools b) Only painting c) applying a clean outer layer over a finished article d) Only a price
**Answer: c) applying a clean outer layer over a finished article**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson teach, in one line?
a) Only decoration b) Nothing to do with this lesson c) Only history d) thin card raises the standard: measure and trace in centimetres with rule and set square, use the template, and join pieces with fileteado strips and tabs (pestañas) — then cover (forrado) the finished article.
**Answer: d) thin card raises the standard: measure and trace in centimetres with rule and set square, use the template, and join pieces with fileteado strips and tabs (pestañas) — then cover (forrado) the finished article.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** How does Cuba's book teach this?
a) As theory only, never practised b) Only in a glossary c) Only as an exam d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Spanish
**Answer: d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Spanish**
