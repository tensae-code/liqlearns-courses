# Working with paper and thin card — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In your own words, what is tab (pestaña)?
**Answer:** A small flap folded from one piece and glued to another to join them.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In your own words, what is covering (forrado)?
**Answer:** Applying a clean outer layer over a finished article.
