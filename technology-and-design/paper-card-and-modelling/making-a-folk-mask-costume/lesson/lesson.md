# Making a folk mask / costume

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Moldova's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Materials and tools — the traditional mask
Grade 6 folk-mask work starts with what masks are made from: traditionally, animal skins for the zoomorphic masks — the animal masks of the folk tradition. The materials and tools are learned as the tradition's own kit before any cutting starts.

## Sub lesson 2 — The making technology and the winter project
The mask (or costume) is then made by its technology — the ordered making steps — as a project for the winter holidays, when the masks are worn. Making and using meet: the project ends at the celebration.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: a folk mask is made by a technology: traditional materials — animal skins for the zoomorphic masks — the tools, the making steps, and a winter-holiday project that carries it. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.

## Do It Yourself
1. Do the Do it first task again, carefully this time, using everything the lesson taught.
2. Check your work against the lesson's own checks — fix one thing you find.
3. Keep your work: it is practice evidence, and the next lesson builds on it.
