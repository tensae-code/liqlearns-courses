# Making a folk mask / costume — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Zoomorphic masks were traditionally made from animal skins.
**Answer: True** — The lesson names animal skins as the traditional material.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The mask project belongs to the summer holidays.
**Answer: False** — It is a making project for the winter holidays.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Materials and tools are learned before the making technology.
**Answer: True** — The lesson orders kit first, then the technology of making.
