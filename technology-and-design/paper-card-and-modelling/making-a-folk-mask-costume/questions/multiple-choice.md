# Making a folk mask / costume — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What are zoomorphic masks?
a) Animal-shaped folk masks b) Masks of stars c) Paper hats d) Face paints
**Answer: a) Animal-shaped folk masks**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What material is traditional for them?
a) Plastic b) Animal skins c) Glass d) Metal sheet
**Answer: b) Animal skins**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the lesson teach beside materials?
a) The technology of making a mask (or costume) b) Mask prices c) Mask songs only d) Nothing else
**Answer: a) The technology of making a mask (or costume)**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The making is organised as what?
a) A project for the winter holidays b) A test c) A shop order d) A drawing only
**Answer: a) A project for the winter holidays**

**5. (Sub lesson 1)** When are the masks worn?
a) In summer b) Never — they are only drawn c) At the winter holidays d) At harvest
**Answer: c) At the winter holidays**
