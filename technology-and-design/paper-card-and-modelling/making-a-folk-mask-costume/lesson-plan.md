# Lesson plan — Making a folk mask / costume

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Paper, Card and Modelling · **Lesson:** Making a folk mask / costume
**Place in the curriculum:** Paper, Card and Modelling (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Moldova expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** a folk mask is made by a technology: traditional materials — animal skins for the zoomorphic masks — the tools, the making steps, and a winter-holiday project that carries it.
**This lesson's own level:** stocked from Moldova's *Educație tehnologică* (Technological Education) textbooks — see lesson/sources.md. Moldova counts in the acceptance count (student books, not evidence-only).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **Materials and tools — the traditional mask** — Grade 6 folk-mask work starts with what masks are made from: traditionally, animal skins for the zoomorphic masks — the animal masks of the folk tradition.
2. **The making technology and the winter project** — The mask (or costume) is then made by its technology — the ordered making steps — as a project for the winter holidays, when the masks are worn.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of Paper, Card and Modelling. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name the traditional materials and tools for folk masks.
2. Make a folk mask or costume as a winter-holiday project.
3. Explain how Moldova's book teaches it.
## What you need
Mask materials (card, fabric, or traditional substitutes), cutting and joining tools.
