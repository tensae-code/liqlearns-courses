# Hands-on — Writing a small-business project

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: the seven sections (summary, basis, the team, the product, market study, production plan, finance with income, costs, investment and loan repayment). Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
