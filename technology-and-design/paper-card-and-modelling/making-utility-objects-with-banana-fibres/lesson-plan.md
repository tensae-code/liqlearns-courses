# Lesson plan — Making utility objects with banana fibres

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Paper, Card and Modelling · **Lesson:** Making utility objects with banana fibres
**Place in the curriculum:** Paper, Card and Modelling (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Rwanda expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the banana plant gives fibre as well as fruit: Rwanda's Primary 4 learners make a mat and a dustbin from banana fibre — utility woven from the garden's own material.
**This lesson's own level:** stocked from Rwanda's *Science and Elementary Technology* pupil's books (Rwanda Education Board) — see lesson/sources.md. Rwanda counts in the acceptance count (student books, not evidence-only).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **Banana fibre as a material** — Banana fibre is the garden's making material: stripped from the banana plant, it is flexible, workable and near every home.
2. **Making a mat and a dustbin** — Two utility objects are made: a mat and a dustbin, both from banana fibre.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
