# Making utility objects with banana fibres

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Rwanda's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Banana fibre as a material
Banana fibre is the garden's making material: stripped from the banana plant, it is flexible, workable and near every home. Learning it is learning to see a crop twice — as food and as material.

## Sub lesson 2 — Making a mat and a dustbin
Two utility objects are made: a mat and a dustbin, both from banana fibre. A mat is worked flat and even; a dustbin must stand and hold — two different demands on the same fibre, in one lesson.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: the banana plant gives fibre as well as fruit: Rwanda's Primary 4 learners make a mat and a dustbin from banana fibre — utility woven from the garden's own material. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
