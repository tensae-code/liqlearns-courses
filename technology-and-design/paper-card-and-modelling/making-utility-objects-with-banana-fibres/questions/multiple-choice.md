# Making utility objects with banana fibres — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What material are the mat and dustbin made from?
a) Wire b) Banana fibre c) Clay d) Paper
**Answer: b) Banana fibre**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Where does banana fibre come from?
a) A factory b) The banana plant c) The river d) Imported shops only
**Answer: b) The banana plant**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What must the mat be?
a) Round and tall b) Flat and even c) Heavy d) Painted
**Answer: b) Flat and even**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** What must the dustbin do?
a) Lie flat b) Stand and hold c) Float d) Sing
**Answer: b) Stand and hold**

**5. (Sub lesson 1)** Seeing a crop as food and as material is…
a) Wasteful b) The lesson's way of seeing — the banana gives fibre as well as fruit c) Forbidden d) Useless
**Answer: b) The lesson's way of seeing — the banana gives fibre as well as fruit**
