# Making utility objects with banana fibres — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Banana fibre is bought as a factory material in this lesson.
**Answer: False** — It is stripped from the banana plant — the garden's own material.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The mat and the dustbin make the same demands on the fibre.
**Answer: False** — The mat must be flat and even; the dustbin must stand and hold — different demands, one fibre.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Two utility objects are made from banana fibre.
**Answer: True** — A mat and a dustbin.
