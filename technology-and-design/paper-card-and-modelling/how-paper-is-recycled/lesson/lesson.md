# How paper is recycled

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Mongolia's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — How paper is recycled
Mongolia's book teaches it this way (*Art & Technology 5 (Mongolia)*, printed p. 38): what paper products are for, how used paper is collected and reprocessed, and the paper production loop from forest to shop and back. The learner works through the named materials and steps in order, saying what each step does before doing the next one.

## Sub lesson 2 — How paper is recycled — doing it yourself
Now the learner does the whole task unaided: what paper products are for, how used paper is collected and reprocessed, and the paper production loop from forest to shop and back. Working from the first sub lesson's sequence, the learner plans the make, works through it step by step, and checks the finished piece against what Mongolia's book shows.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: How paper is recycled — what paper products are for, how used paper is collected and reprocessed, and the paper production loop from forest to shop and back. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
