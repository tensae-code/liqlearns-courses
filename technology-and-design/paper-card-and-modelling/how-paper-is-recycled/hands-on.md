# Hands-on — How paper is recycled

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: what paper products are for, how used paper is collected and reprocessed, and the paper production loop from forest to shop and back. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
