# How paper is recycled — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Say in one sentence what this lesson teaches.
**Answer:** How paper is recycled — what paper products are for, how used paper is collected and reprocessed, and the paper production loop from forest to shop and back.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name one material, tool or step this lesson works with.
**Answer:** Any one named in the lesson text — for example, the first step of sub lesson 1: How paper is recycled.
