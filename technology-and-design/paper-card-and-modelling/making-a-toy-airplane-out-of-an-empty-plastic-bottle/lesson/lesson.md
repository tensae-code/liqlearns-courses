# Making a toy airplane out of an empty plastic bottle

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Rwanda's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — The material: an empty plastic bottle
The make begins with rescue: an empty plastic bottle. Its shape is the lesson's gift — a ready-made body for the airplane, waiting in what others throw away.

## Sub lesson 2 — Building the airplane
The bottle is worked into an airplane — body from the bottle, the flying parts added — until the toy reads as a plane. The make teaches the second life of objects: with work, waste becomes a toy.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: an empty plastic bottle — rubbish to most eyes — becomes a toy airplane: Rwanda's making turns a thrown-away object into a toy. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.

3. Keep your work: it is practice evidence, and the next lesson builds on it.
