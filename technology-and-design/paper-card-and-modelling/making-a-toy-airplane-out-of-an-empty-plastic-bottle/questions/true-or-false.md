# Making a toy airplane out of an empty plastic bottle — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The airplane's body must be bought new.
**Answer: False** — The body is the empty plastic bottle itself — a rescued object.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Making the bottle plane teaches the second life of objects.
**Answer: True** — With work, waste becomes a toy — the lesson is taught by the make itself.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The bottle's shape is part of why it is chosen.
**Answer: True** — Its form is a ready-made airplane body.
