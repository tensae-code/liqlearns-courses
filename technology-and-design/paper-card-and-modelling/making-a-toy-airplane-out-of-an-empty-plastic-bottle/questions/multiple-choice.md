# Making a toy airplane out of an empty plastic bottle — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the airplane made from?
a) A new toy kit b) An empty plastic bottle c) Clay d) Sorghum
**Answer: b) An empty plastic bottle**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Why does the bottle suit the make?
a) It is expensive b) Its shape is a ready-made body for the plane c) It is heavy d) It is glass
**Answer: b) Its shape is a ready-made body for the plane**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the make teach about thrown-away objects?
a) They are useless b) They can have a second life as toys c) They must be burned d) They are dangerous always
**Answer: b) They can have a second life as toys**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** What is added to the bottle?
a) Nothing b) The flying parts that make it read as a plane c) Water d) Paint only
**Answer: b) The flying parts that make it read as a plane**

**5. (Sub lesson 1)** The make begins with…
a) Shopping b) Rescuing an empty bottle c) Drawing d) Clay
**Answer: b) Rescuing an empty bottle**
