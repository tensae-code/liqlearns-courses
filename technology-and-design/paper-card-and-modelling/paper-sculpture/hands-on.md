# Hands-on — Paper sculpture

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: a crane built over a crumpled-paper and foil armature, following a sequence; the kinds of sculpture (standing, relief, small). Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
