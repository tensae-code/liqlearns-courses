# Hands-on — Instrument construction

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: the dombra's parts named on a labelled diagram (neck, pegs, strings, soundboard) and a model dombra built step by step (Topic 14; diagram and steps viewed by me). Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
