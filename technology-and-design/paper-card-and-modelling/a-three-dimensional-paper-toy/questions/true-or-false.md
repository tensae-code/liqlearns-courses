# A three-dimensional paper toy — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A three-dimensional toy lies flat like a picture.
**Answer: False** — It stands with a front, sides and a back.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** If the hare falls over, the construction needs fixing.
**Answer: True** — Standing on its own is the test of the build.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Glue alone, with no folding, makes paper solid.
**Answer: False** — The form comes from folding and joining along the edges.
