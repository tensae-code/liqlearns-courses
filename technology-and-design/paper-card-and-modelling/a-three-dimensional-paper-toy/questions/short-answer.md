# A three-dimensional paper toy — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why are the joins so important in a volumetric toy?
**Answer:** The folds and joins along the edges are what make flat paper hold its solid form.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In what order is the hare made?
**Answer:** The body's solid form first, then the head, ears and details are added and fixed.
