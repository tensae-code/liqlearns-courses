# A three-dimensional paper toy — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What makes a paper toy three-dimensional?
a) It stands with a front, sides and a back instead of lying flat b) It is painted in three colours c) It is very large d) It has three pages
**Answer: a) It stands with a front, sides and a back instead of lying flat**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** How does flat paper take a solid form?
a) It is folded and joined along its edges b) It is wetted c) It is sewn to cloth d) It is left flat
**Answer: a) It is folded and joined along its edges**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is built first in the hare?
a) The body's solid form b) The whiskers c) The colour d) The tail only
**Answer: a) The body's solid form**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** What is added after the body?
a) Head, ears and details b) Wheels c) A second sheet left flat d) Nothing
**Answer: a) Head, ears and details**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** What proves the construction is right?
a) The hare stands on its own b) It weighs the most c) It uses the most glue d) It lies flat
**Answer: a) The hare stands on its own**
