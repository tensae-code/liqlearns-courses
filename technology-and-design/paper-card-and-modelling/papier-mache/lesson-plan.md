# Lesson plan — Papier-mâché

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Paper, Card and Modelling · **Lesson:** Papier-mâché
**Place in the curriculum:** Paper, Card and Modelling (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Cuba expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** papier-mâché (papel maché) turns paper and paste into solid articles: build the form, layer the maché, dry it, finish it — and beside it, origami makes its articles by folding alone.
**This lesson's own level:** stocked from Cuba's *Educación Laboral — 9no grado* and the Cuba books in lesson/sources.md — see lesson/sources.md. Cuba counts in the acceptance count for its student-book placements; its programme and methodological-orientations placements are evidence only, counted nowhere.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **Making and using papier-mâché** — Cuba's Grade 9 book teaches papel maché inside its paper, card and cardstock chapter: the paste-and-paper material is made, applied over a form in layers, dried into a solid skin, and finished as an article.
2. **Origami, the folding companion** — The same chapter teaches origami: articles made from a flat sheet by folds alone — no cutting, no glue. The two techniques are opposites that complete each other: one adds material layer by layer, the other only persuades the sheet to become the thing.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
