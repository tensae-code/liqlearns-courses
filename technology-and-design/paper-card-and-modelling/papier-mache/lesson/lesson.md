# Papier-mâché

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Cuba's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Making and using papier-mâché
Cuba's Grade 9 book teaches papel maché inside its paper, card and cardstock chapter: the paste-and-paper material is made, applied over a form in layers, dried into a solid skin, and finished as an article.

## Sub lesson 2 — Origami, the folding companion
The same chapter teaches origami: articles made from a flat sheet by folds alone — no cutting, no glue. The two techniques are opposites that complete each other: one adds material layer by layer, the other only persuades the sheet to become the thing.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: papier-mâché (papel maché) turns paper and paste into solid articles: build the form, layer the maché, dry it, finish it — and beside it, origami makes its articles by folding alone. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
