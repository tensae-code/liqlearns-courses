# Hands-on — Papier-mâché

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## Plan a maché article
Choose an article for papier-mâché. List: the form you will build over, the layers you will apply, the drying time, and the finish.

## Fold first
Fold one origami article from a single sheet. Afterwards say: what did folding alone achieve that maché would do differently?
