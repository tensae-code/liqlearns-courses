# Papier-mâché — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is papier-mâché (papel maché)?
a) paper worked with paste into a solid material for articles b) Only a price c) Only decoration d) Nothing to do with this lesson
**Answer: a) paper worked with paste into a solid material for articles**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is form (molde)?
a) Only history b) the shape over which papier-mâché is layered c) Only storage d) A kind of glue
**Answer: b) the shape over which papier-mâché is layered**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is origami?
a) Only old tools b) Only painting c) making articles by folding paper, without cutting or glue d) Only a price
**Answer: c) making articles by folding paper, without cutting or glue**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson teach, in one line?
a) Only decoration b) Nothing to do with this lesson c) Only history d) papier-mâché (papel maché) turns paper and paste into solid articles: build the form, layer the maché, dry it, finish it — and beside it, origami makes its articles by folding alone.
**Answer: d) papier-mâché (papel maché) turns paper and paste into solid articles: build the form, layer the maché, dry it, finish it — and beside it, origami makes its articles by folding alone.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** How does Cuba's book teach this?
a) As theory only, never practised b) Only in a glossary c) Only as an exam d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Spanish
**Answer: d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Spanish**
