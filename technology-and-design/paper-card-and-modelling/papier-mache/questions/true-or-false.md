# Papier-mâché — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Papier-mâché (papel maché) is paper worked with paste into a solid material for articles.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Form (molde) has nothing to do with this lesson.
**Answer: False** — It is part of the lesson: the shape over which papier-mâché is layered.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Origami is making articles by folding paper, without cutting or glue.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.
