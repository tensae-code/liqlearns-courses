# Papier-mâché — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In your own words, what is papier-mâché (papel maché)?
**Answer:** Paper worked with paste into a solid material for articles.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In your own words, what is origami?
**Answer:** Making articles by folding paper, without cutting or glue.
