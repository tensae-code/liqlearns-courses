# Making toys in clay and wire — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name two clay toys and one wire toy from Primary 4.
**Answer:** Clay: the doll and the cat. Wire: the toy bicycle (a wire doll is also made).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why make the same toy in both materials?
**Answer:** So the hands learn what each material does better — clay takes any solid form; wire bends, springs and holds a line.
