# Making toys in clay and wire — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The toy bicycle is modelled in clay.
**Answer: False** — The bicycle is bent from wire; the doll and cat are the clay makes.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Wire holds a line in the air in a way clay cannot.
**Answer: True** — Wire bends and springs — its toys are frames and outlines, clay's are solid forms.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Primary 6 returns to both clay and wire toys.
**Answer: True** — A doll and animals in clay, and a motorcycle from wires, are both Primary 6 makes.
