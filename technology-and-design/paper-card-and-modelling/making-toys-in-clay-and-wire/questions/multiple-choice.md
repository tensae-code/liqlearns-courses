# Making toys in clay and wire — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which toy is bent from wire in Primary 4?
a) A doll b) A toy bicycle c) A cat d) A car
**Answer: b) A toy bicycle**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What does clay do well?
a) Springs back b) Takes any form the hand gives it c) Conducts current d) Floats
**Answer: b) Takes any form the hand gives it**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What does wire do that clay cannot?
a) Nothing b) Bends, springs and holds a line in the air c) Dissolves d) Burns
**Answer: b) Bends, springs and holds a line in the air**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which toy is made in both materials across the books?
a) A house b) A motorcycle — from clay in Primary 4's list, from wires in Primary 6 c) A boat d) A clock
**Answer: b) A motorcycle — from clay in Primary 4's list, from wires in Primary 6**

**5. (Sub lesson 1)** What animals and figures are modelled in clay?
a) A doll and a cat (Primary 4); a doll and animals (Primary 6) b) Only fish c) None d) Only birds
**Answer: a) A doll and a cat (Primary 4); a doll and animals (Primary 6)**
