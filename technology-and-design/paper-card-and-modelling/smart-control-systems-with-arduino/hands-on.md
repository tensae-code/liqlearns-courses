# Hands-on — Smart control systems with Arduino

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: wiring an LED and resistor (220 Ω) to pin 11, writing blink programs with new timings, a traffic-light build, and driving DC motors through the L293 driver (ports give only 5–20 mA, a motor needs 500 mA). Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
