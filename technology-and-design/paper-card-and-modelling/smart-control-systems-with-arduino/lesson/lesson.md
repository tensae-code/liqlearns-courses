# Smart control systems with Arduino

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Mongolia's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Smart control systems with Arduino
Mongolia's book teaches it this way (*Design Drawing & Technology 12 (Mongolia)*, printed p. 117): wiring an LED and resistor (220 Ω) to pin 11, writing blink programs with new timings, a traffic-light build, and driving DC motors through the L293 driver (ports give only 5–20 mA, a motor needs 500 mA). The learner works through the named materials and steps in order, saying what each step does before doing the next one.

## Sub lesson 2 — Smart control systems with Arduino — doing it yourself
Now the learner does the whole task unaided: wiring an LED and resistor (220 Ω) to pin 11, writing blink programs with new timings, a traffic-light build, and driving DC motors through the L293 driver (ports give only 5–20 mA, a motor needs 500 mA). Working from the first sub lesson's sequence, the learner plans the make, works through it step by step, and checks the finished piece against what Mongolia's book shows.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: Smart control systems with Arduino — wiring an LED and resistor (220 Ω) to pin 11, writing blink programs with new timings, a traffic-light build, and driving DC motors through the L293 driver (ports give only 5–20 mA, a motor needs 500 mA). Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
