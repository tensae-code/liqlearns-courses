# Smart control systems with Arduino — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Say in one sentence what this lesson teaches.
**Answer:** Smart control systems with Arduino — wiring an LED and resistor (220 Ω) to pin 11, writing blink programs with new timings, a traffic-light build, and driving DC motors through the L293 driver (ports give only 5–20 mA, a motor needs 500 mA).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name one material, tool or step this lesson works with.
**Answer:** Any one named in the lesson text — for example, the first step of sub lesson 1: Smart control systems with Arduino.
