# Hands-on — Schematic diagrams

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: the kinds of scheme (electrical Э, kinematic К, pneumatic П, hydraulic Г, optical Л) and their codes (Э4 connection scheme, К3 kinematic scheme), and how to read a kinematic scheme. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
