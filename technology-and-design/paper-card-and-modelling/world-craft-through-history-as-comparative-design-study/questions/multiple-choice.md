# World craft through history as comparative design study — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson teach?
a) World craft through history as comparative design study b) Cooking rice c) Map reading d) Letter writing
**Answer: a) World craft through history as comparative design study**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In the Indonesian book, where is this topic worked?
a) In a sports unit b) At Unit 1 Analisis Ragam Kerajinan Mancanegara c) Only in an exam d) It is not in the book
**Answer: b) At Unit 1 Analisis Ragam Kerajinan Mancanegara**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What comes first in the Prakarya sequence?
a) Selling the product b) Throwing materials away c) Observing, exploring and planning before making d) Marking the work
**Answer: c) Observing, exploring and planning before making**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Which detail belongs to this topic?
a) The detail named in the book: Roman amphora, Ming ceramics, stamped leather, weaving traditions, Bauhaus, da Vinci's design sketches b) Parts of a bicycle pump only c) Parts of a television only d) No details are named
**Answer: a) The detail named in the book: Roman amphora, Ming ceramics, stamped leather, weaving traditions, Bauhaus, da Vinci's design sketches**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** What does "local potential" (potensi lokal) mean in Prakarya?
a) Importing every material b) Building from the materials and traditions of the learner's own region c) Working only from photographs d) Skipping the planning stage
