# Hands-on — Materials for making

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: meeting the course materials by name (paper kinds, natural materials, plasticine) and the folding, cutting and joining ways of working with them (Lessons 1–4, teacher-guide evidence). Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
