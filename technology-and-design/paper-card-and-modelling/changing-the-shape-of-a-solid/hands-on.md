# Hands-on — Changing the shape of a solid

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: how cubes, cuboids, cylinders and cones are modified (cut, stepped, sloped) — and where the changed shapes appear in vehicle models. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
