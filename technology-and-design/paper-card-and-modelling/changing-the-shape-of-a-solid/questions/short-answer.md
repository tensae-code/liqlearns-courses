# Changing the shape of a solid — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Say in one sentence what this lesson teaches.
**Answer:** Changing the shape of a solid — how cubes, cuboids, cylinders and cones are modified (cut, stepped, sloped) — and where the changed shapes appear in vehicle models.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name one material, tool or step this lesson works with.
**Answer:** Any one named in the lesson text — for example, the first step of sub lesson 1: Changing the shape of a solid.
