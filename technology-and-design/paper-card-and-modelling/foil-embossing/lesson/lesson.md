# Foil embossing

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Kazakhstan's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Foil embossing
Kazakhstan's books teach it this way (the student book, printed p. 56): Making a national-style jewellery piece from thick foil: transfer the ornament, emboss with a blunt tool, add fine detail. The learner works through the named materials and steps in order, saying what each step does before doing the next one.

## Sub lesson 2 — Foil embossing
The Kazakh treatment continues and completes the topic: Foil is sheet metal up to 0.3 mm, soft and plastic: a relief image pressed into it with simple tools (§9). The learner finishes the make, checks it against the book's own sequence, and fixes one thing before presenting the work.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: Foil embossing — making a national-style jewellery piece from thick foil: transfer the ornament, emboss with a blunt tool, add fine detail. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.

## Do It Yourself
1. Do the Do it first task again, carefully this time, using everything the lesson taught.
2. Check your work against the lesson's own checks — fix one thing you find.
3. Keep your work: it is practice evidence, and the next lesson builds on it.
