# The component parts of things — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson teach?
a) The component parts of things — naming the parts of a pen, a bag, a shirt and a bowl, and how changing the number of parts changes the design b) Only how to draw pictures c) Nothing practical d) A song to memorise
**Answer: a) The component parts of things — naming the parts of a pen, a bag, a shirt and a bowl, and how changing the number of parts changes the design**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Sub lesson 1 is about…
a) The component parts of things b) Cooking lunch c) A sports match d) The weather
**Answer: a) The component parts of things**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Sub lesson 2 asks the learner to…
a) Complete the making in full and check it b) Stop before starting c) Copy a partner's work d) Throw the materials away
**Answer: a) Complete the making in full and check it**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Mongolia teaches this topic inside which school course?
a) Visual Art & Technology (Дүрслэх урлаг, технологи) b) Only mathematics c) Only history d) It is not taught at school

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** What should the learner do first in this lesson?
