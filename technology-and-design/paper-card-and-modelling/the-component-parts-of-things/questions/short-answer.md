# The component parts of things — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Say in one sentence what this lesson teaches.
**Answer:** The component parts of things — naming the parts of a pen, a bag, a shirt and a bowl, and how changing the number of parts changes the design.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name one material, tool or step this lesson works with.
**Answer:** Any one named in the lesson text — for example, the first step of sub lesson 1: The component parts of things.
