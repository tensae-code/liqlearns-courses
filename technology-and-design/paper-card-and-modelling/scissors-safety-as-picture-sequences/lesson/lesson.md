# Scissors safety as picture sequences

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Kazakhstan's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Scissors safety as picture sequences
Kazakhstan's books teach it this way (the student book, printed pp. 8–16): Holding scissors, passing them closed, cutting on a line; the course materials (coloured paper, glue, plasticine, leaves) (Lessons 1–4, pictures only, EVIDENCE-LIMITED). This part is placed from pictures only, because Book 932.pdf is PARTIAL and its early text is lost — it is filed EVIDENCE-LIMITED in sources. The learner works through the named materials and steps in order, saying what each step does before doing the next one.

## Sub lesson 2 — Scissors safety as picture sequences — doing it yourself
Now the learner does the whole task unaided: holding scissors, passing them closed, cutting on a line; the course materials (coloured paper, glue, plasticine, leaves) (Lessons 1–4, pictures only, EVIDENCE-LIMITED). Working from the first sub lesson's sequence, the learner plans the make, works through it step by step, and checks the finished piece against what the Kazakh book shows.

## Say it to your companion

Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
