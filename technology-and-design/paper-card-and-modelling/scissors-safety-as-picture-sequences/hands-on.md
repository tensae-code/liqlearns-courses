# Hands-on — Scissors safety as picture sequences

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: holding scissors, passing them closed, cutting on a line; the course materials (coloured paper, glue, plasticine, leaves) (Lessons 1–4, pictures only, EVIDENCE-LIMITED). Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
