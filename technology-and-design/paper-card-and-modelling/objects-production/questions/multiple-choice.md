# Objects production — making useful things — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which books sequence Objects Production as a unit?
a) The pupil's books only b) The Primary 5 and Primary 6 teacher guides c) No book d) The Primary 2 book
**Answer: b) The Primary 5 and Primary 6 teacher guides**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** How does this lesson count?
a) As pupil placements b) As teacher-guide evidence only, counted nowhere c) As two countries d) As a full read
**Answer: b) As teacher-guide evidence only, counted nowhere**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What does objects production produce?
a) Only drawings b) Toys, utility objects and learning materials c) Only exams d) Only stories
**Answer: b) Toys, utility objects and learning materials**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Objects production is best described as…
a) One afternoon's activity b) The umbrella unit over all the making lessons c) A shop d) A material
**Answer: b) The umbrella unit over all the making lessons**

**5. (Sub lesson 1)** The making work in the guides is…
a) Scattered activities b) A planned unit, sequenced lesson by lesson c) Unplanned d) Homework only
**Answer: b) A planned unit, sequenced lesson by lesson**
