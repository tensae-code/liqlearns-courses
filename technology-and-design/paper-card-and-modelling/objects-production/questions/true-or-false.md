# Objects production — making useful things — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Objects production is stocked from pupil's books.
**Answer: False** — It stands on the two teacher guides — evidence only, counted nowhere.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Learning materials in paper belong under objects production.
**Answer: True** — Toys, utilities and learning materials are the unit's three product families.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Both the Primary 5 and Primary 6 guides sequence the unit.
**Answer: True** — Guide p. 30 (Primary 5) and guide p. 75 (Primary 6).
