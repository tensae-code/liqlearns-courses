# Objects production — making useful things

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Rwanda's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — The making unit as the guides sequence it
Both the Primary 5 and Primary 6 teacher guides give Objects Production its own lesson sequence (guide p. 30 and guide p. 75): the making work is not scattered activities but a unit, planned and taught as one. This lesson stands on that guide evidence.

## Sub lesson 2 — What objects production produces
Across the years the unit produces the whole making catalogue: toys in clay, wire, paper and sorghum; utility objects in sticks, banana fibre, thread and wood; learning materials in paper. Objects production is the umbrella — every making lesson in this course is one of its rooms.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: objects production is Rwanda's name for the making unit itself — toys, utilities and learning materials produced by the learners — and the two teacher guides sequence it as a unit in its own right. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.

3. Keep your work: it is practice evidence, and the next lesson builds on it.
