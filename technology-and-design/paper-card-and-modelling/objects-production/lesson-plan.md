# Lesson plan — Objects production — making useful things

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Paper, Card and Modelling · **Lesson:** Objects production — making useful things
**Place in the curriculum:** Paper, Card and Modelling (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Rwanda expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** objects production is Rwanda's name for the making unit itself — toys, utilities and learning materials produced by the learners — and the two teacher guides sequence it as a unit in its own right.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on teacher-guide evidence only — Rwanda's *Science and Elementary Technology* teacher guides are the books in hand for this topic (see lesson/sources.md). Teacher guides are evidence, counted nowhere in the acceptance count.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **The making unit as the guides sequence it** — Both the Primary 5 and Primary 6 teacher guides give Objects Production its own lesson sequence (guide p.
2. **What objects production produces** — Across the years the unit produces the whole making catalogue: toys in clay, wire, paper and sorghum; utility objects in sticks, banana fibre, thread and wood; learning materials in paper.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
