# Hands-on — Paper and its working ways

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: kinds of paper, cutting and folding ways, making a paper boat (Lessons 2–5, teacher-guide evidence). Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
