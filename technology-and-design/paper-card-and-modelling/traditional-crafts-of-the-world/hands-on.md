# Hands-on — Traditional crafts of the world

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: comparing national crafts, and metal grille-work (торон хийц) — forging a grille from black metal, inlay (шигтгээ) of one metal into another, and the blacksmith's hot-working sequence in photos. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
