# The fold-and-cut method

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Mongolia's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — The fold-and-cut method
Mongolia's book teaches it this way (*Art & Technology 1 (Mongolia)*, printed p. 59): folding paper and cutting on the fold to make symmetrical shapes (pear, flower, leaf, fish, apple). The learner works through the named materials and steps in order, saying what each step does before doing the next one. Mongolia's *Art & Technology 3 (Mongolia)* adds (printed p. 47): accordion-folding and cutting to make chains of trees and people

## Sub lesson 2 — The fold-and-cut method — doing it yourself
Now the learner does the whole task unaided: folding paper and cutting on the fold to make symmetrical shapes (pear, flower, leaf, fish, apple). Working from the first sub lesson's sequence, the learner plans the make, works through it step by step, and checks the finished piece against what Mongolia's book shows.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: The fold-and-cut method — folding paper and cutting on the fold to make symmetrical shapes (pear, flower, leaf, fish, apple). Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
