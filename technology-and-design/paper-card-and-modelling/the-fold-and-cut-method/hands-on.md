# Hands-on — The fold-and-cut method

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: folding paper and cutting on the fold to make symmetrical shapes (pear, flower, leaf, fish, apple). Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
