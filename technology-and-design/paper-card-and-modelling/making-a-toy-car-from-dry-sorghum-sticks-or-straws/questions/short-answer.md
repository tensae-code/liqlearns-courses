# Making a toy car from dry sorghum sticks or straws — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the car's material, exactly as the book gives it.
**Answer:** Dry sorghum sticks or straws.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What rule does the make teach?
**Answer:** The material suggests the object — choose material that already suits what you will make.
