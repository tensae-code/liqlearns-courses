# Making a toy car from dry sorghum sticks or straws — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The toy car is made from an empty bottle.
**Answer: False** — The bottle makes the toy airplane; the car is made from dry sorghum sticks or straws.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Material choice is part of the making lesson.
**Answer: True** — A dry, straight, light stalk is chosen because it already suits the object.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Sorghum is a local crop material in Rwanda's book.
**Answer: True** — The farm's own stalks become the toy — local materials carry the making.
