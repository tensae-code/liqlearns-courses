# Making a toy car from dry sorghum sticks or straws — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the toy car made from?
a) Plastic b) Dry sorghum sticks or straws c) Metal d) Clay
**Answer: b) Dry sorghum sticks or straws**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Why dry sticks?
a) They are prettier b) Dry, straight and light — already half a toy car c) They are expensive d) No reason
**Answer: b) Dry, straight and light — already half a toy car**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the maker join the sticks into?
a) A house b) The car's form — body and parts c) A mat d) A basket
**Answer: b) The car's form — body and parts**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** What is the maker's rule this lesson teaches?
a) Buy the best b) The material suggests the object c) Always use plastic d) Steps do not matter
**Answer: b) The material suggests the object**

**5. (Sub lesson 1)** Where does the material come from?
a) A shop abroad b) The farm — sorghum, the local crop c) The sea d) A factory
**Answer: b) The farm — sorghum, the local crop**
