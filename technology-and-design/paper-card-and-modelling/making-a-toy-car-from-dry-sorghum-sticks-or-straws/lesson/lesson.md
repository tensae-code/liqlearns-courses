# Making a toy car from dry sorghum sticks or straws

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Rwanda's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — The material: dry sorghum sticks or straws
Rwanda's making uses what the farm gives: dry sorghum sticks or straws. The lesson's first teaching is material choice — a dry, straight, light stalk is already half a toy car before any work begins.

## Sub lesson 2 — Building the car
The sticks are joined into the car's form — body, and the parts that let it stand and roll as a car. A toy made from stalks teaches the great maker's rule: the material suggests the object.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: a toy car from the farm's own leftovers: dry sorghum sticks or straws become the body and parts of a car the learner builds. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.

3. Keep your work: it is practice evidence, and the next lesson builds on it.
