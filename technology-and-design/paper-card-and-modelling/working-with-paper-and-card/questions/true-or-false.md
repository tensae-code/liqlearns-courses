# Working with paper and card — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Folding (plegado) is creasing paper or card along a line to shape it.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Tearing (rasgado) has nothing to do with this lesson.
**Answer: False** — It is part of the lesson: shaping paper by controlled tearing along a line.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Gluing (pegado) is joining paper and card with adhesive, applied cleanly.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.
