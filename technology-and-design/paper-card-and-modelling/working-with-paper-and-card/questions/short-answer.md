# Working with paper and card — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In your own words, what is folding (plegado)?
**Answer:** Creasing paper or card along a line to shape it.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In your own words, what is gluing (pegado)?
**Answer:** Joining paper and card with adhesive, applied cleanly.
