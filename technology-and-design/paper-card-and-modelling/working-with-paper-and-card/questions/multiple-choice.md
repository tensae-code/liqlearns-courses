# Working with paper and card — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is folding (plegado)?
a) creasing paper or card along a line to shape it b) Only a price c) Only decoration d) Nothing to do with this lesson
**Answer: a) creasing paper or card along a line to shape it**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is tearing (rasgado)?
a) Only history b) shaping paper by controlled tearing along a line c) Only storage d) A kind of glue
**Answer: b) shaping paper by controlled tearing along a line**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is gluing (pegado)?
a) Only old tools b) Only painting c) joining paper and card with adhesive, applied cleanly d) Only a price
**Answer: c) joining paper and card with adhesive, applied cleanly**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson teach, in one line?
a) Only decoration b) Nothing to do with this lesson c) Only history d) paper and card are worked by three founding techniques — tearing (rasgado), folding (plegado) and cutting (recortado) — combined to make articles, and joined with glue: plan the order of operations, then make.
**Answer: d) paper and card are worked by three founding techniques — tearing (rasgado), folding (plegado) and cutting (recortado) — combined to make articles, and joined with glue: plan the order of operations, then make.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** How does Cuba's book teach this?
a) As theory only, never practised b) Only in a glossary c) Only as an exam d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Spanish
**Answer: d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Spanish**
