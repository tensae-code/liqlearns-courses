# Lesson plan — Working with paper and card

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Paper, Card and Modelling · **Lesson:** Working with paper and card
**Place in the curriculum:** Paper, Card and Modelling (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Cuba expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** paper and card are worked by three founding techniques — tearing (rasgado), folding (plegado) and cutting (recortado) — combined to make articles, and joined with glue: plan the order of operations, then make.
**This lesson's own level:** stocked from Cuba's Educación Laboral programmes and methodological orientations (teacher programme documents) — see lesson/sources.md. Cuba is evidence only for this lesson, counted nowhere in the acceptance count.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **Tearing, folding, cutting** — The Grade 2 programme founds paper work on rasgado, plegado and recortado with round-point scissors: folds divide into basic, secondary and derived, and the three techniques combine in one article. The methodological orientations add card (cartulina) as the new material and the second and third closed envelopes as set makes.
2. **Planning and gluing** — Two habits govern the work: plan the order of operations before starting, and join with glue (pegamento) applied with care for the work station's cleanliness. Gluing is taught as an operation with its own technique, not a dab and hope.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
