# Working with paper and card

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Cuba's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Tearing, folding, cutting
The Grade 2 programme founds paper work on rasgado, plegado and recortado with round-point scissors: folds divide into basic, secondary and derived, and the three techniques combine in one article. The methodological orientations add card (cartulina) as the new material and the second and third closed envelopes as set makes.

## Sub lesson 2 — Planning and gluing
Two habits govern the work: plan the order of operations before starting, and join with glue (pegamento) applied with care for the work station's cleanliness. Gluing is taught as an operation with its own technique, not a dab and hope.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: paper and card are worked by three founding techniques — tearing (rasgado), folding (plegado) and cutting (recortado) — combined to make articles, and joined with glue: plan the order of operations, then make. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.

## Do It Yourself
1. Do the Do it first task again, carefully this time, using everything the lesson taught.
2. Check your work against the lesson's own checks — fix one thing you find.
3. Keep your work: it is practice evidence, and the next lesson builds on it.
