# The history of the bicycle — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson teach?

**Answer: a) The history of the bicycle — from the 1815 wooden draisine through the penny-farthing to the modern bicycle, 1815–2015**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Sub lesson 1 is about…
a) The history of the bicycle b) Cooking lunch c) A sports match d) The weather
**Answer: a) The history of the bicycle**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Sub lesson 2 asks the learner to…
