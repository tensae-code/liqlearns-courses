# Hands-on — The history of the bicycle

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: from the 1815 wooden draisine through the penny-farthing to the modern bicycle, 1815–2015. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
