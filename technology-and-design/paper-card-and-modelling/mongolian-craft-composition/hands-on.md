# Hands-on — Mongolian craft composition

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: the parts and proportions of the saddle (small, medium and large эмээл sizes drawn to scale), and the composition rules of national craft. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
