# Mongolian craft composition — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson teach?
a) Mongolian craft composition — the parts and proportions of the saddle (small, medium and large эмээл sizes drawn to scale), and the composition rules of national craft b) Only how to draw pictures c) Nothing practical d) A song to memorise

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Sub lesson 1 is about…
a) Mongolian craft composition b) Cooking lunch c) A sports match d) The weather
