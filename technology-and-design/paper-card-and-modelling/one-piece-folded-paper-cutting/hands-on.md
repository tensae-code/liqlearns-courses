# Hands-on — One-piece folded-paper cutting

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: folded-paper cutting (a hanging ornament, a horse) and an 18-step folded swallow (Lessons 10–11). Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
