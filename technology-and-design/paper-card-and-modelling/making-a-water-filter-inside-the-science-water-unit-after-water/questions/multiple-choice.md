# Making a water filter — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Where does the water filter make stand?
a) In the electricity unit b) Inside the science Water unit, after purification methods c) In Primary 2 d) In the waste unit
**Answer: b) Inside the science Water unit, after purification methods**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What comes before the make?
a) Nothing b) The teaching of water purification methods c) The exam d) Drawing
**Answer: b) The teaching of water purification methods**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The filter built is…
a) A drawing b) A working object, not only a model c) A song d) A list
**Answer: b) A working object, not only a model**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** How is its success seen?
a) By its colour b) Water that enters dirty leaves clearer c) By its price d) By applause
**Answer: b) Water that enters dirty leaves clearer**

**5. (Sub lesson 1)** The order — theory, then make — teaches that…
a) Theory is useless b) The make is the science applied c) Science has no hands d) Making replaces knowing
**Answer: b) The make is the science applied**
