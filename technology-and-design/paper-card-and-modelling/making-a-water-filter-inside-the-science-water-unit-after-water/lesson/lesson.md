# Making a water filter

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Rwanda's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Purification first, then the make
The filter stands inside the Water unit, after water purification methods are taught. The order is the pedagogy: know how water is purified, then build the device that does it — the make is the science applied.

## Sub lesson 2 — Building the water filter
The learners make a water filter — a working object, not a model of one. Its success is visible: water that enters dirty leaves clearer. Technology here is science with hands: the unit's knowledge leaves the book and stands on the table.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: inside the science unit on water, Rwanda stops the theory and makes: after the purification methods, the Primary 5 learners build a water filter with their own hands. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
