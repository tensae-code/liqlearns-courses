# Hands-on — The toy's working drawing

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: a dimensioned drawing with Ø and R sizes, and the four kinds of tangent blend (arc to line, arc to arc) used in the profile. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
