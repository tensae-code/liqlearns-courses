# Hands-on — Technology professions

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: the book's professions glossary defines the technology trades: designer, engineer-mechanic, joiner, technician-technologist, technician-electrician, turner, tractorist; agricultural machines and electromontage tools defined alongside. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
