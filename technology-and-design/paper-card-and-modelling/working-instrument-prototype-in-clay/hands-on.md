# Hands-on — Working instrument prototype in clay

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: a sazsyrnai clay flute modelled around a finger with pierced sound holes, a working prototype (Lesson 12). Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
