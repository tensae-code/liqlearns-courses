# Clay modelling — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What three things open the clay module?
**Answer:** The modelling materials, the tools, and the personal-hygiene and work-protection norms.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the two hand-modelling techniques and their makes.
**Answer:** Free modelling (the whistle toy) and modelling on a mould (the decorative plate).
