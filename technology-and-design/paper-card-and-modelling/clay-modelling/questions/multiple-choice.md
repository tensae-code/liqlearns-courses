# Clay modelling — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What opens the clay module?
a) Materials and tools, with hygiene and work-protection norms b) The kiln c) A competition d) Painting only
**Answer: a) Materials and tools, with hygiene and work-protection norms**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Why do the norms matter especially for clay?
a) Clay is expensive b) Clay is a hand material — hands, tools and workplace must stay clean and safe c) Clay is loud d) Clay is electric
**Answer: b) Clay is a hand material — hands, tools and workplace must stay clean and safe**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is modelled freely by hand?
a) A clay whistle toy b) A plate c) A brick d) A wheel pot
**Answer: a) A clay whistle toy**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** What is a calapod?
a) A mould the plate is modelled on b) A colour c) A tool for cutting d) A kiln shelf
**Answer: a) A mould the plate is modelled on**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** What finishes the plate?
a) Varnish b) A price tag c) Traditional ceramic decor and chromatics d) Nothing
**Answer: c) Traditional ceramic decor and chromatics**
