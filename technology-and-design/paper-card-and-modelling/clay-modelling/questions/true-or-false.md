# Clay modelling — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Hygiene and protection norms are part of clay modelling from the start.
**Answer: True** — The module opens with materials, tools and the norms.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The whistle toy is modelled on a mould.
**Answer: False** — The whistle is modelled freely; the plate is the mould (calapod) work.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Traditional decor and chromatics belong to the plate lesson.
**Answer: True** — The decorative plate closes with traditional ceramic decor and chromatics.
