# Lesson plan — Clay modelling

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Paper, Card and Modelling · **Lesson:** Clay modelling
**Place in the curriculum:** Paper, Card and Modelling (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Moldova expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** clay modelling works by hand under its own norms: know the materials and tools, keep the hygiene and protection rules — then model freely, or on a mould (calapod), with traditional decor and chromatics.
**This lesson's own level:** stocked from Moldova's *Educație tehnologică* (Technological Education) textbooks — see lesson/sources.md. Moldova counts in the acceptance count (student books, not evidence-only).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **Materials, tools and the norms for clay work** — Grade 4 clay work opens with its kit and its rules: the modelling materials and tools, and the personal-hygiene and work-protection norms for clay work.
2. **Hand-modelling — whistle, plate on a mould, decor** — The techniques are two: free modelling by hand, carried out on a clay whistle toy; and modelling on a mould (calapod), carried out on a decorative plate.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of Paper, Card and Modelling. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name the modelling materials, tools and safety norms.
2. Model a whistle toy freely and a plate on a mould, with decor.
3. Explain how Moldova's book teaches it.
## What you need
Clay or modelling material, modelling tools, a mould (calapod) if available, and water for hands.
