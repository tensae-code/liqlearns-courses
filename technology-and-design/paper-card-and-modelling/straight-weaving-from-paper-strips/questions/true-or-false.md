# Straight weaving from paper strips — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In plain weaving every row starts the same way.
**Answer: False** — Each row starts the opposite way to the row before — that is what locks the pattern.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A woven paper panel can hold together without glue.
**Answer: True** — The interlaced strips lock each other.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Weaving crosses strips only in circles.
**Answer: False** — Straight weaving crosses strips in straight rows, over and under.
