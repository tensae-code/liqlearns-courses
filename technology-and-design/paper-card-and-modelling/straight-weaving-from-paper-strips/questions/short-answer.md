# Straight weaving from paper strips — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Describe the over-under rule in one sentence.
**Answer:** The working strip crosses the fixed strips alternately — over one, under the next — and the next row starts the opposite way.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What two things must you watch while weaving the fence?
**Answer:** Keep the strips straight and push the rows close together, so the panel holds together evenly.
