# Straight weaving from paper strips — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How does the working strip cross the fixed strips?
a) Over one, under the next, alternately b) Over all of them c) Under all of them d) In circles
**Answer: a) Over one, under the next, alternately**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** How does the next row begin?
a) The opposite way to the row before b) Exactly like the row before c) From the bottom only d) It is glued, not woven
**Answer: a) The opposite way to the row before**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** What pattern does plain weaving make?
a) A chequered pattern b) A spiral c) A zigzag only d) Plain stripes with no crossing
**Answer: a) A chequered pattern**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** What is woven in this lesson's make?
a) A fence b) A bowl c) A hat d) A truck
**Answer: a) A fence**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** How does the woven panel hold together?
a) The interlaced strips lock each other b) It is glued at every crossing c) It is sewn d) It is held by pins
**Answer: a) The interlaced strips lock each other**
