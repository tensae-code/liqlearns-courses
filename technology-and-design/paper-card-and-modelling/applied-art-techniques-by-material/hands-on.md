# Hands-on — Applied-art techniques by material

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: metal is cast, forged, engraved and chased; wood is carved and turned; the Kazakh crafts classified by material and process (§8). Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
