# Hands-on — Metal foil chasing/embossing

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: pressing a relief design into foil on a soft mat, alternating raised and sunken parts (the zerger's chasing technique) (Lesson 13). Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
