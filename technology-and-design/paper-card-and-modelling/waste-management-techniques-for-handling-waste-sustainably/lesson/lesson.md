# Waste management techniques for handling waste sustainably

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Rwanda's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — From knowing waste to handling it
The unit's second lesson moves from classification to technique. A classified waste is a waste with a destiny: the technique chosen for it follows from the kind and source the learner recorded in the first lesson.

## Sub lesson 2 — Techniques for sustainable handling
The techniques are the community's working answers — the ways waste is handled so that it harms least and, where possible, serves again. Taught as techniques, not slogans: each is a procedure a learner can carry out and check.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: classification was the knowledge; this is the practice: Rwanda's Primary 6 unit continues into the techniques for handling waste sustainably — what is actually done with each kind of waste. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
