# Waste management techniques for handling waste sustainably — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson add to the waste unit?
a) More classification b) Techniques for handling waste sustainably c) Prices d) History
**Answer: b) Techniques for handling waste sustainably**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the technique for a waste follow from?
a) Its colour b) Its kind and source, recorded in classification c) Its owner d) Chance
**Answer: b) Its kind and source, recorded in classification**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** How are the techniques taught?
a) As slogans b) As procedures a learner can carry out and check c) As songs d) As secrets
**Answer: b) As procedures a learner can carry out and check**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A classified waste is…
a) Forgotten b) A waste with a destiny — its handling chosen c) Always burned d) Always buried
**Answer: b) A waste with a destiny — its handling chosen**

**5. (Sub lesson 1)** The unit's two steps are…
a) Buy, throw b) Classify, then handle c) Hide, burn d) Draw, sing
**Answer: b) Classify, then handle**
