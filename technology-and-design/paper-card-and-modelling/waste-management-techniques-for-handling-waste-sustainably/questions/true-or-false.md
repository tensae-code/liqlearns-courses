# Waste management techniques for handling waste sustainably — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Techniques come before classification in Rwanda's unit.
**Answer: False** — Classification is first; techniques follow from the kind and source recorded.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Sustainable techniques are slogans to recite.
**Answer: False** — They are procedures to carry out and check.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The technique chosen depends on the waste's kind.
**Answer: True** — Kind and source decide the handling.
