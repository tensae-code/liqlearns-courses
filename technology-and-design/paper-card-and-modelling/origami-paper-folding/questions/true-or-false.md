# Origami — paper folding — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Origami figures are glued together at the end.
**Answer: False** — Origami uses folding alone — no glue.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A figure of a person can be folded from a sheet of paper.
**Answer: True** — That is the second make of this lesson.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The folds can be made in any order and the owl will still appear.
**Answer: False** — The folds follow a fixed order; each fold prepares the next.
