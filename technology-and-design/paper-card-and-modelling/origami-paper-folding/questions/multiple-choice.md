# Origami — paper folding — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does origami start from?
a) One square sheet of paper b) A long strip of cloth c) A ball of plasticine d) Cardboard boxes
**Answer: a) One square sheet of paper**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What does origami use to join the figure?
a) Nothing — folding alone b) Glue c) Stitches d) Pins
**Answer: a) Nothing — folding alone**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Why must each fold be sharp and exact?
a) So the later folds meet where they should b) To make the paper heavier c) To tear the paper neatly d) It does not matter
**Answer: a) So the later folds meet where they should**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which figure is folded in the second part of the lesson?
a) A figure of a person b) A truck c) A bowl d) A fence
**Answer: a) A figure of a person**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** In what order are origami folds made?
a) In a fixed order, step by step b) In any order at all c) All at the same time d) Only the last fold matters
**Answer: a) In a fixed order, step by step**
