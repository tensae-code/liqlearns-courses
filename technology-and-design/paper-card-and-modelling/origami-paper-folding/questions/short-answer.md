# Origami — paper folding — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What two tools does origami NOT use?
**Answer:** Scissors and glue — the figure is made by folding alone (no cutting, no sticking).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What should you do when one fold goes wrong?
**Answer:** Unfold just that one step and redo it carefully, instead of unfolding the whole figure.
