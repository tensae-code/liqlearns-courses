# Hands-on — Properties of plastic

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: what plastic is, testing its properties, and building a desk organiser from waste plastic two ways (bottle; cup wrapped in twisted bag strips) (Lesson 8). Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
