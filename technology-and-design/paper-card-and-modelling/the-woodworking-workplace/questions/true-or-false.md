# The woodworking workplace — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Bench order is the first operation of the later woodwork.
**Answer: True** — The workplace is organised before the operations begin.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Tools may be left anywhere on the bench.
**Answer: False** — They are arranged by the technological requirements of the work.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Hygiene and labour protection are part of the workplace lesson.
**Answer: True** — The norms for woodwork are taught with the bench organisation.
