# The woodworking workplace — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is organised first in Grade 5 woodwork?
a) The woodworking workplace — the joiner's bench b) The final product c) The exam d) The storeroom
**Answer: a) The woodworking workplace — the joiner's bench**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** How are tools arranged on the bench?
a) By colour b) By the technological requirements of the work c) By price d) At random
**Answer: b) By the technological requirements of the work**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** What else is fixed in the set-up?
a) Where the workpiece sits b) The weather c) The music d) Nothing else
**Answer: a) Where the workpiece sits**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which norms govern the workplace?
a) Hygiene and labour-protection norms for woodwork b) Kitchen norms c) Road rules d) Library rules
**Answer: a) Hygiene and labour-protection norms for woodwork**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Why are the norms taught with the bench?
a) To fill time b) They are unrelated c) Because the workplace is where they live d) Only for marking
**Answer: c) Because the workplace is where they live**
