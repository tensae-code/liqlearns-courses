# Hands-on — The woodworking workplace

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## Bench set-up
Organise a bench (or draw one organised) for a sawing job: tools arranged by the work's requirements, workpiece placed. Label every decision.

## Norms walk
Go through a wood workplace with the norms in hand. List one kept norm and one broken norm, and the fix for the broken one.
