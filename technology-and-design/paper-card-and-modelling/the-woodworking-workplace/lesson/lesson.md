# The woodworking workplace

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Moldova's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Organising the joiner's bench
Grade 5 woodwork begins at the bench: the joiner's bench is organised — tools arranged by the technological requirements of the work, each in its place — and the workpiece's place on the bench is fixed. Order at the bench is the first operation of every later one.

## Sub lesson 2 — Hygiene and labour-protection norms for woodwork
The workplace carries its norms with it: the hygiene and labour-protection norms for woodwork govern how the bench is kept and how the worker works at it. The norms are taught with the bench itself, because the workplace is where they live.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: the woodworking workplace is organised before work: tools arranged on the joiner's bench by the technological requirements, the workpiece placed, and the hygiene and labour-protection norms in force. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
