# Coil construction in plasticine

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Kazakhstan's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Coil construction in plasticine
Kazakhstan's books teach it this way (the student book, printed pp. 86–90): A jug (kumyra) built from plasticine coils; working with waste materials (a draughts board and bottle-cap pieces) (Lessons 32–34). The learner works through the named materials and steps in order, saying what each step does before doing the next one.

## Sub lesson 2 — Coil construction in plasticine — doing it yourself
Now the learner does the whole task unaided: a jug (kumyra) built from plasticine coils; working with waste materials (a draughts board and bottle-cap pieces) (Lessons 32–34). Working from the first sub lesson's sequence, the learner plans the make, works through it step by step, and checks the finished piece against what the Kazakh book shows.
