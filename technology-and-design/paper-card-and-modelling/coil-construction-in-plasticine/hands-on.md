# Hands-on — Coil construction in plasticine

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: a jug (kumyra) built from plasticine coils; working with waste materials (a draughts board and bottle-cap pieces) (Lessons 32–34). Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
