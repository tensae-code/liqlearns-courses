# Hands-on — Making a working musical instrument as technology

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: a zhetygen from a box with strings on supports, a dauylpaz drum from tins, a sound-maker from a forked branch, wire and buttons, tested for its sound (Lessons 22–23). Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
