# Hands-on — Spatial composition exercises

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: folded-paper relief structures, cut-surface structures, stick (савхан) structures, and frontal, volumetric and kinetic compositions modelled in paper and card. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
