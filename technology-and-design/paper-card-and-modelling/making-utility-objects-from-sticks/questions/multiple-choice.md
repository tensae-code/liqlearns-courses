# Making utility objects from sticks — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What utility object is made from sticks?
a) A doll b) A basket c) A clock d) A bicycle
**Answer: b) A basket**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What changes when sticks move from toys to utility?
a) The material b) The object must serve — carry, hold, last — not only please c) Nothing d) The colour
**Answer: b) The object must serve — carry, hold, last — not only please**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** How is a basket judged?
a) By its price b) By its work — what it holds and how long it holds together c) By its colour d) By its maker
**Answer: b) By its work — what it holds and how long it holds together**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Accuracy in this making is…
a) Decoration b) Utility itself c) Wasted effort d) Only for exams
**Answer: b) Utility itself**

**5. (Sub lesson 1)** Which unit does this make belong to?
a) Toys only b) Utility objects — objects that work for their living c) Drawing d) Electricity
**Answer: b) Utility objects — objects that work for their living**
