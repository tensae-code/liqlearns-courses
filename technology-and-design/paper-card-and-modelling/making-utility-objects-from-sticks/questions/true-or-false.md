# Making utility objects from sticks — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A basket from sticks is a toy.
**Answer: False** — It is a utility object — made to serve, carrying and holding in daily use.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A basket is judged by what it holds and how it holds together.
**Answer: True** — Utility objects are judged by their work.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Sticks were used for toys in earlier makes.
**Answer: True** — The sorghum-stick car came first; the basket turns the same kind of material to use.
