# Making utility objects from sticks

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Rwanda's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — From toy sticks to utility
Earlier makes used sticks for toys; now the same material is worked for use. The shift is the lesson: a utility object must serve — carry, hold, last — not only please.

## Sub lesson 2 — Making a basket from sticks
The named make is a basket from sticks: sticks arranged and bound into a container. A basket is judged by its work — what it holds and how long it holds together — so accuracy in the making is utility, not neatness for its own sake.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: sticks are not only for toys: Rwanda's Primary 4 learners weave them into utility — a basket from sticks, an object that works for its living. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
