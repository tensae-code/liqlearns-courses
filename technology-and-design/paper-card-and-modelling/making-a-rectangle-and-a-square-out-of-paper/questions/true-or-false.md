# Making a rectangle and a square out of paper — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The square and rectangle are only drawn in this lesson.
**Answer: False** — They are made out of paper — produced by hand, not only drawn.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A square's sides agree with each other.
**Answer: True** — The square is the equal-sided shape; making both shapes teaches the difference by hand.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** A shape you have made is a shape you know.
**Answer: True** — Its sides and corners are learned through the fingers, not only the page.
