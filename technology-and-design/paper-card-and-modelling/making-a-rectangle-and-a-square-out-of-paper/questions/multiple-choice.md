# Making a rectangle and a square out of paper — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What are the two shapes made in this lesson?
a) Circle and triangle b) Rectangle and square c) Oval and star d) Cube and sphere
**Answer: b) Rectangle and square**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What are they made out of?
a) Clay b) Paper c) Wire d) Wood
**Answer: b) Paper**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What makes the square different?
a) Its colour b) Its sides agree — it is the equal-sided one c) It has no corners d) It is bigger
**Answer: b) Its sides agree — it is the equal-sided one**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** How does making teach the difference?
a) By hand — the sides and corners are in your fingers b) By price c) By name only d) It does not
**Answer: a) By hand — the sides and corners are in your fingers**

**5. (Sub lesson 1)** When does Rwanda teach this?
a) Primary 6 b) Primary 2, in the making unit c) Secondary school d) Never
**Answer: b) Primary 2, in the making unit**
