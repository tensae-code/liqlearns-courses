# Hands-on — Decorating eggs (folk craft technique)

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## Motif study
Copy three traditional egg motifs onto paper egg shapes. Name what each motif shows, as far as the lesson tells.

## Decorate for the holiday
Decorate and dye eggs (or egg shapes) for a spring holiday using the motifs. Present them with one line on each motif used.
