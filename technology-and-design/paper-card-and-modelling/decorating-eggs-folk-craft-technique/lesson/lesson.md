# Decorating eggs (folk craft technique)

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Moldova's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — The motifs of egg painting
The Grade 5–6 book teaches încondeierea ouălor — egg painting — as a folk craft technique with a named stock of motifs: the patterns tradition hands down for the egg's small curved surface. Learning the craft starts with reading the motifs, not with inventing at random.

## Sub lesson 2 — Decorating and dyeing for the spring holidays
The decorated eggs belong to the spring holidays: eggs are decorated and dyed for them, the motifs applied in the craft's own way. The holiday gives the craft its calendar; the craft gives the holiday its objects.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: egg decorating (încondeierea ouălor) is a folk craft with its own motifs: the decorated and dyed eggs of the spring holidays carry inherited patterns, learned motif by motif. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.

1. Do the Do it first task again, carefully this time, using everything the lesson taught.
2. Check your work against the lesson's own checks — fix one thing you find.
3. Keep your work: it is practice evidence, and the next lesson builds on it.
