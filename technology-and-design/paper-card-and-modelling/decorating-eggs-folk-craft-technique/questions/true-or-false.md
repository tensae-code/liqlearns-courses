# Decorating eggs (folk craft technique) — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Egg decorating is a folk craft technique with its own motifs.
**Answer: True** — The book teaches it as folk craft, motif by motif.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The motifs are invented fresh for every egg.
**Answer: False** — The craft hands its motifs down; learning starts by reading them.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Decorated eggs belong to the spring holidays.
**Answer: True** — The eggs are decorated and dyed for the spring holidays.
