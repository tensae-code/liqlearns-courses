# Decorating eggs (folk craft technique) — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is încondeierea ouălor?
a) Egg painting — the folk craft of decorating eggs b) Egg cooking c) Egg selling d) A spring song
**Answer: a) Egg painting — the folk craft of decorating eggs**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the craft hand down?
a) Its prices b) Its motifs — inherited patterns for the egg c) Its shops d) Nothing — each egg is random
**Answer: b) Its motifs — inherited patterns for the egg**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** For which season's holidays are the eggs decorated?
a) The spring holidays b) Winter only c) Harvest d) No holiday — any day
**Answer: a) The spring holidays**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** How are the eggs finished?
a) Decorated and dyed b) Carved from wood c) Cast in metal d) Woven
**Answer: a) Decorated and dyed**

**5. (Sub lesson 1)** How does learning the craft begin?
a) By breaking eggs b) By buying dyes only c) By reading the traditional motifs d) By guessing
**Answer: c) By reading the traditional motifs**
