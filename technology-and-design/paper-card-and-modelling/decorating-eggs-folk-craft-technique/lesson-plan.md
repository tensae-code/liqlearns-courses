# Lesson plan — Decorating eggs (folk craft technique)

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Paper, Card and Modelling · **Lesson:** Decorating eggs (folk craft technique)
**Place in the curriculum:** Paper, Card and Modelling (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Moldova expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** egg decorating (încondeierea ouălor) is a folk craft with its own motifs: the decorated and dyed eggs of the spring holidays carry inherited patterns, learned motif by motif.
**This lesson's own level:** stocked from Moldova's *Educație tehnologică* (Technological Education) textbooks — see lesson/sources.md. Moldova counts in the acceptance count (student books, not evidence-only).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **The motifs of egg painting** — The Grade 5–6 book teaches încondeierea ouălor — egg painting — as a folk craft technique with a named stock of motifs: the patterns tradition hands down for the egg's small curved surface.
2. **Decorating and dyeing for the spring holidays** — The decorated eggs belong to the spring holidays: eggs are decorated and dyed for them, the motifs applied in the craft's own way.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of Paper, Card and Modelling. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Describe the folk craft of egg decorating and its motifs.
2. Decorate eggs for the spring holidays using traditional motifs.
3. Explain how Moldova's book teaches it.
## What you need
Eggs or egg shapes to decorate, dyes and drawing tools.
