# Hands-on — Making a toy from a drawing (plan)

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: an aeroplane — drawing the body and wing plan, copying the drawing, cutting the parts and assembling the model. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
