# Making learning materials in paper

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Rwanda's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Shapes from a sheet of paper
Primary 4 makes the first set as learning materials: triangle, rectangle and square from a sheet of paper. They are called learning materials because the made shapes are then used to learn with — production feeding study.

## Sub lesson 2 — Harder shapes and regular polygons
Primary 5 raises the set: rhombus, parallelogram and trapezium in paper and manila paper. Primary 6 completes the climb with regular polygons from manila paper. Each year the shapes grow less familiar and the making must grow more exact.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: paper teaches geometry twice in Rwanda's course: first the shapes are made as learning materials — triangle, rectangle and square; then rhombus, parallelogram and trapezium; then regular polygons from manila paper. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.

3. Keep your work: it is practice evidence, and the next lesson builds on it.
