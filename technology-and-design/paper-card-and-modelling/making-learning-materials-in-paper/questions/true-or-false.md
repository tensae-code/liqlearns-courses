# Making learning materials in paper — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Primary 4 set is rhombus, parallelogram and trapezium.
**Answer: False** — Primary 4 makes triangle, rectangle and square; the harder set is Primary 5's.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Regular polygons from manila paper are the Primary 6 make.
**Answer: True** — The ladder ends at regular polygons in Primary 6.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Learning materials are made to be learned with afterwards.
**Answer: True** — Production feeds study — the shapes are used as learning materials.
