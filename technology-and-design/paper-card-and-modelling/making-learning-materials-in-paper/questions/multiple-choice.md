# Making learning materials in paper — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which shapes are made in Primary 4?
a) Rhombus and trapezium b) Triangle, rectangle and square c) Polygons only d) Circles
**Answer: b) Triangle, rectangle and square**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Why are they called learning materials?
a) They are sold b) The made shapes are then used to learn with c) They are decorations d) They are exam papers
**Answer: b) The made shapes are then used to learn with**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Which shapes join in Primary 5?
a) Rhombus, parallelogram and trapezium b) Only squares c) Stars d) Cubes
**Answer: a) Rhombus, parallelogram and trapezium**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** What does Primary 6 add?
a) Nothing b) Regular polygons from manila paper c) Clay shapes d) Wire shapes
**Answer: b) Regular polygons from manila paper**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** How does the sequence climb?
a) Randomly b) Each year the shapes grow less familiar and the making more exact c) It gets easier d) It repeats the square
**Answer: b) Each year the shapes grow less familiar and the making more exact**
