# Making learning materials in paper — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the three shape sets in order.
**Answer:** Primary 4: triangle, rectangle, square. Primary 5: rhombus, parallelogram, trapezium. Primary 6: regular polygons.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What material joins paper in the later sets?
**Answer:** Manila paper.
