# Hands-on — Materials and their groups

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: the four groups of materials; composing a picture from mixed materials; images built from natural materials step by step (Lessons 1–3). Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
