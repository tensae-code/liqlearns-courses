# Hands-on — Production types

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: single, batch and mass production compared, the stages of mass production (prepare materials, make parts, assemble, quality-check), and choosing the type by quantity. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
