# Production types

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Mongolia's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Production types
Mongolia's book teaches it this way (*Design Drawing & Technology 12 (Mongolia)*, printed p. 180): single, batch and mass production compared, the stages of mass production (prepare materials, make parts, assemble, quality-check), and choosing the type by quantity. The learner works through the named materials and steps in order, saying what each step does before doing the next one.

## Sub lesson 2 — Production types — doing it yourself
Now the learner does the whole task unaided: single, batch and mass production compared, the stages of mass production (prepare materials, make parts, assemble, quality-check), and choosing the type by quantity. Working from the first sub lesson's sequence, the learner plans the make, works through it step by step, and checks the finished piece against what Mongolia's book shows.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: Production types — single, batch and mass production compared, the stages of mass production (prepare materials, make parts, assemble, quality-check), and choosing the type by quantity. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
