# Making a toy bicycle and a doll (guide's lesson sequence)

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Rwanda's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — The guide's sequence for Objects Production
This lesson stands on the guide alone: the Primary 4 Teacher's Guide sets out the lesson sequence for the pupil book's Objects Production unit, and its named makes are a toy bicycle and a doll. The sequence is evidence of how the unit is taught, lesson by lesson.

## Sub lesson 2 — The bicycle and the doll
The pair is the unit in miniature: the bicycle — wire's toy, bent and wheeled — and the doll — clay's toy, modelled solid. A teacher planning from the guide meets the two materials through these two makes.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: the Primary 4 teacher's guide sequences the Objects Production unit as lessons on a toy bicycle and a doll — the guide's own ordering of the making the pupils do in clay and wire. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
