# Making a toy bicycle and a doll (guide's lesson sequence) — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Whose sequence is this lesson built from?
a) The pupil's book b) The Primary 4 Teacher's Guide c) Another country d) No book
**Answer: b) The Primary 4 Teacher's Guide**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which unit does the sequence serve?
a) Electricity b) Objects Production c) Waste d) Drawing
**Answer: b) Objects Production**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Which two makes does the sequence name?
a) A car and a house b) A toy bicycle and a doll c) A mat and a basket d) A clock and a bird
**Answer: b) A toy bicycle and a doll**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The bicycle and doll represent which pair?
a) Two papers b) Wire's toy and clay's toy — the unit's two materials c) Two shops d) Two grades
**Answer: b) Wire's toy and clay's toy — the unit's two materials**

**5. (Sub lesson 1)** How does this lesson count?
a) As a pupil placement b) As teacher-guide evidence only, counted nowhere c) As two countries d) It does not appear
**Answer: b) As teacher-guide evidence only, counted nowhere**
