# Lesson plan — Making a toy bicycle and a doll (guide's lesson sequence)

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Paper, Card and Modelling · **Lesson:** Making a toy bicycle and a doll (guide's lesson sequence)
**Place in the curriculum:** Paper, Card and Modelling (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Rwanda expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the Primary 4 teacher's guide sequences the Objects Production unit as lessons on a toy bicycle and a doll — the guide's own ordering of the making the pupils do in clay and wire.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on teacher-guide evidence only — Rwanda's *Science and Elementary Technology* teacher guides are the books in hand for this topic (see lesson/sources.md). Teacher guides are evidence, counted nowhere in the acceptance count.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **The guide's sequence for Objects Production** — This lesson stands on the guide alone: the Primary 4 Teacher's Guide sets out the lesson sequence for the pupil book's Objects Production unit, and its named makes are a toy bicycle and a doll.
2. **The bicycle and the doll** — The pair is the unit in miniature: the bicycle — wire's toy, bent and wheeled — and the doll — clay's toy, modelled solid.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
