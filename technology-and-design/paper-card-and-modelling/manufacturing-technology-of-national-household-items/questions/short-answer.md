# Manufacturing technology of national household items — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Say in one sentence what this lesson teaches.
**Answer:** Manufacturing technology of national household items — studying the materials and the making technology of Kazakh household items (торсық leather flask, round wooden table); developing the sketch and making the pattern (лекала); assembling materials and making the model (§§8–10).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name one material, tool or step this lesson works with.
**Answer:** Any one named in the lesson text — for example, the first step of sub lesson 1: Manufacturing technology of national household items.
