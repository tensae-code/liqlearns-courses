# Hands-on — Manufacturing technology of national household items

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: studying the materials and the making technology of Kazakh household items (торсық leather flask, round wooden table); developing the sketch and making the pattern (лекала); assembling materials and making the model (§§8–10). Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
