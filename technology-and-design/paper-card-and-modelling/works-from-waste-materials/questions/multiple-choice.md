# Works from waste materials — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson teach?
a) Works from waste materials — a party hat and an owl made from paper plates by radial cutting and folding b) Only how to draw pictures c) Nothing practical d) A song to memorise
**Answer: a) Works from waste materials — a party hat and an owl made from paper plates by radial cutting and folding**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Sub lesson 1 is about…
a) Works from waste materials b) Cooking lunch c) A sports match d) The weather
**Answer: a) Works from waste materials**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Sub lesson 2 asks the learner to…
a) Complete the making in full and check it b) Stop before starting c) Copy a partner's work d) Throw the materials away
**Answer: a) Complete the making in full and check it**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Mongolia teaches this topic inside which school course?
a) Visual Art & Technology (Дүрслэх урлаг, технологи) b) Only mathematics c) Only history d) It is not taught at school
**Answer: a) Visual Art & Technology (Дүрслэх урлаг, технологи)**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** What should the learner do first in this lesson?
a) Try the Do it first task in their own way b) Skip to the last page c) Wait to be given the answer d) Nothing at all
**Answer: a) Try the Do it first task in their own way**
