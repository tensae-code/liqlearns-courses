# Hands-on — Traditional head ornaments

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: designing a head-dress ornament — measuring round the head, laying out beads, coral and silver in a net pattern, and setting the requirements (beauty, function, ergonomics, economy). Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
