# Hands-on — Geometric solids as building forms

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: cone, sphere, cube named and used in the New Year workshop: paper fir trees and hanging decorations built from them. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
