# Mosaic — pictures from small pieces — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** The cockerel mosaic is made from flat coloured paper only.
**Answer: False** — It is a raised mosaic from crushed eggshell.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Seeds and rice can be mosaic materials.
**Answer: True** — The swallow is a raised mosaic from seeds and rice.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Sponge pieces are laid one by one until the picture is covered.
**Answer: True** — That is how the raspberry mosaic is built.
