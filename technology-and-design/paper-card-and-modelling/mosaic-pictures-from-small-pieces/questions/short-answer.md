# Mosaic — pictures from small pieces — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the placing rule for every mosaic in this lesson?
**Answer:** Small pieces are placed close together, following the drawing underneath, until the picture is covered.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name two mosaic materials and the picture each one makes.
**Answer:** Any two of: paper pieces — poplar; seeds and rice — swallow; pencil shavings — hedgehog; crushed eggshell — cockerel; crumpled napkin balls — lamb; sponge — raspberries.
