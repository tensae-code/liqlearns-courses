# Mosaic — pictures from small pieces — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is a mosaic?
a) A picture covered with many small pieces placed close together b) A folded paper figure c) A sewn line of stitches d) A single painted brushstroke
**Answer: a) A picture covered with many small pieces placed close together**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which natural materials make the swallow?
a) Seeds and rice b) Sponge and napkin c) Fabric and thread d) Sand and stones
**Answer: a) Seeds and rice**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Why do pencil shavings suit the hedgehog?
a) Their texture matches its prickles b) They are the cheapest material c) They smell nice d) They are waterproof
**Answer: a) Their texture matches its prickles**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** What is the lamb made from?
a) Balls of crumpled paper napkin b) Crushed eggshell c) Coloured paper pieces d) Rice
**Answer: a) Balls of crumpled paper napkin**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** What do sponge pieces give the raspberries?
a) A soft, dotted texture b) A hard shell c) A shiny coat d) A torn edge
**Answer: a) A soft, dotted texture**
