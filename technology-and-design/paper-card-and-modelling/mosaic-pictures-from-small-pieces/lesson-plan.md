# Lesson plan — Mosaic — pictures from small pieces

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Paper, Card and Modelling · **Lesson:** Mosaic — pictures from small pieces
**Place in the curriculum:** Paper, Card and Modelling (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Azerbaijan expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** A mosaic covers a drawn picture with many small pieces — paper, seeds, shavings, shell, napkin balls or sponge — each material giving its own texture.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on a student textbook — Azerbaijan's *Технология (Technology), Grade 2*, Russian edition as held (see lesson/sources.md). It counts in the acceptance count (student book, not evidence-only).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **Mosaic from paper pieces and natural materials** — The first mosaics cover the picture with small pieces of coloured paper — a poplar tree — and then with natural materials: seeds and rice laid as a raised mosaic make a swallow.
2. **Mosaic from pencil shavings, eggshell and crumpled napkin** — Workshop leftovers become mosaic materials: pencil shavings make a hedgehog's prickles, crushed eggshell makes a raised cockerel, and balls of crumpled paper napkin make a lamb's wool.
3. **Mosaic from sponge** — Pieces of sponge, cut small, cover the picture with a soft, dotted texture — here raspberries.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## What you need
Coloured paper scraps, seeds or rice, pencil shavings, clean crushed eggshell, paper napkins, a piece of sponge, glue, a drawn outline.
