# Constructing from cardboard boxes — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why is a cardboard box a good building block?
a) It already has a solid shape with walls and corners b) It is made of metal c) It folds itself d) It needs no joining
**Answer: a) It already has a solid shape with walls and corners**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the constructor ask about a box first?
a) What its shape could become in the build b) How much it costs c) What was inside it d) Who made it
**Answer: a) What its shape could become in the build**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the main box give the wolf?
a) The head's main form b) Only the teeth c) The colour d) Nothing — it is thrown away
**Answer: a) The head's main form**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which parts are added in cardboard?
a) Muzzle, ears and teeth b) Wheels and windows c) Wings d) A tail of string only
**Answer: a) Muzzle, ears and teeth**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** How is the finished construction checked?
a) From the front and the sides b) Only by weighing it c) It is never checked d) Only from underneath
**Answer: a) From the front and the sides**
