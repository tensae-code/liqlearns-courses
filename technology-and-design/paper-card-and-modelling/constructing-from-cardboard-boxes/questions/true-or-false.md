# Constructing from cardboard boxes — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** The wolf's head is made from one box with nothing added.
**Answer: False** — The main box gives the form; muzzle, ears and teeth are added in cardboard.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A box's shape can suggest what it becomes in a build.
**Answer: True** — That is the constructor's first question about any box.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Cardboard boxes are too weak to construct with.
**Answer: False** — Joined and shaped, boxes construct a whole figure — here a wolf's head.
