# Constructing from cardboard boxes — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How do boxes of different sizes help a construction?
**Answer:** They combine into bigger constructions, each box's shape playing a different part.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What do you do if the figure does not read as a wolf from one side?
**Answer:** Fix that side — adjust or add cardboard shapes until the figure reads clearly from the front and sides.
