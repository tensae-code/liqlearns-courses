# Lesson plan — Constructing from cardboard boxes

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Paper, Card and Modelling · **Lesson:** Constructing from cardboard boxes
**Place in the curriculum:** Paper, Card and Modelling (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Azerbaijan expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Cardboard boxes are ready-made building blocks: joined and shaped, they construct a whole figure — here a wolf's head.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on a student textbook — Azerbaijan's *Технология (Technology), Grade 2*, Russian edition as held (see lesson/sources.md). It counts in the acceptance count (student book, not evidence-only).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **Boxes as building material** — A cardboard box already has a solid shape — walls, corners and a hollow inside.
2. **Building a wolf's head** — Boxes and cardboard are joined and shaped into a wolf's head: the main box gives the head's form, added cardboard shapes the muzzle, ears and teeth.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of Paper, Card and Modelling. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Work through: Boxes as building material.
2. Work through: Building a wolf's head.
## What you need
Cardboard boxes of different sizes, extra cardboard, glue or tape, scissors (with care), colouring materials.
