# Making traditional celebration objects — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The “Steaua” is a star made for the carol tradition.
**Answer: True** — The lesson teaches its making technology.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Christmas-tree decorations are only bought, never made, in this lesson.
**Answer: False** — Their making technology is taught beside the star's.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Traditional objects carry the celebration — they are part of the holiday itself.
**Answer: True** — The book teaches the winter holidays through their traditional objects and accessories.
