# Making traditional celebration objects — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which holidays do the traditional objects belong to?
a) The winter holidays b) Harvest time c) The summer holidays d) Birthdays only
**Answer: a) The winter holidays**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the “Steaua”?
a) A tree b) The star carried with the carol c) A cake d) A song book
**Answer: b) The star carried with the carol**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What does 'making technology' mean here?
a) The ordered way of making the object b) A machine c) A shop d) A price
**Answer: a) The ordered way of making the object**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which objects' technologies are taught?
a) The “Steaua” star and Christmas-tree decorations b) Easter eggs c) Kites d) Masks
**Answer: a) The “Steaua” star and Christmas-tree decorations**

**5. (Sub lesson 1)** How are these objects treated in the book?
a) As things to buy b) As pictures only c) As things learners make themselves d) As history only
**Answer: c) As things learners make themselves**
