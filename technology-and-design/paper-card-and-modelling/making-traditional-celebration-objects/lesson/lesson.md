# Making traditional celebration objects

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Moldova's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — The traditional winter-holiday objects
Moldova's Grade 4 book teaches the winter holidays through their objects: the traditional objects and accessories that Moldovan celebration uses — made things that carry the holiday, led by the “Steaua”, the star carried with the carol.

## Sub lesson 2 — The making technology — star and decorations
Each object has its making technology: the “Steaua” (star) is constructed step by step, and Christmas-tree decorations are made beside it. Technology here means the ordered way of making, learned by making.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: traditional celebration objects are made, not bought: the winter holidays have their own objects and accessories — the carol “Steaua” (star) and Christmas-tree decorations — each with its making technology. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
