# Hands-on — Properties of materials for making

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: gouache, watercolour, acrylic (works on cardboard, wood, glass, fabric; fast-drying, light-fast), pencil graphite hardness H/B, pastel, plasticine (clay + wax + fats, does not dry out) — what each material is made of and how it behaves (opening section "Это нужно знать"). Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
