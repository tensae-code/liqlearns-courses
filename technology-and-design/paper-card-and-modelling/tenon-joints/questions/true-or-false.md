# Tenon joints — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A multiple tenon joint has several tenons side by side.
**Answer: True** — Single means one tenon; multiple (compound) means several.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Marking out comes after cutting, to check the result.
**Answer: False** — Tenon and mortise are marked out first, so the halves agree before cutting.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The wooden mallet works with the flat chisels in cutting the joint.
**Answer: True** — The tool list joins mallet to chisels, with the fine-tooth saw and planes.
