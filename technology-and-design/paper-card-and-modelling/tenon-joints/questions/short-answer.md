# Tenon joints — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How do single and multiple tenon joints differ?
**Answer:** A single (simple) joint has one tenon; a multiple (compound) joint has several tenons side by side.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why mark out both halves before cutting?
**Answer:** So tenon and mortise dimensions agree — the fit is decided at the marking.
