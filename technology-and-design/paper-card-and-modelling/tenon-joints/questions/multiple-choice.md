# Tenon joints — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is a tenon?
a) A shaped tongue on one piece that fits a mortise in the other b) A nail c) A glue d) A hinge
**Answer: a) A shaped tongue on one piece that fits a mortise in the other**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is a mortise?
a) A saw b) The matching slot that receives the tenon c) A finish d) A kind of wood
**Answer: b) The matching slot that receives the tenon**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** What are the two forms of the joint?
a) Single (simple) and multiple (compound) b) Long and short c) Wet and dry d) Cheap and dear
**Answer: a) Single (simple) and multiple (compound)**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which tools mark out the joint?
a) Set square and marking gauge b) Compasses and brush c) Hammer and nails d) Rasp and file
**Answer: a) Set square and marking gauge**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Which tools cut it?
a) Fretsaw and dies b) Brace and bit only c) Fine-tooth saw, planes, flat chisels and wooden mallet d) Pyrograph
**Answer: c) Fine-tooth saw, planes, flat chisels and wooden mallet**
