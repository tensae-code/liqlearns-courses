# Lesson plan — Appliqué

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Paper, Card and Modelling · **Lesson:** Appliqué
**Place in the curriculum:** Paper, Card and Modelling (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Azerbaijan expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Appliqué builds a picture from pieces stuck onto a background — torn paper, cut fabric, or pieces built up in relief so the picture stands out.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on a student textbook — Azerbaijan's *Технология (Technology), Grade 2*, Russian edition as held (see lesson/sources.md). It counts in the acceptance count (student book, not evidence-only).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **Torn-paper appliqué — a mushroom on a meadow** — Torn-paper appliqué uses pieces torn by hand, not cut: the torn edge is soft and uneven, which suits natural shapes.
2. **Fabric appliqué — a duck** — Fabric appliqué cuts the picture's shapes from cloth instead of paper.
3. **Three-dimensional appliqué — a cat and a fish** — Three-dimensional appliqué is built up in relief: pieces are layered or raised so the picture stands out from the background instead of lying flat.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
