# Appliqué — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Torn-paper appliqué is cut neatly with scissors.
**Answer: False** — Its pieces are torn by hand — the soft torn edge is the point.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In fabric appliqué the shapes are cut from cloth.
**Answer: True** — The duck's shapes are cut from fabric pieces and stuck on.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A three-dimensional appliqué lies completely flat.
**Answer: False** — It is built up in relief so the picture stands out.
