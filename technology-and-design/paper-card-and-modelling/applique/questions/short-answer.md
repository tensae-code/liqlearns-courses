# Appliqué — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** What does fabric add to an appliqué picture?
**Answer:** The cloth's own colour and texture — the picture is made of real fabric pieces, not paper.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** How can you tell a three-dimensional appliqué by touch?
**Answer:** Its pieces are layered or raised, so the picture stands out from the background and can be felt standing off the page.
