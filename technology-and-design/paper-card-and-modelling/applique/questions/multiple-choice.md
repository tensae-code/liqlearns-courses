# Appliqué — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How are the pieces for torn-paper appliqué made?
a) Torn by hand, not cut b) Cut with a ruler c) Printed by machine d) Folded in half
**Answer: a) Torn by hand, not cut**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Why does a torn edge suit the mushroom picture?
a) Its soft, uneven edge suits natural shapes b) It is sharper than scissors c) It uses less glue d) It dries faster
**Answer: a) Its soft, uneven edge suits natural shapes**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What are the duck's shapes cut from in fabric appliqué?
a) Pieces of fabric b) Plasticine c) Eggshell d) Sponge
**Answer: a) Pieces of fabric**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** What makes three-dimensional appliqué different?
a) The pieces are built up in relief so the picture stands out b) It uses no glue c) It is always black and white d) It is drawn, not stuck
**Answer: a) The pieces are built up in relief so the picture stands out**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Which two animals are made in relief in this lesson?
a) A cat and a fish b) A duck and a turtle c) A hare and a wolf d) A lamb and a hedgehog
**Answer: a) A cat and a fish**
