# Making utility objects in thread — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which two objects are made in thread?
a) A mat and a basket b) Socks and a scarf c) A spoon and a handle d) A doll and a cat
**Answer: b) Socks and a scarf**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What do socks teach about thread work?
a) It is decoration b) It is construction — the object has a form to achieve c) It needs no shape d) It is drawing
**Answer: b) It is construction — the object has a form to achieve**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** How is the scarf judged?
a) By price b) On the neck it will warm — length, evenness, finish c) By colour alone d) By weight
**Answer: b) On the neck it will warm — length, evenness, finish**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Thread joins which utility materials?
a) None b) Sticks, banana fibre and wood c) Clay only d) Wire only
**Answer: b) Sticks, banana fibre and wood**

**5. (Sub lesson 1)** These objects are…
a) Toys b) Wearable utility objects c) Learning materials d) Models
**Answer: b) Wearable utility objects**
