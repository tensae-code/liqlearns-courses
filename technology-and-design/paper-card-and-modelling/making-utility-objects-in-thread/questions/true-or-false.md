# Making utility objects in thread — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Socks and a scarf are toys in this course.
**Answer: False** — They are utility objects — wearables made in thread.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Thread work is construction with a form to achieve.
**Answer: True** — The sock must follow the foot; the scarf is judged on the neck.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Thread is the fourth utility material in the series.
**Answer: True** — After sticks, banana fibre and wood, thread completes the utility materials.
