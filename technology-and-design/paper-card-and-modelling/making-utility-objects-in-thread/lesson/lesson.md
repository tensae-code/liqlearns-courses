# Making utility objects in thread

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Rwanda's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Making socks in thread
The first thread utility is socks: a shaped, fitted object whose making must follow the foot. Socks teach that thread work is construction — the object has a form to achieve, not only a surface to cover.

## Sub lesson 2 — Making a scarf
The second is a scarf: length, evenness and finish judged on the neck it will warm. Two wearables in one lesson make thread a full utility material beside sticks, banana fibre and wood.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: thread becomes utility in Primary 6: socks and a scarf are made — wearable objects, worked stitch by stitch, that warm the maker's own family. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.

## Do It Yourself
1. Do the Do it first task again, carefully this time, using everything the lesson taught.
2. Check your work against the lesson's own checks — fix one thing you find.
3. Keep your work: it is practice evidence, and the next lesson builds on it.
