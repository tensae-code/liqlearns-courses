# Where paper comes from and how it is made

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Mongolia's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Where paper comes from and how it is made
Mongolia's book teaches it this way (*Art & Technology 4 (Mongolia)*, printed p. 38): the industrial paper-making process in nine stages, from forest to finished paper, and why forests must be protected. The learner works through the named materials and steps in order, saying what each step does before doing the next one.

## Sub lesson 2 — Where paper comes from and how it is made — doing it yourself
Now the learner does the whole task unaided: the industrial paper-making process in nine stages, from forest to finished paper, and why forests must be protected. Working from the first sub lesson's sequence, the learner plans the make, works through it step by step, and checks the finished piece against what Mongolia's book shows.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: Where paper comes from and how it is made — the industrial paper-making process in nine stages, from forest to finished paper, and why forests must be protected. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
