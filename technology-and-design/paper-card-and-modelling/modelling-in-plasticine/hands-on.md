# Hands-on — Modelling in plasticine

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## Four methods, four tries
Make a tiny bowl by shaping; add one applied decoration; pull a simple animal from one piece; join two parts into one figure. Name the method each time.

## Which method?
A partner describes a finished figure — one piece pulled out, or parts joined. Say which method made it and how you know.
