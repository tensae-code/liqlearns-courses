# Modelling in plasticine — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How do you start the bowl?
**Answer:** Warm a ball of plasticine in the hands, then press and hollow it with the thumbs until the walls stand even all round.

**2. (Sub lesson 4)** How is the constructive method different from the pulling method?
**Answer:** Constructive modelling joins parts that were modelled separately; pulling draws the whole figure out of one single piece with nothing added.
