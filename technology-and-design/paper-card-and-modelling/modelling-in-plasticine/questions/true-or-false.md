# Modelling in plasticine — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 3)** In the pulling method, extra pieces of plasticine are stuck on to make the legs.
**Answer: False** — Nothing is added — head, legs and tail are pulled out of the one piece.

**2. (Sub lesson 4)** Joins in the constructive method are pressed and smoothed so the figure holds.
**Answer: True** — Firm, smoothed joins keep the joined figure together.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The bowl is hollowed by pressing with the thumbs.
**Answer: True** — The thumbs press and hollow the ball into a bowl shape.
