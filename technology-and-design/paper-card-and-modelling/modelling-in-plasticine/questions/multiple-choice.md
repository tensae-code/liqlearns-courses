# Modelling in plasticine — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How is the bowl made?
a) Pressed and hollowed from a ball of plasticine b) Cut from paper c) Folded from a sheet d) Woven from strips
**Answer: a) Pressed and hollowed from a ball of plasticine**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is applied decoration?
a) Ornament modelled separately and pressed onto the surface b) Paint poured over the work c) A hole cut in the base d) Glitter shaken off
**Answer: a) Ornament modelled separately and pressed onto the surface**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In the pulling method, where do the turtle's legs come from?
a) Pulled and stretched out of the one piece b) Modelled separately and stuck on c) Drawn with a pencil d) Bought ready-made
**Answer: a) Pulled and stretched out of the one piece**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** In the constructive method, how is the cat built?
a) By joining separately modelled parts b) By pulling everything from one lump only c) By folding paper d) By carving wood
**Answer: a) By joining separately modelled parts**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Which make is decorated with a rose in this lesson?
a) The applied-decoration make b) The bowl c) The turtle d) The cat
**Answer: a) The applied-decoration make**
