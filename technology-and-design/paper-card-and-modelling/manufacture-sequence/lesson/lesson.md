# Manufacture sequence

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Kazakhstan's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Manufacture sequence
Kazakhstan's books teach it this way (the student book, printed pp. 60–64): Processing the separate details, assembly and finishing of the details (§16); decorating the product with appliqué and ornament (§17). The learner works through the named materials and steps in order, saying what each step does before doing the next one.

## Sub lesson 2 — Manufacture sequence — doing it yourself
Now the learner does the whole task unaided: processing the separate details, assembly and finishing of the details (§16); decorating the product with appliqué and ornament (§17). Working from the first sub lesson's sequence, the learner plans the make, works through it step by step, and checks the finished piece against what the Kazakh book shows.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: Manufacture sequence — processing the separate details, assembly and finishing of the details (§16); decorating the product with appliqué and ornament (§17). Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
