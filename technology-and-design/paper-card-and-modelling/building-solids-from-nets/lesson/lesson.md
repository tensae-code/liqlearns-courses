# Building solids from nets

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Kazakhstan's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Assembly from flat-pack nets
Kazakhstan's books teach it this way (the student book, printed pp. 74–81): Assembling flat-pack paper toys (giraffe, elephant); a toy vehicle built from boxes and bottles (Section 7 lessons). The learner works through the named materials and steps in order, saying what each step does before doing the next one.

## Sub lesson 2 — Box construction from a net
The Kazakh treatment continues and completes the topic: Gift wrapping: a folding box built from its net; a festive card with a volume carnation (Section 8 topics). The learner finishes the make, checks it against the book's own sequence, and fixes one thing before presenting the work.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: Building solids from nets — assembling flat-pack paper toys (giraffe, elephant); a toy vehicle built from boxes and bottles (Section 7 lessons). Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.

## Do It Yourself
1. Do the Do it first task again, carefully this time, using everything the lesson taught.
2. Check your work against the lesson's own checks — fix one thing you find.
3. Keep your work: it is practice evidence, and the next lesson builds on it.
