# Building solids from nets — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson teach?
a) Building solids from nets — assembling flat-pack paper toys (giraffe, elephant); a toy vehicle built from boxes and bottles (Section 7 lessons) b) Only how to draw pictures c) Nothing practical d) A song to memorise
**Answer: a) Building solids from nets — assembling flat-pack paper toys (giraffe, elephant); a toy vehicle built from boxes and bottles (Section 7 lessons)**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Sub lesson 1 is about…
a) Assembly from flat-pack nets b) Cooking lunch c) A sports match d) The weather
**Answer: a) Assembly from flat-pack nets**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Sub lesson 2 asks the learner to…
a) Complete the making in full and check it b) Stop before starting c) Copy a partner's work d) Throw the materials away
**Answer: a) Complete the making in full and check it**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Kazakhstan teaches this topic inside which school subjects?
a) Artistic Labour and Labour Training b) Only mathematics c) Only history d) It is not taught at school
**Answer: a) Artistic Labour and Labour Training**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** What should the learner do first in this lesson?
a) Try the Do it first task in their own way b) Skip to the last page c) Wait to be given the answer d) Nothing at all
**Answer: a) Try the Do it first task in their own way**
