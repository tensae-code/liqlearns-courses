# Lesson plan — Modelling clay on the potter's wheel

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Paper, Card and Modelling · **Lesson:** Modelling clay on the potter's wheel
**Place in the curriculum:** Paper, Card and Modelling (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Moldova expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** on the potter's wheel (roata olarului) clay is shaped while it turns: the wheel does the rotating, the hands do the shaping, and the form rises from the turning clay.
**This lesson's own level:** stocked from Moldova's *Educație tehnologică* (Technological Education) textbooks — see lesson/sources.md. Moldova counts in the acceptance count (student books, not evidence-only).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **The wheel — modelarea la roata olarului** — Grade 6 ceramics closes at the potter's wheel: modelling clay on the wheel (modelarea la roata olarului), where the clay turns and the potter's hands shape it as it spins.
2. **Wheel work against hand work** — The wheel lesson completes the clay path the books built: free hand-modelling and mould work in Grade 4, the ceramics room in Grade 6 — and now the wheel, where shaping is continuous and the form is raised, opened and refined without stopping the turn..

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of Paper, Card and Modelling. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Describe how clay is shaped on the wheel.
2. Tell wheel modelling from hand modelling.
3. Explain how Moldova's book teaches it.
## What you need
Access to a potter's wheel demonstration, or clear moving pictures of wheel work; clay.
