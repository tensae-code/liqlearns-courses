# Modelling clay on the potter's wheel — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** On the wheel, symmetry comes from the turning itself.
**Answer: True** — The clay turns while the hands shape it.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Wheel modelling replaces the hands — no hand skill is needed.
**Answer: False** — The wheel rotates; the hands do the shaping.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The wheel lesson completes the books' clay path.
**Answer: True** — It follows Grade 4 hand-modelling and the Grade 6 ceramics room.
