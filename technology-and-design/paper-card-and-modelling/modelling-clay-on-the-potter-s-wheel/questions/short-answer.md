# Modelling clay on the potter's wheel — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How is clay shaped on the wheel?
**Answer:** The clay turns on the wheel while the potter's hands shape it — raising, opening and refining the form continuously.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How does wheel work differ from hand modelling?
**Answer:** The form comes from shaping turning clay, so roundness and symmetry come from the rotation itself.
