# Modelling clay on the potter's wheel — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is roata olarului?
a) The potter's wheel b) A kiln c) A mould d) A carving tool
**Answer: a) The potter's wheel**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the wheel do?
a) Fires the clay b) Turns the clay while the hands shape it c) Paints the clay d) Dries the clay instantly
**Answer: b) Turns the clay while the hands shape it**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the turning give the form?
a) Roundness and even symmetry b) Colour c) Weight only d) Nothing
**Answer: a) Roundness and even symmetry**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Where does the wheel lesson sit in the clay path?
a) After hand-modelling and the ceramics room — its completion b) Before any clay is touched c) In the food module d) Nowhere — it is unrelated
**Answer: a) After hand-modelling and the ceramics room — its completion**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** How is shaping on the wheel described?
a) In separate glued pieces b) By carving after firing c) Continuous — the form is raised and refined without stopping the turn d) By stamping
**Answer: c) Continuous — the form is raised and refined without stopping the turn**
