# Modelling clay on the potter's wheel

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Moldova's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — The wheel — modelarea la roata olarului
Grade 6 ceramics closes at the potter's wheel: modelling clay on the wheel (modelarea la roata olarului), where the clay turns and the potter's hands shape it as it spins. Round, even forms are the wheel's gift — symmetry comes from the turning itself.

## Sub lesson 2 — Wheel work against hand work
The wheel lesson completes the clay path the books built: free hand-modelling and mould work in Grade 4, the ceramics room in Grade 6 — and now the wheel, where shaping is continuous and the form is raised, opened and refined without stopping the turn.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: on the potter's wheel (roata olarului) clay is shaped while it turns: the wheel does the rotating, the hands do the shaping, and the form rises from the turning clay. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
