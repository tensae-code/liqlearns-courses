# Lesson plan — Modelling

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Paper, Card and Modelling · **Lesson:** Modelling
**Place in the curriculum:** Paper, Card and Modelling (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Greece expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Working with plastic materials (dough/plasticine) with children.
**This lesson's own level:** stocked from Greece's *Creative Occupation & Visual Aids* student book(s) (Ministry of Education, Religious Affairs and Sports / Institute of Educational Policy, printed by ITYE Diophantos) — see lesson/sources.md. It counts in the acceptance count (student book, not evidence-only).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **Modelling** — The first part of the topic, exactly as Greece's book presents it.
2. **Modelling — doing it yourself** — The topic completed: the making done in full and checked.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say what modelling is and what it is for.
2. Work through the topic's steps in order, as Greece's book teaches them.
3. Explain how Greece's book teaches it.
