# Hands-on — Designing and making a board game (draughts)

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: planning by a design diagram (type, colour, model, material, technology), making the board from 28×28 cm card with 3.5 cm squares, weaving a paper board, and making pieces and a storage box. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
