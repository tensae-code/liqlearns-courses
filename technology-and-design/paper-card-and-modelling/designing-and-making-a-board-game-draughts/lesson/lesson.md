# Designing and making a board game (draughts)

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Mongolia's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Designing and making a board game (draughts)
Mongolia's book teaches it this way (*Art & Technology 5 (Mongolia)*, printed p. 59): planning by a design diagram (type, colour, model, material, technology), making the board from 28×28 cm card with 3.5 cm squares, weaving a paper board, and making pieces and a storage box. The learner works through the named materials and steps in order, saying what each step does before doing the next one.

## Sub lesson 2 — Designing and making a board game (draughts) — doing it yourself
Now the learner does the whole task unaided: planning by a design diagram (type, colour, model, material, technology), making the board from 28×28 cm card with 3.5 cm squares, weaving a paper board, and making pieces and a storage box. Working from the first sub lesson's sequence, the learner plans the make, works through it step by step, and checks the finished piece against what Mongolia's book shows.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: Designing and making a board game (draughts) — planning by a design diagram (type, colour, model, material, technology), making the board from 28×28 cm card with 3.5 cm squares, weaving a paper board, and making pieces and a storage box. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.

## Do It Yourself
1. Do the Do it first task again, carefully this time, using everything the lesson taught.
2. Check your work against the lesson's own checks — fix one thing you find.
3. Keep your work: it is practice evidence, and the next lesson builds on it.
