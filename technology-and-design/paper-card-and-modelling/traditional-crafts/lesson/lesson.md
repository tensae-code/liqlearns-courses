# Traditional crafts

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Mongolia's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Traditional crafts
Mongolia's book teaches it this way (*Дизайн зураг зүй, технологи X*, printed p. 162): studying the national traditional crafts: why they are studied, their types and features. The learner works through the named materials and steps in order, saying what each step does before doing the next one. Mongolia's *Дизайн зураг зүй, технологи X* adds (printed p. 167): wooden goods, the blacksmith's craft (дархан) and sculpture — the traditional wood and metal crafts Mongolia's *Дизайн зураг зүй, технологи X* adds (printed p. 171): the technology of wood carving (модон сийлбэр), artistic metalwork (уран дархан) and related traditional arts Mongolia's *Дизайн зураг зүй, технологи X* adds (printed p. 187): project work: traditional handicraft (уламжлалт гар урлал) — making in the traditional way

## Sub lesson 2 — Traditional crafts — doing it yourself
Now the learner does the whole task unaided: studying the national traditional crafts: why they are studied, their types and features. Working from the first sub lesson's sequence, the learner plans the make, works through it step by step, and checks the finished piece against what Mongolia's book shows.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: Traditional crafts — studying the national traditional crafts: why they are studied, their types and features. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
