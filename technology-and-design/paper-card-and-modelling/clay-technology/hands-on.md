# Hands-on — Clay technology

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: clay as a material; a coil-built flower pot in pictured steps (knead the air out, roll an even coil, spiral the base and score it, wet and stack wall coils, smooth inside and out) (§18). Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
