# Hands-on — Testing fabric and laying out the pattern

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: experiments for creasing, shrinkage and stretch, and placing pattern pieces on 75–90 cm and 120–150 cm fabric widths. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
