# Lesson plan — Preserving and serving

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Paper, Card and Modelling · **Lesson:** Preserving and serving
**Place in the curriculum:** Paper, Card and Modelling (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Mongolia expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Preserving and serving — making jam (1 kg berries, 350 ml water, 1.2 kg sugar), sterilising jars at 70–90°C, folding napkins, and laying a table (the 15 items of a place setting).
**This lesson's own level:** stocked from Mongolia's *Design Technology (Дизайн технологи)* student books (Ministry of Education, Culture, Science and Sports of Mongolia) — see lesson/sources.md. It counts in the acceptance count (student book, not evidence-only). Mongolia's books are image-only scans read by a VISUAL-READ method, as filed in lesson/sources.md.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
