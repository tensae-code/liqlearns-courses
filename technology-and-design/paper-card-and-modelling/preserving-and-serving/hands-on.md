# Hands-on — Preserving and serving

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: making jam (1 kg berries, 350 ml water, 1.2 kg sugar), sterilising jars at 70–90°C, folding napkins, and laying a table (the 15 items of a place setting). Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
