# Preserving and serving — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Say in one sentence what this lesson teaches.
**Answer:** Preserving and serving — making jam (1 kg berries, 350 ml water, 1.2 kg sugar), sterilising jars at 70–90°C, folding napkins, and laying a table (the 15 items of a place setting).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name one material, tool or step this lesson works with.
**Answer:** Any one named in the lesson text — for example, the first step of sub lesson 1: Preserving and serving.
