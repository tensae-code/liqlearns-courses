# The structure of a machine — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Say in one sentence what this lesson teaches.
**Answer:** The structure of a machine — engine, transmission and working part; the kinematic symbols for gear pair, motor, shaft, pulley, screw, bearing and rack.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name one material, tool or step this lesson works with.
**Answer:** Any one named in the lesson text — for example, the first step of sub lesson 1: The structure of a machine.
