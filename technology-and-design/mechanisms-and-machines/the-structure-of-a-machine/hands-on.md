# Hands-on — The structure of a machine

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: engine, transmission and working part; the kinematic symbols for gear pair, motor, shaft, pulley, screw, bearing and rack. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
