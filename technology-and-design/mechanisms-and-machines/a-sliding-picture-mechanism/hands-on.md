# Hands-on — A sliding picture mechanism

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: a sun that slides behind a cloud on a glued strip, with measured slots (18 mm / 20 mm). Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
