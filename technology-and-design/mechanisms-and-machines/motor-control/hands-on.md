# Hands-on — Motor control

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: the L298 motor driver, its truth table for two motors, and PWM speed control (duty cycle 20%, 50%, 90%, 100%). Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
