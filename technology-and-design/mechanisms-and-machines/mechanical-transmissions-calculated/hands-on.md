# Hands-on — Mechanical transmissions calculated

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: the gear ratio i = n₁/n₂ = Z₂/Z₁ = D₂/D₁, rim speed v = ω·R, power P = F·V and torque M = P/ω — with worked belt-drive examples and problem sets (pulley tables, gear trains Z₁=16, Z₂=80…). Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
