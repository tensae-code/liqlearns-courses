# Hands-on \u2014 Making a parachute

Real practice \u2014 this subject is learned by doing.

## Build and drop
Make the parachute: cut the canopy, tape the line points, tie equal strings, attach the clay weight. Drop it three times from the same height and time each fall.

## Fix one fault
If your parachute tips, spins or falls fast, diagnose from the fall: unequal lines? off-centre weight? torn canopy? Fix one fault and re-test.
