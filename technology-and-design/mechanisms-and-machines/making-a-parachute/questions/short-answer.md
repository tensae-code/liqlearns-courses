# Making a parachute \u2014 short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the making steps in order.
**Answer:** Cut the canopy, fix the lines at even points, measure them equal, attach the clay weight where the lines meet.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Your parachute tips to one side. What do you check first?
**Answer:** The line lengths \u2014 an unequal line tips the canopy and spills its air; equalise and re-test.
