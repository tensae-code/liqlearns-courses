# Making a parachute \u2014 multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which materials make the model parachute?
a) Wood, metal, glass b) Plastic sheet or paper, tape, string, modelling clay c) Cloth, needles, thread d) Cardboard and cork only
**Answer: b) Plastic sheet or paper, tape, string, modelling clay**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the modelling clay for?
a) Colouring the canopy b) The load the parachute carries down c) Gluing the lines d) Cutting the sheet
**Answer: b) The load the parachute carries down**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Why must the suspension lines be equal?
a) For beauty only b) Unequal lines tip the canopy and spill its air c) Equal lines cost less d) The tape requires it
**Answer: b) Unequal lines tip the canopy and spill its air**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** How is a parachute tested?
a) By weighing it b) By dropping it and watching the fall c) By folding it away d) By painting it
**Answer: b) By dropping it and watching the fall**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** A spinning fall tells the maker to check\u2026
a) The colour b) Line lengths and the canopy's centre c) The time of day d) The clay's colour
**Answer: b) Line lengths and the canopy's centre**
