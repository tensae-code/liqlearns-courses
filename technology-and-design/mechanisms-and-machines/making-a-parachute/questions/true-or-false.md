# Making a parachute \u2014 true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Tape strengthens the canopy's edge and line points.
**Answer: True** \u2014 Tape is in the materials list for exactly that reinforcing job.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A parachute that falls fast and straight is a success.
**Answer: False** \u2014 Success is a slow, straight, gentle landing; a fast fall means the canopy is not holding air.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Testing is part of making, not separate from it.
**Answer: True** \u2014 Making, testing and fixing is the method \u2014 the fall grades the work.
