# Constructing a position plan of a flat mechanism — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Say in one sentence what this lesson teaches.
**Answer:** Constructing a position plan of a flat mechanism — drawing a crank-slider in 12 equal crank positions, finding each slider position and the trajectory of point C, and applying the method to a sewing-machine and a rocker (кулисн) mechanism.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name one material, tool or step this lesson works with.
**Answer:** Any one named in the lesson text — for example, the first step of sub lesson 1: Constructing a position plan of a flat mechanism.
