# Hands-on — Constructing a position plan of a flat mechanism

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: drawing a crank-slider in 12 equal crank positions, finding each slider position and the trajectory of point C, and applying the method to a sewing-machine and a rocker (кулисн) mechanism. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
