# Worm gears and adjusting the automatic product

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Mongolia's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Worm gears and adjusting the automatic product
Mongolia's book teaches it this way (*Design Drawing & Technology 11 (Mongolia)*, printed p. 165): the worm transmission ratio i = Z₂/Z₁, minimum teeth Zmin = 17, and trials measuring how the step changes when the crank moves between the 8, 10, 12 and 14 mm holes. The learner works through the named materials and steps in order, saying what each step does before doing the next one.

Working from the first sub lesson's sequence, the learner plans the make, works through it step by step, and checks the finished piece against what Mongolia's book shows.
