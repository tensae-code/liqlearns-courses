# Lesson plan — Worm gears and adjusting the automatic product

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Mechanisms and Machines · **Lesson:** Worm gears and adjusting the automatic product
**Place in the curriculum:** Mechanisms and Machines (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Mongolia expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Worm gears and adjusting the automatic product — the worm transmission ratio i = Z₂/Z₁, minimum teeth Zmin = 17, and trials measuring how the step changes when the crank moves between the 8, 10, 12 and 14 mm holes.
**This lesson's own level:** stocked from Mongolia's *Visual Art & Technology (Дүрслэх урлаг, технологи)* student books (Ministry of Education, Culture, Science and Sports of Mongolia) — see lesson/sources.md. It counts in the acceptance count (student book, not evidence-only). Mongolia's books are image-only scans read by a VISUAL-READ method, as filed in lesson/sources.md.
