# Hands-on — Worm gears and adjusting the automatic product

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: the worm transmission ratio i = Z₂/Z₁, minimum teeth Zmin = 17, and trials measuring how the step changes when the crank moves between the 8, 10, 12 and 14 mm holes. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
