# Hands-on — Kinematic diagrams of machines

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: reading the sewing machine's mechanism scheme and the lathe's and drill's kinematic symbols, and the operations a lathe performs (turning cylinders and cones, drilling, facing, grooving, parting). Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
