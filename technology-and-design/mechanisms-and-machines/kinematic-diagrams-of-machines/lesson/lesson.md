# Kinematic diagrams of machines

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Mongolia's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Kinematic diagrams of machines
Mongolia's book teaches it this way (*Design Technology 9 (Mongolia)*, printed p. 81): reading the sewing machine's mechanism scheme and the lathe's and drill's kinematic symbols, and the operations a lathe performs (turning cylinders and cones, drilling, facing, grooving, parting). The learner works through the named materials and steps in order, saying what each step does before doing the next one.

## Sub lesson 2 — Kinematic diagrams of machines — doing it yourself
Now the learner does the whole task unaided: reading the sewing machine's mechanism scheme and the lathe's and drill's kinematic symbols, and the operations a lathe performs (turning cylinders and cones, drilling, facing, grooving, parting). Working from the first sub lesson's sequence, the learner plans the make, works through it step by step, and checks the finished piece against what Mongolia's book shows.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: Kinematic diagrams of machines — reading the sewing machine's mechanism scheme and the lathe's and drill's kinematic symbols, and the operations a lathe performs (turning cylinders and cones, drilling, facing, grooving, parting). Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
