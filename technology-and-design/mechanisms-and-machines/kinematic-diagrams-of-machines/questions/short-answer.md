# Kinematic diagrams of machines — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Say in one sentence what this lesson teaches.
**Answer:** Kinematic diagrams of machines — reading the sewing machine's mechanism scheme and the lathe's and drill's kinematic symbols, and the operations a lathe performs (turning cylinders and cones, drilling, facing, grooving, parting).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name one material, tool or step this lesson works with.
**Answer:** Any one named in the lesson text — for example, the first step of sub lesson 1: Kinematic diagrams of machines.
