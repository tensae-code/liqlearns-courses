# Lesson plan — Analysing a mechanism part by part

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Mechanisms and Machines · **Lesson:** Analysing a mechanism part by part
**Place in the curriculum:** Mechanisms and Machines (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Iran expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** a mechanism is understood by taking it apart with the eyes: part by part, asking of each — what does this part do, and what would fail without it?
**This lesson's own level:** stocked from Iran's *Work & Technology, Grade 9* (student book) — see lesson/sources.md. Iran counts in the acceptance count (student books, not evidence-only).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **The part-by-part method** — Analysis proceeds in order: name the whole mechanism's job; list its parts; for each part, state what it does — what motion or force it receives, and what it passes on; then trace the chain from input to output.
2. **Two studies: the lock and the bench vice** — The module's two studies are a lock and a bench vice.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
