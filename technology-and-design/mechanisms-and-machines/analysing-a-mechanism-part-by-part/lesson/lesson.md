# Analysing a mechanism part by part

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Iran's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — The part-by-part method
Analysis proceeds in order: name the whole mechanism's job; list its parts; for each part, state what it does — what motion or force it receives, and what it passes on; then trace the chain from input to output. A part whose job cannot be stated has not been understood yet. Iran's Grade 9 module teaches the method as class work, not theory.

## Sub lesson 2 — Two studies: the lock and the bench vice
The module's two studies are a lock and a bench vice. In the lock, each part — key, pins or levers, bolt — guards or moves in turn; in the vice, the screw transforms rotation into the jaw's straight, powerful closing. Two familiar mechanisms, analysed part by part, prove the method works on anything.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: a mechanism is understood by taking it apart with the eyes: part by part, asking of each — what does this part do, and what would fail without it? Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
