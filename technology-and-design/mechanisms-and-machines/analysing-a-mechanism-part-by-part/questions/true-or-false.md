# Analysing a mechanism part by part — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Analysis is studying a thing part by part to understand what each part does.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Bench vice has nothing to do with this lesson.
**Answer: False** — It is part of the lesson: the workshop clamp whose screw drives its jaw to grip work.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Input and output is the motion a mechanism receives and the motion it delivers.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.
