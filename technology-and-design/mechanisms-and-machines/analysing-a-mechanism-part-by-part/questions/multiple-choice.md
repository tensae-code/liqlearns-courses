# Analysing a mechanism part by part — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is analysis?
a) studying a thing part by part to understand what each part does b) Only a price c) Only decoration d) Nothing to do with this lesson
**Answer: a) studying a thing part by part to understand what each part does**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is bench vice?
a) Only history b) the workshop clamp whose screw drives its jaw to grip work c) Only storage d) A kind of glue
**Answer: b) the workshop clamp whose screw drives its jaw to grip work**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is input and output?
a) Only old tools b) Only painting c) the motion a mechanism receives and the motion it delivers d) Only a price
**Answer: c) the motion a mechanism receives and the motion it delivers**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson teach, in one line?
a) Only decoration b) Nothing to do with this lesson c) Only history d) a mechanism is understood by taking it apart with the eyes: part by part, asking of each — what does this part do, and what would fail without it?
**Answer: d) a mechanism is understood by taking it apart with the eyes: part by part, asking of each — what does this part do, and what would fail without it?**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** How does Iran's book teach this?
a) As theory only, never practised b) Only in a glossary c) Only as an exam d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Persian
**Answer: d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Persian**
