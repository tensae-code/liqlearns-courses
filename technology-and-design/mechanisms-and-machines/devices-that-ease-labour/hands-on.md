# Hands-on — Devices that ease labour

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: what a device (төхөөрөмж) is, analysing aids (trays, jigs, the sewing machine, the lathe), and redesigning a paint roller (өнхрүүш) — changing the working surface's material and form. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
