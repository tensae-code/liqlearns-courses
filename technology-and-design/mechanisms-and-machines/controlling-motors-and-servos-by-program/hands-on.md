# Hands-on — Controlling motors and servos by program

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: the annotated Motor_test code (analogWrite speeds, digitalWrite directions), writing code to drive a robot along drawn paths (square, diamond), and the Servo-Sweep and Servo-Knob programs with a potentiometer. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
