# Gear mechanisms — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Gear wheel (چرخ دنده) is a wheel with teeth round its rim that meshes with another toothed part.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Mesh has nothing to do with this lesson.
**Answer: False** — It is part of the lesson: the fitting of one gear's teeth into another's gaps so rotation passes without slipping.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Bevel gear is a gear that turns a drive round a corner between meeting shafts.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.
