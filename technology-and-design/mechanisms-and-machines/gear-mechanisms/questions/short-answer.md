# Gear mechanisms — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In your own words, what is gear wheel (چرخ دنده)?
**Answer:** A wheel with teeth round its rim that meshes with another toothed part.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In your own words, what is bevel gear?
**Answer:** A gear that turns a drive round a corner between meeting shafts.
