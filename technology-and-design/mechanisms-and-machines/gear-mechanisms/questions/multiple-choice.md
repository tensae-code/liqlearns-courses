# Gear mechanisms — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is gear wheel (چرخ دنده)?
a) a wheel with teeth round its rim that meshes with another toothed part b) Only a price c) Only decoration d) Nothing to do with this lesson
**Answer: a) a wheel with teeth round its rim that meshes with another toothed part**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is mesh?
a) Only history b) the fitting of one gear's teeth into another's gaps so rotation passes without slipping c) Only storage d) A kind of glue
**Answer: b) the fitting of one gear's teeth into another's gaps so rotation passes without slipping**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is bevel gear?
a) Only old tools b) Only painting c) a gear that turns a drive round a corner between meeting shafts d) Only a price
**Answer: c) a gear that turns a drive round a corner between meeting shafts**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson teach, in one line?
a) Only decoration b) Nothing to do with this lesson c) Only history d) gear wheels (چرخ دنده) mesh teeth to transmit rotation exactly — and each gear type exists to solve one problem of direction, position or motion.
**Answer: d) gear wheels (چرخ دنده) mesh teeth to transmit rotation exactly — and each gear type exists to solve one problem of direction, position or motion.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** How does Iran's book teach this?
a) As theory only, never practised b) Only in a glossary c) Only as an exam d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Persian
**Answer: d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Persian**
