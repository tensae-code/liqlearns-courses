# Gear mechanisms

## Do it first

## Sub lesson 1 — Meshing teeth
A gear wheel carries teeth round its rim. When two gears mesh, the teeth of one fit the gaps of the other, so rotation passes without slipping — unlike belts, the ratio is exact. A small gear driving a large one slows the output and strengthens its turning force; a large driving a small speeds it up and weakens it. Gear size is a trade between speed and force.

## Sub lesson 2 — Types of gear wheels
Iran's Grade 9 module surveys the types of gear wheels (چرخ دنده) and what each type does: straight (spur) gears join parallel shafts; bevel gears turn the drive round a corner between meeting shafts; the worm gear drives a wheel slowly with great force and cannot be driven backward; rack and pinion turns rotation into straight-line motion. The type is chosen by the job the mechanism must do.
