# Mechanisms that transmit and transform movement

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Iran's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — A mechanism is a system
Iran's Grade 9 Motion Mechanisms module opens by recalling the Grade 7 definition: a system is parts working together for one purpose. A mechanism is a moving system: an input motion enters, the parts transmit it from part to part, and an output motion leaves — changed. Rotary motion may become straight-line (reciprocating) motion, fast may become slow, a small force may become a large one.

## Sub lesson 2 — Transmit and transform
Two jobs run through every mechanism. Transmission carries motion from where it is made to where it is needed — across a machine, round a corner, up a shaft. Transformation changes the motion itself: its kind (rotary to linear), its direction, its speed or its force. Naming which job each part does is the first analysis of any mechanism — the skill the module's next lessons practise on gears, locks and vices.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: a mechanism is a system whose parts work together to change one motion into another — taking movement in, and delivering it transformed: different direction, speed or kind. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
