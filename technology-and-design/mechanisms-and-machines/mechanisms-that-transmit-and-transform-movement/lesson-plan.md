# Lesson plan — Mechanisms that transmit and transform movement

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Mechanisms and Machines · **Lesson:** Mechanisms that transmit and transform movement
**Place in the curriculum:** Mechanisms and Machines (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Iran expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** a mechanism is a system whose parts work together to change one motion into another — taking movement in, and delivering it transformed: different direction, speed or kind.
**This lesson's own level:** stocked from Iran's *Work & Technology, Grade 9* (student book) — see lesson/sources.md. Iran counts in the acceptance count (student books, not evidence-only).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **A mechanism is a system** — Iran's Grade 9 Motion Mechanisms module opens by recalling the Grade 7 definition: a system is parts working together for one purpose.
2. **Transmit and transform** — Two jobs run through every mechanism.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
