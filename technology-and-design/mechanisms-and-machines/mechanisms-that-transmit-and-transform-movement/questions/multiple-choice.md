# Mechanisms that transmit and transform movement — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is mechanism?
a) a system whose parts work together to change one motion into another b) Only a price c) Only decoration d) Nothing to do with this lesson
**Answer: a) a system whose parts work together to change one motion into another**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is transmission?
a) Only history b) carrying motion from where it is made to where it is needed c) Only storage d) A kind of glue
**Answer: b) carrying motion from where it is made to where it is needed**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is transformation?
a) Only old tools b) Only painting c) changing a motion's kind, direction, speed or force d) Only a price
**Answer: c) changing a motion's kind, direction, speed or force**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson teach, in one line?
a) Only decoration b) Nothing to do with this lesson c) Only history d) a mechanism is a system whose parts work together to change one motion into another — taking movement in, and delivering it transformed: different direction, speed or kind.
**Answer: d) a mechanism is a system whose parts work together to change one motion into another — taking movement in, and delivering it transformed: different direction, speed or kind.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** How does Iran's book teach this?
a) As theory only, never practised b) Only in a glossary c) Only as an exam d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Persian
**Answer: d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Persian**
