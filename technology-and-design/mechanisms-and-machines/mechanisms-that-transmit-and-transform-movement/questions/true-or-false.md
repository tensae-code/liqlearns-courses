# Mechanisms that transmit and transform movement — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Mechanism is a system whose parts work together to change one motion into another.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Transmission has nothing to do with this lesson.
**Answer: False** — It is part of the lesson: carrying motion from where it is made to where it is needed.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Transformation is changing a motion's kind, direction, speed or force.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.
