# Lesson plan — Building the mechanism

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Mechanisms and Machines · **Lesson:** Building the mechanism
**Place in the curriculum:** Mechanisms and Machines (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Iran expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the mechanism is built part by part — pulley, belt wheel and frame for the class crane — until rotary motion enters and linear motion does the lifting.
**This lesson's own level:** stocked from Iran's *Work & Technology, Grade 9* (student book) — see lesson/sources.md. Iran counts in the acceptance count (student books, not evidence-only).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **Making the parts** — The build makes the mechanism's parts one by one: the pulley that guides and redirects, the belt wheel that carries the drive, and the frame that holds every part in its true position.
2. **The crane and the movable bridge** — Assembled, the parts form a small crane: rotary motion at the handle becomes the linear rising of the load — the book's prescribed design-and-build project.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
