# Building the mechanism

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Iran's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Making the parts
The build makes the mechanism's parts one by one: the pulley that guides and redirects, the belt wheel that carries the drive, and the frame that holds every part in its true position. Each part is made to the design the simulation proved. Iran's Grade 9 module runs the making as a build sequence, part after part, toward the prescribed project.

## Sub lesson 2 — The crane and the movable bridge
Assembled, the parts form a small crane: rotary motion at the handle becomes the linear rising of the load — the book's prescribed design-and-build project. The module also studies a creative mechanism example, the movable bridge, whose span itself moves to let traffic pass: proof that mechanism-building is invention, not only exercise.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: the mechanism is built part by part — pulley, belt wheel and frame for the class crane — until rotary motion enters and linear motion does the lifting. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
