# Building the mechanism — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is pulley?
a) a wheel that guides a rope or belt and redirects its pull b) Only a price c) Only decoration d) Nothing to do with this lesson
**Answer: a) a wheel that guides a rope or belt and redirects its pull**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is belt wheel?
a) Only history b) a wheel that carries a moving belt to transmit rotation c) Only storage d) A kind of glue
**Answer: b) a wheel that carries a moving belt to transmit rotation**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is frame?
a) Only old tools b) Only painting c) the structure that holds a mechanism's parts in their true positions d) Only a price
**Answer: c) the structure that holds a mechanism's parts in their true positions**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson teach, in one line?
a) Only decoration b) Nothing to do with this lesson c) Only history d) the mechanism is built part by part — pulley, belt wheel and frame for the class crane — until rotary motion enters and linear motion does the lifting.
**Answer: d) the mechanism is built part by part — pulley, belt wheel and frame for the class crane — until rotary motion enters and linear motion does the lifting.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** How does Iran's book teach this?
a) As theory only, never practised b) Only in a glossary c) Only as an exam d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Persian
**Answer: d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Persian**
