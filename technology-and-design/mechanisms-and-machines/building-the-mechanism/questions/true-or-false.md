# Building the mechanism — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Pulley is a wheel that guides a rope or belt and redirects its pull.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Belt wheel has nothing to do with this lesson.
**Answer: False** — It is part of the lesson: a wheel that carries a moving belt to transmit rotation.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Frame is the structure that holds a mechanism's parts in their true positions.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.
