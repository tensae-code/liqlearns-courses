# Hands-on — Shapes and solids for movement

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## Shape census
Draw a bicycle or car from a picture. Label at least five shapes or solids on your drawing, each with its movement job.

## Build a composite
From card, make two simple solids (a cuboid and a cylinder), then join them into one composite object. Draw the result in 2D and in 3D.
