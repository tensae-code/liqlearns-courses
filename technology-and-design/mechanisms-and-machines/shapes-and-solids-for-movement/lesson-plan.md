# Lesson plan — Shapes and solids for movement

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Mechanisms and Machines · **Lesson:** Shapes and solids for movement
**Place in the curriculum:** Mechanisms and Machines (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Jamaica expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the shapes and solids a machine is built from decide how it moves — a car's round wheels, flat body panels and solid chassis each do a movement job — and makers draw those shapes in 2D and build them in 3D.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Jamaica's *National Standards Curriculum Guide — Grades 4–6 — Resource & Technology* (Ministry of Education, Youth and Information, Kingston, Jamaica, © 2018), a curriculum guide with full unit teaching content, read COMPLETE (see lesson/sources.md). It counts in the acceptance count (curriculum guide with full teaching content, not evidence-only).

## Sub lessons
1. **Shapes on a working thing** — Find the shapes first: circles roll, triangles brace, rectangles carry and cover.
2. **Solids and composite objects** — Real parts are solids — and most real parts are composites, several simple solids joined.
3. **Drawing shapes in 2D and 3D** — The same part is drawn flat (2D) to measure it and solid (3D) to see it whole.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of Mechanisms and Machines. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Identify the shapes and solids on a car and say how each influences its operation.
2. Name the simple solids in a composite object like a toy car.
3. Draw a car component in both 2D and 3D orientation.
## What you need
A toy car or clear picture of one, paper and a pencil.
