# Shapes and solids for movement — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 2) Every part of a car is one simple solid.**
**Answer: False — The car is a composite of many simple solids joined.**

**2. (Sub lesson 3) A 3D drawing shows the solid as the eye sees it.**
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) The shapes on a machine have nothing to do with how it moves.**
**Answer: False — Shape decides movement: circles roll, triangles brace.**
