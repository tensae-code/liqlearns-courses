# Shapes and solids for movement — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, or by doing the step named.

**1. (Sub lesson 2) Break a toy car into its simple solids.**
**Answer:** Wheels: short cylinders; axles: long thin cylinders; body/chassis: cuboid (flat box) — joined as one composite object.

**2. (Sub lesson 3) When would a maker draw a part in 2D, and when in 3D?**
**Answer:** 2D to measure and cut the part truly; 3D to check the whole form as the eye will see it.
