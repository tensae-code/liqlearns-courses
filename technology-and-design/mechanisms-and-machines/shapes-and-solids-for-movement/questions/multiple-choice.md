# Shapes and solids for movement — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Why are wheels circular?**
a) Circles are easiest to paint b) Only a circle rolls smoothly c) Circles are the cheapest shape d) Tradition only
**Answer: b) Only a circle rolls smoothly**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) A triangle is used in structures because it…**
a) Rolls well b) Holds its shape c) Is flat d) Is round
**Answer: b) Holds its shape**

**3. (Sub lesson 2) A wheel is best described as which solid?**
a) A cube b) A short cylinder c) A pyramid d) A sphere
**Answer: b) A short cylinder**

**4. (Sub lesson 2) A composite object is…**
a) One simple solid b) Several simple solids joined into one thing c) A drawing only d) A broken solid
**Answer: b) Several simple solids joined into one thing**

**5. (Sub lesson 3) A 2D drawing of a part is the one you use to…**
a) Admire its colour b) Measure and cut it truly c) See it as the eye sees it d) Weigh it
**Answer: b) Measure and cut it truly**
