# Video script — Refrigeration and air-conditioning

A short demonstration video, made later, in a super-simple voice. The demonstrator works through the topic while the voice speaks.

---

This lesson is about refrigeration and air-conditioning.
Refrigeration terminology (refrigeration effect, ton of refrigeration, COP, ice-making capacity), properties of a good refrigerant, the refrigeration mechanic's tools (gauge manifold, yoke vice, tape, hacksaw, drill), servicing refrigerators (thermostat/vents, coils, condenser fan, circuit board), air-conditioning principles and classification, psychrometric terms (dry/moist air, dry/wet-bulb temperature, dew point, humidity), the 10 steps of servicing an air conditioner.
Work step by step. Check your work as you go.
