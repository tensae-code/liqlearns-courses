# Lesson plan — How a piston engine moves a snowmobile

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Mechanisms and Machines · **Lesson:** How a piston engine moves a snowmobile
**Place in the curriculum:** Mechanisms and Machines (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Canada expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** burning gasoline inside a cylinder makes energy, the energy pushes pistons up and down, and that up-and-down movement is turned into the turning that drives a snowmobile's tracks.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on a student reader — Canada's *Technology in the Community* (Tulliniliara Reading Series, Level 14), Government of Nunavut / Inhabit Education (see lesson/sources.md). It counts in the acceptance count (student book, not evidence-only).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **Burning fuel makes energy** — Inside the engine is a closed tube called a cylinder.
2. **Pistons move, and the movement drives the tracks** — A piston is a plug that fits tightly inside the cylinder and can slide.
3. **The same engine in cars and trucks — and the mechanic's job** — Snowmobile, car and truck engines work the same way: fuel burns in cylinders and pistons carry the movement to the wheels or tracks.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of Mechanisms and Machines. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Explain where an engine's energy comes from.
2. Describe how a piston moves and what its movement drives on a snowmobile.
3. Say how car and truck engines are like a snowmobile engine, and what a mechanic does.
## What you need
Nothing special — a partner to talk with. Never open or run a real engine for this lesson; engines are hot, loud and dangerous, and this lesson is learned from the explanation and the activities.
