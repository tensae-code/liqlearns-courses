# Hands-on — How a piston engine moves a snowmobile

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## Act the piston
Hold one fist inside the tube of your other hand like a piston in a cylinder. Push it down sharply, let it rise, and repeat in a steady rhythm while a partner turns an imaginary track in time with your pushes. Swap roles and explain the chain as you go.

## Same engine, three machines
Draw three columns: snowmobile, car, truck. In each, write what the engine's movement drives (tracks or wheels) and one job the machine does. Finish with one sentence a mechanic would agree with about keeping engines running.
