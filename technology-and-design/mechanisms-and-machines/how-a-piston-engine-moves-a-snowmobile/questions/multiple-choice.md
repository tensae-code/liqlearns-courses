# How a piston engine moves a snowmobile — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Where does a piston engine's energy come from?
a) Burning gasoline inside the cylinder b) The cold snow c) The driver's feet d) The wind only
**Answer: a) Burning gasoline inside the cylinder**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is a cylinder?
a) The closed tube the piston slides in b) A wheel c) A seat d) A fuel tank
**Answer: a) The closed tube the piston slides in**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** How does a piston move?
a) Up and down, again and again b) Side to side once c) It never moves d) In circles only
**Answer: a) Up and down, again and again**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the piston's movement drive on a snowmobile?
a) The tracks b) The headlights only c) The seat d) Nothing
**Answer: a) The tracks**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** What does a mechanic do for engines?
a) Maintains, fixes and replaces engine parts b) Paints them only c) Sells fuel only d) Nothing
**Answer: a) Maintains, fixes and replaces engine parts**
