# How a piston engine moves a snowmobile — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The piston sits inside the cylinder.
**Answer: True** — The piston is the tight-fitting plug that slides inside the cylinder.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The piston's movement has nothing to do with the tracks turning.
**Answer: False** — The engine turns the piston's up-and-down movement into the turning that drives the tracks.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Car engines work in a completely different way from snowmobile engines.
**Answer: False** — Snowmobile, car and truck engines work the same way.
