# How a piston engine moves a snowmobile — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Tell the energy chain from fuel to movement in one or two sentences.
**Answer:** Gasoline burns in the cylinder and the burst of energy pushes the piston up and down; the engine turns that movement into turning that drives the tracks.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** How are car and truck engines like a snowmobile engine?
**Answer:** They work the same way — fuel burns in cylinders and pistons carry the movement to the wheels instead of tracks.
