# How a bicycle works \u2014 multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which elements perform propulsion on a bicycle?
a) Handlebar and bell b) Pedals, chain and rear wheel c) Brakes and pads d) Saddle and frame
**Answer: b) Pedals, chain and rear wheel**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The chain's job in propulsion is to\u2026
a) Slow the wheel b) Carry the pedals' turning to the wheel c) Steer the bicycle d) Hold the saddle
**Answer: b) Carry the pedals' turning to the wheel**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Steering is performed by\u2026
a) The handlebar joined to the front wheel b) The chain c) The brake pads d) The pedals
**Answer: a) The handlebar joined to the front wheel**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Brakes slow the wheel by\u2026
a) Pushing it forward b) Friction \u2014 pads gripping the wheel c) Cutting the chain d) Turning the handlebar
**Answer: b) Friction \u2014 pads gripping the wheel**

**5. (Sub lesson 1)** The pattern for explaining a machine is\u2026
a) Colour, price, age b) Function \u2192 working principle \u2192 elements c) Name, shop, owner d) Size, weight, colour
**Answer: b) Function \u2192 working principle \u2192 elements**
