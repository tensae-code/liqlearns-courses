# How a bicycle works \u2014 true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The chain steers the bicycle.
**Answer: False** \u2014 The chain belongs to propulsion; the handlebar and front wheel steer.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Braking turns the rider's squeeze into friction.
**Answer: True** \u2014 The levers drive pads against the wheel, and friction does the slowing.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Every function of a machine is performed by named elements.
**Answer: True** \u2014 That is the analysis pattern: each function links to a working principle and its elements.
