# How a bicycle works \u2014 short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain propulsion in one chain of action.
**Answer:** The rider's legs turn the pedals, the chain carries the turning to the rear wheel, and the wheel pushes the bicycle forward.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why squeeze harder to stop faster?
**Answer:** A harder squeeze presses the pads harder against the wheel, increasing friction, so the wheel slows faster.
