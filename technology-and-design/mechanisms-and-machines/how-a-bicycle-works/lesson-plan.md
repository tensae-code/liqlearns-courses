# Lesson plan \u2014 How a bicycle works

**Course:** Technology and Design \u00b7 **Title:** Mechanisms and Machines \u00b7 **Lesson:** How a bicycle works
**Place in the curriculum:** Mechanisms and Machines (CURRICULUM.md) \u2014 stocked in the Gabon expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** a bicycle works by chosen technical solutions: one for propulsion, one for steering, one for braking \u2014 link each function to the elements that perform it.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on teacher-guide evidence only \u2014 Gabon's *Super Efficace* Sciences teacher guides (EDIG / \u00c9dicef, Institut P\u00e9dagogique National, 2024) are the books in hand for this topic (see lesson/sources.md). Teacher guides are evidence, counted nowhere in the acceptance count.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on \u2014 an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of \u2026" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **Propulsion \u2014 moving the bicycle** \u2014 Pedals, chain and wheel: the rider's effort becomes forward movement.
2. **Steering and braking** \u2014 Handlebar and front wheel aim the bicycle; brakes slow and stop it.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson \u2014 no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of Mechanisms and Machines. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Explain propulsion: pedals, chain, wheel.
2. Explain steering and braking and their elements.
3. Use the pattern: function \u2192 working principle \u2192 elements.
## What you need
A bicycle to examine if one is available; otherwise the lesson's descriptions.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** \u2014 the opening doing task in lesson/lesson.md.
2. **The explanation** \u2014 the sub lessons in order (lesson/lesson.md).
   - ADD IMAGE: a picture of a picture of a bicycle with three callouts \u2014 pedals and chain, handlebar, brake lever \u2014 the student matches each callout to propulsion, steering or braking.
   - ADD GIF: a GIF of a GIF of a chain drive in motion: pedal turning, chain moving, rear wheel spinning \u2014 the student follows the turning from leg to road.
   - ADD AUDIO: a voice reading the three functions aloud \u2014 propulsion, steering, braking \u2014 the student repeats them and names one element for each.
   - ADD VIDEO: a short video of the lesson demonstration \u2014 see video-script.md.
3. **Say it to your companion** \u2014 the learner explains the lesson's key rule aloud.
4. **Questions** \u2014 the questions/ files, spread over the sub lessons.
5. **Hands-on** \u2014 hands-on.md.
6. **Do It Yourself** \u2014 the closing doing block in lesson/lesson.md.
