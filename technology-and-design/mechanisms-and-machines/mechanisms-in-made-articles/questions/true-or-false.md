# Mechanisms in made articles — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Axle (eje) is the bar that joins two wheels and turns with them.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Shaft (árbol) has nothing to do with this lesson.
**Answer: False** — It is part of the lesson: a rod that carries turning movement along a machine.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Crank (manivela) is a bent handle that turns a shaft by hand.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.
