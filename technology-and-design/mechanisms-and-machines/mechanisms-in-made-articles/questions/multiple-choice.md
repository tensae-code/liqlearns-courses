# Mechanisms in made articles — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is axle (eje)?
a) the bar that joins two wheels and turns with them b) Only a price c) Only decoration d) Nothing to do with this lesson
**Answer: a) the bar that joins two wheels and turns with them**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is shaft (árbol)?
a) Only history b) a rod that carries turning movement along a machine c) Only storage d) A kind of glue
**Answer: b) a rod that carries turning movement along a machine**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is crank (manivela)?
a) Only old tools b) Only painting c) a bent handle that turns a shaft by hand d) Only a price
**Answer: c) a bent handle that turns a shaft by hand**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson teach, in one line?
a) Only decoration b) Nothing to do with this lesson c) Only history d) mechanisms live inside made articles: the wheel, the axle and the shaft (rueda, eje y árbol) turn a build into a moving toy — a cart, a crane, a roundabout, a mill.
**Answer: d) mechanisms live inside made articles: the wheel, the axle and the shaft (rueda, eje y árbol) turn a build into a moving toy — a cart, a crane, a roundabout, a mill.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** How does Cuba's book teach this?
a) As theory only, never practised b) Only in a glossary c) Only as an exam d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Spanish
**Answer: d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Spanish**
