# Lesson plan — Mechanisms in made articles

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Mechanisms and Machines · **Lesson:** Mechanisms in made articles
**Place in the curriculum:** Mechanisms and Machines (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Cuba expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** mechanisms live inside made articles: the wheel, the axle and the shaft (rueda, eje y árbol) turn a build into a moving toy — a cart, a crane, a roundabout, a mill.
**This lesson's own level:** stocked from Cuba's *Programa: Educación Laboral — 7mo grado* and the Cuba books in lesson/sources.md — see lesson/sources.md. Cuba counts in the acceptance count for its student-book placements; its programme and methodological-orientations placements are evidence only, counted nowhere.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **Wheel, axle and shaft** — Three elements carry most toy movement: the wheel that rolls, the axle (eje) that joins two wheels, and the shaft (árbol) that carries turning power along a machine. Cuba's Grade 7 programme names the trio inside its wood unit, for toy construction.
2. **Mechanisms in the models** — In technical modelling the same thinking builds working models: pulley and cable, gears, hook and crank (polea, cable, engranes, gancho, manivela) applied in a cart, a crane, a roundabout, a mill — built from a model and its graphic representation.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
