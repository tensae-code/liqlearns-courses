# Mechanisms in made articles

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Cuba's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Wheel, axle and shaft
Three elements carry most toy movement: the wheel that rolls, the axle (eje) that joins two wheels, and the shaft (árbol) that carries turning power along a machine. Cuba's Grade 7 programme names the trio inside its wood unit, for toy construction.

## Sub lesson 2 — Mechanisms in the models
In technical modelling the same thinking builds working models: pulley and cable, gears, hook and crank (polea, cable, engranes, gancho, manivela) applied in a cart, a crane, a roundabout, a mill — built from a model and its graphic representation.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: mechanisms live inside made articles: the wheel, the axle and the shaft (rueda, eje y árbol) turn a build into a moving toy — a cart, a crane, a roundabout, a mill. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.

3. Keep your work: it is practice evidence, and the next lesson builds on it.
