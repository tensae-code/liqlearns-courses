# Forces and friction in mechanisms — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Which set names the three force actions?**
a) Push, pull, turn b) Push, paint, polish c) Lift, drop, throw d) Start, stop, float
**Answer: a) Push, pull, turn**

**2. (Sub lesson 2) A boat stays up on water because a force makes it…**
a) Sink b) Float c) Melt d) Turn
**Answer: b) Float**

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Squeezing clay is a force changing a thing's…**
a) Colour b) Name c) Shape d) Owner
**Answer: c) Shape**

**4. (Sub lesson 3) Friction acts…**
a) With the sliding b) Against the sliding c) Only in water d) Only on wheels
**Answer: b) Against the sliding**

**5. (Sub lesson 3) Why do makers oil moving parts?**
a) To increase friction b) To reduce friction and wear c) To make parts heavier d) To stop all movement
**Answer: b) To reduce friction and wear**
