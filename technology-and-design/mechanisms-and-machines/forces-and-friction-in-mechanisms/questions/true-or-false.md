# Forces and friction in mechanisms — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) A thing can start moving with no force acting on it.**
**Answer: False — A force starts every movement.**

**2. (Sub lesson 3) Friction is always harmful and never useful.**
**Answer: False — Brakes, tyres and shoes all depend on friction's grip.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Forces can change a thing's direction without stopping it.**
**Answer: True — Turning the front wheels changes a car's direction while it moves.**
