# Forces and friction in mechanisms — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, or by doing the step named.

**1. (Sub lesson 3) A car rolls across the floor and stops by itself. What stopped it?**
**Answer:** Friction (at the wheels and axles, and air resistance) acting against its movement until its kinetic energy is used up.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Give one everyday example each of a push, a pull and a turn.**
**Answer:** Push: closing a door; pull: opening a drawer; turn: twisting a tap (any correct examples).
