# Simple machines — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A simple machine needs an engine to work.
**Answer: False** — A simple machine makes work easier by shape and arrangement alone — no engine, no power.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Wedges and screws are types of simple machines.
**Answer: True** — Rwanda's list is levers, wheel and axle, pulleys, inclined planes, wedges and screws.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Once you know the types, you can find simple machines all around you.
**Answer: True** — Each type is one idea repeated everywhere — the wheelbarrow, the windlass, the ladder.
