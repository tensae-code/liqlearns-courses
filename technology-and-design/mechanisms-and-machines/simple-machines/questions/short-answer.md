# Simple machines — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name Rwanda's six types of simple machines.
**Answer:** Levers, wheel and axle, pulleys, inclined planes, wedges and screws.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Give the book's example for the inclined plane and for wheel and axle.
**Answer:** The ladder for the inclined plane; the windlass for wheel and axle.
