# Simple machines — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is a simple machine?
a) A fast engine b) A device that makes work easier c) A kind of computer d) A drawing tool
**Answer: b) A device that makes work easier**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The wheelbarrow and the crowbar are examples of which type?
a) Pulleys b) Levers c) Screws d) Wedges
**Answer: b) Levers**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The windlass is Rwanda's example of which type?
a) Wheel and axle b) Inclined plane c) Wedge d) Lever
**Answer: a) Wheel and axle**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Elevators and cargo lifts use which type?
a) Screws b) Pulleys c) Wedges d) Planes
**Answer: b) Pulleys**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The ladder is the book's example of which type?
a) A wedge b) An inclined plane c) A pulley d) A screw
**Answer: b) An inclined plane**
