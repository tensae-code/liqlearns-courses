# Lesson plan \u2014 How a parachute works

**Course:** Technology and Design \u00b7 **Title:** Mechanisms and Machines \u00b7 **Lesson:** How a parachute works
**Place in the curriculum:** Mechanisms and Machines (CURRICULUM.md) \u2014 stocked in the Gabon expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** a parachute slows a fall for a safe landing: its canopy catches the air, air resistance holds the fall back, and suspension lines carry the load.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on teacher-guide evidence only \u2014 Gabon's *Super Efficace* Sciences teacher guides (EDIG / \u00c9dicef, Institut P\u00e9dagogique National, 2024) are the books in hand for this topic (see lesson/sources.md). Teacher guides are evidence, counted nowhere in the acceptance count.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on \u2014 an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of \u2026" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **Slowing a fall** \u2014 The parachute's function: turn a dangerous fall into a slow, safe descent.
2. **Canopy, air resistance and lines** \u2014 A wide canopy pushes against the air; lines spread the load to the harness.
3. **Other uses of the parachute idea** \u2014 Braking an aircraft on landing, and resistance training.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson \u2014 no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of Mechanisms and Machines. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State a parachute's function.
2. Explain canopy, air resistance and suspension lines.
3. Name two other uses of parachute drag.
## What you need
Two sheets of paper for the opening comparison.
