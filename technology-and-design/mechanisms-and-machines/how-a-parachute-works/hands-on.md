# Hands-on \u2014 How a parachute works

Real practice \u2014 this subject is learned by doing.

## Flat vs crumpled
Time the fall of a flat sheet and a crumpled ball of the same paper from the same height, three drops each. Record and explain the difference in one sentence.

## Drag hunt
List three places where catching the air is useful (parachute, sail, braking chute, training chute). For each, write what the resistance does.
