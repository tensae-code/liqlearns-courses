# How a parachute works \u2014 true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A parachute defeats gravity completely.
**Answer: False** \u2014 Gravity still pulls; the parachute only slows the descent to a safe speed.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Air resistance grows with the canopy's area.
**Answer: True** \u2014 A wider canopy meets more air, so the push-back is stronger.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Parachute drag is useful only for people falling from aircraft.
**Answer: False** \u2014 It also brakes landing aircraft and gives resistance in training.
