# How a parachute works \u2014 short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Why does a wider canopy slow the fall more?
**Answer:** It pushes against a larger body of air, so the air's push-back \u2014 resistance \u2014 is greater.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Why is a parachute landing called safe?
**Answer:** Because the descent is slowed enough for the person or load to land without the damage a fast fall would cause.
