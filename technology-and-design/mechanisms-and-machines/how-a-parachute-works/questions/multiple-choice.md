# How a parachute works \u2014 multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is a parachute's function?
a) To speed up a fall b) To slow a fall for a safe landing c) To steer an aircraft d) To carry loads upward
**Answer: b) To slow a fall for a safe landing**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Does a parachute stop the fall completely?
a) Yes, it hovers forever b) No \u2014 it makes the descent slow enough for a safe landing c) Yes, it flies upward d) Only over water
**Answer: b) No \u2014 it makes the descent slow enough for a safe landing**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What pushes back against the canopy as it descends?
a) The lines b) Air resistance c) The harness d) Gravity alone
**Answer: b) Air resistance**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** What do the suspension lines do?
a) Catch the wind for speed b) Spread the load from canopy to harness and hold the canopy's shape c) Measure the height d) Colour the canopy
**Answer: b) Spread the load from canopy to harness and hold the canopy's shape**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** A parachute behind a landing aircraft is used to\u2026
a) Lift the aircraft b) Brake it by dragging against the air c) Steer it left d) Refuel it
**Answer: b) Brake it by dragging against the air**
