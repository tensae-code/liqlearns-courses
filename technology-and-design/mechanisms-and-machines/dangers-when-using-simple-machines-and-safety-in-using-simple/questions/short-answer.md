# Dangers when using simple machines, and safety in using simple machines — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name two dangers of simple machines.
**Answer:** Any two from the lesson: loads slipping or dropping, bars springing back, wedges and screws biting (answers vary by example).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name two safety practices for simple machines.
**Answer:** Check the machine before loading it, and keep body parts out of the line of force (also: use each machine for its work; never trust an unsecured load).
