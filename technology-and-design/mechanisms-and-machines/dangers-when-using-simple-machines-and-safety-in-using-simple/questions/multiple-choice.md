# Dangers when using simple machines, and safety in using simple machines — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why can a simple machine be dangerous?
a) It is alive b) The arrangement that makes work easier can also hurt — loads slip, bars spring back c) It is expensive d) It is loud only
**Answer: b) The arrangement that makes work easier can also hurt — loads slip, bars spring back**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Where do the dangers in the book belong?
a) To machines nobody uses b) To the very examples the class has learned c) To engines only d) To drawings
**Answer: b) To the very examples the class has learned**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What should you do before loading a simple machine?
a) Close your eyes b) Check the machine first c) Hurry d) Add extra load
**Answer: b) Check the machine first**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Where should body parts stay?
a) In the line of force b) Out of the line of force c) Under the load d) On the rope
**Answer: b) Out of the line of force**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** When does Rwanda teach this safety lesson?
a) Before the machines b) As the closing lesson of the simple-machines unit c) In another subject d) Never
**Answer: b) As the closing lesson of the simple-machines unit**
