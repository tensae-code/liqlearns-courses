# Electric motors and the classes of machine

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Mongolia's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Electric motors and the classes of machine
Mongolia's book teaches it this way (*Design Technology 7 (Mongolia)*, printed p. 143): information, energy, working and cybernetic machines. The learner works through the named materials and steps in order, saying what each step does before doing the next one.

## Sub lesson 2 — Electric motors and the classes of machine — doing it yourself
Now the learner does the whole task unaided: information, energy, working and cybernetic machines. Working from the first sub lesson's sequence, the learner plans the make, works through it step by step, and checks the finished piece against what Mongolia's book shows.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: Electric motors and the classes of machine — information, energy, working and cybernetic machines. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
