# Families of mechanisms — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Say in one sentence what this lesson teaches.
**Answer:** Families of mechanisms — cam, crank-and-slider, Maltese cross (Geneva), ratchet and gear mechanisms — what each does and where it is used (sewing machine, engine, clock, winch, drill).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name one material, tool or step this lesson works with.
**Answer:** Any one named in the lesson text — for example, the first step of sub lesson 1: Families of mechanisms.
