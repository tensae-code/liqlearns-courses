# Lesson plan — Mechanical energy in devices and transport

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Mechanisms and Machines · **Lesson:** Mechanical energy in devices and transport
**Place in the curriculum:** Mechanisms and Machines (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Jamaica expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** mechanical energy is the energy of movement and position — stored as potential energy, moving as kinetic energy — and devices and transport machines work by changing one into the other.

## Sub lessons
1. **Work, energy and movement words** — Work, energy, acceleration, impact, velocity and mass are the working words for how things move.
2. **Kinetic and potential energy** — Moving things carry kinetic energy; things lifted or stretched store potential energy — and each can become the other.
3. **Mechanical energy in devices and transport** — Solids, liquids and gases all carry and pass on mechanical energy in the machines that move us.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of Mechanisms and Machines. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Define work, energy, velocity, acceleration, mass and impact in plain words.
2. Tell kinetic energy from potential energy and give an example of one becoming the other.
3. Name devices and transport mechanisms that use mechanical energy through solids, liquids and gases.
## What you need
A ball or small toy car, and a ramp or sloping book.
