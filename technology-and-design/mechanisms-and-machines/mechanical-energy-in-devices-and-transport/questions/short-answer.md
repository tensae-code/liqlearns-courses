# Mechanical energy in devices and transport — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, or by doing the step named.

**1. (Sub lesson 2) A stretched elastic band is let go and snaps back. Name the energy change.**
**Answer:** Potential (stored) energy in the stretched band becomes kinetic energy as it snaps back.

**2. (Sub lesson 3) Give one transport use each for a solid, a liquid and a gas.**
**Answer:** Solid: wheels/gears/pistons; liquid: hydraulic brakes or lifts; gas: steam or expanding engine gases pushing pistons (any correct examples).
