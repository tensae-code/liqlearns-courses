# Mechanical energy in devices and transport — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is energy?**
a) A kind of wheel b) The ability to do work c) A measuring tool d) A type of force only
**Answer: b) The ability to do work**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) A change in velocity over time is called…**
a) Mass b) Impact c) Acceleration d) Friction
**Answer: c) Acceleration**

**3. (Sub lesson 2) A car held at the top of a ramp stores…**
a) Kinetic energy b) Potential energy c) No energy d) Electrical energy
**Answer: b) Potential energy**

**4. (Sub lesson 2) Kinetic energy grows when a moving thing is…**
a) Slower and lighter b) Faster and heavier c) Stopped d) Lifted and held
**Answer: b) Faster and heavier**

**5. (Sub lesson 3) Hydraulic brakes and lifts pass energy through…**
a) Solids only b) Gases only c) Liquids d) Light
**Answer: c) Liquids**
