# Mechanical energy in devices and transport — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 2) Potential energy can change into kinetic energy.**
**Answer: True — A held car released down a ramp turns stored energy into movement.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Mass means how fast a thing moves.**
**Answer: False — Mass is how much matter a thing has; velocity is how fast it moves.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3) Only solids are used in transport mechanisms.**
**Answer: False — Solids, liquids and gases are all used in the make and operation of transport mechanisms.**
