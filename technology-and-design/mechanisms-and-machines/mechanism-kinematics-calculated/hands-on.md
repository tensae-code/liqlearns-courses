# Hands-on — Mechanism kinematics calculated

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: angle in radians (φ = S/R; 1 radian = 57°17′45″), angular speed ω = πn/30, and gear-train problems solved step by step (n₁/n₂ = Z₂/Z₁ through three shafts to ω₅ = 0.83 rad/s, V = 0.2 m/s). Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
