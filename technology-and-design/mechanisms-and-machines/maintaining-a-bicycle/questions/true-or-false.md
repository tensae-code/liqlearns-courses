# Maintaining a bicycle \u2014 true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A dry chain wears faster than an oiled one.
**Answer: True** \u2014 Lubrication makes the chain run smoothly and wear slowly.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Brake pads never need replacing.
**Answer: False** \u2014 Pads wear down in use and are a named spare part.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Maintenance checks are best done after something fails.
**Answer: False** \u2014 They are done regularly, before failure \u2014 that is what makes them maintenance, not repair.
