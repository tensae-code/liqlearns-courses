# Maintaining a bicycle \u2014 short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** List the maintenance operations in the round.
**Answer:** Lubricate the chain; check the brakes; check the tyres; check the cables.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the spare parts and the consumable in the kit.
**Answer:** Spare parts: brake pads, a tyre, cables. Consumable: oil.
