# Maintaining a bicycle \u2014 multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which operation serves the chain?
a) Inflating it b) Lubricating it with oil c) Replacing it weekly d) Painting it
**Answer: b) Lubricating it with oil**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which parts are checked in the maintenance round?
a) Saddle and bell only b) Brakes, tyres and cables c) Frame colour d) The rider's shoes
**Answer: b) Brakes, tyres and cables**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Which is in the maintainer's spare-parts kit?
a) Brake pads b) A new frame c) A second bicycle d) Handlebar tape only
**Answer: a) Brake pads**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Cables are renewed when they\u2026
a) Change colour b) Stretch and fray c) Get wet once d) Are a year old exactly
**Answer: b) Stretch and fray**

**5. (Sub lesson 1)** Why do the checks regularly instead of after failure?
a) Checks are decoration b) Small checks prevent failures on the road c) Parts enjoy attention d) It uses more oil
**Answer: b) Small checks prevent failures on the road**
