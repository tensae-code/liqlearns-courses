# Hands-on \u2014 Maintaining a bicycle

Real practice \u2014 this subject is learned by doing.

## Maintenance round
On a real bicycle (with permission), do the round: oil the chain, test the brakes, inspect tyres and cables. Record each item: good, adjusted, or needs a part.

## Kit list
Write the maintainer's kit for a household bicycle: the spare parts and consumables, where each is used, and the sign that says it is needed.
