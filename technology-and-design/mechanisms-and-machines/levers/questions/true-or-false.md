# Levers — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The effort is where the user pushes or pulls the lever.
**Answer: True** — Effort, fulcrum and load are the lever's three parts — effort is the push or pull.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** All levers have the fulcrum in the middle.
**Answer: False** — Levers are classed by which part is in the middle — fulcrum, load or effort.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Classing a lever is a reading skill: find the middle part.
**Answer: True** — The class follows from the arrangement of effort, fulcrum and load.
