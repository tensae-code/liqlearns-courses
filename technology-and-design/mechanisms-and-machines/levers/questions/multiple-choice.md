# Levers — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What are the three parts of a lever?
a) Handle, blade, edge b) Effort, fulcrum/pivot, load c) Rope, wheel, weight d) Base, arm, motor
**Answer: b) Effort, fulcrum/pivot, load**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the fulcrum?
a) The thing moved b) The point the lever turns on c) Where you push d) The heaviest part
**Answer: b) The point the lever turns on**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the load?
a) The pivot b) The push c) The thing moved d) The bar itself
**Answer: c) The thing moved**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** How are levers classed?
a) By colour b) By which of the three parts sits in the middle c) By size d) By price
**Answer: b) By which of the three parts sits in the middle**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** What does finding the middle part give you?
a) The lever's class b) Its price c) Its age d) Its owner
**Answer: a) The lever's class**
