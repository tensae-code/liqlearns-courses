# Hands-on — The landing-gear system

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: types and main assemblies, shock absorbers, retraction/extension (electric and hydraulic) and emergency extension, indication/warning systems, steering, shimmy dampers, landing-gear maintenance. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
