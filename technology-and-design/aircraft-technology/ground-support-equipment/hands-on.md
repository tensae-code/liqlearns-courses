# Hands-on — Ground support equipment

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: air compressors, air-conditioning/heating units, ground power units, hydraulic/pneumatic servicing units, pre-oiling and lubrication equipment, nitrogen/oxygen servicing, towing, fuel servicing, jacks and lifting, mooring the aircraft and securing control surfaces. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
