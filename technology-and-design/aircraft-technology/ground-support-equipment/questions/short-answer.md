# Ground support equipment — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Say in one sentence what this lesson teaches.
**Answer:** Air compressors, air-conditioning/heating units, ground power units, hydraulic/pneumatic servicing units, pre-oiling and lubrication equipment, nitrogen/oxygen servicing, towing, fuel servicing, jacks and lifting, mooring the aircraft and securing control surfaces.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name one material, tool or step this lesson works with.
**Answer:** Any one named in the lesson text — for example, the first step of sub lesson 1: Ground support equipment.
