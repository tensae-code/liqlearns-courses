# Hands-on — Fasteners

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: standardisation and specifications, rivets, thread types, bolts and screws, half-turn fasteners, studs, nuts, washers, pins, installing and removing fasteners, locking methods, wire ropes/cables. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
