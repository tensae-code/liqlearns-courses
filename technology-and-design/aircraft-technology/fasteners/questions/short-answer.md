# Fasteners — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Say in one sentence what this lesson teaches.
**Answer:** Standardisation and specifications, rivets, thread types, bolts and screws, half-turn fasteners, studs, nuts, washers, pins, installing and removing fasteners, locking methods, wire ropes/cables.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name one material, tool or step this lesson works with.
**Answer:** Any one named in the lesson text — for example, the first step of sub lesson 1: Fasteners.
