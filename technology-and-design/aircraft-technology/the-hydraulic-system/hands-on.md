# Hands-on — The hydraulic system

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: hydraulic terms and Pascal's law, mechanical advantage, hydraulic fluids and contamination, the basic hydraulic circuit (reservoirs, pumps, valves, accumulators, actuators, motors), open- and closed-centre systems, emergency hydraulic systems. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
