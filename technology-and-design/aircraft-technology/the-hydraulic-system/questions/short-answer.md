# The hydraulic system — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Say in one sentence what this lesson teaches.
**Answer:** Hydraulic terms and Pascal's law, mechanical advantage, hydraulic fluids and contamination, the basic hydraulic circuit (reservoirs, pumps, valves, accumulators, actuators, motors), open- and closed-centre systems, emergency hydraulic systems.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name one material, tool or step this lesson works with.
**Answer:** Any one named in the lesson text — for example, the first step of sub lesson 1: The hydraulic system.
