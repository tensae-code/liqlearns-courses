# Hands-on — Aerodynamics of conventional aircraft

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: air and the standard atmosphere, flow laws, wing theory (airfoil geometry and standardisation, boundary layer, pressure distribution, aerodynamic forces and coefficients, drag), stall, control surfaces, stability, high-lift devices, the wind tunnel. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
