# Aerodynamics of conventional aircraft — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Say in one sentence what this lesson teaches.
**Answer:** Air and the standard atmosphere, flow laws, wing theory (airfoil geometry and standardisation, boundary layer, pressure distribution, aerodynamic forces and coefficients, drag), stall, control surfaces, stability, high-lift devices, the wind tunnel.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name one material, tool or step this lesson works with.
**Answer:** Any one named in the lesson text — for example, the first step of sub lesson 1: Aerodynamics of conventional aircraft.
