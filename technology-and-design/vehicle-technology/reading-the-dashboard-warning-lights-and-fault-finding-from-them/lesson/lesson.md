# Reading the dashboard warning lights and fault-finding from them

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Iran's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — What the lights say
Warning lights are the car's systems reporting on themselves: oil pressure, coolant temperature, battery charging, brakes and more, each with its symbol and colour. Colour carries urgency — red commands stopping now; amber commands checking soon. Iran's Grade 9 Car module teaches the lights as a language to be read, not decorations to be ignored.

## Sub lesson 2 — Fault-finding from the lights
A lit light starts the finding: name the system, check its simplest causes first (the fluid lessons give most of them), and answer at the light's urgency. A light ignored becomes a breakdown — or worse. The module joins the two skills in one lesson: reading the lights, and fault-finding from them.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: the dashboard speaks in warning lights: each light names a system in trouble, and fault-finding begins by reading the light truly and answering at its level of urgency. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
