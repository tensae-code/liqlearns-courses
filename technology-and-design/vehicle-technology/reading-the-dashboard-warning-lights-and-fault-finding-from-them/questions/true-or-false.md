# Reading the dashboard warning lights and fault-finding from them — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Warning light is a dashboard light by which a car system reports trouble.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Fault-finding has nothing to do with this lesson.
**Answer: False** — It is part of the lesson: tracing a fault from its symptom to its cause in ordered steps.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Urgency is how quickly a warning must be answered, carried by its colour.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.
