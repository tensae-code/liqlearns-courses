# Reading the dashboard warning lights and fault-finding from them — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is warning light?
a) a dashboard light by which a car system reports trouble b) Only a price c) Only decoration d) Nothing to do with this lesson
**Answer: a) a dashboard light by which a car system reports trouble**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is fault-finding?
a) Only history b) tracing a fault from its symptom to its cause in ordered steps c) Only storage d) A kind of glue
**Answer: b) tracing a fault from its symptom to its cause in ordered steps**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is urgency?
a) Only old tools b) Only painting c) how quickly a warning must be answered, carried by its colour d) Only a price
**Answer: c) how quickly a warning must be answered, carried by its colour**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson teach, in one line?
a) Only decoration b) Nothing to do with this lesson c) Only history d) the dashboard speaks in warning lights: each light names a system in trouble, and fault-finding begins by reading the light truly and answering at its level of urgency.
**Answer: d) the dashboard speaks in warning lights: each light names a system in trouble, and fault-finding begins by reading the light truly and answering at its level of urgency.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** How does Iran's book teach this?
a) As theory only, never practised b) Only in a glossary c) Only as an exam d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Persian
**Answer: d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Persian**
