# Hands-on — The motor car

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## Construction walk
Walk around a car (or its diagram) and list its main construction parts in order, front to back, with one line on each part's job.

## Maintenance plan
Write a correct-maintenance plan for a family car: what is checked, what is serviced, and how often — then add the road-safety rule each check protects.
