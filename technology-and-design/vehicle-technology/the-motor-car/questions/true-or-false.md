# The motor car — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A car can run properly for long on luck alone.
**Answer: False** — It must be understood — construction first — and correctly maintained.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Maintenance methods are part of the motor-car lesson.
**Answer: True** — Correct maintenance follows construction in the part.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Road-traffic safety rules concern all road users, not drivers alone.
**Answer: True** — The part closes with rules for all road users.
