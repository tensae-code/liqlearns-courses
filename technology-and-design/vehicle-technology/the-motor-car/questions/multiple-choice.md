# The motor car — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What must a car be, to run properly?
a) Understood — its construction known b) Expensive c) New d) Painted
**Answer: a) Understood — its construction known**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What does construction knowledge give?
a) Speed b) Early hearing of trouble and maintenance on knowledge, not luck c) Louder music d) Nothing
**Answer: b) Early hearing of trouble and maintenance on knowledge, not luck**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What keeps the car running properly?
a) The methods of correct maintenance b) Washing only c) Driving faster d) Ignoring noises
**Answer: a) The methods of correct maintenance**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** For whom are the closing safety rules?
a) For all road users b) Only drivers c) Only pedestrians d) Only police
**Answer: a) For all road users**

**5. (Sub lesson 1)** The part's order is…
a) Safety, then selling b) Maintenance only c) Construction, then maintenance, then road safety d) No order
**Answer: c) Construction, then maintenance, then road safety**
