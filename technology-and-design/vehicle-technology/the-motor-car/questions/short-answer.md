# The motor car — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why must the car's construction be understood?
**Answer:** So it runs properly — trouble is heard early and maintenance is done on knowledge, not luck.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What two things follow construction in the part?
**Answer:** The methods of correct maintenance, and road-traffic safety rules for all road users.
