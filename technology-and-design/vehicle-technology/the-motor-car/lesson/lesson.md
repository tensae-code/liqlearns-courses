# The motor car

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Moldova's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Construction — understanding the car
Grade 9's motor-car part begins with understanding: to run properly, a car must be understood — its construction, how its parts are built and joined into a working machine. The driver-mechanic who knows the construction hears trouble early and maintains on knowledge, not on luck.

## Sub lesson 2 — Correct maintenance and road safety
Understanding becomes care: the methods of correct maintenance that keep the car running properly. And the part closes outward — road-traffic safety rules for all road users, because the maintained car shares the road with everyone.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: a car runs properly only when it is understood: its construction, and the methods of correct maintenance — and around the car stand the road-traffic safety rules for all road users. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
