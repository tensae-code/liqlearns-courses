# Lesson plan — The motor car

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Vehicle Technology · **Lesson:** The motor car
**Place in the curriculum:** Vehicle Technology (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Moldova expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** a car runs properly only when it is understood: its construction, and the methods of correct maintenance — and around the car stand the road-traffic safety rules for all road users.
**This lesson's own level:** stocked from Moldova's *Educație tehnologică* (Technological Education) textbooks — see lesson/sources.md. Moldova counts in the acceptance count (student books, not evidence-only).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **Construction — understanding the car** — Grade 9's motor-car part begins with understanding: to run properly, a car must be understood — its construction, how its parts are built and joined into a working machine.
2. **Correct maintenance and road safety** — Understanding becomes care: the methods of correct maintenance that keep the car running properly.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of Vehicle Technology. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Describe the motor car's construction in outline.
2. State the methods of correct maintenance and the road-safety frame.
3. Explain how Moldova's book teaches it.
## What you need
A car to study (or a labelled construction diagram), and the maintenance schedule of a real car if available.
