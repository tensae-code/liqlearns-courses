# Hands-on — Fuel supply systems — laboratory practice

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: electronically controlled carburettor, air intake/measurement, and the Mono-Jetronic, Motronic and Mono-Motronic electronic injection systems — building their circuits, measuring, diagnosing and repairing faults. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
