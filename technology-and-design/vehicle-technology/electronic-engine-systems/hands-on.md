# Hands-on — Electronic engine systems

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: the three subsystems of electronic fuel injection (fuel supply; air intake and metering; electronic control — sensors, ECU, actuators) and electronic ignition (contact-breaker problems, Hall generator, transistor ignition circuits). Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
