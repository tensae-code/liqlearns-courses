# Hands-on — Measuring instruments

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: how instruments are classified, accuracy class of an instrument, scale resolution and measurement error, protecting instruments, analogue vs digital multimeters and how each works, temperature-measuring instruments. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
