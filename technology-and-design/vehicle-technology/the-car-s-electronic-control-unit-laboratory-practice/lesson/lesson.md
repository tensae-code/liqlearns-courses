# The car's electronic control unit — laboratory practice

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Greece's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — The car's electronic control unit — laboratory practice
Greece's book teaches it this way: recognising the vehicle microcomputer, measuring the ECU, decoding and clearing ECM and PCM (injection/automatic gearbox) faults, interconnecting ECUs with CAN-bus and diagnosing bus faults (*Vehicle Control Lab*, printed p. 76). The learner works through the named materials and steps in order, saying what each step does before doing the next one.

## Sub lesson 2 — The car's electronic control unit — laboratory practice — doing it yourself
Now the learner does the whole task unaided: recognising the vehicle microcomputer, measuring the ECU, decoding and clearing ECM and PCM (injection/automatic gearbox) faults, interconnecting ECUs with CAN-bus and diagnosing bus faults. Working from the first sub lesson's sequence, the learner plans the make, works through it step by step, and checks the finished piece against what Greece's book shows.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: Recognising the vehicle microcomputer, measuring the ECU, decoding and clearing ECM and PCM (injection/automatic gearbox) faults, interconnecting ECUs with CAN-bus and diagnosing bus faults. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
