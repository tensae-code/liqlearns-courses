# Hands-on — The car's electronic control unit — laboratory practice

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: recognising the vehicle microcomputer, measuring the ECU, decoding and clearing ECM and PCM (injection/automatic gearbox) faults, interconnecting ECUs with CAN-bus and diagnosing bus faults. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
