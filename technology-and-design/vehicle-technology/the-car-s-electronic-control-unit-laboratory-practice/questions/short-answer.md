# The car's electronic control unit — laboratory practice — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Say in one sentence what this lesson teaches.
**Answer:** Recognising the vehicle microcomputer, measuring the ECU, decoding and clearing ECM and PCM (injection/automatic gearbox) faults, interconnecting ECUs with CAN-bus and diagnosing bus faults.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name one material, tool or step this lesson works with.
**Answer:** Any one named in the lesson text — for example, the first step of sub lesson 1: The car's electronic control unit — laboratory practice.
