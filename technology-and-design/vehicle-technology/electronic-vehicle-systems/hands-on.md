# Hands-on — Electronic vehicle systems

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: ABS operation (friction, wheel slip, braking distance), immobiliser and its hidden key code, electronically controlled air conditioning (compressor, condenser, expansion valve, evaporator — the refrigeration cycle), electronically controlled gearboxes and clutch types. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
