# Electronic vehicle systems — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Say in one sentence what this lesson teaches.
**Answer:** ABS operation (friction, wheel slip, braking distance), immobiliser and its hidden key code, electronically controlled air conditioning (compressor, condenser, expansion valve, evaporator — the refrigeration cycle), electronically controlled gearboxes and clutch types.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name one material, tool or step this lesson works with.
**Answer:** Any one named in the lesson text — for example, the first step of sub lesson 1: Electronic vehicle systems.
