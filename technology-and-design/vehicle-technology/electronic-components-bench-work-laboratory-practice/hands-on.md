# Hands-on — Electronic components bench work — laboratory practice

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: designing and building rectifier circuits with diodes, voltage stabilisers, switch circuits with BJT, MOSFET and IGBT, power control with a thyristor, the op-amp as comparator, photoresistor/photodiode/LED and seven-segment displays, phototransistors and optocouplers. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
