# Hands-on — Knowing the workshop's measuring instruments — laboratory practice

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: recognising and using the exhaust-gas analyser, infrared thermometer, battery tester, lambda meter, pressure gauge, digital multimeter, fault analyser and oscilloscope (setting sensitivity/timebase, measuring battery voltage from the waveform, interference effects), and interpreting a measurement. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
