# Checking tyres and the periodic technical inspection — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In your own words, what is tyre pressure?
**Answer:** The air pressure inside a tyre, set to the car's stated figure.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In your own words, what is technical inspection?
**Answer:** The periodic official examination of a car's roadworthiness.
