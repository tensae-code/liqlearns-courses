# Checking tyres and the periodic technical inspection — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Tyre pressure is the air pressure inside a tyre, set to the car's stated figure.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Tread depth has nothing to do with this lesson.
**Answer: False** — It is part of the lesson: the depth of the tyre's tread pattern, which clears water for wet grip.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Technical inspection is the periodic official examination of a car's roadworthiness.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.
