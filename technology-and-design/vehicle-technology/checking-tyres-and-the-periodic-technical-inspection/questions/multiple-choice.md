# Checking tyres and the periodic technical inspection — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is tyre pressure?
a) the air pressure inside a tyre, set to the car's stated figure b) Only a price c) Only decoration d) Nothing to do with this lesson
**Answer: a) the air pressure inside a tyre, set to the car's stated figure**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is tread depth?
a) Only history b) the depth of the tyre's tread pattern, which clears water for wet grip c) Only storage d) A kind of glue
**Answer: b) the depth of the tyre's tread pattern, which clears water for wet grip**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is technical inspection?
a) Only old tools b) Only painting c) the periodic official examination of a car's roadworthiness d) Only a price
**Answer: c) the periodic official examination of a car's roadworthiness**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson teach, in one line?
a) Only decoration b) Nothing to do with this lesson c) Only history d) tyres are checked by pressure and tread: correct pressure keeps grip and life, tread depth keeps wet grip, snow chains answer winter — and the whole car faces its periodic technical inspection.
**Answer: d) tyres are checked by pressure and tread: correct pressure keeps grip and life, tread depth keeps wet grip, snow chains answer winter — and the whole car faces its periodic technical inspection.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** How does Iran's book teach this?
a) As theory only, never practised b) Only in a glossary c) Only as an exam d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Persian
**Answer: d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Persian**
