# Checking tyres and the periodic technical inspection

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Iran's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Pressure and tread
Tyre pressure is set to the car's stated figure: under-inflated tyres wear their edges, waste fuel and run hot; over-inflated tyres wear their centres and grip less. Tread depth is the wet-weather grip — worn tread cannot clear water, so depth is measured and judged against its limit. Iran's Grade 9 Car module teaches checking and adjusting pressure and judging tread together, as the tyre's two numbers.

## Sub lesson 2 — Snow chains and the inspection
Where snow rules, chains are fitted over the driving tyres before the snow stops the car — fitted in practice order, at the roadside's safety. Over the whole car stands the periodic technical inspection: the car's roadworthiness examined on its cycle, the owner's checks and the state's inspection meeting. The module closes its owner-care sequence with chains and the inspection.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: tyres are checked by pressure and tread: correct pressure keeps grip and life, tread depth keeps wet grip, snow chains answer winter — and the whole car faces its periodic technical inspection. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
