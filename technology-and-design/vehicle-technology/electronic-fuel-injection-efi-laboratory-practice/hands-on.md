# Hands-on — Electronic fuel injection EFI — laboratory practice

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: relieving fuel pressure and leak checks, fuel-pressure testing and regulator replacement, fuel-pump removal/testing, cold-start injector circuit, injector voltage and sprayed-volume checks, inspection-form completion. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
