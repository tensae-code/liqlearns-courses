# Hands-on — What diagnosis is

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: measurement as comparison against a reference, the stages of diagnosis, the fault-finding flow chart, fault history, simulating and confirming a fault, self-diagnosis. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
