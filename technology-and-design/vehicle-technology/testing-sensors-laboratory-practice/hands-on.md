# Hands-on — Testing sensors — laboratory practice

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: Hall-effect and magnetic-resistance speed sensors, potentiometer rotation-angle sensor, temperature sensor, piezoresistive vacuum sensor, oxygen/lambda sensor, knock sensor. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
