# Electronic components for diagnosis

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Greece's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Electronic components for diagnosis
Greece's book teaches it this way: diodes (rectifier, Zener, LED, photodiode), transistors and phototransistors/optocouplers, integrated circuits (*Checks & Diagnoses*, printed p. 53). The learner works through the named materials and steps in order, saying what each step does before doing the next one.

## Sub lesson 2 — Electronic components for diagnosis — doing it yourself
Now the learner does the whole task unaided: diodes (rectifier, Zener, LED, photodiode), transistors and phototransistors/optocouplers, integrated circuits. Working from the first sub lesson's sequence, the learner plans the make, works through it step by step, and checks the finished piece against what Greece's book shows.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: Diodes (rectifier, Zener, LED, photodiode), transistors and phototransistors/optocouplers, integrated circuits. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.

## Do It Yourself
1. Do the Do it first task again, carefully this time, using everything the lesson taught.
2. Check your work against the lesson's own checks — fix one thing you find.
3. Keep your work: it is practice evidence, and the next lesson builds on it.
