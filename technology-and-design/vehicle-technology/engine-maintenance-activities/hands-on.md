# Hands-on — Engine maintenance activities

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## Work the topic
Carry out the topic's core task on a fresh example: the petrol engine's parts (cylinder block), the ignition system, spark-plug gap (0.60 mm) and the magneto ignition system. Work in order, naming each part or step as you go.

## Check it
Check your finished work against the lesson's own rules. Find one thing that is wrong or weak, fix it, and say what you changed and why.
