# Hands-on — Sensors in the automobile

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: the sensor's role, active contact vs passive non-contact sensors, environmental influences, speed/revolution sensors, position and angle sensors (potentiometer, Hall). Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
