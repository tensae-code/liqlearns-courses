# Testing electronic engine systems — laboratory practice

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Greece's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Testing electronic engine systems — laboratory practice
Greece's book teaches it this way: conventional ignition, inductive and capacitive ignition, distributorless integrated ignition, and the fuel-supply system (*Checks & Diagnoses Lab Guide*, printed p. 93). The learner works through the named materials and steps in order, saying what each step does before doing the next one.

## Sub lesson 2 — Testing electronic engine systems — laboratory practice — doing it yourself
Now the learner does the whole task unaided: conventional ignition, inductive and capacitive ignition, distributorless integrated ignition, and the fuel-supply system. Working from the first sub lesson's sequence, the learner plans the make, works through it step by step, and checks the finished piece against what Greece's book shows.
