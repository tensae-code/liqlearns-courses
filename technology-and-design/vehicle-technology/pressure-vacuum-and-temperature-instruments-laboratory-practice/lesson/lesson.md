# Pressure, vacuum and temperature instruments — laboratory practice

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Greece's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Pressure, vacuum and temperature instruments — laboratory practice
Greece's book teaches it this way: pressure units and conversions, the compression test and interpreting the compression ratio, checking a catalyst by inlet/outlet temperature (*Checks & Diagnoses Lab Guide*, printed p. 15). The learner works through the named materials and steps in order, saying what each step does before doing the next one.

## Sub lesson 2 — Pressure, vacuum and temperature instruments — laboratory practice — doing it yourself
Now the learner does the whole task unaided: pressure units and conversions, the compression test and interpreting the compression ratio, checking a catalyst by inlet/outlet temperature. Working from the first sub lesson's sequence, the learner plans the make, works through it step by step, and checks the finished piece against what Greece's book shows.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: Pressure units and conversions, the compression test and interpreting the compression ratio, checking a catalyst by inlet/outlet temperature. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
