# Using the owner's manual

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Iran's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — The manual as authority
Every car model differs in its details, so the owner's manual — the book written for that exact car — is the authority for its care. Iran's Grade 9 Car module teaches the habit itself: find the check in the manual, follow its levels, grades and intervals, rather than guessing from another car's practice.

## Sub lesson 2 — The five fluid checks
The periodic checks are five fluids, each with its reservoir, its level marks and its danger if neglected: coolant level (overheating), brake fluid (braking itself), engine oil (the engine's life), battery fluid (starting), and windscreen-washer fluid (seeing the road). The module teaches the checks as a periodic set — done together, on the manual's cycle, before failure announces them.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: the owner's manual is the car's own book: it fixes the periodic fluid checks — coolant, brake fluid, engine oil, battery fluid and windscreen-washer fluid — and how each is done. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
