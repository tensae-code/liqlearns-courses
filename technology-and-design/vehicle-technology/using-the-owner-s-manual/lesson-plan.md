# Lesson plan — Using the owner's manual

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Vehicle Technology · **Lesson:** Using the owner's manual
**Place in the curriculum:** Vehicle Technology (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Iran expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the owner's manual is the car's own book: it fixes the periodic fluid checks — coolant, brake fluid, engine oil, battery fluid and windscreen-washer fluid — and how each is done.
**This lesson's own level:** stocked from Iran's *Work & Technology, Grade 9* (student book) — see lesson/sources.md. Iran counts in the acceptance count (student books, not evidence-only).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **The manual as authority** — Every car model differs in its details, so the owner's manual — the book written for that exact car — is the authority for its care.
2. **The five fluid checks** — The periodic checks are five fluids, each with its reservoir, its level marks and its danger if neglected: coolant level (overheating), brake fluid (braking itself), engine oil (the engine's life), battery fluid (starting), and windscreen-washer fluid (seeing the road).

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of Vehicle Technology. Recommended, never forced.

1. Explain using the owner's manual and its main parts, in their own words.
2. Carry out the lesson's central task unaided.
3. Explain how Iran's book teaches it.
## What you need
