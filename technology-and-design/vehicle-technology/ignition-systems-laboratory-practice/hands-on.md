# Hands-on — Ignition systems — laboratory practice

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: checking conventional and electronic ignition, inductive and capacitive ignition, building a distributorless integrated-ignition circuit, measuring and diagnosing ignition faults. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
