# Hands-on — Self-diagnosis systems

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: how they differ from traditional diagnosis, the car's microcomputer as their base, maker-specific vs general diagnostic devices, OBD emissions self-diagnosis, the diagnostic chart. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
