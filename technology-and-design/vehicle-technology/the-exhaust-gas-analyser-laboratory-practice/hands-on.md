# Hands-on — The exhaust-gas analyser — laboratory practice

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: exhaust analysis and fault diagnosis at idle and 2,500 rpm, emission limits, cylinder-balance (power-balance) test from HC, measuring catalyst efficiency from HC in/out. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
