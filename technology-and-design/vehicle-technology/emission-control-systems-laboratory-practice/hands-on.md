# Hands-on — Emission-control systems — laboratory practice

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: fault diagnosis by simulation, preparing a vehicle for the exhaust-emission test card, evaporative-emission system maintenance, EGR and PCV system checks, catalyst types/efficiency/checks and catalyst replacement with protective measures. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
