# The car and its systems — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is chassis?
a) the car's frame, carrying the body and every system b) Only a price c) Only decoration d) Nothing to do with this lesson
**Answer: a) the car's frame, carrying the body and every system**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is transmission?
a) Only history b) the system carrying the power source's force to the wheels c) Only storage d) A kind of glue
**Answer: b) the system carrying the power source's force to the wheels**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is suspension?
a) Only old tools b) Only painting c) the system cushioning the ride and keeping wheels on the road d) Only a price
**Answer: c) the system cushioning the ride and keeping wheels on the road**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson teach, in one line?
a) Only decoration b) Nothing to do with this lesson c) Only history d) the car is a system of systems: body and chassis carry all, and power source, transmission, steering, brakes, suspension, electrical and safety systems each do one job in the whole.
**Answer: d) the car is a system of systems: body and chassis carry all, and power source, transmission, steering, brakes, suspension, electrical and safety systems each do one job in the whole.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** How does Iran's book teach this?
a) As theory only, never practised b) Only in a glossary c) Only as an exam d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Persian
**Answer: d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Persian**
