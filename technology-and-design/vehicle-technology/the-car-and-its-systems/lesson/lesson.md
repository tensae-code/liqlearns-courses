# The car and its systems

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Iran's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Body, chassis and power
The car's structure is its body on its chassis — the frame that carries every other system. Its power source, the engine or motor, makes the turning force; the transmission carries that force to the wheels, changing it on the way. Iran's Grade 9 Car module opens with a diagram of the whole car and its systems, so every later check belongs to a named system.

## Sub lesson 2 — Control, comfort and safety systems
Steering directs the wheels; brakes stop them; suspension cushions the ride and keeps the wheels on the road; the electrical system starts the engine, lights the way and runs the instruments; safety systems protect the people inside. A fault is found by asking which system it lives in — the module's opening diagram is the map for the owner's-manual checks that follow.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: the car is a system of systems: body and chassis carry all, and power source, transmission, steering, brakes, suspension, electrical and safety systems each do one job in the whole. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
