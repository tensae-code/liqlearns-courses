# Lesson plan — The car and its systems

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Vehicle Technology · **Lesson:** The car and its systems
**Place in the curriculum:** Vehicle Technology (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Iran expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the car is a system of systems: body and chassis carry all, and power source, transmission, steering, brakes, suspension, electrical and safety systems each do one job in the whole.
**This lesson's own level:** stocked from Iran's *Work & Technology, Grade 9* (student book) — see lesson/sources.md. Iran counts in the acceptance count (student books, not evidence-only).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **Body, chassis and power** — The car's structure is its body on its chassis — the frame that carries every other system.
2. **Control, comfort and safety systems** — Steering directs the wheels; brakes stop them; suspension cushions the ride and keeps the wheels on the road; the electrical system starts the engine, lights the way and runs the instruments; safety systems protect the people inside.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
