# Actuators in the automobile — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Say in one sentence what this lesson teaches.
**Answer:** Solenoid (electromagnetic) valves and duty-cycle fuel metering, fuel cut-off safety valves, ABS valves, relays with one or two coils controlling high-power circuits.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name one material, tool or step this lesson works with.
**Answer:** Any one named in the lesson text — for example, the first step of sub lesson 1: Actuators in the automobile.
