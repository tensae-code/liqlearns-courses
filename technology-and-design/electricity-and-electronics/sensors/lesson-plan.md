# Lesson plan — Sensors

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Electricity and Electronics · **Lesson:** Sensors
**Place in the curriculum:** Electricity and Electronics (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Morocco expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** a sensor is the acquire function made hardware: each sensor type measures one thing — temperature, pressure, light, presence — by a physical principle that turns the measured fact into an electrical signal.

## Sub lessons
1. **The acquire function — sensor types and what each measures** — Morocco's sensors lesson catalogues the weather station: a thermistor measures temperature, a pressostat watches pressure, other sensors take wind, rain and light. The rule for reading any sensor: name what it measures (its input quantity), what it delivers (its output signal), and where it sits in the chain — always at acquire.
2. **What each sensor measures and its principle** — Each sensor rests on a physical effect. The thermistor: resistance falls as temperature rises, so a circuit converts resistance into a temperature reading. The pressostat: pressure moves a membrane against a threshold and delivers a logic (TOR) contact. Light, level, position and presence sensors each pair one quantity with one effect in the same way.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. For any sensor, name what it measures, what it delivers, and its place in the chain.
2. Explain the thermistor's and the pressostat's principles.
3. Say why a blind spot at acquire cannot be repaired by processing.
