**Acceptance count: taught in 1 country (Mongolia) — plus teacher-guide evidence only (Morocco), named here, counted nowhere.**

Morocco's own way: Morocco teaches Technologie in collège as a systems course — a technical object is read through its functions (power supply, detection, timing, switching, signalling, transmission), modelled with SysML against a functional specification (Cdcf), and the course closes with enterprise creation and a full design-and-build project. That systems-first, function-by-function way is carried in this lesson exactly as the guides teach it.

Mongolia's own way: Mongolia teaches this inside Visual Art & Technology (Дүрслэх урлаг, технологи) — the types (touch, temperature, light, air pressure, humidity, magnetic, sound) and how they work — photodiode, photoresistor (LDR), ultrasonic sensor (20–20,000 Hz) and PIR motion sensor. The Mongolian classroom's own examples named in the provenance rows above are part of the teaching, not decoration.
