# Sensors — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Each sensor measures one quantity by one physical principle.
**Answer: True** — That pairing — quantity plus principle — is how any sensor is read and chosen.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A pressostat gives a continuous analogue reading of pressure.
**Answer: False** — It answers at a set pressure with a logic (TOR) contact: threshold reached or not.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** A system knows more than its sensors tell it.
**Answer: False** — Acquisition bounds knowledge: what is not sensed is not known to the system.
