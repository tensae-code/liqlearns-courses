# Sensors — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A sensor's place in the information chain is…
a) acquire — the first function b) communicate — the last c) outside the chain d) in the energy chain only
**Answer: a) acquire — the first function**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A thermistor measures temperature by…
a) its resistance changing with temperature b) expanding like a liquid only c) counting heartbeats d) changing colour permanently
**Answer: a) its resistance changing with temperature**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** A pressostat watches…
a) pressure, delivering a logic contact at a threshold b) temperature continuously c) light levels d) wind direction
**Answer: a) pressure, delivering a logic contact at a threshold**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A weather station's sensors feed…
a) the acquire function of its information chain b) the display only, directly c) nothing — they are decorative d) the energy chain's motor
**Answer: a) the acquire function of its information chain**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** If a system's obstacle sensor fails, processing…
a) cannot repair the blind spot — the system no longer knows b) invents the missing information c) works better d) repairs the sensor itself
**Answer: a) cannot repair the blind spot — the system no longer knows**
