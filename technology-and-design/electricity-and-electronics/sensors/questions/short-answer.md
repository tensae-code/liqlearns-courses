# Sensors — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Read a thermistor as a sensor: quantity, principle, signal.
**Answer:** Quantity: temperature. Principle: its electrical resistance falls as temperature rises. Signal: an analogue electrical variation a circuit converts into a temperature reading.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the three acquisitions a garage-door system needs and why.
**Answer:** Door position (to know open/closed/travelling), obstacle presence (to stop safely), user command (to start the cycle) — every later decision reads from these.
