# Hands-on — Microcomputers and microprocessors

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: the structure (CPU, RAM, ROM, input and output devices), Intel's 4004 of 1971, and where programs are stored. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
