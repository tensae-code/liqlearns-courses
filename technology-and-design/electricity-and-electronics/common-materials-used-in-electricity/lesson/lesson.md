# Common materials used in electricity

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Rwanda's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Conductors — materials that carry current
On the same page as its tools, Rwanda's book names the common materials used in electricity. First the conductors: the materials current travels through — the wires themselves and the switch contacts that complete a circuit when they meet.

## Sub lesson 2 — Insulators — materials that block current
Second the insulators: materials that stop current where it must not go. The book's own example is the cable's insulation, which wraps the conductor so currents stay in their paths — without it, touching conductors short-circuit and the work becomes danger.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: electrical materials divide by what they do to current: conductors carry it — wires, switch contacts — and insulators block it, the cable's insulation standing between the current and a short circuit. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
