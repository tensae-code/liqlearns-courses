# Lesson plan — Common materials used in electricity

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Electricity and Electronics · **Lesson:** Common materials used in electricity
**Place in the curriculum:** Electricity and Electronics (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Rwanda expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** electrical materials divide by what they do to current: conductors carry it — wires, switch contacts — and insulators block it, the cable's insulation standing between the current and a short circuit.
**This lesson's own level:** stocked from Rwanda's *Science and Elementary Technology* pupil's books (Rwanda Education Board) — see lesson/sources.md. Rwanda counts in the acceptance count (student books, not evidence-only).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **Conductors — materials that carry current** — On the same page as its tools, Rwanda's book names the common materials used in electricity.
2. **Insulators — materials that block current** — Second the insulators: materials that stop current where it must not go.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
