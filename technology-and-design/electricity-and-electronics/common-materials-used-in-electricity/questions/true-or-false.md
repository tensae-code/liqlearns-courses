# Common materials used in electricity — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Switch contacts are conductors.
**Answer: True** — The book names wires and switch contacts as its conductor examples.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Insulation makes a cable conduct better.
**Answer: False** — Insulation blocks current — it keeps currents in their paths and prevents short-circuiting.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Conductors and insulators are both common materials used in electricity.
**Answer: True** — The lesson names both families: those that carry current and those that block it.
