# Common materials used in electricity — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the two families of electrical materials.
**Answer:** Conductors, which carry current, and insulators, which block it.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why does a cable need insulation?
**Answer:** So its conductor's current stays in its path and cannot touch other conductors and short-circuit.
