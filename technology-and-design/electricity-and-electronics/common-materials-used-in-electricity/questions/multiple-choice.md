# Common materials used in electricity — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What do conductors do?
a) Block current b) Carry current c) Store water d) Make light
**Answer: b) Carry current**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which are Rwanda's conductor examples?
a) Wires and switch contacts b) Rubber and plastic c) Wood and paper d) Glass and air
**Answer: a) Wires and switch contacts**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What do insulators do?
a) Carry current faster b) Stop current where it must not go c) Make current d) Store current
**Answer: b) Stop current where it must not go**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** What is the book's insulator example?
a) The switch contact b) The cable's insulation c) The bulb d) The battery
**Answer: b) The cable's insulation**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** What happens when bare conductors touch?
a) Nothing b) A short circuit c) Better light d) Slower current
**Answer: b) A short circuit**
