# Lesson plan — Electrical measurement

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Electricity and Electronics · **Lesson:** Electrical measurement
**Place in the curriculum:** Electricity and Electronics (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Haiti expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** voltage (tension) is measured in volts with a voltmeter and current (intensity) in amperes with an ammeter — and adding receivers to a circuit raises what the measurements show it consumes, counted in watts.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on teacher-guide evidence only — Haiti's ITAP Projet Nectar pedagogical sequences (MENFP) are the books in hand for this topic (see lesson/sources.md). Teacher guides are evidence, counted nowhere in the acceptance count.

Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **Voltage and current — tension and intensity** — Two different quantities, two different units.
2. **Voltmeter and ammeter on the model circuit** — Each instrument measures its own quantity on the working maquette.
3. **Adding receivers — watching consumption change** — More receivers, more consumption: the readings prove it.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of Electricity and Electronics. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Distinguish tension (V) from intensity (A).
2. Take voltmeter and ammeter readings on a model circuit.
3. Show that adding receivers raises consumption.
## What you need
The low-voltage circuit maquette, a voltmeter, an ammeter, extra lamp receivers, and a readings table (quantity / instrument / unit / value).
