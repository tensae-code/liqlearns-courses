# Electrical measurement — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the difference between tension and intensity?
**Answer:** Tension (voltage, in volts) is the electrical push between two points; intensity (current, in amperes) is the flow of electricity through the circuit.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Why does the sizing lesson begin with a list of loads?
**Answer:** Because measurements show consumption rises with every receiver added, so an installation must be sized for what all its receivers together draw.
