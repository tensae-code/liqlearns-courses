# Electrical measurement — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Tension (voltage) is measured in…
a) Amperes b) Volts c) Watts d) Ohms only
**Answer: b) Volts**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Intensity (current) is measured in…
a) Volts b) Amperes c) Watts d) Litres
**Answer: b) Amperes**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A voltmeter measures…
a) Intensity b) Tension c) Power directly in watts d) Resistance only
**Answer: b) Tension**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** An ammeter measures…
a) Tension b) Intensity flowing in the circuit c) The battery's age d) Light
**Answer: b) Intensity flowing in the circuit**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Adding receivers to a circuit makes its consumption…
a) Fall to zero b) Rise c) Stay exactly the same d) Become unmeasurable
**Answer: b) Rise**
