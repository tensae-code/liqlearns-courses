# Electrical measurement — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Volts and amperes measure the same quantity.
**Answer: False** — Volts measure tension and amperes measure intensity — two different quantities.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Measurements in the sequence are taken on a real built circuit model.
**Answer: True** — Learners measure tension and intensity on the low-voltage maquette they built.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A circuit with more receivers consumes the same as one with fewer.
**Answer: False** — The experiment shows consumption rises as receivers are added — the measured basis for sizing.
