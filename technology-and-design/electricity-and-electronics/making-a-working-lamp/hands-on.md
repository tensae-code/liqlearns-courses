# Hands-on — Making a working lamp

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: the "Starry Sky" lamp: a dark-paper cylinder pierced and drawn with a constellation, foil stars glued on, a lamp put inside (steps viewed by me). Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
