# Electrical design

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Malaysia's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — The four elements
Every electrical system has four elements: source, medium, load and control. Malaysia designs from the four — an electrical equipment circuit is drawn so each element is present and named.

## Sub lesson 2 — Calculating with Ohm’s law
Design is calculated: voltage, current and resistance by Ohm’s law, and electrical power P beside them. A circuit that is not calculated is a guess, and the book does not guess.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: an electrical system has four elements — source, medium, load, control — and its circuit is calculated with Ohm’s law (V, I, R) and power (P). Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
