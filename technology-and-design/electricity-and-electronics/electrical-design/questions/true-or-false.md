# Electrical design — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The four elements is source, medium, load and control — the parts of every electrical system.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Ohm’s law has nothing to do with this lesson.
**Answer: False** — It is part of the lesson: the law relating voltage, current and resistance (V, I, R).

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Electrical power (p) is the rate at which the circuit uses electrical energy.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.
