# Electrical design — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the four elements?
a) source, medium, load and control — the parts of every electrical system b) Only a price c) Only decoration d) Nothing to do with this lesson
**Answer: a) source, medium, load and control — the parts of every electrical system**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is Ohm’s law?
a) Only decoration b) the law relating voltage, current and resistance (V, I, R) c) Only a price d) A kind of glue
**Answer: b) the law relating voltage, current and resistance (V, I, R)**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is electrical power (P)?
a) Only history b) Only painting c) the rate at which the circuit uses electrical energy d) Only storage
**Answer: c) the rate at which the circuit uses electrical energy**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson teach, in one line?
a) an electrical system has four elements — source, medium, load, control — and its circuit is calculated with Ohm’s law (V, I, R) and power (P). b) Only prices c) Only old tools d) Nothing practical
**Answer: a) an electrical system has four elements — source, medium, load, control — and its circuit is calculated with Ohm’s law (V, I, R) and power (P).**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** How does Malaysia's book teach this?
a) As theory only, never practised b) Only in the glossary c) Only as an exam d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Malay (RBT)
**Answer: d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Malay (RBT)**
