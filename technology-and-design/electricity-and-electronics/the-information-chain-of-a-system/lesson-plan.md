# Lesson plan — The information chain of a system

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Electricity and Electronics · **Lesson:** The information chain of a system
**Place in the curriculum:** Electricity and Electronics (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Morocco expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** every automated system runs an information chain — acquire, process, communicate — and the information travelling it comes in three kinds: analogue (continuous), logic (two-state) and digital (coded in numbers).

## Sub lessons
1. **Acquire, process, communicate** — The information chain (chaîne d'information) has three functions in order. Acquire: sensors gather information from the system and its surroundings. Process: a control part (relay logic, a programmed controller) treats the acquired information against instructions and decides. Communicate: the decisions are passed on — to the energy chain's command parts and to the user, through signals and displays.
2. **Analogue, logic and digital information** — Information travelling the chain takes three forms. Analogue information varies continuously — a temperature, a voltage that follows it. Logic information has two states only — the TOR signals of detection: present/absent, 1/0. Digital information is coded in numbers — values sampled and expressed in binary code, ready for a programmed processor.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name the three functions of the information chain in order and the job of each.
2. Tell analogue, logic and digital information apart with an example of each.
3. Say how the information chain relates to the energy chain.
## What you need
A familiar automated system to analyse (street lamp, automatic door, washing machine).
