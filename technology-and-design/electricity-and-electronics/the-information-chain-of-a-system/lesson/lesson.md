# The information chain of a system

## Do it first
A street lamp that lights itself at dusk: something senses the darkness, something decides, something switches and shows. Sense, decide, act-and-tell: acquire, process, communicate. Name the three stages on any machine you know.

## Sub lesson 1 — Acquire, process, communicate
Morocco's Grade 9 Module 2 builds the whole module on this chain, lesson by lesson: acquire is the sensors lesson, process is the flowchart lesson, communicate is the displays lesson. The chain is the information twin of the energy chain: energy makes the system act; information makes it act at the right moment, in the right way.

## Sub lesson 2 — Analogue, logic and digital information
The three kinds meet at conversions: a sensor may deliver an analogue signal; a comparator turns a threshold crossing into logic information; an analogue-to-digital converter turns a measured value into digital code. Naming the kind at each link of the chain tells you what the next link must be able to receive.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: every automated system runs an information chain — acquire, process, communicate — and the information travelling it comes in three kinds: analogue (continuous), logic (two-state) and digital (coded in numbers). Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
