# The information chain of a system — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Analogue information varies continuously.
**Answer: True** — A temperature or a voltage that follows it is analogue — it takes every value in between.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Processing happens before acquisition in the chain.
**Answer: False** — Nothing can be processed until it is acquired; the order is acquire → process → communicate.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A threshold crossing can turn analogue information into logic information.
**Answer: True** — A comparator watching an analogue signal delivers a two-state answer at the threshold.
