# The information chain of a system — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The information chain runs…
a) acquire, process, communicate b) communicate, acquire, process c) process only — there is no chain d) buy, sell, store
**Answer: a) acquire, process, communicate**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The acquire function is carried by…
a) sensors b) displays c) motors d) the user's eyes only
**Answer: a) sensors**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Logic information has…
a) two states only (TOR: 1/0) b) continuous values c) binary-coded numbers only d) colours
**Answer: a) two states only (TOR: 1/0)**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Digital information is…
a) coded in numbers (binary code) b) always continuous c) the same as analogue d) written on paper only
**Answer: a) coded in numbers (binary code)**

**5. (Sub lesson 1)** The information chain relates to the energy chain as…
a) information decides; energy acts b) rivals — only one exists c) the same chain twice d) unrelated chains
**Answer: a) information decides; energy acts**
