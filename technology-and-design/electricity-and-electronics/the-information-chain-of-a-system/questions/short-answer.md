# The information chain of a system — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Give one example of each kind of information in one system.
**Answer:** Example, room heating: analogue — the temperature sensor's varying voltage; logic — 'too cold / warm enough' from a thermostat threshold; digital — the controller's coded set-point value, e.g. 21 stored in binary.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the chain's three functions and one device for each.
**Answer:** Acquire — a sensor (e.g. a light sensor). Process — a controller or relay logic. Communicate — a display/pilot lamp to the user and command signals to the energy chain.
