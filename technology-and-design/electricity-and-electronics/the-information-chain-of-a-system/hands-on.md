# Hands-on — The information chain of a system

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## Chain the machine
Take an automatic door (or a washing machine). Write its information chain: what is acquired (by which sensor), what processes it (against which instructions), what is communicated (to the motor, to the user). Mark the kind of information at each link.

## Kind spotting
Classify: a thermometer's rising column; a door contact's open/closed; a controller's stored value 101101; a dimmer's smooth voltage. Analogue, logic or digital — justify each in one phrase.
