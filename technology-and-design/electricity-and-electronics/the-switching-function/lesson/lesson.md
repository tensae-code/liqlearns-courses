# The switching function

## Do it first
Press a light switch: click — circuit made, lamp on; click — circuit broken, lamp off. Now imagine switching a factory motor with that finger switch. The relay exists so a small safe command can switch that big load.

## Sub lesson 1 — Switches — making and breaking a circuit
Making and breaking sound simple, but the switch must carry the circuit's current without heating and break it without a lasting arc. That limit is why big loads are not switched by small command switches directly — the switching function grows a second device for exactly that case.

## Sub lesson 2 — The electromagnetic relay
Morocco's operating principle, step by step: coil energised → magnetic field → armature attracted → contacts move (NO closes, NC opens) → load circuit made or broken → coil released → spring restores rest state. The relay is the switching function's bridge between the information world (small signals) and the energy world (real loads).

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: the switching function makes and breaks a circuit: a switch commands by hand, and the electromagnetic relay lets a small control current switch a bigger circuit — command and power kept apart. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
