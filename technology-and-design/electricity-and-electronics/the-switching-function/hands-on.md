# Hands-on — The switching function

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## NO or NC?
For three jobs — a lamp lit while a button is held; an alarm that sounds when a wire is cut; a motor started by a push-button — choose NO or NC contacts and justify each choice.

## Relay trace
Write the relay's operation as a numbered chain from 'coil energised' to 'load circuit made', then the return chain when the coil is released. Mark the exact step where the two circuits 'meet' — and say why 'magnetically, not electrically' matters.
