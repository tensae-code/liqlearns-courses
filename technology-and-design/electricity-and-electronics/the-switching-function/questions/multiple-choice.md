# The switching function — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Making a circuit means…
a) closing it so current can flow b) drawing it on paper c) naming its parts d) painting the wires
**Answer: a) closing it so current can flow**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A normally open (NO) contact…
a) closes when its device is commanded b) is always closed c) opens on command d) carries no current ever
**Answer: a) closes when its device is commanded**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** In a relay, the coil's current creates…
a) a magnetic field that attracts the armature b) heat that melts the contacts c) light for the load d) a sound signal
**Answer: a) a magnetic field that attracts the armature**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Command and power circuits in a relay are…
a) electrically separate, linked only magnetically b) the same circuit c) joined by a wire d) both carried by the spring
**Answer: a) electrically separate, linked only magnetically**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** When the relay coil is released, the contacts return by…
a) a spring b) gravity alone c) the load's current d) hand pressure
**Answer: a) a spring**
