# Lesson plan — The switching function

## Sub lessons
1. **Switches — making and breaking a circuit** — A switch is a controlled gap in a circuit. Closed, it makes the circuit and current flows; open, it breaks the circuit and current stops. Switches are read by their contacts and their rest state: normally open (NO) contacts close on command; normally closed (NC) contacts open on command.
2. **The electromagnetic relay** — A relay is an electromagnet switching contacts. A small current in the coil magnetises the core, the armature is attracted, and the contacts change state — switching a separate, larger circuit. Release the coil and a spring returns the contacts. Command circuit and power circuit meet only magnetically: they stay electrically separate.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say what making and breaking a circuit mean, and tell NO from NC contacts.
2. Trace the relay's operating principle step by step.
3. Say why command and power circuits are kept separate.
## What you need
A battery, a lamp and a switch for a bench circuit if available; relay diagram.
