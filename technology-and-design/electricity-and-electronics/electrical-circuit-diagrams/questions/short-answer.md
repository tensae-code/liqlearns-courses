# Electrical circuit diagrams — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Why does the sequence compare existing installations before the schema is drawn?
**Answer:** So the schema records the best solution the group could justify from real examples, instead of freezing the first unexamined idea.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Name the two checks applied to a finished schema.
**Answer:** It is checked against the chosen solution (does the drawing match the decision?) and against the components list (is everything needed present and connected?).
