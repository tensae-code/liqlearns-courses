# The power-supply function — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Fill one correspondence row for a mains fan.
**Answer:** Input: electrical energy from the mains. Output: mechanical energy (moving air), with some heat and sound.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why is the supply function taught first among the functions?
**Answer:** Because every later function depends on the energy it delivers — detection, conversion, transmission and action all fail without a matched supply.
