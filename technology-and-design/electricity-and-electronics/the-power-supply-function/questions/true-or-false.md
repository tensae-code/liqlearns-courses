# The power-supply function — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Every technical object takes energy in and gives work out.
**Answer: True** — The supply reading names both ends: the form that enters and the form the action takes.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Any supply can feed any object if the plug fits.
**Answer: False** — Form and quantity must both match; a wrong voltage overheats or stalls the object.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Muscles can be a power supply.
**Answer: True** — A bicycle is fed by muscular energy at its input.
