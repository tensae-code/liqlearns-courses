# The power-supply function — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The power-supply function delivers…
a) energy to the object, in the form and quantity it needs b) instructions to the user c) the object's colour d) waste to the bin
**Answer: a) energy to the object, in the form and quantity it needs**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A torch's input energy form is…
a) chemical (stored in its cells) b) light c) sound d) steam
**Answer: a) chemical (stored in its cells)**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** A bicycle's output energy form is…
a) mechanical (movement) b) chemical c) electrical from the mains d) nuclear
**Answer: a) mechanical (movement)**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A supply fails its object when…
a) its form or quantity does not match the object's need b) it is new c) it is painted black d) the object is old
**Answer: a) its form or quantity does not match the object's need**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The energy-form correspondence table matches, for each object…
a) input form to output form b) price to weight c) colour to size d) maker to seller
**Answer: a) input form to output form**
