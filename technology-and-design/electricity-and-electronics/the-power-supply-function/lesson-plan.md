# Lesson plan — The power-supply function

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Electricity and Electronics · **Lesson:** The power-supply function
**Place in the curriculum:** Electricity and Electronics (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Morocco expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the power-supply function (fonction alimenter) feeds a technical object with energy: name the form of energy at the input, the form at the output, and match them — an object can only use energy in the form its supply delivers.

## Sub lessons
1. **Forms of energy at input and output** — Every technical object takes energy in one form and gives work out in another: electrical, mechanical, thermal, light, chemical, muscular. The power-supply function's job is to deliver energy to the object in the form and quantity it needs — battery, mains, spring, fuel or muscles are all supplies.
2. **Matching supply to object** — A supply that delivers the wrong form or the wrong quantity fails the object: a motor wound for one voltage overheats or stalls on another. The correspondence table is the check — for each object, the supply's output form must equal the object's input form, and its quantity must cover the object's need across its working time.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name the energy form at the input and output of familiar objects.
2. Complete an energy-form correspondence table for a set of objects.
3. Say why a supply must match its object in form and quantity.
## What you need
Familiar objects or pictures (torch, bicycle, kettle, fan) and a blank table.
