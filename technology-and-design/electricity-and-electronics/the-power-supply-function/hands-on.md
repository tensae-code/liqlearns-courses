# Hands-on — The power-supply function

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## Correspondence table
Build the table for five objects: columns Object / Energy in (form + source) / Energy out (form). Include one object fed by muscles and one by the mains.

## Wrong supply
A 3 V torch is fed from a 12 V supply, and a mains fan is offered two small cells. For each, say what goes wrong and which matching rule (form, quantity) is broken.
