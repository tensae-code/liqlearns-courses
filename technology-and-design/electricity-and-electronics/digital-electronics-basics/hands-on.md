# Hands-on — Digital electronics basics

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: number systems (binary, octal, decimal, hexadecimal side by side), the 7-segment display and its digit codes, LED displays and scoreboards. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
