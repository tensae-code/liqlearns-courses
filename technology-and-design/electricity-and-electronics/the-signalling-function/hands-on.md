# Hands-on — The signalling function

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## Diagram reading
Take a developed diagram with one push-button, one contactor and lamp H1. List every label and its device. Then answer in writing: button pressed — what energises, what lights, what runs?

## Signal audit
Find three signals in your school or home (pilot lamps, bells, indicators). For each: what state does it announce, could it lie (light without the state being true), and how would you make it unambiguous?
