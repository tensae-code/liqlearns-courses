# The signalling function — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Lamp H1 lit signals…
a) the receiver is in operation b) the room is dark c) the button is broken d) the supply is off
**Answer: a) the receiver is in operation**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The signalling rule is…
a) one sign, one meaning b) many signs, any meaning c) signs are decoration d) lamps light at random
**Answer: a) one sign, one meaning**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A developed diagram draws…
a) power and control circuits separately, contacts at rest, devices labelled b) the machine's photograph c) only the casing d) wires as they lie in the box, tangled
**Answer: a) power and control circuits separately, contacts at rest, devices labelled**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In diagram labels, H names…
a) a signalling lamp (H1) b) a motor c) a push-button d) a fuse
**Answer: a) a signalling lamp (H1)**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Reading a developed diagram means…
a) following current paths and asking what changes when a button is pressed b) reading it like a story, once c) memorising the labels only d) measuring the paper
**Answer: a) following current paths and asking what changes when a button is pressed**
