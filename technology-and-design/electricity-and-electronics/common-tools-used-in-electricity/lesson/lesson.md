# Common tools used in electricity

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Rwanda's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Identifying the common electrical tools
Rwanda's Primary 5 electricity unit opens with the toolkit: the common tools used in electricity, identified one by one. Identification comes first because electrical work allows no guessing — the right tool is part of the safety of the job.

## Sub lesson 2 — What each tool is used for
Each tool is learned with its use: the tool exists for a task in electrical work — cutting, stripping, joining, testing, fixing. Name plus use is the complete learning: a tool you can name but not place in the work is only half learned.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: electrical work has its own common tools, and the first electricity lesson is simply to identify them and know what each is used for. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
