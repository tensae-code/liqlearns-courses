# Electronic design

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Malaysia's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Microcontroller against microprocessor
Electronic design begins with a distinction: a microcontroller against a microprocessor — the self-contained control chip against the bare processing heart. Malaysia teaches the difference before any building.

## Sub lesson 2 — Inside the microcontroller
The microcontroller is opened up: its inside parts are named and placed, so the chip that will run the learner’s circuits is understood as a system, not a black box.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: a microcontroller is a self-contained control chip, distinct from a microprocessor — and its inside parts are learned before it is used. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
