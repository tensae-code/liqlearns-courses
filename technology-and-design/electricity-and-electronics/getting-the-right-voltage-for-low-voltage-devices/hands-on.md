# Hands-on — Getting the right voltage for low-voltage devices

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## Work the topic
Carry out the topic's core task on a fresh example: taking mains 230V down to the small voltage a device needs, rectifiers and their types and uses. Work in order, naming each part or step as you go.

## Check it
Check your finished work against the lesson's own rules. Find one thing that is wrong or weak, fix it, and say what you changed and why.
