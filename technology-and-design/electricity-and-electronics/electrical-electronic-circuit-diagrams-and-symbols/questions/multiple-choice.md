# Electrical/electronic circuit diagrams and symbols — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why does every component have a standard symbol?
a) To decorate the drawing b) So anyone can read the circuit drawing c) To make circuits harder d) Symbols are optional
**Answer: b) So anyone can read the circuit drawing**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A battery symbol shows…
a) One cell only b) Cells joined together c) A lamp d) A resistor
**Answer: b) Cells joined together**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A schematic diagram shows…
a) Where parts physically sit b) How the circuit is connected and works c) Only the box the product comes in d) The price of the parts
**Answer: b) How the circuit is connected and works**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A layout diagram shows…
a) The circuit's logic only b) The real positions of parts and wires c) Music notation d) Nothing useful
**Answer: b) The real positions of parts and wires**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** When converting a layout to a schematic, you…
a) Drop parts that are hard to draw b) Replace each part with its symbol, keeping the connections in order c) Change the circuit to a better one d) Draw the parts as pictures
**Answer: b) Replace each part with its symbol, keeping the connections in order**
