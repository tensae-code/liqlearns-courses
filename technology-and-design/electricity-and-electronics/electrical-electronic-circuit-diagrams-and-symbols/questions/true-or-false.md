# Electrical/electronic circuit diagrams and symbols — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** A schematic and a layout are two drawings of the same circuit.
**Answer: True** — One shows the connections, the other the physical positions.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** You may invent your own symbol if you draw it neatly.
**Answer: False** — Symbols are standard and agreed — that is what makes drawings readable by everyone.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** A solar cell has its own standard symbol in the workbook's set.
**Answer: True** — It is one of the eight symbols drawn and named.
