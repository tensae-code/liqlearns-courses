# Hands-on — Electrical/electronic circuit diagrams and symbols

Real drawing practice with pencil and paper — this subject is learned by doing.

## Draw the symbol set
Draw and label all eight symbols from memory: cell, battery, lamp, switch, resistor, LED, buzzer, solar cell. Check against the workbook and fix any that are wrong.

## Convert a layout
Take a simple layout (battery, switch, lamp in a loop). Draw its schematic with symbols, keeping the connection order. Count the parts in both drawings — the counts must match.
