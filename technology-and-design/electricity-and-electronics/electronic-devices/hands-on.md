# Hands-on — Electronic devices

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: their structure (control unit, power supply, working part), semiconductors, and the component symbol table (LED, transistor, variable resistor, capacitor µF/V, battery, motor, switch). Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
