# Electronic devices

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Mongolia's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Electronic devices
Mongolia's book teaches it this way (*Design Drawing & Technology 11 (Mongolia)*, printed p. 139): their structure (control unit, power supply, working part), semiconductors, and the component symbol table (LED, transistor, variable resistor, capacitor µF/V, battery, motor, switch). The learner works through the named materials and steps in order, saying what each step does before doing the next one.

## Sub lesson 2 — Electronic devices — doing it yourself
Now the learner does the whole task unaided: their structure (control unit, power supply, working part), semiconductors, and the component symbol table (LED, transistor, variable resistor, capacitor µF/V, battery, motor, switch). Working from the first sub lesson's sequence, the learner plans the make, works through it step by step, and checks the finished piece against what Mongolia's book shows.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: Electronic devices — their structure (control unit, power supply, working part), semiconductors, and the component symbol table (LED, transistor, variable resistor, capacitor µF/V, battery, motor, switch). Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.

## Do It Yourself
1. Do the Do it first task again, carefully this time, using everything the lesson taught.
2. Check your work against the lesson's own checks — fix one thing you find.
3. Keep your work: it is practice evidence, and the next lesson builds on it.
