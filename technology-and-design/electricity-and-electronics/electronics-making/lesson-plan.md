# Lesson plan — Electronics making

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Electricity and Electronics · **Lesson:** Electronics making
**Place in the curriculum:** Electricity and Electronics (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Morocco expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** electronics making produces a printed circuit that works: tracks carry the connections on a board, components are read before they are placed — resistor values by their colour codes, LEDs by their polarity.

## Sub lessons
1. **Producing a printed circuit** — A printed circuit board (PCB) replaces loose wires with copper tracks fixed on an insulating board: the circuit diagram becomes a track layout, the board is produced (tracks protected, the rest etched away, holes drilled), and components are soldered into their places. The result is compact, repeatable and repairable by diagram.
2. **Resistor colour codes and LED polarity** — A resistor announces its value in coloured bands: two value bands, a multiplier band, a tolerance band — read from the band nearest the end. An LED is polarised: the long leg is the anode (+), the short leg and the flat rim mark the cathode (−); reversed, it does not light and may be damaged by a wrong circuit.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. List the steps of producing a printed circuit in order.
2. Decode a resistor's colour bands into a value.
3. Identify an LED's anode and cathode two ways.
## What you need
A scrap printed circuit board to examine, a few resistors and LEDs, a colour-code chart.
