# Electronics making — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** List the printed-circuit making chain in order.
**Answer:** Circuit diagram → track layout → board production (protect tracks, etch, drill) → component placing → soldering → test against the diagram.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Give two ways to identify an LED's cathode before soldering.
**Answer:** The cathode leg is the shorter leg; and the LED's rim has a flat side on the cathode side.
