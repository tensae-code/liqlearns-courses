# Electronics making — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A printed circuit replaces loose wires with…
a) copper tracks fixed on an insulating board b) paper strips c) glass tubes d) wooden pegs
**Answer: a) copper tracks fixed on an insulating board**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The making chain runs…
a) diagram, layout, board production, placing, soldering, test b) soldering first, diagram later c) test, then diagram, then guessing d) painting, packing, selling
**Answer: a) diagram, layout, board production, placing, soldering, test**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A resistor's value is read from…
a) its coloured bands b) its weight c) its smell d) the box it came in
**Answer: a) its coloured bands**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** An LED's anode is…
a) the long leg (+) b) the short leg c) the flat rim side d) either leg — LEDs are not polarised
**Answer: a) the long leg (+)**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** A reversed LED in a circuit…
a) does not light (and may be damaged) b) lights twice as bright c) lights a different colour d) works exactly the same
**Answer: a) does not light (and may be damaged)**
