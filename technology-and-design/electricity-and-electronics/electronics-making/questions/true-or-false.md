# Electronics making — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A track error on the layout is copied onto every board made from it.
**Answer: True** — That is why each making step is checked against the diagram.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Resistor bands are read starting from the band nearest the middle.
**Answer: False** — They are read from the band nearest the end: value bands, multiplier, tolerance.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** An LED's cathode can be found by the flat side of its rim.
**Answer: True** — Short leg and flat rim both mark the cathode (−).
