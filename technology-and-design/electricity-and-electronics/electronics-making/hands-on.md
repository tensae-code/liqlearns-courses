# Hands-on — Electronics making

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## Decode and sort
Take five resistors. Decode each one's bands with the chart, write its value, and sort the five from smallest to largest. Check one against the parts list of a real diagram.

## Polarity check
Take three LEDs. For each, record two polarity proofs (leg length; flat rim). Then draw the LED symbol and mark which side of your drawing is the anode, ready for placing on a board.
