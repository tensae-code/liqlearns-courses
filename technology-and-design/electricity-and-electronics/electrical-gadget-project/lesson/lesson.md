# Electrical gadget project

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Malaysia's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Sketch and analyse
The electrical project begins on paper: the circuit design is sketched, and the electrical-system elements inside the gadget are analysed — source, medium, load and control each accounted for.

## Sub lesson 2 — Build, test, evaluate, improve
The working electrical gadget is built, then tested and evaluated on its function — does it do what its circuit promised? — and improvements are proposed from the test evidence.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: the electrical gadget project sketches the circuit, builds the gadget working, tests and evaluates its function, and proposes improvements. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.

## Do It Yourself
1. Do the Do it first task again, carefully this time, using everything the lesson taught.
2. Check your work against the lesson's own checks — fix one thing you find.
3. Keep your work: it is practice evidence, and the next lesson builds on it.
