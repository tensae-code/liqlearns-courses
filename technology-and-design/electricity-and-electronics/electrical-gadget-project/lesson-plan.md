# Lesson plan — Electrical gadget project

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Electricity and Electronics · **Lesson:** Electrical gadget project
**Place in the curriculum:** Electricity and Electronics (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Malaysia expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the electrical gadget project sketches the circuit, builds the gadget working, tests and evaluates its function, and proposes improvements.
**This lesson's own level:** stocked from Malaysia's *Reka Bentuk dan Teknologi* (RBT — Design and Technology) textbooks — see lesson/sources.md. Malaysia counts in the acceptance count (student books, not evidence-only).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **Sketch and analyse** — The electrical project begins on paper: the circuit design is sketched, and the electrical-system elements inside the gadget are analysed — source, medium, load and control each accounted for.
2. **Build, test, evaluate, improve** — The working electrical gadget is built, then tested and evaluated on its function — does it do what its circuit promised? — and improvements are proposed from the test evidence.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of Electricity and Electronics. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Sketch a circuit design and analyse its elements.
2. Build, test and evaluate a working electrical gadget.
3. Explain how Malaysia's book teaches it.
## What you need
Simple circuit components (with supervision); sketch paper.
