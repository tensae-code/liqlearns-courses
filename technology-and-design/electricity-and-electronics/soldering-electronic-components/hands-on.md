# Hands-on — Soldering electronic components

Real drawing practice with pencil and paper — this subject is learned by doing.

## Label the assembly
Draw a stripboard assembly from the workbook: component side and soldering side, copper strips, one component, one solder joint. Label every part.

## Sketch the iron
Make the workbook's labelled 2-D sketch of a soldering iron: tip, safe-handling part, heating-element part and mains cord, each labelled.
