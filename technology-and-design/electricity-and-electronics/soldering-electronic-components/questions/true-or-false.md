# Soldering electronic components — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Components are soldered on the same side they stand on.
**Answer: False** — They stand on the component side; soldering happens on the copper-strip side.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The tip is the safe part of the iron to hold.
**Answer: False** — The tip is the hot end; you hold the safe-handling part.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A stripboard layout is drawn before any soldering begins.
**Answer: True** — The layout is the checked plan the soldering copies.
