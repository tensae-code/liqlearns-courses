# Soldering electronic components — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, or by doing the drawing step named.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the three parts of the soldering iron the workbook labels, plus its cord.
**Answer:** Tip, safe-handling part and heating-element part, with a mains power cord.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What two jobs does one solder joint do?
**Answer:** It holds the component mechanically and connects it electrically to the copper strip.
