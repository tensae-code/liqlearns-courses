# Soldering electronic components — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** On stripboard, the copper strips are on…
a) The component side b) The soldering side c) Both sides d) Neither side
**Answer: b) The soldering side**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does a solder joint do?
a) Decorates the board b) Joins a leg to the copper strip, holding it and connecting it electrically c) Cuts the strip d) Cools the iron
**Answer: b) Joins a leg to the copper strip, holding it and connecting it electrically**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The hot end of the soldering iron is the…
a) Handle b) Tip c) Cord d) Stand
**Answer: b) Tip**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which part of the iron do you hold?
a) The tip b) The heating-element part c) The safe-handling part (handle) d) The cord's plug pins
**Answer: c) The safe-handling part (handle)**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Why draw the stripboard layout before soldering?
a) So the hot work copies a checked plan b) Drawing is not needed c) To use up paper d) The iron needs warming first
**Answer: a) So the hot work copies a checked plan**
