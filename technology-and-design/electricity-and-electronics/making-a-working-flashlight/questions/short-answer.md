# Making a working flashlight — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, or by doing the step named.

**1. (Sub lesson 2) Why must the bulb be connected at two points, not one?**
**Answer:** Current must enter the bulb and leave it to complete the loop — one contact alone leaves the circuit open and the bulb dark.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Why are housing and circuit designed together, not one after the other?**
**Answer:** The housing must hold the batteries, wires and bulb exactly where the circuit needs them; a housing made without the circuit in mind leaves parts unfitted or connections unreachable.
