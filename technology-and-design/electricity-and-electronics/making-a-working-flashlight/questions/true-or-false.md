# Making a working flashlight — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 2) Insulation can stay on the wire where it meets a contact.**
**Answer: False — Bare copper must meet the contact; 2.5 cm is stripped.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Wire cut too long is just as good as wire cut to length.**
**Answer: False — Excess wire tangles and can short; length is part of accuracy.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3) In a circuit, a small inaccuracy can be the difference between light and no light.**
**Answer: True**
