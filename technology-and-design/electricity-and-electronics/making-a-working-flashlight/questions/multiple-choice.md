# Making a working flashlight — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 2) How much insulation is stripped from the wire end?**
a) 0.5 cm b) 2.5 cm c) 10 cm d) None
**Answer: b) 2.5 cm**

**2. (Sub lesson 2) The bulb is contacted at which two points?**
a) Top and top b) Screw-thread and foot contact c) Glass and glass d) Wire and housing
**Answer: b) Screw-thread and foot contact**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) The housing is made from…**
a) Steel b) Cardboard c) Glass d) Stone
**Answer: b) Cardboard**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Components in the housing are connected with…**
a) String only b) Adhesive strip where specified c) Paint d) Nothing
**Answer: b) Adhesive strip where specified**

**5. (Sub lesson 3) The conical shade's job is to…**
a) Hide the bulb b) Gather the light and aim it forward c) Hold the batteries d) Switch the circuit
**Answer: b) Gather the light and aim it forward**
