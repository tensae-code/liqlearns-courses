# Lesson plan — Magnets and electromagnetism for technology

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Electricity and Electronics · **Lesson:** Magnets and electromagnetism for technology
**Place in the curriculum:** Electricity and Electronics (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Jamaica expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** magnets have poles and a field, like poles repel and unlike poles attract; an electromagnet is a magnet made by current, switched on and off at will — and the motor effect turns that force into movement.

## Sub lessons
1. **Magnets, poles and field** — Every magnet has two poles and a magnetic field around it; its laws are simple: like poles repel, unlike poles attract.
2. **Permanent magnets and electromagnets** — A permanent magnet is always on; an electromagnet — a coil carrying current — is a magnet only while the current flows, and its strength can be controlled.
3. **The motor effect** — A current-carrying wire in a magnetic field feels a force — the motor effect — and arranged rightly, that force turns a coil: the principle of every electric motor.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Describe a magnet's poles and field and state the laws of attraction and repulsion.
2. Tell a permanent magnet from an electromagnet and say how an electromagnet's strength is controlled.
3. Explain the motor effect in plain words.
## What you need
Two bar magnets, and small iron/steel objects to test.
