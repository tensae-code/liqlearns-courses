# Magnets and electromagnetism for technology — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Like poles brought together will…**
a) Attract b) Repel c) Merge d) Vanish
**Answer: b) Repel**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) The space around a magnet where its influence acts is its…**
a) Shadow b) Magnetic field c) Handle d) Pole
**Answer: b) Magnetic field**

**3. (Sub lesson 2) An electromagnet is magnetic…**
a) Always b) Only while current flows in its coil c) Never d) Only when heated
**Answer: b) Only while current flows in its coil**

**4. (Sub lesson 2) An electromagnet grows stronger with…**
a) Fewer turns b) More turns or more current c) No core d) Less current
**Answer: b) More turns or more current**

**5. (Sub lesson 3) The motor effect is…**
a) A force on a current-carrying wire in a magnetic field b) A kind of paint c) A battery type d) A pole law
**Answer: a) A force on a current-carrying wire in a magnetic field**
