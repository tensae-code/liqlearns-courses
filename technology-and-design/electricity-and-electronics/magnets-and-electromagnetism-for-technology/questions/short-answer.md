# Magnets and electromagnetism for technology — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, or by doing the step named.

**1. (Sub lesson 2) Name the electromagnet's two advantages over a permanent magnet.**
**Answer:** It can be switched on and off with the current, and its strength can be controlled (more turns / more current).

**2. (Sub lesson 3) How does the motor effect become a spinning motor?**
**Answer:** The current-carrying coil sits in a magnetic field; the field pushes its two sides in opposite directions, so the coil turns — arranged and fed continuously, it spins.
