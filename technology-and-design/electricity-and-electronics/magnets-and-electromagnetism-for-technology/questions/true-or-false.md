# Magnets and electromagnetism for technology — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Cutting a magnet in two destroys its poles.**
**Answer: False — Each piece is a whole magnet with both poles.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2) A permanent magnet can be switched off.**
**Answer: False — It is always on; switching on and off is the electromagnet's advantage.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3) Every electric motor works by the motor effect.**
**Answer: True**
