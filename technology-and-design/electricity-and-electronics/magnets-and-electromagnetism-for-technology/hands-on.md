# Hands-on — Magnets and electromagnetism for technology

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## Pole-law tests
Test both pole laws with real magnets and record the evidence: which pairs pushed, which pulled. Then list five everyday devices that use magnets.

## Electromagnet strength
Predict, then (with supervision, following the making lesson) test: does adding turns to a coil change how many paper clips an electromagnet lifts? Record turns against clips.
