# The transmission function — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The transmission function carries…
a) movement from where it is made to where it is used b) electricity to a lamp c) water to a tank d) signals to a user
**Answer: a) movement from where it is made to where it is used**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In a pulley-belt drive, the belt is…
a) the intermediate organ b) the driving organ c) the driven organ d) not an organ at all
**Answer: a) the intermediate organ**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** A small driver turning a large driven pulley gives an output that is…
a) slower and stronger in turning effect b) faster and weaker c) unchanged in every way d) reversed, always
**Answer: a) slower and stronger in turning effect**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Meshing gears transmit…
a) directly, with no intermediate organ b) through a belt c) through a chain only d) not at all — gears cannot transmit
**Answer: a) directly, with no intermediate organ**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** A crossed belt changes…
a) the direction of rotation b) the belt's colour c) the pulley sizes d) nothing
**Answer: a) the direction of rotation**
