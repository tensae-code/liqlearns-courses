# The transmission function — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Give the four-part reading of a bicycle chain drive.
**Answer:** Driver: the pedal sprocket. Driven: the rear-wheel sprocket. Intermediate organ: the chain. Transformation: speed changed by the sprocket sizes, direction kept.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** When would you choose a belt over gears?
**Answer:** When the shafts are far apart, or when slip under overload is wanted as protection — gears need the shafts close and meshing.
