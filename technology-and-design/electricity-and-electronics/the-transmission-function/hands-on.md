# Hands-on — The transmission function

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## Transmission survey
Find or picture three transmissions (bicycle chain, belt drive, gear pair). For each, fill the four-part reading: driver / driven / intermediate organ / transformation (speed and direction).

## Size it
A motor pulley of 10 cm drives a machine pulley of 40 cm. State: faster or slower output? Stronger or weaker turning effect? Then say what a crossed belt would change.
