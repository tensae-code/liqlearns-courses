# The communicate function — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The communicate function…
a) informs the user of states and results, in readable form b) acquires sensor data c) stores energy d) turns the motor
**Answer: a) informs the user of states and results, in readable form**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A 7-segment display draws a digit by…
a) lighting a chosen subset of seven segments b) moving clock hands c) printing on paper d) seven lamps in a row, unrelated
**Answer: a) lighting a chosen subset of seven segments**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The digit 8 lights…
a) all seven segments b) only two segments c) no segments d) the middle segment only
**Answer: a) all seven segments**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The digit 1 lights…
a) only the two right segments b) all but the middle c) the top three d) the bottom three
**Answer: a) only the two right segments**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** A warning should be communicated by…
a) a signal that cannot be missed b) a tiny digit in a corner c) nothing — warnings are internal d) a lamp identical to all others
**Answer: a) a signal that cannot be missed**
