# Hands-on — The communicate function

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## Segment codes
Write the segment list (a–g) for digits 0, 3, 5, 8 and 9. Then swap lists with a partner: from their list alone, draw the digit and check.

## Match the message
For three messages — 'motor running', 'tank at 37 litres', 'overheating danger' — choose a communication device (lamp, digit display, warning signal) and justify the match in one sentence each.
