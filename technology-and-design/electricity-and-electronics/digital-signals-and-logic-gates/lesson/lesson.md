# Digital signals and logic gates

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Mongolia's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Digital signals and logic gates
Mongolia's book teaches it this way (*Design Drawing & Technology 12 (Mongolia)*, printed p. 101): Boole's algebra, the AND, OR and NOT operations with their gate symbols and switch circuits, the SN7400 chip (four NAND gates, pinout, 4.5–5.5 V), and the RS trigger built from gates. The learner works through the named materials and steps in order, saying what each step does before doing the next one.

## Sub lesson 2 — Digital signals and logic gates — doing it yourself
Now the learner does the whole task unaided: Boole's algebra, the AND, OR and NOT operations with their gate symbols and switch circuits, the SN7400 chip (four NAND gates, pinout, 4.5–5.5 V), and the RS trigger built from gates. Working from the first sub lesson's sequence, the learner plans the make, works through it step by step, and checks the finished piece against what Mongolia's book shows.
