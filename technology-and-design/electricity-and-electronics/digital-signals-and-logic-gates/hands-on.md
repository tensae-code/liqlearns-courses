# Hands-on — Digital signals and logic gates

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: Boole's algebra, the AND, OR and NOT operations with their gate symbols and switch circuits, the SN7400 chip (four NAND gates, pinout, 4.5–5.5 V), and the RS trigger built from gates. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
