# Digital signals and logic gates — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Say in one sentence what this lesson teaches.
**Answer:** Digital signals and logic gates — Boole's algebra, the AND, OR and NOT operations with their gate symbols and switch circuits, the SN7400 chip (four NAND gates, pinout, 4.5–5.5 V), and the RS trigger built from gates.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name one material, tool or step this lesson works with.
**Answer:** Any one named in the lesson text — for example, the first step of sub lesson 1: Digital signals and logic gates.
