# Integrated circuits — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Say in one sentence what this lesson teaches.
**Answer:** Integrated circuits — the op-amp and its symbol, the NE555 timer and 4026 counter driving a display, Arduino Uno, and the generations of electronics from vacuum tube (1945–58) to microprocessor (1971– ).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name one material, tool or step this lesson works with.
**Answer:** Any one named in the lesson text — for example, the first step of sub lesson 1: Integrated circuits.
