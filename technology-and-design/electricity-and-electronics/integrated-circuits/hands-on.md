# Hands-on — Integrated circuits

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: the op-amp and its symbol, the NE555 timer and 4026 counter driving a display, Arduino Uno, and the generations of electronics from vacuum tube (1945–58) to microprocessor (1971– ). Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
