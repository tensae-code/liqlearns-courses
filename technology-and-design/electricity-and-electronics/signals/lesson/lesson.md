# Signals

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Mongolia's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Signals
Mongolia's book teaches it this way (*Design Drawing & Technology 11 (Mongolia)*, printed p. 152): analogue and digital signals, how an analogue signal is converted to a digital code, and reading signal graphs (amplitude against time). The learner works through the named materials and steps in order, saying what each step does before doing the next one.

## Sub lesson 2 — Signals — doing it yourself
Now the learner does the whole task unaided: analogue and digital signals, how an analogue signal is converted to a digital code, and reading signal graphs (amplitude against time). Working from the first sub lesson's sequence, the learner plans the make, works through it step by step, and checks the finished piece against what Mongolia's book shows.
