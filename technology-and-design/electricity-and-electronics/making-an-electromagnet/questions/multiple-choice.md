# Making an electromagnet — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) The battery in Jamaica's build is…**
a) A 9 V battery b) An AA 1.5 V battery c) Mains electricity d) Two car batteries
**Answer: b) An AA 1.5 V battery**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) The core is…**
a) A wooden stick b) A 4-inch wire nail c) A copper rod d) A magnet
**Answer: b) A 4-inch wire nail**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) The copper wire provided measures…**
a) 50 cm b) 1 m c) 5 m d) 50 m
**Answer: c) 5 m**

**4. (Sub lesson 2) The coil must be wound…**
a) Loosely, in mixed directions b) Tightly and evenly, in one direction c) Only at one end d) Around the battery
**Answer: b) Tightly and evenly, in one direction**

**5. (Sub lesson 3) The nail's magnetism lasts…**
a) Forever b) Only while the circuit is complete c) One hour d) Until it rusts
**Answer: b) Only while the circuit is complete**
