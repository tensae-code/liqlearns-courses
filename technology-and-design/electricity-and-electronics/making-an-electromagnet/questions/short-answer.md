# Making an electromagnet — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, or by doing the step named.

**1. (Sub lesson 3) A finished electromagnet lifts nothing. Give two build faults to check first.**
**Answer:** Coil circuit not complete (an end not truly connected / insulation not stripped at the contact), or a flat battery — also loose, uneven winding (any two).

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Why leave the wire ends free when winding?**
**Answer:** So they can reach and connect to the battery's terminals — the coil only becomes a magnet when the current can flow through it.
