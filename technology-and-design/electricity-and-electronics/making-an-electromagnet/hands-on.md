# Hands-on — Making an electromagnet

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## Build and test
With supervision, make the electromagnet to the lesson's specification. Test how many paper clips it lifts, then disconnect and watch them fall. Record both observations.

## One change at a time
Predict what happens with more turns wound on, then test with the same battery and clips. Record turns against clips lifted, and state the rule your evidence shows.
