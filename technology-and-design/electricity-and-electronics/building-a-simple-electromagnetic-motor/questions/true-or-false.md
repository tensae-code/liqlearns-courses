# Building a simple electromagnetic motor — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 2) Glue is always the right way to join motor components.**
**Answer: False — The appropriate choice depends on the materials and whether strength or adjustment is needed.**

**2. (Sub lesson 3) A motor you cannot explain is a motor you only copied.**
**Answer: True — Analysis is the proof the principles were learned.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) The motor uses the same electricity-and-magnetism principles as the electromagnet lessons.**
**Answer: True**
