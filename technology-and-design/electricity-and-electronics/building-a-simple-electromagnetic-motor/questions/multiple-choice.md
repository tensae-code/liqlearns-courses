# Building a simple electromagnetic motor — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) The motor's turning comes from…**
a) The motor effect — force on a current-carrying coil in a magnetic field b) The battery's weight c) The base's colour d) Gravity alone
**Answer: a) The motor effect — force on a current-carrying coil in a magnetic field**

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Components are joined to the base with…**
a) Whatever is nearest b) The most appropriate adhesive or fastener for the material c) String always d) Nothing
**Answer: b) The most appropriate adhesive or fastener for the material**

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Each component is tested…**
a) Never b) As it is fitted, at its own stage c) Only after a year d) By another school
**Answer: b) As it is fitted, at its own stage**

**4. (Sub lesson 3) Analysing the motor means tracing…**
a) Its price b) Current, force and rotation c) Its paint d) Its packaging
**Answer: b) Current, force and rotation**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Before building, the learner should be able to…**
a) Sell the motor b) Say what should happen and why c) Skip the principles d) Memorise the checklist only
**Answer: b) Say what should happen and why**
