# Building a simple electromagnetic motor — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, or by doing the step named.

**1. (Sub lesson 2) A metal bracket must later be removable for adjustment. Glue or screws — and why?**
**Answer:** Screws (a mechanical fastener) — the joint must come apart for adjustment; glue would make the joint permanent and damage parts on removal.

**2. (Sub lesson 3) Analyse a simple motor in three stages.**
**Answer:** Electrical: current flows from the battery through the coil. Motor effect: the magnetic field forces the coil's sides in opposite directions. Mechanical: the coil rotates, turning electrical energy into movement.
