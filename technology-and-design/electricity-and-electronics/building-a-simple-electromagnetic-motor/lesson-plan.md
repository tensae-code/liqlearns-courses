# Lesson plan — Building a simple electromagnetic motor

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Electricity and Electronics · **Lesson:** Building a simple electromagnetic motor
**Place in the curriculum:** Electricity and Electronics (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Jamaica expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** a simple electromagnetic motor turns electricity and magnetism into rotation: each component is made by following instructions, joined to the base with the most appropriate adhesive or fastener, and the finished motor is analysed to explain how it operates.

## Sub lessons
1. **The principles it is built on** — The motor is the motor effect at work: current in a coil, inside a magnetic field, feels a force — arranged so the force keeps turning the coil.
2. **Making each component, and joining them** — Every component is made by following instructions step by step, and joined to the motor's base with the adhesive or fastener chosen as most appropriate for its material.
3. **Analysing how the motor operates** — The build ends in analysis: trace the current, name the forces, and explain the turning — a motor you cannot explain is a motor you only copied.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of Electricity and Electronics. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Explain the motor's operating principle before building it.
2. Make each component by instructions and choose the appropriate adhesive/fastener for joining parts to the base.
3. Analyse a finished motor: trace current, forces and rotation.
## What you need
The motor kit/materials of the class build: battery, copper wire, magnets, wooden or metal base components, adhesives and fasteners (adult supervision).
