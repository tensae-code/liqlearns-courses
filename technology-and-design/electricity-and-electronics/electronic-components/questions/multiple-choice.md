# Electronic components — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is LED?
a) a diode that gives light when current passes in the right direction b) Only a price c) Only decoration d) Nothing to do with this lesson
**Answer: a) a diode that gives light when current passes in the right direction**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is multimeter?
a) Only history b) the meter that measures voltage, resistance and more; voltage and resistance are measured in parallel c) Only storage d) A kind of glue
**Answer: b) the meter that measures voltage, resistance and more; voltage and resistance are measured in parallel**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is transistor?
a) Only old tools b) Only painting c) a three-legged component — base, emitter, collector — that switches and amplifies d) Only a price
**Answer: c) a three-legged component — base, emitter, collector — that switches and amplifies**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson teach, in one line?
a) Only decoration b) Nothing to do with this lesson c) Only history d) electronic components each do one job in a circuit — the LED turns current into light under a resistor's protection, the meter proves what the circuit is doing, and the transistor switches and amplifies.
**Answer: d) electronic components each do one job in a circuit — the LED turns current into light under a resistor's protection, the meter proves what the circuit is doing, and the transistor switches and amplifies.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** How does Iran's book teach this?
a) As theory only, never practised b) Only in a glossary c) Only as an exam d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Persian
**Answer: d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Persian**
