# Electronic components — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** LED is a diode that gives light when current passes in the right direction.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Multimeter has nothing to do with this lesson.
**Answer: False** — It is part of the lesson: the meter that measures voltage, resistance and more; voltage and resistance are measured in parallel.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Transistor is a three-legged component — base, emitter, collector — that switches and amplifies.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.
