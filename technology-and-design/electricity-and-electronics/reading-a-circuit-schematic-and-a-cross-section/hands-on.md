# Hands-on — Reading a circuit schematic and a cross-section

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## Trace the loop
On a flashlight schematic, trace the current path aloud from the battery, naming every component in order, until you return to the battery.

## Draw the cut
Draw a cross-section of a familiar container object (a torch, a kettle, a lunchbox) showing its inside arrangement, and label three things the outside view hides.
