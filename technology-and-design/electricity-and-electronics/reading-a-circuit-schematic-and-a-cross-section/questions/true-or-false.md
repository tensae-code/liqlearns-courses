# Reading a circuit schematic and a cross-section — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Lines in a schematic mean real connections.**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2) A cross-section can show things the outside view hides.**
**Answer: True — It reveals the inside arrangement.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3) A maker needs only one of the two views, never both.**
**Answer: False — Connections and arrangement are different questions; the pair answers both.**
