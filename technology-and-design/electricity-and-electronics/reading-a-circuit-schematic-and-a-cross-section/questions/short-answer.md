# Reading a circuit schematic and a cross-section — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, or by doing the step named.

**1. (Sub lesson 3) A wire inside a flashlight has come loose. Which drawing helps first, and how?**
**Answer:** The schematic — it shows where that wire should connect; the cross-section then shows where it should physically lie inside the housing.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) What must you imagine to read a cross-section?**
**Answer:** The object cut through on a plane, with the cut face showing the arrangement of the parts inside.
