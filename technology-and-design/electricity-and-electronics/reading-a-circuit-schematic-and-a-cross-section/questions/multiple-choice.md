# Reading a circuit schematic and a cross-section — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) A schematic diagram uses…**
a) Photographs b) Standard symbols joined by connection lines c) Only words d) Colours only
**Answer: b) Standard symbols joined by connection lines**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) To read a schematic, you start at…**
a) The bulb b) The source and trace the loop c) The switch only d) The end
**Answer: b) The source and trace the loop**

**3. (Sub lesson 2) A cross-sectional view shows…**
a) The outside only b) The inside arrangement, as if cut through c) The circuit symbols d) The price
**Answer: b) The inside arrangement, as if cut through**

**4. (Sub lesson 3) Which view answers 'how are the components connected?'**
a) The cross-section b) The schematic c) Neither d) A photograph
**Answer: b) The schematic**

**5. (Sub lesson 3) Which view answers 'where does the battery sit inside the housing?'**
a) The schematic b) The cross-section c) Neither d) The title block
**Answer: b) The cross-section**
