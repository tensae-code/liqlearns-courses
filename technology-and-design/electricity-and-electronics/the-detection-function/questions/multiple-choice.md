# The detection function — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A sensor converts…
a) a physical fact into an electrical signal the system can use b) electricity into smoke c) drawings into parts d) sound into colour
**Answer: a) a physical fact into an electrical signal the system can use**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** TOR information has…
a) two states only — all or nothing, 1 or 0 b) ten states c) any value at all d) no states
**Answer: a) two states only — all or nothing, 1 or 0**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** In the fire-alarm example, the sensor detects…
a) gas/smoke b) the tank level c) the robot's position d) the time of day
**Answer: a) gas/smoke**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Level detection in automatic filling says…
a) 'reached' or 'not reached' — the inflow stops at 'reached' b) the exact litres, always c) nothing until the tank overflows d) the water's colour
**Answer: a) 'reached' or 'not reached' — the inflow stops at 'reached'**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Detection is which link of the information chain?
a) The first — before processing and signalling b) The last c) Outside the chain d) The chain has no detection link
**Answer: a) The first — before processing and signalling**
