# The detection function — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does TOR mean, and what are a level detector's two states?
**Answer:** TOR = tout ou rien, all-or-nothing logic information with two states only; the level detector's states are 'level reached' (1) and 'level not reached' (0).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why is detection taught as a function of the whole system, not as a gadget?
**Answer:** Because the sensor serves the system's information chain: its report is what processing decides on and signalling announces — remove detection and the system is blind.
