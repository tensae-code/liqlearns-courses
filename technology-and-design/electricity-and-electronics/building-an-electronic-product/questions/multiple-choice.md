# Building an electronic product — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is UM66?
a) a melody integrated circuit that holds and plays a tune b) Only a price c) Only decoration d) Nothing to do with this lesson
**Answer: a) a melody integrated circuit that holds and plays a tune**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is perforated board?
a) Only history b) a board with a grid of holes for assembling electronic circuits c) Only storage d) A kind of glue
**Answer: b) a board with a grid of holes for assembling electronic circuits**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is microswitch?
a) Only old tools b) Only painting c) a small switch worked by a light press, used here to trigger the sound d) Only a price
**Answer: c) a small switch worked by a light press, used here to trigger the sound**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson teach, in one line?
a) Only decoration b) Nothing to do with this lesson c) Only history d) an electronic product is built by assembling a tested circuit on board: Iran's musical warning box joins a UM66 melody IC, a transistor and a speaker on perforated board, powered by a battery through a microswitch.
**Answer: d) an electronic product is built by assembling a tested circuit on board: Iran's musical warning box joins a UM66 melody IC, a transistor and a speaker on perforated board, powered by a battery through a microswitch.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** How does Iran's book teach this?
a) As theory only, never practised b) Only in a glossary c) Only as an exam d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Persian
**Answer: d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Persian**
