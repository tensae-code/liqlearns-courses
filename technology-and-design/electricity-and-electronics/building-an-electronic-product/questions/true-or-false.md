# Building an electronic product — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** UM66 is a melody integrated circuit that holds and plays a tune.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Perforated board has nothing to do with this lesson.
**Answer: False** — It is part of the lesson: a board with a grid of holes for assembling electronic circuits.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Microswitch is a small switch worked by a light press, used here to trigger the sound.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.
