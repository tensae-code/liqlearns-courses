# Lesson plan — Building an electronic product

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Electricity and Electronics · **Lesson:** Building an electronic product
**Place in the curriculum:** Electricity and Electronics (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Iran expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** an electronic product is built by assembling a tested circuit on board: Iran's musical warning box joins a UM66 melody IC, a transistor and a speaker on perforated board, powered by a battery through a microswitch.
**This lesson's own level:** stocked from Iran's *Work & Technology, Grade 8* (student book) — see lesson/sources.md. Iran counts in the acceptance count (student books, not evidence-only).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **The sound circuit** — The product's heart is a sound circuit: the UM66 melody integrated circuit holds the tune; the transistor strengthens its signal; the speaker turns the signal into sound.
2. **Assembling on perforated board** — The circuit is assembled on perforated board in ordered steps: parts placed and joined, then the battery connected as the power source and the microswitch fitted as the control — press, and the box sounds its warning tune.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
