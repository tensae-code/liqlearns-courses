# Building an electronic product

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Iran's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — The sound circuit
The product's heart is a sound circuit: the UM66 melody integrated circuit holds the tune; the transistor strengthens its signal; the speaker turns the signal into sound. Each part's job is known from the components lesson — the build is where the jobs combine into one working chain.

## Sub lesson 2 — Assembling on perforated board
The circuit is assembled on perforated board in ordered steps: parts placed and joined, then the battery connected as the power source and the microswitch fitted as the control — press, and the box sounds its warning tune. Iran's Grade 8 module runs the assembly as steps 1–3 of its musical-box project, so the product grows in a checked order, not all at once.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: an electronic product is built by assembling a tested circuit on board: Iran's musical warning box joins a UM66 melody IC, a transistor and a speaker on perforated board, powered by a battery through a microswitch. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
