# Hands-on — Electrical circuits — parts and types

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## Draw both circuits
Draw one lamp and a switch in series style (one path) and two lamps in parallel, using norm symbols. Say what happens in each when one lamp fails.

## Trace a real circuit
Choose a pictured device — the electric bell is the course's own example. Trace its circuit from source to load and name every part's job.
