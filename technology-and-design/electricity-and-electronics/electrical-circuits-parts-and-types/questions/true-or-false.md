# Electrical circuits — parts and types — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Series circuit is a circuit whose parts share a single path for the current.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Parallel circuit has nothing to do with this lesson.
**Answer: False** — It is part of the lesson: a circuit whose loads each have their own path.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Load is the part of a circuit that does the work — a lamp, a bell, a motor.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.
