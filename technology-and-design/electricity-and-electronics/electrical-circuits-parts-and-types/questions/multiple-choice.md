# Electrical circuits — parts and types — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is series circuit?
a) a circuit whose parts share a single path for the current b) Only a price c) Only decoration d) Nothing to do with this lesson
**Answer: a) a circuit whose parts share a single path for the current**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is parallel circuit?
a) Only history b) a circuit whose loads each have their own path c) Only storage d) A kind of glue
**Answer: b) a circuit whose loads each have their own path**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is load?
a) Only old tools b) Only painting c) the part of a circuit that does the work — a lamp, a bell, a motor d) Only a price
**Answer: c) the part of a circuit that does the work — a lamp, a bell, a motor**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson teach, in one line?
a) Only decoration b) Nothing to do with this lesson c) Only history d) a circuit is a closed path with parts that have jobs: source, conductors, control and load — and circuits join in series or in parallel, drawn with norm symbols, so that bells ring, lamps light and appliances work as drawn.
**Answer: d) a circuit is a closed path with parts that have jobs: source, conductors, control and load — and circuits join in series or in parallel, drawn with norm symbols, so that bells ring, lamps light and appliances work as drawn.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** How does Cuba's book teach this?
a) As theory only, never practised b) Only in a glossary c) Only as an exam d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Spanish
**Answer: d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Spanish**
