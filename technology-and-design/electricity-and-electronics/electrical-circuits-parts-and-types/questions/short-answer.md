# Electrical circuits — parts and types — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In your own words, what is series circuit?
**Answer:** A circuit whose parts share a single path for the current.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In your own words, what is load?
**Answer:** The part of a circuit that does the work — a lamp, a bell, a motor.
