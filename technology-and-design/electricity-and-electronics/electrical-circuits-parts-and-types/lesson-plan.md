# Lesson plan — Electrical circuits — parts and types

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Electricity and Electronics · **Lesson:** Electrical circuits — parts and types
**Place in the curriculum:** Electricity and Electronics (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Cuba expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** a circuit is a closed path with parts that have jobs: source, conductors, control and load — and circuits join in series or in parallel, drawn with norm symbols, so that bells ring, lamps light and appliances work as drawn.
**This lesson's own level:** stocked from Cuba's *Programa: Educación Laboral — 8vo grado* and the Cuba books in lesson/sources.md — see lesson/sources.md. ## Sub lessons
1. **Parts of a circuit; series and parallel** — Every circuit has its parts — energy source, conductors, a control such as a switch, and the load that does the work. Joined in series, the parts share one path; in parallel, each load has its own. Cuba's books teach both arrangements with the norm symbols that draw them.
2. **Circuits at work** — The Grade 8 course lands the circuit in real devices: how the induction cooker, the microwave oven, energy-saving bulbs and LED lamps work, and the installing of an electric bell. The 1989 book adds the quantities — intensity, resistance, voltage, power — and the transformer and fluorescent lamp.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
