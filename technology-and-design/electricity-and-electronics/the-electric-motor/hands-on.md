# Hands-on — The electric motor

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: Faraday's first motor (1821) to modern motors and generators, and the four kinds of transmission (mechanical, electric, pneumatic, hydraulic). Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
