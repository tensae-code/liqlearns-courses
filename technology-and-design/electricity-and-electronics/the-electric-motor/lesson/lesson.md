# The electric motor

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Mongolia's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — The electric motor
Mongolia's book teaches it this way (*Design Technology 9 (Mongolia)*, printed p. 80): Faraday's first motor (1821) to modern motors and generators, and the four kinds of transmission (mechanical, electric, pneumatic, hydraulic). The learner works through the named materials and steps in order, saying what each step does before doing the next one.

## Sub lesson 2 — The electric motor — doing it yourself
Now the learner does the whole task unaided: Faraday's first motor (1821) to modern motors and generators, and the four kinds of transmission (mechanical, electric, pneumatic, hydraulic). Working from the first sub lesson's sequence, the learner plans the make, works through it step by step, and checks the finished piece against what Mongolia's book shows.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: The electric motor — Faraday's first motor (1821) to modern motors and generators, and the four kinds of transmission (mechanical, electric, pneumatic, hydraulic). Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
