# The electric motor — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Say in one sentence what this lesson teaches.
**Answer:** The electric motor — Faraday's first motor (1821) to modern motors and generators, and the four kinds of transmission (mechanical, electric, pneumatic, hydraulic).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name one material, tool or step this lesson works with.
**Answer:** Any one named in the lesson text — for example, the first step of sub lesson 1: The electric motor.
