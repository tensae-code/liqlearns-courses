# Lesson plan — The simple electric circuit

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Electricity and Electronics · **Lesson:** The simple electric circuit
**Place in the curriculum:** Electricity and Electronics (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Jamaica expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** a simple circuit is a complete loop — battery, conductors, bulb and switch — around which current flows; break the loop anywhere and the flow stops.

## Sub lessons
1. **Where electrical energy works** — Electrical energy runs the devices of daily life — fans, flashlights, calculators, radios — and every one of them is a circuit.
2. **The components of a circuit** — Four parts make the simple circuit: the battery gives the energy, conductors carry it, the bulb uses it as light, and the switch controls it.
3. **Types of circuits, and the complete loop** — Circuits differ in how their parts are joined, but every working circuit shares one rule: the loop must be complete.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name devices that use electrical energy and say what they share.
2. Name the four components of a simple circuit and each one's job, with a diagram.
3. Explain why current flows only in a complete circuit, and what any break does.
## What you need
A battery, a small bulb, wires and a simple switch (or a clear circuit diagram).
