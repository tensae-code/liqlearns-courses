# The simple electric circuit — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 3) An open switch stops the current in a simple circuit.**
**Answer: True — It breaks the loop.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2) The switch gives the circuit its energy.**
**Answer: False — The battery is the source; the switch only controls the loop.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Fans, calculators and radios all contain circuits.**
**Answer: True**
