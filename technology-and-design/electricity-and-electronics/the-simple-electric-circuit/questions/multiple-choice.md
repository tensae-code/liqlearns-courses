# The simple electric circuit — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 2) Which component is the energy source of a simple circuit?**
a) The bulb b) The switch c) The battery d) The wire
**Answer: c) The battery**

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Conductors in a circuit are usually…**
a) Batteries b) Wires that carry the current c) Switches d) Bulbs
**Answer: b) Wires that carry the current**

**3. (Sub lesson 2) The bulb's job is to…**
a) Push the current b) Turn electrical energy into light c) Break the loop d) Store energy
**Answer: b) Turn electrical energy into light**

**4. (Sub lesson 3) Current flows when the circuit is…**
a) Open b) Broken c) A complete loop d) Drawn neatly
**Answer: c) A complete loop**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Which of these devices works by an electric circuit?**
a) A candle b) A flashlight c) A wood fire d) A kite
**Answer: b) A flashlight**
