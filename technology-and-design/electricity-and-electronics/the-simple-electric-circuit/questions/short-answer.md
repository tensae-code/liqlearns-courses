# The simple electric circuit — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, or by doing the step named.

**1. (Sub lesson 3) A flashlight's bulb goes dark. Give three different circuit faults that could cause it.**
**Answer:** Open switch (loop broken), a loose/disconnected wire, or a dead/flat battery (any three breaks of the loop or source failures).

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Draw the simple circuit in words: name the loop in order.**
**Answer:** Battery → conductor → switch → conductor → bulb → conductor → back to the battery — one complete loop.
