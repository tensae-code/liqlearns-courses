# Building circuits — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson teach?
a) Building circuits — the breadboard (2.54 mm hole spacing for DIP chips), universal strip boards, soldering in sequence, and covering wire joints with heat-shrink tube b) Only how to draw pictures c) Nothing practical d) A song to memorise

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Sub lesson 1 is about…
a) Building circuits b) Cooking lunch c) A sports match d) The weather
