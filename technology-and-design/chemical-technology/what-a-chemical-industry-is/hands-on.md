# Hands-on — What a chemical industry is

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## Work the topic
Carry out the topic's core task on a fresh example: raw materials (inputs) changed by a process into products (outputs), the industry's place in the economy, and the classes of chemical materials (basic, ... consumer chemicals such as soaps and detergents). Work in order, naming each part or step as you go.

## Check it
Check your finished work against the lesson's own rules. Find one thing that is wrong or weak, fix it, and say what you changed and why.
