# Video script — What a chemical industry is

A short demonstration video, made later, in a super-simple voice. The demonstrator works through the topic while the voice speaks.

This lesson is about what a chemical industry is.
Raw materials (inputs) changed by a process into products (outputs), the industry's place in the economy, and the classes of chemical materials (basic, ... consumer chemicals such as soaps and detergents).
Work step by step. Check your work as you go.
