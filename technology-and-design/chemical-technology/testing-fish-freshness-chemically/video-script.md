# Video script — Testing fish freshness chemically

A short demonstration video, made later, in a super-simple voice. The demonstrator works through the topic while the voice speaks.

---

This lesson is about testing fish freshness chemically.
Measuring the pH of fish flesh and reading the grade from the pH scale (6.2–5.9 best, falling as spoilage advances), with Salmonella named as a spoilage organism to guard against.
Work step by step. Check your work as you go.
