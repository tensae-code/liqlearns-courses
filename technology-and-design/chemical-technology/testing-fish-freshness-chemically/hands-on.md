# Hands-on — Testing fish freshness chemically

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## Work the topic
Carry out the topic's core task on a fresh example: measuring the pH of fish flesh and reading the grade from the pH scale (6.2–5.9 best, falling as spoilage advances), with Salmonella named as a spoilage organism to guard against. Work in order, naming each part or step as you go.

## Check it
Check your finished work against the lesson's own rules. Find one thing that is wrong or weak, fix it, and say what you changed and why.
