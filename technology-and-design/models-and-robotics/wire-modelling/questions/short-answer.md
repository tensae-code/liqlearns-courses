# Wire modelling — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Say in one sentence what this lesson teaches.
**Answer:** Wire modelling — how wire is rolled and drawn through dies; steel, aluminium and copper wire 1.5–2 mm; a decorative wire chain in ten steps and an elephant wire puzzle in eight steps on a template (§19).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name one material, tool or step this lesson works with.
**Answer:** Any one named in the lesson text — for example, the first step of sub lesson 1: Wire modelling.
