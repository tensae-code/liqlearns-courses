# Hands-on — Wire modelling

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: how wire is rolled and drawn through dies; steel, aluminium and copper wire 1.5–2 mm; a decorative wire chain in ten steps and an elephant wire puzzle in eight steps on a template (§19). Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
