# Building a walking robot — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Say in one sentence what this lesson teaches.
**Answer:** Building a walking robot — parts from 5×18×225 mm slats with Ø3 holes, assembling the frame in axonometric order, fitting wheels and the crank mechanism, and wiring its electric motor circuit.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name one material, tool or step this lesson works with.
**Answer:** Any one named in the lesson text — for example, the first step of sub lesson 1: Building a walking robot.
