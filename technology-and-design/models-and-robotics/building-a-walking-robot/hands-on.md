# Hands-on — Building a walking robot

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: parts from 5×18×225 mm slats with Ø3 holes, assembling the frame in axonometric order, fitting wheels and the crank mechanism, and wiring its electric motor circuit. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
