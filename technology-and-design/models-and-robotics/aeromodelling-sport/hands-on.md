# Hands-on — Aeromodelling sport

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: the FAI classes (F1 free flight, F2 control line, F3 radio control, F4 scale, F5 electric) and their limits — 25 kg, 500 dm², 250 g/dm², 42 V; aeromodelling in Mongolia since 1965. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
