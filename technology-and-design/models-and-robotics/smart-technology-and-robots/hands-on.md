# Hands-on — Smart technology and robots

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: the history (Čapek's "robot" 1921, the 1912 radio-controlled boat, Grey Walter's tortoise 1948), robot types — stationary and mobile — and their uses in industry, medicine, service and the home. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
