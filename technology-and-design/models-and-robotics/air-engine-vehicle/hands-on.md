# Hands-on — Air-engine vehicle

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: the car designer (конструктор) and the air engine: building a balloon- or sail-driven car, testing it and measuring the distance it runs (Lessons 33–34). Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
