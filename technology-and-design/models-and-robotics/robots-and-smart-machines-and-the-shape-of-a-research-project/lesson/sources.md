| robots and smart machines, and the shape of a research project in this field: the robot as a kind of machine controlled by a computer to do work automatically (industrial robots widely used in production — welding robots, car-assembly robots — and intelligent robots such as vacuum-cleaner robots, medical robots and reception robots), smart machines as machines able to monitor and adjust their own behaviour to complete a task, the ones with perception and data-processing ability from artificial intelligence (AI) or machine learning being the intelligent robots and smart machines of today (the robot Sophia communicating naturally and using facial expressions like a human; virtual assistants Siri, Alexa, Cortana and Google Assistant using speech recognition to follow instructions), the characteristics of robotics and smart-machine projects (they combine mechanics, electronics and information technology; the product carries artificial intelligence that can replace humans in processing information or directly join production; participants need knowledge of the related fields — mechanics, electronics, IT; and the technical dossier must include design calculations, mechanical fabrication drawings, electronic circuit design drawings, documents describing the product assembly process, software programs, product technical specifications and product user guides), the project's research sub-fields (biomechanics — researching and designing mechanical movements like those of living creatures in nature; perception systems — researching how machines think and process information like humans; control engineering — researching the control of actuator behaviour through feedback mechanisms to reach the desired target; machine learning — researching the mathematical algorithms on data; robot dynamics — researching the robot's movement), and the related contents (mechanics: materials for making the chassis and body, mechanical machining such as 3D printers, cutting and milling machines for suitable materials, and transmission and motion-conversion mechanisms — motors, gears, tracks — suited to the robot's working method, power source and weight; electronics: components and circuits receiving and processing signals, such as sensor circuits, motor-control circuits and microcontrollers, and assembling printed circuits — designing the schematic, connecting components and circuits so the product works to its function, safely; programming: the programming language suited to the computer or microcontroller type, and modular programming technique so the code is easy to follow, fix and extend with new features) (original string `robots and smart machines, and the shape of a research project in this field`) | Vietnam, *Chuyên đề học tập Công nghệ 12 — Công nghệ điện – điện tử*, printed p. 35 |
