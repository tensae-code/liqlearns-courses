# Modifying a mechatronic product — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Modifying a product is changing one part of an existing product to improve it.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Motor torque has nothing to do with this lesson.
**Answer: False** — It is part of the lesson: the turning strength of a motor, weighed in the worked examples.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Evaluating the modification is testing whether the changed product’s function truly improved.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.
