# Lesson plan — Modifying a mechatronic product

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Models and Robotics · **Lesson:** Modifying a mechatronic product
**Place in the curriculum:** Models and Robotics (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Malaysia expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** modify from the block diagram: sketch the part, make the improvement, and evaluate the changed product’s function.
**This lesson's own level:** stocked from Malaysia's *Reka Bentuk dan Teknologi* (RBT — Design and Technology) textbooks — see lesson/sources.md. Malaysia counts in the acceptance count (student books, not evidence-only).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **From diagram to change** — Modification starts from the block diagram: the part to be changed is sketched from it, so the improvement is aimed at the product’s logic, not guessed at its shell.
2. **Make and evaluate the improvement** — The improvement is made, and the modified product’s function is evaluated — the book’s worked examples even weigh motor torque, the turning strength the change must respect.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of Models and Robotics. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Sketch a part for change from a block diagram.
2. Evaluate a modified product’s function, including torque where it matters.
3. Explain how Malaysia's book teaches it.
## What you need
A block diagram from the previous lesson; sketch paper.
