# Modifying a mechatronic product

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Malaysia's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — From diagram to change
Modification starts from the block diagram: the part to be changed is sketched from it, so the improvement is aimed at the product’s logic, not guessed at its shell.

## Sub lesson 2 — Make and evaluate the improvement
The improvement is made, and the modified product’s function is evaluated — the book’s worked examples even weigh motor torque, the turning strength the change must respect. A modification is proved, not hoped.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: modify from the block diagram: sketch the part, make the improvement, and evaluate the changed product’s function. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.

## Do It Yourself
1. Do the Do it first task again, carefully this time, using everything the lesson taught.
2. Check your work against the lesson's own checks — fix one thing you find.
3. Keep your work: it is practice evidence, and the next lesson builds on it.
