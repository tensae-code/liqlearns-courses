# Hands-on — Drawing the net of the walking-toy body

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: Drawing the net of the walking-toy body with dimensions (45, 25, 40 mm) and building the finished toy from a drinks can. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
