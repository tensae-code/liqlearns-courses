# Making a balloon-powered car \u2014 multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What pushes the balloon car forward?
a) A motor b) The push-back of escaping air \u2014 reaction c) Magnets d) The wind outside
**Answer: b) The push-back of escaping air \u2014 reaction**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The air rushes out backward; the car moves\u2026
a) Backward too b) Forward \u2014 the opposite way c) Upward d) In circles only
**Answer: b) Forward \u2014 the opposite way**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The car's wheels are made from\u2026
a) Coins b) Bottle caps c) Corks only d) Paper discs
**Answer: b) Bottle caps**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** What are the wooden sticks for?
a) Fuel b) Axles for the wheels c) Steering d) Brakes
**Answer: b) Axles for the wheels**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** A car that veers sideways probably has\u2026
a) Too much air b) Crooked wheels or an off-centre balloon c) New tape d) A smooth floor
**Answer: b) Crooked wheels or an off-centre balloon**
