# Making a balloon-powered car \u2014 short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain reaction propulsion in one sentence.
**Answer:** Air rushing out of the balloon one way pushes the car the opposite way; that push-back is reaction.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the car's materials.
**Answer:** Cardboard, straws, bottle caps, wooden sticks, tape and a balloon.
