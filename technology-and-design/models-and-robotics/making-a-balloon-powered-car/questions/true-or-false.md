# Making a balloon-powered car \u2014 true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The balloon car is pulled by the escaping air.
**Answer: False** \u2014 It is pushed \u2014 the escaping air pushes back on the car, driving it forward.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Straight wheels help the car run straight.
**Answer: True** \u2014 Crooked wheels or an off-centre balloon make the run veer.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The car's run tests the build.
**Answer: True** \u2014 Distance and straightness grade the making, like every make's test.
