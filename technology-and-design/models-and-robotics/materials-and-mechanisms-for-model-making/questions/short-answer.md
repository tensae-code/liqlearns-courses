# Materials and mechanisms for model-making — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Say in one sentence what this lesson teaches.
**Answer:** Choosing materials per part (soft wood/plywood base you can nail and screw into, compressed insulation board, reusing "useless" materials), planning and economy of materials.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name one material, tool or step this lesson works with.
**Answer:** Any one named in the lesson text — for example, the first step of sub lesson 1: Materials and mechanisms for model-making.
