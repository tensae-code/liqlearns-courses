# Hands-on — Materials and mechanisms for model-making

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: choosing materials per part (soft wood/plywood base you can nail and screw into, compressed insulation board, reusing "useless" materials), planning and economy of materials. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
