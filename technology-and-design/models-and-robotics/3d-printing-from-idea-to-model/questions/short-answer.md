# 3D printing, from idea to model — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Say in one sentence what 3D printing is.
**Answer:** 3D printing builds a real object layer by layer from a digital model, adding material until the shape is complete.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Name the three stages from idea to printed object.
**Answer:** The idea is fixed, the idea is drawn as a 3D model at real size, and the model is checked and printed.
