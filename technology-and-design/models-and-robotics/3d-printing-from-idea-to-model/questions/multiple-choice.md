# 3D printing, from idea to model — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does a 3D printer build an object from?
a) A digital model, layer by layer b) A block it carves away c) A photograph only d) A mould filled once
**Answer: a) A digital model, layer by layer**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** How does 3D printing add material?
a) Layer by layer until the shape is complete b) All at once in one pour c) By gluing paper sheets only d) By bending metal
**Answer: a) Layer by layer until the shape is complete**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is a filament?
a) A thin plastic thread wound on a spool, melted and laid down in fine lines b) A kind of paper c) A metal wire for sewing d) A liquid paint
**Answer: a) A thin plastic thread wound on a spool, melted and laid down in fine lines**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Why does the printing material matter?
a) Different materials give different strength, flexibility and heat resistance b) It only changes the colour c) It makes the printer faster in every case d) It does not matter at all
**Answer: a) Different materials give different strength, flexibility and heat resistance**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** What must an idea become before it can be printed?
a) A printable 3D model at real size b) A song c) A paragraph of text only d) A photograph of another object
**Answer: a) A printable 3D model at real size**
