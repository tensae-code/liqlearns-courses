# Hands-on — 3D printing, from idea to model

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## Idea to model on paper
Pick one small object. Draw it from the front, the side and the top. Write its real measurements beside the drawings. That set of views and sizes is what a 3D model must hold.

## Filament check
If a spool of filament is available, look at it closely: how thick is the thread, how is it wound, what does its label say? If no spool is available, find those answers from a picture or the spool's packaging and write them down.
