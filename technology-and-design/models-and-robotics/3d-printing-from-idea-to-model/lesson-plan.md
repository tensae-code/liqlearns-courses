# Lesson plan — 3D printing, from idea to model

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Models and Robotics · **Lesson:** 3D printing, from idea to model
**Place in the curriculum:** Models and Robotics (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Kuwait expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** 3D printing builds a real object layer by layer from a digital model — and every print starts as an idea that is turned into a printable 3D model, on a printer type chosen for the job, in a printing material such as filament.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on a student book — Kuwait's *الذكاء الرقمي 8* (*Digital Intelligence, Grade 8, Term 2*), Ministry of Education, State of Kuwait (see lesson/sources.md). It counts in the acceptance count (student book, not evidence-only).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **What 3D printing is** — 3D printing makes a real, solid object from a digital design by adding material layer by layer, until the layers build the whole shape.
2. **Printer types and printing materials (filaments)** — Not all 3D printers work the same way.
3. **Turning an idea into a printable 3D model** — A print begins before the printer starts.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of Models and Robotics. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say what 3D printing is in your own words.
2. Name printer types and say what filaments are.
3. Put the steps from idea to printable 3D model in order.
## What you need
No materials are needed to learn the idea; if available, a 3D printer and a spool of filament to look at, or pictures of them.
