# Hands-on — Print quality and printing speed

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## Surface comparison
Compare two printed samples (or clear pictures of them). For each, judge: thin or thick layers? Write the evidence your eyes and fingers give.

## Choose for the job
For three jobs — a quick size test, a gift that will be handled every day, a spare part hidden inside a machine — choose layer height and speed for each, and write the reason in one sentence.
