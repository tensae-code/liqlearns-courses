# Print quality and printing speed — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Thick layers leave clearer ridges on a printed surface.
**Answer: True** — Fewer, thicker passes leave visible ridges.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Faster printing always gives a better finish.
**Answer: False** — Too much speed can blur detail and weaken layer bonding.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A quick prototype can fairly use thick layers and fast speed.
**Answer: True** — Its job is to be checked, not admired, so speed is a fair trade.
