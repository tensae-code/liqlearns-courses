# Print quality and printing speed — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is layer height?
a) The thickness of each printed layer b) The height of the printer c) The length of the filament d) The size of the printer bed
**Answer: a) The thickness of each printed layer**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What do thin layers give?
a) A smoother surface, with many more layers to print b) A rougher surface c) A taller printer d) No layers at all
**Answer: a) A smoother surface, with many more layers to print**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What happens when print speed rises?
a) The job finishes sooner b) The surface always gets smoother c) The model gets bigger d) The filament lasts forever
**Answer: a) The job finishes sooner**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** What can too much speed spoil?
a) Fine detail, sharp corners and how layers bond b) The colour of the room c) The name of the file d) Nothing at all
**Answer: a) Fine detail, sharp corners and how layers bond**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** For a final piece that will be seen and handled, choose…
a) Thin layers and a steadier speed b) The thickest layers and fastest speed c) No layers d) Speed only, with no check
**Answer: a) Thin layers and a steadier speed**
