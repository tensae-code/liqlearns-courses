# Print quality and printing speed — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Say in one sentence how layer height affects quality.
**Answer:** Thinner layers follow the shape more closely and give a smoother surface, but take longer because there are more of them.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** A learner needs a rough test piece in ten minutes. Which way do the settings go, and why?
**Answer:** Thick layers and faster speed, because the piece only needs to be checked, so finishing sooner matters more than a fine surface.
