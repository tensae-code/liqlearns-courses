# Robotics — how robots are built and move — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which name belongs to the automata age?
a) Jaquet-Droz b) Asimov the engineer c) No names are given d) A factory brand
**Answer: a) Jaquet-Droz**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What two firsts stand in the history?
a) The first car and plane b) The first programmable industrial robot and the first computer c) The first bulb and motor d) No firsts
**Answer: b) The first programmable industrial robot and the first computer**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Whose three laws stand over robotics?
a) Asimov's b) Newton's c) The school's d) No one's
**Answer: a) Asimov's**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** What holds a robot's components and modules?
a) The chassis / housing b) The antenna c) The battery alone d) The program
**Answer: a) The chassis / housing**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Which locomotion systems are named?
a) Only wheels b) Wings and fins c) Wheels, tracks, legs and conveyor belts d) None
**Answer: c) Wheels, tracks, legs and conveyor belts**
