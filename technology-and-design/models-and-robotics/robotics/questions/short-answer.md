# Robotics — how robots are built and move — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the history's landmarks.
**Answer:** Automata (Jaquet-Droz), the first computer, and the first programmable industrial robot — with Asimov's three laws over the field.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How is a robot built to move?
**Answer:** A chassis holds components and modules; gear-wheel or worm transmissions produce movement; wheels, tracks, legs or belts move it over its surfaces.
