# Robotics — how robots are built and move — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Robots have a history that begins before computers, in automata.
**Answer: True** — Jaquet-Droz and the automata open the line.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** One locomotion system suits every surface.
**Answer: False** — Wheels, tracks, legs and belts are each fitted to different surfaces.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Transmissions with gear wheels or worm drives produce the robot's movement.
**Answer: True** — That is the construction the lesson teaches.
