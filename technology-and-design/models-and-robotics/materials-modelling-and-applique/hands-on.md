# Hands-on — Materials, modelling and applique

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: Section 1 "All about me": the course materials, modelling a friend, applique self-portrait (Uyghur ed.). Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
