# Hands-on — The yurt model

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: the frame named part by part (qanat, shanyrak, uyk, kerege, esik); a model yurt raised over a plastic bottle and set in an ethnoaul (Topics 6–8). Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
