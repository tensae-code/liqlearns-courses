# The yurt model

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Kazakhstan's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Yurt model construction
Kazakhstan's books teach it this way (the student book, printed pp. 25–33): The frame named part by part (qanat, shanyrak, uyk, kerege, esik); a model yurt raised over a plastic bottle and set in an ethnoaul (Topics 6–8). The learner works through the named materials and steps in order, saying what each step does before doing the next one.

## Sub lesson 2 — Yurt model
The Kazakh treatment continues and completes the topic: The yurt's parts named on a diagram (shanyrak, uyk, kerege, bosaga) and a yurt model made (Lesson 10). The learner finishes the make, checks it against the book's own sequence, and fixes one thing before presenting the work.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: The yurt model — the frame named part by part (qanat, shanyrak, uyk, kerege, esik); a model yurt raised over a plastic bottle and set in an ethnoaul (Topics 6–8). Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.

1. Do the Do it first task again, carefully this time, using everything the lesson taught.
2. Check your work against the lesson's own checks — fix one thing you find.
3. Keep your work: it is practice evidence, and the next lesson builds on it.
