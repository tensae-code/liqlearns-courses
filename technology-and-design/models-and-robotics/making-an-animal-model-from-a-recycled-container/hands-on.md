# Hands-on — Making an animal model from a recycled container

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: an octopus from a plastic cup, cut, painted and finished with glued paper eyes. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
