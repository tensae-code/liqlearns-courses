# The model's strength from the inside — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Say in one sentence what infill is.
**Answer:** Infill is the repeating pattern printed inside a model's walls instead of solid material.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the two choices that shape infill.
**Answer:** The pattern — the shape of the filling — and the density — how much of the inside is filled.
