# The model's strength from the inside — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Infill supports the outer walls of a printed model from inside.
**Answer: True** — The filling holds the walls apart and supported.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Low density makes a model stronger than high density.
**Answer: False** — Low density leaves wide spaces and a lighter, weaker model.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A display model that only sits on a shelf can fairly use light infill.
**Answer: True** — It carries no load, so light infill is a fair choice.
