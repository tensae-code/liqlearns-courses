# The model's strength from the inside — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is infill?
a) The inside filling pattern of a printed model b) The paint on the outside c) The printer's power cable d) The name of the model file
**Answer: a) The inside filling pattern of a printed model**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Why is a model not printed solid inside?
a) Infill saves material and time and keeps the model light while supporting the walls b) Solid is impossible in every case c) The printer cannot move inside d) It makes the model invisible
**Answer: a) Infill saves material and time and keeps the model light while supporting the walls**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is infill density?
a) How much of the inside is filled b) The colour of the filling c) The speed of the printer d) The size of the bed
**Answer: a) How much of the inside is filled**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** What does a high density give?
a) A model closer to solid — heavier and stronger b) A lighter, weaker model c) No inside at all d) A smaller model
**Answer: a) A model closer to solid — heavier and stronger**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** A hook that must hold a heavy bag needs…
a) A stronger pattern and higher density where the force falls b) The lightest infill possible c) No infill d) Infill chosen by colour
**Answer: a) A stronger pattern and higher density where the force falls**
