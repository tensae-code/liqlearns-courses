# Lesson plan — The model's strength from the inside

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Models and Robotics · **Lesson:** The model's strength from the inside
**Place in the curriculum:** Models and Robotics (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Kuwait expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** a printed model is not solid inside — it is filled with a pattern called infill, and the pattern and density of that filling decide much of how strong or weak the finished model is.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on a student book — Kuwait's *الذكاء الرقمي 8* (*Digital Intelligence, Grade 8, Term 2*), Ministry of Education, State of Kuwait (see lesson/sources.md). It counts in the acceptance count (student book, not evidence-only).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **What infill is** — Infill is the inside filling of a printed model.
2. **Pattern and density** — Two choices shape the infill.
3. **Choosing infill for strength** — Choose infill for the job the model must do.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of Models and Robotics. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say what infill is in your own words.
2. Explain pattern and density as the two infill choices.
3. Choose infill for a named job and give the reason.
## What you need
No materials are needed; if available, a cut-open or partly printed model showing its inside pattern.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — the opening doing task in lesson/lesson.md.
2. **The explanation** — the sub lessons in order (lesson/lesson.md).
   - ADD IMAGE: a picture of a picture of a printed block cut open, showing its outer walls and the grid pattern of infill inside, with one side filled sparsely and the other densely — the student points to the walls, the pattern and the denser side and names each.
   - ADD GIF: a GIF of a GIF of a printer laying down infill lines inside a model's walls, the pattern growing row by row — the student watches the inside fill and names the pattern.
