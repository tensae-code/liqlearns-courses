# Hands-on — The model's strength from the inside

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## Read the inside
Look at a cut-open print or a slicer preview of infill. Sketch the pattern you see, and estimate the density — mostly empty, about half, or nearly solid.

## Infill for the job
For three models — a shelf ornament, a hook that must hold a bag, a wheel that will roll under weight — choose low, medium or high density for each and write the reason.
