# City of the future

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Kazakhstan's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Model of the city of the future
Kazakhstan's books teach it this way (the student book, printed p. 122): A group model built from cardboard, wire, clay and found materials, with one clear main object (viewed by me). The learner works through the named materials and steps in order, saying what each step does before doing the next one.

## Sub lesson 2 — City of the future
The Kazakh treatment continues and completes the topic: Research — city planning, landscape design and architecture, architectural styles, small architectural forms, the master plan (генеральный план). Realisation — Smart City, the Venus Project, extraordinary buildings, digital architecture, bridges as structures. The learner finishes the make, checks it against the book's own sequence, and fixes one thing before presenting the work.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: City of the future — a group model built from cardboard, wire, clay and found materials, with one clear main object (viewed by me). Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.

## Do It Yourself
1. Do the Do it first task again, carefully this time, using everything the lesson taught.
2. Check your work against the lesson's own checks — fix one thing you find.
3. Keep your work: it is practice evidence, and the next lesson builds on it.
