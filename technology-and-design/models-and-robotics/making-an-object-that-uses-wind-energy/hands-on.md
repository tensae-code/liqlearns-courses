# Hands-on \u2014 Making an object that uses wind energy

Real practice \u2014 this subject is learned by doing.

## Build the windsock
Build a windsock from plastic bags, wooden sticks and bottles. Mount it where air moves freely and watch it for five minutes, noting direction and how fully it flies.

## Wind log
Three times in a day, record your windsock: direction pointed, flying state (limp, half, full). Compare the three readings and write what changed in the wind.
