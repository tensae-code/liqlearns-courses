# Making an object that uses wind energy \u2014 short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How do you read wind strength from a windsock?
**Answer:** By how fully it flies and lifts: limp means weak wind, fully extended and horizontal means strong wind.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What energy does the windsock use, and what work does it do?
**Answer:** Wind energy; it does the work of indicating \u2014 showing direction and strength at a glance.
