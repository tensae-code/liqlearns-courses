# Making an object that uses wind energy \u2014 true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A windsock points into the wind's eye, against the flow.
**Answer: False** \u2014 It streams downwind \u2014 the wind enters the wide mouth and the sock points the way the wind is going.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A limp, hanging windsock shows a weak wind.
**Answer: True** \u2014 With little wind energy, the sock cannot lift or fly.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Windsocks help pilots judge the wind before landing or take-off.
**Answer: True** \u2014 That airfield use is why the instrument exists.
