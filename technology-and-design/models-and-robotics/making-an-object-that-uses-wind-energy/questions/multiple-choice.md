# Making an object that uses wind energy \u2014 multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does a windsock show?
a) Temperature and rain b) The wind's direction and strength c) The time d) Only direction, never strength
**Answer: b) The wind's direction and strength**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A windsock flying full and horizontal means\u2026
a) No wind b) A strong wind c) Rain is coming d) The sock is broken
**Answer: b) A strong wind**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Where are real windsocks used?
a) In kitchens b) At airfields, so pilots can read the wind c) In libraries d) Under water
**Answer: b) At airfields, so pilots can read the wind**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The classroom windsock is built from\u2026
a) Metal and glass b) Plastic bags, wooden sticks and bottles c) Clay and string d) Paper and cork only
**Answer: b) Plastic bags, wooden sticks and bottles**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Why must the sock swing freely?
a) For decoration b) So it can turn to face the wind and show direction c) To make noise d) It must not swing
**Answer: b) So it can turn to face the wind and show direction**
