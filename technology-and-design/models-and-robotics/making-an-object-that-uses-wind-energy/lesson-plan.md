# Lesson plan \u2014 Making an object that uses wind energy

**Course:** Technology and Design \u00b7 **Title:** Models and Robotics \u00b7 **Lesson:** Making an object that uses wind energy
**Place in the curriculum:** Models and Robotics (CURRICULUM.md) \u2014 stocked in the Gabon expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** a windsock (manche \u00e0 air) uses wind energy to show the wind's direction and strength \u2014 built from plastic bags, wooden sticks and bottles, as at airfields.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on teacher-guide evidence only \u2014 Gabon's *Super Efficace* Sciences teacher guides (EDIG / \u00c9dicef, Institut P\u00e9dagogique National, 2024) are the books in hand for this topic (see lesson/sources.md). Teacher guides are evidence, counted nowhere in the acceptance count.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on \u2014 an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of \u2026" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **What a windsock shows** \u2014 Direction by where it points; strength by how fully it flies.
2. **Building the windsock** \u2014 Plastic bags, wooden sticks and bottles become a flying indicator.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson \u2014 no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of Models and Robotics. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say what a windsock shows and where real ones are used.
2. Build a windsock from the three materials.
3. Test it: pointing and flying with the wind.
## What you need
Plastic bags, wooden sticks, bottles, tape or string, and a breezy place to test.

## The run of the lesson (in order)

2. **The explanation** \u2014 the sub lessons in order (lesson/lesson.md).
