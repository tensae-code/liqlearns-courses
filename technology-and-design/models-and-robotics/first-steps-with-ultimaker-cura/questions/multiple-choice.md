# First steps with UltiMaker Cura — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does a slicer program do?
a) Cuts the model into thin layers and writes printer instructions b) Draws the first sketch of an idea c) Melts the filament itself d) Paints the finished object
**Answer: a) Cuts the model into thin layers and writes printer instructions**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Why does a printer need a slicer?
a) It cannot read the 3D model on its own b) It has no power without one c) It only prints photographs d) It needs music to work
**Answer: a) It cannot read the 3D model on its own**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Where does an imported model appear in Cura?
a) Standing on the virtual printer bed b) Inside the filament spool c) On the classroom wall d) In the printed book
**Answer: a) Standing on the virtual printer bed**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** What should you check when a model is imported?
a) Its size, which way up it stands, and that it sits flat on the bed b) Only its colour c) Only its name d) Nothing at all
**Answer: a) Its size, which way up it stands, and that it sits flat on the bed**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** What do you do in the preview before printing?
a) Step through the layers and check time and material b) Skip it and hope c) Delete the model d) Turn the printer off
**Answer: a) Step through the layers and check time and material**
