# First steps with UltiMaker Cura — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Say in one sentence what a slicer does.
**Answer:** A slicer cuts a 3D model into thin layers and writes the instructions a printer follows to build them.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name two checks you make on an imported model in Cura.
**Answer:** Any two of: right size, standing the right way up, sitting flat on the bed.
