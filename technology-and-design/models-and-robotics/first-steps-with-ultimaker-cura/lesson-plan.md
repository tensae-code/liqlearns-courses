# Lesson plan — First steps with UltiMaker Cura

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Models and Robotics · **Lesson:** First steps with UltiMaker Cura
**Place in the curriculum:** Models and Robotics (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Kuwait expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** before a printer can build anything, a slicer program — UltiMaker Cura — must turn the 3D model into thin layers and printer instructions; this lesson covers importing a model into Cura and preparing it for printing.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on a student book — Kuwait's *الذكاء الرقمي 8* (*Digital Intelligence, Grade 8, Term 2*), Ministry of Education, State of Kuwait (see lesson/sources.md). It counts in the acceptance count (student book, not evidence-only).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **What a slicer does** — A slicer is the bridge between the model and the printer.
2. **Importing a 3D model into Cura** — The first step in UltiMaker Cura is to import the model file: open it, and the model appears standing on Cura's virtual printer bed.
3. **Preparing the model for printing** — With the model placed, choose the print settings for the job and let Cura slice it.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of Models and Robotics. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say what a slicer does in your own words.
2. Put the steps for importing a model into Cura in order.
3. Say what you check in the preview before a print is sent.
## What you need
A computer with UltiMaker Cura if available; if not, follow the steps on paper with a picture of the Cura screen.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — the opening doing task in lesson/lesson.md.
2. **The explanation** — the sub lessons in order (lesson/lesson.md).
   - ADD IMAGE: a picture of a picture of the UltiMaker Cura screen with a 3D model standing on the virtual printer bed, its size and position labels showing — the student points to the model, the bed, and the size labels and names each one.
   - ADD GIF: a GIF of a GIF of a 3D model being sliced: a line sweeps up the model and thin layers appear one by one — the student watches the model become layers.
