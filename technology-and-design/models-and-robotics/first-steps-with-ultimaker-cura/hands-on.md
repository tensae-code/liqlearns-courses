# Hands-on — First steps with UltiMaker Cura

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## Walk the import
In Cura (or on a printed picture of its screen), follow one model through: import it, place it flat on the bed, and say aloud each check — size, way up, sitting flat.

## Preview detective
Slice a model and step through its preview ten layers at a time. Write down the layer where the shape changes most, and what changes there.
