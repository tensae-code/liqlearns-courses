# Hands-on — Transport model-making

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: kinds of transport vehicles; developing a model of a transport vehicle (sketch, choosing materials); creating the model: making the separate parts, assembly and finishing (§§23–25). Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
