# Transport model-making — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Say in one sentence what this lesson teaches.
**Answer:** Transport model-making — kinds of transport vehicles; developing a model of a transport vehicle (sketch, choosing materials); creating the model: making the separate parts, assembly and finishing (§§23–25).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name one material, tool or step this lesson works with.
**Answer:** Any one named in the lesson text — for example, the first step of sub lesson 1: Transport model-making.
