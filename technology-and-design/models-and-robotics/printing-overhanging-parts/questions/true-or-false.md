# Printing overhanging parts — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** An overhang failure is a matter of geometry — a layer asked to float.
**Answer: True** — The melted line has nothing to hold it, so it droops or falls.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Supports stay on the model forever as part of its design.
**Answer: False** — Supports are temporary and are broken or cut away after printing.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Supports cost time and material, so they are used where needed, not everywhere.
**Answer: True** — They also can leave marks where they touch the model.
