# Printing overhanging parts — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why does a steep overhang fail?
a) Its layers have nothing below to rest on, so they droop or fall b) The filament is the wrong colour c) The printer is too tall d) The model file is too old
**Answer: a) Its layers have nothing below to rest on, so they droop or fall**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What kind of slope prints without supports?
a) A gentle slope, where each layer rests mostly on the one below b) A layer floating in mid-air c) An upside-down ceiling d) No slope can ever print
**Answer: a) A gentle slope, where each layer rests mostly on the one below**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What are supports in 3D printing?
a) Temporary scaffolding printed under an overhang and removed afterwards b) Permanent decorations c) The printer's legs d) A kind of filament paint
**Answer: a) Temporary scaffolding printed under an overhang and removed afterwards**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** When are supports removed?
a) After the print finishes b) Before printing starts c) Never — they are the model d) During slicing only
**Answer: a) After the print finishes**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Name a way to use supports well.
a) Turn the model so less of it overhangs b) Support every part always c) Never check the surface afterwards d) Print supports in solid gold
**Answer: a) Turn the model so less of it overhangs**
