# Printing overhanging parts — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Say in one sentence why overhangs fail without support.
**Answer:** Each layer must rest on the one below, and a steep overhang leaves a layer with nothing to rest on, so it droops or falls.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Say what a support is in one sentence.
**Answer:** A support is temporary scaffolding printed under an overhang so its layers have something to rest on, removed after the print.
