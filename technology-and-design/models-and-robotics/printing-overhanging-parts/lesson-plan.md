# Lesson plan — Printing overhanging parts

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Models and Robotics · **Lesson:** Printing overhanging parts
**Place in the curriculum:** Models and Robotics (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Kuwait expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** a printed layer needs the layer below to rest on — parts that lean out too far (overhangs) droop or fail unless temporary supports are printed under them and removed afterwards.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on a student book — Kuwait's *الذكاء الرقمي 8* (*Digital Intelligence, Grade 8, Term 2*), Ministry of Education, State of Kuwait (see lesson/sources.md). It counts in the acceptance count (student book, not evidence-only).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **Why overhangs fail without support** — Each new layer is laid on the layer below.
2. **What supports are** — Supports are temporary scaffolding the printer builds under an overhang.
3. **Using supports well** — Supports cost time and material, and where they touch the model they can leave marks, so they are used where they are needed, not everywhere.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of Models and Robotics. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say why an overhang fails without support.
2. Say what supports are and when they are removed.
3. Name two ways to use supports well.
## What you need
No materials are needed; if available, a printed model with an overhang, with its supports still attached or just removed.

2. **The explanation** — the sub lessons in order (lesson/lesson.md).
   - ADD IMAGE: a picture of a picture of a printed shape with a steep overhang, held up by a lattice of thin supports underneath, with one support partly broken away — the student points to the overhang and to the supports and says what each does.
