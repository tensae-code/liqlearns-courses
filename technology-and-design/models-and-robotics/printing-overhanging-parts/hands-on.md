# Hands-on — Printing overhanging parts

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## Spot the overhang
Look at a model or its slicer preview. Mark every part that leans out steeply, and for each one say: supported by the layer below, or needs a support?

## Turn and save
Take one overhanging model and try two positions on the bed in the slicer (or on paper). Which position needs fewer supports? Write the saving.
