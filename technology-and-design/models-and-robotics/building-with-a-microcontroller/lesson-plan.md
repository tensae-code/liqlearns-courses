# Lesson plan — Building with a microcontroller

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Models and Robotics · **Lesson:** Building with a microcontroller
**Place in the curriculum:** Models and Robotics (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Malaysia expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** build with a microcontroller in order: sketch, simulate in software, connect inputs and outputs, program simply, test and improve.
**This lesson's own level:** stocked from Malaysia's *Reka Bentuk dan Teknologi* (RBT — Design and Technology) textbooks — see lesson/sources.md. Malaysia counts in the acceptance count (student books, not evidence-only).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **Circuit, simulation, connections** — Building runs in safe order: the electronic circuit is sketched, a working circuit simulation is built with special software, and only then are input and output circuits connected to the microcontroller itself.
2. **Program, test, improve** — Simple programming is written for those connections; the circuit is tested and evaluated on its behaviour, and improvements are proposed.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of Models and Robotics. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Sketch and simulate an electronic circuit.
2. Connect input/output circuits, program them and test the result.
3. Explain how Malaysia's book teaches it.
## What you need
Circuit simulation software (or diagrams); a microcontroller board if available.
