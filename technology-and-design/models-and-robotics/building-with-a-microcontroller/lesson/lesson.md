# Building with a microcontroller

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Malaysia's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Circuit, simulation, connections
Building runs in safe order: the electronic circuit is sketched, a working circuit simulation is built with special software, and only then are input and output circuits connected to the microcontroller itself.

## Sub lesson 2 — Program, test, improve
Simple programming is written for those connections; the circuit is tested and evaluated on its behaviour, and improvements are proposed. Simulation first means the real board meets a tested idea.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: build with a microcontroller in order: sketch, simulate in software, connect inputs and outputs, program simply, test and improve. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.

## Do It Yourself
1. Do the Do it first task again, carefully this time, using everything the lesson taught.
2. Check your work against the lesson's own checks — fix one thing you find.
3. Keep your work: it is practice evidence, and the next lesson builds on it.
