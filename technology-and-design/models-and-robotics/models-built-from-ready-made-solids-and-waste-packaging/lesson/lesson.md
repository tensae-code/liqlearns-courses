# Models built from ready-made solids and waste packaging

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Mongolia's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Models built from ready-made solids and waste packaging
Mongolia's book teaches it this way (*Art & Technology 4 (Mongolia)*, printed p. 49): reading a vehicle as cylinders and cuboids, and the design process — draw the model, prepare tools and materials, build to the plan, exhibit. The learner works through the named materials and steps in order, saying what each step does before doing the next one.

## Sub lesson 2 — Models built from ready-made solids and waste packaging — doing it yourself
Now the learner does the whole task unaided: reading a vehicle as cylinders and cuboids, and the design process — draw the model, prepare tools and materials, build to the plan, exhibit. Working from the first sub lesson's sequence, the learner plans the make, works through it step by step, and checks the finished piece against what Mongolia's book shows.
