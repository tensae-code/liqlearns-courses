# Hands-on — Models built from ready-made solids and waste packaging

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: reading a vehicle as cylinders and cuboids, and the design process — draw the model, prepare tools and materials, build to the plan, exhibit. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
