# Reading and drawing the block diagram (gambar rajah blok) of an existing mechatronic product — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In your own words, what is gambar rajah blok?
**Answer:** A block diagram.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In your own words, what is the control block?
**Answer:** The block that decides what the product does.
