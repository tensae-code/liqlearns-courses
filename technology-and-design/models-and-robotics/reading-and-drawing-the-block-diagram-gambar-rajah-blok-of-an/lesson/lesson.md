# Reading and drawing the block diagram (gambar rajah blok) of an existing mechatronic product

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Malaysia's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Reading a product as blocks
A block diagram — gambar rajah blok — reads a mechatronic product as three kinds of block: input blocks sense, the control block decides, output blocks act. Any product, however complex, first surrenders to the three.

## Sub lesson 2 — Drawing the diagram
The learner draws the diagram of an existing product: blocks placed, arrows showing what flows between them. The drawing is the product’s logic made visible — and the first step to changing it.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: a block diagram (gambar rajah blok) reduces a mechatronic product to input, control and output blocks — read first, then drawn. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
