# Hands-on — An animal model from shaped stiff card

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: a chick and a turtle built from coiled card strips, parts labelled and assembled (body, head, wing, tail, legs). Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
