# Mechatronic design — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Mechatronic design is design joining mechanical transmission, electronics and microcontroller control.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A sensor has nothing to do with this lesson.
**Answer: False** — It is part of the lesson: an electronic component that detects and reports.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Microcontroller control is the control system directing the product’s mechanical and electronic parts.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.
