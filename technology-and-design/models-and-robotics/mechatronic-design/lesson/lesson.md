# Mechatronic design

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Malaysia's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — What mechatronic design means
Mechatronic design joins three worlds in one product: mechanical movement and transmission, electronic components — resistor, diode, circuit board, switch, sensor, transistor, capacitor — and microcontroller control systems thinking over both.

## Sub lesson 2 — Each element’s function
The elements are learned inside an existing product: each named part’s function is explained where it actually works, so the robot hand on the book’s cover is a system the learner can read.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: mechatronic design joins mechanical transmission, electronic components and microcontroller control — each element’s function read in a real product. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
