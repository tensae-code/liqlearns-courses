# Hands-on — Making vehicles

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: transport kinds (land, air, water, underground) and making them: an origami paper plane step by step, a car built from a cardboard box (Lesson 19). Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
