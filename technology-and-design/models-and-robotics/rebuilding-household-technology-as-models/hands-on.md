# Hands-on — Rebuilding household technology as models

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: a kebezhe chest, a wooden bed (agash tosek), an arba cart and a kubi churn: what each was for and how it was made, each rebuilt as a model (Topics 18–21). Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
