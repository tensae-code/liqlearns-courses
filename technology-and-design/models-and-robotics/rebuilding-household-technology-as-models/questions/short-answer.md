# Rebuilding household technology as models — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Say in one sentence what this lesson teaches.
**Answer:** Rebuilding household technology as models — a kebezhe chest, a wooden bed (agash tosek), an arba cart and a kubi churn: what each was for and how it was made, each rebuilt as a model (Topics 18–21).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name one material, tool or step this lesson works with.
**Answer:** Any one named in the lesson text — for example, the first step of sub lesson 1: Rebuilding household technology as models.
