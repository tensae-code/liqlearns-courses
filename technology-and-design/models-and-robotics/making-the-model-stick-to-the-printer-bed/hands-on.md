# Hands-on — Making the model stick to the printer bed

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First-layer inspection
Watch (or picture-watch) a first layer being printed. Score it: lines joined? gaps? edges flat? Write the verdict and the evidence.

## Fix the failure
Take one failed, lifted print or its picture. List its likely causes from this lesson, in the order you would check them, and the fix for each.
