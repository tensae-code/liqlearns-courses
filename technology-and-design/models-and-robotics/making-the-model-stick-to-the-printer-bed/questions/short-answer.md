# Making the model stick to the printer bed — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Say in one sentence why first layers detach.
**Answer:** If the first layer does not grip — through heat changes, a dirty or uneven bed, or being laid too far from the bed — the model lifts or comes loose.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Describe a good first layer in one sentence.
**Answer:** A good first layer has lines joined evenly, with no gaps and no lifting edges.
