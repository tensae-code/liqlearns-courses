# Making a toy car — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, or by doing the step named.

**1. (Sub lesson 2) Your car's wheel rubs against the body. What adjustment does the lesson suggest, and why?**
**Answer:** Trim the axle length (adjust the fit) so the wheels sit correctly and turn freely — the specification, free-running wheels, is the judge.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Write the five making stages in order.**
**Answer:** Cut to dimension; bore to diameter; assemble to specification; adjust; finish (safely).
