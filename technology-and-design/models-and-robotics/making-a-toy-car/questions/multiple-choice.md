# Making a toy car — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) 'Cut to dimension' means…**
a) Cut any size b) Cut to the size on the plan c) Cut the largest piece d) Cut without measuring
**Answer: b) Cut to the size on the plan**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) An axle hole bored too loose makes the wheel…**
a) Turn faster b) Wobble c) Stop forever d) Grow
**Answer: b) Wobble**

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Trimming an axle to length is an example of…**
a) A mistake to hide b) Adjusting to meet the specification c) Finishing d) Boring
**Answer: b) Adjusting to meet the specification**

**4. (Sub lesson 2) During assembly, what judges whether the work is right?**
a) The maker's mood b) The specification c) The clock d) The paint colour
**Answer: b) The specification**

**5. (Sub lesson 3) A finish such as paint or wrapping is applied…**
a) First, before cutting b) Never c) Last, and safely d) Instead of assembling
**Answer: c) Last, and safely**
