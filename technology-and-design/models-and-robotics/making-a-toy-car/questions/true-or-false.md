# Making a toy car — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) A hole's diameter does not matter as long as a hole exists.**
**Answer: False — Too tight will not turn; too loose wobbles.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Needing an adjustment means the making has failed.**
**Answer: False — Adjusting is a normal part of meeting the specification.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3) The finished car is tested for accuracy and workability.**
**Answer: True**
