# Lesson plan — Making a toy car

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Models and Robotics · **Lesson:** Making a toy car
**Place in the curriculum:** Models and Robotics (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Jamaica expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** a toy car is made in order: cut the material to dimension, bore the holes to the right diameter, assemble the components to specification, adjust until it runs true, and finish it safely.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Jamaica's *National Standards Curriculum Guide — Grades 4–6 — Resource & Technology* (Ministry of Education, Youth and Information, Kingston, Jamaica, © 2018), a curriculum guide with full unit teaching content, read COMPLETE (see lesson/sources.md). It counts in the acceptance count (curriculum guide with full teaching content, not evidence-only).

## Sub lessons
1. **Cutting and boring to dimension** — Every part is cut to the size on the plan, and every hole is bored to the diameter its axle or screw needs.
2. **Assembling to specification, and adjusting** — Parts are joined in the planned order and checked against the specification — then adjusted, like trimming an axle to length, until the car runs as specified.
3. **Finishing, safely** — The last step gives the car its finish — paint or wrapping — applied safely, with tools and materials handled safely at every stage.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of Models and Robotics. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Cut material to a given dimension and bore a hole to a given diameter.
2. Assemble components to a specification and adjust a part (e.g. trim an axle) to meet it.
3. Apply a finish safely and test the finished car for accuracy and workability.
## What you need
Card or thin wood, a ruler, scissors or a craft knife (with supervision), a gimlet or punch, dowel axles, wheels, paint or wrapping paper.
