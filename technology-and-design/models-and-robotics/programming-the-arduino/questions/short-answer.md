# Programming the Arduino — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Say in one sentence what this lesson teaches.
**Answer:** Programming the Arduino — the board's anatomy (14 digital pins, 6 PWM, 6 analogue inputs, 7–12 V supply, 40 mA per pin), installing the IDE, and the Blink program — pinMode(13, OUTPUT), digitalWrite, delay(1000).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name one material, tool or step this lesson works with.
**Answer:** Any one named in the lesson text — for example, the first step of sub lesson 1: Programming the Arduino.
