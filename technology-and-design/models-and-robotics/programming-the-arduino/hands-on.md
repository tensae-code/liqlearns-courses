# Hands-on — Programming the Arduino

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: the board's anatomy (14 digital pins, 6 PWM, 6 analogue inputs, 7–12 V supply, 40 mA per pin), installing the IDE, and the Blink program — pinMode(13, OUTPUT), digitalWrite, delay(1000). Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
