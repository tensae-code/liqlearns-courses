# Hands-on — Designing a technical model

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: drives for a model car: steam, petrol and electric motors in history, and five model drives diagrammed — falling weight, twisted elastic, sail, rubber band on the axle, and balloon jet. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
