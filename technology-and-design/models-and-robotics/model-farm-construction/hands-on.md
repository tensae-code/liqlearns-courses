# Hands-on — Model farm construction

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: building a farm model: choosing scale, planning the layout to a table of steps, wire-bending techniques (wave, ring, spiral), fences from cardboard, wire figures (Lesson 15). Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
