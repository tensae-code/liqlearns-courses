# Modelling a solid by transformation

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Mongolia's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Modelling a solid by transformation
Mongolia's book teaches it this way (*Design Technology 7 (Mongolia)*, printed p. 21): creating new model shapes by adding, removing and joining parts of a cube. The learner works through the named materials and steps in order, saying what each step does before doing the next one.

## Sub lesson 2 — Modelling a solid by transformation — doing it yourself
Now the learner does the whole task unaided: creating new model shapes by adding, removing and joining parts of a cube. Working from the first sub lesson's sequence, the learner plans the make, works through it step by step, and checks the finished piece against what Mongolia's book shows.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: Modelling a solid by transformation — creating new model shapes by adding, removing and joining parts of a cube. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.

## Do It Yourself
1. Do the Do it first task again, carefully this time, using everything the lesson taught.
2. Check your work against the lesson's own checks — fix one thing you find.
3. Keep your work: it is practice evidence, and the next lesson builds on it.
