# Hands-on — Model-car sport

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: the competition classes for cord, electric and radio-controlled models (ЭЛ-1, ЭЛ-2, RC), and building a model car to the rules — body no wider than 140 mm across the axles, wheels within 2–3 mm. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
