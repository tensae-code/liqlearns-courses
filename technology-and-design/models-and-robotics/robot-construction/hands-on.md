# Hands-on — Robot construction

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: developing a robot from prefabricated parts; experimenting and creating the robot; a second robot developed (sketch, choosing materials) and created, experimenting with making the robot's MOVING PARTS (§§19–22). Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
