# Robot construction — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Say in one sentence what this lesson teaches.
**Answer:** Robot construction — developing a robot from prefabricated parts; experimenting and creating the robot; a second robot developed (sketch, choosing materials) and created, experimenting with making the robot's MOVING PARTS (§§19–22).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name one material, tool or step this lesson works with.
**Answer:** Any one named in the lesson text — for example, the first step of sub lesson 1: Robot construction.
