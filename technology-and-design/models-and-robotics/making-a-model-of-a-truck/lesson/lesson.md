# Making a model of a truck

## Do it first
Picture a real truck. Shrink it in your mind until it fits on your desk — what parts must the small one keep? Then learn how this book builds that model from matchboxes.

## Sub lesson 1 — A model (макет) of a real thing
A макет is a model: a smaller copy of a real thing that keeps its main parts and their arrangement. Before building, the maker studies the real truck — cab, body, wheels — and decides which part each material will make.

## Sub lesson 2 — Building the lorry
Matchboxes give the truck its solid parts, paper covers and details them, and plasticine joins and shapes the smaller pieces. The learner assembles cab, body and wheels, then checks the model against the real truck: does it have every main part, in the right place?

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: a model (макет) is a smaller copy that keeps the real thing's main parts — and this truck is built from matchboxes, paper and plasticine. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.

3. Keep your work: it is practice evidence, and the next lesson builds on it.
