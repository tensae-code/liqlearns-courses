# Lesson plan — Making a model of a truck

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Models and Robotics · **Lesson:** Making a model of a truck
**Place in the curriculum:** Models and Robotics (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Azerbaijan expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** A model (макет) copies a real thing at a smaller size: the truck model is built from matchboxes, paper and plasticine, part by part.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on a student textbook — Azerbaijan's *Технология (Technology), Grade 2*, Russian edition as held (see lesson/sources.md). It counts in the acceptance count (student book, not evidence-only).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **A model (макет) of a real thing** — A макет is a model: a smaller copy of a real thing that keeps its main parts and their arrangement.
2. **Building the lorry** — Matchboxes give the truck its solid parts, paper covers and details them, and plasticine joins and shapes the smaller pieces.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of Models and Robotics. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends

2. Work through: Building the lorry.
## What you need
Matchboxes, paper, plasticine, glue; a picture of a real truck.
