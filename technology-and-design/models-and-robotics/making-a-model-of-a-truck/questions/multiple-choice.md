# Making a model of a truck — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is a макет?
a) A smaller copy of a real thing keeping its main parts b) A full-size truck c) A drawing with no parts d) A kind of stitch
**Answer: a) A smaller copy of a real thing keeping its main parts**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the maker study before building?
a) The real truck — cab, body, wheels b) Only the box lid c) A flower d) Nothing
**Answer: a) The real truck — cab, body, wheels**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What do matchboxes give the model?
a) Its solid parts b) Its colour only c) Its sound d) Nothing — they are decoration
**Answer: a) Its solid parts**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** What is plasticine used for in the build?
a) Joining and shaping the smaller pieces b) Covering the whole truck c) Making the wheels roll d) Painting
**Answer: a) Joining and shaping the smaller pieces**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** How is the finished model checked?
a) Against the real truck — every main part present, in the right place b) By its weight c) It is never checked d) By throwing it
**Answer: a) Against the real truck — every main part present, in the right place**
