# Making a model of a truck — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What must a model keep from the real thing?
**Answer:** Its main parts and their arrangement — cab, body and wheels in the truck's case — at a smaller size.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which materials build the truck, and what does each do?
**Answer:** Matchboxes give the solid parts, paper covers and details them, and plasticine joins and shapes the smaller pieces.
