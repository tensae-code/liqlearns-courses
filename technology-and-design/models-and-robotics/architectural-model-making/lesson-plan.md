# Lesson plan — Architectural model making

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Models and Robotics · **Lesson:** Architectural model making
**Place in the curriculum:** Models and Robotics (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Iran expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** an architectural model is a building at scale: its materials are chosen and cut by exact dimensions taken from the drawings — Iran's module builds a mosque at 1:50 and a whole city quarter in miniature.
**This lesson's own level:** stocked from Iran's *Work & Technology, Grade 8* (student book) — see lesson/sources.md. Iran counts in the acceptance count (student books, not evidence-only).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **Materials, tools and exact dimensions** — The model's materials are light and cuttable: foam sheet (یونولیت), woods and card, cut with tools including the hot-wire cutter that slices foam cleanly.
2. **Building to a scale drawing: the mosque at 1:50** — The worked model is a mosque at 1:50.
3. **A city and traffic model** — The second project widens the scale: a city/traffic model begins from a street-network plan, its parts — streets, blocks, buildings — are made separately, and the final assembly (اتصال) joins them in their places.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of Models and Robotics. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Explain architectural model making and its main parts, in their own words.
2. Carry out the lesson's central task unaided.
3. Explain how Iran's book teaches it.
## What you need
Foam sheet, card, wood pieces, hot-wire cutter or craft knife (with supervision), ruler, glue and paints.
