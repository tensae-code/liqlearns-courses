**Acceptance count: taught in 2 countries (Iran, Kazakhstan).**

Kazakhstan's own way: Kazakhstan teaches this inside Художественный труд / Көркем еңбек (Artistic Labour) and Трудовое обучение / Еңбекке баулу (Labour Training) — What an architectural model (макет) is, that builders work from drawings (чертежи), and a city model assembled from juice cartons covered in paper with windows, roofs and details fixed to a cardboard base (Lesson 16). The Kazakh classroom's own examples, materials and national crafts named in the Kazakhstan provenance rows above are part of the teaching, not decoration.

Iran's own way: Iran's Grade 8 architecture module builds two models in full: a mosque at 1:50 — plan and elevations transferred to foam sheet (یونولیت), walls cut and joined, a foam dome, painting before final assembly, in six stages — and a city/traffic model assembled (اتصال) from a street-network plan.
