# Architectural model making — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In your own words, what is foam sheet (یونولیت)?
**Answer:** The light foam board cut to form model walls and domes.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In your own words, what is scale 1:50?
**Answer:** A model scale where 1 unit on the model is 50 units on the real building.
