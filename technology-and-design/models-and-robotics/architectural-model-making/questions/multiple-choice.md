# Architectural model making — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is foam sheet (یونولیت)?
a) the light foam board cut to form model walls and domes b) Only a price c) Only decoration d) Nothing to do with this lesson
**Answer: a) the light foam board cut to form model walls and domes**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is hot-wire cutter?
a) Only history b) the heated wire tool that cuts foam cleanly c) Only storage d) A kind of glue
**Answer: b) the heated wire tool that cuts foam cleanly**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is scale 1:50?
a) Only old tools b) Only painting c) a model scale where 1 unit on the model is 50 units on the real building d) Only a price
**Answer: c) a model scale where 1 unit on the model is 50 units on the real building**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson teach, in one line?
a) Only decoration b) Nothing to do with this lesson c) Only history d) an architectural model is a building at scale: its materials are chosen and cut by exact dimensions taken from the drawings — Iran's module builds a mosque at 1:50 and a whole city quarter in miniature.
**Answer: d) an architectural model is a building at scale: its materials are chosen and cut by exact dimensions taken from the drawings — Iran's module builds a mosque at 1:50 and a whole city quarter in miniature.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** How does Iran's book teach this?
a) As theory only, never practised b) Only in a glossary c) Only as an exam d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Persian
**Answer: d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Persian**
