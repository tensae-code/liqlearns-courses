# Hands-on — Making and modelling a cylinder and a cone

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: drawing their nets with compass and ruler, allowing 1 cm for the overlap, cutting, folding and gluing; tool safety rules. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
