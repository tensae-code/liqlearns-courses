# Hands-on — Model boats

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: the history of ships, and the model-ship sport classes (A/B straight-running, C, D sailing, E, F radio-controlled) with their rules. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
