# Model boats — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson teach?
a) Model boats — the history of ships, and the model-ship sport classes (A/B straight-running, C, D sailing, E, F radio-controlled) with their rules b) Only how to draw pictures c) Nothing practical d) A song to memorise
**Answer: a) Model boats — the history of ships, and the model-ship sport classes (A/B straight-running, C, D sailing, E, F radio-controlled) with their rules**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Sub lesson 1 is about…
a) Model boats b) Cooking lunch c) A sports match d) The weather
**Answer: a) Model boats**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Sub lesson 2 asks the learner to…
a) Complete the making in full and check it b) Stop before starting c) Copy a partner's work d) Throw the materials away
**Answer: a) Complete the making in full and check it**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Mongolia teaches this topic inside which school course?
a) Design Technology (Дизайн технологи) b) Only mathematics c) Only history d) It is not taught at school
**Answer: a) Design Technology (Дизайн технологи)**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** What should the learner do first in this lesson?
a) Try the Do it first task in their own way b) Skip to the last page c) Wait to be given the answer d) Nothing at all
**Answer: a) Try the Do it first task in their own way**
