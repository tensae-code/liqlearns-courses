# Lesson plan — Technical modelling

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Models and Robotics · **Lesson:** Technical modelling
**Place in the curriculum:** Models and Robotics (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Cuba expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** technical modelling (modelismo técnico) builds working models from a model, from a graphic representation, and freely — with the modelling box's parts, mechanisms included, under the workshop's safety and organisation norms.
**This lesson's own level:** stocked from Cuba's Educación Laboral programmes and methodological orientations (teacher programme documents) — see lesson/sources.md. Cuba is evidence only for this lesson, counted nowhere in the acceptance count.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **Building from model and drawing** — The primary programmes build modelling in steps: first from a complete model in front of the learner, then from a graphic representation, then free construction. The technical-modelling box (caja de modelismo) — nuts, bolts, wrench, screwdriver — is its toolkit.
2. **Models with mechanisms** — By Grade 3 the models move: pulley and cable, gears, hook and crank are applied in a cart, a crane, a roundabout, a mill. Grade 4 extends the work with mechanics boxes I and II — complete models, incomplete models, and problem situations the learner must complete by thinking.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
