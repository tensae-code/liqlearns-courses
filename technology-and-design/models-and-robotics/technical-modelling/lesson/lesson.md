# Technical modelling

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Cuba's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Building from model and drawing
The primary programmes build modelling in steps: first from a complete model in front of the learner, then from a graphic representation, then free construction. The technical-modelling box (caja de modelismo) — nuts, bolts, wrench, screwdriver — is its toolkit.

## Sub lesson 2 — Models with mechanisms
By Grade 3 the models move: pulley and cable, gears, hook and crank are applied in a cart, a crane, a roundabout, a mill. Grade 4 extends the work with mechanics boxes I and II — complete models, incomplete models, and problem situations the learner must complete by thinking.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: technical modelling (modelismo técnico) builds working models from a model, from a graphic representation, and freely — with the modelling box's parts, mechanisms included, under the workshop's safety and organisation norms. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.

## Do It Yourself
1. Do the Do it first task again, carefully this time, using everything the lesson taught.
2. Check your work against the lesson's own checks — fix one thing you find.
3. Keep your work: it is practice evidence, and the next lesson builds on it.
