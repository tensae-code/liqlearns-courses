# Why food spoils — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Bacteria, moulds and yeasts need which three conditions to grow?
a) Warmth, moisture and food b) Cold, dryness and darkness c) Salt, acid and sugar d) Light, air and handling
**Answer: a) Warmth, moisture and food**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Enzymes in spoilage are…
a) The food's own working chemicals b) A kind of bacteria c) Always added by handling d) Harmless in all foods
**Answer: a) The food's own working chemicals**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Drying preserves food by removing…
a) Moisture b) Warmth c) The food itself d) Oxygen only
**Answer: a) Moisture**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Lemon juice and vinegar preserve by…
a) Making the food acid, an environment micro-organisms cannot grow in b) Removing all water c) Freezing the food d) Sealing out light
**Answer: a) Making the food acid, an environment micro-organisms cannot grow in**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Which list names spoilage factors from the book?
a) Warmth, moisture, light, oxygen and poor handling b) Price, colour, size, brand and season c) Cooking, drying, chilling, salting and sugaring d) Plates, cups, tables, chairs and cloths
**Answer: a) Warmth, moisture, light, oxygen and poor handling**
