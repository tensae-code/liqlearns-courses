# Why food spoils — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain spoilage in one sentence using the word conditions.
**Answer:** Answer: Food spoils when bacteria, moulds, yeasts and enzymes find the conditions they need — warmth, moisture and food — and grow or work in it.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A food is preserved three ways in one product (dried, salted, sealed from air). Why does combining methods work?
**Answer:** Answer: Each method removes or blocks a different condition or factor, so the spoilers face several closed doors at once.
