# Why food spoils — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Spoilage is growth of micro-organisms plus enzyme action.
**Answer: True** — That is the mechanism the lesson teaches.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Freezing adds a condition that helps bacteria grow.
**Answer: False** — Cold turns temperature against growth; it removes a favourable condition.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Keeping food cool, dry, dark, sealed and cleanly handled fights all five spoilage factors.
**Answer: True** — The storage rule is the factor list read backwards.
