# Hands-on — Why food spoils

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## Conditions diary
Set up (with a teacher) two food samples in different conditions and record daily changes for three days. Conclude which conditions drove the difference.

## Method matcher
List six preservation moves (drying, refrigerating, freezing, cooking, pickling in vinegar, salting). Beside each write the condition it removes or turns hostile.
