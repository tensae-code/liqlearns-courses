# Hands-on — Solutions to nutritional problems by design process

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: Nutrition related illnesses; Meals for special needs; Guidelines to healthy eating; Plan and design a nutritious meal. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
