# Hands-on — The food industry described

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: jobs and technician specialties in food industries, and the industry's chain — raw-material supply and reception, production and processing, packaging and storage, quality assurance, research & development. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
