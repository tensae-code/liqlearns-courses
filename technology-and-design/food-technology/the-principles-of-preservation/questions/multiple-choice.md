# The principles of preservation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The principle of preservation is that methods…
a) Destroy bacteria or slow them b) Feed bacteria slowly c) Only improve flavour d) Work without touching bacteria
**Answer: a) Destroy bacteria or slow them**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Raw food is described as…
a) A breeding ground for bacteria b) Naturally sterile c) Safe forever d) Preserved by itself
**Answer: a) A breeding ground for bacteria**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Freezing's mechanism is to…
a) Slow bacteria, not destroy them b) Kill all bacteria c) Exclude air d) Remove moisture
**Answer: a) Slow bacteria, not destroy them**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Pickling in vinegar works by…
a) Making the food's environment acid b) Heating and sealing c) Binding water with salt d) Smoking the surface
**Answer: a) Making the food's environment acid**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Blanching is done at…
a) 95-100°C, briefly b) 30-40°C, for hours c) Exactly 0°C d) Any temperature, overnight
**Answer: a) 95-100°C, briefly**
