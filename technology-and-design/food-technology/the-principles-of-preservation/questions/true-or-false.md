# The principles of preservation — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Thawed frozen food can spoil again because freezing only slowed the bacteria.
**Answer: True** — That is freezing's stated limit.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Blanching targets bacteria as its main purpose.
**Answer: False** — Blanching inactivates the food's own enzymes, which discolour and flavour-spoil preserved food.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Salt and sugar preserve by binding the water bacteria need.
**Answer: True** — That is their mechanism in the book's account.
