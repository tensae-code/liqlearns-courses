# The principles of preservation — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** State the preservation principle in one sentence.
**Answer:** Answer: Because raw food breeds bacteria, preservation destroys them or slows them — by freezing, heating and sealing, pickling, salt or sugar, or smoking or drying.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Why can perfectly sterile bottled fruit still spoil in quality?
**Answer:** Answer: Its own enzymes were never inactivated by blanching, so they keep working in storage, discolouring it and spoiling its flavour.
