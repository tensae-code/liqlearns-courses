# Food-design project (my attractive sandwich) — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is a labelled design sketch?
a) a design drawing with every part named on it b) Only a price c) Only decoration d) Nothing to do with this lesson
**Answer: a) a design drawing with every part named on it**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is costing ingredients?
a) Only decoration b) calculating what the food product’s ingredients cost c) Only a price d) A kind of glue
**Answer: b) calculating what the food product’s ingredients cost**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is making to the design?
a) Only history b) Only painting c) producing the product exactly as the sketch specifies d) Only storage
**Answer: c) producing the product exactly as the sketch specifies**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson teach, in one line?
a) the sandwich project designs first — labelled sketch, evaluation, ingredient costing — then makes to the design and presents it. b) Only prices c) Only old tools d) Nothing practical
**Answer: a) the sandwich project designs first — labelled sketch, evaluation, ingredient costing — then makes to the design and presents it.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** How does Malaysia's book teach this?
a) As theory only, never practised b) Only in the glossary c) Only as an exam d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Malay (RBT)
**Answer: d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Malay (RBT)**
