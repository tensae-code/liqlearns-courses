# Lesson plan — Food-design project (my attractive sandwich)

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Food Technology · **Lesson:** Food-design project (my attractive sandwich)
**Place in the curriculum:** Food Technology (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Malaysia expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the sandwich project designs first — labelled sketch, evaluation, ingredient costing — then makes to the design and presents it.
**This lesson's own level:** stocked from Malaysia's *Reka Bentuk dan Teknologi* (RBT — Design and Technology) textbooks — see lesson/sources.md. Malaysia counts in the acceptance count (student books, not evidence-only).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **Designing the sandwich first** — The sandwich project designs before it makes: ideas generated, a labelled design sketch drawn — every layer and ingredient named on the drawing — and the sketch evaluated before a single ingredient is bought.
2. **Cost, make, present** — Ingredients are costed, the sandwich is made to the design — not improvised — and the finished product is presented against its sketch.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of Food Technology. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Make and evaluate a labelled design sketch for a food product.
2. Cost ingredients, make to the design and present the product.
3. Explain how Malaysia's book teaches it.
## What you need
Sketch paper; sandwich ingredients (with supervision and hygiene).
