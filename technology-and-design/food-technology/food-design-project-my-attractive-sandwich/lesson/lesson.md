# Food-design project (my attractive sandwich)

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Malaysia's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Designing the sandwich first
The sandwich project designs before it makes: ideas generated, a labelled design sketch drawn — every layer and ingredient named on the drawing — and the sketch evaluated before a single ingredient is bought.

## Sub lesson 2 — Cost, make, present
Ingredients are costed, the sandwich is made to the design — not improvised — and the finished product is presented against its sketch. Food design obeys the same cycle as any product.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: the sandwich project designs first — labelled sketch, evaluation, ingredient costing — then makes to the design and presents it. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
