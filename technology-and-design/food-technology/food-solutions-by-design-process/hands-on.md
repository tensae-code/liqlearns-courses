# Hands-on — Food solutions by design process

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: Create and evaluate a food product using the design process; also: Design Process; Design Brief; Evaluate the new product. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
