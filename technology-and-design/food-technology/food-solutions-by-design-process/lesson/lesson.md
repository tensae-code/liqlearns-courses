# Food solutions by design process

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Papua New Guinea's guide teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Food solutions by design process
Papua New Guinea's guides teach it this way (the teacher guide — evidence, printed p. 40): Create and evaluate a food product using the design process; also: Design Process; Design Brief; Evaluate the new product. The learner works through the named materials and steps in order, saying what each step does before doing the next one.

## Sub lesson 2 — Food solutions by design process — doing it yourself
Now the learner does the whole task unaided: Create and evaluate a food product using the design process; also: Design Process; Design Brief; Evaluate the new product. Working from the first sub lesson's sequence, the learner plans the make, works through it step by step, and checks the finished piece against what the PNG guide shows.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: Food solutions by design process — Create and evaluate a food product using the design process; also: Design Process; Design Brief; Evaluate the new product. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.

## Do It Yourself
1. Do the Do it first task again, carefully this time, using everything the lesson taught.
2. Check your work against the lesson's own checks — fix one thing you find.
3. Keep your work: it is practice evidence, and the next lesson builds on it.
