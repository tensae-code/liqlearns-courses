# Hands-on — Traditional dairy technology

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: the цагаан идээ family tree (milk → cream, yoghurt, ааруул, cheese, butter oil), how ааруул is made and dried, and designing a new dairy product. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
