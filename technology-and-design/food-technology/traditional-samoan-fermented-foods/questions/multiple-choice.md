# Traditional Samoan fermented foods — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In sami lolo, the preserving acid is produced by…
a) Bacteria b) Yeasts making alcohol c) Added vinegar only d) The coconut shell
**Answer: a) Bacteria**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The acid preserves by…
a) Stopping other micro-organisms and stabilising the food b) Making the food alcoholic c) Drying the food d) Freezing the food
**Answer: a) Stopping other micro-organisms and stabilising the food**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Beer's fermentation differs because it produces…
a) Alcohol, by yeasts b) Acid, by bacteria c) Salt d) Smoke
**Answer: a) Alcohol, by yeasts**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Calling fermentation 'controlled spoilage' means…
a) The right micro-organisms are guided to win first b) Any rot is acceptable c) No control is needed d) The food is left to chance
**Answer: a) The right micro-organisms are guided to win first**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Leaves in the traditional ferments serve to…
a) Wrap and cover the food, portioning it and holding its conditions b) Flavour it as a spice only c) Replace fermentation d) Decorate the serving
**Answer: a) Wrap and cover the food, portioning it and holding its conditions**
