# Traditional Samoan fermented foods — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Sami lolo is a fermented coconut sauce.
**Answer: True** — It is the book's worked example of acid fermentation.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Acid fermentation and alcohol fermentation are the same process.
**Answer: False** — Bacteria produce acid; yeasts produce alcohol — different agents, different products.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Breadfruit, banana, cassava and fish are among Samoa's traditional ferments.
**Answer: True** — The book lists them with coconut toddy.
