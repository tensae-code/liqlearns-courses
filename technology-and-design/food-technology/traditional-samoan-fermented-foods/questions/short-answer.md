# Traditional Samoan fermented foods — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain how the acid in an acid ferment protects the food.
**Answer:** Answer: The acid makes the food an environment other micro-organisms cannot grow in, so spoilage is stopped and the food is stabilised.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Name three traditional Samoan ferments besides sami lolo.
**Answer:** Answer: Any three of: breadfruit, banana, cassava, fish, coconut toddy.
