# Traditional Samoan fermented foods

## Do it first
Fresh coconut cream sours if left. Samoa learned to guide that souring into sami lolo — a sauce that keeps. The souring that ruins one food, directed, preserves another. What makes the difference? Control.

## Sub lesson 1 — Acid fermentation — bacteria guard the food
Sami lolo, fermented coconut sauce, is the book's worked example: coconut left to ferment sours by bacterial action, and the souring is the preservation — the acid environment stops the spoilers and the food keeps. Fermentation is controlled spoilage: the right micro-organisms, winning first.

## Sub lesson 2 — Not alcohol — the beer contrast
The contrast fixes the idea: fermentation names a family. Yeast fermentation gives alcohol; bacterial fermentation gives acid. Confuse them and both are misunderstood — the Samoan ferments belong to the acid family.

## Sub lesson 3 — The traditional ferments and their leaves
Each ferment is a local answer to the same problem — a glut today, hunger tomorrow. Leaves are part of the technology: wrapping and covering portion the food, keep it clean, and hold the ferment's conditions while it works.
