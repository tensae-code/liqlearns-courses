# Lesson plan — Traditional Samoan fermented foods

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Food Technology · **Lesson:** Traditional Samoan fermented foods
**Place in the curriculum:** Food Technology (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Samoa expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Samoa's traditional ferments preserve food by acid fermentation: in sami lolo (fermented coconut sauce) bacteria produce an acid that stops other micro-organisms and stabilises the food — unlike the alcohol fermentation of beer — alongside ferments of breadfruit, banana, cassava and fish, coconut toddy, and leaves used for wrapping and covering food.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on student book evidence — Samoa's MESC textbooks (see lesson/sources.md). It counts in the acceptance count (student book, not evidence-only).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **Acid fermentation — bacteria guard the food** — In acid fermentation, bacteria make acid from the food; the acid then protects the food from other micro-organisms.
2. **Not alcohol — the beer contrast** — Beer ferments to alcohol by yeasts; Samoa's food ferments preserve by acid from bacteria — two different fermentations, two different products.
3. **The traditional ferments and their leaves** — Breadfruit, banana, cassava, fish and coconut toddy are Samoa's traditional ferments; leaves wrap and cover food in the process.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of Food Technology. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Explain how acid fermentation preserves.
2. Contrast acid fermentation with beer's alcohol fermentation.
3. Name Samoa's traditional ferments and the leaves' role.
## What you need
No tasting of home ferments; pictures or a teacher description of sami lolo if available.
