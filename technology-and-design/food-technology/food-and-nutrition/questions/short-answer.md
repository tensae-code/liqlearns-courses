# Food and nutrition — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Say in one sentence what this lesson teaches.
**Answer:** Food and nutrition — what food contains (protein, fat, carbohydrate, vitamins, minerals), energy values of foods in kcal, and daily needs for 11–14-year-olds.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name one material, tool or step this lesson works with.
**Answer:** Any one named in the lesson text — for example, the first step of sub lesson 1: Food and nutrition.
