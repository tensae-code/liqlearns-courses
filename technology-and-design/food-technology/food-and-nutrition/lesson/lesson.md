# Food and nutrition

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Mongolia's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Food and nutrition
Mongolia's book teaches it this way (*Design Technology 6 (Mongolia)*, printed p. 62): what food contains (protein, fat, carbohydrate, vitamins, minerals), energy values of foods in kcal, and daily needs for 11–14-year-olds. The learner works through the named materials and steps in order, saying what each step does before doing the next one.

## Sub lesson 2 — Food and nutrition — doing it yourself
Now the learner does the whole task unaided: what food contains (protein, fat, carbohydrate, vitamins, minerals), energy values of foods in kcal, and daily needs for 11–14-year-olds. Working from the first sub lesson's sequence, the learner plans the make, works through it step by step, and checks the finished piece against what Mongolia's book shows.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: Food and nutrition — what food contains (protein, fat, carbohydrate, vitamins, minerals), energy values of foods in kcal, and daily needs for 11–14-year-olds. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
