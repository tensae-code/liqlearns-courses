# Hands-on — Food additives

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## Label audit
Take three packaged foods. List every additive on each label, assign its kind from the chart, and state its purpose in that food. Flag any you cannot classify and research it.

## Chart building
From your audit, build your own mini-chart: kind, purpose, one real example product. Compare with a partner's chart and merge the differences.
