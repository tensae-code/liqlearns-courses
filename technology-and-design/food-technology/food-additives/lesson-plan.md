# Lesson plan — Food additives

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Food Technology · **Lesson:** Food additives
**Place in the curriculum:** Food Technology (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Samoa expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** processed food carries additives — substances added on purpose, each for a job: the book's chart sets kinds against purposes, preservatives and pH-adjusting agents among them, and the teaching is checked against real packaged foods.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on student book evidence — Samoa's MESC textbooks (see lesson/sources.md). It counts in the acceptance count (student book, not evidence-only).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **What an additive is** — An additive is a substance added to food deliberately, in small amounts, to do a defined job.
2. **Kinds and purposes — the chart** — The book's chart pairs kinds with purposes: preservatives against spoiling, pH-adjusting agents to set acidity, and others for colour, flavour and texture.
3. **Checking real packaged foods** — Labels of real packaged foods are the test bench: find the additives, name their kinds, state their purposes.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of Food Technology. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Define a food additive.
2. Use the chart: match preservatives and pH adjusters to their purposes.
3. Read a real label and identify additives with their jobs.
## What you need
Three packaged foods with labels for the checking work.
