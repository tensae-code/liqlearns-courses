# Food additives — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A food additive is…
a) A substance added deliberately, in small amounts, for a defined job b) Any contamination in food c) The main ingredient of a food d) Only a colouring
**Answer: a) A substance added deliberately, in small amounts, for a defined job**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** An additive is not…
a) An ingredient like flour or water that forms the food's bulk b) Intentional c) Functional d) Listed on the label
**Answer: a) An ingredient like flour or water that forms the food's bulk**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Preservatives as additives serve to…
a) Extend the food's safe keeping life b) Adjust acidity c) Add colour d) Sweeten
**Answer: a) Extend the food's safe keeping life**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** pH-adjusting agents are added to…
a) Set and hold the product's acidity b) Preserve by drying c) Thicken the food d) Replace sugar
**Answer: a) Set and hold the product's acidity**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Checking additives against real packaged foods means…
a) Reading real labels and matching names to kinds and purposes b) Memorising the chart only c) Avoiding all packaged food d) Trusting advertisements
**Answer: a) Reading real labels and matching names to kinds and purposes**
