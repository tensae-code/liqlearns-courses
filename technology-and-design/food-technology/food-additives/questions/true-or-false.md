# Food additives — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Every additive in a processed food is there to do a job.
**Answer: True** — Additives are intentional and functional by definition.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A preservative and a pH adjuster do the same job.
**Answer: False** — Preservatives guard keeping quality; pH adjusters set acidity.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Ingredient lists can be read against the chart to judge a product.
**Answer: True** — That label literacy is the lesson's endpoint.
