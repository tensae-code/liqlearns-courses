# Food additives — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Define a food additive in one sentence.
**Answer:** Answer: A substance added to food deliberately, in small amounts, to do a defined job such as preserving, colouring, flavouring or adjusting acidity.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** You find an unknown name on a label. Give your three-step response.
**Answer:** Answer: Find it on the additive chart, name its kind, and state its purpose in this food (researching it if the chart does not carry it).
