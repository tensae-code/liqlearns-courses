# Choosing and storing meat — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Say in one sentence what this lesson teaches.
**Answer:** Choosing and storing meat — primary processing of meat, cooking methods and temperatures (boiling 100°C, frying 100–160°C, roasting 200–250°C), and storage — freezing at −18…−20°C, salting above 10%, drying and smoking.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name one material, tool or step this lesson works with.
**Answer:** Any one named in the lesson text — for example, the first step of sub lesson 1: Choosing and storing meat.
