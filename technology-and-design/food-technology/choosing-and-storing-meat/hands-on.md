# Hands-on — Choosing and storing meat

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: primary processing of meat, cooking methods and temperatures (boiling 100°C, frying 100–160°C, roasting 200–250°C), and storage — freezing at −18…−20°C, salting above 10%, drying and smoking. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
