# Hands-on — Catering establishments

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: restaurants, cafés, canteens (цайны газар) and buffets, the fast-food chains, and the kinds of reception — business, banquet, standing buffet (а ля фуршет), cocktail, tea. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
