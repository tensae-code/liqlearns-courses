# Hands-on — Influences on food product development

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: Food ingredients; Principles of cooking methods; Food management; Economic, Social and technological influences on food product development; also: Social influences on food production; Economic influences; Technological influences; Environmental influences. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
