# Hands-on — Making snacks (зууш) safely

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: washing hands and food, cutting ingredients to size (0.5–1 cm pieces, 3×4 cm slices) and assembling canapés on skewers. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
