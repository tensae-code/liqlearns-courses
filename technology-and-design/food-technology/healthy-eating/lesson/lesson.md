# Healthy eating

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Mongolia's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Healthy eating
Mongolia's book teaches it this way (*Design Technology 8 (Mongolia)*, printed p. 51): the food pyramid, daily meal proportions (breakfast 50%, lunch 30%, dinner 20% options), and planning salads and drinks. The learner works through the named materials and steps in order, saying what each step does before doing the next one.

## Sub lesson 2 — Healthy eating — doing it yourself
Now the learner does the whole task unaided: the food pyramid, daily meal proportions (breakfast 50%, lunch 30%, dinner 20% options), and planning salads and drinks. Working from the first sub lesson's sequence, the learner plans the make, works through it step by step, and checks the finished piece against what Mongolia's book shows.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: Healthy eating — the food pyramid, daily meal proportions (breakfast 50%, lunch 30%, dinner 20% options), and planning salads and drinks. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
