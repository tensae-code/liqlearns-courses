# Hands-on — Healthy eating

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: the food pyramid, daily meal proportions (breakfast 50%, lunch 30%, dinner 20% options), and planning salads and drinks. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
