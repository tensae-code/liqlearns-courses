# Food preservation methods, past and present — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The five traditional methods each attack spoilage in a different way.
**Answer: True** — Moisture out, air out, acid in, heat through — different doors closed.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Modern methods rest on different principles from traditional ones.
**Answer: False** — The principles are the same; the control improved.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Fermenting is one of Samoa's five traditional methods.
**Answer: True** — It stands with smoking, drying, burying and baking in the review.
