# Food preservation methods, past and present — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Preservation is for…
a) Keeping food safe and edible beyond its fresh moment b) Making food more expensive c) Changing food's colour d) Speeding up spoilage
**Answer: a) Keeping food safe and edible beyond its fresh moment**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A method is judged by…
a) Whether the food keeps, safely, until needed b) How modern it is c) How much fuel it uses d) Its age alone
**Answer: a) Whether the food keeps, safely, until needed**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Burying preserves mainly by…
a) Sealing the food in the earth, away from air and pests b) Heating it c) Drying it in wind d) Adding acid
**Answer: a) Sealing the food in the earth, away from air and pests**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Smoking works by…
a) Drying the food and coating it with smoke b) Freezing it c) Sealing it in tins d) Fermenting it
**Answer: a) Drying the food and coating it with smoke**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Canning is the perfected descendant of…
a) Baking-and-sealing (heat through, then sealed from air) b) Burying alone c) Sun drying alone d) No traditional method
**Answer: a) Baking-and-sealing (heat through, then sealed from air)**
