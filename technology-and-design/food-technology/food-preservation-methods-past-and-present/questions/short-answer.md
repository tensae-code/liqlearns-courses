# Food preservation methods, past and present — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** State preservation's purpose in one sentence.
**Answer:** Answer: Preservation keeps food safe and edible beyond its fresh moment, carrying a glut into a scarcity.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the five traditional Samoan methods.
**Answer:** Answer: Smoking, drying, burying, fermenting and baking.
