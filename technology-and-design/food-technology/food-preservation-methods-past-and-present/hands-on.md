# Hands-on — Food preservation methods, past and present

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## Method analyst
For each of the five traditional methods, write: what it does to the food, which spoilage condition it attacks, and one food it suits.

## Ancestor match
Take three modern methods (canning, freezing, vacuum packing). For each, name its closest traditional ancestor from the five and defend the match in one sentence.
