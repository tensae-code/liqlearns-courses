# Hands-on — Commercial drying of food as a preservation method

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## Round trip
Weigh a dried food portion, soak it as directed, and weigh again (teacher-guided). Record the water taken back and one texture observation at each stage.

## Control audit
List what could go wrong in sun drying (rain, dust, insects, uneven drying) and beside each, the commercial control that answers it.
