# Commercial drying of food as a preservation method — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why does removing water stop spoilage growth?
**Answer:** Answer: Micro-organisms need moisture to grow; below the needed level they cannot multiply, so the food keeps.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name two failures of sun drying that factory control answers.
**Answer:** Answer: Any two of: weather interruption (rain), contamination (dust, insects), uneven drying leaving wet pockets.
