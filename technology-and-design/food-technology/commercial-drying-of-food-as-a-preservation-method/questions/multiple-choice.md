# Commercial drying of food as a preservation method — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Drying preserves because it removes…
a) The moisture micro-organisms need to grow b) All flavour c) The food's weight only d) Bacteria by killing them with heat alone
**Answer: a) The moisture micro-organisms need to grow**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Dried food keeps without…
a) Cold storage b) Any packaging c) Ever being touched d) Water returning at cooking
**Answer: a) Cold storage**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Commercial control adds…
a) Evenness, safety and speed over sun drying b) A different principle c) No benefit d) Colour only
**Answer: a) Evenness, safety and speed over sun drying**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A wet pocket left in dried food risks…
a) Mould growing where moisture remains b) Nothing c) Better flavour d) Faster drying later
**Answer: a) Mould growing where moisture remains**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Dried food returns to use by…
a) Taking water back at soaking or cooking b) Being eaten only dry forever c) Freezing d) Fermenting
**Answer: a) Taking water back at soaking or cooking**
