# Commercial drying of food as a preservation method — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Drying is one of the oldest preservation methods, commercialised.
**Answer: True** — The principle is ancient; the control is modern.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Dried food can be stored anywhere, even damp.
**Answer: False** — It must be stored dry, or moisture returns and growth resumes.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Drying concentrates flavour.
**Answer: True** — Removing water leaves the flavour substances in less bulk.
