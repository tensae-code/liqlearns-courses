| food safety and hygiene in processing: its role (keeps the food's nutrients to the maximum, reduces the risk of acute and chronic diseases from contaminated or poisoned food, limits the danger of weakening the population's health, and builds the national food-processing industry's reputation); the hazard groups that make food unsafe — biological agents (bacteria such as Clostridium botulinum and Salmonella, hepatitis A virus, roundworms, the mould Aspergillus flavus…), chemical agents (plant-protection chemical residues, feed residues, lead, dioxins, tributyltin… in packaging and cooking tools, natural toxins such as tetrodotoxin in puffer-fish liver and ovaries and solanine in sprouted potatoes), and physical agents (foreign objects — hair, nails, bone, metal fragments — and radioactive substances); and the ten golden rules for ensuring food hygiene and safety: (1) choose fresh, safe food of clear origin, properly labelled and packaged, washed carefully under running water, vegetables peeled before eating; (2) cook food thoroughly before eating — the centre temperature must reach 70 °C; (3) eat immediately after cooking, because the longer cooked food stands at room temperature the more microorganisms grow; (4) store cooked food carefully — keep it hot (near or above 60 °C) or cold (below 10 °C), properly stored cooked food keeps 4–5 hours, food for young children should not be stored; (5) reheat thoroughly before eating — food stored over 6 hours must be recooked at 70 °C or more; (6) do not let raw and cooked food mix — cooked food can be contaminated by contact through shared processing and containing tools, so use separate tools for raw and cooked, or wash tools carefully when switching; (7) always keep food-preparation hands clean — wash hands carefully with soap and clean water before preparing food, no long nails or jewellery on the hands while processing, cover cuts with waterproof gauze and wear gloves for direct food contact (the six hand-washing steps per the Ministry of Health); (8) keep food-contact surfaces and the kitchen dry and clean — wash and dry preparation areas and surfaces, and wash, boil and sun-dry dish cloths and tools frequently; (9) cover food to avoid insects and other animals — store food in sealed containers (boxes, cupboards, cages, bags) away from flies, cockroaches, ants and rats, which carry disease microorganisms to people; (10) use clean, safe water — water for processing and drinking from the local authority's clean supply, and boil water for direct drinking or ice (original string `food safety and hygiene in processing`) | Vietnam, *Công nghệ 9 — Mô đun Chế biến thực phẩm*, printed p. 35 |
