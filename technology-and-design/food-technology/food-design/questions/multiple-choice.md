# Food design — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is food design?
a) giving food pattern and form on purpose b) Only a price c) Only decoration d) Nothing to do with this lesson
**Answer: a) giving food pattern and form on purpose**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is fruit and vegetable carving?
a) Only decoration b) food design cut into produce c) Only a price d) A kind of glue
**Answer: b) food design cut into produce**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is commercial value?
a) Only history b) Only painting c) the worth a design adds to a product for selling d) Only storage
**Answer: c) the worth a design adds to a product for selling**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson teach, in one line?
a) food design gives food pattern and form — and matters because it follows trends, offers choice, raises value and introduces cultures. b) Only prices c) Only old tools d) Nothing practical
**Answer: a) food design gives food pattern and form — and matters because it follows trends, offers choice, raises value and introduces cultures.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** How does Malaysia's book teach this?
a) As theory only, never practised b) Only in the glossary c) Only as an exam d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Malay (RBT)
**Answer: d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Malay (RBT)**
