# Food design

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Malaysia's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — The kinds of food design
Food is designed like any product: Malaysia shows fruit and vegetable carving, bakery products, and moulded cakes and jellies — food given pattern and form on purpose, to be eaten with the eyes first.

## Sub lesson 2 — Why food design matters
Food design matters commercially and culturally: it follows trends, gives consumers choice, meets new tastes, raises commercial value, and introduces cultures to one another at the table.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: food design gives food pattern and form — and matters because it follows trends, offers choice, raises value and introduces cultures. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
