# Hands-on — Properties of fruits, vegetables and legumes

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: Classification and properties of fruits, vegetables and legumes; Ways of preparing and preserving fruits, vegetables and legumes; Principles behind cooking fruits, vegetables and legumes. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
