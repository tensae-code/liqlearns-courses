# Hands-on — How commercial canning developed

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## Timeline
Build a three-step timeline: the prize problem, Appert's jars, the 1809 metal container, and add a fourth step — today's conventional method — with one control improvement per step.

## Principle check
