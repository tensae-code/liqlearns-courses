# How commercial canning developed — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain canning's logic in two clauses.
**Answer:** Answer: Heating destroys the spoilers sealed inside; the seal excludes new ones — destroy, then exclude.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Why did armies and navies fund the search for preservation?
**Answer:** Answer: Their food rotted faster than they could travel; keeping food safely for long periods and journeys was a logistics necessity.
