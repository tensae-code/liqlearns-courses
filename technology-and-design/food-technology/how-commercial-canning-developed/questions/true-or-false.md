# How commercial canning developed — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Appert understood bacteria caused spoilage when he designed his method.
**Answer: False** — Germ theory came later; his method was empirical but already obeyed destroy-and-exclude.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The metal container traded the jar's fragility for strength.
**Answer: True** — That is why the can replaced the jar for transport.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A bulging can should be rejected.
**Answer: True** — Bulging signals the seal-and-heat logic has failed inside.
