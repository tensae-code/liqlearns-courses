# How commercial canning developed — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The prize was offered to solve…
a) Keeping food safely for long periods and journeys b) Making food taste better c) Growing more food d) Cheaper jars
**Answer: a) Keeping food safely for long periods and journeys**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Appert won with experiments sealing food in…
a) Glass jars heated in boiling water b) Metal cans heated in ovens c) Bottles buried in earth d) Pots smoked over fires
**Answer: a) Glass jars heated in boiling water**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The seal's job in Appert's method is to…
a) Exclude new spoilers after heating destroys the ones inside b) Keep the jar pretty c) Hold the label d) Add flavour
**Answer: a) Exclude new spoilers after heating destroys the ones inside**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The 1809 development was…
a) Food in sealed metal containers b) The first glass jar c) Refrigeration d) Freeze-drying
**Answer: a) Food in sealed metal containers**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Modern conventional canning changed Appert's method chiefly by…
a) Control — scheduled temperatures and times, tested seals, standard cans b) Abandoning heat c) Removing the seal d) Using no containers
**Answer: a) Control — scheduled temperatures and times, tested seals, standard cans**
