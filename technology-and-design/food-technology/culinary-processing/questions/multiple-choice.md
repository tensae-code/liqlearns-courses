# Culinary processing — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What are the two main methods of culinary processing?
a) Primary processing and thermal processing b) Washing and serving c) Buying and storing d) Cutting and tasting
**Answer: a) Primary processing and thermal processing**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What does primary processing do?
a) Cooks the product b) Prepares the product before heat — cleaning, sorting, cutting, assembling c) Serves the product d) Stores the product
**Answer: b) Prepares the product before heat — cleaning, sorting, cutting, assembling**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** What does thermal processing do?
a) Changes the product with heat b) Cleans the product c) Buys the product d) Draws the product
**Answer: a) Changes the product with heat**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which Grade 4 dish shows primary processing?
a) Open sandwiches (tartine) b) Fruit compote c) Soup d) Roast
**Answer: a) Open sandwiches (tartine)**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Which Grade 4 dish shows thermal processing?
a) Open sandwiches b) A salad c) A fruit compote d) A menu
**Answer: c) A fruit compote**
