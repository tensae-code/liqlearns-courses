# Lesson plan — Culinary processing

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Food Technology · **Lesson:** Culinary processing
**Place in the curriculum:** Food Technology (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Moldova expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** food reaches the table by two main methods — primary processing, which prepares the product, and thermal processing, which cooks it with heat.
**This lesson's own level:** stocked from Moldova's *Educație tehnologică* (Technological Education) textbooks — see lesson/sources.md. Moldova counts in the acceptance count (student books, not evidence-only).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **Primary and thermal processing — the two methods** — Moldova's books name two main methods of culinary processing.
2. **First dishes — sandwiches and compote** — In Grade 4 the two methods are carried out at once on first dishes: open sandwiches (tartine), assembled by primary processing, and a fruit compote, made by thermal processing.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of Food Technology. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Tell primary processing from thermal processing.
2. Carry out both on a first dish, as the books do.
3. Explain how Moldova's book teaches it.
## What you need
Simple food products, basic kitchen tools, and a heat source used under the kitchen norms.
