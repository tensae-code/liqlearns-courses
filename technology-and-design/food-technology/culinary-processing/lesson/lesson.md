# Culinary processing

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Moldova's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Primary and thermal processing — the two methods
Moldova's books name two main methods of culinary processing. Primary processing prepares the product — cleaning, sorting, cutting, assembling — before any heat. Thermal processing changes the product with heat. The Grade 5–6 book builds the pair out into menus, balanced eating and culinary technologies, and joins them to setting and serving the table.

## Sub lesson 2 — First dishes — sandwiches and compote
In Grade 4 the two methods are carried out at once on first dishes: open sandwiches (tartine), assembled by primary processing, and a fruit compote, made by thermal processing. The learner meets the methods as things done with the hands, not only as definitions.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: food reaches the table by two main methods — primary processing, which prepares the product, and thermal processing, which cooks it with heat. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
