# Food production 1

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Papua New Guinea's guide teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Food production 1
Papua New Guinea's guides teach it this way (the teacher guide — evidence, printed p. 40, 44-45): Modified food products; The process of product development; The design process. The learner works through the named materials and steps in order, saying what each step does before doing the next one.

## Sub lesson 2 — Food production 1 — doing it yourself
Now the learner does the whole task unaided: Modified food products; The process of product development; The design process. Working from the first sub lesson's sequence, the learner plans the make, works through it step by step, and checks the finished piece against what the PNG guide shows.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: Food production 1 — Modified food products; The process of product development; The design process. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.

## Do It Yourself
1. Do the Do it first task again, carefully this time, using everything the lesson taught.
