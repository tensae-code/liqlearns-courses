# Meat and seafood — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Say in one sentence what this lesson teaches.
**Answer:** Meat and seafood — the classification of animal and sea products, fish species and freshness (a laboratory check of eyes, gills, flesh, scales, smell), seafood (prawns, squid, mussels, lobster, seaweed) and how each is prepared.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name one material, tool or step this lesson works with.
**Answer:** Any one named in the lesson text — for example, the first step of sub lesson 1: Meat and seafood.
