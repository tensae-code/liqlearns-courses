# Hands-on — Meat and seafood

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: the classification of animal and sea products, fish species and freshness (a laboratory check of eyes, gills, flesh, scales, smell), seafood (prawns, squid, mussels, lobster, seaweed) and how each is prepared. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
