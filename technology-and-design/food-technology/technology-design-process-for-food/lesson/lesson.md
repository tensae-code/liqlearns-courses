# Technology design process for food

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Papua New Guinea's guide teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Technology design process for food
Papua New Guinea's guides teach it this way (the teacher guide — evidence, printed p. 42): Design brief and the technological process; Using a design product to create a new product; Evaluate the new product. The learner works through the named materials and steps in order, saying what each step does before doing the next one.

## Sub lesson 2 — Technology design process for food — doing it yourself
Now the learner does the whole task unaided: Design brief and the technological process; Using a design product to create a new product; Evaluate the new product. Working from the first sub lesson's sequence, the learner plans the make, works through it step by step, and checks the finished piece against what the PNG guide shows.

## Say it to your companion
