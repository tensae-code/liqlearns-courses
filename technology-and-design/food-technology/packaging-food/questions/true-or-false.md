# Packaging food — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Active packaging manages the atmosphere inside the pack.
**Answer: True** — Nitrogen flushing and permeability-matched films do exactly that.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Microwave packaging is designed to go into the microwave with the food.
**Answer: True** — The pack is part of the cooking design.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** One packaging material is best on both protection and environment for every food.
**Answer: False** — Each material trades strengths; the choice is per food.
