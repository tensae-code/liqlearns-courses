# Packaging food — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Why flush oxygen out of a pack of crisps?
**Answer:** Answer: Oxygen feeds spoilage and staleness (fat oxidation); inert nitrogen takes its place and also cushions the crisps, so quality keeps longer.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** A hot ready meal needs microwave packaging. Name one further material property you must check.
**Answer:** Answer: Any sensible check: heat resistance/food safety at cooking temperature, seal integrity when hot, steam venting behaviour.
