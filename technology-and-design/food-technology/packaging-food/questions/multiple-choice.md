# Packaging food — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Packaging's first job is…
a) Protection — holding the food and keeping dirt and damage out b) Advertising c) Flavouring the food d) Preserving by itself
**Answer: a) Protection — holding the food and keeping dirt and damage out**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A pack's label carries…
a) The maker's information b) No information c) Only decoration d) The price of the shop
**Answer: a) The maker's information**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Nitrogen flushing works by…
a) Replacing oxygen, which feeds spoilage and staleness b) Adding flavour c) Pressurising the pack for fun d) Cooling the food
**Answer: a) Replacing oxygen, which feeds spoilage and staleness**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A film's gas permeability decides…
a) How fast gases pass through the pack wall b) The film's colour c) The pack's price only d) Nothing about the inside atmosphere
**Answer: a) How fast gases pass through the pack wall**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Glass as packaging is…
a) Inert and reusable, but heavy and breakable b) The lightest material c) Unrecyclable d) A kind of plastic
**Answer: a) Inert and reusable, but heavy and breakable**
