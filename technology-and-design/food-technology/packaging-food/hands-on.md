# Hands-on — Packaging food

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## Pack audit
Examine four real packages in different materials. For each: name the material, its protective strengths, its environmental cost, and whether the pack does anything active (gas flush, microwave use).

## Design the pack
Choose a food (fresh fish, dried fruit, a ready meal). Specify its packaging: material, atmosphere management if any, and the environmental trade-off you accepted — each defended in one line.
