# Hands-on — A vitamin-rich salad

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: what vitamins A, B, C and D do and which foods hold them; two salad recipes with quantities and a making sequence; food-safety rules. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
