# Hands-on — Food safety and hygiene

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: Personal hygiene and safety practices; Kitchen hygiene; Hygiene practices and safety in food development. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
