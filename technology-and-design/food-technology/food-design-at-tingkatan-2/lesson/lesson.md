# Food design at Tingkatan 2

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Malaysia's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Food design, defined and tooled
At Tingkatan 2 food design is defined formally with its importance, and practised with its own tools and equipment — the food designer’s kit, learned as deliberately as the fashion toolkit.

## Sub lesson 2 — Sketch, justify, produce, evaluate
The design and its packaging are sketched and justified — the reasons argued, not just drawn — then the food design and packaging are produced, and the result evaluated against the justification.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: food design at Tingkatan 2 is defined, tooled, sketched and justified, produced with its packaging, and evaluated. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
