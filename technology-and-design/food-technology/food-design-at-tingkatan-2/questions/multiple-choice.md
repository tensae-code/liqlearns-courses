# Food design at Tingkatan 2 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is food design (Tingkatan 2)?
a) the formal design of food products, with its tools, sketching and evaluation b) Only a price c) Only decoration d) Nothing to do with this lesson
**Answer: a) the formal design of food products, with its tools, sketching and evaluation**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is justifying a design?
a) Only decoration b) arguing the reasons behind a sketched design c) Only a price d) A kind of glue
**Answer: b) arguing the reasons behind a sketched design**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is evaluating the result?
a) Only history b) Only painting c) judging the produced food design against its justification d) Only storage
**Answer: c) judging the produced food design against its justification**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson teach, in one line?
a) food design at Tingkatan 2 is defined, tooled, sketched and justified, produced with its packaging, and evaluated. b) Only prices c) Only old tools d) Nothing practical
**Answer: a) food design at Tingkatan 2 is defined, tooled, sketched and justified, produced with its packaging, and evaluated.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** How does Malaysia's book teach this?
a) As theory only, never practised b) Only in the glossary c) Only as an exam d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Malay (RBT)
