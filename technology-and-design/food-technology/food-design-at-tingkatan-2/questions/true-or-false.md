# Food design at Tingkatan 2 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Food design (tingkatan 2) is the formal design of food products, with its tools, sketching and evaluation.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Justifying a design has nothing to do with this lesson.
**Answer: False** — It is part of the lesson: arguing the reasons behind a sketched design.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Evaluating the result is judging the produced food design against its justification.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.
