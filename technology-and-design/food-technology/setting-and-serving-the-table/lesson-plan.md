# Lesson plan — Setting and serving the table

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Food Technology · **Lesson:** Setting and serving the table
**Place in the curriculum:** Food Technology (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Azerbaijan expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** A laid table follows a pattern: each piece of tableware has its place and its job, and the pattern changes between breakfast and lunch.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on a student textbook — Azerbaijan's *Технология (Technology), Grade 2*, Russian edition as held (see lesson/sources.md). It counts in the acceptance count (student book, not evidence-only).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **The tableware and what each piece does** — Every meal is eaten with tableware — plates, a cup, a knife, a fork, a spoon and a napkin.
2. **Laying the table for breakfast** — For breakfast the table is laid simply: a plate in front of each seat, the knife and fork beside it, the spoon where the hand reaches it, a cup for the drink, and a napkin.
3. **Laying the table for lunch** — Lunch adds more: a soup plate and the pieces lunch needs join the breakfast setting.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
