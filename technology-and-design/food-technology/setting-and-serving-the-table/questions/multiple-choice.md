# Setting and serving the table — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the knife do at the table?
a) Cuts the food b) Holds the drink c) Holds the soup d) Cleans the hands
**Answer: a) Cuts the food**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the napkin for?
a) Keeping hands and mouth clean b) Cutting food c) Holding the plate d) Stirring the drink
**Answer: a) Keeping hands and mouth clean**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** How is the breakfast table laid?
a) Simply, piece by piece in the pictured order b) All pieces in one pile c) Only a cup d) With no plates
**Answer: a) Simply, piece by piece in the pictured order**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** How does the lunch setting differ from breakfast?
a) It adds pieces such as a soup plate b) It removes all plates c) It is identical d) It uses no tableware
**Answer: a) It adds pieces such as a soup plate**

**5. (Sub lesson 1)** Which piece holds the drink?
a) The cup b) The fork c) The napkin d) The knife
**Answer: a) The cup**
