# Setting and serving the table — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** The breakfast table is laid piece by piece in a set order.
**Answer: True** — The setting follows a pictured pattern, laid one piece at a time.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Breakfast and lunch tables are laid in exactly the same way.
**Answer: False** — Lunch adds pieces, such as a soup plate, that breakfast does not need.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Every piece of tableware has its own job.
**Answer: True** — That is the lesson's rule: plate, knife, fork, spoon, cup and napkin each do one job.
