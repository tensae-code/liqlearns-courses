# Setting and serving the table — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name three pieces of tableware and the job of each.
**Answer:** Any three of: plate holds the food; knife cuts; fork lifts; spoon takes soup and drinks; cup holds the drink; napkin keeps hands and mouth clean.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Why does lunch need more tableware than breakfast?
**Answer:** Lunch is a fuller meal — for example soup is served — so extra pieces such as a soup plate join the setting.
