# Food processing and packaging

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Papua New Guinea's guide teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Food processing and packaging
Papua New Guinea's guides teach it this way (the teacher guide — evidence, printed p. 42): Factors that influence food processing; The role of food packaging; Developments in packaging and distribution; Techniques to evaluate products and processes. The learner works through the named materials and steps in order, saying what each step does before doing the next one.

## Sub lesson 2 — Food processing and packaging — doing it yourself
Now the learner does the whole task unaided: Factors that influence food processing; The role of food packaging; Developments in packaging and distribution; Techniques to evaluate products and processes. Working from the first sub lesson's sequence, the learner plans the make, works through it step by step, and checks the finished piece against what the PNG guide shows.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: Food processing and packaging — Factors that influence food processing; The role of food packaging; Developments in packaging and distribution; Techniques to evaluate products and processes. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.

## Do It Yourself
1. Do the Do it first task again, carefully this time, using everything the lesson taught.
2. Check your work against the lesson's own checks — fix one thing you find.
3. Keep your work: it is practice evidence, and the next lesson builds on it.
