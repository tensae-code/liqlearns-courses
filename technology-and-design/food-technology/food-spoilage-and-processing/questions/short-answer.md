# Food spoilage and processing — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Say in one sentence what this lesson teaches.
**Answer:** What spoilage is, its causes (bacteria, yeasts, moulds) and factors, toxic substances in foods; processing methods (refrigeration and the other preservation processes through sugar/salt addition); hygiene in the food industry; labs — heat/storage effects on milk spoilage, canning fruit in syrup, making jam and yoghurt.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name one material, tool or step this lesson works with.
**Answer:** Any one named in the lesson text — for example, the first step of sub lesson 1: Food spoilage and processing.
