# Hands-on — Food spoilage and processing

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: what spoilage is, its causes (bacteria, yeasts, moulds) and factors, toxic substances in foods; processing methods (refrigeration and the other preservation processes through sugar/salt addition); hygiene in the food industry; labs — heat/storage effects on milk spoilage, canning fruit in syrup, making jam and yoghurt. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
