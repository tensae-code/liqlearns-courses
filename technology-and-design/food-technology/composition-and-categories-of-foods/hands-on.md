# Hands-on — Composition and categories of foods

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: the main constituents (proteins, fats, carbohydrates, minerals/trace elements, water, vitamins), fresh and processed foods of plant and animal origin, nutritional and caloric value, the dietary balance. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
