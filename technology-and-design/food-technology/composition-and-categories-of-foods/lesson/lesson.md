# Composition and categories of foods

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Greece's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Composition and categories of foods
Greece's book teaches it this way: the main constituents (proteins, fats, carbohydrates, minerals/trace elements, water, vitamins), fresh and processed foods of plant and animal origin, nutritional and caloric value, the dietary balance (*Food Tech Intro*, printed p. 33). The learner works through the named materials and steps in order, saying what each step does before doing the next one.

## Sub lesson 2 — Composition and categories of foods — doing it yourself
Now the learner does the whole task unaided: the main constituents (proteins, fats, carbohydrates, minerals/trace elements, water, vitamins), fresh and processed foods of plant and animal origin, nutritional and caloric value, the dietary balance. Working from the first sub lesson's sequence, the learner plans the make, works through it step by step, and checks the finished piece against what Greece's book shows.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: The main constituents (proteins, fats, carbohydrates, minerals/trace elements, water, vitamins), fresh and processed foods of plant and animal origin, nutritional and caloric value, the dietary balance. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
