# Hygiene and work-protection norms in the kitchen

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Moldova's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Hygiene norms in the kitchen
Moldova's Grade 4 book sets the hygiene norms before the cooking begins: the personal and kitchen cleanliness that food work demands. These are not one lesson's rules — they govern all the book's food work, from the first sandwich to the last compote.

## Sub lesson 2 — Work-protection norms in the kitchen
Beside hygiene stand the work-protection norms: the safety rules that protect the worker while food is prepared — careful handling of tools and heat, an orderly workplace, and habits that prevent accidents in the kitchen.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: the kitchen has hygiene and work-protection norms, and they govern every piece of food work that follows — clean hands, clean tools, safe handling, every time. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
