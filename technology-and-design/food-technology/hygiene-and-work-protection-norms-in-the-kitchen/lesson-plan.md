# Lesson plan — Hygiene and work-protection norms in the kitchen

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Food Technology · **Lesson:** Hygiene and work-protection norms in the kitchen
**Place in the curriculum:** Food Technology (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Moldova expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the kitchen has hygiene and work-protection norms, and they govern every piece of food work that follows — clean hands, clean tools, safe handling, every time.
**This lesson's own level:** stocked from Moldova's *Educație tehnologică* (Technological Education) textbooks — see lesson/sources.md. Moldova counts in the acceptance count (student books, not evidence-only).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **Hygiene norms in the kitchen** — Moldova's Grade 4 book sets the hygiene norms before the cooking begins: the personal and kitchen cleanliness that food work demands.
2. **Work-protection norms in the kitchen** — Beside hygiene stand the work-protection norms: the safety rules that protect the worker while food is prepared — careful handling of tools and heat, an orderly workplace, and habits that prevent accidents in the kitchen..

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of Food Technology. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the hygiene and work-protection norms for kitchen work.
2. Apply the norms before, during and after a food task.
3. Explain how Moldova's book teaches it.
## What you need
The kitchen rules as listed in the lesson, soap and water, and a clean work surface to practise on.
