# Hygiene and work-protection norms in the kitchen — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why are the norms taught before any cooking?
**Answer:** Because they govern all the food work that follows; every recipe in the book is done under them.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name two things work protection asks of the cook.
**Answer:** Careful handling of tools and heat, and an orderly workplace (any two protection habits the lesson names).
