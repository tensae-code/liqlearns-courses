# Hygiene and work-protection norms in the kitchen — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** When do the kitchen hygiene norms apply in Moldova's book?
a) To all the book's food work b) Only to the first lesson c) Only when a teacher watches d) Only for big meals
**Answer: a) To all the book's food work**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What do the hygiene norms cover?
a) Only the floor b) Personal and kitchen cleanliness for food work c) Only the cook's clothes d) Only the serving table
**Answer: b) Personal and kitchen cleanliness for food work**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What do the work-protection norms protect?
a) The worker, during food preparation b) The food's colour c) The kitchen's decoration d) The shopping list
**Answer: a) The worker, during food preparation**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which of these belongs to work protection?
a) Careful handling of tools and heat b) Faster chopping c) Louder music d) Bigger portions
**Answer: a) Careful handling of tools and heat**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Where do the norms sit in the book's order?
a) After the last recipe b) In an appendix nobody reads c) Before the cooking lessons, governing them all d) Only in the glossary
**Answer: c) Before the cooking lessons, governing them all**
