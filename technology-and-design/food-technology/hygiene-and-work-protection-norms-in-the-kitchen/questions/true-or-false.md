# Hygiene and work-protection norms in the kitchen — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The hygiene norms govern all the food work in the book, not just one lesson.
**Answer: True** — The placements describe them as the rules that govern all the book's food work.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Work protection is only about the food, not about the worker.
**Answer: False** — Work-protection norms protect the person working — safe handling of tools and heat.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** An orderly workplace is part of kitchen safety.
**Answer: True** — Order prevents accidents and is taught with the protection norms.
