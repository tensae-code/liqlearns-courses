# Hands-on — Hygiene and work-protection norms in the kitchen

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## Norms audit
Walk through a kitchen (or a picture of one) with the norms in hand. List every norm you can see being kept, and every one being broken.

## Before-work routine
Write and rehearse the before-work routine the norms require: what you wash, what you check, what you put in order — in the right sequence.
