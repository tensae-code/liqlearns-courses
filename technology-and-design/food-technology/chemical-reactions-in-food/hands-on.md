# Hands-on — Chemical reactions in food

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: Chemical reactions in food; in starch and proteins; in oils, fats, vitamins and minerals; Food fermentation. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
