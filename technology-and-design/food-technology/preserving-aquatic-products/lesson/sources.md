| preserving aquatic products: the cold preservation method (using low temperature to inhibit the activity of enzymes and of spoilage microorganisms — products stored in cold ice, in refrigerators or in cold stores, usually below 4 °C; depending on the species and temperature, storage lasts from a few days to a few weeks or months), the drying method (sun-drying or drying with specialised equipment to a suitable moisture; dried products are packed tightly in nylon bags or vacuum bags and stored at room temperature or in a refrigerator, keeping from 6 months to a year), the salting method (based on the difference in salt concentration between the salting environment and the microorganisms' cells, inhibiting their activity and development; applied to fish and shrimp — the cleaned, pre-processed products are salted dry or soaked in brine at suitable ratios, or the two methods combined; depending on the species and size, environmental temperature, salt grain size and purpose, the salting time runs from a few hours to a few days, after which the product is taken out, drained, packed tightly in nylon or vacuum bags and stored at room temperature or refrigerated), and the high-technology applications (nano UFB (Ultra Fine Bubble) technology for preserving oceanic tuna — creating ultra-fine nitrogen bubbles of nanometre size that displace the dissolved oxygen in the water, reducing the activity and growth of aerobic bacteria and so removing oxidation from the outer surface and inside the fish's body, preventing the flesh from changing quality and keeping it fresh longer: the full process drawn — make circulating chilled seawater at an ice-to-seawater ratio of 3:2, temperature −1.5 to −1 °C, salinity 20–25‰; run the nano-nitrogen bubble generator the first time for 4 hours until DO < 1 mg/L, then 1–2 hours per run over 5 days with DO < 1 mg/L and the circulation pump 5 minutes per run, twice a day; catch the tuna by hand combined with light, stunning it so it stops struggling; pre-process — bleed, break the spinal cord, gut and wash, the pre-processing time at most 5 minutes; preserve the product soaked in the chilled seawater containing nano nitrogen bubbles at DO < 1 mg/L, −1.5 to −1 °C, salinity 20–25‰, adding ice every 2–3 days and running the circulation pump twice a day and the nano bubble generator every 5 days; unload the product — and polyurethane insulation material in preservation (good insulation, high adhesion, light weight, little water absorption, high elasticity and no mould, easy to use in construction; polyurethane used to line the holds of aquatic-product exploitation vessels lengthens preservation time and raises catch quality, letting vessels fish farther and longer and cut production costs — preservation time in a hold lined with polyurethane reaches 20 days, against only 7 days in a traditional hold, while the product quality still meets export standards) (original string `preserving aquatic products`) | Vietnam, *Công nghệ 12 — Lâm nghiệp – Thuỷ sản*, printed p. 108 |
