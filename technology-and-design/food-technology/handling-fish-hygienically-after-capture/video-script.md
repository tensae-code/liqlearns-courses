# Video script — Handling fish hygienically after capture

A short demonstration video, made later, in a super-simple voice. The demonstrator works through the topic while the voice speaks.

---

This lesson is about handling fish hygienically after capture.
Washing in clean water, keeping handlers and equipment clean, and chilling fish with ice so it does not spoil on the way to market (bicycle/motorbike fish selling pictured).
Work step by step. Check your work as you go.
