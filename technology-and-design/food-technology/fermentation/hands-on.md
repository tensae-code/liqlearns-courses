# Hands-on — Fermentation

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## Pit plan
Draw a labelled cross-section of a masi pit from the lesson: lining, fill, cover. Under the drawing, write one line per part: its job in the ferment.

## Ferment reasoning
A ferment fails and the food rots instead. List three things that could have gone wrong (covering, cleanliness, micro-organisms beaten by spoilers) and how the traditional method guards each.
