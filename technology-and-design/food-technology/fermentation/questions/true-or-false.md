# Fermentation — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The covering of the pit seals the ferment from air and pests.
**Answer: True** — Covering holds the ferment's conditions.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A fermenting pit is not equipment, just a hole.
**Answer: False** — Built and lined to a practiced method, the pit does a vessel's work.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The acid in a ferment bars spoilers from growing.
**Answer: True** — Acid is the preservation mechanism.
