# Fermentation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Fermentation preserves because the micro-organisms produce…
a) An acid that stops spoiling b) Alcohol in every case c) Salt d) Smoke
**Answer: a) An acid that stops spoiling**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The fermenting micro-organisms named are…
a) Yeasts and bacteria b) Moulds only c) Enzymes only d) Insects
**Answer: a) Yeasts and bacteria**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Masi ulu is…
a) Breadfruit fermented in a leaf-lined pit b) A coconut sauce c) A smoked fish d) A baked pudding served fresh
**Answer: a) Breadfruit fermented in a leaf-lined pit**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The pit solves which problem?
a) Breadfruit ripens all at once and cannot wait b) Breadfruit is poisonous raw in all cases c) Pits are easier than cooking d) Leaves are scarce
**Answer: a) Breadfruit ripens all at once and cannot wait**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** The leaf lining's jobs include…
a) Keeping the food clean from the earth and holding moisture b) Adding acid directly c) Heating the ferment d) Keeping light in
**Answer: a) Keeping the food clean from the earth and holding moisture**
