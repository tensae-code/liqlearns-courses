# Fermentation — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Why is masi called a reserve or bank?
**Answer:** Answer: It stores a seasonal glut of breadfruit in edible form long past the harvest, to be drawn on when food is scarce.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** List the pit's parts in building order.
**Answer:** Answer: Dig the pit, line it with leaves, fill with breadfruit, cover it (leaves and earth).
