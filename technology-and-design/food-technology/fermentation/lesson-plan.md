# Lesson plan — Fermentation

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Food Technology · **Lesson:** Fermentation
**Place in the curriculum:** Food Technology (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Samoa expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** fermentation preserves food with special micro-organisms — yeasts and bacteria — whose acid stops spoiling: Samoa's worked case is masi ulu, breadfruit fermented in a leaf-lined pit, and how that pit is built and covered.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on student book evidence — Samoa's MESC textbooks (see lesson/sources.md). It counts in the acceptance count (student book, not evidence-only).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **The preserving acid** — Yeasts and bacteria, given the food, produce acid; the acid preserves the food and stops it spoiling.
2. **Masi ulu — breadfruit in a pit** — Masi is breadfruit fermented in a pit lined with leaves and covered: the pit is the fermenting vessel.
3. **Building and covering the pit** — The pit is dug, lined with leaves, filled, and covered — each step holds the ferment's conditions.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of Food Technology. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Explain how fermentation's acid preserves.
2. Describe masi ulu and its purpose.
3. Give the pit's building steps and each one's job.
## What you need
No materials; a diagram or description of a fermenting pit if available.
