# Hands-on — Healthy eating and food safety

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: meal timing and food combining (the А/Б/В food groups and the compatibility table), GMO products and reading their label codes, E-number additives, and fats — cholesterol and trans fats. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
