| keeping the nutrients in food before and during processing: before processing — do not soak meat and fish in water after cutting (it loses minerals); preserve fresh produce carefully right after slaughter; keep fresh vegetables in the cold for a short time only; peel vegetables to be eaten raw thinly; keep beans, dried seeds and rice in sealed containers in a dry, cool place; do not wash rice too thoroughly (it loses vitamin B₁); during processing — how nutrients change (boiling too long or repeatedly loses vitamins, especially water-soluble B, C, PP; prolonged frying loses much vitamin, especially fat-soluble A, D, E, K), so: put food into water that is already boiling or cook it straight away; cook at a temperature suited to each food; when cooking, limit stirring and turning; limit reheating food many times after preservation (original string `keeping the nutrients in food before and during processing`) | Vietnam, *Công nghệ 9 — Mô đun Chế biến thực phẩm*, printed p. 21 |
