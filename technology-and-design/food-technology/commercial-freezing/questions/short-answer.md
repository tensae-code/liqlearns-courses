# Commercial freezing — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain the speed-quality chain in two sentences.
**Answer:** Answer: Fast freezing forms small ice crystals that leave the food's cells intact, so it thaws close to fresh; slow freezing grows large crystals that rupture cells, and the food weeps and turns mushy.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Why did contact freezing change commerce, not just kitchens?
**Answer:** Answer: It froze packaged food fast and evenly at scale, so frozen food could be manufactured, transported and sold with its quality kept.
