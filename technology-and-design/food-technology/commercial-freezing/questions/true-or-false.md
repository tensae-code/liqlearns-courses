# Commercial freezing — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Fast freezing keeps ice crystals small.
**Answer: True** — Small crystals leave cells intact; the thawed food holds.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Every food freezes well if frozen fast enough.
**Answer: False** — Some foods' structures are ruined by freezing at any speed; the book distinguishes them.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Immersion freezing plunges food into a freezing liquid medium.
**Answer: True** — Liquid contact is its heat-removal route.
