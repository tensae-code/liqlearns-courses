# Commercial freezing — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Birdseye's contact freezing works by…
a) Pressing packaged food between cold plates b) Blowing warm air c) Immersing in oil d) Sun-drying first
**Answer: a) Pressing packaged food between cold plates**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Contact with the plates matters because…
a) It draws heat out fast with no insulating air gap b) It flavours the food c) It sterilises the package d) It adds water
**Answer: a) It draws heat out fast with no insulating air gap**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Blast freezing removes heat by…
a) Very cold air driven over the food b) Cold plates only c) Freezing liquid only d) Vacuum
**Answer: a) Very cold air driven over the food**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Snap freezing names…
a) Freezing very fast, before quality drains away b) Freezing in small snaps of time over a week c) Freezing only snacks d) Breaking food into pieces
**Answer: a) Freezing very fast, before quality drains away**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Slow freezing harms quality because…
a) Large ice crystals tear the food's structure b) It is too cold c) Crystals cannot form slowly d) It cooks the food
**Answer: a) Large ice crystals tear the food's structure**
