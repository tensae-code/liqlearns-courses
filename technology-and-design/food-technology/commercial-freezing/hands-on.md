# Hands-on — Commercial freezing

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## Method matcher
For three products (flat fish fillets in packs, loose peas, whole soft fruit), choose a freezing method (contact, blast, immersion) and defend the choice by shape and delicacy.

## Crystal reasoning
Write the chain in order: slow freezing → crystal size → cell damage → thawed quality. Then write the fast-freezing chain. End with one sentence: why 'frozen' on a label is not a quality promise by itself.
