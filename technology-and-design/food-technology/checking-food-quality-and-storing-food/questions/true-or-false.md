# Checking food quality and storing food — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Quality is verified before food is used, not after cooking.
**Answer: True** — The module opens with verifying the quality of food products.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Any storage place is as good as any other for keeping food quality.
**Answer: False** — The book teaches set conditions and methods for storing food products.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Checking and storing are two halves of one job: keeping food safe and good.
**Answer: True** — Verification comes first; correct storage then preserves the quality that was checked.
