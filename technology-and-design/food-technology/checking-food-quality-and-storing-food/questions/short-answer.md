# Checking food quality and storing food — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How do you verify a food product's quality?
**Answer:** You look at it, smell it and check the product and its packaging; only good food enters the kitchen.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why is a checked product still stored carefully?
**Answer:** Because storage conditions and methods decide whether it keeps its quality until it is used.
