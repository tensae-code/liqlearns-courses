# Checking food quality and storing food — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the first step before cooking with a food product in Moldova's Grade 4 book?
a) Verify its quality b) Wash it c) Cook it at once d) Serve it
**Answer: a) Verify its quality**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** How is a product's quality verified?
a) By its price b) By looking, smelling and checking the product and its packaging c) By its colour alone d) By asking the seller only
**Answer: b) By looking, smelling and checking the product and its packaging**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** What happens to a product that fails the quality check?
a) It is not used b) It is cooked longer c) It is stored first d) It is served to guests
**Answer: a) It is not used**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Why does storage matter after the quality check?
a) So the food keeps its quality until it is needed b) To make the kitchen tidy only c) To hide the food d) To change its taste
**Answer: a) So the food keeps its quality until it is needed**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** What does good storing decide?
a) The price of the product b) Who eats first c) The right place, protection and order of use for each product d) The colour of the shelves
**Answer: c) The right place, protection and order of use for each product**
