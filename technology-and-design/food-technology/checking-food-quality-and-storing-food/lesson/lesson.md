# Checking food quality and storing food

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Moldova's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Verifying the quality of food products
Moldova's Grade 4 food module opens with quality: before any cooking, the learner verifies the quality of food products — looking, smelling and checking the product and its packaging so that only good food enters the kitchen. A product that fails the check is not used.

## Sub lesson 2 — Conditions and methods for storing food
Good food stays good only if it is stored well. The book teaches the conditions and methods for storing food products — the right place, the right protection and the right order of use — so stored food keeps its quality until it is needed.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: food is checked before it is used — its quality is verified — and then stored under the right conditions and by the right method, so it stays safe and good. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
