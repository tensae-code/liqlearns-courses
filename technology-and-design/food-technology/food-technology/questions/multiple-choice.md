# Food technology — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What frames the Grade 6 food-technology module?
a) Keeping food safe — rational nutrition and preventing food poisoning b) Speed of cooking c) Expensive ingredients d) Restaurant service
**Answer: a) Keeping food safe — rational nutrition and preventing food poisoning**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What does rational nutrition mean here?
a) Eating as much as possible b) Eating in a balanced, sensible way c) Eating only one food group d) Skipping meals
**Answer: b) Eating in a balanced, sensible way**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** What must food work prevent?
a) Food poisoning b) Leftovers c) Washing up d) Menus
**Answer: a) Food poisoning**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which groups are worked as technologies?
a) Cereals and pasta, meat, and fish b) Only sweets c) Only drinks d) Only fruit
**Answer: a) Cereals and pasta, meat, and fish**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** What closes the module?
a) A test only b) A shopping trip c) The national cuisine d) A drawing lesson
**Answer: c) The national cuisine**
