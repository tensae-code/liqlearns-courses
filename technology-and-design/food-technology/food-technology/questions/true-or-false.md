# Food technology — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Preventing food poisoning is part of food technology, not an extra.
**Answer: True** — The module teaches it beside rational nutrition as the safety frame.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Each food group is worked by exactly the same technology.
**Answer: False** — Cereals and pasta, meat and fish are each worked as technologies of their own.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The national cuisine carries the module's technologies in Moldova's tradition.
**Answer: True** — The module closes with the national cuisine.
