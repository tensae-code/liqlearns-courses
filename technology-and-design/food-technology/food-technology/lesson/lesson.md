# Food technology

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Moldova's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Rational nutrition and preventing food poisoning
Moldova's Grade 6 half teaches food technology as keeping food safe: the principles of rational nutrition — eating in a balanced, sensible way — and the prevention of food poisoning, the danger that careless food work invites. Safety is the frame for everything cooked afterwards.

## Sub lesson 2 — The food groups as technologies, and the national cuisine
Each food group is then worked as a technology of its own: cereals and pasta, meat, and fish — how each is processed and cooked. The module closes with the national cuisine, the dishes that carry these technologies in Moldova's own tradition.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: food technology keeps food safe and nourishing: eat rationally, prevent food poisoning, and work each food group — cereals and pasta, meat, fish — by its own technology. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
