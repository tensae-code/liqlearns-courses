**Acceptance count: taught in 3 countries (Iran, Moldova, Mongolia).**

Moldova's own way: Moldova's Grade 6 food technology is organised as safety first — rational nutrition and poisoning prevention — then each food group worked as its own technology, closing in the national cuisine.

Iran's own way: Iran's Grade 7 food-industry module teaches preservation as the technology — refrigeration, drying, pickling with preservatives (cucumber pickling, drying herbs) — and carries it to market: home packaging of legumes with the required label information.

Mongolia's own way: Mongolia teaches this inside Visual Art & Technology (Дүрслэх урлаг, технологи) — flour products (гурилан бүтээгдэхүүн): the products, their materials and making.
