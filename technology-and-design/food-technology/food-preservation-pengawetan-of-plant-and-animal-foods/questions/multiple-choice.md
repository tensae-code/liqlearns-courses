# Food preservation (pengawetan) of plant and animal foods — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson teach?
a) Food preservation (pengawetan) of plant and animal foods b) Cooking rice c) Map reading d) Letter writing
**Answer: a) Food preservation (pengawetan) of plant and animal foods**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In the Indonesian book, where is this topic worked?
a) In a sports unit b) At Unit III Produk Pengawetan Pangan Nabati dan Hewani c) Only in an exam d) It is not in the book
**Answer: b) At Unit III Produk Pengawetan Pangan Nabati dan Hewani**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What comes first in the Prakarya sequence?
a) Selling the product b) Throwing materials away c) Observing, exploring and planning before making d) Marking the work
**Answer: c) Observing, exploring and planning before making**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Which detail belongs to this topic?
a) The detail named in the book: preservation methods and preserved-product making, packaging and labels b) Parts of a bicycle pump only c) Parts of a television only d) No details are named
**Answer: a) The detail named in the book: preservation methods and preserved-product making, packaging and labels**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** What does "local potential" (potensi lokal) mean in Prakarya?
a) Importing every material b) Building from the materials and traditions of the learner's own region c) Working only from photographs d) Skipping the planning stage
**Answer: b) Building from the materials and traditions of the learner's own region**
