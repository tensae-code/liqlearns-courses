# Technology & Design course — CURRICULUM (the path)

Technology & Design is one world course, organised by **levels,
not grades**: a learner climbs when they are ready, not when a
school calendar says so. The school grades the books come from
appear only in each lesson's sources (provenance), never as the
course's organisation.

## Ethiopia, Technical Drawing (Wave 4, built 2026-11-12; pictures 26800–26999)

The first stocked part is **Technical Drawing**, built from
Ethiopia's Grade 12 Technical Drawing **teacher guide only** —
no pupil book has been read, so every lesson stands on labelled
teacher-guide evidence and carries the 0-countries acceptance
line, and every sources file carries the era note: the guide is
an older GEQIP-generation book (2003 E.C.), taught as its era's
course, never presented as the current syllabus unchecked.

- **Band A — Units 1–3** (9 lessons, 123 questions): free-hand
  sketching, sketching techniques, isometric pictorial sketching,
  orthographic projection, auxiliary views (primary and
  secondary), sectioning words and practice, types of section,
  conventional practice in sectioning. Pictures 26800–26808,
  GIFs 26810–26815 (drawn, frame-checked).
- **Band B — Units 4–5** (7 lessons, 93 questions):
  dimensioning (lines and symbols, size and location, the two
  arranging methods, placing dimensions), surface development,
  developing solids (parallel line, radial line, triangulation)
  and intersections of solids. Pictures 26900–26904, 26906,
  26908; GIFs 26905, 26907 (drawn, frame-checked; two renders
  remade when their geometry was wrong). Honesty carried: Unit
  5's misnumbering (its lessons are numbered 6.1–6.3, there is
  no Unit 6) taught corrected and flagged in sources.

Build logs: ethiopia-2026-11-11/build-logs/wave4-td-A-units1-3.md
and wave4-td-B-units4-5.md.

## Canada, Technology in the Community (T&D expansion, stocked 2026-11-17)

Stocked from Canada's *Technology in the Community*
(Tulliniliara Reading Series, Level 14) — Government of Nunavut
(Department of Education / Department of Family Services),
developed and published in Canada by Inhabit Education, written
by Jordan Hoffman, illustrated by Ali Hinch, © 2019,
ISBN 978-0-2287-0447-8 (English edition; the Inuktitut edition
is a parallel edition of the same book, read once, placed
once). A student reader for nursery / early level, read
COMPLETE to the last teaching page (printed p. 32). All 7
placements were NEW LESSON NEEDED; each was resolved through
the T&D slug registry to its canonical lesson and built new.
Canada counts in the acceptance count (student book, not
evidence-only): every lesson below stands at taught in
1 country (Canada).

- **First Steps** (3 lessons, 13 questions each): What
  technology is (printed p. 2); Computers as community
  technology (printed pp. 5–9); GPS technology
  (printed pp. 14–17).
- **Mechanisms and Machines** (1 lesson, 13 questions): How a
  piston engine moves a snowmobile (printed pp. 10–13).
- **Electrical Installations and the Home** (1 lesson,
  13 questions): Heating technology at home
  (printed pp. 18–21).
- **Textiles and Fashion** (1 lesson, 13 questions): The
  sewing machine — stocked from the placement "How a sewing
  machine makes a stitch" (printed pp. 22–25).
- **Radiography Technology** (1 lesson, 13 questions):
  X-ray machines (printed pp. 26–30).

Canada's own way is content and is carried in each lesson's
sources: technology is taught through the learner's own
Nunavut community — home, school, store and health centre,
the snowmobile as the engine example, Coleman stoves and
qulliit beside the boiler furnace, hand sewing with needle
and sinew beside the sewing machine, and GPS through the
hunters, boaters, pilots, fishers, prospectors and coast
guard who travel the North.

Placements at
liqlearns-courses-work/expansion-td-2026-11-17/canada-placements.md.
Three further Canada records in that file are RECORD-ONLY
(Inuktitut early-reading and art books, out of scope) and are
not stocked.
