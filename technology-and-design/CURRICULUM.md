# Technology & Design course — CURRICULUM (the path)

Technology & Design is one world course, organised by **levels,
not grades**: a learner climbs when they are ready, not when a
school calendar says so. The school grades the books come from
appear only in each lesson's sources (provenance), never as the
course's organisation.

## Ethiopia, Technical Drawing (Wave 4, built 2026-11-12; pictures 26800–26999)

The first stocked part is **Technical Drawing**, built from
Ethiopia's Grade 12 Technical Drawing **teacher guide only** —
no pupil book has been read, so every lesson stands on labelled
teacher-guide evidence and carries the 0-countries acceptance
line, and every sources file carries the era note: the guide is
an older GEQIP-generation book (2003 E.C.), taught as its era's
course, never presented as the current syllabus unchecked.

- **Band A — Units 1–3** (9 lessons, 123 questions): free-hand
  sketching, sketching techniques, isometric pictorial sketching,
  orthographic projection, auxiliary views (primary and
  secondary), sectioning words and practice, types of section,
  conventional practice in sectioning. Pictures 26800–26808,
  GIFs 26810–26815 (drawn, frame-checked).
- **Band B — Units 4–5** (7 lessons, 93 questions):
  dimensioning (lines and symbols, size and location, the two
  arranging methods, placing dimensions), surface development,
  developing solids (parallel line, radial line, triangulation)
  and intersections of solids. Pictures 26900–26904, 26906,
  26908; GIFs 26905, 26907 (drawn, frame-checked; two renders
  remade when their geometry was wrong). Honesty carried: Unit
  5's misnumbering (its lessons are numbered 6.1–6.3, there is
  no Unit 6) taught corrected and flagged in sources.
