# Hands-on — Construction specifications

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: calculating sizes (pattern sizes and production size quantities), calculating raw-material and auxiliary-material quantities, raw materials and cost, technical analysis during pattern-making. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
