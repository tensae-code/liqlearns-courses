# Hands-on — Technical sewing problems

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: defects of the material to be sewn and their effect on garment construction, defects arising during sewing, sewability of fabrics, sewing-machine faults and their likely causes (with a fault-finding lab). Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
