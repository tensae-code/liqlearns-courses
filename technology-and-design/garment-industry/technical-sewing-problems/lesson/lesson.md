# Technical sewing problems

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Greece's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Technical sewing problems
Greece's book teaches it this way: defects of the material to be sewn and their effect on garment construction, defects arising during sewing, sewability of fabrics, sewing-machine faults and their likely causes (with a fault-finding lab) (*Garment Production Tech*, printed p. 231). The learner works through the named materials and steps in order, saying what each step does before doing the next one.

## Sub lesson 2 — Technical sewing problems — doing it yourself
Now the learner does the whole task unaided: defects of the material to be sewn and their effect on garment construction, defects arising during sewing, sewability of fabrics, sewing-machine faults and their likely causes (with a fault-finding lab). Working from the first sub lesson's sequence, the learner plans the make, works through it step by step, and checks the finished piece against what Greece's book shows.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: Defects of the material to be sewn and their effect on garment construction, defects arising during sewing, sewability of fabrics, sewing-machine faults and their likely causes (with a fault-finding lab). Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.

## Do It Yourself
1. Do the Do it first task again, carefully this time, using everything the lesson taught.
2. Check your work against the lesson's own checks — fix one thing you find.
3. Keep your work: it is practice evidence, and the next lesson builds on it.
