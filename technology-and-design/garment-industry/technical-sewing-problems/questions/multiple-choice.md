# Technical sewing problems — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson teach?
a) Defects of the material to be sewn and their effect on garment construction, defects arising during sewing, sewability of fabrics, sewing-machine faults and their likely causes (with a fault-finding lab) b) Only how to draw pictures c) Nothing practical d) A song to memorise
**Answer: a) Defects of the material to be sewn and their effect on garment construction, defects arising during sewing, sewability of fabrics, sewing-machine faults and their likely causes (with a fault-finding lab)**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Sub lesson 1 is about…
a) Technical sewing problems b) Cooking lunch c) A sports match d) The weather
**Answer: a) Technical sewing problems**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Sub lesson 2 asks the learner to…
a) Complete the making in full and check it b) Stop before starting c) Copy a partner's work d) Throw the materials away
**Answer: a) Complete the making in full and check it**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Greece teaches this topic inside which school book?
a) Garment Production Tech b) Only mathematics c) Only history d) It is not taught at school
**Answer: a) Garment Production Tech**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** What should the learner do first in this lesson?
a) Try the Do it first task in their own way b) Skip to the last page c) Wait to be given the answer d) Nothing at all
**Answer: a) Try the Do it first task in their own way**
