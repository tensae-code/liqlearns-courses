# Technical sewing problems — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Say in one sentence what this lesson teaches.
**Answer:** Defects of the material to be sewn and their effect on garment construction, defects arising during sewing, sewability of fabrics, sewing-machine faults and their likely causes (with a fault-finding lab).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name one material, tool or step this lesson works with.
**Answer:** Any one named in the lesson text — for example, the first step of sub lesson 1: Technical sewing problems.
