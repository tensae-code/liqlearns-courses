# Hands-on — Producing ready-made garments

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: the branches of the clothing industry, the production stages, and the equipment of each department (design–pattern, cutting, sewing, finishing, ironing, packaging), raw materials and auxiliary materials. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
