# Producing ready-made garments — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Say in one sentence what this lesson teaches.
**Answer:** The branches of the clothing industry, the production stages, and the equipment of each department (design–pattern, cutting, sewing, finishing, ironing, packaging), raw materials and auxiliary materials.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name one material, tool or step this lesson works with.
**Answer:** Any one named in the lesson text — for example, the first step of sub lesson 1: Producing ready-made garments.
