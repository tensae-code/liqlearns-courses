# Hands-on — Promoting a collection and production decisions

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: marketing, seller networks, showrooms, exhibitions, advertising, sampling; forecasting production from samples, from fashion/market trends and from statistics. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
