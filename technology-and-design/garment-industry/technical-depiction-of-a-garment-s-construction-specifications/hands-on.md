# Hands-on — Technical depiction of a garment's construction specifications

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: the technical drawing of the garment (line and style), seam types, buttoning distances, finishes — and reading/interpreting garments' technical analyses. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
