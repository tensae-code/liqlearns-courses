# Technical depiction of a garment's construction specifications

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Greece's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Technical depiction of a garment's construction specifications
Greece's book teaches it this way: the technical drawing of the garment (line and style), seam types, buttoning distances, finishes — and reading/interpreting garments' technical analyses (*Technical Analysis & Collection*, printed p. 61). The learner works through the named materials and steps in order, saying what each step does before doing the next one.

## Sub lesson 2 — Technical depiction of a garment's construction specifications — doing it yourself
Now the learner does the whole task unaided: the technical drawing of the garment (line and style), seam types, buttoning distances, finishes — and reading/interpreting garments' technical analyses. Working from the first sub lesson's sequence, the learner plans the make, works through it step by step, and checks the finished piece against what Greece's book shows.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: The technical drawing of the garment (line and style), seam types, buttoning distances, finishes — and reading/interpreting garments' technical analyses. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
