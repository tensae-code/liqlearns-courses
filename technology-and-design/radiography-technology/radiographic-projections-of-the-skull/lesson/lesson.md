# Radiographic projections of the skull

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Greece's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Radiographic projections of the skull
Greece's book teaches it this way: planes and topographic landmarks, and the standard projections (face, profile, Waters, Towne, skull base, mastoids — Schuller/Stenvers, TMJ, sella, nasal bones, zygomatic arches) (*Radiotechnology Theory*, printed p. 33); Radiographic projections of the skull (laboratory exercises) (*Radiotech Laboratory*, Ch. 2). The learner works through the named materials and steps in order, saying what each step does before doing the next one.

## Sub lesson 2 — Radiographic projections of the skull — doing it yourself
Now the learner does the whole task unaided: planes and topographic landmarks, and the standard projections (face, profile, Waters, Towne, skull base, mastoids — Schuller/Stenvers, TMJ, sella, nasal bones, zygomatic arches). Working from the first sub lesson's sequence, the learner plans the make, works through it step by step, and checks the finished piece against what Greece's book shows.
