# Hands-on — Radiographic projections of the skull

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: planes and topographic landmarks, and the standard projections (face, profile, Waters, Towne, skull base, mastoids — Schuller/Stenvers, TMJ, sella, nasal bones, zygomatic arches). Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
