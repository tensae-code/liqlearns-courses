# Radiographic projections of the skull — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Say in one sentence what this lesson teaches.
**Answer:** Planes and topographic landmarks, and the standard projections (face, profile, Waters, Towne, skull base, mastoids — Schuller/Stenvers, TMJ, sella, nasal bones, zygomatic arches).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name one material, tool or step this lesson works with.
**Answer:** Any one named in the lesson text — for example, the first step of sub lesson 1: Radiographic projections of the skull.
