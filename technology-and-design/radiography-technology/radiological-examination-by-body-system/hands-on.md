Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: the projection technique for: chest bones and shoulder girdle; spine; pelvic zone; lower limbs; upper limbs. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
