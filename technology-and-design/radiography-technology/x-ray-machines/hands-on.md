# Hands-on — X-ray machines

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## Light and shadow test
Shine a torch through a thin cloth, then through a thick book. Notice what passes and what is blocked, and how the shadow differs. Write two sentences linking this to why soft parts and bone show differently on an X-ray picture.

## Read a picture
Look at the lesson's X-ray picture of a hand. Count the bones you can pick out in one finger, then find the same finger's outline in the ordinary photo. Tell a partner how a doctor could use the X-ray to check for a break.
