# X-ray machines — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What are X-rays?
a) Invisible energy like light b) A kind of sound c) A liquid medicine d) Visible coloured light
**Answer: a) Invisible energy like light**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why does bone show white on the X-ray picture?
a) X-rays cannot pass through dense bone, so it shows white b) Bone is painted white c) Bone glows by itself d) The paper is white everywhere
**Answer: a) X-rays cannot pass through dense bone, so it shows white**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** How do skin and muscle show on the special paper?
a) Black, because X-rays pass through them b) White c) Bright red d) They do not show at all
**Answer: a) Black, because X-rays pass through them**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Which is a use of X-ray machines?
a) Checking for broken bones b) Heating homes c) Sewing clothing d) Finding satellites
**Answer: a) Checking for broken bones**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Who uses X-rays to check teeth?
a) Dentists b) Plumbers c) Pilots d) Mechanics
**Answer: a) Dentists**
