# X-ray machines — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** X-rays can pass through things that light cannot.
**Answer: True** — That passing-through power is what makes the inside picture possible.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Bone shows black on the X-ray picture because X-rays pass through it easily.
**Answer: False** — Bone stops X-rays and shows white; soft parts let them pass and show black.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** X-ray pictures can help a dentist check hidden parts of teeth.
**Answer: True** — Dentists checking teeth is one of the named uses.
