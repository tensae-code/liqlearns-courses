# X-ray machines — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain in one or two sentences why an X-ray picture shows the inside of the body.
**Answer:** X-rays pass through skin and muscle, which show black on the special paper, but they cannot pass through dense bone, which shows white — so the bones stand out as a picture of the inside.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Name three uses of X-ray machines.
**Answer:** Checking broken bones, looking for illness inside the body, and dentists checking teeth.
