# Hands-on — Factors that determine the quality of the radiographic image — laboratory practice

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: using the radiology-lab equipment, radiation–matter interaction, setting exposure factors, focal–film distance, subject movement, focal-spot size, anti-scatter grid choice, collimating the radiation field. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
