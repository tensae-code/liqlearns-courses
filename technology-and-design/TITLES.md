# Technology & Design course — titles, FROM THE BOTTOM

Technology & Design is one world course: making and drawing
things works the same everywhere; only the way of teaching
differs, and the way shows inside as examples. The path climbs
from the hand to the drawing board to the workshop.

**First Steps → Technical Drawing → Design and Making**

The first stocked title is **Technical Drawing** (technical-drawing/),
built from Ethiopia's Grade 12 teacher guide (2003 E.C.,
GEQIP generation) as labelled evidence — see CURRICULUM.md and
ABOUT.md for the honesty terms.
