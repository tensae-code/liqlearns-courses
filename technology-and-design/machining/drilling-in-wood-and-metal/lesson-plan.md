# Lesson plan — Drilling in wood and metal

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Machining · **Lesson:** Drilling in wood and metal
**Place in the curriculum:** Machining (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Cuba expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** drilling makes a true hole in wood or metal: the brace (berbiquí) turns the bit by hand, the drilling machine by power — and the skill is choosing the bit, holding the work, and drilling straight, centred and to depth.
**This lesson's own level:** stocked from Cuba's *Educación Laboral — Trabajos de Taller y Dibujo Básico — 7mo grado (1989 edition)* and the Cuba books in lesson/sources.md — see lesson/sources.md. Cuba counts in the acceptance count for its student-book placements; its programme and methodological-orientations placements are evidence only, counted nowhere.

## Sub lessons
1. **The brace, bits and drills** — Cuba's 1989 book teaches drilling inside its tools chapter: the berbiquí (brace) with its chuck, the barrenas and brocas (bits and drills) it turns, and the drilling machines that do the same work by power. Each bit belongs to its material.
2. **Drilling technique** — A good hole is made before the bit turns: mark the centre, secure the work, start true, and keep the drill square to the surface. Wood and metal differ in bit, speed and feel — the driller reads the material while drilling.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
