# Drilling in wood and metal — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In your own words, what is brace (berbiquí)?
**Answer:** A hand tool that turns a bit by a cranked handle.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In your own words, what is chuck?
**Answer:** The clamp that grips the bit in the brace or machine.
