# Drilling in wood and metal — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is brace (berbiquí)?
a) a hand tool that turns a bit by a cranked handle b) Only a price c) Only decoration d) Nothing to do with this lesson
**Answer: a) a hand tool that turns a bit by a cranked handle**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is bit/drill (broca)?
a) Only history b) the cutting tool that makes the hole c) Only storage d) A kind of glue
**Answer: b) the cutting tool that makes the hole**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is chuck?
a) Only old tools b) Only painting c) the clamp that grips the bit in the brace or machine d) Only a price
**Answer: c) the clamp that grips the bit in the brace or machine**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson teach, in one line?
a) Only decoration b) Nothing to do with this lesson c) Only history d) drilling makes a true hole in wood or metal: the brace (berbiquí) turns the bit by hand, the drilling machine by power — and the skill is choosing the bit, holding the work, and drilling straight, centred and to depth.
**Answer: d) drilling makes a true hole in wood or metal: the brace (berbiquí) turns the bit by hand, the drilling machine by power — and the skill is choosing the bit, holding the work, and drilling straight, centred and to depth.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** How does Cuba's book teach this?
a) As theory only, never practised b) Only in a glossary c) Only as an exam d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Spanish
**Answer: d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Spanish**
