# Drilling in wood and metal — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Brace (berbiquí) is a hand tool that turns a bit by a cranked handle.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Bit/drill (broca) has nothing to do with this lesson.
**Answer: False** — It is part of the lesson: the cutting tool that makes the hole.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Chuck is the clamp that grips the bit in the brace or machine.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.
