# Hands-on — Drilling in wood and metal

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## Choose bit and tool
For four jobs — a screw hole in a board, a bolt hole in sheet metal, a deep hole in a beam, a small hole in thin plastic — name the tool and bit, and say why.

## Mark and check a hole
On scrap (or on paper over scrap), mark three hole centres, show how the work is secured, and check each mark for squareness of approach before any drilling.
