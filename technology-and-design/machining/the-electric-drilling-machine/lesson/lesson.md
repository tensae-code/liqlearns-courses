# The electric drilling machine

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Moldova's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — The machine part by part, and its transmission
Grade 6 teaches the electric drilling machine (the drill press) by construction: base, work table, machine vice, chuck, fixed head, belt pulley, spindle, transmission belt, column, guard, electric motor and feed lever. The parts are one story — rotation passes from the electric motor through the transmission belt and pulley to the spindle, and so to the drill in the chuck.

## Sub lesson 2 — Safety norms and the set hole
Operation is governed by norms: fix the piece firmly, fix the bit securely, and never touch a bit just after drilling — it can be hot. The lesson's set work proves the machine: drilling a 12 mm blind hole, 40 mm deep.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: the electric drilling machine is learned part by part — base to feed lever — as one transmission story: the motor's rotation passes through belt and spindle to the drill, under safety norms that fix piece and bit before any hole. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
