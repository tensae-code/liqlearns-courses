# The electric drilling machine — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The feed lever drives the motor.
**Answer: False** — The motor drives rotation through belt and spindle; the feed lever feeds the drill into the work.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A drill bit can be hot just after drilling and must not be touched.
**Answer: True** — That is one of the lesson's stated safety norms.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A blind hole of set depth is the lesson's proving work.
**Answer: True** — The set work is a 12 mm blind hole, 40 mm deep.
