# The electric drilling machine — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Trace the transmission in order.
**Answer:** Electric motor → transmission belt and belt pulley → spindle → drill in the chuck.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** State the three safety norms.
**Answer:** Fix the piece firmly; fix the bit securely; never touch a bit just after drilling — it can be hot.
