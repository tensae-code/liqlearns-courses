# The electric drilling machine — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which part holds the workpiece?
a) The machine vice, on the work table b) The chuck c) The guard d) The feed lever
**Answer: a) The machine vice, on the work table**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which part holds the drill bit?
a) The base b) The chuck c) The column d) The belt
**Answer: b) The chuck**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** How does rotation reach the drill?
a) From the motor, through the transmission belt and pulley, to the spindle b) By hand cranking c) Through the column d) By the feed lever alone
**Answer: a) From the motor, through the transmission belt and pulley, to the spindle**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** What are the safety norms?
a) Fix the piece firmly, fix the bit securely, never touch a hot bit just after drilling b) Work fast, hold by hand, touch to test heat c) No norms are given d) Only gloves matter
**Answer: a) Fix the piece firmly, fix the bit securely, never touch a hot bit just after drilling**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** What is the lesson's set hole?
a) A 40 mm through hole b) A 12 mm hole 12 mm deep c) A 12 mm blind hole, 40 mm deep d) No set hole
**Answer: c) A 12 mm blind hole, 40 mm deep**
