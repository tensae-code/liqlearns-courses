# Hands-on — The machine shop

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: description and organisation of the workshop, its main mechanical and electrical equipment, layout of spaces, safe movement of materials and tools, workshop safety rules, PPE and hygiene rules. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
