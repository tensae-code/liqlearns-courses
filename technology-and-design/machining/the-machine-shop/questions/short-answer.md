# The machine shop — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Say in one sentence what this lesson teaches.
**Answer:** Description and organisation of the workshop, its main mechanical and electrical equipment, layout of spaces, safe movement of materials and tools, workshop safety rules, PPE and hygiene rules.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name one material, tool or step this lesson works with.
**Answer:** Any one named in the lesson text — for example, the first step of sub lesson 1: The machine shop.
