# Turning and the lathe — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What must come before starting the lathe?
a) The teacher's permission b) A finished drawing only c) A partner's guess d) Nothing
**Answer: a) The teacher's permission**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is checked before starting?
a) The weather b) Guards and fixings c) The clock d) The price
**Answer: b) Guards and fixings**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** How is the worker dressed for the lathe?
a) Head covered, overall fastened, eyes protected b) Loose sleeves, bare head c) Gloves only d) Any way
**Answer: a) Head covered, overall fastened, eyes protected**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** What cuts an external thread?
a) A die (filieră) b) A tap c) A file d) A plane
**Answer: a) A die (filieră)**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** What cuts an internal thread?
a) A die b) A chisel c) A tap (tarod) d) A die and tap together
**Answer: c) A tap (tarod)**
