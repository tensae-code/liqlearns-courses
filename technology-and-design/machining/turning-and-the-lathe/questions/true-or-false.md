# Turning and the lathe — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Workpiece and tool must both be fixed securely on the lathe.
**Answer: True** — That is one of the stated safety rules.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Dies cut internal threads and taps cut external ones.
**Answer: False** — It is the other way: dies (filiere) outside, taps (tarozi) inside.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The threading lathe cuts threads with special threading tools.
**Answer: True** — The third route to a thread is the lathe's own threading tools.
