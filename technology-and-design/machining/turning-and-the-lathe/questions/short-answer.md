# Turning and the lathe — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** List the lathe safety rules.
**Answer:** Start only with the teacher's permission; check guards and fixings; cover the head; fasten the overall; protect the eyes; fix workpiece and tool securely.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the three threading routes and their tools.
**Answer:** External threads with dies (filiere), internal threads with taps (tarozi), and threads on the threading lathe with special threading tools.
