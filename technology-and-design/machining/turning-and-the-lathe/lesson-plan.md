# Lesson plan — Turning and the lathe

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Machining · **Lesson:** Turning and the lathe
**Place in the curriculum:** Machining (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Moldova expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** lathe work begins with its safety rules — teacher's permission, guards and fixings checked, head covered, overall fastened, eyes protected, workpiece and tool secure — and rises to cutting threads: outside with dies, inside with taps, on the threading lathe with its special tools.
**This lesson's own level:** stocked from Moldova's *Educație tehnologică* (Technological Education) textbooks — see lesson/sources.md. Moldova counts in the acceptance count (student books, not evidence-only).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **Safety rules of the lathe** — Grade 9 lathe work opens with rules, and they are strict: start only with the teacher's permission; check guards and fixings; cover the head; fasten the overall; protect the eyes; fix workpiece and tool securely.
2. **Cutting threads — dies, taps and the threading lathe** — The taught work is threading.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of Machining. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lathe safety rules in order.
2. Tell how external and internal threads are cut, and with what.
3. Explain how Moldova's book teaches it.
## What you need
A lathe to study under supervision, dies (filiere) and taps (tarozi) to handle.
