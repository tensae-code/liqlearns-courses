# Hands-on — Turning and the lathe

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## Rules rehearsal
Write the lathe safety rules as a before-start checklist in order. Rehearse it at the machine (switched off) until it is word-perfect.

## Thread routes
Make a three-row table: external thread, internal thread, lathe-cut thread — with the tool for each (die, tap, threading tool) and one example of each.
