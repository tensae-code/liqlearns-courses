# Turning and the lathe

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Moldova's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Safety rules of the lathe
Grade 9 lathe work opens with rules, and they are strict: start only with the teacher's permission; check guards and fixings; cover the head; fasten the overall; protect the eyes; fix workpiece and tool securely. The lathe is the book's most powerful machine, and its rules are the lesson's first content.

## Sub lesson 2 — Cutting threads — dies, taps and the threading lathe
The taught work is threading. External threads are cut with dies (filiere); internal threads with taps (tarozi); and on the threading lathe, threads are cut with special threading tools. Three routes to the screw thread, each with its tool.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: lathe work begins with its safety rules — teacher's permission, guards and fixings checked, head covered, overall fastened, eyes protected, workpiece and tool secure — and rises to cutting threads: outside with dies, inside with taps, on the threading lathe with its special tools. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
