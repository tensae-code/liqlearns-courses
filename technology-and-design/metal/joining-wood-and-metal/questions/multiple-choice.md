# Joining wood and metal — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is joint?
a) the place and method where two parts are connected b) Only a price c) Only decoration d) Nothing to do with this lesson
**Answer: a) the place and method where two parts are connected**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is sharpening (afilado)?
a) Only history b) restoring a tool's cutting edge c) Only storage d) A kind of glue
**Answer: b) restoring a tool's cutting edge**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is workshop article?
a) Only old tools b) Only painting c) a useful article made in the school workshop d) Only a price
**Answer: c) a useful article made in the school workshop**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson teach, in one line?
a) Only decoration b) Nothing to do with this lesson c) Only history d) workshop articles join across materials: the wood joints of the article and the metal joints that complete it are one design problem — choose the joint for both materials, sharpen the tools, and finish the whole.
**Answer: d) workshop articles join across materials: the wood joints of the article and the metal joints that complete it are one design problem — choose the joint for both materials, sharpen the tools, and finish the whole.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** How does Cuba's book teach this?
a) As theory only, never practised b) Only in a glossary c) Only as an exam d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Spanish
**Answer: d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Spanish**
