# Hands-on — Joining wood and metal

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## Choose joints for one article
Design a small article of wood and metal (a box with a metal handle, a stand with a bracket). Name the joint for each connection and say why it suits both materials.

## Sharpening check
Examine a cutting tool (or its picture). Say how you can tell it needs sharpening, and what a bad edge would do to a joint.
