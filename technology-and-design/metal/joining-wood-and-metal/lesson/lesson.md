# Joining wood and metal

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Cuba's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Joining in workshop articles
Cuba's Grade 9 workshop chapter (Trabajos de taller) treats joining as the article's own problem: wood joints in workshop articles first, then, closing the chapter, metal joints in workshop articles. The article decides the joint, not habit.

## Sub lesson 2 — Sharpening and finishing the joined article
Joining stands between two companions: sharpening (afilado), because a blunt tool makes a bad joint, and finishing, because the joined article is completed and protected as one piece. The chapter teaches all three together.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: workshop articles join across materials: the wood joints of the article and the metal joints that complete it are one design problem — choose the joint for both materials, sharpen the tools, and finish the whole. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.

## Do It Yourself
1. Do the Do it first task again, carefully this time, using everything the lesson taught.
2. Check your work against the lesson's own checks — fix one thing you find.
3. Keep your work: it is practice evidence, and the next lesson builds on it.
