# Lesson plan — Sheet metal work — marking out, cutting, sawing, filing and drilling

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Metal · **Lesson:** Sheet metal work — marking out, cutting, sawing, filing and drilling
**Place in the curriculum:** Metal (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Iran expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** sheet metal work follows one order: mark out truly with scriber and needle, cut with the tool that fits the material, then saw, file and drill to the line — each step with its safety rule.
**This lesson's own level:** stocked from Iran's *Work & Technology, Grade 8* (student book) — see lesson/sources.md. Iran counts in the acceptance count (student books, not evidence-only).

## Sub lessons
1. **Marking out and cutting** — Work begins on the flat sheet: the pattern is marked out with a scriber and needle, whose fine lines survive handling as pencil would not.
2. **Sawing and filing** — Sawing starts with a small groove so the blade does not skate, and proceeds with steady strokes; filing follows its own safety rules — handle secure, work clamped, strokes in one direction — until the surface is filed clean and bright, rust removed.
3. **Centre-punching and drilling** — Before drilling, the hole's centre is marked with a centre punch (سنبه نشان): the punched dent stops the drill point wandering across the sheet.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
