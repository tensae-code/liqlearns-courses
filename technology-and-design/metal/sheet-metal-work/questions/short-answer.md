# Sheet metal work — marking out, cutting, sawing, filing and drilling — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In your own words, what is scriber?
**Answer:** The pointed tool that scratches fine marking lines on metal.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In your own words, what is snips?
**Answer:** The shears that cut thin sheet metal along marked lines.
