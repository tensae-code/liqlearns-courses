# Texturing and patina

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Iran's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Texturing with the cluster punch
A smooth field makes raised design hard to read; texture gives it contrast. The cluster punch — a punch with a group of points — roughens the surface with many small marks at once, worked over the background so the raised design stands smooth against it. Iran's Grade 9 module names the tool and its job: roughening the surface, deliberately and evenly.

## Sub lesson 2 — Patina: bitumen and heat
Colour comes last. Bitumen is worked over the copper and heat applied: the bitumen darkens in the hollows and textures while the raised parts take the heat's colour — the patina. Cleaned back, the design reads in dark and bright copper. The module's finishing sequence — texture, colour with bitumen and heat, finish — completes the plaque the flowchart promised.
