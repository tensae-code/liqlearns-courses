# Texturing and patina — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In your own words, what is cluster punch?
**Answer:** A punch with a group of points that roughens a surface with many marks at once.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In your own words, what is bitumen?
**Answer:** The dark tarry substance used to colour the copper's hollows.
