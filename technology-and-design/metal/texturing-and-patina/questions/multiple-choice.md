# Texturing and patina — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is cluster punch?
a) a punch with a group of points that roughens a surface with many marks at once b) Only a price c) Only decoration d) Nothing to do with this lesson
**Answer: a) a punch with a group of points that roughens a surface with many marks at once**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is patina?
a) Only history b) the coloured surface finish on metal, here from bitumen and heat c) Only storage d) A kind of glue
**Answer: b) the coloured surface finish on metal, here from bitumen and heat**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is bitumen?
a) Only old tools b) Only painting c) the dark tarry substance used to colour the copper's hollows d) Only a price
**Answer: c) the dark tarry substance used to colour the copper's hollows**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson teach, in one line?
a) Only decoration b) Nothing to do with this lesson c) Only history d) the plaque is finished by texture and colour: the surface is roughened with the cluster punch, then coloured with bitumen and heat — the patina that completes the work.
**Answer: d) the plaque is finished by texture and colour: the surface is roughened with the cluster punch, then coloured with bitumen and heat — the patina that completes the work.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** How does Iran's book teach this?
a) As theory only, never practised b) Only in a glossary c) Only as an exam d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Persian
**Answer: d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Persian**
