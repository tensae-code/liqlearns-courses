# Texturing and patina — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Cluster punch is a punch with a group of points that roughens a surface with many marks at once.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Patina has nothing to do with this lesson.
**Answer: False** — It is part of the lesson: the coloured surface finish on metal, here from bitumen and heat.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Bitumen is the dark tarry substance used to colour the copper's hollows.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.
