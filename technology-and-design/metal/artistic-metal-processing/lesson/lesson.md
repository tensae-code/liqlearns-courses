# Artistic metal processing

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Moldova's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Traditional objects of forged metal
Grade 9's metal part looks at the tradition: the objects of forged metal that Moldovan craft hands down — iron shaped by the forge into things of use that carry art in their making. The objects come first, as the standard the techniques serve.

## Sub lesson 2 — Executing metal decor
The techniques follow: how decor is executed on forged metal — the forge's ways of giving plain iron its ornament. Decor is not added from another craft; it is executed in the metal, by the metalworker's own techniques.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: artistic metal processing is the forge's art: traditional objects of forged metal, and the techniques for executing metal decor upon them. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
