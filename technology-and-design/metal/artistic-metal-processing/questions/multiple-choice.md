# Artistic metal processing — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the metal part study first?
a) Traditional objects of forged metal b) Metal prices c) Modern factories only d) Metal drawing
**Answer: a) Traditional objects of forged metal**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** How are the traditional objects made?
a) Cast in sand only b) Forged — shaped at the forge c) Woven d) Carved from wood
**Answer: b) Forged — shaped at the forge**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is executed on the objects?
a) Metal decor b) Paint only c) Paper patterns d) Nothing — they stay plain
**Answer: a) Metal decor**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** How is the decor made?
a) In the metal, by the metalworker's own techniques b) Glued on afterwards c) Printed d) Sewn on
**Answer: a) In the metal, by the metalworker's own techniques**

**5. (Sub lesson 1)** What do the traditional objects carry?
a) Only use b) Only art c) Use and art together d) Neither
**Answer: c) Use and art together**
