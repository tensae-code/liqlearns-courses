# Artistic metal processing — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the part study?
**Answer:** Traditional objects of forged metal, and the techniques for executing metal decor.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How does decor belong to forged metal?
**Answer:** It is executed in the metal itself by the metalworker's techniques, not added from another craft.
