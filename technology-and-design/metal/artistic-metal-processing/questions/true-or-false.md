# Artistic metal processing — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Forged-metal objects are traditional craft objects in Moldova's book.
**Answer: True** — The part studies the traditional objects of forged metal.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Metal decor is glued or painted on from another craft.
**Answer: False** — Decor is executed in the metal by the metalworker's techniques.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The objects are studied before the decor techniques.
**Answer: True** — Objects first, as the standard; techniques after, in their service.
