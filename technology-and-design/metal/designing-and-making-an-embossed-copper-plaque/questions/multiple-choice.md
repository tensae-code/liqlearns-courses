# Designing and making an embossed copper plaque — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is copper sheet?
a) copper rolled flat, sold in graded thicknesses b) Only a price c) Only decoration d) Nothing to do with this lesson
**Answer: a) copper rolled flat, sold in graded thicknesses**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is plaque?
a) Only history b) a flat decorative plate, here of embossed copper c) Only storage d) A kind of glue
**Answer: b) a flat decorative plate, here of embossed copper**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is flowchart?
a) Only old tools b) Only painting c) a diagram planning work as ordered steps d) Only a price
**Answer: c) a diagram planning work as ordered steps**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson teach, in one line?
a) Only decoration b) Nothing to do with this lesson c) Only history d) the embossed copper plaque is planned before it is touched: the copper sheet and its thicknesses chosen, and the whole make planned with a flowchart.
**Answer: d) the embossed copper plaque is planned before it is touched: the copper sheet and its thicknesses chosen, and the whole make planned with a flowchart.**
