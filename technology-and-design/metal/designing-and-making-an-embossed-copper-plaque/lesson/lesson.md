# Designing and making an embossed copper plaque

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Iran's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — The sheet and its thicknesses
Copper sheet comes in thicknesses that decide the work: too thin tears and buckles under the tools; too thick refuses the design's fine relief. The plaque's size and design choose the thickness. Iran's Grade 9 Copper-Embossing module teaches the sheet and its thicknesses as the project's first decision.

## Sub lesson 2 — Planning with a flowchart
The make is planned with a flowchart — the steps drawn in order, each step's need known before work starts: prepare the sheet, transfer the design, raise the lines, raise the surfaces, texture, colour, finish. The flowchart is the project's map; the embossing lessons that follow walk it step by step.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: the embossed copper plaque is planned before it is touched: the copper sheet and its thicknesses chosen, and the whole make planned with a flowchart. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.

## Do It Yourself
1. Do the Do it first task again, carefully this time, using everything the lesson taught.
2. Check your work against the lesson's own checks — fix one thing you find.
3. Keep your work: it is practice evidence, and the next lesson builds on it.
