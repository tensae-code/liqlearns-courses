# Hands-on — Wire puzzle games

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: the Mongolian wire puzzles of Ж.Дамбарал, judging a puzzle by material, tool use and difficulty, and making one from 3 mm wire. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
