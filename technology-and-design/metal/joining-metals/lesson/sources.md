**Acceptance count: taught in 3 countries (Cuba, Fiji, Greece).**

Cuba's own way: Cuba closes the Grade 9 workshop chapter with metal joints in workshop articles — joining is taught as the article's own problem, in wood first and then in metal.

Fiji's own way: Fiji teaches joining metals in Year 10 by riveting (including mushroom-head rivets), screws with pilot holes, and soldering with its fluxes — zinc chloride and resin.

Greece's own way: Greece teaches this inside its Greek-language Gymnasio and EPAL technology books (Ministry of Education / ITYE Diophantos) — kinds of connection, safety/PPE, and carrying out joining work. The Greek classroom's own examples named in the provenance rows above are part of the teaching, not decoration.
