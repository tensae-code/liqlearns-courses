# Maintenance of metals

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Rwanda's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Why metals need maintenance
The materials unit's order is the teaching: classification first, uses second, maintenance last. A metal object in daily use meets air, water and work; without care it loses the very characteristics that made it worth choosing.

## Sub lesson 2 — Keeping metals in good condition
Maintenance of metals means the regular care that keeps a metal object doing its job — the same keeping habit Rwanda teaches for tools and made objects, now applied to the metal family itself. Care is scheduled, not accidental: the object is looked after before it fails, not after.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: metals serve long only when they are maintained — kept clean, protected and cared for — and Rwanda teaches that care as its own lesson, after the uses of metals are known. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
