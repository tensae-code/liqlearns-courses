# Maintenance of metals — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In what order does Rwanda's materials unit teach metals?
a) Maintenance, uses, classification b) Classification, uses, maintenance c) Uses only d) Prices first
**Answer: b) Classification, uses, maintenance**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What does a metal object meet in daily use?
a) Nothing b) Air, water and work c) Only praise d) Only storage
**Answer: b) Air, water and work**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** When is care given?
a) Only after the object fails b) Regularly, before it fails c) Never d) Once a decade
**Answer: b) Regularly, before it fails**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Maintenance of metals belongs to which wider habit?
a) The keeping habit taught for tools and made objects b) Shopping c) Drawing d) Guessing
**Answer: a) The keeping habit taught for tools and made objects**

**5. (Sub lesson 1)** What does an unmaintained metal lose?
a) Its name b) The characteristics that made it worth choosing c) Its owner d) Nothing
**Answer: b) The characteristics that made it worth choosing**
