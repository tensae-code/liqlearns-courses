# Maintenance of metals — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Maintenance is the last of the three metals lessons in Rwanda's unit.
**Answer: True** — The order is classification, uses of common metals, then maintenance of metals.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Care is something you do only after a metal object breaks.
**Answer: False** — Maintenance is regular care given before failure.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The keeping habit for metals is the same habit taught for tools and made objects.
**Answer: True** — Rwanda applies one care discipline across tools, made objects and metals.
