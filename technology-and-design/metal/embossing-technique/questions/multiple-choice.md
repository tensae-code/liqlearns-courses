# Embossing technique — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is relief?
a) a design raised above its background surface b) Only a price c) Only decoration d) Nothing to do with this lesson
**Answer: a) a design raised above its background surface**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is embossing tool?
a) Only history b) a smooth-ended tool that pushes metal into relief without cutting it c) Only storage d) A kind of glue
**Answer: b) a smooth-ended tool that pushes metal into relief without cutting it**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is tool pressure?
a) Only old tools b) Only painting c) the force applied through the tool, which decides the relief's depth d) Only a price
**Answer: c) the force applied through the tool, which decides the relief's depth**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson teach, in one line?
a) Only decoration b) Nothing to do with this lesson c) Only history d) embossing raises the design in two passes — the lines first, then the surfaces between them — with tool pressure tested before the real work begins.
**Answer: d) embossing raises the design in two passes — the lines first, then the surfaces between them — with tool pressure tested before the real work begins.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** How does Iran's book teach this?
a) As theory only, never practised b) Only in a glossary c) Only as an exam d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Persian
**Answer: d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Persian**
