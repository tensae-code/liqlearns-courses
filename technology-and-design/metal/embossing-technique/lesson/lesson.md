# Embossing technique

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Iran's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Raise the lines
The design's lines are raised first: working from the back of the sheet with the embossing tools, the lines are pushed out along the transferred design. Line work fixes the design's skeleton — a line raised wrong cannot be hidden by later work, so the pass is slow and checked.

## Sub lesson 2 — Raise the surfaces — pressure tested first
The surfaces between the lines are raised next, giving the design its full relief. Tool pressure decides everything: too light leaves the relief flat; too heavy tears the sheet. So the module's rule comes first — test tool pressure on a spare piece before the plaque itself. Iran's Grade 9 module fixes that order: pressure tested, lines raised, surfaces raised.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: embossing raises the design in two passes — the lines first, then the surfaces between them — with tool pressure tested before the real work begins. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
