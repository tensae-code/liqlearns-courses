# Metals and alloys — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Alloy is a metal mixed with other elements for better properties.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Malleability has nothing to do with this lesson.
**Answer: False** — It is part of the lesson: a metal's capacity to be hammered or rolled into sheet without breaking.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Corrosion is the eating-away of a metal by its environment, as in rusting and oxidation.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.
