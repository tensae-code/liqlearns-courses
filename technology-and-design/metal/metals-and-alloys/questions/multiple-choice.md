# Metals and alloys — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is alloy?
a) a metal mixed with other elements for better properties b) Only a price c) Only decoration d) Nothing to do with this lesson
**Answer: a) a metal mixed with other elements for better properties**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is malleability?
a) Only history b) a metal's capacity to be hammered or rolled into sheet without breaking c) Only storage d) A kind of glue
**Answer: b) a metal's capacity to be hammered or rolled into sheet without breaking**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is corrosion?
a) Only old tools b) Only painting c) the eating-away of a metal by its environment, as in rusting and oxidation d) Only a price
**Answer: c) the eating-away of a metal by its environment, as in rusting and oxidation**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson teach, in one line?
a) Only decoration b) Nothing to do with this lesson c) Only history d) metals are known by their properties — physical, chemical, mechanical and technological — and sheet metal and wire by their own: plasticity, hardness and conductivity decide what each metal can become.
**Answer: d) metals are known by their properties — physical, chemical, mechanical and technological — and sheet metal and wire by their own: plasticity, hardness and conductivity decide what each metal can become.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** How does Cuba's book teach this?
a) As theory only, never practised b) Only in a glossary c) Only as an exam d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Spanish
**Answer: d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Spanish**
