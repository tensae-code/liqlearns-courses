# Metals and alloys — matching

Match each item on the left with the one on the right.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** alloy — a metal mixed with other elements for better properties

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** malleability — a metal's capacity to be hammered or rolled into sheet without breaking
