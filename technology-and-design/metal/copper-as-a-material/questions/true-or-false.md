# Copper as a material — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Copper is the reddish metal, among the first humans worked, that bends and takes impressions readily.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Hammering has nothing to do with this lesson.
**Answer: False** — It is part of the lesson: shaping metal by repeated blows, copper's first working method.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Embossing is raising a design in relief on a metal sheet.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.
