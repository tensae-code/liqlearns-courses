# Copper as a material — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is copper?
a) the reddish metal, among the first humans worked, that bends and takes impressions readily b) Only a price c) Only decoration d) Nothing to do with this lesson
**Answer: a) the reddish metal, among the first humans worked, that bends and takes impressions readily**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is hammering?
a) Only history b) shaping metal by repeated blows, copper's first working method c) Only storage d) A kind of glue
**Answer: b) shaping metal by repeated blows, copper's first working method**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is embossing?
a) Only old tools b) Only painting c) raising a design in relief on a metal sheet d) Only a price
**Answer: c) raising a design in relief on a metal sheet**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson teach, in one line?
a) Only decoration b) Nothing to do with this lesson c) Only history d) copper was humanity's first worked metal: early humans discovered it and learned to shape it to meet their needs — the opening of Iran's copper-embossing craft.
**Answer: d) copper was humanity's first worked metal: early humans discovered it and learned to shape it to meet their needs — the opening of Iran's copper-embossing craft.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** How does Iran's book teach this?
a) As theory only, never practised b) Only in a glossary c) Only as an exam d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Persian
**Answer: d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Persian**
