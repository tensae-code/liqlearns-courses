# Copper as a material — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In your own words, what is copper?
**Answer:** The reddish metal, among the first humans worked, that bends and takes impressions readily.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In your own words, what is embossing?
**Answer:** Raising a design in relief on a metal sheet.
