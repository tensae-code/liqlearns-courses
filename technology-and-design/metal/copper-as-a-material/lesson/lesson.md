# Copper as a material

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Iran's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — The discovery of copper
Copper appears in nature sometimes as pure metal, needing no smelting to reveal it — which is why it was the first metal humans used. Early people found it, noticed it could be hammered without breaking, and learned that heating and working changed its hardness. Iran's Grade 9 Copper-Embossing module opens with this discovery story, because the craft it teaches is one of humanity's oldest.

## Sub lesson 2 — Shaping copper to needs
Copper's gift is its obedience: it bends, stretches and takes impressions under tools, yet holds the shape given. Early humans shaped it into tools, vessels and ornaments — each need teaching a technique. The module's embossing craft stands in that line: the same shaping, raised to decoration.
