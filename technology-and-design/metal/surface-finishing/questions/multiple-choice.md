# Surface finishing — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is surface preparation?
a) cleaning and smoothing a surface so a finish can take hold b) Only a price c) Only decoration d) Nothing to do with this lesson
**Answer: a) cleaning and smoothing a surface so a finish can take hold**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is finishing material?
a) Only history b) a coating — paint, varnish, lacquer and their kin — applied as the final layer c) Only storage d) A kind of glue
**Answer: b) a coating — paint, varnish, lacquer and their kin — applied as the final layer**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is finish?
a) Only old tools b) Only painting c) the final protective and decorative surface of an article d) Only a price
**Answer: c) the final protective and decorative surface of an article**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson teach, in one line?
a) Only decoration b) Nothing to do with this lesson c) Only history d) finishing is the last making stage and its own skill: prepare the surface of wood or metal, know the finishing materials, and give the article its final face and its protection.
**Answer: d) finishing is the last making stage and its own skill: prepare the surface of wood or metal, know the finishing materials, and give the article its final face and its protection.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** How does Cuba's book teach this?
a) As theory only, never practised b) Only in a glossary c) Only as an exam d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Spanish
**Answer: d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Spanish**
