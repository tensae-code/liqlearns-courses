# Surface finishing — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Surface preparation is cleaning and smoothing a surface so a finish can take hold.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Finishing material has nothing to do with this lesson.
**Answer: False** — It is part of the lesson: a coating — paint, varnish, lacquer and their kin — applied as the final layer.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Finish is the final protective and decorative surface of an article.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.
