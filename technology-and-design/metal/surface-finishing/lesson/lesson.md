# Surface finishing

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Cuba's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Preparing the surface
Cuba's 1989 book closes its making chapter with surface finishing: preparing wood and metal surfaces — cleaning, smoothing, levelling — because no finish can hide a badly prepared surface.

## Sub lesson 2 — Finishing materials
On the prepared surface go the finishing materials, chosen for the material beneath and the use ahead: the finish decorates, but its first job is protection. The learner names the material, the surface it suits, and the order of application.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: finishing is the last making stage and its own skill: prepare the surface of wood or metal, know the finishing materials, and give the article its final face and its protection. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.

## Do It Yourself
1. Do the Do it first task again, carefully this time, using everything the lesson taught.
2. Check your work against the lesson's own checks — fix one thing you find.
3. Keep your work: it is practice evidence, and the next lesson builds on it.
