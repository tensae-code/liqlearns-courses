# Hands-on — Jewellery design-and-make

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: Kazakh zerger jewellery art; developing a sketch of a jewellery product; making and decorating the product (§§12–14). Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
