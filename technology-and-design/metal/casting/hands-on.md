# Hands-on — Casting

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## Work the topic
Carry out the topic's core task on a fresh example: cast objects in daily life (brass oil lamp, statue, pot, door hinge) and cast iron as a casting metal. Work in order, naming each part or step as you go.

## Check it
Check your finished work against the lesson's own rules. Find one thing that is wrong or weak, fix it, and say what you changed and why.
