# Hands-on — A candle stand from sheet metal

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: the technology card — marking a 150×150 mm sheet, cutting the net, folding up the sides 30 mm and finishing; cutting sheet metal with tin snips held correctly. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
