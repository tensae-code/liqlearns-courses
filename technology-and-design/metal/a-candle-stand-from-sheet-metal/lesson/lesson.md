# A candle stand from sheet metal

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Mongolia's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — A candle stand from sheet metal
Mongolia's book teaches it this way (*Design Technology 7 (Mongolia)*, printed p. 106): the technology card — marking a 150×150 mm sheet, cutting the net, folding up the sides 30 mm and finishing; cutting sheet metal with tin snips held correctly. The learner works through the named materials and steps in order, saying what each step does before doing the next one.

## Sub lesson 2 — A candle stand from sheet metal — doing it yourself
Now the learner does the whole task unaided: the technology card — marking a 150×150 mm sheet, cutting the net, folding up the sides 30 mm and finishing; cutting sheet metal with tin snips held correctly. Working from the first sub lesson's sequence, the learner plans the make, works through it step by step, and checks the finished piece against what Mongolia's book shows.
