# Hands-on — Making products from metal

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## Work the topic
Carry out the topic's core task on a fresh example: metals through history (copper, bronze, iron from c. 4000 years ago), structural shapes, and the great metal structures (Eiffel Tower, steel bridge, electricity pylons). Work in order, naming each part or step as you go.

## Check it
Check your finished work against the lesson's own rules. Find one thing that is wrong or weak, fix it, and say what you changed and why.
