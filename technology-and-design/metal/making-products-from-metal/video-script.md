# Video script — Making products from metal

A short demonstration video, made later, in a super-simple voice. The demonstrator works through the topic while the voice speaks.

---

This lesson is about making products from metal.
Metals through history (copper, bronze, iron from c. 4000 years ago), structural shapes, and the great metal structures (Eiffel Tower, steel bridge, electricity pylons).
Work step by step. Check your work as you go.
