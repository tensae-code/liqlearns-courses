# Lesson plan — Carving, blacksmith and stone craft technology

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Metal · **Lesson:** Carving, blacksmith and stone craft technology
**Place in the curriculum:** Metal (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Mongolia expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Carving, blacksmith and stone craft technology — carving tools by material (wood, bone, stone, metal), the Batnorov and Dardanga silverwork traditions, and a stone keyring project worked with a rotary burr machine and polishing discs.
**This lesson's own level:** stocked from Mongolia's *Visual Art & Technology (Дүрслэх урлаг, технологи)* student books (Ministry of Education, Culture, Science and Sports of Mongolia) — see lesson/sources.md. It counts in the acceptance count (student book, not evidence-only). Mongolia's books are image-only scans read by a VISUAL-READ method, as filed in lesson/sources.md.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.
