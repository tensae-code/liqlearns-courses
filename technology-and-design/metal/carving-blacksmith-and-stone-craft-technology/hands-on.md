# Hands-on — Carving, blacksmith and stone craft technology

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: carving tools by material (wood, bone, stone, metal), the Batnorov and Dardanga silverwork traditions, and a stone keyring project worked with a rotary burr machine and polishing discs. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
