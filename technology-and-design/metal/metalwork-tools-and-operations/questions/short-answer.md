# Metalwork — tools and operations — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In your own words, what is centre punch (granete)?
**Answer:** A pointed tool that marks a centre so a drill starts true.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In your own words, what is riveted joint?
**Answer:** A joint made by rivets hammered to clamp the parts.
