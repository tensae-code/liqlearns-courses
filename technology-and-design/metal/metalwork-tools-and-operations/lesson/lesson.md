# Metalwork — tools and operations

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Cuba's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — The metalworker's tools
Cuba's Grade 8 course names the bench set: files (limas), tinsmith's shears, the centre punch (granete), drill bit and drill, the hacksaw (segueta), the ball-peen hammer and the bench vice. The 1989 book adds the cold chisel (cortafrío) and the mechanic's hammer.

## Sub lesson 2 — The operations, in order
Work runs: measure, trace (trazado), mark, check, cut, smooth (file), perforate, bend (doblado) — and join, with riveted joints among the course's joins. Finishing and protecting the metal closes the work, as the Grade 8 student book teaches it.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: metalwork is a named set of tools and operations: files, shears, centre punch, drill, hacksaw and hammer at the bench vice — measuring, tracing, marking, checking, cutting, smoothing, perforating and bending, with riveted joints to join. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
