# Joining with screws — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In your own words, what is thread?
**Answer:** The spiral ridge on a screw that grips the material as it turns.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In your own words, what is stripping?
**Answer:** Ruining a thread by overdriving, so the screw no longer grips.
