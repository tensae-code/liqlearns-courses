# Joining with screws — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is thread?
a) the spiral ridge on a screw that grips the material as it turns b) Only a price c) Only decoration d) Nothing to do with this lesson
**Answer: a) the spiral ridge on a screw that grips the material as it turns**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is screw head?
a) Only history b) the top of the screw, shaped to fit its driver c) Only storage d) A kind of glue
**Answer: b) the top of the screw, shaped to fit its driver**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is stripping?
a) Only old tools b) Only painting c) ruining a thread by overdriving, so the screw no longer grips d) Only a price
**Answer: c) ruining a thread by overdriving, so the screw no longer grips**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson teach, in one line?
a) Only decoration b) Nothing to do with this lesson c) Only history d) a screw joins by its thread: the spiral ridge grips the material as the head drives it home — and driving screws safely is a technique, not a force.
**Answer: d) a screw joins by its thread: the spiral ridge grips the material as the head drives it home — and driving screws safely is a technique, not a force.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** How does Iran's book teach this?
a) As theory only, never practised b) Only in a glossary c) Only as an exam d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Persian
**Answer: d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Persian**
