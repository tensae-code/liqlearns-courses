# Joining with screws — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Thread is the spiral ridge on a screw that grips the material as it turns.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Screw head has nothing to do with this lesson.
**Answer: False** — It is part of the lesson: the top of the screw, shaped to fit its driver.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Stripping is ruining a thread by overdriving, so the screw no longer grips.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.
