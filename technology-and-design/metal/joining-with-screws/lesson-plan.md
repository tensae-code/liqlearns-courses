# Lesson plan — Joining with screws

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Metal · **Lesson:** Joining with screws
**Place in the curriculum:** Metal (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Iran expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** a screw joins by its thread: the spiral ridge grips the material as the head drives it home — and driving screws safely is a technique, not a force.
**This lesson's own level:** stocked from Iran's *Work & Technology, Grade 8* (student book) — see lesson/sources.md. Iran counts in the acceptance count (student books, not evidence-only).

Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **Threads and heads** — A screw's thread is a spiral ridge wrapped round its shaft; turning the screw winds the thread into the material, pulling the parts together with great force from little effort.
2. **Driving screws safely** — Safe driving is technique: choose the screw for the materials, prepare the hole where the material demands it, keep the driver seated and straight, and stop at firm — overdriving strips the thread and ruins the joint.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
