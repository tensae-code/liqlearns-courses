# Hands-on — Repoussé

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: raising a design in thin sheet metal — annealing copper at 750°C, transferring the drawing, working from the back with punches, filling with wax/resin, and finishing at 250–300°C. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
