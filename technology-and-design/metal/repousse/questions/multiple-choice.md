# Repoussé — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson teach?
a) Repoussé — raising a design in thin sheet metal — annealing copper at 750°C, transferring the drawing, working from the back with punches, filling with wax/resin, and finishing at 250–300°C b) Only how to draw pictures c) Nothing practical d) A song to memorise
**Answer: a) Repoussé — raising a design in thin sheet metal — annealing copper at 750°C, transferring the drawing, working from the back with punches, filling with wax/resin, and finishing at 250–300°C**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Sub lesson 1 is about…

**Answer: a) Repoussé**
