# Repoussé — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Say in one sentence what this lesson teaches.
**Answer:** Repoussé — raising a design in thin sheet metal — annealing copper at 750°C, transferring the drawing, working from the back with punches, filling with wax/resin, and finishing at 250–300°C.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name one material, tool or step this lesson works with.
**Answer:** Any one named in the lesson text — for example, the first step of sub lesson 1: Repoussé.
