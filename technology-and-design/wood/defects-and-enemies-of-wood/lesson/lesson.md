# Defects and enemies of wood

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Greece's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Defects and enemies of wood
Greece's book teaches it this way: wood faults and the organisms/agents that attack wood (*Wood Tech & Measurements*, printed p. 32). The learner works through the named materials and steps in order, saying what each step does before doing the next one.

## Sub lesson 2 — Defects and enemies of wood — doing it yourself
Now the learner does the whole task unaided: wood faults and the organisms/agents that attack wood. Working from the first sub lesson's sequence, the learner plans the make, works through it step by step, and checks the finished piece against what Greece's book shows.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: Wood faults and the organisms/agents that attack wood. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
