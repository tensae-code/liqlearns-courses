# Wood joints — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What three assemblies does the lesson teach?
a) Nailing, screwing and gluing b) Welding, riveting, sewing c) Sawing, planing, sanding d) Drawing, measuring, marking
**Answer: a) Nailing, screwing and gluing**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is taught about nails besides their use?
a) Their colour b) Their sizes and heads c) Their price history d) Their maker
**Answer: b) Their sizes and heads**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the trade-off of long thick nails?
a) They hold stronger but can split the piece b) They are cheaper but rust c) They shine but bend d) They are light but short
**Answer: a) They hold stronger but can split the piece**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Where are glued pieces held while the joint sets?
a) In the vice b) In the hand c) On the floor d) In water
**Answer: a) In the vice**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the vice give the glue joint?
a) Heat b) Paint c) Pressure and alignment while it sets d) Holes
**Answer: c) Pressure and alignment while it sets**
