# Wood joints — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Long thick nails always make the best joint.
**Answer: False** — They hold stronger but can split the piece — the trade-off must be judged.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Nail sizes and heads are part of learning to nail.
**Answer: True** — The lesson teaches nailing with nail sizes and heads.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A glued joint needs no holding while it sets.
**Answer: False** — Pieces are glued and held in the vice while the joint sets.
