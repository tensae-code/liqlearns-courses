# Lesson plan — Wood joints

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Wood · **Lesson:** Wood joints
**Place in the curriculum:** Wood (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Moldova expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** pieces are assembled three simple ways — nailed, screwed, or glued in the vice — and the nail teaches the trade-off: long thick nails hold stronger, but they can split the piece.
**This lesson's own level:** stocked from Moldova's *Educație tehnologică* (Technological Education) textbooks — see lesson/sources.md. Moldova counts in the acceptance count (student books, not evidence-only).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **Nailing and screwing** — Grade 5 assembling (asamblare) begins with fasteners.
2. **Gluing in the vice** — The third assembly is gluing: pieces are glued and held in the vice while the joint sets.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of Wood. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Assemble pieces by nailing, screwing and gluing.
2. Choose a nail knowing the strength-versus-splitting trade-off.
3. Explain how Moldova's book teaches it.
## What you need
Practice pieces, nails of different sizes, screws and screwdriver, glue and a vice.
