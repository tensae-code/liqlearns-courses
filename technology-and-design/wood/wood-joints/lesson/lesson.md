# Wood joints

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Moldova's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Nailing and screwing
Grade 5 assembling (asamblare) begins with fasteners. Nailing is taught with nail sizes and heads, and with its honest trade-off stated plainly: long thick nails hold stronger, but they can split the piece. Screwing follows as the second fastening, stronger and removable where nailing is quick and fixed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Gluing in the vice
The third assembly is gluing: pieces are glued and held in the vice while the joint sets. The vice is part of the joint — pressure, alignment and patience turn glue into a bond.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: pieces are assembled three simple ways — nailed, screwed, or glued in the vice — and the nail teaches the trade-off: long thick nails hold stronger, but they can split the piece. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
