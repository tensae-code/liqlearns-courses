# Lesson plan — Artistic wood processing technologies

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Wood · **Lesson:** Artistic wood processing technologies
**Place in the curriculum:** Wood (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Moldova expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** artistic wood technologies have a history and a choice: know how they evolved, know the techniques — pyrography with a heated tool among them — then analyse and select the technique a designed article needs.
**This lesson's own level:** stocked from Moldova's *Educație tehnologică* (Technological Education) textbooks — see lesson/sources.md. Moldova counts in the acceptance count (student books, not evidence-only).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **How the technologies evolved** — Grade 9 opens the artistic-wood part historically: how wood-processing technologies evolved, from hand craft toward the present range of techniques.
2. **Analysing and selecting the technique** — The part's skill is selection: the artistic wood technologies — pyrography with a heated tool among them — are analysed, and the technique is selected for a designed article.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of Wood. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Outline how wood-processing technologies evolved.
2. Analyse a designed article and select its artistic technique.
3. Explain how Moldova's book teaches it.
## What you need
Examples or pictures of artistically processed wood articles.
