# Artistic wood processing technologies — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why does the part begin with evolution?
**Answer:** So the techniques are understood as answers found over time, not random tricks.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How is a technique chosen for an article?
**Answer:** By analysing the candidate techniques against the designed article's needs, and selecting the one that serves the design.
