# Artistic wood processing technologies — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How does the Grade 9 part open?
a) With how wood-processing technologies evolved b) With an exam c) With prices d) With a single technique only
**Answer: a) With how wood-processing technologies evolved**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Why teach the evolution?
a) For dates to memorise only b) So techniques are understood as answers found over time c) To fill the lesson d) It is not taught
**Answer: b) So techniques are understood as answers found over time**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Which technique is named among the artistic technologies?
a) Pyrography with a heated tool b) Welding c) Weaving d) Casting
**Answer: a) Pyrography with a heated tool**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** What is the part's central skill?
a) Analysing and selecting the technique for a designed article b) Copying one technique forever c) Avoiding techniques d) Selling articles
**Answer: a) Analysing and selecting the technique for a designed article**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** What decides the technique?
a) Habit b) The alphabet c) The designed article's needs d) The nearest tool
**Answer: c) The designed article's needs**
