# Artistic wood processing technologies

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Moldova's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — How the technologies evolved
Grade 9 opens the artistic-wood part historically: how wood-processing technologies evolved, from hand craft toward the present range of techniques. The history is taught so the techniques are understood as answers found over time, not as a random shelf of tricks.

## Sub lesson 2 — Analysing and selecting the technique
The part's skill is selection: the artistic wood technologies — pyrography with a heated tool among them — are analysed, and the technique is selected for a designed article. The article's design decides; the technique serves it.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: artistic wood technologies have a history and a choice: know how they evolved, know the techniques — pyrography with a heated tool among them — then analyse and select the technique a designed article needs. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
