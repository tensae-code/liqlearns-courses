**Acceptance count: taught in 2 countries (Cuba, Moldova).**

Cuba's own way: Cuba teaches finishing wood (acabado) in the Grade 7 programme — sanding technique, stains (tintes) and varnishes (barnices) — and in the 2023 book as acabado y protección: sanding, stains, varnishes, lacquers and paints applied with the brush.

Moldova's own way: Moldova teaches finishing in Grade 5 as a fixed order — rasp and file, abrasive paper along the grain, paint or varnish — with ventilation against dust and fumes taught as part of the finish, not an afterthought.
