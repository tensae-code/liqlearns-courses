# Finishing wood

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Moldova's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Smoothing and sanding
Finishing starts by smoothing: the rasp and the file take the surface down, then abrasive paper sands it — always along the grain, so the paper refines the surface instead of scratching across it. Each step leaves the surface ready for the next.

## Sub lesson 2 — Painting or varnishing, with air
Only then comes the coat: painting or varnishing the prepared surface. The lesson pairs the coat with its safety rule — ventilate the room against the dust of sanding and the fumes of the finish.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: a wooden article is finished in order — rasp and file, abrasive paper along the grain, then paint or varnish — with the room ventilated against dust and fumes. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
