# Finishing wood — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Sanding across the grain is the taught direction.
**Answer: False** — Abrasive paper is used along the grain.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Painting comes before sanding in the finishing order.
**Answer: False** — The order is rasp and file, sanding, then painting or varnishing.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Ventilation is part of the finishing lesson's safety.
**Answer: True** — The room is ventilated against dust and fumes.
