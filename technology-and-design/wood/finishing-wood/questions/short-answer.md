# Finishing wood — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Give the finishing order.
**Answer:** Smooth with rasp and file, sand with abrasive paper along the grain, then paint or varnish.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why must the room be ventilated?
**Answer:** Against the dust of sanding and the fumes of paint or varnish.
