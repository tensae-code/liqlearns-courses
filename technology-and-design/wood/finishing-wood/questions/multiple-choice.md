# Finishing wood — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What smooths the surface first?
a) The rasp and the file b) The paintbrush c) The varnish d) The vice
**Answer: a) The rasp and the file**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** How is abrasive paper used?
a) Across the grain b) Along the grain c) In circles only d) Wet only
**Answer: b) Along the grain**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the order of finishing?
a) Smooth, sand, then paint or varnish b) Paint, then sand c) Varnish first d) Any order works
**Answer: a) Smooth, sand, then paint or varnish**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** When is paint or varnish applied?
a) After smoothing and sanding b) Before sanding c) Instead of sanding d) Never
**Answer: a) After smoothing and sanding**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Why ventilate the room?
a) To dry the wood faster b) To cool the tools c) Against dust and fumes d) For light
**Answer: c) Against dust and fumes**
