# Lesson plan — Finishing wood

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Wood · **Lesson:** Finishing wood
**Place in the curriculum:** Wood (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Moldova expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** a wooden article is finished in order — rasp and file, abrasive paper along the grain, then paint or varnish — with the room ventilated against dust and fumes.
**This lesson's own level:** stocked from Moldova's *Educație tehnologică* (Technological Education) textbooks — see lesson/sources.md. Moldova counts in the acceptance count (student books, not evidence-only).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **Smoothing and sanding** — Finishing starts by smoothing: the rasp and the file take the surface down, then abrasive paper sands it — always along the grain, so the paper refines the surface instead of scratching across it.
2. **Painting or varnishing, with air** — Only then comes the coat: painting or varnishing the prepared surface.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of Wood. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Finish an article through the full sequence in order.
2. Work safely: sand along the grain and ventilate the room.
3. Explain how Moldova's book teaches it.
## What you need
A rasp, a file, abrasive paper, paint or varnish, and a made article to finish.
