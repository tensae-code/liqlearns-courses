# Hands-on — A wooden toy car

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: designing in Paint, copying the working drawing full-size (hole positions Ø, 15 and 20 mm dimensions), sawing the curved profile and drilling the axle holes. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
