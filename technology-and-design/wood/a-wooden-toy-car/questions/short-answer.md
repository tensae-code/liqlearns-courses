# A wooden toy car — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Say in one sentence what this lesson teaches.
**Answer:** A wooden toy car — designing in Paint, copying the working drawing full-size (hole positions Ø, 15 and 20 mm dimensions), sawing the curved profile and drilling the axle holes.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name one material, tool or step this lesson works with.
**Answer:** Any one named in the lesson text — for example, the first step of sub lesson 1: A wooden toy car.
