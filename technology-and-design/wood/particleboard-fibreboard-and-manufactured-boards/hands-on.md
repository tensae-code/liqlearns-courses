# Hands-on — Particleboard, fibreboard and manufactured boards

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## Read the edge
Examine any board samples edge-on. Sketch each edge's structure and name the board it proves. If no samples, work from the lesson's picture description.

## Choose the board
For three jobs (a boat's interior panel, a painted cupboard door, a drawer bottom), choose a board and defend it by bond, finish and fixing.
