# Particleboard, fibreboard and manufactured boards — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Particle board's inside is coarser than MDF's.
**Answer: True** — Chips against fibres — the edge shows it.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** An overlay can give plywood a finished face.
**Answer: True** — Overlays are one of plywood's graded options.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Manufactured boards can be wider than any plank from one tree.
**Answer: True** — Re-forming small wood into sheets is the point of manufacture.
