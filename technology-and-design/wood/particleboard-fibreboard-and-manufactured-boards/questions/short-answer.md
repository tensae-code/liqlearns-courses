# Particleboard, fibreboard and manufactured boards — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why does crossing the grain make plywood strong in every direction?
**Answer:** Answer: Each layer resists splitting along its own grain; crossed, every direction is some layer's strong direction, and the glue locks the layers together.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A shelf in MDF sags its screws out of the edge. What rule was broken?
**Answer:** Answer: The face-only fixing rule — MDF holds fixings in its face, not its weak edge.
