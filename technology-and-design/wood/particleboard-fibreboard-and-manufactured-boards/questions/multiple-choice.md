# Particleboard, fibreboard and manufactured boards — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Plywood stays flat and resists splitting because…
a) Its veneer layers are glued with grains crossing b) It is one thick veneer c) It has no glue d) It is never pressed
**Answer: a) Its veneer layers are glued with grains crossing**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Bond types A–D grade…
a) The glue's weather resistance b) The board's colour c) The tree species d) The sheet size
**Answer: a) The glue's weather resistance**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** MDF is made from wood broken down to…
a) Fibres, formed and pressed b) Large planks c) Whole veneers d) Bark strips
**Answer: a) Fibres, formed and pressed**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The face-only fixing rule for MDF says screws hold in…
a) The face, not the edge b) The edge only c) Neither face nor edge d) The corners only
**Answer: a) The face, not the edge**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Tempered hardboard is…
a) Hardboard treated to be harder and more moisture-resistant b) Untreated soft board c) A kind of plywood d) Particle board with overlay
**Answer: a) Hardboard treated to be harder and more moisture-resistant**
