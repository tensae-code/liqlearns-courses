# Lesson plan — Particleboard, fibreboard and manufactured boards

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Wood · **Lesson:** Particleboard, fibreboard and manufactured boards
**Place in the curriculum:** Wood (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Samoa expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** manufactured boards are engineered from wood pieces: plywood is veneers glued in crossed layers, MDF is fibres pressed to a smooth board fixed through the face, particle board is chips formed and pressed, and hardboard is a dense fibre board, tempered for hardness — each graded and bonded for its use.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on student book evidence — Samoa's MESC textbooks (see lesson/sources.md). It counts in the acceptance count (student book, not evidence-only).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **Plywood — crossed veneers** — Plywood is built up: thin veneers glued and pressed with each layer's grain crossing the last, in bond types A–D, sometimes with overlays.
2. **MDF and particle board — fibres and chips** — MDF is wood broken to fibres, formed and pressed into a smooth, even board; particle board is chips formed and pressed the same way, coarser inside.
3. **Hardboard — dense and tempered** — Hardboard is a thin, dense fibre board; tempered hardboard is treated to be harder and more moisture-resistant.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of Wood. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Explain plywood's crossed-layer build and bond grades.
2. Tell MDF from particle board and state the face-only fixing rule.
3. Say what tempered hardboard is for.
## What you need
Samples of any manufactured boards if available; otherwise pictures.
