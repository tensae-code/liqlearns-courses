# Properties of wood

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Moldova's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Physical and mechanical properties
Moldova's Grade 5 module teaches wood by its properties: the physical properties — what the material is like — and the mechanical properties — how it bears working and load. Properties are learned as facts about each species, not as decoration.

## Sub lesson 2 — Properties decide the use
The lesson's point is the match: what a species is used for follows from its properties. A wood is picked for a stool, a carving or a handle because its properties fit that job — and rejected, however familiar, when they do not.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: a wood's physical and mechanical properties decide what it is used for — the species is chosen by its properties, not by habit. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
