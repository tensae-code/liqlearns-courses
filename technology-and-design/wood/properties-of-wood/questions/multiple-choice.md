# Properties of wood — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What two families of properties does the module teach?
a) Physical and mechanical properties b) Cheap and dear properties c) Old and new properties d) Local and foreign properties
**Answer: a) Physical and mechanical properties**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What do mechanical properties describe?
a) Its colour b) How the wood bears working and load c) Its price d) Its smell
**Answer: b) How the wood bears working and load**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What decides what a species is used for?
a) Its properties b) Habit c) The alphabet d) The nearest shop
**Answer: a) Its properties**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A familiar wood whose properties do not fit the job should be…
a) Rejected for that job b) Used anyway c) Painted to fit d) Renamed
**Answer: a) Rejected for that job**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Choosing wood well means…
a) Always choosing the hardest b) Always choosing the cheapest c) Matching properties to the job d) Choosing at random
**Answer: c) Matching properties to the job**
