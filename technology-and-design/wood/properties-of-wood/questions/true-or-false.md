# Properties of wood — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Properties are facts about each species, learned before choosing.
**Answer: True** — The module teaches physical and mechanical properties species by species.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Any wood can serve any job equally well.
**Answer: False** — What a species is used for is decided by its properties.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Habit is a safe guide for choosing a species.
**Answer: False** — The species is chosen by its properties, not by habit.
