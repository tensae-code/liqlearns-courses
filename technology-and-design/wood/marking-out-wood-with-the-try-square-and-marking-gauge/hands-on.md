# Hands-on — Marking out wood with the try square and marking gauge

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: Marking out wood before sawing: reading the end grain, marking the width with a marking gauge (рейсмус) and squaring lines with the try square; the kinds of hand saw. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
