# Video script — Turning wood on the lathe

A short demonstration video, made later, in a super-simple voice.

Turning wood on the lathe.
Turning wood on the lathe — the blank planed to an octagon, centres marked and drilled, mounted; roughing with the half-round chisel (1–2 mm shavings), finishing with the skew chisel, diameter checked with calipers as you go; parting, sanding on the lathe, sawing off; three complete technological cards with drawings — a rolling pin in ten operations, a potato masher in thirteen, a file handle in nine turned to a template (§15).
Watch the steps. Then do them yourself.
