# Hands-on — Turning wood on the lathe

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: the blank planed to an octagon, centres marked and drilled, mounted; roughing with the half-round chisel (1–2 mm shavings), finishing with the skew chisel, diameter checked with calipers as you go; parting, sanding on the lathe, sawing off; three complete technological cards with drawings — a rolling pin in ten operations, a potato masher in thirteen, a file handle in nine turned to a template (§15). Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
