# Turning wood on the lathe — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Say in one sentence what this lesson teaches.
**Answer:** Turning wood on the lathe — the blank planed to an octagon, centres marked and drilled, mounted; roughing with the half-round chisel (1–2 mm shavings), finishing with the skew chisel, diameter checked with calipers as you go; parting, sanding on the lathe, sawing off; three complete technological cards with drawings — a rolling pin in ten operations, a potato masher in thirteen, a file handle in nine turned to a template (§15).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name one material, tool or step this lesson works with.
**Answer:** Any one named in the lesson text — for example, the first step of sub lesson 1: Turning wood on the lathe.
