# Turning wood on the lathe

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Kazakhstan's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Turning wood on the lathe
Kazakhstan's books teach it this way (the student book, printed p. 56): The blank planed to an octagon, centres marked and drilled, mounted; roughing with the half-round chisel (1–2 mm shavings), finishing with the skew chisel, diameter checked with calipers as you go; parting, sanding on the lathe, sawing off; three complete technological cards with drawings — a rolling pin in ten operations, a potato masher in thirteen, a file handle in nine turned to a template (§15). The learner works through the named materials and steps in order, saying what each step does before doing the next one.

## Sub lesson 2 — Turning wood on the lathe — doing it yourself
Now the learner does the whole task unaided: the blank planed to an octagon, centres marked and drilled, mounted; roughing with the half-round chisel (1–2 mm shavings), finishing with the skew chisel, diameter checked with calipers as you go; parting, sanding on the lathe, sawing off; three complete technological cards with drawings — a rolling pin in ten operations, a potato masher in thirteen, a file handle in nine turned to a template (§15). Working from the first sub lesson's sequence, the learner plans the make, works through it step by step, and checks the finished piece against what the Kazakh book shows.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: Turning wood on the lathe — the blank planed to an octagon, centres marked and drilled, mounted; roughing with the half-round chisel (1–2 mm shavings), finishing with the skew chisel, diameter checked with calipers as you go; parting, sanding on the lathe, sawing off; three complete technological cards with drawings — a rolling pin in ten operations, a potato masher in thirteen, a file handle in nine turned to a template (§15). Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.

## Do It Yourself
