# Protecting and preserving timber — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** CCA is a water-borne preservative.
**Answer: True** — It is carried into the wood in water.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Any treatment class suits any place the timber serves.
**Answer: False** — The class must match the hazard — interior dry to marine.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The chrome in CCA fixes the preservatives into the wood.
**Answer: True** — Fixing keeps the copper and arsenic in place to do their work.
