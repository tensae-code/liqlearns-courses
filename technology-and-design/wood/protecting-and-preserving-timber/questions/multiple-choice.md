# Protecting and preserving timber — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Preservation differs from paint because it…
a) Changes the wood deep inside, not just its surface b) Only changes the colour c) Makes the wood heavier by half d) Removes the grain
**Answer: a) Changes the wood deep inside, not just its surface**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Untreated outdoor timber fails mainly because…
a) Fungi rot it and insects eat it b) It gets rained on once c) It is too strong d) Sunlight polishes it
**Answer: a) Fungi rot it and insects eat it**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Pressure treatment works by…
a) Forcing preservative deep into the wood in a sealed vessel b) Brushing preservative on the surface c) Soaking timber in rain water d) Heating the wood dry
**Answer: a) Forcing preservative deep into the wood in a sealed vessel**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Brushing cannot match pressure treatment because…
a) It coats only the surface b) It is too fast c) It uses too much preservative d) It darkens the wood
**Answer: a) It coats only the surface**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** In CCA, the copper's job is to…
a) Fight fungi b) Fix the preservatives in the wood c) Fight insects d) Give the colour
**Answer: a) Fight fungi**
