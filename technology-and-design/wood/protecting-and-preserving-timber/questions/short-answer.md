# Protecting and preserving timber — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Why is the whole sapwood impregnated, not just the surface?
**Answer:** Answer: Damage and cuts expose inner wood; if only the surface were treated, the first cut or crack would open untreated food to rot and insects.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** A post will stand in the ground. What class of treatment do you demand and why?
**Answer:** Answer: A high-hazard class rated for ground contact — the ground line is the wettest, most attacked zone, and a lower class would rot out early.
