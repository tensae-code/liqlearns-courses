# Hands-on — Protecting and preserving timber

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## Read the stamp
Find a treated-timber stamp or label (on a post, a pack, or in a picture). Record its class and write which service it suits: interior, exterior above ground, ground contact, or marine.

## Match class to place
List five timber jobs around a home. For each, state the hazard (dry interior to ground contact) and the treatment class it needs, with a one-line reason.
