# Fretwork

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Moldova's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — The hand fretsaw and its materials
Fretwork — traforaj — cuts ornaments out of thin material. The hand tool is the fretsaw: a U-frame holding a fine blade, made to turn inside cut-out shapes. Its materials are thin by definition: plywood first, and also plexiglas, soft metals and bone.

## Sub lesson 2 — Electric saws and the fretwork table
The same cutting is done by machine with electric jigsaw and scroll saws. The work sits at the fretwork table, with its own tool set — the workplace is part of the technique, holding the thin sheet so the fine blade can travel.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: fretwork (traforaj) cuts ornaments in thin materials — plywood, plexiglas, soft metals, bone — with the hand fretsaw's U-frame and fine blade, or with electric jigsaw and scroll saws, at the fretwork table.
