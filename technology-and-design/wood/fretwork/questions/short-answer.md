# Fretwork — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the fretwork materials.
**Answer:** Plywood, and other thin materials: plexiglas, soft metals and bone.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Describe the hand fretsaw.
**Answer:** A U-frame holding a fine blade, made for cutting ornaments and turning inside cut-out shapes.
