# Fretwork — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is traforaj?
a) Fretwork — cutting ornaments in thin materials b) Turning on a lathe c) Burning designs d) Gluing veneer
**Answer: a) Fretwork — cutting ornaments in thin materials**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What shape is the hand fretsaw's frame?
a) A T-frame b) A U-frame c) A box frame d) No frame
**Answer: b) A U-frame**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** What blade does it hold?
a) A fine blade b) A coarse ripping blade c) A circular blade d) A chain
**Answer: a) A fine blade**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Which materials are cut?
a) Plywood, plexiglas, soft metals and bone b) Thick beams only c) Stone and concrete d) Cloth only
**Answer: a) Plywood, plexiglas, soft metals and bone**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Which electric saws do the same work?
a) Chainsaws b) Circular saws only c) Jigsaw and scroll saws d) Band saws only
**Answer: c) Jigsaw and scroll saws**
