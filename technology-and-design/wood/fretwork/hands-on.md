# Hands-on — Fretwork

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## Frame study
Examine a fretsaw (or a clear drawing of one). Label the U-frame and the fine blade, and write why the frame is deep and the blade fine.

## Ornament plan
Draw a small ornament for plywood fretwork. Mark the inside cuts the U-frame must reach, and say whether you would cut it by hand or by scroll saw, and why.
