# Artistic wood carving

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Moldova's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — The woods for carving
Grade 6 carving (cioplirea) begins with the material: the suitable soft hardwoods — lime, poplar, birch, alder and maple — and, among the hard woods, beech and walnut. The wood must take the cut cleanly, because the ornament lives or dies by its edges.

## Sub lesson 2 — Fixing, preparing, transferring — then carving
The procedure is ordered: fix the piece so it cannot move, prepare the surface, and transfer the ornament onto it with pencil, ruler, set square, compasses and templates. Only then does carving begin, taught through geometric carving procedures — ornament built from measured geometry.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: carving (cioplirea) decorates wood: choose a suitable wood, fix the piece, prepare the surface, transfer the ornament with drawing tools and templates — then carve, geometrically first. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.

## Do It Yourself
1. Do the Do it first task again, carefully this time, using everything the lesson taught.
2. Check your work against the lesson's own checks — fix one thing you find.
3. Keep your work: it is practice evidence, and the next lesson builds on it.
