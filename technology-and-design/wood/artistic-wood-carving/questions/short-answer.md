# Artistic wood carving — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the suitable soft hardwoods for carving.
**Answer:** Lime, poplar, birch, alder and maple (with beech and walnut among the hard woods).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Give the order of preparation before carving.
**Answer:** Fix the piece, prepare the surface, transfer the ornament with drawing tools and templates — then carve.
