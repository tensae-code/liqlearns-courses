# Artistic wood carving — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Any wood is equally suitable for carving.
**Answer: False** — The lesson names suitable woods — soft hardwoods, and beech and walnut among the hard.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The ornament is transferred before carving begins.
**Answer: True** — Fixing, surface preparation and transfer all precede the carving.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Geometric carving builds ornament from measured geometry.
**Answer: True** — The taught procedures are geometric carving procedures.
