# Artistic wood carving — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is cioplirea?
a) Carving — decoration cut into wood b) Burning c) Weaving d) Turning
**Answer: a) Carving — decoration cut into wood**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which soft hardwoods does the lesson name?
a) Oak and pine only b) Lime, poplar, birch, alder and maple c) Teak and mahogany d) None are named
**Answer: b) Lime, poplar, birch, alder and maple**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Which hard woods are named?
a) Beech and walnut b) Ash and elm c) Cedar and fir d) None
**Answer: a) Beech and walnut**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** What is done before carving begins?
a) Fix the piece, prepare the surface, transfer the ornament b) Paint the piece c) Sell the piece d) Sand the ornament away
**Answer: a) Fix the piece, prepare the surface, transfer the ornament**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Which tools transfer the ornament?
a) Hammer and nails b) Saw and plane c) Pencil, ruler, set square, compasses and templates d) Brush and varnish
**Answer: c) Pencil, ruler, set square, compasses and templates**
