# Combination and CNC woodworking machines — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Say in one sentence what this lesson teaches.
**Answer:** Common combination machines, surface formers (ραμποτέζες), the angular combination machine, squaring/forming and edge-forming machines, and CNC work centres (κέντρα εργασίας).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name one material, tool or step this lesson works with.
**Answer:** Any one named in the lesson text — for example, the first step of sub lesson 1: Combination and CNC woodworking machines.
