# Hands-on — Bone and horn carving

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: Mongolian chess pieces carved from bone — the processing sequence (boiling, sawing with the fret saw, filing, gluing) and safety with sharp tools. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
