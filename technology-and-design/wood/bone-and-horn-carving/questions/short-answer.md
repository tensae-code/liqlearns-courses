# Bone and horn carving — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Say in one sentence what this lesson teaches.
**Answer:** Bone and horn carving — Mongolian chess pieces carved from bone — the processing sequence (boiling, sawing with the fret saw, filing, gluing) and safety with sharp tools.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name one material, tool or step this lesson works with.
**Answer:** Any one named in the lesson text — for example, the first step of sub lesson 1: Bone and horn carving.
