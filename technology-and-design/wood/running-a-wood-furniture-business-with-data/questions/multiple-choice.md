# Running a wood/furniture business with data — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson teach?
a) Keeping statistics, building indicators, and computerisation (μηχανοργάνωση) of wood and furniture enterprises b) Only how to draw pictures c) Nothing practical d) A song to memorise
**Answer: a) Keeping statistics, building indicators, and computerisation (μηχανοργάνωση) of wood and furniture enterprises**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Sub lesson 1 is about…
a) Running a wood/furniture business with data b) Cooking lunch c) A sports match d) The weather
**Answer: a) Running a wood/furniture business with data**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Sub lesson 2 asks the learner to…
a) Complete the making in full and check it b) Stop before starting c) Copy a partner's work d) Throw the materials away
**Answer: a) Complete the making in full and check it**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Greece teaches this topic inside which school book?
a) Wood Tech & Measurements b) Only mathematics c) Only history d) It is not taught at school
**Answer: a) Wood Tech & Measurements**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** What should the learner do first in this lesson?
a) Try the Do it first task in their own way b) Skip to the last page c) Wait to be given the answer d) Nothing at all
**Answer: a) Try the Do it first task in their own way**
