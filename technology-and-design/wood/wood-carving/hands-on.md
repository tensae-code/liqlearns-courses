# Hands-on — Wood carving

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: the kinds of relief (flat, half-relief, full-round, pierced), holding the knife correctly in each hand, reading grain direction in a 10×10 blank, and the carver's tools. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
