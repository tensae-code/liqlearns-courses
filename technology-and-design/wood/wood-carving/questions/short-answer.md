# Wood carving — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Say in one sentence what this lesson teaches.
**Answer:** Wood carving — the kinds of relief (flat, half-relief, full-round, pierced), holding the knife correctly in each hand, reading grain direction in a 10×10 blank, and the carver's tools.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name one material, tool or step this lesson works with.
**Answer:** Any one named in the lesson text — for example, the first step of sub lesson 1: Wood carving.
