# Marquetry/inlay work — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is marquetry (tarso)?
a) making a design by fitting cut pieces of wood together in a surface b) Only a price c) Only decoration d) Nothing to do with this lesson
**Answer: a) making a design by fitting cut pieces of wood together in a surface**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is inlay?
a) Only history b) setting one material into the surface of another, flush c) Only storage d) A kind of glue
**Answer: b) setting one material into the surface of another, flush**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is grain?
a) Only old tools b) Only painting c) the pattern of fibres in wood, used in tarso as colour and texture d) Only a price
**Answer: c) the pattern of fibres in wood, used in tarso as colour and texture**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson teach, in one line?
a) Only decoration b) Nothing to do with this lesson c) Only history d) marquetry (tarso) makes pictures and patterns in wood itself: pieces of wood are cut to a design and set together into the surface, so the decoration is the material, not a coating on it.
**Answer: d) marquetry (tarso) makes pictures and patterns in wood itself: pieces of wood are cut to a design and set together into the surface, so the decoration is the material, not a coating on it.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** How does Cuba's book teach this?
a) As theory only, never practised b) Only in a glossary c) Only as an exam d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Spanish
**Answer: d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Spanish**
