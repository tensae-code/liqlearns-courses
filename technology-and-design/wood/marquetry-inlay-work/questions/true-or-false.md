# Marquetry/inlay work — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Marquetry (tarso) is making a design by fitting cut pieces of wood together in a surface.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Inlay has nothing to do with this lesson.
**Answer: False** — It is part of the lesson: setting one material into the surface of another, flush.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Grain is the pattern of fibres in wood, used in tarso as colour and texture.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.
