# Marquetry/inlay work — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In your own words, what is marquetry (tarso)?
**Answer:** Making a design by fitting cut pieces of wood together in a surface.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In your own words, what is grain?
**Answer:** The pattern of fibres in wood, used in tarso as colour and texture.
