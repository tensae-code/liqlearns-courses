# Hands-on — Marquetry/inlay work

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## Design in wood tones
Draw a simple tarso design (a leaf, a fish, a star). Shade each part with the wood tone you would cut it from, and number the pieces in cutting order.

## Fit on paper
Cut your design from two tones of paper and fit the pieces together. Note where the fit failed and what a wood cutter would do differently.
