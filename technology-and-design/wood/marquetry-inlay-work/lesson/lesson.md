# Marquetry/inlay work

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Cuba's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — The tarso technique
Cuba's Grade 5 guidance teaches marquetry as the tarso technique (§2.4 of the wood unit): a design is realised by cutting wood pieces to shape and fitting them together into one surface.

## Sub lesson 2 — Realising a tarso article
The realisation runs: design first, pieces cut to the design, fitted edge to edge, the whole set and finished as one article. The grain and tone of each piece are part of the palette — the maker chooses wood the way a painter chooses colour.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: marquetry (tarso) makes pictures and patterns in wood itself: pieces of wood are cut to a design and set together into the surface, so the decoration is the material, not a coating on it. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
