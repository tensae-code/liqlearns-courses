# Sharpening and adjusting woodworking tools — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does sharpening restore?
a) The cutting edge b) The handle c) The paint d) The tool's box
**Answer: a) The cutting edge**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What does a blunt tool do?
a) Cuts better b) Tears, slips and tires the worker c) Works silently d) Sharpens itself
**Answer: b) Tears, slips and tires the worker**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What besides sharpening must be done?
a) Adjusting — setting the tool up b) Painting c) Oiling the bench d) Nothing
**Answer: a) Adjusting — setting the tool up**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** What is the aim of sharpening and adjusting together?
a) That the tool cuts correctly b) That the tool looks new c) That the tool is heavier d) That work is avoided
**Answer: a) That the tool cuts correctly**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** When are edge and adjustment checked?
a) After the work is ruined b) Never c) Before the real workpiece is touched d) Only at the year's end
**Answer: c) Before the real workpiece is touched**
