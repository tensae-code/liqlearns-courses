# Sharpening and adjusting woodworking tools — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why are tools sharpened?
**Answer:** To restore the cutting edge — a sharp tool cuts where it is guided; a blunt one tears and slips.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why is sharpening not enough on its own?
**Answer:** The tool must also be adjusted — set up — so that it cuts correctly.
