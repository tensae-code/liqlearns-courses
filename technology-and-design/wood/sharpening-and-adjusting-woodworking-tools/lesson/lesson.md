# Sharpening and adjusting woodworking tools

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Moldova's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Sharpening — restoring the edge
Grade 6 teaches tool care as a skill of its own: sharpening restores the cutting edge that work has dulled. A sharp tool cuts where it is guided; a blunt one tears, slips and tires the worker.

## Sub lesson 2 — Adjusting — setting up to cut correctly
Sharpening alone is not enough: the tools of wood processing are also adjusted — set up — so they cut correctly. Edge and adjustment together decide the cut, and both are checked before the real workpiece is touched.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: woodworking tools must be sharpened and adjusted — set up — so they cut correctly; a tool out of adjustment does bad work however skilled the hand. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
