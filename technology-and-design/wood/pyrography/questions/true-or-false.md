# Pyrography — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Pyrography decorates by burning the design into the surface.
**Answer: True** — That is the technique the lesson teaches.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Leather and ivory cannot be used for pyrography.
**Answer: False** — The lesson names plywood, leather and ivory beside the suitable woods.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Stamp dies draw freehand lines.
**Answer: False** — Dies press a ready design; freehand burning is done with the heated tool.
