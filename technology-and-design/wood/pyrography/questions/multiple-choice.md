# Pyrography — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is pyrography?
a) Decorating by burning b) Decorating by painting c) Decorating by carving only d) Decorating by gluing
**Answer: a) Decorating by burning**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which woods does the lesson name as suitable?
a) Oak, pine and teak only b) Lime, poplar, beech and maple c) Any wood at all d) No wood — metal only
**Answer: b) Lime, poplar, beech and maple**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Which other materials take the burn?
a) Plywood, leather and ivory b) Glass and steel c) Paper and cloth d) Clay and stone
**Answer: a) Plywood, leather and ivory**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** What tools do the burning?
a) The pyrograph and soldering tools b) The rasp and file c) The brace and bit d) The plane
**Answer: a) The pyrograph and soldering tools**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** What do stamp dies do?
a) Cut the wood b) Sand the surface c) Press a ready design into the surface d) Heat the room
**Answer: c) Press a ready design into the surface**
