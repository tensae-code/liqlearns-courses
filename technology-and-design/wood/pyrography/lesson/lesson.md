# Pyrography

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Moldova's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Decorating by burning — the materials
Pyrography is decoration by burning: the design is burned into the surface. Moldova's Grade 5 lesson names the suitable woods — lime, poplar, beech, maple — and the other materials that take the burn: plywood, leather and ivory. The material must suit the heat before any design is drawn.

## Sub lesson 2 — The tools — pyrograph and stamp dies
The burning is done with the pyrograph and soldering tools, whose heated points draw the lines. Beside freehand burning stand the stamp dies, which press a ready design into the surface — two ways to the same decorated article.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: pyrography decorates by burning: the heated tool draws on suitable woods — lime, poplar, beech, maple — and on plywood, leather and ivory, freehand or with stamp dies. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
