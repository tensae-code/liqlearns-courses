# Woodwork — tools and operations

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Moldova's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Sawing and drilling
Grade 5 teaches the first operations together. Sawing: the kinds of joinery saws and their teeth — foxtail, bow saw and the rest — and the two cutting techniques, cross-cutting across the grain and rip-cutting along it. Drilling: through holes and blind holes, bored with the hand brace (coarbă) and with the electric drill.

## Sub lesson 2 — Planing
Grade 6 adds planing (rindeluirea): shaving thin layers off a workpiece's surface until it is true and smooth. The tools are hand planes — with wooden or metal bodies — and electric planes, and the operation is judged by the surface it leaves.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: the woodworker's core operations are sawing, drilling and planing — each with its own tools, teeth and technique: cross-cut and rip-cut, through and blind holes, thin shavings off the surface. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
