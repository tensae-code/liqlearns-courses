Moldova's own way: Moldova builds the wood operations in grade order — sawing and drilling in Grade 5 (joinery saws and their teeth, the coarbă hand brace beside the electric drill), planing (rindeluirea) in Grade 6 — each operation named in Romanian and taught with its tools.
