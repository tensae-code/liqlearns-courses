# Woodwork — tools and operations — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How do cross-cutting and rip-cutting differ?
**Answer:** Cross-cutting cuts across the grain; rip-cutting cuts along it.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is planing?
**Answer:** Shaving thin layers off a workpiece's surface — with hand planes or electric planes — until it is true and smooth.
