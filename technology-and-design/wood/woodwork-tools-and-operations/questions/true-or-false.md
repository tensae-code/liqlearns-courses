# Woodwork — tools and operations — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Cross-cutting goes across the grain and rip-cutting along it.
**Answer: True** — The two techniques are named by their direction against the grain.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A blind hole passes right through the piece.
**Answer: False** — A blind hole stops inside the piece; a through hole passes through.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Hand planes exist with wooden and with metal bodies.
**Answer: True** — The Grade 6 lesson names both, beside electric planes.
