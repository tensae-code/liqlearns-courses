# Woodwork — tools and operations — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which saws does the Grade 5 lesson name?
a) Joinery saws such as the foxtail and the bow saw b) Only the chainsaw c) Only the fretsaw d) No saws are named
**Answer: a) Joinery saws such as the foxtail and the bow saw**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What are the two sawing techniques?
a) Pushing and pulling b) Cross-cutting and rip-cutting c) Wet and dry cutting d) Fast and slow cutting
**Answer: b) Cross-cutting and rip-cutting**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Which holes are drilled?
a) Through holes and blind holes b) Only square holes c) Only decorative holes d) No holes — drilling is theoretical
**Answer: a) Through holes and blind holes**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Which drilling tools are taught?
a) The hand brace (coarbă) and the electric drill b) Only the hammer c) Only the chisel d) Only the plane
**Answer: a) The hand brace (coarbă) and the electric drill**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** What does planing do?
a) Cuts the piece in two b) Bores holes c) Shaves thin layers off the workpiece's surface d) Glues pieces
**Answer: c) Shaves thin layers off the workpiece's surface**
