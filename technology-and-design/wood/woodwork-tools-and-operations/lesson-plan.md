# Lesson plan — Woodwork — tools and operations

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Wood · **Lesson:** Woodwork — tools and operations
**Place in the curriculum:** Wood (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Moldova expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the woodworker's core operations are sawing, drilling and planing — each with its own tools, teeth and technique: cross-cut and rip-cut, through and blind holes, thin shavings off the surface.
**This lesson's own level:** stocked from Moldova's *Educație tehnologică* (Technological Education) textbooks — see lesson/sources.md. Moldova counts in the acceptance count (student books, not evidence-only).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **Sawing and drilling** — Grade 5 teaches the first operations together.
2. **Planing** — Grade 6 adds planing (rindeluirea): shaving thin layers off a workpiece's surface until it is true and smooth.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of Wood. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Saw across and along the grain with the right saw, and drill through and blind holes.
2. Plane a surface with hand and electric planes.
3. Explain how Moldova's book teaches it.
## What you need
Joinery saws, a hand brace and electric drill, hand planes, and practice wood.
