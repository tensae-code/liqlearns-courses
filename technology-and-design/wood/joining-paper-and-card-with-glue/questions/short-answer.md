# Joining paper and card with glue — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In your own words, what is adhesive (pegamento)?
**Answer:** The glue used to join paper and card.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In your own words, what is work station cleanliness?
**Answer:** Keeping the gluing place clean so finished work is not marked.
