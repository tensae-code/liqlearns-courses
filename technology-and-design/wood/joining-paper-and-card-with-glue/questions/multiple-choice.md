# Joining paper and card with glue — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is adhesive (pegamento)?
a) the glue used to join paper and card b) Only a price c) Only decoration d) Nothing to do with this lesson
**Answer: a) the glue used to join paper and card**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is application?
a) Only history b) spreading the adhesive evenly where the join belongs c) Only storage d) A kind of glue
**Answer: b) spreading the adhesive evenly where the join belongs**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is work station cleanliness?
a) Only old tools b) Only painting c) keeping the gluing place clean so finished work is not marked d) Only a price
**Answer: c) keeping the gluing place clean so finished work is not marked**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson teach, in one line?
a) Only decoration b) Nothing to do with this lesson c) Only history d) paper and card are joined with glue by a technique of its own: the right adhesive for paper, applied in the right amount, pressed and held — with care and cleanliness of the work station as part of the operation.
**Answer: d) paper and card are joined with glue by a technique of its own: the right adhesive for paper, applied in the right amount, pressed and held — with care and cleanliness of the work station as part of the operation.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** How does Cuba's book teach this?
a) As theory only, never practised b) Only in a glossary c) Only as an exam d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Spanish
**Answer: d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Spanish**
