# Lesson plan — Joining paper and card with glue

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Wood · **Lesson:** Joining paper and card with glue
**Place in the curriculum:** Wood (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Cuba expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** paper and card are joined with glue by a technique of its own: the right adhesive for paper, applied in the right amount, pressed and held — with care and cleanliness of the work station as part of the operation.
**This lesson's own level:** stocked from Cuba's Educación Laboral programmes and methodological orientations (teacher programme documents) — see lesson/sources.md. Cuba is evidence only for this lesson, counted nowhere in the acceptance count.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **Adhesives for paper and card** — Cuba's Grade 2 programme names pegamentos — the adhesives for paper and card — as content to learn: which glue suits paper work, and how it behaves on thin and thick stock.
2. **The gluing technique** — Application is the skill: a thin, even layer, placed where the join belongs, pressed and held until it takes — and the work station kept clean throughout, because stray glue ruins paper work faster than any other fault. Care and cleanliness are written into the programme's own line.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
