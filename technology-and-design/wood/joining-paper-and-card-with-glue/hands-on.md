# Hands-on — Joining paper and card with glue

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## Glue three joins cleanly
Join three paper/card pieces: a lap, an edge and a tab. Aim for no visible glue on any face. Inspect and say which join is cleanest and why.

## Amount matters
On scrap, glue one piece with too little, one with too much, one with the right amount. Compare the three joins and state the rule you found.
