# Joining paper and card with glue

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Cuba's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Adhesives for paper and card
Cuba's Grade 2 programme names pegamentos — the adhesives for paper and card — as content to learn: which glue suits paper work, and how it behaves on thin and thick stock.

## Sub lesson 2 — The gluing technique
Application is the skill: a thin, even layer, placed where the join belongs, pressed and held until it takes — and the work station kept clean throughout, because stray glue ruins paper work faster than any other fault. Care and cleanliness are written into the programme's own line.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: paper and card are joined with glue by a technique of its own: the right adhesive for paper, applied in the right amount, pressed and held — with care and cleanliness of the work station as part of the operation. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
