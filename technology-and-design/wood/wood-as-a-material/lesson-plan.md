# Lesson plan — Wood as a material

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Wood · **Lesson:** Wood as a material
**Place in the curriculum:** Wood (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Moldova expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** wood materials begin as trees: logs are sawn into timber at the frame saw, veneer is glued into plywood, and every method of processing a material is what the book calls technology.
**This lesson's own level:** stocked from Moldova's *Educație tehnologică* (Technological Education) textbooks — see lesson/sources.md. Moldova counts in the acceptance count (student books, not evidence-only).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **From tree to timber** — Moldova's Grade 5 wood module starts where wood starts: tree species, and logs sawn into timber — boards, laths, beams — at the frame saw (gater).
2. **What technology means** — The module fixes its key word at the start: all the methods and procedures for processing a material are called technology.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of Wood. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Trace wood materials from tree species to timber, plywood and chipboard.
2. State the book's definition of technology.
3. Explain how Moldova's book teaches it.
## What you need
Samples or clear pictures of boards, laths, beams, plywood and chipboard.
