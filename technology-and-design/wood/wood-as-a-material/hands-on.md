# Hands-on — Wood as a material

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## Material line
Draw the line for one board: tree, log, frame saw, timber. Draw a second line for plywood: tree, veneer sheets, gluing, sheet.

## Name the semi-finished
From samples or pictures, identify a board, a lath, a beam, plywood and chipboard. Say for each whether it is solid timber or a made sheet.
