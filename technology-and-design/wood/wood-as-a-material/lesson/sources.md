**Acceptance count: taught in 2 countries (Iran, Moldova).**

Moldova's own way: Moldova opens its Grade 5 wood module at the sawmill — species, the frame saw (gater), plywood and chipboard as semi-finished products — and fixes the subject's key definition there: technology is all the methods and procedures for processing a material.

Iran's own way: Iran teaches wood by a property table — fibre, colour, hardness, weight and uses, walnut among the woods named — and marks the shift from solid wood to manufactured board, MDF included (Grade 7, Module 9).
