# Wood as a material

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Moldova's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — From tree to timber
Moldova's Grade 5 wood module starts where wood starts: tree species, and logs sawn into timber — boards, laths, beams — at the frame saw (gater). Beside solid timber stand the sheet materials: plywood, made by gluing veneer sheets, and chipboard. These are the semi-finished products the woodworker actually buys and uses.

## Sub lesson 2 — What technology means
The module fixes its key word at the start: all the methods and procedures for processing a material are called technology. Every later lesson — sawing, joining, finishing — is one of those methods, named and learned in order.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: wood materials begin as trees: logs are sawn into timber at the frame saw, veneer is glued into plywood, and every method of processing a material is what the book calls technology. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
