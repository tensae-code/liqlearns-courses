# Wood as a material — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Where are logs sawn into timber?
a) At the frame saw (gater) b) At the workbench c) In the forest only d) At the kiln
**Answer: a) At the frame saw (gater)**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which of these is solid timber from the saw?
a) Plywood b) Boards, laths and beams c) Chipboard d) Veneer glue
**Answer: b) Boards, laths and beams**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** How is plywood made?
a) By gluing veneer sheets b) By sawing one thick log c) By pressing sawdust only d) By carving
**Answer: a) By gluing veneer sheets**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the book call technology?
a) All the methods and procedures for processing a material b) Only machines c) Only hand tools d) Only drawing
**Answer: a) All the methods and procedures for processing a material**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** What are boards and plywood to the woodworker?
a) Finished furniture b) Waste c) Semi-finished products to work from d) Tools
**Answer: c) Semi-finished products to work from**
