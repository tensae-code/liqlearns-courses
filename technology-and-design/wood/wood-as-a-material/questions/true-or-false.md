# Wood as a material — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Plywood is a single solid piece of wood.
**Answer: False** — Plywood is made by gluing veneer sheets together.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Boards, laths and beams are sawn from logs at the frame saw.
**Answer: True** — The module traces timber to the gater (frame saw).

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Technology, in the book's definition, means only power machines.
**Answer: False** — It means all methods and procedures for processing a material.
