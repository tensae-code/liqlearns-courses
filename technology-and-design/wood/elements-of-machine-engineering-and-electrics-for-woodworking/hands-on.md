# Hands-on — Elements of machine engineering and electrics for woodworking machines

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: kinds of machines, naming of machine parts, materials machines are made of, basic ways of transmitting motion, safety rules for electric current and for woodworking machines. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
