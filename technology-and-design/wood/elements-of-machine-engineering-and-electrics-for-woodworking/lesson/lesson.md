# Elements of machine engineering and electrics for woodworking machines

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Greece's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Elements of machine engineering and electrics for woodworking machines
Greece's book teaches it this way: kinds of machines, naming of machine parts, materials machines are made of, basic ways of transmitting motion, safety rules for electric current and for woodworking machines (*Wood Tech Machines*, printed p. 20). The learner works through the named materials and steps in order, saying what each step does before doing the next one.

## Sub lesson 2 — Elements of machine engineering and electrics for woodworking machines — doing it yourself
Now the learner does the whole task unaided: kinds of machines, naming of machine parts, materials machines are made of, basic ways of transmitting motion, safety rules for electric current and for woodworking machines. Working from the first sub lesson's sequence, the learner plans the make, works through it step by step, and checks the finished piece against what Greece's book shows.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: Kinds of machines, naming of machine parts, materials machines are made of, basic ways of transmitting motion, safety rules for electric current and for woodworking machines. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.

## Do It Yourself
1. Do the Do it first task again, carefully this time, using everything the lesson taught.

3. Keep your work: it is practice evidence, and the next lesson builds on it.
