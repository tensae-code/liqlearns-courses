# Glues for wood — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is adhesive (cola)?
a) a substance that joins surfaces by sticking to both b) Only a price c) Only decoration d) Nothing to do with this lesson
**Answer: a) a substance that joins surfaces by sticking to both**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is hot carpenter's glue?
a) Only history b) glue applied warm, the traditional carpenter's adhesive c) Only storage d) A kind of glue
**Answer: b) glue applied warm, the traditional carpenter's adhesive**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is clamping?
a) Only old tools b) Only painting c) holding glued parts firmly in position while the glue sets d) Only a price
**Answer: c) holding glued parts firmly in position while the glue sets**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson teach, in one line?
a) Only decoration b) Nothing to do with this lesson c) Only history d) wood is glued with adhesives of different kinds, each with its use: know the types, choose by the joint and the conditions, prepare the surfaces, and apply and clamp until the glue holds.
**Answer: d) wood is glued with adhesives of different kinds, each with its use: know the types, choose by the joint and the conditions, prepare the surfaces, and apply and clamp until the glue holds.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** How does Cuba's book teach this?
a) As theory only, never practised b) Only in a glossary c) Only as an exam d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Spanish
**Answer: d) Through the book's own examples, steps and project work, in Spanish**
