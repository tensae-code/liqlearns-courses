# Glues for wood — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Adhesive (cola) is a substance that joins surfaces by sticking to both.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Hot carpenter's glue has nothing to do with this lesson.
**Answer: False** — It is part of the lesson: glue applied warm, the traditional carpenter's adhesive.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Clamping is holding glued parts firmly in position while the glue sets.
**Answer: True** — That is how the book teaches it.
