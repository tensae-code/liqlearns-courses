# Lesson plan — Glues for wood

**Course:** Technology and Design · **Title:** Wood · **Lesson:** Glues for wood
**Place in the curriculum:** Wood (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked in the Cuba expansion, 2026-11-17.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** wood is glued with adhesives of different kinds, each with its use: know the types, choose by the joint and the conditions, prepare the surfaces, and apply and clamp until the glue holds.
**This lesson's own level:** stocked from Cuba's *Educación Laboral y Dibujo Básico — 7mo grado (2023 Biblioteca edition)* and the Cuba books in lesson/sources.md — see lesson/sources.md. Cuba counts in the acceptance count for its student-book placements; its programme and methodological-orientations placements are evidence only, counted nowhere.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Picture and GIF numbers are assigned in a later media pass; the slots below are described in words.

## Sub lessons
1. **Types of adhesives** — Cuba's 2023 Grade 7 book gives glues their own section (§3.5): the types of adhesives used for wood and what each is for. The Grade 7 programme adds the workshop's pair — hot carpenter's glue (cola caliente) and the cold glues — with their different handling.
2. **Using glue well** — A glued joint is made by preparation: surfaces clean and fitting, glue applied evenly, parts clamped in position until set. The glue is chosen for the joint's work — a glue that suits a quiet joint fails a stressed or wet one.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
