# Glues for wood

## Do it first
Before reading on: take the materials this lesson names and try the task in your own way first. Then read how Cuba's book teaches it, and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — Types of adhesives
Cuba's 2023 Grade 7 book gives glues their own section (§3.5): the types of adhesives used for wood and what each is for. The Grade 7 programme adds the workshop's pair — hot carpenter's glue (cola caliente) and the cold glues — with their different handling.

## Sub lesson 2 — Using glue well
A glued joint is made by preparation: surfaces clean and fitting, glue applied evenly, parts clamped in position until set. The glue is chosen for the joint's work — a glue that suits a quiet joint fails a stressed or wet one.

## Say it to your companion
Explain in your own words: wood is glued with adhesives of different kinds, each with its use: know the types, choose by the joint and the conditions, prepare the surfaces, and apply and clamp until the glue holds. Say it once plainly, then answer one question your companion asks about it.
