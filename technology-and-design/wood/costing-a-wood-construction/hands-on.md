# Hands-on — Costing a wood construction

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: labour costs, machine-employment costs, production costing methods and process, overheads and VAT, a worked furniture costing example. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
