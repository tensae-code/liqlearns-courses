# Hands-on — Acids, bases, salts, pH and redox in cosmetic materials

Real practice — this subject is learned by doing.

## First make
Do the lesson's central task with real materials, following the sequence in lesson/lesson.md: electrolytes and neutralisation, the pH of skin and why pH matters in aesthetics/hairdressing materials, oxidation and reduction. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Check and compare
Compare your finished work with the lesson's own checks. Fix one thing you find, and tell your partner what you changed and why.
