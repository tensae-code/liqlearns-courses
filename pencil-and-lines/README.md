# Pencil and Lines — the prerequisite course

**A normal course of its own** (his ruling, 2026-10-25), and the
prerequisite that stands **before every language course** — English,
and every other language on the platform. The reason, in his words'
sense: they have to learn the words, and words are written — so the
hand learns first: how to hold the pencil, and how to draw and
understand lines. Only then letters, in any language.

It is a recommended "start here instead," never a wall (the
prerequisite rule): a learner can jump straight into a language
course, but this course sits in front of it as the true bottom.

## Inside
- **Title: The hand and the line**
  - Lesson: Holding a pencil — the five-step grip
  - Lesson: Drawing lines — the wavy line (curves) and the zigzag
    line (straights and corners), understood, not just traced:
    every letter is made of these two moves

Built from the first pages of the finished countries' KG/Grade 1
books, like every course (Egypt first — its KG1 p.1 teaches both
lessons). As other countries' first books are read, their versions
merge in here, wherever the same material is found — the basics get
put where they belong as they are found.
