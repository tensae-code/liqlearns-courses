# Hands-on — Drawing patterns

## Say it, draw it

In pairs: one partner describes a pattern without showing it
— "three waves, two lines, one circle, again" — the other
draws from the words alone. Then compare with the real
pattern. Change places.

## Draw my answer

One partner reads a two-sentence story aloud. The other draws
the answer — its main thing — in one minute. The reader says
whether the drawing caught it. Change places.
