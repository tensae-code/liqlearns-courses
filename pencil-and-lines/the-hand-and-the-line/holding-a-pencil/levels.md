# Holding a pencil — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB
LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure — README, "Units, Lessons and Sub
Lessons"). Practice is tied to the skill number, as before. This
lesson has no questions/ files; its practice is the hand itself,
and this file is the by-sub-lesson view. The sub lessons are the
depths inside this one thing — the book's five-step grip — cut at
the steps themselves: taking the pencil, resting it, writing
with it.

Mapping from the old depth layers: old Level 1 (hold the
five-step grip without being shown again) covered all five
steps, so it maps across Sub lessons 1–3, split at the step
jumps below; old Level 2 (explain the grip while doing it) also
covered the whole grip, and its pass mark is carried whole in
Sub lesson 3's section.

## Sub lesson 1 — Picking up the pencil
Pass mark (its part of old Level 1's, split at the steps): the
learner's hand comes down open, and thumb and first finger take
the pencil like a bird's beak, without being shown again.

## Sub lesson 2 — Resting the pencil
Pass mark (its part of old Level 1's, split at the steps): the
pencil rests on the middle finger underneath and tilts back
into the soft place, resting not squeezed, without being shown
again.

## Sub lesson 3 — Writing with the grip
Pass mark: the learner holds the pencil the five-step way
without being shown again (old Level 1, whole), and can explain
the grip to their companion — thumb and first finger on top,
pencil resting on the middle finger, no squeezing — while doing
it (old Level 2). (Tracing wavy and zigzag lines is the next
lesson, Drawing lines — it is not tested here.)
