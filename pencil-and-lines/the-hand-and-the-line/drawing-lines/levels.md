# Drawing lines — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB
LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure — README, "Units, Lessons and Sub
Lessons"). Practice is tied to the skill number, as before. This
lesson has no questions/ files; its practice is the hand itself,
and this file is the by-sub-lesson view. The sub lessons are the
depths inside this one thing — the two roads every letter is
made of — the wavy road first, then the zigzag road.

Mapping from the old depth layers: old Level 1 (both roads drawn
slow and controlled) maps across Sub lessons 1–2, split at the
two roads below; old Level 2 (say which roads a letter is made
of) covers both roads together, and its pass mark is carried
whole in Sub lesson 2's section.

## Sub lesson 1 — Drawing the wavy line
Pass mark (its half of old Level 1's, split at the two roads):
a smooth wavy line, drawn slow and controlled, starting from
the left.

## Sub lesson 2 — Drawing the zigzag line
Pass mark: a clean-cornered zigzag line, drawn slow and
controlled, starting from the left (the other half of old
Level 1); and the learner can look at any capital letter and
say which roads it is made of — curves, straights and corners,
or both mixed (old Level 2, whole).
