# My family — who is in it and why each person matters — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** State this lesson's one thing in your own words.
**Expected:** Pupils first talk in pairs about what they already know of family, then write the importance of family members, discuss what they wrote with each other and share it.

**2.** Explain the lesson's first part: What a family is.
**Expected:** an answer drawn from the lesson text for that part, in the learner's own words.

Difficulty: 2 = restating the teaching accurately without copying it.
