# My family — who is in it and why each person matters — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) Pupils first talk in pairs about what they already know of family, then write the importance of family members, discuss what they wrote with each other and share it.  b) A topic from a different level of this course  c) The syllabus of the teacher guide  d) none of these
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) What a family is  b) a topic from another course  c) today's weather  d) none of these
**Answer: a)** — That is this lesson's first part.

Difficulty: 1 = recall of the lesson's one thing; 2 = telling this lesson's parts from other lessons' parts.
