# Video script — Self-control — what it is and how a self-controlled child acts

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The lesson is text-first: this script says nothing the lesson text does not already teach.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Self-control is presented as the right moral behaviour a child develops and keeps. The teacher notes describe such children in concrete acts: respecting their parents, keeping themselves clean, helping their parents and others grow in ability, eating properly, and playing with friends safely and honestly.

Self-control is presented as the right moral behaviour a child develops and keeps. The teacher notes describe such children in concrete acts: respecting their parents, keeping themselves clean, helping their parents and others grow in ability, eating properly, and playing with friends safely and honestly.

The fuller portrait in the notes: they try hard to learn and understand the rules of good conduct and practise them daily, they have the courage to ask when they face a moral problem, they do not wait to be told before doing a good thing, and they are peaceful and hardworking in the neighbourhood, at school and in the family. Pupils discuss the concept in groups with examples, and the key behaviours in pairs.

Remember it this way: Self-control is presented as the right moral behaviour a child develops and keeps. Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
