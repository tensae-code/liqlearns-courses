# Video script — Growing self-control — why it matters and how to build it

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The lesson is text-first: this script says nothing the lesson text does not already teach.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The chapter teaches the importance of self-control and the ways of improving it, carried in the student book by examples and stories (the guide's testimony); in class, pupils discuss in short bursts how a child's self-control can be improved, and why it matters, in between the other chapter work.
