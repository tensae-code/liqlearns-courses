# Hands-on — The good neighbour — traits, and help in good times and hard

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
You and a companion show the traits in role-play, with stories and pictures used to mark what a good neighbour looks like.

## Show it with real things
Carry the lesson's teaching into one real moment today — at home, at school or among your neighbours — and do the act the lesson teaches, naming each step aloud as you do it: what you are doing, why, and what the lesson says it builds. Compare what happened against the lesson's one thing: The traits of good neighbours are, the note says, given exactly in the book, and the importance of a good neighbour is explained fully for both good times and hard times.

## Check it for one day
For one day, watch for this lesson's teaching in real life around you — in your family, your classroom, your street. Each time you see it (or see it missing), note what you saw and what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
