# Hands-on — When self-control is missing — what follows

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
The chapter closes by having you identify the results of not controlling oneself, and act them out: the teaching strategy is role-play, with you showing the negative outcomes of a lack of self-control in a short drama.

## Show it with real things
Carry the lesson's teaching into one real moment today — at home, at school or among your neighbours — and do the act the lesson teaches, naming each step aloud as you do it: what you are doing, why, and what the lesson says it builds. ## Check it for one day
For one day, watch for this lesson's teaching in real life around you — in your family, your classroom, your street. Each time you see it (or see it missing), note what you saw and what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
