# Hands-on — Living well with neighbours — building it and not spoiling it

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Various ways of bringing positive relations between neighbours are listed in the chapter, together with guidance for removing the conditions that damage good neighbourliness; you discuss in groups what can harm it.

## Show it with real things
Carry the lesson's teaching into one real moment today — at home, at school or among your neighbours — and do the act the lesson teaches, naming each step aloud as you do it: what you are doing, why, and what the lesson says it builds. Compare what happened against the lesson's one thing: Various ways of bringing positive relations between neighbours are listed in the chapter, together with guidance for removing the conditions that damage good neighbourliness; pupils discuss in groups what can harm it.

## Check it for one day
For one day, watch for this lesson's teaching in real life around you — in your family, your classroom, your street. Each time you see it (or see it missing), note what you saw and what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
