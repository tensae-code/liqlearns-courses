# Hands-on — Human rights — what they are

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
The definition session: rights are what every human being is owed simply by being human; you meet the idea first as a concept before any particular right is named.

## Show it with real things
Carry the lesson's teaching into one real moment today — at home, at school or among your neighbours — and do the act the lesson teaches, naming each step aloud as you do it: what you are doing, why, and what the lesson says it builds. Compare what happened against the lesson's one thing: The definition session: rights are what every human being is owed simply by being human; pupils meet the idea first as a concept before any particular right is named.

## Check it for one day
For one day, watch for this lesson's teaching in real life around you — in your family, your classroom, your street. Each time you see it (or see it missing), note what you saw and what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
