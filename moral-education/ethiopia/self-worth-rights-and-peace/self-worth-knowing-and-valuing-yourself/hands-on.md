# Hands-on — Self-worth — knowing and valuing yourself

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Take this lesson's teaching into one real situation today — at home, at school or in your neighbourhood: do what the lesson teaches, then say in one sentence what you did and what it changed.

## Show it with real things
Carry the lesson's teaching into one real moment today — at home, at school or among your neighbours — and do the act the lesson teaches, naming each step aloud as you do it: what you are doing, why, and what the lesson says it builds. Compare what happened against the lesson's one thing: The course opens by defining the value a person gives themselves: pupils say what they think and know about themselves, and the teacher builds the idea that knowing and valuing yourself is the ground the whole subject stands on.

## Check it for one day
For one day, watch for this lesson's teaching in real life around you — in your family, your classroom, your street. Each time you see it (or see it missing), note what you saw and what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
