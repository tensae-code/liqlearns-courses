# Responsibility — owning what is yours to do — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** This statement is the lesson's one thing: Responsibility and accountability defined: to be responsible is to own what is yours to do and to answer for how you did it. Pupils meet the concept before any duty is listed.
**Answer: True** — it states the lesson's teaching in full.

**Answer: False** — earlier levels are recommended first, never a forced gate; every lesson stands alone.
