# Hands-on — Responsibility — owning what is yours to do

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
You and a companion meet the concept before any duty is listed.

## Show it with real things
Carry the lesson's teaching into one real moment today — at home, at school or among your neighbours — and do the act the lesson teaches, naming each step aloud as you do it: what you are doing, why, and what the lesson says it builds. Compare what happened against the lesson's one thing: Responsibility and accountability defined: to be responsible is to own what is yours to do and to answer for how you did it. Pupils meet the concept before any duty is listed.

## Check it for one day
For one day, watch for this lesson's teaching in real life around you — in your family, your classroom, your street. Each time you see it (or see it missing), note what you saw and what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
