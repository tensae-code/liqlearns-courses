## Our moral responsibilities

Keeping promises, helping and care are the morals part; paying tax and respecting the rules are the civics part. (The lesson header carries the chapter's 19-period total — see Verification block.)

> Tip — from us, not from the books: Act it, don't just say it. A value you have practised once in a real moment — at home, at school, in your street — is yours in a way a value you have only read about is not.
