# Video script — Our moral responsibilities — promises, tax, rules, care

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The lesson is text-first: this script says nothing the lesson text does not already teach.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The chapter's duty list, taught as moral responsibilities: keep promises, help others, use resources well, take an active part in the community, pay tax, respect rules including road safety, and care for the environment — practised with a traffic-light card activity.
