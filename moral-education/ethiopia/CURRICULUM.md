# Moral Education — Ethiopia course — CURRICULUM (the path)

One course for Ethiopia inside the Moral Education folder,
organised by **levels, not grades**: a learner climbs when
they have proved the level, not when a school calendar says
so. The school grade each book comes from appears only in
each lesson's sources (provenance), never as the course's
organisation.

**How the levels derive:** the course stands on six books —
Ethiopia's Moral Education **teacher guides** for Grades 1–6
(2022–2023 GEQIP-E family, with the Grade 4 guide first
published 2015 E.C.) — and its ladder is the guides' own
progression across the six grades, in the guides' own order:
character built in widening circles, from the child's own
self-control outward through family, neighbours, school,
community and culture (Grade 1), then deepened grade by
grade — self-respect, honesty and justice; human dignity,
respect and equality; self-worth, rights and peace;
responsibility, work and moral decisions; and finally
integrity, law-abidingness, patriotism, peace and
cooperation (Grade 6). Twelve levels, two from each grade's
six chapters, in the books' own order. Both of the subject's
strands climb the ladder together, exactly as the guides
teach them: the **manners/morals** strand, and the
**constitution/civics** strand (dignity and rights, equality,
human rights, the law, patriotism) that the guides weave
into Moral Education — each lesson's sources carry its
strand tag. The ladder is a recommended path, never a
forced gate: every lesson stands alone for a learner who
lands on it directly.

**The first page (what learners are told):** everything in
this course comes from government teacher guides —
Ethiopia's own Ministry of Education Moral Education teacher
guides, Grades 1–6. No student book for this subject is in
hand for any grade, and the course says so plainly: by the
owner's ruling of 2026-11-24, Ethiopian teacher guides can
be used to create courses, because the guide explains the
student textbook and so carries the teaching. The one
exception to "the lessons are the books'" is the **Tip
boxes**: a tip in a box is from us, the people making
LiqLearns. Tips are ours; the lessons are the guides'. See
ABOUT.md for the full book records and the acceptance
reckoning.

## The levels (the path, in order)

1. **Level 1 — Myself, My Family and My Neighbours** — 11 lessons. *From the guides: Grade 1, Chapters 1-3: self-control, manners in the family, neighbourliness.*
2. **Level 2 — School, Community Values and Local Culture** — 14 lessons. *From the guides: Grade 1, Chapters 4-6: manners at school, the community's values, the local culture.*
3. **Level 3 — Self-Respect, Honesty and Justice** — 14 lessons. *From the guides: Grade 2, Chapters 1-3: self-respect and staying safe, truth and honesty, justice.*
4. **Level 4 — Friends, the Common Good and Our Heritage** — 16 lessons. *From the guides: Grade 2, Chapters 4-6: positive social interaction, standing for the common good, cultural heritage.*
5. **Level 5 — Human Dignity, Respect and Equality** — 16 lessons. *From the guides: Grade 3, Chapters 1-3: respecting humanity, respecting others, equality — the first level where the guides' constitution/civics strand stands in strength.*
6. **Level 6 — Healthy Social Life, Teamwork and Many Cultures** — 12 lessons. *From the guides: Grade 3, Chapters 4-6: healthy social life, cooperation and group work, multiculturalism.*
7. **Level 7 — Self-Worth, Human Rights and Peace** — 13 lessons. *From the guides: Grade 4, Units 1-3: self-worth, human rights (the grade's full constitution/civics unit), peace.*
8. **Level 8 — Right Conduct, Tolerance and Living With Diversity** — 8 lessons. *From the guides: Grade 4, Units 4-6: ethics in social activities, tolerance, accommodating cultural diversity.*
9. **Level 9 — Responsibility, Work and Deciding Well** — 14 lessons. *From the guides: Grade 5, Chapters 1-3: responsibility, strong work culture, moral decision-making.*
10. **Level 10 — Caring for the Environment, Life Online and Cultures Meeting** — 13 lessons. *From the guides: Grade 5, Chapters 4-6: environmental care, social media use ethics, cultural linkage.*
11. **Level 11 — Integrity, the Law and Good Behaviour** — 12 lessons. *From the guides: Grade 6, Units 1-3: moral integrity, law-abidingness (the grade's full constitution/civics unit), good behaviour.*
12. **Level 12 — Taking Part, Patriotism, Peace and Cooperation** — 12 lessons. *From the guides: Grade 6, Units 4-6: participating in socio-economic activities, patriotism, peace and cooperation.*

**The whole course stands at 155 lessons.**
