The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
The chapter's outcomes require you to explain the concept of self-respect, value its importance, identify the ways of showing it, and explain how self-respect is built.

## Show it with real things
Carry the lesson's teaching into one real moment today — at home, at school or among your neighbours — and do the act the lesson teaches, naming each step aloud as you do it: what you are doing, why, and what the lesson says it builds. Compare what happened against the lesson's one thing: The chapter's outcomes require pupils to explain the concept of self-respect, value its importance, identify the ways of showing it, and explain how self-respect is built.

## Check it for one day
For one day, watch for this lesson's teaching in real life around you — in your family, your classroom, your street. Each time you see it (or see it missing), note what you saw and what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
