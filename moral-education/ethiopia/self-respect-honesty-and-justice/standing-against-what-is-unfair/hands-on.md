# Hands-on — Standing against what is unfair

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Mixed placement: the manners part is the fair person's character, which you must show in your own behaviour; the civics part is the chapter outcome that you explain the need to struggle against what is unjust in the school and in society.

## Show it with real things
Carry the lesson's teaching into one real moment today — at home, at school or among your neighbours — and do the act the lesson teaches, naming each step aloud as you do it: what you are doing, why, and what the lesson says it builds. Compare what happened against the lesson's one thing: Mixed placement: the manners part is the fair person's character, which pupils must show in their own behaviour; the civics part is the chapter outcome that pupils explain the need to struggle against what is unjust in the school and in society.

## Check it for one day
For one day, watch for this lesson's teaching in real life around you — in your family, your classroom, your street. Each time you see it (or see it missing), note what you saw and what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
