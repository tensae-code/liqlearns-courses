# Honesty — no lying, no cheating, no stealing — depth

This lesson's parts are distinct and sequential, so they stand as its sub lessons (2026-11-09 ruling: sub lessons only where a lesson genuinely has distinct sequential parts). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: Honesty is defined as not even thinking of lying, cheating or stealing — the opposite of lying, which is knowingly saying what is false. The reading goes on in the guide's own adult register: stealing is taking what is not the thief's; cheating breaks the law and is kin to lying; lies differ in weight — from the "white lie" to lies that wreck social dealings — and the reading warns that even a lie told for a good end remains a lie, works through why people lie (to escape difficulty, or to gain from the lie), and notes that many pupils cheat or copy. — shown in the learner's own words and in the lesson's closing act done in a real situation.
