# Honesty — no lying, no cheating, no stealing — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** This statement is the lesson's one thing: Honesty is defined as not even thinking of lying, cheating or stealing — the opposite of lying, which is knowingly saying what is false. The reading goes on in the guide's own adult register: stealing is taking what is not the thief's; cheating breaks the law and is kin to lying; lies differ in weight — from the "white lie" to lies that wreck social dealings — and the reading warns that even a lie told for a good end remains a lie, works through why people lie (to escape difficulty, or to gain from the lie), and notes that many pupils cheat or copy.
**Answer: True** — it states the lesson's teaching in full.

**2.** A learner who lands on this lesson directly must finish every earlier level before starting it.
**Answer: False** — earlier levels are recommended first, never a forced gate; every lesson stands alone.

Difficulty: 1 = direct recall; 2 = applying the course's direct-entry rule.
