# Honesty — no lying, no cheating, no stealing — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** State this lesson's one thing in your own words.
**Expected:** Honesty is defined as not even thinking of lying, cheating or stealing — the opposite of lying, which is knowingly saying what is false. The reading goes on in the guide's own adult register: stealing is taking what is not the thief's; cheating breaks the law and is kin to lying; lies differ in weight — from the "white lie" to lies that wreck social dealings — and the reading warns that even a lie told for a good end remains a lie, works through why people lie (to escape difficulty, or to gain from the lie), and notes that many pupils cheat or copy.

**2.** Explain the lesson's first part: What honesty is.
**Expected:** an answer drawn from the lesson text for that part, in the learner's own words.

Difficulty: 2 = restating the teaching accurately without copying it.
