# Video script — Honesty — no lying, no cheating, no stealing

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The lesson is text-first: this script says nothing the lesson text does not already teach.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Honesty is defined as not even thinking of lying, cheating or stealing — the opposite of lying, which is knowingly saying what is false. The reading goes on in the guide's own adult register: stealing is taking what is not the thief's; cheating breaks the law and is kin to lying; lies differ in weight — from the "white lie" to lies that wreck social dealings — and the reading warns that even a lie told for a good end remains a lie, works through why people lie (to escape difficulty, or to gain from the lie), and notes that many pupils cheat or copy.
