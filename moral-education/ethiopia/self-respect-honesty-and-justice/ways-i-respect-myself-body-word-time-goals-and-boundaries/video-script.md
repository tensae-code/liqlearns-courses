# Video script — Ways I respect myself — body, word, time, goals and boundaries

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The lesson is text-first: this script says nothing the lesson text does not already teach.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The guide prints a numbered nineteen-point reading on respecting yourself, summarised here in substance: respect your own beliefs and values and do not change them to please anyone; respect your body — care for it, live healthily, make exercise a way of life; respect your surroundings — clean and beautify them; respect your wishes — share your real feelings openly; respect your word — be firm in honesty; respect your personal boundaries — know them and be able to say no; respect your fears — face them rather than fleeing; respect your failures — treat them as feedback and learn from them; respect your time — use it effectively; respect the advice of your elders; respect your weaknesses — take them as chances to grow; respect your goals and dreams — list them in order and start from the first; respect your feelings — do not stay in work or relationships that harm you; respect your opportunities — say yes to new experiences; put your own needs first before serving others' wishes; cut off people who wish you ill and use you; respect your actions — act with a good attitude; respect your responsibilities and accountability; and respect your thoughts — let them range, then train them toward the positive.
