# Ways I respect myself — body, word, time, goals and boundaries — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** State this lesson's one thing in your own words.
**Expected:** The guide prints a numbered nineteen-point reading on respecting yourself, summarised here in substance: respect your own beliefs and values and do not change them to please anyone; respect your body — care for it, live healthily, make exercise a way of life; respect your surroundings — clean and beautify them; respect your wishes — share your real feelings openly; respect your word — be firm in honesty; respect your personal boundaries — know them and be able to say no; respect your fears — face them rather than fleeing; respect your failures — treat them as feedback and learn from them; respect your time — use it effectively; respect the advice of your elders; respect your weaknesses — take them as chances to grow; respect your goals and dreams — list them in order and start from the first; respect your feelings — do not stay in work or relationships that harm you; respect your opportunities — say yes to new experiences; put your own needs first before serving others' wishes; cut off people who wish you ill and use you; respect your actions — act with a good attitude; respect your responsibilities and accountability; and respect your thoughts — let them range, then train them toward the positive.

**2.** Explain the lesson's first part: Nineteen ways to respect yourself.
**Expected:** an answer drawn from the lesson text for that part, in the learner's own words.

Difficulty: 2 = restating the teaching accurately without copying it.
