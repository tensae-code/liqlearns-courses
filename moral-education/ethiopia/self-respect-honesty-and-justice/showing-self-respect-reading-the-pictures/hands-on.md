# Hands-on — Showing self-respect — reading the pictures

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
The chapter's worked activities are picture-led: you look at pictures and connect the ways of showing self-respect to what the pictures show, discuss a "safe child" activity, and answer closing questions (set as weekend homework) whose keys the guide prints section by section.

## Show it with real things
Carry the lesson's teaching into one real moment today — at home, at school or among your neighbours — and do the act the lesson teaches, naming each step aloud as you do it: what you are doing, why, and what the lesson says it builds. Compare what happened against the lesson's one thing: The chapter's worked activities are picture-led: pupils look at pictures and connect the ways of showing self-respect to what the pictures show, discuss a "safe child" activity, and answer closing questions (set as weekend homework) whose keys the guide prints section by section.

## Check it for one day
For one day, watch for this lesson's teaching in real life around you — in your family, your classroom, your street. Each time you see it (or see it missing), note what you saw and what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
