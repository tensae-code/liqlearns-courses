# Video script — Fairness where we live — rules, respect, nobody shut out

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The lesson is text-first: this script says nothing the lesson text does not already teach.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Fairness is accepting everyone equally and excluding no one; fair people follow the rules in sports, games, activities and their community, and are honest and trusted.

Fairness is accepting everyone equally and excluding no one; fair people follow the rules in sports, games, activities and their community, and are honest and trusted.

The reading uses Eleanor Roosevelt's line in its own form — it is not fair to ask of others what you would not do yourself — and pictures fairness in a community as people keeping the law, respecting neighbours, guarding their animals and their rubbish, helping one another; without it, the chapter summary says, no one agrees, people quarrel, no one works together and no one wants to live there.

Remember it this way: Fairness is accepting everyone equally and excluding no one; fair people follow the rules in sports, games, activities and their community, and are honest and trusted.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
