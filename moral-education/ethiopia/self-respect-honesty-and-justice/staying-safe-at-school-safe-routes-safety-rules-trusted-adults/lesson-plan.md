# Lesson plan — Staying safe at school — safe routes, safety rules, trusted adults

**Course:** Moral Education — Ethiopia · **Level folder:** Level 3 — Self-Respect, Honesty and Justice · **Lesson:** Staying safe at school — safe routes, safety rules, trusted adults
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — Self-Respect, Honesty and Justice (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Under the heading "staying safe" (1.2 in the body, 1.5 in the chapter-head contents) the guide prints a six-point reading, addressed to parents and school staff, summarised in substance: learn the school's emergency procedures and keep emergency plans and phone numbers where they are posted; know the routes children travel to and from school, including alternative routes if a road is blocked; know and follow the school's security rules — signing in when visiting, being accompanied inside buildings, carrying a visitor pass — and set children an example by following them; talk with your child about safety, say what to do if he or she feels unsafe (find a teacher, call 995 — the police number as printed in the reading — reach a trusted neighbour, and know how to reach you); tell school staff about a child's health conditions, food allergies, disability or bullying so staff can protect the child; and talk with school leaders about what you can contribute to safety, such as parents watching before and after school in coordination with neighbours.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 3 — Self-Respect, Honesty and Justice (recommended, never forced).
**Strand:** manners/morals — the strand the Grade 2 teacher guide itself gives this teaching (manners/morals, or constitution/civics where the guide teaches rights, law or the state inside Moral Education).
