# Hands-on — When justice is missing — what breaks

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
The chapter's contents close with the damages of the absence of justice, and you list them as an outcome; in the chapter's own summary logic, a society without fairness rules falls into disagreement, quarrelling, refusal to cooperate and places nobody wants to live in.

## Show it with real things
Carry the lesson's teaching into one real moment today — at home, at school or among your neighbours — and do the act the lesson teaches, naming each step aloud as you do it: what you are doing, why, and what the lesson says it builds. Compare what happened against the lesson's one thing: The chapter's contents close with the damages of the absence of justice, and pupils list them as an outcome; in the chapter's own summary logic, a society without fairness rules falls into disagreement, quarrelling, refusal to cooperate and places nobody wants to live in.

## Check it for one day
For one day, watch for this lesson's teaching in real life around you — in your family, your classroom, your street. Each time you see it (or see it missing), note what you saw and what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
