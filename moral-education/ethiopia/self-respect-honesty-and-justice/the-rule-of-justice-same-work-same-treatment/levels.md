# The rule of justice — same work, same treatment — depth

This lesson's parts are distinct and sequential, so they stand as its sub lessons (2026-11-09 ruling: sub lessons only where a lesson genuinely has distinct sequential parts). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: The main principle, credited to Aristotle more than two thousand years ago: equals are treated equally and unequals unequally — in today's form, people are treated equally unless they differ in a way that is relevant to the treatment. — shown in the learner's own words and in the lesson's closing act done in a real situation.
