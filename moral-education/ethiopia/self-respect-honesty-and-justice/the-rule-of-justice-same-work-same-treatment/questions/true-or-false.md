# The rule of justice — same work, same treatment — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** This statement is the lesson's one thing: The main principle, credited to Aristotle more than two thousand years ago: equals are treated equally and unequals unequally — in today's form, people are treated equally unless they differ in a way that is relevant to the treatment.
**Answer: True** — it states the lesson's teaching in full.

**2.** A learner who lands on this lesson directly must finish every earlier level before starting it.
**Answer: False** — earlier levels are recommended first, never a forced gate; every lesson stands alone.

Difficulty: 1 = direct recall; 2 = applying the course's direct-entry rule.
