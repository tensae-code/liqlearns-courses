# Video script — The rule of justice — same work, same treatment

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The lesson is text-first: this script says nothing the lesson text does not already teach.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The main principle, credited to Aristotle more than two thousand years ago: equals are treated equally and unequals unequally — in today's form, people are treated equally unless they differ in a way that is relevant to the treatment.

The main principle, credited to Aristotle more than two thousand years ago: equals are treated equally and unequals unequally — in today's form, people are treated equally unless they differ in a way that is relevant to the treatment.

The worked example: Jack and Jill who do the same work equally must be paid equally under justice; paying Jack more because he is white, or because of sex, is an unjust difference, since colour and sex are not relevant to a healthy workplace. (Names and example are the book's own, in English in the original reading.)

Remember it this way: The main principle, credited to Aristotle more than two thousand years ago: equals are treated equally and unequals unequally — in today's form, people are treated equally unless they differ in a way that is relevant to the treatment.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
