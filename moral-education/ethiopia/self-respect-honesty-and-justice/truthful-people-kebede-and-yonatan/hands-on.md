# Hands-on — Truthful people — Kebede and Yonatan

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
In the other, you follow the story "Yonatan tells the truth" in the student book, first write your own view, then discuss in groups the good traits of a truthful and honest person (the story itself is not printed in the guide — plot not recoverable from the guide alone).

## Show it with real things
Carry the lesson's teaching into one real moment today — at home, at school or among your neighbours — and do the act the lesson teaches, naming each step aloud as you do it: what you are doing, why, and what the lesson says it builds. Compare what happened against the lesson's one thing: Two story anchors are named in the activity answers. In one, the teacher Kebede is a truthful man, and pupils are to learn the trait from him.

## Check it for one day
For one day, watch for this lesson's teaching in real life around you — in your family, your classroom, your street. Each time you see it (or see it missing), note what you saw and what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
