# Video script — Truthful people — Kebede and Yonatan

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The lesson is text-first: this script says nothing the lesson text does not already teach.

---

This lesson is about one thing.

In the other, pupils follow the story "Yonatan tells the truth" in the student book, first write their own view, then discuss in groups the good traits of a truthful and honest person (the story itself is not printed in the guide — plot not recoverable from the guide alone). A third activity works on the harms of cheating and stealing from parents, friends and neighbours.
