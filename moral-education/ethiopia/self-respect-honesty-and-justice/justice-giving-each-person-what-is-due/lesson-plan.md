# Lesson plan — Justice — giving each person what is due

**Course:** Moral Education — Ethiopia · **Level folder:** Level 3 — Self-Respect, Honesty and Justice · **Lesson:** Justice — giving each person what is due
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — Self-Respect, Honesty and Justice (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Justice is defined as giving every person what is due to him or her. The reading places the idea in a long line — from Plato's Republic to John Rawls's Theory of Justice, justice is presented as the central pillar of morality — and distinguishes justice from fairness (ሚዛናዊነት): justice is chiefly the standard or measure of rightness, while fairness is the capacity to judge without putting one's own feeling or interest into the judgement.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 3 — Self-Respect, Honesty and Justice (recommended, never forced).
**Strand:** manners/morals — the strand the Grade 2 teacher guide itself gives this teaching (manners/morals, or constitution/civics where the guide teaches rights, law or the state inside Moral Education).
