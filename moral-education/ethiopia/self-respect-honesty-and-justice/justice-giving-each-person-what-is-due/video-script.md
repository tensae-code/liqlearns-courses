# Video script — Justice — giving each person what is due

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The lesson is text-first: this script says nothing the lesson text does not already teach.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Justice is defined as giving every person what is due to him or her. The reading places the idea in a long line — from Plato's Republic to John Rawls's Theory of Justice, justice is presented as the central pillar of morality — and distinguishes justice from fairness (ሚዛናዊነት): justice is chiefly the standard or measure of rightness, while fairness is the capacity to judge without putting one's own feeling or interest into the judgement.
