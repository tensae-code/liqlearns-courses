# How honesty is taught — praise truth, keep your word, choose honest friends — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) The guide prints ten ways, summarised in substance: (1) pay for truth with strong praise and hugs rather than rushing to punish or scold — praise builds trust; (2) teach children to speak truth and to keep silent rather than speak empty or false words; (3) speak the hard truth — a child whose friend cheats on a test should counsel the friend and, if refused, tell the teacher; (4) be the example — "a small habit of lying leads to a big habit of lying", and children who hear even a harmless white lie conclude lying is acceptable; (5) do not set children up to lie with trap questions — if you found the juice spilled on the sofa, say "wipe it up" instead of asking who did it; (6) take action when lying is caught — lying must have a consequence because it is wrong; (7) correct mistakes — ask why the child lied, explain how lying destroys trust and how hard trust is to regain, and show what else could have been done; (8) keep your word — teach children to treat their word as a sacred word; (9) care for others' property — borrowing is good, but what is borrowed must always be returned; (10) seek honest friends — "show me your friend and I will tell you who you are".  b) A topic from a different level of this course  c) The syllabus of the teacher guide  d) none of these
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

a) Ten ways to teach a child honesty  b) a topic from another course  c) today's weather  d) none of these
