# Lesson plan — How honesty is taught — praise truth, keep your word, choose honest friends

**Course:** Moral Education — Ethiopia · **Level folder:** Level 3 — Self-Respect, Honesty and Justice · **Lesson:** How honesty is taught — praise truth, keep your word, choose honest friends
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — Self-Respect, Honesty and Justice (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The guide prints ten ways, summarised in substance: (1) pay for truth with strong praise and hugs rather than rushing to punish or scold — praise builds trust; (2) teach children to speak truth and to keep silent rather than speak empty or false words; (3) speak the hard truth — a child whose friend cheats on a test should counsel the friend and, if refused, tell the teacher; (4) be the example — "a small habit of lying leads to a big habit of lying", and children who hear even a harmless white lie conclude lying is acceptable; (5) do not set children up to lie with trap questions — if you found the juice spilled on the sofa, say "wipe it up" instead of asking who did it; (6) take action when lying is caught — lying must have a consequence because it is wrong; (7) correct mistakes — ask why the child lied, explain how lying destroys trust and how hard trust is to regain, and show what else could have been done; (8) keep your word — teach children to treat their word as a sacred word; (9) care for others' property — borrowing is good, but what is borrowed must always be returned; (10) seek honest friends — "show me your friend and I will tell you who you are".
**This lesson's own level:** Level 3 — Self-Respect, Honesty and Justice (recommended, never forced).
**Strand:** manners/morals — the strand the Grade 2 teacher guide itself gives this teaching (manners/morals, or constitution/civics where the guide teaches rights, law or the state inside Moral Education).
