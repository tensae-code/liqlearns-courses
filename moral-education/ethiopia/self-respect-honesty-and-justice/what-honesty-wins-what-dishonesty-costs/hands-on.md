# Hands-on — What honesty wins, what dishonesty costs

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
The contents close the chapter with the importance of truthfulness and honesty, the benefits of being a truthful and honest person, and the damages of your absence; you must show the marks of a truthful person in your own conduct, and identify the harms of dishonesty, as chapter outcomes.

## Show it with real things
Carry the lesson's teaching into one real moment today — at home, at school or among your neighbours — and do the act the lesson teaches, naming each step aloud as you do it: what you are doing, why, and what the lesson says it builds. Compare what happened against the lesson's one thing: The contents close the chapter with the importance of truthfulness and honesty, the benefits of being a truthful and honest person, and the damages of their absence; pupils must show the marks of a truthful person in their own conduct, and identify the harms of dishonesty, as chapter outcomes.

## Check it for one day
For one day, watch for this lesson's teaching in real life around you — in your family, your classroom, your street. Each time you see it (or see it missing), note what you saw and what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
