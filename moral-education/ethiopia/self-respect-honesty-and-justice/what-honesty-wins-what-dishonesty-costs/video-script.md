# Video script — What honesty wins, what dishonesty costs

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The lesson is text-first: this script says nothing the lesson text does not already teach.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The contents close the chapter with the importance of truthfulness and honesty, the benefits of being a truthful and honest person, and the damages of their absence; pupils must show the marks of a truthful person in their own conduct, and identify the harms of dishonesty, as chapter outcomes.
