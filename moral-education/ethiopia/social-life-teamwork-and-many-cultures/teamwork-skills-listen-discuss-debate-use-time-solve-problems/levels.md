# Teamwork skills — listen, discuss, debate, use time, solve problems — depth

This lesson's parts are distinct and sequential, so they stand as its sub lessons (2026-11-09 ruling: sub lessons only where a lesson genuinely has distinct sequential parts). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: The skills are defined one by one in the guide: goodwill/frankness of heart (ቅን-አሳቢነት), communication, organisation, thinking deeply, adaptability — fitting yourself or things to changing conditions — debate (a formal discussion on a special matter at a meeting, where debaters try to persuade opponents or audience; a sample debate topic is "which is better, money or knowledge", with a warning to keep debate topics free of religious or other sensitive content), using time well — dividing time by task, doing tasks in order, finishing within the plan — and problem-solving, whose steps the closing answers fix: identify the problem, analyse it, prepare criteria, consider possible solutions, decide on the solution, carry the decision out, and follow up the decision. — shown in the learner's own words and in the lesson's closing act done in a real situation.
