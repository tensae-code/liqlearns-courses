# Lesson plan — Teamwork skills — listen, discuss, debate, use time, solve problems

**Course:** Moral Education — Ethiopia · **Level folder:** Level 6 — Healthy Social Life, Teamwork and Many Cultures · **Lesson:** Teamwork skills — listen, discuss, debate, use time, solve problems
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 6 — Healthy Social Life, Teamwork and Many Cultures (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The skills are defined one by one in the guide: goodwill/frankness of heart (ቅን-አሳቢነት), communication, organisation, thinking deeply, adaptability — fitting yourself or things to changing conditions — debate (a formal discussion on a special matter at a meeting, where debaters try to persuade opponents or audience; a sample debate topic is "which is better, money or knowledge", with a warning to keep debate topics free of religious or other sensitive content), using time well — dividing time by task, doing tasks in order, finishing within the plan — and problem-solving, whose steps the closing answers fix: identify the problem, analyse it, prepare criteria, consider possible solutions, decide on the solution, carry the decision out, and follow up the decision.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 6 — Healthy Social Life, Teamwork and Many Cultures (recommended, never forced).
**Strand:** manners/morals — the strand the Grade 3 teacher guide itself gives this teaching (manners/morals, or constitution/civics where the guide teaches rights, law or the state inside Moral Education).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The lesson teaches through its text first: every picture's and GIF's teaching is also in the text, so the lesson is fully learnable by listening to its text read aloud.
