# Hands-on — Teamwork skills — listen, discuss, debate, use time, solve problems

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Take this lesson's teaching into one real situation today — at home, at school or in your neighbourhood: do what the lesson teaches, then say in one sentence what you did and what it changed.

## Show it with real things
Carry the lesson's teaching into one real moment today — at home, at school or among your neighbours — and do the act the lesson teaches, naming each step aloud as you do it: what you are doing, why, and what the lesson says it builds. Compare what happened against the lesson's one thing: The skills are defined one by one in the guide: goodwill/frankness of heart (ቅን-አሳቢነት), communication, organisation, thinking deeply, adaptability — fitting yourself or things to changing conditions — debate (a formal discussion on a special matter at a meeting, where debaters try to persuade opponents or audience; a sample debate topic is "which is better, money or knowledge", with a warning to keep debate topics free of religious or other sensitive content), using time well — dividing time by task, doing tasks in order, finishing within the plan — and problem-solving, whose steps the closing answers fix: identify the problem, analyse it, prepare criteria, consider possible solutions, decide on the solution, carry the decision out, and follow up the decision.

## Check it for one day
For one day, watch for this lesson's teaching in real life around you — in your family, your classroom, your street. Each time you see it (or see it missing), note what you saw and what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
