# Video script — Teamwork skills — listen, discuss, debate, use time, solve problems

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The lesson is text-first: this script says nothing the lesson text does not already teach.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The skills are defined one by one in the guide: goodwill/frankness of heart (ቅን-አሳቢነት), communication, organisation, thinking deeply, adaptability — fitting yourself or things to changing conditions — debate (a formal discussion on a special matter at a meeting, where debaters try to persuade opponents or audience; a sample debate topic is "which is better, money or knowledge", with a warning to keep debate topics free of religious or other sensitive content), using time well — dividing time by task, doing tasks in order, finishing within the plan — and problem-solving, whose steps the closing answers fix: identify the problem, analyse it, prepare criteria, consider possible solutions, decide on the solution, carry the decision out, and follow up the decision.
