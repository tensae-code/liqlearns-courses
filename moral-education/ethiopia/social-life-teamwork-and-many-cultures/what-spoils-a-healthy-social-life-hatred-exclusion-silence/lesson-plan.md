# Lesson plan — What spoils a healthy social life — hatred, exclusion, silence

**Course:** Moral Education — Ethiopia · **Level folder:** Level 6 — Healthy Social Life, Teamwork and Many Cultures · **Lesson:** What spoils a healthy social life — hatred, exclusion, silence
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 6 — Healthy Social Life, Teamwork and Many Cultures (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The factors, from the activity answers on the family: hatred and exclusion; the quality of the parent-child relationship; parenting types and practices; the absence of a culture of openness — without openness and open communication, the answers say, there can be no talk of a healthy social life, since it requires both; family separation by divorce or other causes, which increases single parenthood; unemployment; carelessness/harming others; and the spread of crime.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 6 — Healthy Social Life, Teamwork and Many Cultures (recommended, never forced).
**Strand:** manners/morals — the strand the Grade 3 teacher guide itself gives this teaching (manners/morals, or constitution/civics where the guide teaches rights, law or the state inside Moral Education).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The lesson teaches through its text first: every picture's and GIF's teaching is also in the text, so the lesson is fully learnable by listening to its text read aloud.
