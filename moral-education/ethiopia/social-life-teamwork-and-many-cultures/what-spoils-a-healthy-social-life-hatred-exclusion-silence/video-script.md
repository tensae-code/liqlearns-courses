# Video script — What spoils a healthy social life — hatred, exclusion, silence

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The lesson is text-first: this script says nothing the lesson text does not already teach.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The factors, from the activity answers on the family: hatred and exclusion; the quality of the parent-child relationship; parenting types and practices; the absence of a culture of openness — without openness and open communication, the answers say, there can be no talk of a healthy social life, since it requires both; family separation by divorce or other causes, which increases single parenthood; unemployment; carelessness/harming others; and the spread of crime.
