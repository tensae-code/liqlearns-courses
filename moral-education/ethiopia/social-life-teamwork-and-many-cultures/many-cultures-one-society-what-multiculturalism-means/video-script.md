# Video script — Many cultures, one society — what multiculturalism means

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The lesson is text-first: this script says nothing the lesson text does not already teach.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Multiculturalism is introduced as the presence of many cultures together, and the summary point the teacher may add is that, since every culture has its own social life and organisation, cultural diversity gives individuals the chance to have meaningful choices.
