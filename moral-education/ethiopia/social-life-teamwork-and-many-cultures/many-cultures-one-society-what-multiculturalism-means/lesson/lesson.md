# Many cultures, one society — what multiculturalism means

## Do it first

Before you read, try the lesson's act once with what you already know: You and a companion discuss the concept in pairs and bring living examples from your own society. Keep what you did in mind — you will compare it with your work at the end.

## ℹ Info

Multiculturalism is introduced as the presence of many cultures together, and the summary point the teacher may add is that, since every culture has its own social life and organisation, cultural diversity gives individuals the chance to have meaningful choices.

## Multiculturalism
