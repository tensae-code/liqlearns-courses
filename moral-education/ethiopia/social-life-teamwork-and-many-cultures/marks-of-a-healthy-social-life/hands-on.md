# Hands-on — Marks of a healthy social life

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
You and a companion read the characteristics, reflect on them, and discuss each with examples in groups.

## Show it with real things
Carry the lesson's teaching into one real moment today — at home, at school or among your neighbours — and do the act the lesson teaches, naming each step aloud as you do it: what you are doing, why, and what the lesson says it builds. Compare what happened against the lesson's one thing: The closing answers list the marks: building strong relationships with family and friends; expressing your own outlook and accepting others' ideas; cooperating and working together; and treating each other with respect.

## Check it for one day
For one day, watch for this lesson's teaching in real life around you — in your family, your classroom, your street. Each time you see it (or see it missing), note what you saw and what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
