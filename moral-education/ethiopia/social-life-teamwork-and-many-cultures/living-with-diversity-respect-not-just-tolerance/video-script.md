# Video script — Living with diversity — respect, not just tolerance

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The lesson is text-first: this script says nothing the lesson text does not already teach.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The ways are listed in full: recognise multiculturalism; remove the tendency to count your own culture superior or inferior; respect difference; remove counting an individual's or group's way of life as backward; recognise the correctness and contributions of different cultural expressions; value what other cultures give; be ready to admire other cultures; encourage different groups' contributions; encourage people, freed from partiality, to develop their potential for themselves and others; and — the list's own climax — to bring unity in diversity, respect differences rather than merely tolerating them.
