# Hands-on — Living with diversity — respect, not just tolerance

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Pupils' own communities' ways of accommodating diversity are the discussion matter, with pictures of people celebrating, listening, respecting and getting to know each other together.

## Show it with real things
Carry the lesson's teaching into one real moment today — at home, at school or among your neighbours — and do the act the lesson teaches, naming each step aloud as you do it: what you are doing, why, and what the lesson says it builds. Compare what happened against the lesson's one thing: The ways are listed in full: recognise multiculturalism; remove the tendency to count your own culture superior or inferior; respect difference; remove counting an individual's or group's way of life as backward; recognise the correctness and contributions of different cultural expressions; value what other cultures give; be ready to admire other cultures; encourage different groups' contributions; encourage people, freed from partiality, to develop their potential for themselves and others; and — the list's own climax — to bring unity in diversity, respect differences rather than merely tolerating them.

## Check it for one day
For one day, watch for this lesson's teaching in real life around you — in your family, your classroom, your street. Each time you see it (or see it missing), note what you saw and what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
