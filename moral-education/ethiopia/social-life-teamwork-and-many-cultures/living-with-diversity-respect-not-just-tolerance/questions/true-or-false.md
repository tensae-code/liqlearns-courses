# Living with diversity — respect, not just tolerance — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** This statement is the lesson's one thing: The ways are listed in full: recognise multiculturalism; remove the tendency to count your own culture superior or inferior; respect difference; remove counting an individual's or group's way of life as backward; recognise the correctness and contributions of different cultural expressions; value what other cultures give; be ready to admire other cultures; encourage different groups' contributions; encourage people, freed from partiality, to develop their potential for themselves and others; and — the list's own climax — to bring unity in diversity, respect differences rather than merely tolerating them.
**Answer: True** — it states the lesson's teaching in full.

**2.** A learner who lands on this lesson directly must finish every earlier level before starting it.
**Answer: False** — earlier levels are recommended first, never a forced gate; every lesson stands alone.

Difficulty: 1 = direct recall; 2 = applying the course's direct-entry rule.
