# Living with diversity — respect, not just tolerance — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** State this lesson's one thing in your own words.
**Expected:** The ways are listed in full: recognise multiculturalism; remove the tendency to count your own culture superior or inferior; respect difference; remove counting an individual's or group's way of life as backward; recognise the correctness and contributions of different cultural expressions; value what other cultures give; be ready to admire other cultures; encourage different groups' contributions; encourage people, freed from partiality, to develop their potential for themselves and others; and — the list's own climax — to bring unity in diversity, respect differences rather than merely tolerating them.

**2.** Explain the lesson's first part: Ways of accommodating multiculturalism.
**Expected:** an answer drawn from the lesson text for that part, in the learner's own words.

Difficulty: 2 = restating the teaching accurately without copying it.
