# Hands-on — How Ethiopia works together — debo, idir, equb

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
You and a companion identify the cooperation and teamwork culture in your own community and school, and the teacher is told to learn the names of the groups in the community before the lesson.

## Show it with real things
Carry the lesson's teaching into one real moment today — at home, at school or among your neighbours — and do the act the lesson teaches, naming each step aloud as you do it: what you are doing, why, and what the lesson says it builds. Compare what happened against the lesson's one thing: The chapter names the country's own cooperation culture in the community — debo (ጂጊ), the communal work party; idir, the mutual-aid association; and equb, the rotating savings association (named in the chapter introduction) — and, at school, group work.

## Check it for one day
For one day, watch for this lesson's teaching in real life around you — in your family, your classroom, your street. Each time you see it (or see it missing), note what you saw and what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
