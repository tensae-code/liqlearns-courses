# How Ethiopia works together — debo, idir, equb

## Do it first

Before you read, try the lesson's act once with what you already know: You and a companion identify the cooperation and teamwork culture in your own community and school, and the teacher is told to learn the names of the groups in the community before the lesson. Keep what you did in mind — you will compare it with your work at the end.

## ℹ Info

The chapter names the country's own cooperation culture in the community — debo (ጂጊ), the communal work party; idir, the mutual-aid association; and equb, the rotating savings association (named in the chapter introduction) — and, at school, group work.
