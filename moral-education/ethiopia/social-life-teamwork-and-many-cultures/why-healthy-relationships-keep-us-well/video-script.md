# Video script — Why healthy relationships keep us well

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The lesson is text-first: this script says nothing the lesson text does not already teach.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The benefits are listed in the answers: people with healthy social relationships and friends feel belonging and purpose; when people stand beside you in joy and in sorrow, your level of worry falls; a healthy social life refreshes the mind, raises the body's power to resist disease, and even lengthens life; friends support each other in hard times; it makes the value one person gives another grow; it helps people live in unity; it helps an individual's physical, social and other behaviours develop; and in general it improves a society's social, economic and political affairs.
