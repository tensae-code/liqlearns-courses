# Seeing diversity — peoples, languages, dress, music, food, weddings

## Do it first

Before you read, try the lesson's act once with what you already know: You and a companion in groups of five identify the kinds in your own community. Keep what you did in mind — you will compare it with your work at the end.

## ℹ Info
