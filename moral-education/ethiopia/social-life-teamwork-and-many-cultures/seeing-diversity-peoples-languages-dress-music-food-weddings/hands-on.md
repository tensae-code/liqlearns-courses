# Hands-on — Seeing diversity — peoples, languages, dress, music, food, weddings

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
You and a companion in groups of five identify the kinds in your own community.

## Show it with real things
Carry the lesson's teaching into one real moment today — at home, at school or among your neighbours — and do the act the lesson teaches, naming each step aloud as you do it: what you are doing, why, and what the lesson says it builds. Compare what happened against the lesson's one thing: The chapter lists the forms under which diversity appears in the community: ethnic group (ጎሳ), language, religion, the state of dress, traditional dress, traditional music, traditional food and the wedding ceremony — the contents and syllabus name ethnic group, traditional clothing, traditional music, traditional food and wedding ceremony, and the chapter introduction adds language and religion.

## Check it for one day
For one day, watch for this lesson's teaching in real life around you — in your family, your classroom, your street. Each time you see it (or see it missing), note what you saw and what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
