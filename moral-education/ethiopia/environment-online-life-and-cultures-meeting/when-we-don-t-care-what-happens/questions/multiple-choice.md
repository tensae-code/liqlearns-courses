# When we don't care — what happens — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

a) The consequences of not caring, listed as a chain: water shortage, pollution, food shortage, loss of biodiversity, health problems, flooding and storms, acid rain.  b) A topic from a different level of this course  c) The syllabus of the teacher guide  d) none of these
