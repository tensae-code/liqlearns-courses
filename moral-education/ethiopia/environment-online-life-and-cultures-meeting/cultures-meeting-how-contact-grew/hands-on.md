# Hands-on — Cultures meeting — how contact grew

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Linkage widening in the you' own lifetimes: phone, fax, email and the internet have made contact between cultures constant — the chapter opens by noticing it.

## Show it with real things
Carry the lesson's teaching into one real moment today — at home, at school or among your neighbours — and do the act the lesson teaches, naming each step aloud as you do it: what you are doing, why, and what the lesson says it builds. Compare what happened against the lesson's one thing: Linkage widening in the pupils' own lifetimes: phone, fax, email and the internet have made contact between cultures constant — the chapter opens by noticing it.

## Check it for one day
For one day, watch for this lesson's teaching in real life around you — in your family, your classroom, your street. Each time you see it (or see it missing), note what you saw and what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
