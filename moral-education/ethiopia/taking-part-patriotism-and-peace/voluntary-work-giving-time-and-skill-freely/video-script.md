# Video script — Voluntary work — giving time and skill freely

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The lesson is text-first: this script says nothing the lesson text does not already teach.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Volunteering defined: giving your time, energy and knowledge freely for the community's benefit, without seeking profit — social service as free service to where you live, alone or together.

The picture-based answers name its forms: helping people who need help, planting seedlings, cleaning the surroundings, farm work.

Remember it this way: Volunteering defined: giving your time, energy and knowledge freely for the community's benefit, without seeking profit — social service as free service to where you live, alone or together.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
