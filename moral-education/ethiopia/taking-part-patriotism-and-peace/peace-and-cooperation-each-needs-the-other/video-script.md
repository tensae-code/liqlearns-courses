# Video script — Peace and cooperation — each needs the other

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The lesson is text-first: this script says nothing the lesson text does not already teach.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The closing teaching: peace is kept by people working together — war and conflict are what come from failing to work together for peace — and the unit's values are gathered in the syllabus's list: for peaceful behaviour, respect, understanding, patience, sharing, discussion and settling conflict peacefully; for cooperation, brotherhood, sincerity, openness and thinking of others.
