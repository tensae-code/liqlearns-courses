# Paying tax honestly — and the habit of saving — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** This statement is the lesson's one thing: Trade and market defined first (trade: buying, selling and exchanging goods and services; a market: where goods are bought and sold), then the session's core: tax — the payment individuals and organisations make to government from their income, which everyone has a legal duty to pay, because without tax the common services (education, health, defence) cannot be had — and saving: putting aside money or time for the future instead of consuming it all, with banks and insurance companies named as modern saving institutions.
**Answer: True** — it states the lesson's teaching in full.

**2.** A learner who lands on this lesson directly must finish every earlier level before starting it.
**Answer: False** — earlier levels are recommended first, never a forced gate; every lesson stands alone.
