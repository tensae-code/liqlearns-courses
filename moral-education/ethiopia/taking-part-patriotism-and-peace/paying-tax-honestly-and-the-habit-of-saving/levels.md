# Paying tax honestly — and the habit of saving — depth

This lesson's parts are distinct and sequential, so they stand as its sub lessons (2026-11-09 ruling: sub lessons only where a lesson genuinely has distinct sequential parts). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: Trade and market defined first (trade: buying, selling and exchanging goods and services; a market: where goods are bought and sold), then the session's core: tax — the payment individuals and organisations make to government from their income, which everyone has a legal duty to pay, because without tax the common services (education, health, defence) cannot be had — and saving: putting aside money or time for the future instead of consuming it all, with banks and insurance companies named as modern saving institutions. — shown in the learner's own words and in the lesson's closing act done in a real situation.
