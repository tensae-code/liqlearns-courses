# Video script — Paying tax honestly — and the habit of saving

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The lesson is text-first: this script says nothing the lesson text does not already teach.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Trade and market defined first (trade: buying, selling and exchanging goods and services; a market: where goods are bought and sold), then the session's core: tax — the payment individuals and organisations make to government from their income, which everyone has a legal duty to pay, because without tax the common services (education, health, defence) cannot be had — and saving: putting aside money or time for the future instead of consuming it all, with banks and insurance companies named as modern saving institutions.
