# How patriotism shows

## Do it first

Before you read, try the lesson's act once with what you already know: Take this lesson's teaching into one real situation today — at home, at school or in your neighbourhood: do what the lesson teaches, then say in one sentence what you did and what it changed. ## ℹ Info

How love of country shows: pride in the country, a feeling that the country is one's own, readiness to sacrifice for it, fighting corruption, honouring the country's heroes — and in acts: respecting the flag, guarding sovereignty, putting the country's interest before private interest, pride in every national achievement.
