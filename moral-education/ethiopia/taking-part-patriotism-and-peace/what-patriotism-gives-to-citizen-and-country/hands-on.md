# Hands-on — What patriotism gives — to citizen and country

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Take this lesson's teaching into one real situation today — at home, at school or in your neighbourhood: do what the lesson teaches, then say in one sentence what you did and what it changed.

## Show it with real things
Carry the lesson's teaching into one real moment today — at home, at school or among your neighbours — and do the act the lesson teaches, naming each step aloud as you do it: what you are doing, why, and what the lesson says it builds. Compare what happened against the lesson's one thing: What loving the country gives, seen from citizen and country both: citizens grow their capacity, the next generation inherits a better country, and the country gains defence against outside attack; the benefit to those in authority is taken up in the chapter answers (placement 22's territory) — power used to build a better country instead of feeding corruption.

## Check it for one day
For one day, watch for this lesson's teaching in real life around you — in your family, your classroom, your street. Each time you see it (or see it missing), note what you saw and what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
