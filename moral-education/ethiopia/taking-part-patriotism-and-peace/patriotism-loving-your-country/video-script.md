# Video script — Patriotism — loving your country

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The lesson is text-first: this script says nothing the lesson text does not already teach.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Patriotism defined as a person's deep love for their country — and, in the second definition the answers give, love for its citizens too; the opening question is "what does being Ethiopian mean?" The duty-to-country part is civics and leads; the love itself is the morals part.
