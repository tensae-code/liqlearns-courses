# The patriotic citizen — characteristics — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) The portrait: patriotic citizens love their country and are proud of its culture and history, raise their children to know and love it, treat neighbours' children with love, plant and water greenery, and respect others' beliefs and cultures.  b) A topic from a different level of this course  c) The syllabus of the teacher guide  d) none of these
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) Characteristics of a patriotic citizen  b) a topic from another course  c) today's weather  d) none of these
**Answer: a)** — That is this lesson's first part.

Difficulty: 1 = recall of the lesson's one thing; 2 = telling this lesson's parts from other lessons' parts.
