## What peace is

Peace defined three ways in the answers: a quiet, settled state; the presence of security and order in a society; the absence of war and conflict.

## Peace

Pupils are given their own role in keeping it — as members of the community they share in solving its problems, including early marriage and violence — and the cost of its absence: conflict, war, loss of freedom.
