# Video script — Peace — a settled, secure community

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The lesson is text-first: this script says nothing the lesson text does not already teach.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Peace defined three ways in the answers: a quiet, settled state; the presence of security and order in a society; the absence of war and conflict.

Peace defined three ways in the answers: a quiet, settled state; the presence of security and order in a society; the absence of war and conflict.

Pupils are given their own role in keeping it — as members of the community they share in solving its problems, including early marriage and violence — and the cost of its absence: conflict, war, loss of freedom.

Remember it this way: Peace defined three ways in the answers: a quiet, settled state; the presence of security and order in a society; the absence of war and conflict.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
