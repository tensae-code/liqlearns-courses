# Lesson plan — The person of integrity — a portrait

**Course:** Moral Education — Ethiopia · **Level folder:** Level 11 — Integrity, the Law and Good Behaviour · **Lesson:** The person of integrity — a portrait
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 11 — Integrity, the Law and Good Behaviour (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The characteristics gathered into a portrait: such people live by their principles, do what they said they would do, hold their principles under outside pressure, never claim to know what they do not know, and approach people for being people, not for their rank.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 11 — Integrity, the Law and Good Behaviour (recommended, never forced).
**Strand:** manners/morals — the strand the Grade 6 teacher guide itself gives this teaching (manners/morals, or constitution/civics where the guide teaches rights, law or the state inside Moral Education).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The lesson teaches through its text first: every picture's and GIF's teaching is also in the text, so the lesson is fully learnable by listening to its text read aloud.

Nothing but yourself — and, for the discussion and role-play acts, a companion or a small group. Nothing else.

4. **Questions** — from questions/: multiple-choice, true-or-false, short-answer, fill-in-the-blank, matching — served at the learner's level (levels.md).
