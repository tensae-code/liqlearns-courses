## Characteristics of a person of good morals

The marks listed: integrity, trustworthiness, accountability, giving others value, respect and admiration, self-control, being worthy of trust, keeping secrets.

## And respect

The session's long activity set then teaches respect directly, in the answers: what respecting means (honouring people for their personhood, ability and good qualities), respecting others' property, who an honest person is (always truthful, never steals), and why respect is needed — conscience's satisfaction, solving problems, showing honesty, building better relationships — beginning from self-respect and extending even beyond people.

> Tip — from us, not from the books: Act it, don't just say it. A value you have practised once in a real moment — at home, at school, in your street — is yours in a way a value you have only read about is not.
