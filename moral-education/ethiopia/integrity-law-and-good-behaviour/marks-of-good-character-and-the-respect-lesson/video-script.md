# Video script — Marks of good character — and the respect lesson

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The lesson is text-first: this script says nothing the lesson text does not already teach.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The marks listed: integrity, trustworthiness, accountability, giving others value, respect and admiration, self-control, being worthy of trust, keeping secrets.

The marks listed: integrity, trustworthiness, accountability, giving others value, respect and admiration, self-control, being worthy of trust, keeping secrets.

The session's long activity set then teaches respect directly, in the answers: what respecting means (honouring people for their personhood, ability and good qualities), respecting others' property, who an honest person is (always truthful, never steals), and why respect is needed — conscience's satisfaction, solving problems, showing honesty, building better relationships — beginning from self-respect and extending even beyond people.

Remember it this way: The marks listed: integrity, trustworthiness, accountability, giving others value, respect and admiration, self-control, being worthy of trust, keeping secrets.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
