# Video script — The law-abiding citizen — and what lawlessness brings

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The lesson is text-first: this script says nothing the lesson text does not already teach.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
One placement for the unit's paired close. The law-abiding person: understands laws are established by people's will and are instruments of social change, respects law and order at all times, and reports acts that threaten the community's peaceful life, avoiding what such a citizen must avoid — violating others' rights, and acts like theft and lying that disturb peaceful coexistence.
