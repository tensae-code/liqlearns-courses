## Characteristics and problems of disobedience

The problems of not being law-abiding are then named at full strength in the answers: absence of peace, human-rights violations, corruption, addiction, child trafficking and tax evasion (the 2.4.1–2.4.5 sub-contents the syllabus adds — see Verification block).
