# Video script — Why obey the law — freedom, peace, fairness

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The lesson is text-first: this script says nothing the lesson text does not already teach.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The purposes of obedience listed in the answers: enjoying freedom fully, keeping peace, fair distribution of wealth, securing sovereignty, protecting citizens' private interests — with the citizen's part spelled out: know that your freedom has limits where others' rights begin, know and obey the laws, and cooperate with the authorities, including reporting suspicious movements to the responsible government bodies.
