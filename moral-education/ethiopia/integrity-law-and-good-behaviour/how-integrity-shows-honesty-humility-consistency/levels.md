# How integrity shows — honesty, humility, consistency — depth

This lesson's parts are distinct and sequential, so they stand as its sub lessons (2026-11-09 ruling: sub lessons only where a lesson genuinely has distinct sequential parts). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: How integrity shows: honesty (telling the truth, not stealing, not cheating), strength to pass through difficulties without fear, seeing others' problems as your own, humility, accountability, and consistency — the same person in private and in public; classroom examples include respecting teachers, doing set tasks on time and caring for school property. — shown in the learner's own words and in the lesson's closing act done in a real situation.
