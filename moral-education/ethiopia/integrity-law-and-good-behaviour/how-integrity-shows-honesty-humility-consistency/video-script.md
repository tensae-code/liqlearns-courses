# Video script — How integrity shows — honesty, humility, consistency

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The lesson is text-first: this script says nothing the lesson text does not already teach.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
How integrity shows: honesty (telling the truth, not stealing, not cheating), strength to pass through difficulties without fear, seeing others' problems as your own, humility, accountability, and consistency — the same person in private and in public; classroom examples include respecting teachers, doing set tasks on time and caring for school property.
