# How integrity shows — honesty, humility, consistency

## Do it first

Before you read, try the lesson's act once with what you already know: Take this lesson's teaching into one real situation today — at home, at school or in your neighbourhood: do what the lesson teaches, then say in one sentence what you did and what it changed. Keep what you did in mind — you will compare it with your work at the end.

## ℹ Info

How integrity shows: honesty (telling the truth, not stealing, not cheating), strength to pass through difficulties without fear, seeing others' problems as your own, humility, accountability, and consistency — the same person in private and in public; classroom examples include respecting teachers, doing set tasks on time and caring for school property.
