# Video script — Good behaviour — what it is, and that it is chosen

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The lesson is text-first: this script says nothing the lesson text does not already teach.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Behaviour defined as the indicator of how we treat family, friends, teachers and community; good behaviour as feeling well and doing good for others as for yourself — humanity, generosity, compassion, sharing others' feelings, thoughtfulness, friendliness, humility.

The guide insists behaviour is a result of choices, shaped by what children see at home and among friends: a child who watches cheating learns to think cheating.

Remember it this way: Behaviour defined as the indicator of how we treat family, friends, teachers and community; good behaviour as feeling well and doing good for others as for yourself — humanity, generosity, compassion, sharing others' feelings, thoughtfulness, friendliness, humility.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
