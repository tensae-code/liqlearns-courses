Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) Behaviour defined as the indicator of how we treat family, friends, teachers and community; good behaviour as feeling well and doing good for others as for yourself — humanity, generosity, compassion, sharing others' feelings, thoughtfulness, friendliness, humility.  b) A topic from a different level of this course  c) The syllabus of the teacher guide  d) none of these
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
