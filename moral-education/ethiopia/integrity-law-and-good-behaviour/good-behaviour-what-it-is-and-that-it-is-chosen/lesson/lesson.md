## What good behaviour is

Behaviour defined as the indicator of how we treat family, friends, teachers and community; good behaviour as feeling well and doing good for others as for yourself — humanity, generosity, compassion, sharing others' feelings, thoughtfulness, friendliness, humility.

## Good behaviour

The guide insists behaviour is a result of choices, shaped by what children see at home and among friends: a child who watches cheating learns to think cheating.

> Tip — from us, not from the books: Act it, don't just say it. A value you have practised once in a real moment — at home, at school, in your street — is yours in a way a value you have only read about is not.
