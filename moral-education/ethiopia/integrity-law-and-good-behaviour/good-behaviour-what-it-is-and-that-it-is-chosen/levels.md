# Good behaviour — what it is, and that it is chosen — depth

This lesson's parts are distinct and sequential, so they stand as its sub lessons (2026-11-09 ruling: sub lessons only where a lesson genuinely has distinct sequential parts). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: Behaviour defined as the indicator of how we treat family, friends, teachers and community; good behaviour as feeling well and doing good for others as for yourself — humanity, generosity, compassion, sharing others' feelings, thoughtfulness, friendliness, humility. — shown in the learner's own words and in the lesson's closing act done in a real situation.
