# Video script — Law-abidingness — what law is for

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The lesson is text-first: this script says nothing the lesson text does not already teach.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The grade's civics foundation: law-abidingness as readiness and commitment to obey one's country's laws, opened through the purposes of law — to make citizens know their rights and duties, to direct behaviour, and to secure citizens' equality.

A law-abiding citizen's duties follow: be ready to obey, know the country's laws, settle problems peacefully; at school, know the school's rules, complete teachers' tasks, treat peers with respect.

Remember it this way: The grade's civics foundation: law-abidingness as readiness and commitment to obey one's country's laws, opened through the purposes of law — to make citizens know their rights and duties, to direct behaviour, and to secure citizens' equality.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
