# Video script — Why good morals are worth having — live and let live

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The lesson is text-first: this script says nothing the lesson text does not already teach.

This lesson is about one thing.
The value of wearing good behaviour, taught through the strategies that build it — developing self-control, accepting and strengthening moral values and principles, being loyal to the law — and through the follow-up question "live and let live": wanting for others the life you want for yourself, because people need each other.
