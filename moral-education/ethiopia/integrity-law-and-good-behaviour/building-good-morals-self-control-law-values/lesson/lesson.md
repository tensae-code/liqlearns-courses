## Ways of developing good morals

The unit's longest session: at least five ways are named in the answers — self-control, respecting the law, accepting and following the community's values and principles, respecting the school's rules, respecting the community's principles. Self-control is defined (restraining yourself from needless thoughts and acts) and justified (it saves from harm and makes a balanced life); the Thomas case — a disruptive pupil — is worked for advice: count what your acts do to yourself and others.

## Building good morals

The respect for law and school rules is the civics part; the self-control and values parts are manners/morals, and dominate.
