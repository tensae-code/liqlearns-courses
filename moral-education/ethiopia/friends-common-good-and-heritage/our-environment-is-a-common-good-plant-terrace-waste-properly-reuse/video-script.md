# Video script — Our environment is a common good — plant, terrace, waste properly, reuse

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The lesson is text-first: this script says nothing the lesson text does not already teach.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The second part treated (5.5.3): the environment is where we live and everything around us, and protecting it is the responsibility of everyone — individuals, groups, communities, government and non-governmental organisations worldwide; the environment is called a global common good.

The second part treated (5.5.3): the environment is where we live and everything around us, and protecting it is the responsibility of everyone — individuals, groups, communities, government and non-governmental organisations worldwide; the environment is called a global common good. Protecting it means guarding it from danger for sustainability and human wellbeing: planting seedlings, building terraces, disposing of dry and liquid waste properly, and using alternative energy (hydro, solar, wind).

Further ways: oppose dumping waste in ditches, rivers, ponds and green places; cut misuse of water, fuel and electricity; reuse materials, repair broken things for long use, and recycle plastic, paper and glass. Chapter 6's opening table (printed p.37) reinforces the lesson with a protected/unprotected environment contrast — clean air and water, lasting resources, healthy life and economic growth against pollution, wildfire, disease, drought, displacement and resource conflict.

Remember it this way: The second part treated (5.5.3): the environment is where we live and everything around us, and protecting it is the responsibility of everyone — individuals, groups, communities, government and non-governmental organisations worldwide; the environment is called a global common good.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
