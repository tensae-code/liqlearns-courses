## Protecting the environment

The second part treated (5.5.3): the environment is where we live and everything around us, and protecting it is the responsibility of everyone — individuals, groups, communities, government and non-governmental organisations worldwide; the environment is called a global common good. Protecting it means guarding it from danger for sustainability and human wellbeing: planting seedlings, building terraces, disposing of dry and liquid waste properly, and using alternative energy (hydro, solar, wind).

## A global common good

Further ways: oppose dumping waste in ditches, rivers, ponds and green places; cut misuse of water, fuel and electricity; reuse materials, repair broken things for long use, and recycle plastic, paper and glass. Chapter 6's opening table (printed p.37) reinforces the lesson with a protected/unprotected environment contrast — clean air and water, lasting resources, healthy life and economic growth against pollution, wildfire, disease, drought, displacement and resource conflict.
