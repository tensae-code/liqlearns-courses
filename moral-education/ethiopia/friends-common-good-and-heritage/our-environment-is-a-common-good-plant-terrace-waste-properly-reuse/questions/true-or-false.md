# Our environment is a common good — plant, terrace, waste properly, reuse — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** This statement is the lesson's one thing: The second part treated (5.5.3): the environment is where we live and everything around us, and protecting it is the responsibility of everyone — individuals, groups, communities, government and non-governmental organisations worldwide; the environment is called a global common good.
**Answer: True** — it states the lesson's teaching in full.

**2.** A learner who lands on this lesson directly must finish every earlier level before starting it.
**Answer: False** — earlier levels are recommended first, never a forced gate; every lesson stands alone.

Difficulty: 1 = direct recall; 2 = applying the course's direct-entry rule.
