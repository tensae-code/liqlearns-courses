## What positive social interaction is

The chapter opens with Aristotle's saying that man is a social animal: people by nature seek social bonds with many others. A positive social bond is a good relationship — one of good character, whose participants share common interests and ideas, are ready to give and receive, and share thoughts, feelings, experiences and materials; the guide adds that a good relationship matters from the home to the workplace and through the whole of life.

## Man is a social animal

Its opposite is also defined: negative interaction is improper behaviour or skill that damages a good relationship or friendship, and an obstacle is whatever blocks or hinders achieving a thing.
