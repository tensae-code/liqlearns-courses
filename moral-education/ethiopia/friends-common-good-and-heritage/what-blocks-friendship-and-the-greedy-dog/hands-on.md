# Hands-on — What blocks friendship — and the greedy dog

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Its story anchor is the tale of the greedy (selfish) dog, which you read and draw the lesson from (the story is in the student book; the guide names it and assigns reading time, but does not print its text — plot not recoverable from the guide alone).

## Show it with real things
Carry the lesson's teaching into one real moment today — at home, at school or among your neighbours — and do the act the lesson teaches, naming each step aloud as you do it: what you are doing, why, and what the lesson says it builds. Compare what happened against the lesson's one thing: The chapter closes with the obstacles to positive social interaction, identified in pair and group work from a table of examples, notes and demonstrations; the activity answers point to misunderstanding, mistrust and the failure to build lasting friendship.

## Check it for one day
For one day, watch for this lesson's teaching in real life around you — in your family, your classroom, your street. Each time you see it (or see it missing), note what you saw and what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
