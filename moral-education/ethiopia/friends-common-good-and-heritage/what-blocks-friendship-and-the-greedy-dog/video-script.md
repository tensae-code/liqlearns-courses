# Video script — What blocks friendship — and the greedy dog

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The lesson is text-first: this script says nothing the lesson text does not already teach.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The chapter closes with the obstacles to positive social interaction, identified in pair and group work from a table of examples, notes and demonstrations; the activity answers point to misunderstanding, mistrust and the failure to build lasting friendship.
