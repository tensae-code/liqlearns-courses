# Keeping heritage safe — teach, record, watch, protect by law, belong — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** State this lesson's one thing in your own words.
**Expected:** Protection means keeping a thing as it is — guarding its safety, original identity, size, shape, colour, character and meaning. The ways, in the book's order: first, awareness — teach the public what heritage is worth, what can harm it and how its safety is kept, including putting heritage into the school curriculum and teaching through radio and television; second, records — keep information on each heritage item, in digital and paper archives, saying what it holds, when and from what and why and by whom it was made; third, continuing and periodic monitoring of the heritage and its site's history and condition; fourth, legal frameworks that protect it; fifth, let local people visit for a small fee so they feel ownership of the heritage.

**2.** Explain the lesson's first part: How heritage is protected.
**Expected:** an answer drawn from the lesson text for that part, in the learner's own words.

Difficulty: 2 = restating the teaching accurately without copying it.
