# Keeping heritage safe — teach, record, watch, protect by law, belong — depth

This lesson's parts are distinct and sequential, so they stand as its sub lessons (2026-11-09 ruling: sub lessons only where a lesson genuinely has distinct sequential parts). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: Protection means keeping a thing as it is — guarding its safety, original identity, size, shape, colour, character and meaning. The ways, in the book's order: first, awareness — teach the public what heritage is worth, what can harm it and how its safety is kept, including putting heritage into the school curriculum and teaching through radio and television; second, records — keep information on each heritage item, in digital and paper archives, saying what it holds, when and from what and why and by whom it was made; third, continuing and periodic monitoring of the heritage and its site's history and condition; fourth, legal frameworks that protect it; fifth, let local people visit for a small fee so they feel ownership of the heritage. — shown in the learner's own words and in the lesson's closing act done in a real situation.
