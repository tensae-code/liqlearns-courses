# Growing good friendships — sharing, trust, keeping confidences

## Do it first

Before you read, try the lesson's act once with what you already know: You and a companion work in pairs and groups on the ways, and one activity turns on what friends like and dislike and on speaking about hidden wants inside a friendship. Keep what you did in mind — you will compare it with your work at the end.

## ℹ Info

The chapter teaches ways of increasing positive social interaction at school and among the public, and lists the marks of a good relationship or friendship: a shared interest or idea in particular; readiness to give and to receive — sharing thoughts, feelings, experiences and materials; and, from the activity answers, the acceptable traits to name include sociability, openness, keeping respect, trust, caring for others, keeping confidences and team spirit.
