# Hands-on — Growing good friendships — sharing, trust, keeping confidences

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
You and a companion work in pairs and groups on the ways, and one activity turns on what friends like and dislike and on speaking about hidden wants inside a friendship.

## Show it with real things
Carry the lesson's teaching into one real moment today — at home, at school or among your neighbours — and do the act the lesson teaches, naming each step aloud as you do it: what you are doing, why, and what the lesson says it builds. Compare what happened against the lesson's one thing: The chapter teaches ways of increasing positive social interaction at school and among the public, and lists the marks of a good relationship or friendship: a shared interest or idea in particular; readiness to give and to receive — sharing thoughts, feelings, experiences and materials; and, from the activity answers, the acceptable traits to name include sociability, openness, keeping respect, trust, caring for others, keeping confidences and team spirit.

## Check it for one day
For one day, watch for this lesson's teaching in real life around you — in your family, your classroom, your street. Each time you see it (or see it missing), note what you saw and what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
