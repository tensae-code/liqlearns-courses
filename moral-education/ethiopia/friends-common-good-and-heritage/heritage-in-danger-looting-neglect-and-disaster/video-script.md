# Video script — Heritage in danger — looting, neglect and disaster

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The lesson is text-first: this script says nothing the lesson text does not already teach.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The threats are listed plainly: foreign invasion and internal religious, ethnic and political conflicts have plundered and destroyed heritage — Ethiopian treasures including crowns, books and gold crosses were looted by British forces, the book's own example; theft and illegal trafficking; lack of awareness, which leaves heritage uncared for; illegal settlement, hunting and mining around heritage sites; and natural disasters — flood, earthquake and heavy rain.
