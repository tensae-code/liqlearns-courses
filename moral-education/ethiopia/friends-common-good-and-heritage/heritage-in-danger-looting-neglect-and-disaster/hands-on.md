# Hands-on — Heritage in danger — looting, neglect and disaster

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Take this lesson's teaching into one real situation today — at home, at school or in your neighbourhood: do what the lesson teaches, then say in one sentence what you did and what it changed.

## Show it with real things
Carry the lesson's teaching into one real moment today — at home, at school or among your neighbours — and do the act the lesson teaches, naming each step aloud as you do it: what you are doing, why, and what the lesson says it builds. Compare what happened against the lesson's one thing: The threats are listed plainly: foreign invasion and internal religious, ethnic and political conflicts have plundered and destroyed heritage — Ethiopian treasures including crowns, books and gold crosses were looted by British forces, the book's own example; theft and illegal trafficking; lack of awareness, which leaves heritage uncared for; illegal settlement, hunting and mining around heritage sites; and natural disasters — flood, earthquake and heavy rain.

## Check it for one day
For one day, watch for this lesson's teaching in real life around you — in your family, your classroom, your street. Each time you see it (or see it missing), note what you saw and what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
