# The common good — what belongs to all of us — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** This statement is the lesson's one thing: The common good is defined as the materials, benefits, institutions, services and culture that are a benefit or wealth for all human beings — individuals, groups, the community, the country's people and the world.
**Answer: True** — it states the lesson's teaching in full.

**2.** A learner who lands on this lesson directly must finish every earlier level before starting it.
**Answer: False** — earlier levels are recommended first, never a forced gate; every lesson stands alone.
