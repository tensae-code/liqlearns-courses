## What the common good is

The common good is defined as the materials, benefits, institutions, services and culture that are a benefit or wealth for all human beings — individuals, groups, the community, the country's people and the world. It serves everyone without difference of sex, age, religion, culture or race; people may hold different identities in their social, economic, political and cultural lives, the reading says, yet all are human beings, so the value of humankind as a whole is the common good of all its members, and the growth of the members is the growth of all.

## The common good

In short, common goods are the things we all use together and share equally.
