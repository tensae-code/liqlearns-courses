# Video script — The common good — what belongs to all of us

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The lesson is text-first: this script says nothing the lesson text does not already teach.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The common good is defined as the materials, benefits, institutions, services and culture that are a benefit or wealth for all human beings — individuals, groups, the community, the country's people and the world.

The common good is defined as the materials, benefits, institutions, services and culture that are a benefit or wealth for all human beings — individuals, groups, the community, the country's people and the world. It serves everyone without difference of sex, age, religion, culture or race; people may hold different identities in their social, economic, political and cultural lives, the reading says, yet all are human beings, so the value of humankind as a whole is the common good of all its members, and the growth of the members is the growth of all.

In short, common goods are the things we all use together and share equally.

Remember it this way: The common good is defined as the materials, benefits, institutions, services and culture that are a benefit or wealth for all human beings — individuals, groups, the community, the country's people and the world.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
