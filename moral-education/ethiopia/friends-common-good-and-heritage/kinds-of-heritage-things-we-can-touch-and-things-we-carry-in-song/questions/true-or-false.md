# Kinds of heritage — things we can touch and things we carry in song — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** This statement is the lesson's one thing: Heritage divides by its outer form and purpose into tangible and intangible. Tangible heritage is what can be seen and touched; it divides again into movable heritage — coins, writings, clothes and means of transport, which can be carried from place to place — and immovable heritage, whose base is tied to the land: monuments, castles, graves, mosques, churches, palaces and bridges.
**Answer: True** — it states the lesson's teaching in full.

**2.** A learner who lands on this lesson directly must finish every earlier level before starting it.
**Answer: False** — earlier levels are recommended first, never a forced gate; every lesson stands alone.

Difficulty: 1 = direct recall; 2 = applying the course's direct-entry rule.
