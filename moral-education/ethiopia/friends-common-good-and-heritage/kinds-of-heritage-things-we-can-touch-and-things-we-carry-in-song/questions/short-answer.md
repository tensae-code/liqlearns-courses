# Kinds of heritage — things we can touch and things we carry in song — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** State this lesson's one thing in your own words.
**Expected:** Heritage divides by its outer form and purpose into tangible and intangible. Tangible heritage is what can be seen and touched; it divides again into movable heritage — coins, writings, clothes and means of transport, which can be carried from place to place — and immovable heritage, whose base is tied to the land: monuments, castles, graves, mosques, churches, palaces and bridges.
