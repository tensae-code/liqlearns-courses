# Kinds of heritage — things we can touch and things we carry in song — depth

This lesson's parts are distinct and sequential, so they stand as its sub lessons (2026-11-09 ruling: sub lessons only where a lesson genuinely has distinct sequential parts). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: Heritage divides by its outer form and purpose into tangible and intangible. Tangible heritage is what can be seen and touched; it divides again into movable heritage — coins, writings, clothes and means of transport, which can be carried from place to place — and immovable heritage, whose base is tied to the land: monuments, castles, graves, mosques, churches, palaces and bridges. — shown in the learner's own words and in the lesson's closing act done in a real situation.
