# Cultural heritage — what our grandparents hand us — depth

This lesson's parts are distinct and sequential, so they stand as its sub lessons (2026-11-09 ruling: sub lessons only where a lesson genuinely has distinct sequential parts). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: Two base ideas: culture — the complex whole holding knowledge, belief, art, morality, custom and the other things a society wants; not one person's, but a people's shared way of living together and their way of relating to the natural environment — and heritage, the part of culture one generation receives and hands to the future as wealth. — shown in the learner's own words and in the lesson's closing act done in a real situation.
