## Culture and heritage

Two base ideas: culture — the complex whole holding knowledge, belief, art, morality, custom and the other things a society wants; not one person's, but a people's shared way of living together and their way of relating to the natural environment — and heritage, the part of culture one generation receives and hands to the future as wealth. Culture has four basic characteristics in the reading: it is symbolic (expressed in language, art and image), it is shared by nature, it is learned and passed from generation to generation, and it can be adapted as people find fitting.
