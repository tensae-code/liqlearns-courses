# Video script — Cultural heritage — what our grandparents hand us

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The lesson is text-first: this script says nothing the lesson text does not already teach.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Two base ideas: culture — the complex whole holding knowledge, belief, art, morality, custom and the other things a society wants; not one person's, but a people's shared way of living together and their way of relating to the natural environment — and heritage, the part of culture one generation receives and hands to the future as wealth.

Culture has four basic characteristics in the reading: it is symbolic (expressed in language, art and image), it is shared by nature, it is learned and passed from generation to generation, and it can be adapted as people find fitting.
