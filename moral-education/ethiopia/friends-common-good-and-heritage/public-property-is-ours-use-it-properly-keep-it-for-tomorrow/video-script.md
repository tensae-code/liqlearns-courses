# Video script — Public property is ours — use it properly, keep it for tomorrow

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The lesson is text-first: this script says nothing the lesson text does not already teach.

This lesson is about one thing.
Under the main parts of the common good, the guide treats the proper use of public property (5.5.2): public wealth is the natural and man-made materials, services and other things that contribute to human benefit.

Under the main parts of the common good, the guide treats the proper use of public property (5.5.2): public wealth is the natural and man-made materials, services and other things that contribute to human benefit. Using it properly means handling and guarding it carefully, responsibly, lawfully, scientifically and morally; proper use saves it from destruction, theft, shortage and unfair distribution, creates an equal chance for the community to use it, and keeps it for the coming generation.

What is expected of the public: use resources responsibly and economically and build a saving culture; know what correct use is; prevent acts that destroy resources; be willing to share, maintain and protect what is for common use; and fight selfishness, waste, unnecessary pollution and over-use.

Remember it this way: Under the main parts of the common good, the guide treats the proper use of public property (5.5.2): public wealth is the natural and man-made materials, services and other things that contribute to human benefit.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
