# Hands-on — Tolerance — what it is and what it is made of

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Take this lesson's teaching into one real situation today — at home, at school or in your neighbourhood: do what the lesson teaches, then say in one sentence what you did and what it changed.

## Show it with real things
Carry the lesson's teaching into one real moment today — at home, at school or among your neighbours — and do the act the lesson teaches, naming each step aloud as you do it: what you are doing, why, and what the lesson says it builds. Compare what happened against the lesson's one thing: What a culture of tolerance is, and what it is made of: equality practised across the fields the guide names — language, politics, law, religion and social life — so tolerance is shown as equal treatment, not mere patience.

## Check it for one day
For one day, watch for this lesson's teaching in real life around you — in your family, your classroom, your street. Each time you see it (or see it missing), note what you saw and what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
