# Video script — Serving the community the right way — rights, confidentiality, consent

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The lesson is text-first: this script says nothing the lesson text does not already teach.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
How to serve the community ethically: the principles named are basic rights, confidentiality and consent — you may not help people in ways that trample their rights, repeat what they told you in trust, or act without their agreement — practised through examples the guide gives: donating clothes, helping neighbours, planting trees, cleaning the classroom after lessons.
