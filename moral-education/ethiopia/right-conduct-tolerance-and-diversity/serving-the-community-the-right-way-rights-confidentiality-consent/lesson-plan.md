# Lesson plan — Serving the community the right way — rights, confidentiality, consent

**Course:** Moral Education — Ethiopia · **Level folder:** Level 8 — Right Conduct, Tolerance and Living With Diversity · **Lesson:** Serving the community the right way — rights, confidentiality, consent
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 8 — Right Conduct, Tolerance and Living With Diversity (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** How to serve the community ethically: the principles named are basic rights, confidentiality and consent — you may not help people in ways that trample their rights, repeat what they told you in trust, or act without their agreement — practised through examples the guide gives: donating clothes, helping neighbours, planting trees, cleaning the classroom after lessons.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 8 — Right Conduct, Tolerance and Living With Diversity (recommended, never forced).
**Strand:** manners/morals / constitution/civics — the strand the Grade 4 teacher guide itself gives this teaching (manners/morals, or constitution/civics where the guide teaches rights, law or the state inside Moral Education).
