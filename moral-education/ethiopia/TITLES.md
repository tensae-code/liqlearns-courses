# Moral Education — Ethiopia course — titles, FROM THE BOTTOM

Moral Education belongs to the country: this course is
Ethiopia's own — built from the Ministry of Education's
Moral Education **teacher guides** for Grades 1–6 alone. No
student textbook for this subject exists in any record we
hold, and no title below pretends otherwise: every lesson
stands on teacher-guide evidence, marked as such in its
sources. Inside the course the organisation is by **levels,
not grades**: the titles below sit inside their levels, in
path order — what the learner proves, step by step. The
grade each book comes from lives only in each lesson's
sources.

Titles are the common-sense name of the thing taught, never
the books' chapter or unit names: the guides' chapters and
units (Self-Control; Justice; Multiculturalism; Law-
Abidingness; and the rest) survive as the level names and
the path's shape, not as lesson titles.
