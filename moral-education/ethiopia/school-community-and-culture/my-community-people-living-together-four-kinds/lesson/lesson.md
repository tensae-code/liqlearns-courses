## What a community is

The guide defines a community as a group of people living together in one place, and names its kinds: rural, pastoralist, urban and religious communities. The rural community lives in small villages and is mostly farmers; the pastoralist community makes its life from herding livestock; the urban community lives in towns and cities; people are also organised by their religion.
