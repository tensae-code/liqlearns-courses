# Video script — My community — people living together, four kinds

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The lesson is text-first: this script says nothing the lesson text does not already teach.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The guide defines a community as a group of people living together in one place, and names its kinds: rural, pastoralist, urban and religious communities.

The guide defines a community as a group of people living together in one place, and names its kinds: rural, pastoralist, urban and religious communities. The rural community lives in small villages and is mostly farmers; the pastoralist community makes its life from herding livestock; the urban community lives in towns and cities; people are also organised by their religion.

Pupils discuss the concept in pairs and identify the kinds in groups.

Remember it this way: The guide defines a community as a group of people living together in one place, and names its kinds: rural, pastoralist, urban and religious communities.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
