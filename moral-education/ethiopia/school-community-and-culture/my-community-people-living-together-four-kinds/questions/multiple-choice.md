# My community — people living together, four kinds — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) The guide defines a community as a group of people living together in one place, and names its kinds: rural, pastoralist, urban and religious communities.  b) A topic from a different level of this course  c) The syllabus of the teacher guide  d) none of these
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) What a community is  b) a topic from another course  c) today's weather  d) none of these
**Answer: a)** — That is this lesson's first part.

Difficulty: 1 = recall of the lesson's one thing; 2 = telling this lesson's parts from other lessons' parts.
