# Video script — My rights and my responsibilities at school

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The lesson is text-first: this script says nothing the lesson text does not already teach.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Mixed placement: the manners part is the responsibility side — using school property properly, being considerate to others and showing good conduct; the civics part is the rights side — pupils have the right to learn and to get correct information from their teachers.
