# Hands-on — My rights and my responsibilities at school

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Mixed placement: the manners part is the responsibility side — using school property properly, being considerate to others and showing good conduct; the civics part is the rights side — you have the right to learn and to get correct information from your teachers.

## Show it with real things
Carry the lesson's teaching into one real moment today — at home, at school or among your neighbours — and do the act the lesson teaches, naming each step aloud as you do it: what you are doing, why, and what the lesson says it builds. Compare what happened against the lesson's one thing: Mixed placement: the manners part is the responsibility side — using school property properly, being considerate to others and showing good conduct; the civics part is the rights side — pupils have the right to learn and to get correct information from their teachers.

## Check it for one day
For one day, watch for this lesson's teaching in real life around you — in your family, your classroom, your street. Each time you see it (or see it missing), note what you saw and what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
