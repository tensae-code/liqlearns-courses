# My rights and my responsibilities at school — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** State this lesson's one thing in your own words.
**Expected:** Mixed placement: the manners part is the responsibility side — using school property properly, being considerate to others and showing good conduct; the civics part is the rights side — pupils have the right to learn and to get correct information from their teachers.

**2.** Explain the lesson's first part: Rights and responsibilities at school.
**Expected:** an answer drawn from the lesson text for that part, in the learner's own words.

Difficulty: 2 = restating the teaching accurately without copying it.
