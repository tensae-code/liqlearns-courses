# My rights and my responsibilities at school — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** This statement is the lesson's one thing: Mixed placement: the manners part is the responsibility side — using school property properly, being considerate to others and showing good conduct; the civics part is the rights side — pupils have the right to learn and to get correct information from their teachers.
**Answer: True** — it states the lesson's teaching in full.

**2.** A learner who lands on this lesson directly must finish every earlier level before starting it.
**Answer: False** — earlier levels are recommended first, never a forced gate; every lesson stands alone.
