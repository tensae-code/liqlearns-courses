# My rights and my responsibilities at school — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) Mixed placement: the manners part is the responsibility side — using school property properly, being considerate to others and showing good conduct; the civics part is the rights side — pupils have the right to learn and to get correct information from their teachers.  b) A topic from a different level of this course  c) The syllabus of the teacher guide  d) none of these
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) Rights and responsibilities at school  b) a topic from another course  c) today's weather  d) none of these
**Answer: a)** — That is this lesson's first part.

Difficulty: 1 = recall of the lesson's one thing; 2 = telling this lesson's parts from other lessons' parts.
