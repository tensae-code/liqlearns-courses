# Video script — Culture — a people's way of life

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The lesson is text-first: this script says nothing the lesson text does not already teach.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Culture is defined as a group's way of life, and it can be expressed in many ways — music, belief, dress, foods and other practices; it is passed from generation to generation in writing and in oral narratives.

Culture is defined as a group's way of life, and it can be expressed in many ways — music, belief, dress, foods and other practices; it is passed from generation to generation in writing and in oral narratives.

One activity shows an elder telling a story to a group of children as one way culture is handed on; another answers that culture is the way of life practised by a group of people.

Remember it this way: Culture is defined as a group's way of life, and it can be expressed in many ways — music, belief, dress, foods and other practices; it is passed from generation to generation in writing and in oral narratives.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
