# Local culture — what we share where we live — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** This statement is the lesson's one thing: Local culture is anything people share in their daily lives in the place where they live — their shared experiences and customs. The chapter's worked example is the Hamer community: two pictures show Hamer local culture in dress, hairstyle and ornaments, and pupils discuss what the people in the pictures share.
**Answer: True** — it states the lesson's teaching in full.

**2.** A learner who lands on this lesson directly must finish every earlier level before starting it.
**Answer: False** — earlier levels are recommended first, never a forced gate; every lesson stands alone.

Difficulty: 1 = direct recall; 2 = applying the course's direct-entry rule.
