# Local culture — what we share where we live — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** State this lesson's one thing in your own words.
**Expected:** Local culture is anything people share in their daily lives in the place where they live — their shared experiences and customs. The chapter's worked example is the Hamer community: two pictures show Hamer local culture in dress, hairstyle and ornaments, and pupils discuss what the people in the pictures share.
