# Local culture — what we share where we live — depth

This lesson's parts are distinct and sequential, so they stand as its sub lessons (2026-11-09 ruling: sub lessons only where a lesson genuinely has distinct sequential parts). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: Local culture is anything people share in their daily lives in the place where they live — their shared experiences and customs. The chapter's worked example is the Hamer community: two pictures show Hamer local culture in dress, hairstyle and ornaments, and pupils discuss what the people in the pictures share. — shown in the learner's own words and in the lesson's closing act done in a real situation.
