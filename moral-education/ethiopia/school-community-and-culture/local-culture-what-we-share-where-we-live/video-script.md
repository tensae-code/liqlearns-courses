# Video script — Local culture — what we share where we live

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The lesson is text-first: this script says nothing the lesson text does not already teach.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Local culture is anything people share in their daily lives in the place where they live — their shared experiences and customs. The chapter's worked example is the Hamer community: two pictures show Hamer local culture in dress, hairstyle and ornaments, and pupils discuss what the people in the pictures share.
