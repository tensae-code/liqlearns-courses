# Lesson plan — Local culture — what we share where we live

**Course:** Moral Education — Ethiopia · **Level folder:** Level 2 — School, Community Values and Local Culture · **Lesson:** Local culture — what we share where we live
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 2 — School, Community Values and Local Culture (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Local culture is anything people share in their daily lives in the place where they live — their shared experiences and customs. The chapter's worked example is the Hamer community: two pictures show Hamer local culture in dress, hairstyle and ornaments, and pupils discuss what the people in the pictures share.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 2 — School, Community Values and Local Culture (recommended, never forced).
**Strand:** manners/morals — the strand the Grade 1 teacher guide itself gives this teaching (manners/morals, or constitution/civics where the guide teaches rights, law or the state inside Moral Education).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video.
