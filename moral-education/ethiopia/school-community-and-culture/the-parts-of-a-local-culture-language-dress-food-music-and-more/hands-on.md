# Hands-on — The parts of a local culture — language, dress, food, music and more

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
You and a companion identify the main elements in your own locality in pairs or groups.

## Show it with real things
Carry the lesson's teaching into one real moment today — at home, at school or among your neighbours — and do the act the lesson teaches, naming each step aloud as you do it: what you are doing, why, and what the lesson says it builds. Compare what happened against the lesson's one thing: The notes name language, values and material things among the main elements; the activity answers widen the list to language, music, art and the like, and the chapter's closing list of the main local cultures runs: language, signs/symbols, customs, values, dress, music, arts, beliefs, food and religion.

## Check it for one day
For one day, watch for this lesson's teaching in real life around you — in your family, your classroom, your street. Each time you see it (or see it missing), note what you saw and what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
