# The parts of a local culture — language, dress, food, music and more

## Do it first

Before you read, try the lesson's act once with what you already know: You and a companion identify the main elements in your own locality in pairs or groups. Keep what you did in mind — you will compare it with your work at the end.

## ℹ Info

The notes name language, values and material things among the main elements; the activity answers widen the list to language, music, art and the like, and the chapter's closing list of the main local cultures runs: language, signs/symbols, customs, values, dress, music, arts, beliefs, food and religion.
