# Video script — The parts of a local culture — language, dress, food, music and more

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The lesson is text-first: this script says nothing the lesson text does not already teach.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The notes name language, values and material things among the main elements; the activity answers widen the list to language, music, art and the like, and the chapter's closing list of the main local cultures runs: language, signs/symbols, customs, values, dress, music, arts, beliefs, food and religion.
