# Hands-on — Cultural values — the principles a community stands on

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
You and a companion are to value your own cultural values and show them.

## Show it with real things
Carry the lesson's teaching into one real moment today — at home, at school or among your neighbours — and do the act the lesson teaches, naming each step aloud as you do it: what you are doing, why, and what the lesson says it builds. Compare what happened against the lesson's one thing: The chapter closes on cultural values as the basic principles and ideas on which a whole community is founded, distinguished in the activity answers from customs (traditions and ceremonies) and from culture itself, which the answer calls the community's guide, while values are beliefs.

## Check it for one day
For one day, watch for this lesson's teaching in real life around you — in your family, your classroom, your street. Each time you see it (or see it missing), note what you saw and what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
