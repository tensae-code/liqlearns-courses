# Hands-on — Nine values our community lives by

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
One activity puts you in nine groups, each taking one core value and matching it to a picture; another works from a sign carrying four of the values. (The chapter's prose list differs slightly — see the verification block, error 8; teach the contents list, corrected.)

## Show it with real things
Carry the lesson's teaching into one real moment today — at home, at school or among your neighbours — and do the act the lesson teaches, naming each step aloud as you do it: what you are doing, why, and what the lesson says it builds. Compare what happened against the lesson's one thing: The contents name the community's core values one by one: cooperation, tolerance, inclusiveness, meaningful participation, mutual respect, hospitality, integrity, accepting others' ideas, and hope.

## Check it for one day
For one day, watch for this lesson's teaching in real life around you — in your family, your classroom, your street. Each time you see it (or see it missing), note what you saw and what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
