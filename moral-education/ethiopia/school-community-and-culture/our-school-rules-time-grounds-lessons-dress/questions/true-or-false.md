# Our school rules — time, grounds, lessons, dress — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** This statement is the lesson's one thing: The note lists what the rules cover: coming to school on time, using the school grounds and the latrines properly, attending lessons and assemblies regularly, and coming in the proper dress or uniform.
**Answer: True** — it states the lesson's teaching in full.
