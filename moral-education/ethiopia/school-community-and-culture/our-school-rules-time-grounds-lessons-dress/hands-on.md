# Hands-on — Our school rules — time, grounds, lessons, dress

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
One activity is a true/false check on the rules (answers printed: true, false, true), and you and a companion must be able to say why they chose true or false.

## Show it with real things
Carry the lesson's teaching into one real moment today — at home, at school or among your neighbours — and do the act the lesson teaches, naming each step aloud as you do it: what you are doing, why, and what the lesson says it builds. Compare what happened against the lesson's one thing: The note lists what the rules cover: coming to school on time, using the school grounds and the latrines properly, attending lessons and assemblies regularly, and coming in the proper dress or uniform.

## Check it for one day
For one day, watch for this lesson's teaching in real life around you — in your family, your classroom, your street. Each time you see it (or see it missing), note what you saw and what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
