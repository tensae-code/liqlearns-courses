# Hands-on — Why shared values help a community

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
You and a companion write the importance, and one activity has groups identify the uses of the values; the chapter closes with fill-in answers (community; urban community; integrity; community values; the importance of community values) and a true/false set (false, true, true, true, true).

## Show it with real things
Carry the lesson's teaching into one real moment today — at home, at school or among your neighbours — and do the act the lesson teaches, naming each step aloud as you do it: what you are doing, why, and what the lesson says it builds. Compare what happened against the lesson's one thing: The guide's reason, in its own framing: community values are useful because they make us steer our behaviour properly, appreciate diversity, cooperate with each other and respect the other members of the community.

## Check it for one day
For one day, watch for this lesson's teaching in real life around you — in your family, your classroom, your street. Each time you see it (or see it missing), note what you saw and what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
