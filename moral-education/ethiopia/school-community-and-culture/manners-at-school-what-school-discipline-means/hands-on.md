# Hands-on — Manners at school — what school discipline means

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
You and a companion discuss the school's code of conduct in groups.

## Show it with real things
Carry the lesson's teaching into one real moment today — at home, at school or among your neighbours — and do the act the lesson teaches, naming each step aloud as you do it: what you are doing, why, and what the lesson says it builds. Compare what happened against the lesson's one thing: The teacher note frames school discipline as strategic guidance given to the pupil to bring a distinct standard of conduct, passing on knowledge and skill so as to prevent behaviour problems in children at school.

## Check it for one day
For one day, watch for this lesson's teaching in real life around you — in your family, your classroom, your street. Each time you see it (or see it missing), note what you saw and what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
