# Video script — Marks of humanity — volunteering, politeness, patience, peace, respect, fairness

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The lesson is text-first: this script says nothing the lesson text does not already teach.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The rest of the lesson's list, each defined: volunteering — serving others' benefit with your time and skill expecting no payment (pupils exchange experiences such as the Green Legacy planting, cleaning the surroundings and giving blood); politeness/gentleness (ጨዋነት) — showing respect to others, caring for a person's identity, wishes and belief without degrading them, of which humility and good behaviour are marks, and serving others with humility; brotherhood and sisterhood — groups of men, and of women, formed on shared goals and interests, including people who work or learn in the same place; patience — bearing things calmly without complaint; peace — in general social friendship and unity, the absence of suffering and of rights violations, commonly the absence of conflict or war, with inner peace (peace of mind) distinguished from outer peace between people, groups and at national level — one activity has pupils show the COVID-19-era greeting and separate peace from greeting; respect — the ability to honour and value another person, in word or deed; and fairness — reasonable and just treatment of people and applying accepted laws and principles, under which all people must be seen equally.
