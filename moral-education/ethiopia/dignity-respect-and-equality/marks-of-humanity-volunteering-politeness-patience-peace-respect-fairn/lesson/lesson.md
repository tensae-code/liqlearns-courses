# Marks of humanity — volunteering, politeness, patience, peace, respect, fairness

## Do it first

Before you read, try the lesson's act once with what you already know: The rest of the lesson's list, each defined: volunteering — serving others' benefit with your time and skill expecting no payment (you exchange experiences such as the Green Legacy planting, cleaning the surroundings and giving blood); politeness/gentleness (ጨዋነት) — showing respect to others, caring for a person's identity, wishes and belief without degrading them, of which humility and good behaviour are marks, and serving others with humility; brotherhood and sisterhood — groups of men, and of women, formed on shared goals and interests, including people who work or learn in the same place; patience — bearing things calmly without complaint; peace — in general social friendship and unity, the absence of suffering and of rights violations, commonly the absence of conflict or war, with inner peace (peace of mind) distinguished from outer peace between people, groups and at national level — one activity has you show the COVID-19-era greeting and separate peace from greeting; respect — the ability to honour and value another person, in word or deed; and fairness — reasonable and just treatment of people and applying accepted laws and principles, under which all people must be seen equally. Keep what you did in mind — you will compare it with your work at the end.

## Volunteering, politeness, brotherhood, patience, peace, respect, fairness

Not practising these marks is itself taught as harmful.

> Tip — from us, not from the books: Act it, don't just say it. A value you have practised once in a real moment — at home, at school, in your street — is yours in a way a value you have only read about is not.
