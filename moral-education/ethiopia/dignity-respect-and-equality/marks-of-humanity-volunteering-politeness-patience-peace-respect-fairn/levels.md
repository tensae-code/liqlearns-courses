# Marks of humanity — volunteering, politeness, patience, peace, respect, fairness — depth

This lesson's parts are distinct and sequential, so they stand as its sub lessons (2026-11-09 ruling: sub lessons only where a lesson genuinely has distinct sequential parts). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: The rest of the lesson's list, each defined: volunteering — serving others' benefit with your time and skill expecting no payment (pupils exchange experiences such as the Green Legacy planting, cleaning the surroundings and giving blood); politeness/gentleness (ጨዋነት) — showing respect to others, caring for a person's identity, wishes and belief without degrading them, of which humility and good behaviour are marks, and serving others with humility; brotherhood and sisterhood — groups of men, and of women, formed on shared goals and interests, including people who work or learn in the same place; patience — bearing things calmly without complaint; peace — in general social friendship and unity, the absence of suffering and of rights violations, commonly the absence of conflict or war, with inner peace (peace of mind) distinguished from outer peace between people, groups and at national level — one activity has pupils show the COVID-19-era greeting and separate peace from greeting; respect — the ability to honour and value another person, in word or deed; and fairness — reasonable and just treatment of people and applying accepted laws and principles, under which all people must be seen equally. — shown in the learner's own words and in the lesson's closing act done in a real situation.
