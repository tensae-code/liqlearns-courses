# Video script — Disrespect — what it is and what it breaks

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The lesson is text-first: this script says nothing the lesson text does not already teach.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Disrespect is defined as the opposite of respect: giving no regard to another's feelings, wishes, freedom, dignity, humanity or social standing, showing no appreciation or humility; it can show itself in insulting behaviour — rude, unmannerly and disgusting acts.

Disrespect is defined as the opposite of respect: giving no regard to another's feelings, wishes, freedom, dignity, humanity or social standing, showing no appreciation or humility; it can show itself in insulting behaviour — rude, unmannerly and disgusting acts. The chapter's answers add its fruits: it weakens friendship, cooperation, mutual respect and humility, and instead supports suffering and unhealthy relationships in society.

The closing answers collect the chapter: the importance of respecting others includes strengthening unity and peaceful relations, making you feel happiness and safety, and helping you be served by others and respected in return.

Remember it this way: Disrespect is defined as the opposite of respect: giving no regard to another's feelings, wishes, freedom, dignity, humanity or social standing, showing no appreciation or humility; it can show itself in insulting behaviour — rude, unmannerly and disgusting acts.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
