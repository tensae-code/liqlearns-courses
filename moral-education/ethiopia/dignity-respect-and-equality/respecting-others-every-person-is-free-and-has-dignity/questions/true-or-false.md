# Respecting others — every person is free and has dignity — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** This statement is the lesson's one thing: Respecting others begins, the brief explanation says, from recognising that the other person is self-standing, special and free; respecting a person confirms the person's human dignity — it is giving value to people's human dignity — and it benefits both the one who respects and the one respected.
**Answer: True** — it states the lesson's teaching in full.

**2.** A learner who lands on this lesson directly must finish every earlier level before starting it.
**Answer: False** — earlier levels are recommended first, never a forced gate; every lesson stands alone.

Difficulty: 1 = direct recall; 2 = applying the course's direct-entry rule.
