# Hands-on — Respecting others — every person is free and has dignity

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
You and a companion show in role-play how they respect each other, and are encouraged to hold a positive attitude to others.

## Show it with real things
Carry the lesson's teaching into one real moment today — at home, at school or among your neighbours — and do the act the lesson teaches, naming each step aloud as you do it: what you are doing, why, and what the lesson says it builds. Compare what happened against the lesson's one thing: Respecting others begins, the brief explanation says, from recognising that the other person is self-standing, special and free; respecting a person confirms the person's human dignity — it is giving value to people's human dignity — and it benefits both the one who respects and the one respected.

## Check it for one day
For one day, watch for this lesson's teaching in real life around you — in your family, your classroom, your street. Each time you see it (or see it missing), note what you saw and what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
