## Respecting others

Respecting others begins, the brief explanation says, from recognising that the other person is self-standing, special and free; respecting a person confirms the person's human dignity — it is giving value to people's human dignity — and it benefits both the one who respects and the one respected. "Others" is itself defined for pupils: anyone besides the speaker — friends, brothers and sisters, mother and father, neighbours and so on.

## The concept and why it matters

Pupils show in role-play how they respect each other, and are encouraged to hold a positive attitude to others.

> Tip — from us, not from the books: Act it, don't just say it. A value you have practised once in a real moment — at home, at school, in your street — is yours in a way a value you have only read about is not.
