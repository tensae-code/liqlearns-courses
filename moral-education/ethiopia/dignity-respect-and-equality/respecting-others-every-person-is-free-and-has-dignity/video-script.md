# Video script — Respecting others — every person is free and has dignity

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The lesson is text-first: this script says nothing the lesson text does not already teach.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Respecting others begins, the brief explanation says, from recognising that the other person is self-standing, special and free; respecting a person confirms the person's human dignity — it is giving value to people's human dignity — and it benefits both the one who respects and the one respected.

"Others" is itself defined for pupils: anyone besides the speaker — friends, brothers and sisters, mother and father, neighbours and so on.

Pupils show in role-play how they respect each other, and are encouraged to hold a positive attitude to others.

Remember it this way: Respecting others begins, the brief explanation says, from recognising that the other person is self-standing, special and free; respecting a person confirms the person's human dignity — it is giving value to people's human dignity — and it benefits both the one who respects and the one respected.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
