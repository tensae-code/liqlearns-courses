# Video script — When treatment is unequal — discrimination and its damage

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The lesson is text-first: this script says nothing the lesson text does not already teach.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Mixed placement, civics dominant. Discrimination is reduced, the answers say, by applying the principles of equal treatment, by living together in peace and agreement, by giving every individual and group equal rights, and by listing the places where partiality is mostly practised — home, play places, the school compound, the workplace; where there is no equality, partiality is practised everywhere.
