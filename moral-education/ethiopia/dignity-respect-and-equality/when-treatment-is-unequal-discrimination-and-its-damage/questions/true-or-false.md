# When treatment is unequal — discrimination and its damage — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** This statement is the lesson's one thing: Mixed placement, civics dominant. Discrimination is reduced, the answers say, by applying the principles of equal treatment, by living together in peace and agreement, by giving every individual and group equal rights, and by listing the places where partiality is mostly practised — home, play places, the school compound, the workplace; where there is no equality, partiality is practised everywhere.
**Answer: True** — it states the lesson's teaching in full.

**2.** A learner who lands on this lesson directly must finish every earlier level before starting it.
**Answer: False** — earlier levels are recommended first, never a forced gate; every lesson stands alone.
