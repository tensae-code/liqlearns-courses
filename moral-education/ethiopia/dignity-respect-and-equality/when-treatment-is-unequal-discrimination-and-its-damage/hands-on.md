# Hands-on — When treatment is unequal — discrimination and its damage

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Take this lesson's teaching into one real situation today — at home, at school or in your neighbourhood: do what the lesson teaches, then say in one sentence what you did and what it changed.

## Show it with real things
Carry the lesson's teaching into one real moment today — at home, at school or among your neighbours — and do the act the lesson teaches, naming each step aloud as you do it: what you are doing, why, and what the lesson says it builds. Compare what happened against the lesson's one thing: Mixed placement, civics dominant. Discrimination is reduced, the answers say, by applying the principles of equal treatment, by living together in peace and agreement, by giving every individual and group equal rights, and by listing the places where partiality is mostly practised — home, play places, the school compound, the workplace; where there is no equality, partiality is practised everywhere.

## Check it for one day
For one day, watch for this lesson's teaching in real life around you — in your family, your classroom, your street. Each time you see it (or see it missing), note what you saw and what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
