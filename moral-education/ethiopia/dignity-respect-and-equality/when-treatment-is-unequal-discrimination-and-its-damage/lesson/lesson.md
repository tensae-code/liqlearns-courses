## Reducing discrimination

The chapter's closing short answers give the sources of gender inequality (society's attitude, and the existence of discriminatory laws) and the common results of inequality: violation of human rights, conflict, injustice, instability, unrest and misunderstandings between individuals and groups; the benefits of gender equality named are narrowing the education gap and the political-participation and work gaps between men and women, and supporting women and girls to use their rights.
