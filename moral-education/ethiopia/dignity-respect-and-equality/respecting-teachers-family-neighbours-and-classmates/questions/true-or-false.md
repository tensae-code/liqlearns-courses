# Respecting teachers, family, neighbours and classmates — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** This statement is the lesson's one thing: The remaining named respects, each with its own small-group activity (2.8–2.11): respecting teachers — pupils express appreciation for teachers they see as good examples; respecting family — with exchange of experiences and the importance stated; respecting neighbours — for example showing respect in greeting, with class discussion of its importance and of building peaceful mutual interaction; and respecting classmates/friends — pupils show in practice, inside and outside the classroom, how much they respect each other, with the message that mutual respect shrinks disagreements between them.
**Answer: True** — it states the lesson's teaching in full.

**2.** A learner who lands on this lesson directly must finish every earlier level before starting it.
**Answer: False** — earlier levels are recommended first, never a forced gate; every lesson stands alone.

Difficulty: 1 = direct recall; 2 = applying the course's direct-entry rule.
