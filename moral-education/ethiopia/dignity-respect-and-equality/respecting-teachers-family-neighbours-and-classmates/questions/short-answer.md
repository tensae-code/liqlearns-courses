# Respecting teachers, family, neighbours and classmates — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** State this lesson's one thing in your own words.
**Expected:** The remaining named respects, each with its own small-group activity (2.8–2.11): respecting teachers — pupils express appreciation for teachers they see as good examples; respecting family — with exchange of experiences and the importance stated; respecting neighbours — for example showing respect in greeting, with class discussion of its importance and of building peaceful mutual interaction; and respecting classmates/friends — pupils show in practice, inside and outside the classroom, how much they respect each other, with the message that mutual respect shrinks disagreements between them.

**2.** Explain the lesson's first part: Respecting teachers, family, neighbours and classmates.
**Expected:** an answer drawn from the lesson text for that part, in the learner's own words.

Difficulty: 2 = restating the teaching accurately without copying it.
