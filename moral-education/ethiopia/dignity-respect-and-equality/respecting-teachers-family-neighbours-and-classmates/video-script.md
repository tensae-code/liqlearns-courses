# Video script — Respecting teachers, family, neighbours and classmates

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The lesson is text-first: this script says nothing the lesson text does not already teach.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The remaining named respects, each with its own small-group activity (2.8–2.11): respecting teachers — pupils express appreciation for teachers they see as good examples; respecting family — with exchange of experiences and the importance stated; respecting neighbours — for example showing respect in greeting, with class discussion of its importance and of building peaceful mutual interaction; and respecting classmates/friends — pupils show in practice, inside and outside the classroom, how much they respect each other, with the message that mutual respect shrinks disagreements between them.
