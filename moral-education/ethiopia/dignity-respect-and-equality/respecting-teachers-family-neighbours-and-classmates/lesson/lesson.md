# Respecting teachers, family, neighbours and classmates

## Do it first

Before you read, try the lesson's act once with what you already know: The remaining named respects, each with its own small-group activity (2.8–2.11): respecting teachers — you express appreciation for teachers they see as good examples; respecting family — with exchange of experiences and the importance stated; respecting neighbours — for example showing respect in greeting, with class discussion of its importance and of building peaceful mutual interaction; and respecting classmates/friends — you show in practice, inside and outside the classroom, how much they respect each other, with the message that mutual respect shrinks disagreements between them. Keep what you did in mind — you will compare it with your work at the end.
