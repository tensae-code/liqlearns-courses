## What equality is

Mixed placement, civics dominant: the guide's own definition is rights language — equality is having equal rights, dignity and freedom with others; using rights and benefits equally; carrying responsibilities equally; treating people equally without making race, religion or language a basis; and giving special honour to individuals and groups alike. The manners part is that pupils must show equality in acts, not words.

## Equal rights, dignity and freedom

The lesson opens by linking equality to "moral integrity" (ግብረ-ገባዊ ምሉዕነት), the phrase the guide uses to start the discussion.

> Tip — from us, not from the books: Act it, don't just say it. A value you have practised once in a real moment — at home, at school, in your street — is yours in a way a value you have only read about is not.
