# Equality — equal rights, equal dignity, equal freedom — depth

This lesson's parts are distinct and sequential, so they stand as its sub lessons (2026-11-09 ruling: sub lessons only where a lesson genuinely has distinct sequential parts). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: Mixed placement, civics dominant: the guide's own definition is rights language — equality is having equal rights, dignity and freedom with others; using rights and benefits equally; carrying responsibilities equally; treating people equally without making race, religion or language a basis; and giving special honour to individuals and groups alike. — shown in the learner's own words and in the lesson's closing act done in a real situation.
