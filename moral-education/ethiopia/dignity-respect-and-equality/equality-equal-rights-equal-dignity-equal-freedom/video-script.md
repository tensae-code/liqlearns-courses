# Video script — Equality — equal rights, equal dignity, equal freedom

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The lesson is text-first: this script says nothing the lesson text does not already teach.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Mixed placement, civics dominant: the guide's own definition is rights language — equality is having equal rights, dignity and freedom with others; using rights and benefits equally; carrying responsibilities equally; treating people equally without making race, religion or language a basis; and giving special honour to individuals and groups alike.

The manners part is that pupils must show equality in acts, not words.

The lesson opens by linking equality to "moral integrity" (ግብረ-ገባዊ ምሉዕነት), the phrase the guide uses to start the discussion.
