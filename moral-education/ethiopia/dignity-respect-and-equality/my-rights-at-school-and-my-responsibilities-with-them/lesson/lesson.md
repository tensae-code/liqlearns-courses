# My rights at school — and my responsibilities with them

## Do it first

Before you read, try the lesson's act once with what you already know: The matching responsibilities (the manners part) are listed beside them: respecting the school's rules, being an example to other pupils, doing homework, respecting teachers, doing homework and extra tasks on time, and protecting school property from damage and theft. Keep what you did in mind — you will compare it with your work at the end.

## ℹ Info

Mixed placement, civics dominant. The activity answers list the equality rights children hold at school: the right to ask questions, the right to receive answers, the right to take part in clubs, the right to participate in matters that concern them, the right to handle and use school property properly, and the right to protection from discrimination / not to be excluded.
