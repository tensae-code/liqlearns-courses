# My rights at school — and my responsibilities with them — depth

This lesson's parts are distinct and sequential, so they stand as its sub lessons (2026-11-09 ruling: sub lessons only where a lesson genuinely has distinct sequential parts). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: Mixed placement, civics dominant. The activity answers list the equality rights children hold at school: the right to ask questions, the right to receive answers, the right to take part in clubs, the right to participate in matters that concern them, the right to handle and use school property properly, and the right to protection from discrimination / not to be excluded. — shown in the learner's own words and in the lesson's closing act done in a real situation.
