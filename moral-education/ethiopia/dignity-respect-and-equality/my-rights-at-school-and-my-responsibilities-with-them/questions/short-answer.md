# My rights at school — and my responsibilities with them — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** State this lesson's one thing in your own words.
**Expected:** Mixed placement, civics dominant. The activity answers list the equality rights children hold at school: the right to ask questions, the right to receive answers, the right to take part in clubs, the right to participate in matters that concern them, the right to handle and use school property properly, and the right to protection from discrimination / not to be excluded.

**2.** Explain the lesson's first part: Pupils' rights and responsibilities at school.
**Expected:** an answer drawn from the lesson text for that part, in the learner's own words.

Difficulty: 2 = restating the teaching accurately without copying it.
