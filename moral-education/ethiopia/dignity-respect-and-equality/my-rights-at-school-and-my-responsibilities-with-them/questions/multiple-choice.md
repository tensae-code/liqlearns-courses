# My rights at school — and my responsibilities with them — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) Mixed placement, civics dominant. The activity answers list the equality rights children hold at school: the right to ask questions, the right to receive answers, the right to take part in clubs, the right to participate in matters that concern them, the right to handle and use school property properly, and the right to protection from discrimination / not to be excluded.  b) A topic from a different level of this course  c) The syllabus of the teacher guide  d) none of these
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) Pupils' rights and responsibilities at school  b) a topic from another course  c) today's weather  d) none of these
**Answer: a)** — That is this lesson's first part.

Difficulty: 1 = recall of the lesson's one thing; 2 = telling this lesson's parts from other lessons' parts.
