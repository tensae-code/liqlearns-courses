# Hands-on — My rights at school — and my responsibilities with them

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
The matching responsibilities (the manners part) are listed beside them: respecting the school's rules, being an example to other pupils, doing homework, respecting teachers, doing homework and extra tasks on time, and protecting school property from damage and theft.

## Show it with real things
Carry the lesson's teaching into one real moment today — at home, at school or among your neighbours — and do the act the lesson teaches, naming each step aloud as you do it: what you are doing, why, and what the lesson says it builds. Compare what happened against the lesson's one thing: Mixed placement, civics dominant. The activity answers list the equality rights children hold at school: the right to ask questions, the right to receive answers, the right to take part in clubs, the right to participate in matters that concern them, the right to handle and use school property properly, and the right to protection from discrimination / not to be excluded.

## Check it for one day
For one day, watch for this lesson's teaching in real life around you — in your family, your classroom, your street. Each time you see it (or see it missing), note what you saw and what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
