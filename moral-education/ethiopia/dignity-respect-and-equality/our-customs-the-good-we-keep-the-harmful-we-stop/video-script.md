# Video script — Our customs — the good we keep, the harmful we stop

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The lesson is text-first: this script says nothing the lesson text does not already teach.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Mixed placement inside Lesson 2's spread: the guide sorts cultural practices into good practices that are a basis for the country's development and change — respecting elders, idir and equb (the community's mutual-aid and rotating-savings associations), helping the needy, working together and hospitality — and harmful practices: female circumcision, under-age marriage, cutting the face in mourning, cutting the body with blades, wastefulness and abduction (ጠለፋ).

Teach the harmful list plainly and without pictures of the acts.
