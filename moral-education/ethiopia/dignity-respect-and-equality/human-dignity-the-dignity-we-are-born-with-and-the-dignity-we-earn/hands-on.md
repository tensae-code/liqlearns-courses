# Hands-on — Human dignity — the dignity we are born with and the dignity we earn

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
The guide's example of earned dignity is the Ethiopian woman Abebech Gobena, who won high human dignity for her contribution to her society; you are set homework to name highly respected people in your own locality.

## Show it with real things
Carry the lesson's teaching into one real moment today — at home, at school or among your neighbours — and do the act the lesson teaches, naming each step aloud as you do it: what you are doing, why, and what the lesson says it builds. Compare what happened against the lesson's one thing: Mixed placement. The manners/morals part: conditional dignity — the dignity a person gains or loses by conduct in society; good behaviour wins dignity, bad behaviour loses it, and the guide names selfishness, copying/cheating, fraud and lying as the chief ways people lose it.

## Check it for one day
For one day, watch for this lesson's teaching in real life around you — in your family, your classroom, your street. Each time you see it (or see it missing), note what you saw and what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
