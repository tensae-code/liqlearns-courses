## The inherited kind and the earned kind

The civics part: inherited dignity — the dignity all human beings have simply by being members of the human race, needing no qualification beyond humanity and human capacity. The guide's example of earned dignity is the Ethiopian woman Abebech Gobena, who won high human dignity for her contribution to her society; pupils are set homework to name highly respected people in their own locality.
