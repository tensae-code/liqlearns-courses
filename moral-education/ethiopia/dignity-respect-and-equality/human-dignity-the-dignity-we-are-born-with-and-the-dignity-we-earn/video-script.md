# Video script — Human dignity — the dignity we are born with and the dignity we earn

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The lesson is text-first: this script says nothing the lesson text does not already teach.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Mixed placement. The manners/morals part: conditional dignity — the dignity a person gains or loses by conduct in society; good behaviour wins dignity, bad behaviour loses it, and the guide names selfishness, copying/cheating, fraud and lying as the chief ways people lose it.

The civics part: inherited dignity — the dignity all human beings have simply by being members of the human race, needing no qualification beyond humanity and human capacity. The guide's example of earned dignity is the Ethiopian woman Abebech Gobena, who won high human dignity for her contribution to her society; pupils are set homework to name highly respected people in their own locality.
