# Humanity — the human race, and the mark of being good — depth

This lesson's parts are distinct and sequential, so they stand as its sub lessons (2026-11-09 ruling: sub lessons only where a lesson genuinely has distinct sequential parts). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: The lesson teaches two concepts of humanity. In the first sense it names the human race itself — humankind, distinct from the other animals: human beings are precious and irreplaceable (a thing can be replaced thing-for-thing; a human being cannot), they differ in race, height, sex and language, yet in humanity all human beings are equal, and no one is to be excluded for being different. — shown in the learner's own words and in the lesson's closing act done in a real situation.
