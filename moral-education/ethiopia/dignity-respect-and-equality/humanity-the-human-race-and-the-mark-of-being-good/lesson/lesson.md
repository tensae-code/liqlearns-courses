## Humanity

The lesson teaches two concepts of humanity. In the first sense it names the human race itself — humankind, distinct from the other animals: human beings are precious and irreplaceable (a thing can be replaced thing-for-thing; a human being cannot), they differ in race, height, sex and language, yet in humanity all human beings are equal, and no one is to be excluded for being different.

## The two senses of the word

In the second sense it names the mark of goodness — being human as a quality: helping those who need help without partiality or exclusion, which teaches mutual understanding and friendship and builds cooperation and a peaceful, healthy way of life among pupils and in society. Pupils compare objects in the classroom with the human beings in it, and use the lesson's Figure 1.
