# How a respected person behaves

## Do it first

Before you read, try the lesson's act once with what you already know: You and a companion in groups of five discuss the traits, and show a respected person's character in role-play; teachers are told to name, as samples, good traits such as humility and honesty against bad ones such as rudeness/coarseness and fraud, so you learn to separate good character from bad, and to recognise your own human values (humility, honesty) and share them with classmates. Keep what you did in mind — you will compare it with your work at the end.

## ℹ Info
