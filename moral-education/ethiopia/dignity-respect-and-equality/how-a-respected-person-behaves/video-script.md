# Video script — How a respected person behaves

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The lesson is text-first: this script says nothing the lesson text does not already teach.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Character is a person's distinguishing mark, so a respected person's traits are that person's identifiers. Pupils in groups of five discuss the traits, and show a respected person's character in role-play; teachers are told to name, as samples, good traits such as humility and honesty against bad ones such as rudeness/coarseness and fraud, so pupils learn to separate good character from bad, and to recognise their own human values (humility, honesty) and share them with classmates.
