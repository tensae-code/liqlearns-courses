# How a respected person behaves — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** State this lesson's one thing in your own words.
**Expected:** Character is a person's distinguishing mark, so a respected person's traits are that person's identifiers. Pupils in groups of five discuss the traits, and show a respected person's character in role-play; teachers are told to name, as samples, good traits such as humility and honesty against bad ones such as rudeness/coarseness and fraud, so pupils learn to separate good character from bad, and to recognise their own human values (humility, honesty) and share them with classmates.

**2.** Explain the lesson's first part: The marks of a respected person.
**Expected:** an answer drawn from the lesson text for that part, in the learner's own words.

Difficulty: 2 = restating the teaching accurately without copying it.
