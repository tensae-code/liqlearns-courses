# How a respected person behaves — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** This statement is the lesson's one thing: Character is a person's distinguishing mark, so a respected person's traits are that person's identifiers. Pupils in groups of five discuss the traits, and show a respected person's character in role-play; teachers are told to name, as samples, good traits such as humility and honesty against bad ones such as rudeness/coarseness and fraud, so pupils learn to separate good character from bad, and to recognise their own human values (humility, honesty) and share them with classmates.
**Answer: True** — it states the lesson's teaching in full.

**2.** A learner who lands on this lesson directly must finish every earlier level before starting it.
**Answer: False** — earlier levels are recommended first, never a forced gate; every lesson stands alone.

Difficulty: 1 = direct recall; 2 = applying the course's direct-entry rule.
