# How a respected person behaves — depth

This lesson's parts are distinct and sequential, so they stand as its sub lessons (2026-11-09 ruling: sub lessons only where a lesson genuinely has distinct sequential parts). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: Character is a person's distinguishing mark, so a respected person's traits are that person's identifiers. Pupils in groups of five discuss the traits, and show a respected person's character in role-play; teachers are told to name, as samples, good traits such as humility and honesty against bad ones such as rudeness/coarseness and fraud, so pupils learn to separate good character from bad, and to recognise their own human values (humility, honesty) and share them with classmates. — shown in the learner's own words and in the lesson's closing act done in a real situation.
