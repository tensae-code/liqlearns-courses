# Video script — Respecting our elders and religious fathers

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The lesson is text-first: this script says nothing the lesson text does not already teach.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Two of the lesson's named respects get their own activities: respecting elders, with pupils naming the customary moral principles of elder-respect found in their society; and respecting religious fathers, where the activity answers note the things to be considered when religious fathers are respected (for example reconciliation/peace-making, እርቅ).
