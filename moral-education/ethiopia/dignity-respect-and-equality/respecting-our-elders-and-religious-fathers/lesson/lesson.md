## Respecting our elders and religious fathers

The chapter's closing short answers add the ways of respecting elders: being humble and gentle when you meet your elders, listening carefully when they counsel you, and not interrupting while they speak.

> Tip — from us, not from the books: Act it, don't just say it. A value you have practised once in a real moment — at home, at school, in your street — is yours in a way a value you have only read about is not.
