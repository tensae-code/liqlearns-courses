# Lesson plan — Marks of humanity — love, compassion, empathy, charity

**Course:** Moral Education — Ethiopia · **Level folder:** Level 5 — Human Dignity, Respect and Equality · **Lesson:** Marks of humanity — love, compassion, empathy, charity
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 5 — Human Dignity, Respect and Equality (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The chapter's long third lesson defines the basic characteristics of humanity one by one, the first group being: love — the strongest feeling for a person or thing, holding attachment, commitment, closeness and desire, and pupils distinguish self-love, family love, a friend's love, love of work and love of country; compassion (ርኅራሄ) — deeply understanding another's harm and wishing it eased, wanting to help even when help is not practical; empathy (ሀዘኔታ) — sharing others' feelings, entering their problem and seeking its solution; and charity/good-doing (በጎ አድራጊነት) — called the highest expression of love, also the organisation that gathers and gives money to help the disabled, the poor and the sick.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 5 — Human Dignity, Respect and Equality (recommended, never forced).
**Strand:** manners/morals — the strand the Grade 3 teacher guide itself gives this teaching (manners/morals, or constitution/civics where the guide teaches rights, law or the state inside Moral Education).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The lesson teaches through its text first: every picture's and GIF's teaching is also in the text, so the lesson is fully learnable by listening to its text read aloud.
