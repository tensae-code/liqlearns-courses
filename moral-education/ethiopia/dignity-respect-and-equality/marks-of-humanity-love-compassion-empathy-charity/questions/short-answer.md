# Marks of humanity — love, compassion, empathy, charity — short answer

**Expected:** The chapter's long third lesson defines the basic characteristics of humanity one by one, the first group being: love — the strongest feeling for a person or thing, holding attachment, commitment, closeness and desire, and pupils distinguish self-love, family love, a friend's love, love of work and love of country; compassion (ርኅራሄ) — deeply understanding another's harm and wishing it eased, wanting to help even when help is not practical; empathy (ሀዘኔታ) — sharing others' feelings, entering their problem and seeking its solution; and charity/good-doing (በጎ አድራጊነት) — called the highest expression of love, also the organisation that gathers and gives money to help the disabled, the poor and the sick.
