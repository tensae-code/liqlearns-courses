# Marks of humanity — love, compassion, empathy, charity

## Do it first

Before you read, try the lesson's act once with what you already know: The chapter's long third lesson defines the basic characteristics of humanity one by one, the first group being: love — the strongest feeling for a person or thing, holding attachment, commitment, closeness and desire, and you and a companion distinguish self-love, family love, a friend's love, love of work and love of country; compassion (ርኅራሄ) — deeply understanding another's harm and wishing it eased, wanting to help even when help is not practical; empathy (ሀዘኔታ) — sharing others' feelings, entering their problem and seeking its solution; and charity/good-doing (በጎ አድራጊነት) — called the highest expression of love, also the organisation that gathers and gives money to help the disabled, the poor and the sick. Keep what you did in mind — you will compare it with your work at the end.
