# Hands-on — Marks of humanity — love, compassion, empathy, charity

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
The chapter's long third lesson defines the basic characteristics of humanity one by one, the first group being: love — the strongest feeling for a person or thing, holding attachment, commitment, closeness and desire, and you and a companion distinguish self-love, family love, a friend's love, love of work and love of country; compassion (ርኅራሄ) — deeply understanding another's harm and wishing it eased, wanting to help even when help is not practical; empathy (ሀዘኔታ) — sharing others' feelings, entering their problem and seeking its solution; and charity/good-doing (በጎ አድራጊነት) — called the highest expression of love, also the organisation that gathers and gives money to help the disabled, the poor and the sick.

For one day, watch for this lesson's teaching in real life around you — in your family, your classroom, your street. Each time you see it (or see it missing), note what you saw and what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
