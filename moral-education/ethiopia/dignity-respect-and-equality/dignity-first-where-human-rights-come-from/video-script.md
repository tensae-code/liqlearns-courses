# Video script — Dignity first — where human rights come from

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The lesson is text-first: this script says nothing the lesson text does not already teach.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Mixed placement, civics dominant: the guide states that respecting human dignity is the foundation for human rights, that human rights cannot be seen apart from human dignity, and that respecting dignity lays the basis for human-rights laws to protect and apply; the human-rights laws are quoted in the guide's own Amharic affirmation that all human beings are born free and are equal in dignity and in rights.

The manners part is the daily act that carries it: if your friend needs your help, you must help — with money, clothing, food, water and the like — and respecting another's dignity is also presented as an Ethiopian custom by which your own dignity is respected in return.

One activity answer lists the reasons for respecting dignity: respecting human rights, giving humanitarian aid, and framing human laws.
