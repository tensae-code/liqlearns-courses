## The four equalities

Mixed placement, civics dominant. Gender equality: the equality of men and women in rights of equal benefit — in housework, education, farm work, family responsibility and trade, women and men are to be seen at an equal level; an activity answer adds that women can do what men do and men what women do — women lacked, in past times, not ability but the chance to participate. Language equality: in Ethiopia all languages are equal and must be respected equally; people have the right to use their language for learning, creative writing and literature, and to express themselves easily in their mother tongue; the languages named in the answers are Afar, Amharic, Somali, Oromo and Tigrinya, among others.

## Gender, language, religion, culture

Religion equality: every citizen has the right to follow the faith he or she chooses and to teach or preach its teachings; Ethiopia's faiths — Christianity, Islam and indigenous faiths (Waaqeffanna) among them — live with tolerance and mutual respect. Culture equality: no culture is greater or lesser than another; culture can change with social and economic development. The manners part is the practice of these equalities in the classroom and the neighbourhood.
