# Moral Education

Moral Education is a standalone school subject in the
curricula that teach it under its own name, with its own
textbooks. This folder holds one course per country, built
from that country's own Moral Education books — the same
pattern as the History, Technical Drawing and Civics and
Citizenship folders.

Courses here:

- `ethiopia/` — Moral Education — Ethiopia, built from
  Ethiopia's Moral Education teacher guides for Grades 1–6
  alone (no student book in hand for any grade; see its
  ABOUT.md).

The same Ethiopian guides' content also stands inside the
Civics and Citizenship — Ethiopia course as its named
opening levels (Levels 1–12, "morals and manners") and its
Level 13 civics strand. That course is a different shelf and
stays exactly as it is; a country's own standalone course
here does not replace it.
