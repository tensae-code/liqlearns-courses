**Acceptance count: taught in 3 countries (Sierra Leone, Jordan, Pakistan).** Sierra Leone carries the whole lesson: its SSS 2 Term 1 handbook teaches variation as a block of four lessons — direct (L17, p.45), inverse (L18, p.48), joint (L19, p.51) and partial (L20, p.54), each with numerical and word problems — and returns to variation in review problems later in the SSS course. Jordan carries the base forms only: in Grade 7 Term 2, Unit 5, direct variation (L4, p.23) and inverse variation (L5, p.30), built on unit rate and proportion; Jordan teaches no joint or partial variation there, and the lesson does not claim it does. Pakistan carries a full Variations unit in Class 10 (Unit 3, printed pp.49–73): ratio and proportion first, then direct and inverse variation including variation with powers, third, fourth and mean proportionals and continued proportion, the theorems on proportions (invertendo, alternendo, componendo, dividendo, componendo-dividendo), joint variation, the k-method, and real-life variation problems, with a unit summary on pp.72–73; the filing lists no partial variation for Pakistan, so partial variation still rests on Sierra Leone alone.
