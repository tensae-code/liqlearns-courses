**Acceptance count: taught in 2 countries (Sierra Leone, Jordan).** Sierra Leone carries the whole lesson: its SSS 2 Term 1 handbook teaches variation as a block of four lessons — direct (L17, p.45), inverse (L18, p.48), joint (L19, p.51) and partial (L20, p.54), each with numerical and word problems — and returns to variation in review problems later in the SSS course. Jordan carries the base forms only: in Grade 7 Term 2, Unit 5, direct variation (L4, p.23) and inverse variation (L5, p.30), built on unit rate and proportion; Jordan teaches no joint or partial variation there, and the lesson does not claim it does.
