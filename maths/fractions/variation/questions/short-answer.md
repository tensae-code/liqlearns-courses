# Variation — short answer

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Direct Variation · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Inverse Variation · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Joint and Partial Variation.

Write the law (the equation with k in it) before giving the number.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** y ∝ x, and y = 15 when x = 5. Find y when x = 8.
**Answer:** 24 — k = 3, so y = 3x, and y = 3 × 8 = 24.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The cost C of cloth varies directly as its length L, and 4 m cost 200. Find the cost of 11 m.
**Answer:** 550 — k = 200 ÷ 4 = 50 per metre, and 50 × 11 = 550.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** y = kx and y = 7 when x = 7. Find x when y = 49.
**Answer:** 49 — k = 1, so y = x, and x = 49.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** y ∝ 1/x, and y = 4 when x = 5. Find y when x = 2.
**Answer:** 10 — k = xy = 20, so y = 20/x, and y = 20 ÷ 2 = 10.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** A journey at 30 km/h takes 4 hours. How long at 60 km/h, same journey?
**Answer:** 2 hours — speed × time is fixed at 120, and 120 ÷ 60 = 2.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** z ∝ xy, and z = 40 when x = 4 and y = 2. Find z when x = 5 and y = 3.
**Answer:** 75 — k = 40 ÷ 8 = 5, so z = 5xy, and z = 5 × 5 × 3 = 75.

**7. (Sub lesson 3)** y is partly constant and partly varies as x: y = 9 when x = 1, and y = 15 when x = 3. Find a and b in y = a + bx.
**Answer:** a = 6, b = 3 — growth is 6 over 2 steps of x, so b = 3, and a = 9 − 3 = 6.

**8. (Sub lesson 3)** Using your a and b from question 7, find y when x = 10.
**Answer:** 36 — y = 6 + 3 × 10 = 36.

**9. (Sub lesson 1)** Two numbers are in the ratio 3:4. When 2 is added to each, the ratio becomes 5:6. Find the two numbers (Pakistan, Class 10, Unit 3).
**Answer:** 3 and 4 — write the numbers as 3x and 4x; (3x + 2)/(4x + 2) = 5/6 gives x = 1.

**10. (Sub lesson 3)** If a:b = 5:8, find (3a + 4b) : (5a + 7b) (Pakistan, Class 10, Unit 3).
**Answer:** 47:81 — put a = 5 and b = 8 in each part: 3 × 5 + 4 × 8 = 47 and 5 × 5 + 7 × 8 = 81.
