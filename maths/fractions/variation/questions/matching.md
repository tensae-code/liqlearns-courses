The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Direct Variation · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Inverse Variation · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Joint and Partial Variation.

Match each item on the left to its partner on the right.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Match each law to its kind: (a) y = 5x (b) y = 5/x (c) y = 5 + 2x — partial, direct, inverse
**Answer:** a) direct, b) inverse, c) partial — read the shape of each equation.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Match each pair-start to its constant: (a) direct, y = 14 when x = 2 (b) inverse, y = 14 when x = 2 (c) direct, y = 9 when x = 3 — k = 28, k = 7, k = 3
**Answer:** a) k = 7, b) k = 28, c) k = 3 — direct divides y by x; inverse multiplies them.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Match each story to its kind: (a) a bill with a standing charge plus per-unit use (b) the volume of a box as length and width change (c) workers and days for one job — joint, partial, inverse
**Answer:** a) partial, b) joint, c) inverse — the standing charge is the still part; volume follows two measures together; workers and days pull opposite ways.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Match each real-life law from Pakistan's Class 10 unit to its kind: (a) a spring's stretch against its load, by Hooke's law (b) light against its distance from the lamp (c) a beam's strength against its breadth and the square of its depth — direct, inverse square, joint
**Answer:** a) direct, b) inverse square, c) joint — the stretch grows with the load; the light falls as 1/d²; the strength follows b and d² together as s ∝ bd².
