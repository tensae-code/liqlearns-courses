# Variation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Level 1)** y varies directly as x, and y = 20 when x = 4. The constant k is:
a) 80  b) 5  c) 16
**Answer:** b) 5 — k = y ÷ x = 20 ÷ 4 = 5.

**2. (Level 1)** If y = 3x, what is y when x = 9?
a) 12  b) 27  c) 3
**Answer:** b) 27 — substitute: 3 × 9 = 27.

**3. (Level 1)** Which equation shows direct variation?
a) y = 7x  b) y = 7/x  c) y = 7 + x
**Answer:** a) y = 7x — direct variation is y = kx.

**4. (Level 2)** y varies inversely as x, and y = 10 when x = 3. The constant k is:
a) 30  b) 13  c) 10/3
**Answer:** a) 30 — for inverse variation k = xy = 10 × 3 = 30.

**5. (Level 2)** If y = 24/x, what is y when x = 6?
a) 4  b) 18  c) 144
**Answer:** a) 4 — 24 ÷ 6 = 4.

**6. (Level 2)** Doubling x in an inverse variation makes y:
a) double  b) halve  c) stay the same
**Answer:** b) halve — the product xy is fixed, so y must fall by half.

**7. (Level 3)** z varies jointly as x and y, and z = 12 when x = 1 and y = 2. The constant k is:
a) 3  b) 6  c) 24
**Answer:** b) 6 — k = z ÷ (xy) = 12 ÷ 2 = 6.

**8. (Level 3)** A charge follows y = 8 + 2x. The constant (still) part is:
a) 2  b) x  c) 8
**Answer:** c) 8 — in partial variation y = a + bx, a = 8 never moves.
