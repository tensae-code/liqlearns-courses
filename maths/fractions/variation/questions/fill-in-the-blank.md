# Variation — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the missing number, letter or word.

**1. (Level 1)** Direct variation is written y = ______ x, where the missing letter is the constant.
**Answer:** k — y = kx, with k the constant of variation.

**2. (Level 1)** If y = 6x, then y = ______ when x = 5.
**Answer:** 30 — 6 × 5 = 30.

**3. (Level 2)** In inverse variation the ______ of x and y never changes.
**Answer:** product — xy = k for every pair.

**4. (Level 2)** If y = 18/x, then y = ______ when x = 9.
**Answer:** 2 — 18 ÷ 9 = 2.

**5. (Level 3)** In joint variation z = kxy, z follows x and ______ together.
**Answer:** y — one quantity varying as the product of the two others.

**6. (Level 3)** In partial variation y = a + bx, the part that never moves is ______.
**Answer:** a — a is the constant part; bx is the varying part.
