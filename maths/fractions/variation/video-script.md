# Video script — Variation

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Some things follow each other!

**Direct:** you double, I double.
Twelve when you are three? Then I am four times you.
y equals k x. Find k, and I am yours!

**Inverse:** you double, I HALVE.
The product never changes. x times y equals k!

**Joint:** I follow TWO of you together —
your product, times k!

**Partial:** part of me never moves,
part of me follows you. A still part, plus b times x.

Name the kind. Find the constant. Done!
