# Ratio and Proportion — short answer

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — What a Ratio Is, and Equivalent Ratios · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Sharing in a Ratio, and Ratio with Fractions and Percent · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Proportion, Unit Rate, Scale, Working Together.

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Simplify 12:18.
**Answer:** 2:3.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Men to women is 5:7. There are 45 men. How many
women are there? (Ghana, JICA B6 teacher manual, L63.)
**Answer:** 63.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Share 100 in the ratio 1:4.
**Answer:** 20 and 80.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The ratio 3:2 as a percent — the first part is
what percent of the whole?
**Answer:** 60%.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** A car travels 240 km in 3 hours. What is its
speed per hour?
**Answer:** 80 km per hour.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** One worker finishes a job in 6 hours, another
in 3 hours. Together, how long does the job take?
**Answer:** 2 hours.
