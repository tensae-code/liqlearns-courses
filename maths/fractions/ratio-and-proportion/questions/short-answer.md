# Ratio and Proportion — short answer

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Level 1)** Simplify 12:18.
**Answer:** 2:3.

**2. (Level 1)** Men to women is 5:7. There are 45 men. How many
women are there? (Ghana, JICA B6 teacher manual, L63.)
**Answer:** 63.

**3. (Level 2)** Share 100 in the ratio 1:4.
**Answer:** 20 and 80.

**4. (Level 2)** The ratio 3:2 as a percent — the first part is
what percent of the whole?
**Answer:** 60%.

**5. (Level 3)** A car travels 240 km in 3 hours. What is its
speed per hour?
**Answer:** 80 km per hour.

**6. (Level 3)** One worker finishes a job in 6 hours, another
in 3 hours. Together, how long does the job take?
**Answer:** 2 hours.
