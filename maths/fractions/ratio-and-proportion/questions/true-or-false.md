# Ratio and Proportion — true or false

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Level 1)** 2:3 = 4:6.
**True** — multiply both terms by 2.

**2. (Level 1)** 8:12 = 10:14.
**False** — adding 2 to each term does not keep a ratio.
8/12 is 2/3 and 10/14 is 5/7 (Amany's error, Egypt Grade 6
Term 2, Unit 9, L2).

**3. (Level 2)** Sharing 72 in the ratio 7:11 gives 28 and 44.
**True** — 18 parts of 4: 7 × 4 = 28 and 11 × 4 = 44
(Ghana, JICA B6 teacher manual).

**4. (Level 2)** In the ratio 2:3, the first part is 2/3 of the
whole.
**False** — it is 2 out of 5 parts, which is 2/5.

**5. (Level 3)** If 3 kg cost 6, then 5 kg cost 10.
**True** — direct proportion: 1 kg costs 2, so 5 kg cost 10.

**6. (Level 3)** On a map with scale 1 cm : 10 km, 3 cm is
30 km.
**True** — 3 × 10 = 30.
