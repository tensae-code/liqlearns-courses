# Ratio and Proportion — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — What a Ratio Is, and Equivalent Ratios · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Sharing in a Ratio, and Ratio with Fractions and Percent · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Proportion, Unit Rate, Scale, Working Together.

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** 6 red pens and 4 blue pens. The ratio red to
blue, simplified, is ______.
a) 6:4  b) 3:2  c) 2:3
**Answer: b) 3:2** — divide both terms by 2.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which ratio is equivalent to 2:3?
a) 4:5  b) 8:12  c) 6:8
**Answer: b) 8:12** — multiply both terms by 4.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Share 60 in the ratio 2:3. The shares are
______.
a) 20 and 40  b) 24 and 36  c) 30 and 30
**Answer: b) 24 and 36** — 5 parts of 12.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In the ratio 2:3, the first part is what fraction
of the whole?
a) 2/3  b) 2/5  c) 3/5
**Answer: b) 2/5** — 2 out of 2 + 3 parts.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** 2/3 = x/12. x = ______.
a) 4  b) 8  c) 6
**Answer: b) 8** — cross-multiply: 3x = 24.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** Basma does 570 jumps in 6 minutes. Her rate is
______ jumps per minute.
a) 95  b) 57  c) 90
**Answer: a) 95** — 570 ÷ 6 = 95 (Egypt, Grade 6 Term 2,
Unit 10, L1–3).

**7. (Sub lesson 3)** On a map with scale 1 cm : 10 km, two towns
4.5 cm apart are really ______ apart.
a) 4.5 km  b) 45 km  c) 450 km
**Answer: b) 45 km.**
