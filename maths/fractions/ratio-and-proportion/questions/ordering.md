# Ratio and Proportion — ordering

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — What a Ratio Is, and Equivalent Ratios · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Sharing in a Ratio, and Ratio with Fractions and Percent · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Proportion, Unit Rate, Scale, Working Together.

Put each set in order, smallest first.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** 3:4 · 1:4 · 1:1 · 1:2
**Answer:** 1:4, 1:2, 3:4, 1:1 (first part as a fraction of the whole: 1/5, 1/3, 3/7, 1/2).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Share 60 in 2:3 · Share 60 in 1:2 · Share 60 in
3:2 · Share 60 in 1:1 — order by the first person's share.
**Answer:** Share 60 in 1:2 (20), Share 60 in 2:3 (24), Share 60
in 1:1 (30), Share 60 in 3:2 (36).

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** 2/3 = x/9 · 2/3 = x/3 · 2/3 = x/12 · 2/3 = x/6
**Answer:** 2/3 = x/3, 2/3 = x/6, 2/3 = x/9, 2/3 = x/12
(x = 2, 4, 6, 8).

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** 240 km in 3 hours · 150 km in 5 hours · 200 km
in 4 hours · 120 km in 2 hours — order by speed.
**Answer:** 150 km in 5 hours (30 km/h), 200 km in 4 hours
(50 km/h), 120 km in 2 hours (60 km/h), 240 km in 3 hours
(80 km/h).
