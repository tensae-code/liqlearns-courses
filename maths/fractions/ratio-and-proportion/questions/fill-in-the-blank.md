# Ratio and Proportion — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with a word from the box.

**Box:** ratio · colon · equivalent · cross-multiply · proportion · scale

**1. (Level 1)** A comparison of two quantities is a ______.
**Answer: ratio.**

**2. (Level 1)** A ratio is written with a ______.
**Answer: colon.**

**3. (Level 1)** Ratios that are the same are ______.
**Answer: equivalent.**

**4. (Level 3)** To check a ______, cross-multiply.
**Answer: proportion.**

**5. (Level 3)** To find a missing term, ______ and divide.
**Answer: cross-multiply.**

**6. (Level 3)** A map's ratio is its ______.
**Answer: scale.**
