# Video script — Ratio and Proportion

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Six yellow! Four red! Mix!
Now nine yellow! Six red! Mix!
Same colour! Six to four is nine to six!
Both are three to two!

Share it! Sixty sweets, two to three!
Five parts! Twelve each! Twenty-four… and thirty-six!

Map! One centimetre is ten kilometres!
Four and a half centimetres… forty-five kilometres!

Now you. Two to one! Say it!
Six to four… simplify… three to two! You did it!
