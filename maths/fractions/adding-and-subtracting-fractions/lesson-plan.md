# Lesson plan — Adding and Subtracting Fractions

**Course:** Maths · **Stage:** 4, Fractions, Decimals and Percentages · **Lesson:** Adding and Subtracting Fractions
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 12 of the ladder — the second
lesson of Stage 4 (CURRICULUM.md). It stands on the Fractions
lesson (what a fraction is) and on Stage 3 (multiplication and
division, including the LCM work from factors).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** adding and subtracting
fractions — with the same denominator, then with different
denominators made the same, then mixed numbers — and checking
the answer by estimating.
**This lesson's own level:** it needs the Fractions lesson's
meaning of numerator and denominator, and nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the
exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an
image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture
of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does
with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and
while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is
dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the
student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown
changing — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds per piece.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the Fractions lesson (Level 11). Recommended,
  never forced.
- Multiplying and dividing fractions is the next lesson — this
  lesson only adds and subtracts.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Add and subtract fractions with the same denominator.
2. Explain why the denominator stays the same.
3. Make two fractions share a denominator (with the LCM) and then
   add or subtract them.
4. Add and subtract mixed numbers, carrying and borrowing wholes.
5. Estimate first and say whether an answer is sensible.
6. Solve story problems on adding and subtracting fractions.

## What you need
Paper strips to fold, a fraction wall to twelfths, pencil and paper.

1. **Do it first** — the learner adds quarter-pieces of pizza in

2. **The explanation** — same denominators; why the denominator
   stays; different denominators made the same; mixed numbers;
   estimating; story problems (lesson/lesson.md).

   - ADD IMAGE under different denominators: a picture of a

## If the learner is stuck
- Adds the denominators (1/4 + 1/4 = 2/8) → go back to the pizza:
  two quarter-pieces are still quarters. The answer is 2/4.
- Cannot find the common denominator → use the fraction wall:
  line both fractions up against the twelfths row and read the
  denominator off the wall. The LCM is the shortcut; the wall is
  the sight.
- Loses the borrowed whole in mixed subtraction → write the mixed
  number as an improper fraction and check the answer that way.
- Forgets to simplify at the end → ask: can the top and bottom
  both be divided by the same number? 9/12 is 3/4.
