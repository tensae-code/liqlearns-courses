# Video script — Adding and Subtracting Fractions

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

One quarter of pizza… and one more quarter!
One quarter plus one quarter… is TWO quarters!
The bottom number stays! Quarters stay quarters!

Three fifths… take away one fifth!
Three… take away one… is TWO fifths!
The bottom stays again!

Now halves and thirds. Different pieces!
Make them the same! Sixths!
One half is three sixths. One third is two sixths.
Three sixths plus two sixths… is FIVE sixths!

Now you. One fourth plus two fourths?
Say it! Three fourths! You did it!
