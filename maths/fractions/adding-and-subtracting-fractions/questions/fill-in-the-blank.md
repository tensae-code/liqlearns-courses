# Adding and Subtracting Fractions — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with a word from the box.

**Box:** numerators · denominator · common · twelfths · regrouping · improper

**1. (Level 1)** When the denominators are the same, add the
______.
**Answer: numerators.**

**2. (Level 1)** The ______ never changes when you add like
fractions.
**Answer: denominator.**

**3. (Level 2)** 1/2 and 1/3 share the ______ denominator 6.
**Answer: common.**

**4. (Level 2)** 1/12 + 2/3 = 9/12; 2/3 is 8/______.
**Answer: twelfths.**

**5. (Level 3)** When mixed-number fractions add past a whole, you
are ______.
**Answer: regrouping.**

**6. (Level 3)** 7/4 is an ______ fraction.
**Answer: improper.**
