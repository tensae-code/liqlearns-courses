# Adding and Subtracting Fractions — fill in the blank

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Adding and Subtracting Fractions Same Denominators · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Adding and Subtracting Fractions Different Denominators Made the Same · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Adding and Subtracting Fractions Mixed Numbers, Estimating, Stories.

Fill each blank with a word from the box.

**Box:** numerators · denominator · common · twelfths · regrouping · improper

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** When the denominators are the same, add the
______.
**Answer: numerators.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The ______ never changes when you add like
fractions.
**Answer: denominator.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** 1/2 and 1/3 share the ______ denominator 6.
**Answer: common.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** 1/12 + 2/3 = 9/12; 2/3 is 8/______.
**Answer: twelfths.**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** When mixed-number fractions add past a whole, you
are ______.
**Answer: regrouping.**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** 7/4 is an ______ fraction.
**Answer: improper.**
