# Adding and Subtracting Fractions — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Level 1)** 1/4 + 2/4 = ______.
a) 3/8  b) 3/4  c) 2/4
**Answer: b) 3/4** — add the numerators, keep the denominator.

**2. (Level 1)** 3/5 − 1/5 = ______.
a) 2/5  b) 2/10  c) 4/5
**Answer: a) 2/5.**

**3. (Level 2)** 1/2 + 1/3 = ______.
a) 2/5  b) 5/6  c) 1/5
**Answer: b) 5/6** — make them sixths first: 3/6 + 2/6.

**4. (Level 2)** The smallest common denominator of 1/4 and 1/6 is
______.
a) 10  b) 24  c) 12
**Answer: c) 12.**

**5. (Level 3)** 2 1/4 + 1 1/4 = ______.
a) 3 1/2  b) 3 1/4  c) 4
**Answer: a) 3 1/2.**

**6. (Level 3)** 3/4 − 1/2 = ______.
a) 2/2  b) 1/4  c) 1/2
**Answer: b) 1/4** — 3/4 − 2/4 = 1/4.

**7. (Level 3)** Is 4/5 + 2/3 more than 1?
a) No, it is less than 1  b) Yes, it is more than 1  c) It is
exactly 1
**Answer: b) Yes, it is more than 1** — 4/5 + 2/3 = 22/15
(Egypt, Grade 5 Term 2, Unit 7, L2).
