# Adding and Subtracting Fractions — short answer

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Level 1)** 1/3 + 1/3 = ______
**Answer:** 2/3.

**2. (Level 1)** 5/6 − 1/6 = ______ (simplify it).
**Answer:** 2/3.

**3. (Level 2)** 1/2 + 1/4 = ______
**Answer:** 3/4.

**4. (Level 2)** 2/3 − 1/6 = ______
**Answer:** 1/2.

**5. (Level 3)** 1 1/2 + 1 1/2 = ______
**Answer:** 3.

**6. (Level 3)** 3 1/4 − 1 3/4 = ______
**Answer:** 1 1/2.
