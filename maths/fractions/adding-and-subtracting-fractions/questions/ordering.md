# Adding and Subtracting Fractions — ordering

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Adding and Subtracting Fractions Same Denominators · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Adding and Subtracting Fractions Different Denominators Made the Same · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Adding and Subtracting Fractions Mixed Numbers, Estimating, Stories.

Put each set in order, smallest first.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** 1/5 + 4/5 · 1/5 + 1/5 · 1/5 + 3/5 · 1/5 + 2/5
**Answer:** 1/5 + 1/5, 1/5 + 2/5, 1/5 + 3/5, 1/5 + 4/5
(2/5, 3/5, 4/5, 1).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** 1 · 1/2 + 1/3 · 1/2 · 1/2 + 1/4
**Answer:** 1/2, 1/2 + 1/4, 1/2 + 1/3, 1 (1/2, 3/4, 5/6, 1).

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** 2 1/4 · 1 3/4 · 3 · 2 3/4
**Answer:** 1 3/4, 2 1/4, 2 3/4, 3.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** 5/6 − 1/6 · 5/6 − 1/3 · 5/6 − 1/2 · 5/6 − 2/3
**Answer:** 5/6 − 2/3, 5/6 − 1/2, 5/6 − 1/3, 5/6 − 1/6
(1/6, 1/3, 1/2, 2/3).
