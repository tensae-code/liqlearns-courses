# Adding and Subtracting Fractions — matching

Match each sum or difference to its answer.

**Set 1 (Level 1)** — same denominators.
1. 1/4 + 1/4 — **1/2**
2. 2/5 + 1/5 — **3/5**
3. 3/7 − 1/7 — **2/7**
4. 5/8 − 3/8 — **1/4**
5. 4/9 + 2/9 — **2/3**

**Set 2 (Level 2)** — different denominators made the same.
1. 1/2 + 1/4 — **3/4**
2. 1/3 + 1/6 — **1/2**
3. 2/3 − 1/6 — **1/2**
4. 3/4 − 1/2 — **1/4**
5. 1/5 + 1/10 — **3/10**

**Set 3 (Level 3)** — mixed numbers.
1. 1 1/2 + 1 1/2 — **3**
2. 2 1/4 + 1 3/4 — **4**
3. 3 1/2 − 1 1/4 — **2 1/4**
4. 4 3/4 − 2 1/4 — **2 1/2**
5. 1 2/3 + 1 1/3 — **3**

**Set 4 (Level 1–2)** — the words.
1. like fractions — **same denominator**
2. unlike fractions — **different denominators**
3. common denominator — **shared denominator**
4. LCM — **smallest shared multiple**
5. simplify — **divide top and bottom by the same number**
