Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: given the price or measure of many, find the one by dividing, and write the "1 … =" line with its unit.

## Sub lesson 2 — The Unitary Method One to Many, Direct Proportion
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: pass through the one and multiply up to any number asked, in direct-proportion problems (prices, cloth, travel at one speed).
