# The Unitary Method — questions by level

Practice by level, tied to the skill number. The questions live in
the type files; this file is the by-level view. The levels are the
depths inside this one thing — the unitary method — many to one first, then one to many in direct proportion, then inverse proportion.

## Level 1 — many to one
Level 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: given the price or measure of many, find the one by dividing, and write the "1 … =" line with its unit.

## Level 2 — one to many, direct proportion
Level 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: pass through the one and multiply up to any number asked, in direct-proportion problems (prices, cloth, travel at one speed).

## Level 3 — inverse proportion, and choosing the kind
Level 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: decide direct or inverse before calculating, and solve inverse problems (workers and days, taps and hours, food and days) through the one.
