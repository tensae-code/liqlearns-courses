**Acceptance count: taught in 2 countries (Pakistan, Bangladesh).** Pakistan carries the unitary method as a named, drilled method in Grade 5 — its Unit 6 names the method, defines ratio and both kinds of proportion inside the same unit, and solves direct problems (pp.92–93) and inverse problems (pp.94–95) by the many → one → many road. Bangladesh carries the method twice: in Class 5 as the price of one item, then of many (§3.3, p.17), and again in Class 6 in the chapter that joins the unitary method to percentages and ratio (Ch. 5, pp.103–137).
