# Video script — The Unitary Method

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Five pens cost seventy-five.
Hard? Go to ONE pen first!

Seventy-five divided by five.
One pen costs fifteen.
Now eight pens? Fifteen times eight.
One hundred and twenty. Done!

Many — to ONE — to many.
That is the road. Always through one!

And workers? Six workers, ten days.
One worker alone? Sixty days.
Twelve workers? Sixty divided by twelve — five days!
More workers, fewer days. Still through one.

You try it. Many, one, many!
