# Hands-on — The Unitary Method

The lesson priced real things and shared real work, the way the books drill it.

## Price the bunch, price the one
Take a bunch with one price — 6 oranges for 90, a dozen eggs, a packet of pens. Divide to find the price of ONE. Write the line whole: "1 orange costs 15." Then price 1, 2, 5 and 10 of them by multiplying up from the one.

## The stall game
One partner is the seller with a price card for a bunch ("4 notebooks = 200"). The buyer asks for strange numbers — 1, 7, 13. The seller must pass through the one every time, saying both steps aloud. Swap roles after five sales.

## Workers and days
Act out a job: moving 60 stones is "the job". One person alone moves 1 stone a minute, so the job takes 60 minutes. How long for 2 people? 5 people? 10 people? Build the table and watch the time shrink as the team grows — inverse proportion with your own hands.

## Food for days
A bag of rice feeds 1 person for 24 days. How many days does it feed 4 people? 8 people? Work through the one-person line first, then share. Check: more mouths, fewer days.
