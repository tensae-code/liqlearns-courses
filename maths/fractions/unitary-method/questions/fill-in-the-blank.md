# The Unitary Method — fill in the blank

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — The Unitary Method Many to One · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — The Unitary Method One to Many, Direct Proportion · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — The Unitary Method Inverse Proportion, and Choosing the Kind.

Fill each blank with the missing number or word.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** If 5 pens cost 75, then 1 pen costs ______.
**Answer:** 15 — divide the many-price by 5.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The unitary method gets its name from finding the value of ______ first.
**Answer:** one (a single unit) — "unitary" means "of one".

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** 1 egg costs 9, so 24 eggs cost ______.
**Answer:** 216 — 9 × 24 = 216.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A worker packs 28 boxes in 4 hours, so in 1 hour the worker packs ______ boxes.
**Answer:** 7 — 28 ÷ 4 = 7.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** 5 taps fill a tank in 12 hours, so 1 tap fills it in ______ hours.
**Answer:** 60 — inverse: the whole job is 5 × 12 = 60 tap-hours.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** In ______ proportion, when one quantity grows the other shrinks.
**Answer:** inverse — the two pull opposite ways, as with workers and days.
