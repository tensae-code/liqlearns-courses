# The Unitary Method — true or false

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — The Unitary Method Many to One · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — The Unitary Method One to Many, Direct Proportion · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — The Unitary Method Inverse Proportion, and Choosing the Kind.

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The unitary method always finds the value of one first.
**Answer:** True — many → one → many is the whole method.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** If 4 pens cost 80, then 1 pen costs 320.
**Answer:** False — divide, do not multiply: 80 ÷ 4 = 20.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** If 1 metre of cloth costs 150, then 6 metres cost 900.
**Answer:** True — 150 × 6 = 900.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In direct proportion, doubling one quantity doubles the other.
**Answer:** True — the pair grows together at a fixed rate.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** More workers on a fixed job means more days are needed.
**Answer:** False — inverse proportion: more workers means fewer days.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** A job that takes 3 workers 20 days would take 1 worker 60 days.
**Answer:** True — the job holds 3 × 20 = 60 worker-days.
