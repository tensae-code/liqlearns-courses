# The Unitary Method — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — The Unitary Method Many to One · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — The Unitary Method One to Many, Direct Proportion · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — The Unitary Method Inverse Proportion, and Choosing the Kind.

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** 6 pens cost 90. What does 1 pen cost?
a) 12  b) 15  c) 18
**Answer:** b) 15 — many to one: divide, 90 ÷ 6 = 15.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** 10 kg of potatoes cost 250. What does 1 kg cost?
a) 20  b) 25  c) 30
**Answer:** b) 25 — 250 ÷ 10 = 25.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** 1 notebook costs 40. What do 9 notebooks cost?
a) 320  b) 360  c) 400
**Answer:** b) 360 — one to many: multiply, 40 × 9 = 360.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** 5 shirts cost 1,000. What do 3 shirts cost?
a) 500  b) 600  c) 750
**Answer:** b) 600 — one shirt is 1,000 ÷ 5 = 200, then 200 × 3 = 600.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** A bus travels 240 km on 20 litres. How far on 35 litres, at the same rate?
a) 400 km  b) 420 km  c) 480 km
**Answer:** b) 420 km — one litre gives 240 ÷ 20 = 12 km, then 12 × 35 = 420 km.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** 4 workers finish a job in 15 days. How long do 10 workers need for the same job?
a) 6 days  b) 12 days  c) 37.5 days
**Answer:** a) 6 days — inverse: the job is 4 × 15 = 60 worker-days, and 60 ÷ 10 = 6.

**7. (Sub lesson 3)** Which pair is in inverse proportion?
a) pens and their total cost  b) workers and days for a fixed job  c) metres of cloth and its cost
**Answer:** b) workers and days — as one grows the other shrinks; the others grow together.

**8. (Sub lesson 3)** A stock of food lasts 20 people for 12 days. How long does it last 30 people?
a) 8 days  b) 18 days  c) 24 days
**Answer:** a) 8 days — inverse: 20 × 12 = 240 person-days, and 240 ÷ 30 = 8.
