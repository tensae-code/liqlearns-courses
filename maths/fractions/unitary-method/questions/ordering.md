# The Unitary Method — ordering

Put the items in the order asked. Check each step before you write the answer.

**1. (Level 1)** Put the steps of the method in order: (i) multiply up to the number asked (ii) write what you know about the many (iii) divide to find the one.
**Answer:** (ii), (iii), (i) — many, then one, then many.

**2. (Level 2)** A stall sells 1 pen for 15. Put these totals in rising order: 5 pens, 2 pens, 9 pens, 4 pens.
**Answer:** 2 pens (30), 4 pens (60), 5 pens (75), 9 pens (135) — each total is 15 times its count.

**3. (Level 3)** A job needs 48 worker-days. Put these teams in order of days taken, fastest first: 4 workers, 12 workers, 6 workers, 24 workers.
**Answer:** 24 workers (2 days), 12 workers (4 days), 6 workers (8 days), 4 workers (12 days) — days = 48 ÷ workers.
