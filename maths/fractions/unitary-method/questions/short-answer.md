# The Unitary Method — short answer

Show the "1 … =" line in every answer.

**1. (Level 1)** 8 oranges cost 120. Find the cost of 1 orange.
**Answer:** 15 — 120 ÷ 8 = 15.

**2. (Level 1)** A rope 9 m long costs 540. Find the cost of 1 m.
**Answer:** 60 — 540 ÷ 9 = 60.

**3. (Level 2)** 7 books cost 560. Find the cost of 12 books.
**Answer:** 960 — one book is 560 ÷ 7 = 80, and 80 × 12 = 960.

**4. (Level 2)** A tap fills 36 litres in 4 minutes. How many litres in 15 minutes, at the same rate?
**Answer:** 135 litres — one minute gives 36 ÷ 4 = 9 litres, and 9 × 15 = 135.

**5. (Level 2)** If 15 m of wire weighs 45 kg, what do 23 m weigh?
**Answer:** 69 kg — 1 m weighs 45 ÷ 15 = 3 kg, and 3 × 23 = 69 kg.

**6. (Level 3)** 12 workers build a wall in 8 days. How many days do 16 workers need?
**Answer:** 6 days — the job is 12 × 8 = 96 worker-days, and 96 ÷ 16 = 6.

**7. (Level 3)** A car travelling for 1 hour covers the whole journey's plan: the journey takes 6 hours at 40 km/h. How long at 60 km/h?
**Answer:** 4 hours — the distance is 6 × 40 = 240 km, and 240 ÷ 60 = 4 hours.

**8. (Level 3)** State, with a reason, whether "number of pages printed and minutes taken by one printer" is direct or inverse proportion.
**Answer:** Direct — both grow together at a fixed rate, so the unitary road divides then multiplies.
