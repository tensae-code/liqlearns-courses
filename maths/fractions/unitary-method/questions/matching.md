Match each item on the left to its partner on the right.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Match each bunch to the price of one: (a) 4 pens for 60 (b) 9 pens for 90 (c) 6 pens for 96 — 10, 15, 16
**Answer:** a) 15, b) 10, c) 16 — divide each bunch-price by its count.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Match each "one" to its "many": (a) 1 book = 85 → 6 books (b) 1 book = 60 → 6 books (c) 1 book = 45 → 6 books — 270, 360, 510
**Answer:** a) 510, b) 360, c) 270 — multiply the one-price by 6.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Match each situation to its kind: (a) more taps, less time to fill (b) more cloth, more cost (c) more people sharing, less food each — direct, inverse, inverse
**Answer:** a) inverse, b) direct, c) inverse — grow–shrink pairs are inverse; grow–grow pairs are direct.
