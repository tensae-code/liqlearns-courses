# Percentages — true or false

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Level 1)** 50% = 1/2.
**True** — fifty out of a hundred is one half.

**2. (Level 1)** 1% is 1/10.
**False** — 1% is 1/100.

**3. (Level 2)** 20% of 50 = 10.
**True** — 1% of 50 is 0.5, and 20 × 0.5 is 10.

**4. (Level 2)** 40% of a number is 80, so the number is 320.
**False** — 80 ÷ 40 × 100 = 200.

**5. (Level 3)** 20% off 100, then 10% off the result, is 30% off
in total.
**False** — 100 → 80 → 72, which is 28% off in total. The
second cut acts on the already-cut price.

**6. (Level 3)** 5% tax on 340 is 17.
**True** — 340 ÷ 100 × 5 = 17.
