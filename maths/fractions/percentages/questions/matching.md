# Percentages — matching

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every set's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Percent Means Out of 100 · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Percentage of a Number, and the Whole · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Increase, Decrease, Discount, Tax, Tip.

Match each item on the left to its partner on the right.

**Set 1 (Sub lesson 1)** — fractions and percents.
1. 1/2 — **50%**
2. 1/4 — **25%**
3. 3/4 — **75%**
4. 1/5 — **20%**
5. 1/10 — **10%**

**Set 2 (Sub lesson 2)** — percent of a number.
1. 20% of 50 — **10**
2. 30% of 90 — **27**
3. 75% of 240 — **180**
4. 5% of 340 — **17**
5. 25% of 2400 — **600**

**Set 3 (Sub lesson 3)** — increase, decrease, tax.
1. 100 increased by 20% — **120**
2. 200 decreased by 15% — **170**
3. 120 discounted by 25% — **90**
4. 90 with 10% tax added — **99**
5. 340 with 5% tax added — **357**
