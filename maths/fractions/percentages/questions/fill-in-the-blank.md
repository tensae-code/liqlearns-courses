# Percentages — fill in the blank

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Percent Means Out of 100 · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Percentage of a Number, and the Whole · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Increase, Decrease, Discount, Tax, Tip.

Fill each blank with a word from the box.

**Box:** hundred · percent · whole · increase · discount · tip

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Percent means out of a ______.
**Answer: hundred.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The sign % is read as ______.
**Answer: percent.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** To find the ______, divide the part by the
percent and multiply by 100.
**Answer: whole.**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** A price rise is a percent ______.
**Answer: increase.**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** A percent off a price is a ______.
**Answer: discount.**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** A percent added by choice on a bill is a ______.
**Answer: tip.**
