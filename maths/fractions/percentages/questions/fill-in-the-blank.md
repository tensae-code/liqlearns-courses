# Percentages — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with a word from the box.

**Box:** hundred · percent · whole · increase · discount · tip

**1. (Level 1)** Percent means out of a ______.
**Answer: hundred.**

**2. (Level 1)** The sign % is read as ______.
**Answer: percent.**

**3. (Level 2)** To find the ______, divide the part by the
percent and multiply by 100.
**Answer: whole.**

**4. (Level 3)** A price rise is a percent ______.
**Answer: increase.**

**5. (Level 3)** A percent off a price is a ______.
**Answer: discount.**

**6. (Level 3)** A percent added by choice on a bill is a ______.
**Answer: tip.**
