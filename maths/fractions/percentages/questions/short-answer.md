# Percentages — short answer

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Level 1)** 3/5 written as a percent is ______.
**Answer:** 60%.

**2. (Level 1)** 45% written as a decimal is ______.
**Answer:** 0.45.

**3. (Level 2)** 75% of 240 = ______
**Answer:** 180.

**4. (Level 2)** 12 out of 48 is what percent?
**Answer:** 25%.

**5. (Level 3)** A jacket costs 120 and is discounted by 25%.
What do you pay?
**Answer:** 90.

**6. (Level 3)** 15% tip on a bill of 340 is ______.
**Answer:** 51.
