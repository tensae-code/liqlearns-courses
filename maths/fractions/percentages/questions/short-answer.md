# Percentages — short answer

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Percent Means Out of 100 · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Percentage of a Number, and the Whole · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Increase, Decrease, Discount, Tax, Tip.

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** 3/5 written as a percent is ______.
**Answer:** 60%.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** 45% written as a decimal is ______.
**Answer:** 0.45.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** 75% of 240 = ______
**Answer:** 180.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** 12 out of 48 is what percent?
**Answer:** 25%.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** A jacket costs 120 and is discounted by 25%.
What do you pay?
**Answer:** 90.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** 15% tip on a bill of 340 is ______.
**Answer:** 51.
