# Percentages — ordering

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Percent Means Out of 100 · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Percentage of a Number, and the Whole · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Increase, Decrease, Discount, Tax, Tip.

Put each set in order, smallest first.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** 10% · 50% · 25% · 75%
**Answer:** 10%, 25%, 50%, 75%.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** 30% of 90 · 10% of 50 · 50% of 40 · 20% of 50
**Answer:** 10% of 50, 20% of 50, 50% of 40, 30% of 90
(5, 10, 20, 27).

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** 100 + 20% · 100 − 10% · 100 − 20% · 100 + 10%
**Answer:** 100 − 20%, 100 − 10%, 100 + 10%, 100 + 20%
(80, 90, 110, 120).

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** 200 + 10% · 200 − 25% · 200 + 25% · 200 − 10%
**Answer:** 200 − 25%, 200 − 10%, 200 + 10%, 200 + 25%
(150, 180, 220, 250).
