# Percentages — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Level 1)** 1/4 written as a percent is ______.
a) 14%  b) 25%  c) 40%
**Answer: b) 25%.**

**2. (Level 1)** 0.6 written as a percent is ______.
a) 6%  b) 60%  c) 0.6%
**Answer: b) 60%.**

**3. (Level 2)** 20% of 50 is ______.
a) 20  b) 5  c) 10
**Answer: c) 10** — 1% of 50 is 0.5, and 20 × 0.5 is 10.

**4. (Level 2)** 30% of a number is 60. The number is ______.
a) 180  b) 200  c) 90
**Answer: b) 200** — 60 ÷ 30 × 100 = 200.

**5. (Level 3)** 100 increased by 20% is ______.
a) 20  b) 120  c) 102
**Answer: b) 120.**

**6. (Level 3)** 200 decreased by 15% is ______.
a) 185  b) 170  c) 150
**Answer: b) 170** — 85% of 200 is 170.

**7. (Level 3)** 5% tax on a bill of 340 is ______.
a) 17  b) 15  c) 34
**Answer: a) 17** — Egypt's Grade 6 book works this bill
(340 LE, 5% tax, 15% tip).
