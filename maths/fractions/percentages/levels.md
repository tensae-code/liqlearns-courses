# Percentages — questions by level

Practice by level, tied to the skill number. The questions live in
the type files; this file is the by-level view. The levels are the
depths inside this one thing — percentages — set by the pattern
across the countries' books: percent as part of 100 first, then
conversions and percent of a number, then the whole, increase
and decrease, and money.

## Level 1 — percent means out of 100
Level 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: say what a
percent is, and turn fractions and decimals into percents and
back.

## Level 2 — percentage of a number, and the whole
Level 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: find a
percentage of a number, find the whole from a part and a
percent, and find what percentage one number is of another.

## Level 3 — increase, decrease, discount, tax, tip
Level 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: work out a new
price after an increase or a decrease, and work out a discount,
a tax and a tip.
