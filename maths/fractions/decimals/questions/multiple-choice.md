# Decimals — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Decimals Tenths, Hundredths, Thousandths · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Decimals Comparing, Ordering, Rounding, Adding, Subtracting · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Decimals Multiplying, Dividing, and by 10, 100, 1000.

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** 3/10 written as a decimal is ______.
a) 0.3  b) 0.03  c) 3.0
**Answer: a) 0.3.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** 0.25 as a fraction is ______.
a) 1/2  b) 1/4  c) 2/5
**Answer: b) 1/4.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Which is bigger?
a) 0.45  b) 0.5  c) 0.405
**Answer: b) 0.5** — 0.5 is 0.50, and 50 hundredths beat 45.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** 1.2 + 2.3 = ______.
a) 3.5  b) 3.4  c) 2.5
**Answer: a) 3.5** — Ghana's B4 teacher manual works this sum
(L62–73).

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** 0.3 × 0.4 = ______.
a) 1.2  b) 0.12  c) 0.7
**Answer: b) 0.12** — one place plus one place is two places.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** 4.8 ÷ 0.6 = ______.
a) 0.8  b) 8  c) 80
**Answer: b) 8** — make the divisor whole: 48 ÷ 6 = 8.

**7. (Sub lesson 3)** 2.5 × 10 = ______.
a) 0.25  b) 25  c) 250
**Answer: b) 25** — the point moves one place right.
