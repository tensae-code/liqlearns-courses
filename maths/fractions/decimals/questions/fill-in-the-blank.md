# Decimals — fill in the blank

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. Fill each blank with a word from the box.

**Box:** tenths · hundredths · thousandths · point · places · divisor

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The first place after the point is ______.
**Answer: tenths.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** 0.07 is seven ______.
**Answer: hundredths.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** 0.009 is nine ______.
**Answer: thousandths.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Line up the ______ when you add decimals.
**Answer: point.**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** When you multiply decimals, count the ______.
**Answer: places.**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** Make the ______ whole before you divide.
**Answer: divisor.**
