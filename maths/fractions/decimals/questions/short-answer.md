# Decimals — short answer

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Level 1)** 7/100 written as a decimal is ______.
**Answer:** 0.07.

**2. (Level 1)** 0.6 written as a fraction (simplify it) is
______.
**Answer:** 3/5.

**3. (Level 2)** 5.6 − 2.35 = ______
**Answer:** 3.25.

**4. (Level 2)** Round 3.47 to one decimal place.
**Answer:** 3.5.

**5. (Level 3)** 1.5 × 4 = ______
**Answer:** 6.

**6. (Level 3)** 3.66 ÷ 0.3 = ______ (Egypt, Grade 6 Term 2,
Unit 8, L4).
**Answer:** 12.2.
