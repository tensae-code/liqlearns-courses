# Decimals — ordering

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Decimals Tenths, Hundredths, Thousandths · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Decimals Comparing, Ordering, Rounding, Adding, Subtracting · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Decimals Multiplying, Dividing, and by 10, 100, 1000.

Put each set in order, smallest first.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** 0.7 · 0.07 · 0.77 · 0.17
**Answer:** 0.07, 0.17, 0.7, 0.77.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** 0.45 · 0.5 · 0.405 · 0.54
**Answer:** 0.405, 0.45, 0.5, 0.54.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** 0.2 × 0.5 · 0.2 × 0.3 · 0.2 × 0.6 · 0.2 × 0.4
**Answer:** 0.2 × 0.3, 0.2 × 0.4, 0.2 × 0.5, 0.2 × 0.6
(0.06, 0.08, 0.10, 0.12).

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** 2.5 × 10 · 2.5 ÷ 100 · 2.5 × 100 · 2.5 ÷ 10
**Answer:** 2.5 ÷ 100, 2.5 ÷ 10, 2.5 × 10, 2.5 × 100
(0.025, 0.25, 25, 250).
