# Decimals — ordering

Put each set in order, smallest first.

**1. (Level 1)** 0.7 · 0.07 · 0.77 · 0.17
**Answer:** 0.07, 0.17, 0.7, 0.77.

**2. (Level 2)** 0.45 · 0.5 · 0.405 · 0.54
**Answer:** 0.405, 0.45, 0.5, 0.54.

**3. (Level 3)** 0.2 × 0.5 · 0.2 × 0.3 · 0.2 × 0.6 · 0.2 × 0.4
**Answer:** 0.2 × 0.3, 0.2 × 0.4, 0.2 × 0.5, 0.2 × 0.6
(0.06, 0.08, 0.10, 0.12).

**4. (Level 3)** 2.5 × 10 · 2.5 ÷ 100 · 2.5 × 100 · 2.5 ÷ 10
**Answer:** 2.5 ÷ 100, 2.5 ÷ 10, 2.5 × 10, 2.5 × 100
(0.025, 0.25, 25, 250).
