# Video script — Decimals

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Look! One strip. Cut it into ten!
One tenth… two tenths… three tenths!
Three tenths is 0.3! Point three!

One half is 0.5! One quarter is 0.25!
Same number! Two writings!

Add them! 1.2 plus 2.3!
Point under point! One… two… three point five!
1.2 + 2.3 = 3.5!

Multiply! 0.3 times 0.4!
Count the places! One… and one… is two!
0.12! Twelve hundredths!

Now you. What is 0.7? Seven tenths!
What is 0.25? One quarter! You did it!
