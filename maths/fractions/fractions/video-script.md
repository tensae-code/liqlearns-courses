# Video script — Fractions

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Look! One bread. I cut it. One half… two halves!
Two pieces, the same size. That is a fraction!

One more cut. Now four pieces. Four quarters!
One quarter is small. One half is big.
The pieces are small when there are many of them!

Here is a sweet box. Six sweets. Three friends.
Two sweets each. One friend gets one third of the sweets!
One third — one of three.

Write it: 3 over 4. Three quarters!
The number down below says: the whole is cut into 4.
The number up above says: we take 3.

One half… two quarters… four eighths!
Same amount! Same! Different writing!

Now you. Fold your paper. Cut your bread.
How many pieces? What is one piece called?
That is a fraction — a share of a whole!
