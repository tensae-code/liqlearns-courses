# Fractions — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Halves, Quarters, and What a Fraction Says · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Fractions of Sets, the Number Line, Comparing · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Equal Fractions, and Fractions Bigger Than One.

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A shape is split into 2 equal parts and 1 part is
shaded. The fraction shaded is ______.
a) 1/4  b) 1/2  c) 2/1
**Answer: b) 1/2.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In the fraction 3/4, the number 4 is the ______.
a) numerator  b) denominator  c) whole
**Answer: b) denominator** — it says into how many equal parts
the whole was split (Armenia, Grade 4, Part II §3, p.86).

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Which one is a unit fraction?
a) 2/3  b) 1/5  c) 3/4
**Answer: b) 1/5** — a unit fraction has numerator 1.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** 1/2 of 10 apples is ______ apples.
a) 2  b) 5  c) 20
**Answer: b) 5.**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Which fraction is equal to 1/2?
a) 2/4  b) 2/3  c) 3/4
**Answer: a) 2/4** — Egypt's equivalent-fractions chapter
(Grade 3 Term 2, Ch.10, p.67).

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** Which one is an improper fraction?
a) 2/3  b) 5/4  c) 1/2
**Answer: b) 5/4** — its numerator is bigger than its
denominator (Kazakhstan, Grade 5, §3.3, p.142).

**7. (Sub lesson 3)** 2 1/3 written as an improper fraction is ______.
a) 5/3  b) 7/3  c) 6/3
**Answer: b) 7/3** — 2 × 3 + 1 = 7 thirds.
