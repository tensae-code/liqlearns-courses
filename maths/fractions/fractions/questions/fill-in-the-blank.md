# Fractions — fill in the blank

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Halves, Quarters, and What a Fraction Says · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Fractions of Sets, the Number Line, Comparing · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Equal Fractions, and Fractions Bigger Than One.

Fill each blank with a word from the box.

**Box:** half · quarter · numerator · denominator · equivalent · mixed

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** 1/2 is read "one ______."
**Answer: half.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In 5/8, the 5 is the ______.
**Answer: numerator.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** In 5/8, the 8 is the ______.
**Answer: denominator.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** 1/4 is one ______.
**Answer: quarter.**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** 2/4 and 1/2 are ______ fractions.
**Answer: equivalent.**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** 1 1/2 is a ______ number.
**Answer: mixed.**
