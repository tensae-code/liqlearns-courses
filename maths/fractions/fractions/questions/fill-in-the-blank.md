# Fractions — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with a word from the box.

**Box:** half · quarter · numerator · denominator · equivalent · mixed

**1. (Level 1)** 1/2 is read "one ______."
**Answer: half.**

**2. (Level 1)** In 5/8, the 5 is the ______.
**Answer: numerator.**

**3. (Level 2)** In 5/8, the 8 is the ______.
**Answer: denominator.**

**4. (Level 2)** 1/4 is one ______.
**Answer: quarter.**

**5. (Level 3)** 2/4 and 1/2 are ______ fractions.
**Answer: equivalent.**

**6. (Level 3)** 1 1/2 is a ______ number.
**Answer: mixed.**
