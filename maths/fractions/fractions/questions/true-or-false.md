# Fractions — true or false

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Halves, Quarters, and What a Fraction Says · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Fractions of Sets, the Number Line, Comparing · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Equal Fractions, and Fractions Bigger Than One.

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The parts of a fraction must be equal.
**True** — Jordan's Grade 1 book starts with congruent (equal)
parts (Unit 9, L1, p.50).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In 2/5, the denominator is 2.
**False** — 2 is the numerator; the denominator is 5.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** 1/3 is bigger than 1/2.
**False** — a third-piece is smaller than a half-piece.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** 3/3 is equal to one whole.
**True** — three thirds fill the whole.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** 4/3 is a proper fraction.
**False** — 4/3 is improper: its numerator is bigger than its
denominator.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** 1/2 = 3/6.
**True** — they are equivalent fractions.
