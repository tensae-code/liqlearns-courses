# Fractions — ordering

Put each set in order, smallest first, unless the question says
otherwise.

**1. (Level 1)** 1/4 · 1/2 · 1/3
**Answer:** 1/4, 1/3, 1/2 — the fewer the parts, the bigger each
piece.

**2. (Level 2)** 2/5 · 4/5 · 1/5 · 3/5
**Answer:** 1/5, 2/5, 3/5, 4/5 — same denominator, bigger
numerator wins.

**3. (Level 3)** 3/4 · 1/2 · 1/4 · 1
**Answer:** 1/4, 1/2, 3/4, 1.

**4. (Level 3)** 5/4 · 1 1/2 · 3/4 · 2
**Answer:** 3/4, 5/4, 1 1/2, 2 — 5/4 is 1 1/4, which sits
between 3/4 and 1 1/2.
