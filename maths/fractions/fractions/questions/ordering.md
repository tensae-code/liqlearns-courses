# Fractions — ordering

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Halves, Quarters, and What a Fraction Says · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Fractions of Sets, the Number Line, Comparing · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Equal Fractions, and Fractions Bigger Than One.

Put each set in order, smallest first, unless the question says
otherwise.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** 1/4 · 1/2 · 1/3
**Answer:** 1/4, 1/3, 1/2 — the fewer the parts, the bigger each
piece.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** 2/5 · 4/5 · 1/5 · 3/5
**Answer:** 1/5, 2/5, 3/5, 4/5 — same denominator, bigger
numerator wins.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** 3/4 · 1/2 · 1/4 · 1
**Answer:** 1/4, 1/2, 3/4, 1.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** 5/4 · 1 1/2 · 3/4 · 2
**Answer:** 3/4, 5/4, 1 1/2, 2 — 5/4 is 1 1/4, which sits
between 3/4 and 1 1/2.
