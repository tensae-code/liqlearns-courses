# Fractions — short answer

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Halves, Quarters, and What a Fraction Says · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Fractions of Sets, the Number Line, Comparing · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Equal Fractions, and Fractions Bigger Than One.

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Write one half as a fraction.
**Answer:** 1/2.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the numerator of a fraction tell you?
**Answer:** How many of the equal parts are taken.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Write three quarters as a fraction.
**Answer:** 3/4.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Find 1/3 of 12.
**Answer:** 4.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Write 7/4 as a mixed number.
**Answer:** 1 3/4.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** A whole is shared into 8 equal parts and 3 are
taken. Write the fraction.
**Answer:** 3/8.
