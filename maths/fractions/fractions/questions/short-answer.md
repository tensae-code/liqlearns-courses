# Fractions — short answer

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Level 1)** Write one half as a fraction.
**Answer:** 1/2.

**2. (Level 1)** What does the numerator of a fraction tell you?
**Answer:** How many of the equal parts are taken.

**3. (Level 2)** Write three quarters as a fraction.
**Answer:** 3/4.

**4. (Level 2)** Find 1/3 of 12.
**Answer:** 4.

**5. (Level 3)** Write 7/4 as a mixed number.
**Answer:** 1 3/4.

**6. (Level 3)** A whole is shared into 8 equal parts and 3 are
taken. Write the fraction.
**Answer:** 3/8.
