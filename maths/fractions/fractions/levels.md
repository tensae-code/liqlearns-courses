# Fractions — questions by level

Practice by level, tied to the skill number. The questions live in
the type files; this file is the by-level view. The levels are the
depths inside this one thing — what a fraction is — set by the
pattern across the countries' books: halves and quarters first,
then reading, writing and comparing, then equivalence and the
forms bigger than one.

## Level 1 — halves, quarters, and what a fraction says
Level 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: split a whole
into equal parts, name a half and a quarter, read and write a
fraction, and say what the numerator and the denominator each
mean.

## Level 2 — fractions of sets, the number line, comparing
Level 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: find a fraction
of a set, place fractions on a number line, and compare two
fractions (same denominator, same numerator).

## Level 3 — equal fractions, and fractions bigger than one
Level 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: find fractions
equal to a given fraction, tell proper, improper and mixed numbers
apart and move between improper and mixed, and find a fraction of
a number and a number from its fraction.
