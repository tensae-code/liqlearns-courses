# Multiplying and Dividing Fractions — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — A Fraction Times a Whole Number, and "Of" · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — A Fraction Times a Fraction, and Mixed Numbers · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Dividing Fractions.

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** 3 × 2/5 = ______.
a) 6/5  b) 6/15  c) 5/6
**Answer: a) 6/5** — 3 × 2/5 is 2/5 + 2/5 + 2/5.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** 2/5 of 30 is ______.
a) 6  b) 12  c) 15
**Answer: b) 12.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** 1/2 × 1/3 = ______.
a) 1/5  b) 2/5  c) 1/6
**Answer: c) 1/6.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** 2/3 × 3/4 = ______.
a) 5/7  b) 1/2  c) 6/7
**Answer: b) 1/2** — cancel first: the 3s cancel, 2/4 is 1/2.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** 3 ÷ 1/2 = ______.
a) 1 1/2  b) 6  c) 2/3
**Answer: b) 6** — each whole holds two halves.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** The reciprocal of 4/5 is ______.
a) 5/4  b) 4/5  c) 1/4
**Answer: a) 5/4** — flip it over.

**7. (Sub lesson 3)** 2/3 ÷ 1/6 = ______.
a) 1/9  b) 2  c) 4
**Answer: c) 4** — 2/3 × 6/1 = 12/3 = 4.
