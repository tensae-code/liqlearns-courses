# Multiplying and Dividing Fractions — true or false

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — A Fraction Times a Whole Number, and "Of" · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — A Fraction Times a Fraction, and Mixed Numbers · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Dividing Fractions.

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** 2 × 3/4 = 1 1/2.
**True** — 2 × 3/4 is 6/4, which is 1 1/2.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** 1/2 of 1/2 is 1.
**False** — half of a half is 1/4.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** 2/3 × 3/4 = 1/2.
**True** — top times top, bottom times bottom, then simplify.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** To multiply mixed numbers, multiply the wholes
and the fractions separately.
**False** — make them improper first, then multiply.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** 4 ÷ 1/2 = 2.
**False** — 4 ÷ 1/2 is 8: each whole holds two halves.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** Dividing by a fraction smaller than 1 gives a
bigger answer.
**True** — the pieces are smaller, so more of them fit.
