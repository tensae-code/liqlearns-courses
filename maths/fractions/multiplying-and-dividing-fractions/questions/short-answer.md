# Multiplying and Dividing Fractions — short answer

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Level 1)** 4 × 1/3 = ______ (as a mixed number).
**Answer:** 1 1/3.

**2. (Level 1)** 3/4 of 20 = ______
**Answer:** 15.

**3. (Level 2)** 3/5 × 5/6 = ______
**Answer:** 1/2.

**4. (Level 2)** 1 1/2 × 2/3 = ______
**Answer:** 1.

**5. (Level 3)** 1/4 ÷ 2 = ______
**Answer:** 1/8.

**6. (Level 3)** 2/5 of a number is 10. What is the number?
**Answer:** 25.
