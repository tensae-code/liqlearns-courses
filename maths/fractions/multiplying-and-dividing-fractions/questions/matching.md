# Multiplying and Dividing Fractions — matching

Match each sum to its answer.

**Set 1 (Level 1)** — fraction times a whole number, and "of".
1. 2 × 1/3 — **2/3**
2. 3 × 2/5 — **1 1/5**
3. 1/2 of 10 — **5**
4. 1/4 of 16 — **4**
5. 2/3 of 9 — **6**

**Set 2 (Level 2)** — fraction times a fraction.
1. 1/2 × 1/4 — **1/8**
2. 2/3 × 3/5 — **2/5**
3. 3/4 × 4/5 — **3/5**
4. 1 1/2 × 2/3 — **1**
5. 2 1/3 × 3/7 — **1**

**Set 3 (Level 3)** — dividing fractions.
1. 3 ÷ 1/2 — **6**
2. 1/2 ÷ 2 — **1/4**
3. 2/3 ÷ 1/3 — **2**
4. 5/6 ÷ 5/6 — **1**
5. 1/3 ÷ 4 — **1/12**

**Set 4 (Level 3)** — reciprocals.
1. reciprocal of 2/3 — **3/2**
2. reciprocal of 5 — **1/5**
3. reciprocal of 1/4 — **4**
4. reciprocal of 7/2 — **2/7**
5. reciprocal of 1 — **1**
