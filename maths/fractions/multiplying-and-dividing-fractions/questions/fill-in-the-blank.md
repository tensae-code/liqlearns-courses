# Multiplying and Dividing Fractions — fill in the blank

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — A Fraction Times a Whole Number, and "Of" · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — A Fraction Times a Fraction, and Mixed Numbers · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Dividing Fractions.

Fill each blank with a word from the box.

**Box:** multiply · reciprocal · cancel · improper · divide · flip

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In fractions, "of" means ______.
**Answer: multiply.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** ______ before you multiply, to keep the numbers
small.
**Answer: cancel.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Multiply mixed numbers by making them ______
first.
**Answer: improper.**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** To divide by a fraction, ______ it and multiply.
**Answer: flip.**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** The flipped fraction is called the ______.
**Answer: reciprocal.**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** To ______ a whole number by a unit fraction,
count how many pieces fit in the whole.
**Answer: divide.**
