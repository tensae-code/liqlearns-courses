# Multiplying and Dividing Fractions — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with a word from the box.

**Box:** multiply · reciprocal · cancel · improper · divide · flip

**1. (Level 1)** In fractions, "of" means ______.
**Answer: multiply.**

**2. (Level 2)** ______ before you multiply, to keep the numbers
small.
**Answer: cancel.**

**3. (Level 2)** Multiply mixed numbers by making them ______
first.
**Answer: improper.**

**4. (Level 3)** To divide by a fraction, ______ it and multiply.
**Answer: flip.**

**5. (Level 3)** The flipped fraction is called the ______.
**Answer: reciprocal.**

**6. (Level 3)** To ______ a whole number by a unit fraction,
count how many pieces fit in the whole.
**Answer: divide.**
