# Lesson plan — Multiplying and Dividing Fractions

**Course:** Maths · **Unit:** 4, Fractions, Decimals and Percentages · **Lesson:** Multiplying and Dividing Fractions
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 13 of the ladder — the third
lesson of Unit 4 (CURRICULUM.md). It stands on the Fractions
lesson (what a fraction is) and on Adding and Subtracting
Fractions.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** multiplying and dividing
fractions — a fraction times a whole number, a fraction times a
fraction, mixed numbers, dividing by flipping and multiplying,
and whole numbers with unit fractions.
**This lesson's own level:** it needs the Fractions lesson and
the adding/subtracting lesson, and nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the
exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an
image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture
of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does
with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and
while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is
dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the
student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown
changing — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds per fold.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees
from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before
it is proved (the gate: README, "Units, Lessons and Sub Lessons
(ruled 2026-11-08)"):

1. **A Fraction Times a Whole Number, and "Of"** — multiplying a fraction by a whole number, and finding a fraction of a number. (Was depth Level 1.)
2. **A Fraction Times a Fraction, and Mixed Numbers** — multiplying a fraction by a fraction, cancelling first, and multiplying mixed numbers made improper. (Was depth Level 2.)
3. **Dividing Fractions** — dividing fractions by the reciprocal, the easy divisions, and finding a number from its fraction. (Was depth Level 3.)
Note: this sub lesson's last part revisits a fraction of a number (Sub lesson 1's "of") on its way to finding the whole from its fraction — the part is kept whole here, in the order the books run it.
Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson (no hardness ranking is written in this lesson's question files).

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the Fractions lesson (Level 11) and Adding
  and Subtracting Fractions (Level 12). Recommended, never forced.
- Decimals are the next lesson — this lesson only multiplies and
  divides fractions.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Multiply a fraction by a whole number, as repeated addition.
2. Multiply a fraction by a fraction, cancelling first.
3. Multiply mixed numbers by making them improper first.
4. Divide fractions by multiplying by the reciprocal.
5. Divide a whole number by a unit fraction, and a unit fraction
   by a whole number.
6. Find a fraction of a number, and a number from its fraction.

## What you need
Paper rectangles to shade, a ruler, pencil and paper.

2. **The explanation** — fraction × whole number; fraction ×
   fraction; mixed numbers; dividing by the reciprocal; whole

## If the learner is stuck
- Divides by a fraction and gets a smaller answer → check: the
  divisor is smaller than 1, so the answer must be bigger than
  the dividend. If it is smaller, the fraction was not flipped.
- Forgets to flip → the rule is one sentence: turn it round and
  multiply. Write the flipped fraction first, then multiply.
- Multiplies mixed numbers as they stand → make them improper
  first. 1 1/2 is 3/2, never 1 × 1/2.
- Cancels across the wrong way → cancel top-to-bottom or
  diagonally, never across one fraction's own top and bottom
  (Sierra Leone JSS 1: cancelling top-to-bottom or diagonally,
  never across).
