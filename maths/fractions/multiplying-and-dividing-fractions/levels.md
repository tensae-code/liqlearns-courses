# Multiplying and Dividing Fractions — questions by level

Practice by level, tied to the skill number. The questions live in
the type files; this file is the by-level view. The levels are the
depths inside this one thing — multiplying and dividing fractions —
set by the pattern across the countries' books: fraction times a
whole number first, then fraction times fraction, then division
by the reciprocal.

## Level 1 — fraction times a whole number, and "of"
Level 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: multiply a
fraction by a whole number, and find a fraction of a number.

## Level 2 — fraction times a fraction, and mixed numbers
Level 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: multiply a
fraction by a fraction (cancelling first), and multiply mixed
numbers by making them improper first.

## Level 3 — dividing fractions
Level 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: divide fractions
by multiplying by the reciprocal, divide whole numbers by unit
fractions and unit fractions by whole numbers, and find a number
from its fraction.
