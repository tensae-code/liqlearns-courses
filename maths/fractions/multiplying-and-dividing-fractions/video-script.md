# Video script — Multiplying and Dividing Fractions

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Half a pizza. Now half of that half!
Fold it! One quarter! Half of a half is one quarter!

Two thirds of twelve? "Of" means times!
Twelve shared in three… four each… two shares is eight!
Two thirds of twelve is eight!

Now divide! Three pizzas. Cut them into halves!
One, two, three, four, five, six!
Three divided by one half… is SIX!
Dividing by a half makes it BIGGER!

Flip it! Two thirds… flip… three halves!
Multiply now! That is dividing!

Now you. One half of one half? Say it!
One quarter! You did it!
