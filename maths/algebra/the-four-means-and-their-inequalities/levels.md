# Levels — The Four Means and Their Inequalities

One lesson, one level (Level 156). The depth layers inside it are ranks.

## Depth layers

- **Layer 1 — The four definitions.** State all four means for positive numbers — arithmetic, geometric, harmonic, quadratic (root-mean-square) — and compute each one for a pair of numbers. Pass mark: 4 of 5.
- **Layer 2 — The chain.** State the order QM ≥ AM ≥ GM ≥ HM and place a computed set of four means in it; spot a claimed order that breaks the chain. Pass mark: 4 of 5.
- **Layer 3 — Equality.** Say when the four means are all equal, verify it on an equal set, and predict how the chain opens as a set spreads out. Pass mark: 4 of 5.
- **Layer 4 — Three or more numbers.** Compute the four means for a triple of positive numbers and check the chain holds. Pass mark: 4 of 5.
- **Layer 5 — Using the chain.** Turn the chain into a bound: greatest product for a fixed sum (AM ≥ GM), and quick estimates that replace a hard mean by an easier one on the correct side. Pass mark: 4 of 5.

## What "maxed" means here

The learner can define and compute all four means, can recite and use the chain QM ≥ AM ≥ GM ≥ HM, knows it is proved in North Macedonia's Grade 12 Algebra book (§3.4) with QM ≥ AM proved first, and can call on the equality case — all means equal exactly when all the numbers are equal — the exact skills that book's mean-values unit teaches (printed p. 58).
