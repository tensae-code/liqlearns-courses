**Acceptance count: taught in 5 countries (North Macedonia, Uzbekistan, Ecuador, Bolivia, Tajikistan).** North Macedonia is the one read country whose course defines all four means side by side and proves the chain between them: its Grade 12 Algebra elective, in the mean-values unit (§3.4, printed p. 58), sets the quadratic, arithmetic, geometric and harmonic means of positive numbers next to each other and proves QM ≥ AM ≥ GM ≥ HM, opening with the proof of QM ≥ AM. Elsewhere in the library "the mean" is the single arithmetic mean of data handling (the averages-and-spread lesson), and the inequalities lesson carries named single inequalities — China's Cauchy–Schwarz as an exploration exercise — but no means and no chain, so under the everything-goes-in ruling (2026-11-06) the topic gets its own lesson, built at the size of its source.
