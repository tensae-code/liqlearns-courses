1. (Sub lesson 2) Put the four means of any set of positive numbers in order, largest first: (i) harmonic (ii) quadratic (iii) geometric (iv) arithmetic.
   **Answer: (ii), (iv), (iii), (i)** — QM ≥ AM ≥ GM ≥ HM, the chain proved in the book. North Macedonia, АЛГЕБРА ЗА ТРЕТА ГОДИНА (Grade 12, elective), §3.4, printed p. 58.

2. (Sub lessons 1–2) For the pair 1 and 9, put the four means in order, smallest first: (i) 5 (ii) 3 (iii) 1.8 (iv) √41 ≈ 6.40.
   **Answer: (iii), (ii), (i), (iv)** — HM = 1.8 ≤ GM = 3 ≤ AM = 5 ≤ QM = √41. North Macedonia, АЛГЕБРА ЗА ТРЕТА ГОДИНА (Grade 12, elective), §3.4, printed p. 58.
