1. (Sub lessons 1–3) Match each mean of the pair 2 and 8 to its value:
   (a) arithmetic mean
   (b) geometric mean
   (c) harmonic mean
   (d) quadratic mean

   (i) 3.2
   (ii) 4
   (iii) 5
   (iv) √34 ≈ 5.83

   **Answer: (a)–(iii), (b)–(ii), (c)–(i), (d)–(iv)** — and the matched values themselves stand in chain order HM ≤ GM ≤ AM ≤ QM. North Macedonia, АЛГЕБРА ЗА ТРЕТА ГОДИНА (Grade 12, elective), §3.4, printed p. 58.

2. (Sub lessons 1–2) Match each mean to its recipe for two positive numbers a and b:
   (a) harmonic mean
   (b) quadratic mean
   (c) geometric mean
   (d) arithmetic mean

   (i) (a + b)/2
   (ii) √(ab)
   (iii) 2ab/(a + b)
   (iv) √((a² + b²)/2)

   **Answer: (a)–(iii), (b)–(iv), (c)–(ii), (d)–(i).** North Macedonia, АЛГЕБРА ЗА ТРЕТА ГОДИНА (Grade 12, elective), §3.4, printed p. 58.
