1. (Sub lesson 1) The mean √((a² + b²)/2) is called the ______ mean (its other name is the root-mean-square).
   **Answer: quadratic.** North Macedonia, АЛГЕБРА ЗА ТРЕТА ГОДИНА (Grade 12, elective), §3.4, printed p. 58.

2. (Sub lesson 1) The geometric mean of 4 and 16 is ______.
   **Answer: 8** — √(4·16) = √64. North Macedonia, АЛГЕБРА ЗА ТРЕТА ГОДИНА (Grade 12, elective), §3.4, printed p. 58.

3. (Sub lesson 2) In the chain of the four means, the ______ mean is always the largest and the harmonic mean always the smallest.
   **Answer: quadratic.** North Macedonia, АЛГЕБРА ЗА ТРЕТА ГОДИНА (Grade 12, elective), §3.4, printed p. 58.

4. (Sub lesson 3) For the numbers 5 and 5, all four means equal ______.
   **Answer: 5.** North Macedonia, АЛГЕБРА ЗА ТРЕТА ГОДИНА (Grade 12, elective), §3.4, printed p. 58.

5. (Sub lesson 4) Two positive numbers have sum 10. By AM ≥ GM, their product is at most ______.
   **Answer: 25** — AM = 5 ≥ GM = √(product). North Macedonia, АЛГЕБРА ЗА ТРЕТА ГОДИНА (Grade 12, elective), §3.4, printed p. 58.
