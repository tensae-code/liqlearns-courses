# Multiple choice — The Four Means and Their Inequalities

1. (Layer 1) The harmonic mean of 2 and 8 is:
   A. 5 B. 4 C. 3.2 D. 6.4
   **Answer: C** — HM = 2ab/(a + b) = 32/10 = 3.2. North Macedonia, АЛГЕБРА ЗА ТРЕТА ГОДИНА (Grade 12, elective), §3.4, printed p. 58.

2. (Layer 1) The quadratic mean (root-mean-square) of 2 and 8 is:
   A. 5 B. √34 C. √17 D. 34
   **Answer: B** — QM = √((2² + 8²)/2) = √(68/2) = √34 ≈ 5.83. North Macedonia, АЛГЕБРА ЗА ТРЕТА ГОДИНА (Grade 12, elective), §3.4, printed p. 58.

3. (Layer 2) For any set of positive numbers, the four means always satisfy:
   A. HM ≥ GM ≥ AM ≥ QM B. QM ≥ AM ≥ GM ≥ HM C. AM ≥ QM ≥ HM ≥ GM D. GM ≥ QM ≥ AM ≥ HM
   **Answer: B** — the chain, proved in the book with QM ≥ AM proved first. North Macedonia, АЛГЕБРА ЗА ТРЕТА ГОДИНА (Grade 12, elective), §3.4, printed p. 58.

4. (Layer 3) The four means of a set of positive numbers are all equal exactly when:
   A. the set has two numbers B. the sum is 10 C. all the numbers are equal D. the product is a perfect square
   **Answer: C** — equality anywhere in the chain forces all the numbers to be equal; for 3, 3, 3 every mean is 3. North Macedonia, АЛГЕБРА ЗА ТРЕТА ГОДИНА (Grade 12, elective), §3.4, printed p. 58.

5. (Layer 5) Two positive numbers have sum 20. Their product is at most:
   A. 40 B. 80 C. 100 D. 400
   **Answer: C** — by AM ≥ GM: 10 = AM ≥ GM = √(product), so product ≤ 100, reached at 10 and 10. North Macedonia, АЛГЕБРА ЗА ТРЕТА ГОДИНА (Grade 12, elective), §3.4, printed p. 58.
