1. (Sub lesson 2) For positive numbers, the geometric mean is never bigger than the arithmetic mean.
   **Answer: True** — AM ≥ GM is the middle link of the chain QM ≥ AM ≥ GM ≥ HM. North Macedonia, АЛГЕБРА ЗА ТРЕТА ГОДИНА (Grade 12, elective), §3.4, printed p. 58.

2. (Sub lesson 1) The harmonic mean of 2 and 8 is 5.
   **Answer: False** — 5 is the arithmetic mean; the harmonic mean is 2·2·8/(2 + 8) = 3.2. North Macedonia, АЛГЕБРА ЗА ТРЕТА ГОДИНА (Grade 12, elective), §3.4, printed p. 58.

3. (Sub lesson 3) If the four means of a set are all different, the set's numbers cannot all be equal.
   **Answer: True** — equal numbers make all four means equal to that number; different means mean a spread set. North Macedonia, АЛГЕБРА ЗА ТРЕТА ГОДИНА (Grade 12, elective), §3.4, printed p. 58.

4. (Sub lesson 2) The quadratic mean of a set of positive numbers can be smaller than its harmonic mean.
   **Answer: False** — QM is the top of the chain and HM the bottom: QM ≥ AM ≥ GM ≥ HM always. North Macedonia, АЛГЕБРА ЗА ТРЕТА ГОДИНА (Grade 12, elective), §3.4, printed p. 58.

5. (Sub lesson 1) For 1, 3, 9 the geometric mean is 3.
   **Answer: True** — GM = (1·3·9)^(1/3) = 27^(1/3) = 3. North Macedonia, АЛГЕБРА ЗА ТРЕТА ГОДИНА (Grade 12, elective), §3.4, printed p. 58.
