# Levels — Factorisation

One lesson, one level (Level 152). The depth layers inside it are ranks.

## Depth layers

- **Layer 1 — Common factors.** Say what factorisation is (the reverse of expanding) and factorise by taking out the common factor, leaving irreducible factors. Pass mark: 4 of 5.
- **Layer 2 — Regrouping and identities.** Factorise four-term expressions by regrouping, and recognise and use the identity forms a² ± 2ab + b² and a² − b². Pass mark: 4 of 5.
- **Layer 3 — Trinomials.** Factorise x² + bx + c into (x + a)(x + b), taking a common factor out first when there is one; and spot the planted errors in worked lines set for correction. Pass mark: 4 of 5.
- **Layer 4 — Quadratics and cubics (Class 9).** Factorise ax² + bx + c by splitting the middle term, and factorise a cubic by finding one zero with the Factor Theorem, dividing, and factorising the quotient. Pass mark: 4 of 5.

## What "maxed" means here

The learner can take an algebraic expression and write it as a product of irreducible factors by whichever method fits — common factor, regrouping, identities, middle-term splitting, or the Factor Theorem — and can expand the answer back to check it, the exact ladder India's Class 8 factorisation chapter climbs (printed pp. 217–226) with its Class 9 continuation (printed pp. 41–42).
