1. (Sub lessons 1–4) Match each expression to its factorisation:
   (a) 5x² + 15x
   (b) x² − 9
   (c) x² + 5x + 6
   (d) m² − 6m + 9
   (e) x³ − 6x² + 11x − 6

   Factorisations: (i) (m − 3)² (ii) (x + 3)(x − 3) (iii) 5x(x + 3) (iv) (x − 1)(x − 2)(x − 3) (v) (x + 2)(x + 3)

   **Answer: (a)–(iii), (b)–(ii), (c)–(v), (d)–(i), (e)–(iv).** India, Mathematics Class 8, printed pp. 217, 220, 221; Mathematics Class 9, printed p. 42.
