Other read countries teach factorisation as a part inside their expressions chapters, and their merges stocked it there; India's own Class 9 identity work ((x + y + z)² and x³ + y³ + z³ − 3xyz, applied to factorise) is likewise stocked in expressions, and its Class 8 division of algebraic expressions in polynomial-division. One slip in the Class 8 chapter is taught corrected: the chapter summary (printed p. 229) prints the trinomial form as "x² + (a + b) + ab", dropping the x — the correct form x² + (a + b)x + ab stands in the formula list on the same page and is the form this lesson teaches. The §14.5 "Can you find the error?" page (printed p. 226) is the book's deliberate wrong-work exercise, not a book error. North Macedonia enters from its main set — Grade 11 (2026) and the Grade 12 Algebra elective.

Libya carries factorisation in Grade 9, Chapter 1, where expansion and factorisation are taught as one chapter (stocked by the 2026-11-08 re-verification sweep).

Venezuela enters through the Colección Bicentenario, the MPPE's one-book-per-grade course: *Conciencia Matemática* (Grade 8) and *La Matemática y el Vivir Bien* (Grade 11). From its books: from Grade 8: geometric derivation — (a + b)² built from a square of side a + b split into a², b² and two rectangles ab; from Grade 11: every real polynomial splits into linear factors and irreducible quadratic factors (stated as form A).

Afghanistan enters through its Ministry of Education course — the Grade 1 course in two language editions (Pashto and Dari, one work with identical page maps) and Dari student books for Grades 4, 6, 7, 8, 10, 11 and 12, all printing year 1398 HS except the Grade 8 book, which prints 1399 HS on its cover and title page and 1398 HS on its specifications page. From its books: from Grade 8: factorising algebraic expressions; from Grade 10: factor theorem.

Albania enters through its Mediaprint teacher's books (Libër Mësuesi, in Albanian) — TEACHER EVIDENCE only: no pupil's book is in hand for any grade, so Albania is never cited as a pupil-textbook source; what the teacher's books evidence is what is taught and in what order, through their lesson plans, worked answers and answer keys. From its books: from Grade 8 (teacher's book): .3 Factorising expressions (plan 32). from Grade 9 (teacher's book): Factorising given expressions; isolating a letter in a formula; index rules in simplification; Expanding double brackets; squaring (x+1); solving equations from expanded forms. from Grade 10 (teacher's book): Expanding single brackets and factorising; area model for 5a(2a + 9) = 10a² + 45a; Double brackets, difference of two squares, factorising x² + bx + c. from Grade 12 (teacher's book): Factorisation: factorise linear, quadratic and simple cubic expressions; difference of two squares; exercises…; Teorema e faktorëve (The factor theorem, used on a cubic expression).

Bolivia enters through the Área Matemática sections of its Ministerio de Educación (Estado Plurinacional de Bolivia) Textos de Aprendizaje — the student books for Grades 7, 8, 10 and 12, each the maths section of that year's integrated year book, keeping the whole book's printed foliation, with no imprint or ISBN printed in any of them — and through the Compendio para Maestras y Maestros 2023–2024, the ministry's teacher compilation of the official student texts for all six Secundaria years (Grades 7–12), cited as teacher-compilation evidence only, never as or in place of a pupil text; its Secundaria 3 and Secundaria 5 sections are the only in-hand source for Grades 9 and 11, whose student texts are not in the record. From its books: from the Compendium's Secundaria 3 section (Grade 9): Factorización de expresiones algebraicas; Factorización de expresiones algebraicas (second unit of the same title).

Tajikistan enters through its Ministry-approved pupil's textbooks in Tajik (Cyrillic), all published in Dushanbe — Математика for Grade 5 (Маориф, 2022) and Grade 6 (5th edition, Маориф, 2021), Алгебра for Grades 7 (4th improved edition, 2023) and 8 (4th edition, 2023), and Геометрия — the continuation of stereometry — for Grade 11 (3rd edition, Лавҳ, 2020). From its books: from Grade 7: Factorising by taking out the common factor; Factorising by grouping terms; Factorising the difference of two squares; Sum of cubes: a^3 + b^3 = (a + b)(a^2 - ab + b^2); Difference of cubes: a^3 - b^3 = (a - b)(a^2 + ab + b^2); Applying the sum- and difference-of-cubes formulas.

Armenia enters through its Algebra 7. From its books: factorising a polynomial; factorising a polynomial by the identities.
