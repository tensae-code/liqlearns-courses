# Levels — Linear Programming

One lesson, one level (Level 139). The depth layers inside it are ranks.

## Depth layers

- **Layer 1 — Write the limits.** Turn a situation's limits into linear inequalities, including x ≥ 0, y ≥ 0 where they belong, and read a given region against a system. Pass mark: 4 of 5.
- **Layer 2 — Draw the region.** Boundary lines solid or dashed by the inequality sign, a test point chosen, and the feasible region shaded so that every drawn inequality holds inside it. Pass mark: 5 of 6.
- **Layer 3 — Optimise.** State an objective function, list the region's corners, evaluate the objective at each, and give the maximum or minimum with its meaning in the situation. Pass mark: 4 of 5.

## What "maxed" means here

The learner can take a fresh two-quantity situation — a workshop, a farm, a stall — and carry it all the way: constraints, region, corners, objective, answer in words, without being handed any of the steps.
