# Hands-on — Linear Programming

## Activity 1 — The string region (15 minutes)

On squared paper, draw the axes and peg out the region x ≥ 0, y ≥ 0, x + y ≤ 10 with string or a rubber band on a pegboard. The learner names five whole-number points inside and checks each against all three inequalities, then finds two points that break exactly one limit each and says which one.

## Activity 2 — Stall-keeper (15 minutes)

A stall sells fruit cups (x) and salads (y). There are 40 cups' worth of fruit salad base... concretely: fruit allows x + y ≤ 40; ice allows 2x + y ≤ 60; both counts are ≥ 0. Shade the region, list the corners — (0, 0), (40, 0), (0, 40)... find the true corners by solving the pairs — then price cups at RM3 and salads at RM5 and find the best mix by testing corners.

## Activity 3 — Slide the profit line (10 minutes)

Draw lines of 3x + 5y = k for k = 60, 120, 180 over the Activity 2 region with a ruler. Watch the line move out as k grows and note the last corner it touches. The learner states, in their own words, why the optimum must be a corner.
