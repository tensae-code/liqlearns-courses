# Short answer — Linear Programming

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Layer 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Writing the Limits · (Layer 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Drawing the Feasible Region · (Layer 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Finding the Optimum.

1. (Sub lesson 2) For the system x ≥ 0, y ≥ 0, x + y ≤ 8, list the corners of the feasible region.
   **Answer: (0, 0), (8, 0), (0, 8).** Malaysia, Additional Mathematics Form 5, Ch. 7 §7.1, printed pp. 237–239.

2. (Sub lesson 3) With corners (0, 0), (6, 0), (4, 4), (0, 6), find the maximum of P = 3x + 2y.
   **Answer: P values 0, 18, 20, 12 — maximum 20 at (4, 4).** Malaysia, Additional Mathematics Form 5, Ch. 7 §7.2, printed pp. 242–244.

3. (Sub lesson 1) Write the inequality for: "a van can carry at most 500 kg; boxes weigh 20 kg (x of them) and sacks 50 kg (y of them)."
   **Answer: 20x + 50y ≤ 500.** Malaysia, Additional Mathematics Form 5, Ch. 7 §7.1, printed pp. 234–237.
