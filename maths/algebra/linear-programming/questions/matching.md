# Matching — Linear Programming

1. (Layer 1) Match each phrase to its inequality (x, y ≥ 0 understood):
   (a) "no more than 24 in total" (b) "at least 10 of x" (c) "twice x plus y cannot exceed 30" (d) "y is at least x"
   Inequalities: x ≥ 10, 2x + y ≤ 30, x + y ≤ 24, y ≥ x.
   **Answer: (a) x + y ≤ 24, (b) x ≥ 10, (c) 2x + y ≤ 30, (d) y ≥ x.** Malaysia, Additional Mathematics Form 5, Ch. 7 §7.3, printed pp. 234–237.
