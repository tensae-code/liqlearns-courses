# Fill in the blank — Linear Programming

1. (Layer 1) The quantity to be made largest or smallest is called the ___ function.
   **Answer: objective.** Malaysia, Additional Mathematics Form 5, Ch. 7 §7.3, printed pp. 240–242.

2. (Layer 2) The set of points satisfying ALL the inequalities at once is the ___ region.
   **Answer: feasible.** Malaysia, Additional Mathematics Form 5, Ch. 7 §7.3, printed pp. 237–240.

3. (Layer 3) The corner where two boundary lines 2x + y = 100 and x + y = 60 cross is (___ , ___).
   **Answer: (40, 20).** Malaysia, Additional Mathematics Form 5, Ch. 7 §7.3, printed pp. 237–240.
