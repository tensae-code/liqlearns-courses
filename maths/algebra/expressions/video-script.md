# Video script — Expressions

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A letter stands for a number we do not know yet.
3x means three times x. x is the VARIABLE.
The 3 is the COEFFICIENT — the number in front.

Like terms? Same letter, same power — they join!
3x plus 2x is 5x. But 3x plus 2y? They STAY apart.
Different letters never join!

Brackets? Multiply EVERYTHING inside!
2 times (x + 3) is 2x + 6. Not 2x + 3 — the 2 reaches the 3 too!

Minus before a bracket? FLIP every sign!
a minus (b minus c) is a minus b PLUS c.

Factorising is expanding BACKWARDS.
x squared plus 5x plus 6 is (x + 2) times (x + 3).
Expand it back — and check!
