**Acceptance count: taught in 14 countries (Kazakhstan, Jordan, Armenia, Greece, South Africa, Egypt, Libya, Vietnam, Ghana, Laos, Yemen, Indonesia, Nicaragua, Ethiopia).** Kazakhstan carries the fullest treatment — brackets and coefficients in Grade 6, then monomials, polynomials and the abridged-multiplication formulas in Grade 7. Jordan carries terms and expressions in Grade 7. Armenia carries letter expressions, monomials and polynomials in Grade 7. Greece carries the whole expressions chapter in Grade 9, including the notable identities. South Africa carries expressions as worksheet practice across Grades 8 and 9, from vocabulary to binomial products to factorising. Egypt carries expressions in Grade 6 and the four factorisation methods in Grade 8. Libya opens Grade 9 with expansions and factorisation. Vietnam carries one-variable polynomials in Grade 7 and multi-variable polynomials with identities and factorisation in Grade 8. Ghana carries only teacher-manual evidence (JICA Basic 5, Lessons 79–85). Mauritania is not in this lesson: its 1AS teaches no algebra, and its later algebra is filed at chapter level without the lesson-level pages this lesson pins. Laos carries expressions from recognising and expanding products in Grade 7, through notable products and factorisation in Grade 8, to polynomials and rational expressions in Grade 11. Yemen carries expressions from first symbolic expressions and substitution in Grade 6, through algebraic terms and expressions in Grade 7, to multiplying, dividing and factorising expressions and polynomials in Grades 8–10. Indonesia carries expressions from algebraic forms — variables, terms, coefficients, constants, the distributive property, factoring and like terms — in Grade 7 (Merdeka and K13 editions), to polynomials with division, factors, zeros and identities in the Grade 11 Tingkat Lanjut book. Nicaragua carries expressions from algebraic expressions with like terms in Grade 7, through polynomial operations in the Grade 8 student book, to notable products, factorisation and algebraic fractions in the Grade 8 module (module evidence), and notable products with factorisation in Grade 9 (cubes only as Desafío material there). Ethiopia carries expressions from working with variables in Grade 5 — growing patterns generalised with variables, expressions and terms, verbal phrases as expressions, collecting like terms and substitution — to algebraic expressions and terms with simplifying and writing expressions and equations in Grade 7.
