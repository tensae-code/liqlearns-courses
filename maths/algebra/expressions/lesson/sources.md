Pakistan (Punjab) carries expressions from Class 6's letters-for-numbers through Classes 7–8's polynomial arithmetic and identities to Class 9's factorisation types, HCF and LCM of expressions, and the remainder and factor theorems again in Class 11.

Syria introduces expressions in Grade 6, works terms, like terms and evaluation in Grade 7, gives symbolic calculation its own unit in Grade 8 Algebra — bracket removal as opposites, simplification to the fewest terms, expanding from rectangle areas, and a claimed identity disproved by one counterexample — and completes expanding, factorising and the three famous identities in Grade 9 Algebra, whose Unit 5 opens with al-Khwarizmi and the origin of the word algebra.

Saudi Arabia opens its Grade 4 patterns-and-algebra chapter with the expression itself — one expression shown three ways (drawing, words, numbers), then the clean distinction between a numerical expression and a numerical sentence.

Malaysia starts algebra in Form 1 (letters, terms, coefficients, like terms, adding and subtracting expressions) and continues in Form 2 with expansion and factorisation as a matched pair — expand one way, factorise back the other.

Rwanda teaches expressions in Primary 6: writing algebraic expressions from words in Unit 10, then collecting like terms and substituting values, including negative values, in Unit 11.
