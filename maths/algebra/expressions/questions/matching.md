# Expressions — matching

Match each expression to its simplified, expanded or factorised form.

**Set 1 (Level 1)**
1. 5a + 2a − a — **6a**
2. 4x + 3y − 2x — **2x + 3y**
3. 3x² − x + 2 at x = 2 — **12**
4. 7 + 2x (terms) — **7 and 2x**
5. −x (coefficient) — **−1**

**Set 2 (Level 3)**
1. x² + 8x + 15 — **(x + 3)(x + 5)**
2. x² − 25 — **(x − 5)(x + 5)**
3. 6x + 9 — **3(2x + 3)**
4. (a + b)² — **a² + 2ab + b²**
5. (a + b)(a − b) — **a² − b²**
