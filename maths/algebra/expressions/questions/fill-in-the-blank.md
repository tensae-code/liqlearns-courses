# Expressions — fill in the blank

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. Fill the gap.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** 3x + 2y ______ be simplified to 5xy.
**Answer:** cannot (or: can never) — unlike terms do not combine.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** a − (b − c) = a − b ______ c.
**Answer:** + c — a minus before the brackets flips every sign.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** x² − 9 = (x − 3)(x ______ 3).
**Answer:** + 3.
