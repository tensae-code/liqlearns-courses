# Expressions — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Level 1)** In 3x², the coefficient is ______.
a) 2  b) 3  c) x
**Answer: b) 3** — the number in front of the variable.

**2. (Level 1)** 3x + 2x = ______.
a) 5x  b) 5x²  c) 6x
**Answer: a) 5x** — like terms combine.

**3. (Level 2)** 2(x + 3) = ______.
a) 2x + 3  b) 2x + 6  c) x + 6
**Answer: b) 2x + 6** — the 2 reaches every term inside.

**4. (Level 3)** x² + 5x + 6 factorised is ______.
a) (x + 1)(x + 6)  b) (x + 2)(x + 3)  c) (x + 5)(x + 1)
**Answer: b) (x + 2)(x + 3)** — 2 and 3 multiply to 6 and add to 5.
