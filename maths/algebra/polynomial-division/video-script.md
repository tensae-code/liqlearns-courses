# Video script — Polynomial Division

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Dividing polynomials? Same as dividing numbers!
A quotient. A remainder. And a check:
divisor times quotient, plus remainder, gives it back!

Long way: divide, multiply, subtract, bring down.
Short way — by x minus a — synthetic!
Coefficients in a row. Bring down. Multiply. Add.

And here is the free gift:
the remainder IS p of a. Just put a in!
No dividing needed!

Remainder zero? Then x minus a is a FACTOR.
Find one factor, divide, and the whole cubic opens up.

Divide. Check. Use the gift!
