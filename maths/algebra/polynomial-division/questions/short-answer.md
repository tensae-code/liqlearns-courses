# Polynomial Division — short answer

Show the working or the p(a) evaluation in every answer.

**1. (Level 1)** Divide x² + 7x + 12 by (x + 3).
**Answer:** x + 4, remainder 0 — (x + 3)(x + 4) = x² + 7x + 12.

**2. (Level 1)** Divide x³ − 1 by (x − 1), writing the missing powers first.
**Answer:** x² + x + 1, remainder 0 — write x³ + 0x² + 0x − 1 and divide term by term.

**3. (Level 2)** Use synthetic division to divide x³ − 4x² + x + 6 by (x − 2).
**Answer:** Quotient x² − 2x − 3, remainder 0 — the table on 1, −4, 1, 6 with 2 gives the bottom row 1, −2, −3, 0.

**4. (Level 2)** Without dividing, find the remainder when x² − 5x + 9 is divided by (x − 3).
**Answer:** 3 — p(3) = 9 − 15 + 9 = 3, Yemen's Grade 10 worked check.

**5. (Level 2)** Without dividing, find the remainder when 3x³ − 2x + 1 is divided by (2x + 1).
**Answer:** 13/8 — the remainder is p(−1/2) = −3/8 + 1 + 1 = 13/8, Bangladesh's Class 9 worked example.

**6. (Level 3)** Show that (x − 1) is a factor of x³ − 2x² − x + 2, and factorise the cubic completely.
**Answer:** p(1) = 0, so (x − 1) is a factor; dividing gives x² − x − 2 = (x − 2)(x + 1), so the cubic is (x − 1)(x − 2)(x + 1).

**7. (Level 3)** Find k so that (x − 3) is a factor of x² + kx − 15.
**Answer:** k = 2 — p(3) = 9 + 3k − 15 = 0 gives 3k = 6.

**8. (Level 3)** List all possible rational zeros of x³ − 2x² − x + 2.
**Answer:** ±1, ±2 — p divides the constant term 2 and q divides the leading coefficient 1.
