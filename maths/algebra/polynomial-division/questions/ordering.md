# Polynomial Division — ordering

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Long Division of Polynomials · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Synthetic Division and the Remainder Theorem · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — The Factor Theorem and Rational Zeros.

Put the items in the order asked. Check each step before you write the answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Put the long-division steps in order: (i) subtract (ii) divide the leading terms (iii) bring down the next term (iv) multiply the divisor by the term just found.
**Answer:** (ii), (iv), (i), (iii) — divide, multiply, subtract, bring down, and repeat.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Put the synthetic-division steps for (x³ − 4x² + x + 6) ÷ (x − 2) in order: (i) add −4 and 2 to get −2 (ii) write the coefficients 1, −4, 1, 6 and the side number 2 (iii) bring down the 1 and multiply by 2 (iv) continue multiplying and adding to the last cell, 0.
**Answer:** (ii), (iii), (i), (iv) — that order builds the bottom row 1, −2, −3, 0.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Put the factor-hunt steps in order: (i) divide out the found factor (ii) test a candidate value a in p(a) (iii) factorise the quotient (iv) list the possible rational zeros p/q.
**Answer:** (iv), (ii), (i), (iii) — list, test, divide, factorise what is left.
