# Polynomial Division — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Level 1)** In p(x) = d(x)q(x) + R(x), the degree of R(x) must be:
a) equal to the degree of d(x)  b) smaller than the degree of d(x)  c) bigger than the degree of p(x)
**Answer:** b) smaller — division stops when the remainder is too small to divide again.

**2. (Level 1)** (x² + 5x + 6) ÷ (x + 2) gives:
a) x + 3, remainder 0  b) x + 2, remainder 2  c) x + 4, remainder −2
**Answer:** a) x + 3, remainder 0 — (x + 2)(x + 3) = x² + 5x + 6.

**3. (Level 1)** (x² − 9) ÷ (x − 3) gives:
a) x − 3  b) x + 3  c) x + 9
**Answer:** b) x + 3 — (x − 3)(x + 3) = x² − 9.

**4. (Level 2)** The remainder when x² + 3x + 1 is divided by (x − 1) is:
a) 3  b) 5  c) 1
**Answer:** b) 5 — by the remainder theorem it is p(1) = 1 + 3 + 1 = 5.

**5. (Level 2)** In synthetic division by (x + 4), the number written at the side is:
a) 4  b) −4  c) 1/4
**Answer:** b) −4 — the table takes a from (x − a), and x + 4 = x − (−4).

**6. (Level 2)** The remainder when 2x³ − x + 5 is divided by (x + 1) is:
a) 2  b) 4  c) 8
**Answer:** b) 4 — p(−1) = −2 + 1 + 5 = 4.

**7. (Level 3)** (x − 2) is a factor of which polynomial?
a) x² − 3x + 2  b) x² − 3x + 1  c) x² + 3x + 2
**Answer:** a) x² − 3x + 2 — p(2) = 4 − 6 + 2 = 0, so the factor theorem applies.

**8. (Level 3)** Which of these is NOT a possible rational zero of 2x³ + x² − 5x + 1?
a) 1/2  b) −1  c) 2
**Answer:** c) 2 — candidates are p/q with p | 1 and q | 2: ±1, ±1/2 only.
