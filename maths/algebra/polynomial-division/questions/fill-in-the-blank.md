# Polynomial Division — fill in the blank

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Long Division of Polynomials · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Synthetic Division and the Remainder Theorem · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — The Factor Theorem and Rational Zeros.

Fill each blank with the missing number, term or word.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Dividing 47 by 5 gives quotient 9 and remainder 2, because 5 × 9 + ______ = 47.
**Answer:** 2 — dividend = divisor × quotient + remainder, the shape every polynomial division copies.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** (x² + 6x + 8) ÷ (x + 2) = x + ______.
**Answer:** 4 — (x + 2)(x + 4) = x² + 6x + 8.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** By the remainder theorem, the remainder when p(x) is divided by (x − a) is ______.
**Answer:** p(a) — put x = a in the division identity and the quotient term vanishes.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The remainder when x³ + 1 is divided by (x + 1) is ______.
**Answer:** 0 — p(−1) = −1 + 1 = 0.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** (x − a) is a factor of p(x) exactly when p(a) = ______.
**Answer:** 0 — a zero remainder means the divisor divides exactly; that is the factor theorem.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** For a possible rational zero p/q in lowest terms, p must divide the ______ term of the polynomial.
**Answer:** constant — and q must divide the leading coefficient.
