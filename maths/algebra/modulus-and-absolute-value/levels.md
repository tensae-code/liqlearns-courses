## Sub lesson 1 — What |x| Is
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Use the definition: |x| = x when x ≥ 0, |x| = −x when x < 0; know that |x| ≥ 0 always; read |x| as the distance of x from 0 on the number line, and the distance between two points a and b as |a − b|. Pass mark: 4 of 5.

## Sub lesson 2 — First Modulus Equations
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Solve |ax + b| = c by cases (the inside equals c, or it equals −c) and by the distance method on the line; and say at once why an equation like |2x − 5| = −7 has no solution. Pass mark: 4 of 5.

## Sub lesson 3 — Harder Modulus Equations
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Solve |f(x)| = g(x), first demanding g(x) ≥ 0; solve |ax + b| = |cx + d| by case systems; open nested moduli from the outside in. Pass mark: 4 of 5.

## Sub lesson 4 — Modulus Inequalities
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Solve |x| ≤ c as −c ≤ x ≤ c; solve |x − a| > c by cases and geometrically, reading the answer as the points far enough from a on the line. Pass mark: 4 of 5.

## Sub lesson 5 — The Squaring Theorems
Sub lesson 5 questions from the type files. Use |x|² = x², |x| = √(x²) and |a| = |b| ⇒ a² = b², and turn |f| > |g| by squaring into (f − g)(f + g) > 0 — knowing each of these is proved, not just stated. Pass mark: 4 of 5.

## What "maxed" means here

The learner can meet any modulus expression and say what it means as a distance; can solve modulus equations of every family the book teaches — by cases, by distance, and by squaring — including spotting the ones with no solution before touching them; and can solve modulus inequalities and read their answers off the number line — the exact skills Mongolia's Grade 12 book teaches and exercises in Chapter II, §2.1 (printed pp. 5–16), standing on the distance definition of its Grade 7 book and the first modulus inequalities of Grade 9.
