1. (Sub lesson 1) |x| is the ______ of x from 0 on the number line.
   **Answer: distance.** Mongolia, Математик VII (Grade 7), printed p. 5; Математик XII (Grade 12), printed p. 5.

2. (Sub lesson 2) |x − 5| = 3 has the two solutions x = 2 and x = ______.
   **Answer: 8.** Mongolia, Математик XII (Grade 12), printed p. 6.

3. (Sub lesson 4) |x − 5| > 8 holds exactly when x < ______ or x > 13.
   **Answer: −3** — the points more than 8 away from 5 start below 5 − 8 = −3. Mongolia, Математик XII (Grade 12), printed p. 9.

4. (Sub lesson 5) For every real number x, |x| = √(______).
   **Answer: x²** — the proved theorem |x| = √(x²). Mongolia, Математик XII (Grade 12), printed p. 12.
