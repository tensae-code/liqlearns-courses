1. (Sub lessons 2–4) Match each equation or inequality to its solution set:
   (a) |x| = 6
   (b) |x − 5| = 3
   (c) |x| ≤ 4
   (d) |2x − 5| = −7
   (e) |x − 5| > 8

   (i) no solution
   (ii) −4 ≤ x ≤ 4
   (iii) x = −6 or x = 6
   (iv) x = 2 or x = 8
   (v) x < −3 or x > 13

   **Answer: (a)–(iii), (b)–(iv), (c)–(ii), (d)–(i), (e)–(v).** Mongolia, Математик XII (Grade 12), printed pp. 6, 9.
