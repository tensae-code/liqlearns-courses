**Acceptance count: taught in 7 countries (Mongolia, Venezuela, North Macedonia, Afghanistan, Uzbekistan, Bolivia, Tajikistan).** Mongolia is the one read country whose course gives modulus a chapter of its own: Grade 7 defines it as distance from the origin, with the distance between two points as |a − b|; Grade 9 meets the first simple modulus inequalities; and Grade 12, Chapter II §2.1 (printed pp. 5–16), gives the complete treatment — the piecewise definition, equations by cases and by the distance method (with |2x − 5| = −7 shown to have no solution), the |f(x)| = g(x) and two-modulus families, inequalities read geometrically on the line, and the three squaring theorems, each proved. Elsewhere in the library modulus is met only in passing — inside the negative-numbers and real-number-system lessons as distance, inside the function lessons as the absolute-value function, and inside complex numbers as |a + bi| — so under the everything-goes-in ruling (2026-11-06) the topic gets its own lesson, built at the size of its source. The Grade 7 and Grade 9 rows are also stocked in the negative-numbers and inequalities lessons respectively, where the same pages do that work too; this lesson is the topic's own home.
