# Partial Fractions — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the missing number, term or word.

**1. (Level 1)** A fraction is ______ when the numerator's degree is below the denominator's.
**Answer:** proper — only proper fractions are split directly.

**2. (Level 1)** In (3x + 1)/(x² − 1) = A/(x + 1) + B/(x − 1), B is found by putting x = ______.
**Answer:** 1 — that choice kills the A piece, leaving 4 = 2B.

**3. (Level 2)** A repeated factor (x − 1)² demands pieces over (x − 1) and over ______.
**Answer:** (x − 1)² — one piece for every power of the factor.

**4. (Level 2)** (3x² + 1)/(x² − 1) divided out gives quotient 3 and remainder ______.
**Answer:** 4 — 3(x² − 1) = 3x² − 3, and (3x² + 1) − (3x² − 3) = 4.

**5. (Level 3)** Over an irreducible quadratic, the numerator of the piece has the form ______.
**Answer:** Ax + B — linear, one degree below the quadratic below it.

**6. (Level 3)** Kuwait's Grade 12 book uses partial fractions in integration for ______ linear factors only.
**Answer:** distinct — its §5-6 teaches no repeated and no quadratic case.
