# Partial Fractions — matching

Match each item on the left to its partner on the right.

**1. (Level 1)** Match each denominator to its set-up shape: (a) (x − 1)(x + 4) (b) (x − 1)² (c) (x² + 9) alone with numerator degree below 2 — A/(x − 1) + B/(x − 1)², A/(x − 1) + B/(x + 4), (Ax + B)/(x² + 9)
**Answer:** a) A/(x − 1) + B/(x + 4), b) A/(x − 1) + B/(x − 1)², c) (Ax + B)/(x² + 9) — the shape follows the factors.

**2. (Level 2)** Match each fraction to its first move: (a) (2x + 1)/(x² − 9) (b) (x² + 5)/(x² − 9) (c) (x + 2)/((x − 1)²(x + 1)) — divide first, split at once (proper), set up three pieces
**Answer:** a) split at once (proper), b) divide first, c) set up three pieces — degree test first, then the factor shapes.

**3. (Level 3)** Match each split to its rejoin: (a) 1/(x + 1) + 2/(x − 1) (b) 2/(x + 3) + 3/(x − 5) (c) 1/x − x/(x² + 1) — (5x − 1)/((x + 3)(x − 5)), (3x + 1)/(x² − 1), 1/(x(x² + 1))
**Answer:** a) (3x + 1)/(x² − 1), b) (5x − 1)/((x + 3)(x − 5)), c) 1/(x(x² + 1)) — rejoin over the common denominator to check.
