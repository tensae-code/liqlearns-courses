# Video script — Partial Fractions

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

One big fraction. Hard to use!
Split it! One piece for each factor!

Top smaller than bottom? Proper — split away!
Top too big? Divide FIRST. Then split the rest.

Each linear factor gets a piece.
A REPEATED factor? A piece for EVERY power!
A quadratic factor? Its piece wears A x plus B on top.

Find the letters: put in the killing value,
or compare the coefficients.
Then check — add your pieces back into one!

Split it. Solve it. Put it back!
