# Equations — fill in the blank

Fill the gap.

**1. (Level 1)** To solve x − 9 = −3, ______ 9 to both sides.
**Answer:** add.

**2. (Level 2)** Moving 3x across the equals sign turns it into ______.
**Answer:** −3x.

**3. (Level 3)** 2(x − 3) = 8 gives x = ______.
**Answer:** 7 — expand first: 2x − 6 = 8, so 2x = 14.
