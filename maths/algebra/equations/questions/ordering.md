# Equations — ordering

Put each set in order — here, in the order of the steps you take.

**1. (Level 2)** Steps to solve 8x − 9 = 3x + 8: divide by 5 · move 3x left and −9 right · check by substitution · read the equation
**Answer:** read the equation, move 3x left and −9 right, divide by 5, check by substitution.

**2. (Level 3)** Steps to solve (2x − 1)/4 = 1: solve 2x − 1 = 4 · multiply everything by 4 · check by substitution · read the equation
**Answer:** read the equation, multiply everything by 4, solve 2x − 1 = 4, check by substitution.
