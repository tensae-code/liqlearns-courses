# Equations — short answer

Answer in a few words, and say how you checked.

**1. (Level 1)** Solve 3 + x = 12.
**Answer:** x = 9 — check: 3 + 9 = 12.

**2. (Level 2)** Solve 5x + 3 = 0.
**Answer:** x = −0.6 — check: 5 × (−0.6) + 3 = 0.

**3. (Level 3)** A gym charges a 100 joining fee and 20 per session. With a 300 budget, how many sessions?
**Answer:** 10 — 100 + 20n = 300, so n = 10 (Egypt, Grade 8 Term 2, 2-4).
