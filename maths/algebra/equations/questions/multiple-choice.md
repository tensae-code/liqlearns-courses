# Equations — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Level 1)** x + 5 = −4. What is x?
a) 1  b) −1  c) −9
**Answer: c) −9** — subtract 5 from both sides (South Africa, Grade 8 Book 1, WS35).

**2. (Level 2)** 8x − 9 = 3x + 8. What is x?
a) 3.4  b) 17  c) −3.4
**Answer: a) 3.4** — 5x = 17 (Kazakhstan, Grade 6, §4.2).

**3. (Level 3)** (2x − 1)/4 = 1. What is x?
a) 2  b) 2½  c) 5
**Answer: b) 2½** — multiply through by 4 first (South Africa, Grade 9 Book 1, WS37).

**4. (Level 2)** 0x = 4 has ______.
a) one root  b) no root  c) every number as a root
**Answer: b) no root** — 0 times anything is 0, never 4.
