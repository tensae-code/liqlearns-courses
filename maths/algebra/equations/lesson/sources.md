**Acceptance count: taught in 14 countries (Kazakhstan, Jordan, Greece, South Africa, Armenia, Egypt, Libya, Vietnam, Sierra Leone, Ghana, Laos, Yemen, Indonesia, Nicaragua).** Kazakhstan carries the fullest treatment — the definition, the equivalence rules, the three cases, and a checked word problem. Greece carries the six elementary forms in Grade 7 and first-degree equations in full in Grade 8. Jordan carries one-variable linear equations in Grade 7. South Africa carries balancing in Grade 8 (x + 5 = −4) and equations with fractions in Grade 9. Armenia carries letter expressions and equations in Grade 7. Egypt carries one-step equations in Grade 6 and first-degree equations with the equality properties in Grade 8 Term 2. Libya carries equations with algebraic fractions in Grade 9. Vietnam carries first-degree equations in one unknown in Grade 8. Sierra Leone carries variable-on-both-sides equations in JSS 2 and two-variable linear equations in JSS 3. Ghana carries only teacher-manual evidence (JICA Basic 5, Lessons 86–88). Mauritania is not in this lesson: its 1AS teaches no algebra. Laos carries equations from letter sentences in Grade 4, through one-unknown equations in Grade 6, to first-degree equations with word problems in Grade 7 and rational equations with extraneous roots in Grade 9. Yemen carries equations from first-degree equations in one variable in Grade 7, through second-degree equations and equations in two variables, to solving systems by matrices in Grade 11. Indonesia carries equations from addition and subtraction sentences with an unknown in Grade 3, through linear equations in one variable in Grade 7 K13 and straight-line equations in Grade 8 K13, to systems of linear equations and inequalities and quadratic equations in Grade 10 Revisi; Grade 7 Merdeka teaches no equation solving in its algebra chapter. Nicaragua carries equations from first-degree equations with transposing, grouping signs and fractional coefficients in Grade 7, through first-degree equations in one and two variables opening the Grade 8 systems unit, to linear equations and their classification in the Grade 8 module (module evidence).
