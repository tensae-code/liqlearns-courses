China touches equations only at exercise and review level in the held books — formal solve-the-equation exercises inside Grade 5's fraction sets and the Grade 6 general review — because the chapters that teach equations sit in the unheld Volume 1 books, so China enters this lesson as a partial.

Pakistan (Punjab) carries equations from Class 6's balance method through Class 9's absolute-value equations and Class 10's radical equations to the Class 12 line equations.

Syria defines equations and solutions in Grade 6, solves ax = c formally in Grade 7, builds the two equality rules on a balance in Grade 8 Algebra — with transposition and the four stages of building an equation from a text — and in Grade 9 gives equivalent equations, the standard first-degree method and x² = a with its three cases; the intermediate value theorem closes the arc in Grade 12 by guaranteeing a root for every odd-degree polynomial.
