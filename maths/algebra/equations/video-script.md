# Video script — Equations

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

An equation is a BALANCE. Two sides, EQUAL.
x plus 5 equals minus 4. The scale is level!

What do you do? The SAME to both sides!
Take 5 from the left? Take 5 from the right too!
x equals minus 9. Check it: minus 9 plus 5 is minus 4. Level!

Move a term across? It CHANGES its sign!
8x minus 9 equals 3x plus 8.
Move 3x across: it becomes minus 3x. Move 9 across: plus 9.
5x equals 17. x equals 17 over 5 — that is 3.4. Check it!

Fractions in the way? Multiply EVERYTHING through!
Clear them first, then solve. Always check by putting
the answer BACK in. The two sides must agree!
