# Equations — questions by level

Practice by level, tied to the skill number. The questions live in
the type files; this file is the by-level view. The levels are the
depths inside this one thing — one-step and balancing first, then two-step and multi-step, then brackets, fractions and word problems.

## Level 1 — one-step equations and balancing
Level 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: solve one-step equations by the inverse operation, and check every answer by substitution.

## Level 2 — two-step and multi-step equations
Level 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: collect the x-terms on one side and the numbers on the other, divide by the coefficient, and name the no-root and every-number cases.

## Level 3 — brackets, fractions and word problems
Level 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: clear brackets and fractions before solving, turn a word problem into an equation, and check the answer against the story.
