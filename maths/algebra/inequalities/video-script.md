# Video script — Inequalities

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Less than! Greater than! Or EQUAL to!
Four signs: < > and the two with a line under:
less than OR EQUAL, greater than OR EQUAL.

Driving licence? 18 or over! Age is MORE than or EQUAL to 18.
x is more than or equal to 18. Write it!

Show it on the LINE. Open circle means NOT included.
Closed circle means INCLUDED. Arrow shows which way!

Solve it like an equation — SAME moves!
Add, subtract, same both sides. Multiply, divide, same both sides.
BUT — multiply or divide by a MINUS?
FLIP the sign! That is the one new rule!

Two signs at once? 3 is less than or equal to x,
and x is less than or equal to 7. Split it in two!
x is between 3 and 7. Done!
