# Inequalities — fill in the blank

Fill the gap.

**1. (Level 1)** The symbol ≤ means less than or ______ to.
**Answer:** equal.

**2. (Level 2)** Multiplying an inequality by a negative number ______ the symbol.
**Answer:** reverses (or: flips).

**3. (Level 3)** 0x > 9 has ______ solutions.
**Answer:** no (or: zero) — 0 is never more than 9.
