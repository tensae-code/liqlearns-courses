Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** "18 or over" is written ______.
a) a < 18  b) a ≥ 18  c) a ≤ 18
**Answer: b) a ≥ 18** — "or over" means 18 or more (Egypt, Grade 7 Term 2, 2-1).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** 5x + 2 > 12 gives ______.
a) x > 2  b) x < 2  c) x > 14
**Answer: a) x > 2** (Egypt, Grade 8 Term 2, 2-4).

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** −2x < 6 gives ______.
a) x < −3  b) x > −3  c) x < 3
**Answer: b) x > −3** — dividing by a negative reverses the symbol.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** 3 ≤ x ≤ 7 means ______.
a) x is less than 3 or more than 7  b) x is between 3 and 7, including both  c) x = 3 or x = 7
**Answer: b) x is between 3 and 7, including both** (Sierra Leone, JSS 3, L114).
