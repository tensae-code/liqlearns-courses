# Video script — Quadratic Equations

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

x squared! That is a QUADRATIC!
ax squared plus bx plus c equals zero!
a, b, c — three numbers. The x is squared!

First way: FACTORISE!
x squared plus 5x plus 6 is (x + 2) times (x + 3).
Equals zero? Then one of them IS zero!
x is minus 2, OR x is minus 3. TWO answers!

Second way: COMPLETE the square!
x squared plus 6x. Half of 6 is 3. Add 9 — and TAKE 9 away!
(x + 3) squared minus 9. Now solve it!

Third way: THE FORMULA!
x equals minus b, plus or minus root of (b squared minus 4ac),
ALL over 2a. It always works!

b squared minus 4ac — the DISCRIMINANT!
More than zero? TWO roots! Zero? ONE root!
Less than zero? NO real roots!
