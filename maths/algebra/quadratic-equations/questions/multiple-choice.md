# Quadratic Equations — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Level 1)** In ax² + bx + c = 0, the coefficient of x² is ______.
a) b  b) a  c) c
**Answer: b) a.**

**2. (Level 1)** x² + 5x + 6 = 0 factorises to (x + 2)(x + 3) = 0. The roots are ______.
a) 2 and 3  b) −2 and −3  c) 5 and 6
**Answer: b) −2 and −3** — each factor gives a root.

**3. (Level 2)** x² + 6x = (x + 3)² − ______.
a) 3  b) 6  c) 9
**Answer: c) 9** — the added 9 must be taken away again.

**4. (Level 3)** For x² − 5x + 6 = 0, the discriminant is ______.
a) 1  b) 13  c) 25
**Answer: a) 1** — b² − 4ac = 25 − 24 = 1, so there are two roots.
