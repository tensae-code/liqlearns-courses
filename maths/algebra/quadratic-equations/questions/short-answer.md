# Quadratic Equations — short answer

Answer in a few words.

**1. (Level 1)** Solve x² − 25 = 0.
**Answer:** x = 5 or x = −5 (South Africa, Grade 9 Book 2, WS83).

**2. (Level 2)** Complete the square: x² + 6x = ______.
**Answer:** (x + 3)² − 9.

**3. (Level 3)** Use the formula on x² − 5x + 6 = 0.
**Answer:** x = 2 or x = 3 — check with Vieta: 2 + 3 = 5, 2 × 3 = 6.
