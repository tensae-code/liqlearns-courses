# Quadratic Equations — fill in the blank

Fill the gap.

**1. (Level 1)** A product is zero only if one of its ______ is zero.
**Answer:** factors.

**2. (Level 2)** To complete the square on x² + bx, add (b/2)² and ______ it again.
**Answer:** subtract (or: take away).

**3. (Level 3)** If the discriminant is less than zero, there are ______ real roots.
**Answer:** no (or: zero).
