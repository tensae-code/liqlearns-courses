**Acceptance count: taught in 25 countries (Kazakhstan, Jordan, Egypt, Greece, Libya, Armenia, Vietnam, Sierra Leone, South Africa, Laos, Yemen, Indonesia, Nicaragua, Ethiopia, South Sudan, Iraq, Bangladesh, Nepal, Cambodia, Kuwait, Iran, China, Pakistan, Syria, Malaysia).** Nepal carries quadratic equations from factorising in Grade 8 compulsory (Unit 22 §22.4: x²+2x+1 = 0, x²−16 = 0, 16x²−49 = 0 → x = ±7/4), through verbal problems on quadratic equations in Grade 10 compulsory (Unit 12 §12.2, quadratic formula used, not derived), to vertex form and graphical solutions in Grade 10 Optional (Lesson 4, pp. 36–50). Grade 9 compulsory teaches no quadratic equations (simultaneous linear only). Bangladesh carries quadratic equations from quadratic equations formed from area problems in Class 7 (Exp. 12, pp. 227–240) — x(2x+1) = 10, i.e. 2x²+x−10 = 0, in standard form ax²+bx+c = 0, solved by factorisation worksheets only, with no formula and no completing the square — to solving by middle-term splitting, the quadratic formula derived by completing the square, the discriminant and the nature of roots, and solving by graphs in Class 9 (Ch. 5, pp. 130–134). Iraq carries quadratic equations from second-degree one-variable equations in R in Grade 8 — by square root and the zero-product property, with no quadratic formula by name (Ch. 4 L4-3, p. 83) — through quadratics by square root, trial factoring, completing the square and the general law (quadratic formula) with discriminant Δ = b² − 4ac in Grade 9 (Ch. 3, 3-2–3-5, pp. 60–72), to the quadratic formula in Grade 10 Literary (Ch. 2, 2-1; ex. 4, p. 28: 4x² − 4x + 1 = 0 → x = ½, equal roots). Jordan carries the deliberate ladder: graphically, factorising, completing the square, then the formula with the discriminant. Sierra Leone carries the JSS 3 part-ladder (expanding, factorising, completing the square — no formula, no solve-for-x lesson) and the full SSS 1 ladder including the formula. South Africa carries only the factorising route (product-equals-zero in Grade 9 Book 1, factorising in Book 2) — no formula and no completing the square, and the lesson says so. Ghana and Mauritania are not in this lesson on the filed stock. Laos carries quadratic equations from factorisation and the formula in Grade 8 to discriminant, biquadratic equations and Viète's relations in Grade 10. Yemen carries quadratic equations from second-degree equations in Grade 8, through sum and product of roots in Grade 10, to solving them over the complex numbers in Grade 12 science. Indonesia carries quadratic equations in Grade 10 — solving quadratic equations as its own section in the Revisi edition, with quadratic functions (characteristics, constructing quadratics, applied problems) in the Merdeka edition. Nicaragua carries quadratic equations whole in Grade 9 — completing the square as the main method, the general formula as a regular lesson (only its proof is a Desafío), factorisation, the discriminant and Vieta's relations; the unverified x²−5x+1 = 0 worked example is never cited. Ethiopia carries quadratic equations from factorisation, completing the square and the quadratic formula with the discriminant and roots–coefficients relations in Grade 9, to solving quadratic equations graphically — intercepts, vertex by completing the square, axis of symmetry and shifts — in Grade 10. South Sudan carries quadratic equations from factorising quadratics x² + bx + c in Secondary 1 — expressions only, no solving as a topic — to solving quadratic equations by factorisation in Secondary 2.
