# Fill in the blank — Roots and Coefficients of a Cubic Equation

Sub-lesson markers: every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to — Sub lesson 1 — The Three Relations · Sub lesson 2 — Building a Cubic from Its Roots · Sub lesson 3 — Using the Relations. This lesson was built new with its sub lessons (Dominica expansion merge, 2026-11-11), so its markers are original — there are no older depth layers to map from.

1. (Sub lesson 1) For x³ + bx² + cx + d = 0 with roots α, β, γ: α + β + γ = ___.
   **Answer: −b.** Item constructed for this lesson from its founding teaching. Dominica, *Pure Mathematics: A Complete Course for CAPE, Unit 1* (student book), Grade 12, Module 1, Ch. 10, printed p. 207.

2. (Sub lesson 1) With the same notation, αβ + βγ + γα = ___.
   **Answer: c.** Item constructed for this lesson from its founding teaching. Dominica, *Pure Mathematics: A Complete Course for CAPE, Unit 1* (student book), Grade 12, Module 1, Ch. 10, printed p. 207.

3. (Sub lesson 2) A monic cubic is built as x³ − (sum)x² + (pairs)x ___ (product) = 0.
   **Answer: −** — the sign marches minus, plus, minus. Item constructed for this lesson from its founding teaching. Dominica, *Pure Mathematics: A Complete Course for CAPE, Unit 1* (student book), Grade 12, Module 1, Ch. 10, printed p. 207.

4. (Sub lesson 2) The roots 1, 2 and 3 give the cubic x³ − 6x² + ___x − 6 = 0.
   **Answer: 11** — the sum of the products in pairs. Item constructed for this lesson from its founding teaching. Dominica, *Pure Mathematics: A Complete Course for CAPE, Unit 1* (student book), Grade 12, Module 1, Ch. 10, printed p. 207.

5. (Sub lesson 3) If α + β = 5 and α + β + γ = 4, then γ = ___.
   **Answer: −1.** Item constructed for this lesson from its founding teaching. Dominica, *Pure Mathematics: A Complete Course for CAPE, Unit 1* (student book), Grade 12, Module 1, Ch. 10, printed p. 207.

6. (Sub lesson 3) For 3x³ − 6x² + 2x − 9 = 0, the product of the roots is ___.
   **Answer: 3** — −d/a = 9/3. Item constructed for this lesson from its founding teaching. Dominica, *Pure Mathematics: A Complete Course for CAPE, Unit 1* (student book), Grade 12, Module 1, Ch. 10, printed p. 207.
