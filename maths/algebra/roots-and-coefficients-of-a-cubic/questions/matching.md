# Matching — Roots and Coefficients of a Cubic Equation

Sub-lesson markers: every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to — Sub lesson 1 — The Three Relations · Sub lesson 2 — Building a Cubic from Its Roots · Sub lesson 3 — Using the Relations. This lesson was built new with its sub lessons (Dominica expansion merge, 2026-11-11), so its markers are original — there are no older depth layers to map from.

1. (Sub lesson 1) Match each expression in the roots α, β, γ to its name:
   (a) α + β + γ
   (b) αβ + βγ + γα
   (c) αβγ
   (i) the product of the roots
   (ii) the sum of the roots
   (iii) the sum of the products in pairs
   **Answer: (a)–(ii); (b)–(iii); (c)–(i).** Item constructed for this lesson from its founding teaching. Dominica, *Pure Mathematics: A Complete Course for CAPE, Unit 1* (student book), Grade 12, Module 1, Ch. 10, printed p. 207.

2. (Sub lesson 2) Match each set of roots to its monic cubic:
   (a) 1, 2, 3
   (b) 0, 2, 4
   (c) −1, 2, 5
   (i) x³ − 6x² + 3x + 10 = 0
   (ii) x³ − 6x² + 11x − 6 = 0
   (iii) x³ − 6x² + 8x = 0
   **Answer: (a)–(ii); (b)–(iii); (c)–(i).** Item constructed for this lesson from its founding teaching. Dominica, *Pure Mathematics: A Complete Course for CAPE, Unit 1* (student book), Grade 12, Module 1, Ch. 10, printed p. 207.

3. (Sub lesson 3) Match each equation to the sum of its roots:
   (a) x³ − 7x² + 14x − 8 = 0
   (b) x³ − 4x² + x + 6 = 0
   (c) 2x³ + 3x² − 5x + 1 = 0
   (i) 4
   (ii) −3/2
   (iii) 7
   **Answer: (a)–(iii); (b)–(i); (c)–(ii).** Item constructed for this lesson from its founding teaching. Dominica, *Pure Mathematics: A Complete Course for CAPE, Unit 1* (student book), Grade 12, Module 1, Ch. 10, printed p. 207.
