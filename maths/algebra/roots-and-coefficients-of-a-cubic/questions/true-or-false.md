# True or false — Roots and Coefficients of a Cubic Equation

Sub-lesson markers: every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to — Sub lesson 1 — The Three Relations · Sub lesson 2 — Building a Cubic from Its Roots · Sub lesson 3 — Using the Relations. This lesson was built new with its sub lessons (Dominica expansion merge, 2026-11-11), so its markers are original — there are no older depth layers to map from.

1. (Sub lesson 1) For a monic cubic, the sum of the roots is the x² coefficient with its sign flipped.
   **Answer: True.** Item constructed for this lesson from its founding teaching. Dominica, *Pure Mathematics: A Complete Course for CAPE, Unit 1* (student book), Grade 12, Module 1, Ch. 10, printed p. 207.

2. (Sub lesson 1) For a monic cubic, the product of the roots equals the constant term.
   **Answer: False** — it equals the constant term with its sign flipped: αβγ = −d. Item constructed for this lesson from its founding teaching. Dominica, *Pure Mathematics: A Complete Course for CAPE, Unit 1* (student book), Grade 12, Module 1, Ch. 10, printed p. 207.

3. (Sub lesson 2) If every root of a cubic is doubled, the new cubic is found from the new sum, the new pairs and the new product.
   **Answer: True.** Item constructed for this lesson from its founding teaching. Dominica, *Pure Mathematics: A Complete Course for CAPE, Unit 1* (student book), Grade 12, Module 1, Ch. 10, printed p. 207.

4. (Sub lesson 2) The cubic with roots 0, 1 and 2 has no constant term.
   **Answer: True** — the product of the roots is 0, so d = 0. Item constructed for this lesson from its founding teaching. Dominica, *Pure Mathematics: A Complete Course for CAPE, Unit 1* (student book), Grade 12, Module 1, Ch. 10, printed p. 207.

5. (Sub lesson 3) A third root can be found from two known roots and the sum of the roots, without solving the cubic.
   **Answer: True.** Item constructed for this lesson from its founding teaching. Dominica, *Pure Mathematics: A Complete Course for CAPE, Unit 1* (student book), Grade 12, Module 1, Ch. 10, printed p. 207.

6. (Sub lesson 3) α² + β² + γ² always equals (α + β + γ)².
   **Answer: False** — twice the sum of the products in pairs must be subtracted: α² + β² + γ² = (α + β + γ)² − 2(αβ + βγ + γα). Item constructed for this lesson from its founding teaching.
