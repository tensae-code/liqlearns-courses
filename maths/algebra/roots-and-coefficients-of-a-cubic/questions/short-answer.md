# Short answer — Roots and Coefficients of a Cubic Equation

Sub-lesson markers: every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to — Sub lesson 1 — The Three Relations · Sub lesson 2 — Building a Cubic from Its Roots · Sub lesson 3 — Using the Relations. This lesson was built new with its sub lessons (Dominica expansion merge, 2026-11-11), so its markers are original — there are no older depth layers to map from.

1. (Sub lesson 1) For x³ − 7x² + 14x − 8 = 0, write down α + β + γ, αβ + βγ + γα and αβγ.
   **Answer: 7; 14; 8.** Item constructed for this lesson from its founding teaching. Dominica, *Pure Mathematics: A Complete Course for CAPE, Unit 1* (student book), Grade 12, Module 1, Ch. 10, printed p. 207.

2. (Sub lesson 1) For 2x³ + 3x² − 5x + 1 = 0, find the sum of the roots.
   **Answer: −b/a = −3/2.** Item constructed for this lesson from its founding teaching. Dominica, *Pure Mathematics: A Complete Course for CAPE, Unit 1* (student book), Grade 12, Module 1, Ch. 10, printed p. 207.

3. (Sub lesson 2) Build the monic cubic with roots 0, 2 and 4.
   **Answer: x³ − 6x² + 8x = 0** — sum 6, pairs 0 + 8 + 0 = 8, product 0. Item constructed for this lesson from its founding teaching. Dominica, *Pure Mathematics: A Complete Course for CAPE, Unit 1* (student book), Grade 12, Module 1, Ch. 10, printed p. 207.

4. (Sub lesson 2) Build the monic cubic with roots −1, 2 and 5.
   **Answer: x³ − 6x² + 3x + 10 = 0** — sum 6, pairs 3, product −10. Item constructed for this lesson from its founding teaching. Dominica, *Pure Mathematics: A Complete Course for CAPE, Unit 1* (student book), Grade 12, Module 1, Ch. 10, printed p. 207.

5. (Sub lesson 3) The equation x³ − 4x² + x + 6 = 0 has roots 2 and −1 and a third root γ. Find γ.
   **Answer: γ = 3** — the sum of the roots is 4, and 4 − 2 − (−1) = 3; the product check gives 2 × (−1) × 3 = −6 = −d. Item constructed for this lesson from its founding teaching. Dominica, *Pure Mathematics: A Complete Course for CAPE, Unit 1* (student book), Grade 12, Module 1, Ch. 10, printed p. 207.

6. (Sub lesson 3) Using the relations only, find α² + β² + γ² for the roots 1, 2 and 3.
   **Answer: (α + β + γ)² − 2(αβ + βγ + γα) = 36 − 22 = 14.** Item constructed for this lesson from its founding teaching. Dominica, *Pure Mathematics: A Complete Course for CAPE, Unit 1* (student book), Grade 12, Module 1, Ch. 10, printed p. 207.
