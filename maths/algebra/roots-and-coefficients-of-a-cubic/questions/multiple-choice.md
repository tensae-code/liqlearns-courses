# Multiple choice — Roots and Coefficients of a Cubic Equation

Sub-lesson markers: every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to — Sub lesson 1 — The Three Relations · Sub lesson 2 — Building a Cubic from Its Roots · Sub lesson 3 — Using the Relations. This lesson was built new with its sub lessons (Dominica expansion merge, 2026-11-11), so its markers are original — there are no older depth layers to map from.

1. (Sub lesson 1) For x³ − 6x² + 11x − 6 = 0, the sum of the roots is:
   (a) −6
   (b) 6 ✔
   (c) 11
   (d) −11
   **Answer: (b)** — the x² coefficient with its sign flipped. Item constructed for this lesson from its founding teaching. Dominica, *Pure Mathematics: A Complete Course for CAPE, Unit 1* (student book), Grade 12, Module 1, Ch. 10, printed p. 207.

2. (Sub lesson 1) For the same equation, the product of the roots is:
   (a) −6
   (b) 11
   (c) 6 ✔
   (d) −11
   **Answer: (c)** — the constant term with its sign flipped: −(−6). Item constructed for this lesson from its founding teaching. Dominica, *Pure Mathematics: A Complete Course for CAPE, Unit 1* (student book), Grade 12, Module 1, Ch. 10, printed p. 207.

3. (Sub lesson 2) The monic cubic with roots 1, 2 and 3 is:
   (a) x³ − 6x² + 11x − 6 = 0 ✔
   (b) x³ + 6x² + 11x + 6 = 0
   (c) x³ − 6x² − 11x − 6 = 0
   (d) x³ − 11x² + 6x − 6 = 0
   **Answer: (a)** — sum 6, pairs 11, product 6, written minus, plus, minus. Item constructed for this lesson from its founding teaching. Dominica, *Pure Mathematics: A Complete Course for CAPE, Unit 1* (student book), Grade 12, Module 1, Ch. 10, printed p. 207.

4. (Sub lesson 2) A monic cubic has roots −1, 2 and 5. Its x coefficient is:
   (a) −3
   (b) 3 ✔
   (c) 10
   (d) −6
   **Answer: (b)** — the sum of the products in pairs: (−1)(2) + (2)(5) + (5)(−1) = −2 + 10 − 5 = 3. Item constructed for this lesson from its founding teaching. Dominica, *Pure Mathematics: A Complete Course for CAPE, Unit 1* (student book), Grade 12, Module 1, Ch. 10, printed p. 207.

5. (Sub lesson 3) The equation x³ − 2x² + kx + 6 = 0 has roots 1, 3 and γ. The third root γ is:
   (a) −2 ✔
   (b) 2
   (c) −6
   (d) 6
   **Answer: (a)** — the product of the roots is −6, so 1 × 3 × γ = −6. Item constructed for this lesson from its founding teaching. Dominica, *Pure Mathematics: A Complete Course for CAPE, Unit 1* (student book), Grade 12, Module 1, Ch. 10, printed p. 207.

6. (Sub lesson 3) In the same equation, k is:
   (a) 5
   (b) −5 ✔
   (c) −2
   (d) 2
   **Answer: (b)** — k is the sum of the products in pairs: (1)(3) + (3)(−2) + (−2)(1) = 3 − 6 − 2. Item constructed for this lesson from its founding teaching. Dominica, *Pure Mathematics: A Complete Course for CAPE, Unit 1* (student book), Grade 12, Module 1, Ch. 10, printed p. 207.
