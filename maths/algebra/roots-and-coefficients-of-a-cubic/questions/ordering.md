# Ordering — Roots and Coefficients of a Cubic Equation

Sub-lesson markers: every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to — Sub lesson 1 — The Three Relations · Sub lesson 2 — Building a Cubic from Its Roots · Sub lesson 3 — Using the Relations. This lesson was built new with its sub lessons (Dominica expansion merge, 2026-11-11), so its markers are original — there are no older depth layers to map from.

1. (Sub lesson 1) Put the steps for reading the relations off 2x³ − 4x² + 6x − 8 = 0 in order:
   read off sum = 2, pairs = 3, product = 4 / name a = 2, b = −4, c = 6, d = −8 / divide through by a in the formulas −b/a, c/a, −d/a
   **Answer: name a = 2, b = −4, c = 6, d = −8 → divide through by a in the formulas −b/a, c/a, −d/a → read off sum = 2, pairs = 3, product = 4.** Item constructed for this lesson from its founding teaching. Dominica, *Pure Mathematics: A Complete Course for CAPE, Unit 1* (student book), Grade 12, Module 1, Ch. 10, printed p. 207.

2. (Sub lesson 2) Put the steps for building a cubic from the roots 1, 2 and 3 in order:
   find the product 6 / find the sum 6 / find the sum of the products in pairs, 11 / write x³ − 6x² + 11x − 6 = 0
   **Answer: find the sum 6 → find the sum of the products in pairs, 11 → find the product 6 → write x³ − 6x² + 11x − 6 = 0.** Item constructed for this lesson from its founding teaching. Dominica, *Pure Mathematics: A Complete Course for CAPE, Unit 1* (student book), Grade 12, Module 1, Ch. 10, printed p. 207.

3. (Sub lesson 3) Put the steps for finding the third root of x³ − 4x² + x + 6 = 0, given roots 2 and −1, in order:
   add the known roots: 2 + (−1) = 1 / check with the product: 2 × (−1) × 3 = −6 = −d / read the sum of the roots, 4, from the equation / subtract: 4 − 1 = 3
   **Answer: read the sum of the roots, 4, from the equation → add the known roots: 2 + (−1) = 1 → subtract: 4 − 1 = 3 → check with the product: 2 × (−1) × 3 = −6 = −d.** Item constructed for this lesson from its founding teaching. Dominica, *Pure Mathematics: A Complete Course for CAPE, Unit 1* (student book), Grade 12, Module 1, Ch. 10, printed p. 207.
