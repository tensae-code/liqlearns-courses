# Roots and Coefficients of a Cubic Equation — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, expressed as its SUB
LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure — README, "Units, Lessons and
Sub Lessons"). One lesson, one level (Level 170). The lesson
was built new in the expansion era (Dominica expansion merge,
2026-11-11), so there are no old depth layers to map: the sub
lessons below are its depths as built, cut at the founding
section's own shape — the three relations stated first, then
the equation built from its roots, then the relations put to
work on unknowns. The questions live in the type files; this
file is the by-sub-lesson view.

Pass marks: the founding section names its topic
(relationships between roots and coefficients of a cubic
equation) and states no pass mark, so no mark is invented —
each sub lesson's pass mark is stated as the observable thing
the learner can do.

## Sub lesson 1 — The Three Relations
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: state the three relations for a monic cubic (sum = −b, sum of pairs = c, product = −d), divide through by a first when the cubic is not monic, and check all three on a cubic whose roots are known.

## Sub lesson 2 — Building a Cubic from Its Roots
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: given three roots, form the sum, the sum of pairs and the product, and write the monic cubic x³ − (sum)x² + (pairs)x − (product) = 0, checking it by expanding the three factors.

## Sub lesson 3 — Using the Relations
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: find a third root from two roots and one relation, find an unknown coefficient from the pairs or the product, and form α² + β² + γ² from the sum and the pairs without finding the roots.

## What "maxed" means here

The learner handles a cubic the way Dominica's Grade 12 CAPE
course book teaches it beside the quadratic case: the three
relations read off any cubic's coefficients, the equation
rebuilt from any three roots, and unknowns recovered from
the relations alone (*Pure Mathematics: A Complete Course
for CAPE, Unit 1*, Module 1, Ch. 10, printed p. 207).
