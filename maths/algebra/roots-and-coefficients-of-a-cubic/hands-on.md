# Hands-on — Roots and Coefficients of a Cubic Equation

## Activity 1 — Expand and compare (10 minutes)

On paper, expand (x − 2)(x − 3)(x − 4) step by step. Before
expanding, predict the three coefficients from the roots
(sum 9, pairs 26, product 24) and write the prediction down.
Then expand and compare line by line: x³ − 9x² + 26x − 24.
This is Sub lesson 1 checked by hand.

## Activity 2 — Build-a-cubic cards (15 minutes)

Write root triples on cards: (−2, 1, 3), (0, −1, 4),
(2, 2, −3), (−1, −2, −3). For each card, build the monic
cubic from the sum, pairs and product, then check by
expanding the factors. Swap cards with a partner and check
each other's built equations by substituting one root —
it must give 0. This is Sub lesson 2 as a game.

## Activity 3 — Recover the unknown (15 minutes)

Partner A writes a cubic and covers one coefficient and one
root: for example x³ − 6x² + [?]x − 6 = 0 with roots 1, [?]
and 3. Partner B recovers both from the relations alone —
the product gives the missing root (1 × γ × 3 = 6, so
γ = 2), the pairs give the covered coefficient (2 + 6 + 3 =
11). Three rounds each way. This is Sub lesson 3 played:
the relations must do all the work, no solving allowed.
