# Video script — Roots and Coefficients of a Cubic Equation

Super-simple voice. The tutor talks; the cubic and its three
roots are on screen, and each relation lights up as it is
spoken.

---

A cubic has three roots hiding inside it. One, two, three.
And its numbers — the coefficients — already know them.

Add the roots. That is the first number, with its sign
flipped. Multiply them in pairs and add. That is the second
number, just as it stands. Multiply all three. That is the
last number, sign flipped again.

Watch. Roots one, two, three. Sum: six. Pairs: eleven.
Product: six. And the equation is x cubed, minus six x
squared, plus eleven x, minus six.

It runs backwards too. Give me three roots, and I will
build their equation. Sum, pairs, product — minus, plus,
minus — done.

And it finds secrets. Two roots known? The sum tells you
the third. No solving. The coefficients confess.
