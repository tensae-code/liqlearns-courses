**Acceptance count: taught in 8 countries (Kazakhstan, Jordan, Libya, Greece, Vietnam, Mauritania, Laos, Yemen).** Kazakhstan carries the fullest treatment — two-variable equations and both algebraic methods in Grade 6, the graphical method in Grade 7, and symmetric systems in Grade 9. Jordan carries the three methods in order in Grade 8: graphical, substitution, elimination. Libya carries the hens-and-roosters puzzle and all three algebraic methods plus the graphical interpretation in Grade 9. Greece carries systems in Grade 9, graphical first and algebraic second. Vietnam carries two-unknown linear systems in Grade 9. Mauritania carries equations, inequalities and systems in its 5AS-LO book, Chapter 3. South Africa is not in this lesson: its Grade 9 book states plainly that it teaches no simultaneous equations. Sierra Leone is not in this lesson: its JSS never teaches simultaneous equations. Egypt, Armenia and Ghana are not in this lesson on the filed stock. Laos carries simultaneous equations by elimination, substitution and graphically in Grade 8 (only the checker-verified systems cited — the filing's two unverified L14/L15 items are not used), through quadratic and symmetric systems in Grade 9, to linear systems in Grade 12. Yemen carries simultaneous equations from systems in two variables in Grade 9 to solving them by matrices in Grade 11, in both tracks.
