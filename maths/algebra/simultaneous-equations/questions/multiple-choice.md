# Simultaneous Equations — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Level 1)** A solution of a system is ______.
a) one number  b) a pair that fits both equations  c) any pair that fits one equation
**Answer: b) a pair that fits both equations** — the pair must satisfy both at once.

**2. (Level 1)** Two lines that never meet mean the system has ______.
a) one solution  b) no solution  c) infinitely many solutions
**Answer: b) no solution** — parallel lines never cross.

**3. (Level 2)** In x = 7 − 5y, substitution means ______.
a) putting 7 − 5y in place of x in the other equation  b) adding the equations  c) drawing both lines
**Answer: a) putting 7 − 5y in place of x in the other equation.**

**4. (Level 3)** To eliminate y from x + y = 7 and 2x − y = 5, you ______.
a) subtract the equations  b) add the equations  c) multiply the first by 2
**Answer: b) add the equations** — +y and −y cancel, giving 3x = 12.
