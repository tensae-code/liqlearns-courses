# Simultaneous Equations — fill in the blank

Fill the gap.

**1. (Level 1)** One equation with two unknowns has ______ many pairs.
**Answer:** infinitely.

**2. (Level 2)** Substitution turns two equations in two unknowns into one equation in ______ unknown.
**Answer:** one.

**3. (Level 3)** If adding the equations gives 0 = 5, the system has ______ solution.
**Answer:** no.
