# Simultaneous Equations — short answer

Answer in a few words.

**1. (Level 1)** Solve graphically in words: x + y = 5 and x − y = 1.
**Answer:** The lines cross at (3, 2); check: 3 + 2 = 5 and 3 − 2 = 1.

**2. (Level 2)** Solve by substitution: x = 7 − 5y, and the other equation gives −7y + 42 = 0.
**Answer:** y = 6, x = −23 (Kazakhstan, Grade 6, §54).

**3. (Level 3)** 3 hens + 4 roosters = 99 dinars; 2 hens + 3 roosters = 71 dinars. What does a rooster cost?
**Answer:** 15 dinars (a hen costs 13).
