# Video script — Simultaneous Equations

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Two equations! Two unknowns! ONE pair that fits BOTH!
That is a SYSTEM. The answer is a PAIR — an x AND a y!

Draw them! Two lines! Where do they CROSS?
The crossing IS the answer! Check it in both!

Or SUBSTITUTE. One equation says x equals 7 minus 5y.
Put that x into the OTHER equation. Now it is one unknown!
Solve it! Then find the other one!

Or ELIMINATE. Stack them up! Make one letter CANCEL!
Add them — poof, y is gone! One unknown left!
Solve it, then go back for the other!

No crossing? PARALLEL lines! No solution!
Same line twice? INFINITE solutions! Every point fits!
