1. (Sub lesson 1) A polynomial whose coefficients read the same in both directions is called a ______ polynomial.
   **Answer: reciprocal.** Venezuela, La Matemática y el Vivir Bien (Grade 11), printed p. 75.

2. (Sub lesson 3) Every reciprocal equation of odd degree has the root x = ______.
   **Answer: −1.** Venezuela, La Matemática y el Vivir Bien (Grade 11), printed p. 76.

3. (Sub lesson 4) With y = x + 1/x, the expression x² + 1/x² equals ______.
   **Answer: y² − 2.** Venezuela, La Matemática y el Vivir Bien (Grade 11), printed p. 77.

4. (Sub lesson 2) If −3 is a root of a reciprocal equation, then ______ is a root too.
   **Answer: −1/3** — the reciprocal of −3. Venezuela, La Matemática y el Vivir Bien (Grade 11), printed p. 75.
