**Acceptance count: taught in 1 country (Venezuela).** Venezuela is the one read country whose course names this class: its Grade 11 book, inside the polynomials lesson (Lesson 3), defines the reciprocal polynomial by p(1/x) = p(x)/xⁿ — symmetric coefficients — proves that an odd-degree reciprocal polynomial always has the root −1, works the cubic 2x³ + 3x² + 3x + 2 = 0 through that door, and solves the quartic 3x⁴ + 4x³ − 14x² + 4x + 3 = 0 by dividing by x² and substituting y = x + 1/x. Elsewhere in the library polynomial equations are met through division, the remainder and factor theorems and root hunts — the polynomial-division lesson — but the symmetry class and its fold are taught nowhere else, so under the everything-goes-in ruling (2026-11-06) the topic gets its own lesson, built at the size of its source. The root-pairing fact (a root r ≠ 0 forces the root 1/r) is the definition read at a root, and the lesson states it as such.
