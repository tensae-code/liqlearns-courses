# Hands-on — Algebraic Fractions

## Activity 1 — Cancel the pairs (10 minutes)

Write fractions on cards: 4x/6x², 10ab/(15a²b), 8xy²/(12x²y). The learner strikes out common factors one pair at a time — saying each pair aloud ("an x here, an x there — gone") — before writing the simplified fraction.

## Activity 2 — Build the bridge (10 minutes)

Give two fractions with different denominators (1/(3a) and 1/(2a); then 2/(5x) and 3/(4x)). For each pair the learner first writes the LCM of the denominators on a "bridge" card, then rewrites both fractions over it, then adds. The bridge card is never skipped: denominator first, numerators after.

## Activity 3 — Highest power hunt (5 minutes)

List pairs of terms — 4x²y and 6xy³; 8a³ and 12a²b — and find the LCM of each pair. Rule recited each time: numbers take the LCM; every letter takes its highest power; a letter in only one term still comes along.
