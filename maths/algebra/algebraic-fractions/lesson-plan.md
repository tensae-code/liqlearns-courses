# Lesson plan — Algebraic Fractions

**One thing:** fractions whose numerator or denominator carries letters — simplifying them, and adding and subtracting them by building a common denominator out of the lowest common multiple of the terms.

**Unit:** Algebra (Unit 6). **Level 138** (Malaysia merge, 2026-11-07).

**Why this lesson exists:** Malaysia's Form 2 book teaches algebraic fractions as the closing section of its factorisation chapter: LCM of algebraic terms first, then the fractions. Fractions with numbers are already a lesson (fractions, Unit 4) and partial fractions is a different, later thing (splitting a fraction apart); combining letter-fractions into one fraction had no home. Under the everything-goes-in ruling (2026-11-06), it gets its own lesson.

1. **Simplifying Algebraic Fractions** — what an algebraic fraction is, and cancelling common factors top and bottom to simplify one. (Was depth Layer 1.)
2. **Algebraic Fractions over a Common Denominator** — the LCM of algebraic terms — each letter at its highest power — and adding or subtracting fractions that already share a denominator. (Was depth Layer 2.)
3. **Algebraic Fractions as One Single Fraction** — rewriting fractions with different denominators over the lowest common denominator and combining them — or a whole number and a fraction — into one single fraction. (Was depth Layer 3.)

## The climb

1. **What an algebraic fraction is** — a fraction with letters in the numerator or denominator, e.g. 3/(2x), (x + 1)/5.
2. **Simplifying** — cancel common factors top and bottom: 6x²y/(9xy²) = 2x/(3y).
3. **The LCM of algebraic terms** — numbers take their LCM, each letter takes its highest power: LCM of 3a²b and 6ab³ is 6a²b³.
4. **Same denominator** — add or subtract the numerators, keep the denominator.
5. **Different denominators** — build the lowest common denominator from the LCM, rewrite each fraction, then combine into a single fraction.
