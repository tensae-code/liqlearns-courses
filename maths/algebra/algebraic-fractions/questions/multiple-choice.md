# Multiple choice — Algebraic Fractions

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Layer 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Simplifying Algebraic Fractions · (Layer 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Algebraic Fractions over a Common Denominator · (Layer 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Algebraic Fractions as One Single Fraction.

1. (Sub lesson 1) 10x²/(15x) simplifies to:
   A. 2x/3 B. 2/3x C. 5x D. x/3
   **Answer: A** — Malaysia, Mathematics Form 2, Ch. 2 §2.3, printed pp. 34–36.

2. (Sub lesson 2) The LCM of 4a²b and 6ab² is:
   A. 12a²b² B. 24a³b³ C. 12ab D. 6a²b²
   **Answer: A** — numbers take LCM(4, 6) = 12; each letter its highest power. Malaysia, Mathematics Form 2, Ch. 2 §2.3, printed pp. 36–37.

3. (Sub lesson 3) 1/(3x) + 1/(2x) equals:
   A. 2/(5x) B. 5/(6x) C. 1/(6x) D. 5/6x²
   **Answer: B** — common denominator 6x: 2/(6x) + 3/(6x). Malaysia, Mathematics Form 2, Ch. 2 §2.3, printed pp. 38–40.

4. (Sub lesson 1) Which of these can NOT be simplified by cancelling?
   A. 6x/(9x) B. (x + 4)/x C. 8xy/(12y) D. 5a²/(10a)
   **Answer: B** — the x above is part of a sum, not a factor. Malaysia, Mathematics Form 2, Ch. 2 §2.3, printed pp. 34–36.
