# Ordering — Algebraic Fractions

1. (Layer 3) Put the steps for working out 2/(3a) + 3/(5a) in the right order:
   (a) Add the new numerators: 10 + 9 (b) Find the LCM of 3a and 5a: 15a (c) Rewrite both fractions over 15a (d) Write the single fraction 19/(15a)
   **Answer: b, c, a, d.** Malaysia, Mathematics Form 2, Ch. 2 §2.3, printed pp. 38–40.
