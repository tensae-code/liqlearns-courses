# Short answer — Algebraic Fractions

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Layer 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Simplifying Algebraic Fractions · (Layer 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Algebraic Fractions over a Common Denominator · (Layer 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Algebraic Fractions as One Single Fraction.

1. (Sub lesson 1) Simplify 14a²b²/(21ab).
   **Answer: 2ab/3.** Malaysia, Mathematics Form 2, Ch. 2 §2.3, printed pp. 34–36.

2. (Sub lesson 2) Work out 7/(5m) − 2/(5m).
   **Answer: 5/(5m) = 1/m.** Malaysia, Mathematics Form 2, Ch. 2 §2.3, printed pp. 37–38.

3. (Sub lesson 3) Express 4 − 3/(y + 2) as a single fraction.
   **Answer: [4(y + 2) − 3]/(y + 2) = (4y + 5)/(y + 2).** Malaysia, Mathematics Form 2, Ch. 2 §2.3, printed pp. 38–40.
