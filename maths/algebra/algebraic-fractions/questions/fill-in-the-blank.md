# Fill in the blank — Algebraic Fractions

1. (Layer 2) The LCM of 2x and 3x² is ___.
   **Answer: 6x².** Malaysia, Mathematics Form 2, Ch. 2 §2.3, printed pp. 36–37.

2. (Layer 3) 3/(4a) + 1/(4a) = ___.
   **Answer: 1/a.** Malaysia, Mathematics Form 2, Ch. 2 §2.3, printed pp. 37–38.

3. (Layer 3) To combine 2/(5b) and 1/(3b), the lowest common denominator is ___.
   **Answer: 15b.** Malaysia, Mathematics Form 2, Ch. 2 §2.3, printed pp. 38–40.
