# True or false — Algebraic Fractions

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Layer 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Simplifying Algebraic Fractions · (Layer 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Algebraic Fractions over a Common Denominator · (Layer 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Algebraic Fractions as One Single Fraction.

1. (Sub lesson 1) (x + 5)/x simplifies to 5. **False** — terms of a sum never cancel. Malaysia, Mathematics Form 2, Ch. 2 §2.3, printed pp. 34–36.
2. (Sub lesson 2) The LCM of 3a²b and 6ab³ is 6a²b³. **True.** Malaysia, Mathematics Form 2, Ch. 2 §2.3, printed pp. 36–37.
3. (Sub lesson 3) 1/(2x) + 1/(2x) = 1/x. **True.** Malaysia, Mathematics Form 2, Ch. 2 §2.3, printed pp. 37–38.
