# True or false — Algebraic Fractions

1. (Layer 1) (x + 5)/x simplifies to 5. **False** — terms of a sum never cancel. Malaysia, Mathematics Form 2, Ch. 2 §2.3, printed pp. 34–36.
2. (Layer 2) The LCM of 3a²b and 6ab³ is 6a²b³. **True.** Malaysia, Mathematics Form 2, Ch. 2 §2.3, printed pp. 36–37.
3. (Layer 3) 1/(2x) + 1/(2x) = 1/x. **True.** Malaysia, Mathematics Form 2, Ch. 2 §2.3, printed pp. 37–38.
