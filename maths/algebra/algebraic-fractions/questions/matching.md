1. (Sub lesson 1) Match each fraction to its simplified form:
   (a) 9x²/(6x) (b) 4ab/(8b) (c) 12y²/(9y) (d) 10a/(25a²)
   Simplified: 3x/2, a/2, 4y/3, 2/(5a).
   **Answer: (a) 3x/2, (b) a/2, (c) 4y/3, (d) 2/(5a).** Malaysia, Mathematics Form 2, Ch. 2 §2.3, printed pp. 34–36.
