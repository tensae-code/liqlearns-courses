# Video script — Algebraic Fractions

Super-simple voice. The tutor talks; the fractions on screen change as they are named.

---

A fraction can carry letters. Three over two-x. X plus one, over five. These are algebraic fractions.

First, make them small. Six x-squared y over nine x y-squared. Cross out what is shared — top and bottom. Two x over three y. Small and clean.

To add two, the bottoms must match. Same bottom? Just add the tops.

Different bottoms? Build one. Find the LCM — the smallest thing both bottoms divide into. For letters, each letter takes its biggest power.

One over two-x, plus three over four-x. The common bottom is four-x. The first fraction becomes two over four-x. Now add: five over four-x. One fraction. Done.

Numbers first, letters after. That is the whole game.
