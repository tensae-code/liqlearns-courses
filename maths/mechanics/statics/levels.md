# Statics: forces in balance — questions by level

Practice by level, tied to the skill number. The questions live in
the type files; this file is the by-level view. The levels are the
depths inside this one thing — statics — forces and resolution first, then equilibrium and friction, then moments, parallel forces and couples, the order Egypt's books teach it in.

## Level 1 — forces, resolution and resultants
Level 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: name a force as a vector with its unit, resolve a force into its two right-angled components, and find the resultant of forces meeting at a point.

## Level 2 — equilibrium and friction
Level 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: use the two balance conditions at a point, work friction up to its limit, and handle limiting equilibrium on a flat plane and on an incline.

## Level 3 — moments, parallel forces and couples
Level 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: find a moment about a point and balance moments, find the resultant of parallel forces and where it acts, and find the moment of a couple and of a resultant couple.
