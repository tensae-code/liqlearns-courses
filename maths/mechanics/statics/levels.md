# Statics: forces in balance — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB
LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure — README, "Units, Lessons and Sub
Lessons"). Practice is tied to the skill number, as before. The
questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson
view.

The sub lessons are the depths inside this one thing — statics — forces and resolution first, then equilibrium and friction, then moments, parallel forces and couples, the order Egypt's books teach it in.

Mapping from the old depth layers: old Level 1 = Sub lesson 1; old Level 2 = Sub lesson 2; old Level 3 = Sub lesson 3.

## Sub lesson 1 — Forces, Resolution and Resultants
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: name a force as a vector with its unit, resolve a force into its two right-angled components, and find the resultant of forces meeting at a point.

## Sub lesson 2 — Equilibrium and Friction
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: use the two balance conditions at a point, work friction up to its limit, and handle limiting equilibrium on a flat plane and on an incline.

## Sub lesson 3 — Moments, Parallel Forces and Couples
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: find a moment about a point and balance moments, find the resultant of parallel forces and where it acts, and find the moment of a couple and of a resultant couple.
