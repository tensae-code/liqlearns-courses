# Statics: forces in balance — short answer

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Forces, Resolution and Resultants · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Equilibrium and Friction · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Moments, Parallel Forces and Couples.

Answer in a word or a short line, with your working.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Resolve a force of 20 N at 30° above the horizontal.
**Answer:** Horizontal component 20 cos 30° = 10√3 ≈ 17.3 N; vertical component 20 sin 30° = 10 N — cosine next to the angle, sine across from it (Egypt, Applied Grade 11 Term 1, lesson 1-2, printed p.20).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Find the resultant of 6 N east and 8 N north.
**Answer:** 10 N at tan⁻¹(8/6) ≈ 53.1° north of east — √(6² + 8²) = 10 (lesson 1-3, printed p.25).

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Two forces of 9 N and 4 N act at a point in opposite directions along one line. Find their resultant.
**Answer:** 5 N in the direction of the 9 N force — opposite forces on one line subtract (lesson 1-3, printed p.25).

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The horizontal components of the forces at a point are 5 N, −2 N and one unknown force F. The point is in equilibrium horizontally. Find F.
**Answer:** F = −3 N, that is 3 N in the negative direction — equilibrium needs ΣFx = 0, so 5 − 2 + F = 0 (lesson 1-4, printed p.31).

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** A body with normal reaction 40 N rests on a rough horizontal plane with μ = 0.25. Find the limiting friction force.
**Answer:** 10 N — limiting friction = μR = 0.25 × 40 (lesson 1-5, printed p.43).

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** A body rests in limiting equilibrium on an incline of 30°. Find μ.
**Answer:** μ = tan 30° = 1/√3 ≈ 0.58 — at the limiting tilt, μ = tan α (lesson 1-6, printed p.50).

**7. (Sub lesson 3)** Parallel forces of 4 N and 6 N in the same direction act 5 m apart. Find their resultant and where it acts.
**Answer:** 10 N, acting 3 m from the 4 N force (and 2 m from the 6 N force) — the resultant splits the distance in the inverse ratio, and 4 × 3 = 6 × 2 balances its moments (Egypt, Applied Grade 12, lesson 2-1, printed p.22).

**8. (Sub lesson 3)** About one point, a force gives a clockwise moment of 30 N·m and another gives an anticlockwise moment of 10 N·m × 3 m. Is the turning balanced?
**Answer:** Yes — the anticlockwise moment is 10 × 3 = 30 N·m, equal to the clockwise 30 N·m, so the total moment is zero (Grade 12, lesson 1-1, printed p.10).
