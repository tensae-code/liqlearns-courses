# Statics: forces in balance — fill in the blank

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Forces, Resolution and Resultants · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Equilibrium and Friction · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Moments, Parallel Forces and Couples.

Fill each blank with the missing word or number.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A force is measured in ______.
**Answer:** newtons (N) — the unit of force (Egypt, Applied Grade 11 Term 1, lesson 1-1, printed p.12).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** For a force F at angle θ above the horizontal, the vertical component is F ______ θ.
**Answer:** sin — the component across from the angle takes sine (lesson 1-2, printed p.20).

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** In equilibrium at a point, the sum of the horizontal components is ______.
**Answer:** 0 — and the sum of the vertical components is 0 as well (lesson 1-4, printed p.31).

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Limiting friction is given by F = ______ × R.
**Answer:** μ (the coefficient of friction) — the limit is μR (lesson 1-5, printed p.43).

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** The moment of a force is the force multiplied by the ______ distance from the point to the force's line.
**Answer:** perpendicular — only the perpendicular distance counts (Egypt, Applied Grade 12, lesson 1-1, printed p.10).

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** A couple consists of two forces that are equal, opposite and ______.
**Answer:** parallel (but not in the same line) — that is what leaves pure turning (Grade 12, lesson 3-1, printed p.42).
