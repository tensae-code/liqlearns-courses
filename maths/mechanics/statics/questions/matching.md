# Statics: forces in balance — matching

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Forces, Resolution and Resultants · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Equilibrium and Friction · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Moments, Parallel Forces and Couples.

Match each item on the left with the one right item on the right.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Match the term: (i) resultant; (ii) component; (iii) force. Meanings: (a) one of the two right-angled parts a force is split into; (b) a push or pull with size and direction; (c) the single force that does the same job as several forces together.
**Answer:** (i)–(c), (ii)–(a), (iii)–(b) — resultant replaces, component splits, force pushes or pulls (Egypt, Applied Grade 11 Term 1, lessons 1-1 to 1-3, printed pp.12, 20, 25).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Match the situation: (i) a body just about to slide; (ii) a body resting with a small pull that it easily holds; (iii) a body on a tilted plane about to slide down. Friction: (a) below its limit; (b) at its limit μR, acting up the slope; (c) at its limit μR.
**Answer:** (i)–(c), (ii)–(a), (iii)–(b) — the limit is reached only at limiting equilibrium (lessons 1-5 and 1-6, printed pp.43, 50).

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Match the quantity: (i) moment of a force; (ii) moment of a couple; (iii) resultant of two like parallel forces P and Q. Rules: (a) P + Q; (b) force × distance between the two lines; (c) force × perpendicular distance from the point.
**Answer:** (i)–(c), (ii)–(b), (iii)–(a) — moments multiply a force by its distance; like parallel forces add (Egypt, Applied Grade 12, lessons 1-1, 2-1 and 3-1, printed pp.10, 22, 42).

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Match the book part: (i) moments; (ii) parallel forces; (iii) couples. Grade 12 printed pages: (a) p.22; (b) p.42; (c) p.10.
**Answer:** (i)–(c), (ii)–(a), (iii)–(b) — the Grade 12 statics part runs moments, then parallel forces, then couples (Egypt, Applied Grade 12, lessons 1-1, 2-1 and 3-1).
