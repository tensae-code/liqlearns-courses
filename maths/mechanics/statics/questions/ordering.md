# Statics: forces in balance — ordering

Put the items in the order asked. Check each step before you write the answer.

**1. (Level 1)** Put these steps for finding a resultant in working order: (A) rebuild the size √(Rx² + Ry²); (B) resolve every force into components; (C) add the horizontal components into Rx and the vertical components into Ry.
**Answer:** B, C, A — resolve first, add by direction, then rebuild the one force (Egypt, Applied Grade 11 Term 1, lesson 1-3, printed p.25).

**2. (Level 2)** Put these pulls on a body with limiting friction 10 N in order, smallest first: 12 N, 4 N, 10 N.
**Answer:** 4 N, 10 N, 12 N — 4 N is held easily, 10 N is the limiting pull, and 12 N passes the limit so the body slides (lesson 1-5, printed p.43).

**3. (Level 2)** Put these steps for an incline problem in working order: (A) set the down-slope part equal to limiting friction; (B) split the weight into parts down and into the plane; (C) read off μ = tan α.
**Answer:** B, A, C — split the weight first, balance along the slope, then the tangent rule falls out (lesson 1-6, printed p.50).

**4. (Level 3)** Put these moments about the same point in order, smallest first: a force of 5 N at 2 m; a force of 20 N at 1 m; a couple of two 6 N forces 2 m apart.
**Answer:** 5 N at 2 m (10 N·m), the couple (6 × 2 = 12 N·m), 20 N at 1 m (20 N·m) — each moment is a force times its distance (Egypt, Applied Grade 12, lessons 1-1 and 3-1, printed pp.10, 42).
