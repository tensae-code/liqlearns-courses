# Statics: forces in balance — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Forces, Resolution and Resultants · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Equilibrium and Friction · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Moments, Parallel Forces and Couples.

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The horizontal component of a force of 10 N acting at 60° above the horizontal is ______.
a) 5 N  b) 8.66 N  c) 10 N
**Answer:** a) 5 N — the component next to the angle takes cosine: 10 cos 60° = 5 (Egypt, Applied Grade 11 Term 1, lesson 1-2, printed p.20).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The resultant of a force of 3 N east and a force of 4 N north is ______.
a) 5 N  b) 6 N  c) 7 N
**Answer:** a) 5 N — √(3² + 4²) = 5 (lesson 1-3, printed p.25).

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** A force is fully described by ______.
a) its size only  b) its size and its direction  c) its unit only
**Answer:** b) its size and its direction — a force is a vector, measured in newtons (lesson 1-1, printed p.12).

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A body at a point is in equilibrium exactly when ______.
a) ΣFx = 1 and ΣFy = 1  b) ΣFx = 0 and ΣFy = 0  c) every force is equal
**Answer:** b) ΣFx = 0 and ΣFy = 0 — the resultant is zero (lesson 1-4, printed p.31).

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** With normal reaction R = 20 N and coefficient of friction μ = 0.5, the limiting friction force is ______.
a) 5 N  b) 10 N  c) 40 N
**Answer:** b) 10 N — limiting friction is μR = 0.5 × 20 (lesson 1-5, printed p.43).

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** A body rests in limiting equilibrium on a rough incline. The coefficient of friction μ and the tilt angle α satisfy ______.
a) μ = sin α  b) μ = cos α  c) μ = tan α
**Answer:** c) μ = tan α — from W sin α = μW cos α with the weight cancelling (lesson 1-6, printed p.50).

**7. (Sub lesson 3)** The moment about a hinge of a force of 12 N acting perpendicularly 0.5 m from the hinge is ______.
a) 6 N·m  b) 12 N·m  c) 24 N·m
**Answer:** a) 6 N·m — moment = force × perpendicular distance = 12 × 0.5 (Egypt, Applied Grade 12, lesson 1-1, printed p.10).

**8. (Sub lesson 3)** The moment of a couple made of two forces of 8 N whose lines are 0.25 m apart is ______.
a) 1 N·m  b) 2 N·m  c) 8 N·m
**Answer:** b) 2 N·m — moment of a couple = F × d = 8 × 0.25 (Grade 12, lesson 3-1, printed p.42).
