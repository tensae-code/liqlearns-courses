**Acceptance count: taught in 3 countries (Egypt, Venezuela, Uzbekistan).** Egypt teaches statics whole in one Applied Mathematics run: forces, resolution, resultants and equilibrium at a point, then friction and limiting equilibrium on flat and inclined rough planes, in Grade 11 Term 1; and moments, parallel forces and couples in Part 1 (Statics) of its Grade 12 Applied Mathematics (Mechanics) book. No other read country teaches this material, so this lesson is built at that honest size — one country's full treatment, not a merged treatment.

Venezuela enters through the Colección Bicentenario, the MPPE's one-book-per-grade course: *Naturaleza Matemática* (Grade 10). From its books: from Grade 10: scalar vs vector magnitudes (age, temperature vs displacement, weight, velocity; speed as a scalar).
