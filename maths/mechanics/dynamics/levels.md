# Dynamics: motion, momentum and energy — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB
LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure — README, "Units, Lessons and Sub
Lessons"). Practice is tied to the skill number, as before. The
questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson
view.

The sub lessons are the depths inside this one thing — dynamics — straight-line motion and free fall first, then momentum, Newton's laws and impulse, then work, energy, power and the advanced Grade 12 run, the order Egypt's books teach it in.

Mapping from the old depth layers: the lesson text meets its parts in the order motion, momentum and Newton's laws, motion as a function of time, impulse, then work, energy and power, while old Level 2 grouped impulse with momentum and the laws and old Level 3 grouped the Grade 12 motion work with work, energy and power. The sub lessons follow the text's order, so old Level 2 splits — momentum and the laws are Sub lesson 2 and impulse is Sub lesson 4 — and old Level 3 splits — motion as a function of time and connected bodies are Sub lesson 3 and work, energy and power are Sub lesson 5 — and their pass marks split the same way below. Old Level 1 = Sub lesson 1.

## Sub lesson 1 — Rectilinear Motion and Free Fall
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: describe motion by position, velocity and acceleration, use the constant-acceleration equations, and work a free-fall problem with one sign direction held all the way through.

## Sub lesson 2 — Momentum and Newton's Laws
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: find momentum, and state and use Newton's three laws including F = ma with friction.

## Sub lesson 3 — Motion as a Function of Time, and Connected Bodies
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: differentiate or integrate a simple motion with respect to time, and handle a connected-bodies setup.

## Sub lesson 4 — Impulse
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: find impulse as force × time and as change of momentum.

## Sub lesson 5 — Work, Energy and Power
Sub lesson 5 questions from the type files. Pass mark: find work, kinetic and potential energy and power (including P = Fv).
