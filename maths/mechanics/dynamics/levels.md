# Dynamics: motion, momentum and energy — questions by level

Practice by level, tied to the skill number. The questions live in
the type files; this file is the by-level view. The levels are the
depths inside this one thing — dynamics — straight-line motion and free fall first, then momentum, Newton's laws and impulse, then work, energy, power and the advanced Grade 12 run, the order Egypt's books teach it in.

## Level 1 — rectilinear motion and free fall
Level 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: describe motion by position, velocity and acceleration, use the constant-acceleration equations, and work a free-fall problem with one sign direction held all the way through.

## Level 2 — momentum, Newton's laws and impulse
Level 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: find momentum, state and use Newton's three laws including F = ma with friction, and find impulse as force × time and as change of momentum.

## Level 3 — work, energy, power and advanced dynamics
Level 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: find work, kinetic and potential energy and power (including P = Fv), differentiate or integrate a simple motion with respect to time, and handle a connected-bodies setup.
