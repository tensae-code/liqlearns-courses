# Dynamics: motion, momentum and energy — fill in the blank

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Rectilinear Motion and Free Fall · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Momentum and Newton's Laws, except impulse items, which are Sub lesson 4 — Impulse · (Level 3) splits item by item: motion-as-a-function-of-time and connected-bodies items are Sub lesson 3, impulse items are Sub lesson 4, and work, energy and power items are Sub lesson 5 — Work, Energy and Power. Items that match or order several of these quantities together are marked with the last sub lesson they cover.

Fill each blank with the missing word or number.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In v = u + at, the letter a stands for the ______.
**Answer:** acceleration — the constant acceleration in uniformly accelerated motion (Egypt, Applied Grade 11 Term 2, lesson 1-2, printed p.26).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A freely falling body near the Earth accelerates downwards at the constant rate ______.
**Answer:** g (the acceleration due to gravity) — free fall is uniformly accelerated motion with a = g (lesson 1-3, printed p.35).

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Momentum is mass multiplied by ______.
**Answer:** velocity — p = mv (lesson 2-1, printed p.42).

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Newton's second law reads F = ______.
**Answer:** ma — resultant force equals mass times acceleration (lesson 2-3, printed p.53).

**5. (Sub lesson 4)** Impulse equals force multiplied by ______, and it equals the change of momentum.
**Answer:** time — J = Ft = mv − mu (Egypt, Applied Grade 12, lesson 2-3, printed p.112).

**6. (Sub lesson 5)** For a force driving a body along its own line, power is P = ______.
**Answer:** Fv — force times the velocity of its point of application (Grade 12, lesson 3-3, printed p.150).
