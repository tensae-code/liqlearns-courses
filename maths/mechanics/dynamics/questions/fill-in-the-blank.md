# Dynamics: motion, momentum and energy — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the missing word or number.

**1. (Level 1)** In v = u + at, the letter a stands for the ______.
**Answer:** acceleration — the constant acceleration in uniformly accelerated motion (Egypt, Applied Grade 11 Term 2, lesson 1-2, printed p.26).

**2. (Level 1)** A freely falling body near the Earth accelerates downwards at the constant rate ______.
**Answer:** g (the acceleration due to gravity) — free fall is uniformly accelerated motion with a = g (lesson 1-3, printed p.35).

**3. (Level 2)** Momentum is mass multiplied by ______.
**Answer:** velocity — p = mv (lesson 2-1, printed p.42).

**4. (Level 2)** Newton's second law reads F = ______.
**Answer:** ma — resultant force equals mass times acceleration (lesson 2-3, printed p.53).

**5. (Level 3)** Impulse equals force multiplied by ______, and it equals the change of momentum.
**Answer:** time — J = Ft = mv − mu (Egypt, Applied Grade 12, lesson 2-3, printed p.112).

**6. (Level 3)** For a force driving a body along its own line, power is P = ______.
**Answer:** Fv — force times the velocity of its point of application (Grade 12, lesson 3-3, printed p.150).
