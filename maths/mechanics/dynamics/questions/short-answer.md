# Dynamics: motion, momentum and energy — short answer

Answer in a word or a short line, with your working.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A body starts at 2 m/s and accelerates uniformly at 3 m/s² for 5 s. Find its final velocity.
**Answer:** 17 m/s — v = u + at = 2 + 3 × 5 (Egypt, Applied Grade 11 Term 2, lesson 1-2, printed p.26).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** For the body in question 1, find the distance covered in those 5 s.
**Answer:** 47.5 m — s = ut + ½at² = 2 × 5 + ½ × 3 × 25 = 10 + 37.5 (lesson 1-2, printed p.26).

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Taking g as 9.8 m/s² and down as positive, find the velocity of a body dropped from rest after 3 s.
**Answer:** 29.4 m/s downwards — v = gt = 9.8 × 3 (lesson 1-3, printed p.35).

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** State Newton's first law in one sentence.
**Answer:** A body with no resultant force on it keeps its velocity — at rest it stays at rest, and moving it keeps the same speed in the same straight line (lesson 2-2, printed p.46).

**5. (Sub lesson 4)** A resultant force of 8 N acts for 5 s on a body at rest of mass 5 kg. Find the impulse and the body's final velocity.
**Answer:** Impulse = Ft = 40 N·s, which is the change of momentum, so the final momentum is 40 kg·m/s and the velocity is 40 ÷ 5 = 8 m/s (Egypt, Applied Grade 12, lesson 2-3, printed p.112; Grade 11 Term 2, lesson 2-1, printed p.42).

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** If position is s = t³ (in its units), find the velocity and the acceleration, and their values at t = 2.
**Answer:** v = 3t² and a = 6t; at t = 2, v = 12 and a = 12 — velocity is the derivative of position, and acceleration is the derivative of velocity (Grade 12, lesson 1-1, printed p.72).

**7. (Sub lesson 5)** A force of 20 N pushes a body 3 m along its line, steadily, in 2 s. Find the work and the power.
**Answer:** Work = Fs = 60 J; power = W ÷ t = 60 ÷ 2 = 30 W (Grade 12, lessons 3-1 and 3-3, printed pp.124, 150).

**8. (Sub lesson 3)** Two bodies of masses 3 kg and 2 kg are joined by a light string and pulled together by a resultant force of 10 N on a smooth surface. Find their shared acceleration.
**Answer:** 2 m/s² — connected bodies move as one system of mass 3 + 2 = 5 kg, so a = F ÷ m = 10 ÷ 5 (Grade 12, lesson 2-2, printed p.95).
