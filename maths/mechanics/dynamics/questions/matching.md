# Dynamics: motion, momentum and energy — matching

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Rectilinear Motion and Free Fall · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Momentum and Newton's Laws, except impulse items, which are Sub lesson 4 — Impulse · (Level 3) splits item by item: motion-as-a-function-of-time and connected-bodies items are Sub lesson 3, impulse items are Sub lesson 4, and work, energy and power items are Sub lesson 5 — Work, Energy and Power. Items that match or order several of these quantities together are marked with the last sub lesson they cover.

Match each item on the left with the one right item on the right.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Match the quantity: (i) position; (ii) velocity; (iii) acceleration. Meanings: (a) the rate of change of velocity; (b) where a body is along its line; (c) the rate of change of position.
**Answer:** (i)–(b), (ii)–(c), (iii)–(a) — each quantity is the rate of change of the one before it (Egypt, Applied Grade 11 Term 2, lesson 1-1, printed p.15).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Match the law: (i) Newton's first law; (ii) Newton's second law; (iii) Newton's third law. Statements: (a) F = ma; (b) every action has an equal and opposite reaction; (c) a body with no resultant force keeps its velocity.
**Answer:** (i)–(c), (ii)–(a), (iii)–(b) — the three laws of the Grade 11 Term 2 unit (lessons 2-2, 2-3 and 2-4, printed pp.46, 53, 61).

**3. (Sub lesson 4)** Match the quantity to its rule: (i) impulse; (ii) work; (iii) power. Rules: (a) work ÷ time; (b) force × time; (c) force × distance along its line.
**Answer:** (i)–(b), (ii)–(c), (iii)–(a) — impulse, work and power from the Grade 12 book (lessons 2-3, 3-1 and 3-3, printed pp.112, 124, 150).

**4. (Sub lesson 5)** Match the Grade 12 dynamics part: (i) impulse; (ii) work; (iii) power. Printed pages: (a) p.150; (b) p.112; (c) p.124.
**Answer:** (i)–(b), (ii)–(c), (iii)–(a) — the advanced run runs impulse, then work, energy and power (Egypt, Applied Grade 12, lessons 2-3, 3-1 and 3-3).
