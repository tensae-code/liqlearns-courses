# Dynamics: motion, momentum and energy — matching

Match each item on the left with the one right item on the right.

**1. (Level 1)** Match the quantity: (i) position; (ii) velocity; (iii) acceleration. Meanings: (a) the rate of change of velocity; (b) where a body is along its line; (c) the rate of change of position.
**Answer:** (i)–(b), (ii)–(c), (iii)–(a) — each quantity is the rate of change of the one before it (Egypt, Applied Grade 11 Term 2, lesson 1-1, printed p.15).

**2. (Level 2)** Match the law: (i) Newton's first law; (ii) Newton's second law; (iii) Newton's third law. Statements: (a) F = ma; (b) every action has an equal and opposite reaction; (c) a body with no resultant force keeps its velocity.
**Answer:** (i)–(c), (ii)–(a), (iii)–(b) — the three laws of the Grade 11 Term 2 unit (lessons 2-2, 2-3 and 2-4, printed pp.46, 53, 61).

**3. (Level 3)** Match the quantity to its rule: (i) impulse; (ii) work; (iii) power. Rules: (a) work ÷ time; (b) force × time; (c) force × distance along its line.
**Answer:** (i)–(b), (ii)–(c), (iii)–(a) — impulse, work and power from the Grade 12 book (lessons 2-3, 3-1 and 3-3, printed pp.112, 124, 150).

**4. (Level 3)** Match the Grade 12 dynamics part: (i) impulse; (ii) work; (iii) power. Printed pages: (a) p.150; (b) p.112; (c) p.124.
**Answer:** (i)–(b), (ii)–(c), (iii)–(a) — the advanced run runs impulse, then work, energy and power (Egypt, Applied Grade 12, lessons 2-3, 3-1 and 3-3).
