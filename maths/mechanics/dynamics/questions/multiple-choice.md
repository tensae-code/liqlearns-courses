# Dynamics: motion, momentum and energy — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A body starts from rest with constant acceleration 3 m/s². Its velocity after 4 s is ______.
a) 7 m/s  b) 12 m/s  c) 24 m/s
**Answer:** b) 12 m/s — v = u + at = 0 + 3 × 4 (Egypt, Applied Grade 11 Term 2, lesson 1-2, printed p.26).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Taking g as 9.8 m/s², a body dropped from rest falls, in the first 2 s, ______.
a) 9.8 m  b) 19.6 m  c) 39.2 m
**Answer:** b) 19.6 m — s = ½gt² = ½ × 9.8 × 4 (lesson 1-3, printed p.35).

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The momentum of a body of mass 2 kg moving at 5 m/s is ______.
a) 2.5 kg·m/s  b) 7 kg·m/s  c) 10 kg·m/s
**Answer:** c) 10 kg·m/s — momentum p = mv = 2 × 5 (lesson 2-1, printed p.42).

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A resultant force of 20 N acts on a mass of 4 kg. Its acceleration is ______.
a) 5 m/s²  b) 16 m/s²  c) 80 m/s²
**Answer:** a) 5 m/s² — a = F ÷ m = 20 ÷ 4, by F = ma (lesson 2-3, printed p.53).

**5. (Sub lesson 4)** A force of 10 N acts for 3 s. The impulse it delivers is ______.
a) 3 N·s  b) 10 N·s  c) 30 N·s
**Answer:** c) 30 N·s — impulse = Ft = 10 × 3 (Egypt, Applied Grade 12, lesson 2-3, printed p.112).

**6. (Sub lesson 5)** A force of 15 N pushes a body 4 m along its own line. The work done is ______.
a) 19 J  b) 60 J  c) 120 J
**Answer:** b) 60 J — work W = Fs = 15 × 4 (Grade 12, lesson 3-1, printed p.124).

**7. (Sub lesson 5)** The kinetic energy of a body of mass 2 kg moving at 3 m/s is ______.
a) 6 J  b) 9 J  c) 18 J
**Answer:** b) 9 J — kinetic energy = ½mv² = ½ × 2 × 9 (Grade 12, lesson 3-2, printed p.136).

**8. (Sub lesson 5)** A driving force of 15 N acts on a body moving at 4 m/s along the force's line. The power is ______.
a) 19 W  b) 30 W  c) 60 W
**Answer:** c) 60 W — power P = Fv = 15 × 4 (Grade 12, lesson 3-3, printed p.150).
