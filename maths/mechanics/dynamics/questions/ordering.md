# Dynamics: motion, momentum and energy — ordering

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Rectilinear Motion and Free Fall · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Momentum and Newton's Laws, except impulse items, which are Sub lesson 4 — Impulse · (Level 3) splits item by item: motion-as-a-function-of-time and connected-bodies items are Sub lesson 3, impulse items are Sub lesson 4, and work, energy and power items are Sub lesson 5 — Work, Energy and Power. Items that match or order several of these quantities together are marked with the last sub lesson they cover.

Put the items in the order asked. Check each step before you write the answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Put these velocities of a body dropped from rest, taking g as 10 m/s², in the order it reaches them: 30 m/s, 10 m/s, 20 m/s.
**Answer:** 10 m/s, 20 m/s, 30 m/s — it gains 10 m/s every second, at 1 s, 2 s and 3 s (Egypt, Applied Grade 11 Term 2, lesson 1-3, printed p.35).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Put these momenta in order, smallest first: a mass of 2 kg at 6 m/s; a mass of 3 kg at 3 m/s; a mass of 1 kg at 20 m/s.
**Answer:** 3 kg at 3 m/s (9 kg·m/s), 2 kg at 6 m/s (12 kg·m/s), 1 kg at 20 m/s (20 kg·m/s) — momentum is mv in each case (lesson 2-1, printed p.42).

**3. (Sub lesson 5)** Put these steps for finding power from a push in working order: (A) divide the work by the time taken; (B) multiply the force by the distance moved along its line; (C) read off the power in watts.
**Answer:** B, A, C — work first (W = Fs), then the rate (P = W ÷ t) (Egypt, Applied Grade 12, lessons 3-1 and 3-3, printed pp.124, 150).

**4. (Sub lesson 5)** Put these Grade 12 topics in the order the book teaches them: power; differentiation of the vector functions of motion; impulse; work.
**Answer:** Differentiation of the vector functions of motion (printed p.72), impulse (printed p.112), work (printed p.124), power (printed p.150) — the order of Part 2, Dynamics, in the Grade 12 book.
