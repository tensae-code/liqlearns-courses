# Dynamics: motion, momentum and energy — ordering

Put the items in the order asked. Check each step before you write the answer.

**1. (Level 1)** Put these velocities of a body dropped from rest, taking g as 10 m/s², in the order it reaches them: 30 m/s, 10 m/s, 20 m/s.
**Answer:** 10 m/s, 20 m/s, 30 m/s — it gains 10 m/s every second, at 1 s, 2 s and 3 s (Egypt, Applied Grade 11 Term 2, lesson 1-3, printed p.35).

**2. (Level 2)** Put these momenta in order, smallest first: a mass of 2 kg at 6 m/s; a mass of 3 kg at 3 m/s; a mass of 1 kg at 20 m/s.
**Answer:** 3 kg at 3 m/s (9 kg·m/s), 2 kg at 6 m/s (12 kg·m/s), 1 kg at 20 m/s (20 kg·m/s) — momentum is mv in each case (lesson 2-1, printed p.42).

**3. (Level 3)** Put these steps for finding power from a push in working order: (A) divide the work by the time taken; (B) multiply the force by the distance moved along its line; (C) read off the power in watts.
**Answer:** B, A, C — work first (W = Fs), then the rate (P = W ÷ t) (Egypt, Applied Grade 12, lessons 3-1 and 3-3, printed pp.124, 150).

**4. (Level 3)** Put these Grade 12 topics in the order the book teaches them: power; differentiation of the vector functions of motion; impulse; work.
**Answer:** Differentiation of the vector functions of motion (printed p.72), impulse (printed p.112), work (printed p.124), power (printed p.150) — the order of Part 2, Dynamics, in the Grade 12 book.
