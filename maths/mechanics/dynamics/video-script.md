# Video script — Dynamics: motion, momentum and energy

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Roll a ball. It moves. It slows. It stops.
Something changed its motion. Dynamics asks: what, and by how much?

A body moves along a line. Where it is — that is its **position**.
How fast its place changes — **velocity**.
How fast its velocity changes — **acceleration**.

Let a stone fall. Gravity pulls it down.
Every second it gains the same speed. That is **free fall**.

A moving body carries **momentum**: mass times velocity.
Heavy and fast is hard to stop. Light and slow is easy.

Newton said it three ways.
One: no resultant force, no change — a body keeps its velocity.
Two: force equals mass times acceleration. **F = ma**.
Three: every action has an equal and opposite reaction.

Push for a time and you give an **impulse**: force times time.
The impulse is exactly the change of momentum.

Push a box along the floor and you do **work**: force times distance.
A lifted box holds **energy**. A moving box holds energy too.
Do the same work faster and you have more **power**.

Now you. Drop, roll, push, lift — and measure each one.
Say it whole. That is dynamics.
