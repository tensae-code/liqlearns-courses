# Video script — Hydrostatics

Super-simple voice. The tutor talks; a tank of water, a floating
block and a small press stay on screen.

---

Still water pushes. It pushes down, it pushes sideways, it
pushes up. The deeper you go, the harder it pushes. That is
pressure. And the whole push on a wall is thrust — pressure
times the area.

Watch the tank. Three holes. The lowest hole spurts the
furthest — the pressure down there is the greatest. A dam knows
this: thick at the bottom, thin at the top.

Now the block. Drop it in — it floats. The water pushes it up.
That up-push is exactly the weight of the water the block
pushes aside. When the pushed-aside water weighs the same as
the block, the block rests. A ship is the same trick, bigger.

Last, the press. Push the small piston — the pressure spreads
through the water, the same everywhere. On the big piston, the
same pressure meets a bigger area — and lifts a car.

Pressure, thrust, floating, the press. Still water, hard at
work.
