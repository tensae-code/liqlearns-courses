# Matching — Hydrostatics

Sub-lesson markers: every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to — Sub lesson 1 — Pressure in a Liquid · Sub lesson 2 — Thrust on Immersed Surfaces · Sub lesson 3 — Floating Bodies · Sub lesson 4 — Gases, Machines and Flow. This lesson was built new with its sub lessons (Sri Lanka expansion merge, 2026-11-11), so its markers are original — there are no older depth layers to map from.

1. (Sub lesson 1) Match each word to its meaning:
   (a) pressure
   (b) thrust
   (c) density
   Meanings: the whole push on a surface / push per unit area / how much matter a liquid packs into its volume.
   **Answer: (a) push per unit area; (b) the whole push on a surface; (c) how much matter a liquid packs into its volume.** Item constructed for this lesson from its founding teaching. Sri Lanka — Ramsey, *Hydrostatics* (Cambridge University Press, 1967) and Priedel, *Intermediate Hydrostatics* (Edward Arnold, 1969), the Sri Lanka-held GCE A/L texts, each read as a whole book.

2. (Sub lesson 3) Match each word to its meaning:
   (a) upthrust
   (b) displacement
   (c) hydrometer
   Meanings: the liquid a body pushes aside / the upward push of the liquid on a body / a floating instrument that reads a liquid's density.
   **Answer: (a) the upward push of the liquid on a body; (b) the liquid a body pushes aside; (c) a floating instrument that reads a liquid's density.** Item constructed for this lesson from its founding teaching. Sri Lanka — Ramsey, *Hydrostatics* (Cambridge University Press, 1967) and Priedel, *Intermediate Hydrostatics* (Edward Arnold, 1969), the Sri Lanka-held GCE A/L texts, each read as a whole book.

3. (Sub lesson 4) Match each device to the idea it runs on:
   (a) hydraulic press
   (b) barometer
   (c) siphon
   Ideas: air pressure carries liquid up and over a bend / the same pressure on a bigger area gives a bigger thrust / air pressure holds up a column of liquid.
   **Answer: (a) the same pressure on a bigger area gives a bigger thrust; (b) air pressure holds up a column of liquid; (c) air pressure carries liquid up and over a bend.** Item constructed for this lesson from its founding teaching.
