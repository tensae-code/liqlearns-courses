# Multiple choice — Hydrostatics

Sub-lesson markers: every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to — Sub lesson 1 — Pressure in a Liquid · Sub lesson 2 — Thrust on Immersed Surfaces · Sub lesson 3 — Floating Bodies · Sub lesson 4 — Gases, Machines and Flow. This lesson was built new with its sub lessons (Sri Lanka expansion merge, 2026-11-11), so its markers are original — there are no older depth layers to map from.

1. (Sub lesson 1) The pressure in a still liquid is greatest:
   (a) at the surface
   (b) deep down ✔
   (c) at the vessel's edges
   (d) the same everywhere
   **Answer: (b).** Item constructed for this lesson from its founding teaching. Sri Lanka — Ramsey, *Hydrostatics* (Cambridge University Press, 1967) and Priedel, *Intermediate Hydrostatics* (Edward Arnold, 1969), the Sri Lanka-held GCE A/L texts, each read as a whole book.

   (c) water is heavy
