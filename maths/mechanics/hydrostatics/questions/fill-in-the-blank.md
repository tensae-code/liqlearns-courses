# Fill in the blank — Hydrostatics

Sub-lesson markers: every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to — Sub lesson 1 — Pressure in a Liquid · Sub lesson 2 — Thrust on Immersed Surfaces · Sub lesson 3 — Floating Bodies · Sub lesson 4 — Gases, Machines and Flow. This lesson was built new with its sub lessons (Sri Lanka expansion merge, 2026-11-11), so its markers are original — there are no older depth layers to map from.

1. (Sub lesson 1) In a still liquid, pressure ___ as you go deeper.
   **Answer: increases (grows).** Item constructed for this lesson from its founding teaching. Sri Lanka — Ramsey, *Hydrostatics* (Cambridge University Press, 1967) and Priedel, *Intermediate Hydrostatics* (Edward Arnold, 1969), the Sri Lanka-held GCE A/L texts, each read as a whole book.

2. (Sub lesson 1) Thrust = pressure × ___.
   **Answer: area.** Item constructed for this lesson from its founding teaching. Sri Lanka — Ramsey, *Hydrostatics* (Cambridge University Press, 1967) and Priedel, *Intermediate Hydrostatics* (Edward Arnold, 1969), the Sri Lanka-held GCE A/L texts, each read as a whole book.
