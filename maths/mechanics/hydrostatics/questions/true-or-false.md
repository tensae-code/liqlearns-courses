# True or false — Hydrostatics

Sub-lesson markers: every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to — Sub lesson 1 — Pressure in a Liquid · Sub lesson 2 — Thrust on Immersed Surfaces · Sub lesson 3 — Floating Bodies · Sub lesson 4 — Gases, Machines and Flow. This lesson was built new with its sub lessons (Sri Lanka expansion merge, 2026-11-11), so its markers are original — there are no older depth layers to map from.

1. (Sub lesson 1) Pressure at a point in a still liquid pushes equally in all directions.
   **Answer: True.** Item constructed for this lesson from its founding teaching. Sri Lanka — Ramsey, *Hydrostatics* (Cambridge University Press, 1967) and Priedel, *Intermediate Hydrostatics* (Edward Arnold, 1969), the Sri Lanka-held GCE A/L texts, each read as a whole book.

2. (Sub lesson 1) A wide vessel always has greater pressure at its bottom than a narrow vessel filled to the same depth with the same liquid.
   **Answer: False** — at the same depth in the same liquid the pressure is the same, whatever the vessel's shape. Item constructed for this lesson from its founding teaching. Sri Lanka — Ramsey, *Hydrostatics* (Cambridge University Press, 1967) and Priedel, *Intermediate Hydrostatics* (Edward Arnold, 1969), the Sri Lanka-held GCE A/L texts, each read as a whole book.

3. (Sub lesson 2) The centre of pressure on a vertical wall is at the wall's exact middle.
   **Answer: False** — it lies below the middle, because pressure grows with depth. Item constructed for this lesson from its founding teaching. Sri Lanka — Ramsey, *Hydrostatics* (Cambridge University Press, 1967) and Priedel, *Intermediate Hydrostatics* (Edward Arnold, 1969), the Sri Lanka-held GCE A/L texts, each read as a whole book.

4. (Sub lesson 3) A body that can displace its own weight of water will float.
   **Answer: True.** Item constructed for this lesson from its founding teaching. Sri Lanka — Ramsey, *Hydrostatics* (Cambridge University Press, 1967) and Priedel, *Intermediate Hydrostatics* (Edward Arnold, 1969), the Sri Lanka-held GCE A/L texts, each read as a whole book.

5. (Sub lesson 4) Air can press on things — that pressure is what holds up a barometer's column and drives a siphon.
   **Answer: True.** Item constructed for this lesson from its founding teaching. Sri Lanka — Ramsey, *Hydrostatics* (Cambridge University Press, 1967) and Priedel, *Intermediate Hydrostatics* (Edward Arnold, 1969), the Sri Lanka-held GCE A/L texts, each read as a whole book.

6. (Sub lesson 4) In a hydraulic press, the pressure in the liquid is bigger under the big piston than under the small one.
   **Answer: False** — the pressure is the same throughout; the bigger area turns it into a bigger thrust. Item constructed for this lesson from its founding teaching.
