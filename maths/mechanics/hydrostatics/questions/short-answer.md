# Short answer — Hydrostatics

Sub-lesson markers: every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to — Sub lesson 1 — Pressure in a Liquid · Sub lesson 2 — Thrust on Immersed Surfaces · Sub lesson 3 — Floating Bodies · Sub lesson 4 — Gases, Machines and Flow. This lesson was built new with its sub lessons (Sri Lanka expansion merge, 2026-11-11), so its markers are original — there are no older depth layers to map from.

1. (Sub lesson 1) Why must a deep-sea diver's equipment stand far more pressure than a swimmer's at the surface?
   **Answer: pressure in a liquid grows with depth — deep down, the water presses far harder on every unit of area.** Item constructed for this lesson from its founding teaching. Sri Lanka — Ramsey, *Hydrostatics* (Cambridge University Press, 1967) and Priedel, *Intermediate Hydrostatics* (Edward Arnold, 1969), the Sri Lanka-held GCE A/L texts, each read as a whole book.

2. (Sub lesson 2) The average pressure on a tank wall is 300 units per square metre, and the wall's area is 2 square metres. What thrust acts on the wall?
   **Answer: 300 × 2 = 600 units of thrust.** Item constructed for this lesson from its founding teaching. Sri Lanka — Ramsey, *Hydrostatics* (Cambridge University Press, 1967) and Priedel, *Intermediate Hydrostatics* (Edward Arnold, 1969), the Sri Lanka-held GCE A/L texts, each read as a whole book.

3. (Sub lesson 3) A body weighs 5 N and pushes aside water weighing 5 N. What does it do?
   **Answer: it floats in balance — the upthrust (5 N) equals its weight (5 N).** Item constructed for this lesson from its founding teaching. Sri Lanka — Ramsey, *Hydrostatics* (Cambridge University Press, 1967) and Priedel, *Intermediate Hydrostatics* (Edward Arnold, 1969), the Sri Lanka-held GCE A/L texts, each read as a whole book.

4. (Sub lesson 3) State Archimedes' principle in your own words.
   **Answer: the upthrust on a body in a fluid equals the weight of the fluid the body displaces (pushes aside).** Item constructed for this lesson from its founding teaching. Sri Lanka — Ramsey, *Hydrostatics* (Cambridge University Press, 1967) and Priedel, *Intermediate Hydrostatics* (Edward Arnold, 1969), the Sri Lanka-held GCE A/L texts, each read as a whole book.

5. (Sub lesson 4) In a hydraulic press the pressure under both pistons is the same. The small piston has area 1 unit and is pressed with a thrust of 10 units; the big piston has area 5 units. What thrust can the big piston exert?
   **Answer: 50 units** — the same pressure on five times the area gives five times the thrust. Item constructed for this lesson from its founding teaching. Sri Lanka — Ramsey, *Hydrostatics* (Cambridge University Press, 1967) and Priedel, *Intermediate Hydrostatics* (Edward Arnold, 1969), the Sri Lanka-held GCE A/L texts, each read as a whole book.

6. (Sub lesson 4) Why does water spurt faster from a lower hole in a tank than from a higher one?
   **Answer: the pressure is greater at the greater depth, and it drives the jet out harder.** Item constructed for this lesson from its founding teaching.
