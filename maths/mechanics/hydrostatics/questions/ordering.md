# Ordering — Hydrostatics

Sub-lesson markers: every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to — Sub lesson 1 — Pressure in a Liquid · Sub lesson 2 — Thrust on Immersed Surfaces · Sub lesson 3 — Floating Bodies · Sub lesson 4 — Gases, Machines and Flow. This lesson was built new with its sub lessons (Sri Lanka expansion merge, 2026-11-11), so its markers are original — there are no older depth layers to map from.

1. (Sub lesson 1) Put these points in order of pressure in a tank of water, least first:
   at the surface / 1 metre down / 3 metres down / 5 metres down
   **Answer: at the surface → 1 metre down → 3 metres down → 5 metres down.** Item constructed for this lesson from its founding teaching. Sri Lanka — Ramsey, *Hydrostatics* (Cambridge University Press, 1967) and Priedel, *Intermediate Hydrostatics* (Edward Arnold, 1969), the Sri Lanka-held GCE A/L texts, each read as a whole book.

2. (Sub lesson 3) Put in order what happens as a wooden block is lowered into water and let go:
   held at the surface / pushed down so it displaces more and more water / upthrust grows until it matches the block's weight / the block settles, floating
   **Answer: held at the surface → pushed down, displacing more and more water → upthrust grows to match its weight → the block settles, floating.** Item constructed for this lesson from its founding teaching. Sri Lanka — Ramsey, *Hydrostatics* (Cambridge University Press, 1967) and Priedel, *Intermediate Hydrostatics* (Edward Arnold, 1969), the Sri Lanka-held GCE A/L texts, each read as a whole book.

3. (Sub lesson 4) Put the hydraulic press's action in order:
   the small piston is pushed down / the pressure spreads through the whole liquid / the big piston rises / the load is lifted
   **Answer: the small piston is pushed down → the pressure spreads through the whole liquid → the big piston rises → the load is lifted.** Item constructed for this lesson from its founding teaching. Sri Lanka — Ramsey, *Hydrostatics* (Cambridge University Press, 1967) and Priedel, *Intermediate Hydrostatics* (Edward Arnold, 1969), the Sri Lanka-held GCE A/L texts, each read as a whole book.
