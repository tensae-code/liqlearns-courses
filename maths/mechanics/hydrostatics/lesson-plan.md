The statics of liquids and gases: how a still liquid pushes,
what it pushes with, why things float, and the machines that run
on liquid pressure. The course's Mechanics unit teaches statics
and dynamics of solid bodies; nothing in it taught fluids at
rest. Sri Lanka's A/L set holds two whole books on exactly this
one thing — Ramsey's *Hydrostatics* (1967) and Priedel's
*Intermediate Hydrostatics* (1969), the books its GCE A/L
candidates study from — and the expansion read flagged the topic
NEW LESSON NEEDED. Prerequisite (soft hint only — every lesson
stands alone): statics for forces in balance, and
volume-and-surface-area for the areas thrust acts on. A learner
who opens this lesson directly still gets the whole thing: every
idea is met and named here.
