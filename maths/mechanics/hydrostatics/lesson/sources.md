**Book S19:** *Hydrostatics* (Tamil medium; EPD Tamil translation of A. S. Ramsey's *Hydrostatics*, Cambridge University Press, by arrangement) — Sri Lanka, Educational Publications Department (EPD), first edition 1967; 37,855,944 bytes, 192 PDF pp. (Noolaham scan). A single-subject A/L treatise: fluid pressure, thrust on immersed surfaces, flotation and stability, in Ramsey's order.

**Book S20:** *Intermediate Hydrostatics* (Tamil medium; EPD Tamil translation of E. E. Priedel's *Intermediate Hydrostatics*, University Tutorial Press, by arrangement) — Sri Lanka, Educational Publications Department (EPD), first edition 1969; 318,098,726 bytes, 293 PDF pp. (Noolaham scan). A single-subject A/L treatise in thirteen chapters: fluid pressure; density and specific gravity; pressure on immersed plane surfaces; equilibrium of floating bodies; liquids determinations; gases; hydrostatic machines; flow in pipes.
