# Mean Deviation and Variation — ordering

Put the items in the order asked. Check each step before you write the answer.

**1. (Level 1)** Order these spreads of the absent students 3, 6, 6, 7, 8, 11, 15, 16 from smallest to largest: range 13, mean deviation 3.75, quartile deviation 3.75.
Answer in order, smallest first.
**Answer:** Mean deviation 3.75 and quartile deviation 3.75 (equal), then range 13 — the range, using only two values, is the largest and the crudest.

**2. (Level 2)** Order the steps for a standard deviation by the assumed-mean method (Bangladesh, pp.229–232): divide Σd² by n and subtract (Σd/n)²; choose an assumed mean A; take the square root; write each deviation d = x − A.
Answer in order, first step first.
**Answer:** Choose A → write d = x − A → divide and subtract → take the square root — the book's weights example ends at σ = √40.5 ≈ 6.36.

**3. (Level 3)** Order these sets by coefficient of variation, steadiest first: P (mean 20, σ 4), Q (mean 50, σ 5), R (mean 10, σ 3).
Answer in order, steadiest first.
**Answer:** Q (10%), P (20%), R (30%) — CV, not raw σ, gives the fair order.
