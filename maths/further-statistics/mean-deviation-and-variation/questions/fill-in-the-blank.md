# Mean Deviation and Variation — fill-in-the-blank

Fill in the missing number or word.

**1. (Level 1)** Range = highest value − ___ value.
**Answer:** lowest

**2. (Level 1)** The mean deviation is the average of the ___ from the centre.
**Answer:** distances (absolute deviations)

**3. (Level 1)** Quartile deviation = (Q3 − Q1)/___.
**Answer:** 2

**4. (Level 2)** Variance is the average of the ___ deviations from the mean.
**Answer:** squared

**5. (Level 2)** The standard deviation is the ___ root of the variance.
**Answer:** square

**6. (Level 3)** Coefficient of variation = (σ/x̄) × ___%.
**Answer:** 100
