# Mean Deviation and Variation — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Range, Mean Deviation and Quartile Deviation · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Variance, Standard Deviation and the Two Conventions · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — The Coefficient of Variation and Judging Consistency.

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Batsmen A and B both have mean 53 (Bangladesh, Class 9, Ch. 9). What does that fact alone tell you?
a) Their spreads are equal  b) Nothing about their spreads  c) B is steadier
**Answer:** b) Nothing about their spreads — the centre is not the spread; that is the chapter's opening point (pp.212–214).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The range of 3, 6, 6, 7, 8, 11, 15, 16 is ______.
a) 9  b) 13  c) 16
**Answer:** b) 13 — range = highest − lowest = 16 − 3 (Bangladesh, p.215).

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The mean deviation of the absent students 3, 6, 6, 7, 8, 11, 15, 16 (mean 9) is ______.
a) 3.75  b) 9  c) 30
**Answer:** a) 3.75 — the distances from 9 total 30, and 30/8 = 3.75 (Bangladesh, p.218).

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** The quartile deviation of a set with Q1 = 6 and Q3 = 14 is ______.
a) 20  b) 10  c) 4
**Answer:** c) 4 — QD = (Q3 − Q1)/2 = 8/2 (Pakistan, Ch. 4).

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Karl Pearson introduced the standard deviation in ______.
a) 1983  b) 1893  c) 1793
**Answer:** b) 1893 — Bangladesh's p.223 prints 1983; that is a book error, taught corrected.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** A sample's variance is found by Laos's Grade 9 book as s² = Σ(x − x̄)²/(n − 1). For n = 5 values with Σ(x − x̄)² = 40, s² is ______.
a) 8  b) 10  c) 40
**Answer:** b) 10 — the sample door divides by n − 1 = 4 (Laos, L44, p.239).

**7. (Sub lesson 2)** The same Σ(x − x̄)² = 40 with n = 5, worked by Pakistan's Statistics Class 11 convention for a sample, gives S² = ______.
a) 10  b) 8  c) 4
**Answer:** b) 8 — Pakistan's book defines sample variance with denominator n too (Ch. 4).

**8. (Sub lesson 3)** Set X has mean 50, σ = 5. Set Y has mean 80, σ = 8. By coefficient of variation, ______.
a) X is steadier  b) Y is steadier  c) They are equally consistent
**Answer:** c) They are equally consistent — CV(X) = 10% and CV(Y) = 10% (Pakistan, Ch. 4).
