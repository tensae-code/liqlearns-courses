# Mean Deviation and Variation — matching

Match each item on the left to its partner on the right.

**1. (Level 1)** Match each measure to its formula: (a) range (b) quartile deviation (c) mean deviation — (i) average of |x − x̄| (ii) highest − lowest (iii) (Q3 − Q1)/2
**Answer:** a) (ii), b) (iii), c) (i).

**2. (Level 2)** Match each book to its sample-variance door: (a) Pakistan, Statistics Class 11 (b) Laos, Grade 9 — (i) divide by n − 1 (ii) divide by n
**Answer:** a) (ii), b) (i) — both conventions kept with their countries' names.

**3. (Level 2)** Match each corrected fact to its book slip: (a) Pearson's year (b) the larger family dispersion in Bangladesh's example — (i) Aleya's, not Thomas's (ii) 1893, not 1983
**Answer:** a) (ii), b) (i).

**4. (Level 3)** Match each job to its measure: (a) quick first look at spread (b) fair comparison across different means (c) average distance from the centre — (i) mean deviation (ii) coefficient of variation (iii) range
**Answer:** a) (iii), b) (ii), c) (i).
