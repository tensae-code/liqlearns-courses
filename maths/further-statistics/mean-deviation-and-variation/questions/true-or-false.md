# Mean Deviation and Variation — true or false

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Level 1)** Two sets with the same mean must have the same spread.
**Answer:** False — Bangladesh's two batsmen both average 53 with very different spreads (pp.212–214).

**2. (Level 1)** Bangladesh's Class 9 book teaches quartile deviation in Chapter 9.
**Answer:** False — it names quartile deviation in the opener diagram (p.211) but never teaches it; this lesson's quartile deviation stands on Pakistan.

**3. (Level 2)** Karl Pearson introduced the standard deviation in 1983.
**Answer:** False — the year is 1893; 1983 is the book's printed error on p.223, taught corrected.

**4. (Level 2)** Pakistan's Statistics Class 11 book defines sample variance with denominator n − 1.
**Answer:** False — it defines both population and sample variance with denominator n (Ch. 4); the n − 1 sample rule here is Laos's.

**5. (Level 2)** The signed deviations of a set from its own mean add to zero.
**Answer:** True — which is why spread measures drop the signs or square (Bangladesh, pp.217–222).

**6. (Level 3)** A set with the larger standard deviation always has the larger coefficient of variation.
**Answer:** False — CV divides by the mean; σ = 8 around a mean of 80 and σ = 5 around a mean of 50 both give CV = 10%.
