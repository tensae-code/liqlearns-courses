# Mean Deviation and Variation — questions by level

Practice by level, tied to the skill number. The questions live in
the type files; this file is the by-level view. The levels are the
depths inside this one thing — first the simple spreads, then variance and its two conventions, then fair comparison.

## Level 1 — range, mean deviation and quartile deviation
Level 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: find a range, a mean deviation from the mean, and a quartile deviation with its coefficient, and say in one sentence what each number means.

## Level 2 — variance, standard deviation and the two conventions
Level 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: find variance and standard deviation by the direct method, state Pearson's year correctly as 1893, and say which denominator — n or n − 1 — a calculation used and which country's book uses it.

## Level 3 — the coefficient of variation and judging consistency
Level 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: compute a coefficient of variation, compare two sets with it, and defend the verdict — including when the raw standard deviations point the other way.
