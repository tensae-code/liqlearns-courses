# Video script — Mean Deviation and Variation

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Two players. Same average: **fifty-three**.
One is steady. One is wild — zero, then a hundred!
The average cannot tell them apart. **Spread** can.

How far is each score from the middle?
Add the distances. Share them out. That is **mean deviation**.

Square the distances instead, and you get **variance**.
Its square root is the **standard deviation**.

Careful! Two books, two doors.
Pakistan divides by **n**. Laos divides a sample by **n minus one**.
Both are right — in their own books. Say which door you used!

And to compare fairly? Spread as a share of the mean —
the **coefficient of variation**. Smaller share, steadier set.

Now you. Take your numbers. Measure the spread. Say it whole!
