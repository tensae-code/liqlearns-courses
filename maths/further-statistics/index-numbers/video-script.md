# Video script — Index Numbers

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Bread cost 20 last year. Bread costs 25 now.
Twenty-five is 125 percent of twenty.
That 125 is an **index number** — with last year set to 100.

One item is easy. But a family buys hundreds of things.
So we take a whole basket, price it in the base year,
price the same basket now, and compare. One number for the whole change.

Whose basket? That is the whole game.
The base year's basket: **Laspeyres**.
This year's basket: **Paasche**.
Halfway between them, the geometric mean: **Fisher** — the ideal index.

Do this for everything a family buys and you get the CPI,
the consumer price index.
If the CPI doubles, your money buys half. Purchasing power is one over the CPI.
And when the index itself rises, that rise is **inflation**.

One basket. One base year. One number. That is an index.
