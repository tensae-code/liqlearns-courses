# Index Numbers — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the missing word or number.

**1. (Level 1)** The period an index is compared against, set to 100, is the ______ period.
**Answer:** base — every index is a comparison against it (Pakistan, Ch. 5).

**2. (Level 1)** A link relative compares each year with the year ______ before it.
**Answer:** just — chaining the links rebuilds a fixed-base index (Pakistan, Ch. 5).

**3. (Level 2)** Fisher's index is the ______ mean of Laspeyres and Paasche.
**Answer:** geometric — F = √(L × P) (Pakistan, Ch. 5).

**4. (Level 2)** In Pakistan's Example 5.7, taught corrected, Paasche = ______.
**Answer:** 110.4 — not the printed 112.8 (p.143, corrected).

**5. (Level 3)** Purchasing power of money = 1 / ______.
**Answer:** CPI — Pakistan, Ch. 5.

**6. (Level 3)** Iran defines ______ as the change of the price index.
**Answer:** inflation — Mathematics and Statistics (2), Grade 11, p.60.
