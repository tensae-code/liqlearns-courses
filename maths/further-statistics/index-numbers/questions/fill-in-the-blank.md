# Index Numbers — fill in the blank

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Simple Indices, Relatives and Chain Indices · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — The Weighted Indices · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — CPI, Purchasing Power, Changing the Base and Inflation.

Fill each blank with the missing word or number.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The period an index is compared against, set to 100, is the ______ period.
**Answer:** base — every index is a comparison against it (Pakistan, Ch. 5).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A link relative compares each year with the year ______ before it.
**Answer:** just — chaining the links rebuilds a fixed-base index (Pakistan, Ch. 5).

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Fisher's index is the ______ mean of Laspeyres and Paasche.
**Answer:** geometric — F = √(L × P) (Pakistan, Ch. 5).

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In Pakistan's Example 5.7, taught corrected, Paasche = ______.
**Answer:** 110.4 — not the printed 112.8 (p.143, corrected).

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Purchasing power of money = 1 / ______.
**Answer:** CPI — Pakistan, Ch. 5.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** Iran defines ______ as the change of the price index.
**Answer:** inflation — Mathematics and Statistics (2), Grade 11, p.60.
