# Index Numbers — short answer

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Level 1)** Relatives for three items are 120, 125, 110. Find their average (mean).
**Answer:** About 118.3 — (120+125+110)/3 (Pakistan, Ch. 5).

**2. (Level 1)** State the corrected potato link relatives in Pakistan's Example 5.6, for 1981 and 1982.
**Answer:** 111.1 and 100.0 — not the printed 112.5 and 105.6 (p.140, corrected).

**3. (Level 2)** Whose quantities does Paasche use, in symbols?
**Answer:** The current year's, qₙ — P = Σpₙqₙ/Σp₀qₙ × 100 (Pakistan, Ch. 5).

**4. (Level 2)** In Pakistan's Example 5.7, taught corrected, what is Σp₀qₙ?
**Answer:** 480 — not the printed 470; B's cell is 180 and D's is 50 (p.143, corrected).

**5. (Level 2)** Given Laspeyres 118.8 and Paasche 110.4, find Fisher.
**Answer:** About 114.5 — F = √(L × P) = √(118.8 × 110.4).

**6. (Level 3)** How many items and groups does Pakistan's CPI use, as its Class 11 book describes?
**Answer:** 464 items in 12 groups, by the family budget method ΣIW/ΣW (Pakistan, Ch. 5).

**7. (Level 3)** A series reads 120, 150, 180 on an old base. Rebase it to the middle year.
**Answer:** 80, 100, 120 — divide each by 150 and multiply by 100 (Libya, Ch. 2).

**8. (Level 3)** Iran's basket example gives an index of about 140.5. Prices rose by what percentage?
**Answer:** About 40.5% — the rise is the index minus the base's 100 (Iran, Mathematics and Statistics (2), p.59).
