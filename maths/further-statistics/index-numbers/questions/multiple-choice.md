# Index Numbers — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Level 1)** An index number of 125 for prices, base year = 100, means prices are:
a) 125% higher than the base year  b) 25% higher than the base year  c) 125 times the base year
**Answer:** b) 25% higher — the index counts the base's own 100 inside itself (Pakistan, Ch. 5).

**2. (Level 1)** Base prices 20, 30, 50; current prices 25, 36, 60. The simple aggregative index is:
a) 121  b) 111  c) 131
**Answer:** a) 121 — (25+36+60)/(20+30+50) × 100 = 121/100 × 100.

**3. (Level 1)** Pakistan's Example 5.6, taught corrected: the potato link relative for 1982 is:
a) 105.6  b) 111.1  c) 100.0
**Answer:** c) 100.0 — 30/30 × 100; the printed 105.6 was copied from the onion column (p.140, corrected).

**4. (Level 2)** Laspeyres weighs prices with:
a) base-year quantities q₀  b) current-year quantities qₙ  c) the sum of both years' quantities
**Answer:** a) base-year quantities — it prices the old basket at new prices (Pakistan, Ch. 5).

**5. (Level 2)** Marshall–Edgeworth weighs with:
a) q₀ only  b) qₙ only  c) q₀ + qₙ
**Answer:** c) q₀ + qₙ — both years' quantities added (Libya, Ch. 2, pp.33–58).

**6. (Level 2)** Pakistan's Example 5.7, taught corrected, gives Fisher's index as:
a) 115.8  b) 114.5  c) 118.8
**Answer:** b) 114.5 — √(118.8 × 110.4); the printed 115.8 rests on the misprinted Σp₀qₙ = 470 (p.143, corrected).

**7. (Level 3)** With the CPI at 250 (base = 100), the purchasing power of money is:
a) 2.50  b) 0.40  c) 250
**Answer:** b) 0.40 — purchasing power = 1/CPI = 1/2.50 (Pakistan, Ch. 5).

**8. (Level 3)** Prices rise from 28 to 32. The inflation rate is:
a) exactly 14%  b) about 12.5%  c) about 14.3%
**Answer:** c) about 14.3% — (32−28)/28 × 100 = 14.2857…%; Iran's printed 14% is a rounding taught corrected (Iran, Statistics and Probability, pp.89–90, corrected).
