# Video script — Geometric and Experimental Probability

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Throw a dart at the board. No counting now — too many landing spots!
So **measure**. The small part over the whole part.
That share is the chance. That is **geometric probability** —
on a line, on a flat board, even in a box.

No picture? Then **try it**. Toss the coin. Ten times — wobbly!
A hundred times — steadier. A thousand — almost still.
The share of heads **settles** near the true chance.
That is **experimental probability**.

Pearson tossed a coin twenty-four **thousand** times.
Heads: twelve thousand and twelve. The share? Point five, oh, oh, five.
The more you try, the stiller it gets.

Now you. Measure one chance. Toss for another. Say both — whole sentences!
