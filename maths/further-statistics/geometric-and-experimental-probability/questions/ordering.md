# Geometric and Experimental Probability — ordering

Put the items in the order asked. Check each step before you write the answer.

**1. (Level 1)** Order the steps for an area probability: divide favourable area by whole area; draw the region; measure both areas.
Answer in order, first step first.
**Answer:** Draw the region → measure both areas → divide favourable by whole.

**2. (Level 2)** Order Soltan's coin frequencies by the tosses behind them, fewest first: 0.508, 0.49, 0.505.
Answer in order, fewest tosses first.
**Answer:** 0.49 (100 tosses), 0.505 (200), 0.508 (250) — settling toward 1/2 (§32, p.234).

**3. (Level 3)** Order these estimates of a fair coin's heads probability by the trials behind them, weakest evidence first: Pearson's 24,000 tosses; Nicaragua's 500 tosses; your own 20 tosses.
Answer in order, weakest first.
**Answer:** 20 tosses, 500 tosses, 24,000 tosses — the more trials, the steadier the frequency.
