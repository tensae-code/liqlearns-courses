# Geometric and Experimental Probability — matching

Match each item on the left to its partner on the right.

**1. (Level 1)** Match each setting to its measure: (a) a point on a segment (b) a dart on a board (c) a point in a box — (i) volume (ii) length (iii) area
**Answer:** a) (ii), b) (iii), c) (i) — Abylkasymova's three measures (§32, p.113).

**2. (Level 2)** Match each problem to its book: (a) the meeting problem (b) the broken stick, answer 1/4 (c) the dartboard — (i) Soltan (ii) Shynybekov (iii) Yelemessov
**Answer:** a) (ii), b) (i), c) (iii).

**3. (Level 3)** Match each frequency story to its value: (a) Pearson's heads share (b) girls among newborns (c) Soltan's series at 250 tosses — (i) 0.48 (ii) 0.508 (iii) 0.5005
**Answer:** a) (iii), b) (i), c) (ii).

**4. (Level 3)** Match each kind to its birth: (a) geometric probability (b) experimental probability (c) classical probability — (i) counting equally likely outcomes (ii) measuring a favourable share (iii) a settling relative frequency
**Answer:** a) (ii), b) (iii), c) (i).
