# Geometric and Experimental Probability — questions by level

Practice by level, tied to the skill number. The questions live in
the type files; this file is the by-level view. The levels are the
depths inside this one thing — area and length ratios first, then the harder regions, then probability from frequency.

## Level 1 — probability from length and area
Level 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: state geometric probability as favourable measure over whole measure and work a segment and a dartboard item without help.

## Level 2 — regions, meetings and the broken stick
Level 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: set up a region probability from a picture or an inequality, and explain the meeting problem and the broken-stick answer of 1/4 in your own words.

## Level 3 — probability from frequency
Level 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: compute a relative frequency, say what "stabilising" means with a book's numbers behind you, and judge when a frequency is — and is not — a fair estimate of a probability.
