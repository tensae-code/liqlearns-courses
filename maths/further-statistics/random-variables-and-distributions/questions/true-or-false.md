# Random Variables and Distributions — true or false

Say true or false, and why.

**1. (Level 1)** A pmf table whose probabilities sum to 1.07 is a valid distribution.
**Answer:** False — Pakistan's p.206 example sums to 1.07 as printed and is taught corrected; a valid table sums to exactly 1.

**2. (Level 1)** The uniform distribution is taught in Indonesia's TL Revisi book.
**Answer:** False — the uniform distribution rests on the 2022 TL book only; it does not occur in the Revisi book.

**3. (Level 2)** For a discrete variable, P(a ≤ X < b) always equals F(b) − F(a).
**Answer:** False — Kuwait's p.169 summary says so, but for discrete variables the point masses matter; teach the corrected forms.

**4. (Level 2)** The variance of a binomial variable with n = 5, p = 1/2 is 1.25.
**Answer:** True — Var = npq = 5 × 1/2 × 1/2 = 1.25 (Kuwait Grade 12 Statistics, Ex. 19).

**5. (Level 3)** Vietnam's books teach the normal distribution.
**Answer:** False — Vietnam's only distributions are discrete variables and the binomial in the Grade 12 study book; no continuous distributions anywhere, as filed.

**6. (Level 3)** Pakistan's Statistics Class 12 appendix tables at printed pp.301–306 may be cited for the χ² and t variables.
**Answer:** False — those shuffled appendix leaves are cited nowhere; the χ² and t variables are cited at pp.287–288.
