## Sub lesson 1 — Variables, pmf and pdf
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: say what a random variable is, split discrete from continuous, write a pmf table and check it sums to 1, and say what a pdf's total area means.

## Sub lesson 2 — Expectation, Variance and the CDF
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: find E(X) and Var(X) from a table, build F(x) = P(X ≤ x) step by step, and use the corrected discrete interval forms.

## Sub lesson 3 — The Named Distributions, Z-Scores and the Empirical Rule
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: choose and work the right named distribution — binomial, hypergeometric, Poisson, uniform, geometric or normal — standardise with a z-score, and apply the empirical rule 68–95–99.7.
