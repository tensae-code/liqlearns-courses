# Video script — Monte Carlo and Simulation

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Two hundred coin tosses? Your arm says **no**.
So build a **factory** for chance!

**Random digits** — no favourites, no patterns.
Pair them up: even is heads, odd is tails. Toss without tossing!

Or cook your own: multiply, add, wrap around.
Seed five, times thirteen, wrap at sixteen:
**one, thirteen, nine, five** — and round again!
That is the **congruential generator**.

Run the experiment a thousand times — in the factory.
Count how often your event happens.
That share estimates its chance. That is **Monte Carlo**.

Then hold a model up to the real world:
peas — one quarter, one half, one quarter. Does nature agree?

Now you. Generate. Simulate. Estimate. Say it whole!
