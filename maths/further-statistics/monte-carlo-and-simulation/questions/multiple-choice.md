# Monte Carlo and Simulation — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Random Numbers and Digits · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — The Congruential Generator and PPS Ranges · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Monte Carlo and Testing a Model.

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Random digits are used in a simulation to ______ (Pakistan, Ch. 7).
a) Decorate the table  b) Stand in for the outcomes of a real experiment  c) Replace probability itself
**Answer:** b) Stand in for the outcomes of a real experiment — under a rule fixed before the run (pp.186–196).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Pakistan's Table 7.1 simulations, the digits are spent on ______ (p.189).
a) Weather records  b) Coin, two-coin and three-coin experiments  c) Multiplication practice
**Answer:** b) Coin, two-coin and three-coin experiments (Ch. 7).

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** With seed 5, a = 13, b = 0, m = 16, the generator's first value is ______.
a) 5  b) 13  c) 1
**Answer:** c) 1 — 5 × 13 = 65, and 65 mod 16 = 1 (Pakistan, Ch. 7).

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Pakistan's full worked run from seed 5 is ______.
a) 1, 13, 9, 5  b) 5, 10, 15, 0  c) 13, 9, 5, 2
**Answer:** a) 1, 13, 9, 5 — and then the seed returns, so the cycle repeats (Ch. 7).

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** PPS assignment gives an outcome a stretch of the random-number range that is ______.
a) The same for every outcome  b) As long as its probability  c) As long as its name
**Answer:** b) As long as its probability — ranges are assigned from cumulative probabilities, in proportion to size (Pakistan, Ch. 7).

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** The Monte Carlo method estimates a probability by ______ (China, §10.3.2, p.255).
a) A single careful trial  b) Simulating the experiment many times and taking the frequency  c) Asking an expert
**Answer:** b) Simulating the experiment many times and taking the frequency — its name in China's book is random simulation / Monte Carlo.

**7. (Sub lesson 3)** In the Mendel reading's model, the probabilities of DD, Dd, dd are ______ (China, pp.259–260).
a) 1/3, 1/3, 1/3  b) 0.25, 0.5, 0.25  c) 0.5, 0.25, 0.25
**Answer:** b) 0.25, 0.5, 0.25 — giving the visible ≈3:1 split, as the reading is filed.

**8. (Sub lesson 3)** A Monte Carlo estimate should always be reported with ______.
a) Its run length  b) A guarantee  c) The seed's birthday
**Answer:** a) Its run length — the estimate is a frequency, and a frequency's steadiness depends on how many runs stand behind it.
