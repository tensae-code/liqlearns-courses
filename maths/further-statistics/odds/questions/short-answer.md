# Odds — short answer

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Stating Odds · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Odds ↔ Probability · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Odds and Fairness.

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What two counts do odds compare?
**Answer:** Favourable outcomes against unfavourable outcomes (Kuwait, 4-6, pp.204–210).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** From the pens box, what is P(yellow), and why?
**Answer:** 0 — the box holds no yellow pens (Kuwait, 4-6).

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Odds are 3:10. What is the probability?
**Answer:** 3/13 — 3 out of 3 + 10 (Kuwait, 4-6).

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A probability is 2/7 (the pens box's blue). What are the odds?
**Answer:** 2:5 — 2 favourable against 7 − 2 = 5 unfavourable, matching the book's 4:10 simplified.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Kuwait's book calls prime (2, 3, 5) against even (2, 4, 6) on one die a fair game. What makes it fair?
**Answer:** The two camps carry equal weight — the odds between the sides balance at 1:1 in value (Unit 4 assessment).
