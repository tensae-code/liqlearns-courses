# Odds — matching

Match each item on the left to its partner on the right.

**1. (Level 2)** Match each odds statement to its probability: (a) 2:3 (b) 1:7 (c) 3:10 — (i) 3/13 (ii) 2/5 (iii) 1/8
**Answer:** a) (ii), b) (iii), c) (i) — Kuwait's own conversion pairs (4-6 and its assessment).

**2. (Level 2)** Match each probability to its odds: (a) 5/13 (b) 2/7 (c) 1/6 — (i) 1:5 (ii) 5:8 (iii) 2:5
**Answer:** a) (ii), b) (iii), c) (i).

**3. (Level 3)** Match each game to its verdict: (a) doubles vs different numbers on two dice (b) prime vs even faces on one die — (i) fair (ii) one-sided
**Answer:** a) (ii), b) (i) — 6 outcomes against 30 cannot balance; the prime/even camps do (Kuwait, 4-6).
