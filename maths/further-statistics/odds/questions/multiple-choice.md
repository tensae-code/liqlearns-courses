# Odds — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Level 1)** The odds of rolling a 2 on a fair die are ______ (Kuwait, 4-6).
a) 1:6  b) 1:5  c) 5:1
**Answer:** b) 1:5 — one favourable face against five unfavourable faces.

**2. (Level 1)** From the pens box (7 red, 3 green, 4 blue), the odds of blue are ______.
a) 4:14  b) 2:5  c) 5:2
**Answer:** b) 2:5 — 4 for against 10 against, simplified (Kuwait, 4-6).

**3. (Level 2)** Odds of 2:3 give the probability ______.
a) 2/3  b) 3/5  c) 2/5
**Answer:** c) 2/5 — 2 favourable out of 2 + 3 = 5 in all (Kuwait, 4-6 assessment).

**4. (Level 2)** Odds of 1:7 give the probability ______.
a) 1/7  b) 1/8  c) 7/8
**Answer:** b) 1/8 — 1 out of 1 + 7 = 8 (Kuwait, 4-6).

**5. (Level 2)** A probability of 5/13 gives odds of ______.
a) 5:13  b) 8:5  c) 5:8
**Answer:** c) 5:8 — 5 favourable against 13 − 5 = 8 unfavourable (Kuwait, 4-6 assessment).

**6. (Level 3)** Two dice: doubles win for one player, different numbers for the other. The doubles player's odds position is ______.
a) 30:6 in his favour  b) 6:30 against him  c) 1:1, a fair game
**Answer:** b) 6:30 against him — 6 double outcomes against 30 different ones, so the other side holds the better chance (Kuwait, 4-6).
