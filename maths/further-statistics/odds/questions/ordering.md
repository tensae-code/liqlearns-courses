# Odds — ordering

Put the items in the order asked. Check each step before you write the answer.

**1. (Level 2)** Order the steps for turning odds 2:3 into a probability: write 2 over the total; add the two sides; read off the favourable side.
Answer in order, first step first.
**Answer:** Read off the favourable side (2) → add the two sides (2 + 3 = 5) → write 2 over the total (2/5).

**2. (Level 3)** Order these chances from least likely to most likely, using their odds: blue from the pens box (2:5), a 2 on a die (1:5), a multiple of 3 on a die (1:2).
Answer in order, least likely first.
**Answer:** A 2 on a die (probability 1/6), blue from the pens box (2/7), a multiple of 3 on a die (1/3) — convert each odds pair to its probability, then order.
