# Odds — fill-in-the-blank

Fill in the missing number or word.

**1. (Level 1)** Odds = favourable : ___.
**Answer:** unfavourable

**2. (Level 1)** The odds of a multiple of 3 on a die are 1:___.
**Answer:** 2

**3. (Level 2)** Odds a : b give probability a/(a + ___).
**Answer:** b

**4. (Level 2)** Probability 5/13 gives odds 5:___.
**Answer:** 8
