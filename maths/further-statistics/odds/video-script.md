# Video script — Odds

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Roll a die. You want a **two**.
One face helps you. **Five** faces do not.
The odds? **One to five**! For — against.

Odds compare the two sides.
Two for, three against — odds **two to three**.
Add the sides: five. Two out of five — that is the **probability**!

Back again? Probability five thirteenths.
Five for — and thirteen take five is eight against.
Odds: **five to eight**.

Same chance, two ways of speaking.
Now you. Take a die. Say a chance both ways — whole sentences!
