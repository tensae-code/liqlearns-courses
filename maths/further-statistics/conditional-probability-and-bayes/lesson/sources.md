**Acceptance count: taught in 8 countries (Kuwait, Egypt, Cambodia, Greece, Kazakhstan, Nicaragua, Iraq, Sierra Leone).** Kuwait carries the fullest treatment twice over — Grade 10 lesson 10-5 builds independent and dependent events, conditional probability and Bayes with the quality-test example, and Grade 11 lesson 11-3 re-teaches the rules and returns to conditional probability and Bayes. Egypt carries conditional probability and independent events as two named lessons side by side in its Grade 12 Statistics book. Cambodia carries the topic at three levels: introduced in Grade 11 Basic, worked with the P(B|A) notation and Bayes-type problems in Grade 11 Advanced, and given in full — Bayes' theorem with the total-probability formula — in the PRIVATE Khem Putthi Probability booklet (PRIVATE, not MoEYS). Greece carries conditional probability with independent events in its Grade 12 EPAL statistics book. Kazakhstan carries the count-based proof of the multiplication form in Shynybekov's Algebra 9. Nicaragua carries independent events and then conditional probability with its formula box, worked on dice and a bus problem. Iraq carries the dependent/independent groundwork in Grade 9 and the probability rules in Grade 11 Scientific. Sierra Leone carries the dependent-draw idea through tree diagrams only, with no P(A|B) notation and no Bayes, as filed — its trees are this lesson's picture of a changing chance, not a source for the symbol or the theorem.
