# Conditional Probability and Bayes' Theorem — fill in the blank

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Reading "Given" · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Independence, Dependence and Trees · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Total Probability and Bayes.

Fill each blank with the missing word or number.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The bar in P(A|B) is read "______".
**Answer:** given — P(A|B) is the probability of A given B.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** P(A|B) = P(A∩B) / ______.
**Answer:** P(B) — you divide by the probability of the event you are given.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A draw without ______ is dependent, because the bag has changed.
**Answer:** replacement — Sierra Leone's L106 trees are built on draws without replacement.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** If A and B are independent, then P(A|B) = ______.
**Answer:** P(A) — being given B changes nothing when the events are independent.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** P(A∩B) = P(A) · ______, the multiplication rule in conditional form.
**Answer:** P(B|A) — the form Cambodia's Grade 11 Advanced book prints at p.129.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** With P(A∪B) = 0.9, the complement of the union is 1 − 0.9 = ______ (Kuwait Grade 10, p.197, taught corrected).
**Answer:** 0.1 — the complement uses the union's own probability.
