# Conditional Probability and Bayes' Theorem — ordering

Put the steps in the right order.

**1. (Level 1)** Working a conditional probability from counts: (a) divide by the count of B (b) name the given event B (c) count the outcomes in both A and B.
**Answer:** b, c, a — given first, then the overlap, then divide by the given.

**2. (Level 2)** Drawing a without-replacement tree: (a) write the changed second fractions (b) draw the first branches with their fractions (c) take one item out and recount the bag.
**Answer:** b, c, a — the second branches can only be written after the bag is recounted.

**3. (Level 2)** Testing independence: (a) compare with P(A∩B) (b) multiply P(A) by P(B) (c) conclude independent or not.
**Answer:** b, a, c — independence holds exactly when the product equals the joint probability.

**4. (Level 3)** A Bayes calculation: (a) build P(B) by total probability (b) write P(A)·P(B|A) (c) divide (b) by (a).
**Answer:** b, a, c — numerator first, then the evidence total, then the turn.

**5. (Level 3)** Kuwait's Grade 10 lesson 10-5 order (pp.192–205): (a) Bayes' theorem (b) independent and dependent events (c) conditional probability.
**Answer:** b, c, a — the book builds dependence first, then the conditional idea, then the turn.
