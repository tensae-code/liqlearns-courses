# Video script — Conditional Probability and Bayes' Theorem

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A bag. Five red balls. Five blue balls. Ten balls.

Draw one. Do NOT put it back. The bag has changed. Now nine balls.

What is the chance the second ball is red — GIVEN the first was red?

Given means: we know it happened. The world got smaller. Only four red balls are left, out of nine. Four out of nine.

That is conditional probability. The chance of one thing, given another thing.

Now turn it around. A test says a machine part is good. What is the chance it is really bad? That is a different question — and Bayes' theorem turns the first question into the second one.

Given. Smaller world. Count again. That is the whole lesson.
