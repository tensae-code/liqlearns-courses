# Sampling and Sampling Distributions — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Populations, Censuses and Kinds of Data · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — The Sampling Methods, Bad Samples and Errors · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Sampling Distributions and the Central Limit Theorem.

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A school examines every pupil's eyesight. This is a ______.
a) sample  b) census  c) bias
**Answer:** b) census — every unit of the population is examined (Pakistan, Ch. 11, pp.27–28).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which of these is continuous data?
a) goals in a match  b) tickets sold  c) weights of pupils
**Answer:** c) weights of pupils — measured data can fall anywhere on a scale (Kuwait workbook, Unit 6, pp.85–86, workbook evidence).

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Types of toothpaste sold in a shop are ______ data.
a) discrete  b) continuous  c) qualitative nominal
**Answer:** c) qualitative nominal — names with no order (Kuwait workbook, Unit 6, pp.85–86, workbook evidence).

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Strata of 100, 300 and 1,200 are sampled at fraction 0.05. The samples are ______.
a) 5, 15, 60  b) 20, 20, 20  c) 50, 150, 600
**Answer:** a) 5, 15, 60 — each stratum gives the same fraction of itself (Kuwait, Unit 2, p.48).

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** A systematic sample has interval 60 and first unit 31. The next two units are ______.
a) 61, 91  b) 91, 151  c) 31, 31
**Answer:** b) 91, 151 — add the interval each time: 31, 91, …, 871 (Kuwait, Unit 2, p.50).

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** A statistic T has E(T) = θ + 3. Its bias is ______.
a) 0  b) θ  c) 3
**Answer:** c) 3 — bias = E(T) − θ (Pakistan, Ch. 11, pp.32–33).

**7. (Sub lesson 3)** A population has σ = 12. The standard error of x̄ for n = 36 is ______.
a) 12  b) 2  c) 0.33
**Answer:** b) 2 — σ/√n = 12/6 = 2, the Central Limit Theorem's spread (Pakistan, Ch. 11, pp.36–51).

**8. (Sub lesson 3)** In Libya's population statistics, the sex ratio and the crude birth rate come from ______.
a) a small sample  b) the census and vital records  c) a random-number table
**Answer:** b) the census and vital records — population statistics are whole-country counts (Libya, Humanities, Ch. 4, pp.89–107).
