**Answer:** (i)–(b), (ii)–(c), (iii)–(a) — Kuwait, Grade 11 Statistics T1, Unit 2, pp.38–41.

**Answer:** (i)–(c), (ii)–(a), (iii)–(b) — Kuwait, Unit 2, pp.45–50.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Match the country to its way: (i) Libya Humanities; (ii) China; (iii) Mauritania. Ways: (a) randomised response for sensitive questions; (b) sampling as a named Échantillonnage chapter; (c) population statistics — density, growth rate, sex ratio.
