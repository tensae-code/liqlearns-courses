# Sampling and Sampling Distributions — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the one right word or number.

**1. (Level 1)** Examining every unit of a population is called a ______.
**Answer:** census — the alternative is a sample survey (Pakistan, Ch. 11, pp.27–28).

**2. (Level 1)** Counted data, like the number of tickets sold, are called ______ data.
**Answer:** discrete — measured data are continuous (Kuwait workbook, Unit 6, pp.85–86, workbook evidence).

**3. (Level 2)** In stratified sampling, every stratum is sampled in ______ to its size.
**Answer:** proportion — the same sampling fraction is taken from each stratum (Kuwait, Unit 2, pp.47–48).

**4. (Level 2)** The bias of a statistic T for a parameter θ is E(T) − ______.
**Answer:** θ — bias is the average error over all possible samples (Pakistan, Ch. 11, pp.32–33).

**5. (Level 3)** The standard deviation of a sampling distribution is called the standard ______.
**Answer:** error — e.g. σ/√n for the sample mean (Pakistan, Ch. 11, pp.35–36).

**6. (Level 3)** By the Central Limit Theorem, x̄ is approximately normal for large n with mean μ and standard error ______.
**Answer:** σ/√n — whatever the population's own shape (Pakistan, Ch. 11, Theorem 11.6, pp.36–51).
