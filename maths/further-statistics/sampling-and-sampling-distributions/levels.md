# Sampling and Sampling Distributions — questions by level

Practice by level, tied to the skill number. The questions live in
the type files; this file is the by-level view. The levels are the
depths inside this one thing — sampling — populations and data kinds first, then the methods and their errors, then sampling distributions and the Central Limit Theorem.

## Level 1 — populations, censuses and kinds of data
Level 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: say what a population, a sample, a frame and a census are, choose census against sample for a given job, and sort data into discrete, continuous, nominal and ordinal.

## Level 2 — the sampling methods, bad samples and errors
Level 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: draw simple random, stratified and systematic samples correctly, criticise an unrepresentative survey, separate sampling from non-sampling errors, and compute a bias as E(T) − θ.

## Level 3 — sampling distributions and the Central Limit Theorem
Level 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: enumerate a small sampling distribution whole, read its mean and standard error off it, state the Central Limit Theorem, and read population statistics and the randomised-response idea.
