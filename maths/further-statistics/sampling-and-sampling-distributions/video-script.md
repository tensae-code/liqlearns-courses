# Video script — Sampling and Sampling Distributions

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

You cannot ask EVERYONE. So you ask SOME — and you choose them fairly.

The everyone is the **population**. The some is the **sample**.
Ask everyone and it is a **census** — slow, and dear.

Choose by lottery! Every name the same chance.
Or split the people into groups, and take a fair share from each group.
Or take every tenth name on the list.

But beware: ask only the people at the bus stop,
and your answer speaks for bus-stop people — not for everyone.
A big bad sample is still a bad sample!

Now take many samples. Work out the mean of each one.
The means make their own pattern — and for big samples,
that pattern is a **bell**, centred on the true mean.
That is the Central Limit Theorem.

Now you. Choose fairly. Say who your sample can speak for.
That is sampling.
