**Acceptance count: taught in 15 countries (Pakistan, Kuwait, Indonesia, Libya, Mauritania, North Macedonia, Iraq, Kazakhstan, Nicaragua, Ghana, China, Mongolia, Venezuela, Afghanistan, Jordan).** Pakistan carries the lesson's spine: frames and designs, sampling against non-sampling errors, bias = E(T) − θ, sampling distributions built by complete enumeration, and the Central Limit Theorem. Kuwait carries the working methods — census against sampling, simple random, stratified and systematic sampling with the random-number table — in its Grade 11 Statistics book, with its Grade 11 Term 1 workbook drilling the kinds of data and the same methods as workbook evidence only. Indonesia carries estimating a population from samples in Grade 9. Libya carries the sampling distribution of the mean in its Science book and a whole population-statistics chapter — census, density, growth rate, sex ratio, vital statistics — in its Humanities book. Mauritania carries sampling (Échantillonnage) as a named chapter across its 6AS streams. North Macedonia carries representative samples and the bus-stop criticism in Grade 8 (2010). Iraq carries survey design and misleading statistics in Grade 9. Kazakhstan carries the kinds-of-data chart in its Grade 8 NIS statistics chapter. Nicaragua carries population, sample and variable fixed on one small case in Grade 9. Ghana carries discrete against continuous data as teacher evidence in the JICA B6 manual. China carries randomised response as a reading in its A-edition Compulsory Volume 2.


Mongolia enters through its ministry textbook Математик VIII (Grade 8): population against sample, sample size, and a biased survey criticised for not representing its population.

Venezuela enters through the Colección Bicentenario, the MPPE's one-book-per-grade course: *Naturaleza Matemática* (Grade 10). From its books: from Grade 10: number of samples with replacement N^n (5^2 = 25), sample table.

Afghanistan enters through its Ministry of Education course — the Grade 1 course in two language editions (Pashto and Dari, one work with identical page maps) and Dari student books for Grades 4, 6, 7, 8, 10, 11 and 12, all printing year 1398 HS except the Grade 8 book, which prints 1399 HS on its cover and title page and 1398 HS on its specifications page. From its books: from Grade 12: sampling and types of sample; sampling distribution of the mean; central limit theorem; sampling distribution of a proportion.
