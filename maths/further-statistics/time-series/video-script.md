# Video script — Time Series

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Write a number down every week. Every month. Every year.
Join the dots. That wavy line is a time series.

The line is really four lines hiding in one.
One line climbs slowly, year after year. That is the **trend**.
One wave comes back every year, like rain in its season. That is the **seasonal** part.
One slow swing takes years — good times, bad times, good times again. That is the **cyclical** part.
And the little jumps no one can explain — that is the **irregular** part.

How do we find the trend hiding under the waves?
Draw it by eye — the freehand way.
Or split the series in two, average each half, and join the two points.
Or let a window slide along, averaging as it goes — the moving average.
Or fit the best straight line by least squares, the fairest line of all.

And the seasons? Average each quarter over many years,
compare each quarter with the average quarter,
and you get a seasonal index for every quarter.

One line. Four parts. That is a time series.
