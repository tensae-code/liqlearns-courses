# Time Series — questions by level

Practice by level, tied to the skill number. The questions live in
the type files; this file is the by-level view. The levels are the
depths inside this one thing — the series and its components first,
then the trend by the picture methods, then least squares and the seasonal indices.

## Level 1 — the series and its four components
Level 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: say what a time series is, draw its historigram, name the four components T, S, C, I, walk the business cycle in order, and state the additive and multiplicative models.

## Level 2 — the trend by freehand, semi-averages and moving averages
Level 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: draw a freehand trend and state its limits, work semi-averages from two half-means plotted at their midpoints, and compute moving averages, centred for an even period.

## Level 3 — least-squares trend and seasonal indices
Level 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: fit a coded least-squares trend with a = ȳ and b = Σxy/Σx², use it to forecast, say how a parabolic trend is compared by Σe², and find seasonal indices by simple averages that average to 100.
