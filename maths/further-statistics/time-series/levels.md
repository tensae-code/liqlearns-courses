This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB
LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure — README, "Units, Lessons and Sub
Lessons"). Practice is tied to the skill number, as before. The

## Sub lesson 1 — The Series and Its Four Components
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: say what a time series is, draw its historigram, name the four components T, S, C, I, walk the business cycle in order, and state the additive and multiplicative models.

## Sub lesson 2 — The Trend by Freehand, Semi-Averages and Moving Averages
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: draw a freehand trend and state its limits, work semi-averages from two half-means plotted at their midpoints, and compute moving averages, centred for an even period.

## Sub lesson 3 — Least-Squares Trend and Seasonal Indices
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: fit a coded least-squares trend with a = ȳ and b = Σxy/Σx², use it to forecast, say how a parabolic trend is compared by Σe², and find seasonal indices by simple averages that average to 100.
