Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A time series is:
a) values of one variable recorded at successive times, in time order  b) any list of numbers sorted smallest to largest  c) a table of frequencies
**Answer:** a) values in time order — Pakistan Statistics Class 12, Ch. 16, p.241.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In the four components T, S, C, I, the letter S stands for:
a) sum  b) seasonal  c) standard
**Answer:** b) seasonal — the wave that repeats inside every year (Pakistan, pp.242–243).

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The stages of a business cycle, in order, are:
a) peak, trough, recession, expansion  b) trough, expansion, peak, recession  c) expansion, recession, trough, peak
**Answer:** b) trough, expansion, peak, recession — Pakistan, pp.242–243.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The 3-term moving averages of 3, 5, 7, 9, 11 are:
a) 5, 7, 9  b) 4, 6, 8, 10  c) 15, 21, 27
**Answer:** a) 5, 7, 9 — each is the mean of one window of three (Pakistan, pp.250–253).

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** In semi-averages, each half's mean is plotted:
a) at the first time of its half  b) at the last time of its half  c) at the middle time of its half
**Answer:** c) at the middle time of its half — the trend line joins those two plotted points (Pakistan, pp.247–250).

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** With coded time (Σx = 0), values 10, 12, 15, 17, 21 give Σxy = 27 and Σx² = 10. The least-squares trend is:
a) ŷ = 15 + 2.7x  b) ŷ = 2.7 + 15x  c) ŷ = 15 − 2.7x
**Answer:** a) ŷ = 15 + 2.7x — a = ȳ = 15, b = Σxy/Σx² = 2.7 (Pakistan, pp.253–261).

**7. (Sub lesson 3)** A quarter's seasonal index of 120.8 means that quarter runs about:
a) 120.8% above a typical quarter  b) 20.8% above a typical quarter  c) 20.8% below a typical quarter
**Answer:** b) 20.8% above — an index of 100 is a typical quarter (Libya, Ch. 3, pp.59–88).

**8. (Sub lesson 3)** A parabolic trend is preferred over a straight line when:
a) it has the smaller Σe²  b) it has the larger Σe²  c) it passes through the first point
**Answer:** a) the smaller Σe² — the sum of squared errors is the comparison (Pakistan, pp.253–261).
