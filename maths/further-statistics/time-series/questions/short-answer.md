# Time Series — short answer

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — The Series and Its Four Components · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — The Trend by Freehand, Semi-Averages and Moving Averages · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Least-Squares Trend and Seasonal Indices.

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the graph of a time series called in Pakistan's Statistics Class 12 book?
**Answer:** A historigram — time on one axis, the value plotted against it (Ch. 16, p.241).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A flood destroys one month's sales, which crash and then recover. Which component is that?
**Answer:** The irregular component I — a leftover jump no pattern explains (Pakistan, pp.242–243).

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Why does an even-period moving average need centring?
**Answer:** Its averages fall between time points, so neighbouring averages are averaged again to line them up with time points (Pakistan, pp.250–253).

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** State one weakness of the freehand trend.
**Answer:** Two people draw two different lines, and it gives no equation to forecast with (Pakistan, pp.245–246).

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Find the 3-term moving averages of 4, 6, 5, 8, 10.
**Answer:** 5, 6.33, 7.67 — (4+6+5)/3, (6+5+8)/3, (5+8+10)/3.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** A coded least-squares trend is ŷ = 25 + 2.7x. Forecast the value at x = 3.
**Answer:** 33.1 — 25 + 2.7 × 3 (Pakistan, pp.253–261).

**7. (Sub lesson 3)** Quarterly seasonal indices by simple averages must average to what number?
**Answer:** 100 — the four quarterly indices sum to 400 (Libya, Ch. 3, pp.59–88).

**8. (Sub lesson 3)** Iran's Grade 11 book, filed for study: City A rainfall 15, 20, 30, 35, 30, 30 mm — what is its mean?
**Answer:** About 26.7 mm — 160/6 (Iran, Mathematics and Statistics (2), pp.63–70, filed "for study").
