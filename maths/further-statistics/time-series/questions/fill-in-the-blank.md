Fill each blank with the missing word or number.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In the multiplicative model, Y = T × S × C × ______.
**Answer:** I — the irregular component completes the four (Pakistan, pp.243–244).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The bottom of a business cycle is called the ______.
**Answer:** trough — then expansion, peak, recession (Pakistan, pp.242–243).

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A moving average has no value at the first or ______ point of the series.
**Answer:** last — the window cannot sit there (Pakistan, pp.250–253).

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** With coded time, Σx = ______, which shrinks the least-squares formulas.
**Answer:** 0 — the code is centred on a middle origin (Pakistan, pp.253–261).

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** With coded time, the least-squares intercept is a = ______.
**Answer:** ȳ — the mean of the values (Pakistan, pp.253–261).

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** Estimating a value inside a series from its neighbours is called ______.
**Answer:** interpolation — extending beyond the end is extrapolation (Iran, pp.63–70, filed "for study").
