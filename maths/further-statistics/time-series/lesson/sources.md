**Acceptance count: taught in 9 countries (Pakistan, Libya, Greece, Jordan, Iran, Venezuela, Colombia, Australia, Samoa).** Pakistan carries the deepest treatment: one full chapter that defines the series, names the four components and the business cycle, states both models, and then measures the trend four ways — freehand, semi-averages, moving averages and least squares, linear and parabolic — while computing no seasonal index anywhere, as filed. Libya carries the components and the same trend methods in its Humanities Grade 12 book, and adds the part Pakistan omits: seasonal indices by simple averages, with the cyclical changes beside them. Greece carries the components and the linear trend in a vocational business book, taught through business data for economic activity. Jordan carries time series as a named lesson in its Grade 12 Business book, with variance in time series as the follow-on lesson. Iran carries a reading-level treatment in its Grade 11 Humanities book — comparing two cities' rainfall and using interpolation and extrapolation — filed "for study" and worded as filed.
