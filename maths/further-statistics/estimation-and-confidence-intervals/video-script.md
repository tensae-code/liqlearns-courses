# Video script — Estimation and Confidence Intervals

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

One sample. One number. That is a **point estimate** — a single best guess.

But one guess can miss! So give a RANGE:
the guess, plus and minus a **margin of error**.
That range is a **confidence interval**.

Ninety-five per cent confident means THIS:
if you built the interval again and again, the same way,
about ninety-five in a hundred of your intervals would catch the truth.
Not magic. A method that mostly works — and says how mostly.

Sigma known? Use the **z** road.
Sigma unknown, small sample? Use the **t** road.
Wider sample — narrower road. That is the whole trade.

And the tanks? The biggest serial number you have seen,
stretched a little — (n + 1) over n — estimates ALL the tanks.
Even generals estimate.

Now you. One guess, then a range around it. Say how confident.
That is estimation.
