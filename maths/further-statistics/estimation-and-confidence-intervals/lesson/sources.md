**Acceptance count: taught in 8 countries (Pakistan, Jordan, Kuwait, Egypt, Libya, Laos, Iran, China).** Pakistan carries the full run: point against interval, unbiasedness with the n − 1 proof, and intervals for a mean, a proportion and both differences. Jordan carries the same inference in its Business book, sample-mean distributions before confidence intervals. Kuwait carries point estimation, the margin of error and the t interval at 95% only, with its p.171 label taught corrected — |x̄ − μ| is the point estimation error; the standard error is σ/√n. Egypt carries estimation and confidence intervals at the close of its normal-distribution unit. Libya carries point and interval estimation with the interval for a population mean. Laos carries estimation as its own Grade 12 lesson. Iran carries point and interval estimation taught conceptually, without z-machinery — and, recorded plainly, Iran does not teach hypothesis testing and is no source for Lesson 121. China carries the German tank problem as a reading.
