# Estimation and Confidence Intervals — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the one right word or number.

**1. (Level 1)** An interval estimate is a ______ built around a point estimate, with a stated confidence.
**Answer:** range — point against interval is the chapter's opening split (Pakistan, Ch. 12, p.73).

**2. (Level 1)** Among unbiased estimators, the best is the one with the smallest ______.
**Answer:** variance — the minimum-variance unbiased estimator (Pakistan, Ch. 12, pp.76–79).

**3. (Level 2)** The margin of error for a mean with σ known is E = z_{α/2} · ______.
**Answer:** σ/√n — the standard error carries the margin (Kuwait, 4-1, pp.168–176).

**4. (Level 2)** In Kuwait's book, the only confidence level used anywhere is ______%.
**Answer:** 95 — the book's recorded discipline (Kuwait, 4-1, pp.168–176).

**5. (Level 3)** Before intervals for differences, Pakistan separates independent samples from ______ samples.
**Answer:** paired — the two designs get different machinery (Pakistan, Ch. 12, p.96).

**6. (Level 3)** The German tank estimate stretches the largest serial by the factor (n + 1)/______.
**Answer:** n — N ≈ x_max·(n + 1)/n (China, reading, pp.208–209).
