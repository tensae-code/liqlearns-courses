# Estimation and Confidence Intervals — matching

Match each item on the left with the one right item on the right.

**1. (Level 1)** Match the word: (i) estimator; (ii) estimate; (iii) bias. Meanings: (a) E(T) − θ; (b) the rule; (c) the number the rule gives on a sample.
**Answer:** (i)–(b), (ii)–(c), (iii)–(a) — Pakistan, Ch. 12, pp.73–76.

**2. (Level 2)** Match the case to its road: (i) σ known; (ii) σ unknown, n = 25; (iii) σ unknown, n = 400. Roads: (a) z; (b) t, ν = 24; (c) z with s for σ (large sample).
**Answer:** (i)–(a), (ii)–(b), (iii)–(c) — Kuwait, 4-1, pp.168–176; Pakistan, Ch. 12, pp.82–90.

**3. (Level 2)** Match the interval to its givens: (i) (72.4262, 80.1738); (ii) (10.872, 19.128); (iii) (58.6933, 61.3067). Givens: (a) n = 36, x̄ = 60, s = 4; (b) n = 40, σ = 12.5, x̄ = 76.3; (c) n = 25, S = 10, x̄ = 15, t = 2.064.
**Answer:** (i)–(b), (ii)–(c), (iii)–(a) — Kuwait's three worked intervals (4-1, pp.168–176).

**4. (Level 3)** Match the country to its way: (i) Iran; (ii) China; (iii) Jordan. Ways: (a) estimation inside an inference unit, sample means first; (b) the German tank reading; (c) estimation without z-machinery.
**Answer:** (i)–(c), (ii)–(b), (iii)–(a) — Iran, Ch. 4, pp.112–121; China, reading, pp.208–209; Jordan, Unit 6, pp.102–128.
