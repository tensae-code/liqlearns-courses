# Estimation and Confidence Intervals — ordering

Put the steps or values in the right order.

**1. (Level 1)** Order the build of a confidence interval: (a) compute the point estimate; (b) choose the confidence level; (c) compute the standard error; (d) form estimate ± margin of error.
**Answer:** (b), (a), (c), (d) — level chosen, estimate and error computed, interval formed (Pakistan, Ch. 12, pp.81–90).

**2. (Level 2)** Order Kuwait's pulse interval build: (a) E ≈ 3.8738; (b) x̄ = 76.3, σ = 12.5, n = 40; (c) (72.4262, 80.1738); (d) σ/√n ≈ 1.9764.
**Answer:** (b), (d), (a), (c) — givens, standard error, margin, interval (Kuwait, 4-1, pp.168–176).

**3. (Level 2)** Order by width, narrowest first, same x̄ and level: (a) n = 25, σ known; (b) n = 100, σ known; (c) n = 25, σ unknown (t).
**Answer:** (b), (a), (c) — bigger n narrows; unknown σ widens.

**4. (Level 3)** Order Jordan's inference unit as taught: (a) hypothesis testing; (b) distribution of sample means; (c) confidence intervals.
**Answer:** (b), (c), (a) — Unit 6 runs L1 p.102, L3 p.128, L4 p.141 (Jordan, Business T2).
