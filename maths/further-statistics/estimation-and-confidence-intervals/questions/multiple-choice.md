# Estimation and Confidence Intervals — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Level 1)** A single number used to estimate a parameter is a ______.
a) interval estimate  b) point estimate  c) confidence level
**Answer:** b) point estimate — one number, like x̄ for μ (Pakistan, Ch. 12, p.73).

**2. (Level 1)** An estimator T is unbiased for θ when ______.
a) E(T) = θ  b) T = θ always  c) Var(T) = 0
**Answer:** a) E(T) = θ — its sampling distribution centres on the parameter (Pakistan, Ch. 12, pp.74–76).

**3. (Level 1)** The standard error of the sample mean x̄ is ______.
a) |x̄ − μ|  b) σ  c) σ/√n
**Answer:** c) σ/√n — a property of the method; |x̄ − μ| is the point estimation error (Kuwait, 4-1, p.171, taught corrected).

**4. (Level 2)** Kuwait's pulse-rate study (n = 40, σ = 12.5, x̄ = 76.3) gives the 95% interval ______.
a) (72.4262, 80.1738)  b) (76.3, 88.8)  c) (63.8, 76.3)
**Answer:** a) (72.4262, 80.1738) — E ≈ 3.8738 around x̄ (Kuwait, 4-1, pp.168–176).

**5. (Level 2)** σ is unknown and n = 25. The interval for μ uses ______.
a) z with ν = 24  b) t with ν = n − 1 = 24  c) z with σ guessed as 0
**Answer:** b) t with ν = n − 1 = 24 — the t road for σ unknown and small n (Kuwait, 4-1; Pakistan, Ch. 12, pp.82–90).

**6. (Level 2)** A 95% confidence level means ______.
a) μ has a 95% chance of being in this one interval  b) 95% of the data lie in the interval  c) 95% of intervals built this way catch the parameter
**Answer:** c) — the level is a property of the method, not of one finished interval (Pakistan, Ch. 12, pp.81–82).

**7. (Level 3)** Five captured parts have largest serial 48. The German tank estimate of the run N is about ______.
a) 48  b) 58  c) 240
**Answer:** b) 58 — N ≈ 48 × (5 + 1)/5 = 57.6 (China, reading, pp.208–209).

**8. (Level 3)** Iran's Grade 11 book teaches interval estimation ______.
a) with full z-tables  b) conceptually, without z-machinery  c) not at all
**Answer:** b) conceptually, without z-machinery — the sample-mean picture carries it (Iran, Ch. 4, pp.112–121).
