# Hypothesis Testing — questions by level

Practice by level, tied to the skill number. The questions live in
the type files; this file is the by-level view. The levels are the
depths inside this one thing — hypothesis testing — the vocabulary and the errors first, then tests of one mean, then proportions, differences and paired tests.

## Level 1 — hypotheses, tails and the two errors
Level 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: state H₀ and H₁ for a claim, tell one-tailed from two-tailed and simple from composite, and name the Type I and Type II errors with α and β.

## Level 2 — the procedure and tests of a mean
Level 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: run the nine-step procedure, choose Z or t correctly, and decide from the critical region — including Kuwait's two-sided-only cases with their recorded limits.

## Level 3 — proportions, differences and paired tests
Level 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: test a proportion, a difference of two means, a paired difference by the paired t, and a difference of two proportions pooled under H₀ — and state the recorded negatives (no testing in the Egypt, Cambodia, Greece or Iran filings; no p-values or χ² in Kuwait).
