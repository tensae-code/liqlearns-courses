Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: state H₀ and H₁ for a claim, tell one-tailed from two-tailed and simple from composite, and name the Type I and Type II errors with α and β.

## Sub lesson 2 — The Procedure and Tests of a Mean
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: run the nine-step procedure, choose Z or t correctly, and decide from the critical region — including Kuwait's two-sided-only cases with their recorded limits.

## Sub lesson 3 — Proportions, Differences and Paired Tests
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: test a proportion, a difference of two means, a paired difference by the paired t, and a difference of two proportions pooled under H₀ — and state the recorded negatives (no testing in the Egypt, Cambodia, Greece or Iran filings; no p-values or χ² in Kuwait).
