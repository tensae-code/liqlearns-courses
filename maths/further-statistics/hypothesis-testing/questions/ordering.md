# Hypothesis Testing — ordering

Put the steps or values in the right order.

**1. (Level 1)** Order Pakistan's nine-step procedure, first four steps: (a) choose α; (b) set H₁; (c) state the claim; (d) set H₀.
**Answer:** (c), (d), (b), (a) — claim, H₀, H₁ (which fixes the tails), then α (Pakistan, Ch. 13, pp.115–117).

**2. (Level 2)** Order the back half of the procedure: (a) compute the statistic; (b) choose the test statistic; (c) decide; (d) fix the critical region; (e) state the statistic's distribution under H₀.
**Answer:** (b), (e), (d), (a), (c) — the region is fixed before the computation speaks (Pakistan, Ch. 13, pp.115–117).

**3. (Level 2)** Order by distance from H₀ in standard errors, nearest first: (a) Z = −3.33; (b) Z ≈ 0.999; (c) Z = −1.66; (d) Z = −2.
**Answer:** (b), (c), (d), (a) — Kuwait's worked statistics (4-2, pp.177–181); only (d) and (a) fall outside (−1.96, 1.96).

**4. (Level 3)** Order Jordan's inference unit as taught: (a) hypothesis testing; (b) confidence intervals; (c) distribution of sample means.
**Answer:** (c), (b), (a) — Unit 6 runs L1 p.102, L3 p.128, L4 p.141 (Jordan, Business T2).
