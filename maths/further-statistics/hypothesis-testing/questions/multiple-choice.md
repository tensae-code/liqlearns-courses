# Hypothesis Testing — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Level 1)** The null hypothesis H₀ is ______.
a) the claim the researcher hopes to prove  b) the standing claim of no effect or no difference  c) always false
**Answer:** b) the standing claim — the test only rejects it or fails to reject it (Pakistan, Ch. 13, pp.111–114).

**2. (Level 1)** Rejecting a TRUE H₀ is a ______ error.
a) Type I  b) Type II  c) sampling
**Answer:** a) Type I — the false alarm, with probability α (Pakistan, Ch. 13, pp.115–117).

**3. (Level 1)** H₁: μ > μ₀ makes the test ______.
a) two-tailed  b) one-tailed, right  c) one-tailed, left
**Answer:** b) one-tailed, right — all of α sits in the right tail (Pakistan, Ch. 13, pp.111–114).

**4. (Level 2)** Kuwait's salary case (μ₀ = 4000, n = 25, x̄ = 3950, σ = 125) gives Z = −2. At 95% the verdict is ______.
a) keep H₀  b) reject H₀  c) no decision is possible
**Answer:** b) reject H₀ — −2 lies outside the acceptance region (−1.96, 1.96) (Kuwait, 4-2, pp.177–181).

**5. (Level 2)** σ is unknown and n = 10. The test statistic for a mean is ______.
a) Z with s  b) t with ν = 9  c) χ²
**Answer:** b) t with ν = 9 — σ unknown, n < 30 (Kuwait's table, 4-2, pp.177–181; Pakistan, Ch. 13, pp.120–130).

**6. (Level 2)** In Pakistan's procedure, the tails and α are fixed ______.
a) after seeing the data  b) before computing the statistic  c) never
**Answer:** b) before computing the statistic — steps 3–4 precede the computation at step 8 (Pakistan, Ch. 13, pp.115–117).

**7. (Level 3)** In a test of two proportions, the standard error is built with ______.
a) each sample's own p only  b) the proportion pooled under H₀  c) no proportion at all
**Answer:** b) the proportion pooled under H₀ — under the null the two populations share one π (Pakistan, Ch. 13, pp.169–174).

**8. (Level 3)** The same pupils are measured before and after training. The right test is ______.
a) the two-sample Z on the two means  b) the paired t on the differences  c) a proportion test
**Answer:** b) the paired t on the differences — the pairs are the sample (Pakistan, Ch. 13, pp.158–165).
