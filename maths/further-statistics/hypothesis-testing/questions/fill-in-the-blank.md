# Hypothesis Testing — fill in the blank

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Hypotheses, Tails and the Two Errors · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — The Procedure and Tests of a Mean · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Proportions, Differences and Paired Tests.

Fill each blank with the one right word or number.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The probability of a Type I error is written ______.
**Answer:** α — the level of significance, chosen in advance (Pakistan, Ch. 13, pp.115–117).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The values of the statistic that are too extreme for H₀ form the ______ region.
**Answer:** rejection (critical) — the rest is the acceptance region (Pakistan, Ch. 13, pp.111–114).

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Pakistan runs every test through ______ fixed steps.
**Answer:** nine — hypothesis to decision, always in the same order (Pakistan, Ch. 13, pp.115–117).

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In Kuwait's book, H₁ always takes the form μ ______ μ₀.
**Answer:** ≠ — two-sided tests ONLY in that book (Kuwait, 4-2, pp.177–181).

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** The paired t test has ν = ______ degrees of freedom for n pairs.
**Answer:** n − 1 — one sample of differences (Pakistan, Ch. 13, pp.158–165).

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** Kuwait's book contains no ______-values and no χ² tests.
**Answer:** p — recorded negatives: decisions there are by critical values only (Kuwait, 4-2, pp.177–181).
