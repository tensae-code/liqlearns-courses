# Hypothesis Testing — matching

Match each item on the left with the one right item on the right.

**1. (Level 1)** Match the error: (i) reject a true H₀; (ii) keep a false H₀; (iii) reject a false H₀. Names: (a) Type II error; (b) a correct decision (power side); (c) Type I error.
**Answer:** (i)–(c), (ii)–(a), (iii)–(b) — Pakistan, Ch. 13, pp.115–117.

**2. (Level 2)** Match Kuwait's case to its verdict: (i) salary, Z = −2; (ii) mean 37, Z ≈ 0.999; (iii) water, new study, Z = −3.33. Verdicts: (a) keep H₀; (b) reject H₀; (c) reject H₀.
**Answer:** (i)–(b), (ii)–(a), (iii)–(c) — against the acceptance region (−1.96, 1.96) (Kuwait, 4-2, pp.177–181).

**3. (Level 2)** Match the case to its statistic: (i) σ known, any n; (ii) σ unknown, n ≥ 30; (iii) σ unknown, n < 30. Statistics: (a) t with S; (b) Z with σ; (c) Z with S.
**Answer:** (i)–(b), (ii)–(c), (iii)–(a) — Kuwait's choice table (4-2, pp.177–181).

**4. (Level 3)** Match the country to its place in this lesson: (i) Libya; (ii) Laos; (iii) Greece. Places: (a) hypotheses, errors and t-tests for a mean, Ch. 6; (b) recorded negative — no testing taught; (c) testing as its own lesson, L15.
**Answer:** (i)–(a), (ii)–(c), (iii)–(b) — Libya, Ch. 6 (p.161); Laos, Ch. 5, L15, p.156; Greece, negative as filed.
