**Acceptance count: taught in 5 countries (Pakistan, Jordan, Libya, Kuwait, Laos).** Pakistan carries the whole machinery: the vocabulary, both errors, the nine steps, and the Z, t, proportion, difference and paired tests. Jordan carries testing as the closing lesson of its Business inference unit. Libya carries hypotheses, the errors of decision and t-tests for a mean in its Science book's final chapter. Kuwait carries two-sided tests only, decided by critical values and the acceptance region at 95% — with its recorded negatives standing as part of the count: no p-values and no χ² tests anywhere in its book. Laos carries statistical hypothesis testing as its own Grade 12 lesson. The four recorded negatives — Egypt, Cambodia, Greece and Iran — teach no hypothesis testing and are cited nowhere in this lesson; Iran's estimation in Lesson 120 is not evidence for tests and is never carried over as such.
