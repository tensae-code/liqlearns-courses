# Video script — Hypothesis Testing

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Someone makes a CLAIM. You play judge — a careful judge.

The claim of "nothing special" is **H-nought**.
The other claim is **H-one**.
You start by believing H-nought — innocent, until the evidence shouts.

Gather the evidence. Shrink it to ONE number — the **test statistic**.
If that number lands in the wild tail — too wild for H-nought —
you **reject** H-nought. If not, you keep it. You never PROVE it.

Two ways to be wrong!
Reject a TRUE H-nought — a false alarm. That is **Type One**,
and YOU choose its rate: alpha.
Keep a FALSE H-nought — a missed catch. That is **Type Two**.

Step by step, the same nine steps every time.
That is a test.

Now you. State the claims. Run the steps. Give the verdict — carefully.
That is hypothesis testing.
