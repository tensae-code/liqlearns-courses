# Quartiles, Percentiles and Box Plots — fill in the blank

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Quartiles, the Five-Number Summary and the Box Plot · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Deciles, Percentiles, Grouped Interpolation and the Semi-Interquartile Range · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Cumulative Frequency Curves and Comparing Plots.

Fill each blank with the missing word or number.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Q1 is the median of the ______ half of the ordered data.
**Answer:** lower — and Q3 is the median of the upper half (Kuwait Grade 11 Statistics, p.12).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The line inside the box of a box-and-whisker plot marks the ______.
**Answer:** median — Kuwait Grade 9, pp.198–203.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Deciles split ordered data into ______ equal parts.
**Answer:** ten — D1 to D9 are the nine cuts (Cambodia Grade 11 Basic, pp.220–227).

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The semi-interquartile range is (Q3 − Q1) / ______.
**Answer:** 2 — Kuwait Grade 11 Statistics, lesson 4-3, p.23.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** A cumulative frequency curve is also called an ______.
**Answer:** ogive — Iraq Grade 10 Literary, pp.80–82.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** To read Q3 off an ogive, run across from cumulative frequency ______.
**Answer:** 3n/4 — three quarters of the total (South Sudan S2, pp.76–82).
