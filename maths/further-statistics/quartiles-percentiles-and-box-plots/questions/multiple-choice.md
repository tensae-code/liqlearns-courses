# Quartiles, Percentiles and Box Plots — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Level 1)** For the data 75, 76, 77, 80, 82, 84, 85 (Kuwait Grade 9), the median is:
a) 77  b) 80  c) 82
**Answer:** b) 80 — the middle of seven ordered values (Kuwait Grade 9, pp.198–203).

**2. (Level 1)** The five-number summary is:
a) mean, median, mode, range, count  b) smallest, Q1, median, Q3, largest  c) Q1, Q2, Q3, D5, P99
**Answer:** b) smallest, Q1, median, Q3, largest — the box plot's five landmarks (Kuwait Grade 10, pp.170–175).

**3. (Level 1)** For data with Q1 = 5 and Q3 = 7, the interquartile range is:
a) 12  b) 6  c) 2
**Answer:** c) 2 — IQR = Q3 − Q1 (Kuwait Grade 11 Statistics, p.23).

**4. (Level 2)** For the same data, the semi-interquartile range is:
a) 1  b) 2  c) 4
**Answer:** a) 1 — (Q3 − Q1)/2 = (7 − 5)/2 (Kuwait Grade 11 Statistics, lesson 4-3, p.23).

**5. (Level 2)** Kuwait's grouped ages example (frequencies 3, 3, 5, 2, 5, 2) gives the median, by interpolation, as:
a) 25  b) 28  c) 30
**Answer:** b) 28 — 20 + ((10 − 6)/5) × 10, median class 20–30 (Kuwait Grade 11 Statistics, pp.15–18).

**6. (Level 2)** Ethiopia's Grade 11 guide, taught corrected as teacher evidence: D3 for the internet-time data is:
a) 21.5  b) 24.25  c) 20.15
**Answer:** c) 20.15 — the printed 21.5 drops the division by the class frequency (guide, p.228, corrected).

**7. (Level 3)** On an ogive, the median is read by running across from cumulative frequency:
a) n  b) n/2  c) 3n/4
**Answer:** b) n/2 — half the total (South Sudan S2, pp.76–82; Iraq Grade 10 Literary, pp.80–82).

**8. (Level 3)** Comparing Kuwait's two box plots (Grade 10, pp.174–175), the Arab countries' median against Europe's is:
a) lower — 480 against 780  b) the same  c) higher — 780 against 480
**Answer:** c) higher — 780 against 480, with the longer box too (Q1 510, Q3 975).
