# Quartiles, Percentiles and Box Plots — short answer

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Quartiles, the Five-Number Summary and the Box Plot · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Deciles, Percentiles, Grouped Interpolation and the Semi-Interquartile Range · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Cumulative Frequency Curves and Comparing Plots.

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Write the five-number summary of 75, 76, 77, 80, 82, 84, 85.
**Answer:** 75, 76, 80, 84, 85 — Kuwait Grade 9's worked data (pp.198–203).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In a box plot, what does the box itself stretch between?
**Answer:** Q1 and Q3 — the middle half of the data; the whiskers run to the smallest and largest values.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Cambodia's teacher guide (teacher evidence) gives what Q1 for the 460-investor grouped table?
**Answer:** 53.31 — a guide answer, cited as teacher evidence (guide, printed p.222).

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Ethiopia's guide, taught corrected as teacher evidence: what is Q1 in the n = 72 quartiles exercise, and what did the guide print?
**Answer:** Q1 = 34; the guide printed 33, which its own working line contradicts (guide, p.227, corrected).

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Which percentile is Q3?
**Answer:** P75 — the 75th percentile; likewise Q1 = P25 and the median = P50 = D5.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** Ethiopia's Exercise 7.15 table (teacher evidence, corrected): what cumulative frequency belongs to the class 81–90?
**Answer:** 92 — the table prints "9"; 68 + 24 = 92, and the final class shows 100 (guide, p.230, corrected).

**7. (Sub lesson 3)** Sierra Leone's SSS3 lesson L109 uses the cumulative frequency curve to estimate which two things?
**Answer:** The quartiles and the interquartile range (SSS3, L109, p.39).

**8. (Sub lesson 3)** Kuwait's phone-card example (n = 11): state its median, Q1 and Q3.
**Answer:** Median 350, Q1 = 300, Q3 = 400 — Grade 11 Statistics, lesson 4-1, p.12.
