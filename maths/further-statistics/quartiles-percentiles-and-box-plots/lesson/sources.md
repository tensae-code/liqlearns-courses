Cambodia carries deciles and percentiles in its Grade 11 Basic student book, with the grouped exercises' answers resting on its teacher guide — teacher evidence, worded as such. Ethiopia carries grouped deciles, quartiles and percentiles only as teacher evidence from its Grade 11 guide, three of whose values this lesson teaches corrected — D3 = 20.15, Q1 = 34, and the cumulative frequency 92 — never at their printed values. Jordan carries quartiles, the IQR, outliers and the box plot in Grade 8, examined in its final test. Kazakhstan carries the box plot and the ogive in the deepest Grade 8 statistics chapter in its band. Nepal carries quartiles and percentiles for individual and discrete data in Grade 9 Optional and box plots for discrete and continuous data across its two Optional books, with ogives in its school statistics units as filed. Iraq carries the cumulative frequency table and the ogive curve step by step in Grade 10 Literary. South Sudan carries the ogive with medians, quartiles and percentiles read from it in S2. Sierra Leone carries the cumulative frequency curve in SSS3, estimating quartiles and the interquartile range from it.
