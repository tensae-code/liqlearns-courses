# Video script — Quartiles, Percentiles and Box Plots

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Line your numbers up, smallest to biggest.
Find the middle one. That is the **median**.
Now find the middle of the bottom half. That is the **first quartile**.
The middle of the top half is the **third quartile**.
Four equal parts — quarters of your data.

Write five numbers: the smallest, the first quartile, the median,
the third quartile, the biggest. That is the **five-number summary**.
Draw it — a box from quartile to quartile, a line at the median,
and whiskers out to the ends. A box-and-whisker plot.

Want finer cuts? Split the data into ten parts: **deciles**.
Into a hundred parts: **percentiles**.
The 90th percentile beats ninety of every hundred values.

And draw the running total as a curve — the ogive.
Run across from half the total, drop down, and there is your median.
Run across from three quarters, and there is your third quartile.

Five numbers, one box, every percentile on one curve.
