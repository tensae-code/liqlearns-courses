# Moments, Skewness and Kurtosis — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Level 1)** The first moment about the mean, m₁, is always ______.
a) 1  b) 0  c) The variance
**Answer:** b) 0 — the signed deviations from the mean cancel (Pakistan, Ch. 4).

**2. (Level 1)** The second moment about the mean, m₂, is ______.
a) The standard deviation  b) The mean deviation  c) The variance
**Answer:** c) The variance (Pakistan, Ch. 4, pp.104–120).

**3. (Level 1)** Sheppard's correction for a grouped m₂ with class width h = 10 and m₂ = 50 gives ______.
a) 58.33  b) 41.67  c) 50
**Answer:** b) 41.67 — corrected m₂ = m₂ − h²/12 = 50 − 100/12 (Pakistan, Ch. 4).

**4. (Level 2)** A frequency curve tails to the right and its mean is pulled past its median. The skew is ______ (Kuwait, lesson 4-2).
a) Negative  b) Zero  c) Positive
**Answer:** c) Positive — the long tail names the skew (Kuwait, pp.19–20).

**5. (Level 2)** Bowley's coefficient for Q1 = 32, median 41.5, Q3 = 46 is about ______.
a) −0.36  b) 0.36  c) 0
**Answer:** a) −0.36 — (46 + 32 − 83)/(46 − 32) = −5/14; negative, matching Kuwait's box-plot verdict (p.21).

**6. (Level 2)** Cambodia's skewness value of −0.05 is filed in this lesson as ______.
a) A student-book worked example  b) Teacher evidence from the guide  c) A book error
**Answer:** b) Teacher evidence from the guide — it is the Grade 11 Basic guide's answer (guide p.236), worded as teacher evidence.

**7. (Level 3)** A distribution has β₂ = 1.7. It is ______.
a) Leptokurtic  b) Mesokurtic  c) Platykurtic
**Answer:** c) Platykurtic — β₂ < 3 means flatter than the bell (Pakistan, Ch. 4).

**8. (Level 3)** In Pakistan's Example 4.7, the correct Q1 is ______.
a) 40.15  b) 40.51  c) 41.50
**Answer:** b) 40.51 — the book computes 40.51 then transposes it to 40.15 in the working; taught corrected (p.91).
