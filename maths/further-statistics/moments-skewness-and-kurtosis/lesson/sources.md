**Acceptance count: taught in 4 countries (Pakistan, Kuwait, Cambodia, Afghanistan).** Pakistan carries the whole moments machinery alone — moments about the mean, an origin and zero, Sheppard's corrections, the moment skewness measures, Pearson's and Bowley's coefficients and β₂ kurtosis — with its p.91 transposition (Q1 = 40.51, not 40.15) and its p.102 denominator ((Q3 + Q1), not (Q3 − Q1)) taught corrected. Kuwait carries skewness as student-book stock, judged against mean, median and mode and against the box plot. Cambodia enters only as teacher evidence: its guide's skewness answer of −0.05, worded as teacher evidence wherever it appears.

Afghanistan enters through its Ministry of Education course — the Grade 1 course in two language editions (Pashto and Dari, one work with identical page maps) and Dari student books for Grades 4, 6, 7, 8, 10, 11 and 12, all printing year 1398 HS except the Grade 8 book, which prints 1399 HS on its cover and title page and 1398 HS on its specifications page. From its books: from Grade 11: shape indices of a distribution: skewness (moment coefficient and Pearson's) and kurtosis.
