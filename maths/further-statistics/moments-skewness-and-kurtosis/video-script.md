# Video script — Moments, Skewness and Kurtosis

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Balance the numbers on a see-saw — at the **mean**.
Now weigh the distances. Distances themselves? They cancel — **zero**.
Distances **squared**? That is variance — the second **moment**.
Distances **cubed**? They keep their sign — they feel the **lean**.
Distances to the **fourth**? They feel the **peak**.

A long tail on the right? The mean is pulled right.
That lean is **skewness** — Pearson measures it, Bowley reads it from quartiles.

Peak like a tent, or flat like a table?
Divide the fourth moment by the second moment, squared.
Three is the middle bell. More is sharp. Less is flat.
That is **kurtosis**.

Now you. Take five numbers. Find the moments. Name the shape — whole sentences!
