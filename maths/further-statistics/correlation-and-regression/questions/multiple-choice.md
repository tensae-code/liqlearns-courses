# Correlation and Regression — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Level 1)** A scatter diagram shows:
a) one variable against time  b) one point for each pair of measurements  c) bars for categories
**Answer:** b) one point for each pair — the opening move of Egypt's Statistics Unit 1 (1-1, p.4) and Cambodia's §5.1 (p.146).

**2. (Level 1)** Cambodia's Grade 12 Basic baby table (p.146), taught corrected, contains how many babies?
a) 8  b) 7  c) 6
**Answer:** c) 6 — the text says 8, but the table has 6 data columns; the count is 6, taught corrected as filed.

**3. (Level 1)** The mean point of bivariate data is:
a) (x̄, ȳ), the point of the two means  b) the middle data point  c) where the axes cross
**Answer:** a) (x̄, ȳ) — Cambodia §5.1 (p.146) builds the picture around it, and the regression line always passes through it.

**4. (Level 2)** Pearson's r must always lie in:
a) [0, 1]  b) [−1, 1]  c) any range, depending on the units
**Answer:** b) [−1, 1] — a stated property in Pakistan Ch. 14 (pp.200–202) and Kuwait 4-3 (pp.182–193); r is also unchanged by shifts or rescaling.

**5. (Level 2)** By Kuwait's strength bands, r = −0.85 is:
a) weak negative  b) medium negative  c) strong negative
**Answer:** c) strong negative — |r| in (0.7, 1] is strong (Kuwait Grade 12 Term 1, 4-3, pp.182–193).

**6. (Level 2)** Six pairs have rank differences with Σd² = 6. Spearman's r_s =
a) 0.829  b) 0.171  c) 1.0
**Answer:** a) 0.829 — 1 − 6×6/(6×35) = 1 − 36/210, Iraq Grade 11 Literary's verified example (pp.116–117).

**7. (Level 3)** The least-squares line ŷ = a + bx has slope:
a) b = s_xy/s²_x  b) b = s²_x/s_xy  c) b = x̄/ȳ
**Answer:** a) b = s_xy/s²_x — Pakistan Ch. 14, Theorem 14.1 (pp.189–194), with a = ȳ − bx̄.

**8. (Level 3)** A study finds r = 0.9 between ice-cream sales and drowning deaths. The right conclusion is:
a) ice cream causes drowning  b) drowning causes ice-cream sales  c) the two travel together — a third thing, such as hot weather, may drive both; correlation does not imply causation
**Answer:** c) — Pakistan Ch. 14, p.206: correlation does not imply causation.
