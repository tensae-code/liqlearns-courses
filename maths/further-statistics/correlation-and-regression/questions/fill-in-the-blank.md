# Correlation and Regression — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the missing word or number.

**1. (Level 1)** Data that come in pairs, like a baby's mass and height, are called ______ data.
**Answer:** bivariate — Cambodia Grade 12 Basic, Ch. 5 (p.145–146); Indonesia Bab 3 (pp.79–130).

**2. (Level 1)** A single point far from the scatter cloud is called an ______; it can drag a measured correlation up or down.
**Answer:** outlier — Indonesia Grade 11 Edisi Revisi, Bab 1 (pp.1–80).

**3. (Level 2)** Spearman's formula is r_s = 1 − 6Σd²/(n(n² − ______)).
**Answer:** 1 — Pakistan Statistics Class 12, Ch. 15 (pp.236–237).

**4. (Level 2)** Pearson's r is the covariance of the ______ values: r = Cov(z_x, z_y).
**Answer:** standardised — Pakistan Ch. 14 (pp.200–202).

**5. (Level 3)** The least-squares line always passes through the point (______, ȳ).
**Answer:** x̄ — from a = ȳ − bx̄, Pakistan Ch. 14, Theorem 14.1 (pp.189–194).

**6. (Level 3)** The leftover between a real value and the line's prediction, y − ŷ, is called the ______; least squares makes the sum of their squares smallest, and they sum to zero.
**Answer:** residual — Pakistan Ch. 14 (pp.185–194).
