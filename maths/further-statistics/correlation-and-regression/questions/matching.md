# Correlation and Regression — matching

Match each item on the left with the one right partner on the right.

**1. (Level 1)** The point (x̄, ȳ) —
**Answer:** the mean point — Cambodia §5.1 (p.146); the regression line passes through it.

**2. (Level 1)** A fat, shapeless scatter cloud —
**Answer:** little or no linear correlation — r near 0 (Pakistan Ch. 14, p.199: positive, negative, perfect, none).

**3. (Level 2)** r ≈ −0.172 —
**Answer:** a weak negative correlation — Kuwait's worked example and bands (4-3, pp.182–193).

**4. (Level 2)** Judges' placings, or grades from weakest to excellent —
**Answer:** Spearman's rank coefficient — for order data, not measurements (Libya Ch. 6, pp.113–133; Iraq pp.116–117).

**5. (Level 3)** r as the geometric mean of two slopes —
**Answer:** r = √(b_yx × b_xy) — the two regression coefficients (Pakistan Ch. 14, Theorems 14.4–14.6, pp.203–206).

**6. (Level 3)** Comparing the means of several groups by splitting variation —
**Answer:** analysis of variance (ANOVA) — Laos Grade 12, Ch. 5, L16 (p.175).
