# Correlation and Regression — short answer

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Level 1)** For Cambodia's six babies (p.146, taught corrected), state the mean point.
**Answer:** (3.3, 40) — mean mass 19.8/6 = 3.3 kg, mean height 240/6 = 40 cm.

**2. (Level 1)** A scatter cloud climbs steeply and tightly from bottom-left to top-right. Describe it in the books' words.
**Answer:** A strong positive (direct) linear relation — the direction/strength reading of Egypt Unit 1, 1-1 (p.4) and Libya Ch. 6 (pp.113–133).

**3. (Level 2)** Iraq's price/quantity example has Σ(x − x̄)² = 16, Σ(y − ȳ)² = 44, Σ(x − x̄)(y − ȳ) = 24 (p.112). Find r and name its strength by Kuwait's bands.
**Answer:** r = 24/√(16 × 44) ≈ 0.90 — strong positive.

**4. (Level 2)** In Iraq's second Spearman example, two values tie for ranks 4 and 5. What rank does each get, and what is Σd² there?
**Answer:** Each gets the averaged rank 4.5; Σd² = 50.5, giving r_s = −0.43 (p.117, verified pages).

**5. (Level 3)** Iraq's regression Ŷ = −0.95X + 12.65 (pp.123–124), taught corrected: give the line's values at X = 4 and X = 8.
**Answer:** 8.85 and 5.05 — the book's printed (4, 8.58) and (8, 5.5) are wrong, corrected as filed.

**6. (Level 3)** Kuwait's line is ŷ = 0.75 + 1.45x and the real y at x = 5 is 9. Find ŷ(5) and the error of prediction.
**Answer:** ŷ(5) = 8; error of prediction |9 − 8| = 1 (Kuwait 4-3, pp.182–193).

**7. (Level 3)** Pakistan's checked example gives r = 0.956. What is the coefficient of determination, and what does it say?
**Answer:** r² ≈ 0.914 — about 91% of y's variation is accounted for by the line; the determination coefficient is Indonesia Bab 3's closing measure (pp.79–130).

**8. (Level 3)** What two parts does ANOVA split total variation into, and what question does it answer?
**Answer:** A between-groups part and a within-groups part; it answers whether several group means differ more than the scatter inside the groups would explain (Laos Grade 12, L16, p.175).
