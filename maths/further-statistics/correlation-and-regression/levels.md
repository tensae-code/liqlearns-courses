Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: plot paired data as a scatter diagram, find and plot the mean point (x̄, ȳ), and describe the cloud's direction and strength in whole sentences.

## Sub lesson 2 — Pearson's r and Spearman's Rank Coefficient
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: compute Pearson's r from deviations or sums, place it in Kuwait's strength bands, rank paired data (ties averaged), and compute Spearman's r_s from Σd².

## Sub lesson 3 — The Regression Line, Determination, Causation and ANOVA
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: fit ŷ = a + bx by least squares, predict with it and state the error of prediction, use both regression lines and r², explain why correlation is not causation, and say what ANOVA compares and how it splits variation.
