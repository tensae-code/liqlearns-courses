# Video script — Correlation and Regression

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Two numbers come together. A baby's mass. A baby's height. Plot one point for each baby.

Look at the cloud. Does it climb? Does it fall? Is it tight, or spread out? That picture is a scatter diagram.

Now measure the climb. Pearson's r is one number, from minus one to plus one. Near plus one: the two rise together, strongly. Near minus one: one rises, the other falls. Near zero: no straight-line link at all.

No measuring tape? Use order instead. Rank the first list. Rank the second. Compare the ranks. That is Spearman's way.

Now draw the best straight line through the cloud — the line that makes the squared misses smallest. That is the regression line. Use it to guess a new value. But stay inside the data. Outside, the line is only guessing.

And remember this, always: two things moving together does not prove one makes the other happen. Correlation is not causation.

That is correlation and regression.
