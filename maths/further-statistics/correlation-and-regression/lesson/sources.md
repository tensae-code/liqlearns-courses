**Acceptance count: taught in 14 countries (Pakistan, Libya, Indonesia, Yemen, Egypt, Greece, Iraq, Cambodia, Jordan, Kuwait, Laos, Syria, Malaysia, Rwanda).** Pakistan carries the deepest treatment: functional against statistical relations, the scatter diagram, covariance, the least-squares estimators with the line through (x̄, ȳ), the limitations of the linear model at p.195, Pearson's r with its properties, the two regression lines with r as the geometric mean of their coefficients, the causation warning at p.206, and Spearman's coefficient derived in Ch. 15. Libya carries Pearson, Spearman and least-squares regression in two books — Grade 11 Science Ch. 6 and the same ground repeated for Grade 12 Humanities Ch. 1, a deliberate overlap as filed. Indonesia carries scatter, least-squares regression, product-moment correlation and the coefficient of determination in its Grade 11 (2021) Bab 3, and the outlier warning with two-way tables in the 2024 Edisi Revisi Bab 1. Yemen carries scatter shapes and regression in Grade 11 Science and the full sum-form slope in Grade 12 Literary. Egypt carries scatter, Pearson and both regression lines in its Statistics Unit 1. Greece carries regression and correlation in three books — W9 §VI in full business form, W11's fitted-line treatment, and W13's named sections. Iraq carries Pearson in both forms, the verified Spearman worked examples at pp.116–117, and regression — with its p.124 points taught corrected as (4, 8.85) and (8, 5.05), its Spearman introduction at pp.113–115 cited nowhere, and its Grade 10 books at concept level. Cambodia carries bivariate statistics, the mean point and the regression line in Grade 12 Basic Ch. 5 — with its baby table taught corrected as 6 babies, not 8. Jordan carries correlation and regression as a named lesson in Grade 12 Business, and scatter shapes with a best-fit lab only in Grade 10. Kuwait carries Pearson with its strength bands, least squares, prediction and the error of prediction in Grade 12 Term 1. Laos carries the closing ANOVA part, taught beside correlation in Grade 12 L16. South Africa (scatter plots only) and Iran (none of this ground) are recorded negatives and are cited nowhere as sources.

Syria reads correlation off scatter diagrams in Grade 7 — positive, negative and none — with the explicit warning that a diagram with no correlation cannot be used for prediction.

Malaysia's 'linear law' chapter is its doorway into regression: scatter recognised as linear or not, a line of best fit drawn by eye and checked, and non-linear laws straightened into lines whose gradient and intercept carry the constants. (The equation of the line of best fit itself is filed under coordinates-and-graphs; correlation coefficients are not in Malaysia's books.)

Rwanda teaches correlation and regression in Advanced Senior Five — bivariate data and covariance, both least-squares regression lines, Pearson's r with its bounds and properties, and Spearman's rank correlation, with regression fitted to real pulse-rate recovery data.
