# Video script — Association of Attributes

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Some things you cannot measure. You can only say yes or no. Can read: yes or no. Has a job: yes or no.

Put the people in four boxes. Read and has a job. Read, no job. Cannot read, has a job. Cannot read, no job. Four boxes, and everybody is in exactly one.

Now ask: if the two qualities had nothing to do with each other, how many people would land in the first box? Multiply the row total by the column total, divide by everybody. That is the independence number.

More people in the box than that number? The qualities lean together. Fewer? They lean apart. Yule's Q turns that leaning into one number, from minus one to plus one.

And the chi-square test adds up the quarrel between the real table and the independence table, box by box. A big quarrel: the qualities are not independent.

Qualities in boxes, the independence number, and one honest test. That is association.
