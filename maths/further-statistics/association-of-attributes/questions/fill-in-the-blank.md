# Association of Attributes — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the missing word or number.

**1. (Level 1)** Splitting a population by one attribute into present and absent is called a ______.
**Answer:** dichotomy — Pakistan Ch. 15 (pp.215–216).

**2. (Level 1)** A count like (A), which includes everyone in a class, is a ______ frequency; a count like (AB) is an ultimate class frequency.
**Answer:** class — the chapter's opening distinction (pp.215–216).

**3. (Level 2)** If (AB) is greater than (A)(B)/n, the association is ______.
**Answer:** positive — pp.217–220.

**4. (Level 3)** The expected frequency of cell (i, j) is e_ij = (row total × ______ total)/n.
**Answer:** column — the independence criterion applied cell by cell (pp.224–232).

**5. (Level 3)** The coefficient of contingency is C = √(χ²/(______ + χ²)).
**Answer:** n — p.226.
