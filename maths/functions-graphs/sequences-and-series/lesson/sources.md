**Acceptance count: taught in 11 countries (Kazakhstan, Jordan,
Sierra Leone, Mauritania, South Africa, Laos, Yemen, Indonesia, Nicaragua, Ethiopia, South Sudan).** Kazakhstan carries
the fullest treatment — all four Grade 9 Algebra books prove
the nth-term formulas, the characteristic properties, both
sum formulas and the infinite geometric sum, with applied
problems. Jordan carries arithmetic and geometric sequences
and series together in Grade 11. Sierra Leone carries
sequences and series as SSS 2 practice lessons (L49–L56) and
arithmetic and geometric patterns in JSS 2; its geometric
series sum arrives only in the SSS 3 review. Mauritania
carries numerical sequences in 5AS. South Africa carries
number patterns and sequences as Grade 9 worksheet practice.
Honest negatives: Egypt's Grade 11 Pure book explicitly
teaches no sequences or series, so Egypt is not claimed for
this lesson; Sierra Leone's SSS 2 teaches the arithmetic
series sum but no geometric series sum, and its SSS 3 lesson's
claim that no infinite sum can be found is not taught here —
the infinite sum rests on Kazakhstan's theorem and Jordan. Laos carries sequences from number sequences and puzzles in Grade 5 to arithmetic and geometric sequences with limits, sums and applications in Grade 12. Yemen carries sequences and series from Σ notation in Grade 10 to arithmetic and geometric sequences and their sums in Grade 11, in both tracks — with separate sum lessons in the literary track and sums folded into the sequence lessons in the science track. Indonesia carries sequences from number patterns in Grade 8 — where the geometric sequence is named but deferred to SMA — to arithmetic and geometric sequences and series with the infinite geometric series in Grades 10 and 12. Nicaragua carries sequences and series whole in Grade 11 — arithmetic and geometric sequences with general terms and finite sums, and sigma notation with the sums of the first n naturals and of their squares — but finite sums only: no infinite geometric series is taught, and the stray "Sección 4 — Notación de sumatoria" header claimed for p. 1 was not found by the citation check and is never cited. Ethiopia carries sequences and series whole in Grade 12 — identifying sequences, the general term, sums, sigma notation with its four properties, arithmetic-series sums and convergence and divergence of infinite series — with pattern work as the early ladder in Grades 3–5 (predicting far terms, the 52nd term, patterns generalised with variables). South Sudan carries only the edges of this lesson — counting in 2s and 5s in Primary 1 and missing-number sequences in Primary 2, then summation inside the limit-and-area section of Secondary 4 Additional Mathematics, with a guide-only "Limits and series" section in the Additional teacher's guide; it teaches no sequences or series as a topic, no nth term and no arithmetic or geometric series formulas.

**Flagged, not taught as printed:** the Grade 12 student book's sigma-notation property (2) (printed p. 24) prints Σ a_i where the sigma index is k; it should read Σ a_k. A typesetting slip; the lesson teaches Σ a_k.
