# Video script — Sequences and series

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Two, four, six, eight, ten! Add two each time!
That is **arithmetic**. Same add, every step!

Three, six, twelve, twenty-four! Times two each time!
That is **geometric**. Same multiply, every step!

Add or multiply? That is the whole question!

Now add them up! One to a hundred?
Pair them! One with a hundred — 101!
Two with ninety-nine — 101!
Fifty pairs of 101 — five thousand and fifty!
That is a **series** — a sum!

And half, quarter, eighth, sixteenth?
It never passes one! Half plus a quarter plus an eighth —
closer and closer to one, forever!
That is the **infinite sum**!

Now you. Make your sequence. Add or multiply?
Find your tenth term. Add them up!
