# Sequences and series — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Sequences, Rules and the nth Term · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Series — Adding the Terms · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — The Infinite Geometric Sum.

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The next term of 2, 5, 8, 11, … is —
a) 13  b) 14  c) 15
**Answer: b) 14** — add 3 each time.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The next term of 3, 6, 12, 24, … is —
a) 36  b) 48  c) 30
**Answer: b) 48** — multiply by 2 each time.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The 10th term of 2, 5, 8, 11, … is —
a) 26  b) 29  c) 32
**Answer: b) 29** — a₁₀ = 2 + 9 × 3.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The sum of the first 5 terms of 2, 5, 8, 11, 14 is —
a) 40  b) 35  c) 50
**Answer: a) 40** — 5/2 × (2 + 14) = 40.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The sum of 1 + 2 + … + 100 is —
a) 5,000  b) 5,050  c) 10,100
**Answer: b) 5,050** — fifty pairs of 101.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** The sum of the first 4 terms of 3, 6, 12, 24 is —
a) 42  b) 45  c) 48
**Answer: b) 45** — 3 + 6 + 12 + 24.

**7. (Sub lesson 3)** The sum to infinity of 1/2 + 1/4 + 1/8 + … is —
a) 2  b) 1  c) 1/2
**Answer: b) 1** — (1/2)/(1 − 1/2) = 1.

**8. (Sub lesson 3)** The sum to infinity of 8 + 4 + 2 + 1 + … is —
a) 12  b) 16  c) 15
**Answer: b) 16** — 8/(1 − 1/2) = 16.
