# Sequences and series — fill in the blank

Put the missing word or number in the space.

**1. (Level 1)** The numbers in a sequence are called its ______.
**Answer:** terms.

**2. (Level 1)** In an arithmetic sequence, each term is the last term plus the common ______.
**Answer:** difference.

**3. (Level 1)** In a geometric sequence, each term is the last term times the common ______.
**Answer:** ratio.

**4. (Level 2)** The nth term of an arithmetic sequence is aₙ = a₁ + (n − 1)______.
**Answer:** d.

**5. (Level 2)** The nth term of a geometric sequence is bₙ = b₁ × r to the power ______.
**Answer:** n − 1.

**6. (Level 2)** The sum of an arithmetic series is Sₙ = n/2 × (a₁ + ______).
**Answer:** aₙ.

**7. (Level 3)** The infinite geometric sum S = b₁/(1 − r) works only when |r| is less than ______.
**Answer:** 1.

**8. (Level 3)** 0.18̄ as a fraction is 2/______.
**Answer:** 11.
