# Sequences and series — matching

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Sequences, Rules and the nth Term · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Series — Adding the Terms · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — The Infinite Geometric Sum.

Join the two sides that go together.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Match each sequence to its kind.
- 2, 5, 8, 11 — a) geometric
- 2, 6, 18, 54 — b) arithmetic
- 5, 5, 5, 5 — c) both (difference 0, ratio 1)
**Answer:** 2, 5, 8, 11–b; 2, 6, 18, 54–a; 5, 5, 5, 5–c.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Match each formula to its name.
- aₙ = a₁ + (n − 1)d — a) geometric nth term
- bₙ = b₁rⁿ⁻¹ — b) arithmetic series sum
- Sₙ = n/2 × (a₁ + aₙ) — c) arithmetic nth term
- Sₙ = b₁(1 − rⁿ)/(1 − r) — d) geometric series sum
**Answer:** aₙ–c, bₙ–a, arithmetic Sₙ–b, geometric Sₙ–d.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Match each infinite series to its sum (or "no sum").
- 1/2 + 1/4 + 1/8 + … — a) 2
- 1 + 1/2 + 1/4 + … — b) 1
- 1 + 2 + 4 + 8 + … — c) no finite sum
**Answer:** 1/2 + …–b (sum 1), 1 + 1/2 + …–a (sum 2), 1 + 2 + …–c.
