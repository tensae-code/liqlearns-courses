# Sequences and series — short answer

Answer in a word or a short sentence.

**1. (Level 1)** What is the difference between a sequence and a series?
**Answer:** A sequence is the list of terms; a series is the sum of the terms.

**2. (Level 1)** Is 5, 10, 15, 20 arithmetic or geometric?
**Answer:** Arithmetic (add 5 each time).

**3. (Level 1)** What is the common ratio of 2, 6, 18, 54, …?
**Answer:** 3.

**4. (Level 2)** Find the sum of the first 10 terms of 1, 2, 3, 4, … (the numbers 1 to 10).
**Answer:** 55 (10/2 × (1 + 10)).

**5. (Level 2)** Find the 6th term of 3, 6, 12, 24, …
**Answer:** 96 (3 × 2⁵).

**6. (Level 2)** A geometric series has first term 5 and ratio 2. What is the sum of its first 3 terms?
**Answer:** 35 (5 + 10 + 20).

**7. (Level 3)** Write 0.333… as a fraction, using the infinite geometric sum.
**Answer:** 1/3.

**8. (Level 3)** A ball is dropped from 8 m and each bounce rises half as high as the last. What is the total distance travelled up and down, to the end? (Down 8, then up 4 + down 4, up 2 + down 2, …)
**Answer:** 24 m (down: 8 + 4 + 2 + … = 16; up: 4 + 2 + 1 + … = 8; total 16 + 8 = 24).
