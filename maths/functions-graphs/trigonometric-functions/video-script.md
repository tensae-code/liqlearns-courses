# Video script — Trigonometric Functions

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Walk around a circle with me!

Start at the right. Walk up! Your height grows — that height is **sine**!
Walk to the top! Height is **1**!
Walk left and down. Height shrinks… zero… now BELOW!
Sine goes **negative**! The circle allows it!

One full walk — one full wave! Walk again — the SAME wave!
That is the **period**. The wave repeats, forever!

Stretch the wave tall — that is **amplitude**!
Squeeze it short — a smaller period!
Slide it sideways — that is **phase**!

Tides do this! Wheels do this! The sea breathes in a sine wave!

Now you. Walk the circle. Draw the wave.
Tall it, squeeze it, slide it — it is still sine!
