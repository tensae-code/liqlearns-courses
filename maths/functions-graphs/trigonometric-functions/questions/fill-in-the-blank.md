# Trigonometric Functions — fill in the blank

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Trig Functions of Any Angle · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — The Graphs of the Trig Functions · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Amplitude, Period, Phase and Models.

Fill each blank with the missing number or words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Sine is the point's ______ on the unit circle, and cosine is its ______ distance.
**Answer:** height; sideways — the two coordinates of the point the angle lands on (Iraq, Grade 11, Ch. 4).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Two angles that land on the same point of the circle are called ______ angles.
**Answer:** coterminal — they differ by whole turns and share every function value.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The period of y = tan x is ______, half the period of sine.
**Answer:** π — the tangent's wave completes in half a turn.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The graph of y = cos x is the graph of y = sin x shifted ______ by π/2.
**Answer:** left (equivalently: cosine starts at its peak where sine starts at zero).

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** In a + b sin(cθ + d), the period is ______ and the maximum value is ______.
**Answer:** 2π/c; a + |b| — Pakistan's sinusoidal family (Unit 11, pp.200–213).

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** China's tide model y = A sin(ωx + φ) + b gets its period from the time between two ______ tides.
**Answer:** high (successive) — one full wave per tide cycle (§7.4, pp.64–65).
