# Trigonometric Functions — matching

Match each item on the left with the one right item on the right.

**1. (Level 1)** Third quadrant → **Answer:** tangent positive, sine and cosine negative — the quadrant where height and sideways distance agree in sign (both negative).

**2. (Level 2)** Period π → **Answer:** y = tan x — the shortest period of the three graphed functions (Ethiopia, Grade 10, Unit 4).

**3. (Level 2)** Range [−1, 1] → **Answer:** y = sin x and y = cos x — the two coordinates of a point on the unit circle (Pakistan, Unit 11).

**4. (Level 3)** Amplitude → **Answer:** |b| in a + b sin(cθ + d) — half the distance from the wave's top to its bottom.

**5. (Level 3)** y = A sin(ωx + φ) + b fitted to a harbour → **Answer:** China's tide model (§7.4, pp.64–65).

Right-hand items in full: tangent positive, sine and cosine negative · y = tan x · y = sin x and y = cos x · |b| in a + b sin(cθ + d) · China's tide model.
