# Trigonometric Functions — short answer

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Trig Functions of Any Angle · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — The Graphs of the Trig Functions · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Amplitude, Period, Phase and Models.

Answer in a word or a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Give the signs of sin(150°), cos(150°) and tan(150°).
**Answer:** Sine positive, cosine negative, tangent negative — second quadrant (Iraq, Grade 11, Ch. 4).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Reduce 765° to its coterminal angle between 0° and 360°.
**Answer:** 45° — subtract two full turns: 765° − 720° = 45°.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** What are sin(−x), cos(−x) and tan(−x) in terms of the functions of x?
**Answer:** sin(−x) = −sin x; cos(−x) = cos x; tan(−x) = −tan x — turning the other way flips sine and tangent only (Iraq, Grade 11, Ch. 4).

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** State the domain and range of y = sec x in words.
**Answer:** Domain: all reals except odd multiples of π/2 (where cosine is zero); range: (−∞, −1] ∪ [1, ∞) — Pakistan's six-function table (Unit 11, pp.200–217).

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** At which angles in [0, 2π) does y = tan x have asymptotes?
**Answer:** π/2 and 3π/2 — the odd multiples of π/2 (Ethiopia, Grade 10, Unit 4).

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** Give the amplitude, period and middle line of y = 1 + 5 cos(4θ).
**Answer:** Amplitude 5; period 2π/4 = π/2; middle line 1 — read off a + b cos(cθ) in Pakistan's form (Unit 11).

**7. (Sub lesson 3)** A Ferris wheel's rider height is h = 10 + 8 sin(πt/5). How long does one full turn take?
**Answer:** 10 time units — period = 2π / (π/5) = 10 (Pakistan's Ferris-wheel model, Unit 11).

**8. (Sub lesson 3)** Iraq's Grade 10 book prints tan(−36°) = −0.588. Why is that wrong, and what is the true value?
**Answer:** −0.588 is sin(−36°), not tan(−36°); the true value is ≈ −0.7265 — taught corrected (Iraq, Grade 10 Scientific, p.84).
