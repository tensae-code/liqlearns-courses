# Video script — Functions

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Here is a machine! Put a number in. Out comes a number!

Put in 3. Double it. Out comes 6!
Put in 5. Double it. Out comes 10!
Same in, same out. Every time. That is a **function**!

One in, one out. That is the rule.
If one in gives two different outs — that is NOT a function!

The ins are the **domain**. The outs are the **range**.

Now two machines in a row! Double, then add three.
In goes 4. Double — 8! Add three — 11!
That is a **composite** — machines in a row!

And backwards? The machine doubled it. Undo it — halve it!
That is the **inverse**. It takes you home!

Now you. Build your machine. Feed it numbers.
One in, one out — that is a function!
