fullest build — Cartesian product, relation, function, domain

in both Grade 9 Algebra books. Vietnam carries the function

Science. Mauritania carries the general study of functions in

Laos carries functions from mappings and linear and affine functions in Grade 8, through quadratic functions in Grade 9, to the full function course — relations, constant, linear, quadratic, cubic, root, rational, exponential, logarithmic, trigonometric, hyperbolic and inverse functions — in Grades 10–12. Yemen carries functions from the function and the linear function in Grade 9, through mappings — including composition of mappings, taught under the mappings name — in Grade 10, to real functions, their types and monotonicity in Grade 11 science. Indonesia carries functions from relations and linear functions in Grade 8, through exponential and quadratic functions in Grade 10, to composition and inverses and transformations of functions in Grades 11–12, with trigonometric, logarithmic and algebraic functions in Grade 11 Tingkat Lanjut. Note: Indonesia teaches NO function composition or inverses in Grade 10 — composition is taught in Grade 11 — so the lesson's 'composite functions' label rests on Grade 11 teaching, as with Yemen's Grade 10 mappings; Grade 8 functions are linear only, and piecewise functions are not a named topic in the held books. Nicaragua carries functions from direct proportion as y = ax in Grade 6, through the function concept with y = ax and y = a/x in Grade 7 and the first-degree function in Grade 8, to quadratic functions in vertex form with maxima and minima in Grade 9, then piecewise, absolute-value, greatest-integer, square-root and rational functions in Grade 10 — in both the student book and the module, which adds exponential, logarithmic and inverse functions — and exponential and logarithmic functions with their equations in Grade 11. Ethiopia carries functions from relations and functions in Grade 10 — relations as ordered pairs, operations on functions, one-to-one, onto and bijective functions, and graphs of constant, linear and quadratic functions — to relations, compositions and inverses in Grade 11, with the inverse of f(x) = x³ − x + 1 shown not to be a function; Grade 10 teaches NO composite functions (f∘g) and NO inverse functions as a standalone treatment (only the exp/log inverse relationship), while Grade 11 teaches compositions explicitly — so the lesson's 'composite functions' label rests on the Grade 11 teaching. South Sudan adds a fourth: composition IS taught there in Secondary 2, under the name "composite functions" (p. 62: "Composite functions; order matters"), alongside Yemen's mappings, Indonesia's Grade 11 and Ethiopia's Grade 11. Iraq carries functions from functions in tables in Grade 7, through an introduction to functions and linear functions in Grade 8, to mappings in Grade 9 — relations, domain and range, injective/surjective/bijective, and composition f∘g and g∘f taught explicitly — and real functions with their widest domains in both Grade 10 tracks, and circular functions in Grade 11 Scientific. Its Grade 9 composition of mappings joins Yemen's Grade 10 composition of mappings, Indonesia's Grade 11 composition of functions and Ethiopia's Grade 11 compositions as real evidence for the lesson's 'composite functions' label. Nepal carries functions only in part — only in the Optional books: relations, functions, domain and range and graphs of y = xⁿ in Grade 9 Optional, and composite and inverse functions in Grade 10 Optional; the compulsory Grade 9 and Grade 10 books teach no functions. Cambodia carries functions only in part — the definition, domain and range, even and odd functions, graphs, inverses and exponential, logarithmic and trigonometric functions in Grades 10–12, with composite functions in the Limits booklet and piecewise functions at chapter level in the Grade 12 Basic continuity section; relation versus function and codomain are not taught anywhere in the set, and the one composite-functions example in the Grade 12 Basic guide is guide-only and is not cited.
