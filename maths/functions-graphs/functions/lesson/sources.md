**Acceptance count: taught in 6 countries (Egypt, Jordan,
Kazakhstan, Vietnam, Libya, Mauritania).** Egypt carries the
fullest build — Cartesian product, relation, function, domain
and range in Preparatory 3; the sign of a function in
Secondary 1; the inverse function in Grade 11 Pure. Jordan
carries deciding whether a relation is a function, the
quadratic function and its graph in Grade 9, and piecewise
functions in Grade 11. Kazakhstan carries functions chapters
in both Grade 9 Algebra books. Vietnam carries the function
concept and the first-degree function in Grade 8 and the
quadratic function y = ax² in Grade 9. Libya carries
relations, functions and the algebra of functions in Grade 11
Science. Mauritania carries the general study of functions in
5AS. Honest negatives: Libya Grade 9 teaches no functions as
such (linear models and equations only, no function notation
or mapping) — Libya enters this lesson from Grade 11, not
Grade 9; Mauritania's 1AS/2AS teach no functions, so
Mauritania enters from 5AS. Composite functions are taught
here as the books' function work put together (two functions
in a row, inside-out) — the word "composite" is the lesson's
own label for that book-taught operation, and the inverse is
taught from Egypt's Grade 11 lesson, not invented.
