**Acceptance count: taught in 8 countries (Jordan, Kuwait, Ethiopia, South Sudan, Laos, Iran, Syria, Malaysia).** Jordan carries the point-to-line distance and the coordinate proof in Grade 9 and the circle's equation in Grade 10. Kuwait carries the full analytic-geometry unit in Grade 10 Term 2 — the section formula with internal and external division as its own section, and the point-to-line distance and circle in the same unit. Ethiopia carries the circle's equation in the Grade 10 student book's §7.5 — student book only, since its Grade 10 guide does not cover §7.5. South Sudan carries the circle's equation in Secondary 4, from the origin-centred circle to completing the square. Laos carries the section formula as a minor source inside its Grade 9 vectors lesson. Iran carries the analytic circle in two Grade 12 branch books, with the expanded-form test stated exactly in Geometry (3).

Syria computes a parallelogram's fourth vertex from the midpoint formula in Grade 7.

Malaysia teaches coordinate geometry in Form 2 (distance from Pythagoras, midpoint, gradient) and completes it in Additional Mathematics Form 4 with the section formula and the parallel/perpendicular gradient conditions.
