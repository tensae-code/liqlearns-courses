# Coordinate Geometry — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Level 1)** The point dividing A(0, 0) to B(6, 3) internally in the ratio 1:2 is —
a) (4, 2)  b) (2, 1)  c) (3, 1.5)
**Answer:** b) (2, 1) — the weight on A is 2 (the far part), on B is 1: ((2·0 + 1·6)/3, (2·0 + 1·3)/3).

**2. (Level 1)** In the section formula for the ratio m:n, the weight on the first point's coordinates is —
a) m  b) n  c) m + n
**Answer:** b) n — the weight sits at the far end, as Kuwait's section 9-2 states it.

**3. (Level 1)** The midpoint of A(2, 4) and B(8, 10) is —
a) (5, 7)  b) (6, 6)  c) (10, 14)
**Answer:** a) (5, 7) — the section formula with equal weights is the average of the ends.

**4. (Level 2)** The distance from (0, 0) to the line 3x + 4y − 10 = 0 is —
a) 10  b) 2  c) 5
**Answer:** b) 2 — |−10| / √(9 + 16) = 10/5.

**5. (Level 2)** To prove a quadrilateral is a parallelogram by coordinates, one clean test is —
a) all four sides equal  b) the diagonals have the same midpoint  c) one angle is 90°
**Answer:** b) the diagonals have the same midpoint — Jordan's coordinate-proof test (Grade 9, p.177).

**6. (Level 3)** The centre and radius of (x − 3)² + (y + 4)² = 36 are —
a) centre (3, −4), radius 6  b) centre (−3, 4), radius 6  c) centre (3, −4), radius 36
**Answer:** a) centre (3, −4), radius 6 — read (h, k) from (x − h)² + (y − k)² = r², and r = √36.

**7. (Level 3)** The point dividing A(1, 1) to B(5, 5) externally in the ratio 3:1 is —
a) (4, 4)  b) (7, 7)  c) (2, 2)
**Answer:** b) (7, 7) — external division: ((−1·1 + 3·5)/(3 − 1), same for y) = (14/2, 14/2), past B on the line.

**8. (Level 3)** For x² + y² + 2ax + 2by + c = 0 with a² + b² < 4c, the equation names —
a) a circle  b) a single point  c) no points at all
**Answer:** c) no points at all — Iran's Geometry (3) test (p.42): greater is a real circle, equal is a point, less is nothing.
