# Coordinate Geometry — true or false

Say true or false, and why in one line.

**1. (Level 1)** The midpoint is the section formula with ratio 1:1. — **True.** Equal weights give the average of the two ends.

**2. (Level 1)** In the ratio m:n, the point is m parts from B. — **False.** It is m parts from A and n parts from B; the weight on A's coordinates is n.

**3. (Level 2)** A slanted segment from a point to a line can be shorter than the perpendicular one. — **False.** The perpendicular is the shortest — that is why it is the distance (Jordan, Grade 9, p.168).

**4. (Level 2)** If two diagonals have the same midpoint, the quadrilateral is a parallelogram. — **True.** That is Jordan's coordinate-proof test (p.177).

**5. (Level 3)** External division in the ratio 1:1 gives a point far away on the line. — **False.** It gives no point at all: the denominator m − n is zero (Kuwait, 9-2, pp.124–130).

**6. (Level 3)** Every equation x² + y² + 2ax + 2by + c = 0 is a circle. — **False.** With a² + b² < 4c it names no points at all (Iran, Geometry (3), p.42).
