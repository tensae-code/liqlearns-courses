# Coordinate Geometry — fill in the blank

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Dividing a Segment Inside · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 3 — Distance to a Line and Coordinate Proof · (Level 3) splits by what the item asks: external-division items -> Sub lesson 2 — Dividing a Segment Outside, circle items -> Sub lesson 4 — The Equation of a Circle. One exception: ordering item 4, about distances from a point to lines, is Sub lesson 3's work and is marked Sub lesson 3.

Fill each blank with the missing number or words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The point dividing a segment in the ratio 1:1 is called the ______.
**Answer:** midpoint — equal weights in the section formula give the average of the ends.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A point 3:2 along from A to B is closer to ______.
**Answer:** B — it has walked 3 of the 5 parts, so more than half the journey is done.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The distance from a point to a line is measured along the ______ from the point to the line.
**Answer:** perpendicular — the shortest rope is the one at right angles to the line (Jordan, Grade 9, p.168).

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** The distance from (1, 2) to the line 3x + 4y + 5 = 0 is ______.
**Answer:** 16/5 — |3 + 8 + 5| / √(9 + 16) = 16/5.

**5. (Sub lesson 4)** The circle (x + 2)² + (y − 3)² = 25 has centre ______ and radius ______.
**Answer:** centre (−2, 3), radius 5 — the signs inside the brackets flip for the centre.

**6. (Sub lesson 4)** In Iran's test for x² + y² + 2ax + 2by + c = 0, when a² + b² = 4c the "circle" is just a single ______.
**Answer:** point — equal means the radius is zero (Geometry (3), p.42).
