# Coordinate Geometry — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the missing number or words.

**1. (Level 1)** The point dividing a segment in the ratio 1:1 is called the ______.
**Answer:** midpoint — equal weights in the section formula give the average of the ends.

**2. (Level 1)** A point 3:2 along from A to B is closer to ______.
**Answer:** B — it has walked 3 of the 5 parts, so more than half the journey is done.

**3. (Level 2)** The distance from a point to a line is measured along the ______ from the point to the line.
**Answer:** perpendicular — the shortest rope is the one at right angles to the line (Jordan, Grade 9, p.168).

**4. (Level 2)** The distance from (1, 2) to the line 3x + 4y + 5 = 0 is ______.
**Answer:** 16/5 — |3 + 8 + 5| / √(9 + 16) = 16/5.

**5. (Level 3)** The circle (x + 2)² + (y − 3)² = 25 has centre ______ and radius ______.
**Answer:** centre (−2, 3), radius 5 — the signs inside the brackets flip for the centre.

**6. (Level 3)** In Iran's test for x² + y² + 2ax + 2by + c = 0, when a² + b² = 4c the "circle" is just a single ______.
**Answer:** point — equal means the radius is zero (Geometry (3), p.42).
