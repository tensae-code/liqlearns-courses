# Video script — Coordinate Geometry

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Here are two points. A and B. Walk from A to B!

Walk three steps out of five. Stop! You are at P!
P splits the line. Three parts behind you. Two parts in front.
Near an end? You take that end's numbers! That is the **section formula**!

Now a point. And a line below it.
Drop a rope! Swing it! Shorter… shorter…
Straight up and down — STOP! That is the shortest!
That shortest rope is the **distance from the point to the line**!

Now a circle! It has a middle — the **centre**.
Every point on the circle is the same walk from the centre.
That walk is the **radius**.
Centre and radius — that is the whole circle, in one short line:
(x − h)² + (y − k)² = r²!

Now you. Split a segment. Drop a rope. Write a circle!
