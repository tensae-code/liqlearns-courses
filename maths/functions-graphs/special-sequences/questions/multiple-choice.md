# Special Sequences — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Square, Cube and Triangular Numbers · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Harmonic Progressions and the Harmonic Mean · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Arithmetico-Geometric Progressions.

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The 5th square number is…
a) 20  b) 25  c) 32
**Answer:** b) 25 — 5² = 25 (Sierra Leone Primary 6, Lesson 116, p. 143 — teacher evidence).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The sequence 1, 3, 6, 10, 15, … is the…
a) cube numbers  b) square numbers  c) triangular numbers
**Answer:** c) triangular numbers — running totals of 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 (Lesson 120, p. 151 — teacher evidence).

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The next cube number after 27 is…
a) 36  b) 64  c) 81
**Answer:** b) 64 — 4³ = 64 (Lesson 118, p. 147 — teacher evidence).

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which sequence is harmonic?
a) 1/2, 1/4, 1/8  b) 1/2, 1/3, 1/5  c) 1/2, 1/3, 1/4
**Answer:** c) 1/2, 1/3, 1/4 — its reciprocals 2, 3, 4 form an A.P. with difference 1 (Pakistan, Class 11, Unit 6).

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The harmonic mean of 4 and 12 is…
a) 8  b) 6  c) 7
**Answer:** b) 6 — 2 × 4 × 12/(4 + 12) = 96/16 = 6.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** In the arithmetico-geometric progression 1, 1, 3/4, 1/2, …, the G.P. part has ratio…
a) 2  b) 1/2  c) 1/4
**Answer:** b) 1/2 — the terms are (1, 2, 3, 4, …) × (1, 1/2, 1/4, 1/8, …).

**7. (Sub lesson 3)** The sum to infinity of 1 + 2·(1/2) + 3·(1/4) + 4·(1/8) + … is…
a) 2  b) 3  c) 4
**Answer:** c) 4 — by the multiply-by-r-and-subtract method (Pakistan, Class 11, Unit 6).

**8. (Sub lesson 1)** The gaps between square numbers 1, 4, 9, 16, 25 are…
a) 2, 4, 6, 8  b) 3, 5, 7, 9  c) 1, 2, 3, 4
**Answer:** b) 3, 5, 7, 9 — the odd numbers in order (Lesson 117, p. 145 — teacher evidence).
