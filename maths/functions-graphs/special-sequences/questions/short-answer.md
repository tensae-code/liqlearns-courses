# Special Sequences — short answer

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Square, Cube and Triangular Numbers · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Harmonic Progressions and the Harmonic Mean · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Arithmetico-Geometric Progressions.

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Write the first five triangular numbers.
**Answer:** 1, 3, 6, 10, 15 — running totals of 1, 2, 3, 4, 5.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Find the 6th triangular number.
**Answer:** 21 — 6 × 7/2 = 21.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Find the 5th cube number.
**Answer:** 125 — 5³ = 125.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Which family is 1, 8, 27, 64, 125, …?
**Answer:** The cube numbers — each term is n³.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Find the harmonic mean of 6 and 12.
**Answer:** 8 — 2 × 6 × 12/(6 + 12) = 144/18 = 8; check: 1/6, 1/8, 1/12 has gaps −1/24, −1/24.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Find the 6th term of the harmonic progression 1/2, 1/5, 1/8, …
**Answer:** 1/17 — flip to the A.P. 2, 5, 8, …; its 6th term is 2 + 5 × 3 = 17; flip back.

**7. (Sub lesson 2)** Insert one harmonic mean between 3 and 6.
**Answer:** 4 — 2 × 3 × 6/(3 + 6) = 36/9 = 4; 1/3, 1/4, 1/6 has gaps −1/12, −1/12.

**8. (Sub lesson 3)** Write the first four terms of the A.G.P. made from A.P. 2, 5, 8, 11, … and G.P. 1, 1/3, 1/9, 1/27, …
**Answer:** 2, 5/3, 8/9, 11/27 — multiply the two ladders term by term.
