# Special Sequences — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the missing word or number.

**1. (Level 1)** The nth triangular number is n(n + ______)/2.
**Answer:** 1 — two staircases make an n by (n + 1) rectangle.

**2. (Level 1)** The 7th square number is ______.
**Answer:** 49 — 7² = 49.

**3. (Level 2)** A progression is harmonic when its ______ form an arithmetic progression.
**Answer:** reciprocals — flip, do A.P. work, flip back (Pakistan, Class 11, Unit 6).

**4. (Level 2)** The harmonic mean of a and b is 2ab/(a + ______).
**Answer:** b — the denominator is the sum a + b.

**5. (Level 3)** An arithmetico-geometric progression is an A.P. ______ a G.P., term by term.
**Answer:** multiplied by (times) — each term is an A.P. term times the matching G.P. term.

**6. (Level 3)** The sum to infinity of an A.G.P. is finite only when the G.P. ratio r obeys |r| < ______.
**Answer:** 1 — otherwise the tail never dies away.
