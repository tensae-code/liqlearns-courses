# Special Sequences — fill in the blank

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Square, Cube and Triangular Numbers · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Harmonic Progressions and the Harmonic Mean · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Arithmetico-Geometric Progressions.

Fill each blank with the missing word or number.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The nth triangular number is n(n + ______)/2.
**Answer:** 1 — two staircases make an n by (n + 1) rectangle.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The 7th square number is ______.
**Answer:** 49 — 7² = 49.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A progression is harmonic when its ______ form an arithmetic progression.
**Answer:** reciprocals — flip, do A.P. work, flip back (Pakistan, Class 11, Unit 6).

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The harmonic mean of a and b is 2ab/(a + ______).
**Answer:** b — the denominator is the sum a + b.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** An arithmetico-geometric progression is an A.P. ______ a G.P., term by term.
**Answer:** multiplied by (times) — each term is an A.P. term times the matching G.P. term.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** The sum to infinity of an A.G.P. is finite only when the G.P. ratio r obeys |r| < ______.
**Answer:** 1 — otherwise the tail never dies away.
