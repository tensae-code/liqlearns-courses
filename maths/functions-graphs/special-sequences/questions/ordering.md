# Special Sequences — ordering

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Square, Cube and Triangular Numbers · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Harmonic Progressions and the Harmonic Mean · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Arithmetico-Geometric Progressions.

Put each list in order, or continue it in order.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Continue in order: 1, 4, 9, 16, ______, ______.
**Answer:** 25, 36 — the square numbers 5² and 6².

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Put in increasing order: 27, 1, 64, 8, 125.
**Answer:** 1, 8, 27, 64, 125 — the cube numbers in their own order.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Continue in order: 1, 3, 6, 10, ______, ______.
**Answer:** 15, 21 — add 5, then add 6: the triangular numbers.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Continue the harmonic progression in order: 1, 1/2, 1/3, ______, ______.
**Answer:** 1/4, 1/5 — the reciprocals continue the A.P. 1, 2, 3, 4, 5.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Order the three means of 4 and 12 from biggest to smallest: harmonic mean, arithmetic mean, geometric mean.
**Answer:** Arithmetic mean 8, geometric mean √48 ≈ 6.93, harmonic mean 6 — biggest to smallest: arithmetic, geometric, harmonic.
