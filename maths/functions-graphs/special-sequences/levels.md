# Special Sequences — questions by level

Practice by level, tied to the skill number. The questions live in
the type files; this file is the by-level view. The levels are the
depths inside this one thing — special sequences — the pictured families first, then harmonic progressions, then arithmetico-geometric progressions, the order the books teach them in.

## Level 1 — square, cube and triangular numbers
Level 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: continue each pictured family, state its rule, and find a named term (for example the 6th triangular number).

## Level 2 — harmonic progressions and the harmonic mean
Level 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: flip a harmonic progression into its A.P., find an nth term and a harmonic mean, and insert a harmonic mean between two numbers.

## Level 3 — arithmetico-geometric progressions
Level 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: spot the A.P. part and the G.P. part of an arithmetico-geometric progression, and find its sum to infinity by the multiply-and-subtract method.
