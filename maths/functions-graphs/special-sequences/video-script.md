# Video script — Special Sequences

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Stack the counters. One. Then two under it. Then three.
Count all: **1, 3, 6, 10**. Triangle numbers!

Lay them flat in a square: 1, 4, 9, 16. **Square numbers.**
Build them up in a cube: 1, 8, 27, 64. **Cube numbers.**

Now a ladder: 1, 2, 3, 4.
Flip every rung upside down: 1, one-half, one-third, one-quarter.
That flipped ladder is a **harmonic** progression.
To work with it, flip it, do the easy ladder work, flip it back.

And when a ladder is two ladders multiplied —
the counting ladder times the doubling ladder —
that is **arithmetico-geometric**.

Every family has a rule. Find the rule, and the sequence is yours.
