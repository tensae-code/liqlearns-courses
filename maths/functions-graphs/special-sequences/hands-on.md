# Hands-on — Special Sequences

The lesson built the families with counters first, the way Sierra Leone's Primary 6 plans teach them (teacher evidence).

## Triangle stacks
Make rows of counters: a row of 1, a row of 2 under it, a row of 3, a row of 4. After each row, count the whole stack and write the running total: 1, 3, 6, 10. Add a row of 5 and predict the total before you count it. Then check: the nth triangular number is n(n + 1)/2 — test it at n = 5.

## Squares and cubes on the table
Lay out square arrays 1, 4, 9, 16 with counters and write the gaps between them: 3, 5, 7 — the odd numbers. Then stack blocks into cubes 1, 8, 27 and say each side length aloud. Which family grows faster after 10 terms? List both to the 6th term and look.

## Flip the ladder
Write the A.P. 2, 5, 8, 11 on card. Under each term write its reciprocal. You have built a harmonic progression: 1/2, 1/5, 1/8, 1/11. Now hide the top line, take only the bottom line, and recover the 5th term of the harmonic progression by flipping, extending the A.P., and flipping back.

## Two ladders multiplied
Write the A.P. 1, 2, 3, 4 across the top and the G.P. 1, 1/2, 1/4, 1/8 under it. Multiply each pair and add the products one at a time, keeping a running total: 1, 2, 2.75, 3.25 … The running total creeps towards 4 and never passes it — you are watching a sum to infinity arrive.
