**Acceptance count: taught in 4 countries (Pakistan, Sierra Leone, Syria, Malaysia).** Pakistan carries the two advanced families in full in its Class 11 sequences unit — arithmetico-geometric progressions with sums to n and to infinity, and harmonic progressions with the harmonic mean and inserted harmonic means, all inside Unit 6 (printed pp. 88–122), beyond the unit's A.P./G.P. core which lives in sequences-and-series. Sierra Leone carries the three pictured families — square numbers, cube numbers and triangular numbers, each taught as a sequence lesson followed by a rule lesson — in its Primary 6 Term 2 lesson plans (Lessons 116–120, printed pp. 143–151); this is teacher evidence, worded as such, because Sierra Leone's Primary set holds lesson plans and no pupil book. The catch-up plan's Mauritania row for this lesson ("harmonic and arithmetico-geometric sequences as filed") has no page-level filing for those families in maths/books/mauritania.md — its senior books file number sequences as a chapter topic only — so Mauritania is not cited here.

Syria solves the arithmetico-geometric recurrence in general in Grade 12 Part One, inside its sequences unit.

Malaysia meets the named sequences inside its Form 2 patterns chapter — Fibonacci from sunflowers and rabbits, triangular numbers, and Pascal's triangle built by hand.
