# Video script — Step and Piecewise Functions

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Some functions climb smooth. These ones JUMP!

Walk up stairs! Flat… flat… JUMP! Flat… JUMP!
That is the **floor function**! It drops you to the whole number below.
2.7? Down to **2**! −1.2? Down to **−2**! Always DOWN!

What is left over? The little piece!
That is the **fractional part** — always between 0 and 1!

And the **sign function**? It only asks one question:
plus, minus, or zero? **1, −1, or 0**. That is all it says!

A taxi charges in steps. A letter costs in steps.
Different rule here, different rule there?
That is **piecewise** — pick the rule that matches!

And the V! |x| folds the line at zero and lifts it up!
Down to the point, then up again. A perfect **V**!

Now you. Drop a number to its floor. Find its little piece.
Read its sign. Pick the right rule!
