# Step and Piecewise Functions — matching

Match each item on the left with the one right item on the right.

**1. (Level 1)** [x] → **Answer:** the greatest integer not bigger than x — the floor (Iran, Grade 11, p.37).

**2. (Level 2)** {x} → **Answer:** the leftover x − [x], always in [0, 1) — the fractional part (Kazakhstan, Algebra 9, §21, p.24).

**3. (Level 2)** sgn(x) → **Answer:** 1, 0 or −1 by the sign of x (Laos, Grade 10, p.83).

**4. (Level 2)** A postage table → **Answer:** a step function in prices — flat in each band, jumping at the edges (Iran, Grade 11 Humanities, p.34).

**5. (Level 3)** f(x) = |x + 1| + 2 → **Answer:** a V with vertex (−1, 2) (Kuwait, Grade 10 Term 1, pp.16–32).

Right-hand items in full: the greatest integer not bigger than x · the leftover x − [x], always in [0, 1) · 1, 0 or −1 by the sign of x · a step function in prices · a V with vertex (−1, 2).
