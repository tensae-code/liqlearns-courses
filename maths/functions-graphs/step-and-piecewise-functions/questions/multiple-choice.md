# Step and Piecewise Functions — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — The Floor Function and Its Staircase · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Fractional Part, Sign and Stepped Charges · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Piecewise Rules and the Absolute-Value Graph.

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** [3.7] = —
a) 4  b) 3  c) 3.7
**Answer:** b) 3 — the greatest integer not bigger than 3.7.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** [−2.3] = —
a) −2  b) −3  c) −2.3
**Answer:** b) −3 — the floor walks DOWN; −2 is bigger than −2.3, so it cannot be the floor.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** On the staircase graph of [x], at a whole number the step's value sits at —
a) an open dot  b) a filled dot at the left end of the step  c) the middle of the step
**Answer:** b) a filled dot at the left end of the step — the step holds its left end's value all the way to the next whole number (Nicaragua, Grade 10, pp.269–271).

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** {5.25} = —
a) 5  b) 0.25  c) −0.75
**Answer:** b) 0.25 — {x} = x − [x] = 5.25 − 5.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** sgn(−0.001) = —
a) 0  b) 1  c) −1
**Answer:** c) −1 — any negative number, however small, has sign −1 (Laos, Grade 10, p.83).

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** A stepped tariff charges 5 up to 1 kg, 8 over 1 kg to 2 kg, 11 over 2 kg to 3 kg. A 2 kg parcel costs —
a) 11  b) 8  c) 5
**Answer:** b) 8 — 2 kg is the top of the second step; the jump to 11 happens just past 2 kg.

**7. (Sub lesson 3)** f(x) = 2x for x < 1 and f(x) = x + 4 for x ≥ 1. f(1) = —
a) 2  b) 5  c) both rules agree
**Answer:** b) 5 — 1 belongs to the x ≥ 1 side, so the second rule runs.

**8. (Sub lesson 3)** The vertex of f(x) = |x + 1| + 2 is —
a) (1, 2)  b) (−1, 2)  c) (−1, −2)
**Answer:** b) (−1, 2) — the vertex is where the inside is zero, x = −1, at height 2 (Kuwait, Grade 10 Term 1, pp.16–32).
