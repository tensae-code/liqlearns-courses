# Step and Piecewise Functions — true or false

Say true or false, and why in one line.

**1. (Level 1)** [7] = 7. — **True.** A whole number is its own floor.

**2. (Level 1)** [−3.1] = −3. — **False.** −3 is bigger than −3.1; the floor is −4.

**3. (Level 2)** {x} can equal 1 for some x. — **False.** {x} < 1 always; at the moment it would reach 1, [x] jumps and {x} drops to 0.

**4. (Level 2)** sgn(x) is a step function. — **True.** Its graph is three flat pieces with jumps at 0 (Laos, Grade 10, p.83).

**5. (Level 3)** A piecewise function is really two different functions, not one. — **False.** It is one function with different rules on different parts of its domain (Iran, Hesaban (1), pp.44–53).

**6. (Level 3)** The graph of f(x) = |ax + b| + c opens downward. — **False.** It is a V opening upward, with its vertex where the inside is zero (Kuwait, Grade 10 Term 1, pp.16–32).
