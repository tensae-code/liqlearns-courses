# Function Families: Graphs with a Parameter — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, expressed as its SUB
LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure — README, "Units, Lessons and
Sub Lessons"). One lesson, one level (Level 172). The lesson
was built new in the expansion era (Germany expansion merge,
2026-11-11), so there are no old depth layers to map: the sub
lessons below are its depths as built, cut at the founding
sections' own shape — the family and its parameter first,
then the investigation of the family in terms of the
parameter, then the locus curve its extreme points trace.
The questions live in the type files; this file is the
by-sub-lesson view.

Pass marks: the founding sections name their topic
(Funktionenscharen — families of functions — and Ortskurven,
locus curves) and state no pass mark, so no mark is invented
— each sub lesson's pass mark is stated as the observable
thing the learner can do.

## Sub lesson 1 — One Formula, Many Graphs
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: say what a parameter is (a letter held fixed for one graph and varied across the family), write out members of f_t(x) = x² − 2tx + 1 for named values of t, and recognise each member as the same parabola shape shifted.

## Sub lesson 2 — Investigating a Family
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: compute a family's zeros and extreme point in terms of t — for f_t, the vertex (t, 1 − t²) and the discriminant condition 4t² − 4 — and split the answer into cases (|t| > 1 two zeros, t = ±1 one touching zero, |t| < 1 none).

## Sub lesson 3 — Locus Curves
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: find a locus curve by the elimination method — special point's coordinates as functions of t, then t eliminated — for the vertices of f_t (the locus y = 1 − x²) and of h_t(x) = (x − t)² + t (the locus y = x).

## What "maxed" means here

The learner handles a family the way Germany's Grade 11 and
Grade 12 books teach it: one formula read as many graphs, the
investigation carried out in terms of the parameter with its
cases split honestly, and the extreme points gathered onto
their locus curve by eliminating the parameter (Lambacher
Schweizer *Mathematik 11*, Ausgabe Bayern, Kapitel V,
Exkursion, printed p. 181; *Mathematik 12*, Ausgabe Bayern,
Kapitel I, printed p. 6).
