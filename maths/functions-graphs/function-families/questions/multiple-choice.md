# Multiple choice — Function Families: Graphs with a Parameter

Sub-lesson markers: every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to — Sub lesson 1 — One Formula, Many Graphs · Sub lesson 2 — Investigating a Family · Sub lesson 3 — Locus Curves. This lesson was built new with its sub lessons (Germany expansion merge, 2026-11-11), so its markers are original — there are no older depth layers to map from.

1. (Sub lesson 1) In f_t(x) = x² − 2tx + 1, the letter t is:
   (a) a second variable that runs along the graph
   (b) a parameter — fixed for one graph, varied across the family ✔
   (c) always equal to 0
   (d) the y-intercept of every member
   **Answer: (b)** — x is the variable; t picks the member. Item constructed for this lesson from its founding teaching. 2. (Sub lesson 1) The member f_2 of the family f_t(x) = x² − 2tx + 1 is:
   (a) x² − 2x + 1
   (b) x² + 4x + 1
   (c) x² − 4x + 1 ✔
   (d) 2x² − 2x + 1
   **Answer: (c)** — set t = 2: the term −2tx becomes −4x. Item constructed for this lesson from its founding teaching. 3. (Sub lesson 2) The family f_t(x) = x² − 2tx + 1 has two distinct zeros exactly when:
   (a) |t| < 1
   (b) t = ±1
   (c) |t| > 1 ✔
   (d) never
   **Answer: (c)** — the discriminant 4t² − 4 is positive exactly when |t| > 1. Item constructed for this lesson from its founding teaching. 4. (Sub lesson 3) The vertices of all members of f_t(x) = x² − 2tx + 1 lie on the locus curve:
   (a) y = x
   (b) y = 1 − x² ✔
   (c) y = x² − 1
   (d) y = −2x³
   **Answer: (b)** — the vertex is (t, 1 − t²); eliminating t gives y = 1 − x². Item constructed for this lesson from its founding teaching.
