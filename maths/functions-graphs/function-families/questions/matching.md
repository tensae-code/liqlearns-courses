# Matching — Function Families: Graphs with a Parameter

Sub-lesson markers: every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to — Sub lesson 1 — One Formula, Many Graphs · Sub lesson 2 — Investigating a Family · Sub lesson 3 — Locus Curves. This lesson was built new with its sub lessons (Germany expansion merge, 2026-11-11), so its markers are original — there are no older depth layers to map from.

1. (Sub lesson 2) Match each statement about f_t(x) = x² − 2tx + 1 to the values of t it belongs to.
   - The parabola touches the x-axis in one point → t = 1 or t = −1
   - The parabola has no zero → |t| < 1
   - The parabola cuts the x-axis twice → |t| > 1
   **Answer:** touches ↔ t = ±1; no zero ↔ |t| < 1; cuts twice ↔ |t| > 1. Item constructed for this lesson from its founding teaching. Germany, *Lambacher Schweizer Mathematik 11, Ausgabe Bayern* (student book, Grade 11), Kapitel V, Exkursion (Funktionenscharen und Ortskurven), printed p. 181; *Lambacher Schweizer Mathematik 12, Ausgabe Bayern* (student book, Grade 12), Kapitel I, printed p. 6.

2. (Sub lesson 3) Match each term to its meaning.
   - Ortskurve → the German name for the locus curve of a family's special points
   - Funktionenschar → the German name for a family of functions
   - The vertex's x-coordinate in f_t(x) = x² − 2tx + 1 → equal to the parameter t itself
   **Answer:** Ortskurve ↔ locus curve; Funktionenschar ↔ family of functions; vertex x-coordinate ↔ t. Item constructed for this lesson from its founding teaching. Germany, *Lambacher Schweizer Mathematik 11, Ausgabe Bayern* (student book, Grade 11), Kapitel V, Exkursion (Funktionenscharen und Ortskurven), printed p. 181; *Lambacher Schweizer Mathematik 12, Ausgabe Bayern* (student book, Grade 12), Kapitel I, printed p. 6.
