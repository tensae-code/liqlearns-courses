# Fill in the blank — Function Families: Graphs with a Parameter

Sub-lesson markers: every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to — Sub lesson 1 — One Formula, Many Graphs · Sub lesson 2 — Investigating a Family · Sub lesson 3 — Locus Curves. This lesson was built new with its sub lessons (Germany expansion merge, 2026-11-11), so its markers are original — there are no older depth layers to map from.

1. (Sub lesson 1) The member for t = 3 of the family f_t(x) = x² − 2tx + 1 is f_3(x) = x² − ______x + 1. **Answer: 6** — the term −2tx becomes −6x. 2. (Sub lesson 2) The vertex of f_t(x) = x² − 2tx + 1 sits at x = ______. **Answer: t** — from f_t′(x) = 2x − 2t = 0, or from the completed square (x − t)² + 1 − t². 3. (Sub lesson 3) The family h_t(x) = (x − t)² + t has vertex (t, t), so its locus curve is the line y = ______. **Answer: x**. Item constructed for this lesson from its founding teaching. 4. (Sub lesson 3) For the cubic family f_t(x) = x³ − 3tx with t > 0, both extreme points lie on the locus y = ______. **Answer: −2x³** — the maximum and minimum branches land on the same cubic.
