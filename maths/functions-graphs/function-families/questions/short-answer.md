# Short answer — Function Families: Graphs with a Parameter

Sub-lesson markers: every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to — Sub lesson 1 — One Formula, Many Graphs · Sub lesson 2 — Investigating a Family · Sub lesson 3 — Locus Curves. This lesson was built new with its sub lessons (Germany expansion merge, 2026-11-11), so its markers are original — there are no older depth layers to map from.

1. (Sub lesson 1) Write out the members f_0, f_1 and f_−1 of the family f_t(x) = x² − 2tx + 1, in factored form where possible, and say how their graphs differ.
   **Answer:** f_0(x) = x² + 1 (vertex (0, 1), no zeros); f_1(x) = x² − 2x + 1 = (x − 1)² (vertex (1, 0), touching the axis); f_−1(x) = x² + 2x + 1 = (x + 1)² (vertex (−1, 0), touching the axis). Same parabola shape; the parameter slides the vertex along. Item constructed for this lesson from its founding teaching. 2. (Sub lesson 2) For which values of t does f_t(x) = x² − 2tx + 1 touch the x-axis, and where?
   **Answer:** the discriminant 4t² − 4 is zero for t = 1 and t = −1; the touching point is the vertex, (1, 0) for t = 1 and (−1, 0) for t = −1. 3. (Sub lesson 3) Find the locus curve of the vertices of f_t(x) = x² − 2tx + 1, showing the elimination.
   **Answer:** completing the square gives the vertex (t, 1 − t²). Set X = t and Y = 1 − t²; since t = X, Y = 1 − X². The locus curve is y = 1 − x².
