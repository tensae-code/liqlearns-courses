# Ordering — Function Families: Graphs with a Parameter

Sub-lesson markers: every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to — Sub lesson 1 — One Formula, Many Graphs · Sub lesson 2 — Investigating a Family · Sub lesson 3 — Locus Curves. This lesson was built new with its sub lessons (Germany expansion merge, 2026-11-11), so its markers are original — there are no older depth layers to map from.

1. (Sub lesson 3) Put the steps of finding a locus curve in order.
   - Eliminate the parameter between the two equations
   - Find the special point's coordinates in terms of t
   - State the locus equation in x and y
   - Write the point's coordinates as X(t) and Y(t)
   **Answer:** find the special point's coordinates in terms of t → write them as X(t) and Y(t) → eliminate the parameter → state the locus equation. 2. (Sub lesson 2) Put the steps of investigating a family's zeros in order.
   - Split the answer into cases on t
   - Set the family member equal to zero
   - Read the discriminant's sign for each case
   - Compute the discriminant in terms of t
   **Answer:** set the member equal to zero → compute the discriminant in terms of t → read its sign for each case → split the answer into cases on t.
