# Video script — Function Families: Graphs with a Parameter

Super-simple voice. The tutor talks; one parabola is on
screen with a slider for the parameter, and the vertices
leave a dotted trail as the slider moves.

---

One formula. But look — it has an extra letter. t. That
letter is not x. x runs along the graph. t picks which graph
we get.

Watch the slider. t is zero: a parabola sitting high. t is
one: it slid right and down, and it kisses the ground. t is
two: it slid further, and now it cuts the ground twice. One
formula — a whole family of graphs.

Now watch the top... the bottom point, the vertex. As the
slider moves, the vertex moves too. It leaves a trail. And
the trail is a curve of its own! An upside-down parabola.
Every vertex of the family lives on that one curve. That
trail is called the locus curve.

So: the parameter makes the family. The investigation tells
the family's story. And the special points line up — on the
locus.
