# Hands-on — Coordinates and graphs

## Treasure map
Draw a coordinate plane on big paper. Hide a small object in
the room and give its position as coordinates ("3 right, 2
up from the door"). A partner finds it from the coordinates
alone. Swap: they hide, you find. Coordinates are directions
you can trust.

## The distance walk
Mark two points on a field with flags. Pace the distance
between them in steps, then work it out from the coordinates
you gave the flags. Pythagoras on the plane, checked by
walking.

## Gradient steps
Draw lines with gradients 1/2, 1, 2, −1 and 0 on squared
paper. Walk each one with your finger: up the rise, across
the run, saying the steps aloud. Then draw a line and ask a
partner to read its gradient from the steps alone.

## The line game
One person secretly picks m and c and draws y = mx + c. The
other reads the gradient and the y-intercept off the graph
and says the equation. Three correct guesses in a row wins —
then swap.
