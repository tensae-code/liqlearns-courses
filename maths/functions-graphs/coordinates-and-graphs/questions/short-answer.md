# Coordinates and graphs — short answer

Answer in a word or a short sentence.

**1. (Level 1)** What are the coordinates of the origin?
**Answer:** (0, 0).

**2. (Level 1)** Which quadrant is the point (−3, 2) in?
**Answer:** The second quadrant (top left).

**3. (Level 1)** Find the midpoint of (0, 2) and (4, 6).
**Answer:** (2, 4).

**4. (Level 2)** What is the gradient of y = 5x + 3?
**Answer:** 5.

**5. (Level 2)** A line rises 4 for every 2 it runs. What is its gradient?
**Answer:** 2 (4/2).

**6. (Level 2)** What is the x-intercept of y = x − 4?
**Answer:** 4 (put y = 0: x = 4).

**7. (Level 3)** Write the equation of the line with gradient −1 and y-intercept 5.
**Answer:** y = −x + 5.

**8. (Level 3)** Two lines have gradients 2 and −1/2. Are they parallel, perpendicular, or neither?
**Answer:** Perpendicular (2 × −1/2 = −1).
