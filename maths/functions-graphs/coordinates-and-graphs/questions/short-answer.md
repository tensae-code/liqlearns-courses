# Coordinates and graphs — short answer

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Plotting, Distance and Midpoint · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Gradient and Intercepts · (Level 3) splits by what the item asks: items about parallel or perpendicular gradients, and reading an intercept off a given equation, -> Sub lesson 2, where the gradient part teaches those rules; items about writing a line's equation -> Sub lesson 3 — The Equation y = mx + c.

Answer in a word or a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What are the coordinates of the origin?
**Answer:** (0, 0).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which quadrant is the point (−3, 2) in?
**Answer:** The second quadrant (top left).

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Find the midpoint of (0, 2) and (4, 6).
**Answer:** (2, 4).

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** What is the gradient of y = 5x + 3?
**Answer:** 5.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** A line rises 4 for every 2 it runs. What is its gradient?
**Answer:** 2 (4/2).

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** What is the x-intercept of y = x − 4?
**Answer:** 4 (put y = 0: x = 4).

**7. (Sub lesson 3)** Write the equation of the line with gradient −1 and y-intercept 5.
**Answer:** y = −x + 5.

**8. (Sub lesson 2)** Two lines have gradients 2 and −1/2. Are they parallel, perpendicular, or neither?
**Answer:** Perpendicular (2 × −1/2 = −1).
