# Coordinates and graphs — fill in the blank

Put the missing word or number in the space.

**1. (Level 1)** In (3, 2), the first number is the ______-coordinate.
**Answer:** x.

**2. (Level 1)** The point where the axes cross is the ______.
**Answer:** origin.

**3. (Level 1)** The midpoint of (x₁, y₁) and (x₂, y₂) is ((x₁ + x₂)/2, (y₁ + y₂)/______).
**Answer:** 2.

**4. (Level 2)** Gradient = change in y / change in ______.
**Answer:** x.

**5. (Level 2)** A line that rises to the right has a ______ gradient.
**Answer:** positive.

**6. (Level 2)** The y-intercept is where the line crosses the y-axis, at x = ______.
**Answer:** 0.

**7. (Level 3)** In y = mx + c, m is the ______ and c is the y-intercept.
**Answer:** gradient.

**8. (Level 3)** Parallel lines have ______ gradients.
**Answer:** equal.
