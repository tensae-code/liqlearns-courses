# Coordinates and graphs — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Level 1)** The point (3, 2) is —
a) 2 right, 3 up  b) 3 right, 2 up  c) 3 up, 2 right
**Answer: b) 3 right, 2 up** — x first, then y.

**2. (Level 1)** The distance between (0, 0) and (6, 8) is —
a) 10  b) 14  c) 48
**Answer: a) 10** — √(6² + 8²) = √100 = 10.

**3. (Level 1)** The midpoint of (2, 4) and (6, 10) is —
a) (4, 7)  b) (8, 14)  c) (3, 5)
**Answer: a) (4, 7)** — ((2+6)/2, (4+10)/2).

**4. (Level 2)** The gradient of the line through (1, 2) and (4, 8) is —
a) 2  b) 3  c) 1/2
**Answer: a) 2** — (8 − 2)/(4 − 1) = 6/3 = 2.

**5. (Level 2)** A line with gradient −3 —
a) rises to the right  b) falls to the right  c) is flat
**Answer: b) falls to the right.**

**6. (Level 2)** The y-intercept of y = 2x + 1 is —
a) 2  b) 1  c) 0
**Answer: b) 1.**

**7. (Level 3)** The line y = 3x − 2 crosses the x-axis at —
a) x = 2/3  b) x = −2  c) x = 3
**Answer: a) x = 2/3** — put y = 0: 0 = 3x − 2, so x = 2/3.

**8. (Level 3)** Which line is parallel to y = 2x + 1?
a) y = 2x − 5  b) y = −2x + 1  c) y = (1/2)x + 1
**Answer: a) y = 2x − 5** — parallel lines have equal gradients.
