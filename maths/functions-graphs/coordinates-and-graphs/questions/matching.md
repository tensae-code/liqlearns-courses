# Coordinates and graphs — matching

Join the two sides that go together.

**1. (Level 1)** Match each point to its quadrant.
- (3, 2) — a) second quadrant
- (−3, 2) — b) fourth quadrant
- (3, −2) — c) first quadrant
- (−3, −2) — d) third quadrant
**Answer:** (3, 2)–c, (−3, 2)–a, (3, −2)–b, (−3, −2)–d.

**2. (Level 2)** Match each line to its gradient.
- y = 2x + 1 — a) −3
- y = −3x + 4 — b) 2
- y = (1/2)x − 1 — c) 1/2
**Answer:** y = 2x + 1–b, y = −3x + 4–a, y = (1/2)x − 1–c.

**3. (Level 3)** Match each pair of lines to their relationship.
- y = 2x + 1 and y = 2x + 5 — a) perpendicular
- y = 2x + 1 and y = −(1/2)x + 1 — b) parallel
- y = 2x + 1 and y = 3x + 1 — c) neither (they meet at an angle)
**Answer:** 2x pair–b, 2x and −1/2x–a, 2x and 3x–c.
