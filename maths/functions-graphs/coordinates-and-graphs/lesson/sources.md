Laos carries coordinates and graphs from broken-line graphs in Grade 4, through step and line graphs and coordinate enlargement in Grade 5, to ordered pairs, proportion graphs and full straight-line work — slope, parallels, perpendiculars and point-to-line distance — in Grades 7–8. Yemen carries coordinates and graphs from the coordinate system in Grade 7, through axis-parallel then general distance and midpoint, to slope and the equation of a line and the equation of the circle. Indonesia carries coordinates and graphs from grid locations and (B,3) notation in Grade 6, through Cartesian coordinates and straight-line equations — graphing, gradient, parallel and perpendicular lines — in Grade 8, to the equation of the circle in Grade 12 Tingkat Lanjut (2022 book and the 2025 Revisi edition); its Grade 8 Merdeka straight-line chapter is now read in the student book itself (Bab 5, printed pp. 207–254). Nicaragua carries coordinates and graphs from direct proportion as y = ax with its graph in Grade 6, through the function concept and direct/inverse proportion graphs in Grade 7 and the full first-degree function — slope, intercepts, domain and range — in Grade 8, to the Cartesian plane and the parabola y = x² in Grade 9 and the full analytic-geometry course — distance, midpoint, section formula, every form of the line, parallel/perpendicular conditions and the circle — in Grade 11. Ethiopia carries coordinates and graphs from plotting points only in Grade 6, through the Cartesian system and graphs of linear equations in Grade 7 and slope, intercepts and graphs of linear inequalities in Grade 8, to graphing relations and inequalities and coordinate geometry — distance, midpoint, slope, every form of the line, parallel and perpendicular lines, and the equation of a circle — in Grade 10; its Grade 10 guide has no teaching notes or answers for the circle-equation section, so circle equations rest on the student book. South Sudan carries coordinates from x and y axes, scale and co-ordinates with ordered pairs in Primary 6, through ordered pairs, negative values and the four quadrants in Primary 8 and coordinate geometry — plotting points, the equation of a line and gradient — in Secondary 2, to graphs of trigonometric functions in Secondary 4 Mathematics; its Primary 6 book sets no plotting exercises and its Primary 8 book sets no exercises on coordinates, and the trig graphs come from the regular Mathematics book, not the Additional book. Iraq carries coordinates and graphs from plotting points and quadrants in Grade 6, through the coordinate plane in Grades 7–8 and linear functions with their graphs in Grade 8, to full coordinate geometry — slope, the equation of the line, parallel and perpendicular lines, distance and midpoint — in Grade 9 and in both Grade 10 tracks, and the circle in coordinates in Grade 11 Scientific. Bangladesh carries coordinates and graphs from the number line in Class 6, through Cartesian coordinates and quadrants with distance, midpoint, slope and the equations of straight lines in Class 8, to plotting points and the graphs of linear, quadratic and cubic polynomials in Class 9; the straight-line equation rests on Class 8, since the Class 9 book itself teaches no straight-line equation — with two corrections taught, never as printed: the English-only p. 85 call of the vertical line the x-axis (it is the y-axis; the Bangla is correct), and the English-only p. 86 instruction to join polynomial-graph points with a straight line (join them with a smooth curve; the Bangla is correct). Nepal carries coordinates and graphs from ordered pairs in Grade 4, through reading points in Grade 5 and coordinate geometry — axes, quadrants, distance and midpoint — in Grades 6–8, to straight-line equations in Grade 9 Optional and lines, the angle between lines and circle equations in Grade 10 Optional; the compulsory Grade 9 and Grade 10 books teach no coordinate geometry, and the Grade 8 coordinate exercises are taught corrected — the P(1,6), Q(4,1), R(−4,3) triangle is isosceles, not scalene, and (24,3), (0,2), (−2,−1) are not collinear.
