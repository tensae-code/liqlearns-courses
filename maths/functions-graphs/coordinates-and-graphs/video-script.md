# Video script — Coordinates and graphs

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Two lines cross. Flat is x. Up is y. Where they meet is zero,
zero — the origin!

Walk along first, then climb up. Three along, two up —
that is (3, 2)! x first, then y. Always!

Now a line. y equals two x plus one.
x is zero, y is one. x is one, y is three. x is two, y is five.
Plot them — they sit in a straight line!

How steep? Up two, across one. The gradient is two!
Where does it cross the y-axis? At one. That is the intercept!

Every straight line is y equals m x plus c.
m is the steepness. c is where it crosses.
Now you. Plot your points. Find your gradient.
Write your line!
