# Video script — Polar Coordinates

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Pin a string at the corner. Swing it to the point.

Two things name the point now.
**How long** is the string? That is **r**.
**How far** did it turn? That is **θ** — the angle.

So the point is (r, θ). Length first. Turn second.

To go back to x and y:
x is r times cos θ. y is r times sin θ.

To come the other way:
r is the square root of x squared plus y squared.
And the angle? Look first — which corner of the page is the point in?
The angle must point there, truly there.

One point, many polar names — turn all the way round, and you are back.
That is polar coordinates.
