# Polar Coordinates — fill in the blank

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — The Polar Name (r, θ) · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Polar to Cartesian · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Cartesian to Polar and the Many Names of a Point.

Fill each blank with the missing word or number.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In (r, θ), the letter r stands for the ______ of the point from the origin.
**Answer:** distance — r is measured from the origin to the point.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** x = r cos θ and y = r ______ θ.
**Answer:** sin — the vertical leg of the triangle is r sin θ.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** For (6, 30°), x = 6 cos 30° = ______.
**Answer:** 3√3 — cos 30° = √3/2, so x = 6 × √3/2 = 3√3.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** r = √(x² + ______) .
**Answer:** y² — Pythagoras on the legs x and y gives the hypotenuse r.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** The corrected distance for the point (−√3, 1) is r = ______, not √5.
**Answer:** 2 — √((−√3)² + 1²) = √4 = 2 (Bangladesh, p. 174, corrected).

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** Kuwait's conditions for the standard trigonometric form are r ≥ 0 and the argument in [0, ______).
**Answer:** 2π — one full turn, in radians (Kuwait, 7-2, pp. 25–32).
