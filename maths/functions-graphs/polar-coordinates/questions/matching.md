Match each polar name on the left with the same point in Cartesian form on the right.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** (2, 0°)
**Answer:** (2, 0) — on the positive x-axis, no turn.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** (2, 180°)
**Answer:** (−2, 0) — a half turn points along the negative x-axis.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** (4, 90°)
**Answer:** (0, 4) — a quarter turn points straight up the y-axis.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** (2, 150°)
**Answer:** (−√3, 1) — x = 2 cos 150° = −√3, y = 2 sin 150° = 1: the corrected Bangladesh point.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** (√6, 135°)
**Answer:** (−√3, √3) — Kuwait's third point in 7-2 Example 1 (p. 26), converted.
