# Polar Coordinates — short answer

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Level 1)** A point is 7 units from the origin at an angle of 35°. Write its polar name.
**Answer:** (7, 35°) — distance first, angle second.

**2. (Level 1)** What does θ = 0° point along?
**Answer:** The positive x-axis — no turn has been made.

**3. (Level 2)** Convert (10, 180°) to Cartesian coordinates.
**Answer:** (−10, 0) — x = 10 cos 180° = −10, y = 10 sin 180° = 0.

**4. (Level 2)** Convert (2, 45°) to Cartesian coordinates.
**Answer:** (√2, √2) — x = y = 2 × √2/2 = √2 (Kuwait's (√2, √2) point, 7-2 Example 1, p. 26, read backwards).

**5. (Level 3)** Convert (1, √3) to polar form, angle in [0, 2π).
**Answer:** (2, 60°) — r = √(1 + 3) = 2, first quadrant, tan θ = √3 gives θ = 60°.

**6. (Level 3)** Convert (√3, −1) to polar form, angle in [0, 2π).
**Answer:** (2, 330°) — r = 2, fourth quadrant, so θ = 360° − 30° = 330°.

**7. (Level 3)** Convert (−√3, √3) to polar form, angle in [0, 2π).
**Answer:** (√6, 135°) — r = √(3 + 3) = √6, second quadrant at 135°.

**8. (Level 3)** Convert (3, −4) to polar form, angle in [0°, 360°), to one decimal place.
**Answer:** (5, 306.9°) — r = 5, reference angle ≈ 53.1°, fourth quadrant: 360° − 53.1° = 306.9°.
