# Polar Coordinates — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Level 1)** In the polar name (r, θ), what is r?
a) The angle from the x-axis  b) The distance from the origin  c) The x-coordinate
**Answer:** b) The distance from the origin — r is the length from the origin to the point.

**2. (Level 1)** In the polar name (r, θ), θ is measured from…
a) the positive x-axis, turning anticlockwise  b) the y-axis  c) the point itself
**Answer:** a) the positive x-axis, turning anticlockwise — that is the standard position both books use.

**3. (Level 2)** The Cartesian form of (5, 240°) is…
a) (5/2, 5√3/2)  b) (−5/2, −5√3/2)  c) (−5√3/2, −5/2)
**Answer:** b) (−5/2, −5√3/2) — x = 5 cos 240° = −5/2, y = 5 sin 240° = −5√3/2 (Bangladesh, §10.2).

**4. (Level 2)** The Cartesian form of (4, 60°) is…
a) (2, 2√3)  b) (2√3, 2)  c) (4, 4)
**Answer:** a) (2, 2√3) — x = 4 cos 60° = 2, y = 4 sin 60° = 2√3.

**5. (Level 3)** The polar form of (−√3, 1), taught corrected, is…
a) (√5, 150°)  b) (2, 30°)  c) (2, 150°)
**Answer:** c) (2, 150°) — r = √(3 + 1) = 2, and the point is in the second quadrant, so θ = 150° (Bangladesh, §10.2 Example 1, p. 174, corrected).

**6. (Level 3)** The distance r of the point (3, −4) is…
a) 5  b) 7  c) 25
**Answer:** a) 5 — r = √(9 + 16) = √25 = 5 (Kuwait, 7-2 Example 1, p. 26).

**7. (Level 3)** Which of these is the same point as (2, 150°)?
a) (2, 510°)  b) (2, 60°)  c) (−2, 150°)
**Answer:** a) (2, 510°) — adding a full turn of 360° names the same point.

**8. (Level 2)** The point (2, 90°) in Cartesian form is…
a) (2, 0)  b) (0, 2)  c) (0, −2)
**Answer:** b) (0, 2) — x = 2 cos 90° = 0, y = 2 sin 90° = 2.
