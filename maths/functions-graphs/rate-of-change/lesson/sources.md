**Acceptance count: taught in 1 country (Sudan).** Sudan's Grade 8 book teaches rate of change as a complete pre-calculus topic in its ratio-and-rate unit (the lesson runs printed pp. 52–55, with the constant-rate lesson closing at printed p. 58): defined from a growing school's admissions, worked on a child's height growth, signed for falling quantities, and tested for constancy in tables. No other read country's filing gives the average rate of change a standalone treatment of its own — elsewhere it appears as the opening step of the derivatives lesson, where it also lives — so this lesson is built at the size of its one source, and the derivatives lesson carries Sudan's two Grade 12 treatments of the same doorway.
