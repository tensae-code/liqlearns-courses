**Acceptance count: taught in 2 countries (Sudan, India).** Sudan's Grade 8 book teaches rate of change as a complete pre-calculus topic in its ratio-and-rate unit (the lesson runs printed pp. 52–55, with the constant-rate lesson closing at printed p. 58): defined from a growing school's admissions, worked on a child's height growth, signed for falling quantities, and tested for constancy in tables. No other read country's filing gives the average rate of change a standalone treatment of its own — elsewhere it appears as the opening step of the derivatives lesson, where it also lives — so this lesson is built at the size of its one source, and the derivatives lesson carries Sudan's two Grade 12 treatments of the same doorway.

India enters rate of change from Mathematics Class 11–12: average and instantaneous rate; rate of change of a quantity y = f(x): dy/dx as (change in y)/(change in x); related-rates problems (area of circle, volume of cube, ripple circles, rectangle).
