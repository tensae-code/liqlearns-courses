# Lesson plan — Rate of Change

**One thing:** how fast one quantity changes as another quantity changes — the change in the first divided by the change in the second — including negative and zero rates, and testing a table to decide whether its rate of change is constant.

**Stage:** Functions, Graphs and Sequences (Stage 10). **Level 150** (Sudan merge, 2026-11-07).

**Why this lesson exists:** Sudan's Grade 8 book teaches rate of change as a complete school topic of its own — defined, worked with growth and temperature examples, and tested for constancy in tables — two to three years before any calculus. It had no home: the derivatives lesson opens with the average rate of change only as the doorway to the instantaneous rate, and no other lesson takes the table-tested, pre-calculus treatment. Under the everything-goes-in ruling (2026-11-06), it gets its own lesson. It is a soft hint before derivatives, never a gate: a learner who wants the derivative can start there directly.

## The climb

1. **What a rate of change is** — a quantity changes; divide its change by the change in the quantity it follows (per year, per hour, per minute). Sudan opens with school admissions growing year by year.
2. **Working a rate** — a height growing from 135 cm to 155 cm between the ages of 9 and 13 changes by 20 cm in 4 years: a rate of 5 cm per year.
3. **Negative and zero rates** — a falling temperature changes at −2° per hour; a quantity that does not change has rate zero.
4. **Constant rate of change** — a table's rate is constant when every step changes by the same amount: a typing table in lines per minute passes the test; the book's sugar price table fails it and is judged non-constant; workers against time close the lesson.

## Add-on slots

- slot-rate-0 (IMAGE): a picture of a child's height marked on a door frame at two ages — 135 cm at age 9 and 155 cm at age 13 — with the 20 cm growth band shaded. What the student does: reads off the change in height and the change in age before dividing.
- slot-rate-1 (GIF, 1–2 s): a strip showing a thermometer's reading dropping step by step, each step labelled −2°. What the student does: says the rate of change aloud as the steps land.
- slot-rate-2 (IMAGE): a picture of two small tables side by side — one where every step grows by the same amount, one where the steps differ — neither labelled. What the student does: works both tables and points to the constant one.
