# Hands-on — Rate of Change

## Activity 1 — Your own growth rate (10 minutes)

Find two height marks or records from different ages (a door frame, a clinic card, a school record). Work the rate of change: the change in height divided by the change in age, in centimetres per year. If the two records are your own, compare your rate with Sudan's worked example — 135 cm to 155 cm between ages 9 and 13, a rate of 5 cm per year.

## Activity 2 — A falling rate (10 minutes)

On a cold evening (or with a fridge thermometer), read the temperature at the start of an hour and again at the end. Work the rate of change in degrees per hour. If the temperature fell, say the rate aloud with its sign and say what the sign means. If it did not move at all, you have found a zero rate — say that too.

## Activity 3 — Constant or not? (10 minutes)

Time yourself copying a passage by hand (or typing) for three one-minute rounds, counting the lines each round. Build the table: lines after 1, 2 and 3 minutes. Test it the book's way — is the step the same every minute? Decide, and say the sentence in full: "The rate is constant" or "The rate is not constant, because the steps are …".
