# Levels — Rate of Change

One lesson, one level (Level 150). The depth layers inside it are ranks.

## Depth layers

- **Layer 1 — Read a rate.** Say what a rate of change is and work one from two values: the change in the quantity divided by the change in the quantity it follows, with its unit (cm per year, degrees per hour). Pass mark: 4 of 5.
- **Layer 2 — Sign of a rate.** Work negative rates (a falling temperature) and zero rates (no change), and say in words what the sign means. Pass mark: 4 of 5.
- **Layer 3 — Test a table.** Decide whether a table's rate of change is constant by checking every step, and say why a failing table is non-constant. Pass mark: 4 of 5.

## What "maxed" means here

The learner can take any two readings of a changing quantity and give its rate of change with the right unit and sign, and can look at a table and prove — step by step — whether its rate is constant, the exact skills Sudan's Grade 8 book teaches and exercises.
