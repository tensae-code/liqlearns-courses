# Rate of Change — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB
LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure — README, "Units, Lessons and Sub
Lessons"). Practice is tied to the skill number, as before. The
questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson
view. The sub lessons are the depths inside this one thing —
rate of change — reading a rate first, then its sign, then
testing a table for a constant rate, the order the book
teaches it in.

One lesson, one level (Level 150).

Mapping from the old depth layers: old Layer 1 = Sub lesson 1;
old Layer 2 = Sub lesson 2; old Layer 3 = Sub lesson 3.

## Sub lesson 1 — What a Rate of Change Is
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Say what a rate of change is and work one from two values: the change in the quantity divided by the change in the quantity it follows, with its unit (cm per year, degrees per hour). Pass mark: 4 of 5.

## Sub lesson 2 — Negative and Zero Rates
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Work negative rates (a falling temperature) and zero rates (no change), and say in words what the sign means. Pass mark: 4 of 5.

## Sub lesson 3 — Constant Rate of Change
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Decide whether a table's rate of change is constant by checking every step, and say why a failing table is non-constant. Pass mark: 4 of 5.

## What "maxed" means here

The learner can take any two readings of a changing quantity and give its rate of change with the right unit and sign, and can look at a table and prove — step by step — whether its rate is constant, the exact skills Sudan's Grade 8 book teaches and exercises.
