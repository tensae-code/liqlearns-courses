# True or false — Rate of Change

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Layer 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — What a Rate of Change Is · (Layer 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Negative and Zero Rates · (Layer 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Constant Rate of Change. The matching exercise is one set spanning Sub lessons 1–3; its marker names that span.

1. (Sub lesson 1) A rate of change is worked by dividing the change in a quantity by the change in the quantity it follows.
   **Answer: True.** Sudan, Grade 8 Mathematics (Second Year Intermediate), printed p. 52.

2. (Sub lesson 2) A falling temperature has a positive rate of change.
   **Answer: False — a falling quantity has a negative rate of change, such as −2° per hour.** Sudan, Grade 8 Mathematics (Second Year Intermediate), printed p. 54.

3. (Sub lesson 2) If a quantity stays exactly the same over the whole time watched, its rate of change is zero.
   **Answer: True.** Sudan, Grade 8 Mathematics (Second Year Intermediate), printed p. 54.

4. (Sub lesson 3) A table whose steps are 12, 12, 12 has a non-constant rate of change.
   **Answer: False — equal steps mean the rate is constant.** Sudan, Grade 8 Mathematics (Second Year Intermediate), printed p. 58.
