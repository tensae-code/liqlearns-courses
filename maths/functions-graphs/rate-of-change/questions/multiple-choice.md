# Multiple choice — Rate of Change

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Layer 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — What a Rate of Change Is · (Layer 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Negative and Zero Rates · (Layer 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Constant Rate of Change. The matching exercise is one set spanning Sub lessons 1–3; its marker names that span.

1. (Sub lesson 1) A height grows from 135 cm to 155 cm between ages 9 and 13. Its rate of change is:
   A. 20 cm per year B. 4 cm per year C. 5 cm per year D. 13 cm per year
   **Answer: C** — change 20 cm ÷ change 4 years = 5 cm per year. Sudan, Grade 8 Mathematics (Second Year Intermediate), printed p. 53.

2. (Sub lesson 2) A temperature falls steadily by 2 degrees every hour. Its rate of change is:
   A. 2 degrees per hour B. −2 degrees per hour C. 0 degrees per hour D. −1 degree per hour
   **Answer: B** — the quantity is going down, so the rate is negative. Sudan, Grade 8 Mathematics (Second Year Intermediate), printed p. 54.

3. (Sub lesson 3) A table reads: minutes 1, 2, 3; lines typed 12, 24, 36. The rate of change is:
   A. Constant, 12 lines per minute B. Constant, 24 lines per minute C. Not constant D. Zero
   **Answer: A** — every step adds the same 12 lines. Sudan, Grade 8 Mathematics (Second Year Intermediate), printed p. 58.

4. (Sub lesson 3) A sugar price table steps up by 10, then by 25, then by 5. Its rate of change is:
   A. Constant B. Zero C. Not constant D. Negative
   **Answer: C** — the steps are not the same, so the rate is judged non-constant. Sudan, Grade 8 Mathematics (Second Year Intermediate), printed p. 58.
