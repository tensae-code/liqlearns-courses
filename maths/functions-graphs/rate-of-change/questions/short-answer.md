# Short answer — Rate of Change

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Layer 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — What a Rate of Change Is · (Layer 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Negative and Zero Rates · (Layer 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Constant Rate of Change. The matching exercise is one set spanning Sub lessons 1–3; its marker names that span.

1. (Sub lesson 1) A school's admissions grow from 400 pupils to 460 pupils in 3 years, growing steadily. Work the rate of change and say it in full, with its unit.
   **Answer: Change 60 pupils ÷ 3 years = 20 pupils per year.** Sudan, Grade 8 Mathematics (Second Year Intermediate), printed pp. 52–53.

2. (Sub lesson 2) In one sentence, say what a rate of change of −2 degrees per hour means.
   **Answer: The temperature is falling by 2 degrees in every hour.** Sudan, Grade 8 Mathematics (Second Year Intermediate), printed p. 54.

3. (Sub lesson 3) Workers finish 30, 60, 95, 120 items after 1, 2, 3, 4 hours. Is the rate of change constant? Say why.
   **Answer: No. The steps are 30, 35, 25 — not the same amount each hour, so the rate is non-constant.** Sudan, Grade 8 Mathematics (Second Year Intermediate), printed p. 58.
