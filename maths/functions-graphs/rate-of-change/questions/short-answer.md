# Short answer — Rate of Change

1. (Layer 1) A school's admissions grow from 400 pupils to 460 pupils in 3 years, growing steadily. Work the rate of change and say it in full, with its unit.
   **Answer: Change 60 pupils ÷ 3 years = 20 pupils per year.** Sudan, Grade 8 Mathematics (Second Year Intermediate), printed pp. 52–53.

2. (Layer 2) In one sentence, say what a rate of change of −2 degrees per hour means.
   **Answer: The temperature is falling by 2 degrees in every hour.** Sudan, Grade 8 Mathematics (Second Year Intermediate), printed p. 54.

3. (Layer 3) Workers finish 30, 60, 95, 120 items after 1, 2, 3, 4 hours. Is the rate of change constant? Say why.
   **Answer: No. The steps are 30, 35, 25 — not the same amount each hour, so the rate is non-constant.** Sudan, Grade 8 Mathematics (Second Year Intermediate), printed p. 58.
