# Matching — Rate of Change

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Layer 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — What a Rate of Change Is · (Layer 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Negative and Zero Rates · (Layer 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Constant Rate of Change. The matching exercise is one set spanning Sub lessons 1–3; its marker names that span.

1. (Sub lessons 1–3) Match each situation to its rate of change:
   (a) Height 135 cm → 155 cm over 4 years
   (b) Temperature falls 2 degrees each hour
   (c) A price that does not move all week
   (d) Typing 12, 24, 36 lines after 1, 2, 3 minutes

   Rates: (i) 0 per week (ii) 5 cm per year (iii) −2 degrees per hour (iv) a constant 12 lines per minute

   **Answer: (a)–(ii), (b)–(iii), (c)–(i), (d)–(iv).** Sudan, Grade 8 Mathematics (Second Year Intermediate), printed pp. 53, 54, 58.
