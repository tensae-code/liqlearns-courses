# Ordering — Rate of Change

1. (Layer 1) Put the steps of working a rate of change in order:
   (a) Divide the change in the quantity by the change in what it follows.
   (b) Find the change in the quantity (second reading − first reading).
   (c) Say the rate with its two units.
   (d) Find the change in what the quantity follows (e.g. the time between the readings).
   **Answer: (b), (d), (a), (c).** Sudan, Grade 8 Mathematics (Second Year Intermediate), printed pp. 52–53.

2. (Layer 3) Put these rates of change of a height in order from smallest to largest: 0 cm per year, −2 cm per year, 5 cm per year, 3 cm per year.
   **Answer: −2, 0, 3, 5 cm per year.** Sudan, Grade 8 Mathematics (Second Year Intermediate), printed pp. 53–54.
