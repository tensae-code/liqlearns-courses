# Fill in the blank — Rate of Change

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Layer 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — What a Rate of Change Is · (Layer 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Negative and Zero Rates · (Layer 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Constant Rate of Change. The matching exercise is one set spanning Sub lessons 1–3; its marker names that span.

1. (Sub lesson 1) Rate of change = (change in the quantity) ÷ (change in ______).
   **Answer: the quantity it follows (e.g. the time).** Sudan, Grade 8 Mathematics (Second Year Intermediate), printed p. 52.

2. (Sub lesson 1) A quantity grows by 20 cm in 4 years. Its rate of change is ______ cm per year.
   **Answer: 5.** Sudan, Grade 8 Mathematics (Second Year Intermediate), printed p. 53.

3. (Sub lesson 2) A quantity that does not change at all has a rate of change of ______.
   **Answer: zero.** Sudan, Grade 8 Mathematics (Second Year Intermediate), printed p. 54.

4. (Sub lesson 3) A table's rate of change is constant when every step changes by ______ amount.
   **Answer: the same.** Sudan, Grade 8 Mathematics (Second Year Intermediate), printed p. 58.
