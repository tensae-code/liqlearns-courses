# Addition — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Level 1)** 8 + 5 = ______.
a) 12  b) 13  c) 14
**Answer:** b) 13 — 8 needs 2 to make 10, and 5 gives 2 and leaves 3.

**2. (Level 1)** 27 + 5 = ______.
a) 31  b) 32  c) 33
**Answer:** b) 32 — 27 needs 3 to make 30, and 5 gives 3 and leaves 2.

**3. (Level 1)** 32 + 45 = ______.
a) 67  b) 77  c) 87
**Answer:** b) 77 — ones 2 + 5 = 7, tens 3 + 4 = 7.

**4. (Level 2)** 74 + 63 = ______.
a) 127  b) 137  c) 147
**Answer:** b) 137 — ones 7, tens 7 + 6 = 13 tens: write 3, carry 1 (Egypt, Grade 2).

**5. (Level 2)** 47 + 35 = ______.
a) 72  b) 82  c) 92
**Answer:** b) 82 — ones 7 + 5 = 12: write 2, carry 1; tens 4 + 3 + 1 = 8.

**6. (Level 3)** 658 + 24 = ______.
a) 672  b) 682  c) 692
**Answer:** b) 682 — ones 8 + 4 = 12: write 2, carry 1; tens 5 + 2 + 1 = 8; hundreds 6.

**7. (Level 3)** The best check for 74 + 63 = 137 is ______.
a) 137 − 63  b) 63 + 74  c) 74 + 63 again the same way
**Answer:** b) 63 + 74 — adding the other way round must give the same answer.
