# Addition — fill-in-the-blank

Fill in the missing number or word.

**1. (Level 1)** 8 + 5 = ___.
**Answer:** 13

**2. (Level 1)** To add 8 + 5, make ten: 8 needs ___ to make 10.
**Answer:** 2

**3. (Level 2)** 74 + 63: the ones are ___, the tens are 13 tens, so the answer is ___.
**Answer:** 7; 137

**4. (Level 2)** When a column adds to ten or more, carry 1 to the ___ column.
**Answer:** next

**5. (Level 3)** 658 + 24 = ___.
**Answer:** 682
