# Addition — matching

Match each item on the left to its partner on the right.

**1. (Level 1)** Match each sum to its answer: (a) 8 + 5 (b) 9 + 7 (c) 27 + 5 — 13, 16, 32
**Answer:** a) 13, b) 16, c) 32.

**2. (Level 2)** Match each column sum to its answer: (a) 32 + 45 (b) 47 + 35 (c) 74 + 63 — 77, 82, 137
**Answer:** a) 77, b) 82, c) 137.

**3. (Level 3)** Match each three-digit sum to its answer: (a) 658 + 24 (b) 347 + 185 (c) 926 + 138 — 682, 532, 1,064
**Answer:** a) 682, b) 532, c) 1,064.

**4. (Level 3)** Match each sum to its estimate: (a) 247 + 45 (b) 48 + 36 — about 300, about 90
**Answer:** a) about 300, b) about 90.
