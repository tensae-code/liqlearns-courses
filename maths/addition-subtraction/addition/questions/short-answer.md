# Addition — short answer

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Addition In the Head · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Addition In Columns with Carrying · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Addition Three Digits and Checking. Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Add in your head: 9 + 7.
**Answer:** 16 — 9 needs 1 to make 10, and 7 gives 1 and leaves 6.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Add in your head: 46 + 8.
**Answer:** 54 — 46 needs 4 to make 50, and 8 gives 4 and leaves 4.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Add in columns: 56 + 28.
**Answer:** 84 — ones 6 + 8 = 14: write 4, carry 1; tens 5 + 2 + 1 = 8.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Add in columns: 38 + 47.
**Answer:** 85 — ones 8 + 7 = 15: write 5, carry 1; tens 3 + 4 + 1 = 8.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Add in columns: 347 + 185.
**Answer:** 532 — ones 7 + 5 = 12: write 2, carry 1; tens 4 + 8 + 1 = 13: write 3, carry 1; hundreds 3 + 1 + 1 = 5.

**6. (Sub lesson 4)** Add in columns: 487,650 + 275,483. Say where the carry crosses a period.
**Answer:** 763,133 — the ones period makes 1,133, so 1 thousand carries into the thousands period.

**7. (Sub lesson 4)** Estimate 631,286 + 331,989 first, then work it exactly.
**Answer:** About 630,000 + 330,000 = 960,000; exactly 963,275 (Ethiopia's teacher-guide example).
