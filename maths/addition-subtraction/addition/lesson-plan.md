# Lesson plan — Addition

**Course:** Maths · **Unit:** Unit 2 — Addition and Subtraction · **Lesson:** Addition
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 7, the first lesson of Unit 2 (maths/CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** adding — in the head and in columns, with carrying — and checking the answer.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Counting (Level 2) and Place-value (Level 3) — the learner counts and reads numbers by their places. It needs nothing else.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees
from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before
it is proved (the gate: README, "Units, Lessons and Sub Lessons
(ruled 2026-11-08)"):

1. **Addition In the Head** — add one- and two-digit numbers in the head by counting on and making ten. (Was depth Level 1.)
2. **Addition In Columns with Carrying** — add two-digit numbers in columns — the plain columns first, then carrying from ones to tens — and say each step aloud. (Was depth Level 2.)
3. **Addition Three Digits and Checking** — add three-digit numbers in columns with carrying, and check an answer by adding the other way round and by estimating. (Was depth Level 3.)

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson (no hardness ranking is written in this lesson's question files).

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Counting and Place-value (Stage 1) — adding is counting on, and carrying is regrouping tens. Recommended, never forced.
- A soft hint only: the inverse check (subtract to check an addition) is taught in the Subtraction lesson next door.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Add two numbers in the head, counting on and making ten (8 + 5 = 13).
2. Add two-digit numbers in columns without carrying, then with carrying (74 + 63 = 137 — Egypt's worked example).
3. Add three-digit numbers in columns with carrying in ones and tens (658 + 24 = 682).
4. Check an addition by adding the other way round, and by estimating first.

## What you need
Bundles of ten sticks and single sticks; squared paper.
Paper and a pencil.

## If the learner is stuck
- Adds 8 + 5 by counting all the fingers again → make ten: 8 needs 2 to make 10, and 5 gives 2 and leaves 3 — 10 + 3 = 13. Jordan's Grade 1 book teaches doubles and make-ten.
- Forgets the carried 1 → write it small above the next column, and say it aloud before adding the column: "7 tens and 6 tens, plus the carried ten."
- Lines the numbers up wrong (47 under 386) → line up by place value, ones under ones, starting from the ones place — Egypt's Grade 2 rule.
- This lesson's next door: Subtraction (same stage) — taking away is adding's inverse, and it checks every addition.
