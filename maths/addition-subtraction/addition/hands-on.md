# Hands-on — Addition

The lesson added with stones, bundles and squared paper.

## Pile addition
Two piles of stones: 8 and 5. Push them together and count the pile: 13. Now make ten first: move 2 stones from the 5-pile to the 8-pile — 10 and 3 left. Same answer, faster.

## Bundle carrying
Add 47 + 35 with sticks. Ones: 7 + 5 = 12 singles — bundle ten of them into a new ten-bundle and carry it to the tens. Tens: 4 + 3 bundles, plus the carried bundle = 8 bundles. Answer: 82.

## Squared-paper columns
Write 74 + 63 in columns on squared paper, one digit per square, ones under ones. Add the ones, then the tens, writing the carried 1 small above the tens. Check: add the other way round.

## Estimate then add
Before adding 247 + 45, estimate: about 300 (Jordan's printed estimate). Now add exactly: 292. Close to the estimate — the work is right.
