**Acceptance count: taught in 26 countries (Egypt, Jordan, Libya, Kazakhstan, Greece, Armenia, Vietnam, Mauritania, Laos, Yemen, Indonesia, Nicaragua, Ethiopia, South Sudan, Iraq, Bangladesh, Nepal, Cambodia, Kuwait, North Macedonia, Iran, China, Pakistan, Syria, Saudi Arabia, Rwanda).**. Egypt carries the column algorithm whole — regrouping ones, carrying to hundreds, three-digit columns. Jordan carries make-ten and doubles in Grade 1 and regrouping in Grade 2. Libya carries adding without regrouping to 100 in Grade 1, regrouping to 1000 in Grade 2, and to 10,000 in Grade 3. Kazakhstan carries the written column algorithm on named cases in Grade 2. Greece carries vertical addition with carrying in Grade 1 Teuxos B. Armenia carries adding with place transitions in Grade 2. Vietnam carries adding without regrouping in Grade 1 and with regrouping in Grade 2. Mauritania carries addition with carrying in Grade 1. Other read countries teach adding inside their own Grade 1–3 books and join by merge.** Laos carries addition from joining groups within 10 and crossing 10 by making 10 in Grade 1, through 2-digit regrouping and 3-digit place-value addition in Grade 2, to whole-number and decimal addition in Grade 5. Yemen carries addition from joining within 10 in Grade 1 through carrying within 1000, 10,000 and 999,999, with estimating and checking by rounding, to hundreds of millions in Grade 5. Indonesia carries addition from joining to 10 and to 20 in Grade 1, through addition under 20 in Grade 2, column addition to 100 with carrying in Grade 3 and to 1,000 in Grade 4, to column addition to 100,000 in Grade 5, then integer, fraction and decimal addition in Grade 7 (Merdeka and K13 Semester 1). Nicaragua carries addition from sums to 10 in Grade 1, through carrying to 100 in Grade 2 and to 1,000 in Grade 3, to a 4–5-digit worked box in Grade 4 (its only addition teaching there), and signed-number addition in Grade 7. Ethiopia carries addition from adding to 10 with commutativity, counting on, making 10 and doubles in Grade 1, through adding to 1000 without regrouping and with carrying in Grade 2, to adding to 10,000 in Grade 3 and to 1,000,000 with commutativity, associativity and identity in Grade 4. South Sudan carries addition from two-digit sums, column addition and missing addends in Grade 1, through number-line addition and column addition with carrying in Grade 2 (26 + 37 = 63; 384 + 208 = 592), to addition to 4 digits in Grade 3 (4321 + 3178 = 7499), and addition of fractions in Secondary 1. One book slip is never taught as fact: the Grade 2 book's p. 17 step "1+8+0=0" in the 384 + 208 worked example is wrong (it should be 9; the final answer 592 is right) — teach the correct step. Iraq carries addition from to 10 and to 20 in Grade 1 — no regrouping, making 10 — through regrouping to 999 in Grade 2 and to 9999 in Grade 3, to addition within millions in Grade 4, and to adding large numbers within billions in Grade 5. Bangladesh carries addition from the concept within 10 with the making-10 strategy in Class 1, through addition with and without carry to 3 digits in Class 2 and to 5 digits with carry in Class 3, to 5-digit addition in Class 4, and addition on the number line and of integers in Class 6. Nepal carries addition from bonds to 5, 9, 10 and 19 and two-digit addition without carrying in Grade 1, through 2- and 3-digit addition with carrying in Grade 2 (both books) and addition to 4 digits with carrying in Grade 3, to addition of fractions and decimals in Grades 5–6. Cambodia carries addition whole from single digits in Grade 1 through decimals, fractions and rationals in Grades 4–8, with the properties in Grade 7. Kuwait carries addition up its whole ladder — to 10 in Grade 1, within 100 in Grades 1–2, 3-digit numbers in Grade 2, to 10,000 in Grade 3, to millions in Grade 4, integers in Grade 6 and rational numbers in Grade 8. North Macedonia carries addition at Grade 1 (2022) — sums to 5, to 10 and to 20, number bonds, commutativity met as adding in a different order, and the make-ten strategy for three numbers. Iran carries addition from the first written expressions in Grade 1 (§13) through carrying in Grade 2 to larger numbers in Grade 3, and keeps it in use through the primary years.

China carries addition from Grade 1 carrying work to the Grade 4 treatment of the operation's part-relations and its laws stated in letters.

Pakistan (Punjab) carries addition from the Primer's “one more” to 9-digit column addition in Class 5 and integer addition in Class 6.

Syria builds addition slowly and explicitly — number bonds for every number to 12 before the + sign appears, completing-to-ten in Grade 1 Semester 2, carrying introduced in Grade 2 as ringing ten ones and exchanging them for a ten-rod, columns to four digits in Grade 3 and five in Grade 4, a restatement with the commutative law in Grades 5–6, and rational-number addition in Grade 7.

Saudi Arabia builds addition from part–whole stories and number bonds in Grade 1, through counting on, doubles and making ten in Grade 2 Part One, to regrouped three-digit addition in Part Two, the three properties and estimation-checked answers in Grade 3, and mental grouping with five-digit columns in Grade 4, with the older 1445 Term-3 volume witnessing the Grade 2 Part Two chapter.

Rwanda teaches addition as a staged climb — to 200, 500 and 999 in Year 2, to 2,000, 5,000 and 10,000 in Year 3 with one-step word problems in a given / request / steps-and-solution format, column addition in P4, column addition of 6/7-digit numbers in P5, addition beyond 1,000,000 in P6, and addition of natural numbers and of integers, with its properties, in Senior 1.
