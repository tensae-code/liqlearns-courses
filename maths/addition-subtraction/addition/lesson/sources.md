**Acceptance count: taught in 11 countries (Egypt, Jordan, Libya, Kazakhstan, Greece, Armenia, Vietnam, Mauritania, Laos, Yemen, Indonesia). Egypt carries the column algorithm whole — regrouping ones, carrying to hundreds, three-digit columns. Jordan carries make-ten and doubles in Grade 1 and regrouping in Grade 2. Libya carries adding without regrouping to 100 in Grade 1, regrouping to 1000 in Grade 2, and to 10,000 in Grade 3. Kazakhstan carries the written column algorithm on named cases in Grade 2. Greece carries vertical addition with carrying in Grade 1 Teuxos B. Armenia carries adding with place transitions in Grade 2. Vietnam carries adding without regrouping in Grade 1 and with regrouping in Grade 2. Mauritania carries addition with carrying in Grade 1. Other read countries teach adding inside their own Grade 1–3 books and join by merge.** Laos carries addition from joining groups within 10 and crossing 10 by making 10 in Grade 1, through 2-digit regrouping and 3-digit place-value addition in Grade 2, to whole-number and decimal addition in Grade 5. Yemen carries addition from joining within 10 in Grade 1 through carrying within 1000, 10,000 and 999,999, with estimating and checking by rounding, to hundreds of millions in Grade 5. Indonesia carries addition from joining to 10 and to 20 in Grade 1, through addition under 20 in Grade 2, column addition to 100 with carrying in Grade 3 and to 1,000 in Grade 4, to column addition to 100,000 in Grade 5, then integer, fraction and decimal addition in Grade 7 (Merdeka and K13 Semester 1).
