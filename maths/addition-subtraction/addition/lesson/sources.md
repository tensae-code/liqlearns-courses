Barbados publishes no government pupil textbooks — primary mathematics books are commercial titles parents buy, and the syllabi's Suggested Texts appendices name them (*Caribbean Primary Mathematics* Levels 1–6; *Nelson Primary Maths for Caribbean Schools* 1–4; *Steps To Common Entrance Mathematics* 1–3) as named by the syllabus only: none is in hand and none is cited. Barbados is therefore named in this lesson's countries line and its syllabus requirements are cited in the table as curriculum evidence, but Barbados is not counted in the taught-in figure.

different signs — subtract the smaller absolute value from the larger and take the sign of the larger.
