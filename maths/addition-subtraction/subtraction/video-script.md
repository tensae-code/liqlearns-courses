# Video script — Subtraction

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Thirteen stones! Take five away!
Count back! Twelve, eleven, ten, nine, EIGHT!
Or count UP from five to thirteen!
Five to ten is five! Ten to thirteen is three!
Five and three — EIGHT!

Now columns! 137 − 54!
Ones first! 7 take 4 = 3!
Tens! 3 take 5? Too small — BORROW!
13 tens take 5 = 8 tens!
Eighty-three!

Check it! 83 + 54 = 137!
Adding proves the taking away!
Now you — subtract the numbers I show you!
