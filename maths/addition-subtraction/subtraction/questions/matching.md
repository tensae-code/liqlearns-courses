# Subtraction — matching

Match each item on the left to its partner on the right.

**1. (Level 1)** Match each subtraction to its answer: (a) 13 − 5 (b) 16 − 9 (c) 52 − 48 — 8, 7, 4
**Answer:** a) 8, b) 7, c) 4.

**2. (Level 2)** Match each column subtraction to its answer: (a) 78 − 43 (b) 54 − 37 (c) 137 − 54 — 35, 17, 83
**Answer:** a) 35, b) 17, c) 83.

**3. (Level 3)** Match each subtraction to its answer: (a) 107 − 59 (b) 503 − 268 (c) 1,000 − 437 — 48, 235, 563
**Answer:** a) 48, b) 235, c) 563.

**4. (Level 3)** Match each subtraction to its check: (a) 137 − 54 = 83 (b) 107 − 59 = 48 — 83 + 54 = 137, 48 + 59 = 107
**Answer:** a) 83 + 54 = 137, b) 48 + 59 = 107.
