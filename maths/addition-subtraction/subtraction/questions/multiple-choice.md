# Subtraction — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Subtraction In the Head · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Subtraction In Columns with Borrowing · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Subtraction Borrowing Across Zero and Checking.

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** 13 − 5 = ______.
a) 7  b) 8  c) 9
**Answer:** b) 8 — count back, or count up from 5 to 13.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** 52 − 48 = ______.
a) 3  b) 4  c) 5
**Answer:** b) 4 — count up from 48: 4 jumps to 52.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** 78 − 43 = ______.
a) 25  b) 35  c) 45
**Answer:** b) 35 — ones 8 − 3 = 5, tens 7 − 4 = 3.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** 137 − 54 = ______.
a) 73  b) 83  c) 93
**Answer:** b) 83 — ones 3, tens borrow: 13 tens − 5 tens = 8 tens (Egypt, Grade 2).

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** 54 − 37 = ______.
a) 17  b) 23  c) 27
**Answer:** a) 17 — ones borrow: 14 − 7 = 7; tens 4 − 3 = 1. Never swap digits in a column.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** 107 − 59 = ______.
a) 38  b) 48  c) 58
**Answer:** b) 48 — borrow across zero: 17 ones − 9 = 8, 9 tens − 5 = 4 (Egypt, Grade 2).

**7. (Sub lesson 3)** The check for 137 − 54 = 83 is ______.
a) 137 − 83  b) 83 + 54  c) 54 − 83
**Answer:** b) 83 + 54 — the difference plus what was taken away must give 137.
