# Subtraction — short answer

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Level 1)** Subtract in your head: 16 − 9.
**Answer:** 7 — count back, or count up from 9 to 16.

**2. (Level 1)** Subtract in your head: 31 − 7.
**Answer:** 24 — 31 needs 1 to reach 30, and 7 gives 1 and leaves 6: 30 − 6 = 24.

**3. (Level 2)** Subtract in columns: 92 − 46.
**Answer:** 46 — ones borrow: 12 − 6 = 6; tens 8 − 4 = 4.

**4. (Level 2)** Subtract in columns: 73 − 28.
**Answer:** 45 — ones borrow: 13 − 8 = 5; tens 6 − 2 = 4.

**5. (Level 3)** Subtract in columns: 503 − 268.
**Answer:** 235 — ones borrow: 13 − 8 = 5; tens borrow across zero: 9 − 6 = 3; hundreds 4 − 2 = 2.
