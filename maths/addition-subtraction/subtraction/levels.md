# Subtraction — questions by level

Practice by level, tied to the skill number. The questions live in
the type files; this file is the by-level view. The levels are the
depths inside this one thing — subtracting — in the head first, then in columns with borrowing, then borrowing across zero with checking..


## Level 1 — in the head
Level 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: subtract small numbers in the head by counting back and by counting up.

## Level 2 — in columns with borrowing
Level 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: subtract two-digit numbers in columns, with borrowing from tens to ones, and say each step aloud.

## Level 3 — borrowing across zero and checking
Level 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: subtract three-digit numbers in columns including borrowing across zero, and check every subtraction by adding.
