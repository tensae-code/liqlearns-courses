# Subtraction — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB
LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure — README, "Units, Lessons and Sub
Lessons"). Practice is tied to the skill number, as before. The
questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson
view. The sub lessons are the depths inside this one thing —
subtracting — in the head first, then in columns with borrowing,
then borrowing across zero with checking..

Mapping from the old depth layers: old Level 1 = Sub lesson 1; old Level 2 = Sub lesson 2; old Level 3 = Sub lesson 3. Sub lesson 4 was added 2026-11-08 under the whole-arc rule, from the lesson's own stocked sources (subtraction to 100,000, to 1,000,000 and beyond, with borrowing across periods — stocked but capped out of the old text at three digits). The books state no separate pass mark for the big-number band, so Sub lesson 4 keeps the same observable form — the learner can work the band and check it by adding — rather than an invented mark.

## Sub lesson 1 — Subtraction In the Head
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: subtract small numbers in the head by counting back and by counting up.

## Sub lesson 2 — Subtraction In Columns with Borrowing
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: subtract two-digit numbers in columns, with borrowing from tens to ones, and say each step aloud.

## Sub lesson 3 — Subtraction Borrowing Across Zero and Checking
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: subtract three-digit numbers in columns including borrowing across zero, and check every subtraction by adding.

## Sub lesson 4 — Big Subtraction: To a Million and Beyond
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: subtract
numbers to 1,000,000 and beyond in columns, borrowing inside
and across periods, and check every answer by adding.
