# Hands-on — Subtraction

The lesson took away with stones, bundles and squared paper.

## Take-away piles
13 stones. Take 5 away. Count what is left: 8. Now count UP from 5 to 13 instead: 5 to 10 is 5 jumps, 10 to 13 is 3 jumps — 8 jumps. Same answer, two ways.

## Bundle borrowing
Subtract 54 − 37 with sticks: 5 bundles and 4 singles, take 3 bundles and 7 singles. The ones are short — unbundle one ten-bundle into 10 singles: 14 singles take 7 leaves 7; 4 bundles take 3 leaves 1. Answer: 17.

## Squared-paper columns
Write 137 − 54 in columns on squared paper. Borrow one ten for the ones, crossing out the 3 and writing 2 above it. Subtract ones, then tens. Check: 83 + 54 = 137.

## Borrow across zero
Write 107 − 59. The ones need borrowing but the tens are 0 — borrow from the hundreds: 107 becomes 0 hundreds, 10 tens, 7 ones; then borrow one ten: 9 tens, 17 ones. Subtract: 17 − 9 = 8, 9 − 5 = 4. Answer: 48.
