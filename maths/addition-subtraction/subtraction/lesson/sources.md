**Acceptance count: taught in 12 countries (Egypt, Jordan, Libya, Kazakhstan, Greece, Armenia, Vietnam, Ghana, Mauritania, Laos, Yemen, Indonesia). Egypt carries the column algorithm whole — borrowing from hundreds to tens, borrowing in both places, borrowing across zero. Jordan carries subtracting without regrouping in Grade 1 and with regrouping in Grade 2. Libya carries subtracting without regrouping to 100 in Grade 1, regrouping to 1000 in Grade 2, and to 10,000 in Grade 3. Kazakhstan carries the written column algorithm on named cases in Grade 2. Greece carries vertical subtraction with borrowing in Grade 1 Teuxos B. Armenia carries subtracting with place transitions in Grade 2. Vietnam carries subtracting without regrouping in Grade 1 and with regrouping in Grade 2. Ghana carries subtracting to 20 as teacher evidence — and its workbook's printed '15 − 3 = 18' is flagged wrong in the filing and is never taught. Mauritania carries subtraction with and without carrying in Grade 2. Other read countries teach subtracting inside their own Grade 1–3 books and join by merge.** Laos carries subtraction from take-away stories within 10 and crossing 10 by splitting to 10 in Grade 1, through 2-digit borrowing and 3-digit place-value subtraction in Grade 2, to whole-number and decimal subtraction in Grade 5. Yemen carries subtraction from take-away within 10 in Grade 1 through borrowing within 1000, 10,000 and 999,999, with estimating and checking by addition, to hundreds of millions in Grade 5. Indonesia carries subtraction from taking away to 10 and to 20 in Grade 1, through subtraction under 20 in Grade 2, column subtraction to 100 with borrowing in Grade 3 and to 1,000 in Grade 4, to column subtraction to 100,000 in Grade 5, then integer, fraction and decimal subtraction in Grade 7 (Merdeka and K13 Semester 1).
