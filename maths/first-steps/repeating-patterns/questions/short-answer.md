# Repeating Patterns — short answer

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Patterns Continuing a Repeating Pattern · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Patterns The Rule, Sounds and Movements, and Fixing Errors · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Patterns Growing and Shrinking.

Answer in a word or a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Red, red, blue, red, red, blue, red, red, ___. What comes next?
**Answer:** Blue — the repeating part "red, red, blue" starts again.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What do we call the small part that comes back again and again in a pattern?
**Answer:** The repeating part (the pattern rule) — naming it is Ghana's rule-finding work (teacher evidence: JICA B3, Lessons 77–79).

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** This row has one wrong piece: square, circle, square, circle, circle, circle. Which piece is wrong, and what should it be?
**Answer:** The fifth piece — it should be a square, so the rule "square, circle" holds all the way.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Name one kind of pattern that uses no things at all, from Indonesia's Grade 2 chapter.
**Answer:** A sound pattern (also right: a movement pattern) — the chapter teaches shape, colour, movement and sound patterns (printed pp. 101–124).

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** A staircase pattern has steps of 2 squares, 5 squares, 8 squares. How much does it grow by each step, and how many squares does the next step have?
**Answer:** It grows by 3 each time; the next step has 11 squares — 8 + 3 (Indonesia, Grade 4, printed pp. 85–102).

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** Yemen's Grade 1 book returns to "discovering patterns" inside three later units. Name one of them.
**Answer:** Addition (also right: subtraction, shapes) — lessons 3-8 (printed p. 84), 4-7 (printed p. 110), 5-3 (printed p. 126).
