# Repeating Patterns — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Patterns Continuing a Repeating Pattern · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Patterns The Rule, Sounds and Movements, and Fixing Errors · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Patterns Growing and Shrinking.

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Red, blue, red, blue, red, ___. What comes next?
a) red  b) blue  c) green
**Answer:** b) blue — the repeating part is "red, blue", so blue follows red.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Circle, square, circle, square, circle, ___
a) square  b) circle  c) triangle
**Answer:** a) square — the shapes take turns; after circle comes square (Kuwait, Grade 1, printed p. 24).

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is the repeating part of: big, small, small, big, small, small?
a) big, small  b) big, small, small  c) small, small, big, small
**Answer:** b) big, small, small — the smallest part that copies to make the whole row.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A sound pattern goes clap, clap, stomp, clap, clap, stomp, clap, ___, ___
a) stomp, clap  b) clap, stomp  c) stomp, stomp
**Answer:** b) clap, stomp — the part "clap, clap, stomp" starts again (Indonesia, Grade 2, printed pp. 101–124).

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** 1, 3, 5, 7, ___ — the pattern grows by 2 each time.
a) 8  b) 9  c) 11
**Answer:** b) 9 — 7 + 2 = 9 (Indonesia, Grade 4, printed pp. 85–102).

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** Ghana's halving chain (teacher evidence) goes 560, 280, 140, 70, ___
a) 60  b) 40  c) 35
**Answer:** c) 35 — each number is half the one before: half of 70 is 35 (Ghana, JICA B5, Lesson 78 — teacher evidence).
