# Repeating Patterns — matching

Match each row on the left with its rule on the right.

**1. (Level 1)** Triangle, circle, triangle, circle, triangle, circle
**Answer:** The rule is "triangle, circle" — a two-piece repeating part.

**2. (Level 1)** Red, red, red, blue, red, red, red, blue
**Answer:** The rule is "red, red, red, blue" — count the reds before you name the part.

**3. (Level 2)** Stomp, clap, stomp, clap, stomp, clap (heard, not seen)
**Answer:** A sound-and-movement pattern, rule "stomp, clap" — patterns live in sounds and movements too (Indonesia, Grade 2, printed pp. 101–124).

**4. (Level 3)** 2, 5, 8, 11, 14
**Answer:** A growing pattern: add 3 each time (Indonesia, Grade 4, printed pp. 85–102).

**5. (Level 3)** Pictures of floor tiles: 1 tile, 4 tiles, 9 tiles in growing squares
**Answer:** A growing picture pattern of the kind Indonesia's Grade 8 chapter studies as tile patterns (K13 Bab 1, printed pp. 1–40).
