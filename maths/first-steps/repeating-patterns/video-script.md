# Video script — Repeating Patterns

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Red, blue. Red, blue. What comes next? **Red!**
Red, blue, red, blue — again and again!

Clap, clap, **stomp**! Clap, clap, **stomp**!
Your body can do patterns too!

Find the little part that repeats.
That little part is the **rule**.
Say the rule — then you can go on and on!

Now grow! One square. Three squares. Five squares.
It grows by **two** — what comes next? **Seven!**

Patterns are everywhere. Find one today!
