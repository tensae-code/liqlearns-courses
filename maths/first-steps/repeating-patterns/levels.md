# Repeating Patterns — questions by level

Practice by level, tied to the skill number. The questions live in
the type files; this file is the by-level view. The levels are the
depths inside this one thing — patterns — continuing a repeat first,
then naming its rule and fixing it, then growing and shrinking, the
order the books teach them in.

## Level 1 — continuing a repeating pattern
Level 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: continue a repeating pattern of shapes or colours and say what comes next, for repeats of two and of three.

## Level 2 — the pattern rule, sounds and movements, fixing errors
Level 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: name the smallest part that repeats, continue a sound or movement pattern, and find and fix the wrong piece in a broken pattern.

## Level 3 — growing and shrinking patterns
Level 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: continue a growing or shrinking pattern, say how much it changes by each step, and follow a halving chain.
