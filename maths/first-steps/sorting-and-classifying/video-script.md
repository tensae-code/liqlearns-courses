# Video script — Sorting and Classifying

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Look! A big mess of buttons!

Sort them! **Big** ones here. **Small** ones here.
Now they are in groups. What is the rule? **Size!**

Mix them up! Sort again!
**Red** ones here. **Not red** ones here.
The rule now? **Colour!** Same buttons. New groups!

Now two rules together!
Big **and** red — here. Big, not red — here.
Small **and** red — here. Small, not red — here.
Four groups!

Every thing in a group. Every group has a rule.
Say the rule out loud!
That is sorting.
