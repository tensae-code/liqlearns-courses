# Sorting and Classifying — matching

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Sorting By One Attribute · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Sorting Re-Sorting and the Not Group · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Sorting By Two Attributes and Sorting for Data.

Match each pile on the left with the rule that sorted it on the right.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A bag of only big stones beside a bag of only small stones
**Answer:** Sorted by size — small to the small bag, big to the big bag (Yemen, Grade 1 Part 1, printed p. 8).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A group of all the spoons, forks in another group, knives in a third
**Answer:** Sorted by use / kind — grouping by what a thing is for is one of Nicaragua's answers (teacher evidence: Grade 1 guide, Unit 1).

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The same shells in two photos: circled by colour in one, circled by shape in the other
**Answer:** One set, two sorts — the rule changed, so the groups changed (China, Grade 1, Unit 3, printed p. 27).

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** A circle labelled "small and red"
**Answer:** A two-attribute group — its rule has two parts, size and colour (Yemen, Grade 1 Part 1, printed p. 12).

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** A lone green button sitting outside the red and blue groups, in its own chalk circle
**Answer:** The "not" group — not red and not blue is a rule too, and it leaves nothing homeless.
