# Sorting and Classifying — short answer

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Sorting By One Attribute · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Sorting Re-Sorting and the Not Group · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Sorting By Two Attributes and Sorting for Data.

Answer in a word or a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Small stones go to the small bag, big stones to the big bag. What attribute did you sort by?
**Answer:** Size — Yemen's first sorting lesson sorts small to the small bag, big to the big bag (Grade 1 Part 1, printed p. 8).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A teacher asks: "Why can we group these?" Name one likeness from Nicaragua's guide you could answer with.
**Answer:** By their shape (also right: by their use, by their colours) — the guide's expected answers (teacher evidence: Grade 1 guide, Unit 1).

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** You sort leaves by colour. One leaf is brown; your colour groups are green and yellow. What do you do?
**Answer:** Make a "not green and not yellow" group for it — every thing needs a home in the sort.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** What does Kazakhstan's book call a group of things gathered by a shared feature?
**Answer:** A set — composing and classifying sets is its Grade 2 work (Ospanov Part 1, Lessons 56–57).

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Name the four groups when you sort buttons by colour (red / not red) and size (big / small).
**Answer:** Big and red; big and not red; small and red; small and not red — two passes give four two-part rules.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** Why does sorting come before drawing a pictogram?
**Answer:** Because the pictogram shows the sorted groups — Yemen's statistics unit sorts first (printed p. 140), then represents pictorially (printed p. 142).

**7. (Sub lesson 1)** Indonesia's Grade 1 book groups shapes three ways. Name one of them.
**Answer:** By shape (also right: by colour, by size) — the shapes chapter groups all three ways (printed pp. 115–144).
