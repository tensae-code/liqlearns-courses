# Lesson plan — Position and Direction

**Course:** Maths · **Unit:** Unit 18 — First Steps · **Lesson:** Position and Direction
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 134 of 136, the first lesson of Unit 18 — First Steps (maths/CURRICULUM.md). Unit 18 stands last by numbering, but its lessons are for the youngest learners: this one teaches the words for where things are.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the position words — in and out, above and below, over and under, in front and behind, left and right, far and near, before and after, between — the counting words for places in a line (first, second, third … tenth), and the eight points of the compass at primary level.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on nothing but the learner's own body and room. It needs no numbers at all. Pencil and Lines (Level 1, the shared course) is its companion for drawing the maps and lines this lesson plays with.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the
exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an
image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture

while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is
dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the
student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph,
or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be
shown changing.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees
from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before
it is proved (the gate: README, "Units, Lessons and Sub Lessons
(ruled 2026-11-08)"):

1. **Position The Pairs for One Thing** — use in/out, above/below, over/under and inside/outside/between correctly for real things and pictures. (Was depth Level 1.)
2. **Position Front, Back, Left, Right and Places in a Line** — use front/back, left/right, far/near and before/after correctly, and name places first to tenth from a named end of a line. (Was depth Level 2.)
3. **Position The Compass Points and Routes** — name all eight compass points on a north-up map and follow or give a short route in steps. (Was depth Level 3.)

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson (no hardness ranking is written in this lesson's question files).

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Pencil and Lines (Level 1, the shared course) — the learner draws the line of children and the little maps. Recommended, never forced.
- Recommended alongside: Counting (Unit 1) — the ordinals name places in a counted line. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Use over/under, above/below and right/left for real things, the way Kuwait's Grade 1 book opens its numbers-and-position unit (lesson 1-1, printed p. 17).
2. Use inside, between and outside, the way Kuwait's Grade 1 book teaches next (lesson 1-2, printed p. 20), and again in Grade 2 (lesson 1-1, printed p. 17).
3. Use the whole first set of pairs — inside/outside, above/below, over/under, far/near, before/after — the way Pakistan's Grade 1 book teaches them in its geometry unit (printed pp. 116–126).
4. Say left and right, in, out, up, down, front and back about a pictured place, the way Pakistan's Primer shows them with a dog and its kennel (printed pp. 134–135).
5. Use the playground pairs — in front of / behind, under / above, beside / on / in, up / down, forwards / backwards, near / far — the way North Macedonia's Grade 1 book teaches them on one playground scene (printed p. 10), then one pair at a time (printed pp. 13–18).
6. Name places in a line — first to tenth — and answer "who is third from the front?", the way Bangladesh's Class 2 book teaches the ordinals (printed pp. 34–39).
7. Put things and people in order, the way Laos's Grade 1 book teaches order as its own chapter (Chapter 3, printed p. 22), and the way Yemen's Grade 1 book teaches relative positions (lesson 1-4, printed p. 18; inside/outside, printed p. 20).
8. Name the eight compass points and follow a short route of steps — north, east, south, west and the points between — the way China's Grade 3 book teaches the eight directions on north-up maps (Unit 1, printed p. 2), and the way Ghana's teacher manuals walk routes in steps (teacher evidence: JICA B5, Lessons 103–105; JICA B6, Lesson 93).

## What you need
A box or bag a toy can go in and out of. A table the toy can go over and under. Your own body — hands for left and right. A line of children, chairs or books for the ordinals. Paper and a pencil for the little map.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — the learner hides a toy and says where it is: in the box, under the table, behind their back; the companion must find it from the words alone (lesson/lesson.md).
2. **The explanation** — the pairs one at a time (in/out; above/below; over/under; front/back; left/right; far/near; before/after; between); places in a line (first … tenth); the eight compass points and a steps route (lesson/lesson.md).
   - ADD GIF at the pairs part: a picture of a toy dog hopping in and out of its kennel, then over a low wall and under it — the student says the word for each place as the dog lands there.
   - ADD IMAGE under the line part: a picture of ten children in a line at a race start, each with a big number badge 1st to 10th → the student points and names the third child from the front and the third from the back, and sees they are not the same child.
   - ADD IMAGE in the compass part: a picture of a simple map with north at the top and the eight compass points drawn as a star in the corner → the student traces a route of three steps on the map with a finger and says each direction word.
3. **Say it to your companion** — give hiding-place clues and route clues in whole sentences, and follow your companion's clues (lesson/lesson.md).
   - ADD VIDEO: the script in video-script.md, filmed — the position-word game in the super-simple voice (made later, when videos are made).
4. **Questions** — from questions/: multiple-choice, short-answer, fill-in-the-blank, true-or-false, matching, ordering — served at the learner's level and teaching-mix (levels.md).
5. **Hands-on** — hands-on.md.
6. **Do It Yourself** — hide, line up, and map: three small jobs at home (lesson/lesson.md).

## If the learner is stuck
- Mixes left and right → Kuwait's and North Macedonia's way: work from the learner's own body first — the hand they write with, a band tied on the right wrist — and only then left and right of a picture (Kuwait, Grade 1, printed p. 17; North Macedonia, Grade 1, printed pp. 16–18). Left and right swap when you face someone: stand side by side, never face to face, while learning them.
- Says "up there" instead of the pair word → give the two words as a choice — "Is the bird above the tree or below the tree?" — and let the learner pick; the pair is learned as a pair, the way North Macedonia's book prints them in pairs (printed p. 10).
- Counts a place from the wrong end → Bangladesh's line work: always name the end first — "third from the front" — and walk the line from that end, touching each child (Class 2, printed pp. 34–39).
- Compass points spin → fix north first, the way China's maps do: north at the top of the page, then the other points are built from it (Grade 3, Unit 1, printed p. 2).
- This lesson's next door: Sorting and Classifying (Level 135, same unit) — things found by their places are next grouped by their likenesses.
