# Video script — Position and Direction

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Where is the ball?

The ball is **in** the box. Now it is **out**!
The ball is **over** the box. Now **under**!
**Above** the table. **Below** the table.

Stand up! Your hand is **in front** of you.
Now **behind** you! This is my **left** hand.
This is my **right** hand.

You are **near** me. Now walk away — you are **far**!

Line up! You are **first**. You are **second**. You are **third**!

Now point: **north** is up. **East** is right.
**South** is down. **West** is left.

Say where things are. Say it whole!
That is position.
