# Position and Direction — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right position word.

**1. (Level 1)** The fish swim ___ the bridge.
**Answer:** under — the bridge is over the river and the fish are under it.

**2. (Level 1)** Put the cup ___ the box — not on it, not under it, right inside it.
**Answer:** in — in means inside the box.

**3. (Level 2)** Hold up the hand you do not write with. For most learners in this lesson that hand is called the ___ hand. (If you write with that hand, its name swaps.)
**Answer:** left — left and right are named from your own body (Kuwait, Grade 1, printed p. 17).

**4. (Level 2)** First, second, third, fourth, ___, sixth.
**Answer:** fifth — the place words run in order (Bangladesh, Class 2, printed pp. 34–39).

**5. (Level 3)** On a map, ___ is always put at the top.
**Answer:** north — China's maps are north-up (Grade 3, Unit 1, printed p. 2).

**6. (Level 3)** North, north-east, east, south-east, south, south-west, ___, north-west.
**Answer:** west — the eight points run round the compass in order.
