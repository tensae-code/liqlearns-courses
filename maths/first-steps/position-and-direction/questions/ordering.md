# Position and Direction — ordering

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Position The Pairs for One Thing · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Position Front, Back, Left, Right and Places in a Line · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Position The Compass Points and Routes.

Put each list in the right order.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A morning line: the teacher says "line up!" Put in place order: third, first, second.
**Answer:** first, second, third — place words run in line order (Laos, Grade 1, Ch. 3 — Order, printed p. 22).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Put in place order: tenth, seventh, ninth, eighth.
**Answer:** seventh, eighth, ninth, tenth — the places keep counting up (Bangladesh, Class 2, printed pp. 34–39).

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Four runners finish a race. Put in finishing order: the runner who came second, the runner who came fourth, the winner, the runner who came third.
**Answer:** the winner (first), second, third, fourth — first place is the winner's place.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Walking clockwise round a compass starting at north, put in order: east, north-east, north, south-east.
**Answer:** north, north-east, east, south-east — the points follow each other round the compass (China, Grade 3, printed p. 2).
