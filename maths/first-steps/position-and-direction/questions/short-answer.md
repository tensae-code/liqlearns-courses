# Position and Direction — short answer

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Position The Pairs for One Thing · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Position Front, Back, Left, Right and Places in a Line · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Position The Compass Points and Routes.

Answer in a word or a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The dog is in its kennel. Where is the dog when it runs out to play — in or out?
**Answer:** Out — Pakistan's Primer shows the dog in and out of its kennel (printed pp. 134–135).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A bird sits on the roof. A cat sits under the tree. Which animal is above?
**Answer:** The bird — on the roof is the higher place.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the twin word: the twin of "near" is ___.
**Answer:** Far — near/far is one pair (Pakistan, Grade 1, printed pp. 123–124).

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In a line of children, you stand behind Sam. Are you before Sam or after Sam?
**Answer:** After Sam — the one behind comes after (Pakistan, Grade 1, printed pp. 125–126).

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Five children line up. What place word names the last child, counted from the front?
**Answer:** Fifth — the places run first, second, third, fourth, fifth (Bangladesh, Class 2, printed pp. 34–39).

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** From home, the school is 3 steps east and the post office is 2 steps south (Ghana's route, teacher evidence). Which place needs only south steps from home?
**Answer:** The post office — its route is 2 steps south, with no east steps (Ghana, JICA B5, Lesson 103 — teacher evidence).
