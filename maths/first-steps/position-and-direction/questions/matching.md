# Position and Direction — matching

Match each picture-in-words on the left with its position sentence on the right.

**1. (Level 1)** A dog inside a kennel
**Answer:** The dog is in the kennel — in/inside, as Pakistan's Primer shows it (printed pp. 134–135).

**2. (Level 1)** A lamp hanging high over a table
**Answer:** The lamp is above the table — above names the higher place.

**3. (Level 2)** A little house a long walk away, drawn tiny on the page
**Answer:** The house is far — far is the twin of near (Pakistan, Grade 1, printed pp. 123–124).

**4. (Level 2)** The middle book of three books in a row
**Answer:** The book is between the other two — between is the middle word (Kuwait, Grade 1, printed p. 20).

**5. (Level 3)** An arrow on a map pointing to the top-right corner, halfway between up and right
**Answer:** The arrow points north-east — on a north-up map, up is north and right is east, so halfway is north-east.
