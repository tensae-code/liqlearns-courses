# Position and Direction — true or false

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Position The Pairs for One Thing · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Position Front, Back, Left, Right and Places in a Line · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Position The Compass Points and Routes.

Say true or false, and say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A ball under the bed is above the bed.
**Answer:** False — under the bed is the low place; above would be the high place.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A thing between two chairs has a chair on each side of it.
**Answer:** True — between is the middle of two things (Kuwait, Grade 1, printed p. 20).

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** If you stand behind me in the line, you are before me.
**Answer:** False — behind in the line is after, not before (Pakistan, Grade 1, printed pp. 125–126).

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The third child from the front is always the same child as the third from the back.
**Answer:** False — the place depends on the end you count from (Bangladesh, Class 2, printed pp. 34–39).

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** South-east is halfway between south and east.
**Answer:** True — the four halfway points sit between their main points.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** On a north-up map, west is on the right side of the page.
**Answer:** False — with north at the top, east is on the right and west is on the left (China, Grade 3, printed p. 2).
