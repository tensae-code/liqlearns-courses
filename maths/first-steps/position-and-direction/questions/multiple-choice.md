# Position and Direction — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Position The Pairs for One Thing · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Position Front, Back, Left, Right and Places in a Line · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Position The Compass Points and Routes.

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The cat is sleeping under the table. Where is the cat?
a) on the table  b) below the table  c) in the table
**Answer:** b) below the table — under and below are the lower pair words.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The ball is between the two shoes. The ball is…
a) on top of both shoes  b) in the middle, with a shoe on each side  c) far away
**Answer:** b) in the middle, with a shoe on each side — between means in the middle of two things (Kuwait, Grade 1, printed p. 20).

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Which word is the twin of "above"?
a) under  b) below  c) near
**Answer:** b) below — above and below are the pair (Kuwait, Grade 1, printed p. 17).

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** You face your friend. Your friend's left hand is…
a) on your left side  b) on your right side  c) behind you
**Answer:** b) on your right side — left and right belong to the person; facing people swap sides.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** In a line, Ana is third from the front and fifth from the back. How many children are in the line?
a) 8  b) 7  c) 5
**Answer:** b) 7 — 2 children in front of Ana, Ana, then 4 behind her: 2 + 1 + 4 = 7 (Bangladesh's line questions, Class 2, printed pp. 34–39).

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** On a map with north at the top, the point halfway between north and east is…
a) south-east  b) north-west  c) north-east
**Answer:** c) north-east — the halfway points sit between their two main points (China, Grade 3, printed p. 2).
