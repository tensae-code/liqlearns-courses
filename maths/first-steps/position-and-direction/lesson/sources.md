**Acceptance count: taught in 9 countries (Kuwait, Bangladesh, Laos, North Macedonia, Pakistan, Yemen, Indonesia, China, Ghana).** Kuwait opens its Grade 1 course with position: over/under, above/below, right/left, then inside/between/outside (printed pp. 17, 20), and revisits position in space at Grade 2 (printed p. 17). Bangladesh carries the ordinals — first to tenth, counted from the front, the back, the left and the right (Class 2, printed pp. 34–39). Laos carries order as a named Grade 1 chapter (printed p. 22). North Macedonia carries the full set of pairs on one playground scene (printed p. 10) and then pair by pair, with movement (printed pp. 13–18). Pakistan carries the widest pair set in its Grade 1 geometry unit — inside/outside, above/below, over/under, far/near, before/after (printed pp. 116–126) — and the Primer's dog-and-kennel pages for left/right, in/out, up/down, front/back (printed pp. 134–135). Yemen carries relative positions inside its sorting-and-patterns unit (Grade 1 Part 1, printed pp. 18, 20). Indonesia carries position in its Grade 2 position-and-patterns chapter (printed pp. 101–124). China carries the eight compass directions, north-up maps and zoo-map routes at Grade 3 (Volume 2, Unit 1, printed p. 2). Ghana carries position and direction as teacher evidence only — step-by-step routes in the JICA B5 manual (Lessons 103–105) and the B6 manual (Lesson 93); Ghana's set holds no pupil textbook, so those rows are worded as teacher evidence wherever they are used.
