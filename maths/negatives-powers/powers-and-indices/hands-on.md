# Hands-on — Powers and Indices

The lesson opens powers out into repeated multiplication, the way every book opens them.

## The long way first
Write 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 out in full, then write it short: 2⁵. Do the
same for 3⁴, 5³ and 10⁶. Feel the saving: the short way exists because
the long way is tedious, and the index is only a count of the factors.

## The opening-out strip
Take a strip of paper. Write 2³ × 2² on it, then unfold the strip to
show 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 × 2, then fold it back to 2⁵. Do it again for
5⁴ ÷ 5², crossing out two fives. The laws are what you see the strip
do: multiply, add the counts; divide, take the counts away.

## The countdown to zero
Write the powers of 2 downwards: 2⁴ = 16, 2³ = 8, 2² = 4, 2¹ = 2.
Each step halves. The next step must be 1 — so 2⁰ = 1. Keep going
down: 2⁻¹ = 1/2, 2⁻² = 1/4. The pattern, not a rule from nowhere,
gives the zero and negative powers.

## Bracket or no bracket
Two piles of cards: (−2)² and −2². Work both out on paper and hold
up the answers: 4 and −4. Swap piles with a partner and check each
other. The brackets decide whether the minus is squared too.
