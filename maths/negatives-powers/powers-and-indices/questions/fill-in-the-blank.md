# Powers and Indices — fill in the blank

Fill the gap.

**1. (Level 1)** In 2³, the 2 is the ______ and the 3 is the ______.
**Answer:** base; index (or exponent).

**2. (Level 2)** aᵐ × aⁿ = a to the power ______.
**Answer:** m + n.

**3. (Level 3)** (−2)² = ______ and −2² = ______.
**Answer:** 4; −4 — the brackets decide whether the minus is squared.
