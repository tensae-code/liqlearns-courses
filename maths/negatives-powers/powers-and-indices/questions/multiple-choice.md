# Powers and Indices — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Powers As Repeated Multiplication · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Powers The Multiply and Divide Laws · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Powers Power of a Power, Zero and Negative Powers.

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** 2³ means ______.
a) 2 × 3  b) 2 × 2 × 2  c) 3 × 3
**Answer: b) 2 × 2 × 2** — three twos multiplied, which is 8.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** 2³ × 2² = ______.
a) 2⁵  b) 2⁶  c) 4⁵
**Answer: a) 2⁵** — same base, add the indices: 3 + 2 = 5.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** 5⁶ ÷ 5² = ______.
a) 5³  b) 5⁴  c) 5⁸
**Answer: b) 5⁴** — subtract the indices: 6 − 2 = 4.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** 2⁰ = ______.
a) 0  b) 2  c) 1
**Answer: c) 1** — any non-zero number to the power zero is 1.
