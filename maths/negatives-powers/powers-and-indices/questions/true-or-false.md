# Powers and Indices — true or false

Say true or false, and fix the false ones.

**1. (Level 1)** 2³ = 6.
**Answer: False.** 2³ = 2 × 2 × 2 = 8.

**2. (Level 2)** 2³ × 3² = 6⁵.
**Answer: False.** The bases differ, so the laws do not join them; 2³ × 3² = 8 × 9 = 72.

**3. (Level 3)** 5⁻¹ = 5.
**Answer: False.** 5⁻¹ = 1/5.
