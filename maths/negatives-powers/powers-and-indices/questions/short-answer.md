# Powers and Indices — short answer

Answer in a few words.

**1. (Level 1)** Write 625 as a power of 5.
**Answer:** 5⁴ (Egypt, Grade 7 Term 2, 1-1).

**2. (Level 2)** Work out (2³)².
**Answer:** 2⁶ = 64 — multiply the indices: 3 × 2 = 6.

**3. (Level 3)** Work out 2⁻³ as a fraction.
**Answer:** 1/8.
