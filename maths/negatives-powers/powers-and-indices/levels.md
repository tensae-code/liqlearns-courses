## Sub lesson 1 — Powers As Repeated Multiplication
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: write any repeated multiplication as a power, give its value for small bases, and read the base and the index apart.

## Sub lesson 2 — Powers The Multiply and Divide Laws
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: add indices when multiplying same-base powers, subtract when dividing, and refuse to join different bases.

## Sub lesson 3 — Powers Power of a Power, Zero and Negative Powers
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: multiply indices for a power of a power, give any zero power as 1, and turn a negative index into a reciprocal.
