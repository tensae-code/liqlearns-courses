# Video script — Powers and Indices

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Two times two times two. Long to write!
Short way: two to the power three. 2³.
The small number says HOW MANY twos. Three twos!

Same base, multiply? ADD the small numbers!
2³ times 2² is 2⁵. Count the twos: three and two is five!

Divide? TAKE AWAY the small numbers!
2⁵ divided by 2² is 2³.

A power of a power? MULTIPLY the small numbers!
(2³)² is 2⁶.

Anything to the power ZERO is ONE.
2⁰ is 1. 5⁰ is 1. Even 100⁰ is 1!

A MINUS power flips it over:
2⁻³ is one over 2³. One eighth.
Small number, big idea!
