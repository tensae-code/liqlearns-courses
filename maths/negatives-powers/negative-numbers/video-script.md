# Video script — Negative Numbers

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Below zero. That is all a negative number is!

Zero is the line we agree. Above it — plus.
Below it — minus. Minus five is colder than minus two.
Minus two is bigger. Nearer to zero is bigger!

Add two debts: minus three, minus four more.
Minus seven. Debts add up!

Take away a debt? Minus minus four — that is PLUS four.
Taking away a debt makes you richer!

Multiply: minus times minus is PLUS.
Minus times plus is MINUS.
Count the minuses: odd — minus. Even — plus.
Sign first. Then size. That is the whole game!
