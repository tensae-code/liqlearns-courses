# Negative Numbers — fill in the blank

Fill the gap.

**1. (Level 1)** Zero is neither ______ nor ______.
**Answer:** positive; negative.

**2. (Level 2)** 5 − (−4) = ______.
**Answer:** 9 — subtracting a negative is adding a positive.

**3. (Level 3)** (−2) × (−2) × (−2) = ______.
**Answer:** −8 — three negative factors, an odd count, so the answer is negative.
