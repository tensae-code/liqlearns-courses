# Negative Numbers — short answer

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Negative Numbers Below Zero and the Line · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Negative Numbers Adding and Subtracting · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Negative Numbers Multiplying and Dividing.

Answer in a few words, showing the sign first where it matters.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A diver is 3 metres below the surface. Write the number.
**Answer:** −3.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Work out −5 + 3, and say in one line why the answer is negative.
**Answer:** −2 — 5 beats 3, and 5 carries the minus, so the sum keeps the minus.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Work out (−12) ÷ (−3).
**Answer:** 4 — like signs give a positive quotient.
