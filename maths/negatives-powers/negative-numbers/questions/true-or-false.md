# Negative Numbers — true or false

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Negative Numbers Below Zero and the Line · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Negative Numbers Adding and Subtracting · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Negative Numbers Multiplying and Dividing.

Say true or false, and fix the false ones.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** −5 is bigger than −2.
**Answer: False.** −2 is bigger — it lies to the right of −5 on the line.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The book's printed "−3 + 4 = −1" is correct.
**Answer: False.** −3 + 4 = 1 (Ghana, JICA Basic 5, Lesson 79 — the printed answer is a mistake).

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** An even count of negative factors gives a positive product.
**Answer: True.**
