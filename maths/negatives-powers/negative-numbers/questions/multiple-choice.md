# Negative Numbers — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Negative Numbers Below Zero and the Line · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Negative Numbers Adding and Subtracting · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Negative Numbers Multiplying and Dividing.

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which number is below zero?
a) 5  b) 0  c) −3
**Answer: c) −3** — below zero means a minus sign.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which is bigger, −2 or −5?
a) −5  b) −2  c) They are equal
**Answer: b) −2** — the larger number lies to the right on the line; −2 is nearer zero (Kazakhstan, Grade 6, §2.5).

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** −3 + (−4) = ______.
a) 1  b) −1  c) −7
**Answer: c) −7** — add the sizes, keep the minus (Kazakhstan, Grade 6, §2.8).

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** (−7) × (−5) = ______.
a) −35  b) 35  c) −12
**Answer: b) 35** — two negatives give a plus (Kazakhstan, Grade 6, §2.13).
