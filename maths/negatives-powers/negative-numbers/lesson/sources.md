**Acceptance count: taught in 11 countries (Kazakhstan, Jordan, Libya, South Africa, Greece, Armenia, Vietnam, Ghana, Laos, Yemen, Indonesia).** Kazakhstan carries the fullest treatment — the agreed zero, the line, the bracket table, and all four operations with the Euler justification for two negatives. Jordan carries the first introduction (Grade 5, temperatures on the line) and the full integer unit a year later, taught with counting chips. Libya carries integers as a ten-lesson chapter in Grade 7, each sign case its own lesson. South Africa carries integers late, in Grade 7 Term 4, motivated by temperature and money. Greece carries positives and negatives as a whole chapter in Grade 7. Armenia carries negatives in Grade 6, including negative fractions. Vietnam carries integers in Grade 6, with negative fractions in Bài 23. Ghana carries only teacher-manual evidence (JICA Basic 5, Lesson 79), including one flagged wrong printed answer that this lesson teaches against. Mauritania is not in this lesson: its Grade 6 teaches no negatives and its 1AS teaches none either — the honest limit is stated, not filled. Laos carries negative numbers whole in Grade 6 — integers and the number line, addition and subtraction, sign rules for multiplication and division, and distance and absolute value with equations and inequalities. Yemen carries negative numbers from integers in Grade 7 — comparing and all four operations — to rational numbers in Grade 8, on the number line and in decimal form. Indonesia carries negative numbers whole in Grade 7 — integers on the number line with all four operations in both the Merdeka and the K13 Semester-1 editions.
