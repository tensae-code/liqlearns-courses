# Roots and Surds — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Level 1)** √144 = ______.
a) 12  b) 14  c) 24
**Answer: a) 12** — 12² = 144 (Egypt, Grade 7 Term 2, 1-3).

**2. (Level 1)** ∛729 = ______.
a) 9  b) 27  c) 81
**Answer: a) 9** — 9³ = 729.

**3. (Level 2)** √32 in basic form is ______.
a) 8√2  b) 4√2  c) 2√8
**Answer: b) 4√2** — 32 = 16 × 2, and √16 = 4.

**4. (Level 3)** √2 × √3 = ______.
a) √5  b) 6  c) √6
**Answer: c) √6** — roots multiply under one root.
