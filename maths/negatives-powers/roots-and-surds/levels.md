# Roots and Surds — questions by level

Practice by level, tied to the skill number. The questions live in
the type files; this file is the by-level view. The levels are the
depths inside this one thing — square and cube roots first, then surds in basic form, then operating on surds and rationalising.

## Level 1 — square and cube roots
Level 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: give square and cube roots of perfect powers, name both roots of a perfect square, and estimate a root that is not exact.

## Level 2 — surds in basic form
Level 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: pull the biggest perfect square (or cube) out of a surd and write it in basic form, checking by squaring back.

## Level 3 — operating on surds and rationalising
Level 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: add, subtract and multiply surds exactly, and rationalise a denominator.
