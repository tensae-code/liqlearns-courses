1. (Sub lesson 1) The book's opening question is: "Does every 0 show the same ___?"
   **Answer: start** — Turkey, *Matematik 7. Sınıf Ders Kitabı, 1. Kitap* (Grade 7), printed p. 15.

2. (Sub lesson 2) Mass and length have a ___ starting point: their zero means none of the quantity.
   **Answer: natural** — Turkey, Grade 7, printed p. 16.

3. (Sub lesson 3) On the Celsius scale, zero is the temperature at which ___ freezes — a mark people chose.
   **Answer: water** — Turkey, Grade 7, printed pp. 15–16.

4. (Sub lesson 3) Values below a chosen zero are written with a ___ sign.
   **Answer: minus (negative)** — Turkey, Grade 7, printed p. 16 (the sign itself is introduced on printed p. 18).

5. (Sub lesson 4) Kelvin's zero is called ___ zero, because no temperature can lie below it.
   **Answer: absolute** — Turkey, Grade 7, printed p. 34.
