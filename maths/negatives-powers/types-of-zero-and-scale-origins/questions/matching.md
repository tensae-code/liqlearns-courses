# Matching — Types of Zero and Scale Origins

1. (Layers 1–3) Match each reading to the kind of zero its scale carries:
   (a) 0 kg on a scale
   (b) 0 °C on a thermometer
   (c) 0 MB of data left
   (d) the year 0 on a history timeline
   (e) 0 m of ribbon

   (i) a chosen zero — values continue below it
   (ii) a natural zero — none of the quantity

   **Answer: (a)–(ii), (b)–(i), (c)–(ii), (d)–(i), (e)–(ii)** — Turkey, *Matematik 7. Sınıf Ders Kitabı, 1. Kitap* (Grade 7), printed pp. 15–16.

2. (Layer 4) Match each temperature zero to its description:
   (a) Celsius zero
   (b) Kelvin zero

   (i) absolute — the complete absence of heat; nothing is colder
   (ii) relative — the chosen mark where water freezes; colder temperatures exist below it

   **Answer: (a)–(ii), (b)–(i)** — Turkey, Grade 7, printed p. 34.
