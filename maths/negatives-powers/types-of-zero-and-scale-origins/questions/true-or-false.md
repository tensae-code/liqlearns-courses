# True or false — Types of Zero and Scale Origins

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Layer 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Zeros The Question of the Four Zeros · (Layer 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Zeros Natural — Zero Means None of It · (Layer 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Zeros Chosen — A Mark People Picked · (Layer 4) -> Sub lesson 4 — Zeros Absolute Against Relative. Two items carried range markers: the matching item marked (Layers 1–3) is marked Sub lesson 3 — Zeros Chosen — A Mark People Picked, the part where the natural/chosen sorting is completed; the ordering item marked (Layers 2–4) is marked Sub lesson 4 — Zeros Absolute Against Relative, the part it culminates in.

1. (Sub lesson 1) Every scale's zero means "none of the quantity."
   **Answer: False** — that holds only for scales with a natural starting point; chosen zeros are marks mid-scale. Turkey, *Matematik 7. Sınıf Ders Kitabı, 1. Kitap* (Grade 7), printed pp. 15–16.

2. (Sub lesson 2) There can be a mass of −2 kg.
   **Answer: False** — mass has a natural zero: 0 kg is no mass, and nothing lies below it. Turkey, Grade 7, printed pp. 15–16.

3. (Sub lesson 3) 0 °C means there is no temperature.
   **Answer: False** — 0 °C is the chosen mark at which water freezes; temperatures continue below it, to −9 °C in the book's own city readings. Turkey, Grade 7, printed pp. 15–16, 19.

4. (Sub lesson 3) Negative numbers are needed exactly on scales whose zero is chosen, because values below the mark are real but are not "none of it."
   **Answer: True** — that is the book's route into the integers. Turkey, Grade 7, printed pp. 15–16.

5. (Sub lesson 4) A temperature of −5 K is possible on a very cold day.
   **Answer: False** — Kelvin's zero is absolute, the absence of heat itself; no temperature lies below 0 K. Turkey, Grade 7, printed p. 34.

6. (Sub lesson 4) Celsius's zero is a relative zero and Kelvin's zero is an absolute zero.
   **Answer: True** — the book's performance-task contrast. Turkey, Grade 7, printed p. 34.
