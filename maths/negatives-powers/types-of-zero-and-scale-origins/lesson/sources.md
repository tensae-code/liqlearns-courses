**Acceptance count: taught in 1 country (Turkey).**

This lesson exists because Turkey's Grade 7 book teaches the kinds of zero as named, developed material: its integers theme opens by asking whether every 0 shows the same start and classifying quantities by whether they have a natural starting point (printed pp. 15–16), motivates the negative numbers from quantities with no natural origin (printed p. 18 — stocked in the negative-numbers lesson), and returns to the contrast in a performance task on absolute versus relative zero, Celsius against Kelvin (printed p. 34). The course's negative-numbers lesson teaches working below zero and carries an agreed-zero part about sign conventions, but no read book classifies scales by the kind of zero they carry, and no other country in the course teaches the Celsius/Kelvin absolute-zero contrast. Under the everything-goes-in ruling (2026-11-06) the topic gets its own lesson, built at the size of its source.

Turkey enters through the pupil's textbooks of its Millî Eğitim Bakanlığı (MEB — Ministry of National Education), all in Turkish, first volumes (1. Kitap) are not in hand and are cited nowhere.
