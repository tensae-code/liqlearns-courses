**Acceptance count: taught in 8 countries (Kazakhstan, Egypt, Greece, South Africa, Sierra Leone, Libya, Laos, Indonesia).** Egypt carries the fullest lesson — scientific notation for large and small numbers, conversion, ordering and operations, all on real data (planet masses, the Earth–Sun distance, the hydrogen atom, the bee). Kazakhstan carries standard form inside its powers chapter in two Grade 7 books, with word problems and sums in standard form. Greece carries it twice in Grade 7 — large numbers, then large and small together. South Africa carries scientific notation as one worksheet in Grade 8. Sierra Leone carries it as three JSS 3 lessons with an explicit rule of thumb for when to use it. Libya carries it as one section in Grade 8. Jordan is not in this lesson: its Grade 8 Term 1 book is not held, so no standard-form page can be pinned from Jordan. Armenia, Vietnam, Ghana and Mauritania are not in this lesson on the filed stock. Laos carries standard form through powers of 10 and scientific-notation forms in Grade 7, used again as the stated prerequisite form A × 10ⁿ in Grade 8. Indonesia carries standard form only as guide evidence — integer exponents, radicals and standard form in the Grade 8 Merdeka teacher guide (Bab 1), with no student book in hand teaching it; the guide's own nanometre and Earth–Sun-distance slips in that chapter are never taught as fact.
