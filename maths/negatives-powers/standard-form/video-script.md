# Video script — Standard Form

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

One hundred forty-nine million, six hundred thousand kilometres!
Too long to write! Too easy to miscount the zeros!

Short way: 1.496 times ten to the power 8.
1.496 × 10⁸ km. The point moved EIGHT places!

Big numbers: the power is PLUS. How many places did the point move?
That is your power!

Small numbers: the power is MINUS.
0.004 is 4 times ten to the power MINUS 3.
The point moved three places the other way!

Compare? Look at the POWER first!
Ten to the 6 beats ten to the 5 — always!
Then look at the first number. Easy!
