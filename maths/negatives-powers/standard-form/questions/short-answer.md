Answer in a few words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Write 230 (a bee's wingbeats per second) in standard form.
**Answer:** 2.3 × 10².

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Write 0.0000007 in standard form.
**Answer:** 7 × 10⁻⁷.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Write 10 × 10⁵ in proper standard form.
