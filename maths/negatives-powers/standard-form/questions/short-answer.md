# Standard Form — short answer

Answer in a few words.

**1. (Level 1)** Write 230 (a bee's wingbeats per second) in standard form.
**Answer:** 2.3 × 10².

**2. (Level 2)** Write 0.0000007 in standard form.
**Answer:** 7 × 10⁻⁷.

**3. (Level 3)** Write 10 × 10⁵ in proper standard form.
**Answer:** 1 × 10⁶ — 10 is not allowed as the first number.
