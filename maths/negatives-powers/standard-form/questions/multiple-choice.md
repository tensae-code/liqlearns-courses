# Standard Form — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Standard Form Big Numbers · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Standard Form Small Numbers · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Standard Form Converting, Comparing and Choosing.

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** 149,600,000 km in standard form is ______.
a) 1.496 × 10⁸ km  b) 14.96 × 10⁷ km  c) 149.6 × 10⁶ km
**Answer: a) 1.496 × 10⁸ km** — the first number must sit between 1 and 10 (Egypt, Grade 7 Term 2, 1-2).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** 1.5 × 10⁷ as an ordinary number is ______.
a) 150,000  b) 1,500,000  c) 15,000,000
**Answer: c) 15,000,000.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** 0.004 in standard form is ______.
a) 4 × 10³  b) 4 × 10⁻³  c) 0.4 × 10⁻²
**Answer: b) 4 × 10⁻³.**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Which is biggest: 3 × 10⁵, 2 × 10⁶, 9 × 10⁴?
a) 3 × 10⁵  b) 2 × 10⁶  c) 9 × 10⁴
**Answer: b) 2 × 10⁶** — the power decides first.
