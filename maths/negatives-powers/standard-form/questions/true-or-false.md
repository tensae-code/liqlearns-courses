# Standard Form — true or false

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Standard Form Big Numbers · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Standard Form Small Numbers · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Standard Form Converting, Comparing and Choosing.

Say true or false, and fix the false ones.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** 25 × 10³ is in standard form.
**Answer: False.** The first number must be under 10: 2.5 × 10⁴.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Small numbers take negative powers of ten.
**Answer: True.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** 9 × 10⁴ is bigger than 1 × 10⁵.
**Answer: False.** 10⁵ beats 10⁴, so 1 × 10⁵ is bigger.
