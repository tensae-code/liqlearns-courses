# Standard Form — fill in the blank

Fill the gap.

**1. (Level 1)** In a × 10ⁿ, the first number a must satisfy 1 ≤ a < ______.
**Answer:** 10.

**2. (Level 2)** 1.67 × 10⁻² as an ordinary number is a decimal with the point moved 2 places to the ______.
**Answer:** left.

**3. (Level 3)** Sierra Leone's rule of thumb: use standard form for numbers under 0.001 or over ______.
**Answer:** 10,000.
