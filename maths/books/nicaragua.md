## The set at a glance

- **Two series run in parallel.** Grades 1–6 are the primary
  **NICAMATE 2** series (2024–2026, *Libro de Texto* + *Guía
  Metodológica*, JICA/UNAN-Managua), with the older **Versión
  Validada / PROMETAM** books still held for Grades 1–3 (old
  student books for Grades 2–3, old teacher guides for Grades
  1–3) and a 2019 **Adecuación Curricular** guide for Grade 5.
  Grades 7–11 are the secondary **NICAMATE** series (2019,
  *Libro de Texto* + *Solucionario*), with the parallel
  **Módulo Autoformativo** distance-learning series (2016–2017,
  *Secundaria a Distancia en el Campo*) held for Grades 7, 8
  and 10.
- **Grades 1–4 (NICAMATE 2):** numbers to 100,000, addition and
  subtraction with carrying/borrowing, multiplication tables
  and multi-digit multiplication, division to two- and
  three-digit divisors, decimals to thousandths, first
  fractions (Grade 4 only), angles in degrees with a protractor,
  triangles/quadrilaterals, area of rectangles/squares,
  bar charts and two-way tables. The old G2/G3 books add
  time (clocks, elapsed time), statistics, lines/angles,
  money, capacity in litres and weight — much of it dropped
  or re-sequenced in the 2025 rewrites.
- **Grades 5–6 (NICAMATE 2):** decimal ×/÷ (by naturals, then
  decimal by decimal), fractions through multiplication and
  division, ratio/percent/proportion and the rule of three,
  area of parallelograms/triangles/trapezium/rhombus/circle,
  volume of prisms/cubes, polygons and symmetry, multiples/
  divisors/LCM/GCD, prime factorisation, combinatorics
  (arrangements/combinations), line graphs and averages.
- **Grades 7–8:** signed numbers, algebra (expressions,
  first-degree equations, polynomials, 2×2 systems),
  direct/inverse proportion as functions y = ax / y = a/x,
  linear functions (slope, intercepts, domain/range),
  radicals and real numbers, congruence/proof (ASA/SSS/SAS),
  parallelograms, solids (surface area and volume). The
  modules add in depth: divisibility/LCM/GCD, integer powers,
  scientific notation, SI units and commercial arithmetic
  (simple interest, discount, compound rule of three) in
  Grade 7; notable products, factorisation and algebraic
  fractions, and circle theorems, in Grade 8.
- **Grades 9–11:** notable products and factorisation,
  quadratic equations (completing the square is the main
  method; the general formula is a regular lesson, its proof
  a Desafío) and quadratic functions (vertex form, max/min
  on intervals), similarity/Thales/Pythagoras, circle angles,
  descriptive statistics (tables/graphs only); then
  probability, trigonometry (functions, graphs, inverse
  functions, identities, equations, sine/cosine laws),
  3×3 systems with matrices/determinants/Cramer and a
  cryptography application, piecewise/absolute-value/
  greatest-integer/square-root/rational functions, solids
  including the regular polyhedra; then sequences
  (arithmetic/geometric, finite sums, sigma notation),
  powers/roots/rational exponents, exponential and logarithmic
  functions and equations, analytic geometry (lines, circles),
  conics (parabola, ellipse, hyperbola, all centred at the
  origin), and counting with conditional probability.
- **Language:** Spanish throughout, every book. Publisher
  throughout: Ministerio de Educación (MINED), República de
  Nicaragua, with JICA technical assistance (NICAMATE 2 and
  NICAMATE) and PROSEN/EU support (modules, Grade 10 student
  book). **No ISBN was found printed in any of the 26 files.**
- **Lesson shape:** primary NICAMATE 2 books use
  *Problema → Ejemplo → Ejercicios* with the *PO*
  (*planteamiento de operación*) written before calculating,
  *Recordemos* openers, *Repaso y Mini prueba* per section,
  *Practiquemos lo aprendido* and a timed *Prueba de Unidad*
  (25 min, /10), plus answers and mental-calculation annexes.
  Secondary NICAMATE books use one lesson per page with
  *Problema → Solución → Conclusión → Ejemplo → Ejercicios*,
  *Comprobemos lo aprendido* reviews and a *Solucionario*.
  The modules are expository self-study texts with worked
  examples, *Actividades* and end-of-unit *Autoevaluación*
  (no answers anywhere). The old PROMETAM books use lettered
  activities with PO + R, worked in the pupil's notebook,
  and M:/RP: scripted teacher guides.
- **Answer keys:** the NICAMATE 2 student books carry
  *Practiquemos* answers in their annexes; the NICAMATE 2
  guides carry the unit-test answers; the G7/G8/G9/G11
  student books carry a full *Solucionario*. The G10 student
  book has NO answer key. The three modules have NO answer
  key anywhere. The old G3 guide has no answer key of any
  kind; the old G2 guide answers only its complementary
  unit, and only in part ("Se omite la solución" throughout).

## Grade 1, NICAMATE 2 student — g01-student (2024; 170 PDF pp.; printed = PDF − 10)

Title as printed: *Matemática — Primer grado — NICAMATE 2*
(*Libro de Texto, Matemática, Primer Grado*). Authors Armando
José Huete Fuentes, Marlon José Espinoza Espinoza, Primitivo
Herrera Herrera (UNAN-Managua); coordinator Juan Carlos Salgado
Andino; JICA assistance. Front matter: *Presentación* (PDF p.5,
GRUN / Plan Nacional de Educación 2022–2026, "Continuidad de
los Aprendizajes de primero a tercer grado"); credits (PDF p.3);
letter to pupils (PDF p.7); *Índice* (PDF pp. 8–11). Structure:
9 units of *Secciones*/*Contenidos*; each unit opens with
*Recordemos*, closes sections with *Repaso y Mini prueba*,
ends with *Practiquemos lo aprendido* and a *Prueba de Unidad*.
Lessons follow *Problema → Ejemplo → Ejercicios* with the *PO*
written before calculating; pupils work in notebooks. Annexes:
*Respuestas de Practiquemos lo Aprendido* (printed pp. 154–156)
and *Ejercicios de Cálculo Mental* (pp. 157–160, four timed
drill sheets of 30 items each, /30).

- Unit 1: Números hasta 10 (pp. 2–31): numbers 1–5, then 6–10;
  comparing/ordering to 10, the number line; ordinal numbers
  1st–10th; forming numbers (composition/decomposition to 10).
- Unit 2: Suma (1) (pp. 32–47): adding as grouping and
  adding-on; sums with total ≤ 10; sums equal to 10; sums with
  zero; making addition problems; number cards.
- Unit 3: Resta (1) (pp. 48–67): subtraction as taking away
  and as comparing; subtracting from 10; subtraction with zero;
  making problems; number cards. Answers annex e.g. "PO:
  7 − 2 = 5; R: 5 bombones".
- Unit 4: Cuerpos geométricos y líneas (pp. 68–75): solids
  (curved/flat parts, grouping by shape); straight and curved
  lines, open and closed lines. Shortest unit (8 pp.).
- Unit 5: Números hasta 20 (pp. 76–89): 11–15, then 16–20;
  forming numbers to 20 (tens and ones); counting by intervals;
  number line to 20; order 0–20.
- Unit 6: Suma (2) (pp. 90–109): sums to 18; commutativity
  ("changing the order" does not change the sum); mental
  calculation; number cards.
- Unit 7: Resta (2) (pp. 110–123): subtraction with minuend
  to 18; mental calculation; making problems.
- Unit 8: Números hasta 100 (pp. 124–145): units and tens;
  30–59, then 60–99; the number 100; number line to 100;
  comparing two-digit numbers.
- Unit 9: Longitud (pp. 146–153): comparing lengths directly
  and indirectly; measuring with body units — the book itself
  names *jeme*, *brazada*, *paso*, *pulgada* (PDF pp. 158–159)
  — and with objects (counting squares/clips).

Negatives: no multiplication, division, fractions or decimals;
no money; no time (no clock reading); no capacity or weight;
no data/statistics; no angles; no area or perimeter; no
plane-figure naming beyond the solids/lines of Unit 4
(triangles, squares, rectangles, circles are NOT named as
figures here — the old guide's complementary unit teaches
them, but this book does not). No ordinals beyond 10th; no
numbers beyond 100; no subtraction with borrowing; no
standard length units (no centimetre/metre — body and object
units only).

Slips: no typos or wrong answers spotted; the answers annex
was checked against the visible *Practiquemos* answers and
the arithmetic is consistent. Held copy complete, cover to
last mental-calculation sheet (printed p. 160, PDF p. 170).

This is the first book of the NICAMATE 2 series and its
foundation: the only book in Band A that teaches ordinals
(1st–10th), the only one that teaches length with body units
only, and the shortest student book in the band. Its
*Recordemos / Repaso y Mini prueba / Practiquemos / Prueba
de Unidad* structure, the *PO* convention and the answers +
mental-calculation annexes are apparatus the old series
did not have.

## Grade 1, NICAMATE 2 teacher guide — g01-teacher-nicamate2 (2024; 210 PDF pp.; printed = PDF − 6)

Title as printed: *Guía Metodológica de Matemática, 1er grado*
(GM1; NICAMATE 2). Companion to g01-student — covers exactly
its 9 units in the same order and structure. It adds method,
not topics: lesson plans, board plans, expected learning,
materials, answers.

- Introduction (printed pp. 2–15): I. Introducción; II.
  Estructura del Libro de Texto; III. Estructura de la Guía
  Metodológica (each unit: (1) *Competencia*, (2) *Secuencia
  de Aprendizaje*, (3) *Puntos Esenciales*, (4) *Ejemplos de
  Plan de Pizarra y Cuadernos*); IV. Propuesta de Plan Anual
  (p. 7, **120 hours total, March–November**: U1 24 h, U2 14 h,
  U3 15 h, U4 7 h, U5 9 h, U6 17 h, U7 14 h, U8 14 h, U9 6 h);
  V. P/S/C/E lesson method (pp. 8–10): **P — Problema**,
  **S — Solución**, **C — Conclusión (Resumen)**,
  **E — Ejercicios**, with at least 20 minutes of active
  learning per lesson; the teacher is facilitator; pupils
  check their own solutions and, if wrong, write the correct
  solution without erasing errors; homework daily, checked
  periodically, self-marking cultivated; VI. Facilitation
  points (p. 11); VII. Use of unit tests (p. 13); VIII.
  Multigrade example (p. 15, two or more grades at once).
- Unit introductions prescribe: U1 essentials — counting by
  one-to-one correspondence in three stages (concrete →
  semi-concrete: bottle caps/*tapitas*, mark cards, circles →
  abstract numeral); zero's three meanings (1: no elements in
  a set, taught here; 2: empty place in decimal numeration;
  3: reference point on the number line — meanings 2 and 3
  come in Units 5 and 8); cardinals vs ordinals;
  decomposition/composition of 10. U2 — addition's two
  meanings: joining (*agrupando*) and adding-on (*agregando*).
  U6 — the make-a-ten strategy (12 as 10 and 2; "how many
  remain after making the group of 10?"; worked 12 + 4 oranges).
  U8 — a group of ten is a *decena*, each single one a
  *unidad*; the difficulty of "0" in round tens like "40";
  36 vs 63 (same digits, different numbers by position).
  U9 — non-standard length units *jeme*, *brazada*, *paso*,
  *pulgada*, squares on squared paper, paper clips.
- Lesson pages (printed pp. 16–203): every student-book page
  gets a spread headed "No. X / N" with *Aprendizaje esperado*,
  *Materiales* and scripted **P1/P2** prompts with **S**
  (guided solution). Unit tests are timed: *Prueba de Unidad 9*
  is 25 minutes, /10 (p. 203).
- Annex (printed p. 204): *Respuestas de Pruebas de Unidad*
  for all 9 unit tests (e.g. Unit 6: "PO: 5 + 7 = 12; R:
  12 patos"), keyed with LT/GM page cross-references.

Negatives: no answer key for the *Repaso y Mini prueba*
items or the mental-calculation annexes — only the unit tests
are answered here (the *Practiquemos* answers are in the
student book's annex). No guidance for early finishers beyond
homework. No inclusive-education section (unlike the G2–G4
guides, which add section IX *Educación Inclusiva*); no
separate *Plan de clase* template section (added in G2–G4
as section VII).

Slips: **index slip** — the index (PDF p.6) lists the
introduction sections as I, II, III, IV, V, **IV**, VII, VIII:
"IV. Puntos importantes…" (p. 11) should be numbered **VI**;

answers annex (printed p. 204, PDF p. 210); there is no
PDF p.211 (the filing's "empty trailing page" claim was

This guide is the template-setter but the thinnest
