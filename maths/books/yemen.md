## How this filing was made — read this first

- This is a **contents-complete filing, not a paragraph-by-paragraph
  read**: every cover, title page, foreword/introduction, contents
  list, unit opener and tail page was read visually from rendered
  pages; at least one lesson page per unit was read visually; and
  whole-file page scans were run where stated (Band C: render + ink
  check of all 1,316 pages). The Arabic text layers are broken or
  absent (Band C's is a broken custom-font encoding; Band B's returns
  only the watermark on most pages; six of the seven Band A files are
  image-only), and full-page Arabic OCR runs several minutes per
  dense page, so body pages were NOT individually transcribed.
  What is filed per lesson is its title, its printed start page, and
  a short content note from the contents list plus sampled pages of
  the same unit. No lesson is invented: where a detail comes only
  from a contents title, it is filed as a title, not as verified body
  content. No textbook passage is transcribed — summaries only, with
  only short terms quoted.
- **261 anchors were verified on exact rendered pages** by three
  independent citation checks (one per band), with **0 not found**.
  The 16 corrections are all folded into this filing and listed under
  each band's slips.
- Printed-page rules are verified per file and stated per file; they
  differ (PDF − 1, PDF − 2, printed = PDF, and the G12 science
  student book's skipped printed 163). All page numbers below are
  PRINTED pages unless marked "PDF". For the G12 science student
  book, lessons from 6-4 onward and all of Unit 7 are cited by the
  book's own contents/PDF number, not by footer (footer reads one
  higher — see File 9).
- Citation rule: [Yemen, Mathematics, Grade N, Part P (and track
  where applicable), printed p. X, year/edition as printed on that
  file's own cover/title page].

## Band A cross-notes (for course building)

- **Number progression:** G1: 0–10, then 11–20, then to 100 (no
  regrouping). G2-P2: to 1000, +/− with carrying/borrowing,
  estimation. G3: to 10,000, +/− with carrying/borrowing across
  places, rounding to 10/100/1000. G4: to 999,999, rounding (تدوير),
  estimation of all four operations, ×/÷ up to three-digit ×
  three-digit and six-digit ÷ two-digit.
- **Multiplication/division fact progression (do not re-order):**
  G2-P2: ×2, ×5, ×3, ×4 (in that order), commutativity, ÷2, ÷5, ÷3+÷4
  combined, fact families. G3-P1: ×0, ×1, then new facts only for ×6,
  ×7, ×8, ×9 (×2–×5 assumed known). G3-P2: ÷6, ÷7, ÷8, ÷9, ÷1 and ÷
  by itself, two-digit ÷ one-digit. G4: facts assumed; work is
  multi-digit algorithms + divisibility rules (2, 5, 10, 3) +
  estimation.

  printed; no numerator/denominator terms). G3-P2: reading/writing,
  equivalence, comparing/ordering, +/− with equal denominators.
  G4-P2: equivalence, simplifying, comparing/ordering, improper
  fractions and mixed numbers, +/− where one denominator divides the
  other; then decimals (+/− only).
- **Geometry/measurement progression:** G1: shape names, half/quarter;
  length/weight/capacity compared, day parts, week days, coins. G2-P2:
  length/capacity/weight measured, money, clock reading, time
  estimation, Hijri months, area (title only). G3-P1: solids, polygons,
  symmetry, lines, segment drawing, angle defined, transformations.
  G3-P2: length/capacity/weight units (m, cm, L, mL, g, kg), clock,
  perimeter, area, volume. G4-P2: angle types, perpendicular/parallel
  lines, rectangle/square properties and drawing, triangle types and
  congruence, perimeter (shapes, rectangle, square), area (units,
  rectangle, square), Hijri and Gregorian calendars.
- **Statistics/probability progression:** G2-P2: sorting, pictograms,
  tally tables, bar charts, reading bar data, certain/impossible/more

- **What Band A never teaches (across all 7 files):** negative numbers;

## Band B cross-cutting notes (for the courses merge)

1. **Progression spine, Grades 5–9:** whole numbers to billions →
   fractions (ordinary, then decimal) → ratio, percentage, proportion
   → integers → algebra (terms, expressions, factorisation) →
   equations (one variable, then two variables, then second-degree)
   → sets/relations/functions → geometry (angles, triangles,
   congruence, similarity, Thales) → measurement (area, surface area,
   volume, sphere) → trigonometry (right-triangle only) → circle
   theorems → transformations (reflection, translation, rotation,
   enlargement) → statistics (pictorial → columns/lines/pies → mean →
   mode/median/cumulative frequency).
2. **Repeats that are NOT duplicates:** prime factorisation / GCD / LCM
   appear at number level (G5P1), then again inside algebra (G8P1
   factorisation, G9P1 algebraic LCM); roots appear at whole-number
   level (G5P1), rational level (G8P1) and inside powers (G6P1, G7);
   distance/midpoint appear axis-parallel only (G8P1) then generally
   (G9P2); volume of pyramid/cone appears in G7 and again inside G8P2's
   solids unit. File each at its own level.
3. **Religious-content applications:** zakat as a percentage application
   (G6P1, 4-5, printed 136) and inheritance as proportional division
   (G6P2, 5-7, printed 43). These are maths lessons with religious
   contexts, not religion lessons.
4. **Edition mixing inside the band (cite per file, never per band):**
   2027/1448 — G5P1, G7, G8P1, G9P1. 2026/1447 — G5P2, G6P2, G8P2.
   2017/1438 — G6P1, G9P2.
5. **What a reader might expect but the band never teaches:**
   probability (nowhere in Grades 5–9); quadratic functions as graphs;
   sine/cosine rules; logarithms; vectors/matrices; formal proof beyond
   congruence/similarity cases and Thales. If a course needs those,
   they must come from Grades 10–12, not from this band.

## Band C cross-file summary — what Band C teaches, by track (do NOT merge tracks)

- GRADE 10 (common year, Parts 1–2 + workbook): logic, mappings,
  powers/roots, polynomials, algebraic structures (incl. groups),
  equations/inequalities, trigonometry (right-triangle only), coordinate
  geometry + transformations, vectors, Σ notation and descriptive
  statistics. No functions unit, no logarithms, no calculus, no
  probability.
- GRADE 11 SCIENCE (Parts 1–2 + workbook): rings/fields, real
  functions, sequences, logarithms (via exponential function),
  limits/continuity/differentiation, matrices/determinants (+ inverses,
  solving systems), circle geometry, solid geometry (planes, parallels),
  trigonometry (compound/double/half angles, trig equations, triangle
  solving), statistics (correlation, regression) + probability (single
  closing lesson). No vectors, no complex numbers, no integration in
  Grade 11 science.
- GRADE 11 LITERARY (full-year + workbook): sequences (with separate
  sum lessons), logarithms, matrices/determinants, introductory calculus
  (limits/continuity, derivatives, applied problems). No trigonometry,
  vectors, complex numbers, integration, probability or conics.
- GRADE 12 SCIENCE (full-year + workbook): complex numbers (incl. polar
  form, powers/roots, quadratics), counting/binomial theorem,
  probability (full unit: theorems, models, conditional probability,
  binomial law, sampling), conic sections (parabola, ellipse, hyperbola,
  axis translation/rotation), solid geometry (perpendiculars, dihedral
  angle), differentiation (trig/log/exp/implicit/chain, Rolle/mean-value,
  maxima-minima, curve sketching), integration (definite, indefinite,
  substitution, parts, rational functions, areas, volumes of revolution).
- GRADE 12 LITERARY (full-year + workbook): counting (permutations,
  combinations), statistics (review, correlation/scatter, regression),
  basic integration (indefinite, definite, area applications). No
  probability unit, no differentiation unit in this volume, no complex
  numbers, conics or solid geometry.
