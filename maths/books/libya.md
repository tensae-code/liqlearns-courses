## SET VERIFICATION — the English precedent does NOT repeat here

Libya's English record had Grade 4 files that were really Grade 3 books under Grade 4 covers. That was explicitly tested for in this band and **no misfile was found**:

- Every cover's grade matches its contents and its first lesson (e.g. the file recorded as Grade 4 pupil Pt 1 opens on numbers to 100,000 with place value to ten-thousands; the file recorded as Grade 3 pupil Pt 1 opens on numbers to 10,000; Grade 2 on numbers to 1,000; Grade 1 on sets and numbers to 10).

## Band A progression summary (for the merger)

- **Number range:** G1 to 100 → G2 to 1,000 → G3 to 10,000 → G4 to 100,000. Place value is re-taught at each new range with blocks and tables.
- **Operations:** G1: add/sub to 20, multiplication and division introduced as repeated addition / sharing. G2: regrouped add/sub to 1,000; tables 2, 3, 4, 5, 10. G3: add/sub to 10,000; tables 6–9 (completing 2–10); multi-digit × 1-digit; division with remainders; two-step problems; mental maths by rounding. G4: rounding, estimation, factors, multiples; ×/÷ by 1- and 2-digit numbers; fraction and decimal arithmetic; no new whole-number add/sub chapter.
- **Fractions:** none in G1 → parts of a whole, comparing/ordering in G2 → numerator/denominator, equivalents, comparing in G3 → add/subtract (same and different denominators), mixed/improper, fraction of a set, bar-model problems in G4.
- **Decimals:** first and only in G4 (tenths, hundredths, thousandths; compare, order, convert with common fractions; add, subtract, multiply, divide, estimate).
- **Measure:** G1 non-standard comparison (length, mass) → G2 metres/centimetres, kilograms/grams, litres → G3 kilometres, millilitres, conversions, add/subtract measures → G4 measures and money only as ×/÷ applications.
- **Money:** G1 recognition → G2 dinars/dirhams, conversion (1 د = 1000 درهم), add/subtract → G3 conversion, counting, add/subtract → G4 ×/÷ with money.
- **Time:** G1 hour/half-hour → G2 minutes, am/pm, elapsed time → G3 conversions, add/subtract, seconds, other units → no new time chapter in G4.
- **Geometry/data:** G1 shapes/patterns, ordinals → G2 pictograms, segments, surfaces, circle halves/quarters → G3 bar charts, angles, right angle, area/perimeter → G4 protractor angles to 180°, perpendicular/parallel, quadrilaterals/triangles, compound area/perimeter, symmetry, solids, volume.
- **Assessment structure:** every workbook carries 4 numbered reviews per grade (Reviews 1–4, numbered continuously across its two parts), plus challenge and problem-solving pages in Grade 4 workbooks; Grade 1 workbook Pt 1 alone carries the monthly pupil assessment card.

## Cross-band notes (Grades 5–9 as one run)

- **Progression.** Numbers: whole numbers to 10 million and rounding (G5) →
  integers, HCF/LCM, squares and roots (G7) → standard form (G8). Fractions:
  similar/dissimilar and mixed numbers (G5) → full fraction arithmetic and
  rational/irrational numbers (G7) → algebraic fractions (G9). Decimals: ×/÷ by
  powers of ten (G5) → full decimal arithmetic and significant figures/estimation
  (G7). Ratio/percentage: ratio (G5) → ratio and proportion (G6) → three
  percentage chapters (G6) → ratio, proportion, percentages, financial
  transactions, standard form (G8). Algebra: none (G5) → letters as numbers and
  simplifying (G6) → expressions and collecting terms (G7) → equations (G8) →
  expansion, factorisation, algebraic fractions, simultaneous equations, coordinate
  geometry (G9). Geometry: triangle area, angles, constructions (G5) → angles,
  solids/nets, circles, sectors, composite area/perimeter (G6) → angle types and
  relations (G7) → parallel-line angles, constructions, triangles/polygons,
  plane and solid mensuration (G8) → polygons, symmetry, congruence/similarity,
  pyramid/cone/sphere mensuration (G9). Statistics: mean and rate (G5) →
  representation only (G8) → mean, median, mode (G9).
- **Pythagoras is absent from the whole band.** No Grade 5–9 book in this band
  teaches Pythagoras' theorem, even though Grade 9 teaches congruence and
  similarity in depth. Trigonometry is likewise absent, and probability is absent
  from all five grades. Course builders should not assume these topics from the
  Libya sequence at this stage; they must come from other countries' books or a
  later Libya stage.
- **Money and measures stay concrete and Libyan:** dinar prices, Tripoli–Ghadames
  trips, local names (Rami, Fatima, Tariq, Tawfiq, Hani, Haroun, Hilal, Marwan,
  Khairi). π is 3.14 and 22/7 in Grade 6; Grade 8's π note is the conceptual
  treatment.
- **Series break between Grade 6 and Grade 7** (Friends Love Mathematics primary
  series vs the Singapore-adapted secondary series) is visible in layout, lesson
  routine, bilingual titles, and the notation reference page that only Grades 7–9
  carry. The Grade 7 introduction explicitly frames the secondary series as three
  books for the second stage of basic education plus three for secondary.
- **Workbooks are practice companions, not textbooks.** The Grade 5 and 6
  workbooks add no new topics; their value for courses is worked-practice
  structure (challenge questions, non-routine problem solving, reviews) rather
  than new content.

## Band-C gaps summary (what these eight books do NOT cover)

Across Grades 10–12 as a whole: no complex numbers anywhere; no
three-dimensional geometry or 3D vectors; no differential equations;
no discrete mathematics beyond Grade 11 sequences; no financial
mathematics beyond the Humanities index-numbers chapter; no
programming or numerical methods. Statistics splits by track and
never recombines: Humanities pupils get descriptive statistics
(Grade 11) then applied statistics (Grade 12: correlation, index
numbers, time series, population) but no probability or inference;
Science pupils get descriptive statistics plus correlation/regression
(Grade 11) then probability and inference (Grade 12) but no index
numbers, time series or population statistics. Calculus is Science
only, starting in Grade 11 and completed in Grade 12; Humanities
pupils get no calculus in this band.
