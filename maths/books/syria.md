**What is taught, chapter by chapter** (printed page ranges; lesson = teaching pages plus its activity pages)

- Ch. 1 الأنماط — Patterns (pp. 3–6). L1 patterns from two elements (AB), L2 patterns from three elements (ABC): continue, complete the missing element, build your own pattern from a stated rule. Patterns use pictures, shapes, colours and sound (clap/stamp).
- Ch. 2 استكشاف الأعداد إلى العدد ٩ — Exploring numbers to 9 (pp. 7–22). Numbers in pairs: 1 and 2 (p. 7), 3 and 4 (p. 9), 5 and 6 (p. 11), then 7, 8 and 9 together (p. 13) — numeral, word, counting pictured groups, tracing/writing, completing a group to the target number, dot pictures of the number. Zero (الصفر) as the number of an empty group (p. 16). Bigger and smaller (الأكبر والأصغر) by one-to-one matching of two groups (p. 18). Equality (المساواة): the = sign introduced in word form (٦ عناصر تساوي ٦ عناصر) and symbol form ٣ = ٣ (p. 20). Activities after each lesson; a join-the-dots 1–9 fun page (p. 22).

- Ch. 4 الأعداد حتى ١٢ — Numbers to 12 (pp. 33–47). The number 10 as a full ten-frame (p. 33); components of 10 (p. 35); ascending and descending counting 1–10 and back to 0 (p. 37); numbers 11 and 12 as ten plus 1 or 2 (p. 40); components of 11 and 12 (p. 42); bigger and smaller within 12 (p. 45).
- Ch. 5 مفاهيم الجمع والطرح — Concepts of addition and subtraction (pp. 48–62). Exploring addition: the + (زائد) and = signs, the addition sentence (عبارة الجمع), sums to 10 (p. 48); horizontal and vertical addition (p. 51); exploring subtraction by crossing out: the − (ناقص) sign, the subtraction sentence (p. 54); horizontal and vertical subtraction, including ٥ − ٥ = ٠ (p. 56); solving problems choosing + or − (p. 59). A kite game of +/− results (p. 62) closes the chapter's activity pages (pp. 61–62).
- Ch. 6 مسائل ومخطط إلى العدد ١٢ — Problems and the number line to 12 (pp. 63–75). Addition is commutative (p. 63); adding a number to itself — doubles to ٦ + ٦ (p. 65); using the number line in addition (p. 67) and in subtraction (p. 69); solving problems (p. 71); ordinal numbers first to tenth (الأعداد الترتيبية, p. 73); a ferris-wheel page of choosing the + or − sign to complete a sentence (p. 75) closes the chapter.
- Ch. 7 الهندسة — Geometry (pp. 76–84). Solids (المجسمات): cube, pyramid, sphere, cuboid, cylinder, cone, matched to everyday objects; which solids roll (pp. 76–78). Plane shapes as faces of solids: rectangle, square, triangle, circle (pp. 79–81). Congruence (التطابق): congruent = same shape and same size (pp. 82–83). The book closes with a trace-and-draw activity (p. 84).

**Contents-page defect (confirmed).** The contents page (printed p. 2) gives wrong start pages for Chapters 1 and 2 only; from Chapter 3 onward every entry matches the body exactly. Contents vs actual lesson-start folio: Ch. 1 — 8 vs 3, 10 vs 5 (contents +5). Ch. 2 — 13 vs 7, 15 vs 9, 17 vs 11, 19 vs 13, 22 vs 16, 24 vs 18, 26 vs 20 (contents +6). Ch. 2's listed range even overlaps Ch. 3's listed entries, so the contents is internally inconsistent as printed. All 25 contents entries for Chapters 3–7 were checked against the body and are exact.

**Honest negatives.** This book does NOT teach: any number above 12; place value or the names "ones/tens"; the symbols < and > (comparison is in words only, by matching and counting); multiplication or division in any form; fractions (they start in Semester 2); money; time or the clock; any measurement (length/mass come in Semester 2); data handling or graphs; symmetry; negative numbers; written column addition with carrying (vertical setting-out is present, all sums ≤ 12 without regrouping).

**Confirmed book errors: none.** Every printed worked answer that appears in the book was recomputed or recounted during the read (worked bonds, ten-frame counts, addition/subtraction sentences with printed results, number-line examples, the twelve ferris-wheel sign items on p. 75) and all are correct. Two exercise-design observations, neither of which makes any printed statement false: (1) printed p. 28 — in the "join two groups that make 9" matching, the six groups count 4, 3, 6, 3, 4, 5, so only two pairs can be formed (4+5, 3+6) and two groups are necessarily left over; (2) printed p. 61 — the equal-result card matching has seven cards, so one card is necessarily left over.

**Held-copy defects.** None found: the copy is complete, 84 pages, ending with the closing drawing activity on printed p. 84; all pages legible. The contents misprint above is a defect of the edition as printed, not of this copy.
