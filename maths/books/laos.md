## Method note

Stated honestly, per band, because the three bands were not read the
same way:

- **Band A (Grades 1–4):** the new-series books (2016 curriculum) are
  born-digital PDFs with a clean Lao Unicode text layer; they were read
  from the extracted text, checked against page renders at covers,
  contents, chapter starts and last pages. The old-series books carry
  no usable text layer (their Lao fonts have no ToUnicode map), so
  they were read visually from renders — covers, title/imprint pages,
  approval letters, full contents, the first page of every lesson,
  and last pages — plus targeted body-page checks and, for the two
  old student books, a contact-sheet sweep of the full body with
  folios checked in order. Body pages of the old guides were not
  translated line by line; their contents, lesson starts and sampled
  plans were.
- **Bands B and C (Grades 5–12):** the text layers are legacy-Lao-font
  glyph salad — character counts look rich but yield no readable Lao —
  so these books were mapped visually from contents pages plus
  lesson/plan openers and sampled body pages, NOT line-by-line text
  reading. Lesson titles below are the readers' English paraphrases
  of the printed Lao titles, not transcriptions.
- **The backstop is the citation check:** 497 anchors, each verified
  on an exact rendered page (never on extracted text alone), across
  all 28 distinct works. Where a filing claim could not be located on
  its cited pages, it is carried below marked UNVERIFIED BY CHECK,
  not silently dropped and not presented as verified fact (see
  Grade 8, lessons 14–15).
- No word-for-word transcription: summaries and structural notes
  only, very short quoted fragments at most. No story text or long
  passage is copied anywhere in this file.

## Proven gaps (whole set)

- No ISBN on most books (see "Languages, publisher, editions" — only
  the four Grade 1–2 new-series books print one).
- No grade lacks a guide or a student book: every grade G1–G12 holds
  both a student book and at least one teacher guide (Grades 1, 2
  and 4 hold two guides each, old + new series; Grade 1 holds its
  student book twice). No grade is guide-only or student-only.
- No new-series Grade 3 material at all: Grade 3 exists only as the
  2009 old-series pair, complete (student book + guide) but from the
  pre-2016 curriculum.
- Grade 6 guide structure: 30 plans for 31 lessons — its plan 30
  covers both student-book lesson 30 (percentages) and lesson 31
  (inverse proportion), with objectives split in two parts. Not a
  missing page; a design difference from the strict 1:1 guides.
- Grade 7 guide predates its student book by a year: guide title page
  2010, student book title page 2011 (same four authors, same
  51 lessons, 1:1 plans). A dating difference, not a content error.
- Grade 11 guide regrouping: the exponential-functions summary (student
  book lesson 20, closing Chapter 4) opens Chapter 5 in the guide
  (guide p. 133), so guide Chapter 5 runs lessons 20–26. Placement
  difference only; the lesson content is identical.
- Guide-only additions (no student-book counterpart): the Grade 12
  guide's closing UbD-style test-design section (printed pp. 214–220);
  the Grade 10 guide's unnumbered closing landscape mindmap page for
  the logic chapter. Neither adds mathematics.
- Content ceilings, proven grade by grade below and summarised here:
  no multiplication/division in Grade 1 (new series); no division in
  Grade 2 (new series); no decimals before Grade 4; no negative
  numbers before Grade 6; no simultaneous equations before Grade 8
  (and no trigonometry, vectors or probability in Grade 8); no
  probability before Grade 11 (Grade 9 statistics stops at
  variance/standard deviation); no logarithms/exponentials before
  Grade 11; no matrices, complex numbers or differential equations
  before Grade 12; no integral calculus taught as a topic in Grade 12
  (differential equations are taught without a preceding integration
  chapter in that volume; integrals were Grade 10's Chapter VIII).
