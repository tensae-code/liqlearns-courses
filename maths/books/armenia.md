**The course, in outline:** Grades 1–4 run a Mathematics series
(Mkrtchyan Grade 1; Arakelyan/Beghlaryan Grade 2; Harutyunyan Grades
3–4) with a methodical guide per grade except Grade 2, which has no
guide in the record. Grades 5–6 run Gogyan's Mathematics 5–6
("Participating School"/Qarakusi), built on the segment-pictured
"parts" method before any algebra, with the Mathemyan family and the
cricket Trchkan as recurring characters. Grade 7 splits into Gogyan's
Algebra 7 (expressions, polynomials, sets, statistics, linear
functions, short multiplication formulas) and Aghekyan's Geometry 7,
the first proof-based course (definitions, axioms, theorems,
constructions). Grade 8 continues Mikayelyan's Algebra 8 (reals,
rational fractions, inequalities, quadratics, linear systems,
modulus/root equations, probability) and Aghekyan's Geometry 8
(quadrilaterals, area, Pythagoras, similarity, right-triangle
trigonometry, coordinates and vectors). Grade 9 continues Mikayelyan's
Algebra 9 (functions, polynomials, rational equations, quadratic and
rational inequalities, progressions, probability/statistics) and
Hakobyan/Ghazaryan's Geometry 9 (circle, triangle-solving
trigonometry, regular polygons, revision). Grade 10 runs Aghekyan's
Geometry 10 (stereometry: lines, angles, planes, polyhedra — the
core-programme book) and Arakelyan's Algebra & Analysis 10 (reals,
powers/roots, functions, trig functions, probability). Grade 11 runs
Hakobyan's Geometry 11 (solids of revolution, vectors and coordinates
in space) and Gevorgyan/Sahakyan's Algebra & Analysis 11 in two
streams (general/humanitarian, 129 pp.; natural-mathematics, 202 pp. —
the fuller version of the same course). Grade 12's held student book
is Aghekyan's single-topic core-programme book (mathematical logic
only). Alongside the older books sit NEW-FORMAT teacher's guides
(the "AB generation" lesson-plan format: lesson counts, aims and
outcomes, pair work, summative assessments per section) — Grade 10
Chapters 1–2 and semester-2 Chapters 3–5, Grade 11 Chapters 1–2, and
Grade 12 semester 1 — whose new-programme student books are not held,
except that the Grade 11 Chapters 1–2 guide serves both held stream
books' first two chapters.
