### 4. Structure summary

- **Rationale (printed pp. iii–iv):** argues for a shift away from computation-only teaching, citing cheap calculators, computer technology and the EduTech 2000 project; stresses child-centred alongside teacher-centred approaches, problem solving as the focus of instruction, reasoning over memorised rules, manipulatives moving pupils from concrete to pictorial to abstract, links to other subjects and everyday life, calculators and computers as tools, multi-media instruction, and multi-faceted assessment. The document is presented as a pilot-and-consultation text that will be modified from teacher feedback.
- **General Objectives (printed p. v):** six objectives for the whole primary syllabus — acquire mathematical techniques and skills; awareness of accuracy in computation; awareness of mathematics in the environment; apply mathematics to daily-life problems; think logically, creatively and critically; use technology to explore mathematical situations. - **Format of the Syllabus (printed pp. vi–vii):** explains that the document contains the Scope and Sequence, the Attainment Targets, and the syllabus body with Suggested Activities and Assessment Procedures; that technology integration and critical/creative/decision-making skills are highlighted; that the Scope and Sequence marks, for each topic, the class where it is begun and the classes where it is maintained and developed; that Attainment Targets state what a pupil should achieve by the end of the year, cumulative over lower classes; and that assessment should be continuous and varied (tests, portfolios, journals, discussions, mathematics profiles).

### 8. Document errors and inconsistencies — Class 1

1. **Duplicated objective (printed p. 14):** the Measurement/Linear objective "Estimate, measure and compare lengths of various objects using non-standard units" is printed **twice in succession** in the body. The second occurrence was almost certainly meant to read "standard units" (the Attainment Targets, printed p. 6, carry both the non-standard and the standard versions), so as printed the body never states the standard-units length objective in its own words.
2. **Attainment Targets omit Set Theory:** the AT section (printed pp. 4–8) has target groups for problem solving, number operations, fractions, measurement, geometry and data handling, but **no target group for strand 6.0 Set Theory**, although the strand has S&S items and body objectives.

4. **Body objectives with no S&S or AT counterpart:** lines of symmetry and open/closed curves (Body p. 16) appear in neither the Scope and Sequence (strand 5.0 lists only 5.0.1–5.0.3) nor the Attainment Targets; ordinal numbers and Roman numerals likewise have body objectives but no S&S numbered item.
5. **Scope/sequence symbol key not machine-readable in this copy:** the "begin" and "maintain/develop" markers in the Class 1 S&S are graphical symbols that do not survive text extraction; the per-class begin/maintain pattern for Class 1 topics was therefore read from the chart layout, and the identical chart in the Class 2 document (where the markers do extract) corroborates introduction at Class 1 for the strands listed.
6. Minor: the body table header is misprinted "ACTITIVES" for "Activities" throughout; cover uses "CLASS I" where the contents and body use "Class 1".
