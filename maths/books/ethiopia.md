## The set at a glance

- **Two eras, one spine.** Grades 1–8 are the regional-bureau primary/middle
  books (© 2015 / 2015 E.C.): every student book is an **Amhara National
  Regional State Education Bureau** book, every teacher guide an **Addis
  Ababa City Administration Education Bureau** book — a bureau split that
  explains most of the companion mismatches below. Grades 9–12 are the
  federal **Ministry of Education** books (First Published August 2023,
  GEQIP-E, printed by GRAVITY GROUP IND LLC, Sharjah), student book and
  guide by the same authors in each grade.
- **Language:** Amharic throughout Grades 1–6, English throughout Grades
  7–12. All filings in this document are in English; Amharic book content
  is summarised in English, with only very short quoted fragments.
- **Grades 1–4:** numbers to 1,000,000, add/subtract with carrying and
  borrowing, multiplication tables and multi-digit multiplication,
  division to 1,000,000 with remainders, unit fractions then
  same-numerator fractions and decimals to hundredths, Ethiopian
  (Ge'ez) numerals, the Ethiopian calendar, non-standard then standard
  measure, rectangle perimeter/area, pictographs then bar graphs,
  patterns with variables. No decimals until Grade 4; no division until
  Grade 3; no fraction symbols until Grade 3.
- **Grades 5–6:** fractions through all four operations, decimals,
  percent (with trade tax, discount and simple interest in Grade 6),
  variables and equality sentences by substitution, divisibility/HCF/LCM,
  scientific notation, linear equations and inequalities by substitution,
  direct/inverse proportionality, coordinates (plotting only), angles
  with a protractor, circle parts, data with mean/mode/median and pie
  charts. No negative numbers until Grade 7; no formal equation solving
  by inverse operations.
- **Grades 7–8:** sets, integers, ratio/proportion/percent with
  profit/loss, simple and compound interest and taxes, linear equations
  and inequalities with graphs, rational numbers, squares/roots/cubes,
  similarity and triangle theorems (Euclid, Pythagoras), circle theorems,
  surface area and volume of solids, probability of simple events.
  No simultaneous or quadratic equations; no trigonometry; no functions
  or sequences; no statistics in Grade 8.
- **Grades 9–12:** sets, the number system through reals and surds,
  systems of linear equations, quadratics, inequalities (linear, systems,
  absolute-value, quadratic), trigonometry (ratios, then functions,
  identities, equations), regular polygons, congruency/similarity,
  2-D vectors, statistics and probability; then relations and functions,
  polynomials, exponentials and logarithms, trigonometric functions,
  circles, solid figures, coordinate geometry; then functions and
  inverses, rational expressions, matrices, determinants, vectors with
  dot/cross products, plane transformations, grouped statistics,
  counting/permutations/combinations/binomial theorem; then sequences
  and series, calculus (derivatives and introductory integration),
  dispersion/sampling statistics, graphical linear programming and
  business mathematics. Calculus only in Grade 12; matrices and
  determinants only in Grade 11; no complex numbers and no differential
  equations anywhere in the set.
- **Teacher guides are half-detailed throughout.** In Grades 1–8 every
  guide gives detailed lesson notes and answer keys for its first three
  units/chapters only, then summary outcome tables for the rest; in
  Grades 9–12 the guides mostly companion fully, with the exceptions
  recorded per book below (Grade 9 renumbering, Grade 10 §7.5 missing,
  Grade 11 §7.5 teaching missing, Grade 12 period lines missing for
  three units).
- **Confirmed book errors:** 10 in Band A (Grade 2 student 4, Grade 2
  guide 3, Grade 3 student 1, Grade 4 student 1, Grade 4 guide 1),
  plus in Band B the Grade 6 student inequality misprint, the Grade 7
  student contents and exercise slips, the Grade 8 student interest-line
  typo, and in Band C the Grade 9 quadratic-inequality blanks, the
  Grade 9/10 guide slips, the Grade 11 matrix and statistics slips and
  the Grade 12 guide wire typo. Every confirmed error is carried below
  with its correct value, to be taught corrected, never as fact.
- **Held-copy defects are recorded per book below;** the sharpest are
  the Grade 2 student book's broken body text layer (read visually from
  renders of all 191 pages), the Grade 1 guide's garbled second half
  (printed pp. 45–81 sampled, not read word by word), and the
  image-only Grade 7 student and Grade 11/12 books (contents-complete
  filings from rendered pages, not page-by-page text reads).

## Grade 1, student — g01-student (© 2015 E.C.; 182 PDF pp.; printed = PDF − 10)

Clean, picture-heavy Grade 1 book: numbers to 100, add/subtract to
10, time, shapes, non-standard measure, money; ends mid-exercise at
printed p. 172. No arithmetic errors found. Printed pages 1–172 plus
front matter (contents i–iv, introduction pp. v–vi). Every lesson is
built the same way: a learning-outcomes page followed by exercise
page(s); every chapter ends with game time and a chapter summary
exercise.

- **1. Numbers to 20 — pp. 1–24.** Counting 1–5 (p. 2), 6–9 (p. 4),
  reading and writing 1–5 (p. 6) and 6–9 (p. 12), zero (p. 17),
  numbers 10–15 (p. 19), 16–20 (p. 21). Each number gets its own page
  with the numeral, the word and counting pictures.
- **2. Comparing numbers to 20 — pp. 25–40.** Ordering and comparing
  to 10 and to 20; pairing and separating numbers to 10 — number bonds
  (p. 34).
- **3. Addition to 10 — pp. 41–64.** Addition stories and sentences,
  adding zero, making 4–10 by adding, column addition, adding in any
  order (commutativity), counting on, making 10, doubles.
- **4. Subtraction to 10 — pp. 65–84.** Subtraction stories and
  sentences, subtracting zero and subtracting everything, subtracting
  from 4–10, column subtraction, counting back, the
  addition–subtraction relationship.
- **5. Time — pp. 85–96.** Order of activities, whole hours, half
  hours, days of the week.
- **6. Numbers to 100 — pp. 97–120.** Counting by tens, estimating in
  tens, counting to 100 by ones, skip counting, tens and ones, place
  value (worked example p. 108), comparing and ordering to 100.
- **7. Two-digit numbers: addition and subtraction — pp. 121–136.**
  Adding/subtracting tens, 1-digit to/from 2-digit numbers, two
  2-digit numbers, estimating sums and differences (round to the
  nearest ten, then subtract).
- **8. Geometry shapes — pp. 137–148.** Solid shapes, faces of solids,
  solid/flat relationship, sides and vertices of flat shapes.
- **9. Measure — pp. 149–164.** Comparing length, non-standard units
  of length, comparing mass/weight, non-standard units of mass,
  comparing capacity, non-standard units of capacity.
- **10. Ethiopian money — pp. 165–172.** Ethiopian santim coins,
  counting amounts of money.

## Gaps

**Set-wide proven gaps — no grade in Ethiopia teaches these:**
- Complex numbers — nowhere in Grades 1–12.
- Differential equations — nowhere in Grades 1–12.
- Probability distributions (normal, binomial distribution as a
  topic) — nowhere; the binomial *theorem* is taught in Grade 11,
  but not the binomial distribution.

**Grade-bound topics (taught in one grade only):**
- Calculus (derivatives + introductory integration) — Grade 12 only.
- Matrices and determinants — Grade 11 only.
- Sequences and series (incl. infinite series, convergence/
  divergence) — Grade 12 only.
- Linear programming (graphical, two-variable only; no simplex) —
  Grade 12 only.
- Vectors with dot/cross products — Grade 11 only (2-D basics in
  Grade 9).
- Transformations of the plane (translation, reflection, rotation
  only) — Grade 11 only as a topic (sliding/reflecting/turning
  appears in Grade 3 geometry as an early form).
- Logarithms — Grade 10 only (exponents in Grade 9; no logs before
  or after as a standalone topic).
- Functions as a standalone topic — Grades 10–11 only (relations
  and functions in Grade 10; relations, inverses, composition in
  Grade 11).
- Coordinate geometry — Grade 10 only (plotting points in Grade 6;
  graphs of linear equations in Grades 7–8).
- Circle theorems — Grades 8 and 10 only.
- Trigonometry — Grade 9 (acute-angle ratios + table) and Grade 10
  (functions, identities, equations); no trig in Grades 11–12 beyond
  what Grades 9–10 covered, and no sine/cosine rules for general
  triangles anywhere.
- Statistics: mean only in Grade 5; mean/mode/median in Grades 6–7;
  no statistics in Grade 8; variance/standard deviation in Grade 9;
  no statistics in Grade 10; central tendency only in Grade 11;
  dispersion/frequency curves/sampling in Grade 12. No quartiles, no
  box plots, no correlation anywhere.

**Band A gaps (Grades 1–4 never touch):** percentages,
ratio-and-proportion, negative numbers, powers-and-indices,

fraction symbols until G3; no decimals until G4; no angles measured

**Band B gaps:** Grade 5 — no negatives, powers/roots, inequalities,
circles, symmetry as its own topic, Pythagoras, coordinates,
