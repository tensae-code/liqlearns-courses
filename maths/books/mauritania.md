# Mauritania — Maths books

**Mauritania is CLOSED in the library, and its maths set is 32 files
in hand of 32 recorded — 23 student books + 2 workbooks + 7 teacher
guides / pedagogical documents, Grades 1–13, all IPN Mauritania.**
The 32 held files were read and filed below in four bands on
2026-11-05: **4,880 PDF pages** — Band A (Grades 1–3 + Cycle-2
document) 1,334, Band B (Grades 4–6 + Cycle-3 document) 804, Band C
(1AS–4AS, Grades 7–10) 987, Band D (5AS–7AS streams, Grades 11–13)
1,755. Every band's filing then passed a separate citation check
(checker ≠ reader): **511 anchors checked in total — 489 passed on
the stated page, 22 image-verified, 0 corrected, 0 not found**
(Band A 127: 105 passed + 22 image-verified; Band B 96: 96 passed;
Band C 80: 80 passed; Band D 208: 208 passed). The per-band counts
stand in the check block at the end of each band below. Where this
header and a band section disagree, the section wins: the header was
cross-checked against the sections, and the sections carry the
per-book evidence.

**Reconciliation — 32 of 32, no owed file, no extra file.** The
queue row said "32 in hand (25 student + 7 teacher)"; the staging
records hold exactly 32 Mathematics rows for gov/mauritania, and
all 32 files were grabbed direct from the official source URLs in
those records (IPN digital library Koutoubi, files served from
docs.bsimr.com) — no GitHub route was needed, no file failed, and
every file's cover/title page was checked against its record row.
The kind recount differs only in presentation: the queue row folds
the 2 workbooks into the student count (25 student-side), while
this filing counts them separately — 23 student books + 2
workbooks (cahiers d'exercices, Grades 1–2) + 7 teacher guides /
pedagogical documents = 32. Every file is a distinct work: no
duplicate scans and no parallel-edition pairs were found.

**Languages.** Fondamental Grades 1–6 student books, workbooks and
guides are Arabic. Secondaire 1AS–4AS (Grades 7–10) are French.
The streamed 5AS–7AS books are French for the M, D, C and LM
tracks and Arabic for the LO (lettres originales) track — the LO
books are Arabic originals for that stream, not translations of the
French books. The two cycle training documents are Arabic.
Lesson language in the filings below is English translation; the
books' own languages are noted per book.

**Page rule.** Body printed page = PDF page throughout the set
(cover = p. 1), verified on body footers across the books — so
citations below give one number, the PDF/body page. EXCEPTIONS are
in the books' own contents listings, never in their bodies, and are
flagged wherever they occur: the 6AS-D contents run 2 pages short
throughout; the 6AS-LO contents run 2 pages short at chapter
starts; the 7AS-LO contents ranges are stale from an earlier
pagination (its contents page itself sits on PDF p. 8 while
listing itself as p. 3); the 1AS guide's contents run 2 pages
short except chapter 6; the 6AS-C contents list chapters 10 and 11
one page early; the Grade 2 contents list the relative-positions
lesson at p. 7 while its body lesson sits on p. 17; the Grade 3
contents list the rectangle at p. 33 while its body lesson sits on
p. 34. Citations in this filing use body pages throughout.

**The two duplicated guide rows — settled, both genuine and
distinct.** The record lists two guides under Grade 2 and two under
Grade 5; neither pair is a duplicate record. Under Grade 2: (a) the
genuine Grade-2-only guide (212 pp., week-by-week Grade 2 lesson
plans), and (b) a teacher-training document for the whole second
cycle, Grades 2–4 (310 pp.) — filed as A7. Under Grade 5: (a) the
genuine Grade-5 guide (102 pp., 2025), and (b) a teacher-training
document for the third cycle, Grades 5–6 (137 pp.) — filed as B5.
Neither training document is a grade guide: the Cycle-2 document
contains NO prepared lessons for Grade 2 itself (its prepared
lessons are Grades 3 and 4 only), and the Cycle-3 document is NOT
maths-only (it trains mathematics AND natural sciences) — only its
mathematics axis is filed in this maths read.

**The "plain" upper-secondary books are distinct track books, not
duplicates.** The 5AS plain book (M, 178 pp., 15 chapters) is not
the 5AS série D book (104 pp., 12 chapters): the M book adds
transformations géométriques, arithmétique dans ℕ and géométrie
dans l'espace. The 6AS plain book (M, 192 pp., 14 chapters) is not
the 6AS série C book (212 pp., 15 chapters): the C book adds a full
Géométrie dans l'espace chapter and longer treatments. The 6AS-C
avant-propos wording ("séries M") against its C filing is a wording
slip in the book, not evidence of duplication.

**Guide runs ahead of its student book (proven).** The Grade 2
guide's week 16, day 5 revision (p. 100) works with numbers
121–130 — ordering, decomposition of 123 into hundreds/tens/ones,
operations 37 + 90, 100 + 30, 113 + 13, a school problem (56 girls +
73 boys), inside/outside geometry and centimetre-ruler measurement
— while the held Grade 2 student book tops out at 99. Either the
guide's later weeks teach beyond the held student edition, or the
held student book is a shorter edition than the guide's programme.
Three-digit numbers and hundreds place value in Grade 2 are
therefore guide-only evidence in this set, not student-book
evidence.

**Held-copy defects.** Most newer IPN PDFs are AES-256 encrypted
with an empty user password (print yes, copy no) but open and
extract normally. The Grade 1 student book is scanned page images
with no usable text layer (read from page images). The Grade 1/2
guides and workbooks use a legacy Arabic font encoding whose
extracted text is garbled though the books print normally (read
from page images and week tables where the text layer fails).
Several French secondary PDFs have font-encoding artefacts in
extraction (titles verified against printed contents pages); the
Grade 2 student book's text layer is fragmented scan-OCR (missing
glyphs, control characters), so its anchors use fragment tokens
and one rendered page. No book is missing its final section; no
file is truncated.

**What the set does NOT cover (honest negatives, per band).**
Grade 1: no column addition/subtraction, no multiplication, no
numbers above 20 as objects of study, no fractions, no ruler
units. Grade 2 student book: no division, no multiplication beyond
concept/table, no fractions, time only morning/evening and days of
week. Grade 3: no fractions/decimals, division only two lessons
(concept + division by 10), no area/perimeter. Grade 4: no
fractions/decimals, no area formulas, no degree measure. Grade 5:
no fraction division as an operation, no decimal
multiplication/division beyond natural-number operations applied
to decimals, no volume. Grade 6: no negative numbers, powers/roots,
algebra with unknowns, or statistics beyond proportionality/
percentage. 1AS: no negatives, algebra, Pythagoras, Thales or
functions. 2AS: no Pythagoras/Thales, functions, trigonometry or
probability. 3AS: no logs/exponentials, sequences, derivatives,
integrals, complex numbers or matrices. 4AS: no functions beyond
affine, sequences, logs, calculus, complex numbers, matrices,
statistics chapter, or space geometry beyond pyramid/cone. The
streamed books' negatives are per-book in Band D — notably 6AS-LM
has no geometry chapter at all, and 7AS-LM has no geometry or
trigonometry chapter, with limits only inside the functions
chapter.

  - Doubles and halves: p. 110 — double of 1 is 2, of 2 is 4, of 3 is
    6, of 4 is 8, with halves alongside (chocolate bars to halve,
    double/halve tables, a sharing word problem: 20 cards shared

    4 years?"), a 30-ouguiya fruit purchase (8 + 6 + 11), solids
    (cylinders, cubes) to colour and name, and plane shapes to build

    subtraction as meanings (p. 120) — adding things together and
    taking away, with a marble problem (6 + 4 won), a dates problem
    (10 − 4 eaten), and computations 4 + 3, 6 + 3, 9 + 2, 11 + 9,
    7 − 5, 10 − 5, 9 − 3; and price comparisons (p. 130) — cheaper
    than / more expensive than (أرخص/أغلى) with prices in ouguiya

  - Measurement woven through: longer/shorter, nearer/farther,
    lighter/heavier, more/less; time: day/night, yesterday/today/
    tomorrow; money: coins and small purchases; equality/inequality
    and the idea of addition and subtraction.
- Negatives (honest): no written column addition or subtraction; no
