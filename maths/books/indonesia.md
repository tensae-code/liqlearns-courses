## Method note

- Band A (Grades 1–4): full text extraction plus a per-page
  first-lines pass over every page of every book, with full-page reads
  of front matter, every chapter opener, and every end-of-chapter test
  page. Grades 1–2 are picture-heavy, so the text captures prompts,
  instructions and worked examples, not the pictures themselves. The
  Grade 4 pair's decorative "=" / "÷" glyphs come first in extraction
  order, so its pass used symbol-filtered first-lines.
- Band B (Grades 5–8): full reads from clean text layers, with 30+
  pages rendered at 150 dpi to confirm suspected slips (notably the
  Grade 8 K13 student book's 54 slips). The G7/G8 filings on disk are
  the corrected versions written by a finisher: the original Band B
  reader's G7/G8 filings contained verifiable errors (pool-tiles
  misread, cover quirk, chapter spans, slip counts, a garbled
  function-machine description), corrected in place and documented in
  a closing note of each filing.
- Band C (Grades 9–12): full text-layer read — front matter,
  contents, every chapter opener, all headings, summaries/tests, end
  matter, plus targeted body reads — not a literal word-by-word visual
  read of all 3,888 pages. The text layers are clean; the only pages
  read visually were the garbled-formula pages (Grade 12 Revisi
  binomial, printed pp. 115–117), garbled contents pages, the graphic
  cover of the Grade 11 TL student book, and near-empty figure pages,
  all noted in the relevant filings.
- The 679 exact-page anchor checks (separate checkers, checker ≠
  reader) are the backstop for all three bands.
