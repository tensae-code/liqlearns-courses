## Band A — Saudi Arabia, Mathematics, Grade 1 (three books read in full)

Reader filing, Band A of the Saudi Arabia mathematics read. Books: `pdfs/g01-p1.pdf`, `pdfs/g01-t2.pdf`, `pdfs/g01-t3.pdf`. All summaries are my own words; no passages are transcribed beyond short titles/phrases.

**Method and honesty note.** The books are Arabic throughout, with text layers of varying quality. The main reading was done from the extracted text layer page by page (every page of all three books), which is reliable for lesson titles, lesson ideas, vocabulary lists, and the printed teacher instructions that summarise each exercise. Where a lesson's two pages are a worked page plus a practice page in the book's fixed pattern, both pages were read.

**Record labels corrected by the books' own pages.** The held records called g01-p1 "Part 1 / full-year", g01-t2 "Term 2", and g01-t3 "Term 3". #### Honest negatives — what this book does NOT teach
No subtraction anywhere. No place value (no tens/ones work; numbers stop at 20, built by counting). No number-line addition or counting-on strategy (that is Part 2, Ch.8). No skip counting. No measurement, no mass or capacity. No money. No named plane shapes or solids and no fractions (shapes appear only as sorting material). No time or calendars. No data tables or picture graphs — sorting is the only data-like work. No estimation. Addition is capped at making 12; there is no addition of two-digit numbers and no carrying.
