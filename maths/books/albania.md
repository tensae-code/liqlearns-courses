### (b) Structure
An Albanian adaptation of the Oxford International Primary Maths 1 teacher's guide. The introduction (printed pp. 6–8) explains the fixed five-step lesson shape used throughout: Diskuto (Discuss) / Zbulo (Discover) / Praktiko (Practise) / Unë tani di – Provov veten (I now know – Test yourself) / Kontrollo njohuritë (Check knowledge). Every lesson cites the pupil's book by page ("Libri i nxënësit, faqe …") and reproduces the pupil page as a thumbnail; many lessons end with photocopiable resource sheets (Fletë burimore). Twelve chapters, contents at printed p. 5: 1 Numrat dhe numërimi (p. 9); 2 Të zbulojmë numrat (27); 3 Përbërësit e numrave (48); 4 Mbledhja (59); 5 Zbritja dhe ndryshesa (68); 6 Numrat çift dhe tek (86); 7 Të numërojmë dhe të llogaritim me afërsi (104); 8 Shumëzimi dhe pjesëtimi (128); 9 Matjet (143); 10 Figurat dhe trupat gjeometrike (161); 11 Koha (179); 12 Paraqitja e të dhënave (195). Each chapter closes with Unë tani di (revision) and Provov veten (test yourself) sheets.

### (c) Unit-by-unit teaching record
- Ch. 1 Numrat dhe numërimi (pp. 9–26): counting to 20 and beyond, zero, number tracks and lines, ordering, one more/one less; simple totals such as 9 + 3 + 4 = 16 in counting contexts.
- Ch. 2 Të zbulojmë numrat (pp. 27–47): place value (15 = 1 ten and 5 ones), two-digit numbers from digits (2 and 6 make 26 or 62), ordinal and money contexts (ten 1q coins exchange for one 10q coin).
- Ch. 3 Përbërësit e numrave (pp. 48–58): number bonds for 6–10 (pairs for 8 listed in full 0+8 … 8+0), making 10 lekë from 1L and 5L coins.
- Ch. 4 Mbledhja (pp. 59–67): addition strategies — commutativity, associativity, making 10 first, counting on (+2 then +6 "machine": 4+2 = 6, 6+6 = 12).
- Ch. 5 Zbritja dhe ndryshesa (pp. 68–85): subtraction as the inverse of addition; partition, reduction and comparison meanings kept distinct; worked values 10−1 = 9, 9−2 = 7, 7−5 = 2, 8−3 = 5, 14−8 = 6; number triples (8, 5, 3); story totals (bees: 14 taken, 15 left).
- Ch. 6 Numrat çift dhe tek (pp. 86–103): even/odd by pairing and by last digit; doubles and halves (double 4, double 6, half of 12, half of 2; halves of fruit: four halves make 2 oranges); near doubles (65 + 66 worked as 65+65+1 or 66+66−1; dice near-doubles 1, 3, 5, 7, 9, 11, 13); test fact 13 = double 6 + 1.
- Ch. 7 Të numërojmë dhe të llogaritim me afërsi (pp. 104–127): number-line counting on (+2 steps 8→10→12→predict 14); 10 more / 10 less on the 100-grid (+11 as +10 then +1; +9 as +10 then −1); missing-number equations (6+□ = 10, □+3 = 9; steps 3→+3→6, 7→−5→2); money (coins 5, 10, 20, 50, 100 lekë; 100 lekë = 10+20+20+50; 50 lekë = 5 × 10 lekë); estimating sets to about 30; coin cut-out sheet (1, 5, 10, 25 lekë).
- Ch. 8 Shumëzimi dhe pjesëtimi (pp. 128–142): multiplication/division as inverses (4×5 = 20 → 20÷4 = 5); arrays (3×5 = 15, 15÷3 = 5); equal sharing (10 cubes → 5 each; 7 cubes = 2 groups of 3 + 1 left over); grouping by repeated addition (3 groups of 4; 3 rows of 4 vs 4 rows of 3, both 12); closing sheet: 5 cats × 4 legs = 4+4+4+4+4 = 20, 4×5 = 20.
- Ch. 9 Matjet (pp. 143–160): measuring by direct comparison and by non-standard uniform units; conservation of length/capacity; capacity by filling with cubes; comparative language (longer/shorter, taller). No numeric answers in this chapter.
- Ch. 10 Figurat dhe trupat gjeometrike (pp. 161–178): 2D shapes by sides and corners; regular shape = all sides and angles equal; a square IS a rectangle; a rectangle's diagonal is correctly captioned NOT a line of symmetry; 3D shapes (cylinder rolls one way, sphere every way; three dimensions); cube facts offered as choose-the-right-one prompts (6 faces, 8 vertices, 12 edges); symmetry with mirrors; position and movement words.
- Ch. 11 Koha (pp. 179–194): 1 hour = 60 minutes; the week = 7 days; today/yesterday/tomorrow (if today is Tuesday, tomorrow is Wednesday); sequencing the day; 12 month cards; the clock (long hand = minutes, short hand = hour; one full turn of the long hand moves the short hand one number; "4 fiks" = 4 o'clock exactly); the resource clock face 1–12 with 60 minute ticks is correctly laid out.
- Ch. 12 Paraqitja e të dhënave (pp. 195–210): the data-handling cycle (ask, collect, analyse, present, interpret — "seven is a number, 'seven pupils wear red' is data"); block graphs; pictograms; Venn diagrams (four legs / stripes: deer, cat, leopard, lion, horse, zebra, giraffe fit; bird, spider, seal fit neither); Carroll diagrams (each object in exactly one of four boxes); closing party-planning data project (pupil pp. 168–169).

## Across the set

The sequence Grade 9 → Grade 11 is one continuous Oxford-origin course: Grade 10 adds right-triangle trigonometry and quadratic solving; Grade 11 adds conditional probability, the sine/cosine rules and trig graphs, circle theorems, vectors, histograms/cumulative frequency, and composite/inverse functions. Grade 12 then runs a separate base-plus-elective course whose base book covers calculus (derivatives and integrals), vectors, conics/circles, binomial expansion and exponential/logarithmic functions, with the elective adding radians, partial fractions, chain/product/quotient rules, substitution and parts integration, the scalar product and conditional probability. Calculus and logarithms are absent from Grades 9–11. Every book in the set is a teacher's book: at merge, all eight stock lessons only as named teacher evidence.
