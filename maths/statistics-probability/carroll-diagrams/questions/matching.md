# Carroll Diagrams — matching

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. This lesson's parts did not cut by the old depth layers — the text's own order governs — so items are marked item by item by what each item actually asks: table-shape and label items are Sub lesson 1 — The Carroll Table; Venn-comparison items are Sub lesson 2 — Carroll Beside Venn; reading, counting and placing items are Sub lesson 3 — Reading and Placing in a Box.

Match each thing on the left with the box it belongs in on the right. The diagram is: columns — fish / not a fish; rows — red / not red.

**1. (Sub lesson 3)** A red goldfish
**Answer:** Fish and red — a goldfish is a fish, and this one is red.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A grey stone
**Answer:** Not a fish and not red — grey is not red, and a stone is not a fish: the "not, not" box.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A red bus
**Answer:** Not a fish and red — the bus is red but no fish.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** A green frog
**Answer:** Not a fish and not red — a frog is not a fish (it is an amphibian), and green is not red.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** A silver herring
**Answer:** Fish and not red — a herring is a fish, and silver is not red.
