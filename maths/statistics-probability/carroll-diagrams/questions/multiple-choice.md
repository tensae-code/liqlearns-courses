# Carroll Diagrams — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Level 1)** A Carroll diagram sorts things into how many boxes (with two properties)?
a) 2  b) 4  c) 8
**Answer:** b) 4 — two labels across times two labels down makes four boxes.

**2. (Level 1)** In a fish / not a fish × red / not red diagram, a blue fish goes in the box…
a) fish and red  b) fish and not red  c) not a fish and not red
**Answer:** b) fish and not red — it is a fish, and blue is not red.

**3. (Level 2)** The labels down the side of a Carroll diagram are "round" and…
a) square  b) not round  c) big
**Answer:** b) not round — each direction carries one property and its "not" twin.

**4. (Level 2)** You sort numbers 1–12 by even / not even × greater than 6 / not greater than 6. The number 9 goes in…
a) even and greater than 6  b) not even and greater than 6  c) not even and not greater than 6
**Answer:** b) not even and greater than 6 — 9 is odd, and 9 > 6.

**5. (Level 3)** In a Venn diagram, things that are neither property sit…
a) in their own drawn box  b) outside the loops  c) they cannot be sorted
**Answer:** b) outside the loops — that is the gap the Carroll table fills with its "not, not" box (Laos, Grade 6, pp. 193–197).

**6. (Level 3)** The Carroll diagram is named for…
a) John Venn  b) Lewis Carroll  c) a Kuwaiti teacher
**Answer:** b) Lewis Carroll — Laos's book credits Lewis Carroll 1832–1898 and John Venn 1834–1923 (pp. 193–197).
