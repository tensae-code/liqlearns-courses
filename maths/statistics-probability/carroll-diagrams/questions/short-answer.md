# Carroll Diagrams — short answer

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Level 1)** A diagram has columns fish / not a fish and rows red / not red. The top-left box (fish × red) holds 3 things, fish × not red holds 5, not a fish × red holds 2, not a fish × not red holds 4. How many things were sorted?
**Answer:** 14 — 3 + 5 + 2 + 4; every thing is counted exactly once.

**2. (Level 1)** How many fish are in the diagram of question 1?
**Answer:** 8 — the whole fish column: 3 + 5 (North Macedonia's count-and-compare style, pp. 157–158).

**3. (Level 2)** Where does the number 12 go in an even / not even × greater than 10 / not greater than 10 diagram?
**Answer:** Even and greater than 10 — 12 is even, and 12 > 10.

**4. (Level 2)** Where does the word "cat" go in a diagram labelled animal name / not an animal name × three letters / not three letters, sorting words?
**Answer:** Animal name and three letters — "cat" names an animal and has exactly three letters.

**5. (Level 3)** Your class has 20 pupils. In a glasses / no glasses × walked today / did not walk diagram, the boxes hold 4, 6, 3, 7. A classmate says the total is wrong. Check it.
**Answer:** The total is right — 4 + 6 + 3 + 7 = 20, every pupil in exactly one box.

**6. (Level 3)** Name the one question a Carroll row pair always asks, in the labels "long" and "not long".
**Answer:** "Is it long?" — yes goes with "long", no goes with "not long"; one question, two answers.
