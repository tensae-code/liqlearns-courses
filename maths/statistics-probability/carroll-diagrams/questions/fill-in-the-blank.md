# Carroll Diagrams — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the missing word or number.

**1. (Level 1)** Every thing sorted into a Carroll diagram lands in exactly ______ box.
**Answer:** one — the two yes/no answers name a single crossing.

**2. (Level 1)** The opposite label of "fish" in a Carroll diagram is "______ a fish".
**Answer:** not — each property travels with its "not" twin (North Macedonia, Grade 1, pp. 157–158).

**3. (Level 2)** A Carroll diagram with rows red / not red and columns round / not round has ______ boxes.
**Answer:** four (4) — 2 × 2 crossings.

**4. (Level 2)** The box where "not red" meets "not round" holds things that are not red ______ not round.
**Answer:** and — a box is read by joining its two labels with "and".

**5. (Level 3)** Kuwait teaches Carroll diagrams in its Grade 4 data unit, in lesson ______.
**Answer:** 4-10 — printed p. 158, straight after Venn diagrams in lesson 4-9.

**6. (Level 3)** Laos's Grade 6 book credits John Venn with the years 1834–______.
**Answer:** 1923 — and Lewis Carroll with 1832–1898 (Laos, Grade 6, pp. 193–197).
