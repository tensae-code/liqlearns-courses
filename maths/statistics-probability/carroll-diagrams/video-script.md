# Video script — Carroll Diagrams

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Draw a table. Four boxes.

Across the top: **fish** — and **not a fish**.
Down the side: **red** — and **not red**.

Now ask two questions about every thing.
Is it a fish? Is it red?
Two answers. One box. Every thing has a box.

A red fish: here. A fish that is not red: here.
Not a fish, but red: here. Not a fish, not red: the last box.

No thing is lost. No thing sits between boxes.
That is a **Carroll diagram** — sorting, in a table.
