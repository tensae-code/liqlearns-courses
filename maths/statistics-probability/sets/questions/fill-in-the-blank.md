# Sets — fill in the blank

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Naming a Set · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Union and Intersection · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Complement, Laws and Counting.

Fill each blank with a word from the box.

**Box:** union · intersection · complement · empty · subset · Venn

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A ______ is a collection of things put together.
**Answer: set.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The set with no elements is the ______ set.
**Answer: empty.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A ∪ B is the ______ of A and B.
**Answer: union.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A ∩ B is the ______ of A and B.
**Answer: intersection.**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Aᶜ is the ______ of A.
**Answer: complement.**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** A Venn ______ draws sets as overlapping circles.
**Answer: diagram.**
