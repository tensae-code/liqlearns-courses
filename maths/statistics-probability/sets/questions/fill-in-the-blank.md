# Sets — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with a word from the box.

**Box:** union · intersection · complement · empty · subset · Venn

**1. (Level 1)** A ______ is a collection of things put together.
**Answer: set.**

**2. (Level 1)** The set with no elements is the ______ set.
**Answer: empty.**

**3. (Level 2)** A ∪ B is the ______ of A and B.
**Answer: union.**

**4. (Level 2)** A ∩ B is the ______ of A and B.
**Answer: intersection.**

**5. (Level 3)** Aᶜ is the ______ of A.
**Answer: complement.**

**6. (Level 3)** A Venn ______ draws sets as overlapping circles.
**Answer: diagram.**
