Nicaragua carries sets only in part — a brief revision (unit set, empty set, extension vs comprehension, ordered pairs, Cartesian product) inside Grade 10 probability, union and intersection of events in Grade 11, and the number sets ℤ and ℝ named as sets in the Grade 7 and Grade 8 modules; it has no standalone sets chapter, no subsets, no De Morgan and no power set, and Grade 1's zero-as-empty-set is counting, not sets.
