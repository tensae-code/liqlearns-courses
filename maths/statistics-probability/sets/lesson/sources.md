**Acceptance count: taught in 19 countries (Egypt, Jordan, Libya, Armenia, Sierra Leone, Greece, Kazakhstan, Laos, Yemen, Indonesia, Nicaragua, Ethiopia, South Sudan, Iraq, Bangladesh, Nepal, Cambodia, Kuwait, North Macedonia).**.** Egypt carries the first full set lesson (listing, set-builder, kinds of sets, union and intersection on Venn diagrams); Jordan carries sets as intervals; Libya carries the laws of set operations, De Morgan and the Cartesian product; Armenia carries sets, intersection and union, and subsets; Sierra Leone carries the worked intersection/union/complement lesson and the union-counting formula; Greece carries sets beside sample spaces and events; Kazakhstan carries sets at their youngest, on pictures. Laos carries sets from listing, Venn diagrams, subsets, Carroll diagrams, intersection, union and power sets in Grade 6 to full set operations with inclusion–exclusion and Cartesian products in Grade 10. Yemen carries sets from sets and relations in Grade 7, through partial sets and the universal set in Grade 8, to defining-property form, difference and complement sets and equivalence relations in Grade 9. Indonesia carries sets whole in Grade 7 K13 — presenting sets, Venn diagrams, subsets, power sets and all four operations with De Morgan — with sets again — presentation, cardinality and subsets, no operations — in the Grade 8 Merdeka student book; the Cartesian product is not a named topic in the held Indonesian sets chapter. Nicaragua carries sets only in part — a brief revision (unit set, empty set, extension vs comprehension, ordered pairs, Cartesian product) inside Grade 10 probability, union and intersection of events in Grade 11, and the number sets ℤ and ℝ named as sets in the Grade 7 and Grade 8 modules; it has no standalone sets chapter, no subsets, no De Morgan and no power set, and Grade 1's zero-as-empty-set is counting, not sets. Ethiopia carries sets from the first introduction — listing and verbal description, empty, finite and infinite sets, subsets, union and intersection on Venn diagrams — in Grade 7, to the full further course — set-builder description, power sets, complement, difference and symmetric difference, De Morgan's laws, the Cartesian product and union counting — in Grade 9. South Sudan carries sets from set notations, equivalent and equal sets and Venn diagrams in Primary 7, through the number sets ℕ, ℤ, ℤ₀⁺ and ℚ with finite and infinite sets, cardinal number, the empty set and the universal set in Primary 8, to describing sets, subsets, complements, set operations and three-set Venn survey problems in Secondary 2 — with three corrections taught, never as printed: Primary 8 p. 73 says the universal set is "denoted by the symbol (epsilon)" (it is U or ξ); Primary 8 p. 70 prints B = {l, 2, 3, 6, 9, 18} ("l" for 1) and I = {a, e, i, 0, u} ("0" for "o"); Primary 7 p. 61 prints the equivalent-sets symbol as 'ļ', which is not standard — teach ~ or ↔. Iraq carries sets only in part — the first set lesson in Grade 7 (set, element, subset, finite and infinite sets, intersection and union) and ordered pairs with the Cartesian product inside the Grade 9 mappings lesson; it teaches no complement, difference, power set, De Morgan's laws or set algebra as a topic, and sets appear in Grade 10 Scientific Chapter 1 only inside quantified propositions, so Iraq enters this lesson as a partial. Bangladesh carries sets whole in Class 9 — the concept and types of sets, number of elements, all five operations with Venn diagrams and the Cartesian product, with survey applications — with three corrections taught, never as printed: p. 26 n(A\B) = n(A) − n(A ∪ B) is wrong (correct n(A\B) = n(A) − n(A∩B)); p. 27 (A ∪ B) ∩ C = (A ∩ C) ∩ (B ∩ C) is wrong (correct (A∪B)∩C = (A∩C)∪(B∩C)); and p. 28 (P × Q) ∩ R is a type error (a set of ordered pairs cannot intersect a set of elements as written; presumably intended (P×Q) ∩ (P×R)). De Morgan's laws are exercised in the chapter but never named, so the name rests on Libya. Nepal carries sets whole, from grouping like objects in Grade 1, through sets and members in Grade 2 and set notation in Grade 3, to well-defined collections and listing in Grade 4, listing only in Grade 5, set-builder notation in Grade 6, subsets and Venn diagrams with union and intersection in Grade 7, difference, complement and cardinality in Grade 8, full set operations with cardinality in Grade 9, and two- and three-set problems in Grade 10 — with one correction taught, never as printed: Grade 10 Unit 1 Example 2 (pp. 2–3) prints '6 like neither' for 52 students, but the regions sum to 40, not 52; the correct 'neither' is 18. Cambodia carries sets only in part — what a set is, listing and set-builder notation, finite, infinite and empty sets, union, intersection, subsets, complement, difference and De Morgan's laws in Grade 10, with sample space and events as sets in Grade 11; the Cartesian product, power set, cardinality and inclusion–exclusion are not taught anywhere in the set. North Macedonia touches sets at Grade 1 (2022) with grouping by one criterion in a Venn diagram — no operations are taught.

**Flagged, not taught:** the Grade 7 K13 book's Sabulan livestock problem (printed pp. 166–169) double-counts the triple region and its data are inconsistent, and its sports survey (p. 170) gives badminton-only as −2 — this lesson teaches neither; correct counting is used throughout. Kuwait carries sets partially in Grade 4 through Venn and Carroll diagrams in its data unit, and in full in Grade 8 — subsets, union, intersection, difference and Venn diagrams opening the sets-and-relations unit.
