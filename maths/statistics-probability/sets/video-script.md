# Video script — Sets

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Look at these things on the table. Buttons. Coins. Stones.

Put the round things here. That group is a SET. A set is a group of things, all together, with a name.

This set is the round things. That set is the red things. One button is round AND red — it sits in BOTH sets. Where the two circles cross, that is the INTERSECTION. Both.

Everything in one circle OR the other — that is the UNION. Either.

And everything outside the circle? That is the COMPLEMENT. Not in the set.

Now you. Make your own two sets. Where do they cross? What is left outside? Say it whole!
