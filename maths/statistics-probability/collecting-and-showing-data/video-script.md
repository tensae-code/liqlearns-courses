# Video script — Collecting and Showing Data

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Ask a question. Any question. What is your favourite fruit?

Ask ten people. Tally their answers. One mark per answer. That is DATA. You collected it.

Now show it. Tallies become a TABLE. The table becomes BARS. Tall bar — many people. Short bar — few people.

Or cut a PIE. Each slice is a share of the whole. Big slice — many people. Small slice — few.

Same answers, three pictures. Which picture tells it best? You choose. That is showing data.
