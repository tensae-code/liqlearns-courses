# Collecting and Showing Data — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Collecting and Tabling · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Picture Displays · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Histograms and Choosing.

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A sample is ______.
a) the whole population  b) a part of the population  c) a kind of chart
**Answer: b) a part of the population** (Libya, Grade 11 Statistics, Chapter 1).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In a tally, the fifth mark ______.
a) starts a new row  b) crosses the four before it  c) is written in red
**Answer: b) crosses the four before it.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A pictogram needs a ______ to be read.
a) key  b) protractor  c) ruler
**Answer: a) key** — the key says what one picture stands for.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A group that is one quarter of the whole has a pie sector of ______.
a) 25°  b) 90°  c) 180°
**Answer: b) 90°** — one quarter of 360°.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** A histogram differs from a bar chart because ______.
a) its bars touch  b) it has no numbers  c) it is always a pie
**Answer: a) its bars touch** — it shows numbers grouped into intervals (Egypt, Grade 6 Term 1, pp.85–89).

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** Change over time is best shown with a ______.
a) pie chart  b) line graph  c) pictogram
**Answer: b) line graph** (Sierra Leone, JSS 3 Term 3, L119).
