# Collecting and Showing Data — matching

Match each left side to its right side.

**Set (Level 1)** — displays
1. tally marks — **counting answers one by one**
2. frequency table — **answers with their counts**
3. pictogram — **pictures with a key**
4. bar chart — **bars for separate groups**

**Set (Level 2)** — more displays
1. pie chart — **sectors of one whole**
2. line graph — **change over time**
3. histogram — **touching bars for grouped numbers**
4. stem-and-leaf — **digits kept as a display**

**Set (Level 3)** — choosing
1. separate categories — **bar chart or pie chart**
2. numbers along a line — **histogram**
3. change over time — **line graph**
