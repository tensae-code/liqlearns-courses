Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A census counts the whole population.
**True** — a sample counts only a part (Libya, Grade 11 Statistics, Chapter 1).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Tally marks are grouped in fives.
**True** — four strokes, then a crossing fifth.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** In a pie chart the sectors always add to 360°.
**True** — the sectors are shares of one whole.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A pictogram can be read without its key.
**False** — without the key you do not know what one picture stands for.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** A histogram is the right display for separate categories like colours.
**False** — separate categories take a bar chart with gaps; a histogram takes numbers grouped into intervals.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** Frequency density matters when the class intervals are not all the same width.
**True** — Jordan, Grade 9 Term 2, Lesson 3.
