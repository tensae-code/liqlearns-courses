# Collecting and Showing Data — short answer

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Level 1)** You ask 20 people a question and 7 answer "apples". What is the frequency of "apples"?
**Answer:** 7.

**2. (Level 1)** Name two sources of statistical data.
**Answer:** Any two the books name — for example a survey and a census; school records and measurements (Libya, Grade 11 Statistics, Chapter 1).

**3. (Level 2)** A pie chart shows 40% for football. What is the sector angle?
**Answer:** 144° (0.40 × 360°).

**4. (Level 2)** Draw a bar chart in words: three bars for cats (5), dogs (8), birds (3). Which bar is tallest?
**Answer:** The dogs bar, at 8.

**5. (Level 3)** Data: 12, 15, 18, 21 grouped into 10–14, 15–19, 20–24. Which interval holds 18?
**Answer:** 15–19.
