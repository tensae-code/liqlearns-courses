**Acceptance count: taught in 16 countries (Egypt, Greece, Jordan, Libya, Vietnam, Sierra Leone, South Africa, Kazakhstan, Armenia, Ghana, Laos, Yemen, Indonesia, Nicaragua, Ethiopia, South Sudan).** Libya carries the fullest collecting-and-showing course (sources, errors, sampling, tables, every graph form); Egypt carries pie charts and histograms; Greece carries collecting, bar charts, pictograms and pie diagrams; Jordan carries choosing between displays, grouped tables and histograms with frequency density; Vietnam carries bar, double-bar and pie charts; Sierra Leone carries the whole display ladder in one term; South Africa and Kazakhstan carry the youngest pictographs and first bar graphs; Armenia carries collecting, grouping and the histogram; Ghana's evidence is teacher evidence only. Laos carries collecting and showing data from picture data and tallying in Grade 2, through collecting data and graphs in Grades 3–5, to frequency tables, bar charts, pie charts and grouped bar charts in Grades 7–8 and data types in Grade 9. Yemen carries collecting and showing data from sorting, pictograms, tally tables and bar charts in Grade 2, through collecting and organising data, to columns, lines and pie sectors in Grade 6 and tabulation in Grades 7–8. Indonesia carries collecting and showing data from lists, tables and picture diagrams in Grade 1, through tally and picture diagrams, tables, pictograms and bar charts in Grades 2–5, to statistical investigation with bar and pie charts and choosing the right diagram in Grade 7, histograms and data representation in Grade 10, and scatter diagrams and two-way tables in Grade 11. Nicaragua carries collecting and showing data from pictograms in Grade 2, through a statistics survey unit and frequency tables in Grade 3, to bar charts and two-way tables in Grade 4, line graphs in Grade 5 and the full presentation course — frequencies, bar, strip and pie charts and the ogive — in Grade 9. Ethiopia carries collecting and showing data from pictographs and tally tables in Grades 2–3, through bar graphs in Grade 4 and bar and dot graphs in Grade 5, to frequency tables and pie charts in Grade 6, pie charts and line graphs in Grade 7, and pie charts, bar charts, histograms and frequency polygons in Grade 9, with grouped data and graphical representation in Grade 11 and frequency curves and sampling in Grade 12; there is no statistics in Grade 8 or Grade 10. South Sudan carries collecting and showing data from pictographs and block graphs in Primary 3, through data collection, tallying, bar, line and pie charts in Primary 4, bar, line and pie charts with the sector-angle rule (quantity/total × 360°) in Primary 5, tables, tally/frequency tables, bar charts, pictograms, pie charts and line graphs in Primary 6, frequency distribution, scale drawings, bar graphs and pie charts in Primary 7 and the data-collection process with tables and graphs in Primary 8, to mean, mode and median with frequency tables and grouped data in Secondary 1 and cumulative frequency tables with the ogive in Secondary 2 — with two corrections: Primary 5 p. 76 prints a wrong frequency table (its frequencies sum to 45; the raw list has 42 marks, 50 occurring 10 times not 8, 35 four times not 9), so no answers are cited from that table; and Primary 5 p. 88 prints wrong pie-chart percentages (chicken 66.7% not 58%; goats 16.7% not 23%; cows 11.1% not 10%; ducks 5.6% not 9%), so the sector angles (240°, 40°, 60°, 20°) are cited, not the percentage labels.
