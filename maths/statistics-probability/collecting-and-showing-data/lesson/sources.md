Nicaragua carries collecting and showing data from pictograms in Grade 2, through a statistics survey unit and frequency tables in Grade 3, to bar charts and two-way tables in Grade 4, line graphs in Grade 5 and the full presentation course — frequencies, bar, strip and pie charts and the ogive — in Grade 9. Ethiopia carries collecting and showing data from pictographs and tally tables in Grades 2–3, through bar graphs in Grade 4 and bar and dot graphs in Grade 5, to frequency tables and pie charts in Grade 6, pie charts and line graphs in Grade 7, and pie charts, bar charts, histograms and frequency polygons in Grade 9, with grouped data and graphical representation in Grade 11 and frequency curves and sampling in Grade 12; there is no statistics in Grade 8 or Grade 10. South Sudan carries collecting and showing data from pictographs and block graphs in Primary 3, through data collection, tallying, bar, line and pie charts in Primary 4, bar, line and pie charts with the sector-angle rule (quantity/total × 360°) in Primary 5, tables, tally/frequency tables, bar charts, pictograms, pie charts and line graphs in Primary 6, frequency distribution, scale drawings, bar graphs and pie charts in Primary 7 and the data-collection process with tables and graphs in Primary 8, to mean, mode and median with frequency tables and grouped data in Secondary 1 and cumulative frequency tables with the ogive in Secondary 2 — with two corrections: Primary 5 p. 76 prints a wrong frequency table (its frequencies sum to 45; the raw list has 42 marks, 50 occurring 10 times not 8, 35 four times not 9), so no answers are cited from that table; and Primary 5 p. 88 prints wrong pie-chart percentages (chicken 66.7% not 58%; goats 16.7% not 23%; cows 11.1% not 10%; ducks 5.6% not 9%), so the sector angles (240°, 40°, 60°, 20°) are cited, not the percentage labels. Iraq carries collecting and showing data whole, from sorting and picture graphs in Grade 1, through tables, tally marks and bar graphs in Grades 2–4, dot plots in Grade 5 and double bar graphs, pie charts and outliers in Grade 6, to collecting and organising data with pie charts, frequency polygons and stem-and-leaf in Grade 7, box-and-whisker plots in Grade 8, survey design and misleading data in Grade 9, and cumulative frequency tables with ogive curves in Grade 10 Literary and the statistics chapter of Grade 11 Literary.
