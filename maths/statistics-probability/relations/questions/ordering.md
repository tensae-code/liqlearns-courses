# Relations — ordering

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Ordered Pairs and the Cartesian Product · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Relations, Domain, Range and Inverse · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Transitive and Equivalence Relations.

Put each list in order, or build it in the order asked.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** List the pairs of {a, b} × {1, 2} in dictionary order (by first member, then second).
**Answer:** (a, 1), (a, 2), (b, 1), (b, 2) — all of a's pairs before all of b's.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A relation is built in steps. Put the steps in order: choose pairs by a link; write the Cartesian product; name the two sets.
**Answer:** Name the two sets → write the Cartesian product → choose pairs by a link. The relation is chosen from the product.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Put the three equivalence tests in the order this lesson runs them: transitivity; reflexivity; symmetry.
**Answer:** Reflexivity → symmetry → transitivity. One failed test ends the claim at that step.
