# Relations — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Level 1)** The ordered pair (5, 2) is equal to…
a) (2, 5)  b) (5, 2)  c) {2, 5}
**Answer:** b) (5, 2) — pairs are equal only when both places match, in order.

**2. (Level 1)** If A has 3 members and B has 4 members, A × B has…
a) 7 pairs  b) 12 pairs  c) 34 pairs
**Answer:** b) 12 pairs — every A member pairs with every B member: 3 × 4 = 12.

**3. (Level 1)** For A = {1, 2} and B = {a, b}, which pair is NOT in A × B?
a) (1, a)  b) (2, b)  c) (a, 1)
**Answer:** c) (a, 1) — first members must come from A; (a, 1) belongs to B × A. Yemen's point: S × T ≠ T × S (Grade 7, p. 34).

**4. (Level 2)** The domain of R = {(2, 4), (2, 6), (3, 6), (3, 9)} is…
a) {4, 6, 9}  b) {2, 3}  c) {2, 3, 4, 6, 9}
**Answer:** b) {2, 3} — the domain is the set of first members that appear.

**5. (Level 2)** The range of the same R is…
a) {4, 6, 9}  b) {2, 3}  c) {6}
**Answer:** a) {4, 6, 9} — the second members that actually appear in a pair.

**6. (Level 2)** The inverse of R = {(1, a), (2, b)} is…
a) {(a, 1), (b, 2)}  b) {(1, b), (2, a)}  c) {(a, b), (1, 2)}
**Answer:** a) {(a, 1), (b, 2)} — the inverse turns every pair round (Libya, Grade 10, Ch. 1).

**7. (Level 3)** Which relation on the counting numbers is transitive?
a) "is greater than"  b) "is the next number after"  c) "differs by 1"
**Answer:** a) "is greater than" — a > b and b > c always give a > c (Yemen, Grade 9, lesson 1-3, p. 17).

**8. (Level 3)** An equivalence relation must be…
a) reflexive only  b) reflexive, symmetric and transitive  c) transitive only
**Answer:** b) reflexive, symmetric and transitive — all three tests (Yemen, Grade 9, lesson 1-4, p. 25).
