# Relations — matching

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Ordered Pairs and the Cartesian Product · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Relations, Domain, Range and Inverse · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Transitive and Equivalence Relations.

Match each relation on the left with its right description on the right.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** R = {(1, 1), (2, 2), (3, 3)} on {1, 2, 3}
**Answer:** The identity relation — every member paired with itself only.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** "Has the same remainder mod 3 as" on the counting numbers
**Answer:** An equivalence relation — reflexive, symmetric and transitive, with three classes.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** "Is the father of" on a family
**Answer:** None of reflexive, symmetric or transitive — a father is not his own father, and the relation does not read backwards.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The relation {(4, 2), (6, 2), (6, 3), (9, 3)}
**Answer:** The inverse of "is a factor of" on the matching pairs — it is "is a multiple of" read pair by pair.

**5. (Sub lesson 1)** B × A, when A = {1} and B = {x, y}
**Answer:** {(x, 1), (y, 1)} — first members from B, second from A: the turned product.
