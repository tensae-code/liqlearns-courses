# Relations — fill in the blank

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Ordered Pairs and the Cartesian Product · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Relations, Domain, Range and Inverse · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Transitive and Equivalence Relations.

Fill each blank with the missing word or number.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In the pair (a, b), a is in the ______ place.
**Answer:** first — the first place always comes from the first set.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A × B is called the ______ product of A and B.
**Answer:** Cartesian — named for Descartes, the product of pairing everything with everything.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A relation from A to B is a ______ of A × B.
**Answer:** subset (chosen part) — a relation holds some of the product's pairs, not necessarily all.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The set of second members that appear in a relation is its ______.
**Answer:** range — the first members make the domain, the second members the range.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** A relation that is reflexive, symmetric and ______ is an equivalence relation.
**Answer:** transitive — all three tests must hold (Yemen, Grade 9, p. 25).

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** An equivalence relation sorts its set into ______.
**Answer:** classes (equivalence classes) — boxes with everything inside related, nothing across.
