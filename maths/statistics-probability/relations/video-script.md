# Video script — Relations

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A pair has an order. (Ana, apple) is not (apple, Ana).
**First place, first set.** Always.

Take set A and set B. Pair every one of A with every one of B.
That big family of pairs is the **Cartesian product**, A times B.

Now choose some of those pairs — the ones where something is true.
"Ana likes apple." Those chosen pairs are a **relation**.

The first members you used — that is the **domain**.
The second members you used — that is the **range**.
Turn every pair round, and you have the **inverse** relation.

And when a relation sorts one set into boxes —
everything in a box related to everything in its box —
that is an **equivalence**, and the boxes are its classes.

Pairs, product, relation. That is the whole road.
