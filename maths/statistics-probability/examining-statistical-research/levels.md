## Sub lesson 1 — The Audit
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Examine a finished study step by step against the four research steps and find where it breaks. Pass mark: 4 of 5.

## Sub lesson 2 — Question, Population and Sample
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Tell whether a study's question, population and sample are about the same people, and check its chart against its own counts. Pass mark: 4 of 5.

## Sub lesson 3 — Tracing the Source
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Trace data to a named source, judge a source by its origin, and say why a source-less claim fails the audit. Pass mark: 4 of 5.

## Sub lesson 4 — Personal Data and Privacy
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Name the kinds of question that ask for personal data, spot privacy failures, and say what refusals do to a study's results. Pass mark: 4 of 5.

## Sub lesson 5 — Consent and the Law
Sub lesson 5 questions from the type files. Say why an unread consent box is not real consent, and name the law (KVKK) the book states as the frame. Pass mark: 4 of 5.

## What "maxed" means here
The learner can take a finished study apart the way Turkey's Grade 7 book does (Theme 3, printed pp. 190–196, with the personal-data rule of printed p. 170): audit it against the four steps, catch a population/sample/question mismatch and a chart that disagrees with its counts, trace its data to a named reliable source or reject it as source-less, identify personal-data questions and privacy failures, and state the consent and KVKK rules — and can run the same audit on a published report, as the book's performance task (printed p. 194) demands.
