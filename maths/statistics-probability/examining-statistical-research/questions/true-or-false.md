# True or false — Examining Statistical Research

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Layer 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — The Audit · (Layer 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Question, Population and Sample · (Layer 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Tracing the Source · (Layer 4) -> Sub lesson 4 — Personal Data and Privacy · (Layer 5) -> Sub lesson 5 — Consent and the Law. Two matching/ordering items span two sub lessons and keep a span marker, e.g. (Sub lessons 1–2).

1. (Sub lesson 1) Examining a study means checking it step by step against the research process it should have followed.
   **Answer: True** — question, collect and prepare, analyse, interpret and decide. Turkey, *Matematik 7. Sınıf Ders Kitabı, 1. Kitap* (Grade 7), printed pp. 190, 195–196.

2. (Sub lesson 2) If a survey asks one class a question about that class, its results can be announced as the whole school's view.
   **Answer: False** — population, sample and question must match; the book's dissected survey fails exactly here. Turkey, Grade 7, printed p. 190.

3. (Sub lesson 2) A study's chart does not need to agree with the study's own counts.
   **Answer: False** — the audit checks the pie chart against the counts it was drawn from. Turkey, Grade 7, printed p. 190.

4. (Sub lesson 3) A health claim with no source named can still be accepted as research.
   **Answer: False** — a source-less claim cannot be examined, so it cannot be believed as research; reliable information traces to named sources and edu/gov/org domains. Turkey, Grade 7, printed pp. 191–192.

5. (Sub lesson 4) Questions about a respondent's health, bank account and income are personal-data questions.
   **Answer: True** — health, identity, bank, address and income are the book's named kinds. Turkey, Grade 7, printed p. 170.

6. (Sub lesson 5) Ticking "I accept all permissions" without reading it is the informed consent the KVKK frame requires.
   **Answer: False** — consent ticked unread is one of the failures the book examines. Turkey, Grade 7, printed p. 193.
