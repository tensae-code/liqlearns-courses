# Ordering — Examining Statistical Research

1. (Layer 1) Put the audit's walk through a finished study in the order of the research process:
   (a) check the interpretation and the decision it claims
   (b) check the research question
   (c) check the analysis — the displays and summaries
   (d) check the collection and preparation of the data

   **Answer: (b), (d), (c), (a)** — the four steps in order: question, collect and prepare, analyse, interpret and decide. Turkey, *Matematik 7. Sınıf Ders Kitabı, 1. Kitap* (Grade 7), printed pp. 190, 195–196.

2. (Layers 2–3) A study claims "the school's favourite lesson is art," sampled from one class, with data "from the internet." Rank the audit's objections from the study's design outward:
   (a) the claim cannot be traced to a named reliable source
   (b) the sample is one class, not the school
   (c) the question was about the class, not the school

   **Answer: (c), (b), (a)** — first the question and the sample fail to match the population claimed; then the untraceable sourcing fails the source check. Turkey, Grade 7, printed pp. 190–191.
