# Fill in the blank — Examining Statistical Research

1. (Layer 1) A finished study is examined step by step against the four steps of the research ___.
   **Answer: process** — Turkey, *Matematik 7. Sınıf Ders Kitabı, 1. Kitap* (Grade 7), printed pp. 190, 195–196.

2. (Layer 2) In the book's dissected survey, the population claimed was the whole school but the ___ was only one class.
   **Answer: sample** — Turkey, Grade 7, printed p. 190.

3. (Layer 3) The ice-sheet data is traced to its named source, the ___.
   **Answer: NSIDC** — Turkey, Grade 7, printed p. 191.

4. (Layer 4) Health, identity, bank, address and income questions ask for ___ data.
   **Answer: personal** — Turkey, Grade 7, printed p. 170.

5. (Layer 5) Turkey's personal-data law, stated in the book as the research-ethics frame, is the ___.
   **Answer: KVKK** — Turkey, Grade 7, printed p. 193.
