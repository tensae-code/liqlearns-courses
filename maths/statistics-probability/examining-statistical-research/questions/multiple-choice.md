# Multiple choice — Examining Statistical Research

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. Two matching/ordering items span two sub lessons and keep a span marker, e.g. (Sub lessons 1–2).

1. (Sub lesson 1) A finished study is examined against:
   (a) the teacher's answer key
   (b) the same four research steps it should have followed — question, collect and prepare, analyse, interpret and decide
   (c) the studies published before it
   (d) the size of its sample only

   **Answer: (b)** — the audit walks the study through the process and finds the step where it breaks. Turkey, *Matematik 7. Sınıf Ders Kitabı, 1. Kitap* (Grade 7), printed pp. 190, 195–196.

2. (Sub lesson 2) A survey's stated population is the whole school, its sample is one class, and its question is about that class. The audit's verdict is:
   (a) the study is fine, because one class is part of the school
   (b) the study proves nothing until the chart is coloured in
   (c) question, population and sample do not match, so no school-wide claim can stand
   (d) the study must be repeated with a pie chart instead

   **Answer: (c)** — the book's dissected favourite-lesson survey. Turkey, Grade 7, printed p. 190.

3. (Sub lesson 3) Ice-sheet data in the book is trusted because:
   (a) it was shared many times on social media
   (b) it can be traced to a named reliable source, the NSIDC
   (c) its chart uses bright colours
   (d) nobody has questioned it

   **Answer: (b)** — reliable information is reached from named sources and edu, gov and org domains. Turkey, Grade 7, printed p. 191.

4. (Sub lesson 4) Which of these survey questions asks for personal data?
   (a) "How many siblings do you have?"
   (b) "What is your favourite sport?"
   (c) "What is your home address?"
   (d) "How many books did you read this month?"

   **Answer: (c)** — health, identity, bank, address and income questions are personal data. Turkey, Grade 7, printed p. 170.

5. (Sub lesson 4) Which is one of the privacy failures the book itemises?
   (a) answering a survey honestly
   (b) sharing a friend's photograph without permission
   (c) reading a consent box before ticking it
   (d) asking a question about favourite lessons

   **Answer: (b)** — with a public profile, a visible birth date and a named school. Turkey, Grade 7, printed p. 192.

6. (Sub lesson 5) A consent box that a user ticked without reading ("I accept all permissions") is treated by the book as:
   (a) full informed consent
   (b) consent that fails the ethics audit — consent must be informed, and the KVKK frame requires it
   (c) a spelling exercise
   (d) proof the data may be sold

   **Answer: (b)** — Turkey, Grade 7, printed p. 193.
