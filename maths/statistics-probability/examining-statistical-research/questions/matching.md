# Matching — Examining Statistical Research

1. (Layers 1–2) Match each audit finding to the study it condemns:
   (a) population, sample and question about different people
   (b) chart slices that disagree with the study's own counts
   (c) a health claim with no source named

   (i) a pie chart drawn from counts it does not match
   (ii) a social-media post believed as research
   (iii) a favourite-lesson survey of one class announced as the school's view

   **Answer: (a)–(iii), (b)–(i), (c)–(ii)** — Turkey, *Matematik 7. Sınıf Ders Kitabı, 1. Kitap* (Grade 7), printed pp. 190–192.
