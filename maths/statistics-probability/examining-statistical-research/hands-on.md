# Hands-on — Examining Statistical Research

## Activity 1 — Audit a poster (15 minutes)

Find any survey result you can see this week — a school poster, a news chart, a post online. Write the four audit lines from Turkey's Grade 7 book (printed p. 190): (1) What was the research question? (2) Who is the population — everyone the conclusion talks about? (3) Who was actually asked — the sample? (4) Do the three match? Then check one number: pick a chart slice or bar and test it against the counts the study itself gives.

## Activity 2 — Trace the source (10 minutes)

Take one claim from Activity 1 and follow it back: is a source named? Is it an official or educational address (the book points to edu, gov and org domains, printed p. 191), a named body like the NSIDC ice-sheet centre, or no source at all? Write the verdict the book teaches (printed p. 192): traced and reliable, or source-less and not to be believed yet.

## Activity 3 — The privacy sweep (10 minutes)

Write five survey questions of your own on any harmless topic, then sweep them with the book's rule (printed p. 170): does any question ask for health, identity, bank, address or income information? Those are personal data. Rewrite each such question so it collects no private information — or write, beside it, exactly what consent you would need and how you would protect the answers, the way the book's consent and KVKK discussion requires (printed p. 193).
