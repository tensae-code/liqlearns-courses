Barbados enters as CURRICULUM EVIDENCE only, through the *Primary Mathematics Syllabus* (Classes 1–4) of its Ministry of Education and Human Resource Development — its Class 3 syllabus requires probability terms (the concept of probability), the probability of outcomes, and using probability to make predictions, all beginning at Class 3; Class 4 maintains the same programme.
