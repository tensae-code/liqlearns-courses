# Video script — Probability

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Toss a coin. Heads or tails? Two things can happen. The chance of heads is one out of two. One half.

Roll a die. Six things can happen. The chance of a four is one out of six.

Now toss the coin again and again. Fifty times. Count the heads. Is it exactly half? Maybe not. Toss it a hundred times. Closer to half now. The more you toss, the closer it gets. That is experimental probability walking toward the theory.

Now two coins. Heads AND heads? One out of four. The chances multiply.

That is probability. How likely. Count what can happen, count what you want, and divide.
