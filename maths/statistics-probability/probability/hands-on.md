# Hands-on — Probability

## The coin run
Toss a coin 50 times and tally the heads. Work out the experimental probability (heads ÷ 50) and compare it with the theoretical one half. Now toss 50 more and pool the 100: watch the fraction move closer to one half, the way Egypt's book shows with 100 tosses giving 41 heads.

## The bag
Put 1 red, 6 blue and 3 green balls in a bag, the way Egypt's book does. Without looking, draw one ball, note its colour, and put it back. Do it 20 times. Which colour came up most? Work out the theoretical probabilities (blue 0.6, green 0.3, red 0.1) and compare.

## Tree on paper
Draw the tree for two coin tosses: four ends — HH, HT, TH, TT. Now draw the tree for drawing two counters from a bag without putting the first back: watch the second branches change, because the bag has changed. That change is conditional probability.

## The spinner game
Make a spinner with five equal colours. Spin it 50 times and tally. Egypt's book spun one 50 times and got yellow 13 times against a theoretical one fifth. How close did you get?
