# Probability — short answer

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Level 1)** A bag holds 1 red, 6 blue and 3 green balls. What is P(blue)?
**Answer:** 0.6 (6 out of 10) — Egypt, Grade 7 Term 2, 4-2.

**2. (Level 1)** Write the sample space of two coin tosses.
**Answer:** {(H,H), (H,T), (T,H), (T,T)} — four outcomes.

**3. (Level 2)** P(A) = 1/3. What is P(not A)?
**Answer:** 2/3.

**4. (Level 2)** Two dice are rolled. How many outcomes are in the sample space?
**Answer:** 36 (6 × 6).

**5. (Level 3)** A bag has 4 red and 6 blue counters. Two are drawn without replacement. What is P(both red)?
**Answer:** 4/10 × 3/9 = 12/90 = 2/15.
