# Probability — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Level 1)** The sample space of one die is ______.
a) {1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6}  b) {1, 2}  c) {even, odd}
**Answer: a) {1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6}** — all six outcomes (Egypt, Grade 7 Term 2, 4-1).

**2. (Level 1)** The probability of rolling a 4 on a fair die is ______.
a) 1/2  b) 1/6  c) 4/6
**Answer: b) 1/6** — one favourable outcome out of six.

**3. (Level 1)** An impossible event has probability ______.
a) 1  b) 0  c) 1/2
**Answer: b) 0** (Egypt, Grade 7 Term 2, 4-1).

**4. (Level 2)** P(not A) = 1 − P(A). If P(A) = 0.3, P(not A) = ______.
a) 0.3  b) 0.7  c) 1.3
**Answer: b) 0.7** — the complement (Egypt, Grade 7 Term 2, 4-2).

**5. (Level 2)** Two coins are tossed. P(two heads) = ______.
a) 1/2  b) 1/4  c) 3/4
**Answer: b) 1/4** — one outcome out of four (Egypt, Grade 7 Term 2, 4-2).

**6. (Level 3)** Two counters are drawn from a bag without replacement. The second draw is ______.
a) independent  b) conditional on the first  c) impossible
**Answer: b) conditional on the first** — the bag has changed (Sierra Leone, SSS 3 Term 3, L106).
