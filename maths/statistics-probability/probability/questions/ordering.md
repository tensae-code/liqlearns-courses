# Probability — ordering

Put each set in order, as the question says.

**1. (Level 1)** Order these probabilities from smallest to largest: P(rolling a 6) = 1/6, P(rolling an even number) = 1/2, P(rolling a 7) = 0, P(rolling less than 7) = 1.
**Answer:** P(rolling a 7) = 0, P(rolling a 6) = 1/6, P(rolling an even number) = 1/2, P(rolling less than 7) = 1.

**2. (Level 2)** Order the steps for P(A and B) with independent events: multiply the two probabilities; find P(A); find P(B).
**Answer:** Find P(A); find P(B); multiply the two probabilities.

**3. (Level 3)** Order these from the books' teaching order: conditional probability, sample space, theoretical probability.
**Answer:** Sample space, theoretical probability, conditional probability — Egypt's Grade 7 book teaches the space first, then the probability, and conditional probability comes in Grade 12.
