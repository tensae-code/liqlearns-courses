# Probability — matching

Match each left side to its right side.

**Set (Level 1)** — words
1. random experiment — **outcomes known, result not predictable**
2. sample space — **all possible outcomes**
3. event — **a set of outcomes**
4. certain event — **the whole sample space**

**Set (Level 2)** — laws
1. addition law — **mutually exclusive events: add**
2. multiplication law — **independent events: multiply**
3. complement — **P(not A) = 1 − P(A)**
4. geometric probability — **probability as a ratio of lengths or areas**

**Set (Level 3)** — distributions
1. binomial — **counting successes in fixed trials**
2. geometric — **trials until the first success**
3. normal — **the bell-shaped distribution**
