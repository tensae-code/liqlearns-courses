# Video script — Averages and Spread

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Here are five numbers. 2, 4, 6, 8, 10.

Add them up: 30. Share them out fairly between five: 6 each. That is the MEAN. The fair share.

Now line them up in order. The middle one is 6. That is the MEDIAN. The middle.

Which number comes up most? None — they are all different. No MODE here.

Now how spread out are they? From 2 to 10 — the RANGE is 8.

Mean, median, mode, range. Four numbers that tell you the whole story of a list.
