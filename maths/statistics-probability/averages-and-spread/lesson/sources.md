**Acceptance count: taught in 13 countries (Egypt, Jordan, Greece, Libya, Vietnam, Sierra Leone, Mauritania, Laos, Yemen, Indonesia, Nicaragua, Ethiopia, South Sudan).** Egypt carries the mean as a balance point, median/mode/outliers, the range, and quartiles with the semi-interquartile range; Jordan carries quartiles with the box-and-whisker plot and dispersion for grouped data; Greece carries the full ladder from mean to coefficient of variation in three books; Libya carries the weighted mean and the four dispersion measures; Vietnam carries central tendency and spread for ungrouped and grouped samples; Sierra Leone carries the whole ladder from the JSS mean to the SSS standard deviation and mean deviation; Mauritania carries mean, mean deviation, variance and standard deviation. Indonesia carries averages and spread from mean, median, mode, range, quartiles and IQR in the Grade 8 Merdeka guide, through grouped mean, median, mode, quartiles, percentiles, variance and standard deviation in Grade 10, to regression and correlation in Grade 11. Its Grade 7 Merdeka book teaches NO mean/median/mode, so Indonesia enters this lesson from Grade 8 (guide) and Grade 10. Nicaragua carries this lesson only in part — the average (promedio) in Grade 5, with the total found from the average and the average as a decimal; it teaches no median, no mode, no range and no variance anywhere in the set, and its Grade 9 statistics unit is presentation only.

**Flagged wording, not taught:** Sierra Leone's JSS 3 Term 3 Lesson 124 phrases the frequency-table mean as "add the frequencies given and divide by the total number of rows", with no worked frequency-table example to clarify it. This lesson does not teach that wording: it teaches the correct rule from the same book's Lesson 128 — the estimated mean x̄ = Σfx / Σf, value times frequency, divided by the total frequency. Laos carries averages and spread whole in Grade 9 — mean, mode and median with grouped formulas, and range, variance and standard deviation. Yemen carries averages and spread from the arithmetic mean in Grades 6–7, through mean, mode, cumulative frequency and median in Grade 9 and central tendency and dispersion in Grade 10, to correlation and regression in Grades 11–12. Ethiopia carries averages and spread from the mean only in Grade 5, through mean, mode and median in Grade 6 and mean, mode, median and range in Grade 7, to central tendency with grouped means and population variance and standard deviation in Grade 9, central tendency only — median of grouped data, with deciles and percentiles in the guide — in Grade 11, and mean deviation, quartile deviation, variance, standard deviation and the coefficient of variation in Grade 12; the Grade 12 book uses population variance (÷n) throughout, and there are no quartiles, no box plots and no correlation anywhere in Ethiopia. South Sudan carries averages and spread from mean, median and mode in Primary 7, through mean, mode and median with frequency tables and grouped data in Secondary 1, to the assumed mean and quartiles and percentiles read from the ogive in Secondary 2, with the interquartile range from the Secondary 2 teacher's guide — with one correction: Primary 7 p. 69 prints the median of 3, 9, 15, 17, 44 as "150"; the correct median is 15. No range is taught as a named topic in Primary 7; spread is carried by the Secondary 2 quartiles and interquartile range.

**Flagged, not taught as printed:** the Grade 11 teacher guide's Solutions to Exercise 7.14 print Decile D3 as 21.5 (correct 20.15 — the book dropped the division by f_c = 10), First quartile Q1 as 33 (correct 34), and a Decile D2 working that as printed gives 5, not 5.67 (the final value 5.67 is correct for the right inputs); its Exercise 7.15 table prints the cumulative frequency for class 81–90 as 9 (correct 92). The lesson teaches the corrected values.
