# Averages and Spread — short answer

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Level 1)** Find the mean of 4, 8, 15, 16, 23, 42.
**Answer:** 18 (total 108 ÷ 6).

**2. (Level 1)** Find the median of 11, 3, 8, 20, 5.
**Answer:** 8 (ordered: 3, 5, 8, 11, 20).

**3. (Level 2)** Find the median of 2, 5, 7, 10.
**Answer:** 6 (the mean of the middle two, 5 and 7).

**4. (Level 2)** The five-number summary is 2, 5, 9, 14, 20. What is the interquartile range?
**Answer:** 9 (14 − 5).

**5. (Level 3)** A set has variance 25. What is its standard deviation?
**Answer:** 5.
