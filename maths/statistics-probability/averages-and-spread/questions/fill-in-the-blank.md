# Averages and Spread — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with a word from the box.

**Box:** mean · median · mode · range · quartile · standard deviation

**1. (Level 1)** The total divided by how many numbers there are is the ______.
**Answer: mean.**

**2. (Level 1)** The middle value in an ordered list is the ______.
**Answer: median.**

**3. (Level 1)** The value that happens most often is the ______.
**Answer: mode.**

**4. (Level 2)** The largest minus the smallest is the ______.
**Answer: range.**

**5. (Level 2)** Q3 − Q1 is the ______ range.
**Answer: interquartile.**

**6. (Level 3)** The square root of the variance is the ______ deviation.
**Answer: standard.**
