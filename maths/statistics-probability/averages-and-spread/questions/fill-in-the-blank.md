# Averages and Spread — fill in the blank

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Mean, Median, Mode · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Quartiles and the Box Plot · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Spread: Deviation, Variance, Standard Deviation.

Fill each blank with a word from the box.

**Box:** mean · median · mode · range · quartile · standard deviation

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The total divided by how many numbers there are is the ______.
**Answer: mean.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The middle value in an ordered list is the ______.
**Answer: median.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The value that happens most often is the ______.
**Answer: mode.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The largest minus the smallest is the ______.
**Answer: range.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Q3 − Q1 is the ______ range.
**Answer: interquartile.**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** The square root of the variance is the ______ deviation.
**Answer: standard.**
