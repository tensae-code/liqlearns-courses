# Averages and Spread — matching

Match each left side to its right side.

**Set (Level 1)** — averages
1. mean — **total ÷ how many**
2. median — **middle value in order**
3. mode — **most often**
4. range — **largest − smallest**

**Set (Level 2)** — quartiles
1. Q1 — **first quartile**
2. Q2 — **the median**
3. Q3 — **third quartile**
4. IQR — **Q3 − Q1**

**Set (Level 3)** — spread
1. mean deviation — **average distance from the mean**
2. variance — **average of squared distances**
3. standard deviation — **square root of the variance**
4. coefficient of variation — **spread beside the mean**
