# Averages and Spread — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Level 1)** The mean of 2, 4, 6, 8, 10 is ______.
a) 5  b) 6  c) 8
**Answer: b) 6** — the total 30 shared between 5.

**2. (Level 1)** The median of 3, 7, 9 is ______.
a) 7  b) 6  c) 9
**Answer: a) 7** — the middle value in order.

**3. (Level 1)** The mode of 2, 3, 3, 5, 3, 7 is ______.
a) 2  b) 3  c) 7
**Answer: b) 3** — it happens most often.

**4. (Level 2)** The range of 4, 9, 15, 22 is ______.
a) 18  b) 11  c) 26
**Answer: a) 18** — 22 − 4.

**5. (Level 2)** The interquartile range is ______.
a) Q3 − Q1  b) Q3 + Q1  c) the largest minus the smallest
**Answer: a) Q3 − Q1** — it holds the middle half of the numbers.

**6. (Level 3)** Standard deviation is ______.
a) the average of the numbers  b) the square root of the variance  c) the middle value
**Answer: b) the square root of the variance** (Greece, Business Statistics, Chapter V).
