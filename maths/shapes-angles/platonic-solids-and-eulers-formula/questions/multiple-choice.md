# Platonic solids and Euler's formula — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Platonic Solids The Five Solids · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Platonic Solids Counting Faces, Vertices and Edges · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Platonic Solids Euler's Formula.

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How many regular polyhedra are there?
a) 4  b) 5  c) 6
**Answer:** b) 5 — Nicaragua, Grade 10, Unit 6, pp.304–308.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The regular polyhedron with 12 pentagonal faces is the…
a) octahedron  b) icosahedron  c) dodecahedron
**Answer:** c) dodecahedron — Nicaragua, Grade 10, pp.304–308.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** A polyhedron is regular when…
a) it is large  b) every face is the same regular polygon and the same number of faces meets at every vertex  c) it has a square base
**Answer:** b) every face is the same regular polygon and the same number of faces meets at every vertex — Nicaragua, Grade 10, Unit 6, pp.300–308.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The cube has F, V, E equal to…
a) 6, 8, 12  b) 6, 6, 12  c) 8, 6, 12
**Answer:** a) 6, 8, 12 — South Africa, Grade 8 Workbook, WS130, pp.168–191.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The octahedron has how many faces?
a) 6  b) 8  c) 20
**Answer:** b) 8 — eight triangular faces (Nicaragua, Grade 10, pp.304–308).

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** The icosahedron has how many vertices?
a) 20  b) 30  c) 12
**Answer:** c) 12 — F 20, V 12, E 30 — it swaps counts with the dodecahedron.

**7. (Sub lesson 3)** Euler's formula is…
a) V + F + E = 2  b) V + F − E = 2  c) V − F − E = 2
**Answer:** b) V + F − E = 2 — China, B edition, Ch.11, pp.66–72; Armenia, Geometry 10, p.74.

**8. (Sub lesson 3)** Why is there no regular polyhedron with hexagonal faces?
a) hexagons are too big  b) three hexagon corners already sum to 360°, so the vertex would lie flat  c) hexagons have too many sides to cut out
**Answer:** b) three hexagon corners already sum to 360°, so the vertex would lie flat — 3 × 120° = 360° — a tiling, not a solid corner.
