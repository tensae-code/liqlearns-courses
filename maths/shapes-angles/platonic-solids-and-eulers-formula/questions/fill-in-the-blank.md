# Platonic solids and Euler's formula — fill in the blank

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Platonic Solids The Five Solids · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Platonic Solids Counting Faces, Vertices and Edges · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Platonic Solids Euler's Formula.

Fill each blank with the right word or number.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The cube is also called the regular ______.
**Answer:** hexahedron — six faces (Nicaragua, Grade 10, pp.304–308).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The five regular polyhedra are called the ______ solids.
**Answer:** Platonic — known since the ancient Greeks (South Africa, Grade 8 Workbook, WS128).

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The dodecahedron has ______ edges.
**Answer:** 30 — F 12, V 20, E 30.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The tetrahedron has ______ vertices.
**Answer:** 4 — F 4, V 4, E 6.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** For every polyhedron in this lesson, V + F − E = ______.
**Answer:** 2 — Euler's formula (Armenia, Geometry 10, p.74).
