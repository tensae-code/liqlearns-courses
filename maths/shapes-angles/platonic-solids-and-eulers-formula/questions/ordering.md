# Platonic solids and Euler's formula — ordering

Put the steps or items in the right order.

**1. (Level 1)** Order the five solids by number of faces, fewest first: octahedron, tetrahedron, icosahedron, cube, dodecahedron
**Answer:** tetrahedron (4), cube (6), octahedron (8), dodecahedron (12), icosahedron (20) — the counted table in the lesson.

**2. (Level 3)** Order the vertex test for a new candidate solid: A) add the face angles at one vertex B) pick a regular face shape C) check the sum is less than 360° D) pick how many meet at each vertex
**Answer:** B, D, A, C — the why-only-five argument in the lesson.
