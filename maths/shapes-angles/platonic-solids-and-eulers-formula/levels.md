# Platonic solids and Euler's formula — questions by level

Practice by level, tied to the skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-level view. The levels are the depths inside this one thing — the regular polyhedra — naming and building the five first, then counting F, V and E, then Euler's formula and why there are five, the order the books teach it in.

## Level 1 — the five solids
Level 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: name the five regular polyhedra, say what makes a polyhedron regular, and build one from a net.

## Level 2 — counting faces, vertices and edges
Level 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: give the correct F, V and E for each of the five solids.

## Level 3 — Euler's formula
Level 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: verify V + F − E = 2 on the five solids and on prisms and pyramids, and explain why a sixth regular solid cannot exist.
