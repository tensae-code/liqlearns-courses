# Video script — Platonic solids and Euler's formula

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Five perfect solids. Only five. In the whole world!

Four triangles: a tetrahedron. Six squares: a cube.
Eight triangles: an octahedron.
Twelve pentagons: a dodecahedron. Twenty triangles: an icosahedron!

Now count with me. Corners, plus faces, minus edges.
Cube: eight, plus six, minus twelve… **TWO!**
Try another one. Always two. Always!

Euler found it. Now you have it too.
