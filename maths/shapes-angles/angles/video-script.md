# Video script — Angles

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Open a door a little. That is an angle — small, sharp. **Acute!**
Open it more — square corner. **Right angle — 90 degrees!**
Open it wider — **obtuse!** Flat against the wall — **straight —
180!** All the way round — **360!**

Now the facts that never change.
On a straight line, the angles add to **180**.
Round a point, they add to **360**.
Two angles that make a corner — **complementary — 90**.
Two that make a straight line — **supplementary — 180**.
Across from each other — **vertically opposite — equal!**

And inside a triangle? Tear off the three corners. Put them
together. They make a straight line — **180 degrees, every
triangle, every time!**

Now you. Find a missing angle. Which fact did you use? Say it!
That is angles.
