# Angles — matching

Match each angle or pair to its size.

**Set 1 (Level 1)**
1. acute — **less than 90°**
2. right — **exactly 90°**
3. obtuse — **more than 90°, less than 180°**
4. straight — **exactly 180°**
5. reflex — **more than 180°, less than 360°**

**Set 2 (Level 2)**
1. angles on a straight line — **add to 180°**
2. angles around a point — **add to 360°**
3. complementary — **add to 90°**
4. supplementary — **add to 180°**
5. vertically opposite — **equal**

**Set 3 (Level 3)**
1. angles in a triangle — **add to 180°**
2. angles in a quadrilateral — **add to 360°**
3. angles in a pentagon — **add to 540°**
4. corresponding angles — **equal**
5. alternate angles — **equal**
