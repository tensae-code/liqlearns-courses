# Angles — ordering

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Angles Kinds and Measuring · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Angles on a Straight Line and Around a Point · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Angles in Shapes and Between Parallel Lines.

Put each set in order, smallest first. (This question type exists
for this lesson because angle kinds ARE an order of size.)

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** obtuse · acute · right · straight
**Answer:** acute (under 90°), right (90°), obtuse (90°–180°), straight (180°).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** 120° · 30° · 90° · 200° · 180°
**Answer:** 30°, 90°, 120°, 180°, 200°.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Order these pairs by their sum, smallest first: supplementary · complementary · angles on a straight line.
**Answer:** complementary (90°), supplementary (180°), angles on a straight line (180°) — supplementary and a straight line tie at 180°.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Order these three triangles by their missing angle, smallest missing first: angles 80° and 70° · angles 50° and 60° · angles 90° and 45°.
**Answer:** missing 30° (80° + 70°), missing 45° (90° + 45°), missing 70° (50° + 60°) — order: 30°, 45°, 70°.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Order these shapes by their interior-angle sum, smallest first: triangle · quadrilateral · pentagon.
**Answer:** triangle (180°), quadrilateral (360°), pentagon (540°).
