# Angles — fill in the blank

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Angles Kinds and Measuring · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Angles on a Straight Line and Around a Point · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Angles in Shapes and Between Parallel Lines.

Fill each blank with a word from the box.

**Box:** acute · right · 180 · 360 · complementary · supplementary

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** An angle smaller than 90° is ______.
**Answer: acute.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** An angle of exactly 90° is a ______ angle.
**Answer: right.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Angles on a straight line add to ______°.
**Answer: 180.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Angles around a point add to ______°.
**Answer: 360.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Two angles that add to 90° are ______.
**Answer: complementary.**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Two angles that add to 180° are ______.
**Answer: supplementary.**
