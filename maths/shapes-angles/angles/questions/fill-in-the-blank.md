# Angles — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with a word from the box.

**Box:** acute · right · 180 · 360 · complementary · supplementary

**1. (Level 1)** An angle smaller than 90° is ______.
**Answer: acute.**

**2. (Level 1)** An angle of exactly 90° is a ______ angle.
**Answer: right.**

**3. (Level 2)** Angles on a straight line add to ______°.
**Answer: 180.**

**4. (Level 2)** Angles around a point add to ______°.
**Answer: 360.**

**5. (Level 2)** Two angles that add to 90° are ______.
**Answer: complementary.**

**6. (Level 2)** Two angles that add to 180° are ______.
**Answer: supplementary.**
