# Angles — short answer

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Level 1)** Name the angle kind: 150°.
**Answer:** Obtuse — more than 90° but less than 180°.

**2. (Level 1)** Where do you put the centre of the protractor when you measure an angle?
**Answer:** On the vertex, with the baseline along one arm (Sierra Leone, JSS 1, L93).

**3. (Level 2)** Angles around a point are 90°, 120° and 100°. What is the fourth angle?
**Answer:** 50° — 360 − 90 − 120 − 100 = 50 (Jordan, Grade 5, p.62).

**4. (Level 2)** The supplement of 110° is ______.
**Answer:** 70° — supplementary angles add to 180°.

**5. (Level 3)** A triangle has angles 70° and 55°. Find the third angle.
**Answer:** 55° — 180 − 70 − 55 = 55.

**6. (Level 3)** A pentagon has interior angles 100°, 110°, 120°, 130° and x. Find x.
**Answer:** 80° — a pentagon's angles add to (5 − 2) × 180° = 540°; 540 − 100 − 110 − 120 − 130 = 80 (Egypt, Grade 7, 4-5).
