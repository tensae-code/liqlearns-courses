# Angles — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Angles Kinds and Measuring · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Angles on a Straight Line and Around a Point · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Angles in Shapes and Between Parallel Lines.

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** An angle of 45° is ______.
a) right  b) acute  c) obtuse
**Answer: b) acute** — less than 90° (Egypt, Grade 7, 4-1).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A right angle measures ______.
a) 45°  b) 90°  c) 180°
**Answer: b) 90°.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Two angles on a straight line are 130° and x. x = ______.
a) 50°  b) 60°  c) 130°
**Answer: a) 50°** — angles on a straight line add to 180° (Jordan, Grade 5, p.62).

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The complement of 35° is ______.
a) 55°  b) 145°  c) 65°
**Answer: a) 55°** — complementary angles add to 90° (Sierra Leone, JSS 1, L96–L97).

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Two straight lines cross. One angle is 70°. The angle opposite it is ______.
a) 110°  b) 70°  c) 20°
**Answer: b) 70°** — vertically opposite angles are equal (Sierra Leone, JSS 1, L99).

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** A triangle has angles 60° and 80°. The third angle is ______.
a) 40°  b) 60°  c) 120°
**Answer: a) 40°** — angles in a triangle add to 180° (Egypt, Grade 7, 4-3).

**7. (Sub lesson 3)** A quadrilateral has angles 90°, 90° and 90°. The fourth angle is ______.
a) 90°  b) 180°  c) 270°
**Answer: a) 90°** — angles in a quadrilateral add to 360° (Egypt, Grade 7, 4-4).
