# Circles — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with a word from the box.

**Box:** radius · diameter · circumference · pi · area · centre

**1. (Level 1)** The point in the middle of a circle is its ______.
**Answer: centre.**

**2. (Level 1)** The line across a circle through its centre is the ______.
**Answer: diameter.**

**3. (Level 1)** The distance from the centre to the edge is the ______.
**Answer: radius.**

**4. (Level 2)** The distance around a circle is its ______.
**Answer: circumference.**

**5. (Level 2)** Circumference divided by diameter is always ______, about 3.14.
**Answer: pi.**

**6. (Level 3)** The flat space a circle covers is its ______.
**Answer: area.**
