# Circles — fill in the blank

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Circles Parts of the Circle · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Circles Circumference · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Circles Area of the Disc.

Fill each blank with a word from the box.

**Box:** radius · diameter · circumference · pi · area · centre

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The point in the middle of a circle is its ______.
**Answer: centre.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The line across a circle through its centre is the ______.
**Answer: diameter.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The distance from the centre to the edge is the ______.
**Answer: radius.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The distance around a circle is its ______.
**Answer: circumference.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Circumference divided by diameter is always ______, about 3.14.
**Answer: pi.**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** The flat space a circle covers is its ______.
**Answer: area.**
