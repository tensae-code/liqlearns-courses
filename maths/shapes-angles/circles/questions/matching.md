Match each part or formula to its meaning.

**Set 1 (Sub lesson 1)**
1. centre — **the point in the middle**
2. radius — **centre to edge**
3. diameter — **edge to edge through the centre**
4. chord — **a straight line joining two points on the edge**
5. arc — **a curved piece of the edge**

**Set 2 (Sub lesson 2)**
1. d = 2r — **diameter from radius**
2. r = d ÷ 2 — **radius from diameter**
3. C = πd — **circumference from diameter**
4. C = 2πr — **circumference from radius**
5. π ≈ 3.14 — **circumference divided by diameter**

**Set 3 (Sub lesson 3)**
1. A = πr² — **area of a disc**
2. cm — **the unit of a circumference**
3. cm² — **the unit of an area**
4. 22/7 — **another value of pi the books use (Libya, Grade 6)**
5. disc — **the flat inside of a circle**
