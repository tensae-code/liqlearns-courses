# Circles — ordering

Put each set in order, smallest first. (This question type exists
for this lesson because circles ARE ordered by size — radius,
circumference and area all grow together.)

**1. (Level 1)** Order these lengths, smallest first: a radius of 5 cm · a diameter of 5 cm · a radius of 10 cm.
**Answer:** diameter 5 cm (a radius of 2.5 cm), radius 5 cm, radius 10 cm — the diameter of 5 cm is the shortest because it is only twice a radius of 2.5 cm.

**2. (Level 2)** Order these circumferences (π ≈ 3.14), smallest first: diameter 4 cm · diameter 10 cm · diameter 6 cm.
**Answer:** 12.56 cm (d = 4), 18.84 cm (d = 6), 31.4 cm (d = 10).

**3. (Level 3)** Order these areas (π ≈ 3.14), smallest first: radius 2 cm · radius 5 cm · radius 3 cm.
**Answer:** 12.56 cm² (r = 2), 28.26 cm² (r = 3), 78.5 cm² (r = 5).

**4. (Level 3)** Order these by area, smallest first: radius 1 cm · radius 4 cm · radius 2 cm.
**Answer:** 3.14 cm² (r = 1), 12.56 cm² (r = 2), 50.24 cm² (r = 4).

**5. (Level 2)** A wheel rolls once. Order these rolls by distance: diameter 20 cm · diameter 50 cm · diameter 30 cm.
**Answer:** 62.8 cm (d = 20), 94.2 cm (d = 30), 157 cm (d = 50).
