# Circles — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Circles Parts of the Circle · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Circles Circumference · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Circles Area of the Disc.

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The line from the centre of a circle to its edge is the ______.
a) diameter  b) radius  c) chord
**Answer: b) radius** (Vietnam, Toán 3, Bài 17, p.52).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A circle has radius 5 cm. Its diameter is ______.
a) 2.5 cm  b) 10 cm  c) 25 cm
**Answer: b) 10 cm** — d = 2r (Sierra Leone, JSS 1, L81).

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A circle has diameter 10 cm. Its circumference (π ≈ 3.14) is ______.
a) 31.4 cm  b) 62.8 cm  c) 314 cm
**Answer: a) 31.4 cm** — C = πd = 3.14 × 10 (Kazakhstan, Grade 6, §1.9).

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A circle has radius 7 cm. Its circumference (π ≈ 3.14) is ______.
a) 21.98 cm  b) 43.96 cm  c) 153.86 cm
**Answer: b) 43.96 cm** — C = 2πr = 2 × 3.14 × 7 (Sierra Leone, JSS 2, L65).

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** A circle has radius 10 cm. Its area (π ≈ 3.14) is ______.
a) 62.8 cm²  b) 314 cm²  c) 31.4 cm²
**Answer: b) 314 cm²** — A = πr² = 3.14 × 100 (Jordan, Grade 7, p.91).

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** A circle has radius 3 cm. Its area (π ≈ 3.14) is ______.
a) 28.26 cm²  b) 18.84 cm²  c) 9.42 cm²
**Answer: a) 28.26 cm²** — A = 3.14 × 3 × 3.

**7. (Sub lesson 3)** A circumference is 62.8 cm (π ≈ 3.14). The diameter is ______.
a) 10 cm  b) 20 cm  c) 31.4 cm
**Answer: b) 20 cm** — d = C ÷ π = 62.8 ÷ 3.14.
