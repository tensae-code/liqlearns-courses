# Circles — short answer

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Level 1)** What is a chord?
**Answer:** A straight line joining two points on the edge of a circle (Egypt, Grade 9, Unit 4, 4-1).

**2. (Level 1)** A circle has diameter 14 cm. What is its radius?
**Answer:** 7 cm — r = d ÷ 2.

**3. (Level 2)** What number do you always get when you divide a circle's circumference by its diameter?
**Answer:** Pi, about 3.14 (Kazakhstan, Grade 6, §1.9 — found with a thread round a glass).

**4. (Level 2)** A circle has diameter 8 cm. Find its circumference (π ≈ 3.14).
**Answer:** 25.12 cm — C = 3.14 × 8.

**5. (Level 3)** A circle has radius 2 cm. Find its area (π ≈ 3.14).
**Answer:** 12.56 cm² — A = 3.14 × 2 × 2.

**6. (Level 3)** A circle has diameter 20 cm. Find its area (π ≈ 3.14).
**Answer:** 314 cm² — radius 10 cm, A = 3.14 × 10 × 10.
