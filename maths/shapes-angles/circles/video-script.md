# Video script — Circles

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Draw a circle! The point in the middle — the **centre**.
From the centre to the edge — the **radius**.
Right across, through the centre — the **diameter**.
The diameter is **twice** the radius. Always!

Now roll a wheel once. How far does it go?
That distance is the **circumference** — the round edge.
And here is the wonder: the circumference divided by the diameter
is always the same number — **pi, about 3.14** — for every circle
in the world!

So: circumference = pi times diameter. **C = πd!**
Or with the radius: **C = 2πr!**

And the inside? The area — how much the circle covers?
**A = πr²** — pi times radius times radius!

A plate of radius 10 cm. Its edge: 2 × 3.14 × 10 = **62.8 cm**.
Its area: 3.14 × 10 × 10 = **314 cm²**!

Now you. Measure a lid. Find its edge and its area!
That is circles.
