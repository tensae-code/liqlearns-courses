# Video script — Tessellations

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Cover the floor. No gaps! No overlaps!

One tile. Slide it — again, again, again.
Flip it! Turn it! **The floor is covered!**

Squares fit. Triangles fit. Hexagons fit — like a honeycomb!
Pentagons? A gap. Always a gap. The corners cannot meet.

Around every meeting point: three hundred and sixty degrees. Exactly.
That is the rule. That is a tessellation!
