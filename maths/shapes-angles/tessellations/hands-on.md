# Hands-on — Tessellations

Tiling with real cut-out shapes.

## Three-shape tiling (Nepal Grade 6)
Cut out ten equilateral triangles, ten squares and ten rectangles. Tile a sheet with each shape alone, the work Nepal's Grade 6 lesson sets (pp.215–223). Photograph or sketch each finished patch.

## Slide, flip, turn (Laos Grade 5)
Cut one non-symmetric tile (an L-shape or a scalene triangle). Make three patches: one using slides only, one using slides and flips, one using turns about a point, including a 90° turn — the moves of Laos's L49 and L50 (pp.112–123).

## The pentagon test
Cut out regular pentagons and try to tile with them. Sketch the smallest gap you cannot close, measure the corner angle, and write the 360° calculation that proves the gap must stay.

## Hexagon honeycomb (Laos Grade 5, L50)
Tile regular hexagons into a honeycomb patch of at least twelve hexagons. Count the meeting points and check: three hexagons meet at each one, 120° + 120° + 120° = 360°.
