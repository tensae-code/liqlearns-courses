**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Match the move: 1) slide, 2) flip, 3) turn about a point. A) rotation B) translation C) reflection
**Answer:** 1–B, 2–C, 3–A — Laos, Grade 5, L49–L50, pp.112–123.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Match the shape to the number meeting at a point: 1) equilateral triangle, 2) square, 3) regular hexagon. A) 4 B) 6 C) 3
**Answer:** 1–B, 2–A, 3–C — 60°, 90° and 120° corners against 360°.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Match the tiling: 1) hexagons only, 2) squares with octagons, 3) mixed non-regular shapes. A) semi-regular B) irregular C) regular
**Answer:** 1–C, 2–A, 3–B — Nepal, Grade 7, Unit 9, pp.75–83.
