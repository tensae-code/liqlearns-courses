Fill each blank with the right word or number.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A covering with no gaps and no ______ is a tessellation.
**Answer:** overlaps — Pakistan, Grade 7, p.163.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Turning a tile about a point is a ______.
**Answer:** rotation — Laos, Grade 5, L50, pp.112–123.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Six equilateral triangles meet at a point: 6 × 60° = ______°.
**Answer:** 360 — the meeting-point rule.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A regular hexagon's corner is ______ degrees.
**Answer:** 120 — 3 × 120° = 360°.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** A tessellation using only squares is a ______ tessellation.
**Answer:** regular — Nepal, Grade 7, Unit 9, pp.75–83.
