# Tessellations — ordering

Put the steps or items in the right order.

**1. (Level 1)** Order the moves in Laos's build: A) tile with translation and rotation B) tile with translation and reflection C) define the covering with no gaps and no overlaps
**Answer:** C, B, A — L49 comes before L50 (pp.112–123).

**2. (Level 2)** Order by corner size, smallest first: square, regular hexagon, equilateral triangle
**Answer:** equilateral triangle (60°), square (90°), regular hexagon (120°) — the corners that pass the 360° test.
