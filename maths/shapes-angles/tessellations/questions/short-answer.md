# Tessellations — short answer

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Level 1)** What are the three moves Laos's Grade 5 lessons use to build tilings?
**Answer:** Translation (slide), reflection (flip) and rotation (turn) — L49 and L50, pp.112–123.

**2. (Level 1)** In one line: what two faults must a tessellation never have?
**Answer:** No gaps and no overlaps.

**3. (Level 2)** How many equilateral triangles meet at a point in their tessellation, and why?
**Answer:** Six — 6 × 60° = 360°.

**4. (Level 2)** Why do rectangles tessellate even though they are not regular?
**Answer:** Their corners are 90°, and 4 × 90° = 360°, so they meet exactly (Nepal, Grade 6, pp.215–223).

**5. (Level 3)** Name Nepal Grade 7's three kinds of tessellation.
**Answer:** Regular, semi-regular and irregular (Unit 9, pp.75–83).

**6. (Level 3)** Give one real place a hexagon tessellation is found.
**Answer:** A honeycomb (also tiled floors in the hexagon pattern of Laos, Grade 5, L50).
