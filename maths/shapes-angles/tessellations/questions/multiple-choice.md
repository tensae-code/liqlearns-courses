# Tessellations — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Level 1)** A tessellation is a covering with…
a) small equal gaps  b) no gaps and no overlaps  c) overlaps allowed at corners
**Answer:** b) no gaps and no overlaps — Pakistan, Grade 7, p.163.

**2. (Level 1)** Sliding a tile without turning or flipping it is a…
a) reflection  b) rotation  c) translation
**Answer:** c) translation — Laos, Grade 5, L49, pp.112–123.

**3. (Level 1)** Flipping a tile over is a…
a) reflection  b) translation  c) glide
**Answer:** a) reflection — Laos, Grade 5, L49, pp.112–123.

**4. (Level 2)** The angles around a meeting point in a tessellation must add to…
a) 180°  b) 270°  c) 360°
**Answer:** c) 360° — a full turn around the point, with nothing left over.

**5. (Level 2)** How many regular hexagons meet at each point of a honeycomb?
a) 3  b) 4  c) 6
**Answer:** a) 3 — 3 × 120° = 360° (Laos, Grade 5, L50, pp.112–123).

**6. (Level 2)** Regular pentagons alone cannot tessellate because their corner is…
a) 60°  b) 108°, and 108° does not divide 360° evenly in a meeting  c) 90°
**Answer:** b) 108°, and 108° does not divide 360° evenly in a meeting — three give 324° (gap), four give 432° (overlap).

**7. (Level 3)** A tiling of squares and regular octagons in one repeated pattern is…
a) regular  b) semi-regular  c) irregular
**Answer:** b) semi-regular — more than one regular shape, one repeated pattern (Nepal, Grade 7, Unit 9, pp.75–83).

**8. (Level 3)** A slide joined to a flip is a…
a) rotation  b) glide reflection  c) translation only
**Answer:** b) glide reflection — South Africa workbook rows as filed.
