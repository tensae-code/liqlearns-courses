# True or false — Types of Lines

1. (Layer 1) A horizontal line stands upright, from top to bottom. **False** — that is a vertical line; a horizontal line runs flat from side to side. Rwanda, Primary pupil's book, Year 2 (English), Unit 10, printed p. 209.
2. (Layer 2) A zigzag line can be a closed line. **True** — if its end meets its start. Rwanda, Primary pupil's book, Year 2 (English), Unit 10, printed pp. 210, 212.
3. (Layer 3) A curved line is made of short straight parts. **False** — that describes a zigzag line; a curved line bends smoothly. Rwanda, Primary pupil's book, Year 2 (English), Unit 10, printed p. 212.
