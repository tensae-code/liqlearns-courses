# True or false — Types of Lines

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Layer 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Lines Straight in Four Directions · (Layer 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Lines Closed and Open · (Layer 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Lines Curved, Zigzag and Naming Pictured Lines.

1. (Sub lesson 1) A horizontal line stands upright, from top to bottom. **False** — that is a vertical line; a horizontal line runs flat from side to side. Rwanda, Primary pupil's book, Year 2 (English), Unit 10, printed p. 209.
2. (Sub lesson 2) A zigzag line can be a closed line. **True** — if its end meets its start. Rwanda, Primary pupil's book, Year 2 (English), Unit 10, printed pp. 210, 212.
3. (Sub lesson 3) A curved line is made of short straight parts. **False** — that describes a zigzag line; a curved line bends smoothly. Rwanda, Primary pupil's book, Year 2 (English), Unit 10, printed p. 212.
