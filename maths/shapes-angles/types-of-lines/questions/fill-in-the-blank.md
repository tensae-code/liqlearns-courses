# Fill in the blank — Types of Lines

1. (Layer 1) A straight line that runs flat from side to side, like the top of a table, is called ___.
   **Answer: horizontal.** Rwanda, Primary pupil's book, Year 2 (English), Unit 10, printed p. 209.

2. (Layer 1) A straight line that slants is called an ___ line.
   **Answer: oblique.** Rwanda, Primary pupil's book, Year 2 (English), Unit 10, printed p. 209.

3. (Layer 2) A line whose end comes back and meets its start, with no gap, is a ___ line.
   **Answer: closed.** Rwanda, Primary pupil's book, Year 2 (English), Unit 10, printed p. 210.
