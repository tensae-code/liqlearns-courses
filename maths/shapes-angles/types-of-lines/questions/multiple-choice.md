# Multiple choice — Types of Lines

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Layer 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Lines Straight in Four Directions · (Layer 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Lines Closed and Open · (Layer 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Lines Curved, Zigzag and Naming Pictured Lines.

1. (Sub lesson 1) The edge of a standing door, running from top to bottom, is a:
   A. Horizontal line B. Vertical line C. Curved line D. Zigzag line
   **Answer: B** — Rwanda, Primary pupil's book, Year 2 (English), Unit 10, printed p. 209.

2. (Sub lesson 2) A line that does NOT come back to its starting point is:
   A. A closed line B. An open line C. A vertical line D. A closed zigzag line
   **Answer: B** — Rwanda, Primary pupil's book, Year 2 (English), Unit 10, printed p. 211.

3. (Sub lesson 3) A line made of short straight parts joined at sharp turns, like a row of mountain peaks, is a:
   A. Curved line B. Horizontal line C. Zigzag line D. Closed line
   **Answer: C** — Rwanda, Primary pupil's book, Year 2 (English), Unit 10, printed p. 212.
