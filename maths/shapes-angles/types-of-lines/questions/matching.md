1. (Sub lesson 1) Match each description to its line:
   (a) runs flat from side to side (b) stands upright from top to bottom (c) slants, leaning to the right as it rises (d) slants, leaning to the left as it rises
   Lines: vertical, oblique towards the left, horizontal, oblique towards the right.
   **Answer: (a) horizontal, (b) vertical, (c) oblique towards the right, (d) oblique towards the left.** Rwanda, Primary pupil's book, Year 2 (English), Unit 10, printed p. 209.
