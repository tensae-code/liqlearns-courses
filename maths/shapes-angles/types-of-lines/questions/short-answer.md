# Short answer — Types of Lines

1. (Layer 2) A zigzag line is drawn so that its end joins exactly onto its start. Is it open or closed?
   **Answer: Closed — the end meets the start, with no gap.** Rwanda, Primary pupil's book, Year 2 (English), Unit 10, printed pp. 210, 212.

2. (Layer 3) In one sentence, say how a curved line and a zigzag line are different.
   **Answer: A curved line bends smoothly with no straight part; a zigzag line is made of short straight parts joined at sharp turns.** Rwanda, Primary pupil's book, Year 2 (English), Unit 10, printed p. 212.
