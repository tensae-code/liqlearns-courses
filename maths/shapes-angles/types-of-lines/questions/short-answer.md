# Short answer — Types of Lines

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Layer 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Lines Straight in Four Directions · (Layer 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Lines Closed and Open · (Layer 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Lines Curved, Zigzag and Naming Pictured Lines.

1. (Sub lesson 2) A zigzag line is drawn so that its end joins exactly onto its start. Is it open or closed?
   **Answer: Closed — the end meets the start, with no gap.** Rwanda, Primary pupil's book, Year 2 (English), Unit 10, printed pp. 210, 212.

2. (Sub lesson 3) In one sentence, say how a curved line and a zigzag line are different.
   **Answer: A curved line bends smoothly with no straight part; a zigzag line is made of short straight parts joined at sharp turns.** Rwanda, Primary pupil's book, Year 2 (English), Unit 10, printed p. 212.
