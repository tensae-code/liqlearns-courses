## Sub lesson 1 — Lines Straight in Four Directions
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Name a straight line's direction: horizontal, vertical, oblique towards the right, oblique towards the left. Pass mark: 4 of 5.

## Sub lesson 2 — Lines Closed and Open
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Tell a closed line from an open line, including a closed zigzag, by checking whether the line comes back to its start. Pass mark: 4 of 5.

## Sub lesson 3 — Lines Curved, Zigzag and Naming Pictured Lines
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Give the full name of any pictured line — straight with its direction, closed, open, curved or zigzag — without prompting. Pass mark: 4 of 5.

## What "maxed" means here

The learner can look at any line in a picture — in a book, on a fence, in the edge of a field — and name it correctly and completely, the exact skill Rwanda's Primary 2 book practises in its application activities.
