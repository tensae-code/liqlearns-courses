# Levels — Types of Lines

One lesson, one level (Level 145). The depth layers inside it are ranks.

## Depth layers

- **Layer 1 — Straight lines.** Name a straight line's direction: horizontal, vertical, oblique towards the right, oblique towards the left. Pass mark: 4 of 5.
- **Layer 2 — Closed or open.** Tell a closed line from an open line, including a closed zigzag, by checking whether the line comes back to its start. Pass mark: 4 of 5.
- **Layer 3 — Name any pictured line.** Give the full name of any pictured line — straight with its direction, closed, open, curved or zigzag — without prompting. Pass mark: 4 of 5.

## What "maxed" means here

The learner can look at any line in a picture — in a book, on a fence, in the edge of a field — and name it correctly and completely, the exact skill Rwanda's Primary 2 book practises in its application activities.
