# Hands-on — Types of Lines

## Activity 1 — Lines in the room (10 minutes)

The learner finds and points to one line of each straight kind in the room: a horizontal line (a table edge, a shelf), a vertical line (a door edge, a wall corner), an oblique line towards the right and one towards the left (a leaning broom, a roof edge). Each find is named aloud in full before the next hunt starts.

## Activity 2 — String lines (10 minutes)

With a piece of string on the table, the learner makes: a closed line (the ends meet), an open line (the ends stay apart), a closed zigzag (sharp turns, ends meeting) and an open curved line. A partner names each one; then they swap roles.

## Activity 3 — Draw and name (5 minutes)

The learner draws one line of every kind on one page — horizontal, vertical, oblique right, oblique left, closed, open, curved, zigzag — and writes or says the name under each drawing.
