# Shapes — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB
LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure — README, "Units, Lessons and Sub
Lessons"). Practice is tied to the skill number, as before. The
questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson
view. The sub lessons are the depths inside this one thing — naming and
sorting flat shapes — set by the pattern across the countries'
books: polygons named by sides first, then triangles sorted two
ways, then the quadrilateral family.

Mapping from the old depth layers: old Level 1 = Sub lesson 1; old Level 2 = Sub lesson 2; old Level 3 = Sub lesson 3.

## Sub lesson 1 — Shapes Polygons by Their Sides
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: name a triangle,
a quadrilateral, a pentagon and a hexagon by counting sides; say
what a side, a vertex and a regular polygon are.

## Sub lesson 2 — Shapes Triangles Two Ways
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: sort a triangle
by its sides (equilateral, isosceles, scalene) and by its angles
(acute, right, obtuse) without mixing the two sortings.

## Sub lesson 3 — Shapes The Quadrilateral Family
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: place any
quadrilateral in the parallelogram family — parallelogram,
rectangle, rhombus, square, trapezium, kite — and say why a square
is both a rectangle and a rhombus.
