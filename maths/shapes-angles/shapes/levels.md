# Shapes — questions by level

Practice by level, tied to the skill number. The questions live in
the type files; this file is the by-level view. The levels are the
depths inside this one thing — naming and sorting flat shapes — set
by the pattern across the countries' books: polygons named by sides
first, then triangles sorted two ways, then the quadrilateral family.

## Level 1 — polygons by their sides
Level 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: name a triangle,
a quadrilateral, a pentagon and a hexagon by counting sides; say
what a side, a vertex and a regular polygon are.

## Level 2 — triangles two ways
Level 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: sort a triangle
by its sides (equilateral, isosceles, scalene) and by its angles
(acute, right, obtuse) without mixing the two sortings.

## Level 3 — the quadrilateral family
Level 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: place any
quadrilateral in the parallelogram family — parallelogram,
rectangle, rhombus, square, trapezium, kite — and say why a square
is both a rectangle and a rhombus.
