# Video script — Shapes

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Look around you. Flat shapes are everywhere!

A shape with straight sides, closed all the way round — that is a
**polygon**.

Count the sides. Three sides — **triangle**!
Four sides — **quadrilateral**!
Five sides — **pentagon**! Six sides — **hexagon**!

Now the triangle, two ways.
By its sides: all three equal — **equilateral**.
Two equal — **isosceles**. None equal — **scalene**.

By its angles: all sharp — **acute**.
One square corner — **right**. One wide angle — **obtuse**.

And the four-sided family!
A **parallelogram** — opposite sides parallel, opposite sides equal.
Give it four right angles — **rectangle**!
Give it four equal sides — **rhombus**!
Give it both — **square**!

Now you. Point at a shape. Count its sides. Name it!
That is shapes.
