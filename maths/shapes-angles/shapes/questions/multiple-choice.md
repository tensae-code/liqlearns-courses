# Shapes — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Shapes Polygons by Their Sides · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Shapes Triangles Two Ways · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Shapes The Quadrilateral Family.

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A polygon with 5 sides is a ______.
a) quadrilateral  b) pentagon  c) hexagon
**Answer: b) pentagon.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The corners where two sides of a polygon meet are called ______.
a) vertices  b) edges  c) faces
**Answer: a) vertices** — one corner is a vertex (Egypt, Grade 3, Chapter 4).

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A triangle with all three sides equal is ______.
a) isosceles  b) scalene  c) equilateral
**Answer: c) equilateral** (Jordan, Grade 5, p.70).

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A triangle with one right angle is a ______ triangle.
a) acute  b) right  c) obtuse
**Answer: b) right** (Jordan, Grade 5, p.76).

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** A parallelogram with four right angles is a ______.
a) rhombus  b) rectangle  c) trapezium
**Answer: b) rectangle** (Jordan, Grade 8, p.91).

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** A quadrilateral with four equal sides and four right angles is a ______.
a) square  b) rhombus  c) kite
**Answer: a) square** — it is both a rectangle and a rhombus (Jordan, Grade 8, p.91).

**7. (Sub lesson 3)** A quadrilateral with one pair of parallel sides is a ______.
a) parallelogram  b) trapezium  c) kite
**Answer: b) trapezium** (Egypt, Grade 5, pp.54–58).
