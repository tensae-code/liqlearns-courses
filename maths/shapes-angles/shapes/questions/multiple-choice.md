# Shapes — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Level 1)** A polygon with 5 sides is a ______.
a) quadrilateral  b) pentagon  c) hexagon
**Answer: b) pentagon.**

**2. (Level 1)** The corners where two sides of a polygon meet are called ______.
a) vertices  b) edges  c) faces
**Answer: a) vertices** — one corner is a vertex (Egypt, Grade 3, Chapter 4).

**3. (Level 2)** A triangle with all three sides equal is ______.
a) isosceles  b) scalene  c) equilateral
**Answer: c) equilateral** (Jordan, Grade 5, p.70).

**4. (Level 2)** A triangle with one right angle is a ______ triangle.
a) acute  b) right  c) obtuse
**Answer: b) right** (Jordan, Grade 5, p.76).

**5. (Level 3)** A parallelogram with four right angles is a ______.
a) rhombus  b) rectangle  c) trapezium
**Answer: b) rectangle** (Jordan, Grade 8, p.91).

**6. (Level 3)** A quadrilateral with four equal sides and four right angles is a ______.
a) square  b) rhombus  c) kite
**Answer: a) square** — it is both a rectangle and a rhombus (Jordan, Grade 8, p.91).

**7. (Level 3)** A quadrilateral with one pair of parallel sides is a ______.
a) parallelogram  b) trapezium  c) kite
**Answer: b) trapezium** (Egypt, Grade 5, pp.54–58).
