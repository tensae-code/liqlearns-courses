# Shapes — matching

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Shapes Polygons by Their Sides · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Shapes Triangles Two Ways · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Shapes The Quadrilateral Family.

Match each shape to its description.

**Set 1 (Sub lesson 1)**
1. triangle — **3 sides**
2. quadrilateral — **4 sides**
3. pentagon — **5 sides**
4. hexagon — **6 sides**
5. regular polygon — **all sides equal and all angles equal**

**Set 2 (Sub lesson 2)**
1. equilateral — **all three sides equal**
2. isosceles — **two sides equal**
3. scalene — **no sides equal**
4. acute — **all three angles sharp**
5. obtuse — **one angle wide, more than a right angle**

**Set 3 (Sub lesson 3)**
1. parallelogram — **both pairs of opposite sides parallel**
2. rectangle — **parallelogram with four right angles**
3. rhombus — **parallelogram with four equal sides**
4. square — **four equal sides AND four right angles**
5. trapezium — **one pair of parallel sides**
