# Shapes — short answer

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Shapes Polygons by Their Sides · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Shapes Triangles Two Ways · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Shapes The Quadrilateral Family.

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the polygon with 3 sides, and the polygon with 6 sides.
**Answer:** triangle; hexagon.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is a regular polygon?
**Answer:** A polygon with all its sides equal and all its angles equal (Greece, Grade 6, ch.56).

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A triangle has sides 5 cm, 5 cm and 8 cm. What kind is it by its sides?
**Answer:** Isosceles — two sides equal (Jordan, Grade 5, p.70).

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A triangle has angles 90°, 45° and 45°. Name it two ways.
**Answer:** Right-angled (by angles) and isosceles (by sides — the two 45° angles sit on equal sides).

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** How is a rhombus different from a square?
**Answer:** A rhombus has four equal sides but its angles are not right angles; a square has four equal sides AND four right angles (Jordan, Grade 8, p.91).

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** A shape has two pairs of adjacent sides equal. What is it?
**Answer:** A kite (Egypt, Grade 5, pp.54–58).
