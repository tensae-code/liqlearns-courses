# Shapes — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with a word from the box.

**Box:** triangle · quadrilateral · pentagon · equilateral · rhombus · trapezium

**1. (Level 1)** A polygon with 4 sides is a ______.
**Answer: quadrilateral.**

**2. (Level 1)** A polygon with 5 sides is a ______.
**Answer: pentagon.**

**3. (Level 2)** A triangle with all three sides equal is ______.
**Answer: equilateral.**

**4. (Level 2)** A triangle with no sides equal is scalene; a triangle with three sides is a ______.
**Answer: triangle.**

**5. (Level 3)** A parallelogram with four equal sides is a ______.
**Answer: rhombus.**

**6. (Level 3)** A quadrilateral with one pair of parallel sides is a ______.
**Answer: trapezium.**
