# Shapes — fill in the blank

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Shapes Polygons by Their Sides · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Shapes Triangles Two Ways · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Shapes The Quadrilateral Family.

Fill each blank with a word from the box.

**Box:** triangle · quadrilateral · pentagon · equilateral · rhombus · trapezium

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A polygon with 4 sides is a ______.
**Answer: quadrilateral.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A polygon with 5 sides is a ______.
**Answer: pentagon.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A triangle with all three sides equal is ______.
**Answer: equilateral.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A triangle with no sides equal is scalene; a triangle with three sides is a ______.
**Answer: triangle.**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** A parallelogram with four equal sides is a ______.
**Answer: rhombus.**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** A quadrilateral with one pair of parallel sides is a ______.
**Answer: trapezium.**
