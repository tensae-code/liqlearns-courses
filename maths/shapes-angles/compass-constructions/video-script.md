# Video script — Constructions with ruler and compass

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

No measuring! Only two tools: a ruler, and compasses.

Open the compasses wide — more than half the line.
Arc up. Arc down. Do it again from the other end.
The arcs cross. Join the crossings. **The line is cut in half!**

Now the angle. Point in the corner. One arc cuts both sides.
Two small arcs inside. Join to the corner. **Half and half!**

Sixty degrees — the compass does it alone.
Cut sixty in half: thirty! Join sixty and ninety: one hundred and fifty!

Every arc has a reason. Say the reason, and the drawing is yours.
