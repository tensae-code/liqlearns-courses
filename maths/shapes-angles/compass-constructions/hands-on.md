# Hands-on — Constructions with ruler and compass

Real compass work, the way the books set it.

## Bisect a segment (Sierra Leone SSS 2, L79)
Draw a segment 10 cm long. Open the compasses to more than half of it. From each end, draw an arc above and an arc below. Join the two crossing points with the ruler. Measure the two halves: each must be 5 cm.

## Bisect an angle and check it (Sierra Leone SSS 2, L80)
Draw an angle of about 70° with a protractor, then put the protractor away. Bisect the angle with compasses only. Check with the protractor: each half must measure 35°.

## The angle ladder
Construct 60°. Bisect it to make 30°. Construct 90°. Join 60° and 90° on one line to make 150°. Label every angle you made and write, beside each one, the steps that made it.

## No-compass construction
Repeat the segment bisection using string or a strip of paper as the compass, the substitute Sierra Leone's book teaches (L79, p.90). Compare the two results: the halves must agree.
