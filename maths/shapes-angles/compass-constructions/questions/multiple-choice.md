# Constructions with ruler and compass — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Constructions Bisections · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Constructions The Special Angles · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Constructions Parallels, Copying and Reasons.

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** To bisect a segment, the compass opening must be…
a) less than half the segment  b) exactly the segment  c) more than half the segment
**Answer:** c) more than half the segment — with less than half, the arcs from the two ends never cross (Sierra Leone SSS 2, L79, p.90).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The line joining the crossing arcs in a segment bisection is called the…
a) parallel  b) perpendicular bisector  c) diameter
**Answer:** b) perpendicular bisector — it cuts the segment in half at 90° (Pakistan, Grade 6, pp.140–141).

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** In angle bisection, the first arc must…
a) cut both sides of the angle  b) touch the vertex only  c) be drawn with the ruler
**Answer:** a) cut both sides of the angle — the two cut points carry the next arcs (Sierra Leone SSS 2, L80, p.93).

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Bisecting a constructed 90° angle gives…
a) 30°  b) 45°  c) 60°
**Answer:** b) 45° — half of 90° is 45° (Sierra Leone SSS 2, L82, p.99).

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** 75° is constructed by joining…
a) 60° + 15° only by protractor  b) 45° and 30°  c) 90° and 90°
**Answer:** b) 45° and 30° — 75 = 45 + 30 (Sierra Leone SSS 2, L83, p.101).

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** A compass stepped around its own arc at the segment's own length constructs…
a) 90°  b) 45°  c) 60°
**Answer:** c) 60° — the equal-sided triangle the stepping makes has 60° corners (Sierra Leone SSS 2, L81, p.96).

**7. (Sub lesson 3)** Every point on a perpendicular bisector is…
a) nearer one end  b) the same distance from both ends of the segment  c) on the segment
**Answer:** b) the same distance from both ends of the segment — that equal distance is the justification Iran's book requires (Geometry (1), pp.10–16).

**8. (Sub lesson 3)** With no compasses, Sierra Leone's book allows a substitute made from…
a) string or a strip of paper  b) a protractor  c) a set square only
**Answer:** a) string or a strip of paper — the substitute is taught in L79 itself (p.90).
