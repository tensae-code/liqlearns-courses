# Constructions with ruler and compass — ordering

Put the steps or items in the right order.

**1. (Level 1)** Order the segment-bisection steps: A) join the crossing points B) draw arcs from the second end C) open the compasses to more than half D) draw arcs from the first end
**Answer:** C, D, B, A — Sierra Leone SSS 2, L79, p.90.

**2. (Level 2)** Order the making of 15°: A) bisect 30° B) construct 60° C) bisect 60°
**Answer:** B, C, A — each bisection halves the angle (Sierra Leone SSS 2, L82, p.99).
