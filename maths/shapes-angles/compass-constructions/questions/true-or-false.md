# Constructions with ruler and compass — true or false

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Constructions Bisections · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Constructions The Special Angles · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Constructions Parallels, Copying and Reasons.

Say true or false, and say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A protractor constructs an angle in this lesson.
**Answer:** False — the protractor only checks; the compasses construct (Sierra Leone SSS 2, L80, p.93).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The two inner arcs in an angle bisection must use one equal opening.
**Answer:** True — unequal openings would not meet on the bisector.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** 15° is made by joining 90° and 60°.
**Answer:** False — 15° is made by bisecting 30°; joining 90° and 60° makes 150°.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** 120° can be constructed with compasses.
**Answer:** True — Sierra Leone SSS 2, L81, p.96.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** A construction in Iran's Geometry (1) is complete without a justification.
**Answer:** False — Iran teaches constructions with justifications (pp.10–16).
