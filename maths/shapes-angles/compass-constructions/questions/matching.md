**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Match: 1) bisect a segment, 2) bisect an angle, 3) construct 60°. A) compass stepped at the segment's own length B) arcs from both ends crossing above and below C) arc cutting both sides, then two inner arcs
**Answer:** 1–B, 2–C, 3–A — Sierra Leone SSS 2, L79–L81, pp.90, 93, 96.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Match the result: 1) half of 90°, 2) half of 60°, 3) 90° joined to 60°. A) 30° B) 150° C) 45°
**Answer:** 1–C, 2–A, 3–B — Sierra Leone SSS 2, L82–L83, pp.99, 101.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Match the job: 1) copy a segment, 2) copy an angle, 3) check a bisection. A) protractor B) carry the length in the compasses C) carry the opening in the compasses
**Answer:** 1–B, 2–C, 3–A — Laos, Grade 6, pp.87–107; Sierra Leone SSS 2, L80, p.93.
