# Constructions with ruler and compass — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word or number.

**1. (Level 1)** A line that cuts a segment in half at 90° is its perpendicular ______.
**Answer:** bisector — Pakistan, Grade 6, pp.140–141.

**2. (Level 1)** To bisect an angle, the compass point starts on the ______.
**Answer:** vertex — Sierra Leone SSS 2, L80, p.93.

**3. (Level 2)** Half of 30° is ______ degrees.
**Answer:** 15 — Sierra Leone SSS 2, L82, p.99.

**4. (Level 2)** 90° + 60° makes ______ degrees.
**Answer:** 150 — Sierra Leone SSS 2, L83, p.101.

**5. (Level 3)** Copying a segment means carrying its ______ in the compasses to the new line.
**Answer:** length — Laos, Grade 6, pp.87–107.
