# Constructions with ruler and compass — short answer

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Constructions Bisections · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Constructions The Special Angles · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Constructions Parallels, Copying and Reasons.

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What two tools does a ruler-and-compass construction allow?
**Answer:** A ruler and a pair of compasses.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** You bisect a 14 cm segment. How long is each half?
**Answer:** 7 cm.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** You bisect 60° twice. What angle do you get the second time?
**Answer:** 15° — first 30°, then half of 30°.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which two constructed angles join to make 105°?
**Answer:** 60° and 45°.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** In your own words: why do the crossing arcs in a segment bisection find the middle?
**Answer:** Both arcs use the same compass opening from each end, so their crossing points are the same distance from both ends — the line through them passes through the middle.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** Name the four stages of a construction problem as Armenia's book teaches the method.
**Answer:** What is given; what is to be made; the construction steps; the proof that the steps work (Geometry 7, Ch.II §10, §17).
