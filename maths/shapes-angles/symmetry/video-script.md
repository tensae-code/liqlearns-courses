# Video script — Symmetry

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Take a paper shape. Fold it in half.
Do the two halves land exactly on each other?
Yes? Then the fold is a **line of symmetry**!

A square — fold it four ways! **Four lines!**
A rectangle — fold it two ways! **Two lines!**
An equilateral triangle — **three lines!**
A circle — fold it ANY way! **Endless lines!**

Now the turn test. Turn the shape.
Does it look the same again before it comes all the way round?
A plus sign — turn it a quarter, it matches! Half, it matches!
Three quarters, it matches! All the way round, it matches!
**Four matches — order 4!**

A square — **order 4!** A rectangle — **order 2!**
An equilateral triangle — **order 3!**

Now you. Fold it. Turn it. Count the matches!
That is symmetry.
