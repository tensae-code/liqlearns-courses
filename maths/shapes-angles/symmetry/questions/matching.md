# Symmetry — matching

Match each shape to its symmetry counts.

**Set 1 (Level 1) — lines of symmetry**
1. square — **4**
2. rectangle (not a square) — **2**
3. equilateral triangle — **3**
4. circle — **endless**
5. scalene triangle — **0**

**Set 2 (Level 2) — order of rotational symmetry**
1. square — **4**
2. rectangle (not a square) — **2**
3. equilateral triangle — **3**
4. plus sign — **4**
5. circle — **endless**

**Set 3 (Level 3) — both kinds together**
1. square — **4 lines, order 4**
2. parallelogram (not a rectangle) — **0 lines, order 2**
3. kite — **1 line, order 1**
4. regular hexagon — **6 lines, order 6**
5. scalene triangle — **0 lines, order 1**
