# Symmetry — ordering

Put each set in order, fewest first. (This question type exists
for this lesson because symmetry counts ARE an order.)

**1. (Level 1)** Order by lines of symmetry: square · rectangle · equilateral triangle.
**Answer:** rectangle (2), equilateral triangle (3), square (4).

**2. (Level 1)** Order by lines of symmetry: circle · scalene triangle · kite.
**Answer:** scalene triangle (0), kite (1), circle (endless).

**3. (Level 2)** Order by order of rotational symmetry: equilateral triangle · rectangle · square.
**Answer:** rectangle (2), equilateral triangle (3), square (4).

**4. (Level 3)** Order by lines of symmetry: regular pentagon · regular hexagon · square.
**Answer:** square (4), regular pentagon (5), regular hexagon (6).

**5. (Level 3)** Order by order of rotational symmetry: plus sign · rectangle · equilateral triangle.
**Answer:** rectangle (2), equilateral triangle (3), plus sign (4).
