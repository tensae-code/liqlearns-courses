# Symmetry — true or false

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Symmetry Line Symmetry · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Symmetry Rotational Symmetry · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Symmetry Both Kinds Together.

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A line of symmetry must make the two halves match exactly, edge for edge.
**True** — the fold test (Greece, Grade 3, ch.43).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A rectangle has 4 lines of symmetry.
**False** — a rectangle (not a square) has 2; a fold through opposite corners does not match.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The order of rotational symmetry counts the matches in one full turn, including the starting position come round again.
**True** — the 360° match is one of the matches (Sierra Leone, JSS 2, L96).

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A shape with order 1 has no rotational symmetry worth the name.
**True** — it matches only when it comes all the way round to the start.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Every shape with line symmetry also has rotational symmetry.
**False** — a kite has a line of symmetry and order 1.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** A regular hexagon has 6 lines of symmetry.
**True** — a regular polygon with n sides has n lines of symmetry (Libya, Grade 9, 7-3).
