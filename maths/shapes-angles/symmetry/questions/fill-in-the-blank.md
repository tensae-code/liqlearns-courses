# Symmetry — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with a word from the box.

**Box:** fold · axis · order · rotational · endless · zero

**1. (Level 1)** Test line symmetry by ______ing the shape.
**Answer: fold.**

**2. (Level 1)** The fold line is also called the ______ of symmetry.
**Answer: axis.**

**3. (Level 2)** A shape that matches itself when turned has ______ symmetry.
**Answer: rotational.**

**4. (Level 2)** How many times a shape matches in one full turn is its ______.
**Answer: order.**

**5. (Level 3)** A circle has ______ lines of symmetry.
**Answer: endless.**

**6. (Level 3)** A scalene triangle has ______ lines of symmetry.
**Answer: zero.**
