# Symmetry — fill in the blank

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Symmetry Line Symmetry · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Symmetry Rotational Symmetry · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Symmetry Both Kinds Together.

Fill each blank with a word from the box.

**Box:** fold · axis · order · rotational · endless · zero

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Test line symmetry by ______ing the shape.
**Answer: fold.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The fold line is also called the ______ of symmetry.
**Answer: axis.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A shape that matches itself when turned has ______ symmetry.
**Answer: rotational.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** How many times a shape matches in one full turn is its ______.
**Answer: order.**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** A circle has ______ lines of symmetry.
**Answer: endless.**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** A scalene triangle has ______ lines of symmetry.
**Answer: zero.**
