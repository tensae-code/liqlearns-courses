# Symmetry — short answer

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Symmetry Line Symmetry · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Symmetry Rotational Symmetry · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Symmetry Both Kinds Together.

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the fold line of a symmetrical shape called?
**Answer:** The line of symmetry (or axis of symmetry) (Greece, Grade 6, ch.60).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** How many lines of symmetry does an equilateral triangle have?
**Answer:** 3.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is the order of rotational symmetry of a rectangle (not a square)?
**Answer:** 2 — it matches at 180° and 360°.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A shape matches itself 3 times in one full turn. What is its order?
**Answer:** 3.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** A shape has 1 line of symmetry and order 1. Name a shape it could be.
**Answer:** A kite (or a heart, or a house shape).

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** What is central symmetry?
**Answer:** Symmetry about a point: every point of the shape has a matching point exactly opposite it through the centre (Kazakhstan, Grade 6, §6.6).
