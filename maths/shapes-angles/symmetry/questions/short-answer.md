# Symmetry — short answer

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Level 1)** What is the fold line of a symmetrical shape called?
**Answer:** The line of symmetry (or axis of symmetry) (Greece, Grade 6, ch.60).

**2. (Level 1)** How many lines of symmetry does an equilateral triangle have?
**Answer:** 3.

**3. (Level 2)** What is the order of rotational symmetry of a rectangle (not a square)?
**Answer:** 2 — it matches at 180° and 360°.

**4. (Level 2)** A shape matches itself 3 times in one full turn. What is its order?
**Answer:** 3.

**5. (Level 3)** A shape has 1 line of symmetry and order 1. Name a shape it could be.
**Answer:** A kite (or a heart, or a house shape).

**6. (Level 3)** What is central symmetry?
**Answer:** Symmetry about a point: every point of the shape has a matching point exactly opposite it through the centre (Kazakhstan, Grade 6, §6.6).
