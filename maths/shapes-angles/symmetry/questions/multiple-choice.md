# Symmetry — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Level 1)** A shape has line symmetry when ______.
a) it can be turned to match itself  b) it can be folded so the two halves match exactly  c) it has four sides
**Answer: b) it can be folded so the two halves match exactly** (Greece, Grade 3, ch.43).

**2. (Level 1)** How many lines of symmetry does a square have?
a) 2  b) 3  c) 4
**Answer: c) 4** (Libya, Grade 9, 7-3).

**3. (Level 1)** How many lines of symmetry does a rectangle (not a square) have?
a) 0  b) 2  c) 4
**Answer: b) 2.**

**4. (Level 2)** The order of rotational symmetry of a square is ______.
a) 2  b) 3  c) 4
**Answer: c) 4** — it matches at 90°, 180°, 270° and 360°.

**5. (Level 2)** A plus sign turned a quarter-turn matches itself. Its order is ______.
a) 2  b) 4  c) 1
**Answer: b) 4** (Sierra Leone, JSS 2, L96 — the plus-sign example at 90°).

**6. (Level 3)** Which shape has rotational symmetry but NO lines of symmetry?
a) a kite  b) a parallelogram (not a rectangle)  c) an equilateral triangle
**Answer: b) a parallelogram (not a rectangle)** — order 2, 0 lines.

**7. (Level 3)** A circle has ______ lines of symmetry.
a) 1  b) 4  c) endless — any line through the centre
**Answer: c) endless — any line through the centre.**
