# Video script — Constructing triangles and quadrilaterals

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Three sides are enough! Draw the base.
Arc from this end. Arc from that end. They cross — **there is the top corner!**

Two sides and the angle BETWEEN them — enough again!
Two angles and the side between them — enough again!

Four sides now. A square: four equal sides, four square corners.
Push the top sideways — a parallelogram! Keep the sides, lose the square corners.
Equal sides, slanted: a rhombus. One pair of sides running together: a trapezium.

Give me the right pieces, and I will build the exact shape. Every time.
