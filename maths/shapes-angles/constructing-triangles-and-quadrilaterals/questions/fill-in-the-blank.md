# Constructing triangles and quadrilaterals — fill in the blank

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Constructing Triangles from Sides and Angles · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Constructing Quadrilaterals · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Constructing Right-Angled Triangles and Centres.

Fill each blank with the right word or number.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** SSS stands for side, ______, side.
**Answer:** side — the three given sides.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In ASA, the given side lies ______ the two given angles.
**Answer:** between — Sierra Leone SSS 2, L86, p.109.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A square is a rhombus with ______ angles.
**Answer:** right — four equal sides plus right angles make the square.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A trapezium has ______ pair(s) of parallel sides.
**Answer:** one — Sierra Leone SSS 2, L89, p.118.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** The three angle bisectors of a triangle meet at the ______.
**Answer:** incentre — Pakistan, Grade 8, pp.161–170.
