# Constructing triangles and quadrilaterals — short answer

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Level 1)** How many triangles can be built from sides 5 cm, 6 cm and 7 cm (ignoring flips and turns)?
**Answer:** One — SSS fixes the shape.

**2. (Level 1)** Name the construction: two angles of 50° and 70° and the side between them are given.
**Answer:** ASA.

**3. (Level 2)** What extra given turns a parallelogram construction into a rectangle construction?
**Answer:** A right angle (90°) as the given angle.

**4. (Level 2)** A kite's equal sides come in what arrangement?
**Answer:** Two pairs of adjacent sides equal — the pairs meet at opposite corners.

**5. (Level 3)** Where is the circumcentre found?
**Answer:** At the meeting point of the perpendicular bisectors of the three sides (Pakistan, Grade 8, pp.161–170).

**6. (Level 3)** A word problem asks for a plot shaped like a triangle with sides 30 m, 40 m, 50 m on paper at a smaller size. Which construction do you use?
**Answer:** SSS — the three sides are the givens (Sierra Leone SSS 2, L90, p.121).
