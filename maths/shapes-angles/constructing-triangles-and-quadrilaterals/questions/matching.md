**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Match the givens to the name: 1) three sides, 2) two sides + included angle, 3) two angles + side between. A) ASA B) SSS C) SAS

**Answer:** 1–B, 2–C, 3–A — Yemen, Grade 4 Part 2, pp.100, 103; Sierra Leone SSS 2, L88–L89, pp.115, 118.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Match the centre to its lines: 1) incentre, 2) circumcentre. A) perpendicular bisectors of the sides B) bisectors of the angles
**Answer:** 1–B, 2–A — Pakistan, Grade 8, pp.161–170.
