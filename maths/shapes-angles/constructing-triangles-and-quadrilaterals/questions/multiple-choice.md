# Constructing triangles and quadrilaterals — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Level 1)** An SSS construction is given…
a) three sides  b) two angles and a side  c) one side and one angle
**Answer:** a) three sides — Sierra Leone SSS 2, L84, p.103.

**2. (Level 1)** In SAS, the given angle must be…
a) opposite the longer side  b) between the two given sides  c) the right angle
**Answer:** b) between the two given sides — the included angle (Yemen, Grade 5 Part 2, p.145).

**3. (Level 1)** Sides of 2 cm, 3 cm and 6 cm…
a) make one triangle  b) make many triangles  c) make no triangle
**Answer:** c) make no triangle — 2 + 3 is less than 6, so the arcs never meet.

**4. (Level 2)** A rectangle construction starts from…
a) one side only  b) its length and its width  c) its diagonal only
**Answer:** b) its length and its width — Yemen, Grade 4 Part 2, pp.100, 103.

**5. (Level 2)** A rhombus is constructed from…
a) two sides and the angle between them  b) three angles  c) its area
**Answer:** a) two sides and the angle between them — Sierra Leone SSS 2, L88, p.115 (a rhombus's sides are all equal, so one side length and an angle fix it).

**6. (Level 2)** Which quadrilateral needs only ONE pair of parallel sides in its givens?
a) square  b) trapezium  c) rhombus
**Answer:** b) trapezium — Sierra Leone SSS 2, L89, p.118.

**7. (Level 3)** The incentre is where a triangle's… meet.
a) perpendicular bisectors  b) angle bisectors  c) medians drawn by eye
**Answer:** b) angle bisectors — Pakistan, Grade 8, pp.161–170.

**8. (Level 3)** RHS fixes a right-angled triangle from…
a) its hypotenuse and one other side  b) its two acute angles  c) its perimeter
**Answer:** a) its hypotenuse and one other side — Pakistan, Grade 8, pp.161–170.
