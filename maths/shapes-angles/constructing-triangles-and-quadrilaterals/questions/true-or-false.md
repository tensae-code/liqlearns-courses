# Constructing triangles and quadrilaterals — true or false

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Constructing Triangles from Sides and Angles · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Constructing Quadrilaterals · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Constructing Right-Angled Triangles and Centres.

Say true or false, and say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Any three lengths can form a triangle.
**Answer:** False — the two shorter lengths together must pass the longest, or the arcs do not meet.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** An SAS triangle can be built with the given angle anywhere.
**Answer:** False — the angle must be the included angle, between the two given sides.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A rectangle's diagonals are equal.
**Answer:** True — a check on the Yemen Grade 4 rectangle construction (pp.100, 103).

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Every rhombus construction with a 90° angle gives a square.
**Answer:** True — equal sides plus a right angle is the square.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** The incentre and circumcentre are always the same point.
**Answer:** False — they coincide only in special triangles such as the equilateral; in general they are different points.
