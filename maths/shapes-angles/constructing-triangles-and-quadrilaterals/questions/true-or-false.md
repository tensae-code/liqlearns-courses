# Constructing triangles and quadrilaterals — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1. (Level 1)** Any three lengths can form a triangle.
**Answer:** False — the two shorter lengths together must pass the longest, or the arcs do not meet.

**2. (Level 1)** An SAS triangle can be built with the given angle anywhere.
**Answer:** False — the angle must be the included angle, between the two given sides.

**3. (Level 2)** A rectangle's diagonals are equal.
**Answer:** True — a check on the Yemen Grade 4 rectangle construction (pp.100, 103).

**4. (Level 2)** Every rhombus construction with a 90° angle gives a square.
**Answer:** True — equal sides plus a right angle is the square.

**5. (Level 3)** The incentre and circumcentre are always the same point.
**Answer:** False — they coincide only in special triangles such as the equilateral; in general they are different points.
