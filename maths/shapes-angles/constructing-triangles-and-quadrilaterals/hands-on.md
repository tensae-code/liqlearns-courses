# Hands-on — Constructing triangles and quadrilaterals

Real compass work, building whole figures.

## SSS triangle (Sierra Leone SSS 2, L84)
Construct a triangle with sides 6 cm, 5 cm and 4 cm: draw the 6 cm base, arc 5 cm from one end and 4 cm from the other, join the crossing point to both ends. Measure all three sides of your finished triangle to check.

## SAS in five steps (Yemen Grade 5, 8-3)
Construct a triangle with two sides 7 cm and 5 cm and the angle between them 60° (construct the 60°, do not measure it). Write your five steps down as you do them, the way Yemen's book steps the build.

## Rectangle from length and width (Yemen Grade 4)
Construct a rectangle 8 cm by 5 cm using perpendicular constructions for the corners — no set square. Check the diagonals: in a true rectangle they are equal.

## Two centres (Pakistan Grade 8)
On one large triangle, construct the incentre (bisect the three angles) and the circumcentre (bisect the three sides at right angles). Circle each point and label it. Say, in one sentence each, what each point is the centre of.
