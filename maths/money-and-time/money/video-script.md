# Video script — Money

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This is money. Every piece has a value.

Small pieces. Big pieces.
Line them up: small, big, bigger!

The small pieces make the big piece.
One hundred small ones can make one big one.
Two middle pieces can make one big piece too.

You want to buy a thing. The thing has a price.
Count your money. Is it enough?
More than the price — yes, you can buy.
Less than the price — no. Not yet.

Now pay! You give a big piece.
The price is small. Money comes back. That is **change**.

Count the change. Slowly.
Price plus change must equal what you paid.
Check it every time!

Now you. Price a thing. Pay for it. Count the change.
That is money.
