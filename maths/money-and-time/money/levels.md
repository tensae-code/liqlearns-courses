# Money — questions by level

Practice by level, tied to the skill number. The questions live in
the type files; this file is the by-level view. The levels are the
depths inside this one thing — money — knowing the pieces first,
then making and exchanging amounts, then paying, change and money
arithmetic, the order the books teach it in..


## Level 1 — knowing the pieces
Level 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: name the coins and notes of your own country, order them by value, and say how the small unit makes the big unit (for example 100 paisa = 1 taka, 100 santim = 1 birr).

## Level 2 — making and exchanging amounts
Level 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: make one amount in at least two different ways, exchange a big piece for smaller pieces of exactly the same value, total a set of notes piece by piece, and judge whether money in hand is enough for a price.

## Level 3 — paying, change and money arithmetic
Level 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: pay for one or two items, work out the change and check it (price + change = payment), and add and subtract money amounts including small-unit amounts written as decimals.
