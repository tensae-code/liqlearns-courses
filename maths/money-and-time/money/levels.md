# Money — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB
LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure — README, "Units, Lessons and Sub
Lessons"). Practice is tied to the skill number, as before. The
questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson
view. The sub lessons are the depths inside this one thing —
money — knowing the pieces first, then making and exchanging amounts, then paying, change and money arithmetic, the order the books teach it in..

Mapping from the old depth layers: old Level 1 = Sub lesson 1; old Level 2 = Sub lesson 2; old Level 3 = Sub lesson 3.

## Sub lesson 1 — Money — Knowing the Pieces
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: name the coins and notes of your own country, order them by value, and say how the small unit makes the big unit (for example 100 paisa = 1 taka, 100 santim = 1 birr).

## Sub lesson 2 — Money — Making and Exchanging Amounts
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: make one amount in at least two different ways, exchange a big piece for smaller pieces of exactly the same value, total a set of notes piece by piece, and judge whether money in hand is enough for a price.

## Sub lesson 3 — Money — Paying, Change and Money Arithmetic
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: pay for one or two items, work out the change and check it (price + change = payment), and add and subtract money amounts including small-unit amounts written as decimals.
