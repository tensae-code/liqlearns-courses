# Money — fill-in-the-blank

Fill in the missing number or word.

**1. (Level 1)** 100 paisa = ___ taka.
**Answer:** 1 — Bangladesh's Class 3 join.

**2. (Level 1)** In Nicaragua, 1 córdoba = ___ centavos.
**Answer:** 100 — Nicaragua's Grade 3 currency unit teaches this join.

**3. (Level 1)** In South Africa, R1,00 = ___ c.
**Answer:** 100 — South Africa's Grade 3 workbook gives the rand–cent join.

**4. (Level 2)** Two 50-riel notes = one ___-riel note.
**Answer:** 100 — Cambodia's Grade 1 money lesson starts exchanging here.

**5. (Level 2)** In Libya, 2 dinars 50 dirhams is written ___ (as a decimal of a dinar).
**Answer:** 2.050 — because 1 dinar = 1,000 dirhams, Libya's Grade 3 form.

**6. (Level 3)** Pay 5000 riel for a 3500-riel total: change = ___ riel.
**Answer:** 1,500 — payment minus price, Cambodia's Grade 4 pattern.

**7. (Level 3)** A target costs 240. Saving 30 a week reaches it in ___ weeks.
**Answer:** 8 — 240 ÷ 30 = 8, the saving pattern of Egypt's Grade 2 money chapter.
