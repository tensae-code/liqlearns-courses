# Money — matching

Match each item on the left to its partner on the right.

**1. (Level 1)** Match each country to its money: (a) Bangladesh (b) Ethiopia (c) Libya — taka, birr, dinar
**Answer:** a) taka, b) birr, c) dinar — the denominations each country's books teach first.

**2. (Level 1)** Match each join to its country: (a) 100 paisa = 1 unit (b) 10 jiao = 1 unit (c) 1,000 dirhams = 1 unit — Bangladesh, China, Libya
**Answer:** a) Bangladesh, b) China, c) Libya — the three joins are 100, 10 and 1,000.

**3. (Level 2)** Match each group to its total in riel: (a) 2 × 500 (b) 3 × 1000 + 4 × 500 (c) two 50s — 100, 1000, 5000
**Answer:** a) 1000, b) 5000, c) 100 — Cambodia's Grades 1–3 exchanging groups.

**4. (Level 2)** Match each Nepal amount to its name or value: (a) 50 paisa (b) 25 paisa (c) Rs 1 in paisa — Mohar, Suka, 100 paisa
**Answer:** a) Mohar, b) Suka, c) 100 paisa — Nepal's Grade 2 names and join.

**5. (Level 3)** Match each payment to its change: (a) pay R10 for R8 (b) pay 5000 riel for 3500 riel (c) pay Rs 100 for Rs 40 — R2, 1500 riel, Rs 60
**Answer:** a) R2, b) 1500 riel, c) Rs 60 — each is payment minus price.

**6. (Level 3)** Match each source to how it is worded: (a) Ghana JICA manuals (b) Sierra Leone Primary plans (c) Kuwait Grade 3 p.147 item — teacher evidence, teacher evidence (pre-redenomination, re-based), error-corrected (no correct option)
**Answer:** a) teacher evidence, b) teacher evidence (pre-redenomination, re-based), c) error-corrected (no correct option) — the lesson's three standing flags.
