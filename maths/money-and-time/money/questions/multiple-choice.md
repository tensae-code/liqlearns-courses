# Money — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Level 1)** In Bangladesh, how many paisa make 1 taka?
a) 10  b) 100  c) 1,000
**Answer:** b) 100 — Bangladesh's Class 3 book teaches 100 paisa = 1 taka.

**2. (Level 1)** In China, 1 yuan is how many jiao?
a) 10  b) 100  c) 5
**Answer:** a) 10 — China's Grade 1 RMB unit builds on tens: 1 yuan = 10 jiao, 1 jiao = 10 fen.

**3. (Level 1)** In Libya, 1 dinar is how many dirhams?
a) 100  b) 10  c) 1,000
**Answer:** c) 1,000 — Libya's Grade 2 and Grade 3 money units use 1 dinar = 1,000 dirhams.

**4. (Level 2)** Two 500-riel notes are worth the same as which note?
a) One 100-riel note  b) One 1000-riel note  c) One 5000-riel note
**Answer:** b) One 1000-riel note — Cambodia's Grade 2 book teaches two 500s = one 1000.

**5. (Level 2)** You total notes piece by piece: 3 × 1000 + 4 × 500. What is the total, in riel?
a) 4,500  b) 5,000  c) 7,000
**Answer:** b) 5,000 — 3,000 + 2,000 = 5,000, Cambodia's Grade 3 worked group.

**6. (Level 2)** Sara has 120 LE. She wants things costing 50 LE and 60 LE. Can she buy them?
a) Yes  b) No  c) Only one of them
**Answer:** a) Yes — 50 + 60 = 110 and 120 > 110, Egypt's Grade 2 comparison.

**7. (Level 3)** Bread costs R8. You pay with R10. How much change?
a) R1  b) R2  c) R18
**Answer:** b) R2 — 10 − 8 = 2, South Africa's Grade 1 workbook example.

**8. (Level 3)** You pay 5000 riel for things costing 2000 and 1500 riel. How much change?
a) 1,500 riel  b) 2,500 riel  c) 3,500 riel
**Answer:** a) 1,500 riel — 2000 + 1500 = 3500, and 5000 − 3500 = 1500, Cambodia's Grade 4 example.
