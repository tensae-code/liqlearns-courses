# Money — short answer

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Money — Knowing the Pieces · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Money — Making and Exchanging Amounts · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Money — Paying, Change and Money Arithmetic.

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Write the join for Ethiopia: how many santim make 1 birr?
**Answer:** 100 santim — Ethiopia's Grade 2 book teaches 100 santim = 1 birr.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A price tag in China reads 59.90 yuan. Say it in yuan and jiao.
**Answer:** 59 yuan 9 jiao — China's Grade 1 book reads decimal-style price tags this way.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** In Nepal, what are 50 paisa and 25 paisa called?
**Answer:** 50 paisa is a Mohar and 25 paisa is a Suka — Nepal's Grade 2 money lesson names them.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Make Rs 100 in Pakistan's Grade 1 way: give one way using only 50-rupee notes.
**Answer:** Two Rs 50 notes — Pakistan's Grade 1 book makes Rs 50 and Rs 100 from smaller denominations.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Total Ghana's teacher-evidence workbook set: 1 × GH¢5, 3 × GH¢20, 2 × GH¢50, 1 × GH¢100, 1 × GH¢200.
**Answer:** GH¢465 — 5 + 60 + 100 + 100 + 200 = 465, the workbook's worked total (teacher evidence).

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Exchange: how many 1-pound notes make a 20-pound note, in South Sudan's Primary 2 change work?
**Answer:** 20 — South Sudan's Primary 2 book gives change by counting how many small notes fill a big one.

**7. (Sub lesson 3)** Add: Rs 52.25 + Rs 50.50.
**Answer:** Rs 102.75 — Nepal's Grade 3 book adds rupees and paisa in decimal form.

**8. (Sub lesson 3)** Multiply: Rs 10.25 × 4.
**Answer:** Rs 41 — Nepal's Grade 4 book multiplies rupees and paisa the same way.

**9. (Sub lesson 3)** You spend Rs 40 of Rs 100 on a copy book. How much change, as in Nepal's Grade 2 example?
**Answer:** Rs 60 — 100 − 40 = 60.
