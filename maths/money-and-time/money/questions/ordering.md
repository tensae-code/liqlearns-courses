# Money — ordering

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Money — Knowing the Pieces · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Money — Making and Exchanging Amounts · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Money — Paying, Change and Money Arithmetic.

Put the items in the order asked. Check each step before you write the answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Put Egypt's notes in value order, smallest first: 200, 5, 50, 1, 100, 20, 10 LE.
**Answer:** 1, 5, 10, 20, 50, 100, 200 LE — the note set of Egypt's Grade 2 money chapter.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Put South Sudan's notes in value order, smallest first: 100, 5, 50, 1, 20, 10 SSP.
**Answer:** 1, 5, 10, 20, 50, 100 SSP — the Bank of South Sudan notes in Primary 1.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Put these riel notes in value order, smallest first: 1000, 100, 5000, 500, 200.
**Answer:** 100, 200, 500, 1000, 5000 riel — Cambodia's Grades 2–3 note ladder.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Put these amounts in order, smallest first: 2.050 dinars, 50 dirhams, 1 dinar, 1,000 dirhams.
**Answer:** 50 dirhams, 1 dinar, 1,000 dirhams (= 1 dinar, equal), then 2.050 dinars — with Libya's join, 1,000 dirhams is exactly 1 dinar, so the middle two tie.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Put these prices in order, smallest first: 724, 28, 206, 39, 149, 126 LE.
**Answer:** 28, 39, 126, 149, 206, 724 LE — the priced items of Egypt's Grade 2 money chapter.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** Put the steps of paying in order: (a) check the change (b) say the total price (c) pay with a bigger piece (d) count the change back.
**Answer:** b, c, d, a — total first, then payment, then change, then the check price + change = payment.
