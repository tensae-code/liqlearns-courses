# Video script — Speed, Distance and Time

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Walk 120 steps in 3 minutes. How FAST are you walking?
Divide! 120 ÷ 3 = 40 steps a minute. That divide is **speed**.

Speed is distance ÷ time. Cover one corner of the triangle —
distance on top, speed and time below — and the triangle tells you the rest.

Out slow, back fast? Your **average** speed is ALL the distance ÷ ALL the time.
Never just add the two speeds and halve them!

Two walkers, one road, walking TOWARDS each other.
The gap closes at BOTH speeds added. That is a **meeting** problem.

And the same divide works everywhere: heavy for its size is **density**;
work done in an hour is a **work rate**.

Now you. Time your walk. Divide. That is your speed.
