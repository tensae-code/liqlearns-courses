# Speed, Distance and Time — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Level 1)** A ship travels 120 km in 3 h (Cambodia, Grade 6). Its speed is:
a) 30 km/h  b) 40 km/h  c) 360 km/h
**Answer:** b) 40 km/h — speed = distance ÷ time = 120 ÷ 3

**2. (Level 1)** At 70 km/h, the time for 147 km (Cambodia, Grade 6) is:
a) 2.1 h  b) 0.21 h  c) 21 h
**Answer:** a) 2.1 h — time = distance ÷ speed = 147 ÷ 70

**3. (Level 1)** At 90 km/h for 1.5 h, the distance covered is:
a) 60 km  b) 135 km  c) 900 km
**Answer:** b) 135 km — distance = speed × time = 90 × 1.5

**4. (Level 1)** 54 km/h in m/s (South Sudan, Primary 7) is:
a) 15 m/s  b) 54 m/s  c) 194.4 m/s
**Answer:** a) 15 m/s — km/h → m/s multiplies by 5/18: 54 × 5/18 = 15

**5. (Level 2)** Out at 100 km/h and back at 90 km/h over the same road, the average speed is:
a) exactly 95 km/h  b) less than 95 km/h  c) more than 95 km/h
**Answer:** b) less than 95 km/h — average = total distance ÷ total time; the slower leg takes longer, so it weighs more (Kazakhstan, Grade 6, §47)

**6. (Level 2)** On a distance–time graph, a flat (horizontal) part means:
a) moving slowly  b) stopped  c) moving backwards
**Answer:** b) stopped — the distance is not changing at all

**7. (Level 2)** A car's return leg runs from (0, 200) to (2.5, 0) on a distance–time graph (Egypt, Grade 8). Its return speed is:
a) 80 km/h  b) 200 km/h  c) 500 km/h
**Answer:** a) 80 km/h — 200 km in 2.5 h: 200 ÷ 2.5 = 80

**8. (Level 2)** Two walkers approach each other at 70 m/min and 50 m/min. The gap closes at:
a) 20 m/min  b) 60 m/min  c) 120 m/min
**Answer:** c) 120 m/min — meeting motion adds the speeds (Kazakhstan, Grade 4 Pt2)

**9. (Level 3)** Gold's density is 19.3 g/cm³ (Cambodia, Grade 8). The mass of 5 cm³ is:
a) 3.86 g  b) 24.3 g  c) 96.5 g
**Answer:** c) 96.5 g — mass = density × volume = 19.3 × 5

**10. (Level 3)** One brigade paves a road in 6 days, another in 3 days. Together they take:
a) 9 days  b) 2 days  c) 4.5 days
**Answer:** b) 2 days — rates add: 1/6 + 1/3 = 1/2 of the road a day (Kazakhstan joint work)
