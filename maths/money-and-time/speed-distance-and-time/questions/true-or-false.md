# Speed, Distance and Time — true or false

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Speed, Distance and Time — the Triangle and Its Units · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Speed, Distance and Time — Journeys: Average Speed, Graphs and Meeting Problems · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Speed, Distance and Time — Density and Work Rates.

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** 15 m/s is the same speed as 54 km/h.
**Answer:** True — South Sudan Primary 8, §2.6: 15 × 18/5 = 54

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** South Sudan's Primary 8 guide key can be used to check Exercise 10.
**Answer:** False — its key is wrong in 6 of 10 — this lesson cites the student book only

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The average of 100 km/h and 90 km/h is always the average speed of the round trip.
**Answer:** False — average speed is total distance ÷ total time; the slower leg takes longer

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A steeper distance–time slope means a slower speed.
**Answer:** False — steeper means MORE distance per hour — faster

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** In opposite-direction motion the gap grows at the sum of the speeds.
**Answer:** True — Kazakhstan Grade 4 Pt2, Lessons 74–76

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** Cambodia Grade 8 prints d² = (2×10⁻³ m)² = 4×10⁻⁶ m².
**Answer:** False — that is the CORRECTED form taught here; the book's printed line drops the brackets and the ×10⁻³ step

**7. (Sub lesson 3)** Population density is worked by the same divide as material density.
**Answer:** True — people ÷ area, like mass ÷ volume (Sierra Leone SSS 3, L58)

**8. (Sub lesson 3)** If one machine does a job in 5 days, two such machines do it in 10 days.
**Answer:** False — their rates add: the pair does it in 2.5 days
