# Speed, Distance and Time — short answer

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Level 1)** A cyclist rides at 80 m/min (Yemen, Grade 6). How far in 10 minutes?
**Answer:** 800 m — distance = speed × time.

**2. (Level 1)** Change 18 km/h to m/s (Cambodia, Grade 7).
**Answer:** 5 m/s — 18 × 5/18.

**3. (Level 1)** Change 20 m/s to km/h.
**Answer:** 72 km/h — 20 × 18/5.

**4. (Level 2)** A journey covers 240 km in 4 hours with stops. What is the average speed?
**Answer:** 60 km/h — total distance ÷ total time, stops included.

**5. (Level 2)** On a travel graph, which is faster: a slope rising 100 km in 2 h, or one rising 100 km in 4 h?
**Answer:** The 2-hour slope — 50 km/h against 25 km/h; the steeper slope is faster.

**6. (Level 2)** Two towns are 240 km apart; cars approach at 70 km/h and 50 km/h. When do they meet?
**Answer:** After 2 hours — the gap closes at 120 km/h; 240 ÷ 120 = 2.

**7. (Level 2)** A chaser rides at 60 km/h behind a rider at 45 km/h, 30 km ahead. How long to catch up?
**Answer:** 2 hours — the gap closes at the difference, 15 km/h; 30 ÷ 15 = 2.

**8. (Level 3)** Iron's density is 7.874 g/cm³. Find the volume of 78.74 g of iron.
**Answer:** 10 cm³ — volume = mass ÷ density.

**9. (Level 3)** Paper is 80 g/m². Find the mass of 10 m² of it.
**Answer:** 800 g — mass per area × area.

**10. (Level 3)** A worker does a job in 5 hours alone. What is their work rate, and what does it mean?
**Answer:** 1/5 of the job per hour — rate = 1 ÷ solo time (Kazakhstan, Grade 5, §3.15).
