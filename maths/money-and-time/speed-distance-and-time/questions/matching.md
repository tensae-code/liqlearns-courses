# Speed, Distance and Time — matching

Match each item on the left to its partner on the right.

**1. (Level 1)** Match each traveller to its speed (Yemen and Cambodia lists): (a) cyclist (b) car (c) plane — 750 km/h; 80 m/min; 90 km/h
**Answer:** a) 80 m/min, b) 90 km/h, c) 750 km/h.

**2. (Level 1)** Match each unknown to its triangle move: (a) speed (b) distance (c) time — speed × time; distance ÷ time; distance ÷ speed
**Answer:** a) distance ÷ time, b) speed × time, c) distance ÷ speed.

**3. (Level 2)** Match each graph part to its meaning: (a) rising slope (b) flat part (c) falling slope — returning; stopped; moving away
**Answer:** a) moving away, b) stopped, c) returning.

**4. (Level 3)** Match each rate to its formula: (a) density (b) work rate (c) population density — people ÷ area; mass ÷ volume; 1 ÷ solo time
**Answer:** a) mass ÷ volume, b) 1 ÷ solo time, c) people ÷ area.

**5. (Level 3)** Match each density to its metal (Cambodia, Grade 8): (a) 19.3 (b) 7.874 (c) 10.49 g/cm³ — silver; gold; iron
**Answer:** a) gold, b) iron, c) silver.
