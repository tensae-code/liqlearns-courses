# Speed, Distance and Time — fill-in-the-blank

Fill in the missing number or word.

**1. (Level 1)** Speed = distance ÷ ___.
**Answer:** time

**2. (Level 1)** Distance = speed × ___.
**Answer:** time

**3. (Level 1)** To change km/h to m/s, multiply by ___/18.
**Answer:** 5 — South Sudan, Primary 7, §2.4

**4. (Level 2)** Average speed = total ___ ÷ total time.
**Answer:** distance — Kazakhstan, Grade 6, §8.1

**5. (Level 2)** The speed of sound, Cambodia Grade 8's list, is ___ m/s.
**Answer:** 343

**6. (Level 2)** In a chase, the gap closes at the ___ of the two speeds.
**Answer:** difference

**7. (Level 3)** Density = mass ÷ ___.
**Answer:** volume

**8. (Level 3)** Two workers together ___ their rates; they never add their times.
**Answer:** add
