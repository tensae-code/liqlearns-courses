# Speed, Distance and Time — ordering

Put the items in the order asked. Check each step before you write the answer.

**1. (Level 1)** Put these speeds in order, slowest first: 80 km/h (car), 40 km/h (motorbike), 800 km/h (plane), 90 km/h (Libya's Tripoli–Ghadames trip).
Answer in order, smallest first.
**Answer:** 40 km/h (motorbike), 80 km/h (car), 90 km/h (Tripoli–Ghadames trip), 800 km/h (plane) — Cambodia Grade 8's real-world list, with Libya's trip speed placed

**2. (Level 1)** Put these in order as m/s values, smallest first: 54 km/h; 18 km/h; 36 km/h.
Answer in order, smallest first.
**Answer:** 18 km/h (5 m/s), 36 km/h (10 m/s), 54 km/h (15 m/s) — each divides by 3.6 — the ×5/18 conversion

**3. (Level 2)** Put a return journey's graph parts in time order: the flat stop at the far end; the outward slope; the return slope.
Answer in order, smallest first.
**Answer:** the outward slope, the flat stop at the far end, the return slope — Laos Grade 5's graph reading: away, stopped, back

**4. (Level 3)** Put these densities in order, lightest first: gold 19.3, iron 7.874, silver 10.49, copper 8.94 g/cm³.
Answer in order, smallest first.
**Answer:** iron 7.874, copper 8.94, silver 10.49, gold 19.3 g/cm³ — Cambodia Grade 8's density table

**5. (Level 3)** Put these joint jobs in order of finishing time, quickest first: one worker alone (5 h); two such workers together; three such workers together.
Answer in order, smallest first.
**Answer:** three together, two together, one alone — rates 3/5, 2/5 and 1/5 of the job per hour — more hands, higher rate, less time
