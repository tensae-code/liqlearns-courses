# Video script — Time and calendars

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Look at the clock. Two hands.

The **short** hand says the hour.
The **long** hand counts minutes — five, ten, fifteen…

Quarter past. Half past. Quarter to.
Morning is **a.m.** Afternoon is **p.m.**

Now the long count. The day has twenty-four hours.
One o'clock in the day is **13:00**.
Eight fifteen at night is **20:15**.
The end of the day is **24:00**.

How long did it take? Start time. End time.
Count the time between. That is the duration.

Sixty minutes make one hour.
Sixty seconds make one minute.
Seven days make one week.

And the year? Every people counts it their own way —
Gregorian, Hijri, Ethiopian, and more.
Same sun. Different calendars.

Now you. Read a clock. Say it both ways.
That is time.
