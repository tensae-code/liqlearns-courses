# Time and calendars — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB
LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure — README, "Units, Lessons and Sub
Lessons"). Practice is tied to the skill number, as before. The
questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson
view. The sub lessons are the depths inside this one thing —
time — reading clocks first, then the two clock systems and durations, then time arithmetic and the calendars of the world, the order the books teach it in..

Mapping from the old depth layers: old Level 1 = Sub lesson 1; old Level 2 = Sub lesson 2; old Level 3 = Sub lesson 3.

## Sub lesson 1 — Time and Calendars — Reading Clocks and the Parts of the Day
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: read analogue and digital clocks to the minute, say times as quarter past / half past / quarter to, use a.m. and p.m. correctly, and convert the time units (hour, minute, second, day, week).

## Sub lesson 2 — Time and Calendars — 12 Hours, 24 Hours and Durations
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: change between 12-hour and 24-hour time both ways (including 0:35 for 12:35 a.m. and 24:00 for the end of the day), and work out durations from start and end times, including across noon on a timeline.

## Sub lesson 3 — Time and Calendars — Time Arithmetic and the Calendars of the World
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: add and subtract compound times with the 60-carry and 60-borrow (including the corrected 6h 15m + 3h 10m = 9h 25m), and read a calendar page — naming its system (Gregorian, Hijri, Ethiopian, Bikram Sambat, Bangla, Khmer), its months, and whether its year is a leap year.
