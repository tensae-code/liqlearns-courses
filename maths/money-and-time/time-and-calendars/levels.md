# Time and calendars — questions by level

Practice by level, tied to the skill number. The questions live in
the type files; this file is the by-level view. The levels are the
depths inside this one thing — time — reading clocks first, then
the two clock systems and durations, then time arithmetic and the
calendars of the world, the order the books teach it in..


## Level 1 — reading clocks and the parts of the day
Level 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: read analogue and digital clocks to the minute, say times as quarter past / half past / quarter to, use a.m. and p.m. correctly, and convert the time units (hour, minute, second, day, week).

## Level 2 — 12 hours, 24 hours and durations
Level 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: change between 12-hour and 24-hour time both ways (including 0:35 for 12:35 a.m. and 24:00 for the end of the day), and work out durations from start and end times, including across noon on a timeline.

## Level 3 — time arithmetic and the calendars of the world
Level 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: add and subtract compound times with the 60-carry and 60-borrow (including the corrected 6h 15m + 3h 10m = 9h 25m), and read a calendar page — naming its system (Gregorian, Hijri, Ethiopian, Bikram Sambat, Bangla, Khmer), its months, and whether its year is a leap year.
