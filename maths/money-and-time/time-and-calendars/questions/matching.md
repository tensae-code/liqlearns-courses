# Time and calendars — matching

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Time and Calendars — Reading Clocks and the Parts of the Day · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Time and Calendars — 12 Hours, 24 Hours and Durations · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Time and Calendars — Time Arithmetic and the Calendars of the World.

Match each item on the left to its partner on the right.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Match each hand to its job: (a) short hand (b) long hand (c) second hand — minutes, hours, seconds
**Answer:** a) hours, b) minutes, c) seconds — Cambodia's Grade 2 book adds the second hand to the two main hands.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Match each word form to its figures: (a) quarter past two (b) half past two (c) quarter to three — 2:15, 2:30, 2:45
**Answer:** a) 2:15, b) 2:30, c) 2:45 — the quarter and half forms of Egypt's and Nepal's Grade 2 books.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Match each 12-hour time to its 24-hour form: (a) 1:00 p.m. (b) 8:15 p.m. (c) 12:35 a.m. — 13:00, 20:15, 0:35
**Answer:** a) 13:00, b) 20:15, c) 0:35 — Cambodia's and South Sudan's worked conversions.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Match each calendar system to its filed fact: (a) Hijri (b) Ethiopian (c) Bikram Sambat — Ramazan is the 9th month, festivals in Meskerem, first month Baisakh
**Answer:** a) Ramazan is the 9th month, b) festivals in Meskerem, c) first month Baisakh — Pakistan's, Ethiopia's and Nepal's books respectively.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Match each system to the book that teaches it side by side with another: (a) Yemen Grade 4 (b) Kuwait Grade 1 (c) Bangladesh Class 2 — Hijri with Gregorian, Gregorian with Hijri months, Bengali with English months
**Answer:** a) Hijri with Gregorian, b) Gregorian with Hijri months, c) Bengali with English months — the three side-by-side calendar teachings as filed.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** Match each item to how it is worded: (a) Cambodia's end-of-day clock (b) Nepal's 6h 15m + 3h 10m (c) Ghana's time rows — error-corrected (24:00), error-corrected (9h 25m), teacher evidence
**Answer:** a) error-corrected (24:00), b) error-corrected (9h 25m), c) teacher evidence — the lesson's three standing flags for this topic.
