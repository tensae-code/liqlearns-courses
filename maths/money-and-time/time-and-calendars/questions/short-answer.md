# Time and calendars — short answer

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Level 1)** Read this clock in figures and words: long hand at 12, short hand just past 6.
**Answer:** 6:00, six o'clock — Cambodia's Grade 1 book starts reading at o'clock times like 6:00 and 7:00.

**2. (Level 1)** How many hours are in 1 day, and how many days in 1 week?
**Answer:** 24 hours and 7 days — South Sudan's Primary 3 unit chain.

**3. (Level 2)** Change 14:55 to 12-hour time.
**Answer:** 2:55 p.m. — subtract 12 and add p.m., South Sudan's Primary 4 worked form.

**4. (Level 2)** Change 4:45 p.m. to 24-hour time.
**Answer:** 16:45 — add 12 to the p.m. hour, South Sudan's Primary 4 worked form.

**5. (Level 2)** A thing starts at 2:05 and lasts 1 h 15 min. When does it end, as in Nicaragua's Grade 2 book?
**Answer:** 3:20 — 2:05 + 1 hour = 3:05, + 15 minutes = 3:20.

**6. (Level 3)** Add: 4h 47m 32s + 3h 50m 45s.
**Answer:** 8h 38m 17s — seconds 32 + 45 = 77 = 1m 17s, minutes 47 + 50 + 1 = 98 = 1h 38m, hours 4 + 3 + 1 = 8, Cambodia's Grade 6 sum.

**7. (Level 3)** Convert 2 hours into minutes and into seconds.
**Answer:** 120 minutes and 7,200 seconds — South Sudan's Primary 5 conversion.

**8. (Level 3)** Name the twelve Bikram Sambat months in order, as in Nepal's Grade 1 book.
**Answer:** Baisakh, Jestha, Ashad, Shrawan, Bhadra, Ashwin, Kartik, Mangsir, Poush, Magh, Falgun, Chaitra — Nepal's Lesson 45 list.

**9. (Level 3)** November 2026: on which weekday does 1 November fall, using the corrected note on Kuwait's printed page?
**Answer:** Sunday — Kuwait's p.76 November page is the 2025 layout (1 November on a Saturday); for 2026 it starts on a Sunday, taught corrected.
