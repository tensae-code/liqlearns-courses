# Time and calendars — fill-in-the-blank

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Time and Calendars — Reading Clocks and the Parts of the Day · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Time and Calendars — 12 Hours, 24 Hours and Durations · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Time and Calendars — Time Arithmetic and the Calendars of the World.

Fill in the missing number or word.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** 1 minute = ___ seconds.
**Answer:** 60 — the base of the time unit chain.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Before noon is ___; after noon is ___.
**Answer:** a.m.; p.m. — Egypt's Grade 2 new series reads the labels off a day timeline.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** 1:00 in the day is ___:00 in 24-hour time.
**Answer:** 13 — Cambodia's Grade 5 conversion, 1 + 12 = 13.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The end of the day, taught corrected, is ___:00.
**Answer:** 24 — Cambodia's Grade 5 book prints 12:00 there; the correct end-of-day form is 24:00.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** 63 days = ___ weeks.
**Answer:** 9 — 63 ÷ 7 = 9, South Sudan's Primary 3 conversion.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** A common year has ___ days; a leap year has ___.
**Answer:** 365; 366 — Cambodia's Grade 2 calendar teaching.

**7. (Sub lesson 3)** 400 days = 1 year + 1 month + ___ days, in Ethiopia's Grade 3 book.
**Answer:** 5 — Ethiopia's Grade 3 unit-relation worked form (p.191).
