# Time and calendars — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Time and Calendars — Reading Clocks and the Parts of the Day · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Time and Calendars — 12 Hours, 24 Hours and Durations · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Time and Calendars — Time Arithmetic and the Calendars of the World.

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The long hand of a clock points at 2. How many minutes past the hour is it?
a) 2 minutes  b) 10 minutes  c) 20 minutes
**Answer:** b) 10 minutes — the long hand counts in 5s, and past 2 is 10, the reading method of Cambodia's Grade 3 book.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What does 9:45 say in words, as in South Africa's Grade 3 workbook?
a) Quarter past nine  b) Half past nine  c) Quarter to ten
**Answer:** c) Quarter to ten — 45 minutes is 15 minutes before the next hour.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** How many minutes are in 1 hour?
a) 60  b) 100  c) 24
**Answer:** a) 60 — 1 hour = 60 minutes, stated in Cambodia's Grade 3 and Nepal's Grade 2 books.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Change 8:15 at night to 24-hour time, as in Cambodia's Grade 5 book.
a) 08:15  b) 18:15  c) 20:15
**Answer:** c) 20:15 — add 12 to a p.m. hour: 8 + 12 = 20.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** In 24-hour time, what is 12:35 a.m., as in South Sudan's Primary 4 book?
a) 12:35  b) 0:35  c) 13:35
**Answer:** b) 0:35 — 12 a.m. is the start of the day, hour 0, South Sudan's worked form.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** A motocross runs from 9:40 a.m. to 11:30 a.m. (Nicaragua, Grade 3). How long did it last?
a) 1 h 50 min  b) 2 h 10 min  c) 1 h 30 min
**Answer:** a) 1 h 50 min — 20 minutes to 10:00, then 1 h 30 min more.

**7. (Sub lesson 3)** Add, taught corrected: 6h 15m + 3h 10m.
a) 3h 5m  b) 9h 25m  c) 9h 5m
**Answer:** b) 9h 25m — hours 6 + 3 = 9, minutes 15 + 10 = 25; Nepal's printed "3h 5m" is an error and is never taught.

**8. (Sub lesson 3)** Which month is Ramazan (Ramadan) in the Islamic calendar, as in Pakistan's Grade 1 book?
a) The 1st  b) The 9th  c) The 12th
**Answer:** b) The 9th — Pakistan's Grade 1 book names Ramazan the 9th of the 12 Islamic months.
