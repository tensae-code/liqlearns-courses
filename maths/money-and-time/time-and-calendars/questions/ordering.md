# Time and calendars — ordering

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Time and Calendars — Reading Clocks and the Parts of the Day · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Time and Calendars — 12 Hours, 24 Hours and Durations · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Time and Calendars — Time Arithmetic and the Calendars of the World.

Put the items in the order asked. Check each step before you write the answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Put the parts of the day in order: night, morning, evening, midday.
**Answer:** Morning, midday, evening, night — the day order of Cambodia's Grade 1 and Yemen's Grade 1 books.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Put these clock times in order, earliest first: 11:45, 6:10, 10:38, 8:07.
**Answer:** 6:10, 8:07, 10:38, 11:45 — the to-the-minute readings of Cambodia's Grade 3 book, sorted.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Put these 24-hour times in order, earliest first: 20:15, 0:35, 13:00, 12:00.
**Answer:** 0:35, 12:00, 13:00, 20:15 — the day runs 00:00 to 24:00 in Cambodia's Grade 5 timeline.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Put the steps of a timeline duration in order, for 9:40 a.m. to 11:30 a.m.: (a) add the last part, 1 h 30 min (b) fill to the next whole hour, 20 min to 10:00 (c) say the total, 1 h 50 min.
**Answer:** b, a, c — Jordan's timeline method: whole hour first, then the rest, then the total.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Put the twelve Bikram Sambat months in order from the start: Chaitra, Baisakh, Kartik, Jestha, Poush, Ashad.
**Answer:** Baisakh, Jestha, Ashad, Kartik, Poush, Chaitra — their places in Nepal's Grade 1 list are 1st, 2nd, 3rd, 7th, 9th and 12th.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** Put these spans in order, shortest first: century, day, millennium, week, year, decade.
**Answer:** Day, week, year, decade, century, millennium — the long spans of Greece's Grade 4 and Bangladesh's Class 5 calendar work.
