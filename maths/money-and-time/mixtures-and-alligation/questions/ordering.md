1. (Sub lesson 3) Put the steps for splitting a 10 kg total into both quantities in the right order:
   (a) Split the 10 kg in the balancing ratio (b) Compute the two price gaps from the mixture price (c) Check by rebuilding the total value (d) Write the ratio cheaper : dearer as (dearer − mixture) : (mixture − cheaper)
   **Answer: b, d, a, c.** Rwanda, Primary 6 Mathematics, Learner's Book, Unit 6, printed pp. 115–116.
