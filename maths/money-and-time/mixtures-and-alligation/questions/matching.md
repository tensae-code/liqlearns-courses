1. (Sub lesson 2) Match each mixture to its balancing ratio (cheaper ingredient : dearer ingredient):
   (a) Prices 200 and 500, mixture 350 (b) Prices 300 and 600, mixture 400 (c) Prices 250 and 400, mixture 310 (d) Prices 200 and 400, mixture 300
   Ratios: 2:1, 1:1, 3:2, 1:1.
   **Answer: (a) 1:1 (gaps 150 and 150), (b) 2:1 (gaps 200 and 100), (c) 3:2 (gaps 90 and 60), (d) 1:1 (gaps 100 and 100).** Rwanda, Primary 6 Mathematics, Learner's Book, Unit 6, printed pp. 112–116.
