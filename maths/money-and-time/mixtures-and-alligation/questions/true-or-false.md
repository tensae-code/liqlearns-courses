# True or false — Mixtures and Alligation

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Layer 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Mixtures and Alligation — the Average Price · (Layer 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Mixtures and Alligation — How Much of Each: the Balance · (Layer 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Mixtures and Alligation — Working Backwards.

1. (Sub lesson 1) The average price of a mixture can be higher than both ingredient prices. **False** — it always lies between them. Rwanda, Primary 6 Mathematics, Learner's Book, Unit 6, printed pp. 111–112.
2. (Sub lesson 2) If the two price gaps from the mixture price are equal, the mixture contains equal quantities of the two ingredients. **True.** Rwanda, Primary 6 Mathematics, Learner's Book, Unit 6, printed pp. 112–114.
3. (Sub lesson 2) The ingredient whose price is further from the mixture price is the one there is more of. **False** — the bigger gap takes the smaller quantity; quantities follow the opposite gaps. Rwanda, Primary 6 Mathematics, Learner's Book, Unit 6, printed pp. 112–114.
