# Multiple choice — Mixtures and Alligation

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Layer 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Mixtures and Alligation — the Average Price · (Layer 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Mixtures and Alligation — How Much of Each: the Balance · (Layer 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Mixtures and Alligation — Working Backwards.

1. (Sub lesson 1) 3 kg at 300 Frw/kg are mixed with 2 kg at 450 Frw/kg. The average price is:
   A. 375 Frw/kg B. 360 Frw/kg C. 390 Frw/kg D. 350 Frw/kg
   **Answer: B** — (900 + 900) / 5 = 360. Rwanda, Primary 6 Mathematics, Learner's Book, Unit 6, printed pp. 111–112.

2. (Sub lesson 1) A mixture's average price must always be:
   A. Equal to the dearer price B. Equal to the cheaper price C. Between the two ingredient prices D. Above both prices
   **Answer: C** — Rwanda, Primary 6 Mathematics, Learner's Book, Unit 6, printed pp. 111–112.

3. (Sub lesson 2) Ingredients at 200 Frw/kg and 500 Frw/kg are mixed to a price of 275 Frw/kg. The ratio cheaper : dearer is:
   A. 1:3 B. 3:1 C. 1:1 D. 2:1
   **Answer: B** — gaps are 500 − 275 = 225 and 275 − 200 = 75, ratio 225:75 = 3:1. Rwanda, Primary 6 Mathematics, Learner's Book, Unit 6, printed pp. 112–114.

4. (Sub lesson 3) A 5 kg mixture priced at 380 Frw/kg contains 2 kg at 500 Frw/kg. The price of the other ingredient is:
   A. 260 Frw/kg B. 300 Frw/kg C. 340 Frw/kg D. 380 Frw/kg
   **Answer: B** — total value 1,900; known part 1,000; remaining 900 over 3 kg = 300. Rwanda, Primary 6 Mathematics, Learner's Book, Unit 6, printed pp. 114–115.
