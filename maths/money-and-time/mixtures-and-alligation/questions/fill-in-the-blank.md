# Fill in the blank — Mixtures and Alligation

1. (Layer 1) The average price of a mixture is the total cost divided by the total ___.
   **Answer: quantity (mass).** Rwanda, Primary 6 Mathematics, Learner's Book, Unit 6, printed pp. 111–112.

2. (Layer 1) 1 kg at 200 Frw/kg mixed with 1 kg at 400 Frw/kg has an average price of ___ Frw/kg.
   **Answer: 300.** Rwanda, Primary 6 Mathematics, Learner's Book, Unit 6, printed pp. 111–112.

3. (Layer 2) In the balancing method, the quantity of an ingredient is proportional to the ___ ingredient's price gap from the mixture price.
   **Answer: other (opposite).** Rwanda, Primary 6 Mathematics, Learner's Book, Unit 6, printed pp. 112–114.
