# Short answer — Mixtures and Alligation

1. (Layer 1) Find the average price of 4 kg at 250 Frw/kg mixed with 6 kg at 400 Frw/kg.
   **Answer: (1,000 + 2,400) / 10 = 340 Frw/kg.** Rwanda, Primary 6 Mathematics, Learner's Book, Unit 6, printed pp. 111–112.

2. (Layer 2) Ingredients cost 200 Frw/kg and 450 Frw/kg. In what ratio (cheaper : dearer) must they be mixed for a mixture price of 300 Frw/kg?
   **Answer: Gaps 450 − 300 = 150 and 300 − 200 = 100, so 150:100 = 3:2.** Rwanda, Primary 6 Mathematics, Learner's Book, Unit 6, printed pp. 112–114.

3. (Layer 3) A 15 kg mixture priced at 340 Frw/kg is made from ingredients at 300 Frw/kg and 420 Frw/kg. Find both quantities.
   **Answer: Gaps 420 − 340 = 80 and 340 − 300 = 40, ratio 2:1; splitting 15 kg in 2:1 gives 10 kg of the cheaper ingredient and 5 kg of the dearer. Check: 10 × 300 + 5 × 420 = 3,000 + 2,100 = 5,100 = 15 × 340.** Rwanda, Primary 6 Mathematics, Learner's Book, Unit 6, printed pp. 115–116.
