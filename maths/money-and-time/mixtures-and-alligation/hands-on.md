# Hands-on — Mixtures and Alligation

## Activity 1 — Mix the counters (10 minutes)

Use two colours of counters as two "ingredients" priced at 200 Frw and 500 Frw. Mix 4 of the first with 6 of the second; the learner computes the total value (4 × 200 + 6 × 500 = 3,800 Frw) and the average price per counter (380 Frw), then checks the answer lies between 200 and 500 — it must.

## Activity 2 — Balance the scale (15 minutes)

Draw a balance for a mixture priced at 350 Frw from ingredients at 200 Frw and 500 Frw. The learner writes the two gaps (150 and 150), states the balancing ratio (1:1), and explains in one sentence why equal gaps mean equal quantities: the gain per kilo on one side must exactly cancel the loss per kilo on the other, so the kilos must match.

## Activity 3 — Split the sack (10 minutes)

A 10 kg mixture of ingredients at 250 Frw/kg and 400 Frw/kg sells at 310 Frw/kg. The learner finds the gaps (90 and 60), writes the ratio (3:2, cheaper ingredient first — the bigger gap takes the smaller share), splits the 10 kg into 6 kg and 4 kg, and verifies: 6 × 250 + 4 × 400 = 3,100 Frw = 10 × 310.
