# Levels — Mixtures and Alligation

One lesson, one level (Level 146). The depth layers inside it are ranks.

## Depth layers

- **Layer 1 — Average price.** Compute the average (mixture) price of a two-ingredient mixture from the quantities and prices of both ingredients. Pass mark: 4 of 5.
- **Layer 2 — The balance.** Use profit/loss balancing to find the ratio, or one quantity, of the ingredients in a mixture of a given price — quantities are in the inverse ratio of the price gaps. Pass mark: 5 of 6.
- **Layer 3 — Working backwards.** Find the price of an unknown ingredient, and split a total mass into both quantities from the mixture price, inside mixed problems. Pass mark: 4 of 5.

## What "maxed" means here

The learner can take any two-ingredient mixture problem Rwanda's Primary 6 book sets — forwards to the mixture price, sideways to a quantity, or backwards to an ingredient price or a full split — set up the balance or the total-value equation, and land the answer, checking it by rebuilding the mixture price.
