## Sub lesson 1 — Mixtures and Alligation — the Average Price
Compute the average (mixture) price of a two-ingredient mixture from the quantities and prices of both ingredients. Pass mark: 4 of 5.

## Sub lesson 2 — Mixtures and Alligation — How Much of Each: the Balance
Use profit/loss balancing to find the ratio, or one quantity, of the ingredients in a mixture of a given price — quantities are in the inverse ratio of the price gaps. Pass mark: 5 of 6.

## Sub lesson 3 — Mixtures and Alligation — Working Backwards
Find the price of an unknown ingredient, and split a total mass into both quantities from the mixture price, inside mixed problems. Pass mark: 4 of 5.

## What "maxed" means here

The learner can take any two-ingredient mixture problem Rwanda's Primary 6 book sets — forwards to the mixture price, sideways to a quantity, or backwards to an ingredient price or a full split — set up the balance or the total-value equation, and land the answer, checking it by rebuilding the mixture price.
