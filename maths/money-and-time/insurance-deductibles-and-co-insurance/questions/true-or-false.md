# True or false — Insurance: Deductibles and Co-insurance

1. (Layer 1) A deductible is paid by the insurer. **False** — the insured pays it; the insurer pays the rest. Malaysia, Mathematics Form 5, Ch. 3 §3.1, printed pp. 86–87.
2. (Layer 2) Insuring at or above the required percentage removes the co-insurance scaling. **True.** Malaysia, Mathematics Form 5, Ch. 3 §3.1, printed pp. 87–88.
3. (Layer 3) Under-insuring saves money overall whenever a large loss happens. **False** — the payout is scaled down by the same proportion, and a total loss is capped at the sum insured. Malaysia, Mathematics Form 5, Ch. 3 §3.1, printed pp. 87–88.
