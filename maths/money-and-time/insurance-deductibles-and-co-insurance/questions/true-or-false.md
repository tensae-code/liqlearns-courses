# True or false — Insurance: Deductibles and Co-insurance

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Layer 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Insurance: Deductibles and Co-insurance — the Deductible · (Layer 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Insurance: Deductibles and Co-insurance — Property Co-insurance · (Layer 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Insurance: Deductibles and Co-insurance — Split Bills: Health Co-insurance.

1. (Sub lesson 1) A deductible is paid by the insurer. **False** — the insured pays it; the insurer pays the rest. Malaysia, Mathematics Form 5, Ch. 3 §3.1, printed pp. 86–87.
2. (Sub lesson 2) Insuring at or above the required percentage removes the co-insurance scaling. **True.** Malaysia, Mathematics Form 5, Ch. 3 §3.1, printed pp. 87–88.
3. (Sub lesson 3) Under-insuring saves money overall whenever a large loss happens. **False** — the payout is scaled down by the same proportion, and a total loss is capped at the sum insured. Malaysia, Mathematics Form 5, Ch. 3 §3.1, printed pp. 87–88.
