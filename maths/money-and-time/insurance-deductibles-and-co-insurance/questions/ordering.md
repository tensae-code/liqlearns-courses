# Ordering — Insurance: Deductibles and Co-insurance

1. (Layer 3) Put the steps for settling a health claim with both mechanics in order:
   (a) Split the remainder by the co-insurance percentage (b) Subtract the deductible (c) Receive the hospital bill (d) Patient pays deductible + their percentage share
   **Answer: c, b, a, d.** Malaysia, Mathematics Form 5, Ch. 3 §3.1, printed pp. 86–89.
