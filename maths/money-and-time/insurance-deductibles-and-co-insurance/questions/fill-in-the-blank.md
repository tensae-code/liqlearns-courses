# Fill in the blank — Insurance: Deductibles and Co-insurance

1. (Layer 1) The fixed first amount the insured pays on a claim is the ___.
   **Answer: deductible.** Malaysia, Mathematics Form 5, Ch. 3 §3.1, printed pp. 86–87.

2. (Layer 2) Payout under property co-insurance = (sum insured ÷ ___) × loss.
   **Answer: required sum insured.** Malaysia, Mathematics Form 5, Ch. 3 §3.1, printed pp. 87–88.

3. (Layer 3) In an 80/20 health split, the insurer's share of a RM6,500 bill is RM___.
   **Answer: 5,200.** Malaysia, Mathematics Form 5, Ch. 3 §3.1, printed pp. 88–89.
