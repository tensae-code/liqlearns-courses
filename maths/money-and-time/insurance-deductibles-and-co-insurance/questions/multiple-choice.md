# Multiple choice — Insurance: Deductibles and Co-insurance

1. (Layer 1) A RM1,800 claim under a RM250 deductible pays:
   A. RM1,800 B. RM1,550 C. RM250 D. RM2,050
   **Answer: B** — Malaysia, Mathematics Form 5, Ch. 3 §3.1, printed pp. 86–87.

2. (Layer 1) A RM200 claim under a RM300 deductible pays:
   A. RM200 B. RM100 C. RM0 D. RM300
   **Answer: C** — the whole claim sits inside the insured's slice. Malaysia, Mathematics Form 5, Ch. 3 §3.1, printed pp. 86–87.

3. (Layer 2) Property co-insurance scales a partial-loss payout when:
   A. The premium was late B. The sum insured is below the required percentage of the value C. The loss is small D. The owner has claimed before
   **Answer: B** — Malaysia, Mathematics Form 5, Ch. 3 §3.1, printed pp. 87–88.

4. (Layer 3) A RM9,000 hospital bill under an 80/20 split costs the patient:
   A. RM1,800 B. RM7,200 C. RM900 D. RM0
   **Answer: A** — Malaysia, Mathematics Form 5, Ch. 3 §3.1, printed pp. 88–89.
