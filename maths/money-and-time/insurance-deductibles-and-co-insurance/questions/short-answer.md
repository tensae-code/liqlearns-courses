# Short answer — Insurance: Deductibles and Co-insurance

1. (Layer 2) A house worth RM600,000 must be insured for at least 80% of value; the owner insures RM360,000. A partial loss of RM80,000 occurs. What is paid?
   **Answer: required = RM480,000; payout = (360,000 ÷ 480,000) × 80,000 = RM60,000.** Malaysia, Mathematics Form 5, Ch. 3 §3.1, printed pp. 87–88.

2. (Layer 2) In the same policy, the house is totally destroyed. What is paid, and why not RM600,000?
   **Answer: RM360,000 — a payout never exceeds the sum insured.** Malaysia, Mathematics Form 5, Ch. 3 §3.1, printed pp. 87–88.

3. (Layer 3) A bill of RM5,600 has a RM400 deductible, then a 75/25 split. What does the patient pay in total?
   **Answer: 5,600 − 400 = 5,200; 25% = RM1,300; plus the deductible: RM1,700.** Malaysia, Mathematics Form 5, Ch. 3 §3.1, printed pp. 86–89.
