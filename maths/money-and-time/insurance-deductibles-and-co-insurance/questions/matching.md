# Matching — Insurance: Deductibles and Co-insurance

1. (Layer 2) Match each claim story to the mechanic that decides it:
   (a) A RM450 car repair under a RM500 deductible (b) A fire in a house insured at half its required sum (c) A hospital bill under an 80/20 policy (d) A RM10,000 car repair under a RM500 deductible
   Mechanics: percentage split, fixed first slice (claim inside it — pays nothing), proportional scaling, fixed first slice (insurer pays the rest).
   **Answer: (a) pays nothing — claim inside the deductible, (b) proportional scaling, (c) percentage split, (d) insurer pays RM9,500 after the slice.** Malaysia, Mathematics Form 5, Ch. 3 §3.1, printed pp. 86–89.
