# Video script — Insurance: Deductibles and Co-insurance

Super-simple voice. The tutor talks; each bill is sliced as it is named.

---

The pool pays your claim. But not all of it. The first slice is yours.

A repair costs 3,500. Your deductible is 500. You pay 500. The pool pays 3,000. That first slice is the deductible. It keeps small claims small, and it keeps everyone careful.

Now a house. The rule says: insure at least 80 percent of its value. You insured half. Fire takes 100,000. The pool scales your claim down — half of what you should have carried. You do not get 100,000.

And a hospital bill? That splits by percent. Eighty for the pool. Twenty for you.

First slice, scale, or split. Always ask: which one is mine?
