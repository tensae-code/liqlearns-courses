## Sub lesson 1 — Insurance: Deductibles and Co-insurance — the Deductible
Say what a deductible is and compute insurer-pays = claim − deductible, including the nothing-paid case when the claim is smaller. Pass mark: 4 of 5.

## Sub lesson 2 — Insurance: Deductibles and Co-insurance — Property Co-insurance
From a property's value and the required percentage, find the required sum insured; judge whether a policy is under-insured; scale a partial loss by (sum insured ÷ required sum); state the total-loss cap. Pass mark: 5 of 6.

## Sub lesson 3 — Insurance: Deductibles and Co-insurance — Split Bills: Health Co-insurance
Apply a health co-insurance percentage split (with a deductible first, when there is one) and combine the two mechanics in one claim story. Pass mark: 4 of 5.

## What "maxed" means here

The learner can take any claim story in Malaysia's chapter — a motor repair, a fire in an under-insured house, a hospital bill — and return exactly what the insurer pays and what the insured pays, and explain WHY the policy pays less than the loss.
