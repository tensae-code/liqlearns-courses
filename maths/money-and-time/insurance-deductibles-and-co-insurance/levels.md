# Levels — Insurance: Deductibles and Co-insurance

One lesson, one level (Level 143). The depth layers inside it are ranks.

## Depth layers

- **Layer 1 — The deductible.** Say what a deductible is and compute insurer-pays = claim − deductible, including the nothing-paid case when the claim is smaller. Pass mark: 4 of 5.
- **Layer 2 — Property co-insurance.** From a property's value and the required percentage, find the required sum insured; judge whether a policy is under-insured; scale a partial loss by (sum insured ÷ required sum); state the total-loss cap. Pass mark: 5 of 6.
- **Layer 3 — Split bills.** Apply a health co-insurance percentage split (with a deductible first, when there is one) and combine the two mechanics in one claim story. Pass mark: 4 of 5.

## What "maxed" means here

The learner can take any claim story in Malaysia's chapter — a motor repair, a fire in an under-insured house, a hospital bill — and return exactly what the insurer pays and what the insured pays, and explain WHY the policy pays less than the loss.
