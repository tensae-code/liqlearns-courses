# Hands-on — Insurance: Deductibles and Co-insurance

## Activity 1 — Slice the bills (10 minutes)

Five claim cards (RM300, RM750, RM2,000, RM5,400, RM12,000) against a RM500 deductible. For each: who pays what? Include the RM300 card deliberately — the insurer pays nothing, and the learner must say why that is the rule working, not a trick.

## Activity 2 — The under-insured house (15 minutes)

A house worth RM500,000; the policy demands cover of at least 80% of value; the owner insured RM250,000. Losses: RM60,000 (partial), then RM500,000 (total). Compute both payouts: partial — (250,000 ÷ 400,000) × 60,000 = RM37,500; total — capped at the sum insured, RM250,000. The learner writes the one-sentence lesson of the activity.

## Activity 3 — Split the hospital bill (10 minutes)

Bills of RM4,800 and RM16,250 under an 80/20 health co-insurance, the second with a RM300 deductible applied first. Compute both splits: RM3,840/RM960; then (16,250 − 300) = 15,950 → RM12,760/RM3,190 plus the deductible.
