# Hands-on — Taxation

## Activity 1 — Shrink the bar (10 minutes)

Draw an income bar of RM60,000. Cut off relief blocks: self RM9,000, spouse RM4,000, two children RM4,000, EPF/life RM7,000. The remaining bar — RM36,000 — is the chargeable income. The learner states, before any rates appear, what was reduced and what was not.

## Activity 2 — Fill the staircase (15 minutes)

Using the lesson's example bands, compute the tax on RM36,000 twice: the wrong way (one top rate on everything) and the right way (band by band). Compare the two answers and have the learner explain, in one sentence, why the wrong way overstates the tax — this is the "pay rise made me poorer" myth, killed with arithmetic.

## Activity 3 — The year's balance (10 minutes)

The worker's PCB was RM55 a month. Final tax after rebate: RM510. Total paid: RM660. The learner computes the refund (RM150) and explains to a partner why a refund is not a gift — it is their own overpaid tax returning.
