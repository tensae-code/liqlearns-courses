## Sub lesson 1 — Taxation — What Is Taxed: Reliefs and Chargeable Income
Say what income tax is, what a relief does, and what chargeable income means. Pass mark: 4 of 5.

## Sub lesson 2 — Taxation — Band by Band
Apply a progressive rate table to a chargeable income slice by slice, and explain why a single flat rate on the whole income is wrong. Pass mark: 5 of 6.

## Sub lesson 3 — Taxation — the Full Computation: Rebate and PCB
From total income and a relief list to chargeable income, to tax, minus rebate, balanced against PCB already paid — ending in a refund or a balance due. Pass mark: 4 of 5.

## What "maxed" means here

The learner can take a payslip year's figures — income, reliefs, monthly deductions — and reproduce the tax office's arithmetic end to end, and can explain to a worried friend why entering a higher band never makes a pay rise shrink their take-home.
