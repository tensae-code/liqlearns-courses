# Levels — Taxation

One lesson, one level (Level 144). The depth layers inside it are ranks.

## Depth layers

- **Layer 1 — The shape of it.** Say what income tax is, what a relief does, and what chargeable income means. Pass mark: 4 of 5.
- **Layer 2 — Band by band.** Apply a progressive rate table to a chargeable income slice by slice, and explain why a single flat rate on the whole income is wrong. Pass mark: 5 of 6.
- **Layer 3 — The full computation.** From total income and a relief list to chargeable income, to tax, minus rebate, balanced against PCB already paid — ending in a refund or a balance due. Pass mark: 4 of 5.

## What "maxed" means here

The learner can take a payslip year's figures — income, reliefs, monthly deductions — and reproduce the tax office's arithmetic end to end, and can explain to a worried friend why entering a higher band never makes a pay rise shrink their take-home.
