# True or false — Taxation

1. (Layer 2) If your income rises into a higher band, ALL your income is taxed at the higher rate. **False** — only the slice in that band is. Malaysia, Mathematics Form 5, Ch. 4 §4.1, printed pp. 104–107.
2. (Layer 1) A rebate is subtracted from the computed tax. **True** — unlike a relief, which is subtracted from income before the bands. Malaysia, Mathematics Form 5, Ch. 4 §4.1, printed pp. 100–111.
3. (Layer 3) A tax refund means the government is giving you extra money. **False** — it is your own overpaid PCB returned. Malaysia, Mathematics Form 5, Ch. 4 §4.1, printed pp. 107–111.
