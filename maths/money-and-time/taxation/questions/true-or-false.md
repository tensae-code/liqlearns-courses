# True or false — Taxation

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Layer 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Taxation — What Is Taxed: Reliefs and Chargeable Income · (Layer 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Taxation — Band by Band · (Layer 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Taxation — the Full Computation: Rebate and PCB.

1. (Sub lesson 2) If your income rises into a higher band, ALL your income is taxed at the higher rate. **False** — only the slice in that band is. Malaysia, Mathematics Form 5, Ch. 4 §4.1, printed pp. 104–107.
2. (Sub lesson 1) A rebate is subtracted from the computed tax. **True** — unlike a relief, which is subtracted from income before the bands. Malaysia, Mathematics Form 5, Ch. 4 §4.1, printed pp. 100–111.
3. (Sub lesson 3) A tax refund means the government is giving you extra money. **False** — it is your own overpaid PCB returned. Malaysia, Mathematics Form 5, Ch. 4 §4.1, printed pp. 107–111.
