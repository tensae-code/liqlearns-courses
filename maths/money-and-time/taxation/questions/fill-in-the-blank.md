# Fill in the blank — Taxation

1. (Layer 1) Personal relief in Malaysia's table is RM___.
   **Answer: 9,000.** Malaysia, Mathematics Form 5, Ch. 4 §4.1, printed pp. 100–103.

2. (Layer 2) The first RM5,000 of chargeable income is taxed at ___%.
   **Answer: 0.** Malaysia, Mathematics Form 5, Ch. 4 §4.1, printed pp. 104–107.

3. (Layer 3) PCB stands for Monthly Tax ___.
   **Answer: Deduction.** Malaysia, Mathematics Form 5, Ch. 4 §4.1, printed pp. 107–111.
