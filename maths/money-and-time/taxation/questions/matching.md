1. (Sub lesson 1) Match each term to its meaning:
   (a) Relief (b) Chargeable income (c) Rebate (d) PCB
   Meanings: an amount taken off the computed tax; income left after reliefs, the amount the bands are applied to; monthly tax withheld by the employer; an amount taken off income before taxing.
   **Answer: (a) taken off income before taxing, (b) income left after reliefs, (c) taken off the computed tax, (d) monthly tax withheld by the employer.** Malaysia, Mathematics Form 5, Ch. 4 §4.1, printed pp. 100–111.
