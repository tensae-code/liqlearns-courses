# Multiple choice — Taxation

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Layer 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Taxation — What Is Taxed: Reliefs and Chargeable Income · (Layer 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Taxation — Band by Band · (Layer 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Taxation — the Full Computation: Rebate and PCB.

1. (Sub lesson 1) Chargeable income is:
   A. Total income B. Total income minus reliefs C. The tax due D. Income after tax
   **Answer: B** — Malaysia, Mathematics Form 5, Ch. 4 §4.1, printed pp. 100–103.

2. (Sub lesson 1) A relief reduces:
   A. The tax directly B. The income that gets taxed C. The rate D. The PCB
   **Answer: B** — Malaysia, Mathematics Form 5, Ch. 4 §4.1, printed pp. 100–103.

3. (Sub lesson 2) In a progressive table, the highest rate applies to:
   A. The whole income B. The whole chargeable income C. Only the top slice of income D. Nothing — it is never used
   **Answer: C** — Malaysia, Mathematics Form 5, Ch. 4 §4.1, printed pp. 104–107.

4. (Sub lesson 3) If PCB paid over the year is MORE than the final tax, the worker:
   A. Pays the difference B. Gets a refund C. Pays a fine D. Loses the difference
   **Answer: B** — Malaysia, Mathematics Form 5, Ch. 4 §4.1, printed pp. 107–111.
