# Short answer — Taxation

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Layer 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Taxation — What Is Taxed: Reliefs and Chargeable Income · (Layer 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Taxation — Band by Band · (Layer 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Taxation — the Full Computation: Rebate and PCB.

1. (Sub lesson 1) Total income RM55,000; reliefs total RM19,000. Find the chargeable income.
   **Answer: RM36,000.** Malaysia, Mathematics Form 5, Ch. 4 §4.1, printed pp. 100–103.

2. (Sub lesson 2) Bands: 0% on the first 5,000; 1% on the next 15,000; 3% above that. Compute the tax on a chargeable income of RM26,000.
   **Answer: 0 + 150 + 3% × 6,000 = 150 + 180 = RM330.** Malaysia, Mathematics Form 5, Ch. 4 §4.1, printed pp. 104–107.

3. (Sub lesson 3) Final tax after rebate is RM640. PCB paid was RM70 a month. Refund or balance, and how much?
   **Answer: PCB total = RM840 > RM640 → refund of RM200.** Malaysia, Mathematics Form 5, Ch. 4 §4.1, printed pp. 107–111.
