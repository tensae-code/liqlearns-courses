# Short answer — Taxation

1. (Layer 1) Total income RM55,000; reliefs total RM19,000. Find the chargeable income.
   **Answer: RM36,000.** Malaysia, Mathematics Form 5, Ch. 4 §4.1, printed pp. 100–103.

2. (Layer 2) Bands: 0% on the first 5,000; 1% on the next 15,000; 3% above that. Compute the tax on a chargeable income of RM26,000.
   **Answer: 0 + 150 + 3% × 6,000 = 150 + 180 = RM330.** Malaysia, Mathematics Form 5, Ch. 4 §4.1, printed pp. 104–107.

3. (Layer 3) Final tax after rebate is RM640. PCB paid was RM70 a month. Refund or balance, and how much?
   **Answer: PCB total = RM840 > RM640 → refund of RM200.** Malaysia, Mathematics Form 5, Ch. 4 §4.1, printed pp. 107–111.
