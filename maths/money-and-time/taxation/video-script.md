# Video script — Taxation

Super-simple voice. The tutor talks; the income bar and the staircase fill as they are named.

---

The government takes a share of what you earn. But not of all of it.

First, reliefs. Money for yourself, your spouse, your children, your pension savings — these shrink the part that is taxed. What is left is called chargeable income.

Then the staircase. The first slice is taxed gently. The next slice, a little more. Only the top slice pays the top rate. Nobody pays the top rate on everything. A pay rise always leaves you with more.

Each month, your job already pays a little tax for you. At year's end, the two numbers meet. Paid too much? Money comes back. Paid too little? You settle the rest.

Reliefs, bands, balance. That is income tax.
