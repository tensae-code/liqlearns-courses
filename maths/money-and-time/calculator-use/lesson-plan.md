A lesson that teaches the calculator itself. Sri Lanka's Grade 9
pupil books give the machine a whole chapter of its own — "The
Calculator" (English Part II, printed p. 12; the Sinhala twin's
chapter 11) — inside the numbers work: the scientific calculator's
keys, with =, %, x² and √x named in the chapter's own outcomes,
and the term-by-term Grade 9 guides carry the same calculator
teaching in every term. No lesson in this course taught the
machine before; the expansion read flagged it NEW LESSON NEEDED
and this lesson is built from that flag. Prerequisite (soft hint
only — every lesson stands alone): percentages for the % key,
powers-and-indices for x², roots-and-surds for √x. A learner who
opens this lesson directly still gets the whole thing: each key
is met, named and used here.
