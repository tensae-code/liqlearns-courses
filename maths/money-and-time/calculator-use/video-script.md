# Video script — Calculator Use

Super-simple voice. The tutor talks; a real calculator sits on
screen, and each named key lights up as it is pressed.

---

This is a calculator. It answers what you press. Not what you
meant — what you press. So we learn to press exactly what we
mean.

Here is the window. Your numbers show here. Here are the number
keys. Four then eight makes forty-eight. Here are the working
keys: add, take away, times, share. And here is the finishing
key: equals. Press it, and the answer comes.

Pressed the wrong key? Do not hope. Clear it. Start that sum
again, clean.

Now three keys that do big jobs. The percent key. Fifteen
percent of two hundred and forty — press, and it shows
thirty-six. The square key: thirteen squared is one hundred and
sixty-nine. And the root key: the root of one hundred and
forty-four is twelve. Square and root undo each other.

Last, the best trick. Before you press, guess roughly. Forty-
eight times fifty-two is about fifty times fifty — about two
thousand five hundred. Now press. Two thousand four hundred and
ninety-six. Close to the guess — good. If the window showed
something far away, a finger slipped. The guess catches it.

The machine answers what you press. Press what you mean.
