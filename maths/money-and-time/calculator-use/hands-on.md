# Hands-on — Calculator Use

## Activity 1 — The key hunt (10 minutes)

On a real calculator (or a clear photograph of one), find and
touch each named key in turn: the display, 0–9, the point, +,
−, ×, ÷, =, the clear key, %, x², √x. For each one, say its job
in one short sentence before moving on — "equals finishes and
shows the answer." This is Sub lesson 1 done with your own
fingers.

## Activity 2 — Shop percentages (15 minutes)

Write five prices (for example 850, 1,200, 460, 2,750, 999).
For each one, find a 20% discount with the % key, then check
the answer the percentages way — 10% first, doubled. Both
routes must agree before you write the sale price (price minus
discount). Finish with a 15% discount on the last price and
check it as 10% + 5%.

## Activity 3 — Estimate, press, catch (15 minutes)

Partner A secretly writes a calculation with its answer (say
48 × 52 = 2,496) and a "trapped" version with one mis-press
(249.6 or 24,960). Partner B sees only the two displays, must
estimate first — "about 50 × 50, about 2,500" — and points to
the trapped one, saying what slipped. Swap roles three times.
This is Sub lesson 3 as a game: the estimate catches what the
fingers did wrong.
