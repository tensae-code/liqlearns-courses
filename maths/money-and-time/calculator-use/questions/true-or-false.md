# True or false — Calculator Use

Sub-lesson markers: every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to — Sub lesson 1 — Meet the Calculator · Sub lesson 2 — The Everyday Keys: %, x² and √x · Sub lesson 3 — The Calculator as a Checking Tool. This lesson was built new with its sub lessons (Sri Lanka expansion merge, 2026-11-11), so its markers are original — there are no older depth layers to map from.

1. (Sub lesson 1) A calculator answers exactly what you pressed, not what you meant to press.
   **Answer: True.** Item constructed for this lesson from its founding teaching. Sri Lanka, *Mathematics Part II*, Grade 9 pupil's book (English medium), "The Calculator", printed p. 12 (the Sinhala Grade 9 Part II twin's chapter 11 carries the same teaching).

2. (Sub lesson 1) The % key is the key that finishes every calculation.
   **Answer: False** — the = key finishes a calculation; % works out a percentage. Item constructed for this lesson from its founding teaching. Sri Lanka, *Mathematics Part II*, Grade 9 pupil's book (English medium), "The Calculator", printed p. 12 (the Sinhala Grade 9 Part II twin's chapter 11 carries the same teaching).

3. (Sub lesson 2) The √x key and the x² key undo each other: the square root of 15² is 15.
   **Answer: True.** Item constructed for this lesson from its founding teaching. Sri Lanka, *Mathematics Part II*, Grade 9 pupil's book (English medium), "The Calculator", printed p. 12 (the Sinhala Grade 9 Part II twin's chapter 11 carries the same teaching).

4. (Sub lesson 2) 10% of 50 is 500.
   **Answer: False** — 10% of 50 is 5. Item constructed for this lesson from its founding teaching. Sri Lanka, *Mathematics Part II*, Grade 9 pupil's book (English medium), "The Calculator", printed p. 12 (the Sinhala Grade 9 Part II twin's chapter 11 carries the same teaching).

5. (Sub lesson 3) An estimate made before pressing the keys can catch a mis-pressed digit.
   **Answer: True.** Item constructed for this lesson from its founding teaching. Sri Lanka, *Mathematics Part II*, Grade 9 pupil's book (English medium), "The Calculator", printed p. 12 (the Sinhala Grade 9 Part II twin's chapter 11 carries the same teaching).

6. (Sub lesson 3) If the calculator's display shows an answer, the answer must be right.
   **Answer: False** — the machine answers what was pressed; a slipped key gives a wrong answer, neatly displayed. Item constructed for this lesson from its founding teaching. Sri Lanka, *Mathematics Part II*, Grade 9 pupil's book (English medium), "The Calculator", printed p. 12 (the Sinhala Grade 9 Part II twin's chapter 11 carries the same teaching).
