# Ordering — Calculator Use

Sub-lesson markers: every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to — Sub lesson 1 — Meet the Calculator · Sub lesson 2 — The Everyday Keys: %, x² and √x · Sub lesson 3 — The Calculator as a Checking Tool. This lesson was built new with its sub lessons (Sri Lanka expansion merge, 2026-11-11), so its markers are original — there are no older depth layers to map from.

1. (Sub lesson 1) Put the steps of a calculation in order:
   press = / type the first number / press the working key (×, +, …) / type the second number
   **Answer: type the first number → press the working key → type the second number → press =.** Item constructed for this lesson from its founding teaching. Sri Lanka, *Mathematics Part II*, Grade 9 pupil's book (English medium), "The Calculator", printed p. 12 (the Sinhala Grade 9 Part II twin's chapter 11 carries the same teaching).

2. (Sub lesson 2) Put these in order, smallest first:
   5², √25, 50% of 30, 3²
   **Answer: √25 (= 5) → 3² (= 9) → 50% of 30 (= 15) → 5² (= 25).** Item constructed for this lesson from its founding teaching. Sri Lanka, *Mathematics Part II*, Grade 9 pupil's book (English medium), "The Calculator", printed p. 12 (the Sinhala Grade 9 Part II twin's chapter 11 carries the same teaching).

3. (Sub lesson 3) Put the checking routine in order:
   calculate / estimate first / compare the two / re-enter if they are far apart
   **Answer: estimate first → calculate → compare the two → re-enter if they are far apart.** Item constructed for this lesson from its founding teaching. Sri Lanka, *Mathematics Part II*, Grade 9 pupil's book (English medium), "The Calculator", printed p. 12 (the Sinhala Grade 9 Part II twin's chapter 11 carries the same teaching).
