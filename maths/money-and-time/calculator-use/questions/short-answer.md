1. (Sub lesson 1) Write the keys you press, in order, to work out 36 + 48.

4. (Sub lesson 2) A shirt costs 850 rupees and is reduced by 20%. How much do you save?

6. (Sub lesson 3) A learner's calculator shows 63 ÷ 9 = 63. Without pressing anything, say why it must be wrong, and give the right answer.
   **Answer: sharing 63 into 9 parts must give something smaller than 63 — a key was mis-pressed; 63 ÷ 9 = 7.** Item constructed for this lesson from its founding teaching.
