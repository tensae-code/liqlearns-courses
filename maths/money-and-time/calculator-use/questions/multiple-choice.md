1. (Sub lesson 1) Which key finishes a calculation and shows the answer?
   (a) %
   (b) = ✔
   (c) x²
   (d) √x
   **Answer: (b).** Item constructed for this lesson from its founding teaching. Sri Lanka, *Mathematics Part II*, Grade 9 pupil's book (English medium), "The Calculator", printed p. 12 (the Sinhala Grade 9 Part II twin's chapter 11 carries the same teaching).

   (c) press the % key to fix it
   (d) switch the calculator off and on

3. (Sub lesson 2) Pressing 240 × 15 % gives:
   (a) 255
   (b) 36 ✔
   (c) 15
   (d) 2,400
   **Answer: (b)** — 15% of 240 is 36. Item constructed for this lesson from its founding teaching. Sri Lanka, *Mathematics Part II*, Grade 9 pupil's book (English medium), "The Calculator", printed p. 12 (the Sinhala Grade 9 Part II twin's chapter 11 carries the same teaching).
