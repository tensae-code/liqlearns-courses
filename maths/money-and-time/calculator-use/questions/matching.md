# Matching — Calculator Use

Sub-lesson markers: every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to — Sub lesson 1 — Meet the Calculator · Sub lesson 2 — The Everyday Keys: %, x² and √x · Sub lesson 3 — The Calculator as a Checking Tool. This lesson was built new with its sub lessons (Sri Lanka expansion merge, 2026-11-11), so its markers are original — there are no older depth layers to map from.

1. (Sub lesson 1) Match each key to its job:
   (a) =
   (b) %
   (c) C
   Jobs: finishes the calculation and shows the answer / works out a percentage / wipes a wrong entry so you can start again.
   **Answer: (a) finishes and shows the answer; (b) works out a percentage; (c) wipes a wrong entry.** Item constructed for this lesson from its founding teaching. Sri Lanka, *Mathematics Part II*, Grade 9 pupil's book (English medium), "The Calculator", printed p. 12 (the Sinhala Grade 9 Part II twin's chapter 11 carries the same teaching).

2. (Sub lesson 2) Match each key-press to its result:
   (a) 12, then x²
   (b) 81, then √x
   (c) 90 × 50 %
   Results: 9 / 45 / 144.
   **Answer: (a) 144; (b) 9; (c) 45.** Item constructed for this lesson from its founding teaching. Sri Lanka, *Mathematics Part II*, Grade 9 pupil's book (English medium), "The Calculator", printed p. 12 (the Sinhala Grade 9 Part II twin's chapter 11 carries the same teaching).

3. (Sub lesson 3) Match each display to "plausible" or "mis-keyed", knowing the question each time:
   (a) 7 × 8 = 56
   (b) 7 × 8 = 560
   (c) 19 × 21 = 399
   **Answer: (a) plausible; (b) mis-keyed — an extra 0 crept in; (c) plausible — near the estimate 20 × 20 = 400.** Item constructed for this lesson from its founding teaching. Sri Lanka, *Mathematics Part II*, Grade 9 pupil's book (English medium), "The Calculator", printed p. 12 (the Sinhala Grade 9 Part II twin's chapter 11 carries the same teaching).
