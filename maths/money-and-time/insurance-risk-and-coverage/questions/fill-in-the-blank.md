# Fill in the blank — Insurance: Risk and Coverage

1. (Layer 1) The regular payment a policyholder makes is the ___; the amount the policy promises to pay is the ___ insured.
   **Answer: premium; sum.** Malaysia, Mathematics Form 5, Ch. 3 §3.1, printed pp. 74–76.

2. (Layer 2) A medical card pays for ___ and surgery bills.
   **Answer: hospitalisation.** Malaysia, Mathematics Form 5, Ch. 3 §3.1, printed pp. 78–79.

3. (Layer 3) Name one factor that raises a premium: ___.
   **Answer: any of age, smoking status, poor health, longer travel, larger sum insured.** Malaysia, Mathematics Form 5, Ch. 3 §3.1, printed pp. 80–83.
