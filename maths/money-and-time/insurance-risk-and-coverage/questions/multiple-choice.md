1. (Sub lesson 1) Insurance works because:
   A. Everyone claims every year B. Many people's premiums pay the few people's losses C. The government pays all losses D. Premiums are invested in houses
   **Answer: B** — Malaysia, Mathematics Form 5, Ch. 3 §3.1, printed pp. 74–76.

2. (Sub lesson 2) Which motor policy is the legal minimum and covers third-party injury only?
   A. Comprehensive B. Third party, fire and theft C. The Act policy D. Houseowner
   **Answer: C** — Malaysia, Mathematics Form 5, Ch. 3 §3.1, printed pp. 79–80.

3. (Sub lesson 2) Householder insurance covers:
   A. The building B. The contents C. The land D. The car
   **Answer: B** — Malaysia, Mathematics Form 5, Ch. 3 §3.1, printed pp. 80–81.

4. (Sub lesson 3) Which change raises a life premium?
   A. Younger age B. Giving up smoking C. A larger sum insured D. A shorter term
   **Answer: C** — Malaysia, Mathematics Form 5, Ch. 3 §3.1, printed pp. 80–83.
