# Matching — Insurance: Risk and Coverage

1. (Layer 2) Match each loss to the cover that pays it:
   (a) Hospital surgery bill (b) Stolen car (c) Burnt sofa in your rented flat (d) A lump sum on a cancer diagnosis
   Covers: medical card, third party fire and theft (or comprehensive), householder, critical-illness cover.
   **Answer: (a) medical card, (b) third party fire and theft, (c) householder, (d) critical-illness cover.** Malaysia, Mathematics Form 5, Ch. 3 §3.1, printed pp. 78–81.
