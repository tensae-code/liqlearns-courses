# Short answer — Insurance: Risk and Coverage

1. (Layer 1) In one sentence, say where the money comes from when an insured house burns down.
   **Answer: from the pool of premiums paid by all the insured households, most of whom had no fire.** Malaysia, Mathematics Form 5, Ch. 3 §3.1, printed pp. 74–76.

2. (Layer 2) State the difference between whole life and term life insurance.
   **Answer: whole life covers for life and builds savings value; term covers a fixed period only, with no payout or savings at the end — and cheaper premiums.** Malaysia, Mathematics Form 5, Ch. 3 §3.1, printed pp. 76–78.

3. (Layer 3) A rate table charges RM2.50 per RM1,000 per year. What is the yearly premium for RM80,000 of cover?
   **Answer: 80 × RM2.50 = RM200.** Malaysia, Mathematics Form 5, Ch. 3 §3.1, printed pp. 80–83.
