# True or false — Insurance: Risk and Coverage

1. (Layer 1) Insurance is a way to make a profit when nothing goes wrong. **False** — it buys protection; premiums are not returned when there is no claim (term cover). Malaysia, Mathematics Form 5, Ch. 3 §3.1, printed pp. 74–78.
2. (Layer 2) Comprehensive motor cover includes damage to your own vehicle. **True.** Malaysia, Mathematics Form 5, Ch. 3 §3.1, printed pp. 79–80.
3. (Layer 3) Two people of the same age insuring the same sum always pay the same premium. **False** — health and smoking status also move the price. Malaysia, Mathematics Form 5, Ch. 3 §3.1, printed pp. 80–83.
