# True or false — Insurance: Risk and Coverage

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Layer 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Insurance: Risk and Coverage — Risk and the Pool · (Layer 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Insurance: Risk and Coverage — the Kinds of Cover · (Layer 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Insurance: Risk and Coverage — Pricing a Cover.

1. (Sub lesson 1) Insurance is a way to make a profit when nothing goes wrong. **False** — it buys protection; premiums are not returned when there is no claim (term cover). Malaysia, Mathematics Form 5, Ch. 3 §3.1, printed pp. 74–78.
2. (Sub lesson 2) Comprehensive motor cover includes damage to your own vehicle. **True.** Malaysia, Mathematics Form 5, Ch. 3 §3.1, printed pp. 79–80.
3. (Sub lesson 3) Two people of the same age insuring the same sum always pay the same premium. **False** — health and smoking status also move the price. Malaysia, Mathematics Form 5, Ch. 3 §3.1, printed pp. 80–83.
