# Levels — Insurance: Risk and Coverage

One lesson, one level (Level 142). The depth layers inside it are ranks.

## Depth layers

- **Layer 1 — The idea.** Explain risk pooling in your own words and say what a premium and a sum insured are. Pass mark: 4 of 5.
- **Layer 2 — The kinds.** Tell whole life from term life, medical card from critical-illness cover, houseowner from householder, and the three motor policies from each other — in order of how much they cover. Pass mark: 5 of 6.
- **Layer 3 — Price a cover.** Read a premium from a rate table and say which way each factor (age, smoking, sum insured, travel length) pushes it, and why. Pass mark: 4 of 5.

## What "maxed" means here

The learner can be shown any everyday policy — a life policy, a medical card, a motor policy — and say what risk it pools, what it covers and what it leaves out, and estimate how its premium was set. Claims mechanics (deductibles and co-insurance) are the next lesson.
