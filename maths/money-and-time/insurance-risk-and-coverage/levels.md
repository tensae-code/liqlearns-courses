## Sub lesson 1 — Insurance: Risk and Coverage — Risk and the Pool
Explain risk pooling in your own words and say what a premium and a sum insured are. Pass mark: 4 of 5.

## Sub lesson 2 — Insurance: Risk and Coverage — the Kinds of Cover
Tell whole life from term life, medical card from critical-illness cover, houseowner from householder, and the three motor policies from each other — in order of how much they cover. Pass mark: 5 of 6.

## Sub lesson 3 — Insurance: Risk and Coverage — Pricing a Cover
Read a premium from a rate table and say which way each factor (age, smoking, sum insured, travel length) pushes it, and why. Pass mark: 4 of 5.

## What "maxed" means here

The learner can be shown any everyday policy — a life policy, a medical card, a motor policy — and say what risk it pools, what it covers and what it leaves out, and estimate how its premium was set. Claims mechanics (deductibles and co-insurance) are the next lesson.
