# Video script — Insurance: Risk and Coverage

Super-simple voice. The tutor talks; the pool picture fills as it is described.

---

A fire can take a house. It will not take every house. Nobody knows whose.

So everyone pays a small coin. The coins make one big pool. When the fire comes, the pool pays. That is insurance. Many small payments guard one big loss.

You pay a premium. The pool promises a sum. That sum is your cover.

Life cover pays when a life ends. Health cover pays the hospital. Motor cover has three sizes — the smallest guards other people; the biggest guards your car too. House cover guards the building, or the things inside.

Old age, sickness, a big sum — the premium grows. Risk is priced. That is the whole idea.
