# Hands-on — Insurance: Risk and Coverage

## Activity 1 — Run a tiny pool (15 minutes)

Ten learners each put in 2 counters as a "premium". Roll a die for each learner: a 6 is a "fire". A fire costs 15 counters from the pool. Run three rounds and count: the pool holds 20 per round; one fire is payable, two fires break it. Discuss what a real insurer does that the class pool cannot (thousands of members, reserves, pricing by risk).

## Activity 2 — Policy sorting (10 minutes)

Cards name situations: "my car hits a fence post", "my car is stolen", "I break a stranger's leg with my car", "my house burns", "my television burns with it". Sort each card under: Act policy / third party, fire and theft / comprehensive / houseowner / householder / none of these. Defend each sort in one sentence.

## Activity 3 — Read the table (10 minutes)

Given a simple premium rate table (per RM1,000 of sum insured, by age band and smoker status), price three people: a 25-year-old non-smoker, a 45-year-old non-smoker, a 45-year-old smoker, each for RM100,000 of term cover. Say which single change moved the price most.
