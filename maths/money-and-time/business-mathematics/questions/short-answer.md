# Business Mathematics — short answer

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Level 1)** A bicycle is bought for SSP 3,390 and sold for SSP 3,820 (South Sudan, Pupil's Book 6). Find the profit.
**Answer:** SSP 430 — 3,820 − 3,390.

**2. (Level 1)** Total Nepal's Grade 3 first bill: Rs 40 + Rs 45 + Rs 18 + Rs 8.
**Answer:** Rs 111 — the book's checked total.

**3. (Level 1)** Nepal's Grade 3 fruit-shop bill prints a total of 70. What is the true total?
**Answer:** Rs 90 — taught corrected; the printed 70 is wrong.

**4. (Level 1)** South Sudan's Primary 7 bill, Example 7, prints SSP 10,500. What is the true total?
**Answer:** SSP 1,750 — taught corrected.

**5. (Level 2)** Find the simple interest on Birr 2,200 at 12% for 3 years (Ethiopia, Grade 6).
**Answer:** 792 — 2,200 × 12% × 3.

**6. (Level 2)** A motorbike is bought for 32,000 riel after a 60% discount (Cambodia, Grade 9). What was the marked price?
**Answer:** 80,000 riel — 32,000 is 40% of the marked price; 32,000 ÷ 0.40 = 80,000.

**7. (Level 2)** Nepal's VAT chain (Grade 9) charges VAT of Rs 1,300, Rs 325, Rs 325 and Rs 390 at its four steps. Find the total VAT.
**Answer:** Rs 2,340 — 1,300 + 325 + 325 + 390.

**8. (Level 2)** Interest of Rs 800 is taxed at 5% (Nepal, Grade 8). Find the tax and the net interest.
**Answer:** Tax Rs 40; net interest Rs 760.

**9. (Level 3)** A widow's share of an inheritance is 1/8 (Pakistan, Grade 8). What fraction is left for the children?
**Answer:** 7/8 — the rest is shared between sons and daughters at 2 : 1.

**10. (Level 3)** In Iraq's Grade 11 Literary insurance working, log 1.06 is printed as 0.253. Give the correct value.
**Answer:** 0.0253 — taught corrected; every value after it in the book is right.
