# Business Mathematics — ordering

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Business Mathematics — the Shop · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Business Mathematics — Borrowing and Lending · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Business Mathematics — Long Money.

Put the items in the order asked. Check each step before you write the answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Put Nepal's VAT chain sellers in the order the goods pass through them: retailer, producer, wholesaler, dealer.
Answer in order, smallest first.
**Answer:** producer, dealer, wholesaler, retailer — Nepal Grade 9, Unit 2 — prices 10,000 → 12,500 → 15,000 → 18,000

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Put Ethiopia Grade 12's compound table for Birr 6,000 at 8% in growing order: 7,558.27; 6,480; 8,815.97; 6,998.40; 8,162.93.
Answer in order, smallest first.
**Answer:** 6,480, 6,998.40, 7,558.27, 8,162.93, 8,815.97 — each year multiplies the last by 1.08 (Unit 5, §5.2)

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Put the hire-purchase steps in order: pay the last instalment; pay the deposit; take the goods home; pay the monthly instalments.
Answer in order, smallest first.
**Answer:** pay the deposit, take the goods home, pay the monthly instalments, pay the last instalment — South Africa Grade 8 WS9 and South Sudan P7 teach deposit first

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Put the inheritance steps in Pakistan Grade 8's order: share the rest 2 : 1; take out the widow's 1/8; value the whole estate.
Answer in order, smallest first.
**Answer:** value the whole estate, take out the widow's 1/8, share the rest 2 : 1 — the fixed share comes off before the children's sharing

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Put an amortising loan's payments in the order of their interest slices, biggest interest slice first: the last payment; the first payment; a middle payment.
Answer in order, smallest first.
**Answer:** the first payment, a middle payment, the last payment — the balance only falls, so the interest slice only shrinks (Greece EPAL, Ch.9)
