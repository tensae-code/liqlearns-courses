# Business Mathematics — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A book is bought for SSP 20 and sold for SSP 30 (South Sudan, Pupil's Book 4). The profit is:
a) SSP 10  b) SSP 20  c) SSP 50
**Answer:** a) SSP 10 — profit = selling price − buying price = 30 − 20

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A radio is bought for SSP 1,200 and sold for SSP 1,000. This is:
a) a profit of 200  b) a loss of 200  c) neither
**Answer:** b) a loss of 200 — selling below cost is a loss of 1,200 − 1,000

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** A marked price of Rs 750 takes a 10% discount (Nepal, Grade 8). You pay:
a) Rs 675  b) Rs 750  c) Rs 825
**Answer:** a) Rs 675 — 10% of 750 is 75; 750 − 75 = 675

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Commission at 15% on a sale of SSP 25,000 (South Sudan, Pupil's Book 8) is:
a) SSP 2,500  b) SSP 3,750  c) SSP 25,000
**Answer:** b) SSP 3,750 — commission = selling price × rate = 25,000 × 15%

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Simple interest on 3,500,000 riel at 3% for 4 years (Cambodia, Grade 6) is:
a) 105,000 riel  b) 420,000 riel  c) 3,920,000 riel
**Answer:** b) 420,000 riel — I = PRT/100 = 3,500,000 × 3 × 4 ÷ 100

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** In Bangladesh Class 8 Exp.4 (English p.76), the principal that gives the printed answer is:
a) 1,500 tk  b) 15,000 tk  c) 150,000 tk
**Answer:** b) 15,000 tk — taught corrected: the English printing drops a zero; the Bangla version and the answer both use 15,000

**7. (Sub lesson 2)** A heater marked Rs 1,700 takes 13% VAT with no discount (Nepal, Grade 8). You pay:
a) Rs 1,700  b) Rs 1,921  c) Rs 2,091
**Answer:** b) Rs 1,921 — VAT = 13% of 1,700 = 221; 1,700 + 221 = 1,921

**8. (Sub lesson 2)** Compound interest for 2 years on 8,000,000 riel at 15% (Cambodia, Grade 8) is:
a) 2,400,000 riel  b) 2,580,000 riel  c) 10,580,000 riel
**Answer:** b) 2,580,000 riel — year 1: 1,200,000; year 2 on 9,200,000: 1,380,000; total 2,580,000 (10,580,000 is the amount, not the interest)

**9. (Sub lesson 3)** Zakat at 2.5% on savings of 500,000 riyals above the nisab (Yemen, Grade 6) is:
a) 5,000 riyals  b) 12,500 riyals  c) 25,000 riyals
**Answer:** b) 12,500 riyals — zakat = 1/40 of 500,000 = 12,500

**10. (Sub lesson 3)** In Nicaragua's Grade 10 module, monthly compounding uses the factor:
a) (1.015)¹²⁰  b) (1.005)¹²⁰  c) (1.05)¹²⁰
**Answer:** b) (1.005)¹²⁰ — taught corrected: the printed (1.015)¹²⁰ is a slip; the book's own result C$ 18,193.96 comes from 1.005
