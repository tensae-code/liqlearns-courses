# Business Mathematics — true or false

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Level 1)** A discount of 12% on SSP 62.50 gives SSP 55.00.
**Answer:** True — South Sudan S1 guide (teacher evidence): 12% of 62.50 is 7.50

**2. (Level 1)** South Sudan Primary 7's hire-purchase interest is 7,000 − 2,000 = 5,000.
**Answer:** False — taught corrected: the interest is SSP 2,000 (7,000 − 5,000)

**3. (Level 2)** Compound interest in year 2 is worked on the original principal only.
**Answer:** False — it is worked on the grown amount — Cambodia Grade 8's year 2 runs on 9,200,000, not 8,000,000

**4. (Level 2)** Ethiopia Grade 12's examples at pp.330 and 380 may be cited as verified.
**Answer:** False — both are unverified by check and are cited nowhere in this lesson

**5. (Level 2)** A hire purchase always costs the same as the cash price.
**Answer:** False — deposit plus instalments totals more than the cash price — the difference is the price of paying slowly

**6. (Level 3)** A son's inheritance share is twice a daughter's in Pakistan's Grade 8 shares.
**Answer:** True — sons : daughters = 2 : 1, after the widow's 1/8

**7. (Level 3)** Mauritania's gold nisab for zakat is 85 g.
**Answer:** True — 6AS-LO, Ch.1, pp.8–13; silver's is 595 g

**8. (Level 3)** On a currency round trip you get back exactly what you started with.
**Answer:** False — buying and selling rates differ; the bank keeps the gap
