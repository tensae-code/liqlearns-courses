# Business Mathematics — matching

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Business Mathematics — the Shop · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Business Mathematics — Borrowing and Lending · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Business Mathematics — Long Money.

Match each item on the left to its partner on the right.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Match each name to its meaning: (a) commission (b) discount (c) deposit — a cut off the marked price; the first part-payment of a hire purchase; a percentage fee on a sale
**Answer:** a) a percentage fee on a sale, b) a cut off the marked price, c) the first part-payment of a hire purchase.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Match each formula to its job: (a) I = PRT/100 (b) A = P(1+r)ⁿ (c) value × (1−r)ⁿ — compound amount; compound depreciation; simple interest
**Answer:** a) simple interest, b) compound amount, c) compound depreciation.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Match each country to its money lesson: (a) Nepal (b) Pakistan (c) Greece — bills of exchange and amortisation; the VAT chain; zakat, ushr and inheritance shares
**Answer:** a) the VAT chain, b) zakat, ushr and inheritance shares, c) bills of exchange and amortisation.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Match each corrected value to its book: (a) 15,000 tk (b) Rs 90 (c) SSP 1,750 — South Sudan Primary 7 bill; Bangladesh Class 8 principal; Nepal Grade 3 fruit bill
**Answer:** a) Bangladesh Class 8 principal, b) Nepal Grade 3 fruit bill, c) South Sudan Primary 7 bill.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Match each stream to its name: (a) equal payments growing in a fund (b) a loan cut payment by payment (c) a promise to pay on a future date sold early at a discount — bill of exchange; annuity; amortisation
**Answer:** a) annuity, b) amortisation, c) bill of exchange.
