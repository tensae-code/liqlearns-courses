# Business Mathematics — fill-in-the-blank

Fill in the missing number or word.

**1. (Level 1)** Profit = selling price − ___ price.
**Answer:** buying (cost)

**2. (Level 1)** Percentage profit is always reckoned on the ___ price.
**Answer:** cost — Sierra Leone, JSS 2, L37

**3. (Level 2)** I = P × ___ × T ÷ 100.
**Answer:** R (the rate) — Laos, Grade 9, p.32

**4. (Level 2)** Compound amount: A = P(1 + ___)ⁿ.
**Answer:** r — Laos, Grade 9, p.32

**5. (Level 2)** Nepal's refrigerator: Rs 27,876.10 + VAT Rs ___ = Rs 31,500.
**Answer:** 3,623.90

**6. (Level 3)** Zakat is ___ % of wealth above the nisab.
**Answer:** 2.5

**7. (Level 3)** Ushr on naturally irrigated crops is ___ %; on artificially irrigated crops it is 5%.
**Answer:** 10 — Pakistan, Grade 7, p.63

**8. (Level 3)** Iraq's Grade 11 Literary annuity of 59,875,000 is a log-table ___, not an error.
**Answer:** rounding — its true value is about 60,030,578
