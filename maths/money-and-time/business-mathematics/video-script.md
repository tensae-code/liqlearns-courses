# Video script — Business Mathematics

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Buy low. Sell high. The gap is your **profit**.
Buy high, sell low — that gap is your **loss**. Count both on what you PAID.

A price tag says 750. Ten percent off! You pay 675. That cut is a **discount**.

Lend money, and it grows. Simple growth: the same amount every year.
Compound growth: growth ON the growth — bigger every year. That is **interest**.

The shop adds tax. You pay the price PLUS the tax. That is **VAT**.

Sell for someone else? You keep a small slice of every sale. That is **commission**.

Pay a little now, a little every month? You pay MORE in the end. That is **hire purchase**.

Now you. Price one trade. Grow one loan. Total one bill.
That is business mathematics.
