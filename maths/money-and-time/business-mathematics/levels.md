# Business Mathematics — questions by level

Practice by level, tied to the skill number. The questions live in
the type files; this file is the by-level view. The levels are the
depths inside this one thing — business mathematics — shop arithmetic first, then borrowing and lending, then the long-money instruments.

## Level 1 — the shop: profit, loss, discount, commission, wages, bills
Level 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: find profit, loss and their percentages on the cost price; take a discount off a marked price; find a commission; total a bill correctly, checking each line.

## Level 2 — borrowing and lending: interest, tax, hire purchase, currency, depreciation
Level 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: use I = PRT/100 in all four directions; grow compound interest year by year and by formula; add VAT; price a hire purchase against its cash price; convert currency at a given rate; depreciate an amount.

## Level 3 — long money: annuities, present value, amortisation, partnership, insurance, zakat and inheritance, bills of exchange
Level 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: find an annuity's present and final values and a discounted present value; read an amortisation; share partnership profit by capital × time; work an insurance premium; find zakat and ushr; share an inheritance in the books' shares; find a bill of exchange's discounted value and mean maturity.
