# Video script — Computation Short Cuts

Super-simple voice. The tutor talks; each calculation is
written big on screen, and the short cut's steps appear one
at a time as they are spoken.

---

Some sums look hard. They are not hard. They are hiding a
short cut.

Times ten? The numbers just step to the left. Forty-six
times ten is four hundred and sixty. Times five? Times ten,
then take half. Thirty-four times ten is three hundred and
forty. Half is one hundred and seventy. Done.

Nine is ten in a disguise. Twenty-three times ten is two
hundred and thirty. Take twenty-three away. Two hundred and
seven. That is twenty-three times nine.

Eleven splits. Thirty-five times eleven: three, three plus
five, five. Three hundred and eighty-five.

Last trick. Double one side, halve the other. Sixteen times
twenty-five becomes eight times fifty, becomes four times
one hundred. Four hundred.

Never fight the numbers you are given. Find the easy
neighbours hiding inside them.
