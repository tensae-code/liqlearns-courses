# Hands-on — Computation Short Cuts

## Activity 1 — Beat the written way (10 minutes)

Write six multiplications on cards: 38 × 10, 38 × 100,
27 × 5, 27 × 50, 415 × 10, 96 × 100. Partner A works each
one the slow written way on paper; Partner B uses only the
tens short cuts, aloud. Time both. Then swap roles with six
new cards. This is Sub lesson 1 with a stopwatch: the shift
must feel faster than the columns.

## Activity 2 — Nine, ninety-nine, eleven (15 minutes)

Deal out two-figure numbers (14, 23, 35, 56, 67, 82). For
each one, the player must give ×9, ×99 and ×11 — saying the
cut before the answer ("times ten, take away"). A wrong
answer is re-done the slow way on paper, side by side with
the cut, until the two agree. This is Sub lesson 2 done by
voice.

## Activity 3 — Rearrange it (15 minutes)

Write 16 × 25, 28 × 25, 18 × 15, 36 × 25, 7 × 12 and
98 + 47 on a sheet. For each, draw the rearrangement as a
chain of arrows (16 × 25 → 8 × 50 → 4 × 100 = 400) before
writing any answer. Partner checks each chain: does every
step keep the value the same? This is Sub lesson 3 on paper:
no answer is trusted until its chain is legal.
