1. (Sub lesson 1) 46 × 100 =
   (a) 460
   (b) 4,600 ✔
   (c) 46,000
   (d) 146
   **Answer: (b).** Item constructed for this lesson from its founding teaching. Dominica, *Mathematics: A Complete Course with CXC Questions, Volume One*, 2nd ed. (student book), Grade 10, Ch. 3 — Computation, printed p. 75.

2. (Sub lesson 1) The short cut for ×5 is:
   (a) ×10, then halve ✔
   (b) ×10, then double
   (c) add 5 ten times
   (d) ×100, then halve
   **Answer: (a)** — ×100 then halve would be the cut for ×50. Item constructed for this lesson from its founding teaching. Dominica, *Mathematics: A Complete Course with CXC Questions, Volume One*, 2nd ed. (student book), Grade 10, Ch. 3 — Computation, printed p. 75.

3. (Sub lesson 2) 23 × 9 =
   (a) 197
   (b) 207 ✔
   (c) 217
   (d) 230
   **Answer: (b)** — 23 × 10 − 23 = 230 − 23. Item constructed for this lesson from its founding teaching. Dominica, *Mathematics: A Complete Course with CXC Questions, Volume One*, 2nd ed. (student book), Grade 10, Ch. 3 — Computation, printed p. 75.

4. (Sub lesson 2) 35 × 11 =
   (a) 335
   (b) 385 ✔
   (c) 355
   (d) 3,535
   **Answer: (b)** — split the figures and put their sum in the middle: 3, 3 + 5, 5. Item constructed for this lesson from its founding teaching. Dominica, *Mathematics: A Complete Course with CXC Questions, Volume One*, 2nd ed. (student book), Grade 10, Ch. 3 — Computation, printed p. 75.

5. (Sub lesson 3) By doubling and halving, 16 × 25 becomes:
   (a) 8 × 25
   (b) 32 × 25
   (c) 4 × 100 ✔
   (d) 16 × 100
   **Answer: (c)** — halve 16 twice (16 → 8 → 4) and double 25 twice (25 → 50 → 100); the product is 400 either way. Item constructed for this lesson from its founding teaching. Dominica, *Mathematics: A Complete Course with CXC Questions, Volume One*, 2nd ed. (student book), Grade 10, Ch. 3 — Computation, printed p. 75.

6. (Sub lesson 3) 98 + 47 is quickest worked as:
   (a) 98 + 50
   (b) 100 + 45 ✔
   (c) 90 + 47
   (d) 98 + 40
   **Answer: (b)** — move 2 across from the 47 to make 100; 100 + 45 = 145. Item constructed for this lesson from its founding teaching. Dominica, *Mathematics: A Complete Course with CXC Questions, Volume One*, 2nd ed. (student book), Grade 10, Ch. 3 — Computation, printed p. 75.
