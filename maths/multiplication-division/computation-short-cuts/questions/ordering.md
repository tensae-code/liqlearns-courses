# Ordering — Computation Short Cuts

Sub-lesson markers: every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to — Sub lesson 1 — Short Cuts with Tens and Hundreds · Sub lesson 2 — Near-Ten Short Cuts: 9, 99 and 11 · Sub lesson 3 — Breaking Apart, Doubling and Halving. This lesson was built new with its sub lessons (Dominica expansion merge, 2026-11-11), so its markers are original — there are no older depth layers to map from.

1. (Sub lesson 1) Put the steps for 26 × 50 in order:
   halve 2,600 / multiply 26 × 100 / read the answer 1,300 / choose the ×50 short cut
   **Answer: choose the ×50 short cut → multiply 26 × 100 → halve 2,600 → read the answer 1,300.** Item constructed for this lesson from its founding teaching. Dominica, *Mathematics: A Complete Course with CXC Questions, Volume One*, 2nd ed. (student book), Grade 10, Ch. 3 — Computation, printed p. 75.

2. (Sub lesson 2) Put the steps for 23 × 9 in order:
   subtract 23 / multiply 23 × 10 / read the answer 207 / write 230
   **Answer: multiply 23 × 10 → write 230 → subtract 23 → read the answer 207.** Item constructed for this lesson from its founding teaching. Dominica, *Mathematics: A Complete Course with CXC Questions, Volume One*, 2nd ed. (student book), Grade 10, Ch. 3 — Computation, printed p. 75.

3. (Sub lesson 3) Put the steps for 16 × 25 in order:
   halve and double again to reach 4 × 100 / start from 16 × 25 / read the answer 400 / halve 16 and double 25 to reach 8 × 50
   **Answer: start from 16 × 25 → halve 16 and double 25 to reach 8 × 50 → halve and double again to reach 4 × 100 → read the answer 400.** Item constructed for this lesson from its founding teaching. Dominica, *Mathematics: A Complete Course with CXC Questions, Volume One*, 2nd ed. (student book), Grade 10, Ch. 3 — Computation, printed p. 75.
