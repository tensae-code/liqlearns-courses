# Matching — Computation Short Cuts

Sub-lesson markers: every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to — Sub lesson 1 — Short Cuts with Tens and Hundreds · Sub lesson 2 — Near-Ten Short Cuts: 9, 99 and 11 · Sub lesson 3 — Breaking Apart, Doubling and Halving. This lesson was built new with its sub lessons (Dominica expansion merge, 2026-11-11), so its markers are original — there are no older depth layers to map from.

1. (Sub lesson 1) Match each calculation to its short cut:
   (a) 64 × 10
   (b) 64 × 5
   (c) 64 × 50
   (i) ×100, then halve
   (ii) shift every figure one place left
   (iii) ×10, then halve
   **Answer: (a)–(ii) = 640; (b)–(iii) = 320; (c)–(i) = 3,200.** Item constructed for this lesson from its founding teaching. Dominica, *Mathematics: A Complete Course with CXC Questions, Volume One*, 2nd ed. (student book), Grade 10, Ch. 3 — Computation, printed p. 75.

2. (Sub lesson 2) Match each multiplier to its short cut:
   (a) ×9
   (b) ×99
   (c) ×11
   (i) split the figures and put their sum in the middle
   (ii) ×100, then take the number away
   (iii) ×10, then take the number away
   **Answer: (a)–(iii); (b)–(ii); (c)–(i).** Item constructed for this lesson from its founding teaching. Dominica, *Mathematics: A Complete Course with CXC Questions, Volume One*, 2nd ed. (student book), Grade 10, Ch. 3 — Computation, printed p. 75.

3. (Sub lesson 3) Match each product to its doubled-and-halved twin:
   (a) 16 × 25
   (b) 28 × 25
   (c) 18 × 15
   (i) 9 × 30
   (ii) 4 × 100
   (iii) 7 × 100
   **Answer: (a)–(ii); (b)–(iii); (c)–(i).** Item constructed for this lesson from its founding teaching. Dominica, *Mathematics: A Complete Course with CXC Questions, Volume One*, 2nd ed. (student book), Grade 10, Ch. 3 — Computation, printed p. 75.
