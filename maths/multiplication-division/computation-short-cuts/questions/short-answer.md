1. (Sub lesson 1) Work out 3.7 × 100.

2. (Sub lesson 1) Use the ×5 short cut to work out 34 × 5, showing the two steps.
   **Answer: 34 × 10 = 340; half of 340 is 170.** Item constructed for this lesson from its founding teaching. Dominica, *Mathematics: A Complete Course with CXC Questions, Volume One*, 2nd ed. (student book), Grade 10, Ch. 3 — Computation, printed p. 75.

3. (Sub lesson 2) Use the ×99 short cut to work out 14 × 99.
   **Answer: 14 × 100 − 14 = 1,400 − 14 = 1,386.** Item constructed for this lesson from its founding teaching. Dominica, *Mathematics: A Complete Course with CXC Questions, Volume One*, 2nd ed. (student book), Grade 10, Ch. 3 — Computation, printed p. 75.

4. (Sub lesson 2) Work out 67 × 11, saying what happens at the middle figure.
   **Answer: 737** — the middle sum 6 + 7 = 13 passes 9, so write 3 and carry the 1 onto the 6. Item constructed for this lesson from its founding teaching. Dominica, *Mathematics: A Complete Course with CXC Questions, Volume One*, 2nd ed. (student book), Grade 10, Ch. 3 — Computation, printed p. 75.

5. (Sub lesson 3) Work out 28 × 25 by doubling and halving.
   **Answer: 28 × 25 → 14 × 50 → 7 × 100 = 700.** Item constructed for this lesson from its founding teaching. Dominica, *Mathematics: A Complete Course with CXC Questions, Volume One*, 2nd ed. (student book), Grade 10, Ch. 3 — Computation, printed p. 75.

6. (Sub lesson 3) Work out 104 − 99 by compensation.
   **Answer: 104 − 100 + 1 = 5.** Item constructed for this lesson from its founding teaching. Dominica, *Mathematics: A Complete Course with CXC Questions, Volume One*, 2nd ed. (student book), Grade 10, Ch. 3 — Computation, printed p. 75.
