A lesson that teaches rapid calculation strategies — the short
cuts that turn a slow written calculation into a few seconds of
mental work. Dominica's Grade 10 course book (Toolsie,
*Mathematics: A Complete Course with CXC Questions, Volume One*,
2nd ed.) teaches them as a named section of its Computation
chapter — "Short cuts in computation", printed p. 75. No lesson
in this course taught them before; the expansion read flagged
it NEW LESSON NEEDED and this lesson is built from that flag.
Prerequisite (soft hint only — every lesson stands alone):
multiplication and the-order-of-operations. A learner who opens
this lesson directly still gets the whole thing: every short
cut is met, shown on worked numbers and practised here.

## Sub lessons

1. Short Cuts with Tens and Hundreds
2. Near-Ten Short Cuts: 9, 99 and 11
3. Breaking Apart, Doubling and Halving
