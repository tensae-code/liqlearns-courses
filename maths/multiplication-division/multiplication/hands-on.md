# Hands-on — Multiplication

The lesson multiplied with groups, chants and squared paper.

## Equal groups
Make 3 groups of 4 stones. Count them two ways: 4 + 4 + 4 = 12, and 3 × 4 = 12. Make 5 groups of 3, and 4 groups of 6. The groups are the multiplication.

## Table chant
Chant the 2s, then the 3s, then the 4s, then the 5s — clapping on each multiple. Then the 6s, 7s, 8s and 9s, one table a day, the way Kazakhstan's Grade 3 book orders them.

## Column multiplication
Write 23 × 4 in columns on squared paper. Multiply the ones (3 × 4 = 12: write 2, carry 1 ten), then the tens (2 × 4 = 8 tens, plus the carried ten = 9 tens). Answer: 92. Try 132 × 3.

## Tens and hundreds
Multiply 30 × 4: 3 tens × 4 = 12 tens = 120. Multiply 300 × 4: 3 hundreds × 4 = 12 hundreds = 1,200. The zeros follow the place.
