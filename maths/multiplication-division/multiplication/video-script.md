# Video script — Multiplication

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Three plates! Four apples on each plate!
Four and four and four — twelve!
Three times four is TWELVE!

Two times table! 2, 4, 6, 8, 10!
Three times table! 3, 6, 9, 12, 15!
Five times table! 5, 10, 15, 20, 25!

Now columns! 23 × 4!
Ones first! 3 × 4 = 12 — write 2, carry 1!
Tens! 2 × 4 = 8, plus the carried ten — 9!
Ninety-two!

Now you — multiply the numbers I show you!
