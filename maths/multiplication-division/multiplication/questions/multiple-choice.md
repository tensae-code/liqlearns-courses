# Multiplication — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Level 1)** 3 × 4 means ______.
a) 3 + 4  b) 3 groups of 4  c) 3 take away 4
**Answer:** b) 3 groups of 4 — equal groups added together.

**2. (Level 1)** 3 × 4 = ______.
a) 7  b) 12  c) 34
**Answer:** b) 12 — 4 + 4 + 4.

**3. (Level 1)** 6 × 7 = ______.
a) 42  b) 13  c) 67
**Answer:** a) 42 — the 6s and 7s meet at 42.

**4. (Level 2)** 23 × 4 = ______.
a) 82  b) 92  c) 812
**Answer:** b) 92 — ones 3 × 4 = 12: write 2, carry 1; tens 2 × 4 + 1 = 9.

**5. (Level 2)** 41 × 6 = ______.
a) 236  b) 246  c) 256
**Answer:** b) 246 — ones 1 × 6 = 6; tens 4 × 6 = 24.

**6. (Level 3)** 132 × 3 = ______.
a) 396  b) 369  c) 936
**Answer:** a) 396 — ones 6, tens 9, hundreds 3.

**7. (Level 3)** 30 × 4 = ______.
a) 34  b) 120  c) 1,200
**Answer:** b) 120 — 3 tens × 4 = 12 tens.

**8. (Level 3)** 300 × 4 = ______.
a) 120  b) 1,200  c) 12,000
**Answer:** b) 1,200 — 3 hundreds × 4 = 12 hundreds.
