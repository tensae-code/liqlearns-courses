# Multiplication — matching

Match each item on the left to its partner on the right.

**1. (Level 1)** Match each multiplication to its answer: (a) 3 × 4 (b) 5 × 6 (c) 7 × 8 — 12, 30, 56
**Answer:** a) 12, b) 30, c) 56.

**2. (Level 2)** Match each column multiplication to its answer: (a) 23 × 4 (b) 41 × 6 (c) 35 × 7 — 92, 246, 245
**Answer:** a) 92, b) 246, c) 245.

**3. (Level 3)** Match each multiplication to its answer: (a) 132 × 3 (b) 30 × 4 (c) 300 × 4 — 396, 120, 1,200
**Answer:** a) 396, b) 120, c) 1,200.

**4. (Level 3)** Match each multiplication by tens to its answer: (a) 70 × 8 (b) 500 × 3 (c) 60 × 9 — 560, 1,500, 540
**Answer:** a) 560, b) 1,500, c) 540.
