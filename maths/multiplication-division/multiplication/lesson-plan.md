# Lesson plan — Multiplication

**Course:** Maths · **Unit:** Unit 3 — Multiplication and Division · **Lesson:** Multiplication
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 9, the first lesson of Unit 3 (maths/CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** multiplying — the times tables, multiplying in columns, and multiplying by tens and hundreds.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Addition (Level 7) — multiplication is repeated adding. It needs nothing else.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Addition (Stage 2) — multiplication is adding the same number again and again. Recommended, never forced.
- A soft hint only: the tables are learned by heart a little at a time — 2s, 3s, 4s, 5s first, the way Kazakhstan's Grade 2 book orders them.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say what multiplication is: equal groups added together (3 groups of 4 is 3 × 4 = 12).
2. Know the times tables for 2, 3, 4 and 5, and for 6, 7, 8 and 9.
3. Multiply a two-digit or three-digit number by a one-digit number in columns (23 × 4 = 92; 132 × 3 = 396).
4. Multiply by tens and hundreds (30 × 4 = 120; 300 × 4 = 1,200).

## What you need
Counters or stones; a multiplication table grid.
Paper and a pencil.

## If the learner is stuck
- Counts 3 × 4 as 3 + 4 → multiplication is equal groups: 3 groups of 4, not 3 and 4. Count the groups: 4, 8, 12.
- Forgets the carried ten in 23 × 4 → multiply the ones first (3 × 4 = 12: write 2, carry 1 ten), then the tens (2 × 4 = 8 tens, plus the carried ten = 9 tens).
- Mixes up 6 × 7 and 7 × 6 → they are the same: multiplication works in either order. 6 × 7 = 7 × 6 = 42.
- This lesson's next door: Division (same stage) — sharing undoes multiplying, and the tables check every division.
