**Acceptance count: taught in 19 countries (Kazakhstan, Egypt, Greece, Libya, Jordan, Armenia, Vietnam, Laos, Yemen, Indonesia, Nicaragua, Ethiopia, South Sudan, Iraq, Bangladesh, Nepal, Cambodia, Kuwait, North Macedonia).**. Kazakhstan carries the tables in order — 2 to 5 in Grade 2, 6 to 9 in Grade 3 — and multiplication by 10 and 100. Egypt carries multiplication from arrays as repeated addition in Grade 2 Term 2 (old Discover series — rows and columns counted, no × sign yet), through vertical multiplication, 2-digit and 3-digit by 1-digit, with multiplying by tens and hundreds, in Grade 3. Greece carries the tables from Grade 2 and multiplication with division in Grade 3. Libya carries column multiplication with regrouping to hundreds in Grade 3. Jordan carries the tables for 2 to 5 in Grade 3. Armenia carries finding the unknown factor in Grade 4. Vietnam carries multiplication within 1000 in Grade 3. Other read countries teach multiplying inside their own Grade 2–4 books and join by merge.** Laos carries multiplication as repeated addition with tables ×2–×9 in the new Grade 2 book (to ×10 in the old guide) and multiplication with carrying, including by 10 and 100, in Grade 3. Yemen carries multiplication from repeated addition and the ×2, ×5, ×3, ×4 facts in Grade 2, through the ×6–×9 facts and multiplying by 10, 100 and 1000, to three-digit by three-digit multiplication with estimating by rounding. Indonesia carries multiplication from repeated addition to 100 in Grade 3 and column multiplication to 100 in Grade 4, through fraction multiplication by natural numbers in Grade 6, to integer, fraction and decimal multiplication in Grade 7 (Merdeka and K13 Semester 1). Grades 1–2 teach no multiplication, as the filing states. Nicaragua carries multiplication from the tables in Grade 2 (2, 5, 3, 4, 6, 7, 8, 9 in the old book; 5, 2, 3, 4, then 6, 7, 8, 9 in the 2025 book), through vertical multiplication in Grade 3 and 12 × 23 with the named properties in Grade 4, to decimal and fraction multiplication in Grades 5–6 and signed-number multiplication in Grade 7. One book error is never taught as fact: the old Grade 3 teacher guide's multiplication-tables page misprints '1 x 10 = 80' — the true value is 8 x 10 = 80. Ethiopia carries multiplication from the tables ×2, ×10, ×5, ×0/×1, ×3, ×4, ×6, ×7 and ×8 in Grade 2 — there is no ×9 section, ×9 appearing only in the full 0–10 table — through multiplying to 1,000 in Grade 3 and to 10,000, to multiplying to 1,000,000 with 2-digit and larger multipliers in Grade 4. South Sudan carries multiplication only in part — multiplication as repeated addition and groups with the 1–10 table and word problems in Grade 2, and multiplication of fractions in Secondary 1. Missing: column and multi-digit multiplication; no 2-digit × 1-digit is taught anywhere in the set. Iraq carries multiplication from repeated addition to 5×5 in Grade 2, through ×0–×10 and 2-digit × 1-digit without carrying in Grade 3, to 2-digit × 2-digit in Grade 4, 3-digit × 2-digit in Grade 5, fraction and decimal multiplication in Grade 6, and multiplying algebraic terms and expressions in Grades 7–8. Bangladesh carries multiplication from the tables of 1–10 with commutativity in Class 2, through tables of 13, 14 and 15 with 2-digit × 2-digit and 3-digit × 2-digit in Class 3, to 3-digit × 3-digit in Class 4, short methods in Class 5, multiplication on the number line in Class 6, and multiplication of exponents in Class 7. Two book errors are never taught as fact: the Class 3 tables print 6 × 9 as 55 (correct 54, printed p. 39) and 2 × 9 as 19 (correct 18, printed p. 45) — teach the correct values. Nepal carries multiplication from repeated addition with the ×2–×5 tables in Grade 1 (teach the p. 82 butterflies corrected: 4 × 4 = 16), through the ×6–×10 tables in Grade 2 (both books) and ×1–×12 with 2-digit × 2-digit and 3-digit × 2-digit in Grade 3, to 3–4 digit by up to 3-digit multiplication in Grade 4, and multiplication of fractions and decimals in Grades 5–6. Cambodia carries multiplication whole from repeated addition in Grade 2 through decimals, fractions and rationals in Grades 5–8. Kuwait carries multiplication from the concept itself in Grade 2 (repeated addition, arrays, ×2–×5), through the full ×1–×9 tables and 1-digit × 2-/3-digit work in Grade 3 and 2-digit × 2-or-more-digit work in Grade 4, to multiplying by one- and two-digit numbers in Grade 5. North Macedonia touches multiplication only at its very start — doubling to 10 at Grade 1 (2022), as readiness for multiplying by 2.
