**Acceptance count: taught in 10 countries (Kazakhstan, Egypt, Greece, Libya, Jordan, Armenia, Vietnam, Laos, Yemen, Indonesia). Kazakhstan carries the tables in order — 2 to 5 in Grade 2, 6 to 9 in Grade 3 — and multiplication by 10 and 100. Egypt carries vertical multiplication, 2-digit and 3-digit by 1-digit, with multiplying by tens and hundreds, in Grade 3. Greece carries the tables from Grade 2 and multiplication with division in Grade 3. Libya carries column multiplication with regrouping to hundreds in Grade 3. Jordan carries the tables for 2 to 5 in Grade 3. Armenia carries finding the unknown factor in Grade 4. Vietnam carries multiplication within 1000 in Grade 3. Other read countries teach multiplying inside their own Grade 2–4 books and join by merge.** Laos carries multiplication as repeated addition with tables ×2–×9 in the new Grade 2 book (to ×10 in the old guide) and multiplication with carrying, including by 10 and 100, in Grade 3. Yemen carries multiplication from repeated addition and the ×2, ×5, ×3, ×4 facts in Grade 2, through the ×6–×9 facts and multiplying by 10, 100 and 1000, to three-digit by three-digit multiplication with estimating by rounding. Indonesia carries multiplication from repeated addition to 100 in Grade 3 and column multiplication to 100 in Grade 4, through fraction multiplication by natural numbers in Grade 6, to integer, fraction and decimal multiplication in Grade 7 (Merdeka and K13 Semester 1). Grades 1–2 teach no multiplication, as the filing states.
