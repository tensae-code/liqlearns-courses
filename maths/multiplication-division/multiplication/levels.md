# Multiplication — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB
LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure — README, "Units, Lessons and Sub
Lessons"). Practice is tied to the skill number, as before. The
questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson
view. The sub lessons are the depths inside this one thing —
multiplying — equal groups and the first tables first, then the
tables to nine and columns, then three digits and tens..

Mapping from the old depth layers: old Level 1 = Sub lesson 1; old Level 2 = Sub lesson 2; old Level 3 = Sub lesson 3. Sub lesson 4 was added 2026-11-08 under the whole-arc rule, from the lesson's own stocked sources (partial products for 2-digit multipliers; multiplying to 10,000, to 1,000,000 and beyond — stocked but capped out of the old text at three digits × one digit). The books state no separate pass mark for the big-number band, so Sub lesson 4 keeps the same observable form — the learner can work the band and check it by dividing — rather than an invented mark.

## Sub lesson 1 — Multiplication Equal Groups and the First Tables
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: explain multiplication as equal groups, and know the tables for 2, 3, 4 and 5.

## Sub lesson 2 — Multiplication The Tables to Nine and Columns
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: know the tables for 6, 7, 8 and 9, and multiply two-digit numbers by one digit in columns.

## Sub lesson 3 — Multiplication Three Digits, Tens and Hundreds
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: multiply three-digit numbers by one digit in columns, and multiply by tens and hundreds.

## Sub lesson 4 — Big Multiplication: Partial Products, To a Million and Beyond
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: multiply
by a 2-digit multiplier in partial products, multiply numbers
to 10,000 in columns, and carry a product past 1,000,000,
checking by dividing.
