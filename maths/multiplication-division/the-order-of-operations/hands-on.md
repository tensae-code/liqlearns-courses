# Hands-on — The-order-of-operations

The lesson ordered operations with boxes, cards and a race.

## Two ways
Work out 2 + 3 × 4 two ways: left to right gives 20; times first gives 14. Only one is the books' answer: 14. The order is the lesson.

## Brackets box
Write (2 + 3) × 4. Draw a box around 2 + 3 — the brackets are the box. Do the box first (5), then 5 × 4 = 20. Try (10 − 4) ÷ 2 = 3 and 10 − 4 ÷ 2 = 8 — the brackets change the answer.

## Operation cards
Number cards and operation cards on the table. Your partner builds an expression: 6 + 2 × 5. Work it out aloud: "2 × 5 = 10 first, then 6 + 10 = 16." Ten rounds, then swap.

## Left-to-right race
Two learners, one expression: 12 ÷ 3 × 2. Race to the answer — but the one who works left to right wins: 4, then 8. The one who works right to left gets 2, and loses.
