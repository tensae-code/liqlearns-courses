# Lesson plan — The-order-of-operations

**Course:** Maths · **Stage:** Stage 3 — Multiplication and Division · **Lesson:** The-order-of-operations
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 11, the third lesson of Stage 3 — the last lesson of the stage (maths/CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the order of operations — brackets first, then multiply and divide, then add and subtract, left to right.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Addition (Level 7), Subtraction (Level 8), Multiplication (Level 9) and Division (Level 10) — the learner can do all four operations. It needs nothing else.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: all four operations (Stages 2–3) — this lesson only orders them, it does not teach them. Recommended, never forced.
- A soft hint only: South Africa calls the rule BODMAS; the books here say the same order in words.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Work out an expression with no brackets: multiply and divide before adding and subtracting (2 + 3 × 4 = 14).
2. Work out an expression with brackets: brackets first, then the same order inside and outside ((2 + 3) × 4 = 20).
3. Work left to right when two operations are on the same level (12 ÷ 3 × 2 = 8).
4. Check a multi-step answer by working it in the book's order and saying each step aloud.

## What you need
Number cards and operation cards (+ − × ÷ and brackets).
Paper and a pencil.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — the learner works out 2 + 3 × 4 two ways and sees the answers differ (lesson/lesson.md).
2. **The explanation** — multiply and divide first; brackets first; left to right; the full order (lesson/lesson.md).
   - ADD IMAGE at the brackets part: a picture of (2 + 3) × 4 with the brackets drawn as a box around 2 + 3 → the student points to the box first and says "brackets first: 5 — then 5 × 4 = 20".
   - ADD GIF in the left-to-right part: a picture of 12 ÷ 3 × 2 with a finger moving left to right, each step lighting up → the student says each step as it lights: "12 ÷ 3 = 4, then 4 × 2 = 8".
3. **Say it to your companion** — work out expressions your companion builds with operation cards (lesson/lesson.md).
   - ADD VIDEO: the script in video-script.md, filmed — the order chant in the super-simple voice (made later, when videos are made).
4. **Questions** — from questions/: multiple-choice, short-answer, fill-in-the-blank, true-or-false, ordering, matching — served at the learner's level and teaching-mix (levels.md).
5. **Hands-on** — hands-on.md.
6. **Do It Yourself** — work out five expressions without brackets, five with brackets, and three left-to-right chains (lesson/lesson.md).

## If the learner is stuck
- Works 2 + 3 × 4 left to right and gets 20 → multiply and divide come before add and subtract: 3 × 4 = 12 first, then 2 + 12 = 14. Kazakhstan's Grade 2 book teaches it with brackets first, then the same order inside.
- Forgets the brackets in (2 + 3) × 4 → brackets are a box: do what is inside the box first, then the rest. (2 + 3) × 4 = 5 × 4 = 20.
- Works 12 ÷ 3 × 2 right to left and gets 2 → same-level operations go left to right: 12 ÷ 3 = 4, then 4 × 2 = 8.
- This lesson's next door: Fractions (Stage 4) — the next stage starts with parts of a whole, and the order of operations follows every fraction sum.
