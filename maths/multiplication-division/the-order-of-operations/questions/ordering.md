# The-order-of-operations — ordering

Put the items in the order asked. Check each step before you write the answer.

**1. (Level 1)** Put these steps in the order the books teach: add and subtract; brackets; multiply and divide.
Answer in order.
**Answer:** Brackets; multiply and divide; add and subtract — the full order.

**2. (Level 2)** Put these steps for (6 + 2) × 3 − 10 ÷ 2 in order: 24 − 5; 8 × 3; 6 + 2; 10 ÷ 2.
Answer in order.
**Answer:** 6 + 2; 8 × 3; 10 ÷ 2; 24 − 5 — brackets, then multiply and divide, then subtract. (Answer: 19.)

**3. (Level 3)** Put these answers in order, smallest first: 2 + 3 × 4; (2 + 3) × 4; 12 ÷ 3 × 2.
Answer the values in order.
**Answer:** 8, 14, 20 — 12 ÷ 3 × 2 = 8; 2 + 3 × 4 = 14; (2 + 3) × 4 = 20.
