# The-order-of-operations — fill-in-the-blank

Fill in the missing number or word.

**1. (Level 1)** 2 + 3 × 4 = ___.
**Answer:** 14

**2. (Level 1)** Multiply and divide come ___ add and subtract.
**Answer:** before

**3. (Level 2)** (2 + 3) × 4 = ___.
**Answer:** 20

**4. (Level 2)** Brackets come ___.
**Answer:** first

**5. (Level 3)** 12 ÷ 3 × 2 = ___.
**Answer:** 8

**6. (Level 3)** Same-level operations go from ___ to ___.
