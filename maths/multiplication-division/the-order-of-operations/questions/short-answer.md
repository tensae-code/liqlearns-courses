# The-order-of-operations — short answer

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Level 1)** Work out: 5 × 3 + 7.
**Answer:** 22 — 5 × 3 = 15 first, then 15 + 7.

**2. (Level 1)** Work out: 20 − 4 × 3.
**Answer:** 8 — 4 × 3 = 12 first, then 20 − 12.

**3. (Level 2)** Work out: (10 − 4) ÷ 2.
**Answer:** 3 — brackets first: 6, then 6 ÷ 2.

**4. (Level 2)** Work out: 18 ÷ (3 + 3).
**Answer:** 3 — brackets first: 6, then 18 ÷ 6.

**5. (Level 3)** Work out: 100 ÷ 10 ÷ 2.
**Answer:** 5 — left to right: 10, then 5.

**6. (Level 3)** Work out: 4 + 12 ÷ (2 + 2).
**Answer:** 7 — brackets: 4; 12 ÷ 4 = 3; 4 + 3 = 7.
