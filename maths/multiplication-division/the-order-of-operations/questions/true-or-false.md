# The-order-of-operations — true or false

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Order of Operations Multiply and Divide First · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Order of Operations Brackets First · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Order of Operations Left to Right and the Full Order.

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** 2 + 3 × 4 = 20.
**Answer:** False — multiply first: 3 × 4 = 12, then 2 + 12 = 14.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** 10 − 6 ÷ 2 = 7.
**Answer:** True — divide first: 6 ÷ 2 = 3, then 10 − 3.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** (2 + 3) × 4 = 20.
**Answer:** True — brackets first: 5, then 5 × 4.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** 10 − 4 ÷ 2 = 3.
**Answer:** False — divide first: 4 ÷ 2 = 2, then 10 − 2 = 8. (10 − 4) ÷ 2 = 3 is the bracketed one.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** 12 ÷ 3 × 2 = 2.
**Answer:** False — left to right: 12 ÷ 3 = 4, then 4 × 2 = 8.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** BODMAS means brackets first.
**Answer:** True — South Africa's Grade 6 book spells it out: Brackets, Order, Division and Multiplication, Addition and Subtraction.
