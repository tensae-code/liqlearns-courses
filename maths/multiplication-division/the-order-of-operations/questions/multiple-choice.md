# The-order-of-operations — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Order of Operations Multiply and Divide First · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Order of Operations Brackets First · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Order of Operations Left to Right and the Full Order.

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** 2 + 3 × 4 = ______.
a) 14  b) 20  c) 9
**Answer:** a) 14 — multiply first: 3 × 4 = 12, then 2 + 12.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** 10 − 6 ÷ 2 = ______.
a) 2  b) 7  c) 8
**Answer:** b) 7 — divide first: 6 ÷ 2 = 3, then 10 − 3.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** 8 + 12 ÷ 4 = ______.
a) 5  b) 11  c) 20
**Answer:** b) 11 — divide first: 12 ÷ 4 = 3, then 8 + 3.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** (2 + 3) × 4 = ______.
a) 14  b) 20  c) 11
**Answer:** b) 20 — brackets first: 2 + 3 = 5, then 5 × 4.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** 10 − (4 + 2) = ______.
a) 4  b) 8  c) 12
**Answer:** a) 4 — brackets first: 4 + 2 = 6, then 10 − 6.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** 12 ÷ 3 × 2 = ______.
a) 2  b) 8  c) 18
**Answer:** b) 8 — left to right: 12 ÷ 3 = 4, then 4 × 2.

**7. (Sub lesson 3)** 20 − 8 + 3 = ______.
a) 9  b) 15  c) 12
**Answer:** b) 15 — left to right: 20 − 8 = 12, then 12 + 3.

**8. (Sub lesson 3)** (6 + 2) × 3 − 10 ÷ 2 = ______.
a) 19  b) 14  c) 24
**Answer:** a) 19 — brackets: 8; 8 × 3 = 24; 10 ÷ 2 = 5; 24 − 5 = 19.
