Match each item on the left to its partner on the right.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Match each expression to its answer: (a) 2 + 3 × 4 (b) 10 − 6 ÷ 2 (c) 8 + 12 ÷ 4 — 14, 7, 11
**Answer:** a) 14, b) 7, c) 11.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Match each expression to its answer: (a) (2 + 3) × 4 (b) (10 − 4) ÷ 2 (c) 10 − (4 + 2) — 20, 3, 4
**Answer:** a) 20, b) 3, c) 4.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Match each chain to its answer: (a) 12 ÷ 3 × 2 (b) 20 − 8 + 3 (c) 100 ÷ 10 ÷ 2 — 8, 15, 5
**Answer:** a) 8, b) 15, c) 5.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Match each expression to its first step: (a) 2 + 3 × 4 (b) (2 + 3) × 4 (c) 12 ÷ 3 × 2 — 3 × 4, 2 + 3, 12 ÷ 3
**Answer:** a) 3 × 4, b) 2 + 3, c) 12 ÷ 3.
