# Video script — The-order-of-operations

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Two plus three times four!
Left to right? Twenty! WRONG!
Times first! Three times four is twelve!
Two plus twelve — FOURTEEN!

Brackets! (Two plus three) times four!
Brackets FIRST! Two plus three is five!
Five times four — TWENTY!

Twelve divided by three times two!
Left to right! Twelve divided by three is four!
Four times two — EIGHT!

Brackets! Times and divide! Add and take away!
Left to right!
Now you — work out the one I show you, step by step!
