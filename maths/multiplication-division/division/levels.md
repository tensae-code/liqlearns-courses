# Division — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB
LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure — README, "Units, Lessons and Sub
Lessons"). Practice is tied to the skill number, as before. The
questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson
view. The sub lessons are the depths inside this one thing — dividing —
sharing and the tables first, then remainders, then columns with
checking..

Mapping from the old depth layers: old Level 1 = Sub lesson 1; old Level 2 = Sub lesson 2; old Level 3 = Sub lesson 3. Sub lesson 4 was added 2026-11-08 under the whole-arc rule, from the lesson's own stocked sources (long division to 1,000,000 and beyond, by 2- and 3-digit divisors — stocked but capped out of the old text at introductory columns). The books state no separate pass mark for the big-number band, so Sub lesson 4 keeps the same observable form — the learner can work the band and check it by multiplying back — rather than an invented mark.

## Sub lesson 1 — Division Sharing and the Tables
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: share a number into equal parts, and divide using the times tables.

## Sub lesson 2 — Division Remainders
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: divide with a remainder, say what the remainder means, and know the remainder must be smaller than the divisor.

## Sub lesson 3 — Division In Columns and Checking
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: divide two- and three-digit numbers by one digit in columns, and check every division by multiplying.

## Sub lesson 4 — Long Division: Big Numbers, Two- and Three-Digit Divisors
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: divide
numbers to 1,000,000 and beyond in the long-division layout,
by 1-digit, 2-digit and 3-digit divisors, and check by
quotient × divisor (+ remainder) = dividend.
