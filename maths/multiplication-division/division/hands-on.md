# Hands-on — Division

The lesson divided by dealing stones and by the long-division chant.

## Deal it out
12 stones, 3 friends. Deal the stones one at a time into 3 piles — each pile gets 4. That is division: 12 ÷ 3 = 4. Try 20 stones among 4 friends, and 18 among 3.

## Remainder stones
17 stones, 5 piles. Deal them out: 3 in each pile, 2 left over. Say it: "17 ÷ 5 = 3 remainder 2." The leftover is the remainder — and it is smaller than 5, the divisor.

## Tables backwards
Your partner calls a division from the tables: "56 ÷ 7." Ask the table: "7 times what is 56?" Answer: 8. Ten rounds, then swap.

## Long division
Write 96 ÷ 4 in columns. Divide: 9 ÷ 4 = 2, remainder 1. Multiply: 2 × 4 = 8. Subtract: 9 − 8 = 1. Bring down the 6: 16 ÷ 4 = 4. Answer: 24. Check: 24 × 4 = 96.
