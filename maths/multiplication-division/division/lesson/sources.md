**Acceptance count: taught in 9 countries (Egypt, Jordan, Vietnam, Kazakhstan, Armenia, Greece, Laos, Yemen, Indonesia). Egypt carries division whole — sharing equally, remainders as a named topic, and long division layout. Jordan carries division with remainder in Grade 3 and long division with zeros in the quotient in Grade 4. Vietnam carries exact division and division with remainder in Grade 3. Kazakhstan carries division as equal sharing in Grade 2 and non-table division with remainders in Grade 3. Armenia carries unknown divisors and dividends in Grade 4 and division with remainder in Grade 5. Greece carries sharing and the tables only — it never teaches formal long division, stated honestly here. Other read countries teach dividing inside their own Grade 2–4 books and join by merge.** Laos carries division from sharing equally in the old Grade 2 guide, through the Grade 3 long-division model and Grade 4 division with remainder, to decimal division in Grade 5 — with the Grade 3 book's misprinted divisor (3 for 22) corrected to 462 ÷ 22 = 21. Yemen carries division from sharing and grouping with ÷2, ÷5, ÷3 and ÷4 in Grade 2, through the ÷6–÷9 facts and the long-division bracket, to six-digit division by two-digit divisors with estimating by rounding. Indonesia carries division from repeated subtraction and sharing to 100 in Grade 3 and column division to 100 in Grade 4, through fraction division by and of natural numbers in Grade 6, to integer, fraction and decimal division in Grade 7 (Merdeka and K13 Semester 1). Grades 1–2 teach no division, as the filing states.
