# Division — true or false

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Level 1)** 12 ÷ 3 = 4.
**Answer:** True — deal 12 into 3 equal piles: 4 each.

**2. (Level 1)** 20 ÷ 4 = 4.
**Answer:** False — 20 ÷ 4 = 5; four fives are twenty.

**3. (Level 2)** 17 ÷ 5 = 2 remainder 7.
**Answer:** False — the remainder 7 is bigger than the divisor 5; one more share fits: 3 remainder 2.

**4. (Level 2)** 23 ÷ 4 = 5 remainder 3.
**Answer:** True — four fives are 20, and 3 are left over.

**5. (Level 3)** 24 × 4 = 96 checks 96 ÷ 4 = 24.
**Answer:** True — multiply the answer back.

**6. (Level 3)** 132 ÷ 3 = 44.
**Answer:** True — 13 ÷ 3 = 4 remainder 1, bring down 2: 12 ÷ 3 = 4.
