# Division — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** 12 ÷ 3 = ______.
a) 3  b) 4  c) 6
**Answer:** b) 4 — deal 12 stones into 3 equal piles: 4 each.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** 20 ÷ 4 = ______.
a) 4  b) 5  c) 6
**Answer:** b) 5 — four fives are twenty.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** 56 ÷ 7 = ______.
a) 7  b) 8  c) 9
**Answer:** b) 8 — seven eights are fifty-six.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** 17 ÷ 5 = ______.
a) 3 remainder 2  b) 2 remainder 7  c) 3 remainder 5
**Answer:** a) 3 remainder 2 — five threes are 15, and 2 are left over.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** 23 ÷ 4 = ______.
a) 5 remainder 3  b) 4 remainder 7  c) 6 remainder 1
**Answer:** a) 5 remainder 3 — four fives are 20, and 3 are left over.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** 96 ÷ 4 = ______.
a) 14  b) 24  c) 26
**Answer:** b) 24 — 9 ÷ 4 = 2 remainder 1, bring down 6: 16 ÷ 4 = 4.

**7. (Sub lesson 3)** 96 ÷ 6 = ______.
a) 14  b) 16  c) 19
**Answer:** b) 16 — 9 ÷ 6 = 1 remainder 3, bring down 6: 36 ÷ 6 = 6.

**8. (Sub lesson 3)** The check for 96 ÷ 4 = 24 is ______.
a) 96 − 24  b) 24 × 4  c) 96 + 4
**Answer:** b) 24 × 4 — multiply the answer back: 96.

**9. (Sub lesson 4)** 564,923 ÷ 8 = ______.
a) 70,615 remainder 3  b) 70,615 remainder 5  c) 76,015 remainder 3
**Answer:** a) 70,615 remainder 3 — Ethiopia's Grade 4 worked division, to 1,000,000.

**10. (Sub lesson 4)** Nepal's check for a division with a remainder is ______.
a) quotient + divisor = dividend  b) quotient × divisor + remainder = dividend  c) dividend × quotient = divisor
**Answer:** b) quotient × divisor + remainder = dividend.
