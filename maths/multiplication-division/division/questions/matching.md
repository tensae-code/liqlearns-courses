# Division — matching

Match each item on the left to its partner on the right.

**1. (Level 1)** Match each division to its answer: (a) 12 ÷ 3 (b) 20 ÷ 4 (c) 18 ÷ 3 — 4, 5, 6
**Answer:** a) 4, b) 5, c) 6.

**2. (Level 2)** Match each division to its answer: (a) 17 ÷ 5 (b) 23 ÷ 4 (c) 31 ÷ 6 — 3 remainder 2, 5 remainder 3, 5 remainder 1
**Answer:** a) 3 remainder 2, b) 5 remainder 3, c) 5 remainder 1.

**3. (Level 3)** Match each column division to its answer: (a) 96 ÷ 4 (b) 84 ÷ 4 (c) 96 ÷ 6 — 24, 21, 16
**Answer:** a) 24, b) 21, c) 16.

**4. (Level 3)** Match each division to its check: (a) 96 ÷ 4 = 24 (b) 17 ÷ 5 = 3 remainder 2 — 24 × 4 = 96, 3 × 5 + 2 = 17
**Answer:** a) 24 × 4 = 96, b) 3 × 5 + 2 = 17.
