Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Share equally: 18 ÷ 3.
**Answer:** 6 — three sixes are eighteen.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Divide from the tables: 81 ÷ 9.
**Answer:** 9 — nine nines are eighty-one.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Divide with a remainder: 31 ÷ 6.
**Answer:** 5 remainder 1 — six fives are 30, and 1 is left over.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Divide with a remainder: 29 ÷ 3.
**Answer:** 9 remainder 2 — three nines are 27, and 2 are left over.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Divide in columns: 84 ÷ 4.
**Answer:** 21 — 8 ÷ 4 = 2, 4 ÷ 4 = 1.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** Divide in columns: 132 ÷ 3.
**Answer:** 44 — 13 ÷ 3 = 4 remainder 1, bring down 2: 12 ÷ 3 = 4.
