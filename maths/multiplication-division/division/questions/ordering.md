**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Put these quotients in order, smallest first: 12 ÷ 3; 20 ÷ 4; 18 ÷ 3.
Answer the quotients in order.
**Answer:** 4, 5, 6 — the quotients in order.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Put these long-division steps in order: subtract; bring down; divide; multiply.
Answer in order.
**Answer:** Divide; multiply; subtract; bring down — the chant, in order.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Put these quotients in order, smallest first: 96 ÷ 6; 84 ÷ 4; 96 ÷ 4.
Answer the quotients in order.
**Answer:** 16, 21, 24 — the quotients in order.

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Put steps from 564,923 ÷ 8 in the order they run: (a) bring down the 9: 49 ÷ 8 = 6, remainder 1 (b) 56 ÷ 8 = 7, remainder 0 (c) finish: 43 ÷ 8 = 5, remainder 3 — quotient 70,615 r3.
**Answer:** b, a, c — the chant runs digit by digit from the front of the dividend.
