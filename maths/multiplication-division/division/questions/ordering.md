# Division — ordering

Put the items in the order asked. Check each step before you write the answer.

**1. (Level 1)** Put these quotients in order, smallest first: 12 ÷ 3; 20 ÷ 4; 18 ÷ 3.
Answer the quotients in order.
**Answer:** 4, 5, 6 — the quotients in order.

**2. (Level 2)** Put these long-division steps in order: subtract; bring down; divide; multiply.
Answer in order.
**Answer:** Divide; multiply; subtract; bring down — the chant, in order.

**3. (Level 3)** Put these quotients in order, smallest first: 96 ÷ 6; 84 ÷ 4; 96 ÷ 4.
Answer the quotients in order.
**Answer:** 16, 21, 24 — the quotients in order.
