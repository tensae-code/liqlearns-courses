# Numerals of the World — ordering

Put the items in the order asked. Check each step before you write the answer.

**1. (Level 1)** Put these Roman numerals in counting order, smallest first: XX, IX, XL, IV.
**Answer:** IV, IX, XX, XL — 4, 9, 20, 40.

**2. (Level 2)** Put these in counting order, smallest first: १० (Devanagari 10), VIII, 15, XII.
**Answer:** VIII, १०, XII, 15 — 8, 10, 12, 15; the scripts differ, the counts order the same.

**3. (Level 3)** Put the South Asian ladder in order, smallest first: crore, thousand, lakh, ayut.
**Answer:** thousand, ayut, lakh, crore — 1,000; 10,000; 100,000; 10,000,000 (Bangladesh, Classes 3–4).
