Fill in the missing number or word.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Roman sign for 500 is ___.
**Answer:** D — the seventh sign in Laos's list I, V, X, L, C, D, M.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** VI = ___, because the smaller sign comes after the bigger one and adds.
**Answer:** 6 — South Sudan's pair to remember: VI = 6, IX = 9.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Cambodia's Grade 1 children learn to write Khmer numerals with their ___ order.
**Answer:** stroke — L3–L4, pp.6–15.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Jordan's Grade 3 book teaches the ___/Indian numerals as a lesson in its numbers unit.
**Answer:** Hindi — Grade 3, Term 1, Unit 1 L7, p.20.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** A crore is ___ lakhs.
**Answer:** 100 — 10,000,000 ÷ 100,000 = 100 (Bangladesh, Class 4 ladder).
