# Numerals of the World — matching

Match each item on the left to its partner on the right.

**1. (Level 1)** Match each Roman sign to its value: (a) L (b) C (c) M — 100, 1000, 50
**Answer:** a) 50, b) 100, c) 1000 — Laos, Grade 6, L1, pp.1–9.

**2. (Level 2)** Match each system to its country book: (a) Ge'ez numerals to 100 (b) Lao digits ໐–໙ (c) Khmer numerals with stroke order — Cambodia Grade 1, Ethiopia Grade 4, Laos Grade 1
**Answer:** a) Ethiopia Grade 4, b) Laos Grade 1, c) Cambodia Grade 1.

**3. (Level 3)** Match each South Asian name to its value: (a) ayut (b) lakh (c) crore — 100,000, 10,000,000, 10 thousand
**Answer:** a) 10 thousand, b) 100,000, c) 10,000,000 — Bangladesh, Classes 3–4.
