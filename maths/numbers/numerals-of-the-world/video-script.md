# Video script — Numerals of the World

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

One number — **many writings**!

In Rome they wrote: **I, V, X**.
One, five, ten!
Four is one **before** five: **IV**.
Nine is one **before** ten: **IX**.

In Ethiopia, Ge'ez signs count to one hundred.
In Nepal, the digits look like this: **१, २, ३**.
In Laos: **໐ to ໙**. In Cambodia, Khmer signs with their own strokes.

And a big number?
One hundred thousand is **one lakh**!
Ten million is **one crore**!

Same numbers. Different clothes.
Now you — write your age the Roman way!
