# Video script — Counting

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Count with me!

**One** stone. **Two** stones. **Three**!
Four, five, six, seven, eight, nine, **ten**!
Ten fingers — count them!

Now past ten: eleven, twelve,
thirteen, fourteen, fifteen,
sixteen, seventeen, eighteen, nineteen, **twenty**!

And JUMP by tens: ten, twenty, thirty,
forty, fifty, sixty,
seventy, eighty, ninety — **ONE HUNDRED**!

Now you. Count the chairs. Count the spoons.
How many are there? Say it whole!
That is counting.
