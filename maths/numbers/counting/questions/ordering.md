# Counting — ordering

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure; extended 2026-11-08 under the whole-arc rule): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. Map: Sub lesson 1 — Counting 1 to 10 · Sub lesson 2 — Counting 11 to 20 · Sub lesson 3 — Counting by Tens to 100 · Sub lesson 4 — Counting in Hundreds to 1,000 · Sub lesson 5 — Counting in Thousands to 10,000 · Sub lesson 6 — Counting Big Numbers to 1,000,000 and Beyond. The old Sub lesson 4 ("Counting Past 100") folds into the new Sub lesson 4: its item about the ones filling in past 100 opens the hundreds climb and keeps its Sub lesson 4 marker.

Put the items in the order asked. Check each step before you write the answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Put these in counting order: 17, 12, 20, 14.
Answer in order, smallest first.
**Answer:** 12, 14, 17, 20 — checked one by one against the 11–20 line.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Put these tens in order: 70, 30, 90, 50.
Answer in order, smallest first.
**Answer:** 30, 50, 70, 90 — the tens line jumps by ten.

**3. (Sub lesson 4)** Put these counts in order: 101, 99, 100, 98.
Answer in order, smallest first.
**Answer:** 98, 99, 100, 101 — the ones fill in between the tens past 100.

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Put these hundreds in order: 500, 200, 900, 400.
Answer in order, smallest first.
**Answer:** 200, 400, 500, 900 — the hundreds line jumps by a hundred.

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** Put these counts in order: 7,000, 2,000, 9,000, 5,000.
Answer in order, smallest first.
**Answer:** 2,000, 5,000, 7,000, 9,000 — checked against the thousands line.

**6. (Sub lesson 6)** Put these counts in order: 100,000, 9,000, 1,000,000, 90,000.
Answer in order, smallest first.
**Answer:** 9,000, 90,000, 100,000, 1,000,000 — read each number in its groups: thousands, then ten-thousands, then a hundred thousand, then a million.
