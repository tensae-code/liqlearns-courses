**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Match each count to its figure: (a) three stones (b) seven stones (c) ten stones — 3, 7, 10
**Answer:** a) 3, b) 7, c) 10.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Match each number to the next one: (a) 14 (b) 18 (c) 11 — 15, 19, 12
**Answer:** a) 15, b) 19, c) 12.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Match each tens jump to its landing: (a) after 30 (b) after 60 (c) after 80 — 40, 70, 90
**Answer:** a) 40, b) 70, c) 90.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Match each bundle count to its total: (a) 4 bundles of ten (b) 7 bundles of ten (c) 10 bundles of ten — 40, 70, 100
**Answer:** a) 40, b) 70, c) 100.

**5. (Sub lesson 4)** Match each hundreds jump to its landing: (a) after 400 (b) after 700 (c) after 900 — 500, 800, 1,000
**Answer:** a) 500, b) 800, c) 1,000.

**6. (Sub lesson 5)** Match each number to its words: (a) 6,000 (b) 60,000 (c) 600,000 — six thousand, sixty thousand, six hundred thousand
**Answer:** a) six thousand, b) sixty thousand, c) six hundred thousand.

**7. (Sub lesson 6)** Match each group count to its total: (a) 3 groups of 10,000 (b) 3 groups of 100,000 (c) 10 groups of 100,000 — 30,000, 300,000, 1,000,000
**Answer:** a) 30,000, b) 300,000, c) 1,000,000.
