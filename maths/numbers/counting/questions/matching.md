# Counting — matching

Match each item on the left to its partner on the right.

**1. (Level 1)** Match each count to its figure: (a) three stones (b) seven stones (c) ten stones — 3, 7, 10
**Answer:** a) 3, b) 7, c) 10.

**2. (Level 2)** Match each number to the next one: (a) 14 (b) 18 (c) 11 — 15, 19, 12
**Answer:** a) 15, b) 19, c) 12.

**3. (Level 3)** Match each tens jump to its landing: (a) after 30 (b) after 60 (c) after 80 — 40, 70, 90
**Answer:** a) 40, b) 70, c) 90.

**4. (Level 3)** Match each bundle count to its total: (a) 4 bundles of ten (b) 7 bundles of ten (c) 10 bundles of ten — 40, 70, 100
**Answer:** a) 40, b) 70, c) 100.
