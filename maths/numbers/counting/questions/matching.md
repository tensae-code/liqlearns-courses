# Counting — matching

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Counting 1 to 10 · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Counting 11 to 20 · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Counting by Tens to 100, except items about counting past 100, which are Sub lesson 4 — Counting Past 100.

Match each item on the left to its partner on the right.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Match each count to its figure: (a) three stones (b) seven stones (c) ten stones — 3, 7, 10
**Answer:** a) 3, b) 7, c) 10.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Match each number to the next one: (a) 14 (b) 18 (c) 11 — 15, 19, 12
**Answer:** a) 15, b) 19, c) 12.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Match each tens jump to its landing: (a) after 30 (b) after 60 (c) after 80 — 40, 70, 90
**Answer:** a) 40, b) 70, c) 90.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Match each bundle count to its total: (a) 4 bundles of ten (b) 7 bundles of ten (c) 10 bundles of ten — 40, 70, 100
**Answer:** a) 40, b) 70, c) 100.
