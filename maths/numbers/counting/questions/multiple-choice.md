# Counting — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure; extended 2026-11-08 under the whole-arc rule): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. Map: Sub lesson 1 — Counting 1 to 10 · Sub lesson 2 — Counting 11 to 20 · Sub lesson 3 — Counting by Tens to 100 · Sub lesson 4 — Counting in Hundreds to 1,000 · Sub lesson 5 — Counting in Thousands to 10,000 · Sub lesson 6 — Counting Big Numbers to 1,000,000 and Beyond. The old Sub lesson 4 ("Counting Past 100") folds into the new Sub lesson 4: its item about the ones filling in past 100 opens the hundreds climb and keeps its Sub lesson 4 marker.

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Count the stones. (picture: 7 stones) How many are there?
a) 6  b) 7  c) 9
**Answer:** b) 7 — one number said for each stone, the last number said is the count.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which number comes just after 9?
a) 8  b) 10  c) 11
**Answer:** b) 10 — the counting line never changes its order.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** You count 4 spoons, then 3 more spoons. How many spoons have you counted?
a) 6  b) 7  c) 8
**Answer:** b) 7 — count on: 5, 6, 7.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which number comes just before 15?
a) 13  b) 14  c) 16
**Answer:** b) 14 — the 11–20 line runs one by one.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Count by ones from 17. The next number is ______.
a) 16  b) 18  c) 20
**Answer:** b) 18.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** Counting by tens, after 40 comes ______.
a) 41  b) 50  c) 14
**Answer:** b) 50 — the tens line jumps by ten.

**7. (Sub lesson 3)** How many tens make 100?
a) 5  b) 10  c) 100
**Answer:** b) 10 — ten bundles of ten is one hundred.

**8. (Sub lesson 4)** Counting by hundreds, after 600 comes ______.
a) 610  b) 700  c) 601
**Answer:** b) 700 — the hundreds line jumps by a hundred.

**9. (Sub lesson 4)** How many hundreds make 1,000?
a) 10  b) 100  c) 9
**Answer:** a) 10 — ten hundreds is one thousand.

**10. (Sub lesson 5)** Counting in thousands, after 4,000 comes ______.
a) 4,100  b) 5,000  c) 4,010
**Answer:** b) 5,000 — the thousands line jumps by a thousand.

**11. (Sub lesson 5)** How do you read 47,300?
a) forty-seven hundred, three
b) forty-seven thousand, three hundred
c) four thousand, seven hundred thirty
**Answer:** b) forty-seven thousand, three hundred — read a big number in groups.

**12. (Sub lesson 6)** Counting by 100,000s, after 300,000 comes ______.
a) 310,000  b) 400,000  c) 301,000
**Answer:** b) 400,000 — the count jumps by a hundred thousand.

**13. (Sub lesson 6)** Ten packages of 100,000 make ______.
a) 100,000  b) 1,000,000  c) 10,000,000
**Answer:** b) 1,000,000 — ten 100,000-packages make one million (Tanzania's Standard Five book, p.8).
