# Counting — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Counting 1 to 10 · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Counting 11 to 20 · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Counting by Tens to 100, except items about counting past 100, which are Sub lesson 4 — Counting Past 100.

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Count the stones. (picture: 7 stones) How many are there?
a) 6  b) 7  c) 9
**Answer:** b) 7 — one number said for each stone, the last number said is the count.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which number comes just after 9?
a) 8  b) 10  c) 11
**Answer:** b) 10 — the counting line never changes its order.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** You count 4 spoons, then 3 more spoons. How many spoons have you counted?
a) 6  b) 7  c) 8
**Answer:** b) 7 — count on: 5, 6, 7.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which number comes just before 15?
a) 13  b) 14  c) 16
**Answer:** b) 14 — the 11–20 line runs one by one.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Count by ones from 17. The next number is ______.
a) 16  b) 18  c) 20
**Answer:** b) 18.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** Counting by tens, after 40 comes ______.
a) 41  b) 50  c) 14
**Answer:** b) 50 — the tens line jumps by ten.

**7. (Sub lesson 3)** How many tens make 100?
a) 5  b) 10  c) 100
**Answer:** b) 10 — ten bundles of ten is one hundred.
