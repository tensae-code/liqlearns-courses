# Counting — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB
LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure — README, "Units, Lessons and Sub
Lessons"). Practice is tied to the skill number, as before. The
questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson
view. The sub lessons are the depths inside this one thing —
counting — 1–10 first, then 11–20, then the tens to 100, then the
hundreds to 1,000, the thousands to 10,000, and big numbers to
1,000,000 and beyond: the whole climb the books teach, in the
order they teach it (whole-arc rule, ruled 2026-11-08).

Mapping from the old depth layers: old Level 1 = Sub lesson 1;
old Level 2 = Sub lesson 2; old Level 3 covered two natural
jumps, so it splits at the jump — its tens-to-100 part is Sub
lesson 3 and its past-100 part opened what is now Sub lesson 4.
Sub lessons 4 (hundreds part), 5 and 6 were added 2026-11-08
under the whole-arc rule, from the lesson's own stocked sources:
the old text stopped at "Counting Past 100" while the sources
stock the climb to 1,000, 10,000, 1,000,000 and billions.

Pass marks: Sub lessons 1–3 carry the old layers' pass marks.
The books state no separate pass mark for the hundreds,
thousands and big-number bands, so Sub lessons 4–6 keep the
same observable form — the learner can run the band's count and
answer How-many inside it — rather than an invented mark.

## Sub lesson 1 — Counting 1 to 10
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: count 1–10 on real things, touching each thing once, and match a count of things to its number.

## Sub lesson 2 — Counting 11 to 20
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: run the 11–20 line whole, forward and back, and answer How-many for counts to 20.

## Sub lesson 3 — Counting by Tens to 100
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: count by tens to 100, and count in tens and ones to 100.

## Sub lesson 4 — Counting in Hundreds to 1,000
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. (Old Level 3's pass mark covered this ground as "count past 100 in tens, with the ones filled in between the tens" — that fill-in part stands as the first half of this pass mark.) Pass mark: count past 100 in tens with the ones filled in between the tens, count by hundreds to 1,000, and say how many hundreds make 1,000.

## Sub lesson 5 — Counting in Thousands to 10,000
Sub lesson 5 questions from the type files. Pass mark: count in groups of 1,000 to 10,000, fill in the hundreds, tens and ones between the thousands, and read and write numbers to 100,000.

## Sub lesson 6 — Counting Big Numbers to 1,000,000 and Beyond
Sub lesson 6 questions from the type files. Pass mark: count in groups of 10,000 and 100,000 to 1,000,000, read and write numbers to 1,000,000, and read numbers in the millions and billions by their groups of three.
