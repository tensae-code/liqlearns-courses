# Video script — Numbers in Stories

Super-simple voice. The tutor talks; the story count, the pairing arcs and the Roman clock stay on screen.

---

Stories can carry numbers. Count with the story: ten, twenty, thirty — the tens are stepping stones, and the story walks you all the way to one hundred.

Now a race. Add one, two, three, all the way to one hundred. Slow, yes? A schoolboy in a story did it in seconds. He did not add them one by one. He paired them. One with a hundred: one hundred and one. Two with ninety-nine: one hundred and one. Every pair is one hundred and one! Fifty pairs. Fifty times one hundred and one — five thousand and fifty. Done.

Tables like to ride in songs. Five, ten, fifteen, twenty — the beat lands, the answer lands. The song does not change the answer. It just helps you keep it.

And books have their own old numbers. I is one. V is five. X is ten. Chapter VIII is chapter eight. Now you can read the book's own numbers too.

Numbers live in stories. Read them there, and they stay with you.
