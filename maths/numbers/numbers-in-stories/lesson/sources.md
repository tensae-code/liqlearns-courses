No Suriname row existed in any maths lesson before this merge, so every placed item is new here. **Set verdict, proved from the books themselves:** the expansion map's count is right for the Mathematics-labelled set (13 pupil + 3 teacher = 16 files), but its kind labels are wrong in three places: (1) the LJ6 “Leerkrachtenhandleiding Deel 1” of the records is the programme's **Algemene Inleiding** volume (Book 14), not a lesson-by-lesson guide to Deel 1; (2) the two LJ7 teacher books are **oplossingenboeken — answer keys** (Books 15–16), teacher evidence; (3) Book 4 is a **rekenboek** (lesson book), not the “Leerlingenboek” textbooks.tsv names it — the same book. **Maths was hiding under another subject label once:** the KG *Spelend leren* Themaboek 3 and 4 (Books 17–18), filed as “Play-based learning” with textbooks.tsv kind “student” — also wrong: they are **teacher curriculum books** — each carry twelve *voorbereidend rekenen* (preparatory maths) sections with stated learning goals; their 24 items are stocked as **curriculum evidence**, never pupil teaching. **Partial volumes, named as such:** Books 4 and 5 are **Deel A only** and Book 12 is **Deel 1 only**; the download list's remark that LJ8 Deel 2 is “already held” is **not borne out by any record in hand** (textbooks.tsv, the staging books.json and the push-state carry only Deel 1 for LJ8) — LJ8 Deel 2 is treated as not in hand, and the LJ4/LJ5 B-volumes and LJ8 Deel 2–3 are link-only or unrecorded, cited nowhere. Book 8 (LJ6 Deel 3) is staged and pushed but has no textbooks.tsv row — the map counted it, the TSV does not carry it. **The six flagged claims are carried exactly:** (F1) **Book 11 (LJ7 Deel 3) has no Les 24** — contents and body both jump from Les 23 (tangram) to Les 25 (tekenen op schaal); the book holds 38 lessons, not 39, and the rows cite the 38 that exist; (F2) the LJ8-Deel-2 “already held” claim, above — contradicted, not in hand; (F3) **book error, Book 6 Les 6 (printed p. 23):** the information block adds a quarter hour to 15.45 and gives the result as “06:00” — the correct result is **16:00**, and the row teaches the corrected form; (F4) **book error, Book 7 Les 14 (printed p. 54):** the information states the area of a rectangle with a garbled formula line (the perimeter formula 2l + 2b is given correctly, and the area is worked by counting in the same lesson) — the row teaches perimeter = 2l + 2b and area by counting/calculation, the forms the lesson's own worked examples use; (F5) **book error, Book 6 Les 30 (printed p. 93):** the split-subtraction check line prints minus signs where the method needs the parts added (7,986 − 5,234 split as 2,000 + 700 + 50 + 2 = 2,752) — the method itself is taught correctly in Les 21 and the rows follow the correct form; (F6) **Book 13's kind:** textbooks.tsv files the LJ10 Reader as a pupil book; its foreword states it is a guide “voor de reken-leerkracht en leerling” (for teacher and pupil) — its items are placed as teaching content, worded as a **programme reader**, not a pupil textbook. **Text-layer caveat (not a claim):** Book 3's text layer drops the digit “1” in several headings (“splitsingen van 0” for the splitting of 10); the rows use the true values, read from the sums and context. **Checked and NOT flagged:** the answer keys (Books 15–16) match their pupil books lesson for lesson (39 and 38 lessons respectively), and Book 14's contents listing of the kwartaal-1 lessons matches Book 6. Byte sizes checkable against the gov.sr records matched exactly (Books 7, 8, 10, 11). Printed-page basis: printed page = PDF page in every book except Books 17–18 (printed = PDF − 5).
