# Ordering — Numbers in Stories

Sub-lesson markers: every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to — Sub lesson 1 — Counting to 100 in a Story · Sub lesson 2 — The Young Gauss: Adding 1 to 100 · Sub lesson 3 — Times Tables in Verse · Sub lesson 4 — Roman Numerals in Books. This lesson was built new with its sub lessons (Ecuador sweep merge, 2026-11-08), so its markers are original — there are no older depth layers to map from.

1. (Sub lesson 1) Put the story count's stepping stones in order:
   30, 10, 100, 60, 20

   **Answer: 10, 20, 30, 60, 100** — the tens in their climbing order. Item constructed for this lesson from the anthology's counting-to-100 stories. Ecuador, *Matemática (Talento matemático 3)*, Grade 3 student book, closing "Yo Leo" anthology, printed pp. 210–224.

2. (Sub lesson 2) Put the young Gauss's pairs in order by their first number:
   (3, 98), (1, 100), (4, 97), (2, 99)

   **Answer: (1, 100), (2, 99), (3, 98), (4, 97)** — and every pair still adds to 101. Ecuador, *Matemática (Talento matemático 3)*, Grade 3 student book, closing "Yo Leo" anthology, printed pp. 210–224.

3. (Sub lesson 3) Put the answers of the 4 times table in order:
   12, 4, 20, 8, 16

   **Answer: 4, 8, 12, 16, 20** — the order the verse chants them in.

4. (Sub lesson 4) Put the Roman numerals in order of value:
   X, I, L, V

   **Answer: I (1), V (5), X (10), L (50)** — smallest letter-value first. Item constructed for this lesson from the anthology's Roman-numeral reading. Ecuador, *Matemática (Talento matemático 3)*, Grade 3 student book, closing "Yo Leo" anthology, printed pp. 210–224.
