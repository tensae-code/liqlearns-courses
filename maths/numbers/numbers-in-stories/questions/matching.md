# Matching — Numbers in Stories

Sub-lesson markers: every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to — Sub lesson 1 — Counting to 100 in a Story · Sub lesson 2 — The Young Gauss: Adding 1 to 100 · Sub lesson 3 — Times Tables in Verse · Sub lesson 4 — Roman Numerals in Books. This lesson was built new with its sub lessons (Ecuador sweep merge, 2026-11-08), so its markers are original — there are no older depth layers to map from.

1. (Sub lesson 1) Match each tens word to its figure:
   (a) forty
   (b) seventy
   (c) ninety
   (d) one hundred

   (i) 90
   (ii) 40
   (iii) 100
   (iv) 70

   **Answer: (a)–(ii), (b)–(iv), (c)–(i), (d)–(iii)** — the stepping stones in words and figures. Item constructed for this lesson from the anthology's counting-to-100 stories. Ecuador, *Matemática (Talento matemático 3)*, Grade 3 student book, closing "Yo Leo" anthology, printed pp. 210–224.

2. (Sub lesson 2) Match each number to its partner in the young Gauss's pairing of 1 to 100:
   (a) 1
   (b) 2
   (c) 25
   (d) 50

   (i) 99
   (ii) 100
   (iii) 51
   (iv) 76

   **Answer: (a)–(ii), (b)–(i), (c)–(iv), (d)–(iii)** — every match adds to 101. Ecuador, *Matemática (Talento matemático 3)*, Grade 3 student book, closing "Yo Leo" anthology, printed pp. 210–224.

3. (Sub lesson 3) Match each answer the verse carries to its multiplication:
   (a) 12
   (b) 20
   (c) 21
   (d) 36

   (i) 3 × 7
   (ii) 6 × 6
   (iii) 2 × 6
   (iv) 4 × 5

   **Answer: (a)–(iii), (b)–(iv), (c)–(i), (d)–(ii)** — 2 × 6 = 12, 4 × 5 = 20, 3 × 7 = 21, 6 × 6 = 36.

4. (Sub lesson 4) Match each Roman numeral to its value:
   (a) I
   (b) V
   (c) X
   (d) L
   (e) C

   (i) 50
   (ii) 1
   (iii) 100
   (iv) 10
   (v) 5

   **Answer: (a)–(ii), (b)–(v), (c)–(iv), (d)–(i), (e)–(iii)** — I = 1, V = 5, X = 10, L = 50, C = 100. Item constructed for this lesson from the anthology's Roman-numeral reading. Ecuador, *Matemática (Talento matemático 3)*, Grade 3 student book, closing "Yo Leo" anthology, printed pp. 210–224.
