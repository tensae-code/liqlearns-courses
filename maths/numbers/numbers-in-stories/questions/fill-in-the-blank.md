# Fill in the blank — Numbers in Stories

Sub-lesson markers: every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to — Sub lesson 1 — Counting to 100 in a Story · Sub lesson 2 — The Young Gauss: Adding 1 to 100 · Sub lesson 3 — Times Tables in Verse · Sub lesson 4 — Roman Numerals in Books. This lesson was built new with its sub lessons (Ecuador sweep merge, 2026-11-08), so its markers are original — there are no older depth layers to map from.

1. (Sub lesson 1) Counting by tens along the story's road: 10, 20, 30, ___, 50.
   **Answer: 40** — the tens climb by ten each time. Item constructed for this lesson from the anthology's counting-to-100 stories. Ecuador, *Matemática (Talento matemático 3)*, Grade 3 student book, closing "Yo Leo" anthology, printed pp. 210–224.

2. (Sub lesson 1) Ten tens make ___.
   **Answer: 100** — ten stepping stones of ten land on one hundred.

3. (Sub lesson 2) In the young Gauss's pairing, 2 + 99 = ___.
   **Answer: 101** — every pair in 1 to 100 makes 101. Ecuador, *Matemática (Talento matemático 3)*, Grade 3 student book, closing "Yo Leo" anthology, printed pp. 210–224.

4. (Sub lesson 2) Fifty pairs of 101 give 1 + 2 + … + 100 = ___.
   **Answer: 5,050** — 50 × 101 = 5,050.

5. (Sub lesson 3) Carried by the verse: 6 × 3 = ___.
   **Answer: 18** — six threes are eighteen, in verse and out of it.

6. (Sub lesson 4) The Roman numeral XII is the number ___.
   **Answer: 12** — X + I + I, ten and one and one. Item constructed for this lesson from the anthology's Roman-numeral reading. Ecuador, *Matemática (Talento matemático 3)*, Grade 3 student book, closing "Yo Leo" anthology, printed pp. 210–224.
