# Place-value — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Level 1)** 47 is ______.
a) 4 ones + 7 tens  b) 4 tens + 7 ones  c) 47 tens
**Answer:** b) 4 tens + 7 ones — the bundles are the tens.

**2. (Level 1)** In 83, the digit 8 is worth ______.
a) 8  b) 80  c) 800
**Answer:** b) 80 — it stands in the tens place.

**3. (Level 1)** Which number is 2 tens + 6 ones?
a) 26  b) 62  c) 206
**Answer:** a) 26.

**4. (Level 2)** 385 is ______.
a) 3 hundreds, 8 tens, 5 ones  b) 3 tens, 8 hundreds, 5 ones  c) 38 tens, 5 ones
**Answer:** a) 3 hundreds, 8 tens, 5 ones — Egypt's worked example (Grade 3, p.30).

**5. (Level 2)** In 204, the 0 means ______.
a) the number is 24  b) the tens place is empty  c) there are no hundreds
**Answer:** b) the tens place is empty — the zero holds the place open (Egypt, Grade 2).

**6. (Level 3)** In 5,048, the digit 5 is worth ______.
a) 5  b) 500  c) 5,000
**Answer:** c) 5,000 — it stands in the thousands place.

**7. (Level 3)** 723 in expanded form is ______.
a) 700 + 20 + 3  b) 7 + 2 + 3  c) 70 + 20 + 3
**Answer:** a) 700 + 20 + 3.
