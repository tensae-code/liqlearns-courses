# Place-value — matching

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Place Value Tens and Ones · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Place Value Hundreds · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Place Value Thousands and Beyond.

Match each item on the left to its partner on the right.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Match each number to its tens and ones: (a) 43 (b) 28 (c) 70 — 4 tens + 3 ones, 2 tens + 8 ones, 7 tens + 0 ones
**Answer:** a) 4 tens + 3 ones, b) 2 tens + 8 ones, c) 7 tens + 0 ones.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Match each number to its hundreds: (a) 512 (b) 907 (c) 386 — 5 hundreds, 9 hundreds, 3 hundreds
**Answer:** a) 5 hundreds, b) 9 hundreds, c) 3 hundreds.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Match each expanded form to its number: (a) 4000 + 300 + 20 + 1 (b) 9000 + 50 + 6 (c) 700 + 8 — 4,321; 9,056; 708
**Answer:** a) 4,321, b) 9,056, c) 708.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Match each digit to its worth in 5,048: (a) 5 (b) 4 (c) 8 — 5,000; 40; 8
**Answer:** a) 5,000, b) 40, c) 8.
