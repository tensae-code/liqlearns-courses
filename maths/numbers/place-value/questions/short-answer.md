# Place-value — short answer

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Level 1)** Split 56 into tens and ones.
**Answer:** 5 tens + 6 ones.

**2. (Level 1)** Build the number: 3 tens + 9 ones.
**Answer:** 39.

**3. (Level 2)** Split 472 into hundreds, tens and ones.
**Answer:** 4 hundreds, 7 tens, 2 ones.

**4. (Level 2)** What is 600 + 30 + 4 as one number?
**Answer:** 634.

**5. (Level 3)** Split 8,295 into places.
**Answer:** 8 thousands, 2 hundreds, 9 tens, 5 ones.
