# Place-value — fill-in-the-blank

Fill in the missing number or word.

**1. (Level 1)** 35 = ___ tens + 5 ones.
**Answer:** 3

**2. (Level 1)** In 91, the 9 is worth ___.
**Answer:** 90

**3. (Level 2)** 234 = 2 hundreds, ___ tens, 4 ones.
**Answer:** 3

**4. (Level 2)** 500 = ___ hundreds.
**Answer:** 5

**5. (Level 3)** 4,321 = 4 thousands, 3 hundreds, ___ tens, 1 one.
**Answer:** 2
