**Acceptance count: taught in 15 countries (Egypt, Armenia, Jordan, Kazakhstan, South Africa, Laos, Yemen, Indonesia, Nicaragua, Ethiopia, South Sudan, Iraq, Bangladesh, Nepal, Cambodia). Egypt carries the full climb — ones, tens and hundreds in Grade 2, three-digit place value in Grade 3, big numbers to the milliards in Grade 4. Armenia carries tens and ones in Grade 2 and number classes to millions in Grade 3. Jordan carries place value from Grade 1 ones and tens to Grade 4 hundred-thousands. Kazakhstan carries place-value components with graphic models in Grade 2. South Africa carries hundreds, tens and units boxes in Grades 2–3. Other read countries teach place value inside their own Grade 2–3 books and join by merge.** Laos carries place value from tens-and-ones to 100 in Grade 1, through hundreds, expanded forms and numbers to 100000 in Grades 2–3, to millions and billions in Grade 5. Yemen carries place value from ones and tens in Grade 1 through expanded form to 1000, 10,000 and 999,999, then to hundreds of millions and billions. Indonesia carries place value from tens and ones in Grades 1–2, through three-digit place value in Grade 3 and place value to 10,000 in Grade 4, to ten-thousands and then to 1,000,000 in Grade 5. Nicaragua carries place value from tens and ones to 100 in Grade 1, through the hundreds table and expanded form to 1,000 in Grade 2 and to 10,000 in Grade 3, to the hundred-thousands table in Grade 4. Ethiopia carries place value from tens and ones in Grade 1, through 249 as 2 hundreds + 4 tens + 9 ones in Grade 2 and place value to 10,000 in Grade 3, to place value of 6-digit numbers in Grade 4. South Sudan carries place value from tens and ones in Grade 1 (12 = 1 ten, 2 ones), through hundreds, tens and ones with 734 in Grade 2 and the place-value table to thousands with expanded form 319 = 300 + 10 + 9 in Grade 3, to ten-thousands in Grade 4, hundred-thousands with 583,216 worked in Grade 5, and place value in groups (millions/thousands/ones) to a million in Grade 6, with place value again in Secondary 1. One book slip is never taught as fact: the Grade 4 book asks for 3647 in words but answers 3467 — teach the 3467 naming only. Iraq carries place value from tens and ones to 99 in Grade 1, through place value to 999 in Grade 2 and to 9999 in Grade 3, to place value within millions in Grade 4, and to place value within billions in Grade 5. Bangladesh carries place value from tens and ones in Class 1, through hundreds, tens and ones with thousands in Class 2 and place value up to Ayuts in Class 3, to lac, nijut and crore with South Asian commas in Class 4, and the decimal number system and place value in Class 6. Nepal carries place value from tens and ones in Grade 1, through place value to 1000 with blocks, abacus and expanded form in Grade 2 and to 9,99,999 with lakh in Grade 3, to crore in Grade 4 and 10 crore in Grade 5, and whole-number place value again in Grade 6. Cambodia carries place value from tens and ones in Grade 1 to 10 digits in Grade 6, with decimal place value in Grade 6.
