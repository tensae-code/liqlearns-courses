# Hands-on — Place-value

The lesson built with sticks and trays, the way the books teach it.

## Bundle it
Count out 47 sticks. Bundle them in tens. How many bundles? How many singles left? Say it: "4 tens and 7 ones." Do it again for 26, 83 and 59.

## Trays
Three trays: hundreds, tens, ones. Your partner puts counters in the trays — 3 in hundreds, 8 in tens, 5 in ones. Read the number: 385. Swap: you fill the trays for a number your partner calls.

## Digit cards
Cards 0–9. Your partner lays out 4 cards, for example 2, 0, 4, 7. Read the number place by place: "2 thousands, 0 hundreds, 4 tens, 7 ones — two thousand and forty-seven." Then write it in expanded form: 2000 + 40 + 7.

## Zero holds a place
Build 305 and 350 with trays. The trays look different — in 305 the tens tray is empty, in 350 the ones tray is empty. Say each number and say why they are not the same.
