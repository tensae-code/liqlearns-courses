# Video script — Place-value

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Look at 47!

Four bundles of ten — forty!
Seven single sticks — seven!
Forty-seven!

Now 385!
Three hundreds! Eight tens! Five ones!
Three hundred and eighty-five!

Big one: 3,456!
Three thousands! Four hundreds! Five tens! Six ones!
Three thousand, four hundred and fifty-six!

Every digit has a place.
The place tells you what it is worth.
Now you — read the number I show you, place by place!
