# Factors-multiples-and-primes — short answer

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Level 1)** List the factors of 18.
**Answer:** 1, 2, 3, 6, 9, 18.

**2. (Level 1)** List the first five multiples of 4.
**Answer:** 4, 8, 12, 16, 20.

**3. (Level 2)** Is 29 prime? Say why in one line.
**Answer:** Yes — no prime up to 5 divides it (not even, digits do not sum to a multiple of 3, does not end in 0 or 5).

**4. (Level 2)** Is 33 prime or composite? Say why.
**Answer:** Composite — 3 × 11 = 33, so 3 and 11 are factors.

**5. (Level 3)** Find the HCF of 8 and 12.
**Answer:** 4 — factors of 8: 1, 2, 4, 8; factors of 12: 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 12; biggest shared: 4.

**6. (Level 3)** Find the LCM of 3 and 5.
**Answer:** 15 — multiples of 3: 3, 6, 9, 12, 15; multiples of 5: 5, 10, 15; first shared: 15.
