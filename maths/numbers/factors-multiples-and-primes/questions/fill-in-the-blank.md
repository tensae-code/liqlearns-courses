# Factors-multiples-and-primes — fill-in-the-blank

Fill in the missing number or word.

**1. (Level 1)** The factors of 10 are 1, 2, 5 and ___.
**Answer:** 10

**2. (Level 1)** The multiples of 5 start 5, 10, 15, ___.
**Answer:** 20

**3. (Level 2)** 7 is ___ because its only factors are 1 and 7.
**Answer:** prime

**4. (Level 2)** 12 is ___ because it has more than two factors.
**Answer:** composite

**5. (Level 3)** The HCF of 12 and 18 is ___.
**Answer:** 6

**6. (Level 3)** The LCM of 4 and 6 is ___.
**Answer:** 12
