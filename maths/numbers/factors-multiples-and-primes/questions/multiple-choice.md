# Factors-multiples-and-primes — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The factors of 12 are ______.
a) 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 12  b) 1, 2, 4, 8, 12  c) 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12
**Answer:** a) 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 12 — the pile sizes that share 12 equally.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which of these is a multiple of 3?
a) 10  b) 14  c) 15
**Answer:** c) 15 — 3 × 5 = 15.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Which number is a factor of 20 but not of 12?
a) 3  b) 5  c) 6
**Answer:** b) 5 — 5 divides 20, not 12.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Which number is prime?
a) 9  b) 15  c) 11
**Answer:** c) 11 — its only factors are 1 and 11.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Which number is composite?
a) 7  b) 13  c) 21
**Answer:** c) 21 — its factors are 1, 3, 7, 21.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** 1 is ______.
a) prime  b) composite  c) neither
**Answer:** c) neither — it has only one factor (Egypt, Grade 5).

**7. (Sub lesson 4)** The HCF of 12 and 18 is ______.
a) 3  b) 6  c) 9
**Answer:** b) 6 — the biggest factor they share.

**8. (Sub lesson 4)** The LCM of 4 and 6 is ______.
a) 10  b) 12  c) 24
**Answer:** b) 12 — the smallest multiple they share.

**9. (Sub lesson 2)** 453 is divisible by 3 because ______.
a) it ends in 3  b) its digits add to 12  c) it is odd
**Answer:** b) its digits add to 12 — the digit-sum test (Ethiopia, Grade 6, ch. 1).

**10. (Sub lesson 2)** Which number is divisible by 4?
a) 1,246  b) 1,248  c) 1,250
**Answer:** b) 1,248 — its last two digits, 48, divide by 4 (South Sudan, Pupil's Book 5, 1.2).

**11. (Sub lesson 2)** A number that ends in 0 passes the tests for ______.
a) 2, 5 and 10  b) 3 and 9 only  c) 4 and 8
**Answer:** a) 2, 5 and 10 (Yemen, Grade 4, Unit 4).

**12. (Sub lesson 4)** In successive division for GCD(195, 104), the divisions run 195 ÷ 104, then 104 ÷ 91, then ______, and the GCD is the last divisor.
a) 91 ÷ 13, GCD 13  b) 91 ÷ 7, GCD 7  c) 104 ÷ 13, GCD 104
**Answer:** a) 91 ÷ 13 = 7 remainder 0, so GCD(195, 104) = 13 (Tajikistan, Grade 6, p.22).
