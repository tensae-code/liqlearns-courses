# Factors-multiples-and-primes — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Factors and Multiples · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Prime and Composite · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — HCF and LCM.

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The factors of 12 are ______.
a) 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 12  b) 1, 2, 4, 8, 12  c) 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12
**Answer:** a) 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 12 — the pile sizes that share 12 equally.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which of these is a multiple of 3?
a) 10  b) 14  c) 15
**Answer:** c) 15 — 3 × 5 = 15.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Which number is a factor of 20 but not of 12?
a) 3  b) 5  c) 6
**Answer:** b) 5 — 5 divides 20, not 12.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which number is prime?
a) 9  b) 15  c) 11
**Answer:** c) 11 — its only factors are 1 and 11.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Which number is composite?
a) 7  b) 13  c) 21
**Answer:** c) 21 — its factors are 1, 3, 7, 21.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** 1 is ______.
a) prime  b) composite  c) neither
**Answer:** c) neither — it has only one factor (Egypt, Grade 5).

**7. (Sub lesson 3)** The HCF of 12 and 18 is ______.
a) 3  b) 6  c) 9
**Answer:** b) 6 — the biggest factor they share.

**8. (Sub lesson 3)** The LCM of 4 and 6 is ______.
a) 10  b) 12  c) 24
**Answer:** b) 12 — the smallest multiple they share.
