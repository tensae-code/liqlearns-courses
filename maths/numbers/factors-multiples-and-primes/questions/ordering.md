# Factors-multiples-and-primes — ordering

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Factors and Multiples · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 3 — Prime and Composite · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 4 — HCF and LCM. Sub lesson 2 — Divisibility Tests — was added 2026-11-08 under the whole-arc rule, ahead of the old Sub lessons 2–3, which moved to 3–4; their item markers were renumbered to match.

Put the items in the order asked. Check each step before you write the answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Put these factor lists in order by how many factors each number has, fewest first: 7 (2 factors), 12 (6 factors), 10 (4 factors).
Answer the numbers in order.
**Answer:** 7, 10, 12 — 2, 4, then 6 factors.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Put these numbers in order, primes first then composites, smallest first in each group: 21, 7, 13, 9.
Answer in order.
**Answer:** 7, 13, 9, 21 — primes 7 and 13, then composites 9 and 21.

**3. (Sub lesson 4)** Put these LCMs in order, smallest first: LCM(4, 6) = 12; LCM(3, 5) = 15; LCM(6, 8) = 24.
Answer the LCM values in order.
**Answer:** 12, 15, 24 — the LCM values in order.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** From this list, write in counting order the numbers that are divisible by 3: 41, 36, 52, 27, 60.
**Answer:** 27, 36, 60 — their digit sums (9, 9, 6) are multiples of 3; 41 (5) and 52 (7) fail the test.

**5. (Sub lesson 4)** Put the steps of successive division for GCD(195, 104) in the order they run: (a) 91 ÷ 13 = 7, remainder 0 (b) 195 ÷ 104 = 1, remainder 91 (c) 104 ÷ 91 = 1, remainder 13.
**Answer:** b, c, a — each divisor becomes the next dividend's divisor until the remainder is 0.
