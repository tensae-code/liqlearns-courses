Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Factors and Multiples · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 3 — Prime and Composite · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 4 — HCF and LCM. Sub lesson 2 — Divisibility Tests — was added 2026-11-08 under the whole-arc rule, ahead of the old Sub lessons 2–3, which moved to 3–4; their item markers were renumbered to match.

Match each item on the left to its partner on the right.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Match each number to its factors: (a) 10 (b) 15 (c) 8 — 1, 2, 5, 10; 1, 3, 5, 15; 1, 2, 4, 8
**Answer:** a) 1, 2, 5, 10; b) 1, 3, 5, 15; c) 1, 2, 4, 8.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Match each number to prime or composite: (a) 13 (b) 21 (c) 17 — prime, composite, prime
**Answer:** a) prime, b) composite, c) prime.

**3. (Sub lesson 4)** Match each pair to its HCF: (a) 12 and 18 (b) 8 and 12 (c) 9 and 15 — 6, 4, 3
**Answer:** a) 6, b) 4, c) 3.

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Match each pair to its LCM: (a) 4 and 6 (b) 3 and 5 (c) 6 and 8 — 12, 15, 24
**Answer:** a) 12, b) 15, c) 24.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Match each number to a divisibility test it passes: (a) 450 (b) 372 (c) 515 — divisible by 9 (digit sum 9); divisible by 4 (last two digits 72); divisible by 5 (ends in 5)
**Answer:** a) divisible by 9; b) divisible by 4; c) divisible by 5.

**6. (Sub lesson 4)** Match each pair to its GCD: (a) 195 and 104 (b) 12 and 18 (c) 8 and 12 — 13, 6, 4
**Answer:** a) 13 (by successive division), b) 6, c) 4.
