# Factors-multiples-and-primes — matching

Match each item on the left to its partner on the right.

**1. (Level 1)** Match each number to its factors: (a) 10 (b) 15 (c) 8 — 1, 2, 5, 10; 1, 3, 5, 15; 1, 2, 4, 8
**Answer:** a) 1, 2, 5, 10; b) 1, 3, 5, 15; c) 1, 2, 4, 8.

**2. (Level 2)** Match each number to prime or composite: (a) 13 (b) 21 (c) 17 — prime, composite, prime
**Answer:** a) prime, b) composite, c) prime.

**3. (Level 3)** Match each pair to its HCF: (a) 12 and 18 (b) 8 and 12 (c) 9 and 15 — 6, 4, 3
**Answer:** a) 6, b) 4, c) 3.

**4. (Level 3)** Match each pair to its LCM: (a) 4 and 6 (b) 3 and 5 (c) 6 and 8 — 12, 15, 24
**Answer:** a) 12, b) 15, c) 24.
