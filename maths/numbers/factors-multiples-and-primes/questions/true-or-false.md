# Factors-multiples-and-primes — true or false

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Level 1)** 6 is a factor of 18.
**Answer:** True — 18 ÷ 6 = 3 exactly.

**2. (Level 1)** 14 is a multiple of 3.
**Answer:** False — 14 is not in the 3s: 3, 6, 9, 12, 15.

**3. (Level 2)** 2 is prime.
**Answer:** True — its only factors are 1 and 2.

**4. (Level 2)** 1 is prime.
**Answer:** False — 1 has only one factor, so it is neither prime nor composite.

**5. (Level 3)** The HCF of two numbers is bigger than either number.
**Answer:** False — the HCF divides both, so it can never be bigger than the smaller one.

**6. (Level 3)** The LCM of 3 and 5 is 15.
**Answer:** True.
