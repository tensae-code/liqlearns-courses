Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** 6 is a factor of 18.
**Answer:** True — 18 ÷ 6 = 3 exactly.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** 14 is a multiple of 3.
**Answer:** False — 14 is not in the 3s: 3, 6, 9, 12, 15.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** 2 is prime.
**Answer:** True — its only factors are 1 and 2.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** 1 is prime.
**Answer:** False — 1 has only one factor, so it is neither prime nor composite.

**5. (Sub lesson 4)** The HCF of two numbers is bigger than either number.
**Answer:** False — the HCF divides both, so it can never be bigger than the smaller one.

**6. (Sub lesson 4)** The LCM of 3 and 5 is 15.
**Answer:** True.

**7. (Sub lesson 2)** Every number divisible by 9 is also divisible by 3.
**Answer:** True — a digit sum that is a multiple of 9 is also a multiple of 3.

**8. (Sub lesson 2)** A number ending in 5 is divisible by 2.
**Answer:** False — the test for 2 needs an even last digit: 0, 2, 4, 6, 8.

**9. (Sub lesson 4)** In successive division you stop when the remainder is 0, and the last divisor is the GCD.
**Answer:** True — that is the method's stopping rule (Tajikistan, Grade 6, p.22).
