# Video script — Factors-multiples-and-primes

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Twelve stones! Share them equal!
One pile of twelve! Two piles of six!
Three piles of four! Four piles of three!
The factors of 12: 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 12!

Multiples of 3! Three, six, nine, twelve!
Count by threes — forever!

Prime! Seven! Only 1 and 7 divide it!
Composite! Twelve! Lots of factors!
One? Neither! Only one factor!

Biggest shared factor — HCF!
Smallest shared multiple — LCM!
Now you — is 11 prime? Is 15? You tell me!
