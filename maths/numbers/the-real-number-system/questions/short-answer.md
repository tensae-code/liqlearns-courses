# The Real Number System — short answer

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — The Real Number System The Families of Numbers · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — The Real Number System The Laws on Whole Numbers, Fractions and Integers · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — The Real Number System The Laws for Rationals and for All Real Numbers.

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the family added at each step: naturals, then ______ (0 joins), then ______ (negatives join).
**Answer:** whole numbers, then integers — the Grade 8 nesting (Unit 1, pp.1–3).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Is 0.333… rational or irrational? Say why in five words or fewer.
**Answer:** Rational — its decimal recurs (Grade 8, Unit 1, pp.1–3).

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Verify with numbers: is fraction addition commutative for 1/2 and 1/3?
**Answer:** Yes — 1/2 + 1/3 = 1/3 + 1/2 = 5/6, the Grade 5 verification (pp.31–32).

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the additive identity and the multiplicative identity for integers.
**Answer:** 0 and 1 — Grade 6 verifies them in turn (pp.32–33, 38–39).

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Give the additive inverse of −7 and of 2/5.
**Answer:** 7 and −2/5 — each pair sums to 0, the additive identity (Grade 8, Unit 1, pp.6–9).

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** Check distributivity on rationals: does 1/2 × (1/4 + 3/4) equal 1/2 × 1/4 + 1/2 × 3/4?
**Answer:** Yes — both sides are 1/2 (the Grade 7 verification pattern, pp.16–19).

**7. (Sub lesson 3)** State the closure property for real numbers under multiplication.
**Answer:** A real number times a real number is always a real number (Grade 8, Unit 1, pp.6–9).

**8. (Sub lesson 3)** Place √5 in its smallest family.
**Answer:** The irrationals — it sits in R but not in Q (Grade 8, Unit 1, pp.1–3).
