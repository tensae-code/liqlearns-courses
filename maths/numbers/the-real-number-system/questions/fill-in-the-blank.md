# The Real Number System — fill in the blank

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — The Real Number System The Families of Numbers · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — The Real Number System The Laws on Whole Numbers, Fractions and Integers · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — The Real Number System The Laws for Rationals and for All Real Numbers.

Fill in the missing number or word.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** N ⊂ W ⊂ Z ⊂ ___ ⊂ R.
**Answer:** Q — the rationals are the last ring before all reals.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The irrationals are written with the letter ___′ in Pakistan's Grade 8 book.
**Answer:** Q — Q′ is everything real that is not rational.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** For whole numbers, a × (b − c) = a × b ___ a × c.
**Answer:** − (minus) — distributivity holds over subtraction too (Grade 5, pp.13–15).

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The additive identity for integers is ___.
**Answer:** 0 — adding it changes nothing (Grade 6, pp.32–33).

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** The multiplicative inverse of −2/3 is ___.
**Answer:** −3/2 — their product is (+1), the multiplicative identity.
