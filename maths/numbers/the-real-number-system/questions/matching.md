Match each item on the left to its partner on the right.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Match each number to its smallest family: (a) 0 (b) −8 (c) 5/9 — N, W, Z, Q (one family is spare)
**Answer:** a) W, b) Z, c) Q — 0 joins at W, negatives at Z, fractions at Q.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Match each law to its check: (a) commutative (b) associative (c) distributive — (2 + 3) + 4 = 2 + (3 + 4); 2 × (3 + 4) = 2 × 3 + 2 × 4; 2 + 3 = 3 + 2
**Answer:** a) 2 + 3 = 3 + 2, b) (2 + 3) + 4 = 2 + (3 + 4), c) 2 × (3 + 4) = 2 × 3 + 2 × 4 — the three laws Grade 7 verifies on rationals (pp.16–19).

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Match each number to its multiplicative inverse: (a) 5 (b) 1/5 (c) −1 — −1, 1/5, 5
**Answer:** a) 1/5, b) 5, c) −1 — each pair multiplies to 1.
