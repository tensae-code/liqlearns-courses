# Hands-on — Rounding-and-estimating

The lesson rounded on the line and estimated before counting.

## Nearest ten on the line
A number line 0–100 with the tens marked big. Your partner calls a number — 47. You stand on it. Which ten is nearer — 40 or 50? Step to it. That is the rounded number.

## Jar estimates
A jar of stones. Guess how many — write the guess down. Now count. How close was the guess? Guess again with a second jar, and see if the second guess is closer.

## Estimate before you add
Two piles of stones, about 40 and about 30. Estimate the total before counting: "about 70." Now count both piles exactly. Is the exact total close to the estimate? It should be.

## Round the room
Walk the room with a partner. They point at a count — books on a shelf, about 38. Round it to the nearest ten: "about 40." Five rounds each.
