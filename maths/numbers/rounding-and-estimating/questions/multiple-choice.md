# Rounding-and-estimating — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Rounding to the Nearest Ten · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Rounding to the Nearest Hundred and Thousand · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Estimating.

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** 47 rounded to the nearest ten is ______.
a) 40  b) 50  c) 45
**Answer:** b) 50 — the ones digit is 7, five or more rounds up.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** 43 rounded to the nearest ten is ______.
a) 30  b) 40  c) 50
**Answer:** b) 40 — the ones digit is 3, four or less rounds down.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** 45 rounded to the nearest ten is ______.
a) 40  b) 50  c) 45
**Answer:** b) 50 — halfway rounds up.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** 382 rounded to the nearest hundred is ______.
a) 300  b) 380  c) 400
**Answer:** c) 400 — the tens digit is 8.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** 341 rounded to the nearest hundred is ______.
a) 300  b) 400  c) 340
**Answer:** a) 300 — the tens digit is 4.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** 4,736 rounded to the nearest thousand is ______.
a) 4,000  b) 5,000  c) 4,700
**Answer:** b) 5,000 — the hundreds digit is 7.

**7. (Sub lesson 3)** Estimate 247 + 45 by rounding first.
a) about 200  b) about 300  c) about 400
**Answer:** b) about 300 — 250 + 50 = 300, Jordan's own printed estimate.
