Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** 44 rounds to 40 to the nearest ten.
**Answer:** True — the ones digit is 4.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** 45 rounds to 40 to the nearest ten.
**Answer:** False — halfway rounds up: 45 rounds to 50.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** 382 is nearer 300 than 400.
**Answer:** False — 382 stands nearer 400; its tens digit is 8.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Rounding to the nearest hundred looks at the tens digit.
**Answer:** True.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** If your exact answer is far from your estimate, the work is certainly right.
**Answer:** False — a far-apart answer means something went wrong; work it again.
