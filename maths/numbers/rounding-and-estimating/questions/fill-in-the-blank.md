# Rounding-and-estimating — fill-in-the-blank

Fill in the missing number or word.

**1. (Level 1)** 47 rounds to ___ to the nearest ten.
**Answer:** 50

**2. (Level 1)** Five or more rounds ___; four or less rounds ___.
**Answer:** up; down

**3. (Level 2)** 382 rounds to ___ to the nearest hundred.
**Answer:** 400

**4. (Level 2)** 350 rounds to ___ to the nearest hundred.
**Answer:** 400

**5. (Level 3)** Estimate 73 − 29 by rounding: about ___ − ___ = about ___.
**Answer:** 70 − 30 = 40
