## Sub lesson 1 — Even and Odd Numbers The Last-Digit Test
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: 4 of 5. Say whether a number is even or odd by looking only at its last digit: even ends in 0, 2, 4, 6, 8; odd ends in 1, 3, 5, 7, 9.

## Sub lesson 2 — Even and Odd Numbers Hunting in a Range
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: 4 of 5. List the even numbers and the odd numbers between two given numbers, and continue a run of evens or odds with the missing terms filled in.

## Sub lesson 3 — Even and Odd Numbers Adding Rules
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: 4 of 5. Use odd + 1 = even, even + 1 = odd, even + odd = odd and odd + odd = even to say what kind of number a sum will be before working it out.

## What "maxed" means here

The learner can look at any whole number, however long, and say at once whether it is even or odd and why; can list the evens and odds inside any range; and can say, before adding, whether a sum of two numbers will come out even or odd — the exact skills India's Class 3 book teaches and exercises on printed pp. 151–152.
