# Video script — Even and Odd Numbers

Super-simple voice. The tutor talks; each number appears on screen as it is named.

---

Look at this number: 248. Do not read the whole number. Look only at the last digit. It is 8. That makes 248 an even number.

Even numbers end in 0, 2, 4, 6, 8. Odd numbers end in 1, 3, 5, 7, 9. The last digit decides. Nothing else matters.

Here is 347. Last digit: 7. Odd. Here is 90. Last digit: 0. Even. Here is 1,001. Last digit: 1. Odd — even though the number is big.

Now hunt. Odd numbers between 400 and 410: 401, 403, 405, 407, 409. Even numbers between 155 and 165: 156, 158, 160, 162, 164.

One more step. Take an odd number and add 1: you land on an even number. Take an even number and add 1: you land on an odd number. One step always flips the kind.

And even plus odd? Say 4 + 7. The answer is 11 — odd. Even plus odd is always odd. Odd plus odd, like 7 + 5, is 12 — even.

Last digit to tell them apart, ranges to hunt them, rules for their sums. That is even and odd numbers.
