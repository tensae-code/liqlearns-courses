1. (Sub lesson 2) Put these numbers in order from smallest to largest, and say which kind each one is: 409, 401, 405, 407, 403.
   **Answer: 401, 403, 405, 407, 409 — every one of them odd (they are the odd numbers between 400 and 410).** India, Math-Magic Class 3, printed p. 152.

2. (Sub lesson 2) Put these numbers in order from smallest to largest, and say which kind each one is: 162, 156, 164, 160, 158.
   **Answer: 156, 158, 160, 162, 164 — every one of them even (they are the even numbers between 155 and 165).** India, Math-Magic Class 3, printed p. 152.
