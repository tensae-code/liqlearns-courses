# Matching — Even and Odd Numbers

1. (Layers 1–3) Match each number or sum to its kind:
   (a) 89
   (b) 240
   (c) 517
   (d) 26 + 39
   (e) 81 + 47

   Kinds: (i) even (ii) odd

   **Answer: (a)–(ii), (b)–(i), (c)–(ii), (d)–(ii) — even + odd = odd, (e)–(i) — odd + odd = even.** India, Math-Magic Class 3, printed pp. 151–152.
