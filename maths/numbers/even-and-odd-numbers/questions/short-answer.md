# Short answer — Even and Odd Numbers

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Layer 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Even and Odd Numbers The Last-Digit Test · (Layer 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Even and Odd Numbers Hunting in a Range · (Layer 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Even and Odd Numbers Adding Rules.

1. (Sub lesson 1) Say whether 1,006 is even or odd, and name the digit that decides it.
   **Answer: Even — its last digit is 6, one of 0, 2, 4, 6, 8; the size of the number does not matter.** India, Math-Magic Class 3, printed p. 151.

2. (Sub lesson 2) List all the odd numbers between 998 and 1,010.
   **Answer: 999, 1,001, 1,003, 1,005, 1,007, 1,009.** India, Math-Magic Class 3, printed p. 152.

3. (Sub lesson 3) Without adding, say whether 150 + 151 is even or odd, and why.
   **Answer: Odd — it is even + odd, and even + odd is always odd (the sum is 301, if you check).** India, Math-Magic Class 3, printed p. 152.
