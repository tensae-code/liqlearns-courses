# Comparing-and-ordering — questions by level

Practice by level, tied to the skill number. The questions live in
the type files; this file is the by-level view. The levels are the
depths inside this one thing — comparing — two-digit comparisons first, then bigger numbers by their biggest place, then the number line..


## Level 1 — bigger, smaller, equal
Level 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: compare two two-digit numbers with > , < and = , and say the comparison in words.

## Level 2 — the biggest place decides
Level 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: compare three-digit and bigger numbers by the biggest place first, and order three or more numbers.

## Level 3 — the number line
Level 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: place numbers on the number line, order numbers to 200,000 by their places, and compare numbers of different lengths.
