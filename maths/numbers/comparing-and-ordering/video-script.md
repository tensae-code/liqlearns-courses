# Video script — Comparing-and-ordering

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Which is bigger? 47 or 39?

Look at the tens! Four tens beats three tens!
47 is BIGGER than 39!
Write it: 47 > 39!

Which is smaller? 128 or 182?
Hundreds tie! Look at the tens!
Two tens beats eight tens? No!
128 is SMALLER than 182!
Write it: 128 < 182!

On the line, bigger lives to the right!
Smaller lives to the left!
Now you — point! Which is bigger? Say it whole!
