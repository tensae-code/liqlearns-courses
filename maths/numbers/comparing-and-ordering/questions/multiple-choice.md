# Comparing-and-ordering — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Comparing and Ordering Bigger, Smaller, Equal · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Comparing and Ordering The Biggest Place Decides · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Comparing and Ordering The Number Line.

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which sign goes between 47 and 39?
a) 47 < 39  b) 47 > 39  c) 47 = 39
**Answer:** b) 47 > 39 — the tens are 4 and 3, and 4 beats 3.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which is the smallest: 56, 49, 63?
a) 56  b) 49  c) 63
**Answer:** b) 49 — its tens digit is the smallest.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** 34 ___ 43. The missing sign is ______.
a) >  b) <  c) =
**Answer:** b) < — 34 is less than 43.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which is bigger: 128 or 182?
a) 128  b) 182  c) they are equal
**Answer:** b) 182 — the hundreds tie at 1, so the tens decide: 8 beats 2.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Which is bigger: 98 or 102?
a) 98  b) 102  c) they are equal
**Answer:** b) 102 — three places beats two places.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** On the number line, 47 stands ______ 39.
a) to the left of  b) to the right of  c) on top of
**Answer:** b) to the right of — bigger numbers live to the right.

**7. (Sub lesson 3)** Which list is in order, smallest to biggest?
a) 31, 29, 27  b) 27, 29, 31  c) 29, 31, 27
**Answer:** b) 27, 29, 31.
