# Comparing-and-ordering — short answer

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Comparing and Ordering Bigger, Smaller, Equal · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Comparing and Ordering The Biggest Place Decides · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Comparing and Ordering The Number Line.

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Write > , < or = : 56 ___ 49.
**Answer:** 56 > 49.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Say in words: 73 > 68.
**Answer:** "73 is greater than 68."

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Which is bigger: 246 or 264? Say why in one line.
**Answer:** 264 — the hundreds tie at 2, so the tens decide: 6 beats 4.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Order smallest to biggest: 91, 47, 58.
**Answer:** 47, 58, 91.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Order biggest to smallest: 1,005; 999; 1,050.
**Answer:** 1,050; 1,005; 999.
