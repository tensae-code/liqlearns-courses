# Comparing-and-ordering — short answer

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Level 1)** Write > , < or = : 56 ___ 49.
**Answer:** 56 > 49.

**2. (Level 1)** Say in words: 73 > 68.
**Answer:** "73 is greater than 68."

**3. (Level 2)** Which is bigger: 246 or 264? Say why in one line.
**Answer:** 264 — the hundreds tie at 2, so the tens decide: 6 beats 4.

**4. (Level 2)** Order smallest to biggest: 91, 47, 58.
**Answer:** 47, 58, 91.

**5. (Level 3)** Order biggest to smallest: 1,005; 999; 1,050.
**Answer:** 1,050; 1,005; 999.
