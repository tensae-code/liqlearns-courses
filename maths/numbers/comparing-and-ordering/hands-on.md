# Hands-on — Comparing-and-ordering

The lesson compared with piles, cards and a floor number line.

## Pile vs pile
Two piles of stones. Which pile is bigger? Say it whole: "This pile is bigger." Now count both piles and write the comparison with > or < .

## Sign detective
Your partner lays out two number cards. You write > , < or = between them, then read the whole sentence aloud: "56 is greater than 49." Ten rounds, then swap.

## Number-line walk
A number line on the floor, 0 to 100. Your partner calls a number; you stand on it. They call a second number; a second learner stands on it. Who stands further right? That number is bigger. Now order four learners on the line, smallest to biggest.

## Order the cards
Ten number cards, shuffled. Order them smallest to biggest on the table. Check by reading them left to right — each next card must be bigger than the last.
