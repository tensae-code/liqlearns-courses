Barbados enters as CURRICULUM EVIDENCE only, through the *Primary Mathematics Syllabus* (Classes 1–4) of its Ministry of Education and Human Resource Development — its Class 1 syllabus requires the commutative and associative properties under addition and in multiplication, the identity property of zero under addition and subtraction, the identity property of one under multiplication, and multiplication by zero; Class 2 keeps the same properties as scope-and-sequence rules to be used in problem solving (named only — the Class 2 body has no Properties section); Classes 3 and 4 require the same five properties in their scope and sequence. A syllabus states what is to be taught, in what order and to what depth; it teaches nothing itself. Barbados publishes no government pupil textbooks — primary mathematics books are commercial titles parents buy, and the syllabi's Suggested Texts appendices name them (*Caribbean Primary Mathematics* Levels 1–6; *Nelson Primary Maths for Caribbean Schools* 1–4; *Steps To Common Entrance Mathematics* 1–3) as named by the syllabus only: none is in hand and none is cited. Barbados is therefore named in this lesson's countries line and its syllabus requirements are cited in the table as curriculum evidence, but Barbados is not counted in the taught-in figure.

From its books: from Grade 3: addition is commutative and can be grouped: shop prices added in either order give the same total (175 + 120 = 120 + 175 = 295); more than two addends, written in columns or in a row, brackets done first — (25 + 12) + 36 = 73; same three prices grouped differently: (book + bag) + notebook against book + (bag + notebook) and the other pairings — the total is the same however the addends are grouped; equal totals with three addends: two schools' three-day visit totals (102 + 80 + 213) = (213 + 102 + 80) — if two addends are the same, the third must be the same (the missing third day is 80); a reading-pages version (25 + 45 + 30 against 45 + 30 + ?); from Grade 7: Addition of rationals: commutative, associative, identity element 0, and additive inverse properties, discovered through an order-swapping activity; Multiplication of rationals: commutative, associative, identity element 1, absorbing element 0, and multiplicative inverse (reciprocal) properties.
