# True or false — The Laws of Arithmetic Operations

1. (Layer 2) 16 ÷ 4 = 4 ÷ 16. **False** — division is not commutative. Malaysia, Mathematics Form 1, Ch. 1 §1.2, printed p. 11.
2. (Layer 2) Adding 0 never changes a number, whatever the number is. **True** — the identity law. Malaysia, Mathematics Form 1, Ch. 1 §1.2, printed p. 11.
3. (Layer 3) (2.5 + 7.5) + 9 = 2.5 + (7.5 + 9). **True** — the associative law holds for decimals too. Malaysia, Mathematics Form 1, Ch. 1 §1.2, printed pp. 11–12.
