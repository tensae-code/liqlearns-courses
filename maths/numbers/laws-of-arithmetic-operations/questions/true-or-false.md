# True or false — The Laws of Arithmetic Operations

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth layers ran across the four laws together, so the items are split item by item, by the law each item actually asks about: identity -> Sub lesson 1 — Arithmetic Laws The Identity Law · commutative -> Sub lesson 2 — Arithmetic Laws The Commutative Law · associative -> Sub lesson 3 — Arithmetic Laws The Associative Law · distributive -> Sub lesson 4 — Arithmetic Laws The Distributive Law · calculating cleverly (old Layer 3) -> Sub lesson 5 — Arithmetic Laws Calculating Cleverly.

1. (Sub lesson 2) 16 ÷ 4 = 4 ÷ 16. **False** — division is not commutative. Malaysia, Mathematics Form 1, Ch. 1 §1.2, printed p. 11.
2. (Sub lesson 1) Adding 0 never changes a number, whatever the number is. **True** — the identity law. Malaysia, Mathematics Form 1, Ch. 1 §1.2, printed p. 11.
3. (Sub lesson 3) (2.5 + 7.5) + 9 = 2.5 + (7.5 + 9). **True** — the associative law holds for decimals too. Malaysia, Mathematics Form 1, Ch. 1 §1.2, printed pp. 11–12.
