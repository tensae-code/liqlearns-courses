# Ordering — The Laws of Arithmetic Operations

1. (Layer 3) Put the steps for calculating 17 × 5 × 20 cleverly in the right order:
   (a) Answer: 1700 (b) Group 5 × 20 = 100 (c) Look for a pair that makes a round number (d) Multiply 17 × 100
   **Answer: c, b, d, a.** Malaysia, Mathematics Form 1, Ch. 1 §1.2, printed pp. 11–12.
