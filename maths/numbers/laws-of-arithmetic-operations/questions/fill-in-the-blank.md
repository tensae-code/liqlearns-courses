1. (Sub lesson 1) a × 1 = ___ (identity law).
   **Answer: a.** Malaysia, Mathematics Form 1, Ch. 1 §1.2, printed p. 11.

2. (Sub lesson 4) In a × (b − c) = a × b − a × c, it is ___ that is distributed over the subtraction.
   **Answer: multiplication.** Malaysia, Mathematics Form 1, Ch. 1 §1.2, printed p. 11 (taught corrected — the book's sentence on that page states the law backwards).

3. (Sub lesson 5) 4 × 37 × 25 = (4 × ___) × 37 = ___.
   **Answer: 25; 3700.** Malaysia, Mathematics Form 1, Ch. 1 §1.2, printed pp. 11–12.
