# Multiple choice — The Laws of Arithmetic Operations

1. (Layer 1) Which law says 13 + 27 = 27 + 13?
   A. Identity B. Commutative C. Associative D. Distributive
   **Answer: B** — Malaysia, Mathematics Form 1, Ch. 1 §1.2, printed p. 11.

2. (Layer 1) (4 + 9) + 1 = 4 + (9 + 1) is an example of which law?
   A. Associative B. Distributive C. Identity D. Commutative
   **Answer: A** — Malaysia, Mathematics Form 1, Ch. 1 §1.2, printed p. 11.

3. (Layer 2) Which of these is FALSE?
   A. 8 × 5 = 5 × 8 B. 20 − 6 = 6 − 20 C. 3 + 9 = 9 + 3 D. 7 × 1 = 7
   **Answer: B** — the commutative law does not hold for subtraction. Malaysia, Mathematics Form 1, Ch. 1 §1.2, printed p. 11.

4. (Layer 3) The clever way to do 25 × 13 × 4 starts with:
   A. 25 × 13 B. 13 × 4 C. 25 × 4 D. 25 + 4
   **Answer: C** — 25 × 4 = 100. Malaysia, Mathematics Form 1, Ch. 1 §1.2, printed pp. 11–12.
