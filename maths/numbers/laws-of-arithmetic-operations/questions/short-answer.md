# Short answer — The Laws of Arithmetic Operations

1. (Layer 2) Use the distributive law to work out 6 × 99.
   **Answer: 6 × (100 − 1) = 600 − 6 = 594.** Malaysia, Mathematics Form 1, Ch. 1 §1.2, printed p. 11.

2. (Layer 2) Give a counter-example showing the associative law fails for subtraction.
   **Answer: e.g. (10 − 6) − 2 = 2 but 10 − (6 − 2) = 6.** Malaysia, Mathematics Form 1, Ch. 1 §1.2, printed p. 11.

3. (Layer 3) Calculate 58 + 76 + 24 the clever way, and name the law you used.
   **Answer: 58 + (76 + 24) = 58 + 100 = 158; associative law (76 + 24 grouped first).** Malaysia, Mathematics Form 1, Ch. 1 §1.2, printed pp. 11–12.
