# Matching — The Laws of Arithmetic Operations

1. (Layer 1) Match each calculation to the law it uses:
   (a) 9 + 0 = 9 (b) 5 × 8 = 8 × 5 (c) (2 + 3) + 4 = 2 + (3 + 4) (d) 3 × (10 + 2) = 30 + 6
   Laws: identity, commutative, associative, distributive.
   **Answer: (a) identity, (b) commutative, (c) associative, (d) distributive.** Malaysia, Mathematics Form 1, Ch. 1 §1.2, printed p. 11.
