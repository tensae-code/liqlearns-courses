**Acceptance count: taught in 12 countries (Sierra Leone, Laos, Bangladesh, Nepal, Pakistan, Malaysia, Sudan, Venezuela, Uzbekistan, Albania, Tajikistan, Mauritania)**.** Sierra Leone carries the fullest bases course: JSS 3 opens base 2 and compares in it, and SSS 1 runs the complete course — expansion, repeated division, all four operations in non-decimal bases, and equations with an unknown digit or base. Laos carries bases inside one Grade 6 lesson that starts from base-ten expanded form and reaches a general base a, with arithmetic in bases 2, 3, 4, 5 and 7. Bangladesh carries binary twice — as a story in Class 7 and as a full system with the radix point and complements in Class 8. Nepal carries binary and base 5 across Grades 7 and 8, with the 2⁸…2⁰ place-value table. Pakistan carries the computer side only: binary, octal and hexadecimal place values and the 0–16 conversion table in its Class 12 Statistics book.

Malaysia teaches number bases whole in Form 4 as a chapter of its own — representation by bundling, conversion both ways, the binary–octal shortcut by grouping digits in threes, and addition and subtraction carried out inside the base.

Sudan teaches the binary system whole in Grade 7 — base 2 introduced through electric circuits and computers, conversion both ways with the answer checked by converting back, and binary addition and subtraction with the carry and borrow rules — inside a unit that opens with the history of numeral systems.

Venezuela enters through the Colección Bicentenario, the MPPE's one-book-per-grade course: *Hecho en Venezuela* (Grade 6). From its books: from Grade 6: world systems — The Warao counting system: body-based, 20 = one person (warao jisaka), 40 = two persons, 100 = five persons; 6–8 built as "hand and one/two/three of the other hand".

Albania enters through its Mediaprint teacher's books (Libër Mësuesi, in Albanian) — TEACHER EVIDENCE only: no pupil's book is in hand for any grade, so Albania is never cited as a pupil-textbook source; what the teacher's books evidence is what is taught and in what order, through their lesson plans, worked answers and answer keys. From its books: from Grade 6 (teacher's book): : invented/alien counting systems and other bases (base 4, 6, 8 counting).

Tajikistan enters through its Ministry-approved pupil's textbooks in Tajik (Cyrillic), all published in Dushanbe — Математика for Grade 5 (Маориф, 2022) and Grade 6 (5th edition, Маориф, 2021), Алгебра for Grades 7 (4th improved edition, 2023) and 8 (4th edition, 2023), and Геометрия — the continuation of stereometry — for Grade 11 (3rd edition, Лавҳ, 2020). From its books: from Grade 6: Positional number systems; The binary system: writing naturals in binary, expanding (101011)₂ = 43, binary addition…; from Grade 7: Positional number systems; The binary system and its applications.
