# Matching — Algorithmic Thinking

Sub-lesson markers: every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to — Sub lesson 1 — Algorithms and Flowcharts · Sub lesson 2 — Working with Lists of Data. This lesson was built new with its sub lessons (Australia expansion merge, 2026-11-11), founded on Book A3's Chapter 11, so its markers are original — there are no older depth layers to map from.

1. (Sub lesson 1) Match each flowchart shape to its job:
   (a) the rounded oval
   (b) the slanted parallelogram
   (c) the rectangle
   (d) the diamond
   Jobs: a decision question / Start or End / an input or an output / a process step.
   **Answer: (a) Start or End; (b) an input or an output; (c) a process step; (d) a decision question.** Item constructed for this lesson from its founding chapter. Australia, *Essential Mathematics for the Victorian Curriculum Year 8, Third Edition* (Greenwood et al., Cambridge University Press, ©2024), Chapter 11 (Algorithmic thinking), Introduction + Activity 1 — algorithms and flowcharts, printed pp. 926–927.

2. (Sub lesson 1) Match each part of the "divisible by 3" algorithm to its kind:
   (a) "the number 342"
   (b) "add the digits"
   (c) "Yes — it is divisible"
   Kinds: a process step / an output / an input.
   **Answer: (a) an input; (b) a process step; (c) an output.** Item constructed for this lesson from its founding chapter. Australia, *Essential Mathematics for the Victorian Curriculum Year 8, Third Edition* (Greenwood et al., Cambridge University Press, ©2024), Chapter 11 (Algorithmic thinking), Introduction + Activity 1 — algorithms and flowcharts, printed pp. 926–927.

3. (Sub lesson 2) Match each action to its name:
   (a) putting 7, 2, 9, 4 into the order 2, 4, 7, 9
   (b) checking 2, 4, 7, 9 one by one to find the 7
   (c) recording twenty die rolls in order
   Names: generating by simulation / sorting / searching.
   **Answer: (a) sorting; (b) searching; (c) generating by simulation.** Item constructed for this lesson from its founding chapter. Australia, *Essential Mathematics for the Victorian Curriculum Year 8, Third Edition* (Greenwood et al., Cambridge University Press, ©2024), Chapter 11 (Algorithmic thinking), Activity 2 — working with lists of data, printed p. ~929.

   (a) search for 4
   (b) search for 9
   (c) search for 5
   Outcomes: found at the fourth check / not in the list — the search can stop once it reaches 7 / found at the second check.
   **Answer: (a) found at the second check; (b) found at the fourth check; (c) not in the list — the search can stop once it reaches 7.** Item constructed for this lesson from its founding chapter.
