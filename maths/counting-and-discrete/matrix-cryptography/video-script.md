# Video script — Matrix Cryptography

Super-simple voice. The tutor talks; the letters, numbers and matrices change as they are named.

---

Here is a secret trick with matrices. First, turn letters into numbers. A is 1, B is 2, all the way to Z is 26. Careful: J is 10 and K is 11.

Take the word HI. H is 8, I is 9. Now multiply the pair by a secret matrix — say, two-one, one-one. Eight and nine become twenty-five and seventeen. That pair is the code. Anyone reading it sees only numbers.

To read it back, you need the matrix's inverse — its undoing matrix. Multiply the code pair by the inverse, and out come 8 and 9. H and I. The message is back.

No inverse, no decoding. So the secret matrix must have one — its determinant must not be zero.

Letters to numbers, numbers through a matrix, back through its inverse. That is a matrix cryptogram.
