# Levels — Matrix Cryptography

One lesson, one level (Level 149). The depth layers inside it are ranks.

## Depth layers

- **Layer 1 — Letters and numbers.** Convert letters to numbers and numbers back to letters with the letter–number table, with J = 10 and K = 11. Pass mark: 4 of 5.
- **Layer 2 — Encode.** Group a short message's numbers in pairs and multiply each pair by a given 2×2 coding matrix to produce the cryptogram. Pass mark: 4 of 5.
- **Layer 3 — Decode.** Find the coding matrix's inverse and use it to decode a cryptogram back into its message; say why a coding matrix with determinant 0 cannot be used. Pass mark: 4 of 5.

## What "maxed" means here

The learner can take a short message, encode it with a 2×2 matrix, hand over only the cryptogram and the matrix, and watch a partner recover the exact message with the inverse — the complete worked round trip Rwanda's Senior Six Advanced book presents.
