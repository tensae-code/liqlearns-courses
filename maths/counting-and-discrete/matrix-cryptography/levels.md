## Sub lesson 1 — Letters into Numbers
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Convert letters to numbers and numbers back to letters with the letter–number table, with J = 10 and K = 11. Pass mark: 4 of 5.

## Sub lesson 2 — Encoding with the Coding Matrix
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Group a short message's numbers in pairs and multiply each pair by a given 2×2 coding matrix to produce the cryptogram. Pass mark: 4 of 5.

## Sub lesson 3 — Decoding with the Inverse
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Find the coding matrix's inverse and use it to decode a cryptogram back into its message; say why a coding matrix with determinant 0 cannot be used. Pass mark: 4 of 5.

## What "maxed" means here

The learner can take a short message, encode it with a 2×2 matrix, hand over only the cryptogram and the matrix, and watch a partner recover the exact message with the inverse — the complete worked round trip Rwanda's Senior Six Advanced book presents.
