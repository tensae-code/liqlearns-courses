# Congruences and Diophantine equations — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Level 1)** The simplest form of 38 in mod 5 is…
a) 8  b) 3  c) 5
**Answer:** b) 3 — 38 = 7×5 + 3 (Sierra Leone, L22, p.62).

**2. (Level 1)** In mod 6, 4 + 5 equals…
a) 9  b) 3  c) 1
**Answer:** b) 3 — 9 reduced mod 6 is 3 (Sierra Leone, L23, p.65).

**3. (Level 2)** The digit sum of 7,254 is 18. Mod 9, 7,254 is congruent to…
a) 0  b) 9  c) 2
**Answer:** a) 0 — a number is congruent mod 9 to its digit sum (Iran, p.23).

**4. (Level 2)** gcd(680, 612), by Euclid's algorithm, is…
a) 34  b) 68  c) 17
**Answer:** b) 68 — 680 = 1×612 + 68 and 612 = 9×68 + 0 (Kazakhstan, Grade 8, starred chapter).

**5. (Level 3)** The equation 4x + 6y = 7 has whole-number solutions…
a) yes, many  b) exactly one  c) none
**Answer:** c) none — gcd(4, 6) = 2 does not divide 7 (Greece, Ch.4).

**6. (Level 3)** One solution of 3y + 4x = 30 in whole numbers is…
a) x = 3, y = 6  b) x = 1, y = 9  c) x = 5, y = 4
**Answer:** a) — 3×6 + 4×3 = 30 (Kazakhstan, Grade 6, §10.1, p.178).
