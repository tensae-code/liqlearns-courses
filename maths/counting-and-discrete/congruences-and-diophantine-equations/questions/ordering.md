Put the steps in the right order.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Order Euclid's algorithm for gcd(252, 198): (a) 54 = 36 + 18, (b) 252 = 198 + 54, (c) 36 = 2×18 + 0 — stop; gcd is 18, (d) 198 = 3×54 + 36.
**Answer:** (b), (d), (a), (c) — always divide the last divisor by the last remainder.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Order the solving of ax + by = c: (a) check gcd(a, b) divides c, (b) find one solution, (c) write the general solution by stepping, (d) keep the solutions the problem allows (e.g. non-negative).
**Answer:** (a), (b), (c), (d) (Iran, pp.26–29; Greece, Ch.4).

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Order a mod-6 addition: (a) reduce 9 to 3, (b) add 4 + 5 = 9, (c) answer 3.
**Answer:** (b), (a), (c) (Sierra Leone, L23, p.65).
