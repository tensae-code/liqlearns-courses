# Congruences and Diophantine equations — fill in the blank

Write the missing word or number.

**1. (Level 1)** 17 ≡ ______ (mod 5), in simplest form.
**Answer:** 2 — 17 = 3×5 + 2 (Sierra Leone, L22, p.62).

**2. (Level 1)** The number m in a ≡ b (mod m) is called the ______.
**Answer:** modulus (Sierra Leone, L21, p.59).

**3. (Level 2)** a ≡ b (mod m) means m divides ______.
**Answer:** a − b (Iran, pp.18–22).

**4. (Level 2)** In Euclid's algorithm, the gcd is the last ______ remainder.
**Answer:** non-zero (Kazakhstan, Grade 8, starred chapter, pp.160–177).

**5. (Level 3)** ax + by = c has whole-number solutions exactly when gcd(a, b) divides ______.
**Answer:** c (Greece, Ch.4, pp.135–184).

**6. (Level 3)** Equations that ask for whole-number solutions are called ______ equations.
**Answer:** Diophantine (Iran, pp.26–29).
