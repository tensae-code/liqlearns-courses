# Video script — Congruences and Diophantine equations

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Divide 17 by 5. You get 3, and **2 left over**.
The left-over is the star of this lesson.

Two numbers with the **same left-over** are the same for us.
17 and 7 both leave 2 when divided by 5.
We write: 17 ≡ 7, mod 5. Same remainder. Same team.

Clocks live here. 13 o'clock is 1 o'clock. Days live here too:
in 100 days, only the remainder by 7 moves the weekday.

Big common divisor? Do not list factors. Use **Euclid**:
divide, keep the remainder, divide again.
Last non-zero remainder — that is your gcd.

Last, the puzzle: 3y + 4x = 30, whole numbers only.
That is a **Diophantine** equation. Check the gcd first.
Then find one answer — and step to all the others.

Remainders rule this lesson. Count what is left over.
