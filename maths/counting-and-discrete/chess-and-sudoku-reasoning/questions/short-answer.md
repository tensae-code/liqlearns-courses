# Short answer — Chess and Sudoku Reasoning

1. (Layer 2) White: Kf6, Qg5. Black: Kh8. Find the mate in one and prove it with the three exits.
   **Answer: 1. Qg7# — the queen checks from g7, beside the king. Move: g8 and h7 are attacked by the queen. Block: impossible against an adjacent queen. Capture: the king cannot take her because the white king on f6 protects g7, and nothing else black exists.** Position constructed for this lesson in the style of the mate-in-one exercises of Bolivia, *Compendio para Maestras y Maestros 2023–2024* (teacher compendium), Secundaria 1 section, printed p. 82.

2. (Layer 3) In the lesson's 4×4 mini-sudoku, the top row is `1 . 3 .` and the second column already holds 4 (row 2) and 3 (row 4). What goes in the top row's second cell, and why?
   **Answer: 2 — the row allows only 2 or 4, the column allows only 1 or 2, and 2 is the only digit in both lists: a single candidate.** Training grid constructed for this lesson; the sudoku strand is Bolivia, *Compendio para Maestras y Maestros 2023–2024* (teacher compendium), Secundaria 1 section, printed p. 82.

3. (Layer 4) White: Kg6, Qb1. Black: Kh8, Ra8. Give a mate in two, with the reason Black has no other reply.
   **Answer: 1. Qb2+ Kg8 2. Qg7#. After the check on the long diagonal, the king's other squares g7 and h7 are covered by the white king on g6, and the rook on a8 can neither block the diagonal (c3, d4, e5, f6, g7 are none of them on its rank or file) nor capture the queen — so Kg8 is forced, and Qg7 is mate because the queen is protected by her king and covers h8 and f8.** Position constructed for this lesson in the style of the mate-in-two puzzles of Bolivia, *Compendio para Maestras y Maestros 2023–2024* (teacher compendium), Secundaria 6 section, printed p. 172.

   **Answer: Secundaria 1 (Chess I and Sudoku), Secundaria 2 (Chess II and Sudoku), Secundaria 5 (Chess V) and Secundaria 6 (Chess VI) — the last adds advanced rules and mate-in-two puzzles.** Bolivia, *Compendio para Maestras y Maestros 2023–2024* (teacher compendium), Secundaria 1 section, printed p. 82; Secundaria 6 section, printed p. 172.
