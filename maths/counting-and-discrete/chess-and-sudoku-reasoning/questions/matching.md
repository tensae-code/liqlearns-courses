1. (Sub lesson 1) Match each piece to its move:
   (a) king
   (b) rook
   (c) bishop
   (d) knight
   (e) queen

   (i) slides along diagonals only
   (ii) steps one square in any direction
   (iii) jumps in an L, over other pieces
   (iv) slides along ranks and files only
   (v) slides any distance along ranks, files and diagonals

   **Answer: (a)–(ii), (b)–(iv), (c)–(i), (d)–(iii), (e)–(v)** — the basic notions of Chess I. Bolivia, *Compendio para Maestras y Maestros 2023–2024* (teacher compendium), Secundaria 1 section, printed p. 82.

2. (Sub lessons 2 and 4) Match each term to its meaning:
   (a) checkmate
   (b) single candidate
   (c) hidden single
   (d) candidate check

   (i) a digit that fits in only one cell of a row, column or box
   (ii) check with no escape by moving, blocking or capturing
   (iii) the method of listing checking moves and testing the king's three exits against each
   (iv) a cell whose row, column and box leave exactly one possible digit

   **Answer: (a)–(ii), (b)–(iv), (c)–(i), (d)–(iii)** — the chess half from Chess I (Secundaria 1, printed p. 82), the sudoku half from the laboratory's Sudoku (Secundaria 2, printed p. 80). Bolivia, *Compendio para Maestras y Maestros 2023–2024* (teacher compendium).
