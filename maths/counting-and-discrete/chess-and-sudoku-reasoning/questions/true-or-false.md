# True or false — Chess and Sudoku Reasoning

1. (Sub lesson 1) A knight moves in an L: two squares in one direction and one square to the side, and it may jump over other pieces.
   **Answer: True** — the knight is the only jumping piece. Bolivia, *Compendio para Maestras y Maestros 2023–2024* (teacher compendium), Secundaria 1 section, printed p. 82 (Chess I, basic notions).

2. (Sub lesson 2) If the defending king can legally capture the piece that is giving check, the position is not checkmate.
   **Answer: True** — capturing the attacker is one of the three exits; mate requires all three to fail. Bolivia, *Compendio para Maestras y Maestros 2023–2024* (teacher compendium), Secundaria 1 section, printed p. 82.

3. (Sub lesson 4) In sudoku, a digit may appear twice inside one 3×3 box as long as it is not repeated in any row or column.
   **Answer: False** — each box, like each row and column, must hold the digits 1 to 9 exactly once. Bolivia, *Compendio para Maestras y Maestros 2023–2024* (teacher compendium), Secundaria 1 section, printed p. 82 (Sudoku).

4. (Sub lesson 3) To prove a mate in two, it is enough to find a mate against the defender's most natural reply.
   **Answer: False** — the proof must cover every legal reply; a single unmated reply refutes the first move. Bolivia, *Compendio para Maestras y Maestros 2023–2024* (teacher compendium), Secundaria 6 section, printed p. 172 (Chess VI, mate-in-two puzzles).

5. (Sub lesson 1) The compendium's mathematics laboratory appears in the Secundaria 3 section as well as in Secundaria 1, 2, 5 and 6.
   **Answer: False** — the read compendium carries the laboratory in four sections only: Secundaria 1, 2, 5 and 6. Bolivia, *Compendio para Maestras y Maestros 2023–2024* (teacher compendium), Secundaria 5 section, printed p. 171.
