1. (Sub lesson 1) What is the *Laboratorio matemático* in Bolivia's Secundaria books?
   (a) a chapter of harder calculations at the end of each unit
   (b) a mathematics laboratory section that sets chess problems and sudoku as logic training
   (c) a list of formulas to memorise before the exam
   (d) the answer key for the year's exercises

   **Answer: (b)** — the strand runs Chess I and Sudoku in Secundaria 1, Chess II and Sudoku in Secundaria 2, Chess V in Secundaria 5 and Chess VI in Secundaria 6. Bolivia, *Compendio para Maestras y Maestros 2023–2024* (teacher compendium), Secundaria 1 section, printed p. 82.

2. (Sub lesson 1) A king is in checkmate when:
   (a) it is attacked by two pieces at once
   (b) it is in check and the player has no pieces left
   (c) it is in check and cannot escape by moving, blocking or capturing
   (d) it has not moved yet in the game

   **Answer: (c)** — mate is check with all three exits closed. Bolivia, *Compendio para Maestras y Maestros 2023–2024* (teacher compendium), Secundaria 1 section, printed p. 82 (Chess I, basic notions).

3. (Sub lesson 2) White: Kg1, Ra1. Black: Kh8, pawns g7 and h7. Which move is checkmate?
   (a) Ra8+
   (b) Rh1+
   (c) Ra7
   (d) Kf2

   **Answer: (a)** — the rook checks along the eighth rank; g8 is guarded by the rook, g7 and h7 are blocked by the king's own pawns, and nothing can block or capture. Position constructed for this lesson in the style of the mate-in-one exercises of Bolivia, *Compendio para Maestras y Maestros 2023–2024* (teacher compendium), Secundaria 1 section, printed p. 82.

4. (Sub lesson 4) In sudoku, a cell is a "single candidate" when:
   (a) it is the only empty cell in its row
   (b) the digits allowed by its row, its column and its box overlap in exactly one digit
   (c) it contains a digit written in pencil
   (d) its box is already complete

   **Answer: (b)** — intersect the three units' possibilities; one survivor means the digit is forced. Bolivia, *Compendio para Maestras y Maestros 2023–2024* (teacher compendium), Secundaria 2 section, printed p. 80 (Sudoku).

5. (Sub lesson 3) In the position White: Kg6, Qb1; Black: Kh8, Ra8, after 1. Qb2+, Black's only legal reply is Kg8 because:
   (a) the rook is pinned against the king
   (b) g7 and h7 are covered by the white king, and the rook can neither block the b2–h8 diagonal nor capture the queen
   (c) Black is not allowed to move the rook while in check
   (d) the queen on b2 also guards g8

   **Answer: (b)** — every other king square is covered, and the rook on a8 reaches none of the diagonal's squares (c3, d4, e5, f6, g7). Position constructed for this lesson in the style of the mate-in-two puzzles of Bolivia, *Compendio para Maestras y Maestros 2023–2024* (teacher compendium), Secundaria 6 section, printed p. 172.
