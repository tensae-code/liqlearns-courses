# Fill in the blank — Chess and Sudoku Reasoning

1. (Layer 1) The chessboard has ___ files and ___ ranks, so every square can be named by a letter and a number.
   **Answer: 8; 8** — files a–h, ranks 1–8. Bolivia, *Compendio para Maestras y Maestros 2023–2024* (teacher compendium), Secundaria 1 section, printed p. 82 (Chess I, basic notions).

2. (Layer 2) In the back-rank mate (White: Kg1, Ra1; Black: Kh8, pawns g7, h7), 1. Ra8 is mate partly because the black king's own ___ on g7 and h7 close two of its escape squares.
   **Answer: pawns** — with the rook guarding g8 along the rank, all three exits fail. Position constructed for this lesson in the style of the mate-in-one exercises of Bolivia, *Compendio para Maestras y Maestros 2023–2024* (teacher compendium), Secundaria 1 section, printed p. 82.

3. (Layer 3) A sudoku cell's candidates are cut down by three units: its row, its column and its ___.
   **Answer: box** — the 3×3 box; the digit that survives all three is forced. Bolivia, *Compendio para Maestras y Maestros 2023–2024* (teacher compendium), Secundaria 2 section, printed p. 80 (Sudoku).

4. (Layer 1) The strand this lesson teaches is called, in Bolivia's books, the Laboratorio ___.
   **Answer: matemático** — the mathematics laboratory of chess and sudoku. Bolivia, *Compendio para Maestras y Maestros 2023–2024* (teacher compendium), Secundaria 1 section, printed p. 82.

5. (Layer 4) White: Kg6, Qb1; Black: Kh8, Ra8. The mate in two runs 1. Qb2+ Kg8 2. ___.
   **Answer: Qg7#** — the queen mates from g7, protected by the king on g6. Position constructed for this lesson in the style of the mate-in-two puzzles of Bolivia, *Compendio para Maestras y Maestros 2023–2024* (teacher compendium), Secundaria 6 section, printed p. 172.
