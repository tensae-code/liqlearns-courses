# Video script — Algebraic structures

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Take a set — a box of things. Take a rule that eats **two** things
and gives back one. That rule is a **binary operation**.
Adding is one. Multiplying is one.

Now ask questions about the rule.
Does the answer **stay in the box**? That is closure.
Does the **order** matter? That is commutativity.
With three things, does the **bracketing** matter? Associativity.

A set with a rule, and the answers to those questions —
that is a **mathematical system**.

One special system is a **group**: the rule is associative,
one element does **nothing** — the identity —
and every element has an **undoer** — its inverse.
If order never matters, the group is **abelian**.

Two rules make a **ring**: adding forms an abelian group,
multiplying is associative, and multiplying **spreads over** adding.
Add inverses for multiplying too, and you have a **field** —
the real numbers are one.

Set, rule, questions, names. That is the whole ladder.
