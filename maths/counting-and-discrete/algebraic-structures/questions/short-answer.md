# Algebraic structures — short answer

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Level 1)** Is subtraction closed on the natural numbers {1, 2, 3, …}? Say why.
**Answer:** No — 2 − 5 leaves the set; closure fails (Yemen, Grade 10, 5-1, p.96).

**2. (Level 1)** An operation's table is symmetric across its main diagonal. Which property does that show?
**Answer:** Commutativity — a * b = b * a everywhere (Yemen, Grade 10, 5-2, p.100).

**3. (Level 2)** In multiplication mod 5 on {1, 2, 3, 4}, what is the inverse of 2?
**Answer:** 3 — 2 × 3 = 6 ≡ 1 (mod 5), and 1 is the identity (Yemen, Grade 10, 5-4, p.110).

**4. (Level 2)** Is (ℤ, −) a group? Name the first axiom that fails.
**Answer:** No — associativity fails: (5 − 3) − 1 = 1 but 5 − (3 − 1) = 3 (Yemen, Grade 10, 5-4, p.110).

**5. (Level 3)** State the distributive demand in Yemen's ring definition.
**Answer:** The second operation ∘ distributes over the first: a ∘ (b * c) = (a ∘ b) * (a ∘ c) (Yemen, Grade 11 Science, 1-2, p.14).

**6. (Level 3)** What does a field have that a ring may lack?
**Answer:** Multiplicative inverses for every non-zero element, so division (except by zero) is possible (Yemen, Grade 11 Science, 1-3, p.21).
