Match each term to its meaning.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Match: (a) identity, (b) inverse, (c) closure — with: (i) answers stay in the set, (ii) the element that changes nothing, (iii) the element that undoes another back to the identity.
**Answer:** (a)–(ii), (b)–(iii), (c)–(i) (Yemen, Grade 10, 5-1–5-4, pp.96–110).

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Match: (a) group, (b) ring, (c) field — with: (i) two operations, the second distributing over an abelian-group first, (ii) one associative operation with identity and inverses, (iii) a ring whose non-zero elements all have inverses under the second operation.
**Answer:** (a)–(ii), (b)–(i), (c)–(iii) (Yemen, Grade 10, 5-4, p.110; Grade 11 Science, 1-2, p.14; 1-3, p.21).
