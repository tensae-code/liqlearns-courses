**Acceptance count: taught in 3 countries (China, Syria, India).** China is the sole source, as filed: its Grade 5 second volume carries the balance search for the defective product with its 3ⁿ guarantee table (p.111), the phone-call notification tree with its doubling 2ⁿ growth (p.102), and the painted-cube exploration (p.44); its Grade 6 second volume's general review carries segment counting n(n−1)/2 among its mathematical-thinking exhibits (pp.100–104). No other read book files these four investigations together as a topic.

Syria's investigations are its magic squares — additive in Grade 7, multiplicative in Grade 8 — and its Archimedes crown opener in Grade 9.

India enters mathematical investigations from its Class 5 Math-Magic pattern chapters: pentomino puzzles — all 12 five-square shapes, their areas and differing perimeters, and tiling rectangles with them — magic squares, magic hexagons whose boxes hold the product of the numbers in the two circles next to them, and palindromic numbers built by reversing and adding.
