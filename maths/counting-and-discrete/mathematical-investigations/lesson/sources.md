## Fiji added 2026-11-11 (expansion merge)

The 2026-11-10 expansion read read Fiji's whole in-hand Maths set from the PDFs — **13 pupil textbooks of the Ministry of Education / Curriculum Development Unit series, one for each of Years 1–13** — and placed 1,012 items. The expansion map's count is exact: staging `books.json` holds exactly 13 Mathematics entries (one per year, all `kind: student`) and `phase3-push-state.json` lists all 13 as pushed; no Maths teacher guide is in hand (the AQEP Maths Guides for Lower Primary exist only as AnyFlip reader copies in the download list — not in hand, not counted).
