# Mathematical investigations — matching

Match each investigation to its rule.

**1. (Level 1)** Match: (a) 3 products, (b) 9 products, (c) 81 products — with: (i) 4 weighings, (ii) 1 weighing, (iii) 2 weighings.
**Answer:** (a)–(ii), (b)–(iii), (c)–(i) (China, Grade 5, p.111).

**2. (Level 3)** Match: (a) corner cube, (b) edge cube, (c) centre cube — with: (i) 0 painted faces, (ii) 3 painted faces, (iii) 2 painted faces.
**Answer:** (a)–(ii), (b)–(iii), (c)–(i) (China, Grade 5, p.44).
