# Mathematical investigations — short answer

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Level 1)** Among 27 products one is lighter. How many weighings guarantee finding it?
**Answer:** 3 — 27 = 3³, three rounds of splitting in three (China, Grade 5, p.111).

**2. (Level 1)** In the first weighing of 9 coins (3–3–3), the balance stays level. Where is the fake?
**Answer:** In the pile of 3 left aside (China, Grade 5, p.111).

**3. (Level 2)** A message must reach at least 100 people by the phone tree. What is the smallest number of minutes?
**Answer:** 7 — 2⁶ = 64 is too few; 2⁷ = 128 reaches 100 (China, Grade 5, p.102).

**4. (Level 3)** In a painted 4×4×4 cube, how many small cubes have no painted face at all?
**Answer:** 8 — the inside 2×2×2 block (China, Grade 5, p.44).

**5. (Level 3)** Count the segments for 6 points on a line, as a sum.
**Answer:** 5 + 4 + 3 + 2 + 1 = 15 segments (China, Grade 6, pp.100–104).

**6. (Level 3)** In a painted 3×3×3 cube, how many small cubes have exactly 1 painted face?
**Answer:** 6 — one face-middle cube on each of the 6 faces (China, Grade 5, p.44).
