# Mathematical investigations — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — The Balance Search · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — The Phone-Call Tree · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Painted Cubes and Counting Segments.

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** One defective product is lighter among 9. The guaranteed number of balance weighings to find it is…
a) 1  b) 2  c) 3
**Answer:** b) 2 — split in three, twice (China, Grade 5, p.111).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** With 4 weighings, the balance search can guarantee finding one lighter product among up to…
a) 27  b) 81  c) 40
**Answer:** b) 81 — 3⁴ = 81 (China, Grade 5, p.111).

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** In the phone-call tree, after 5 minutes the number of people who know is…
a) 10  b) 25  c) 32
**Answer:** c) 32 — 2⁵ = 32 (China, Grade 5, p.102).

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A phone tree has run for some minutes and 64 people know. The minutes run were…
a) 6  b) 8  c) 32
**Answer:** a) 6 — 2⁶ = 64 (China, Grade 5, p.102).

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** In a painted 3×3×3 cube, the number of small cubes with exactly 2 painted faces is…
a) 6  b) 8  c) 12
**Answer:** c) 12 — the edge cubes that are not corners (China, Grade 5, p.44).

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** The number of segments determined by 8 points on a line is…
a) 28  b) 16  c) 56
**Answer:** a) 28 — 8×7/2 = 28 (China, Grade 6, pp.100–104).
