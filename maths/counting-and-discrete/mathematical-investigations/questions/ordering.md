# Mathematical investigations — ordering

Put the steps in the right order.

**1. (Level 1)** Order the balance search for 9 coins: (a) weigh 1 against 1 from the guilty pile of 3, (b) weigh 3 against 3 with 3 aside, (c) name the fake.
**Answer:** (b), (a), (c) (China, Grade 5, p.111).

**2. (Level 2)** Order the phone tree's totals: (a) 8 know, (b) 2 know, (c) 16 know, (d) 4 know.
**Answer:** (b), (d), (a), (c) — minutes 1, 2, 3, 4 (China, Grade 5, p.102).
