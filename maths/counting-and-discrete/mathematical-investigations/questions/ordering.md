# Mathematical investigations — ordering

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — The Balance Search · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — The Phone-Call Tree · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Painted Cubes and Counting Segments.

Put the steps in the right order.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Order the balance search for 9 coins: (a) weigh 1 against 1 from the guilty pile of 3, (b) weigh 3 against 3 with 3 aside, (c) name the fake.
**Answer:** (b), (a), (c) (China, Grade 5, p.111).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Order the phone tree's totals: (a) 8 know, (b) 2 know, (c) 16 know, (d) 4 know.
**Answer:** (b), (d), (a), (c) — minutes 1, 2, 3, 4 (China, Grade 5, p.102).
