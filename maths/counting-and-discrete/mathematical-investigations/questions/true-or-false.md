# Mathematical investigations — true or false

Say true or false, and why.

**1. (Level 1)** Splitting the products in two is the best balance strategy.
**Answer:** False — the balance has three outcomes, so splitting in three searches faster (China, Grade 5, p.111).

**2. (Level 1)** Three weighings can guarantee finding one lighter product among 30.
**Answer:** False — 3 weighings guarantee up to 27; 30 needs 4 (China, Grade 5, p.111).

**3. (Level 2)** After 10 minutes of the phone tree, 20 people know.
**Answer:** False — 2¹⁰ = 1,024 know; the growth doubles, it does not add 2 (China, Grade 5, p.102).

**4. (Level 3)** Every small cube of a painted 3×3×3 cube has at least one painted face.
**Answer:** False — the centre cube has none (China, Grade 5, p.44).

**5. (Level 3)** Doubling the number of points on a line doubles the number of segments.
**Answer:** False — the count is n(n−1)/2, which grows faster than doubling (China, Grade 6, pp.100–104).

**6. (Level 2)** In the phone tree, the person told first is counted among those who know after n minutes.
**Answer:** True — the 2ⁿ includes everyone told so far, starting from the first knower (China, Grade 5, p.102).
