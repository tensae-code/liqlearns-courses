# Mathematical investigations — fill in the blank

Write the missing word or number.

**1. (Level 1)** The balance search splits the products into ______ groups.
**Answer:** three — matching the balance's three outcomes (China, Grade 5, p.111).

**2. (Level 1)** Up to ______ products can be searched with 3 weighings.
**Answer:** 27 — 3³ (China, Grade 5, p.111).

**3. (Level 2)** After n minutes of the phone tree, ______ people know the message.
**Answer:** 2ⁿ (China, Grade 5, p.102).

**4. (Level 2)** Each minute, the number who know ______.
**Answer:** doubles (China, Grade 5, p.102).

**5. (Level 3)** A corner small cube of a painted big cube has ______ painted faces.
**Answer:** 3 (China, Grade 5, p.44).

**6. (Level 3)** n points on a line determine n(n − 1)/______ segments.
**Answer:** 2 (China, Grade 6, pp.100–104).
