# Video script — Mathematical investigations

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Nine coins. One is **lighter**. You have a balance.
How few weighings find the fake?

Split in **three**: three, three, three.
Three on the left, three on the right, three aside.
It tips? The fake is in the light pile.
It stays level? The fake is in the pile aside.
One more weighing finds it. **Two weighings for nine.** Three splits win.

Now a message. One person calls one person. Then each calls one more.
Minute 1: two know. Minute 2: four. Minute 3: eight.
After n minutes, **2 to the n** know. Doubling is fast.

Paint a big cube, cut it into small cubes.
Corners have **3** painted faces. Edges, **2**. Face middles, **1**.
The one in the centre? **None**. Its paint never touched it.

Points on a line: 5 points make 10 segments — 4 + 3 + 2 + 1.
Split it, tabulate it, spot the pattern. That is investigating.
