# Graph theory — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Level 1)** A graph has vertices of degrees 3, 3, 2, 2, 2. How many edges does it have?
a) 12  b) 6  c) 5
**Answer:** b) 6 — the degrees sum to 12, and the sum is twice the number of edges (Iran, p.40).

**2. (Level 1)** The complete graph K₅ has how many edges?
a) 10  b) 5  c) 25
**Answer:** a) 10 — each pair of the 5 vertices is joined: 5×4/2 = 10 (Iran, pp.34–42).

**3. (Level 2)** A connected graph has exactly two vertices of odd degree. An Euler trail in it must…
a) not exist  b) start at one odd vertex and end at the other  c) start and end at the same vertex
**Answer:** b) — the two-odd-vertices case (Vietnam, Bài 9, p.41).

**4. (Level 2)** Which walk uses every VERTEX exactly once?
a) an Euler path  b) a Hamilton path  c) a cycle
**Answer:** b) — Hamilton is about vertices; Euler is about edges (Vietnam, Bài 9, p.41).

**5. (Level 3)** Bins hold 10. Items arrive in the order 6, 5, 4. By first-fit, where does the 4 go?
a) into a new bin  b) with the 6  c) with the 5
**Answer:** b) — the first bin (with 6) has exactly room for 4 (Jordan, p.68).

**6. (Level 3)** A graph has n = 12 vertices and Δ = 3. The domination bound gives γ(G) ≥ …
a) 3  b) 4  c) 12
**Answer:** a) 3 — ⌈12/(3+1)⌉ = 3 (Iran, p.49).
