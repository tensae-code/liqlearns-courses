# Graph theory — short answer

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Level 1)** A vertex has a loop and two ordinary edges. What is its degree?
**Answer:** 4 — the loop counts twice, plus the two edges (Iran, pp.34–37).

**2. (Level 1)** K_p has 36 edges. Find p.
**Answer:** p = 9 — p(p−1)/2 = 36 gives p = 9 (Iran, exercises pp.41–42).

**3. (Level 2)** The Königsberg graph has four vertices, all of odd degree. Can an Euler trail cross every bridge once? Say why.
**Answer:** No — an Euler trail needs zero or two odd-degree vertices (Iran, pp.32–33; Vietnam, Bài 9, p.41).

**4. (Level 2)** Roads from A: A–B = 4, A–C = 2, C–B = 1, B–D = 3, C–D = 5. What is the shortest route from A to D, and its length?
**Answer:** A–C–B–D, length 2+1+3 = 6 (Vietnam, Bài 10, p.46).

**5. (Level 3)** For the path on 6 vertices P₆, what is γ(P₆)?
**Answer:** 2 — vertices 2 and 5 dominate all six; one vertex covers at most 3 (Iran, pp.52–54).

**6. (Level 3)** When is γ(G) = 1?
**Answer:** Exactly when some vertex is joined to every other vertex, so it alone dominates the graph (Iran, pp.52–54).
