# Video script — Graph theory

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Draw dots. Join the dots with lines.
That is a **graph**. The dots are places. The lines are roads.

Count the lines at one dot. That number is its **degree**.
Add all the degrees. You get **two times** the number of lines. Always.

Now walk. Use every **line** once — that is an **Euler** walk.
Visit every **dot** once — that is a **Hamilton** walk.
E for edges. H visits homes.

Shortest road? Try the roads, add the lengths, keep the smallest total.

Packing boxes? Put each thing in the **first** box with room. That is first-fit.

Last: the guards. Put a guard on a dot.
The guard watches that dot **and** its neighbours.
How few guards watch the whole graph? That number is **gamma**.

Dots, lines, walks, guards. That is graph theory.
