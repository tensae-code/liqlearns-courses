# Latin squares — matching

Match each term to its meaning.

**1. (Level 1)** Match: (a) order, (b) symbol, (c) Latin square — with: (i) one of the entries used in the grid, (ii) the side length of the grid, (iii) a grid with each symbol once per row and column.
**Answer:** (a)–(ii), (b)–(i), (c)–(iii) (Iran, pp.62–71).

**2. (Level 3)** Match: (a) orthogonal pair, (b) permuting symbols, (c) work scheduling — with: (i) renaming symbols everywhere, (ii) two squares whose overlaid pairs are all different, (iii) the book's application of an orthogonal pair.
**Answer:** (a)–(ii), (b)–(i), (c)–(iii) (Iran, pp.62–71).
