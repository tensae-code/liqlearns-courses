# Latin squares — fill in the blank

Write the missing word or number.

**1. (Level 1)** In a Latin square, each symbol appears exactly ______ in every row and every column.
**Answer:** once (Iran, pp.62–71).

**2. (Level 1)** A Latin square of order 5 has ______ cells.
**Answer:** 25 — it is a 5×5 grid (Iran, pp.62–71).

**3. (Level 2)** Renaming the symbols of a Latin square is called ______ the symbols.
**Answer:** permuting (Iran, pp.62–71).

**4. (Level 3)** Two Latin squares are orthogonal when all the ______ formed by laying one on the other are different.
**Answer:** pairs (Iran, pp.62–71).

**5. (Level 1)** Building by the shift method, the second row is the first row shifted by ______ step.
**Answer:** one (Iran, pp.62–71).
