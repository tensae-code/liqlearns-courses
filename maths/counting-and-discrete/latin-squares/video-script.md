# Video script — Latin squares

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Here is a square, 3 by 3. Fill it with A, B, C.
One rule: **each letter once in every row, and once in every column**.

Row one: A, B, C. Row two: slide them — B, C, A.
Row three: slide again — C, A, B. Done. That is a **Latin square**.

Rename the letters? Still a Latin square. Same pattern, new dress.

Now take **two** Latin squares. Lay one on top of the other.
Read the pairs. If every pair is **different** — all 9 pairs, no repeats —
the two squares are **orthogonal**. They fit together perfectly.

Farmers and timetables use these pairs:
every pairing happens **exactly once**. Neat, and fair.

One rule, two squares, no repeats. That is the Latin square.
