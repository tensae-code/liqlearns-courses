# Inclusion–Exclusion and Venn Counting — fill in the blank

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Two Sets and the Venn Regions · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Three Sets, Neither and Only · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Survey Problems and Onto Functions.

Fill each blank with the missing number or word.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** n(A∪B) = n(A) + n(B) − ______.
**Answer:** n(A∩B) — the overlap, counted twice, is removed once.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Two overlapping circles divide the plane's surveyed world into ______ regions, counting the outside.
**Answer:** 4 — A only, B only, both, neither.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** "Only A" = n(A) − ______.
**Answer:** n(A∩B) — take the shared part out of A's total.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Three overlapping circles make ______ regions, counting the outside.
**Answer:** 8 — all three, three pair-only, three single-only, none (Sierra Leone, SSS1, L64–70).

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** In the three-set formula the triple overlap is added back exactly ______ time(s).
**Answer:** 1 (once) — it was added 3 times and subtracted 3 times.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** All functions from a 4-element set to a 3-element set number 3⁴ = ______; the onto ones are fewer.
**Answer:** 81 — the onto count is 81 − 48 + 3 = 36 (Iran, Discrete, pp.73–78).
