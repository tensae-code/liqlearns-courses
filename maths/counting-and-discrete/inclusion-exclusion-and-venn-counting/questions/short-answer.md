# Inclusion–Exclusion and Venn Counting — short answer

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** State the two-set inclusion–exclusion formula.
**Answer:** n(A∪B) = n(A) + n(B) − n(A∩B) (Laos, Grade 10, Ch.II L5).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** n(A∪B) = 45, n(A) = 30, n(A∩B) = 5. Find n(B).
**Answer:** 20 — 45 = 30 + n(B) − 5, so n(B) = 20.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** A survey of 60 finds 41 in A∪B. How many are in neither?
**Answer:** 19 — 60 − 41.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** n(B) = 18, n(A∩B) = 7. How many are in B only?
**Answer:** 11 — the overlap removed from the group total.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** In Nepal's filed survey example, what is the corrected "neither" count?
**Answer:** 18 — taught corrected in this lesson, per the filing.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Three sets: singles add to 75, pair overlaps add to 25, triple overlap is 4. Find the union.
**Answer:** 54 — 75 − 25 + 4 (Sierra Leone, SSS1, L64–70 shape).

**7. (Sub lesson 3)** How many integers from 1 to 100 are divisible by 2 or by 5?
**Answer:** 60 — 50 + 20 − 10; the multiples of 10 were counted twice.

**8. (Sub lesson 3)** How many functions from a 3-element set to a 2-element set are onto?
**Answer:** 6 — 2³ − 2 × 1³ = 8 − 2 (Iran, Discrete, pp.73–78 method).
