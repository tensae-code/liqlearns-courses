# Inclusion–Exclusion and Venn Counting — matching

Match each description (1–4) with its region or value (a–d). Use n(A) = 24, n(B) = 18, n(A∩B) = 9, surveyed group 50.

**1. (Level 1)** In A or B (or both).
**Answer:** b) 33 — 24 + 18 − 9.

**2. (Level 1)** In neither.
**Answer:** d) 17 — 50 − 33.

**3. (Level 2)** In A only.
**Answer:** a) 15 — 24 − 9.

**4. (Level 2)** In B only.
**Answer:** c) 9 — 18 − 9; equal to the overlap here by the numbers given, not by rule.

Values: a) 15  b) 33  c) 9  d) 17
