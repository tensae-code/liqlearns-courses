# Inclusion–Exclusion and Venn Counting — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Level 1)** n(A) = 15, n(B) = 12, n(A∩B) = 5. What is n(A∪B)?
a) 27  b) 22  c) 32
**Answer:** b) 22 — 15 + 12 − 5; the overlap is taken away once (Laos, Grade 10, pp.38–48).

**2. (Level 1)** In a Venn diagram, which region do you fill first?
a) the outside  b) the "only A" crescent  c) the overlap
**Answer:** c) the overlap — every other region is built by subtracting from it.

**3. (Level 1)** A group of 40: 25 in A, and n(A∪B) = 33. How many are in neither set?
a) 7  b) 15  c) 8
**Answer:** a) 7 — the whole group minus the in-either count: 40 − 33.

**4. (Level 2)** n(A) = 20, n(A∩B) = 6. How many are in A only?
a) 26  b) 14  c) 20
**Answer:** b) 14 — the group total with the overlap removed.

**5. (Level 2)** In the three-set formula, after subtracting the three pair overlaps, the triple overlap must be:
a) subtracted again  b) ignored  c) added back once
**Answer:** c) added back once — it was added three times and subtracted three times, so it vanished (Sierra Leone, SSS3, L121).

**6. (Level 2)** n(A) = 10, n(B) = 10, n(C) = 10, each pair overlap 3, triple overlap 1, no other overlaps counted wrongly — what is n(A∪B∪C)?
a) 30  b) 22  c) 21
**Answer:** b) 22 — 30 − 9 + 1.

**7. (Level 3)** Of 50 surveyed, 30 like tea, 25 like coffee, 12 like both. How many like neither? (Compare Nepal's survey shape, Grades 8–10.)
a) 7  b) 5  c) 12
**Answer:** a) 7 — in either: 30 + 25 − 12 = 43; neither: 50 − 43 = 7.

**8. (Level 3)** The number of onto functions from a 4-element set to a 3-element set is:
a) 81  b) 36  c) 64
**Answer:** b) 36 — 3⁴ − 3 × 2⁴ + 3 × 1⁴ = 81 − 48 + 3 (Iran, Discrete, pp.73–78).
