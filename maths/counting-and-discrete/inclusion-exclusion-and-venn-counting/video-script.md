# Video script — Inclusion–Exclusion and Venn Counting

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Count group A. Count group B. Add them.
But wait — the middle was counted **twice**!
Take the overlap away, **once**.
That is inclusion–exclusion!

Draw the circles. Fill the **middle first**.
Then the only-parts. Then the outside — the **neithers**.

Three circles? Add the singles,
take away the pairs,
and put the middle back **once**.

Nothing counted twice. Nothing lost.
Now you. Draw the circles. Count!
