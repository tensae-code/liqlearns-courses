**Acceptance count: taught in 1 country (North Macedonia).** North Macedonia is the one read country whose course names multiplicative functions and Euler's totient: its Grade 12 Algebra elective teaches the concept in §2.2 (printed p. 28) and Euler's function in §2.5 (printed p. 37) — its definition as the size of a reduced residue system, the table of values to 12, φ(p) = p − 1, and multiplicativity, which this lesson teaches corrected, since the printed Theorem 1 drops the coprimality condition its own proof assumes and its own table contradicts. The rest of the same book's number-theory theme — congruences, residue systems, the theorems of Euler, Fermat and Wilson, Diophantine equations (§§2.3–2.4, 2.6–2.9) — lives in the congruences-and-diophantine-equations lesson, which carries no φ; no other read book teaches the totient, so under the everything-goes-in ruling (2026-11-06) the topic gets its own lesson, built at the size of its source.
