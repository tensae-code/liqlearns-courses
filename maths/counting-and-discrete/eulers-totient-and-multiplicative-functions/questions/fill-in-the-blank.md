# Fill in the blank — Euler's Totient and Multiplicative Functions

1. (Layer 1) Euler's function φ(m) counts the numbers from 1 to m that are ______ to m.
   **Answer: coprime.** North Macedonia, АЛГЕБРА ЗА ТРЕТА ГОДИНА (Grade 12, elective), §2.5, printed p. 37.

2. (Layer 2) For a prime p, φ(p) = ______.
   **Answer: p − 1.** North Macedonia, АЛГЕБРА ЗА ТРЕТА ГОДИНА (Grade 12, elective), §2.5, printed p. 37.

3. (Layer 2) In the book's table, φ(9) = ______.
   **Answer: 6** — the coprime numbers are 1, 2, 4, 5, 7, 8. North Macedonia, АЛГЕБРА ЗА ТРЕТА ГОДИНА (Grade 12, elective), §2.5, printed p. 37.

4. (Layer 3) Multiplicativity splits f(mn) into f(m)·f(n) whenever m and n are ______.
   **Answer: coprime (gcd(m, n) = 1).** North Macedonia, АЛГЕБРА ЗА ТРЕТА ГОДИНА (Grade 12, elective), §2.2, printed p. 28.

5. (Layer 4) The pair that exposes the printed Theorem 1 is m = n = 2: φ(4) = 2, but φ(2)·φ(2) = ______.
   **Answer: 1.** North Macedonia, АЛГЕБРА ЗА ТРЕТА ГОДИНА (Grade 12, elective), §2.5, printed p. 37.
