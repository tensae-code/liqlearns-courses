1. (Sub lesson 2) φ(10) is:
   A. 3 B. 4 C. 5 D. 9
   **Answer: B** — the numbers from 1 to 10 coprime to 10 are 1, 3, 7, 9. North Macedonia, АЛГЕБРА ЗА ТРЕТА ГОДИНА (Grade 12, elective), §2.5, printed p. 37.

2. (Sub lesson 3) φ(11) is:
   A. 5 B. 10 C. 11 D. 1
   **Answer: B** — 11 is prime, and φ(p) = p − 1. North Macedonia, АЛГЕБРА ЗА ТРЕТА ГОДИНА (Grade 12, elective), §2.5, printed p. 37.

3. (Sub lesson 1) A function f is multiplicative when f(mn) = f(m)·f(n):
   A. for all m, n B. for m = n C. for coprime m, n D. for prime m, n only
   **Answer: C** — the coprimality condition is part of the concept; nothing is promised when m and n share a factor. North Macedonia, АЛГЕБРА ЗА ТРЕТА ГОДИНА (Grade 12, elective), §2.2, printed p. 28.

4. (Sub lesson 5) The printed claim "φ(m·n) = φ(m)·φ(n) for all m, n" is refuted by the book's own table at:
   A. m = 3, n = 4 B. m = n = 2 C. m = 2, n = 5 D. m = 1, n = 7
   **Answer: B** — φ(4) = 2 but φ(2)·φ(2) = 1·1 = 1; the other pairs are coprime, where the split honestly holds. North Macedonia, АЛГЕБРА ЗА ТРЕТА ГОДИНА (Grade 12, elective), §2.5, printed p. 37.

5. (Sub lesson 4) φ(36) is:
   A. 6 B. 8 C. 12 D. 18
   **Answer: C** — split into coprime factors: φ(36) = φ(4)·φ(9) = 2·6 = 12. North Macedonia, АЛГЕБРА ЗА ТРЕТА ГОДИНА (Grade 12, elective), §2.5, printed p. 37.
