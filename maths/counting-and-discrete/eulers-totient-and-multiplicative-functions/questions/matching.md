1. (Sub lessons 2–3) Match each number to its totient:
   (a) 2
   (b) 6
   (c) 10
   (d) 9
   (e) 11

   (i) φ = 10
   (ii) φ = 4
   (iii) φ = 2
   (iv) φ = 1
   (v) φ = 6

   **Answer: (a)–(iv), (b)–(iii), (c)–(ii), (d)–(v), (e)–(i)** — all values from the book's table; (e) also follows from the prime rule φ(p) = p − 1. North Macedonia, АЛГЕБРА ЗА ТРЕТА ГОДИНА (Grade 12, elective), §2.5, printed p. 37.

2. (Sub lessons 3–5) Match each claim to its verdict:
   (a) φ(12) = φ(3)·φ(4)
   (b) φ(16) = φ(2)·φ(8)
   (c) φ(7) = 6
   (d) φ(4) = φ(2)·φ(2)

   (i) legal — the factors are coprime
   (ii) not licensed — the factors share a factor
   (iii) true — prime rule φ(p) = p − 1
   (iv) false as numbers — 2 ≠ 1, from the table

   **Answer: (a)–(i), (b)–(ii), (c)–(iii), (d)–(iv).** North Macedonia, АЛГЕБРА ЗА ТРЕТА ГОДИНА (Grade 12, elective), §§2.2 and 2.5, printed pp. 28 and 37.
