# True or false — Euler's Totient and Multiplicative Functions

1. (Sub lesson 2) φ(m) counts the numbers from 1 to m that are coprime to m.
   **Answer: True** — that is the definition: the count of the elements of a reduced residue system mod m. North Macedonia, АЛГЕБРА ЗА ТРЕТА ГОДИНА (Grade 12, elective), §2.5, printed p. 37.

2. (Sub lesson 3) φ(7) = 7.
   **Answer: False** — 7 is prime, so φ(7) = 7 − 1 = 6, as the book's table shows. North Macedonia, АЛГЕБРА ЗА ТРЕТА ГОДИНА (Grade 12, elective), §2.5, printed p. 37.

3. (Sub lesson 4) φ(12) = φ(3)·φ(4) is a legal use of multiplicativity.
   **Answer: True** — gcd(3, 4) = 1, so the split holds: 2·2 = 4 = φ(12). North Macedonia, АЛГЕБРА ЗА ТРЕТА ГОДИНА (Grade 12, elective), §2.5, printed p. 37.

4. (Sub lesson 5) The book's Theorem 1, exactly as printed, is correct: φ(m·n) = φ(m)·φ(n) for all m, n.
   **Answer: False** — taught corrected: the printed statement drops the coprimality hypothesis; φ(4) = 2 ≠ φ(2)·φ(2) = 1 from the book's own table, and the book's proof assumes gcd(m, n) = 1. North Macedonia, АЛГЕБРА ЗА ТРЕТА ГОДИНА (Grade 12, elective), §2.5, printed p. 37.

5. (Sub lesson 2) φ(1) = 1.
   **Answer: True** — the first entry of the table: the single number 1 is coprime to 1. North Macedonia, АЛГЕБРА ЗА ТРЕТА ГОДИНА (Grade 12, elective), §2.5, printed p. 37.
