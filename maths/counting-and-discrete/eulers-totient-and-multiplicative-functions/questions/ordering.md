# Ordering — Euler's Totient and Multiplicative Functions

1. (Layers 3–5) Put the steps for computing φ(36) by splitting in order:
   (a) split 36 = 4 × 9 and check gcd(4, 9) = 1
   (b) read φ(4) = 2 and φ(9) = 6 from the table
   (c) conclude φ(36) = 12 without listing 36 numbers
   (d) multiply the two values

   **Answer: (a), (b), (d), (c)** — the split must be coprime before multiplicativity applies. North Macedonia, АЛГЕБРА ЗА ТРЕТА ГОДИНА (Grade 12, elective), §2.5, printed p. 37.

2. (Layers 1–2) Put these totients in order, smallest first: (i) φ(7) (ii) φ(12) (iii) φ(11) (iv) φ(6).
   **Answer: (iv), (ii), (i), (iii)** — φ(6) = 2, φ(12) = 4, φ(7) = 6, φ(11) = 10, from the book's table and the prime rule. North Macedonia, АЛГЕБРА ЗА ТРЕТА ГОДИНА (Grade 12, elective), §2.5, printed p. 37.
