## Sub lesson 1 — Multiplicative Functions
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: state multiplicativity correctly — f(mn) = f(m)·f(n) for coprime m, n. (This is the definition half of old Layer 3; its pass mark splits here and at Sub lesson 4.)

## Sub lesson 2 — Euler's Function: The Count and the Table
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: say what φ(m) counts — the numbers from 1 to m coprime to m, the elements of a reduced residue system — and find φ(m) for small m by listing (old Layer 1), and read values off the book's table φ(1)…φ(12) (the table half of old Layer 2).

## Sub lesson 3 — Euler's Function at a Prime
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: use φ(p) = p − 1 at primes. (This is the prime half of old Layer 2; its pass mark splits here and at Sub lesson 2.)

## Sub lesson 4 — Computing by Splitting
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: compute φ of a composite from a coprime split (the computing half of old Layer 3), including bigger values beyond the table by splitting into coprime factors whose values are known, e.g. φ(36) = φ(4)·φ(9) = 12 (old Layer 5).

## Sub lesson 5 — The Printed Slip — and the Repair
Sub lesson 5 questions from the type files. Show, from the table, why φ does NOT split over non-coprime factors: φ(4) = 2 against φ(2)·φ(2) = 1; name the hypothesis the printed Theorem 1 dropped. Pass mark: 4 of 5. (Was depth Layer 4.)

## What "maxed" means here

The learner can define a multiplicative function with its coprimality condition, can say what Euler's φ counts, can read and extend its table, can use φ(p) = p − 1 and the coprime split to compute values without listing, and can catch the book's own printed overstatement using the book's own table — the exact skills North Macedonia's Grade 12 Algebra book teaches in §§2.2 and 2.5 (printed pp. 28 and 37), with Theorem 1 taught corrected.
