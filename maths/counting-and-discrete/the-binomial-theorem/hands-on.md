# Hands-on — The Binomial Theorem

The lesson built the triangle by hand and read expansions off it.

## Build the triangle
On squared paper, write row 0 (just 1) at the top. Build each next row: 1 at both ends, every middle entry the sum of the two above it. Build to row 8. This is the pattern work of Indonesia's Grade 8 K13 book (Bab 1, pp.1–40) and the triangle Indonesia's Grade 12 Revisi book prints inside its binomial lesson (pp.113–118).

## Multiply and compare
Multiply out (a+b)², then (a+b)³ = (a+b)(a+b)², by hand. Write only the coefficients in a row: 1, 2, 1 and 1, 3, 3, 1. Hold them against your triangle — the rows match. The triangle is the multiplication, remembered.

## Coin rows
Toss 4 coins 32 times and tally how many heads each toss gives (0 to 4). The tallies bunch near the middle, in the shape of the row 1, 4, 6, 4, 1 — the coefficients are counting the ways each head-count can happen, C(4,k) for k heads.

## Approximate a root
Use the series (1+x)^(1/2) ≈ 1 + x/2 − x²/8 with x = 0.02 to estimate √1.02. Then square your answer on a calculator and see how close you land to 1.02. This is the closing-part method of Pakistan's Class 11 book (pp.158–159), valid because |x| < 1.
