# Video script — The Binomial Theorem

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

One. One, one. One, two, one.
One, three, three, one!
Each number is the two above it, added.
That is **Pascal's triangle**!

Now (a + b) to the power n.
The coefficients? Read the triangle!
The powers of a fall. The powers of b climb.
They always add to n.

One named term? Use the **general term** —
you do not need the whole expansion!

And when the power is a fraction, or below zero,
the expansion never ends — a **series**,
safe only when x is between −1 and 1.

Now you. Build the triangle. Expand!
