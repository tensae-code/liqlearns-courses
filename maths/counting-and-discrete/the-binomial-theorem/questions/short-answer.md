# The Binomial Theorem — short answer

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Level 1)** Write row 5 of Pascal's triangle.
**Answer:** 1, 5, 10, 10, 5, 1 — each entry the sum of the two above (Indonesia, Grade 12 Revisi, pp.113–118).

**2. (Level 1)** How many terms has the expansion of (a+b)⁷?
**Answer:** 8 — a whole power n gives n+1 terms.

**3. (Level 2)** Expand (a+b)².
**Answer:** a² + 2ab + b² — row 2 coefficients, powers falling and climbing.

**4. (Level 2)** State the binomial theorem for (a+b)ⁿ in Σ form. (Yemen's Grade 12 Science book prints it at p.65.)
**Answer:** (a+b)ⁿ = Σ C(n,k) aⁿ⁻ᵏ bᵏ for k = 0 to n.

**5. (Level 2)** What is the coefficient of x⁴ in (1+x)⁶?
**Answer:** 15 — C(6,4) = 15.

**6. (Level 3)** Find the middle term of (a+b)⁴.
**Answer:** 6a²b² — the third of five terms, C(4,2) = 6.

**7. (Level 3)** Write (1+x)^(1/2) as a series up to the x² term.
**Answer:** 1 + x/2 − x²/8 — from the general series with n = 1/2 (Pakistan, pp.158–159).

**8. (Level 3)** Using that series, estimate √1.02 to 3 decimal places.
**Answer:** ≈ 1.010 — with x = 0.02: 1 + 0.01 − 0.00005 ≈ 1.00995.

**9. (Level 3)** The term free of x in (x² − 1/x)¹⁵ is the 11th term. What is its value? (Iraq, Grade 12 Literary, Ch.1, pp.26–29.)
**Answer:** 3003 — the x-powers cancel in that term, leaving the number 3003.

**10. (Level 3)** Use the first three terms of (1 + x)⁵ to estimate (1.04)⁵. (Kazakhstan, Soltan Grade 9, §11, p.82.)
**Answer:** ≈ 1.216 — 1 + 5(0.04) + 10(0.04)² = 1 + 0.2 + 0.016.
