# The Binomial Theorem — true or false

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Pascal's Triangle · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — The Theorem and Full Expansions · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — The General Term and the Binomial Series.

Say true or false, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** True or false: row 3 of Pascal's triangle is 1, 3, 3, 1.
**Answer:** True — 3 = 1 + 2 on both sides of the row.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** True or false: Indonesia's Grade 12 Merdeka (2022) book teaches Pascal's triangle.
**Answer:** False — Pascal's triangle was checked and NOT FOUND in that book; this lesson cites the Grade 12 Revisi (pp.113–118) and Grade 8 K13 instead.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** True or false: the coefficients of (a+b)ⁿ are the combination numbers C(n,k).
**Answer:** True — that is the content of the theorem (Yemen, Grade 12 Science, p.65).

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** True or false: in (a−b)ⁿ all the terms are negative.
**Answer:** False — the signs alternate; terms with even b-powers are positive.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** True or false: the expansion of (1+x)^(−1) never ends.
**Answer:** True — a negative power gives an infinite series, valid for |x| < 1 (Pakistan, pp.158–159).

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** True or false: Pakistan's Class 11 book proves the binomial theorem by induction.
**Answer:** True — the proof by induction is filed at Unit 8, pp.151–168.

**7. (Sub lesson 3)** True or false: Kazakhstan's Shynybekov Grade 9 book proves the Pascal rule Cₙ₊₁ᵏ⁺¹ = Cₙᵏ + Cₙᵏ⁺¹ by counting lattice paths on a grid.
**Answer:** True — the lattice-path counting proof is filed at §2.6, p.54.
