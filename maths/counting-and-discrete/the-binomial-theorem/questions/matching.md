Match each expansion or series (1–4) with its opening (a–d).

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** (a+b)³
**Answer:** b) a³ + 3a²b + … — row 3 coefficients 1, 3, 3, 1.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** (1+x)⁴
**Answer:** a) 1 + 4x + 6x² + … — row 4 read off the triangle (Indonesia, Grade 12 Revisi, pp.113–118).

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** (1+x)^(−1), |x| < 1
**Answer:** d) 1 − x + x² − … — the alternating series (Pakistan, pp.158–159).

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** (1+x)^(1/2), |x| < 1
**Answer:** c) 1 + x/2 − x²/8 + … — the fractional-power series.

Openings: a) 1 + 4x + 6x² + …  b) a³ + 3a²b + …  c) 1 + x/2 − x²/8 + …  d) 1 − x + x² − …
