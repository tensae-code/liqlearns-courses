# The Binomial Theorem — fill in the blank

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Pascal's Triangle · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — The Theorem and Full Expansions · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — The General Term and the Binomial Series.

Fill each blank with the missing number or word.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In Pascal's triangle every middle entry is the ______ of the two entries above it.
**Answer:** sum — the triangle is built by addition (Indonesia, Grade 8 K13, Bab 1, pp.1–40).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The first and last entries of every row of Pascal's triangle are ______.
**Answer:** 1 — the edges never change.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The coefficient of aⁿ⁻ᵏbᵏ in (a+b)ⁿ is C(n, ______).
**Answer:** k — the coefficient counts the choices of the k b's.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In any single term of (a+b)ⁿ, the two powers add to ______.
**Answer:** n — a's power plus b's power is always n.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** The general term of (a+b)ⁿ, the (k+1)-th term, is ______ aⁿ⁻ᵏ bᵏ.
**Answer:** C(n,k) — the combination number for that place (Pakistan, Class 11, Unit 8).

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** The binomial series for a fractional power holds only for |x| ______ 1.
**Answer:** < (less than) — the validity condition at Pakistan, pp.158–159.

**7. (Sub lesson 3)** In Iraq's Grade 11 Scientific form, the r-th term of (a+b)ⁿ is P_r = C(n, ______) · aⁿ⁻ʳ⁺¹ · bʳ⁻¹.
**Answer:** r−1 — the r-th term (counted from r = 1) carries C(n, r−1) ([8-6], pp.211–215).
