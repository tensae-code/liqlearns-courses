# The Binomial Theorem — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Pascal's Triangle · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — The Theorem and Full Expansions · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — The General Term and the Binomial Series.

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In Pascal's triangle, the entry below and between 3 and 3 is:
a) 3  b) 6  c) 9
**Answer:** b) 6 — each entry is the sum of the two above it.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Row 4 of Pascal's triangle is:
a) 1, 3, 3, 1  b) 1, 4, 6, 4, 1  c) 1, 5, 10, 10, 5, 1
**Answer:** b) 1, 4, 6, 4, 1 — built by addition from row 3 (Indonesia, Grade 8 K13, Bab 1).

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The coefficient of a²b² in (a+b)⁴ is:
a) 4  b) 2  c) 6
**Answer:** c) 6 — C(4,2) = 6, the middle entry of row 4.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** (1+x)³ expands to:
a) 1 + 3x + 3x² + x³  b) 1 + x³  c) 1 + 3x + x² + 3x³
**Answer:** a) 1 + 3x + 3x² + x³ — row 3 coefficients with x-powers climbing.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** In (a−b)⁵, the sign of the a³b² term is:
a) plus  b) minus  c) zero
**Answer:** a) plus — (−b)² is positive; signs alternate with the b-power, and even b-powers are positive.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** The term in x³ in (2+x)⁵ is:
a) 10x³  b) 40x³  c) 80x³
**Answer:** b) 40x³ — C(5,3) · 2² · x³ = 10 · 4 · x³ (Pakistan, Class 11, Unit 8).

**7. (Sub lesson 3)** The binomial series for (1+x)ⁿ with n negative or fractional is valid when:
a) all x  b) |x| < 1  c) x > 1
**Answer:** b) |x| < 1 — the series is promised only inside that zone (Pakistan, pp.158–159).

**8. (Sub lesson 3)** (1+x)^(−1) begins:
a) 1 + x + x² + …  b) 1 − x + x² − x³ + …  c) 1 − x − x² − …
**Answer:** b) 1 − x + x² − x³ + … — plug n = −1 into the series formula.

**9. (Sub lesson 2)** (101)³, worked as (1 + 100)³ by the binomial theorem, equals: (Iraq, Grade 11 Scientific, [8-6], pp.211–215.)
a) 103,030  b) 1,030,301  c) 1,003,001
**Answer:** b) 1,030,301 — 1 + 3·100 + 3·100² + 100³ = 1 + 300 + 30,000 + 1,000,000.

**10. (Sub lesson 2)** The coefficients of (a+b)ⁿ add up to: (Kazakhstan, Shynybekov Grade 9, §2.6, p.54.)
a) n²  b) 2ⁿ  c) n!
**Answer:** b) 2ⁿ — Σ C(n,k) = 2ⁿ, the sum of the whole Pascal row.
