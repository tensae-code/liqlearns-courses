# Counting Principles — matching

Match each situation (1–4) with its count (a–d).

**1. (Level 1)** Three shirts and two trousers: the outfits.
**Answer:** c) 6 — 3 × 2 (Yemen, Grade 12 Literary, pp.6–16).

**2. (Level 2)** Four runners in a line for 1st and 2nd place only.
**Answer:** b) 12 — ⁴P₂ = 4 × 3.

**3. (Level 3)** Four teams, every pair plays once.
**Answer:** c) 6 — C(4,2) = 6; the same number as item 1 by a different road (China, Grade 3 Volume 2, pp.101–105).

**4. (Level 3)** Five people around a round table.
**Answer:** a) 24 — (5−1)! = 4!.

Counts: a) 24  b) 12  c) 6  d) 120
