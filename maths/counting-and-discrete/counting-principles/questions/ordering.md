# Counting Principles — ordering

Put each list in order, smallest first.

**1. (Level 1)** Order by size: 3!, 2 × 3, 0!, 4 × 2.
**Answer:** 0! (1), 2 × 3 (6) and 3! (6) equal, 4 × 2 (8) — 0! is smallest, then the two 6s, then 8.

**2. (Level 1)** Order by size: 1!, 2!, 3!, 4!.
**Answer:** 1, 2, 6, 24 — each factorial multiplies by the next whole number.

**3. (Level 2)** Order by size: ⁵P₁, ⁵P₂, ⁵P₃.
**Answer:** 5, 20, 60 — filling more places multiplies by more falling factors.

**4. (Level 3)** Order by size: C(6,1), C(6,2), C(6,3).
**Answer:** 6, 15, 20 — the combination counts grow towards the middle choice.
