# Video script — Counting Principles

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Three shirts. Two trousers.
Three times two — **six** outfits!
Choices in steps? **Multiply!**

Line things up: five, four, three, two, one —
five times four times three times two times one is **one hundred and twenty**.
That is five factorial!

Order matters? That is a **permutation**.
Order does not matter? That is a **combination**.
Swap two things. New outcome? Permutation. Same outcome? Combination.

Around a round table, one seat is fixed first —
so five people sit in **four factorial** ways.

Now you. Count the choices. Do not list them all — multiply!
