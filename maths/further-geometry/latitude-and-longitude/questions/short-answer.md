# Short answer — Latitude and Longitude

Sub-lesson markers: every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to — Sub lesson 1 — The Earth's Grid: Latitude and Longitude · Sub lesson 2 — Distances along Great Circles · Sub lesson 3 — Distances along Parallels of Latitude. This lesson was built new with its sub lessons (Dominica expansion merge, 2026-11-11), so its markers are original — there are no older depth layers to map from.

1. (Sub lesson 1) State the latitude and the longitude of a point on the equator on the Greenwich meridian.
   **Answer: latitude 0°, longitude 0°.** Item constructed for this lesson from its founding teaching. Dominica, *Mathematics: A Complete Course with CXC Questions and SBA Guidelines, Volume 2* (student book), Grade 11, Ch. 23 — Trigonometry 4, printed p. 1127.

2. (Sub lesson 1) Through how many degrees of longitude does the earth turn in one full rotation?
   **Answer: 360°.** Item constructed for this lesson from its founding teaching. Dominica, *Mathematics: A Complete Course with CXC Questions and SBA Guidelines, Volume 2* (student book), Grade 11, Ch. 23 — Trigonometry 4, printed p. 1127.

3. (Sub lesson 2) Two places on the same meridian differ in latitude by 36°. Find their distance apart, taking the earth's circumference as 40,192 km.
   **Answer: 36/360 × 40,192 = 4,019.2 km.** Item constructed for this lesson from its founding teaching. Dominica, *Mathematics: A Complete Course with CXC Questions and SBA Guidelines, Volume 2* (student book), Grade 11, Ch. 23 — Trigonometry 4, printed p. 1127.

4. (Sub lesson 2) A ship sails along the equator through 180° of longitude. How far does it sail?
   **Answer: half the circumference = 20,096 km.** Item constructed for this lesson from its founding teaching. Dominica, *Mathematics: A Complete Course with CXC Questions and SBA Guidelines, Volume 2* (student book), Grade 11, Ch. 23 — Trigonometry 4, printed p. 1127.

5. (Sub lesson 3) Find the circumference of the parallel at latitude 60°, taking the equator's circumference as 40,192 km.
   **Answer: 40,192 × cos 60° = 40,192 × 0.5 = 20,096 km.** Item constructed for this lesson from its founding teaching. Dominica, *Mathematics: A Complete Course with CXC Questions and SBA Guidelines, Volume 2* (student book), Grade 11, Ch. 23 — Trigonometry 4, printed p. 1127.

6. (Sub lesson 3) Two places on the parallel of latitude 60° differ in longitude by 180°. How far apart are they along the parallel?
   **Answer: half of 20,096 km = 10,048 km.** Item constructed for this lesson from its founding teaching. Dominica, *Mathematics: A Complete Course with CXC Questions and SBA Guidelines, Volume 2* (student book), Grade 11, Ch. 23 — Trigonometry 4, printed p. 1127.
