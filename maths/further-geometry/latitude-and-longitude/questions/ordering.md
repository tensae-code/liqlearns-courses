# Ordering — Latitude and Longitude

Sub-lesson markers: every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to — Sub lesson 1 — The Earth's Grid: Latitude and Longitude · Sub lesson 2 — Distances along Great Circles · Sub lesson 3 — Distances along Parallels of Latitude. This lesson was built new with its sub lessons (Dominica expansion merge, 2026-11-11), so its markers are original — there are no older depth layers to map from.

1. (Sub lesson 1) Put the steps for giving a place's position in order:
   name E or W / measure the latitude from the equator / measure the longitude from Greenwich / name N or S
   **Answer: measure the latitude from the equator → name N or S → measure the longitude from Greenwich → name E or W.** Item constructed for this lesson from its founding teaching. Dominica, *Mathematics: A Complete Course with CXC Questions and SBA Guidelines, Volume 2* (student book), Grade 11, Ch. 23 — Trigonometry 4, printed p. 1127.

2. (Sub lesson 2) Put the steps for a distance along a meridian in order:
   find the difference of latitude / multiply by the circumference / divide the angle by 360 / state the distance
   **Answer: find the difference of latitude → divide the angle by 360 → multiply by the circumference → state the distance.** Item constructed for this lesson from its founding teaching. Dominica, *Mathematics: A Complete Course with CXC Questions and SBA Guidelines, Volume 2* (student book), Grade 11, Ch. 23 — Trigonometry 4, printed p. 1127.

3. (Sub lesson 3) Put the steps for a distance along a parallel in order:
   take the longitude difference over 360 of the parallel's circumference / find the cosine of the latitude / multiply the equator's circumference by the cosine / state the distance
   **Answer: find the cosine of the latitude → multiply the equator's circumference by the cosine → take the longitude difference over 360 of the parallel's circumference → state the distance.** Item constructed for this lesson from its founding teaching. Dominica, *Mathematics: A Complete Course with CXC Questions and SBA Guidelines, Volume 2* (student book), Grade 11, Ch. 23 — Trigonometry 4, printed p. 1127.
