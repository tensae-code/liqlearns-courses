# Matching — Latitude and Longitude

Sub-lesson markers: every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to — Sub lesson 1 — The Earth's Grid: Latitude and Longitude · Sub lesson 2 — Distances along Great Circles · Sub lesson 3 — Distances along Parallels of Latitude. This lesson was built new with its sub lessons (Dominica expansion merge, 2026-11-11), so its markers are original — there are no older depth layers to map from.

1. (Sub lesson 1) Match each word to its meaning:
   (a) latitude
   (b) longitude
   (c) equator
   (i) the great circle at latitude 0°
   (ii) the angle east or west of the Greenwich meridian
   (iii) the angle north or south of the equator
   **Answer: (a)–(iii); (b)–(ii); (c)–(i).** Item constructed for this lesson from its founding teaching. Dominica, *Mathematics: A Complete Course with CXC Questions and SBA Guidelines, Volume 2* (student book), Grade 11, Ch. 23 — Trigonometry 4, printed p. 1127.

   (a) 180°
   (b) 90°
   (c) 36°
   (i) 4,019.2 km
   (ii) 20,096 km
   (iii) 10,048 km
   **Answer: (a)–(ii); (b)–(iii); (c)–(i).** Item constructed for this lesson from its founding teaching. Dominica, *Mathematics: A Complete Course with CXC Questions and SBA Guidelines, Volume 2* (student book), Grade 11, Ch. 23 — Trigonometry 4, printed p. 1127.
