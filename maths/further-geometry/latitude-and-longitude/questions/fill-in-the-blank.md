# Fill in the blank — Latitude and Longitude

Sub-lesson markers: every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to — Sub lesson 1 — The Earth's Grid: Latitude and Longitude · Sub lesson 2 — Distances along Great Circles · Sub lesson 3 — Distances along Parallels of Latitude. This lesson was built new with its sub lessons (Dominica expansion merge, 2026-11-11), so its markers are original — there are no older depth layers to map from.

1. (Sub lesson 1) Lines of constant longitude, running from pole to pole, are called ___.
   **Answer: meridians.** Item constructed for this lesson from its founding teaching. Dominica, *Mathematics: A Complete Course with CXC Questions and SBA Guidelines, Volume 2* (student book), Grade 11, Ch. 23 — Trigonometry 4, printed p. 1127.

2. (Sub lesson 1) Latitude is measured north and south of the ___.
   **Answer: equator.** Item constructed for this lesson from its founding teaching. Dominica, *Mathematics: A Complete Course with CXC Questions and SBA Guidelines, Volume 2* (student book), Grade 11, Ch. 23 — Trigonometry 4, printed p. 1127.

3. (Sub lesson 2) Distance along a great circle = (angle ÷ 360) × ___.
   **Answer: the circumference of the earth (2πR).** Item constructed for this lesson from its founding teaching. Dominica, *Mathematics: A Complete Course with CXC Questions and SBA Guidelines, Volume 2* (student book), Grade 11, Ch. 23 — Trigonometry 4, printed p. 1127.

4. (Sub lesson 2) With R = 6,400 km and π = 3.14, the earth's circumference is ___ km.
   **Answer: 40,192.** Item constructed for this lesson from its founding teaching. Dominica, *Mathematics: A Complete Course with CXC Questions and SBA Guidelines, Volume 2* (student book), Grade 11, Ch. 23 — Trigonometry 4, printed p. 1127.

5. (Sub lesson 3) The radius of the parallel at latitude φ is R × ___ φ.
   **Answer: cos.** Item constructed for this lesson from its founding teaching. Dominica, *Mathematics: A Complete Course with CXC Questions and SBA Guidelines, Volume 2* (student book), Grade 11, Ch. 23 — Trigonometry 4, printed p. 1127.

6. (Sub lesson 3) At the poles the parallel shrinks to a point, because cos 90° = ___.
   **Answer: 0.** Item constructed for this lesson from its founding teaching. Dominica, *Mathematics: A Complete Course with CXC Questions and SBA Guidelines, Volume 2* (student book), Grade 11, Ch. 23 — Trigonometry 4, printed p. 1127.
