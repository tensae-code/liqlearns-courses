# Multiple choice — Latitude and Longitude

Sub-lesson markers: every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to — Sub lesson 1 — The Earth's Grid: Latitude and Longitude · Sub lesson 2 — Distances along Great Circles · Sub lesson 3 — Distances along Parallels of Latitude. This lesson was built new with its sub lessons (Dominica expansion merge, 2026-11-11), so its markers are original — there are no older depth layers to map from.

1. (Sub lesson 1) Latitude is measured from:
   (a) the Greenwich meridian
   (b) the equator ✔
   (c) the North Pole
   (d) the place itself
   **Answer: (b).** Item constructed for this lesson from its founding teaching. Dominica, *Mathematics: A Complete Course with CXC Questions and SBA Guidelines, Volume 2* (student book), Grade 11, Ch. 23 — Trigonometry 4, printed p. 1127.

2. (Sub lesson 1) A place at latitude 40° N, longitude 75° W is:
   (a) south of the equator and east of Greenwich
   (b) north of the equator and west of Greenwich ✔
   (c) north of the equator and east of Greenwich
   (d) south of the equator and west of Greenwich
   **Answer: (b).** Item constructed for this lesson from its founding teaching. Dominica, *Mathematics: A Complete Course with CXC Questions and SBA Guidelines, Volume 2* (student book), Grade 11, Ch. 23 — Trigonometry 4, printed p. 1127.

3. (Sub lesson 2) Two towns on the same meridian differ in latitude by 90°. With R = 6,400 km and π = 3.14, their distance apart along the meridian is about:
   (a) 40,192 km
   (b) 20,096 km
   (c) 10,048 km ✔
   (d) 5,024 km
   **Answer: (c)** — a quarter of the circumference 40,192 km. Item constructed for this lesson from its founding teaching. Dominica, *Mathematics: A Complete Course with CXC Questions and SBA Guidelines, Volume 2* (student book), Grade 11, Ch. 23 — Trigonometry 4, printed p. 1127.

4. (Sub lesson 2) Along the equator, a longitude difference of 45° gives a distance of about:
   (a) 5,024 km ✔
   (b) 10,048 km
   (c) 2,512 km
   (d) 40,192 km
   **Answer: (a)** — 45/360 × 40,192. Item constructed for this lesson from its founding teaching. Dominica, *Mathematics: A Complete Course with CXC Questions and SBA Guidelines, Volume 2* (student book), Grade 11, Ch. 23 — Trigonometry 4, printed p. 1127.

5. (Sub lesson 3) The radius of the parallel at latitude 60° is:
   (a) R
   (b) R cos 60° = R/2 ✔
   (c) R sin 60°
   (d) 2R
   **Answer: (b).** Item constructed for this lesson from its founding teaching. Dominica, *Mathematics: A Complete Course with CXC Questions and SBA Guidelines, Volume 2* (student book), Grade 11, Ch. 23 — Trigonometry 4, printed p. 1127.

6. (Sub lesson 3) Two places on the parallel of latitude 60° differ in longitude by 90°. Their distance apart along the parallel is about:
   (a) 10,048 km
   (b) 5,024 km ✔
   (c) 20,096 km
   (d) 2,512 km
   **Answer: (b)** — the parallel's circumference is 40,192 × cos 60° = 20,096 km; a quarter of it is 5,024 km. Item constructed for this lesson from its founding teaching.
