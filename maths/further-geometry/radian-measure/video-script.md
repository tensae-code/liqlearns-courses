# Video script — Radian measure

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Take a plate. Take a string.

Cut the string as long as the plate's radius — centre to rim.
Lay it along the rim. The angle at the centre — that is **one radian**!

Lay it again. Two radians! Again — three radians!
A half turn is a little more than three. It is **π radians** — 180 degrees!

A whole turn? Two π radians. 360 degrees. Same turn, two names!

Now the rule. Arc equals radius times angle — **s equals r θ**.
Radius 6, angle π over 3? The arc is 2π. About 6.28!

Degrees, radians, gradians — one turn, three rulers.
Say it with me: 180 degrees is π radians!
