# Radian measure — fill-in-the-blank

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Degrees, Minutes and Seconds · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Radians and Gradians · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Directed Angles and Arc Length.

Fill in the missing number or word.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** 1′ = ___″.
**Answer:** 60 — each sexagesimal step splits into 60.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** 1° = ___″.
**Answer:** 3600 — 60 × 60, the ladder Nicaragua's module prints.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** 360° = ___ rad.
**Answer:** 2π — the whole rim is 2πr, so a full turn is 2π radians.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** 270° = ___ rad.
**Answer:** 3π/2 — three quarter turns of π/2.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** A full turn in gradians is ___.
**Answer:** 400 — the centesimal system (Nicaragua module; Nepal Optional §2.1).

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** In s = rθ, the angle θ must be measured in ___.
**Answer:** radians — only in radians does the /360 factor disappear.

**7. (Sub lesson 3)** Angles that end pointing the same way are called ___ angles.
**Answer:** coterminal — they differ by whole turns of 360°.

**8. (Sub lesson 3)** The area of a sector is A = ___ r²θ.
**Answer:** ½ — Pakistan's Class 10 form, A = ½r²θ.
