# Radian measure — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Degrees, Minutes and Seconds · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Radians and Gradians · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Directed Angles and Arc Length.

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** 1° is equal to —
a) 100′  b) 60′  c) 10′
**Answer:** b) 60′ — the sexagesimal ladder splits each step into 60.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** How many seconds are in 5°?
a) 300  b) 3,000  c) 18,000
**Answer:** c) 18,000 — 5 × 3600″, Bangladesh's Class Nine exercise (Ch. 7, printed p. 178).

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** 45.36° in DMS is —
a) 45°36′  b) 45°21′36″  c) 45°3′6″
**Answer:** b) 45°21′36″ — 0.36° = 21.6′ and 0.6′ = 36″ (Pakistan, Class 10, Unit 7).

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** π rad is equal to —
a) 90°  b) 180°  c) 360°
**Answer:** b) 180° — the anchor every book prints: 180° = π rad.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** 60° in radians is —
a) π/6  b) π/4  c) π/3
**Answer:** c) π/3 — 60 × π/180 = π/3.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** A right angle in gradians is —
a) 50  b) 100  c) 200
**Answer:** b) 100 — the centesimal system puts 400 gradians in a full turn.

**7. (Sub lesson 3)** An angle coterminal with 750° (between 0° and 360°) is —
a) 30°  b) 150°  c) 330°
**Answer:** a) 30° — 750° − 2 × 360° = 30°.

**8. (Sub lesson 3)** A circle of radius 6 has an arc cut by an angle of π/3 rad. The arc is —
a) 2  b) 2π  c) 18π
**Answer:** b) 2π — s = rθ = 6 × π/3 = 2π ≈ 6.28.
