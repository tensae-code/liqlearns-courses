# Radian measure — ordering

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Degrees, Minutes and Seconds · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Radians and Gradians · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Directed Angles and Arc Length.

Put the items in the order asked. Check each step before you write the answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Put these angles in order, smallest first: π/2, π/6, π, π/3.
**Answer:** π/6, π/3, π/2, π — they are 30°, 60°, 90°, 180°.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Put these in order, smallest first: 200 gradians, 90°, π rad, 100 gradians.
**Answer:** 90° and 100 gradians tie first (both are the right angle), then 200 gradians and π rad tie last (both are the half turn, 180°) — checked by changing everything to degrees: 90°, 90°, 180°, 180°.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Put these arcs in order, shortest first, all on a circle of radius 4: angle 0.5 rad, angle 1 rad, angle π/2 rad.
**Answer:** 0.5 rad (arc 2), 1 rad (arc 4), π/2 rad (arc 2π ≈ 6.28) — s = rθ grows with θ.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Put these steps for changing 75°30′ to radians in order: (a) multiply by π/180, (b) write the angle as 75.5°, (c) read off ≈ 1.3177 rad.
**Answer:** (b) write as 75.5°, (a) multiply by π/180, (c) ≈ 1.3177 rad — Bangladesh's exercise order (Ch. 7, printed p. 178).
