# Radian measure — matching

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Degrees, Minutes and Seconds · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Radians and Gradians · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Directed Angles and Arc Length.

Match each item on the left to its partner on the right.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Match each DMS part to its size: (a) 1° (b) 1′ (c) one full turn — 60′, 60″, 360°
**Answer:** a) 60′, b) 60″, c) 360° — the sexagesimal ladder and the full turn.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Match each angle to its radian measure: (a) 30° (b) 45° (c) 90° — π/4, π/2, π/6
**Answer:** a) π/6, b) π/4, c) π/2 — each is degrees × π/180.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Match each turn to its gradian measure: (a) quarter turn (b) half turn (c) full turn — 200, 400, 100
**Answer:** a) 100, b) 200, c) 400 — 400 gradians make the full turn.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Match each angle to a coterminal angle between 0° and 360°: (a) 750° (b) −20° (c) 1125° — 340°, 45°, 30°
**Answer:** a) 30°, b) 340°, c) 45° — take away or add whole turns of 360° (1125° is Ethiopia's example).
