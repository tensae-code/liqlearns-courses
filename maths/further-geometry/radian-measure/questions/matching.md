# Radian measure — matching

Match each item on the left to its partner on the right.

**1. (Level 1)** Match each DMS part to its size: (a) 1° (b) 1′ (c) one full turn — 60′, 60″, 360°
**Answer:** a) 60′, b) 60″, c) 360° — the sexagesimal ladder and the full turn.

**2. (Level 2)** Match each angle to its radian measure: (a) 30° (b) 45° (c) 90° — π/4, π/2, π/6
**Answer:** a) π/6, b) π/4, c) π/2 — each is degrees × π/180.

**3. (Level 2)** Match each turn to its gradian measure: (a) quarter turn (b) half turn (c) full turn — 200, 400, 100
**Answer:** a) 100, b) 200, c) 400 — 400 gradians make the full turn.

**4. (Level 3)** Match each angle to a coterminal angle between 0° and 360°: (a) 750° (b) −20° (c) 1125° — 340°, 45°, 30°
**Answer:** a) 30°, b) 340°, c) 45° — take away or add whole turns of 360° (1125° is Ethiopia's example).
