# Radian measure — short answer

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Level 1)** Write 12°23′35″ as decimal degrees (Pakistan's checked value).
**Answer:** 12.393° — minutes and seconds divided by 60 and 3600.

**2. (Level 1)** How many sexagesimal seconds are in 60°15′30″?
**Answer:** 216,930 — 60 × 3600 + 15 × 60 + 30 (Nepal, Optional Grade 9, §2.1).

**3. (Level 2)** Define a radian in one line.
**Answer:** The angle that cuts off an arc equal to the radius — Nicaragua's module definition (Unit IV, printed pp. 130–133).

**4. (Level 2)** Change 135° to radians.
**Answer:** 3π/4 — 135 × π/180.

**5. (Level 2)** Change 5π/6 to degrees.
**Answer:** 150° — 5π/6 × 180/π.

**6. (Level 3)** Find a coterminal angle between 0° and 360° for −210°, and name its quadrant.
**Answer:** 150°, second quadrant — add 360°; Bangladesh's book places −210° in the second quadrant (Ch. 7, printed pp. 162–163).

**7. (Level 3)** A sector has radius 16 cm and angle 60°. Find its area.
**Answer:** About 134.1 cm² — A = ½r²θ = ½ × 256 × π/3 (Pakistan, Class 10, Unit 7).

**8. (Level 3)** A door's edge traces an arc of 1.40 m on a radius of 1.07 m. Through what angle did it open, in degrees?
**Answer:** About 75° — θ = 1.40/1.07 ≈ 1.31 rad ≈ 75° (Bangladesh, Ch. 7, printed pp. 175–177).
