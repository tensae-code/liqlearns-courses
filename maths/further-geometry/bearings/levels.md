# Bearings — questions by level

Practice by level, tied to the skill number. The questions live in
the type files; this file is the by-level view. The levels are the
depths inside this one thing — direction clockwise from north in
three figures — in the order Sierra Leone's course teaches it:
read and draw first, then reverse, then solve.

## Level 1 — reading, writing and drawing bearings
Level 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: read a compass bearing, write any bearing in three figures measured clockwise from north, and draw a bearing statement with a protractor.

## Level 2 — reverse bearings and drawn journeys
Level 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: find any reverse bearing by the ±180° rule, draw a two-leg journey to scale, and write a position in distance-bearing form (r, θ).

## Level 3 — solving bearing problems
Level 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: solve a bearing problem with a right triangle (Pythagoras and the ratios), and solve one with a non-right triangle by choosing the sine or cosine rule.
