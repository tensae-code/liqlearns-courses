# Bearings — ordering

Put each list in the right order.

**1. (Level 1)** Order these bearings clockwise starting from north: 270°, 090°, 000°, 180°.
**Answer:** 000°, 090°, 180°, 270° — north, east, south, west in turning order.

**2. (Level 2)** Order the steps for a two-leg journey problem: (a) pick the rule the closing triangle needs, (b) draw a north arrow at the start, (c) draw the closing line, (d) draw the two legs in order with a north arrow at the joint.
**Answer:** (b), (d), (c), (a) — north first, legs next, closing line last, rule chosen from the drawing.

**3. (Level 3)** Order Sierra Leone's bearing problem-solving parts: Part 2 (right triangles), Part 4 (mixed, choosing the rule), Part 1 (diagrams only), Part 3 (sine and cosine rules).
**Answer:** Part 1 (p. 72), Part 2 (p. 78), Part 3 (p. 81), Part 4 (p. 84) — diagrams, then right triangles, then the rules, then mixed choice.
