# Bearings — fill in the blank

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Reading, Writing and Drawing Bearings · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Reverse Bearings and Drawn Journeys · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Solving Bearing Problems.

Fill each blank with the one right word or value.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Bearings are always written in ______ figures.
**Answer:** three — pad with leading zeros, as in 045°.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The bearing of due east is ______.
**Answer:** 090° — a quarter clockwise turn from north.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** To find a reverse bearing, add or subtract ______ degrees.
**Answer:** 180 — the way back is exactly half a turn away.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The reverse bearing of 200° is ______.
**Answer:** 020° — 200° − 180° = 20°, written in three figures.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** In distance-bearing form (r, θ), r is the ______ and θ is the bearing.
**Answer:** distance — Sierra Leone's L121 form for a point's position.
