# Bearings — short answer

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Reading, Writing and Drawing Bearings · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Reverse Bearings and Drawn Journeys · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Solving Bearing Problems.

Answer in a word or a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Write the bearing of due south in three figures.
**Answer:** 180°.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** From which direction, and turning which way, is every bearing measured?
**Answer:** From north, clockwise.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The bearing from P to Q is 128°. What is the bearing from Q to P?
**Answer:** 308° — 128° + 180°.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A point is written (8, 040°) in distance-bearing form. What do the 8 and the 040° each tell you?
**Answer:** 8 is the distance from the start point; 040° is the bearing to travel on.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** In Nepal's Grade 8 exercise, a walker goes from B on bearing 030° for 5 km to C. In which compass quarter (NE, SE, SW, NW) does C lie from B?
**Answer:** NE (north-east) — bearing 030° lies between north (000°) and east (090°).
