# Bearings — matching

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Reading, Writing and Drawing Bearings · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Reverse Bearings and Drawn Journeys · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Solving Bearing Problems.

Match each item on the left with the one right item on the right.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Match the direction to its bearing: (i) north, (ii) east, (iii) south, (iv) west — (a) 090°, (b) 180°, (c) 000°, (d) 270°.
**Answer:** (i)–(c), (ii)–(a), (iii)–(b), (iv)–(d) — quarter turns clockwise from north.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Match the bearing to its reverse: (i) 040°, (ii) 190°, (iii) 300° — (a) 120°, (b) 220°, (c) 010°.
**Answer:** (i)–(b) 220°, (ii)–(c) 010°, (iii)–(a) 120° — each pair differs by exactly 180°.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Match the Sierra Leone lesson to its work: (i) L117, (ii) L121, (iii) L123 — (a) bearings with acute and obtuse triangles, (b) compass bearings, (c) distance-bearing form (r, θ).
**Answer:** (i)–(b), (ii)–(c), (iii)–(a) — the course order as filed (printed pp. 63, 75, 81).
