# Bearings — true or false

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Reading, Writing and Drawing Bearings · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Reverse Bearings and Drawn Journeys · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Solving Bearing Problems.

Say true or false, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A bearing of 45 degrees may be written "45°" in bearing work. True or false?
**Answer:** False — bearings are always three figures: 045°.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Bearings are measured anti-clockwise from north. True or false?
**Answer:** False — clockwise from north, in every book cited here.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The reverse bearing of 075° is 255°. True or false?
**Answer:** True — 075° + 180° = 255°, Nepal's Grade 8 worked pair.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The reverse bearing of 350° is 530°. True or false?
**Answer:** False — subtract 180° (or add and wrap past 360°): the reverse is 170°.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Nepal's bearings in this lesson are cited from its Grade 8 teacher guide. True or false?
**Answer:** False — Nepal is cited from the Grade 8 student book only; its Grade 8 guide is a mismatch guide and is cited nowhere here.
