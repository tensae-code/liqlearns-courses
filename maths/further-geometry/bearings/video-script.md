# Video script — Bearings

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Where are you going? "That way" is not enough!
We need a NUMBER for direction.

Stand at your point. Face NORTH. Now turn — clockwise only!
Count the turn. That count is the **bearing**.

North is zero-zero-zero. East is zero-nine-zero.
South is one-eight-zero. West is two-seven-zero.
Always THREE figures! Forty-five is zero-four-five!

Coming back? Add one hundred and eighty — or take it away.
The way back is the reverse bearing.

Now you. I point. You give me the bearing — three figures,
clockwise from north!
