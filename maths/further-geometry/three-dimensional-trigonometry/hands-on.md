# Hands-on — Three-dimensional trigonometry

## The stick in the box
Take a shoebox and a stick or ruler longer than its base diagonal. Rest the stick from one bottom corner up to the opposite top corner. Measure the base edges and the height, work the stick's length out in two flat steps, then measure the stick and compare.

## Shadow angle
On a sunny spot or under a lamp, lean a stick against a wall. Mark its shadow line on the ground. The stick, its shadow and the wall edge form the hidden triangle: measure the shadow and the height, work out the angle between stick and ground, then check with a protractor laid along the shadow.

## Pyramid card model
Fold a square-based pyramid from card (base 10 cm, vertical height 6 cm). First find the base diagonal flat, then the slant edge up the middle of a face, then the angle the slant edge makes with the base — each step on paper before you measure the model.
