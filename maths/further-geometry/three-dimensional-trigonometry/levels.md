# Three-dimensional trigonometry — questions by level

Practice by level, tied to the skill number. The questions live in
the type files; this file is the by-level view. The levels are the
depths inside this one thing — trigonometry in three dimensions —
in Jordan's order: find the hidden triangle first, then work it in
two steps, then angles to faces.

## Level 1 — finding the hidden triangle
Level 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: given a cuboid or pyramid, name the flat right triangle the problem lives in, with its three vertices.

## Level 2 — the two flat steps
Level 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: work a space diagonal or slant length by Pythagoras first, then use that length in a trig ratio to finish the problem.

## Level 3 — angles to faces and mixed problems
Level 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: find the angle between a line in space and a face via its shadow, and choose between a ratio, the sine rule and the cosine rule when the hidden triangle is not right-angled.
