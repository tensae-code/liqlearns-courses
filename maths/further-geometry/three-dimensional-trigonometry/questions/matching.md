# Three-dimensional trigonometry — matching

Match each item on the left with the one right item on the right.

**1. (Level 1)** Match the part to its role: (i) the space diagonal, (ii) the base diagonal, (iii) the vertical edge — (a) the shadow in the standing triangle, (b) the hypotenuse of the standing triangle, (c) the upright side of the standing triangle.
**Answer:** (i)–(b), (ii)–(a), (iii)–(c) — the standing triangle is diagonal, shadow and height.

**2. (Level 2)** Match the solid to its space diagonal: (i) cuboid 3 × 4 × 12, (ii) cuboid 6 × 8 × 5, (iii) cube of side 1 — (a) √125 ≈ 11.2, (b) 13, (c) √3 ≈ 1.73.
**Answer:** (i)–(b), (ii)–(a), (iii)–(c) — each by base diagonal first, then the standing triangle.

**3. (Level 3)** Match the Jordan Unit 4 lesson to its page: (i) bearings, (ii) sine rule, (iii) solving 3-D problems — (a) printed p. 124, (b) printed p. 100, (c) printed p. 106.
**Answer:** (i)–(b), (ii)–(c), (iii)–(a) — the unit order as filed.
