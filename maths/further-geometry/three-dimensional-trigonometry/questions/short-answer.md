# Three-dimensional trigonometry — short answer

Answer in a word or a short sentence.

**1. (Level 1)** In Jordan's Grade 10 book, which unit and lesson teach solving 3-D problems?
**Answer:** Unit 4, Lesson 5 (printed p. 124).

**2. (Level 1)** A box's base is 3 cm by 4 cm. What is its base diagonal?
**Answer:** 5 cm — the 3-4-5 triangle.

**3. (Level 2)** That box is 12 cm tall. What is its space diagonal?
**Answer:** 13 cm — √(5² + 12²) = √169.

**4. (Level 2)** In the two-step method, which step comes first: an angle by a ratio, or a length by Pythagoras?
**Answer:** A length by Pythagoras — the triangle must exist on paper before any ratio.

**5. (Level 3)** A square pyramid has base side 10 cm. What length is half its base diagonal, to 2 decimal places?
**Answer:** About 7.07 cm — the full diagonal is 10√2 ≈ 14.14 cm.
