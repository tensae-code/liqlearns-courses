# Three-dimensional trigonometry — fill in the blank

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Finding the Hidden Triangle · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — The Two Flat Steps · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Angles to Faces and Mixed Problems.

Fill each blank with the one right word or value.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Every 3-D problem hides a ______ triangle inside the solid.
**Answer:** flat (right) — find it, draw it flat, then work it.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Step one of the method works a length with ______.
**Answer:** Pythagoras — on one flat face of the solid.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A cuboid 6 by 8 by 5 has space diagonal √125 ≈ ______ cm.
**Answer:** 11.2 — base diagonal 10 first, then √(100 + 25).

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** The angle between a line and a face is the angle between the line and its ______ on the face.
**Answer:** shadow — never the angle to an edge of the solid.
