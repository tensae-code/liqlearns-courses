# Video script — Three-dimensional trigonometry

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Here is a box. Here is a stick, corner to corner, through the air.
How long is the stick? What angle does it make with the table?

Do not panic. There is a FLAT triangle hiding inside the box!
The stick. Its shadow on the base. And the tall edge. Three sides!

Step one: the shadow. Flat Pythagoras on the base.
Step two: now the triangle stands up — stick, shadow, height.
Sine, cosine, tangent — the old friends — finish the job.

Every 3-D problem is the same hunt:
find the flat triangle, then work it flat.

Now you. Take a box. Hide a stick inside it. Find your triangle!
