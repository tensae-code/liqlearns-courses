# Video script — Right-triangle trigonometry

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Look at the triangle. One corner is square — 90 degrees.
Pick another corner. Call it theta.

The side across from the square corner — the longest side —
is the **hypotenuse**.
The side across from theta is the **opposite**.
The side touching theta is the **adjacent**.

Three little fractions run the whole thing:

Sine is opposite over hypotenuse!
Cosine is adjacent over hypotenuse!
Tangent is opposite over adjacent!

SOH — CAH — TOA! Say it with me!

A ladder, 5 metres, leans at 60 degrees.
How high does it reach? Five times sine of 60.
Sine of 60 is root three over two.
About four point three metres up the wall!

Now you. Draw your triangle. Name the sides.
Pick your ratio. Find your missing side!
