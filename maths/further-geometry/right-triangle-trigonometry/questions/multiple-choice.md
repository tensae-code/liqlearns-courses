# Right-triangle trigonometry — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Naming the Sides and the Ratios, except items about the special angles, which are Sub lesson 4 — The Special Angles · (Level 2) splits item by item: side-finding items are Sub lesson 2 — Finding Missing Sides, special-angle items are Sub lesson 4, and ratio-identity items (tan θ = sin θ/cos θ, sin²θ + cos²θ = 1) are Sub lesson 1 · (Level 3) splits item by item: angle-finding items are Sub lesson 3 — Finding Angles, and elevation/depression items are Sub lesson 5 — Using It Outside.

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In a right triangle, the hypotenuse is —
a) the side across from the marked angle  b) the side across from the right angle  c) the shortest side
**Answer: b) the side across from the right angle.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** sin θ = —
a) adjacent / hypotenuse  b) opposite / hypotenuse  c) opposite / adjacent
**Answer: b) opposite / hypotenuse.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** tan θ = —
a) opposite / adjacent  b) adjacent / hypotenuse  c) hypotenuse / opposite
**Answer: a) opposite / adjacent.**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** sin 30° = —
a) √3/2  b) 1/2  c) 1
**Answer: b) 1/2.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** A right triangle has hypotenuse 10 and angle 30°. The opposite side is —
a) 5  b) 8.66  c) 20
**Answer: a) 5** — 10 × sin 30° = 10 × 0.5.

**6. (Sub lesson 4)** cos 60° = —
a) 1/2  b) √3/2  c) 1/√3
**Answer: a) 1/2.**

**7. (Sub lesson 3)** sin θ = 3/5. The angle θ is about —
a) 30°  b) 37°  c) 53°
**Answer: b) 37°** — sin⁻¹(0.6) ≈ 36.9°.

**8. (Sub lesson 5)** A ladder 5 m long makes 60° with the ground. It reaches about —
a) 2.5 m  b) 4.33 m  c) 5 m
**Answer: b) 4.33 m** — 5 × sin 60° = 5 × √3/2 ≈ 4.33.
