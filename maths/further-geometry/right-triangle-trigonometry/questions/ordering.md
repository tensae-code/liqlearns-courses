# Right-triangle trigonometry — ordering

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. Put each set in order, smallest to largest.

**1. (Sub lesson 4)** Order sin 30°, sin 45°, sin 60° from smallest to largest.
**Answer:** sin 30° (1/2 = 0.5), sin 45° (1/√2 ≈ 0.707), sin 60° (√3/2 ≈ 0.866).

**2. (Sub lesson 4)** Order cos 30°, cos 45°, cos 60° from smallest to largest.
**Answer:** cos 60° (0.5), cos 45° (≈ 0.707), cos 30° (≈ 0.866).

**3. (Sub lesson 4)** Order tan 30°, tan 45°, tan 60° from smallest to largest.
**Answer:** tan 30° (1/√3 ≈ 0.577), tan 45° (1), tan 60° (√3 ≈ 1.732).

**4. (Sub lesson 5)** A ladder of fixed length leans at 30°, 45° and 60° to the ground. Order the heights it reaches, smallest to largest.
**Answer:** 30° (lowest), 45°, 60° (highest) — height = length × sin(angle), and sine grows with the angle.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Order these steps for finding a missing angle: (a) work out the ratio from the two known sides, (b) name the sides, (c) use the inverse ratio.
**Answer:** (b) name the sides, (a) work out the ratio, (c) use the inverse ratio.
