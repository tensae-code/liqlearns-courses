Join the two sides that go together.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Match each ratio to its sides.
- sin θ — a) adjacent / hypotenuse
- cos θ — b) opposite / hypotenuse
- tan θ — c) opposite / adjacent
**Answer:** sin–b, cos–a, tan–c.

**2. (Sub lesson 4)** Match each angle to its sine.
- 30° — a) √3/2
- 45° — b) 1/2
- 60° — c) 1/√2
**Answer:** 30°–b, 45°–c, 60°–a.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Match each problem to the ratio it needs.
- Know hypotenuse, want opposite — a) tangent
- Know adjacent, want hypotenuse — b) sine
- Know opposite and adjacent, want the angle — c) cosine
**Answer:** hypotenuse→opposite–b, adjacent→hypotenuse–c, opposite+adjacent→angle–a.
