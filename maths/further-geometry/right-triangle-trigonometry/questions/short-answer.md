# Right-triangle trigonometry — short answer

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Naming the Sides and the Ratios, except items about the special angles, which are Sub lesson 4 — The Special Angles · (Level 2) splits item by item: side-finding items are Sub lesson 2 — Finding Missing Sides, special-angle items are Sub lesson 4, and ratio-identity items (tan θ = sin θ/cos θ, sin²θ + cos²θ = 1) are Sub lesson 1 · (Level 3) splits item by item: angle-finding items are Sub lesson 3 — Finding Angles, and elevation/depression items are Sub lesson 5 — Using It Outside.

Answer in a word or a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What do the letters SOH stand for?
**Answer:** Sine = Opposite / Hypotenuse.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In a right triangle, which side is always the longest?
**Answer:** The hypotenuse.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Write cos θ as a ratio of two sides.
**Answer:** Adjacent / hypotenuse.

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** What is tan 45°?
**Answer:** 1.

**5. (Sub lesson 4)** A right triangle has adjacent 6 and angle 60° at the corner. The hypotenuse is 12. Check: what is cos 60°?
**Answer:** 6/12 = 1/2.

**6. (Sub lesson 1)** sin²θ + cos²θ always equals what?
**Answer:** 1.

**7. (Sub lesson 3)** A right triangle has opposite 4 and hypotenuse 5 for angle θ. What is θ, to the nearest degree?
**Answer:** About 53° (sin⁻¹(0.8) ≈ 53.1°).

**8. (Sub lesson 5)** You stand 10 m from a building and the angle of elevation to its top is 45°. How high is the top above your eye level?
**Answer:** 10 m (10 × tan 45° = 10 × 1).
