# Right-triangle trigonometry — short answer

Answer in a word or a short sentence.

**1. (Level 1)** What do the letters SOH stand for?
**Answer:** Sine = Opposite / Hypotenuse.

**2. (Level 1)** In a right triangle, which side is always the longest?
**Answer:** The hypotenuse.

**3. (Level 1)** Write cos θ as a ratio of two sides.
**Answer:** Adjacent / hypotenuse.

**4. (Level 2)** What is tan 45°?
**Answer:** 1.

**5. (Level 2)** A right triangle has adjacent 6 and angle 60° at the corner. The hypotenuse is 12. Check: what is cos 60°?
**Answer:** 6/12 = 1/2.

**6. (Level 2)** sin²θ + cos²θ always equals what?
**Answer:** 1.

**7. (Level 3)** A right triangle has opposite 4 and hypotenuse 5 for angle θ. What is θ, to the nearest degree?
**Answer:** About 53° (sin⁻¹(0.8) ≈ 53.1°).

**8. (Level 3)** You stand 10 m from a building and the angle of elevation to its top is 45°. How high is the top above your eye level?
**Answer:** 10 m (10 × tan 45° = 10 × 1).
