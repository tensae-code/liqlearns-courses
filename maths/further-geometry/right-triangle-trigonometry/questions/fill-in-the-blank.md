# Right-triangle trigonometry — fill in the blank

Put the missing word or number in the space.

**1. (Level 1)** The side across from the angle θ is called the ______.
**Answer:** opposite.

**2. (Level 1)** The side touching the angle θ that is not the hypotenuse is the ______.
**Answer:** adjacent.

**3. (Level 1)** sin θ = opposite / ______.
**Answer:** hypotenuse.

**4. (Level 2)** tan θ = sin θ / ______.
**Answer:** cos θ.

**5. (Level 2)** sin 45° = 1 / ______.
**Answer:** √2.

**6. (Level 2)** cos 30° = √3 / ______.
**Answer:** 2.

**7. (Level 3)** To find an angle from a ratio, use the ______ of sine, cosine or tangent.
**Answer:** inverse.

**8. (Level 3)** Looking up at the top of a building, the angle you measure is the angle of ______.
**Answer:** elevation.
