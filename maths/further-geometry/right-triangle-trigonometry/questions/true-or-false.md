# Right-triangle trigonometry — true or false

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Naming the Sides and the Ratios, except items about the special angles, which are Sub lesson 4 — The Special Angles · (Level 2) splits item by item: side-finding items are Sub lesson 2 — Finding Missing Sides, special-angle items are Sub lesson 4, and ratio-identity items (tan θ = sin θ/cos θ, sin²θ + cos²θ = 1) are Sub lesson 1 · (Level 3) splits item by item: angle-finding items are Sub lesson 3 — Finding Angles, and elevation/depression items are Sub lesson 5 — Using It Outside.

Say true or false, and why in one line.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The hypotenuse is always the longest side of a right triangle. — **True.** It sits across from the 90° angle.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** sin θ is always bigger than 1 for a sharp angle. — **False.** Sine of a sharp angle is always less than 1 (opposite < hypotenuse).

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Opposite and adjacent swap when you pick the other sharp angle. — **True.** The side across from one angle touches the other.

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** sin 30° = cos 60°. — **True.** Both are 1/2.

**5. (Sub lesson 4)** tan 45° = 1. — **True.** In a 45°–45°–90° triangle the opposite and adjacent are equal.

**6. (Sub lesson 1)** sin²θ + cos²θ = 0 for every angle θ. — **False.** It equals 1 (the Pythagorean identity).

**7. (Sub lesson 3)** If tan θ = 1, then θ = 45°. — **True** (for a sharp angle).

**8. (Sub lesson 5)** The angle of depression is measured looking up. — **False.** Depression is looking down; elevation is looking up.
