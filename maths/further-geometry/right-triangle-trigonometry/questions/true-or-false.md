# Right-triangle trigonometry — true or false

Say true or false, and why in one line.

**1. (Level 1)** The hypotenuse is always the longest side of a right triangle. — **True.** It sits across from the 90° angle.

**2. (Level 1)** sin θ is always bigger than 1 for a sharp angle. — **False.** Sine of a sharp angle is always less than 1 (opposite < hypotenuse).

**3. (Level 1)** Opposite and adjacent swap when you pick the other sharp angle. — **True.** The side across from one angle touches the other.

**4. (Level 2)** sin 30° = cos 60°. — **True.** Both are 1/2.

**5. (Level 2)** tan 45° = 1. — **True.** In a 45°–45°–90° triangle the opposite and adjacent are equal.

**6. (Level 2)** sin²θ + cos²θ = 0 for every angle θ. — **False.** It equals 1 (the Pythagorean identity).

**7. (Level 3)** If tan θ = 1, then θ = 45°. — **True** (for a sharp angle).

**8. (Level 3)** The angle of depression is measured looking up. — **False.** Depression is looking down; elevation is looking up.
