# Video script — Transformations

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Four moves. Watch them all!

Slide it! Three right, two up. Same shape, new place.
That is a **translation**.

Mirror it! Flip it over the line. Back to front, same size.
That is a **reflection**.

Turn it! Round and round the middle. Quarter-turn, half-turn.
Same shape, new way round. That is a **rotation**.

Grow it! Twice as big from one corner. Same shape, bigger size.
That is an **enlargement**.

Slide, mirror, turn — the shape stays the same size.
Grow — the shape stays the same shape, but bigger!

Now you. Draw a triangle. Slide it. Mirror it. Turn it. Grow it.
Four moves, one triangle, four new pictures!
