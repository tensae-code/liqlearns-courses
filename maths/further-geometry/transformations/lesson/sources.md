**Acceptance count: taught in 15 countries (Egypt, Jordan,
Kazakhstan, Sierra Leone, South Africa, Greece, Mauritania,
Ghana, Laos — Ghana as teacher evidence only, Yemen, Indonesia, Nicaragua, Ethiopia, South Sudan, Iraq).** Egypt carries the
fullest single treatment — all four moves defined with
coordinate rules plus composition in Grade 7 Term 2. Jordan
carries translation and reflection in Grade 6, rotation in
Grade 7, enlargement in Grade 7 and dilation in Grade 8.
Kazakhstan carries the proved treatment in Grade 9 Geometry —
symmetries, translation, motion, homothety. Sierra Leone
carries the four moves as JSS practice with combining and
classifying. South Africa carries reflection, rotation and
translation as Grade 9 worksheet practice. Greece carries
central and axial symmetry in Lykeio Geometry. Mauritania
carries transformations in the plane in 4AS. Ghana carries
reflection only, in a teacher manual — worded as teacher
evidence, never as pupil-book teaching. Laos carries transformations from squared-paper translation, reflection and rotation with coordinates in Grade 5, through line reflection in Grade 6, to full isometries with the coordinate rotation formula in Grade 9. Yemen carries transformations from translation, reflection and rotation in Grade 3, through reflection in the axes and translation, to reflection, translation, rotation and enlargement and their analytic forms in Grade 10. Indonesia carries transformations from translation, reflection, rotation and dilation in Grade 9, to geometric transformations on the Cartesian plane with matrices and composition in Grade 11 Tingkat Lanjut (2021 student book), with the 2024 TL guide's transformations chapter as guide-only evidence for its own edition. Not stocked here: the transformations of functions in Grade 11 Revisi (Bab 3) and Grade 12 Merdeka (Bab 1) are function transformations, not geometric transformations. Nicaragua touches this lesson only in part — translation, rotation and reflection in Grade 7 only, introduced descriptively and not with coordinates. No dilation/enlargement is taught as a transformation, and no coordinate rules are given. The Grade 9 parabola translations and the Grade 10 trig-graph shifts are function transformations, not geometric transformations, and are not stocked here. Ethiopia touches this lesson only in part — translation, reflection and rotation only: moving shapes by sliding, reflecting and turning in Grade 3 as an early form, and the full transformations chapter in Grade 11. No dilation/enlargement and no shear are taught anywhere in Ethiopia's set, and transformations as a standalone topic exist only in Grade 11. South Sudan carries transformations whole in Secondary 1 — translation (with vectors), rotation, reflection and enlargement with scale factor and centre — with a translation review inside vectors in Secondary 2. Iraq carries transformations whole across Grades 7–9 — translation and reflection in Grade 7, reflection and rotation with translation in the coordinate plane in Grade 8, and dilation in the coordinate plane in Grade 9 — covering all four moves: translation, reflection, rotation and dilation.
