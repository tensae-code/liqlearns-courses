# Transformations — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Reflection and Translation · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Rotation and Enlargement · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Combining Moves.

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Reflect (3, 2) in the x-axis. The image is —
a) (−3, 2)  b) (3, −2)  c) (−3, −2)
**Answer: b) (3, −2)** — (x, y) → (x, −y).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Reflect (3, 2) in the y-axis. The image is —
a) (−3, 2)  b) (3, −2)  c) (−3, −2)
**Answer: a) (−3, 2)** — (x, y) → (−x, y).

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Translate (1, 1) by (2, −1). The image is —
a) (3, 0)  b) (−1, 2)  c) (2, 0)
**Answer: a) (3, 0).**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Rotate (2, 3) by 90° anticlockwise about the origin. The image is —
a) (3, −2)  b) (−3, 2)  c) (−2, −3)
**Answer: b) (−3, 2)** — (x, y) → (−y, x).

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Rotate (2, 3) by 180° about the origin. The image is —
a) (−2, −3)  b) (3, 2)  c) (−3, 2)
**Answer: a) (−2, −3)** — (x, y) → (−x, −y).

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Enlarge a shape by scale factor 3. Its image is —
a) congruent  b) similar  c) neither
**Answer: b) similar.**

**7. (Sub lesson 3)** Translate by (2, 1), then by (1, 3). The single translation that does the same is —
a) (3, 4)  b) (2, 3)  c) (1, 4)
**Answer: a) (3, 4)** — translations add.

**8. (Sub lesson 3)** Reflect in the x-axis, then in the y-axis. The single move that does the same is —
a) a 90° turn  b) a 180° turn  c) a translation
**Answer: b) a 180° turn.**
