# Transformations — ordering

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Reflection and Translation · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Rotation and Enlargement · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Combining Moves.

Put each set in order, as the steps are done or from smallest
to largest.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A point is reflected in the x-axis, then translated by (1, 1). Order the steps: reflect, then translate. What is the image of (2, 3)?
**Answer:** Reflect first: (2, −3). Then translate: (3, −2).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Order these turns about the origin from smallest to largest: half-turn, quarter-turn clockwise, full turn, quarter-turn anticlockwise.
**Answer:** quarter-turn (90°), half-turn (180°), full turn (360°) — the two quarter-turns are equal in size, opposite in direction.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Order these enlargements from smallest image to largest: scale factor 1/2, 1, 2, 3.
**Answer:** 1/2 (half size), 1 (same size), 2 (double), 3 (triple).

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** A shape is translated by (1, 0), then reflected in the y-axis, then translated by (0, 2). Track the point (3, 1) step by step.
**Answer:** (3, 1) → (4, 1) → (−4, 1) → (−4, 3).

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Order these combined moves by what they do to size: reflect then rotate; enlarge by 2 then shrink by 1/2; enlarge by 3.
**Answer:** reflect then rotate (size unchanged), enlarge by 2 then shrink by 1/2 (size unchanged — back to the start), enlarge by 3 (three times as big).
