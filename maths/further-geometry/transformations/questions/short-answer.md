# Transformations — short answer

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Reflection and Translation · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Rotation and Enlargement · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Combining Moves.

Answer in a word or a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the mirror line called in a reflection?
**Answer:** The mirror line (or line of reflection).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A translation moves a shape 5 squares left and 1 square down. Write the rule for a point (x, y).
**Answer:** (x − 5, y − 1).

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Does a reflection change the size of a shape?
**Answer:** No — the image is congruent to the object.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Rotate the point (4, 0) by 90° clockwise about the origin. Where does it land?
**Answer:** (0, −4) — (x, y) → (y, −x).

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** A triangle is enlarged by scale factor 1/2. A side of 8 cm becomes how long?
**Answer:** 4 cm.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the move that grows a shape from a fixed point by a scale factor.
**Answer:** Enlargement (dilation / homothety).

**7. (Sub lesson 3)** Two translations, (1, 2) then (3, 1), are done in a row. What single translation does the same?
**Answer:** (4, 3).

**8. (Sub lesson 3)** A shape is reflected, then translated. Is the final image congruent or similar to the first shape?
**Answer:** Congruent — neither move changes the size.
