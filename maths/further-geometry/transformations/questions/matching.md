# Transformations — matching

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Reflection and Translation · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Rotation and Enlargement · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Combining Moves.

Join the two sides that go together.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Match each move to what it does.
- Reflection — a) slides every point the same way
- Translation — b) flips over a line
- Rotation — c) turns round a fixed point
- Enlargement — d) grows from a centre by a scale factor
**Answer:** Reflection–b, Translation–a, Rotation–c, Enlargement–d.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Match each rule to its move.
- (x, y) → (x, −y) — a) reflection in the y-axis
- (x, y) → (−x, y) — b) reflection in the x-axis
- (x, y) → (−y, x) — c) 90° anticlockwise about the origin
- (x, y) → (y, −x) — d) 90° clockwise about the origin
**Answer:** (x, −y)–b, (−x, y)–a, (−y, x)–c, (y, −x)–d.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Match each pair of moves to its single equal move.
- Two translations (2, 1) then (1, 3) — a) half-turn
- Reflect in x, then in y — b) translation (3, 4)
- Two half-turns — c) no move at all (back to the start)
- Enlarge by 2, then by 3 — d) enlarge by 6
**Answer:** translations–b, two reflections–a, two half-turns–c, enlargements–d.
