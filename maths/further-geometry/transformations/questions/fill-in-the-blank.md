# Transformations — fill in the blank

Put the missing word or number in the space.

**1. (Level 1)** In a reflection, every point jumps to the other side of the mirror line, the same ______ from the line.
**Answer:** distance.

**2. (Level 1)** Reflecting (x, y) in the x-axis gives (x, ______).
**Answer:** −y.

**3. (Level 1)** A translation by (a, b) sends (x, y) to (x + a, ______).
**Answer:** y + b.

**4. (Level 2)** A quarter-turn anticlockwise about the origin sends (x, y) to (______, x).
**Answer:** −y.

**5. (Level 2)** A half-turn about the origin sends (x, y) to (−x, ______).
**Answer:** −y.

**6. (Level 2)** In an enlargement, every length is multiplied by the ______ factor, and every angle stays the same.
**Answer:** scale.

**7. (Level 3)** Two translations add: (2, 1) then (1, 3) is the same as one translation by (3, ______).
**Answer:** 4.

**8. (Level 3)** Reflection, translation and rotation all keep a shape ______; only enlargement changes its size.
**Answer:** congruent.
