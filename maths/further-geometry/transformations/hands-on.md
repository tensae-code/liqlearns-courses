# Hands-on — Transformations

## Mirror drawing
Draw half a butterfly, a face, a leaf. Hold a mirror on the
fold line and draw what the mirror shows. The two halves
together are one shape, reflected. Then write your name and
read it in the mirror — the letters flip, the way "ambulance"
does.

## The slide game
On squared paper, one person calls a translation ("4 right,
3 down") and the other moves a shape without lifting the
pencil from the rule: every corner moves the same way. Check
by measuring: the image must sit exactly parallel to the
object.

## Quarter-turns
Pin a paper triangle to a board with one pin through a corner.
Turn it a quarter-turn, trace it. Turn it again, trace it.
Four traces make the full turn. Mark which corner is where
after each turn — A, B, C become A′, B′, C′ and travel round.

## Growing from a point
Pick a centre point on paper. Draw a small triangle near it.
Draw straight lines from the centre through each corner, and
mark the points twice as far along each line. Join them: the
enlarged triangle, scale factor 2, made by hand the way
Kazakhstan's homothety works.
