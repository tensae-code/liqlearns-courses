# Congruence and similarity — short answer

Answer in a word or a short sentence.

**1. (Level 1)** What does congruent mean?
**Answer:** The same shape and the same size — an exact fit.

**2. (Level 1)** Name the congruence check that uses two angles and the side between them.
**Answer:** ASA.

**3. (Level 1)** Name the congruence check used only for right-angled triangles, using the hypotenuse and one other side.
**Answer:** RHS.

**4. (Level 2)** What stays the same in two similar shapes, even though the size changes?
**Answer:** The angles (and the shape).

**5. (Level 2)** A shape is enlarged by scale factor 4. A side of 3 cm becomes how long?
**Answer:** 12 cm.

**6. (Level 2)** A stick 1 m tall casts a 2 m shadow. At the same time a tree casts a 10 m shadow. How tall is the tree?
**Answer:** 5 m.

**7. (Level 3)** Two similar solids have length scale factor 3. By what factor does the volume grow?
**Answer:** 27 (3³).

**8. (Level 3)** A map has scale factor 1/100 from real life. A real field has area 10,000 m². What area does it take on the map?
**Answer:** 1 m² (10,000 × (1/100)² = 10,000 × 1/10,000).
