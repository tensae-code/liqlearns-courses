# Congruence and similarity — true or false

Say true or false, and why in one line.

**1. (Level 1)** Two congruent shapes always have the same area. — **True.** An exact fit means the same area.

**2. (Level 1)** If two triangles have one matching angle, they are congruent. — **False.** One angle is not enough; a whole check (SSS, SAS, ASA, AAS or RHS) is needed.

**3. (Level 1)** Turning a shape over can change whether it is congruent to another shape. — **False.** Flipping and turning keep congruence; that is how the cut-out test works.

**4. (Level 2)** Two similar shapes always have equal angles. — **True.** Similarity keeps every angle.

**5. (Level 2)** All squares are similar to each other. — **True.** Every square has the same shape; only the size changes.

**6. (Level 2)** All rectangles are similar to each other. — **False.** Rectangles can have different side ratios (a long thin one is not the same shape as a nearly square one).

**7. (Level 3)** Doubling every length of a shape doubles its area. — **False.** Doubling lengths multiplies area by 2² = 4.

**8. (Level 3)** Two similar cubes, one with edges twice the other, hold 8 times as much. — **True.** Volume takes k³, and 2³ = 8.
