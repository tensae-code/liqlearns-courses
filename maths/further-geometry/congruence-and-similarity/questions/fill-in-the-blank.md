# Congruence and similarity — fill in the blank

Put the missing word or number in the space.

**1. (Level 1)** Congruent shapes have the same shape and the same ______.
**Answer:** size.

**2. (Level 1)** The congruence check SSS uses three matching ______.
**Answer:** sides.

**3. (Level 1)** In SAS, the angle used is the one ______ the two sides.
**Answer:** between.

**4. (Level 2)** Similar shapes have matching angles equal and matching sides in the same ______.
**Answer:** ratio (or proportion).

**5. (Level 2)** The number that links a shape to its enlargement is the ______ factor.
**Answer:** scale.

**6. (Level 2)** A triangle with sides 5, 12, 13 enlarged by scale factor 2 has sides 10, 24, ______.
**Answer:** 26.

**7. (Level 3)** If lengths are multiplied by k, areas are multiplied by k to the power ______.
**Answer:** 2.

**8. (Level 3)** If lengths are multiplied by k, volumes are multiplied by k to the power ______.
**Answer:** 3.
