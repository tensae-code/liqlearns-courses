Join the two sides that go together.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Match each check to what it uses.
- SSS — a) two sides and the angle between them
- SAS — b) three sides
- ASA — c) two angles and the side between them
- RHS — d) hypotenuse and one other side (right triangles)
**Answer:** SSS–b, SAS–a, ASA–c, RHS–d.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Match each word to its meaning.
- Congruent — a) same shape, different size
- Similar — b) same shape and same size
- Scale factor — c) the number every length is multiplied by
- Similarity ratio — d) another name for the scale factor
**Answer:** Congruent–b, Similar–a, Scale factor–c, Similarity ratio–d.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Match each scale factor k to what it does to area.
- k = 2 — a) area × 9
- k = 3 — b) area × 4
- k = 4 — c) area × 16
- k = 5 — d) area × 25
**Answer:** 2–b, 3–a, 4–c, 5–d.
