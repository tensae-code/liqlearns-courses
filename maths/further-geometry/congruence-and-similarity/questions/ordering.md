# Congruence and similarity — ordering

Put each set in order, smallest to largest (or in the order the
steps are done).

**1. (Level 1)** Order these congruence checks by how much they ask you to match: ASA (2 angles + 1 side), SSS (3 sides), SAS (2 sides + 1 angle) — fewest pieces of information first.
**Answer:** ASA and SAS both use 3 pieces, SSS uses 3 pieces — all three use three pieces. (The point: every check needs exactly three matching pieces.)

**2. (Level 2)** A shape is enlarged by scale factor 2, then the result is enlarged by scale factor 3. Order the scale factors of the steps and give the overall scale factor: step 1, step 2, overall.
**Answer:** 2, then 3, overall 6.

**3. (Level 2)** Order these from smallest to largest scale factor: a photo shrunk to half, a photo doubled, a photo tripled, a photo unchanged.
**Answer:** half (1/2), unchanged (1), doubled (2), tripled (3).

**4. (Level 3)** Lengths × 2, areas × 4, volumes × 8. Order the growth factors from smallest to largest.
**Answer:** 2 (lengths), 4 (areas), 8 (volumes).

**5. (Level 3)** Order these area growth factors from smallest to largest: k = 2, k = 5, k = 3, k = 4.
**Answer:** k = 2 (×4), k = 3 (×9), k = 4 (×16), k = 5 (×25).
