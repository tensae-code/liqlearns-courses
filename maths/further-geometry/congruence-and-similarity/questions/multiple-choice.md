# Congruence and similarity — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Level 1)** Two shapes are congruent when they are —
a) the same shape, any size  b) the same shape AND the same size  c) the same size, any shape
**Answer: b) the same shape AND the same size.**

**2. (Level 1)** Which check proves two triangles congruent from all three sides?
a) SSS  b) AA  c) SAS for similarity
**Answer: a) SSS.**

**3. (Level 1)** Two triangles have two sides and the angle between them equal. They are congruent by —
a) ASA  b) SAS  c) RHS
**Answer: b) SAS.**

**4. (Level 2)** Two shapes are similar when they are —
a) the same shape, different size  b) the same size, different shape  c) exactly the same in every way
**Answer: a) the same shape, different size.**

**5. (Level 2)** Two triangles have two matching angles. They are similar by —
a) SSS  b) SAS  c) AA
**Answer: c) AA.**

**6. (Level 2)** A triangle with sides 3, 4, 5 is compared with one with sides 6, 8, 10. The scale factor (small to big) is —
a) 2  b) 3  c) 1/2
**Answer: a) 2.**

**7. (Level 3)** Lengths are multiplied by 3. Areas are multiplied by —
a) 3  b) 6  c) 9
**Answer: c) 9** — area takes k², and 3² = 9.

**8. (Level 3)** Lengths are multiplied by 2. Volumes are multiplied by —
a) 2  b) 4  c) 8
**Answer: c) 8** — volume takes k³, and 2³ = 8.
