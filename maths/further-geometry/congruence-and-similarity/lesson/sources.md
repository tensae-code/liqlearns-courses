**Acceptance count: taught in 18 countries (Libya, Jordan, Egypt,
Kazakhstan, Vietnam, Sierra Leone, South Africa, Armenia, Greece, Laos, Yemen, Indonesia, Nicaragua, Ethiopia, South Sudan, Iraq, Bangladesh, Nepal).**
Libya carries the fullest single treatment — congruence tests
including RHS, similarity, applications, and the k² area and k³
volume rules in one chapter (Grade 9, Chapter 8, pp.156–193).
Jordan carries congruence, scale drawing, similarity and
enlargement in Grade 7 and the three similarity cases plus
dilation in Grade 8. Egypt carries congruence with AAS in Grade
8, similarity and dilation in Preparatory 3, and the proved
area-ratio theorem in Secondary 1. Kazakhstan carries the
congruence criteria in Grade 7 Geometry and the similarity
transformations, homothety and the three criteria in Grade 9
Geometry. Vietnam carries congruent triangles in Grade 7 and
similar triangles and similar figures in Grade 8. Sierra Leone
carries the whole block as JSS 3 practice lessons (L48–L55).
South Africa carries congruent and similar triangles as
worksheet practice in Grades 8–9. Armenia carries the congruence
criteria in Grade 7 Geometry and similar triangles in Grade 8.
Greece carries congruence, Thales, homothety and similarity in
Grade 9. Honest negative: Libya's Grade 9 teaches all of this
without Pythagoras — similarity is done without it, and the
lesson does not lean on Pythagoras anywhere. Laos touches this lesson only in part — similarity only: the midpoint theorem, Thales' theorem and similarity cases in Grade 7 — and teaches no congruence cases. Yemen carries congruence and similarity whole — triangle congruence by tracing and overlay in Grade 4, the four congruence cases in Grade 7, and Thales' theorem and similarity in Grade 8. Its Grade 8 Part 2 Unit 7 teaches equivalence (equal area), not congruence, and is not stocked here. Indonesia carries similarity in Grade 7 Merdeka — similar triangles through equal corresponding angles and proportional sides, with congruence explicitly not taught and no formal SSS/SAS/AA labels — and congruence in polygons and triangles in Grade 9. Nicaragua carries congruence and similarity from intuitive congruence in Grade 5, through the ASA/SSS/SAS criteria with proof and the right-triangle criteria in Grade 8, to similarity with the AA/LLL/LAL criteria, proofs, Thales' theorem and its converse and the mid-segment theorem in Grade 9, with similarity of triangles as the basis for trigonometry in Grade 10. Book correction carried here: the Grade 9 book's p. 98 conclusion box mislabels the LLL similarity criterion "congruencia" — LLL is a similarity criterion, not a congruence criterion, and it is taught here as similarity. Ethiopia carries congruence and similarity from congruency by SSS, SAS, ASA and RHS with an introduction to similarity in Grade 7, through similarity of figures with the AA, SAS and SSS tests and the mid-point theorem in Grade 8, to congruency and similarity with short proofs, the perimeter and area ratios and construction by the ray method in Grade 9 — with the Grade 7 book's p. 206 similarity conclusion corrected to carry the ~ between ΔDEF and ΔXYZ. South Sudan touches this lesson only in part — similarity and enlargement only: enlargement with scale factor and centre and similar figures in Secondary 1, with the Teacher's Guide's fuller treatment of enlargement, scale factors, centre of enlargement and similar figures as teacher evidence (pp. 58–60). No congruence cases or tests are taught anywhere in South Sudan's set. Iraq carries congruence and similarity from a first meeting in Grade 4, through congruence and similarity in Grade 7 and triangle congruence in Grade 8, to similarity with medians and the 2:1 centroid, Thales and dilation in the coordinate plane in Grade 9. Bangladesh carries congruence and similarity from the concept taught through leaves, books, photos and a paper aeroplane in Grade 7, to similar triangles in Grade 8 — with the honest limit that Grade 9 teaches no congruence or similarity theorems, so the SSS/SAS/ASA checks and the k²/k³ area and volume rules in this lesson rest on the other countries. Nepal carries congruence and similarity from similar vs congruent figures in Grade 7, through SSS and RHS congruence with AA/SSS/SAS similarity in Grade 8 — with the book's similar-triangles answer corrected (x = 45 cm, not 20 cm) — to similar triangles in Grade 9.
