# Video script — Congruence and similarity

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Cut a triangle. Cut it again, the same.

Lay one on the other. Same sides. Same corners.
Turn it over. Still the same!
That is **congruent**. Same shape AND same size.

Now draw it twice as big. Every side, twice.
Same shape — but it will not fit on top.
That is **similar**. Same shape, different size.

How do you prove it? Three sides match — SSS!
Two sides and the angle between — SAS!
Two angles — AA — and the triangles are similar!

And the big secret: double the length,
four times the area, eight times the volume.
Two, four, eight. Length, area, volume.

Now you. Take a stick. Measure its shadow.
Measure the tree's shadow. Same time, same sun.
The small triangle and the big triangle are similar —
so the tree's height is yours to find!
