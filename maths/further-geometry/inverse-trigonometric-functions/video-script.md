# Video script — Inverse trigonometric functions

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Sine takes an angle. It gives a number.
Can we go back? Number in — angle out!

Yes! But careful. One number comes from MANY angles.
So first we keep only ONE piece of sine.
From minus ninety to plus ninety. That piece can be undone.

The undoing of sine is **arcsine**.
Arcsine of one half is thirty degrees — the angle whose sine is one half!

Arccosine answers between zero and one hundred and eighty.
Arctangent answers between minus ninety and ninety.

Now you. I give you a number. You give me the angle.
And check: is your angle inside the range? That is undoing.
