Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: state the restricted domain and the range for arcsine, arccosine and arctangent, and say why a restriction is needed at all.

## Sub lesson 2 — Evaluating Inverse Values
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: give exact inverse values for the special ratios, and use a calculator for other values, always landing inside the range.
