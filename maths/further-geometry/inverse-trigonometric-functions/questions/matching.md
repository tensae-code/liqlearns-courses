**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Match the inverse to its range: (i) arcsine, (ii) arccosine, (iii) arctangent — (a) (−90°, 90°), (b) [0°, 180°], (c) [−90°, 90°].
**Answer:** (i)–(c), (ii)–(b), (iii)–(a) — each range is the restricted domain of its function.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Match the value to its inverse result: (i) arcsin(√2/2), (ii) arccos(√2/2), (iii) arctan(0) — (a) 0°, (b) 45°, (c) 45°.
**Answer:** (i)–(b) 45°, (ii)–(c) 45°, (iii)–(a) 0° — sin 45° = cos 45° = √2/2, and tan 0° = 0.
