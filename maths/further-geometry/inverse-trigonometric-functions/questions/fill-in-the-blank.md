# Inverse trigonometric functions — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the one right word or value.

**1. (Level 1)** Sine is restricted to ______ before arcsine is defined.
**Answer:** [−90°, 90°] — the one rising piece, where sine never repeats a value.

**2. (Level 1)** Arccosine answers always lie between ______ and 180°.
**Answer:** 0° — its range is [0°, 180°].

**3. (Level 2)** arctan(√3) = ______.
**Answer:** 60° — tan 60° = √3, inside the arctangent range.

**4. (Level 2)** arcsin(1) = ______.
**Answer:** 90° — the top of the arcsine range.

**5. (Level 3)** If θ = arcsin x, a right triangle for θ has hypotenuse 1 and opposite ______.
**Answer:** x — because sin θ = x/1 = x.
