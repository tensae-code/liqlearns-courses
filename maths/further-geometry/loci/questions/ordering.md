# Loci: paths of moving points — ordering

Put each list in the right order.

**1. (Level 1)** Order Sierra Leone's loci lessons as the course teaches them: L95 (distance from a line), L92 (distance from a point), L96 (mixed practice), L93 (equidistant from two points).
**Answer:** L92 (p. 126), L93 (p. 129), L95 (p. 135), L96 (p. 138) — point, two points, line, then mixed (L94, equidistant from two lines, p. 132, sits between L93 and L95).

**2. (Level 2)** Order the steps for constructing the perpendicular bisector of AB: (a) draw the line through the two crossing points, (b) open the compasses wider than half of AB, (c) draw arcs from A crossing above and below, (d) draw arcs from B with the same opening.
**Answer:** (b), (c), (d), (a) — set the opening, arc from both ends, then rule the line through the crossings.

**3. (Level 3)** Order the two halves of Iran's both-directions locus proof for the circle, as a proof is written: (a) show every point at the fixed distance from O lies on the circle, (b) fix the claim to be proved, (c) show every point on the circle is at the fixed distance from O.
**Answer:** (b), (c), (a) — state the claim, prove the path obeys the rule, then prove the rule gives nothing outside the path (Geometry (3), Ch. 2 L1, printed pp. 36–38).
