# Loci: paths of moving points — fill in the blank

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. Fill each blank with the one right word or value.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A locus is the set of all points satisfying a given ______.
**Answer:** condition — the rule the moving point must obey (Turapbekov, Grade 7, p. 142).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The locus of points at a fixed distance from a fixed point is a ______, with the fixed point as its centre.
**Answer:** circle — drawn with one fixed compass opening (Sierra Leone, L92).

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The locus of points equidistant from two given points is the ______ bisector of the segment joining them.
**Answer:** perpendicular — the line through the segment's midpoint at 90° (L93).

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** The locus of points at a given distance from a given straight line consists of ______ parallel lines.
**Answer:** two — one on each side of the line (L95).

**5. (Sub lesson 4)** Smirnov's Geometry 7 places its loci section, §21, immediately before the chapter's ______ problems.
**Answer:** construction — loci first, then the constructions built from them (Chapter 4, as filed).

**6. (Sub lesson 4)** In Greece's book, the basic locus in a circle is the ______ of a circle subtending a known angle.
**Answer:** arc — the points on it see the chord under that same angle (Euclidean Geometry Volume A, Ch. 6, chapter opens printed p. 127).
