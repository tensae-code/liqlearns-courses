# Loci: paths of moving points — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the one right word or value.

**1. (Level 1)** A locus is the set of all points satisfying a given ______.
**Answer:** condition — the rule the moving point must obey (Turapbekov, Grade 7, p. 142).

**2. (Level 1)** The locus of points at a fixed distance from a fixed point is a ______, with the fixed point as its centre.
**Answer:** circle — drawn with one fixed compass opening (Sierra Leone, L92).

**3. (Level 2)** The locus of points equidistant from two given points is the ______ bisector of the segment joining them.
**Answer:** perpendicular — the line through the segment's midpoint at 90° (L93).

**4. (Level 2)** The locus of points at a given distance from a given straight line consists of ______ parallel lines.
**Answer:** two — one on each side of the line (L95).

**5. (Level 3)** Smirnov's Geometry 7 places its loci section, §21, immediately before the chapter's ______ problems.
**Answer:** construction — loci first, then the constructions built from them (Chapter 4, as filed).
