# Loci: paths of moving points — short answer

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Level 1)** In one line, say what the word "locus" means in this lesson.
**Answer:** The set of all points that satisfy a given condition (Turapbekov, Grade 7, Ch. 4 §1, printed p. 142).

**2. (Level 1)** A point moves so that it is always 4 cm from a fixed point O. Name the locus and its radius.
**Answer:** A circle, centre O, radius 4 cm (Sierra Leone, L92, printed p. 126).

**3. (Level 1)** A point moves so that it is always 3 cm from a straight line. Describe the locus fully.
**Answer:** Two straight lines, parallel to the given line, one on each side at 3 cm from it (L95, printed p. 135).

**4. (Level 2)** Name the locus of points equidistant from two fixed points A and B.
**Answer:** The perpendicular bisector of the segment AB (L93, printed p. 129).

**5. (Level 2)** Two straight roads cross. A street light must be the same perpendicular distance from both roads. Where can it stand?
**Answer:** On either of the two bisectors of the angles between the roads (L94, printed p. 132).

**6. (Level 2)** Why does the circumcentre of a triangle lie on the perpendicular bisector of every side? Answer in locus language.
**Answer:** It must be equidistant from the two endpoints of every side, and the points equidistant from two points form exactly that side's perpendicular bisector.

**7. (Level 3)** Iran's catalogue includes the centres of all circles tangent to a given line at one fixed point of it. What is the locus of those centres?
**Answer:** The line perpendicular to the given line at that fixed point (Geometry (3), Ch. 2 L1, printed pp. 38–39).

**8. (Level 3)** A point P is equidistant from points A and B, and is 5 cm from point C. Drawing the two loci, what are the possible positions of P, in words?
**Answer:** Where the perpendicular bisector of AB meets the circle centre C radius 5 cm — the crossing points of the two loci (mixed method, L96, printed p. 138).

**9. (Level 3)** In Greece's inscribed-figures chapter, what locus does a point form when it sees a fixed chord AB under a known, unchanging angle?
**Answer:** An arc of a circle over AB — the arc subtending that known angle (Euclidean Geometry Volume A, Ch. 6, chapter opens printed p. 127).
