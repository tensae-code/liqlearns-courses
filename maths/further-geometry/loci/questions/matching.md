# Loci: paths of moving points — matching

Match each item on the left with the one right item on the right.

**1. (Level 1)** Match the rule to its locus: (i) fixed distance from a point, (ii) fixed distance from a straight line, (iii) equidistant from two points — (a) the perpendicular bisector, (b) a circle, (c) two parallel lines.
**Answer:** (i)–(b), (ii)–(c), (iii)–(a) — Sierra Leone's L92, L95 and L93 in that order.

**2. (Level 2)** Match the Sierra Leone lesson to its locus: (i) L92, (ii) L94, (iii) L96 — (a) points equidistant from two straight lines, (b) points at a given distance from a given point, (c) mixed construction practice.
**Answer:** (i)–(b), (ii)–(a), (iii)–(c) — the course order as filed (printed pp. 126, 132, 138).

**3. (Level 3)** Match the book to its loci work: (i) Iran, Geometry (3), (ii) Turapbekov, Geometry 7, (iii) Smirnov, Geometry 7 — (a) the locus defined as a topic, Ch. 4 §1 p. 142, (b) loci as their own section, §21, before the construction problems, (c) the circle proved as a locus in both directions, with a catalogue of standard loci.
**Answer:** (i)–(c), (ii)–(a), (iii)–(b) — the three countries' loci treatments as filed.
