# Video script — The special lines and centres of a triangle

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

One triangle. Four special lines. Four centres hiding inside!

A **median** runs from a corner to the middle of the other side.
Three medians meet at the **centroid** — the balancing point!
And the centroid cuts every median two to one.

An **angle bisector** cuts a corner angle in half.
Three bisectors meet at the **incentre** —
the centre of the circle that fits INSIDE, touching all three sides.

A **perpendicular bisector** cuts a side in half, standing straight up.
Three of them meet at the **circumcentre** —
the same distance from all three corners. OA equals OB equals OC!

An **altitude** drops straight down from a corner.
Three altitudes meet at the **orthocentre**.

And outside? Three more circles — the **excircles** —
each one touching one side from outside!

Now you. I name a centre. You tell me its lines!
