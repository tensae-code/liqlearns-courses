# The special lines and centres of a triangle — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB
LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure — README, "Units, Lessons and Sub
Lessons"). Practice is tied to the skill number, as before. The
questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson
view.

The sub lessons are the depths inside this one thing — a triangle's special lines and the
centres they make — in the order Kuwait's Grade 9 book teaches
them: the lines first, then the centres, then the circles outside.

Mapping from the old depth layers: old Level 1 = Sub lesson 1; old Level 2 = Sub lesson 2; old Level 3 = Sub lesson 3. One paragraph of the lesson text — the median to the hypotenuse and the side opposite 30° — mixes old Level 2's median fact with old Level 3's 30° side; it is kept whole in Sub lesson 2, where it stands in the text, and the overlap is noted in the plan's Sub lessons list.

## Sub lesson 1 — The Four Special Lines and the Mid-Segment
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: draw and name a median, an angle bisector, a perpendicular bisector and an altitude; state and use the mid-segment theorem (parallel to the third side, half its length).

## Sub lesson 2 — The Four Centres and Their Facts
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: name the centre each family of lines makes, split a median 2:1 at the centroid, use OA = OB = OC at the circumcentre, and use the median to the hypotenuse equalling half the hypotenuse.

## Sub lesson 3 — Divisions, the 30° Side and Excircles
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: divide a side by the angle-bisector theorem, find the side opposite 30° in a right triangle, explain an excircle and its centre, and spot the corrected incentre label.
