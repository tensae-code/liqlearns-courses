# The special lines and centres of a triangle — questions by level

Practice by level, tied to the skill number. The questions live in
the type files; this file is the by-level view. The levels are the
depths inside this one thing — a triangle's special lines and the
centres they make — in the order Kuwait's Grade 9 book teaches
them: the lines first, then the centres, then the circles outside.

## Level 1 — the four special lines and the mid-segment
Level 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: draw and name a median, an angle bisector, a perpendicular bisector and an altitude; state and use the mid-segment theorem (parallel to the third side, half its length).

## Level 2 — the four centres and their facts
Level 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: name the centre each family of lines makes, split a median 2:1 at the centroid, use OA = OB = OC at the circumcentre, and use the median to the hypotenuse equalling half the hypotenuse.

## Level 3 — divisions, the 30° side and excircles
Level 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: divide a side by the angle-bisector theorem, find the side opposite 30° in a right triangle, explain an excircle and its centre, and spot the corrected incentre label.
