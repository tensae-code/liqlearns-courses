# The special lines and centres of a triangle — matching

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — The Four Special Lines and the Mid-Segment · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — The Four Centres and Their Facts · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Divisions, the 30° Side and Excircles.

Match each item on the left with the one right item on the right.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Match the line to its definition: (i) median, (ii) altitude, (iii) angle bisector, (iv) perpendicular bisector — (a) splits a vertex angle into two equal angles, (b) cuts a side at its midpoint at 90°, (c) vertex to the midpoint of the opposite side, (d) from a vertex at 90° to the opposite side.
**Answer:** (i)–(c), (ii)–(d), (iii)–(a), (iv)–(b) — the four special lines of Kuwait's Unit 7 and Laos's Lesson 33.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Match the centre to the lines that make it: (i) centroid, (ii) incentre, (iii) circumcentre, (iv) orthocentre — (a) altitudes, (b) angle bisectors, (c) medians, (d) perpendicular bisectors.
**Answer:** (i)–(c), (ii)–(b), (iii)–(d), (iv)–(a) — each centre is the concurrency point of one family.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Match the centre to its special fact: (i) centroid, (ii) circumcentre, (iii) excentre — (a) OA = OB = OC, (b) splits every median 2:1, (c) sits at the intersection of external bisectors, outside the triangle.
**Answer:** (i)–(b), (ii)–(a), (iii)–(c) — the facts as filed by Cambodia (Lesson 16) and Kazakhstan (Geometry 9 §20).
