# The special lines and centres of a triangle — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — The Four Special Lines and the Mid-Segment · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — The Four Centres and Their Facts · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Divisions, the 30° Side and Excircles.

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A median of a triangle joins:
a) A vertex to the midpoint of the opposite side  b) Two midpoints  c) A vertex to the opposite side at 90°
**Answer:** a) — a median runs vertex to midpoint (Kuwait, Grade 9 Term 2, Unit 7).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The segment joining the midpoints of two sides of a triangle is 14 cm at the third side. The mid-segment itself is parallel to that side and measures:
a) 28 cm  b) 14 cm  c) 7 cm
**Answer:** c) 7 cm — the mid-segment is half the third side and parallel to it (Kuwait 7-1; Yemen 7-2).

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** A perpendicular bisector of a side:
a) Always passes through the opposite vertex  b) Cuts the side at its midpoint at 90°  c) Splits a vertex angle in half
**Answer:** b) — it bisects the SIDE at right angles; passing through a vertex is not part of it.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The three medians of a triangle meet at:
a) The orthocentre  b) The circumcentre  c) The centroid
**Answer:** c) — the centroid, the balancing point found by Laos's pin experiment (Grade 7, Part 5, Lesson 33).

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** A median is 18 cm long. The distance from the vertex to the centroid along it is:
a) 9 cm  b) 12 cm  c) 6 cm
**Answer:** b) 12 cm — the centroid splits the median 2:1, so the vertex part is (2/3) × 18 (Cambodia, Lesson 16 §1).

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** At the circumcentre O of a triangle, the true equality is:
a) OA = OB = OC  b) OA = AB = AC  c) OA = the longest side
**Answer:** a) — O is the same distance from all three vertices (Laos, Part 5, Lesson 33; Cambodia, Lesson 16 §2).

**7. (Sub lesson 2)** A right-angled triangle has hypotenuse 10 cm. The median from the right-angle vertex to the hypotenuse is:
a) 10 cm  b) 20 cm  c) 5 cm
**Answer:** c) 5 cm — the median to the hypotenuse equals half the hypotenuse (Kuwait 7-2; Egypt 3-4).

**8. (Sub lesson 3)** In a right triangle the hypotenuse is 12 cm. The side opposite the 30° angle is:
a) 6 cm  b) 4 cm  c) 12 cm
**Answer:** a) 6 cm — the side opposite 30° is half the hypotenuse (Yemen, 7-4, printed p. 77).

**9. (Sub lesson 3)** The centre of a triangle's excircle is found at the intersection of:
a) The three medians  b) The external bisectors  c) The three altitudes
**Answer:** b) — excentres sit at the intersections of the external bisectors (Kazakhstan, Geometry 9 §20).
