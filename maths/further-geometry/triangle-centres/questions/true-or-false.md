# The special lines and centres of a triangle — true or false

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — The Four Special Lines and the Mid-Segment · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — The Four Centres and Their Facts · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Divisions, the 30° Side and Excircles.

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A mid-segment is parallel to the third side of the triangle. True or false?
**Answer:** True — parallel and half as long (Kuwait 7-1; Yemen 7-2, printed p. 70).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Every perpendicular bisector of a triangle passes through a vertex. True or false?
**Answer:** False — it cuts a side at its midpoint at 90°; it passes through a vertex only in special (isosceles) cases.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The centroid cuts each median into two equal halves. True or false?
**Answer:** False — it divides the median 2:1, the longer part at the vertex (Cambodia, Lesson 16 §1).

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In an obtuse triangle, the orthocentre can lie outside the triangle. True or false?
**Answer:** True — two altitudes fall on extended sides, and their meeting point sits outside.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** The intersection of a triangle's internal angle bisectors is correctly labelled the "Circum Center", as printed in Kuwait's Grade 10 Term 2 book, p. 23. True or false?
**Answer:** False — that printed label is a book error, taught corrected in this lesson: the angle bisectors meet at the Incenter; the circumcentre is made by the perpendicular bisectors.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** An excircle touches one side of the triangle and the extensions of the other two sides. True or false?
**Answer:** True — that outside touching is what makes it an excircle (Kazakhstan, Geometry 8 §1.7; Geometry 9 §20).
