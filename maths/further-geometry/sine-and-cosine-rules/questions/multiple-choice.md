# Sine and cosine rules — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Level 1)** In the sine rule, side a is matched with —
a) sin B  b) sin A  c) sin C
**Answer: b) sin A** — each side goes with the angle across from it.

**2. (Level 1)** a / sin A = b / sin B. If a = 6, A = 30°, B = 45°, then b is about —
a) 8.49  b) 6  c) 4.24
**Answer: a) 8.49** — b = 6 × sin 45° / sin 30° = 6 × 0.707 / 0.5.

**3. (Level 1)** The sine rule is used when you know —
a) three sides  b) a side and the angle across from it  c) two sides and the angle between them
**Answer: b) a side and the angle across from it.**

**4. (Level 2)** The cosine rule says a² = —
a) b² + c² + 2bc cos A  b) b² + c² − 2bc cos A  c) b² − c² + 2bc cos A
**Answer: b) b² + c² − 2bc cos A.**

**5. (Level 2)** Two sides are 5 and 7 with 60° between them. The third side squared is —
a) 74  b) 39  c) 109
**Answer: b) 39** — 25 + 49 − 70 × 0.5 = 39.

**6. (Level 2)** When angle A = 90°, the cosine rule becomes —
a) the sine rule  b) Pythagoras  c) the area formula
**Answer: b) Pythagoras** — cos 90° = 0, so a² = b² + c².

**7. (Level 3)** Two sides are 8 and 10 with 30° between them. The area is —
a) 40  b) 20  c) 80
**Answer: b) 20** — ½ × 8 × 10 × sin 30° = 40 × 0.5.

**8. (Level 3)** You know three sides and want an angle. Use —
a) the sine rule  b) the cosine rule  c) SOHCAHTOA
**Answer: b) the cosine rule.**
