# Sine and cosine rules — short answer

Answer in a word or a short sentence.

**1. (Level 1)** Write the sine rule.
**Answer:** a / sin A = b / sin B = c / sin C.

**2. (Level 1)** In the sine rule, what does a / sin A also equal?
**Answer:** 2R, where R is the circumradius (the radius of the circle through the three corners).

**3. (Level 1)** A triangle has A = 30°, a = 4, B = 60°. Find b.
**Answer:** About 6.93 (b = 4 × sin 60° / sin 30° = 4 × 0.866 / 0.5).

**4. (Level 2)** Write the cosine rule for side a.
**Answer:** a² = b² + c² − 2bc cos A.

**5. (Level 2)** Two sides are 3 and 4 with 90° between them. What is the third side?
**Answer:** 5 (3² + 4² = 25, and cos 90° = 0).

**6. (Level 2)** A triangle has sides 5, 7, 8. The angle across from the side of length 7 is 60°. How was that found?
**Answer:** With the cosine rule: cos B = (25 + 64 − 49) / 80 = 0.5.

**7. (Level 3)** What is the area of a triangle with sides 6 and 8 and 90° between them?
**Answer:** 24 (½ × 6 × 8 × sin 90° = 24 × 1).

**8. (Level 3)** What is the ambiguous case?
**Answer:** When the sine rule gives two possible angles (a sharp one and its supplement) and both must be checked.
