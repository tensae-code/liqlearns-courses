# Sine and cosine rules — fill in the blank

Put the missing word or number in the space.

**1. (Level 1)** Side a sits across from angle ______.
**Answer:** A.

**2. (Level 1)** The sine rule says a / sin A = b / sin ______.
**Answer:** B.

**3. (Level 1)** Each fraction in the sine rule also equals 2R, where R is the radius of the ______.
**Answer:** circumcircle (the circle through the triangle's three corners).

**4. (Level 2)** The cosine rule: a² = b² + c² − 2bc cos ______.
**Answer:** A.

**5. (Level 2)** When A = 90°, cos A = ______, so the cosine rule becomes Pythagoras.
**Answer:** 0.

**6. (Level 2)** To find an angle from three sides, first find its ______ with the cosine rule, then use the inverse cosine.
**Answer:** cosine.

**7. (Level 3)** Area = ½ ab sin ______ (the angle between sides a and b).
**Answer:** C.

**8. (Level 3)** If sin θ = 0.6, the two possible angles are about 37° and ______°.
**Answer:** 143.
