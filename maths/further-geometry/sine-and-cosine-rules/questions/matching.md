# Sine and cosine rules — matching

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — The Sine Rule · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — The Cosine Rule · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Area and Choosing the Rule.

Join the two sides that go together.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Match each rule to what it needs.
- Sine rule — a) two sides and the angle between them
- Cosine rule — b) a side and the angle across from it
- Area formula — c) two sides and the angle between them
**Answer:** Sine rule–b, Cosine rule–a, Area formula–c. (The cosine rule and the area formula need the same knowns, but they find different things: a side or angle, and the area.)

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Match each triangle to the rule that solves it first.
- Three sides known — a) sine rule
- Side and its angle across known — b) cosine rule
- Two sides and the included angle — c) cosine rule
**Answer:** Three sides–b, side + angle across–a, two sides + included angle–c.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Match each formula to its name.
- a / sin A = b / sin B — a) cosine rule
- a² = b² + c² − 2bc cos A — b) area formula
- Area = ½ab sin C — c) sine rule
**Answer:** a/sin A–c, a²–a, Area–b.
