# Sine and cosine rules — true or false

Say true or false, and why in one line.

**1. (Level 1)** The sine rule works on any triangle, not only right-angled ones. — **True.** It needs no right angle.

**2. (Level 1)** In the sine rule, side b is matched with sin A. — **False.** Side b goes with sin B, the angle across from it.

**3. (Level 1)** a / sin A = b / sin B = c / sin C, all three equal. — **True.** That is the sine rule.

**4. (Level 2)** The cosine rule needs the angle between the two known sides. — **True.** The formula uses the included angle.

**5. (Level 2)** Pythagoras is a special case of the sine rule. — **False.** Pythagoras is the cosine rule with a 90° angle (cos 90° = 0).

**6. (Level 2)** If a² > b² + c², the angle at A is obtuse. — **True.** Comparing a² with b² + c² decides sharp, right or wide.

**7. (Level 3)** The area formula ½ab sin C needs the angle between sides a and b. — **True.** C is the included angle.

**8. (Level 3)** The sine rule can never give two answers for an angle. — **False.** That is the ambiguous case: a sharp angle and its supplement share a sine.
