# Sine and cosine rules — ordering

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — The Sine Rule · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — The Cosine Rule · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Area and Choosing the Rule.

Put each set in order, smallest to largest or in the order the
steps are done.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Order these steps for using the sine rule: (a) write the matching pairs, (b) put the knowns into a/sin A = b/sin B, (c) solve for the unknown.
**Answer:** (a), (b), (c).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Two sides are fixed at 5 and 7. The angle between them grows: 30°, 60°, 90°. Order the third side from smallest to largest.
**Answer:** 30° (smallest), 60°, 90° (largest) — the third side grows as the included angle grows.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Order these triangles by their largest angle, using a² against b² + c²: sides 5-6-7, sides 3-4-5, sides 5-5-9.
**Answer:** 3-4-5 (right, 90°), 5-6-7 (acute — 7² = 49 < 25 + 36 = 61), 5-5-9 (obtuse — 9² = 81 > 25 + 25 = 50). Smallest largest-angle first: 5-6-7 (about 78°), 3-4-5 (90°), 5-5-9 (about 129°).

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Order these areas from smallest to largest: sides 8 and 10 with 30° between, sides 8 and 10 with 60° between, sides 8 and 10 with 90° between.
**Answer:** 30° (area 20), 60° (area ≈ 34.6), 90° (area 40).

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Order the four standard solving cases as Kazakhstan's book lists them: (a) side + two angles, (b) two sides + included angle, (c) three sides, (d) the river-width problem.
**Answer:** (a), (b), (c), (d) — the river problem comes last, using both rules in sequence.
