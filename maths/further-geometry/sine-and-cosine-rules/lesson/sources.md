**Acceptance count: taught in 12 countries (Kazakhstan, Egypt,
Jordan, Libya, Sierra Leone, Vietnam, Armenia, Greece, Laos, Yemen, Indonesia, Nicaragua).**
Kazakhstan carries the fullest treatment — all three Grade 9
Geometry books prove both rules (by vectors, by coordinates,
by altitude) and work the standard solving cases, including
the ambiguous-data discussion and the 2R form of the sine
rule. Egypt carries both rules in Grade 11 Pure (with the
ambiguous case) and in Grade 11 General. Jordan carries both
rules with bearings, the area formula ½ab·sin C, and 3-D
applications in Grade 10. Libya carries both rules with
bearings in Grade 11 Science. Sierra Leone carries both rules
as SSS 2 practice lessons with bearing applications. Vietnam
carries both rules with area formulas in Grade 10. Armenia
carries the sine and cosine laws with solving triangles in
Grade 9 Geometry. Greece carries the sine and cosine laws in
Grade 9. Honest negative: Sierra Leone's JSS never teaches
sine/cosine rules — the lesson's Sierra Leone evidence is SSS
only; Jordan's Grade 9 teaches right-triangle trigonometry
only and explicitly no sine/cosine rules, so Jordan enters
this lesson from Grade 10, not Grade 9. Laos carries the sine and cosine rules whole in Grade 9 — the unit-semicircle extension to 0–180°, the sine rule via the circumcircle, the cosine rule with the ambiguous case, and triangle area from sin A. Yemen touches this lesson only in part, at title level — Grade 11 science, Unit 9, lesson 9-6, "Solving triangles and applications", printed p. 113; the filing pins the title and page only, not whether the sine and cosine rules are the method taught. Indonesia carries the sine and cosine rules as guide-only evidence — the Grade 11 Tingkat Lanjut Revisi 2024 teacher guide teaches them with the unit circle, graphs and identities (Bab 3, pp. 115–174), but the matching Revisi student book is not in hand and the held 2021 TL student book has no sine/cosine rules, so it is worded as guide evidence throughout, like Ghana's teacher-manual evidence elsewhere. Nicaragua carries the sine and cosine rules whole in Grade 10 — the law of sines with the ambiguous case and the law of cosines with SSS and SAS cases in the student book, with the module's parallel derivation and application. Book correction carried here: the module's p. 181 introduces the law of sines with an "equilateral triangle" — the law of sines is for general (oblique) triangles, not only equilateral ones, and it is taught here in its general form.
