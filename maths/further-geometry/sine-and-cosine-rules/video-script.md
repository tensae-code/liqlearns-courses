# Video script — Sine and cosine rules

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

No right angle? No problem!

Name your triangle first. Side a sits across from angle A.
Side b across from B. Side c across from C. Small and big,
across from each other.

Now the sine rule! Side over sine of its angle.
a over sin A equals b over sin B equals c over sin C.
All three the same! Know a side and its angle — you can find
the rest!

And the cosine rule! Two sides and the angle between them?
a squared equals b squared plus c squared
minus two b c cos A.
When the angle is 90 degrees, the last bit is zero —
and it is Pythagoras again!

Area? Half a b sin C. Two sides and the angle between them —
half their product times the sine!

Now you. A river is wide. You cannot cross it.
Measure from your side — the sine rule and the cosine rule
together will tell you how wide it is!
