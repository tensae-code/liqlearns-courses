# Video script — Trigonometric equations

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

An identity is true for **every** angle.
An equation asks: **which** angles?

Sin x equals one half. Point at the circle!
The line cuts the circle **twice** — π over 6, and 5π over 6!
**Two** answers in one turn. Never stop at one!

Now go round again. And again!
Add 2π, add 4π — the **general** solution, with n in ℤ!

Stuck? Make it one ratio. Factor it. Substitute!
And for "greater than" — shade the arc on the circle!

Which angles? **Those** angles. Solved!
