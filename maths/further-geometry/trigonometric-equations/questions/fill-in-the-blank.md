# Trigonometric equations — fill-in-the-blank

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — The Basic Forms on the Unit Circle · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — The General Solution and Identity Equations · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — The Single-Term Form, Graphs and Inequalities.

Fill in the missing number or word.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** sin x = a has no solution when |a| > ___.
**Answer:** 1 — sine and cosine stay between −1 and 1.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** tan x repeats every ___ radians.
**Answer:** π — a half turn brings the same tangent back.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The general solution adds whole turns of ___ to each particular solution.
**Answer:** 2π — one full turn of the circle (for sine and cosine).

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In the general solution, n ∈ ___.
**Answer:** ℤ — n runs over all integers, forwards and backwards (Nicaragua, Unit 3).

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** To open a mixed equation, first express everything in terms of a single ___.
**Answer:** ratio — Nicaragua's module rule (Unit IV, printed pp. 177–180).

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** An equation like 2cos²x − 3cos x + 1 = 0 is a ___ in cos x.
**Answer:** quadratic — solve it by the quadratic formula or by factorising, then solve for x.

**7. (Sub lesson 3)** For an inequality, first solve the matching ___ to find the boundary angles.
**Answer:** equation — then read the satisfying arc off the unit circle (Laos, L31 then L32).

**8. (Sub lesson 3)** Cambodia's Basic book solves the basic forms in this order: cos x = a, sin x = a, tan x = a, ___ x = m.
**Answer:** cot — Ch. 3 Lesson 3, printed pp. 98–112.
