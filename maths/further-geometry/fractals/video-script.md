# Video script — Fractals: shapes inside shapes

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Look at this triangle. Now look INSIDE it. The same triangle!
Smaller — but the same! That is a **fractal**!

One step, again and again: take out the middle.
One triangle becomes three. Three become nine.
Nine become twenty-seven! The pattern never changes — only the size!

Now a snowflake. Every straight piece grows a point in its middle.
More and more pieces — and the edge gets LONGER and LONGER,
inside the same small shape!

And on a computer? One tiny rule: square the number, add c.
Again, again, again! If the number stays small — paint the point.
If it runs away — leave it. A whole world of shapes grows!

Now you. One step. Again and again. What do YOU get?
