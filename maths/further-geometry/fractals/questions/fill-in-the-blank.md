# Fractals: shapes inside shapes — fill in the blank

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Self-Similarity and the Sierpiński Figures · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — The Koch Snowflake · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Iteration: Julia and Mandelbrot.

Fill each blank with the one right word or value.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In the Sierpiński triangle, the ______ triangle is removed at every step.
**Answer:** middle — each split makes 4, and the middle one is taken away (Grade 9 Volume 2 reading, pp. 45–46).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** After two Sierpiński carpet steps, the number of surviving smallest squares is ______.
**Answer:** 64 — survivors multiply by 8 per step: 1, 8, 64.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** In the Koch step, each new segment is ______ the length of the segment it came from.
**Answer:** one third (1/3) — the segment is cut into thirds and the middle third rebuilt as a point.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** The iteration in China's senior reading is z → z² + ______, where the added value stays fixed during one orbit.
**Answer:** c — the fixed value whose fate (bounded or escaping) the reading tests (B edition, Ch. 10 reading, pp. 40–41).
