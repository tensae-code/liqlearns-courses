# Fractals: shapes inside shapes — matching

Match each item on the left with the one right item on the right.

**1. (Level 1)** Match the figure to its one step: (i) Sierpiński triangle, (ii) Sierpiński carpet, (iii) Koch snowflake — (a) split a segment in thirds and build a point on the middle third, (b) split into 4 triangles and remove the middle one, (c) split into 9 squares and remove the centre one.
**Answer:** (i)–(b), (ii)–(c), (iii)–(a) — the three figures of the Grade 9 Volume 2 reading (printed pp. 45–46).

**2. (Level 3)** Match the c value to the fate of the orbit of 0 under z → z² + c: (i) c = 0, (ii) c = 1, (iii) c = −1 — (a) escapes: 1, 2, 5, 26…, (b) stays at 0, (c) cycles: −1, 0, −1, 0…
**Answer:** (i)–(b), (ii)–(a), (iii)–(c) — bounded, escaping, and bounded-cycling, the three fates worked in the lesson from the B-edition reading (printed pp. 40–41).
