# Fractals: shapes inside shapes — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Level 1)** A fractal is a figure in which:
a) No pattern repeats  b) The same pattern appears at every scale  c) Every part is a different shape
**Answer:** b) — self-similarity: the part looks like the whole (China, Grade 9 Volume 2 reading, printed pp. 45–46).

**2. (Level 1)** In one Sierpiński triangle step, each surviving triangle produces:
a) 2 smaller surviving triangles  b) 4 smaller surviving triangles  c) 3 smaller surviving triangles
**Answer:** c) — split into 4, the middle removed, 3 survive.

**3. (Level 1)** In one Sierpiński carpet step, each surviving square produces:
a) 8 smaller surviving squares  b) 9 smaller surviving squares  c) 6 smaller surviving squares
**Answer:** a) — split into 9, the centre removed, 8 survive (Grade 9 Volume 2 reading, pp. 45–46).

**4. (Level 2)** In one Koch step, one segment becomes:
a) 3 segments  b) 4 segments, each 1/3 as long  c) 2 segments
**Answer:** b) — the middle third is replaced by two sides of a point, giving 4 segments of 1/3 length.

**5. (Level 2)** At each Koch step, the snowflake's total perimeter is multiplied by:
a) 4/3  b) 3/4  c) 4
**Answer:** a) — 4 times as many segments, each 1/3 as long: 4 × 1/3 = 4/3.

**6. (Level 3)** The Mandelbrot set, in China's senior reading, is the set of values c for which the orbit of 0 under z → z² + c:
a) Escapes at once  b) Stays bounded  c) Is not defined
**Answer:** b) — bounded orbits of 0 mark the c values inside the set (Compulsory Volume 4, B edition, Ch. 10 reading, printed pp. 40–41).
