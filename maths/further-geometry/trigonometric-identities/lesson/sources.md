**Acceptance count: taught in 12 countries (Pakistan, Libya, Yemen, Laos, China, Armenia, Kuwait, Vietnam, Cambodia, Indonesia, Ethiopia, Nicaragua).** Pakistan carries the fullest proved chain — the fundamental law on the unit circle, the addition formulas deduced, then double, half and triple proved, with one printed value taught corrected (cot(−225°) = −1, not 1). Libya carries compound, double and half formulas with the single-term form and identity proving. Yemen carries the chain in three lessons: sum/difference, double/half, and sum↔product. Laos proves cos(a − b) its own way, by the dot product, and carries the whole chain with the sin 18° prize in its exercises. China proves the chain link by link in its B-edition Volume 3. Armenia carries the basic identities, the addition formulas and sum↔product in Grade 10. Kuwait carries the basic and Pythagorean identities with proving in Grade 10 and the full formula set again in Grade 11. Vietnam carries the formula set in Bài 2 of Grade 11. Cambodia carries the addition, sum-to-product and product-to-sum formulas in Grade 11 Basic. Indonesia carries identities in its Grade 11 Tingkat Lanjut Revisi trigonometry chapter. Ethiopia carries sum/difference and double-angle work in Grade 10. Nicaragua carries the fundamental identities, the identity-versus-equation line, and step-by-step verifying in Grade 10, with the module's parallel statement of the sum, double and half formulas.
