One formula is the mother of them all!

Cosine of α minus β — cos α cos β **plus** sin α sin β.
Two unit vectors prove it. Watch!

Change β to minus β — now you have cos of α **plus** β!
Turn cosine into sine — now you have sin of α plus β!

Put β equal to α — **double angle**!
sin 2α is 2 sin α cos α!

Halve it back — **half angle**!
And here is the prize: sin 18 degrees is root five minus one, over four!

An identity is true for **every** angle.
You do not solve it. You **prove** it — one side becomes the other!
