# Trigonometric identities — fill-in-the-blank

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — The Basic Identities · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — The Addition Formulas · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Double, Half, Sums to Products, and Proving.

Fill in the missing number or word.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** sec θ = 1/___.
**Answer:** cos θ — secant is the reciprocal of cosine.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** tan²θ + 1 = ___²θ.
**Answer:** sec — divide sin²θ + cos²θ = 1 by cos²θ.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** cos(α + β) is got from cos(α − β) by replacing β with ___.
**Answer:** −β — the first move of the addition chain.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** sin 2α = ___ sin α cos α.
**Answer:** 2 — set β = α in sin(α + β).

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** sin A + sin B = 2 sin((A + B)/2) ___((A − B)/2).
**Answer:** cos — the sum-to-product form (Yemen, lesson 9-4).

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** An equation true for every allowed angle is called an ___.
**Answer:** identity — an equation true only for some angles is solved, not proved (Nicaragua, Unit 3).

**7. (Sub lesson 3)** To disprove a claimed identity, one failing ___ is enough.
**Answer:** value (counter-value) — sin(α + β) = sin α + sin β already fails at α = β = 30°.

**8. (Sub lesson 3)** In R = √(a² + b²) for a·cosθ + b·sinθ, with a = 6 and b = 8, R = ___.
**Answer:** 10 — √(36 + 64) = √100.

**9. (Sub lesson 2)** Yelemessov's Grade 9 book derives the sine addition formula from a triangle's ___.
**Answer:** area — its own proof route; cosine then comes from sine via the co-function relation (Kazakhstan, §4.4, printed p. 164).

**10. (Sub lesson 3)** In the universal substitution, cos 2α = (1 − ___)/(1 + tan²α).
**Answer:** tan²α — Soltan's double-angle forms (Kazakhstan, Algebra 9, §27, printed p. 201).

**11. (Sub lesson 2)** For a supplementary angle, sin(180° − θ) = ___ θ.
**Answer:** sin — one of the related-angle relations Iran's Grade 11 Experimental book works on the unit circle (Mathematics (2), printed pp. 77–87).

**12. (Sub lesson 3)** Jordan's Grade 12 book spends ___ full lessons on trigonometric identities before it solves equations.
**Answer:** two — lessons 1 and 2 of Unit 2 (printed pp. 38, 50); solving equations is lesson 3.
