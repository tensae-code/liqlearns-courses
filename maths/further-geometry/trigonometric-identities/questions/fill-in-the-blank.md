# Trigonometric identities — fill-in-the-blank

Fill in the missing number or word.

**1. (Level 1)** sec θ = 1/___.
**Answer:** cos θ — secant is the reciprocal of cosine.

**2. (Level 1)** tan²θ + 1 = ___²θ.
**Answer:** sec — divide sin²θ + cos²θ = 1 by cos²θ.

**3. (Level 2)** cos(α + β) is got from cos(α − β) by replacing β with ___.
**Answer:** −β — the first move of the addition chain.

**4. (Level 2)** sin 2α = ___ sin α cos α.
**Answer:** 2 — set β = α in sin(α + β).

**5. (Level 3)** sin A + sin B = 2 sin((A + B)/2) ___((A − B)/2).
**Answer:** cos — the sum-to-product form (Yemen, lesson 9-4).

**6. (Level 3)** An equation true for every allowed angle is called an ___.
**Answer:** identity — an equation true only for some angles is solved, not proved (Nicaragua, Unit 3).

**7. (Level 3)** To disprove a claimed identity, one failing ___ is enough.
**Answer:** value (counter-value) — sin(α + β) = sin α + sin β already fails at α = β = 30°.

**8. (Level 3)** In R = √(a² + b²) for a·cosθ + b·sinθ, with a = 6 and b = 8, R = ___.
**Answer:** 10 — √(36 + 64) = √100.
