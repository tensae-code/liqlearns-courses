# Trigonometric identities — short answer

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Level 1)** State the two Pythagorean partner identities beside sin²θ + cos²θ = 1.
**Answer:** tan²θ + 1 = sec²θ and 1 + cot²θ = csc²θ — Kuwait's Grade 10 list (printed pp. 107–113).

**2. (Level 1)** Given cos θ = −4/5 with θ in the second quadrant, find tan θ if sin θ = 3/5.
**Answer:** −3/4 — tan θ = sin θ/cos θ, and tangent is negative in the second quadrant.

**3. (Level 2)** How does Laos prove cos(a − b)?
**Answer:** By the dot product of two unit vectors — its own proof, Grade 10 Ch. IX L22.

**4. (Level 2)** Evaluate sin 75° exactly.
**Answer:** (√6 + √2)/4 — sin(45° + 30°), Ethiopia's worked value.

**5. (Level 2)** Write sin(α − β) in terms of sin and cos of α and β.
**Answer:** sin α cos β − cos α sin β — the addition chain, from cos(α − β) via the complement.

**6. (Level 3)** Write cos 2α in its three forms.
**Answer:** cos²α − sin²α = 2cos²α − 1 = 1 − 2sin²α — set β = α, then use sin²α + cos²α = 1.

**7. (Level 3)** Write 3cos θ + 4sin θ in the form R cos(θ − φ): give R.
**Answer:** R = 5 — R = √(3² + 4²), Libya's single-term form.

**8. (Level 3)** Give sin 18° exactly (Laos's exercise prize).
**Answer:** (√5 − 1)/4 — from sin 2θ = cos 3θ at θ = 18°.
