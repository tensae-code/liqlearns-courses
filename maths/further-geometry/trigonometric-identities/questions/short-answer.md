# Trigonometric identities — short answer

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — The Basic Identities · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — The Addition Formulas · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Double, Half, Sums to Products, and Proving.

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** State the two Pythagorean partner identities beside sin²θ + cos²θ = 1.
**Answer:** tan²θ + 1 = sec²θ and 1 + cot²θ = csc²θ — Kuwait's Grade 10 list (printed pp. 107–113).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Given cos θ = −4/5 with θ in the second quadrant, find tan θ if sin θ = 3/5.
**Answer:** −3/4 — tan θ = sin θ/cos θ, and tangent is negative in the second quadrant.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** How does Laos prove cos(a − b)?
**Answer:** By the dot product of two unit vectors — its own proof, Grade 10 Ch. IX L22.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Evaluate sin 75° exactly.
**Answer:** (√6 + √2)/4 — sin(45° + 30°), Ethiopia's worked value.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Write sin(α − β) in terms of sin and cos of α and β.
**Answer:** sin α cos β − cos α sin β — the addition chain, from cos(α − β) via the complement.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** Write cos 2α in its three forms.
**Answer:** cos²α − sin²α = 2cos²α − 1 = 1 − 2sin²α — set β = α, then use sin²α + cos²α = 1.

**7. (Sub lesson 3)** Write 3cos θ + 4sin θ in the form R cos(θ − φ): give R.
**Answer:** R = 5 — R = √(3² + 4²), Libya's single-term form.

**8. (Sub lesson 3)** Give sin 18° exactly (Laos's exercise prize).
**Answer:** (√5 − 1)/4 — from sin 2θ = cos 3θ at θ = 18°.

**9. (Sub lesson 2)** How do three of Kazakhstan's Grade 9 Algebra books prove cos(α − β)?
**Answer:** By the scalar (dot) product of vectors — Shynybekov (§4.5), Abylkasymova (Part 2, §24) and Soltan (§26) all prove it this way, as Laos does.

**10. (Sub lesson 3)** Write sin 2α using tan α alone (the universal substitution).
**Answer:** sin 2α = 2 tan α/(1 + tan²α) — Kazakhstan's Soltan book names this form (Algebra 9, §27, printed p. 201).

**11. (Sub lesson 2)** Which two lessons carry the identities work in Egypt's Pure Mathematics Grade 11, Term 2, Unit 4?
**Answer:** Lesson 4-2, trig functions of the sum and difference of two angles (printed p. 121), and lesson 4-3, trig functions of the double angle (printed p. 128) — double angle only; there is no half-angle lesson.

**12. (Sub lesson 1)** Given cos θ = 4/5 with θ acute, find sin θ the way Iran's Grade 10 book does it.
**Answer:** 3/5 — from the fundamental identity sin²θ + cos²θ = 1, positive in the first quadrant (Mathematics (1), printed pp. 42–46).
