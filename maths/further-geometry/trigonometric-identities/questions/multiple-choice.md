# Trigonometric identities — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — The Basic Identities · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — The Addition Formulas · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Double, Half, Sums to Products, and Proving.

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** sin²θ + cos²θ = —
a) 0  b) 1  c) tan θ
**Answer:** b) 1 — the Pythagorean identity, true for every θ.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** tan θ = —
a) cos θ/sin θ  b) sin θ/cos θ  c) 1/sin θ
**Answer:** b) sin θ/cos θ — the quotient identity (Nicaragua, Unit 2).

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** If sin θ = 3/5 and θ is acute, cos θ = —
a) 4/5  b) 3/4  c) 5/4
**Answer:** a) 4/5 — cos²θ = 1 − 9/25 = 16/25, positive in the first quadrant.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** cos(α − β) = —
a) cos α cos β − sin α sin β  b) cos α cos β + sin α sin β  c) sin α cos β + cos α sin β
**Answer:** b) cos α cos β + sin α sin β — the fundamental law (Pakistan, Unit 10).

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** cos 105° = —
a) (√6 + √2)/4  b) (√2 − √6)/4  c) −1/2
**Answer:** b) (√2 − √6)/4 — cos(60° + 45°), Laos's worked value.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** tan(α + β) when tan α = 1/2 and tan β = 1/3 is —
a) 5/6  b) 1  c) 6/5
**Answer:** b) 1 — (1/2 + 1/3)/(1 − 1/6) = (5/6)/(5/6).

**7. (Sub lesson 3)** sin 2x when sin x = 3/5 and cos x = −4/5 is —
a) 24/25  b) −24/25  c) −7/25
**Answer:** b) −24/25 — 2 × (3/5) × (−4/5), Ethiopia's worked check.

**8. (Sub lesson 3)** cot(−225°) = —
a) 1  b) −1  c) 0
**Answer:** b) −1 — coterminal with 135°, second quadrant, where cotangent is negative; taught corrected (Pakistan prints 1 at p. 184).

**9. (Sub lesson 3)** The greatest value of cos α − √3 sin α is —
a) 1  b) √3  c) 2
**Answer:** c) 2 — the expression rewrites as 2cos(α + π/6), a single term of amplitude 2 (Kazakhstan, Shynybekov Algebra 9, §4.5).

**10. (Sub lesson 2)** cos 15° = —
a) (√6 − √2)/4  b) (√6 + √2)/4  c) √3/2
**Answer:** b) (√6 + √2)/4 — derived from the double-angle block in Iran's Grade 12 Experimental book (Mathematics (3), printed p. 43).

**11. (Sub lesson 1)** 1 + tan²θ = —
a) sec²θ  b) csc²θ  c) cos²θ
**Answer:** a) sec²θ — one of the basic identities Egypt's Grade 10 book simplifies with (Term 2, Unit 5, lesson 5-1).

**12. (Sub lesson 3)** tan(π/8) = —
a) √2 + 1  b) √2 − 1  c) 1
**Answer:** b) √2 − 1 — the half-angle value worked in Kazakhstan's Abylkasymova Algebra 9 (Part 2, §26).
