**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Match each function to its reciprocal: (a) sin θ (b) cos θ (c) tan θ — sec θ, cot θ, csc θ
**Answer:** a) csc θ, b) sec θ, c) cot θ — each pair multiplies to 1.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Match each formula to its name: (a) cos(α − β) proved first (b) β = α case (c) β replaced by −β — double-angle, fundamental law, cos(α + β)
**Answer:** a) fundamental law, b) double-angle, c) cos(α + β) — the chain's order in Pakistan and Laos.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Match each angle to its exact value: (a) sin 75° (b) cos 105° (c) sin 18° — (√2 − √6)/4, (√5 − 1)/4, (√6 + √2)/4
**Answer:** a) (√6 + √2)/4, b) (√2 − √6)/4, c) (√5 − 1)/4 — all three come off the chain.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Match each expression to its single-term or product form: (a) sin A + sin B (b) 3cos θ + 4sin θ (c) 2 sin A cos B — sin(A + B) + sin(A − B), 2 sin((A + B)/2)cos((A − B)/2), R cos(θ − φ) with R = 5
**Answer:** a) 2 sin((A + B)/2)cos((A − B)/2), b) R cos(θ − φ) with R = 5, c) sin(A + B) + sin(A − B) — sum-to-product, Libya's single term, product-to-sum.
