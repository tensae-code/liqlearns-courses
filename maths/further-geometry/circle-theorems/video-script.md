# Video script — Circle theorems

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Draw a circle. Mark the middle. Mark two points on the edge.

The angle at the middle — big!
The angle at the edge, on the same arc — half as big!
Centre is double. Edge is half. Always!

Now make the two points the ends of a line through the middle —
a diameter. The angle at the edge is half of 180.
Ninety degrees! Every time, any point on the circle!

Four corners on one circle? Opposite corners add to 180.
Seventy and one-ten. Eighty and one hundred. Always 180!

Chords crossing inside? Multiply the pieces!
Three times eight equals four times six!
The circle keeps the products equal!

Now you. Draw your circle. Find your double angle.
Find your right angle in a semicircle. The circle is full of
rules — and you found the first one yourself!
