# Circle theorems — ordering

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — The Inscribed Angle · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Chords and Tangents · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Cyclic Quadrilaterals and Segment Products.

Put each set in order, smallest to largest.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A central angle grows: 40°, 80°, 120°, 160°. Order the matching inscribed angles from smallest to largest.
**Answer:** 20°, 40°, 60°, 80° — each inscribed angle is half its central angle.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Order these from smallest to largest: the angle in a semicircle, the inscribed angle on a 60° central angle, the inscribed angle on a 200° central angle.
**Answer:** Inscribed on 60° (30°), angle in a semicircle (90°), inscribed on 200° (100°).

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Order these chords of one circle from shortest to longest: a chord 2 cm from the centre, a chord 5 cm from the centre, a diameter.
**Answer:** The chord 5 cm from the centre (shortest), the chord 2 cm from the centre, the diameter (longest).

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** A cyclic quadrilateral has angles 60°, 120°, 80°, 100°. Order the angles from smallest to largest and pair the opposites.
**Answer:** 60°, 80°, 100°, 120°. Opposite pairs: 60° with 120° (sum 180°), 80° with 100° (sum 180°).

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Two chords cross, cut into parts 2 and 10, and 4 and 5. Order the four parts from smallest to largest.
**Answer:** 2, 4, 5, 10 — and the products check: 2 × 10 = 4 × 5 = 20.
