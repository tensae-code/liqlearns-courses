# Circle theorems — true or false

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — The Inscribed Angle · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Chords and Tangents · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Cyclic Quadrilaterals and Segment Products.

Say true or false, and why in one line.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The inscribed angle on an arc is half the central angle on the same arc. — **True.** That is the inscribed-angle theorem.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** An angle in a semicircle is always 90°. — **True.** Half of the diameter's 180° central angle.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Moving the point on the circle changes the inscribed angle on a fixed arc. — **False.** Angles in the same segment are equal.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A chord through the centre is a diameter. — **True.** The diameter is the longest chord, through the centre.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** A tangent can cross a circle at two points. — **False.** A tangent touches at exactly one point; a line crossing at two points is a secant.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** The angle between a tangent and a chord equals the angle in the alternate segment. — **True.** That is the tangent–chord angle theorem.

**7. (Sub lesson 3)** In a cyclic quadrilateral, adjacent angles add to 180°. — **False.** Opposite angles add to 180°; adjacent angles need not.

**8. (Sub lesson 3)** If two chords cross inside a circle, the products of their parts are equal. — **True.** AE × BE = CE × DE.
