# Circle theorems — matching

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. Join the two sides that go together.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Match each angle to its size when the central angle is 80°.
- Central angle — a) 40°
- Inscribed angle on the same arc — b) 80°
- Angle in a semicircle — c) 90°
**Answer:** Central–b, Inscribed–a, Semicircle–c.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Match each line to its description.
- Chord — a) touches at one point
- Tangent — b) joins two points on the circle
- Secant — c) cuts the circle at two points
- Diameter — d) a chord through the centre
**Answer:** Chord–b, Tangent–a, Secant–c, Diameter–d.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Match each rule to its formula.
- Intersecting chords — a) KP² = BP × AP
- Two secants from outside — b) AE × BE = CE × DE
- Tangent and secant — c) AP × BP = CP × DP
- Cyclic quadrilateral — d) opposite angles add to 180°
**Answer:** Intersecting chords–b, Two secants–c, Tangent and secant–a, Cyclic quadrilateral–d.
