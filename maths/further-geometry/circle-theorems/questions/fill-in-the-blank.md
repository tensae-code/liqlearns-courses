Put the missing word or number in the space.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The angle at the centre is ______ the angle at the circumference on the same arc.
**Answer:** twice (double).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Angles in the same ______ are equal.
**Answer:** segment.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The angle in a ______ is a right angle.
**Answer:** semicircle.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A radius through the midpoint of a chord is ______ to the chord.
**Answer:** perpendicular (at right angles).

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The angle between a tangent and a chord equals the angle in the ______ segment.
**Answer:** alternate.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** A tangent meets the radius at the point of contact at ______°.
**Answer:** 90.

**7. (Sub lesson 3)** In a cyclic quadrilateral, opposite angles add to ______°.
**Answer:** 180.

**8. (Sub lesson 3)** Intersecting chords: AE × BE = CE × ______.
**Answer:** DE.
