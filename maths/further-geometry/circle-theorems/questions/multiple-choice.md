# Circle theorems — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Level 1)** A central angle is 100°. The inscribed angle on the same arc is —
a) 100°  b) 50°  c) 200°
**Answer: b) 50°** — the inscribed angle is half the central angle.

**2. (Level 1)** The angle in a semicircle is —
a) 45°  b) 90°  c) 180°
**Answer: b) 90°.**

**3. (Level 1)** Two inscribed angles stand on the same arc. They are —
a) equal  b) supplementary  c) double each other
**Answer: a) equal** — angles in the same segment are equal.

**4. (Level 2)** A radius through the midpoint of a chord meets the chord at —
a) 45°  b) 90°  c) 180°
**Answer: b) 90°.**

**5. (Level 2)** The angle between a tangent and a chord through the point of contact equals —
a) the angle in the alternate segment  b) 90° always  c) half the radius
**Answer: a) the angle in the alternate segment.**

**6. (Level 2)** A tangent touches a circle at one point. The radius to that point meets the tangent at —
a) 45°  b) 90°  c) 180°
**Answer: b) 90°.**

**7. (Level 3)** A cyclic quadrilateral has one angle 75°. The opposite angle is —
a) 75°  b) 105°  c) 180°
**Answer: b) 105°** — opposite angles add to 180°.

**8. (Level 3)** Two chords cross inside a circle, cut into 2 and 12, and 4 and x. x is —
a) 6  b) 8  c) 3
**Answer: a) 6** — 2 × 12 = 4 × 6.
