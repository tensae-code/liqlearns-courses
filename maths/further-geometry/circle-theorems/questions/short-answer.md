# Circle theorems — short answer

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — The Inscribed Angle · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Chords and Tangents · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Cyclic Quadrilaterals and Segment Products.

Answer in a word or a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** State the inscribed-angle theorem in one sentence.
**Answer:** The angle at the centre is twice the angle at the circumference on the same arc (the inscribed angle is half the central angle).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A central angle is 140°. What is the inscribed angle on the same arc?
**Answer:** 70°.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** AB is a diameter. C is on the circle. What is angle ACB?
**Answer:** 90°.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** What is a chord?
**Answer:** A straight line joining two points on the circle.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** What is a tangent?
**Answer:** A straight line that touches the circle at exactly one point.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** A tangent of length 6 touches a circle at K. A secant from the same outside point goes through the circle, with outside part 4 and whole length 9. Check the tangent–secant rule.
**Answer:** Tangent² = 36; whole secant × outside part = 9 × 4 = 36 — they match.

**7. (Sub lesson 3)** A quadrilateral has opposite angles 80° and 100°. Can a circle pass through all four corners?
**Answer:** Yes — opposite angles add to 180°, so it is cyclic (the converse).

**8. (Sub lesson 3)** Two secants from an outside point have whole lengths 10 and 8, with outside parts 4 and 5. Check the rule.
**Answer:** 10 × 4 = 40 and 8 × 5 = 40 — the products are equal.
