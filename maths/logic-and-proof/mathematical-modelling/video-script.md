# Video script — Mathematical Modelling

Super-simple voice. The tutor talks; the six stations light up one by one.

---

Some questions do not come with sums. Should Pak Mat drive to town for cheaper rice? No numbers are given. You must build them.

That building is modelling. It has six steps.

One: say the problem plainly. Two: make assumptions — and say them out loud. Three: name the things that can change. Four: do the mathematics. Five: check the answer against real life. Six: report what you found.

His car trip costs petrol and toll. The bus costs a fare. The rice saves money per bag. Put costs on both sides. The models say: if the town is closer than 16.6 kilometres, the trip pays.

And if the answer looks wrong? Go round again. Add what you left out. A model is a ring, not a road.
