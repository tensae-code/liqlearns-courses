# Hands-on — Mathematical Modelling

## Activity 1 — Two ways to school (20 minutes)

Model the question "is the bus pass worth it?" Variables: fare per ride, rides per week, pass price, weeks. Assumptions stated on paper first (no free rides, no holidays — then add one). Build both cost models, find the break-even number of rides, and report in one sentence.

## Activity 2 — Assumptions audit (10 minutes)

Take the finished Activity 1 model. List everything it pretends is simple: constant fares, no sick days, no trips with friends. For each, say whether including it would push the break-even up, down, or neither — then add the one that matters most and rebuild.

## Activity 3 — Scale it (10 minutes)

Measure a table (or a room) and build its scale model plan at 1 : 20 on squared paper. Check three lengths on the drawing against the real ones divided by 20. State the model's limitation in one sentence (it carries lengths, not weights or strength).
