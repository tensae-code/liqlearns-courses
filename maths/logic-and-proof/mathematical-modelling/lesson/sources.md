## Andorra added 2026-11-11 (expansion merge)

The 2026-11-10 expansion read read Andorra's whole in-hand Maths set from the PDFs — **4 documents, all of them official Escola Andorrana curriculum programmes published by decree in the Butlletí Oficial del Principat d'Andorra (BOPA); 0 pupil (student) books** — and placed 679 curriculum-requirement items. A programme states what is REQUIRED to be taught; it teaches nothing itself. Every Andorra item in this section is therefore TEACHER EVIDENCE — a curriculum requirement, worded as a requirement — cited as the evidence kind it is; Andorra is counted in NO lesson's taught-in figure, on the standing Ghana evidence-only precedent, until Andorran pupil books are in hand and read. Set correction, as the placements prove it: the expansion map recorded 3 books in hand; the true set is 4 — the grades 1–6 programme (Decret 376/2021, Annex VII) is held in the GitHub repo but sits in no staging books.json, which is why the map missed it. All four programmes are Andorra's OWN (Govern d'Andorra, in Catalan); the Spanish and French systems also taught in Andorra (Spain's Anaya and Innovamat manuals; France's Le Livre Scolaire) are list-only names on official school book lists — no file is held, so they are cited nowhere in this course and credited to no country — and the Escola Andorrana itself has no commercial pupil-textbook series, so programmes are all that exist to hold. The placements' counting grain is one item per distinct stated requirement — each specific competency, and each distinct entry in the programmes' learning-resource tables, tagged by cycle where the table splits by cycle — and a procedure entry that merely restates a concept entry is folded into it, not double-counted. The placements' named exclusions — the decree preambles, the introductions and competency rationale, the didactic orientations, the assessment criteria and end-of-cycle expectations, the synthesis tables (verified verbatim repeats of the competency sections), the attitudes/values lists, Book 1's glossary and opening summary table, and the Council of Europe cross-references — are stocked nowhere.
