# Lesson plan — Mathematical Modelling

**One thing:** turning a messy real-world question into mathematics and back again — the six components of a model (define the problem, make assumptions, name the variables, apply mathematics, verify and interpret, report), and how a model is improved by adding what it first left out.

**Stage:** Logic and Proof (Stage 13). **Level 141** (Malaysia merge, 2026-11-07).

**Why this lesson exists:** Malaysia's Form 5 book gives mathematical modelling its own closing chapter — the only full treatment in the read set — including the discipline of telling a modelling question from a closed problem-solving question, and a complete worked case. Problem-solving-strategies teaches attack plans for problems that arrive already mathematical; modelling is the step before, where the mathematics itself must be chosen. Under the everything-goes-in ruling (2026-11-06), it gets its own lesson.

## The climb

1. **Modelling against problem solving** — a closed problem hands you the mathematics; a modelling question hands you a situation and asks "is it worth it?" The first strategic act is telling them apart.
2. **The six components** — (1) identify and define the problem, (2) make assumptions, (3) identify the variables, (4) apply mathematics, (5) verify and interpret the solution, (6) report the findings. Then refine and repeat.
3. **The worked case** — drive to town for cheaper rice, or buy locally? Two cost models (car: petrol and toll; bus fare for comparison), a break-even distance (under 16.6 km the trip pays) and a break-even price (under RM25.24 for the 10 kg bag it pays) read off the models.
4. **Models beyond money** — scale models, where a ratio carries the model; enhancing a model by adding variables, and naming a model's limitations honestly.

- slot-modelling-1 (IMAGE): a picture of a shopper holding two receipts — the local shop's rice price and the town trip's petrol-and-toll cost — over a map with a distance scale. What the student does: names the variables in the picture before any formula appears.
- slot-modelling-2 (GIF, 1–2 s): a strip showing a model house shrinking by a fixed ratio next to its scale written as 1 : 50. What the student does: watches, then explains what the ratio promises about every length in the model.
