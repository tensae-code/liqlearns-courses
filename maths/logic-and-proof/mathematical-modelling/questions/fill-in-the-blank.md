# Fill in the blank — Mathematical Modelling

1. (Layer 1) A question whose mathematics you must choose yourself is a ___ question; one with the mathematics given is a problem-solving question.
   **Answer: modelling.** Malaysia, Mathematics Form 5, Ch. 8 §8.1, printed pp. 228–229.

2. (Layer 2) In the rice case, the trip pays when the distance is less than ___ km.
   **Answer: 16.6.** Malaysia, Mathematics Form 5, Ch. 8 §8.1, printed pp. 230–233.

3. (Layer 3) After reporting the findings, the cycle continues by ___ the model.
   **Answer: refining (improving/enhancing).** Malaysia, Mathematics Form 5, Ch. 8 §8.1, printed pp. 237–239.
