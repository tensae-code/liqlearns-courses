# Matching — Mathematical Modelling

1. (Layer 2) Match each modelling act to its component:
   (a) "We ignore the toll road's traffic." (b) "Let d be the distance in km." (c) "The answer means: drive only if the shop is under 17 km away." (d) "Cost = 2 × 0.25d + 3.60."
   Components: make assumptions, identify the variables, apply mathematics, verify and interpret.
   **Answer: (a) make assumptions, (b) identify the variables, (c) verify and interpret, (d) apply mathematics.** Malaysia, Mathematics Form 5, Ch. 8 §8.1, printed pp. 229–233.
