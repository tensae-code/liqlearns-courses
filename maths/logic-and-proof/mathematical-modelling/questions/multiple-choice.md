1. (Sub lesson 1) Which of these is a modelling question?
   A. Solve 3x + 2 = 14 B. Factorise x² − 9 C. Is it worth buying a bus pass? D. Find the area of a 5 by 8 rectangle
   **Answer: C** — Malaysia, Mathematics Form 5, Ch. 8 §8.1, printed pp. 228–229.

2. (Sub lesson 2) The FIRST component of mathematical modelling is:
   A. Apply mathematics B. Identify and define the problem C. Report the findings D. Make assumptions
   **Answer: B** — Malaysia, Mathematics Form 5, Ch. 8 §8.1, printed pp. 229–230.

3. (Sub lesson 3) In the rice case, a break-even answer is best described as:
   A. A yes B. A no C. A boundary value in the situation's units D. A graph
   **Answer: C** — Malaysia, Mathematics Form 5, Ch. 8 §8.1, printed pp. 230–233.

4. (Sub lesson 4) A model is improved by:
   A. Hiding its assumptions B. Adding a variable it left out and checking again C. Making it longer D. Removing the verification step
   **Answer: B** — Malaysia, Mathematics Form 5, Ch. 8 §8.1, printed pp. 237–239.
