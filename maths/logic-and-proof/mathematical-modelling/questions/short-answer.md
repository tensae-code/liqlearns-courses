1. (Sub lesson 2) List the six components of mathematical modelling in order.
   **Answer: identify and define the problem; make assumptions; identify the variables; apply mathematics; verify and interpret the solution; report the findings.** Malaysia, Mathematics Form 5, Ch. 8 §8.1, printed pp. 229–230.

2. (Sub lesson 2) For the question "should I cycle or take the bus to college?", name two variables and one assumption.
   **Answer: variables e.g. distance, bus fare, cycling time (any two); assumption e.g. the fare stays constant / the weather is ignored (any one, stated).** Malaysia, Mathematics Form 5, Ch. 8 §8.1, printed pp. 229–230.

3. (Sub lesson 4) A scale model is built at 1 : 250. The real bridge is 75 m long. How long is the model?
   **Answer: 75 ÷ 250 = 0.3 m = 30 cm.** Malaysia, Mathematics Form 5, Ch. 8 §8.1, printed pp. 234–236.
