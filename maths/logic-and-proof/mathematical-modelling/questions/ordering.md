# Ordering — Mathematical Modelling

1. (Layer 1) Put the six components in order:
   (a) Apply mathematics (b) Report the findings (c) Identify and define the problem (d) Verify and interpret the solution (e) Make assumptions (f) Identify the variables
   **Answer: c, e, f, a, d, b.** Malaysia, Mathematics Form 5, Ch. 8 §8.1, printed pp. 229–230.
