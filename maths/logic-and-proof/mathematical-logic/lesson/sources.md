**Acceptance count: taught in 24 countries (Laos, Armenia, Iraq, Greece, Cambodia, Yemen, Sierra Leone, Iran, Vietnam, Kazakhstan, Malaysia, Rwanda, Sudan, India, Afghanistan, Uzbekistan, Ecuador, Bolivia, China, Sri Lanka, Moldova, Cabo Verde, Finland, Dominica).** Laos carries the fullest single treatment: propositions, all six connectives with circuit models, 2ⁿ-row truth tables, equivalence with De Morgan and the contrapositive, tautology and contradiction, and quantifiers with their negations, all in Grade 10 Chapter I (pp.1–29). Armenia carries logic twice over — briefly in both Grade 11 streams and as a whole Grade 12 book (Aghekyan) that adds definition/axiom/theorem and converse/opposite statements. Iraq teaches logic as Chapter 1 of Grade 10 Scientific, with quantified propositions as its closing section. Greece opens Grade 10 Algebra with the vocabulary of logic. Cambodia teaches propositions, the logical operations with truth tables, and De Morgan's laws inside its Logic, Sets and Numbers chapter. Yemen opens Grade 10 with a logic unit running from the single proposition through equivalence and inference to quantifiers. Sierra Leone teaches logical reasoning as an eight-lesson block of SSS 2 Term 1, from simple statements to the validity of arguments. Iran teaches formal logic with truth tables in two separate Grade 11 branch books. Vietnam opens Grade 10 with propositions as Bài 1 of its first chapter. Kazakhstan plants statements (true/false) and logic problems already in Grade 1.

Malaysia gives logic a full Form 4 chapter of 23 worked examples — the heaviest school treatment in this course: statements, negation, compound statements, implications with their converse, inverse and contrapositive, and then arguments, deductive and inductive.

Rwanda's held books stock this lesson at one point only: Advanced Senior Six tests the validity of a syllogism with diagrams in a summative evaluation question.

Sudan's Grade 10 logic unit is the operating system of its whole secondary course: propositions and open sentences, negation, truth tables, conjunction, disjunction, the conditional and biconditional, implication and equivalence, the contrapositive, the laws of logic through De Morgan, and tautology and contradiction — the same tables then prove the set laws in the next unit.

India enters mathematical logic from Mathematics Class 11: statements.
