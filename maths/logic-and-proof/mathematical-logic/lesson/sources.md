Yemen opens Grade 10 with a logic unit running from the single proposition through equivalence and inference to quantifiers. Sierra Leone teaches logical reasoning as an eight-lesson block of SSS 2 Term 1, from simple statements to the validity of arguments. Iran teaches formal logic with truth tables in two separate Grade 11 branch books. Vietnam opens Grade 10 with propositions as Bài 1 of its first chapter. Kazakhstan plants statements (true/false) and logic problems already in Grade 1.

Malaysia gives logic a full Form 4 chapter of 23 worked examples — the heaviest school treatment in this course: statements, negation, compound statements, implications with their converse, inverse and contrapositive, and then arguments, deductive and inductive.

Rwanda's held books stock this lesson at one point only: Advanced Senior Six tests the validity of a syllogism with diagrams in a summative evaluation question.
