# Video script — Mathematical Logic

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Some sentences can be TRUE or FALSE. "7 = 4 + 5." False!
"Taiz is a city in Yemen." True! A sentence like that is a **proposition**.

And some sentences are NOT propositions. "Close the door!" —
not true, not false. It is a command. It does not count.

Now join two. "It rains AND the ground is wet" — both must be true.
"It rains OR the ground is wet" — one is enough.
"NOT" flips it: true becomes false, false becomes true.

"If it rains, THEN the ground is wet." When is that a lie?
Only one way: it rains, and the ground is dry. Only that one way!

Make a table. Every case in its own row. Two statements — four rows.
Three statements — eight rows. The table never lies.

And "for ALL" and "there EXISTS". Not all — means: there exists one that is not!

Now you. Judge a sentence. Join two. Negate one. That is logic.
