# Mathematical Logic — fill in the blank

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Propositions and Connectives · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Truth Tables, Equivalence and De Morgan · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Quantifiers, Open Sentences and Inference.

Fill each blank with the missing word or value.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A sentence that is either true or false is called a ______.
**Answer:** proposition — it must carry a truth value (Laos, Grade 10, Ch.I L1, p.1).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The symbol ∧ stands for the connective ______.
**Answer:** and — the logical product (Armenia, Grade 11, Ch.3 §1, p.51).

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The negation of a true proposition is ______.
**Answer:** false — negation flips the truth value (Yemen, Grade 10 Part 1, Unit 1 §1-1, p.7).

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A compound proposition that is true in every row of its table is called a ______.
**Answer:** tautology (Laos, Grade 10, Ch.I L2, pp.14–19).

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** (p⇒q) is equivalent to (~q⇒~p), which is called the ______ of p⇒q.
**Answer:** contrapositive (Laos, Grade 10, Ch.I L2, p.14).

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** The symbol ∃ is read "there ______".
**Answer:** exists — the existential quantifier (Iraq, Grade 10 Scientific, Ch.1 §1-7).
