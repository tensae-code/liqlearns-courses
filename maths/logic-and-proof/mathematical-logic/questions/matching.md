# Mathematical Logic — matching

Match each item on the left with the one item on the right that belongs with it.

**1. (Level 1)** Match the symbol to its reading: (i) ∧ (ii) ∨ (iii) ⇒ (iv) ⇔ (v) ~
Options: (A) if and only if (B) not (C) and (D) or (E) if…then
**Answer:** (i)–C, (ii)–D, (iii)–E, (iv)–A, (v)–B — the connective table (Laos, Grade 10, Ch.I L1, pp.1–13).

**2. (Level 1)** Match the sentence to its kind: (i) "9 is a square number." (ii) "Draw a square." (iii) "Is 9 a square number?" (iv) "What a big square!"
Options: (A) question (B) exclamation (C) proposition (D) command
**Answer:** (i)–C, (ii)–D, (iii)–A, (iv)–B — only the first can be true or false (Laos, Grade 10, Ch.I L1, p.1).

**3. (Level 2)** Match the law to its pair: (i) ~(p∨q) (ii) ~(p∧q) (iii) (p⇒q) (iv) p⇔q
Options: (A) (p⇒q)∧(q⇒p) (B) ~p∧~q (C) ~p∨q (D) ~p∨~q
**Answer:** (i)–B, (ii)–D, (iii)–C, (iv)–A — the equivalence laws proved by table (Laos, Grade 10, Ch.I L2, p.14).

**4. (Level 2)** Match the circuit to its connective: (i) two switches in series, one lamp (ii) two switches in parallel, one lamp
Options: (A) or, ∨ (B) and, ∧
**Answer:** (i)–B, (ii)–A — series needs both switches closed; parallel needs one (Laos, Grade 10, Ch.I L1).

**5. (Level 3)** Match the statement to its negation: (i) ∀x, P(x) (ii) ∃x, P(x)
Options: (A) ∃x, ~P(x) (B) ∀x, ~P(x)
**Answer:** (i)–A, (ii)–B — swap the quantifier and negate the inside (Iran, Statistics and Probability Grade 11, Ch.1, p.13).
