**Acceptance count: taught in 4 countries (Iran, China, North Macedonia, Kazakhstan).** Iran teaches the principle and its generalised kn + 1 form in the counting chapter of its Grade 12 Discrete Mathematics book, with pigeonhole proofs closing the chapter's exercises. China teaches it to 11-year-olds as the drawer principle in Grade 6, built first by drawing every case and then by the division-with-remainder method. North Macedonia teaches it in Grade 8 under Dirichlet's name as a genuine proof method, with the 30-pupil error-count proof as its sight-read example. Kazakhstan carries it in Grade 8 Algebra only as starred, optional additional material (§6.5*), in "rabbits into hutches" form — an optional home, recorded as filed.
