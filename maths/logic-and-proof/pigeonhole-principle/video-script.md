# Video script — The pigeonhole principle

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Four boxes. Five pencils.

Put one pencil in each box. One, two, three, four.
One pencil is still in your hand!
It must go in a box. That box now has **two**.

You cannot stop it. More things than boxes —
some box gets two. Always.

Now seven balls, three drawers.
Two in each drawer: two, two, two.
One ball left! It goes somewhere.
That drawer has **three**.

Thirty children. Nine piles.
Three rounds of nine — and three children left.
Some pile has **four**.

That is the pigeonhole principle.
You do not know which box. You know it must happen.
