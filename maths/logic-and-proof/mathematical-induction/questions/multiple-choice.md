# Mathematical Induction — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — The Principle and the Three Steps · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Series and Divisibility Proofs · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Extended Induction, Inequalities and the Counter-Example Warning.

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The three steps of an induction proof, in order, are:
a) Step, base, conclusion  b) Base case, hypothesis, induction step  c) Check, check, check
**Answer:** b) Base case, hypothesis, induction step — Laos's three steps (Grade 11, Ch.2 L7, p.28).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In the step, what may you assume?
a) The statement for every n  b) The statement for n = k + 1  c) The statement for n = k
**Answer:** c) The statement for n = k — the hypothesis; k + 1 is what must be earned (Laos, Grade 11, Ch.2 L7, p.28).

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The base case of "1 + 3 + … + (2n − 1) = n²" at n = 1 says:
a) 1 = 1²  b) 1 + 3 = 2²  c) 3 = 3²
**Answer:** a) 1 = 1² (Cambodia, Grade 11 Advanced, Ch.1 L3, pp.24–34).

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In the proof that 1 + 2 + … + n = n(n + 1)/2, the step adds which term to the k-sum?
a) k  b) k + 1  c) 2k + 1
**Answer:** b) k + 1 — the next term of the series (Kazakhstan, Yelemessov Algebra 9, §3.1, p.80).

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The base case of "6²ⁿ − 1 is divisible by 35" is:
a) 6² − 1 = 35  b) 6¹ − 1 = 5  c) 6⁰ − 1 = 0
**Answer:** a) 6² − 1 = 35 — and 35 is divisible by 35 (Kazakhstan, Abylkasymova Algebra 9, §18, p.153).

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** Euler's n² − n + 41 first fails to be prime at:
a) n = 40  b) n = 41  c) n = 42
**Answer:** b) n = 41 — there it equals 41² (Pakistan, Class 11, Unit 8, pp.140–150).

**7. (Sub lesson 3)** Extended induction (Pakistan) differs from the plain principle in that:
a) It needs no base case  b) Its hypothesis/step can rest on a later start or several earlier cases  c) It checks cases up to 100
**Answer:** b) Its hypothesis/step can rest on a later start or several earlier cases (Pakistan, Class 11, Unit 8, pp.140–150).

**8. (Sub lesson 2)** Vietnam's study book proves which theorem by induction in its next lesson?
a) Pythagoras' theorem  b) Newton's binomial theorem  c) The angle-sum theorem
**Answer:** b) Newton's binomial theorem (Vietnam, Grade 10 study book, Bài 4, p.32).
