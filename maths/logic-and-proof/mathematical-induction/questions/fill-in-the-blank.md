# Mathematical Induction — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the missing word or value.

**1. (Level 1)** The first step of an induction proof, checking the statement at n = 1, is called the ______ case.
**Answer:** base (Laos, Grade 11, Ch.2 L7, p.28).

**2. (Level 1)** The domino picture: the first domino falling stands for the base case, and each domino knocking the next stands for the ______ step.
**Answer:** induction (Kazakhstan, Yelemessov Algebra 9, §3.1, p.80).

**3. (Level 2)** 1 + 2 + … + 10 = 10 × 11 / 2 = ______.
**Answer:** 55 (Kazakhstan, Yelemessov Algebra 9, §3.1, p.80).

**4. (Level 2)** In the n² proof, the step uses k² + (2k + 1) = (k + ______)².
**Answer:** 1 — (k + 1)² (Cambodia, Grade 11 Advanced, pp.24–34).

**5. (Level 3)** Euler's polynomial n² − n + 41 is prime for n = 1 to ______ and fails at the next value.
**Answer:** 40 (Pakistan, Class 11, Unit 8, pp.140–150).

**6. (Level 3)** The induction section of Shynybekov's Algebra 9 is marked with a star, meaning it is ______ as filed.
**Answer:** optional (Kazakhstan, Shynybekov Algebra 9, §3.2*, p.72).
