# Mathematical Induction — matching

Match each item on the left with the one item on the right that belongs with it.

**1. (Level 1)** Match the part of the proof to its content for P(n): "Σ(2k − 1) = n²": (i) base case (ii) hypothesis (iii) step
Options: (A) 1 + 3 + … + (2k − 1) = k² is supposed (B) 1 = 1² is checked (C) k² + (2k + 1) = (k + 1)² is shown
**Answer:** (i)–B, (ii)–A, (iii)–C (Cambodia, Grade 11 Advanced, pp.24–34).

**2. (Level 2)** Match the claim to its kind of induction work: (i) 1 + 2 + … + n = n(n + 1)/2 (ii) 6²ⁿ − 1 divisible by 35 (iii) 2ⁿ > n² for n ≥ 5
Options: (A) divisibility proof (B) series proof (C) inequality proof with a later base case
**Answer:** (i)–B, (ii)–A, (iii)–C (Kazakhstan, Abylkasymova Algebra 9, §18, p.153; Soltan Algebra 9, §14, p.100).

**3. (Level 2)** Match the book to its induction item: (i) Vietnam, Grade 10 study book (ii) Laos, Grade 11 (iii) Iraq, Grade 12 Scientific
Options: (A) the three steps stated for statements A(n) (B) De Moivre by induction, filed "for information only" (C) Newton's binomial proved by induction
**Answer:** (i)–C, (ii)–A, (iii)–B (Vietnam Bài 4, p.32; Laos Ch.2 L7, p.28; Iraq pp.42–43).

**4. (Level 3)** Match the polynomial to its book: (i) n² − n + 41 (ii) n² + n + 41
Options: (A) Kazakhstan, Shynybekov — a false "proof" dissected (B) Pakistan, Class 11 — Euler's warning exhibit
**Answer:** (i)–B, (ii)–A — both fail at n = 41, and the books file them separately (Pakistan, Unit 8; Shynybekov §3.2*, p.72).

**5. (Level 1)** Match the country to where induction sits in its book: (i) Greece (ii) Armenia, natural-mathematics stream (iii) Cambodia
Options: (A) the number-theory chapter of the Grade 11 orientation book (B) Chapter 4, with a section of further applications (C) the opening chapter of Grade 11 Advanced, with sequences
**Answer:** (i)–A, (ii)–B, (iii)–C (Greece W7 Ch.4; Armenia Ch.4 §§2–3, pp.87, 94; Cambodia Ch.1, pp.1–34).
