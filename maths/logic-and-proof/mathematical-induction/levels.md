# Mathematical Induction — questions by level

Practice by level, tied to the skill number. The questions live in
the type files; this file is the by-level view. The levels are the
depths inside this one thing — induction — the principle and its three steps first, then series and divisibility proofs, then extended induction, inequalities and broken proofs.

## Level 1 — the principle and the three steps
Level 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: state the principle, name the three steps in order, and check a base case correctly.

## Level 2 — series and divisibility proofs
Level 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: carry a series formula and a divisibility claim through a complete induction proof, hypothesis stated and used.

## Level 3 — extended induction, inequalities and the counter-example warning
Level 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: explain the Euler counter-example, use or recognise extended induction, and find the fault in a broken induction.
