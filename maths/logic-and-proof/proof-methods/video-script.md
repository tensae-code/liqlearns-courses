The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the

---

Drawing is not proving. Draw ten triangles — pretty, neat —
and the NEXT triangle can still break your rule.
One drawing that breaks it kills it. That is a **counterexample**.

A **proof** is different. It is a staircase.
The bottom steps: definitions and things we accept — **axioms**.
Every step above rests on a step below. No jumps. No wishes.

Two lines cross. The opposite angles — are they equal?
Angle one plus angle two is 180 — a straight line.
Angle three plus angle two is 180 — a straight line.
So angle one equals angle three. Proved. For EVERY crossing, forever.

And sometimes you prove it backwards: suppose it is NOT true…
follow the road… and you hit a wall. Impossible!
So it IS true. That is proof by **contradiction**.

Now you. Take a claim. Build the staircase. Or find the one case that breaks it.
That is proof.
