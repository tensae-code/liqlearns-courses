# Proof Methods — questions by level

Practice by level, tied to the skill number. The questions live in
the type files; this file is the by-level view. The levels are the
depths inside this one thing — proof — the deductive method and the shape of a proof first, then direct, two-column, contradiction and contrapositive proofs, then proving and refuting quantified statements.

## Level 1 — the deductive method and the shape of a proof
Level 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: say what deduction starts from, separate givens from what is required, and order the steps of a proof correctly.

## Level 2 — direct proof, two columns, contradiction, contrapositive
Level 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: complete a two-column proof, run a proof by contradiction to its conclusion, and replace a statement by its contrapositive.

## Level 3 — proving and refuting ∀ and ∃; necessary and sufficient
Level 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: prove a ∀ statement in general, refute one by counterexample, prove an ∃ statement by a witness, and classify conditions as necessary, sufficient, both or neither.
