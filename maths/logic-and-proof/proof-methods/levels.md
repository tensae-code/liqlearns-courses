# Proof Methods — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB
LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure — README, "Units, Lessons and Sub
Lessons"). Practice is tied to the skill number, as before. The
questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson
view. The sub lessons are the depths inside this one thing — proof — the deductive method and the shape of a proof first, then direct, two-column, contradiction and contrapositive proofs, then proving and refuting quantified statements.

Mapping from the old depth layers: old Level 1 = Sub lesson 1; old Level 2 = Sub lesson 2; old Level 3 = Sub lesson 3 — each old layer is one sub lesson, in the same order.

## Sub lesson 1 — The Deductive Method and the Shape of a Proof
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: say what deduction starts from, separate givens from what is required, and order the steps of a proof correctly.

## Sub lesson 2 — Direct Proof, Two Columns, Contradiction and Contrapositive
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: complete a two-column proof, run a proof by contradiction to its conclusion, and replace a statement by its contrapositive.

## Sub lesson 3 — Proving and Refuting ∀ and ∃; Necessary and Sufficient
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: prove a ∀ statement in general, refute one by counterexample, prove an ∃ statement by a witness, and classify conditions as necessary, sufficient, both or neither.
