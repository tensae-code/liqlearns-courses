# Proof Methods — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the missing word or value.

**1. (Level 1)** A starting statement accepted without proof is called an ______.
**Answer:** axiom (Kazakhstan, Smirnov & Tuyakov Geometry 7, Ch.1 §1, p.5).

**2. (Level 1)** One example that shows a "for all" claim is false is called a ______.
**Answer:** counterexample (Iran, Grade 9, Ch.3, p.33).

**3. (Level 2)** Proof by ______ assumes the statement is false and reasons to an impossibility.
**Answer:** contradiction (Kazakhstan, Turapbekov Geometry 7, §3, p.36).

**4. (Level 2)** Egypt's Grade 7 book names the writing routine "Steps to Write a ______" (p.81).
**Answer:** Proof (Egypt, Grade 7 Term 1, Unit 4-2, p.81).

**5. (Level 3)** To prove an ∃ statement, you produce a ______, one object that works.
**Answer:** witness (Armenia, Grade 12 logic book, §5, p.30).

**6. (Level 3)** If P⇒Q, then P is a ______ condition for Q.
**Answer:** sufficient — P holding is enough to guarantee Q (Armenia, Grade 12 logic book, §7, p.52).
