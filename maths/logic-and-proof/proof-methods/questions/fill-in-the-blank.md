# Proof Methods — fill in the blank

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — The Deductive Method and the Shape of a Proof · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Direct Proof, Two Columns, Contradiction and Contrapositive · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Proving and Refuting ∀ and ∃; Necessary and Sufficient.

Fill each blank with the missing word or value.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A starting statement accepted without proof is called an ______.
**Answer:** axiom (Kazakhstan, Smirnov & Tuyakov Geometry 7, Ch.1 §1, p.5).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** One example that shows a "for all" claim is false is called a ______.
**Answer:** counterexample (Iran, Grade 9, Ch.3, p.33).

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Proof by ______ assumes the statement is false and reasons to an impossibility.
**Answer:** contradiction (Kazakhstan, Turapbekov Geometry 7, §3, p.36).

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Egypt's Grade 7 book names the writing routine "Steps to Write a ______" (p.81).
**Answer:** Proof (Egypt, Grade 7 Term 1, Unit 4-2, p.81).

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** To prove an ∃ statement, you produce a ______, one object that works.
**Answer:** witness (Armenia, Grade 12 logic book, §5, p.30).

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** If P⇒Q, then P is a ______ condition for Q.
**Answer:** sufficient — P holding is enough to guarantee Q (Armenia, Grade 12 logic book, §7, p.52).
