# Proof Methods — matching

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — The Deductive Method and the Shape of a Proof · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Direct Proof, Two Columns, Contradiction and Contrapositive · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Proving and Refuting ∀ and ∃; Necessary and Sufficient.

Match each item on the left with the one item on the right that belongs with it.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Match the part of a proof to its job: (i) givens (ii) required (iii) proof
Options: (A) the chain of justified steps (B) what is known at the start (C) what must be shown
**Answer:** (i)–B, (ii)–C, (iii)–A — Kuwait's frame (Grade 9 Term 2, Unit 7, pp.135–199).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Match the method to its opening move: (i) direct proof (ii) proof by contradiction (iii) disproof of a ∀ claim (iv) proof of an ∃ claim
Options: (A) assume the statement is false (B) produce one counterexample (C) start from the givens and walk to the claim (D) produce one witness
**Answer:** (i)–C, (ii)–A, (iii)–B, (iv)–D (Armenia, Grade 12 logic book, §5, p.30; Kazakhstan, Turapbekov Geometry 7, §3, p.36).

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Match the argument to its verdict: (i) p⇒q and p, so q (ii) p⇒q and ~q, so ~p (iii) p⇒q and q, so p
Options: (A) valid — modus tollens (B) invalid — affirming the consequent (C) valid — modus ponens
**Answer:** (i)–C, (ii)–A, (iii)–B (Laos, Grade 10, Ch.I L3, pp.20–29).

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Match the condition pair: for "n is divisible by 6": (i) "n is divisible by 3" (ii) "n is divisible by 12" (iii) "n is divisible by 6 if and only if n is even and divisible by 3" — the combined condition
Options: (A) sufficient but not necessary (B) necessary and sufficient (C) necessary but not sufficient
**Answer:** (i)–C, (ii)–A, the combined condition–B (Armenia, Grade 12 logic book, §7, p.52).

**5. (Sub lesson 1)** Match the book to its proof opening: (i) Greece, Euclidean Geometry Vol. A (ii) Egypt, Grade 7 (iii) Nicaragua, Grade 8
Options: (A) "Steps to Write a Proof" in the parallelism lesson (B) the first formal proof of the series, in the congruence unit (C) proof as the stated point of the whole book
**Answer:** (i)–C, (ii)–A, (iii)–B (Greece Vol. A Ch.1; Egypt Grade 7 Term 1, p.81; Nicaragua Grade 8, Unit 6, pp.105–124).
