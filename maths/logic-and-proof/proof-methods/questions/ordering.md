# Proof Methods — ordering

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — The Deductive Method and the Shape of a Proof · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Direct Proof, Two Columns, Contradiction and Contrapositive · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Proving and Refuting ∀ and ∃; Necessary and Sufficient.

Put the items in the right order.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Put the steps of writing a proof in order: (A) Write the chain of steps, each with its reason. (B) Write down the givens. (C) State the conclusion. (D) Write down what is required.
**Answer:** B, D, A, C — givens, required, chain, conclusion (Kuwait, Grade 9 Term 2, Unit 7, pp.135–199; Egypt, Grade 7 Term 1, p.81).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Put the steps of a proof by contradiction in order: (A) State that the assumption is false, so the statement is true. (B) Assume the statement is false. (C) Reach an impossibility. (D) Reason correctly from the assumption.
**Answer:** B, D, C, A (Kazakhstan, Turapbekov Geometry 7, §3, p.36).

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Put the rows of the vertical-angles proof in order: (A) So angle 1 = angle 3. (B) Angle 1 + angle 2 = 180°. (C) Angle 3 + angle 2 = 180°. (D) Two lines cross, making angles 1, 2, 3.
**Answer:** D, B, C, A (China, Grade 6 Volume 2, review, pp.100–104).

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Order Armenia Grade 11's logic sections as the book teaches them: (A) proof and refutation — the main methods (B) statements and the logical sum, product and negation (C) deductive reasoning (D) inductive reasoning
**Answer:** B, C, D, A — Ch.3 §§1, 3, 4, 5 (pp.51, 63, 68, 72).
