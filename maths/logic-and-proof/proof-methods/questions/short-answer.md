# Proof Methods — short answer

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Level 1)** Smirnov & Tuyakov compare axioms to what, in their chess analogy?
**Answer:** The rules of the game — everything after must obey them (Kazakhstan, Geometry 7, Ch.1 §1, p.5).

**2. (Level 1)** A pupil measures the angles of 50 triangles and finds 180° every time. Has the angle-sum theorem been proved?
**Answer:** No — examples and measuring are not a proof; deduction is needed (Iran, Grade 9, Ch.3, p.33).

**3. (Level 2)** In the vertical-angles proof, both angle 1 + angle 2 and angle 3 + angle 2 equal what?
**Answer:** 180° — each pair sits on a straight line (China, Grade 6 Volume 2, review, pp.100–104).

**4. (Level 2)** Iran's Discrete book shows Fermat's F₅ is not prime by giving its factors. Name the method.
**Answer:** Disproof by counterexample — F₅ = 641 × 6,700,417 (Iran, Discrete Mathematics, Ch.1 L1, p.2).

**5. (Level 2)** State the contrapositive of "If a figure is a square, it has four equal sides."
**Answer:** If a figure does not have four equal sides, it is not a square (Laos, Grade 10, Ch.I L3, pp.20–29).

**6. (Level 3)** What must a proof of ∀x, P(x) work with — a chosen special x, or an arbitrary x?
**Answer:** An arbitrary x — a special one proves nothing about all (Armenia, Grade 12 logic book, §5, p.30).

**7. (Level 3)** Is "n ends in 0" necessary, sufficient, both or neither for "n is even"?
**Answer:** Sufficient but not necessary — it guarantees evenness, but 12 is even without it (Armenia, Grade 12 logic book, §7, p.52).

**8. (Level 1)** In Iran's flawed printed proof, what made the proof invalid?
**Answer:** A step used an angle equality (Ĉ₁ = Ĉ₂) that nothing had established (Iran, Grade 9, Ch.3, p.37).
