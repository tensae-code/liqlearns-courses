# Video script — Mathematical Communication

Super-simple voice. The tutor talks; the same little piece of
mathematics keeps reappearing on screen — in words, in symbols,
in a table, in a picture — and the learner watches it change
clothes without changing itself.

---

You worked it out. Good. Now say it — so that someone else can
follow. An answer alone is not enough. Say what you used. Show
how you did it. Then say what you found.

Words are one way to say mathematics. "Three more than a
number." Signs are another way. n + 3. Same meaning, shorter
coat.

Look — the same mathematics in many clothes. Said in words:
double the number. Written in signs: y = 2x. Put in a table:
one makes two, two make four, three make six. Drawn as a line
going up. Four clothes. One mathematics.

Now carry it across. Take the table and make the sentence.
Take the sentence and make the signs. Take the picture and say
it in words.

But check your carrying. Did everything arrive? The numbers?
The units? Which one is biggest? If a fact fell out on the way,
the saying is broken — go back and carry it again.

Mathematics you cannot say is mathematics you cannot share.
Say it, in every way you can.
