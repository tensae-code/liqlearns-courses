1. (Sub lesson 1) A classmate asks what you found in a problem. A complete communication of a result says:
   (a) only the final number
   (b) the concept, the process you used, and the result ✔
   (c) "it was easy"
   (d) the page number of the exercise
   **Answer: (b).** Item constructed for this lesson from its founding requirement. 2. (Sub lesson 1) "Five less than a number," written in mathematical language, is:
   (a) 5 − n
   (b) n − 5 ✔
   (c) n + 5
   (d) 5n
   **Answer: (b)** — the number loses the five, so the five stands second. Item constructed for this lesson from its founding requirement. 3. (Sub lesson 2) Which of these is NOT a representation of mathematics?
   (a) a table
   (b) a graph
   (c) a diagram
   (d) keeping the working in your head and telling no one ✔
   **Answer: (d)** — a representation shows the mathematics to someone else; unshared working shows it to nobody. Item constructed for this lesson from its founding requirement. 6. (Sub lesson 3) A word problem says: "a bus carries 24 pupils and makes 3 trips." The symbolic translation of the total carried is:
   (a) 24 + 3
   (b) 24 × 3 ✔
   (c) 24 − 3
   (d) 3 ÷ 24
   **Answer: (b)** — each trip carries 24, so the total is 3 groups of 24. Item constructed for this lesson from its founding requirement.
