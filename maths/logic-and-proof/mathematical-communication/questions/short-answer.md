# Short answer — Mathematical Communication

1. (Sub lesson 1) Say 3/4 in words, as a part of a whole.
   **Answer: "three quarters" — three of the four equal parts of the whole.** Item constructed for this lesson from its founding requirement. 2. (Sub lesson 1) A learner writes only "42" for a whole problem. What two things are missing from the communication?
   **Answer: the process (how 42 was reached) and the result's meaning (what the 42 counts — its unit and the question it answers).** Item constructed for this lesson from its founding requirement. 6. (Sub lesson 3) A graph shows a class's favourite fruits, but its translation into words says only "apples won." Name two facts the translation lost.
   **Answer: any two of — the count for each fruit; the other fruits and how they placed; how many pupils were asked (the total); how close the result was.** Item constructed for this lesson from its founding requirement.
