A lesson that teaches saying mathematics so someone else can
follow it. All four of Andorra's Escola Andorrana programmes
carry communication as one of their specific competencies — the
Primera programme's C3 requires pupils "to communicate
mathematical concepts, processes and results using a diversity
of representations" (printed pp. 8–21), and the same competency
stands as C4 in the Segona programme (printed pp. 8–24) and in
both Batxillerat programmes (printed pp. 8–21). No lesson in
this course taught communication itself before; the expansion
read flagged it NEW LESSON NEEDED and this lesson is built from
that flag. It is founded on curriculum-requirement evidence
only: the programmes STATE the competency, no pupil textbook in
the course teaches the lesson yet, so Andorra is evidence here
and is counted nowhere. Prerequisite (soft hint only — every
lesson stands alone): none; the representations used as
examples (tables, graphs, symbols) are each met inside the
lesson itself.

**Unit:** Logic and Proof (Unit 13). **Level 166** (Andorra
expansion merge, 2026-11-11).
