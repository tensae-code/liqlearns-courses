# Video script — Problem-solving strategies

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A hard problem? Do not panic. Walk the steps.

**One:** understand it. What is asked?
**Two:** make a plan. Pick a tool.
**Three:** do the plan.
**Four:** look back. Does the answer fit?

Now the tools. **Draw it!**
**List** everything, in order.
**Cross out** what cannot be.
Find the **pattern**.
**Guess — and check** your guess.
Split it into **small problems**.
Solve a **simpler** one first.
Turn the words into **symbols**.

Thirty-five heads. Ninety-four feet.
Chickens and rabbits!
Pretend all are chickens: seventy feet.
Twenty-four feet missing. Twelve rabbits!
Two ways to solve it — listing, and pretending.

Pick a tool. Try it. Look back.
That is problem-solving.
