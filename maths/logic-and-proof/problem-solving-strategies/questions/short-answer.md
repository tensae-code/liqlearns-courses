# Problem-solving strategies — short answer

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — The Steps and the Plan · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — The Eight Named Strategies · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Choosing and Combining: China's Tools.

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name Iraq's lesson that closes the teaching of every chapter of its Grade 1 book.
**Answer:** The problem-solving-plan lesson — one in every chapter (use a model, write a number sentence, draw a picture, and the rest), running through Grade 8 as filed.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the LAST of Iran's four steps, and what do you do in it?
**Answer:** Look back — check the answer against the story and ask whether it fits (Grade 7, Ch.1, pp.1–2, with the step spelled out in the review pages pp.10–12).

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Continue the pattern 1, 5, 9, 13, 17, … with the next term, and name the strategy.
**Answer:** 21 — pattern-spotting: each term is 4 more than the last (the pattern in Iran's Grade 2 problem-solving unit, pp.44–45).

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A bus carries 18 passengers. At each of three stops, 2 get off and nobody gets on. Solve it as sub-problems: how many are left after each stop?
**Answer:** 16, then 14, then 12 — three small subtractions in order (Iran, Grade 1, p.151).

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Iran's Grade 7 symbolic-methods example is 4x + 20000 = 200000. Find x by guessing and checking.
**Answer:** x = 45,000 — 4 × 45,000 = 180,000, and 180,000 + 20,000 = 200,000. ✓

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** In how many ways can 36 be written as a product of two natural numbers, counting 4 × 9 and 9 × 4 as the same way?
**Answer:** 5 ways — 1 × 36, 2 × 18, 3 × 12, 4 × 9, 6 × 6 (Iran, Grade 7 review set, pp.10–12).

**7. (Sub lesson 3)** Solve chickens-and-rabbits by listing: 8 heads, 22 feet. Start from 8 chickens and trade up.
**Answer:** 8 chickens give 16 feet; each trade adds 2 feet; 22 − 16 = 6 feet means 3 trades — 5 chickens and 3 rabbits (check: 5 × 2 + 3 × 4 = 22 ✓).

**8. (Sub lesson 3)** By differencing: a bottle with 4 cups of water weighs 20 units; the same bottle with 2 cups weighs 14 units. What does one cup weigh, and what does the bottle weigh?
**Answer:** The two pictures differ by 2 cups and by 6 units, so one cup is 3 units; the bottle is 20 − 4 × 3 = 8 units (China, Grade 3, p.114).

**9. (Sub lesson 3)** Symbol substitution: △ + △ + △ = 21 and □ × □ = 16 (□ positive). Find △ and □.
**Answer:** △ = 7 and □ = 4 (China, Grade 6 review, pp.100–104).

**10. (Sub lesson 3)** Elimination: Sara, Tom and Uma each play one instrument — piano, drum, flute. Sara does not play piano. Tom does not play piano or flute. Who plays what?
**Answer:** Tom plays the drum (the only one left for him); Sara cannot take piano, so Sara takes the flute; Uma takes the piano (China, Grade 2, Unit 9, pp.109–112).
