# Problem-solving strategies — matching

Match each item on the left with the one that fits it on the right.

**1. (Level 2)** Match each of Iran's eight strategies (Grade 7, Ch.1) with what it does: (a) drawing a figure, (b) systematic listing, (c) eliminating unwanted cases, (d) pattern-spotting, (e) guess and check, (f) sub-problems, (g) solving a simpler problem, (h) symbolic methods.
a) draw the situation and read the answer off the drawing  b) list every case in an order that misses none  c) strike the cases the conditions forbid  d) build the first cases and jump ahead by the rule  e) try a value, test it, steer by the miss  f) split into small questions and answer them in order  g) shrink the problem until the method shows  h) turn the words into an equation
**Answer:** (a) → draw and read off; (b) → list every case in order; (c) → strike forbidden cases; (d) → jump ahead by the rule; (e) → try, test, steer; (f) → split and answer in order; (g) → shrink until the method shows; (h) → turn words into an equation (pp.2–9).

**2. (Level 1)** Match each country with its frame: (a) Iran, Grade 7, (b) Greece, Grade 5, (c) Iraq, Grade 1.
a) four steps: understand, choose a strategy, solve, look back  b) five steps: read/distinguish, plan, solve, answer, reflect  c) a plan lesson of its own in every chapter
**Answer:** (a) → the four steps; (b) → the five steps; (c) → the plan lesson in every chapter.

**3. (Level 3)** Match each Chinese tool with its home: (a) elimination tables and proto-Sudoku, (b) differencing (cup-and-bottle), (c) chickens-and-rabbits by listing and assumption, (d) 0/1 tables and symbol substitution.
a) Grade 2, Unit 9  b) Grade 3, review p.114  c) Grade 4, Unit 9  d) Grade 6, review pp.100–104
**Answer:** (a) → Grade 2, Unit 9; (b) → Grade 3, p.114; (c) → Grade 4, Unit 9; (d) → Grade 6, pp.100–104.

**4. (Level 2)** Match each classroom move with its strategy: (a) "Let me try 20 — too small. Try 25.", (b) "First: how many pencils in the two givings, separately?", (c) "Cross out every number that is not divisible by 3."
a) guess and check  b) sub-problems  c) eliminating unwanted cases
**Answer:** (a) → guess and check; (b) → sub-problems; (c) → eliminating unwanted cases.
