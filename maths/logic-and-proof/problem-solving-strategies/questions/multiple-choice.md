# Problem-solving strategies — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — The Steps and the Plan · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — The Eight Named Strategies · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Choosing and Combining: China's Tools.

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the FIRST of Iran's four steps for solving a problem?
a) Solve it  b) Understand the problem  c) Look back
**Answer:** b) Understand the problem — the frame is understand, choose a strategy, solve, look back (Grade 7, Ch.1, pp.1–2).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Greece's Grade 5 book, the plan step comes just after which step?
a) Read the problem and distinguish what is given from what is asked  b) Reflect  c) Answer
**Answer:** a) Read and distinguish — the five steps run read/distinguish → plan → solve → answer → reflect (Grade 5, Ch.3, pp.11–12).

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** You write down every possible three-digit number from digit cards, in order, so none is missed. Which strategy is that?
a) Guess and check  b) Systematic listing  c) Solving a simpler problem
**Answer:** b) Systematic listing — an order that cannot miss or repeat a case (Iran, Grade 7, p.3).

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A problem is too big to hold in your head, so you answer three small questions hiding inside it, one at a time. Which strategy is that?
a) Sub-problems  b) Pattern-spotting  c) Symbolic methods
**Answer:** a) Sub-problems — split, then answer the parts in order (Iran, Grade 7, p.7).

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Which strategy turns the words of a problem into an equation like 4x + 20000 = 200000?
a) Drawing a figure  b) Eliminating unwanted cases  c) Symbolic methods
**Answer:** c) Symbolic methods — Iran's Grade 7 book works exactly that equation as its example (p.9).

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** In the assumption method for chickens and rabbits, what do you assume first?
a) All the animals are rabbits  b) All the animals are chickens  c) Half and half
**Answer:** b) All the animals are chickens — then count how many feet are missing and trade up (China, Grade 4, Unit 9).

**7. (Sub lesson 3)** A cage has 10 heads and 28 feet, chickens and rabbits only. How many rabbits?
a) 2  b) 4  c) 6
**Answer:** b) 4 — all chickens give 20 feet; the 8 extra feet come in pairs, 8 ÷ 2 = 4 rabbits.

**8. (Sub lesson 3)** In an elimination table, you place a tick for one pairing. What else must you do at once?
a) Nothing — one tick is enough  b) Cross out the rest of that row and column  c) Start a new table
**Answer:** b) Cross out the rest of that row and column — one pairing claimed means no other pairing can use it (China, Grade 2, Unit 9).
