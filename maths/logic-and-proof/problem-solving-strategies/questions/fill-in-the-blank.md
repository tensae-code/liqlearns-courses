# Problem-solving strategies — fill in the blank

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — The Steps and the Plan · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — The Eight Named Strategies · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Choosing and Combining: China's Tools.

Fill each blank with the missing word or number.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Iran's four steps are: understand the problem, choose a suitable ______, solve, and look back.
**Answer:** strategy — choosing the tool is its own step, before any solving (Grade 7, Ch.1, pp.1–2).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Greece's five steps end with "answer" and then ______.
**Answer:** reflect — the fifth step looks back over the whole solution (Grade 5, Ch.3, pp.11–12).

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The strategy of striking out every case the conditions forbid is called ______ unwanted cases.
**Answer:** eliminating — Iran, Grade 7, p.4.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In guess and check, a guess that comes out too big tells you the next guess should be ______.
**Answer:** smaller — the miss steers the next guess; that steering is the strategy (Iran, Grade 7, p.6).

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Chickens and rabbits: 35 heads, 94 feet. Assuming all chickens gives ______ feet, which is 24 short.
**Answer:** 70 — 35 × 2 = 70, and the missing 24 feet make 12 rabbits (China, Grade 4, Unit 9, pp.103–107).

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** In China's Grade 2 Reasoning unit, a 4×4 grid must hold the numbers 1–4 once in every row and every ______.
**Answer:** column — the proto-Sudoku rule (Grade 2, Unit 9, pp.109–112).
