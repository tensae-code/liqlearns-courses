# Video script — Pythagoras

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Draw a right-angled triangle! Sides 3 and 4.
Build a square on each side. Count the squares:
**9! 16! 25!** And **9 + 16 = 25!**

That is the rule — **Pythagoras**!
The square of the hypotenuse equals the sum of the squares of
the other two sides! **a² + b² = c²!**

The hypotenuse is the side across from the right angle — the
longest side, always!

Find it: sides 6 and 8. Square them: **36 and 64!**
Add them: **100!** Square root: **10!** The hypotenuse is 10!

Find a shorter side: hypotenuse 13, one side 5.
**169 minus 25 is 144!** Square root: **12!**

Is it right-angled? Check the squares! **3, 4, 5 — yes!**
**5, 5, 6 — no!**

Now you. Draw a triangle. Square the sides. Add them up!
That is Pythagoras.
