# Pythagoras — true or false

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Level 1)** Pythagoras' theorem works for every triangle.
**False** — it works only for right-angled triangles. No right angle, no Pythagoras.

**2. (Level 1)** In a 3-4-5 triangle, 9 + 16 = 25.
**True** — the squares of the legs add to the square of the hypotenuse (Sierra Leone, JSS 3, L42).

**3. (Level 2)** To find the hypotenuse you add the legs and take the square root.
**False** — you square the legs first, then add, then take the square root: √(a² + b²).

**4. (Level 2)** The diagonal of a rectangle is the hypotenuse of the two triangles it makes.
**True** — the diagonal cuts the rectangle into two right-angled triangles (Sierra Leone, JSS 3, L45).

**5. (Level 3)** To find a shorter side you subtract the squares: a = √(c² − b²).
**True** (Sierra Leone, JSS 3, L44).

**6. (Level 3)** If a² + b² is less than c², the triangle is acute.
**False** — if a² + b² is less than c², the triangle is obtuse; if more, it is acute (Egypt, Grade 8, 3-2).
