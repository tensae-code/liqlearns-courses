# Pythagoras — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In a right-angled triangle, the hypotenuse is ______.
a) the side forming the right angle  b) the side opposite the right angle  c) the shortest side
**Answer: b) the side opposite the right angle** — and the longest side (Sierra Leone, JSS 3, L44).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** 3² + 4² = ______.
a) 7  b) 12  c) 25
**Answer: c) 25** — 9 + 16 = 25, so the hypotenuse is 5.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A right-angled triangle has legs 6 and 8. Its hypotenuse is ______.
a) 10  b) 14  c) 100
**Answer: a) 10** — √(36 + 64) = √100 = 10.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A right-angled triangle has legs 5 and 12. Its hypotenuse is ______.
a) 13  b) 17  c) 169
**Answer: a) 13** — √(25 + 144) = √169 = 13.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** A right-angled triangle has hypotenuse 13 and one leg 5. The other leg is ______.
a) 8  b) 12  c) 18
**Answer: b) 12** — √(169 − 25) = √144 = 12 (Sierra Leone, JSS 3, L44).

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** A right-angled triangle has hypotenuse 10 and one leg 6. The other leg is ______.
a) 4  b) 8  c) 16
**Answer: b) 8** — √(100 − 36) = √64 = 8.

**7. (Sub lesson 3)** Sides 5, 5, 6: is the triangle right-angled?
a) Yes — 25 + 25 = 36  b) No — 25 + 25 = 50, not 36  c) Yes — the two equal sides make it right-angled
**Answer: b) No — 25 + 25 = 50, not 36** (Egypt, Grade 8, 3-2 — the converse test).
