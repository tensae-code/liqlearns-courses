# Pythagoras — short answer

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Write Pythagoras' theorem in symbols.
**Answer:** a² + b² = c², where c is the hypotenuse (Sierra Leone, JSS 3, L42).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Why is the hypotenuse always the longest side?
**Answer:** Because its square equals the sum of the other two squares, so it is bigger than either one alone.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A rectangle is 12 cm by 18 cm. Find its diagonal to one decimal place.
**Answer:** About 21.6 cm — √(144 + 324) = √468 ≈ 21.6 (Sierra Leone, JSS 3, L45).

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A ladder's foot is 5 m from a wall and its top reaches 12 m. How long is the ladder?
**Answer:** 13 m — √(25 + 144) = √169 = 13 (Egypt, Grade 8, 3-2).

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** A right-angled triangle has hypotenuse 25 and one leg 7. Find the other leg.
**Answer:** 24 — √(625 − 49) = √576 = 24.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** Sides 7, 24, 25: right-angled or not? Show the squares.
**Answer:** Right-angled — 49 + 576 = 625 = 25².
