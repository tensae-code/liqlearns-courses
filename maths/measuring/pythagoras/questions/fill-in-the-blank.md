# Pythagoras — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with a word from the box.

**Box:** hypotenuse · legs · squares · square root · converse · longest

**1. (Level 1)** The side opposite the right angle is the ______.
**Answer: hypotenuse.**

**2. (Level 1)** The two sides forming the right angle are the ______.
**Answer: legs.**

**3. (Level 1)** The hypotenuse is the ______ side of the triangle.
**Answer: longest.**

**4. (Level 2)** Pythagoras' rule is about the ______ of the sides, not the sides themselves.
**Answer: squares.**

**5. (Level 2)** After adding the squares, take the ______ to get the side.
**Answer: square root.**

**6. (Level 3)** Testing whether the squares add up is the ______ of the theorem.
**Answer: converse.**
