# Pythagoras — fill in the blank

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Pythagoras — the Rule · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Pythagoras — Finding the Hypotenuse · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Pythagoras — Finding a Shorter Side and the Converse.

Fill each blank with a word from the box.

**Box:** hypotenuse · legs · squares · square root · converse · longest

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The side opposite the right angle is the ______.
**Answer: hypotenuse.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The two sides forming the right angle are the ______.
**Answer: legs.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The hypotenuse is the ______ side of the triangle.
**Answer: longest.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Pythagoras' rule is about the ______ of the sides, not the sides themselves.
**Answer: squares.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** After adding the squares, take the ______ to get the side.
**Answer: square root.**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** Testing whether the squares add up is the ______ of the theorem.
**Answer: converse.**
