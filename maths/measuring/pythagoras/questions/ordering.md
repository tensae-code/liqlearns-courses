# Pythagoras — ordering

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Pythagoras — the Rule · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Pythagoras — Finding the Hypotenuse · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Pythagoras — Finding a Shorter Side and the Converse.

Put each set in order, smallest first. (This question type exists
for this lesson because hypotenuses ARE ordered by the squares
that make them.)

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Order these by the size of their square: 3² · 4² · 5².
**Answer:** 9, 16, 25.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Order these hypotenuses, smallest first: legs 3, 4 · legs 5, 12 · legs 6, 8.
**Answer:** 5 (legs 3, 4), 10 (legs 6, 8), 13 (legs 5, 12).

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Order these diagonals, smallest first: rectangle 3 by 4 · rectangle 6 by 8 · rectangle 9 by 12.
**Answer:** 5 cm (3 by 4), 10 cm (6 by 8), 15 cm (9 by 12).

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Order these shorter sides, smallest first: hypotenuse 13 leg 5 · hypotenuse 25 leg 7 · hypotenuse 10 leg 6.
**Answer:** 8 (hypotenuse 10, leg 6), 12 (hypotenuse 13, leg 5), 24 (hypotenuse 25, leg 7).

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Order these c² values, smallest first: 13² · 5² · 10².
**Answer:** 25 (5²), 100 (10²), 169 (13²).
