# Cavalieri's Principle — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Level 1)** Zu Geng's principle says two solids have equal volumes if ______.
a) they have the same surface area  b) they have the same height and equal cross-section areas at every height  c) they look the same shape
**Answer:** b) same height and equal cross-section areas at every height (China, B edition, §11.1.6, p.82).

**2. (Level 1)** A neat stack of 20 coins is pushed into a lean. Its volume ______.
a) grows  b) shrinks  c) stays the same
**Answer:** c) stays the same — every slice is the same coin, only shifted (China, B edition, §11.1.6, p.82).

**3. (Level 2)** A cylinder has base area 20 cm² and height 9 cm. Its volume is ______.
a) 29 cm³  b) 180 cm³  c) 60 cm³
**Answer:** b) 180 cm³ — V = Sh = 20 × 9 (China, A edition, §8.3, pp.121–123).

**4. (Level 2)** An oblique (leaning) prism has base area 15 cm² and height 8 cm. Its volume is ______.
a) 120 cm³  b) less than 120 cm³  c) more than 120 cm³
**Answer:** a) 120 cm³ — the lean only shifts the slices; V = Sh still holds.

**5. (Level 3)** A pyramid has base area 30 cm² and height 6 cm. Its volume is ______.
a) 180 cm³  b) 90 cm³  c) 60 cm³
**Answer:** c) 60 cm³ — V = ⅓Sh = ⅓ × 30 × 6 (China, A edition, §8.3, pp.121–123).
