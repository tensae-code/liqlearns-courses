# Cavalieri's Principle — short answer

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Level 1)** In China's B-edition book, whose name does the principle carry?
**Answer:** Zu Geng (祖暅原理) — Compulsory Volume 4, B edition, §11.1.6, p.82.

**2. (Level 1)** Two solids have the same height, and their cross-sections match in area at the base and at the top, but not in between. Does the principle say their volumes are equal?
**Answer:** No — the sections must be equal at EVERY height (B edition, §11.1.6, p.82).

**3. (Level 2)** Write the volume formula for a prism or cylinder, and say what S and h are.
**Answer:** V = Sh — S is the base area, h the height (A edition, §8.3, pp.121–123).

**4. (Level 3)** Why is a pyramid's volume ⅓Sh and not Sh?
**Answer:** A triangular prism splits into three pyramids of equal volume, so one pyramid is one third of the prism's Sh (A edition, §8.3, pp.121–123).

**5. (Level 3)** A cone has base area 24 cm² and height 5 cm. Find its volume.
**Answer:** 40 cm³ — ⅓ × 24 × 5.
