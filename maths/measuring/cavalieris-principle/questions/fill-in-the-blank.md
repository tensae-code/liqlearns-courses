# Cavalieri's Principle — fill in the blank

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Cavalieri's Principle — Equal Sections, Equal Volumes · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Cavalieri's Principle — V = Sh for Prisms and Cylinders · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Cavalieri's Principle — V = ⅓Sh for Pyramids and Cones.

Fill each blank with the missing number or words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** If two solids have the same ______ and their cross-sections at every height have equal ______, their volumes are equal.
**Answer:** height; areas (China, B edition, §11.1.6, p.82).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A prism with base area 18 cm² and height 7 cm has volume ______ cm³.
**Answer:** 126 — V = Sh = 18 × 7 (China, A edition, §8.3, pp.121–123).

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A triangular prism splits into ______ pyramids of equal volume, so each pyramid is ______ of the prism.
**Answer:** three; one third (China, A edition, §8.3, pp.121–123).

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** A cone and a cylinder share a base area and height. The cone's volume is ______ times the cylinder's.
**Answer:** one third (⅓) — V = ⅓Sh against V = Sh.
