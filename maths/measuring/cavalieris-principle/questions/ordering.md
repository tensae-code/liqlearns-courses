# Cavalieri's Principle — ordering

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Cavalieri's Principle — Equal Sections, Equal Volumes · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Cavalieri's Principle — V = Sh for Prisms and Cylinders · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Cavalieri's Principle — V = ⅓Sh for Pyramids and Cones.

Put each list in the right order.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Order the steps of a Cavalieri check: (a) compare the cross-section areas at every height  (b) check the two solids have the same height  (c) conclude the volumes are equal  (d) slice both solids at the same heights.
**Answer:** (b), (d), (a), (c) (China, B edition, §11.1.6, p.82).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Order these solids by volume, smallest first — all have base area 20 cm² and height 6 cm: pyramid, prism, cone.
**Answer:** Pyramid and cone tie smallest (40 cm³ each, ⅓Sh), then the prism (120 cm³, Sh) (China, A edition, §8.3, pp.121–123).

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Order the A-edition inquiry's build: (a) the cuboid's volume is base area × height  (b) a triangular prism is split into three equal-volume pyramids  (c) the prism and cylinder take V = Sh by the principle  (d) the pyramid and cone take V = ⅓Sh.
**Answer:** (a), (c), (b), (d) — cuboid first, then Sh by equal slices, then the split, then the third (§8.3, pp.121–123).
