# Cavalieri's Principle — matching

Match each item on the left with the one right partner on the right.

**1. (Level 1)** Match each pair of solids to the principle's verdict.
Pairs: (i) straight prism and leaning prism, same base and height  (ii) cone and cylinder, same base and height  (iii) two stacks of the same coins, one neat, one slid
Verdicts: (a) equal volumes  (b) the principle cannot declare them equal — sections differ  (c) equal volumes
**Answer:** (i)–(a), (ii)–(b), (iii)–(c) — equal sections at every height decide it (China, B edition, §11.1.6, p.82).

**2. (Level 2)** Match each solid (base area S = 20 cm², height h = 6 cm) to its volume.
Solids: (i) prism  (ii) cylinder  (iii) pyramid
Volumes: (a) 120 cm³  (b) 40 cm³  (c) 120 cm³ — two solids share this one
**Answer:** (i)–(a) Sh, (ii)–(c) Sh, (iii)–(b) ⅓Sh (China, A edition, §8.3, pp.121–123).

**3. (Level 3)** Match each formula piece to its meaning.
Pieces: (i) S  (ii) h  (iii) ⅓
Meanings: (a) the height of the solid  (b) the share left when three equal pyramids fill a prism  (c) the area of the base (and of every cross-section parallel to it)
**Answer:** (i)–(c), (ii)–(a), (iii)–(b).
