# Cavalieri's Principle — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB
LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure — README, "Units, Lessons and Sub
Lessons"). Practice is tied to the skill number, as before. The
questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson
view. The sub lessons are the depths inside this one thing —
Cavalieri's principle — the order China's books present it in: the principle itself first, then V = Sh, then V = ⅓Sh.

Mapping from the old depth layers: old Level 1 = Sub lesson 1; old Level 2 = Sub lesson 2; old Level 3 = Sub lesson 3.

## Sub lesson 1 — Cavalieri's Principle — Equal Sections, Equal Volumes
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: state the
principle (same height, equal cross-section areas at every
height, equal volumes) and apply it to a shifted stack or a
leaning solid.

## Sub lesson 2 — Cavalieri's Principle — V = Sh for Prisms and Cylinders
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: find the volume
of a prism or cylinder as base area × height, including an
oblique one, and say why the lean does not change it.

## Sub lesson 3 — Cavalieri's Principle — V = ⅓Sh for Pyramids and Cones
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: find the volume
of a pyramid or cone as ⅓ × base area × height, explain the
third by the three-pyramid split of a prism, and compare solids
by the principle.
