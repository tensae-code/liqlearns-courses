# Cavalieri's Principle — questions by level

Practice by level, tied to the skill number. The questions live in
the type files; this file is the by-level view. The levels are the
depths inside this one thing — Cavalieri's principle — the order
China's books present it in: the principle itself first, then
V = Sh, then V = ⅓Sh.

## Level 1 — the principle
Level 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: state the
principle (same height, equal cross-section areas at every
height, equal volumes) and apply it to a shifted stack or a
leaning solid.

## Level 2 — V = Sh for prisms and cylinders
Level 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: find the volume
of a prism or cylinder as base area × height, including an
oblique one, and say why the lean does not change it.

## Level 3 — V = ⅓Sh for pyramids and cones
Level 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: find the volume
of a pyramid or cone as ⅓ × base area × height, explain the
third by the three-pyramid split of a prism, and compare solids
by the principle.
