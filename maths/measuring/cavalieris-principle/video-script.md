# Video script — Cavalieri's Principle

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A stack of coins. Push it — it leans!
Did the amount of coin change? **No!**
Every coin is the same coin. Only shifted!

That is the big idea — **Zu Geng's principle**!
Slice both solids at the same height.
Same slice area, every height, same height —
**same volume!**

So a prism, straight or leaning: **base area times height!**
A cylinder too: **V = S × h!**

And a pyramid? Three pyramids fill one prism!
So a pyramid is **one third** — **V = one-third S × h!**
A cone the same!

Now you. Lean a stack. The slices did not change — neither did
the volume. That is Cavalieri's principle.
