# Scale Drawing and Maps — questions by level

Practice by level, tied to the skill number. The questions live in
the type files; this file is the by-level view. The levels are the
depths inside this one thing — scale — the order the books teach
it in: what a scale is first, then the two directions of
conversion with bar scales, then drawing to scale with compass
points and geographic coordinates.

## Level 1 — what a scale is
Level 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: say scale =
drawing length ÷ real length in the same unit, and read what
1:n means on simple scales like 1:100 and 1:10.

## Level 2 — the two directions and bar scales
Level 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: turn a real
length into a drawing length and a drawing length back into a
real length, and read a bar scale (for example 1 cm = 5 km is
1:500,000).

## Level 3 — drawing to scale, points and coordinates
Level 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: plan a drawing
at a chosen scale, enlarge and reduce by a scale, use the eight
compass points on a map, and read a place's latitude and
longitude.
