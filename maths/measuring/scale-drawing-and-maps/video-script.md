# Video script — Scale Drawing and Maps

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The classroom is big. The page is small.
So we **shrink** the classroom — the same shrink for every wall!

That shrink is the **scale**!
The wall is 6 metres. On the plan it is 6 centimetres.
**6 cm divided by 600 cm — the scale is 1 to 100!**

One centimetre on the plan — **one metre** in the room!

A map of the whole country shrinks much more.
One centimetre is **5 kilometres** — the scale is **1 to 500,000!**

Drawing to real: **multiply**. Real to drawing: use the scale.
And the bar on the map? Measure it — it tells the truth!

Now you. Draw your room. Small page, big room — one scale!
That is scale drawing.
