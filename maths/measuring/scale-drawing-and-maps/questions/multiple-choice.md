# Scale Drawing and Maps — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Level 1)** A plan has scale 1:100. What does the 100 mean?
a) The real room is 100 cm long  b) 1 cm on the plan is 100 cm in real life  c) The plan is 100 times the room
**Answer:** b) 1 cm on the plan is 100 cm in real life — scale = drawing ÷ real, in the same unit (Laos, Grade 6, L21).

**2. (Level 1)** A drawing is made at scale 5:1, like Laos's enlargement. The drawing is ______ the real thing.
a) smaller than  b) the same size as  c) bigger than
**Answer:** c) bigger than — the first number is the drawing, and 5 > 1 (Laos, Grade 6, L21).

**3. (Level 2)** A wall is 6 m long. On a plan at 1:100 it is drawn ______.
a) 6 cm  b) 60 cm  c) 0.6 cm
**Answer:** a) 6 cm — drawing = real × scale = 600 cm × 1/100 (Laos, Grade 6, L22).

**4. (Level 2)** On a map with bar scale 1 cm = 5 km, two towns are 4 cm apart on the map. The real distance is ______.
a) 9 km  b) 20 km  c) 45 km
**Answer:** b) 20 km — 4 × 5 km (Laos, Grade 6, L21).

**5. (Level 2)** A bar scale says 5 cm = 2 km. The ratio scale is ______.
a) 1:400  b) 1:4,000  c) 1:40,000
**Answer:** c) 1:40,000 — 2 km is 200,000 cm, and 200,000 ÷ 5 = 40,000 (Laos, Grade 6, L21).

**6. (Level 3)** Kuwait's main tower is 187 m tall. In the 1:500 model (1 cm = 5 m) it stands ______.
a) 18.7 cm  b) 37.4 cm  c) 93.5 cm
**Answer:** b) 37.4 cm — 187 ÷ 5 (Kuwait, Grade 10 Term 1, project, pp.81–82).

**7. (Level 2)** A map has scale 1:250,000, the kind Indonesia's Grade 7 book teaches as a ratio application. On this map, 1 cm stands for ______ in real life.
a) 250 m  b) 2.5 km  c) 25 km
**Answer:** b) 2.5 km — 250,000 cm is 2.5 km, with both lengths in the same unit first (Indonesia, Grade 7, Ch. 3, pp.83–122).
