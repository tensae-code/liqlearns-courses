# Scale Drawing and Maps — fill in the blank

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Scale Drawing and Maps — What a Scale Is · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Scale Drawing and Maps — the Two Directions and Bar Scales · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Scale Drawing and Maps — Drawing to Scale, Compass Points and Coordinates.

Fill each blank with the missing number or words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Scale = ______ length ÷ ______ length, with both lengths in the same unit.
**Answer:** drawing; real — Laos's Grade 6 definition (L21).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Drawing length = real length × ______. Real length = drawing length ÷ ______.
**Answer:** scale; scale — the two formulas of Laos, Grade 6, L22.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A bar scale of 1 cm = 5 km is the ratio scale 1:______, because 5 km is 500,000 cm.
**Answer:** 500,000 (Laos, Grade 6, L21).

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** At 1:500, 1 cm in the model is ______ m in reality, so a 113 m tower is modelled by ______ cm.
**Answer:** 5; 22.6 — 113 ÷ 5 (Kuwait, Grade 10 Term 1, project, pp.81–82).

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** In Nepal's compass points, the angle from N to E is ______° and from N to NE is ______°.
**Answer:** 90; 45 (Nepal, Grade 7, Unit 10).

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Indonesia's Grade 7 book teaches map and plan scale inside its ______ chapter, and the fixed multiplier it applies to every length is called the scale ______.
**Answer:** Ratio; factor — scale is taught as a ratio application (Indonesia, Grade 7, Ch. 3, pp.83–122).
