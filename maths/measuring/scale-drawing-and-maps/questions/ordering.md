# Scale Drawing and Maps — ordering

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Scale Drawing and Maps — What a Scale Is · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Scale Drawing and Maps — the Two Directions and Bar Scales · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Scale Drawing and Maps — Drawing to Scale, Compass Points and Coordinates.

Put each list in the right order.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Order these scales from the biggest shrink to the smallest shrink: 1:100, 1:500,000, 1:10.
**Answer:** 1:500,000, 1:100, 1:10 — the bigger the second number, the bigger the shrink.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Order the steps for finding a scale from a drawing: (a) divide drawing ÷ real  (b) measure the drawing length  (c) write the ratio as 1:n  (d) measure (or look up) the real length in the same unit.
**Answer:** (b), (d), (a), (c) — measure both in one unit, divide, then write 1:n (Laos, Grade 6, L21–L22).

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Order these real distances from shortest to longest, all read on the bar scale 1 cm = 5 km: map lengths 3 cm, 8 cm, 1 cm, 5 cm.
**Answer:** 1 cm (5 km), 3 cm (15 km), 5 cm (25 km), 8 cm (40 km) — real distance grows with map length at a fixed scale.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Order the steps for drawing a room at 1:100: (a) draw the walls at the plan lengths  (b) measure the room  (c) divide every real length by 100  (d) check one drawn wall against its real wall.
**Answer:** (b), (c), (a), (d) — measure, convert, draw, check (Laos, Grade 6, L21, room plan at 1:100).
