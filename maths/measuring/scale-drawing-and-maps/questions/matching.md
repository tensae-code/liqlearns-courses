# Scale Drawing and Maps — matching

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Scale Drawing and Maps — What a Scale Is · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Scale Drawing and Maps — the Two Directions and Bar Scales · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Scale Drawing and Maps — Drawing to Scale, Compass Points and Coordinates.

Match each item on the left with the one right partner on the right.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Match the scale to its meaning.
Scales: (i) 1:100  (ii) 5:1  (iii) 1:10
Meanings: (a) an enlargement — drawing 5 times the real thing  (b) 1 cm drawing = 10 cm real  (c) 1 cm drawing = 1 m real
**Answer:** (i)–(c), (ii)–(a), (iii)–(b) — read drawing : real in the same unit; Laos's Grade 6 book lists 1:100, 5:1 (L21) and a 1:10 drawing (L16).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Match each map reading to its real distance on the bar scale 1 cm = 5 km.
Readings: (i) 2 cm  (ii) 6 cm  (iii) 0.5 cm
Distances: (a) 30 km  (b) 2.5 km  (c) 10 km
**Answer:** (i)–(c), (ii)–(a), (iii)–(b) — multiply the map length by 5 km (Laos, Grade 6, L21).

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Match each Kuwait tower to its model height at 1:500 (1 cm = 5 m).
Towers: (i) 187 m  (ii) 147 m  (iii) 113 m
Model heights: (a) 22.6 cm  (b) 37.4 cm  (c) 29.4 cm
**Answer:** (i)–(b), (ii)–(c), (iii)–(a) — divide each height by 5 (Kuwait, Grade 10 Term 1, project, pp.81–82).

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Match each compass point to its angle clockwise from N, the Nepal way.
Points: (i) NE  (ii) E  (iii) SE
Angles: (a) 90°  (b) 135°  (c) 45°
**Answer:** (i)–(c), (ii)–(a), (iii)–(b) — the eight points split the full turn into 45° steps (Nepal, Grade 7, Unit 10).

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Match each scale factor, in Indonesia's Grade 7 sense, to what it does to a drawing.
Factors: (i) 1/100  (ii) 1  (iii) 3
Effects: (a) the drawing is the same size as the real thing  (b) the drawing is an enlargement, 3 times the real thing  (c) the drawing is a reduction, each length 1/100 of the real length
**Answer:** (i)–(c), (ii)–(a), (iii)–(b) — multiply every real length by the one scale factor (Indonesia, Grade 7, Ch. 3, pp.83–122).
