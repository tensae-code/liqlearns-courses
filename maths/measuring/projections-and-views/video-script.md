# Video script — Projections and Views

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Here is a box. It is solid. It has height, width, depth.
Paper is flat. How do we draw the box on paper?

Stand in front of it. Draw what you see — a rectangle!
That is the **front view**.
Fly above it like a bird. Draw what you see.
That is the **top view**.
Stand at the side. Draw again. The **side view**.
Three flat drawings — and the box is fully told!

The sun does it too. A pole's **shadow** is its projection on the ground.

Want to show all three faces at once?
Draw from the corner — that is an **axonometric** drawing.
Engineers draw this way. It is called **technical drawing**.

And unfold the box flat — six squares — that is its **net**.
Fold it up, the box is back. That is projections and views!
