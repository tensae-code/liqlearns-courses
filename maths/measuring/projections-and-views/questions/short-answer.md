# Projections and Views — short answer

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Projections and Views — the Three Views · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Projections and Views — Drawings That Show Depth · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Projections and Views — Reading Drawings.

Give a short answer, with the working where it helps.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the three standard views of a solid, and the two measurements each one gives.
**Answer:** Front view (height and width), top view (width and depth), side view (depth and height) — together they give all three dimensions.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Laos's book photographs a birdhouse from different viewpoints. Why is one photograph not enough to build the birdhouse from?
**Answer:** One viewpoint shows only the faces turned towards it — sizes in the hidden direction (depth) are missing; other views are needed to fix the solid completely.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is the difference between an orthogonal view and a perspective drawing of the same building?
**Answer:** The orthogonal view shows the building as it is — parallel edges stay parallel and sizes are true; perspective shows it as the eye sees it — parallel lines run together to a vanishing point, as South Africa's enrichment work on photographs and railway lines shows.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Draw (describe in words) a net of a square pyramid, and count its faces.
**Answer:** A square with a triangle joined to each of its four sides — 5 faces in all (1 square base, 4 triangular faces).

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** A cuboid's views are: front — a rectangle 6 by 3; top — a rectangle 6 by 2; side — a rectangle 2 by 3. Give the cuboid's length, width (depth) and height.
**Answer:** Length 6, depth 2, height 3 — the front view gives length and height, the top view confirms length and gives depth, the side view confirms depth and height.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** What is a technical drawing for, and which country's study book teaches it as mathematics?
**Answer:** It is a scaled drawing with measurements that a builder or maker can build from; Vietnam's Grade 11 study book teaches it (Bài 12, technical drawing).
