# Projections and Views — fill in the blank

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. Fill in the missing word or number.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The view of a solid from directly above is called the ______ view.
**Answer:** top — what the bird sees; it gives width and depth.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A shadow is a ______ of the solid that casts it, made by light on a flat surface.
**Answer:** projection — the shape carried by the light rays onto the ground, as in Laos's pole shadows.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A flat pattern that folds up into a solid is called its ______.
**Answer:** net — Jordan covers nets beside views and cross-sections.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** An ______ drawing tilts a solid so that three faces show at once, with edges kept parallel.
**Answer:** axonometric — taught in Vietnam's study book beside orthogonal projection; isometric drawing is its school form.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** The flat shape shown when a plane slices through a solid is called a ______.
**Answer:** cross-section — Jordan's Grade 8 book teaches cross-sections with projections and views.
