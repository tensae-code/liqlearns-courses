# Projections and Views — ordering

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Projections and Views — the Three Views · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Projections and Views — Drawings That Show Depth · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Projections and Views — Reading Drawings.

Put the items in order.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Put the steps of making a front view in order: (i) draw the flat shape your eye sees (ii) label the drawing FRONT (iii) stand square in front of one face.
**Answer:** (iii), (i), (ii) — stand square first, draw what is flat against your eye, then label the drawing before its name can be swapped.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Put these drawing ways in order of exactness for a builder, least exact first: the three orthogonal views with measurements (a technical drawing); a photograph from one viewpoint; a perspective drawing.
**Answer:** A photograph from one viewpoint, a perspective drawing, the three orthogonal views with measurements — only the last can be built from exactly.
