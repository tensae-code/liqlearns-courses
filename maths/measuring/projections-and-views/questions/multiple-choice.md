# Projections and Views — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Level 1)** What is the top view of a tin (a cylinder standing upright)?
a) A rectangle  b) A circle  c) A triangle
**Answer:** b) A circle — from directly above, only the round top face shows.

**2. (Level 1)** In an orthogonal projection, the projecting lines meet the plane at what angle?
a) 45°  b) 90°  c) Any angle
**Answer:** b) 90° — orthogonal means at right angles to the plane.

**3. (Level 1)** A pole's shadow on flat ground is an example of what?
a) A net  b) A cross-section  c) A projection
**Answer:** c) A projection — the solid's shape carried by light onto a flat surface, as Laos's Grade 7 book opens the topic.

**4. (Level 2)** Which drawing shows three faces of a box at once, with edges kept parallel?
a) The front view  b) An axonometric (isometric) drawing  c) The top view
**Answer:** b) An axonometric (isometric) drawing — Vietnam teaches axonometric beside orthogonal projection; Jordan's isometric drawing is its school form.

**5. (Level 2)** A cube is unfolded into its net. How many squares must the net show?
a) 4  b) 5  c) 6
**Answer:** c) 6 — a cube has six faces, and a net shows every face exactly once.

**6. (Level 3)** A solid's front view is a triangle and its top view is a circle. What is the solid?
a) A cylinder  b) A cone  c) A cube
**Answer:** b) A cone — the triangle is its profile; the circle is its base seen from above.

**7. (Level 3)** A plane slices a cylinder straight across its axis. What shape is the cross-section?
a) A rectangle  b) A circle  c) A triangle
**Answer:** b) A circle — the cut face is congruent to the cylinder's base.

**8. (Level 3)** In Monge's method, as China's reading tells it, a solid is fixed exactly by orthographic projection onto how many perpendicular planes?
a) One  b) Two  c) Four
**Answer:** b) Two — front and top views on two perpendicular planes fix the solid (reading as filed).
