**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Match each solid to its front view: (a) a standing cylinder (b) a cone (c) a cube — square, rectangle, triangle
**Answer:** a) rectangle, b) triangle, c) square — each front view is the flat shape square against that face.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Match each flat drawing to how it is made: (a) a net (b) a cross-section (c) a shadow — cut by a plane; unfold the faces; light on a surface
**Answer:** a) unfold the faces, b) cut by a plane, c) light on a surface — the three ways a solid becomes a flat shape, beside looking at it.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Match each country to its part in this lesson: (a) Vietnam (b) Laos (c) China (d) North Macedonia — projections and shadows with a birdhouse; technical drawing; descriptive geometry and Monge (reading); parallel and orthogonal projection at Grade 8
**Answer:** a) technical drawing, b) projections and shadows with a birdhouse, c) descriptive geometry and Monge (reading), d) parallel and orthogonal projection at Grade 8.
