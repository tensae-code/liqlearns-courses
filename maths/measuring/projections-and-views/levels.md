# Projections and Views — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB
LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure — README, "Units, Lessons and Sub
Lessons"). Practice is tied to the skill number, as before. The
questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson
view. The sub lessons are the depths inside this one thing —
projections and views — the three orthogonal views first, then drawings that show depth, then reading a solid back from its drawings, the order the books teach it in.

Mapping from the old depth layers: old Level 1 = Sub lesson 1; old Level 2 = Sub lesson 2; old Level 3 = Sub lesson 3.

## Sub lesson 1 — Projections and Views — the Three Views
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: draw and name the front, top and side views of a simple solid, and explain a shadow as a projection.

## Sub lesson 2 — Projections and Views — Drawings That Show Depth
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: tell orthogonal, axonometric/isometric and perspective drawings apart, unfold a solid into its net, and fold a net back.

## Sub lesson 3 — Projections and Views — Reading Drawings
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: rebuild a solid from its three views, read a cross-section, and say what technical drawing and descriptive geometry are for.
