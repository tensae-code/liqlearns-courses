# Projections and Views — questions by level

Practice by level, tied to the skill number. The questions live in
the type files; this file is the by-level view. The levels are the
depths inside this one thing — projections and views — the three
orthogonal views first, then drawings that show depth, then
reading a solid back from its drawings, the order the books teach
it in.

## Level 1 — the three views
Level 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: draw and name the front, top and side views of a simple solid, and explain a shadow as a projection.

## Level 2 — drawings that show depth
Level 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: tell orthogonal, axonometric/isometric and perspective drawings apart, unfold a solid into its net, and fold a net back.

## Level 3 — reading drawings
Level 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: rebuild a solid from its three views, read a cross-section, and say what technical drawing and descriptive geometry are for.
