# Volume and Surface Area — ordering

Put each set in order, smallest first. (This question type exists
for this lesson because volumes and surface areas ARE ordered by
size.)

**1. (Level 1)** Order these volumes, smallest first: 500 cm³ · 2 L · 750 cm³.
**Answer:** 500 cm³, 750 cm³, 2 L (2,000 cm³).

**2. (Level 2)** Order these cuboid volumes, smallest first: 2 by 2 by 2 · 4 by 3 by 2 · 5 by 4 by 3.
**Answer:** 8 cm³ (2 × 2 × 2), 24 cm³ (4 × 3 × 2), 60 cm³ (5 × 4 × 3).

**3. (Level 2)** Order these by volume, smallest first: cube side 2 · cube side 4 · cube side 3.
**Answer:** 8 cm³ (side 2), 27 cm³ (side 3), 64 cm³ (side 4).

**4. (Level 3)** Order these surface areas, smallest first: cube side 2 · cube side 3 · cube side 5.
**Answer:** 24 cm² (6 × 4), 54 cm² (6 × 9), 150 cm² (6 × 25).

**5. (Level 3)** Order these cylinder volumes (π ≈ 3.14), smallest first: radius 2 height 5 · radius 3 height 10 · radius 1 height 10.
**Answer:** 31.4 cm³ (r = 1, h = 10), 62.8 cm³ (r = 2, h = 5), 282.6 cm³ (r = 3, h = 10).
