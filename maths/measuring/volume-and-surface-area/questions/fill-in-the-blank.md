# Volume and Surface Area — fill in the blank

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Volume and Surface Area — Volume by Counting Cubes · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Volume and Surface Area — Cuboids, Cubes, Prisms and Cylinders · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Volume and Surface Area — Surface Area from Nets.

Fill each blank with a word from the box.

**Box:** volume · surface area · l × w × h · l³ · πr²h · net

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How much a solid holds is its ______.
**Answer: volume.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A solid unfolded flat is its ______.
**Answer: net.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The volume of a cuboid is ______.
**Answer: l × w × h.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The volume of a cube of side l is ______.
**Answer: l³.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The volume of a cylinder is ______.
**Answer: πr²h.**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** How much skin a solid has is its ______.
**Answer: surface area.**
