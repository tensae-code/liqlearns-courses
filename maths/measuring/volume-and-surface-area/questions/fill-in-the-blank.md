# Volume and Surface Area — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with a word from the box.

**Box:** volume · surface area · l × w × h · l³ · πr²h · net

**1. (Level 1)** How much a solid holds is its ______.
**Answer: volume.**

**2. (Level 1)** A solid unfolded flat is its ______.
**Answer: net.**

**3. (Level 2)** The volume of a cuboid is ______.
**Answer: l × w × h.**

**4. (Level 2)** The volume of a cube of side l is ______.
**Answer: l³.**

**5. (Level 2)** The volume of a cylinder is ______.
**Answer: πr²h.**

**6. (Level 3)** How much skin a solid has is its ______.
**Answer: surface area.**
