# Volume and Surface Area — matching

Match each solid or formula to its meaning.

**Set 1 (Level 1)**
1. volume — **how much a solid holds**
2. surface area — **how much skin a solid has**
3. cm³ — **the unit of volume**
4. cm² — **the unit of surface area**
5. 1,000 cm³ — **1 litre**

**Set 2 (Level 2)**
1. cuboid — **V = l × w × h**
2. cube, side l — **V = l³**
3. prism — **V = cross-section × length**
4. cylinder — **V = πr²h**
5. pyramid — **V = ⅓ × base area × height**

**Set 3 (Level 3)**
1. cube, side l — **SA = 6l²**
2. cuboid — **SA = 2(lw + lh + wh)**
3. cylinder — **SA = 2πr² + 2πrh**
4. triangular prism — **two triangles + three rectangles in the net**
5. net — **the solid unfolded flat**
