# Volume and Surface Area — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Level 1)** Volume is measured in ______.
a) cm  b) cm²  c) cm³
**Answer: c) cm³.**

**2. (Level 1)** 1 litre is ______ cm³.
a) 100  b) 1,000  c) 10,000
**Answer: b) 1,000** (Sierra Leone, JSS 1, L90).

**3. (Level 2)** A cuboid is 8 cm by 4 cm by 5 cm. Its volume is ______.
a) 160 cm³  b) 17 cm³  c) 184 cm³
**Answer: a) 160 cm³** — 8 × 4 × 5 (Libya, Grade 8).

**4. (Level 2)** A cube has side 3 cm. Its volume is ______.
a) 9 cm³  b) 27 cm³  c) 54 cm³
**Answer: b) 27 cm³** — V = l³ = 3 × 3 × 3 (Sierra Leone, JSS 2, L69).

**5. (Level 2)** A cylinder has radius 3 cm and height 10 cm (π ≈ 3.14). Its volume is ______.
a) 94.2 cm³  b) 282.6 cm³  c) 188.4 cm³
**Answer: b) 282.6 cm³** — V = πr²h = 3.14 × 9 × 10 (Sierra Leone, JSS 2, L72).

**6. (Level 3)** A cube has side 3 cm. Its surface area is ______.
a) 27 cm²  b) 54 cm²  c) 9 cm²
**Answer: b) 54 cm²** — SA = 6l² = 6 × 9 (Sierra Leone, JSS 2, L76).

**7. (Level 3)** A cuboid is 8 cm by 4 cm by 5 cm. Its surface area is ______.
a) 160 cm²  b) 184 cm²  c) 92 cm²
**Answer: b) 184 cm²** — SA = 2 × (32 + 40 + 20).
