# Volume and Surface Area — true or false

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Volume and Surface Area — Volume by Counting Cubes · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Volume and Surface Area — Cuboids, Cubes, Prisms and Cylinders · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Volume and Surface Area — Surface Area from Nets.

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Volume is counted in cubic units.
**True** — a 1 cm cube has volume 1 cm³.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** 1,000 cm³ = 1 litre.
**True** — volume and capacity are the same thing in different clothes.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A cuboid and a rectangular prism are different solids.
**False** — a cuboid IS a rectangular prism; l × w × h is the cross-section rule in disguise.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The volume of a prism is its cross-section times its length.
**True** (Sierra Leone, JSS 2, L68).

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** The surface area of a cube of side 3 cm is 27 cm².
**False** — 27 cm³ is its volume; its surface area is 6 × 9 = 54 cm².

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** A cylinder's surface area is its two circular ends plus one curved rectangle.
**True** — SA = 2πr² + 2πrh (Sierra Leone, JSS 2, L78).
