# Volume and Surface Area — short answer

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Level 1)** A box holds 4 × 3 cubes in one layer and has 2 layers. How many cubes is that?
**Answer:** 24 cubes — 12 × 2 (Egypt, Grade 5, Unit 11).

**2. (Level 1)** What is a net?
**Answer:** A solid unfolded flat — its faces laid out on paper (Vietnam, Toán 5, Bài 49).

**3. (Level 2)** A triangular prism has a triangular face of area 12 cm² and a length of 10 cm. Find its volume.
**Answer:** 120 cm³ — V = cross-section × length (Sierra Leone, JSS 2, L71).

**4. (Level 2)** A cuboid has volume 160 cm³ and two edges 8 cm and 4 cm. Find the third edge.
**Answer:** 5 cm — 160 ÷ 8 ÷ 4 = 5 (Libya, Grade 8).

**5. (Level 3)** A cylinder has radius 3 cm and height 10 cm (π ≈ 3.14). Find its surface area.
**Answer:** 244.92 cm² — 2πr² + 2πrh = 56.52 + 188.4 (Sierra Leone, JSS 2, L78).

**6. (Level 3)** A cube has volume 125 cm³. Find its side and its surface area.
**Answer:** Side 5 cm (5 × 5 × 5 = 125); surface area 150 cm² (6 × 25).
