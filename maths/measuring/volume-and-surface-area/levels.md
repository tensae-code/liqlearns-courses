# Volume and Surface Area — questions by level

Practice by level, tied to the skill number. The questions live in
the type files; this file is the by-level view. The levels are the
depths inside this one thing — volume and surface area — set by the
pattern across the countries' books: counting cubes first, then the
cuboid and cube formulas, then prisms and cylinders with surface
area from nets.

## Level 1 — volume by counting cubes
Level 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: find a volume by
counting unit cubes in layers, say what a cubic centimetre is, and
convert between cm³ and litres (1,000 cm³ = 1 L).

## Level 2 — cuboids, cubes, prisms and cylinders
Level 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: find volumes with
V = l × w × h (cuboid), V = l³ (cube), V = cross-section × length
(prism) and V = πr²h (cylinder); and find a missing edge from a
known volume.

## Level 3 — surface area from nets
Level 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: find surface
areas from nets — a cube (6l²), a cuboid, a triangular prism and a
cylinder (2πr² + 2πrh) — and tell a volume (cm³) from a surface
area (cm²) every time.
