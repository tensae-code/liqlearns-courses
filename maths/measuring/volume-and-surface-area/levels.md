# Volume and Surface Area — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB
LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure — README, "Units, Lessons and Sub
Lessons"). Practice is tied to the skill number, as before. The
questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson
view. The sub lessons are the depths inside this one thing —
volume and surface area — set by the pattern across the countries' books: counting cubes first, then the cuboid and cube formulas, then prisms and cylinders with surface area from nets.

Mapping from the old depth layers: old Level 1 = Sub lesson 1; old Level 2 = Sub lesson 2; old Level 3 = Sub lesson 3.

## Sub lesson 1 — Volume and Surface Area — Volume by Counting Cubes
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: find a volume by
counting unit cubes in layers, say what a cubic centimetre is, and
convert between cm³ and litres (1,000 cm³ = 1 L).

## Sub lesson 2 — Volume and Surface Area — Cuboids, Cubes, Prisms and Cylinders
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: find volumes with
V = l × w × h (cuboid), V = l³ (cube), V = cross-section × length
(prism) and V = πr²h (cylinder); and find a missing edge from a
known volume.

## Sub lesson 3 — Volume and Surface Area — Surface Area from Nets
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: find surface
areas from nets — a cube (6l²), a cuboid, a triangular prism and a
cylinder (2πr² + 2πrh) — and tell a volume (cm³) from a surface
area (cm²) every time.
