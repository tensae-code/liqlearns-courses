# Video script — Volume and Surface Area

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Take a box. Fill it with little cubes!
Count one layer — 4 by 3 — **12 cubes!**
Three layers — **12 × 3 = 36 cubes!** That is the volume!

Short cut: length times width times height!
**V = l × w × h!** A box 4 by 3 by 2 — **24 cubic centimetres!**
A cube, side 3 — **3 × 3 × 3 = 27!**

A prism? Its face stays the same all the way through.
**Volume = the face times the length!**
A cylinder? Its face is a circle! **V = πr²h!**

Now the skin — the **surface area**! Unwrap the box!
Six faces. Add them all!
A cube, side 3 — six faces of 9 — **54 square centimetres!**
A cylinder — two circles and a rolled-up rectangle!

Now you. Fill a box. Unwrap a box. Volume? Surface area?
That is volume and surface area.
