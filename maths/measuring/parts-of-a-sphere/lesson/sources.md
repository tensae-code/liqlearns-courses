**Acceptance count: taught in 5 countries (Tajikistan, Armenia, Libya, Vietnam, Syria).**

Vietnam joins with the sphere in Grade 9 Volume 2 (Bài 32). Stocked 2026-11-11 by the Vietnam re-verification sweep merge.

Libya carries the surface area and volume of a sphere in Grade 9, Chapter 5 (stocked by the 2026-11-08 re-verification sweep).

Syria names the sphere itself, with its two whole-solid formulas, in Grade 9 Geometry (stocked by the 2026-11-08 re-verification sweep merge).

This lesson exists because Tajikistan's Grade 11 Геометрия is the one read book that teaches the ball's parts as a topic: Topic 35 (printed pp. 123–125) defines the spherical segment and derives its volume V = πH²(R − H/3) by the integral formula, defines the spherical layer between two parallel planes with its volume formula, and defines the spherical sector — segment plus or minus the cone from the centre — with V = (2/3)πR²H and a worked rotation problem; Topic 36's notes (printed p. 129) state the three part surface areas — cap, zone and sector — with a worked total-area problem. The course's volume-and-surface-area lesson carries the whole sphere and ball (S = 4πR², V = 4πR³/3) from many countries but contains no spherical segment, layer or sector content from any country, so the parts have no genuine home there; under the everything-goes-in ruling (2026-11-06) the topic gets its own lesson, built at the size of its source — the same call the four-means and Euler's totient lessons were built on. The book states the layer volume and all three part areas without derivation, and the lesson says so; the answer key to its exercise 326 cannot stand with its statement and is taught corrected on the row above, as the filing directs.

Tajikistan enters through its Ministry-approved pupil's textbooks in Tajik (Cyrillic), all published in Dushanbe — Математика for Grade 5 (Маориф, 2022) and Grade 6 (5th edition, Маориф, 2021), Алгебра for Grades 7 (4th improved edition, 2023) and 8 (4th edition, 2023), and Геометрия — the continuation of stereometry — for Grade 11 (3rd edition, Лавҳ, 2020). From its books: from Grade 11: Spherical segment defined (part of a ball cut off by a plane; its height); Spherical layer (zone solid) defined (part of a ball between two parallel cutting planes; its bases and height); Spherical sector defined (segment plus or minus the cone from the centre); Surface areas of parts of a sphere, all stated without derivation: spherical cap (segment surface) S = 2πRH; zone surface S = 2πRH (excluding the two base discs); spherical sector surface S = πR(2H + r); worked problem: total area of the smaller segment cut off at distance 3 from the centre of a sphere of radius 5 → cap 20π + base disc 16π = 36π cm².

Armenia enters through its Geometry 11. From its books: the spherical surface and the sphere (գնդային մակերևույթ և գունդ); tangent plane to a spherical surface; surface area of the spherical surface.
