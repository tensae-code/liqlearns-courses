# Fill in the blank — Parts of a Sphere

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth layers do not map 1:1 onto the sub lessons here — the sub lessons are the sphere's parts, so items were assigned item by item, by what the item asks: old Layer 1 (the three definitions) divides across Sub lessons 1–3 by part; old Layer 2 divides between Sub lesson 1 (segment volume) and Sub lesson 2 (layer volume); old Layer 3 -> Sub lesson 3 — Parts of a Sphere — the Spherical Sector; old Layer 4 -> Sub lesson 4 — Parts of a Sphere — Areas of the Parts.

1. (Sub lesson 2) The part of a ball between two parallel cutting planes is called a spherical ___.
   **Answer: layer** (zone solid) — Tajikistan, *Геометрия* (Grade 11), printed p. 125.

2. (Sub lesson 1) The volume of a spherical segment is V = πH²(R − ___).
   **Answer: H/3** — Tajikistan, *Геометрия* (Grade 11), printed p. 124.

3. (Sub lesson 3) The volume of a spherical sector is V = ___ πR²H.
   **Answer: 2/3** — Tajikistan, *Геометрия* (Grade 11), printed p. 125.

4. (Sub lesson 4) The curved area of a spherical cap of height H on a ball of radius R is S = ___.
   **Answer: 2πRH** — the same form as the zone's area. Tajikistan, *Геометрия* (Grade 11), printed p. 129.

5. (Sub lesson 4) In the book's worked problem the smaller segment of a sphere of radius 5, cut at distance 3 from the centre, has cap height H = ___ cm and base radius r = ___ cm.
   **Answer: 2; 4** — H = 5 − 3 and r = √(5² − 3²). Tajikistan, *Геометрия* (Grade 11), printed p. 129.
