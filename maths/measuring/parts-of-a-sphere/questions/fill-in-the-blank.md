# Fill in the blank — Parts of a Sphere

1. (Layer 1) The part of a ball between two parallel cutting planes is called a spherical ___.
   **Answer: layer** (zone solid) — Tajikistan, *Геометрия* (Grade 11), printed p. 125.

2. (Layer 2) The volume of a spherical segment is V = πH²(R − ___).
   **Answer: H/3** — Tajikistan, *Геометрия* (Grade 11), printed p. 124.

3. (Layer 3) The volume of a spherical sector is V = ___ πR²H.
   **Answer: 2/3** — Tajikistan, *Геометрия* (Grade 11), printed p. 125.

4. (Layer 4) The curved area of a spherical cap of height H on a ball of radius R is S = ___.
   **Answer: 2πRH** — the same form as the zone's area. Tajikistan, *Геометрия* (Grade 11), printed p. 129.

5. (Layer 4) In the book's worked problem the smaller segment of a sphere of radius 5, cut at distance 3 from the centre, has cap height H = ___ cm and base radius r = ___ cm.
   **Answer: 2; 4** — H = 5 − 3 and r = √(5² − 3²). Tajikistan, *Геометрия* (Grade 11), printed p. 129.
