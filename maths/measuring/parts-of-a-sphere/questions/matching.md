1. (Sub lesson 1) Match each part to its description:
   (a) spherical segment
   (b) spherical layer
   (c) spherical sector

   (i) a segment combined with the cone from the centre to its base
   (ii) the part of a ball cut off by one plane
   (iii) the part of a ball between two parallel planes

   **Answer: (a)–(ii), (b)–(iii), (c)–(i)** — Tajikistan, *Геометрия* (Grade 11), printed pp. 124–125.

2. (Sub lesson 2) Match each formula to what it measures:
   (a) πH²(R − H/3)
   (b) (πH/6)(H² + 3R₁² + 3R₂²)
   (c) (2/3)πR²H
   (d) 2πRH
   (e) πR(2H + r)

   (i) volume of a spherical sector
   (ii) volume of a spherical segment
   (iii) curved area of a cap, and also of a zone
   (iv) volume of a spherical layer
   (v) surface area of a spherical sector

   **Answer: (a)–(ii), (b)–(iv), (c)–(i), (d)–(iii), (e)–(v)** — Tajikistan, *Геометрия* (Grade 11), printed pp. 124–125, 129.
