1. (Sub lesson 4) Put the steps of the book's worked total-area problem (smaller segment of a sphere of radius 5, plane at distance 3 from the centre) in order:
   (a) add the two areas: 20π + 16π = 36π cm²
   (b) find the cap height H = 5 − 3 = 2 cm
   (c) compute the base disc πr² = 16π cm²
   (d) find the cut circle's radius r = √(5² − 3²) = 4 cm
   (e) compute the cap's curved area 2πRH = 20π cm²

   **Answer: (b), (d), (e), (c), (a)** — Tajikistan, *Геометрия* (Grade 11), printed p. 129.

2. (Sub lesson 2) A ball is cut by two parallel planes on the same side of the centre. Put the volume computations in the order the book's layer argument uses them:
   (a) take the difference of the two segment volumes
   (b) compute the volume of the larger segment, down to the lower plane
   (c) compute the volume of the smaller segment, above the upper plane
   (d) read off the layer's volume

   **Answer: (b), (c), (a), (d)** — the layer is a difference of segments; its closed formula is then stated without derivation. Tajikistan, *Геометрия* (Grade 11), printed p. 125.
