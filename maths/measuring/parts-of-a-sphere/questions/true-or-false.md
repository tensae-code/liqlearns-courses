# True or false — Parts of a Sphere

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth layers do not map 1:1 onto the sub lessons here — the sub lessons are the sphere's parts, so items were assigned item by item, by what the item asks: old Layer 1 (the three definitions) divides across Sub lessons 1–3 by part; old Layer 2 divides between Sub lesson 1 (segment volume) and Sub lesson 2 (layer volume); old Layer 3 -> Sub lesson 3 — Parts of a Sphere — the Spherical Sector; old Layer 4 -> Sub lesson 4 — Parts of a Sphere — Areas of the Parts.

1. (Sub lesson 2) A spherical layer is the part of a ball between two parallel cutting planes, and its two flat faces are circles.
   **Answer: True** — the faces are its bases, of radii R₁ and R₂. Tajikistan, *Геометрия* (Grade 11), printed p. 125.

2. (Sub lesson 2) The book derives the spherical layer's volume formula from the segment formula; it does not merely state it.
   **Answer: False** — the book notes the volume is a difference of two segment volumes but states the closed formula V = (πH/6)(H² + 3R₁² + 3R₂²) without derivation ("it can be shown"). Tajikistan, *Геометрия* (Grade 11), printed p. 125.

3. (Sub lesson 3) A spherical sector is a segment combined with the cone from the ball's centre to the segment's base — added for a segment smaller than a hemisphere, subtracted for a larger one.
   **Answer: True** — Tajikistan, *Геометрия* (Grade 11), printed p. 125.

4. (Sub lesson 4) The zone surface area S = 2πRH includes the areas of the layer's two flat base discs.
   **Answer: False** — the formula is for the curved surface only; the book states it excluding the two base discs. Tajikistan, *Геометрия* (Grade 11), printed p. 129.

5. (Sub lesson 1) The volume of a spherical segment is derived in the book by the integral formula for solids of revolution.
   **Answer: True** — integrating the discs from R − H to R gives V = πH²(R − H/3). Tajikistan, *Геометрия* (Grade 11), printed p. 124.
