# True or false — Parts of a Sphere

1. (Layer 1) A spherical layer is the part of a ball between two parallel cutting planes, and its two flat faces are circles.
   **Answer: True** — the faces are its bases, of radii R₁ and R₂. Tajikistan, *Геометрия* (Grade 11), printed p. 125.

2. (Layer 2) The book derives the spherical layer's volume formula from the segment formula; it does not merely state it.
   **Answer: False** — the book notes the volume is a difference of two segment volumes but states the closed formula V = (πH/6)(H² + 3R₁² + 3R₂²) without derivation ("it can be shown"). Tajikistan, *Геометрия* (Grade 11), printed p. 125.

3. (Layer 3) A spherical sector is a segment combined with the cone from the ball's centre to the segment's base — added for a segment smaller than a hemisphere, subtracted for a larger one.
   **Answer: True** — Tajikistan, *Геометрия* (Grade 11), printed p. 125.

4. (Layer 4) The zone surface area S = 2πRH includes the areas of the layer's two flat base discs.
   **Answer: False** — the formula is for the curved surface only; the book states it excluding the two base discs. Tajikistan, *Геометрия* (Grade 11), printed p. 129.

5. (Layer 2) The volume of a spherical segment is derived in the book by the integral formula for solids of revolution.
   **Answer: True** — integrating the discs from R − H to R gives V = πH²(R − H/3). Tajikistan, *Геометрия* (Grade 11), printed p. 124.
