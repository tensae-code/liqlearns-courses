# Multiple choice — Parts of a Sphere

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth layers do not map 1:1 onto the sub lessons here — the sub lessons are the sphere's parts, so items were assigned item by item, by what the item asks: old Layer 1 (the three definitions) divides across Sub lessons 1–3 by part; old Layer 2 divides between Sub lesson 1 (segment volume) and Sub lesson 2 (layer volume); old Layer 3 -> Sub lesson 3 — Parts of a Sphere — the Spherical Sector; old Layer 4 -> Sub lesson 4 — Parts of a Sphere — Areas of the Parts.

1. (Sub lesson 1) A spherical segment is:
   (a) half of a ball, exactly
   (b) the part of a ball cut off by one plane
   (c) the part of a ball between two parallel planes
   (d) a segment plus the cone from the centre

   **Answer: (b)** — any single plane cut, large or small; the part between two planes is the layer, and (d) is the sector. Tajikistan, *Геометрия* (Grade 11), printed pp. 124–125.

2. (Sub lesson 1) The volume of a spherical segment of height H in a ball of radius R is:
   (a) πH²(R − H/3)
   (b) (2/3)πR²H
   (c) (πH/6)(H² + 3R₁² + 3R₂²)
   (d) 2πRH

   **Answer: (a)** — derived in the book by the integral formula; (b) is the sector's volume, (c) the layer's, (d) a surface area. Tajikistan, *Геометрия* (Grade 11), printed p. 124.

3. (Sub lesson 3) Rotating a 120° circular sector of radius R about its bisector diameter generates a spherical sector. Its volume is:
   (a) πR³
   (b) (4/3)πR³
   (c) πR³/3
   (d) 2πR³/3

   **Answer: (c)** — the cap height is H = R − R cos 60° = R/2, so V = (2/3)πR² · (R/2) = πR³/3. Tajikistan, *Геометрия* (Grade 11), printed p. 125.

4. (Sub lesson 4) The smaller segment cut from a sphere of radius 5 cm by a plane 3 cm from the centre has total area:
   (a) 20π cm²
   (b) 16π cm²
   (c) 25π cm²
   (d) 36π cm²

   **Answer: (d)** — cap 2πRH = 2π · 5 · 2 = 20π plus base disc π · 4² = 16π gives 36π cm². Tajikistan, *Геометрия* (Grade 11), printed p. 129.

5. (Sub lesson 4) The curved surface area of a spherical zone (layer surface) of height H in a ball of radius R is:
   (a) πR(2H + r)
   (b) 2πRH — it depends only on R and H, wherever the layer sits
   (c) 4πR²
   (d) (πH/6)(H² + 3R₁² + 3R₂²)

   **Answer: (b)** — stated in the book without derivation, like the cap's identical formula; (a) is the sector's surface, (c) the whole sphere, (d) the layer's volume. Tajikistan, *Геометрия* (Grade 11), printed p. 129.
