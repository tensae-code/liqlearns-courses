LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure — README, "Units, Lessons and Sub

Mapping from the old depth layers: the sub lessons are the sphere's parts, so the old layers regroup — old Layer 1 (the three parts defined) divides across Sub lessons 1–3, one part each; old Layer 2 (segment and layer volumes) divides between Sub lesson 1 (the segment's volume) and Sub lesson 2 (the layer's volume); old Layer 3 (sector volume) is Sub lesson 3's depth; old Layer 4 (areas of the parts) is Sub lesson 4.

## Sub lesson 2 — Parts of a Sphere — the Spherical Layer

## Sub lesson 4 — Parts of a Sphere — Areas of the Parts
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: use cap S = 2πRH, zone S = 2πRH and sector S = πR(2H + r), and assemble a part's total area from its curved surface and its base disc (old Layer 4). Pass mark: 4 of 5.

## What "maxed" means here

The learner can cut a ball in words into its segment, layer and sector, state and use all three volume formulas and all three surface-area formulas, and reproduce the book's two worked results — the rotated 120° sector's volume πR³/3 and the smaller segment's total area 36π cm² — knowing which formulas the book derives (the segment's volume, by the integral) and which it states without derivation (the layer's volume and all the part areas): the parts-of-a-sphere teaching of Tajikistan's Grade 11 Геометрия, Topic 35 (printed pp. 123–125) and Topic 36's notes (printed p. 129).
