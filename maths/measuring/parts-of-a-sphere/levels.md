# Levels — Parts of a Sphere

One lesson, one level (Level 160). The depth layers inside it are ranks.

## Depth layers

- **Layer 1 — The three parts.** Define the spherical segment, the spherical layer (zone solid) and the spherical sector, and give each one's height. Pass mark: 4 of 5.
- **Layer 2 — Segment and layer volumes.** Use V = πH²(R − H/3) for a segment and V = (πH/6)(H² + 3R₁² + 3R₂²) for a layer, and say why the layer's volume is a difference of two segment volumes. Pass mark: 4 of 5.
- **Layer 3 — Sector volume.** Use V = (2/3)πR²H, including finding H from a rotation problem the way the book's 120° example does. Pass mark: 4 of 5.
- **Layer 4 — Areas of the parts.** Use cap S = 2πRH, zone S = 2πRH and sector S = πR(2H + r), and assemble a part's total area from its curved surface and its base disc. Pass mark: 4 of 5.

## What "maxed" means here

The learner can cut a ball in words into its segment, layer and sector, state and use all three volume formulas and all three surface-area formulas, and reproduce the book's two worked results — the rotated 120° sector's volume πR³/3 and the smaller segment's total area 36π cm² — knowing which formulas the book derives (the segment's volume, by the integral) and which it states without derivation (the layer's volume and all the part areas): the parts-of-a-sphere teaching of Tajikistan's Grade 11 Геометрия, Topic 35 (printed pp. 123–125) and Topic 36's notes (printed p. 129).
