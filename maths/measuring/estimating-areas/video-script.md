# Video script — Estimating Areas

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A leaf has no straight sides. No formula fits it!
So we **estimate**!

Put it on squared paper. **Count the squares!**
Whole squares — count one. Part squares — join two parts into one!
That is estimate number one!

Now cut the shape into **equal strips**.
Measure the middle line of every strip — the **mid-ordinate**!
Add them. Times the strip width. Estimate number two!

Or join the tops — little **trapeziums**!
First plus last, plus **twice** the middles, times half the width.
Estimate number three!

Three estimates, close together — that is a good answer!
Now you. Find a leaf. Estimate it!
That is estimating areas.
