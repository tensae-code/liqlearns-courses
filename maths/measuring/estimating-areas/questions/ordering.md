# Estimating Areas — ordering

The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Estimating Areas — Counting Squares · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Estimating Areas — the Mid-Ordinate Rule · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Estimating Areas — the Trapezium Rule.

Put each list in the right order.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Order the steps for counting squares: (a) count the whole squares  (b) lay the shape on grid paper  (c) join the part squares into wholes  (d) multiply the total by the area of one grid square.
**Answer:** (b), (a), (c), (d) (South Sudan, S4, pp.21–24).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Order the steps for the mid-ordinate rule: (a) add the mid-ordinates  (b) split the width into equal strips  (c) multiply by the strip width  (d) measure the ordinate at the middle of each strip.
**Answer:** (b), (d), (a), (c) (South Sudan, S4, pp.25–26).

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Order the steps for the trapezium rule: (a) double the sum of the middle ordinates  (b) measure the ordinate at every strip edge  (c) add the first and last ordinates  (d) multiply the bracket total by half the strip width.
**Answer:** (b), (c), (a), (d) — measure, then ends once, middles doubled, then the half-width factor (South Sudan, S4, pp.26–29).
