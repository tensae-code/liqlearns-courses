# Estimating Areas — true or false

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Estimating Areas — Counting Squares · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Estimating Areas — the Mid-Ordinate Rule · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Estimating Areas — the Trapezium Rule.

Say true or false, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The trapezium rule gives the exact area of a shape with a curved edge.
**Answer:** False — it gives an estimate; the straight strip tops only follow the curve roughly (South Sudan, S4, pp.26–29).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In the mid-ordinate rule the ordinates are measured at the edges of the strips.
**Answer:** False — they are measured at the middle of each strip; edge ordinates belong to the trapezium rule (South Sudan, S4, pp.25–26).

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In the trapezium rule, the first and last ordinates are doubled like the middle ones.
**Answer:** False — the first and last are counted once; only the middle ordinates are doubled (South Sudan, S4, pp.26–29).

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Using more, narrower strips usually gives a better estimate.
**Answer:** True — narrower straight pieces hug a curved edge more closely (South Sudan, S4, pp.20–29).
