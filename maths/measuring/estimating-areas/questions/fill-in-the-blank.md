# Estimating Areas — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the missing number or words.

**1. (Level 1)** Counting squares: whole squares count ______, and part squares are ______ up to make whole ones.
**Answer:** one each; joined (paired) — South Sudan, S4, pp.21–24.

**2. (Level 2)** In the mid-ordinate rule, each ordinate is measured at the ______ of its strip.
**Answer:** middle (South Sudan, S4, pp.25–26).

**3. (Level 2)** Both rules need the strips to have ______ widths.
**Answer:** equal (South Sudan, S4, pp.25–29).

**4. (Level 3)** Trapezium rule: area ≈ (strip width ÷ 2) × [first + ______ + ______ × (middle ordinates)].
**Answer:** last; 2 — the ends count once, the middles twice (South Sudan, S4, pp.26–29).
