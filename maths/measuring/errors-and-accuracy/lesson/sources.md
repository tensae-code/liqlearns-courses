**Acceptance count: taught in 3 countries (Kazakhstan, Sierra Leone, Malaysia).** Kazakhstan carries absolute and relative error in two independent Grade 7 algebra books — Shynybekov's as an exercise set inside approximate numbers (§1.3, p.42), and Yelemessov's as taught approximation errors (Ch. 1, p.66, with approximation itself at p.62). Sierra Leone carries the senior-school half of the topic: percentage error when rounded values are used (SSS 2, L7, p.19) and degree of accuracy — choosing an appropriate degree of accuracy for given data (SSS 2, L8, p.22). No other read country's filing teaches errors and accuracy as its own topic; plain estimation and rounding live in the rounding-and-estimating lesson, not here.

Malaysia carries degree of accuracy in Form 3 as the doorway into significant figures, and meets approximation again at the top end, where a small change in a quantity is estimated from its derivative.
