# Video script — Errors and Accuracy

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

You measure a book. You say: twenty-four centimetres.
Is it exactly twenty-four? No!
Every measure is a little bit off. That little bit is the **error**.

How big is the gap? That is the **absolute error**.
True is fifty. You measured forty-nine. The gap is one. Easy!

But is one big, or small?
On a pencil, one centimetre is big.
On a road, one centimetre is nothing!
So we compare the gap with the true size.
That is the **relative error**. Times one hundred — the **percentage error**.

And do not measure a road to the millimetre.
Do not measure a fingernail to the metre.
Pick the **right degree of accuracy** for the job.
That is errors and accuracy.
