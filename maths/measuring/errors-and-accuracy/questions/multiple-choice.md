# Errors and Accuracy — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Level 1)** The true length is 50 cm. You measure 48 cm. What is the absolute error?
a) 48 cm  b) 2 cm  c) −2 cm
**Answer:** b) 2 cm — the absolute error is the size of the gap; it is never negative.

**2. (Level 1)** The true mass is 200 g. A scale reads 205 g. What is the absolute error?
a) 5 g  b) 205 g  c) 195 g
**Answer:** a) 5 g — 205 − 200 = 5.

**3. (Level 1)** A length is given as 30 cm, to the nearest cm. What is the biggest possible error?
a) 1 cm  b) 0.5 cm  c) 5 cm
**Answer:** b) 0.5 cm — half of the last place kept.

**4. (Level 2)** A true value is 80 and the absolute error is 4. What is the relative error?
a) 0.05  b) 20  c) 0.5
**Answer:** a) 0.05 — relative error = absolute error ÷ true value = 4 ÷ 80.

**5. (Level 2)** A true value is 80 and the absolute error is 4. What is the percentage error?
a) 0.05%  b) 5%  c) 20%
**Answer:** b) 5% — 0.05 × 100 = 5%.

**6. (Level 2)** Two scales are each 1 kg out. Scale A weighs a true 10 kg; scale B weighs a true 200 kg. Which has the bigger percentage error?
a) Scale A  b) Scale B  c) They are equal
**Answer:** a) Scale A — the same gap is 10% of 10 kg but only 0.5% of 200 kg.

**7. (Level 3)** A road between two towns is 47.382 km long. Which is the most sensible way to report it?
a) 47.382 km  b) 47.4 km  c) 50 km
**Answer:** b) 47.4 km — a road is sensibly given to a tenth of a kilometre; millimetre precision pretends to knowledge nobody has.
