This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB
LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure — README, "Units, Lessons and Sub
Lessons"). Practice is tied to the skill number, as before. The

## Sub lesson 1 — Temperature and Traditional Measures — Temperature and the Thermometer
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: read a thermometer in degrees Celsius, place the fixed points (0 °C ice, 100 °C boiling water, body temperature about 37 °C), and measure a length in body units.

## Sub lesson 2 — Temperature and Traditional Measures — Converting Celsius and Fahrenheit
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: convert both ways with the correct formulas, including the 32 offset, and check a conversion by converting back.

## Sub lesson 3 — Temperature and Traditional Measures — the Traditional Measures
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: work in Nepal's land units, use the values Mauritania's book gives for the cubit, raṭl and ṣāʿ, and name the traditional units of Nicaragua, Kazakhstan and Laos.
