# Temperature and Traditional Measures — fill in the blank

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. Old Level 1 items about measuring length in body units are Sub lesson 3 — Temperature and Traditional Measures — the Traditional Measures, because that is where the lesson text teaches body units; they were split item by item, by what the item asks.

Fill in the missing word or number.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Ice melts at ______ °F.
**Answer:** 32 — the Fahrenheit scale starts its water story at 32, not at 0.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A ______ is the tool that measures temperature.
**Answer:** thermometer — South Africa's workbooks teach reading it in weather contexts.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** °F = °C × 9/5 + ______.
**Answer:** 32 — without the 32 the formula is wrong; it is the offset between the two scales' zeros.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** 100 °C = ______ °F.
**Answer:** 212 — 100 × 9/5 + 32 = 212.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** In Nepal, 1 ropani = ______ aana.
**Answer:** 16 — with paisa and daam below the aana.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** In Laos's Grade 5 book, the traditional mass units taught beside kg, g and t are the baht, the salung, the ______ and the carat.
**Answer:** hun — baht, salung, hun, and the carat.
