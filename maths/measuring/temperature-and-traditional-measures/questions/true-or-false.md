# Temperature and Traditional Measures — true or false

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Level 1)** 0 °C and 0 °F are the same temperature.
**Answer:** False — 0 °C is 32 °F; the scales have different zeros.

**2. (Level 1)** A hand-span (jeme) is the same length for every person.
**Answer:** False — spans differ from person to person; that is why standard units replaced body units.

**3. (Level 2)** To convert Celsius to Fahrenheit you multiply by 9/5 only.
**Answer:** False — you must also add 32; without it, 50 °C comes out as 90 °F instead of the true 122 °F.

**4. (Level 2)** Converting 32 °F to Celsius gives 0 °C.
**Answer:** True — (32 − 32) × 5/9 = 0.

**5. (Level 3)** In Nepal's books, the katha and the ropani are units of the same land system.
**Answer:** False — ropani/aana/paisa/daam form one system; katha/dhur form the other.

**6. (Level 3)** Mauritania's ṣāʿ is used as the measure of zakāt al-fiṭr — one ṣāʿ of food.
**Answer:** True — the 6AS-LO chapter works the traditional measures into zakat exercises.
