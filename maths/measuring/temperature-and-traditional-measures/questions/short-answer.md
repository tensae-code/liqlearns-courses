# Temperature and Traditional Measures — short answer

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Temperature and Traditional Measures — Temperature and the Thermometer · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Temperature and Traditional Measures — Converting Celsius and Fahrenheit · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Temperature and Traditional Measures — the Traditional Measures. Old Level 1 items about measuring length in body units are Sub lesson 3 — Temperature and Traditional Measures — the Traditional Measures, because that is where the lesson text teaches body units; they were split item by item, by what the item asks.

Give a short answer, with the working where it helps.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A thermometer in a drink reads 5 °C. Is the drink hot or cold? Say why in one sentence.
**Answer:** Cold — 5 °C is just above the melting point of ice, 0 °C.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Measure a book in jemes (hand-spans). Write your count. Your partner's count differs. Why?
**Answer:** Any honest count is accepted; the counts differ because hand-spans differ from person to person — body units are not the same for everyone.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Convert 35 °C to Fahrenheit.
**Answer:** 95 °F — 35 × 9/5 = 63, then + 32 = 95.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Convert 104 °F to Celsius.
**Answer:** 40 °C — (104 − 32) × 5/9 = 72 × 5/9 = 40.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** A learner converts 10 °C and gets 50 °F by working 10 × 5 = 50. Find the two mistakes and the correct answer.
**Answer:** The mistakes are multiplying by 5 instead of 9/5, and leaving out the + 32. Correct: 10 × 9/5 + 32 = 18 + 32 = 50 °F — the learner's answer is right only by accident; the working is wrong and fails for every other temperature (try 0 °C: the method gives 0 °F, the truth is 32 °F).

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** A field is 4 ropani. How many aana is it, and what is its area in square metres? (1 aana = 31.79 m².)
**Answer:** 64 aana; 64 × 31.79 = 2,034.56 m².

**7. (Sub lesson 3)** Using Mauritania's values, find: a length of 2 fathoms in centimetres; a mass of 4 raṭl in grams.
**Answer:** 2 × 198 cm = 396 cm; 4 × 312.5 g = 1,250 g.

**8. (Sub lesson 3)** Name the four body units Nicaragua's Grade 1 book teaches, and the two land units its Grade 4 book works in.
**Answer:** Jeme, brazada, paso, pulgada; vara cuadrada and manzana.
