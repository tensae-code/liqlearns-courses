# Temperature and Traditional Measures — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Level 1)** At what Celsius temperature does ice melt?
a) 0 °C  b) 32 °C  c) 100 °C
**Answer:** a) 0 °C — the Celsius scale is fixed by water: ice at 0, steam at 100.

**2. (Level 1)** Water boils at 100 °C. What is that in Fahrenheit?
a) 100 °F  b) 180 °F  c) 212 °F
**Answer:** c) 212 °F — 100 × 9/5 + 32 = 212.

**3. (Level 1)** Which is normal body temperature?
a) 25 °C  b) About 37 °C  c) 50 °C
**Answer:** b) About 37 °C — Nepal's book gives 36.5 °C / 98 °F; South Sudan's gives 37 °C / 98.6 °F.

**4. (Level 2)** Convert 20 °C to Fahrenheit.
a) 36 °F  b) 52 °F  c) 68 °F
**Answer:** c) 68 °F — 20 × 9/5 = 36, then + 32 = 68.

**5. (Level 2)** Convert 50 °C to Fahrenheit.
a) 90 °F  b) 122 °F  c) 82 °F
**Answer:** b) 122 °F — 50 × 9/5 + 32 = 122, the worked conversion in South Sudan's Pupil's Book 7; 90 °F is the wrong answer the formula without the 32 gives.

**6. (Level 2)** Convert 77 °F to Celsius.
a) 25 °C  b) 45 °C  c) 9 °C
**Answer:** a) 25 °C — (77 − 32) × 5/9 = 45 × 5/9 = 25.

**7. (Level 3)** How many aana are in 1 ropani (Nepal)?
a) 10  b) 16  c) 100
**Answer:** b) 16 — Nepal's Grade 9 book: 1 ropani = 16 aana.

**8. (Level 3)** In Mauritania's book, the cubit is about how long?
a) About 2 cm  b) About 49.5 cm  c) About 198 cm
**Answer:** b) About 49.5 cm — 198 cm is the fathom, the cubit is the forearm measure of about 49.5 cm.
