**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Match each event to its Celsius temperature: (a) ice melting (b) water boiling (c) normal body — 0 °C, 100 °C, about 37 °C
**Answer:** a) 0 °C, b) 100 °C, c) about 37 °C — the three fixed points of the lesson.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Match each Celsius value to its Fahrenheit partner: (a) 0 °C (b) 50 °C (c) 100 °C — 32 °F, 122 °F, 212 °F
**Answer:** a) 32 °F, b) 122 °F, c) 212 °F — each by × 9/5 + 32.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Match each country to its traditional measures: (a) Nicaragua (b) Nepal (c) Mauritania (d) Laos — ropani and aana; jeme and brazada; cubit and ṣāʿ; baht and salung
**Answer:** a) jeme and brazada, b) ropani and aana, c) cubit and ṣāʿ, d) baht and salung — each country's own measures, as its books teach them.
