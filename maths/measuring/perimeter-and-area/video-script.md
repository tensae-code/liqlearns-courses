# Video script — Perimeter and Area

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Walk round the garden. That walk is the **perimeter** — the
distance round a shape. Add the sides!
A square, side 4 — **4 + 4 + 4 + 4 = 16!** Or short: **4 × 4!**
A rectangle, 5 by 3 — **5 + 3 + 5 + 3 = 16!** Or short:
**2 × 5 + 2 × 3!**

Now the inside — the **area**! How many squares cover it?
A rectangle, 5 by 3 — **5 × 3 = 15 square centimetres!**
A square, side 4 — **4 × 4 = 16!**

A triangle? Cut a rectangle in half — there it is!
**Half of base times height!** Base 6, height 4 —
**half of 24 is 12!**

A parallelogram? Slide it straight — same base, same height,
same area! **Base times height!**
A trapezium? Two parallel sides — add them, halve them, times
the height!

Now you. Walk a room. Cover a table. Perimeter? Area?
That is perimeter and area.
