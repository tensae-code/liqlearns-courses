# Perimeter and Area — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Level 1)** A rectangle is 5 cm by 3 cm. Its perimeter is ______.
a) 8 cm  b) 15 cm  c) 16 cm
**Answer: c) 16 cm** — 5 + 3 + 5 + 3 (Sierra Leone, JSS 1, L76).

**2. (Level 1)** A square has side 6 cm. Its perimeter is ______.
a) 12 cm  b) 24 cm  c) 36 cm
**Answer: b) 24 cm** — P = 4l = 4 × 6.

**3. (Level 2)** A rectangle is 5 cm by 3 cm. Its area is ______.
a) 8 cm²  b) 15 cm²  c) 16 cm²
**Answer: b) 15 cm²** — A = l × w = 5 × 3.

**4. (Level 2)** A triangle has base 6 cm and height 4 cm. Its area is ______.
a) 24 cm²  b) 12 cm²  c) 10 cm²
**Answer: b) 12 cm²** — A = ½ × 6 × 4 (Sierra Leone, JSS 1, L78).

**5. (Level 3)** A parallelogram has base 8 cm and height 5 cm. Its area is ______.
a) 40 cm²  b) 26 cm²  c) 20 cm²
**Answer: a) 40 cm²** — A = base × height (Sierra Leone, JSS 2, L62).

**6. (Level 3)** A trapezium has parallel sides 6 cm and 10 cm and height 4 cm. Its area is ______.
a) 64 cm²  b) 32 cm²  c) 16 cm²
**Answer: b) 32 cm²** — A = ½ × (6 + 10) × 4 (Sierra Leone, JSS 2, L63).

**7. (Level 3)** A rectangle has area 24 cm² and length 6 cm. Its width is ______.
a) 4 cm  b) 6 cm  c) 18 cm
**Answer: a) 4 cm** — 24 ÷ 6 = 4 (Sierra Leone, JSS 1, L80).
