# Perimeter and Area — short answer

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Level 1)** A triangle has sides 3 cm, 4 cm and 5 cm. Find its perimeter.
**Answer:** 12 cm — 3 + 4 + 5.

**2. (Level 1)** A fence runs round a square garden of side 9 m. How long is the fence?
**Answer:** 36 m — P = 4 × 9.

**3. (Level 2)** A square has side 7 cm. Find its area.
**Answer:** 49 cm² — A = 7 × 7.

**4. (Level 2)** A triangle has area 20 cm² and base 8 cm. Find its height.
**Answer:** 5 cm — height = (20 × 2) ÷ 8.

**5. (Level 3)** A parallelogram has base 12 cm and height 3 cm. Find its area.
**Answer:** 36 cm² — A = 12 × 3.

**6. (Level 3)** An L-shape is two rectangles: 6 cm by 4 cm, and 3 cm by 2 cm. Find its area.
**Answer:** 30 cm² — 24 + 6.
