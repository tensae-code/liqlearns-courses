# Perimeter and Area — true or false

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Level 1)** Perimeter is measured in cm².
**False** — perimeter is a length, measured in cm; cm² is area.

**2. (Level 1)** The perimeter of a rectangle 5 cm by 3 cm is 16 cm.
**True** — 5 + 3 + 5 + 3 = 16.

**3. (Level 2)** The height of a triangle is its slant side.
**False** — the height is perpendicular to the base, the straight drop (Sierra Leone, JSS 1, L78).

**4. (Level 2)** A triangle is half of its rectangle, so its area is half of base times height.
**True** — cut the rectangle along its diagonal (Greece, Grade 6, ch.63).

**5. (Level 3)** A parallelogram and a rectangle with the same base and height have the same area.
**True** — slanting does not change the area (Greece, Grade 6, ch.62).

**6. (Level 3)** Rectangles with the same area always have the same perimeter.
**False** — area 24 cm² can be 6 by 4 (perimeter 20 cm) or 24 by 1 (perimeter 50 cm) (Egypt, Grade 3, Chapter 5).
