# Perimeter and Area — ordering

Put each set in order, smallest first. (This question type exists
for this lesson because perimeters and areas ARE ordered by size.)

**1. (Level 1)** Order these perimeters, smallest first: square side 3 cm · square side 5 cm · rectangle 4 cm by 2 cm.
**Answer:** rectangle 4 by 2 (P = 12 cm), square side 3 (P = 12 cm), square side 5 (P = 20 cm) — the rectangle and the small square tie at 12 cm.

**2. (Level 2)** Order these areas, smallest first: rectangle 3 by 2 · square side 3 · rectangle 5 by 3.
**Answer:** 6 cm² (3 by 2), 9 cm² (square side 3), 15 cm² (5 by 3).

**3. (Level 2)** Order these triangle areas, smallest first: base 4 height 3 · base 6 height 4 · base 10 height 2.
**Answer:** 6 cm² (½ × 4 × 3), 10 cm² (½ × 10 × 2), 12 cm² (½ × 6 × 4).

**4. (Level 3)** Order these areas, smallest first: parallelogram base 5 height 4 · trapezium parallel sides 6 and 10, height 3 · triangle base 8 height 6.
**Answer:** 20 cm² (parallelogram), 24 cm² (trapezium: ½ × 16 × 3), 24 cm² (triangle: ½ × 8 × 6) — trapezium and triangle tie at 24 cm².

**5. (Level 3)** Rectangles with area 24 cm²: order their perimeters, smallest first: 6 by 4 · 8 by 3 · 12 by 2.
**Answer:** 20 cm (6 by 4), 22 cm (8 by 3), 28 cm (12 by 2).
