# Perimeter and Area — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with a word from the box.

**Box:** perimeter · area · 4l · l × w · ½bh · base × height

**1. (Level 1)** The distance round a shape is its ______.
**Answer: perimeter.**

**2. (Level 1)** The perimeter of a square of side l is ______.
**Answer: 4l.**

**3. (Level 2)** The flat space a shape covers is its ______.
**Answer: area.**

**4. (Level 2)** The area of a rectangle is ______.
**Answer: l × w.**

**5. (Level 2)** The area of a triangle is ______.
**Answer: ½bh.**

**6. (Level 3)** The area of a parallelogram is ______.
**Answer: base × height.**
