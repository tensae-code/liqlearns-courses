# Perimeter and Area — fill in the blank

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Perimeter and Area — Perimeter · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Perimeter and Area — Areas of Rectangles, Squares and Triangles · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Perimeter and Area — Parallelograms, Trapeziums and Composite Shapes.

Fill each blank with a word from the box.

**Box:** perimeter · area · 4l · l × w · ½bh · base × height

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The distance round a shape is its ______.
**Answer: perimeter.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The perimeter of a square of side l is ______.
**Answer: 4l.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The flat space a shape covers is its ______.
**Answer: area.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The area of a rectangle is ______.
**Answer: l × w.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The area of a triangle is ______.
**Answer: ½bh.**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** The area of a parallelogram is ______.
**Answer: base × height.**
