# Perimeter and Area — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB
LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure — README, "Units, Lessons and Sub
Lessons"). Practice is tied to the skill number, as before. The
questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson
view. The sub lessons are the depths inside this one thing —
perimeter and area — set by the pattern across the countries' books: perimeter first, then the rectangle and square areas, then the triangle, parallelogram and trapezium areas with composite shapes.

Mapping from the old depth layers: old Level 1 = Sub lesson 1; old Level 2 = Sub lesson 2; old Level 3 = Sub lesson 3.

## Sub lesson 1 — Perimeter and Area — Perimeter
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: find a perimeter
by adding the sides, and by the short cuts for a square (4l), a
rectangle (2l + 2w) and a triangle (a + b + c), in the right unit
(cm, never cm²).

## Sub lesson 2 — Perimeter and Area — Areas of Rectangles, Squares and Triangles
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: find areas with
A = l × w, A = l² and A = ½bh, with the height perpendicular to
the base, and find a missing side from a known area.

## Sub lesson 3 — Perimeter and Area — Parallelograms, Trapeziums and Composite Shapes
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: find areas with
A = base × height and A = ½(a + b)h; split a composite shape and
add its pieces; and say why the same area can have different
perimeters.
