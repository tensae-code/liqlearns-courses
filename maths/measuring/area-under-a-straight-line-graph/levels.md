# Levels — Area Under a Straight-Line Graph

One lesson, one level (Level 140). The depth layers inside it are ranks.

## Depth layers

- **Layer 1 — See the shape.** Given a straight-line graph and an interval, name the shape under the line and read the heights at the edges from the equation. Pass mark: 4 of 5.
- **Layer 2 — Compute it.** Triangles by ½ × base × height; trapeziums by ½ × (sum of parallel sides) × distance. Pass mark: 5 of 6.
- **Layer 3 — Pieces and meanings.** Split a region at an axis crossing, handle a part below the axis, and interpret an area in context (distance under a speed–time graph). Pass mark: 4 of 5.

## What "maxed" means here

The learner can be handed any straight-line graph on any interval and return its exact area under the curve from shape areas alone — and can say how this differs from estimating by counting squares and from the calculus method in integrals.
