# True or false — Area Under a Straight-Line Graph

1. (Layer 1) The area under y = 2x + 1 from x = 0 to x = 2 is a triangle. **False** — the line does not pass through the origin; the region is a trapezium. Malaysia, Mathematics Form 3, Exploring Mathematics, printed p. 251.
2. (Layer 2) The area under y = 3x from x = 0 to x = 4 is 24. **True** — ½ × 4 × 12 = 24. Malaysia, Mathematics Form 3, Exploring Mathematics, printed p. 251.
3. (Layer 3) Counting squares is the only way to find the area under a straight line. **False** — the shape areas give it exactly; counting is for curved boundaries. Malaysia, Mathematics Form 3, Exploring Mathematics, printed p. 251.
