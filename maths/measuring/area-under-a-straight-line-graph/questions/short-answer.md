# Short answer — Area Under a Straight-Line Graph

1. (Layer 2) Find the area under y = 4x from x = 0 to x = 2.
   **Answer: ½ × 2 × 8 = 8 square units.** Malaysia, Mathematics Form 3, Exploring Mathematics, printed p. 251.

2. (Layer 2) Find the area under y = 10 − x from x = 0 to x = 10, naming the shape.
   **Answer: a triangle, ½ × 10 × 10 = 50 square units.** Malaysia, Mathematics Form 3, Exploring Mathematics, printed p. 251.

3. (Layer 3) A speed–time graph is the straight line from (0, 2) to (6, 8). Find the distance travelled.
   **Answer: trapezium ½ × (2 + 8) × 6 = 30 m.** Malaysia, Mathematics Form 4, Ch. 7 §7.2, printed pp. 195–207.
