# Ordering — Area Under a Straight-Line Graph

1. (Layer 2) Put the steps for the area under y = 2x + 2 from x = 0 to x = 3 in order:
   (a) Compute ½ × (2 + 8) × 3 = 15 (b) Read the heights at the edges: 2 and 8 (c) Sketch and shade the region (d) Name the shape: trapezium
   **Answer: c, d, b, a.** Malaysia, Mathematics Form 3, Exploring Mathematics, printed p. 251.
