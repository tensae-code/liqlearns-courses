# Fill in the blank — Area Under a Straight-Line Graph

1. (Layer 1) Under a line through the origin, the shaded region is always a ___.
   **Answer: triangle.** Malaysia, Mathematics Form 3, Exploring Mathematics, printed p. 251.

2. (Layer 2) The area under y = x from x = 0 to x = 6 is ___ square units.
   **Answer: 18.** Malaysia, Mathematics Form 3, Exploring Mathematics, printed p. 251.

3. (Layer 3) For a trapezium region, the two parallel sides are the line's ___ at the two edges of the interval.
   **Answer: heights (values).** Malaysia, Mathematics Form 3, Exploring Mathematics, printed p. 251.
