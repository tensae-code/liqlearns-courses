1. (Sub lesson 2) Match each region to its area:
   (a) y = x, x from 0 to 4 (b) y = 2x + 2, x from 0 to 2 (c) y = 6 − 2x, x from 0 to 3 (d) y = 5, x from 0 to 3
   Areas: 8, 10, 9, 15.
   **Answer: (a) 8, (b) 10 — trapezium ½ × (2 + 6) × 2, (c) 9 — triangle ½ × 3 × 6, (d) 15 — rectangle 5 × 3.** Malaysia, Mathematics Form 3, Exploring Mathematics, printed p. 251.
