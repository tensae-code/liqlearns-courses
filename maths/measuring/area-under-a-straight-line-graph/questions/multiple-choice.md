# Multiple choice — Area Under a Straight-Line Graph

1. (Layer 1) The region under y = 5x from x = 0 to x = 2 is a:
   A. Rectangle B. Triangle C. Trapezium D. Circle
   **Answer: B** — Malaysia, Mathematics Form 3, Exploring Mathematics, printed p. 251.

2. (Layer 2) The area under y = 2x from x = 0 to x = 3 is:
   A. 6 B. 18 C. 9 D. 12
   **Answer: C** — ½ × 3 × 6 = 9. Malaysia, Mathematics Form 3, Exploring Mathematics, printed p. 251.

3. (Layer 2) The area under y = x + 2 from x = 0 to x = 4 is:
   A. 16 B. 20 C. 12 D. 24
   **Answer: B** — trapezium: ½ × (2 + 6) × 4 = 20. Malaysia, Mathematics Form 3, Exploring Mathematics, printed p. 251.

4. (Layer 3) The area under a speed–time graph gives:
   A. Acceleration B. Distance travelled C. Final speed D. Time taken
   **Answer: B** — Malaysia, Mathematics Form 4, Ch. 7 §7.2, printed pp. 195–207.
