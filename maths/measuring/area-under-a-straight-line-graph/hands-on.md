# Hands-on — Area Under a Straight-Line Graph

## Activity 1 — Shade and name (10 minutes)

On squared paper draw y = 2x (x from 0 to 3), y = x + 2 (x from 0 to 4), and y = 6 − x (x from 0 to 6). Shade each region under the line. The learner names each shape first — triangle, trapezium, triangle — before computing anything.

## Activity 2 — Prove it by counting (10 minutes)

For the first graph of Activity 1, count whole and half squares under the line and compare with the triangle formula's answer (9). They agree: the formula is the counting, done exactly. Say explicitly how this differs from estimating-areas, where the boundary is a curve and counting is all you have.

## Activity 3 — The journey (10 minutes)

Draw a speed–time graph: speed rises in a straight line from 0 to 12 m/s over 6 s, then stays level for 4 s. Find the distance by splitting the area into a triangle and a rectangle (½ × 6 × 12 + 4 × 12 = 36 + 48 = 84 m) — the same shapes, wearing a story.
