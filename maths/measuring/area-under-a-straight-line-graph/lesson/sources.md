On Malaysia in this lesson: Malaysia's Form 3 book is the first in the read set to compute exact areas under straight-line graphs as geometry. It is kept apart from two neighbours on purpose: estimating-areas (counting squares and the numerical rules for curved boundaries) and integrals (the calculus method, where Malaysia's own Form 5 Additional Mathematics treatment lives). The Form 4 speed–time source is context for the same areas, filed in its own lesson; it is named here as the second book because the lesson's "where you have met this" part rests on it.
