# Video script — Area Under a Straight-Line Graph

Super-simple voice. The tutor talks; the region under each line is shaded as it is named.

---

Draw a line. Shade under it, down to the axis. What shape is that shade?

If the line starts at zero, the shade is a triangle. Base along the bottom. Height up the side. Half base times height. Done.

If the line starts higher, the shade is a trapezium. Two heights — one at each edge. Add them. Halve. Times the width. Done.

No counting squares. No guessing. The exact area, from shapes you already know.

And here is a secret. A speed graph works the same way. The area under it is the distance travelled. One idea, two lessons.
