# Measuring — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB
LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure — README, "Units, Lessons and Sub
Lessons"). Practice is tied to the skill number, as before. The
questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson
view. The sub lessons are the depths inside this one thing —
measuring — set by the pattern across the countries' books: length first, then mass, then capacity, each with its own units and its own conversions.

Mapping from the old depth layers: old Level 1 = Sub lesson 1; old Level 2 = Sub lesson 2; old Level 3 = Sub lesson 3.

## Sub lesson 1 — Measuring Length
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: measure in mm,
cm, m and km; convert with 10 mm = 1 cm, 100 cm = 1 m and
1,000 m = 1 km; and choose the sensible length unit.

## Sub lesson 2 — Measuring Mass
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: measure in g,
kg and tonnes; convert with 1,000 g = 1 kg and 1,000 kg = 1
tonne; and choose the sensible mass unit.

## Sub lesson 3 — Measuring Capacity
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: measure in mL
and L; convert with 1,000 mL = 1 L; and choose the sensible
capacity unit — and convert mixed measures (4 kg 500 g) without
losing the parts.
