# Measuring — questions by level

Practice by level, tied to the skill number. The questions live in
the type files; this file is the by-level view. The levels are the
depths inside this one thing — measuring — set by the pattern
across the countries' books: length first, then mass, then
capacity, each with its own units and its own conversions.

## Level 1 — length
Level 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: measure in mm,
cm, m and km; convert with 10 mm = 1 cm, 100 cm = 1 m and
1,000 m = 1 km; and choose the sensible length unit.

## Level 2 — mass
Level 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: measure in g,
kg and tonnes; convert with 1,000 g = 1 kg and 1,000 kg = 1
tonne; and choose the sensible mass unit.

## Level 3 — capacity
Level 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: measure in mL
and L; convert with 1,000 mL = 1 L; and choose the sensible
capacity unit — and convert mixed measures (4 kg 500 g) without
losing the parts.
