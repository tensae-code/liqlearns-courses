# Measuring — short answer

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Level 1)** Convert 3 m to centimetres.
**Answer:** 300 cm — 3 × 100.

**2. (Level 1)** Convert 2,500 mm to centimetres and to metres.
**Answer:** 250 cm; 2.5 m.

**3. (Level 2)** Convert 4 kg 500 g to grams.
**Answer:** 4,500 g (South Africa, Grade 4, ws 114).

**4. (Level 2)** A lorry carries 2 tonnes. How many kilograms is that?
**Answer:** 2,000 kg — 1,000 kg = 1 tonne.

**5. (Level 3)** Convert 750 mL to litres.
**Answer:** 0.75 L.

**6. (Level 3)** A bottle holds 1.5 L. How many 250 mL cups can you fill from it?
**Answer:** 6 cups — 1,500 mL ÷ 250 mL = 6.
