# Measuring — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Level 1)** 1 metre is ______ centimetres.
a) 10  b) 100  c) 1,000
**Answer: b) 100** — 100 cm = 1 m (Sierra Leone, JSS 1, L72).

**2. (Level 1)** A pencil is about 15 ______.
a) mm  b) cm  c) km
**Answer: b) cm.**

**3. (Level 2)** 1 kilogram is ______ grams.
a) 100  b) 1,000  c) 10,000
**Answer: b) 1,000** — 1,000 g = 1 kg (Sierra Leone, JSS 1, L73).

**4. (Level 2)** A bag of rice weighs 5 kg. In grams that is ______.
a) 500 g  b) 5,000 g  c) 50,000 g
**Answer: b) 5,000 g.**

**5. (Level 3)** 1 litre is ______ millilitres.
a) 100  b) 1,000  c) 10,000
**Answer: b) 1,000** — 1,000 mL = 1 L (Sierra Leone, JSS 1, L74).

**6. (Level 3)** A jug holds 2 L. In millilitres that is ______.
a) 200 mL  b) 2,000 mL  c) 20,000 mL
**Answer: b) 2,000 mL.**

**7. (Level 1)** A road between towns is measured in ______.
a) centimetres  b) metres  c) kilometres
**Answer: c) kilometres.**
