# Measuring — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Measuring Length · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Measuring Mass · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Measuring Capacity.

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** 1 metre is ______ centimetres.
a) 10  b) 100  c) 1,000
**Answer: b) 100** — 100 cm = 1 m (Sierra Leone, JSS 1, L72).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A pencil is about 15 ______.
a) mm  b) cm  c) km
**Answer: b) cm.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** 1 kilogram is ______ grams.
a) 100  b) 1,000  c) 10,000
**Answer: b) 1,000** — 1,000 g = 1 kg (Sierra Leone, JSS 1, L73).

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A bag of rice weighs 5 kg. In grams that is ______.
a) 500 g  b) 5,000 g  c) 50,000 g
**Answer: b) 5,000 g.**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** 1 litre is ______ millilitres.
a) 100  b) 1,000  c) 10,000
**Answer: b) 1,000** — 1,000 mL = 1 L (Sierra Leone, JSS 1, L74).

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** A jug holds 2 L. In millilitres that is ______.
a) 200 mL  b) 2,000 mL  c) 20,000 mL
**Answer: b) 2,000 mL.**

**7. (Sub lesson 1)** A road between towns is measured in ______.
a) centimetres  b) metres  c) kilometres
**Answer: c) kilometres.**
