# Measuring — matching

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Measuring Length · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Measuring Mass · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Measuring Capacity.

Match each unit to what it measures, or each conversion to its value.

**Set 1 (Sub lesson 1) — length**
1. mm — **millimetre, about a grain of rice**
2. cm — **centimetre, about a finger's width**
3. m — **metre, about a big step**
4. km — **kilometre, a road between towns**
5. 10 mm — **1 cm**

**Set 2 (Sub lesson 2) — mass**
1. g — **gram, about an apple's slice**
2. kg — **kilogram, a bag of rice**
3. t — **tonne, a lorry**
4. 1,000 g — **1 kg**
5. 1,000 kg — **1 tonne**

**Set 3 (Sub lesson 3) — capacity**
1. mL — **millilitre, a teaspoon**
2. L — **litre, a jug**
3. 1,000 mL — **1 L**
4. 1,000 cm³ — **1 L**
5. half a litre — **about two glasses (Greece, Grade 1, ch.54)**
