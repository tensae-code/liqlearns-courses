# Measuring — true or false

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Measuring Length · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Measuring Mass · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Measuring Capacity.

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** 1 cm = 10 mm.
**True** (Egypt, Grade 2 new series, Chapter 5).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** To convert metres to centimetres, you divide by 100.
**False** — you multiply by 100; down the ladder means multiply.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** 1 tonne = 1,000 kg.
**True** (Sierra Leone, JSS 1, L73).

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** You can add 2 m and 50 cm as 52 without converting.
**False** — the units must match first: 2 m is 200 cm, so the sum is 250 cm.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** 1 litre of water fills almost four glasses.
**True** — Greece's own picture (Grade 1, ch.54).

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** 1,000 cm³ = 1 litre.
**True** — volume and capacity meet here (Sierra Leone, JSS 1, L90).
