# Measuring — fill in the blank

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Measuring Length · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Measuring Mass · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Measuring Capacity.

Fill each blank with a word from the box.

**Box:** millimetre · centimetre · metre · kilometre · gram · litre

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** 10 mm = 1 ______.
**Answer: centimetre.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** 100 cm = 1 ______.
**Answer: metre.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** 1,000 m = 1 ______.
**Answer: kilometre.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** 1,000 g = 1 kilogram; a small mass is measured in ______s.
**Answer: gram.**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** 1,000 mL = 1 ______.
**Answer: litre.**

**6. (Sub lesson 1)** A grain of rice is about 5 ______s long.
**Answer: millimetre.**
