# Measuring — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with a word from the box.

**Box:** millimetre · centimetre · metre · kilometre · gram · litre

**1. (Level 1)** 10 mm = 1 ______.
**Answer: centimetre.**

**2. (Level 1)** 100 cm = 1 ______.
**Answer: metre.**

**3. (Level 1)** 1,000 m = 1 ______.
**Answer: kilometre.**

**4. (Level 2)** 1,000 g = 1 kilogram; a small mass is measured in ______s.
**Answer: gram.**

**5. (Level 3)** 1,000 mL = 1 ______.
**Answer: litre.**

**6. (Level 1)** A grain of rice is about 5 ______s long.
**Answer: millimetre.**
