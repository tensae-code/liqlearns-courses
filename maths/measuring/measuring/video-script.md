# Video script — Measuring

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Measure with me! Length first.
A pencil — about **15 centimetres**!
A room — about **4 metres**! A road — **3 kilometres**!

And the ladder: **10 millimetres** make 1 centimetre.
**100 centimetres** make 1 metre. **1,000 metres** make
1 kilometre! Up the ladder, divide. Down the ladder, multiply!

Now mass. An apple — about **150 grams**!
A bag of rice — **5 kilograms**! A lorry — **tonnes**!
**1,000 grams** make 1 kilogram. **1,000 kilograms** make
1 tonne!

Now capacity. A spoon — **millilitres**! A jug — **litres**!
A tank — **thousands of litres**!
**1,000 millilitres** make 1 litre!

Now you. Measure your hand. How long? In centimetres!
That is measuring.
