# Video script — Equal Areas

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Three shapes. All different! Count their squares.
Ten! Ten! **Ten!** Same area — they are **equivalent**!

Now a triangle. Slide its top corner along the line.
It leans! It stretches! It looks new!
But the base is the same. The height is the same.
**Half base times height — the area did not move!**

A parallelogram can lean too. Upright, slanted, very slanted —
same base, same height, **same area!**

Different shape is not different area!
Now you. Make two shapes that look different and hold the same.
That is equal areas.
