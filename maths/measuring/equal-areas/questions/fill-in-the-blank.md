# Equal Areas — fill in the blank

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Equal Areas — What Equivalent Means · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Equal Areas — Equivalent Triangles · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Equal Areas — Equivalent Parallelograms.

Fill each blank with the missing number or words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Two shapes are equivalent if they have equal ______. Equivalent does not mean ______.
**Answer:** area; congruent (Yemen, G8 Pt2, 7-7, p.87).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A triangle on base 8 cm with its vertex on a parallel line 5 cm away has area ½ × 8 × ______ = ______ cm², wherever the vertex stands.
**Answer:** 5; 20 (Yemen, G8 Pt2, 7-7, p.87).

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A parallelogram's area is base × ______, and the lean changes only the ______ side, never that product.
**Answer:** height; slant (Yemen, G8 Pt2, 7-8, p.94).

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Parallelograms sharing a base are equivalent when their top sides lie on a line ______ to the base.
**Answer:** parallel (Yemen, G8 Pt2, 7-8, p.94).
