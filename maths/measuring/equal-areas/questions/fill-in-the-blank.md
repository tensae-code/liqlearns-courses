# Equal Areas — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the missing number or words.

**1. (Level 1)** Two shapes are equivalent if they have equal ______. Equivalent does not mean ______.
**Answer:** area; congruent (Yemen, G8 Pt2, 7-7, p.87).

**2. (Level 2)** A triangle on base 8 cm with its vertex on a parallel line 5 cm away has area ½ × 8 × ______ = ______ cm², wherever the vertex stands.
**Answer:** 5; 20 (Yemen, G8 Pt2, 7-7, p.87).

**3. (Level 3)** A parallelogram's area is base × ______, and the lean changes only the ______ side, never that product.
**Answer:** height; slant (Yemen, G8 Pt2, 7-8, p.94).

**4. (Level 3)** Parallelograms sharing a base are equivalent when their top sides lie on a line ______ to the base.
**Answer:** parallel (Yemen, G8 Pt2, 7-8, p.94).
