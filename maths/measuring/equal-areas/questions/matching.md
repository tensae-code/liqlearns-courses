Match each item on the left with the one right partner on the right.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Match each word to its meaning.
Words: (i) congruent  (ii) equivalent  (iii) similar
Meanings: (a) equal in area only  (b) equal in shape and size — sides, angles and area  (c) same shape, possibly different size
**Answer:** (i)–(b), (ii)–(a), (iii)–(c) — Yemen's equivalence is (a), checker-corrected: not congruence (G8 Pt2, 7-7, p.87).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Match each triangle to its area.
Triangles: (i) base 8, height 5  (ii) base 10, height 4  (iii) base 6, height 5
Areas: (a) 15 cm²  (b) 20 cm²  (c) 20 cm² — two triangles share this one
**Answer:** (i)–(b) ½ × 40, (ii)–(c) ½ × 40, (iii)–(a) ½ × 30 — (i) and (ii) are equivalent to each other.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Match each parallelogram to its area.
Parallelograms: (i) base 6, height 4, upright  (ii) base 6, height 4, leaning hard  (iii) base 6, height 3
Areas: (a) 18 cm²  (b) 24 cm²  (c) 24 cm² — the lean changes nothing
**Answer:** (i)–(b), (ii)–(c), (iii)–(a) — same base and height give the same area at any lean (Yemen, G8 Pt2, 7-8, p.94).
