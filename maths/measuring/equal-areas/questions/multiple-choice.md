# Equal Areas — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Equal Areas — What Equivalent Means · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Equal Areas — Equivalent Triangles · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Equal Areas — Equivalent Parallelograms.

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In Yemen's Grade 8 book, two shapes are "equivalent" when ______.
a) they are congruent  b) they have equal area  c) they have equal perimeter
**Answer:** b) they have equal area — the definition box of lesson 7-7, p.87.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A rectangle 5 × 2, an L-shape and a parallelogram each cover 10 unit squares on a grid. The three shapes are ______.
a) congruent  b) equivalent  c) similar
**Answer:** b) equivalent — same area count, different shapes (Yemen, G8 Pt2, 7-7, p.87).

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Triangles stand on the same base of 8 cm, with their top vertices on a line parallel to the base, 5 cm from it. Every such triangle has area ______.
a) 40 cm²  b) 20 cm²  c) it depends where the vertex stands
**Answer:** b) 20 cm² — ½ × 8 × 5; the height is fixed by the parallel line (Yemen, G8 Pt2, 7-7, p.87).

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A triangle has base 10 cm and height 4 cm. Which triangle is equivalent to it?
a) base 10 cm, height 8 cm  b) base 5 cm, height 4 cm  c) base 8 cm, height 5 cm
**Answer:** c) base 8 cm, height 5 cm — ½ × 8 × 5 = 20 cm² = ½ × 10 × 4.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** A parallelogram on a 6 cm base between parallels 4 cm apart is pushed so it leans much further. Its area is now ______.
a) 24 cm²  b) more than 24 cm²  c) less than 24 cm²
**Answer:** a) 24 cm² — base and height are unchanged by the lean; only the slant side grew (Yemen, G8 Pt2, 7-8, p.94).
