# Video script — Matrices

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Look at this table. Two factories, three products. Rows are factories, columns are products. That table is a MATRIX.

Add two weeks together? Add the tables, entry by entry. Same shape, same places. Easy.

Multiply two tables? A row meets a column. Multiply the pairs, add them up. One number comes out. Do it for every row and every column.

And the best part: two equations, two unknowns — write them as a matrix, flip the matrix over (that is the INVERSE), and the answers fall out.

Tables that do arithmetic. That is a matrix.
