# Hands-on — Matrices

## Factory table
Write a table: two factories down the side, three products across the top, with how many each made in a week. That is a 2 × 3 matrix. Now write next week's table and add the two, entry by entry. Check: the answer is the two weeks together.

## Row meets column
Take A = [[1, 2], [3, 4]] and B = [[5, 6], [7, 8]]. Work out AB by hand: the top-left entry is 1×5 + 2×7 = 19. Finish the other three entries (22, 43, 50). Now try BA and see that it is different — order matters.

## Determinant race
Work out the determinant of [[2, 3], [1, 4]]: 2×4 − 3×1 = 5. Now swap the rows and work it out again: the sign flips. That flip is why the determinant tells you whether a matrix has an inverse.

## Solve by inverse
Solve x + y = 5 and 2x − y = 1 by writing the matrix [[1, 1], [2, −1]], finding its inverse, and multiplying. Check the answer (x = 2, y = 3) back in both equations.
