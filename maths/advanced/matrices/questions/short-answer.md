# Matrices — short answer

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — What a Matrix Is · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Multiplication and Determinants · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Inverses and Solving Systems.

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Write the order of a matrix with 4 rows and 5 columns.
**Answer:** 4 × 5.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Add [[1, 2], [3, 4]] and [[5, 6], [7, 8]].
**Answer:** [[6, 8], [10, 12]].

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Multiply [[1, 2], [3, 4]] by [[5, 6], [7, 8]] — give the top-left entry only.
**Answer:** 19 (1×5 + 2×7).

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Find the determinant of [[3, 1], [2, 5]].
**Answer:** 13 (3×5 − 1×2).

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Find the inverse of [[1, 1], [2, −1]].
**Answer:** [[1/3, 1/3], [2/3, −1/3]] (determinant −3; swap the diagonal, change the signs off it, divide by −3).
