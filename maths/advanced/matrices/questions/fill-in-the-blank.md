# Matrices — fill in the blank

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — What a Matrix Is · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Multiplication and Determinants · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Inverses and Solving Systems.

Fill each blank with a word from the box.

**Box:** matrix · order · transpose · determinant · inverse · Cramer

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A rectangle of numbers in rows and columns is a ______.
**Answer: matrix.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A matrix's size is called its ______.
**Answer: order.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Flipping a matrix over its main diagonal gives its ______.
**Answer: transpose.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The single number worked out from a square matrix is its ______.
**Answer: determinant.**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** A matrix times its ______ gives the unit matrix.
**Answer: inverse.**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** Solving by ratios of determinants is ______'s rule.
**Answer: Cramer.**
