# Matrices — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with a word from the box.

**Box:** matrix · order · transpose · determinant · inverse · Cramer

**1. (Level 1)** A rectangle of numbers in rows and columns is a ______.
**Answer: matrix.**

**2. (Level 1)** A matrix's size is called its ______.
**Answer: order.**

**3. (Level 2)** Flipping a matrix over its main diagonal gives its ______.
**Answer: transpose.**

**4. (Level 2)** The single number worked out from a square matrix is its ______.
**Answer: determinant.**

**5. (Level 3)** A matrix times its ______ gives the unit matrix.
**Answer: inverse.**

**6. (Level 3)** Solving by ratios of determinants is ______'s rule.
**Answer: Cramer.**
