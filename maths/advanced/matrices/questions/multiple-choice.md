# Matrices — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — What a Matrix Is · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Multiplication and Determinants · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Inverses and Solving Systems.

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A 2 × 3 matrix has ______ rows and ______ columns.
a) 2 rows, 3 columns  b) 3 rows, 2 columns  c) 2 rows, 2 columns
**Answer: a) 2 rows, 3 columns.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The transpose of a matrix ______.
a) flips rows and columns  b) doubles every entry  c) finds its inverse
**Answer: a) flips rows and columns.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A 2 × 3 matrix times a 3 × 4 matrix gives a ______ matrix.
a) 2 × 4  b) 3 × 3  c) 4 × 2
**Answer: a) 2 × 4** — the middle numbers match, the outer numbers are the answer's shape.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The determinant of [[2, 3], [1, 4]] is ______.
a) 11  b) 5  c) 2
**Answer: b) 5** — 2×4 − 3×1.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** A matrix with determinant zero ______.
a) has no inverse  b) is the identity  c) is a zero matrix
**Answer: a) has no inverse.**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** Cramer's rule finds each unknown as ______.
a) a ratio of two determinants  b) the sum of two matrices  c) a row of the inverse
**Answer: a) a ratio of two determinants.**
