Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: name a matrix's order, recognise the special matrices, and add matrices of the same order.

## Sub lesson 2 — Multiplication and Determinants
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: multiply two matrices when it is defined, and find 2 × 2 and 3 × 3 determinants.
