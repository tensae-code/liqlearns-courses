# Lesson plan — Matrices

**Course:** Maths · **Stage:** Stage 12 — Advanced Mathematics · **Lesson:** Lesson 48 of 52
**Place in the curriculum:** Stage 12, the third lesson of the stage (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** matrices — arranging numbers in rows and columns, and adding, multiplying, inverting and using them to solve equations

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the simultaneous-equations lesson (matrices solve the same systems, faster) and the sets lesson (a matrix is a table with rules). Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say what a matrix is, name its order, and recognise the special matrices.
2. Add, subtract and multiply matrices by a number, and say when two matrices can be added.
3. Multiply two matrices, and say when the multiplication is defined.
4. Find a determinant of order 2 × 2 and 3 × 3, and use it for the area of a triangle.
5. Find the inverse of a 2 × 2 matrix, and solve two equations by inverse matrix and by Cramer's rule.
6. Solve a linear system by the Gauss method.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. A table of numbers to arrange — factory production, or a shop's prices, the way the books do it.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — the learner writes a week's production of two factories as a table, and adds two weeks together (lesson/lesson.md).
2. **The explanation** — what a matrix is; adding and multiplying by a number; multiplying matrices; determinants; inverses and Cramer's rule; Gauss (lesson/lesson.md).
3. **Say it to your companion** — give your companion two matrices and ask whether they can be added, whether they can be multiplied, and in which order (lesson/lesson.md).
4. **Questions** — from questions/: multiple-choice, short-answer, fill-in-the-blank, true-or-false, ordering, matching — served at the learner's level and teaching-mix (levels.md).
5. **Hands-on** — hands-on.md.
6. **Do It Yourself** — build a matrix from real numbers, multiply it, and solve a system with it (lesson/lesson.md).
   - ADD IMAGE at the multiplication part: a picture of a row meeting a column, dot by dot, to make one entry of the answer — the student traces one row and one column with a finger.
   - ADD IMAGE at the inverse part: a picture of a 2 × 2 matrix beside its inverse, with the product shown as the identity matrix — the student checks one entry of the product.

## If the learner is stuck
- Tries to add matrices of different orders → addition needs the same shape: same rows, same columns, or it is not defined.
- Multiplies in the wrong order → AB is usually not BA: rows of the first meet columns of the second, and the order matters.
- Forgets the determinant's minus sign → for a 2 × 2, it is ad − bc: the main diagonal minus the other one.
- This lesson's next door: vectors (same stage) — another object with its own addition and multiplication.
