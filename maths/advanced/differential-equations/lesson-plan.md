# Lesson plan — Differential Equations

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the derivatives lesson (an equation that holds a derivative) and the integrals lesson (solving means integrating). Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say what a differential equation is, and what its order is.
2. Recognise a separable first-order equation, and separate its variables.
3. Solve a separable equation by integrating both sides.
4. Use an initial condition to find the one solution through a point.
5. Check a solution by differentiating it back into the equation.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. A growth example to watch — money growing, or a population growing.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — the learner is given a rate and asked for the thing itself: if it grows this fast, what is it? (lesson/lesson.md).
2. **The explanation** — what a differential equation is; separating the variables; solving by integrating; initial conditions; checking a solution (lesson/lesson.md).
3. **Say it to your companion** — give your companion a differential equation and ask for its general solution, then for the one through your chosen point (lesson/lesson.md).
4. **Questions** — from questions/: multiple-choice, short-answer, fill-in-the-blank, true-or-false, ordering, matching — served at the learner's level and teaching-mix (levels.md).
5. **Hands-on** — hands-on.md.
6. **Do It Yourself** — solve two separable equations and check both answers (lesson/lesson.md).

## If the learner is stuck
- Cannot see how to separate → get all the y's (with dy) on one side and all the x's (with dx) on the other; if a term holds both, it is not separable this way.
- Forgets the constant after integrating → integrating gives a + C, and the constant is what the initial condition finds; without it there is a family, not one answer.
- Does not check the answer → differentiate your answer and put it back in the equation: if it fits, the answer is right.
