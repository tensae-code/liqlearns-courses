You know how fast it grows. Can you find what it is?

A population grows by 10 percent a year. That is a RATE. The rate is a derivative. The population itself is the thing the derivative belongs to.

An equation that HOLDS a derivative is a DIFFERENTIAL EQUATION. It says: the rate of change of y is this much.

How do you solve it? Split it. All the y's on one side, all the x's on the other. Then integrate both sides. The y's integrate to something with y. The x's integrate to something with x. Put them together — and there is your answer.

Now you. If dy over dx equals 2x, what is y? x squared, plus C. You just solved a differential equation.
