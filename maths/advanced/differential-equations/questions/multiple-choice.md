# Differential Equations — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Level 1)** A differential equation is an equation that holds ______.
a) a matrix  b) a derivative  c) a logarithm
**Answer: b) a derivative.**

**2. (Level 1)** dy/dx = 2x is a ______-order equation.
a) first  b) second  c) zero
**Answer: a) first** — the highest derivative is the first.

**3. (Level 2)** To separate dy/dx = 2x, you write ______.
a) dy = 2x dx  b) y = 2x  c) dy/dx = x²
**Answer: a) dy = 2x dx** — the y's on one side, the x's on the other.

**4. (Level 2)** Integrating dy = 2x dx gives ______.
a) y = x²  b) y = x² + C  c) y = 2x + C
**Answer: b) y = x² + C** — the constant is part of the answer.

**5. (Level 3)** The solution of dy/dx = y through (0, 2) is ______.
a) y = 2eˣ  b) y = eˣ + 2  c) y = 2x
**Answer: a) y = 2eˣ.**

**6. (Level 3)** To check a solution, you ______.
a) integrate it again  b) differentiate it and put it back in the equation  c) draw its graph
**Answer: b) differentiate it and put it back in the equation.**
