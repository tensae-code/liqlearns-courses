# Differential Equations — matching

Match each left side to its right side.

**Set (Level 1)** — words
1. differential equation — **an equation holding a derivative**
2. order — **the highest derivative in it**
3. general solution — **the whole family of curves**
4. particular solution — **the one curve through a point**

**Set (Level 2)** — moves
1. separate — **y's on one side, x's on the other**
2. integrate both sides — **the solving step**
3. initial condition — **the point that fixes C**
4. check — **differentiate back into the equation**

**Set (Level 3)** — solutions
1. dy/dx = 2x — **y = x² + C**
2. dy/dx = 3x² — **y = x³ + C**
3. dy/dx = y — **y = Aeˣ**
