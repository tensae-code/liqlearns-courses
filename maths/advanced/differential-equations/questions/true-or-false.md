# Differential Equations — true or false

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — What a Differential Equation Is · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Separating and Integrating · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Initial Conditions and Checking.

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** dy/dx = 2x is a differential equation.
**True** — it holds a derivative.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A first-order equation holds a second derivative.
**False** — first order means the highest derivative is the first.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Separating means getting all the y's on one side and all the x's on the other.
**True** — that is the separable form (Greece, Grade 12 Analysis, Chapter 3).

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The general solution of dy/dx = 2x is y = x².
**False** — it is y = x² + C; without the constant it is one curve, not the family.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** An initial condition finds the constant C.
**True** — the point the curve passes through fixes C.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** Checking a solution means differentiating it and putting it back in the equation.
**True** — if it fits, the answer is right.
