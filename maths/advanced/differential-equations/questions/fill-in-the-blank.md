# Differential Equations — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with a word from the box.

**Box:** differential · order · separable · general solution · particular solution · initial condition

**1. (Level 1)** An equation that holds a derivative is a ______ equation.
**Answer: differential.**

**2. (Level 1)** The ______ of a differential equation is the order of its highest derivative.
**Answer: order.**

**3. (Level 2)** An equation that splits into y-terms and x-terms is ______.
**Answer: separable.**

**4. (Level 2)** Solving a separable equation means ______ both sides.
**Answer: integrating.**

**5. (Level 3)** The family of curves is the ______ solution.
**Answer: general.**

**6. (Level 3)** One point picks out the ______ solution.
**Answer: particular.**
