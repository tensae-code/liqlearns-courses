# Differential Equations — fill in the blank

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — What a Differential Equation Is · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Separating and Integrating · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Initial Conditions and Checking.

Fill each blank with a word from the box.

**Box:** differential · order · separable · general solution · particular solution · initial condition

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** An equation that holds a derivative is a ______ equation.
**Answer: differential.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The ______ of a differential equation is the order of its highest derivative.
**Answer: order.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** An equation that splits into y-terms and x-terms is ______.
**Answer: separable.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Solving a separable equation means ______ both sides.
**Answer: integrating.**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** The family of curves is the ______ solution.
**Answer: general.**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** One point picks out the ______ solution.
**Answer: particular.**
