# Differential Equations — short answer

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Level 1)** What is the order of d²y/dx² = x?
**Answer:** Second order.

**2. (Level 1)** Is dy/dx = x + y separable by this lesson's method? Answer yes or no.
**Answer:** No — the x and y are stuck together in one sum and cannot be split into a y-side and an x-side.

**3. (Level 2)** Solve dy/dx = 3x².
**Answer:** y = x³ + C.

**4. (Level 2)** Solve dy/dx = 4x.
**Answer:** y = 2x² + C.

**5. (Level 3)** Find the solution of dy/dx = 2x through (0, 3).
**Answer:** y = x² + 3.
