**Acceptance count: taught in 3 countries (Jordan, Greece, Mauritania).** Jordan carries the lesson as the last step of its integration course, after substitution, partial fractions and parts, with the initial condition taught the year before; Greece carries the equations in elementary separable first-order form inside its integral-calculus chapter; Mauritania carries a whole chapter on differential equations in each of its two terminal streams.

**Honest limit, stated in the lesson:** this is the thinnest advanced lesson — three countries, one lesson or section each, all in separable first-order form. No read country teaches second-order equations, integrating factors, or any other method in the held set (Libya has no differential equations; Vietnam has none; Sierra Leone's SSS never teaches them; Kazakhstan's set tops out at Grade 9; Egypt teaches no differential equations as a topic; Armenia's Grade 12 guide teaches integrals, combinatorics and equations but no differential equations). This lesson teaches only what those three countries teach, and says so.
