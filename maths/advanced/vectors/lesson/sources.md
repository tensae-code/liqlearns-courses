**Acceptance count: taught in 10 countries (Egypt, Jordan, Greece, Vietnam, Libya, Mauritania, Laos, Yemen, Indonesia, Ethiopia).** Egypt carries vectors from directed segments and the parallelogram rule to 3-D vectors with dot and cross products; Jordan carries plane vectors with the dot product and space vectors with lines in space; Greece carries the orientation group's vector chapter with the inner product; Vietnam carries plane vectors with the dot product and space vectors with coordinates; Libya carries plane vectors with the scalar product; Mauritania carries vector calculus in two chapters. Indonesia carries vectors in Grade 10 Merdeka — 2-D and 3-D vectors with components, position and unit vectors, addition, subtraction and scalar multiplication — and, as guide evidence for the newer TL edition, plane vectors with the dot product in the Grade 11 TL guide; the Revisi drops vectors entirely and the held TL student book has none. Ethiopia carries vectors from 2-D basics in Grade 9 — directed segments and column vectors, magnitude and direction, the triangle and parallelogram laws, scalar multiples and position vectors — to vectors with dot and cross products in Grade 11, in two dimensions with i/j notation; there are no 3-D vectors in Ethiopia.

**Honest limit:** Libya's vectors are plane vectors only — no 3-D vector work — and Sierra Leone's JSS never teaches vectors or matrices. This lesson says so where it matters. Laos carries vectors from plane vectors with the dot product in Grade 9 to vectors in space with 3D coordinates, distance and direction cosines in Grade 10. Yemen carries vectors whole in Grade 10 — vectors, geometric operations, parallel and perpendicular vectors, the unit vector and the dot product.

**Flagged, not taught as printed:** the Grade 9 teacher guide's printed p. 143 swaps the horizontal and vertical components for the wagon pulled by a 100 N rope at 60° — the horizontal component is 100 cos 60° = 50 N and the vertical component is 100 sin 60° ≈ 87 N; the guide labels them the other way round. The lesson teaches the corrected labelling.
