# Vectors — short answer

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Level 1)** Is velocity a scalar or a vector?
**Answer:** A vector — speed with a direction.

**2. (Level 1)** Find the magnitude of (6, 8).
**Answer:** 10.

**3. (Level 2)** Add (2, 3) and (−1, 5).
**Answer:** (1, 8).

**4. (Level 2)** Find the dot product of (1, 2) and (3, 4).
**Answer:** 11 (1×3 + 2×4).

**5. (Level 3)** Find the magnitude of (1, 2, 2).
**Answer:** 3 (√(1 + 4 + 4)).
