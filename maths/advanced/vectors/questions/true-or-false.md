# Vectors — true or false

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Arrows: Size and Direction · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Coordinates and the Dot Product · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Vectors in Space.

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A vector drawn in a different place is a different vector.
**False** — equal vectors need the same magnitude and direction, not the same place.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The magnitude of (3, 4) is 5.
**True** — √(9 + 16) = 5.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Vector addition follows the parallelogram rule.
**True** — Egypt, Secondary 1 Term 2, Lesson 3-3.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The dot product of two vectors is a vector.
**False** — the dot product is a scalar, one number.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Two perpendicular vectors have dot product zero.
**True** — cos 90° = 0.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** The cross product a × b points along a.
**False** — it is perpendicular to both a and b.
