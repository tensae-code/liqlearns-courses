# Vectors — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with a word from the box.

**Box:** scalar · vector · magnitude · parallelogram · dot product · cross product

**1. (Level 1)** A quantity with size only is a ______.
**Answer: scalar.**

**2. (Level 1)** A quantity with size and direction is a ______.
**Answer: vector.**

**3. (Level 2)** The length of a vector is its ______.
**Answer: magnitude.**

**4. (Level 2)** Adding vectors head-to-tail, the sum is the arrow from the first ______ to the last head.
**Answer: tail.**

**5. (Level 3)** The ______ product of two vectors is a number.
**Answer: dot.**

**6. (Level 3)** The ______ product of two vectors is a new vector.
**Answer: cross.**
