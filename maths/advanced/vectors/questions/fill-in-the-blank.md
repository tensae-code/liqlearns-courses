# Vectors — fill in the blank

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Arrows: Size and Direction · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Coordinates and the Dot Product · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Vectors in Space.

Fill each blank with a word from the box.

**Box:** scalar · vector · magnitude · parallelogram · dot product · cross product

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A quantity with size only is a ______.
**Answer: scalar.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A quantity with size and direction is a ______.
**Answer: vector.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The length of a vector is its ______.
**Answer: magnitude.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Adding vectors head-to-tail, the sum is the arrow from the first ______ to the last head.
**Answer: tail.**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** The ______ product of two vectors is a number.
**Answer: dot.**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** The ______ product of two vectors is a new vector.
**Answer: cross.**
