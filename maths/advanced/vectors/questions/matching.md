# Vectors — matching

Match each left side to its right side.

**Set (Level 1)** — words
1. scalar — **size only**
2. vector — **size and direction**
3. magnitude — **length of the arrow**
4. position vector — **arrow from the origin to a point**

**Set (Level 2)** — operations
1. a + b — **head-to-tail sum**
2. a − b — **difference arrow**
3. k·a — **stretched (or flipped) arrow**
4. a·b — **dot product, a number**

**Set (Level 3)** — space
1. 3-D magnitude — **√(x² + y² + z²)**
2. line in space — **a point plus a direction vector**
3. cross product — **a new vector, perpendicular to both**
