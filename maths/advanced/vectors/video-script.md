# Video script — Vectors

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Walk three steps north. Now four steps east. Where are you?

Five steps from where you started — in a straight line. Three, four, five! That straight line is the SUM of your two walks.

A walk has a SIZE — how many steps — and a DIRECTION — which way. A thing with size and direction is a VECTOR. A thing with size only, like the temperature, is a SCALAR.

Draw it as an arrow. The length is the size. The way it points is the direction. Two arrows the same length, same way — the same vector, wherever you draw them.

Now you. Walk your own two arrows. Where is the sum? Draw it!
