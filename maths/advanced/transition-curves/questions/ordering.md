# Ordering — Transition Curves: Joining Curves Smoothly

Sub-lesson markers: every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to — Sub lesson 1 — Meeting Without a Kink · Sub lesson 2 — Matching the Bending · Sub lesson 3 — Designing the Join. This lesson was built new with its sub lessons (Germany expansion merge, 2026-11-11), so its markers are original — there are no older depth layers to map from.

1. (Sub lesson 1) Put in order what f, f′ and f′′ each control at a join, from the road's height to its bending.
   - f′′ — the bending
   - f — the height
   - f′ — the slope
   **Answer:** f (height), f′ (slope), f′′ (bending). Item constructed for this lesson from its founding teaching. Germany, *Lambacher Schweizer Mathematik 10, Ausgabe Baden-Württemberg* (student book, Grade 10), Kapitel IV, GFS-Thema (Trassierungen), printed p. 122.

2. (Sub lesson 3) Put the steps of designing a transition curve in order.
   - Solve for the coefficients
   - Write down the join conditions
   - Check every condition on the finished piece
   - Choose the degree: conditions − 1
   **Answer:** write down the join conditions → choose the degree (conditions − 1) → solve for the coefficients → check every condition on the finished piece.
