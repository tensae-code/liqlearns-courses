# Matching — Transition Curves: Joining Curves Smoothly

Sub-lesson markers: every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to — Sub lesson 1 — Meeting Without a Kink · Sub lesson 2 — Matching the Bending · Sub lesson 3 — Designing the Join. This lesson was built new with its sub lessons (Germany expansion merge, 2026-11-11), so its markers are original — there are no older depth layers to map from.

1. (Sub lesson 1) Match each join condition to the fault it prevents.
   - Equal values at the join → a step (jump) in the road
   - Equal first derivatives at the join → a kink (corner) in the road
   **Answer:** values ↔ step; first derivatives ↔ kink. Item constructed for this lesson from its founding teaching. Germany, *Lambacher Schweizer Mathematik 10, Ausgabe Baden-Württemberg* (student book, Grade 10), Kapitel IV, GFS-Thema (Trassierungen), printed p. 122.

2. (Sub lesson 2) Match each join condition to the fault it prevents.
   - Equal second derivatives at the join → a curvature jump (Krümmungsruck)
   - Equal values only, slopes free → a kink (corner) in the road
   **Answer:** second derivatives ↔ curvature jump; values only ↔ kink. Item constructed for this lesson from its founding teaching. Germany, *Lambacher Schweizer Mathematik 10, Ausgabe Baden-Württemberg* (student book, Grade 10), Kapitel IV, GFS-Thema (Trassierungen), printed p. 122.

3. (Sub lesson 3) Match each design to the conditions it can meet.
   - A cubic piece (4 coefficients) → value and slope at both ends
   - A quintic piece (6 coefficients) → value, slope and curvature at both ends
   **Answer:** cubic ↔ value and slope at both ends; quintic ↔ value, slope and curvature at both ends. Item constructed for this lesson from its founding teaching. Germany, *Lambacher Schweizer Mathematik 10, Ausgabe Baden-Württemberg* (student book, Grade 10), Kapitel IV, GFS-Thema (Trassierungen), printed p. 122.
