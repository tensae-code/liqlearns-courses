# Video script — Transition Curves: Joining Curves Smoothly

Super-simple voice. The tutor talks; two road pieces and the
join between them are on screen, and each condition lights up
as it is spoken.

---

Two roads. One low, one high. Between them, a gap. We have
to join them — and the driver must feel nothing.

First: the join must start exactly where the low road ends.
No step. And it must arrive exactly where the high road
begins. No step there either.

Second: the join must leave flat, and arrive flat. If the
slope jumps, there is a corner — a kink. The driver feels a
jolt. So the slopes must match too.

Here is a curve that does all that. Smooth. No step. No
kink. But the driver still feels something. The bending!
The straight road does not bend. Our curve bends at once.
The bending jumps — and the steering wheel jumps with it.

So we match the bending too. Flat, flat, and not bending —
at both ends. Six promises. A bigger curve keeps them all:
it climbs like an S, gentle at the start, gentle at the
end, and the driver feels... nothing. That is a transition
curve.
