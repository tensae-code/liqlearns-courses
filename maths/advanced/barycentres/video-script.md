# Video script — Barycentres

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Balance a ruler on your finger. It balances in the MIDDLE. Two equal ends.

Now tape a coin near one end. The balance point MOVES — towards the coin. The heavy side needs the short arm.

That moving balance point is the **barycentre**. Points carry weights. The barycentre is where they balance.

Two points, weights α and β: α times GA plus β times GB equals zero — the pulls cancel. Equal weights? The midpoint. More weight at B? G slides towards B.

Three points? Average their places — weighted. A triangle with equal weights balances at the **centroid**, where the medians cross.

Now you. Weight the ends. Slide the point. Make it balance. That is a barycentre.
