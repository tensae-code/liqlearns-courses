## Sub lesson 1 — The Barycentre of Two Points
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: state the vector condition α·GA + β·GB = 0, find a two-point barycentre on a segment, and say why it sits nearer the heavier point; the equal-weights case gives the midpoint.

## Sub lesson 2 — A System of Points and Grouping
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: compute a three-point barycentre in coordinates with x_G = (αx_A + βx_B + γx_C)/(α + β + γ), and reach the same point by grouping two points first.

## Sub lesson 3 — The Centroid and the Balance Point
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: find a triangle's centroid as the equal-weights barycentre, connect it to the medians, and explain the barycentre as a centre of mass.
