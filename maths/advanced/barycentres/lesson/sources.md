**Acceptance count: taught in 2 countries (Mauritania, Laos).** Mauritania carries the barycentre as a chapter of its own in four senior streams — 5AS-M (Ch.6, p.57), 5AS-D (Ch.6, p.47), 6AS-M (Ch.4, p.51, titled for the general system of weighted points) and 6AS-C (Ch.4, p.51) — cited at chapter level as filed. Laos carries the full working treatment in Grade 11 Chapter 9 (pp.249–260): the barycentre of two points (L33, p.249), then of a system of points (L34, p.254), with the vector condition α·GA + β·GB + γ·GC = 0 and the weighted coordinate formula the book itself prints on its cover.
