**Acceptance count: taught in 5 countries (Mauritania, Laos, Syria, Rwanda, Bolivia).** Mauritania carries the barycentre as a chapter of its own in four senior streams — 5AS-M (Ch.6, p.57), 5AS-D (Ch.6, p.47), 6AS-M (Ch.4, p.51, titled for the general system of weighted points) and 6AS-C (Ch.4, p.51) — cited at chapter level as filed. Laos carries the full working treatment in Grade 11 Chapter 9 (pp.249–260): the barycentre of two points (L33, p.249), then of a system of points (L34, p.254), with the vector condition α·GA + β·GB + γ·GC = 0 and the weighted coordinate formula the book itself prints on its cover.

Rwanda teaches the barycentre in Advanced Mathematics Senior Five Unit 8, as the centroid of a set of points in 3D.

Syria's barycentres are in Grade 12 Part Two — in space with Leibniz' theorem, and by complex affixes.

Bolivia enters through the Área Matemática sections of its Ministerio de Educación (Estado Plurinacional de Bolivia) Textos de Aprendizaje — the student books for Grades 7, 8, 10 and 12, each the maths section of that year's integrated year book, keeping the whole book's printed foliation, with no imprint or ISBN printed in any of them — and through the Compendio para Maestras y Maestros 2023–2024, the ministry's teacher compilation of the official student texts for all six Secundaria years (Grades 7–12), cited as teacher-compilation evidence only, never as or in place of a pupil text; its Secundaria 3 and Secundaria 5 sections are the only in-hand source for Grades 9 and 11, whose student texts are not in the record. From its books: from Grade 7: Baricentro.
