# Barycentres — fill-in-the-blank

Fill in the missing number, function or word.

**1. (Level 1)** The two-point condition is α·GA + β·___ = 0 (vectors).
**Answer:** GB (Laos, Grade 11, L33, printed p.249).

**2. (Level 1)** G = (αA + βB)/(α + ___) coordinate by coordinate.
**Answer:** β (Laos, Grade 11, L33, printed p.249).

**3. (Level 2)** For three points, x_G = (αx_A + βx_B + γx_C)/(α + β + ___).
**Answer:** γ (Laos, Grade 11, L34, printed p.254).

**4. (Level 2)** Grouping works because replacing two points by their barycentre keeps the system's ___ unchanged.
**Answer:** barycentre (Laos, Grade 11, L34, printed p.254).

**5. (Level 3)** The centroid is where the triangle's three ___ meet.
**Answer:** medians (Laos, Grade 11, L33–L34, printed pp.249, 254).

**6. (Level 3)** Laos's barycentre chapter is Chapter ___ of Grade 11.
**Answer:** 9 (Laos, Grade 11, Ch.9, printed pp.249–260).
