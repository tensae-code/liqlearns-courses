# Barycentres — true or false

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Level 1)** The barycentre always sits at the midpoint of AB.
**Answer:** False — only for equal weights; otherwise it moves towards the heavier point (Laos, Grade 11, L33, printed p.249).

**2. (Level 1)** Doubling both weights leaves the barycentre where it was.
**Answer:** True — only the ratio of the weights matters (Laos, Grade 11, L33, printed p.249).

**3. (Level 2)** The plain average of three points' coordinates is their barycentre for any weights.
**Answer:** False — the average is the barycentre only when the weights are equal; otherwise weight first (Laos, Grade 11, L34, printed p.254).

**4. (Level 2)** Grouping two points first, then merging with the third, gives the same barycentre as the direct formula.
**Answer:** True — associativity (Laos, Grade 11, L34, printed p.254).

**5. (Level 3)** The centroid of a triangle is the meeting point of its medians.
**Answer:** True — each median ends at a two-point equal-weights barycentre (Laos, Grade 11, L33–L34, printed pp.249, 254).

**6. (Level 3)** Mauritania teaches the barycentre in only one stream.
**Answer:** False — four streams carry the chapter: 5AS-M, 5AS-D, 6AS-M, 6AS-C (Mauritania, as filed).
