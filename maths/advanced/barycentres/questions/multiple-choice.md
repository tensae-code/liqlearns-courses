# Barycentres — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — The Barycentre of Two Points · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — A System of Points and Grouping · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — The Centroid and the Balance Point.

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The barycentre of (A, α) and (B, β) is the point G with ______.
a) GA = GB  b) α·GA + β·GB = 0 (vectors)  c) α·GA = β·GB as vectors in the same direction
**Answer:** b) α·GA + β·GB = 0 (vectors) — the weighted pulls cancel (Laos, Grade 11, L33, printed p.249).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** With equal weights, the barycentre of A and B is ______.
a) A  b) the midpoint of AB  c) B
**Answer:** b) the midpoint of AB (Laos, Grade 11, L33, printed p.249).

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** If β is much bigger than α, G sits ______.
a) nearer A  b) in the middle  c) nearer B
**Answer:** c) nearer B — the heavier point gets the shorter arm (Laos, Grade 11, L33, printed p.249).

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** The barycentre of A(0, 0) weight 2 and B(6, 0) weight 1 is ______.
a) (2, 0)  b) (3, 0)  c) (4, 0)
**Answer:** a) (2, 0) — (2·0 + 1·6)/3 = 2 (Laos, Grade 11, L33, printed p.249).

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** x_G for weights α, β, γ at A, B, C is ______.
a) (x_A + x_B + x_C)/3  b) (αx_A + βx_B + γx_C)/(α + β + γ)  c) (α + β + γ)/(x_A + x_B + x_C)
**Answer:** b) (αx_A + βx_B + γx_C)/(α + β + γ) (Laos, Grade 11, L34, printed p.254).

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** When two points are grouped first, their replacement point carries weight ______.
a) the larger weight  b) the sum of the two weights  c) 1
**Answer:** b) the sum of the two weights — grouping keeps the total (Laos, Grade 11, L34, printed p.254).

**7. (Sub lesson 3)** The centroid of a triangle is its barycentre with ______.
a) equal weights  b) weights equal to the side lengths  c) one weight only
**Answer:** a) equal weights — the average of the three vertices (Laos, Grade 11, L33–L34, printed pp.249, 254).

**8. (Sub lesson 3)** Mauritania's 6AS-M chapter title names the barycentre of ______.
a) two points only  b) a system of weighted points  c) a circle
**Answer:** b) a system of weighted points — "Barycentre d'un système de points pondérés" (Mauritania, 6AS-M, Ch.4, printed p.51).
