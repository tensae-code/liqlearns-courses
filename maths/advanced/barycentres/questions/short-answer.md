# Barycentres — short answer

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Level 1)** State GA/GB for the barycentre of (A, α), (B, β) in terms of the weights.
**Answer:** GA/GB = β/α — each arm answers to the opposite weight (Laos, Grade 11, L33, printed p.249).

**2. (Level 1)** Find the barycentre of weights 1 and 4 at 0 and 10 on a number line.
**Answer:** 8 — (1·0 + 4·10)/5, near the heavier weight (Laos, Grade 11, L33, printed p.249).

**3. (Level 2)** Find the barycentre of A(0, 0) weight 1, B(4, 0) weight 2, C(0, 4) weight 1.
**Answer:** (2, 1) — ((0 + 8 + 0)/4, (0 + 0 + 4)/4) (Laos, Grade 11, L34, printed p.254).

**4. (Level 2)** Group A(0, 0) weight 1 with B(6, 0) weight 1 first: what point and weight result?
**Answer:** (3, 0) with weight 2 — the midpoint, carrying the sum (Laos, Grade 11, L34, printed p.254).

**5. (Level 3)** Find the centroid of (0, 0), (6, 0), (0, 6).
**Answer:** (2, 2) — the plain average of the vertices (Laos, Grade 11, L33–L34, printed pp.249, 254).

**6. (Level 3)** In physics language, the barycentre of weighted points is their what?
**Answer:** Centre of mass — the weights read as masses (Laos, Grade 11, Ch.9, printed pp.249–260).

**7. (Level 3)** Name Mauritania's four barycentre-chapter streams with their chapter numbers.
**Answer:** 5AS-M Ch.6, 5AS-D Ch.6, 6AS-M Ch.4, 6AS-C Ch.4 (Mauritania, printed pp.57, 47, 51, 51 as filed).
