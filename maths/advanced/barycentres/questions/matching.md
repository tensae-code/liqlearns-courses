# Barycentres — matching

Match each item on the left to its partner on the right.

**1. (Level 1)** Match each weight pair at A, B to its barycentre on a segment from 0 to 6: (a) weights 1, 1 (b) weights 2, 1 (c) weights 1, 2 — 3, 2, 4
**Answer:** a) 3, b) 2, c) 4 — nearer the heavier end (Laos, Grade 11, L33, printed p.249).

**2. (Level 2)** Match each Mauritania book to its barycentre chapter page: (a) 5AS-M (b) 5AS-D (c) 6AS-M and 6AS-C — p.57, p.47, p.51
**Answer:** a) p.57, b) p.47, c) p.51 (Mauritania, as filed).

**3. (Level 2)** Match each Laos lesson to its content: (a) L33 (b) L34 — barycentre of two points, barycentre of a system of points
**Answer:** a) barycentre of two points, b) barycentre of a system of points (Laos, Grade 11, Ch.9, printed pp.249, 254).

**4. (Level 3)** Match each name to its point: (a) midpoint (b) centroid (c) barycentre — two points equal weights, triangle vertices equal weights, weighted points in general
**Answer:** a) two points equal weights, b) triangle vertices equal weights, c) weighted points in general (Laos, Grade 11, L33–L34, printed pp.249, 254).
