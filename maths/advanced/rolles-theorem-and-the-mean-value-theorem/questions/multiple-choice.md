# Rolle's theorem and the Mean Value Theorem — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Level 1)** Rolle's theorem needs, besides continuity and differentiability, ______.
a) f(a) = f(b)  b) f(a) = 0  c) f′(a) = f′(b)
**Answer:** a) f(a) = f(b) — equal endpoint values give the flat tangent.

**2. (Level 1)** For f(x) = x² − 4x + 3 on [1, 3], Rolle's c is ______.
a) 1  b) 2  c) 3
**Answer:** b) 2 — f′(x) = 2x − 4 = 0 at x = 2, strictly inside.

**3. (Level 2)** The Mean Value Theorem says some c inside has f′(c) equal to ______.
a) f(b) − f(a)  b) (f(b) − f(a))/(b − a)  c) 0 always
**Answer:** b) the chord's slope — the average rate over the interval.

**4. (Level 2)** For f(x) = x² on [1, 3], the Mean Value Theorem's c is ______.
a) 2  b) 3  c) 4
**Answer:** a) 2 — the chord slope is 4 and 2x = 4 at x = 2.

**5. (Level 3)** L'Hôpital's rule may be applied directly when the limit has the form ______.
a) 1/0  b) 0/0 or ∞/∞  c) any form
**Answer:** b) 0/0 or ∞/∞ only — other forms are reduced to quotients first (Cambodia, PRIVATE booklet, pp.53–54).

**6. (Level 3)** lim(x→0) (eˣ − 1)/x by L'Hôpital's rule equals ______.
a) 0  b) e  c) 1
**Answer:** c) 1 — differentiate top and bottom: eˣ/1 → 1.
