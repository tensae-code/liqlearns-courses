# Rolle's theorem and the Mean Value Theorem — short answer

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Level 1)** State Rolle's three conditions.
**Answer:** Continuous on [a, b]; differentiable on (a, b); f(a) = f(b).

**2. (Level 1)** Find every c for f(x) = x³ − 3x on [−√3, √3] under Rolle's theorem.
**Answer:** c = −1 and c = 1 — f′ = 3x² − 3 = 0 at x = ±1, both inside.

**3. (Level 2)** A car covers 120 km in 2 h. What does the Mean Value Theorem guarantee?
**Answer:** At some moment its instantaneous speed was exactly 60 km/h, the average speed.

**4. (Level 2)** Find c for f(x) = 1/x on [1, 2].
**Answer:** c = √2 — chord slope is −1/2 and −1/x² = −1/2 gives x² = 2; √2 lies in (1, 2).

**5. (Level 3)** lim(x→∞) (3x + 1)/(x − 2) by L'Hôpital's rule?
**Answer:** 3 — the form is ∞/∞; differentiating gives 3/1.

**6. (Level 3)** In the booklet's proof, how is the Mean Value Theorem obtained from Rolle's theorem?
**Answer:** Subtract the chord's line from f to get an auxiliary function with equal (zero) endpoint values, then apply Rolle (Cambodia, PRIVATE Derivatives booklet, pp.50–52).
