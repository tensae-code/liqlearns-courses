Match each item on the left with the one right item on the right.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Match: (i) Rolle; (ii) Mean Value; (iii) neither. Cases: (a) continuous, differentiable, f(0) = f(2), flat tangent claimed; (b) continuous, differentiable, chord-slope tangent claimed; (c) a corner inside, flat tangent claimed from equal endpoints.
**Answer:** (i)–(a), (ii)–(b), (iii)–(c) — the corner kills differentiability, so no theorem speaks.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Match the c: (i) x² − 4x + 3 on [1, 3], Rolle; (ii) x² on [1, 3], MVT; (iii) 1/x on [1, 2], MVT. Values: (a) √2; (b) 2; (c) 2.
**Answer:** (i)–(b), (ii)–(c), (iii)–(a) — two different theorems can land on the same c.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Match: (i) Greece; (ii) Cambodia PRIVATE Derivatives booklet; (iii) Yemen. Ways: (a) both theorems proved, pp.50–52; (b) both theorems in the Analysis Ch.2 with De L'Hospital's rules; (c) both theorems as lesson 6-7 of the differentiation unit.
**Answer:** (i)–(b), (ii)–(a), (iii)–(c).

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Match the condition: (i) continuity on [a, b]; (ii) differentiability on (a, b); (iii) f(a) = f(b). Roles: (a) Rolle only; (b) both theorems — the graph must not break; (c) both theorems — the slope must exist inside.
**Answer:** (i)–(b), (ii)–(c), (iii)–(a).

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Match: (i) average rate; (ii) instantaneous rate; (iii) chord. Meanings: (a) (f(b) − f(a))/(b − a); (b) the line joining the endpoints; (c) f′(c).
**Answer:** (i)–(a), (ii)–(c), (iii)–(b) — the theorem sets (a) equal to (c).

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** Match the form: (i) sin x/x at 0; (ii) (3x + 1)/(x − 2) at ∞; (iii) x·ln x at 0⁺. Handling: (a) ∞/∞ — apply directly; (b) 0/0 — apply directly; (c) 0·∞ — rewrite as a quotient first.
**Answer:** (i)–(b), (ii)–(a), (iii)–(c).
