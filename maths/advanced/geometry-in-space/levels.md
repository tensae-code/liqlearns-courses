# Geometry in Space — questions by level

Practice by level, tied to the skill number. The questions live in
the type files; this file is the by-level view. The levels are the
depths inside this one thing — geometry in space — the basic facts and positions first, then the parallelism system, then perpendicularity, the three-perpendiculars theorem, dihedral angles and distances.

## Level 1 — basic facts and positions in space
Level 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: state the facts that fix a plane, and classify any two lines, a line and a plane, and two planes — including skew lines.

## Level 2 — parallelism in space
Level 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: use the line–plane and plane–plane parallelism criteria and properties, the transitivity of parallel lines in space, and the equal-angles theorem.

## Level 3 — perpendicularity, dihedral angles and distances
Level 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: use the line–plane perpendicularity criterion, the three-perpendiculars theorem, the plane angle of a dihedral angle, the perpendicular-planes criterion, and distances and projections in space.
