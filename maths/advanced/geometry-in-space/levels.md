## Sub lesson 1 — Basic Facts and Positions in Space
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: state the facts that fix a plane, and classify any two lines, a line and a plane, and two planes — including skew lines.

## Sub lesson 2 — Parallelism in Space
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: use the line–plane and plane–plane parallelism criteria and properties, the transitivity of parallel lines in space, and the equal-angles theorem.

## Sub lesson 3 — Perpendicularity, Dihedral Angles and Distances
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: use the line–plane perpendicularity criterion, the three-perpendiculars theorem, the plane angle of a dihedral angle, the perpendicular-planes criterion, and distances and projections in space.
