# Geometry in Space — true or false

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Basic Facts and Positions in Space · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Parallelism in Space · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Perpendicularity, Dihedral Angles and Distances.

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Two lines in space that never meet must be parallel.
**Answer: False** — they may be skew: never meeting, with no plane holding both.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** All of plane geometry holds inside each single plane in space.
**Answer: True** — stated among the stereometry axioms (Kazakhstan, Smirnov Geometry 10, §§1–2).

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A line parallel to a plane is parallel to every line in the plane.
**Answer: False** — it is parallel to the line in which a plane through it cuts the given plane, and skew to most other lines in it.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Kuwait's Grade 11 space geometry uses vectors and coordinates in space.
**Answer: False** — Kuwait's Unit 10 is synthetic only, as filed; vectors and coordinates in space are lesson 92's home.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** The Soltan stereometry book from Kazakhstan is the Grade 10 textbook itself.
**Answer: False** — it is a worked-solutions manual for that textbook; the parent textbook is not held, worded as filed.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** Cambodia's Space Geometry booklet is a MoEYS government textbook, and its Lesson 1 answer key can be trusted as printed.
**Answer: False** — it is a PRIVATE Khem Putthi booklet, and its Lesson 1 answer key is unreliable as printed.
