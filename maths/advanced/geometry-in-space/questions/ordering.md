# Geometry in Space — ordering

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Basic Facts and Positions in Space · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Parallelism in Space · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Perpendicularity, Dihedral Angles and Distances.

Put the items in the right order.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Order the run of Kuwait's Unit 10 as the book teaches it: the dihedral angle (10-4); lines and planes, postulates and skew lines (10-1); perpendicular planes (10-5); a line perpendicular to a plane (10-3); parallel lines and planes (10-2).
**Answer:** 10-1; 10-2; 10-3; 10-4; 10-5 (Kuwait, Grade 11 Term 2, pp.116–149).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Order the steps for classifying two lines in space: decide if a plane holds both; check if they meet; if no plane holds both, they are skew.
**Answer:** check if they meet; decide if a plane holds both; if no plane holds both, they are skew.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Order the steps to prove a line parallel to a plane: conclude the line is parallel to the plane; find a line in the plane parallel to the given line; check the given line is outside the plane.
**Answer:** check the line is outside the plane; find a parallel line in the plane; conclude the line is parallel to the plane.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Order the steps to measure a dihedral angle: draw a ray in the second face at right angles to the edge; pick a point on the edge; measure the angle between the two rays; draw a ray in one face at right angles to the edge.
**Answer:** pick a point on the edge; draw a ray in one face at right angles to the edge; draw a ray in the second face at right angles to the edge; measure the angle between the two rays.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Order the three perpendiculars in the three-perpendiculars theorem, from the ground up: the in-plane line perpendicular to the slanted line; the perpendicular from a point to the plane; the in-plane line perpendicular to the projection.
**Answer:** the perpendicular to the plane; the in-plane line perpendicular to the projection; the in-plane line perpendicular to the slanted line.
