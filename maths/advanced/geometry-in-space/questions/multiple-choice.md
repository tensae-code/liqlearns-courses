# Geometry in Space — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Level 1)** Through three points not on one line there passes ______.
a) no plane  b) exactly one plane  c) infinitely many planes
**Answer: b) exactly one plane** — the first basic fact (China, B-edition, pp.91–93).

**2. (Level 1)** Two lines in space that never meet and lie in no common plane are called ______.
a) parallel  b) intersecting  c) skew
**Answer: c) skew** — space's third way for two lines (China, B-edition, pp.97–99).

**3. (Level 1)** A line with two points in a plane ______.
a) lies wholly in the plane  b) meets the plane once  c) is parallel to the plane
**Answer: a) lies wholly in the plane** — a basic fact of planes (Kazakhstan, Smirnov Geometry 10, §§1–2).

**4. (Level 2)** A line outside a plane is parallel to the plane if it is parallel to ______.
a) any point of the plane  b) a line in the plane  c) the plane's normal
**Answer: b) a line in the plane** — the line–plane parallelism criterion (Vietnam, Grade 11 Volume 1, p.84).

**5. (Level 2)** Lines in space that are each parallel to the same third line are ______.
a) skew to each other  b) perpendicular  c) parallel to each other
**Answer: c) parallel to each other** — transitivity of parallel lines in space (China, B-edition, pp.96–97).

**6. (Level 3)** To prove a line perpendicular to a plane, it is enough for the line to be perpendicular to ______ in the plane.
a) one line  b) two intersecting lines  c) two parallel lines
**Answer: b) two intersecting lines** — the line–plane perpendicularity criterion (Kuwait, Grade 11 Term 2, pp.130–136).

**7. (Level 3)** The size of a dihedral angle is measured by ______.
a) its plane angle  b) its edge length  c) the area of its faces
**Answer: a) its plane angle** — drawn at right angles to the edge, one ray in each face (Greece, Volume B, Chapter 12).

**8. (Level 3)** The distance from a point to a plane is the length of ______ from the point to the plane.
a) the longest slanted line  b) any slanted line  c) the perpendicular
**Answer: c) the perpendicular** — the shortest length from the point (Vietnam, Grade 11 Volume 2, p.54).
