# Geometry in Space — matching

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Basic Facts and Positions in Space · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Parallelism in Space · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Perpendicularity, Dihedral Angles and Distances.

Match each item on the left with the one right match on the right.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Three points not on one line
**Answer:** fix exactly one plane — the first basic fact (China, B-edition, pp.91–93).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Two intersecting lines
**Answer:** fix exactly one plane — a corollary of the basic facts (China, B-edition, pp.93–94).

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A line parallel to a line in a plane
**Answer:** is parallel to the plane — the line–plane criterion (Vietnam, Grade 11 Volume 1, p.84).

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** A line perpendicular to two intersecting lines in a plane
**Answer:** is perpendicular to the plane — the line–plane perpendicularity criterion (Armenia, Geometry 10, §5, pp.42–46).

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Two half-planes on a common edge
**Answer:** form a dihedral angle — measured by its plane angle (Yemen, Grade 12 Science, p.147).
