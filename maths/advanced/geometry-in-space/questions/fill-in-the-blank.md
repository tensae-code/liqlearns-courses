Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Basic Facts and Positions in Space · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Parallelism in Space · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Perpendicularity, Dihedral Angles and Distances.

Fill in the missing word or value.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A plane is also fixed by a line and a ______ not on the line.
**Answer:** point — one of the corollaries of the basic facts (China, B-edition, pp.93–94).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Two planes that do not meet are called ______ planes.
**Answer:** parallel — the only other case is meeting in a line.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Parallel planes cut by a third plane meet it in ______ lines.
**Answer:** parallel — a property of parallel planes (Vietnam, Grade 11 Volume 1, p.88).

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Through a point outside a line in space there is exactly ______ line parallel to the given line.
**Answer:** one — Cambodia's Grade 10 book states it for space (Chapter 11, pp.162–183).

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** A dihedral angle is the union of two ______ on a common edge.
**Answer:** half-planes — Yemen's definition (Grade 12 Science, p.147).

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** When a dihedral angle is a right angle, the two planes are ______.
**Answer:** perpendicular (Kuwait, Grade 11 Term 2, pp.143–149).
