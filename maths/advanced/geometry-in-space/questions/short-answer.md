# Geometry in Space — short answer

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Basic Facts and Positions in Space · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Parallelism in Space · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Perpendicularity, Dihedral Angles and Distances.

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the three ways a line and a plane can sit in space.
**Answer:** The line lies in the plane, meets it in one point, or is parallel to it (Armenia, Geometry 10, §3, pp.27–33).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In a cube, how many edges are skew to one given edge?
**Answer:** 4 — 4 meet it, 3 are parallel to it, 4 are skew to it, of the other 11.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A plane contains two intersecting lines, each parallel to a second plane. What follows?
**Answer:** The two planes are parallel — the plane–plane parallelism criterion (China, B-edition, pp.103–106).

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** How is the angle between two skew lines defined?
**Answer:** Through one point, draw lines parallel to both; the angle between those lines is the angle between the skew lines (Kazakhstan, Smirnov Geometry 10, §9).

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** State the three-perpendiculars theorem in one line.
**Answer:** A line in a plane at right angles to the projection of a slanted line is also at right angles to the slanted line itself (Kuwait, Grade 11 Term 2, pp.130–136).

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** A plane contains a line perpendicular to another plane. What follows?
**Answer:** The two planes are perpendicular — the perpendicular-planes criterion (China, B-edition, pp.118–120).

**7. (Sub lesson 3)** In Iraq's Grade 12 Science book, a triangle is projected onto a plane. What projection area does the read-and-checked worked example give?
**Answer:** 30 cm² (Iraq, Grade 12 Scientific, pp.238–240).

**8. (Sub lesson 3)** What is the line–plane angle of a slanted line?
**Answer:** The angle between the line and its projection on the plane (Vietnam, Grade 11 Volume 2, p.38).
