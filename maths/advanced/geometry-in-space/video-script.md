# Video script — Geometry in Space

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

In a plane, two lines do two things: they meet, or they run parallel.

In SPACE, there is a third way. Hold one pencil along the top edge of a box. Hold another along a side edge at the back. They never meet. But no flat sheet can hold them both. They are SKEW — space's own way for two lines.

A plane — a flat sheet in space — is fixed by three points that are not in a line. Three legs hold a stool steady because three points fix the plane it stands in.

Now the two big systems. PARALLEL: a line that never meets a plane and runs parallel to a line in it is parallel to the whole plane. PERPENDICULAR: a line that stands at right angles to TWO crossing lines in a plane stands at right angles to the WHOLE plane — and to every line in it.

Two planes can meet like an open book. The opening between the pages is the DIHEDRAL angle, and you measure it flat, at right angles to the spine.

Now you. Look at the edges of your room. Find two that are skew. That is geometry in space.
