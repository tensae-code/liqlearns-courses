# Analytic Geometry in Space — ordering

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Coordinates, Octants and Distance in Space · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Vectors in Space and Their Products · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Equations of Lines and Planes in Space.

Put each list in the right order.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Order the steps for the distance between two points in space: (a) square and add the three differences, (b) subtract the coordinates one by one, (c) take the square root.
**Answer:** (b), (a), (c) — differences first, then squares, then the root (Laos, Grade 10, Lesson 23).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Order the steps for the direction cosines of (2, 3, 6): (a) divide each component by 7, (b) find the magnitude 7, (c) check the squares sum to 1.
**Answer:** (b), (a), (c) — magnitude first, then the divisions, then the check (Laos, Grade 10, Lesson 23).

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Order the steps for the equation of a plane through three points: (a) write a(x−x₀)+b(y−y₀)+c(z−z₀) = 0, (b) form two vectors in the plane, (c) cross them to get the normal.
**Answer:** (b), (c), (a) — the normal must be found before the equation can be written (Egypt, Pure Grade 12, Lessons 3-3 p.63, 4-2 p.90).

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Order Egypt's space course: (a) equations of planes, (b) the 3D coordinate system, (c) vector multiplication, (d) equations of lines.
**Answer:** (b), (c), (d), (a) — Lessons 3-1 p.48, 3-3 p.63, 4-1 p.80, 4-2 p.90 (Egypt, Pure Grade 12).
