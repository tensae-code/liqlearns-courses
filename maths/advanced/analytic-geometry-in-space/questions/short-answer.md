# Analytic Geometry in Space — short answer

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Level 1)** How many octants does space have?
**Answer:** 8 — the three coordinate planes divide space into eight octants (Laos, Grade 10, Lesson 23, p.253).

**2. (Level 1)** Laos's Grade 10 book opens the topic on a cuboid with which named point?
**Answer:** A(3,3,4) — named by its three distances (Laos, Grade 10, Lesson 23, p.253).

**3. (Level 1)** Find the distance from the origin to (2, 3, 6).
**Answer:** 7 — √(4 + 9 + 36) = √49 = 7.

**4. (Level 2)** Write the vector with components 1, −2, 3 using i, j, k.
**Answer:** i − 2j + 3k — one basis vector per component (Egypt, Pure Grade 12, Lesson 3-2, p.54).

**5. (Level 2)** The vectors AB = (−6,−3,2) and AC = (2,−6,−3) from Laos's book: what is AB · AC, and what does it prove?
**Answer:** 0 — so the angle at A is a right angle (Laos, Grade 10, Lesson 23).

**6. (Level 2)** What does the mixed product u · (v × w) = 0 tell you about the three vectors?
**Answer:** They are coplanar — the box they span has volume zero.

**7. (Level 3)** Write the parametric equations of the line through (1, 2, 3) with direction (2, −1, 4).
**Answer:** x = 1 + 2t, y = 2 − t, z = 3 + 4t (Laos, Grade 12, Lesson 28, p.270).

**8. (Level 3)** Write the plane through (1, −1, 2) with normal (3, 2, 3).
**Answer:** 3x+2y+3z−7 = 0 — 3·1 + 2·(−1) + 3·2 = 7 (Cambodia, Grade 11 Advanced teacher guide, p.236).
