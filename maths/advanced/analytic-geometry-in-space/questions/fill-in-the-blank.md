# Analytic Geometry in Space — fill in the blank

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Coordinates, Octants and Distance in Space · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Vectors in Space and Their Products · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Equations of Lines and Planes in Space.

Fill each blank with the missing word or number.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A point in space is written P(x, y, ____).
**Answer:** z — the third coordinate is the height (Laos, Grade 10, Lesson 23, p.253).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The distance formula in space is derived by applying ____ twice.
**Answer:** Pythagoras — once flat, once standing (Laos, Grade 10, Lesson 23).

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** For every vector, cos²α + cos²β + cos²γ = ____.
**Answer:** 1 — the direction cosines always square-sum to 1 (Laos, Grade 10, Lesson 23).

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The magnitude of the cross product |u × v| is |u||v| ____ θ.
**Answer:** sin — it is the area of the parallelogram the vectors span (Egypt, Pure Grade 12, Lesson 3-3, p.63).

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** A plane is fixed by one point and one ____ vector.
**Answer:** normal — a vector perpendicular to the plane (Egypt, Pure Grade 12, Lesson 4-2, p.90).

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** The angle between a line and a plane is the angle between the line and its ____ on the plane.
**Answer:** projection — its shadow on the plane (Laos, Grade 12, Lesson 29, p.278).
