# Analytic Geometry in Space — matching

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Coordinates, Octants and Distance in Space · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Vectors in Space and Their Products · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Equations of Lines and Planes in Space.

Match each item on the left with the one right partner on the right.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Match the book to its space lesson: (i) Egypt Pure Grade 12, (ii) Laos Grade 10, (iii) Jordan Grade 12 Term 2 — with (a) vectors in space, Unit 6 Lesson 1, p.108, (b) vectors in space, Lesson 3-2, p.54, (c) vectors in space on a cuboid, Lesson 23, p.253.
**Answer:** (i)–(b), (ii)–(c), (iii)–(a) — each book opens space vectors in its own lesson.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Match the product to what it gives: (i) dot product, (ii) cross product, (iii) mixed product — with (a) a vector perpendicular to both, (b) a number testing perpendicularity, (c) a number giving a volume.
**Answer:** (i)–(b), (ii)–(a), (iii)–(c) — dot gives a number, cross gives a vector, mixed gives a volume (Egypt, Pure Grade 12, Lesson 3-3, p.63).

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Match the vector to its magnitude: (i) (2, 3, 6), (ii) (1, 2, 3) to (4, 6, 3) as a displacement, (iii) (0, 0, 5) — with (a) 5, (b) 7, (c) 5.
**Answer:** (i)–(b) 7, (ii)–(a) 5, (iii)–(c) 5 — magnitudes by the distance formula (Laos, Grade 10, Lesson 23).

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Match the data to the object it fixes: (i) a point and a direction, (ii) a point and a normal, (iii) a point and two directions in it — with (a) a plane, (b) a line, (c) a plane.
**Answer:** (i)–(b), (ii)–(a), (iii)–(c) — a line needs a direction; a plane needs a normal or two directions (Egypt, Pure Grade 12, Lessons 4-1 p.80, 4-2 p.90).
