# Analytic Geometry in Space — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Level 1)** The three coordinate planes divide space into ______.
a) 4 parts  b) 6 parts  c) 8 octants
**Answer:** c) 8 octants — the XY, XZ and YZ planes cut space into eight (Laos, Grade 10, Lesson 23, p.253).

**2. (Level 1)** The distance from (1, 2, 3) to (4, 6, 3) is ______.
a) 5  b) 7  c) 25
**Answer:** a) 5 — √(3² + 4² + 0²) = 5 (Laos, Grade 10, Lesson 23).

**3. (Level 2)** The magnitude of (2, 3, 6) is ______.
a) 7  b) 11  c) 49
**Answer:** a) 7 — √(4 + 9 + 36) = √49 = 7 (Egypt, Pure Grade 12, Lesson 3-2, p.54).

**4. (Level 2)** The direction cosines of (2, 3, 6) are ______.
a) 2, 3, 6  b) 2/7, 3/7, 6/7  c) 1/2, 1/3, 1/6
**Answer:** b) 2/7, 3/7, 6/7 — each component divided by the magnitude 7 (Laos, Grade 10, Lesson 23).

**5. (Level 2)** Two vectors in space are perpendicular exactly when their dot product is ______.
a) 1  b) 0  c) their magnitudes multiplied
**Answer:** b) 0 — u·v = 0 is the perpendicularity test (Laos, Grade 10, Lesson 23).

**6. (Level 2)** The cross product (2i + j − 2k) × (i − 3j + 2k) is ______.
a) −4i − 6j − 7k  b) 4i + 6j + 7k  c) −7
**Answer:** a) −4i − 6j − 7k — verified in Cambodia's book (Grade 12 Advanced, §7.1, p.174).

**7. (Level 3)** The symmetric equation of the line through (1, 2, 3) with direction (2, −1, 4) is ______.
a) (x−1)/2 = (y−2)/(−1) = (z−3)/4  b) x/2 = y/(−1) = z/4  c) (x+1)/2 = (y+2)/(−1) = (z+3)/4
**Answer:** a) (x−1)/2 = (y−2)/(−1) = (z−3)/4 — point subtracted, direction underneath (Egypt, Pure Grade 12, Lesson 4-1, p.80).

**8. (Level 3)** The plane through (5, 3, 6) with normal (3, 2, 4), taught corrected, is ______.
a) 3x+2y+4z−42 = 0  b) 3x+2y+4z−45 = 0  c) 3x+2y+4z = 0
**Answer:** b) 3x+2y+4z−45 = 0 — 3·5 + 2·3 + 4·6 = 45; the guide's printed −42 is wrong (Cambodia, Grade 11 Advanced teacher guide, p.236, taught corrected).
