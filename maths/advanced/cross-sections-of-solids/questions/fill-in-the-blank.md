# Fill in the blank — Cross-Sections of Solids

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Layer 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — What a Section Is · (Layer 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Prism and Pyramid Sections · (Layer 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Cylinder and Cone Sections · (Layer 4) -> Sub lesson 4 — Sphere Sections.

1. (Sub lesson 1) The flat face where a cutting plane passes through a solid is called a ___ of the solid.
   **Answer: section** — the common part of the solid and the plane. Tajikistan, *Геометрия* (Grade 11), printed p. 13.

2. (Sub lesson 2) By Theorem 3, sections of a prism by parallel planes that cut all the lateral edges are ___.
   **Answer: equal** — so a section parallel to the bases reproduces the base. Tajikistan, *Геометрия* (Grade 11), printed p. 14.

3. (Sub lesson 3) A section of a cone parallel to the base divides the height and the generators ___; the areas of section and base stand as the ___ of the corresponding ratios.
   **Answer: proportionally; squares** — Theorem 15 and its corollaries. Tajikistan, *Геометрия* (Grade 11), printed p. 69.

4. (Sub lesson 4) A sphere of radius R is cut by a plane at distance d from the centre; the section's radius is ___.
   **Answer: √(R² − d²)** — Theorem 18. Tajikistan, *Геометрия* (Grade 11), printed p. 84.

5. (Sub lesson 4) A section of a sphere by a plane through its centre is called a ___ circle, and the plane is called a ___ plane.
   **Answer: great; diametral** — Tajikistan, *Геометрия* (Grade 11), printed p. 84.
