# Matching — Cross-Sections of Solids

1. (Layers 2–3) Match each solid and cut to its section:
   (a) prism, cut parallel to the lateral edges
   (b) tetrahedron, cut by any plane
   (c) right cylinder, cut through a generator
   (d) right cone, cut through the vertex
   (e) sphere, cut by a plane through the centre

   (i) a great circle
   (ii) a parallelogram
   (iii) a rectangle
   (iv) an isosceles triangle
   (v) a triangle or a quadrilateral

   **Answer: (a)–(ii), (b)–(v), (c)–(iii), (d)–(iv), (e)–(i)** — prism sections, Tajikistan, *Геометрия* (Grade 11), printed p. 14; tetrahedron, printed p. 35; cylinder (Theorem 12), printed p. 59; cone (Theorem 14), printed p. 68; sphere (Theorem 18), printed p. 84.

2. (Layers 2–4) Match each theorem to what it proves:
   (a) Theorem 2
   (b) Theorem 3
   (c) Theorem 15
   (d) Theorem 18

   (i) parallel sections of a prism cutting all lateral edges are equal
   (ii) every section of a sphere is a circle of radius √(R² − d²)
   (iii) a section of a prism is a convex polygon
   (iv) a parallel section of a cone is a disc centred on the axis, dividing height and generators proportionally

   **Answer: (a)–(iii), (b)–(i), (c)–(iv), (d)–(ii)** — Tajikistan, *Геометрия* (Grade 11), printed pp. 13, 14, 69, 84.
