1. (Sub lesson 2) Put the steps of the book's construction of a prism section through three points on three different lateral edges in order:
   (a) draw the two diagonal sections through the given points' edges
   (b) mark the three given points on their lateral edges
   (c) use the diagonal sections' common segment to fix the section's remaining vertices
   (d) join the vertices in order to close the section polygon

   **Answer: (b), (a), (c), (d)** — the worked construction of Tajikistan, *Геометрия* (Grade 11), printed p. 15.

2. (Sub lesson 4) A sphere of radius 13 cm is cut by planes at distances 0, 5, 12 and 13 cm from the centre. Put the four sections in order from largest to smallest radius:
   (a) the section at distance 12 cm
   (b) the section at distance 0 cm
   (c) the section at distance 13 cm
   (d) the section at distance 5 cm

   **Answer: (b), (d), (a), (c)** — radii 13, 12, 5 and 0 cm by √(R² − d²); the last "section" is a single point. Tajikistan, *Геометрия* (Grade 11), printed p. 84.
