# Multiple choice — Cross-Sections of Solids

1. (Layer 1) A section of a solid by a plane is:
   (a) the shadow the solid casts on the plane
   (b) the part the solid and the cutting plane have in common — the flat face where the plane passes through the solid
   (c) the net of the solid folded flat
   (d) any face of the solid

   **Answer: (b)** — the section is the common part of solid and plane. Tajikistan, *Геометрия* (Grade 11), printed p. 13.

2. (Layer 2) Theorem 2 says a section of a prism is always:
   (a) a triangle
   (b) a parallelogram
   (c) a convex polygon
   (d) a shape congruent to the base

   **Answer: (c)** — proved in the book; only special sections (parallel to the lateral edges, diagonal sections) are parallelograms, and only the base-parallel one equals the base. Tajikistan, *Геометрия* (Grade 11), printed p. 13.

3. (Layer 3) A plane cuts a cone of height 15 cm and base radius 6 cm parallel to the base, 10 cm above the base. The section's radius is:
   (a) 6 cm
   (b) 4 cm
   (c) 3 cm
   (d) 2 cm

   **Answer: (d)** — the section is 5 cm from the apex, and by Theorem 15 it divides the height proportionally: 6 × 5/15 = 2 cm (area 4π cm²). Tajikistan, *Геометрия* (Grade 11), printed pp. 69–70.

4. (Layer 4) A plane cuts a sphere of radius 13 cm at a distance of 5 cm from the centre. The section's radius is:
   (a) 8 cm
   (b) 12 cm
   (c) 13 cm
   (d) 18 cm

   **Answer: (b)** — √(R² − d²) = √(169 − 25) = √144 = 12 cm, by Theorem 18. Tajikistan, *Геометрия* (Grade 11), printed p. 84.

5. (Layer 3) The axial section of a right cone is:
   (a) a circle
   (b) a rectangle
   (c) an isosceles triangle standing on a base diameter
   (d) a sector of a circle

   **Answer: (c)** — the section through the vertex and the axis, by Theorem 14. Tajikistan, *Геометрия* (Grade 11), printed p. 68.
