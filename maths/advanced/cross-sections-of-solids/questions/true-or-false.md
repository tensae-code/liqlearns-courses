# True or false — Cross-Sections of Solids

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Layer 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — What a Section Is · (Layer 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Prism and Pyramid Sections · (Layer 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Cylinder and Cone Sections · (Layer 4) -> Sub lesson 4 — Sphere Sections.

1. (Sub lesson 2) A diagonal section of a prism — one through two lateral edges that do not lie in one face — is a parallelogram.
   **Answer: True** — as are all sections parallel to the lateral edges. Tajikistan, *Геометрия* (Grade 11), printed p. 14.

2. (Sub lesson 2) A section of a tetrahedron can be a pentagon.
   **Answer: False** — a tetrahedron has four faces, so its sections are triangles or quadrilaterals only. Tajikistan, *Геометрия* (Grade 11), printed p. 35.

3. (Sub lesson 3) A section of a right cylinder by a plane through a generator is a circle.
   **Answer: False** — by Theorem 12 it is a parallelogram, and in a right cylinder a rectangle; a circle comes from a section parallel to the bases. Tajikistan, *Геометрия* (Grade 11), printed pp. 59–60.

4. (Sub lesson 4) Through any two points of a sphere exactly one great circle can be drawn.
   **Answer: False** — taught corrected: for antipodal points there are infinitely many great circles, one for every diametral plane through their diameter. The book states the property without the exception (printed p. 85); the exception follows from its own definition of antipodal points (printed p. 81).

5. (Sub lesson 4) A section of a ball by a plane is a disc, and the section of the sphere bounding it by the same plane is the circle around that disc.
   **Answer: True** — Theorem 18 and the ball's sections, Tajikistan, *Геометрия* (Grade 11), printed pp. 84–85.
