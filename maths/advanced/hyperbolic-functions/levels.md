## Sub lesson 1 — Definitions, Graphs and Identities
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: define sinh, cosh and tanh from eˣ, evaluate them at 0 and at ln 2, sketch the three graphs with domains and ranges, and use cosh²x − sinh²x = 1.

## Sub lesson 2 — Derivatives and Log Forms
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: differentiate all six hyperbolic functions, stating the two corrected rules with their minus signs, and write the functions and their inverses in log form.

## Sub lesson 3 — Inverse Hyperbolic Functions
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: define sinh⁻¹, cosh⁻¹ and tanh⁻¹ with their domains, convert between the inverse and log forms, and differentiate the three main inverses.
