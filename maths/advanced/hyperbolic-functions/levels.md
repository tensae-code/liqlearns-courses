# Hyperbolic Functions — questions by level

Practice by level, tied to the skill number. The questions live in
the type files; this file is the by-level view. The levels are the
depths inside this one thing — hyperbolic functions — definitions, graphs and identities first, then derivatives, then the inverse functions.

## Level 1 — definitions, graphs and identities
Level 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: define sinh, cosh and tanh from eˣ, evaluate them at 0 and at ln 2, sketch the three graphs with domains and ranges, and use cosh²x − sinh²x = 1.

## Level 2 — derivatives and log forms
Level 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: differentiate all six hyperbolic functions, stating the two corrected rules with their minus signs, and write the functions and their inverses in log form.

## Level 3 — inverse hyperbolic functions
Level 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: define sinh⁻¹, cosh⁻¹ and tanh⁻¹ with their domains, convert between the inverse and log forms, and differentiate the three main inverses.
