# Video script — Hyperbolic Functions

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Take e to the x. Take e to the minus x. Two curves.

Add them. Halve the sum. That is **cosh x** — it hangs like a chain, and it never drops below 1.

Subtract them. Halve the difference. That is **sinh x** — it climbs through zero and keeps climbing.

Divide sinh by cosh. That is **tanh x** — it lives between minus 1 and 1, like a gentle S.

And here is the magic: cosh squared minus sinh squared is **1**. Always. Try it with your calculator!

Differentiate them? sinh becomes cosh. Cosh becomes sinh. And sech and cosech keep a **minus** sign — the book's summary drops it, so say it loud: minus!

Now you. Build cosh 1 and sinh 1 from e. Check the squares. That is hyperbolic.
