# Logarithms and Exponentials — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with a word from the box.

**Box:** exponent · logarithm · base · growth · decay · inverse

**1. (Level 1)** In 2⁵, 2 is the ______ and 5 is the ______.
**Answer: base; exponent.**

**2. (Level 2)** The logarithm is the ______ of the exponential function.
**Answer: inverse.**

**3. (Level 2)** log(ab) = log a + log b is the ______ law.
**Answer: product.**

**4. (Level 3)** log(aⁿ) = n log a is the ______ law.
**Answer: power.**

**5. (Level 3)** The natural logarithm uses base ______.
**Answer: e.**
