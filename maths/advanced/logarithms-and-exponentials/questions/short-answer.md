**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Write 8^(1/3) as a whole number.
**Answer:** 2.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A population doubles every year from 100. How many after 3 years?
**Answer:** 800 (100 × 2³).

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Write log₁₀ 1000 as a whole number.
**Answer:** 3 (10³ = 1000).

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Use the power law: log(5²) in terms of log 5.
**Answer:** 2 log 5.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Solve log₃ x = 4.
**Answer:** x = 81 (3⁴).
