# Logarithms and Exponentials — matching

Match each left side to its right side.

**Set (Level 1)** — powers
1. 2³ — **8**
2. 2⁰ — **1**
3. 8^(1/3) — **2**
4. 10² — **100**

**Set (Level 2)** — logs
1. log₂ 8 — **3**
2. log₁₀ 100 — **2**
3. log₁₀ 1000 — **3**
4. logₐ 1 — **0**

**Set (Level 3)** — laws
1. log(ab) — **log a + log b**
2. log(a/b) — **log a − log b**
3. log(aⁿ) — **n log a**
