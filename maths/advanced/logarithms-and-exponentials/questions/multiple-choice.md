# Logarithms and Exponentials — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Powers and the Exponential Function · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — The Logarithm and Its Laws · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Log Functions, Graphs and Equations.

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** 2³ means ______.
a) 2 × 3  b) 2 × 2 × 2  c) 3 × 3
**Answer: b) 2 × 2 × 2 = 8.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** An exponential function grows by ______ each step.
a) adding the same amount  b) multiplying by the same amount  c) subtracting
**Answer: b) multiplying by the same amount** (Egypt, Pure Grade 11 Term 1, 2-2).

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** log₂ 8 = ______.
a) 2  b) 3  c) 8
**Answer: b) 3** — because 2³ = 8.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** log(ab) = ______.
a) log a + log b  b) log a × log b  c) log a − log b
**Answer: a) log a + log b** — the product law.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** The graph of y = logₐ x is the mirror of y = aˣ across the line ______.
a) y = 0  b) y = x  c) x = 0
**Answer: b) y = x** — inverse functions mirror across y = x.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** 2ˣ = 64. Then x = ______.
a) 5  b) 6  c) 8
**Answer: b) 6** — 2⁶ = 64.
