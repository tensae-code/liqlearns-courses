**Acceptance count: taught in 11 countries (Egypt, Jordan, Greece, Armenia, Vietnam, Libya, Mauritania, Laos, Yemen, Indonesia, Nicaragua).** Egypt carries the full arc from rational exponents through the log laws to exp/log calculus; Jordan carries exponential growth and decay, the log laws and exp/log equations; Greece carries the exponential and logarithmic functions in one chapter; Armenia carries both streams' exp/log chapters with equations and inequalities; Vietnam carries the arc from real exponents to exp/log equations; Libya carries the index laws and logarithms from Grade 10 and exp/log calculus in Grade 12; Mauritania carries log and exponential functions in both terminal streams. Indonesia carries exponentials and logarithms from exponent laws, the exponential function and logarithms as the inverse of exponentiation in Grade 10 — including the Revisi Pengayaan on logarithms — to logarithmic functions in Grade 11 Tingkat Lanjut; exponential and logarithmic equations are not named topics in the held books. Nicaragua carries exponentials and logarithms from integer powers in the Grade 7 module, through the first full exposure in the Grade 10 module — aˣ, eˣ, compound interest, growth and decay, inverse functions, log laws and both kinds of equations — to the more formal second pass in Grade 11, with powers, roots and rational exponents, graphs with stated properties, exponential and logarithmic equations and base-10 logarithms, but without eˣ, natural logarithms or the module's applications.

**Honest limit:** Sierra Leone's SSS never teaches logarithms, and Vietnam teaches no logs or exponentials before Grade 11 Volume 2 — those countries are not counted here, and this lesson stands on the ten countries that do teach the topic. Laos carries exponentials and logarithms whole in Grade 11 — both functions with graphs, properties, equations, inequalities, maxima and minima and domain and range. Yemen carries logarithms and exponentials from powers and roots in Grade 10 to the exponential function and logarithms — concept, rules, common and natural logs and applications — in Grade 11, in both tracks.
