---

Start with 1. Double it: 2. Double it: 4. Double it: 8, 16, 32.

That is an EXPONENTIAL. Growing by multiplying, again and again. Not adding — multiplying.

Now ask backwards: how many doublings make 32? Five. That backwards question is a LOGARITHM. Log base 2 of 32 is 5. The log is the POWER.

2 to the power 3 is 8. Log base 2 of 8 is 3. Same fact, said two ways. Forwards is a power. Backwards is a log.

Now you. How many doublings make 64? Six! Log base 2 of 64 is 6. You just did a logarithm.
