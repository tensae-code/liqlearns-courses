Practice by level, tied to the skill number. The questions live in
the type files; this file is the by-level view. The levels are the
depths inside this one thing — powers and the exponential function first, then the logarithm and its laws, then the log function, graphs and equations.

## Level 1 — powers and the exponential function
Level 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: use real exponents and the index laws, and describe exponential growth and decay.

## Level 2 — the logarithm and its laws
Level 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: say what a logarithm is, and use the product, quotient and power laws.

## Level 3 — log functions, graphs and equations
Level 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: draw the log function as the mirror of the exponential function, and solve exponential and logarithmic equations.
