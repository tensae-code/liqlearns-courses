---

Throw a ball up. It rises, slows, stops at the top, and falls.

How fast is it moving? At the start, fast upward. At the top, not moving at all. On the way down, fast downward.

The speed at ONE instant — that is the DERIVATIVE. How fast the height is changing, right now.

On a graph, the height is a curve. The speed is the SLOPE of the curve — how steep it is, at that one point. Steep up: fast rising. Flat: stopped. Steep down: fast falling.

At the very top, the curve is flat. Slope zero. Speed zero. The derivative is zero at the top — and that is how you FIND the top!

Now you. Draw a hill. Where is the slope zero? At the top. That is the derivative, doing its job.
