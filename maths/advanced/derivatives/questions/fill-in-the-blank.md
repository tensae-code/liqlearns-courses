# Derivatives — fill in the blank

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Rates of Change and the Derivative · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — The Rules and the Special Functions · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Higher Derivatives and Applications.

Fill each blank with a word from the box.

**Box:** derivative · tangent · chain rule · second derivative · maximum · minimum

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The slope of the tangent is the ______.
**Answer: derivative.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The derivative of xⁿ is ______.
**Answer: n·xⁿ⁻¹ (n x to the n minus 1).**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Differentiate the outside, then multiply by the derivative of the inside: the ______ rule.
**Answer: chain.**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** The derivative of the derivative is the ______ derivative.
**Answer: second.**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Where the derivative is zero, the curve is ______.
**Answer: flat.**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** The derivative of distance is ______.
**Answer: velocity.**
