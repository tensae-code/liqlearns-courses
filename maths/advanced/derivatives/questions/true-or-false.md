# Derivatives — true or false

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Rates of Change and the Derivative · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — The Rules and the Special Functions · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Higher Derivatives and Applications.

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The derivative is the slope of the tangent line.
**True** — that is what the derivative means geometrically.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The instantaneous rate is the average rate over a shrinking interval.
**True** — the limit as the interval shrinks to nothing.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The derivative of a sum is the sum of the derivatives.
**True** — the sum rule.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The chain rule says to differentiate the inside first.
**False** — differentiate the outside first, then multiply by the derivative of the inside.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** A zero derivative always means a maximum.
**False** — it is a candidate for a maximum or a minimum (or neither): check each side.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** The second derivative of distance is acceleration.
**True** — velocity is the first derivative, acceleration the second (Armenia, Algebra & Analysis 11, Chapter 4).
