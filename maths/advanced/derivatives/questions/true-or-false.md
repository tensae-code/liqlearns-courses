# Derivatives — true or false

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Level 1)** The derivative is the slope of the tangent line.
**True** — that is what the derivative means geometrically.

**2. (Level 1)** The instantaneous rate is the average rate over a shrinking interval.
**True** — the limit as the interval shrinks to nothing.

**3. (Level 2)** The derivative of a sum is the sum of the derivatives.
**True** — the sum rule.

**4. (Level 2)** The chain rule says to differentiate the inside first.
**False** — differentiate the outside first, then multiply by the derivative of the inside.

**5. (Level 3)** A zero derivative always means a maximum.
**False** — it is a candidate for a maximum or a minimum (or neither): check each side.

**6. (Level 3)** The second derivative of distance is acceleration.
**True** — velocity is the first derivative, acceleration the second (Armenia, Algebra & Analysis 11, Chapter 4).
