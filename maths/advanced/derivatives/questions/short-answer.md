# Derivatives — short answer

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Level 1)** A ball's height is h(t) = 20t − 5t². What is its velocity at t = 1?
**Answer:** 10 (h'(t) = 20 − 10t; at t = 1, 20 − 10 = 10).

**2. (Level 1)** Differentiate f(x) = x² from first principles: what is f'(x)?
**Answer:** 2x.

**3. (Level 2)** Differentiate 3x⁴.
**Answer:** 12x³.

**4. (Level 2)** Differentiate eˣ.
**Answer:** eˣ.

**5. (Level 3)** Find the maximum of f(x) = −x² + 4x.
**Answer:** 4 at x = 2 (f'(x) = −2x + 4 = 0 gives x = 2; f(2) = 4).
