# Derivatives — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Rates of Change and the Derivative · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — The Rules and the Special Functions · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Higher Derivatives and Applications.

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The derivative of a function at a point is the slope of the ______ at that point.
a) secant line  b) tangent line  c) x-axis
**Answer: b) tangent line.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The average rate of change over an interval is ______.
a) the change divided by the interval  b) the change times the interval  c) always zero
**Answer: a) the change divided by the interval.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The derivative of x³ is ______.
a) 3x²  b) x²  c) 3x
**Answer: a) 3x²** — the power rule.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The derivative of sin x is ______.
a) −sin x  b) cos x  c) −cos x
**Answer: b) cos x.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** To differentiate (x² + 1)³ you need the ______ rule.
a) product  b) quotient  c) chain
**Answer: c) chain** — outside first, then the inside's derivative.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** At a maximum point, the derivative is ______.
a) positive  b) zero  c) undefined
**Answer: b) zero** — the curve is flat at the top.
