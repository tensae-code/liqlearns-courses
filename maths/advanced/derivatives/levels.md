# Derivatives — questions by level

Practice by level, tied to the skill number. The questions live in
the type files; this file is the by-level view. The levels are the
depths inside this one thing — rates of change first, then the derivative and its rules, then the special functions, higher derivatives and applications.

## Level 1 — rates of change and the derivative
Level 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: find an average and an instantaneous rate, and a derivative from first principles for a polynomial.

## Level 2 — the rules and the special functions
Level 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: use the sum, product, quotient and chain rules, and differentiate powers, trig, exp and log functions.

## Level 3 — higher derivatives and applications
Level 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: find second derivatives, and use derivatives for maxima, minima, tangents, related rates and curve sketching.
