You know the speed. Can you find the distance?

Speed is the derivative of distance. So distance is the REVERSE of the derivative. That reverse is the INTEGRAL.

Differentiate x squared and you get 2x. So integrate 2x and you get x squared — plus a little extra, C, because any constant disappears when you differentiate.

Now the area. Under a curve, between two lines. Slice it into thin strips. Each strip is almost a rectangle. Add them all up. That sum IS the integral. The thinner the strips, the truer the area.

That is an integral. Differentiation backwards, and area by adding strips.
