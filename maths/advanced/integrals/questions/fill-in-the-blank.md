# Integrals — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with a word from the box.

**Box:** antiderivative · indefinite · definite · substitution · Newton–Leibniz · revolution

**1. (Level 1)** Integration is the ______ of differentiation.
**Answer: reverse.**

**2. (Level 1)** A function whose derivative is f is an ______ of f.
**Answer: antiderivative.**

**3. (Level 2)** ∫f(x)dx = F(x) + ______.
**Answer: C.**

**4. (Level 2)** Replacing the inner function by u is integration by ______.
**Answer: substitution.**

**5. (Level 3)** The definite integral is F(b) − F(a) by the ______ formula.
**Answer: Newton–Leibniz.**

**6. (Level 3)** Spinning a curve round an axis makes a solid of ______.
**Answer: revolution.**
