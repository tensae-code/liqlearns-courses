# Integrals — fill in the blank

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Antiderivatives · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Methods of Integration · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Definite Integrals, Areas and Volumes.

Fill each blank with a word from the box.

**Box:** antiderivative · indefinite · definite · substitution · Newton–Leibniz · revolution

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Integration is the ______ of differentiation.
**Answer: reverse.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A function whose derivative is f is an ______ of f.
**Answer: antiderivative.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** ∫f(x)dx = F(x) + ______.
**Answer: C.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Replacing the inner function by u is integration by ______.
**Answer: substitution.**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** The definite integral is F(b) − F(a) by the ______ formula.
**Answer: Newton–Leibniz.**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** Spinning a curve round an axis makes a solid of ______.
**Answer: revolution.**
