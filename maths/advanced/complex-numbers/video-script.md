# Video script — Complex Numbers

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Try to solve x squared equals minus 1.

What number times itself is minus 1? 1 times 1 is 1. Minus 1 times minus 1 is 1. No real number works!

So the books invent a new number. Call it i. i squared is minus 1. That is the whole invention.

Now a number can have two parts: a real part and an imaginary part. 3 plus 4i. Real part 3, imaginary part 4.

Draw it! Across is real, up is imaginary. 3 across, 4 up. There it is — a point on the page. That picture is the Argand diagram.

Now you. Where is 1 plus 2i? One across, two up. Point at it!
