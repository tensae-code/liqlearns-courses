# Complex Numbers — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Level 1)** i² = ______.
a) 1  b) −1  c) 0
**Answer: b) −1** — the definition of the imaginary unit.

**2. (Level 1)** In 3 + 4i, the imaginary part is ______.
a) 3  b) 4  c) 7
**Answer: b) 4.**

**3. (Level 2)** The conjugate of 1 + 2i is ______.
a) 1 − 2i  b) −1 + 2i  c) 1 + 2i
**Answer: a) 1 − 2i** (Mauritania, 7AS-D, Chapter 1).

**4. (Level 2)** (2 + i) + (3 − i) = ______.
a) 5  b) 5 + 2i  c) 6
**Answer: a) 5** — the imaginary parts cancel.

**5. (Level 3)** The modulus of 3 + 4i is ______.
a) 7  b) 5  c) 25
**Answer: b) 5** — √(9 + 16).

**6. (Level 3)** De Moivre's theorem says [r(cos θ + i sin θ)]² = ______.
a) r²(cos 2θ + i sin 2θ)  b) r(cos 2θ + i sin 2θ)  c) r²(cos θ + i sin θ)
**Answer: a) r²(cos 2θ + i sin 2θ)** — the modulus is squared and the argument doubled.
