# Complex Numbers — matching

Match each left side to its right side.

**Set (Level 1)** — parts
1. 3 + 4i — **real part 3, imaginary part 4**
2. i — **the imaginary unit**
3. i² — **−1**
4. z̄ — **the conjugate of z**

**Set (Level 2)** — operations
1. (a + bi) + (c + di) — **(a + c) + (b + d)i**
2. conjugate of 1 + 2i — **1 − 2i**
3. (1 + i)(1 − i) — **2**

**Set (Level 3)** — geometry
1. modulus — **distance from zero**
2. argument — **angle from the real axis**
3. trigonometric form — **r(cos θ + i sin θ)**
4. locus |z| = 2 — **a circle of radius 2**
