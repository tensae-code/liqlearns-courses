# Complex Numbers — short answer

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Level 1)** Write i⁵ in its simplest form.
**Answer:** i (the cycle i, −1, −i, 1 repeats).

**2. (Level 1)** What are the real and imaginary parts of −2 + 5i?
**Answer:** Real part −2, imaginary part 5.

**3. (Level 2)** Multiply (1 + i)(1 − i).
**Answer:** 2 (1 − i² = 1 + 1).

**4. (Level 2)** What is the conjugate of 5i?
**Answer:** −5i (Mauritania, 7AS-D, Chapter 1).

**5. (Level 3)** A complex number has modulus 2 and argument 30°. Write it in trigonometric form.
**Answer:** 2(cos 30° + i sin 30°).
