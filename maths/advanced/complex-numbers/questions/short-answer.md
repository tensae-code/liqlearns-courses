# Complex Numbers — short answer

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — The Imaginary Unit and a + bi · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Operations and Conjugates · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Trigonometric Form, De Moivre and Loci.

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Write i⁵ in its simplest form.
**Answer:** i (the cycle i, −1, −i, 1 repeats).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What are the real and imaginary parts of −2 + 5i?
**Answer:** Real part −2, imaginary part 5.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Multiply (1 + i)(1 − i).
**Answer:** 2 (1 − i² = 1 + 1).

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** What is the conjugate of 5i?
**Answer:** −5i (Mauritania, 7AS-D, Chapter 1).

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** A complex number has modulus 2 and argument 30°. Write it in trigonometric form.
**Answer:** 2(cos 30° + i sin 30°).
