# Complex Numbers — true or false

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Level 1)** x² = −1 has a real solution.
**False** — no real number squared is negative; that is why i is invented.

**2. (Level 1)** i⁴ = 1.
**True** — the powers of i cycle every four.

**3. (Level 2)** The conjugate of a sum is the sum of the conjugates.
**True** — Mauritania, 7AS-D, Chapter 1.

**4. (Level 2)** To divide by a complex number, multiply top and bottom by its conjugate.
**True** — the bottom becomes the real number a² + b².

**5. (Level 3)** The modulus of 3 + 4i is 7.
**False** — it is 5, the distance √(9 + 16).

**6. (Level 3)** The cubic roots of unity are the three numbers whose cube is 1.
**True** — Egypt, Pure Grade 12, Lesson 2-3.
