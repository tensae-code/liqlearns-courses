# Complex Numbers — fill in the blank

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — The Imaginary Unit and a + bi · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Operations and Conjugates · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Trigonometric Form, De Moivre and Loci.

Fill each blank with a word from the box.

**Box:** imaginary · conjugate · modulus · argument · De Moivre · locus

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The imaginary unit i satisfies i² = ______.
**Answer: −1.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A complex number is written a + ______.
**Answer: bi.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The ______ of a + bi is a − bi.
**Answer: conjugate.**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** The distance of z from zero is its ______.
**Answer: modulus.**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** The angle a complex number makes with the real axis is its ______.
**Answer: argument.**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** De ______'s theorem raises the modulus to the power and multiplies the argument.
**Answer: Moivre.**
