# Complex Numbers — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with a word from the box.

**Box:** imaginary · conjugate · modulus · argument · De Moivre · locus

**1. (Level 1)** The imaginary unit i satisfies i² = ______.
**Answer: −1.**

**2. (Level 1)** A complex number is written a + ______.
**Answer: bi.**

**3. (Level 2)** The ______ of a + bi is a − bi.
**Answer: conjugate.**

**4. (Level 3)** The distance of z from zero is its ______.
**Answer: modulus.**

**5. (Level 3)** The angle a complex number makes with the real axis is its ______.
**Answer: argument.**

**6. (Level 3)** De ______'s theorem raises the modulus to the power and multiplies the argument.
**Answer: Moivre.**
