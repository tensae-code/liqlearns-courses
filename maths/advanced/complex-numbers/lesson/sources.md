**Acceptance count: taught in 12 countries (Egypt, Jordan, Mauritania, Laos, Yemen, Indonesia, South Sudan, Iraq, Cambodia, Kuwait, China, Pakistan).** Egypt carries the subject from its first lesson (the imaginary unit, the algebraic form, powers of i) to its Grade 12 finish (trigonometric form, De Moivre, cubic roots of unity); Jordan carries complex numbers, their operations and Argand-plane loci; Mauritania carries complex numbers in two chapters of its C stream and one chapter of its D stream, with conjugates worked in full. Indonesia carries complex numbers in Grade 11 Tingkat Lanjut — the algebraic form, the complex plane, operations, conjugate, modulus and argument — but stops there: no trigonometric form, no De Moivre, no polar-form powers or roots. Iraq carries complex numbers whole in a single full chapter in Grade 12 Scientific — the need to extend R, operations on C, the conjugate, square roots of a complex number, solving quadratics in C, cube roots of unity, geometric representation, polar form and De Moivre's theorem, with worked examples verified in the filing (ex. 3 p. 10; ex. 6 p. 13; ex. 9–10 pp. 17–19; ex. 14–15 pp. 21–22; ex. 16–17 pp. 24–25; ex. 19–21 pp. 29–30; ex. 22 p. 34; ex. 23 p. 37; ex. 26 p. 40; ex. 28 p. 43; ex. 31 pp. 45–46). The Literary track never meets complex numbers. Cambodia carries complex numbers whole — the imaginary unit in Grade 10 Part 1, the algebraic form, operations, modulus, conjugate, trigonometric form, De Moivre's theorem and nth powers and roots in Grade 11 Advanced, and the same ground again in the private Khem Putthi Complex Numbers booklet, since the Grade 12 MoEYS books teach no complex numbers at all; the exponential form z = re^(iθ) and square roots of a complex number as a named method are the only parts not taught anywhere in the set. Kuwait carries complex numbers whole in Grade 11 Term 2 (scientific track) — the algebraic form, the Argand plane, modulus and conjugate, the polar (trigonometric) form, and solving equations including square roots of complex numbers and Δ < 0 quadratics; it stops there (no De Moivre theorem and no nth roots).

**Honest limit:** only nine read countries teach complex numbers at all. Sierra Leone's SSS never teaches them; Libya has no complex numbers anywhere; Vietnam teaches none; Greece teaches none; Kazakhstan's set tops out at Grade 9; Armenia teaches none; Mauritania's 3AS/4AS streams teach none.
