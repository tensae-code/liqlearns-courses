# Limits — matching

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — The Limit Idea and One-Sided Limits · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Computing Limits · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Infinity, Sequences, the Sandwich Theorem and ε-δ.

Match each item on the left with the one right item on the right.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Match: (i) lim(x→a⁻) f(x); (ii) lim(x→a⁺) f(x); (iii) lim(x→∞) f(x). Meanings: (a) as x runs away to infinity; (b) from the left; (c) from the right.
**Answer:** (i)–(b), (ii)–(c), (iii)–(a) — the signs and the ∞ name the direction of approach.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Match the method: (i) (x² − 9)/(x − 3); (ii) (√x − 2)/(x − 4); (iii) x³ + 1 at x = 2. Methods: (a) direct substitution; (b) factorise and cancel; (c) multiply by the conjugate.
**Answer:** (i)–(b), (ii)–(c), (iii)–(a) — each 0/0 is removed its own way; the polynomial needs no removal.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Match the value: (i) lim(x→∞) 1/x; (ii) lim(θ→0) sin θ/θ; (iii) lim(x→0) 1/x². Values: (a) 1; (b) ∞; (c) 0.
**Answer:** (i)–(c), (ii)–(a), (iii)–(b) — settling, the standard trig limit, and running away.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Match the country to its way: (i) Greece; (ii) Armenia; (iii) South Sudan. Ways: (a) sequence limits from infinitesimals, reaching e; (b) a full Analysis chapter on limits; (c) estimating limits from tables and graphs first.
**Answer:** (i)–(b), (ii)–(a), (iii)–(c) — Greece W12 Ch.1; Armenia G11 Ch.3; South Sudan S4 Additional pp.3–13.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Match: (i) indeterminate; (ii) convergent; (iii) asymptote. Meanings: (a) a line a graph settles towards or runs away beside; (b) a sequence with a limit; (c) a form like 0/0 that needs more work.
**Answer:** (i)–(c), (ii)–(b), (iii)–(a).

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** Match the corrected value: (i) sin x/x at 37π/7; (ii) cos x/x at 100; (iii) cos x/x at 450. Values: (a) +0.00862; (b) −0.00162256; (c) ≈ −0.0471.
**Answer:** (i)–(c), (ii)–(a), (iii)–(b) — Kuwait p.47, taught corrected.

**7. (Sub lesson 2)** Match the Mauritania stream to its limits chapter: (i) 6AS-D; (ii) 7AS-D; (iii) 7AS-LO. Chapters: (a) Ch.2 "Limite, continuité et dérivation", p.23; (b) Ch.IV "Limites et continuité d'une fonction numérique", p.64; (c) Ch.2 — limits, comparisons and infinite branches, p.24.
**Answer:** (i)–(b), (ii)–(a), (iii)–(c) — each stream pairs limits with continuity, in its own chapter shape.
