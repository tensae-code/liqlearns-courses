# Limits — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — The Limit Idea and One-Sided Limits · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Computing Limits · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Infinity, Sequences, the Sandwich Theorem and ε-δ.

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** lim(x→1) (x² − 1)/(x − 1) equals ______.
a) 0  b) 1  c) 2
**Answer:** c) 2 — factorise and cancel: the expression is x + 1 for x ≠ 1.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The left-hand limit lim(x→a⁻) f(x) is the value f approaches as x comes to a from ______.
a) above  b) the left  c) infinity
**Answer:** b) the left — the minus sign marks the left side (Laos, Grade 10, pp.166–175).

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** lim(x→2) (4x³ + x − 4) equals ______.
a) 30  b) 34  c) 26
**Answer:** a) 30 — direct substitution: 32 + 2 − 4.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A 0/0 result on substitution means the limit ______.
a) is 0  b) is 1  c) is indeterminate — more work is needed
**Answer:** c) indeterminate — factorise, cancel or rationalise (Iran, Hesaban (1), p.141).

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** lim(x→∞) 1/xⁿ for n > 0 equals ______.
a) 0  b) 1  c) ∞
**Answer:** a) 0 — the workhorse limit at infinity.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** lim(θ→0) sin θ/θ (θ in radians) equals ______.
a) 0  b) 1  c) it does not exist
**Answer:** b) 1 — proved by the sandwich theorem (Pakistan, Class 12, Ch.1).

**7. (Sub lesson 2)** lim(x→∞) (2x + 1)/(x − 3) equals ______.
a) 0  b) 2  c) ∞
**Answer:** b) 2 — degrees equal, so the limit is the ratio of the leading coefficients, 2/1 (Mauritania, 6AS-D, Ch.IV, p.64).

**8. (Sub lesson 2)** In Jordan's Grade 11 exercise book (workbook evidence), the limits-and-continuity exercises sit in the same unit as ______.
a) differentiation  b) integration  c) vectors
**Answer:** a) differentiation — Unit 3 is Limits and Derivatives, limits/continuity set at p.46 (Jordan, Grade 11 Term 1 Exercise Book, orphan workbook).

**9. (Sub lesson 2)** lim(x→2) (x² − 4)/(x − 2) equals ______.
a) 0  b) 2  c) 4
**Answer:** c) 4 — factorise and cancel to x + 2, then substitute (Jordan, Grade 11 Term 1 Student Book, Limits and continuity, p.50; its companion Exercise Book, limits/continuity set, p.34).
