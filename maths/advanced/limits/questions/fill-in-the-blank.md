# Limits — fill in the blank

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — The Limit Idea and One-Sided Limits · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Computing Limits · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Infinity, Sequences, the Sandwich Theorem and ε-δ.

Fill each blank with the one right word or number.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** lim(x→a) f(x) = L describes where the values of f ______ as x gets close to a.
**Answer:** head / settle — the limit is the value approached, not necessarily the value at a.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The two-sided limit exists only when the left-hand and right-hand limits are ______.
**Answer:** equal — the existence condition (Laos, Grade 10, pp.166–175).

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The limit of a sum is the ______ of the limits.
**Answer:** sum — one of the limit theorems (Pakistan, Class 11, Unit 12).

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** To remove a 0/0 form with square roots, multiply top and bottom by the ______.
**Answer:** conjugate — rationalising, as Egypt's Pure Grade 11 teaches (Unit 3).

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** A sequence that has a limit is called ______.
**Answer:** convergent — one without a limit is divergent (Cambodia, Grade 12 Advanced, Ch.1).

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** In the ε-δ definition, for every ε > 0 there must be a ______ > 0 that works.
**Answer:** δ (delta) — |x − a| < δ forces |f(x) − L| < ε (Cambodia, Grade 12 Basic, §1.1).

**7. (Sub lesson 1)** In Mauritania's 6AS-M and 6AS-C books, limits are taught in the same chapter as ______.
**Answer:** continuity — Ch.3 is "Fonctions: généralités – limite – continuité" in both books (p.31).

**8. (Sub lesson 2)** A function is continuous at x = a when its limit as x → a equals ______.
**Answer:** f(a) — the limit must meet the function's own value at the point (Jordan, Grade 11 Term 1 Student Book, Limits and continuity, p.50; its companion Exercise Book, limits/continuity set, p.34).
