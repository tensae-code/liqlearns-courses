Libya teaches indeterminate values, one-sided limits and limits at infinity in its Grade 11 Science limits chapter. Vietnam teaches limits of sequences then limits of functions in Grade 11. Armenia builds sequence limits from infinitesimals and reaches the number e. Iran teaches limits intuitively with no ε-δ in Hesaban (1), then infinite limits and asymptotes in Hesaban (2). Egypt teaches limits numerically, graphically and algebraically, including trigonometric limits, in Pure Grade 11. Cambodia teaches sequence limits in Grade 12 Advanced, the ε-δ definition in Grade 12 Basic, and formal proofs by definition in its PRIVATE Khem Putthi Limits and Continuity booklet. Kuwait teaches limits, limits involving ±∞, indeterminate forms and trigonometric limits in Grade 12 Term 1, with its p.47 table signs taught corrected. Laos teaches the intuitive definition, one-sided limits and the existence condition in Grade 10. South Sudan teaches the full limits unit — estimation, theorems, one-sided limits, limits at infinity and the squeeze theorem — in Secondary 4 Additional. Indonesia teaches limits as a standalone chapter, including left/right limits, indeterminate forms and trigonometric limits, in Grade 12 Tingkat Lanjut. Mauritania teaches limits with continuity as one chapter across its senior streams — 6AS-M and 6AS-C (Ch.3, p.31), 6AS-D (Ch.IV, p.64) and 6AS-LO (Ch.3, p.28) — and returns to limits inside the final-year functions study in 7AS-C (Ch.5, p.63), 7AS-D (Ch.2, p.23), 7AS-LM (Ch.2, p.23) and 7AS-LO (Ch.2, p.24, with comparisons and infinite branches). Syria's limit theory is the core of Grade 12 Part One's functions unit — ε-definitions at infinity and at a point, the reference limits, the operations tables with the four indeterminate forms, and the squeeze and comparison theorems — with the sequence-limit theory stated alongside it.
