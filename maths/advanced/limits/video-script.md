# Video script — Limits

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Walk towards a wall. Half the way. Half again. Half again.
You get CLOSE — closer and closer — and the steps settle near the wall.

That is a **limit**. Not where you land. Where you are HEADING.

Take (x² − 1)/(x − 1). At x = 1 you cannot use it — it is 0 over 0.
But come close: x = 0.9 gives 1.9. x = 0.99 gives 1.99. x = 0.999 gives 1.999.
The values head for **2**. The limit is 2 — even though x = 1 itself is missing!

Come from the left. Come from the right. Both sides must agree,
or there is no limit at all.

And a sequence: 1/2, 1/4, 1/8 … heads for 0. 1, 1/2, 1/3 … heads for 0.
A list of numbers can head for one number — and that number is its limit.

Now you. Make a table. Come close from both sides. Say where it heads.
That is a limit.
