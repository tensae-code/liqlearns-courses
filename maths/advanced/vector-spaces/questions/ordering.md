1. (Sub lesson 2) Put the working order for two subspaces given by conditions in the right order:
   (a) Apply Grassmann to get dim(F + G) (b) Solve both condition sets together to find F ∩ G (c) Read dim(F ∩ G) off the solution set (d) Check whether the intersection is {0} to judge directness
   **Answer: b, c, a, d.** Rwanda, Advanced Mathematics, Learner's Book, Senior Six, Unit 6, printed pp. 331–344.
