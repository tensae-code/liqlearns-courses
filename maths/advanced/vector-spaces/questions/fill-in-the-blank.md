# Fill in the blank — Vector Spaces

1. (Layer 1) A non-empty subset of R³ is a subspace if it is closed under addition and under ___ multiplication.
   **Answer: scalar.** Rwanda, Advanced Mathematics, Learner's Book, Senior Five, Unit 6, printed pp. 180–186.

2. (Layer 2) Grassmann's formula reads dim(F + G) = dim F + dim G − dim(___).
   **Answer: F ∩ G (the intersection).** Rwanda, Advanced Mathematics, Learner's Book, Senior Six, Unit 6, printed pp. 342–343.

3. (Layer 3) The number of vectors in a basis of a space is called its ___.
   **Answer: dimension.** Rwanda, Advanced Mathematics, Learner's Book, Senior Five, Unit 6, printed pp. 194–196.
