# Fill in the blank — Vector Spaces

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Layer 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Subspaces · (Layer 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Sums and Dimensions · (Layer 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Basis and Coordinates.

1. (Sub lesson 1) A non-empty subset of R³ is a subspace if it is closed under addition and under ___ multiplication.
   **Answer: scalar.** Rwanda, Advanced Mathematics, Learner's Book, Senior Five, Unit 6, printed pp. 180–186.

2. (Sub lesson 2) Grassmann's formula reads dim(F + G) = dim F + dim G − dim(___).
   **Answer: F ∩ G (the intersection).** Rwanda, Advanced Mathematics, Learner's Book, Senior Six, Unit 6, printed pp. 342–343.

3. (Sub lesson 3) The number of vectors in a basis of a space is called its ___.
   **Answer: dimension.** Rwanda, Advanced Mathematics, Learner's Book, Senior Five, Unit 6, printed pp. 194–196.
