# Matching — Vector Spaces

1. (Layer 2) Match each construction to its description:
   (a) F ∩ G (b) F ∪ G (c) F + G (d) F ⊕ G
   Descriptions: the sum when the intersection is {0}, with unique decompositions; all vectors satisfying both spaces' conditions — always a subspace; all sums u + v — the smallest subspace containing both; a subspace only when one space contains the other.
   **Answer: (a) all vectors satisfying both conditions — always a subspace, (b) a subspace only when one contains the other, (c) all sums u + v — the smallest subspace containing both, (d) the sum when the intersection is {0}, with unique decompositions.** Rwanda, Advanced Mathematics, Learner's Book, Senior Five, Unit 6, printed pp. 186–190; Rwanda, Advanced Mathematics, Learner's Book, Senior Six, Unit 6, printed pp. 331–344.
