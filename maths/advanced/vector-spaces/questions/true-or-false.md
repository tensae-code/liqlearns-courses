# True or false — Vector Spaces

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Layer 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Subspaces · (Layer 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Sums and Dimensions · (Layer 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Basis and Coordinates.

1. (Sub lesson 1) The intersection of two subspaces is always a subspace. **True.** Rwanda, Advanced Mathematics, Learner's Book, Senior Five, Unit 6, printed pp. 186–188.
2. (Sub lesson 1) Every subspace contains the zero vector. **True** — closure under scalar multiplication with scalar 0 forces it. Rwanda, Advanced Mathematics, Learner's Book, Senior Five, Unit 6, printed pp. 180–186.
3. (Sub lesson 2) If F ∩ G = {0}, every vector of F + G can be written as u + v (u in F, v in G) in exactly one way. **True** — that uniqueness is what makes the sum direct. Rwanda, Advanced Mathematics, Learner's Book, Senior Six, Unit 6, printed pp. 343–344.
