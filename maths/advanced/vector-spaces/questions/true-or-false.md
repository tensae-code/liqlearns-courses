# True or false — Vector Spaces

1. (Layer 1) The intersection of two subspaces is always a subspace. **True.** Rwanda, Advanced Mathematics, Learner's Book, Senior Five, Unit 6, printed pp. 186–188.
2. (Layer 1) Every subspace contains the zero vector. **True** — closure under scalar multiplication with scalar 0 forces it. Rwanda, Advanced Mathematics, Learner's Book, Senior Five, Unit 6, printed pp. 180–186.
3. (Layer 2) If F ∩ G = {0}, every vector of F + G can be written as u + v (u in F, v in G) in exactly one way. **True** — that uniqueness is what makes the sum direct. Rwanda, Advanced Mathematics, Learner's Book, Senior Six, Unit 6, printed pp. 343–344.
