## Sub lesson 1 — Subspaces
Test a set for being a subspace by closure under addition and scalar multiplication; state that an intersection of subspaces is a subspace and that a union is one only when one contains the other. Pass mark: 4 of 5.

## Sub lesson 2 — Sums and Dimensions
Form the sum of two subspaces, apply the Grassmann formula, recognise a direct sum by its trivial intersection and unique decompositions, and find an intersection — and its dimension — by solving systems. Pass mark: 5 of 6.

## Sub lesson 3 — Basis and Coordinates
Decide independence, build combinations and spans, identify a basis, read off a dimension, and write a vector's coordinates relative to a given basis. Pass mark: 4 of 5.

## What "maxed" means here

The learner can take two subspaces of R³ given by conditions, find their intersection by solving the system, their sum and both dimensions by Grassmann, say whether the sum is direct and whether a complement exists — and can move freely between a set of vectors, its span, its independence, a basis and coordinates, as Rwanda's two Advanced books together require.
