---

Every complex number is a point. And every point has TWO numbers that find it.

How FAR from zero — that is r. And what ANGLE — that is theta. Distance and angle: r and theta. That is the trigonometric form!

Now multiply two numbers in this form. The distances MULTIPLY. The angles ADD. Multiply — distances multiply, angles add. That is the whole secret!

So raise a number to the power n — multiply it by itself n times. The distance goes to the power n. And the angle is multiplied by n. That is DE MOIVRE'S THEOREM: r to the n, angle n times theta!

Run it BACKWARD and you get roots. Every number has exactly n nth roots — n answers, standing evenly around a circle, like the numbers on a clock.

The cube roots of 8? Distance 2, angles 0, 120 and 240 degrees. The answers: 2, minus 1 plus i root 3, minus 1 minus i root 3. Three roots, evenly spaced!

Now you. One plus i. Distance root 2, angle 45 degrees. Say its trigonometric form!
