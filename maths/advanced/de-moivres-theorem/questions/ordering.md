# De Moivre's Theorem — ordering

Put each list in the right order.

**1. (Level 1)** Order the steps for the trig form of √3 + i: (a) find θ = 30° from the point's quadrant, (b) plot the point, (c) find r = 2, (d) write 2(cos 30° + i sin 30°).
**Answer:** (b), (c), (a), (d) — plot, modulus, argument, write (South Sudan, Additional Book 4, pp.182–184).

**2. (Level 2)** Order (1 + i)⁸ by De Moivre: (a) get 16(cos 360° + i sin 360°) = 16, (b) write 1 + i = √2(cos 45° + i sin 45°), (c) raise: (√2)⁸ and 8 × 45°.
**Answer:** (b), (c), (a) — form first, then the power, then the value (Laos, Grade 12, Lesson 13, p.134).

**3. (Level 3)** Order the steps for the cube roots of 8: (a) list the roots 2, −1 + i√3, −1 − i√3, (b) take the cube root of the modulus: 2, (c) write 8 = 8(cos 0° + i sin 0°), (d) share the angles: 0°, 120°, 240°.
**Answer:** (c), (b), (d), (a) — form, root the modulus, share the angles, list (South Sudan, Additional Book 4, pp.190–195, taught corrected).

**4. (Level 3)** Order South Sudan's Unit 6: (a) roots of complex numbers, (b) the Argand diagram, (c) De Moivre's theorem, (d) polar form.
**Answer:** (b), (d), (c), (a) — pp.178–181, 182–184, 185–189, 190–195 (South Sudan, Additional Book 4, Unit 6).
