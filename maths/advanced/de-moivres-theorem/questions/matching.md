**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Match the number to its trig form: (i) 1 + i, (ii) √3 + i, (iii) −1 + i√3 — with (a) 2(cos 120° + i sin 120°), (b) √2(cos 45° + i sin 45°), (c) 2(cos 30° + i sin 30°).
**Answer:** (i)–(b), (ii)–(c), (iii)–(a) — modulus first, argument by quadrant (South Sudan, Additional Book 4, pp.182–184).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Match the operation to its rule: (i) multiply, (ii) divide, (iii) raise to power n — with (a) modulus to the n, angle times n, (b) moduli multiply, angles add, (c) moduli divide, angles subtract.
**Answer:** (i)–(b), (ii)–(c), (iii)–(a) — the trig-form rules (Kuwait, Lesson 7-2; Laos, Lesson 13, p.134).

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Match the identity to its corrected value: (i) cos 3θ, (ii) sin 3θ — with (a) 3 sin θ − 4 sin³θ, (b) 4cos³θ − 3cosθ.
**Answer:** (i)–(b), (ii)–(a) — both taught corrected as filed (South Sudan, Additional Book 4, pp.185–189).
