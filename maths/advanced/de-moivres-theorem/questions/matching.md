# De Moivre's Theorem — matching

Match each item on the left with the one right partner on the right.

**1. (Level 1)** Match the number to its trig form: (i) 1 + i, (ii) √3 + i, (iii) −1 + i√3 — with (a) 2(cos 120° + i sin 120°), (b) √2(cos 45° + i sin 45°), (c) 2(cos 30° + i sin 30°).
**Answer:** (i)–(b), (ii)–(c), (iii)–(a) — modulus first, argument by quadrant (South Sudan, Additional Book 4, pp.182–184).

**2. (Level 2)** Match the operation to its rule: (i) multiply, (ii) divide, (iii) raise to power n — with (a) modulus to the n, angle times n, (b) moduli multiply, angles add, (c) moduli divide, angles subtract.
**Answer:** (i)–(b), (ii)–(c), (iii)–(a) — the trig-form rules (Kuwait, Lesson 7-2; Laos, Lesson 13, p.134).

**3. (Level 2)** Match the identity to its corrected value: (i) cos 3θ, (ii) sin 3θ — with (a) 3 sin θ − 4 sin³θ, (b) 4cos³θ − 3cosθ.
**Answer:** (i)–(b), (ii)–(a) — both taught corrected as filed (South Sudan, Additional Book 4, pp.185–189).

**4. (Level 3)** Match the book to what it teaches: (i) South Sudan Additional Book 4, (ii) Laos Grade 12, (iii) Kuwait Grade 11 Term 2 — with (a) trig form and division only, no De Moivre, no roots, (b) trig form, De Moivre's formula, nth roots, Lesson 13, (c) polar form, De Moivre and roots, Unit 6.
**Answer:** (i)–(c), (ii)–(b), (iii)–(a) — Kuwait's limit stated plainly, as filed.
