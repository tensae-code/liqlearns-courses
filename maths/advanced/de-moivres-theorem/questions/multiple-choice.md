# De Moivre's Theorem — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — The Trigonometric (Polar) Form · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Multiplication, Division and De Moivre Powers · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Nth Roots.

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The trigonometric form of 1 + i is ______.
a) √2(cos 45° + i sin 45°)  b) 2(cos 45° + i sin 45°)  c) √2(cos 30° + i sin 30°)
**Answer:** a) √2(cos 45° + i sin 45°) — modulus √2, argument 45° (South Sudan, Additional Book 4, pp.182–184).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The modulus and argument of √3 + i are ______.
a) r = 2, θ = 60°  b) r = 2, θ = 30°  c) r = 4, θ = 30°
**Answer:** b) r = 2, θ = 30° — √(3 + 1) = 2 and tan θ = 1/√3 (Laos, Grade 12, Lesson 13, p.134).

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** When two complex numbers in trig form are multiplied, ______.
a) moduli add, arguments add  b) moduli multiply, arguments multiply  c) moduli multiply, arguments add
**Answer:** c) moduli multiply, arguments add — the rule De Moivre is built from (South Sudan, Additional Book 4, pp.185–189).

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** 6(cos 60° + i sin 60°) ÷ 2(cos 20° + i sin 20°) is ______.
a) 3(cos 40° + i sin 40°)  b) 3(cos 80° + i sin 80°)  c) 12(cos 40° + i sin 40°)
**Answer:** a) 3(cos 40° + i sin 40°) — moduli divide, arguments subtract (Kuwait, Grade 11 Term 2, Lesson 7-2, pp.25–32).

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** By De Moivre, (cos 30° + i sin 30°)³ is ______.
a) cos 30° + i sin 30°  b) i  c) −1
**Answer:** b) i — cos 90° + i sin 90° = i (Laos, Grade 12, Lesson 13, p.134).

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** sin 3θ, taught corrected, is ______.
a) 3 sin θ + 4 sin³θ  b) 3 sin θ − 4 sin³θ  c) 4 sin³θ − 3 sin θ
**Answer:** b) 3 sin θ − 4 sin³θ — the guide's printed plus-sign version is wrong, as filed (South Sudan, taught corrected).

**7. (Sub lesson 3)** The cube roots of 8, taught corrected, are ______.
a) 2 only  b) 2, 2i, −2i  c) 2, −1 + i√3, −1 − i√3
**Answer:** c) 2, −1 + i√3, −1 − i√3 — the book prints them wrong on p.194; these are the filed corrections (South Sudan, Additional Book 4, p.194, taught corrected).

**8. (Sub lesson 3)** A complex number has exactly how many fifth roots?
a) 1  b) 5  c) 10
**Answer:** b) 5 — exactly n nth roots, evenly spaced (South Sudan, Additional Book 4, pp.190–195).
