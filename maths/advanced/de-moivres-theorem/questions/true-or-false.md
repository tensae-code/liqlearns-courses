# De Moivre's Theorem — true or false

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — The Trigonometric (Polar) Form · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Multiplication, Division and De Moivre Powers · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Nth Roots.

Say true or false, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** 1 + i has modulus 2.
**Answer:** False — its modulus is √2; 2 is the modulus of √3 + i (South Sudan, Additional Book 4, pp.182–184).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A trig form in Kuwait's book may use a negative r.
**Answer:** False — Kuwait requires r ≥ 0 and an argument in [0, 2π) (Kuwait, Grade 11 Term 2, Lesson 7-2).

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** (cos 30° + i sin 30°)³ = i.
**Answer:** True — De Moivre gives cos 90° + i sin 90° = i (Laos, Grade 12, Lesson 13, p.134).

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** sin 3θ = 3 sin θ + 4 sin³θ, as a South Sudan guide line prints it.
**Answer:** False — taught corrected: sin 3θ = 3 sin θ − 4 sin³θ (South Sudan, taught corrected, as filed).

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** The only cube root of 8 is 2.
**Answer:** False — there are three: 2, −1 + i√3, −1 − i√3, taught corrected (South Sudan, Additional Book 4, p.194, taught corrected).

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** Kuwait's Grade 11 book teaches nth roots of complex numbers.
**Answer:** False — Kuwait teaches NO nth roots and NO De Moivre; trig form and division in trig form only, as filed.
