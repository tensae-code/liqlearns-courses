# De Moivre's Theorem — short answer

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Level 1)** In South Sudan's polar form, x and y are written with r and θ how?
**Answer:** x = r cos θ, y = r sin θ (South Sudan, Additional Book 4, pp.182–184).

**2. (Level 1)** What two conditions does Kuwait's book put on a proper trig form?
**Answer:** r ≥ 0 and an argument in [0, 2π) (Kuwait, Grade 11 Term 2, Lesson 7-2, pp.25–32).

**3. (Level 2)** State De Moivre's theorem.
**Answer:** [r(cos θ + i sin θ)]ⁿ = rⁿ(cos nθ + i sin nθ) (Laos, Grade 12, Lesson 13, p.134).

**4. (Level 2)** Find (1 + i)⁸ by De Moivre.
**Answer:** 16 — (√2)⁸ = 16 and 8 × 45° = 360° (South Sudan, Additional Book 4, pp.185–189).

**5. (Level 2)** State cos 3θ as South Sudan's book derives it.
**Answer:** cos 3θ = 4cos³θ − 3cosθ — the result is cited, not the faulty printed working (South Sudan, Additional Book 4, pp.185–189).

**6. (Level 3)** Find all the fourth roots of 16.
**Answer:** 2, 2i, −2, −2i — modulus 2, angles 0°, 90°, 180°, 270° (South Sudan, Additional Book 4, pp.190–195).

**7. (Level 3)** How far apart, in angle, do the cube roots of a number stand?
**Answer:** 120° apart — 360° shared among 3 roots (Laos, Grade 12, Lesson 13, p.134).

**8. (Level 3)** Does Kuwait's book teach De Moivre's theorem or nth roots?
**Answer:** No — Kuwait teaches the trig form and division in trig form only; NO De Moivre, NO nth roots (Kuwait, Grade 11 Term 2, Lesson 7-2).
