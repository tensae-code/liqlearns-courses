# De Moivre's Theorem — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the missing word or number.

**1. (Level 1)** In z = r(cos θ + i sin θ), r is called the ____.
**Answer:** modulus — the distance from zero (South Sudan, Additional Book 4, pp.182–184).

**2. (Level 1)** The angle θ in the trig form is called the ____.
**Answer:** argument — measured from the positive real axis (Kuwait, Grade 11 Term 2, Lesson 7-2, pp.25–32).

**3. (Level 2)** In division in trig form, the moduli divide and the arguments ____.
**Answer:** subtract — the quotient's angle is the difference (Kuwait, Grade 11 Term 2, Lesson 7-2).

**4. (Level 2)** By De Moivre, raising to the power n multiplies the argument by ____.
**Answer:** n — and raises the modulus to the power n (Laos, Grade 12, Lesson 13, p.134).

**5. (Level 3)** The nth roots of a number all have modulus ____.
**Answer:** ⁿ√r — the nth root of the original modulus (South Sudan, Additional Book 4, pp.190–195).

**6. (Level 3)** South Sudan's book also gives roots in the exponential form z = ____.
**Answer:** re^(iθ) — the exponential form of a complex number (South Sudan, Additional Book 4, pp.190–195).
