# De Moivre's Theorem — questions by level

Practice by level, tied to the skill number. The questions live in
the type files; this file is the by-level view. The levels are the
depths inside this one thing — De Moivre's theorem — the trigonometric form first, then multiplication, division and powers, then nth roots.

## Level 1 — the trigonometric (polar) form
Level 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: find a modulus and an argument, and write a complex number in trigonometric form.

## Level 2 — multiplication, division and De Moivre powers
Level 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: multiply and divide in trig form, and use De Moivre's theorem for powers and for the multiple-angle identities.

## Level 3 — nth roots
Level 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: find all nth roots of a complex number, give the corrected cube roots of 8, and describe the roots' spacing on a circle.
