**Acceptance count: taught in 3 countries (Pakistan, Syria, Rwanda).** Pakistan was the sole full source for this lesson when it was built: its Class 12 book teaches power-series expansion of functions in one section, §2.17 of the differentiation chapter (ch-pp.74–81) — the Maclaurin expansions of 1/(1+x), sin x, aˣ, eˣ and (1+x)ⁿ (ch-pp.74–78), then Taylor series, with ln(1+x) about x = 2 and the numerical computation sin 31° ≈ 0.5150 (ch-pp.78–81). No other read book then taught the topic as a full treatment; Rwanda's Senior Six Advanced book has since added a full treatment of its own (next paragraph), and the lesson stands at that honest size rather than padded from other books' series work, which belongs to the sequences-and-series lesson.

Rwanda teaches Taylor and Maclaurin series whole in Advanced Mathematics Senior Six Unit 3 — Taylor's formula with the Lagrange remainder, the standard Maclaurin series, and numerical estimation of e, π, roots and logarithms from series.

Syria's polynomial bounds for sin and cos in Grade 12 Part One are the Taylor polynomials of sin and cos at 0 in all but name, proved by comparing derivatives and used with explicit error bounds.
