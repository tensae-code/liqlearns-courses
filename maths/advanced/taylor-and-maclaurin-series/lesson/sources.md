**Acceptance count: taught in 2 countries (Pakistan, Syria).** Pakistan is the sole full source for this lesson, as filed: its Class 12 book teaches power-series expansion of functions in one section, §2.17 of the differentiation chapter (ch-pp.74–81) — the Maclaurin expansions of 1/(1+x), sin x, aˣ, eˣ and (1+x)ⁿ (ch-pp.74–78), then Taylor series, with ln(1+x) about x = 2 and the numerical computation sin 31° ≈ 0.5150 (ch-pp.78–81). No other read book teaches the topic as a full treatment, and the lesson is built at that honest size rather than padded from other books' series work, which belongs to the sequences-and-series lesson.

Syria's polynomial bounds for sin and cos in Grade 12 Part One are the Taylor polynomials of sin and cos at 0 in all but name, proved by comparing derivatives and used with explicit error bounds.
