# Taylor and Maclaurin Series — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer. All questions trace to Pakistan, Class 12, §2.17.

**1. (Level 1)** In the Maclaurin series, the coefficient of x² is ______.
a) f″(0)  b) f″(0)/2!  c) f(0)/2!
**Answer:** b) f″(0)/2! — each coefficient divides by the factorial of its power (§2.17, ch-pp.74–81).

**2. (Level 1)** The Maclaurin series of eˣ begins ______.
a) 1 + x + x² + x³  b) x + x²/2 + x³/6  c) 1 + x + x²/2! + x³/3!
**Answer:** c) 1 + x + x²/2! + x³/3! — every derivative of eˣ at 0 is 1 (§2.17, ch-pp.74–78).

**3. (Level 1)** Using 1 + 1 + 1/2 + 1/6 + 1/24, e is approximately ______.
a) 2.708  b) 2.5  c) 3.0
**Answer:** a) 2.708 — the running sum of the eˣ series at x = 1 (§2.17, ch-pp.74–78).

**4. (Level 2)** The Maclaurin series of sin x is ______.
a) x + x³/3! + x⁵/5!  b) x − x³/3! + x⁵/5! − …  c) 1 − x²/2! + x⁴/4!
**Answer:** b) x − x³/3! + x⁵/5! − … — odd powers only, signs alternating (§2.17, ch-pp.74–78).

**5. (Level 2)** The series 1 − x + x² − x³ + … for 1/(1+x) may be used when ______.
a) x is any number  b) |x| < 1  c) x > 1
**Answer:** b) |x| < 1 — outside that range the partial sums never settle (§2.17, ch-pp.74–78).

**6. (Level 2)** In the series for aˣ, the powers that appear are powers of ______.
a) a  b) x ln a  c) ln x
**Answer:** b) x ln a — aˣ = e^(x ln a) (§2.17, ch-pp.74–78).

**7. (Level 3)** A Taylor series about a = 2 is built from powers of ______.
a) x  b) x²  c) (x − 2)
**Answer:** c) (x − 2) — the centre is 2, not 0 (§2.17, ch-pp.78–81).
