# Taylor and Maclaurin Series — fill-in-the-blank

Fill in the missing number, function or word.

**1. (Level 1)** The Maclaurin series is the Taylor series centred at ___.
**Answer:** 0 (§2.17, ch-pp.74–81).

**2. (Level 1)** In eˣ = 1 + x + x²/2! + x³/___ + …, the missing denominator is 6.
**Answer:** 3! — 3! = 6 (§2.17, ch-pp.74–78).

**3. (Level 2)** sin x = x − x³/3! + x⁵/___ − …
**Answer:** 5! (§2.17, ch-pp.74–78).

**4. (Level 2)** aˣ = 1 + x ln a + (x ln a)²/___ + …
**Answer:** 2! (§2.17, ch-pp.74–78).

**5. (Level 3)** ln(1+x) about x = 2 starts ln ___ + (x−2)/3 − …
**Answer:** 3 — because 1 + x = 3 at the centre x = 2 (§2.17, ch-pp.78–81).

**6. (Level 3)** sin 31° ≈ ___, by the book's Taylor computation about 30°.
**Answer:** 0.5150 (§2.17, ch-pp.78–81).
