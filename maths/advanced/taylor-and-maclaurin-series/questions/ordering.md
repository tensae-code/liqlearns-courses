# Taylor and Maclaurin Series — ordering

Put the items in the order asked. Check each step before you write the answer.

**1. (Level 1)** Put the running totals for e in order, as terms are added one by one: 2.667, 2, 2.5, 2.708.
Answer in the order the totals appear.
**Answer:** 2, 2.5, 2.667, 2.708 — after the terms 1, 1/2, 1/6, 1/24 (§2.17, ch-pp.74–78).

**2. (Level 2)** Put the steps for building the Maclaurin series of a function in order: (i) differentiate repeatedly (ii) evaluate each derivative at 0 (iii) divide by the factorials (iv) write the powers of x in rising order.
Answer in order.
**Answer:** (i), (ii), (iii), (iv) (§2.17, ch-pp.74–81).

**3. (Level 3)** Put the steps of the book's sin 31° computation in order: (i) convert 1° to radians (ii) centre at 30° (iii) add three terms (iv) read off 0.5150.
Answer in order.
**Answer:** (ii), (i), (iii), (iv) (§2.17, ch-pp.78–81).
