# Taylor and Maclaurin Series — questions by level

Practice by level, tied to the skill number. The questions live in
the type files; this file is the by-level view. The levels are the
depths inside this one thing — power series of functions — the Maclaurin template and eˣ first, then the other standard series, then Taylor series about another point and numerical use. All questions trace to the lesson's sole full source, Pakistan Class 12 §2.17.

## Level 1 — the Maclaurin template and eˣ
Level 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: write the Maclaurin template, produce the series for eˣ, and use its first terms to estimate e.

## Level 2 — the standard Maclaurin series
Level 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: produce the series for sin x, aˣ, 1/(1+x) and (1+x)ⁿ, each with its range of x where the book requires one.

## Level 3 — Taylor series and computing numbers
Level 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: build ln(1+x) about x = 2, and compute sin 31° ≈ 0.5150 the book's way, in radians, around 30°.
