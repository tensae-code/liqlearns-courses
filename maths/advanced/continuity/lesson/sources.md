**Acceptance count: taught in 11 countries (Kuwait, Laos, Armenia, Vietnam, Libya, Pakistan, Yemen, Indonesia, Nepal, Egypt, Cambodia).** Kuwait teaches continuity, its theorems and continuity on an interval as three lessons of Grade 12 Term 1. Laos states the three conditions and works discontinuities in Grade 10. Armenia teaches continuity of a function and then of the elementary functions in Grade 11. Vietnam closes its limits chapter with continuous functions as their own lesson. Libya closes its limits chapter with continuity at a point and revisits it with differentiation. Pakistan teaches the three conditions with removable and jump discontinuities in Classes 11 and 12. Yemen teaches continuity as its own Grade 11 Science lesson and applies it to trigonometric functions in Grade 12. Indonesia states continuity as a definition in the Revisi Grade 12 book. Nepal teaches the three conditions in Grade 10 Optional, with the worked (x² + x − 6)/(x + 3) case, and its calculus stops at continuity. Egypt closes its Pure Grade 11 limits unit with continuity at a point and on an interval. Cambodia teaches continuity in Grade 12 Basic and states the three conditions, interval continuity and the Intermediate Value Theorem in its PRIVATE Khem Putthi Limits and Continuity booklet.
