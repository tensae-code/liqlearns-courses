# Continuity — short answer

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — The Three Conditions · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Removable and Jump Discontinuities · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Continuity on an Interval and the Theorems.

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** State the three conditions for continuity at a.
**Answer:** f(a) exists; lim(x→a) f(x) exists; f(a) equals that limit.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Is f(x) = 5x − 1 continuous at x = 3? Give the check in one line.
**Answer:** Yes — f(3) = 14, the limit is 14, and they are equal.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Find the value that repairs g(x) = (x² − 4)/(x − 2) at x = 2.
**Answer:** 4 — the limit is 4, so define g(2) = 4.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The function is x for x < 3 and 7 for x ≥ 3. Name the break at 3.
**Answer:** A jump — left limit 3, right limit and value 7; the sides disagree.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Find a so that x + a (x < 1) and 3x (x ≥ 1) meet continuously at 1.
**Answer:** a = 2 — left limit 1 + a must equal 3 (Cambodia, G12 Basic, §1.2 type).

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** Why is every polynomial continuous everywhere?
**Answer:** The continuity theorems: sums and products of continuous functions are continuous (Kuwait, 1-6, pp.54–60).
