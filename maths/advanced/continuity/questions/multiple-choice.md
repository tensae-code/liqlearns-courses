# Continuity — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — The Three Conditions · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Removable and Jump Discontinuities · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Continuity on an Interval and the Theorems.

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** f is continuous at a exactly when ______.
a) f(a) exists  b) the limit exists  c) f(a) exists, the limit exists, and they are equal
**Answer:** c) all three together — the three conditions (Laos, Grade 10, pp.166–175).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** For f(x) = (x² + x − 6)/(x + 3) at x = −3, the failed condition is ______.
a) the value exists  b) the two-sided limit's sides agree  c) none fail
**Answer:** a) f(−3) is undefined, though the limit is −5 (Nepal, G10 Optional, pp.275–293).

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A break where both one-sided limits agree but the point is missing is ______.
a) a jump  b) removable  c) infinite
**Answer:** b) removable — one redefined point repairs it (Pakistan, Class 12, Ch.1).

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A break where the left limit is 1 and the right limit is 4 is ______.
a) removable  b) a jump  c) not a break
**Answer:** b) a jump — the sides disagree, so no single point repairs it.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** On a closed interval [a, b], continuity at a needs ______.
a) the two-sided limit  b) the right-hand limit equal to f(a)  c) nothing
**Answer:** b) the right-hand limit equal to f(a) — endpoints take the one-sided test (Kuwait, pp.61–71).

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** If f is continuous on [0, 2] with f(0) = −1 and f(2) = 3, then f(x) = 0 ______.
a) has no solution  b) has a solution between 0 and 2  c) has solution x = 0 only
**Answer:** b) has a solution — the Intermediate Value Theorem (Cambodia, PRIVATE booklet, pp.20–22).
