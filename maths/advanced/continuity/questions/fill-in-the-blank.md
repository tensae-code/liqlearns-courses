# Continuity — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the one right word or number.

**1. (Level 1)** Continuity at a point needs ______ conditions to hold.
**Answer:** three — value, limit, and equality (Laos, Grade 10, pp.166–175).

**2. (Level 1)** If the limit exists but f(a) is a different number, condition ______ fails.
**Answer:** three (the equality condition) — the point sits at the wrong height.

**3. (Level 2)** A discontinuity that one redefined point can repair is called ______.
**Answer:** removable.

**4. (Level 2)** In a ______ discontinuity the left-hand and right-hand limits are different.
**Answer:** jump.

**5. (Level 3)** A function continuous at every point of (a, b) is continuous on that ______ interval.
**Answer:** open — endpoints are not part of the test there (Kuwait, pp.61–71).

**6. (Level 3)** The theorem that guarantees a root when f(a)·f(b) < 0 is the ______ Value Theorem.
**Answer:** Intermediate — Cambodia, PRIVATE Limits and Continuity booklet, pp.20–22.
