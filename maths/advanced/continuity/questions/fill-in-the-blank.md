# Continuity — fill in the blank

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — The Three Conditions · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Removable and Jump Discontinuities · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Continuity on an Interval and the Theorems.

Fill each blank with the one right word or number.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Continuity at a point needs ______ conditions to hold.
**Answer:** three — value, limit, and equality (Laos, Grade 10, pp.166–175).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** If the limit exists but f(a) is a different number, condition ______ fails.
**Answer:** three (the equality condition) — the point sits at the wrong height.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A discontinuity that one redefined point can repair is called ______.
**Answer:** removable.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In a ______ discontinuity the left-hand and right-hand limits are different.
**Answer:** jump.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** A function continuous at every point of (a, b) is continuous on that ______ interval.
**Answer:** open — endpoints are not part of the test there (Kuwait, pp.61–71).

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** The theorem that guarantees a root when f(a)·f(b) < 0 is the ______ Value Theorem.
**Answer:** Intermediate — Cambodia, PRIVATE Limits and Continuity booklet, pp.20–22.
