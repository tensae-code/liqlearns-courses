# Video script — Continuity

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Draw a curve. Do NOT lift your pen. If you can draw it in one go — it is **continuous**.

Now a curve with a HOLE. Your pen must jump the hole. Not continuous.

Now a STEP. Low on the left, high on the right. Your pen must jump. Not continuous.

The test has THREE parts. One: the point is there. Two: the limit is there.
Three: they are the SAME. All three — continuous. One fails — it breaks.

A hole can be FIXED. Drop the missing point in, and the curve is whole.
A jump can never be fixed by one point. The two sides will never meet.

Now you. Draw a break. Name it. Fix the ones that can be fixed.
That is continuity.
