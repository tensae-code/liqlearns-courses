1. (Sub lessons 1–5) Match each map or notion to its correct partner:
   (a) the zero map T(v) = 0
   (b) the shear T(x, y) = (x + 2y, y)
   (c) an isomorphism
   (d) an endomorphism
   (e) T(x, y) = (x + y, x + y)

   (i) a linear map from a space into itself
   (ii) its kernel is all of V
   (iii) a monomorphism that is also an epimorphism
   (iv) its kernel is {(0, 0)}
   (v) its kernel is {(x, −x)}, and dim N(T) + dim Im(T) = 2 checks rank–nullity

   **Answer: (a)–(ii), (b)–(iv), (c)–(iii), (d)–(i), (e)–(v).** Venezuela, La Matemática y el Vivir Bien (Grade 11), printed pp. 144, 147, 149, 152–154.
