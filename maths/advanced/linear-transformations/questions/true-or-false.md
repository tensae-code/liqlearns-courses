# True or false — Linear Transformations

1. (Layer 1) The zero map, T(v) = 0 for every v, is a linear transformation.
   **Answer: True** — it passes both laws, and the book verifies it first. Venezuela, La Matemática y el Vivir Bien (Grade 11), printed p. 144.

2. (Layer 2) If T is linear and T(v) = w, then T(3v) = 3w.
   **Answer: True** — the scalar law T(ku) = kT(u) with k = 3, one case of preservation of linear combinations. Venezuela, La Matemática y el Vivir Bien (Grade 11), printed p. 149.

3. (Layer 4) The images of a basis of V under a linear map T always form a basis of Im(T).
   **Answer: False** — taught corrected: they always span Im(T) but need not be independent; under T(x, y) = (x + y, x + y) the two basis images are both (1, 1). Venezuela, La Matemática y el Vivir Bien (Grade 11), printed pp. 150–152 (claim taught corrected).

4. (Layer 5) A rotation about the origin is a linear transformation.
   **Answer: True** — its formula comes from the angle-sum identities and it is a matrix map. Venezuela, La Matemática y el Vivir Bien (Grade 11), printed pp. 154–155.
