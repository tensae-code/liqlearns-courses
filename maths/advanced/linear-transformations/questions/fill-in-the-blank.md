# Fill in the blank — Linear Transformations

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Layer 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — The Two Laws · (Layer 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — First Properties · (Layer 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — The Five Kinds · (Layer 4) -> Sub lesson 4 — Kernel and Image · (Layer 5) -> Sub lesson 5 — Rank–Nullity.

1. (Sub lesson 1) The addition law says T(u + v) = T(u) + ______.
   **Answer: T(v).** Venezuela, La Matemática y el Vivir Bien (Grade 11), printed p. 144.

2. (Sub lesson 3) A linear map from a space V into itself is called an ______.
   **Answer: endomorphism.** Venezuela, La Matemática y el Vivir Bien (Grade 11), printed p. 149.

3. (Sub lesson 4) The set of all vectors that T sends to 0 is called the ______ of T.
   **Answer: kernel (N(T)).** Venezuela, La Matemática y el Vivir Bien (Grade 11), printed p. 152.

4. (Sub lesson 5) Sylvester's theorem says dim V = dim N(T) + dim ______.
   **Answer: Im(T)** — the crushed part plus the kept part fills the space. Venezuela, La Matemática y el Vivir Bien (Grade 11), printed pp. 153–154.
