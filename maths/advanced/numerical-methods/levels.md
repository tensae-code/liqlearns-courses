## Sub lesson 1 — Estimate and Locate
Interpolate and extrapolate linearly between two known points, and locate a root inside an interval by a sign change, from a table or a graph. Pass mark: 4 of 5.

## Sub lesson 2 — Newton–Raphson
Carry out Newton–Raphson iterations from a given start to a stated accuracy, and say when the method can fail. Pass mark: 5 of 6.

## Sub lesson 3 — Fixed-Point Iteration
Rearrange an equation as x = g(x), iterate it, and compare two rearrangements of the same equation — predicting from |g′(x)| which one converges. Pass mark: 4 of 5.

## What "maxed" means here

The learner can take an equation with no exact solution, trap its root in an interval, then close in by Newton–Raphson or by a well-chosen rearrangement — and can look at two rearrangements and say, before iterating, which one will work and why.
