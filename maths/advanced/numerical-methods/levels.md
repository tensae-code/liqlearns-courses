# Levels — Numerical Methods

One lesson, one level (Level 147). The depth layers inside it are ranks.

## Depth layers

- **Layer 1 — Estimate and locate.** Interpolate and extrapolate linearly between two known points, and locate a root inside an interval by a sign change, from a table or a graph. Pass mark: 4 of 5.
- **Layer 2 — Newton–Raphson.** Carry out Newton–Raphson iterations from a given start to a stated accuracy, and say when the method can fail. Pass mark: 5 of 6.
- **Layer 3 — Fixed-point iteration.** Rearrange an equation as x = g(x), iterate it, and compare two rearrangements of the same equation — predicting from |g′(x)| which one converges. Pass mark: 4 of 5.

## What "maxed" means here

The learner can take an equation with no exact solution, trap its root in an interval, then close in by Newton–Raphson or by a well-chosen rearrangement — and can look at two rearrangements and say, before iterating, which one will work and why.
