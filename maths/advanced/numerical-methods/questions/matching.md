# Matching — Numerical Methods

1. (Layer 1) Match each method to what it does:
   (a) Linear interpolation (b) Sign-change location (c) Newton–Raphson (d) Fixed-point iteration
   Descriptions: follows tangents to the axis, x − f(x)/f′(x); estimates on the straight line between two known points; traps a root where f takes opposite signs; feeds x = g(x) back into itself repeatedly.
   **Answer: (a) estimates on the line between two points, (b) traps a root by opposite signs, (c) follows tangents, x − f(x)/f′(x), (d) iterates x = g(x).** Rwanda, Advanced Mathematics, Learner's Book, Senior Five, Unit 4, printed pp. 105–126.
