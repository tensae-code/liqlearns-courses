# Short answer — Numerical Methods

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Layer 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Estimate and Locate · (Layer 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Newton–Raphson · (Layer 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Fixed-Point Iteration.

1. (Sub lesson 1) f(1) = −3 and f(2) = 5. Give the interpolated estimate of the root of f(x) = 0.
   **Answer: x ≈ 1 + (0 − (−3)) × (2 − 1)/(5 − (−3)) = 1 + 3/8 = 1.375.** Rwanda, Advanced Mathematics, Learner's Book, Senior Five, Unit 4, printed pp. 105–112.

2. (Sub lesson 2) Carry out one Newton–Raphson step for x² − 7 = 0 from x₀ = 3.
   **Answer: x₁ = 3 − (9 − 7)/6 = 3 − 1/3 ≈ 2.667.** Rwanda, Advanced Mathematics, Learner's Book, Senior Five, Unit 4, printed pp. 118–122.

3. (Sub lesson 3) Why can two rearrangements of the same equation, started at the same point, behave differently?
   **Answer: Convergence depends on the rearrangement's derivative at the root — iteration settles only when |g′(x)| < 1 near the root; one rearrangement may satisfy this while another cycles or diverges.** Rwanda, Advanced Mathematics, Learner's Book, Senior Five, Unit 4, printed pp. 122–126.
