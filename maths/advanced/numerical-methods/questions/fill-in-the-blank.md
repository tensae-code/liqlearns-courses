Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. 1. (Sub lesson 1) Estimating a value beyond the two known points, on the line extended, is called ___.
   **Answer: extrapolation.** Rwanda, Advanced Mathematics, Learner's Book, Senior Five, Unit 4, printed pp. 105–112.

2. (Sub lesson 1) If f(2) = −1 and f(3) = 4, a root of f(x) = 0 lies between 2 and 3 because the function changes ___.
   **Answer: sign.** Rwanda, Advanced Mathematics, Learner's Book, Senior Five, Unit 4, printed pp. 111–115.

3. (Sub lesson 3) Fixed-point iteration xₙ₊₁ = g(xₙ) converges near a root when |g′(x)| is ___ than 1 there.
   **Answer: less (smaller).** Rwanda, Advanced Mathematics, Learner's Book, Senior Five, Unit 4, printed pp. 122–126.
