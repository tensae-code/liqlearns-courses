# Quaternions — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — The Form and the Defining Rules · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Addition and the Multiplication Table · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Conjugate and Modulus.

Pick the one right answer. All questions trace to China, B-edition Compulsory Volume 4, Ch.10 extended reading, printed pp.47–48.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A quaternion has how many parts?
a) 2  b) 3  c) 4
**Answer:** c) 4 — one real part and three imaginary parts.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** j² equals ______.
a) 1  b) −1  c) k
**Answer:** b) −1 — i² = j² = k² = −1.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The real part of 5 − 2i + j + 3k is ______.
a) 5  b) −2  c) 3
**Answer:** a) 5 — the part with no unit.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** ij equals ______.
a) −k  b) k  c) 1
**Answer:** b) k — forwards around the cycle.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** ji equals ______.
a) k  b) −k  c) −1
**Answer:** b) −k — backwards around the cycle, the sign flips.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** (2 + i) + (1 + 3j) equals ______.
a) 3 + i + 3j  b) 3 + 4i + 3j  c) 2 + i + 3j + 1k
**Answer:** a) 3 + i + 3j — addition goes part by part.
