# Quaternions — fill-in-the-blank

Fill in the missing number, function or word.

**1. (Level 1)** A quaternion is written a + bi + cj + ___.
**Answer:** dk (China, B-edition Vol. 4, extended reading, printed pp.47–48).

**2. (Level 1)** k² = ___.
**Answer:** −1 (China, B-edition Vol. 4, extended reading, printed pp.47–48).

**3. (Level 2)** ki = ___.
**Answer:** j — forwards around the cycle (China, B-edition Vol. 4, extended reading, printed pp.47–48).

**4. (Level 2)** ik = ___.
**Answer:** −j — backwards around the cycle (China, B-edition Vol. 4, extended reading, printed pp.47–48).
