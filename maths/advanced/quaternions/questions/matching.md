# Quaternions — matching

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — The Form and the Defining Rules · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Addition and the Multiplication Table · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Conjugate and Modulus.

Match each item on the left to its partner on the right.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Match each unit to its square: (a) i (b) j (c) k — −1, −1, −1
**Answer:** a) −1, b) −1, c) −1 (China, B-edition Vol. 4, extended reading, printed pp.47–48).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Match each product to its value: (a) ij (b) jk (c) ki — k, i, j
**Answer:** a) k, b) i, c) j (China, B-edition Vol. 4, extended reading, printed pp.47–48).

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Match each reversed product to its value: (a) ji (b) kj (c) ik — −k, −i, −j
**Answer:** a) −k, b) −i, c) −j (China, B-edition Vol. 4, extended reading, printed pp.47–48).

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Match each quaternion to its conjugate: (a) 1 + k (b) i − j (c) 2 − 3k — 1 − k, −i + j, 2 + 3k
**Answer:** a) 1 − k, b) −i + j, c) 2 + 3k (China, B-edition Vol. 4, extended reading, printed pp.47–48).
