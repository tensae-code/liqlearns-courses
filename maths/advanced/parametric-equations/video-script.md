# Video script — Parametric Equations

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A point is travelling. Where is it? You need TWO answers — across, and up.

One clock drives both. The clock is called **t**, the parameter.
x = f of t tells the across. y = g of t tells the up. At each tick, one point.

Watch: x = t, y = t squared. t = 1: point (1, 1). t = 2: point (2, 4). The points draw a **parabola** — and the clock tells you the point is moving RIGHT as it climbs.

Erase the clock — eliminate t — and the path stays: y = x squared. But the journey is gone. The parameter keeps the journey.

A flying ball is the same idea: x runs evenly, y rises and falls — x = 24 root 2 t, y = minus 16 t squared plus 24 root 2 t. One clock, one arc, one landing.

Now you. Make the table. Plot the points. Follow the traveller. That is parametric.
