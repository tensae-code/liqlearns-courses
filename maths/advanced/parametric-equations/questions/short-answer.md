# Parametric Equations — short answer

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Plotting and Eliminating the Parameter · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Circles, Ellipses and the Slope · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Projectile Motion.

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** For x = 3t, y = t², give the point at t = 2.
**Answer:** (6, 4) (Cambodia, Grade 12 Advanced, Ch.9, printed p.235).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Eliminate t from x = 2t, y = t² − 1.
**Answer:** y = x²/4 − 1 — t = x/2 substituted (Cambodia, Grade 12 Advanced, Ch.9, printed pp.235–252).

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Write the Cartesian equation of x = 4 sin θ, y = 4 cos θ.
**Answer:** x² + y² = 16 — square and add (Cambodia, Grade 12 Advanced guide, printed pp.227–236).

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** For x = t³, y = t², find dy/dx at t = 2.
**Answer:** 1/3 — (2t)/(3t²) = 2/(3t), at t = 2 gives 1/3 (Cambodia, Grade 12 Advanced, Ch.9, printed pp.235–252).

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** State the position of Cambodia's projectile at t = 1.
**Answer:** (24√2, 24√2 − 16) (Cambodia, Grade 12 Advanced, §9.1, printed p.236).

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** Find the projectile's landing time.
**Answer:** t = 3√2/2 ≈ 2.12 s — from −16t² + 24√2 t = 0 (Cambodia, Grade 12 Advanced, §9.1, printed p.236).

**7. (Sub lesson 3)** What value of g does Cambodia's projectile example use?
**Answer:** 32 cm/s² — the example works in cm/s throughout (Cambodia, Grade 12 Advanced, §9.1, printed p.236).
