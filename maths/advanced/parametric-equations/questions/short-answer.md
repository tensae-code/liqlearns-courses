# Parametric Equations — short answer

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Level 1)** For x = 3t, y = t², give the point at t = 2.
**Answer:** (6, 4) (Cambodia, Grade 12 Advanced, Ch.9, printed p.235).

**2. (Level 1)** Eliminate t from x = 2t, y = t² − 1.
**Answer:** y = x²/4 − 1 — t = x/2 substituted (Cambodia, Grade 12 Advanced, Ch.9, printed pp.235–252).

**3. (Level 2)** Write the Cartesian equation of x = 4 sin θ, y = 4 cos θ.
**Answer:** x² + y² = 16 — square and add (Cambodia, Grade 12 Advanced guide, printed pp.227–236).

**4. (Level 2)** For x = t³, y = t², find dy/dx at t = 2.
**Answer:** 1/3 — (2t)/(3t²) = 2/(3t), at t = 2 gives 1/3 (Cambodia, Grade 12 Advanced, Ch.9, printed pp.235–252).

**5. (Level 3)** State the position of Cambodia's projectile at t = 1.
**Answer:** (24√2, 24√2 − 16) (Cambodia, Grade 12 Advanced, §9.1, printed p.236).

**6. (Level 3)** Find the projectile's landing time.
**Answer:** t = 3√2/2 ≈ 2.12 s — from −16t² + 24√2 t = 0 (Cambodia, Grade 12 Advanced, §9.1, printed p.236).

**7. (Level 3)** What value of g does Cambodia's projectile example use?
**Answer:** 32 cm/s² — the example works in cm/s throughout (Cambodia, Grade 12 Advanced, §9.1, printed p.236).
