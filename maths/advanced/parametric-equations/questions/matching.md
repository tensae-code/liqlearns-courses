# Parametric Equations — matching

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Plotting and Eliminating the Parameter · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Circles, Ellipses and the Slope · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Projectile Motion.

Match each item on the left to its partner on the right.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Match each t to its point on x = t, y = t²: (a) t = −1 (b) t = 0 (c) t = 3 — (−1, 1), (0, 0), (3, 9)
**Answer:** a) (−1, 1), b) (0, 0), c) (3, 9) (Cambodia, Grade 12 Advanced, Ch.9, printed p.235).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Match each parametric pair to its Cartesian equation: (a) x = t, y = t² (b) x = 4 sin θ, y = 4 cos θ (c) x = h + a sin θ, y = k + b cos θ — y = x², x² + y² = 16, (x − h)²/a² + (y − k)²/b² = 1
**Answer:** a) y = x², b) x² + y² = 16, c) (x − h)²/a² + (y − k)²/b² = 1 (Cambodia, Grade 12 Advanced, Ch.9, printed p.235; guide, printed pp.227–236).

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Match each projectile moment to its fact: (a) t = 0 (b) t = 1 (c) landing — (0, 0), (24√2, 24√2 − 16), x = 72 cm
**Answer:** a) (0, 0), b) (24√2, 24√2 − 16), c) x = 72 cm (Cambodia, Grade 12 Advanced, §9.1, printed p.236).

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Match each book to its parametric item: (a) Cambodia Grade 12 Advanced (b) Cambodia Grade 12 Advanced guide (c) Mauritania 7AS-C — the projectile with v₀ = 48, the projectile misprinted with v₀ = 45, the chapter "Courbes paramétrées"
**Answer:** a) the projectile with v₀ = 48, b) the projectile misprinted with v₀ = 45, c) the chapter "Courbes paramétrées" (Cambodia, §9.1, printed p.236; guide, printed p.227; Mauritania, 7AS-C, Ch.13, printed p.159).
