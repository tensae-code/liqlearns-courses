# Parametric Equations — fill-in-the-blank

Fill in the missing number, function or word.

**1. (Level 1)** Each value of t gives one point (f(t), ___) on the curve.
**Answer:** g(t) (Cambodia, Grade 12 Advanced guide, printed pp.227–236).

**2. (Level 1)** For x = t, y = t² with t ≥ 0, only the ___ half of y = x² is drawn.
**Answer:** right (Cambodia, Grade 12 Advanced, Ch.9, printed p.235).

**3. (Level 2)** dy/dx = (dy/dt) ÷ (___).
**Answer:** dx/dt (Cambodia, Grade 12 Advanced, Ch.9, printed pp.235–252).

**4. (Level 2)** The ellipse (x − h)²/a² + (y − k)²/b² = ___ is the Cartesian partner of the parametric circle form.
**Answer:** 1 (Cambodia, Grade 12 Advanced guide, printed pp.227–236).

**5. (Level 3)** Taught corrected, the projectile's v₀ is ___ cm/s, not 45.
**Answer:** 48 (Cambodia, Grade 12 Advanced, §9.1, printed p.236, taught corrected).

**6. (Level 3)** In x = 24√2 t, the horizontal velocity component is ___.
**Answer:** 24√2 cm/s — v₀ cos 45° with v₀ = 48 (Cambodia, Grade 12 Advanced, §9.1, printed p.236).
