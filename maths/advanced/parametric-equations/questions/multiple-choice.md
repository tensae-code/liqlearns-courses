# Parametric Equations — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Plotting and Eliminating the Parameter · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Circles, Ellipses and the Slope · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Projectile Motion.

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In x = f(t), y = g(t), t is called the ______.
a) parameter  b) asymptote  c) modulus
**Answer:** a) parameter — one t gives one point (f(t), g(t)) (Cambodia, Grade 12 Advanced guide, printed pp.227–236).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** For x = t, y = t², the point at t = 2 is ______.
a) (2, 2)  b) (4, 2)  c) (2, 4)
**Answer:** c) (2, 4) (Cambodia, Grade 12 Advanced, Ch.9, printed p.235).

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Eliminating t from x = t, y = t² gives ______.
a) y = x²  b) x = y² only for t ≥ 0  c) y = 2x
**Answer:** a) y = x² — substitute t = x (Cambodia, Grade 12 Advanced, Ch.9, printed p.235).

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The centre of x = h + a sin θ, y = k + b cos θ is ______.
a) (a, b)  b) (h, k)  c) (0, 0)
**Answer:** b) (h, k) — a and b are the radii (Cambodia, Grade 12 Advanced guide, printed pp.227–236).

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** For x = t, y = t², dy/dx is ______.
a) 2t  b) t²  c) 1/(2t)
**Answer:** a) 2t — (dy/dt)/(dx/dt) = 2t/1 (Cambodia, Grade 12 Advanced, Ch.9, printed pp.235–252).

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** A parametric circle with a = b traces a closed curve as θ runs ______.
a) 0 to π  b) 0 to 2π  c) −1 to 1
**Answer:** b) 0 to 2π — the full turn (Cambodia, Grade 12 Advanced guide, printed pp.227–236).

**7. (Sub lesson 3)** In Cambodia's projectile, v₀ — taught corrected — is ______.
a) 45 cm/s  b) 48 cm/s  c) 32 cm/s
**Answer:** b) 48 cm/s — the guide's 45 is a slip; the equations require 48 (Cambodia, Grade 12 Advanced, §9.1, printed p.236, taught corrected).

**8. (Sub lesson 3)** The projectile's path, after eliminating t, is ______.
a) y = −x²/72 + x  b) y = −16x² + 24√2 x  c) y = x²/72 − x
**Answer:** a) y = −x²/72 + x (Cambodia, Grade 12 Advanced, §9.1, printed p.236).

**9. (Sub lesson 3)** The projectile lands at x = ______.
a) 24√2 cm  b) 48 cm  c) 72 cm
**Answer:** c) 72 cm — the non-zero root of −x²/72 + x = 0 (Cambodia, Grade 12 Advanced, §9.1, printed p.236).

**10. (Sub lesson 3)** Mauritania's 7AS-C book titles its parametric chapter ______.
a) Coniques  b) Courbes paramétrées  c) Calcul intégral
**Answer:** b) Courbes paramétrées (Mauritania, 7AS-C, Ch.13, printed p.159).
