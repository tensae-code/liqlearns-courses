# Parametric Equations — ordering

Put the items in the order asked. Check each step before you write the answer.

**1. (Level 1)** Put the points of x = t, y = t² in travelling order for t = −2, 0, 2, −1: (2, 4), (−2, 4), (0, 0), (−1, 1).
Answer in travelling order.
**Answer:** (−2, 4), (−1, 1), (0, 0), (2, 4) — t rising (Cambodia, Grade 12 Advanced, Ch.9, printed p.235).

**2. (Level 2)** Put the steps for eliminating t from x = 24√2 t, y = −16t² + 24√2 t in order: (i) substitute into y (ii) solve x = 24√2 t for t (iii) simplify to y = −x²/72 + x.
Answer in order.
**Answer:** (ii), (i), (iii) (Cambodia, Grade 12 Advanced, §9.1, printed p.236).

**3. (Level 3)** Put the projectile's events in time order: landing at x = 72, launch at (0, 0), position (24√2, 24√2 − 16), highest point.
Answer in time order.
**Answer:** Launch at (0, 0), then position (24√2, 24√2 − 16) at t = 1, then the highest point, then landing at x = 72 (Cambodia, Grade 12 Advanced, §9.1, printed p.236).
