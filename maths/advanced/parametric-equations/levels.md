Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: build a table for x = f(t), y = g(t), plot the points, and eliminate t to get a Cartesian equation, as in x = t, y = t² → y = x².

## Sub lesson 2 — Circles, Ellipses and the Slope
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: read a parametric circle or ellipse (centre, radii, Cartesian form), and find dy/dx as (dy/dt)/(dx/dt).
