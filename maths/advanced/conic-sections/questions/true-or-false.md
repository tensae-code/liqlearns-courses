# Conic Sections — true or false

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Level 1)** Every conic section is the cut of a plane with a double cone.
**Answer: True** — that is how Greece, Pakistan and Iraq all open the topic.

**2. (Level 1)** On a parabola, every point is as far from the focus as from the directrix.
**Answer: True** — that is e = 1 (Cambodia, Grade 12 Basic, pp.178–191).

**3. (Level 2)** An ellipse can have eccentricity greater than 1.
**Answer: False** — an ellipse has 0 < e < 1; e > 1 is the hyperbola.

**4. (Level 2)** Indonesia's Tingkat Lanjut Revisi book teaches the equations of the hyperbola.
**Answer: False** — Revisi teaches the hyperbola's definition, elements and asymptotes only; its equation is left to an external link (p.77).

**5. (Level 2)** Iran's Geometry (3) teaches the hyperbola.
**Answer: False** — the hyperbola is explicitly not taught there; Iran is a source for the ellipse and the parabola only.

**6. (Level 3)** Nepal's Grade 10 Optional book works out the equations of the parabola, ellipse and hyperbola.
**Answer: False** — §8.3 is an introduction only: parts of a cone, with no conic equations developed.
