# Conic Sections — short answer

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Level 1)** A point moves with PF ÷ (distance to the directrix) = 1/2. Which conic is it?
**Answer:** An ellipse — 0 < e < 1 (Laos, Grade 10, pp.278–282).

**2. (Level 1)** Write the equation of the parabola with focus (3, 0) and vertex at the origin.
**Answer:** y² = 12x (Iraq, Grade 12 Science, p.53).

**3. (Level 1)** A parabola has directrix y = 7 and vertex at the origin. Write its equation.
**Answer:** x² = −28y (Iraq, Grade 12 Science, p.59).

**4. (Level 2)** For x²/100 + y²/25 = 1, give the bottom vertex, taught corrected.
**Answer:** (0, −5) — the answers graph in Cambodia's book prints (−5, 0); the corrected vertex is (0, −5).

**5. (Level 2)** An ellipse worked in Pakistan's Class 12 book has a latus rectum printed as 4/3. What is the corrected value?
**Answer:** 8/3 (Pakistan, Class 12, Chapter 6, ch-p.42, taught corrected).

**6. (Level 2)** For the hyperbola x²/16 − y²/9 = 1, find e.
**Answer:** 5/4 — c² = 16 + 9 = 25, c = 5, e = c/a = 5/4.

**7. (Level 3)** A parabola worked in Pakistan's Class 12 book has its focus printed as (1/26, 3/52). What is the focus, taught corrected?
**Answer:** (−1/26, 3/52) (Pakistan, Class 12, Chapter 6, ch-p.75, taught corrected).

**8. (Level 3)** Write the equation of the ellipse x²/25 + y²/9 = 1 after its centre moves to (2, −1).
**Answer:** (x − 2)²/25 + (y + 1)²/9 = 1 — translation replaces x by (x − 2) and y by (y + 1).
