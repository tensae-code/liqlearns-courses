# Conic Sections — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Level 1)** In the definition PF ÷ (distance to the directrix) = e, the value e = 1 gives ______.
a) an ellipse  b) a parabola  c) a hyperbola
**Answer: b) a parabola** — e = 1 is the balancing case (Laos, Grade 10, pp.278–282).

**2. (Level 1)** For the parabola y² = 12x, the focus is ______.
a) (12, 0)  b) (0, 3)  c) (3, 0)
**Answer: c) (3, 0)** — y² = 4cx with c = 3 (Iraq, Grade 12 Science, p.53).

**3. (Level 1)** The latus rectum of y² = 4cx has length ______.
a) c  b) 2c  c) 4c
**Answer: c) 4c** — the chord through the focus, at right angles to the axis.

**4. (Level 2)** For the ellipse x²/25 + y²/9 = 1, c is ______.
a) 4  b) 5  c) 3
**Answer: a) 4** — a² = b² + c², so c² = 25 − 9 = 16.

**5. (Level 2)** The eccentricity of x²/25 + y²/9 = 1 is ______.
a) 5/4  b) 4/5  c) 3/5
**Answer: b) 4/5** — e = c/a < 1 for every ellipse.

**6. (Level 2)** The asymptotes of x²/16 − y²/9 = 1 are ______.
a) y = ±(4/3)x  b) y = ±x  c) y = ±(3/4)x
**Answer: c) y = ±(3/4)x** — the asymptotes are y = ±(b/a)x (Laos, Grade 10, p.308).

**7. (Level 2)** A hyperbola worked in Pakistan's Class 12 book has, taught corrected, c² = ______.
a) 34  b) 41  c) 25
**Answer: b) 41** — the book prints 34; the corrected value is 41, with foci (±√41, 0) and e = √41/4.

**8. (Level 3)** Translating a conic's axes changes ______.
a) its centre or vertex only  b) its a, b and c  c) its eccentricity
**Answer: a) its centre or vertex only** — the shape does not change (Yemen, Grade 12 Science, p.124).
