# Conic Sections — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word or value.

**1. (Level 1)** The fixed point in the focus–directrix definition is called the ______.
**Answer:** focus — with the fixed line called the directrix (Laos, Grade 10, pp.278–282).

**2. (Level 1)** The number e in MF/MH = e is called the ______.
**Answer:** eccentricity — Kuwait gives it a lesson of its own (Grade 12 Term 2, pp.126–133).

**3. (Level 2)** For an ellipse, a² = b² + ______.
**Answer:** c² — so a is the biggest of the three (Laos, Grade 10, p.299).

**4. (Level 2)** For a hyperbola, c² = a² + ______.
**Answer:** b² — so c is the biggest of the three (Laos, Grade 10, p.308).

**5. (Level 2)** On an ellipse drawn with string around two pins, PF₁ + PF₂ is always ______.
**Answer:** constant (equal to 2a) — the string construction (Iran, Geometry (3), pp.47–48).

**6. (Level 3)** A conic whose equation carries an xy term can be simplified by ______ of the axes.
**Answer:** rotation — Yemen's Grade 12 tool (Unit 4, p.129).
