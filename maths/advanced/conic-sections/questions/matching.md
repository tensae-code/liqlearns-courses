Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — The Cone, the Locus Definition and the Parabola · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — The Ellipse and the Hyperbola · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Moving, Turning and Classifying Conics.

Match each item on the left with the one right match on the right.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** e = 1
**Answer:** the parabola — the balancing case of MF/MH = e.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** 0 < e < 1
**Answer:** the ellipse — the pulled-in case of MF/MH = e.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** e > 1
**Answer:** the hyperbola — the pushed-out case of MF/MH = e.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** PF₁ + PF₂ = 2a
**Answer:** the ellipse's locus rule — the string construction (Iran, Geometry (3), pp.47–49; Nicaragua, Grade 11, pp.104–109).

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** |PF₁ − PF₂| = 2a
**Answer:** the hyperbola's locus rule — the constant difference (Nicaragua, Grade 11, pp.110–117).
