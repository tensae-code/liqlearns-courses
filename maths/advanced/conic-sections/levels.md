# Conic Sections — questions by level

Practice by level, tied to the skill number. The questions live in
the type files; this file is the by-level view. The levels are the
depths inside this one thing — conic sections — the cone and the one locus definition first, then the parabola, then the ellipse and the hyperbola with their elements, then translated conics, tangents and classification.

## Level 1 — the cone, the locus definition and the parabola
Level 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: name each conic from its cone cut and from its value of e in MF/MH = e, and write a parabola y² = 4cx with its focus, directrix and latus rectum.

## Level 2 — the ellipse and the hyperbola
Level 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: for x²/a² + y²/b² = 1 and x²/a² − y²/b² = 1 find a, b, c, the foci, the vertices, the eccentricity and, for the hyperbola, the asymptotes — using the corrected values where a book printed a slip.

## Level 3 — moving, turning and classifying conics
Level 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: read and write translated conics, say what translation and rotation of axes do, find a tangent or normal at a point, and classify a conic from its general second-degree equation.
