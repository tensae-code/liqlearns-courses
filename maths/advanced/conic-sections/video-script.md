# Video script — Conic Sections

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Take a cone. Cut it with a flat knife.

Cut it level: a CIRCLE. Tilt a little: an ELLIPSE — a squashed circle.
Tilt until the cut runs along the cone's side: a PARABOLA — one open curve.
Tilt more, and cut both halves of a double cone: a HYPERBOLA — two open curves.

One cut family. Three curves to learn.

Now the rule that makes them. A point moves. Its distance to a fixed point — the FOCUS — divided by its distance to a fixed line — the DIRECTRIX — stays the same. That number is e.

e is 1? PARABOLA. The point is as far from the focus as from the line.
e is less than 1? ELLIPSE. Two pins, a loop of string, keep it tight — the two distances ADD to the same total, always.
e is more than 1? HYPERBOLA. The two distances DIFFER by the same amount, always — and the curve runs away along two slanted guide lines, its asymptotes.

Parabola: y² = 4cx. Ellipse: x²/a² + y²/b² = 1. Hyperbola: x²/a² − y²/b² = 1.

Now you. I give you e. You give me the curve. That is conic sections.
