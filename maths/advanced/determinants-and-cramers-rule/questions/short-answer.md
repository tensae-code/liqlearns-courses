# Determinants and Cramer's Rule — short answer

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — The 2×2 Determinant · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — The 3×3 Determinant and Two-Unknown Systems · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Three-Unknown Systems, the D = 0 Cases and Codes.

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** State the 2×2 determinant rule in words.
**Answer:** Main diagonal product minus other diagonal product: ad − bc (Kuwait, Grade 10 Term 2, Lesson 7-4).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is a triangular determinant equal to?
**Answer:** The product of its diagonal entries (Egypt, Secondary 1 Term 2, Lesson 1-4).

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** In Nicaragua's worked cofactors, what are M₂₁ and M₃₂?
**Answer:** M₂₁ = −1 and M₃₂ = −2 — minors of its worked 3×3 (Nicaragua, Grade 10, pp.227–244).

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Solve 3x + 7y = −6, 4x − 2y = 26 by Cramer's rule.
**Answer:** x = 5, y = −3 — D = −34, D_x = −170, D_y = 102 (Cambodia, Grade 11 Basic, p.135).

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Write Laos's inverse-matrix method as a formula.
**Answer:** AX = B ⇒ X = A⁻¹B (Laos, Grade 12, Lesson 8, p.67).

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** Nicaragua's 3×3 Cramer system has det A = 10, A₁ = 27, A₂ = 9, A₃ = 32. Find x, y, z.
**Answer:** x = 2.7, y = 0.9, z = 3.2 — each numerator divided by 10 (Nicaragua, Grade 10, pp.235–238).

**7. (Sub lesson 3)** Cambodia's dependent system is answered how?
**Answer:** With a free parameter: x = −(1+3l)/7, y = (1−4l)/7, z = l (Cambodia, Grade 11 Basic, p.135).

**8. (Sub lesson 3)** Why must a coding matrix have a non-zero determinant?
**Answer:** So its inverse exists — without A⁻¹ the message cannot be decoded (Nicaragua, Grade 10, pp.243–244).
