# Determinants and Cramer's Rule — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** det [[3, 7], [4, −2]] is ______.
a) 34  b) −34  c) −22
**Answer:** b) −34 — 3·(−2) − 7·4 = −6 − 28 (Cambodia, Grade 11 Basic, p.135).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A square matrix has an inverse exactly when its determinant is ______.
a) zero  b) not zero  c) negative
**Answer:** b) not zero — a determinant of 0 marks a singular matrix (Kuwait, Grade 10 Term 2, Lesson 7-4, pp.74–78).

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The area of the triangle (0,0), (4,0), (0,3), from a determinant, is ______.
a) 12  b) 6  c) 7
**Answer:** b) 6 — half of det [[4, 0], [0, 3]] = 12 (Egypt, Secondary 1 Term 2, Lesson 1-4).

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The determinant of [[1, 2, 3], [0, 1, 4], [5, 6, 0]] is ______.
a) 1  b) −1  c) 49
**Answer:** a) 1 — by Sarrus or by cofactors: −24 + 40 − 15 (Nicaragua, Grade 10, pp.233–234).

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** For x + y = 3, x − y = 7, Cramer's rule gives ______.
a) (5, −2)  b) (−2, 5)  c) (2, 1)
**Answer:** a) (5, −2) — D = −2, D_x = −10, D_y = 4 (Kuwait, Grade 10 Term 2, Lesson 7-5, pp.79–82).

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** The inverse of [[2, 1], [5, 3]] is ______.
a) [[3, −1], [−5, 2]]  b) [[2, −1], [−5, 3]]  c) it has none
**Answer:** a) [[3, −1], [−5, 2]] — the determinant is 1, so swap the diagonal and flip the other signs (Egypt, Secondary 1 Term 2, Lesson 1-5).

**7. (Sub lesson 3)** Cambodia's 3×3 Cramer system has D = 12, D_x = 4, D_y = −20, D_z = 20. Then x is ______.
a) 3  b) 1/3  c) −5/3
**Answer:** b) 1/3 — x = D_x/D = 4/12 (Cambodia, Grade 11 Basic, p.135).

**8. (Sub lesson 3)** If D = 0 and D_x ≠ 0, the system has ______.
a) one solution  b) no solution  c) infinitely many solutions
**Answer:** b) no solution — Cambodia's case: D = 0, D_x = 170 ≠ 0 (Grade 11 Basic, p.135).
