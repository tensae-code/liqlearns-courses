# Determinants and Cramer's Rule — ordering

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — The 2×2 Determinant · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — The 3×3 Determinant and Two-Unknown Systems · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Three-Unknown Systems, the D = 0 Cases and Codes.

Put each list in the right order.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Order the steps for det [[a, b], [c, d]]: (a) subtract, (b) multiply the main diagonal, (c) multiply the other diagonal.
**Answer:** (b), (c), (a) — ad first, bc second, then ad − bc (Kuwait, Grade 10 Term 2, Lesson 7-4).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Order the Cramer steps for x: (a) divide D_x by D, (b) find D, (c) swap the answers into the x-column to get D_x.
**Answer:** (b), (c), (a) — D first, then the swap, then the division (Cambodia, Grade 11 Basic, pp.130–138).

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Order Sarrus' rule: (a) subtract the rising diagonal products, (b) copy the first two columns on the right, (c) add the falling diagonal products.
**Answer:** (b), (c), (a) — copy, add down, subtract up (Nicaragua, Grade 10, pp.233–234).

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Order the codes application: (a) multiply by A⁻¹, (b) turn letters into numbers, (c) multiply by A, (d) read the letters.
**Answer:** (b), (c), (a), (d) — numbers, code, decode, read (Nicaragua, Grade 10, pp.243–244).
