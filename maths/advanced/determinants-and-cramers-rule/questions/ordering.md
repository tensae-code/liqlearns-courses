# Determinants and Cramer's Rule — ordering

Put each list in the right order.

**1. (Level 1)** Order the steps for det [[a, b], [c, d]]: (a) subtract, (b) multiply the main diagonal, (c) multiply the other diagonal.
**Answer:** (b), (c), (a) — ad first, bc second, then ad − bc (Kuwait, Grade 10 Term 2, Lesson 7-4).

**2. (Level 2)** Order the Cramer steps for x: (a) divide D_x by D, (b) find D, (c) swap the answers into the x-column to get D_x.
**Answer:** (b), (c), (a) — D first, then the swap, then the division (Cambodia, Grade 11 Basic, pp.130–138).

**3. (Level 2)** Order Sarrus' rule: (a) subtract the rising diagonal products, (b) copy the first two columns on the right, (c) add the falling diagonal products.
**Answer:** (b), (c), (a) — copy, add down, subtract up (Nicaragua, Grade 10, pp.233–234).

**4. (Level 3)** Order the codes application: (a) multiply by A⁻¹, (b) turn letters into numbers, (c) multiply by A, (d) read the letters.
**Answer:** (b), (c), (a), (d) — numbers, code, decode, read (Nicaragua, Grade 10, pp.243–244).
