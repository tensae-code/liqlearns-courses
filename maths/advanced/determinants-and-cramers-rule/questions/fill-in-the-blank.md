# Determinants and Cramer's Rule — fill in the blank

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — The 2×2 Determinant · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — The 3×3 Determinant and Two-Unknown Systems · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Three-Unknown Systems, the D = 0 Cases and Codes.

Fill each blank with the missing word or number.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** det [[a, b], [c, d]] = ad − ____.
**Answer:** bc — the other diagonal's product (Kuwait, Grade 10 Term 2, Lesson 7-4).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A matrix with determinant 0 is called ____.
**Answer:** singular — it has no inverse (Kuwait, Grade 10 Term 2, Lesson 7-4, pp.74–78).

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Sarrus' rule begins by copying the first two ____ of the determinant on its right.
**Answer:** columns — then the falling diagonals are added and the rising ones subtracted (Nicaragua, Grade 10, pp.233–234).

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In Cramer's rule, x = D_x / ____.
**Answer:** D — every unknown is its swapped determinant divided by D (Cambodia, Grade 11 Basic, pp.130–138).

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Swapping two rows of a determinant changes its ____.
**Answer:** sign — a determinant property Nicaragua verifies (Grade 10, pp.227–244).

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** det(A⁻¹) = ____ / det(A).
**Answer:** 1 — the determinant of the inverse is the reciprocal (Nicaragua, Grade 10, pp.227–244).
