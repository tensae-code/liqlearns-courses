# Surinames Cultural Heritage — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Material heritage includes…
a) rituals and stories  b) monuments, cultural landscapes, archaeological finds, books and museum objects  c) only songs
**Answer: b) monuments, cultural landscapes, archaeological finds, books and museum objects**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which is named as NOT Surinamese heritage in the leerjaar 7 book?
a) pom  b) the hamburger  c) the apinti drum
**Answer: b) the hamburger**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The culture experiment asks what happens when you…
a) throw heritage away  b) place your heritage object into another culture and reason out what that does to its meaning  c) copy another culture exactly
**Answer: b) place your heritage object into another culture and reason out what that does to its meaning**
