# Surinames Cultural Heritage — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Intangible heritage includes traditions, rituals, music, dance, stories and customs.
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The St. Petrus en Paulus cathedral and the Neveh Shalom synagogue beside the Ahmadiyya main mosque are pictured as Suriname's heritage.
**Answer: True.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In heritage research, different answers from different sources should be hidden.
**Answer: False — they are noted, and sources are checked critically.**
