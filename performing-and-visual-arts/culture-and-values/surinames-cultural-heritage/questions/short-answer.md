# Surinames Cultural Heritage — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the two kinds of heritage.
**Answer: Material heritage and intangible, living heritage.**

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Name the four research steps.
**Answer: Frame your questions; explore your sources; work out the answers, noting where sources differ; draw your conclusion.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Name three Surinamese heritage dishes from the books.
**Answer: Any three of: pom, podosiri, pepre watra, roti, bami, saoto, tjauw min, her'heri, atyupo.**
