# Video script — Surinames Cultural Heritage

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

What must a country keep?

Heritage: testimonies of a people's history and identity.

Material heritage — monuments, books, museum objects.

Living heritage — traditions, rituals, music, dance, stories.

Pom and roti. Apinti and gamelan. Koto and angisa. Anansi.

Research it in four steps. Judge why it must be kept.

That is Suriname's cultural heritage.
