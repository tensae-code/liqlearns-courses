# Hands-on — Surinames Cultural Heritage

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Observe one heritage thing
Choose one object or tradition named in this lesson. Use the book's observation way: look, and where safe smell, feel and listen; note its size, colour, material, the link between form and function, and its decoration.

## Run the four steps
In pairs, research your partner's heritage object with the four steps, then write two arguments for keeping it as Surinamese heritage — and defend them aloud.

## Try the culture experiment
Take one worked example — Anansi in a Birbal story, or the dragon dance to papa-dance music — and say what happens to its meaning. Serious to ridiculous? Joyful to serious? Argue it.
