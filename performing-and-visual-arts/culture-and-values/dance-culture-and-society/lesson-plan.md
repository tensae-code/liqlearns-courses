# Lesson plan — Dance, Culture and Society

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Culture and Values · **Lesson:** Dance, Culture and Society
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 5 — Culture and Values (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** culture is a group's characteristics and knowledge; dance — the oldest, most primal art — carries it; and culture itself has dimensions, material, normative and symbolic, in which identity lives (teacher-guide evidence).
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on teacher-guide evidence — the Grade 8 guide's body (printed pp. 45–50), which is within the guide's surviving body pages.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the
exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an
image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture
of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does

shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved (the gate: README, "Units, Lessons and Sub Lessons (ruled 2026-11-08)"):

1. **What culture is — and dance, the oldest art (teacher guide — evidence)** — culture = the characteristics and knowledge of a group: language, religion, food, traditions, values, music and arts; dance is the oldest, most primal art, with its own purposes, and dance education has its own definition (Grade 8 teacher guide, printed pp. 45–47).
2. **Culture's dimensions and identity (teacher guide — evidence)** — culture's three dimensions — material, normative, symbolic; the five cultural problem areas (power distance, uncertainty avoidance, individualism/collectivism among them); and cultural identity (Grade 8 teacher guide, printed pp. 48–50).
