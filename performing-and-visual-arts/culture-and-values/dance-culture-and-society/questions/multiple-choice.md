# Dance, Culture and Society — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The guide defines culture as —
a) the characteristics and knowledge of a group — language, religion, food, traditions, values, music and arts  b) only its food  c) only its laws
**Answer: a) the characteristics and knowledge of a group — language, religion, food, traditions, values, music and arts** — that is the guide's own definition.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The guide calls dance —
a) the oldest, most primal art  b) the newest art  c) a minor decoration
**Answer: a) the oldest, most primal art** — the body moved in pattern before instruments or paint.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Culture's three dimensions in the guide are —
a) material, normative and symbolic  b) big, medium and small  c) old, new and future
**Answer: a) material, normative and symbolic** — those three structure the guide's account.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** "Power distance" names —
a) how a group lives with authority — one of the five cultural problem areas  b) a dance jump  c) a stage measurement
**Answer: a) how a group lives with authority — one of the five cultural problem areas** — it is one of the guide's named five.
