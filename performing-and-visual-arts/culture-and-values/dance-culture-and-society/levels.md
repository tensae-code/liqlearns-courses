# Dance, Culture and Society — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure — README, "Units, Lessons and Sub Lessons"). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number, as before. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — What culture is — and dance, the oldest art (teacher guide — evidence)
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: the learner can do what this part teaches, in their own words — culture = the characteristics and knowledge of a group: language, religion, food, traditions, values, music and arts; dance is the oldest, most primal art, with its own purposes, and dance education has its own definition (Grade 8 teacher guide, printed pp. 45–47).

## Sub lesson 2 — Culture's dimensions and identity (teacher guide — evidence)
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: the learner can do what this part teaches, in their own words — culture's three dimensions — material, normative, symbolic; the five cultural problem areas (power distance, uncertainty avoidance, individualism/collectivism among them); and cultural identity (Grade 8 teacher guide, printed pp. 48–50).
