## Sub lesson 1 — Arts & Crafts G11 teacher guide — Lao Art History

The Sikhottabong period (6th–10th centuries) and the Lai Fong period (11th–13th), their Buddha images and the rock-carved Phra Siva Thep, with the spread of art along the Mekong; three great stupas in photographs and text — Wat Phou Champasak, That Ing Hang (about 25 m, rebuilt in the 16th century) and That Sikhottabong, and That Luang in Vientiane (45 m) with its building history; the four regional schools of Lao art — Luang Prabang (Wat Xieng Thong, its Buddha images), Vientiane, Champasak and Pakse — with photographs of temples and Buddha statues of each school; the two schools of the golden age (the Xaysetthathirath school and the Souriyavongsa school, 1638–1695), and its architecture — the Ho Phra Keo (built 1565), Wat Ong Teu and Wat Sisaket's ordination hall, in photographs; the art of the three successor kingdoms — Vientiane, Luang Prabang and Champasak — and of the Siamese period, with Wat Sisaket (built 1818) and its cloister of Buddha images described in detail
