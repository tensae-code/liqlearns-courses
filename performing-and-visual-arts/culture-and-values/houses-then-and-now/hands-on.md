# Hands-on — Houses Then and Now

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The two-house walk
Walk your street with an adult. Choose the oldest house and the newest house you pass. For each: what are the walls made of? The roof? Who might have built it? Tell the two stories at home.

## Build both houses
With clay, sticks, stones, grass and card, model two small houses: one in the old way, one in today's way. Put them side by side like Figure 4 and show a visitor the differences.
