This lesson rests on a single placement — Grade 3 §3.1(ሐ), printed p. 48, with its Figure 4 — and teaches only what that placement states: houses are artworks made from different things for dwelling, and their making has improved and grown from time to time. No materials list, region or building technique is claimed, because the placement names none; the old/new materials in the hands-on are the learner's own local observation, not book content.
