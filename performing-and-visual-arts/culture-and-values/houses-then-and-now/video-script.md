# Video script — Houses Then and Now

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

What is a house?
An **artwork**. Made from different things. **For dwelling.**

Look — houses of **old times**.
Made from what the land gave. Made by hand.
Look — houses of **today**.
The making has **improved and grown**.

Old and new. One story.
Both shelter the family. Both are art.
