# Lesson plan — Houses Then and Now

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Culture and Values · **Lesson:** Houses Then and Now
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 5 — Culture and Values (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** houses are artworks made for dwelling — and their making has improved and grown from old times to today.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the child's own home and the homes they pass every day.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the
exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an
image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture
of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does
with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and
while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is
dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the
student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph,
or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be
shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Traditional Dress — the 'then and now' way of looking. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say what a house is in the book's words: an artwork made from different things, for dwelling.
2. Tell how house-making has changed: it has improved and grown from time to time.
3. Look at the book's Figure 4 and point out the old-times houses and today's houses.
4. Compare a house of old times near you with a house of today, in two true sentences.

## What you need
A walk past real houses — old and new — or the lesson picture.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — Look at your own home. (lesson/lesson.md).

## If the learner is stuck
- Calls old houses 'bad' houses → the book calls every house an artwork made for dwelling; judge a house by its time and its materials.
- Thinks nothing changed → walk Figure 4 slowly, side to side: materials, shapes, roofs.
