# Houses Then and Now — short answer

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is a house made for, in the book's sentence?
**Answer:** For dwelling.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Finish the book's sentence: 'Their making has …'
**Answer:** '…improved and grown from time to time.'

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Name one thing that is the same in an old house and a new house.
**Answer:** The need underneath: a place for the family to dwell (accept any true sameness the learner can defend).
