# Houses Then and Now — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In the book's words, a house is —
a) an artwork made from different things, for dwelling  b) a natural thing, like a tree  c) only a place to sleep
**Answer: a) an artwork made from different things, for dwelling**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Figure 4 shows —
a) only today's houses  b) houses in old times and now  c) a school and a church
**Answer: b) houses in old times and now**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The book says houses' making has —
a) stopped changing  b) improved and grown from time to time  c) become worse every year
**Answer: b) improved and grown from time to time**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** An old house is best described as —
a) a failed new house  b) the artwork of its time  c) not a house at all
**Answer: b) the artwork of its time**
