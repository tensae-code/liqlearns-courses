# Colour Symbolism in Mongolian Art — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** In Mongolian life, yellow (шар) is the colour of…
a) gold, wisdom and the most honoured things  b) shame and hiding  c) cold water only
**Answer: a) gold, wisdom and the most honoured things**

**2.** Red (улаан) in Mongolian symbolism comes from…
a) fire — youth, strength and joy  b) the sea  c) winter snow
**Answer: a) fire — youth, strength and joy**

**3.** The Soyombo's top carries…
a) three flames  b) five stars  c) two fish
**Answer: a) three flames**

**4.** The colours are read in the lesson through…
a) Ts. Minjuur's painting "Öndör khangaid namar" (1967)  b) a photograph of a car  c) a map of Europe
**Answer: a) Ts. Minjuur's painting "Öndör khangaid namar" (1967)**
