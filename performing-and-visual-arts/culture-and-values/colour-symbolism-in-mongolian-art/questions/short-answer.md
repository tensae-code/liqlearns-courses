# Colour Symbolism in Mongolian Art — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1.** What does yellow mean in Mongolian life?
**Answer: Gold, wisdom and the most honoured things.**

**2.** Which painting is used to read the colours?
**Answer: Ts. Minjuur's "Öndör khangaid namar" (Autumn in the High Khangai, 1967).**
