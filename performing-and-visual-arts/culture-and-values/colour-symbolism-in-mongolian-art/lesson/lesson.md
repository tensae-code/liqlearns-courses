## Sub lesson 1 — the colour chapter: what colours mean in Mongolian life

the colour chapter: what colours mean in Mongolian life — yellow (шар) from "шороо/шир будаг", the colour of gold, wisdom and the most honoured things; red (улаан) from fire, the colour of youth, strength and joy, the three flames of the Soyombo — read through Ц.Минжүүр's painting "Өндөр хангайд намар" (1967)
