# Theatre in Society — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The guide's first way of seeing theatre in society is —
a) from faith  b) from trade  c) from sport
**Answer: a) from faith** — faith is §3.3's first standpoint.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Acted sacred stories help a community —
a) hold its beliefs in common, seen together  b) forget its stories  c) argue
**Answer: a) hold its beliefs in common, seen together** — seeing together is theatre's faith-work.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** From culture, theatre works as —
a) a mirror and storehouse of the community's way of life  b) a stranger's import only  c) pure fantasy with no community in it
**Answer: a) a mirror and storehouse of the community's way of life** — the culture on stage is the content.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A people's theatre cannot simply be swapped for another's because —
a) the culture performed is its content  b) stages differ in size  c) costumes are costly
**Answer: a) the culture performed is its content** — swap the culture, change the play.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** From history, theatre —
a) acts the community's past so memory is performed  b) ignores the past  c) only predicts the future
**Answer: a) acts the community's past so memory is performed** — performed memory is the history standpoint.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** The guide's three standpoints are —
a) faith, culture and history  b) price, size and speed  c) song, dance and drawing
**Answer: a) faith, culture and history** — those three structure §§3.3–3.3.3.
