# Video script — Theatre in Society

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Theatre from faith.
The sacred story — acted.
Seen by the whole community.
Belief, held in common.

Theatre from culture.
Customs, dress, speech — on stage.
A mirror and a storehouse.
The community sees itself.

Theatre from history.
The past — acted, not only told.
Faith, culture, history:
one stage, three services.
