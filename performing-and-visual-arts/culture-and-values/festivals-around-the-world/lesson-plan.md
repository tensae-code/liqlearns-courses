# Lesson plan — Festivals Around the World

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Culture and Values · **Lesson:** Festivals Around the World
**Place in the curriculum:** Culture and Values (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Festivals Around the World — Syria's own teaching of this topic, kept as named parts: Teacher's Day, celebrated every March to honour the teacher; the Hijri New Year, tied to the Prophet's migration on 1 Muharram; and the Gregorian New Year tied to the birth of Christ
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the course's earlier lessons in its title; recommended first, never forced.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of ..." in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **"The Holidays on Which Schools Close"** — Teacher's Day, celebrated every March to honour the teacher; the Hijri New Year, tied to the Prophet's migration on 1 Muharram; and the Gregorian New Year tied to the birth of Christ
2. **Enrichment "The Feasts and Occasions on Which Schools Close"** — Mother's Day, at spring's start on 21 March — the children race to express their joy and love for their mothers, drawing gifts for them, making them with their hands or offering flowers; think of drawing an artwork to gift your mother; the Prophet's Birthday (al-Mawlid al-Nabawi) — decorations rise in the homes and markets, and Syrians exchange greetings; imagine and create a scene of the feast's appearances; and Glorious Christmas — people decorate their homes and the Christmas tree, and make a cave imitating the cave in which Jesus was born in Bethlehem in Palestine; in Syria every home has its Christmas tree, and the children wait for Baba Noel carrying gifts
