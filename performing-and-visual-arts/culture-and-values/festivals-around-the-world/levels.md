## Sub lesson 2 — Enrichment "The Feasts and Occasions on Which Schools Close"
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Mother's Day, at spring's start on 21 March — the children race to express their joy and love for their mothers, drawing gifts for them, making them with their hands or offering flowers; think of drawing an artwork to gift your mother; the Prophet's Birthday (al-Mawlid al-Nabawi) — decorations rise in the homes and markets, and Syrians exchange greetings; imagine and create a scene of the feast's appearances; and Glorious Christmas — people decorate their homes and the Christmas tree, and make a cave imitating the cave in which Jesus was born in Bethlehem in Palestine; in Syria every home has its Christmas tree, and the children wait for Baba Noel carrying gifts
