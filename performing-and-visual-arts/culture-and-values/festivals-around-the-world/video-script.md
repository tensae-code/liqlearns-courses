# Video script — Festivals Around the World

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This is Syria's own lesson.

**"The Holidays on Which Schools Close".** Teacher's Day, celebrated every March to honour the teacher; the Hijri New Year, tied to the Prophet's migration on 1 Muharram; and the Gregorian New Year tied to the birth of Christ.

**Enrichment "The Feasts and Occasions on Which Schools Close".** Mother's Day, at spring's start on 21 March — the children race to express their joy and love for their mothers, drawing gifts for them, making them with their hands or offering flowers; think of drawing an artwork to gift your mother; the Prophet's Birthday (al-Mawlid al-Nabawi) — decorations rise in the homes and markets, and Syrians exchange greetings; imagine and create a scene of the feast's appearances; and Glorious Christmas — people decorate their homes and the Christmas tree, and make a cave imitating the cave in which Jesus was born in Bethlehem in Palestine; in Syria every home has its Christmas tree, and the children wait for Baba Noel carrying gifts.

That is Festivals Around the World — Syria's way, kept by name.
