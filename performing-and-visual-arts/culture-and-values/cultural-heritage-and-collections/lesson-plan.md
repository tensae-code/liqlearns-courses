# Lesson plan — Cultural Heritage and Collections

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Culture and Values · **Lesson:** Cultural Heritage and Collections
**Place in the curriculum:** Culture and Values (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** heritage and collections: what a collection is, how museums communicate it, heritage tangible and intangible — and how a community gathers, registers and exhibits its own heritage.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this course in the same title.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Collections and heritage, tangible and intangible** — From collecting to heritage: a **collection** is a gathered set of things kept together, and **museums communicate collections** to their public.
2. **Colombia's own heritage anchors** — Two anchors carry the idea home.
3. **Cultural management — the gestor cultural** — The older book names the profession: heritage is kept by **cultural management** — administering a cultural organisation's resources so its public is best served.
4. **The heritage project — gather, register, exhibit** — The older course ends in a real project: **formulate a cultural project** — definition, justification, objectives, methodology, resources, chronogram, evaluation — to **gather the community's cultural and musical heritage** (what music each generation of the community hears), **register it in written, visual and audio formats**, keep the project **logbook (bitácora)**, and **exhibit the findings to the community**.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
