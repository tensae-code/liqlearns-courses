# Hands-on — Cultural Heritage and Collections

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Make your own inventory
List five pieces of your community's heritage — two tangible, three intangible — and mark any that are in danger of disappearing, as the guide directs.

## Start a bitácora
Open a project logbook: today's date, what you gathered, from whom, and in which format — written, visual or audio.
