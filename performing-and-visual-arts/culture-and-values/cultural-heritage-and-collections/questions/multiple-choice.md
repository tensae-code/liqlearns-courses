# Cultural Heritage and Collections — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Heritage you can touch — objects and instruments — is called…
a) tangible heritage  b) intangible heritage  c) a chronogram
**Answer: a) tangible heritage**.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A museum's work with a collection is to…
a) communicate it to its public  b) hide it from everyone  c) sell it piece by piece
**Answer: a) communicate it to its public**.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The Muisca raft is tied to the legend of…
a) El Dorado  b) the Yuruparí  c) the Yellow Kid
**Answer: a) El Dorado**.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** A gestor cultural…
a) administers a cultural organisation's resources for its public  b) paints only portraits  c) builds skate bowls
**Answer: a) administers a cultural organisation's resources for its public**.

**5. (Sub lesson 4)** A heritage project registers findings in…
a) written, visual and audio formats  b) only in memory  c) only in gold
**Answer: a) written, visual and audio formats**.
