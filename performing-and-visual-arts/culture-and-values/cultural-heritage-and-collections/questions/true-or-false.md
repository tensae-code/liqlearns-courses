# Cultural Heritage and Collections — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Traditions and skills can be heritage, not only objects.
**Answer: True** — traditions, skills, objects and instruments are all named as heritage.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The class inventories only heritage that is safe and famous.
**Answer: False** — the inventory includes what is in danger of disappearing.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Cultural management means serving a cultural organisation's public as well as possible.
**Answer: True** — administering its resources so its public is best served.

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** The heritage project ends by exhibiting the findings to the community.
**Answer: True** — gathering, registering and exhibiting are its three moves.
