# Cultural Heritage and Collections — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name one tangible and one intangible piece of heritage.
**Answer: Any object or instrument for tangible; any tradition or skill for intangible.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why does the visual-arts guide call the Río Guayabero rock paintings heritage?
**Answer: They are the first images of this land — heritage to know and value.**

**3. (Sub lesson 4)** What is the bitácora of the heritage project?
**Answer: The project logbook, where the gathering work is kept as it goes.**
