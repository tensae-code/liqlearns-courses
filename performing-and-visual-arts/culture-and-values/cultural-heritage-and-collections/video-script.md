# Video script — Cultural Heritage and Collections

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Some things we keep in boxes. Some things we keep in our hands and songs.

A **collection** is things gathered and kept together. A museum **communicates** its collections — it shows them and tells them.

**Heritage** is bigger. Objects and instruments — that is **tangible**. Traditions and skills — that is **intangible**.

Colombia keeps the **Muisca raft** of El Dorado, and the **rock paintings of Río Guayabero**, the first images of this land.

And the work has a name: **cultural management**, and a worker: the **gestor cultural**.

Your project: gather your community's heritage. Register it — written, visual, audio. Keep your **bitácora**. Then exhibit it to the community.
