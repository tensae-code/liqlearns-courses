# Lesson plan — Arts of Many Cultures

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Culture and Values · **Lesson:** Arts of Many Cultures
**Place in the curriculum:** Culture and Values (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Arts of Many Cultures — Armenia's own teaching of this topic, kept as named parts: Do ALL nations have folk songs — every nation has its culture; the French sing theirs in French: "He was a little shepherd" heard and performed
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the course's earlier lessons in its title; recommended first, never forced.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of ..." in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Do ALL nations have folk songs** — Do ALL nations have folk songs — every nation has its culture; the French sing theirs in French: "He was a little shepherd" heard and performed
2. **What calls are heard in other cultures** — What calls are heard in other cultures — the Maori of far New Zealand and their song "Tutira Mai — Tatou Tatou E": a call-and-answer song of another people
3. **Is spring heralded only in Armenia** — Is spring heralded only in Armenia — Japan's sakura: the cherry trees bloom at spring's start and the sakura is Japan's symbol; the song "Sakura" sung
4. **Intercultural links (teacher-guide evidence)** — Intercultural links (teacher-guide evidence) — through songs and dances of different cultures learners compare them and think critically; the Armenian ashugh songs are the home example
5. **Folk song and dance** — Folk song and dance — a people's music is shaped by its language, climate and geography: northern and southern peoples sing differently
6. **JOURNEY AROUND THE WORLD** — JOURNEY AROUND THE WORLD — pre-art; a visit to Ancient Egypt; a visit to the Armenian GOLDEN AGE; a visit to the Armenian MIDDLE AGES; a visit to the Renaissance; a visit to JAPAN; a journey into the future — art in the world's different corners, alike and unlike
7. **Contemporary music genres in the world and Armenia** — Contemporary music genres in the world and Armenia — the first concert programme: Blues, Jazz, Armenian jazz, Rock, British beat
8. **Theme 1** — Theme 1: Contemporary music videos in the world and in Armenia; ETHNIC MUSIC — through modern communications everything in the world becomes reachable: ethnic music as a living kind (teacher-guide evidence)
9. **Pop music in the yearly plan (teacher-guide evidence)** — Pop music in the yearly plan (teacher-guide evidence) — Frank Sinatra and the Russian pop stage named in the plan's pop hours
10. **National music in the world context (teacher-guide evidence)** — National music in the world context (teacher-guide evidence) — develop the understanding and valuing of national music within world music, and reveal the role of tradition and innovation in national and world music alike
11. **Art in the land of the rising sun** — Art in the land of the rising sun — the arts of Japan met as another people's heritage

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
