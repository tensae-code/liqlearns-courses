## Sub lesson 1 — Do ALL nations have folk songs

Do ALL nations have folk songs — every nation has its culture; the French sing theirs in French: "He was a little shepherd" heard and performed.

## Sub lesson 2 — What calls are heard in other cultures

What calls are heard in other cultures — the Maori of far New Zealand and their song "Tutira Mai — Tatou Tatou E": a call-and-answer song of another people.

## Sub lesson 3 — Is spring heralded only in Armenia

Is spring heralded only in Armenia — Japan's sakura: the cherry trees bloom at spring's start and the sakura is Japan's symbol; the song "Sakura" sung.

## Sub lesson 4 — Intercultural links (teacher-guide evidence)

Intercultural links (teacher-guide evidence) — through songs and dances of different cultures learners compare them and think critically; the Armenian ashugh songs are the home example.

## Sub lesson 5 — Folk song and dance

Folk song and dance — a people's music is shaped by its language, climate and geography: northern and southern peoples sing differently.

## Sub lesson 6 — JOURNEY AROUND THE WORLD

JOURNEY AROUND THE WORLD — pre-art; a visit to Ancient Egypt; a visit to the Armenian GOLDEN AGE; a visit to the Armenian MIDDLE AGES; a visit to the Renaissance; a visit to JAPAN; a journey into the future — art in the world's different corners, alike and unlike.

## Sub lesson 7 — Contemporary music genres in the world and Armenia

Contemporary music genres in the world and Armenia — the first concert programme: Blues, Jazz, Armenian jazz, Rock, British beat.

## Sub lesson 8 — Theme 1

Theme 1: Contemporary music videos in the world and in Armenia; ETHNIC MUSIC — through modern communications everything in the world becomes reachable: ethnic music as a living kind (teacher-guide evidence).

## Sub lesson 9 — Pop music in the yearly plan (teacher-guide evidence)

Pop music in the yearly plan (teacher-guide evidence) — Frank Sinatra and the Russian pop stage named in the plan's pop hours.

## Sub lesson 10 — National music in the world context (teacher-guide evidence)

National music in the world context (teacher-guide evidence) — develop the understanding and valuing of national music within world music, and reveal the role of tradition and innovation in national and world music alike.

## Sub lesson 11 — Art in the land of the rising sun

Art in the land of the rising sun — the arts of Japan met as another people's heritage.
