# Video script — Arts of Many Cultures

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This is Armenia's own lesson.

**Do ALL nations have folk songs.** Do ALL nations have folk songs — every nation has its culture; the French sing theirs in French: "He was a little shepherd" heard and performed.

**What calls are heard in other cultures.** What calls are heard in other cultures — the Maori of far New Zealand and their song "Tutira Mai — Tatou Tatou E": a call-and-answer song of another people.

**Is spring heralded only in Armenia.** Is spring heralded only in Armenia — Japan's sakura: the cherry trees bloom at spring's start and the sakura is Japan's symbol; the song "Sakura" sung.

**Intercultural links (teacher-guide evidence).** Intercultural links (teacher-guide evidence) — through songs and dances of different cultures learners compare them and think critically; the Armenian ashugh songs are the home example.

**Folk song and dance.** Folk song and dance — a people's music is shaped by its language, climate and geography: northern and southern peoples sing differently.

**JOURNEY AROUND THE WORLD.** JOURNEY AROUND THE WORLD — pre-art; a visit to Ancient Egypt; a visit to the Armenian GOLDEN AGE; a visit to the Armenian MIDDLE AGES; a visit to the Renaissance; a visit to JAPAN; a journey into the future — art in the world's different corners, alike and unlike.

**Contemporary music genres in the world and Armenia.** Contemporary music genres in the world and Armenia — the first concert programme: Blues, Jazz, Armenian jazz, Rock, British beat.

**Theme 1.** Theme 1: Contemporary music videos in the world and in Armenia; ETHNIC MUSIC — through modern communications everything in the world becomes reachable: ethnic music as a living kind (teacher-guide evidence).

**Pop music in the yearly plan (teacher-guide evidence).** Pop music in the yearly plan (teacher-guide evidence) — Frank Sinatra and the Russian pop stage named in the plan's pop hours.

**National music in the world context (teacher-guide evidence).** National music in the world context (teacher-guide evidence) — develop the understanding and valuing of national music within world music, and reveal the role of tradition and innovation in national and world music alike.

**Art in the land of the rising sun.** Art in the land of the rising sun — the arts of Japan met as another people's heritage.

That is Arts of Many Cultures — Armenia's way, kept by name.
