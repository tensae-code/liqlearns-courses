# Video script — North Macedonian Calendar Customs and Ritual Songs

---

This is North Macedonia's own lesson.

**Традиција и обичаи кај муслиманскиот народ.** Традиција и обичаи кај муслиманскиот народ — Ramadan and Bajram: the month of fasting, prayer, solidarity and mercy; suhoor before dawn and iftar at sunset; the Bajram prayer in every mosque, the family table, the baklava, and gifts for the poor and the sick.

**Ѓурѓовден (Ederlezi) among the Roma and all the country's peoples.** Ѓурѓовден (Ederlezi) among the Roma and all the country's peoples: green branches on doors and windows, wreaths woven of "St George's flowers", washing in water steeped with plants, bathing in the river before sunrise, and the songs of the waking vegetation.

**music in the customs and traditions of North Macedonia's peoples.** music in the customs and traditions of North Macedonia's peoples: ritual songs and dances of the calendar (Varvara songs, Christmas and Vodici songs, Lazarus songs, Easter songs, Gjurgjovden herb-gathering songs, dodole rain-calling, Albanian Buzmi and Letnik, Bajram songs) and of the life cycle (lullabies, children's play and counting songs, circumcision songs, wedding songs, funeral laments) — tradition carried orally, collectively, in variants.

**Женска поворка.** Женска поворка — the women's processions: the Vodici (Epiphany) games that close the Unbaptised Days, singing the crosses and the water that gains protective power; and the Lazarici, the girls' processions of Lazarus Saturday that visit houses, meadows and fields and announce the spring.

**Народни обичаи во Македонија: Бадник.** Народни обичаи во Македонија: Бадник — the folk customs of Macedonia, and Badnik (Christmas Eve): the koledari children going house to house with bags and staffs, gathering koledashki buns, coins and apples; the bonfires with warm rakija, wine, ora and songs; the cutting and burning of the Badnik log; the ritual breads, and the lepche broken for the coin that brings luck all year; the blessed oak branches hung at the door.

**Божик.** Божик — Christmas: the day of Christ's birth, the second greatest feast of Christianity after Easter — 25 December for the churches of the Gregorian calendar, 7 January for those of the Julian; with Badnik's evening the pre-Christmas fast ends.

**Божикна песна.** Божикна песна — a Christmas song (carol) by an unknown author, in score for the children's instruments to play.

That is North Macedonian Calendar Customs and Ritual Songs — North Macedonia's way, kept by name.
