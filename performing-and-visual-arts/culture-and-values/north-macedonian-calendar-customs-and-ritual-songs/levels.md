# North Macedonian Calendar Customs and Ritual Songs — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — Традиција и обичаи кај муслиманскиот народ
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Традиција и обичаи кај муслиманскиот народ — Ramadan and Bajram: the month of fasting, prayer, solidarity and mercy; suhoor before dawn and iftar at sunset; the Bajram prayer in every mosque, the family table, the baklava, and gifts for the poor and the sick.

## Sub lesson 2 — Ѓурѓовден (Ederlezi) among the Roma and all the country's peoples
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Ѓурѓовден (Ederlezi) among the Roma and all the country's peoples: green branches on doors and windows, wreaths woven of "St George's flowers", washing in water steeped with plants, bathing in the river before sunrise, and the songs of the waking vegetation.

## Sub lesson 3 — music in the customs and traditions of North Macedonia's peoples
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: music in the customs and traditions of North Macedonia's peoples: ritual songs and dances of the calendar (Varvara songs, Christmas and Vodici songs, Lazarus songs, Easter songs, Gjurgjovden herb-gathering songs, dodole rain-calling, Albanian Buzmi and Letnik, Bajram songs) and of the life cycle (lullabies, children's play and counting songs, circumcision songs, wedding songs, funeral laments) — tradition carried orally, collectively, in variants.

## Sub lesson 4 — Женска поворка
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Женска поворка — the women's processions: the Vodici (Epiphany) games that close the Unbaptised Days, singing the crosses and the water that gains protective power; and the Lazarici, the girls' processions of Lazarus Saturday that visit houses, meadows and fields and announce the spring.

## Sub lesson 5 — Народни обичаи во Македонија: Бадник
Sub lesson 5 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Народни обичаи во Македонија: Бадник — the folk customs of Macedonia, and Badnik (Christmas Eve): the koledari children going house to house with bags and staffs, gathering koledashki buns, coins and apples; the bonfires with warm rakija, wine, ora and songs; the cutting and burning of the Badnik log; the ritual breads, and the lepche broken for the coin that brings luck all year; the blessed oak branches hung at the door.

## Sub lesson 6 — Божик
Sub lesson 6 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Божик — Christmas: the day of Christ's birth, the second greatest feast of Christianity after Easter — 25 December for the churches of the Gregorian calendar, 7 January for those of the Julian; with Badnik's evening the pre-Christmas fast ends.

Sub lesson 7 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Божикна песна — a Christmas song (carol) by an unknown author, in score for the children's instruments to play.
