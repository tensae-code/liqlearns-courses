# Sasna Tsrer Epic in Art

## Do it first

Say what it is in your own words. If you can name one, you are already inside the lesson.

## Sub lesson 1 — The EPIC (էպոս) in art

The EPIC (էպոս) in art — "the epic is the splendid treasury of the Armenian people's life": Yervand Kochar's illustrations and the Sasuntsi Davit statue (second version, set up 3 Dec 1959; the first, gypsum, 1939), Hakob Kojoyan's Davit, and Davit as a wall-relief (որմնաքանդակ) at the centre.

## Sub lesson 2 — EPIC SONGS (վիպական երգեր)

EPIC SONGS (վիպական երգեր) — tales sung since ancient times: the Sasna Tsrer epic, much of it sung in Davit's own voice; Kochar's painting "Sasuntsi Davit with Kurkik Jalali" (1939); hear the epic's sung fragments by the Kayt ensemble; gather the other nations' epics.

## Sub lesson 3 — The SASNA TSRER epic on the opera stage

The SASNA TSRER epic on the opera stage — in folk music the epic was told and sung whole; in opera art the first turn to the "Sasna Tsrer" theme was Komitas's; Edgar Hovhannisyan (1930-1998) made the opera-ballet "Sasuntsi Davit" on the epic; Spendiaryan wrote no ballet, but ballets were made from his works.
