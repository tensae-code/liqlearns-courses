# Video script — Sasna Tsrer Epic in Art

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This is Armenia's own lesson.

**The EPIC (էպոս) in art.** The EPIC (էպոս) in art — "the epic is the splendid treasury of the Armenian people's life": Yervand Kochar's illustrations and the Sasuntsi Davit statue (second version, set up 3 Dec 1959; the first, gypsum, 1939), Hakob Kojoyan's Davit, and Davit as a wall-relief (որմնաքանդակ) at the centre.

**EPIC SONGS (վիպական երգեր).** EPIC SONGS (վիպական երգեր) — tales sung since ancient times: the Sasna Tsrer epic, much of it sung in Davit's own voice; Kochar's painting "Sasuntsi Davit with Kurkik Jalali" (1939); hear the epic's sung fragments by the Kayt ensemble; gather the other nations' epics.

**The SASNA TSRER epic on the opera stage.** The SASNA TSRER epic on the opera stage — in folk music the epic was told and sung whole; in opera art the first turn to the "Sasna Tsrer" theme was Komitas's; Edgar Hovhannisyan (1930-1998) made the opera-ballet "Sasuntsi Davit" on the epic; Spendiaryan wrote no ballet, but ballets were made from his works.

That is Sasna Tsrer Epic in Art — Armenia's way, kept by name.
