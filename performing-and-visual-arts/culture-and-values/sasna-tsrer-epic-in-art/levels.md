## Sub lesson 3 — The SASNA TSRER epic on the opera stage
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: The SASNA TSRER epic on the opera stage — in folk music the epic was told and sung whole; in opera art the first turn to the "Sasna Tsrer" theme was Komitas's; Edgar Hovhannisyan (1930-1998) made the opera-ballet "Sasuntsi Davit" on the epic; Spendiaryan wrote no ballet, but ballets were made from his works
