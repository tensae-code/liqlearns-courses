# Festivals and Their Arts — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which list is the book's festival set?
a) Meskel, Arafa, Genna, Mawlid, Timket, Fasika  b) only Meskel and Timket  c) Christmas, Easter and New Year only
**Answer: a) Meskel, Arafa, Genna, Mawlid, Timket, Fasika**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Meskel's great pictured art is —
a) the Demera fire  b) a boat procession  c) the Genna game
**Answer: a) the Demera fire**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Timket is celebrated every year in the month of —
a) Tahsas  b) Tir  c) Meskerem
**Answer: b) Tir** — with the tabots carried in procession.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Arafa is a great festival of —
a) Muslim believers  b) Orthodox believers only  c) farmers only
**Answer: a) Muslim believers**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** The Genna game is played with —
a) round shields  b) curved sticks like the degan, and a ball  c) ropes
**Answer: b) curved sticks like the degan, and a ball**

**6. (Sub lesson 4)** At New Year, the girls sing at doors carrying —
a) flowers, with a kebero hung on them  b) water jars  c) school books
**Answer: a) flowers, with a kebero hung on them**

**7. (Sub lesson 5)** Adwa Victory Day is celebrated with —
a) silence  b) fukera, shilela, qererito and songs  c) the Genna game
**Answer: b) fukera, shilela, qererito and songs**
