# Festivals and Their Arts — true or false

Say if the line is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Meskel is among the most highly honoured festivals of Orthodox believers.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Genna is celebrated at the end of Tahsas.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 4)** The New Year song the girls sing is 'አበባዬሽ ሆይ' (Abebayesh Hoy).
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 5)** The Adwa patriots fought with shield, spear and sword.
**Answer: True**

**5. (Sub lesson 1)** The book's festival set names six festivals.
**Answer: True** — Meskel, Arafa, Genna, Mawlid, Timket and Fasika.
