# Festivals and Their Arts — short answer

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the six festivals in the book's set.
**Answer:** Meskel, Arafa, Genna, Mawlid, Timket and Fasika (Easter).

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** What is the Genna game called in Amharic?
**Answer:** እንቁር.

**3. (Sub lesson 4)** What does the book's Activity 4 ask you to collect?
**Answer:** The melodies and poems sung at Genna, Arafa, Timket and Eid al-Fitr in your area, to show in song and dance.

**4. (Sub lesson 5)** Name the three praise arts of Adwa Day.
**Answer:** Fukera, shilela and qererito (and different songs).
