# Festivals and Their Arts — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure — README, "Units, Lessons and Sub Lessons"). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number, as before. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — The festivals of the year
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: the learner can do what this part teaches, in their own words and with their own hands — the book's set: Meskel, Arafa, Genna, Mawlid, Timket and Fasika (Easter).

## Sub lesson 2 — Meskel and Timket
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: the learner can do what this part teaches, in their own words and with their own hands — meskel, honoured among Orthodox believers, and its Demera fire; Timket in Tir, tabots in procession.

## Sub lesson 3 — Arafa and Genna — the Genna game
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: the learner can do what this part teaches, in their own words and with their own hands — arafa, a great festival of Muslim believers; Genna at the end of Tahsas, and the እንቁር game with curved sticks.

## Sub lesson 4 — New Year — girls singing Abebayesh
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: the learner can do what this part teaches, in their own words and with their own hands — the New Year (ዘመን መለወጫ): girls with flowers and a kebero, singing አበባዬሽ ሆይ from door to door.

## Sub lesson 5 — Adwa Victory Day and its praise songs
Sub lesson 5 questions from the type files. Pass mark: the learner can do what this part teaches, in their own words and with their own hands — the patriots' victory, celebrated with fukera, shilela, qererito and song.
