# Hands-on — Festivals and Their Arts

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The festival year wheel
Draw a big circle — the year. Around it, place the six festivals of the book's set, each with a tiny drawing of its art: fire, procession, game, flowers, praise singers. Hang the wheel where your family can see it.

## Collect your area's festival arts
As the book's Activity 4 asks: with your family, collect the melodies and poems sung at Genna, Arafa, Timket and Eid al-Fitr in your area. Learn one by heart — by listening, the way such songs have always travelled.
