# Lesson plan — Festivals and Their Arts

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Culture and Values · **Lesson:** Festivals and Their Arts
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 5 — Culture and Values (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Ethiopia's great festivals and the arts that carry them — Meskel's fire, Timket's procession, Arafa, Genna and its game, New Year's singing girls, and Adwa Victory Day's praise songs.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the festivals the child has already seen and heard in their own community's year.

exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved (the gate: README, "Units, Lessons and Sub Lessons (ruled 2026-11-08)"):

1. **The festivals of the year** — The book's set: Meskel, Arafa, Genna, Mawlid, Timket and Fasika (Easter).
2. **Meskel and Timket** — Meskel, honoured among Orthodox believers, and its Demera fire; Timket in Tir, tabots in procession.
3. **Arafa and Genna — the Genna game** — Arafa, a great festival of Muslim believers; Genna at the end of Tahsas, and the እንቁር game with curved sticks.
4. **New Year — girls singing Abebayesh** — The New Year (ዘመን መለወጫ): girls with flowers and a kebero, singing አበባዬሽ ሆይ from door to door.
5. **Adwa Victory Day and its praise songs** — The patriots' victory, celebrated with fukera, shilela, qererito and song.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Our Community's Values — festivals are values celebrated. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name Ethiopia's religious festivals as the book sets them: Meskel, Arafa, Genna, Mawlid, Timket and Fasika.
2. Tell Meskel's and Timket's arts: the Demera fire; the tabots carried in procession in Tir.
3. Tell Arafa's and Genna's arts — and how the Genna game is played, with curved sticks.
4. Tell the New Year singing (አበባዬሽ ሆይ, Abebayesh Hoy) and Adwa Victory Day's praise arts: fukera, shilela, qererito.

## What you need
Your memory of a festival — its sounds, colours and crowds.

## If the learner is stuck
- Mixes Meskel's fire with Timket's procession → anchor each to its picture: Meskel = Demera fire; Timket = tabots carried, in Tir.
- Thinks Genna is only a date → Genna has a game: curved sticks, the ball, እንቁር — and a game song.
