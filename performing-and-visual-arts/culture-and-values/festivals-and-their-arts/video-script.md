# Video script — Festivals and Their Arts

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Six festivals crown the year.
**Meskel. Arafa. Genna. Mawlid. Timket. Fasika.**

Meskel: the **Demera fire**, tall and bright.
Timket, in **Tir**: the **tabots**, carried in procession.
Arafa: the ceremonies of Muslim believers.
Genna, at **Tahsas'** end: the game — curved sticks, the ball, **እንቁር**!
New Year: girls with flowers, singing **አበባዬሽ ሆይ** at your door.
Adwa Day: **fukera, shilela, qererito** — praise for the heroes.

Every festival has its arts.
