# Cyril and Methodius

## Do it first

Name one thing that belongs in this lesson — cyril and Methodius — its forms, history and cultural meaning, taught as one topic lesson. Say what it is in your own words. If you can name one, you are already inside the lesson.

## Sub lesson 1 — Св. Кирил и Методиј

Св. Кирил и Методиј — Saints Cyril and Methodius: the Thessaloniki brothers, missionaries to the Slavs of Great Moravia and Pannonia, inventors of the Glagolitic alphabet, the first letters in which Old Slavonic was written; honoured as Equal to the Apostles and, since 1980, co-patrons of Europe.

## Sub lesson 2 — "Мојата песна"

"Мојата песна" — Marko K. Cepenkov's song of the two brothers: Macedonia spread from south to north, the brothers in the flowers holding the book, praying God to let them write the Slavic letters — az, buke, vede, glagole — so the Slavs might sing, learn and pray; the brothers and their work as inspiration to writers, poets, painters and composers, Trajko Prokopiev among them.

## Do It Yourself

1. Choose one named part of this lesson and find out one more true thing about it — from a person who knows it, or from the book your part came from.
2. Make or perform a small piece in the way of that part: a rhythm, a step, a drawing, a shaped piece of clay.
3. Write one sentence: what does this part show about North Macedonia's own way of teaching its arts?
