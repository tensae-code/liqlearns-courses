# Lesson plan — Cyril and Methodius

**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 5 — Culture and Values (CURRICULUM.md).

**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the course's earlier lessons in its title; recommended first, never forced.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of ..." in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Св. Кирил и Методиј** — Св. Кирил и Методиј — Saints Cyril and Methodius: the Thessaloniki brothers, missionaries to the Slavs of Great Moravia and Pannonia, inventors of the Glagolitic alphabet, the first letters in which Old Slavonic was written; honoured as Equal to the Apostles and, since 1980, co-patrons of Europe.
2. **"Мојата песна"** — "Мојата песна" — Marko K. Cepenkov's song of the two brothers: Macedonia spread from south to north, the brothers in the flowers holding the book, praying God to let them write the Slavic letters — az, buke, vede, glagole — so the Slavs might sing, learn and pray; the brothers and their work as inspiration to writers, poets, painters and composers, Trajko Prokopiev among them.
