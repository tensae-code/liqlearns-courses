## Sub lesson 1 — Св. Кирил и Методиј
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Св. Кирил и Методиј — Saints Cyril and Methodius: the Thessaloniki brothers, missionaries to the Slavs of Great Moravia and Pannonia, inventors of the Glagolitic alphabet, the first letters in which Old Slavonic was written; honoured as Equal to the Apostles and, since 1980, co-patrons of Europe.

## Sub lesson 2 — "Мојата песна"
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: "Мојата песна" — Marko K. Cepenkov's song of the two brothers: Macedonia spread from south to north, the brothers in the flowers holding the book, praying God to let them write the Slavic letters — az, buke, vede, glagole — so the Slavs might sing, learn and pray; the brothers and their work as inspiration to writers, poets, painters and composers, Trajko Prokopiev among them.
