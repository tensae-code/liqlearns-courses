# Dominican Carnival — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name two things that fill the carnival.
**Answer: Any two of: masquerades, comparsas, dances, rejoicing.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does a comparsa do at carnival?
**Answer: It parades as a costumed troupe.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What ritual object accompanies the carnival costume?
**Answer: The fuete — an inflated cow-bladder whip.**
