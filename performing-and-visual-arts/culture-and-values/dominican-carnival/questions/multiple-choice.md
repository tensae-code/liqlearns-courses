# Dominican Carnival — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Carnival is…
a) a popular festival of masquerades, comparsas and dances  b) a quiet exam  c) a harvest only
**Answer: a) a popular festival of masquerades, comparsas and dances**.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The Guloyas are typical folklore characters of…
a) San Pedro de Macorís  b) another country  c) the sea
**Answer: a) San Pedro de Macorís**.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A comparsa is…
a) a costumed troupe  b) a drum  c) a mask shop
**Answer: a) a costumed troupe**.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** The Grade 4 costume is made from…
a) an old long-sleeved shirt covered in strips of newspaper or snack bags  b) new silk  c) cardboard only
**Answer: a) an old long-sleeved shirt covered in strips of newspaper or snack bags**.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Two named careta types are…
a) Pepinera and Joyera  b) Red and Blue  c) Big and Small
**Answer: a) Pepinera and Joyera**.
