# Hands-on — Dominican Carnival

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Make a comparsa plan
With your group, choose your comparsa's theme and name, the way the Grade 2 class does — then list the costume each member will make from recycled materials.

## Start a careta
Begin a carnival mask on paper or card: draw its face, choose its type — Pepinera, Joyera, or your own — and mark where the colour will go.
