# Folk Crafts of Ethiopia — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** የህዝብ እጅ ሥራዎች means…
  a) The people's hand work — folk handicrafts b) Factory goods c) Imported ornaments
**Answer: a) The people's hand work — folk handicrafts** — የህዝብ እጅ ሥራዎች is the people's hand work.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Embroidery works thread into…
  a) Cloth b) Leather c) Clay
**Answer: a) Cloth** — embroidery is thread worked into cloth.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Leather is worked by…
  a) Cutting, stitching and finishing b) Only painting c) Only burning
**Answer: a) Cutting, stitching and finishing** — leather craft cuts, stitches and finishes skin into goods.

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** The two named jewellery pieces are…
  a) ማርዳ and ድሪ b) ሰንሰለት and ቀለበት c) ክዳን and ጠፍጣፍ
**Answer: a) ማርዳ and ድሪ** — ማርዳ (marda) and ድሪ (diri) — named exactly as the book prints them.

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** The crafts' benefit chapter teaches that crafts…
  a) Earn livings, carry identity, and make local wealth b) Belong only in museums c) Are finished and gone
**Answer: a) Earn livings, carry identity, and make local wealth** — livings, identity in usable form, local materials into local wealth.

**6. (Sub lesson 6)** The Grade 8 opener says folk handcraft works are found —
a) among all the country's nations and peoples  b) in one town only  c) only abroad
**Answer: a) among all the country's nations and peoples** — the opener is country-wide.

**7. (Sub lesson 6)** Each people's handcraft works carry —
a) their own cultural meaning and benefit  b) no meaning  c) only a price
**Answer: a) their own cultural meaning and benefit** — meaning and benefit are the opener's two words.

**8. (Sub lesson 7)** Which of these is in the Grade 8 embroidery-goods list?
a) wenfit (a sieve)  b) a motor car  c) a printing press
**Answer: a) wenfit (a sieve)** — wenfit is one of the book's named goods.

**9. (Sub lesson 7)** The book names which thread inputs?
a) gramta, qetema and kesha  b) wire and rope only  c) none — it names no inputs
**Answer: a) gramta, qetema and kesha** — those are the listed thread inputs.

**10. (Sub lesson 8)** In the guide's framing, traditional clothing is —
a) heritage worn on the body, made by the crafts  b) unrelated to craft  c) only for museums
**Answer: a) heritage worn on the body, made by the crafts** — the connection chapter carries crafts into dress.

**11. (Sub lesson 8)** A heritage artist is —
a) a craftsperson whose work keeps a people's heritage alive  b) only a painter of portraits  c) a machine operator
**Answer: a) a craftsperson whose work keeps a people's heritage alive** — keeping-by-making is the guide's framing.
