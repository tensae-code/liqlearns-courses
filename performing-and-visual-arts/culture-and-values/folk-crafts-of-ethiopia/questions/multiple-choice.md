# Folk Crafts of Ethiopia — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** የህዝብ እጅ ሥራዎች means…
  a) The people's hand work — folk handicrafts b) Factory goods c) Imported ornaments
**Answer: a) The people's hand work — folk handicrafts** — የህዝብ እጅ ሥራዎች is the people's hand work.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Embroidery works thread into…
  a) Cloth b) Leather c) Clay
**Answer: a) Cloth** — embroidery is thread worked into cloth.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Leather is worked by…
  a) Cutting, stitching and finishing b) Only painting c) Only burning
**Answer: a) Cutting, stitching and finishing** — leather craft cuts, stitches and finishes skin into goods.

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** The two named jewellery pieces are…
  a) ማርዳ and ድሪ b) ሰንሰለት and ቀለበት c) ክዳን and ጠፍጣፍ
**Answer: a) ማርዳ and ድሪ** — ማርዳ (marda) and ድሪ (diri) — named exactly as the book prints them.

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** The crafts' benefit chapter teaches that crafts…
  a) Earn livings, carry identity, and make local wealth b) Belong only in museums c) Are finished and gone
**Answer: a) Earn livings, carry identity, and make local wealth** — livings, identity in usable form, local materials into local wealth.
