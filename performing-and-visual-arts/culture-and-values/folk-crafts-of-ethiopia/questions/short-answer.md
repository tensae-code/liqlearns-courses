# Folk Crafts of Ethiopia — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** From what are folk crafts made?
**Answer: From the people's own materials, near at hand.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What do both crafts begin with?
**Answer: A plant or a thread — and patient hands.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which crafts meet in a traditional outfit?
**Answer: Woven and embroidered cloth, leather work, and ornaments/jewellery.**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** What does a piece's name carry inside it?
**Answer: Its people, its material and its occasion.**

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** Give the benefit phrase in Amharic.
**Answer: የህዝብ እጅ ሥራዎች ለህብረተሰቡ ያላቸው ፋይዳ.**
