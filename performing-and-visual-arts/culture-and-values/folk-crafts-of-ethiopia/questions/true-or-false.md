# Folk Crafts of Ethiopia — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Folk craft skills are passed hand to hand.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Woven grass makes useful things like containers and mats.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Old dress shows the crafts worn together as one outfit.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** A jewellery piece's name is kept in its own language, not translated away.
**Answer: True**

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** Naming the crafts helps a society keep them.
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 6)** The Grade 8 book asks both what a craft is for and what it means to its people.
**Answer: True**

**7. (Sub lesson 7)** The Grade 8 book records that the embroidery goods are made mostly by women.
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 8)** This part stands on teacher-guide evidence, not on a pupil textbook.
**Answer: True**
