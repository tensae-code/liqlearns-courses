# Hands-on — Folk Crafts of Ethiopia

The lesson with your hands, your feet and your voice together, not only your eyes.

## Craft sorting table
Lay out (or draw) one piece per family — embroidered cloth, woven grass, leather, jewellery. Sort them by material, then by method: stitched, woven, cut, cast. The sorting IS the lesson.

## One stitch, one coil
Try one elementary move of two crafts: a running stitch on scrap cloth, and the start of a grass coil. Ten minutes each teaches what the words cannot: why these crafts take skill and time.
