# Video script — Folk Crafts of Ethiopia

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Hands first. The folk crafts are Ethiopia's oldest factories.
Thread on cloth: embroidery. Grass in coils: weaving.
Leather cut and stitched. Jewellery named piece by piece.
A craft is a living — and a memory that keeps working.
