# Video script — Syrian Folk Costumes

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

This is Syria's own lesson.

**"Syrian Folk Costumes".** the folk dress of Syria's regions — the Badia, Hauran, Deir ez-Zor, and Circassian folk dress — let us draw a folk celebration.

**Enrichment "The Syrian Folk Costumes".** the folk dress of Palmyra, of the Homs countryside and of Hama; and the Newroz feast — costumes from the Kurdish folklore.

That is Syrian Folk Costumes — Syria's way, kept by name.
