# Syrian Folk Costumes

## Do it first

Name one thing that belongs in this lesson — Syrian Folk Costumes — Syria's own teaching of this topic, kept as named parts: the folk dress of Syria's regions — the Badia, Hauran, Deir ez-Zor, and Circassian folk dress — let us draw a folk celebration. Say what it is in your own words. If you can name one, you are already inside the lesson.

## Sub lesson 1 — "Syrian Folk Costumes"

the folk dress of Syria's regions — the Badia, Hauran, Deir ez-Zor, and Circassian folk dress — let us draw a folk celebration.

## Sub lesson 2 — Enrichment "The Syrian Folk Costumes"

the folk dress of Palmyra, of the Homs countryside and of Hama; and the Newroz feast — costumes from the Kurdish folklore.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson holds, part by part, in your own words. Companion checks: did every part get named, with its Syrian name kept where the book gives one?

## Do It Yourself

1. Choose one named part of this lesson and find out one more true thing about it — from a person who knows it, or from the book your part came from.
2. Make or perform a small piece in the way of that part: a rhythm, a step, a drawing, a shaped piece of clay.
3. Write one sentence: what does this part show about Syria's own way of teaching its arts?
