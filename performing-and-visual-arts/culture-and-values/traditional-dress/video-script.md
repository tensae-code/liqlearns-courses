# Video script — Traditional Dress

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

White. Bright white.
This is the **ቀሚስ** — the qemis.
See the borders? Coloured. Woven. shining.
That is **tibeb** — the weaver's art, worn.

Long ago: clothes of **skin, of hair, of cotton**.
Today: clothes made in **modern ways**.
Both are ours. Both we **protect**.
Both we **pass on**.
