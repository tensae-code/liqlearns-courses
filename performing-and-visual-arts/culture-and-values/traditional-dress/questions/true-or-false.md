# Traditional Dress — true or false

Say if the line is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** T-shirts can carry traditional tibeb patterns.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Appreciating dress means never wearing it.
**Answer: False** — it is worn with care on the community's great days.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Today, no clothes are made in modern ways.
**Answer: False** — many clothes are made in modern ways today.
