# Traditional Dress — short answer

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name two forms of traditional dress the photographs show.
**Answer:** T-shirts printed with tibeb patterns; the full white qemis with coloured borders and a cross.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** When is traditional dress worn — name two occasions.
**Answer:** Any two of: festivals, church, weddings, the community's great days.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does Figure 3 in Grade 3 picture?
**Answer:** Clothes made today (modern-made clothes), beside Figure 2's ancient dress.
