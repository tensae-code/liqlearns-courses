# Traditional Dress — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The full white traditional dress in the photographs is the —
a) qemis (ቀሚስ)  b) school uniform  c) rain coat
**Answer: a) qemis (ቀሚስ)**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Tibeb is —
a) a kind of food  b) the woven coloured border patterns on the dress  c) a festival drum
**Answer: b) the woven coloured border patterns on the dress**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** In the appreciating chapter, the three children wear —
a) full white traditional dress with tibeb borders  b) sports clothes  c) school uniform
**Answer: a) full white traditional dress with tibeb borders**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** In old times, people made clothes from —
a) plastic and nylon  b) animal skin and hair, and cotton  c) paper
**Answer: b) animal skin and hair, and cotton**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** The book's ruling is that old and today's dress must both be —
a) thrown away  b) protected and passed to the generation  c) hidden
**Answer: b) protected and passed to the generation**
