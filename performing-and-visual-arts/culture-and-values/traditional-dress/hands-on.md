# Hands-on — Traditional Dress

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Read a real dress
With permission, lay a traditional dress or netela flat. Smooth it gently. Find: the white ground, the tibeb borders, any cross or pattern. Say who wove such cloth, and for what days it is worn.

## Design a tibeb border
On paper, rule a long band. Fill it with a repeating pattern — diamonds, crosses, zigzags — in two or three colours. That band is your tibeb design; borders like it take weavers many hours.
