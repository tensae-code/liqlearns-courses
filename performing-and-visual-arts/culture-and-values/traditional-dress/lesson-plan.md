# Lesson plan — Traditional Dress

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Culture and Values · **Lesson:** Traditional Dress
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 5 — Culture and Values (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Ethiopia's traditional dress — the white ቀሚስ (qemis) with its coloured tibeb borders, tibeb-pattern shirts, and how dress has changed from old times to today, and must still be protected.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the child's own eyes — the dress worn at festivals in their own community.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved (the gate: README, "Units, Lessons and Sub Lessons (ruled 2026-11-08)"):

1. **Traditional dress** — The photographs: tibeb-pattern shirts and the full white qemis with coloured borders and a cross.
2. **Appreciating traditional dress** — Children in full white traditional dress with tibeb borders — dress appreciated by wearing and honouring it.
3. **Dress then and now** — Old times: skin, hair and cotton. Today: modern making. Both protected and passed on.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the Culture chapter's opening lessons in this title. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name traditional dress: the white qemis with tibeb borders, and clothes printed with tibeb patterns.
2. Say when traditional dress is worn and what it is for, from the appreciating chapter.
3. Tell how people made clothes in old times, and how many are made today.
4. Say why both old and today's dress must be protected and passed on.

## What you need
Your eyes — and, if your family has one, a traditional dress or netela to look at closely.

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks traditional dress means only old dress → today's tibeb-pattern shirts are in the same photographs; tradition lives in today's clothes too.
- Cannot name the parts → point and name on a real dress or the lesson picture: qemis — tibeb — cross.
