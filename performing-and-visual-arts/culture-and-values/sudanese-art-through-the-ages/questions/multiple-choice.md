# Sudanese Art Through the Ages — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Sudan's artistic heritage includes the woven…
a) birsh mat  b) silk carpet  c) paper fan
**Answer: a) birsh mat** — the birsh mat, the quffa basket, decorated jebena coffee pots and the Jirtig set are the heritage objects pictured.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Ancient Nubian wall painting was laid on a ground of…
a) gypsum  b) glass  c) iron
**Answer: a) gypsum** — a gypsum ground, with colours prepared from lime, sand, egg white and ground stone.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In Christian Nubia, wall painting carried…
a) icons and Bible scenes  b) only patterns  c) sea battles
**Answer: a) icons and Bible scenes** — sculpture was held back by religious tradition, so wall painting carried the icons and Bible scenes.

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** The Khartoum School used which element as a plastic element?
a) the Arabic letter  b) the photograph  c) the machine
**Answer: a) the Arabic letter** — its students discovered the Arabic letter's aesthetics and used calligraphy as a plastic element.
