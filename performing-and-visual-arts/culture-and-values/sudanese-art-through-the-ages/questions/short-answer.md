# Sudanese Art Through the Ages — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Where are the ancient Nubian works now kept?
**Answer: In the Sudan National Museum, Khartoum.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name two masters in whom the Khartoum School matured.
**Answer: Ibrahim Al-Salahi and Ahmed Mohamed Shibrain.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What binds Sudan's folk heritage together, in the Grade 4 book's words?
**Answer: It binds the nation together and keeps its authentic aesthetic and creative values alive.**
