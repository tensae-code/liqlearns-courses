# Sudanese Art Through the Ages — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Kerma pottery belongs to Sudan's ancient Nubian art.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Christian-era churches in Sudan were built only of wood.
**Answer: False** — Faras church was green brick coated with white gypsum; later churches used red sandstone and granite.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The Khartoum School's panels smell of the African and Arab environment.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Sudan's heritage is described as a storehouse at the meeting of Africa's civilisations.
**Answer: True**
