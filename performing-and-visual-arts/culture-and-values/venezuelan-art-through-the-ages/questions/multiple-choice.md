# Venezuelan Art Through the Ages — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson — Venezuelan Art Through the Ages — opens with…
a) Arte cinético venezolano  b) an examination  c) nothing at all
**Answer: a) Arte cinético venezolano.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The Venezuela book teaches “Arte cinético venezolano” as…
a) a part of venezuelan art through the ages  b) a sports rule  c) a cooking recipe
**Answer: a) a part of venezuelan art through the ages.**

**3.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) Juan Félix Sánchez  b) long division  c) map coordinates
**Answer: a) Juan Félix Sánchez.**

**4.** The parts of this lesson come from…
a) Venezuela's own Educación Artística books  b) a newspaper  c) a song chorus
**Answer: a) Venezuela's own Educación Artística books.**

**5.** “Arte colonial venezolano: panorama” is taught in this lesson as…
