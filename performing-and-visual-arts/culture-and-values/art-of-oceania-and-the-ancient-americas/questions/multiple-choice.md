# Art of Oceania and the Ancient Americas — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson teaches mainly about…
a) the weather only  b) art of oceania and the ancient americas  c) none of these
**Answer: b) art of oceania and the ancient americas.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The first part of the lesson is…
a) the last part of another lesson  b) Изкуството през различни периоди и на различни места (Art in Different Periods and in Different Places) — part one  c) none of these
**Answer: b) Изкуството през различни периоди и на различни места (Art in Different Periods and in Different Places) — part one.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Bulgaria's books teach this lesson as…
a) a secret kept from pupils  b) a named part of the school art course  c) none of these
**Answer: b) a named part of the school art course.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Another part of the lesson is…
a) a part of a different course  b) Изкуството през различни периоди и на различни места (Art in Different Periods and in Different Places) — part two  c) none of these
**Answer: b) Изкуството през различни периоди и на различни места (Art in Different Periods and in Different Places) — part two.**

**5. (Sub lesson 1)** Every part of this lesson comes from…
