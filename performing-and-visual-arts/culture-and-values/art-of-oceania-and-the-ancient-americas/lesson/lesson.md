## Sub lesson 1 — Изкуството през различни периоди и на различни места (Art in Different Periods and in Different Places) — part one

in different places on Earth art developed differently, reflecting the local way of life, beliefs and customs: the art of Oceania, created by the inhabitants of the many islands of the Pacific Ocean and bound to their gods and the rituals connected with them — an abundance of masks, statues and statuettes (a mask from New Guinea with a strongly elongated stylised image, the giant stone Moai statues of Easter Island, religious statues in the Musée du Quai Branly), the islanders' beliefs reflected in the objects they create, their other great theme the ocean and all its inhabitants, and their conviction that whoever likes their art and takes home their small, lovingly made objects is a happy person;

## Sub lesson 2 — Изкуството през различни периоди и на различни места (Art in Different Periods and in Different Places) — part two

and the art of the Maya and the Aztecs, who lived and created in America — the mighty Inca empire and the Aztecs creating exquisite works, some for everyday household use, others for the rituals in whose power they deeply believed, others representing instruments for surgical interventions on which the god of health was obligatorily depicted (the Aztec calendar stone, a painting inspired by Aztec art, the Inca ceramic "Wari" bottle, a Maya ceramic vessel, an Inca medical instrument with the image of the god Viracocha), their gods' images decorating dwellings and public buildings — with research tasks on other peoples' specific arts (Toltecs and Olmecs, Vikings and others)
