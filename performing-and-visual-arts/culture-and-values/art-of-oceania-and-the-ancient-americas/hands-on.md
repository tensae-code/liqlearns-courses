# Hands-on — Art of Oceania and the Ancient Americas

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the lesson's thing
Work through the lesson's first part yourself: Изкуството през различни периоди и на различни места (Art in Different Periods and in Different Places) — part one. Show a companion what you made or did, and say what the book calls it.

## Show and tell
Each learner shows their work and names the part of the lesson it comes from. The companion checks the name against the lesson.
