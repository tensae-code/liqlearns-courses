## Sub lesson 1 — The aims (teacher-guide evidence)

The aims (teacher-guide evidence) — among them: the uniqueness of world culture, and the development of medieval and Renaissance heritage.

## Sub lesson 2 — The ornament belt (զարդագոտի) of Etchmiadzin's Mother Cathedral; Armenian blue

The ornament belt (զարդագոտի) of Etchmiadzin's Mother Cathedral; Armenian blue ceramics (հայկական կապույտ խեցի); St Andreas church in Jerusalem — Armenia's decorated things and places.

## Sub lesson 3 — Reading the monument

Reading the monument — the SUNDIAL (արևային ժամացույց): how its shadow tells the hour; and building a model of Zvartnots with your friends from cardboard, glue and paints, following the pictured stages and proportions.

## Sub lesson 4 — The Dance Art park and the costume map

The Dance Art park and the costume map — Yerevan's Dance Art grove by Charents's monument; Arakel Patrik's 1958 map of Armenian folk costume (19th-20th c.), kept in the Museum of the History of Armenian Dance Art; Sargis Muradyan's "Wedding in Hrazdan" (1960).

## Sub lesson 5 — The aims (teacher-guide evidence)

The aims (teacher-guide evidence) — help the learner know the periods and styles, the values of national and world cultural heritage, and their own national culture within world culture.

## Sub lesson 6 — A special place for national art (teacher-guide evidence)

A special place for national art (teacher-guide evidence) — the aims expressly reserve a special place for national (Armenian) art within the course.

## Sub lesson 7 — The textbook's aims (teacher-guide evidence)

The textbook's aims (teacher-guide evidence) — recognise the creative periods and styles, the values of national and world heritage; know one's national culture within the world cultural heritage.
