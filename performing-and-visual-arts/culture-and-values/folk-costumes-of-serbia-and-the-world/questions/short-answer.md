# Folk Costumes of Serbia and the World — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which three Serbian uniforms does the book picture?
**Answer: A soldier of the Serbian Cossack regiment of 1813, a Serbian soldier and corporal of 1830, and an artillery lieutenant of 1942.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is folk costume called in Serbian?
**Answer: Народна ношња (narodna nošnja).**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Name three costumes of the world's peoples pictured in the book.
**Answer: Any three of: Arctic dress, an Indian sari, Chinese dress, African dress, and a Serbian couple in folk costume.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the book ask you to draw?
**Answer: A girl and a boy in the costume of your own region, found out first — then coloured.**
