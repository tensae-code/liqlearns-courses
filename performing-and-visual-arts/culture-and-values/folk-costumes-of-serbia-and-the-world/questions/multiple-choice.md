# Folk Costumes of Serbia and the World — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** From a uniform we can conclude…
a) what a person's occupation is  b) what a person's name is  c) where a person lives
**Answer: a) what a person's occupation is**.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The book's pictured uniforms include…
a) a Serbian Cossack-regiment soldier of 1813 and an artillery lieutenant of 1942  b) only police uniforms  c) only school uniforms
**Answer: a) a Serbian Cossack-regiment soldier of 1813 and an artillery lieutenant of 1942**.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Folk costume in the past was…
a) everyday dress  b) worn only by soldiers  c) never worn
**Answer: a) everyday dress**.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Today, folk costume is worn…
a) on particular occasions  b) every day by everyone  c) only in museums, never worn
**Answer: a) on particular occasions**.
