# Folk Costumes of Serbia and the World — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A uniform tells nothing about its wearer.
**Answer: False** — from a uniform we can conclude the person's occupation.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Folk costume was once everyday dress.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The Indian sari is one of the world's costumes pictured.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** An artillery lieutenant of 1942 is one of the pictured uniforms.
**Answer: True**
