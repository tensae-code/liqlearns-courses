# Hands-on — Folk Costumes of Serbia and the World

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Draw your region's costume
Find out what the costume of your own region looks like — ask, look at pictures. Draw a girl and a boy wearing it, and colour them carefully.

## Read the uniform
Draw a uniform from the book's three — 1813, 1830 or 1942. Under it, write what the uniform tells a viewer about its wearer.
