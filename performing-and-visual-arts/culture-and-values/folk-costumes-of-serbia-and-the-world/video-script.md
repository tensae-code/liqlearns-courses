# Video script — Folk Costumes of Serbia and the World

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Clothes can speak before a person says a word.

A uniform tells the job: soldier, corporal, lieutenant.

Folk costume once dressed every day — today it dresses the special days.

Arctic furs, an Indian sari, a Serbian couple in nošnja.

Find your own region's costume. Draw it. Colour it.
