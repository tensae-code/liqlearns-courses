# Folk Costumes of Serbia and the World

## Do it first

Quick start: say in one sentence what you think this lesson is about — dress tells who a person is. Then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — Uniforms — dress that tells a job

**From a uniform we can conclude what a person's occupation is.** The book pictures a **soldier of the Serbian Cossack regiment of 1813**, a **Serbian soldier and corporal of 1830**, and an **artillery lieutenant of 1942** — three uniforms, three readings.

## Sub lesson 2 — Folk costumes of Serbia and the world

**Folk costume (народна ношња) was worn in the past as everyday dress, and today it is worn on particular occasions.** The costumes of the world's peoples are pictured — **Arctic dress, an Indian sari, Chinese dress, African dress, and a Serbian couple in folk costume**. The book's task: **try to find out what the costume of your own region looks like, then draw a girl and a boy in it, and colour them.**
