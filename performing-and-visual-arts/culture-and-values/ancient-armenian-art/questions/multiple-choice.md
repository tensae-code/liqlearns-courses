Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson teaches...
a) another country's arts only  b) Ancient Armenian Art — Armenia's own teaching of this topic, kept as named parts: EREBUNI (Էրեբունի) — at the end of the 19th century a cuneiform (սեպագիր) inscription stone was found on Arin Berd hill: Argishti's inscription, one of 23 cuneiform inscriptions found in Armenia; the fortress-city Erebuni-Yerevan and its painted walls (lions and the tree of life)  c) nothing — it is just names
**Answer: b)** — that is the lesson's one thing.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The first sub lesson is...
a) EREBUNI (Էրեբունի)  b) an examination  c) a song from another continent
**Answer: a) EREBUNI (Էրեբունի).**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Armenia's own forms in this lesson are...
