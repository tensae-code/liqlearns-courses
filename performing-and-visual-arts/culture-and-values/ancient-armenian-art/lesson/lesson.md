# Ancient Armenian Art

## Do it first

Name one thing that belongs in this lesson — Ancient Armenian Art — Armenia's own teaching of this topic, kept as named parts: EREBUNI (Էրեբունի) — at the end of the 19th century a cuneiform (սեպագիր) inscription stone was found on Arin Berd hill: Argishti's inscription, one of 23 cuneiform inscriptions found in Armenia; the fortress-city Erebuni-Yerevan and its painted walls (lions and the tree of life). Say what it is in your own words. If you can name one, you are already inside the lesson.

## Sub lesson 1 — EREBUNI (Էրեբունի)

EREBUNI (Էրեբունի) — at the end of the 19th century a cuneiform (սեպագիր) inscription stone was found on Arin Berd hill: Argishti's inscription, one of 23 cuneiform inscriptions found in Armenia; the fortress-city Erebuni-Yerevan and its painted walls (lions and the tree of life).

## Sub lesson 2 — Art of the Van Kingdom (Վանի թագավորության արվեստը)

Art of the Van Kingdom (Վանի թագավորության արվեստը) — the art of Urartu, the ancient kingdom of the Armenian highland: metalwork, fortresses, painting.

## Sub lesson 3 — Hellenism in Armenia (Հելլենիզմը Հայաստանում)

Hellenism in Armenia (Հելլենիզմը Հայաստանում) — how the Hellenistic world met Armenian art.

## Do It Yourself

1. Choose one named part of this lesson and find out one more true thing about it — from a person who knows it, or from the book your part came from.
