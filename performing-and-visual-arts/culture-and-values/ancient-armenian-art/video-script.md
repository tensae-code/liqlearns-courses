This is Armenia's own lesson.

**EREBUNI (Էրեբունի).** EREBUNI (Էրեբունի) — at the end of the 19th century a cuneiform (սեպագիր) inscription stone was found on Arin Berd hill: Argishti's inscription, one of 23 cuneiform inscriptions found in Armenia; the fortress-city Erebuni-Yerevan and its painted walls (lions and the tree of life).

**Art of the Van Kingdom (Վանի թագավորության արվեստը).** Art of the Van Kingdom (Վանի թագավորության արվեստը) — the art of Urartu, the ancient kingdom of the Armenian highland: metalwork, fortresses, painting.

**Hellenism in Armenia (Հելլենիզմը Հայաստանում).** Hellenism in Armenia (Հելլենիզմը Հայաստանում) — how the Hellenistic world met Armenian art.

That is Ancient Armenian Art — Armenia's way, kept by name.
