# Hands-on — Casas de Cultura: Cuba's Culture Houses

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Work the lesson's own way
Work through the sub lessons in order — What a casa de cultura is; An open home for the community's art; The cultural system around it — doing each part the way Cuba's books direct: by making, moving, singing or looking closely, as the part asks. Show your companion what you made or did, and name the part it belongs to.
