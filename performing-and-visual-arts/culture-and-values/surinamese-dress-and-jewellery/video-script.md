# Video script — Surinamese Dress and Jewellery

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Every people dresses its own way.

Koto and angisa. Sari and pagri. Djarik and sarong. Qipao. Pangi.

Read the design — and you know the people.

And the jewellery speaks too: the slavenboei, worn for beauty and for faith.

Suriname's dress and jewellery — identity you can wear.
