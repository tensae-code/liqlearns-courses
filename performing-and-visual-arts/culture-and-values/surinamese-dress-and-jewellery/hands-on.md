# Hands-on — Surinamese Dress and Jewellery

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Read a garment
Show a pictured garment from this lesson. Name its people from its design — its form, colours and motifs — and say what told you.

## Name the piece, say its meaning
Pick one named jewellery piece. Say its name, who wears it, and what it means or does for the wearer.
