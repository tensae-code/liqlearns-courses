# Surinamese Dress and Jewellery — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Creole dress includes…
a) the qipao  b) koto, kotobere, yaki and angisa  c) djarik and sarong
**Answer: b) koto, kotobere, yaki and angisa**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The slavenboei's name comes from…
a) a kind of boat  b) the shackles put on enslaved people's arms  c) a famous jeweller
**Answer: b) the shackles put on enslaved people's arms**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The series' research question asks how you recognise, from a garment's design…
a) its price  b) which people it comes from  c) its age
**Answer: b) which people it comes from**
