# Surinamese Dress and Jewellery — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the Marron dress pieces.
**Answer: Pangi and kamisa.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why is the Hindustani god pendant worn?
**Answer: So the worshipped god protects the wearer.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Name two named jewellery pieces besides the slavenboei.
**Answer: Any two of: ogri ai krara, boto keti, kromanti ketting, mattenklopper, bamboeketting, koningsschakelketting, pie-piet ketting.**
