# Surinamese Dress and Jewellery — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The qipao is Chinese dress.
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The slavenboei was first hollow and later flat.
**Answer: False — first flat and later hollow.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The Muslim Allah pendant is worn against evil eyes and for protection.
**Answer: True.**
