# Brazilian Art and Folk Culture — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In your own words, what does this part teach?
**Answer: What culture and popular culture are; folk culture made by the people and handed down orally (artesanato, festas, cantigas de roda, Festas Juninas) against erudite culture**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In your own words, what does this part teach?
**Answer: Brazilian folk art: Mestre Vitalino's clay figures of Northeastern life, and the carrancas, great carved heads on São Francisco river boats; carving a miniature carranca in soap or chalk**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In your own words, what does this part teach?
**Answer: The French Artistic Mission (1816) and Neoclassicism: Pedro Américo's Independência ou Morte in an imported style; Debret's watercolours recording the daily life of Black, Indigenous and white Brazilians**
