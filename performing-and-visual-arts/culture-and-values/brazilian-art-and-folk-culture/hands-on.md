# Hands-on — Brazilian Art and Folk Culture

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## What culture and popular culture are
Do the practice this part names, with real materials and a partner: what culture and popular culture are; folk culture made by the people and handed down orally (artesanato, festas, cantigas de roda, Festas Juninas) against erudite culture

## Brazilian folk art
Do the practice this part names, with real materials and a partner: Brazilian folk art: Mestre Vitalino's clay figures of Northeastern life, and the carrancas, great carved heads on São Francisco river boats; carving a miniature carranca in soap or chalk

## The French Artistic Mission (1816) and Neoclassicism
Do the practice this part names, with real materials and a partner: the French Artistic Mission (1816) and Neoclassicism: Pedro Américo's Independência ou Morte in an imported style; Debret's watercolours recording the daily life of Black, Indigenous and white Brazilians

## Romantic indianism
Do the practice this part names, with real materials and a partner: Romantic indianism: Medeiros' Iracema, the Indigenous heroine as the image of the new nation

## Paraná's art
Do the practice this part names, with real materials and a partner: Paraná's art: Alfredo Andersen, "father of Paraná painting"; the Paranismo movement and its araucaria symbols; Lange de Morretes' geometric pinha pavement in Curitiba; stylising your own city's symbol into a repeating pattern
