# Video script — Brazilian Art and Folk Culture

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

**What culture and popular culture are.** What culture and popular culture are; folk culture made by the people and handed down orally (artesanato, festas, cantigas de roda, Festas Juninas) against erudite culture

**Brazilian folk art.** Brazilian folk art: Mestre Vitalino's clay figures of Northeastern life, and the carrancas, great carved heads on São Francisco river boats; carving a miniature carranca in soap or chalk

**The French Artistic Mission (1816) and Neoclassicism.** The French Artistic Mission (1816) and Neoclassicism: Pedro Américo's Independência ou Morte in an imported style; Debret's watercolours recording the daily life of Black, Indigenous and white Brazilians

**Romantic indianism.** Romantic indianism: Medeiros' Iracema, the Indigenous heroine as the image of the new nation

**Paraná's art.** Paraná's art: Alfredo Andersen, "father of Paraná painting"; the Paranismo movement and its araucaria symbols; Lange de Morretes' geometric pinha pavement in Curitiba; stylising your own city's symbol into a repeating pattern
