# Vietnamese Festival Arts

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about vietnamese festival arts. Then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — Vietnam Grade 1 Mĩ thuật 1

The group art activity of arranging a mâm quả (ceremonial fruit tray) in two pictured styles — bananas, pomelo, dragon fruit, papaya, kumquat and chillies built into a display — then drawing or tear-pasting the arranged tray

## Sub lesson 2 — Vietnam Grade 2 Mĩ thuật 2

Vietnam's traditional folk toys — the tò he figurine (a horse kneaded from coloured dough/clay), the cardboard lion head danced at festivals, and painted cardboard masks — when they are played with and what animals they show

## Sub lesson 3 — Vietnam Grade 6 Mĩ thuật 6

The five-colour flag (cờ ngũ sắc) used in festivals — its five colours correspond, in Eastern cultural thought, to the five basic elements: green to wood (mộc), red to fire (hỏa), yellow to earth (thổ), white to metal (kim) and blue to water (thủy) — and designing a wall calendar decorated with festival colours
