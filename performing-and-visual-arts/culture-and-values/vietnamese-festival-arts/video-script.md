# Video script — Vietnamese Festival Arts

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is Vietnamese Festival Arts.

Vietnamese Festival Arts — its forms, history and cultural meaning, taught as one topic lesson. — as Vietnam's school books teach it: The group art activity of arranging a mâm quả (ceremonial fruit tray) in two pictured styles — bananas, pomelo, dragon fruit, papaya, kumquat and chillies built into a display — then drawing or tear-pasting the arranged tray.

Watch, listen — then say it back in your own words.
