# Hands-on — Easter in Song and Custom

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Motif sampler
On paper eggs, draw one motif from each family — geometric, cosmic (star, Sun, Moon), floral (carnation, fir sprig, wheat ear), Christian (cross, fish) — and set them symmetrically, the încondeiere way.

## The egg doll
Blow an egg, draw its face, fix it on a cardboard roll, give it thread hair and a fabric dress. Or make the page's project: a little bee from an egg.
