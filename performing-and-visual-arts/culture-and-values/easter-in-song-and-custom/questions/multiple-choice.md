# Easter in Song and Custom — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Painted Easter eggs are a symbol of…
a) life and nature's renewal  b) winter  c) harvest tools
**Answer: a) life and nature's renewal**.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The red of Christian Easter eggs stands for…
a) the fire's purifying power and Christ's blood  b) ripe tomatoes  c) autumn leaves
**Answer: a) the fire's purifying power and Christ's blood**.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Încondeiere is the wax-pattern egg art of…
a) Bucovina and Maramureș  b) the seaside  c) Bucharest only
**Answer: a) Bucovina and Maramureș** — the folk masters' art.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Green dye comes from…
a) boiled nettle juice or spinach with vinegar  b) green paint  c) grass clippings alone
**Answer: a) boiled nettle juice or spinach with vinegar**.

**5. (Sub lesson 4)** To blow an egg, you pierce it…
a) at both ends and blow out the contents  b) in the middle  c) not at all
**Answer: a) at both ends and blow out the contents**.
