# Easter in Song and Custom — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the four motif families of încondeiere.
**Answer: Geometric, cosmic, floral and Christian motifs.**

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Which plants give the red dye?
**Answer: Raw beetroot juice or boiled red-onion leaves.**

**3. (Sub lesson 4)** What does the egg doll stand on?
**Answer: A cardboard roll, fixed with glue.**
