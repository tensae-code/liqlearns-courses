# Lesson plan — Easter in Song and Custom

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Culture and Values · **Lesson:** Easter in Song and Custom
**Place in the curriculum:** Culture and Values (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Romania's Easter egg customs — painted eggs as a symbol of life and renewal, the red egg's meaning, încondeiere (the wax-pattern egg art of Bucovina and Maramureș) with its motifs and plant dyes, and the blown-egg doll.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this course in the same title.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. ## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **We celebrate Easter: painting eggs** — the custom of painting eggs — symbol of life and nature's renewal — and the red of Christian Easter eggs, for the fire's purifying power and Christ's blood.
2. **Încondeiere: the wax-pattern eggs** — the wax-pattern egg art of the folk masters of Bucovina and Maramureș — geometric motifs, cosmic symbols (stars, Sun, Moon), floral ones (carnation, fir sprig, wheat ear) and Christian ones (cross, fish), repeated and set symmetrically so the egg suggests perfection and harmony.
3. **Dyes from plants** — how eggs are încondeiate and dyed from plants — beeswax and vegetable colours: red from raw beetroot juice or boiled red-onion leaves, yellow from orange, lemon, onion peel and carrot, green from boiled nettle juice or spinach with vinegar.
4. **Cheerful Easter eggs: the egg doll** — turn a blown egg into a doll — pierce it at both ends and blow out the contents, draw eyes, nose and mouth, fix it on a cardboard roll, thread strands for hair, a piece of fabric for dress; project: a little bee made from an egg.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
