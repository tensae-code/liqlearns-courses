# Video script — Easter in Song and Custom

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

An egg — new life, nature waking again.

Red for the fire that purifies. Red for Christ's blood.

In Bucovina and Maramureș, wax writes the pattern — stars, suns, wheat ears, crosses.

And the colours come from plants — beetroot, onion, nettle.

That is **încondeiere** — Romania's Easter egg art.
