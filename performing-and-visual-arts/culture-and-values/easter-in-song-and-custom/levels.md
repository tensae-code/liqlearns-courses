# Easter in Song and Custom — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — We celebrate Easter: painting eggs
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: say what painted eggs symbolise — life and nature's renewal — and what the red of Christian Easter eggs stands for: the fire's purifying power and Christ's blood.

## Sub lesson 2 — Încondeiere: the wax-pattern eggs
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: name where încondeiere lives — Bucovina and Maramureș — and its four motif families: geometric, cosmic, floral and Christian, set symmetrically for perfection and harmony.

## Sub lesson 3 — Dyes from plants
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: give the plant dyes in order — red from beetroot juice or red-onion leaves, yellow from orange, lemon, onion peel and carrot, green from nettle juice or spinach with vinegar — with beeswax for the pattern.

## Sub lesson 4 — Cheerful Easter eggs: the egg doll
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: give the egg doll's steps in order — pierce both ends and blow out the contents, draw the face, fix the egg on a cardboard roll, thread hair, fabric dress.
