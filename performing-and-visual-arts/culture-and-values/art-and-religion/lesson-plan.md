# Lesson plan — Art and Religion

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Culture and Values · **Lesson:** Art and Religion
**Place in the curriculum:** Culture and Values (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** religion (from "religio" — a bond) as a form of understanding the world, uniting moral norms, rites and cult actions around the temple, its church utensils, vestments, hymns and sacred texts: the Alexander Nevsky Memoria
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the learner's earlier work in this title — recommended first, never forced.
