# Dance in Society — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The guide (§4.1.3) gives dance —
a) a place in the community's life and occasions  b) no social place  c) only a stage place
**Answer: a) a place in the community's life and occasions** — the section is dance's place in the community.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In the guide's framing, dance appears where the community —
a) gathers, celebrates, mourns and remembers  b) is absent  c) sleeps
**Answer: a) gathers, celebrates, mourns and remembers** — the community's occasions carry its dance.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A customary folk dance (ልማዳዊ የሃገረሰብ ውዝዋዜ) is —
a) the dance a people keeps by custom, done their own way at their own occasions  b) a dance invented for examinations  c) a solo stage act
**Answer: a) the dance a people keeps by custom, done their own way at their own occasions** — custom is the word the guide loads.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The customary folk dance is guarded by —
a) custom, not by any school  b) a ministry syllabus only  c) nobody
**Answer: a) custom, not by any school** — the people keep it through custom.
