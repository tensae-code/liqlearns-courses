| Kazakhstan — 1.1. Мәдени бірегейлік (Cultural identity) — what it teaches: any person is a representative of a definite society and is distinguished by the signs of the common culture formed in that social environment; cultural identity (мәдени бірегейлік) is the set of an ethnos's age-old customs and traditions unlike others'; every ethnos assimilates it from the environment it lives in, and gradually it becomes that society's common cultural identity; the psychologist's explanation of the word «бірегейлік» (identity) — «Your I-concept or I-image. Who you are, where you are...» (Erik Erikson's identity research is cited); identity's components in the scheme — language (тіл), national dishes (ұлттық тағамдар), way of life (өмір салты), social rules (қоғамдық қағидалар), national musical instruments (ұлттық музыкалық аспаптар), national traditions and customs (ұлттық дәстүр және салт), national music, culture, art (ұлттық музыка, мәдениет, өнер); preserving cultural identity is one of today's topical problems, because the globalisation process can gradually destroy national cultures' peculiarities; Kazakhstan pays constant attention to the interethnic accord, peace and unity question — the national idea «ұлттық бірегейлік» (national identity) is closely tied to the «ұлттық интеграция» (national integration) concept; national cultural identity also shows clearly in sport: the pictured Kazakhstani sportsmen — Gennady Golovkin (boxing), Alexander Vinokurov (cycling), Elmira Ospanova (?) (judo), Denis Ten (figure skating); one bright example of national cultural identity is the national game — қазақша күрес (Kazakh wrestling), which in 2016 UNESCO entered into humanity's Intangible Cultural Heritage list, and the «Әлем барысы» tournament is held yearly with the world's strongest sportsmen taking part; likewise Togyzkumalak — world championships have been held since 2010; the pictured paintings by artists of different countries (Oleg Kompanez, Russia; Suyutbek Torobekov, Kyrgyzstan; Dolonbaev, China; Timoteo Akioshi Montoya, USA; Dzhakhon Umarbekov, Uzbekistan; Ronald Kayer, Netherlands) show how each artist expresses national elements (student book; original target `culture-and-values/cultural-identity`) | Book 13, printed p. 6 |
| Kazakhstan — 1.2. Мәдени бірегейлік (Cultural identity, continued) — what it teaches: the matryoshka (Матрешка) is the Russian people's age-old traditional doll; the doll-making idea was actively propagandised by the «Russian style» movement, and the professional artist Sergei Malyutin thought it up; the matryoshka is a woman-mother, a symbol of continuing the generation; as a result of cultures' mutual influence such dolls appeared in other countries too; Bolat Mekebayev is a Kazakh artist living in Germany — his twenty years of life abroad did not separate him from his native Homeland's history: «From any of his works it is known at once that he is a Kazakh artist.
