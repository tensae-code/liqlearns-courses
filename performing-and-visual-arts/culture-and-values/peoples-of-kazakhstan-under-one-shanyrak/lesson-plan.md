# Lesson plan — Peoples of Kazakhstan Under One Shanyrak

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Culture and Values · **Lesson:** Peoples of Kazakhstan Under One Shanyrak
**Place in the curriculum:** Culture and Values (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** peoples of Kazakhstan Under One Shanyrak — its forms, history and cultural meaning, taught as one topic lesson..
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this course in the same title.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Kazakhstan Grade 2 Labour Guide KZ** — "The dastarkhan": the laid table of welcome made as a work
2. **Kazakhstan Grade 2 Labour Guide RU** — "The dastarkhan"
3. **Kazakhstan Grade 3 Tolebiev Visual Art** — the "Friendship tree" collective composition for Unity Day (hands, doves, flowers assembled as one work) and "My yard": a courtyard fountain modelled
4. **Kazakhstan Grade 3 Zhanaev Visual Art** — family monuments (monumental sculpture) and a round sculpture made; a flower panel (panno) for the vase; design in Paint and a paper sandyk (chest) built; Khan Shatyr: interior, exterior, façade
5. **Kazakhstan Grade 3 Adamkulov Labour** — a holiday dove by origami; modern leisure places and an item made from waste plastic; spiritual leisure: museums, and a kumyra jug built; family holidays: a flower made
6. **Kazakhstan Grade 3 Syzdykov Labour** — hanging compositions and mobiles ("Birds"); a festive butterfly mobile; gift wrapping (the korzhyn bag; a folding box built from its net); a festive card with a volume carnation
7. **Kazakhstan Grade 3 Rabilova Visual Art** — Unity Day: a multi-figure composition modelled (the peoples of Kazakhstan in national dress); design ideas: a collage "Tourist routes of Kazakhstan"; architecture and design: a recreation park (aquapark) modelled
8. **Kazakhstan Grade 3 Raupova Art Labour** — a model amusement park built collectively; rest and travel
9. **Kazakhstan Grade 3 Valiullina Music RU** — the Nauryz holiday in song; the country's holidays in music
10. **Kazakhstan Grade 3 Kulmanova Music KZ** — festive joy; let's step merrily; festive May; the children's holiday
11. **Kazakhstan Grade 4 Tulebiev Visual Art RU** — the plant symbols of Japan and China (sakura, lotus, bamboo) in painting; an author's work "An exotic plant"
12. **Kazakhstan Grade 5 Music (Kulmanova)** — язык дружбы», Урок 27 — Kazakhstan, one people of many nations living under one shanyrak; the game-journey "Silk Road — Kazakhstan"; the Russian dance-song «Во поле береза стояла» and the Beryozka ensemble's floating step; the plyasovaya song — singer and dancer in one; Russian instruments balalaika, gusli, dudka, garmon and spoons; the Assembly of the People of Kazakhstan (founded 1 March 1995) that unites every ethnos around one goal; groups prepare presentations on the peoples living in Kazakhstan, with the "Киностудия" and "Movie Maker" programmes
13. **Kazakhstan Grade 9 NIS Art (Akhmetkalieva)** — what it teaches: any person is a representative of a definite society and is distinguished by the signs of the common culture formed in that social environment; cultural identity (мәдени бірегейлік) is the set of an ethnos's age-old customs and traditions unlike others'; every ethnos assimilates it from the environment it lives in, and gradually it becomes that society's common cultural identity; the psychologist's explanation of the word «бірегейлік» (identity) — «Your I-concept or I-image. Who you are, where you are...» (Erik Erikson's identity research is cited); identity's components in the scheme — language (тіл), national dishes (ұлттық тағамдар), way of life (өмір салты), social rules (қоғамдық қағидалар), national musical instruments (ұлттық музыкалық аспаптар), national traditions and customs (ұлттық дәстүр және салт), national music, culture, art (ұлттық музыка, мәдениет, өнер); preserving cultural identity is one of today's topical problems, because the globalisation process can gradually destroy national cultures' peculiarities; Kazakhstan pays constant attention to the interethnic accord, peace and unity question — the national idea «ұлттық бірегейлік» (national identity) is closely tied to the «ұлттық интеграция» (national integration) concept; national cultural identity also shows clearly in sport: the pictured Kazakhstani sportsmen — Gennady Golovkin (boxing), Alexander Vinokurov (cycling), Elmira Ospanova (?) (judo), Denis Ten (figure skating); one bright example of national cultural identity is the national game — қазақша күрес (Kazakh wrestling), which in 2016 UNESCO entered into humanity's Intangible Cultural Heritage list, and the «Әлем барысы» tournament is held yearly with the world's strongest sportsmen taking part; likewise Togyzkumalak — world championships have been held since 2010; the pictured paintings by artists of different countries (Oleg Kompanez, Russia; Suyutbek Torobekov, Kyrgyzstan; Dolonbaev, China; Timoteo Akioshi Montoya, USA; Dzhakhon Umarbekov, Uzbekistan; Ronald Kayer, Netherlands) show how each artist expresses national elements; what it teaches: the matryoshka (Матрешка) is the Russian people's age-old traditional doll; the doll-making idea was actively propagandised by the «Russian style» movement, and the professional artist Sergei Malyutin thought it up; the matryoshka is a woman-mother, a symbol of continuing the generation; as a result of cultures' mutual influence such dolls appeared in other countries too; Bolat Mekebayev is a Kazakh artist living in Germany — his twenty years of life abroad did not separate him from his native Homeland's history: «From any of his works it is known at once that he is a Kazakh artist. In any work he turns to the national theme... 'Wherever I am, I must know my people's history. In Germany they count me a Kazakh artist, and in the press they write about me as Kazakhstan's representative,' he says»; his paintings «Скифтер алтыны» (Scythian Gold) and «Шығыс базары» (Eastern Bazaar) are pictured; the Kasteev State Museum of Arts director G.K. Shalabaeva: «Keeping one's own peculiarities, being open to world history and culture, understanding other cultures and establishing mutual connection with them, and having the ability to put one's culture in a new bright channel — contemporary national spiritual culture must develop openly, in an organised and fast system»; the famous dombra player Andrei Fendrikov answers why he chose the dombra
