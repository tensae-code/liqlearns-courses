# Hands-on — Symbols of Guatemalas Peoples

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Reading a symbol
Draw the snail, the sun and the heart-planet from the lesson's description. Under each, write its meaning in your own words: zero, food and ceremony instrument; nawal Ajpú; nawal Noj, wisdom.

## A symbol of your own place
Choose one thing from your own community that carries a meaning beyond itself. Draw it and tell a partner what it means — the way the guide tells what its symbols mean.
