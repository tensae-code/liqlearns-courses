# Symbols of Guatemalas Peoples — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** For the Maya, the snail stands for…
a) zero  b) a king  c) a river
**Answer: a) zero — the snail is the Maya sign for zero.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** For the Garífuna, the snail is…
a) a food  b) a tool for building  c) a dance
**Answer: a) a food.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The conch — the snail's great shell — is used as…
a) a musical instrument of important ceremonies  b) a cooking pot only  c) a hat
**Answer: a) a musical instrument of important ceremonies.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The sun is read as the Maya nawal…
a) Ajpú  b) Noj  c) Keme
**Answer: a) Ajpú.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The heart-planet carries the nawal Noj, which means…
a) wisdom  b) rain  c) war
**Answer: a) wisdom.**
