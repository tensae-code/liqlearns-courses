# Symbols of Guatemalas Peoples — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the snail's three meanings in the guide.
**Answer: Maya zero; a Garífuna food; and the conch as a musical instrument of important ceremonies.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which nawal does the sun stand for?
**Answer: The nawal Ajpú.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the heart-planet's nawal Noj mean?
**Answer: Wisdom.**
