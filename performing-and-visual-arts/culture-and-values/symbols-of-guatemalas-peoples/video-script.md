# Video script — Symbols of Guatemalas Peoples

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A picture can carry a people's knowledge.

The snail: for the Maya, zero. For the Garífuna, food. And its shell, the conch, sounds in important ceremonies.

The sun is the nawal Ajpú. The heart-planet carries Noj — wisdom.

Guatemala's guide teaches its own symbols first — because art begins by reading what a culture put into its signs.
