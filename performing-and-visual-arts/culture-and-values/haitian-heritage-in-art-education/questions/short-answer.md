# Haitian Heritage in Art Education — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name EEA's three volets.
**Answer: Arts plastiques et visuels; arts de la scène (mainly music); knowledge of Haitian culture and heritage.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name two places a heritage visit can go.
**Answer: Historic sites, workshops, laboratories, building sites, art galleries and museums — any two.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the 9e year's cultural theme?
**Answer: « Entrepreneuriat culturel et métiers des arts » — cultural entrepreneurship and the arts professions.**
