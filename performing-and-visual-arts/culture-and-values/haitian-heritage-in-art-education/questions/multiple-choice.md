# Haitian Heritage in Art Education — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How many volets does EEA have?
a) three  b) two  c) five
**Answer: a) three** — three — visual arts, stage arts, and Haitian culture and heritage.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Competency 3 is about…
a) knowing and upholding the national heritage  b) only examinations  c) foreign heritage only
**Answer: a) knowing and upholding the national heritage** — knowing and upholding the patrimoine national, while appreciating universal heritage.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The 8e year's cultural theme is…
a) « Expressions culturelles et valorisation du patrimoine »  b) « Entrepreneuriat »  c) « Éveil » only
**Answer: a) « Expressions culturelles et valorisation du patrimoine »** — Expressions culturelles et valorisation du patrimoine.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Reappropriating the heritage means…
a) creating from elements of the heritage  b) forgetting the heritage  c) selling the heritage
**Answer: a) creating from elements of the heritage** — creating from elements of heritage, art, craft and society.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Heritage learning happens by visits to…
a) historic sites, workshops, galleries and museums  b) only cinemas  c) only shops
**Answer: a) historic sites, workshops, galleries and museums** — historic sites, workshops, laboratories, building sites, art galleries and museums.
