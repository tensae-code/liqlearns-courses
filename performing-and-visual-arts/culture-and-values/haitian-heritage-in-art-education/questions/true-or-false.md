# Haitian Heritage in Art Education — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** EEA includes knowledge of Haitian culture and heritage as a volet.
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Upholding national heritage excludes appreciating universal heritage.
**Answer: False — Competency 3 holds both together.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Heritage is learned only from books.
**Answer: False — visits to sites, workshops, galleries and museums are part of it.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Projects on the arts and heritage professions belong to this unit.
**Answer: True.**
