# Lesson plan — Haitian Heritage in Art Education

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Culture and Values · **Lesson:** Haitian Heritage in Art Education
**Place in the curriculum:** Culture and Values (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Haiti's EEA subject is built on its heritage — three volets, a competency that upholds the national heritage (patrimoine national), and learning that creates from the heritage and visits the places that keep it.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this course in the same title.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks heritage is only old buildings → the programme's heritage is art, craft and society together — learned by creating from it and by visiting sites, workshops, galleries and museums.
- Thinks EEA is only drawing → EEA has three volets: visual arts, the stage arts (mainly music), and knowledge of Haitian culture and heritage.
