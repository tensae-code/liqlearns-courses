## Sub lesson 1 — EEA's three volets and the heritage competency

Haiti's **Éducation Esthétique et Artistique (EEA)** is built in **three volets**: **arts plastiques et visuels**, **arts de la scène** (mainly music), and **knowledge of Haitian culture and heritage (patrimoine haïtien)**. Its general competencies are expressing yourself through the arts, artistic techniques — and **Competency 3: knowing and upholding the national heritage (patrimoine national) while appreciating universal heritage**. Each year carries its own cultural theme: 7e « Éveil artistique et environnement », 8e « Expressions culturelles et valorisation du patrimoine », 9e « Entrepreneuriat culturel et métiers des arts ».

## Sub lesson 2 — Creating from the heritage

Unité 4 — **Créativité et découverte du patrimoine** — teaches **reappropriating the heritage**: creating from elements of the **heritage, art, craft and society**, and learning it by **visits to historic sites, workshops, laboratories, building sites, art galleries and museums**, with projects on the **arts and heritage professions**.
