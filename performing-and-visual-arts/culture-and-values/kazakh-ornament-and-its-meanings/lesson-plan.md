# Lesson plan — Kazakh Ornament and Its Meanings

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Culture and Values · **Lesson:** Kazakh Ornament and Its Meanings
**Place in the curriculum:** Culture and Values (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** kazakh Ornament and Its Meanings — its forms, history and cultural meaning, taught as one topic lesson..
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this course in the same title.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Kazakhstan Grade 4 Artistic Labour** — the families of Kazakh ornament: zoomorphic (ram's horn, goose neck), plant (trefoil, tree), geometric (chain, crutch) and cosmogonic (star, crescent) motifs and what they mean; celestial bodies in Kazakh folk ornament: star, moon and sun motifs cut in clay and foil for the lamp project
2. **Kazakhstan Grade 4 Music (Gorchakova)** — Kazakh star lore: the esepchi, hereditary calculators who read weather and seasons from the sky; Kazakh names for the Great Bear (Zhetigen, Zheti kart, Zheti karakshy) and the Little Bear (Akboz at, Kokboz at), tied to shepherd life
3. **Kazakhstan Grade 5 Artistic Labour (girls, Alimsaeva)** — what it teaches: the four families of Kazakh ornament motifs, each pictured and named — zoomorphic (қошқар мүйіз ram's horn, қаз мойын goose neck, түйе табан camel foot), plant (ағаш tree, гүл flower), cosmogonic (шеңбер circle, жұлдыз star, айшық crescent) and geometric (ирек, тұмарша) — on syrmak carpets, tuskiiz, ayak kap bags and torsyk flasks; what it teaches: what colours mean in Kazakh applied art — blue the sky, white the pure path, truth and happiness, yellow wisdom, green renewal and youth, black strength and majesty, and red above all, the sun and sacred cleansing fire; harmonious colour matching is the kolorit
4. **Kazakhstan Grade 5 Artistic Labour (boys, Chukalin)** — what it teaches: ornament on the tekemet felt carpet — felted wool strips laid in patterns, the «қос мүйіз» paired-horns cross at the centre of the rhombic figures; what it teaches: what colours mean in Kazakh applied art — blue the sky, white the pure path, truth and happiness, yellow wisdom, green renewal and youth, black strength and majesty, and red above all, the sun and sacred cleansing fire; harmonious colour matching is the kolorit
5. **Kazakhstan Grade 6 Music (Musakhajaeva)** — what it teaches: ornament as an information text that teaches the world order; the Baiterek world-tree legend and its three worlds, mirrored in the three parts of the tokpe kui (bas buyn, orta buyn, saga) and in the endless «бітпес» balance of ornament
6. **Kazakhstan Grade 8 Artistic Labour (girls, Alimsaeva)** — what it teaches: decorating is a main element of Kazakh national clothing; decorating a kamzol and headwear mostly used ornament; clothing and headwear were decorated through embroidery (кестелеу) — in embroidery the Kazakh people's worldview, the national culture's magical and aesthetic sides are depicted; mostly imported threads in colours contrasting with or matching the ground fabric — wool, cotton, silk threads — were embroidered; for decorating a kamzol and headwear, тана fasteners made of precious and semi-precious stones were used; appliqué decoration (жапсырмалы әшекей) was also widely used — pieces of one-colour or multi-colour fabric, thin felt, suede, leather and fur scraps are glued or sewn onto the base fabric along the pattern's edge and the decoration's edge is embroidered with beads; mostly the decoration was placed along the clothing edges — ornaments sewn along the sleeve, hem and neckline edges were believed to protect the body's open places left open from evil forces; what it teaches: from ancient times a person strove to distinguish himself in his surroundings and catch the eye; for this in early times he put varied marks on his body, later put varied patterns on his clothing and dressed decoratively; as clothing samples developed and improved, decorating methods formed — appliqué (жапсырмалау), putting a pattern, decorating, perforating (перфорациялау), relief (бедерлеу), processing with metal, embroidering (кестелеу); ornament is often used in decorating a product — ornament is put on a garment's varied parts: the dress's sleeve, collar, hem and chest part, the kamzol, the fastened белдемше, the headwear worn at a wedding (сәукеле) parts, the headwear of brides going out, the халат, the жейде's collar, the тақия and so on; embroidered ornament fragments are pictured
7. **Kazakhstan Grade 9 NIS Art (Akhmetkalieva)** — what it teaches: our ancestors held air and water, fire and sun as life's eye and source and valued them specially; therefore from early times they worshipped sky and earth, fire and water; each of them has its mysterious power; symbolism (символдармен түсіндіру) is the system of conditional signs formed from old times in human history expressing the understanding of nature's mysterious power; the beginning of creation — knowledge about nature's elements was known in the East 2500 years ago; the Greek philosopher Empedocles (Эмпедокл) who lived in Sicily around 490–430 BC counted earth, air, fire and water as creation's basis — four elements (төрт стихия); Aristotle explained the four elements as matter's four states — each matter's part has one quality property: fire — hot and dry; air — hot and damp; earth — cold and dry; water — cold and damp; if one of these four properties changes it passes to another element — for example, in a definite case water turns to vapour (air element) or ice (earth element); so all four elements are closely connected and pass into one another; sometimes they fight each other — for example, fire takes water, water puts fire out, but water's fire...; the elements in the planet's structure (earth — lithosphere, water — hydrosphere, air — atmosphere, fire — magnetosphere of charged particles); in the creation model the four elements stand by hierarchy law (fire — the creative world, air — the spiritual world, water — the star world, earth — the material world); in psychology the four elements correspond to the temperament types (холерик — fire element, меланхолик — earth, сангвиник — air, флегматик — water); the elements' mutual action pairs; the elements' symbols — joining the triangle signs (fire △, water ▽, air and earth triangles) into one figure a six-pointed star can be taken; the symbol tables: earth symbols (Табиғат/Жер-Ана — Nature/Mother Earth; the Tarot's Empress arcana; the Egyptian goddess Demeter (?); Әулие Құдай-Ана) and the planets Saturn and Venus (Шолпан); air symbols (Қылыш; the Tarot...; Ақ құс — the white bird) and Mercury; water symbols (Балықтар — fish; Теңіз түбі — the sea depth; Құмыра — the cup) and Neptune and the Moon (Ай); fire symbols (Оттың жалыны — flame; Найзағайдың жарқылы — lightning's flash; Жанартаудың атқылауы — a volcano's eruption) and the Sun (Күн) and Mars (Қызылжұлдыз); Japan and the water element — the Japanese people's life is closely tied to water: water purification ceremonies (the Mitarai festival, washing hands and mouth at shrine entrances — the pictured photographs) and the wave painting; Slavic culture's elements — Макошь (the earth element's symbol — a rhombus divided in four: the sown field), Хлябь (the water symbol — wavy lines under a rhombus: waters under the earth), Агни (the fire symbol) — the Slavic people called fire and earthly fire (flame, lightning) by these names; what it teaches: people expressed their understanding of the world and their beliefs not only with symbols but also with images (бейнелер); for example, woman, year (?), horse, bird, tree-of-life and other images are closely connected with definite belief-understandings; the Earth was compared to a Mother-woman (Әйел-ана), so the woman's image was taken as the symbol of fertility and abundance; likewise the Tree of Life (Өмір ағашы) image expresses life's continuing, nourishing, growing-spreading; therefore it was recognised as the earth's nourishing symbol; in the year's course birds take the sun's warmth away on their wings and bring spring flying; by scholars' opinion, all the named images are connected with nature and its owners — the Fire, Water and Air elements' symbols; the Ukrainian people's embroidered towel (кестелі сүлгі — rushnik) is a specially sacred, purity and protection symbol: in Ukrainian folk culture the rushnik has had a big place to this day — it is a stitched picture with magical symbols; in the embroidery the shown nature signs, elements and person symbols can be seen; so the rushnik serves as a connecting link between person and the powerful forces; табиғат символдары (Kazakh ornament as nature symbols) — what it teaches: Turkmen carpets are Turkmenistan's national symbol — there is a saying «Су — түркменнің өмірі, ат — түркменнің қанаты, кілем — түркменнің жаны» (Water is the Turkmen's life, the horse is the Turkmen's wing, the carpet is the Turkmen's soul); in the Turkmen carpet's centre big patterns called «гөл» are laid, and the carpet's patterns are symmetric; the Kazakh киіз үй is nomad culture's answer to nature: formed by a law coming from the tradition of daily life, the yurt's inner parts divide in the four elements' correct order (От — Ауа, Жер, Су), its lower parts are placed according to its position, and colours not met in nature are not chosen in the Түс таңдау (colour choice) either — colours taken from nature are used (iron-red below, rising upward lighter colours are used); in ою-өрнектер (ornaments) the composition's main two tools — symmetry and rhythm (симметрия мен ырғақ) — are used; the Kazakh's ою-өрнек is closely connected with nature and its phenomena: earth, water, sky, sun, fire are the main powers in nomads' imagination, so signs showing them are very many in the ою-өрнек world; water is shown by wavy lines (иректер), and together with it a star, the moon and stars are divided ою-өрнектер; the oldest pattern is шеңбер (the circle) — it is a sun symbol; the ornaments become nature symbols: Күн шеңбері (sun circle), Жұлдыз (star), Шаңырақ, Шұғыла, Қимылдақ (?), Тұмарша, Ағаш (tree), Жарты Ай (crescent moon); each colour of Kazakh ою-өрнек has its own meaning: Көк (blue) — the sky, Tengri's symbol; Қызыл (red) — fire, the sun's warmth's symbol; Ақ (white) — the road, purity, happiness's sign; Сары (yellow) — ақыл-ой (intellect's) sign; Қара (black) — earth, strength's, greatness's sign; Жасыл (green) — spring, youth's token; Shämshibai Baidilda (Шәмшібай Байділда), who knows Kazakh ornament's secret deeply, can speak for hours about Kazakh ою-өрнек: «In any ornament the world image, good and evil's eternal struggle is shown,» he says; Kamila Zhaparova's (Камила Жапарованың) paintings «Жайлау», «Көне қала», «Құрметті қонақ» are pictured
