| Kazakhstan — § 11. Сәндеу (Decorating) — what it teaches: decorating is a main element of Kazakh national clothing; decorating a kamzol and headwear mostly used ornament; clothing and headwear were decorated through embroidery (кестелеу) — in embroidery the Kazakh people's worldview, the national culture's magical and aesthetic sides are depicted; mostly imported threads in colours contrasting with or matching the ground fabric — wool, cotton, silk threads — were embroidered; for decorating a kamzol and headwear, тана fasteners made of precious and semi-precious stones were used; appliqué decoration (жапсырмалы әшекей) was also widely used — pieces of one-colour or multi-colour fabric, thin felt, suede, leather and fur scraps are glued or sewn onto the base fabric along the pattern's edge and the decoration's edge is embroidered with beads; mostly the decoration was placed along the clothing edges — ornaments sewn along the sleeve, hem and neckline edges were believed to protect the body's open places left open from evil forces (student book) | Book 6, printed p. 44 |

| Kazakhstan — 2.1. Әртүрлі мәдениеттегі табиғат стихиялары (Nature's elements in various cultures) — what it teaches: our ancestors held air and water, fire and sun as life's eye and source and valued them specially; therefore from early times they worshipped sky and earth, fire and water; each of them has its mysterious power; symbolism (символдармен түсіндіру) is the system of conditional signs formed from old times in human history expressing the understanding of nature's mysterious power; the beginning of creation — knowledge about nature's elements was known in the East 2500 years ago; the Greek philosopher Empedocles (Эмпедокл) who lived in Sicily around 490–430 BC counted earth, air, fire and water as creation's basis — four elements (төрт стихия); Aristotle explained the four elements as matter's four states — each matter's part has one quality property: fire — hot and dry; air — hot and damp; earth — cold and dry; water — cold and damp; if one of these four properties changes it passes to another element — for example, in a definite case water turns to vapour (air element) or ice (earth element); so all four elements are closely connected and pass into one another; sometimes they fight each other — for example, fire takes water, water puts fire out, but water's fire...; the elements in the planet's structure (earth — lithosphere, water — hydrosphere, air — atmosphere, fire — magnetosphere of charged particles); in the creation model the four elements stand by hierarchy law (fire — the creative world, air — the spiritual world, water — the star world, earth — the material world); in psychology the four elements correspond to the temperament types (холерик — fire element, меланхолик — earth, сангвиник — air, флегматик — water); the elements' mutual action pairs; the elements' symbols — joining the triangle signs (fire △, water ▽, air and earth triangles) into one figure a six-pointed star can be taken; the symbol tables: earth symbols (Табиғат/Жер-Ана — Nature/Mother Earth; the Tarot's Empress arcana; the Egyptian goddess Demeter (?); Әулие Құдай-Ана) and the planets Saturn and Venus (Шолпан); air symbols (Қылыш; the Tarot...; Ақ құс — the white bird) and Mercury; water symbols (Балықтар — fish; Теңіз түбі — the sea depth; Құмыра — the cup) and Neptune and the Moon (Ай); fire symbols (Оттың жалыны — flame; Найзағайдың жарқылы — lightning's flash; Жанартаудың атқылауы — a volcano's eruption) and the Sun (Күн) and Mars (Қызылжұлдыз); Japan and the water element — the Japanese people's life is closely tied to water: water purification ceremonies (the Mitarai festival, washing hands and mouth at shrine entrances — the pictured photographs) and the wave painting; Slavic culture's elements — Макошь (the earth element's symbol — a rhombus divided in four: the sown field), Хлябь (the water symbol — wavy lines under a rhombus: waters under the earth), Агни (the fire symbol) — the Slavic people called fire and earthly fire (flame, lightning) by these names (student book; original target `culture-and-values/nature-elements-and-their-symbols`) | Book 7, printed p. 40 |
| Kazakhstan — 2.2. Табиғат стихиялары халық шығармашылығында (Nature's elements in folk creativity) — what it teaches: people expressed their understanding of the world and their beliefs not only with symbols but also with images (бейнелер); for example, woman, year (?), horse, bird, tree-of-life and other images are closely connected with definite belief-understandings; the Earth was compared to a Mother-woman (Әйел-ана), so the woman's image was taken as the symbol of fertility and abundance; likewise the Tree of Life (Өмір ағашы) image expresses life's continuing, nourishing, growing-spreading; therefore it was recognised as the earth's nourishing symbol; in the year's course birds take the sun's warmth away on their wings and bring spring flying; by scholars' opinion, all the named images are connected with nature and its owners — the Fire, Water and Air elements' symbols; the Ukrainian people's embroidered towel (кестелі сүлгі — rushnik) is a specially sacred, purity and protection symbol: in Ukrainian folk culture the rushnik has had a big place to this day — it is a stitched picture with magical symbols; in the embroidery the shown nature signs, elements and person symbols can be seen; so the rushnik serves as a connecting link between person and the powerful forces (student book; original target `culture-and-values/nature-elements-and-their-symbols`) | Book 7, printed p. 44 |
| Kazakhstan — 2.2 (continued). Қазақ ою-өрнегі — табиғат символдары (Kazakh ornament as nature symbols) — what it teaches: Turkmen carpets are Turkmenistan's national symbol — there is a saying «Су — түркменнің өмірі, ат — түркменнің қанаты, кілем — түркменнің жаны» (Water is the Turkmen's life, the horse is the Turkmen's wing, the carpet is the Turkmen's soul); in the Turkmen carpet's centre big patterns called «гөл» are laid, and the carpet's patterns are symmetric; the Kazakh киіз үй is nomad culture's answer to nature: formed by a law coming from the tradition of daily life, the yurt's inner parts divide in the four elements' correct order (От — Ауа, Жер, Су), its lower parts are placed according to its position, and colours not met in nature are not chosen in the Түс таңдау (colour choice) either — colours taken from nature are used (iron-red below, rising upward lighter colours are used); in ою-өрнектер (ornaments) the composition's main two tools — symmetry and rhythm (симметрия мен ырғақ) — are used; the Kazakh's ою-өрнек is closely connected with nature and its phenomena: earth, water, sky, sun, fire are the main powers in nomads' imagination, so signs showing them are very many in the ою-өрнек world; water is shown by wavy lines (иректер), and together with it a star, the moon and stars are divided ою-өрнектер; the oldest pattern is шеңбер (the circle) — it is a sun symbol; the ornaments become nature symbols: Күн шеңбері (sun circle), Жұлдыз (star), Шаңырақ, Шұғыла, Қимылдақ (?), Тұмарша, Ағаш (tree), Жарты Ай (crescent moon); each colour of Kazakh ою-өрнек has its own meaning: Көк (blue) — the sky, Tengri's symbol; Қызыл (red) — fire, the sun's warmth's symbol; Ақ (white) — the road, purity, happiness's sign; Сары (yellow) — ақыл-ой (intellect's) sign; Қара (black) — earth, strength's, greatness's sign; Жасыл (green) — spring, youth's token; Shämshibai Baidilda (Шәмшібай Байділда), who knows Kazakh ornament's secret deeply, can speak for hours about Kazakh ою-өрнек: «In any ornament the world image, good and evil's eternal struggle is shown,» he says; Kamila Zhaparova's (Камила Жапарованың) paintings «Жайлау», «Көне қала», «Құрметті қонақ» are pictured (student book) | Book 7, printed p. 45 |
