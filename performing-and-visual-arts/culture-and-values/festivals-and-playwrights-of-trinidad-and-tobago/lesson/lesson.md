# Festivals and Playwrights of Trinidad and Tobago

## Do it first

Quick start: from the title alone, guess what this lesson's work is. Then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — The festival calendar as drama material

Trinidad and Tobago's Drama guide lists the country's **festivals as cultural forms for drama**: **Carnival, Christmas Parang, Divali, Easter Passion Play, Eid ul Fitr, Hosay, Phagwa, Ramleela, Santa Rosa, Sipari Mai, St Peter's Day, Tobago Heritage Festival and Harvest**. The whole year, from every community, is drama material.

## Sub lesson 2 — The country's playwrights

The same recommended lists name the country's **playwrights**: **Ronald Amoroso, Zeno Constance, Victor Edwards, Rawle Gibbons, Tony Hall, Errol Hill, Errol John, Freddie Kissoon, Arthur Lewis, Ralph Maraj, Eric Roach, Pearl Eintou Springer, Davlin Thomas, Derek Walcott and Efebo Wilkinson** — the writers who made Trinidad and Tobago's theatre.
