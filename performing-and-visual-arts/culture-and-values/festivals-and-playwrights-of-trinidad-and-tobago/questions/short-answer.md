# Festivals and Playwrights of Trinidad and Tobago — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name four festivals from the guide's list.
**Answer: Any four of: Carnival, Christmas Parang, Divali, Easter Passion Play, Eid ul Fitr, Hosay, Phagwa, Ramleela, Santa Rosa, Sipari Mai, St Peter's Day, Tobago Heritage Festival, Harvest.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name three playwrights of Trinidad and Tobago from the list.
**Answer: Any three of: Ronald Amoroso, Zeno Constance, Victor Edwards, Rawle Gibbons, Tony Hall, Errol Hill, Errol John, Freddie Kissoon, Arthur Lewis, Ralph Maraj, Eric Roach, Pearl Eintou Springer, Davlin Thomas, Derek Walcott, Efebo Wilkinson.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Which two festivals come from the country's Indian heritage traditions in the list?
**Answer: Any two of: Divali, Phagwa, Ramleela, Hosay.**
