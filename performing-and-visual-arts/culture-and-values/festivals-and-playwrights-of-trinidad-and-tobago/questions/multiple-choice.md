# Festivals and Playwrights of Trinidad and Tobago — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which festival is named in the guide's list?
a) Ramleela  b) the Edinburgh Festival  c) a school sports day only
**Answer: a) Ramleela**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Parang belongs to which season in the list?
a) Christmas  b) Easter only  c) no season
**Answer: a) Christmas**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Derek Walcott is named as…
a) one of the country's playwrights  b) a costume designer only  c) a festival
**Answer: a) one of the country's playwrights**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Pearl Eintou Springer is…
a) a playwright of Trinidad and Tobago  b) a dance step  c) a musical instrument
**Answer: a) a playwright of Trinidad and Tobago**

**5. (Sub lesson 1)** The Tobago festival named is the…
a) Tobago Heritage Festival  b) Tobago Boat Race only  c) Harvest of another country
**Answer: a) Tobago Heritage Festival**
