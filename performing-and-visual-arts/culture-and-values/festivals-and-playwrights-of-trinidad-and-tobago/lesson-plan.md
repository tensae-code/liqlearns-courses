# Lesson plan — Festivals and Playwrights of Trinidad and Tobago

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Culture and Values · **Lesson:** Festivals and Playwrights of Trinidad and Tobago
**Place in the curriculum:** Culture and Values (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Trinidad and Tobago's festival calendar as drama material — Carnival, Ramleela, Hosay, Phagwa, Divali, Parang and more — and the country's own playwrights, from Errol Hill and Errol John to Derek Walcott and Pearl Eintou Springer.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this course in the same title.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The festival calendar as drama material** — Carnival, Christmas Parang, Divali, Easter Passion Play, Eid ul Fitr, Hosay, Phagwa, Ramleela, Santa Rosa, Sipari Mai, St Peter's Day, Tobago Heritage Festival and Harvest — the year's festivals as material for drama.
2. **The country's playwrights** — Ronald Amoroso, Zeno Constance, Victor Edwards, Rawle Gibbons, Tony Hall, Errol Hill, Errol John, Freddie Kissoon, Arthur Lewis, Ralph Maraj, Eric Roach, Pearl Eintou Springer, Davlin Thomas, Derek Walcott and Efebo Wilkinson.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
