# Video script — The We Tree

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This is Moldova's own lesson.

**Proiect de creație "Arborele NOI".** Unit V §22, Proiect de creație "Arborele NOI" — the WE Tree creation project: a group builds its tree — branches for each member, leaves inscribed with each one's own traits and skills, and a second set of leaves naming the nation's culture and the cultures of the peoples living together in Moldova (Mărțișor, sarmale, Casa Mare, Hederlez, Martenița, Vyshyvanka, Hanukkah) — then exhibits the trees, taking the roles of artist, guide, critic, spectator, reporter.

That is The We Tree — Moldova's way, kept by name.
