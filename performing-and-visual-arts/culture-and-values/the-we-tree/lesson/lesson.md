# The We Tree

## Do it first

Name one thing that belongs in this lesson — The We Tree — its forms, history and cultural meaning, taught as one topic lesson. Say what it is in your own words. If you can name one, you are already inside the lesson.

## Sub lesson 1 — Proiect de creație "Arborele NOI"

Unit V §22, Proiect de creație "Arborele NOI" — the WE Tree creation project: a group builds its tree — branches for each member, leaves inscribed with each one's own traits and skills, and a second set of leaves naming the nation's culture and the cultures of the peoples living together in Moldova (Mărțișor, sarmale, Casa Mare, Hederlez, Martenița, Vyshyvanka, Hanukkah) — then exhibits the trees, taking the roles of artist, guide, critic, spectator, reporter.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson holds, part by part, in your own words. Companion checks: did every part get named, with its Moldovan name kept where the book gives one?

## Do It Yourself

1. Choose one named part of this lesson and find out one more true thing about it — from a person who knows it, or from the book your part came from.
2. Make or perform a small piece in the way of that part: a rhythm, a step, a drawing, a shaped piece of clay.
3. Write one sentence: what does this part show about Moldova's own way of teaching its arts?
