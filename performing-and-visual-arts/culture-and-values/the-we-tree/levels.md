## Sub lesson 1 — Proiect de creație "Arborele NOI"
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Unit V §22, Proiect de creație "Arborele NOI" — the WE Tree creation project: a group builds its tree — branches for each member, leaves inscribed with each one's own traits and skills, and a second set of leaves naming the nation's culture and the cultures of the peoples living together in Moldova (Mărțișor, sarmale, Casa Mare, Hederlez, Martenița, Vyshyvanka, Hanukkah) — then exhibits the trees, taking the roles of artist, guide, critic, spectator, reporter.
