## Sub lesson 4 — Kazakhstan Grade 8 Artistic Labour (girls, Alimsaeva)

what it teaches: Kazakh national clothing is a unique sample of material culture fitted to the life of the steppe — hot summers, cold winters, dry winds, sharp temperature changes; it is simple, beautiful, harmonious, convenient, made to protect from cold, heat and wind; most of today's masters use national patterns when preparing a clothing model — this style is called «этно» (folk); ethnostyle revives the people's national clothing: preparing a costume uses that people's own cut sample, materials, colour, ornament and accessories; using the national costume tradition is widely spread in modern clothes; ethnostyle has taken its place in the fashion world as a special sample of clothing — Kazakhstan today has several large fashion houses systematically producing collections in the ethnic direction, and ornament is used as a style element for decorating; the main thing is not likeness to national clothing but creatively improving it and making new samples fit for the time; ethnostyle models by the designers Aya Bapani and Nurzhamal Nurpeisova are pictured; headwear, kamzol) — what it teaches: modern designers take the national costume as a sample when preparing modern clothing types; national dress divides by age into girls' dress, brides' dress, middle-aged and old women's dress; көйлек, қамзол and тақия count as a girl-child's national dress; the көйлек until the second half of the 19th century was cut in one piece from top to bottom, later the dress's cut became complex — cut separately to the waist line, a wide gathered белдемше sewn on, sleeves and hem decorated with ruffles (желбіршек), embroidered, decorated with silver coins and special тана fasteners; the dress's outer side was sewn loose-fitting, sleeveless, collarless; girls going out wore a тақия — decorated with beads and embroidered ornament, an owl feather (үкі) attached as a тұмар (amulet); by custom at a wedding feast the girl wore a сәукеле on her head (up to 70 cm high), and after going out wore it only at feasts for a year; a new mother wore a кимешек sewn of white cloth, not showing the hair, especially the temple part; an old person had to cover the hair; older women wore a қамзол over a wide dress; in winter women wore a шапан sewn of velvet, a бөрік edged with animal fur, an орамал scarf

## Sub lesson 5 — Kazakhstan Grade 9 Artistic Labour (girls, Alimsaeva)

what it teaches: a photo gallery of Kazakhstan's fashion designers and their runway work: Балнұр Асанова (Balnur Asanova) and her embroidered black dresses; Құралай Нұрқаділова (Kuralai Nurkadilova) with her grey layered gowns; Аида Кауменова (Aida Kaumenova) with richly patterned dresses and headdresses; Ая Бапани (Aya Bapani) in a Kazakh-embroidered jacket, with models in bright national-style outfits and a white cape with red ornament; Айгүл Қасымова (Aigul Kasymova) with minimalist red and lilac outfits; and Лария Джакамбаева (Lariya Dzhakambaeva) with floral-print and long patterned dresses
