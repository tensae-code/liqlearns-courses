# Video script — Our Community's Values

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

What are **cultural values**?
They are **what the community does**.

**Receiving guests.** **Respect and love.**
The elders' **shimglina** — making peace.
**Eating together** from one **mesob**.
**Helping each other.** **Loving our country.**

And at home? **Be the example.**
Fair work. Kind deeds. Respect. Helping.
Values are not said. Values are **done**.
