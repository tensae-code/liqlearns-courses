# Hands-on — Our Community's Values

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The values picture hunt
On a walk with an adult, hunt values in action: a greeting, a helping hand, a shared meal, elders talking peace. Count how many of the book's list you can see in one walk. Report the hunt at home.

## Be an example week
For one week, choose one value each day and do it on purpose: receive a guest well, help without being asked, divide work fairly, give to someone in need. Each night, tell your family which value you practised — exemplarity, practised.
