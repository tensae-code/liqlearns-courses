# Lesson plan — Our Community's Values

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Culture and Values · **Lesson:** Our Community's Values
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 5 — Culture and Values (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** what our community holds good — receiving guests, respect and love, the elders' mediation, eating together, helping each other, loving our country — and being an example of those values at home.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the child's own community — the values practised in their own street and home.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the
exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an
image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture
of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does
with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and
while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is
dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the
student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph,
or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be
shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved (the gate: README, "Units, Lessons and Sub Lessons (ruled 2026-11-08)"):

1. **Naming our values** — The definition and the list — what the community does — with the picture practice folded in.
2. **What our community values** — The chapter opener: values expressed in music, dance, colours, drama and theatre — and the review fold.
3. **Values in art — being an example at home** — Exemplarity: fair division of work, charitable deeds, respect, helping — shown by parents, pictured in eating together.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the other Culture and Values lessons. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Define cultural values in the book's words: what the community does.
2. Name the values: receiving guests, mutual respect and love, shimglina, eating together from one mesob, helping each other, loving one's country, heroism.
3. Read pictures of values in action and name the value each picture shows.
4. Say what exemplarity means at home: the good actions parents show their children.

## What you need
Your own community — and one elder to listen to.

## If the learner is stuck
- Lists values as feelings ('kindness in the heart') → the book's definition is doing: values are what the community DOES — point to the action.
- Does not know shimglina → it is the elders' mediation: quarrels brought to the elders, peace made by listening and judgement.
