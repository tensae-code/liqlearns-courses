# Our Community's Values — short answer

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name four values from the book's list.
**Answer:** Any four of: receiving guests; mutual respect and love; shimglina (elder mediation); eating together from one mesob; helping each other; loving one's country and heroism; community activities; crafts.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is the review's short-answer task?
**Answer:** List the cultural values practised in your area.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name three exemplary actions parents show.
**Answer:** Any three of: fair division of work; charitable deeds; respect; helping each other.
