# Our Community's Values — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Cultural values are defined as —
a) what the community does  b) what one person feels  c) rules from a foreign book
**Answer: a) what the community does**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Shimglina is —
a) a kind of dress  b) elder mediation and reconciliation  c) a festival game
**Answer: b) elder mediation and reconciliation**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Eating together, in the book's list, is —
a) from one mesob  b) each person alone  c) only at restaurants
**Answer: a) from one mesob**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The values chapter's history story is —
a) the Battle of Adwa  b) a sea voyage  c) the first aeroplane
**Answer: a) the Battle of Adwa** — because loving one's country and heroism are values.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** In the review, cultural values can be expressed by —
a) music only  b) theatre only  c) music, theatre and drawing — all
**Answer: c) music, theatre and drawing — all**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** Exemplarity means —
a) the good, community-accepted actions parents show their children  b) punishing children loudly  c) owning many things
**Answer: a) the good, community-accepted actions parents show their children**
