# Lesson plan — The Kazakh Yurt and Its Interior

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Culture and Values · **Lesson:** The Kazakh Yurt and Its Interior
**Place in the curriculum:** Culture and Values (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the Kazakh Yurt and Its Interior — its forms, history and cultural meaning, taught as one topic lesson..
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this course in the same title.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Kazakhstan Grade 1 Visual Art Guide** — modelling the yurt and its ornament in relief
2. **Kazakhstan Grade 1 Kulmanova Music** — the shanyrak (the yurt's crown wheel, the home itself) as the sign of family health and unity in song
3. **Kazakhstan Grade 2 Labour Guide KZ** — "The yurt (ui)": the Kazakh dwelling made as a model
4. **Kazakhstan Grade 2 Labour Guide RU** — "Model of a yurt"
5. **Kazakhstan Grade 2 Korolkova Art Labour** — the yurt's crown wheel as the sign of the home
6. **Kazakhstan Grade 2 Ermilova Art Labour (Uyghur), contents pp. 4-5** — the home's crown as the people's sign
7. **Kazakhstan Grade 3 Muratkyzy Labour** — the kiiz ui (yurt): its parts (kerege lattice, uyk poles, shanyrak crown, sykyrlauyk door) and how it is raised
8. **Kazakhstan Grade 3 Tolebiev Visual Art** — the yurt in painting (Ibaev's "Zhailau da"), its parts (uyk, kerege, shanyrak, door), then designed and built from cardboard as a designer would
9. **Kazakhstan Grade 3 Zhanaev Visual Art** — architecture kinds (housing, industry, public, memorial); the felt house as the first Kazakh architecture, its design drawn
10. **Kazakhstan Grade 3 Syzdykov Labour** — the frame named part by part (qanat, shanyrak, uyk, kerege, esik); a model yurt raised over a plastic bottle, decorated with ornament by stamping and plasticine, and set in an ethnoaul with a cardboard horse
11. **Kazakhstan Grade 3 Raupova Art Labour** — the Kazakh people's traditional dwelling; we make an ancient dwelling (a tipi)
12. **Kazakhstan Grade 4 Mamyrova Visual Art** — the symbol of the Kazakh people's unity" — graphics (line, hatching, tone); the shanyrak drawn as a graphic composition with its fastenings and decorative elements
13. **Kazakhstan Grade 4 Muratkyzy Labour** — the yurt's parts named on a diagram (shanyrak, uyk, kerege, bosaga) and a yurt model made
14. **Kazakhstan Grade 4 Artistic Labour** — inside the Kazakh felt yurt: bosaga threshold, tor place of honour, the hearth, and how the interior is furnished and decorated; the shanyrak: the yurt's sacred roof ring, symbol of home, family unity and blessing
15. **Kazakhstan Grade 4 Labour Training (Ormanbekova)** — шанырак!» — the shanyrak as the image of one home for all the peoples of Kazakhstan; a composition of the country's map, a paper-strip shanyrak and symmetric folded-paper figures of people
16. **Kazakhstan Grade 4 Labour Training (Muratkыzy)** — the yurt's structure in a labelled cutaway (shanyrak, uyk poles, kerege lattice, kiiz felt, double door) and building a yurt model from waste cardboard, decorating inside and out with Kazakh ornament; the besik, the Kazakh cradle of pine, birch or willow, light and strong; its named parts in a labelled diagram; "upbringing begins with the cradle"
17. **Kazakhstan Grade 9 NIS Art (Akhmetkalieva)** — what it teaches: the Kazakh people's киіз үй (felt house), very convenient for the nomadic life, is counted one of the dwellings come from old times; although centuries have passed since it first appeared among the steppe's inhabitants, it has not changed much; yurts even today have their own special demand — tourists are interested in the киіз үй's structure; therefore the киіз үй's image and inner equipment are often seen in artists' works; Evgeny Kim (Евгений Ким) is a Kazakhstani artist whose works are seen in the world's famous galleries — the main theme of his works is the Kazakh people's life: in E. Kim's pictures the free steppe's spirit, Kazakh culture's peculiarities and the nomads' voice can be felt; in the artist's works the киіз үй is not only a dwelling but an inseparable part of Kazakh life (his «Көшпенділер өмірі» (Nomads' Life) series, XIX–early XX century life paintings, are pictured); by purpose киіз үйs divide into types: the тұрғын үй (dwelling yurt) for everyday life — it is warm in winter and does not let heat in summer, protecting from wind, snow and rain; the салтанатты киіз үй (ceremonial yurt) — a big yurt of twelve or more қанат (wall sections), made of felt for celebrations; the жорық үй (campaign yurt) — a 3–4 қанат small yurt taken on campaign; today companies (Belgium's Be Yurt) make modern felt yurts with electric equipment, plastic windows and eco-materials — the practical task is to sketch a modern киіз үй interior in ethnostyle, using minimalism's peculiarities
18. **Kazakhstan Grade 9 Artistic Labour (boys, Chukalin)** — what it teaches: the harmony of decorative art and interior space can be seen in the Kazakh киіз үй (felt house): its interior consists of the things needed for life, and the things in it, besides being very needed in everyday life, give the house beauty and attract the eye; a row of things are directly connected with the yurt's architecture — the yurt's frame patterned with bone, silvered, with varied ою-өрнек (ornament) inlaid in its wooden frame; to decorate the киіз үй textile items are used much — тоқыма бұйымдар (woven items) are very needed for everyday life, the wall-hung items protect from the cold, and ұықбау, үзікбау, басқұр (woven bands) and other items give the киіз үй's interior beauty; every item in the house has its own place — түскиіз (wall felt carpets), varied қоржындар (saddlebags) and the rest divide the interior into separate zones; what it teaches: in the Kazakh tradition, the киіз үйдің іші (the felt yurt's inside) was conditionally (шартты түрде) divided into several zones: in it the man (ер адам — the right side, оң жақ) and the woman (әйел адам — the left side, сол жақ) have their own place; in the yurt's middle the ошақ (hearth) is placed; the house's most respected place (ең сыйлы орын) is the төр — it is above the hearth (ошақтан жоғары), facing the threshold (босағаға қарама-қарсы) — there the guests (қонақтар) are seated; towards the төр's left are the house owners' (үй иелерінің), and to the right the young people's (жастардың) rest place is counted
