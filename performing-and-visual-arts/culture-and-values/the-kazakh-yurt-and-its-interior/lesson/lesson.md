## Sub lesson 17 — Kazakhstan Grade 9 NIS Art (Akhmetkalieva)

what it teaches: the Kazakh people's киіз үй (felt house), very convenient for the nomadic life, is counted one of the dwellings come from old times; although centuries have passed since it first appeared among the steppe's inhabitants, it has not changed much; yurts even today have their own special demand — tourists are interested in the киіз үй's structure; therefore the киіз үй's image and inner equipment are often seen in artists' works; Evgeny Kim (Евгений Ким) is a Kazakhstani artist whose works are seen in the world's famous galleries — the main theme of his works is the Kazakh people's life: in E. Kim's pictures the free steppe's spirit, Kazakh culture's peculiarities and the nomads' voice can be felt; in the artist's works the киіз үй is not only a dwelling but an inseparable part of Kazakh life (his «Көшпенділер өмірі» (Nomads' Life) series, XIX–early XX century life paintings, are pictured); by purpose киіз үйs divide into types: the тұрғын үй (dwelling yurt) for everyday life — it is warm in winter and does not let heat in summer, protecting from wind, snow and rain; the салтанатты киіз үй (ceremonial yurt) — a big yurt of twelve or more қанат (wall sections), made of felt for celebrations; the жорық үй (campaign yurt) — a 3–4 қанат small yurt taken on campaign; today companies (Belgium's Be Yurt) make modern felt yurts with electric equipment, plastic windows and eco-materials — the practical task is to sketch a modern киіз үй interior in ethnostyle, using minimalism's peculiarities

## Sub lesson 18 — Kazakhstan Grade 9 Artistic Labour (boys, Chukalin)

what it teaches: the harmony of decorative art and interior space can be seen in the Kazakh киіз үй (felt house): its interior consists of the things needed for life, and the things in it, besides being very needed in everyday life, give the house beauty and attract the eye; a row of things are directly connected with the yurt's architecture — the yurt's frame patterned with bone, silvered, with varied ою-өрнек (ornament) inlaid in its wooden frame; to decorate the киіз үй textile items are used much — тоқыма бұйымдар (woven items) are very needed for everyday life, the wall-hung items protect from the cold, and ұықбау, үзікбау, басқұр (woven bands) and other items give the киіз үй's interior beauty; every item in the house has its own place — түскиіз (wall felt carpets), varied қоржындар (saddlebags) and the rest divide the interior into separate zones; what it teaches: in the Kazakh tradition, the киіз үйдің іші (the felt yurt's inside) was conditionally (шартты түрде) divided into several zones: in it the man (ер адам — the right side, оң жақ) and the woman (әйел адам — the left side, сол жақ) have their own place; in the yurt's middle the ошақ (hearth) is placed; the house's most respected place (ең сыйлы орын) is the төр — it is above the hearth (ошақтан жоғары), facing the threshold (босағаға қарама-қарсы) — there the guests (қонақтар) are seated; towards the төр's left are the house owners' (үй иелерінің), and to the right the young people's (жастардың) rest place is counted
