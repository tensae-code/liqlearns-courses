Suriname's own way: one integrated arts course — Kunst- en cultuureducatie (KCE) — in six domains (Music, Dance, Visual Arts, Cultural Heritage, Theatre, Media), each domain a series of one-hour lessons built on the same frame: the IKA lesson shape (Introductie–Kern–Afsluiting, the core in three phases — warm-up, technique, own work and showing moment) and the Big Idea design process in four phases (orient, research, experiment, reflect), every series answering one research question and ending in a presentation; each domain is deliberately fused with another school subject (music with arithmetic, dance with geography, visual arts with history, theatre with language); the course's heart is Suriname itself — its many peoples' dances, dress, jewellery, heritage and theatre forms — held together by the Alakondre ideal, "Alakondre na wan, wan na Alakondre" (unity in diversity); assessment is by evaluation card and portfolio, process over product. Suriname's own way: the deepening year runs the same six domains on the same frame as leerjaar 7, but at competency levels B/C — pupils switch independently between the four design phases, master the craft itself, argue their choices, handle the emotions of others in their work, and present independently in an unknown space for an unknown audience; music gives meaning to a calculated music work built on the rhythmic patterns of Suriname's five peoples; dance travels a safe route from home through the Amazon interior into Brazil, in awasa, indigenous and Latin American steps; visual arts add hatching and pattern design to the clothing work and end in a fashion show instead of an exhibition; heritage places an object into another culture and performs what that does to its meaning as an oral art form; theatre adapts a known story into an Alakondre Mato; media builds the one-minute film on a storyboard and uploads it to YouTube. Named, not placed, as filed: each domain's begrippenlijst (glossary — Music printed p. 48, Dance pp. 87–88, Visual Arts pp. 128–129, Heritage pp. 158–159, Theatre pp. 197–199, Media pp. 223–224) — inventory only (content-bearing single terms from them are carried inside the placement lines where the lessons use them); each domain's evaluatiekaart (evaluation card — Music pp. 49–51, Dance pp. 90–92, Visual Arts pp. 130–131, Heritage pp. 160–162, Theatre pp. 200–202, Media pp. 225–227) — assessment apparatus, not teaching content; the closing appendices (the KCE lesson form, printed p. 229; deepening questions for preparing a lesson, p. 230; the general KCE evaluation card, p. 231) — teacher apparatus; the Bronnenlijst (sources list, printed pp. 232–234) — inventory only.
