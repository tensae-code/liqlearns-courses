# Hands-on — Alakondre Unity in Diversity

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say the ideal
Say "Alakondre na wan, wan na Alakondre" aloud with a partner, one line each, then say in your own words what it promises a class where many peoples learn together.

## Find the mixture
Name one mixture from daily life — a soup, a piece of clothing, a song — where things from different peoples meet, and say what each part brings.
