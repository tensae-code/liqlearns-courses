# Alakondre Unity in Diversity — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Say the Alakondre saying and what it means.
**Answer: "Alakondre na wan, wan na Alakondre" — Alakondre is one, one is Alakondre: the country's many peoples and cultures as one wealth.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does total inclusivity mean in the deepening year's account?
**Answer: Many truths standing side by side, all equally true and equally valued.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Where did the word Alakondre originally come from?
**Answer: It was originally an Afro-Surinamese word of the town-dwellers, now taken over by all Suriname's peoples.**
