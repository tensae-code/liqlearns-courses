# Alakondre Unity in Diversity — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Alakondre means…
a) only one culture may be taught  b) the many peoples and cultures of Suriname as one wealth  c) a kind of soup only
**Answer: b) the many peoples and cultures of Suriname as one wealth**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The Hindustani word the book matches to Alakondre is…
a) sawiji  b) ekta  c) mato
**Answer: b) ekta**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Sranan is Alakondre's symbol because it is…
a) the oldest language in the world  b) based on all 22 languages spoken or once spoken in Suriname  c) spoken by one people only
**Answer: b) based on all 22 languages spoken or once spoken in Suriname**
