# Video script — Alakondre Unity in Diversity

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Many peoples. One country. One ideal.

Alakondre na wan — wan na Alakondre.

Alakondre is one. One is Alakondre.

Every people's culture is wealth. Together they are one wealth.

Many truths can stand side by side — all equally true, all equally valued.

That is Alakondre — unity in diversity.
