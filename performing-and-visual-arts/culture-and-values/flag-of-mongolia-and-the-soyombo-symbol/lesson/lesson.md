## Sub lesson 1 — Part I: Дүрсийн хэлбэр хэмжэээг өөрчлөх (opening teaching)

Part I: Дүрсийн хэлбэр хэмжэээг өөрчлөх (opening teaching) — the state flag of Mongolia studied: red, blue, red, with the golden Soyombo standing at the centre of the red part beside the hoist; blue is the colour of the eternal sky, red of flourishing; and blue, red and yellow named as basic colours
