# The Flag of Mongolia and the Soyombo Symbol — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Mongolia's flag is…
a) red, blue, red, with the golden Soyombo beside the hoist  b) all green  c) blue and white stripes
**Answer: a) red, blue, red, with the golden Soyombo beside the hoist**

**2.** Blue on the flag is the colour of…
a) the eternal sky  b) the sea only  c) night
**Answer: a) the eternal sky**

**3.** Red on the flag stands for…
a) flourishing  b) sleep  c) winter
**Answer: a) flourishing**
