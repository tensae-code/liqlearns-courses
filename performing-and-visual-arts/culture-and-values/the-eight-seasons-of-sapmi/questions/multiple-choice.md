# The Eight Seasons of Sapmi — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Sápmi's year has…
a) four seasons  b) eight seasons  c) twelve seasons
**Answer: b) eight seasons**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The season after winter is…
a) spring-summer  b) autumn  c) spring-winter
**Answer: c) spring-winter**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The eight seasons carry nature's round and…
a) the reindeer-herding work round  b) the school timetable only  c) nothing else
**Answer: a) the reindeer-herding work round**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Shoe-hay (kenkäheinä) is…
a) hay for horses  b) sedge grass dried as warm shoe insulation  c) a kind of shoe
**Answer: b) sedge grass dried as warm shoe insulation**
