# The Eight Seasons of Sapmi — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the eight seasons in order.
**Answer: Autumn-summer, autumn, autumn-winter, winter, spring-winter, spring, spring-summer, summer.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** When is shoe-hay gathered, and where do you learn to find it?
**Answer: In autumn-summer, found on a nature walk — sedges fit for shoe-hay.**
