# The Eight Seasons of Sapmi — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Customs in the eight-season year pass from generation to generation.
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Shoe-hay is gathered in deep winter.
**Answer: False** — it is gathered in autumn-summer.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The unit teaches the eight seasons in Finnish and in North Sámi.
**Answer: True.**
