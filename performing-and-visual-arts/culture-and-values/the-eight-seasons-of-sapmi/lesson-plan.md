# Lesson plan — The Eight Seasons of Sapmi

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Culture and Values · **Lesson:** The Eight Seasons of Sapmi
**Place in the curriculum:** Culture and Values (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the eight seasons of Sápmi — one turning year of eight seasons, nature's round and the reindeer-herding work round that follows it, with customs passing from generation to generation.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier making and drawing lessons of this course.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The eight seasons — one turning year** — autumn-summer, autumn, autumn-winter, winter, spring-winter, spring, spring-summer, summer — nature's round and the reindeer-herding work round that follows it.
2. **Shoe-hay — the craft-year tradition** — gathering shoe-hay (kenkäheinä — sedge grasses dried as warm shoe insulation) in autumn-summer, and finding sedges fit for it on a nature walk.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier drawing and making lessons of this course. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name the eight seasons of Sápmi in order.
2. Say what shoe-hay is and when it is gathered.

## What you need
A year wheel to draw, and — for the second part — a nature walk where sedges grow.
