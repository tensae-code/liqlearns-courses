# Video script — The Eight Seasons of Sapmi

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Eight seasons turn. Autumn-summer… autumn… autumn-winter… winter…

Spring-winter… spring… spring-summer… summer… and the year turns again.

The reindeer work turns with it. The customs turn with it — grandmother to grandchild.
