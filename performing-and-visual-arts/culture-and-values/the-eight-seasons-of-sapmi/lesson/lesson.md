# The Eight Seasons of Sapmi

## Do it first

Most of the world counts four seasons. The Sámi year turns through EIGHT. Each one brings its own nature — and its own reindeer work.

## Sub lesson 1 — The eight seasons — one turning year

The Finnish–North Sámi teaching unit is built on **Sápmi's eight seasons**: **autumn-summer, autumn, autumn-winter, winter, spring-winter, spring, spring-summer, summer** — **one turning year**. The eight seasons carry **nature's round** and the **reindeer-herding work round that follows it**, with **customs passing from generation to generation**. The school year itself is Sámi in this unit: the seasons, not the four-season calendar, organise the teaching, in Finnish and in North Sámi alike.

## Sub lesson 2 — Shoe-hay — the craft-year tradition

An extra idea of the unit's first chapter keeps a **Sámi craft-year tradition**: in **autumn-summer**, gather **shoe-hay — kenkäheinä, sedge grasses dried as warm shoe insulation** — and learn to **find sedges fit for it on a nature walk**. A craft timed by the eight-season year: the right grass, in the right season, dried to keep feet warm through winter.
