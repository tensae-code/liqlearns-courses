## Sub lesson 1 — The Romanesque of Andorra

Every country course asks: what is **our own** art? In Andorra's batxillerat history-of-art programme, the country's own heritage is placed inside the **art and power** block: **the Romanesque of Andorra** — Andorra's own **Romanesque churches and monasteries**, the stone churches of the Pyrenean valleys.

## Sub lesson 2 — Reading our churches as art and power

The programme reads those churches a second way: as **expressions of the religious power of the early Middle Ages in the Pyrenees**. A small mountain church is therefore two things at once — a work of art to be looked at closely, and a sign of the power that built it. That double reading is how Andorra teaches its own art.
