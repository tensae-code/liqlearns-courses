# Art of Our Country — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Romanesque of Andorra is part of the programme's art-and-power block.
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The programme treats Andorra's churches as modern buildings.
**Answer: False — they are Romanesque, from the early Middle Ages.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A church can be read both as art and as a sign of power.
**Answer: True.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Andorra's heritage is placed outside the programme's questions.
**Answer: False — it is placed inside art and power.**
