# Art of Our Country — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What two kinds of Romanesque buildings are named?
**Answer: Churches and monasteries.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What power do the churches express?
**Answer: The religious power of the early Middle Ages.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** In which mountains does Andorra stand?
**Answer: The Pyrenees.**
