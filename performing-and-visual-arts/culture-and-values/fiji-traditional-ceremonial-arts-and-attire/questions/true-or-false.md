# Fiji Traditional Ceremonial Arts and Attire — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Masi is worn at meke ceremonies.
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Show and Tell asks learners to describe their traditional attire.
**Answer: True.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Sevusevu welcomes and farewells people.
**Answer: True.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Diwali is a Fijian drum.
**Answer: False — Diwali is the festival of light.**
