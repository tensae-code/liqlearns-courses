# Fiji Traditional Ceremonial Arts and Attire — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is masi made from?
**Answer: Tapa cloth — bark cloth (explained by the wearer in Show and Tell).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name Fiji's two worked ceremonial examples.
**Answer: Sevusevu and Diwali.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What do learners do with one of their own ceremonial items?
**Answer: Prepare and demonstrate it in a group, explaining its meaning.**
