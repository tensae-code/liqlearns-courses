# Fiji Traditional Ceremonial Arts and Attire — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Masi is…
a) tapa cloth  b) a drum  c) a song
**Answer: a) tapa cloth.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Masi attire is worn at…
a) weddings and meke ceremonies  b) exams  c) races
**Answer: a) weddings and meke ceremonies.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** A masi costume is often made by…
a) mother, aunty or grandmother  b) a factory only  c) the teacher
**Answer: a) mother, aunty or grandmother.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Sevusevu is the ceremony to…
a) welcome and farewell people  b) open a shop  c) start a race
**Answer: a) welcome and farewell people.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Diwali is…
a) the festival of light  b) a drum  c) a dance step
**Answer: a) the festival of light.**
