# Fiji Traditional Ceremonial Arts and Attire

## Do it first

Quick start: from the title alone, guess what this lesson's work is. Then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — Masi — Fiji's traditional attire

In **Show and Tell**, learners wear and describe their **traditional attire**: the Fijian **masi (tapa cloth)** costume worn at **weddings and meke ceremonies**, **made by mother, aunty or grandmother** — and explain **what it is made of**.

## Sub lesson 2 — Sevusevu, Diwali and Fiji's ceremonial items

Learners identify **their community's ceremonial and religious items and their meanings**: **sevusevu**, the ceremony to **welcome and farewell people**, and **Diwali, the festival of light**, as worked examples — with the **instruments used in Fiji's different places of worship** — and **prepare and demonstrate one of their own items in a group**.
