Verdict: COMPLETE (cover/title page verified against the record title). Fiji's own way: a pupil workbook in four sub-strands (Arts Ideas, Arts Skills and Processes, Arts Appreciation, Arts in Society and Culture); two solfa systems taught side by side — Western tonic solfa and Hindi sargam — and the culture strand ends in performing your own people's dance (meke for iTaukei) in your own traditional attire (masi). Fiji's own way: the four sub-strands deepen — music elements (duration, tempo, dynamics) first, then building a full drama from a story, judging performance quality, and investigating your own community's ceremonial and religious items (sevusevu, Diwali) and performing one.
