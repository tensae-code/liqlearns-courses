# Traditional Music and What It Does — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure — README, "Units, Lessons and Sub Lessons"). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number, as before. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — What culture is
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: the learner can do what this part teaches, in their own words — culture is a society's marks — language, religion, art, dress, food, history, ceremonies: the sum of spiritual and material products, passed generation to generation (Grade 5, printed p. 85).

## Sub lesson 2 — Every society makes its music
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: the learner can do what this part teaches, in their own words — every society creates and uses its own music — to entertain, teach, keep the group's order, praise God; it carries joy, grief and desires and lives with the society (Grade 5, printed p. 85).

## Sub lesson 3 — Traditional music's uses
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: the learner can do what this part teaches, in their own words — it introduces a place's cultural events, carries views, feelings and heroism as social communication, transmits knowledge and values, creates national feeling, guards borders and plants love of country (Grade 5, printed pp. 85–86).
