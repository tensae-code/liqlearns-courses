# Traditional Music and What It Does — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Culture, in the Grade 5 definition, is —
a) a society's marks: language, religion, art, dress, food, history, ceremonies  b) only clothing  c) only food
**Answer: a) a society's marks: language, religion, art, dress, food, history, ceremonies** — that is §3.1.1's own list.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Culture is the sum of a society's —
a) spiritual and material products  b) imports only  c) laws only
**Answer: a) spiritual and material products** — spiritual and material — the book's pair.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The book names music's works as —
a) entertain, teach, keep the group's order, praise God  b) only entertain  c) only sell
**Answer: a) entertain, teach, keep the group's order, praise God** — those four works are §3.1.1's list.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Traditional music carries —
a) joy, grief and desires  b) only joy  c) nothing
**Answer: a) joy, grief and desires** — feeling is music's freight in the book.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Traditional music transmits —
a) knowledge, skill, experience, thought, religious order, ethics and cultural values  b) only melodies  c) nothing
**Answer: a) knowledge, skill, experience, thought, religious order, ethics and cultural values** — transmission is the book's third use.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** For the country, traditional music —
a) creates national feeling, guards borders psychologically, encourages heroism, plants love of country  b) does nothing  c) only entertains visitors
**Answer: a) creates national feeling, guards borders psychologically, encourages heroism, plants love of country** — the national uses close the book's list.
