# Video script — Traditional Music and What It Does

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Culture — a society's marks.
Language, religion, art.
Dress, food, history, ceremonies.
Spiritual and material — handed down.

Every society makes music.
To entertain. To teach.
To keep order. To praise God.
Joy, grief, desires — carried in sound.

It introduces the occasion.
It carries views and feelings.
It transmits knowledge and values.
It plants love of country.
