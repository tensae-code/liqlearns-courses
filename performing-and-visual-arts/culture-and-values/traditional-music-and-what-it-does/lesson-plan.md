# Lesson plan — Traditional Music and What It Does

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Culture and Values · **Lesson:** Traditional Music and What It Does
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 5 — Culture and Values (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** culture is a society's marks, and traditional music is one of its strongest: it entertains, teaches, keeps order, praises God, and carries the society's joy, grief and desires.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the culture chapter of the Grade 5 book and the music lessons the learner already knows.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved (the gate: README, "Units, Lessons and Sub Lessons (ruled 2026-11-08)"):

1. **What culture is** — culture is a society's marks — language, religion, art, dress, food, history, ceremonies: the sum of spiritual and material products, passed generation to generation (Grade 5, printed p. 85).
2. **Every society makes its music** — every society creates and uses its own music — to entertain, teach, keep the group's order, praise God; it carries joy, grief and desires and lives with the society (Grade 5, printed p. 85).
3. **Traditional music's uses** — it introduces a place's cultural events, carries views, feelings and heroism as social communication, transmits knowledge and values, creates national feeling, guards borders and plants love of country (Grade 5, printed pp. 85–86).
