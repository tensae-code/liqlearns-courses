# Video script — What the Arts Do for Society

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

What is art? A lens society looks through.
Art expresses. Art remembers. Art gathers.
Art can speak against harm — loudly, beautifully.
The issues of today are art's subjects too.
Nature is the first artwork. We copy her, and we care for her.
