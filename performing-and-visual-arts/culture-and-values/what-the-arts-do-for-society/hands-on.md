# Hands-on — What the Arts Do for Society

The lesson with your hands, your feet and your voice together, not only your eyes.

## Art for an issue, for real
Choose one current issue in your area. In groups, make the art for it — poster, song or short drama — and present it to the class as the books direct: art speaking for society, not about it.

## Nature through art walk
Walk outside with drawing tools. Choose one piece of nature — the book's way is trees, mountains, rivers and sky — draw it from life, then sing or write one line of a nature song for it. Art reading nature, twice over.
