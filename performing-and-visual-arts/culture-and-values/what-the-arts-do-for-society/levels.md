# What the Arts Do for Society — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure — README, "Units, Lessons and Sub Lessons"). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number, as before. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. The sub lessons climb inside this one lesson, from the youngest books to the oldest.

## Sub lesson 1 — What is art?
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: give Sakadeg's definition and Rumi's two faces in your own words.

## Sub lesson 2 — The role of the arts in society
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: name art's four roles from this sub lesson, with one example each.

## Sub lesson 3 — Art speaks for society — and against harm
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: state the sorting rule for traditions and name three harms the arts speak against.

## Sub lesson 4 — Art for current issues
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: name the Grade 6 issues and two of the Grade 8 community issues art works on.

## Sub lesson 5 — Nature through art
Sub lesson 5 questions from the type files. Pass mark: name nature's art forms and the two arts that answer them in this part.

## Sub lesson 6 — The arts at work — the eight plates
Sub lesson 6 questions from the type files. Pass mark: the learner can do what this part teaches, in their own words — the Grade 4 book's eight 'arts at work' plates, described one by one: society helping and eating together, the mill and cotton spinning, religious teaching, Tis Abay, the printing of money, children's feelings, the coffee ceremony and games, honouring custom (Grade 4, printed pp. 56–60).

## Sub lesson 7 — The Unit 3 practice set — blessing, visiting, expressing
Sub lesson 7 questions from the type files. ## Sub lesson 8 — Why the community needs its arts; cross-cutting issues performed (teacher guide — evidence)

## Sub lesson 12 — Arts make content easy; art, the chief tool; the issue list
Sub lesson 12 questions from the type files.
