# What the Arts Do for Society — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** ብልህነት (akilignnet) names art as ingenuity.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The arts are at work in industries and professions.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The arts speak against alcohol, drugs and AIDS in dances and songs.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Grade 8's book has art working against alcohol and drug abuse.
**Answer: True**

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** Nature songs answer what the eye has seen in nature.
**Answer: True**
