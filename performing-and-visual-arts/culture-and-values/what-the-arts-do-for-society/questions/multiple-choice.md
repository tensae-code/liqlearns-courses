# What the Arts Do for Society — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Sakadeg's definition centres art on…
  a) The skilled person practising a craft b) Expensive materials c) Museums
**Answer: a) The skilled person practising a craft** — art as the skilled person's practised craft — mastery in hands.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Heritage in pictures in the Grade 4 book is shown by…
  a) Lalibela and the festivals b) Only banknotes c) Only road signs
**Answer: a) Lalibela and the festivals** — the Lalibela churches and festivals picture the heritage.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Useful traditions are…
  a) Kept, because they benefit people and land b) Always dropped c) Only for elders
**Answer: a) Kept, because they benefit people and land** — traditions that benefit are kept; harmful ones are questioned.

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Grade 6's Art for Current Issues names…
  a) Deforestation, pollution and girls' education b) Only examination stress c) Only traffic
**Answer: a) Deforestation, pollution and girls' education** — the chapter's own three: deforestation, pollution, girls' education.

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** Drawing natural things is done…
  a) From life b) Only from memory of a photograph c) Never in this course
**Answer: a) From life** — the book's nature work is drawn from life.

**6. (Sub lesson 6)** Fig. 3.17, the economic plate, shows —
a) the mill system and cotton spinning  b) a sea port  c) a railway
**Answer: a) the mill system and cotton spinning** — the economic plate is mill and cotton.

**7. (Sub lesson 6)** Fig. 3.20 shows —
a) the printing of the country's money  b) a market fire  c) a school bell
**Answer: a) the printing of the country's money** — money printing is the plate's subject.

**8. (Sub lesson 7)** Activity 3.2 first asks learners to act out —
a) the family blessing at coffee, meals and discussion  b) a market quarrel  c) nothing
**Answer: a) the family blessing at coffee, meals and discussion** — the blessing scene is the first task.

**9. (Sub lesson 7)** The visited historic places are expressed in —
a) drawing, song/poem, theatre and dance  b) silence only  c) a single essay only
**Answer: a) drawing, song/poem, theatre and dance** — four arts carry the visit.

**10. (Sub lesson 8)** The guide's §4.2 teaches the —
a) necessity of visual and folk/craft arts within the community  b) uselessness of arts  c) price of crafts
**Answer: a) necessity of visual and folk/craft arts within the community** — necessity, not mere enjoyment, is §4.2's word.

**11. (Sub lesson 8)** The guide's §5.1 performs cross-cutting issues such as —
a) traffic, HIV and addictive drugs  b) weather only  c) spelling
**Answer: a) traffic, HIV and addictive drugs** — those topics are named in §5.1.

**12. (Sub lesson 9)** "Qaqe Wirdiwet" is named as —
a) a women's-rights theatre work  b) a love song  c) a building
**Answer: a) a women's-rights theatre work** — the play stands for women's rights.

**13. (Sub lesson 9)** The section's practicals make theatre and short films on —
a) traffic, gender and cleanliness  b) nothing  c) astronomy only
**Answer: a) traffic, gender and cleanliness** — those topics are the practicals' subjects.

**14. (Sub lesson 10)** The Traffic Light chant teaches —
a) traffic-light law  b) baking  c) weaving
**Answer: a) traffic-light law** — the chant carries the road law.

**15. (Sub lesson 10)** The Plants chant teaches that green plants are —
a) a guarantee for human life  b) unimportant  c) only decoration
**Answer: a) a guarantee for human life** — that is the chant's stated teaching.

**16. (Sub lesson 11)** The Grade 8 opener says performing arts enable the community to have —
a) a successful life and a good way of living  b) nothing in particular  c) only entertainment
**Answer: a) a successful life and a good way of living** — that is §4.1's opening claim.

**17. (Sub lesson 11)** In §5.2, art's benefit for current issues is —
a) introducing them easily and raising the community's awareness  b) hiding them  c) delaying them
**Answer: a) introducing them easily and raising the community's awareness** — easy introduction and awareness are the stated benefits.

**18. (Sub lesson 12)** In the Grade 5 account, children learning through song, melody, drawing and theatre —
a) grasp easily and remember long  b) forget at once  c) learn nothing
**Answer: a) grasp easily and remember long** — easy grasp and long memory are §5.1.1's words.

**19. (Sub lesson 12)** The Grade 7 §5.3.1 teaches letters by —
a) varied chants, in Amharic and English  b) silence  c) copying only
**Answer: a) varied chants, in Amharic and English** — chants carry the letters in the Grade 7 account.

**20. (Sub lesson 13)** Grade 4 Chapter 3 opens by asking learners to grasp the arts'…
  a) Meaning and use b) Prices and taxes c) Rules for examinations
**Answer: a) Meaning and use** — the first aim is the arts' meaning and use.

**21. (Sub lesson 13)** The chapter's history aim says the arts' history is known together with…
  a) Human civilisation b) The weather c) Foreign languages only
**Answer: a) Human civilisation** — know their history with human civilisation.

**22. (Sub lesson 14)** Heritage, in the Grade 4 book's closing exercise, is expressed in…
  a) Song, drawing and dance — all of them b) Song only c) Dance only
**Answer: a) Song, drawing and dance — all of them** — the exercise's own answer: all of them.

**23. (Sub lesson 14)** The Chapter 3 closing exercise checks the uses of…
  a) Handicraft b) Motor cars c) Telephones
**Answer: a) Handicraft** — handicraft uses are one of its named topics.
