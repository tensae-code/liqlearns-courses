# What the Arts Do for Society — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Sakadeg's definition centres art on…
  a) The skilled person practising a craft b) Expensive materials c) Museums
**Answer: a) The skilled person practising a craft** — art as the skilled person's practised craft — mastery in hands.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Heritage in pictures in the Grade 4 book is shown by…
  a) Lalibela and the festivals b) Only banknotes c) Only road signs
**Answer: a) Lalibela and the festivals** — the Lalibela churches and festivals picture the heritage.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Useful traditions are…
  a) Kept, because they benefit people and land b) Always dropped c) Only for elders
**Answer: a) Kept, because they benefit people and land** — traditions that benefit are kept; harmful ones are questioned.

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Grade 6's Art for Current Issues names…
  a) Deforestation, pollution and girls' education b) Only examination stress c) Only traffic
**Answer: a) Deforestation, pollution and girls' education** — the chapter's own three: deforestation, pollution, girls' education.

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** Drawing natural things is done…
  a) From life b) Only from memory of a photograph c) Never in this course
**Answer: a) From life** — the book's nature work is drawn from life.
