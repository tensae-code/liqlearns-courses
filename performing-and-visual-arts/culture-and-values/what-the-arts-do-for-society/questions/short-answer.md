# What the Arts Do for Society — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What are Rumi's two faces of art?
**Answer: Art in literature and art in the arts — the making and the made.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name two roles of the arts in society.
**Answer: Any two of: expressing feelings, carrying culture and heritage, gathering community at festivals, earning makers a living in industries and professions.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Art is described as what, on community life?
**Answer: A lens — expressing feeling and passing heritage.**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Name two community issues from Grade 8's list.
**Answer: Any two of: flood, erosion, land degradation, early marriage through abduction.**

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** Name two of nature's art forms in this part.
**Answer: Any two of: trees, mountains, rivers, sky.**
