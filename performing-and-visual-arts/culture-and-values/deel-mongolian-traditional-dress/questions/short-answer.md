# Deel: Mongolian Traditional Dress — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1.** Name two parts of the deel.
**Answer: Any two of: зах (collar), эмжээр (edging trim), шилбэ (sleeve/cuff part), товч (button).**

**2.** How is a tsatsag made?
**Answer: Cut a shiny plastic bag square, fold it in the middle, fringe it leaving 4 cm uncut, roll it around its core and tie it.**
