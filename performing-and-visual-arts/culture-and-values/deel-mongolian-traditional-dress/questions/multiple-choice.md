# Deel: Mongolian Traditional Dress — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** The deel studied in the lesson is…
a) a real photographed Mongolian garment, its parts named by label lines  b) a European suit  c) a school uniform from another country
**Answer: a) a real photographed Mongolian garment, its parts named by label lines**

**2.** Зах (zakh) on the deel is the…
a) collar  b) button  c) sleeve
**Answer: a) collar**

**3.** Товч (tovch) is the deel's…
a) button  b) collar  c) belt
**Answer: a) button**

**4.** A tsatsag is Mongolia's…
a) tassel ornament  b) hat  c) shoe
**Answer: a) tassel ornament**
