# Deel: Mongolian Traditional Dress

## Do it first

Start with the first part below — do it, sing it or make it before you read any explanation.

## Sub lesson 1 — Part I: Шүлгийн агуулгыг зургаар илэрхийлэх

Part I: Шүлгийн агуулгыг зургаар илэрхийлэх — the deel itself studied on a photographed real garment, its parts named by label lines: зах (collar), эмжээр (edging trim), шилбэ (sleeve/cuff part) and товч (button) — Mongolia's own dress as a named, part-by-part thing

## Sub lesson 2 — Part II: Цацаг хийх (Making a tsatsag)

Part II: Цацаг хийх (Making a tsatsag) — the tsatsag, Mongolia's tassel ornament, made from a shiny plastic bag: cut square, folded in the middle, fringed leaving 4 cm uncut, rolled around its core and tied with string or tape — children waving the blue and yellow tassels they made

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson's Mongolian way is, and show one part with your hands or your voice. Companion checks: is the named Mongolian thing in your answer?
