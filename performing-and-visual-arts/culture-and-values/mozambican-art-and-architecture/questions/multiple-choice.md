# Mozambican Art and Architecture — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The first phase of Mozambican painting is…
a) the colonial phase  b) the prehistoric rock paintings  c) murals of independence
**Answer: b) the prehistoric rock paintings** — hunting and worship scenes at named rock-art sites.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** João Craveirinha's Heroes' Square mural (1979) belongs to…
a) the rock-painting phase  b) the colonial phase  c) the independence/contemporary phase
**Answer: c) the independence/contemporary phase**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A traditional house with a circular plan has…
a) a conical roof  b) a flat cement roof  c) four waters
**Answer: a) a conical roof**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** José Forjaz is Mozambique's outstanding…
a) ceramicist  b) architect  c) comics maker
**Answer: b) architect** — designer of the BCI bank headquarters, Maputo, 2017.
