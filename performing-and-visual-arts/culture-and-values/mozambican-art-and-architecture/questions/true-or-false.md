# Mozambican Art and Architecture — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The rock paintings show hunting and worship.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Urban architecture in Mozambique is built of thatch and grass.
**Answer: False** — urban architecture uses conventional materials — block, brick and cement.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Knowledge of the traditional house is passed from generation to generation.
**Answer: True**
