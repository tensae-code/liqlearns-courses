# Mozambican Art and Architecture — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name two of Mozambique's outstanding painters.
**Answer: Any two of: Malangatana Valente Ngwenha, Elias Abdula Naguib, Noel Langa, Roberto Chichorro and João Timane.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name three local materials of the traditional house.
**Answer: Any three of: thatch, grass, mud, wood, bamboo, stakes.**
