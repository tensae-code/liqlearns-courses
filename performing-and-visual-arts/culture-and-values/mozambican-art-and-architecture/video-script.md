# Video script — Mozambican Art and Architecture

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This is Mozambique's own art.

Painting first — three times. The rock paintings of the first hunters. The paintings of the struggle. The great murals of independence.
Malangatana. Chichorro. Craveirinha's mural on Heroes' Square.

Then building. The round house with its cone roof. The square house with four waters. Local materials, knowledge handed down.
And in the city — José Forjaz.

Your country's art is heritage. Know its names.
