# Hands-on — Mozambican Art and Architecture

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Painting phases timeline
Draw three boxes. In the first, sketch a rock painting of a hunt. In the second, a scene of struggle. In the third, a big public mural. Label each with its phase.

## House forms walk
Walk and find houses. Sketch one of each form you can find — circular with conical roof, quadrangular with pyramidal roof, rectangular with one or two waters — and write the materials of each.
