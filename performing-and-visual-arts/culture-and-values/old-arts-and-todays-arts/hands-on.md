# Hands-on — Old Arts and Today's Arts

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The mouth-to-mouth chain
Learn a short song, riddle or story from an elder, by listening only. Write nothing. Teach it to a friend by voice only. You have just done what communities did for a thousand years — feel how careful you must be with what you carry.

## Old art, new keeping
Choose one old art of your area. Make its 'protection page': draw it, name it, name who still does it, and write one line on how your generation can keep it. Show it to the elder who taught you.
