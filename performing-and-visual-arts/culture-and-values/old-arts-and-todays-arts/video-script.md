# Video script — Old Arts and Today's Arts

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Whose song is this? Grandmother's.
Who taught her? **Her** grandmother.
Mouth to mouth. Heart to heart.

Those are the **old arts**.
**Unspoiled. Unrevised.** From old times — **to now**.
At weddings. At work. In joy and in grief.

Today, we also **record** them. They travel far.
But the ruling stands:
**Culture must be cared for — and passed on.**
