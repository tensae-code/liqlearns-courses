# Old Arts and Today's Arts — short answer

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does culture show, in the summary's words?
**Answer:** A society's way of life, level of growth and civilisation — food, dress, dance, traditional song.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name two long-handed-down performances the book names.
**Answer:** Any two of: handicrafts; the melodies, dances, fukera and qererito sung at weddings, farm work and communal work (debo).

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name two occasions where ancient music and artworks were used.
**Answer:** Any two of: festivals, weddings, war journeys, expressing grief and joy.
