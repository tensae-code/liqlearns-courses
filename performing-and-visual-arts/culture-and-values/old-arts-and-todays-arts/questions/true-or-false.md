# Old Arts and Today's Arts — true or false

Say if the line is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Caring for and preserving the heritages is expected of every community member.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Wedding poems and melodies are the same in all areas.
**Answer: False** — the book's closing exercise keys this false — each area's arts are its own.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Today, old arts can be recorded on cassette and CD and pass easily between generations.
**Answer: True**
