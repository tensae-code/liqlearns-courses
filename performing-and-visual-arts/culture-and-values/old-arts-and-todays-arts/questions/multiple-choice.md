# Old Arts and Today's Arts — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Grade 3's chapter opens: Ethiopia is a country where —
a) only one people lives  b) different peoples live together, each with its own food, dress and dance  c) culture has been forgotten
**Answer: b) different peoples live together, each with its own food, dress and dance**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Ancient arts are works that are —
a) new and fashionable  b) long-connected to the community, unspoiled and unrevised  c) made only in factories
**Answer: b) long-connected to the community, unspoiled and unrevised**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The old arts are used to express feeling at —
a) joy, grief, work, social life and daily activities  b) only funerals  c) only school
**Answer: a) joy, grief, work, social life and daily activities**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Old arts were passed —
a) only in books  b) orally, from community to community  c) by aeroplane
**Answer: b) orally, from community to community**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** The book's ruling: culture must be —
a) sold and forgotten  b) cared for and protected, to pass generation to generation  c) kept secret from children
**Answer: b) cared for and protected, to pass generation to generation**
