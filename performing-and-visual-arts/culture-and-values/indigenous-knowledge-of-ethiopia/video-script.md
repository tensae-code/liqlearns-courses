# Video script — Indigenous Knowledge of Ethiopia

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Indigenous knowledge.
Knowledge a people grows itself.
In its own place. Over generations.
Held in hands. Handed down by doing.

Nine examples. One store.
Bale — a trick that guards the crops.
Konso — steps that hold the hillside.
Horn worked by skilled hands.
Aksum, Lalibela, Gondar — knowledge in stone.

A library in hands.
It lasts while it is practised.
Ask. Watch. Practise.
Every learner a librarian.
