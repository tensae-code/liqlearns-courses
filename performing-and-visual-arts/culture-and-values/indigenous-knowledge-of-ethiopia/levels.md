# Indigenous Knowledge of Ethiopia — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure — README, "Units, Lessons and Sub Lessons"). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number, as before. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — What indigenous knowledge is
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: the learner can do what this part teaches, in their own words — Ethiopia's many communities each have their own culture, language, belief, dress and food — and their own knowledge, grown inside that life (Grade 7, printed pp. 110–112).

## Sub lesson 2 — The named store — nine examples
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: the learner can do what this part teaches, in their own words — the book names nine examples: among them the Bale farmers' porcupine trick, Konso terracing, horn craft, and the building knowledge of Aksum, Lalibela and Gondar (Grade 7, printed pp. 111–113).

## Sub lesson 3 — Keeping the store
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: the learner can do what this part teaches, in their own words — indigenous knowledge lives only while it is practised and handed on — valuing it is part of keeping it (Grade 7, §3.6).
