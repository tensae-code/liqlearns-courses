The Grade 7 placement names nine examples of indigenous knowledge; the audit and placement summaries name the anchor examples taught here (Bale, Konso, horn craft, and the Aksum/Lalibela/Gondar building knowledge). This lesson names those anchors as 'among the nine' and does not invent the remaining names.
