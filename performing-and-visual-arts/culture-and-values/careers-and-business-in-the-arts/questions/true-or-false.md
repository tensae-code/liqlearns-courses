# Careers and Business in the Arts — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Photography and cinema are arts professions in the programme.
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Cultural entrepreneurship is the 7e year's theme.
**Answer: False — it is the 9e year's theme; 7e is « Éveil artistique et environnement ».**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A cultural agenda is drawn up as part of this teaching.
**Answer: True.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Arts professions are only for other countries.
**Answer: False — this is Haiti's own EEA programme teaching.**
