# Careers and Business in the Arts — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The 9e year's theme is…
a) cultural entrepreneurship and the arts professions  b) only farming  c) only sport
**Answer: a) cultural entrepreneurship and the arts professions** — « Entrepreneuriat culturel et métiers des arts ».

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which is one of the métiers de l'art named?
a) ironwork  b) banking  c) driving
**Answer: a) ironwork** — ironwork — beside sculpture, painting, photography, cinema and more.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Competency 8 teaches about…
a) the cultural industries and institutions  b) only scales  c) only drawing
**Answer: a) the cultural industries and institutions** — cultural entrepreneurship and the cultural industries.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Drawing up a cultural agenda is part of…
a) managing cultural work  b) singing practice  c) clay modelling
**Answer: a) managing cultural work** — the 9e cultural-entrepreneurship teaching.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Managing artistic and cultural projects means…
a) organising the work so art reaches its public  b) hiding the art  c) ending the art
**Answer: a) organising the work so art reaches its public** — organising artistic and cultural projects.
