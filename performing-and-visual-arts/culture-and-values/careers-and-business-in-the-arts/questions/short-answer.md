# Careers and Business in the Arts — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name four arts professions from the programme's list.
**Answer: Sculpture, painting, screen printing, graphics, animation, illustration, design, architecture, ironwork, photography, cinema — any four.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the two practical tasks the 9e teaching sets.
**Answer: Drawing up a cultural agenda, and managing artistic and cultural projects.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** What are the cultural institutions' place in this lesson?
**Answer: They are part of the cultural industries the learner comes to know — the bodies that carry the arts.**
