# Careers and Business in the Arts

## Do it first

Quick start: from the title alone, guess what this lesson's work is. Then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — The cultural industries and the arts professions

The EEA programme's 9e year — theme « Entrepreneuriat culturel et métiers des arts » — and its **Competency 8** teach **cultural entrepreneurship and the cultural industries**: the **cultural institutions**, and the **arts professions (métiers de l'art)** — **sculpture, painting, screen printing, graphics, animation, illustration, design, architecture, ironwork, photography, cinema**.

## Sub lesson 2 — A cultural agenda and managing projects

The same teaching goes practical: **drawing up a cultural agenda**, and **managing artistic and cultural projects** — the organising work that lets art reach its public.
