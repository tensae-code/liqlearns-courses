# Lesson plan — Looking At Buildings and Places

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Culture and Values · **Lesson:** Looking At Buildings and Places
**Place in the curriculum:** Culture and Values (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Looking At Buildings and Places — Syria's own teaching of this topic, kept as named parts: Africa's distinct nature and its own architecture — thatch, mud and painted geometric walls — discussing the African style's special character and its spontaneous, primitive decoration, and creating a building that reflects it
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the course's earlier lessons in its title; recommended first, never forced.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of ..." in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **"The Human Architectural Heritage: African Architecture"** — Africa's distinct nature and its own architecture — thatch, mud and painted geometric walls — discussing the African style's special character and its spontaneous, primitive decoration, and creating a building that reflects it
2. **Lesson 10 "Folk and Naive Art in Rural Architecture"** — describing the houses in the pictures; drawing a home inspired by the houses of Nubia, decorating and colouring it your way

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
