The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

This is Syria's own lesson.

**"The Human Architectural Heritage: African Architecture".** Africa's distinct nature and its own architecture — thatch, mud and painted geometric walls — discussing the African style's special character and its spontaneous, primitive decoration, and creating a building that reflects it.

**Lesson 10 "Folk and Naive Art in Rural Architecture".** describing the houses in the pictures; drawing a home inspired by the houses of Nubia, decorating and colouring it your way.

That is Looking At Buildings and Places — Syria's way, kept by name.
