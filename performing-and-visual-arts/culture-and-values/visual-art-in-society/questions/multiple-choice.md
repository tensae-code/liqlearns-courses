# Visual Art in Society — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Culturally, visual art in society is —
a) the community's identity made visible in public  b) invisible by nature  c) only for galleries abroad
**Answer: a) the community's identity made visible in public** — the painted wall is the guide's own picture slot.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The guide's picture for this part is —
a) visual art in public life — a painted wall in a town  b) a sealed vault  c) an empty field
**Answer: a) visual art in public life — a painted wall in a town** — public visibility is the cultural role.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Historically, visual art serves society as —
a) its record — how later ages know it  b) a passing fashion only  c) a secret code
**Answer: a) its record — how later ages know it** — remaining works are the record.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The two standpoints of §§3.4–3.4.2 are —
a) cultural and historical  b) near and far  c) loud and soft
**Answer: a) cultural and historical** — culture and history structure the guide's treatment.
