# National Art and Identity

## Do it first

Name one thing that belongs in this lesson — National Art and Identity — its forms, history and cultural meaning, taught as one topic lesson. Say what it is in your own words. If you can name one, you are already inside the lesson.

## Sub lesson 1 — Specificul artei naționale

Unit IV §21, Specificul artei naționale — what makes art national: the semantic aspect — a culture's mythology, history, language, religion, traditions, climate and geography read inside the work (Romanescu's Moldovan landscape colour, Mudrea's village architecture, Grecu's hospitality, Negură's folklore tapestry, Plămădeală's Stephen the Great in Chișinău) — and the territorial aspect, the artists living in the country, whose subjects may be traditional or cosmopolitan.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson holds, part by part, in your own words. Companion checks: did every part get named, with its Moldovan name kept where the book gives one?

## Do It Yourself

1. Choose one named part of this lesson and find out one more true thing about it — from a person who knows it, or from the book your part came from.
2. Make or perform a small piece in the way of that part: a rhythm, a step, a drawing, a shaped piece of clay.
3. Write one sentence: what does this part show about Moldova's own way of teaching its arts?
