# Video script — National Art and Identity

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This is Moldova's own lesson.

**Specificul artei naționale.** Unit IV §21, Specificul artei naționale — what makes art national: the semantic aspect — a culture's mythology, history, language, religion, traditions, climate and geography read inside the work (Romanescu's Moldovan landscape colour, Mudrea's village architecture, Grecu's hospitality, Negură's folklore tapestry, Plămădeală's Stephen the Great in Chișinău) — and the territorial aspect, the artists living in the country, whose subjects may be traditional or cosmopolitan.

That is National Art and Identity — Moldova's way, kept by name.
