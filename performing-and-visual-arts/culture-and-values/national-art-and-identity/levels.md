## Sub lesson 1 — Specificul artei naționale
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Unit IV §21, Specificul artei naționale — what makes art national: the semantic aspect — a culture's mythology, history, language, religion, traditions, climate and geography read inside the work (Romanescu's Moldovan landscape colour, Mudrea's village architecture, Grecu's hospitality, Negură's folklore tapestry, Plămădeală's Stephen the Great in Chișinău) — and the territorial aspect, the artists living in the country, whose subjects may be traditional or cosmopolitan.
