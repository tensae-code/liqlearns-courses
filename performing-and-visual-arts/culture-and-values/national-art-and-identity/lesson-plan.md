# Lesson plan — National Art and Identity

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Culture and Values · **Lesson:** National Art and Identity
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 5 — Culture and Values (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** National Art and Identity — its forms, history and cultural meaning, taught as one topic lesson.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the course's earlier lessons in its title; recommended first, never forced.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of ..." in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Specificul artei naționale** — Unit IV §21, Specificul artei naționale — what makes art national: the semantic aspect — a culture's mythology, history, language, religion, traditions, climate and geography read inside the work (Romanescu's Moldovan landscape colour, Mudrea's village architecture, Grecu's hospitality, Negură's folklore tapestry, Plămădeală's Stephen the Great in Chișinău) — and the territorial aspect, the artists living in the country, whose subjects may be traditional or cosmopolitan.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks Moldova's arts are copies of other countries' → they are the country's own: named forms, kept in the country's own books, taught in the country's own way.
