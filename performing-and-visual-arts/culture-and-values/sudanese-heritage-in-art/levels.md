# Sudanese Heritage in Art — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — Drawing Lesson 4 "Folk Heritage"
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: say what the book teaches here — Sudan's folk heritage defined as the tools and instruments of daily life, work and celebration — and drawn: the Jirtig wedding henna-day set, the decorated tray (siniyyat al-jirtig) with al-rab'a, al-huq, the prayer beads, the incense burners and al-farka, drawn with the utensil method and coloured in their bright natural colours; the tanbur, the frame drums (dufuf) and the daluka named among the heritage instruments

## Sub lesson 2 — Drawing & Colouring Topic "Tourism in Sudan"
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: say what the book teaches here — Sudan's archaeological and tourist sites as subjects to draw and colour, region by region — Jebel Marra's volcanic peaks, waterfalls and forests in Darfur; Suakin's coral-stone houses with their ornaments, balconies and five-gated wall, and Kassala under its mountains in the east; Kerma and its great mud-brick Deffufa, Meroe's 861 pyramids at Bajrawiya with Naqa and Musawwarat, and old Halfa in the north; and Khartoum at the meeting of the two Niles with the National Museum

## Sub lesson 3 — Design & Ornament Topic "Colours in Sudanese Ornament"
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: say what the book teaches here — ornament as the decoration of things, places and tools in a people's visual language — in Sudan on utensils and leather, cotton and palm-frond work — and Sudan's heritage colour system, the integrated set of red, green, black, gold, yellow, beige, orange and white (with violet), whose colours carry meanings in Sudanese culture; heritage ornaments of overlapping triangles coloured in degrees of red and green or violet and yellow with black, and repeated designs built from the plant element, the strongest ornament element of the civilisations
