# Sudanese Heritage in Art — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The tanbur and daluka are named among Sudan's heritage instruments.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Heritage colours in Sudan carry no meanings.
**Answer: False** — The colours carry meanings in Sudanese culture.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Khartoum stands at the meeting of the two Niles.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** The Jirtig is a harvest dance.
**Answer: False** — The Jirtig is the wedding henna-day set and its decorated tray.
