# Sudanese Heritage in Art — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Jirtig set is drawn on…
a) the decorated tray (siniyyat al-jirtig)  b) a blackboard  c) a boat
**Answer: a) the decorated tray (siniyyat al-jirtig)** — the tray with al-rab'a, al-huq, prayer beads, incense burners and al-farka.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Meroe's pyramids stand at…
a) Bajrawiya  b) Suakin  c) Kassala
**Answer: a) Bajrawiya** — Meroe's 861 pyramids at Bajrawiya, with Naqa and Musawwarat.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Sudan's heritage colour system includes…
a) red, green, black and gold  b) only blue  c) only white
**Answer: a) red, green, black and gold** — red, green, black, gold, yellow, beige, orange and white (with violet).

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Suakin's houses are built of…
a) coral stone  b) glass  c) iron
**Answer: a) coral stone** — coral-stone houses with ornaments, balconies and a five-gated wall.
