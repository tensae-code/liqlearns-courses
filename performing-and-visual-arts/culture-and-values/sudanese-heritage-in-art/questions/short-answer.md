# Sudanese Heritage in Art — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is folk heritage, as the Grade 5 book defines it?
**Answer: The tools and instruments of daily life, work and celebration.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the great mud-brick building of Kerma.
**Answer: The Deffufa.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which ornament element is called the strongest of the civilisations?
**Answer: The plant element.**
