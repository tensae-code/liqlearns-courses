# Art as Protest — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** American social realism includes Dorothea Lange's photography.
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Realism avoided ordinary people's lives.
**Answer: False — it painted the working class.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Brasília is an example of protesting architecture.
**Answer: True.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Rodin is named a revolutionary of painting.
**Answer: False — of sculpture.**
