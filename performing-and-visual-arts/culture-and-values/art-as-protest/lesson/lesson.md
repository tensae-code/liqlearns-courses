## Sub lesson 1 — Realism that protests

Andorra's history-of-art programme asks what art can **protest**. **19th-century pictorial realism** turned to the **working class** — **Daumier, Millet and Courbet** painted ordinary working life as a claim for dignity. **American social realism** continued it — **Hopper, Grant Wood, Dorothea Lange's photography and Lucien Freud**.

## Sub lesson 2 — Sculpture revolutionaries and a protesting city

Protest also remade **sculpture** — **Rodin and Giacometti** are named **revolutionaries of sculpture**, breaking its old forms. And protest can be **built**: **Brasília**, planned by **Lúcio Costa** and **Oscar Niemeyer**, is urbanism built to create a **social, protesting architecture** — a capital city itself as the protest.
