# Hands-on — Moroccos Cities Present Themselves

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## My place speaks
Write your town or neighbourhood's short speech — its name, where it is, and the thing it is proudest of. Present it to a partner, who then presents theirs.

## One closing
As a class, finish your presentations with one shared song or dance everyone joins — the way the cities close together.
