# Lesson plan — Moroccos Cities Present Themselves

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Culture and Values · **Lesson:** Moroccos Cities Present Themselves
**Place in the curriculum:** Culture and Values (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Morocco's cities presenting themselves — children in each city's regional dress speak for Oujda, Tangier, Fez, Marrakech, Agadir and the Souss, and the Sahara cities Laayoune and Dakhla — closing by dancing and singing the homeland song.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this course in the same title.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The cities speak** — each city presented by a child in its regional dress — Oujda, Tangier, Fez, Marrakech, Agadir and the Souss, Laayoune and Dakhla — and what each city is known as.
2. **Closing together** — the cast closing by dancing and singing the homeland song — a country presented by its own children.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of this title. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. name Morocco's cities as the play presents them and say what at least three of them are known for
2. say how the play closes — the cast dancing and singing the homeland song together

## What you need
Your voice, your hands and a friend to work with.
