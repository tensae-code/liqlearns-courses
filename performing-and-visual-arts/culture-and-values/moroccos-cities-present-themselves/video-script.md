# Video script — Moroccos Cities Present Themselves

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Come and meet the cities of Morocco!

I am Oujda — gateway of the east!
I am Tangier — bride of the north!
I am Fez — city of knowledge, long ago a capital!
I am Marrakech — the Red City!
And Agadir, and Laayoune, and Dakhla in the Sahara!

Each city speaks — in its own dress.
Then — all together — they dance and sing for their country!
