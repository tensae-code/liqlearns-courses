# Moroccos Cities Present Themselves — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In the play, the cities are presented by…
a) children in each city's regional dress  b) maps only  c) photographs
**Answer: a) children in each city's regional dress.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Tangier is called…
a) the Red City  b) the bride of the north  c) the gateway of the east
**Answer: b) the bride of the north.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The ancient capital of knowledge is…
a) Fez  b) Agadir  c) Oujda
**Answer: a) Fez.**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Marrakech is known for…
a) Saidia's beaches  b) Jamaa el-Fna and the Koutoubia  c) the strait
**Answer: b) Jamaa el-Fna and the Koutoubia.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The play closes with the cast…
a) leaving silently  b) dancing and singing the homeland song  c) reading a list
**Answer: b) dancing and singing the homeland song.**
