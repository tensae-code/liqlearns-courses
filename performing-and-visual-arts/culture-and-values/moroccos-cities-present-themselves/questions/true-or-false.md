# Moroccos Cities Present Themselves — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Fez is presented as an ancient capital of knowledge.
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Tangier is called the Red City.
**Answer: False** — Marrakech is the Red City; Tangier is the bride of the north.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Each city speaks alone and the play ends without a shared closing.
**Answer: False** — the cast closes together, dancing and singing the homeland song.**
