# Moroccos Cities Present Themselves

## Do it first

If your own town could speak, what would it say first? In Morocco's play, the cities speak for themselves.

## Sub lesson 1 — The cities speak

Morocco's Grade 3 guide closes its year with **"Our Beautiful Cities" (مدننا الجميلة)**: the cities present themselves through **children in each city's regional dress** (plot only):

- **Oujda** — gateway of the east, with Saidia's beaches.
- **Tangier** — bride of the north, on the strait.
- **Fez** — ancient capital of knowledge.
- **Marrakech** — the Red City of Jamaa el-Fna and the Koutoubia.
- **Agadir and the Souss**.
- **The Sahara cities — Laayoune and Dakhla.**

## Sub lesson 2 — Closing together

The cast closes by **dancing and singing the homeland song** together — the cities, who spoke one by one, end as one country.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion three cities from the play and what each is known as. Companion checks: Oujda the gateway of the east? Tangier the bride of the north? Fez the capital of knowledge? Marrakech the Red City?

## Do It Yourself

1. Choose a city or town you know. Write two sentences it would say about itself.
2. Present your place to the class in one minute, dressed or holding a sign of the place.
3. Write one sentence: why does the play end with everyone singing together?
