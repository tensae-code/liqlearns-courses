# Performing Arts — Three Benefits — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The aesthetic benefit's named example is —
a) poems with beautiful melodies expressing hidden feeling  b) silent reading only  c) marching drills
**Answer: a) poems with beautiful melodies expressing hidden feeling** — that is §4.1.1's own example.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The book says performing art serves —
a) belief, governing skills, love, beauty and country  b) only ticket sales  c) nothing beyond the stage
**Answer: a) belief, governing skills, love, beauty and country** — those five are the benefit's reach.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Performing arts are created from —
a) the community's experience and knowledge  b) foreign scripts only  c) nothing in particular
**Answer: a) the community's experience and knowledge** — creation from the community is §4.1.2's ground.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The cultural benefit transmits —
a) identity and patriotism to the next generation  b) only dance steps  c) prices
**Answer: a) identity and patriotism to the next generation** — transmission is the benefit's work.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** The three benefits in the book's order are —
a) aesthetic, cultural (national), psychological  b) economic, military, legal  c) visual, written, spoken
**Answer: a) aesthetic, cultural (national), psychological** — that is Chapter 4's own order.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** The benefits opener calls performing arts branches enabling —
a) a successful life and a good way of living for the community  b) only individual fame  c) nothing
**Answer: a) a successful life and a good way of living for the community** — the opener frames the whole set.
