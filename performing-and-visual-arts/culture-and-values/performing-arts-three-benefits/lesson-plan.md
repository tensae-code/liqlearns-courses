# Lesson plan — Performing Arts — Three Benefits

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Culture and Values · **Lesson:** Performing Arts — Three Benefits
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 5 — Culture and Values (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the Grade 8 book sorts performing art's benefits to society into three: the aesthetic, the cultural (national) and the psychological.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the Grade 8 book's Chapter 4 opening — the learner meets the full benefits set here, one benefit per part.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved (the gate: README, "Units, Lessons and Sub Lessons (ruled 2026-11-08)"):

1. **The aesthetic benefit** — poems with beautiful melodies express hidden feeling; performing art serves belief, governing skill, love, beauty and country (Grade 8, printed p. 105).
2. **The cultural (national) benefit** — performing arts are created from the community's experience and knowledge and speak the community's own voice — carrying its identity and patriotism (Grade 8, printed pp. 105–106).
3. **The three benefits together** — the opener and the set: aesthetic, cultural (national) and psychological — performing art enabling the community's successful life and good way of living (Grade 8, printed pp. 105–107).

4. **The Grade 8 chapter opens — aesthetic usefulness (chapter opening — Grade 8)** — the Grade 8 book's Chapter 4 opener and aims: grasp performing art's benefit; compare performing and visual arts' function; identify monuments' and handcraft's making materials and the messages they transmit; appreciate artworks (Grade 8, printed p. 104).

5. **The Grade 8 chapter closes — the three benefits and the two families (chapter closing — Grade 8)** — the Grade 8 book's Chapter 4 summary, its ✓/✗ checklist (7 items) and its closing exercises of explanation questions — the printed numbering skips item 6, as the placements record (Grade 8, printed pp. 117–119).

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say the aesthetic benefit and its example.
2. Say the cultural benefit and why only a community's own performing art can give it.
3. Name the three benefits in the book's order.
