# Video script — The Macedonian Wedding Cycle

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This is North Macedonia's own lesson.

**the Macedonian wedding customs.** the Macedonian wedding customs: kneading the little bread for the couple's luck, decking the houses, pinning ornaments on the wedding guests, blocking the bride's door until the groom pays, the bride seeing the groom through the ring ("Through the ring I see you, into your heart I enter"), and the bride's too-large shoes filled with money.

**the Galičnik wedding: five days of ceremony at Petrovden, kept under UNESCO protection.** the Galičnik wedding: five days of ceremony at Petrovden, kept under UNESCO protection — inviting the dead relatives, shaving the groom, fetching the bride on horseback behind the flag-bearer, the bride filling vessels at the fountain and beginning the bridal dance, in the famed Galičnik costume.

**the wedding traditions of the Muslim and Albanian peoples.** the wedding traditions of the Muslim and Albanian peoples: the bride-painting of Dolna Reka, the bajraktari processions of Skopje, the asking for the bride (sweet or bitter coffee giving the answer), the miraz, and the Albanian henna night (kana gece) when the bride leaves her handprints on the wall.

**Turkish, Bosniak, Vlach and Serbian wedding customs.** Turkish, Bosniak, Vlach and Serbian wedding customs: the Turkish betrothal bundle and hamam; the Bosniak three-to-seven-day wedding; the Vlach sewing of the wedding flag and flour-sowing songs; the Serbian buklija invitation, shooting the apple from the gate, buying the bride, throwing the bouquet and the sieve of grain onto the roof.

**the wedding songs of every people: a song appointed for each moment of the rite.** the wedding songs of every people: a song appointed for each moment of the rite — kneading the pogacha, shaving the groom, decorating the flag, fetching the bride, her farewell — in the Macedonian wedding, and the ordered song-groups of the Albanian, Turkish, Romani, Bosniak, Serbian and Vlach weddings.

That is The Macedonian Wedding Cycle — North Macedonia's way, kept by name.
