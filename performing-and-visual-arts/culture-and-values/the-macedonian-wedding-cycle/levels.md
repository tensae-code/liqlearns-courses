# The Macedonian Wedding Cycle — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — the Macedonian wedding customs
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: the Macedonian wedding customs: kneading the little bread for the couple's luck, decking the houses, pinning ornaments on the wedding guests, blocking the bride's door until the groom pays, the bride seeing the groom through the ring ("Through the ring I see you, into your heart I enter"), and the bride's too-large shoes filled with money.

## Sub lesson 2 — the Galičnik wedding: five days of ceremony at Petrovden, kept under UNESCO protection
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: the Galičnik wedding: five days of ceremony at Petrovden, kept under UNESCO protection — inviting the dead relatives, shaving the groom, fetching the bride on horseback behind the flag-bearer, the bride filling vessels at the fountain and beginning the bridal dance, in the famed Galičnik costume.

## Sub lesson 3 — the wedding traditions of the Muslim and Albanian peoples
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: the wedding traditions of the Muslim and Albanian peoples: the bride-painting of Dolna Reka, the bajraktari processions of Skopje, the asking for the bride (sweet or bitter coffee giving the answer), the miraz, and the Albanian henna night (kana gece) when the bride leaves her handprints on the wall.

## Sub lesson 4 — Turkish, Bosniak, Vlach and Serbian wedding customs
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Turkish, Bosniak, Vlach and Serbian wedding customs: the Turkish betrothal bundle and hamam; the Bosniak three-to-seven-day wedding; the Vlach sewing of the wedding flag and flour-sowing songs; the Serbian buklija invitation, shooting the apple from the gate, buying the bride, throwing the bouquet and the sieve of grain onto the roof.

## Sub lesson 5 — the wedding songs of every people: a song appointed for each moment of the rite
Sub lesson 5 questions from the type files. Pass mark: the wedding songs of every people: a song appointed for each moment of the rite — kneading the pogacha, shaving the groom, decorating the flag, fetching the bride, her farewell — in the Macedonian wedding, and the ordered song-groups of the Albanian, Turkish, Romani, Bosniak, Serbian and Vlach weddings.
