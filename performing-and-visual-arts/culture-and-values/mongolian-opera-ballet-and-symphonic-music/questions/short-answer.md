# Mongolian Opera, Ballet and Symphonic Music — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1.** Name Mongolia's own opera in this lesson.
**Answer: Luvsansharav's Shivee Khiagt — Onol's aria is heard.**

**2.** Name two Mongolian ballets from the book.
**Answer: Any two of: Choidog's "Shariljin dundakh tsetseg", Khangal's "Erdensiin uuls", Chuluun's "Uran Khas".**
