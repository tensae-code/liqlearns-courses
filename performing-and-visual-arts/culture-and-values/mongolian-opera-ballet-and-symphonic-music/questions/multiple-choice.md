# Mongolian Opera, Ballet and Symphonic Music — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Luvsansharav's opera heard in the lesson is…
a) Shivee Khiagt  b) Aida  c) The Nutcracker
**Answer: a) Shivee Khiagt**

**2.** Mordorj's symphony is called…
a) "My Homeland" (Миний эх орон)  b) the Unfinished  c) the Surprise
**Answer: a) "My Homeland" (Миний эх орон)**

**3.** Sharav's symphony in the lesson is his…
a) Symphony No. 2  b) Symphony No. 40  c) Symphony No. 5
**Answer: a) Symphony No. 2**

**4.** "Uran Khas" is a ballet by…
a) J. Chuluun  b) Tchaikovsky  c) Prokofiev
**Answer: a) J. Chuluun**
