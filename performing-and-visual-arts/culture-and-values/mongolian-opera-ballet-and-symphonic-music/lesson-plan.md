# Lesson plan — Mongolian Opera, Ballet and Symphonic Music

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Culture and Values · **Lesson:** Mongolian Opera, Ballet and Symphonic Music
**Place in the curriculum:** Culture and Values (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Mongolia's own great stage and orchestral music — Luvsansharav's opera, Mordorj's and Sharav's symphonies, and the ballets of Choidog, Khangal and Chuluun.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the learner's earlier art and music lessons. Recommended, never forced.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**.
