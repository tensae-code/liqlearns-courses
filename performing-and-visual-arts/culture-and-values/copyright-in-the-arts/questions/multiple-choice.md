# Copyright in the Arts — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Copyright protects…
a) the author of a work  b) only the buyer  c) only museums
**Answer: a) the author of a work**.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** When you report on a work you appreciated, you should…
a) cite sources and links adequately  b) copy without naming  c) hide the author
**Answer: a) cite sources and links adequately**.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Authors' rights regulate…
a) the protection and use of artistic works  b) the price of paint  c) school timetables
**Answer: a) the protection and use of artistic works**.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A work of yours can be…
a) registered  b) never protected  c) protected only if sold
**Answer: a) registered**.

**5. (Sub lesson 1)** Derecho de autor is learned in the book as part of…
a) appreciating works live or online  b) skipping reports  c) copying classmates
**Answer: a) appreciating works live or online**.
