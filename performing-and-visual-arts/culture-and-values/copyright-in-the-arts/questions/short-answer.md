# Copyright in the Arts — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name one thing you must do when you use another artist's work in a report.
**Answer: Cite its source (and link) adequately.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Whose works do authors' rights protect — only others', or yours too?
**Answer: Both — my own works and others'.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Name two kinds of artistic work the rights cover.
**Answer: Any two of: visual, digital, technological and instrumental works.**
