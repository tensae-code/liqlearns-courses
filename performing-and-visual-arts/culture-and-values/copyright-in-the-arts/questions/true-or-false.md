# Copyright in the Arts — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Copyright belongs to the person who created the work.
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** You may present another artist's work as your own if you found it online.
**Answer: False.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Laws regulate how artistic works may be used.
**Answer: True.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Registration is one way a work's authorship is recorded.
**Answer: True.**
