# Hands-on — Copyright in the Arts

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Cite it properly
Take a work you appreciated — a painting, a song, a video. Write its title, its author and its source or link, the way the book's reports do.

## Register your own work
Choose a work you made. Write its title, your name as author, and the date — the first page of its registration record.
