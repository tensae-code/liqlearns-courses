# Copyright in the Arts

## Do it first

Quick start: A painting on a screen. A name under it. That name matters. Then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — Copyright in the Dominican Republic

**Copyright** — *derecho de autor* — is the right that protects a work and the person who made it. The Grade 11 book teaches it inside the appreciation of works: as you programme the appreciation of works **live or online**, you learn to **cite sources and links adequately** in your reports, so every work you use is named with its author.

## Sub lesson 2 — Authors' rights in the arts

**Authors' rights in the arts** are the **laws that regulate the protection and use of artistic works** — **your own and others'**. The book explores them across **visual, digital, technological and instrumental** manifestations, and how works are **registered**, so an artist's working law is known before a work is used.
