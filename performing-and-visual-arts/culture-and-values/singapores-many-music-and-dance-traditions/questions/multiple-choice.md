# Singapore's Many Music and Dance Traditions — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Goal 3 asks children to learn to…
a) recognise the music and dances of Singapore's diverse heritage  b) play one instrument  c) write songs
**Answer: a) recognise the music and dances of Singapore's diverse heritage**.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which of these is named in the guide's list?
a) dikir barat  b) flamenco  c) tango
**Answer: a) dikir barat** — beside bhangra, Chinese opera, joget and lion dance.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Lion dance is named as…
a) one of Singapore's heritage dances  b) a wider-world dance only  c) a game
**Answer: a) one of Singapore's heritage dances**.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Singapore's traditions are learned beside…
a) music and dance from the wider world  b) nothing else  c) sports
**Answer: a) music and dance from the wider world**.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Bhangra, joget and Chinese opera are…
a) named traditions of Singapore's diverse heritage  b) the same tradition  c) not music or dance
**Answer: a) named traditions of Singapore's diverse heritage**.
