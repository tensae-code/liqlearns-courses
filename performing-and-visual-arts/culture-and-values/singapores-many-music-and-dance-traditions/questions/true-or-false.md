# Singapore's Many Music and Dance Traditions — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Singapore's heritage holds many peoples' music and dances.
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The guide names only one Singapore tradition.
**Answer: False** — it names bhangra, Chinese opera, dikir barat, joget and lion dance.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Children learn the wider world's music and dance beside Singapore's own.
**Answer: True.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Joget is a kind of painting.
**Answer: False** — joget is a dance tradition.
