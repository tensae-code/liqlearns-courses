# Singapore's Many Music and Dance Traditions — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name three of the traditions the guide names.
**Answer: Bhangra, Chinese opera and dikir barat (joget and lion dance are also named).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What does Goal 3 ask children to do with these traditions?
**Answer: Learn to recognise them.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What stands beside Singapore's traditions in Goal 3?
**Answer: Music and dance from the wider world.**
