# Singapore's Many Music and Dance Traditions

## Do it first

Quick start: from the title alone, guess what this lesson's work is. Then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — The heritage named, one by one

Goal 3 of the guide sets the aim plainly: **children learn to recognise the music and dances of Singapore's diverse heritage**. The named traditions are **bhangra, Chinese opera, dikir barat, joget and lion dance** — each people's art, known by its own name and sound.

## Sub lesson 2 — Beside the wider world

Singapore's traditions are learned **beside music and dance from the wider world** — the child's own country's many heritages first, and the world's arts standing next to them, each recognised for itself.
