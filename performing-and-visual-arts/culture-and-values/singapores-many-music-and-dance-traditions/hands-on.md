# Hands-on — Singapore's Many Music and Dance Traditions

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Name that tradition
Listen to or watch short clips of the five named traditions. After each, say its name aloud together — bhangra, Chinese opera, dikir barat, joget, lion dance.

## My heritage, our heritage
Each child names one music or dance from their own family's heritage and one from another Singapore heritage they have learned to recognise.
