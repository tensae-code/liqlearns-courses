# Lesson plan — Singapore's Many Music and Dance Traditions

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Culture and Values · **Lesson:** Singapore's Many Music and Dance Traditions
**Place in the curriculum:** Culture and Values (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** learning to recognise the music and dances of Singapore's diverse heritage — bhangra, Chinese opera, dikir barat, joget and lion dance — beside music and dance from the wider world.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this course in the same title.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks Singapore has one traditional music → Singapore holds many heritages: Malay, Chinese, Indian and more, and the guide names their arts one by one so each is recognised.
- Mixes the traditions up → learn each by its own name and its own sound: lion dance is not dikir barat, and joget is not bhangra.
