# Video script — Singapore's Many Music and Dance Traditions

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

One country — many musics!

Bhangra! Chinese opera! Dikir barat! Joget! Lion dance!

Each has its own name, its own sound, its own people.

Learn to recognise them — Singapore's heritage, beside the music of the whole world.
