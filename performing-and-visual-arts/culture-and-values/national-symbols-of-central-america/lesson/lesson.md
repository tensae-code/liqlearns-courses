# National Symbols of Central America

## Do it first

Think of your own country's flag. It is cloth — but it is not *only* cloth. It stands for something. That standing-for is this lesson, widened to a whole region.

## Sub lesson 1 — The symbols country by country

Honduras's Grade 8 book opens its identity block with **Símbolos nacionales de Centro América — national symbols of Central America**: the **national symbols of the Central American countries**, taught country by country — each country with its own symbols.

## Sub lesson 2 — What the symbols stand for

A symbol is not a decoration. The sequence teaches the symbols together with **what they stand for**: each symbol carries its country's meaning — what the country honours, remembers and holds itself to be. To know a symbol is to know both its face and its meaning.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion: what does the Grade 8 book teach about Central America's symbols? Companion checks: the symbols of the countries, one by one? What each symbol stands for?

## Do It Yourself

1. Name one national symbol of your own country and write what it stands for.
2. Choose another Central American country and write one of its national symbols, as the book teaches it.
3. Write one sentence: why do countries keep national symbols?
