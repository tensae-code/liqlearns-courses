# National Symbols of Central America — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Every Central American country has its own national symbols.
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A national symbol is only a decoration and stands for nothing.
**Answer: False.** — it stands for its country and what it honours.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The book teaches the symbols country by country.
**Answer: True.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Only Honduras has national symbols in this lesson.
**Answer: False.** — the symbols of the Central American countries are taught.
