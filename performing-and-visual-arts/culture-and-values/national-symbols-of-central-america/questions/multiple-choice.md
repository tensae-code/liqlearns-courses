# National Symbols of Central America — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Grade 8 book teaches the national symbols of…
a) one country only  b) the Central American countries  c) another continent
**Answer: b) the Central American countries**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Each Central American country has…
a) no symbols  b) its own national symbols  c) the same symbol as all others
**Answer: b) its own national symbols** — taught country by country.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A national symbol…
a) stands for its country and what it honours  b) means nothing  c) is only decoration
**Answer: a) stands for its country and what it honours**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** To know a symbol fully is to know…
a) only its colours  b) its face and what it stands for  c) its price
**Answer: b) its face and what it stands for**

**5. (Sub lesson 1)** The symbols sequence opens which block of the Grade 8 book?
a) the music block  b) the identity block (Block IV)  c) the drawing block
**Answer: b) the identity block (Block IV)** — Block IV S1.
