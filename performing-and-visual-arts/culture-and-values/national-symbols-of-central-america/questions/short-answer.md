# National Symbols of Central America — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which countries' national symbols does the book teach?
**Answer: The national symbols of the Central American countries.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** In which book and block is this sequence?
