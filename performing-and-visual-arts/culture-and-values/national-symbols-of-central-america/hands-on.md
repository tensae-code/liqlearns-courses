# Hands-on — National Symbols of Central America

The lesson with your eyes, not only your ears.

## Read a symbol
Choose a national symbol of your own country. Show it or describe it to a partner, and say what it stands for — what it honours or remembers.

## Symbols side by side
In a group, each learner names one Central American country and one of its national symbols, as the book teaches them country by country. Say how the symbols differ.
