# Lesson plan — National Symbols of Central America

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Culture and Values · **Lesson:** National Symbols of Central America
**Place in the curriculum:** Culture and Values (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the national symbols of the Central American countries — what each country's symbols are, and what they stand for.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of its title — recommended first, never forced.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The symbols country by country** — the national symbols of the Central American countries, met one country at a time.
2. **What the symbols stand for** — a symbol carries meaning: it stands for its country and what the country honours.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Festivals and Their Arts in this course. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say that every Central American country has national symbols.
2. Say that a national symbol stands for its country and what it honours.

## What you need
Your eyes, and the symbols of your own country to think with.
