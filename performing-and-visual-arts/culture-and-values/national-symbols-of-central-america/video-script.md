# Video script — National Symbols of Central America

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A country speaks without words. How? By its **symbols**!

Every country of Central America has its **national symbols**. Each one **stands for** its country — for what the country honours and remembers.

Learn the symbols. Read what they say.
