# Music in Society

## Do it first

Think of a song that makes people brave together — sung at a send-off, a victory, a hard day. This lesson sorts society's songs by the work they do.

## Sub lesson 1 — War songs (teacher guide — evidence)

*This lesson stands on teacher-guide evidence.* The Grade 4 guide's society teaching (Chapter 3) sorts music by the **work it does in society** — and its first kind is the **war song** (የጦርነት ዘፈኖች): the songs a community sings when its people must be **brave together** — sung before and through hard defence of home and country, to strengthen hearts that must not scatter.

A war song is not entertainment with a louder drum. In the guide's sorting it is a **tool of courage**: it gathers many frightened people into one steady body, and it remembers past defenders so present ones stand taller.

## Sub lesson 2 — Lament and mourning melodies (teacher guide — evidence)

The guide's second kind is the opposite pole of the first: the **lament, the mourning melody** (የለቅሶ ዜማዎች) — the music society keeps for **grief**. When death takes a member, the community does not leave the bereaved alone with silence; it sings with them.

The mourning melody's work is **company and honour**: it tells the grieving family that the lost one mattered to many, and it gives grief a shared shape — a melody many voices can carry when one voice cannot. A society is known by its mourning as much as by its joy.

## Sub lesson 3 — Children's songs — እሹሩሩ (teacher guide — evidence)

The guide's third kind is society's music for its youngest: **children's songs** (የሕፃናት ዜማዎች) — among them the **እሹሩሩ (eshururu)** songs: the lullaby and play songs sung to and with small children.

Their work looks small and is not. The lullaby **settles and guards** the child; the play song **teaches the first words, the first counting, the first manners** — society's values arriving in the first music a person ever hears. The guide's sorting is complete: a society sings its defence, its grief and its children — war, mourning and the cradle — and music stands at all three.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the lesson in your own words: the Grade 4 guide sorts society's songs by their work: war songs, mourning melodies and children's songs each hold a place in the community's life (teacher-guide evidence). Then let your companion ask you one question about any part of it, and answer from the lesson.
