# Music in Society — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** War songs (የጦርነት ዘፈኖች), in the guide's sorting, work to —
a) strengthen hearts for the community's defence  b) put children to sleep  c) sell goods
**Answer: a) strengthen hearts for the community's defence** — courage for defence is the war song's work.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A war song gathers people into —
a) one steady body  b) silence  c) scattered fear
**Answer: a) one steady body** — the song unites the frightened into the steady.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Mourning melodies (የለቅሶ ዜማዎች) work to —
a) keep the bereaved company and honour the lost one  b) celebrate a harvest  c) teach counting
**Answer: a) keep the bereaved company and honour the lost one** — company and honour are the lament's work.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Grief in the lament is carried by —
a) many voices together  b) one voice forced alone  c) instruments only, never voices
**Answer: a) many voices together** — the shared melody is the point.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** እሹሩሩ (eshururu) names —
a) lullaby and play songs for small children  b) war drums  c) mourning dress
**Answer: a) lullaby and play songs for small children** — eshururu is the children's kind in the guide.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** Children's play songs teach —
a) first words, first counting, first manners  b) nothing  c) only tunes
**Answer: a) first words, first counting, first manners** — the first teaching arrives in song.
