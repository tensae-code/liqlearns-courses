# Video script — Music in Society

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

War songs.
የጦርነት ዘፈኖች.
Many hearts — one steady body.
Courage, remembered and sung.

Mourning melodies.
የለቅሶ ዜማዎች.
Grief, carried by many voices.
No family mourns alone.

Children's songs.
የሕፃናት ዜማዎች — እሹሩሩ.
The lullaby settles and guards.
The play song teaches first words.
