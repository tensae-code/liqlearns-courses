# Lesson plan — Music in Society

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Culture and Values · **Lesson:** Music in Society
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 5 — Culture and Values (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the Grade 4 guide sorts society's songs by their work: war songs, mourning melodies and children's songs each hold a place in the community's life (teacher-guide evidence).
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on teacher-guide evidence only — the Grade 4 guide's society chapter — and on the song lessons the learner already knows.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the
exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an
image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture
of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does
with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and
while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is
dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the
student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph,
or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be
shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved (the gate: README, "Units, Lessons and Sub Lessons (ruled 2026-11-08)"):

1. **War songs (teacher guide — evidence)** — society's songs of war and courage (የጦርነት ዘፈኖች) — sung to strengthen hearts for the country's defence (Grade 4 teacher guide, printed pp. 47–56).
2. **Lament and mourning melodies (teacher guide — evidence)** — the melodies of mourning (የለቅሶ ዜማዎች) — society's music for grief, sung with the bereaved (Grade 4 teacher guide, printed pp. 47–56).
3. **Children's songs — እሹሩሩ (teacher guide — evidence)** — children's songs and melodies (የሕፃናት ዜማዎች) — the እሹሩሩ lullaby and play songs with which society raises its youngest (Grade 4 teacher guide, printed pp. 47–56).

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say what work war songs do in society.
2. Say what work mourning melodies do.
3. Name the children's-song kind and its two works.
