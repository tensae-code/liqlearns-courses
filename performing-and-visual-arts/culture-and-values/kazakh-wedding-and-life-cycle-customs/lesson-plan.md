# Lesson plan — Kazakh Wedding and Life Cycle Customs

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Culture and Values · **Lesson:** Kazakh Wedding and Life Cycle Customs
**Place in the curriculum:** Culture and Values (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** kazakh Wedding and Life Cycle Customs — its forms, history and cultural meaning, taught as one topic lesson..
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this course in the same title.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Kazakhstan Grade 5 Music (Kulmanova)** — the rite in brief: when a child takes first steps, the two-coloured cord on its legs is cut with good wishes so it will walk fast and become a good person; the folk song «Тұсаукесер жыры»; M. Arinbaev's «Балама тілек», a father's sung blessing — may the son grow a batyr, an akyn, a zhyrau; the song «Қазағымның дәстүрлері-ай» (music N. Abzhambekov, words T. Anisimov) names the rites still kept: shildekhana, besik toi, tusaukeser, sundet toi, kuda tusu, syrga salu, betashar; the wedding song «Жар-жар» in two texts (words A. Rakhmetov; words A. Asylbek) — tell the wedding one from the bride's farewell by its words; the synsu lament and betashar explained; class project "The Wedding Rite (Жар-жар)": research the wedding songs in books and online, perform «Жар-жар», and rework the rite songs in your own presentation
