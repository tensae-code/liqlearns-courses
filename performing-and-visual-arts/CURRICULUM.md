# Performing and Visual Arts course — CURRICULUM (the path)

Performing and Visual Arts is Ethiopia's own arts subject — drawing,
music, drama and dance taught woven together, the way Ethiopia's own
government books teach it: look and listen first, then imitate, then
make. In the course architecture it stands as its own top-level
course. The path climbs by strand and by the size of the thing a
learner can do: **First Steps → Visual Art → Music → Drama and
Dance → Culture and Values** (see TITLES.md).

**Levels, not grades:** a learner climbs when they are ready, not
when a school calendar says so. The school grades the books come
from appear only in each lesson's sources (provenance), never as
the course's organisation.

**The first page (what learners are told):** everything in this
course comes from Ethiopia's own government Performing and Visual
Arts books — the student textbooks and teacher guides named in each
lesson's sources. The one exception is the **Tip boxes**: a tip in
a box is from us, the people making LiqLearns. Tips are ours; the
lessons are the books'.

## The units

1. **First Steps** — what the arts are, observing our surroundings,
   looking at pictures, how the arts belong together and help every
   subject, appreciating the arts.
2. **Visual Art** — drawing, colours, crafts and making, printing,
   painting, beautiful writing, photographs and photography.
3. **Music** — listening, songs, instruments, and reading notes.
4. **Drama and Dance** — movement, drama, theatre, dance, games,
   stories.
5. **Culture and Values** — traditional dress and houses, festivals
   and their arts, old arts and today's arts, our community's
   values.
