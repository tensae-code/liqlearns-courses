# Performing and Visual Arts course — titles, FROM THE BOTTOM

Built from the bottom, the same way as the other courses: a learner
does not start at the stage — they start with their eyes, ears and
hands. The path climbs:

**First Steps → Visual Art → Music → Drama and Dance → Culture and Values**

That ladder IS the path order (recommended, never forced — a learner
can jump in higher; the "start here instead" path points down this
ladder to the bottom).

The titles, in ladder order:

1. **First Steps** — the arts before their names: caring for your
   book, what the arts are, observing the world with eyes and ears,
   looking at pictures and saying what they tell, the pictures of
   what we do every day, how the three arts belong together and help
   every other subject, and learning to appreciate the arts.
2. **Visual Art** — the hand and the eye: first marks and lines,
   shapes and forms, colouring, colours and mixing them, the crafts
   around us (metal, pottery, weaving), making with clay and paper,
   printing, painting then and now, sculpture and historic places,
   beautiful writing, and photographs — looking at them, and taking
   them to pass a message.
3. **Music** — the ear and the voice: sounds around us, naming
   sounds, the songs (animals, family, nature, movement, festivals,
   school, counting, clean living, our country, colours), the
   instruments of Ethiopia — masenqo, kebero, washint, krar, atamo,
   negarite and the church instruments — hearing each one's sound,
   the kinds of traditional music, and reading music: whole, half
   and quarter notes and rests, played and sung.
4. **Drama and Dance** — the body: how things, animals and people
   move; playing family roles; acting together; playing a person;
   theatre on a stage; acting our history; circle dances and
   traditional steps; the dances of Ethiopia's peoples; moving
   freely to music; traditional games; stories and folktales told
   aloud; riddles.
5. **Culture and Values** — the arts as a people's memory:
   traditional dress then and now, houses then and now, the
   festivals and their arts (Meskel, Timket, Arafa, Genna, New
   Year, Adwa), old arts and today's arts, and the values our
   community holds, carried in art.
