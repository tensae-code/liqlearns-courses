# Styles and Approaches in Theatre and Film — short answer

Answer in your own words, in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In one sentence, what is style?
**Answer: The approach a production takes — its chosen manner across acting, design and pace.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why does this topic exist in the guide?
**Answer: So makers know the approaches open to them and choose a production's style deliberately.**
