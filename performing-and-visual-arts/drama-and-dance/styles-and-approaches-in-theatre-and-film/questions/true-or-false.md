# Styles and Approaches in Theatre and Film — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Style and story are the same thing. True or false?
**Answer: False — style is the story's manner, not the story.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A production can take more than one style approach across different works. True or false?
**Answer: True.**
