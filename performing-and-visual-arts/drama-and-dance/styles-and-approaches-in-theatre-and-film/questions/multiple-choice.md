# Styles and Approaches in Theatre and Film — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Style in production is —
a) the story itself  b) the approach — the manner in which a production does everything  c) the ticket design
**Answer: b) the approach — the manner in which a production does everything.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Two productions of one play in different styles are —
a) identical  b) two different experiences  c) both wrong
**Answer: b) two different experiences.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Style shows itself in —
a) acting, dress, light and pace  b) only the title  c) nothing visible
**Answer: a) acting, dress, light and pace.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Style is best chosen —
a) by habit  b) deliberately, for this story, audience and place  c) by copying any hit
**Answer: b) deliberately, for this story, audience and place.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Once chosen, a style should be —
a) held across the production  b) changed nightly at random  c) kept secret from actors
**Answer: a) held across the production.**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** The maker's first style questions concern —
a) story, audience and place  b) only budget  c) only fame
**Answer: a) story, audience and place.**
