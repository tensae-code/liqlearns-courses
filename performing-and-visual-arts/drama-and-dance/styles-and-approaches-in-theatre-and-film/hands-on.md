# Hands-on — Styles and Approaches in Theatre and Film

Real things to do with what this lesson teaches — at school or at home, with people around you.

## One scene, two styles
Groups perform the same tiny scene twice, in two contrasting approaches. The class names what changed — and confirms the events did not.

## Style spotting
Recall productions or films you know. For each, the class names its approach in a phrase — 'grave and still', 'fast and bright' — building a shared style vocabulary.
