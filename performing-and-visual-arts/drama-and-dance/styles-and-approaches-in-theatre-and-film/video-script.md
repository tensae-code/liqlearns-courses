# Video script — Styles and Approaches in Theatre and Film

A short read-aloud script in a super-simple voice, made later as the lesson's video. It follows the lesson's own order and names only what the lesson teaches.

---

Style is the approach a production takes.
The same story, staged in different styles, becomes different theatre.
A production chooses its style — in acting, in design, in pace.
Knowing styles means choosing deliberately, not by habit.
