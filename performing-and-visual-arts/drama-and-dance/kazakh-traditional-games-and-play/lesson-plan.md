## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Kazakhstan Grade 4 Labour Training (Ormanbekova)** — orteke: the Kazakh musical-theatrical art in which a dombra player makes a carved goat puppet dance on a table
2. **Kazakhstan Grade 4 Labour Training (Muratkыzy)** — altybakan, "six poles": the ancient Kazakh national swing game of evening gatherings, on the national heritage list; how the swing is built; a working altybakan model from wooden sticks, textile threads and felt; asyk, the knucklebone game of sheep and goat bones, UNESCO-listed; the bone's landing positions (alshy, toike, buk, shik) and the giant "Asyk of Luck" in Almaty; a lamb made from a paper ball and cotton balls
3. **Kazakhstan Grade 8 NIS Art (Akhmetkalieva)** — what it teaches: Togyzkumalak (Тоғызқұмалақ) is the Kazakh's ancient national game (қазақтың ежелгі ұлттық ойыны); the game's name comes from the words «тоғыз құмалақ» (nine balls); in ancient times shepherds (малшылар) played it by digging holes in the ground (қойдың құмалағымен — with sheep's droppings/balls); it is also called «шопандар алгебрасы» ("shepherds' algebra"); the game develops mathematical thinking skills unsurpassedly (математикалық ойлау дағдыларын теңдессіз дамытады), so in the old legends the game's first name is also connected with counting; Togyzkumalak is a game rich in tactical methods (тактикалық тәсілдерге бай) and strategic elements (стратегиялық элементтері бар күрделі, позициялық ойын); in any place it can be played quickly and ...; the tuzdyk's (тұздықтардың) strategic placing is important; our ancestors played Togyzkumalak usefully in free time; for example the famous Kazakh poet Abai Kunanbaev (Абай Құнанбаев) was a strong player in his life and played this game without leaving the board for several weeks together with other famous players; research: define Togyzkumalak's peculiarities; of what components does it consist?; why is it so named?; what meaning do the components' names give?; do you know...: national board games (ұлттық үстел ойындары) are a source giving the possibility to research any country's culture (мәдениетін зерттеуге мүмкіндік беретін дереккөз), because people's worldview, ancient beliefs (ежелгі нанымдары) and traditions (дәстүрлері) reach their generations, and together with that separate countries' and cities' development history can be known
4. **Kazakhstan Grade 8 Artistic Labour (boys, Chukalin)** — what it teaches: one of the Kazakhs' ancient national games is togyzkumalak (тоғызқұмалақ); it influences a person's thinking system, develops agility, inclines to mathematics and teaches patience; the number 9 lies at the game's name's basis — the ancient Turks held the number 9 sacred; in the game's course the opponents use all four mathematical operations fully; the game requires, together with a counting system, being tactical
