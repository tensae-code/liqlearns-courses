| Kazakhstan — 3.2 (continued). Тоғызқұмалақ туралы (About Togyzkumalak) — what it teaches: Togyzkumalak (Тоғызқұмалақ) is the Kazakh's ancient national game (қазақтың ежелгі ұлттық ойыны); the game's name comes from the words «тоғыз құмалақ» (nine balls); in ancient times shepherds (малшылар) played it by digging holes in the ground (қойдың құмалағымен — with sheep's droppings/balls); it is also called «шопандар алгебрасы» ("shepherds' algebra"); the game develops mathematical thinking skills unsurpassedly (математикалық ойлау дағдыларын теңдессіз дамытады), so in the old legends the game's first name is also connected with counting; Togyzkumalak is a game rich in tactical methods (тактикалық тәсілдерге бай) and strategic elements (стратегиялық элементтері бар күрделі, позициялық ойын); in any place it can be played quickly and ...; the tuzdyk's (тұздықтардың) strategic placing is important; our ancestors played Togyzkumalak usefully in free time; for example the famous Kazakh poet Abai Kunanbaev (Абай Құнанбаев) was a strong player in his life and played this game without leaving the board for several weeks together with other famous players; research: define Togyzkumalak's peculiarities; of what components does it consist?; why is it so named?; what meaning do the components' names give?; do you know...: national board games (ұлттық үстел ойындары) are a source giving the possibility to research any country's culture (мәдениетін зерттеуге мүмкіндік беретін дереккөз), because people's worldview, ancient beliefs (ежелгі нанымдары) and traditions (дәстүрлері) reach their generations, and together with that separate countries' and cities' development history can be known (student book; original target `culture-and-values/togyzkumalak-kazakh-board-game`) | Book 3, printed p. 69 |
