# Mongolian Dances: Biyelgee and Tovshoo

## Do it first

Start with the first part below — do it, sing it or make it before you read any explanation.

## Sub lesson 1 — Өөригөө сориорой (make-and-compose tasks)

Өөригөө сориорой (make-and-compose tasks) — composing the "Товшоо" (Tovshoo), a traditional Mongolian dance, as musical movement in two-share and three-share measures — heel, toe and stamp steps marked under the beat — and making simple shaker instruments (cups, clips, tubes) from the picture and playing the shown drum-and-shaker patterns on them

## Sub lesson 2 — Дөрвөд биелгээ (the Dörvöd biyelgee

Дөрвөд биелгээ (the Dörvöd biyelgee — Mongolia's own ethnic-group dance) with Хамтдаа бүжиглэе (Let's Dance Together)
