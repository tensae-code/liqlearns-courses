# Hands-on — Mongolian Dances: Biyelgee and Tovshoo

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Өөригөө сориорой (make-and-compose tasks)
Do this part the way the book sets it: Өөригөө сориорой (make-and-compose tasks) — composing the "Товшоо" (Tovshoo), a traditional Mongolian dance, as musical movement in two-share and three-share measures — heel, toe and stamp steps marked under the beat — and making simple sha

## Дөрвөд биелгээ (the Dörvöd biyelgee
Do this part the way the book sets it: Дөрвөд биелгээ (the Dörvöd biyelgee — Mongolia's own ethnic-group dance) with Хамтдаа бүжиглэе (Let's Dance Together)
