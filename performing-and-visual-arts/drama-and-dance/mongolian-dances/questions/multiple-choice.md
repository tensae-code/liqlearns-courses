# Mongolian Dances: Biyelgee and Tovshoo — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Biyelgee is danced mainly with…
a) the upper body  b) only the feet  c) the head alone
**Answer: a) the upper body**

**2.** The Dörvöd biyelgee is the dance of…
a) Mongolia's Dörvöd people  b) a European court  c) sailors
**Answer: a) Mongolia's Dörvöd people**

**3.** Tovshoo steps are marked by…
a) heel, toe and stamp steps under the beat  b) jumps only  c) standing still
**Answer: a) heel, toe and stamp steps under the beat**
