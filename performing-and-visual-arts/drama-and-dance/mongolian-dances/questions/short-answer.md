# Mongolian Dances: Biyelgee and Tovshoo — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1.** What is biyelgee?
**Answer: Mongolia's own dance, danced with the upper body — the Dörvöd biyelgee is its named form here.**

**2.** How is the Tovshoo composed in Grade 1?
**Answer: As musical movement in two-share and three-share measures, with heel, toe and stamp steps marked under the beat.**
