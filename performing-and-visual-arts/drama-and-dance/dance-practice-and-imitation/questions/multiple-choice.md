# Dance Practice and Imitation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Dance practice means —
a) hearing about a dance  b) doing the dance again and again until it sits in the body  c) watching once
**Answer: b) doing the dance again and again until it sits in the body.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Practice builds the bridge between —
a) watching a dance and having it  b) two dancers  c) music and silence
**Answer: a) watching a dance and having it.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Better practice is —
a) one long try, once  b) short and regular  c) never repeated
**Answer: b) short and regular.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A work's theme (ጭብጥ) is —
a) its colours  b) the message it is really about, underneath the surface  c) its length
**Answer: b) the message it is really about, underneath the surface.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** A drama about friends sharing bread has the theme —
a) bread-making  b) generosity  c) farming
**Answer: b) generosity.**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Themes can be found in —
a) drama only  b) drama, video and photograph  c) photographs only
**Answer: b) drama, video and photograph.**

**7. (Sub lesson 3)** Imitation in drama is —
a) copying movements and actions exactly, as a skill  b) making fun of people  c) dancing alone
**Answer: a) copying movements and actions exactly, as a skill.**

**8. (Sub lesson 3)** An actor imitates in order to —
a) mock people  b) play people truly — a farmer, a seller, an elder  c) avoid rehearsing
**Answer: b) play people truly — a farmer, a seller, an elder.**

**9. (Sub lesson 3)** Good imitation is done —
a) with respect  b) to shame others  c) secretly
**Answer: a) with respect.**
