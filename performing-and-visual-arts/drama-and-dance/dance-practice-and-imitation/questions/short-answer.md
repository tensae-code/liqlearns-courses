# Dance Practice and Imitation — short answer

Answer in your own words, in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why is a dance practised more than once?
**Answer: So the steps sit in the body and can be performed truly.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How do you find a theme?
**Answer: Ask what the work is really saying, underneath its surface.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What is imitation (አከታተብ/ማስመሰል)?
**Answer: Copying movements and actions exactly, as a drama skill.**
