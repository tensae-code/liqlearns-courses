# Dance Practice and Imitation — sub lessons and depth

This is the depth record for the lesson, re-expressed as sub lessons: each sub lesson is one step up in depth, proved before the next opens.

## Sub lesson 1 — Dance practice (teacher guide — evidence)
Pass mark: the learner can say what dance practice is and why it is repeated. The gate draws from this sub lesson's whole question set.

## Sub lesson 2 — Finding themes — in drama, video and photograph (teacher guide — evidence)
Pass mark: the learner can find and name the theme of a drama, video or photograph. The gate draws from this sub lesson's whole question set.

## Sub lesson 3 — Imitation — copying as a drama skill (teacher guide — evidence)
Pass mark: the learner can imitate a movement or action exactly enough to be recognised. The gate draws from this sub lesson's whole question set.
