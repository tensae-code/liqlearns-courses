# Hands-on — Dance Practice and Imitation

Real things to do with what this lesson teaches — at school or at home, with people around you.

## Leader and mirror
One learner leads slow movements; the group imitates exactly, like mirrors. Change leaders often — leading and imitating are both the lesson.

## Theme hunt
Look at one photograph or recall one short drama. Together, answer: what is this about, deep down? That deep-about is its theme (ጭብጥ).
