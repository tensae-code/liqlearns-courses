# Video script — Latin American and Caribbean Dances

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

South across the border — the dances change.

Salsa! Merengue! Zouk! Bachata!

Dance them strong. Dance them slack. Feel the force change.

Mirror your partner. Lead — then follow.

One theme, one solo: safety, nature, the forest itself.
