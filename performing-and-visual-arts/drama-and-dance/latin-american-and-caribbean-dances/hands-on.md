# Hands-on — Latin American and Caribbean Dances

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Much force, little force
Dance one short salsa or merengue phrase twice: first with much force and tension, then with little. Say what changed in the dance.

## Mirror in pairs
In pairs, mirror your partner's Latin step exactly; then swap — the follower becomes the leader.

## Adventure solo
Dance a one-minute solo on one theme — safety, nature, fauna, flora, environment or climate — and let the class guess the theme.
