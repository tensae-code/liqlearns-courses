# Latin American and Caribbean Dances

## Do it first

Quick start: from the title alone, guess what this lesson's work is. Then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — Salsa, merengue, zouk and bachata — with force and without

The Latin American steps named are **salsa, merengue, zouk and bachata**, danced **with much and with little force and tension**, inside a **spoken adventure improvisation** — arriving in Brazil: danger? an animal? darkness? a hand, a paw, a branch? — with **mirror work in pairs, leading and following**, and the technique image of **a salsa step on unsafe ground in a burning Amazon forest**.

## Sub lesson 2 — The themed solo

The solo of **one to one and a half minutes** takes **one theme** — **safety, sustainability, nature and culture, fauna, flora, environment or climate** — the themes of the whole Amazon dance-route series.
