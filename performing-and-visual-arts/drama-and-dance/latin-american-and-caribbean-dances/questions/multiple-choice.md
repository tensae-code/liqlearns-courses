# Latin American and Caribbean Dances — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Latin American steps named are…
a) awasa and kathak  b) salsa, merengue, zouk and bachata  c) waltz and polka
**Answer: b) salsa, merengue, zouk and bachata**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The steps are danced with…
a) one fixed force  b) much and with little force and tension  c) no force at all
**Answer: b) much and with little force and tension**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The solo lasts…
a) ten minutes  b) one to one and a half minutes  c) five seconds
**Answer: b) one to one and a half minutes**
