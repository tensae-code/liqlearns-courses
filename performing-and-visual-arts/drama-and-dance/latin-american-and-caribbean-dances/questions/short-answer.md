# Latin American and Caribbean Dances — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the four Latin American dances.
**Answer: Salsa, merengue, zouk and bachata.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What pair work is practised with the steps?
**Answer: Mirror work in pairs, leading and following.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Name two themes a solo may take.
**Answer: Any two of: safety, sustainability, nature and culture, fauna, flora, environment, climate.**
