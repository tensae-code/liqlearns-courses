# Video script — Spanish Golden Age Theatre

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

**Teatro del Siglo de Oro español.** The theatre of Spain's Golden Age — plays written for the corral de comedias, the popular courtyard theatre, and printed after long runs: Lope de Vega (Fuenteovejuna), Tirso de Molina (Don Gil de las calzas verdes) and Calderón de la Barca (La vida es sueño).

That is Spanish Golden Age Theatre — Venezuela's own books teach it this way.
