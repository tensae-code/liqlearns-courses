# Hands-on — Spanish Golden Age Theatre

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Teatro del Siglo de Oro español
Work through this part with real materials or your own body, the way the book sets it: the theatre of Spain's Golden Age — plays written for the corral de comedias, the popular courtyard theatre, and printed after long runs: Lope de Vega (Fuenteovejuna), Tirso de Molina (Don Gil de las calzas verdes) and Calderón de la Barca (La vida es sueño). Show a companion what you made or did, and say what the part teaches.
