# Hands-on — Street Theatre

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Teatro de calle
Work through this part with real materials or your own body, the way the book sets it: street theatre — theatre leaving the halls for plazas, parks, communities and streets since the 1970s, also known as community theatre; the public approached directly, playfully and spontaneously, with everyday social themes under a critical and aesthetic focus. Show a companion what you made or did, and say what the part teaches.
