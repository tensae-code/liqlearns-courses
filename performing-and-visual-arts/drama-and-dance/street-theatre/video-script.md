# Video script — Street Theatre

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

**Teatro de calle.** Street theatre — theatre leaving the halls for plazas, parks, communities and streets since the 1970s, also known as community theatre; the public approached directly, playfully and spontaneously, with everyday social themes under a critical and aesthetic focus.

That is Street Theatre — Venezuela's own books teach it this way.
