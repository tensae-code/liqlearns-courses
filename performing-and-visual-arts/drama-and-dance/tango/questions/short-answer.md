# Tango — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name two of the tango's parents.
**Answer: Any two of: contradanza (from English country dance), habanera, milonga, quebradas and cortes.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which city forged the tango?
**Answer: Buenos Aires.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Whose dances were the quebradas and cortes?
**Answer: The African slaves'.**
