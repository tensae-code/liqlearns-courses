# Tango — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The tango was forged in…
a) Buenos Aires  b) Vienna  c) New Orleans
**Answer: a) Buenos Aires**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** One tango parent is the Cuban…
a) habanera  b) polka  c) minuet
**Answer: a) habanera**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The gauchos contributed the…
a) milonga  b) waltz  c) cakewalk
**Answer: a) milonga**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** The tango began as the dance of…
a) the poor quarters  b) the royal court  c) sailors only
**Answer: a) the poor quarters**

**5. (Sub lesson 1)** After the poor quarters, the tango conquered…
a) Paris's fine salons  b) Iceland's farms  c) schoolrooms only
**Answer: a) Paris's fine salons**
