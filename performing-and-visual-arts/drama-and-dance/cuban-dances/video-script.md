# Video script — Cuban Dances and Steps

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Cuba's dances and their steps, taught in pictured sequences in Cuba's own books — the zapateo, the danzón's paso básico, paseo and vuelta de tornillo, the son, conga, mambo, chachachá, casino, comparsa and rumba steps.
