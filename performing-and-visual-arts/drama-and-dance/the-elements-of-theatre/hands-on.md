# Hands-on — The Elements of Theatre

The lesson with your hands, your feet and your voice together, not only your eyes.

## Three faces exercise
Act the same one-line moment three ways: full-face, half-face, and (wrongly) full back to a frontal audience. Let the audience say which worked and why — the book's stage law learned by the body.

## Hand props and fixed props
Gather hand props (a cane, an umbrella) and set one fixed prop (a cupboard, a table with a cloth). Carry the scene from prop to prop: the hand props travel, the fixed prop stays.
