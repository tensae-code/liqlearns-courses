# Video script — The Elements of Theatre

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A play stands on setting, character, plot and theme.
The actor brings three tools: body, voice, mind.
Frontal stage: faces to the people. Round stage: the people all around.
The director holds the whole play in one pair of hands.
