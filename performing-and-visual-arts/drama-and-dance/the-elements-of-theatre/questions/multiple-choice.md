# The Elements of Theatre — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Setting (መቼት) is made of…
  a) Place and time b) Costume and light c) Song and dance
**Answer: a) Place and time** — setting is the place of the story plus the time when it happens.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The actor's three tools are…
  a) Body, voice and mind b) Costume, props and script c) Stage, light and sound
**Answer: a) Body, voice and mind** — body, voice and mind — the book's three needs of the actor.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** On a round (arena) stage, the audience sits…
  a) All around the stage b) Only behind the stage c) Outside the hall
**Answer: a) All around the stage** — the arena stage stands in the middle with the audience around it.

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Who runs the cast's rehearsals before production?
  a) The director b) The audience c) The ticket seller
**Answer: a) The director** — the director runs the rehearsals and holds the whole process.

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** A cane (ከዘራ) is a…
  a) Hand prop — carried and moved b) Fixed prop c) Kind of stage
**Answer: a) Hand prop — carried and moved** — hand props are carried and moved; fixed props stay in one place.

**6. (Sub lesson 6)** አዘጋጅ in the guide's list is…
  a) The organiser/director b) The audience c) A prop
**Answer: a) The organiser/director** — አዘጋጅ is the organiser/director among the basic elements.

**7. (Sub lesson 7)** Theatre focuses only on —
a) sound  b) the human being  c) colours
**Answer: b) the human being.**

**8. (Sub lesson 7)** Music's focus, by contrast, is —
a) sound  b) the human being  c) stone
**Answer: a) sound.**

**9. (Sub lesson 7)** In theatre, group coordination —
a) stays on stage  b) passes to the audience, who become part of the theatre  c) is not needed
**Answer: b) passes to the audience, who become part of the theatre.**

**10. (Sub lesson 8)** Children in youth theatres take part in —
a) only watching  b) writing, acting, stage preparation, music and dance, costume design and audiencehood  c) selling tickets only
**Answer: b) writing, acting, stage preparation, music and dance, costume design and audiencehood.**

**11. (Sub lesson 8)** Film can be shared in class simply by —
a) a full cinema crew  b) short recordings with a mobile camera in groups  c) never attempting it
**Answer: b) short recordings with a mobile camera in groups.**

**12. (Sub lesson 8)** Collected photographs can be shown as —
a) a photo exhibition  b) a dance  c) a meal
**Answer: a) a photo exhibition.**

**13. (Sub lesson 9)** One closing practical is —
a) a written exam only  b) preparing a short theatre piece in groups and showing it in class  c) silent reading
**Answer: b) preparing a short theatre piece in groups and showing it in class.**

**14. (Sub lesson 9)** After showing the short video, learners must say —
a) its length  b) the feeling and idea it meant to show  c) its price
**Answer: b) the feeling and idea it meant to show.**

**15. (Sub lesson 9)** Fig. 36 shows —
a) a photography exhibition  b) a dance  c) a market
**Answer: a) a photography exhibition.**

**16. (Sub lesson 10)** The preparation grasp-list begins with —
a) the audience  b) the tickets  c) the poster
**Answer: a) the audience.**

**17. (Sub lesson 10)** The director's part in the list is —
a) leading and organising the theatre  b) acting every role  c) selling seats
**Answer: a) leading and organising the theatre.**

**18. (Sub lesson 10)** Visual effects in the list come from —
a) costume, light and landscapes  b) silence  c) the weather
**Answer: a) costume, light and landscapes.**

**19. (Sub lesson 11)** Theatre's six building parts are —
a) setting, character, plot, theme, dialogue, music  b) only dialogue and music  c) paint, wood, stone, clay
**Answer: a) setting, character, plot, theme, dialogue, music.**

**20. (Sub lesson 11)** On stage, actors perform —
a) conflicts, actions and atmospheres  b) only songs  c) written exercises
**Answer: a) conflicts, actions and atmospheres.**

**21. (Sub lesson 11)** Theatre's preparation is described as its own —
a) movement (rehearsal) process  b) exam  c) holiday
**Answer: a) movement (rehearsal) process.**

**22. (Sub lesson 1)** The Grade 7 list names —
a) seven elements of theatre  b) three elements  c) twelve elements
**Answer: a) seven elements of theatre.**

**23. (Sub lesson 1)** Setting (መቼት) means —
a) when + where  b) who + why  c) only the place
**Answer: a) when + where.**

**24. (Sub lesson 1)** A character in the Grade 7 account can be —
a) only an adult man  b) an animal, an object, an abstract idea or a spirit being  c) only a king
**Answer: b) an animal, an object, an abstract idea or a spirit being.**

**25. (Sub lesson 6)** The Grade 6 guide's theatre section covers —
a) writing, elements, genres, and preparation/staging of theatre  b) only costumes  c) only dance
**Answer: a) writing, elements, genres, and preparation/staging of theatre.**

**26. (Sub lesson 6)** የተውኔት አዘገጃጀት names —
a) how theatre is prepared/staged  b) a kind of dance  c) a song
**Answer: a) how theatre is prepared/staged.**

**27. (Sub lesson 6)** Film art, in the guide's section, is —
