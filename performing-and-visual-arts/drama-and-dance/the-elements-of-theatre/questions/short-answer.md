# The Elements of Theatre — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which theme is universal: a village boundary dispute, or love?
**Answer: Love — a universal theme crosses era, culture, time and place.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why must the actor's body be exercised?
**Answer: Theatre needs much breath and movement — the body must be fit for every movement.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What two things meet to make theatre happen?
**Answer: Actors and audience — theatre lives in their interaction.**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Name two things the director organises besides rehearsals.
**Answer: Any two of: costumes, set design, light — and the placing of actions on the stage.**

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** What are the two ways to make a set?
**Answer: Painted set, and built (creative) set from materials.**

**6. (Sub lesson 6)** What does the Grade 6 guide add about genres?
**Answer: Theatre's (technical) genres (የተውኔት ዘውጎች) and how theatre is prepared/staged.**
