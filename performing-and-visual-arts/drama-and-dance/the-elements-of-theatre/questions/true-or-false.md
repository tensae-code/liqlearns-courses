# The Elements of Theatre — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A character can be an abstract idea, like kindness.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Actors are expected to heed the director's directions.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** On a frontal stage an actor may play a whole scene with a full back to the audience.
**Answer: False**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** The director checks actors' movements for fitness and message-match.
**Answer: True**

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** For suspenseful and dream scenes the book directs dim, flickering light.
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 6)** The play script (ጽሁፈ-ተውኔት) is one of the basic elements of theatre.
**Answer: True**

**7. (Sub lesson 11)** The Grade 6 summary traces theatre's name to a Greek root and its age as long.
**Answer: True** — the summary names theatre's long age and its Greek root.
