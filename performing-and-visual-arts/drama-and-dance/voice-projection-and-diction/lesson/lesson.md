# Voice Projection, Diction and Delivery

## Do it first

Say one sentence three ways: like a speaker addressing a crowd, like a poet keeping a poem's beat, and like an actor in a scene. Three different voices — you have already met this lesson.

## Sub lesson 1 — The three kinds of delivery

Morocco's guides teach **delivery (الإلقاء)** as an old art that theatre shares with oratory, in **three kinds**:

- **Oratorical delivery** — in classical Arabic, demanding eloquence and correct pronunciation; the oldest kind, from the old Arab markets and poetry contests.
- **Poetic delivery** — keeping the verse's metre and rhyme while holding the audience.
- **Acting delivery** — free, may be in everyday speech, carried by the actor's suggestion and movement.

The qualities of **good delivery**: clear pronunciation, a voice coloured by the meaning, and not staring at the audience.

## Sub lesson 2 — Expressing by voice alone

The voice alone can express: imitate **animal and bird sounds** (ox, ram, donkey, rooster, dove) and **environment sounds** (an airplane's buzz, running water, a fly's hum). And know the **soft voices** (a whisper) against the **loud voices** (a cry for help, calling someone far away, a scream of pain, an angry voice).

## Sub lesson 3 — Expressive delivery

**Expressive delivery (الإلقاء المعبر)** changes the voice's **level** — from shout to whisper — and its **softness**, and lets the **facial features, head and limb gestures, and movement on the stage** carry the meaning with it.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the three kinds of delivery and one quality of good delivery. Companion checks: three kinds named? A quality named?

## Do It Yourself

1. Deliver the same short line in all three kinds — oratorical, poetic, acting.
2. Imitate three sounds with your voice alone: one animal, one bird, one environment sound.
3. Say one sentence as a whisper, then as a cry for help. Write what changed in your voice.
