# Lesson plan — Voice Projection, Diction and Delivery

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Drama and Dance · **Lesson:** Voice Projection, Diction and Delivery
**Place in the curriculum:** Drama and Dance (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** delivery — speaking on stage — is an old art theatre shares with oratory, in three kinds (oratorical, poetic and acting), carried by clear pronunciation, a voice coloured by the meaning, and the voice alone able to express animals, nature and feeling.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this course in the same title.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. ## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The three kinds of delivery** — oratorical, poetic and acting delivery — what each kind is, where it comes from, and the qualities of good delivery.
2. **Expressing by voice alone** — imitating animal, bird and environment sounds, and the soft voices against the loud voices.
3. **Expressive delivery** — changing the voice's level from shout to whisper and its softness, with the face, head, limbs and stage movement carrying the meaning.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of this title. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. name the three kinds of delivery and say what makes delivery good
2. imitate sounds with the voice alone and tell a soft voice from a loud voice and say when each is used
3. show expressive delivery — change level and softness and let face and movement carry the meaning

## What you need
Your voice, your hands and a friend to work with.
