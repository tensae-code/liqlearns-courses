# Video script — Voice Projection, Diction and Delivery

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Listen — one voice can do so much.

A speaker stands and speaks — clear, strong, every word right. That is oratorical delivery — the oldest kind.
A poet speaks — the words keep their beat and rhyme. That is poetic delivery.
An actor speaks — free, like every day, and his movement speaks too. That is acting delivery.

And the voice alone? A rooster! A dove! Running water!
Soft — a whisper. Loud — a cry for help!

That is delivery — the actor's speaking craft.
