# Hands-on — Voice Projection, Diction and Delivery

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Three deliveries
In pairs, take one short line. Deliver it oratorically (standing tall, classical and clear), poetically (keeping a steady beat), and as acting (free, everyday, with movement). Your partner names the kind each time.

## Voice-alone sounds
One partner imitates a sound with the voice alone — an animal, a bird, or a sound of the environment. The other names it. Five sounds each, then swap.
