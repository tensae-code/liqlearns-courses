# Voice Projection, Diction and Delivery — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Delivery is an old art theatre shares with…
a) painting  b) oratory  c) pottery
**Answer: b) oratory.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which is NOT one of the three kinds of delivery?
a) oratorical  b) poetic  c) silent
**Answer: c) silent** — the three kinds are oratorical, poetic and acting delivery.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Good delivery needs…
a) clear pronunciation and a voice coloured by the meaning  b) staring at the audience  c) mumbling
**Answer: a) clear pronunciation and a voice coloured by the meaning.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Expressing by voice alone includes imitating…
a) only machines  b) animal, bird and environment sounds  c) nothing
**Answer: b) animal, bird and environment sounds.**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Expressive delivery changes the voice's…
a) owner  b) level, from shout to whisper, and its softness  c) language only
**Answer: b) level, from shout to whisper, and its softness.**
