# Voice Projection, Diction and Delivery — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Oratorical delivery is the oldest kind of delivery.
**Answer: True** — from the old Arab markets and poetry contests.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A whisper is one of the loud voices.
**Answer: False** — a whisper is a soft voice.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In expressive delivery the face and movement help carry the meaning.
**Answer: True.**
