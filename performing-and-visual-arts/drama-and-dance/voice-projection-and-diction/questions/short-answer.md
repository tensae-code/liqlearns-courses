# Voice Projection, Diction and Delivery — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the three kinds of delivery.
**Answer: Oratorical, poetic and acting delivery.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Give two examples of a loud voice from the guide.
**Answer: Any two of: a cry for help, calling someone far away, a scream of pain, an angry voice.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Besides the voice, what carries the meaning in expressive delivery?
**Answer: The facial features, head and limb gestures, and movement on the stage.**
