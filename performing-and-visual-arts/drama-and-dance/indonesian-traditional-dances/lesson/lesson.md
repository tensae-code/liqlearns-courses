# Indonesian Traditional Dances

## Do it first

Name one thing that belongs in this lesson — Indonesia's traditional dances — their varied forms, movement techniques such as Sundanese and Malay movement, and supporting elements. Say what it is in your own words. If you can name one, you are already inside the lesson.

## Sub lesson 1 — Bab 1 Keragaman Bentuk Tari Tradisi dan Unsur Pendukungnya

the diversity and uniqueness of the archipelago's traditional dances, by named example: tari totokng (West Kalimantan), tari jepin selendang (Pontianak, West Kalimantan), tari glipang (Probolinggo, East Java) and tari golek ayun-ayun (Central Java), studied from video and photos.

## Sub lesson 2 — Bab 1

performing the movement varieties (ragam gerak) of a chosen traditional dance in groups.

## Sub lesson 3 — Unit Pembelajaran 1 Mengenal Bentuk Dan Teknik Gerak Tari Tradisional

the forms and techniques of traditional dance movement through two named vocabularies — the basic movements of Tari Sunda (Godeg, Gilek, Ukel Tumpang Tali, Tepak Bahu, Nyawang, Lontang Kembar, Duduk Deku, Adeg-Adeg Sembada, Trisi, each pictured) and of Tari Melayu (Lenggang, Mak Inang and Joged basics for girls and boys, and Serampang 12) — with analysis of Malay movement and a comparison of Sunda and Melayu movement.

## Sub lesson 4 — Unit 1

the archipelago's dance kinds pictured and compared — Tari Primitif, Tari Klasik, Tari Rakyat, Tari Bali, Tari Gendhing Sriwijaya, Tari Jawa and Tari Dayak.

## Sub lesson 5 — Bab II

performing the basic movements of traditional dance (unjuk kerja) with attention to the main elements, as the chapter's summative performance.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson holds, part by part, in your own words. Companion checks: did every part get named, with its Indonesian name kept where the book gives one?

## Do It Yourself

1. Choose one named part of this lesson and find out one more true thing about it — from a person who knows it, or from the book your part came from.
2. Make or perform a small piece in the way of that part: a rhythm, a step, a drawing, a shaped piece of clay.
3. Write one sentence: what does this part show about Indonesia's own way of teaching its arts?
