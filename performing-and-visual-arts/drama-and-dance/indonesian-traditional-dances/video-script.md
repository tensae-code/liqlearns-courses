# Video script — Indonesian Traditional Dances

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This is Indonesia's own lesson.

**Bab 1 Keragaman Bentuk Tari Tradisi dan Unsur Pendukungnya.** the diversity and uniqueness of the archipelago's traditional dances, by named example: tari totokng (West Kalimantan), tari jepin selendang (Pontianak, West Kalimantan), tari glipang (Probolinggo, East Java) and tari golek ayun-ayun (Central Java), studied from video and photos.

**Bab 1.** performing the movement varieties (ragam gerak) of a chosen traditional dance in groups.

**Unit Pembelajaran 1 Mengenal Bentuk Dan Teknik Gerak Tari Tradisional.** the forms and techniques of traditional dance movement through two named vocabularies — the basic movements of Tari Sunda (Godeg, Gilek, Ukel Tumpang Tali, Tepak Bahu, Nyawang, Lontang Kembar, Duduk Deku, Adeg-Adeg Sembada, Trisi, each pictured) and of Tari Melayu (Lenggang, Mak Inang and Joged basics for girls and boys, and Serampang 12) — with analysis of Malay movement and a comparison of Sunda and Melayu movement.

**Unit 1.** the archipelago's dance kinds pictured and compared — Tari Primitif, Tari Klasik, Tari Rakyat, Tari Bali, Tari Gendhing Sriwijaya, Tari Jawa and Tari Dayak.

**Bab II.** performing the basic movements of traditional dance (unjuk kerja) with attention to the main elements, as the chapter's summative performance.

That is Indonesian Traditional Dances — Indonesia's way, kept by name.
