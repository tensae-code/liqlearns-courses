# Indian Dance Heritage of Malaysia — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Malaysia's own forms in this lesson are...

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** The last sub lesson is...
a) The Indian dance heritage of Malaysia taken whole  b) the bibliography  c) a review box
**Answer: a) The Indian dance heritage of Malaysia taken whole.**
