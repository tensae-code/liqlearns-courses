# Fiji Traditional Songs and Dances — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How are traditional dance movements learned in Year 1?
**Answer: Observe them, then imitate them.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name two Fijian dances in this lesson.
**Answer: Any two of: Seasea, Meke iWau, Meke Wesi (Island dance and Indian dance also named).**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Where is a learned cultural song performed?
**Answer: At a class concert or school gathering.**
