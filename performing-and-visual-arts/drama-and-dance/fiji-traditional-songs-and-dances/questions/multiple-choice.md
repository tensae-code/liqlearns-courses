# Fiji Traditional Songs and Dances — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Fiji Day singing includes songs of…
a) iTaukei, Hindi and Fiji's other ethnic groups  b) only one group  c) no groups
**Answer: a) iTaukei, Hindi and Fiji's other ethnic groups.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Meke iWau and Meke Wesi are…
a) Fijian dances  b) songs only  c) instruments
**Answer: a) Fijian dances.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Dances are first identified from…
a) photographs  b) maps  c) recipes
**Answer: a) photographs.**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Chamak Raha Fiji is…
a) a Hindi patriotic song praising Fiji  b) a Rotuman song  c) a dance
**Answer: a) a Hindi patriotic song praising Fiji as homeland.**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Ma Ae La Fu'uou is…
a) a Rotuman song  b) an iTaukei song  c) a Hindi song
**Answer: a) a Rotuman song.**
