# Fiji Traditional Songs and Dances

## Do it first

Quick start: from the title alone, guess what this lesson's work is. Then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — Traditional songs of Fiji's peoples

For **Fiji Day** the class sings **traditional iTaukei songs, traditional Hindi songs and songs of Fiji's other ethnic groups**, with percussion — and **observes, then imitates, traditional dance movements**. (Teacher-guide evidence, Year 1.)

## Sub lesson 2 — Dances and costumes of Fiji's other cultures

Fiji's cultural dances are identified **from photographs** — **Island dance, Indian dance, Seasea, Meke iWau and Meke Wesi** — and each item's **features, movements and attire** are described.

## Sub lesson 3 — Songs of Fiji's other cultures

Learners **learn a cultural song of their choice** and perform a cultural dance or song at a class concert or school gathering. The book's named examples are placed by name and purpose only: the iTaukei song **Koi Au Na Veli**, **Hukilau (Yavirau)**, the Hindi patriotic song **Chamak Raha Fiji** praising Fiji as homeland, and the Rotuman song **Ma Ae La Fu'uou**.
