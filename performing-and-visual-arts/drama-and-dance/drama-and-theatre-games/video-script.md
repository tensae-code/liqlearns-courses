# Video script — Drama and Theatre Games

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Two games that train every actor.

The chain: your sentence hooks onto the last — until a story stands.

The echo: a word, a movement — the whole circle copies it exactly.

And every new one different. Two trees — two different trees!

Games first. Theatre grows from them.
