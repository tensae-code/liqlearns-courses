# Drama and Theatre Games — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The chain association builds a story no one person wrote alone.
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In the echo play, copying approximately is enough.
**Answer: False — word and movement are imitated exactly.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** "I got up at seven" can open a chain association.
**Answer: True.**
