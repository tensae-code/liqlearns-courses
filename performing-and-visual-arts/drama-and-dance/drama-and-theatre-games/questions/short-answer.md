# Drama and Theatre Games — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How does a chain-association story get built?
**Answer: Each pupil's sentence hooks onto the last, until a whole story stands that everyone built.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does a player do in the echo play?
**Answer: Speaks a word and makes a movement with it, for the whole circle to imitate exactly.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What do the echo play's improvisations later become?
**Answer: The pupils' own koti — their joining-in pieces inside a Mato performance.**
