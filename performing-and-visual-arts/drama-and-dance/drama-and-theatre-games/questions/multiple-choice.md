# Drama and Theatre Games — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In the chain association, each sentence…
a) starts a new story  b) hooks onto the last  c) must rhyme
**Answer: b) hooks onto the last**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In the echo play, the circle…
a) invents a different movement  b) imitates the word and movement exactly  c) stays silent
**Answer: b) imitates the word and movement exactly**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Two trees in the echo play must be…
a) identical  b) two different trees — a kankantrie and a parwa  c) drawn, not moved
**Answer: b) two different trees — a kankantrie and a parwa**
