# Drama and Theatre Games

## Do it first

Quick start: from the title alone, guess what this lesson's work is. Then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — The chain association

In the **chain association (kettingassociatie)**, each pupil's sentence **hooks onto the last** — "I got up at seven" → "I made tea at once" → "because I love tea and bread in the morning" — until **a whole story stands that everyone built**.

## Sub lesson 2 — The echo play

In the **echo play (echospel)**, a pupil **speaks a word and makes a movement with it** and the **whole circle imitates word and movement exactly**; each new word and movement is **different** — two trees must be **two different trees, a kankantrie and a parwa**. The echo play's improvisations later become the pupils' own **koti** — their joining-in pieces inside a Mato performance.
