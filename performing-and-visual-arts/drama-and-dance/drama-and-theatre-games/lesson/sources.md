| Suriname — Theatre Les 2 "Speltraining: het hoofdverhaal en de rolverdeling" (The Main Story and the Casting): two improvisation games — the chain association (kettingassociatie), in which each pupil's sentence hooks onto the last — "I got up at seven" → "I made tea at once" → "because I love tea and bread in the morning" — until a whole story stands that everyone built; and the echo play (echospel), in which a pupil speaks a word and makes a movement with it and the whole circle imitates word and movement exactly, each new word and movement different — two trees must be two different trees, a kankantrie and a parwa; the echo play's improvisations later become the pupils' own koti (teacher guide — evidence; original target `drama-and-dance/chain-association-and-echo-play`) | Book 1, printed pp. 179–181 |
