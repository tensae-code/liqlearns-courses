# Hands-on — Drama and Theatre Games

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Play the chain
Sit in a circle. One learner starts a story with one sentence; each next learner hooks one sentence onto the last, until the story stands. No breaking the chain.

## Play the echo
One learner speaks a word with a movement; the whole circle echoes word and movement exactly. Each new turn must be different — two trees, two different trees.
