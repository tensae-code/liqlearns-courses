# Hands-on — Renaissance Theatre

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Teatro renacentista
Work through this part with real materials or your own body, the way the book sets it: Renaissance theatre — movable-type printing spreading plays; new theatre buildings; court theatre reaching the people; pastoral comedy; curtains for scene changes and perspective scenery; verse plays; Leone de' Sommi's theory of staging (actor and character, voice, gesture, lighting); Shakespeare's tragedies and comedies. Show a companion what you made or did, and say what the part teaches.
