# Video script — Renaissance Theatre

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

**Teatro renacentista.** Renaissance theatre — movable-type printing spreading plays; new theatre buildings; court theatre reaching the people; pastoral comedy; curtains for scene changes and perspective scenery; verse plays; Leone de' Sommi's theory of staging (actor and character, voice, gesture, lighting); Shakespeare's tragedies and comedies.

That is Renaissance Theatre — Venezuela's own books teach it this way.
