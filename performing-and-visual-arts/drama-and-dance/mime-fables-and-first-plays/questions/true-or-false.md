# Mime, Fables and First Plays — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The named play is placed by name and purpose, not transcribed.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Mime is named as a skill after the fable plays in the guide.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Silent drama (ድምፅ አልባ ተውኔት) is soundless theatre.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** A single prop can be enough to improvise a whole scene.
**Answer: True**
