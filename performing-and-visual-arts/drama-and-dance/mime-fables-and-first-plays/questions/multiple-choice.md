# Mime, Fables and First Plays — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Grade 3 guide introduces drama by…
  a) Performing and reading a named play in class b) Only defining it in a dictionary c) Watching television
**Answer: a) Performing and reading a named play in class** — drama is met through the class play 'ቤት የለሽ ጨዋታ'.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The first fable play is about…
  a) The monkey's judgement b) A monkey's wedding c) A monkey's journey to market
**Answer: a) The monkey's judgement** — 'ስለ ጦጣ ፍርድ' is about the monkey's judgement.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A monologue (መነባነብ) is…
  a) One actor speaking alone b) A play with no voice at all c) A dance in a circle
**Answer: a) One actor speaking alone** — the monologue is speaking alone; silent drama is the no-voice play.

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Improvisation (ድንገቴ ፈጠራ) means…
  a) Creating on the spot b) Memorising for a month c) Reading from a script
**Answer: a) Creating on the spot** — ድንገቴ ፈጠራ is sudden creation — performing invented on the spot.
