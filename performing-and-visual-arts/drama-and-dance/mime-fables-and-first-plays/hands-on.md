# Hands-on — Mime, Fables and First Plays

The lesson with your hands, your feet and your voice together, not only your eyes.

## Invisible rope tug
In pairs, mime pulling an invisible rope against each other — lean, strain, hand over hand, the guide's own GIF need performed live. The watcher judges: which side is winning, and how do you know?

## Fable masks
Make simple masks or headbands for the lion, the leopard, the hyena and the donkey. Act the companionship or the judgement in gesture first, words after.
