# Video script — Mime, Fables and First Plays

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Drama begins with a story acted, not only told.
Take away the words. The body still speaks.
The mime lifts what is not there — and you believe him.
A fable puts animal faces on human lessons.
One voice alone on stage: that is a monologue.
