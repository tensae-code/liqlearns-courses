# Video script — Euripides' Helen

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is Euripides' Helen.

Euripides' tragedy Helen studied in full — prologue, parodos and episodes. — as Greece's school books teach it: Helen herself tells her sufferings — the judgement of the three goddesses, the phantom, Menelaus gone to war for a shadow; Teucer's news; then the chorus joins her lament for relentless fate.

Watch, listen — then say it back in your own words.
