# Euripides' Helen

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about euripides' helen. Then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — Πρόλογος και Πάροδος (στ. 1-436) (Prologue and Parodos)

Helen herself tells her sufferings — the judgement of the three goddesses, the phantom, Menelaus gone to war for a shadow; Teucer's news; then the chorus joins her lament for relentless fate

## Sub lesson 2 — Α΄ Επεισόδιο και Β΄ Επεισόδιο (στ. 437-1219) (Episodes 1-2)

Menelaus, Troy's conqueror, arrives a ragged shipwreck; the recognition of husband and wife; from recognition to the escape plan — the prophetess Theonoe's choice

## Sub lesson 3 — Στάσιμα (στ. 1220-1285, 1425-1499, 1593-1652) (The chorus songs)

the chorus sings between the acts — the futility of war and the gods' unexplored will; the Mother goddess (Demeter) myth, where reversal restores balance; the chorus' wishes for the escaping ship

## Sub lesson 4 — Γ΄ και Δ΄ Επεισόδιο (στ. 1286-1592) (Episodes 3-4)

the invented plan is put into practice — Helen tricks King Theoclymenus with a fake funeral rite at sea for the "dead" Menelaus; the machinery of salvation is completed

## Sub lesson 5 — Έξοδος (στ. 1653-1870) (Exodus)

the messenger reports the escape and the fight aboard; Theoclymenus rages; the Dioscuri appear as gods from the machine and close the story
