# Ballet — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Ballet tells its story through…
a) spoken words  b) dance  c) paintings
**Answer: b) dance — a story set to dance.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A ballet's music is played by…
a) one piano  b) a full live orchestra  c) no musicians
**Answer: b) a full live orchestra.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** In The Nutcracker, Clara visits…
a) the Land of Sweets  b) the Mountain King's hall  c) a village festa
**Answer: a) the Land of Sweets.**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Character and feeling in ballet are carried by the dancers'…
a) costumes only  b) grace and technique  c) singing
**Answer: b) grace and technique.**
