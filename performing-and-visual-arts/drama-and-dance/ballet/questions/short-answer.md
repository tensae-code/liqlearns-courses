# Ballet — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name two things besides dance that a ballet uses to tell its story.
**Answer: Any two of: costumes, scenery, props, music (also plot and characters).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Who is the Sugar Plum Fairy in the story's world?
**Answer: A figure of the Land of Sweets in The Nutcracker.**
