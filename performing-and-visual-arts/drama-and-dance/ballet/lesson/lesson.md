# Ballet

## Do it first

Quick start: say one thing you already know — a sound, a song, a name — that belongs to this lesson's world. Keep it in mind; the parts will meet it.

## Sub lesson 1 — The Ballet and Tchaikovsky's Nutcracker

**Ballet** is a **story set to dance**, told like a play with **plot and sub-plot, main and secondary characters, costumes, scenery and props**; its music is written for it and played by a **full live orchestra**, and the dancers' **grace and technique** carry character and feeling. The guide retells **The Nutcracker** in full — Clara, the broken Nutcracker, the Mouse King battle, the Land of Sweets and the Sugar Plum Fairy — placed here by summary only.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the parts in order, one sentence each. Companion checks: did every part get its sentence, and did the order hold?

## Do It Yourself

1. Write the parts' names from memory, in order.
2. Do the hands-on activity once more, teaching it to someone else.
3. Write one sentence: what is the one thing this lesson teaches?
