This is Moldova's own lesson.

**the sîrba in "Sîrba în căruță".** Theme 2, the sîrba in "Sîrba în căruță" — the sîrba, a very fast Romanian folk dance in a circle or semicircle, hands on neighbours' shoulders, sung and stepped by two groups in turn.

**Constantin Rusnac's "Sărbătoreasca".** Theme 3, Constantin Rusnac's "Sărbătoreasca" — a concert piece ("Poem about Moldova") for folk orchestra whose outer parts dance the sîrba around a quiet, tender middle melody.

**Dansul popular.** Unit 2, Dansul popular — the sârba and its regional styles across Moldova.

That is Sîrba Dance — Moldova's way, kept by name.
