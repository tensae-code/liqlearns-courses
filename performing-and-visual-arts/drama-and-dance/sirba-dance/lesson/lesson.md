# Sîrba Dance

## Do it first

Name one thing that belongs in this lesson — Sîrba, the fast Moldovan and Romanian line dance. Say what it is in your own words. If you can name one, you are already inside the lesson.

## Sub lesson 1 — the sîrba in "Sîrba în căruță"

Theme 2, the sîrba in "Sîrba în căruță" — the sîrba, a very fast Romanian folk dance in a circle or semicircle, hands on neighbours' shoulders, sung and stepped by two groups in turn.

## Sub lesson 2 — Constantin Rusnac's "Sărbătoreasca"

Theme 3, Constantin Rusnac's "Sărbătoreasca" — a concert piece ("Poem about Moldova") for folk orchestra whose outer parts dance the sîrba around a quiet, tender middle melody.

## Sub lesson 3 — Dansul popular

Unit 2, Dansul popular — the sârba and its regional styles across Moldova.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson holds, part by part, in your own words. Companion checks: did every part get named, with its Moldovan name kept where the book gives one?

## Do It Yourself

1. Choose one named part of this lesson and find out one more true thing about it — from a person who knows it, or from the book your part came from.
2. Make or perform a small piece in the way of that part: a rhythm, a step, a drawing, a shaped piece of clay.
3. Write one sentence: what does this part show about Moldova's own way of teaching its arts?
