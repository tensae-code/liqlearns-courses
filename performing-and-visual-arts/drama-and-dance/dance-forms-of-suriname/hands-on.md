# Hands-on — Dance Forms of Suriname

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Name the family
In a small group, sort the named forms into two piles: the dance-school forms and Suriname's own traditions. Say one occasion — a feast, a ritual, a religious event — where a dance from the second pile belongs.

## Show a greeting step
Each learner shows one short movement from a dance of their own family or people, and the group answers by copying it with respect.
