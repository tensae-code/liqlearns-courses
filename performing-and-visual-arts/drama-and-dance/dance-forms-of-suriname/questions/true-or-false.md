# Dance Forms of Suriname — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Marron dances are one of Suriname's own traditions.
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Dance in Suriname belongs to one people only.
**Answer: False — it belongs to the cultures of all Suriname's peoples.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Kathak is named among Suriname's own traditions.
**Answer: True.**
