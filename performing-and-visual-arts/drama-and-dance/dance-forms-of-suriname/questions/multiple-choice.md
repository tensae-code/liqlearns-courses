# Dance Forms of Suriname — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Dance in Suriname is inseparable from…
a) the weather  b) the cultures of Suriname's peoples  c) examinations
**Answer: b) the cultures of Suriname's peoples**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which list holds only Suriname's own traditions named in the book?
a) jazz, urban, latin  b) pentjak silat, bharatanatyam, kathak, Marron and indigenous dances  c) classical, modern, contemporary
**Answer: b) pentjak silat, bharatanatyam, kathak, Marron and indigenous dances**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Suriname's peoples' ancestors brought their dance cultures…
a) from one village  b) from many parts of the world  c) from the moon
**Answer: b) from many parts of the world**
