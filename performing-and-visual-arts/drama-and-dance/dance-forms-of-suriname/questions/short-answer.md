# Dance Forms of Suriname — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name three occasions where Suriname's dances are danced.
**Answer: Feasts, rituals and religious events.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the six dance-school forms.
**Answer: Classical, modern, contemporary, jazz, urban and latin.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Name two of Suriname's own dance traditions.
**Answer: Any two of: pentjak silat, bharatanatyam, kathak, Marron dances, indigenous dances.**
