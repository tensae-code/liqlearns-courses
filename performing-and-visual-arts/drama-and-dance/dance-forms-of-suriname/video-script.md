# Video script — Dance Forms of Suriname

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Dance came to Suriname from many parts of the world — and stayed.

Classical, modern, contemporary. Jazz, urban, latin.

And Suriname's own: pentjak silat. Bharatanatyam. Kathak.

Marron dances. Indigenous dances.

Danced at feasts, in rituals, at religious events.

Dance is inseparable from the cultures of Suriname's peoples.
