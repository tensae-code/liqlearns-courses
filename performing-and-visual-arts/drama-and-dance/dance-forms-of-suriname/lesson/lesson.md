## Sub lesson 1 — Dance belongs to Suriname's peoples

**Dance is inseparable from the cultures of Suriname's peoples**, whose ancestors brought their own dance cultures **from many parts of the world**. The dances live at **feasts, in rituals and at religious events**.

## Sub lesson 2 — The forms by name

The dance forms taught in Suriname's dance schools are named: **classical, modern, contemporary, jazz, urban and latin** — and the country's own traditions: **pentjak silat, bharatanatyam, kathak, Marron dances and indigenous dances**.
