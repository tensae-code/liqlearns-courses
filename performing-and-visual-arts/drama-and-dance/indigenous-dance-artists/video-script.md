# Video script — Indigenous Dance Artists

A short read-aloud script in a super-simple voice, made later as the lesson's video. It follows the lesson's own order and names only what the lesson teaches.

---

Indigenous dance artists perform the dances of their cultures.
Hunting cultures, gathering cultures, farming cultures, herding cultures.
We identify Ethiopia's performers by discussion and by comparing dances on video.
The guide prints no single biography — the artists are known in their communities.
