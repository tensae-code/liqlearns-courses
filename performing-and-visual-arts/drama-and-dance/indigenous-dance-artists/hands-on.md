# Hands-on — Indigenous Dance Artists

Real things to do with what this lesson teaches — at school or at home, with people around you.

## Harvest dance picture-in-words
Learners describe — in words or drawing — dancers performing a harvest dance with farming tools: what tools, what movements do the tools give the dance?

## Community roll
The class builds a roll of their own area's traditional dance performers, from what families and elders say — the community's own identification, as the guide prescribes.
