# Lesson plan — Indigenous Dance Artists

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Drama and Dance · **Lesson:** Indigenous Dance Artists
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 4 — Drama and Dance (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** who indigenous dance artists are: the performers of the dances of hunting, gathering, agricultural and domesticated cultures — and how Ethiopia's indigenous dance performers are identified: by discussion and video comparison, with no single biography printed.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Dance Education and Indigenous Dance — the learner has studied dance by watching; this lesson names the artists themselves.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the
exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Dance Education and Indigenous Dance. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Define an indigenous dance artist.
2. Name the cultures whose dances they perform.
3. Say how Ethiopia's indigenous dance performers are identified.

## What you need
An open space for moving and acting. The pictures in the lesson. Nothing else.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — the learner names a person in their own area famed for a traditional dance, before 'indigenous dance artist' is defined (lesson/lesson.md).
2. **The explanation** — the definition first — performers of hunting, gathering, agricultural and domesticated cultures' dances — then how such artists are identified and studied (lesson/lesson.md).
   - (No picture slots beyond the described slots in the lesson text.)
3. **Say it to your companion** — Tell your companion who an indigenous dance artist is, and how the guide says to identify Ethiopia's.
4. **Questions** — from questions/, served at the learner's level (levels.md).
5. **Hands-on** — hands-on.md.
6. **Do It Yourself** — Ask in your community who performs its traditional dances best, and write down what people say makes them artists.
