# Indigenous Dance Artists — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Indigenous dance artists are —
a) stage managers  b) performers of the dances of hunting, gathering, agricultural and domesticated cultures  c) costume sellers
**Answer: b) performers of the dances of hunting, gathering, agricultural and domesticated cultures.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A farming culture's dance artist performs —
a) its harvest dances  b) only foreign dances  c) no dances
**Answer: a) its harvest dances.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The community trusts the artist to —
a) do the dance truly  b) own the dance alone  c) stop the dance
**Answer: a) do the dance truly.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Ethiopia's indigenous dance performers are identified by —
a) examination  b) discussion and video comparison  c) lottery
**Answer: b) discussion and video comparison.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The guide prints, for this topic —
a) ten biographies  b) no single artist biography  c) a king list
**Answer: b) no single artist biography.**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Artists emerge, in this method, by —
a) their mastery, seen in the dancing  b) their wealth  c) their age alone
**Answer: a) their mastery, seen in the dancing.**
