# Video script — Indigenous Knowledge on Stage and Screen

A short read-aloud script in a super-simple voice, made later as the lesson's video. It follows the lesson's own order and names only what the lesson teaches.

---

Indigenous knowledge is a community's own knowledge for daily life.
It is not taught to the people — it is learned from the people, handed down.
Writers and theatre makers use it as a starting point: terraces, obelisks, medicines, reconciliation.
Old knowledge becomes new theatre and new film.
