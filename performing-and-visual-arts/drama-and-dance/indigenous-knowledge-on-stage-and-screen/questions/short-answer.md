# Indigenous Knowledge on Stage and Screen — short answer

Answer in your own words, in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why is indigenous knowledge called a foundation of a country's existence?
**Answer: Because communities live by it daily — it carries how they farm, build, heal and settle their affairs.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name two engineering treasures the book names.
**Answer: The Axum obelisks and the Lalibela rock churches (also: Konso terracing, traditional metalwork).**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name three kinds of makers who draw on indigenous knowledge.
**Answer: Film writers, book writers, history researchers and theatre makers (any three).**
