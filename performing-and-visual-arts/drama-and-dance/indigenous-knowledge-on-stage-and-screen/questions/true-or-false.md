# Indigenous Knowledge on Stage and Screen — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Indigenous knowledge exists only in books. True or false?
**Answer: False — it lives in the people and is handed down.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Reconciliation systems — how a community makes peace — count as indigenous knowledge. True or false?
**Answer: True.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Indigenous knowledge can become new theatre and film. True or false?
**Answer: True.**
