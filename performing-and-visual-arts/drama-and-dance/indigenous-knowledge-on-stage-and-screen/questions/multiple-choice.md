# Indigenous Knowledge on Stage and Screen — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Indigenous knowledge is —
a) knowledge imported from abroad  b) the cultural knowledge communities use to ease daily life  c) only written knowledge
**Answer: b) the cultural knowledge communities use to ease daily life.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** It is handed down —
a) by examinations  b) generation to generation  c) by shops
**Answer: b) generation to generation.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Its direction is unusual: it is —
a) taught to the people by experts  b) learned from the people  c) kept in one book
**Answer: b) learned from the people.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which belongs to indigenous knowledge?
a) traditional medicines  b) imported factory manuals  c) television schedules
**Answer: a) traditional medicines.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The Konso are named for —
a) terracing and soil protection  b) ship building  c) glass making
**Answer: a) terracing and soil protection.**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Telling seasons and times is —
a) part of the knowledge  b) outside it  c) a modern invention only
**Answer: a) part of the knowledge.**

**7. (Sub lesson 3)** Writers and theatre makers use indigenous knowledge as —
a) decoration only  b) a starting point and reference  c) something to hide
**Answer: b) a starting point and reference.**

**8. (Sub lesson 3)** The visual arts named for carrying this knowledge are —
a) film and theatre  b) posters only  c) radio only
**Answer: a) film and theatre.**

**9. (Sub lesson 3)** A play teaches the knowledge by —
a) listing rules  b) showing the life — a terrace built, a quarrel settled  c) testing the audience
**Answer: b) showing the life — a terrace built, a quarrel settled.**
