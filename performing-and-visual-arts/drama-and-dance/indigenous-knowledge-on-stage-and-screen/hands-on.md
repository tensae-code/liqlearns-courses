# Hands-on — Indigenous Knowledge on Stage and Screen

Real things to do with what this lesson teaches — at school or at home, with people around you.

## Knowledge inventory
Groups list pieces of knowledge held by their own communities — farming, building, healing, settling quarrels — and check: who holds it, and how was it handed down?

## From knowledge to scene
Each group picks one item — the Konso terracing, for example — and outlines a two-minute scene that would teach it to an audience.
