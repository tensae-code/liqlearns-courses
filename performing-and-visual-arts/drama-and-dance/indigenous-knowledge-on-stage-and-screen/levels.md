# Indigenous Knowledge on Stage and Screen — sub lessons and depth

This is the depth record for the lesson, re-expressed as sub lessons: each sub lesson is one step up in depth, proved before the next opens.

## Sub lesson 1 — What indigenous knowledge is
Pass mark: the learner can define indigenous knowledge, including its direction — learned from the people. The gate draws from this sub lesson's whole question set.

## Sub lesson 2 — What the knowledge includes
Pass mark: the learner can name at least four things indigenous knowledge includes. The gate draws from this sub lesson's whole question set.

## Sub lesson 3 — How stage and screen use it
Pass mark: the learner can say who uses indigenous knowledge to create, and how. The gate draws from this sub lesson's whole question set.
