# Indigenous Knowledge on Stage and Screen

## Do it first
An elder near you knows things no textbook printed: when rain is coming, how to settle a quarrel, how a hillside is kept from washing away. Where did that knowledge come from — and can a play carry it? This lesson says yes, and shows how.

## Sub lesson 1 — What indigenous knowledge is

**Indigenous knowledge** is the cultural knowledge communities use to ease the ups and downs of daily life — a foundation of a country's existence.

It has one strange, beautiful direction: it is **not taught to the people — it is learned from the people**, and handed down **generation to generation**. Its school is life itself, and its teachers are the community.

## Sub lesson 2 — What the knowledge includes

The knowledge is wide. It includes **tool-making methods** and farm-implement making and use; **traditional medicines**; **handcraft products**; ancient **star and space study**; **telling seasons and times**; ancient **philosophy**; and **reconciliation systems** — how a community makes peace.

Named treasures the makers combine include the **Konso terracing and soil-protection method**, the engineering craft of the **Axum obelisks** and **Lalibela rock churches**, traditional **metal melting and metalwork**, body adornment and pottery.

## Sub lesson 3 — How stage and screen use it

Who uses this knowledge to create? **Film and book writers, history researchers and theatre makers** take it as their **starting point and reference** to create new things and pass them on.

That is why the book names **film and theatre** for this work: a play can *show* a terrace being built, a medicine being prepared, a quarrel being settled — and a whole audience learns the community's knowledge by watching its own life honoured on stage.

## Say it to your companion
Tell your companion: what is indigenous knowledge, and name two things it includes.

## Do It Yourself
Choose one piece of your community's knowledge — a terrace method, a medicine, a reconciliation custom — and write how a play could show it.
