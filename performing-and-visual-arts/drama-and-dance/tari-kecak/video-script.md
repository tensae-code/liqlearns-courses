# Video script — Tari Kecak

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This is Indonesia's own lesson.

**Bab I.** Tari Kecak of Bali as the worked example — watching the Kecak performance and writing the meaning contained in the dance.

**Bab 2, Tabel 2.4.** Tari Kecak for the youngest learners — exploring it by video, expressing it by imitating the dancers' movements, sounds and facial expressions and performing it for another class, and appreciating it by saying what was liked, such as its costumes.

That is Tari Kecak — Indonesia's way, kept by name.
