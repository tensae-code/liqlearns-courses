# Lesson plan — Tari Kecak

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Drama and Dance · **Lesson:** Tari Kecak
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 4 — Drama and Dance (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Kecak, Bali's chanting dance-drama performed by a chorus of men around a fire.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the course's earlier lessons in its title; recommended first, never forced.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of ..." in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Bab I** — Tari Kecak of Bali as the worked example — watching the Kecak performance and writing the meaning contained in the dance
2. **Bab 2, Tabel 2.4** — Tari Kecak for the youngest learners — exploring it by video, expressing it by imitating the dancers' movements, sounds and facial expressions and performing it for another class, and appreciating it by saying what was liked, such as its costumes

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of this title. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name this lesson's parts, with their Indonesian names kept.
2. Say what each part is and where in Indonesia it lives.

## What you need
Your voice, your hands, and a companion to answer you.

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks Indonesia's arts are copies of other countries' → they are the country's own: named forms, kept in the country's own books, taught in the country's own way.
