# Tari Kecak

## Do it first

Name one thing that belongs in this lesson — Kecak, Bali's chanting dance-drama performed by a chorus of men around a fire. Say what it is in your own words. If you can name one, you are already inside the lesson.

## Sub lesson 1 — Bab I

Tari Kecak of Bali as the worked example — watching the Kecak performance and writing the meaning contained in the dance.

## Sub lesson 2 — Bab 2, Tabel 2.4

Tari Kecak for the youngest learners — exploring it by video, expressing it by imitating the dancers' movements, sounds and facial expressions and performing it for another class, and appreciating it by saying what was liked, such as its costumes.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson holds, part by part, in your own words. Companion checks: did every part get named, with its Indonesian name kept where the book gives one?

## Do It Yourself

1. Choose one named part of this lesson and find out one more true thing about it — from a person who knows it, or from the book your part came from.
2. Make or perform a small piece in the way of that part: a rhythm, a step, a drawing, a shaped piece of clay.
3. Write one sentence: what does this part show about Indonesia's own way of teaching its arts?
