# Ethiopian Traditional Dances — sub lessons and depth

This is the depth record for the lesson, re-expressed as sub lessons: each sub lesson is one step up in depth, proved before the next opens.

## Sub lesson 1 — Traditional dances defined — sacred and secular
Pass mark: the learner can sort dances into sacred and secular, naming the secular occasions. The gate draws from this sub lesson's whole question set.

## Sub lesson 2 — Amhara dances by district
Pass mark: the learner can match at least four districts to their named dances, including minjar for North Shewa. The gate draws from this sub lesson's whole question set.

## Sub lesson 3 — The nation's dances
Pass mark: the learner can name at least two dances each for Oromo, Tigray and Somali. The gate draws from this sub lesson's whole question set.

## Sub lesson 4 — Where dances come from
Pass mark: the learner can give two sources of dance movements, with the Gondar chicken example. The gate draws from this sub lesson's whole question set.
