# Ethiopian Traditional Dances — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A mourning dance can be a traditional dance. True or false?
**Answer: True — grief is one of its occasions.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Men's and women's dance styles can differ. True or false?
**Answer: True.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Every Ethiopian people dances the same single dance. True or false?
**Answer: False — each people names its own dances.**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Dance movements are invented from nothing. True or false?
**Answer: False — they grow from nature, animals and daily work.**
