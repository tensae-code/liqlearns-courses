# Ethiopian Traditional Dances — short answer

Answer in your own words, in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How do you tell a sacred dance from a secular one?
**Answer: By its setting: sacred dances belong to faith and worship; secular dances belong to life's occasions such as joy, grief, heroism and hunting.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name Gojjam's two named dances.
**Answer: Netela and zinab inqiṭqiṭ.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name two dances of the South as the book lists them.
**Answer: Any two of: Silo, Lembo, Wolayta, Alaba, Mezhenger, Dorze.**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Which daily works stand behind the Gurage, Gamo and Minjar dances?
**Answer: Horse-riding, hunting and farming.**
