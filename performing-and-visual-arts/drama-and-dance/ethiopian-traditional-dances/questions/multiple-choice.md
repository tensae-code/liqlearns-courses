# Ethiopian Traditional Dances — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Traditional dances are performed for —
a) social and religious ceremonies  b) exercise only  c) school exams
**Answer: a) social and religious ceremonies.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Sacred dances belong to —
a) weddings  b) the faith branches, as part of worship  c) hunting
**Answer: b) the faith branches, as part of worship.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Which set names secular occasions?
a) joy, grief, heroism, hunting  b) sleeping, eating, reading  c) planting only
**Answer: a) joy, grief, heroism, hunting.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** North Shewa's named dances include —
a) timtim  b) wancha liqliqa and minjar  c) laleguma
**Answer: b) wancha liqliqa and minjar.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Timtim belongs to —
a) Wollo  b) Gondar  c) Raya
**Answer: a) Wollo.**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Wag Himra is known for —
a) men's leaping dance  b) women's eskista  c) a drum dance
**Answer: b) women's eskista.**

**7. (Sub lesson 3)** Regeda and Shegoye are dances of —
a) Tigray  b) the Oromo  c) the Somali
**Answer: b) the Oromo.**

**8. (Sub lesson 3)** Tembien and Enderta belong to —
a) Tigray  b) the South  c) Gurage
**Answer: a) Tigray.**

**9. (Sub lesson 3)** Buranbur and Danto are —
a) Amhara dances  b) Somali dances  c) church dances only
**Answer: b) Somali dances.**

**10. (Sub lesson 4)** The Gondar dance's movement grows from —
a) a chicken drinking water — head down, then neck up  b) a river flowing  c) a drum being made
**Answer: a) a chicken drinking water — head down, then neck up.**

**11. (Sub lesson 4)** Bird movement is the source of —
a) a Tigray dance and Sidama Lembo  b) Wollo timtim  c) only wedding dances
**Answer: a) a Tigray dance and Sidama Lembo.**

**12. (Sub lesson 4)** Dance movements grow from —
a) nature, animals and daily work  b) books only  c) foreign stages
**Answer: a) nature, animals and daily work.**
