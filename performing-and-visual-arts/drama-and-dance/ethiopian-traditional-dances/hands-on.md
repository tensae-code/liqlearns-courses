# Hands-on — Ethiopian Traditional Dances

Real things to do with what this lesson teaches — at school or at home, with people around you.

## Occasion sort
Learners list dances they know and sort them: sacred or secular? joy, grief, heroism or hunting? Compare lists — the same dance can greet more than one occasion.

## Copy the source
Show the Gondar source: a chicken drinking — head down, then neck up. Learners try the movement gently, then watch how a life-movement becomes a dance movement.
