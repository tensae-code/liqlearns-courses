# Video script — Ethiopian Traditional Dances

A short read-aloud script in a super-simple voice, made later as the lesson's video. It follows the lesson's own order and names only what the lesson teaches.

---

Traditional dances are danced for ceremonies — sacred and secular.
Sacred dances belong to the faiths. Secular dances belong to occasions: joy, grief, heroism, hunting.
Every people has its named dances. And every movement was copied from life — from birds, from work, from horses.
