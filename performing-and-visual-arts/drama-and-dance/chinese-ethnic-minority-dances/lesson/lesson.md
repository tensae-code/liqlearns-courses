# Chinese Ethnic Minority Dances

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about chinese ethnic minority dances. Then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — China Grade 3 Music Vol.1

Axi Jumping at the Moon — the Yi people's favourite song-and-dance, heard in a folk-ensemble arrangement; clapping and kicking on the accents and learning Yi dance steps; a Tibetan folk song and the Tibetan tap dance (duixie) — a self-entertaining group song-and-dance of the Tibetan region

## Sub lesson 2 — China Grade 4 Music Vol.1

A song for the Dai people's Water-Splashing Festival (the Dai New Year), and learning Dai dance movements to perform with the music

## Sub lesson 3 — China Grade 4 Music Vol.2

The bamboo-pole dance — a self-entertaining dance of China's southern peoples: dancers step and turn between long bamboo poles that open and close to the beat (open-close, open-close, open-open-close)

## Sub lesson 4 — China Grade 5 Music (jianpu) Vol.1

Duixie, the Tibetan folk dance of kicking and stamping steps — "the Tibetans' tap dance" — learning basic duixie steps and dancing them to the song

## Sub lesson 5 — China Grade 5 Music (jianpu) Vol.2

Guozhuang, the circle song-dance of the Tibetan, Qiang and other peoples, danced to the Qiang-flute song

## Sub lesson 6 — China Grade 6 Music Vol.2

The Love-My-China contest — matching songs and pieces to their peoples, naming instruments (morin khuur, frame drum, hulusi), and groups competing in the dances of China's peoples

## Sub lesson 7 — China HS Dance Performance

Dai dance — hand shapes (palm, claw, leaf, beak), foot positions (T, zigzag, crossed point), the knee's flex-and-stretch married to the breath, and the wing poses (spread, folded, high-folded); then the rehearsed excerpt of Spirit of the Peacock: holding the skirt to spin, the three-curves body, the peacock gazing into water, beak opening and closing, preening — Yang Liping's bird built from classroom steps; the minority-dance combination shelf — two Tibetan combinations, a Mongolian combination, two Uyghur combinations and a Korean combination, each a short full training dance in its people's movement language

## Sub lesson 8 — China Senior Music 5 Music & Dance

Unit 3 中国少数民族舞： China's minority peoples' dances — Tibetan and Mongolian dance combinations practised; Zhuang bronze-drum dance and Hui prop dance from 《复兴之路》 appreciated
