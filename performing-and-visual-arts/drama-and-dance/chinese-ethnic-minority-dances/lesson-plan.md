# Lesson plan — Chinese Ethnic Minority Dances

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Drama and Dance · **Lesson:** Chinese Ethnic Minority Dances
**Place in the curriculum:** Drama and Dance (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The dances of China's minority peoples, such as the Axi moon dance, each with its own music, dress and steps.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this course in the same title.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **China Grade 3 Music Vol.1** — Axi Jumping at the Moon — the Yi people's favourite song-and-dance, heard in a folk-ensemble arrangement; clapping and kicking on the accents and learning Yi dance steps; a Tibetan folk song and the Tibetan tap dance (duixie) — a self-entertaining group song-and-dance of the Tibetan region
2. **China Grade 4 Music Vol.1** — A song for the Dai people's Water-Splashing Festival (the Dai New Year), and learning Dai dance movements to perform with the music
3. **China Grade 4 Music Vol.2** — The bamboo-pole dance — a self-entertaining dance of China's southern peoples: dancers step and turn between long bamboo poles that open and close to the beat (open-close, open-close, open-open-close)
4. **China Grade 5 Music (jianpu) Vol.1** — Duixie, the Tibetan folk dance of kicking and stamping steps — "the Tibetans' tap dance" — learning basic duixie steps and dancing them to the song
5. **China Grade 5 Music (jianpu) Vol.2** — Guozhuang, the circle song-dance of the Tibetan, Qiang and other peoples, danced to the Qiang-flute song
6. **China Grade 6 Music Vol.2** — The Love-My-China contest — matching songs and pieces to their peoples, naming instruments (morin khuur, frame drum, hulusi), and groups competing in the dances of China's peoples
7. **China HS Dance Performance** — Dai dance — hand shapes (palm, claw, leaf, beak), foot positions (T, zigzag, crossed point), the knee's flex-and-stretch married to the breath, and the wing poses (spread, folded, high-folded); then the rehearsed excerpt of Spirit of the Peacock: holding the skirt to spin, the three-curves body, the peacock gazing into water, beak opening and closing, preening — Yang Liping's bird built from classroom steps; the minority-dance combination shelf — two Tibetan combinations, a Mongolian combination, two Uyghur combinations and a Korean combination, each a short full training dance in its people's movement language
8. **China Senior Music 5 Music & Dance** — Unit 3 中国少数民族舞： China's minority peoples' dances — Tibetan and Mongolian dance combinations practised; Zhuang bronze-drum dance and Hui prop dance from 《复兴之路》 appreciated
