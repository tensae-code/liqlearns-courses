# Video script — Chinese Ethnic Minority Dances

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is Chinese Ethnic Minority Dances.

The dances of China's minority peoples, such as the Axi moon dance, each with its own music, dress and steps. — as China's school books teach it: Axi Jumping at the Moon — the Yi people's favourite song-and-dance, heard in a folk-ensemble arrangement; clapping and kicking on the accents and learning Yi dance steps; a Tibetan folk song and the Tibetan tap dance (duixie) — a self-entertaining group song-and-dance of the Tibetan region.

Watch, listen — then say it back in your own words.
