# Writing and Putting On a Play — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In the book's example, where does the telling open?
**Answer: With the wife sick in bed — the husband is brought in by flashback.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the three sources of play ideas.
**Answer: Personal experience; newspapers and magazines; other people's experiences.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What is the actor's first work in rehearsal?
**Answer: Repeated reading of the script, grasping its concept, message and atmosphere.**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Name the narrative arc's four parts.
**Answer: Exposition, rising action, climax, resolution.**

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** Instead of on-the-nose dialogue, the writer should write…
**Answer: Subtext and conflict.**

**6. (Sub lesson 6)** What is the film counterpart of the one-act play in the Grade 9 guide?
**Answer: The short film — its story structured and produced compactly.**
