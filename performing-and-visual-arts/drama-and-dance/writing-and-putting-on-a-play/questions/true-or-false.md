# Writing and Putting On a Play — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Flashback (ምልሰት) lets a play show an earlier event after a later one.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Newspapers and magazines are one of the three sources of play ideas.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Some short traditional pieces are performed unwritten, without a script.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** The rehearsal ladder begins with a table read, sitting down.
**Answer: True**

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** Characters are introduced in ALL CAPS with age and one trait.
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 6)** A one-act play is a short single-act stage work.
**Answer: True**
