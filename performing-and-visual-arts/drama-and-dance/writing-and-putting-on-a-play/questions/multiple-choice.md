# Writing and Putting On a Play — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A plot's events are linked by…
  a) Cause and effect, credibly b) Chance alone c) Alphabetical order
**Answer: a) Cause and effect, credibly** — each effect with its cause, in credible order.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A good script's language must be…
  a) Concise and clear, fitting each character b) Long and tangled to sound grand c) The same for every character
**Answer: a) Concise and clear, fitting each character** — concise, clear, short sentences, language fitting the personality.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A wristwatch used by a character is a…
  a) Personal prop b) Stage prop c) Hand prop
**Answer: a) Personal prop** — the book's four kinds: hand, personal, costume, stage — a watch is personal.

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Before a character's dialogue, the character's name is written…
  a) Centred and capitalised b) In small letters at the page foot c) Not written at all
**Answer: a) Centred and capitalised** — script format centres and capitalises the speaker's name.

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** A screenplay scene heading gives…
  a) EXT./INT., place, and DAY/NIGHT b) Only the date c) The actor's real name
**Answer: a) EXT./INT., place, and DAY/NIGHT** — headings run EXT./INT. + place + DAY/NIGHT (or CONTINUOUS).

**6. (Sub lesson 6)** The three acts run…
  a) Setup, confrontation, resolution b) Rehearsal, costume, opening c) Song, dance, song
**Answer: a) Setup, confrontation, resolution** — Act I setup, Act II confrontation, Act III resolution.

**7. (Sub lesson 7)** Theatre's key stages number —
a) five  b) two  c) nine
**Answer: a) five.**

**8. (Sub lesson 7)** Setting the direction includes —
a) only the poster  b) costumes, jewellery, make-up, musics, sounds, lights — and the budget  c) only casting
**Answer: b) costumes, jewellery, make-up, musics, sounds, lights — and the budget.**

**9. (Sub lesson 7)** Promotion work makes the theatre reach its audience through —
a) varied media — television, flyers  b) silence  c) word of one person
**Answer: a) varied media — television, flyers.**

**10. (Sub lesson 8)** ቃል ጥናት (word study) is —
a) reading aloud forever  b) putting the texts aside and rehearsing by memory  c) writing new lines
**Answer: b) putting the texts aside and rehearsing by memory.**

**11. (Sub lesson 8)** If words are lost while running the whole play, the rule is —
a) stop at once  b) use what you have and continue  c) start the play again
**Answer: b) use what you have and continue.**

**12. (Sub lesson 8)** The costume parade happens —
a) after the run ends  b) before the first technical rehearsal, under full stage light  c) at home
**Answer: b) before the first technical rehearsal, under full stage light.**

**13. (Sub lesson 9)** The model play 'ሰላም' is about —
a) what comes first — peace, or a full stomach  b) a wedding  c) a journey to market
**Answer: a) what comes first — peace, or a full stomach.**

**14. (Sub lesson 9)** Tewabech argues that —
a) work comes first  b) peace is the key thing — without it even going out and returning is hard  c) trade is everything
**Answer: b) peace is the key thing — without it even going out and returning is hard.**

**15. (Sub lesson 9)** The kebele in the play is called —
a) Andinet — 'Unity'  b) Mersa  c) Andasa
**Answer: a) Andinet — 'Unity'.**

**16. (Sub lesson 4)** At first reading, an actor meeting new words should —
a) worry and stop  b) not worry — acting feeling is not needed yet  c) skip the play
**Answer: b) not worry — acting feeling is not needed yet.**

**17. (Sub lesson 4)** The seated reading happens —
a) standing on stage  b) around a table, with the text  c) at home alone
**Answer: b) around a table, with the text.**

**18. (Sub lesson 4)** At the table, the actor makes sure of —
a) when he enters, from where, how he crosses and where he goes  b) ticket sales  c) the poster
**Answer: a) when he enters, from where, how he crosses and where he goes.**

**19. (Sub lesson 4)** The technical rehearsal evaluates —
a) curtain time, music entry, telephone ring, scene change, door opening  b) ticket prices  c) actors' ages
**Answer: a) curtain time, music entry, telephone ring, scene change, door opening.**

**20. (Sub lesson 4)** The final rehearsal is —
a) the whole theatre performed without interruption in full competence  b) a discussion only  c) the first reading
**Answer: a) the whole theatre performed without interruption in full competence.**

**21. (Sub lesson 4)** የስንብት ልምምድ (farewell rehearsal) marks —
a) the theatre's beginning  b) the theatre's end — actors wave and enter the curtain  c) the interval
**Answer: b) the theatre's end — actors wave and enter the curtain.**

**22. (Sub lesson 6)** The Grade 10 guide's Lessons 16–18 teach —
a) the full-length play, three-act structure and screenwriting vocabulary  b) only the one-act play  c) costume fitting
**Answer: a) the full-length play, three-act structure and screenwriting vocabulary.**

**23. (Sub lesson 6)** Three-act structure belongs to —
a) full-length theatre and film story structure  b) dance notation  c) poster design
**Answer: a) full-length theatre and film story structure.**

**24. (Sub lesson 6)** The screenwriting vocabulary here follows on from —
a) Grade 8 basics and Grade 9 one-act/short-film structures  b) Grade 1 songs  c) nothing
**Answer: a) Grade 8 basics and Grade 9 one-act/short-film structures.**
