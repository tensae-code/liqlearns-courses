# Writing and Putting On a Play — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A plot's events are linked by…
  a) Cause and effect, credibly b) Chance alone c) Alphabetical order
**Answer: a) Cause and effect, credibly** — each effect with its cause, in credible order.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A good script's language must be…
  a) Concise and clear, fitting each character b) Long and tangled to sound grand c) The same for every character
**Answer: a) Concise and clear, fitting each character** — concise, clear, short sentences, language fitting the personality.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A wristwatch used by a character is a…
  a) Personal prop b) Stage prop c) Hand prop
**Answer: a) Personal prop** — the book's four kinds: hand, personal, costume, stage — a watch is personal.

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Before a character's dialogue, the character's name is written…
  a) Centred and capitalised b) In small letters at the page foot c) Not written at all
**Answer: a) Centred and capitalised** — script format centres and capitalises the speaker's name.

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** A screenplay scene heading gives…
  a) EXT./INT., place, and DAY/NIGHT b) Only the date c) The actor's real name
**Answer: a) EXT./INT., place, and DAY/NIGHT** — headings run EXT./INT. + place + DAY/NIGHT (or CONTINUOUS).

**6. (Sub lesson 6)** The three acts run…
  a) Setup, confrontation, resolution b) Rehearsal, costume, opening c) Song, dance, song
**Answer: a) Setup, confrontation, resolution** — Act I setup, Act II confrontation, Act III resolution.
