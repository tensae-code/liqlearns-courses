# Hands-on — Writing and Putting On a Play

The lesson with your hands, your feet and your voice together, not only your eyes.

## Table read to blocking
Take one short scene. First the table read: sit and read it through. Then blocking: stand and fix where each actor stands and moves. Feel the play change between sitting and standing — the rehearsal ladder's first two rungs.

## Flashback staging
Stage the same tiny story twice: once in time order, once opening on the ending and flashing back. Let the audience say which telling pulled harder, and why.
