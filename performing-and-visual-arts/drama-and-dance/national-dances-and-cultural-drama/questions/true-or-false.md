# National Dances and Cultural Drama — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A country with many dances has many separate nations inside it. True or false?
**Answer: False — one nation can hold many peoples' dances.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Drama only entertains; it carries no culture. True or false?
**Answer: False — drama carries a people's way of life.**
