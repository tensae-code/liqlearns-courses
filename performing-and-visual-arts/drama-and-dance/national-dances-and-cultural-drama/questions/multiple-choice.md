# National Dances and Cultural Drama — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The country's dances, gathered together, show —
a) only one culture  b) one nation holding many cultures  c) no differences
**Answer: b) one nation holding many cultures.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Each dance in the family belongs to —
a) nobody  b) its people and its place  c) the capital only
**Answer: b) its people and its place.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Studying the dances together adds —
a) nothing  b) the picture of the whole country  c) only steps
**Answer: b) the picture of the whole country.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Drama's cultural aspect means —
a) drama is expensive  b) a play shows how a people live and what they honour  c) drama has no culture
**Answer: b) a play shows how a people live and what they honour.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** A community's truest drama often comes from —
a) foreign stories only  b) its own stories  c) no stories
**Answer: b) its own stories.**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Which of these can a play carry?
a) greetings and celebrations  b) nothing of daily life  c) only kings' stories
**Answer: a) greetings and celebrations.**
