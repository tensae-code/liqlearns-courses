# Hands-on — National Dances and Cultural Drama

Real things to do with what this lesson teaches — at school or at home, with people around you.

## Dance and custom pairs
Each learner names a dance and the custom or occasion it belongs to. The class builds a shared list — the nation's dances, with their occasions.

## Drama carries culture
In groups, act a tiny scene from your own culture's life — a coffee ceremony greeting, a market bargain — and name what custom the scene carries.
