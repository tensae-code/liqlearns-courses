# Video script — National Dances and Cultural Drama

A short read-aloud script in a super-simple voice, made later as the lesson's video. It follows the lesson's own order and names only what the lesson teaches.

---

One country, many dances — together they are the nation's dances.
Each dance carries its people's culture.
And drama, too, has a cultural face: it shows how a people live and what they honour.
