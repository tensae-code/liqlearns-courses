# What Theatre Is — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Theatre is —
a) art presented to an audience  b) a picture hung on a wall  c) a song sung alone
**Answer: a) art presented to an audience.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A play with no speaking at all —
a) is not theatre  b) can still be theatre, organised without voice  c) is only a dance
**Answer: b) can still be theatre, organised without voice.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Theatre is presented to —
a) nobody  b) the actors only  c) an audience
**Answer: c) an audience.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which of these lives inside theatre?
a) literature  b) farming  c) swimming
**Answer: a) literature.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The built place of a play belongs to which art inside theatre?
a) music  b) architecture  c) literature
**Answer: b) architecture.**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** A play holds —
a) only acting  b) many arts working as one  c) only music
**Answer: b) many arts working as one.**

**7. (Sub lesson 3)** The main organising role in theatre belongs to —
a) the audience  b) the director  c) one actor
**Answer: b) the director.**

**8. (Sub lesson 3)** The director holds together —
a) only the costumes  b) the whole work — actors, story, music and place  c) only the tickets
**Answer: b) the whole work — actors, story, music and place.**

**9. (Sub lesson 3)** A play feels like one whole thing because —
a) it is very short  b) the director organised it as one  c) the audience is quiet
**Answer: b) the director organised it as one.**
