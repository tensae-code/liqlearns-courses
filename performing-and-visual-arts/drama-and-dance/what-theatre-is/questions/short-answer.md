# What Theatre Is — short answer

Answer in your own words, in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In your own words, what is theatre?
**Answer: Art prepared and presented for an audience to watch.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name three arts that live inside one play.
**Answer: Any three of: literature, acting, music, drawing, architecture, sculpture.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the director do?
**Answer: Organises the whole play so its parts reach the audience as one work.**
