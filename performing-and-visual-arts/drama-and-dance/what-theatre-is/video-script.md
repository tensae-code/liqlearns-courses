# Video script — What Theatre Is

A short read-aloud script in a super-simple voice, made later as the lesson's video. It follows the lesson's own order and names only what the lesson teaches.

---

Theatre is art made for an audience.
It can speak, or it can be silent.
Inside one play live many arts: stories, acting, music, drawing, building and carving.
One person holds it all together — the director.
