# Hands-on — What Theatre Is

Real things to do with what this lesson teaches — at school or at home, with people around you.

## Name the arts inside a play
Watch a short school play or act a tiny scene with friends. Afterwards, list together: where were the words, the acting, the music, the drawing, the building?

## Silent theatre
In pairs, act a tiny moment — greeting a friend, buying bread — with no voice at all. Your partner says what the moment was. Theatre works with voice, and without voice.
