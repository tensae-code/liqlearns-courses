# Hands-on — Tableau: Living Pictures

The lesson with your whole body, not only your eyes.

## Freeze the scene
In a group, build a tableau of a scene in a financial institution — a teller, a customer, a guard, a person at a desk. Move into your places, then freeze on the signal. Hold the still picture while a watcher names what each figure is doing. If the watcher can read the scene, the tableau works.

## Track the thoughts
Hold the same tableau. A leader touches one frozen figure's shoulder; that figure — still frozen — speaks their thoughts aloud in one or two sentences. Touch each figure in turn. Then swap scenes and roles.
