# Tableau: Living Pictures — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A tableau is…
a) a song sung in a circle  b) a still picture made by people freezing in position  c) a dance with fast steps
**Answer: b) a still picture made by people freezing in position.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Jamaica's guide, tableaux are used to create scenes in…
a) a financial institution  b) a fishing boat  c) a market only
**Answer: a) a financial institution.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** While a tableau is held, the figures…
a) keep walking  b) talk to each other  c) stay frozen and silent
**Answer: c) stay frozen and silent.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Thought tracking means…
a) writing a diary  b) speaking the thoughts of a figure frozen in a tableau  c) counting money
**Answer: b) speaking the thoughts of a figure frozen in a tableau.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** When a figure is thought-tracked, the figure…
a) stays frozen while the thoughts are spoken  b) leaves the scene  c) starts dancing
**Answer: a) stays frozen while the thoughts are spoken.**
