# Tableau: Living Pictures — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** "Tableau" and "still pictures" are two names the guide gives the same drama mode.
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A good tableau can be read by a watcher without any words.
**Answer: True.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** In thought tracking, the figure walks around while speaking.
**Answer: False** — the figure stays frozen; only the thoughts are spoken.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Tableau and thought tracking are both drama modes named in Jamaica's Grade 6 guide.
**Answer: True.**
