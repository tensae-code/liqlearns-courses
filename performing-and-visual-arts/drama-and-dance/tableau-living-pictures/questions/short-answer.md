# Tableau: Living Pictures — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How does a watcher know what a tableau's scene is, if nobody moves or speaks?
**Answer: From the frozen positions and shapes of the figures — the still picture itself shows the scene.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What companion activity does Jamaica's guide set alongside tableau and thought tracking?
**Answer: Role-playing the reading of the annual budget in Gordon House.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What does thought tracking add that a tableau alone cannot show?
**Answer: What the figures inside the frozen scene are thinking.**
