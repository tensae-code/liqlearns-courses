# Video script — Tableau: Living Pictures

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A play does not always move. Sometimes it **freezes**.

Your group becomes a picture — a **tableau**. Still. Silent.
A teller at the counter. A customer waiting. A guard at the door.
Everyone can read the scene without one word.

Now — **thought tracking**.
The picture still does not move. But a voice speaks — the thoughts inside one frozen head.
"I hope the line moves quickly." "I must keep this money safe."

Freeze the scene. Then let its thoughts speak.

That is tableau and thought tracking.
