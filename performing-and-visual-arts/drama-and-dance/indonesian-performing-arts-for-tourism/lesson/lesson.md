# Indonesian Performing Arts For Tourism

## Do it first

Name one thing that belongs in this lesson — Indonesian Performing Arts For Tourism — taught in Indonesia's own way, with the country's own named forms kept as parts. Say what it is in your own words. If you can name one, you are already inside the lesson.

## Sub lesson 1 — Bab 4 Proyek Seni Pertunjukan Wisata

a tourism performing-arts project — designing and staging a performance for visitors as a class project.

## Sub lesson 2 — Bab 4 Proyek Seni Pertunjukan Wisata

building a tourism performance — observing the tourism object, processing creative ideas, forming the creation/production team, packaging scenes, acts and the whole show, testing and refining it, then performing it.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson holds, part by part, in your own words. Companion checks: did every part get named, with its Indonesian name kept where the book gives one?

## Do It Yourself

1. Choose one named part of this lesson and find out one more true thing about it — from a person who knows it, or from the book your part came from.
2. Make or perform a small piece in the way of that part: a rhythm, a step, a drawing, a shaped piece of clay.
3. Write one sentence: what does this part show about Indonesia's own way of teaching its arts?
