# Hands-on — Dance Composition and Modern Dance

The lesson with your hands, your feet and your voice together, not only your eyes.

## Build element by element
The GIF's work, live: the group first steps in time together; then adds one turn; then one gesture. Each addition is agreed before it is danced — composing as a group decision.

## Traditional, then modern
Dance one short phrase in a traditional style. Then the class restyles it — different levels, different pathways, individual timing — and discusses what changed, using the guide's contrast.
