# Dance Composition and Modern Dance — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is ከአጣማሪ ጋር እንቅስቃሴ ማቀናጀት?
**Answer: Coordinating movement with a partner.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the three movements the composed phrase joins.
**Answer: A step, a turn and a gesture.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What two things does the performance lesson ask pupils to do with modern dance styles?
**Answer: Perform them and distinguish them.**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** What is chord leading also called?
**Answer: Voice leading — the basic steps of writing a melody over given chords, and chords under a melody.**
