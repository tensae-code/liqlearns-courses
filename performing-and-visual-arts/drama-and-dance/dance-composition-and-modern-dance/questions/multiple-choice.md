# Dance Composition and Modern Dance — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which is NOT an element of dance in the Grade 5 guide's list?
  a) Costume colour b) The body's shape and posture c) Coordinating with a partner
**Answer: a) Costume colour** — the elements are body shape/posture, space, and partner coordination.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Dance composition means…
  a) Building a dance from its elements b) Copying a dance exactly forever c) Choosing costumes only
**Answer: a) Building a dance from its elements** — composition builds the dance from its elements, in a chosen order.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The guide teaches modern dance first through…
  a) Its difference from traditional dance b) Its costumes only c) Its ticket prices
**Answer: a) Its difference from traditional dance** — first the difference between traditional and modern, then techniques and styles.

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Harmonising a melody means…
  a) Fitting chords that support its notes b) Singing it louder c) Dancing it faster
**Answer: a) Fitting chords that support its notes** — harmonising fits chords to a melody, guided by its structural tones.

**5. (Sub lesson 1)** The Grade 6 guide founds dance on —
a) body movement  b) costumes  c) music theory
**Answer: a) body movement.**

**6. (Sub lesson 1)** ውዝዋዜ means —
a) dance  b) song  c) stage
**Answer: a) dance.**

**7. (Sub lesson 1)** The guide introduces body movement —
a) after the regional styles  b) as its own topic, before the regional styles  c) never
**Answer: b) as its own topic, before the regional styles.**

**8. (Sub lesson 2)** Rhythm in dance, in the guide's account, is —
a) heard only  b) felt and shown in movement  c) written only
**Answer: b) felt and shown in movement.**

**9. (Sub lesson 2)** The dancer's body keeps the rhythm —
a) visibly, stepping on the beat  b) secretly  c) never exactly
**Answer: a) visibly, stepping on the beat.**

**10. (Sub lesson 2)** A composed dance is, first of all —
a) rhythm made visible in an ordered body  b) a costume show  c) a speech
**Answer: a) rhythm made visible in an ordered body.**
