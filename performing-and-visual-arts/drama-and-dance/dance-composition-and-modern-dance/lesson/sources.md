Suriname's own way: one integrated arts course — Kunst- en cultuureducatie (KCE) — in six domains (Music, Dance, Visual Arts, Cultural Heritage, Theatre, Media), each domain a series of one-hour lessons built on the same frame: the IKA lesson shape (Introductie–Kern–Afsluiting, the core in three phases — warm-up, technique, own work and showing moment) and the Big Idea design process in four phases (orient, research, experiment, reflect), every series answering one research question and ending in a presentation; each domain is deliberately fused with another school subject (music with arithmetic, dance with geography, visual arts with history, theatre with language); the course's heart is Suriname itself — its many peoples' dances, dress, jewellery, heritage and theatre forms — held together by the Alakondre ideal, "Alakondre na wan, wan na Alakondre" (unity in diversity); assessment is by evaluation card and portfolio, process over product.
