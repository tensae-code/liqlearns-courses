# Video script — Puppet Theatre

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A puppet starts as a character.

Draw your character. Put it on a stick.

A paper bag! A sock! Cut, arrange, paste.

Now your puppet has a face.

Make it speak. That is puppet theatre.
