# Puppet Theatre — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the two puppet kinds in the first part of this lesson.
**Answer: Cartoon characters (as characters) and stick puppets — the guide names creating cartoon characters and stick puppets.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which two household things become puppets in the second part?
**Answer: A paper bag and a sock.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the three making actions for the bag and sock puppets.
**Answer: Cutting, arranging and pasting.**
