# Puppet Theatre — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Class 1 puppet work begins with creating…
a) cartoon characters and stick puppets  b) stage lighting  c) tickets
**Answer: a) cartoon characters and stick puppets**.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A stick puppet carries its character on…
a) a stick  b) a kite string  c) a drum
**Answer: a) a stick**.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Paper-bag and sock puppets are made by…
a) cutting, arranging and pasting  b) firing in a kiln  c) weaving
**Answer: a) cutting, arranging and pasting**.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The body of one named puppet kind is…
a) a paper bag or a sock  b) a clay pot  c) a bottle
**Answer: a) a paper bag or a sock**.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** This puppet work stands in the guide under…
a) Cultural Awareness  b) Printing  c) Observation
**Answer: a) Cultural Awareness** — Class 1, printed p. 4.
