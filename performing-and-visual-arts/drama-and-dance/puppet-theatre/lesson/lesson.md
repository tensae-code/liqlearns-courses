# Puppet Theatre

## Do it first

Quick start: say in one sentence what you think this lesson is about — puppet theatre from the making side. Then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — Cartoon characters and stick puppets

Barbados's Class 1 Cultural Awareness work begins puppet theatre at the character: **creating cartoon characters and stick puppets** — a character first, then the puppet that carries it.

## Sub lesson 2 — Paper-bag and sock puppets

The making continues with things from home: **cutting, arranging and pasting paper-bag and sock puppets** — the bag or sock becomes the puppet's body, and the maker's hands give it its face and dress.
