# Theatre Criticism and Appreciation

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about theatre criticism and appreciation. Then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — Theatrical Taste

Theatrical taste (al-tadhawwuq al-masrahi) — the learned skill of understanding and appreciating a play in an artistic, critical way — and its six requirements: knowing theatre's basic elements, developing the sense of beauty, developing judgement and analysis, learning and practice, developing innovation and creativity, and precision of observation.

## Sub lesson 2 — The Theatre Critic

Who the theatre critic is — a specialist of wide theatre culture who analyses works deeply, objectively and without bias — and the critic's four tasks: analyse the text, evaluate the actors' performance, evaluate the work's effect on its audience, and evaluate the directing as one integrated whole; practised in pairs on a shipwreck story, one pupil acting while the other criticises.

## Sub lesson 3 — Criticism of the Theatrical Show

How a show is criticised element by element — the text (clarity of idea and message, plot sequence, character building, language), the acting (body and voice expression, team harmony, silences, credibility), the directing (use of space, atmosphere, imagination) and the scenography (decor, lighting, costumes, makeup, sound and visual effects) — so strengths and weaknesses are found and the work improved.
