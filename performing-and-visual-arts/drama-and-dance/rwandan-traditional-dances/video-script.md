# Video script — Rwandan Traditional Dances

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Four dances of Rwanda!

Ikinimba! Umushayayo! Igishakamba! Ikinyemera!

Dance Ikinyemera — hands up, like the long horns of the Inyambo cows.

The song teaches, the dance carries it — together, in public.
