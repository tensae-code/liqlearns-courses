# Rwandan Traditional Dances — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1.** Name two of Rwanda's regional traditional dances.
**Answer: Any two of: ikinimba, umushayayo, igishakamba, ikinyemera.**

**2.** What do the raised hands in Ikinyemera imitate?
**Answer: The long horns of the Inyambo parade cows.**
