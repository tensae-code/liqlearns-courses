# Rwandan Traditional Dances — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these is a Rwandan traditional dance named in the guide?
a) ikinyemera  b) eskesta  c) horo
**Answer: a) ikinyemera**

**2.** In Ikinyemera style, the hands are…
a) extended up, like the long horns of the Inyambo cows  b) hidden behind the back  c) holding drums
**Answer: a) extended up, like the long horns of the Inyambo cows**

**3.** Tubamagane (Let us denounce) is a song against…
a) child labour, drugs and child abuse  b) dancing  c) school
**Answer: a) child labour, drugs and child abuse**

**4.** The dances are practised with songs about…
a) social welfare and good human relationship  b) examinations  c) prices
**Answer: a) social welfare and good human relationship**
