# Rwandan Traditional Dances

## Do it first

Start with the first part below — do it or make it before you read any explanation.

## Sub lesson 1 — Rwanda's regional dances, named

The Primary 3 music guide names Rwanda's regional traditional dances — **ikinimba, umushayayo, igishakamba and ikinyemera** — and learners view them, telling the dances apart, while singing songs that strengthen social welfare.

## Sub lesson 2 — Dancing in the Ikinyemera style

The class practises dancing in the **Ikinyemera style**: hands extended up **like the long horns of the Inyambo parade cows**, clapping the written rhythm. In this style the class performs the song **Tubamagane (Let us denounce)** — against child labour, drugs, hard labour and child abuse — and Twite ku bana, on caring for children.
