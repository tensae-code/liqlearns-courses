# Lesson plan — Traditional Dances and Culture

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Drama and Dance · **Lesson:** Traditional Dances and Culture
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 4 — Drama and Dance (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** two definitions the guide builds everything on: what culture (ባህል) is, and what a traditional dance (ባህላዊ ውዝዋዜ) is — performing the community's dances with their cultural meaning.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Dances of Ethiopia — the learner can perform dances; this lesson gives the words for what those dances are.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the
exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an
image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture
of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does
with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and
while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is
dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the
student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph,
or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be
shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees
from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before
it is proved:

1. **What culture is (teacher guide — evidence)** — ባህል — the community's handed-down way of life.
2. **What a traditional dance is (teacher guide — evidence)** — ባህላዊ ውዝዋዜ — the community's own dance, performed with its cultural meaning.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Dances of Ethiopia. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Define culture (ባህል) in your own words.
2. Define a traditional dance (ባህላዊ ውዝዋዜ).
3. Perform a community dance saying its cultural meaning first.

## What you need
An open space for moving and acting. The pictures in the lesson. Nothing else.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — the learner performs or recalls one traditional dance and says what it means to the people who dance it (lesson/lesson.md).
2. **The explanation** — culture first, then the traditional dance defined through it (lesson/lesson.md).
   - (No picture slots beyond the described slots in the lesson text.)
3. **Say it to your companion** — Tell your companion: what is culture, and what makes a dance a traditional dance?
4. **Questions** — from questions/, served at the learner's level (levels.md).
5. **Hands-on** — hands-on.md.
6. **Do It Yourself** — Write down one custom of your community and the dance or song that carries it.

## If the learner is stuck
Go back to the Do it first moment and do it again slowly, together. The explanation only names what the learner has already done or seen.
