# Traditional Dances and Culture — short answer

Answer in your own words, in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name two things that belong to a people's culture.
**Answer: Any two — e.g. greetings, dress, food, celebrations, mourning customs, work ways.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What three things mark a traditional dance?
**Answer: Its people, its occasions across generations, and its cultural meaning (in its own dress and music).**
