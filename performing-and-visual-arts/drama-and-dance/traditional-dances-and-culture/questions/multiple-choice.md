# Traditional Dances and Culture — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Culture (ባህል) is —
a) one festival a year  b) a people's whole handed-down way of life  c) a kind of dress only
**Answer: b) a people's whole handed-down way of life.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Culture is handed —
a) from shops  b) generation to generation  c) from visitors
**Answer: b) generation to generation.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Which is part of culture?
a) how a people greet and celebrate  b) nothing daily  c) only ancient things
**Answer: a) how a people greet and celebrate.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A traditional dance is —
a) any new dance  b) the community's own dance, handed across generations  c) a dance for one person only
**Answer: b) the community's own dance, handed across generations.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Performing it truly means performing it —
a) faster  b) with its cultural meaning  c) in silence
**Answer: b) with its cultural meaning.**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Steps without meaning are —
a) the full dance  b) only exercise  c) forbidden
**Answer: b) only exercise.**
