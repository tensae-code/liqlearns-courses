# Hands-on — Traditional Dances and Culture

Real things to do with what this lesson teaches — at school or at home, with people around you.

## Meaning before steps
Before practising a traditional dance, the group first says what it means — its occasion, its people, its story. Then dance it.

## Culture carriers
List five things your community hands down — a greeting, a dress, a food, a dance, a story. That list is your culture at work.
