# Video script — Traditional Dances and Culture

A short read-aloud script in a super-simple voice, made later as the lesson's video. It follows the lesson's own order and names only what the lesson teaches.

---

Culture — ባህል — is a people's whole way of life, handed down.
A traditional dance — ባህላዊ ውዝዋዜ — is the community's own dance.
It carries the culture's meaning. Dance it knowing what it means.
