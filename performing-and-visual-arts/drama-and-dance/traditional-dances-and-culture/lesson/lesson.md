# Traditional Dances and Culture

## Do it first
You can probably dance — or have watched — a dance of your own people. What *makes* it traditional? And what is the bigger word it lives inside — culture? This lesson stands on teacher-guide evidence, and says so.

## Sub lesson 1 — What culture is (teacher guide — evidence) (teacher guide — evidence)

**Culture (ባህል)** is a people's way of life as a whole: how they greet, dress, eat, celebrate, mourn, work and rest — learned from the generation before and handed to the generation after.

Culture is not one special thing for special days. It is the water the community swims in every day — and its dances swim in it too.

## Sub lesson 2 — What a traditional dance is (teacher guide — evidence) (teacher guide — evidence)

A **traditional dance (ባህላዊ ውዝዋዜ)** is the community's own dance: the dance its people have danced across generations, in their dress, to their music, at their occasions.

To perform it truly is to perform it **with its cultural meaning** — knowing whose dance it is, when it is danced, and what it says about its people. Steps without meaning are only exercise; steps with meaning are the dance.

## Say it to your companion
Tell your companion: what is culture, and what makes a dance a traditional dance?

## Do It Yourself
Write down one custom of your community and the dance or song that carries it.
