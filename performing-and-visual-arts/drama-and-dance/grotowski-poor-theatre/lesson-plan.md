# Lesson plan — Grotowski and Poor Theatre

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Drama and Dance · **Lesson:** Grotowski and Poor Theatre
**Place in the curriculum:** Drama and Dance (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Jerzy Grotowski's poor theatre — theatre stripped to the actor and the audience alone.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the course's earlier drama and dance lessons — recommended first, never forced.
