# Hands-on — Theatre of the Oppressed

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Augusto Boal's Theatre of the Oppressed and Invisible Theatre
Do the practice this part names, with real materials and a partner: Augusto Boal's Theatre of the Oppressed and Invisible Theatre: spect-actors who join the action; trying invisible theatre in the school break

## Pina Bausch's dance-theatre of social problems
Do the practice this part names, with real materials and a partner: Pina Bausch's dance-theatre of social problems; hip-hop as protest and Gabriel Pensador's "Dança do Desempregado"; choreographing the unemployment dance as a class
