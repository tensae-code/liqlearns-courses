# Theatre of the Oppressed

## Do it first

Look at the sub lesson list. Pick the part you already know something about, and say what you know. Then start at Sub lesson 1 — every part builds on the one before it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Augusto Boal's Theatre of the Oppressed and Invisible Theatre

Augusto Boal's Theatre of the Oppressed and Invisible Theatre: spect-actors who join the action; trying invisible theatre in the school break (Folha 9, Brazil's *ARTE — Ensino Médio*, printed p. 152).

## Sub lesson 2 — Pina Bausch's dance-theatre of social problems

Pina Bausch's dance-theatre of social problems; hip-hop as protest and Gabriel Pensador's "Dança do Desempregado"; choreographing the unemployment dance as a class (Folha 19, Brazil's *ARTE — Ensino Médio*, printed p. 319).

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion this lesson's one thing in your own words, naming each sub lesson as you go. Companion checks: did every part get named?
