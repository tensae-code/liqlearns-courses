# Video script — Indigenous Dances of Suriname

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The first inhabitants dance first.

Step right, step left — knees lifted, arms drawn in, stretched down.

Circle: working the land. Fishing. Hunting. Gathering.

Now the bow and arrow — back on the ball of the foot, arm up as the bow.

Eight counts. Four counts. Two.

The dances of Suriname's indigenous peoples.
