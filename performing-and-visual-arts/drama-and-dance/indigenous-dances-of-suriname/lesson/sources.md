| Suriname — Dance Les 2 "De tijd — Met wie dans ik?" (Time — Who Do I Dance With?): meeting the Inheemsen — the steps of Suriname's indigenous peoples, the country's first inhabitants, mostly farmers, hunters, fishers and gatherers — stepping right and left with knee lift and arms drawn in or stretched down, circling with gestures of working the land, fishing, hunting and gathering; and the bow-and-arrow movement — step back on the ball of the foot, one arm stretched diagonally up as the bow, the other elbow drawn back — practised in eight, then four, then two counts; on the route, dancers meet and take over each other's movements (the glossary also names broko kindi as an indigenous dance, printed p. 197) (teacher guide — evidence) | Book 1, printed pp. 69–72 |
