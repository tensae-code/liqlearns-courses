# Indigenous Dances of Suriname

## Sub lesson 1 — Meeting the Inheemsen

The **Inheemsen** are Suriname's **indigenous peoples, the country's first inhabitants** — mostly **farmers, hunters, fishers and gatherers**. Their dance steps are learned by doing them: **stepping right and left with knee lift and arms drawn in or stretched down**, and **circling with gestures of working the land, fishing, hunting and gathering**. On a dance route, dancers **meet and take over each other's movements**. The book's glossary also names **broko kindi** as an indigenous dance.

## Sub lesson 2 — The bow-and-arrow movement

One movement is practised on its own: the **bow-and-arrow movement** — **step back on the ball of the foot, one arm stretched diagonally up as the bow, the other elbow drawn back** — practised in **eight, then four, then two counts**.
