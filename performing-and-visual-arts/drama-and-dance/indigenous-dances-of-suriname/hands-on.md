# Hands-on — Indigenous Dances of Suriname

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Step and circle
Practise stepping right and left with knee lift, arms drawn in, then stretched down. Then circle with the gestures of working the land, fishing, hunting and gathering.

## Eight, four, two
Practise the bow-and-arrow movement in eight counts, then four, then two — step back on the ball of the foot, bow arm diagonally up, other elbow drawn back.

## Meet and take over
Dance a short route; when you meet a partner, take over their movement and let them take yours.
