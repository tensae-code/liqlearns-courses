# Indigenous Dances of Suriname — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the four livelihoods of the Inheemsen named in the book.
**Answer: Farmers, hunters, fishers and gatherers.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What gestures fill the circling?
**Answer: Gestures of working the land, fishing, hunting and gathering.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What other indigenous dance does the glossary name?
**Answer: Broko kindi.**
