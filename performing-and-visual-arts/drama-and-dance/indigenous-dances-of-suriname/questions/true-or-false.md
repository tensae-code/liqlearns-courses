# Indigenous Dances of Suriname — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Dancers on the route meet and take over each other's movements.
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The bow-and-arrow movement steps forward onto the heel.
**Answer: False — step back on the ball of the foot.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The indigenous peoples are mostly farmers, hunters, fishers and gatherers.
**Answer: True.**
