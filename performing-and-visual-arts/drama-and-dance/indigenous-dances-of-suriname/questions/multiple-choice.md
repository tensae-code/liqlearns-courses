# Indigenous Dances of Suriname — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Inheemsen are…
a) newcomers to Suriname  b) Suriname's indigenous peoples, the country's first inhabitants  c) a dance school
**Answer: b) Suriname's indigenous peoples, the country's first inhabitants**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In the bow-and-arrow movement, one arm is…
a) hanging down  b) stretched diagonally up as the bow  c) behind the back
**Answer: b) stretched diagonally up as the bow**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The bow-and-arrow movement is practised in…
a) eight, then four, then two counts  b) one count only  c) a hundred counts
**Answer: a) eight, then four, then two counts**
