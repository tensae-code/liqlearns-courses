# Video script — Samoan Theatre: Koneseti and Faleaitu

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Samoa has its own theatre.

The **koneseti**. The **faleaitu**. Plays with a purpose — to wake the village up to the questions it faces!

Find such a work. Know it. Then make a short one of your own — Samoa's programme suggests a theme: **illiteracy, and what it costs**.

That is Samoan theatre.
