# Samoan Theatre: Koneseti and Faleaitu — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The koneseti and faleaitu are Samoa's own theatre forms.
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** These works are made only for entertainment, with no social purpose.
**Answer: False** — they are intended to raise political and social awareness.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Pupils only watch koneseti and faleaitu; they never create one.
**Answer: False** — they create a short one of their own.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Illiteracy and its consequences is the programme's example theme.
**Answer: True.**
