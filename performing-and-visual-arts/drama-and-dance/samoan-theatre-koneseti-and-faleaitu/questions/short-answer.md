# Samoan Theatre: Koneseti and Faleaitu — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name Samoa's two theatre forms.
**Answer: The koneseti and the faleaitu.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What are these works intended to raise?
**Answer: Political and social awareness.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What do pupils create, and on what example theme?
**Answer: A short koneseti or faleaitu of their own — the programme's example theme is illiteracy and its consequences.**
