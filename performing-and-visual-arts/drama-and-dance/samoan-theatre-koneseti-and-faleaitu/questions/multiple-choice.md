# Samoan Theatre: Koneseti and Faleaitu — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The koneseti and faleaitu are…
a) Samoa's own theatre forms  b) imported film kinds  c) kinds of drum
**Answer: a) Samoa's own theatre forms.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** These dramatic works are intended to…
a) raise political and social awareness  b) teach spelling  c) sell goods
**Answer: a) raise political and social awareness.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Pupils learn to do what with such works first?
a) identify them  b) ban them  c) translate them
**Answer: a) identify them.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Pupils create…
a) a short koneseti or faleaitu of their own  b) a full-length opera  c) a poster only
**Answer: a) a short koneseti or faleaitu of their own.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The programme's example theme is…
a) illiteracy and its consequences  b) the weather  c) fishing
**Answer: a) illiteracy and its consequences.**
