# Samoan Theatre: Koneseti and Faleaitu

## Do it first

A play can do more than entertain — it can wake a whole village up. Samoa's school programme names the island's own theatre forms built for exactly that. Do you know them?

## Sub lesson 1 — Koneseti and faleaitu — Samoa's own theatre

Samoa's primary Expressive Arts programme names Samoa's own theatre forms: **the koneseti and the faleaitu** — **dramatic works intended to raise political and social awareness**. Pupils learn to **identify such works**: Samoa's theatre is recognised by its purpose as much as by its form.

## Sub lesson 2 — Making your own

The programme's work is making, not only identifying: pupils **create a short koneseti or faleaitu of their own**. The programme's example theme is **illiteracy and its consequences** — a social question carried to an audience in Samoa's own dramatic form.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion: name Samoa's two theatre forms, say what they are intended to do, and name the programme's example theme. Companion checks: koneseti and faleaitu? Raise political and social awareness? Illiteracy and its consequences?

## Do It Yourself

1. Write the two names: koneseti and faleaitu, with their shared purpose in one sentence.
2. Choose a social theme for a short faleaitu of your own and write its message in one sentence.
3. Write the names of two characters for your piece and what each one wants.
