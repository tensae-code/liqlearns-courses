# Hands-on — Samoan Theatre: Koneseti and Faleaitu

## Spot the purpose
In a group, recall or imagine a short dramatic performance. Talk together: what social or political awareness was it raising? Present your answer in two sentences — the work, and the awareness it raised.

## A short one of your own
In your group, plan a two-minute koneseti or faleaitu on the programme's example theme — illiteracy and its consequences — or another social theme your group chooses. Agree the message first, then the characters, then perform your plan's opening scene.
