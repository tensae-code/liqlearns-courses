## Sub lesson 1 — The forms by name

Suriname's theatre teaching names the general forms — **rollenspel, sketch, musical and toneel** — beside the **specifically Surinamese** forms: **Anansitori (Anansi storytelling theatre), mato (the storytelling art) and banya-pre**. The wider list of forms holds **puppet play, shadow play, improvisation, recitation and text theatre**. Stories and plays are placed here by name and kind only — never transcribed.
