# Video script — Surinamese Theatre Forms

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Theatre wears many forms in Suriname.

Rollenspel. Sketch. Musical. Toneel.

And Suriname's own: Anansitori — Anansi told as theatre.

Mato, the storytelling art. Banya-pre.

Puppet play. Shadow play. Improvisation. Recitation.

One family — Suriname's theatre forms.
