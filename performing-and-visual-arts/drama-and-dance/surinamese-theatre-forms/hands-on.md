# Hands-on — Surinamese Theatre Forms

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Sort the family
Sort the named forms into two groups: the general forms and the specifically Surinamese forms. Say what makes the second group Suriname's own.

## Name the form
A learner names a performance situation — a spider story told in a circle, a short funny scene, a play with songs. The class names the form it belongs to.
