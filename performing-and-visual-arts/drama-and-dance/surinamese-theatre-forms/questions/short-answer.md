# Surinamese Theatre Forms — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the three specifically Surinamese theatre forms.
**Answer: Anansitori, mato and banya-pre.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the four general forms named beside them.
**Answer: Rollenspel, sketch, musical and toneel.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Name two forms from the wider list.
**Answer: Any two of: puppet play, shadow play, improvisation, recitation, text theatre.**
