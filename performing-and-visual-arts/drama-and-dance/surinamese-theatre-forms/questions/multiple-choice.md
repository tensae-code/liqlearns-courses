# Surinamese Theatre Forms — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which list holds only the specifically Surinamese forms?
a) rollenspel, sketch, musical  b) Anansitori, mato and banya-pre  c) puppet play, shadow play
**Answer: b) Anansitori, mato and banya-pre**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Anansitori is…
a) a kind of drum  b) Anansi storytelling theatre  c) a costume
**Answer: b) Anansi storytelling theatre**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Mato is…
a) the storytelling art  b) a mask  c) a dance step only
**Answer: a) the storytelling art**
