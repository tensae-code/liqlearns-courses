# Surinamese Theatre Forms — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Banya-pre is a specifically Surinamese theatre form.
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Shadow play is named in the wider list of forms.
**Answer: True.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Anansitori is a European theatre form.
**Answer: False — it is specifically Surinamese: Anansi storytelling theatre.**
