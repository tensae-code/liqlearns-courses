# Dances of Malaysia's Communities

## Do it first

Name one thing that belongs in this lesson — The folk dances of Malaysia's communities together — Malay, Chinese, Indian and the peoples of Sabah and Sarawak. Say what it is in your own words. If you can name one, you are already inside the lesson.

## Sub lesson 1 — The Chinese community's folk dance in Malaysia, exemplified by the

Unit 5 Tarian Rakyat Cina — the Chinese community's folk dance in Malaysia, exemplified by the Yun Nan Hua Deng lantern dance, with floor patterns and staging.

## Sub lesson 2 — The SSeM dance course's community arc

Units 4–5 with Units 5 of Tingkatan 1–2 — the SSeM dance course's community arc: Malay social dances, Sabah and Sarawak ethnic dances, Chinese folk dance, and Indian folk and classical dances as one Malaysian repertoire.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson holds, part by part, in your own words. Companion checks: did every part get named, with its Malaysian name kept where the book gives one?

## Do It Yourself

1. Choose one named part of this lesson and find out one more true thing about it — from a person who knows it, or from the book your part came from.
2. Make or perform a small piece in the way of that part: a rhythm, a step, a drawing, a shaped piece of clay.
3. Write one sentence: what does this part show about Malaysia's own way of teaching its arts?
