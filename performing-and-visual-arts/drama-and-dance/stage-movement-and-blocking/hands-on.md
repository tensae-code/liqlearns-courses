# Hands-on — Stage Movement and Blocking

The lesson with your whole body, not only your eyes.

## Read my stance
In pairs: one learner takes a stance — a guard on duty, a tired traveller, a proud ruler, a frightened child — and holds it, silent. The partner reads it aloud: who is this, and what are they feeling? If the reading matches, the stance works. Swap, and try a harder character.

## Hold it in the scene
In a small group scene, each actor chooses the stance their character needs and holds it correctly through the scene — no drifting into your everyday standing. Watchers say afterwards which stance stayed true and which one slipped, and the scene is played once more with the stances repaired.
