# Stage Movement and Blocking — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A stage stance (وقفة) is…
a) the way an actor stands on stage  b) a song's last note  c) a kind of costume
**Answer: a) the way an actor stands on stage.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Stances on stage…
a) are all the same  b) come in kinds, each used for its own purpose  c) do not matter to the audience
**Answer: b) come in kinds, each used for its own purpose.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Egypt's Grade 8 lesson on stance is called…
a) "My Theatre Team"  b) "Rights and Duties"  c) "My Stance Speaks"
**Answer: c) "My Stance Speaks".**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Inside a scene, the actor should…
a) hold the correct stance the character and moment need  b) stand any way at all  c) keep changing stance so nobody can read it
**Answer: a) hold the correct stance the character and moment need.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Through stance alone, without words, an actor can express…
a) nothing  b) only the time of day  c) feelings and character
**Answer: c) feelings and character.**
