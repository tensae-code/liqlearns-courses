# Stage Movement and Blocking — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A stance is a chosen way of standing, not an accident.
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Every stance is used for the same purpose.
**Answer: False** — stances come in kinds, and each kind is used for its own purpose.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** An actor can express a character through stance alone, without words.
**Answer: True.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Once a scene starts, holding your stance no longer matters.
**Answer: False** — the actor holds the correct stance inside the scene while it goes on.
