# Stage Movement and Blocking — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why does choosing a stance matter before an actor speaks?
**Answer: Because the audience reads the stance first — it already tells them about the character and the moment.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What two things does the book train an actor to show through stance alone?
**Answer: Feelings and character.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** How do you know a wordless stance has worked?
**Answer: The watchers can read the feeling and the character from how the actor stands, without any words.**
