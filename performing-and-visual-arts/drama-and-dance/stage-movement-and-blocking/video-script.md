# Video script — Stage Movement and Blocking

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Before an actor says one word, the audience has already read something: **how the actor stands**.

That is the **stance — وقفة**.
There are kinds of stance, and each kind is used for something.
A guard stands one way. A tired traveller stands another way. A proud king, another way still.

Hold the right stance inside your scene — steady, clear, true.
Then let the stance speak alone. No words.
If we can read your feeling from how you stand — your stance speaks.

That is the actor's stance.
