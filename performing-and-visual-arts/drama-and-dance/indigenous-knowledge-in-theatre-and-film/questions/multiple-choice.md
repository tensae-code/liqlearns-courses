# Indigenous Knowledge in Theatre and Film — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Indigenous knowledge grows from —
a) long interaction with the environment  b) one school year  c) imported manuals
**Answer: a) long interaction with the environment.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which pair is among its thirteen natures?
a) adaptive and cumulative  b) secret and sold  c) new and foreign
**Answer: a) adaptive and cumulative.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** 'Intergenerational' means it —
a) skips generations  b) passes between generations  c) ends with elders
**Answer: b) passes between generations.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which is named as theatre/film material?
a) conflict resolution  b) tax forms  c) train timetables
**Answer: a) conflict resolution.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** A tale, on stage, becomes —
a) the play's story  b) only a song  c) nothing
**Answer: a) the play's story.**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Natural conservation, staged, becomes —
a) the play's lesson  b) its tickets  c) its poster
**Answer: a) the play's lesson.**
