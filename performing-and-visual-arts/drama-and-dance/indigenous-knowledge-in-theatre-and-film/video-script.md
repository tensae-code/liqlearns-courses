# Video script — Indigenous Knowledge in Theatre and Film

A short read-aloud script in a super-simple voice, made later as the lesson's video. It follows the lesson's own order and names only what the lesson teaches.

---

Indigenous knowledge is guarded by its community, grown from long life with its environment.
Its natures: adaptive, cumulative, dynamic, holistic, humble.
Intergenerational, invaluable, moral, observant, relative.
Responsible, spiritual, unique.
Riddles, tales, conservation and conflict resolution become theatre and film.
