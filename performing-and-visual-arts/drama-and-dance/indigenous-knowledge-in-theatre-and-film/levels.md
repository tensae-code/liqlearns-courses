# Indigenous Knowledge in Theatre and Film — sub lessons and depth

This is the depth record for the lesson, re-expressed as sub lessons: each sub lesson is one step up in depth, proved before the next opens.

## Sub lesson 1 — What indigenous knowledge is — and its thirteen natures (teacher guide — evidence)
Pass mark: the learner can define indigenous knowledge and list its thirteen natures. The gate draws from this sub lesson's whole question set.

## Sub lesson 2 — Ethiopian forms as material (teacher guide — evidence)
Pass mark: the learner can name the four Ethiopian forms and one stage use for each. The gate draws from this sub lesson's whole question set.
