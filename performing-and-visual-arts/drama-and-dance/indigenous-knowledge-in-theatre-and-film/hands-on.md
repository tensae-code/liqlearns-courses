# Hands-on — Indigenous Knowledge in Theatre and Film

Real things to do with what this lesson teaches — at school or at home, with people around you.

## Nature spotting
Take one known piece of community knowledge. Groups test it against the thirteen natures: which can you prove from how it lives?

## Forms to stage
Each group chooses one Ethiopian form — a riddle set, a tale, a conservation practice, a conflict resolution — and outlines the play or film it could become.
