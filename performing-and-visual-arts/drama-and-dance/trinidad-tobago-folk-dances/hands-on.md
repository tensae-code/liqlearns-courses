# Hands-on — Trinidad Tobago Folk Dances

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Research one form
Choose one named form — Bele, Limbo, Quadrille/Jharoo, Ghadka or Kalinda — and find out: whose dance is it, when is it danced, and how does it move? Present it in three sentences.

## Heritage map
Draw the country's folk-dance map: French and African influences, Carnival dances, and Indian-heritage Bhangra and Kollattam, with one named form under each.
