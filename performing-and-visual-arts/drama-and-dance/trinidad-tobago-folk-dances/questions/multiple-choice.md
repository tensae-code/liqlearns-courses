# Trinidad Tobago Folk Dances — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The folk forms carry influences from…
a) French and African heritages, and Indian heritage  b) only one country  c) no heritage at all
**Answer: a) French and African heritages, and Indian heritage**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Bhangra and Kollattam come from the country's…
a) Indian heritage  b) French heritage only  c) school timetable
**Answer: a) Indian heritage**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Which is a named folk form in the strand?
a) Bele  b) the waltz  c) tap dancing
**Answer: a) Bele**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Limbo is…
a) a named folk form of Trinidad and Tobago  b) a musical instrument  c) a costume
**Answer: a) a named folk form of Trinidad and Tobago**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The named forms are learned by…
a) researching them as folk forms  b) never naming them  c) guessing
**Answer: a) researching them as folk forms**
