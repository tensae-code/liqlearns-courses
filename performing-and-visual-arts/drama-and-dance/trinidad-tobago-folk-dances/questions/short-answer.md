# Trinidad Tobago Folk Dances — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name two dances from the country's Indian heritage in this strand.
**Answer: Bhangra and Kollattam.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name three of the researched folk forms.
**Answer: Any three of: Bele, Limbo, Quadrille/Jharoo, Ghadka, Kalinda.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** What two older influences are named first for the folk forms?
**Answer: French and African influences.**
