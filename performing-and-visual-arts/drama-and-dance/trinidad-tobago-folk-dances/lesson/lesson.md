## Sub lesson 1 — Folk forms from many heritages

Trinidad and Tobago's Dance curriculum names its **folk dances as heritage content** (Forms 1–3). The forms carry the country's many roots: **French and African influences**, the **Carnival dances**, and — from its **Indian heritage** — **Bhangra and Kollattam**.

## Sub lesson 2 — The named folk forms, researched

The folk forms are **researched** as part of the curriculum: **Bele, Limbo, Quadrille/Jharoo, Ghadka and Kalinda** — each a folk form with its own history and way of moving, kept by the communities that dance them.
