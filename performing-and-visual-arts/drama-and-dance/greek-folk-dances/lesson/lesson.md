# Greek Folk Dances

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about greek folk dances. Then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — ΣΤ1. Χορεύω Ελληνικά (I dance Greek)

Kalamatianos — echoing proverbs in its 7/8 rhythm and comparing the folk songs «Καλότυχα είναι τα βουνά» and «Μήλο μου κόκκινο»; Tsamikos — circle improvisation on its rhythmic patterns

## Sub lesson 2 — ΣΤ1α. Χορεύω Ελληνικά — Καλαματιανός (I dance Greek — Kalamatianos)

learning the 7/8 metre and the Kalamatianos rhythm; the A harmonic minor scale and its raised Sol; Hadjidakis' «Μια μικρή λευκή αχιβάδα» and Baltas' Cypriot Suite as listening

## Sub lesson 3 — ΣΤ1β. Χορεύω Ελληνικά — Τσάμικος (I dance Greek — Tsamikos)

meeting the Tsamikos and the traditional clarinet (single reed); Theodorakis' «Άξιον Εστί» and Skalkottas' Tsamikos from his 36 Greek Dances
