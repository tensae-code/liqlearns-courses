# Video script — Greek Folk Dances

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is Greek Folk Dances.

Greece's folk dances, learned and danced as Greek dances in school. — as Greece's school books teach it: Kalamatianos — echoing proverbs in its 7/8 rhythm and comparing the folk songs «Καλότυχα είναι τα βουνά» and «Μήλο μου κόκκινο»; Tsamikos — circle improvisation on its rhythmic patterns.

Watch, listen — then say it back in your own words.
