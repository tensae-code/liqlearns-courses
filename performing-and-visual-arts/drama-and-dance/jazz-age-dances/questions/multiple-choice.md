# Dances of the Jazz Age — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Two jazz-age dance crazes are…
a) charleston and foxtrot  b) minuet and gavotte  c) waltz and polka only
**Answer: a) charleston and foxtrot**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The class learns to dance the…
a) charleston  b) sarabande  c) cakewalk
**Answer: a) charleston**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The design style of the age is…
a) art deco  b) baroque  c) gothic
**Answer: a) art deco**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** These crazes belong to the…
a) Jazz Age  b) Baroque suite  c) silent-film pit
**Answer: a) Jazz Age**

**5. (Sub lesson 1)** The dances spread through the…
a) entertainment industry  b) fire brigade  c) fishing fleet
**Answer: a) entertainment industry**
