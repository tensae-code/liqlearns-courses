# Video script — Mato: Surinamese Storytelling Theatre

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A circle. A story. Everyone inside it.

Mato — Suriname's storytelling theatre.

First the Syatu mato — a riddle to wake the listeners.

The Matoman holds the tale. The Pikiman confirms: Na so a de!

A song interrupts. A dance interrupts. That is the koti.

And the listeners go home with something to think on.
