# Hands-on — Mato: Surinamese Storytelling Theatre

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Open with a Syatu mato
Open a telling with a riddle or joke of your own collecting, as the Syatu mato opens a Mato, and catch the circle's attention.

## Practise the call and answer
One learner calls "mi ben dape"; a partner, as Pikiman, answers "yu ben dape, sa yu syi?" — then the first learner joins in with a short song, dance or story piece: their koti.

## Adapt a known story
In four groups around one big sheet — beginning, two middles, end — adapt a known story into the Amazon or Caribbean region, answering Who, What, Where for your part.
