# Mato: Surinamese Storytelling Theatre — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the Matoman's and the Pikiman's jobs.
**Answer: The Matoman (Gi-man) is the chief storyteller who wrote the main story and holds its line; the Pikiman tells it through to the public, confirms each part, and directs the piece.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is a koti?
**Answer: A joining-in: a player's own story, song or dance interrupting the main tale, called in with "mi ben dape".**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** How is the known story adapted in class?
**Answer: In four groups writing around one big sheet — beginning, two middles, end — answering the 3 W's, the story set in the Amazon or Caribbean region.**
