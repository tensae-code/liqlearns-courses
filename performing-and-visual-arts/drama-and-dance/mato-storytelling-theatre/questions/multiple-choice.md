# Mato: Surinamese Storytelling Theatre — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A Mato is…
a) a silent film  b) a frame tale built on life experience, performed as total theatre  c) a dance with no story
**Answer: b) a frame tale built on life experience, performed as total theatre**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The Syatu mato is…
a) the closing dance  b) a riddle that opens the evening and tests the listeners' intelligence  c) the chief storyteller
**Answer: b) a riddle that opens the evening and tests the listeners' intelligence**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A player who wants to join in calls…
a) "kweti kweti"  b) "mi ben dape" — I was there  c) "na leti so"
**Answer: b) "mi ben dape" — I was there**
