# Video script — Peking Opera

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

This lesson is Peking Opera.

Peking opera, China's best-known opera form — its singing, painted faces, costumes and hats. — as China's school books teach it: Learning to sing a Peking-opera aria — Li Tiemei's aria from the modern Peking opera The Red Lantern (xipi liushui rhythm, dan role), telling how her family serves the revolution.

Watch, listen — then say it back in your own words.
