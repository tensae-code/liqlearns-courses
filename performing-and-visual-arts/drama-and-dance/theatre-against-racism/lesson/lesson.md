# Theatre Against Racism

## Do it first

Quick start: A boy falls. A man helps him up. Then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — The play: "Mi color no determina mi capacidad"

**"Mi color no determina mi capacidad"** — *My colour does not determine my capacity* — is a **four-character play by Clara Pérez**, printed in the Grade 7 book's theatre annex. **Pedrito falls from a wall**, and the man who helps him is **Arturo González**, a dark-skinned man — who proves to be **the hospital's best doctor** (the story is told here by summary only).

## Sub lesson 2 — What the play teaches

The play's **title is its argument**: **skin colour does not determine a person's capacity**. The story carries the proof — the man judged by his colour is the very man the hospital trusts most. Theatre here is not only a show; it is **an argument a community performs for itself**.
