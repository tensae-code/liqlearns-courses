# Theatre Against Racism — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The play's title says my colour does not determine my…
a) capacity  b) height  c) name
**Answer: a) capacity**.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Who falls from a wall?
a) Pedrito  b) Arturo  c) the author
**Answer: a) Pedrito**.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Arturo González turns out to be…
a) the hospital's best doctor  b) a patient  c) a visitor
**Answer: a) the hospital's best doctor**.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The play teaches that a person must be judged by…
a) their capacity, not their skin colour  b) their clothes  c) their accent
**Answer: a) their capacity, not their skin colour**.

**5. (Sub lesson 1)** The play has…
a) four characters  b) one character  c) forty characters
**Answer: a) four characters**.
