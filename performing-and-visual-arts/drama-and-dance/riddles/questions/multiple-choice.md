# Riddles — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** The answerer in the riddle game replies —
a) 'Tell me again'  b) 'I know it'  c) 'Pass'
**Answer: b) 'I know it'** — the game's own call and answer.

**2.** The asker's opening call is —
a) 'Story, story!'  b) 'Riddle, riddle — what?'  c) 'Ready, steady!'
**Answer: b).**

**3.** A guess to a riddle is right only if it fits —
a) the first clue  b) every clue  c) no clues
**Answer: b) every clue** — tested against them all.

**4.** The second book riddle has —
a) water above and fire below  b) stone above, stone below, a living soul in the middle  c) two doors and no windows
**Answer: b).**
