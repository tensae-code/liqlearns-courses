# Riddles — true or false

Say if it is true or false.

**1.** The student book prints the answers to its two riddles.
**Answer: False** — the answers are not printed in the student book.

**2.** Riddle one points to something prepared in father's garden.
**Answer: True** (as the placement describes it).

**3.** In the riddle game, the asker should keep a straight face.
**Answer: True** — the form and the face are part of the game.

**4.** Guessing fast matters more than fitting the clues.
**Answer: False** — the clues rule; a guess must fit them all.
