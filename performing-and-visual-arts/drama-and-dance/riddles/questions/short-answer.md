# Riddles — short answer

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1.** What is a riddle called in Amharic?
**Answer:** እንቆቅልሽ (enqoqilish).

**2.** How does a circle test a guessed answer?
**Answer:** against every clue — a true answer fits them all.

**3.** Where do riddles live, passing from person to person?
**Answer:** in talk — courtyards, kitchens and storytelling circles; the teacher guides name tales and riddles as the roots of theatre (evidence).
