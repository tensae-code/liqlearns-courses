# Riddles — depth

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner runs the riddle game in its proper form (call, answer, riddle, clue-tested guessing) and plays both book riddles. Practice deepens by widening the set — home-harvested riddles enter the circle — and by hardening the craft: the learner's own riddles go from one clue to three, and from easy (guessed at once) to well-tuned (cracked only after fair clue work).
