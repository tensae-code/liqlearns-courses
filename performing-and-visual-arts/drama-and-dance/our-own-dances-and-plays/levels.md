# Our Own Dances and Plays — sub lessons and depth

This is the depth record for the lesson, re-expressed as sub lessons: each sub lesson is one step up in depth, proved before the next opens.

## Sub lesson 1 — Movements become our own dances (teacher guide — evidence)
Pass mark: the learner can make a short dance from a movement you perceived. The gate draws from this sub lesson's whole question set.

## Sub lesson 2 — Play-shapes become our own plays (teacher guide — evidence)
Pass mark: the learner can build a little play of your own on a recognised shape. The gate draws from this sub lesson's whole question set.
