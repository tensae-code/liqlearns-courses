# Hands-on — Our Own Dances and Plays

Real things to do with what this lesson teaches — at school or at home, with people around you.

## From a real movement to a dance
Each learner picks one movement seen at home or on the road, and builds a short step-phrase from it. The class copies each learner's phrase in turn.

## Our own little play
In groups, build a short play on a shape you recognise — opening, middle, close — from a moment of your own day, and perform it.
