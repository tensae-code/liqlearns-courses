# Our Own Dances and Plays — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Your own dance must be copied from someone else's. True or false?
**Answer: False — it is made from movements you perceived.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Making your own play comes before recognising play shapes. True or false?
**Answer: False — recognising comes first, expressing second.**
