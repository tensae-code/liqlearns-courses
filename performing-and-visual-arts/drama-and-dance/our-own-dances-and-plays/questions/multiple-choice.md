# Our Own Dances and Plays — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In §2.2, dances are made from —
a) movements you have perceived  b) a book of steps only  c) the teacher's dance only
**Answer: a) movements you have perceived.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A dance of your own is —
a) copied exactly  b) made by stretching and turning a real movement  c) never performed
**Answer: b) made by stretching and turning a real movement.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The order in the guide is —
a) making first, perceiving never  b) perceiving first, then expressing  c) only performing
**Answer: b) perceiving first, then expressing.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In §2.3, learners —
a) only watch plays  b) build their own little plays on the shapes they recognised  c) only read plays
**Answer: b) build their own little plays on the shapes they recognised.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** A good source for your own play is —
a) your own day — a market moment, a game, a journey  b) nothing real  c) only dreams
**Answer: a) your own day — a market moment, a game, a journey.**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Your own play still needs —
a) no shape  b) an opening, a middle and a close  c) a real theatre building
**Answer: b) an opening, a middle and a close.**
