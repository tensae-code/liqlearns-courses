# Our Own Dances and Plays — short answer

Answer in your own words, in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Where does your own dance start from?
**Answer: From a movement I have perceived — really watched — in people, animals or things.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does 'drama structures in expression' mean?
**Answer: Using the play-shapes you learned to recognise to make your own plays.**
