# Video script — Costume and Make-Up for Theatre

A short read-aloud script in a super-simple voice, made later as the lesson's video. It follows the lesson's own order and names only what the lesson teaches.

---

Costume — አልባሳት — the clothing builds the character before a word is spoken.
Make-up — የገጽ ቅብና መዋቢያ ግብዓቶች — paints the face into the person: old, young, tired, joyful.
Together they are the craft of stage appearance.
