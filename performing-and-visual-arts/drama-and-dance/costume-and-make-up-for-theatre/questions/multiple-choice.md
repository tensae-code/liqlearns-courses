# Costume and Make-Up for Theatre — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** On stage, costume —
a) only decorates  b) builds the character — the audience reads who the person is  c) hides the actor
**Answer: b) builds the character — the audience reads who the person is.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** From dress, the audience reads —
a) nothing  b) age, work, wealth and occasion  c) the script
**Answer: b) age, work, wealth and occasion.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Choosing costume is like —
a) choosing words in a story — each piece must be true  b) choosing a meal  c) choosing a seat
**Answer: a) choosing words in a story — each piece must be true.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Make-up materials on stage are used to —
a) hide the play  b) paint the actor's face into the character's face  c) colour the stage floor
**Answer: b) paint the actor's face into the character's face.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Age lines in make-up make —
a) a child  b) an elder  c) a singer
**Answer: b) an elder.**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Make-up serves —
a) the actor's vanity  b) the character the story needs  c) the audience's clothes
**Answer: b) the character the story needs.**
