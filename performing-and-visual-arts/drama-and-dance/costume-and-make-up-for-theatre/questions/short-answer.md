# Costume and Make-Up for Theatre — short answer

Answer in your own words, in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why must each costume piece be true to the character?
**Answer: Because the audience reads who the person is from the dress before any word is spoken.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name one make-up change and the character it creates.
**Answer: Any true pair — e.g. age lines create an elder.**
