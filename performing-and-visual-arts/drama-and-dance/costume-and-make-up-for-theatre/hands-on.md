# Hands-on — Costume and Make-Up for Theatre

Real things to do with what this lesson teaches — at school or at home, with people around you.

## Read the costume
Show pictures or real clothes of different people — elder, merchant, bride, farmer. Learners say who each set of clothes belongs to, and what told them.

## One make-up change
With safe face paint or drawing on paper faces, make one change only — age lines for an elder, for example — and see how much of the character it carries.
