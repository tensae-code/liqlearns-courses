# Costume and Make-Up for Theatre — sub lessons and depth

This is the depth record for the lesson, re-expressed as sub lessons: each sub lesson is one step up in depth, proved before the next opens.

## Sub lesson 1 — Costume — clothing builds the character (teacher guide — evidence)
Pass mark: the learner can explain how costume builds a character, with one example. The gate draws from this sub lesson's whole question set.

## Sub lesson 2 — Make-up and face painting (teacher guide — evidence)
Pass mark: the learner can name make-up and face painting as stage craft and give one true use. The gate draws from this sub lesson's whole question set.
