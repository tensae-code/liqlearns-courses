# Video script — Theatre of the Absurd

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

**Teatro del Absurdo.** The Theatre of the Absurd — born of the Second World War's disillusion: no realism, scenic minimalism, dialogues of incommunication, circular time (Camus and Sartre as the book presents them; Ionesco's The Bald Soprano, Beckett's Waiting for Godot).

That is Theatre of the Absurd — Venezuela's own books teach it this way.
