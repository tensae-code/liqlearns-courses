# Hands-on — Theatre of the Absurd

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Teatro del Absurdo
Work through this part with real materials or your own body, the way the book sets it: the Theatre of the Absurd — born of the Second World War's disillusion: no realism, scenic minimalism, dialogues of incommunication, circular time (Camus and Sartre as the book presents them; Ionesco's The Bald Soprano, Beckett's Waiting for Godot). Show a companion what you made or did, and say what the part teaches.
