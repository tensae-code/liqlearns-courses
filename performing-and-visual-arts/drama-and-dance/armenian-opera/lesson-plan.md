## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Listening** — Listening: Tigranian's "Anush" (teacher-guide evidence) — the group dance from the opera "Anush" heard in class: Armenian opera brought to first-graders
2. **The children's opera "The End of Evil" (Չարի վերջը, Azat Manukyan)** — The children's opera "The End of Evil" (Չարի վերջը, Azat Manukyan) — the cuckoo and the wicked forest-dweller's tale staged in song; "The birds' song" from the opera, words by Hovhannes Tumanyan
3. **Operas written for children around the world** — Operas written for children around the world — Ravel's opera of the child and the enchantments, with its dances (waltz, menuet) and animal voices
4. **The first Armenian opera** — The first Armenian opera — created in 1868: Tigran Chukhajian's "Arshak II", a historical-patriotic opera; Armenian opera art in the 20th century
5. **THE BIRTH AND DEVELOPMENT OF ARMENIAN OPERA** — THE BIRTH AND DEVELOPMENT OF ARMENIAN OPERA — opera was born in Florence in the later 16th century, in the search to revive Greek theatre (the Camerata); the first operas took antique subjects
6. **The FIRST ARMENIAN OPERA** — The FIRST ARMENIAN OPERA — written by Tigran Chukhajian (1837-1898); the libretto by Tovmas Terzian; staged in Constantinople ("Arshak II") — and Yerevan's opera house is Alexander Tamanyan's building
7. **Spendiaryan's "ALMAST" and the comic line** — Spendiaryan's "ALMAST" and the comic line — love, betrayal and human relations at the opera's end as the Armenian troops approach; Haro Stepanyan (1897-1966) wrote several operas, among them the comic opera "Kaj Nazar" from the Armenian tale
8. **OPERA AND MUSICAL** — OPERA AND MUSICAL — before turning to the music, discuss the differences between the opera and musical genres; "West Side Story": Tony meets Maria, sister of the Puerto Rican leader Bernardo, and falls in love; Bernstein's "Somewhere" sounds at the meeting after the killing
