# Video script — Technical and Performance Elements of Theatre

A short read-aloud script in a super-simple voice, made later as the lesson's video. It follows the lesson's own order and names only what the lesson teaches.

---

Technical elements build the world: scenery, costumes, props, lights, sound, make-up.
Performance elements fill it: face, body, voice — breath, projection, diction, gesture.
And the general elements hold all: performers, audience, director, playwright, space, design, text.
