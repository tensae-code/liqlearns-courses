# Technical and Performance Elements of Theatre — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which set belongs to technical elements?
a) curtains, flats, backdrops, platforms  b) acts, scenes, verses  c) songs and dances
**Answer: a) curtains, flats, backdrops, platforms.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Lights are tuned by —
a) placement, intensity and colour  b) price only  c) the audience
**Answer: a) placement, intensity and colour.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Wigs and body paint belong to —
a) performance elements  b) technical elements — with make-up  c) neither
**Answer: b) technical elements — with make-up.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Projection is —
a) throwing the voice to reach the audience  b) a kind of light  c) a prop
**Answer: a) throwing the voice to reach the audience.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Character motivation belongs to —
a) technical elements  b) performance elements  c) the audience only
**Answer: b) performance elements.**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** The general elements include —
a) performers, audience, director, playwright, acting space, design, text  b) only the text  c) only the actors
**Answer: a) performers, audience, director, playwright, acting space, design, text.**
