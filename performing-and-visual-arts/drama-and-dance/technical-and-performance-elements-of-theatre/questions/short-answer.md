# Technical and Performance Elements of Theatre — short answer

Answer in your own words, in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name four technical elements.
**Answer: Any four of: scenery/set, costumes, props, lights, sound, make-up/wigs/body paint.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name three vocal crafts of performance.
**Answer: Any three of: breath control, vocal expression, inflection, projection, diction.**
