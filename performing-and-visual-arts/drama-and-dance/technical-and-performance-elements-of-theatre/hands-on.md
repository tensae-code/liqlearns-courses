# Hands-on — Technical and Performance Elements of Theatre

Real things to do with what this lesson teaches — at school or at home, with people around you.

## Strip and restore
Groups list a scene's technical elements one by one; for each, say what the scene loses without it. Then restore them in the order of importance.

## Voice workshop
Learners practise the performance crafts on one line: say it with breath control, projection and clear diction — then with a gesture that matches.
