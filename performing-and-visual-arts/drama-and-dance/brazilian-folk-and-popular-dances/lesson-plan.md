# Lesson plan — Brazilian Folk and Popular Dances

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Drama and Dance · **Lesson:** Brazilian Folk and Popular Dances
**Place in the curriculum:** Drama and Dance (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** brazilian Folk and Popular Dances — its forms, history and cultural meaning, taught as one topic lesson.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the course's earlier drama and dance lessons — recommended first, never forced.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. ## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Samba born from the African umbigada dance and Portuguese modinhas** — samba born from the African umbigada dance and Portuguese modinhas; Caribbean rumba, mambo, merengue and chá-chá-chá from the same mixing
2. **Frevo** — frevo: Recife's carnival dance, the passistas balancing with little umbrellas, the fanfarra brass-and-percussion orchestra
3. **Fandango Paranaense** — Fandango Paranaense: viola, rabeca and adufo with clog-stamping (tamanco) dances at communal harvest work parties (mutirão)
4. **Urban tribes (street dance** — urban tribes (street dance, hip-hop, axé, pagode, samba, sertanejo, punks, hippies) and how the groups we join shape the dances we love
5. **The samba school parade** — the samba school parade: one enredo joining music, fantasy costumes, floats and thousands dancing their own way; researching the kinds of samba (canção, choro, breque, gafieira, morro, carnavalesco)
6. **Indigenous dances of Brazil** — Indigenous dances of Brazil: the caxiri ritual of the Uaupés river peoples — feather headdresses, flutes, two formations (slow interlaced line, fast solo) dancing all night around the caxiri vessel; re-enacting the slow formation
7. **Brazil's folk dances** — Brazil's folk dances: frevo and maracatu of the Northeast, and the quadrilha of the Festas Juninas with its sanfona; organising a quadrilha from grandparents' memories

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
