# Video script — Brazilian Folk and Popular Dances

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

**Samba born from the African umbigada dance and Portuguese modinhas.** Samba born from the African umbigada dance and Portuguese modinhas; Caribbean rumba, mambo, merengue and chá-chá-chá from the same mixing

**Frevo.** Frevo: Recife's carnival dance, the passistas balancing with little umbrellas, the fanfarra brass-and-percussion orchestra

**Fandango Paranaense.** Fandango Paranaense: viola, rabeca and adufo with clog-stamping (tamanco) dances at communal harvest work parties (mutirão)

**Urban tribes (street dance.** Urban tribes (street dance, hip-hop, axé, pagode, samba, sertanejo, punks, hippies) and how the groups we join shape the dances we love

**The samba school parade.** The samba school parade: one enredo joining music, fantasy costumes, floats and thousands dancing their own way; researching the kinds of samba (canção, choro, breque, gafieira, morro, carnavalesco)

**Indigenous dances of Brazil.** Indigenous dances of Brazil: the caxiri ritual of the Uaupés river peoples — feather headdresses, flutes, two formations (slow interlaced line, fast solo) dancing all night around the caxiri vessel; re-enacting the slow formation

**Brazil's folk dances.** Brazil's folk dances: frevo and maracatu of the Northeast, and the quadrilha of the Festas Juninas with its sanfona; organising a quadrilha from grandparents' memories
