# Brazilian Folk and Popular Dances

## Do it first

Look at the sub lesson list. Pick the part you already know something about, and say what you know. Then start at Sub lesson 1 — every part builds on the one before it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Samba born from the African umbigada dance and Portuguese modinhas

Samba born from the African umbigada dance and Portuguese modinhas; Caribbean rumba, mambo, merengue and chá-chá-chá from the same mixing (Folha 13, Brazil's *ARTE — Ensino Médio*, printed p. 209).

## Sub lesson 2 — Frevo

Frevo: Recife's carnival dance, the passistas balancing with little umbrellas, the fanfarra brass-and-percussion orchestra (Folha 13, Brazil's *ARTE — Ensino Médio*, printed p. 211).

## Sub lesson 3 — Fandango Paranaense

Fandango Paranaense: viola, rabeca and adufo with clog-stamping (tamanco) dances at communal harvest work parties (mutirão) (Folha 16, Brazil's *ARTE — Ensino Médio*, printed p. 259).

## Sub lesson 4 — Urban tribes (street dance

Urban tribes (street dance, hip-hop, axé, pagode, samba, sertanejo, punks, hippies) and how the groups we join shape the dances we love (Folha 19 "Quem não dança, dança!", Brazil's *ARTE — Ensino Médio*, printed p. 304).

## Sub lesson 5 — The samba school parade

The samba school parade: one enredo joining music, fantasy costumes, floats and thousands dancing their own way; researching the kinds of samba (canção, choro, breque, gafieira, morro, carnavalesco) (Folha 19, Brazil's *ARTE — Ensino Médio*, printed p. 306).

## Sub lesson 6 — Indigenous dances of Brazil

Indigenous dances of Brazil: the caxiri ritual of the Uaupés river peoples — feather headdresses, flutes, two formations (slow interlaced line, fast solo) dancing all night around the caxiri vessel; re-enacting the slow formation (Folha 19, Brazil's *ARTE — Ensino Médio*, printed p. 310).

## Sub lesson 7 — Brazil's folk dances

Brazil's folk dances: frevo and maracatu of the Northeast, and the quadrilha of the Festas Juninas with its sanfona; organising a quadrilha from grandparents' memories (Folha 19, Brazil's *ARTE — Ensino Médio*, printed p. 312).

## Say it to your companion
