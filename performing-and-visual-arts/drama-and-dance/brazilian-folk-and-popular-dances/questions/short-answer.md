# Brazilian Folk and Popular Dances — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In your own words, what does this part teach?
**Answer: Samba born from the African umbigada dance and Portuguese modinhas; Caribbean rumba, mambo, merengue and chá-chá-chá from the same mixing**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In your own words, what does this part teach?
**Answer: Frevo: Recife's carnival dance, the passistas balancing with little umbrellas, the fanfarra brass-and-percussion orchestra**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In your own words, what does this part teach?
**Answer: Fandango Paranaense: viola, rabeca and adufo with clog-stamping (tamanco) dances at communal harvest work parties (mutirão)**
