# Video script — Kushtdepdi: Turkmen Folk Dance

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

**Kushtdepdi!** Turkmenistan's folk dance.

First — listen. How does it sound? Say it!
Then — dance! Move with the music, together.

The book even prints the music, arranged by Durdy Nuryýew, so you can play it too.
