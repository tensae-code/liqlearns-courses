# Kushtdepdi: Turkmen Folk Dance

## Do it first

Say what you already know about this lesson's subject — one thing you have seen, heard or done — before you read on.

## Sub lesson 1 — Dancing the Kushtdepdi

In the dance-music chapter the book names **Kushtdepdi** a **Turkmen folk dance** and teaches it the dancer's way:

1. **Listen** to the music of the dance "Куштдепди".
2. **Say how it sounds** — its character, in your own words.
3. **Perform the movements of the dance** to its music.

The book's notation appendix gives the dance's music itself (a fragment, arranged by Durdy Nuryýew), so the class can play it as well as dance to it.
