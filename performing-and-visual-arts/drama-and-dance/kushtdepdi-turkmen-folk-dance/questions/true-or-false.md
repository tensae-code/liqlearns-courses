# Kushtdepdi: Turkmen Folk Dance — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Kushtdepdi is a folk dance of Turkmenistan.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The book teaches the dance without any music.
**Answer: False** — listening to its music comes first, and its music is printed in the appendix.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Learners perform the movements of the Kushtdepdi themselves.
**Answer: True**
