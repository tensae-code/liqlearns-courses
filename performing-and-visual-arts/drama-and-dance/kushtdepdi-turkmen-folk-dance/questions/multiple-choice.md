# Kushtdepdi: Turkmen Folk Dance — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Kushtdepdi is…
a) a Turkmen folk dance  b) a lullaby  c) an instrument
**Answer: a) a Turkmen folk dance**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** First, with the Kushtdepdi music, you…
a) draw it  b) listen to it  c) hide it
**Answer: b) listen to it**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** After listening, the book asks you to…
a) say how it sounds, then perform the dance's movements  b) go home  c) sing a different song
**Answer: a) say how it sounds, then perform the dance's movements**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** The appendix gives the dance's music…
a) as a fragment arranged by Durdy Nuryýew  b) not at all  c) as words only
**Answer: a) as a fragment arranged by Durdy Nuryýew**
