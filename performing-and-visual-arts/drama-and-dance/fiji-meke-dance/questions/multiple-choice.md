# Fiji Meke Dance — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Meke is Fiji's…
a) traditional dance-song performance  b) national anthem  c) printmaking
**Answer: a) traditional dance-song performance.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Before performing, learners first…
a) research their community's songs and dances  b) buy costumes  c) write an exam
**Answer: a) research their community's songs and dances.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The meke is performed wearing…
a) traditional attire  b) school uniform only  c) sports kit
**Answer: a) traditional attire.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The iTaukei meke named in the book is…
a) Drau Ni Uto Bota Toka  b) Meke Wesi only  c) Seasea only
**Answer: a) Drau Ni Uto Bota Toka.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The performance happens…
a) at school or in the community  b) only on television  c) only abroad
**Answer: a) at school or in the community.**
