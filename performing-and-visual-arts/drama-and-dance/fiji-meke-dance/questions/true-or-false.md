# Fiji Meke Dance — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Meke is an iTaukei traditional performance.
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Learners perform a dance from a country they have never heard of.
**Answer: False — they perform their own community's dance.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Traditional attire is part of the performance.
**Answer: True.**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Research comes before the performance.
**Answer: True.**
