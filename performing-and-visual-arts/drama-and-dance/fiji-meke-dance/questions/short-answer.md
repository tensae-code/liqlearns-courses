# Fiji Meke Dance — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is meke?
**Answer: Fiji's traditional dance-song performance.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What do learners wear for their own traditional performance?
**Answer: Their traditional attire.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Where does the research for the performance come from?
**Answer: The learner's own community — its songs and dances.**
