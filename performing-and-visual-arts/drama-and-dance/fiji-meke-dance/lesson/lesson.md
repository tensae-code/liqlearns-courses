# Fiji Meke Dance

## Do it first

Quick start: from the title alone, guess what this lesson's work is. Then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — Researching your community's dance

Learners **research their community's songs and dances** — asking and learning at home and in the community — before they perform.

## Sub lesson 2 — Performing the meke in traditional attire

The class **performs its own cultural dance in traditional attire** at school or in the community. The iTaukei example named in the book is the **meke Drau Ni Uto Bota Toka**. Meke is Fiji's traditional dance-song performance, worn with **masi** attire.
