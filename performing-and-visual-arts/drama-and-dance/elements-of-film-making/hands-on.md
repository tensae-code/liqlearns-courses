# Hands-on — Elements of Film Making

Real things to do with what this lesson teaches — at school or at home, with people around you.

## Four shots, one actor
Frame the same classmate four ways — wide, medium, close-up, point-of-view — in drawings or with a phone. Compare: what does each shot let the audience feel?

## Hard light, soft light
In drawings, show the same face in hard light and in soft light. Say what story each light tells.
