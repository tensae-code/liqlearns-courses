# Elements of Film Making — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The four camera shots are —
a) wide, medium, close-up, point-of-view  b) near, nearer, nearest, done  c) day, night, dawn, dusk
**Answer: a) wide, medium, close-up, point-of-view.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A tracking shot —
a) stands still  b) travels with the action  c) looks upward only
**Answer: b) travels with the action.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Deep focus keeps —
a) everything sharp, near and far  b) only the background  c) nothing visible
**Answer: a) everything sharp, near and far.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Mise-en-scène is —
a) the camera's battery  b) everything arranged before the camera — design, costume, colour, lighting, performance, blocking, framing  c) the film's title
**Answer: b) everything arranged before the camera — design, costume, colour, lighting, performance, blocking, framing.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Lighting divides into —
a) hard and soft  b) red and blue only  c) day and imported
**Answer: a) hard and soft.**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Props belong to —
a) the audience  b) setting and props — part of what stands before the camera  c) the editor only
**Answer: b) setting and props — part of what stands before the camera.**

**7. (Sub lesson 3)** Film sound includes —
a) composed score, dialogue, narration, sound effects  b) only dialogue  c) silence only
**Answer: a) composed score, dialogue, narration, sound effects.**

**8. (Sub lesson 3)** Editing's pace choice is —
a) rapid vs slow cutting  b) loud vs quiet  c) colour vs plain
**Answer: a) rapid vs slow cutting.**

**9. (Sub lesson 3)** Performance in film is carried by —
a) facial expression, body language, voice  b) the camera alone  c) the poster
**Answer: a) facial expression, body language, voice.**
