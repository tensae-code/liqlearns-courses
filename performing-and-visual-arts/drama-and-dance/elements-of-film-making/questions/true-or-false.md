# Elements of Film Making — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Point-of-view is one of the camera shots. True or false?
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Costume and make-up are film-making elements. True or false?
**Answer: True.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Voice-over is a kind of film sound. True or false?
**Answer: True.**
