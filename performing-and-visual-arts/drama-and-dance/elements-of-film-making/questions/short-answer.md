# Elements of Film Making — short answer

Answer in your own words, in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is a pan?
**Answer: The camera turning in place to follow or reveal.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name three parts of mise-en-scène.
**Answer: Any three of: production design, costume, colour, lighting, performance, blocking, framing.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name two kinds of film sound.
**Answer: Any two of: composed score, dialogue, narration/voice-over, sound effects.**
