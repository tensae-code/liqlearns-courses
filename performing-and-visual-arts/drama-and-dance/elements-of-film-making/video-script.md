# Video script — Elements of Film Making

A short read-aloud script in a super-simple voice, made later as the lesson's video. It follows the lesson's own order and names only what the lesson teaches.

---

Shots: wide, medium, close-up, point-of-view.
Angles low and high; movements pan and tracking; focus deep or shallow.
Mise-en-scène: everything placed before the camera.
Sound, editing, performance — the toolkit is complete.
