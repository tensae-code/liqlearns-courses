# Elements of Film Making — sub lessons and depth

This is the depth record for the lesson, re-expressed as sub lessons: each sub lesson is one step up in depth, proved before the next opens.

## Sub lesson 1 — Camera — shots, angles, movements, focus (teacher guide — evidence)
Pass mark: the learner can name the shots, angles, movements and focus kinds. The gate draws from this sub lesson's whole question set.

## Sub lesson 2 — Before the camera — mise-en-scène, light, colour (teacher guide — evidence)
Pass mark: the learner can say what mise-en-scène holds and contrast hard and soft light. The gate draws from this sub lesson's whole question set.

## Sub lesson 3 — Sound, editing, performance (teacher guide — evidence)
Pass mark: the learner can name the sound kinds and editing's two paces. The gate draws from this sub lesson's whole question set.
