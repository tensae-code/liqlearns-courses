# Video script — Intore Dance

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Intore! The dance of the Chosen Ones.

Chosen by the king — for strength, and for good behaviour.

They danced for warriors. They danced for victory.

Hear the ingoma drums — umurishyo! The steps answer the drums.

Today the whole country dances Intore — and women dance it too.
