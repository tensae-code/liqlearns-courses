# Intore Dance — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Intore means…
a) Chosen Ones, or Leaders  b) drum makers  c) basket weavers
**Answer: a) Chosen Ones, or Leaders**

**2.** The Intore were elected by…
a) the village weavers  b) the king, the Umwami  c) the school teacher
**Answer: b) the king, the Umwami**

**3.** Intore was danced to…
a) encourage warriors and celebrate victory  b) put children to sleep  c) sell cloth
**Answer: a) encourage warriors and celebrate victory**

**4.** In performance, the dance (umuhamirizo) is matched to…
a) the drum rhythms, umurishyo w'ingoma  b) the school bell  c) silence
**Answer: a) the drum rhythms, umurishyo w'ingoma**
