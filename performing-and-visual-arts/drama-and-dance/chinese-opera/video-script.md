# Video script — Chinese Opera (Xiqu)

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is Chinese Opera (Xiqu).

Chinese opera beyond Peking opera — regional xiqu forms, their singing, acting and stage conventions. — as China's school books teach it: "Watching the Big Opera" — a song with a spoken prologue about a village opera stage, in Henan Yu-opera flavour: the Yu opera （豫剧/河南梆子） band of banhu, small sanxian and dizi, and singing in imitation of its style; "West Lake Beauty", a song in Yue-opera aria style; the Yue opera （越剧） of Zhejiang, Shanghai and Jiangsu — clear, gentle, beautiful singing with a Jiangnan air; an extension listens to the question-and-answer scene of the Yue opera Liang Shanbo and Zhu Yingtai in Wu dialect.

Watch, listen — then say it back in your own words.
