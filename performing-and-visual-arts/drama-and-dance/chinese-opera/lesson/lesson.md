# Chinese Opera (Xiqu)

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about chinese opera (xiqu). Then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — China Grade 5 Music (jianpu) Vol.2

"Watching the Big Opera" — a song with a spoken prologue about a village opera stage, in Henan Yu-opera flavour: the Yu opera （豫剧/河南梆子） band of banhu, small sanxian and dizi, and singing in imitation of its style; "West Lake Beauty", a song in Yue-opera aria style; the Yue opera （越剧） of Zhejiang, Shanghai and Jiangsu — clear, gentle, beautiful singing with a Jiangnan air; an extension listens to the question-and-answer scene of the Yue opera Liang Shanbo and Zhu Yingtai in Wu dialect

## Sub lesson 2 — China Senior Music 6 Music & Drama

Xiqu performance — the role types （行当）, the means and conventions （程式） of performance, and the basic skills and special techniques, through Peking-opera and local-opera excerpts; the voice systems （声腔） and genres of xiqu — the four great voice systems, genre and regional culture, genre singing and dialect
