# Musical Theatre — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Revues spread new songs before recordings did.
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Deleríum Búbónis is a sea shanty.
**Answer: False.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The Árnason brothers made words and music together.
**Answer: True.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Revue songs were never comic.
