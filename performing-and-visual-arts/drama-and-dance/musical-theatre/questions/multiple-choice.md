# Musical Theatre — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Revues gave people…
a) new songs everyone ended up singing  b) only silent plays  c) only marches
**Answer: a) new songs everyone ended up singing**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Revue comic songs were sung…
a) in the intervals  b) at funerals  c) at sea
**Answer: a) in the intervals**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Iceland's musical tradition begins with…
a) Deleríum Búbónis (1954)  b) a waltz  c) Tiger Rag
**Answer: a) Deleríum Búbónis (1954)**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In the Árnason brothers' work, Jónas wrote…
a) the words  b) the drums  c) nothing
**Answer: a) the words**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** A söngleikur is a…
a) musical  b) sea song  c) bugle call
**Answer: a) musical**
