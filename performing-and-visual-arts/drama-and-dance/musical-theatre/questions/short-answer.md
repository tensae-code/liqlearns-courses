# Musical Theatre — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What year begins Iceland's musical tradition?
**Answer: 1954.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Name a later Árnason musical.
**Answer: Any of: Rjúkandi ráð, Allra meina bót, Járnhausinn.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Who wrote the music for Deleríum Búbónis?
**Answer: Jón Múli Árnason.**
