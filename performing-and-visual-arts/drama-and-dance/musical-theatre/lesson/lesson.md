# Musical Theatre

## Do it first

Quick start: say one thing you already know — a tune, a dance, a name — that belongs to this lesson's world. Keep it in mind; the parts will meet it.

## Sub lesson 1 — The Icelandic revue

Before technology, the theatres gave people **the new songs that ended up on everyone's lips**: **Iceland's revues** and their **comic songs, sung in the intervals** — the revue was how a town learned its new music.

## Sub lesson 2 — The Árnason brothers and the Icelandic musical

**Jónas's words and Jón Múli's music**: **Deleríum Búbónis (1954)** begins **Iceland's own musical (söngleikur) tradition**, followed by Rjúkandi ráð, Allra meina bót and Járnhausinn.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the parts in order, one sentence each. Companion checks: did every part get its sentence, and did the order hold?

## Do It Yourself

1. Write the parts' names from memory, in order.
2. Do the hands-on activity once more, teaching it to someone else.
3. Write one sentence: what is the one thing this lesson teaches?
