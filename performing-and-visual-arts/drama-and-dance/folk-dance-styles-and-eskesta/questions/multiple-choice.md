# Folk Dance Styles and Eskesta — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** ዝናብ እንቅጥቅጥ — the rain inqitiqit of Gojjam — moves…
  a) The whole body, shaking b) Only the neck c) Only one foot
**Answer: a) The whole body, shaking** — rain inqitiqit shakes the whole body; single inqitiqit works hand and foot.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In the eskesta steps, the rise comes…
  a) On the count of four, after the shake b) Before the shake c) Only at the song's end
**Answer: a) On the count of four, after the shake** — stoop, shake chest and shoulders, count 1-2-3-4 and rise.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The Gurage dance is nicknamed for…
  a) Marathon stepping without rest b) Sleeping between steps c) Dancing seated
**Answer: a) Marathon stepping without rest** — fast-tempo marathon stepping without rest — a hard-working people's image.

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Wolayta dance is led by…
  a) The waist and haunches b) The neck only c) The fingers
**Answer: a) The waist and haunches** — Wolayta is the southern waist dance — swing and twist the haunches.

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** Slow movement training builds…
  a) Control of the body b) Only speed c) Only singing
**Answer: a) Control of the body** — slow, controlled movement is the control under every style.

**6. (Sub lesson 6)** Performance groups hold —

**Answer: a) 3–4 learners.**
