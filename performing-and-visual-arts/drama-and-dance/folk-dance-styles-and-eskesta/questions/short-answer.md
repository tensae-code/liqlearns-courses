# Folk Dance Styles and Eskesta — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which Gondar styles are named?
**Answer: ድስቅና (disqina) and መንጥቅ (mentiq).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which body parts lead the eskesta?
**Answer: Neck, shoulders and chest.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What decorates the Oromo women's leather costume?
**Answer: Shells — with a very fast sharp neck motion above it.**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** The further south a dance comes from, the more it uses which part?
**Answer: The lower body.**

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** Why is this part cited at section level only?
**Answer: The Grade 2 guide is a scan with no text layer, read visually at section level.**

**6. (Sub lesson 6)** State the protocol in order, in your own words.
**Answer: Choose familiar local songs; group 3–4; practise matching dance to music with entry/finishing; balance groups; repeat on the rhythm pattern; audience comments; praise the best group.**

**7. (Sub lesson 7)** Name three named example dances with their areas.
**Answer: Any three of: Awris (Tenbien), Zinab Inqitqit (Gojjam), Eregeta (Raya Qobo), Fole (Oromia).**

**8. (Sub lesson 8)** Name the three performer-categories.
**Answer: Gender-founded group dances; pair dances (ጥንድ); community/union dances (ህብረት).**

**9. (Sub lesson 9)** What four things does the culture-check confirm?
**Answer: Dress condition, rhythm method, materials used, and choreography.**

**10. (Sub lesson 10)** Recite the Wollo sequence in order.
**Answer: Dress; run and loosen; activate shoulders/neck/waist; hold waist; shoulders up-down; up-down fast with the beat; repeatedly up; finally fast.**

**11. (Sub lesson 11)** Contrast Wollo's and Gojjam's hearts in one line each.
**Answer: Wollo: shoulders and neck, up-down climbing to fast, body calm. Gojjam: chest and shoulders trembling fast — inqitqit.**

**12. (Sub lesson 12)** Name the activity's three song regions.
**Answer: Northern (Tigray/Amhara), eastern (Harari/Somali), and southern.**
