# Video script — Folk Dance Styles and Eskesta

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Every region dances its own name.
Gojjam shakes the rain. Wollo turns its neck. Gondar shivers its shoulders.
Eskesta: stoop, shake the chest and shoulders — one, two, three, four — rise!
The further south, the lower the dance: waist, haunches, the whole body.
Slow first. Control first. Then speed can come.
