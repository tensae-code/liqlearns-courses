# Lesson plan — Folk Dance Styles and Eskesta

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Drama and Dance · **Lesson:** Folk Dance Styles and Eskesta
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 4 — Drama and Dance (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Ethiopia's folk dances up close — the region's named styles (Gojjam, Wollo, Gondar, Shewa, Awi, Wag Himra), the technique steps of Tigray, Amhara eskesta, Somali, Oromo, Gurage, Wolayta and Gambella dances, and the slow movements that train the body first.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Dances of Ethiopia and Circle Dances and Traditional Steps — the learner has already met the dances by region; this lesson teaches their named styles and counted technique steps.

4. **Wolayta and Gambella techniques (teacher guide — evidence)** — Wolayta — the waist leads, the further south the more lower-body movement; Gambella — the agile waist spin and the cowrie fringe that sounds (Grade 7 teacher guide, printed pp. 12–13).

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Dances of Ethiopia and Circle Dances and Traditional Steps. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name the Amhara region's local styles: Gojjam's two inqitiqit styles, Wollo's timtim/megalo/lalegoma, Gondar's disqina and mentiq, Shewa's wancha liqileqa, Awi below the waist, Wag Himra's energetic style.
2. Perform the Tigray circle step and the eskesta technique by their counted steps.
3. Tell the Somali, Oromo, Gurage, Wolayta and Gambella techniques apart by body and step.
4. Move slowly and with control, as the slow-movements lesson trains.

## What you need
Open floor, a drum or clapping for the beat, and modest traditional dress or practice clothes.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — Stand relaxed, feet shoulder-width. Let your shoulders make small shakes while your feet stay still. That shake — neck, shoulder and chest — is eskesta beginning. This lesson teaches the dances step by counted step. (lesson/lesson.md).

## If the learner is stuck
- Eskesta becomes whole-body bouncing → isolate: feet planted, the shake lives in neck, shoulders and chest; count 1-2-3-4 and rise.
- Confuses the regions' leading body parts → North dances lead from above the waist (neck, shoulders); Wolayta and Awi lead from below the waist.
