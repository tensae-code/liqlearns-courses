# Hands-on — Folk Dance Styles and Eskesta

The lesson with your hands, your feet and your voice together, not only your eyes.

## Eskesta laboratory
Practise the guide's eskesta steps in a line: stoop slightly, shake chest and shoulders, count 1-2-3-4 and rise, repeat. Then Gondar/Gojjam/Shoa/Wollo variations — same shake, regional accents.

## Slow movements (the training under the styles)
Move as slowly as a growing plant: one arm rising and opening like a flower over a full slow count of sixteen. The Grade 2 guide's slow-movement lesson is the control that makes every fast style clean.
