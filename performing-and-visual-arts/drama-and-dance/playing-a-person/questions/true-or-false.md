# Playing a Person — true or false

Sub-lesson markers: every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to.

Say if it is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** You should act a person before you have ever watched them.
**Answer: False** — observe first; no watching, no playing.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Every character needs the same things to act them.
**Answer: False** — each character needs their own matching things.

**3. (Sub lesson 4)** The artist in this lesson is a painter at work.
**Answer: True.**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** The health worker's role is acted from the book's picture of the health (medical) role.
**Answer: True.**
