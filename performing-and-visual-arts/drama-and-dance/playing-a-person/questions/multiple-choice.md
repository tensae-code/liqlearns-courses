# Playing a Person — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers: every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to.

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A theatre game imitates —
a) a person's character  b) a building  c) a song's tune
**Answer: a) a person's character** — by imitating their dress, movement, character, actions and dance.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which set of things belongs to acting a father?
a) chalk and a blackboard  b) a gabi wrap, a hat, a stick and a coat  c) a brush and a canvas
**Answer: b)** — the book's own list for the father's role.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The soldier's role is shown by —
a) an upright, disciplined march  b) sitting and serving food  c) painting at an easel
**Answer: a).**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Seller and buyer together make —
a) a solo song  b) a market bargain — a small play of two roles that need each other  c) a circle dance
**Answer: b).**
