# Playing a Person — short answer

Sub-lesson markers: every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to.

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the five things you observe in a person before imitating them.
**Answer:** their dress, movement, character, actions and dance.

**2. (Sub lesson 4)** In the market scene, what does the buyer do?
**Answer:** asks, bargains and decides (while the seller praises the goods).
