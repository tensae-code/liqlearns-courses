# Hands-on — Playing a Person

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The five-minute observation
Sit where workers pass or work — a market, a workshop, a clinic door. Choose one person. For five minutes, only watch: dress, movement, actions. Go home and act the role. Your family guesses who you watched — the job, not the name.

## A market in the yard
With friends, make a market with things from home: stones for potatoes, leaves for greens, sticks for tools. Sellers set out their goods and call out. Buyers walk, ask prices, bargain kindly, and buy. Swap roles and play again — the buyer who was cheated by high prices becomes the seller who must be fair.
