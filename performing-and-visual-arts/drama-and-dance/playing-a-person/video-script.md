# Video script — Playing a Person

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Want to play a person?
First — WATCH them.
Their clothes. Their walk. Their work. Their way.

Father needs: a wrap — **gabi**. A hat. A stick. A coat.
Teacher needs: chalk — and a class!
The painter needs: a brush.
The soldier stands tall. March, march!
The health worker cares. The seller calls out. The buyer asks.

Watch first. Then — become them!
