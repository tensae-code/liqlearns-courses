## Sub lesson 1 — Imagination and the four abhinaya

The classical four channels of acting expression — Angika (body), Sattvika (inner feeling), Aharya (costume/make-up) and Vachika (voice) — with the common features of good acting

## Sub lesson 2 — Let's use Sattvika Abhinaya

Acting from true inner feeling — the Sattvika channel practised on printed play excerpts (a village scene and a lovers' dialogue, summarised here by purpose only): the feeling must arise first and the face and voice follow it
