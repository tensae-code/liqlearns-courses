No game-song text is transcribed: both songs are placed by name, game shape and purpose only, exactly as their placements require — the Hoya Hoye call-and-response cry 'Hoya Hoye ho!' is named only as the placement itself names it. The songs' other homes are cross-referenced, not duplicated: the New Year Hoya Hoye songs live in Traditional and Festival Songs (Music), and the Buhe festival in Festivals and Their Arts (Culture and Values); this lesson stocks the Grade 4 placements' own target — the two songs as traditional play games.
