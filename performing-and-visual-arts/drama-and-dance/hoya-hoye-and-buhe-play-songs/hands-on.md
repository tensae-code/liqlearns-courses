# Hands-on — Hoya Hoye and Buhe: Traditional Play Songs

The lesson with your hands, your feet and your voice together, not only your eyes.

## The full circle game
Play the Hoya Hoye game whole, in groups by its rules, as the book directs — local traditional music game, performed in groups, to the simple beat your class chose and kept.

## Leader and answer practice
Practise the Buhe game's shape in pairs before the full game: one is the አዝማች (leader) with the leader's lines, the other the group with the answers. Change places until both parts are easy.
