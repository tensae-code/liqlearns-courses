# Hoya Hoye and Buhe: Traditional Play Songs — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What must be chosen and kept before the game runs?
**Answer: A simple beat.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is the leader of the Buhe game called?
**Answer: አዝማች (azmach).**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Which festival does the Buhe game belong to?
**Answer: Buhe — the festival of bonfires and fresh bread.**
