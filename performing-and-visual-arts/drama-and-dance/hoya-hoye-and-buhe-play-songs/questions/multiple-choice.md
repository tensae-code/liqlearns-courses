# Hoya Hoye and Buhe: Traditional Play Songs — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Hoya Hoye game is performed…
  a) In groups, by its rules, to a simple beat b) Alone and silently c) Only by teachers
**Answer: a) In groups, by its rules, to a simple beat** — groups, by the rules, in simple beat — the book's placement.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In Buhe Belu, the lines and answers belong to…
  a) Leader lines to the አዝማች, answers to the group b) All lines to one singer alone c) Answers to the leader, lines to nobody
**Answer: a) Leader lines to the አዝማች, answers to the group** — leader (አዝማች) lines and group answers — the second game's shape.
