# Video script — Theatre on a Stage

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Stand tall. Arms up!
You are on the STAGE.
Behind you — the red curtains.
In front of you — the people. Watching. Listening.

Speak BIG. Move BIG.
The last row must hear you!

And the old, loved form — **መነባንብ** (menibanib)!
A reading, performed aloud.
The voice rises… falls… waits…
The listeners SEE the river. They SEE the falls.
That is theatre.
