# Hands-on — Theatre on a Stage

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## A stage from nothing
Mark a stage with stones, sticks or a line in the dust — a rectangle at the front of a yard. The rule: inside the rectangle, you are a character; outside it, you are yourself. Practise stepping in (become the character: stand taller, speak bigger) and stepping out (yourself again). This one step teaches the whole idea of the stage.

## A family menibanib
Choose a passage the family knows — a story opening, a poem, a psalm. One person performs it standing, as a menibanib, pointing to the things the words name. Listeners sit in front, like the audience at the Abbay recitation. Afterwards each listener names one picture the voice made them see.
