# Theatre on a Stage — short answer

Sub-lesson markers: every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to.

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What stands behind the performer in the book's stage picture?
**Answer:** red curtains (with the audience silhouetted in front).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How does a reciting voice help listeners 'see' the words?
**Answer:** it rises, falls and pauses, so listeners picture what the words describe.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Name two gatherings where theatre is presented.
**Answer:** school festivals and public holidays.
