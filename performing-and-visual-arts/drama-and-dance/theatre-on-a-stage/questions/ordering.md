# Theatre on a Stage — ordering

Sub-lesson markers: every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to.

Put the steps in the right order. The answer gives the right order.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Performing a menibanib: (a) pause and let listeners picture it (b) stand before the listeners (c) lift the key words with your voice (d) point to what the words name
**Answer: b, c, d, a** — stand, lift the words, point, and pause for the picture to form.
