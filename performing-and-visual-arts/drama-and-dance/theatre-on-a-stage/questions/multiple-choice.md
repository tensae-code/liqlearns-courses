# Theatre on a Stage — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers: every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to.

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A stage performance needs —
a) only a performer  b) a performer, a stage and an audience  c) only curtains
**Answer: b)** — all three together make theatre on a stage.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Theatre can be presented at —
a) school festivals and public holidays  b) only in big cities  c) only on television
**Answer: a).**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A መነባንብ (menibanib) is —
a) a drum  b) a public recitation — a reading performed aloud before listeners  c) a woven cloth
**Answer: b).**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In the book's menibanib picture, the performance is about —
a) the Abbay (Blue Nile)  b) a market  c) a football match
**Answer: a)** — the presenter points at a photograph of the falls before a seated audience.
