# Theatre on a Stage — true or false

Sub-lesson markers: every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to.

Say if it is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** On a stage, your voice must reach the last row.
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A recitation is best done as flat reading, with no rises or pauses.
**Answer: False** — the voice performs the words: rising, falling, pausing.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** In the stage picture, the audience sits behind the performer.
**Answer: False** — the audience watches from in front; the curtains are behind.
