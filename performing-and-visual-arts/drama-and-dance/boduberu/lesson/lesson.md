# Boduberu

## Do it first

Quick game: tap a big slow beat on the table — boom, boom — and sway as if the drum were carrying you. You are feeling the dance this lesson meets.

## Sub lesson 1 — Boduberu — the big-drum dance

**Boduberu** is the Maldives' own **traditional big-drum dance** — the dance of the big drum, a heritage summit of Maldivian performing art.

## Sub lesson 2 — Reading movement in a drawing

The book studies **Zaeky Zameel's ink drawing of the Boduberu Dance** as an artwork: the class looks at **how the figures are made to look as if they are moving** — a still drawing that carries a dance's motion inside it. The Maldives book itself teaches boduberu through this picture; per its preface, Music and Movement and Drama are taught in the accompanying teacher's guide, which is not in hand, so this lesson claims only what the student book itself studies.
