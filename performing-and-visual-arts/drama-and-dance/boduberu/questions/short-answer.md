# Boduberu — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is boduberu?
**Answer: The Maldives' traditional big-drum dance.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Who drew the Boduberu Dance artwork the book studies?
**Answer: Zaeky Zameel.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the class study in the drawing?
**Answer: How the figures are made to look as if they are moving.**
