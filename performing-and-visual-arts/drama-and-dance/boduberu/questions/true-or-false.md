# Boduberu — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Boduberu is a traditional dance of the Maldives.
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Boduberu is played on a small flute.
**Answer: False — it is the big-drum dance.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A still drawing can be studied for movement.
**Answer: True.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The student book teaches the full boduberu dance steps.
**Answer: False — it studies boduberu through the artwork; music, movement and drama live in the teacher's guide, which is not in hand.**
