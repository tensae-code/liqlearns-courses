# Boduberu — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Boduberu is the Maldives'…
a) national bird  b) traditional big-drum dance  c) capital city
**Answer: b) traditional big-drum dance**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The drum in boduberu is…
a) a tiny hand bell  b) a big drum  c) a flute
**Answer: b) a big drum**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The artist of the Boduberu Dance drawing studied is…
a) Zaeky Zameel  b) Claude Monet  c) Matisse
**Answer: a) Zaeky Zameel**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The drawing is studied for how its figures…
a) stand perfectly still  b) look as if they are moving  c) disappear
**Answer: b) look as if they are moving**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The drawing medium named is…
a) oil paint  b) ink  c) clay
**Answer: b) ink**
