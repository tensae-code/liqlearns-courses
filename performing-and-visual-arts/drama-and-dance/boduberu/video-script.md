# Video script — Boduberu

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Boom — boom — the big drum speaks.

That is **boduberu**, the Maldives' own big-drum dance.

An artist drew it in ink — and the figures still move on the page.

Look closely: that is how a drawing carries a dance.
