# Video script — Stages of Film and Theatre Production

A short read-aloud script in a super-simple voice, made later as the lesson's video. It follows the lesson's own order and names only what the lesson teaches.

---

Story development. Pre-production — planning, locations, casting, budget.
Production, and principal photography — the camera rolls, the costliest phase.
Wrap: strike the set, return everything, keep full records.
Post-production: edit, effects, music, sound, picture lock, delivery. Then distribution.
