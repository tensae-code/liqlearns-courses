# Stages of Film and Theatre Production — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The most expensive phase is —
a) story development  b) principal photography  c) distribution
**Answer: b) principal photography.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Wrap means —
a) the first rehearsal  b) striking the set, returning everything, keeping full records  c) writing the story
**Answer: b) striking the set, returning everything, keeping full records.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Picture lock happens in —
a) pre-production  b) post-production  c) casting
**Answer: b) post-production.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Theatre's production phase is —
a) filming  b) rehearsing  c) printing
**Answer: b) rehearsing.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Theatre's post-production is —
a) audience feedback  b) picture lock  c) set striking
**Answer: a) audience feedback.**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Theatre pre-production holds —
a) play selection and development  b) distribution  c) editing
**Answer: a) play selection and development.**
