# Hands-on — Stages of Film and Theatre Production

Real things to do with what this lesson teaches — at school or at home, with people around you.

## Stage strip
Groups lay the seven stages on a strip in order and defend the two easiest to swap wrongly: why does wrap come before post-production?

## Theatre contrast
Map theatre's three phases beside film's seven: which film stages have no theatre twin, and why? (Theatre has no wrap or picture lock — it lives anew each night.)
