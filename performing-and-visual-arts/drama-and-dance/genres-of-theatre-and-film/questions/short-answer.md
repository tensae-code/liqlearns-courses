# Genres of Theatre and Film — short answer

Answer in your own words, in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What do the thematic and formative lenses each sort by?
**Answer: Thematic: what the work is about. Formative: its form.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the three conventions of film genre.
**Answer: Narrative, stylistic, thematic.**
