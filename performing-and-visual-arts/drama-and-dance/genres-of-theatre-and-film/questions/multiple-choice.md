# Genres of Theatre and Film — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Genre is —
a) a work's kind, by its matter and manner  b) a theatre building  c) an actor's name
**Answer: a) a work's kind, by its matter and manner.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Theatre genres are analysed as —
a) thematic, formative and technical  b) long, short and medium  c) old and new only
**Answer: a) thematic, formative and technical.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Tragedy and comedy are —
a) theatres  b) common sub-genres  c) lighting kinds
**Answer: b) common sub-genres.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Film genres are read through —
a) narrative, stylistic and thematic conventions  b) price and length  c) posters only
**Answer: a) narrative, stylistic and thematic conventions.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Basic film genres include —
a) action, comedy, drama  b) only action  c) news only
**Answer: a) action, comedy, drama.**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** A genre's characteristics are —
a) secret  b) the marks by which the audience knows the kind  c) irrelevant
**Answer: b) the marks by which the audience knows the kind.**
