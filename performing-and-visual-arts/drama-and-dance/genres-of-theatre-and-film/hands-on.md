# Hands-on — Genres of Theatre and Film

Real things to do with what this lesson teaches — at school or at home, with people around you.

## Categorise the shelf
The class lists known plays and films; groups categorise each by theme, then by form — and defend one hard case where the two disagree.

## Genre promise
Each group picks a genre and writes its promise: what will the audience feel, expect and receive? Then checks a real work against the promise.
