# Video script — Genres of Theatre and Film

A short read-aloud script in a super-simple voice, made later as the lesson's video. It follows the lesson's own order and names only what the lesson teaches.

---

Genre is a work's kind.
Theatre genres are read three ways: thematic, formative, technical.
Tragedy and comedy stand among the common sub-genres.
Film genres speak through narrative, stylistic and thematic conventions: action, comedy, drama and more.
