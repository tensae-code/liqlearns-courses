# Lesson plan — Folk Dance Practice: Tigray and Eskesta Steps

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Drama and Dance · **Lesson:** Folk Dance Practice: Tigray and Eskesta Steps
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 4 — Drama and Dance (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the guide's counted practice for two dances: Tigray dance counted one-two with leg, shoulder and neck coordination, and Amhara eskesta counted fast one-two with the same leg pattern plus shoulder and neck work.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Folk Dance Styles and Eskesta — the learner knows the dances; this lesson is the guide's step-by-step practice for two of them.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees
from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before
it is proved:

1. **The shared one-two leg pattern (teacher guide — evidence)** — move right leg and fix, left leg toward it and fix, mirror; repeat right/left, scroll, forward/backward.
2. **Tigray coordination — and eskesta's fast count (teacher guide — evidence)** — Tigray: leg, shoulder and neck coordination; eskesta: the same legs, counted fast, with shoulder/neck coordination.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Folk Dance Styles and Eskesta. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Step the shared one-two leg pattern, mirrored.
2. Add shoulder and neck coordination, Tigray style.
3. Take the same pattern at eskesta's fast count.

## What you need
An open space for moving and acting. The pictures in the lesson. Nothing else.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — the learner steps right-fix, left-toward-it-fix, slowly, counting one-two aloud, before the practice is explained (lesson/lesson.md).
2. **The explanation** — the shared one-two leg pattern first, then Tigray's coordination, then eskesta's fast count (lesson/lesson.md).
   - ADD GIF (described only — not filed): the full one-two leg-step sequence, right and left — a full dance phrase cannot be drawn truthfully in a 2-second loop, so the counted text protocol carries the steps.
3. **Say it to your companion** — Count the pattern aloud for your companion while you step it: right and fix, left toward it and fix, mirror.
4. **Questions** — from questions/, served at the learner's level (levels.md).
5. **Hands-on** — hands-on.md.
6. **Do It Yourself** — Practise the one-two pattern for ten counts daily until your shoulders and neck can join without breaking the steps.
