# Video script — Folk Dance Practice: Tigray and Eskesta Steps

A short read-aloud script in a super-simple voice, made later as the lesson's video. It follows the lesson's own order and names only what the lesson teaches.

---

Both dances share one leg pattern, counted one-two.
Right leg and fix. Left leg toward it and fix. Mirror it.
Tigray coordinates legs with shoulder and neck.
Eskesta takes the same pattern fast, shoulders and neck working.
