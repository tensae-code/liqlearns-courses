# Hands-on — Folk Dance Practice: Tigray and Eskesta Steps

Real things to do with what this lesson teaches — at school or at home, with people around you.

## Counted practice
The class practises the one-two leg pattern slowly together — right and fix, left toward it and fix, mirror — a counter calling the counts.

## Add the upper body
Once the legs hold, add shoulder and neck coordination in the Tigray manner; then try eskesta's fast count for eight counts only, and rest.
