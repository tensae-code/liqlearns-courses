# Folk Dance Practice: Tigray and Eskesta Steps — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The leg pattern is counted —
a) one-two  b) one-two-three-four-five  c) silently only
**Answer: a) one-two.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The pattern's order is —
a) left leg first, never mirrored  b) right leg and fix, left leg toward it and fix, then mirror  c) jumps only
**Answer: b) right leg and fix, left leg toward it and fix, then mirror.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The pattern can also move —
a) underwater  b) scrolling, forward and backward  c) only backwards
**Answer: b) scrolling, forward and backward.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Tigray practice coordinates —
a) legs only  b) leg, shoulder and neck  c) arms only
**Answer: b) leg, shoulder and neck.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Eskesta's count, on the same leg pattern, is —
a) slow  b) fast one-two  c) uncounted
**Answer: b) fast one-two.**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** The two dances share —
a) nothing  b) the leg pattern  c) only costumes
**Answer: b) the leg pattern.**
