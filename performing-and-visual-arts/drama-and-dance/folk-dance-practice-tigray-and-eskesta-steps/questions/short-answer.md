# Folk Dance Practice: Tigray and Eskesta Steps — short answer

Answer in your own words, in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does 'fix' mean in the pattern?
**Answer: The leg lands and holds its place before the next move.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How do Tigray and eskesta practice differ?
**Answer: Same leg pattern and shoulder/neck coordination — Tigray at the steady count, eskesta counted fast.**
