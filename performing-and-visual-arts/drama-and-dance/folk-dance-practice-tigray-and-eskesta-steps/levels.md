# Folk Dance Practice: Tigray and Eskesta Steps — sub lessons and depth

This is the depth record for the lesson, re-expressed as sub lessons: each sub lesson is one step up in depth, proved before the next opens.

## Sub lesson 1 — The shared one-two leg pattern (teacher guide — evidence)
Pass mark: the learner can step the one-two pattern right, left and mirrored. The gate draws from this sub lesson's whole question set.

## Sub lesson 2 — Tigray coordination — and eskesta's fast count (teacher guide — evidence)
Pass mark: the learner can coordinate shoulder and neck with the legs, steady for Tigray and fast for eskesta. The gate draws from this sub lesson's whole question set.
