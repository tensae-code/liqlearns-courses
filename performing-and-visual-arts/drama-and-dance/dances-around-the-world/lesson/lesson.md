## Sub lesson 3 — Kazakhstan Grade 5 Music (Kulmanova)

the game-journey "Silk Road from Asia to Europe" opens in Berlin at a contest of national music: German, Austrian, Italian and Spanish costumes told apart, the countries found on the map; the Estonian folk song «У каждого свой музыкальный инструмент» (arr. Kh. Kirvite) — every neighbour plays something, bagpipe, pipe and horn — sung in two voices; the 3/4 conducting triangle learned, since a waltz lives in it; Germany: song kinds (counting, teasing, comic, game, dance, choral), the reinlender and polka, and instruments born in Germany — svirel, flute, cymbals, harp, xylophone, drum, violin, double bass; Austria: zither, alpine horn, vargan and flugelhorn pictured; the yodel, the Tyrolean shepherds' song that flips the voice from low chest to high register, tried against throat singing; the Austrian polka-polianka; how the Ländler turned into the waltz; Johann Strauss (1825–1899), the Waltz King, and his «Весенние голоса»; Italy, cradle of musical culture: Neapolitan songs; the barcarolle, the Venetian gondoliers' song, in Offenbach's «Сказки Гофмана»; the Sicilian tarantella — a creative game: invent movements for the tarantella, build its rhythm from cards and percussion, and dance it in a group; Italian instruments mandola, mandolin, guitarrón, tambourine, castanets; class project: a "Festival of Folk Music" — each group brings a people's song and dance — or stage a rite of one Turkic people
