This lesson is Yangge Dance.

Yangge, the Han folk dance of northern China, above all the north-eastern yangge with fans and handkerchiefs. — as China's school books teach it: Yangge — a Han folk song-and-dance of northern China: feet walk the "cross step", hands wave red silk or handkerchiefs; learning the dance and its gong-and-drum rhythms.
