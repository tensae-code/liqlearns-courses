## Sub lesson 1 — China Grade 3 Music Vol.1

Yangge — a Han folk song-and-dance of northern China: feet walk the "cross step", hands wave red silk or handkerchiefs; learning the dance and its gong-and-drum rhythms

## Sub lesson 2 — China HS Dance Performance

Northeast yangge from the ground up — the stance (chin in, chest slightly held, knees soft, weight forward); the waist-driven pulse in three paths (up-down, front-back, figure-of-eight circles, accent landing on the upbeat); the heel-press step; the arm "flowers" — single-arm flower (slow rise, quick press, wrist cocked), alternating flower, slicing flower — and the quick front kick; the appreciation piece Yellow Earth, Yellow built on Wanrong flower-drum, and a Yunnan flower-lantern combination as extension
