# Video script — Moving to Music

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Music plays… your shoulders answer.
Your feet answer. YOUR way. No rules!
That is FREE movement.

Set dances teach the country's ways.
Free dance lets YOUR way speak. You need both!

Now — a partner! Two by two. Hold hands!
Jump together! Let go!
Right — left — forward — back!
Stamp, stamp! Turn your partner round!

And a dance you love? Don't just clap.
DANCE it. That is appreciation.
