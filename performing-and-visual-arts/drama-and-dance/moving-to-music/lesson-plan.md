# Lesson plan — Moving to Music

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Drama and Dance · **Lesson:** Moving to Music
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 4 — Drama and Dance (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** when music plays, your body may answer it freely — your own movement, in your own way — and with a partner, song and dance join into directed steps; a community's values ride in its dances, and dancing a dance you love is how you show appreciation.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Circle Dances and Traditional Steps — set dances with set steps. This lesson opens the other door: FREE movement, where the dancer chooses.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees
from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before
it is proved:

1. **Free movement to music** — free movement defined: your own movement to music, without restriction; groups show their area's games and dances.
2. **Free dance movements** — free dance movements against the pictures of traditional dances — set dances and your own way, both needed.
3. **Partner dance with directions** — the partner song ዳንስ (Dans): two by two, hold hands, jump, let go, right-left-forward-back, stamp, turn your partner; then pair practice.
4. **Dances that carry our values — and dancing your appreciation** — pictured dances performed for the values they carry; appreciation shown by dancing the dance yourself.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Circle Dances and Traditional Steps. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Move freely to music, in your own way, without restriction.
2. Say how free dance differs from set dance.
3. Dance the partner direction sequence in time.
4. Combine a known song with body movement in a pair.
5. Perform a values-carrying dance, and show appreciation by dancing.

## What you need
Music or a sung song — ዳንስ (Dans) for the partner work. A partner. An open space.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — the learner lets shoulders and feet answer imagined music before any explaining (lesson/lesson.md).
2. **The explanation** — free movement first (both book targets), then free dance movements, then the partner directions, then values and appreciation (lesson/lesson.md).
   - ADD IMAGE [Picture 9707]: partner dance directions — pairs holding hands, arrows for right, left, forward, back, a turning pair — the student traces the sequence with a finger before dancing it.
   - ADD AUDIO: the partner song ዳንስ (Dans) sung aloud (slot only — no audio file is made in this build) — the pairs dance the directions as the song names them.
3. **Say it to your companion** — tell your companion the difference between free dance and set dance, then call the partner directions from memory.
4. **Questions** — from questions/, served at the learner's level (levels.md).
5. **Hands-on** — hands-on.md.
6. **Do It Yourself** — at home, put on or sing a song and move freely for its whole length — your way, start to finish.

## If the learner is stuck
- 'Free' becomes 'random and careless' → free means YOUR choice, still answering the music: ask, 'what did the music just do — and what did your body answer?'
- Partners lose the directions → call them slowly without music first: jump — let go — right — left — forward — back — stamp — turn. Music joins when the feet know.
- The Dans song's words are placed by name and directions only; its verses are not transcribed (see sources.md).
