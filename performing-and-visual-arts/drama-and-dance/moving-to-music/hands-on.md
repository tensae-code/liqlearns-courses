# Hands-on — Moving to Music

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Free from start to finish
Sing or play a whole song — or have a friend sing ዳንስ (Dans) or another song you know. From the first note to the last, keep moving freely: no stopping to plan, no copying anyone. If you run out of ideas, change only ONE thing: your level (high/low), your speed, or the body part that leads.

## Direction callers
In pairs, take turns being the CALLER. The caller calls the partner-dance directions in any order — right! back! stamp! turn! — and both dancers obey together. The caller must keep a steady beat with claps. Swap every minute. This builds the pair's listening before the song is added.
