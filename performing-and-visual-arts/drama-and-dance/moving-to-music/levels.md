# Moving to Music — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — Free movement to music
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: move freely to music in your own way, without restriction, and show one dance or game from your own area.

## Sub lesson 2 — Free dance movements
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: say how free dance differs from a set dance, and show a dance from your own area in a group.

## Sub lesson 3 — Partner dance with directions
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: dance the direction sequence with a partner — jump, release, right/left/forward/back, stamp, turn partner — in time with the song.

## Sub lesson 4 — Dances that carry our values — and dancing your appreciation
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: perform a pictured dance that carries the community's values, and show appreciation for a dance by dancing it.
