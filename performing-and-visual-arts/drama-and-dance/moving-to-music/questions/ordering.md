# Moving to Music — ordering

Sub-lesson markers: every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to.

Put the steps in the right order. The answer gives the right order.

**1. (Sub lesson 3)** The partner dance ዳንስ (Dans): (a) stamp knees and stamp the ground (b) choose two by two and hold hands (c) turn your partner round (d) jump together, let go — right, left, forward, back
**Answer: b, d, a, c** — pair up and hold hands; jump and release into the directions; stamp; turn the partner.
