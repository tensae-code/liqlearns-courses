# Moving to Music — short answer

Sub-lesson markers: every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to.

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Sub lesson 3)** List the directions named in the partner dance.
**Answer:** right, left, forward, back (with jumping together, letting go, stamping, hands raised up-down, and turning the partner).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Where does the Grade 1 dance strand begin?
**Answer:** moving the body to what is heard — free and guided body movement to music (teacher-guide evidence for the same strand).

**3. (Sub lesson 4)** In the values work, what do learners perform?
**Answer:** a pictured traditional dance they like or know, in groups, for the teacher.
