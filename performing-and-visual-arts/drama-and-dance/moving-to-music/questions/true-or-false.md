# Moving to Music — true or false

Sub-lesson markers: every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to.

Say if it is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In free movement, groups can show cultural games and dances from their own area.
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A dancer needs only set dances, never free dance.
**Answer: False** — the book holds both: set dances teach the country's ways; free dance lets your own way speak.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The partner dance's directions include right, left, forward and back.
**Answer: True** — with jumping, stamping and turning the partner.

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** A community's values can be carried in its dances.
**Answer: True.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Free dance movements are limited to the set dances only.
**Answer: False** — free dance is done to music without being limited to set dances.
