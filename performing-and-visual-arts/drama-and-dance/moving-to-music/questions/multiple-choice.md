# Moving to Music — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers: every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to.

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Free movement to music means moving —
a) only as the teacher shows  b) in your own way, without restriction, following your own wish  c) only seated
**Answer: b).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Free dance movement is dance done to music —
a) without being limited to set dances  b) without any music  c) only alone
**Answer: a).**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In the partner dance ዳንስ (Dans), dancers begin —
a) back to back  b) two by two, holding hands  c) in one long line
**Answer: b) two by two, holding hands.**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Appreciation for a dance is shown by —
a) describing it from a chair  b) dancing it yourself, alone or in a group  c) drawing it only
**Answer: b).**
