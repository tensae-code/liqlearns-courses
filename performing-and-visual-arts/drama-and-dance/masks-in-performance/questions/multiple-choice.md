# Masks in Performance — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The mask in this unit is a…
a) universal non-naturalistic convention  b) naturalistic portrait  c) modern invention only
**Answer: a) universal non-naturalistic convention**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which is NOT one of the mask kinds worked with?
a) neutral mask  b) half mask  c) gas mask
**Answer: c) gas mask**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Moving masked is explored through…
a) space, time, weight and flow  b) only speed  c) only loudness
**Answer: a) space, time, weight and flow**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The unit devises…
a) a ritual, and a choral piece from Greek chorus work  b) a football match  c) a cooking show
**Answer: a) a ritual, and a choral piece from Greek chorus work**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** The mask learners build is…
a) a half mask for an abstract human quality  b) a full-face portrait of a friend  c) a carnival costume only
**Answer: a) a half mask for an abstract human quality**
