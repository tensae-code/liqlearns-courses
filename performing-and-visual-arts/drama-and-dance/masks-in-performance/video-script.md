# Video script — Masks in Performance

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A mask. No face — and yet it speaks.

Neutral. Half. Character. Every culture knows the mask.

Move — space, time, weight, flow — the mask leads.

Voices together — a chorus. Hands together — a mask of your own.

That is mask work.
