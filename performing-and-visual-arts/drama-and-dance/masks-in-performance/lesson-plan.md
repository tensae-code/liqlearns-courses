# Lesson plan — Masks in Performance

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Drama and Dance · **Lesson:** Masks in Performance
**Place in the curriculum:** Drama and Dance (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** mask work — the mask as a universal non-naturalistic convention: neutral, half and character masks, moving masked, devising a ritual, Greek chorus work, and designing and building a half mask for performance.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this course in the same title.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Masks across cultures** — The mask as a universal non-naturalistic convention across cultures — neutral masks, half masks and character masks.
2. **Moving masked, and devising a ritual** — Moving masked through space, time, weight and flow; devising a ritual; Ancient Greek chorus work and a choral piece.
3. **Designing and building a half mask** — Designing and building a half mask for an abstract human quality, for a group performance.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks a mask just hides the face → in this unit the mask is a convention: it changes how the whole body moves and speaks.
- Thinks mask work is only wearing → the unit also devises a ritual, builds chorus work, and designs and builds the mask itself.
