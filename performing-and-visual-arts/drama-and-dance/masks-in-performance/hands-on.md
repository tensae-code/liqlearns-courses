# Hands-on — Masks in Performance

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Move masked
Wearing a neutral mask, cross the space four ways — changing space, time, weight and flow one at a time. Let the mask lead the body.

## Build the half mask
Design and build a half mask for an abstract human quality (for example, Joy, or Fear). Wear it in a short group piece.
