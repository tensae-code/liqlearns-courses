# Masks in Performance

## Do it first

Quick start: from the title alone, guess what this lesson's work is. Then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — Masks across cultures

In Trinidad and Tobago's Drama guide, the **Masks unit (7 weeks)** teaches the mask as a **universal, non-naturalistic convention across cultures**. Three kinds are worked with: **neutral masks, half masks and character masks**.

## Sub lesson 2 — Moving masked, and devising a ritual

Masked work is movement work: **moving masked — space, time, weight, flow**. The unit **devises a ritual**, takes up **Ancient Greek chorus work** and builds a **choral piece** — the masked group speaking and moving as one.

## Sub lesson 3 — Designing and building a half mask

The unit ends in making: each learner **designs and builds a half mask for an abstract human quality**, and the masks are worn in a **group performance**.
