# Nicaraguan Regional Folk Dances — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** El Güegüense is the folk dance of which region?
a) the Atlantic  b) the Pacific  c) the North-Central region
**Answer: b) the Pacific**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** La Polka belongs to which region?
a) the North-Central region  b) the Pacific  c) the Atlantic
**Answer: a) the North-Central region**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Palo de Mayo is the folk dance of…
a) the Pacific  b) the North-Central region  c) the Atlantic
**Answer: c) the Atlantic**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** For each dance, the guide teaches…
a) only the music  b) its history, basic steps, costumes and props  c) only its name
**Answer: b) its history, basic steps, costumes and props**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** The dances are presented…
a) in school activities  b) never  c) only abroad
**Answer: a) in school activities**
