# Nicaraguan Regional Folk Dances — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which dance belongs to the Pacific region?
**Answer: El Güegüense.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which dance belongs to the North-Central region?
**Answer: La Polka.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name all three regional dances with their regions.
**Answer: El Güegüense — Pacific; La Polka — North-Central; Palo de Mayo — Atlantic.**
