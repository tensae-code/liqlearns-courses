# Nicaraguan Regional Folk Dances — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** El Güegüense is the Pacific region's folk dance.
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** La Polka is the Atlantic region's dance.
**Answer: False** — La Polka is the North-Central region's dance; Palo de Mayo is the Atlantic's.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Each dance is taught with its history, steps, costumes and props.
**Answer: True.**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Palo de Mayo belongs to the Atlantic region.
**Answer: True.**
