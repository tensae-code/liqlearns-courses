# Lesson plan — Nicaraguan Regional Folk Dances

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Drama and Dance · **Lesson:** Nicaraguan Regional Folk Dances
**Place in the curriculum:** Drama and Dance (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Nicaragua's regional folk dances — the history of the country's dances by region (Pacific, North, Atlantic): El Güegüense of the Pacific, La Polka of the North-Central region and Palo de Mayo of the Atlantic, with their basic steps, costumes, props and school presentations.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of its title — recommended first, never forced.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **El Güegüense — the Pacific** — the Pacific region's dance, its history, basic steps, costume and props.
2. **La Polka — the North-Central region** — the North-Central region's dance, its history, basic steps, costume and props.
3. **Palo de Mayo — the Atlantic** — the Atlantic region's dance, its history, basic steps, costume and props, and presenting the dances in school activities.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the folk-dance lessons of the Drama and Dance title. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name Nicaragua's three regional folk dances and match each to its region: El Güegüense — Pacific; La Polka — North-Central; Palo de Mayo — Atlantic.
2. Say what is learned for each dance: its history, its basic steps, its costumes and its props, for presentation in school activities.

## If the learner is stuck
- Mixes the dances up → anchor each by its region: Güegüense — Pacific, Polka — North-Central, Palo de Mayo — Atlantic.
- Thinks a folk dance is only steps → the guide teaches each dance with its history, costumes and props too.
