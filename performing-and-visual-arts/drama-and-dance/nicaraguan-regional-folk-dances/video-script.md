# Video script — Nicaraguan Regional Folk Dances

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

One country — three dances!

The **Pacific** dances **El Güegüense**.

The **North-Central** region dances **La Polka**.

The **Atlantic** dances **Palo de Mayo**.

Each dance has its history, its steps, its costume, its props.

Learn your region's dance — and present it at school.
