# Hands-on — Nicaraguan Regional Folk Dances

The lesson with your feet, not only your eyes.

## Match the dance to its region
In pairs, one partner names a region — Pacific, North-Central, Atlantic — and the other names its dance and mimes one basic step. Three rounds, one per region.

## Steps, costume, props
Choose one of the three dances. Show its basic steps as you understand them, name what costume and props belong to it, and present your short dance to the group as the guide's school presentations do.
