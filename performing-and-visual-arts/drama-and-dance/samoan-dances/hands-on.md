# Hands-on — Samoan Dances: Siva and Its History

## Grouping levels
In a group, take one simple dance phrase of your own making and perform it at the three grouping levels the programme names: standing, kneeling, sitting. Say what changed in the phrase at each level.

## Siva beside bhangra
Watch or recall the siva and the Indian bhangra. In pairs, list two likenesses and two differences, then combine one move from each into a single short dance phrase, as Samoa's programme directs — your own practice phrase, performed with respect for both dances.
