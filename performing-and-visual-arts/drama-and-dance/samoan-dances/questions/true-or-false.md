# Samoan Dances: Siva and Its History — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Traditional Samoan dances use grouping levels — standing, kneeling and sitting.
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 4)** In the taualuga, the taupou dances wildly while her companions stay still and solemn.
**Answer: False** — she dances stately and graceful; her companions may 'aiuli in wild joking movement to draw attention to her.

**3. (Sub lesson 5)** The fa'ataupati is a slow, sung dance lasting an hour.
**Answer: False** — it is a wordless, very fast slap dance, over in about a minute.

**4. (Sub lesson 6)** The obsolete dances me and sa survive in a children's game song and a proverb.
**Answer: True.**
