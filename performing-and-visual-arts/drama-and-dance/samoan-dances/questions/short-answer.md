# Samoan Dances: Siva and Its History — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** With which dance does the programme compare and contrast the siva?
**Answer: The Indian bhangra — pupils combine their moves in one dance phrase.**

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Who defended the Samoan people's right to judge the night dances for themselves when missionaries banned them?
**Answer: Samoan ministers.**

**3. (Sub lesson 5)** Name the three men's dances and one mark of each.
**Answer: The 'ailao (club dance to a beat on a kerosene tin), the fa'ataupati (wordless fast slap dance, about a minute) and the sake (stick dance from Uvea, sticks beaten overhead and under the thigh).**
