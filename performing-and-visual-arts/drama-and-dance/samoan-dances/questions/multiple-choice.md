# Samoan Dances: Siva and Its History — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The siva is…
a) Samoa's own dance  b) a dance from India  c) a European ballet
**Answer: a) Samoa's own dance.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which set names Samoa's traditional dances in the programme?
a) ma'ulu'ulu, sasa, faataupati and siva ipu  b) tango and waltz  c) ballet only
**Answer: a) ma'ulu'ulu, sasa, faataupati and siva ipu.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The poula evening began with…
a) a spirit song  b) the finale  c) a slap dance contest
**Answer: a) a spirit song.**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** The taualuga is now the special dance of…
a) the taupou  b) the youngest child  c) visiting sailors
**Answer: a) the taupou** — danced stately and graceful.

**5. (Sub lesson 6)** The ma'ulu'ulu's three parts are…
a) sasa, laulau siva and the ma'ulu'ulu proper  b) three solos  c) a song, a speech and a game
**Answer: a) sasa, laulau siva and the ma'ulu'ulu proper.**
