# Video script — Samoan Dances: Siva and Its History

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Samoa dances its history.

The **siva** — Samoa's own dance, graceful and full of feeling. Beside it, the named dances: **ma'ulu'ulu, sasa, faataupati, siva ipu**.

Long ago, the **poula** filled the night — song, sitting dances, slap dances, theatre in movement — until the missionaries banned the night dances.

And every siva ends with the **taualuga**, the chiefs' dance, danced by the taupou — stately, graceful, unforgettable.

These are Samoa's dances.
