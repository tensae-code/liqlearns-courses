# Indonesian Traditional Theatre

## Do it first

Name one thing that belongs in this lesson — Indonesia's traditional theatre and its stylised Nusantara acting, its forms, stories and stagecraft. Say what it is in your own words. If you can name one, you are already inside the lesson.

## Sub lesson 1 — BAB I Akting Stilistik

stylistic acting — the non-realist, symbolic acting style of Indonesia's traditional theatres, built on stylised gesture, hand movement and voice as the actor's foundation.

## Sub lesson 2 — BAB III Teater Tradisional Indonesia

Indonesian traditional theatre — its concept, functions, history and characteristics, and acting in the traditional style.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson holds, part by part, in your own words. Companion checks: did every part get named, with its Indonesian name kept where the book gives one?

## Do It Yourself

1. Choose one named part of this lesson and find out one more true thing about it — from a person who knows it, or from the book your part came from.
2. Make or perform a small piece in the way of that part: a rhythm, a step, a drawing, a shaped piece of clay.
3. Write one sentence: what does this part show about Indonesia's own way of teaching its arts?
