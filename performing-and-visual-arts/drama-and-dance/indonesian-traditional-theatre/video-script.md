# Video script — Indonesian Traditional Theatre

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This is Indonesia's own lesson.

**BAB I Akting Stilistik.** stylistic acting — the non-realist, symbolic acting style of Indonesia's traditional theatres, built on stylised gesture, hand movement and voice as the actor's foundation.

**BAB III Teater Tradisional Indonesia.** Indonesian traditional theatre — its concept, functions, history and characteristics, and acting in the traditional style.

That is Indonesian Traditional Theatre — Indonesia's way, kept by name.
