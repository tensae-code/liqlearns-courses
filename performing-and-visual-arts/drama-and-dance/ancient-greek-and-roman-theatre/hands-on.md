# Hands-on — Ancient Greek and Roman Theatre

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Teatro griego
Work through this part with real materials or your own body, the way the book sets it: the Greek theatre — semicircular tiers cut into a hillside for sight and sound, the circular orchestra where the chorus sang and danced, actors in masks and cothurni; born from the Dionysus vintage festivals; the classical models of tragedy and comedy: Aeschylus, Sophocles, Euripides, Aristophanes. Show a companion what you made or did, and say what the part teaches.

## Teatro romano y pantomima
Work through this part with real materials or your own body, the way the book sets it: the Roman theatre — built up on flat ground, orchestra reduced to a semicircle, the raised stage before the skené that stages still use; comedy of everyday life (Plautus) and tragedy (Seneca); and pantomime, stories of gods and mortals told by movement and gesture alone. Show a companion what you made or did, and say what the part teaches.
