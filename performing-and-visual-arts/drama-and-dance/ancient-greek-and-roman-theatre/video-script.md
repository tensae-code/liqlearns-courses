# Video script — Ancient Greek and Roman Theatre

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

**Teatro griego.** The Greek theatre — semicircular tiers cut into a hillside for sight and sound, the circular orchestra where the chorus sang and danced, actors in masks and cothurni; born from the Dionysus vintage festivals; the classical models of tragedy and comedy: Aeschylus, Sophocles, Euripides, Aristophanes.

**Teatro romano y pantomima.** The Roman theatre — built up on flat ground, orchestra reduced to a semicircle, the raised stage before the skené that stages still use; comedy of everyday life (Plautus) and tragedy (Seneca); and pantomime, stories of gods and mortals told by movement and gesture alone.

That is Ancient Greek and Roman Theatre — Venezuela's own books teach it this way.
