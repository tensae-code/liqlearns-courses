# Ancient Greek and Roman Theatre

## Do it first

## Sub lesson 1 — Teatro griego

The Greek theatre — semicircular tiers cut into a hillside for sight and sound, the circular orchestra where the chorus sang and danced, actors in masks and cothurni; born from the Dionysus vintage festivals; the classical models of tragedy and comedy: Aeschylus, Sophocles, Euripides, Aristophanes.

## Sub lesson 2 — Teatro romano y pantomima

The Roman theatre — built up on flat ground, orchestra reduced to a semicircle, the raised stage before the skené that stages still use; comedy of everyday life (Plautus) and tragedy (Seneca); and pantomime, stories of gods and mortals told by movement and gesture alone.
