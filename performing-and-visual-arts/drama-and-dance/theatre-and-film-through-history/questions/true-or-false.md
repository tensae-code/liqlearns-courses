# Theatre and Film Through History — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Film overcame drama's limits of place and time.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Film is successive moving images with sound.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Seytan Bet — 'House of the Devil' — was Ethiopia's first cinema hall (1923).
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Contemporary Ethiopian cinema includes the opening of film schools.
**Answer: True**

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** The guide defines African film as a product of African societies' historical experience.
