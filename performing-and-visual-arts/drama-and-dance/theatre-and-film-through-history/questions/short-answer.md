# Theatre and Film Through History — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Who unifies all the theatre's performance parts?
**Answer: The director (አዘጋጅ).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name three of the nine film types.
**Answer: Any three of: adventure, drama, romance, comedy, action, documentary, investigative, scientific, animation.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name the first Ethiopian film the student book names.
**Answer: «ሒሩት አባቷ ማነው» — the 1957 E.C. black-and-white film.**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Film arrived in Ethiopia together with what?
**Answer: Modernisation — the introduction of the motion picture.**

**5. (Sub lesson 5)**  Beside African theatre, the guide also teaches the history of…
**Answer: Theatre in the world — world theatre history.**
