# Theatre and Film Through History — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The word theatre comes from the Greek theatron, meaning…
  a) A place of seeing b) A place of sleeping c) A market
**Answer: a) A place of seeing** — theatron is a place of seeing — viewing is theatre's root.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which is a type of BOTH theatre and film in the book's lists?
  a) Comedy b) Documentary c) Animation
**Answer: a) Comedy** — comedy appears in theatre's types and in film's nine types.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Hager Fikir Theatre was founded in…
  a) 1935, in Piazza b) 1923, in Arada c) 1955, in Gondar
**Answer: a) 1935, in Piazza** — founded 1935 in Piazza — the oldest indigenous theatre in Africa, per the guide.

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** The guide roots Ethiopian theatre in…
  a) Plays, rituals, church ceremonies and school drama b) Only written scripts c) Only radio
**Answer: a) Plays, rituals, church ceremonies and school drama** — everyday public life — plays, rituals, ceremonies, school drama.

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** African film history is told through which two eras?
  a) The colonial era and the post-independence era b) The stone era and the iron era c) The radio era and the television era
**Answer: a) The colonial era and the post-independence era** — colonial-era and post-independence-era African film.

**6. (Sub lesson 6)** The guide's film section teaches —
a) what film art is, and how film art began  b) only film prices  c) only actors' names
**Answer: a) what film art is, and how film art began.**

**7. (Sub lesson 6)** የፊልም ጥበብ ምንነት means —
a) the nature of film art  b) a film poster  c) a camera shop
**Answer: a) the nature of film art.**

**8. (Sub lesson 6)** Film is presented here as —
a) the oldest art  b) an art whose nature and beginnings are learned first  c) not an art
**Answer: b) an art whose nature and beginnings are learned first.**

**9. (Sub lesson 7)** Theatre's two working modes are —
a) improvised and scripted  b) loud and silent  c) old and new
**Answer: a) improvised and scripted.**

**10. (Sub lesson 7)** 'Theatre' can also mean —
a) the building, the activity and the institution  b) only the tickets  c) only the stage floor
**Answer: a) the building, the activity and the institution.**

**11. (Sub lesson 7)** Theatre communicates real-life experience through —
a) gesture, speech, song, music and dance  b) telegrams  c) silence only
**Answer: a) gesture, speech, song, music and dance.**

**12. (Sub lesson 8)** Film's preparation has —
a) seven stages  b) three stages  c) one stage
**Answer: a) seven stages.**

**13. (Sub lesson 8)** Actors are chosen in —
a) pre-preparation  b) distribution  c) sound recording
**Answer: a) pre-preparation.**

**14. (Sub lesson 8)** The last stage is —
a) writing  b) distribution (showing)  c) photography
**Answer: b) distribution (showing).**

**15. (Sub lesson 9)** Theatre and film's historical foundation is —
a) social activities performed in the real world  b) imported stories only  c) dreams
**Answer: a) social activities performed in the real world.**

**16. (Sub lesson 9)** ሽምግልና (shimglina) is —
a) the elder-mediation system  b) a dance step  c) a costume
**Answer: a) the elder-mediation system.**

**17. (Sub lesson 9)** Works built from local histories can teach —
a) peace, unity and mutual understanding  b) only dates  c) nothing
**Answer: a) peace, unity and mutual understanding.**

**18. (Sub lesson 1)** The first dramatic performance was —
a) in 6th-century BC Greece  b) in Rome, 1900  c) in Ethiopia, 1800
**Answer: a) in 6th-century BC Greece.**

**19. (Sub lesson 1)** 'Theatron' is —
a) Greek — a place of seeing  b) a Latin coin  c) an Amharic drum
**Answer: a) Greek — a place of seeing.**

**20. (Sub lesson 1)** Fig. 22 shows —
a) the outsides of theatre performance places  b) costumes  c) a film set
**Answer: a) the outsides of theatre performance places.**
