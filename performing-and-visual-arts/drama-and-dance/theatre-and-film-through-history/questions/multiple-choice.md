# Theatre and Film Through History — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The word theatre comes from the Greek theatron, meaning…
  a) A place of seeing b) A place of sleeping c) A market
**Answer: a) A place of seeing** — theatron is a place of seeing — viewing is theatre's root.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which is a type of BOTH theatre and film in the book's lists?
  a) Comedy b) Documentary c) Animation
**Answer: a) Comedy** — comedy appears in theatre's types and in film's nine types.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Hager Fikir Theatre was founded in…
  a) 1935, in Piazza b) 1923, in Arada c) 1955, in Gondar
**Answer: a) 1935, in Piazza** — founded 1935 in Piazza — the oldest indigenous theatre in Africa, per the guide.

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** The guide roots Ethiopian theatre in…
  a) Plays, rituals, church ceremonies and school drama b) Only written scripts c) Only radio
**Answer: a) Plays, rituals, church ceremonies and school drama** — everyday public life — plays, rituals, ceremonies, school drama.

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** African film history is told through which two eras?
  a) The colonial era and the post-independence era b) The stone era and the iron era c) The radio era and the television era
**Answer: a) The colonial era and the post-independence era** — colonial-era and post-independence-era African film.
