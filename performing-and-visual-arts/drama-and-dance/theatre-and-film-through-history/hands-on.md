# Hands-on — Theatre and Film Through History

The lesson with your hands, your feet and your voice together, not only your eyes.

## Timeline on the wall
Rule a long timeline. Pin the lesson's stops in order: 6th-century BC Greece; 1913 Fabula; 1923 Seytan Bet; 1935 Hager Fikir; 1955 National Theatre; 1957 E.C. first Ethiopian film; 1978 Film Center. Walk it and read it aloud.

## Live or projected?
Act one tiny scene live. Then 'film' it by framing it in a cardboard camera while a narrator describes it. The class lists what the film gained (travel, replay) and what it lost (the living audience).
