# Video script — Theatre and Film Through History

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Theatre is a place of seeing — theatron. Live bodies, live audience.
Fabula, 1913: probably the first original African play.
Hager Fikir, 1935: the oldest indigenous theatre in Africa.
Film arrived with cameras and kings: Seytan Bet, the House of the Devil, 1923.
Africa's films tell Africa's experience — before independence, and after.
