# Hands-on — Women's Traditional Plays

The lesson with your hands, your feet and your voice together, not only your eyes.

## Asking the keepers
Follow the book's study method: in groups, ask the plays' melodies and poems from the women who keep them — or from the culture and tourism office, as the Wollo dance task directs — and write down what you are given, with the giver's name.

## Presenting with respect
Girls' groups prepare and present one of the four plays for the class, saying first whose play it is and where it belongs. The class receives it as guests of the tradition.
