# Women's Traditional Plays — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Shadey belongs to…
  a) Wag Himra b) Lasta Lalibela c) Harar
**Answer: a) Wag Himra** — Fig. 45 is Shadey in Wag Himra; Ashendye is Lasta Lalibela's.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In Exercise 3.4, the girls' groups present…
  a) Shadey, Ashendye, Solel and Engicha Neqela b) Only Nefijele c) The national anthem
**Answer: a) Shadey, Ashendye, Solel and Engicha Neqela** — the four women's plays — asking their melodies and poems.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Tebechisa appears in the book as…
  a) One of the pictured plays (Figs. 46–48) b) A national anthem c) A boys-only game
**Answer: a) One of the pictured plays (Figs. 46–48)** — Solel, Engicha Neqela and Tebechisa players are pictured in Figs. 46–48.
