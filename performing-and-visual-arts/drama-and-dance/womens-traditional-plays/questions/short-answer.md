# Women's Traditional Plays — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What do Wag Himra women play with at Shadei?
**Answer: Shadei flowers.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In which kind of chapter does the book place the women's plays?
**Answer: The history chapter — history in traditional plays.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** How are the plays' melodies and poems learned, in the book's method?
**Answer: By asking them — from the women who keep the plays.**
