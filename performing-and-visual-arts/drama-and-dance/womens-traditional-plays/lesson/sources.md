**Acceptance count: taught in 1 country (Ethiopia).**

The plays' melodies and poems are not transcribed anywhere in this lesson — the book's own study sends pupils to ask them from their keepers, and the lesson keeps that direction instead of printing what the placements do not give. Nefijele and Tebechisa are named as the placements name them (Figs. 46–48 and the mixed-group exercise), without inventing rules for them beyond the exercise the placement prints.
