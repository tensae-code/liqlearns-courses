# Video script — Carabiné Dance of the Dominican Republic

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

On tiptoe! The caller calls, the circle turns.

This is the carabiné — older than merengue, born with soldiers dancing, carbine on shoulder.

Sway, circle, change partners… and bow.

That is the **carabiné dance**.
