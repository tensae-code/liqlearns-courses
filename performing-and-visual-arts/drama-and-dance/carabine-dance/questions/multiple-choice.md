# Carabiné Dance of the Dominican Republic — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The carabiné is a folk dance of…
a) the Dominican Republic  b) France only  c) Spain only
**Answer: a) the Dominican Republic**.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** It is said the first carabiné dancers were…
a) soldiers dancing with the carbine on the shoulder  b) sailors  c) schoolchildren
**Answer: a) soldiers dancing with the carbine on the shoulder**.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The carabiné is an ancestor of…
a) merengue and kompa  b) the waltz  c) ballet
**Answer: a) merengue and kompa**.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The carabiné is danced…
a) on tiptoe  b) sitting down  c) on one foot only
**Answer: a) on tiptoe**.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The bastonero is…
a) the caller who leads the dance  b) the drummer  c) a costume
**Answer: a) the caller who leads the dance**.
