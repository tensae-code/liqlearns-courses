# Carabiné Dance of the Dominican Republic — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the closing bow of the dance.
**Answer: The reverencia.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What do the gentlemen do in the dance's turns?
**Answer: They circle and change ladies until each finds his partner — ron entre ron, circle upon circle.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Name two instruments that play the carabiné.
**Answer: Any two of: tambora, pandero, acordeón.**
