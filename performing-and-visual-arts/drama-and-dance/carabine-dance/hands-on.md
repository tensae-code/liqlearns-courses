# Hands-on — Carabiné Dance of the Dominican Republic

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Dance the order
In a circle, practise the order slowly: the opening sway, circling and changing partners, and the closing reverencia — with one learner calling as bastonero.

## Dress the dance
Draw the two dancers in their carabiné dress: her two-pleat skirt, side bun and rose; his blue jacket, cane hat and bow tie.
