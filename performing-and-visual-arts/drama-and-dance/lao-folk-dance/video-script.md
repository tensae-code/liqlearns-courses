This lesson is Lao Folk Dance: Positions and Poses.

Lao folk dance technique — its named positions and poses and the language that describes them. — as Laos's school books teach it: the named hand positions (chip lum, chip yut, bae tang, bae thuai, bae prot, bae hua siang, bae hua tam) and foot positions (lom tin, lueam na, kadik ao, kadik long, kadik na, yok tin na, yok tin ao), each shown in photographs of a girl and a boy dancer

Watch, listen — then say it back in your own words.
