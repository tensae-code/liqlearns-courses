# Lesson plan — Lao Folk Dance: Positions and Poses

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Drama and Dance · **Lesson:** Lao Folk Dance: Positions and Poses
**Place in the curriculum:** Drama and Dance (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Lao folk dance technique — its named positions and poses and the language that describes them.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this course in the same title.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Music G6 teacher guide — lao folk dance positions** — the named hand positions (chip lum, chip yut, bae tang, bae thuai, bae prot, bae hua siang, bae hua tam) and foot positions (lom tin, lueam na, kadik ao, kadik long, kadik na, yok tin na, yok tin ao), each shown in photographs of a girl and a boy dancer
2. **Arts & Crafts + Music G3 teacher guide, 2009 edition — lao folk dance positions** — the hand positions of Lao dance (the three jeeb finger positions, photographed) and the named body poses that carry feelings — smiling, loving, shy, happy, sad, welcoming — each photographed on pupils; the foundation positions of the hands (arms down, arms spread) and of the feet (the V stance and the side stance), photographed on a girl and a boy
3. **Arts & Crafts and Music G5 teacher guide, 2009 combined guide — lao folk dance positions** — the whole pose language of Lao dance in photographs — the jeeb hand positions, the standing poses, the poses that show feelings, and the dance walk; the numbered basic positions — arm poses 1–8 and foot poses 1–5, each photographed and described
4. **Music G1 teacher guide, 2020 edition — lao folk dance positions** — the first six dance poses, and how the jeeb hand position is made (photographs of the hand and of children in the poses); what the poses say — photographed poses that show feelings and greetings, each with its meaning
5. **Music G2 teacher guide, 2020 edition — lao folk dance positions** — the four basic folk dance poses, photographed and described one by one; seven poses and what each one says (photographs of children in the poses)
6. **Arts & Crafts and Music G4 teacher guide, 2009 combined guide — lao folk dance positions** — the folk dance poses and what each pose says; the technique of the folk dance — the movements of the hands and of the feet
7. **Arts & Crafts and Music G1 teacher guides, original editions — lao folk dance positions** — the folk dance poses and what each pose says
8. **Arts & Crafts and Music G2 teacher guides, original editions — lao folk dance positions** — the folk dance poses and what each pose says
