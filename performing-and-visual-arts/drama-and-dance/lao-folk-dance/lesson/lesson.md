# Lao Folk Dance: Positions and Poses

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about lao folk dance: positions and poses. Then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — Music G6 teacher guide — lao folk dance positions

The named hand positions (chip lum, chip yut, bae tang, bae thuai, bae prot, bae hua siang, bae hua tam) and foot positions (lom tin, lueam na, kadik ao, kadik long, kadik na, yok tin na, yok tin ao), each shown in photographs of a girl and a boy dancer

## Sub lesson 2 — Arts & Crafts + Music G3 teacher guide, 2009 edition — lao folk dance positions

The hand positions of Lao dance (the three jeeb finger positions, photographed) and the named body poses that carry feelings — smiling, loving, shy, happy, sad, welcoming — each photographed on pupils; the foundation positions of the hands (arms down, arms spread) and of the feet (the V stance and the side stance), photographed on a girl and a boy

## Sub lesson 3 — Arts & Crafts and Music G5 teacher guide, 2009 combined guide — lao folk dance positions

The whole pose language of Lao dance in photographs — the jeeb hand positions, the standing poses, the poses that show feelings, and the dance walk; the numbered basic positions — arm poses 1–8 and foot poses 1–5, each photographed and described

## Sub lesson 4 — Music G1 teacher guide, 2020 edition — lao folk dance positions

The first six dance poses, and how the jeeb hand position is made (photographs of the hand and of children in the poses); what the poses say — photographed poses that show feelings and greetings, each with its meaning

## Sub lesson 5 — Music G2 teacher guide, 2020 edition — lao folk dance positions

The four basic folk dance poses, photographed and described one by one; seven poses and what each one says (photographs of children in the poses)

## Sub lesson 6 — Arts & Crafts and Music G4 teacher guide, 2009 combined guide — lao folk dance positions

The folk dance poses and what each pose says; the technique of the folk dance — the movements of the hands and of the feet

## Sub lesson 7 — Arts & Crafts and Music G1 teacher guides, original editions — lao folk dance positions

The folk dance poses and what each pose says

## Sub lesson 8 — Arts & Crafts and Music G2 teacher guides, original editions — lao folk dance positions

The folk dance poses and what each pose says
