## Sub lesson 1 — The Theatrical Text — A Creator from My Country

Rukus bin Zayed Al-Uzaizi (born Madaba 1903, died Amman 2004), Arabic teacher and Jordan's first press correspondent, twice president of the Jordanian Writers Association and holder of the King Hussein Order of Excellence in Literature 1977, whose books on Jordan's people, customs and antiquities made him a founding figure of Jordanian letters.

## Sub lesson 2 — Arab Theatre

Arab theatre's growth — poetry recited at Suq Ukaz and the orator's theatrical presence behind it; Maroun Al-Naqqash, pioneer of organised Arab theatre, with his verse Al-Bakhil after Molière; Abu Khalil Al-Qabbani bringing folk heritage to the stage; Tawfiq Al-Hakim's Ahl Al-Kahf and Pygmalion; then Jordan's own theatre from the 1960s, its pioneers Nabil Sawalha and Khalid Al-Tarifi, Hani Snobar "father of Jordanian theatre", and the Jerash and Jordan theatre festivals.
