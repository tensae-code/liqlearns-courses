# Video script — Arab Theatre

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is Arab Theatre.

Rukus bin Zayed Al-Uzaizi (born Madaba 1903, died Amman 2004), Arabic teacher and Jordan's first press correspondent, twice president of the Jordanian Writers Association and holder of the King Hussein Order of Excellence in Literature 1977, whose books on Jordan's people, customs and antiquities made him a founding figure of Jordanian letters.

Watch, listen — then say it back in your own words.
