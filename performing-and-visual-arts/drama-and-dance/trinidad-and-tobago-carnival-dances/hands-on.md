# Hands-on — Trinidad and Tobago Carnival Dances and Characters

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Research a character
Choose one Traditional Carnival Character and prepare the four-part presentation the curriculum sets: origin, costume, behaviour, movement.

## Isolations from the mas
Take body isolations from the Sailor and Jab Molassie dances and build a short movement phrase from them, to Parang music.
