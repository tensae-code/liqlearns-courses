# Video script — Trinidad and Tobago Carnival Dances and Characters

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Canboulay — where Carnival begins.

Sailor Mas! Jab Molassie! The characters take the road.

Baby Doll, Midnight Robber, Moko Jumbie — each one different.

And the makers — McBurnie, Johnson, Edwards — built this dance.

Carnival is Trinidad and Tobago dancing its history.
