# Lesson plan — Trinidad and Tobago Carnival Dances and Characters

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Drama and Dance · **Lesson:** Trinidad and Tobago Carnival Dances and Characters
**Place in the curriculum:** Drama and Dance (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Carnival in Trinidad and Tobago — canboulay and Carnival's origins, its mas dances and Traditional Carnival Characters, and the makers who built the country's dance.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this course in the same title.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Canboulay and the origins of Carnival** — Meet/part and follow/lead learned inside the Canboulay scenario; the French and African influences in Carnival's history.
2. **Carnival character dances** — Sailor Mas and Jab Molassie taught inside the dynamics/heritage work, with Parang music and movement; body isolations taken from the Sailor and Jab Molassie dances.
3. **Traditional Carnival Characters** — The characters as drama content — Baby Doll, Dame Lorraine, Indian Mas, Jab Molassie, Midnight Robber, Minstrels, Moko Jumbies, Pierrot Grenade, Sailor Mas, stick fighter and Burrokeet — with costume construction for them, and researched presentations on at least four characters (origin, costume, behaviour, movement).
4. **The makers who built its dance** — Beryl McBurnie, Jean Coggins-Simmons, Astor Johnson and Julia Edwards studied as the people who built Trinidad and Tobago dance, and a dance icon/group analysed.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
