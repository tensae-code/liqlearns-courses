# Trinidad and Tobago Carnival Dances and Characters — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Canboulay is taught with the influences of…
a) French and African history in Carnival  b) only modern pop music  c) no history
**Answer: a) French and African history in Carnival**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Sailor Mas and Jab Molassie are taught as…
a) Carnival character dances  b) ballet roles  c) silent statues
**Answer: a) Carnival character dances**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which is a Traditional Carnival Character?
a) Moko Jumbies  b) a traffic policeman  c) a school prefect
**Answer: a) Moko Jumbies**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** A researched character presentation covers…
a) origin, costume, behaviour and movement  b) only the costume's colour  c) only the music
**Answer: a) origin, costume, behaviour and movement**

**5. (Sub lesson 4)** Beryl McBurnie is studied as…
a) one of the makers who built Trinidad and Tobago dance  b) a foreign visitor  c) a costume seller
**Answer: a) one of the makers who built Trinidad and Tobago dance**
