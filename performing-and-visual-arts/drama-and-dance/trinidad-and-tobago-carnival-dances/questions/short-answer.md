# Trinidad and Tobago Carnival Dances and Characters — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** From which two dances are body isolations taken?
**Answer: The Sailor and Jab Molassie dances.**

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Name four Traditional Carnival Characters.
**Answer: Any four of: Baby Doll, Dame Lorraine, Indian Mas, Jab Molassie, Midnight Robber, Minstrels, Moko Jumbies, Pierrot Grenade, Sailor Mas, stick fighter, Burrokeet.**

**3. (Sub lesson 4)** Name two makers who built Trinidad and Tobago dance.
**Answer: Any two of: Beryl McBurnie, Jean Coggins-Simmons, Astor Johnson, Julia Edwards.**
