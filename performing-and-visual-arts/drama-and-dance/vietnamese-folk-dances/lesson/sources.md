**Book 1:** *Âm nhạc 2 (student book, Vietnamese)* — Title as printed: Âm nhạc 2 (Kết nối tri thức với cuộc sống) Grade: 2 (student/teacher: STUDENT) Publisher: Nhà xuất bản Giáo dục Việt Nam; year 2021 (5th reprint); ISBN 978-604-0-25646-1 PDF pages: 62 (cover/title page verified against the record title) Verdict: COMPLETE Vietnam's own way: eight themes on five strands (Sing, Listen, Read music, Instruments, Appreciate). Reading pieces are numbered and grow note by note (La joins in Reading No. 1; eighth notes in No. 3; rests in No. 4), always with hand signs and body percussion. The folk theme (Theme 2, "I love folk melodies") teaches two Vietnamese instruments outright — song loan and đàn bầu — and the appreciation story honours composer Phạm Tuyên and his Ê-đê-inspired elephant song. Excluded per brief: Semester-1 review (printed pp. 30–31) and assessment (p. 32); year-end review (p. 58) and assessment (p. 59); the glossary of music terms (p. 60, inventory only). **Book 2:** *Âm nhạc 3 (student book, Vietnamese)* — Title as printed: Âm nhạc 3 (Kết nối tri thức với cuộc sống) Grade: 3 (student/teacher: STUDENT) Publisher: Nhà xuất bản Giáo dục Việt Nam; year 2022 (4th reprint); ISBN 978-604-0-30705-7 PDF pages: 66 (cover/title page verified against the record title) Verdict: COMPLETE Vietnam's own way: Grade 3 gives the National Anthem its own theme (composer, occasion, solemn stance), builds a whole folk theme from the peoples of the Central Highlands (a Gia-rai-based song, a t'rưng listening piece, a Xê-đăng lullaby), and compares the lullabies of Vietnam's three regions inside a mother-and-child story.
