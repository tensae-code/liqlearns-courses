# Vietnamese Folk Dances

## Do it first

## Sub lesson 1 — Vietnam Grade 2 Âm nhạc 2

Listening to the music of Múa sạp (Mai Sao) — the bamboo-pole dance, dancers stepping between clapping poles — and striking the accompaniment to its rhythm; "Múa sư tử thật là vui" (Phạm Tuyên) — the Mid-Autumn lion dance: the lion's head bobs, its tail follows, everyone claps to the festive drums — listen and move your body to its rhythm

## Sub lesson 2 — Vietnam Grade 3 Âm nhạc 3

The song "Múa lân" (Y Vân – Phùng Sửu) — nothing is more fun than the Mid-Autumn full moon: I beat the gong, you beat the drum; you play ông Địa, I dance the lion; you carry the lantern, I dance with the puppet
