# Lesson plan — Vietnamese Folk Dances

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Drama and Dance · **Lesson:** Vietnamese Folk Dances
**Place in the curriculum:** Drama and Dance (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Vietnam's folk dances, including the bamboo-pole dance Múa sạp and the lion dance song.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this course in the same title.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Vietnam Grade 2 Âm nhạc 2** — Listening to the music of Múa sạp (Mai Sao) — the bamboo-pole dance, dancers stepping between clapping poles — and striking the accompaniment to its rhythm; "Múa sư tử thật là vui" (Phạm Tuyên) — the Mid-Autumn lion dance: the lion's head bobs, its tail follows, everyone claps to the festive drums — listen and move your body to its rhythm
2. **Vietnam Grade 3 Âm nhạc 3** — The song "Múa lân" (Y Vân – Phùng Sửu) — nothing is more fun than the Mid-Autumn full moon: I beat the gong, you beat the drum; you play ông Địa, I dance the lion; you carry the lantern, I dance with the puppet
