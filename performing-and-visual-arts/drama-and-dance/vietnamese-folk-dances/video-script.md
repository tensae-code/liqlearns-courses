This lesson is Vietnamese Folk Dances.

Vietnam's folk dances, including the bamboo-pole dance Múa sạp and the lion dance song. — as Vietnam's school books teach it: Listening to the music of Múa sạp (Mai Sao) — the bamboo-pole dance, dancers stepping between clapping poles — and striking the accompaniment to its rhythm; "Múa sư tử thật là vui" (Phạm Tuyên) — the Mid-Autumn lion dance: the lion's head bobs, its tail follows, everyone claps to the festive drums — listen and move your body to its rhythm.

Watch, listen — then say it back in your own words.
