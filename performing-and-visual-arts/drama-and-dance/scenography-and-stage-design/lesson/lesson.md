## Sub lesson 1 — Stage space and scenography

**Stage space is the place where a theatre performance is performed.** A stage (сцена/позорница) can be in an **open or a closed space**. **Arranging the space of the stage is called scenography.** Theatre has existed since ancient times — the first stages were **wooden planks in the open**, the word "scene" comes from Greek and means a wooden structure, and in ancient Greece **nature itself completed the scenography**, as at **Epidaurus**. Open and closed stages each have good and bad sides. The Grade 2 making task: **make a stage from cardboard boxes, colour it and decorate it** — and on it belong the costumes, masks and props made specially for each performance.

## Sub lesson 2 — Light and special effects on stage

Today there are many ways to achieve **special effects**, used in theatre and in film. The stage is prepared with special care: **with special lighting, our experience of the performance changes** — a scene lit in **cold (blue) light** feels different from one lit in **warm (yellow) light** — and **painted backdrops can create the impression that the stage stretches into the distance**.

## Sub lesson 3 — Build your own stage space

Work **in groups of five**. Each group shapes **its own stage space**: agree that each of you brings from home a part of the materials you will need — **foils, fabrics, mirrors, card, strips, self-adhesive paper in colour, LED strips, sticky tape in colour** and other things. **Choose a story and one scene from that story**, then make the stage space for it. At the end, **make an exhibition of all the works**.
