# Video script — Scenography and Stage Design

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Before the actors step out, the stage is already speaking.

Its space, arranged with care — that is scenography.

Long ago the stage was wooden planks under the open sky,

and nature herself finished the picture.

Blue light feels cold. Yellow light feels warm.

Build your stage — and the story has a home.
