# Scenography and Stage Design — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Where can a stage be?
**Answer: In an open space or in a closed space.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** At Epidaurus in ancient Greece, what completed the scenography?
**Answer: Nature itself.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What can painted backdrops create the impression of?
**Answer: That the stage stretches into the distance.**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Name three materials the groups bring for building a stage space.
**Answer: Any three of: foils, fabrics, mirrors, card, strips, coloured self-adhesive paper, LED strips, coloured sticky tape.**
