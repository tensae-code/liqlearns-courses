# Scenography and Stage Design — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The word "scene" comes from Greek and means a wooden structure.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Stage lighting makes no difference to how a performance feels.
**Answer: False** — special lighting changes our experience of the performance.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Warm light on stage is yellow light.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** The stage-building exercise ends with an exhibition of all the works.
**Answer: True**
