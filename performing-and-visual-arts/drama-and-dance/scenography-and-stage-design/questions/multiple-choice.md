# Scenography and Stage Design — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Arranging the space of the stage is called…
a) scenography  b) calligraphy  c) origami
**Answer: a) scenography**.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The first stages were…
a) wooden planks in the open  b) glass towers  c) underground rooms
**Answer: a) wooden planks in the open**.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A scene lit in cold light is coloured…
a) blue  b) yellow  c) red
**Answer: a) blue**.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** In the stage-building exercise, each group has…
a) five pupils  b) two pupils  c) the whole school
**Answer: a) five pupils**.
