# Hands-on — Scenography and Stage Design

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## A stage from boxes
Make a small stage from cardboard boxes. Colour it and decorate it. Stand two paper figures on it and say what scene is about to happen.

## Cold light, warm light
Draw the same stage scene twice — once as if lit in cold blue light, once in warm yellow light. Show both and let your companion name the feeling of each.

## The group stage
In a group of five, choose a story and one scene, gather foils, fabrics, card and strips, and build the stage space for that scene. Exhibit it beside the other groups' stages.
