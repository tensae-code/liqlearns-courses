# Dominican Comedy Characters

## Do it first

Quick start: One actor walks in… and three characters walk out. Then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — Meeting the characters by name and persona

The Grade 3 book meets the **Dominican comedy characters** **by name and persona** — who each character is, and the manner that makes it itself. Among them stands **Raymond Pozo and his characters**, the country's known comedy actor.

## Sub lesson 2 — One actor, several characters

The book's lesson inside the lesson: **one actor, several characters** — each a **different personage with its own name and manner**. The actor stays one person; the characters are many, and the audience tells them apart by name and manner alone.
