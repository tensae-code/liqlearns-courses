# Dominican Comedy Characters — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Dominican comedy characters are met by…
a) name and persona  b) costume price  c) age
**Answer: a) name and persona**.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** One named Dominican comedy actor is…
a) Raymond Pozo  b) a painter  c) a singer only
**Answer: a) Raymond Pozo**.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** One actor can play…
a) several characters  b) only himself  c) no characters
**Answer: a) several characters**.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Each character has…
a) its own name and manner  b) the same name  c) no manner
**Answer: a) its own name and manner**.

**5. (Sub lesson 1)** The characters belong to…
a) the country's comedy stage and screen  b) another country  c) the carnival only
**Answer: a) the country's comedy stage and screen**.
