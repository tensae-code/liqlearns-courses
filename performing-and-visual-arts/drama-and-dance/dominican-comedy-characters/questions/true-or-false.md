# Dominican Comedy Characters — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The characters in this lesson are Dominican.
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** One actor, several characters — each is the same personage.
**Answer: False.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** A persona is the character's own way of being.
**Answer: True.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A character's manner helps the audience tell characters apart.
**Answer: True.**
