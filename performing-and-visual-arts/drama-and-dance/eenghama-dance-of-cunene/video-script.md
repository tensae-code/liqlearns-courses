# Video script — Eenghama Dance of Cunene

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

**Eenghama of the peoples of Cunene.** The Eenghama of the peoples of Cunene province — singing and dancing with jumps and foot-stamping, pictured in the book with Herero singers. The dance is sung and danced together: the voice, the jump and the stamp are one act.

That is Eenghama Dance of Cunene — Angola's own books teach it this way.
