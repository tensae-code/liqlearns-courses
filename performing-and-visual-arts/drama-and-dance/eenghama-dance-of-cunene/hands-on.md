# Hands-on — Eenghama Dance of Cunene

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Eenghama of the peoples of Cunene
Work through this part with real materials or your own body, the way the book sets it: the Eenghama of the peoples of Cunene province — singing and dancing with jumps and foot-stamping, pictured in the book with Herero singers. The dance is sung and danced together: the voice, the jump and the stamp are one act. Show a companion what you made or did, and say what the part teaches.
