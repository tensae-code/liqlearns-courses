## Sub lesson 1 — Eenghama of the peoples of Cunene

The Eenghama of the peoples of Cunene province — singing and dancing with jumps and foot-stamping, pictured in the book with Herero singers. The dance is sung and danced together: the voice, the jump and the stamp are one act.
