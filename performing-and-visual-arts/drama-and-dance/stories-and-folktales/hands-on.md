# Hands-on — Stories and Folktales

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Tell it three ways
Take Story 1 (Selam's Photograph). Tell it once as yourself. Tell it again as Selam. Tell it a third time as the father lying in the flowers. Same happenings — three voices. Feel how a story changes shape with its teller, while its plot stays true.

## Plant the story's planting
If you can, plant a real seedling the story's way: dig the hole, place the seedling upright, cover the roots with soil, water it well. Care for it on the following days, as Alemu and Hewan did. The story becomes a thing growing outside your door.
