# Video script — Stories and Folktales

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Listen. A story is coming — told, not read.

Selam's father falls — into the flowers! Click!
Selam photographs HIM. Beautiful!

The good dog sulks… hears counsel… says sorry.
And promises. Truly.

Alemu and Hewan clean, and plant, and water…
and the village turns GREEN and beautiful.

Now — YOUR story. Tell it.
And tell us its lesson.
