# Stories and Folktales — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers: every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to.

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In Selam's Photograph, the father falls —
a) into a river  b) onto flowers, unhurt  c) down stairs
**Answer: b)** — he trips on a flower fence; the camera falls on grass, unbroken.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Who takes the beautiful final photograph?
a) Tesfu  b) the father  c) Selam — of her father among the flowers
**Answer: c).**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The obedient dog sulks because —
a) she is refused food  b) she is refused permission to follow Selemon to school  c) her house is painted
**Answer: b).**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Alemu and Hewan's village becomes beautiful through —
a) buying decorations  b) cleaning together and planting, watering and caring  c) painting the houses
**Answer: b).**

**5. (Sub lesson 4)** Telling folk tales is —
a) silent reading  b) oral literature as performance — tales and histories told aloud  c) a written exam
**Answer: b) oral literature as performance — tales and histories told aloud.**

**6. (Sub lesson 4)** የሃገረሰብ ተረትና ታሪኮች መንገር means —
a) telling the community's tales and histories  b) buying books  c) drawing maps
**Answer: a) telling the community's tales and histories.**

**7. (Sub lesson 4)** In the telling, the tale lives in —
a) the teller's performance and the listeners' gathering  b) a locked box  c) one printed page only
**Answer: a) the teller's performance and the listeners' gathering.**
