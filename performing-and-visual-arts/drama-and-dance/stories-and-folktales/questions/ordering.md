# Stories and Folktales — ordering

Sub-lesson markers: every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to.

Put the steps in the right order. The answer gives the right order.

**1. (Sub lesson 3)** Planting a seedling: (a) water it (b) dig the hole (c) cover the roots with soil (d) place the seedling upright
**Answer: b, d, c, a** — dig, place, cover, water.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Selam's Photograph: (a) Selam photographs her father among the flowers (b) the children ask for a picture in a green place (c) the father trips and falls, unhurt (d) the printed photo is beautiful
**Answer: b, c, a, d.**
