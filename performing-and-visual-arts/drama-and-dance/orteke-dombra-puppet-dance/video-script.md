This lesson is Orteke: The Dombra Puppet Dance.

Orteke: The Dombra Puppet Dance — Orteke, the Kazakh art in which a carved goat puppet dances on the dombra as it is played. — as Kazakhstan's school books teach it: the Kazakh puppet-goat dance: a carved goat dances on the dombra player's knee to the kui.

Watch, listen — then say it back in your own words.
