# Video script — Traditional Games

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Games travel — child to child, no book needed!
But learn them RIGHT.

**ሰኞ ማክሰኞ!** Hide and seek — **ድብብቆሽ!**
Have you seen my handkerchief?

**ድምቡሸ ገላ** — hold hands, TURN together!
**ቅልሞሽ** — five little stones. Count: one-yosh, two-yosh…
**ገመድ ዝላይ** — the rope turns… JUMP in time!
**ሳንካ** — hop the grid. Don't touch the lines!

Play them. Teach them. Keep them alive.
