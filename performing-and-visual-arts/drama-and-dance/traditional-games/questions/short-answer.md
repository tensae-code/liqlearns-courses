# Traditional Games — short answer

Sub-lesson markers: every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to.

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Sub lesson 3)** How does the ቅልሞሽ (Qilmosh) counting go?
**Answer:** ቅልም ቅልም ቅልሞሽ — counting one-yosh, two-yosh, three-yosh, four-yosh while playing with the five stones (the song's shape as placed; verses not transcribed).

**2. (Sub lesson 4)** What does the rope-jumping song ገመድ ዝለሉ (Gemed Zilelu) urge?
**Answer:** jumping to be strong in body.

**3. (Sub lesson 5)** Name four traditional play objects.
**Answer:** any four of: clay stove/pot stand, decorated footed bowl, lidded clay cooking pot, round woven tray, three-legged wooden stool, woven basket, jebena, rekebot box.
