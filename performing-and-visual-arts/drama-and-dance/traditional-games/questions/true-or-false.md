# Traditional Games — true or false

Sub-lesson markers: every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to.

Say if it is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** መሀረቤን ያያችሁ means 'Have You Seen My Handkerchief'.
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 4)** Rope jumping (ገመድ ዝላይ) can only be played alone.
**Answer: False** — it is played in groups or alone.

**3. (Sub lesson 4)** In ሳንካ (sanka), the hopper must not touch the grid's lines.
**Answer: True.**

**4. (Sub lesson 5)** A jebena is part of the coffee-ceremony set.
**Answer: True.**

**5. (Sub lesson 1)** The Practical Exercises opener teaches that the arts are only for looking at, never for doing.
**Answer: False** — the arts are for doing, with the whole body, together.
