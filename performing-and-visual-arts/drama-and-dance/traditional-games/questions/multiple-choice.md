# Traditional Games — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers: every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to.

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A traditional game is —
a) handed down and kept alive by playing  b) bought in a shop  c) played only once
**Answer: a).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** ድብብቆሽ (dibibqosh) is —
a) rope jumping  b) hide-and-seek  c) the five-stones game
**Answer: b) hide-and-seek.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** ድምቡሸ ገላ (Dimbushe Gela) is played —
a) alone, seated  b) in pairs, holding hand to hand, turning  c) with a long rope
**Answer: b).**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** ቅልሞሽ (Qilmosh) is played with —
a) five small stones  b) a ball  c) sticks only
**Answer: a) five small stones (ጠጠር).**

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** The rekebot holds —
a) grain  b) rows of small coffee cups  c) stones for games
**Answer: b)** — beside the jebena in the coffee-ceremony set.

**6. (Sub lesson 1)** The boy in the opening pictures is playing —
a) ቅልሞሽ (Qilmosh)  b) the ground-grid game ሳንካ (sanka), hopping with a stone  c) hide-and-seek
**Answer: b)** — a grid on the ground, a stone, and hopping, while a girl watches.
