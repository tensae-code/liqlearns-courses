# Lesson plan — Traditional Games

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Drama and Dance · **Lesson:** Traditional Games
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 4 — Drama and Dance (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the games children have always played — circle games, hiding games, the turning pair game ድምቡሸ ገላ (Dimbushe Gela), the five-stones game ቅልሞሽ (Qilmosh), rope jumping, the ground-grid game ሳንካ (sanka), and play with traditional things — each with its own way, learned by playing.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the child's own play — every learner arrives already knowing games. The Practical Exercises chapter opener folded in here is the books' doorway: the arts practised with the whole body, games among them.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees
from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before
it is proved:

1. **Opening — the arts in practice, and the traditional games** — the folded Practical Exercises opener: the arts practised by doing; the two pictured games — rope skipping and ሳንካ (sanka).
2. **Simple games of our area** — the area's simple games, the book's four named: ሰኞ ማክሰኞ (Senyo Maksenyo), ድብብቆሽ hide-and-seek, መሀረቤን ያያችሁ (Have You Seen My Handkerchief), እቴ እሜቴ የሎሚ ሽታ (Ete Emete).
3. **ድምቡሸ ገላ (Dimbushe Gela) and ቅልሞሽ (Qilmosh)** — ድምቡሸ ገላ (Dimbushe Gela) turning pairs; ቅልሞሽ (Qilmosh) five-stones counting game.
4. **Rope jumping — ገመድ ዝላይ (gemed zilay)** — ገመድ ዝላይ (gemed zilay) rope jumping with its song ገመድ ዝለሉ (Gemed Zilelu); the ሳንካ (sanka) grid hop.
5. **Playing with traditional things** — the traditional things of play: clay pot, woven tray, stool, basket, jebena and rekebot.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: nothing — children arrive knowing play. Circle Dances and Traditional Steps is a good neighbour. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name the two pictured opening games.
2. Name the four area games in Amharic and English.
3. Play ድምቡሸ ገላ (Dimbushe Gela) as a turning pair.
4. Say how ቅልሞሽ (Qilmosh) is played and counted.
5. Jump a turning rope in time; hop the ሳንካ (sanka) grid cleanly.
6. Name the traditional objects and their uses.

## What you need
A long rope, five small stones, chalk or a stick for the ground grid, and household traditional things to name. Nothing bought.

## If the learner is stuck
- Rope timing fails → turn the rope empty and clap its beat first; the jumper enters on a counted 'now', slow, before any speed.
- Grid hops touch lines → make the squares bigger; accuracy first, the smaller grid is the next level, not the first.
- The games' songs are placed by name and shape only — verses are never transcribed (see sources.md).
