# Traditional Games — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — Opening — the arts in practice, and the traditional games
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: say what a traditional game is, and name the two games in the opening pictures — rope skipping and ሳንካ (sanka).

## Sub lesson 2 — Simple games of our area
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: name the book's four area games and play them with friends.

## Sub lesson 3 — ድምቡሸ ገላ (Dimbushe Gela) and ቅልሞሽ (Qilmosh)
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: play ድምቡሸ ገላ (Dimbushe Gela) turning hand-in-hand in a pair, and say how ቅልሞሽ (Qilmosh) is played — five small stones, counting.

## Sub lesson 4 — Rope jumping — ገመድ ዝላይ (gemed zilay)
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: jump a turning long rope in time, and hop the ሳንካ (sanka) grid without touching the lines.

## Sub lesson 5 — Playing with traditional things
Sub lesson 5 questions from the type files. Pass mark: name the traditional play objects — clay pot, woven tray, stool, basket, jebena and rekebot — and say each one's use.
