# Hands-on — Shadow Theatre

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Build the screen
Make the screen from a cardboard lid with baking paper glued behind, and decorate its frame. Cut two story characters from black card and tape a plastic straw behind each.

## Hand shadows and the glove puppet
Play the hand-shadow game against the screen. Then sew a glove puppet from an old glove — buttons or beads for eyes and mouth, thread strands for hair — and give it one line in the show.
