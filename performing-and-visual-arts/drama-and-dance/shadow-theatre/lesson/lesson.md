# Shadow Theatre

## Do it first

Quick start: hold your hand between a lamp and a wall. What plays on the wall — your hand, or its shadow? Then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — Stage a shadow show

**Shadow Theatre**: stage a shadow show with puppets you build. Make the **screen from a cardboard lid with baking paper glued behind** it, and **decorate its frame**. **Draw your story's characters on black card, cut them out and tape a plastic straw behind each**. Put a **lamp behind the screen** — the puppets **block the light** and the audience sees **their shadows**.

## Sub lesson 2 — Where shadow theatre comes from

**Did You Know?** Shadow theatre is said to have **appeared in India**, its technique was **developed by the Chinese**, and it became known in **Europe only in the 18th century**. It was a **source of inspiration for the creators of animated drawings**. The page adds the **hand-shadow game**, and a **glove puppet sewn from an old glove** — buttons or beads for eyes and mouth, thread strands for hair.
