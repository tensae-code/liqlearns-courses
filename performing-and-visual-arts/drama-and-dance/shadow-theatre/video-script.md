# Video script — Shadow Theatre

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A cardboard lid. Baking paper behind it. That is the screen.

Black card figures — a straw behind each one.

The lamp goes behind. The figures block the light…

Shadows! The audience sees shadows dance.

That is **shadow theatre** — an art thousands of years on the road.
