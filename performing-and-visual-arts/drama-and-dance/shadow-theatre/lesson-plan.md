# Lesson plan — Shadow Theatre

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Drama and Dance · **Lesson:** Shadow Theatre
**Place in the curriculum:** Drama and Dance (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** shadow theatre — building the screen and the black-card shadow puppets, playing the show with a lamp, and the art's long road from India through China to Europe, with hand shadows and a glove puppet.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this course in the same title.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Stage a shadow show** — make the screen from a cardboard lid with baking paper glued behind, decorate its frame, draw your story's characters on black card, cut them out and tape a plastic straw behind each; put a lamp behind the screen — the puppets block the light and the audience sees their shadows.
2. **Where shadow theatre comes from** — shadow theatre is said to have appeared in India, its technique developed by the Chinese, and it became known in Europe only in the 18th century; it inspired the creators of animated drawings; with the hand-shadow game and a glove puppet sewn from an old glove.
