# Shadow Theatre — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is taped behind each shadow puppet, to hold it?
**Answer: A plastic straw.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which art did shadow theatre inspire?
**Answer: Animated drawings (animation).**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is the glove puppet sewn from?
**Answer: An old glove — with buttons or beads for eyes and mouth and thread strands for hair.**
