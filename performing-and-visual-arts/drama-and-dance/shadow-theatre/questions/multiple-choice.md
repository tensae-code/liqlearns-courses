# Shadow Theatre — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The shadow screen is made from…
a) a cardboard lid with baking paper glued behind  b) a window  c) a mirror
**Answer: a) a cardboard lid with baking paper glued behind**.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The shadow puppets are drawn on…
a) black card  b) white cloth  c) clay
**Answer: a) black card** — cut out, with a plastic straw taped behind each.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The audience sees…
a) the puppets' shadows  b) the lamp  c) the puppeteers
**Answer: a) the puppets' shadows** — the puppets block the light.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Shadow theatre is said to have appeared in…
a) India  b) France  c) Brazil
**Answer: a) India** — its technique developed by the Chinese.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Shadow theatre became known in Europe in…
a) the 18th century  b) the 1st century  c) the 21st century
**Answer: a) the 18th century**.
