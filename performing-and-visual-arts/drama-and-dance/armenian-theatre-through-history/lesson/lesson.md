## Sub lesson 3 — Theatre in Ancient Greece

Theatre in Ancient Greece — satyrs of the mythology, the clay or tin ticket-token with its row and seat, honoured citizens in the first row, actors on high cothurni in long or short chlamys cloaks.

## Sub lesson 4 — Medieval theatre in Europe

Medieval theatre in Europe — liturgical drama staged not only inside the church but in the yard; the troubadours' work a mixture of satirical and poetic arts — the beginning of the Renaissance's future theatre; the morality plays' authors were early humanists and school lecturers.
