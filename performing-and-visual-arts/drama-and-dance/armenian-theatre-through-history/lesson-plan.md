# Lesson plan — Armenian Theatre Through History

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Drama and Dance · **Lesson:** Armenian Theatre Through History
**Place in the curriculum:** Drama and Dance (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Armenian Theatre Through History — Armenia's own teaching of this topic, kept as named parts: THEATRE IN ANCIENT ARMENIA — the stage in the old Armenian world
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the course's earlier lessons in its title; recommended first, never forced.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of ..." in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **THEATRE IN ANCIENT ARMENIA** — THEATRE IN ANCIENT ARMENIA — the stage in the old Armenian world
2. **MEDIEVAL ARMENIAN THEATRE** — MEDIEVAL ARMENIAN THEATRE — the stage inside medieval Armenia
3. **Theatre in Ancient Greece** — Theatre in Ancient Greece — satyrs of the mythology, the clay or tin ticket-token with its row and seat, honoured citizens in the first row, actors on high cothurni in long or short chlamys cloaks
4. **Medieval theatre in Europe** — Medieval theatre in Europe — liturgical drama staged not only inside the church but in the yard; the troubadours' work a mixture of satirical and poetic arts — the beginning of the Renaissance's future theatre; the morality plays' authors were early humanists and school lecturers
5. **Theatre in Armenia from the ancient period to Hellenism** — Theatre in Armenia from the ancient period to Hellenism — the earliest Armenian stages and performers
6. **Theatre in medieval Armenia and its kinds** — Theatre in medieval Armenia and its kinds: folk dramatic theatre, tightrope walking (լարախաղացություն), religious-ecclesiastical theatre, secular professional theatre, theatre at court — Armenia's own medieval stages
7. **The foundations of the ARMENIAN NEW THEATRE** — The foundations of the ARMENIAN NEW THEATRE — where the modern Armenian stage begins
8. **The MKHITARYAN Armenian school theatre** — The MKHITARYAN Armenian school theatre — the school stage of the Mkhitaryan fathers as an Armenian theatre cradle
