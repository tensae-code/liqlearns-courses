This is Armenia's own lesson.

**THEATRE IN ANCIENT ARMENIA.** THEATRE IN ANCIENT ARMENIA — the stage in the old Armenian world.

**MEDIEVAL ARMENIAN THEATRE.** MEDIEVAL ARMENIAN THEATRE — the stage inside medieval Armenia.

**Theatre in Ancient Greece.** Theatre in Ancient Greece — satyrs of the mythology, the clay or tin ticket-token with its row and seat, honoured citizens in the first row, actors on high cothurni in long or short chlamys cloaks.

**Medieval theatre in Europe.** Medieval theatre in Europe — liturgical drama staged not only inside the church but in the yard; the troubadours' work a mixture of satirical and poetic arts — the beginning of the Renaissance's future theatre; the morality plays' authors were early humanists and school lecturers.

**Theatre in Armenia from the ancient period to Hellenism.** Theatre in Armenia from the ancient period to Hellenism — the earliest Armenian stages and performers.

**Theatre in medieval Armenia and its kinds.** Theatre in medieval Armenia and its kinds: folk dramatic theatre, tightrope walking (լարախաղացություն), religious-ecclesiastical theatre, secular professional theatre, theatre at court — Armenia's own medieval stages.

**The foundations of the ARMENIAN NEW THEATRE.** The foundations of the ARMENIAN NEW THEATRE — where the modern Armenian stage begins.

**The MKHITARYAN Armenian school theatre.** The MKHITARYAN Armenian school theatre — the school stage of the Mkhitaryan fathers as an Armenian theatre cradle.

That is Armenian Theatre Through History — Armenia's way, kept by name.
