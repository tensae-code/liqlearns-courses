# Hands-on — Dances of Guatemala's Peoples

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## What does this dance say?
Watch or recall a dance of your own community. Tell a partner what it means — what the body is saying — before you show any step.

## A respectful first step
Learn one short movement from a dance of another people, from someone who knows it or a recording, and name the people it belongs to as you practise it.
