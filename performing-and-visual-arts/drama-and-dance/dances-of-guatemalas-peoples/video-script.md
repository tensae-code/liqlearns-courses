# Video script — Dances of Guatemala's Peoples

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

My body speaks — that is the name of the experience.

Every people's dance carries a meaning. First ask what the dance says.

Then begin, respectfully, into the choreography and technique of the dances of Guatemala's other peoples, and of Mesoamerica.
