# Dances of Guatemala's Peoples — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does a dance do, in the experience's name?
**Answer: The body speaks — a dance carries a meaning.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What two things does the initiation enter into?
**Answer: The choreographies and the techniques of the dances.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Whose dances does the initiation reach beyond your own people's?
**Answer: The dances of Guatemala's other peoples and of Mesoamerica.**
