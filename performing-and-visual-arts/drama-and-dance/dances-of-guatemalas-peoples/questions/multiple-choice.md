# Dances of Guatemala's Peoples — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Grade 4 experience is called…
a) Mi cuerpo habla — My Body Speaks  b) Publicarte  c) Conviviendo contigo
**Answer: a) Mi cuerpo habla — My Body Speaks.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** According to the theme, dances carry…
a) meanings  b) only exercise  c) no message
**Answer: a) meanings.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Learning a dance begins with…
a) asking what it means  b) the fastest steps  c) the costume
**Answer: a) asking what it means.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The initiation is into the dances of…
a) Guatemala's other peoples and Mesoamerica  b) only Europe  c) only one village
**Answer: a) Guatemala's other peoples and Mesoamerica.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Choreography and technique mean…
a) the shaped movement and the way of doing it  b) the poster and the tickets  c) the music only
**Answer: a) the shaped movement and the way of doing it.**
