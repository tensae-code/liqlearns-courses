# Video script — Fon Jampa Muang Lao

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is Fon Jampa Muang Lao.

The Lao folk dance for Jampa Muang Lao, its poses and the whole dance performed. — as Laos's school books teach it: the classical dance's 55 named poses taught one by one from photographs of a boy dancer — each pose's hand shape (chip), arm line, head turn and foot position described in words; dancing the full pose sequence (poses 1 to the end) to the song, in groups, pairs and solo, keeping the rhythm

Watch, listen — then say it back in your own words.
