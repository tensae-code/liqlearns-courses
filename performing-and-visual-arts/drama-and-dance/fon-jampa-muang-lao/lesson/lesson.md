## Sub lesson 1 — Music G11 teacher guide — Fon Jampa Muang Lao

The classical dance's 55 named poses taught one by one from photographs of a boy dancer — each pose's hand shape (chip), arm line, head turn and foot position described in words; dancing the full pose sequence (poses 1 to the end) to the song, in groups, pairs and solo, keeping the rhythm
