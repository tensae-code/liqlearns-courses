# Hands-on — Making Art Together

Real things to do with what this lesson teaches — at school or at home, with people around you.

## One song, many voices
The class sings a known song together, then in two answering groups. Notice: the song holds everyone, strong and shy singers alike.

## Circle dance, shared step
In a circle, the class learns one shared step and dances it together to a beat — everyone inside the dance, nobody watching from outside.
