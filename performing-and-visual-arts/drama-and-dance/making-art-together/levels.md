# Making Art Together — sub lessons and depth

This is the depth record for the lesson, re-expressed as sub lessons: each sub lesson is one step up in depth, proved before the next opens.

## Sub lesson 1 — Group singing (teacher guide — evidence)
Pass mark: the learner can sing as a group and say what group singing gives each singer. The gate draws from this sub lesson's whole question set.

## Sub lesson 2 — Group dance and role play in a group (teacher guide — evidence)
Pass mark: the learner can name group dance and group role play and their shared rule. The gate draws from this sub lesson's whole question set.
