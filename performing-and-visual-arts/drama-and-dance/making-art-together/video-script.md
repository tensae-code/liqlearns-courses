# Video script — Making Art Together

A short read-aloud script in a super-simple voice, made later as the lesson's video. It follows the lesson's own order and names only what the lesson teaches.

---

Some arts we do alone. These we do together.
Group singing — የቡድን ዝማሬ — one song, many voices.
Group dance, and role play in a group — የሚና ጨዋታ.
Together, the arts connect the whole class.
