# Lesson plan — Making Art Together

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Drama and Dance · **Lesson:** Making Art Together
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 4 — Drama and Dance (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the arts done together and connected across the class: group singing (የቡድን ዝማሬ), group dance and role play in a group (የሚና ጨዋታ) — the Grade 1 guide's Connection chapter.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the learner's first singing, dancing and role play — this lesson joins them: the same arts, done as a group and connected.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the
exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees
from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before
it is proved:

1. **Group singing (teacher guide — evidence)** — የቡድን ዝማሬ — singing as a group, the chapter's first connection.
2. **Group dance and role play in a group (teacher guide — evidence)** — group dance and የሚና ጨዋታ — dancing and playing roles, together.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Acting Together. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name the chapter's three group arts.
2. Sing and dance as a group, each person inside the art.
3. Say what changes when art is made together.

## What you need
An open space for moving and acting. The pictures in the lesson. Nothing else.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — the whole class sings one known song together, then dances one shared step in a circle, before 'making art together' is named (lesson/lesson.md).
2. **The explanation** — group singing first, then group dance and group role play — one chapter's three ways of connecting (lesson/lesson.md).
   - (No picture slots beyond the described slots in the lesson text.)
3. **Say it to your companion** — Tell your companion the three group arts: group singing, group dance, group role play.
4. **Questions** — from questions/, served at the learner's level (levels.md).
5. **Hands-on** — hands-on.md.
6. **Do It Yourself** — With your class or friends, do one art together this week — a song everyone sings — and notice what changes when nobody performs alone.

## If the learner is stuck
Go back to the Do it first moment and do it again slowly, together. The explanation only names what the learner has already done or seen.
