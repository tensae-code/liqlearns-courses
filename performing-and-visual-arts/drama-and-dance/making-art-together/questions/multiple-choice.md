# Making Art Together — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Group singing (የቡድን ዝማሬ) is —
a) one singer performing  b) one song carried by many voices together  c) singing in secret
**Answer: b) one song carried by many voices together.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In group singing, shy singers —
a) must stay silent  b) join safely, carried by the group  c) sing alone first
**Answer: b) join safely, carried by the group.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The chapter's point is that the arts —
a) separate the class  b) connect the class  c) test the class
**Answer: b) connect the class.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Role play in a group (የሚና ጨዋታ) means —
a) one child acts everyone  b) acting people and moments together, each holding a part  c) reading a play silently
**Answer: b) acting people and moments together, each holding a part.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Group dance moves —
a) each dancer alone  b) together — circle and shared steps  c) without music or beat only sometimes
**Answer: b) together — circle and shared steps.**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** The shared rule of the three group arts is —
a) the best child leads everything  b) every child is inside the work  c) only singing counts
**Answer: b) every child is inside the work.**
