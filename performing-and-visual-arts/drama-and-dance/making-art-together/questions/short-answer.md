# Making Art Together — short answer

Answer in your own words, in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What changes when a song is sung by a group instead of one person?
**Answer: It belongs to everyone at once; nobody performs alone.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the three group arts of the chapter.
**Answer: Group singing, group dance, and role play in a group.**
