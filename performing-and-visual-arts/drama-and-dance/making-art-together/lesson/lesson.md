# Making Art Together

## Do it first
Sing one song — but all together. Now dance one step — but in a circle, together. Something changed, did it not? This lesson is about that change. It stands on teacher-guide evidence, and says so.

## Sub lesson 1 — Group singing (teacher guide — evidence) (teacher guide — evidence)

**Group singing (የቡድን ዝማሬ)** is the first of the chapter's group arts: one song carried by many voices together.

In group singing nobody performs alone. The strong voices carry, the shy voices join safely, and the song belongs to everyone at once. That belonging is the point of the chapter: the arts *connect* the class.

## Sub lesson 2 — Group dance and role play in a group (teacher guide — evidence) (teacher guide — evidence)

The chapter joins two more group arts to singing: **group dance** — moving together, in circle and shared steps — and **role play in a group (የሚና ጨዋታ)** — acting people and moments together, each person holding one part of the whole.

In all three arts the rule is the same: the work is bigger than any one child, and every child is inside it.

## Say it to your companion
Tell your companion the three group arts: group singing, group dance, group role play.

## Do It Yourself
With your class or friends, do one art together this week — a song everyone sings — and notice what changes when nobody performs alone.
