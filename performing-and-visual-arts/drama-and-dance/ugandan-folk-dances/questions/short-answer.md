# Ugandan Folk Dances — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name two things that differ from group to group in the dances.
**Answer: Any two of: motifs, instruments, costumes and props.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** To what is the dance choreographed?
**Answer: To the group's folk song.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What two things does the guide ask you to say about your performed dance?
**Answer: Three features of Ugandan dance in it, and the function at which it would be performed.**
