# Ugandan Folk Dances — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Ugandan folk dances have been…
a) invented this year  b) transmitted for centuries as an inheritance  c) imported last term
**Answer: b) transmitted for centuries as an inheritance.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Each ethnic group's dance has its own…
a) motifs, instruments, costumes and props  b) examination number  c) price tag
**Answer: a) motifs, instruments, costumes and props.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The dance is choreographed to…
a) silence  b) your folk song  c) a spoken lecture
**Answer: b) your folk song.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Costumes, props and accompaniment are…
a) improvised by the learners  b) bought from one shop  c) forbidden
**Answer: a) improvised by the learners** (teacher guide — evidence).

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** After performing, learners name…
a) three features of Ugandan dance in the piece, and its function  b) three foreign dances  c) nothing — the work ends at performing
**Answer: a) three features of Ugandan dance in the piece, and its function** (teacher guide — evidence).
