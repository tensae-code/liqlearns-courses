# Video script — Ugandan Folk Dances

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The song begins. The feet answer.

Every people dances its own dance — its steps, its dress, its drums.

Make the dance to your song. Dress it. Dance it.

That is Uganda's folk dances.
