# Lesson plan — Ugandan Folk Dances

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Drama and Dance · **Lesson:** Ugandan Folk Dances
**Place in the curriculum:** Drama and Dance (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Uganda's traditional folk dances — each ethnic group's own inheritance of motifs, instruments, costumes and props, choreographed to its folk song and performed.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this course in the same title.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **An inheritance with its own motifs, instruments, costumes and props** — Ugandan traditional folk dances are an inheritance transmitted for centuries; each ethnic group has its own motifs, instruments, costumes and props.
2. **Choreograph a dance to your folk song** — Choreograph a dance to the folk song, and improvise costumes, props and accompaniment.
3. **Perform it — and name its features and its function** — Perform the dance, name three features of Ugandan dance in it, and say the function at which it would be performed.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
