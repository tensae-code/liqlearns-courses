# Ugandan Folk Dances

## Do it first

Quick game: before you read on, say what you already know about this lesson's work — its name, its look, its story. Then read and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — An inheritance with its own motifs, instruments, costumes and props

Like the folk songs, Uganda's **traditional folk dances** are **an inheritance transmitted for centuries**. **Each ethnic group** has its own **motifs, instruments, costumes and props** — the dance belongs to a people, and you can tell whose it is.

## Sub lesson 2 — Choreograph a dance to your folk song

The learner's work: **choreograph a dance to your folk song** — and, as the Teacher's Guide adds, **improvise costumes, props and accompaniment** for it.

## Sub lesson 3 — Perform it — and name its features and its function

**Perform the dance.** Then do the guide's closing work: **name three features of Ugandan dance** in what you performed, and **the function at which it would be performed**.
