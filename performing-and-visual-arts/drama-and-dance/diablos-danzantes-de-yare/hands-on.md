# Hands-on — Diablos Danzantes de Yare

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Los Diablos danzantes de Yare
Work through this part with real materials or your own body, the way the book sets it: the Dancing Devils of Yare — the Corpus Christi ritual festival where Spanish, indigenous and African roots meet, its devil masks, and making a volumetric interpretation of a Yare devil mask in clay, wood or paper. Show a companion what you made or did, and say what the part teaches.

## Diablos danzantes: la tradición viva
Work through this part with real materials or your own body, the way the book sets it: the Diabladas or Dancing Devils of Corpus Christi, practised in the Americas since 1680 and kept at San Francisco de Yare since 1747 — the Catholic-African syncretism danced despite colonial bans; in 2012 Yare's brotherhood entered UNESCO's Representative List of the Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity together with 11 other Venezuelan brotherhoods (slug first proposed in Book 1). Show a companion what you made or did, and say what the part teaches.
