The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

**Los Diablos danzantes de Yare.** The Dancing Devils of Yare — the Corpus Christi ritual festival where Spanish, indigenous and African roots meet, its devil masks, and making a volumetric interpretation of a Yare devil mask in clay, wood or paper.

**Diablos danzantes: la tradición viva.** The Diabladas or Dancing Devils of Corpus Christi, practised in the Americas since 1680 and kept at San Francisco de Yare since 1747 — the Catholic-African syncretism danced despite colonial bans; in 2012 Yare's brotherhood entered UNESCO's Representative List of the Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity together with 11 other Venezuelan brotherhoods (slug first proposed in Book 1).

That is Diablos Danzantes de Yare — Venezuela's own books teach it this way.
