## Sub lesson 1 — Los Diablos danzantes de Yare

The Dancing Devils of Yare — the Corpus Christi ritual festival where Spanish, indigenous and African roots meet, its devil masks, and making a volumetric interpretation of a Yare devil mask in clay, wood or paper.

## Sub lesson 2 — Diablos danzantes: la tradición viva

The Diabladas or Dancing Devils of Corpus Christi, practised in the Americas since 1680 and kept at San Francisco de Yare since 1747 — the Catholic-African syncretism danced despite colonial bans; in 2012 Yare's brotherhood entered UNESCO's Representative List of the Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity together with 11 other Venezuelan brotherhoods (slug first proposed in Book 1).
