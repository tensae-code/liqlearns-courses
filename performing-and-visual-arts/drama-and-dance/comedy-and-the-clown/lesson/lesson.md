# Comedy and the Clown

## Do it first

Quick start: Big shoes. Bigger gestures. The audience is laughing already. Then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — Comedy in the performing arts

**Comedy** is part of the **performing arts**, and it speaks to our **sense of humour**. The Grade 3 book's *Hazme reír* — make me laugh — practises it directly: **making others laugh by imitating animals as well as possible**.

## Sub lesson 2 — The clown technique

The **clown** — *payaso* — is a comic performer, and the book teaches **the clown technique**: the clown's **exaggerated dress, acts, sayings and gestures** are what **make an audience laugh**. Nothing is small or hidden; everything is bigger than life.

## Sub lesson 3 — Staging a comedy

At Grade 10, *La vida es una comedia* — life is a comedy: the class **chooses a comedy**, notes its **subthemes, facts and key phrases**, **assigns the roles**, and **adjusts the staging through rehearsals** until the comedy stands on its feet.
