# Comedy and the Clown — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Comedy in the performing arts works through…
a) our sense of humour  b) silence  c) fear
**Answer: a) our sense of humour**.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** One way to make others laugh in the book is…
a) imitating animals as well as possible  b) shouting  c) hiding
**Answer: a) imitating animals as well as possible**.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A clown makes the audience laugh with…
a) exaggerated dress, acts, sayings and gestures  b) standing still  c) whispering
**Answer: a) exaggerated dress, acts, sayings and gestures**.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** When you choose a comedy to stage, you note…
a) its subthemes, facts and key phrases  b) only its title  c) nothing
**Answer: a) its subthemes, facts and key phrases**.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Through rehearsals you…
a) assign roles and adjust the staging  b) stop practising  c) change the author
**Answer: a) assign roles and adjust the staging**.
