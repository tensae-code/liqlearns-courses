# Comedy and the Clown — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** What is a clown (payaso)?
**Answer: A comic performer whose exaggerated dress, acts, sayings and gestures make an audience laugh.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What sense does comedy speak to?
**Answer: The sense of humour.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name one thing rehearsals are for.
**Answer: Assigning roles, or adjusting the staging.**
