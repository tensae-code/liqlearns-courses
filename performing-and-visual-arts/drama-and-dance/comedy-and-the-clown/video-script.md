# Video script — Comedy and the Clown

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Big shoes. Bigger gestures. The audience is laughing already.

That is the clown — and comedy is a technique, not an accident.

Choose the comedy. Learn its phrases. Rehearse.

That is **comedy and the clown**.
