# Hands-on — Comedy and the Clown

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Clown minute
Dress one exaggerated thing — a huge bow, enormous shoes — and cross the room in the clown's way: every gesture bigger than life.

## Make me laugh
Play the Grade 3 game: imitate an animal as well as you possibly can, and let your companions guess it through their laughter.
