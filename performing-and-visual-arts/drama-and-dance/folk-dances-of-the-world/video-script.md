# Video script — Folk Dances of the World

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

**Naming the Oriental/Eastern dances.** The Grade 7 guide names Oriental/Eastern dances and where they come from, country by country.

**Techniques, movements, patterns — and the mask.** The dances' different techniques, movements and patterns are explored; a solo Oriental/Eastern dance is demonstrated; an Oriental/Eastern dance mask is made; and the features of the dances are studied, including the costume worn.
