# Hands-on — Folk Dances of the World

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Naming the Oriental/Eastern dances
Do the practice this part names, with real materials and a partner: The Grade 7 guide names Oriental/Eastern dances and where they come from, country by country.

## Techniques, movements, patterns — and the mask
Do the practice this part names, with real materials and a partner: The dances' different techniques, movements and patterns are explored; a solo Oriental/Eastern dance is demonstrated; an Oriental/Eastern dance mask is made; and the features of the dances are studied, including the costume worn.
