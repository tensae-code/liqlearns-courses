# Folk Dances of the World — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Oriental/Eastern dances are named by…
a) their countries of origin  b) their price
**Answer: a) their countries of origin.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A solo dance in this unit is…
a) demonstrated by the learner  b) never performed
**Answer: a) demonstrated by the learner.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Learners make…
a) an Oriental/Eastern dance mask  b) a drum
**Answer: a) an Oriental/Eastern dance mask.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Costume is…
a) part of the features studied  b) ignored
**Answer: a) part of the features studied.**
