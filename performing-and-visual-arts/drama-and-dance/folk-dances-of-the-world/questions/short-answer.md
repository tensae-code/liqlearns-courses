# Folk Dances of the World — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What three things are explored in the dances?
**Answer: Their techniques, movements and patterns.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What two performance-linked things do learners do?
**Answer: Demonstrate a solo dance and make a dance mask.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** What is studied besides movement?
**Answer: The features of the dances, including the costume worn.**
