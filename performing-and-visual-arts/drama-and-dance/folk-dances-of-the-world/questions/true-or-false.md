# Folk Dances of the World — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Each Eastern dance comes from a named place.
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Masks have no place in these dances' study.
**Answer: False — making a dance mask is one of the lessons.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** A solo Eastern dance is demonstrated.
**Answer: True.**
