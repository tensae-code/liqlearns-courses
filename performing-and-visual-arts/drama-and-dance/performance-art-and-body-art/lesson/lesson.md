# Performance Art and Body Art

## Do it first

## Sub lesson 1 — Everyday actions as the starting point of art

The theatre-and-visual course starts art from life itself: **everyday actions are the starting point**. Choose a daily action — **eating, sleeping, dreaming, studying** — and **recreate it to make others reflect**. The action is ordinary; the recreating is the art.

## Sub lesson 2 — Installation, happening and performance

The forms have names: contemporary artists **recreate daily experience through installation, happening and performance**. An installation arranges a space you enter; a happening happens once, live; a performance is the artist's own live doing, presented as the work.
