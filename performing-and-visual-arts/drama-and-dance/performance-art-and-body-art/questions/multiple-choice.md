# Performance Art and Body Art — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The starting point of this art is…
a) everyday actions  b) ancient temples  c) sheet music
**Answer: a) everyday actions**.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Recreating a daily action is meant to make others…
a) reflect  b) sleep  c) buy tickets
**Answer: a) reflect**.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A live, once-only art event is a…
a) happening  b) portrait  c) kiln firing
**Answer: a) happening**.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** An installation is…
a) an arranged space the viewer enters  b) a song verse  c) a clay pot
**Answer: a) an arranged space the viewer enters**.
