# Performance Art and Body Art — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Everyday actions are too ordinary to be art's starting point.
**Answer: False** — the guide starts art exactly there.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Choosing and recreating a daily action can make others reflect.
**Answer: True** — that is the task the guide sets.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Installation, happening and performance are all ways of recreating daily experience.
**Answer: True** — the guide names all three.
