# Video script — Performance Art and Body Art

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Art does not have to be a perfect picture.

Start with your own day. **Eating. Sleeping. Dreaming. Studying.**
Choose one action. **Recreate it** — and make the people watching **reflect**.

That is how today's artists work: not painting exactly, not sculpting realistically, but recreating daily experience — through **installation**, through **happening**, through **performance**.

Your ordinary day is the starting point of art.
