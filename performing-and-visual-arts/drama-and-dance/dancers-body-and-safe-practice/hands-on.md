# Hands-on — The Dancer's Body: Conditioning and Safe Practice

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Water break drill
Dance hard for two minutes, stop, drink water, and say how the body feels — then say why the break mattered.

## Stretch in order
Lead a short stretch before a practice dance: neck, shoulders, arms, back, legs — gently, in order, the way dancers get ready.
