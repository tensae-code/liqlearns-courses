# The Dancer's Body: Conditioning and Safe Practice — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** When you exercise, your body…
a) needs no water  b) uses water up fast — drink extra  c) stores extra water
**Answer: b) uses water up fast — drink extra** — the body uses water up fast, so drink extra.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The dancers in the book drink water…
a) only after the competition  b) while practising, because exercise uses it up  c) never
**Answer: b) while practising, because exercise uses it up** — extra water when exercising.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Stretching is done…
a) after the soreness arrives  b) before dancing, to get the body ready  c) only by judges
**Answer: b) before dancing, to get the body ready** — before dancing.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Stretching helps so that…
a) the music is louder  b) muscles are not so sore  c) the dance is shorter
**Answer: b) muscles are not so sore** — muscles are not so sore.

**5. (Sub lesson 1)** Looking after the body is part of…
a) only sport, never dance  b) dancing itself — the dancer's body is the instrument  c) the audience's job
**Answer: b) dancing itself — the dancer's body is the instrument** — dancers look after the body that dances.
