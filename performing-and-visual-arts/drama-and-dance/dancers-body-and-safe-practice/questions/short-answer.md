# The Dancer's Body: Conditioning and Safe Practice — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why do dancers drink extra water when they exercise?
**Answer: Because the body uses water up fast when it exercises.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** When should dancers stretch?
**Answer: Before dancing.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What does stretching before dancing prevent?
**Answer: It gets the body ready so muscles are not so sore.**
