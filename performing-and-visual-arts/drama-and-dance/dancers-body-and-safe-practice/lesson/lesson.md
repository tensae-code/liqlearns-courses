## Sub lesson 1 — Water for the dancing body

**Dancers look after the body that dances.** Drink **extra water when you exercise**, because the body uses it up fast — in *I Want to Dance!*, the dancers stop for water as they practise (story in summary only).

[Picture — a picture of young dancers drinking water and stretching before they dance]

## Sub lesson 2 — Stretching before dancing

**Stretch before dancing to get the body ready**, so muscles are **not so sore** afterwards.
