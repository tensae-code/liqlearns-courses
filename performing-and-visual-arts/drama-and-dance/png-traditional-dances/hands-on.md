# Hands-on — PNG Traditional Dances

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Planning, practising and performing a traditional dance
Do the practice this part names, with real materials and a partner: Grade 3 plans, practises and performs a Papua New Guinean traditional dance with its instruments, then creates a dance story and judges it.

## Customs and protocols
Do the practice this part names, with real materials and a partner: Grade 4 teaches the dances with their customs and protocols: the steps to teach a traditional dance, performing a short traditional dance, traditional dances in the community and from different parts of PNG, and the customs that go with traditional costumes.

## PNG dancers, and preserving the dances
Do the practice this part names, with real materials and a partner: Grade 5 researches and profiles PNG dancers and choreographers past and present, studies the changes coming over traditional dances and what causes them, and learns ways to preserve PNG traditional dances — their benefits and roles, and creating a PNG traditional dance with its costume.

## Dances of the regions, and when they are danced
Do the practice this part names, with real materials and a partner: Grade 6 names the dances by province, district and ward, and the special characteristics of the Highlands, Papua, Islands and Momase regions; when dances were performed — welcoming, legends, bride price, initiation, burials, pig killing, ceremonial exchange, harvest, trading — and their new occasions; custom deciding who may dance and who may watch; bilas and body painting as part of the dancing; and a case study comparing two PNG traditional dances.
