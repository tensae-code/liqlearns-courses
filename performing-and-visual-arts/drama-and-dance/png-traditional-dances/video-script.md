# Video script — PNG Traditional Dances

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

**Planning, practising and performing a traditional dance.** Grade 3 plans, practises and performs a Papua New Guinean traditional dance with its instruments, then creates a dance story and judges it.

**Customs and protocols.** Grade 4 teaches the dances with their customs and protocols: the steps to teach a traditional dance, performing a short traditional dance, traditional dances in the community and from different parts of PNG, and the customs that go with traditional costumes.

**PNG dancers, and preserving the dances.** Grade 5 researches and profiles PNG dancers and choreographers past and present, studies the changes coming over traditional dances and what causes them, and learns ways to preserve PNG traditional dances — their benefits and roles, and creating a PNG traditional dance with its costume.

**Dances of the regions, and when they are danced.** Grade 6 names the dances by province, district and ward, and the special characteristics of the Highlands, Papua, Islands and Momase regions; when dances were performed — welcoming, legends, bride price, initiation, burials, pig killing, ceremonial exchange, harvest, trading — and their new occasions; custom deciding who may dance and who may watch; bilas and body painting as part of the dancing; and a case study comparing two PNG traditional dances.
