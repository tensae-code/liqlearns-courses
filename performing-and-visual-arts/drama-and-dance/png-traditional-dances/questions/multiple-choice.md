# PNG Traditional Dances — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Bilas and body painting are…
a) part of the dancing itself  b) forbidden in dance
**Answer: a) part of the dancing itself.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Custom decides…
a) who may dance, at what age, and who may watch  b) nothing
**Answer: a) who may dance, at what age, and who may watch.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The four regions named are…
a) Highlands, Papua, Islands and Momase  b) four foreign countries
**Answer: a) Highlands, Papua, Islands and Momase.**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Grade 5 studies…
a) ways to preserve PNG traditional dances  b) ways to forget them
**Answer: a) ways to preserve PNG traditional dances.**
