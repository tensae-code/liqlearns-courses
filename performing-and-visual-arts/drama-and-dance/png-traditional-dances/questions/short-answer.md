# PNG Traditional Dances — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name three traditional occasions for the dances.
**Answer: Welcoming, demonstrating legends, bride price, initiation, burials, pig killing, ceremonial exchange, harvest, trading (any three).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What two things does the case study compare?
**Answer: The history and origins of two PNG traditional dances.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Who does Grade 5 research and profile?
**Answer: PNG dancers and choreographers, past and present.**
