# PNG Traditional Dances — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Traditional dances have customs and protocols that must be followed.
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Anyone may watch any dance at any age, custom says.
**Answer: False — custom decides who may dance and who may watch.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** New occasions for traditional dances include church openings and national celebrations.
**Answer: True.**
