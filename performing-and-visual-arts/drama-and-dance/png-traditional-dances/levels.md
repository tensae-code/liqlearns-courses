# PNG Traditional Dances — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — Planning, practising and performing a traditional dance
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Grade 3 plans, practises and performs a Papua New Guinean traditional dance with its instruments, then creates a dance story and judges it.

## Sub lesson 2 — Customs and protocols
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Grade 4 teaches the dances with their customs and protocols: the steps to teach a traditional dance, performing a short traditional dance, traditional dances in the community and from different parts of PNG, and the customs that go with traditional costumes.

## Sub lesson 3 — PNG dancers, and preserving the dances
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Grade 5 researches and profiles PNG dancers and choreographers past and present, studies the changes coming over traditional dances and what causes them, and learns ways to preserve PNG traditional dances — their benefits and roles, and creating a PNG traditional dance with its costume.

## Sub lesson 4 — Dances of the regions, and when they are danced
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Grade 6 names the dances by province, district and ward, and the special characteristics of the Highlands, Papua, Islands and Momase regions; when dances were performed — welcoming, legends, bride price, initiation, burials, pig killing, ceremonial exchange, harvest, trading — and their new occasions; custom deciding who may dance and who may watch; bilas and body painting as part of the dancing; and a case study comparing two PNG traditional dances.
