# PNG Traditional Dances

## Do it first

Look at the sub lesson list. Pick the part you already know something about, and say what you know. Then start at Sub lesson 1 — every part builds on the one before it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Planning, practising and performing a traditional dance

Grade 3 plans, practises and performs a Papua New Guinean traditional dance with its instruments, then creates a dance story and judges it.

## Sub lesson 2 — Customs and protocols

Grade 4 teaches the dances with their customs and protocols: the steps to teach a traditional dance, performing a short traditional dance, traditional dances in the community and from different parts of PNG, and the customs that go with traditional costumes.

## Sub lesson 3 — PNG dancers, and preserving the dances

Grade 5 researches and profiles PNG dancers and choreographers past and present, studies the changes coming over traditional dances and what causes them, and learns ways to preserve PNG traditional dances — their benefits and roles, and creating a PNG traditional dance with its costume.

## Sub lesson 4 — Dances of the regions, and when they are danced

Grade 6 names the dances by province, district and ward, and the special characteristics of the Highlands, Papua, Islands and Momase regions; when dances were performed — welcoming, legends, bride price, initiation, burials, pig killing, ceremonial exchange, harvest, trading — and their new occasions; custom deciding who may dance and who may watch; bilas and body painting as part of the dancing; and a case study comparing two PNG traditional dances.

## Say it to your companion
