# Sila Rainbow Dance Competition — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Sila Rainbow Dance Competition is held at…
a) the school gym only  b) the community hall  c) the airport
**Answer: b) the community hall** — the community hall.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** At the competition, who performs?
a) one solo dancer  b) different teams perform dances  c) only the judges
**Answer: b) different teams perform dances** — different teams perform their dances.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Who decides which dance is best?
a) the loudest singer  b) the judges  c) the youngest child
**Answer: b) the judges** — the judges decide.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** The competition is in…
a) Nunavut, Canada  b) Andorra  c) Jamaica
**Answer: a) Nunavut, Canada** — Nunavut, Canada.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** A team prepares its competition dance by…
a) never practising  b) practising until it holds together, then performing it  c) copying the judges
**Answer: b) practising until it holds together, then performing it** — practising, then performing before the community and judges.
