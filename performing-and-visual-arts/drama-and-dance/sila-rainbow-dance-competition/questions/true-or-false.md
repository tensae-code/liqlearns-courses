# Sila Rainbow Dance Competition — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Teams perform dances at the Sila Rainbow Dance Competition.
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The competition has no judges — the audience shouts the winner.
**Answer: False — judges decide which dance is best.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A competition dance is performed before the whole community.
**Answer: True.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In the story, nobody prepares for the competition.
**Answer: False — the brother and sister build and practise their dance for it.**
