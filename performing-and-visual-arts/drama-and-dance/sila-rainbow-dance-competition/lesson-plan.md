# Lesson plan — Sila Rainbow Dance Competition

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Drama and Dance · **Lesson:** Sila Rainbow Dance Competition
**Place in the curriculum:** Drama and Dance (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Nunavut's Sila Rainbow Dance Competition at the community hall — teams perform their dances, and judges decide which dance is best.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this course in the same title.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio.
