# Hands-on — From Script to Stage

Real things to do with what this lesson teaches — at school or at home, with people around you.

## Production strip
Groups arrange the journey's stages on a paper strip in order, then defend their order: why must casting come before blocking? Why is the dress rehearsal last?

## Rough budget
For a real class play, list the budget honestly: costumes, props, make-up, publicity — and what can be borrowed or made instead of bought.
