# Lesson plan — Playing Family Roles

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Drama and Dance · **Lesson:** Playing Family Roles
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 4 — Drama and Dance (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** you can step into a family member's place — mother, father, sister, brother — do their work the way they do it, and understand your family from inside their day. This is where creativity opens: bringing out what is inside you through acting.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the child's own home — every learner already knows their family's work by watching it every day. The creativity opener folded into this lesson comes from the books' own chapter doors (Developing Creativity, Grade 1; Expressing Creativity, Grade 2).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees
from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before
it is proved:

1. **Bringing out what is inside you** — the folded chapter openers (Developing Creativity, Grade 1; Expressing Creativity, Grade 2): creativity is bringing out what is inside you.
2. **The family and their work** — the family members and each one's responsibilities — mother cooking and serving, father farming, children helping.
3. **Acting the family** — Exercise 4's work: take a family member's role and act their work so watchers can name it.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: How Animals and People Move — acting grows out of copying movement. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say what creativity is, in your own words.
2. Name the family members and each one's responsibilities.
3. Act a family member's work clearly enough for watchers to name it.
4. Play father and mother with the right clothes and things for each role.
5. Say one thing you understood about a family member by acting their day.

## What you need
Home clothes and simple things for the roles — a wrap, a hat, a stick, a cooking spoon. Nothing bought.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — the learner steps into the cook's place for one minute before any explaining (lesson/lesson.md).
2. **The explanation** — creativity first (the folded openers), then the family's responsibilities, then acting them — Exercise 4's own order (lesson/lesson.md).
   - ADD IMAGE [Picture 9702]: a mother cooking and serving, a father farming, children helping at home — the student names each person and their responsibility.
   - ADD AUDIO: a voice reading a family's morning — who wakes first, who cooks, who farms, who helps — the student says each responsibility as it is named.
3. **Say it to your companion** — act a family member silently for your companion; they name the person and the responsibility, then you swap.
4. **Questions** — from questions/, served at the learner's level (levels.md).
5. **Hands-on** — hands-on.md.
6. **Do It Yourself** — at home, help with one real family responsibility today, the way the person who owns it does it.

## If the learner is stuck
- Acts themselves, not the family member → send them back to watching: one real detail (how mother holds the pot) carries the whole role.
- Cannot list responsibilities → walk through one day at home hour by hour together; the jobs appear by themselves.
- The father/mother roles of this lesson grow, in the next lessons, into playing any person at all.
