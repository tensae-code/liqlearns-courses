# Video script — Playing Family Roles

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Inside you — an idea. A feeling.
Bring it OUT. That is creativity!
Sing it. Draw it. Dance it. ACT it.

Mother cooks. Serve, serve — like her.
Father farms. Dig — like him.
Children help. Carry, clean, care.

Now — YOU be mother. YOU be father.
Show the work. We will guess!
When you stand in their place… you understand their day.
