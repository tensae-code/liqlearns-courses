# Playing Family Roles — writing

Sub-lesson markers: every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to.

Write the lines asked for.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Write your family's list: each member's name and their responsibilities beside it.
**Answer:** a true list from the learner's own home — member, then their jobs.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Write two lines: who you acted, and one thing you understood about their day by acting it.
**Answer:** the learner's own role and their own understanding, in their own words.
