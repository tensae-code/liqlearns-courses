# Playing Family Roles — short answer

Sub-lesson markers: every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to.

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Name three family members and one responsibility of each.
**Answer:** e.g. mother — cooking and serving; father — farming; children — helping at home.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Name three ways of bringing out what is inside you.
**Answer:** any three of: singing, drawing, dancing, acting (the books' own list).

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What is the first step before acting a person?
**Answer:** watch them well — how they move, hold things and work.
