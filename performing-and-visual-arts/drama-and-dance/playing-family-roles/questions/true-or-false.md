# Playing Family Roles — true or false

Sub-lesson markers: every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to.

Say if it is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Acting is one way of bringing out what is inside you.
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Children have no responsibilities in a family.
**Answer: False** — children help at home: carrying, cleaning, caring for little ones.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Acting a family member's day can help you understand that person.
**Answer: True.**
