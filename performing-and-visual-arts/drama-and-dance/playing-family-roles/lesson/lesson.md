# Playing Family Roles

## Do it first

Pretend, right now: you are the person in your home who cooks. Stand where they stand. Hold the spoon the way they hold it. Serve the food the way they serve it. For one minute, BE them. That stepping-into-another-person is what this whole lesson is about.

[Picture 9702 — a mother cooking and serving, a father farming, children helping at home]

## Sub lesson 1 — Bringing out what is inside you

Every child carries something inside — ideas, feelings, talents. **Creativity** is bringing that inside out, so others can see it: in a song, in a drawing, in a dance — and in acting.

In acting, you bring it out by becoming another person for a little while. You already do this in play. This lesson makes it careful: watch a person well, then show what you watched.

Your first stage is your own family — the people you watch most closely of all.

## Sub lesson 2 — The family and their work

A family works together, and each member has their **responsibilities** — the jobs that are theirs.

Mother cooks and serves the food. Father farms the land. The children help at home — carrying, cleaning, caring for the little ones. Sister and brother each have their parts too.

[Picture 9702 — a mother cooking and serving, a father farming, children helping at home]

Before you can act a person, you must be able to SAY their work. List your own family's members, and beside each one, their responsibilities. Watch them today with new eyes: how does mother hold the pot? How does father swing the tool? How do the children carry and help?

## Sub lesson 3 — Acting the family

Now act it. Take the role of one family member and do their work the way they do it — the same movements, the same care, the same words they say while they work.

Act the mother serving. Act the father farming. Act the child helping. Your class watches: can they name who you are, and what your responsibility is, without you telling them? Then that is good acting — the role was clear.

And here is the deeper gift: when you have stood in your mother's or father's place, even in play, you understand their day. Acting teaches the heart as well as the body.

## Say it to your companion

Act a family member silently for your companion; they name the person and the responsibility, then you swap..

## Do It Yourself

1. At home, help with one real family responsibility today, the way the person who owns it does it..
