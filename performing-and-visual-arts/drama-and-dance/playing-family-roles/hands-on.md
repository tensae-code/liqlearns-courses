# Hands-on — Playing Family Roles

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## One real job, done their way
Choose one responsibility in your home — fetching water, sweeping, serving coffee, watching the little ones. Today, do it for real, exactly the way the person who usually does it does it. Afterwards, tell your family what was harder or easier than you thought.

## Family theatre at home
With brothers, sisters or friends, act out one small home scene: cooking and serving a meal. Each player takes one family role. No special clothes are needed at home — the movements and the care carry the role.
