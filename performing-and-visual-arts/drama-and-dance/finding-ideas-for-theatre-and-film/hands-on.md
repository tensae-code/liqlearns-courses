# Hands-on — Finding Ideas for Theatre and Film

Real things to do with what this lesson teaches — at school or at home, with people around you.

## Idea-source hunt
From the book's source list, each group picks one source — a current issue, a custom, a historical moment — and pitches a one-minute play idea from it.

## Worked-example reading
Read the model play's summary together. Then answer: what is Alemitu's idea-advice to her friends, and how does the play show it instead of just saying it?
