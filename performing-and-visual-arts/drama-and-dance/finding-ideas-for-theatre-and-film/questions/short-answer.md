# Finding Ideas for Theatre and Film — short answer

Answer in your own words, in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How many plays does the book credit to Euripides?
**Answer: Up to 25 plays in his lifetime.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name three sources a playwright can draw ideas from.
**Answer: Any three from the book's list — current issues, social life, culture/custom/values, political and economic issues, belief and civilisation, history, weakening shared values, outside influences, nature and human-made things.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What is the model play's one clear idea?
**Answer: That having a goal makes study matter.**
