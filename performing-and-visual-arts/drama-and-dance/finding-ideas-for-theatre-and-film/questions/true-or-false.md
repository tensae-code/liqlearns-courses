# Finding Ideas for Theatre and Film — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Comedy and tragedy both begin among the Greek writers. True or false?
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** History — past and present — is a source of theatre ideas. True or false?
**Answer: True.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** This lesson copies the model play's dialogue. True or false?
**Answer: False — the play is taught in summary only; its dialogue is never transcribed.**
