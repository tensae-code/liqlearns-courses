# Finding Ideas for Theatre and Film — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The first theatre scripts were written —
a) in ancient Greece, around the 5th century BC  b) in Rome, last century  c) in Ethiopia, in 1900
**Answer: a) in ancient Greece, around the 5th century BC.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The father of tragedy writing is —
a) Aristophanes  b) Aeschylus  c) Euripides
**Answer: b) Aeschylus.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Aristophanes wrote —
a) tragedy  b) comedy — up to 40 works, 11 complete  c) only one play
**Answer: b) comedy — up to 40 works, 11 complete.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which is a source of play ideas in the book's list?
a) society's current issues  b) nothing real  c) only dreams
**Answer: a) society's current issues.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** A script can be enriched with —
a) belief, hope, truth, betrayal, love, heroism  b) nothing  c) only jokes
**Answer: a) belief, hope, truth, betrayal, love, heroism.**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** The writer is the main stakeholder, but must work with —
a) nobody  b) audience, actor, director and helpers  c) only printers
**Answer: b) audience, actor, director and helpers.**

**7. (Sub lesson 3)** The model play's student is —
a) Alemitu of Mersa town  b) a teacher in Addis Ababa  c) an unnamed girl
**Answer: a) Alemitu of Mersa town.**

**8. (Sub lesson 3)** Alemitu advises Hana and Ahmed to —
a) count class levels only  b) have a goal and study  c) leave school
**Answer: b) have a goal and study.**

**9. (Sub lesson 3)** Alemitu's own vision is to become —
a) a merchant  b) a medical doctor  c) a singer
**Answer: b) a medical doctor.**
