# Video script — Finding Ideas for Theatre and Film

A short read-aloud script in a super-simple voice, made later as the lesson's video. It follows the lesson's own order and names only what the lesson teaches.

---

The first theatre scripts were written in ancient Greece.
Aeschylus, Sophocles, Euripides wrote tragedies; Aristophanes wrote comedy.
Ideas still come from all around us: our issues, our history, our values, our daily life.
And a model play shows an idea at work — a hopeful student, advising her friends.
