The model play 'ተስፈኛዋ ተማሪ' (The Hopeful Student, printed pp. 70–72) is taught in SUMMARY ONLY — its dialogue is never transcribed, per the course rule. A note on naming: the fix brief called the G7 model play ሰላም ለምድራችን ('Peace for Our Earth'); the Grade 7 placements' model play at §2.4 is 'The Hopeful Student', and the 'ሰላም' (Peace) model play is the Grade 8 book's (printed pp. 61–63), stocked in Writing and Putting on a Play. The placements govern, so this lesson teaches the Grade 7 book's own model play.
