# Finding Ideas for Theatre and Film — sub lessons and depth

This is the depth record for the lesson, re-expressed as sub lessons: each sub lesson is one step up in depth, proved before the next opens.

## Sub lesson 1 — The first playwrights — the Greek writers
Pass mark: the learner can name the four Greek writers and what each is known for. The gate draws from this sub lesson's whole question set.

## Sub lesson 2 — Where ideas come from
Pass mark: the learner can list at least four sources of ideas and say who collaborates with the writer. The gate draws from this sub lesson's whole question set.

## Sub lesson 3 — Worked example — 'The Hopeful Student' (summary only)
Pass mark: the learner can tell the model play's story in summary and name its idea. The gate draws from this sub lesson's whole question set.
