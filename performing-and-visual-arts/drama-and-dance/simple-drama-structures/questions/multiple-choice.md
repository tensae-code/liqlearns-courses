# Simple Drama Structures — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The guide's §1.3 is about —
a) simple drama structures — the shapes a play takes  b) costumes  c) song words
**Answer: a) simple drama structures — the shapes a play takes.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Learning these structures begins with —
a) memorising lines  b) recognising the shapes  c) buying a stage
**Answer: b) recognising the shapes.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** A play's shape is like —
a) the plan of a house — you can see how it is put together  b) a secret  c) a song's tune only
**Answer: a) the plan of a house — you can see how it is put together.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The simplest play shape has —
a) one beat  b) three beats — beginning, middle, end  c) ten beats
**Answer: b) three beats — beginning, middle, end.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** In the middle beat —
a) we meet the people  b) something happens to the people  c) the audience leaves
**Answer: b) something happens to the people.**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** The end beat —
a) finishes the story  b) starts a new story  c) is never needed
**Answer: a) finishes the story.**
