# Simple Drama Structures — short answer

Answer in your own words, in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What are 'simple drama structures'?
**Answer: The simple shapes a play takes — the ways a little play is put together.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Tell a three-beat story in three short sentences.
**Answer: Any tiny story with a clear beginning, middle and end.**
