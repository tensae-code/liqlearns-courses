# Hands-on — Simple Drama Structures

Real things to do with what this lesson teaches — at school or at home, with people around you.

## Three-beat stories
In pairs, each learner tells a tiny story in exactly three beats. The partner names the beginning, the middle and the end.

## Shape spotting
Watch or recall a short class play. Together, find where its beginning ends and its middle begins, and how its end closes the story.
