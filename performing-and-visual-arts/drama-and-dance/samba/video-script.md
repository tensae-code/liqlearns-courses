This lesson is Samba and Brazilian Rhythms.

Samba and the Brazilian rhythms, their steps and carnival energy. — as Greece's school books teach it: "let's play samba!" — each player takes one repeating rhythmic pattern of the bateria; Jobim's "The Girl from Ipanema" (1962, words by Vinícius de Moraes).

Watch, listen — then say it back in your own words.
