# Video script — Acting Our History

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Grandmother tells it. Father tells it.
Now — YOU tell it.

**1888.** The enemy came.
Emperor **Menelik** led. Empress **Taytu** stood with the country.
The patriot fathers fought — and WON.
**Adwa!** Every year — **Yekatit 23** — we celebrate!

We sing **fukera**! We chant **shilela**!
And we ACT the story — so it never fades.
Tell it. Act it. Pass it on.
