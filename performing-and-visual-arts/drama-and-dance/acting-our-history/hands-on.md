# Hands-on — Acting Our History

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Ask, tell, act
At home, ask an elder: 'What do you know about Adwa?' Listen without interrupting. At your next gathering, tell the class the one detail your family added. Then, in your group, choose that detail and build your acting around it — the class play becomes a patchwork of real family tellings.

## The standing-firm line
The patriots' most actable moment needs no battle: a line of people standing firm, shoulder to shoulder, refusing to move. Practise it with your group — feet planted, chins up, fukera rhythm in your chests. Let one actor, as the messenger, bring news to the line. Feel how standing together IS the story.
