# Acting Our History — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers: every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to.

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Known histories travel —
a) only in printed books  b) from generation to generation  c) only on television
**Answer: b)** — grandparents to parents to children.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The Victory of Adwa was in —
a) 1888 E.C.  b) 1988 E.C.  c) 1788 E.C.
**Answer: a) 1888 E.C.** — Ethiopia against Italy.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Ethiopia at Adwa was led by —
a) Emperor Menelik and Empress Taytu  b) two generals only  c) foreign advisors
**Answer: a).**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Adwa Victory is celebrated every year on —
a) Meskerem 1  b) Yekatit 23  c) Tahsas 29
**Answer: b) Yekatit 23.**
