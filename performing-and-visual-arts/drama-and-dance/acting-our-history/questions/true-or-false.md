# Acting Our History — true or false

Sub-lesson markers: every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to.

Say if it is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Fukera, shilela and qererito are performed at the Adwa Victory festival to raise morale and encourage heroes.
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Acting a history makes it easier to forget.
**Answer: False** — acting fixes the telling in the body and the memory.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The book tells learners to ask their families about Adwa and tell the class.
**Answer: True.**
