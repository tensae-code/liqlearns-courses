# Acting Our History — short answer

Sub-lesson markers: every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to.

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What are known histories?
**Answer:** events, famous people who made history, and the history of country and place, passed from generation to generation.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name two things learners do with the Adwa festival arts.
**Answer:** collect the melodies and fukera sung at Adwa time and perform them in song and dance; act the history of the patriot fathers and mothers in drama.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the hero song call out?
**Answer:** ዘራፍ! (Zeraf!) — for the Ethiopian hero who is never broken (named by the placement; verses not transcribed).
