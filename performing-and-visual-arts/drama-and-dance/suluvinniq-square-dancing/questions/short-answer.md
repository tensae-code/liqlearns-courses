# Suluvinniq: Square Dancing — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is square dancing called in Inuktitut?
**Answer: Suluvinniq (ᓱᓗᕕᖕᓂᖅ).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Who brought square dancing to Nunavut?
**Answer: The whalers.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** How do communities keep square dancing lively today?
**Answer: They hold dance competitions, and young people keep the dancing going.**
