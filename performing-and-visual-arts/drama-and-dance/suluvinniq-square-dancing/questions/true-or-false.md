# Suluvinniq: Square Dancing — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Square dancing is an old Inuit dance from before the whalers.
**Answer: False — it came with the whalers.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Dancing sometimes went on all night.
**Answer: True.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Young people have no part in square dancing today.
**Answer: False — young people keep the dancing going.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Communities hold square-dancing competitions.
**Answer: True.**
