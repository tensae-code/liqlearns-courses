# Suluvinniq: Square Dancing — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Square dancing came to Nunavut with…
a) the teachers  b) the whalers  c) the radio
**Answer: b) the whalers** — the whalers brought it.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The whalers' dance tunes were called…
a) katajjaq and pisiit  b) jig and reel  c) waltz and polka
**Answer: b) jig and reel** — "jig" and "reel".

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** People danced to the tunes for…
a) ten minutes  b) 10 to 20 hours, sometimes all night  c) one song only
**Answer: b) 10 to 20 hours, sometimes all night** — 10 to 20 hours, sometimes all night.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** An instrument Inuit learned for the dancing was…
a) the accordion  b) the piano  c) the drum only
**Answer: a) the accordion** — the accordion.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Today, square dancing in Nunavut…
a) has ended  b) is still danced, with community competitions, kept going by young people  c) is danced only by elders
**Answer: b) is still danced, with community competitions, kept going by young people** — still danced today; competitions are held and young people keep it going.
