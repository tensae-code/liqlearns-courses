# Hands-on — Suluvinniq: Square Dancing

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Jig and reel steps
To a jig or reel rhythm (clapped if no recording), practise a simple square-dance figure in fours — step, turn, change partners — keeping to the tune.

## Dance long, dance together
Keep a simple figure going as a group for a full tune without stopping — a small taste of dancing for hours.
