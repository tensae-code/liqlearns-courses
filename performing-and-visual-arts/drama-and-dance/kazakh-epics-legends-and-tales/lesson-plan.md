**Place in the curriculum:** Drama and Dance (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** kazakh Epics Legends and Tales — its forms, history and cultural meaning, taught as one topic lesson..
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this course in the same title.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. ## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Kazakhstan Grade 4 Labour Training (Ormanbekova)** — Aldarkose, hero of Kazakh fairy tales, who teaches kindness, honesty and helping the weak; making a sliding puzzle game from his picture in a frame decorated with Kazakh ornament; the legend in brief: after the Flood the released birds vanished, but the swallow returned with a green twig, and Kazakhs honour it still
2. **Kazakhstan Grade 4 Music (Kulmanova)** — what a value (құндылық) is — homeland, family, mother tongue, state symbols — through A. Seitimbetov's tale «Әйел-Ана» (Mother Woman), told in summary: a mother's counsel saves her people; болашақта» — one heritage in three arts: the «Қыз Жібек» zhyr heard as zhyr, as feature film and as Brusilovsky's opera (whose Jibek is unveiled to Ukili Ybyrai's song «Гәкку»); vocal rock against instrumental rock
