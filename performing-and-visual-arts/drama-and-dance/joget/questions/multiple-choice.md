# Joget — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

a) another country's arts only  b) Joget, Malaysia's lively Malay social folk dance for couples and groups  c) nothing — it is just names
**Answer: b)** — that is the lesson's one thing.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The first sub lesson is...
a) Joget, Malaysia's lively couple social dance  b) an examination  c) a song from another continent
**Answer: a) Joget, Malaysia's lively couple social dance.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Malaysia's own forms in this lesson are...
a) dropped and replaced  b) kept as named parts  c) hidden
**Answer: b) kept as named parts.**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** The last sub lesson is...
a) Joget, Malaysia's lively couple social dance  b) the bibliography  c) a review box
**Answer: a) Joget, Malaysia's lively couple social dance.**

**5. (Sub lesson 1)** Songs in this course are placed...
