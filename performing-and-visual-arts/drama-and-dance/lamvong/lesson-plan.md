# Lesson plan — Lamvong: Lao Circle Dance

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Drama and Dance · **Lesson:** Lamvong: Lao Circle Dance
**Place in the curriculum:** Drama and Dance (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Lamvong, Laos's circle dance, danced to rhythms such as Nok Aen Choet and Soen Suan.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this course in the same title.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Music G9 teacher guide — lamvong lao circle dance** — the national circle dance step by step from photographs — women's steps 1–3 and men's steps 1–3 (hand positions, the 8-count foot pattern, turning as a pair), then dancing as pairs in a circle
2. **Music G6 teacher guide — lamvong lao circle dance** — the circle dance's eight-step pattern, women's and men's parts from photographs, then dancing in pairs
3. **Music G7 teacher guide — lamvong lao circle dance** — the circle dance in this named rhythm taught from photographs — women's poses 1–3 and men's poses 1–3 (starting pose, stepping out, turning), then dancing as pairs

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
