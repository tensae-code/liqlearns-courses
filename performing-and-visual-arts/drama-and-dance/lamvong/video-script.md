This lesson is Lamvong: Lao Circle Dance.

Lamvong, Laos's circle dance, danced to rhythms such as Nok Aen Choet and Soen Suan. — as Laos's school books teach it: the national circle dance step by step from photographs — women's steps 1–3 and men's steps 1–3 (hand positions, the 8-count foot pattern, turning as a pair), then dancing as pairs in a circle

Watch, listen — then say it back in your own words.
