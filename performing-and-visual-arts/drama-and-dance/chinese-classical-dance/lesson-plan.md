# Lesson plan — Chinese Classical Dance

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Drama and Dance · **Lesson:** Chinese Classical Dance
**Place in the curriculum:** Drama and Dance (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** China's classical dance — its trained body language, sleeves, turns and expressive poses.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this course in the same title.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **China HS Dance Performance** — Chinese classical dance — formed in the 1950s from opera dance, ballet training and martial arts, in three schools (body-rhyme, Han-Tang, Dunhuang); the orchid hand and its positions (mountain-arm, pressed palm, held palm); and the core law — the circle: flat circles, vertical circles, figure-of-eight circles, driven by the eight body-rhyme elements (lift, sink, thrust, lean, contract, jut, shift, side-lift); Fan Painting danced with a silk fan as the appreciation jewel, Song of Treading as the rehearsal
2. **China Senior Music 5 Music & Dance** — Unit 4 中国古典舞： Chinese classical dance — its history and the basic abilities a dancer needs; boys' and girls' classical combinations practised; 扇舞丹青 and scenes from 《丝路花雨》 and 《铜雀伎》 appreciated

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
