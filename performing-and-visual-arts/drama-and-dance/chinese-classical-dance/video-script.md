This lesson is Chinese Classical Dance.

China's classical dance — its trained body language, sleeves, turns and expressive poses. — as China's school books teach it: Chinese classical dance — formed in the 1950s from opera dance, ballet training and martial arts, in three schools (body-rhyme, Han-Tang, Dunhuang); the orchid hand and its positions (mountain-arm, pressed palm, held palm); and the core law — the circle: flat circles, vertical circles, figure-of-eight circles, driven by the eight body-rhyme elements (lift, sink, thrust, lean, contract, jut, shift, side-lift); Fan Painting danced with a silk fan as the appreciation jewel, Song of Treading as the rehearsal.

Watch, listen — then say it back in your own words.
