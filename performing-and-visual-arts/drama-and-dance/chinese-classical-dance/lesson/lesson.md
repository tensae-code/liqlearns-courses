## Sub lesson 1 — China HS Dance Performance

Chinese classical dance — formed in the 1950s from opera dance, ballet training and martial arts, in three schools (body-rhyme, Han-Tang, Dunhuang); the orchid hand and its positions (mountain-arm, pressed palm, held palm); and the core law — the circle: flat circles, vertical circles, figure-of-eight circles, driven by the eight body-rhyme elements (lift, sink, thrust, lean, contract, jut, shift, side-lift); Fan Painting danced with a silk fan as the appreciation jewel, Song of Treading as the rehearsal

## Sub lesson 2 — China Senior Music 5 Music & Dance

Unit 4 中国古典舞： Chinese classical dance — its history and the basic abilities a dancer needs; boys' and girls' classical combinations practised; 扇舞丹青 and scenes from 《丝路花雨》 and 《铜雀伎》 appreciated
