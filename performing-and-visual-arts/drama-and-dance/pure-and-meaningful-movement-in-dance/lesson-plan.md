# Lesson plan — Pure and Meaningful Movement in Dance

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Drama and Dance · **Lesson:** Pure and Meaningful Movement in Dance
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 4 — Drama and Dance (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Dance movement of two kinds — pure movement done for its own beauty and meaningful movement that carries a message, with facial expression in dance.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the course's earlier lessons in its title; recommended first, never forced.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of ..." in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Bab II Gerak Tari, Pokok Materi** — gerak murni (pure movement, made for beauty) versus gerak maknawi (meaningful gesture), and how everyday movement becomes dance movement through stilasi (stylising) and distorsi (reshaping)
2. **Unit Pembelajaran 1 Gerak Dan Ekspresi** — the five basic emotions (happy, sad, afraid, angry, surprised, from Eckman's six) read and shown through the lines of eyebrows, eyes and mouth, then through face and body expression drawn from daily activities
3. **Unit 1** — the kinds of gesture — social, functional, ritual and emotional gestures — as meaningful movement

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
