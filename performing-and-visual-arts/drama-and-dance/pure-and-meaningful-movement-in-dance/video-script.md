# Video script — Pure and Meaningful Movement in Dance

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This is Indonesia's own lesson.

**Bab II Gerak Tari, Pokok Materi.** gerak murni (pure movement, made for beauty) versus gerak maknawi (meaningful gesture), and how everyday movement becomes dance movement through stilasi (stylising) and distorsi (reshaping).

**Unit Pembelajaran 1 Gerak Dan Ekspresi.** the five basic emotions (happy, sad, afraid, angry, surprised, from Eckman's six) read and shown through the lines of eyebrows, eyes and mouth, then through face and body expression drawn from daily activities.

**Unit 1.** the kinds of gesture — social, functional, ritual and emotional gestures — as meaningful movement.

That is Pure and Meaningful Movement in Dance — Indonesia's way, kept by name.
