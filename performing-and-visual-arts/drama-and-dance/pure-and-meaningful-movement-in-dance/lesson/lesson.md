# Pure and Meaningful Movement in Dance

## Do it first

Name one thing that belongs in this lesson — Dance movement of two kinds — pure movement done for its own beauty and meaningful movement that carries a message, with facial expression in dance. Say what it is in your own words. If you can name one, you are already inside the lesson.

## Sub lesson 1 — Bab II Gerak Tari, Pokok Materi

gerak murni (pure movement, made for beauty) versus gerak maknawi (meaningful gesture), and how everyday movement becomes dance movement through stilasi (stylising) and distorsi (reshaping).

## Sub lesson 2 — Unit Pembelajaran 1 Gerak Dan Ekspresi

the five basic emotions (happy, sad, afraid, angry, surprised, from Eckman's six) read and shown through the lines of eyebrows, eyes and mouth, then through face and body expression drawn from daily activities.

## Sub lesson 3 — Unit 1

the kinds of gesture — social, functional, ritual and emotional gestures — as meaningful movement.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson holds, part by part, in your own words. Companion checks: did every part get named, with its Indonesian name kept where the book gives one?

## Do It Yourself

1. Choose one named part of this lesson and find out one more true thing about it — from a person who knows it, or from the book your part came from.
2. Make or perform a small piece in the way of that part: a rhythm, a step, a drawing, a shaped piece of clay.
3. Write one sentence: what does this part show about Indonesia's own way of teaching its arts?
