# Video script — Tamil Drama of Sri Lanka

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is Tamil Drama of Sri Lanka.

A complete modern Sri Lankan Tamil stage play studied whole — full script with stage directions, its characters (the villager Kanthappu, Manoharan, the Doctor) and its two griefs carried in dialogue, so A/L pupils meet Tamil theatre as literature that is meant to be acted

Watch, listen — then say it back in your own words.
