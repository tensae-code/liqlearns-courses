# Video script — Awasa Dance

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Awasa — the dance of the Marrons of the interior.

The women depict their beauty. The men show strength and skill.

Bounce — four times eight counts. Step and tap, wrists turning.

Comb the hair. Cook. Bathe in the river. Gather.

Apinti drums at a hundred beats a minute. The kawai rattles the rhythm.

Travel — by land, by river, by air — in awasa steps.
