# Awasa Dance — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Awasa dancers are often true acrobats.
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The warm-up includes circling with everyday hand gestures such as combing hair and cooking.
**Answer: True.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The kawai is worn on the head.
**Answer: False — it is a foot rattle.**
