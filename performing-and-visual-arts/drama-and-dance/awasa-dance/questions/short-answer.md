# Awasa Dance — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Awasa belongs to which people and tradition?
**Answer: The Afro-Surinamese Marrons of the interior.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** At what tempo is the warm-up percussion, and on what drums?
**Answer: 100 BPM, with apinti drums.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** How long is the solo dance journey, and how must it travel?
**Answer: 30 seconds to a minute, travelling mostly in awasa steps by land, river or air, made clear in the dance.**
