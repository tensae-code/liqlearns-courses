# Awasa Dance — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Awasa is the dance of…
a) the Javanese of the coast  b) the Marrons of the interior  c) a European court
**Answer: b) the Marrons of the interior**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In awasa, the women depict… and the men show…
a) sorrow; sleep  b) beauty; strength and skill  c) anger; fear
**Answer: b) beauty; strength and skill**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The kawai is…
a) a drum  b) a foot rattle of Amazon-plant seeds  c) a headdress
**Answer: b) a foot rattle of Amazon-plant seeds**
