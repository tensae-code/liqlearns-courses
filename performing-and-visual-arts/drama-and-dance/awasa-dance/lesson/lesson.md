## Sub lesson 1 — What awasa is

**Awasa** is the **Afro-Surinamese dance of the Marrons of the interior** — a **beauty dance** in which the **women depict their beauty** and the **men show strength and skill**; its dancers are **often true acrobats**.

## Sub lesson 2 — Dancing awasa — the warm-up and the journey

The warm-up works in **counted awasa steps**: **four times eight counts bouncing**; **step and tap side to side with rotating wrists**; **circling with everyday hand gestures — combing hair, cooking, bathing in the river, gathering things** — to **energetic percussion at 100 BPM with apinti drums**. The Saramaccaner dancers' **kawai**, a **foot rattle of Amazon-plant seeds**, keeps the drums' rhythm. The **solo dance journey of 30 seconds to a minute** travels mostly in awasa steps — **by land, river or air**, made clear in the dance.
