| Suriname — Dance Les 1 "De ruimte — Ik ga dansend op reis" (Space — I Go Travelling Dancing): the awasa — awasa, the Afro-Surinamese dance of the Marrons of the interior, a beauty dance in which the women depict their beauty and the men show strength and skill, its dancers often true acrobats — warmed up in counted awasa steps (four times eight counts bouncing; step and tap side to side with rotating wrists; circling with everyday hand gestures — combing hair, cooking, bathing in the river, gathering things) to energetic percussion at 100 BPM with apinti drums; the Saramaccaner dancers' kawai, a foot rattle of Amazon-plant seeds, keeps the drums' rhythm (glossary, printed p. 198); the solo dance journey of 30 seconds to a minute travels mostly in awasa steps, by land, river or air made clear in the dance (teacher guide — evidence) | Book 1, printed pp. 64–68 |
