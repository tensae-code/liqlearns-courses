# Hands-on — Awasa Dance

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Count the warm-up
Practise the counted warm-up: four times eight counts bouncing, then step and tap side to side with rotating wrists, keeping the count together.

## Gesture circle
In a circle, circle with the everyday gestures — combing hair, cooking, bathing in the river, gathering things — each learner leading one gesture for eight counts.

## Travel in awasa steps
Dance a 30-second solo journey in awasa steps. Choose land, river or air, and make the way of travelling clear in the dance.
