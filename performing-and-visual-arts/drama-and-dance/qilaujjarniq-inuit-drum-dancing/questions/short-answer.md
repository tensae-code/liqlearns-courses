# Qilaujjarniq: Inuit Drum Dancing — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the drum stick called?
**Answer: The katuk.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Besides music, what was the qilaut once used as?
**Answer: A shaman's tool.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Why do schools and communities teach children drum dancing?
**Answer: So they learn their ancestors' customs.**
