# Qilaujjarniq: Inuit Drum Dancing — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The drum of drum dancing is the…
a) qilaut — the frame drum  b) side drum  c) steel drum
**Answer: a) qilaut — the frame drum** — the qilaut, beaten with the katuk (drum stick).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Drum dancing could once settle…
a) a hunting route  b) disagreements between men — by song instead of anger  c) a price
**Answer: b) disagreements between men — by song instead of anger** — disagreements settled by song through drum dancing instead of anger.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** In the east, drum dancing has…
a) a whole group singing to rim-beaten drums  b) one singer singing to a big drum with caribou skin wrapped on it  c) no drum at all
**Answer: b) one singer singing to a big drum with caribou skin wrapped on it** — one singer, a big drum with caribou skin wrapped on it.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In the west, drummers beat…
a) the drum's rim with thin wooden beaters, and a group sings  b) only the drum's centre, silently  c) the floor
**Answer: a) the drum's rim with thin wooden beaters, and a group sings** — the rim with thin wooden beaters, a whole group singing together.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Today drum dancing is used for…
a) punishments  b) celebrations and happy occasions  c) hunting calls
**Answer: b) celebrations and happy occasions** — celebrations and happy occasions; schools teach it to children.
