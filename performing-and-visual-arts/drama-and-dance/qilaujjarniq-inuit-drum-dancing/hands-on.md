# Hands-on — Qilaujjarniq: Inuit Drum Dancing

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Beat the rim, beat the skin
With a safe frame or hoop and a soft beater, try the two ways: one singer with a steady beat (east), then the whole group singing while beating the rim (west).

## Settle it with song
In pairs, act a small disagreement, then settle it the drum-dancing way — with a song instead of anger.
