# Video script — Qilaujjarniq: Inuit Drum Dancing

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

An old custom — a song, a drum.

Qilaut! Katuk! East — one singer. West — the rim, and everyone sings.

Still danced today — for joy, and taught to children.

That is qilaujjarniq.
