# Lesson plan — Qilaujjarniq: Inuit Drum Dancing

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Drama and Dance · **Lesson:** Qilaujjarniq: Inuit Drum Dancing

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **ᕿᓚᐅᔾᔭᕐᓂᖅ — songs with the drum** — **Drum dancing** is an old custom: songs are sung while the **qilaut** (frame drum) is beaten with the **katuk** (drum stick).
2. **Two regional ways** — Drum dancing **differs across the Arctic**.
3. **Drum dancing today** — Drum dancing is still performed **more in the west than the east**, and is used today for **celebrations and happy occasions**.

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks drum dancing is the same everywhere → the book teaches two regional ways — east and west differ in drum, beaters and who sings.
- Thinks the drum is only for music → the qilaut was once also a shaman's tool, and drum dancing could settle disagreements.
