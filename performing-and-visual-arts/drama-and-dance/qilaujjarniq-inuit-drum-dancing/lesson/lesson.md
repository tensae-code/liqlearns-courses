## Sub lesson 1 — ᕿᓚᐅᔾᔭᕐᓂᖅ — songs with the drum

**Drum dancing** is an old custom: songs are sung while the **qilaut** (frame drum) is beaten with the **katuk** (drum stick). The qilaut was once also a shaman's tool, and disagreements between men could be settled by song through drum dancing instead of anger.

In your mind's eye: a drum dancer beating the qilaut frame drum with the katuk stick while singing.

## Sub lesson 2 — Two regional ways

Drum dancing **differs across the Arctic**. In the **east**, one singer sings to a big drum with caribou skin wrapped on it; in the **west**, the drummers beat the drum's rim with thin wooden beaters and a whole group sings together.

## Sub lesson 3 — Drum dancing today

Drum dancing is still performed **more in the west than the east**, and is used today for **celebrations and happy occasions**. Schools and communities teach children drum dancing so they learn their ancestors' customs.
