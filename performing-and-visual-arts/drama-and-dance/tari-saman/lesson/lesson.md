# Tari Saman

## Do it first

Name one thing that belongs in this lesson — Tari Saman, Aceh's dance of a thousand hands, performed in a kneeling row with fast clapped patterns. Say what it is in your own words. If you can name one, you are already inside the lesson.

## Sub lesson 1 — Unit Pembelajaran 2

Tari Saman of the Gayo people (Aceh) as the appreciation example — unison sitting dance whose sounding movements (hand claps, chest pats) and silent movements are stylised from prayer postures such as sujud (tungkuk) and doa (langak).

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson holds, part by part, in your own words. Companion checks: did every part get named, with its Indonesian name kept where the book gives one?

## Do It Yourself

1. Choose one named part of this lesson and find out one more true thing about it — from a person who knows it, or from the book your part came from.
2. Make or perform a small piece in the way of that part: a rhythm, a step, a drawing, a shaped piece of clay.
3. Write one sentence: what does this part show about Indonesia's own way of teaching its arts?
