# Video script — Tari Saman

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This is Indonesia's own lesson.

**Unit Pembelajaran 2.** Tari Saman of the Gayo people (Aceh) as the appreciation example — unison sitting dance whose sounding movements (hand claps, chest pats) and silent movements are stylised from prayer postures such as sujud (tungkuk) and doa (langak).

That is Tari Saman — Indonesia's way, kept by name.
