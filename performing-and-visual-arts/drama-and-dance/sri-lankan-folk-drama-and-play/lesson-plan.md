**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this course in the same title.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Let's identify the features of folk drama** — Sri Lanka's folk drama heritage — the Nadagam tradition whole: its Catholic-folk origins, its stock characters, music and all-night village staging, with the folk play's social place
2. **Let's perform the Kanda Vala play** — performing Sri Lanka's own folk play — its signs, style and character work, staged by the class from the guide's production notes
3. **Let's appreciate the Kanda Vala play** — the play honoured as heritage — its first staging (6 September 1958), its makers, its place among the island's folk plays (the Karadippuwa and Sokari named alongside), and how to judge it as art
