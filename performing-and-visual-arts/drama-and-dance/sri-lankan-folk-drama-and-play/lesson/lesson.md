# Sri Lankan Folk Drama and Play

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about sri lankan folk drama and play. Then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — Let's identify the features of folk drama

Sri Lanka's folk drama heritage — the Nadagam tradition whole: its Catholic-folk origins, its stock characters, music and all-night village staging, with the folk play's social place

## Sub lesson 2 — Let's perform the Kanda Vala play

Performing Sri Lanka's own folk play — its signs, style and character work, staged by the class from the guide's production notes

## Sub lesson 3 — Let's appreciate the Kanda Vala play

The play honoured as heritage — its first staging (6 September 1958), its makers, its place among the island's folk plays (the Karadippuwa and Sokari named alongside), and how to judge it as art
