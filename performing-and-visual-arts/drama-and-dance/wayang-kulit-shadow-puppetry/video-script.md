# Video script — Wayang Kulit Shadow Puppetry

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A light. A screen. A flat puppet — and its shadow plays!

That is wayang kulit — the shadow theatre of Malay-Indonesian heritage, alive in Singapore.

Now make your own puppet prop — and let its shadow tell the story.
