# Wayang Kulit Shadow Puppetry

## Do it first

Quick start: from the title alone, guess what this lesson's work is. Then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — Wayang kulit among the shadow arts

In the guide's Goal 2 extension, **artworks that use shadow** are studied and **wayang kulit is named first** — the **shadow theatre of Singapore's Malay-Indonesian heritage**, where flat puppets play as shadows on a lit screen.

## Sub lesson 2 — Your own shadow puppet play

The children answer the heritage art by making: **puppet props for their own shadow puppet play** — the maker's hands learn what the eyes admired.
