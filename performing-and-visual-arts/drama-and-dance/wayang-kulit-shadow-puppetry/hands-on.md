# Hands-on — Wayang Kulit Shadow Puppetry

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Make a shadow puppet prop
Draw a character on card, cut it out, and tape a stick behind it. Hold it between a lamp and a sheet or wall and watch its shadow.

## Play a shadow scene
In a small group, move your puppets behind the screen and give them voices — a short scene, played in shadow only.
