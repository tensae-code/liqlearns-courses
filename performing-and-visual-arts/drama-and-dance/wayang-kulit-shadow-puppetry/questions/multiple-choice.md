# Wayang Kulit Shadow Puppetry — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Wayang kulit is…
a) a shadow puppet theatre  b) a kind of dance  c) a painting style
**Answer: a) a shadow puppet theatre** — Singapore's Malay-Indonesian heritage shadow theatre.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Among artworks that use shadow, the guide names…
a) wayang kulit first  b) photography first  c) batik first
**Answer: a) wayang kulit first**.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The audience in shadow theatre watches…
a) the puppets' shadows  b) the puppets themselves  c) the lamp
**Answer: a) the puppets' shadows** on the screen.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The children make…
a) puppet props for their own shadow puppet play  b) shadow photographs  c) clay puppets only
**Answer: a) puppet props for their own shadow puppet play**.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Wayang kulit belongs to the heritage of…
a) Singapore's Malay-Indonesian community  b) Europe  c) West Africa
**Answer: a) Singapore's Malay-Indonesian community**.
