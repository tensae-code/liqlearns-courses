# Wayang Kulit Shadow Puppetry — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Wayang kulit is named first among the shadow artworks in the guide.
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In wayang kulit the audience watches the puppets, not shadows.
**Answer: False** — the audience watches the shadows.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Children only look at wayang kulit; they never make anything.
**Answer: False** — they make puppet props for their own shadow puppet play.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Wayang kulit is part of Singapore's heritage.
**Answer: True.**
