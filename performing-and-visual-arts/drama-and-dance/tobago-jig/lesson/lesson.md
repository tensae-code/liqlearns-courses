## Sub lesson 1 — The Tobago Jig and its invitation

The **Tobago Jig** is Tobago's own dance, taught in Form 2 of Trinidad and Tobago's Dance guide. It is a **social dance**: it has a **social context**, and it begins with **partner-invitation gestures** — one dancer invites another to join the dance.

## Sub lesson 2 — Its four steps, in a paired sequence

The Jig has **four steps**: the **akimbo stance**, **skips**, **mincing of the feet ball-to-heel**, and **snatches**. They are danced to **Tambrin Band music**, and the lesson composes them into a **one-minute paired sequence** — the steps joined, with your partner, into a short whole dance.
