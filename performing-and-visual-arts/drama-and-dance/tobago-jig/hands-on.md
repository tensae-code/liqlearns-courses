# Hands-on — Tobago Jig

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Invite and answer
Practise the partner-invitation gestures: one partner invites, the other accepts, and you take the akimbo stance together.

## Build the minute
Put the four steps in order — stance, skips, mincing ball-to-heel, snatches — and dance them with your partner to music for one full minute.
