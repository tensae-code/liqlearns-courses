# Tobago Jig — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Tobago Jig begins with…
a) partner-invitation gestures  b) a solo spin  c) sitting down
**Answer: a) partner-invitation gestures**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The Tobago Jig is a…
a) social, paired dance  b) solo dance  c) marching drill
**Answer: a) social, paired dance**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Which is NOT one of the Jig's four steps?
a) the akimbo stance  b) skips  c) the cartwheel
**Answer: c) the cartwheel**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The mincing step moves the feet…
a) ball-to-heel  b) heel-to-ball only, never the ball  c) on the knees
**Answer: a) ball-to-heel**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The steps are composed into…
a) a one-minute paired sequence  b) a ten-minute solo  c) a written essay
**Answer: a) a one-minute paired sequence**
