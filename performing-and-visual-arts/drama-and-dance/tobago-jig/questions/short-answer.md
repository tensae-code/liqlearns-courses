# Tobago Jig — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** To what music is the Tobago Jig danced in the lesson?
**Answer: Tambrin Band music.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the four steps of the Tobago Jig.
**Answer: Akimbo stance, skips, mincing of the feet ball-to-heel, and snatches.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** What kind of dance is the Tobago Jig — solo or social?
**Answer: A social dance, danced with a partner.**
