# Lesson plan — Tobago Jig

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Drama and Dance · **Lesson:** Tobago Jig
**Place in the curriculum:** Drama and Dance (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the Tobago Jig — Tobago's own social dance: its partner-invitation gestures and its four steps, danced to Tambrin Band music and composed into a one-minute paired sequence.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this course in the same title.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The Tobago Jig and its invitation** — The Jig's social context and its partner-invitation gestures — the dance begins by inviting a partner.
2. **Its four steps, in a paired sequence** — The four steps — akimbo stance, skips, mincing of the feet ball-to-heel, and snatches — danced to Tambrin Band music and composed into a one-minute paired sequence.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say what the tobago jig and its invitation teaches, in the book's own way.
2. Say what its four steps, in a paired sequence teaches, in the book's own way.

## What you need
Tambrin Band music (or a recording in its style), a partner, and space to dance in pairs.

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks the Jig is a solo dance → it is a paired social dance, beginning with a partner invitation.
- Misses the mincing step's order → the feet mince ball-to-heel, in that order.
