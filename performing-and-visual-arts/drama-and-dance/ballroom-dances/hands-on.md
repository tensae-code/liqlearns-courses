# Hands-on — Ballroom and Social Dances

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Discuss and demonstrate
In a group, discuss what makes Soca and Dancehall dancing appropriate at a school or family party, then demonstrate an appropriate short dance together.

## Draw the line
List three moves or behaviours on each side of the line — appropriate / inappropriate — and say why each belongs where you put it.
