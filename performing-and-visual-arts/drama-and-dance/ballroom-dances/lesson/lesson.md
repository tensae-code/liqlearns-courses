# Ballroom and Social Dances

## Do it first

Quick start: from the title alone, guess what this lesson's work is. Then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — Appropriate social dancing

In Trinidad and Tobago's Dance guide, **appropriate social dancing** is a lesson in itself (Forms 1–3, Lesson Plan 5). Dancing to **Soca and Dancehall** is **discussed and demonstrated**, and the class draws a **working line between appropriate and inappropriate party dancing**.

## Sub lesson 2 — Responsible choices at adolescence

The second half of the lesson is about **responsible choices at adolescence**: social dancing is for joining in and enjoying the music with others, and the dancer is responsible for keeping their dancing appropriate.
