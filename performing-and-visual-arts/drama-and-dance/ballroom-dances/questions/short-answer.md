# Ballroom and Social Dances — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which two kinds of music are named in the social-dancing lesson?
**Answer: Soca and Dancehall.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What line does the lesson teach dancers to draw?
**Answer: The line between appropriate and inappropriate party dancing.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Who must make responsible choices when dancing socially?
**Answer: The dancer — each learner, at adolescence.**
