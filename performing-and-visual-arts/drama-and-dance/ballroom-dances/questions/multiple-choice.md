# Ballroom and Social Dances — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Appropriate social dancing in the guide is taught through…
a) dancing to Soca and Dancehall, discussed and demonstrated  b) silent sitting  c) only written tests
**Answer: a) dancing to Soca and Dancehall, discussed and demonstrated**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The lesson draws a working line between…
a) appropriate and inappropriate party dancing  b) fast and slow music  c) loud and quiet music
**Answer: a) appropriate and inappropriate party dancing**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Responsible choices at adolescence are made by…
a) the dancer  b) only the teacher  c) the DJ
**Answer: a) the dancer**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Social dancing is for…
a) joining in and enjoying music with others, appropriately  b) showing off inappropriate moves  c) dancing alone in secret
**Answer: a) joining in and enjoying music with others, appropriately**

**5. (Sub lesson 1)** Which music is named for the social-dancing lesson?
a) Soca and Dancehall  b) only classical ballet music  c) no music at all
**Answer: a) Soca and Dancehall**
