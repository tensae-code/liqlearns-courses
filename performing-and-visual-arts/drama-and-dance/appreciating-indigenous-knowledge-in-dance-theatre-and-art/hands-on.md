# Hands-on — Appreciating Indigenous Knowledge in Dance, Theatre and Art

Real things to do with what this lesson teaches — at school or at home, with people around you.

## Paper mosaic
Learners tear or cut coloured paper and lay a mosaic picture of a historical or cultural scene — the syllabus's own practice for this part.

## Three-arts walk
Groups find one example each of indigenous knowledge in a dance, a theatre form and a visual art of their area, and present the three together.
