# Appreciating Indigenous Knowledge in Dance, Theatre and Art — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Indigenous knowledge in dance lives in —
a) the community's traditional dances and instruments  b) imported steps only  c) silence
**Answer: a) the community's traditional dances and instruments.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Traditional theatre (የትውፊታዊ ተውኔት) is —
a) the community's inherited theatre form  b) a cinema  c) a radio play
**Answer: a) the community's inherited theatre form.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The guide studies traditional theatre's —
a) ticket prices  b) meaning, characteristics and performances  c) posters
**Answer: b) meaning, characteristics and performances.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Paper mosaic (የወረቀት ሞዛይክ) is —
a) a picture built from small paper pieces  b) a stone carving  c) a dance
**Answer: a) a picture built from small paper pieces.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Visual-art practice for this part includes drawing —
a) only maps  b) historical and cultural pictures  c) nothing
**Answer: b) historical and cultural pictures.**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Keeping the knowledge alive happens by —
a) practising it  b) hiding it  c) selling it
**Answer: a) practising it.**
