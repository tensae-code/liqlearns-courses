# Video script — Appreciating Indigenous Knowledge in Dance, Theatre and Art

A short read-aloud script in a super-simple voice, made later as the lesson's video. It follows the lesson's own order and names only what the lesson teaches.

---

Indigenous knowledge lives inside the arts.
In dance — the community's own dances, played and danced with its instruments.
In theatre — traditional theatre, የትውፊታዊ ተውኔት, with its own characteristics.
In visual art — historical and cultural pictures, and paper mosaic, የወረቀት ሞዛይክ.
