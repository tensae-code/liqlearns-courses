# Appreciating Indigenous Knowledge in Dance, Theatre and Art — sub lessons and depth

This is the depth record for the lesson, re-expressed as sub lessons: each sub lesson is one step up in depth, proved before the next opens.

## Sub lesson 1 — In dance and in traditional theatre (teacher guide — evidence)
Pass mark: the learner can say what traditional theatre is and how dance carries indigenous knowledge. The gate draws from this sub lesson's whole question set.

## Sub lesson 2 — In visual art (teacher guide — evidence)
Pass mark: the learner can name two visual-art practices carrying indigenous knowledge. The gate draws from this sub lesson's whole question set.
