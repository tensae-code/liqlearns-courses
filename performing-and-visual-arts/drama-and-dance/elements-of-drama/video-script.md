# Video script — Elements of Drama

A short read-aloud script in a super-simple voice, made later as the lesson's video. It follows the lesson's own order and names only what the lesson teaches.

---

Drama has elements — the pieces every play is made of.
Character, relationship, situation. Voice, movement, focus, tension.
Space, time, language, symbol, audience, mood.
And dramatic action moves: introduction — conflict — resolution.
