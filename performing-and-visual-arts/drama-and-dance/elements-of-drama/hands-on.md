# Hands-on — Elements of Drama

Real things to do with what this lesson teaches — at school or at home, with people around you.

## Element spotting
Act or recall a two-minute scene. The class calls out each element as it appears: 'tension!', 'symbol!', 'focus changed!'.

## Three-move story
In groups, build a story in exactly the action's three moves — introduction, conflict, resolution — and perform it in one minute.
