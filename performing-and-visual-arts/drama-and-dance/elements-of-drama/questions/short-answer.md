# Elements of Drama — short answer

Answer in your own words, in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name five elements of drama.
**Answer: Any five of: role/character, relationships, situation, voice, movement, focus, tension, space, time, language/dialogue, symbol, audience, mood and atmosphere.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What happens in the introduction?
**Answer: The audience meets the people and their world.**
