# Elements of Drama — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which of these is an element of drama?
a) tension  b) ticket price  c) the weather outside
**Answer: a) tension.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The audience is —
a) not an element  b) one of the elements of drama  c) only a payer
**Answer: b) one of the elements of drama.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** A scene with no tension feels —
a) stronger  b) weak exactly there  c) shorter only
**Answer: b) weak exactly there.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Dramatic action's three moves are —
a) begin, sing, end  b) introduction, conflict, resolution  c) enter, bow, exit
**Answer: b) introduction, conflict, resolution.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Conflict is —
a) a want colliding with a want  b) an argument about tickets  c) the ending
**Answer: a) a want colliding with a want.**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Resolution comes —
a) first  b) after the conflict  c) never
**Answer: b) after the conflict.**
