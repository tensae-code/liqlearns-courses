# Video script — Theme in Film

A short read-aloud script in a super-simple voice, made later as the lesson's video. It follows the lesson's own order and names only what the lesson teaches.

---

A film's theme is its central idea — one word, or a short phrase.
Theme regulates everything: characters, story, photography, plot, genre.
A huge project still begins and ends in one sentence.
And themes mirror their decade's world.
