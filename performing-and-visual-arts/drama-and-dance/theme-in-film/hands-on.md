# Hands-on — Theme in Film

Real things to do with what this lesson teaches — at school or at home, with people around you.

## One-word themes
Each learner names a known film or story and defends a one-word theme for it. The class challenges: does your word explain the ending?

## Theme shapes choices
Groups take one theme — say, loyalty — and sketch how it would change a story's characters, its ending and even its photography.
