# Theme in Film — sub lessons and depth

This is the depth record for the lesson, re-expressed as sub lessons: each sub lesson is one step up in depth, proved before the next opens.

## Sub lesson 1 — Theme — the film's central idea (teacher guide — evidence)
Pass mark: the learner can state a film's theme in one word and name what theme regulates. The gate draws from this sub lesson's whole question set.

## Sub lesson 2 — Story, plot and the decade (teacher guide — evidence)
Pass mark: the learner can give the guide's equation and say what themes reflect. The gate draws from this sub lesson's whole question set.
