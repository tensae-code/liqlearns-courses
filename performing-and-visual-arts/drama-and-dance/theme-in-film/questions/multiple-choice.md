# Theme in Film — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A film's theme is —
a) its longest scene  b) its central, unifying idea  c) its budget
**Answer: b) its central, unifying idea.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Theme is statable in —
a) one word or a short phrase  b) a whole book  c) numbers only
**Answer: a) one word or a short phrase.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Theme regulates —
a) only the title  b) characters, story, photography, plot and genre  c) only the music
**Answer: b) characters, story, photography, plot and genre.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Story + plot yields theme through —
a) character development  b) longer credits  c) more cameras
**Answer: a) character development.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Themes reflect —
a) nothing outside the film  b) their decade's social, economic and political climate  c) only the director's mood
**Answer: b) their decade's social, economic and political climate.**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** The audience reads the theme from —
a) how the characters change  b) the poster only  c) the ticket
**Answer: a) how the characters change.**
