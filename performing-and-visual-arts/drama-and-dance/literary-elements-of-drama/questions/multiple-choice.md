# Literary Elements of Drama — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Aristotle's six include —
a) plot, theme, character, dialogue, music/rhythm, spectacle  b) only plot and theme  c) costume and props
**Answer: a) plot, theme, character, dialogue, music/rhythm, spectacle.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The modern additions are —
a) convention, genre, audience  b) lights, sound, set  c) acts, scenes, lines
**Answer: a) convention, genre, audience.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** A soliloquy is —
a) a duet  b) a character speaking thoughts alone  c) a dance
**Answer: b) a character speaking thoughts alone.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The climax is —
a) the first event  b) the highest, most breathless moment  c) the final bow
**Answer: b) the highest, most breathless moment.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The denouement —
a) opens the story  b) unties the story's knots at the end  c) is the mountain's peak
**Answer: b) unties the story's knots at the end.**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Rising action comes —
a) after the climax  b) before the climax, climbing toward it  c) after the denouement
**Answer: b) before the climax, climbing toward it.**
