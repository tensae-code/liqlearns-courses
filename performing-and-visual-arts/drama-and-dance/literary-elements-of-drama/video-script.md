# Video script — Literary Elements of Drama

A short read-aloud script in a super-simple voice, made later as the lesson's video. It follows the lesson's own order and names only what the lesson teaches.

---

Aristotle named six elements: plot, theme, character, dialogue, music and rhythm, spectacle.
Modern theatre adds: convention, genre, audience.
And plot climbs a mountain: exposition, rising action, climax, falling action, denouement.
