# Video script — Hora Dance

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This is Moldova's own lesson.

**the hora in "Trageți hora" and Paul Constantinescu's "Joc dobrogean".** Theme 1, the hora in "Trageți hora" and Paul Constantinescu's "Joc dobrogean" — Moldova's great circle dance: singing and dancing the hora slowly (hora mare) and fast (hora-bătuta), and the folk-dance melodies of a Dobrogea dance suite.

**Carol Miculi's "Hora" and Musicescu's "Vine știuca de la baltă".** Theme 3, Carol Miculi's "Hora" and Musicescu's "Vine știuca de la baltă" — the hora's swaying rhythm and held step carried into piano pieces built on hora melodies.

**Dansul popular.** Unit 2, Dansul popular — hora mare, the slow great circle, against hora-bătuta with its shouted strigături calls, in the song "Hai la horă".

**song "Joc de sărbătoare".** Unit 4, song "Joc de sărbătoare" — a holiday dance-song: the hora danced at a spring feast, with calls to the lăutari to play louder.

**Hora satului.** Unit 1, Hora satului — the village hora festival: the great slow hora that opens it, up to sixty dances in a day, and the age (14–15) when dancers join the circle.

That is Hora Dance — Moldova's way, kept by name.
