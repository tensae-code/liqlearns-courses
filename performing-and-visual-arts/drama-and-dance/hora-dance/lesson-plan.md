# Lesson plan — Hora Dance

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Drama and Dance · **Lesson:** Hora Dance
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 4 — Drama and Dance (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The hora, the great circle dance of Moldova and Romania, in its songs and piano and choral settings.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the course's earlier lessons in its title; recommended first, never forced.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of ..." in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **the hora in "Trageți hora" and Paul Constantinescu's "Joc dobrogean"** — Theme 1, the hora in "Trageți hora" and Paul Constantinescu's "Joc dobrogean" — Moldova's great circle dance: singing and dancing the hora slowly (hora mare) and fast (hora-bătuta), and the folk-dance melodies of a Dobrogea dance suite.
2. **Carol Miculi's "Hora" and Musicescu's "Vine știuca de la baltă"** — Theme 3, Carol Miculi's "Hora" and Musicescu's "Vine știuca de la baltă" — the hora's swaying rhythm and held step carried into piano pieces built on hora melodies.
3. **Dansul popular** — Unit 2, Dansul popular — hora mare, the slow great circle, against hora-bătuta with its shouted strigături calls, in the song "Hai la horă".
4. **song "Joc de sărbătoare"** — Unit 4, song "Joc de sărbătoare" — a holiday dance-song: the hora danced at a spring feast, with calls to the lăutari to play louder.
5. **Hora satului** — Unit 1, Hora satului — the village hora festival: the great slow hora that opens it, up to sixty dances in a day, and the age (14–15) when dancers join the circle.
