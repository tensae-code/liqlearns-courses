# Hora Dance

## Do it first

Name one thing that belongs in this lesson — The hora, the great circle dance of Moldova and Romania, in its songs and piano and choral settings. Say what it is in your own words. If you can name one, you are already inside the lesson.

## Sub lesson 1 — the hora in "Trageți hora" and Paul Constantinescu's "Joc dobrogean"

Theme 1, the hora in "Trageți hora" and Paul Constantinescu's "Joc dobrogean" — Moldova's great circle dance: singing and dancing the hora slowly (hora mare) and fast (hora-bătuta), and the folk-dance melodies of a Dobrogea dance suite.

## Sub lesson 2 — Carol Miculi's "Hora" and Musicescu's "Vine știuca de la baltă"

Theme 3, Carol Miculi's "Hora" and Musicescu's "Vine știuca de la baltă" — the hora's swaying rhythm and held step carried into piano pieces built on hora melodies.

## Sub lesson 3 — Dansul popular

Unit 2, Dansul popular — hora mare, the slow great circle, against hora-bătuta with its shouted strigături calls, in the song "Hai la horă".

## Sub lesson 4 — song "Joc de sărbătoare"

Unit 4, song "Joc de sărbătoare" — a holiday dance-song: the hora danced at a spring feast, with calls to the lăutari to play louder.

## Sub lesson 5 — Hora satului

Unit 1, Hora satului — the village hora festival: the great slow hora that opens it, up to sixty dances in a day, and the age (14–15) when dancers join the circle.

## Do It Yourself

1. Choose one named part of this lesson and find out one more true thing about it — from a person who knows it, or from the book your part came from.
2. Make or perform a small piece in the way of that part: a rhythm, a step, a drawing, a shaped piece of clay.
3. Write one sentence: what does this part show about Moldova's own way of teaching its arts?
