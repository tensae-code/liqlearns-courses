# Hora Dance — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — the hora in "Trageți hora" and Paul Constantinescu's "Joc dobrogean"
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Theme 1, the hora in "Trageți hora" and Paul Constantinescu's "Joc dobrogean" — Moldova's great circle dance: singing and dancing the hora slowly (hora mare) and fast (hora-bătuta), and the folk-dance melodies of a Dobrogea dance suite.

## Sub lesson 2 — Carol Miculi's "Hora" and Musicescu's "Vine știuca de la baltă"
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Theme 3, Carol Miculi's "Hora" and Musicescu's "Vine știuca de la baltă" — the hora's swaying rhythm and held step carried into piano pieces built on hora melodies.

## Sub lesson 3 — Dansul popular
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Unit 2, Dansul popular — hora mare, the slow great circle, against hora-bătuta with its shouted strigături calls, in the song "Hai la horă".

## Sub lesson 4 — song "Joc de sărbătoare"
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Unit 4, song "Joc de sărbătoare" — a holiday dance-song: the hora danced at a spring feast, with calls to the lăutari to play louder.

## Sub lesson 5 — Hora satului
Sub lesson 5 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Unit 1, Hora satului — the village hora festival: the great slow hora that opens it, up to sixty dances in a day, and the age (14–15) when dancers join the circle.
