# PNG Theatre Styles and Practices — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Sepik Tambaran is…
a) a PNG dance theatre  b) a Japanese puppet form
**Answer: a) a PNG dance theatre.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Orokolo Eharo is…
a) mask theatre  b) opera
**Answer: a) mask theatre.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Learners observe…
a) a traditional performance in the local community  b) only videos of Europe
**Answer: a) a traditional performance in the local community.**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** A myth or legend is identified from…
a) the learner's local community  b) a foreign textbook
**Answer: a) the learner's local community.**
