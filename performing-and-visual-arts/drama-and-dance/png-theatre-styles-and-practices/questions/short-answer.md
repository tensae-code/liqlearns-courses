# PNG Theatre Styles and Practices — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name three of PNG's named theatre forms.
**Answer: Orokolo Eharo, Sepik Tambaran, Trobriand Kesawaga, Malangan, Kerema Hevehe, Eastern Highlands farce drama (any three).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Against what three environments are dance and drama explored?
**Answer: The physical, spiritual and social environment.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What analysis closes the unit?
**Answer: A summative analysis of the genre of the selected work.**
