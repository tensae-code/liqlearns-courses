# PNG Theatre Styles and Practices

## Do it first

Look at the sub lesson list. Pick the part you already know something about, and say what you know. Then start at Sub lesson 1 — every part builds on the one before it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Theatre rooted in the community

Learners observe a traditional performance in the local community, work on a play by a Papua New Guinean writer, and identify a myth or legend from the local community; the elements of dance and drama are explored against the physical, spiritual and social environment.

## Sub lesson 2 — PNG's named theatre forms

The guide's world-theatre list names Papua New Guinea's own forms: Orokolo Eharo mask theatre, Sepik Tambaran dance theatre, Trobriand Kesawaga dance theatre, Malangan mask theatre, Kerema Hevehe mask theatre, and Eastern Highlands farce drama.

## Sub lesson 3 — Analysing the genre

The unit closes with a summative analysis of the genre of the selected work — the community's own performance, judged as theatre.
