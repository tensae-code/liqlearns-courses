# Video script — PNG Theatre Styles and Practices

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

**Theatre rooted in the community.** Learners observe a traditional performance in the local community, work on a play by a Papua New Guinean writer, and identify a myth or legend from the local community; the elements of dance and drama are explored against the physical, spiritual and social environment.

**PNG's named theatre forms.** The guide's world-theatre list names Papua New Guinea's own forms: Orokolo Eharo mask theatre, Sepik Tambaran dance theatre, Trobriand Kesawaga dance theatre, Malangan mask theatre, Kerema Hevehe mask theatre, and Eastern Highlands farce drama.

**Analysing the genre.** The unit closes with a summative analysis of the genre of the selected work — the community's own performance, judged as theatre.
