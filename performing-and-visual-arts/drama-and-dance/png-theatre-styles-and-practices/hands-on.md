# Hands-on — PNG Theatre Styles and Practices

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Theatre rooted in the community
Do the practice this part names, with real materials and a partner: Learners observe a traditional performance in the local community, work on a play by a Papua New Guinean writer, and identify a myth or legend from the local community; the elements of dance and drama are explored against the physical, spiritual and social environment.

## PNG's named theatre forms
Do the practice this part names, with real materials and a partner: The guide's world-theatre list names Papua New Guinea's own forms: Orokolo Eharo mask theatre, Sepik Tambaran dance theatre, Trobriand Kesawaga dance theatre, Malangan mask theatre, Kerema Hevehe mask theatre, and Eastern Highlands farce drama.

## Analysing the genre
Do the practice this part names, with real materials and a partner: The unit closes with a summative analysis of the genre of the selected work — the community's own performance, judged as theatre.
