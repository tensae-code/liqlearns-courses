# The Waltz — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The waltz is in…
a) triple time  b) two time only  c) five time
**Answer: a) triple time**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The waltz was born from…
a) the Austrian folk Ländler  b) the tango  c) the cakewalk
**Answer: a) the Austrian folk Ländler**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The famous Viennese waltz tune named is…
a) the Blue Danube  b) the Radetzky March  c) Tiger Rag
**Answer: a) the Blue Danube**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** A waltz phrase has…
a) eight bars  b) two bars  c) one bar
**Answer: a) eight bars**

**5. (Sub lesson 1)** Four phrases make…
a) a section  b) a whole opera  c) a march
**Answer: a) a section**
