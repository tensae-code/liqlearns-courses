# The Waltz — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How many beats turn in each waltz bar?
**Answer: Three.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Where did the upper class dance the waltz?
**Answer: In its salons.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** On which famous ship was the waltz played?
**Answer: The Titanic.**
