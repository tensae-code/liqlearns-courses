# The Waltz — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The waltz comes from a folk dance.
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Waltz phrases are eight bars long.
**Answer: True.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The waltz is danced in two time.
**Answer: False.**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** The Blue Danube is a waltz.
