# The Waltz

## Do it first

Quick start: say one thing you already know — a tune, a dance, a name — that belongs to this lesson's world. Keep it in mind; the parts will meet it.

## Sub lesson 1 — The Waltz

The **waltz** turns in **triple time** — one-two-three, one-two-three. It was born from the Austrian folk **Ländler**, grew into the **Viennese waltz**, and its most famous tune is the **Blue Danube**. The upper class took the waltz into its salons — it was even played on the Titanic. Waltz music is built in **phrases of eight bars, four phrases to a section** — count them as you turn.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the parts in order, one sentence each. Companion checks: did every part get its sentence, and did the order hold?
