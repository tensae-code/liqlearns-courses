# Umudiho and Umushayayo Dances — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Umudiho and umushayayo are…
a) Rwanda's rapid dances  b) clay techniques  c) letter styles
**Answer: a) Rwanda's rapid dances**

**2.** Dancing them allies…
a) the song's melody with dance steps and stretched arms  b) paint with paper  c) thread with cloth
**Answer: a) the song's melody with dance steps and stretched arms**

**3.** Which theme is danced with umudiho/umushayayo in the guide?
a) unity and reconciliation  b) examinations  c) farming prices
**Answer: a) unity and reconciliation**

**4.** The dances are performed…
a) while singing in public  b) in silence, alone  c) sitting at a desk
**Answer: a) while singing in public**
