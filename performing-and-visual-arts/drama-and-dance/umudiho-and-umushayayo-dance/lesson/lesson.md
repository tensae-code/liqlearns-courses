# Umudiho and Umushayayo Dances

## Do it first

Start with the first part below — do it or make it before you read any explanation.

## Sub lesson 1 — Rwanda's rapid dances

**Umudiho** and **umushayayo** are Rwanda's rapid dances. To dance them is to **ally the song's melody with dance steps and stretched arms** while singing in public — song, steps and arms moving as one.

## Sub lesson 2 — Dancing the themes

In the Primary 2 guide the dances carry the song themes one by one: **politeness**, **hygiene and environment**, **unity and reconciliation**, **Rwandan culture**, and **religious songs** — each danced with umudiho or umushayayo, matching song, steps and arms, and fine-tuning the voice.
