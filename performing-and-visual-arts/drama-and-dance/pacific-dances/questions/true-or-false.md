# Pacific Dances — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A Pacific dance can be created by learners, not only copied.
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Guest speakers have no place in exploring Pacific dances.
**Answer: False — exploration is by video and guest speakers.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Song words are learned with the steps of a Pacific dance.
**Answer: True.**
