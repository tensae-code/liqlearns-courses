# Pacific Dances — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Manus Garamut Dance comes from…
a) Papua New Guinea's Manus  b) Ireland
**Answer: a) Papua New Guinea's Manus.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Pacific dances are explored through…
a) video and guest speakers  b) books only, never people
**Answer: a) video and guest speakers.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Hula and Tamure are…
a) Pacific dances named in the guide  b) PNG instruments
**Answer: a) Pacific dances named in the guide.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Learners perform…
a) a Pacific dance whose steps and song words they learned  b) nothing
**Answer: a) a Pacific dance whose steps and song words they learned.**
