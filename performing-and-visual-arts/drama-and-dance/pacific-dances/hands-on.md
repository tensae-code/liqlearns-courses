# Hands-on — Pacific Dances

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Exploring Pacific dances
Do the practice this part names, with real materials and a partner: Pacific dances are explored by video and guest speakers: naming dances and their countries across Melanesia, Polynesia, Micronesia and Australasia — the guide names the Manus Garamut Dance, Hula Dance, Tamure, Solomon Dance, Mask Dance, Fire Dance and Tapioka Dance.

## Comparing, creating and performing
Do the practice this part names, with real materials and a partner: Two dances are compared for similarities and differences; then learners create a Pacific dance, learn the steps, movements and song words of a Pacific dance, and perform it.
