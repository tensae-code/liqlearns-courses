# Video script — Pacific Dances

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

**Exploring Pacific dances.** Pacific dances are explored by video and guest speakers: naming dances and their countries across Melanesia, Polynesia, Micronesia and Australasia — the guide names the Manus Garamut Dance, Hula Dance, Tamure, Solomon Dance, Mask Dance, Fire Dance and Tapioka Dance.

**Comparing, creating and performing.** Two dances are compared for similarities and differences; then learners create a Pacific dance, learn the steps, movements and song words of a Pacific dance, and perform it.
