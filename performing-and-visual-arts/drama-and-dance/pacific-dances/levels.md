# Pacific Dances — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — Exploring Pacific dances
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Pacific dances are explored by video and guest speakers: naming dances and their countries across Melanesia, Polynesia, Micronesia and Australasia — the guide names the Manus Garamut Dance, Hula Dance, Tamure, Solomon Dance, Mask Dance, Fire Dance and Tapioka Dance.

## Sub lesson 2 — Comparing, creating and performing
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Two dances are compared for similarities and differences; then learners create a Pacific dance, learn the steps, movements and song words of a Pacific dance, and perform it.
