# Bătuta Dance

## Do it first

Name one thing that belongs in this lesson — Bătuta, the stamped Moldovan and Romanian folk dance-song. Say what it is in your own words. If you can name one, you are already inside the lesson.

## Sub lesson 1 — folk dance-song "Bătuta"

Theme 2, folk dance-song "Bătuta" — the bătuta, Moldova's stamping dance-song ("stamp in place so the basil sprouts"), watched in the southern-Moldova style of the Advahov Brothers folk orchestra.

## Sub lesson 2 — Dansul popular

Unit 2, Dansul popular — the bătuta, the stamped dance, as the National Ballet "Joc" performs Moldova's folk dances.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson holds, part by part, in your own words. Companion checks: did every part get named, with its Moldovan name kept where the book gives one?

## Do It Yourself

1. Choose one named part of this lesson and find out one more true thing about it — from a person who knows it, or from the book your part came from.
2. Make or perform a small piece in the way of that part: a rhythm, a step, a drawing, a shaped piece of clay.
3. Write one sentence: what does this part show about Moldova's own way of teaching its arts?
