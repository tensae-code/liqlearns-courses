The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

This is Moldova's own lesson.

**folk dance-song "Bătuta".** Theme 2, folk dance-song "Bătuta" — the bătuta, Moldova's stamping dance-song ("stamp in place so the basil sprouts"), watched in the southern-Moldova style of the Advahov Brothers folk orchestra.

**Dansul popular.** Unit 2, Dansul popular — the bătuta, the stamped dance, as the National Ballet "Joc" performs Moldova's folk dances.

That is Bătuta Dance — Moldova's way, kept by name.
