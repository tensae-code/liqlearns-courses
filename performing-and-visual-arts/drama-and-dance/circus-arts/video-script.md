The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

**Artes circenses.** The circus arts — acrobatics, juggling, balancing and dance, performed in the circus ring and in avenues and plazas; Venezuela's National Circus Foundation runs an artistic circus training programme for young people of 16 to 25 at the Nuevo Circo de Caracas.

That is Circus Arts — Venezuela's own books teach it this way.
