# Hands-on — Circus Arts

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Artes circenses
Work through this part with real materials or your own body, the way the book sets it: the circus arts — acrobatics, juggling, balancing and dance, performed in the circus ring and in avenues and plazas; Venezuela's National Circus Foundation runs an artistic circus training programme for young people of 16 to 25 at the Nuevo Circo de Caracas. Show a companion what you made or did, and say what the part teaches.
