# Preparing to Perform: Warm-Up, Relaxation and Nerves — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The first step of stop-and-think is…
a) run faster  b) stop walking and breathe deeply, in and out very slowly  c) hide
**Answer: b) stop walking and breathe deeply, in and out very slowly** — stop, and take deep breaths in and out very slowly until calmer.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The breathing continues until…
a) the song ends  b) you feel calmer  c) someone calls you
**Answer: b) you feel calmer** — until you feel calmer.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The second step is to…
a) forget the feeling  b) think about how you feel and why, and what can help  c) blame the audience
**Answer: b) think about how you feel and why, and what can help** — name the feeling, its reason, and what you can do to feel less nervous.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Laughter from an audience can mean…
a) always mockery  b) they are happy and enjoying the dance  c) the dance is over
**Answer: b) they are happy and enjoying the dance** — happiness and enjoyment — not mockery.

**5. (Sub lesson 1)** Tuka needed the routine because…
a) he forgot his costume  b) worry stopped him when a friend laughed  c) he was late
**Answer: b) worry stopped him when a friend laughed** — a friend's laughter shook his nerve before the talent show.
