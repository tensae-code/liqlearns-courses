# Preparing to Perform: Warm-Up, Relaxation and Nerves — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Breathing slowly can calm a nervous performer.
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The routine says to keep walking faster when worry comes.
**Answer: False — first stop walking, then breathe.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Naming the feeling is part of calming down.
**Answer: True.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** If the audience laughs, they are certainly mocking the dancer.
**Answer: False — laughter can mean they are happy and enjoying the dance.**
