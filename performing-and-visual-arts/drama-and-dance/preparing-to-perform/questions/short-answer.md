# Preparing to Perform: Warm-Up, Relaxation and Nerves — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Say the stop-and-think routine in order.
**Answer: Stop walking; breathe in and out very slowly until calmer; then think about how you feel and why, and what you can do to feel less nervous.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What else might audience laughter mean, besides mocking?
**Answer: That the audience is happy and enjoying the dance.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What question about the feeling does the routine ask?
**Answer: How do I feel, and why do I feel it?**
