# Hands-on — Preparing to Perform: Warm-Up, Relaxation and Nerves

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Practise the stop
Walk as if going on stage. At a signal, stop, breathe in and out very slowly five times, name your feeling in one word, then walk on.

## Read the laughter
In pairs: one performs a funny dance move, the other laughs with enjoyment. The performer practises saying: 'They are enjoying it.'
