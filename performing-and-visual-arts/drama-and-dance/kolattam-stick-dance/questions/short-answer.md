# Kolattam Stick Dance — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What do Kolattam dancers hold and strike in rhythm?
**Answer: Sticks.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What two kinds of movement is Kolattam performed for?
**Answer: Locomotor and axial movements (and its body shapes).**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Kolattam is the stick dance of which heritage in Trinidad and Tobago?
**Answer: Its Indian heritage.**
