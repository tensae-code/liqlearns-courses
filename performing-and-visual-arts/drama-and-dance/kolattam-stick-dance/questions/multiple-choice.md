# Kolattam Stick Dance — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Kolattam is a…
a) stick dance  b) mask dance  c) water dance
**Answer: a) stick dance**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Kolattam belongs to the country's…
a) Indian heritage  b) French heritage only  c) school sports
**Answer: a) Indian heritage**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Locomotor movements are movements that…
a) travel through space  b) stay frozen  c) are only in the arms
**Answer: a) travel through space**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Kolattam is taught alongside…
a) the Tobago Jig  b) ballet only  c) no other dance
**Answer: a) the Tobago Jig**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The performance is studied for its…
a) locomotor and axial movements and body shapes  b) costumes only  c) music volume
**Answer: a) locomotor and axial movements and body shapes**
