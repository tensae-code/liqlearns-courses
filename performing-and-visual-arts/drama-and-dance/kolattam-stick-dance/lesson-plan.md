# Lesson plan — Kolattam Stick Dance

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Drama and Dance · **Lesson:** Kolattam Stick Dance
**Place in the curriculum:** Drama and Dance (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Kolattam — the stick dance of Trinidad and Tobago's Indian heritage, performed for its locomotor and axial movements and its body shapes, alongside the Tobago Jig.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this course in the same title.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Kolattam, the stick dance** — Kolattam, the stick dance of the country's Indian heritage — dancers with sticks, in rhythm, in pairs and groups.
2. **Moving in Kolattam** — Kolattam performed for its locomotor and axial movements and its body shapes, alongside the Tobago Jig in the same form's work.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks Kolattam is a solo still dance → it is a stick dance performed with locomotor (travelling) and axial (on-the-spot) movement.
- Misses where it comes from → Kolattam is the Indian-heritage stick dance in Trinidad and Tobago's curriculum.
