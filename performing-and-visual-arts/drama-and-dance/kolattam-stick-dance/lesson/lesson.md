## Sub lesson 1 — Kolattam, the stick dance

**Kolattam** is the **stick dance of Trinidad and Tobago's Indian heritage**, named in Form 2 of the VAPA curriculum. Dancers hold sticks and strike them in rhythm as they dance.

## Sub lesson 2 — Moving in Kolattam

In the curriculum Kolattam is performed **for its locomotor and axial movements and its body shapes** — travelling movements, movements on the spot, and the shapes the body makes — and it is taught **alongside the Tobago Jig**, the two heritage dances in the same term's work.
