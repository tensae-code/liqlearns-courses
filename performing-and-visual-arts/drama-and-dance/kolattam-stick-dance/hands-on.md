# Hands-on — Kolattam Stick Dance

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Shapes and steps
Without sticks first: practise a travelling (locomotor) phrase and an on-the-spot (axial) phrase, finishing each in a clear body shape.

## Sticks in rhythm
With a partner, add the sticks: strike in rhythm on the beat while you repeat the phrases, safely and together.
