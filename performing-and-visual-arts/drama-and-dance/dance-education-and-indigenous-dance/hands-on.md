# Hands-on — Dance Education and Indigenous Dance

Real things to do with what this lesson teaches — at school or at home, with people around you.

## Purpose hunt
Learners recall dances they know and tag each with its purpose — celebrating, worshipping, socialising, healing… One dance can hold several tags.

## Watch, note, compare
The class watches the same short dance twice: first just watching, then noting under art / music / dance / nature-and-food. Compare the two rounds of notes.
