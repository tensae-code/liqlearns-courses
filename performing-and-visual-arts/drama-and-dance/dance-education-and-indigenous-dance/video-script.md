# Video script — Dance Education and Indigenous Dance

A short read-aloud script in a super-simple voice, made later as the lesson's video. It follows the lesson's own order and names only what the lesson teaches.

---

Dance lives in every human culture.
Dance education: performing, improvising, choreographing, critiquing, recording, viewing.
Dance serves: celebration, healing, worship, learning, fitness, communication.
Study a dance by watching it — note it, compare it. The Wolayta Seleme-Seleme circle is our example.
