# Dance Education and Indigenous Dance — short answer

Answer in your own words, in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name four purposes of dance.
**Answer: Any four of: performing, healing, entertaining, celebrating, socialising, learning, worshipping, fitness, communicating.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which dance is the guide's viewing example?
**Answer: The Wolayta Seleme-Seleme circle dance.**
