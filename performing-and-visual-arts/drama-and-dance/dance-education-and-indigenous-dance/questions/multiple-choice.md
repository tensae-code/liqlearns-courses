# Dance Education and Indigenous Dance — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Dance education includes —
a) only performing  b) performing, improvising, choreographing, critiquing, recording, viewing  c) only examinations
**Answer: b) performing, improvising, choreographing, critiquing, recording, viewing.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which is a purpose of dance in the guide's list?
a) healing  b) forgetting  c) competing only
**Answer: a) healing.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Dance is practised —
a) in a few cultures  b) in every human culture  c) only on stage
**Answer: b) in every human culture.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Early evidence of dance appears in —
a) Paleolithic rock paintings  b) medieval books  c) photographs
**Answer: a) Paleolithic rock paintings.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Ritual dances mimicked —
a) machines  b) animals and nature  c) letters
**Answer: b) animals and nature.**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** The observation method is —
a) watch once and forget  b) watch, note, compare  c) only compare prices
**Answer: b) watch, note, compare.**
