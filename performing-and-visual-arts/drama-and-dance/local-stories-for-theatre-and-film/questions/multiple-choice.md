# Local Stories for Theatre and Film — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Local stories pass down —
a) only entertainment  b) traditional and indigenous knowledge  c) nothing
**Answer: b) traditional and indigenous knowledge.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which form is named as material?
a) natural conservation  b) advertising  c) weather reports
**Answer: a) natural conservation.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** In the lesson's image, the knowledge is —
a) the vehicle  b) the passenger carried by the story  c) the road's toll
**Answer: b) the passenger carried by the story.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Intercultural theatre —
a) stages one culture only  b) mixes Ethiopia's cultures and subcultures in one production  c) avoids stories
**Answer: b) mixes Ethiopia's cultures and subcultures in one production.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Theatre serves cultural sustainability by —
a) connecting communities and keeping culture performed  b) freezing culture in museums only  c) replacing elders
**Answer: a) connecting communities and keeping culture performed.**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** In an intercultural production, each culture is —
a) honoured in its own manner  b) blended until invisible  c) ranked
**Answer: a) honoured in its own manner.**
