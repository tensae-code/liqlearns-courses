# Hands-on — Local Stories for Theatre and Film

Real things to do with what this lesson teaches — at school or at home, with people around you.

## Story chain
Each learner traces one local story's chain: who told it, who told them. The class sees the generations a story crosses without paper.

## One production, many cultures
Groups plan a short production that mixes two cultures of Ethiopia respectfully — a tale from one, a dance from another — and say what each contributes.
