# Video script — Local Stories for Theatre and Film

A short read-aloud script in a super-simple voice, made later as the lesson's video. It follows the lesson's own order and names only what the lesson teaches.

---

Local stories pass indigenous knowledge down the generations.
The forms: riddles, tales, natural conservation, conflict resolution.
Intercultural theatre mixes Ethiopia's cultures in one production.
Theatre keeps a society's culture alive — connecting its communities.
