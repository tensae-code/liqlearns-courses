# Dance Props: Cloth and Handkerchief

## Do it first

Name one thing that belongs in this lesson — Dancing with props — how cloth, handkerchiefs and other held things extend the dancer's movement and meaning. Say what it is in your own words. If you can name one, you are already inside the lesson.

## Sub lesson 1 — Unit 3

dance properties (properti) — the selendang cloth and handkerchief: holding techniques pictured step by step (cloth pinched between both hands, ends gripped and the rest spread long or short; the Gerbang team spreading the cloth wide overhead as a gate; the Pelintas team's handkerchiefs passed relay-style to the dancer behind), moving the property in time with the music, and the costume worn for the Slepdur game.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson holds, part by part, in your own words. Companion checks: did every part get named, with its Indonesian name kept where the book gives one?

## Do It Yourself

1. Choose one named part of this lesson and find out one more true thing about it — from a person who knows it, or from the book your part came from.
2. Make or perform a small piece in the way of that part: a rhythm, a step, a drawing, a shaped piece of clay.
3. Write one sentence: what does this part show about Indonesia's own way of teaching its arts?
