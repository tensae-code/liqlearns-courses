This is Indonesia's own lesson.

**Unit 3.** dance properties (properti) — the selendang cloth and handkerchief: holding techniques pictured step by step (cloth pinched between both hands, ends gripped and the rest spread long or short; the Gerbang team spreading the cloth wide overhead as a gate; the Pelintas team's handkerchiefs passed relay-style to the dancer behind), moving the property in time with the music, and the costume worn for the Slepdur game.

That is Dance Props: Cloth and Handkerchief — Indonesia's way, kept by name.
