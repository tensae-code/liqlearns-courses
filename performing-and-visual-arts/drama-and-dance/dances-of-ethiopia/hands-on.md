# Hands-on — Dances of Ethiopia

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Dance detective
When you next see a dance — at a festival, a wedding, on a screen — be a detective with the book's two lenses. What is the DRESS? What are the INSTRUMENTS? Write or draw both. Then say which place's dance it could be, and what clue told you.

## Your area's dance, taught by you
Choose the dance of your own area. Learn its dress and ONE basic step properly — ask a family member or elder to check you. Then teach that one step to your group, the way it was taught to you. Performing begins with receiving.
