# Video script — Dances of Ethiopia

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

One country — MANY dances!

**Afar** — white dress, curved daggers, before the tower.
**Somali** — sticks in hand — LEAP!
The **South** — its own way again.
**Tigray** — compare it: the dress? the instruments?

**Gojjam! Gondar** — hair in albaso style!
**Awi! Kemise! Shewa! Seqota! Wollo! Argoba! Raya** — with the drummer!

Look first. Name the difference.
Then — dance it with respect.
