# Dances of Ethiopia — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — Afar — and Somali and the South
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: name the Afar markers (white dress, curved daggers, the tower) and the Somali signature (sticks, leaping), and state a Somali–Southern difference.

## Sub lesson 2 — Tigray — and comparing dances
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: compare two dances by dress and by instruments, naming real differences.

## Sub lesson 3 — The Amhara region — Gojjam, Gondar, Awi, Kemise, Shewa
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: name Gojjam, Gondar, Awi, Kemise and Shewa, with one pictured detail for at least two of them.

## Sub lesson 4 — Seqota, Wollo, Argoba, Raya — and choosing one to perform
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: choose one appreciated dance and perform it in a group, and describe the dress of your own area's dance.
