# Dances of Ethiopia — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers: every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to.

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Afar dancers are pictured —
a) leaping with sticks  b) in white with curved daggers at the waist, before a tower  c) with hair in albaso style
**Answer: b).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The Somali dance's signature is —
a) dancing seated  b) leaping, with sticks  c) dancing with daggers
**Answer: b) leaping with sticks.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Dances are compared by —
a) dress and music instruments  b) the dancers' ages  c) the size of the town
**Answer: a)** — the book's own two lenses.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** The Gondar women dancers are pictured with —
a) sticks and daggers  b) white embroidered dress and albaso hairstyle  c) school uniforms
**Answer: b).**

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** The guide's way into a community dance is —
a) watch it, then join in simple form  b) read about it only  c) judge it first
**Answer: a) watch it, then join in simple form.**

**6. (Sub lesson 5)** A community dance belongs to —
a) the school only  b) the community — its celebrations and its people  c) visitors
**Answer: b) the community — its celebrations and its people.**

**7. (Sub lesson 5)** Joining in 'simple form' means —
a) performing the hardest version  b) stepping in simply, as a guest of the dance  c) leading at once
**Answer: b) stepping in simply, as a guest of the dance.**

**8. (Sub lesson 6)** The guide introduces dance as —
a) exercise only  b) a way of knowing and communicating  c) a contest
**Answer: b) a way of knowing and communicating.**

**9. (Sub lesson 6)** Every Ethiopian ethnic group has —
a) the same steps  b) its own unique steps and rhythm  c) no dance
**Answer: b) its own unique steps and rhythm.**

**10. (Sub lesson 6)** Folk dance symbolises Ethiopia's —
a) mosaic culture  b) single culture  c) cities only
**Answer: a) mosaic culture.**

**11. (Sub lesson 7)** Before writing existed, traditional dance was used to —
a) transmit words and histories generation to generation  b) decorate books  c) do nothing
**Answer: a) transmit words and histories generation to generation.**

**12. (Sub lesson 7)** Dance was also used, anciently and now, for —
a) healing and expressing feelings  b) building only  c) counting
**Answer: a) healing and expressing feelings.**

**13. (Sub lesson 7)** Ethiopia's dance kinds differ by —
a) nothing  b) dress, method, melody, materials, instruments, gender and time  c) price
**Answer: b) dress, method, melody, materials, instruments, gender and time.**
