# Dances of Ethiopia — true or false

Sub-lesson markers: every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to.

Say if it is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Ethiopia has only one traditional dance style.
**Answer: False** — the country has many dance styles, differing from place to place.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Kemise is the dance of the Oromo of Wollo.
**Answer: True** (as the book names it).

**3. (Sub lesson 4)** The Raya Kobo picture shows a group of women in traditional dress with a drummer.
**Answer: True.**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Learners only look at the dances; they never perform one.
**Answer: False** — they choose an appreciated dance and perform it in a group.
