# Dances of Ethiopia — short answer

Sub-lesson markers: every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to.

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Sub lesson 3)** List the Amhara region dances named across this lesson.
**Answer:** Gojjam, Gondar, Awi, Kemise, Shewa, Seqota, Wollo, Argoba and Raya (Wag Himra is also named in the region's list).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** State one difference between the Somali and Tigray dances.
**Answer:** a true difference in dress or in the music instruments used, from the pictures (the book's Activity 4).

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Which two things do you look at when you compare two dances?
**Answer:** the dress, and the music instruments used.
