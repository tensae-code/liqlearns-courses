# Bolivian Folk Dances — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — Dances of the west
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Caporales, with its athletic turns and bells; the Tinku, the ritual encounter-combat of Macha in Potosi, an offering to Pachamama; the Tobas; the Chutas of La Paz carnival; and the Balseros of Rurrenabaque.

## Sub lesson 2 — Dances of the plains and valleys
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: The Taquirari, dance of the arrow, and the Chobena of the Chiquitania; the Machetero of the Beni; and the valleys' cueca, pujllay, bailecito, huayno and chacarera.

## Sub lesson 3 — Dance as a written record
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: A people without writing recorded its truths in movement: every movement of the originary dances has a meaning — the dances are an instruction notebook that only needs interpreting; and the forms of folk dance music: primary, binary and ternary.
