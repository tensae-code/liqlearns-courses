# Video script — Bolivian Folk Dances

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This is Bolivia's own lesson.

**Dances of the west.** Caporales, with its athletic turns and bells; the Tinku, the ritual encounter-combat of Macha in Potosi, an offering to Pachamama; the Tobas; the Chutas of La Paz carnival; and the Balseros of Rurrenabaque.

**Dances of the plains and valleys.** The Taquirari, dance of the arrow, and the Chobena of the Chiquitania; the Machetero of the Beni; and the valleys' cueca, pujllay, bailecito, huayno and chacarera.

**Dance as a written record.** A people without writing recorded its truths in movement: every movement of the originary dances has a meaning — the dances are an instruction notebook that only needs interpreting; and the forms of folk dance music: primary, binary and ternary.

That is Bolivian Folk Dances — Bolivia's way, kept by name.
