# Bolivian Folk Dances — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer: Bolivia's folk dances by region — Caporales, Tinku and Tobas of the west and Taquirari and Chobena of the plains, with the Machetero of the Beni and the dances of the valleys.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the first sub lesson.
**Answer: Dances of the west.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name the last sub lesson.
**Answer: Dance as a written record.**
