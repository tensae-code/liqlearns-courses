# Lesson plan — Bulgarian Horo Dances

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Drama and Dance · **Lesson:** Bulgarian Horo Dances
**Place in the curriculum:** Drama and Dance (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Ръченица, Дунавско хоро, Пайдушко хоро, Граовски танц, Кюстендилско хоро (dances named, steps as the book teaches them)
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the learner's earlier work in this title — recommended first, never forced.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Topic 41: Български танцов фолклор (Bulgarian dance folklore) and Topic 42: Дайчово хоро, with the listening stock of horo dances** — Ръченица, Дунавско хоро, Пайдушко хоро, Граовски танц, Кюстендилско хоро (dances named, steps as the book teaches them)
2. **Topic 16: Право хоро (the Pravo horo** — Bulgaria's straight-line horo dance)
3. **Topic 8: Пайдушко хоро (the Paidushko horo** — the dance in uneven 5-beat metre)
