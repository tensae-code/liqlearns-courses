# Video script — Play: Every Craft Needs the Others

A short read-aloud script in a super-simple voice, made later as the lesson's video. It follows the lesson's own order and names only what the lesson teaches.

---

Four children argue.
Whose father's work is greatest — the carpenter's, or the weaver's?
Then they see it: the carpenter wears the weaver's cloth.
The weaver sits on the carpenter's chair.
Every craft needs the others. None is above another.
