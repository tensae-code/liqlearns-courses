# Play: Every Craft Needs the Others — short answer

Answer in your own words, in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the children's argument about?
**Answer: Whose father's craft matters most.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How does the carpenter need the weaver?
**Answer: He works wearing the clothes the weaver made (and the weaver sits on the carpenter's chair).**
