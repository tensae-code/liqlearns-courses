# Play: Every Craft Needs the Others — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The four children in the play are —
a) Aster, Halima, Kasa and Abdu  b) only two boys  c) the teacher and three pupils
**Answer: a) Aster, Halima, Kasa and Abdu.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The carpenter in the argument is praised for making —
a) clothes  b) chairs, tables and shelves  c) pots
**Answer: b) chairs, tables and shelves.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The weaver is praised for making —
a) clothes  b) chairs  c) houses
**Answer: a) clothes.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Who settles the argument?
a) the teacher  b) Abdu and Halima  c) a visitor
**Answer: b) Abdu and Halima.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The settling idea is —
a) one craft is the greatest  b) every craft needs the others  c) crafts do not matter
**Answer: b) every craft needs the others.**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** In the play's ending, the blacksmith, potter, plumber and builder are —
a) ranked best to worst  b) none above or below another — all support each other  c) not mentioned
**Answer: b) none above or below another — all support each other.**
