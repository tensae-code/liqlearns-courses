# Hands-on — Play: Every Craft Needs the Others

Real things to do with what this lesson teaches — at school or at home, with people around you.

## Tell the argument, settle the argument
In groups of four, tell the play's story in your own words — the argument first, then how Abdu and Halima settle it. Do not copy a script; the story is yours to tell.

## Crafts that need each other
Each learner names a craft from home or the market. The group finds, for each craft, one product of another craft that it cannot work without.
