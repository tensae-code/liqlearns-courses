# Hands-on — Papua New Guinean Plays and Playwrights

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## PNG plays and their playwrights
Do the practice this part names, with real materials and a partner: The Grade 7 guide studies Papua New Guinea plays and their playwrights, and the settings the plays were written from.

## Reviewing a PNG play
Do the practice this part names, with real materials and a partner: A PNG play and a female playwright are reviewed; a play's storyline, characters, dialogue, scenes and setting are discussed to form an opinion of it.

## Comparing two PNG plays
Do the practice this part names, with real materials and a partner: Two PNG plays are compared — the guide's named texts: Nora Vagi Brash's "Which Way Big Man?", and Russell Soaba's "Scattered by the Wind" and "Sail the Midnight Sun" — summaries only, never transcribed.
