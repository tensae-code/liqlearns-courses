# Papua New Guinean Plays and Playwrights

## Do it first

Look at the sub lesson list. Pick the part you already know something about, and say what you know. Then start at Sub lesson 1 — every part builds on the one before it.

## Sub lesson 1 — PNG plays and their playwrights

The Grade 7 guide studies Papua New Guinea plays and their playwrights, and the settings the plays were written from.

## Sub lesson 2 — Reviewing a PNG play

A PNG play and a female playwright are reviewed; a play's storyline, characters, dialogue, scenes and setting are discussed to form an opinion of it.

## Sub lesson 3 — Comparing two PNG plays

Two PNG plays are compared — the guide's named texts: Nora Vagi Brash's "Which Way Big Man?", and Russell Soaba's "Scattered by the Wind" and "Sail the Midnight Sun" — summaries only, never transcribed.
