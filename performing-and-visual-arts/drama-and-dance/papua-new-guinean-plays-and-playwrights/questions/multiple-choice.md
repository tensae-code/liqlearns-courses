# Papua New Guinean Plays and Playwrights — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** "Which Way Big Man?" is by…
a) Nora Vagi Brash  b) William Shakespeare
**Answer: a) Nora Vagi Brash.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Russell Soaba wrote…
a) "Scattered by the Wind" and "Sail the Midnight Sun"  b) Hamlet
**Answer: a) "Scattered by the Wind" and "Sail the Midnight Sun".**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A review discusses…
a) storyline, characters, dialogue, scenes and setting  b) only the cover
**Answer: a) storyline, characters, dialogue, scenes and setting.**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** PNG plays are studied with…
a) the settings they were written from  b) no context
**Answer: a) the settings they were written from.**
