# Video script — Papua New Guinean Plays and Playwrights

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

**PNG plays and their playwrights.** The Grade 7 guide studies Papua New Guinea plays and their playwrights, and the settings the plays were written from.

**Reviewing a PNG play.** A PNG play and a female playwright are reviewed; a play's storyline, characters, dialogue, scenes and setting are discussed to form an opinion of it.

**Comparing two PNG plays.** Two PNG plays are compared — the guide's named texts: Nora Vagi Brash's "Which Way Big Man?", and Russell Soaba's "Scattered by the Wind" and "Sail the Midnight Sun" — summaries only, never transcribed.
