# Video script — What Performing Art Does for a Community

A short read-aloud script in a super-simple voice, made later as the lesson's video. It follows the lesson's own order and names only what the lesson teaches.

---

A people's art values live with them for ages, handed down.
Performing art lightens heavy shared work — the debo, the farm.
It binds people at weddings, festivals and births.
It carries news and knowledge where letters do not reach.
And it grows children: body, mind, language, memory.
