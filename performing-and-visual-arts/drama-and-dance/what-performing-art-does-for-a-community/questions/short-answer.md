# What Performing Art Does for a Community — short answer

Answer in your own words, in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why is 'foundation' a strong word for art's place?
**Answer: Because a foundation holds the whole house up — art holds the community together, not merely decorates it.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name two things performing art passes down.
**Answer: Information/ideas and indigenous knowledge (generation to generation).**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name the three kinds of change the book names first.
**Answer: Physical, mental and behavioural change.**
