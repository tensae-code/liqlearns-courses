# What Performing Art Does for a Community — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The book calls art —
a) the roof of the community's house  b) the foundation of the community's house  c) a visitor to the house
**Answer: b) the foundation of the community's house.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Art values live with a people —
a) for one season  b) for ages, accumulated generation to generation  c) only in towns
**Answer: b) for ages, accumulated generation to generation.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Art's binding power brings —
a) tolerance and living together  b) competition only  c) silence
**Answer: a) tolerance and living together.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Debo is —
a) a solo task  b) collective work, lightened by traditional music  c) a dance contest
**Answer: b) collective work, lightened by traditional music.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** In unlettered communities, performing art was used to —
a) hide information  b) exchange information and pass it down generations  c) replace farming
**Answer: b) exchange information and pass it down generations.**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** At weddings and festivals, performing art —
a) separates people  b) develops relations and culture and shared feeling  c) is forbidden
**Answer: b) develops relations and culture and shared feeling.**

**7. (Sub lesson 3)** Performing art develops in children —
a) only singing skill  b) language and speech, and memory  c) nothing measurable
**Answer: b) language and speech, and memory.**

**8. (Sub lesson 3)** Learning through performing art is marked by —
a) fear  b) enjoyment — children learn while enjoying themselves  c) silence
**Answer: b) enjoyment — children learn while enjoying themselves.**

**9. (Sub lesson 3)** It grows children's love of —
a) only games  b) their history, culture, environment and family  c) foreign things only
**Answer: b) their history, culture, environment and family.**
