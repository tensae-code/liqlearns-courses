# What Performing Art Does for a Community — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Performing art is only decoration on community life. True or false?
**Answer: False — the book calls it the foundation.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Performing art can accompany film and theatre. True or false?
**Answer: True.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Performing art is a break from learning, not learning itself. True or false?
**Answer: False — for children it is learning, with enjoyment.**
