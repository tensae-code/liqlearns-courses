# Hands-on — What Performing Art Does for a Community

Real things to do with what this lesson teaches — at school or at home, with people around you.

## Work-song test
The class does a shared task — tidying, carrying, arranging — first in silence, then with a steady song. Compare: which way did the work feel lighter, and why?

## Celebration map
Groups map one celebration — wedding, Meskel, a birth — and mark every place performing art appears inside it.
