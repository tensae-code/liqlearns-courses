# Hands-on — Circle Dances and Traditional Steps

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The home circle
At home or in the yard, gather family or neighbours into a circle, hands held. One person claps a simple beat — slow. Turn together one full round, then back the other way. Trade the clapping job round the circle. The teaching is in the hands: nobody pulls, nobody drags; the circle carries everyone.

## Facing steps
With one partner, stand facing each other at arm's length. One steps a short pattern — forward, back, side, side — on a counted beat. The partner answers with the same pattern. Then the partner invents, and the first answers. Keep it slow until the rhythm holds by itself.
