# Video script — Circle Dances and Traditional Steps

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Join hands. Make the circle.
**እንሽከረከራለን!** — We go round in a circle!
Step — step — step — on the beat!
Sing while you turn!

Now face your friend.
Step — answer — step — answer.
The dance lives BETWEEN you.

All together now — the old song, the old steps.
Not invented. RECEIVED.
Dance it well — and pass it on.
