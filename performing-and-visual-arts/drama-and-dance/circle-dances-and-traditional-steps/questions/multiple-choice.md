# Circle Dances and Traditional Steps — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers: every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to.

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The circle dance song is called —
a) እንሽከረከራለን (Enshikerekeralen)  b) እንቅስቃሴ (Enqisqase)  c) ዳንስ (Dans)
**Answer: a) እንሽከረከራለን** — We Go Round in a Circle.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In the circle, the feet must —
a) land on the beat together  b) go as fast as possible  c) never move
**Answer: a)** — the beat is the boss; together, or the ring wobbles.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The pictured traditional dancers stand —
a) back to back  b) facing each other  c) in a straight line
**Answer: b) facing each other** — each answering the other's movement.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Traditional songs and dances in a group are performed —
a) any way at all  b) as the culture prescribes  c) silently
**Answer: b)** — the way they are done, received and passed on.
