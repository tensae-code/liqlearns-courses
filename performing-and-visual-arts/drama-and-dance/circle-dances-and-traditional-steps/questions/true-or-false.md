# Circle Dances and Traditional Steps — true or false

Sub-lesson markers: every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to.

Say if it is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In the circle dance, the dancers hold hands while turning.
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Traditional dance steps are invented fresh each time.
**Answer: False** — they are learned and passed on, done in rhythm.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Song and dance together carry the culture's performance.
**Answer: True.**
