# Circle Dances and Traditional Steps — short answer

Sub-lesson markers: every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to.

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What holds the turning circle together, besides the joined hands?
**Answer:** the simple beat — feet landing on it together.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Between which two people does the facing dance live?
**Answer:** the two partners facing each other, each answering the other's steps.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does 'as the culture prescribes' mean for a group performance?
**Answer:** perform the songs and dances the way they are done — received and passed on, not invented.
