# Moroccan Heritage Dances

## Do it first

Name a dance from your own place — one people dance at celebrations. Morocco's guide names five of its own. Meet them.

## Sub lesson 1 — The named dances

In its first term project, "Our Morocco: Civilisation and Culture", Morocco's Grade 4 guide names the country's **heritage dances**: **Ahidus, Ahwash, Guedra, Gnawa and Reggada** — Morocco's own named dances, carried as living heritage.

## Sub lesson 2 — Performing one

One project group **performs a Moroccan heritage dance, choosing from Ahidus, Ahwash, Guedra, Gnawa and Reggada** — the dance is not only named but danced, inside a project that also paints Morocco's heritage, sings for it and maps it.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the five dances. Companion checks: Ahidus? Ahwash? Guedra? Gnawa? Reggada?

## Do It Yourself

1. In a group, choose one named dance and find out — from family or community — when it is danced.
2. Practise a short, respectful excerpt or its basic rhythm and steps as your community teaches them.
3. Write one sentence: why does the project perform a dance instead of only naming it?
