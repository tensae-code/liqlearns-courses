# Moroccan Heritage Dances — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which list names Morocco's heritage dances from the guide?
a) Ahidus, Ahwash, Guedra, Gnawa and Reggada  b) waltz and tango only  c) ballet only
**Answer: a) Ahidus, Ahwash, Guedra, Gnawa and Reggada.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The dances are presented as…
a) living heritage  b) forgotten history  c) sports
**Answer: a) living heritage.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** In the project, a group…
a) only reads the names  b) chooses one heritage dance and performs it  c) watches a film only
**Answer: b) chooses one heritage dance and performs it.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The dance belongs to a term project called…
a) "Our Morocco: Civilisation and Culture"  b) "Numbers"  c) "The Sea"
**Answer: a) "Our Morocco: Civilisation and Culture".**

**5. (Sub lesson 1)** How many heritage dances does the guide name?
a) two  b) five  c) ten
**Answer: b) five.**
