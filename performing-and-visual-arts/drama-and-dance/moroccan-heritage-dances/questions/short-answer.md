# Moroccan Heritage Dances — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the five Moroccan heritage dances.
**Answer: Ahidus, Ahwash, Guedra, Gnawa and Reggada.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the project group do with the dance it chooses?
**Answer: It performs it, as living heritage, inside the term project.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Name two other things the same project does for Morocco.
**Answer: Any two of: paintings of Moroccan nature, civilisation and heritage for an exhibition; a song or theatre scene glorifying Morocco; a large map of Morocco decorated with postcards of its cities and monuments.**
