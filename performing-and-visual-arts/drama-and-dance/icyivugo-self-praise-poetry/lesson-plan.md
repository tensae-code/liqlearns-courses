# Lesson plan — Icyivugo: Rwandan Self-Praise Poetry

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Drama and Dance · **Lesson:** Icyivugo: Rwandan Self-Praise Poetry
**Place in the curriculum:** Drama and Dance (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Rwanda's icyivugo — the heroic self-praise poem: what ibyivugo are, how they are recited with face, gesture and dance, and how a learner composes one, step by step.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this course in the same title.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. ## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **What ibyivugo are** — An **icyivugo** (plural **ibyivugo**) is Rwanda's self-praise poem — a poem in which the speaker praises great deeds, their own or another's, in lines with their own structure and rhymes.
2. **Reciting ibyivugo on great themes** — The book recites ibyivugo theme by theme.
3. **Composing your own icyivugo** — Composing has clear steps: **think of a person who has done great things; write the story of what they did; change the story into lines following the model poem; check the words sit well together; read aloud to check the uniform rhythm.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
