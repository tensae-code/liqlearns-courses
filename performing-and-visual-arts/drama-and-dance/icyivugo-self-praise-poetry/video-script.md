# Video script — Icyivugo: Rwandan Self-Praise Poetry

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Ibyivugo! Rwanda's self-praise poems.

A hero speaks. He praises great deeds — in lines that march together.

Not a song — a poem, said by heart, with the face, the hands, the dance.

Now make your own: a person, their story, lines like the model —

check the words, read it aloud, feel the rhythm. That is composing an icyivugo.
