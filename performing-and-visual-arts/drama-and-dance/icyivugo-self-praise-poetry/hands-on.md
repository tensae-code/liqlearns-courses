# Hands-on — Icyivugo: Rwandan Self-Praise Poetry

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Recite and compose
In a group, recite a short icyivugo you have made yourselves — four lines praising a real good deed — with facial expressions, gestures and a dance step on the strong beats. Then swap: each group composes one more, following the book's steps — person, story, lines, check the words, read aloud for the rhythm.
