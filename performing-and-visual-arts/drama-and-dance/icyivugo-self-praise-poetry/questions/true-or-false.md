# Icyivugo: Rwandan Self-Praise Poetry — true or false

Say true or false.

**1.** The teacher's guide adds an icyivugo theme of inclusive education — every learner included.
**Answer: True.**

**2.** Ibyivugo are composed by copying a model poem word for word.
**Answer: False — the model's shape is followed, but the story and lines are the composer's own.**
