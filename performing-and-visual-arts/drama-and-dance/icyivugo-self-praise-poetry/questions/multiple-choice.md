# Icyivugo: Rwandan Self-Praise Poetry — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is an icyivugo?
a) Rwanda's self-praise poem, reciting great deeds  b) a drum rhythm  c) a woven basket pattern
**Answer: a) Rwanda's self-praise poem, reciting great deeds**

**2.** The first composing step is…
a) think of a person who has done great things  b) choose a drum  c) cut the fabric
**Answer: a) think of a person who has done great things**

**3.** An icyivugo is performed…
a) silently, sitting still  b) by heart, with facial expressions, gestures and dance movements  c) read from the book only
**Answer: b) by heart, with facial expressions, gestures and dance movements**

**4.** Gukora Cyane (Working Hard) praises…
a) wealth earned through hard work, cooperatives and learning  b) sleeping late  c) the rain
**Answer: a) wealth earned through hard work, cooperatives and learning**
