# Icyivugo: Rwandan Self-Praise Poetry — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1.** How does a composer check a new icyivugo before performing it?
**Answer: Read it aloud to check the words sit well together and the rhythm is uniform.**

**2.** How is an icyivugo different from an ordinary song?
**Answer: It is a self-praise poem with its own structure and rhymes, recited — not sung like an ordinary song.**
