# Merengue: Dance of the Dominican Republic

## Do it first

Quick start: Fast feet, turning skirts — the whole country is dancing. Then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — Merengue, the nation's treasure

**Merengue** is the Dominican Republic's **treasure** and part of its **national identity**: a **joyful, contagious rhythm of celebration**, with its **National Day on 26 November**. The Grade 3 book dances it at the **school dance festival** — *A ritmo de merengue*, at merengue's rhythm.

## Sub lesson 2 — The variants of merengue

Merengue is not one single thing. **Fradique Lizardo classified its eight variants**, told apart by **tambora strokes, instruments, character and region** — the **Cibaeño of the Cibao** among them. The Grade 7 book appreciates them, then dances them: **recognising rhythms, melodies, lyrics and interpreters**, and **dancing at least two variants** from videos.

## Sub lesson 3 — Merengue, heritage of humanity

At Grade 10 the class dances merengue under its world title: **Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity** — and **records the dance for the course blog**, where classmates leave positive comments. The same book researches **Dominican merengue as dance** into a **mural of images, press cuttings, drawings and texts** from different sources.
