# Hands-on — Merengue: Dance of the Dominican Republic

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Dance two variants
From videos, learn and dance two merengue variants, the way the Grade 7 book does — and say what differs between them.

## Merengue mural
Start the research mural: one image, one press cutting or drawing, and one short text about merengue as dance.
