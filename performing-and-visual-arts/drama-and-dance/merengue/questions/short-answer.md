# Merengue: Dance of the Dominican Republic — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How is merengue's rhythm described?
**Answer: Joyful and contagious — a rhythm of celebration.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name one region whose merengue is named in the book.
**Answer: The Cibao — its variant is the Cibaeño.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What did the Grade 10 class do with its dance?
**Answer: Recorded it for the course blog, where classmates leave positive comments.**
