# Merengue: Dance of the Dominican Republic — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Merengue is called the Dominican Republic's…
a) treasure and part of its national identity  b) newest import  c) school anthem
**Answer: a) treasure and part of its national identity**.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Merengue's National Day is…
a) 26 November  b) 1 January  c) 27 February
**Answer: a) 26 November**.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Fradique Lizardo classified merengue into…
a) eight variants  b) two variants  c) twenty variants
**Answer: a) eight variants**.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The variants are told apart by…
a) tambora strokes, instruments, character and region  b) price  c) costume colour only
**Answer: a) tambora strokes, instruments, character and region**.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** UNESCO names merengue…
a) Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity  b) a world wonder  c) an Olympic sport
**Answer: a) Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity**.
