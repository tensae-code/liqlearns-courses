# Merengue: Dance of the Dominican Republic — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Merengue is danced at the school dance festival.
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** All merengue is played exactly the same everywhere.
**Answer: False.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Merengue is Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity.
**Answer: True.**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Researching merengue can be gathered into a mural of images, press cuttings, drawings and texts.
**Answer: True.**
