# Hands-on — How Things Move

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The movement walk
Walk around your home or schoolyard with a friend. Every time you see something move, stop and copy its movement together: a person walking, a hen running, a dog trotting, a bird flying over. Say its name and its way: two legs, four legs, or wings.

## Three corners
Mark three corners of a room or yard: the TWO LEGS corner, the FOUR LEGS corner, the WINGS corner. A caller names a mover — child! hen! dog! cat! bird! — and everyone moves to its corner in that mover's own way.
