# Video script — How Things Move

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Walk. Walk.
Now run — softly, safely.
Two legs! That is you.

The hen — **ትንሽ ዶሮ** — two legs too. Walk, run.
The dog — four legs. Fast, fast!
The cat — four legs. Fast, fast!

Now the bird. The bird is different.
Arms wide — like wings. Up. Down. Fly!

Two legs walk and run.
Four legs run fast.
Wings fly.
Show me all three!
