# How Things Move — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers: every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to.

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The hen moves on —
a) four legs  b) two legs  c) wings
**Answer: b) two legs** — the song puts the child and the hen together on two legs.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which two movers run fast on four legs?
a) the dog and the cat  b) the hen and the bird  c) the child and the hen
**Answer: a) the dog and the cat.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The bird is different because it —
a) runs fastest  b) flies with spread wings  c) walks on four legs
**Answer: b) flies with spread wings.**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** When you run, your feet —
a) never leave the ground  b) both leave the ground for a moment  c) stay still
**Answer: b)** — that is what makes running different from walking.
