# Lesson plan — How Things Move

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Drama and Dance · **Lesson:** How Things Move
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 4 — Drama and Dance (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** things move in different ways — on two legs, on four legs, and on wings — and you can watch the way, name it, and copy it with your own body.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the child's own world — the moving things they have already seen at home and on the road to school. It needs nothing else.

exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees
from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before
it is proved:

1. **Moving on two legs** — the song እንቅስቃሴ (Enqisqase — Movement): the child and the hen walk and run on two legs.
2. **Moving on four legs** — the dog and the cat run fast on four legs.
3. **Moving on wings** — the bird is different — it flies with spread wings.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: nothing. This is the first movement lesson; it starts from the child's own body. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name the three ways of moving in the song: two legs, four legs, wings.
2. Show walking and running on two legs.
3. Show a four-legged run, safely, on hands and knees.
4. Show flying with arms spread as wings.
5. Say which mover — child, hen, dog, cat, bird — moves in which way.

## What you need
A safe open space to move in. Nothing else.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — the learner walks, runs softly, and pretends to fly before any explaining (lesson/lesson.md).
2. **The explanation** — the song እንቅስቃሴ (Enqisqase — Movement) sorts the movers: two legs (child, hen), four legs (dog, cat), and the bird that flies (lesson/lesson.md).
   - ADD IMAGE [Picture 9700]: a picture strip — a child walking, a hen running, a dog running, a bird flying — the student points to each mover and says how it moves.
   - ADD GIF [GIF 9759]: the four movements in sequence — walk, run on two legs, run on four legs, fly — the student copies each movement as it plays.
   - ADD AUDIO: a voice singing the movement song እንቅስቃሴ (Enqisqase) — the student moves along with the verse that names their mover.
3. **Say it to your companion** — name the three ways of moving and show each one while your companion guesses the mover.
4. **Questions** — from questions/, served at the learner's level (levels.md).
5. **Hands-on** — hands-on.md.
6. **Do It Yourself** — watch three moving things at home and show the class how each one moves.

## If the learner is stuck
- Mixes up the hen and the dog → count the legs together, out loud: the hen stands on two thin legs; the dog stands on four.
- Runs unsafely → slow the run down to a fast walk; the lesson is the WAY of moving, not the speed.
- This lesson's next door: How Animals and People Move — the same watching, closer, mover by mover.
