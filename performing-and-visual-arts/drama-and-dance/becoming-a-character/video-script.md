This lesson is Becoming a Character: Imagination, Concentration and Emotion Memory.

Attention and concentration are qualities of the successful actor — necessary for the actor's work in theatre, they make the audience interact with the play, and they help the actor memorise and recall the role.

Watch, listen — then say it back in your own words.
