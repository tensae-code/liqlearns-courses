# Lesson plan — Becoming a Character: Imagination, Concentration and Emotion Memory

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Drama and Dance · **Lesson:** Becoming a Character: Imagination, Concentration and Emotion Memory
**Place in the curriculum:** Drama and Dance (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The actor's inner training — imagination, attention and concentration, emotion memory and communication, in the Stanislavski line of becoming a character.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this course in the same title.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. ## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Attention and Concentration** — attention and concentration are qualities of the successful actor — necessary for the actor's work in theatre, they make the audience interact with the play, and they help the actor memorise and recall the role
2. **Employing Imagination in Theatre** — imagining some characters and roles, human and animal, with movements and sounds; imagination is a mental activity in which the person pictures the existence of things not present in reality; among imagination's benefits — representing characters and roles and imagining their behaviour and feelings by voice and movement; and pieces of set, costumes and music are things that help the actor imagine the character in the play
3. **Imagining Characters and Roles in Theatre** — the actor's representing of characters and roles in the play — imagination helps the actor represent the character in the play, and imagination determines for the actor the clothes of the character he represents and her tools: reading the firefighter's full kit (helmet, jacket, boots, gloves, axe, hose) and the tool sets of the doctor, gardener, tailor, carpenter, rider, fisherman and blacksmith to name each character
