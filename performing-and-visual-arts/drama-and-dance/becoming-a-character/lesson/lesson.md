# Becoming a Character: Imagination, Concentration and Emotion Memory

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about becoming a character: imagination, concentration and emotion memory. Then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — Attention and Concentration

Attention and concentration are qualities of the successful actor — necessary for the actor's work in theatre, they make the audience interact with the play, and they help the actor memorise and recall the role.

## Sub lesson 2 — Employing Imagination in Theatre

Imagining some characters and roles, human and animal, with movements and sounds; imagination is a mental activity in which the person pictures the existence of things not present in reality; among imagination's benefits — representing characters and roles and imagining their behaviour and feelings by voice and movement; and pieces of set, costumes and music are things that help the actor imagine the character in the play.

## Sub lesson 3 — Imagining Characters and Roles in Theatre

The actor's representing of characters and roles in the play — imagination helps the actor represent the character in the play, and imagination determines for the actor the clothes of the character he represents and her tools: reading the firefighter's full kit (helmet, jacket, boots, gloves, axe, hose) and the tool sets of the doctor, gardener, tailor, carpenter, rider, fisherman and blacksmith to name each character.
