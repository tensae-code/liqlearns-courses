# How Animals and People Move — true or false

Sub-lesson markers: every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to.

Say if it is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Everyone in a family walks in exactly the same way.
**Answer: False** — each person has their own gait.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A donkey in the pictures stands still and strong.
**Answer: True.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The cow's calf trots beside her as she walks slowly.
**Answer: True.**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** A kid goat moves by galloping like a horse.
**Answer: False** — the kid goat skips and hops.
