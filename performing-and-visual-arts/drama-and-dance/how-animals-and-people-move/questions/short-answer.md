# How Animals and People Move — short answer

Sub-lesson markers: every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to.

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is a person's own way of walking called?
**Answer:** their gait.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which pictured animal walks with its head bobbing at each step?
**Answer:** the hen.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name the six animals in the practice picture grid.
**Answer:** a bird, a kid goat, a sheep, a hen, a donkey, and a cow with her calf.

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Give one plant movement and one animal movement you could turn into a dance.
**Answer:** Any true pair — e.g. a tree swaying; a bird hopping and flapping.
