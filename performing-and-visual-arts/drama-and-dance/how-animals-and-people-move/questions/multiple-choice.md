# How Animals and People Move — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers: every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to.

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In the family walking picture, the small child's steps are —
a) the longest  b) quick and short  c) not moving
**Answer: b) quick and short** — the small child almost runs to keep up.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A horse's fast movement is called —
a) a prowl  b) a gallop  c) a bob
**Answer: b) a gallop** — all four legs leave the ground together.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The cat walks —
a) loudly and high  b) softly and low  c) backwards
**Answer: b) softly and low** — a quiet prowl, each foot placed with care.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** A bird's take-off starts with —
a) singing  b) a crouch and a push with its legs  c) closing its wings
**Answer: b)** — crouch, push, spread the wings, beat, and lift.
