# How Animals and People Move — ordering

Sub-lesson markers: every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to.

Put the steps in the right order. The answer gives the right order.

**1. (Sub lesson 3)** A bird takes off: (a) beat the wings hard (b) crouch down (c) lift into the air (d) push with the legs (e) spread the wings wide
**Answer: b, d, e, a, c** — crouch, push, spread, beat, lift.
