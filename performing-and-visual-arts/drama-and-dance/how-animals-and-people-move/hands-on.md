# Hands-on — How Animals and People Move

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Watch for one minute
Choose one animal at home — a hen, a goat, a cat, a donkey. Watch it for one whole minute without speaking. Then show its movement to your family. They guess which animal you watched.

## The family walk
Walk somewhere real with your family — to the water, to the shop, to church. Hold hands in a line for part of the way. Notice: who takes long steps? Who takes quick steps? Who sets the speed so nobody is left behind?
