# Video script — How Animals and People Move

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Walk with your family.
Hold hands. Long steps — father.
Steady steps — mother.
Quick, short steps — the little one!

Now the horse. Gallop! All four legs — up together!
The hen walks. Bob, bob, bob.
The cat prowls. Soft. Quiet. Low.
The donkey stands. Still and strong.

And the bird — crouch… push… wings wide… beat, beat… UP!
You did them all.
