# Lesson plan — Interactive Theatre

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Drama and Dance · **Lesson:** Interactive Theatre
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 4 — Drama and Dance (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** interactive theatre — a performance the audience takes part in: what it is, how it differs from traditional theatre, how an issue is proposed for it, how opinions are spoken freely inside it, and how dialogue is improvised from situations with classmates — as Egypt's Grade 7 Theatre Education student book teaches it, ending in the class's interactive performance "Rights and Duties".
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the theatre lessons — the learner already knows what theatre is and how a play is written and put on.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **What interactive theatre is** — a performance the audience joins: how it differs from traditional theatre, proposing an issue for it, speaking your opinion freely inside it, and improvising dialogue from situations with classmates.
2. **An interactive performance — "Rights and Duties"** — taking part in an interactive theatre performance on the theme of rights and duties.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: What Theatre Is; Writing and Putting On a Play. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say what interactive theatre is and how it differs from traditional theatre.
2. Propose an issue for interactive theatre, speak an opinion freely inside it, and improvise dialogue from a situation with classmates.
3. Take part in an interactive performance on rights and duties.

## What you need
Classmates to perform and answer with, and an issue you care about.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — a scene stops in the middle: what should happen next? Say it — you have just done what an interactive-theatre audience does (lesson/lesson.md).
2. **The explanation** — what interactive theatre is first, then the "Rights and Duties" performance (lesson/lesson.md).
   - ADD IMAGE: a picture of performers turning to their audience in the middle of a scene and inviting an answer — the student says what the audience is being invited to do.
   - ADD GIF: a short scene pausing while an audience member speaks and a performer answers in role — the student says whose turn it is at each moment.
3. **Say it to your companion** — explain how interactive theatre differs from traditional theatre (lesson/lesson.md).
   - ADD VIDEO: the script in video-script.md, filmed — made later, when videos are made.
4. **Questions** — from questions/: multiple-choice, true-or-false, short-answer (levels.md).
5. **Hands-on** — hands-on.md.
6. **Do It Yourself** — propose your own issue and improvise its first exchange (lesson/lesson.md).

## Add-ons for this lesson (the full list)

- GIF (described only) — a scene pausing for an audience voice, answered in role → the student tracks whose turn it is.
- VIDEO — the video-script filmed (made later) → the student listens and answers.

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks the audience must always sit silent → in interactive theatre the audience takes part: it proposes, answers and helps shape what happens.
- Thinks improvising means saying anything → the dialogue is improvised from the situation, inside the issue the performance is about.
