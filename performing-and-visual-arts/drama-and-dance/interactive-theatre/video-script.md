# Video script — Interactive Theatre

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

In ordinary theatre, you watch. The play happens in front of you.

In **interactive theatre**, you join in.
The scene stops. A question is asked. **You** speak — your own opinion, freely.
The performers answer you, in role. New dialogue is born, right there, from the situation.

Our class performance is called **"Rights and Duties"**.
What are your rights? What are your duties? Come and say — inside the play.

That is interactive theatre: the audience inside the performance.
