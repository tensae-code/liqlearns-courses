# Interactive Theatre — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the three working parts of an interactive performance.
**Answer: An issue proposed, opinions spoken freely inside it, and dialogue improvised from situations with classmates.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What may a participant do with their opinion in interactive theatre?
**Answer: Speak it freely, inside the performance.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Why do rights and duties fit interactive theatre well?
**Answer: Because everybody in the room holds both — a right they expect others to respect and a duty they owe in return — so everyone has something to say inside the performance.**
