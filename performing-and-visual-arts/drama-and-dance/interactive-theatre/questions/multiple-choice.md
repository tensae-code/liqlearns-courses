# Interactive Theatre — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Interactive theatre is theatre in which…
a) the audience only watches in silence  b) the audience takes part in the performance  c) there are no performers
**Answer: b) the audience takes part in the performance.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** How does interactive theatre differ from traditional theatre?
a) It has no issue  b) Its audience enters the work instead of only receiving a finished performance  c) It is always silent
**Answer: b) Its audience enters the work instead of only receiving a finished performance.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** In interactive theatre, dialogue is…
a) memorised word for word only  b) forbidden  c) improvised from situations, with classmates
**Answer: c) improvised from situations, with classmates.**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** What is proposed at the start of an interactive performance?
a) An issue to discuss and play  b) A ticket price  c) A costume
**Answer: a) An issue to discuss and play.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The class's interactive performance in Egypt's Grade 7 book is on the theme of…
a) saving water  b) rights and duties  c) a sports festival
**Answer: b) rights and duties.**
