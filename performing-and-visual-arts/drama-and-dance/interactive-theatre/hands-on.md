# Hands-on — Interactive Theatre

The lesson with your voice and your classmates, not only your eyes.

## Propose and improvise
In a group, propose one issue for interactive theatre — a disagreement at school, a queue that is not fair, a duty nobody wants. Two classmates start a short scene on it. At the tensest moment, freeze. The rest of the group speaks opinions freely, one at a time, and the performers answer in role, improvising the dialogue from the situation. Then unfreeze and play the ending your discussion found.

## Rights and duties round
Sit in a circle. One learner names a right; the next names the duty that goes with it; the next acts both in two sentences. Keep the circle going until every learner has spoken — speaking your opinion freely is the skill this lesson practises.
