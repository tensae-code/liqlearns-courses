This lesson is Chinese Han Folk Dance.

The folk dances of China's Han people, their regional styles, fans, ribbons and steps. — as China's school books teach it: Unit 2 中国汉族民间舞： Han Chinese folk dance — Shandong 鼓子秧歌 and Yunnan 花灯 basic combinations practised step by step; yangge and the role of props in traditional folk dance; 红绸舞 and 黄土黄 appreciated.

Watch, listen — then say it back in your own words.
