# Hands-on — Karagkiozis: Greek Shadow Theatre

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the lesson's thing
Work through the lesson's first part yourself: listening guide, Chapter 4: excerpts from a Karagkiozis shadow-theatre performance — the characters' voices (Καραγκιόζης, Κολλητήρι, Μπαρμπαγιώργος and the rest), from the museum kit "The musics of Greece through the Shadow Theatre". Show a companion what you made or did, and say what the book calls it.

## Show and tell
Each learner shows their work and names the part of the lesson it comes from. The companion checks the name against the lesson.
