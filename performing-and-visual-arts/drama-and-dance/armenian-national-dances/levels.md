# Armenian National Dances — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — "Shurjpar" (Շուրջպար, Altunyan) heard
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: "Shurjpar" (Շուրջպար, Altunyan) heard — the round dance played by an ensemble of national instruments

## Sub lesson 2 — How is the national dance created
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: How is the national dance created — Armenian national dances are danced in a circle or half-circle, hand in hand; learn the song "Lorik" and join it to the GOVND dance

## Sub lesson 3 — Do all nations have folk dances
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Do all nations have folk dances — the world's dances differ greatly; counting in three for the Polish MAZUREK: hand down on "one", right on "two", back on "three" — a folk dance that became a court dance

## Sub lesson 4 — Govnd and Ververi were danced in Armenian life for long stretches
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Govnd and Ververi were danced in Armenian life for long stretches — developed in variants for even hours at a time, the whole circle taking part

## Sub lesson 5 — The TAMZARA dance-song
Sub lesson 5 questions from the type files. Pass mark: The TAMZARA dance-song — also very widespread: find its refrain and game-verses, perform the song with the Ververi dance, reproduce the Tamzara rhythm

## Sub lesson 6 — Dance in daily life had ritual meaning
Sub lesson 6 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Dance in daily life had ritual meaning — at feasts of the pagan gods dance was an important part; the dance rhythm is flexible, the steps bold and sharp, with great knee-bends
