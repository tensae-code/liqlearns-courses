## Sub lesson 1 — "Shurjpar" (Շուրջպար, Altunyan) heard

"Shurjpar" (Շուրջպար, Altunyan) heard — the round dance played by an ensemble of national instruments.

## Sub lesson 2 — How is the national dance created

How is the national dance created — Armenian national dances are danced in a circle or half-circle, hand in hand; learn the song "Lorik" and join it to the GOVND dance.

## Sub lesson 3 — Do all nations have folk dances

Do all nations have folk dances — the world's dances differ greatly; counting in three for the Polish MAZUREK: hand down on "one", right on "two", back on "three" — a folk dance that became a court dance.

## Sub lesson 4 — Govnd and Ververi were danced in Armenian life for long stretches

Govnd and Ververi were danced in Armenian life for long stretches — developed in variants for even hours at a time, the whole circle taking part.

## Sub lesson 5 — The TAMZARA dance-song

The TAMZARA dance-song — also very widespread: find its refrain and game-verses, perform the song with the Ververi dance, reproduce the Tamzara rhythm.

## Sub lesson 6 — Dance in daily life had ritual meaning

Dance in daily life had ritual meaning — at feasts of the pagan gods dance was an important part; the dance rhythm is flexible, the steps bold and sharp, with great knee-bends.
