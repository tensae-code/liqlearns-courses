# Video script — Armenian National Dances

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This is Armenia's own lesson.

**"Shurjpar" (Շուրջպար, Altunyan) heard.** "Shurjpar" (Շուրջպար, Altunyan) heard — the round dance played by an ensemble of national instruments.

**How is the national dance created.** How is the national dance created — Armenian national dances are danced in a circle or half-circle, hand in hand; learn the song "Lorik" and join it to the GOVND dance.

**Do all nations have folk dances.** Do all nations have folk dances — the world's dances differ greatly; counting in three for the Polish MAZUREK: hand down on "one", right on "two", back on "three" — a folk dance that became a court dance.

**Govnd and Ververi were danced in Armenian life for long stretches.** Govnd and Ververi were danced in Armenian life for long stretches — developed in variants for even hours at a time, the whole circle taking part.

**The TAMZARA dance-song.** The TAMZARA dance-song — also very widespread: find its refrain and game-verses, perform the song with the Ververi dance, reproduce the Tamzara rhythm.

**Dance in daily life had ritual meaning.** Dance in daily life had ritual meaning — at feasts of the pagan gods dance was an important part; the dance rhythm is flexible, the steps bold and sharp, with great knee-bends.

That is Armenian National Dances — Armenia's way, kept by name.
