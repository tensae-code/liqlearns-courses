# Lesson plan — Armenian National Dances

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Drama and Dance · **Lesson:** Armenian National Dances
**Place in the curriculum:** Drama and Dance (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Armenian National Dances — Armenia's own teaching of this topic, kept as named parts: "Shurjpar" (Շուրջպար, Altunyan) heard — the round dance played by an ensemble of national instruments
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the course's earlier lessons in its title; recommended first, never forced.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **"Shurjpar" (Շուրջպար, Altunyan) heard** — "Shurjpar" (Շուրջպար, Altunyan) heard — the round dance played by an ensemble of national instruments
2. **How is the national dance created** — How is the national dance created — Armenian national dances are danced in a circle or half-circle, hand in hand; learn the song "Lorik" and join it to the GOVND dance
3. **Do all nations have folk dances** — Do all nations have folk dances — the world's dances differ greatly; counting in three for the Polish MAZUREK: hand down on "one", right on "two", back on "three" — a folk dance that became a court dance
4. **Govnd and Ververi were danced in Armenian life for long stretches** — Govnd and Ververi were danced in Armenian life for long stretches — developed in variants for even hours at a time, the whole circle taking part
5. **The TAMZARA dance-song** — The TAMZARA dance-song — also very widespread: find its refrain and game-verses, perform the song with the Ververi dance, reproduce the Tamzara rhythm
6. **Dance in daily life had ritual meaning** — Dance in daily life had ritual meaning — at feasts of the pagan gods dance was an important part; the dance rhythm is flexible, the steps bold and sharp, with great knee-bends
