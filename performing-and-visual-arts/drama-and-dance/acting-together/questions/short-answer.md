# Acting Together — short answer

Sub-lesson markers: every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to.

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** List the four simple ways of making drama.
**Answer:** play a character in a real or imagined situation; take an idea from a story and play the character; play a scene before or after a story; place the character inside the scene/event.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Name three things drama builds in the actor.
**Answer:** self-confidence, speaking skill, and growth of body and mind.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** In the book's group picture, how many children act together, and what is one of them doing?
**Answer:** three children; one kneels with arms raised, asking/appealing, and two respond.
