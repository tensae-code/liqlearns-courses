# Acting Together — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers: every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to.

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Group acting works when the actors —
a) all speak at once  b) listen and answer each other  c) never look at each other
**Answer: b)** — a group play is a conversation of bodies and voices.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Playing a scene that happens BEFORE a story teaches —
a) cause and effect  b) singing  c) drawing
**Answer: a)** — things happen because of other things.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** How many simple ways of making drama does the book give?
a) two  b) four  c) ten
**Answer: b) four** — a character in a situation; an idea from a story; a before/after scene; the character inside the event.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** A drama passes its message —
a) only sadly  b) only funnily  c) in a funny way or a sad way
**Answer: c)** — the group chooses on purpose.
