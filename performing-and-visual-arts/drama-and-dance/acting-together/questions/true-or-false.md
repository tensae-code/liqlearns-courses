# Acting Together — true or false

Sub-lesson markers: every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to.

Say if it is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In group acting, when one actor speaks, the others give them room.
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Drama builds self-confidence and speaking skill.
**Answer: True** — and it grows body and mind together.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Drama can only be made from new stories, never from books.
**Answer: False** — the book says: take short dramas from books you find, rehearse, and show your class.
