# Acting Together — writing

Sub-lesson markers: every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to.

Write the lines asked for.

**1. (Sub lesson 3)** Write your group's message in one line, and say: funny or sad?
**Answer:** one clear message line, with the group's chosen way marked.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Write a before-scene in three short lines for a story you know: who is in it, where, and what happens.
**Answer:** a true before-scene — characters, place, and one happening that leads into the story.
