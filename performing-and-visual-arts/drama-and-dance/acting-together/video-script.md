# Video script — Acting Together

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

One actor — one person.
Three actors — a whole story!
Listen to your friends. Answer them.

Take a story. Play its people.
Play what happened BEFORE it.
Play what happened AFTER it.
Why did it happen? Now we see!

And ask first: what is our message?
Funny — or sad?
Act it… and the message flies to the watchers.
