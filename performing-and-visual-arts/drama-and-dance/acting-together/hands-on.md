# Hands-on — Acting Together

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The before-and-after game
Tell a story everyone knows — or use one from the Stories and Folktales lesson. In a group, act only the BEFORE scene: what happened the day before the story starts? Then act only the AFTER scene. Watchers say what the story itself must have been.

## A message in one minute
Choose a message your street needs — keep the water clean; help elders cross; do not litter. Build a one-minute funny drama that carries it. Show it to your family. Ask them afterwards: what was our message? If they can say it, the drama worked.
