# Acting Together

## Do it first

With two or three friends, act this without any words first: one of you is lost, the others help them find the way home. Did the watchers understand? You just acted together — and passed a message. This lesson teaches how groups do that on purpose.

## Sub lesson 1 — Acting together in a group

One actor alone can show one person. A **group** can show a whole happening: three children acting together — one kneeling, arms raised, asking; two answering — already make a small play.

Group acting needs listening. Watch your partners. Answer their movements with yours. When one speaks, the others give them the room; when you speak, take it bravely. A group play is a conversation of bodies and voices.

## Sub lesson 2 — Simple ways of making drama

Drama is acting a written character by becoming it. And there are simple, strong ways to build it:

1. **Play a character in a real or imagined situation** — put the person somewhere and let them act.
2. **Take an idea from a story** and play its character.
3. **Play a scene that happens BEFORE or AFTER a story** — what led to it? What came next? This teaches cause and effect: things happen because of other things.
4. **Place the character inside the event** so the watchers learn the content deeply.

Take short dramas from books you find. Rehearse them. Show your class.

## Sub lesson 3 — Passing a message by drama

A drama is an art where different characters, taken from a story, become the story's owners and perform it for an audience.

Drama **passes a message** — in a funny way or in a sad way. A funny drama can teach without scolding. A sad drama can make watchers feel and remember.

Acting and passing messages grow good things in the actors: it builds **self-confidence**, it develops **speaking skill**, and it grows **body and mind** together.

So before your group acts, agree: what is our message? Funny or sad? Then every scene must carry it.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the four ways of making drama, and the message your group's last scene carried..
