# Theatre and the Five Senses

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about theatre and the five senses. Then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — Theatre and Our Senses of Sight and Hearing

Sight and hearing help us understand a play — we watch the actors with our eyes and listen to their voices with our ears, and so tell shapes, sizes and colours in the play.

## Sub lesson 2 — Theatre and the Senses (Smell, Touch and Taste)

The other senses help actors present their characters — smell tells pleasant and bad smells, touch tells things rough, smooth, cold and hot (a potter at his wheel pictured), and taste tells sweet and bitter.

## Sub lesson 3 — I Listen and Remember

Distinguishing between sounds by way of memory; listening-and-remembering is the ability to recall the sounds — of human, animal and inanimate things — that I heard in my life, and to reproduce them; recalling sounds and reproducing them helps the actor recall the situations, feelings and emotions of the character he represents; imitating every pictured sound (children, a lion, a cat, an owl) once in a sad voice and once in an angry voice.

## Sub lesson 4 — I See and Remember

Distinguishing between the characters and their movements in the play by way of memory; seeing-and-remembering is the ability to recall the images, movements and feelings stored in the memory; memory helps the actor recall the movements and behaviour of the character he represents in the play and remember them — matching a rider's stored kit (saddle, helmet, horseshoe, whip, boots) from memory.

## Sub lesson 5 — I Feel and Remember

Distinguishing between the characters' emotions and their feelings in the play by way of memory; feeling-and-remembering is the ability to recall past events and the feelings and emotions I went through while those events happened — memory helps the actor recall the feelings of the character he represents in the play and her emotions; whether the memory holds negative feelings and positive ones, and naming the feelings in the pictures (pain, fear, cold and shivering, joy, sadness)
