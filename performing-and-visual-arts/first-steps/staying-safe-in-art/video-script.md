# Video script — Staying Safe in Art

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Art is making — and making has rules that keep us whole.

Coat on. Gloves on. Mask and goggles when the material flies or smells strong.

Sharp tools? Slow hands. Heat and hot glue? The teacher helps.

Strong smells? Open air. Finished? Tidy the place, wash your hands.

At home, a parent watches. That is staying safe in art.
