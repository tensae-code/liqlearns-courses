# Hands-on — Staying Safe in Art

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Work the lesson's own way

With a companion, walk the safety page as a drill: name the thing you would wear or do for (1) a strong-smelling colour, (2) a sharp cutting tool, (3) hot glue, (4) the end of the work. Then set up a workspace the safe way and show your companion what you changed.
