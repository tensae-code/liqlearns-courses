# Sharing and Exhibiting Our Art — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How often does the Surat book set this exhibition task?
**Answer: At the close of every term — it is the standing task of the term-closing lessons (printed pp. 23, 40, 60, 76).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Why exhibit drawings at all?
**Answer: So the work is shown, looked at and talked about — sharing completes the making.**
