# Sharing and Exhibiting Our Art — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The best drawings are placed in an exhibition.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The exhibition task is set only once, at the year's end.
**Answer: False** — it stands at every term's close.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The review lessons' questions are part of this exhibition task.
**Answer: False** — they are the book's apparatus, named in the book entry, not placed here.
