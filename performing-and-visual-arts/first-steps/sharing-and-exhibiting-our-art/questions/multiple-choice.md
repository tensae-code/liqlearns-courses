# Sharing and Exhibiting Our Art — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The standing task at each term's close is to…
a) hide your drawings  b) draw on a term theme and exhibit the best drawings  c) copy the teacher
**Answer: b) draw on a term theme and exhibit the best drawings**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A sergi is…
a) a song  b) an exhibition of drawings  c) a pencil
**Answer: b) an exhibition of drawings**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Which drawings go into the exhibition?
a) the best drawings  b) unfinished scraps  c) none
**Answer: a) the best drawings**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Your exhibition picture is drawn on…
a) any theme from outside school  b) one of the term's themes  c) no theme
**Answer: b) one of the term's themes**
