# Sharing and Exhibiting Our Art

## Do it first

Say what you already know about this lesson's subject — one thing you have seen, heard or done — before you read on.

## Sub lesson 1 — The term exhibition

Every term of the Surat book closes the same way — with a **standing task**:

1. **Draw a picture on one of the term's themes** — your choice of the term's work, drawn as your best.
2. **Place the best drawings in an exhibition — a sergi**.

An exhibition is how drawings meet their viewers: the work is finished when it is shown, looked at, and talked about. The review lessons' questions are the book's own apparatus and are not part of this task; the exhibition is.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson taught, part by part. Companion checks each part against the sub lessons above.

## Do It Yourself

1. Make your own piece using this lesson's way, from start to finish.
2. Show it to the class and say what you made, how, and what you learnt.
3. Write one sentence: what will you do differently next time?
