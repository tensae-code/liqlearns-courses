# Hands-on — Museums and Galleries

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Curate a corner
Choose three objects from your classroom or home that are worth keeping. Arrange them as an exhibition, and write a small label for each: what it is and why it is kept.

## Store room game
One player names a museum object. If it is 'exhibited', hold it up for all to see; if it is 'stored', hide it carefully away. Swap roles.
