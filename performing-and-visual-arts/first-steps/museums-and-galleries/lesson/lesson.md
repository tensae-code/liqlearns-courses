## Sub lesson 1 — What a museum is and does

**Museums are institutions that collect, keep, study and exhibit valuable objects and artworks.** That is four jobs in one house: gathering precious things, guarding them, learning about them, and showing them. The Grade 2 book pictures two of Belgrade's museums — the **Museum of Contemporary Art** and the **Museum of Applied Art**, with an exhibition from the latter.

## Sub lesson 2 — Exhibition rooms, store rooms — and teaching

A museum has **special exhibition rooms** for its displays. But **museum objects that are not exhibited are kept in special store rooms** — the museum holds far more than any visitor sees at once. And the museum teaches: it runs **educational programmes, lectures and workshops**.
