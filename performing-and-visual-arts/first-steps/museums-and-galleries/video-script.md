# Video script — Museums and Galleries

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Some houses keep treasures.

A museum collects them. It keeps them. It studies them. It shows them.

What you see stands in the exhibition rooms.

What you do not see waits safe in the store rooms.

And the museum teaches — programmes, lectures, workshops.

That is a museum.
