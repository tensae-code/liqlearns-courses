# Museums and Galleries — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A museum is an institution that…
a) collects, keeps, studies and exhibits valuable objects and artworks  b) sells furniture  c) teaches only sports
**Answer: a) collects, keeps, studies and exhibits valuable objects and artworks**.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which two Belgrade museums does the book picture?
a) The Museum of Contemporary Art and the Museum of Applied Art  b) The Museum of Ships and the Museum of Trains  c) The Museum of Toys and the Museum of Food
**Answer: a) The Museum of Contemporary Art and the Museum of Applied Art**.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Objects that are not exhibited are kept…
a) in special store rooms  b) in the street  c) in the exhibition room windows
**Answer: a) in special store rooms**.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Besides exhibiting, a museum also…
a) runs educational programmes, lectures and workshops  b) prints money  c) builds roads
**Answer: a) runs educational programmes, lectures and workshops**.
