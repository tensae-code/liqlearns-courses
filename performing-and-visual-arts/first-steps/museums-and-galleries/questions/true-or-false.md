# Museums and Galleries — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Everything a museum holds is on display at the same time.
**Answer: False** — what is not exhibited is kept in store rooms.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A museum only shows things; it never teaches.
**Answer: False** — it runs educational programmes, lectures and workshops.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Studying its objects is one of a museum's jobs.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Belgrade has a Museum of Contemporary Art and a Museum of Applied Art.
**Answer: True** — both are pictured in the book.
