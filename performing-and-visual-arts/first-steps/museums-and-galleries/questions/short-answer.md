# Museums and Galleries — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the museum's four jobs.
**Answer: To collect, keep, study and exhibit valuable objects and artworks.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Where do exhibited objects stand?
**Answer: In the museum's special exhibition rooms.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Where do objects wait when they are not exhibited?
**Answer: In special store rooms.**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** What kind of objects does a museum keep?
**Answer: Valuable objects and artworks.**
