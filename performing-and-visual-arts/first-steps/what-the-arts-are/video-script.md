# Video script — What the Arts Are

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

What are the arts?

Look — a **drawing**. It stays. Your eyes visit it.
Look — a **photograph**. It stays too.
Drawing and photograph: the **visual** arts. Arts we **see**.

Listen — a **song**! It happens, then it ends.
Watch — a **dance**! An **acted story**!
Music, dance, theatre, drama: the **performing** arts. Arts we watch **being done**.

Two families. One house. The arts.
