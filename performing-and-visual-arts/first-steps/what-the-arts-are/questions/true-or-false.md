# What the Arts Are — true or false

Say if the line is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A photograph is a visual art.
**Answer: True** — it stays, and we know it by seeing.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A dance stays on the wall after it ends.
**Answer: False** — a dance lives while it is being done.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Identifying the arts means only naming them — we never do them.
**Answer: False** — the books join identifying with expressing: we draw, sing, dance and act.

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** The Grade 6 book restates the arts as two families at its opening.
**Answer: True**

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** In the Grade 8 book, both families can carry the same idea or feeling.
**Answer: True**
