# What the Arts Are — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which of these is a visual art?
a) singing  b) drawing  c) dancing
**Answer: b) drawing** — a drawing stays and is known by seeing.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which of these is a performing art?
a) a photograph  b) a drawing on the wall  c) music
**Answer: c) music** — music happens while it is performed.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The performing arts are —
a) music, dance, theatre and drama  b) drawing and photograph only  c) photograph and film
**Answer: a) music, dance, theatre and drama**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** A friend acts a story in front of the class. Which family?
a) visual  b) performing  c) neither
**Answer: b) performing** — acting happens in front of us, with action and movement.

**5. (Sub lesson 1)** In the Grade 3 book, the visual branch also holds —
a) film and video  b) only singing  c) only dance
**Answer: a) film and video**
