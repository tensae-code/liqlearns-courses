# What the Arts Are — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which of these is a visual art?
a) singing  b) drawing  c) dancing
**Answer: b) drawing** — a drawing stays and is known by seeing.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which of these is a performing art?
a) a photograph  b) a drawing on the wall  c) music
**Answer: c) music** — music happens while it is performed.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The performing arts are —
a) music, dance, theatre and drama  b) drawing and photograph only  c) photograph and film
**Answer: a) music, dance, theatre and drama**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** A friend acts a story in front of the class. Which family?
a) visual  b) performing  c) neither
**Answer: b) performing** — acting happens in front of us, with action and movement.

**5. (Sub lesson 1)** In the Grade 3 book, the visual branch also holds —
a) film and video  b) only singing  c) only dance
**Answer: a) film and video**

**6. (Sub lesson 4)** In the Grade 6 book's opening, which list is the performing family?
a) theatre, drama, music and dance  b) painting and sculpture only  c) drawing and design only
**Answer: a) theatre, drama, music and dance** — the book restates the performing family with those four members.

**7. (Sub lesson 4)** In the Grade 6 opening, design belongs to —
a) the performing arts  b) the visual arts  c) neither family
**Answer: b) the visual arts** — design is named among painting, sculpture, drawing and photography.

**8. (Sub lesson 5)** In the Grade 8 comparison, visual arts express ideas and messages by —
a) photograph, painting, drawing and sculpture  b) song alone  c) dance alone
**Answer: a) photograph, painting, drawing and sculpture** — those are the book's named visual means.

**9. (Sub lesson 5)** The Grade 8 matching rule for a dance is —
a) visual, because costumes are seen  b) performing, because it happens before an audience  c) neither
**Answer: b) performing, because it happens before an audience** — the comparison turns on how the art is known.
