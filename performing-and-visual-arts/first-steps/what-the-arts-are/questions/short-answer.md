# What the Arts Are — short answer

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the two visual arts the Grade 2 book starts with.
**Answer:** Drawing and photograph.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the four performing arts.
**Answer:** Music, dance, theatre and drama.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What is the mark of a performing art — how do you know one?
**Answer:** It happens in front of us and lives while it is being done; when it ends, it is gone until someone performs it again.

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Name the Grade 6 visual-arts list.
**Answer:** Painting, sculpture, drawing, design and photography (with the screen arts the book adds).

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** State the Grade 8 comparison in one line.
**Answer:** Visual arts express idea, feeling and message in works that stay and are seen; performing arts express them live, before an audience.

**6. (Sub lesson 6)** Name the two scale kinds Grade 6 Chapter 1 teaches learners to tell apart, and the two pitch levels.
**Answer:** Major and minor scales; low pitch and high pitch.

**7. (Sub lesson 6)** On which two clefs does Chapter 1 build scales, and with what signs?
**Answer:** The G clef and the F clef, using key-signature signs.
