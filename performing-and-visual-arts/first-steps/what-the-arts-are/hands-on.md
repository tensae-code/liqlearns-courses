# Hands-on — What the Arts Are

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The sorting walk
Walk around your home or classroom with a friend. Every time you find an art, point and say its family: 'photograph — visual!' 'radio song — performing!' Find at least three of each.

## Show both families
Make one visual art — a small drawing — and perform one performing art — a short song or dance — for your family. Say, before each: 'This is a visual art' or 'This is a performing art.'
