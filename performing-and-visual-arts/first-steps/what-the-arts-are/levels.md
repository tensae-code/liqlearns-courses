## Sub lesson 1 — Visual arts — the arts we see
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: the learner can do what this part teaches, in their own words and with their own hands — drawing and photograph — and, as the older books add, film and video — are arts we know by seeing.

Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: the learner can do what this part teaches, in their own words and with their own hands — music, dance, theatre and drama happen in front of us, with voice, body and movement.

Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: the learner can do what this part teaches, in their own words and with their own hands — tell the two families apart, and begin to express each one yourself.

Sub lesson 5 questions from the type files. Pass mark: the learner can do what this part teaches, in their own words — the Grade 8 book compares visual and performing art side by side — match each art to how it expresses (Grade 8, printed p. 108).
