# Lesson plan — Getting Started With Drawing Tools

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** First Steps · **Lesson:** Getting Started With Drawing Tools
**Place in the curriculum:** First Steps (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the first tools of drawing, Turkmenistan's way: the plain pencil and its lines, erasing without tearing, coloured pencils pressed for richer colour, watercolour and gouache mixed with water — and cleaning and putting every tool in its place after work.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this course.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Pencil, eraser and coloured pencils** — the plain pencil makes lines of different thickness; erase without tearing the paper; press coloured pencils for richer colour.
2. **Watercolour, gouache, and putting tools away** — watercolour and gouache are mixed with water; gouache is laid thicker and a mistake can be covered with another colour; after work, clean and put every tool in its place.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier First Steps lessons of this course. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say how to erase without tearing, and how coloured pencils give richer colour.
2. Tell how gouache covers a mistake, and what happens to tools after work.
## What you need
A plain pencil, an eraser, coloured pencils, watercolour, gouache, water and paper.
