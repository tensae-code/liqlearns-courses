# Hands-on — Getting Started With Drawing Tools

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Tool trials
On one page: draw a thin line and a thick line with the plain pencil; make a line and erase it cleanly without tearing; colour one shape lightly and one pressed rich; paint one wash of watercolour and one thick patch of gouache, and cover a dot in it. Then wash, clean and put every tool in its place.
