# Getting Started With Drawing Tools

## Do it first

Say what you already know about this lesson's subject — one thing you have seen, heard or done — before you read on.

## Sub lesson 1 — Pencil, eraser and coloured pencils

**The plain pencil** draws **lines of different thickness** — light and heavy, thin and thick, by how you hold and press it.

**The eraser** takes a line away — rub gently, so you **erase without tearing the paper**.

**Coloured pencils** give more when you ask more: **press them for richer colour**.

## Sub lesson 2 — Watercolour, gouache, and putting tools away

**Watercolour and gouache** are both **mixed with water**.

**Gouache is laid thicker** than watercolour — thick enough that **a mistake can be covered with another colour** laid over it.

And the lesson ends as it should: **clean every tool and put it in its place after work**. Tools cared for are ready for the next drawing.
