# Video script — Getting Started With Drawing Tools

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Your first tools!

Pencil — thin line, thick line. Eraser — gently, no tearing!
Coloured pencils — press, and the colour grows rich.
Watercolour, gouache — mixed with water. Gouache thick — it can cover a mistake!
And at the end: clean, tidy, every tool in its place.
