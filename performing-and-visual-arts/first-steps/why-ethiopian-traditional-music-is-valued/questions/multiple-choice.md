# Why Ethiopian Traditional Music Is Valued — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The guide's first mark of value is that the music —
a) survived the centuries without falling under foreign influence  b) was imported recently  c) is performed once a year
**Answer: a) survived the centuries without falling under foreign influence** — survival unbending is the first mark.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A music survives unbent when a people —
a) keeps choosing it at its occasions, generation after generation  b) locks it away unused  c) forgets it
**Answer: a) keeps choosing it at its occasions, generation after generation** — choosing is the keeping.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The guide notes the music's instruments —
a) exist only in Ethiopia  b) are all imported  c) are unnamed
**Answer: a) exist only in Ethiopia** — home-grown instruments are a mark of value.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The many kinds of music culture live —
a) in different localities, each keeping its own kinds  b) in one city only  c) nowhere
**Answer: a) in different localities, each keeping its own kinds** — locality kinds complete the marks.
