# From Idea to Finished Picture — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Finishing a painting (Гүйцээж будах) means…
a) copying and completing birds, and finishing the half-coloured bird in the notebook  b) throwing the picture away  c) starting a new picture every time
**Answer: a) copying and completing birds, and finishing the half-coloured bird in the notebook**

**2.** A half-done picture should be…
a) carried through to its finished form  b) hidden  c) torn up
**Answer: a) carried through to its finished form**
