# Pictures of What We Do Every Day — true or false

Say if the line is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 3)** Teeth are cleaned every day, morning and night.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 4)** Combing comes in the day before washing, in the morning song's order.
**Answer: False** — the song's order: get up, wash, brush teeth, comb hair, go to school.

**3. (Sub lesson 7)** Reading books is one of the day's activities in the book.
**Answer: True**
