# Pictures of What We Do Every Day — short answer

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the very first activity of the book's day?
**Answer:** Getting up.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Where do the two children clean their teeth?
**Answer:** At a basin.

**3. (Sub lesson 5)** Name two ways of eating the book pictures.
**Answer:** Eating at a table (alone) and eating together around a mesob — also a girl eating, and the coffee-ceremony gathering.

**4. (Sub lesson 7)** Where does the girl read her book?
**Answer:** Under a tree.

**5. (Sub lesson 8)** Name two helping-at-home actions besides pounding grain.
**Answer:** Sweeping (and clearing rubbish) and washing clothes (wringing them over a tub).
