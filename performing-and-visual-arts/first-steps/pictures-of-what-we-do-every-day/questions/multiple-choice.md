# Pictures of What We Do Every Day — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The first picture of the day shows a girl —
a) combing her hair  b) getting up in bed  c) eating at a mesob
**Answer: b) getting up in bed**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In the washing picture, the two children wash —
a) their faces, with water running down  b) only their feet  c) their clothes
**Answer: a) their faces, with water running down**

**3. (Sub lesson 5)** The family's eating picture shows everyone —
a) eating alone in different rooms  b) eating together around a mesob  c) cooking outside
**Answer: b) eating together around a mesob**

**4. (Sub lesson 6)** The safe place to cross the road in the school picture is —
a) anywhere at a run  b) the zebra crossing  c) behind a parked car
**Answer: b) the zebra crossing**

**5. (Sub lesson 8)** The helping-at-home photograph shows —
a) grain being pounded with a tall mortar and pestle  b) a child sleeping  c) a market stall
**Answer: a) grain being pounded with a tall mortar and pestle**

**6. (Sub lesson 9)** In the schoolyard picture, the children are —
a) playing ball, skipping and holding hands in a ring game  b) cleaning their teeth  c) reading under a tree
**Answer: a) playing ball, skipping and holding hands in a ring game**
