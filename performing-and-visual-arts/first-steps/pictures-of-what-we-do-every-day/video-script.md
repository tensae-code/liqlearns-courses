# Video script — Pictures of What We Do Every Day

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This is my day. In pictures.

I **get up**. I **wash** my face and hands.
I **clean my teeth**. I **comb my hair**.
I **eat** — with my family, around the **mesob**.
I **go to school**. I cross on the **zebra crossing**.
I **read** my book. I **help at home**.
And I **play with my friends**.

That is the day. Your day too.
