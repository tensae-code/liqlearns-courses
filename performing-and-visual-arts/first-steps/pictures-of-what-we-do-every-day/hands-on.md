# Hands-on — Pictures of What We Do Every Day

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Mime the day
With friends, deal out the day like cards: one names an activity, everyone mimes it for five breaths. Go through the whole day in order, faster each round.

## The real morning
Tomorrow morning, do the book's order slowly and say each step aloud as you do it: getting up — washing — teeth — hair — eating — school. Say it like the song.
