# Lesson plan — Pictures of What We Do Every Day

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** First Steps · **Lesson:** Pictures of What We Do Every Day
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 1 — First Steps (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the pictures of a child's whole day — getting up, washing, cleaning teeth, combing hair, eating, going to school, reading, helping at home and playing with friends.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Looking at Pictures — reading pictures — turned onto the learner's own day.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the
exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an
image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture
of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does
with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and
while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is
dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the
student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph,
or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be
shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Looking at Pictures. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say what daily-activity pictures show: the actions people do every day.
2. Read each picture of the day and name its action.
3. Put the day in order, from getting up to playing and reading.
4. Perform one daily activity for a teacher or friend, as the book asks.

## What you need
Your own day — and, for the hands-on, a comb, a cup of water and a toothbrush if you have them.

## If the learner is stuck
- Mixes the order of the day → use the morning song's order as the spine: bell, get up, wash, brush teeth, comb hair, school.
- Cannot name an activity → act it out first; the name follows the body.
