# Lesson plan — How the Arts Help Us Learn

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** First Steps · **Lesson:** How the Arts Help Us Learn
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 1 — First Steps (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the arts make every other school subject easier, clearer and unforgettable — language, maths, science and social studies all learn better with song, drawing, dance and drama inside them.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on How the Arts Belong Together — if the arts complete each other, they can also carry other subjects.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the
exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an
image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture
of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does
with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and
while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is
dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the
student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph,
or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be
shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved (the gate: README, "Units, Lessons and Sub Lessons (ruled 2026-11-08)"):

1. **How the arts help every subject** — The principle: learning through melody, dance, drawing and drama — and what it grows in the learner.
2. **Arts for language** — Lines, letters and songs; stories in drawing; poems in melody; stories in drama.
3. **Arts for maths** — Drawing maths shapes; shapes, fractions and picture sums; number songs.
4. **Arts for science** — Surroundings, cleanliness, sounds and care of the world — taught in drawing, music and drama.
5. **Arts for social studies** — A community's life, history, culture and values — taught through drawing, music and theatre.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: How the Arts Belong Together. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say why lessons taught through the arts are not forgotten.
2. Give the arts' way into language: lines and letters, stories in drawing, poems in melody, stories in drama.
3. Give the arts' way into maths: shapes, fractions, picture sums and number songs.
4. Give the arts' way into science and social studies, with an example of each.

## What you need
Paper, a pencil, and one song you know.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — Sing the alphabet — or the fidel — as a song. (lesson/lesson.md).
2. **The explanation** — the parts of lesson/lesson.md, in order.

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks arts are only for the arts lesson → give the fidel-song test from Do it first: the tune carried the letters.
- Thinks picture sums are 'not real maths' → the books use them to balance and count; the number answer still gets written under the picture.
