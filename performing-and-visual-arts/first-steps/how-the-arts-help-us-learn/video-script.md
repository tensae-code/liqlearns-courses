# Video script — How the Arts Help Us Learn

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Letters are hard. Sing them — **easy**!
The tune **carries** them. They stay.

That is what the arts do for **every** subject.
**Language**: lines, letters, stories in drawing, poems in song.
**Maths**: shapes, fractions, sums in pictures, number songs.
**Science**: the world, drawn and sung, is not forgotten.
**Social studies**: our community's life, in drawing, music and theatre.

Learn it through the arts — and it **stays**.
