# Hands-on — How the Arts Help Us Learn

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Teach it with an art
Choose one hard thing from any subject. Teach it to a friend three ways: draw it, sing it, act it. Ask your friend which way made it stay — that way is the arts helping learning, in your own hands.

## A picture-sum card
Make a card: on the front, a sum drawn in pictures (fruits, stones, birds). On the back, the same sum in numbers. Trade cards with a friend and solve each other's.
