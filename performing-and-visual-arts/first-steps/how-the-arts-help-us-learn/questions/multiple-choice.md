# How the Arts Help Us Learn — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Grade 2 says children learn other subjects through the arts —
a) with great boredom  b) without boredom  c) only by tests
**Answer: b) without boredom**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which list is Grade 2's list of what arts learning grows?
a) deep observation, language ability, social relations, decision-making, readiness and creativity  b) only handwriting  c) only singing in tune
**Answer: a) deep observation, language ability, social relations, decision-making, readiness and creativity**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** From the drawing branch, we learn to write the Amharic and English letters —
a) beautifully  b) never  c) only in Grade 8
**Answer: a) beautifully**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Grade 3 says poems are taught —
a) in melody  b) by heart in silence  c) not at all
**Answer: a) in melody**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** A picture sum helps maths by —
a) hiding the numbers  b) replacing numbers with pictures of things to count  c) removing the answer
**Answer: b) replacing numbers with pictures of things to count**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** Grade 3's maths work through the arts includes —
a) shapes, fractions and graphs, calculating with drawings, number songs  b) only graphs  c) only singing
**Answer: a) shapes, fractions and graphs, calculating with drawings, number songs**

**7. (Sub lesson 4)** Grade 2 teaches cleanliness of self, family and area through —
a) drawing, music and drama  b) a written warning only  c) punishment
**Answer: a) drawing, music and drama**

**8. (Sub lesson 5)** A community's history, culture, custom and values are taught through —
a) drawing, music and theatre  b) nothing — they cannot be taught  c) foreign books only
**Answer: a) drawing, music and theatre**

**9. (Sub lesson 6)** The Grade 3 closing exercise judges the claim 'music's relationship with other subjects is low' as —
a) false — the relationship is high  b) true  c) unanswerable
**Answer: a) false — the relationship is high** — the chapter teaches the arts' strong relation to other subjects.

**10. (Sub lesson 6)** The exercise holds that different messages can be transmitted by music —
a) true  b) false  c) only in writing
**Answer: a) true** — music carrying different messages is the exercise's true item.

**11. (Sub lesson 7)** The Grade 4 aims say other subjects' content can be learned through —
a) art products  b) silence only  c) forgetting
**Answer: a) art products** — learning through art products is the first aim.

**12. (Sub lesson 7)** The aims ask the learner to use which arts practically while learning?
a) drawing, music, drama and song  b) none  c) only examinations
**Answer: a) drawing, music, drama and song** — those four are the aims' named tools.

**13. (Sub lesson 8)** The guide's §5.1 connects shapes, forms and colours first with —
a) mathematics  b) no subject  c) only sport
**Answer: a) mathematics** — the syllabus names mathematics first.

**14. (Sub lesson 8)** The syllabus plans for the visual elements to be —
a) applied across the curriculum  b) kept inside art only  c) forgotten after art
**Answer: a) applied across the curriculum** — applying the elements across subjects is the syllabus entry.

**15. (Sub lesson 9)** The Grade 6 book says one picture is worth —
a) a thousand words  b) nothing  c) one word
**Answer: a) a thousand words** — that is the book's saying for visual communication.

**16. (Sub lesson 9)** The book calls drawing —
a) the most ancient communication  b) a new invention  c) only decoration
**Answer: a) the most ancient communication** — people pictured before they wrote.

**17. (Sub lesson 10)** The Yekatit 12 memorial recalls harm done to Ethiopians in —
a) the 1936 Italian invasion  b) a flood  c) no event — it recalls nothing
**Answer: a) the 1936 Italian invasion** — the memorial's history is the invasion-era harm.

**18. (Sub lesson 10)** The book calls the monument a memorial for —
a) the generations — history and identity  b) one family only  c) decoration only
**Answer: a) the generations — history and identity** — it teaches those who never lived the event.

**19. (Sub lesson 11)** In the table, music links with mathematics through —
a) counting in song — ten green bottles  b) silence  c) drawing only
**Answer: a) counting in song — ten green bottles** — the counting song is the maths column's example.

**20. (Sub lesson 11)** The language column's example is —
a) the chant 'Ha Hu Hi I sing'  b) a wordless hum only  c) a proverb book
**Answer: a) the chant 'Ha Hu Hi I sing'** — letters carried by melody is the language link.

**21. (Sub lesson 12)** The Grade 6 book likens choosing music to —
a) choosing the food we eat  b) choosing shoes  c) nothing — music needs no choosing
**Answer: a) choosing the food we eat** — the food comparison is the book's listening rule.

**22. (Sub lesson 12)** The book counsels refraining from —
a) music unsuited to the child's age  b) all music  c) all singing
**Answer: a) music unsuited to the child's age** — age-suited choosing is the counsel.

**23. (Sub lesson 13)** The Grade 7 book says we do what to the home's utensils through visual art?
a) arrange, beautify, organise and design them  b) hide them  c) break them
**Answer: a) arrange, beautify, organise and design them** — those four verbs are the section's own.

**24. (Sub lesson 13)** Visual art joins history because —
a) the things a people made carry their record forward  b) history has no objects  c) pictures stop time entirely
**Answer: a) the things a people made carry their record forward** — made things are history's evidence.

**25. (Sub lesson 14)** The Grade 7 book links music's rhythm with —
a) physics — rhythmic movements measured in time  b) no science  c) only grammar
**Answer: a) physics — rhythmic movements measured in time** — rhythm measured in time is the physics link.

**26. (Sub lesson 14)** Music therapy, in the Grade 7 set, is —

**Answer: a) music used to heal and settle the mind** — therapy is the healing link in §5.3.

**27. (Sub lesson 15)** The Grade 7 book says performing art in teaching brings —

**28. (Sub lesson 15)** Figure 39 shows —

**29. (Sub lesson 16)** The psychological benefit strengthens —

**30. (Sub lesson 16)** The book also names which effects?

**31. (Sub lesson 17)** The Grade 8 relation between performing art and natural science lives in —
a) sound creation, pitch, breath, vibration and oscillation  b) nothing shared  c) colour mixing only
**Answer: a) sound creation, pitch, breath, vibration and oscillation** — those are §5.1.1's named meeting points.

**32. (Sub lesson 17)** "Qenet" in this part names —
a) the tuning systems instruments are built by  b) a dance step  c) a kind of paper

**33. (Sub lesson 18)** History, in the social-science part, is learned from —

**34. (Sub lesson 18)** Visual art helps mathematics by —
a) scale (enlarging/reducing), solid geometric images, perimeter and area by design  b) replacing numbers  c) guessing
**Answer: a) scale (enlarging/reducing), solid geometric images, perimeter and area by design** — scale, solids and measurement are the maths links.

**35. (Sub lesson 19)** The Grade 8 opening claim is that art —
a) relates to every subject  b) relates to none  c) replaces every subject
**Answer: a) relates to every subject** — §5.1 states the universal relation.

**36. (Sub lesson 19)** In the mathematics part, the notes' time values stand as —
