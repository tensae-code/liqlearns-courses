# How the Arts Help Us Learn — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Grade 2 says children learn other subjects through the arts —
a) with great boredom  b) without boredom  c) only by tests
**Answer: b) without boredom**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which list is Grade 2's list of what arts learning grows?
a) deep observation, language ability, social relations, decision-making, readiness and creativity  b) only handwriting  c) only singing in tune
**Answer: a) deep observation, language ability, social relations, decision-making, readiness and creativity**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** From the drawing branch, we learn to write the Amharic and English letters —
a) beautifully  b) never  c) only in Grade 8
**Answer: a) beautifully**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Grade 3 says poems are taught —
a) in melody  b) by heart in silence  c) not at all
**Answer: a) in melody**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** A picture sum helps maths by —
a) hiding the numbers  b) replacing numbers with pictures of things to count  c) removing the answer
**Answer: b) replacing numbers with pictures of things to count**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** Grade 3's maths work through the arts includes —
a) shapes, fractions and graphs, calculating with drawings, number songs  b) only graphs  c) only singing
**Answer: a) shapes, fractions and graphs, calculating with drawings, number songs**

**7. (Sub lesson 4)** Grade 2 teaches cleanliness of self, family and area through —
a) drawing, music and drama  b) a written warning only  c) punishment
**Answer: a) drawing, music and drama**

**8. (Sub lesson 5)** A community's history, culture, custom and values are taught through —
a) drawing, music and theatre  b) nothing — they cannot be taught  c) foreign books only
**Answer: a) drawing, music and theatre**
