# How the Arts Help Us Learn — short answer

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Name three arts ways into language.
**Answer:** Any three of: lines loosening the hand for the fidel; learning letters in drawing and song; stories in visible drawing; poems in melody; short writings and stories in drama; distinguishing fidel shapes and sizes.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Draw and answer: 4 birds + 3 birds = ? Write the number.
**Answer:** 7

**3. (Sub lesson 4)** Name two things Grade 2's science pictures show.
**Answer:** Land (a landform, መልክዓምድር) and flowers — and the shapes of made things.

**4. (Sub lesson 5)** What is the practice at the end of Grade 2's social-studies part?
**Answer:** Tell the teacher what you understood about learning history and social values from the picture and the song.
