# Art Teaches Every Subject — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Grade 8 chapter's closing claim is that art education —
a) relates to every kind of subject, directly or indirectly  b) relates to one subject only  c) should stay separate from all subjects
**Answer: a) relates to every kind of subject, directly or indirectly** — that is the chapter's closing claim.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The relations are worked for —
a) performing art and visual art both  b) visual art only  c) neither
**Answer: a) performing art and visual art both** — both families carry relations in §5.1.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The closing sub-section joins art education with —
a) special-needs education  b) examinations only  c) sports only
**Answer: a) special-needs education** — that is the sub-section (ሠ) on p. 133.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** For a learner who cannot use the usual road, art can be —
a) the way in — by doing, picturing, singing, handling  b) a locked door  c) a distraction
**Answer: a) the way in — by doing, picturing, singing, handling** — art's roads are the alternative entry.
