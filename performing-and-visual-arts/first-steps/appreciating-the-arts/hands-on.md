# Hands-on — Appreciating the Arts

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## A real visit
Plan a gubignit with family or friends: one place with art or history — a church, a craft workshop, a historic building, a photographer's studio. Before you go, list what you hope to see. After, tell the group the one thing you appreciated most.

## The appreciation shelf
Make a small exhibition at home or in class: everyone's drawing, craft or photograph in a row. Walk the row slowly, as visitors. Each visitor must say, for one work, what they appreciate and why — out loud, to its maker.
