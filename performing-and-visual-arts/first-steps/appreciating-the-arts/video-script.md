# Video script — Appreciating the Arts

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

What is **appreciation**?

It is not only a word. It is a **doing**.
You love a song? **Sing it.**
You love a dance? **Dance it.**
You love a drawing? **Draw**, and show your work.

And go on a **visit** — ጉብኝት.
Walk. Look long. At pictures, at crafts, at old places.
The art you honour **grows**.

Appreciation: your feeling, **expressed by doing**.
