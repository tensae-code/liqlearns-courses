# Appreciating the Arts — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In Grade 3, to appreciate means —
a) visiting and looking at art products, and giving appreciation  b) buying the most expensive art  c) closing your eyes and imagining
**Answer: a) visiting and looking at art products, and giving appreciation**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A visit (ጉብኝት) is —
a) a journey, alone or in a group, to enjoy and know heritages and artworks  b) a test at school  c) a kind of dance
**Answer: a) a journey, alone or in a group, to enjoy and know heritages and artworks**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Grade 2's definition: appreciation is —
a) saying 'beautiful' once  b) expressing our inner feeling for the arts by doing them  c) framing a picture
**Answer: b) expressing our inner feeling for the arts by doing them**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In Grade 2's closing check, a poem presented on stage by acting is —
a) a photograph  b) theatre  c) drawing
**Answer: b) theatre**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** The two photographs in the review are —
a) a market and a school  b) the Sof Omar cave and the Tis Abay Falls  c) two portraits
**Answer: b) the Sof Omar cave and the Tis Abay Falls**
