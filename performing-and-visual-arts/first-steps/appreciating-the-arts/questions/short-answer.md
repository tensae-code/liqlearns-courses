# Appreciating the Arts — short answer

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name four branches Grade 3 lists to visit and appreciate.
**Answer:** Any four of: drawing, sculpture, handicraft, photograph, architecture, music, dance, theatre.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** By what kinds of art is appreciation expressed? (Grade 2's own closing question.)
**Answer:** By all of them, done — music, dance, drawing, theatre and photograph are the chapter's five.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the book's photograph activity ask you to say?
**Answer:** Which of the photographs pleased you (Sof Omar or Tis Abay) — and, in this lesson, why.
