# Lesson plan — Appreciating the Arts

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** First Steps · **Lesson:** Appreciating the Arts
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 1 — First Steps (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** appreciation is not just saying 'beautiful' — it is visiting and looking, and showing our feeling for the arts by doing them.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on everything in First Steps — we appreciate what we have learned to see, hear and do.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the
exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved (the gate: README, "Units, Lessons and Sub Lessons (ruled 2026-11-08)"):

1. **Visiting and looking** — Appreciation practised by visiting (ጉብኝት): journeys to enjoy and know artworks and heritages.
2. **Showing what we love** — Grade 2's definition: appreciation is expressing our inner feeling for the arts by doing them.
3. **Saying which we love — and why** — The photograph review folded in: Sof Omar and the Tis Abay Falls — say which pleased you.

4. **The Grade 1 chapter closes — what we appreciated (closing fold)** — the Grade 1 book's Chapter 4 summary: traditional dress, traditional dance, eating together and helping each other were appreciated by performing them (Grade 1, printed p. 66).

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Observing Our Surroundings and Looking at Pictures. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say what appreciation means in the books: expressing our inner feeling for the arts by doing them.
2. Practise appreciation by visiting (ጉብኝት): go and look at art products and historic heritages.
3. Show appreciation by doing: sing, dance, draw, act or photograph — and say what you did.
4. Look at a great photograph — Sof Omar, Tis Abay — and say which pleased you, and why.

## What you need
Your eyes, a place to visit if you can — and one art you can do.

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks appreciation = saying 'nice' → the books' definition is doing: express the feeling by performing the art.
- Cannot travel for a visit → a visit can be small: one craft stall, one church painting, one historic wall near home, looked at long and with respect.
