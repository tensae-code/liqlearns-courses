Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Grade 8 contents name a section on —
a) the value of dance works  b) the price of stages  c) nothing
**Answer: a) the value of {kind} works** — that is the contents line itself.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson is EVIDENCE-LIMITED because —
a) the guide's body ends at p. 56; Unit 4 stands on contents and syllabus only  b) the lesson is unfinished by choice  c) the contents are secret

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The contents set the task to —
a) compare and contrast dance works with music  b) memorise prices  c) copy the works
**Answer: a) compare and contrast** — comparison is the contents' stated method.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A work's value, in this method, is sharpened by —
a) standing it beside a neighbouring art's works  b) hiding it  c) renaming it
