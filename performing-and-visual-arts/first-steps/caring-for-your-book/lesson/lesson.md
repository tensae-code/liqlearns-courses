# Caring for Your Book

## Do it first

Take your book. Look at its cover, its corners and its pages. Is it clean? Is any page bent or torn? Hold it the way you would hold something you love — gently, with clean, dry hands. This lesson is the promise you make to your book at the start of the course.

[Picture 9000 — a child covering a book with paper, with a bookmark strip beside it]

## Looking after your book

A book lasts when we care for it. **Cover the book** with paper, so the cover stays clean and strong. **Keep it dry and clean** — never put it near water, food or dirt, and never touch it with dirty hands. **Use a bookmark** — a small strip of paper or card — to hold your place, instead of folding a page corner down. **Never tear pictures out** of a book: the pictures belong to the whole book, and the next reader needs them too. Turn the pages slowly, from the corner, one by one.

Every book in this course begins with the same promise, in the Grade 1, Grade 2 and Grade 3 books alike. A cared-for book can teach you all year — and then teach someone else after you.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the four promises: cover it, keep it dry and clean, use a bookmark, never tear out pictures. Let your companion check your own book and say which promises you are already keeping.

## Do It Yourself

1. Cover your book with paper, neatly, the way the lesson shows.
2. Make a bookmark from a strip of paper or card and put it in your book.
3. Find one book at home or at school that needs care. Clean its cover gently and mend what you safely can.
4. Show a younger child how to turn a page without tearing it.
