# Caring for Your Book — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson — it has no sub lessons (a short, single-skill lesson; his ruling 2026-11-09).

Pass mark: the learner can do the lesson's one thing unaided — how to look after a book so it stays clean, dry and whole for the whole year. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: more books to care for, worse-kept books to rescue, and teaching the care to someone younger, until the care is a habit, not a task.
