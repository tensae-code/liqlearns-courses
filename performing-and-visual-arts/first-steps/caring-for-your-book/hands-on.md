# Hands-on — Caring for Your Book

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Cover your book
Wrap your book in a clean piece of paper. Fold the edges in, smooth and flat. This cover takes the dirt and the knocks so the book does not.

## Make a bookmark
Cut or tear a clean strip of paper or thin card. Write or draw one small picture on it. Keep it inside your book, at the page where you stopped.

## The gentle page turn
Practise on an old newspaper or a spare book: pinch the top corner of one page only, lift slowly, lay it over. Ten slow turns. Fast turns tear.
