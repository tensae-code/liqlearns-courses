# Caring for Your Book — true or false

Say if the line is true or false.

**1.** It is good to tear a picture you like out of a book.
**Answer: False** — pictures belong to the whole book — never tear them out.

**2.** Clean, dry hands are the right hands for a book.
**Answer: True**

**3.** Folding a page corner is the best way to hold your place.
**Answer: False** — use a bookmark instead.
