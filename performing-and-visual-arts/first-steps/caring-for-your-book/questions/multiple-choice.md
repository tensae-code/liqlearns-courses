# Caring for Your Book — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What do you put on a book to keep its cover safe?
a) a paper cover  b) a cup of water  c) a stone
**Answer: a) a paper cover** — the paper cover takes the dirt and knocks.

**2.** How do you hold your place in a book?
a) fold the page corner  b) tear out the page  c) use a bookmark
**Answer: c) use a bookmark** — a bookmark holds the place without hurting the page.

**3.** What must a book be kept away from?
a) water and dirt  b) your hands  c) the shelf
**Answer: a) water and dirt** — water and dirt spoil a book.
