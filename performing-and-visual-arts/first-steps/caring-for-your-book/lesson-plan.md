# Lesson plan — Caring for Your Book

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** First Steps · **Lesson:** Caring for Your Book
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 1 — First Steps (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** how to look after a book so it stays clean, dry and whole for the whole year.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on nothing but the child's own hands and the book in front of them — it is the course opening.

## Before this lesson

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Cover a book with paper to keep it safe.
2. Keep a book dry and clean, and keep it away from dirt and water.
3. Use a bookmark instead of folding a page corner.
4. Never tear pictures out of a book, and turn pages gently.

## What you need
The learner's own book, a piece of paper to cover it with, and a bookmark (a strip of paper or card).

## If the learner is stuck
- Folds the page corner to hold the place → give them a strip of paper as a bookmark; the fold breaks the page little by little.
- Holds the book with wet or dirty hands → wash and dry the hands first; a book and water are not friends.
