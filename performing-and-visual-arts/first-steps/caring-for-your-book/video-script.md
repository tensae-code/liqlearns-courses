# Video script — Caring for Your Book

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Look. This is my book.

I **cover** it with paper. Now it is safe.
I keep it **dry**. I keep it **clean**.
I do not fold the page. I use a **bookmark**.
I **never tear** a picture out.
I turn the pages slowly. One. By. One.

My book will teach me all year.
