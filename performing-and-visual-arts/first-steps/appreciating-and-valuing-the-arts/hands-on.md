# Hands-on — Appreciating and Valuing the Arts

The lesson with your hands, your feet and your voice together, not only your eyes.

## Reasoned judging circle
Three works before the class. Each student judges one, aloud, in the form: 'I value / I question this, because…' — pointing at the work as they argue. No judgement without its because.

## Value map
On a wall map or list, place the folk arts studied in this course by their peoples, each with its one-line value. Diversity made visible: many beauties, one country.
