# Appreciating and Valuing the Arts — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the chapter's Amharic title.
**Answer: የውበት እሴትና ፍርድ — aesthetic value and judgement.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Grade 7's appreciation units include which skill-lesson?
**Answer: Appreciating an artwork — appreciation taught as a skill.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name the five moves.
**Answer: Perceive, analyse, judge; aesthetic taste; educating society aesthetically (with assessing aesthetic value).**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Before judging another people's art, what four things do you ask?
**Answer: Its form, its function, its material and its context.**

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** With what in hand does the African judge value beauty?
**Answer: Africa's own materials, functions and meanings — with reasons.**

**6. (Sub lesson 6)** List the three Grade 7 aims.
**Answer:** Give value to the aesthetic values around you; appreciate the community's aesthetic values; know the relation between them.
