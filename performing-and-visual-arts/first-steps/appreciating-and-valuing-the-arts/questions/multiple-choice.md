# Appreciating and Valuing the Arts — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** An aesthetic judgement must carry…
  a) Reasons drawn from the work b) Only the judge's mood c) The work's price
**Answer: a) Reasons drawn from the work** — judgement is a claim carried by reasons from the work itself.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Grade 8's valuing study names which philosophers (syllabus)?
  a) Plato, Aristotle, Kant, Hegel and Marx b) Only local elders c) No names at all
**Answer: a) Plato, Aristotle, Kant, Hegel and Marx** — the syllabus names Plato, Aristotle, Kant, Hegel and Marx.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The guide's order for appreciation is…
  a) Perceive, analyse, judge b) Judge first, look later c) Buy, frame, forget
**Answer: a) Perceive, analyse, judge** — perceiving, analysing and judging — in that order.

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Diversity in folk art is studied in…
  a) Form, function, material and context b) Price, size, weight and age only c) Alphabetical order
**Answer: a) Form, function, material and context** — form, function, material and context — the four diversities.

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** African aesthetics values beauty…
  a) In the African context, in its own terms b) Only by foreign rules c) Not at all — it refuses valuing
**Answer: a) In the African context, in its own terms** — valuing beauty in the African context — the strand's closing lesson.

**6. (Sub lesson 6)** The Grade 7 chapter aims ask the learner to give value to —
a) the aesthetic values around them  b) nothing in particular  c) only foreign art
**Answer: a) the aesthetic values around them** — that is the first stated aim.

**7. (Sub lesson 6)** The aims also ask the learner to appreciate —
a) the community's aesthetic values  b) only their own work  c) prices of artworks
**Answer: a) the community's aesthetic values** — the community's kept beauty is the second aim.

**8. (Sub lesson 7)** Which pair of benefits does the Chapter 4 self-check name for music?
  a) For social life, and in teaching b) For trade, and for travel c) For building, and for farming
**Answer: a) For social life, and in teaching** — the table's own two music benefits.
