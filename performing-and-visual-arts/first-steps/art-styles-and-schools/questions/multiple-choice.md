# Art Styles and Schools — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** A style is…
a) the way an artwork is made that marks it as its own kind  b) the price of a painting  c) the frame of a picture
**Answer: a) the way an artwork is made that marks it as its own kind**

**2.** An art school (مکتب) is…
a) a movement of artists sharing a way of working  b) a building only  c) a single colour
**Answer: a) a movement of artists sharing a way of working**
