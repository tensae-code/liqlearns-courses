# Video script — Imagination and Creativity in Art

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Look at the world. Now — close your eyes and move it.

A stairway where no stairway was. A fish in the sky.

That is imagination — and it is not escaping the world.

It is a spur: it builds artworks from the world,

and it finds solutions in daily life too.
