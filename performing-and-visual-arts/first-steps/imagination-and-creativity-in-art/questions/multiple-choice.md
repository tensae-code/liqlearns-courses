# Imagination and Creativity in Art — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** As spurs to creating artworks, reality and imagination are…
a) equally important  b) imagination does not count  c) only reality counts
**Answer: a) equally important**.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Imagination lets a person…
a) imagine and combine different spaces, shapes and events  b) copy only what stands in front of them  c) stop making art
**Answer: a) imagine and combine different spaces, shapes and events**.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Modern artists find inspiration for unreal scenes in…
a) our everyday surroundings  b) things nobody has ever seen  c) empty paper only
**Answer: a) our everyday surroundings**.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which of these artists does the book picture?
a) Magritte, Klee, Tabaković, de Chirico and Miró  b) only photographers  c) only sculptors of stone
**Answer: a) Magritte, Klee, Tabaković, de Chirico and Miró**.
