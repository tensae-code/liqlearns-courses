# Imagination and Creativity in Art — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Besides art, where else can imagination spur us?
**Answer: In finding solutions in daily life.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What can imagination combine that reality keeps apart?
**Answer: Different spaces, shapes and events.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** From what do you build a being from imagination in the book's exercise?
**Answer: From old magazines and newspapers — a collage creature — then you think up a name that suits it.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Name one pictured artist of unreal scenes.
**Answer: Magritte, Klee, Ivan Tabaković, de Chirico or Miró.**
