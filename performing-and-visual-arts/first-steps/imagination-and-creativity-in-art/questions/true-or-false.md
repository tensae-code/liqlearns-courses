# Imagination and Creativity in Art — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Imagination matters less than reality in creating artworks.
**Answer: False** — the book gives them an equally important role.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Imagination can help solve problems in daily life.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Unreal scenes in modern art can begin in everyday surroundings.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A collage creature must copy a real animal exactly.
**Answer: False** — it is a being from imagination.
