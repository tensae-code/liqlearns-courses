# Hands-on — Imagination and Creativity in Art

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## A being from imagination
Cut shapes from old magazines and newspapers. Join them into a being that does not exist. Give it a name that suits it, and say where it lives and what it does.

## Unreal from ordinary
Draw an ordinary street or room. Then change three things by imagination — a fish in the sky, a stairway to the moon. Show your companion which three you changed.
