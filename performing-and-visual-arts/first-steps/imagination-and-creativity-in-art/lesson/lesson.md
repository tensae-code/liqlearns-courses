## Sub lesson 1 — Imagination, a spur to creating

**Reality and imagination have an equally important role as spurs to creating artworks.** Imagination lets a person **imagine and combine different spaces, shapes and events** — things that never stood together in the real world can meet in a picture. And imagination is **irreplaceable as a spur to artistic creation** — and it can also spur **finding solutions in daily life**.

## Sub lesson 2 — Unreal scenes from everyday surroundings

**Modern artists find inspiration for unusual, unreal scenes in our everyday surroundings.** The book pictures works by **Magritte, Klee, Ivan Tabaković, de Chirico and Miró** — artists who took the ordinary world and let imagination rearrange it. The making that belongs with it: from **old magazines and newspapers, build a being from imagination** — a collage creature — and give it a name that suits it.
