# Hands-on — Observing Our Surroundings

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The doorstep census
Stand where you can see your surroundings. Count and name: 3 houses or buildings, 3 plants, 3 animals or birds, 3 people, 3 moving things. Say each one's colour.

## Tell the action
With a friend, take turns being the watcher. One does a small action — pouring water, sweeping, carrying a book. The other watches in silence, then tells it: who, doing what. Five actions each.
