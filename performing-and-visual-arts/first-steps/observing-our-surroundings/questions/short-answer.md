# Observing Our Surroundings — short answer

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name four things in your own surroundings.
**Answer:** Any four true things around the learner — house, tree, animal, person, bird, plant, car, cart…

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Tell one action you can see now, in one sentence: who is doing what.
**Answer:** A true sentence in the shape: (person/animal) is (doing …).

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What two things does the observer name about a thing, besides its name?
**Answer:** Its colour and its shape (and its sound, where it has one).
