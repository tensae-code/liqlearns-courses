# Observing Our Surroundings — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which list is the book's own list of our surroundings?
a) houses, animals, people, birds, plants, cars and carts  b) only trees and rivers  c) only things inside a classroom
**Answer: a) houses, animals, people, birds, plants, cars and carts**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A hen, in the book's sorting —
a) has its own sound and moves  b) has no sound and never moves  c) is not part of our surroundings
**Answer: a) has its own sound and moves**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** In the book's picture, the girl with the wheelchair —
a) is sleeping  b) is pushing her classmate  c) is drawing a picture
**Answer: b) is pushing her classmate**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A good telling of an action says —
a) only the colour  b) who is doing what  c) only the place
**Answer: b) who is doing what**
