# Observing Our Surroundings — true or false

Say if the line is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A house has its own sound and moves from place to place.
**Answer: False** — a house stays and has no sound of its own.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Observing means looking with care, on purpose.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** 'Two children are reading together' tells who and what is happening.
**Answer: True**
