# Lesson plan — Observing Our Surroundings

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** First Steps · **Lesson:** Observing Our Surroundings
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 1 — First Steps (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** to look at the world around us with care — its houses, animals, people, birds, plants and cars — and to tell what we see happening.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the child's own surroundings — the home, the road to school and the yard they already live in.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the
exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an
image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture
of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does
with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and
while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is
dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the
student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph,
or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be
shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: What the Arts Are — the two families. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say what 'surroundings' means: houses, animals, people, birds, plants, cars and carts.
2. Name the sounds, colours and shapes of things at home and around you.
3. Watch something happening and tell the action: who is doing what.
4. Tell what you understood from watching, in your own words.

## What you need
Your eyes and ears, and a place to stand and watch — a doorway, a yard, a window.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — Stand at your door or your window. (lesson/lesson.md).
2. **The explanation** — the parts of lesson/lesson.md, in order.
   - ADD IMAGE [Picture 9002]: a picture of children outdoors looking at houses, animals, birds and trees — the student names each thing, its colour and its shape.
   - ADD AUDIO: a voice naming surroundings one by one — 'a house … a hen … a tree … a car …' — the student says after each: its colour, and whether it has its own sound.
3. **Say it to your companion** — Watch one thing happening near you with your companion. (lesson/lesson.md).
   - ADD VIDEO: the script in video-script.md, filmed (made later, when videos are made).
4. **Questions** — from questions/, served at the learner's level and teaching-mix (levels.md).
5. **Hands-on** — hands-on.md.
6. **Do It Yourself** — the doing block at the end of lesson/lesson.md.

## If the learner is stuck
- Names only things, never actions → ask 'and what is it DOING?' — the action completes the observation.
- Says 'surroundings' means only nature → the book's own list includes houses, people, cars and carts too.
