# Video script — Observing Our Surroundings

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Stand at the door. Look slowly.

A **house**. A **tree**. A **hen**. A **car**.
These are our **surroundings** — አካባቢ.

What colour? What shape? What sound?
The hen has a sound. The house stays still.

Now watch. The girl **is pushing** the wheelchair.
The children **are reading** together.
Who — is doing what? Tell it.

That is observing.
