# Lesson plan — Appreciating Ethiopian Traditional Theatre

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** First Steps · **Lesson:** Appreciating Ethiopian Traditional Theatre
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 1 — First Steps (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the Grade 3 guide's appreciation of Ethiopian traditional theatre — with its named traditional play extract, 'እኔ ነኝ ውሮ', placed by name and purpose (teacher-guide evidence).
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on teacher-guide evidence only — the Grade 3 guide, printed pp. 74–76.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the
exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an
image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture
of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does
with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and
while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is
dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the
student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph,
or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be
shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved (the gate: README, "Units, Lessons and Sub Lessons (ruled 2026-11-08)"):

1. **Traditional theatre as an art (teacher guide — evidence)** — appreciating Ethiopia's traditional theatre as an art — the community's own stage tradition, valued (Grade 3 teacher guide, printed pp. 74–76).
2. **The named play — 'እኔ ነኝ ውሮ' (teacher guide — evidence)** — the guide names a traditional play extract — 'እኔ ነኝ ውሮ' (§4.3.1) — placed by name and purpose only, never transcribed (Grade 3 teacher guide, printed p. 75).

## Before this lesson

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say what traditional theatre holds and how it is to be valued.
2. Name the play and say how it is placed.
