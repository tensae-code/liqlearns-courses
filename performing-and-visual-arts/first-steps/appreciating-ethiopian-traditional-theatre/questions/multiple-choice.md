# Appreciating Ethiopian Traditional Theatre — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The guide sets traditional theatre before the learner —
a) to be appreciated as an art  b) as a foreign import  c) as a sport
**Answer: a) to be appreciated as an art** — appreciation completes the guide's set.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Traditional theatre holds —
a) the community's stories, speech, humour and judgements  b) nothing of the community  c) only scenery
**Answer: a) the community's stories, speech, humour and judgements** — the community's own life is its content.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The named traditional play extract is —
a) 'እኔ ነኝ ውሮ'  b) unnamed  c) a foreign play
**Answer: a) 'እኔ ነኝ ውሮ'** — the title is placed as printed, §4.3.1.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The play is placed in this lesson —
a) by name and purpose only, never transcribed  b) in full text  c) as a summary of every scene
**Answer: a) by name and purpose only, never transcribed** — the standing song-and-play rule applies.
