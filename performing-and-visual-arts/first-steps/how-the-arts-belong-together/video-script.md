# Video script — How the Arts Belong Together

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

One coin. Two sides.

This side: **music, dance, acting** — the performing arts.
This side: **drawing, photograph** — the visual arts.
A coin with one side is **no coin**.

A song needs its **drawing**. Its **dance**. Its **actors**.
Then its message is **complete**.

Act a scene. Now **draw** it.
The message travelled. The arts **belong together**.
