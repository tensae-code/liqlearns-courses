# Hands-on — How the Arts Belong Together

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The travelling message
In a group of three: the first invents a tiny scene and acts it in silence. The second draws the scene. The third looks only at the drawing and sings or tells what it is about. Did the message arrive?

## One song, three arts
Take a song everyone knows. While it is sung, half the group dances it and half draws it. Afterwards lay the drawings out: the song, three ways at once.
