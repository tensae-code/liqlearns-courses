# How the Arts Belong Together — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Grade 1's last chapter teaches how which three arts relate?
a) music, drawing and drama  b) only drawing and photograph  c) only dance and games
**Answer: a) music, drawing and drama**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** 'Two sides of one coin' means —
a) the arts are all the same  b) performing and visual arts each need the other for a complete message  c) coins are used to pay artists
**Answer: b) performing and visual arts each need the other for a complete message**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The book's example: music is made more beautiful by —
a) being sung alone in silence  b) drawings, dance and actors  c) being written in a book
**Answer: b) drawings, dance and actors**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** An idea presented in drama can be —
a) put down in drawing  b) never shown again  c) only sung
**Answer: a) put down in drawing**

**5. (Sub lesson 4)** In Grade 1's classroom picture, the three strands appear as —
a) drawing at the floor, a drum beside them, instrument pictures on the wall  b) only a test on paper  c) a silent film
**Answer: a) drawing at the floor, a drum beside them, instrument pictures on the wall**

**6. (Sub lesson 5)** The Grade 5 chapter opens with which pair of ideas?
a) unity and difference  b) loud and soft  c) big and small
**Answer: a) unity and difference** — the aims name unity ideas and difference ideas in art.

**7. (Sub lesson 5)** Comparing unity and difference in art is —
a) one of the chapter's stated aims  b) forbidden  c) left to another subject
**Answer: a) one of the chapter's stated aims** — the aims include comparing them.

**8. (Sub lesson 6)** The guide's comparison says the three performing arts are united because they —
a) all happen before others, in time, and end  b) are all silent  c) are all drawn
**Answer: a) all happen before others, in time, and end** — that shared happening is their unity.

**9. (Sub lesson 6)** In the guide's comparison, dance's first material is —
a) the moving body  b) paint  c) clay
**Answer: a) the moving body** — dance works first in the moving body.

**10. (Sub lesson 7)** The Grade 3 closing activity asks you to show a sport you like —
a) performed with music  b) in silence only  c) drawn only, never done
**Answer: a) performed with music** — the activity joins the sport to music.

**11. (Sub lesson 7)** Figure 17 on the closing page draws —
a) a football game  b) a harbour  c) a market
**Answer: a) a football game** — the football drawing is the page's figure.
