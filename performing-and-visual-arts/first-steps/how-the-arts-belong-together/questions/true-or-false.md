# How the Arts Belong Together — true or false

Say if the line is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** A coin with only one side is still a whole coin.
**Answer: False** — that is the book's point — each side needs the other.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** A drawing can be acted, and a song can be drawn.
**Answer: True** — the arts are interrelated; messages travel between them.

**3. (Sub lesson 4)** Grade 1's summary says drawing and music share qualities, and both express love and appreciation.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 5)** The Grade 5 chapter also aims to show nations' cultural differences through the arts.
**Answer: True**

**5. (Sub lesson 6)** The guide compares music against dance and theatre, and dance against music and theatre in turn.
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 7)** The closing activity asks for the benefit of learning PE through the arts.
**Answer: True**

**7. (Sub lesson 8)** I can say what ✓ means in the Grade 5 self-check: 'I achieved it.'
**Answer: True** — ✓ is the book's 'I achieved it' mark.

**8. (Sub lesson 8)** I can leave a ✗ item and never return to it — the checklist is finished once marked.
**Answer: False** — crossed items are re-studied until they can honestly be ticked.

**9. (Sub lesson 8)** I can tick a statement I could not actually show or do.
**Answer: False** — a tick means the thing can really be done and shown; otherwise it is a cross and more study.
