# How the Arts Belong Together — short answer

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What two instruments stand side by side on Grade 1's chapter picture page?
**Answer:** The krar (ክራር) and the masenqo (መሰንቆ).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the weaving of the arts for, in Grade 3's words? Name two things.
**Answer:** Any two of: building skill; expressing oneself; knowing history and culture; developing children's creativity.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Which family is on each side of the coin?
**Answer:** Performing arts on one side; visual arts on the other.

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** What does Grade 2's closing check call the arts for each other?
**Answer:** Message-carriers — the arts serve each other as message-carriers.
