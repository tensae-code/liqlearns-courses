# Lesson plan — How the Arts Belong Together

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** First Steps · **Lesson:** How the Arts Belong Together
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 1 — First Steps (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** music, drawing and drama are not strangers — they are two sides of one coin, and one art can be turned into another.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on What the Arts Are — the two families — and shows the families holding hands.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the
exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved (the gate: README, "Units, Lessons and Sub Lessons (ruled 2026-11-08)"):

1. **The arts woven together** — The chapter openers: music, drawing and drama relate, and are worked together.
2. **Two sides of one coin** — Performing arts and visual arts each need the other for a complete message.
3. **Drama into drawing** — An idea presented in drama can be put down in drawing.
4. **Doing all three** — Song, drama and drawing done together in one classroom activity — with the books' reviews folded in.

5. **One or different? — the Grade 5 chapter opens (opening fold)** — the Grade 5 book's Chapter 4 aims: grasp unity ideas in art, present difference ideas through art, and compare them (Grade 5, printed p. 107).

6. **Music, dance and theatre — same and different (teacher guide — evidence)** — the Grade 5 guide compares the three performing arts: how the idea of music differs from and unites with the ideas of dance and theatre (Grade 5 teacher guide, printed pp. 93–94).

7. **Sport through the arts — the Grade 3 chapter closes (closing fold)** — the Grade 3 book's closing activity: list the benefit of learning PE through the arts, and show a sport you like performed with music — with its football-game drawing (Grade 3, printed p. 83).

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: What the Arts Are. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say how the arts belong together: each needs the other to give a complete message.
2. Explain 'two sides of one coin' with the music example.
3. Turn a drama scene into a drawing of the same scene.
4. Do all three together: sing, draw and act in one activity.

## What you need
Paper, a pencil, and your voice.

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks 'belong together' means 'are the same thing' → a coin's sides are different faces of ONE coin; the arts stay themselves, and complete each other.
- Cannot turn drama into drawing → freeze the scene at its strongest moment; draw that frozen picture.
