# Video script — Looking at Pictures

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Look at the picture. Slowly.

**Who** is there? A mother. Children.
**Where?** At home.
**What** is she doing? She is **serving food**.

Now — what does the picture **tell** us?
A family eats together. A mother cares.

That is reading a picture.
Who. Where. What. And what it tells us.
