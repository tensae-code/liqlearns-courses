# Hands-on — Looking at Pictures

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Read the wall
Find a picture or poster on a wall at home or school. Read it fully aloud to someone: who, where, what, and its message. If it is an advertisement or a warning, its message is its reason — find it.

## Make a picture that tells
Draw a scene with a clear message — children helping at home, a family reading, a farmer working. Show it to a friend without explaining. If they can tell its message, your picture speaks.
