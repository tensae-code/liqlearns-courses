# Looking at Pictures — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The first job when you look at a picture is —
a) to guess its message at once  b) to recognise and name what it shows  c) to turn the page quickly
**Answer: b) to recognise and name what it shows**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The book's mother-in-the-picture scene shows —
a) a mother serving food to her children  b) a farmer ploughing alone  c) children at a market
**Answer: a) a mother serving food to her children**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A picture's message is —
a) its colours only  b) what it tells us — what we understand from it  c) its frame
**Answer: b) what it tells us — what we understand from it**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The book's exercise asks you to say what you understood, and —
a) to copy the picture exactly  b) what things you do in your own surroundings  c) to close the book
**Answer: b) what things you do in your own surroundings**
