# Looking at Pictures — true or false

Say if the line is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A slow eye misses nothing; a fast eye misses half the picture.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A picture can tell something about family life.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Reading a picture stops at naming its things.
**Answer: False** — it goes on to the picture's message, and to your own life.
