# Hands-on — The Arts Connect to Everything

The lesson with your hands, your feet and your voice together, not only your eyes.

## Connection hunt
In groups, each group takes one other schoolbook and hunts its arts: pictures, diagrams, stories, songs, patterns. Report back with page numbers — the integration lessons verified in the class's own books.

## Woven timetable
Draw the class timetable as a woven cloth: subjects as one set of threads, arts as the crossing set. Where a thread crosses, write the connection found in the hunt.
