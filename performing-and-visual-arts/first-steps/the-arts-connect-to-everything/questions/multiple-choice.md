# The Arts Connect to Everything — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Visual arts integrate with other subjects by…
  a) Serving the whole curriculum with concepts and skills b) Staying locked in the art room c) Replacing other subjects
**Answer: a) Serving the whole curriculum with concepts and skills** — integration — visual-art concepts and skills serving all subjects.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Performing arts carry other subjects' content through…
  a) Drama, song and movement b) Silence and stillness only c) Examinations

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The Grade 10 closing units study the arts in…

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Grade 8's Unit 5 (contents) turns to…
