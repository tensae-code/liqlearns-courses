# The Arts Connect to Everything — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is visual art called, for the other subjects, in this part?
**Answer: The picture-language all the others speak.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What do performing arts add to a subject?
**Answer: Memory and feeling — content enters through them and stays.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which built unit of this course is the younger form of this study?
**Answer: Culture and Values.**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Grade 7's Unit 5 relation topic joins the arts with what?
**Answer: Other subjects — performing and visual arts and their relation with them.**

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** Name the four stops of this part.
**Answer:** The link map; mathematics (the scale law); social science and technology (history and order passed down); the teaching process and its institutions.

**6. (Sub lesson 6)** Name the five Unit 5 connections in the Grade 8 contents.
**Answer:** Music with other subjects; dance in other subjects; music connected to learning (music and science, music history, integration's meaning); theatre and film in other subjects; visual arts in other subjects.

**7. (Sub lesson 7)** Name visual art's three science meeting points in the Grade 8 book.
**Answer:** Spectrum-colour creation; things' natural forms; colour-combination reactions.
