# The Arts Connect to Everything — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is visual art called, for the other subjects, in this part?
**Answer: The picture-language all the others speak.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What do performing arts add to a subject?
**Answer: Memory and feeling — content enters through them and stays.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which built unit of this course is the younger form of this study?
**Answer: Culture and Values.**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Grade 7's Unit 5 relation topic joins the arts with what?
**Answer: Other subjects — performing and visual arts and their relation with them.**

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** Name the four stops of this part.
**Answer:** The link map; mathematics (the scale law); social science and technology (history and order passed down); the teaching process and its institutions.

**6. (Sub lesson 6)** Name the five Unit 5 connections in the Grade 8 contents.
**Answer:** Music with other subjects; dance in other subjects; music connected to learning (music and science, music history, integration's meaning); theatre and film in other subjects; visual arts in other subjects.

**7. (Sub lesson 7)** Name visual art's three science meeting points in the Grade 8 book.
**Answer:** Spectrum-colour creation; things' natural forms; colour-combination reactions.

**8. (Sub lesson 8)** Name two contributions the Chapter 5 opener asks of theatre, film and photography.
**Answer:** Their contribution to heritage — carrying and protecting it — within the chapter's work of teaching useful issues through the arts (any two of the opener's theatre/film/photography aims).

**9. (Sub lesson 9)** From the exercise: name three visual methods for teaching traffic, gender or health.
**Answer:** Any three visual teaching methods of this course's kind — e.g. posters, emblems and teaching pictures (drawings/paintings) made to carry the issue.

**10. (Sub lesson 10)** State Chapter 5's second aim in your own words.
**Answer:** Explain how art relates to the other school subjects.

**11. (Sub lesson 11)** List Grade 7 Chapter 5's three self-check items.
**Answer:** Decorating home/environment with pottery and ceramics; visual art's links, benefits and teaching use; current issues' nature, divisions and benefit.

**12. (Sub lesson 11)** How is Chapter 5's closing exercise divided?
**Answer:** 3 true/false and 3 short answers.

**13. (Sub lesson 12)** Name the two families whose works Chapter 5's learners appreciate.
**Answer:** Visual arts and performing arts.

**14. (Sub lesson 13)** Name five benefits from the summary's long roll.
**Answer:** Any five of: speech ability; self-confidence; expressing creativity; easy communication; thinking ability; ethics; working-together culture; knowing local and historical people's history; making the community grasp current issues; growing the country's economy.

**15. (Sub lesson 13)** Explain, with an example, art's contribution to transmitting current issues' messages — the exercises' third item.
**Answer:** An explanation in the learner's words with a concrete example (a poster, song or play carrying a current issue to its community), as the exercise directs.
