This lesson is Đàn Đá: The Vietnamese Lithophone.

The đàn đá — Vietnam's stone instrument, tuned stones struck for sound. — as Vietnam's school books teach it: The đàn đá (stone instrument, lithophone) — the đàn đá is one of the most ancient kinds of instrument of Vietnam; the instrument has a simple structure, made from stone bars with different sizes, long, short, thick and thin: a long, thick stone bar gives a deep sound, a short, thin stone bar gives a clear, high sound; the player uses a wooden mallet to strike the stone bars to create the sound; the instrument has a bright timbre that carries far, and today the đàn đá is still used in the festival activities of the Tây Nguyên compatriots (now belonging to the South Central region).

Watch, listen — then say it back in your own words.
