## Sub lesson 1 — Vietnam Grade 9 Âm nhạc 9

The đàn đá (stone instrument, lithophone) — the đàn đá is one of the most ancient kinds of instrument of Vietnam; the instrument has a simple structure, made from stone bars with different sizes, long, short, thick and thin: a long, thick stone bar gives a deep sound, a short, thin stone bar gives a clear, high sound; the player uses a wooden mallet to strike the stone bars to create the sound; the instrument has a bright timbre that carries far, and today the đàn đá is still used in the festival activities of the Tây Nguyên compatriots (now belonging to the South Central region)
