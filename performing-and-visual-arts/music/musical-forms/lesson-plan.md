# Lesson plan — Musical Forms

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Music · **Lesson:** Musical Forms
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 3 — Music (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** How pieces are shaped — musical forms (A, B and beyond), rondo and variations, phrase analysis and the building of a work.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the course's earlier lessons in its title; recommended first, never forced.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of ..." in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Forma muzicală** — Theme 3, Forma muzicală — what musical form is: the way a work's content is organised — one-part, two-part, three-part, rondo, variations and free forms.
2. **Forma strofică and Forma strofică cu refren** — Theme 3, Forma strofică and Forma strofică cu refren — the strophic song form (verses only) and the strophic form with refrain (verse + chorus), in "Moldova", Musicescu's "Răsai, lună" and Porumbescu's march "Haideți, haideți!".
3. **Forma monopartită, bipartită, tripartită** — Theme 3, Forma monopartită, bipartită, tripartită — one-part form (Miculi's and Musicescu's hora piano pieces), two-part form (Tchaikovsky's "Neapolitan Song", Bach's "Badinerie") and three-part form with middle section and reprise (Prokofiev's "Rain and Rainbow").
4. **Forma rondo** — Theme 3, Forma rondo — the rondo: a refrain alternating with episodes like a circle dance (Mozart's "Rondo in Turkish Style", Boccherini's Rondo).
5. **Tema cu variațiuni** — Theme 3, Tema cu variațiuni — theme and variations: a theme restated in changed forms, from lăutari practice to Mozart's variations on a French song and Alexei Stîrcea's 11 piano variations on the folk song "Primăvară, primăvară".
6. **Forma liberă (deschisă)** — Theme 3, Forma liberă (deschisă) — free (open) form: parts following freely yet joined by one message, in Tchaikovsky's "Italian Capriccio" ending with a tarantella.
7. **Kabalevski's "Galop of the Comedians"** — Unit 2, Kabalevski's "Galop of the Comedians" — a Presto gallop in three-part ABA form, its second theme on xylophone, learned by mapping its form.
8. **Forma monopartită** — Unit 3, Forma monopartită — the one-part form: one mood, one melody developed; in songs, the strophic form and the strophic form with refrain (Kabalevski's "Little Song").
9. **Forma bipartită** — Unit 3, Forma bipartită — the two-part form, with reprise or simple, heard in Schumann's "Soldiers' March" and Tchaikovsky's "Morning Prayer".
10. **Forma tripartită** — Unit 3, Forma tripartită — the three-part form A+B+A built on contrast, often with introduction and coda; Schubert's "Musical Moment" No. 3.
11. **Forme muzicale compuse** — Unit 3, Forme muzicale compuse — compound forms built from simple ones: the rondo (A+B+A+C+A…), the sonata and the free fantasy; Chopin's Waltz in A minor heard as a rondo.
12. **Temă cu variațiuni** — Unit 3, Temă cu variațiuni — theme and variations: a theme stated, then transformed again and again — Mozart's 12 Variations on "Ah, vous dirai-je, maman" and the Andante of Haydn's "Surprise" Symphony.
