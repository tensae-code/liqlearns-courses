# Musical Forms

## Do it first

Name one thing that belongs in this lesson — How pieces are shaped — musical forms (A, B and beyond), rondo and variations, phrase analysis and the building of a work. Say what it is in your own words. If you can name one, you are already inside the lesson.

## Sub lesson 1 — Forma muzicală

Theme 3, Forma muzicală — what musical form is: the way a work's content is organised — one-part, two-part, three-part, rondo, variations and free forms.

## Sub lesson 2 — Forma strofică and Forma strofică cu refren

Theme 3, Forma strofică and Forma strofică cu refren — the strophic song form (verses only) and the strophic form with refrain (verse + chorus), in "Moldova", Musicescu's "Răsai, lună" and Porumbescu's march "Haideți, haideți!".

## Sub lesson 3 — Forma monopartită, bipartită, tripartită

Theme 3, Forma monopartită, bipartită, tripartită — one-part form (Miculi's and Musicescu's hora piano pieces), two-part form (Tchaikovsky's "Neapolitan Song", Bach's "Badinerie") and three-part form with middle section and reprise (Prokofiev's "Rain and Rainbow").

## Sub lesson 4 — Forma rondo

Theme 3, Forma rondo — the rondo: a refrain alternating with episodes like a circle dance (Mozart's "Rondo in Turkish Style", Boccherini's Rondo).

## Sub lesson 5 — Tema cu variațiuni

Theme 3, Tema cu variațiuni — theme and variations: a theme restated in changed forms, from lăutari practice to Mozart's variations on a French song and Alexei Stîrcea's 11 piano variations on the folk song "Primăvară, primăvară".

## Sub lesson 6 — Forma liberă (deschisă)

Theme 3, Forma liberă (deschisă) — free (open) form: parts following freely yet joined by one message, in Tchaikovsky's "Italian Capriccio" ending with a tarantella.

## Sub lesson 7 — Kabalevski's "Galop of the Comedians"

Unit 2, Kabalevski's "Galop of the Comedians" — a Presto gallop in three-part ABA form, its second theme on xylophone, learned by mapping its form.

## Sub lesson 8 — Forma monopartită

Unit 3, Forma monopartită — the one-part form: one mood, one melody developed; in songs, the strophic form and the strophic form with refrain (Kabalevski's "Little Song").

## Sub lesson 9 — Forma bipartită

Unit 3, Forma bipartită — the two-part form, with reprise or simple, heard in Schumann's "Soldiers' March" and Tchaikovsky's "Morning Prayer".

## Sub lesson 10 — Forma tripartită

Unit 3, Forma tripartită — the three-part form A+B+A built on contrast, often with introduction and coda; Schubert's "Musical Moment" No. 3.

## Sub lesson 11 — Forme muzicale compuse

Unit 3, Forme muzicale compuse — compound forms built from simple ones: the rondo (A+B+A+C+A…), the sonata and the free fantasy; Chopin's Waltz in A minor heard as a rondo.

## Sub lesson 12 — Temă cu variațiuni

Unit 3, Temă cu variațiuni — theme and variations: a theme stated, then transformed again and again — Mozart's 12 Variations on "Ah, vous dirai-je, maman" and the Andante of Haydn's "Surprise" Symphony.
