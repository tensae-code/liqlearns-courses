# Folk Instruments of Belarus — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the five folk instruments of this lesson.
**Answer: Dudka, tsymbaly, harmonik, buben and skrypka.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Give one ensemble of three the book names.
**Answer: Tsymbaly, dudka and buben — or skrypka, tsymbaly and harmonik.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Which instrument is played loud, bright, hushed or mysterious?
**Answer: The buben.**
