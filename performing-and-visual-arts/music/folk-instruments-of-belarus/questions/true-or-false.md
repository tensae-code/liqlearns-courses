# Folk Instruments of Belarus — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Dudka players are called dudary.
**Answer: True**.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The harmonik is the instrument of dance tunes and refrains.
**Answer: True**.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Folk ensembles of two players are the book's rule.
**Answer: False** — never fewer than three.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Folk tunes in the book imitate the wind, birdsong and animal voices.
**Answer: True**.
