# Video script — Folk Instruments of Belarus

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Five instruments, made by folk masters for centuries.

The **dudka**, the shepherds' pipe. The **tsymbaly**, humming like a waking pine forest. The **harmonik** for dancing. The **buben**, loud or mysterious. And the **skrypka** — the people's queen of music.

Three together, at least — that is a Belarusian folk ensemble.
