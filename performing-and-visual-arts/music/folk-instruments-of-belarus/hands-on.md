# Hands-on — Folk Instruments of Belarus

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Instrument parade
Make the five instruments from classroom things: a paper tube for the dudka, a box with rubber bands and sticks for the tsymbaly, a shaken box for the buben. Hold each up as its name is called and make its sound-word.

## Count the timbres
Play or sing a short dance tune three ways — humming, clapping, stamping — and count the timbres, the way the book counts the quadrille's.
