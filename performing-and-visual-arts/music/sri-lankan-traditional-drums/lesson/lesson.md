## Do it first

## Sub lesson 1 — Let's play rhythm patterns on the traditional drums

The drum language in writing — rhythm patterns for Davula, Thammattama, Maha Davula and Udekki set out in X-and-rest charts, beat by beat, and played in ensemble

## Sub lesson 2 — the local drum instruments

The island's drum set studied and then played — Davula, Thammattama, Gataberaya, Pahatharata Beraya and the rest of the local drums named, their parts learned, their rhythms performed (joins Book 5's drum placement)
