# Hands-on — Making Your Own Instruments

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Build a shaker
Put recycled filling into an empty yoghurt cup, seal it, and decorate it. Shake it soft, shake it loud — hear how the filling changes the sound.

## Accompany the song
Play your shakers together to accompany a recycling song — keep the beat, stop with the song, start with it.
