# Making Your Own Instruments — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The children make their own…
a) shakers  b) drums  c) flutes
**Answer: a) shakers** — from empty yoghurt cups and recycled materials.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The shakers are made from…
a) empty yoghurt cups and recycled materials  b) bought plastic kits  c) clay
**Answer: a) empty yoghurt cups and recycled materials**.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The shakers are played to accompany…
a) a recycling song  b) a lullaby  c) no song at all
**Answer: a) a recycling song**.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This music work teaches…
a) recycling  b) spelling  c) counting only
**Answer: a) recycling** — learned through music, in the guide's themes strategy.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** A home-made shaker is…
a) a real instrument you can play  b) only a toy model  c) a picture of an instrument
**Answer: a) a real instrument you can play**.
