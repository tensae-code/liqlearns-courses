# Making Your Own Instruments

## Do it first

Quick start: from the title alone, guess what this lesson's work is. Then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — Shakers from recycled things

Recycling is learned through music: **children make their own shakers from empty yoghurt cups and recycled materials** — a waste thing becomes an instrument in the child's own hands.

## Sub lesson 2 — Playing them to a recycling song

The home-made shakers are not shelf ornaments: the children **play them to accompany a recycling song** — the instrument, the theme and the song teach one thing together.
