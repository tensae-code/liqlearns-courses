# Video script — Byzantine Chant

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is Byzantine Chant.

Greek ecclesiastical chant — its character and content, and its festal hymns learned by ear. — as Greece's school books teach it: chant's history from the first Christians to the three teachers of Madyta; church music is purely vocal — no instruments are used.

Watch, listen — then say it back in your own words.
