# Lesson plan — Byzantine Chant

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Music · **Lesson:** Byzantine Chant
**Place in the curriculum:** Music (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Greek ecclesiastical chant — its character and content, and its festal hymns learned by ear.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this course in the same title.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Α΄ Γυμνασίου Ενότητα 1: Σύντομη ιστορική επισκόπηση της εκκλησιαστικής μουσικής (A short history of church music)** — chant's history from the first Christians to the three teachers of Madyta; church music is purely vocal — no instruments are used
2. **Α΄ Γυμνασίου: Ύμνοι εξ ακοής (Hymns learned by ear, Grade 7)** — the year's hymn repertoire sung by ear alongside the notation — named by title only: «Σώσον, Κύριε, τὸν λαόν σου», the short Trisagion, «Τῇ ὑπερμάχῳ», «Τὴν Τιμιωτέραν», and the Christmas and Theophany hymns
3. **Β΄ Γυμνασίου: Ύμνοι εξ ακοής (Hymns learned by ear, Grade 8)** — the year's repertoire by ear — named by title only: the Resurrection Evlogitaria in three parts, «Ἀπόστολοι ἐκ περάτων» short and slow, the Short Doxology in two parts
4. **Γ΄ Γυμνασίου Ενότητες 4-11: σύντομο στιχηραρικό είδος (The short sticheraric kind)** — the marks of the kinds of melopoiia; the short sticheraric kind, and the 1st and 4th echoi in it — theory, theseis, melodies and musical accentuation; plagal 4th sticheraric
5. **Γ΄ Γυμνασίου Ενότητες 12-24: ήχοι βαρύς, γ΄, πλ. α΄, β΄, πλ. β΄ στιχηραρικοί (The remaining echoi, sticheraric)** — barys, 3rd, plagal 1st, 2nd and plagal 2nd echoi in the short sticheraric kind; digorgon and trigorgon; interpreting the theseis by the oral tradition; the system; dotted gorga and time-keeping (chroniki agogi)
6. **Γ΄ Γυμνασίου: Ύμνοι εξ ακοής (Hymns learned by ear, Grade 9)** — the final year's repertoire by ear, with prosomoia (model-melody hymns) — named by title only: «Τοῖς μαθηταῖς συνέλθωμεν», the slow and the funeral Trisagion, «Ἐπεφάνης σήμερον τῇ Οἰκουμένῃ»
7. **Εισαγωγή και Α΄ Λυκείου Μάθ. 1: Εκκλησιαστική μουσική — χαρακτήρας και περιεχόμενο (Introduction; Ecclesiastical music — its character and content)** — what this music is and how it is learned — by ear and participation as much as by the book
8. **Β΄ Λυκείου Μάθ. 16–24: Το σύντομο στιχηραρικό είδος στους ήχους α΄, πλ. α΄, πλ. δ΄ (The short sticheraric kind in Echoi 1, Plagal 1, Plagal 4)** — chanting the stichera in the short style
9. **Γ΄ Λυκείου Μάθ. 1–13: Το σύντομο στιχηραρικό είδος στους υπόλοιπους ήχους (The short sticheraric kind in the remaining echoi)** — the short sticheraric style completed across all echoi
10. **Γ΄ Λυκείου Μάθ. 14–21: Το αργό ειρμολογικό είδος (The slow heirmologic kind)** — chanting the heirmoi slowly, echos by echos
11. **Γ΄ Λυκείου Μάθ. 22–24: Το παπαδικό είδος (The papadic kind)** — the elaborate papadic chant — the course's summit
12. **Παράρτημα: Σχεδιαγράμματα ακολουθιών Εσπερινού, Όρθρου και Θείας Λειτουργίας (Appendix: outline diagrams of Vespers, Orthros and the Divine Liturgy)** — the order of the services the chant belongs to — where every hymn stands
13. **Α΄ Gymnasium Lesson 1: a short historical survey of ecclesiastical (Byzantine) music** — Greek ecclesiastical chant — its character and content, and its festal hymns learned by ear.
14. **the hymns-by-ear strand across all three years: each lesson pairs its notation topic with named festal hymns learned by hearing (e.g. Σῶσον Κύριε, Χριστὸς γεννᾶται, Χριστὸς Ἀνέστη, Τῇ ὑπερμάχῳ) — named only** — Greek ecclesiastical chant — its character and content, and its festal hymns learned by ear.
15. **Α΄ Lyceum Lesson 1: Ecclesiastical music — its character and content** — Greek ecclesiastical chant — its character and content, and its festal hymns learned by ear.
