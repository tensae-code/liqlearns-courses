# Hands-on — Byzantine Chant

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the lesson's thing
Work through the lesson's first part yourself: Α΄ Γυμνασίου Ενότητα 1: Σύντομη ιστορική επισκόπηση της εκκλησιαστικής μουσικής (A short history of church music). Show a companion what you made or did, and say what the book calls it.

## Show and tell
Each learner shows their work and names the part of the lesson it comes from. The companion checks the name against the lesson.
