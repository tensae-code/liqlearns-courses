# Byzantine Chant

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about byzantine chant. Then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — Α΄ Γυμνασίου Ενότητα 1: Σύντομη ιστορική επισκόπηση της εκκλησιαστικής μουσικής (A short history of church music)

chant's history from the first Christians to the three teachers of Madyta; church music is purely vocal — no instruments are used

## Sub lesson 2 — Α΄ Γυμνασίου: Ύμνοι εξ ακοής (Hymns learned by ear, Grade 7)

the year's hymn repertoire sung by ear alongside the notation — named by title only: «Σώσον, Κύριε, τὸν λαόν σου», the short Trisagion, «Τῇ ὑπερμάχῳ», «Τὴν Τιμιωτέραν», and the Christmas and Theophany hymns

## Sub lesson 3 — Β΄ Γυμνασίου: Ύμνοι εξ ακοής (Hymns learned by ear, Grade 8)

the year's repertoire by ear — named by title only: the Resurrection Evlogitaria in three parts, «Ἀπόστολοι ἐκ περάτων» short and slow, the Short Doxology in two parts

## Sub lesson 4 — Γ΄ Γυμνασίου Ενότητες 4-11: σύντομο στιχηραρικό είδος (The short sticheraric kind)

the marks of the kinds of melopoiia; the short sticheraric kind, and the 1st and 4th echoi in it — theory, theseis, melodies and musical accentuation; plagal 4th sticheraric

## Sub lesson 5 — Γ΄ Γυμνασίου Ενότητες 12-24: ήχοι βαρύς, γ΄, πλ. α΄, β΄, πλ. β΄ στιχηραρικοί (The remaining echoi, sticheraric)

barys, 3rd, plagal 1st, 2nd and plagal 2nd echoi in the short sticheraric kind; digorgon and trigorgon; interpreting the theseis by the oral tradition; the system; dotted gorga and time-keeping (chroniki agogi)

## Sub lesson 6 — Γ΄ Γυμνασίου: Ύμνοι εξ ακοής (Hymns learned by ear, Grade 9)

the final year's repertoire by ear, with prosomoia (model-melody hymns) — named by title only: «Τοῖς μαθηταῖς συνέλθωμεν», the slow and the funeral Trisagion, «Ἐπεφάνης σήμερον τῇ Οἰκουμένῃ»

## Sub lesson 7 — Εισαγωγή και Α΄ Λυκείου Μάθ. 1: Εκκλησιαστική μουσική — χαρακτήρας και περιεχόμενο (Introduction; Ecclesiastical music — its character and content)

what this music is and how it is learned — by ear and participation as much as by the book

## Sub lesson 8 — Β΄ Λυκείου Μάθ. 16–24: Το σύντομο στιχηραρικό είδος στους ήχους α΄, πλ. α΄, πλ. δ΄ (The short sticheraric kind in Echoi 1, Plagal 1, Plagal 4)

chanting the stichera in the short style

## Sub lesson 9 — Γ΄ Λυκείου Μάθ. 1–13: Το σύντομο στιχηραρικό είδος στους υπόλοιπους ήχους (The short sticheraric kind in the remaining echoi)

the short sticheraric style completed across all echoi

## Sub lesson 10 — Γ΄ Λυκείου Μάθ. 14–21: Το αργό ειρμολογικό είδος (The slow heirmologic kind)

chanting the heirmoi slowly, echos by echos

## Sub lesson 11 — Γ΄ Λυκείου Μάθ. 22–24: Το παπαδικό είδος (The papadic kind)

the elaborate papadic chant — the course's summit

## Sub lesson 12 — Παράρτημα: Σχεδιαγράμματα ακολουθιών Εσπερινού, Όρθρου και Θείας Λειτουργίας (Appendix: outline diagrams of Vespers, Orthros and the Divine Liturgy)

the order of the services the chant belongs to — where every hymn stands
