# Schlager — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Schlager are…
a) German hit songs of the Weimar years  b) Icelandic sea songs  c) Sousa marches
**Answer: a) German hit songs of the Weimar years**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Schlager were well played because the bands held…
a) classically trained musicians  b) only beginners  c) sailors
**Answer: a) classically trained musicians**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The age is the…
a) Weimar Republic  b) Baroque  c) Roaring Twenties only
**Answer: a) Weimar Republic**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** The star named is…
a) Marlene Dietrich  b) Mary Lou Williams  c) Nína Sveinsdóttir
**Answer: a) Marlene Dietrich**

**5. (Sub lesson 1)** Cabaret belongs to this world of…
a) stage song and satire  b) silent prayer  c) marching
**Answer: a) stage song and satire**
