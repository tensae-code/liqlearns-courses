# Rock Music

## Do it first

Name one thing that belongs in this lesson — Rock music — its story and sound. Say what it is in your own words. If you can name one, you are already inside the lesson.

## Sub lesson 1 — rock music

rock music: born in the 1950s USA and Britain on electric guitar, bass, drums and a strong beat — the music of youthful energy and rebellion; The Beatles ("Hey Jude", "Yesterday", "Let It Be") and The Rolling Stones ("Satisfaction").

## Sub lesson 2 — Рок музика

Рок музика — rock music: from the rock'n'roll of the 1940s–50s, fed by blues, country, classical and electronic music; folk rock, blues rock and jazz-rock fusion; the 1970s' soft rock, heavy metal, hard, progressive and punk rock; the 1980s' synth-pop, hardcore and alternative; the 1990s' grunge, britpop, indie and nu metal; the electric guitar at its centre — Hendrix its virtuoso — and the rock band of guitarist, singer, bassist and drummer.
