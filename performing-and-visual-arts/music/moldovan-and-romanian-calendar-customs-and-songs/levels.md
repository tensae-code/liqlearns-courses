## Sub lesson 1 — Caloianul and Paparuda: calling the rain
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: say when the rain customs are practised — after Easter into June — and tell the two apart: the Caloian is the children's clay-doll play sung for the rain gate to open; Paparuda is the girls' leaf-and-flower dance going house to house.
