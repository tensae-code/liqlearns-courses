# Moldovan and Romanian Calendar Customs and Songs — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Caloian doll is modelled from clay.
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Paparuda is performed indoors, sitting down.
**Answer: False** — it is a dance performed going house to house.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Drăgaica is one of the old customs dedicated to the Earth and the Sun.
**Answer: True.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The rain customs belong to winter.
**Answer: False** — they are practised after Easter into June.
