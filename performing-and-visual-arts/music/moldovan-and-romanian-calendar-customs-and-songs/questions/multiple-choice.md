# Moldovan and Romanian Calendar Customs and Songs — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Caloianul and Paparuda are practised…
a) after Easter into June  b) at Christmas  c) at harvest only
**Answer: a) after Easter into June** — to call the rain and well-being.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The Caloian is played by…
a) children, with a modelled clay doll  b) soldiers  c) one singer alone
**Answer: a) children, with a modelled clay doll** — a kind of theatrical representation.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** In the Caloian, the children sing for…
a) the rain gate to open  b) the school bell  c) a wedding
**Answer: a) the rain gate to open**.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Paparuda is danced by girls dressed in…
a) leaves and flowers  b) uniforms  c) cloaks
**Answer: a) leaves and flowers** — going house to house, singing to call the rain.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Cununa grâului means…
a) the Wheat Crown  b) the Rain Gate  c) the Clay Doll
**Answer: a) the Wheat Crown**.
