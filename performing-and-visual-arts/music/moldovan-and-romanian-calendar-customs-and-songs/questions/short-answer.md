# Moldovan and Romanian Calendar Customs and Songs — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What do the Caloian and Paparuda customs call for?
**Answer: Rain — and well-being.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name three other customs dedicated to the Earth and the Sun.
**Answer: Any three of: Călușul, Sânzienele, Rusaliile, Drăgaica, Cununa grâului.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** How does Paparuda move through the village?
**Answer: House to house, the girls dancing and singing to call the rain.**
