# Video script — Moldovan and Romanian Calendar Customs and Songs

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

After Easter, into June — the land is thirsty.

Children model a clay doll — the **Caloian** — and sing: open, rain gate, open!

Girls in leaves and flowers dance house to house — **Paparuda** — singing for rain.

Old customs, sung for the Earth and the Sun.
