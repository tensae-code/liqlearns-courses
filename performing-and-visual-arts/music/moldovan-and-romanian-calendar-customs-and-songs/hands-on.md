# Hands-on — Moldovan and Romanian Calendar Customs and Songs

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Rain-gate song
In a small group, act the Caloian in summary: show the clay doll (modelled in the clay lesson), and sing or chant a short rain-calling line of your own making for the rain gate to open. Songs are placed by kind and purpose only — make your own words, do not copy a book song.

## Leaves and flowers
Dress one dancer in leaves and flowers, Paparuda-style, and walk a short house-to-house round in the classroom, singing your rain call at each 'house'.
