# Moldovan and Romanian Calendar Customs and Songs

## Do it first

Quick start: the fields are dry and the village needs rain. In the old Romanian way, who goes out to call it — and how? Then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — Caloianul and Paparuda: calling the rain

**Caloianul** and **Paparuda** are two very old Romanian customs practised **after Easter into June** to **call the rain and well-being**. The **Caloian** is a kind of **theatrical representation played by children**: they **model a clay doll** and **sing for the rain gate to open**. **Paparuda** is a **dance performed by girls dressed in leaves and flowers**, going **house to house singing** to call the rain. (The clay caloian doll itself is modelled in the Making with Clay and Paper lesson.)

## Sub lesson 2 — More customs for the Earth and the Sun

The book names **other old Romanian customs dedicated to the Earth and the Sun**: **Călușul, Sânzienele, Rusaliile, Drăgaica** and **Cununa grâului — the Wheat Crown**. Like the rain customs, they belong to the year's turning and to the community's care for its land.
