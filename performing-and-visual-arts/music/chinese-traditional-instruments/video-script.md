This lesson is Chinese Traditional Instruments.

China's traditional instruments met through listening — the pieces and sounds by which each one is known. — as China's school books teach it: The suona — a Hebei folk tune played by different-sized suonas in turn over a folk band, picturing the lively jujube-harvest scene; the suona is a loud, bright Chinese wind instrument; Purple Bamboo Tune played by a Chinese folk ensemble — a Shanghai-opera tune in Jiangnan style picturing festival joy; memorising its melody and joining in with body percussion; China's bamboo instruments — bamboo board zithers high/middle/low and bamboo tube zithers that form a bamboo orchestra, and a Miao lusheng tune; trying to make a bamboo instrument yourself.

Watch, listen — then say it back in your own words.
