# Hands-on — Making Traditional Instruments

The lesson with your hands, your feet and your voice together, not only your eyes.

## Material voices
Tap wood, stretched cloth, a hollow pot and a metal tin. Rank their voices: deepest to brightest, and match each to an instrument family.

## Maker interview
Ask a local maker or elder: what material, what first step, what test of sound? Write the three answers.
