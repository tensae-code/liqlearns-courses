# Making Traditional Instruments — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.


**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The kebero is made from…
  a) A hollowed great trunk, hide and cloth b) Bamboo and brass c) Glass and string
**Answer: a) A hollowed great trunk, hide and cloth** — trunk for depth, hide for the drum's speech.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The washint's material is…
  a) Bamboo b) Iron c) Clay
**Answer: a) Bamboo** — the bamboo flute — its hollow is its voice.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The building order runs…
  a) Hollow, stretch, string, tune b) Tune, sell, hollow, paint c) String, break, hide, sing
**Answer: a) Hollow, stretch, string, tune** — body first, skin next, strings next, tuning last.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Instrument-making knowledge passes…
  a) Maker to maker, proved by sound b) Only through printed manuals c) Not at all — each maker invents alone
**Answer: a) Maker to maker, proved by sound** — indigenous knowledge: carried in hands and ears, tested at every occasion.
