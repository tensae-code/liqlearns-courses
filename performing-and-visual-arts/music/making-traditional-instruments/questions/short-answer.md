# Making Traditional Instruments — short answer

Answer in a short sentence or a short list.


**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why is material choice the maker's first knowledge?
**Answer:** Because each material holds its voice — depth, breath or tension — and the instrument can only sing what its material allows.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** When is a maker's knowledge proved?
**Answer:** When the finished instrument sounds right at its occasion — drum, flute or lyre serving its song.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Do it and show it — the Grade 8 Chapter 1 exercise's practical: make a locally used traditional instrument. What must the finished work be?
**Answer:** A playable instrument of a kind used locally, made by the learner — the exercise's own practical task, done in this lesson's making work.
