# Lesson plan — Armenian Folk Songs

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Music · **Lesson:** Armenian Folk Songs
**Place in the curriculum:** Music (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Armenian Folk Songs — Armenia's own teaching of this topic, kept as named parts: "The wooden horse" (Փայտե ձիուկ, words by Komitas) and Pashinyan's "The Horseman" heard — a folk song and a piano picture of riding
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the course's earlier lessons in its title; recommended first, never forced.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of ..." in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **"The wooden horse" (Փայտե ձիուկ, words by Komitas) and Pashinyan's "The Horseman" heard** — "The wooden horse" (Փայտե ձիուկ, words by Komitas) and Pashinyan's "The Horseman" heard — a folk song and a piano picture of riding
2. **"Dombari" (Դոմբարի)** — "Dombari" (Դոմբարի) — an Armenian folk harvest song: "Basen is kind — let this year be plentiful", sung and played as a game
3. **"Alagyaz" (Ալագյազ)** — "Alagyaz" (Ալագյազ) — the folk song of the clouded mountain, with Martin Hakoghlyan's paintings "Alagyaz" and "Komitas" looked at
4. **What is a FOLK SONG** — What is a FOLK SONG — the song, music and dance the people themselves create; the song "The sky has clouded" (Երկինքն ամպել է)
5. **Armenian national songs and dances** — Armenian national songs and dances — what a national song and dance is, and what else it can be called
6. **The peasant songs (գեղջկական երգեր)** — The peasant songs (գեղջկական երգեր) — the village's own genre of song
7. **ARRANGEMENTS OF FOLK AND CLASSICAL WORKS** — ARRANGEMENTS OF FOLK AND CLASSICAL WORKS — how folk songs are arranged (մշակում) for new voices and instruments
8. **Project** — Project: THE LOVE THEME IN ARMENIAN FOLK SONGS — work from materials on the internet or in books (project proposal 2)

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
