# Armenian Folk Songs

## Do it first

Name one thing that belongs in this lesson — Armenian Folk Songs — Armenia's own teaching of this topic, kept as named parts: "The wooden horse" (Փայտե ձիուկ, words by Komitas) and Pashinyan's "The Horseman" heard — a folk song and a piano picture of riding. Say what it is in your own words. If you can name one, you are already inside the lesson.

## Sub lesson 1 — "The wooden horse" (Փայտե ձիուկ, words by Komitas) and Pashinyan's "The Horseman" heard

"The wooden horse" (Փայտե ձիուկ, words by Komitas) and Pashinyan's "The Horseman" heard — a folk song and a piano picture of riding.

## Sub lesson 2 — "Dombari" (Դոմբարի)

"Dombari" (Դոմբարի) — an Armenian folk harvest song: "Basen is kind — let this year be plentiful", sung and played as a game.

## Sub lesson 3 — "Alagyaz" (Ալագյազ)

"Alagyaz" (Ալագյազ) — the folk song of the clouded mountain, with Martin Hakoghlyan's paintings "Alagyaz" and "Komitas" looked at.

## Sub lesson 4 — What is a FOLK SONG

What is a FOLK SONG — the song, music and dance the people themselves create; the song "The sky has clouded" (Երկինքն ամպել է).

## Sub lesson 5 — Armenian national songs and dances

Armenian national songs and dances — what a national song and dance is, and what else it can be called.

## Sub lesson 6 — The peasant songs (գեղջկական երգեր)

The peasant songs (գեղջկական երգեր) — the village's own genre of song.

## Sub lesson 7 — ARRANGEMENTS OF FOLK AND CLASSICAL WORKS

ARRANGEMENTS OF FOLK AND CLASSICAL WORKS — how folk songs are arranged (մշակում) for new voices and instruments.

## Sub lesson 8 — Project

Project: THE LOVE THEME IN ARMENIAN FOLK SONGS — work from materials on the internet or in books (project proposal 2).
