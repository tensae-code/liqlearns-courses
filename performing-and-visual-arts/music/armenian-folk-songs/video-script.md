# Video script — Armenian Folk Songs

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This is Armenia's own lesson.

**"The wooden horse" (Փայտե ձիուկ, words by Komitas) and Pashinyan's "The Horseman" heard.** "The wooden horse" (Փայտե ձիուկ, words by Komitas) and Pashinyan's "The Horseman" heard — a folk song and a piano picture of riding.

**"Dombari" (Դոմբարի).** "Dombari" (Դոմբարի) — an Armenian folk harvest song: "Basen is kind — let this year be plentiful", sung and played as a game.

**"Alagyaz" (Ալագյազ).** "Alagyaz" (Ալագյազ) — the folk song of the clouded mountain, with Martin Hakoghlyan's paintings "Alagyaz" and "Komitas" looked at.

**What is a FOLK SONG.** What is a FOLK SONG — the song, music and dance the people themselves create; the song "The sky has clouded" (Երկինքն ամպել է).

**Armenian national songs and dances.** Armenian national songs and dances — what a national song and dance is, and what else it can be called.

**The peasant songs (գեղջկական երգեր).** The peasant songs (գեղջկական երգեր) — the village's own genre of song.

**ARRANGEMENTS OF FOLK AND CLASSICAL WORKS.** ARRANGEMENTS OF FOLK AND CLASSICAL WORKS — how folk songs are arranged (մշակում) for new voices and instruments.

**Project.** Project: THE LOVE THEME IN ARMENIAN FOLK SONGS — work from materials on the internet or in books (project proposal 2).

That is Armenian Folk Songs — Armenia's way, kept by name.
