# Samoan Traditional Chants — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which names does the guide give for the chant sources?
a) Faatufugaga, Faiva o le Vao and Sami  b) three pop groups  c) three European composers
**Answer: a) Faatufugaga, Faiva o le Vao and Sami.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Pupils study the songs' rhythmic patterns, instruments, purposes, storylines and…
a) their prices  b) their links to tasks  c) their colours
**Answer: b) their links to tasks.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** A traditional chant, as the guide teaches it, is…
a) only a tune with no story  b) a song with a purpose, a storyline and a place in work or ceremony  c) a written exam
**Answer: b) a song with a purpose, a storyline and a place in work or ceremony.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The guide asks pupils to perform a song…
a) as fast as possible  b) within its traditional context  c) in silence
**Answer: b) within its traditional context.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Pupils review the song's role in…
a) oral history  b) spelling  c) sport
**Answer: a) oral history.**
