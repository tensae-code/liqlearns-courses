# Samoan Traditional Chants — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Traditional chants in Samoa are linked to tasks.
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The guide studies only the melodies of the chants, never their storylines.
**Answer: False** — storylines are studied too.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Performing a song within its traditional context is part of the guide's work.
**Answer: True.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Songs play no role in oral history.
**Answer: False** — pupils review the song's role in oral history.
