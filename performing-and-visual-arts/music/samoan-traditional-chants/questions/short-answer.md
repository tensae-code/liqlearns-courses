# Samoan Traditional Chants — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the three chant sources in the guide's unit.
**Answer: Faatufugaga, Faiva o le Vao and Sami.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Name three things pupils study about the songs that came from them.
**Answer: Their rhythmic patterns, instruments, purposes, storylines and links to tasks (any three).**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What role of the song do pupils review?
**Answer: Its role in oral history.**
