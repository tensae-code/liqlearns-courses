# Video script — Samoan Traditional Chants

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Long before books, Samoa's songs carried its history.

**Faatufugaga. Faiva o le Vao. Sami.** From these came traditional songs — each with its rhythm, its instruments, its purpose, its story.

Sing the song where it belongs, the way it belongs — and the song keeps the history alive.

That is the chant's work in **oral history**.
