# Hands-on — Samoan Traditional Chants

## A song and its task
In pairs, take a traditional song you know by name. Talk through the guide's list for it: its rhythmic pattern, its instruments, its purpose, its storyline, and the task it is linked to. Present the list — by name and purpose only, without performing words you have not been given.

## In its context
Talk together: where, when and by whom is your song traditionally sung? Say in one sentence what would be lost if it were sung with no context at all.
