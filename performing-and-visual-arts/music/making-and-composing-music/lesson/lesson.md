# Making and Composing Music

## Do it first

Quick start: say in one sentence what you think this lesson is about — composing is something a beginner can do. Then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — A tune from B, A and G

Barbados's Class 1 syllabus sets composing as real work for beginners: **compose simple tunes from the given notes B, A and G**. Three notes are enough — put them in an order that pleases you, play it back, and change it until it sounds like yours.

## Sub lesson 2 — Fitting the tune to a rhythm pattern

The tunes are composed **to given rhythm patterns** — the rhythm is given first, and the notes B, A and G are fitted into it, long and short where the pattern asks.

## Sub lesson 3 — Simple lyrics of your own

Composing in Class 1 is words as well as notes: **composing simple lyrics** — your own short words, made to be sung with your tune.
