# Video script — Making and Composing Music

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Composing is not only for grown-ups.

Three notes — B, A, G. A rhythm — clap, clap, clap.

Fit the notes into the rhythm. That is your tune!

Now write your own words, and sing them.

You composed a song.
