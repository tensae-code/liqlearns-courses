# Making and Composing Music — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Barbados Class 1 syllabus asks beginners to compose tunes from which notes?
a) B, A and G  b) every note at once  c) no notes — humming only
**Answer: a) B, A and G** — three given notes are the composing material.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A composed tune is different from random sounds because…
a) it is louder  b) its notes are put in an order on purpose  c) it uses a drum
**Answer: b) its notes are put in an order on purpose** — composing is choosing the order.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** In the syllabus, the tunes are composed to…
a) given rhythm patterns  b) no rhythm at all  c) the teacher's singing only
**Answer: a) given rhythm patterns** — the rhythm is given, the notes are fitted into it.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Composing simple lyrics means…
a) copying a famous song's words  b) writing your own simple words to sing  c) spelling practice
**Answer: b) writing your own simple words to sing** — the words are the composer's own.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Which pair does Class 1 composing join together?
a) tunes and lyrics  b) drawing and clay  c) reading and marching
**Answer: a) tunes and lyrics** — tunes from B, A and G, and simple lyrics.
