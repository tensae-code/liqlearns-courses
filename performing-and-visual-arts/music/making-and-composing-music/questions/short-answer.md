# Making and Composing Music — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the three notes the Class 1 tunes are composed from.
**Answer: B, A and G.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is given first, that the notes must fit into?
**Answer: A rhythm pattern — the tunes are composed to given rhythm patterns.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What words-part of composing does the syllabus name?
**Answer: Composing simple lyrics — your own words for the tune.**
