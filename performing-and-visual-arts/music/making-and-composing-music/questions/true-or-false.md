# Making and Composing Music — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Barbados's syllabus gives composing work to Class 1 pupils.
**Answer: True.** — composing is in the Class 1 syllabus, printed p. 6.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The Class 1 tunes may use any notes the pupil likes.
**Answer: False.** — they are composed from the given notes B, A and G.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A given rhythm pattern can hold the same three notes in different tunes.
**Answer: True.** — different orders and fits make different tunes.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Lyrics must be copied from a book before they can be sung.
**Answer: False.** — the syllabus asks pupils to compose their own simple lyrics.
