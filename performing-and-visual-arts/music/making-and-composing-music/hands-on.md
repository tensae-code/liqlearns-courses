# Hands-on — Making and Composing Music

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Three-note tune
Take B, A and G. Clap the given rhythm pattern, then fit the three notes into it. Play or sing your tune twice — the same way both times, so it is a tune, not an accident.

## Words for your tune
Write two short lines of your own and sing them on your tune. If a word will not fit, change the word, not the tune.
