# Making and Composing Music — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — A tune from B, A and G
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Barbados's Class 1 syllabus sets composing as real work for beginners: compose simple tunes from the given notes B, A and G. Three notes are enough — put them in an order that pleases you, play it back, and change it until it sounds like yours.

## Sub lesson 2 — Fitting the tune to a rhythm pattern
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: The tunes are composed to given rhythm patterns — the rhythm is given first, and the notes B, A and G are fitted into it, long and short where the pattern asks.

## Sub lesson 3 — Simple lyrics of your own
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Composing in Class 1 is words as well as notes: composing simple lyrics — your own short words, made to be sung with your tune.
