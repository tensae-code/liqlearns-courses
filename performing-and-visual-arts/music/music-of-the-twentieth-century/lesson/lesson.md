# Music of the Twentieth Century

## Do it first

Name one thing that belongs in this lesson — 20th-century music — an age when no single style rules. Say what it is in your own words. If you can name one, you are already inside the lesson.

## Sub lesson 1 — music of the 20th century: no single style rules

music of the 20th century: no single style rules — atonality, polytonality, dodecaphony, polyrhythm and the avant-garde break the old boundaries while symphony, concerto and sonata are remade more freely; smaller ensembles and winds come forward.

## Do It Yourself

1. Choose one named part of this lesson and find out one more true thing about it — from a person who knows it, or from the book your part came from.
2. Make or perform a small piece in the way of that part: a rhythm, a step, a drawing, a shaped piece of clay.
3. Write one sentence: what does this part show about North Macedonia's own way of teaching its arts?
