# Songs for Clean and Healthy Living

## Do it first

Wash your hands. Really wash them — but slowly. Count what you do: wet them… soap… rub… Did you rub the backs? The thumbs? Most people miss steps. This lesson's first song names all ten steps, in order, so your hands never miss again.

## Sub lesson 1 — The ten steps of handwashing

The song is **"ስለ ንጽህና አጠባበቅ"** — *About Keeping Clean: ten easy steps for clean hands*. It is a numbered-step song: the ten steps are its content, sung in order.

[Picture 9461 — the ten steps of handwashing, one numbered panel for each step]

Here are the ten steps, in the song's order:

1. **Wet** the hands.
2. Apply **soap**.
3. Rub the **palms** hard.
4. Rub the **backs** of the hands.
5. Rub the **interlaced fingers**.
6. Rub the **finger-backs** on the palms.
7. Rub the **thumbs** well.
8. Rub the **palm centres** with the fingertips.
9. **Rinse** with water.
10. **Dry** with a clean cloth.

Sing it at the tap and do each step as its number is sung. Ten steps, every time, in order — that is clean hands.

## Sub lesson 2 — Science, Science, and keeping clean

The second song is **"ሳይንስ ሳይንስ"** — *Science, Science*. It praises science as **"my medicine"** — the teacher that taught me my health: **washing hands and face**, and **eating breakfast**. Around it the book pictures the same teaching: washing hands, a balanced meal, cleaning the surroundings.

The third song is **"ንጽህናችንን እንጠብቅ"** — *Let's Keep Our Cleanliness*. Its rules are plain: clean the surroundings always, to live far from disease; clean our yard; wash hands **with soap**, so communicable disease does not come; live in cleanliness and defeat disease — **our health is protected**.

Sing both songs standing. They are promise songs: we sing what we will do.

## Say it to your companion

Sing the ten steps to your companion, miming each one, in order. Then name the habits of Science, Science and the rules of Let's Keep Our Cleanliness — and say what cleanliness protects us from.

## Do It Yourself

1. Wash your hands today by the song: all ten steps, in order, singing quietly. Miss none.
2. Sweep your yard or your corner of the classroom while singing Let's Keep Our Cleanliness.
3. Teach the ten steps to someone younger, with the song, at a real tap.
