# Lesson plan — Songs for Clean and Healthy Living

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Music · **Lesson:** Songs for Clean and Healthy Living
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 3 — Music (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** health you can sing: the ten steps of handwashing in order, science praised as 'my medicine', and the cleanliness song — clean surroundings, washed hands, disease kept far away.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the learner's own body and home habits; no reading needed — the songs turn washing and cleaning into a sung routine.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved (the gate: README, "Units, Lessons and Sub Lessons (ruled 2026-11-08)"):

1. **The ten steps of handwashing** — "ስለ ንጽህና አጠባበቅ" (About Keeping Clean) — the ten steps in their sung order, from wetting the hands to drying with a clean cloth.
2. **Science, Science, and keeping clean** — "ሳይንስ ሳይንስ" (Science, Science) — science praised as my medicine, teaching washing and breakfast — and "ንጽህናችንን እንጠብቅ" (Let's Keep Our Cleanliness) — clean the yard, wash hands with soap, live far from disease.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: any song lesson. The habit itself — washing hands — the learner already knows from home. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Sing the handwashing song and do its ten steps in order.
2. Say the ten steps from memory: wet, soap, palms, backs, fingers, finger-backs, thumbs, palm centres, rinse, dry.
3. Sing "ሳይንስ ሳይንስ" (Science, Science) and name its habits: washing hands and face, eating breakfast.
4. Sing "ንጽህናችንን እንጠብቅ" (Let's Keep Our Cleanliness) and name its rules: clean surroundings, clean yard, hands washed with soap.
5. Say why cleanliness matters: so communicable disease does not come; our health is protected.

## What you need
Water, soap and a clean cloth for the real washing — the song is best learned at the tap, not only at the desk.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — Wash your hands. Really wash them — but slowly. Count what you do: wet them… soap… rub… Did you rub the backs? The thumbs? Most people miss steps. This lesson's first song names all ten steps, in order, so your hands never miss again. (lesson/lesson.md).

## If the learner is stuck
- Loses the words → go back to the call-and-answer shape: the teacher (or the audio) sings a short piece, the learner answers with the same piece. Short pieces first, whole song after.
- Loses the tune → hum the song together before singing words; the tune first, the words ride on it.
- Sings but cannot say what the song teaches → ask the lesson's own question: what is this song about, and what does it ask us to do? Every song in this unit teaches something that can be said in one sentence.
