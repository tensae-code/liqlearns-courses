# Hands-on — Songs for Clean and Healthy Living

The lesson with your hands, your feet and your voice together, not only your eyes.

## Wash by the song
At a tap or a poured jug, the class sings the ten-steps song slowly and everyone washes in time, step by step. A checker watches for the steps everyone forgets: backs, thumbs, palm centres.

## Clean-yard singing
Take brooms to the schoolyard. Sing Let's Keep Our Cleanliness while sweeping one corner properly clean. The song ends when the corner is done.
