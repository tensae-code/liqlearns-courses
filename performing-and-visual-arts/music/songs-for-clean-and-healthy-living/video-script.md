# Video script — Songs for Clean and Healthy Living

The super-simple voice, for the sing-along video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The songs are named and summarised, never transcribed; the script sings the learner TO the song, not the song itself.

---

Wet your hands. Soap! Rub your palms — hard!
Backs of the hands. Fingers together.
Finger-backs. Thumbs — rub them well!
Palm centres with your fingertips.
Rinse with water. Dry with a clean cloth.
Ten steps — every time!

Science, science — my medicine!
Wash, eat breakfast, keep clean —
and disease stays far away.
