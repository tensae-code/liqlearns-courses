# Songs for Clean and Healthy Living — short answer

Answer in a few words, out loud or in writing.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name steps 3, 4 and 5 in order.
**Answer:** Rub the palms hard; rub the backs of the hands; rub the interlaced fingers.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why do we live in cleanliness, according to the song?
**Answer:** To live far from disease — so communicable disease does not come; our health is protected.
