# Songs for Clean and Healthy Living — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How many steps does the handwashing song name?
a) five  b) ten  c) seven
**Answer: b) ten**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The first two steps, in order, are —
a) rinse, then dry  b) wet the hands, then apply soap  c) rub thumbs, then wet
**Answer: b) wet the hands, then apply soap**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Step 7 is —
a) rub the thumbs well  b) dry with a cloth  c) wet the hands
**Answer: a) rub the thumbs well**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** The last step is —
a) rinse with water  b) apply soap  c) dry with a clean cloth
**Answer: c) dry with a clean cloth**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** "ሳይንስ ሳይንስ" praises science as —
a) my medicine  b) my song  c) my game
**Answer: a) my medicine**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Let's Keep Our Cleanliness says to wash hands —
a) with water only  b) with soap  c) once a week
**Answer: b) with soap** — So communicable disease does not come.
