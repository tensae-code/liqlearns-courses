# Songs for Clean and Healthy Living — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Rubbing the backs of the hands is one of the ten steps.
**Answer: True** — Step 4.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Drying comes before rinsing in the song's order.
**Answer: False** — Rinse is step 9; dry is step 10.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Science, Science teaches washing hands and face and eating breakfast.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The cleanliness song says a dirty yard keeps disease away.
**Answer: False** — Clean surroundings keep disease far away.
