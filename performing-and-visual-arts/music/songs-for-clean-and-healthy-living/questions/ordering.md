# Songs for Clean and Healthy Living — ordering

Put the things in the right order.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Put the handwashing steps in the song's order.
Here they are, mixed up: dry with a clean cloth; rinse with water; rub the thumbs well; rub the palms hard; apply soap; wet the hands.
**Answer (right order):** wet the hands → apply soap → rub the palms hard → rub the thumbs well → rinse with water → dry with a clean cloth
