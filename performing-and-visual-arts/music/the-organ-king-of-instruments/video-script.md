This lesson is The Organ: King of Instruments.

The Organ: King of Instruments — Organ music and the organ, the king of instruments. — as Kazakhstan's school books teach it: what it teaches: the organ — manuals, pedal keyboard, registers and tractura; baroque polyphony from Buxtehude and Bach to Reger; organs in Astana, Almaty, Kokshetau and Karaganda and Kazakh organist Gabit Nesipbaev; Akan seri's song «Маңмаңгер» heard in organ arrangement.

Watch, listen — then say it back in your own words.
