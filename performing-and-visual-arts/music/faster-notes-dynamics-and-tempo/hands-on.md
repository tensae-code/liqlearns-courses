# Hands-on — Faster Notes, Dynamics and Tempo

The lesson with your hands, your feet and your voice together, not only your eyes.

## Walking and running beats
Walk around the room on quarter-note beats, as the book's movement work directs. Then run lightly on eighth-note beats — the beat stays the same, your feet double inside it.

## Dynamics conducting
Choose a known song. One pupil conducts with shrinking and growing arms — small for P, wide for F, opening slowly for crescendo — while the class sings the signs.
