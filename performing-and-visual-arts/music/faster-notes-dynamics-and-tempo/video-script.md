# Video script — Faster Notes, Dynamics and Tempo

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Two eighths live inside one beat.
A dot adds half. Half of two is one — so two becomes three.
P is soft. F is loud. The wedge opens — grow!
Andante walks. Allegro runs. The metronome keeps the speed honest.
Seven sounds only: A to G — Do to Si.
