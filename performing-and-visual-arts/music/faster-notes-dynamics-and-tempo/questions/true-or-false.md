# Faster Notes, Dynamics and Tempo — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Music is made by joining sound and beat.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Two eighth notes fill one quarter-note beat.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A dot adds a whole extra note of the same value.
**Answer: False**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** A crescendo rises gradually from low to high.
**Answer: True**

**5. (Sub lesson 4)** Andante is a calm pace.
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 5)** Musical sound can be measured and has pitch.
**Answer: True**

**7. (Sub lesson 6)** Movement to music steadies posture, breathing and blood circulation.
**Answer: True**
