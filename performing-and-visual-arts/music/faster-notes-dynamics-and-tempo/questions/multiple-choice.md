# Faster Notes, Dynamics and Tempo — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Beat does what to sounds?
  a) Measures their time-length b) Changes their colour c) Makes them louder
**Answer: a) Measures their time-length** — beat measures the time-length of sounds.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** One eighth note lasts…
  a) Half a beat b) One beat c) Two beats
**Answer: a) Half a beat** — a single eighth note is half a beat.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A dotted half note lasts…
  a) 3 beats b) 4 beats c) 2 beats
**Answer: a) 3 beats** — 2 + half of 2 (1) = 3 beats.

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** PP (pianissimo) means…
  a) Very low volume b) Very high volume c) Medium speed
**Answer: a) Very low volume** — PP is very low; P is low; F is high; FF is very high.

**5. (Sub lesson 4)** Tempo is measured by…
  a) The metronome b) The colour wheel c) The staff
**Answer: a) The metronome** — the metronome measures the beat's speed.

**6. (Sub lesson 5)** Which of these is noise, by the book's test?
  a) Breaking glass b) A singer's voice c) A masenqo
**Answer: a) Breaking glass** — noise cannot be measured and disturbs — breaking glass is the book's example.

**7. (Sub lesson 6)** The running beat is written in…
  a) Eighth notes b) Whole notes c) Half rests
**Answer: a) Eighth notes** — the book writes the running beat in eighth notes, walking in quarters.
