# Faster Notes, Dynamics and Tempo — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What are notes?
**Answer: The signs that stand for sounds, measured by beat.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How are travelling eighth notes often joined?
**Answer: Beamed together in pairs.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does a dotted whole note total?
**Answer: 6 beats — 4 + 2.**

**4. (Sub lesson 5)** B in sol-fa is called…
**Answer: Si — the seventh of the seven sounds.**

**5. (Sub lesson 6)** Which beat is written in quarter notes?
**Answer: The walking beat.**
