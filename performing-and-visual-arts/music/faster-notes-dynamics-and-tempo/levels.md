# Faster Notes, Dynamics and Tempo — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure — README, "Units, Lessons and Sub Lessons"). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number, as before. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. The sub lessons climb inside this one lesson, from the youngest books to the oldest.

## Sub lesson 1 — Beat measures sound
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: say what beat measures and hold "ta" for a whole, a half and a quarter value against a steady clap.

## Sub lesson 2 — The eighth note
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: clap a bar of quarter, quarter, and two pairs of eighths, keeping the beat steady.

## Sub lesson 3 — Dotted notes
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: work all three dotted sums and clap a dotted half against three counted beats.

## Sub lesson 4 — Dynamics and tempo: loud–soft, fast–slow
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: read the six dynamics signs aloud with their meanings and sing one phrase growing and one sinking. And: match the four tempo words to four performed speeds and say what instrument measures tempo.

## Sub lesson 5 — Noise or music? The seven sounds
Sub lesson 5 questions from the type files. Pass mark: sort five heard sounds into noise and music, and sing the seven sounds in both namings.

## Sub lesson 6 — Moving to note values
Sub lesson 6 questions from the type files. Pass mark: walk four quarter beats, run eight eighth beats inside the same span, and name the value your feet are playing.
