# Kinds of Traditional Music

## Do it first

Quick game: I name an occasion, you name the kind of song. A wedding! Work in the field! New Year morning! Worship! If each occasion pulled a different song out of you — you already know this lesson.

## Sub lesson 1 — Kinds for every occasion

**Traditional music** is a joyful expression of feeling that shows the community's activities. It is performed with melody on the community's own occasions:

- **New Year songs** — for the turning of the year (ዘመን መለወጫ).
- **Work songs** — sung while the community works.
- **Wedding songs** — for the wedding celebration.
- **Worship songs** — for worship.

And **traditional dance** is body movement performed **with** traditional music — the music and the movement belong together.

## Sub lesson 2 — ሽለላ እና ፉከራ shilela and fukera

One kind stands tall: **ሽለላ እና ፉከራ (shilela and fukera)** — praise chanting.

A performer chants **stirring poems** — the chant calls out *jalo bel, aqrara, megen bel* — while **moving back and forth**. It is **morale-raising** music: war and hero praise poetry that lifts the hearts of those who hear it and praises the deeds of heroes.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion: what is traditional music, and name its four occasions. Then tell what shilela and fukera do to the people who hear them. Companion checks: four occasions? Morale-raising praise in the answer?

## Do It Yourself

1. List four songs you know, one for each occasion: New Year, work, wedding, worship.
2. In a pair, perform a short praise chant of your own making — praise a good deed, move back and forth, and let your partner answer.
3. Write one sentence: why does a community keep so many kinds of songs?
