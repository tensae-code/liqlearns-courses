# Video script — Kinds of Traditional Music

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Every day has its song.

New Year — sing! Work in the field — sing! A wedding — sing! Worship — sing!
That is **traditional music**: our own joyful songs, showing our own life.

Now — a hero comes.
**ሽለላ እና ፉከራ — shilela and fukera!**
Strong poems, chanted loud. The chanter moves — back, forth, back, forth.
Hearts stand up. Courage grows.

That is praise chanting — the hero's music.
