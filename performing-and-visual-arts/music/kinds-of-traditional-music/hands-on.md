# Hands-on — Kinds of Traditional Music

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Songs for occasions
Each player names an occasion — a wedding, harvest work, New Year, worship — and sings or hums a song that fits it. The others say the occasion back. Four occasions minimum.

## Boast and answer in pairs
In pairs, as the book directs: one partner steps forward and back, chanting praise of a hero, a parent, or a friend's good deed — praise words you make yourselves. The partner answers with encouragement. Then swap.
