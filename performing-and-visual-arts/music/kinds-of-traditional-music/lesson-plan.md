# Lesson plan — Kinds of Traditional Music

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Music · **Lesson:** Kinds of Traditional Music
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 3 — Music (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** traditional music is joyful expression that shows the community's life, with a kind for every occasion — and shilela and fukera, the praise chanting that raises heroes' spirits.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the songs lessons — the learner already sings New Year, work and festival songs.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Kinds for every occasion** — New Year songs, work songs, wedding songs and worship songs; traditional dance moves with traditional music.
2. **ሽለላ እና ፉከራ — shilela and fukera** — praise chanting: stirring poems chanted while moving back and forth, raising morale — the hero's music.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the songs lessons (Songs of Our Country; Traditional and Festival Songs). Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say what traditional music is and name four occasions it serves.
2. Tell what shilela and fukera are, how they are performed, and what they do to listeners.

## What you need
Your voice and a friend to answer you.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — name a song for a wedding, a song for work, a song for New Year. Different occasions, different songs (lesson/lesson.md).
2. **The explanation** — the kinds by occasion, then shilela and fukera (lesson/lesson.md).

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks traditional music is only old songs → it is the community's own joyful expression, sung today at weddings, work and festivals.
- Thinks shilela is quiet singing → it is stirring praise chanting, performed moving back and forth, made to raise morale.
