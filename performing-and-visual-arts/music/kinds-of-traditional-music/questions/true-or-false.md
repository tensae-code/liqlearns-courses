# Kinds of Traditional Music — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Work songs are one kind of traditional music.
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Traditional music is performed only in the past, never today.
**Answer: False** — it is sung today at weddings, work and festivals.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The book asks learners to perform their area's shilela and fukera in pairs.
**Answer: True.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Shilela and fukera praise heroes and stir the listeners' spirits.
**Answer: True.**
