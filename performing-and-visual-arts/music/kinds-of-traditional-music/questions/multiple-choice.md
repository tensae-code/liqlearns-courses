# Kinds of Traditional Music — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Traditional music shows…
a) only old kings  b) the community's activities and feelings  c) nothing — it is just noise
**Answer: b) the community's activities and feelings** — joyful expressions performed on the community's occasions.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which is NOT one of the four occasions named?
a) New Year  b) weddings  c) examinations
**Answer: c) examinations.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Traditional dance is…
a) movement performed with traditional music  b) standing still  c) drawing pictures
**Answer: a) movement performed with traditional music.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Shilela and fukera are performed…
a) sitting silently  b) chanting stirring poems while moving back and forth  c) whispering
**Answer: b) chanting stirring poems while moving back and forth.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Praise chanting raises…
a) prices  b) morale — courage and spirit  c) the roof
**Answer: b) morale — courage and spirit.**
