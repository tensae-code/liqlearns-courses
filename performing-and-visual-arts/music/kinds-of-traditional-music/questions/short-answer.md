# Kinds of Traditional Music — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the four occasions of traditional music.
**Answer: New Year, work, weddings and worship.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the praise chanter do with his body while chanting?
**Answer: He moves back and forth.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Whose deeds does praise chanting celebrate?
**Answer: Heroes' deeds — it is war and hero praise poetry.**
