# Arab Instruments: Oud and Qanun

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about arab instruments: oud and qanun. Then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — The oud

The ancient Arab string instrument: its deep bowl body and short bent neck, its five double strings and their tuning names (Yakah, ʿAshiran, Dukah, Nawa, Kirdan), its parts drawn and named, and how it is played with the risha plectrum.

## Sub lesson 2 — The qanun

A trapezoid sound-box carrying some 78 strings in courses of three, played with picks on both index fingers, its tuning levers raising and lowering each course — the instrument that sets the tuning for the whole Arab ensemble.

## Sub lesson 3 — The nay

The end-blown reed flute of the Arabs: an open tube of cane with six finger holes and a thumb hole, made in seven named sizes (37.5 to 68 cm), one for each degree of the maqam.
