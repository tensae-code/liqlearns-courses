# Video script — Arab Instruments: Oud and Qanun

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The oud first — a deep bowl body, a short bent neck, five double strings, played with the risha plectrum.
Then the qanun — a trapezoid box, some 78 strings in courses of three, picks on both index fingers, tuning levers for every course: the instrument that sets the tuning for the whole ensemble.
And the nay — a cane tube, six finger holes and a thumb hole, in seven sizes, one for each degree of the maqam.
