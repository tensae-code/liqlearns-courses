# Hands-on — Arab Instruments: Oud and Qanun

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Name the parts
Draw the oud and label its bowl body, bent neck and five double strings with their tuning names (Yakah, ʿAshiran, Dukah, Nawa, Kirdan). Then draw the qanun’s trapezoid box and mark where the tuning levers sit. Finish by counting the nay’s holes: six finger holes and one thumb hole.
