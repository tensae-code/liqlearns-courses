# Arab Instruments: Oud and Qanun — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The oud is played with…
a) a bow  b) the risha plectrum  c) two mallets
**Answer: b) the risha plectrum**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The oud has…
a) five double strings  b) four single strings  c) 78 strings
**Answer: a) five double strings**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The qanun carries about how many strings?
a) 10  b) some 78 strings, in courses of three  c) 6
**Answer: b) some 78 strings, in courses of three**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Which instrument sets the tuning for the whole Arab ensemble?
a) the nay  b) the daf  c) the qanun
**Answer: c) the qanun**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** The nay is made in how many named sizes?
a) three  b) seven sizes, one for each degree of the maqam  c) twelve
**Answer: b) seven sizes, one for each degree of the maqam**
