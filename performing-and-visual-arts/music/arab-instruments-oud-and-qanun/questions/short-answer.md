# Arab Instruments: Oud and Qanun — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the oud’s five double strings by their tuning names.
**Answer: Yakah, ʿAshiran, Dukah, Nawa, Kirdan.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How is the qanun played?
**Answer: With picks on both index fingers, its tuning levers raising and lowering each course of three strings.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** How many holes does the nay have?
**Answer: Six finger holes and a thumb hole.**
