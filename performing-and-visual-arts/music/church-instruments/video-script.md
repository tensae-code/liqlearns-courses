# Video script — Church Instruments

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Ring! Shake! Listen — the church is singing.

**ደወል — the bell.** Iron. Large. It calls and it rings for the church.
**ቃጭል — qachil.** Iron. It serves the spiritual melody.
**ፅናፅል — tsenatsil.** Iron, brass, sometimes silver. Shake it — it rings with the chant.

Bell. Qachil. Tsenatsil. The metal voices of the church.
