# Church Instruments — true or false

Say true or false.

**1.** The bell is small and made of hide.
**Answer: False** — it is large and made of iron.

**2.** The qachil serves spiritual melody.
**Answer: True.**

**3.** The tsenatsil is a drum.
**Answer: False** — it is a shaken metal instrument (a sistrum).

**4.** The church instruments are made of metal, not wood and hide.
**Answer: True.**
