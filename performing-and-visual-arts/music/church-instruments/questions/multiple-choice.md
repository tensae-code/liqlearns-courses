# Church Instruments — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** The bell (ደወል) is made of…
a) wood  b) iron  c) hide
**Answer: b) iron** — and it is large, and serves the church.

**2.** Which instrument serves spiritual melody and is made of iron?
a) qachil  b) atamo  c) kebero
**Answer: a) qachil.**

**3.** The tsenatsil can be made of iron, brass, and sometimes…
a) wood  b) silver  c) tin
**Answer: b) silver.**

**4.** The tsenatsil makes its sound by being…
a) blown  b) shaken  c) bowed
**Answer: b) shaken.**

**5.** All three instruments of this lesson serve…
a) the palace  b) the market  c) the church
**Answer: c) the church.**
