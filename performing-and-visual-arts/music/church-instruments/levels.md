# Church Instruments — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: name the bell, the qachil and the tsenatsil, say what each is made of (iron; iron; iron, brass, sometimes silver), and say that they serve the church and its spiritual melody.
Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: more examples to sort, faster naming, and mixing this lesson's signs or sounds with ones already known, until the learner is right every time.
