# Hands-on — Church Instruments

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Ring and shake
Make the two sounds with safe things at home: tap a metal cup gently with a spoon for the bell; shake a closed tin with a few stones inside for the tsenatsil. Name each sound as you make it.

## The three cards
Draw the bell, the qachil and the tsenatsil on three cards. Write the metal under each: iron — iron — iron, brass, silver. A friend holds up a card; you name it and its metal.
