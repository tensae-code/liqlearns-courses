# Video script — Songs About My Body

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is Songs About My Body.

"With My Hand I Write" and "With My Hand", the hand's many jobs — writing, colouring shapes, playing, carrying, washing teeth, eating, giving, opening the Qur'an, digging, working, praying (printed pp. 2, 5); "The Hands", which tidy my things, give to the poor and rise in prayer — the right hand to eat and drink, the left to carry toys (printed p. 10); "The Senses", counting one to five: I see with my eye, hear with my ear, smell with my nose, taste with my tongue and touch with my hand (printed p. 10); "How Alike We Are", Adnan and Afnan each with two eyes, one head, two arms, teeth, one nose, one mouth and two feet — we are all human (printed p. 11); "The Teeth", white teeth washed and brushed after prayer and after food (printed p. 12); "How Sweet I Am", describing myself — tooth white as snow, hair black as night, face sweet as the moon (printed p. 15)

Watch, listen — then say it back in your own words.
