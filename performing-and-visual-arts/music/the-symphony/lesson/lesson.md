# The Symphony

## Do it first

Name one thing that belongs in this lesson — The symphony — the cyclic four-movement plan it shares with the sonata and the string quartet. Say what it is in your own words. If you can name one, you are already inside the lesson.

## Sub lesson 1 — Genul de simfonie

Unit 4, Genul de simfonie — the symphony: a great work for orchestra, "a sonata for orchestra", Haydn its father (104 symphonies), Mozart its strengthener, Beethoven its philosopher — through Mozart's Symphony No. 25 in G minor, written at 17, and the classical symphony's four-movement plan.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson holds, part by part, in your own words. Companion checks: did every part get named, with its Moldovan name kept where the book gives one?

## Do It Yourself

1. Choose one named part of this lesson and find out one more true thing about it — from a person who knows it, or from the book your part came from.
2. Make or perform a small piece in the way of that part: a rhythm, a step, a drawing, a shaped piece of clay.
3. Write one sentence: what does this part show about Moldova's own way of teaching its arts?
