# Conducting — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does a conductor cue?
**Answer: Each voice, movement or instrument's entry.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does unifying the musical idea mean?
**Answer: The group plays one shared piece, not many different ideas at once.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Who does the conductor interact with?
**Answer: The performers and the audience.**
