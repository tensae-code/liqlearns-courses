# Conducting — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The conductor keeps…
**Answer: a) the tempo  b) the tickets  c) silence**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Cueing means…
**Answer: a) showing each voice or instrument when to enter  b) stopping the piece  c) choosing the hall**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Pulse and metre are…
**Answer: a) defined by the conductor  b) left to chance  c) played only once**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In rehearsal the conductor…
**Answer: a) coordinates the rehearsal and unifies the musical idea  b) plays every part alone  c) sends everyone home**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** A conductor must also…
**Answer: a) listen to the whole group  b) never listen  c) watch only the audience**
