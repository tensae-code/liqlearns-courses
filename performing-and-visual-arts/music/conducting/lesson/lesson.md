# Conducting

## Sub lesson 1 — Pulse, metre and the cue

Andorra's primary music programme gives the conductor real work, learned in cycles 2–3. The conductor **defines the pulse and the metre** and **keeps the tempo** steady for everyone. And the conductor **cues each entry** — showing each **voice, movement or instrument** exactly when to come in.

## Sub lesson 2 — Listening, rehearsing, unifying

The conductor also **listens as conductor** — hearing the whole while leading a part. The conductor **coordinates rehearsals**, **unifies the musical idea** so the group plays one piece, not many pieces at once, and **interacts with the performers and the audience**.
