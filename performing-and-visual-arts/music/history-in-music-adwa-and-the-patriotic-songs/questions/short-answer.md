# History in Music: Adwa and the Patriotic Songs — short answer

Answer in a short sentence or a short list.


**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is hota (ሆታ)?
**Answer:** The war-whoop — the rousing cry that, with ululation, summoned and stirred the marchers.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How is this song placed in this lesson, and why?
**Answer:** By name, singer and purpose only — songs are never transcribed in this course.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name both exemplar songs and their singers.
**Answer:** “Though My Bones Be Crushed” — Tilahun Gessesse; “Adwa” — Ejigayehu Shibabaw.
