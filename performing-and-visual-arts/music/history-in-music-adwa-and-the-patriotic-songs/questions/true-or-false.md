# History in Music: Adwa and the Patriotic Songs — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 5)** Traditional music never changes.
**Answer: False** — the exercise's keyed false — traditional music lives and changes with its people.

**2. (Sub lesson 5)** All instruments serve the same use.
**Answer: False** — the exercise refuses it — instruments differ in use, as their histories differ.

**3. (Sub lesson 5)** Photography is unable to record history.
**Answer: False** — the summary gives photography the opposite job: it preserves the past as evidence.

**4. (Sub lesson 5)** Songs about the Abay can become future history.
**Answer: True** — the exercise's own item — today's songs about the river are tomorrow's history.
