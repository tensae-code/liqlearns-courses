# Hands-on — History in Music: Adwa and the Patriotic Songs

The lesson with your hands, your feet and your voice together, not only your eyes.

## Summons roll-call
One learner reads the instrument roll slowly; the class answers each name with its sound made by voice or hands.

## Name-and-purpose cards
Make two cards — one per exemplar song: title, singer, purpose. No lyrics; that is the rule and the skill.
