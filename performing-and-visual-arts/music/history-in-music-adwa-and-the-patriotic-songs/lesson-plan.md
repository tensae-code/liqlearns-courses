# Lesson plan — History in Music: Adwa and the Patriotic Songs

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Music · **Lesson:** History in Music: Adwa and the Patriotic Songs
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 3 — Music (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** how music carried Ethiopia's history at Adwa — the march summoned by instruments and war-chants, and the patriotic songs that keep the victory sung, each placed by name, singer and purpose.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Songs of Our Country and Fukera and Shilela — the learner knows praise-chanting; here it marches to Adwa.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The march to Adwa: summoned and roused by music** — the victory's music: all Ethiopia summoned from end to end — with mesenqo, negarit, embilta, atamo, kebero and trumpet — by hota and ululation, shilela and qererito.
2. **Tilahun Gessesse — “Though My Bones Be Crushed”** — the rousing song exemplar (ሀ): Tilahun Gessesse's song placed by name, singer and purpose.
3. **“Adwa” — sung by Ejigayehu Shibabaw** — the second exemplar: the song “Adwa” placed by name and singer, carrying the victory itself.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Songs of Our Country; Fukera and Shilela: Boast and Answer. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Tell how the march to Adwa was summoned and roused, naming the instruments and chant kinds.
2. Name Tilahun Gessesse's rousing song and its teaching.
3. Name the “Adwa” song sung by Ejigayehu Shibabaw and its place.

## What you need
Only voices — the songs are placed by name and purpose, never transcribed.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
