# History in Music: Adwa and the Patriotic Songs

## Do it first

Some history is kept in books. This history was kept in voices. Before anyone marched, the drums and trumpets called; while they marched, the chants praised the brave and shamed the fearful. Say the chant-names aloud: shilela, qererito, hota. You are holding the sound-shape of 1896.

## Sub lesson 1 — The march to Adwa: summoned and roused by music

The Grade 6 book's history chapter gives music its marching orders: the call to Adwa **summoned all Ethiopia's people from end to end to march** — **with mesenqo, negarit, embilta, atamo, kebero, trumpet and the like** — **by hota (ሆታ, the war-whoop) and ululation, by shilela and qererito**, rousing the feeling of those who marched with the hero-patriots, **praising heroes and shaming the fearful**. The instruments were not decoration: they were the summons, the road's rhythm and the army's courage, all in sound.

## Sub lesson 2 — Tilahun Gessesse — “Though My Bones Be Crushed”

The book's first song exemplar (§3.2, exemplar ሀ) is the rousing song by **Tilahun Gessesse, “Though My Bones Be Crushed”** — a patriotic song in the Adwa line: the singer's own body offered to the country's defence, whatever it costs. It stands in the book as proof that the patriotic song did not end at Adwa — each generation's greatest voice takes up the same duty. The song is placed by name, singer and purpose only; its words are not transcribed.

## Sub lesson 3 — “Adwa” — sung by Ejigayehu Shibabaw

The second exemplar is the song **“Adwa”, sung by Ejigayehu Shibabaw (Gigi)** — the victory's own name as a song title, carried by one of Ethiopia's best-known modern voices. Set beside Tilahun's offering, the pair teaches the chapter's whole claim: **history in Ethiopia is not only written — it is sung, by named singers, in songs the country keeps**. Both songs are placed by name and singer only; neither is transcribed.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion how the march to Adwa was summoned, then name both exemplar songs with their singers.
