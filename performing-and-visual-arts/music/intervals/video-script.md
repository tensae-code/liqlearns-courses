# Video script — Intervals

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

One sound. Then another sound — higher.

How far apart are they? That distance has a name: an **interval**.

Count from the first sound: one to two… one to three… all the way to one to eight — the **octave**!

Name it. Build it. Sing it. Play it.

That is an interval.
