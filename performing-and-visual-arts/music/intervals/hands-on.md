# Hands-on — Intervals

The lesson with your voice and your ears, not only your eyes.

## Count the distance
Sing or play one sound. Your partner sings a second, higher sound. Together, count the distance from the first sound — 1–2, 1–3 — and name the interval. Swap roles.

## Build the octave
Start on any sound and climb, sound by sound, counting as you go, until you reach 1–8 — the octave. Sing it back down. Say what changed at the top: the sound is higher, and it is the same sound's octave.
