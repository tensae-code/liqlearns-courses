# Intervals — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** An interval is the distance between two sounds.
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** One sound alone makes an interval.
**Answer: False** — an interval needs two sounds.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The octave is counted 1–8.
**Answer: True.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Learners only name intervals; they never sing or play them.
**Answer: False** — they name, build, sing and play them.
