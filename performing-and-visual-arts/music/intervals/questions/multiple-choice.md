# Intervals — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** An interval is…
a) one sound alone  b) the distance between two sounds  c) a kind of drum
**Answer: b) the distance between two sounds.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** An interval's distance is counted…
a) from the first sound  b) from the last sound only  c) it is never counted
**Answer: a) from the first sound** — 1–2, 1–3, and so on.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The octave is the interval counted…
a) 1–2  b) 1–5  c) 1–8
**Answer: c) 1–8.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In the Albania guide, learners work with intervals by…
a) only reading about them  b) naming, building, singing and playing them  c) ignoring them
**Answer: b) naming, building, singing and playing them.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The simple intervals in this lesson stay…
a) inside the octave  b) far beyond the octave  c) below one sound
**Answer: a) inside the octave.**
