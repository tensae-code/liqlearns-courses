# Greek Song and Its History

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about greek song and its history. Then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — Ελληνικές μουσικές ιστορίες: Η ελληνική μουσική του χτες και του σήμερα (Greek musical stories: Greek music of yesterday and today)

the line from yesterday's Greek song to today's — an excerpt of Lavrentis Machairitsas' «Τερατάκια τσέπης» examined

## Sub lesson 2 — Στις γειτονιές του ελληνικού τραγουδιού: Οδός Μάνου Χατζιδάκι (Hadjidakis Street)

singing Hadjidakis' songs; hearing «Έλα πάρε μου τη λύπη» (words by Nikos Gatsos); his «Τσάμικος» from «Λαϊκή Αγορά»

## Sub lesson 3 — Άξιον Εστί το φως (Axion Esti — the light)

Mikis Theodorakis — «Κυκλάμινο» from the Eighteen Little Songs of the Bitter Homeland; the bouzouki and finale versions of «Το γελαστό παιδί» from the film "Z"; «Τη Ρωμιοσύνη μην την κλαις» on the staff

## Sub lesson 4 — Η «επιστροφή στις ρίζες» του Γ. Μαρκόπουλου (Yiannis Markopoulos' "return to the roots")

Markopoulos' programme of bringing Greek tradition into modern song; singing «Άκρα του τάφου σιωπή» with two kinds of accompaniment

## Sub lesson 5 — Η γειτονιά του Σταύρου Ξαρχάκου (Stavros Xarchakos' neighbourhood)

Xarchakos' «Άπονη Ζωή» and improvising an introduction to a refrain in groups of five

## Sub lesson 6 — Ο δρόμος του Μάνου Λοΐζου (Manos Loizos' road)

hearing and singing Loizos' songs while the teacher accompanies with chords

## Sub lesson 7 — Για τη μικρή Ελλάδα του Δ. Σαββόπουλου (For Dionysis Savvopoulos' little Greece)

Savvopoulos' Greece in song — the "Exodus" from «Ο Αρ...» and his taverna storyteller

## Sub lesson 8 — Ελληνικές μουσικές ιστορίες: Η ελληνική μουσική του χτες και του σήμερα (Greek music of yesterday and today)

the story of Greek song across the century to its present

## Sub lesson 9 — Άξιον εστί το φως (Axion Esti — the light)

Mikis Theodorakis — the «Άξιον Εστί», the little songs of the bitter homeland, and the music of the film "Z"
