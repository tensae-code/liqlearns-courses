# Lesson plan — Greek Song and Its History

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Music · **Lesson:** Greek Song and Its History
**Place in the curriculum:** Music (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Greek music of yesterday and today — the story of Greek song told through its neighbourhoods and its great songwriters.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this course in the same title.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Ελληνικές μουσικές ιστορίες: Η ελληνική μουσική του χτες και του σήμερα (Greek musical stories: Greek music of yesterday and today)** — the line from yesterday's Greek song to today's — an excerpt of Lavrentis Machairitsas' «Τερατάκια τσέπης» examined
2. **Στις γειτονιές του ελληνικού τραγουδιού: Οδός Μάνου Χατζιδάκι (Hadjidakis Street)** — singing Hadjidakis' songs; hearing «Έλα πάρε μου τη λύπη» (words by Nikos Gatsos); his «Τσάμικος» from «Λαϊκή Αγορά»
3. **Άξιον Εστί το φως (Axion Esti — the light)** — Mikis Theodorakis — «Κυκλάμινο» from the Eighteen Little Songs of the Bitter Homeland; the bouzouki and finale versions of «Το γελαστό παιδί» from the film "Z"; «Τη Ρωμιοσύνη μην την κλαις» on the staff
4. **Η «επιστροφή στις ρίζες» του Γ. Μαρκόπουλου (Yiannis Markopoulos' "return to the roots")** — Markopoulos' programme of bringing Greek tradition into modern song; singing «Άκρα του τάφου σιωπή» with two kinds of accompaniment
5. **Η γειτονιά του Σταύρου Ξαρχάκου (Stavros Xarchakos' neighbourhood)** — Xarchakos' «Άπονη Ζωή» and improvising an introduction to a refrain in groups of five
6. **Ο δρόμος του Μάνου Λοΐζου (Manos Loizos' road)** — hearing and singing Loizos' songs while the teacher accompanies with chords
7. **Για τη μικρή Ελλάδα του Δ. Σαββόπουλου (For Dionysis Savvopoulos' little Greece)** — Savvopoulos' Greece in song — the "Exodus" from «Ο Αρ...» and his taverna storyteller
8. **Ελληνικές μουσικές ιστορίες: Η ελληνική μουσική του χτες και του σήμερα (Greek music of yesterday and today)** — the story of Greek song across the century to its present
9. **Άξιον εστί το φως (Axion Esti — the light)** — Mikis Theodorakis — the «Άξιον Εστί», the little songs of the bitter homeland, and the music of the film "Z"
