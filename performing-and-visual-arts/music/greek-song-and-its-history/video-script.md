This lesson is Greek Song and Its History.

Greek music of yesterday and today — the story of Greek song told through its neighbourhoods and its great songwriters. — as Greece's school books teach it: the line from yesterday's Greek song to today's — an excerpt of Lavrentis Machairitsas' «Τερατάκια τσέπης» examined.

Watch, listen — then say it back in your own words.
