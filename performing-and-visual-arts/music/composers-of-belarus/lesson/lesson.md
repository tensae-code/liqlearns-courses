# Composers of Belarus

## Do it first

Before you read: say or sketch what you already know about this lesson's subject — Belarus's composers, from Mikalai Churkin, the first professional Belarusian composer, onward — Hlebov and Luchanka. Start there; the lesson builds on it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Three Belarusian composers

The Grade 4 book's chapter "Belarusian composers" meets three:

- **Mikalai Churkin (1869–1964)** — the **first professional Belarusian composer**, a talented painter too; his Sinfonietta "Belarusian Pictures" paints folk life and Belarusian landscapes.
- **Yauhen Hlebov** — and his ballet music: the Tarantella from the ballet "Alpine Ballad".
- **Ihar Luchanka** — and his song "Cranes Fly to Polesie".
