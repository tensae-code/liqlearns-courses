# Composers of Belarus — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name Belarus's three composers in this lesson.
**Answer: Mikalai Churkin, Yauhen Hlebov and Ihar Luchanka.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What does Churkin's Sinfonietta paint?
**Answer: Folk life and Belarusian landscapes.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Which composer wrote a song, and what is it called?
**Answer: Ihar Luchanka — "Cranes Fly to Polesie".**
