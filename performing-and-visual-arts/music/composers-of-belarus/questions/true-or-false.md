# Composers of Belarus — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Churkin lived 1869–1964.
**Answer: True**.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The Sinfonietta "Belarusian Pictures" paints city traffic.
**Answer: False** — it paints folk life and Belarusian landscapes.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Hlebov wrote ballet music.
**Answer: True**.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Luchanka's song is about cranes flying to Polesie.
**Answer: True**.
