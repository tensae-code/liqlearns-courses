# Hands-on — Composers of Belarus

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Composer cards
Make a card for each composer: name, dates (Churkin's), and the one work from this lesson. Sort the cards by the kind of work — sinfonietta, ballet, song.

## Paint a picture in sound
Churkin painted landscapes in music. Hum a short 'landscape' of your own — slow and wide for fields, quick and light for a river — and let your partner guess the landscape.
