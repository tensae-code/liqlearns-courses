# Video script — Composers of Belarus

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Three names to know — the composers of Belarus.

**Mikalai Churkin**, the first professional of them all, painter as well, whose Sinfonietta paints Belarusian folk life.
**Yauhen Hlebov**, whose ballet Tarantella dances.
**Ihar Luchanka**, whose cranes fly to Polesie in song.

Belarus, composed.
