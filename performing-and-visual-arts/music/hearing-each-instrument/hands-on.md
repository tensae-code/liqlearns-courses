# Hands-on — Hearing Each Instrument

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The listening game
One player imitates an instrument's sound with the voice. The others close their eyes and name the instrument. Right answer earns the next turn as the sound-maker.

## Song with spaces
Sing a song you all know. At the end of each line, stop — one player fills the space with one instrument's sound. Everyone names it before the song goes on.
