# Lesson plan — Hearing Each Instrument

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Music · **Lesson:** Hearing Each Instrument
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 3 — Music (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** to know each instrument by its SOUND alone — masenqo, krar, kebero, washint, and the trumpet ጥሩንባ — by singing the song whose verses leave room for each sound in turn.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Our Instruments — the learner can already name the four instruments and point to them.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
This lesson is one whole lesson — it has no sub lessons (a short, single-skill lesson; his ruling 2026-11-09).

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Our Instruments. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Hear an instrument's sound and name the instrument, without seeing it.
2. Sing the song “እኛም አለን ሙዚቃ” (We Too Have Music) and leave each verse's space for the right instrument's sound.
3. Name the fifth instrument, the trumpet ጥሩንባ (trumpet).

## What you need
Your ears. No instrument is needed — voices imitate the sounds.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — close your eyes; your teacher or companion imitates an instrument's sound-word; name the instrument (lesson/lesson.md).
2. **The explanation** — the song and its five sounds, one by one (lesson/lesson.md).
   - ADD IMAGE [Picture 9601]: a picture of the five instruments — the student listens, then points to the one whose sound was just heard.
   - ADD AUDIO: the five instruments' sounds, one at a time, in mixed order — the student names each sound.
3. **Say it to your companion** — sing the song together, pausing for each sound (lesson/lesson.md).
   - ADD VIDEO: the script in video-script.md, filmed — made later, when videos are made.
4. **Questions** — from questions/: multiple-choice, true-or-false, short-answer (levels.md).
5. **Hands-on** — hands-on.md.
6. **Do It Yourself** — the listening game with a friend (lesson/lesson.md).

## Add-ons for this lesson (the full list)

- VIDEO — the video-script filmed (made later) → the student sings and pauses with it.

## If the learner is stuck
- Names the picture, not the sound → cover the pictures; listen twice; hum the sound back before naming.
- Mixes masenqo and krar → the masenqo's bow sings long and sliding; the krar's strings are touched short, one by one.
