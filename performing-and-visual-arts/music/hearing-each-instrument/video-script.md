# Video script — Hearing Each Instrument

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Listen! Do not look. Listen.

A bow sings — long, sliding. Who is it? መሰንቆ — **masenqo**!
Strings touched, short and bright. ክራር — **krar**!
Boom, boom. ከበሮ — **kebero**!
A high sweet line in the air. ዋሽንት — **washint**!
Bright and bold, like a call. ጥሩንባ — **trumpet**!

We sing: እኛም አለን ሙዚቃ — We Too Have Music.
Sing. Stop. Listen. Name it. That is the game.
