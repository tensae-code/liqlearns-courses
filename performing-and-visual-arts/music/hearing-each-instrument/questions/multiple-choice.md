# Hearing Each Instrument — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** In the song *We Too Have Music*, each verse leaves a space for…
a) dancing  b) one instrument's sound  c) counting
**Answer: b) one instrument's sound.**

**2.** Boom, boom under the singing — which instrument do you hear?
a) washint  b) krar  c) kebero
**Answer: c) kebero** — the drum.

**3.** The bow sliding on one string is the sound of the…
a) masenqo  b) trumpet  c) kebero
**Answer: a) masenqo.**

**4.** The fifth instrument that joins the song in the later book is the…
a) bell  b) trumpet (ጥሩንባ)  c) atamo
**Answer: b) trumpet (ጥሩንባ).**

**5.** The flute's high, sweet line is the…
a) krar  b) washint  c) masenqo
**Answer: b) washint.**
