# Hearing Each Instrument — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1.** Name the four instruments of the song's first verses.
**Answer: masenqo, krar, kebero and washint.**

**2.** How do you know the kebero is playing, if you cannot see it?
**Answer: By its sound — the drum's boom under the music.**

**3.** What does each verse of *We Too Have Music* leave room for?
**Answer: One instrument's sound, so listeners can hear and name it.**
