# Hearing Each Instrument — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: hear each of the five instruments' sounds — masenqo, krar, kebero, washint, trumpet — and name the instrument correctly, without seeing it.
Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: more examples to sort, faster naming, and mixing this lesson's signs or sounds with ones already known, until the learner is right every time.
