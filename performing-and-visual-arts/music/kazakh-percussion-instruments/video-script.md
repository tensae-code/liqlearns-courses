This lesson is Kazakh Percussion Instruments.

Kazakh Percussion Instruments — Kazakhstan's percussion — as Kazakhstan's school books teach it: what it teaches: Kazakhstan's own percussion — the dabyl, a round or square frame drum with jingling pendants; the ancient asatayak, ornamented and hung with bells, ringing metallically when shaken; the dauylpaz war drum, some 30 cm across, once used to signal an attack; the shyndaul.

Watch, listen — then say it back in your own words.
