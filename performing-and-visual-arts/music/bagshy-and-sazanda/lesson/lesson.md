# Bagshy and Sazanda: Turkmenistan's Musicians

## Do it first

Say what you already know about this lesson's subject — one thing you have seen, heard or done — before you read on.

## Sub lesson 1 — Who creates the music

**Kompozitor** — the composer, who **creates** songs and music.

**Şahyr** — the poet, who **writes a song's poem**, the words the music will carry.

## Sub lesson 2 — Who performs, and who listens

**Bagshy** — the traditional singer, who **sings**.

**Sazanda** — the instrumentalist, who **plays an instrument**.

Together, the singer and the player are **ýerine ýetiriji — performers**: the ones who carry the music to the listeners.

**Diňleýji** — the listener, who **listens with attention**. Listening is a part of music too: without the diňleýji, the song has nowhere to land.
