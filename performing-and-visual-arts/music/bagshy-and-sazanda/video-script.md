# Video script — Bagshy and Sazanda: Turkmenistan's Musicians

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Five people make a song.

The **kompozitor** creates it. The **şahyr** writes its poem.
The **bagshy** sings it. The **sazanda** plays it.
Together — **ýerine ýetiriji**, the performers!
And you, listening with attention — the **diňleýji** — you complete the music.
