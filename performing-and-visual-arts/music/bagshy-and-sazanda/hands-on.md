# Hands-on — Bagshy and Sazanda: Turkmenistan's Musicians

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Five roles in a circle
Five learners take the five roles — kompozitor, şahyr, bagshy, sazanda, diňleýji. The kompozitor hums a new tune, the şahyr gives it four words, the bagshy sings it, the sazanda taps or mimes its instrument, and the diňleýji listens and says one true thing about it. Then rotate.
