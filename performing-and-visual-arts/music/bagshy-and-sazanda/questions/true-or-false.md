# Bagshy and Sazanda: Turkmenistan's Musicians — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The sazanda sings the songs.
**Answer: False** — the sazanda plays an instrument; the bagshy sings.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A bagshy and a sazanda performing together are ýerine ýetiriji.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Music needs no listener.
**Answer: False** — the diňleýji who listens with attention completes it.
