# Bagshy and Sazanda: Turkmenistan's Musicians — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What two people stand behind a song before anyone performs it?
**Answer: The kompozitor, who creates the music, and the şahyr, who writes its poem.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the diňleýji do?
**Answer: Listens with attention — listening is the listener's real part in the music.**
