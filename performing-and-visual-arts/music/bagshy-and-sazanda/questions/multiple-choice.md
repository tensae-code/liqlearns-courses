# Bagshy and Sazanda: Turkmenistan's Musicians — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Who creates songs and music?
a) the şahyr  b) the kompozitor  c) the diňleýji
**Answer: b) the kompozitor**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The şahyr writes…
a) the song's poem  b) the instrument  c) the concert hall
**Answer: a) the song's poem**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The bagshy is…
a) the player  b) the traditional singer  c) the listener
**Answer: b) the traditional singer**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Ýerine ýetiriji means…
a) listeners  b) performers — the singer and player together  c) composers
**Answer: b) performers — the singer and player together**
