# Playing in an Ensemble

## Do it first

Quick start: from the title alone, guess what this lesson's work is. Then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — Melody, harmony and accompaniment

Music's parts — **melody, harmony and accompaniment** — are **distinguished by listening** before any ensemble is built.

## Sub lesson 2 — Building the ensemble part by part

A full ensemble performance is built in steps: **sing the song in unison**, **split into harmony voice parts (soprano, alto, tenor, bass)**, and **add instruments by role** — **percussion adding rhythm patterns, stringed instruments adding rhythm patterns and chord harmony, wind instruments accompanying the melody line** — using available or improvised instruments and **balancing the vocal and instrumental sound**. Percussion practice is built on **timbre**: each instrument's sound-features are identified and combined into rhythm patterns, keeping the tempo and the first beat of the bar.

## Sub lesson 3 — Combine and perform

A group or class **chooses one song** and **designs and presents a performance that integrates the singing and playing skills**. The Year 10 book's **List of Songs** appendix is the repertoire for this work, placed by name and purpose only.
