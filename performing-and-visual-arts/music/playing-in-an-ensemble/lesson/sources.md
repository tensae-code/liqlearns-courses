Suriname's own way: the deepening year runs the same six domains on the same frame as leerjaar 7, but at competency levels B/C — pupils switch independently between the four design phases, master the craft itself, argue their choices, handle the emotions of others in their work, and present independently in an unknown space for an unknown audience; music gives meaning to a calculated music work built on the rhythmic patterns of Suriname's five peoples; dance travels a safe route from home through the Amazon interior into Brazil, in awasa, indigenous and Latin American steps; visual arts add hatching and pattern design to the clothing work and end in a fashion show instead of an exhibition; heritage places an object into another culture and performs what that does to its meaning as an oral art form; theatre adapts a known story into an Alakondre Mato; media builds the one-minute film on a storyboard and uploads it to YouTube.
