# Video script — Playing in an Ensemble

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

First — unison. Everyone together.

Then — harmony! Soprano, alto, tenor, bass.

Percussion for rhythm. Strings for chords. Wind for the melody.

Balance it. That is an **ensemble**.
