# Playing in an Ensemble — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The ensemble begins by singing in unison.
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Vocal and instrumental sound must be balanced.
**Answer: True.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Percussion adds the melody line.
**Answer: False — percussion adds rhythm patterns; wind accompanies the melody.**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** One chosen song is designed and presented as a full performance.
**Answer: True.**
