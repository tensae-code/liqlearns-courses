# Playing in an Ensemble — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Music's three parts told apart by listening are…
a) melody, harmony and accompaniment  b) pitch, tempo, dynamics  c) verse, chorus, bridge
**Answer: a) melody, harmony and accompaniment.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The harmony voice parts are…
a) soprano, alto, tenor, bass  b) only soprano  c) two unnamed parts
**Answer: a) soprano, alto, tenor, bass.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Wind instruments in the ensemble…
a) accompany the melody line  b) keep the rhythm only  c) play nothing
**Answer: a) accompany the melody line.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Percussion practice is built on…
a) timbre  b) colour  c) costume
**Answer: a) timbre.**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** The performance is built by…
a) choosing one song and integrating singing and playing  b) singing alone  c) playing alone
**Answer: a) choosing one song and integrating the singing and playing skills.**
