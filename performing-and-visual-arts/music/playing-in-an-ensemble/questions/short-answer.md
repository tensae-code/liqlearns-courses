# Playing in an Ensemble — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name music's three parts in this lesson.
**Answer: Melody, harmony and accompaniment.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What do stringed instruments add?
**Answer: Rhythm patterns and chord harmony.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What is the Year 10 List of Songs for?
**Answer: It is the repertoire from which the group's performance song is chosen (placed by name and purpose only).**
