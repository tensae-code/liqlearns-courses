# Lesson plan — The Bamboo Orchestra

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Music · **Lesson:** The Bamboo Orchestra
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 3 — Music (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Malaysia's bamboo orchestra — an ensemble built from bamboo instruments.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the course's earlier lessons in its title; recommended first, never forced.
