# Video script — Uneven and Asymmetric Metres

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

This lesson is Uneven and Asymmetric Metres.

Aqsaq, an Arab rhythm (darb) written in 9/8 with the quaver as its counting unit and nine quaver forms to the cycle, much used in the muwashshahat; read and clapped in two parts against a steady quaver line, and compared with the Nawakht rhythm.

Watch, listen — then say it back in your own words.
