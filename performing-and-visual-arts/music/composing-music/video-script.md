# Video script — Composing Music

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Small pieces first — a **cell**, a **pattern**, an **ostinato** that repeats.

A question phrase hangs in the air — the answer phrase lands it.

Experiment: play with sound, errors allowed. Improvise: build on a pattern, now.

Compose: choose the piece that lasts — write it, record it, keep it.
