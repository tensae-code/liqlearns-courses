# Composing Music — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is an ostinato?
**Answer: A pattern that repeats.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How is improvisation different from pure experiment?
**Answer: Improvisation is built on pre-established rhythmic, melodic, harmonic or formal patterns.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What makes composition different from experimentation?
**Answer: Composition is the willed, lasting product, kept in a score or a recording.**
