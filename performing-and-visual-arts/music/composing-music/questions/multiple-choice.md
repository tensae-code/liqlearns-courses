# Composing Music — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A piece can be built from…
**Answer: a) rhythmic and melodic cells, patterns and ostinatos  b) only silence  c) copying another piece exactly**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A musical question phrase is…
**Answer: a) suspensive — it sounds unfinished  b) the loudest phrase  c) always the last phrase**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Experimentation, in Andorra's batxillerat programme, is…
**Answer: a) ephemeral, playful exploration where error is allowed  b) an examination  c) copying a score**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Improvisation is built on…
**Answer: a) pre-established patterns  b) nothing at all  c) the audience's shouting**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** A composition is kept…
**Answer: a) in scores or in sound and audiovisual recording  b) only in memory  c) never**
