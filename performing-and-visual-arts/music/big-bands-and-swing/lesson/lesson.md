# Big Bands and Swing

## Do it first

Quick start: say one thing you already know — a tune, a dance, a name — that belongs to this lesson's world. Keep it in mind; the parts will meet it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Kansas City and Count Basie

**Kansas City**: **Count Basie** and the big bands — bands **so large the music had to be written down**, so their players **had to read notation**.

## Sub lesson 2 — Invited up to dance: the swing era

**Swing rhythm**: the **first eighth note is longer than the second, roughly two against one**. **Benny Goodman's integrated band**, and the **Savoy Ballroom in Harlem — "Home of Happy Feet"** — where Black and white dancers danced together.

## Sub lesson 3 — Glenn Miller and the war years

**Glenn Miller** and **In the Mood**: the big-band sound at war.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the parts in order, one sentence each. Companion checks: did every part get its sentence, and did the order hold?

## Do It Yourself

1. Write the parts' names from memory, in order.
2. Do the hands-on activity once more, teaching it to someone else.
3. Write one sentence: what is the one thing this lesson teaches?
