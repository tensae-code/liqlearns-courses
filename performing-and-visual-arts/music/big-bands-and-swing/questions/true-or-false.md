# Big Bands and Swing — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Swing's two eighths are exactly equal.
**Answer: False.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Black and white dancers danced together at the Savoy.
**Answer: True.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Count Basie led a big band.
**Answer: True.**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Glenn Miller's sound belongs to the war years.
**Answer: True.**
