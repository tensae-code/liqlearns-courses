# Big Bands and Swing — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Roughly what ratio is swing's long-short eighth pair?
**Answer: Roughly two against one.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why did big-band players need to read notation?
**Answer: The music had to be written down for so many players.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Who led the integrated band named?
**Answer: Benny Goodman.**
