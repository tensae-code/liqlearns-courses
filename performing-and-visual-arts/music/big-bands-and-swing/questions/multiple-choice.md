# Big Bands and Swing — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Count Basie is tied to…
a) Kansas City  b) Vienna  c) Havana
**Answer: a) Kansas City**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Big bands wrote music down because…
a) the bands were so large  b) paper was cheap  c) no one could play
**Answer: a) the bands were so large**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Swing rhythm makes the first eighth…
a) longer than the second  b) shorter than the second  c) silent
**Answer: a) longer than the second**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The Savoy Ballroom was called…
a) Home of Happy Feet  b) the Quiet Hall  c) Congo Square
**Answer: a) Home of Happy Feet**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** In the Mood is…
a) Glenn Miller's big-band sound  b) a waltz  c) a sea song
**Answer: a) Glenn Miller's big-band sound**
