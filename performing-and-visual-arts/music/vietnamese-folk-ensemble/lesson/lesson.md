## Sub lesson 1 — Vietnam Grade 6 Âm nhạc 6

The folk-instrument ensemble piece "Mừng hội hoa bông" (Celebrating the Flower Festival), arranged by Merited Artist Nguyễn Hồng Thái from the Chèo melody "Tứ Quý" — the joyful, bustling, splendid air of a festival day steeped in national character; the traditional festivals of the Red River delta villages are held in early spring to pray for favourable rain and wind and a good life, and by folk belief whoever receives offerings such as ears of rice or flowers at the festival will have luck and prosperity all year
