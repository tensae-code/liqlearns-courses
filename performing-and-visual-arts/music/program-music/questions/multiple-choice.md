# Program Music — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Programme music is music written to…
a) test instruments  b) tell a story  c) teach notation
**Answer: b) tell a story.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Peter and the Wolf, each character is carried by…
a) a singer  b) an instrument  c) a dancer
**Answer: b) an instrument.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Which of these is named as further programme music?
a) Pictures at an Exhibition  b) a folk song  c) a warm-up
**Answer: a) Pictures at an Exhibition (Mussorgsky).**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Pupils design what for a school production of Peter and the Wolf?
a) a costume list  b) a poster  c) a programme book
**Answer: b) a poster.**
