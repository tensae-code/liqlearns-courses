# Program Music

## Do it first

Quick start: say one thing you already know — a sound, a song, a name — that belongs to this lesson's world. Keep it in mind; the parts will meet it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Programme music — Peter and the Wolf

**Programme music** is music written to **tell a story**. In Prokofiev's **Peter and the Wolf** each character is carried by an instrument — pupils match characters to **oboe, bassoon, flute, clarinet, strings, French horn and timpani**, order seven heard tracks by character, retell the story in their own words and design a poster for a school production. Further programme music is named: Mussorgsky's *Pictures at an Exhibition*, Saint-Saëns' *Carnival of the Animals*, Khachaturian's *Sabre Dance*, Respighi's *The Birds* and de Falla's *Ritual Fire Dance*.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the parts in order, one sentence each. Companion checks: did every part get its sentence, and did the order hold?

## Do It Yourself

1. Write the parts' names from memory, in order.
2. Do the hands-on activity once more, teaching it to someone else.
3. Write one sentence: what is the one thing this lesson teaches?
