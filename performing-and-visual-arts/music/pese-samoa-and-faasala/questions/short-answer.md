# Pese Samoa and Fa'asala — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What determines the melody of a pese Sāmoa?
**Answer: Its text.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The pese Sāmoa is modal with a strong tonal centre and uses two chords — which two, usually?
**Answer: I and V.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the three parts of the authentic way.
**Answer: One melody, another part to support it, and a fa'asala descant over the main melody.**
