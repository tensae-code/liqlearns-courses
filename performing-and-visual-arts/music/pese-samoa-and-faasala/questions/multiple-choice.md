# Pese Samoa and Fa'asala — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In a pese Sāmoa, the melody is determined by…
a) the text  b) the drum  c) chance
**Answer: a) the text.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A pese Sāmoa uses how many chords?
a) ten  b) two only — usually I and V  c) none
**Answer: b) two only — usually I and V.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The melody of a pese Sāmoa ends on…
a) any note  b) the tonic  c) the highest note
**Answer: b) the tonic** — though it can begin on any pitch.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The authentic way has one melody, a supporting part, and…
a) a fa'asala descant over the main melody  b) a drum solo  c) silence
**Answer: a) a fa'asala descant over the main melody.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** In the class work, each pupil writes their own…
a) fa'asala  b) exam  c) drum pattern
**Answer: a) fa'asala.**
