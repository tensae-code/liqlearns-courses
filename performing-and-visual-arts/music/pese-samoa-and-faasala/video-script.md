# Video script — Pese Samoa and Fa'asala

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

In Samoa, the words come first.

A **pese Sāmoa** — its melody grows out of its **text**. It is modal, with a strong home sound, and it sings on **two chords only**.

One melody. A part to hold it up. And over the top — the **fa'asala**, a descant that crowns the song!

Now write your own fa'asala.
