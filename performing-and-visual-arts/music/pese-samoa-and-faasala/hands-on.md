# Hands-on — Pese Samoa and Fa'asala

## Words first
In a group, speak a short line of words aloud together until its natural rise and fall is clear. Hum a melody that follows the words' own shape — the text determining the melody, as the guide teaches.

## Crown it
Sing your group's melody. One singer adds a supporting part under it. Then each person, alone, hums a fa'asala — a descant line over the main melody — and the group chooses one to crown the song.
