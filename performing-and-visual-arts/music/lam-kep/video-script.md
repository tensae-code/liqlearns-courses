# Video script — Lam Kep

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is Lam Kep.

Laos's Lam Kep singing style. — as Laos's school books teach it: the Lam Kep sung-blessing form — the printed New Year blessing verses with their call-and-response parts marked, performed on stage with the khene (drawing of the young performers)

Watch, listen — then say it back in your own words.
