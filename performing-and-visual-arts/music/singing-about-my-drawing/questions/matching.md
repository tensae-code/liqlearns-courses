# Singing About My Drawing — matching

Match each one on the left with its pair on the right.

1. made with my pencil — a
2. beautified with colours — b
3. made as I wished — c
4. mine — d

The pairs on the right:
a. the lines from your own hand
b. the colours made it beautiful
c. nobody drew it for you
d. I am proud of it

**Answer key:** 1 — a; 2 — b; 3 — c; 4 — d.
