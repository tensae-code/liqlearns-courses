# Singing About My Drawing — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1.** The song is a song of pride in your own drawing.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The song says the drawing belongs to the teacher.
**Answer: False** — Mine — I am proud of it.

**3.** Colours came after the pencil lines in the song's making.
**Answer: True** — Made with my pencil, beautified with colours.
