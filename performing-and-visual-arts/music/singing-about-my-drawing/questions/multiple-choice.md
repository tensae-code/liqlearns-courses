# Singing About My Drawing — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** The song "ስዕሌ" means —
a) My Song  b) My Drawing  c) My School
**Answer: b) My Drawing**

**2.** The drawing in the song was made with —
a) a stick  b) my pencil  c) a printing block
**Answer: b) my pencil**

**3.** The drawing was beautified with —
a) colours  b) water  c) sand
**Answer: a) colours**

**4.** The singer says the drawing was made —
a) as the teacher wished  b) as I wished  c) by a friend
**Answer: b) as I wished**
