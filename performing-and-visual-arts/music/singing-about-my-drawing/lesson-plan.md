# Lesson plan — Singing About My Drawing

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Music · **Lesson:** Singing About My Drawing
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 3 — Music (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** one proud song — "ስዕሌ" (My Drawing): a song of pride in your own drawing, made with your pencil, beautified with colours, made as you wished, yours.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the learner having made drawings in the Visual Art lessons; the song is the drawing strand sung back, the mirror of Singing About Colours.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Singing About Colours (its sister song in the same chapter) and any drawing lesson — make a drawing before you sing this one. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Sing "ስዕሌ" (My Drawing) all the way through.
2. Say the song's four prides: made with my pencil; beautified with colours; made as I wished; mine — I am proud of it.
3. Show your own drawing and sing the song over it.

## What you need
A drawing of your own — made in a Visual Art lesson or at home — plus your voice.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — Hold up a drawing you made. Look at it. You chose its lines. You chose its colours. It is yours. This lesson is one short song, and it sings exactly that feeling: pride in what your own hand made. (lesson/lesson.md).

## If the learner is stuck
- Loses the words → go back to the call-and-answer shape: the teacher (or the audio) sings a short piece, the learner answers with the same piece. Short pieces first, whole song after.
- Loses the tune → hum the song together before singing words; the tune first, the words ride on it.
- Sings but cannot say what the song teaches → ask the lesson's own question: what is this song about, and what does it ask us to do? Every song in this unit teaches something that can be said in one sentence.
