# Singing About My Drawing

## Do it first

Hold up a drawing you made. Look at it. You chose its lines. You chose its colours. It is yours. This lesson is one short song, and it sings exactly that feeling: pride in what your own hand made.

## The song of my drawing

The song is **"ስዕሌ"** — *My Drawing* — and everything it teaches is in its four prides.

**Made with my pencil.** The drawing began as lines from your own hand.

**Beautified with colours.** Then the colours came, and made it beautiful.

**Made as I wished.** Nobody drew it for you. It is the way *you* wanted it.

**Mine — I am proud of it.** That is the song's whole heart: the drawing is mine, and I am proud of it.

[Picture 9464 — a child holding up a finished coloured drawing]

Sing it holding your own drawing up, the way the picture does. This song closes the circle of the whole course's first year: what song sings about, drawing draws — and what drawing makes, song can sing.

## Say it to your companion

Show your drawing to your companion and sing the song over it. Then tell them the four prides in your own words: my pencil, my colours, as I wished, mine — I am proud of it.

## Do It Yourself

1. Make a new drawing — anything you wish. Sing My Drawing over it when it is finished.
2. Write your name on your drawing, the way a proud maker signs their work. Keep it somewhere safe.
3. Show your drawing at home and sing the song to your family.
