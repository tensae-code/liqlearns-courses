# Hands-on — Singing About My Drawing

The lesson with your hands, your feet and your voice together, not only your eyes.

## The proud gallery
Pin the class's drawings along the wall. Walk the gallery slowly together, singing My Drawing. At your own drawing, stop, point, and sing the pride line to it.

## Draw, then sing
Make a fresh drawing in class — fifteen quiet minutes, anything you wish. When the pencils stop, stand, hold the drawings up, and sing the song together over all of them.
