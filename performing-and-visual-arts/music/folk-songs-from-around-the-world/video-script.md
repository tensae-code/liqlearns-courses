# Video script — Folk Songs from Around the World

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This is Armenia's own lesson.

**The tale of Queen Music (Հեքիաթ երաժշտություն թագուհու մասին).** The tale of Queen Music (Հեքիաթ երաժշտություն թագուհու մասին) — in Queen Music's heart beats rhythm and her soul is melody: folk songs "Sun, sun" and "Nanik" sung with words, notes and hand signs.

**In what language are folk songs sung.** In what language are folk songs sung — besides literary Armenian there are many dialects: Komitas's noted and arranged "The rain has come"; Naples' song "Santa Lucia" in its own dialect.

That is Folk Songs from Around the World — Armenia's way, kept by name.
