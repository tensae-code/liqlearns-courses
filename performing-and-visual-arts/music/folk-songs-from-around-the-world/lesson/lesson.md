# Folk Songs from Around the World

## Do it first

Name one thing that belongs in this lesson — Folk Songs from Around the World — Armenia's own teaching of this topic, kept as named parts: The tale of Queen Music (Հեքիաթ երաժշտություն թագուհու մասին) — in Queen Music's heart beats rhythm and her soul is melody: folk songs "Sun, sun" and "Nanik" sung with words, notes and hand signs. Say what it is in your own words. If you can name one, you are already inside the lesson.

## Sub lesson 1 — The tale of Queen Music (Հեքիաթ երաժշտություն թագուհու մասին)

The tale of Queen Music (Հեքիաթ երաժշտություն թագուհու մասին) — in Queen Music's heart beats rhythm and her soul is melody: folk songs "Sun, sun" and "Nanik" sung with words, notes and hand signs.

## Sub lesson 2 — In what language are folk songs sung

In what language are folk songs sung — besides literary Armenian there are many dialects: Komitas's noted and arranged "The rain has come"; Naples' song "Santa Lucia" in its own dialect.

## Do It Yourself

1. Choose one named part of this lesson and find out one more true thing about it — from a person who knows it, or from the book your part came from.
2. Make or perform a small piece in the way of that part: a rhythm, a step, a drawing, a shaped piece of clay.
3. Write one sentence: what does this part show about Armenia's own way of teaching its arts?
