# Folk Songs from Around the World — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer: Folk Songs from Around the World — Armenia's own teaching of this topic, kept as named parts: The tale of Queen Music (Հեքիաթ երաժշտություն թագուհու մասին) — in Queen Music's heart beats rhythm and her soul is melody: folk songs "Sun, sun" and "Nanik" sung with words, notes and hand signs.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the first sub lesson.
**Answer: The tale of Queen Music (Հեքիաթ երաժշտություն թագուհու մասին).**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the last sub lesson.
**Answer: In what language are folk songs sung.**
