# Video script — Décima and Dominican Speech

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Three regions, three voices — cibaeño, sureño, capitaleño.

Ten verses, eight syllables each: the décima espinela.

Espinel made it in 1591. Guerra sings it today.

That is the **décima and Dominican speech**.
