# Décima and Dominican Speech

## Do it first

Quick start: Three regions, three voices — cibaeño, sureño, capitaleño. Then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — How the Dominican regions speak

**Dominican Spanish** is not one voice but **three regional voices** — **cibaeño, sureño and capitaleño** — each with its **letter-swaps**, and all carrying **Taíno, African, Andalusian and Canarian roots**. The Grade 6 book learns them the field way: by **interviewing people about how their region speaks**.

## Sub lesson 2 — The décima espinela

The **décima espinela** is a **ten-verse stanza of eight-syllable lines**, rhyming **abbaaccddc**, created by **Vicente Espinel in 1591**. The book sings it as **Juan Luis Guerra's merengue built from décimas**, practised in **karaoke in the three regional voices**, with hats and scarves (the song is named and summarised only).
