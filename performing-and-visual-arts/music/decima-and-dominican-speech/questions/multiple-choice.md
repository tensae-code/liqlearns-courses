# Décima and Dominican Speech — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The three regional voices named are…
a) cibaeño, sureño and capitaleño  b) north, south and east  c) high, low and middle
**Answer: a) cibaeño, sureño and capitaleño**.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Dominican Spanish carries roots from…
a) Taíno, African, Andalusian and Canarian speech  b) only Castilian  c) only French
**Answer: a) Taíno, African, Andalusian and Canarian speech**.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A décima espinela has…
a) ten verses  b) four verses  c) twelve verses
**Answer: a) ten verses**.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Its rhyme scheme is…
a) abbaaccddc  b) abab  c) aaaa
**Answer: a) abbaaccddc**.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The décima was created by…
a) Vicente Espinel in 1591  b) Juan Luis Guerra  c) an unknown singer
**Answer: a) Vicente Espinel in 1591**.
