# Décima and Dominican Speech — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How does the book say to learn the regional voices?
**Answer: By interviewing people about how their region speaks.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Each verse of the décima has how many syllables?
**Answer: Eight — the lines are octosyllabic.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Whose merengue, built from décimas, is sung in the lesson?
**Answer: Juan Luis Guerra's.**
