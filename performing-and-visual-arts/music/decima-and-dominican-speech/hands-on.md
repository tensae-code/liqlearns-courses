# Hands-on — Décima and Dominican Speech

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Interview a voice
Interview someone about how their region speaks: write three words they say their own way, the way the book's learners do.

## Count a décima
Take a décima's first stanza: count its verses (ten) and the syllables of one line (eight), and mark the rhyme abbaaccddc at the line ends.
