# Cuban Music Genres — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson teaches the early genres: contradanza, danzón and punto.
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson teaches the dance genres: son, conga, mambo and chachachá.
**Answer: True.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** This lesson teaches the sung genres: rumba, criolla, trova, bolero and filin.
**Answer: True.**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Songs and stories in this lesson are transcribed in full.
**Answer: False — they are placed by summary, title and purpose only, never transcribed.**
