# Hands-on — Cuban Music Genres

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Work the lesson's own way
Work through the sub lessons in order — The early genres: contradanza, danzón and punto; The dance genres: son, conga, mambo and chachachá; The sung genres: rumba, criolla, trova, bolero and filin — doing each part the way Cuba's books direct: by making, moving, singing or looking closely, as the part asks. Show your companion what you made or did, and name the part it belongs to.
