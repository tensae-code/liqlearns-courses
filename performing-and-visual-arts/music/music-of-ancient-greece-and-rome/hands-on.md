# Hands-on — Music of Ancient Greece and Rome

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## La música griega y romana
Work through this part with real materials or your own body, the way the book sets it: Greek music — first practical and ritual, not written, seeking melodic perfection; vocal music prevailing in the classical age, and purely instrumental music arising in the Hellenistic Greco-Roman time. Show a companion what you made or did, and say what the part teaches.
