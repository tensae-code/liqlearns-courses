**La música griega y romana.** Greek music — first practical and ritual, not written, seeking melodic perfection; vocal music prevailing in the classical age, and purely instrumental music arising in the Hellenistic Greco-Roman time.

That is Music of Ancient Greece and Rome — Venezuela's own books teach it this way.
