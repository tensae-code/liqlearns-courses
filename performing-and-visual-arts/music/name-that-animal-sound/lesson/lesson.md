# Name That Animal Sound

## Do it first

Here is the game. One person makes an animal sound — any animal. Everyone else shouts the animal's name. Were you right? This whole lesson is that game, played carefully with four animals.

## The four animals of the game

Look at the four pictures. A black-and-tan **puppy**. A brown **ram**. A **rooster**. A brown-and-white **cow**.

[Picture 9451 — the four animal cards: puppy, ram, rooster, cow]

Now look at each one and produce its sound. The puppy barks. The ram bleats. The rooster crows — up on his toes, calling the morning. The cow calls out low and long.

Say each sound slowly first, the way the animal really makes it. Then say it quickly when your teacher points. Looking, hearing, naming — the picture shows the animal, the sound tells the animal, and your ear joins them together.

## Say it to your companion

Play the game with your companion. One of you makes one of the four sounds — puppy, ram, rooster or cow — and the other names the animal. Five sounds each, then swap.

## Do It Yourself

1. Point at each of the four picture cards and make its sound without stopping: puppy, ram, rooster, cow.
2. With your eyes closed, listen while a friend makes one of the four sounds. Name the animal. Open your eyes and check.
3. Add one animal of your own from home to the game. Teach its sound to the class.
