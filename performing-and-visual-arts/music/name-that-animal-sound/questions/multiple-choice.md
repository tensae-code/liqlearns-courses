# Name That Animal Sound — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** In the game, what do you do first?
a) look at the animal picture  b) close the book  c) sing a song
**Answer: a) look at the animal picture** — Look, then produce the animal's sound.

**2.** Which animal crows and calls the morning?
a) the cow  b) the rooster  c) the puppy
**Answer: b) the rooster**

**3.** Which animal gives a bark?
a) the ram  b) the cow  c) the puppy
**Answer: c) the puppy**

**4.** The four animals of the game are —
a) puppy, ram, rooster, cow  b) lion, horse, cat, bird  c) goat, donkey, hen, duck
**Answer: a) puppy, ram, rooster, cow**
