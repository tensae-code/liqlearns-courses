# Lesson plan — Name That Animal Sound

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Music · **Lesson:** Name That Animal Sound
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 3 — Music (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the guessing game of listening: look at the animal, hear or make its sound, and name the animal — the puppy, the ram, the rooster and the cow.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Sounds Around Us — the learner can already listen and name a sound's maker; this lesson sharpens the ear on four animals.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Sounds Around Us — naming a sound's maker. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Look at a pictured animal and produce its sound for the teacher and the class.
2. Hear an animal sound and name the animal that makes it.
3. Tell the four animals of the game apart by their sounds: puppy, ram, rooster, cow.

## What you need
Your ears and your voice. The four animal picture cards help, but the game works anywhere.

## If the learner is stuck
- Loses the words → go back to the call-and-answer shape: the teacher (or the audio) sings a short piece, the learner answers with the same piece. Short pieces first, whole song after.
- Loses the tune → hum the song together before singing words; the tune first, the words ride on it.
- Sings but cannot say what the song teaches → ask the lesson's own question: what is this song about, and what does it ask us to do? Every song in this unit teaches something that can be said in one sentence.
