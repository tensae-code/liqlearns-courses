# Hands-on — Name That Animal Sound

The lesson with your hands, your feet and your voice together, not only your eyes.

## Cards up
Make four big cards — draw a puppy, a ram, a rooster and a cow, one on each card. The teacher holds up a card; the class makes the sound together. Then the teacher makes a sound with no card; the child who names the animal holds the card up.

## Sound bingo at home
At home, listen for real animals. Every time you hear one of the four — a dog, a sheep or goat, a rooster, a cow — say its name out loud and count it on your fingers. How many can you hear in one day?
