# Indonesian Regional Songs

## Do it first

Name one thing that belongs in this lesson — Indonesia's regional songs — the song repertoire of the archipelago's regions, sung in class. Say what it is in your own words. If you can name one, you are already inside the lesson.

## Sub lesson 1 — Bab I, Kegiatan 2

recognising and singing lagu daerah, the regional songs of Indonesia's own peoples, and telling them from national songs.

## Sub lesson 2 — Bab I

recognising the local regional songs (lagu daerah setempat) and responding to them.

## Sub lesson 3 — Bab V

singing children's popular songs and regional songs, alone and as a choir (paduan suara) presenting the local regional song.

## Sub lesson 4 — Bab VI

playing one's own music creation on the local regional song (kreasi musik lagu daerah setempat).

## Sub lesson 5 — Bab 3–4

creating with and performing a local regional song (kreasi lagu daerah setempat) on the class's own made instruments, working together.

## Sub lesson 6 — Bab III

regional and national songs sung together, through to a group singing performance (tampil bernyanyi bersama) (summaries only, no lyrics).

## Sub lesson 7 — Bab I Lukisan Tanah Airku

regional songs (lagu daerah) — their form, function and distinctive features, and the values inside them — learned through the song "Lukisan Indonesia" and sung together in groups (summary only, no lyrics).

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson holds, part by part, in your own words. Companion checks: did every part get named, with its Indonesian name kept where the book gives one?

## Do It Yourself

1. Choose one named part of this lesson and find out one more true thing about it — from a person who knows it, or from the book your part came from.
2. Make or perform a small piece in the way of that part: a rhythm, a step, a drawing, a shaped piece of clay.
3. Write one sentence: what does this part show about Indonesia's own way of teaching its arts?
