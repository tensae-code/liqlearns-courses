# Lesson plan — Indonesian Regional Songs

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Music · **Lesson:** Indonesian Regional Songs
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 3 — Music (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Indonesia's regional songs — the song repertoire of the archipelago's regions, sung in class.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the course's earlier lessons in its title; recommended first, never forced.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of ..." in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Bab I, Kegiatan 2** — recognising and singing lagu daerah, the regional songs of Indonesia's own peoples, and telling them from national songs
2. **Bab I** — recognising the local regional songs (lagu daerah setempat) and responding to them
3. **Bab V** — singing children's popular songs and regional songs, alone and as a choir (paduan suara) presenting the local regional song
4. **Bab VI** — playing one's own music creation on the local regional song (kreasi musik lagu daerah setempat)
5. **Bab 3–4** — creating with and performing a local regional song (kreasi lagu daerah setempat) on the class's own made instruments, working together
6. **Bab III** — regional and national songs sung together, through to a group singing performance (tampil bernyanyi bersama) (summaries only, no lyrics)
7. **Bab I Lukisan Tanah Airku** — regional songs (lagu daerah) — their form, function and distinctive features, and the values inside them — learned through the song "Lukisan Indonesia" and sung together in groups (summary only, no lyrics)

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
