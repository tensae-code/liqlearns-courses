# Video script — Indonesian Regional Songs

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This is Indonesia's own lesson.

**Bab I, Kegiatan 2.** recognising and singing lagu daerah, the regional songs of Indonesia's own peoples, and telling them from national songs.

**Bab I.** recognising the local regional songs (lagu daerah setempat) and responding to them.

**Bab V.** singing children's popular songs and regional songs, alone and as a choir (paduan suara) presenting the local regional song.

**Bab VI.** playing one's own music creation on the local regional song (kreasi musik lagu daerah setempat).

**Bab 3–4.** creating with and performing a local regional song (kreasi lagu daerah setempat) on the class's own made instruments, working together.

**Bab III.** regional and national songs sung together, through to a group singing performance (tampil bernyanyi bersama) (summaries only, no lyrics).

**Bab I Lukisan Tanah Airku.** regional songs (lagu daerah) — their form, function and distinctive features, and the values inside them — learned through the song "Lukisan Indonesia" and sung together in groups (summary only, no lyrics).

That is Indonesian Regional Songs — Indonesia's way, kept by name.
