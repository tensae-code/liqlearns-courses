# Indonesian Regional Songs — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — Bab I, Kegiatan 2
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Bab I, Kegiatan 2 — recognising and singing lagu daerah, the regional songs of Indonesia's own peoples, and telling them from national songs

## Sub lesson 2 — Bab I
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Bab I — recognising the local regional songs (lagu daerah setempat) and responding to them

## Sub lesson 3 — Bab V
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Bab V — singing children's popular songs and regional songs, alone and as a choir (paduan suara) presenting the local regional song

## Sub lesson 4 — Bab VI
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Bab VI — playing one's own music creation on the local regional song (kreasi musik lagu daerah setempat)

## Sub lesson 5 — Bab 3–4
Sub lesson 5 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Bab 3–4 — creating with and performing a local regional song (kreasi lagu daerah setempat) on the class's own made instruments, working together

## Sub lesson 6 — Bab III
Sub lesson 6 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Bab III — regional and national songs sung together, through to a group singing performance (tampil bernyanyi bersama) (summaries only, no lyrics)

## Sub lesson 7 — Bab I Lukisan Tanah Airku
Sub lesson 7 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Bab I Lukisan Tanah Airku — regional songs (lagu daerah) — their form, function and distinctive features, and the values inside them — learned through the song "Lukisan Indonesia" and sung together in groups (summary only, no lyrics)
