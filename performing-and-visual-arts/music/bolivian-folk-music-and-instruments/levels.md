Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. ## Sub lesson 2 — The instruments of Bolivia
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Organology and the Hornbostel-Sachs families read through Bolivia's own instruments: the pre-Columbian instruments, the quena, quenacho and tarka, the guitar, the school banda de guerra, and the instruments of the Bolivian east.

## Sub lesson 3 — Taquirari and chobena
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Two rhythms of the Bolivian east, both in 2/4: the Taquirari, the dance of the arrow, and the slower, calmer Chobena of the Chiquitania.
