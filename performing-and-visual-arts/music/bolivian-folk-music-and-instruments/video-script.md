# Video script — Bolivian Folk Music and Instruments

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This is Bolivia's own lesson.

**Bolivia's folk music and its makers.** Criolla, mestiza and folk music, the estudiantinas, the representative ensembles (Los Kjarkas, Savia Andina, Trio Los Cambitas), the composers of the national repertoire, and the artists who carry indigenous knowledge and care for Mother Earth — all placed by name, title and theme only.

**The instruments of Bolivia.** Organology and the Hornbostel-Sachs families read through Bolivia's own instruments: the pre-Columbian instruments, the quena, quenacho and tarka, the guitar, the school banda de guerra, and the instruments of the Bolivian east.

**Taquirari and chobena.** Two rhythms of the Bolivian east, both in 2/4: the Taquirari, the dance of the arrow, and the slower, calmer Chobena of the Chiquitania.

That is Bolivian Folk Music and Instruments — Bolivia's way, kept by name.
