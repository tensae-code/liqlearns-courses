## Sub lesson 1 — Neoclassicism

Neoclassicism: after romantic excess, a return to clarity, balance and the forms of Haydn, Mozart and Bach filled with modern harmony — Igor Stravinsky (1882–1971), from the Russian ballets "The Firebird", "Petrushka" and "The Rite of Spring" to neoclassical discipline; Prokofiev's "Classical" Symphony beside him.

## Sub lesson 2 — Сергеј Сергеевич Прокофјев (1891–1953)

Сергеј Сергеевич Прокофјев (1891–1953) — Prokofiev: composing from early childhood, the Petrograd Conservatory at thirteen (Lyadov, Rimsky-Korsakov, Esipova), the Rubinstein prize of 1914, the years abroad; the opera "War and Peace" after Tolstoy, the ballets "Romeo and Juliet" and "Cinderella", seven symphonies and five piano concertos.
