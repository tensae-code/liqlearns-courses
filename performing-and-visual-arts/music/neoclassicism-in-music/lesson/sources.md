North Macedonia's own way: the year's theory is carried on Macedonian backs — Teshkoto opens the book (in Blazhe Koneski's poem), Shuplevski, Skalovski and Prokopiev are the composer portraits, the folk songs are Bitola's, Christmas and Easter are told in Macedonian custom, and the ballet whose story is retold in full is Prokopiev's "Labin i Dojrana".
