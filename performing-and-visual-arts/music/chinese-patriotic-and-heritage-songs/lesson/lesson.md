# Chinese Patriotic and Heritage Songs

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about chinese patriotic and heritage songs. Then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — China Grade 3 Music Vol.2

The story of composer Nie Er — writing the March of the Volunteers for the 1935 film Children of Troubled Times, and how it became China's national anthem

## Sub lesson 2 — China Grade 6 Music Vol.1

A song of the overseas Chinese heart — written in Hong Kong and sung into fame at the 1984 Spring Festival Gala; the second half's rhythm changes call for careful breath; Lo Ta-yu's song to Hong Kong, the Pearl of the Orient — two songs that witnessed an era of change; sing a few lines along with the recording; a song of one mother with daughters of two peoples (Tibetan and Han), sung with its grace notes and mordents; a song of the singer and the motherland never to be parted — every mountain and river sung as praise; You and Me, theme song of the 2008 Beijing Olympics — breath held back, feeling let through; Hand in Hand, theme of the Seoul Olympics — what an Olympic theme song is: made by the host country to carry its culture and the Olympic spirit; Un'estate italiana, theme of the 1990 football World Cup in Italy — a melody known everywhere; Barcelona, Freddie Mercury's theme for the Barcelona Olympics, sung as solo and duet; Flame, theme of the Sydney Olympics — listening with the English words before you, and completing the prelude's melody by ear

## Sub lesson 3 — China Grade 6 Music Vol.2

Love My China — fifty-six constellations, fifty-six flowers, fifty-six brothers and sisters one family; fifty-six languages gathered into one sentence; the Graduation Song (Tian Han's words, Nie Er's music, theme of the 1934 film Plunder of Peach and Plum) — a march-like mass song of students taking up their country's fate
