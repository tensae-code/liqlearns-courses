# Lesson plan — Chinese Patriotic and Heritage Songs

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Music · **Lesson:** Chinese Patriotic and Heritage Songs
**Place in the curriculum:** Music (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** chinese Patriotic and Heritage Songs — its forms, history and cultural meaning, taught as one topic lesson.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this course in the same title.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **China Grade 3 Music Vol.2** — The story of composer Nie Er — writing the March of the Volunteers for the 1935 film Children of Troubled Times, and how it became China's national anthem
2. **China Grade 6 Music Vol.1** — A song of the overseas Chinese heart — written in Hong Kong and sung into fame at the 1984 Spring Festival Gala; the second half's rhythm changes call for careful breath; Lo Ta-yu's song to Hong Kong, the Pearl of the Orient — two songs that witnessed an era of change; sing a few lines along with the recording; a song of one mother with daughters of two peoples (Tibetan and Han), sung with its grace notes and mordents; a song of the singer and the motherland never to be parted — every mountain and river sung as praise; You and Me, theme song of the 2008 Beijing Olympics — breath held back, feeling let through; Hand in Hand, theme of the Seoul Olympics — what an Olympic theme song is: made by the host country to carry its culture and the Olympic spirit; Un'estate italiana, theme of the 1990 football World Cup in Italy — a melody known everywhere; Barcelona, Freddie Mercury's theme for the Barcelona Olympics, sung as solo and duet; Flame, theme of the Sydney Olympics — listening with the English words before you, and completing the prelude's melody by ear
3. **China Grade 6 Music Vol.2** — Love My China — fifty-six constellations, fifty-six flowers, fifty-six brothers and sisters one family; fifty-six languages gathered into one sentence; the Graduation Song (Tian Han's words, Nie Er's music, theme of the 1934 film Plunder of Peach and Plum) — a march-like mass song of students taking up their country's fate
