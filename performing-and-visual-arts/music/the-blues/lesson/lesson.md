# The Blues

## Do it first

Name one thing that belongs in this lesson — The blues — from gospel to the blues, its twelve-bar form, and the sound that fathered rock. Say what it is in your own words. If you can name one, you are already inside the lesson.

## Sub lesson 1 — Blues

Unit 3 Apresiasi: Muzik Blues — blues: its sound and its instruments (harmonica, kazoo, washboard, slide on guitar frets, banjo, mandolin, viola), listening to named blues recordings, and Blues Gang, the band that popularised blues in Malaysia.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson holds, part by part, in your own words. Companion checks: did every part get named, with its Malaysian name kept where the book gives one?

## Do It Yourself

1. Choose one named part of this lesson and find out one more true thing about it — from a person who knows it, or from the book your part came from.
2. Make or perform a small piece in the way of that part: a rhythm, a step, a drawing, a shaped piece of clay.
3. Write one sentence: what does this part show about Malaysia's own way of teaching its arts?
