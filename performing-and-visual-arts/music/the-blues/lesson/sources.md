| Kazakhstan — Музыка 3-тарау (continued). Блюз (The Blues) — what it teaches: blues (блюз) is a musical style developed in the 19th century and widely known in the USA in 1920–1940; the blues musical style's ancestors are considered the Afro-Americans who came to America as slaves; blues is a musical style born from mixing slaves' songs (жұмыс әндері — work songs) and spiritual musical styles; African slaves conveyed in their songs sorrow, grief and social inequality; this direction's religious songs and work songs were a big part of slaves' lives; in their songs slaves' sufferings were conveyed; after the abolition of slavery in 1865 the former slaves' songs did not stay only among Afro-Americans — they changed them and sang them in their present life as songs; many people belonging to this community lived in difficult conditions; blues was sung in such difficult life periods; the famous performers who sang in the blues direction: Ma Rainey (Блюз анасы — Mother of the Blues), Bessie Smith, Muddy Waters, Robert Johnson and so on; blues developed widely from the 1960s; at first this genre was known only in the USA, later it spread to Europe too; so together with this musical direction the blues and rock-n-roll's (рок-н-роллдың) appearance is also connected (student book; original target `music/the-blues-and-twelve-bar-form`) | Book 5, printed p. 55 |

Музыкалық форма еркіндігі және импровизация (Freedom of musical form and improvisation) — what it teaches: the modes often played in major are called Ionian mode (Ионий лады) — for example a C-major scale; together with it there are other folk modes in folk music: for example in Kazakh folk music Lydian, Mixolydian, Dorian and Phrygian modes are often met; look at the sample below, take out the mode; the modes are written with the help of notation; pentatonic scale (пентатонды гамма) — a scale of five notes in an octave; for example if you start from do, it is do–re–mi–sol–la–do; in this scale there are no F and B sounds (the 4th and 7th degrees); research: what do you know the blues is — research the connection between the blues mode and the pentatonic scale; define the difference between the pentatonic scale and the blues mode; where else can you find samples written in the pentatonic scale and blues mode?; using them, perform the pentatonic scale and blues mode; new words and ideas: when musicians show art on stage, at that time they invent a tune, improvise (импровизация жасайды) — at that time their musical ideas are performed without preparation; musical improvisation (музыкалық импровизация) is connected not only with musical performance — some composers also improvise at creating their works; improvisation in musical performance — this is a peculiarity of the jazz genre (джаз жанрының ерекшелігі); perform: using the knowledge taken in this chapter, perform an improvisation in the 12-bar blues genre; show several variants of performance; perform your improvisation to classmates and the synthesizer (student book; original target `music/the-blues-and-twelve-bar-form`) | Book 5, printed p. 57 |
