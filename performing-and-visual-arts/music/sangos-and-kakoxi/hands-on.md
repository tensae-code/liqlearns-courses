# Hands-on — Sangos and Kakoxi

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Sangos and kakoxi
Work through this part with real materials or your own body, the way the book sets it: the sangos and the kakoxi — two more of Angola's named traditional percussion instruments, pictured and named among the traditional percussion instruments. Show a companion what you made or did, and say what the part teaches.
