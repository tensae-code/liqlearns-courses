# Bands and Brass Bands

## Do it first

Quick start: say one thing you already know — a tune, a dance, a name — that belongs to this lesson's world. Keep it in mind; the parts will meet it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Sousa and the ostinato

**John Philip Sousa's** marches — The Washington Post among them — are played with **repeating rhythm patterns (ostinato, þrástef)** that can be **swapped at a song's section changes**.

## Sub lesson 2 — Iceland's first band

**Helgi Helgason** heard a French ship's band in 1856 and the Danish king's band in 1874, trained in Copenhagen and **founded Iceland's first brass band**. His **bugle signals ran the fire brigade**: two blasts — start spraying; three — stop; four — move.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the parts in order, one sentence each. Companion checks: did every part get its sentence, and did the order hold?
