# Video script — How Music Expresses and Makes Us Feel

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This is Armenia's own lesson.

**The expressive means of music.** The expressive means of music — a letter from Mozart; his "Lullaby" heard: composers choose how music speaks.

**Theme 1.** Theme 1: What does music tell about — musical intonation: the same bird-song sounds hungry, then fed; music speaks in intonations like speech.

**One mood or many.** One mood or many — Schumann's "Grandfather Frost" from the "Album for the Young": a work of one part or several, one mood or changing.

**Romanticism's turn.** Romanticism's turn — artists began to dream unrealised dreams of life: Schubert's "Ave Maria" heard.

**IMPRESSIONISM.** IMPRESSIONISM — after the painters' 1874 Paris exhibition the critics mocked them with the name; music takes the same path of impressions; Pan's flute named from the Greek god.

**Theme 2.** Theme 2: Contemporary arrangements of classical and folk works — the videos for this theme are many for one hour: the teacher chooses and presents (teacher-guide evidence).

**Genres transform with society (teacher-guide evidence).** Genres transform with society (teacher-guide evidence) — the lessons follow the transformations of music art and its separate genres alongside society's development, turning both to classical music and to other forms such as jazz.

**THE FOUR KINDS OF LOVE AND MUSIC.** THE FOUR KINDS OF LOVE AND MUSIC — love is among the most important human feelings: Rachmaninoff the brilliant pianist; another kind in the Greeks is PHILIA — the love of friends; Bach's "Capriccio" in six small parts describes in music not an event but feelings; Grieg's song that later became a piano piece of the same name.

**The LOVE theme (teacher-guide evidence).** The LOVE theme (teacher-guide evidence) — the theme of love is treated through the ancient Greeks' distinction of love's four kinds — storge, philia, eros, agape — and then presented in music in the same way.

That is How Music Expresses and Makes Us Feel — Armenia's way, kept by name.
