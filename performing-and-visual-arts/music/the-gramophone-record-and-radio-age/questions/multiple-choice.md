# The Gramophone, Record and Radio Age — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Radio broadcast dance music…
a) live from dance halls  b) only from studios  c) never
**Answer: a) live from dance halls**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The record of the age was the…
a) 78 record  b) CD  c) tape only
**Answer: a) 78 record**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Jimmy McPartland learned jazz from…
a) records  b) a marching band  c) operetta
**Answer: a) records**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** His method worked…
a) phrase by phrase  b) all at once  c) by reading only
**Answer: a) phrase by phrase**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** After some weeks of this method…
a) a whole song stands  b) nothing is learned  c) the record breaks
**Answer: a) a whole song stands**
