# The Gramophone, Record and Radio Age

## Do it first

Quick start: say one thing you already know — a tune, a dance, a name — that belongs to this lesson's world. Keep it in mind; the parts will meet it.

## Sub lesson 1 — The Roaring Twenties: radio arrives

In the **Roaring Twenties**, **radio arrives**: **dance music broadcast live from dance halls** changes popular music's life — the band now plays in every home at once.

## Sub lesson 2 — The gramophone and the record

The **78 record** was a revolution. **Jimmy McPartland's account** tells how he learned jazz from records: **set the record on, play a few bars, stop, find the notes yourselves, phrase by phrase**, until after some weeks a whole song stands (Farewell Blues).

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the parts in order, one sentence each. Companion checks: did every part get its sentence, and did the order hold?
