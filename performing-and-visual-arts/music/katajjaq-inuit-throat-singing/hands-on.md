# Hands-on — Katajjaq: Inuit Throat Singing

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Face to face
In pairs, stand face to face and take turns making a short repeated sound — a breath sound, a nature sound — answering each other, the way katajjaq partners answer.

## Copy nature
Each pair chooses one sound of nature — geese, wind, water — and builds a short face-to-face pattern from it.
