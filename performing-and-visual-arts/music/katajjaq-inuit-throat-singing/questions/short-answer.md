# Katajjaq: Inuit Throat Singing — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What do some katajjaq songs carry, meant as gifts?
**Answer: Stories.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Who sang katajjaq alone in the old days, and why?
**Answer: Women — the men were out hunting.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** How far do named katajjaq singers travel?
**Answer: They travel the world to perform katajjaq.**
