# Katajjaq: Inuit Throat Singing — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A katajjaq performance needs a drum.
**Answer: False — the sound is made with the throat, mouth and nose.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Some katajjaq sounds copy the sounds of geese and the wind.
**Answer: True.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Katajjaq has come back: young people across the Arctic learn and teach it.
**Answer: True.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Men have always sung katajjaq together with women.
**Answer: False — women sang it alone in the old days; men now sing it too.**
