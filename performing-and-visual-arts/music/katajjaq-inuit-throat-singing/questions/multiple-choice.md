# Katajjaq: Inuit Throat Singing — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Katajjaq singers stand…
a) back to back  b) face to face  c) in a long line
**Answer: b) face to face** — two singers stand face to face.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The sounds are made with…
a) a drum and a flute  b) the throat, mouth and nose  c) the hands only
**Answer: b) the throat, mouth and nose** — throat, mouth and nose.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Some katajjaq sounds copy…
a) machines  b) nature — geese and the wind  c) church bells
**Answer: b) nature — geese and the wind** — nature, such as geese and the wind.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Katajjaq was nearly lost when…
a) radios arrived  b) Inuit were moved off the land  c) men began singing it
**Answer: b) Inuit were moved off the land** — in the years when Inuit were moved off the land.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Today, katajjaq is…
a) gone for good  b) learned, taught and performed by many young people across the Arctic  c) sung by women only, as before
**Answer: b) learned, taught and performed by many young people across the Arctic** — many young people learn it, teach it and perform it; men now sing it too.
