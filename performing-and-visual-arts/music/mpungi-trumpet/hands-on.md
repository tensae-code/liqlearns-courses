# Hands-on — The Mpungi Trumpet

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The mpungi — trumpet of horn and tusk
Work through this part with real materials or your own body, the way the book sets it: the mpungi — Angola's trumpet of antelope horn and of elephant tusk, an aerophone sounded by the air vibrating inside its tube, used for communication and for the enthronement ceremonies of a chief. Show a companion what you made or did, and say what the part teaches.
