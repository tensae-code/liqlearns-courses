**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The Angola book teaches this lesson as…

**Answer: a) the mpungi, Angola's trumpet of horn and tusk.**

**3.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) The mpungi — trumpet of horn and tusk  b) long division  c) map coordinates
**Answer: a) The mpungi — trumpet of horn and tusk.**

**4.** The parts of this lesson come from…
a) Angola's own Educação Manual e Plástica / Educação Musical books  b) a newspaper  c) a song chorus
