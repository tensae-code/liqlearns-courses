# Video script — The Mpungi Trumpet

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

**The mpungi — trumpet of horn and tusk.** The mpungi — Angola's trumpet of antelope horn and of elephant tusk, an aerophone sounded by the air vibrating inside its tube, used for communication and for the enthronement ceremonies of a chief.

That is The Mpungi Trumpet — Angola's own books teach it this way.
