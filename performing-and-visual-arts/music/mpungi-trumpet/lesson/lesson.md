## Sub lesson 1 — The mpungi — trumpet of horn and tusk

The mpungi — Angola's trumpet of antelope horn and of elephant tusk, an aerophone sounded by the air vibrating inside its tube, used for communication and for the enthronement ceremonies of a chief.
