The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

This is Malaysia's own lesson.

**Malay gamelan of Pahang.** Unit 1 Apresiasi: Gamelan — Malay gamelan of Pahang: saron baron, saron pekin, gambang kayu and kromong carry the melody, gendang the beat, gong marks time, kenong marks phrases; Hornbostel-Sachs classes; played at royal and official ceremonies; Unit 1 Gamelan Melayu — Malay gamelan as a performed ensemble: its instruments and discipline, inside the unit Perpaduan dan Keharmonian (filed under the Book 2 lesson slug music/gamelan-melayu).

**Performing named gamelan pieces from scores.** Units 1–3 — performing named gamelan pieces from scores: Timang Burung, Togok and Topeng, and reviewing a gamelan performance (ulasan dan penilaian persembahan gamelan); Units 3–4 Gamelan — performing the gamelan pieces Perang and Kunang-kunang Mabuk (filed under the Book 10 lesson slug music/gamelan-repertoire).

That is Gamelan — Malaysia's way, kept by name.
