# Lesson plan — Gamelan

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Music · **Lesson:** Gamelan
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 3 — Music (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The gamelan of Indonesia and Malaysia — Balinese and Malay gamelan, their gong orchestras and repertoire, played in ensemble.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the course's earlier lessons in its title; recommended first, never forced.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of ..." in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Malay gamelan of Pahang** — Unit 1 Apresiasi: Gamelan — Malay gamelan of Pahang: saron baron, saron pekin, gambang kayu and kromong carry the melody, gendang the beat, gong marks time, kenong marks phrases; Hornbostel-Sachs classes; played at royal and official ceremonies; Unit 1 Gamelan Melayu — Malay gamelan as a performed ensemble: its instruments and discipline, inside the unit Perpaduan dan Keharmonian (filed under the Book 2 lesson slug music/gamelan-melayu).
2. **Performing named gamelan pieces from scores** — Units 1–3 — performing named gamelan pieces from scores: Timang Burung, Togok and Topeng, and reviewing a gamelan performance (ulasan dan penilaian persembahan gamelan); Units 3–4 Gamelan — performing the gamelan pieces Perang and Kunang-kunang Mabuk (filed under the Book 10 lesson slug music/gamelan-repertoire).

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
