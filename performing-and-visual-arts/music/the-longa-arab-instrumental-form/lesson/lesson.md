## Sub lesson 1 — Arab Instrumental Forms: the Longa

The Longa, a light instrumental form that came from the Balkans into Arab music — four sections (khanas) each followed by the repeated taslim refrain, built on simple rhythms such as Fox 2/4, each khana with its own melodic task from stating the maqam to fast modulations; named works by Adham Afandi and Jamil Aweys, and Riyad Al-Sunbati's Longa printed in full for reading.
