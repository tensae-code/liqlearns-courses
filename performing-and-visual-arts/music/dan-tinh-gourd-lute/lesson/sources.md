| Vietnam — Lesson 13, Music appreciation — the đàn tính (tính tẩu) — it appeared in Vietnam long ago and is popular in the folk cultural life of some mountain peoples such as the Tày, Nùng and Thái; the instrument's body (bầu đàn) is made from half of a dried gourd cut across, with a small hole in the shape of a hoa thị cut into it to let the sound escape; the instrument's face is made from a thin piece of wood and its neck is round and has no frets; the đàn tính comes in a two-string kind and a three-string kind; in the traditional way of playing, the player plucks with the index finger of the right hand; the đàn tính's sound is gentle and unhurried (êm dịu, thanh thoát), and it is often used to accompany Then singing (hát then) (student book) | Book 1, printed p. 55 |
