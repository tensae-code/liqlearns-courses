# Doina and Ciocîrlia

## Do it first

Name one thing that belongs in this lesson — Romania and Moldova's doina — the slow, free song of longing — and Ciocîrlia, the lark piece that answers it. Say what it is in your own words. If you can name one, you are already inside the lesson.

## Sub lesson 1 — Doina şi Ciocîrlia

Theme 2, Doina şi Ciocîrlia — the doina, the people's oldest song, expressing longing (dor) and grief; and "Ciocîrlia" (The Lark), the virtuoso lăutărească melody imitating the lark's trill, played on violin and nai by masters such as Nicolae Botgros and Gheorghe Zamfir.

## Sub lesson 2 — Doina

Unit 1, Doina — the doina in its vocal, instrumental and vocal-instrumental kinds, the shepherd's doina and the doina-dance, sung by Maria Lătărețu.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson holds, part by part, in your own words. Companion checks: did every part get named, with its Moldovan name kept where the book gives one?

## Do It Yourself

1. Choose one named part of this lesson and find out one more true thing about it — from a person who knows it, or from the book your part came from.
2. Make or perform a small piece in the way of that part: a rhythm, a step, a drawing, a shaped piece of clay.
3. Write one sentence: what does this part show about Moldova's own way of teaching its arts?
