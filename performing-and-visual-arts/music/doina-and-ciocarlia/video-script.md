# Video script — Doina and Ciocîrlia

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This is Moldova's own lesson.

**Doina şi Ciocîrlia.** Theme 2, Doina şi Ciocîrlia — the doina, the people's oldest song, expressing longing (dor) and grief; and "Ciocîrlia" (The Lark), the virtuoso lăutărească melody imitating the lark's trill, played on violin and nai by masters such as Nicolae Botgros and Gheorghe Zamfir.

**Doina.** Unit 1, Doina — the doina in its vocal, instrumental and vocal-instrumental kinds, the shepherd's doina and the doina-dance, sung by Maria Lătărețu.

That is Doina and Ciocîrlia — Moldova's way, kept by name.
