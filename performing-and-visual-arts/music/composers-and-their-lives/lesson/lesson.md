# Composers and Their Lives

## Do it first

Name one thing that belongs in this lesson — The world's composers told as stories — Mozart the child prodigy, Beethoven, Xian Xinghai and others — and the music each life produced.. Say what it is in your own words. If you can name one, you are already inside the lesson.

## Sub lesson 1 — Lesson 4 (composers)

the piece heard is by the Austrian composer Joseph Haydn, among the Classical era's most important musicians, called "Father of the Symphony" beside Mozart and Beethoven — reading and singing the solfege of part of a Haydn piece.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson holds, part by part, in your own words. Companion checks: did every part get named, with its Syrian name kept where the book gives one?

## Do It Yourself

1. Choose one named part of this lesson and find out one more true thing about it — from a person who knows it, or from the book your part came from.
2. Make or perform a small piece in the way of that part: a rhythm, a step, a drawing, a shaped piece of clay.
3. Write one sentence: what does this part show about Syria's own way of teaching its arts?
