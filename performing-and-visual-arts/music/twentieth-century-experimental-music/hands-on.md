# Hands-on — Twentieth-Century Experimental Music

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the lesson's thing
Work through the lesson's first part yourself: Το ταξίδι της μουσικής στον 20ό αι.: Μουσικοί πειραματισμοί και ανατροπές (Music's 20th-century journey: experiments and overturnings). Show a companion what you made or did, and say what the book calls it.

## Show and tell
Each learner shows their work and names the part of the lesson it comes from. The companion checks the name against the lesson.
