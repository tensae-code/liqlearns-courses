This lesson is Twentieth-Century Experimental Music.

Music's 20th-century journey — the experiments and overturnings that remade what music could be. — as Greece's school books teach it: hearing Satie's "Gymnopédies"; composing your own minimalist piece on the staff from Philip Glass' repeating rhythmic pattern.

Watch, listen — then say it back in your own words.
