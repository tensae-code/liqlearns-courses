# Video script — Musical Genres and Song Kinds

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This is Moldova's own lesson.

**Balada.** Unit 1, Balada — the folk ballad and its kinds (fantastic, haiduc, historical, family, shepherd's), with "Miorița" sung by Nicolae Sulac.

**Varietatea genurilor muzicale.** Unit 4, Varietatea genurilor muzicale — music's genres sorted by their means: vocal genres, instrumental genres, and the dramatic genres where arts join; song, dance and march are the primary genres.

**Cantata și oratoriul.** Unit 4, Cantata și oratoriul — the cantata (arias, recitatives, duets, choruses) against the oratorio, bigger and dramatic; Handel, master of the oratorio, and the "Hallelujah" chorus from "Messiah".

**Genurile instrumentale.** Unit 4, Genurile instrumentale — how instrumental music describes: the sonata (Beethoven's "Spring" Sonata), the concerto (Vivaldi's "Seasons", three movements fast–slow–fast), quartet, suite and symphony.

**creative listening.** Unit 2, creative listening — the ballad "Miorița" heard in Nicolae Sulac's singing, and the verses picked out where man's bond with nature speaks.

**Clasificarea genurilor.** Unit 3, Clasificarea genurilor — the classification of instrumental genres three ways: by means (vocal, instrumental, dramatic), by forces (solo, chamber of 2–30 players, symphonic), and by destination (cultivated, folk, light/jazz/entertainment).

**summary chart.** Unit 3, summary chart — the families of instrumental genres side by side: solo (piece, prelude, nocturne, dance, sonata), chamber (trio to septet) and orchestral (concert, suite, symphony, poem, overture).

That is Musical Genres and Song Kinds — Moldova's way, kept by name.
