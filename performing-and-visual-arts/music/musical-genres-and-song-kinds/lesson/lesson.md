# Musical Genres and Song Kinds

## Do it first

Name one thing that belongs in this lesson — The variety of musical genres — the ballad, cantata and oratorio among them — and the genres of song and instrumental music told apart by character. Say what it is in your own words. If you can name one, you are already inside the lesson.

## Sub lesson 1 — Balada

Unit 1, Balada — the folk ballad and its kinds (fantastic, haiduc, historical, family, shepherd's), with "Miorița" sung by Nicolae Sulac.

## Sub lesson 2 — Varietatea genurilor muzicale

Unit 4, Varietatea genurilor muzicale — music's genres sorted by their means: vocal genres, instrumental genres, and the dramatic genres where arts join; song, dance and march are the primary genres.

## Sub lesson 3 — Cantata și oratoriul

Unit 4, Cantata și oratoriul — the cantata (arias, recitatives, duets, choruses) against the oratorio, bigger and dramatic; Handel, master of the oratorio, and the "Hallelujah" chorus from "Messiah".

## Sub lesson 4 — Genurile instrumentale

Unit 4, Genurile instrumentale — how instrumental music describes: the sonata (Beethoven's "Spring" Sonata), the concerto (Vivaldi's "Seasons", three movements fast–slow–fast), quartet, suite and symphony.

## Sub lesson 5 — creative listening

Unit 2, creative listening — the ballad "Miorița" heard in Nicolae Sulac's singing, and the verses picked out where man's bond with nature speaks.

## Sub lesson 6 — Clasificarea genurilor

Unit 3, Clasificarea genurilor — the classification of instrumental genres three ways: by means (vocal, instrumental, dramatic), by forces (solo, chamber of 2–30 players, symphonic), and by destination (cultivated, folk, light/jazz/entertainment).

## Sub lesson 7 — summary chart

Unit 3, summary chart — the families of instrumental genres side by side: solo (piece, prelude, nocturne, dance, sonata), chamber (trio to septet) and orchestral (concert, suite, symphony, poem, overture).

3. Write one sentence: what does this part show about Moldova's own way of teaching its arts?
