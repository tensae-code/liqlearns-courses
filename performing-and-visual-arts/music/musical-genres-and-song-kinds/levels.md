# Musical Genres and Song Kinds — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — Balada
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Unit 1, Balada — the folk ballad and its kinds (fantastic, haiduc, historical, family, shepherd's), with "Miorița" sung by Nicolae Sulac.

## Sub lesson 2 — Varietatea genurilor muzicale
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Unit 4, Varietatea genurilor muzicale — music's genres sorted by their means: vocal genres, instrumental genres, and the dramatic genres where arts join; song, dance and march are the primary genres.

## Sub lesson 3 — Cantata și oratoriul
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Unit 4, Cantata și oratoriul — the cantata (arias, recitatives, duets, choruses) against the oratorio, bigger and dramatic; Handel, master of the oratorio, and the "Hallelujah" chorus from "Messiah".

## Sub lesson 4 — Genurile instrumentale
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Unit 4, Genurile instrumentale — how instrumental music describes: the sonata (Beethoven's "Spring" Sonata), the concerto (Vivaldi's "Seasons", three movements fast–slow–fast), quartet, suite and symphony.

## Sub lesson 5 — creative listening
Sub lesson 5 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Unit 2, creative listening — the ballad "Miorița" heard in Nicolae Sulac's singing, and the verses picked out where man's bond with nature speaks.

## Sub lesson 6 — Clasificarea genurilor
Sub lesson 6 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Unit 3, Clasificarea genurilor — the classification of instrumental genres three ways: by means (vocal, instrumental, dramatic), by forces (solo, chamber of 2–30 players, symphonic), and by destination (cultivated, folk, light/jazz/entertainment).

## Sub lesson 7 — summary chart
Sub lesson 7 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Unit 3, summary chart — the families of instrumental genres side by side: solo (piece, prelude, nocturne, dance, sonata), chamber (trio to septet) and orchestral (concert, suite, symphony, poem, overture).
