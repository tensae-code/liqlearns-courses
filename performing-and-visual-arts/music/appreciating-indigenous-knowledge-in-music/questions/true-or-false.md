# Appreciating Indigenous Knowledge in Music — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 6)** Playing the krar, mesenqo or begena is itself the indigenous knowledge being appreciated.
**Answer: False** — the book's keyed false — the playing accompanies; the knowledge honoured is the craft and culture the instruments carry.

**2. (Sub lesson 6)** Paper mosaic cannot be used to appreciate indigenous knowledge.
**Answer: False** — keyed false — the visual table checks making indigenous artworks in paper mosaic.

**3. (Sub lesson 6)** In music appreciation, the craftsperson is honoured too, not only the work.
**Answer: True** — the exercise's first item, and the summary's honouring rule.

**4. (Sub lesson 6)** Theatre, film and photography have only a low role in appreciating indigenous knowledge.
**Answer: False** — keyed false — the chapter appreciates through them as through music.
