# Appreciating Indigenous Knowledge in Music — short answer

Answer in a short sentence or a short list.


**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why is unwritten knowledge not lesser knowledge?
**Answer:** It is tested knowledge, proved by centuries of use — the instruments still sound and the songs still fit their occasions.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What do you ask at the weaving workplace?
**Answer:** What clothes weaving can make — the kinds of woven clothes and what each is for.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Pick one instrument and write its appreciation in two sentences.
**Answer:** Sentences naming the instrument, what it is made from, the knowledge its making holds and the occasion it serves.

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Why answer knowledge with performance?
**Answer:** Because the community's knowledge lives in performance — poem, music and eskista honour it in its own currency.
