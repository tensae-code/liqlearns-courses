# Appreciating Indigenous Knowledge in Music — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.


**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Indigenous knowledge is…
  a) Knowledge a community built and carried across generations b) Knowledge copied from foreign books c) Knowledge only teachers hold
**Answer: a) Knowledge a community built and carried across generations** — carried, practised and proved by use across generations.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In music, indigenous knowledge lives in…
  a) Instrument making and playing, and songs fitted to occasions b) Only in printed scores c) Only in examinations
**Answer: a) Instrument making and playing, and songs fitted to occasions** — the making, the playing and the occasion-fitting are the knowledge.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Exercise 4.1 expresses appreciation in…
  a) Poem, music and eskista b) A written examination c) Silence
**Answer: a) Poem, music and eskista** — the three answering arts the exercise names.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** If no weaving workplace is near, the book says to…
  a) Ask family or neighbours about the woven clothes they wear b) Skip the exercise c) Appreciate a factory instead
**Answer: a) Ask family or neighbours about the woven clothes they wear** — the book's own fallback.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** An instrument appreciated fully is named with…
  a) Its materials, its maker-knowledge and its occasion b) Only its price c) Only its size
**Answer: a) Its materials, its maker-knowledge and its occasion** — the appreciation covers making, material and use.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** The washint's indigenous knowledge includes knowing that it is made from…
  a) Bamboo (reed) b) Iron sheet c) Glass
**Answer: a) Bamboo (reed)** — the bamboo flute — material knowledge carried by makers.

**7. (Sub lesson 4)** The guide leads appreciation by…
  a) Visiting, asking and performing back b) Testing first, visiting never c) Reading aloud only
**Answer: a) Visiting, asking and performing back** — the teacher's side of Exercises 4.1/4.2.

**8. (Sub lesson 4)** This part's standing in the lesson is…
  a) Labelled teacher-guide evidence b) Pupil-book teaching c) A transcribed song
**Answer: a) Labelled teacher-guide evidence** — guide material is evidence and is labelled so.

**9. (Sub lesson 5)** Traditional instruments, in Grade 6 Chapter 4's aims, are known as…
  a) Products of indigenous knowledge b) Factory goods c) Foreign gifts
**Answer: a) Products of indigenous knowledge** — the opener's own framing.

**10. (Sub lesson 5)** Appreciation in this chapter is done by eskista as well as by…
  a) Composed melodies and poems b) Examinations c) Silence
**Answer: a) Composed melodies and poems** — compose and sing appreciation melodies and poems.

**11. (Sub lesson 6)** Which list is the summary's INTANGIBLE heritage?
  a) Governance, medicine, food science, farming, calendar, astronomy, literature b) Architecture, painting, sculpture, jewellery c) Clothes and daily tools
**Answer: a) Governance, medicine, food science, farming, calendar, astronomy, literature** — the summary's own intangible list.

**12. (Sub lesson 6)** Traditional medicine, in the summary, stands as…
  a) A starting point for modern medicine b) Opposed to all learning c) A modern invention
**Answer: a) A starting point for modern medicine** — the summary places traditional medicine among the knowledges, as modern medicine's starting point.
