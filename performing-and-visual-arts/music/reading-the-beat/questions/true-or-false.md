# Reading the Beat — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 6)** Sight-reading a melody and reading a beat are the same skill, in the Chapter 2 summary.
**Answer: False** — the summary sets them apart — beat-reading is the fundamental; melody sight-reading builds on it.

**2. (Sub lesson 6)** Beyond C major, accidentals are needed to build and read other scales.
**Answer: True** — table two's own item — accidentals are needed beyond C major.
