# Reading the Beat — short answer

Answer in a short sentence or a short list.


**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why does the book call reading the beat music's most fundamental thing?
**Answer:** Every other reading — notes, melodies, sol-fa — must be held in time; without the beat, the sounds have no shared order.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which instrument's figure accompanies this exercise?
**Answer:** The tsenatsil (Fig. 15 in the book).

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which beat instrument is figured in this part of the exercise?
**Answer:** The atamo (Fig. 16 in the book).

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Name the sol-fa syllables this exercise reads, in order.
**Answer:** Do re mi fa sol la (and back).

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** Name the chapter's five visual-strand aims in one list.
**Answer:** Composition; still-life painting; stencil printing; assembled sculpture; calligraphy.

**6. (Sub lesson 6)** I can… name table one's six visual self-check items.
**Answer:** Composition; still-life steps; assembling; stencil; Amharic calligraphy; expressing feeling in film/theatre/photography.
