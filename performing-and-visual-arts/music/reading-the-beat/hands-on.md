# Hands-on — Reading the Beat

The lesson with your hands, your feet and your voice together, not only your eyes.

## Three-form reading
Read one short row three ways: by its letters, off-staff, and in sol-fa — a partner keeps the beat on an atamo or by clapping.

## No-stop rule
Read a row through without stopping, even over a mistake. Mark the mistake, finish the row, then repair it.
