This lesson is Emotions in Music.

Music is linked to human feelings — joy, sadness or enthusiasm; listening to recordings, describing what I feel with each piece and linking it to the pictured sad girl, military band and dabka dancers; music plays an important role in influencing a person's feeling, so it is used in films, series and stage works as part of the work — it is called pictorial (programme) music; the artist Antonio Vivaldi, the Italian composer and violinist who wrote some 400 pieces, among them The Four Seasons, the most famous of his works.

Watch, listen — then say it back in your own words.
