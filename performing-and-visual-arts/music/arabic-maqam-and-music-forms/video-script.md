# Video script — Arabic Maqam and Music Forms

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Every Arab melody lives in a maqam — a scale with its own character.
ʿAjam first: built on Do, no quarter-tone signs — its steps run 4-4-2-4-4-4-2.
Then Rast, the fundamental maqam: its third and seventh degrees lowered by a quarter tone — the half-flat.
Sing them up. Sing them down. Hear where Rast parts from the C major scale.
