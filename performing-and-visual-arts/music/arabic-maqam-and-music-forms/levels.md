## Sub lesson 1 — Maqam ʿAjam
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. ## Sub lesson 2 — Maqam Rast
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: say what this part teaches, in the book's own terms — Rast, the fundamental maqam of Arabic music: its scale with the third and seventh degrees lowered by a quarter tone (half-flat), its tetrachords (Rast on Do, Nahawand on Sol), a point-by-point comparison with the C major scale, and Rast sung ascending and descending, with reading exercises in the maqam.
