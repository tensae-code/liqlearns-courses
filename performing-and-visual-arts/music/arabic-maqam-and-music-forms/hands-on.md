# Hands-on — Arabic Maqam and Music Forms

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Sing the two maqamat
Sing Maqam ʿAjam ascending and descending, naming its two tetrachords (ʿAjam on Do, ʿAjam on Sol). Then sing Maqam Rast the same way, and point to the degrees — the third and the seventh — where the quarter-tone half-flat makes it differ from C major.
