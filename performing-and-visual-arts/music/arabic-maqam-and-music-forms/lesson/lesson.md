# Arabic Maqam and Music Forms

## Sub lesson 1 — Maqam ʿAjam

ʿAjam defined as the maqam built on Do with no quarter-tone signs in its scale, its intervals measured (4-4-2-4-4-4-2), its two tetrachords named (ʿAjam on Do, ʿAjam on Sol), and the maqam sung ascending and descending.

## Sub lesson 2 — Maqam Rast

Rast, the fundamental maqam of Arabic music: its scale with the third and seventh degrees lowered by a quarter tone (half-flat), its tetrachords (Rast on Do, Nahawand on Sol), a point-by-point comparison with the C major scale, and Rast sung ascending and descending, with reading exercises in the maqam.
