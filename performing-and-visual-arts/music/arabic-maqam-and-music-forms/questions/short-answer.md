# Arabic Maqam and Music Forms — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name ʿAjam’s two tetrachords.
**Answer: ʿAjam on Do and ʿAjam on Sol.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Where do Rast and the C major scale part company?
**Answer: At the third and seventh degrees, which Rast lowers by a quarter tone (half-flat).**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What sign lowers a note by a quarter tone in Arabic music?
**Answer: The half-flat sign.**
