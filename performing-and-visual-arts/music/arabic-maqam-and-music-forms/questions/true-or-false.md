# Arabic Maqam and Music Forms — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Maqam ʿAjam has quarter-tone signs in its scale.
**Answer: False — ʿAjam is built on Do with no quarter-tone signs.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Rast is sung ascending and descending in the guide’s exercises.
**Answer: True.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The maqam is a system of scales in Arab music.
**Answer: True.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Rast and C major are exactly the same scale.
**Answer: False — they part at the third and seventh degrees, lowered by a quarter tone in Rast.**
