# Arabic Maqam and Music Forms — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Maqam ʿAjam is built on…
a) Do, with no quarter-tone signs in its scale  b) Sol, with two quarter tones  c) La, with sharps only
**Answer: a) Do, with no quarter-tone signs in its scale**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The intervals of ʿAjam are measured…
a) 2-2-2-2-2-2-2  b) 4-4-2-4-4-4-2  c) 4-2-4-2-4-2-4
**Answer: b) 4-4-2-4-4-4-2**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Rast is…
a) a European key  b) the fundamental maqam of Arabic music  c) a drum rhythm
**Answer: b) the fundamental maqam of Arabic music**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In Rast, the third and seventh degrees are…
a) raised by a whole step  b) left natural  c) lowered by a quarter tone (half-flat)
**Answer: c) lowered by a quarter tone (half-flat)**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Rast’s two tetrachords are…
a) Rast on Do and Nahawand on Sol  b) ʿAjam on Do and ʿAjam on Sol  c) two C major halves
**Answer: a) Rast on Do and Nahawand on Sol**
