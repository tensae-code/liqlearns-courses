# Papua New Guinea Instruments and Music — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The kundu drum accompanies traditional dance in these guides.
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** PNG's instruments all sound the same.
**Answer: False — learners distinguish garamut, kundu, bamboo, rattles and guitar by sound and duration.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Church hymnals are used to prepare for reading notation.
**Answer: True.**
