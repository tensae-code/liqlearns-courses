# Papua New Guinea Instruments and Music — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The kundu is…
a) a Papua New Guinea drum  b) a string instrument
**Answer: a) a Papua New Guinea drum.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The garamut is…
a) a slit-gong of Papua New Guinea  b) a flute
**Answer: a) a slit-gong of Papua New Guinea.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Grade 5 tells PNG's instruments apart by…
a) their sounds and duration  b) their colour
**Answer: a) their sounds and duration.**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** The class song collection gathers…
a) church hymns, popular songs and PNG music-industry songs  b) only foreign songs
**Answer: a) church hymns, popular songs and PNG music-industry songs.**
