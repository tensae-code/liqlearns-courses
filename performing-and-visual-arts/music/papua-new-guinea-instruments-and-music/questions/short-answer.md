# Papua New Guinea Instruments and Music — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name three local sound makers from the Grade 3 guide.
**Answer: Kundu drums, rattles, coconut-shell percussion and shakers (any three).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What two kinds of instruments does the guide sort at the start?
**Answer: Factory-made instruments and home-made instruments.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the class do with at least one collected song?
**Answer: Performs it to the class.**
