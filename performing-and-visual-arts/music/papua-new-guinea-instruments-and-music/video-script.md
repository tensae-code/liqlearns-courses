# Video script — Papua New Guinea Instruments and Music

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

**Kundu drums and local sound makers.** The Grade 3 guide sorts factory-made instruments from home-made ones, then meets the local sound makers: kundu drums, rattles, coconut-shell percussion and shakers — thump-thumping, the simple shaker, the beat cylinder — with melody and pattern played together.

**Instruments as each community's craft.** In Grade 4 the instruments return as crafted objects: musical instruments from different communities, met as things each community makes.

**Hearing PNG's instruments.** Grade 5 listens closely: distinguishing the sounds of Papua New Guinea's instruments — garamut, kundu, bamboo, rattles and guitar — by their duration, learning the families of instruments, and performing a traditional dance using the instruments.

**The PNG music industry and song collections.** Grades 6–7 collect the nation's music: a class collection of known songs — church hymns, popular songs and songs from the PNG music industry — at least one performed to the class, and songs sung from printed notation in church hymnals, with the National Anthem sung in two- and three-part harmony.
