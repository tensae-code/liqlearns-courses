# Metre and Time Signatures

## Do it first

Name one thing that belongs in this lesson — Metre and time signatures — strong and weak beats, music in two and three beats, and the anacrusis, the upbeat before the bar. Say what it is in your own words. If you can name one, you are already inside the lesson.

## Sub lesson 1 — Anacruza

Unit 3, Anacruza — the upbeat: a melody that begins before the first strong beat, and marking it in your own improvised melodies.

## Sub lesson 2 — creative work

Unit 1, creative work — improvising a melody that begins with an anacrusis (upbeat) in 3/4.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson holds, part by part, in your own words. Companion checks: did every part get named, with its Moldovan name kept where the book gives one?

## Do It Yourself

1. Choose one named part of this lesson and find out one more true thing about it — from a person who knows it, or from the book your part came from.
2. Make or perform a small piece in the way of that part: a rhythm, a step, a drawing, a shaped piece of clay.
3. Write one sentence: what does this part show about Moldova's own way of teaching its arts?
