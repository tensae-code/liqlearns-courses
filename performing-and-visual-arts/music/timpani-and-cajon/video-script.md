This lesson is Timpani and Cajón.

Some percussion instruments produce pitched tones when played — the timpani, large tuned drums played with soft-headed mallets — and some produce unpitched beat sounds only — the cajon, an unpitched instrument like a box, the player sitting on it and playing it with both hands; why drums are beaten in wars.

Watch, listen — then say it back in your own words.
