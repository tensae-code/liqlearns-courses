# Ethnomusicology and Musical Folklore

## Do it first

Name one thing that belongs in this lesson — Ethnomusicology and musical folklore — how a people's music is studied, researched in a project, and presented. Say what it is in your own words. If you can name one, you are already inside the lesson.

## Sub lesson 1 — Етномузикологија

Етномузикологија — ethnomusicology: the science that studies musical folklore — and why Macedonia's crossroads position (the Via Egnatia from Durrës through Ohrid and Bitola to Constantinople) mixed the influences its folk creation carries.

## Sub lesson 2 — Македонски музички фолклор

Македонски музички фолклор — musical folklore and its marks: bound to the people's life and work, passed mouth to mouth, its maker anonymous, forever varying, and syncretic — song, dance, game and mime woven together.

## Sub lesson 3 — Фолклорна плетенка

Фолклорна плетенка — the folklore braid: Macedonia as a multinational land where the cultures, customs and folklore of Macedonians, Albanians, Turks, Serbs, Bosniaks, Roma and the other communities have intertwined for centuries.

## Do It Yourself
