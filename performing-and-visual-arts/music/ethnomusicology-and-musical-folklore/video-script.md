# Video script — Ethnomusicology and Musical Folklore

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This is North Macedonia's own lesson.

**Етномузикологија.** Етномузикологија — ethnomusicology: the science that studies musical folklore — and why Macedonia's crossroads position (the Via Egnatia from Durrës through Ohrid and Bitola to Constantinople) mixed the influences its folk creation carries.

**Македонски музички фолклор.** Македонски музички фолклор — musical folklore and its marks: bound to the people's life and work, passed mouth to mouth, its maker anonymous, forever varying, and syncretic — song, dance, game and mime woven together.

**Фолклорна плетенка.** Фолклорна плетенка — the folklore braid: Macedonia as a multinational land where the cultures, customs and folklore of Macedonians, Albanians, Turks, Serbs, Bosniaks, Roma and the other communities have intertwined for centuries.

That is Ethnomusicology and Musical Folklore — North Macedonia's way, kept by name.
