# The Voice and Its Types — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which is part of the voice's anatomy as the sequence names it?
a) the larynx  b) the elbow  c) the knee
**Answer: a) the larynx** — the larynx — beside pharynx, rib cage, mouth and nose cavities.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Mechanism II is…
a) chest voice (voix de poitrine)  b) whistle voice  c) fry
**Answer: a) chest voice (voix de poitrine)** — chest voice — voix de poitrine.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Whistle voice lies beyond…
a) 1,500 Hz  b) 50 Hz  c) 10 Hz
**Answer: a) 1,500 Hz** — 1,500 Hz — and is rarely used in singing.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Voice types are told apart by…
a) tessitura and timbre  b) height and weight  c) age only
**Answer: a) tessitura and timbre** — tessitura and timbre.

**5. (Sub lesson 4)** In singing, the ear's captured message leads the brain to command…
a) the larynx and vocal cords  b) the fingers  c) the feet
**Answer: a) the larynx and vocal cords** — the larynx and vocal cords, set for the pitch heard.
