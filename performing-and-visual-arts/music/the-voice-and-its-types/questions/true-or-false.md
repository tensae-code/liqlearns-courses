# The Voice and Its Types — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The mouth and nose cavities are part of the voice's anatomy.
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** There are four laryngeal mechanisms.
**Answer: True.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Soprano, tenor, baritone and bass are types of voice.
**Answer: True.**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** The ear plays no part in singing.
**Answer: False — the ear captures the sound and the brain commands the larynx for the pitch heard.**
