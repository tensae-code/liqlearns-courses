# The Voice and Its Types — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name three parts of the voice's anatomy.
**Answer: Larynx, pharynx, rib cage (cage thoracique), mouth and nose cavities — any three.**

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** What is tessitura?
**Answer: The span from the lowest to the highest note a voice can give.**

**3. (Sub lesson 4)** How long does the sequence warm the voice up in scales?
**Answer: 10 to 30 minutes.**
