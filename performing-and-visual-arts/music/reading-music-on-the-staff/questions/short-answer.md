# Reading Music on the Staff — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the staff for?
**Answer: Notes and rests are placed on it and read from it — it is music's writing board.**

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** What is music's smallest distance of sound?
**Answer: The semitone — a half step.**

**3. (Sub lesson 4)** By how much does a flat lower a sound?
**Answer: By a semitone (a half step).**

**4. (Sub lesson 5)** How many ledger-line plates does the book show?
**Answer: Four — G clef above and below, F clef above and below.**

**5. (Sub lesson 6)** What does the bottom number of a time signature tell?
**Answer: Which note-kind measures the beat.**

**6. (Sub lesson 7)** List the sign inventory in order as the part teaches it.
**Answer:** Staff (lines and spaces); G and F clefs; notes and rests with values; accidentals (sharp, flat, natural); bars and bar lines; key signatures; time signatures.

**7. (Sub lesson 8)** Do it and show it: what are the two practical items of the Grade 4 Chapter 2 closing exercise?
**Answer:** Draw both clefs (G and F) on a staff; perform a chosen regional dance.

**8. (Sub lesson 8)** Name three topics of the Grade 4 Chapter 2 closing exercise besides the staff.
**Answer:** Any three of: clef positions; collage materials; dance identification; theatre elements.

**9. (Sub lesson 9)** I can… name three of Grade 5 Chapter 2's aims that are NOT about notes.
**Answer:** Any three of: calligraphy and its tools; mosaic; block (relief) printing; modelling; appreciating children's theatres and staging simple ones.

**10. (Sub lesson 10)** Name three of Chapter 2's making aims.
**Answer:** Any three of: batik's making methods; candle-dyeing in practice; silk-cloth printing in practice; sculpture's making and materials / making varied sculpture kinds.

**11. (Sub lesson 11)** Name the two papier-mâché methods the summary counts.
**Answer:** Kneaded-paper modelling (paper soaked and kneaded, mixed with paste, formed like clay) and pasting the kneaded paper over a built form of wire and wood — the summary's two methods.
