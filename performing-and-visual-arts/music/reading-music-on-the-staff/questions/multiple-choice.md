# Reading Music on the Staff — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A staff has…
  a) Five lines and four spaces b) Six lines and five spaces c) Four lines and five spaces
**Answer: a) Five lines and four spaces** — the staff has five lines and four spaces.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The G clef rises from which line?
  a) The second line b) The fourth line c) The top line
**Answer: a) The second line** — the G clef rises from the staff's second line.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The F clef's two dots stand around which line?
  a) The fourth line b) The second line c) The first line
**Answer: a) The fourth line** — the F clef rises from line 4 and its dots hug that line.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** A bar is…
  a) The space between two bar lines b) A single vertical line c) The last note of a piece
**Answer: a) The space between two bar lines** — a bar is the space between two bar lines; the line itself is the bar line.

**5. (Sub lesson 4)** A natural sign (♮) does what?
  a) Cancels a sharp or flat and restores the sound b) Raises the sound by a semitone c) Lowers the sound by a whole tone
**Answer: a) Cancels a sharp or flat and restores the sound** — the natural cancels a raise or lower and restores the sound.

**6. (Sub lesson 5)** Ledger lines are used for notes that…
  a) Rise above or sink below the staff b) Sit in the middle space c) End a piece
**Answer: a) Rise above or sink below the staff** — ledger lines carry notes beyond the staff, above and below.

**7. (Sub lesson 6)** In 4/4, the top number 4 tells…
  a) How many beats are in a bar b) How fast to play c) How many lines the staff has
**Answer: a) How many beats are in a bar** — the top number tells how many beats in a bar.
