# Reading Music on the Staff — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure — README, "Units, Lessons and Sub Lessons"). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number, as before. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. The sub lessons climb inside this one lesson, from the youngest books to the oldest.

## Sub lesson 1 — The staff: five lines, four spaces
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: draw a staff and count its lines and spaces from the bottom up.

## Sub lesson 2 — The two clefs: G and F
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: draw the G clef starting on line 2 and say which sounds it serves. And: draw the F clef on line 4 with its two dots around the line.

## Sub lesson 3 — Bars and bar lines; whole and half steps
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: write bars closed by a double bar line and say what a semitone is.

## Sub lesson 4 — Accidentals: sharp, flat, natural
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: name the five accidentals and what each does to a sound.

## Sub lesson 5 — Ledger lines: notes beyond the staff
Sub lesson 5 questions from the type files. Pass mark: write one note above and one below a staff, each on its ledger line.

## Sub lesson 6 — Time signatures and reading a melody
Sub lesson 6 questions from the type files. Pass mark: read 4/4, 3/4 and 2/4 aloud by their two numbers and apply the stem rule to three notes.

## Sub lesson 7 — Music as written language: the sign inventory (Grade 8 depth)
Sub lesson 7 questions from the type files. Pass mark: recite the sign inventory whole and say what written language means for music, in two sentences.

## Sub lesson 8 — The Grade 4 chapter closes — the staff, the clefs and working together (chapter closing — Grade 4)
Sub lesson 8 questions from the type files. Pass mark: the learner can state the staff's five lines against the book's six-lines false item, place both clefs, and has done the exercise's two practicals — drawing both clefs and performing a chosen regional dance.

## Sub lesson 9 — The Grade 5 chapter opens — notes, melodies, calligraphy, mosaic and theatre (chapter opening — Grade 5)
Sub lesson 9 questions from the type files. Pass mark: the learner can list the Grade 5 Chapter 2 aims and say which lesson-neighbour keeps each aim beyond this lesson's notes and melodies.

## Sub lesson 10 — The Grade 8 chapter opens — the workshop chapter (chapter opening — Grade 8)
Sub lesson 10 questions from the type files. Pass mark: the learner can list the Grade 8 Chapter 2 aims and say which are doings in practice, as the opener marks them.

## Sub lesson 11 — The Grade 8 chapter closes — music written and read, dances, stages and cloth (chapter closing — Grade 8)
Sub lesson 11 questions from the type files. Pass mark: the learner can state the summary's writable/readable claim for music, name batik's ancient-method place in the summary, run the 11-item checklist, and has sounded a melody by voice and played a beat by hand-clapping.
