# Hands-on — Reading Music on the Staff

The lesson with your hands, your feet and your voice together, not only your eyes.

## Draw the house of music
Rule three staves on paper. Number the lines 1–5 upward in the first, the spaces 1–4 upward in the second, and copy the G clef again and again in the third, as the book's activity directs.

## Clap the bars
Write four bars in 4/4 with claps: four claps in every bar, a double clap at the double bar line. Feel the bars as rooms of equal size.
