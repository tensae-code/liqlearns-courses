# Video script — Reading Music on the Staff

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Music has a notebook: five lines, four spaces.
Count from the bottom. Always from the bottom.
The G clef climbs from line two — high sounds live there.
The F clef sits on line four — low sounds live there.
Bars divide. Sharps raise. Flats lower. The natural restores.
