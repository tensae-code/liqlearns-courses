# National Schools in Music

## Do it first

Name one thing that belongs in this lesson — Romanticism's national schools — composers building a music for their own awakened nation. Say what it is in your own words. If you can name one, you are already inside the lesson.

## Sub lesson 1 — Romanticism in the Slavic lands: national schools arise with national awakening

Romanticism in the Slavic lands: national schools arise with national awakening — composers study their people's melodies, dances, poetry, epics and fairy tales and build a national musical language on folk rhythm, modal harmony and legend.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson holds, part by part, in your own words. Companion checks: did every part get named, with its Macedonian name kept where the book gives one?

## Do It Yourself

1. Choose one named part of this lesson and find out one more true thing about it — from a person who knows it, or from the book your part came from.
2. Make or perform a small piece in the way of that part: a rhythm, a step, a drawing, a shaped piece of clay.
3. Write one sentence: what does this part show about North Macedonia's own way of teaching its arts?
