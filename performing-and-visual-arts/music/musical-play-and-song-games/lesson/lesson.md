## Sub lesson 1 — China Grade 3 Music Vol.1

Partner rhythm play — one sings/plays a rhythm, the other answers and imitates, then the patterns get harder

## Sub lesson 2 — China Grade 4 Music Vol.1

Making the Naadam in music — choosing notes for wrestling, filling rhythms and percussion for the horse race, imitating the arrow's sound, and a group dance
