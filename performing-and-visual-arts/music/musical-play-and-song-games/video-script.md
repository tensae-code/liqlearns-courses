This lesson is Musical Play and Song Games.

Games, songs and musical play — singing games and activity songs where music itself is the game. — as China's school books teach it: Partner rhythm play — one sings/plays a rhythm, the other answers and imitates, then the patterns get harder.

Watch, listen — then say it back in your own words.
