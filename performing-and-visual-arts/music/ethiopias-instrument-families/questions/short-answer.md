# Ethiopia's Instrument Families — short answer

Answer in a short sentence or a short list.


**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why is the kebero called a history-teller?
**Answer:** Its body and playing carry history — a rare, long-lived instrument passing history from generation to generation, at festivals and funerals alike.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the string and wind families' members from earlier lessons.
**Answer:** Strings: begena, masenqo, krar. Winds: washint, embilta, meleket.
