# Ethiopia's Instrument Families — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.


**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The kebero gives…
  a) Thick and thin sound b) Only one fixed note c) No sound at all
**Answer: a) Thick and thin sound** — low boom and high tap from one drum — thick and thin.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The kebero is made from…
  a) A hollowed great trunk, hide and cloth b) Bamboo and iron c) Clay and glass
**Answer: a) A hollowed great trunk, hide and cloth** — the Grade 8 portrait's own materials.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Which roll is the beat-giving family?
  a) Kebero, atamo, negarit, dibe, tsenatsil b) Begena, masenqo, krar c) Washint, embilta, meleket
**Answer: a) Kebero, atamo, negarit, dibe, tsenatsil** — the Grade 8 roll of feeling-givers at social and religious systems.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The beat family's shared job is…
  a) Feeling, in time — the pulse under chant and celebration b) Singing the melody c) Writing the notation
**Answer: a) Feeling, in time — the pulse under chant and celebration** — they give more feeling to cultural musics, together, in time.
