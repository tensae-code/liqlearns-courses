# Video script — Ethiopia's Instrument Families

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. Songs are named and summarised, never transcribed.

---

One drum: thick and thin.

Trunk, hide and cloth.

Festivals and funerals —

the same drum carries both.

Kebero, atamo, negarit, dibe, tsenatsil:

feeling, in time.
