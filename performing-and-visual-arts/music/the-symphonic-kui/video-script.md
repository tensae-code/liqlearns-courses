This lesson is The Symphonic Kui.

The Symphonic Kui — Kazakhstan's written professional music — the kui carried into symphonic form. — as Kazakhstan's school books teach it: what it teaches: the symphonic kui — a folk kui grown into a symphonic genre: Gaziza Zhubanova's five-part symphony «Жігер» built on Dauletkerei's kui, with organ in the fourth part; Rakhmadiev's «Дайрабай» and «Құдаша думан».

Watch, listen — then say it back in your own words.
