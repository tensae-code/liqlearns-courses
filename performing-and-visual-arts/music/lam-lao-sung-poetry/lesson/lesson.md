# Lam: Lao Sung Poetry

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about lam: lao sung poetry. Then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — Music G9 teacher guide — lam phu thai

The Phu Thai people's improvised sung verse form (ລໍາຜູ້ໄທ) performed with khene accompaniment, here carrying an anti-drug message in call-and-answer verses (summary only)

## Sub lesson 2 — Music G7 teacher guide — lam khon sawan

The Khon Sawan people's sung folk-drama form performed by a singer with khene (shown in a photograph), its call-and-answer verses telling children to be good, study and help their parents (summary only)

## Sub lesson 3 — Arts & Crafts + Music G3 teacher guide, 2009 edition — lam tang wai

The Lam Tang Wai sung-poem form — a two-column verse text about keeping clean, sung to the khene (drawing of the singer and khene player), and how the singer answers the verses

## Sub lesson 4 — Music G8 teacher guide, 2012 — lam tang wai

How lam tang wai is sung as an exchange between a young man and a young woman, and this piece's theme of love and shyness (summary only)

## Sub lesson 5 — Arts & Crafts and Music G4 teacher guide, 2009 combined guide — lam khon sawan

The lam singing form of Savannakhet, performed as a group piece for Children's Day (summary only)

## Sub lesson 6 — Arts & Crafts and Music G2 teacher guides, original editions — lam phu thai

The lam singing form of the Phu Thai people, performed as a group piece about being a good pupil (summary only)
