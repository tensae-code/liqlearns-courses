# Lesson plan — Lam: Lao Sung Poetry

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Music · **Lesson:** Lam: Lao Sung Poetry
**Place in the curriculum:** Music (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Laos' lam sung-poetry kinds — Lam Khon Sawan, Lam Phu Thai and Lam Tang Wai — each with its songs, singers and occasions.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this course in the same title.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Music G9 teacher guide — lam phu thai** — the Phu Thai people's improvised sung verse form (ລໍາຜູ້ໄທ) performed with khene accompaniment, here carrying an anti-drug message in call-and-answer verses (summary only)
2. **Music G7 teacher guide — lam khon sawan** — the Khon Sawan people's sung folk-drama form performed by a singer with khene (shown in a photograph), its call-and-answer verses telling children to be good, study and help their parents (summary only)
3. **Arts & Crafts + Music G3 teacher guide, 2009 edition — lam tang wai** — the Lam Tang Wai sung-poem form — a two-column verse text about keeping clean, sung to the khene (drawing of the singer and khene player), and how the singer answers the verses
4. **Music G8 teacher guide, 2012 — lam tang wai** — how lam tang wai is sung as an exchange between a young man and a young woman, and this piece's theme of love and shyness (summary only)
5. **Arts & Crafts and Music G4 teacher guide, 2009 combined guide — lam khon sawan** — the lam singing form of Savannakhet, performed as a group piece for Children's Day (summary only)
6. **Arts & Crafts and Music G2 teacher guides, original editions — lam phu thai** — the lam singing form of the Phu Thai people, performed as a group piece about being a good pupil (summary only)
