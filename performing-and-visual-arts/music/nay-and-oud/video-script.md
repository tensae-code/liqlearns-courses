This lesson is The Nay and the Oud.

The nay is among the wind instruments humans have used since ancient times — made of wood, with six holes in front and one hole behind; the oud is among the basic string instruments in Arabic music, known since ancient times, made of wood and holding five strings, with a sixth string able to be added; artists from my country: the nay player Hassan Al-Faqir and the oud player Abdul Razzaq Al-Tubasi.

Watch, listen — then say it back in your own words.
