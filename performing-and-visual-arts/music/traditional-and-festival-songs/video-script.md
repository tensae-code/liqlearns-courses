# Video script — Traditional and Festival Songs

The super-simple voice, for the sing-along video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The songs are named and summarised, never transcribed; the script sings the learner TO the song, not the song itself.

---

Meskel flowers open, yellow and bright.
Girls sing Abebayehush — call, and answer!
Adey, the little daisy, sings after.

New Year comes! Abebaye Hoy — lemlem, lemlem —
blessings for the house.
Boys go door to door: Hoya Hoye! Good wishes for every home.

Buhe! The bonfire is lit — Say Buhe! — sing across the fire.
And at a wedding, the whole village sings the couple well.
