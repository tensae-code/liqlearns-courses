# Hands-on — Traditional and Festival Songs

The lesson with your hands, your feet and your voice together, not only your eyes.

## Call and answer circle
Form two groups facing each other across a pretend bonfire of sticks. Group one calls a line of Buhe or Abebayehush; group two answers. Swap sides. Keep the answers exactly together.

## House to house
In the classroom, set three chairs as three houses. A small group of singers visits each chair singing Hoya Hoye's good wishes; the 'house' answers with thanks. Every house gets its visit.
