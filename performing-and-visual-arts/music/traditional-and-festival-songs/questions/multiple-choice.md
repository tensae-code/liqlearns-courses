# Traditional and Festival Songs — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Abebayehush belongs to which season?
a) Genna  b) the Meskel season  c) the rainy season
**Answer: b) the Meskel season**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Abebayehush is sung —
a) by boys with sticks  b) by girls, in call and response  c) by one singer alone
**Answer: b) by girls, in call and response**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** "አደይ" (Adey) is —
a) a river  b) the yellow daisy  c) a dance
**Answer: b) the yellow daisy**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The full New Year song's responses are —
a) "ሆ" (ho)  b) "ለምለም" (lemlem)  c) "che che"
**Answer: b) "ለምለም" (lemlem)**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Hoya Hoye is sung —
a) house to house, by boys, at New Year  b) in the field at harvest  c) at a wedding
**Answer: a) house to house, by boys, at New Year**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** New Year songs carry —
a) the day's news  b) the community's good wishes  c) the school timetable
**Answer: b) the community's good wishes**

**7. (Sub lesson 3)** Buhe is celebrated on —
a) Debre Tabor day  b) New Year day itself  c) a market day
**Answer: a) Debre Tabor day**

**8. (Sub lesson 3)** Buhe's game melody is —
a) the Fidel song  b) Hoya Hoye, with "ሆ" (ho) responses  c) the ABC song
**Answer: b) Hoya Hoye, with "ሆ" (ho) responses**

**9. (Sub lesson 4)** The wedding song "ሙሽራየ" wishes the couple —
a) a long road home  b) well on the road of marriage  c) many cattle
**Answer: b) well on the road of marriage**

**10. (Sub lesson 4)** Which list names occasions that have their own songs?
a) weddings, religious festivals, grief, baptism, work  b) breakfast, homework, sleep  c) market day only
**Answer: a) weddings, religious festivals, grief, baptism, work**
