# Traditional and Festival Songs — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Hoya Hoye's words and melody are credited to one named singer.
**Answer: False** — They are the people's (የህዝብ).

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** "ቡሄ በሉ" (Say Buhe) is a call-and-response festival song.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 4)** At a wedding, the gathering is quiet and no one sings.
**Answer: False** — Relatives and neighbours gather in joy, with ululation and shouting.
