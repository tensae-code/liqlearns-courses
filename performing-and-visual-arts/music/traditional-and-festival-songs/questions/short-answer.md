# Traditional and Festival Songs — short answer

Answer in a few words, out loud or in writing.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name three things in Abebayehush's world.
**Answer:** Any three: the flowers, gathering wood, building a house, counting stars, mother's scolding, the Demera fire.

**2. (Sub lesson 4)** Name three life occasions, besides weddings, that carry their own songs.
**Answer:** Any three: religious festivals, grief (mourning), baptism, work (also joy, war, worship in the teacher guide).
