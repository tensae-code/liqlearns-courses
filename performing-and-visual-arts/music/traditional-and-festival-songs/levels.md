# Traditional and Festival Songs — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure — README, "Units, Lessons and Sub Lessons"). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number, as before. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. The sub lessons climb inside this one lesson, from the youngest books to the oldest.

## Sub lesson 1 — Abebayehush and Adey
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: sing Abebayehush in call and response, and say what season it belongs to and who sings it.

## Sub lesson 2 — The full New Year song and Hoya Hoye
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: tell the two New Year songs apart and sing each one's response part.

## Sub lesson 3 — Buhe
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: sing the Buhe response and say when Buhe is celebrated and what is lit.
