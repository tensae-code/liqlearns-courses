This lesson is Fox and Waltz Rhythms.

Rhythmic playing alternates the strong beat (dum, a clap) and the weak beat (tak, a pat on the thighs) using the hands; the Fox rhythm in two gives the regular, fast feel of marching, as in military parades, and the Waltz rhythm in three gives joy and cheer — the waltz dance is named from it; artists: Johann Strauss, famous for waltz dance pieces, and the Egyptian composer Mohamed El-Qasabgi, famous for songs on the waltz and fox rhythms.

Watch, listen — then say it back in your own words.
