# Caribbean Music and Dance — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Headings, pictures and captions can point you to where a text keeps its music and dance information.
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** You must read every word of a text before its features can help you.
**Answer: False** — the features are the map you use first.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Extracting means taking out the explicit information the text gives.
**Answer: True.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** It is fine to add true facts from somewhere else into your extraction.
**Answer: False** — the extraction holds only what this text states plainly.
