# Caribbean Music and Dance — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** To locate information in a social studies text, Jamaica's guide says to use…
a) the features of the text  b) only the last page  c) a guess
**Answer: a) the features of the text.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The information this lesson locates is about…
a) farming tools  b) music and dance in the Caribbean  c) the weather
**Answer: b) music and dance in the Caribbean.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Which of these is a feature of a text?
a) a heading  b) the reader's shoes  c) the school bell
**Answer: a) a heading.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** "Explicit information" means information that is…
a) hidden and must be guessed  b) stated plainly in the text  c) invented by the reader
**Answer: b) stated plainly in the text.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** When you extract, you should…
a) add your own ideas  b) take out only what the text states  c) copy the whole book
**Answer: b) take out only what the text states.**
