# Hands-on — Caribbean Music and Dance

The lesson with your hands and your eyes, not only your memory.

## Hunt with the features
Take a social studies text about the Caribbean. One player names a feature — heading, picture, caption — and the other uses it to locate the text's information about music and dance. Swap roles for a new page.

## Extract and check
Write down, in short lines, the explicit information you extracted about Caribbean music and dance. Your partner checks each line against the text: is it stated plainly there, or did you add it? Keep only what the text says.
