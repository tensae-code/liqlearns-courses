# Video script — Caribbean Music and Dance

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A book can teach you music and dance — if you know how to look inside it.

First, **find**. Look at the heading. Look at the picture. Read the caption.
The features of the text point you to the place.

Then, **extract**. Take out what the text says plainly about music and dance in the Caribbean — only what it says, in its own words.

Find it with the features. Take it out exactly as written.

That is Caribbean music and dance — learned from the text itself.
