# How a Composer Works

## Do it first

Before you read: say or sketch what you already know about this lesson's subject — inside the composer's workshop — inspiration, quoting, varying, and changing the musical colours. Start there; the lesson builds on it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Inside the creative workshop

The Grade 4 book asks: "How does a composer work?" — and looks inside the creative workshop:

- Composers are **inspired** by native nature, pictures of the people's life, and the world of feelings.
- A composer may **quote** a melody that sounded before — another author's, or a folk one (as the Latvian Medynsh arranged a Belarusian lullaby).
- A composer may **vary and develop** his melody.
- A composer may **change the musical colours**: mode, rhythm pattern, strength of sound, tempo — and, for orchestra, timbre, giving the melody to different instruments.
