# How a Composer Works — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A composer may vary and develop his own melody.
**Answer: True**.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Composers may never use folk melodies.
**Answer: False** — quoting a folk melody is one of the book's named ways.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Tempo is one of the musical colours.
**Answer: True**.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Medynsh was a Belarusian composer.
**Answer: False** — Medynsh was Latvian; the lullaby he arranged was Belarusian.
