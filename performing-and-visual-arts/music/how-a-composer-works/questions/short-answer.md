# How a Composer Works — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name three things that inspire composers in the book.
**Answer: Native nature, pictures of the people's life, and the world of feelings.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What two kinds of earlier melody may a composer quote?
**Answer: Another author's melody, or a folk melody.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the musical colours a composer can change.
**Answer: Mode, rhythm pattern, strength of sound, tempo, and (for orchestra) timbre.**
