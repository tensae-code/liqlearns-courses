# How a Composer Works — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Composers in the book are inspired by…
a) only money  b) native nature, the people's life and feelings  c) silence
**Answer: b) native nature, the people's life and feelings** — native nature, pictures of the people's life, and the world of feelings.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** To quote a melody means to…
a) forget it  b) use a melody that sounded before  c) play it backwards
**Answer: b) use a melody that sounded before** — another author's or a folk one.
