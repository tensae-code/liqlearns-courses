# Hands-on — How a Composer Works

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Be the composer
Take a song everyone knows. (1) Quote it exactly. (2) Vary it — change its ending. (3) Change its colours — sing it slower, quieter, in a sadder mode-feeling, and let a different group 'instrument' (hummers, clappers) carry it. You have done the composer's three works.

## Inspiration card
Draw or write what could inspire a composer today: a place in nature, a scene of people's life, a feeling. Keep it as your workshop card.
