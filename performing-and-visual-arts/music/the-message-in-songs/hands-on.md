# Hands-on — The Message in Songs

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Listen twice
Play or sing a short song your group knows. First round: listen (or hum) without the words and write down the feeling. Second round: with the words. Compare — what did the words add?

## Say the message
In pairs, each partner states the song's message in one sentence and points to the words or the music that carry it.
