# The Message in Songs — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The first listening is…
a) without the words  b) with the words printed  c) silent
**Answer: a) without the words.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The song in the activity is about…
a) offering one's heart  b) winning a race  c) building a house
**Answer: a) offering one's heart.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The song's author named in the guide is…
a) Fito Páez  b) Monterroso  c) Ajpú
**Answer: a) Fito Páez.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The second listening adds…
a) the words  b) louder speakers  c) a dance
**Answer: a) the words.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** After listening with the words, the class discusses…
a) the feelings it raises and what its author meant  b) only the singer's clothes  c) the price of the recording
**Answer: a) the feelings it raises and what its author meant.**
