# The Message in Songs — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In what order does the guide set the two listenings?
**Answer: First without the words, then with the words.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What two things does the discussion cover?
**Answer: The feelings the song raises, and what its author meant.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** How is the song placed in this lesson?
**Answer: In summary only — its words are not transcribed.**
