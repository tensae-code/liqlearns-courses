# Video script — The Message in Songs

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Listen twice.

First, a song without its words — hear only the feeling.

Then with the words — and talk together: what feelings does it raise? What did its author mean?

That is how you find the message in a song.
