## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Vietnam Grade 8 Âm nhạc 8** — The đàn nguyệt (in the South called đàn kìm) — it was brought into Vietnam at about the 11th century and is used in many forms of traditional musical activity of the Vietnamese; the instrument's face is round like the Moon (Mặt Trăng), hence its name; the đàn nguyệt has two strings, of silk or nylon; when performing, the player sits or stands, the left hand pressing the instrument's frets (phím đàn) and the right hand using a pick (móng gảy) to pluck the strings; the đàn nguyệt's sound is healthy and vigorous, slightly opaque, its high tones bright and clear, combined with playing techniques such as plucking (gảy), pressing (nhấn), shaking (rung) and trembling (vê); today the đàn nguyệt is still an instrument in wide use, especially indispensable in hát chầu văn — a distinctive folk musical activity of the Vietnamese of the North — and in the Đờn ca tài tử art of the South (shown: a đờn ca tài tử performance)
