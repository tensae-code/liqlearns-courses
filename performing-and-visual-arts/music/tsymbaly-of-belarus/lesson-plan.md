# Lesson plan — Tsymbaly of Belarus

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Music · **Lesson:** Tsymbaly of Belarus
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 3 — Music (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Belarus's tsymbaly — the hammered dulcimer at the front of its folk orchestra, and the folk instruments' new composed life around it.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the course's earlier music lessons — recommended first, never forced.
