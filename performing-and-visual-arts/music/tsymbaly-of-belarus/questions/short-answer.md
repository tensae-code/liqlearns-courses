# Tsymbaly of Belarus — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which orchestra keeps the tsymbaly in its chief place?
**Answer: The State Academic Folk Orchestra of the Republic of Belarus named after I. Zhinovich.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the two folk songs the Grade 4 book sets beside the composed "Tsymbaly".
**Answer: "Zajhraj zha mne, dudarochku" and "Vaitouna".**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** What are padgaloski?
**Answer: Second voices sung under a song.**
