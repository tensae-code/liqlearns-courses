# Tsymbaly of Belarus — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The tsymbaly's strings are struck with sticks.
**Answer: True**.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The Zhinovich orchestra is a brass band.
**Answer: False** — it is the State Academic Folk Orchestra of Belarus.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** "Vaitouna" is named in the book as an ancient folk song.
**Answer: True**.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Padgaloski are sung above the melody as the main tune.
**Answer: False** — they are second voices under the song.
