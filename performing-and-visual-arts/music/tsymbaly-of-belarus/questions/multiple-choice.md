# Tsymbaly of Belarus — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The tsymbaly is a…
a) wind instrument  b) hammered dulcimer  c) drum
**Answer: b) hammered dulcimer** — strings struck with sticks.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The chief place in the Zhinovich folk orchestra belongs to…
a) the piano  b) the tsymbaly  c) the trumpet
**Answer: a) the piano** — the Belarusian tsymbaly.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Luksha wrote a poem called…
a) "Dudka"  b) "Tsymbaly"  c) "Vaitouna"
**Answer: b) "Tsymbaly"** — .

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Padgaloski are…
a) second voices sung under a song  b) drum beats  c) dance steps
**Answer: a) second voices sung under a song** — second voices — answering voices under the melody.

**5. (Sub lesson 1)** The composed song "Tsymbaly" has music by…
a) Voinskaya  b) Tsitavets  c) Luksha
**Answer: a) Voinskaya** — (words by Tsitavets).
