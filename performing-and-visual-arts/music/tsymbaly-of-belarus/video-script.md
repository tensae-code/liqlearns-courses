# Video script — Tsymbaly of Belarus

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Strike the strings — the **tsymbaly** rings!

Belarus's hammered dulcimer stands at the front of the great folk orchestra named after Zhinovich. Poets write poems to it. Composers write songs for it.

And under the song, the second voices sing — padgaloski.

That is the tsymbaly of Belarus.
