# Hands-on — Tsymbaly of Belarus

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Strike the strings
No tsymbaly in class? Stretch rubber bands over a box and tap them with two light sticks — hear how struck strings ring. Then tap a rhythm pattern and let a partner copy it.

## Second voices
Sing a song the class knows. Half the class keeps the melody; the other half hums a quiet second voice under it — padgaloski, the folk way.
