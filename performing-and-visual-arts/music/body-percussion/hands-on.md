# Hands-on — Body Percussion

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Sound map tour
Go through the map in order: tongue clicks, lip-smacks, claps, palm-knocks, finger taps, knee pats, thigh pats, leg slaps, stomps, toe taps. The group copies each sound.

## Body rhythm
Build a four-beat pattern from four different body sounds and repeat it together until it holds — then swap one sound and repeat.
