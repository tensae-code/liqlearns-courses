# Body Percussion

## Do it first

Quick start: from the title alone, guess what this lesson's work is. Then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — The body's sound map

The guide maps the body as an instrument, part by part: **tongue clicks and lip-smacks, claps and palm-knocks, finger taps, knee and thigh pats, leg slaps, stomps and toe taps** — the body is **the most versatile instrument** a child owns.

## Sub lesson 2 — The Orff road into instruments

Body percussion is not a game beside the music: in **the Orff approach** it is **the road into playing instruments** — the rhythms learned on the body travel onto the instruments that follow.
