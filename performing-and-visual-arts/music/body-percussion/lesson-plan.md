# Lesson plan — Body Percussion

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Music · **Lesson:** Body Percussion
**Place in the curriculum:** Music (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the body as the most versatile instrument — a whole map of body sounds, from tongue clicks and claps to stomps and toe taps, the Orff approach's road into playing instruments.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this course in the same title.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks you need an instrument to make percussion → the body is the first instrument: clicks, claps, pats, slaps, stomps and taps are all percussion sounds.
- Thinks body percussion is only clapping → the guide's map is whole-body: mouth sounds, palm-knocks, finger taps, knee and thigh pats, leg slaps, stomps and toe taps.
