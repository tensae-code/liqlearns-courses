# Body Percussion — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name four sounds from the body's sound map.
**Answer: Tongue clicks, lip-smacks, claps and stomps (palm-knocks, finger taps, knee/thigh pats, leg slaps and toe taps are also named).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the guide call the body as an instrument?
**Answer: The most versatile instrument.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** In the Orff approach, where does body percussion lead?
**Answer: Into playing instruments.**
