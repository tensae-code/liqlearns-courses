# Body Percussion — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The body can be a percussion instrument.
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Body percussion is only clapping.
**Answer: False** — the map runs from tongue clicks to toe taps.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Body percussion leads into playing instruments in the Orff approach.
**Answer: True.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Toe taps are made with the fingers.
**Answer: False** — toe taps are made with the feet.
