# Body Percussion — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Body percussion makes music with…
a) the body itself  b) bought drums  c) the voice only
**Answer: a) the body itself** — the most versatile instrument.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which is a body-percussion sound in the guide's map?
a) tongue clicks  b) violin bowing  c) piano keys
**Answer: a) tongue clicks** — beside lip-smacks, claps, palm-knocks and more.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Stomps and toe taps are made with…
a) the feet  b) the hands  c) the mouth
**Answer: a) the feet**.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In the Orff approach, body percussion is…
a) the road into playing instruments  b) a warm-up game only  c) the end of music
**Answer: a) the road into playing instruments**.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Knee and thigh pats, and leg slaps, come from…
a) the legs  b) the mouth  c) the fingers
**Answer: a) the legs**.
