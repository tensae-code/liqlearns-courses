# Video script — Body Percussion

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Click! Clap! Pat! Stomp!

Your body is an instrument — the most versatile one you own.

Tongue clicks, palm-knocks, knee pats, toe taps — a whole band in one body.

Orff says: start on the body — then the instruments follow.
