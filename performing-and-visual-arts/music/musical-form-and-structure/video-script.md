**the elements of musical form.** the elements of musical form: form as music's architecture; development by repetition, variation and contrast; the building blocks — motif (the smallest musical thought), phrase (two bars), sentence (closed by a cadence) and period (two related sentences, question and answer) — with the same building logic shown in architecture, painting, poetry and film.

That is Musical Form and Structure — North Macedonia's way, kept by name.
