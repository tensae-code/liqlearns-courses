# Musical Form and Structure

## Do it first

Name one thing that belongs in this lesson — Music's architecture — form built by repetition, variation and contrast, musical themes, and song structure. Say what it is in your own words. If you can name one, you are already inside the lesson.

## Sub lesson 1 — the elements of musical form

the elements of musical form: form as music's architecture; development by repetition, variation and contrast; the building blocks — motif (the smallest musical thought), phrase (two bars), sentence (closed by a cadence) and period (two related sentences, question and answer) — with the same building logic shown in architecture, painting, poetry and film.
