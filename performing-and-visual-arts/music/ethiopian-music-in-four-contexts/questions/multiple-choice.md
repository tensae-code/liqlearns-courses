# Ethiopian Music in Four Contexts — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.


**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Zenebe Bekele's four categories are…
  a) Social, religious, political, economic b) Strings, winds, drums, shaken c) Tizta, Bati, Anchi Hoye, Ambassel
**Answer: a) Social, religious, political, economic** — the Grade 8 book's §1.2.1 sorting — ideas and contexts, not instruments or scales.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** National anthems fall in the…
  a) Political category b) Economic category c) Religious category
**Answer: a) Political category** — the book places anthems inside the political category.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A threshing work song is sorted…
  a) Economic — bound to work and production b) Religious c) Political
**Answer: a) Economic — bound to work and production** — work songs pace production — the economic category.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Zar shouting songs in customary worship are…
  a) Religious b) Social c) Economic
**Answer: a) Religious** — the book names zar music inside the religious category.
