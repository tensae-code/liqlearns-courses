# Ethiopian Music in Four Contexts — short answer

Answer in a short sentence or a short list.


**1. (Sub lesson 1)** On what basis does Bekele sort music?
**Answer:** By its ideas and its performance contexts — what the music expresses and where it is performed.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A song is sung at both a wedding and a state ceremony. How do you sort it?
**Answer:** By the context in question — social at the wedding, political at the state ceremony — saying which context you sorted by.
