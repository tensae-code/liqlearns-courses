# Lesson plan — Ethiopian Music in Four Contexts

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Music · **Lesson:** Ethiopian Music in Four Contexts
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 3 — Music (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Zenebe Bekele's four categories of Ethiopian music — social, religious, political and economic — sorted by the ideas the music expresses and the contexts where it is performed.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Kinds of Traditional Music — kinds by occasion; here a researcher's sorting of all Ethiopian music into four idea-contexts.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Zenebe Bekele's four categories** — §1.2.1: social, religious, political, economic — classified by ideas and performance contexts.
2. **Sorting music truly** — practice sorting: wedding song, zar music, anthem, work song — each into its category with its reason spoken.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Kinds of Traditional Music; Ethiopian Traditional Music and Its History. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name Zenebe Bekele's four categories and define each.
2. Sort six given pieces of music into their categories with reasons.
3. Say on what basis the sorting is made: ideas and performance contexts.

## What you need
Only the music you already know — anthems, chants, wedding and work songs.
