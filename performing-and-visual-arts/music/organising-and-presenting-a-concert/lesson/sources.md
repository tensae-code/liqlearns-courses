| Kazakhstan — Музыка 1-тарау (continued). Музыкалық туындыны таныстыру (Presenting a musical work) — what it teaches: concert performances are usually planned by the organisers, and announcing (хабарландыру) is done by preparing a poster (афиша); a theatre poster (театр афишасы) is a theatre or concert announcement poster — it shows the concert's or play's name, place, date and time; such announcements are also published in newspapers and journals and placed in crowded places; before a concert the viewers buy the concert programme (концерттік бағдарлама) or they are given out free; in the concert programme the place, day, time are shown, and together with them separate information about the musical works, composers and performers; at a concert the viewers evaluate the musical work and its performance; music critics (музыка сыншысы) analyse and evaluate music in all its aspects; they support their evaluations with examples aimed at presenting their opinions; organising a concert: 1. perform the musical composition you composed in the previous lessons and ask friends to write it down in video recording; 2. watch the video recording — how can the composition or its performance be improved, discuss with classmates; 3. answer the questions: do you understand your musical composition's idea and form?; is there a main part and accompaniment in your composition?; how can the composition be improved?; what parts does your work consist of, and why exactly those parts?; understand your work's difficult and complex parts; 4. finally, improve your composition with others' help — write the improved work and prepare a table helping classmates and your teacher evaluate your and your classmates' works; prepare your own concert programme: compose the programme's model; prepare the information viewers need for the concert programme; and after the concert day, evaluate your work and the whole concert — how did the concert go, was its goal reached, why, what did not work and why, think about planning the concert and adding to it, which musical forms did you choose, how did it affect the concert, what was your role, did you appear in another role, why, what will you perform at the next concert and why? (student book) | Book 2, printed p. 14 |
