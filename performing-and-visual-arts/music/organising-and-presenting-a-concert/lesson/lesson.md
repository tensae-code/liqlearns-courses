# Organising and Presenting a Concert

## Do it first

Name one thing that belongs in this lesson — Putting music before an audience — organising, staging and presenting a concert performance. Say what it is in your own words. If you can name one, you are already inside the lesson.

## Sub lesson 1 — Bab IV Pergelaran Musik Indonesia dan Asia

preparing and staging a performance (pergelaran) of Indonesian and Asian music — the knowledge, skills and creativity a performance preparation needs, through to performing the works for an audience to enjoy.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson holds, part by part, in your own words. Companion checks: did every part get named, with its Indonesian name kept where the book gives one?

## Do It Yourself

1. Choose one named part of this lesson and find out one more true thing about it — from a person who knows it, or from the book your part came from.
2. Make or perform a small piece in the way of that part: a rhythm, a step, a drawing, a shaped piece of clay.
3. Write one sentence: what does this part show about Indonesia's own way of teaching its arts?
