# Cuban Percussion — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name three Cuban percussion instruments.
**Answer: Any three of: conga, tumba, quinto, bongo, claves, cowbell, guiro, vibraslap, maracas.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the three printed dance patterns.
**Answer: Mambo, rumba and cha-cha-cha.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What signs go with the three strokes?
**Answer: Their own notation signs, one per stroke.**
