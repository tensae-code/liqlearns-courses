# Cuban Percussion — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The solo drum among the congas is the…
a) quinto  b) tumba  c) bongo
**Answer: a) quinto**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The conga's three strokes are…
a) open, closed, slap  b) loud, soft, silent  c) fast, slow, stop
**Answer: a) open, closed, slap**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Martillo is the rhythm of the…
a) bongo  b) claves  c) guiro
**Answer: a) bongo**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Cuban rhythms fitted to old songs of…
a) Iceland  b) Vienna  c) Berlin
**Answer: a) Iceland**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Claves are…
a) Cuban percussion instruments  b) wind instruments  c) dances
**Answer: a) Cuban percussion instruments**
