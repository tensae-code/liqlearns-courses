# Lesson plan — Cuban Percussion

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Music · **Lesson:** Cuban Percussion
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 3 — Music (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Cuban percussion playing — the conga and tumba (with quinto as solo drum), bongo, claves, cowbell, guiro, vibraslap and maracas; three conga strokes (open, closed, slap) with their notation signs, and the printed mambo, rumba and cha-cha-cha patterns, learned by doing it in class.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the listening and songs lessons — the learner already listens, sings and keeps a beat.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Music in Cuba: the pupils play** — The Cuban instruments join the classroom: congas and tumba (with quinto as solo drum), bongo, claves, cowbell, guiro, vibraslap and maracas — and rumba or cha-cha-cha rhythms fit old Icelandic songs.
2. **Conga technique and the patterns** — Three strokes — open, closed, slap — with their notation signs, then the printed mambo, rumba and cha-cha-cha patterns for conga and tumba; and the bongo's martillo rhythm, hand by hand.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
