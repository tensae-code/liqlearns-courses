# Cuban Percussion

## Do it first

Quick start: say one thing you already know — a tune, a dance, a name — that belongs to this lesson's world. Keep it in mind; the parts will meet it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Music in Cuba: the pupils play

The Cuban instruments join the classroom: **congas and tumba (with quinto as solo drum), bongo, claves, cowbell, guiro, vibraslap and maracas** — and **rumba or cha-cha-cha rhythms fit old Icelandic songs**.

## Sub lesson 2 — Conga technique and the patterns

**Three strokes — open, closed, slap — with their notation signs**, then the printed **mambo, rumba and cha-cha-cha patterns** for conga and tumba; and the bongo's **martillo** rhythm, hand by hand.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the parts in order, one sentence each. Companion checks: did every part get its sentence, and did the order hold?

## Do It Yourself

1. Write the parts' names from memory, in order.
2. Do the hands-on activity once more, teaching it to someone else.
3. Write one sentence: what is the one thing this lesson teaches?
