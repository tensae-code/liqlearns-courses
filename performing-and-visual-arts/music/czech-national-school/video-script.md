**Bedřich Smetana (1824–1884), father of Czech national music.** Bedřich Smetana (1824–1884), father of Czech national music: founder of the Czech national symphonic poem and national opera — the cycle "Má vlast" (My Homeland) and the comic opera "The Bartered Bride", composed while completely deaf.

**Antonín Dvořák (1841–1904): Smetana's heir.** Antonín Dvořák (1841–1904): Smetana's heir — the "New World" Symphony written as director of New York's National Conservatory, where he urged American composers to build on African-American and Native melodies; the Slavonic Dances, the Cello Concerto and the opera "Rusalka".

That is The Czech National School — North Macedonia's way, kept by name.
