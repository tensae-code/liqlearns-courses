## Sub lesson 1 — Bedřich Smetana (1824–1884), father of Czech national music
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Bedřich Smetana (1824–1884), father of Czech national music: founder of the Czech national symphonic poem and national opera — the cycle "Má vlast" (My Homeland) and the comic opera "The Bartered Bride", composed while completely deaf.

## Sub lesson 2 — Antonín Dvořák (1841–1904): Smetana's heir
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Antonín Dvořák (1841–1904): Smetana's heir — the "New World" Symphony written as director of New York's National Conservatory, where he urged American composers to build on African-American and Native melodies; the Slavonic Dances, the Cello Concerto and the opera "Rusalka".
