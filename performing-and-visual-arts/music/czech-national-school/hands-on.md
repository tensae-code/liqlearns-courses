# Hands-on — The Czech National School

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Bedřich Smetana (1824–1884), father of Czech national music
Work the part with your hands, voice or body as its own craft directs: make it, perform it, or shape it — then show a companion and name the part aloud.
