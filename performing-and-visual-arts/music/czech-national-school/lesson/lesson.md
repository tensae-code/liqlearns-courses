# The Czech National School

## Do it first

Name one thing that belongs in this lesson — The Czech national school — Smetana, father of Czech national music, and Dvořák his heir — building a nation's symphonic poem and opera. Say what it is in your own words. If you can name one, you are already inside the lesson.

## Sub lesson 1 — Bedřich Smetana (1824–1884), father of Czech national music

Bedřich Smetana (1824–1884), father of Czech national music: founder of the Czech national symphonic poem and national opera — the cycle "Má vlast" (My Homeland) and the comic opera "The Bartered Bride", composed while completely deaf.

## Sub lesson 2 — Antonín Dvořák (1841–1904): Smetana's heir

Antonín Dvořák (1841–1904): Smetana's heir — the "New World" Symphony written as director of New York's National Conservatory, where he urged American composers to build on African-American and Native melodies; the Slavonic Dances, the Cello Concerto and the opera "Rusalka".
