# Icelandic Songwriters — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name Ingibjörg's own recorded song.
**Answer: Á morgun.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name two people from the closing lives.
**Answer: Any two of: Oddgeir Kristjánsson, Ási í Bæ, Haukur Morthens, Ellý Vilhjálms, the KK Sextet.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What was Ingibjörg's radio role?
**Answer: Host of the patients' request programme (and clarinettist and arranger).**
