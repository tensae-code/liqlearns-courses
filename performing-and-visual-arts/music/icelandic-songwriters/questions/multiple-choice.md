# Icelandic Songwriters — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Litla flugan (1952) is by…
a) Sigfús Halldórsson  b) Ingibjörg Þorbergs  c) Sousa
**Answer: a) Sigfús Halldórsson**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Litla flugan woke…
a) Icelandic record publishing  b) the brass band  c) opera
**Answer: a) Icelandic record publishing**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Hvers vegna? surprises with…
a) bassoon and harpsichord  b) drums only  c) silence
**Answer: a) bassoon and harpsichord**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Ingibjörg Þorbergs was the first Icelandic woman to…
a) record a song of her own  b) lead a brass band  c) sing in Vienna
**Answer: a) record a song of her own**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** The closing lives are told by origins, first music, work and…
a) instruments  b) wealth only  c) fame only
**Answer: a) instruments**
