# Icelandic Songwriters

## Do it first

Quick start: say one thing you already know — a tune, a dance, a name — that belongs to this lesson's world. Keep it in mind; the parts will meet it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Sigfús Halldórsson

**Sigfús Halldórsson** — **Litla flugan (1952)**, the catchy song that **woke Icelandic record publishing**; and the surprise of **Hvers vegna?** — a popular song played on **bassoon and harpsichord**.

## Sub lesson 2 — Ingibjörg Þorbergs

**Ingibjörg Þorbergs** — the patients' request-programme host, clarinettist and arranger, and the **first Icelandic woman to record a song of her own (Á morgun)**.

## Sub lesson 3 — The people who made the music

The guide closes with **short lives of Iceland's popular songwriters, lyricists, singers and bandleaders** — Oddgeir Kristjánsson, Ási í Bæ, Haukur Morthens, Ellý Vilhjálms, the KK Sextet and many more — each told by **origins, first music, work and instruments**.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the parts in order, one sentence each. Companion checks: did every part get its sentence, and did the order hold?

## Do It Yourself

1. Write the parts' names from memory, in order.
2. Do the hands-on activity once more, teaching it to someone else.
3. Write one sentence: what is the one thing this lesson teaches?
