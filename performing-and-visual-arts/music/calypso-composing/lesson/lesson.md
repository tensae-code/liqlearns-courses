# Composing Calypso

## Do it first

Quick start: say in one sentence what you think this lesson is about — writing and singing calypso the Barbados way. Then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — A calypso tune on a local topic

From Class 2 onward the syllabus sets **composing simple calypso tunes on local topics** — the calypso takes what is happening around the singers as its subject.

## Sub lesson 2 — A calypso for a real occasion

Calypsos are composed **for graduations, Crop Over and other social events** — a song made for the day it will be sung on, to the people who are there.
