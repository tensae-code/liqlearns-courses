# Composing Calypso — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Barbados pupils compose calypso tunes on…
a) local topics  b) foreign news only  c) no topic
**Answer: a) local topics** — the syllabus names local topics.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The calypso tunes pupils compose are described as…
a) simple  b) silent  c) written only, never sung
**Answer: a) simple** — simple calypso tunes, composed and sung.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Which occasions does the syllabus name for composed calypsos?
a) graduations and Crop Over  b) examinations  c) funerals only
**Answer: a) graduations and Crop Over** — and other social events.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A calypso composed for Crop Over is made for…
a) a real social event  b) a cupboard  c) nobody
**Answer: a) a real social event**.

**5. (Sub lesson 1)** Calypso composing runs in the syllabus through…
a) Classes 2–4  b) Class 1 only  c) no classes
**Answer: a) Classes 2–4**.
