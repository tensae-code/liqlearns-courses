# Composing Calypso — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What kind of topics do pupils compose calypsos on?
**Answer: Local topics.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name two occasions the syllabus names for composed calypsos.
**Answer: Graduations and Crop Over (and other social events).**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Why compose a calypso for a graduation?
**Answer: Because the syllabus sets calypsos composed for real occasions — the song belongs to the event it is sung at.**
