# Hands-on — Composing Calypso

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Local topic, new tune
Pick something happening in your school or street. Make a simple calypso tune about it and sing it to a friend.

## Song for the day
Choose a real occasion — a graduation or a festival day. Compose a short calypso for it and perform it as if the day had come.
