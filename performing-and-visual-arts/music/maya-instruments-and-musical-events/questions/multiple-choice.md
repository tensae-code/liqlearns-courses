# Maya Instruments and Musical Events — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Grade 5 theme names the instruments of…
a) Maya art  b) a modern factory  c) a school band only
**Answer: a) Maya art.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A people's instruments are part of its…
a) art  b) punishment  c) silence
**Answer: a) art.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The experience that names the theme is…
a) La palabra como arte — The Word as Art  b) Publicarte  c) Mi cuerpo habla
**Answer: a) La palabra como arte — The Word as Art.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Organising a musical event brings together…
a) instruments, players and listeners  b) only posters  c) only tickets
**Answer: a) instruments, players and listeners.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The events are organised especially with…
a) the instruments of Maya art  b) recorded pop only  c) no instruments
**Answer: a) the instruments of Maya art.**
