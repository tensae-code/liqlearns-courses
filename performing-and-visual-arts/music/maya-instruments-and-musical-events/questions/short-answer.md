# Maya Instruments and Musical Events — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Whose instruments does the theme name?
**Answer: The instruments of Maya art.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What act does the theme join to the instruments?
**Answer: Organising musical events.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Name two things an organiser chooses.
**Answer: Any two of: what will be played, who plays it, and who listens.**
