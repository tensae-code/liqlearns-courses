# Lesson plan — Maya Instruments and Musical Events

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Music · **Lesson:** Maya Instruments and Musical Events
**Place in the curriculum:** Music (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** organising musical events, especially with the instruments of Maya art — taught at theme level, because the Grade 5 activity pages are not in the PARTIAL file.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this course in the same title.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The instruments of Maya art** — Guatemala's Grade 5 experience “La palabra como arte” (The Word as Art) names, among its themes, the **instruments of Maya art** — the instruments that belong to the Maya people's own music-making, carried inside their art.
2. **Organising musical events** — The same theme joins the instruments to an act: **organising musical events, especially with the instruments of Maya art**.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of this course in the same title. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say that the instruments of Maya art are a named Grade 5 theme.
2. Say what organising a musical event joins together — instruments, players and listeners, on purpose.

## What you need
Instruments if you have them; otherwise, pictures or names of instruments.
