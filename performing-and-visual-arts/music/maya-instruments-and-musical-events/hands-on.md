# Hands-on — Maya Instruments and Musical Events

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Plan a small event
In a group, plan a short musical event built around traditional instruments you know: name the instruments, who plays each, the order of the pieces, and who is invited to listen.

## Present the instruments
Before the event, each player presents their instrument in two sentences — its name and its place in the people's art.
