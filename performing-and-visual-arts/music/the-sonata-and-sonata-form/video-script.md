The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Vienna! Beethoven writes for a NEW piano — loud, soft, loud!

A sonata goes on a journey: the subjects set out — **exposition**.

They are tossed and changed — **development**. They come home — **recapitulation**.

And the slow Grave keeps returning, like a deep breath.
