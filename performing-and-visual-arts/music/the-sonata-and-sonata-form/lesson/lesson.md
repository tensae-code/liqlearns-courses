## Sub lesson 1 — Beethoven, Vienna and the new piano

**Beethoven** worked in **Vienna**, supported by **patronage**. His music belongs to the **Classical period (c.1750–1820)**. A **sonata** is a large work for a solo instrument (or a small group). The **'Pathétique'** was written for the new **pianoforte**, whose **loud–soft dynamics** the harpsichord could not make — the movement already **foreshadows the Romantic period**.

## Sub lesson 2 — Sonata form — and the returning Grave

**Sonata form** has three great parts: **exposition** (the subjects are presented), **development** (they are worked and changed) and **recapitulation** (they return). The 'Pathétique' movement is built from a **first subject** and a **second subject**, and is opened by a slow **Grave introduction that returns twice**. The score is a **two-stave piano score** with **Italian performance directions**.
