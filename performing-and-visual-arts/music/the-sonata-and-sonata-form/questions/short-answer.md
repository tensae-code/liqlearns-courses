# The Sonata and Sonata Form — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is a sonata?
**Answer: A large work for a solo instrument (or a small group).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How many times does the Grave introduction return?
**Answer: Twice.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What are sonata form's two main themes called?
**Answer: The first subject and the second subject.**
