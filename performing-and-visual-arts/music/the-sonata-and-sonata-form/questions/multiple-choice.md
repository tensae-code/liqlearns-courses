# The Sonata and Sonata Form — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Beethoven's city was…
a) Vienna  b) London  c) Paris
**Answer: a) Vienna**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The Classical period runs about…
a) 1750–1820  b) 1600–1750  c) 1900–2000
**Answer: a) 1750–1820**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The pianoforte could do what the harpsichord could not:…
a) loud–soft dynamics  b) play two notes  c) stand upright
**Answer: a) loud–soft dynamics**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Sonata form's three parts are exposition, development and…
a) recapitulation  b) coda only  c) silence
**Answer: a) recapitulation**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The 'Pathétique' opens with a slow introduction called…
a) Grave  b) Allegro  c) Presto
**Answer: a) Grave**
