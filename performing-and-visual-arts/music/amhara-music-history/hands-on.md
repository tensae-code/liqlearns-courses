# Hands-on — Amhara Music History

The lesson with your hands, your feet and your voice together, not only your eyes.

## Name-the-road game
A caller names a venue — tej house, wedding, theatre. The class answers with who performed there and on what.

## Family tree
Draw the cluster's family tree: Lalibelawoch, azmari, bands — with the book's names on the branches.
