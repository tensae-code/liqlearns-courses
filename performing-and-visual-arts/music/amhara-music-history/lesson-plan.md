# Lesson plan — Amhara Music History

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Music · **Lesson:** Amhara Music History
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 3 — Music (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the Amhara region's music history as the Grade 7 book tells it — the Lalibelawoch pairs, the azmari trade, and the bands, theatres and culture groups that carried the music into the modern age.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Ethiopian Traditional Music and Its History — the national story; here one region's own history in depth.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The Lalibelawoch and the azmari trade** — §3.1's opening: the Lalibelawoch — mostly male–female pairs tying the craft to faith, hereditary, moving place to place; the azmari in bands and theatre houses.
2. **Bands, theatres and the music's widening road** — the region's music carried into bands and theatre houses — the road from tej house to stage that the book's musician lives walk.

3. **The Grade 7 chapter opens — history and culture (chapter opening — Grade 7)** — the Grade 7 book's Chapter 3 opener and aims: the cultural/historical context of art; regional musicians' histories; Saint Yared; indigenous handcraft, paintings and ancient writings; expressing indigenous knowledge in theatre and film (Grade 7, printed pp. 74–75).

4. **The Grade 7 chapter closes — check, summary and exercise (chapter closing — Grade 7)** — the Grade 7 book's Chapter 3 self-check table (6 items), summary and closing exercise — 7 true/false and 7 short answers (Grade 7, printed pp. 123–125). Its two practice tasks (Task-1, Task-2) sit in the Yared, musicians and painters lessons.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Ethiopian Traditional Music and Its History. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Tell who the Lalibelawoch are and how their craft passes down.
2. Describe the azmari's trade and name azmari the book names.
3. Name the region's culture groups the book names.

## What you need
Only voices and memory — the history is carried in names and stories, no songs transcribed.
