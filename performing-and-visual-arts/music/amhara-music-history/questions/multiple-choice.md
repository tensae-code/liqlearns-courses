# Amhara Music History — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.


**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Lalibelawoch are…
  a) Mostly male–female pairs tying the craft to faith b) A modern orchestra c) Solo church cantors only
**Answer: a) Mostly male–female pairs tying the craft to faith** — hereditary pairs, moving place to place, craft tied to faith.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The azmari's craft-centres included…
  a) Tej houses, weddings and theatre houses b) Only radio stations c) Only schools
**Answer: a) Tej houses, weddings and theatre houses** — the azmari performed where people gathered — tej houses, weddings, theatres.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** In the book's history, the music moved from tej houses into…
  a) Bands and theatre houses b) Silence c) Foreign countries only
**Answer: a) Bands and theatre houses** — stages and bands carried the regional music wider.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Through every change of venue, the music…
  a) Survived, keeping its craft b) Was replaced completely c) Lost its melodies
**Answer: a) Survived, keeping its craft** — the history's claim: venues change, the craft endures.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Grade 7 Chapter 3 places art inside its…
  a) Cultural and historical context b) Price list c) Examination hall
**Answer: a) Cultural and historical context** — the opener's first aim.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** Saint Yared stands in Chapter 3's aims beside…
  a) Regional musicians' histories b) Printing techniques c) Dance steps only
**Answer: a) Regional musicians' histories** — the aims name Yared within the musicians' history work.
