# Amhara Music History — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 4)** An artwork can be understood fully without its cultural and historical context.
**Answer: False** — the chapter's first aim denies it — context is part of the work's meaning.

**2. (Sub lesson 4)** Indigenous knowledge can be expressed in theatre and film.
**Answer: True** — the aims and the check both carry it.

**3. (Sub lesson 4)** Saint Yared's work belongs to Ethiopia's music history.
**Answer: True** — Yared stands in the chapter's aims and history.
