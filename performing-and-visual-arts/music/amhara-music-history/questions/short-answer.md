# Amhara Music History — short answer

Answer in a short sentence or a short list.


**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name two azmari the book names.
**Answer:** Any two of: Asefa Abate, Bahru Kanie, Derbaba Abun, Alemayehu Fanta.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why does this history name venues and not only dates?
**Answer:** Because the music's life is in where it was performed — tej house, wedding, theatre, band — and each venue shaped how it was sung.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name the three indigenous things the Chapter 3 opener names.
**Answer:** Indigenous handcraft; paintings; ancient writings.

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** How is Chapter 3's closing exercise divided, and how many self-check items precede it?
**Answer:** 7 true/false and 7 short answers; the self-check table carries 6 items.

**5. (Sub lesson 4)** Where in this course do Task-1 and Task-2 sit?
**Answer:** Task-1 in the Saint Yared and Musicians of the Amhara Region lessons; Task-2 in Great Painters of Ethiopia.
