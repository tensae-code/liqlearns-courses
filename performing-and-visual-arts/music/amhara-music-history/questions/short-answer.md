# Amhara Music History — short answer

Answer in a short sentence or a short list.


**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name two azmari the book names.
**Answer:** Any two of: Asefa Abate, Bahru Kanie, Derbaba Abun, Alemayehu Fanta.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why does this history name venues and not only dates?
**Answer:** Because the music's life is in where it was performed — tej house, wedding, theatre, band — and each venue shaped how it was sung.
