# Video script — Music of the East and the Silk Road

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is Music of the East and the Silk Road.

Music of the East and the Silk Road — The music of the East along the Silk Road. — as Kazakhstan's school books teach it: the Great Silk Road that tied East and West; the virtual game-journey through a museum "Art of the Peoples of the East": pick the Eastern costumes and the Eastern instruments out of the pictures and defend the choice; China's pipa, Japan's koto, India's sitar and the Arab dumbek heard; the Japanese folk song «Сакура» (Cherry Blossom).

Watch, listen — then say it back in your own words.
