# The History of Popular Music — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A dægurlag is…
a) a popular song  b) a church chant only  c) a silent dance
**Answer: a) a popular song**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Popular music obeys…
a) the same laws as all music  b) no laws at all  c) only rhythm laws
**Answer: a) the same laws as all music**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** New art is born…
a) where cultures meet  b) where cultures stay apart  c) only in concert halls
**Answer: a) where cultures meet**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Which is one of music's laws named in the introduction?
a) melody  b) spelling  c) geography
**Answer: a) melody**

**5. (Sub lesson 1)** The Dægurspor journey proves its claim by…
a) listening through popular music's history  b) reading scores silently  c) skipping old music
**Answer: a) listening through popular music's history**
