# The History of Popular Music

## Do it first

Quick start: say one thing you already know — a tune, a dance, a name — that belongs to this lesson's world. Keep it in mind; the parts will meet it.

## Sub lesson 1 — What a popular song is

A **dægurlag** is a popular song — the music people sing, dance to and carry with them every day. Iceland's Dægurspor guide opens by claiming popular music is not a lesser music: it obeys **the same laws as all music** — melody, harmony, rhythm, form, timbre, dynamics and tempo. And **new art is born where cultures meet**: the whole book that follows is a listening journey proving that claim, from court dances to jazz and Cuba.
