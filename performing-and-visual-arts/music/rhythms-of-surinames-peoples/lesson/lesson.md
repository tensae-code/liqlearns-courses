# Rhythms of Surinames Peoples

## Do it first

Quick start: from the title alone, guess what this lesson's work is. Then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — Five peoples, five patterns

The teacher plays **five rhythmic patterns of five peoples** — **Inheems (indigenous), Afro-Surinaams, Hindostaans, Javaans and Chinees** — and pupils listen for **which instruments, sounds and patterns belong to each people**, learning to **hear Suriname's peoples apart by their rhythms**. The patterns then become working material: in the same series each group is given a different people's pattern to arrange, and in the final presentation each group arranges **yet another people's** rhythmic pattern and adds to its music another art domain of that same people — its dance, a short theatre piece, or its traditional dress.
