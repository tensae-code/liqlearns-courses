# Rhythms of Surinames Peoples — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The five peoples whose patterns the teacher plays are…
a) Inheems, Afro-Surinaams, Hindostaans, Javaans and Chinees  b) only Creole and Marron  c) five European peoples
**Answer: a) Inheems, Afro-Surinaams, Hindostaans, Javaans and Chinees**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Pupils listen for…
a) the loudest drum only  b) which instruments, sounds and patterns belong to each people  c) the words of a song
**Answer: b) which instruments, sounds and patterns belong to each people**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** In the final presentation each group must add to another people's pattern…
a) nothing  b) another art domain of that same people — its dance, a short theatre piece, or its traditional dress  c) a pattern of its own people only
**Answer: b) another art domain of that same people — its dance, a short theatre piece, or its traditional dress**
