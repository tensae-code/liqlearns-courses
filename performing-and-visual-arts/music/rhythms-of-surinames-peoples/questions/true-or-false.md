# Rhythms of Surinames Peoples — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Each people's rhythmic pattern is characteristic of that people.
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** All five peoples share one identical rhythm.
**Answer: False — each people has its own instruments, sounds and patterns.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** A group may present another people's pattern wearing that people's traditional dress.
**Answer: True.**
