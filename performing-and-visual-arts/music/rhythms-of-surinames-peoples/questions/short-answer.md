# Rhythms of Surinames Peoples — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the five peoples.
**Answer: Inheems (indigenous), Afro-Surinaams, Hindostaans, Javaans and Chinees.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** How do you tell the peoples' patterns apart?
**Answer: By listening for which instruments, sounds and patterns belong to each people.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the listening skill this lesson builds?
**Answer: Hearing Suriname's peoples apart by their rhythms.**
