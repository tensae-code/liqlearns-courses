## Sub lesson 1 — The Guitar Instrument and the Violin (Kaman) Instrument

The string instruments appeared after the percussion and wind instruments and took many forms; two Western instruments compared — the guitar holds six strings and is played with the fingers or with a plectrum, its sound broken (plucked note by note), while the violin holds four strings and is played with the bow, its sound continuous; artists: the Spanish guitarist Andrés Segovia and the Jordanian violinist Antoun Shimoun.
