# The Overture — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — Tabloul muzical
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Unit 3, Tabloul muzical — Tchaikovsky's overture-fantasy "Romeo and Juliet": Shakespeare's tragedy told in three themes — Friar Lorenzo's Good, the families' Enmity (sword-strokes in the cymbals), and Love (English horn for Romeo, flutes for Juliet).

## Sub lesson 2 — Uvertura "Coriolan"
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Unit 3, Uvertura "Coriolan" — Beethoven's "Coriolan" overture, in sonata form: the hero's defiant unison theme against the pleading lyrical theme of his mother and wife.

## Sub lesson 3 — Uvertura "Ștefan cel Mare"
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Unit 3, Uvertura "Ștefan cel Mare" — Iacob Mureșianu's overture "Ștefan cel Mare" (1883), the first Romanian symphonic poem, on Bolintineanu's poem "The Mother of Stephen the Great": the oboe's uneasy cantilena, the battle, the Victory theme.

## Sub lesson 4 — Uvertura națională "Moldova"
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Unit 3, Uvertura națională "Moldova" — Alexandru Flechtenmacher's national overture "Moldova": the first proof in Romanian music that folk song and dance motives can feed the largest symphonic forms — a sunrise over the land, then a fan of contrasting folk dances.
