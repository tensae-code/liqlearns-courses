# Video script — The Overture

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This is Moldova's own lesson.

**Tabloul muzical.** Unit 3, Tabloul muzical — Tchaikovsky's overture-fantasy "Romeo and Juliet": Shakespeare's tragedy told in three themes — Friar Lorenzo's Good, the families' Enmity (sword-strokes in the cymbals), and Love (English horn for Romeo, flutes for Juliet).

**Uvertura "Coriolan".** Unit 3, Uvertura "Coriolan" — Beethoven's "Coriolan" overture, in sonata form: the hero's defiant unison theme against the pleading lyrical theme of his mother and wife.

**Uvertura "Ștefan cel Mare".** Unit 3, Uvertura "Ștefan cel Mare" — Iacob Mureșianu's overture "Ștefan cel Mare" (1883), the first Romanian symphonic poem, on Bolintineanu's poem "The Mother of Stephen the Great": the oboe's uneasy cantilena, the battle, the Victory theme.

**Uvertura națională "Moldova".** Unit 3, Uvertura națională "Moldova" — Alexandru Flechtenmacher's national overture "Moldova": the first proof in Romanian music that folk song and dance motives can feed the largest symphonic forms — a sunrise over the land, then a fan of contrasting folk dances.

That is The Overture — Moldova's way, kept by name.
