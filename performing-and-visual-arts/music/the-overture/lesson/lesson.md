# The Overture

## Do it first

Name one thing that belongs in this lesson — The overture — the orchestral opening that sets the stage — heard in Beethoven's Coriolan. Say what it is in your own words. If you can name one, you are already inside the lesson.

## Sub lesson 1 — Tabloul muzical

Unit 3, Tabloul muzical — Tchaikovsky's overture-fantasy "Romeo and Juliet": Shakespeare's tragedy told in three themes — Friar Lorenzo's Good, the families' Enmity (sword-strokes in the cymbals), and Love (English horn for Romeo, flutes for Juliet).

## Sub lesson 2 — Uvertura "Coriolan"

Unit 3, Uvertura "Coriolan" — Beethoven's "Coriolan" overture, in sonata form: the hero's defiant unison theme against the pleading lyrical theme of his mother and wife.

## Sub lesson 3 — Uvertura "Ștefan cel Mare"

Unit 3, Uvertura "Ștefan cel Mare" — Iacob Mureșianu's overture "Ștefan cel Mare" (1883), the first Romanian symphonic poem, on Bolintineanu's poem "The Mother of Stephen the Great": the oboe's uneasy cantilena, the battle, the Victory theme.

## Sub lesson 4 — Uvertura națională "Moldova"

Unit 3, Uvertura națională "Moldova" — Alexandru Flechtenmacher's national overture "Moldova": the first proof in Romanian music that folk song and dance motives can feed the largest symphonic forms — a sunrise over the land, then a fan of contrasting folk dances.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson holds, part by part, in your own words. Companion checks: did every part get named, with its Moldovan name kept where the book gives one?

## Do It Yourself

1. Choose one named part of this lesson and find out one more true thing about it — from a person who knows it, or from the book your part came from.
2. Make or perform a small piece in the way of that part: a rhythm, a step, a drawing, a shaped piece of clay.
3. Write one sentence: what does this part show about Moldova's own way of teaching its arts?
