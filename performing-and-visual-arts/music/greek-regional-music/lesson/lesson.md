# Greek Regional Music

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about greek regional music. Then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — Μουσική... πατριδογνωσία (Music... knowing your homeland)

recognising which region each heard song comes from — Thrace, Macedonia, Epirus and the rest; Béla Bartók, the ethnomusicology pioneer who composed from folk sources

## Sub lesson 2 — Ζυγιές και Κομπανίες (Zygies and Kompanies)

using the map of Greece to place each instrumental ensemble — the zygia and the kompania — in the region where it is found
