# Video script — Our Instruments

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Sing! Singing is happy.

What plays with the singing?

This is መሰንቆ — **masenqo**. One string. A bow sings on it.
This is ከበሮ — **kebero**. A drum. Boom, boom.
This is ዋሽንት — **washint**. A long flute. Blow, and it sings.
This is ክራር — **krar**. A lyre. Strings sing under the fingers.

Masenqo. Kebero. Washint. Krar.
Now you. Point. Name it.
