# Hands-on — Our Instruments

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Name and point
Find pictures of instruments — in a book, on a wall, at a festival. Point to each one you know and say its name out loud: መሰንቆ (masenqo), ከበሮ (kebero), ዋሽንት (washint), ክራር (krar).

## Draw the four
Draw each instrument on its own card, big and clear. Hold up a card; a friend names it. Swap and play again.

## Sound words
Take turns: one person makes an instrument's sound-word with the voice; the others name the instrument. The drum goes boom — who am I?
