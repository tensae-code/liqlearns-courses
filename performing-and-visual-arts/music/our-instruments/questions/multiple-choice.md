# Our Instruments — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The መሰንቆ (masenqo) has how many strings?
a) One  b) Five  c) None
**Answer: a) One** — it is the single-string fiddle, played with a bow.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The ከበሮ (kebero) is a kind of…
a) flute  b) drum  c) lyre
**Answer: b) drum.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** You play the ዋሽንት (washint) by…
a) hitting it  b) blowing into it  c) shaking it
**Answer: b) blowing into it** — it is a long flute.

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** The ክራር (krar) is a…
a) lyre  b) drum  c) bell
**Answer: a) lyre.**

**5. (Sub lesson 4)** Which list names only this lesson's four instruments?
a) masenqo, kebero, washint, krar  b) kebero, bell, drum, guitar  c) washint, piano, krar, drum
**Answer: a) masenqo, kebero, washint, krar.**

**6. (Sub lesson 5)** The Grade 6 roll adds which instruments to this lesson's four?
  a) Negarit, embilta and trumpet (among others) b) Only the piano c) Only the violin
**Answer: a) Negarit, embilta and trumpet (among others)** — mesenqo, negarit, embilta, atamo, kebero, trumpet and the like.

**7. (Sub lesson 5)** The negarit's old office was…
  a) Proclamation — carrying summons and decrees b) Keeping grain dry c) Marking the school timetable
**Answer: a) Proclamation — carrying summons and decrees** — the proclamation drum — a drum that was also a messenger.

**8. (Sub lesson 6)** The mesenqo at Grade 6 depth is…
  a) The azmari's one-stringed bowed lyre b) A bamboo flute c) A proclamation drum
**Answer: a) The azmari's one-stringed bowed lyre** — the praise-singer's instrument — Lema's lifelong companion.

**9. (Sub lesson 6)** The kebero serves both…
  a) The summons march and the church chant b) Only weddings c) Only funerals
**Answer: a) The summons march and the church chant** — one drum, thick and thin, in both worlds.
