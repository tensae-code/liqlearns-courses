# Our Instruments — short answer

Answer in a short sentence or a short list.


**1. (Sub lesson 5)** Name all six instruments of the Grade 6 roll.
**Answer:** Mesenqo, negarit, embilta, atamo, kebero and trumpet.

**2. (Sub lesson 6)** What material is the washint made from, and what rides on its sound?
**Answer:** Bamboo; its breath-line (melody) rides over march and song.
