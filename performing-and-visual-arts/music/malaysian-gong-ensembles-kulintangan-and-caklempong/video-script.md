This is Malaysia's own lesson.

**Minangkabau caklempong of Negeri Sembilan.** Unit 2 Apresiasi: Caklempong — Minangkabau caklempong of Negeri Sembilan: gereteh (15 bonang) plays the main melody, tingkah and sauwa (8 bonang) play ostinato, gendang ibu/anak the beat, with serunai, bangsi and salong winds; played at weddings and feasts; Units 1–2 — recognising and playing the instruments of the caklempong ensemble from scores, with a musician's discipline, and performing folk songs in the ensemble.

**Kulintangan, the gong-chime ensemble of Sabah's KadazanDusun, Bajau.** Unit 5 Kulintangan — kulintangan, the gong-chime ensemble of Sabah's KadazanDusun, Bajau and Murut peoples: its knobbed gongs in a row and its place in celebration; Unit 6 Ensembel Kulintang — playing in a kulintang ensemble (filed under the Book 10 lesson slug music/kulintangan).

**Performing named caklempong pieces, Anak Burung Baniong and Cantik.** Units 1–2 Caklempong — performing named caklempong pieces, Anak Burung Baniong and Cantik Manis, with the ensemble musician's discipline (filed under the Book 4 lesson slug music/playing-in-a-caklempong-ensemble).

That is Malaysian Gong Ensembles: Kulintangan and Caklempong — Malaysia's way, kept by name.
