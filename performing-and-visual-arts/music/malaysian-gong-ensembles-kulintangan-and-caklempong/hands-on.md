# Hands-on — Malaysian Gong Ensembles: Kulintangan and Caklempong

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Minangkabau caklempong of Negeri Sembilan
Work the part with your hands, voice or body as its own craft directs: make it, perform it, or shape it — then show a companion and name the part aloud.
