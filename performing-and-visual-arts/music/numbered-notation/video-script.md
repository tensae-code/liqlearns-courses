# Video script — Numbered Notation

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This is Indonesia's own lesson.

**Bab II, Kegiatan 1.** reading rhythm in numbered notation (notasi angka) — the rhythm symbols written as numbers, imitated and played in groups to the beat.

**Bab III, Kegiatan 1–2.** reading notes in numbered notation, intervals and melody patterns, and singing a children's song's melody by solmisation from the numbered score.

That is Numbered Notation — Indonesia's way, kept by name.
