# Hands-on — The Venezuelan Waltz and Salon Music

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## El valse venezolano y la música de salón
Work through this part with real materials or your own body, the way the book sets it: salon music, the bridge between academic and popular — and the Venezuelan waltz at its centre, danced in salons and heard in theatres (Teresa Carreño, concertist and composer); from this popular root grew the joropo and merengue and, later, the Nationalist School of Sojo, Estévez's Cantata Criolla and Lauro's guitar waltzes. Show a companion what you made or did, and say what the part teaches.
