## Sub lesson 1 — El valse venezolano y la música de salón

Salon music, the bridge between academic and popular — and the Venezuelan waltz at its centre, danced in salons and heard in theatres (Teresa Carreño, concertist and composer); from this popular root grew the joropo and merengue and, later, the Nationalist School of Sojo, Estévez's Cantata Criolla and Lauro's guitar waltzes.
