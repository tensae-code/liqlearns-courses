**El valse venezolano y la música de salón.** Salon music, the bridge between academic and popular — and the Venezuelan waltz at its centre, danced in salons and heard in theatres (Teresa Carreño, concertist and composer); from this popular root grew the joropo and merengue and, later, the Nationalist School of Sojo, Estévez's Cantata Criolla and Lauro's guitar waltzes.

That is The Venezuelan Waltz and Salon Music — Venezuela's own books teach it this way.
