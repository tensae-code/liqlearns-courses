# Inuit Traditional Music — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In Nunavut, songs are heard…
a) only in school  b) everywhere — radio, computer, elders singing, feasts  c) only in the past
**Answer: b) everywhere — radio, computer, elders singing, feasts** — everywhere in Nunavut.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which is one of the book's four forms?
a) katajjaq — throat singing  b) opera  c) ballet
**Answer: a) katajjaq — throat singing** — katajjaq (throat singing), with drum dancing, pisiit and square dancing.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Pisiit are…
a) drum dances  b) personal songs  c) square dances
**Answer: b) personal songs** — pisiit — personal songs.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Music is a fine way to…
a) gather people, tell stories and celebrate  b) keep people apart  c) end a feast early
**Answer: a) gather people, tell stories and celebrate** — gather people together, tell stories and celebrate.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Today's Nunavut musicians share with the world…
a) only their instruments  b) their skills, stories and culture  c) nothing
**Answer: b) their skills, stories and culture** — their skills, their stories and their culture.
