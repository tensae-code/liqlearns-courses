# Inuit Traditional Music — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the book's four forms of traditional music in Nunavut.
**Answer: Katajjaq (throat singing), qilaujjarniq (drum dancing), pisiit (personal songs) and square dancing.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** From whom do children hear the old songs?
**Answer: From grandparents and elders singing them.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What question does the book ask the reader at the end?
**Answer: What story would you tell using a song?**
