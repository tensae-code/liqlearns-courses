# Inuit Traditional Music — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Drum dancing in Nunavut is called qilaujjarniq.
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Traditional music is heard only from elders, never on radio or computer.
**Answer: False — it is heard on radio and computer too.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Music can tell stories and bring people together to celebrate.
**Answer: True.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Today's musicians keep their culture to themselves.
**Answer: False — they share their skills, stories and culture with the world.**
