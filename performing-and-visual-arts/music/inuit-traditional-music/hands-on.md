# Hands-on — Inuit Traditional Music

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Four forms hunt
In groups, each group takes one form — katajjaq, drum dancing, pisiit, square dancing — and presents its name, its sound and its occasion to the class.

## A story in a song
Choose a small story you know. Say how you would tell it using a song, as the book's closing asks.
