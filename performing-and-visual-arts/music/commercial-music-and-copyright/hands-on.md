# Hands-on — Commercial Music and Copyright

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Copyright and intellectual property
Do the practice this part names, with real materials and a partner: Copyright law and its social, cultural and economic impact on the music industry; intellectual property rights — ideas and creations protected by law.

## Running a music business
Do the practice this part names, with real materials and a partner: Market research into customer demand; a promotional and marketing business plan; forming a band and designing its group profile; registering with the Investment Promotion Authority; making jingles, promos and advertisements; planning a music festival or concert; and simple bookkeeping and management.
