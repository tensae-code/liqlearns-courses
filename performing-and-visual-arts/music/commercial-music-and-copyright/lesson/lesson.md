# Commercial Music and Copyright

## Do it first

Look at the sub lesson list. Pick the part you already know something about, and say what you know. Then start at Sub lesson 1 — every part builds on the one before it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Copyright and intellectual property

Copyright law and its social, cultural and economic impact on the music industry; intellectual property rights — ideas and creations protected by law.

## Sub lesson 2 — Running a music business

Market research into customer demand; a promotional and marketing business plan; forming a band and designing its group profile; registering with the Investment Promotion Authority; making jingles, promos and advertisements; planning a music festival or concert; and simple bookkeeping and management.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion this lesson's one thing in your own words, naming each sub lesson as you go. Companion checks: did every part get named?

2. Teach one part to someone else, the way this lesson taught it to you.
