# Video script — Commercial Music and Copyright

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

**Copyright and intellectual property.** Copyright law and its social, cultural and economic impact on the music industry; intellectual property rights — ideas and creations protected by law.

**Running a music business.** Market research into customer demand; a promotional and marketing business plan; forming a band and designing its group profile; registering with the Investment Promotion Authority; making jingles, promos and advertisements; planning a music festival or concert; and simple bookkeeping and management.
