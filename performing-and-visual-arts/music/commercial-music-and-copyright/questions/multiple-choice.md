# Commercial Music and Copyright — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Copyright protects…
a) ideas and creations, by law  b) only buildings
**Answer: a) ideas and creations, by law.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A band registers with…
a) the Investment Promotion Authority  b) no one
**Answer: a) the Investment Promotion Authority.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Market research studies…
a) customer demand  b) the weather
**Answer: a) customer demand.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Jingles and promos are…
a) part of the music business  b) banned
**Answer: a) part of the music business.**
