# Commercial Music and Copyright — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What three impacts of copyright law are studied?
**Answer: Its social, cultural and economic impact on the music industry.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name three business tasks in this unit.
**Answer: Market research, a promotional/marketing plan, forming a band and its profile, IPA registration, jingles/promos/advertisements, festival or concert planning, bookkeeping and management (any three).**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** What is intellectual property?
**Answer: Ideas and creations protected by law.**
