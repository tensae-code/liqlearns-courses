# Video script — Maltese Folk Percussion Instruments

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Malta has its **own** percussion.

The **Tanbur** — goatskin and jingles, dancing with the folk dancers.
The **Żafżafa** — a drum you **rub**, not hit!
And the **Ċuqlajta** — wooden clappers that speak when the bells are silent.

Three instruments, one island.
