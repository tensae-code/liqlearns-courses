# Maltese Folk Percussion Instruments — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** It-Tanbur is…
a) a friction drum  b) a goatskin tambourine with metal jingles  c) a wooden clapper
**Answer: b) a goatskin tambourine with metal jingles.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The Żafżafa makes its sound by…
a) a cane stick rubbed in and out of the skin  b) being blown  c) being bowed
**Answer: a) a cane stick rubbed in and out of the skin.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Iċ-Ċuqlajta clappers replace what on Good Friday?
a) the organ  b) the bells  c) the band
**Answer: b) the bells.**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** The Tanbur accompanies…
a) folk dancing  b) the orchestra  c) the train
**Answer: a) folk dancing.**
