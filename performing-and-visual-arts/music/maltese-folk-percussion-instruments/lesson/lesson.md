# Maltese Folk Percussion Instruments

## Do it first

Quick start: say one thing you already know — a sound, a song, a name — that belongs to this lesson's world. Keep it in mind; the parts will meet it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Malta's own three

**It-Tanbur** — the goatskin **tambourine** with metal jingles that accompanies folk dancing. **Iż-Żafżafa / Ir-Rabbaba** — the **friction drum**: a skin-covered clay pot or tin whose sound comes from a cane stick rubbed in and out of the skin, seen at carnival. **Iċ-Ċuqlajta** — the wooden **Holy Week clappers**: hand clappers and great cross-shaped tower clappers with hammers that **replace the bells on Good Friday**.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the parts in order, one sentence each. Companion checks: did every part get its sentence, and did the order hold?

## Do It Yourself

1. Write the parts' names from memory, in order.
2. Do the hands-on activity once more, teaching it to someone else.
3. Write one sentence: what is the one thing this lesson teaches?
