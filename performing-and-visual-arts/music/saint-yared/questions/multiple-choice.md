# Saint Yared — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.


**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Saint Yared lived in the…
  a) 6th century b) 19th century c) 1st century
**Answer: a) 6th century** — the father of Ethiopian church music, fifteen centuries back.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** His three chant styles are…
  a) Ge'ez, Ezil and Araray b) Tizta, Bati and Ambassel c) Do, re and mi
**Answer: a) Ge'ez, Ezil and Araray** — the church chant styles — not the secular qenet.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Yared's notation symbols serve to…
  a) Fix the chant so it can be taught and handed on exactly b) Decorate the church walls c) Replace the singers
**Answer: a) Fix the chant so it can be taught and handed on exactly** — notation is the bridge across centuries.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Church Instruments' tsenatsil rings inside a musical house built by…
  a) Saint Yared b) Tilahun Gessesse c) Ezra Abate
**Answer: a) Saint Yared** — the chant system the instruments serve is Yared's.
