# Saint Yared — short answer

Answer in a short sentence or a short list.


**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why does one chant system need three styles?
**Answer:** The church's seasons and feasts differ in mood — solemn and joyful — and each style fits its occasion.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why can Ethiopian church music claim a written line to the 6th century?
**Answer:** Because Yared's system carries its own notation, the chant was fixed in signs early and handed on exactly.
