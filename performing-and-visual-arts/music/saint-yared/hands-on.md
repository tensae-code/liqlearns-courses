# Hands-on — Saint Yared

The lesson with your hands, your feet and your voice together, not only your eyes.

## Three moods
Hum three short lines: solemn, middle, joyful. Label each with the style it feels nearest — Ge'ez gravity or Araray lightness.

## The bridge
Teach a partner a four-note pattern by voice alone; then draw signs for it and teach it again from the signs. Which teaching drifted less?
