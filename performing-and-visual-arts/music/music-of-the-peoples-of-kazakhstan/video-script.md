# Video script — Music of the Peoples of Kazakhstan

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is Music of the Peoples of Kazakhstan.

Music of the Peoples of Kazakhstan — The musics of all the peoples who live in Kazakhstan, heard side by side. — as Kazakhstan's school books teach it: Ukraine: the dumy, dramatic songs of history sung by kobzars to the kobza (bandura); its instruments bandura, violin, buben, volynka, gusli, svirel; the hopak, the bright, daring dance of complex elements, and the Virsky ensemble.

Watch, listen — then say it back in your own words.
