## Sub lesson 1 — Kazakhstan Grade 5 Music (Kulmanova)

Ukraine: the dumy, dramatic songs of history sung by kobzars to the kobza (bandura); its instruments bandura, violin, buben, volynka, gusli, svirel; the hopak, the bright, daring dance of complex elements, and the Virsky ensemble; the Caucasus: Georgian singing in three voices, men's or women's choirs only, to zurna, three-string panduri and four-string chonguri; the Lezginka, the fiery Georgian folk dance known worldwide; the Adjarian song «Гандагани» — whose choir sings it, men's, women's or mixed?; Azerbaijan: the ashug — poet, composer, singer, dancer and player in one, once called "the mother of the people" — and his saz; the dastan «Короглы» that Azerbaijani and Kazakh epic share; instruments saz, tar, kemancha, nagara; the children's song «Мои цыплята» (music G. Guseinly, words T. Muttalibov); Tatars in Kazakhstan: Abai himself loved Tatar songs sung to the garmon; instruments garmon-talianka, kurai, kubyz, violin, surnai; the folk song «Алмагачлары» (Apple Trees); the Sabantuy festival; Uyghurs: music close to Uzbek, fiery dances (the Ushar ensemble), instruments kin to Uzbek ones
