# Lesson plan — Music of the Peoples of Kazakhstan

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Music · **Lesson:** Music of the Peoples of Kazakhstan
**Place in the curriculum:** Music (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The musics of all the peoples who live in Kazakhstan, heard side by side..
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this course in the same title.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Kazakhstan Grade 5 Music (Kulmanova)** — Ukraine: the dumy, dramatic songs of history sung by kobzars to the kobza (bandura); its instruments bandura, violin, buben, volynka, gusli, svirel; the hopak, the bright, daring dance of complex elements, and the Virsky ensemble; the Caucasus: Georgian singing in three voices, men's or women's choirs only, to zurna, three-string panduri and four-string chonguri; the Lezginka, the fiery Georgian folk dance known worldwide; the Adjarian song «Гандагани» — whose choir sings it, men's, women's or mixed?; Azerbaijan: the ashug — poet, composer, singer, dancer and player in one, once called "the mother of the people" — and his saz; the dastan «Короглы» that Azerbaijani and Kazakh epic share; instruments saz, tar, kemancha, nagara; the children's song «Мои цыплята» (music G. Guseinly, words T. Muttalibov); Tatars in Kazakhstan: Abai himself loved Tatar songs sung to the garmon; instruments garmon-talianka, kurai, kubyz, violin, surnai; the folk song «Алмагачлары» (Apple Trees); the Sabantuy festival; Uyghurs: music close to Uzbek, fiery dances (the Ushar ensemble), instruments kin to Uzbek ones
