## Sub lesson 1 — Spalding sings a Brazilian song of longing

**Esperanza Spalding**, American jazz bassist and singer, recorded the album **Esperanza (2008)**. **'Samba Em Prelúdio'** itself dates from **1962**, by Brazilian guitarist **Baden Powell** and composer **Vinicius de Moraes** — a **romantic love song of longing for an absent beloved**, sung here in **Portuguese**.

## Sub lesson 2 — Bossa nova style and harmony

**Bossa nova** is Brazil's **samba slowed and subdued**, with its **characteristic rhythm pattern**. Its harmony is **jazz-influenced**: **seventh chords** and **extended chords — ninths, elevenths, thirteenths built by stacking thirds**. The structure runs **intro–verse–guitar solo–verse–coda**, opening with a **free-rhythm bass solo**, and the line is **decorated on acoustic bass guitar**.
