# Bossa Nova — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Bossa nova is samba…
a) slowed and subdued  b) made much faster  c) without rhythm
**Answer: a) slowed and subdued**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** 'Samba Em Prelúdio' was written in…
a) 1962  b) 2008  c) 1900
**Answer: a) 1962**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Esperanza Spalding plays…
a) bass and sings  b) only drums  c) only flute
**Answer: a) bass and sings**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Extended chords are built by…
a) stacking thirds  b) removing notes  c) shouting
**Answer: a) stacking thirds**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The song is sung here in…
a) Portuguese  b) Amharic  c) English only
**Answer: a) Portuguese**
