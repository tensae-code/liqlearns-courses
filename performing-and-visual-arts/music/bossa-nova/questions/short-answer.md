# Bossa Nova — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Who wrote 'Samba Em Prelúdio'?
**Answer: Baden Powell and Vinicius de Moraes.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Give the song's structure in order.
**Answer: Intro–verse–guitar solo–verse–coda.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the three extended chords named.
**Answer: Ninths, elevenths and thirteenths.**
