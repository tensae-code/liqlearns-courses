# Video script — Bossa Nova

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Brazil! Samba slows down… softens… — **bossa nova** is born.

A love song of longing, sung in Portuguese. Esperanza Spalding sings — and plays the bass.

Rich chords — sevenths, ninths, elevenths, thirteenths — stacked thirds, like steps to the sky.
