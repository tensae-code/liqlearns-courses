# Hands-on — Music of the Ancient World

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Música y danza en el antiguo Egipto
Work through this part with real materials or your own body, the way the book sets it: music in ancient Egypt, rich in instruments — lute, harp, flute and tambourine — shown in a painted scene of musical celebration. Show a companion what you made or did, and say what the part teaches.
