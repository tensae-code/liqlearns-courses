# Hands-on — Notes and Rests

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Draw the signs
On paper, draw the six signs in a row: whole note, whole rest, half note, half rest, quarter note, quarter rest. Under each, write its beats: 4, 4, 2, 2, 1, 1. Colour the whole notes as the first book does — the shape stays the same in every colour.

## Count with your hands
Use both hands as the measuring tool, as the later book teaches: tap one hand with the other on every beat. Sound “ta” for notes; keep tapping in silence for rests. Partner checks the count never stops.

## Egg-shape hunt
The first practice asks it: list things around you whose shape resembles a whole note — an egg, a lemon, a stone. Draw one beside your whole note.
