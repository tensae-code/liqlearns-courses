# Lesson plan — Notes and Rests

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Music · **Lesson:** Notes and Rests
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 3 — Music (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the six signs of music time — whole, half and quarter notes and their rests — each sign's exact shape and its beat value: 4, 2, 1 sounded beats, and 4, 2, 1 silent beats.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on singing and clapping — the learner can already hold a steady beat. Beat (ምት) is the measure; rhythm (ስልተ-ምት) is the pattern the signs make.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **ሙሉ ኖታ — the whole note and the whole rest** — a note is the drawing of music; the whole note is an open oval counted 4 beats; the whole rest is a block hanging from a line, counted 4 silent beats.
2. **ግማሽ ኖታ — the half note and the half rest** — the whole note plus a stem on its right side, counted 2 beats; the half rest is a block sitting on a line, counted 2 silent beats.
3. **The quarter note and the quarter rest** — a filled note head with a stem, 1 beat; the quarter rest, 1 silent beat.
4. **All the signs together, and the Note-Values Song** — notes sound, rests are silent — same counting; the song ሙዚቃ አጠናለሁ (I Study Music) sings the three values.

5. **The Grade 4 chapter opens — notes, colours, shapes, theatre and photographs (chapter opening — Grade 4)** — the Grade 4 book's Chapter 1 opener and aims: apply simple-beat notes with body movement, perform simple-beat songs and traditional dances, tell colours apart, distinguish shapes and forms by colour, grasp what theatre art is, and read photo compositions (Grade 4, printed p. 1).

6. **The Grade 4 chapter closes — beat, notes, shapes, colours and photographs (chapter closing — Grade 4)** — the Grade 4 book's Chapter 1 summary and closing exercise: 15 items mixing beat, notes vs rests, shape vs form, colour and photo formats (Grade 4, printed pp. 20–21).

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Hearing Each Instrument (listening by ear) and any clapping songs. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Draw each note sign exactly: whole (open oval, no stem), half (open oval, stem), quarter (filled oval, stem).
2. Draw each rest sign exactly: whole rest hanging, half rest sitting, quarter rest.
3. State each value: whole = 4, half = 2, quarter = 1 — sounded for notes, silent for rests.
4. Sound “ta” or “la” for a note's full value and count a rest in silence, without stopping the count.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil — drawing the signs is half the lesson.

## The run of the lesson (in order)

## If the learner is stuck
- Draws the whole rest sitting on the line → hanging = whole (4), sitting = half (2): the whole rest hangs DOWN with its full weight.
- Gives the half note 4 beats → the stem halves it: whole 4, half 2, quarter 1 — and two halves fill one whole.
- Stops counting during a rest → a rest is played, in silence, for its full count; the count never stops.
