# Notes and Rests — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A note is defined as…
a) the drawing of music  b) a kind of drum  c) a dance step
**Answer: a) the drawing of music** — የሙዚቃ ሥዕል.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The whole note is counted…
a) 1 beat  b) 2 beats  c) 4 beats
**Answer: c) 4 beats.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The whole rest's sign is a block that…
a) sits on a line  b) hangs from a line  c) stands alone with a stem
**Answer: b) hangs from a line** — and it is silent for 4 beats.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The half note is made from the whole note by adding…
a) a flag  b) a stem on its right side  c) a dot
**Answer: b) a stem on its right side.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The half rest sits on a line and counts…
a) 4 silent beats  b) 2 silent beats  c) 1 silent beat
**Answer: b) 2 silent beats.**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** The quarter note's head is…
a) open and empty  b) filled (black)  c) missing
**Answer: b) filled (black)** — with a stem, for 1 beat.

**7. (Sub lesson 4)** A half note equals how many quarter notes in duration?
a) one  b) two  c) four
**Answer: b) two.**
