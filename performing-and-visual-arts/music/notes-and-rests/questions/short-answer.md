# Notes and Rests — short answer

Answer in a short sentence or a short list.

**1. (Sub lesson 5)** Name two performance aims of Grade 4 Chapter 1.
**Answer:** Perform simple-beat songs, and perform traditional dances (applying simple-beat notes with body movement is also an aim).

**2. (Sub lesson 5)** What will learners learn to read in Grade 4 Chapter 1?
**Answer:** Photo compositions — what a photograph's arrangement tells.

**3. (Sub lesson 6)** Name the five topics the Grade 4 Chapter 1 closing exercise mixes.
**Answer:** Beat; notes vs rests; shape vs form; colour; photo formats.
