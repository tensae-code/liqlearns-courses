# Video script — Notes and Rests

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A note is the **drawing of music** — የሙዚቃ ሥዕል.

**ሙሉ ኖታ — the whole note.** An open egg. No stem. Sing “ta” — 1, 2, 3, 4. Four beats!
Its rest hangs from the line. Silence — 1, 2, 3, 4.

**ግማሽ ኖታ — the half note.** The egg grows a stem. “Ta” — 1, 2. Two beats!
Its rest sits on the line. Silence — 1, 2.

**The quarter note.** Filled black, with a stem. “Ta!” One beat!
Its rest: silence — 1.

Sound the notes. Count the rests. Never stop counting.
