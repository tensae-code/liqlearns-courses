# Video script — Playing the Guitar

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

**Guitar studies.** The guitar's history and development, its parts, rest stroke, free stroke and picking; chords in C, G and D major and their relative minors; reading staff notes and sight-reading on the fretboard.

**Keyboard studies beside the guitar.** Keyboard/piano studies: black and white keys, finger exercises, the C major scale and chords.

**Grade 12 depth on both instruments.** In Grade 12 both instruments work in the keys of C, G, D and F major — two-octave scales, natural, harmonic and melodic minor scales and blues scales — and pieces are performed in major and minor keys.
