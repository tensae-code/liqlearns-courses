# Playing the Guitar — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — Guitar studies
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: The guitar's history and development, its parts, rest stroke, free stroke and picking; chords in C, G and D major and their relative minors; reading staff notes and sight-reading on the fretboard.

## Sub lesson 2 — Keyboard studies beside the guitar
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Keyboard/piano studies: black and white keys, finger exercises, the C major scale and chords.

## Sub lesson 3 — Grade 12 depth on both instruments
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: In Grade 12 both instruments work in the keys of C, G, D and F major — two-octave scales, natural, harmonic and melodic minor scales and blues scales — and pieces are performed in major and minor keys.
