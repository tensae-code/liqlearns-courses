# Hands-on — Playing the Guitar

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Guitar studies
Do the practice this part names, with real materials and a partner: The guitar's history and development, its parts, rest stroke, free stroke and picking; chords in C, G and D major and their relative minors; reading staff notes and sight-reading on the fretboard.

## Keyboard studies beside the guitar
Do the practice this part names, with real materials and a partner: Keyboard/piano studies: black and white keys, finger exercises, the C major scale and chords.

## Grade 12 depth on both instruments
Do the practice this part names, with real materials and a partner: In Grade 12 both instruments work in the keys of C, G, D and F major — two-octave scales, natural, harmonic and melodic minor scales and blues scales — and pieces are performed in major and minor keys.
