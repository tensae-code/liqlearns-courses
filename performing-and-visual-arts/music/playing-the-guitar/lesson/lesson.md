# Playing the Guitar

## Do it first

Look at the sub lesson list. Pick the part you already know something about, and say what you know. Then start at Sub lesson 1 — every part builds on the one before it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Guitar studies

The guitar's history and development, its parts, rest stroke, free stroke and picking; chords in C, G and D major and their relative minors; reading staff notes and sight-reading on the fretboard.

## Sub lesson 2 — Keyboard studies beside the guitar

Keyboard/piano studies: black and white keys, finger exercises, the C major scale and chords.

## Sub lesson 3 — Grade 12 depth on both instruments

In Grade 12 both instruments work in the keys of C, G, D and F major — two-octave scales, natural, harmonic and melodic minor scales and blues scales — and pieces are performed in major and minor keys.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion this lesson's one thing in your own words, naming each sub lesson as you go. Companion checks: did every part get named?

## Do It Yourself

1. Do each sub lesson's practice once more, on your own.
2. Teach one part to someone else, the way this lesson taught it to you.
3. Write one sentence: what is this lesson's one thing?
