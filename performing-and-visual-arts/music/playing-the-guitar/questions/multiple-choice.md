# Playing the Guitar — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Rest stroke and free stroke are…
a) guitar right-hand techniques  b) dance steps
**Answer: a) guitar right-hand techniques.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The first guitar chords are in…
a) C, G and D major, with their relative minors  b) no keys
**Answer: a) C, G and D major, with their relative minors.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Grade 12 scales include…
a) natural, harmonic and melodic minors and blues scales  b) one scale only
**Answer: a) natural, harmonic and melodic minors and blues scales.**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Keyboard studies begin with…
a) black and white keys and finger exercises  b) pedals only
**Answer: a) black and white keys and finger exercises.**
