# Playing the Guitar — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The guitar's history and parts are part of the study.
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Minor scales are never studied.
**Answer: False — natural, harmonic and melodic minor scales are Grade 12 work.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Pieces are performed in major and minor keys.
**Answer: True.**
