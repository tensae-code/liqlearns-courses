# Playing the Guitar — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What three right-hand ways of sounding the string are named?
**Answer: Rest stroke, free stroke and picking.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In which four keys does Grade 12 work?
**Answer: C, G, D and F major.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What is sight-read, and where?
**Answer: Staff notes, sight-read on the fretboard.**
