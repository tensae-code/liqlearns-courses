# Wagner and Music Drama

## Do it first

Name one thing that belongs in this lesson — German opera's road to Wagner — the singspiel, Weber's 'Der Freischütz' and the music drama. Say what it is in your own words. If you can name one, you are already inside the lesson.

## Sub lesson 1 — opera in Germany

opera in Germany: the singspiel tradition and Weber's "Der Freischütz", foundation of German national opera — then Richard Wagner's (1813–1883) reform: opera becomes music drama, the Gesamtkunstwerk uniting music, poetry and stage; the leitmotif; the four-opera "Ring of the Nibelung"; and Bayreuth, his theatre with the orchestra hidden from sight.
