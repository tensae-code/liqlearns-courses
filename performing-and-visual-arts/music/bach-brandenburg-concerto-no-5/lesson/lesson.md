## Sub lesson 1 — The Baroque period and the concerto grosso

The **Baroque period (c.1600–1750)**. In a **concerto grosso** a small solo group — the **concertino** — plays against the full orchestra, the **ripieno**. In Bach's Brandenburg Concerto No. 5 the concertino is **flute, violin and harpsichord**.

## Sub lesson 2 — The harpsichord and the gigue finale

The **harpsichord** is a **plucked-string keyboard** instrument; here it plays solo and also serves as the **continuo**, the foundation part under the whole work. The **third movement** is a lively **gigue** — a dance movement.
