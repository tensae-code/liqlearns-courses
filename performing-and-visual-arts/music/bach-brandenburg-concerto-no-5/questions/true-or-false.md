# Bach: Brandenburg Concerto No. 5 — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The concerto grosso sets a small solo group against the full orchestra.
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The harpsichord is a wind instrument.
**Answer: False — it is a plucked-string keyboard.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The Brandenburg Concerto No. 5 is a Baroque work.
**Answer: True.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A gigue is a slow, sad dance.
**Answer: False — it is lively.**
