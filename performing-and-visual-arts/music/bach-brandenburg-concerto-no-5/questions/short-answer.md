# Bach: Brandenburg Concerto No. 5 — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the full orchestra in a concerto grosso called?
**Answer: The ripieno.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What second job does the harpsichord do besides solo?
**Answer: It plays the continuo, the foundation part.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the three concertino instruments.
**Answer: Flute, violin and harpsichord.**
