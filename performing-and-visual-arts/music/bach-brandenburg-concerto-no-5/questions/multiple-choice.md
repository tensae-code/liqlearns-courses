# Bach: Brandenburg Concerto No. 5 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Baroque period runs about…
a) 1600–1750  b) 1200–1400  c) 1900–2000
**Answer: a) 1600–1750**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In a concerto grosso, the concertino is…
a) the small solo group  b) the full orchestra  c) the audience
**Answer: a) the small solo group**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Bach's concertino here is flute, violin and…
a) harpsichord  b) trumpet  c) drum
**Answer: a) harpsichord**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The harpsichord makes its sound with…
a) plucked strings  b) blown air  c) hit skins
**Answer: a) plucked strings**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The third movement is a gigue, which is…
a) a lively dance  b) a funeral march  c) a lullaby
**Answer: a) a lively dance**
