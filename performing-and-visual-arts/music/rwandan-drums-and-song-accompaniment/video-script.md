# Video script — Rwandan Drums and Song Accompaniment

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Hear the drums of Rwanda!

A drum, a can, a jerry can — sounding instruments, keeping the rhythm.

Then drums and calabashes together — no discordance!

And ingoma — umurishyo! — the rhythms the Intore dancers answer.
