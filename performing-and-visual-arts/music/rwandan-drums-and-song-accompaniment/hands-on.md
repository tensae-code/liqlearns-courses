# Hands-on — Rwandan Drums and Song Accompaniment

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Accompany a song three ways
Take one song the class knows. First accompany it with claps; then with sounding instruments (drum, can, jerry can); then in two groups — drums and calabash (or its nearest sounding stand-in) together. The class says which accompaniment kept the rhythm truest.
