# Rwandan Drums and Song Accompaniment — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these does the guide name as a sounding instrument?
a) a jerry can  b) a pencil  c) a basket
**Answer: a) a jerry can**

**2.** Accompaniment must above all…
a) respect the song's rhythm  b) drown the voices  c) change the song
**Answer: a) respect the song's rhythm**

**3.** Primary 3 allies songs with…
a) drums and calabash sockets  b) violins only  c) silence
**Answer: a) drums and calabash sockets**

**4.** The ingoma rhythms (umurishyo w'ingoma)…
a) balance the Intore dancer's movements  b) are played by one drummer alone, always  c) have no link to dance
**Answer: a) balance the Intore dancer's movements**
