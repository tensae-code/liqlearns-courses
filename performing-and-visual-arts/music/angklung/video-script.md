The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

This is Indonesia's own lesson.

**Bab V Bermain Alat Musik Ensambel.** the angklung, Indonesia's shaken bamboo instrument, played as the melodic voice of the class ensemble.

**Bab V Bermain Alat Musik.** the angklung of Sunda (West Java) — how it is held and shaken so each bamboo tube sounds its note, and playing together in ensemble.

**Bab I.** the angklung of West Java, pictured and classified by its sound source among Indonesia's traditional instruments.

That is Angklung — Indonesia's way, kept by name.
