# Angklung

## Do it first

Name one thing that belongs in this lesson — Indonesia's angklung — tuned bamboo tubes shaken to sound — played solo and in ensemble. Say what it is in your own words. If you can name one, you are already inside the lesson.

## Sub lesson 1 — Bab V Bermain Alat Musik Ensambel

the angklung, Indonesia's shaken bamboo instrument, played as the melodic voice of the class ensemble.

## Sub lesson 2 — Bab V Bermain Alat Musik

the angklung of Sunda (West Java) — how it is held and shaken so each bamboo tube sounds its note, and playing together in ensemble.

## Sub lesson 3 — Bab I

the angklung of West Java, pictured and classified by its sound source among Indonesia's traditional instruments.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson holds, part by part, in your own words. Companion checks: did every part get named, with its Indonesian name kept where the book gives one?

## Do It Yourself

1. Choose one named part of this lesson and find out one more true thing about it — from a person who knows it, or from the book your part came from.
2. Make or perform a small piece in the way of that part: a rhythm, a step, a drawing, a shaped piece of clay.
3. Write one sentence: what does this part show about Indonesia's own way of teaching its arts?
