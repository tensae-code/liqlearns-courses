# Lesson plan — The Regional Culture Groups

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Music · **Lesson:** The Regional Culture Groups
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 3 — Music (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the Amhara region's named culture groups — Wollo Lalibela Kinet, Wo/ro Sahin's music school in Gondar, the Fasiledes culture group (Gondar) and the Gish Abay culture group (Gojjam) — the organised keepers of the region's music.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Amhara Music History and Musicians of the Amhara Region — the groups are where those lives worked together.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The named groups and their places** — Wollo Lalibela Kinet; Wo/ro Sahin's music school in Gondar; Fasiledes culture group (Gondar); Gish Abay culture group (Gojjam).
2. **What a culture group keeps alive** — repertoire, teaching and occasion: how a group carries songs, dances and instruments across generations.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Amhara Music History; Musicians of the Amhara Region. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name the four culture groups/schools the book names, with their places.
2. Say what a culture group keeps alive for its region.

## What you need
Only names and places — a map of the region helps.
