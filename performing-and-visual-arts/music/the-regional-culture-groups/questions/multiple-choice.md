# The Regional Culture Groups — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.


**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which group belongs to Gojjam?
  a) Gish Abay culture group b) Fasiledes culture group c) Wollo Lalibela Kinet
**Answer: a) Gish Abay culture group** — Gish Abay — Gojjam; Fasiledes — Gondar; Lalibela Kinet — Wollo.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A kinet (culture group) is…
  a) A company keeping a region's songs and dances as repertoire b) A single solo singer c) A building only
**Answer: a) A company keeping a region's songs and dances as repertoire** — singers, players and dancers organised to keep the music alive.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A culture group keeps alive…
  a) Repertoire, teaching and occasions b) Only its building c) Only its costumes
**Answer: a) Repertoire, teaching and occasions** — the songs, the learning and the stages — all three.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Younger members of a group learn…
  a) Inside the company, performing with it b) Only from books c) Never — groups take only masters
**Answer: a) Inside the company, performing with it** — teaching happens inside the performing company.
