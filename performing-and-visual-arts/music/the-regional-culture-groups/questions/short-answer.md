# The Regional Culture Groups — short answer

Answer in a short sentence or a short list.


**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name Gondar's two named music-keepers.
**Answer:** Wo/ro Sahin's music school and the Fasiledes culture group.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How does a group's keeping differ from an azmari's?
**Answer:** The azmari carried music alone from place to place; the group carries it together, in one place, on purpose.
