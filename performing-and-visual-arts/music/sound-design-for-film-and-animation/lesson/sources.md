Анимация және дыбыспен жұмыс (Animation and working with sound) — what it teaches: at shooting a real film, together with the sound operator (дыбыс операторы) the audio engineer (аудиоинженер) also works at the same time; at creating an art film the sound operator works in a professional group; the sound operator must understand sound's physical properties and music; the sound operator writes all the film's component sounds, where and how, processes them, joins them; for this he needs a sound recording studio (дыбыс жазу студиясы) equipped with technical devices; research: research Kazakhstani sound operators, discuss your research in class; to listen to your favourite song and watch your favourite production, not staying at home — for this a smartphone, computer, television can be used; television is watched at home, in the street, mobile (қолдағы); such devices' joining and cultural events' spreading, their place and time are also shown; usually mass media are used everywhere; where do you use what kind of music?; create your own work and perform it: imagine yourself as an animation film's sound operator; to create your own work you need to find sound effects and music and write the work; get acquainted with additional information using Internet possibilities: remember — group work is a wonderful production's basis!; group work together with an art theatre gives a result like an animation film's appearance; if you are in a creative group, work together, you will reach much success; evaluate your work creatively as an artist: 1. why did you choose exactly these sound effects and music for accompanying the animation?; 2. how important are music and sound effects at creating animation?; 3. how do music and sounds interact with each other?; 4. what would you change, what would you add?; the Metro-Goldwyn-Mayer cinematography company's lion's roar's writing moment, 1928, pictured (student book) | Book 1, printed p. 76 |
