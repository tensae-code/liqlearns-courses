# Armenian Urban Songs

## Do it first

Name one thing that belongs in this lesson — Armenian Urban Songs — Armenia's own teaching of this topic, kept as named parts: Theme 3: Author songs and the URBAN SONG (քաղաքային երգ, 19th-20th centuries) — the urban song is a collective creation whose texts became the advanced patriotic literature of the time; patriotic songs form its largest and most characteristic part (teacher-guide evidence). ## Do It Yourself

1. Choose one named part of this lesson and find out one more true thing about it — from a person who knows it, or from the book your part came from.
2. Make or perform a small piece in the way of that part: a rhythm, a step, a drawing, a shaped piece of clay.
3. Write one sentence: what does this part show about Armenia's own way of teaching its arts?
