# Armenian Urban Songs — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson teaches...
a) another country's arts only  b) Armenian Urban Songs — Armenia's own teaching of this topic, kept as named parts: Theme 3: Author songs and the URBAN SONG (քաղաքային երգ, 19th-20th centuries) — the urban song is a collective creation whose texts became the advanced patriotic literature of the time; patriotic songs form its largest and most characteristic part (teacher-guide evidence)  c) nothing — it is just names
**Answer: b)** — that is the lesson's one thing.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The first sub lesson is...
a) Theme 3  b) an examination  c) a song from another continent
**Answer: a) Theme 3.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Armenia's own forms in this lesson are...
a) dropped and replaced  b) kept as named parts  c) hidden
**Answer: b) kept as named parts.**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** The last sub lesson is...
a) Theme 3  b) the bibliography  c) a review box
**Answer: a) Theme 3.**

**5. (Sub lesson 1)** Songs in this course are placed...
