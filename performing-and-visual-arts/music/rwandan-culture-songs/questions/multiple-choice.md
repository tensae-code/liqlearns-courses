# Rwandan Culture Songs — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** The songs of this lesson are about…
a) Rwandan culture and health  b) foreign kings  c) examinations
**Answer: a) Rwandan culture and health**

**2.** The songs are performed…
a) in public, with instrument accompaniment  b) silently  c) only by the teacher
**Answer: a) in public, with instrument accompaniment**

**3.** First the class learns the song by…
a) imitation  b) guessing  c) drawing it
**Answer: a) imitation**

**4.** The accompaniment must…
a) respect the rhythm  b) play faster than the song  c) stop the singing
**Answer: a) respect the rhythm**
