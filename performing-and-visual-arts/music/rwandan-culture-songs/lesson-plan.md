# Lesson plan — Rwandan Culture Songs

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Music · **Lesson:** Rwandan Culture Songs
**Place in the curriculum:** Music (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Songs about Rwandan culture (and health) — imitated, sung correctly and performed in public with instrument accompaniment. A slim lesson, as its evidence is slim: it stands on one teacher-guide lesson.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this course in the same title.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Songs about Rwandan culture** — The Primary 2 music guide's culture lesson teaches **songs about Rwandan culture (and health)**: songs that carry the country's own way of life, sung as Rwanda's own songs are sung.
2. **Singing them in public, with instruments** — The songs are **imitated and sung correctly in public, with instrument accompaniment** — the class first copies the song as it is given, then performs it whole, instruments under the voices, respecting the rhythm.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of this course in the same title. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say and show what 'Songs about Rwandan culture' teaches, in the book's own way.
2. Say and show what 'Singing them in public, with instruments' teaches, in the book's own way.
