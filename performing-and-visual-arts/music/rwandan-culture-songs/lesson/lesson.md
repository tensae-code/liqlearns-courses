# Rwandan Culture Songs

## Do it first

Start with the first part below — do it or make it before you read any explanation.

## Sub lesson 1 — Songs about Rwandan culture

The Primary 2 music guide's culture lesson teaches **songs about Rwandan culture (and health)**: songs that carry the country's own way of life, sung as Rwanda's own songs are sung.

## Sub lesson 2 — Singing them in public, with instruments

The songs are **imitated and sung correctly in public, with instrument accompaniment** — the class first copies the song as it is given, then performs it whole, instruments under the voices, respecting the rhythm.
