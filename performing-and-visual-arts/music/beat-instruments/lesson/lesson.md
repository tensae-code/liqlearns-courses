# Beat Instruments

## Do it first

Clap once. Tap the table once. Stamp once. Every sound you just made came from one thing hitting another. Hold that thought — it is the whole lesson.

## Sub lesson 1 — What a beat instrument is

A **beat instrument** (a percussion instrument) gives sound by **striking one body against another, by clashing, or by beating with a beating stick**.

The set of this lesson is six: **አታሞ (atamo)**, **ከበሮ (kebero)**, **ደወል (bell)**, **ቃጭል (qachil)**, **ፅናፅል (tsenatsil)** and **ነጋሪት (negarit)**. The bell, the qachil and the tsenatsil have their own lesson next — the church instruments. Here we meet the three drums closely.

## Sub lesson 2 — አታሞ atamo

The **አታሞ (atamo)** is a short drum. It is open wood or tin, covered with hide at the front and the back alike. It is made for **secular music** — everyday music, not church music — and it is mostly used by girls and women.

## Sub lesson 3 — ከበሮ kebero

The **ከበሮ (kebero)** is made of **wood, with cattle hide at the front and the back**. It serves in various **spiritual chants**. You met its boom in the songs lessons; now you know what it is made of.

[GIF 9623 — kebero beaten]

## Sub lesson 4 — ነጋሪት negarit, the proclamation drum

The **ነጋሪት (negarit)** is beaten with a **large beating stick**. In ancient times it served **around the palace**: when a decree — an order of the ruler — was to be proclaimed, the negarit called the people to hear it, and it sounded at **coronation festivals**, when a ruler was crowned. Its beat was the voice of news itself.

## Say it to your companion

Name the six beat instruments. Then tell your companion the negarit's old job, in one sentence. Companion checks: six names? The palace, decrees and coronations in the sentence?

## Do It Yourself

1. List three beat sounds you can make at home, and what strikes what for each.
2. Draw the atamo, the kebero and the negarit, and write each name under its drum: አታሞ, ከበሮ, ነጋሪት.
3. Say which of the six belongs to the church set — bell, qachil, tsenatsil — and which drum is for secular music.
