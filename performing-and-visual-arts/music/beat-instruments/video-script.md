# Video script — Beat Instruments

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Tap the table. Clap your hands. Stamp!

You made sound by **striking**. That is a beat instrument.

**አታሞ — atamo.** A short drum. Wood or tin, hide at both ends. For everyday music.
**ከበሮ — kebero.** Wood and cattle hide. It serves in spiritual chants.
**ነጋሪት — negarit.** The great drum of long ago. At the palace it called out the king's words!

Strike. Clash. Beat with a stick. Beat instruments!
