# Beat Instruments — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What makes an instrument a beat instrument?
**Answer: It gives sound by striking one body against another, by clashing, or by beating with a beating stick.**

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** What is the kebero made of, and what does it serve?
**Answer: Wood with cattle hide at front and back; it serves in various spiritual chants.**

**3. (Sub lesson 4)** Where did the negarit serve in ancient times, and for what?
**Answer: Around the palace — for proclaiming decrees and for coronation festivals.**
