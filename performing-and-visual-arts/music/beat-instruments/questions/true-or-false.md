# Beat Instruments — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Clashing two things together is one way a beat instrument sounds.
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** The kebero is made of iron.
**Answer: False** — wood and cattle hide. The iron instruments are the church set.

**3. (Sub lesson 4)** The negarit was beaten with a large beating stick.
**Answer: True.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The atamo is a church instrument.
**Answer: False** — it is made for secular music.
