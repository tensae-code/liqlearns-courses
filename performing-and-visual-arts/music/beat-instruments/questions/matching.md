# Beat Instruments — matching

Match each instrument to its true line.

1. (Sub lesson 2) አታሞ (atamo) — ?
2. (Sub lesson 3) ከበሮ (kebero) — ?
3. (Sub lesson 4) ነጋሪት (negarit) — ?

a) wood and cattle hide; serves in spiritual chants
b) the great palace drum of proclamations, beaten with a large stick
c) short drum of wood or tin and hide, for secular music

**Answer key:** 1 — c; 2 — a; 3 — b.
