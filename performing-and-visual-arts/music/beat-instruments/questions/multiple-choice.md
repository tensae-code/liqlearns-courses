# Beat Instruments — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A beat instrument gives sound by…
a) blowing  b) striking, clashing, or beating with a stick  c) bowing a string
**Answer: b) striking, clashing, or beating with a stick.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The atamo is mostly used by…
a) palace guards  b) girls and women  c) priests only
**Answer: b) girls and women.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The atamo is made for…
a) church chant  b) secular (everyday) music  c) proclamations
**Answer: b) secular (everyday) music.**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** The kebero's skins are…
a) tin  b) cattle hide  c) cloth
**Answer: b) cattle hide** — at front and back, on a wooden body.

**5. (Sub lesson 4)** In ancient times the negarit served to…
a) call people to hear proclamations  b) grind grain  c) carry water
**Answer: a) call people to hear proclamations** — and it sounded at coronation festivals.
