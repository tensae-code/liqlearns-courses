# Hands-on — Beat Instruments

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Three ways to strike
Make a beat three ways: strike hand on table, clash two lids or stones gently, beat a book with a spoon as the stick. Say the definition as you play: sound by striking, clashing, beating.

## Palace proclamation
One player beats a slow, great beat on a table — the negarit. The others stand straight like people hearing a decree. Then say together what the negarit was for.

## Atamo circle
Sit in a circle. Pass one beat around — each person taps once, in turn, keeping the beat even. That shared tapping is what beat instruments do for a song.
