# Percussion Instruments of Africa — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Donno talking drum bends its pitch by…
a) adding water  b) squeezing its hourglass skins under the arm  c) hitting harder
**Answer: b) squeezing its hourglass skins under the arm.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The Balafon is a wooden xylophone with…
a) metal bells  b) gourd resonators  c) a skin head
**Answer: b) gourd resonators.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The Axatse is…
a) a seed-net gourd rattle  b) a metal bell  c) a talking drum
**Answer: a) a seed-net gourd rattle.**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Gankogui and Atoke are…
a) paired metal bells  b) water bowls  c) friction drums
**Answer: a) paired metal bells.**
