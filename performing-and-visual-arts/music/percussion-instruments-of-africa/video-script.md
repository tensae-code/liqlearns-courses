# Video script — Percussion Instruments of Africa

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Africa's drums can **talk**.

Squeeze the **Donno** — its pitch bends, like a voice!
Wood and gourds sing in the **Balafon**.
Seeds rattle, bells ring in pairs.

That is **Africa's percussion**.
