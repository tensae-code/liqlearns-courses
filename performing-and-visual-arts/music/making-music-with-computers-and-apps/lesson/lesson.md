## Sub lesson 1 — Copyright and music publishing

**Copyright** protects a work against **copying, distribution, public performance, adaptation and broadcast** without permission. A song and its **master recording** carry **separate copyrights**. **Royalties** are the payment for permission to use the work. In the UK the composer's copyright lasts for **their life plus 70 years**; recordings are protected for **70 years from release**.

## Sub lesson 2 — What music publishers do

**Publishers administer copyrights** — **protecting, licensing and collecting royalties** through **PRS for Music and MCPS**. The rights come as a **bundle of separate rights — print, mechanical, performance and synchronisation** — and publishers may pay **advances against royalties**. Arranging a **copyright work needs permission**; an **out-of-copyright work can be freely arranged**, and the arranger gains a **new copyright in the arrangement**. A song's **music and lyrics carry separate copyrights**.
