# Making Music with Computers and Apps — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A work and its master recording have separate copyrights.
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A song's music and lyrics share one single copyright.
**Answer: False — they carry separate copyrights.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Publishers protect, license and collect royalties.
**Answer: True.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Anyone may arrange a copyright work freely without permission.
**Answer: False — permission is needed while the work is in copyright.**
