# Making Music with Computers and Apps — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Copyright protects against unlicensed…
a) copying, performance and broadcast  b) listening at home  c) practising an instrument
**Answer: a) copying, performance and broadcast**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Royalties are…
a) payment for permission to use a work  b) a kind of drum  c) free copies
**Answer: a) payment for permission to use a work**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** UK copyright in a composition lasts the composer's life plus…
a) 70 years  b) 7 years  c) 700 years
**Answer: a) 70 years**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Publishers collect royalties through…
a) PRS for Music and MCPS  b) the post office  c) the concert usher
**Answer: a) PRS for Music and MCPS**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Arranging a work still in copyright needs…
a) permission  b) nothing at all  c) a new title only
**Answer: a) permission**
