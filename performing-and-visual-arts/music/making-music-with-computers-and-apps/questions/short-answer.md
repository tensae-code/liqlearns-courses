# Making Music with Computers and Apps — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How long are recordings protected in the UK?
**Answer: 70 years from release.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What new right does an arranger of an out-of-copyright work gain?
**Answer: A new copyright in the arrangement.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Name two of the separate rights in the bundle.
**Answer: Any two of: print, mechanical, performance, synchronisation.**
