## Sub lesson 1 — Frédéric Chopin (1810–1849), "poet of the piano": almost all his music is for piano alone

Frédéric Chopin (1810–1849), "poet of the piano": almost all his music is for piano alone — mazurkas, polonaises, nocturnes, ballades, waltzes and études raised from exercises into concert poems, Polish in rhythm and spirit without quoting folk tunes directly.
