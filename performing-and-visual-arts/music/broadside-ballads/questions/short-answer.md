# Broadside Ballads — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is a götuvisa?
**Answer: A cheap printed street ballad, usually anonymous, carrying news.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Name two things street ballads reported.
**Answer: Any two of: news, crimes, accidents.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Who wrote Mack the Knife?
**Answer: Kurt Weill.**
