# Broadside Ballads — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Götuvísur were sold…
a) on markets and street corners  b) only in palaces  c) only at sea
**Answer: a) on markets and street corners**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Their author was usually…
a) unknown  b) the king  c) a famous composer
**Answer: a) unknown**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Street ballads carried…
a) news, crimes and accidents  b) only love poems  c) only dances
**Answer: a) news, crimes and accidents**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** They were the social media of…
a) their time  b) next year  c) no time
**Answer: a) their time**

**5. (Sub lesson 1)** Mack the Knife is heard in versions by Weill and…
a) Louis Armstrong  b) Sousa  c) Debussy
**Answer: a) Louis Armstrong**
