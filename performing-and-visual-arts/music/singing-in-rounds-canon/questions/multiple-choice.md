# Singing in Rounds: Canon — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A round is also called…
a) a canon  b) a scale  c) a rest
**Answer: a) a canon.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In a round, groups sing…
a) the same song, starting one after another  b) different songs at random  c) silently
**Answer: a) the same song, starting one after another.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Lodoni Sa Kama is London's Burning in…
a) iTaukei  b) Hindi  c) Rotuman
**Answer: a) iTaukei.**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** The song in this lesson is…
a) London's Burning  b) the national anthem  c) a birthday song
**Answer: a) London's Burning.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Singing a round needs groups to…
a) listen and keep their own start  b) all start together  c) sing faster and faster only
**Answer: a) listen and keep their own start.**
