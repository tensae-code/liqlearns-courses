# Singing in Rounds: Canon — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is a canon?
**Answer: A round — the same song sung by groups starting one after another.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the iTaukei version of London's Burning.
**Answer: Lodoni Sa Kama.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Which song is sung as a round in this lesson?
**Answer: London's Burning.**
