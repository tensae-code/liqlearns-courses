# Singing in Rounds: Canon

## Do it first

Quick start: from the title alone, guess what this lesson's work is. Then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — London's Burning as a round

**London's Burning** is sung **as a round (canon)** — one group starts, the next group follows with the same song, so the song chases itself. (Teacher-guide evidence, Year 2.)

## Sub lesson 2 — Lodoni Sa Kama — the round in iTaukei

The same round is sung **in its iTaukei version, Lodoni Sa Kama** — the canon works in both languages, English and iTaukei.
