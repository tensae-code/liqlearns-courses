# Lesson plan — Scales and Modes of the World

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Music · **Lesson:** Scales and Modes of the World
**Place in the curriculum:** Music (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The scales and modes the world's musics are built from — European modes among them.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the course's earlier music lessons — recommended first, never forced.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The harmonic series and how cultures choose their notes** — the harmonic series and how cultures choose their notes; the pentatonic scale of China, Indonesia, Africa and Native America, built from chained fifths on the monochord
2. **India's ragas and China's pentatonic notes** — India's ragas, each mode tied to an hour, a season and a god, with no fixed tuning; China's pentatonic notes standing for prince, ministers, people, affairs and things
3. **The diatonic scale of Pythagoras and Guido** — the diatonic scale of Pythagoras and Guido, then the tempered chromatic scale: Bach's Well-Tempered Clavier and tuning to A = 440 Hz

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
