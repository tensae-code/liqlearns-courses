# Scales and Modes of the World

## Do it first

Look at the sub lesson list. Pick the part you already know something about, and say what you know. Then start at Sub lesson 1 — every part builds on the one before it.

## Sub lesson 1 — The harmonic series and how cultures choose their notes

The harmonic series and how cultures choose their notes; the pentatonic scale of China, Indonesia, Africa and Native America, built from chained fifths on the monochord (Folha 20 "Como fazer a cobra subir?", Brazil's *ARTE — Ensino Médio*, printed p. 324).

## Sub lesson 2 — India's ragas and China's pentatonic notes

India's ragas, each mode tied to an hour, a season and a god, with no fixed tuning; China's pentatonic notes standing for prince, ministers, people, affairs and things (Folha 20, Brazil's *ARTE — Ensino Médio*, printed p. 327).

## Sub lesson 3 — The diatonic scale of Pythagoras and Guido

The diatonic scale of Pythagoras and Guido, then the tempered chromatic scale: Bach's Well-Tempered Clavier and tuning to A = 440 Hz (Folha 20, Brazil's *ARTE — Ensino Médio*, printed p. 327).
