# Scales and Modes of the World — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In your own words, what does this part teach?
**Answer: The harmonic series and how cultures choose their notes; the pentatonic scale of China, Indonesia, Africa and Native America, built from chained fifths on the monochord**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In your own words, what does this part teach?
**Answer: India's ragas, each mode tied to an hour, a season and a god, with no fixed tuning; China's pentatonic notes standing for prince, ministers, people, affairs and things**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In your own words, what does this part teach?
**Answer: The diatonic scale of Pythagoras and Guido, then the tempered chromatic scale: Bach's Well-Tempered Clavier and tuning to A = 440 Hz**
