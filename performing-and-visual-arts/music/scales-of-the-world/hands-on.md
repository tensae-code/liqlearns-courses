# Hands-on — Scales and Modes of the World

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## The harmonic series and how cultures choose their notes
Do the practice this part names, with real materials and a partner: the harmonic series and how cultures choose their notes; the pentatonic scale of China, Indonesia, Africa and Native America, built from chained fifths on the monochord

## India's ragas and China's pentatonic notes
Do the practice this part names, with real materials and a partner: India's ragas, each mode tied to an hour, a season and a god, with no fixed tuning; China's pentatonic notes standing for prince, ministers, people, affairs and things

## The diatonic scale of Pythagoras and Guido
Do the practice this part names, with real materials and a partner: the diatonic scale of Pythagoras and Guido, then the tempered chromatic scale: Bach's Well-Tempered Clavier and tuning to A = 440 Hz
