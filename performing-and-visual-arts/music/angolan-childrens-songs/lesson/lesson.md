# Angolan Children's Songs

## Do it first

## Sub lesson 1 — Passeio à Huíla — a sung trip

"Passeio à Huíla" by Alice Berenguel — a sung trip to Huíla's farms and fruit, the Serra da Leba mountain road to Namibe and its waterfalls, sung with rhythmic body expression, standing and seated. The song is placed by summary only, as this course always places songs.
