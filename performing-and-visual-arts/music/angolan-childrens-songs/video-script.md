# Video script — Angolan Children's Songs

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

**Passeio à Huíla — a sung trip.** "Passeio à Huíla" by Alice Berenguel — a sung trip to Huíla's farms and fruit, the Serra da Leba mountain road to Namibe and its waterfalls, sung with rhythmic body expression, standing and seated. The song is placed by summary only, as this course always places songs.

That is Angolan Children's Songs — Angola's own books teach it this way.
