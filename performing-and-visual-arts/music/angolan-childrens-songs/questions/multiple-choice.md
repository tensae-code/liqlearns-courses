# Angolan Children's Songs — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson — Angolan Children's Songs — opens with…
a) Passeio à Huíla — a sung trip  b) an examination  c) nothing at all
**Answer: a) Passeio à Huíla — a sung trip.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The Angola book teaches this lesson as…
a) Angola's own children's songs, sung with the body  b) a sports rule  c) a cooking recipe
**Answer: a) Angola's own children's songs, sung with the body.**

**3.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) Passeio à Huíla — a sung trip  b) long division  c) map coordinates
**Answer: a) Passeio à Huíla — a sung trip.**

**4.** The parts of this lesson come from…
a) Angola's own Educação Manual e Plástica / Educação Musical books  b) a newspaper  c) a song chorus
**Answer: a) Angola's own books.**

**5.** "Passeio à Huíla — a sung trip" is taught in this lesson as…
a) one of its named parts  b) an unrelated game  c) a spelling list
**Answer: a) one of its named parts.**
