# Hands-on — Angolan Children's Songs

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Passeio à Huíla — a sung trip
Work through this part with real materials or your own body, the way the book sets it: "Passeio à Huíla" by Alice Berenguel — a sung trip to Huíla's farms and fruit, the Serra da Leba mountain road to Namibe and its waterfalls, sung with rhythmic body expression, standing and seated. The song is placed by summary only, as this course always places songs. Show a companion what you made or did, and say what the part teaches.
