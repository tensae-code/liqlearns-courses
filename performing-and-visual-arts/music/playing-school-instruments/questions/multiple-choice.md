# Playing School Instruments — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** On the recorder, the hand on top is…
a) the left hand  b) the right hand  c) either hand
**Answer: a) the left hand.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Recorder fingerings are charted in…
a) solfa  b) only pictures  c) numbers only
**Answer: a) solfa.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The ukulele chord chart includes…
a) C, F, G, G7, Am, D, D7, Dm, E  b) only C  c) no chords
**Answer: a) C, F, G, G7, Am, D, D7, Dm, E.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The three principal chords are…
a) I, IV and V (with V7)  b) I, II and III  c) only I
**Answer: a) I, IV and V (with V7).**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** In an anthem performance, rhythm is given to…
a) improvised percussion  b) the recorders  c) nobody
**Answer: a) improvised percussion.**
