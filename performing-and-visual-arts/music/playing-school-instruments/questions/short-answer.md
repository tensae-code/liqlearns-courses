# Playing School Instruments — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How is the recorder held?
**Answer: Left hand on top, thumb hole covered, double holes covered by one finger.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the keys whose principal chords are practised.
**Answer: C, G and F.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** How are parts allocated for the anthem performance?
**Answer: Melody to voices and recorders, chords to guitar/ukulele/piano/keyboard, rhythm to improvised percussion.**
