# Playing School Instruments

## Do it first

Quick start: from the title alone, guess what this lesson's work is. Then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — Recorder fingerings

Hold the **recorder** with the **left hand on top**; the **thumb hole** and the **double holes covered by one finger** are shown in a **full fingering chart in solfa**.

## Sub lesson 2 — Ukulele, guitar and keyboard chords

**Ukulele** tuning is set by the head pegs (strings **la/mi/do/so**) with a chord chart (**C, F, G, G7, Am, D, D7, Dm, E**); **guitar** tuning is **mi/ti/so/re/la/mi** with the same chord set; **keyboard** chord diagrams show each chord's pitch alphabets. For performance, the **three principal chords (I, IV, V, V7) plus the relative minor** are read from chord diagrams in the keys of **C, G and F**.

## Sub lesson 3 — Demonstrating and performing with your instrument

Learners **demonstrate their selected instrument** — a woodwind melody, or string/keyboard chord-harmony accompaniment to singing — and prepare an **anthem performance** by allocating the parts: melody to voices and recorders, chords to guitar, ukulele, piano or keyboard, rhythm to improvised percussion.
