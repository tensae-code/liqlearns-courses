This lesson is Music in Our Everyday Lives.

Music in Our Everyday Lives — its forms, history and cultural meaning, taught as one topic lesson. — as China's school books teach it: Foreign film music — how a film's beautiful, characterful music makes the film more vivid and moving, an irreplaceable part of the whole art; John Williams's Olympic Fanfare, written for Los Angeles 1984; the music card on sports music — music and sport both live by rhythm, and sports music is now a kind of music of its own.

Watch, listen — then say it back in your own words.
