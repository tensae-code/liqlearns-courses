# Lesson plan — Singing About Colours

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Music · **Lesson:** Singing About Colours
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 3 — Music (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the colour lessons sung back as songs: the colours song of Chapter 5, the rainbow that gathers the colours, and the primary colours song — yellow, blue and red, the mother and father of colours.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the colour lessons of the Visual Art unit — the learner can already name the colours; here they are carried by melody so they are not forgotten.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved (the gate: README, "Units, Lessons and Sub Lessons (ruled 2026-11-08)"):

1. **The colours song** — "ቀለማት" (Colours) — red strawberry, yellow banana, orange, rolling on green grass, sky-blue sky, seeking the pink rose: the colour lesson played through in song.
2. **The rainbow** — "ቀስተ ዳመና" (The Rainbow) — the rainbow as a gathering of colours seen together, green-yellow-red and more, a covenant in the cloud in its time.
3. **The primary colours song** — "መስራች ቀለማት" (Primary Colours) — yellow, blue and red named as the mother and father of colours.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the colour lessons of Visual Art (the primary colours; colours around us). Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Sing "ቀለማት" (Colours) and match its colours: red strawberry, yellow banana, orange, green grass, sky-blue sky, pink rose.
2. Sing "ቀስተ ዳመና" (The Rainbow) and say what a rainbow is in the song: a gathering of colours.
3. Sing "መስራች ቀለማት" (Primary Colours) and name the three primaries: yellow, blue, red.
4. Say why songs carry colours: a song is a way to know and remember them.

## What you need
Your voice. Real colour things help: a banana, an orange, a strawberry or red fruit, a look at the sky.

## If the learner is stuck
- Loses the words → go back to the call-and-answer shape: the teacher (or the audio) sings a short piece, the learner answers with the same piece. Short pieces first, whole song after.
- Loses the tune → hum the song together before singing words; the tune first, the words ride on it.
- Sings but cannot say what the song teaches → ask the lesson's own question: what is this song about, and what does it ask us to do? Every song in this unit teaches something that can be said in one sentence.
