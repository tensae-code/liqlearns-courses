# Singing About Colours — short answer

Answer in a few words, out loud or in writing.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the six colour-things of the colours song in order.
**Answer:** Red strawberry; yellow banana; orange; green grass; sky-blue sky; pink rose.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Why are yellow, blue and red called the mother and father of colours?
**Answer:** Because all the other colours come from them.
