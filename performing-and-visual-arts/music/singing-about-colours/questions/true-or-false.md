# Singing About Colours — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In the colours song, the sky is sky-blue.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The colours song's strawberry is yellow.
**Answer: False** — The strawberry is red; the banana is yellow.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The book gives the rainbow song as an example of a song teaching colours.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Green is one of the three primary colours.
**Answer: False** — Yellow, blue and red are the primaries.
