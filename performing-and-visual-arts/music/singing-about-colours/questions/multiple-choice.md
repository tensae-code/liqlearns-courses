# Singing About Colours — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In the colours song, the banana is —
a) red  b) yellow  c) sky-blue
**Answer: b) yellow**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The song's grass is —
a) green  b) pink  c) orange
**Answer: a) green** — Rolling on green grass.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The rose the singer seeks is —
a) red  b) white  c) pink
**Answer: c) pink**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The rainbow is sung as —
a) a river in the sky  b) a gathering of colours  c) a single colour
**Answer: b) a gathering of colours**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The rainbow appears —
a) in the cloud in its time  b) every night  c) under the ground
**Answer: a) in the cloud in its time**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** The three primary colours are —
a) green, orange, purple  b) yellow, blue, red  c) pink, sky-blue, brown
**Answer: b) yellow, blue, red**

**7. (Sub lesson 3)** The primary colours song calls the three —
a) the children of colours  b) the mother and father of colours  c) the kings of colours
**Answer: b) the mother and father of colours**
