# Video script — Singing About Colours

The super-simple voice, for the sing-along video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The songs are named and summarised, never transcribed; the script sings the learner TO the song, not the song itself.

---

Red strawberry! Yellow banana! Orange!
Roll on the green grass, under the sky-blue sky —
seek the pink rose. Sing the colours!

A rainbow! All the colours gathered together,
green, yellow, red and more — beautiful to the eye.

Yellow, blue, red — the mother and father of colours.
Three colours sung — and the world is in your hand.
