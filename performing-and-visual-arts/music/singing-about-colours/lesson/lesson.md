# Singing About Colours

## Do it first

Name the colours you can see from where you sit. Red, yellow, green, blue… You learned them by looking. This lesson sings them — because a colour you can sing is a colour you keep.

## Sub lesson 1 — The colours song

The song is **"ቀለማት"** — *Colours* — and it plays through the colours one by one, each riding on a thing:

- **red** — the strawberry
- **yellow** — the banana
- **orange** — the orange fruit
- rolling on **green** grass
- the **sky-blue** sky
- seeking the **pink** rose

It is the colour lesson of the colours chapter, sung back as a song. Sing it pointing: at something red, something yellow, the grass, the sky. The song is a pointing game with a melody.

## Sub lesson 2 — The rainbow

The song is **"ቀስተ ዳመና"** — *The Rainbow*.

[Picture 9463 — a rainbow arching over green hills]

The rainbow is sung as **a gathering of colours** — beautiful to the eye, **green-yellow-red and other colours seen together in it**, a covenant seen in the cloud in its time. The book gives this song as its example of one art teaching another: the arts help each other, and **a song is a way to know colours**.

Sing it slowly, and draw the bow in the air with your hand as you sing — down one side, up the other.

## Sub lesson 3 — The primary colours song

The last song is **"መስራች ቀለማት"** — *Primary Colours*.

It names **yellow, blue and red** — and it calls them **the mother and father of colours**: the three from whom all the other colours come. The book shows them first as balloons — yellow, blue, red — and as glasses of coloured liquid, then sings them.

Sing it holding up one finger for each primary: yellow — one; blue — two; red — three. Three colours, sung, and every colour in the world is in your hand.

## Say it to your companion

Sing the colours song to your companion, pointing at real things: something red, something yellow, the grass, the sky. Then name the three primary colours and say what the song calls them.

## Do It Yourself

1. Find the six things of the colours song around your home: a red thing, a yellow thing, an orange thing, grass, the sky, a pink flower. Sing the song to them.
2. After rain, look for a rainbow. Name every colour you can see gathered in it.
3. Draw three balloons — yellow, blue, red — and sing the primary colours song over your drawing.
