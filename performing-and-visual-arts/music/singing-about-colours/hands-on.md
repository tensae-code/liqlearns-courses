# Hands-on — Singing About Colours

The lesson with your hands, your feet and your voice together, not only your eyes.

## Point-and-sing colour hunt
In the schoolyard, sing the colours song slowly. At each colour, the whole class must point at a real thing of that colour before the next line. No pointing, no next line.

## Rainbow in the air
Sing The Rainbow while tracing a huge bow in the air with both arms, naming a colour at each part of the arc. On a wet day, finish by scanning the real sky together.
