# Music of Colombia's Regions — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Pacific ensemble's most representative instrument is the…
a) marimba de chonta  b) arpa llanera  c) guacharaca
**Answer: a) marimba de chonta**.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The Caribbean bands play…
a) porro, puyas and bullerengues  b) only joropo  c) only rock
**Answer: a) porro, puyas and bullerengues**.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The guacharaca is…
a) bird and instrument in one  b) a kind of drum skin  c) a flute
**Answer: a) bird and instrument in one**.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** In the Pacific ensembles, the cununo accompanies the…
a) currulao  b) pasaje llanero  c) porro
**Answer: a) currulao**.

**5. (Sub lesson 4)** The base of all Orinoquía rhythms is the…
a) joropo  b) currulao  c) bullerengue
**Answer: a) joropo**.

**6. (Sub lesson 5)** Jorge Veloza is presented as a master of…
a) the Andean region's music  b) Amazonian music  c) Pacific marimba
**Answer: a) the Andean region's music**.
