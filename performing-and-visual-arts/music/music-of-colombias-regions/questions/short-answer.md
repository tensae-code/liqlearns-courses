# Music of Colombia's Regions — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the three instruments of the llanero ensemble.
**Answer: Arpa llanera (harp), cuatro and capachos.**

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** How does the older book teach the marimba de chonta?
**Answer: A word-rhythm ostinato — each line the pulse — is learned by the whole group, and one student improvises a melody over it on the marimba.**

**3. (Sub lesson 4)** What is the pasaje llanero?
**Answer: A slow joropo where singer and harp alternate.**

**4. (Sub lesson 5)** How do the Eastern Plains' indigenous communities' musics stand beside llanera music?
**Answer: They keep their own musical expressions, different from llanera music.**
