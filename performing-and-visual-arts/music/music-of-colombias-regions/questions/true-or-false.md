# Music of Colombia's Regions — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Rock's arrival in Colombia is part of the ensembles story.
**Answer: True** — the guide names it among the ensembles of instruments and voice.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The cantaoras belong to the Pacific region in these guides.
**Answer: False** — they are the Caribbean (Atlántico) region's singers.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The arpa llanera developed from the European harp.
**Answer: True** — the guide says so.

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** The Cauca ensemble is built on transverse flutes.
**Answer: True** — the guide names it so.
