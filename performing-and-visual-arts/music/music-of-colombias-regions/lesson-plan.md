# Lesson plan — Music of Colombia's Regions

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Music · **Lesson:** Music of Colombia's Regions
**Place in the curriculum:** Music (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Colombia heard region by region: its own ensembles — vallenato, gaitas, marimba de chonta, arpa llanera — and the musics of the Caribbean, the Pacific, the Orinoquía and the Andes.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this course in the same title.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Ensembles of Colombia** — Colombia's own ensembles, named one by one: the **conjunto vallenato**, the **conjunto de gaitas**, and the **Pacific ensemble whose most representative instrument is the marimba de chonta**; then ensembles of instruments and voice, **rock's arrival in Colombia**, the **llanero ensemble of arpa llanera, cuatro and capachos**, the **Cauca ensemble of transverse flutes**, and **indigenous Colombian ensembles**.
2. **The Caribbean — bands, cantaoras and drums** — The Caribbean (Atlántico) region: its **chibcha, arawak and caribe roots**; the **bands of European wind and percussion instruments that play porro, puyas and bullerengues**; the **cantaoras** and their cultural practice; **"La Gota Fría"**; and the **guacharaca, bird and instrument in one**.
3. **The Pacific — marimba de chonta and currulao** — The Pacific's most representative instrument is the **marimba de chonta**.
4. **Orinoquía — joropo, arpa, cuatro and capachos** — The llanos: the **joropo is the base of all its rhythms**, danced by a couple; the **pasaje llanero is a slow joropo where singer and harp alternate**; the **arpa, melodic instrument par excellence, developed from the European harp**; the **cuatro** that accompanies it; and the ensemble of **arpa, cuatro and capachos**.
5. **The Andean region and Jorge Veloza** — The music guide finds the **Andean region on the map and hears it — its musical rhythms, instrumentation, songs and composers** — and meets its popular master **Jorge Veloza, exponent of the region's music**, met again among the percussion instruments.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
