# Hands-on — Music of Colombia's Regions

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Keep the Pacific pulse
In a group, speak a word-rhythm ostinato together — each line is the pulse — while one person improvises a simple melody over it with voice or hands, as the guide directs.

## Match the ensemble
Cards or drawings: match each ensemble of this lesson to its region and its instruments — vallenato, gaitas, Pacific, llanero, Cauca, Andean.
