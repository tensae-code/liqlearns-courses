# Video script — Music of Colombia's Regions

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

One country. Many musics.

The **conjunto vallenato**. The **conjunto de gaitas**. The Pacific ensemble and its **marimba de chonta**.

The Caribbean: bands playing **porro, puyas and bullerengues**, and the **cantaoras** singing.
The Pacific: the **cununo** under the **currulao**.
The plains: **joropo** — **arpa, cuatro and capachos**.
The Andes: its rhythms and songs, and **Jorge Veloza**, its popular master.

Hear Colombia region by region.
