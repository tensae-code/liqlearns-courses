# Video script — Kazakh Singer-Poets and Folk Song

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is Kazakh Singer-Poets and Folk Song.

Kazakh Singer-Poets and Folk Song — Kazakhstan's singing poets — the sal and seri, zhyrau and akyn, Aset and Danesh, and the bard's author's song — and the folk-song schools they carried. — as Kazakhstan's school books teach it: zhyrau and akyn: the zhyrau served the state with counsel and foresight, the akyn sang everyday life at family feasts; Zhayau Musa Baizhanuly and his song «Ақ сиса» ("White Calico"), which mocks a rich man's foul deeds — a white cloth washes clean, a soul's dirt does not.

Watch, listen — then say it back in your own words.
