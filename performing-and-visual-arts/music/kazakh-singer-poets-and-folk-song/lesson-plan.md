# Lesson plan — Kazakh Singer-Poets and Folk Song

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Music · **Lesson:** Kazakh Singer-Poets and Folk Song
**Place in the curriculum:** Music (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Kazakhstan's singing poets — the sal and seri, zhyrau and akyn, Aset and Danesh, and the bard's author's song — and the folk-song schools they carried..
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this course in the same title.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Kazakhstan Grade 4 Music (Gorchakova)** — zhyrau and akyn: the zhyrau served the state with counsel and foresight, the akyn sang everyday life at family feasts; Zhayau Musa Baizhanuly and his song «Ақ сиса» ("White Calico"), which mocks a rich man's foul deeds — a white cloth washes clean, a soul's dirt does not; sal and seri, the singer-poets of the steppe who taught youth its etiquette: Birzhan-sal, famous for his aitys with Sara, and Akhan-seri, hunter and horseman, whose song «Манмангер» mourns his beloved horse Kulager; the author's (bard) song, where one person is poet, composer and performer: Okudzhava, Vizbor, Vysotsky, Yakusheva, the Nikitins, Berkovsky, Sukhanov; listening to Krylatov's «Прекрасное далёко»
2. **Kazakhstan Grade 4 Music (Kulmanova)** — the zhyrau: maker and performer of zhyr, teacher, counsellor, akyn and orator in one; Bukar zhyrau Kalkamanuly (1693–1781), Abylai Khan's counsellor; his terme «Биік тауға жарасар» performed in a group from notation; Estai Berkimbaiuly (1874–1946), akyn-singer and composer, a sal-seri heir; the story in brief of his song «Құсни-Қорлан», composed for two sisters he met at a wedding feast; Zhayau Musa Baizhanuly (1835–1929), who played dombra, syrnai, violin and tatar songs and wrote «Ақ сиса» and «Хаулау»; the song «Ақ сиса» learned; the contest-game «Мен сыныппын»: two teams compare the versions of «Ақ сиса» — R. Stamgaziev's singers, a symphony orchestra, the Dos-Mukasan ensemble — and name each style
3. **Kazakhstan Grade 5 Music (Kulmanova)** — the folk song «Бір бала»: what a song and a genre are; the 2/4 metre sign; the 11-syllable Kazakh "white verse" (қазақтық қара өлең) with its melodic exclamations; the first line sung from notation, then in two voices; Birzhan Kozhagululy (1834–1897), the great sal of Arka: his majestic song «Айтбай» praising Kokshetau's lakes and pines, heard and sung; an excerpt of S. Seifullin's poem «Кокшетау» read over its refrain; Birzhan's school — Akhan seri, Estai, Ybyrai, Baluan Sholak; Kenen Azerbaev (1884–1976) of Semirechye and his comic song «Кокшолак», in summary: teased for his poor old tailless horse, the young akyn answers the mocking girls in song instead of taking offence; performed as a two-group contest; the regional singing schools — Arka's spacious songs, melodic Semirechye, the West and Syrdarya; class projects: "Traditional Song Art" — present a singing school of Arka, Semirechye or the West and perform its song; or a portrait project on Kenen Azerbaev, founder of Semirechye's performing school
4. **Kazakhstan Grade 6 Music (Musakhajaeva)** — what it teaches: the oral-professional singers — Birzhan sal, founder of the Arka school, and his protest and farewell songs («Темиртас»); Mukhit Meralyuly's high, wide-ranging songs («Зауреш», «Кіші айдай» from the horseman legend); Kenen Azerbaev of Zhetisu, author of some 150 songs («Туған жер», «Базар-Назар»); what it teaches: a Kazakh folk song about the homeland's pastures and a gosling flown from the nest; pupils research the song's history and sort it among historical, lyrical, ritual and everyday songs; what it teaches: a Kazakh folk song of life's brevity — people are guests to one another in a short world, and youth fades like a tulip; what it teaches: two beloved singers — Aset Naimanbaev, Abay's philosophical heir in the Arka singing tradition, master of aitys, author of «Інжу-маржан»; Danesh Rakishev, «nightingale of Zhetysu», whose lyrical songs («Жетісу», «Балжан-ай») passed from mouth to mouth
5. **Kazakhstan Grade 7 NIS Art (Akhmetkalieva)** — what it teaches: perform Abai Kunanbaev's song «Көзімнің қарасы» — what methods can be used to help the performance?; after the song is performed as solo, perform it in different line-ups (дует, трио, квартет — duet, trio, quartet); discuss with classmates and evaluate your performance; group and individual performance tasks: discuss with the group the performance's peculiarities; perform the song «Көзімнің қарасы» by group performance; how can the song's interpretation be strengthened through group performance?; invent a melody with your own voice or instrument and perform it — listen to your classmates' opinions; find a rhythmic accompaniment (ырғақтық сүйемел) for your melody and perform it together with the musical composition; discuss the questions with classmates: what rhythmic accompaniment suits the melody, how does the rhythmic accompaniment change the melody, how much does the rhythmic accompaniment help the melody's performance?; perform your melody in a different way and think how the manner of presentation can be changed; prepare the project of your favourite song — research the ways of presenting this work's musical interpretation; invent your own rhythmic accompaniment
