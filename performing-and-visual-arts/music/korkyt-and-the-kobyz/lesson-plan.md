# Lesson plan — Korkyt and the Kobyz

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Music · **Lesson:** Korkyt and the Kobyz
**Place in the curriculum:** Music (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The legend of Korkyt, father of the kobyz, and the ancient sounds of Kazakhstan's bowed kobyz..
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this course in the same title.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Kazakhstan Grade 2 Valiullina Music, contents pp. 141-142** — the Kazakh bowed instrument of Korkyt, its sound and legend
2. **Kazakhstan Grade 2 Kulmanova Music, contents pp. 142-143** — Korkyt, father of kui, and the kobyz he made
3. **Kazakhstan Grade 2 Orazalieva Music** — the father of kui" (Lesson 7 in the book's section numbering; 34-lesson list) — Korkyt who made the kobyz and the first kui
4. **Kazakhstan Grade 4 Music (Kulmanova)** — the kobyz, the ancient bowed instrument bound to Korkyt ata's name (his heritage entered UNESCO's list in 2018); Ykylas Dukenuly (1843–1916), the great kobyzshi who carried the art to our time; an excerpt from I. Zhakanov's novel «Ықылас» in summary — the boy Ykylas secretly makes his own kobyz, choosing the wood, the skin and the horsehair; Ykylas's kui «Жезкиік» heard in two versions, solo kobyz and orchestra; an excerpt played in the la mode following the printed rhythm
