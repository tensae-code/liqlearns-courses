# Playing a Simple Melody (Papua New Guinea) — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — Sight-reading a melody
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: The Grade 8 guided lessons build fluency: sight-reading a melody line, and sight-reading with sol-fa — Do, Re, Mi — until a simple melody and rhythm on standard notation can be read at sight.

## Sub lesson 2 — Song, expression and dynamics
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: The PNG National Anthem is sung in two-part harmony with tuned and untuned accompaniment — guitar, kundu drum, bamboo drum — working on song and expression and song and dynamics, loud and soft.

## Sub lesson 3 — Hymns in three and four parts, tunes from memory
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: A familiar hymn is sung in three- and four-part harmony — soprano, alto, tenor, bass; familiar tunes are performed from memory and from notation independently; and local and Western wind instruments are named, with how each makes its sound.
