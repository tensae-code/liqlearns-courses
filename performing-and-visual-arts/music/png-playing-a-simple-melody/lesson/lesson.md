# Playing a Simple Melody (Papua New Guinea)

## Do it first

Look at the sub lesson list. Pick the part you already know something about, and say what you know. Then start at Sub lesson 1 — every part builds on the one before it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Sight-reading a melody

The Grade 8 guided lessons build fluency: sight-reading a melody line, and sight-reading with sol-fa — Do, Re, Mi — until a simple melody and rhythm on standard notation can be read at sight.

## Sub lesson 2 — Song, expression and dynamics

The PNG National Anthem is sung in two-part harmony with tuned and untuned accompaniment — guitar, kundu drum, bamboo drum — working on song and expression and song and dynamics, loud and soft.

## Sub lesson 3 — Hymns in three and four parts, tunes from memory

A familiar hymn is sung in three- and four-part harmony — soprano, alto, tenor, bass; familiar tunes are performed from memory and from notation independently; and local and Western wind instruments are named, with how each makes its sound.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion this lesson's one thing in your own words, naming each sub lesson as you go. Companion checks: did every part get named?

## Do It Yourself

1. Do each sub lesson's practice once more, on your own.
2. Teach one part to someone else, the way this lesson taught it to you.
3. Write one sentence: what is this lesson's one thing?
