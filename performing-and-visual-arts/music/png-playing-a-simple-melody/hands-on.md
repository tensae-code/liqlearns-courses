# Hands-on — Playing a Simple Melody (Papua New Guinea)

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Sight-reading a melody
Do the practice this part names, with real materials and a partner: The Grade 8 guided lessons build fluency: sight-reading a melody line, and sight-reading with sol-fa — Do, Re, Mi — until a simple melody and rhythm on standard notation can be read at sight.

## Song, expression and dynamics
Do the practice this part names, with real materials and a partner: The PNG National Anthem is sung in two-part harmony with tuned and untuned accompaniment — guitar, kundu drum, bamboo drum — working on song and expression and song and dynamics, loud and soft.

## Hymns in three and four parts, tunes from memory
Do the practice this part names, with real materials and a partner: A familiar hymn is sung in three- and four-part harmony — soprano, alto, tenor, bass; familiar tunes are performed from memory and from notation independently; and local and Western wind instruments are named, with how each makes its sound.
