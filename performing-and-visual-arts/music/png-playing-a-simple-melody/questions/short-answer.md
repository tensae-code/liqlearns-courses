# Playing a Simple Melody (Papua New Guinea) — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What two ways are familiar tunes performed?
**Answer: From memory and from notation, independently.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What two 'song and…' studies does the guide name?
**Answer: Song and expression, and song and dynamics (loud and soft).**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What must a learner name about wind instruments?
**Answer: Local and Western wind instruments, and how each makes its sound.**
