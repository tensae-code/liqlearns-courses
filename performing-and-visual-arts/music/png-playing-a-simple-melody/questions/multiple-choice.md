# Playing a Simple Melody (Papua New Guinea) — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Sight-reading with sol-fa uses…
a) Do, Re, Mi  b) random shouts
**Answer: a) Do, Re, Mi.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The National Anthem is sung in…
a) two-part harmony  b) unison only, always
**Answer: a) two-part harmony.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Four-part harmony names…
a) soprano, alto, tenor, bass  b) four drummers
**Answer: a) soprano, alto, tenor, bass.**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** The accompaniment named includes…
a) guitar, kundu drum and bamboo drum  b) only piano
**Answer: a) guitar, kundu drum and bamboo drum.**
