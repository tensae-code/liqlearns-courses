# Video script — Playing a Simple Melody (Papua New Guinea)

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

**Sight-reading a melody.** The Grade 8 guided lessons build fluency: sight-reading a melody line, and sight-reading with sol-fa — Do, Re, Mi — until a simple melody and rhythm on standard notation can be read at sight.

**Song, expression and dynamics.** The PNG National Anthem is sung in two-part harmony with tuned and untuned accompaniment — guitar, kundu drum, bamboo drum — working on song and expression and song and dynamics, loud and soft.

**Hymns in three and four parts, tunes from memory.** A familiar hymn is sung in three- and four-part harmony — soprano, alto, tenor, bass; familiar tunes are performed from memory and from notation independently; and local and Western wind instruments are named, with how each makes its sound.
