# Lao Composers

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about lao composers. Then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — Music G9 teacher guide — lao composer suban souvannavong

The life of composer Suban Souvannavong (born 1942, Luang Prabang), his path into music from childhood, and listening to his best-known song "Dao Lom Deuan"

## Sub lesson 2 — Music G6 teacher guide — lao composer utama chulamani

Utama Chulamani (1917–1984) of Vientiane, self-taught musician and bandleader, and listening to his song "Champa Muang Lao" (Dok Champa of Laos, 1940)

## Sub lesson 3 — Music G11 teacher guide — lao composer bouangeun saphouvong

The life of Bouangeun Saphouvong (born 1949, Phonhong, Vientiane province), his schooling in Laos and the USSR and his work for Lao music education, and listening to his song "Jot Jam Boun Khoun Khong Phak" (Remembering the Party's Merit)

## Sub lesson 4 — Music G7 teacher guide — lao composer sisana sisane

The life of Sisana Sisane (1922–1998, born in Savannakhet), his French schooling, his thirty-plus songs for the revolution, and listening to his "Daen Haeng Itsaraphap" (Land of Freedom, 1967)

## Sub lesson 5 — Music G8 teacher guide, 2012 — lao composer parith sichanon

His life and work (portrait), and singing his song "Praise for the Air Defence"
