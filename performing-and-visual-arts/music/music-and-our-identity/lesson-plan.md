# Lesson plan — Music and Our Identity

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Music · **Lesson:** Music and Our Identity
**Place in the curriculum:** Music (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Music and Our Identity — Syria's own teaching of this topic, kept as named parts: discussing with friends music's effect on feelings and sensations; the song (words: Firas Habib) — music, the language of all existence, in the scale of degrees
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the course's earlier lessons in its title; recommended first, never forced.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of ..." in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Lesson 1 "The World of Music"** — discussing with friends music's effect on feelings and sensations; the song (words: Firas Habib) — music, the language of all existence, in the scale of degrees
2. **My Project (research)** — forming research groups with friends on pictures and information about one of two subjects — the life of a musician, or music and the Syrian civilisation; and preparing a dictionary of all the musical terms we can obtain

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
