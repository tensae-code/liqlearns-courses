# Video script — Music and Our Identity

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This is Syria's own lesson.

**Lesson 1 "The World of Music".** discussing with friends music's effect on feelings and sensations; the song (words: Firas Habib) — music, the language of all existence, in the scale of degrees.

**My Project (research).** forming research groups with friends on pictures and information about one of two subjects — the life of a musician, or music and the Syrian civilisation; and preparing a dictionary of all the musical terms we can obtain.

That is Music and Our Identity — Syria's way, kept by name.
