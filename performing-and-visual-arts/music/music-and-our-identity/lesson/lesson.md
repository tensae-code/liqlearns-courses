# Music and Our Identity

## Do it first

Name one thing that belongs in this lesson — Music and Our Identity — Syria's own teaching of this topic, kept as named parts: discussing with friends music's effect on feelings and sensations; the song (words: Firas Habib) — music, the language of all existence, in the scale of degrees. Say what it is in your own words. If you can name one, you are already inside the lesson.

## Sub lesson 1 — Lesson 1 "The World of Music"

discussing with friends music's effect on feelings and sensations; the song (words: Firas Habib) — music, the language of all existence, in the scale of degrees.

## Sub lesson 2 — My Project (research)

forming research groups with friends on pictures and information about one of two subjects — the life of a musician, or music and the Syrian civilisation; and preparing a dictionary of all the musical terms we can obtain.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson holds, part by part, in your own words. Companion checks: did every part get named, with its Syrian name kept where the book gives one?

## Do It Yourself

1. Choose one named part of this lesson and find out one more true thing about it — from a person who knows it, or from the book your part came from.
2. Make or perform a small piece in the way of that part: a rhythm, a step, a drawing, a shaped piece of clay.
3. Write one sentence: what does this part show about Syria's own way of teaching its arts?
