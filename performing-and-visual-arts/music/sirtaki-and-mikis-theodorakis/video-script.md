This lesson is Sirtaki and Mikis Theodorakis.

Sirtaki and Mikis Theodorakis — The sirtaki and its composer Mikis Theodorakis, in Kazakhstan's book of human values. — as Kazakhstan's school books teach it: Человек! — Mikis Theodorakis and "Sirtaki": a dance the composer built from the Greek syrtos for the film "Zorba the Greek", now a symbol of Greece, compared with Kazakh mass dances.

Watch, listen — then say it back in your own words.
