# Hands-on — Playing Notes and Rests

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Relay — ቅብብሎሽ
In a line or circle: the first player plays a whole note on a table-drum; the next answers with a whole rest, hands marking silent beats; pass it on. If the count breaks, start the line again — kindly.

## Write it, play it
Each player writes a short line of four signs on paper — notes and rests mixed. Swap papers. Play the line you received, on a book with a stick, while its writer checks every value.

## Movement values
Match movements to values, as the book's exercise does: a whole note — one long stretch held for 4; a half note — two steps; a quarter note — one quick clap. A caller names signs; the group moves them.
