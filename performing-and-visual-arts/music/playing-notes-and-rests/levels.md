# Playing Notes and Rests — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — Playing whole notes and whole rests
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: perform a whole note (4 sounded beats) and a whole rest (4 silent beats) on a beat instrument, and hand a mixed line over in relay without losing the count.

## Sub lesson 2 — Playing half notes and their mixes
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: perform half notes (2 beats each; two fill a whole note's time) mixed with whole rests, in a group, to a local beat instrument.

## Sub lesson 3 — Playing quarter notes and mixing everything
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: perform quarter notes and rests (1 beat each) inside full mixed lines, for classmates, with the count unbroken.
