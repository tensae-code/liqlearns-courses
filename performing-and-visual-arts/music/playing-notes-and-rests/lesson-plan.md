# Lesson plan — Playing Notes and Rests

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Music · **Lesson:** Playing Notes and Rests
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 3 — Music (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** to PERFORM the notes and rests — whole, half and quarter, sounded and silent — on beat instruments and in relay, reading a written line and keeping the count through sound and silence alike.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Notes and Rests — every sign and value must already be known — and on Beat Instruments, whose drums do the playing.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Playing whole notes and whole rests** — four sounded beats, four silent beats, played on a beat instrument and handed over in relay (ቅብብሎሽ).
2. **Playing half notes and their mixes** — two-beat notes filling the whole note's time; half notes mixed with whole rests, in groups, to local beat instruments.
3. **Playing quarter notes and mixing everything** — one-beat notes and rests; movements matched to values; full mixed lines performed for classmates.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Notes and Rests; Beat Instruments. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Read a written line of notes and rests and play it on a beat instrument, keeping the count.
2. Play whole notes and whole rests in relay with classmates.
3. Mix half and quarter notes and rests in one line and perform it for others.

## What you need
Any beat instrument — a drum if there is one; a table, a book and a stick serve. Paper and pencil for writing lines.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — clap 4 beats sounding, then 4 beats in silence with hands still moving: play a whole note and a whole rest with no instrument at all (lesson/lesson.md).
2. **The explanation** — the practice in the books' order: wholes, halves, quarters, then everything mixed (lesson/lesson.md).

## If the learner is stuck
- Plays through a rest, filling the silence → the rest is part of the music: hands mark the beats, no sound. Practise the silent line alone first.
- Rushes the quarter notes → quarters are one beat each, no faster than the count: the beat is the ruler for every sign.
