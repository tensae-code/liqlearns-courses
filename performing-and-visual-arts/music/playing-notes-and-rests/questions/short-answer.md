# Playing Notes and Rests — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** On what kind of instruments are the notes played in this lesson?
**Answer: On beat (percussion) instruments — drums and the area's own beat instruments.**

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Write a performed line: how many quarter notes fill one whole note's time?
**Answer: Four quarter notes.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A line goes: half note, whole rest, half note. How many beats in all?
**Answer: 2 + 4 + 2 = 8 beats.**
