# Playing Notes and Rests — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A whole note played on a drum sounds for…
a) 1 beat  b) 2 beats  c) 4 beats
**Answer: c) 4 beats.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In relay playing (ቅብብሎሽ), the players…
a) all play at once  b) hand the music over, one answering the next  c) stop counting between turns
**Answer: b) hand the music over, one answering the next.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Two half notes fill the time of…
a) one quarter note  b) one whole note  c) two whole rests
**Answer: b) one whole note.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The book asks for half notes to be played with rests in groups, accompanied by…
a) local beat instruments  b) a piano only  c) no instruments
**Answer: a) local beat instruments.**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** A quarter note played on a drum lasts…
a) 4 beats  b) 2 beats  c) 1 beat
**Answer: c) 1 beat.**
