# Video script — Playing Notes and Rests

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Now we PLAY the signs.

Whole note on the drum: boom — 2, 3, 4. Hold it!
Whole rest: hands up, count in silence — 1, 2, 3, 4.

Two half notes: boom, boom — each one two.
Four quarters: tap, tap, tap, tap — each one one.

Relay! I play, you answer. Sound hands over to silence.
The count never stops. Play!
