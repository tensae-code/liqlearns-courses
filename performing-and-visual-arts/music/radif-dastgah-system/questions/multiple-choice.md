# The Radif and the Dastgah System — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** The radif is…
a) the ordered repertoire of melodic models of Iranian music  b) a drum rhythm  c) a stage play
**Answer: a) the ordered repertoire of melodic models of Iranian music**

**2.** A dastgah is…
a) one of the modal systems the radif is organised in  b) a kind of paper  c) a dance step
**Answer: a) one of the modal systems the radif is organised in**
