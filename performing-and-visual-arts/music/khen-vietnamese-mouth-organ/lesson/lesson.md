## Sub lesson 1 — Vietnam Grade 6 Âm nhạc 6

The khèn — a wind instrument long present in Vietnam, with many kinds and names (kềnh, đinh năm, ma nhí, khèn bè…): several tubes fitted with reeds (lưỡi lam) are joined together through a resonating body, and when the player blows, the air passing the reeds makes the sound; the khèn plays solo, in ensembles and accompanying singing or dancing, and is often used in the community life of the Mông and Thái peoples
