**Place in the curriculum:** Music (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The khèn, the free-reed mouth organ of Vietnam's highland peoples.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this course in the same title.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Vietnam Grade 6 Âm nhạc 6** — The khèn — a wind instrument long present in Vietnam, with many kinds and names (kềnh, đinh năm, ma nhí, khèn bè…): several tubes fitted with reeds (lưỡi lam) are joined together through a resonating body, and when the player blows, the air passing the reeds makes the sound; the khèn plays solo, in ensembles and accompanying singing or dancing, and is often used in the community life of the Mông and Thái peoples
