This lesson is Khèn: The Vietnamese Mouth Organ.

The khèn, the free-reed mouth organ of Vietnam's highland peoples. — as Vietnam's school books teach it: The khèn — a wind instrument long present in Vietnam, with many kinds and names (kềnh, đinh năm, ma nhí, khèn bè…): several tubes fitted with reeds (lưỡi lam) are joined together through a resonating body, and when the player blows, the air passing the reeds makes the sound; the khèn plays solo, in ensembles and accompanying singing or dancing, and is often used in the community life of the Mông and Thái peoples.

Watch, listen — then say it back in your own words.
