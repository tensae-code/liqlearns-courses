# Video script — Musical Textures

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Musical texture — how the layers of a piece are woven together: monodia (one melodic line), homofonía (a line with accompaniment) and polifonía, heard in pregones, canons and listenings and embodied with the body.
