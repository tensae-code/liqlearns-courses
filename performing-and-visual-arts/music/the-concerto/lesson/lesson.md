# The Concerto

## Do it first

Before you read: say or sketch what you already know about this lesson's subject — the concerto — a show and a great work where orchestra and soloist compete with each other. Start there; the lesson builds on it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Soloist against orchestra

The Grade 2 book journeys to the land **Concert**: a **concerto** is both a show and a great work where the **orchestra and the soloist compete with each other** — one player against the whole band, answering, chasing, outshining.

- **Vivaldi's Violin Concerto "Spring"** (first movement) — and the nature pictures in it.
- **Padkavyrau's Violin Concerto finale** — built on a song the children already know.
- **Kabalevsky's Piano Concerto No. 3** (second movement).

## Say it to your companion
