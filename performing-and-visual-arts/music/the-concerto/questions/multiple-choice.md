# The Concerto — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In a concerto, the soloist and the orchestra…
a) never play together  b) compete with each other  c) only sing
**Answer: b) compete with each other** — one player against the whole band.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Vivaldi's concerto heard in the book is called…
a) "Winter"  b) "Spring"  c) "Autumn"
**Answer: b) "Spring"** — (first movement).

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Padkavyrau's concerto finale is built on…
a) a song the children know  b) a march  c) silence
**Answer: a) a song the children know** — a song the children already know.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Kabalevsky's concerto in the book is for…
a) violin  b) piano  c) flute
**Answer: b) piano** — Piano Concerto No. 3, second movement.

**5. (Sub lesson 1)** A concerto is both a show and…
a) a dance  b) a great work  c) a game
**Answer: b) a great work** — .
