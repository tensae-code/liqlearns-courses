# The Concerto — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A concerto has one soloist playing against the orchestra.
**Answer: True**.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In a concerto the orchestra stays silent throughout.
**Answer: False** — orchestra and soloist answer and chase each other.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Vivaldi's "Spring" carries nature pictures in its music.
**Answer: True**.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Padkavyrau is a Belarusian composer named in this lesson.
**Answer: True**.
