# The Concerto — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is a concerto, in the book's words?
**Answer: A show and a great work where orchestra and soloist compete with each other.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Name two concertos the book listens to.
**Answer: Any two of: Vivaldi's Violin Concerto "Spring"; Padkavyrau's Violin Concerto (finale); Kabalevsky's Piano Concerto No. 3.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** What pictures does Vivaldi's "Spring" paint?
**Answer: Nature pictures — spring itself, heard in the first movement.**
