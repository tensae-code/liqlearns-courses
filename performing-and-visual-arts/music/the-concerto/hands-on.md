# Hands-on — The Concerto

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## One against many
One learner claps a short rhythm — the soloist. The whole class answers with a different rhythm — the orchestra. The soloist tries to outshine; the orchestra answers louder. Swap soloists. That contest is the concerto's game.

## Spring pictures
While listening (or imagining) Vivaldi's "Spring", draw what the music pictures: birds, streams, breezes — the nature pictures the book names.
