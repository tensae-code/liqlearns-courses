The Seven Regional Kui Schools — Kazakhstan's kui tradition — the seven regional schools of oral-professional instrumental playing. — as Kazakhstan's school books teach it: what it teaches: the 19th-century golden age of the kui and its seven regional performance schools — Shygys, Arka, Karatau, Zhetisu, Syr boiy, Batys and Mangistau — kui played on dombra, kobyz and sybyzgy.
