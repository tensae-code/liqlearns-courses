# Lesson plan — Music Professions and Institutions

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Music · **Lesson:** Music Professions and Institutions
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 3 — Music (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The world of music work — the professions, concert halls and academic music institutions, and a journey through them.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the course's earlier lessons in its title; recommended first, never forced.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of ..." in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Instituții de muzică academică** — Unit 3, Instituții de muzică academică — the National Opera and Ballet Theatre "Maria Bieșu" in Chișinău and its international festival of opera and ballet stars.
2. **Instituții de muzică academică (2)** — Unit 3, Instituții de muzică academică — the National Philharmonic "Serghei Lunchevici": its symphony orchestra of 80 players and the Academic Choral Chapel "Doina".
3. **Sala cu Orgă** — Unit 3, Sala cu Orgă — Chișinău's Organ Hall and the organ, king of instruments, with some 4,000 pipes, played by Anna Strezeva.
4. **Palatul Național "Nicolae Sulac"** — Unit 4, Palatul Național "Nicolae Sulac" — the country's biggest culture palace, home of the "Mărțișor" festival and of the "Lăutarii", "Fluieraș" and "Noroc" ensembles.
