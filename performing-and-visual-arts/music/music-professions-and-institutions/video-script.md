The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

This is Moldova's own lesson.

**Instituții de muzică academică.** Unit 3, Instituții de muzică academică — the National Opera and Ballet Theatre "Maria Bieșu" in Chișinău and its international festival of opera and ballet stars.

**Instituții de muzică academică (2).** Unit 3, Instituții de muzică academică — the National Philharmonic "Serghei Lunchevici": its symphony orchestra of 80 players and the Academic Choral Chapel "Doina".

**Sala cu Orgă.** Unit 3, Sala cu Orgă — Chișinău's Organ Hall and the organ, king of instruments, with some 4,000 pipes, played by Anna Strezeva.

**Palatul Național "Nicolae Sulac".** Unit 4, Palatul Național "Nicolae Sulac" — the country's biggest culture palace, home of the "Mărțișor" festival and of the "Lăutarii", "Fluieraș" and "Noroc" ensembles.

That is Music Professions and Institutions — Moldova's way, kept by name.
