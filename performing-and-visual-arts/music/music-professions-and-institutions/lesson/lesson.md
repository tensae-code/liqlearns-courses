# Music Professions and Institutions

## Do it first

Name one thing that belongs in this lesson — The world of music work — the professions, concert halls and academic music institutions, and a journey through them. Say what it is in your own words. If you can name one, you are already inside the lesson.

## Sub lesson 1 — Instituții de muzică academică

Unit 3, Instituții de muzică academică — the National Opera and Ballet Theatre "Maria Bieșu" in Chișinău and its international festival of opera and ballet stars.

## Sub lesson 2 — Instituții de muzică academică (2)

Unit 3, Instituții de muzică academică — the National Philharmonic "Serghei Lunchevici": its symphony orchestra of 80 players and the Academic Choral Chapel "Doina".

## Sub lesson 3 — Sala cu Orgă

Unit 3, Sala cu Orgă — Chișinău's Organ Hall and the organ, king of instruments, with some 4,000 pipes, played by Anna Strezeva.

## Sub lesson 4 — Palatul Național "Nicolae Sulac"

Unit 4, Palatul Național "Nicolae Sulac" — the country's biggest culture palace, home of the "Mărțișor" festival and of the "Lăutarii", "Fluieraș" and "Noroc" ensembles.
