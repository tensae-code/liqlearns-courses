This lesson is Turkic Music and Throat Singing.

Turkic Music and Throat Singing — The musical traditions of the Turkic peoples, throat singing among them. — as Kazakhstan's school books teach it: the Turkic family (Tuvans, Khakass, Bashkirs, Tatars, Uzbeks, Kyrgyz, Uyghurs) and what their arts share; Tuva: over twenty old instruments (doshpuluur, igil, khomus, chadagan, shoor, danbyra) once serving shamans' rites, and the ancient tradition of throat singing; Khakass: the chatkhan zither, takhakh epic singing and the «хай» throat style, khomys and ykh; Bashkirs: the kurai flute as chief instrument, epics of batyrs and biys, terme, songs sung in chorus too, the Toratau ethno-ensemble; the Tatar folk song «Дождик» (arr. D. Faizi).

Watch, listen — then say it back in your own words.
