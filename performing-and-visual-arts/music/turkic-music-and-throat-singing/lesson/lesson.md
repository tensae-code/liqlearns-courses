## Sub lesson 1 — Kazakhstan Grade 5 Music (Kulmanova)

the Turkic family (Tuvans, Khakass, Bashkirs, Tatars, Uzbeks, Kyrgyz, Uyghurs) and what their arts share; Tuva: over twenty old instruments (doshpuluur, igil, khomus, chadagan, shoor, danbyra) once serving shamans' rites, and the ancient tradition of throat singing; Khakass: the chatkhan zither, takhakh epic singing and the «хай» throat style, khomys and ykh; Bashkirs: the kurai flute as chief instrument, epics of batyrs and biys, terme, songs sung in chorus too, the Toratau ethno-ensemble; the Tatar folk song «Дождик» (arr. D. Faizi); Uzbekistan: dastan and makom, exemplary music and folk dances; instruments gidzhak, dutar, chang, rubab, doira, surnai watched in the dance «Катта уйин»; Kyrgyzstan: the komuz, twin of the dombra; akyn-zhyrshy improvisers; the «Манас» epic and its manaschi tellers; aitys (aitysh); the song «На берегу озера» — Issyk-Kul wishing to smile; the research game "Musical Instruments of the Turkic Peoples": five groups fill the classifier table — Kazakh dombra, kobyz, zhetigen, shankobyz, sybyzgy, dangyra against Tuvan, Uzbek, Bashkir and Kyrgyz kin (doshpuluur, dutar, dumbyra, komuz; igil, gidzhak, kyl-kubyz, kyl-kiyak; chadagan, chang, atagan, zhetigen; khomus, kubyz, temir ooz komuz; shoor, nai, kamys-kurai; dambyra, doira, dongora, dool) — and find a legend for one instrument: the Khakass «Волшебный чатхан» and the Kazakh «Легенда о жетыгене» are read; throat singing of four peoples compared, and the folk song «Угой, угой, ай, угой» (perf. E. Kusanova)
