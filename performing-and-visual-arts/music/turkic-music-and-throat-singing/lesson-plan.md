# Lesson plan — Turkic Music and Throat Singing

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Music · **Lesson:** Turkic Music and Throat Singing
**Place in the curriculum:** Music (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The musical traditions of the Turkic peoples, throat singing among them..
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this course in the same title.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Kazakhstan Grade 5 Music (Kulmanova)** — the Turkic family (Tuvans, Khakass, Bashkirs, Tatars, Uzbeks, Kyrgyz, Uyghurs) and what their arts share; Tuva: over twenty old instruments (doshpuluur, igil, khomus, chadagan, shoor, danbyra) once serving shamans' rites, and the ancient tradition of throat singing; Khakass: the chatkhan zither, takhakh epic singing and the «хай» throat style, khomys and ykh; Bashkirs: the kurai flute as chief instrument, epics of batyrs and biys, terme, songs sung in chorus too, the Toratau ethno-ensemble; the Tatar folk song «Дождик» (arr. D. Faizi); Uzbekistan: dastan and makom, exemplary music and folk dances; instruments gidzhak, dutar, chang, rubab, doira, surnai watched in the dance «Катта уйин»; Kyrgyzstan: the komuz, twin of the dombra; akyn-zhyrshy improvisers; the «Манас» epic and its manaschi tellers; aitys (aitysh); the song «На берегу озера» — Issyk-Kul wishing to smile; the research game "Musical Instruments of the Turkic Peoples": five groups fill the classifier table — Kazakh dombra, kobyz, zhetigen, shankobyz, sybyzgy, dangyra against Tuvan, Uzbek, Bashkir and Kyrgyz kin (doshpuluur, dutar, dumbyra, komuz; igil, gidzhak, kyl-kubyz, kyl-kiyak; chadagan, chang, atagan, zhetigen; khomus, kubyz, temir ooz komuz; shoor, nai, kamys-kurai; dambyra, doira, dongora, dool) — and find a legend for one instrument: the Khakass «Волшебный чатхан» and the Kazakh «Легенда о жетыгене» are read; throat singing of four peoples compared, and the folk song «Угой, угой, ай, угой» (perf. E. Kusanova)
