# African Drumming and Rhythms — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Congo Square was in…
a) New Orleans  b) Havana  c) Vienna
**Answer: a) New Orleans**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** At Congo Square, enslaved Africans gathered to…
a) drum  b) sail  c) print ballads
**Answer: a) drum**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Congo Square is now…
a) Louis Armstrong Park  b) a harbour  c) a salon
**Answer: a) Louis Armstrong Park**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** African drumming is placed at…
a) jazz's root  b) opera's root  c) the waltz's root
**Answer: a) jazz's root**

**5. (Sub lesson 1)** The drumming came…
a) before the jazz bands  b) after television  c) only in books
**Answer: a) before the jazz bands**
