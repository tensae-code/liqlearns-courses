This lesson is Pingju and Yueju Opera.

China's regional operas — Pingju of the north and Yueju of Shaoxing. — as China's school books teach it: Pingju （评剧） — its music's urban evolution and development; and Yueju （越剧） — women's Yueju, its music and influence.

Watch, listen — then say it back in your own words.
