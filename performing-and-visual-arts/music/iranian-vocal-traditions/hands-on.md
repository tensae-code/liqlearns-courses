# Hands-on — Iranian Vocal Traditions

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Do the sub lessons
- **Vocal arts L1, Ethnic (folk) vocal music** — do the book's own task for this part: Iranian vocal traditions — آواهای قومی (مردمی): the folk vocal music of Iran's peoples and regions. Show your companion what you made or named.

- **Vocal arts L3, Religious vocal music of Iran** — do the book's own task for this part: آواهای دینی ایران: Iran's religious singing traditions. Show your companion what you made or named.
