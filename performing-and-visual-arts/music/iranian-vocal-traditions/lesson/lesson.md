Iranian vocal traditions — آواهای قومی (مردمی): the folk vocal music of Iran's peoples and regions.

## Sub lesson 2 — Vocal arts L3, Religious vocal music of Iran

آواهای دینی ایران: Iran's religious singing traditions.
