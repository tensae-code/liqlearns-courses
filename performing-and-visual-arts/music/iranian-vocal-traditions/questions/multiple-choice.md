# Iranian Vocal Traditions — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** آواهای قومی (مردمی) means…
a) the folk vocal music of Iran's peoples and regions  b) written notation  c) stage lighting
**Answer: a) the folk vocal music of Iran's peoples and regions**

**2.** Iran's religious vocal music is…
a) a singing tradition of its own, آواهای دینی ایران  b) the same as folk dancing  c) silent
**Answer: a) a singing tradition of its own, آواهای دینی ایران**
