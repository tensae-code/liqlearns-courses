# Lesson plan — Iranian Vocal Traditions

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Music · **Lesson:** Iranian Vocal Traditions
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 3 — Music (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Iran's vocal arts — the ethnic (folk) vocal music of Iran's peoples and regions, and Iran's religious singing traditions.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the learner's earlier art lessons. Recommended, never forced.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Vocal arts L1, Ethnic (folk) vocal music** — Iranian vocal traditions — آواهای قومی (مردمی): the folk vocal music of Iran's peoples and regions.
2. **Vocal arts L3, Religious vocal music of Iran** — آواهای دینی ایران: Iran's religious singing traditions.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier Music lessons. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say and show what vocal arts l1, ethnic (folk) vocal music teaches.
2. Say and show what vocal arts l3, religious vocal music of iran teaches.
