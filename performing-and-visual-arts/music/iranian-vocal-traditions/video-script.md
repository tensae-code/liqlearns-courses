Today we learn **Iranian Vocal Traditions**.

Step 1 — **Vocal arts L1, Ethnic (folk) vocal music**. Iranian vocal traditions — آواهای قومی (مردمی): the folk vocal music of Iran's peoples and regions.

Step 2 — **Vocal arts L3, Religious vocal music of Iran**. آواهای دینی ایران: Iran's religious singing traditions.

That is Iranian Vocal Traditions — Iran's own way, step by step.
