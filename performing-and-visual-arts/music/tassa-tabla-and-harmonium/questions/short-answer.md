# Tassa, Tabla and Harmonium — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the music word for telling instruments by their sound?
**Answer: Timbre.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the three Indian-heritage instruments in the lesson.
**Answer: Tabla, tassa and harmonium.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the four other sound-makers they are set beside.
**Answer: Piano, steel pan, recorder and voice.**
