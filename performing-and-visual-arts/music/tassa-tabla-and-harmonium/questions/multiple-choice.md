# Tassa, Tabla and Harmonium — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Timbre work means…
a) telling instruments by their sound  b) telling instruments by their colour  c) counting instruments
**Answer: a) telling instruments by their sound**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** An instrument in this lesson is known by…
a) its sound, how it is played, and how it produces sound  b) its price  c) its size only
**Answer: a) its sound, how it is played, and how it produces sound**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Which belongs to the country's Indian musical heritage set?
a) tassa  b) recorder  c) piano
**Answer: a) tassa**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The steel pan is set beside…
a) tabla, tassa and harmonium  b) only violins  c) no other instrument
**Answer: a) tabla, tassa and harmonium**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The harmonium is…
a) one of the Indian-heritage instruments in the lesson  b) a dance  c) a drum only
**Answer: a) one of the Indian-heritage instruments in the lesson**
