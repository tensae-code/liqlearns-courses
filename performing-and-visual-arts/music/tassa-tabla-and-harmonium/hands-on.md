# Hands-on — Tassa, Tabla and Harmonium

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Sound first
Listen to recordings with eyes closed. For each sound, name the instrument, then say how it is played and how it makes its sound.

## Two heritages, one lesson
Sort the seven sound-makers into the lesson's two groups: piano, steel pan, recorder, voice — and tabla, tassa, harmonium. Then listen again and check by ear.
