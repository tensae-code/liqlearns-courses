## Sub lesson 1 — Telling instruments by their sound

Form 1 Music in Trinidad and Tobago teaches **timbre**: **telling instruments by their sound**, and knowing **how each is played and how it produces sound**. An instrument is known three ways — its sound, its playing, and its sound-making.

## Sub lesson 2 — Tabla, tassa and harmonium

The comparison set is the country's own sound world: **piano, steel pan, recorder and voice** set beside **tabla, tassa and harmonium — the instruments of the country's Indian musical heritage**. The steel pan and the tassa drum stand in the same lesson, each known by its timbre.
