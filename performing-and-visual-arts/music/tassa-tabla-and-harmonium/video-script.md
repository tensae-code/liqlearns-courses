# Video script — Tassa, Tabla and Harmonium

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Close your eyes. Listen.

A pan rings. A tassa drum rolls. A harmonium breathes.

Each instrument has its own sound — its timbre.

Know it by its sound, and how it is played.

Tabla, tassa, harmonium — Trinidad and Tobago hears them all.
