# Rwandan Patriotic and Unity Songs

## Do it first

Start with the first part below — do it or make it before you read any explanation.

## Sub lesson 1 — Unity and reconciliation: Ubumwe

The song **Ubumwe (Unity)** teaches **forgiveness and reconciliation** — the Gacaca court is the pictured example — sung from memory while **clapping strong beats and stamping weak ones**, moving and dancing to it.

## Sub lesson 2 — Patriotism: love and sacrifice for one's country

**Patriotism is love and sacrifice for one's country.** The book pictures the heroes — Wangari Maathai, Paul Kagame, Mahatma Gandhi and Julius Nyerere — and sings a song about great people who worked for their countries, in tune and traditional rhythm.

## Sub lesson 3 — Remembrance: Twamagane Jenoside

The song **Twamagane Jenoside (No to Genocide)** opens the Primary 4 music book, taught at the **Ntarama Genocide Memorial** context: learners learn its theme — songs carry messages — sing it from memory in tune and traditional rhythm, and learn **what Rwandans must do to avoid genocide**.
