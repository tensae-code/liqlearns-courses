# Rwandan Patriotic and Unity Songs — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Ubumwe teaches…
a) forgiveness and reconciliation  b) farming prices  c) letter styles
**Answer: a) forgiveness and reconciliation**

**2.** In Ubumwe, strong beats are clapped and weak beats are…
a) stamped  b) whispered  c) ignored
**Answer: a) stamped**

**3.** Patriotism is…
a) love and sacrifice for one's country  b) loving only oneself  c) a drum rhythm
**Answer: a) love and sacrifice for one's country**

**4.** Twamagane Jenoside means…
a) No to Genocide  b) Unity  c) Working Hard
**Answer: a) No to Genocide**
