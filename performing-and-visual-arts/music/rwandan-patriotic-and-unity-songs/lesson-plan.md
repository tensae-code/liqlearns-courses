# Lesson plan — Rwandan Patriotic and Unity Songs

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Music · **Lesson:** Rwandan Patriotic and Unity Songs
**Place in the curriculum:** Music (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Rwanda's songs of patriotism, unity and reconciliation — and its remembrance songs for genocide prevention: Ubumwe, the patriotism song of the heroes, and Twamagane Jenoside (No to Genocide). Songs are placed by theme and purpose only, never by lyrics.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this course in the same title.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Unity and reconciliation: Ubumwe** — The song **Ubumwe (Unity)** teaches **forgiveness and reconciliation** — the Gacaca court is the pictured example — sung from memory while **clapping strong beats and stamping weak ones**, moving and dancing to it.
2. **Patriotism: love and sacrifice for one's country** — **Patriotism is love and sacrifice for one's country.
3. **Remembrance: Twamagane Jenoside** — The song **Twamagane Jenoside (No to Genocide)** opens the Primary 4 music book, taught at the **Ntarama Genocide Memorial** context: learners learn its theme — songs carry messages — sing it from memory in tune and traditional rhythm, and learn **what Rwandans must do to avoid genocide**.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of this course in the same title. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say and show what 'Unity and reconciliation: Ubumwe' teaches, in the book's own way.
2. Say and show what 'Patriotism: love and sacrifice for one's country' teaches, in the book's own way.
3. Say and show what 'Remembrance: Twamagane Jenoside' teaches, in the book's own way.

## What you need
Your voice, clapping hands and stamping feet for the strong and weak beats.
