# Macedonian Composers — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — Dimitrije Bužarovski, composer, musicologist and conductor
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Dimitrije Bužarovski, composer, musicologist and conductor, and his comic opera "Деспина и мистер Докс" (1991): Beaumarchais' Barber re-set in an Ohrid boarding house, its arias built on reworked folk songs, the emigrant Mister Doks come home to find a bride.

## Sub lesson 2 — Trajko Prokopiev (1909–1979) of Kumanovo
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Trajko Prokopiev (1909–1979) of Kumanovo, composer and conductor who built Macedonia's musical life — first director of the Skopje music school, conductor of the symphony orchestra and opera, director of the "Tanec" ensemble; his ballet "Лабин и Дојрана" (1958) on the myth of Lake Dojran, its main theme a kaval melody in uneven 5/8, the flute imitating the folk kaval.

## Sub lesson 3 — the Macedonian Composer School: born in the 20th century on the foundation of folk music
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: the Macedonian Composer School: born in the 20th century on the foundation of folk music — uneven rhythms, modal scales, ornamented melody — and raised by new institutions: the Macedonian Philharmonic, the Opera and Ballet, and the Skopje Music Academy.

## Sub lesson 4 — the founders (after 1945)
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: the founders (after 1945): Stefan Gajdov, Trajko Prokopiev (the choral cycle "Kumanovki", the first Macedonian ballet "Labin i Dojrana"), Todor Skalovski, Živko Firfov, Gligor Smokvarski, Blagoja Ivanovski, Vlastimir Nikolovski and Kiril Makedonski (the first Macedonian opera "Goce") — folk tradition wedded to art-music craft.

## Sub lesson 5 — the second group (1960–1980), affirmation and modernism
Sub lesson 5 questions from the type files. Pass mark: the second group (1960–1980), affirmation and modernism: Toma Prošev, Tomislav Zografski, Mihajlo Nikolovski, Risto Avramovski and Stojan Stojkov — modernist techniques with folklore as inspiration rather than quotation, each with a personal style.

## Sub lesson 6 — the third group (from the 1980s), contemporary expression
Sub lesson 6 questions from the type files. Pass mark: the third group (from the 1980s), contemporary expression: Tome Mančev, Dimitrije Bužarovski, Goce Kolarovski, Jana Andreevska, Pande Šahov, Vulnet Tairi and Fatos Lumani — international careers, electronic and computer sound, multimedia and no single ruling style.

## Sub lesson 8 — Кирил Македонски (1925–1984)
Sub lesson 8 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Кирил Македонски (1925–1984) — Kiril Makedonski: composer of the first Macedonian opera, "Goce" — the first step toward a national operatic style — and of "Ilinden" and "Car Samuil", four symphonies and the symphonic poem "Tancharka", his operas breathing with Macedonian folklore motifs.

## Sub lesson 9 — Димитрије Бужаровски (1952)
Sub lesson 9 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Димитрије Бужаровски (1952) — Dimitrije Bužarovski of the fifth generation: composer, musicologist and pioneer of computer and electronic music in Macedonia; over 50 works — symphonies, operas, oratorios, ballets — his style moving from neoclassicism through aleatorics to a multi-genre mix of serious music, pop, rock, jazz and folklore.

## Sub lesson 10 — Драган Шуплевски (1933–2001)
Sub lesson 10 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Драган Шуплевски (1933–2001) — Dragan Shuplevski: choral conductor, pedagogue and composer — first conductor of Radio Skopje's Children's Choir, conductor of the Radio Choir 1968–1990, founder of the Academic Choir "Mirche Acev" (1960); over 80 choral arrangements and more than 20 original choral works in the Macedonian folk spirit, and three capriccios for violin and piano.

## Sub lesson 12 — Тодор Скаловски (1909–2004)
Sub lesson 12 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Тодор Скаловски (1909–2004) — Todor Skalovski of Tetovo: music teacher, wartime conductor in Skopje and Bitola, co-founder of the symphony orchestra, the opera and the Macedonian Philharmonic; his choral works — above all "Makedonsko oro", which catches the rebellious rhythm of Teshkoto — "Makedonska humoreska", "Pesna za Goce", "Bitolka", "Makedonski diptih", "Todorka mlada robinka", and songs for children's choirs.

## Sub lesson 13 — Трајко Прокопиев (1909–1979)
Sub lesson 13 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Трајко Прокопиев (1909–1979) — Trajko Prokopiev of Kumanovo: began on the duduk, then flute, then violin in Skopje (1915), singing in the "Vardar" society; Belgrade's Music School, Prague's Conservatory, opera conductor 1947–1951; of the older generation who sought their expression in the national melos — the lyricist, master of choral miniatures on folklore themes and of film music; his last works the ballet "Labin i Dojrana" and the opera "Razdelba".
