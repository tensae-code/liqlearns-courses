## Sub lesson 1 — The Bendir (Tar) Instrument and the Triangle Instrument

Rhythm instruments have many shapes, each told apart by listening and looking; the bendir is made of a wooden frame with a plastic circle set on it, and the triangle is made of solid metal and gives a ringing sound like a bell's; whether the two give pitched or unpitched sounds, and naming other ringing rhythm instruments.
