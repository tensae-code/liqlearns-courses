a) another country's arts only  b) Armenian Folk and Celebration Songs — Armenia's own teaching of this topic, kept as named parts: The wedding ritual songs (հարսանեկան ծեսի երգեր) — many rites and their songs were forgotten, but the wedding songs have come down from ancient times  c) nothing — it is just names

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Armenia's own forms in this lesson are...
