# Armenian Folk and Celebration Songs

## Do it first

Say what it is in your own words. If you can name one, you are already inside the lesson.

## Sub lesson 1 — The wedding ritual songs (հարսանեկան ծեսի երգեր)

The wedding ritual songs (հարսանեկան ծեսի երգեր) — many rites and their songs were forgotten, but the wedding songs have come down from ancient times.

## Sub lesson 2 — New Year and Christmas (Ամանոր ու Սուրբ Ծնունդ)

New Year and Christmas (Ամանոր ու Սուրբ Ծնունդ) — the AVETIS carols sung slowly at the feast: "Today is the feast of the Nativity".

## Sub lesson 3 — What is sung at a WEDDING

What is sung at a WEDDING — in the Armenian traditional wedding song and dance held a great place; praise-songs (գովքեր) were sung, comic songs teased the groom's mother to come out and greet the pair, and the bride is called a doe in Komitas's noted "Yeghnik".

## Sub lesson 4 — Wedding songs and dances fill the whole wedding; the SHAVALI

Wedding songs and dances fill the whole wedding; the SHAVALI dance's movements reproduce honouring the clan, remembering the ancestors and glorifying new life.

## Sub lesson 5 — Birth songs, or AVETISNER

Birth songs, or AVETISNER — the good-news songs sung at a birth.

## Sub lesson 6 — the Armenian folk and traditional songs carried through the book

the Armenian folk and traditional songs carried through the book as its singing stock (folk song notation task, wedding and dance songs, lullaby "Օրոր") — Armenia's own song heritage taught as content (summaries only).
