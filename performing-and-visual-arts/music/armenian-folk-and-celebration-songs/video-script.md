# Video script — Armenian Folk and Celebration Songs

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This is Armenia's own lesson.

**The wedding ritual songs (հարսանեկան ծեսի երգեր).** The wedding ritual songs (հարսանեկան ծեսի երգեր) — many rites and their songs were forgotten, but the wedding songs have come down from ancient times.

**New Year and Christmas (Ամանոր ու Սուրբ Ծնունդ).** New Year and Christmas (Ամանոր ու Սուրբ Ծնունդ) — the AVETIS carols sung slowly at the feast: "Today is the feast of the Nativity".

**What is sung at a WEDDING.** What is sung at a WEDDING — in the Armenian traditional wedding song and dance held a great place; praise-songs (գովքեր) were sung, comic songs teased the groom's mother to come out and greet the pair, and the bride is called a doe in Komitas's noted "Yeghnik".

**Wedding songs and dances fill the whole wedding; the SHAVALI.** Wedding songs and dances fill the whole wedding; the SHAVALI dance's movements reproduce honouring the clan, remembering the ancestors and glorifying new life.

**Birth songs, or AVETISNER.** Birth songs, or AVETISNER — the good-news songs sung at a birth.

**the Armenian folk and traditional songs carried through the book.** the Armenian folk and traditional songs carried through the book as its singing stock (folk song notation task, wedding and dance songs, lullaby "Օրոր") — Armenia's own song heritage taught as content (summaries only).

That is Armenian Folk and Celebration Songs — Armenia's way, kept by name.
