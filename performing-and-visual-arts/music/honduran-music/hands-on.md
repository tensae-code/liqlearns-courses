# Hands-on — Honduran Music

The lesson with your ears and your voice, not only your eyes.

## A song from home
Sing or hum a song that belongs to your own place. Tell a partner: who makes this music, and what does it sound like? That is the Grade 7 book's way in — a country's music is known by its makers and its sounds.

## Then and now
In pairs, sort what you know of your country's music into two piles: the sounds of today, and the sounds of earlier periods. Say one sentence for each pile.
