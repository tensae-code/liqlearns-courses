# Honduran Music — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Honduran music is taught as Honduras's own music, with its own makers and sounds.
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The Grade 7 book studies the musical genres current in Honduras today.
**Answer: True.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** In these books, Honduran music exists only in the past.
**Answer: False.** — its genres today are studied too.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** The historical evolution of Honduran music runs from its beginnings to today.
**Answer: True.**
