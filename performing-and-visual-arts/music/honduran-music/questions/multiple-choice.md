# Honduran Music — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Grade 7 book's sequence 'Nuestra música' is about…
a) music from other continents  b) Honduras's own music — its makers and its sounds  c) music notation only
**Answer: b) Honduras's own music — its makers and its sounds** — our music is the country's own.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** 'Today's sounds in Honduras' studies…
a) only old music  b) silence  c) the musical genres current in Honduras today
**Answer: c) the musical genres current in Honduras today** — the living genres of now.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The Grade 8 book follows Honduran music…
a) in periods, from its beginnings to today  b) only in one year  c) without any history
**Answer: a) in periods, from its beginnings to today** — the historical evolution, period by period.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** A country's own music is known, in these books, by…
a) its price  b) its makers and its sounds  c) its volume
**Answer: b) its makers and its sounds**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Which book opens its music block with the historical evolution of Honduran music?
a) the Grade 7 book  b) the Grade 8 book  c) neither book
**Answer: b) the Grade 8 book** — Grade 8, Block II S1.
