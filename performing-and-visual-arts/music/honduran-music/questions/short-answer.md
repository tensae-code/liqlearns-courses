# Honduran Music — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is 'nuestra música' in the Honduras books?
**Answer: Honduras's own music — its makers and its sounds.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the sequence on today's sounds study?
**Answer: The musical genres current in Honduras today.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** How does the Grade 8 book organise the history of Honduran music?
**Answer: In periods, from its beginnings to today.**
