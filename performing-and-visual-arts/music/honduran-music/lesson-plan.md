# Lesson plan — Honduran Music

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Music · **Lesson:** Honduran Music
**Place in the curriculum:** Music (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Honduras's own music — its makers and its sounds, the musical genres current in Honduras today, and the periods of its history from its beginnings to today.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of its title — recommended first, never forced.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Our music: Honduran music** — Honduras's own music, its makers and its sounds — the Grade 7 book's 'Nuestra música'.
2. **Today's sounds in Honduras** — the musical genres current in Honduras today.
3. **Historical evolution of Honduran music** — the periods of Honduras's music from its beginnings to today — the Grade 8 book's widening of the same topic.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the songs and instruments lessons of the Music title. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say what Honduran music is in the books' way — a country's own music, known by its makers and its sounds.
2. Name the two ways the books study it: the genres heard in Honduras today, and its historical periods from its beginnings to today.

Your voice, and a song from your own place to think with.

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks a country's music is only its old music → the Grade 8 book follows Honduran music through all its periods to today, and the Grade 7 book names the genres current in Honduras now.
- Thinks 'our music' means any music we like → in these books it is Honduras's own music: its makers, its sounds, its history.
