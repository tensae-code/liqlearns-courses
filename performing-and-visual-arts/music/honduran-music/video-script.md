# Video script — Honduran Music

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Every country has its own music.

Honduras too. Its makers. Its sounds. That is **Honduran music** — *nuestra música*, our music.

And it lives today — the genres people hear in Honduras now.
And it has a long road behind it — period by period, from its beginnings to today.

Know your own music. That is this lesson.
