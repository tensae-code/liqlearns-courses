# Music of Colombia's Amazon — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Amazon's music carries…
a) several cultures at once  b) only one culture  c) no instruments
**Answer: a) several cultures at once**.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Caribbean emigrants brought to the Amazon…
a) congas, marimbas, drums, guitar and bass  b) only violins  c) only pianos
**Answer: a) congas, marimbas, drums, guitar and bass**.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** In the Yuruparí origin story, the keepers of the great Yuruparí became…
a) the women  b) the rivers  c) the harps
**Answer: a) the women**.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The Amazon is named in the guide as a…
a) world biosphere reserve  b) desert  c) capital city
**Answer: a) world biosphere reserve**.
