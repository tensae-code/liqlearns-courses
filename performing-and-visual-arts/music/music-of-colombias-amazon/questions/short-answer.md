# Music of Colombia's Amazon — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name three kinds of music the guide hears in the Amazon.
**Answer: Any three of: baioes, marineras, huaynos, joropos (and the emigrants' musics).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What question does the guide ask about indigenous peoples and music?
**Answer: What music is for the indigenous peoples, and their beliefs.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Why is there no single Amazon sound in this lesson?
**Answer: Because several cultures sound at once there; the mixture is the region.**
