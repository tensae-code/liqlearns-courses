# Video script — Music of Colombia's Amazon

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

One forest. Many musics.

The **Amazon** — a world biosphere reserve. Its music carries **several cultures at once**: baioes, marineras, huaynos, joropos. And the Caribbean emigrants brought **congas, marimbas, drums, guitar and bass**.

For the **indigenous peoples**, music lives inside their beliefs.
And the **Yuruparí** origin story tells how **the women became the keepers of the great Yuruparí**.

Listen for the mixture. The mixture is the region.
