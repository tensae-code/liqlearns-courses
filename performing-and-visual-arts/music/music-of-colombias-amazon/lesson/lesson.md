## Sub lesson 1 — Many cultures in one music

The Amazon is a **world biosphere reserve**, and its music carries **several cultures at once**: **baioes, marineras and huaynos, and joropos** — and from **Caribbean emigrants, congas, marimbas, drums, guitar and bass**. No single sound stands for the region; the mixture is the region.

## Sub lesson 2 — What music is for the indigenous peoples — and the Yuruparí

The guide asks **what music is for the indigenous peoples and their beliefs** — music inside a people's own way of understanding the world. It tells the **Yuruparí origin story, in which the women became the keepers of the great Yuruparí** — placed here by summary only, as the course places every story.
