## Sub lesson 1 — Vietnam Grade 4 Âm nhạc 4

Introducing the đàn tranh — Vietnam's traditional zither: a wooden trapezoid body about 110–120 cm long carrying sixteen or more strings of silk or metal, each over its own movable bridge for tuning, with tuning keys at the narrow end; the player wears three picks — on thumb, index and middle fingers — and its sound turns now deep and warm, now clear and tinkling; it performs alone, in ensembles, and accompanies singing and poetry recitation; listening to its timbre in an excerpt of Xuân Khải's "Xuân quê hương" (Spring in the Homeland)
