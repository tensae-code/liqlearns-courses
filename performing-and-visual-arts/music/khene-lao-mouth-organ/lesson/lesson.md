## Sub lesson 1 — Music G9 teacher guide — Khene: The Lao Mouth Organ

The khene is a bamboo mouth organ; the 7-pipe khene (14 reeds, pentatonic playing), the 8-pipe khene (16 pipes, used for lam singing accompaniment) and the older long Hmong khene, told apart by picture and structure
