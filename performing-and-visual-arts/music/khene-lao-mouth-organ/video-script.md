This lesson is Khene: The Lao Mouth Organ.

Laos's khene — the khene 7, khene 8 and the Hmong khene. — as Laos's school books teach it: the khene is a bamboo mouth organ; the 7-pipe khene (14 reeds, pentatonic playing), the 8-pipe khene (16 pipes, used for lam singing accompaniment) and the older long Hmong khene, told apart by picture and structure

Watch, listen — then say it back in your own words.
