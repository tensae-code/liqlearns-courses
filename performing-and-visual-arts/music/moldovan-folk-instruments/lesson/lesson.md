# Moldovan Folk Instruments

## Do it first

Name one thing that belongs in this lesson — Moldova's folk instruments — the fluier and the cimpoi — met by name and sound. Say what it is in your own words. If you can name one, you are already inside the lesson.

## Sub lesson 1 — the fluier

Theme 1/2, the fluier — the fluier, one of the oldest Moldovan folk wind instruments (a thin wooden tube with finger holes), heard in the song "Doina" about the little bone fluier.

## Sub lesson 2 — song "Cimpoiul"

Theme 2, song "Cimpoiul" — the cimpoi (Moldovan bagpipe): a folk song imitating its drone in two answering groups.

## Sub lesson 3 — Soliști instrumentiști

Unit 2, Soliști instrumentiști — the master players: Gheorghe Zamfir, who rebuilt the nai, Vasile Iovu, and violinist Nicolae Botgros.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson holds, part by part, in your own words. Companion checks: did every part get named, with its Moldovan name kept where the book gives one?

## Do It Yourself

1. Choose one named part of this lesson and find out one more true thing about it — from a person who knows it, or from the book your part came from.
2. Make or perform a small piece in the way of that part: a rhythm, a step, a drawing, a shaped piece of clay.
3. Write one sentence: what does this part show about Moldova's own way of teaching its arts?
