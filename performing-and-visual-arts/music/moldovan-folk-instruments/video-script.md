# Video script — Moldovan Folk Instruments

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This is Moldova's own lesson.

**the fluier.** Theme 1/2, the fluier — the fluier, one of the oldest Moldovan folk wind instruments (a thin wooden tube with finger holes), heard in the song "Doina" about the little bone fluier.

**song "Cimpoiul".** Theme 2, song "Cimpoiul" — the cimpoi (Moldovan bagpipe): a folk song imitating its drone in two answering groups.

**Soliști instrumentiști.** Unit 2, Soliști instrumentiști — the master players: Gheorghe Zamfir, who rebuilt the nai, Vasile Iovu, and violinist Nicolae Botgros.

That is Moldovan Folk Instruments — Moldova's way, kept by name.
