# Komitas — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — Who creates music? Komitas
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Who creates music? Komitas — the composer pictured with a bird in his hands; his children's song "Payte dziuk" (The wooden horse) sung and heard in his own "Mankakan nvagner" (Children's pieces)

## Sub lesson 2 — Komitas on music education (teacher-guide evidence)
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Komitas on music education (teacher-guide evidence) — the guide's introduction quotes Komitas's 1912 Bera Yesayan lecture: music as the balanced education of the child's feelings

## Sub lesson 3 — A letter from Komitas
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: A letter from Komitas — "Hello, little friend, I am Komitas; as a child they called me Soghomon": the collector of Armenia's songs introduces himself (with Artsrunyan's painting "The song-gatherer")

## Sub lesson 4 — Komitas as the founder of the method (teacher-guide evidence)
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Komitas as the founder of the method (teacher-guide evidence) — Catholicos Vazgen I links Mashtots and Komitas: both gave the nation its letters, one of language, one of song; sad songs are never to be given to children — all must be lively

Sub lesson 5 questions from the type files. Pass mark: "Hayr mer" (Հա՜յր մեր, Komitas) — the book opens with Komitas's setting, sung before all the year's themes

## Sub lesson 6 — A second letter from Komitas
Sub lesson 6 questions from the type files. Pass mark: A second letter from Komitas — composer, choirmaster, teacher, collector of Armenian folk song: his portrait in paint and in words

## Sub lesson 7 — How does the folk song reach us
Sub lesson 7 questions from the type files. Pass mark: How does the folk song reach us — people sang in their daily life, not for an audience; collectors record them: hear and learn Komitas's noted song "The incense tree" (Խնկի ծառ)

Sub lesson 8 questions from the type files. Pass mark: The year's plan in hours (teacher-guide evidence) — Armenian national songs and dances take 16 hours, incl. Komitas's "Hoy Nazan" in choral arrangement and the military songs with the Karno Kochari danced shoulder to shoulder

## Sub lesson 9 — Komitas's "Hov areq" (Հով արեք)
Sub lesson 9 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Komitas's "Hov areq" (Հով արեք) — hear the folk song first in one voice, in Komitas's own performance, then in many voices

## Sub lesson 10 — KOMITAS, a full chapter
Sub lesson 10 questions from the type files. Pass mark: KOMITAS, a full chapter — Komitas Vardapet (1869-1935) holds a special place in Armenian folk music: he travelled the settlements, heard and noted songs and tunes on the spot, studied and classed them; his sharakan for the Hripsime virgins hides their names in its first letters
