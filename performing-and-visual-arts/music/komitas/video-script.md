# Video script — Komitas

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This is Armenia's own lesson.

**Who creates music? Komitas.** Who creates music? Komitas — the composer pictured with a bird in his hands; his children's song "Payte dziuk" (The wooden horse) sung and heard in his own "Mankakan nvagner" (Children's pieces).

**Komitas on music education (teacher-guide evidence).** Komitas on music education (teacher-guide evidence) — the guide's introduction quotes Komitas's 1912 Bera Yesayan lecture: music as the balanced education of the child's feelings.

**A letter from Komitas.** A letter from Komitas — "Hello, little friend, I am Komitas; as a child they called me Soghomon": the collector of Armenia's songs introduces himself (with Artsrunyan's painting "The song-gatherer").

**Komitas as the founder of the method (teacher-guide evidence).** Komitas as the founder of the method (teacher-guide evidence) — Catholicos Vazgen I links Mashtots and Komitas: both gave the nation its letters, one of language, one of song; sad songs are never to be given to children — all must be lively.

**"Hayr mer" (Հա՜յր մեր, Komitas).** "Hayr mer" (Հա՜յր մեր, Komitas) — the book opens with Komitas's setting, sung before all the year's themes.

**A second letter from Komitas.** A second letter from Komitas — composer, choirmaster, teacher, collector of Armenian folk song: his portrait in paint and in words.

**How does the folk song reach us.** How does the folk song reach us — people sang in their daily life, not for an audience; collectors record them: hear and learn Komitas's noted song "The incense tree" (Խնկի ծառ).

**The year's plan in hours (teacher-guide evidence).** The year's plan in hours (teacher-guide evidence) — Armenian national songs and dances take 16 hours, incl. Komitas's "Hoy Nazan" in choral arrangement and the military songs with the Karno Kochari danced shoulder to shoulder.

**Komitas's "Hov areq" (Հով արեք).** Komitas's "Hov areq" (Հով արեք) — hear the folk song first in one voice, in Komitas's own performance, then in many voices.

**KOMITAS, a full chapter.** KOMITAS, a full chapter — Komitas Vardapet (1869-1935) holds a special place in Armenian folk music: he travelled the settlements, heard and noted songs and tunes on the spot, studied and classed them; his sharakan for the Hripsime virgins hides their names in its first letters.

**Armenian folk dance-song "Habrban" (Հաբրբան).** Armenian folk dance-song "Habrban" (Հաբրբան) — arranged by Komitas: the boy's and girl's call-and-answer sung as a little scene ("Sing like an ashugh").

That is Komitas — Armenia's way, kept by name.
