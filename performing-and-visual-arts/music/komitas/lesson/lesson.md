# Komitas

## Sub lesson 1 — Who creates music? Komitas

Who creates music? Komitas — the composer pictured with a bird in his hands; his children's song "Payte dziuk" (The wooden horse) sung and heard in his own "Mankakan nvagner" (Children's pieces).

## Sub lesson 2 — Komitas on music education (teacher-guide evidence)

Komitas on music education (teacher-guide evidence) — the guide's introduction quotes Komitas's 1912 Bera Yesayan lecture: music as the balanced education of the child's feelings.

## Sub lesson 3 — A letter from Komitas

A letter from Komitas — "Hello, little friend, I am Komitas; as a child they called me Soghomon": the collector of Armenia's songs introduces himself (with Artsrunyan's painting "The song-gatherer").

## Sub lesson 4 — Komitas as the founder of the method (teacher-guide evidence)

Komitas as the founder of the method (teacher-guide evidence) — Catholicos Vazgen I links Mashtots and Komitas: both gave the nation its letters, one of language, one of song; sad songs are never to be given to children — all must be lively.

## Sub lesson 5 — "Hayr mer" (Հա՜յր մեր, Komitas)

"Hayr mer" (Հա՜յր մեր, Komitas) — the book opens with Komitas's setting, sung before all the year's themes.

## Sub lesson 6 — A second letter from Komitas

A second letter from Komitas — composer, choirmaster, teacher, collector of Armenian folk song: his portrait in paint and in words.

## Sub lesson 7 — How does the folk song reach us

How does the folk song reach us — people sang in their daily life, not for an audience; collectors record them: hear and learn Komitas's noted song "The incense tree" (Խնկի ծառ).

## Sub lesson 8 — The year's plan in hours (teacher-guide evidence)

The year's plan in hours (teacher-guide evidence) — Armenian national songs and dances take 16 hours, incl. Komitas's "Hoy Nazan" in choral arrangement and the military songs with the Karno Kochari danced shoulder to shoulder.

## Sub lesson 9 — Komitas's "Hov areq" (Հով արեք)

Komitas's "Hov areq" (Հով արեք) — hear the folk song first in one voice, in Komitas's own performance, then in many voices.

## Sub lesson 10 — KOMITAS, a full chapter

KOMITAS, a full chapter — Komitas Vardapet (1869-1935) holds a special place in Armenian folk music: he travelled the settlements, heard and noted songs and tunes on the spot, studied and classed them; his sharakan for the Hripsime virgins hides their names in its first letters.

## Sub lesson 11 — Armenian folk dance-song "Habrban" (Հաբրբան)

Armenian folk dance-song "Habrban" (Հաբրբան) — arranged by Komitas: the boy's and girl's call-and-answer sung as a little scene ("Sing like an ashugh").
