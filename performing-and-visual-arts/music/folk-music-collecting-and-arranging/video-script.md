This is Moldova's own lesson.

**Muzica și dansul.** Unit 1, Muzica și dansul — Bartók, founder of ethnomusicology, who collected thousands of Hungarian, Romanian and Slovak folk melodies and turned them into concert music: the suite "Romanian Folk Dances" and its first piece, "Jocul cu bâta".

That is Collecting and Arranging Folk Music — Moldova's way, kept by name.
