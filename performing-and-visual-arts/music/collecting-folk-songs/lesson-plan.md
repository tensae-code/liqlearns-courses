**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Music · **Lesson:** Collecting Folk Songs
**Place in the curriculum:** Music (CURRICULUM.md).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The COLLECTORS (բանահավաքներ)** — The COLLECTORS (բանահավաքներ): Spiridon Melikyan — when folklore is spoken of, Komitas is named most; Melikyan noted the song "Pretty flower" in today's Aragatsotn
2. **The collectors** — The collectors: Mihran Tumajan — Komitas's pupil, who gathered a great collection of Western Armenia's songs, poems and melodies in the towns of Western Armenia and France
3. **Collectors and bearers** — Collectors and bearers — national songs are written down during collecting; the collector wins people's trust: Komitas once hid in a tree hollow to hear a girl singing at her work on a roof
4. **Hayrik Muradyan (1905-1999)** — Hayrik Muradyan (1905-1999) — born in Van in Western Armenia, he learned there and passed on a great store of its songs
5. **ARMENIAN NATIONAL SONGS AND DANCES** — ARMENIAN NATIONAL SONGS AND DANCES — folk song and dance; are national song and dance art?; the genres of national songs; BANAHAVAQ — collecting folklore from its bearers; the national dance

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
