## Sub lesson 1 — The COLLECTORS (բանահավաքներ)

The COLLECTORS (բանահավաքներ): Spiridon Melikyan — when folklore is spoken of, Komitas is named most; Melikyan noted the song "Pretty flower" in today's Aragatsotn.

## Sub lesson 2 — The collectors

The collectors: Mihran Tumajan — Komitas's pupil, who gathered a great collection of Western Armenia's songs, poems and melodies in the towns of Western Armenia and France.

## Sub lesson 3 — Collectors and bearers

Collectors and bearers — national songs are written down during collecting; the collector wins people's trust: Komitas once hid in a tree hollow to hear a girl singing at her work on a roof.

## Sub lesson 4 — Hayrik Muradyan (1905-1999)

Hayrik Muradyan (1905-1999) — born in Van in Western Armenia, he learned there and passed on a great store of its songs.

## Sub lesson 5 — ARMENIAN NATIONAL SONGS AND DANCES

ARMENIAN NATIONAL SONGS AND DANCES — folk song and dance; are national song and dance art?; the genres of national songs; BANAHAVAQ — collecting folklore from its bearers; the national dance.
