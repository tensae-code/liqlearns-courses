# Work and Play Songs of Rwanda — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** The songs of this lesson are about…
a) labour and games  b) examinations  c) weaving
**Answer: a) labour and games**

**2.** They are accompanied by…
a) claps respecting the rhythm  b) drums only  c) nothing
**Answer: a) claps respecting the rhythm**

**3.** 'Games' in the lesson means…
a) sports and play  b) board prices  c) riddles only
**Answer: a) sports and play**

**4.** The claps must…
a) respect the rhythm of the Rwandan song  b) be as loud as possible  c) stop the singing
**Answer: a) respect the rhythm of the Rwandan song**
