# Work and Play Songs of Rwanda

## Do it first

Start with the first part below — do it or make it before you read any explanation.

## Sub lesson 1 — Songs about labour

The Primary 2 guide's lesson sings **songs about labour (work) accompanied by claps** — the claps kept in various ways, always respecting the rhythm of the Rwandan song.

## Sub lesson 2 — Songs about games

The same lesson sings **songs about games (sports), accompanied by claps** — work and play carried by the same clapped rhythm, sung and performed as Rwanda's songs are.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson's Rwandan way is, and show one part with your hands or your voice. Companion checks: is the named Rwandan thing in your answer?
