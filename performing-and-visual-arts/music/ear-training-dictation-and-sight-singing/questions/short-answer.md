# Ear Training, Dictation and Sight-Singing — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What two ways can a sound for intonation practice be made?
**Answer: Played by an instrument, or sung.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the two things the intonation exercises practise.
**Answer: Intervals and note variations.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Across which séances does the intonation strand run in EEA2?
**Answer: Séances 2–5.**
