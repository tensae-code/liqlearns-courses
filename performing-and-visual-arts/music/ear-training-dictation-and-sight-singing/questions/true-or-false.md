# Ear Training, Dictation and Sight-Singing — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The ear can be trained to recognise pitch.
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Intonation is about how loud a sound is.
**Answer: False — it is about pitch, high and low.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Intervals are practised in the intonation exercises.
**Answer: True.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Intonation is done once and never repeated.
**Answer: False — it runs across Séances 2–5.**
