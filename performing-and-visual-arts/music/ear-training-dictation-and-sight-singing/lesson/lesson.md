# Ear Training, Dictation and Sight-Singing

## Do it first

Quick start: from the title alone, guess what this lesson's work is. Then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — Recognising the pitch of sounds

Haiti's EEA2 music sequence gives every séance an **intonation** strand: **ear culture** — **recognising the pitch of sounds played by an instrument or sung**. The ear learns first to say what it hears: this sound, at this height.

## Sub lesson 2 — Pinpointing pitch, intervals and variations

The strand grows: **pinpointing** the pitch — placing the sound exactly — with **intonation exercises on intervals and note variations**. The same ear work returns across Séances 2–5, beside the theory, recorder and appreciation strands.
