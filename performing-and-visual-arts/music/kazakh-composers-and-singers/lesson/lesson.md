# Kazakh Composers and Singers

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about kazakh composers and singers. Then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — Kazakhstan Grade 4 Music (Gorchakova)

folk vocal, the singing manner of a people's own tradition; the lullaby as everyone's first song; the Armenian folk song "Swallow" in Kulyash Baiseitova's folk manner, its virtuoso bird-imitation built on pauses; Bakhytzhan Baikadamov, founder of choral singing in Kazakhstan and first arranger of kuis and folk songs, who also recalculated the dombra's construction; his song «Девушка-водовоз» ("The Water-Carrier Girl") sung with noise instruments imitating hoofbeats

## Sub lesson 2 — Kazakhstan Grade 4 Music (Kulmanova)

Gaziza Zhubanova (1927–1993), daughter of Akhmet Zhubanov, composer of operas, oratorios and ballets; her ballet «Хиросима» (State Prize 1970) in summary: a Japanese family's peaceful life, the bomb, and the requiem ending — its main theme is the Japanese folk song «Сакура», sung here in Kazakh translation

## Sub lesson 3 — Kazakhstan Grade 6 Music (Musakhajaeva)

what it teaches: Dauletkerei Shygaiuly, «Bapas kuishi» — founder of the lyrical-philosophical tokpe kui: «Топан», «Өттің дүние», «Жігер» and his gallery of women's portraits in kui; his kui «Байжұма» heard and analysed
