This lesson is Kazakh Composers and Singers.

Kazakh Composers and Singers — Kazakhstan's named masters — composers Bakhytzhan Baikadamov, Dauletkerei the kui philosopher and Gaziza Zhubanova, and folk singer Kulyash Baiseitova. — as Kazakhstan's school books teach it: folk vocal, the singing manner of a people's own tradition; the lullaby as everyone's first song; the Armenian folk song "Swallow" in Kulyash Baiseitova's folk manner, its virtuoso bird-imitation built on pauses.

Watch, listen — then say it back in your own words.
