# Lesson plan — Scales, Pitch and Key Signatures

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Music · **Lesson:** Scales, Pitch and Key Signatures
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 3 — Music (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the ladder of sounds — major and minor scales and how they are built, pitch as high and low, and the key-signature signs (sharp and flat at the staff's head) that keep a scale's steps true on any starting sound.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Reading Music on the Staff — the learner can already read both clefs and the accidentals; this lesson builds whole scales on that staff.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved (the gate: README, "Units, Lessons and Sub Lessons (ruled 2026-11-08)"):

1. **The major scale: the ladder of sounds** — a scale is the ordering of sounds in sequence; the major scale's half steps fall only between 3–4 and 7–8; C and F major built in both clefs (Grade 6, printed pp. 13–15).
2. **The minor scale: three kinds, and counting back three** — minor scales sound soft; natural, harmonic and melodic kinds, with the natural minor built here; find it by counting back three from its major (Grade 6, printed pp. 18–19).
3. **Pitch: high and low** — pitch (የድምፅ ቃና) is the sound-character of thick and thin; G-clef sounds are high, F-clef sounds low; singing set pitches (Grade 6, printed pp. 20–22).
4. **Key signatures: sharps and flats in order** — the key-signature signs are sharp and flat, written on the music at the staff's head to keep the intervals when building scales (Grade 6, printed pp. 23–25).
5. **Scales and key signatures in the guide (teacher guide — evidence)** — the diatonic major and minor scales and key signatures taught with counting rhythm and both-clef reading (Grade 6 teacher guide, §§1.1 and 2.1, printed pp. 3–16 and 37–44).

6. **Exercise 1.2 — building nine major scales** — the book's nine construction tasks: E, A, B-flat, E-flat, F-sharp majors in named note values and clefs.
7. **Exercises 2.2 and 2.3 — singing high, singing low** — sing the pictured do-mi patterns with the letters under the notes; read the F-clef melody low, holding the syllable 'la'; the poem-melody 'Our Historical Heritage'.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Reading Music on the Staff. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Define a scale as the ordering of sounds — the ladder of sounds.
2. Build a major scale: half steps only between 3–4 and 7–8.
3. Name the three kinds of minor scale and find the natural minor by counting back three.
4. Say what pitch is, and which clef carries high and which low pitch.
5. Write key signatures: sharps and flats at the staff head, in their correct order.

## What you need
Paper staves, a pencil, your voice, and — if one is near — any instrument to check the ladder against.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — Sing up: do, re, mi, fa, sol, la, si, do. You have climbed a ladder of eight sounds and arrived at the first sound's twin, one floor up. That ladder is a scale — this lesson takes it apart to see how its steps are built. (lesson/lesson.md).

## If the learner is stuck
- Loses the major ladder → only two steps are small: 3→4 and 7→8. Every other step is whole. Sing and touch each step as you count.
- Finds the minor by guessing → count BACK THREE from the major's tonic: C major counts back to A, its natural minor.
