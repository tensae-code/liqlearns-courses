# Scales, Pitch and Key Signatures — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Is the step from E to F (3→4 in C major) whole or half?
**Answer: Half — it is one of the two half steps.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How do minor scales sound, in the book's words?
**Answer: Soft in tone and character.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Singing staff notes keeping their time values, joined with the beat, is called…
**Answer: Sight-singing.**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** What do key signatures keep true when a scale is built on a new tonic?
**Answer: The scale's intervals — its whole and half steps.**

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** Which two staffs does the guide's reading cover?
**Answer: The treble (G) staff and the bass (F) staff.**

**6. (Sub lesson 6)** Name three majors Exercise 1.2 builds besides C and F.
**Answer:** Any three of: E, A, B-flat, E-flat, F-sharp (and the exercise's remaining majors).

**7. (Sub lesson 7)** Name the poem-melody Exercise 2.2 reaches.
**Answer:** “Our Historical Heritage”.
