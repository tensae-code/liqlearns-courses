# Scales, Pitch and Key Signatures — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In a major scale, the half steps fall…
  a) Only between 3–4 and 7–8 b) Between every pair of sounds c) Only between 1–2 and 5–6
**Answer: a) Only between 3–4 and 7–8** — the half step is found only from 3→4 and 7→8.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Counting back three from C major lands on…
  a) A — the start of A natural minor b) E c) G
**Answer: a) A — the start of A natural minor** — C counts back 3-2-1 to A, its natural minor.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Pitch is expressed by…
  a) Thick and thin sound-character b) Loud and soft volume c) Fast and slow tempo
**Answer: a) Thick and thin sound-character** — pitch is the thick/thin character of a sound — not dynamics, not tempo.

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Key signatures are written…
  a) At the staff's head, in correct positional order b) At the end of the piece c) Under the title only
**Answer: a) At the staff's head, in correct positional order** — the signs stand at the staff head in their correct order.

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** Counting rhythm in the guide is done with…
  a) Spoken syllables b) Only silent reading c) Finger snapping only
**Answer: a) Spoken syllables** — rhythm is counted with spoken syllables, then practised on written rhythms.
