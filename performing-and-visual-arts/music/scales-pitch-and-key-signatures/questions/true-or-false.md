# Scales, Pitch and Key Signatures — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A scale is the ordering of sounds in a sequence — a ladder of sounds.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The three kinds of minor scale are natural, harmonic and melodic.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Sounds on the F clef are low in pitch.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** The key-signature signs are sharp and flat.
**Answer: True**

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** Key signatures in Amharic are የቁልፍ ምልክቶች.
**Answer: True**
