# Video script — Scales, Pitch and Key Signatures

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A scale is a ladder of sounds.
Whole, whole, half — whole, whole, whole, half. That is major.
Count back three and the natural minor appears.
High pitch lives on the G clef. Low pitch lives on the F clef.
Sharps and flats at the staff's head keep the ladder true.
