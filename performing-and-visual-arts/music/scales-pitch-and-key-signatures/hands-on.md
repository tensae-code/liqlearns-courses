# Hands-on — Scales, Pitch and Key Signatures

The lesson with your hands, your feet and your voice together, not only your eyes.

## Ladder on the floor
Lay eight papers as ladder steps. Stand and sing: step, step, small step (bring the papers closer), step, step, step, small step. Your feet learn the major scale's shape.

## Two-clef singing
In two groups: group one sings a melody written on the G clef, group two one on the F clef — the book's own exercise — then report the pitch difference between the clefs.
