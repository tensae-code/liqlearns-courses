# Hands-on — The Dikanza Scraper

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The dikanza (reco-reco)
Work through this part with real materials or your own body, the way the book sets it: the dikanza, Angola's scraped idiophone (reco-reco), known by this name in Kimbundu and Cokwe — how it looks and how it is played: its sound comes from scraping, not from striking. Show a companion what you made or did, and say what the part teaches.
