# The Dikanza Scraper — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson — The Dikanza Scraper — opens with…
a) The dikanza (reco-reco)  b) an examination  c) nothing at all
**Answer: a) The dikanza (reco-reco).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The Angola book teaches this lesson as…
a) Angola's scraped idiophone, the dikanza (reco-reco)  b) a sports rule  c) a cooking recipe
**Answer: a) Angola's scraped idiophone, the dikanza (reco-reco).**

**3.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) The dikanza (reco-reco)  b) long division  c) map coordinates
**Answer: a) The dikanza (reco-reco).**

**4.** The parts of this lesson come from…
a) Angola's own Educação Manual e Plástica / Educação Musical books  b) a newspaper  c) a song chorus
**Answer: a) Angola's own books.**

**5.** "The dikanza (reco-reco)" is taught in this lesson as…
a) one of its named parts  b) an unrelated game  c) a spelling list
**Answer: a) one of its named parts.**
