# Video script — The Dikanza Scraper

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

**The dikanza (reco-reco).** The dikanza, Angola's scraped idiophone (reco-reco), known by this name in Kimbundu and Cokwe — how it looks and how it is played: its sound comes from scraping, not from striking.

That is The Dikanza Scraper — Angola's own books teach it this way.
