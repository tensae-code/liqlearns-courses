# Lesson plan — Contemporary Inuit Music

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Music · **Lesson:** Contemporary Inuit Music
**Place in the curriculum:** Music (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Nunavut's musicians today — many instruments, the internet's worldwide music, new things added to the old songs, and performers travelling the world.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this course in the same title.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Nunavut's musicians today** — **Traditional music is still important in Nunavut**, and today's musicians use **many different instruments**.
2. **New things on old songs** — Musicians **add new things to the old songs**; some **write their own songs**; some **mix Qallunaaq styles with katajjaq**.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of this course in the same title. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say what nunavut's musicians today teaches, in the book's own way.

A song the group knows, and anything that can add a new sound.

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks 'today's music' means the traditional music is finished → the book opens: traditional music is still important in Nunavut.
- Thinks mixing styles spoils katajjaq by definition → the book teaches the mixing as what today's musicians do — some mix Qallunaaq styles with katajjaq.
