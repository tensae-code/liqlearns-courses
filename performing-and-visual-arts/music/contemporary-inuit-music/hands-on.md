# Hands-on — Contemporary Inuit Music

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Old song, new thing
Take a song your group knows. Add one new thing — a new instrument sound, a new verse, a mixed-in style — and perform both versions.

## World stage
In pairs, introduce a Nunavut musician to an imaginary world audience: name the form, the instruments and what is new in it.
