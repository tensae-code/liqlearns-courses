# Contemporary Inuit Music

## Do it first

Quick game: before you read on, say what you already know about this lesson's work — its name, its look, its story. Then read and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — Nunavut's musicians today

**Traditional music is still important in Nunavut**, and today's musicians use **many different instruments**. The **internet changed Nunavut music** — Inuit can hear music from anywhere in the world.

In your mind's eye: today's Nunavut musicians — singers and players with many instruments, one mixing katajjaq with new styles.

## Sub lesson 2 — New things on old songs

Musicians **add new things to the old songs**; some **write their own songs**; some **mix Qallunaaq styles with katajjaq**. Nunavut singers and musicians now **travel all over the world to perform**.
