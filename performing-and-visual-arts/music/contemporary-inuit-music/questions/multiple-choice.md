# Contemporary Inuit Music — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Traditional music in Nunavut today is…
a) forgotten  b) still important  c) banned
**Answer: b) still important** — still important.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Today's musicians use…
a) one instrument only  b) many different instruments  c) no instruments
**Answer: b) many different instruments** — many different instruments.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The internet changed Nunavut music because…
a) it ended live music  b) Inuit can hear music from anywhere in the world  c) it sells drums
**Answer: b) Inuit can hear music from anywhere in the world** — music from anywhere in the world can now be heard.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Some musicians mix…
a) Qallunaaq styles with katajjaq  b) silence with noise  c) two radios
**Answer: a) Qallunaaq styles with katajjaq** — Qallunaaq styles with katajjaq.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Nunavut performers now…
a) never leave home  b) travel all over the world to perform  c) perform only for recordings
**Answer: b) travel all over the world to perform** — they travel all over the world to perform.
