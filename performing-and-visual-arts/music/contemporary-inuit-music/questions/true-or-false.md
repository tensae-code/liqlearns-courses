# Contemporary Inuit Music — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Today's Nunavut musicians use many different instruments.
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The internet had no effect on Nunavut music.
**Answer: False — the book says the internet changed Nunavut music.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Every musician today has dropped the old songs.
**Answer: False — musicians add new things to the old songs and keep them.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Nunavut singers perform around the world.
**Answer: True.**
