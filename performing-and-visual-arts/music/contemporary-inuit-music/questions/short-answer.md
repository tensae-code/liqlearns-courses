# Contemporary Inuit Music — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What two things do today's musicians do with the old songs?
**Answer: They add new things to them — and some write their own songs.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is mixed with katajjaq by some musicians?
**Answer: Qallunaaq styles.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** What can Inuit hear now, thanks to the internet?
**Answer: Music from anywhere in the world.**
