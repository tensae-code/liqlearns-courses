This is North Macedonia's own lesson.

**Народна песна.** Народна песна — the two Macedonian folk-song worlds: the izvorna (authentic) village song in uneven metres 7/8, 9/8, 11/8, 5/8, sung to tapan, gajda, kaval, zurla and dajre; and the starogradska (old-town) song of the čalgii — kemane, tambura, dajre, harmonica, bass and clarinet — to which townsfolk danced waltzes and ora.

That is Macedonian Folk Song: Izvorna and Starogradska — North Macedonia's way, kept by name.
