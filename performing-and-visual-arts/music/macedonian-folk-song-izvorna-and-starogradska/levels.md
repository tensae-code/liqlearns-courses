## Sub lesson 1 — Народна песна
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Народна песна — the two Macedonian folk-song worlds: the izvorna (authentic) village song in uneven metres 7/8, 9/8, 11/8, 5/8, sung to tapan, gajda, kaval, zurla and dajre; and the starogradska (old-town) song of the čalgii — kemane, tambura, dajre, harmonica, bass and clarinet — to which townsfolk danced waltzes and ora.
