# Macedonian Folk Song: Izvorna and Starogradska

## Do it first

Name one thing that belongs in this lesson — North Macedonia's folk song kinds — the izvorna (source) and starogradska (old-town) songs. Say what it is in your own words. If you can name one, you are already inside the lesson.

## Sub lesson 1 — Народна песна

Народна песна — the two Macedonian folk-song worlds: the izvorna (authentic) village song in uneven metres 7/8, 9/8, 11/8, 5/8, sung to tapan, gajda, kaval, zurla and dajre; and the starogradska (old-town) song of the čalgii — kemane, tambura, dajre, harmonica, bass and clarinet — to which townsfolk danced waltzes and ora.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson holds, part by part, in your own words. Companion checks: did every part get named, with its Macedonian name kept where the book gives one?

## Do It Yourself

1. Choose one named part of this lesson and find out one more true thing about it — from a person who knows it, or from the book your part came from.
2. Make or perform a small piece in the way of that part: a rhythm, a step, a drawing, a shaped piece of clay.
3. Write one sentence: what does this part show about North Macedonia's own way of teaching its arts?
