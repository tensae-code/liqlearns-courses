# Lesson plan — The Dombra and Kazakh Instruments

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Music · **Lesson:** The Dombra and Kazakh Instruments
**Place in the curriculum:** Music (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Kazakhstan's dombra and folk instruments — the dombra's build and sound, and the folk orchestra around it..
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this course in the same title.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Kazakhstan Grade 1 NIS Music** — the instruments of the Kazakh people met by sight and sound
2. **Kazakhstan Grade 2 Korolkova Art Labour** — the dombra made/pictured as the people's instrument
3. **Kazakhstan Grade 2 Valiullina Music, contents pp. 141-142** — dombra and the folk instruments met again in Grade 2
4. **Kazakhstan Grade 2 Gorchakova Music, contents** — the dombra and its family
5. **Kazakhstan Grade 2 NIS Music** — the Kazakh folk instruments
6. **Kazakhstan Grade 3 Adamkulov Labour** — the dombra's parts named on a diagram (neck, pegs, strings, soundboard) and a model dombra built
7. **Kazakhstan Grade 3 Valiullina Music RU** — two lessons on a young Kazakh dombra player and her art
8. **Kazakhstan Grade 3 Kulmanova Music KZ** — the ancient seven-string zhetygen among the Kazakh instruments (dombra, kobyz, sybyzgy, shankobyz, dauylpaz) and how each pictures nature
9. **Kazakhstan Grade 4 Labour Training (Ormanbekova)** — the dombra, Kazakh two-stringed plucked instrument: body, neck, head, pegs, strings and sound hole; Kurmangazy the great dombra player; plucked instruments of other peoples (guitar, balalaika, mandolin) compared by form
10. **Kazakhstan Grade 4 Labour Training (Muratkыzy)** — the dombra's full anatomy (frets, sound hole, strings, soundboard, stand, pegs, head, neck, body); rock drawings show its ancient origin; National Dombra Day in Kazakhstan
11. **Kazakhstan Grade 4 Music (Kulmanova)** — "I speak through the dombra": the instrument as the Kazakh's voice and fate
12. **Kazakhstan Grade 5 Artistic Labour (boys, Chukalin)** — what it teaches: the dombra — the most popular Kazakh instrument, kin to the plucked instruments of all Turkic peoples; finds near ancient Khorezm suggest a like instrument 2,000 years ago, and its prototype may be over 4,000 years old
13. **Kazakhstan Grade 6 Music (Musakhajaeva)** — what it teaches: a song in praise of the dombra, the instrument at the centre of Kazakh music
