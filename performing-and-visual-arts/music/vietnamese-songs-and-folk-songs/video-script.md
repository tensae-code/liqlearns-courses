This lesson is Vietnamese Songs and Folk Songs.

Vietnam's songs — Beloved Vietnam and Tổ quốc ta, its national anthem, and its folk songs from Xúc xắc xúc xẻ to Gà gáy. — as Vietnam's school books teach it: The song "Tổ quốc ta" (Mộng Lân) — a child's praise of the fatherland's green rice fields, high forests and blue sea, one land from North to South; Vietnam's National Anthem (Văn Cao) heard at the flag-saluting ceremony, standing to attention under the red flag with its yellow star; the song "Xúc xắc xúc xẻ" — a Tết (Lunar New Year) children's rhyme (đồng dao) about going house to house at New Year, set to music by Nguyễn Ngọc Thiện; the song "Gà gáy" — a folk song of the Cống Khao people (new words by Huy Trân): the rooster crows at dawn and calls everyone up to the bright terraced fields; "Lí cây bông", a lí folk song of the South (dân ca Nam Bộ) that counts and praises the flowers — green, white and yellow blossoms.

Watch, listen — then say it back in your own words.
