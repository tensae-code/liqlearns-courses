# Video script — Ethiopian Traditional Music and Its History

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Traditional music bears a people's identity. No single owner — the society itself is the author.
Saint Yared gave church music its three styles and its signs.
North, east, south: one country, many musics.
The bone flute is 30,000 years old. The first written song is carved on a stone.
Strings sing, winds breathe, drums speak, shaken instruments shimmer.
