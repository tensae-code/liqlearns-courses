# Hands-on — Ethiopian Traditional Music and Its History

The lesson with your hands, your feet and your voice together, not only your eyes.

## Region presentations
In groups, each group presents and performs one region's music, as the guide's activity directs: northern, eastern or southern — a song, its occasion, and one instrument of the region.

## Instrument family sorting
Lay out pictures or real instruments. Sort them into strings, winds, drums and shaken instruments. Defend each placing in one sentence: how does its sound begin?
