# Ethiopian Traditional Music and Its History — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Who is the author of a folk song, in the book's teaching?
  a) The society itself b) One named composer c) The printing house
**Answer: a) The society itself** — folk music has no individual creator ownership — the society itself is the author.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Saint Yared's three melodic styles are…
  a) Ge'ez, Ezil and Araray b) Tizta, Bati and Ambassel c) Andante, Moderato and Allegro
**Answer: a) Ge'ez, Ezil and Araray** — Ge'ez, Ezil and Araray are Yared's three church-music styles.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The Seikilos Epitaph is…
  a) The oldest complete surviving composition, engraved on a gravestone b) A modern pop song c) A kind of drum
**Answer: a) The oldest complete surviving composition, engraved on a gravestone** — it is the 1st-century AD gravestone song from Turkey.

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** The negarit's old role was…
  a) Sending messages b) Cooking food c) Measuring land
**Answer: a) Sending messages** — the guide's quiz remembers the negarit as a message-sending instrument.
