# Ethiopian Traditional Music and Its History — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name two branches of sacred traditional music.
**Answer: Any two of: Christian Ge'ez/Ezil/Araray chants; Islamic manzuma/neshida/baro; indigenous-religion irecha/magano; folk-belief music.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which region's secular song reflects church melody, per the guide?
**Answer: The northern region.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What is the oldest drum the guide names, and what is it made of?
**Answer: China's Neolithic Alligator Drum — clay and alligator hide.**
