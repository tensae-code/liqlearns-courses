# Ethiopian Traditional Music and Its History — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name two branches of sacred traditional music.
**Answer: Any two of: Christian Ge'ez/Ezil/Araray chants; Islamic manzuma/neshida/baro; indigenous-religion irecha/magano; folk-belief music.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which region's secular song reflects church melody, per the guide?
**Answer: The northern region.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What is the oldest drum the guide names, and what is it made of?
**Answer: China's Neolithic Alligator Drum — clay and alligator hide.**

**4. (Sub lesson 5)** Name the second founder-life the book sets beside Ashenafi Kebede.
**Answer:** Beshah Tekle Mariam.

**5. (Sub lesson 6)** What does Grade 7 add on the chant types?
**Answer:** Their characters — the Ge'ez, Ezil and Araray manners distinguished by weight and movement (p. 91, with pp. 92–94).

**6. (Sub lesson 7)** Which lesson teaches this same meeting from the instruments' side?
**Answer:** Modern Instruments (the Grade 8 lesson).

**7. (Sub lesson 8)** Where is indigenous knowledge expressed, in Chapter 3's last aim?
**Answer:** In theatre and film.

**8. (Sub lesson 9)** What does one of the 14 closing-exercise questions ask about the largest Axum monument?
**Answer:** Its length in metres.

**9. (Sub lesson 9)** Local histories give great benefit to which two arts, per the summary?
**Answer:** Theatre and film.
