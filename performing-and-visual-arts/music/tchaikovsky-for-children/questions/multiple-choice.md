# Tchaikovsky for Children — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Tchaikovsky is introduced as…
a) a painter  b) a composer  c) a dancer
**Answer: b) a composer**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** "The Doll's Illness" comes from…
a) the Children's Album  b) an opera  c) a march book
**Answer: a) the Children's Album**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The Waltz of the Snowflakes is in the ballet…
a) Swan Lake  b) The Nutcracker (Щелкунчик)  c) Sleeping Beauty
**Answer: b) The Nutcracker (Щелкунчик)**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Which piece marches wooden soldiers?
a) Song of the Lark  b) March of the Wooden Soldiers  c) Waltz of the Snowflakes
**Answer: b) March of the Wooden Soldiers**
