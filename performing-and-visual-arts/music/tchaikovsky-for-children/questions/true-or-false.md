# Tchaikovsky for Children — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Children's Album pieces in the book include the Song of the Lark.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The Waltz of the Snowflakes is a song to sing, not ballet music.
**Answer: False** — it is from the ballet The Nutcracker.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The book sets Tchaikovsky's music both to listen to and to play.
**Answer: True**
