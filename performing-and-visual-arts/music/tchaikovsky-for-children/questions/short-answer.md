# Tchaikovsky for Children — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name Tchaikovsky's full name as the book gives it.
**Answer: Pyotr Ilyich Tchaikovsky (Пётр Ильич Чайковский).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What weather does the Nutcracker waltz picture?
**Answer: Falling snow — the Waltz of the Snowflakes.**
