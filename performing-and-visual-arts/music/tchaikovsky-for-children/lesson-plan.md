# Lesson plan — Tchaikovsky for Children

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Music · **Lesson:** Tchaikovsky for Children
**Place in the curriculum:** Music (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Pyotr Ilyich Tchaikovsky introduced to children — his Children's Album pieces and the Waltz of the Snowflakes from The Nutcracker, to listen to and to play.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this course.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The composer and his Children's Album** — Tchaikovsky met as a composer for children, with "Болезнь куклы" (The Doll's Illness), "Марш деревянных солдатиков" (March of the Wooden Soldiers) and "Песнь жаворонка" (Song of the Lark).
2. **The Nutcracker's snowflakes** — the "Вальс снежных хлопьев" (Waltz of the Snowflakes) from the ballet "Щелкунчик" (The Nutcracker).

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier Music lessons of this course. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name the three Children's Album pieces heard.
2. Say which ballet the Waltz of the Snowflakes comes from.
