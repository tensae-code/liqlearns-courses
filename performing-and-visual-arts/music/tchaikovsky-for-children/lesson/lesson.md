# Tchaikovsky for Children

## Do it first

Say what you already know about this lesson's subject — one thing you have seen, heard or done — before you read on.

## Sub lesson 1 — The composer and his Children's Album

The Russian-track book introduces **Pyotr Ilyich Tchaikovsky** to children as a composer whose music they can listen to and play:

- **"Болезнь куклы" (The Doll's Illness)** — from the **Children's Album**.
- **"Марш деревянных солдатиков" (March of the Wooden Soldiers)**.
- **"Песнь жаворонка" (Song of the Lark)**.

Each piece is small, pictured in sound, and made to be heard closely by a child.

## Sub lesson 2 — The Nutcracker's snowflakes

From Tchaikovsky's ballet **"Щелкунчик" (The Nutcracker)** the book sets the **"Вальс снежных хлопьев" (Waltz of the Snowflakes)** — snow falling and turning in a waltz, heard as dance music.
