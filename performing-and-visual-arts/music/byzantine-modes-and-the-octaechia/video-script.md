This lesson is Byzantine Modes and the Octaechia.

The eight-mode Octaechia system — Mode I to the Grave mode and their plagals, diatonic, chromatic and enharmonic forms. — as Greece's school books teach it: interval and tone and the polychordo; the echoi (modes) and the genera, the diatonic genus first; what an echos is made of and its martyria; the kinds of psalmody and of melopoiia, the short heirmologic kind.

Watch, listen — then say it back in your own words.
