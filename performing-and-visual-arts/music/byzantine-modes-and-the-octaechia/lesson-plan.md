# Lesson plan — Byzantine Modes and the Octaechia

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Music · **Lesson:** Byzantine Modes and the Octaechia
**Place in the curriculum:** Music (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The eight-mode Octaechia system — Mode I to the Grave mode and their plagals, diatonic, chromatic and enharmonic forms.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this course in the same title.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Α΄ Γυμνασίου Ενότητες 19-22: διάστημα, τόνος, ήχοι και γένη (Intervals, tone, echoi and genera)** — interval and tone and the polychordo; the echoi (modes) and the genera, the diatonic genus first; what an echos is made of and its martyria; the kinds of psalmody and of melopoiia, the short heirmologic kind
2. **Β΄ Γυμνασίου Ενότητες 3-8: ήχοι πλ. α΄ και δ΄, σύντομο ειρμολογικό είδος (Echoi plagal 1st and 4th, short heirmologic)** — each echos' theory, its theseis (cadence formulas) and its short heirmologic melodies; the attractions (elxeis); argon, diargon, triargon
3. **Β΄ Γυμνασίου Ενότητες 9-13: ήχος πλ. δ΄ (Echos plagal 4th in depth)** — plagal 4th theory, theseis and melodies, also based on Ga; telling oligon, petasti and kentimata apart; musical accentuation of the text
4. **Β΄ Γυμνασίου Ενότητες 14-19: εναρμόνιο γένος, ήχος βαρύς, ήχος γ΄ (Enharmonic genus, barys and 3rd echoi)** — the enharmonic genus, its phthora and martyriai; the barys echos' theory, theseis and melodies; permanent hyphesis and diesis; the 3rd echos
5. **Β΄ Γυμνασίου Ενότητες 20-22: χρωματικό γένος, ήχος πλ. β΄ (Chromatic genus, plagal 2nd)** — the chromatic genus, the intervals of the 2nd echos, and the plagal 2nd's short heirmologic melodies
6. **Γ΄ Γυμνασίου Ενότητες 1-3: χρωματικό γένος — ήχοι β΄ και πλ. β΄ (Chromatic genus deepened)** — the chromatic genus' intervals, martyriai and phthorai of plagal 2nd, and the 2nd echos' theory, theseis and short heirmologic melodies
7. **Α΄ Λυκείου Μάθ. 3: Το Σύστημα της Οκταηχίας (The Octoechos system)** — the eight echoi and how the system turns through the weeks
8. **Α΄ Λυκείου: Ο α΄ ήχος, ο πλ. α΄ και ο πλ. δ΄ ήχος (Echos 1, Plagal 1 and Plagal 4, basic features and practice)** — the first echoi learned whole — their marks and their melodies
9. **Β΄ Λυκείου Μάθ. 1–15: Οι ήχοι δ΄, γ΄, βαρύς, πλ. β΄ και β΄ (Echoi 4, 3, Grave, Plagal 2 and 2)** — the remaining echoi, each in graded lessons
10. **Α΄ Gymnasium Lessons 19–24: intervals and the echoi (modes) — the genera (diatonic first), the components and martyria of a mode, kinds of psalmody and melopoiia, and Mode I in its short heirmologic form, theory and theseis** — The eight-mode Octaechia system — Mode I to the Grave mode and their plagals, diatonic, chromatic and enharmonic forms.
11. **Β΄ Gymnasium Lessons 1–13: phthorai (alterations) and parachorde; the diatonic phthorai; Plagal Mode I and Mode IV in short heirmologic form — theory, theseis and melodies, with the argia (argon/diargon/triargon)** — The eight-mode Octaechia system — Mode I to the Grave mode and their plagals, diatonic, chromatic and enharmonic forms.
12. **Β΄ Gymnasium Lessons 14–19: Plagal Mode IV on Ga, the enharmonic genus and its phthora and martyriai; the Grave (Varys) mode and Mode III, short heirmologic — theory, theseis and melodies** — The eight-mode Octaechia system — Mode I to the Grave mode and their plagals, diatonic, chromatic and enharmonic forms.
13. **Β΄ Gymnasium Lessons 20–24 and Γ΄ Gymnasium opening: the chromatic genus, the intervals of Mode II, Plagal Mode II in short heirmologic form — theory, theseis, melodies and special melodies** — The eight-mode Octaechia system — Mode I to the Grave mode and their plagals, diatonic, chromatic and enharmonic forms.
14. **Α΄ Lyceum Lesson 3: the system of the Octaechia (the eight modes together)** — The eight-mode Octaechia system — Mode I to the Grave mode and their plagals, diatonic, chromatic and enharmonic forms.
15. **Α΄ Lyceum Lessons 4, 11–19: Mode I — its basic characteristics and four graded sets; Plagal Mode I in five graded sets** — The eight-mode Octaechia system — Mode I to the Grave mode and their plagals, diatonic, chromatic and enharmonic forms.
16. **Α΄ Lyceum Lessons 20–24: Plagal Mode IV in five graded sets** — The eight-mode Octaechia system — Mode I to the Grave mode and their plagals, diatonic, chromatic and enharmonic forms.
17. **Β΄ Lyceum Lessons 1–12+: Mode IV (four sets), Mode III (two sets), the Grave mode (two sets), Plagal Mode II (three sets) and Mode II** — The eight-mode Octaechia system — Mode I to the Grave mode and their plagals, diatonic, chromatic and enharmonic forms.
