# Byzantine Modes and the Octaechia — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part is "Α΄ Γυμνασίου Ενότητες 19-22: διάστημα, τόνος, ήχοι και γένη (Intervals, tone, echoi and genera)".
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson was invented with no school book behind it.
**Answer: False** — it is stocked from Greek school books, with printed pages named in the sources.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** "Β΄ Γυμνασίου Ενότητες 3-8: ήχοι πλ. α΄ και δ΄, σύντομο ειρμολογικό είδος (Echoi plagal 1st and 4th, short heirmologic)" is a part of this lesson.
**Answer: True.**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Songs and stories in this course are transcribed in full.
**Answer: False** — they are placed by summary only.
