# Byzantine Modes and the Octaechia

## Sub lesson 1 — Α΄ Γυμνασίου Ενότητες 19-22: διάστημα, τόνος, ήχοι και γένη (Intervals, tone, echoi and genera)

interval and tone and the polychordo; the echoi (modes) and the genera, the diatonic genus first; what an echos is made of and its martyria; the kinds of psalmody and of melopoiia, the short heirmologic kind

## Sub lesson 2 — Β΄ Γυμνασίου Ενότητες 3-8: ήχοι πλ. α΄ και δ΄, σύντομο ειρμολογικό είδος (Echoi plagal 1st and 4th, short heirmologic)

each echos' theory, its theseis (cadence formulas) and its short heirmologic melodies; the attractions (elxeis); argon, diargon, triargon

## Sub lesson 3 — Β΄ Γυμνασίου Ενότητες 9-13: ήχος πλ. δ΄ (Echos plagal 4th in depth)

plagal 4th theory, theseis and melodies, also based on Ga; telling oligon, petasti and kentimata apart; musical accentuation of the text

## Sub lesson 4 — Β΄ Γυμνασίου Ενότητες 14-19: εναρμόνιο γένος, ήχος βαρύς, ήχος γ΄ (Enharmonic genus, barys and 3rd echoi)

## Sub lesson 5 — Β΄ Γυμνασίου Ενότητες 20-22: χρωματικό γένος, ήχος πλ. β΄ (Chromatic genus, plagal 2nd)

the chromatic genus, the intervals of the 2nd echos, and the plagal 2nd's short heirmologic melodies

## Sub lesson 6 — Γ΄ Γυμνασίου Ενότητες 1-3: χρωματικό γένος — ήχοι β΄ και πλ. β΄ (Chromatic genus deepened)
