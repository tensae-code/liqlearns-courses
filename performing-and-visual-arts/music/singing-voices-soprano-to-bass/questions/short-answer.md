# Singing Voices: Soprano to Bass — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the four singing voices the book meets.
**Answer: Soprano, baritone, tenor and bass (met through Mother Goat, Figaro, the Trout and the Marmot).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which voice sings Mother Goat's lullaby?
**Answer: The soprano.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the book say differs from voice to voice?
**Answer: Their colour — what each voice can paint.**
