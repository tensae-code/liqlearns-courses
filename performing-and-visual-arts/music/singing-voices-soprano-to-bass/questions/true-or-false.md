# Singing Voices: Soprano to Bass — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The bass in the book is heard in Beethoven's "The Marmot".
**Answer: True**.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The soprano is the lowest singing voice.
**Answer: False** — the soprano is high; the bass is lowest.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Each singing voice has its own colour.
**Answer: True**.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Figaro's cavatina is by Schubert.
**Answer: False** — it is Mozart's; Schubert's piece here is "The Trout".
