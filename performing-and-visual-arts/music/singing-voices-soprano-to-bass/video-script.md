# Video script — Singing Voices: Soprano to Bass

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Four voices sing.

High and bright — the **soprano**, Mother Goat singing her little ones to sleep.
The **tenor** — Schubert's trout darting in the stream.
The **baritone** — Figaro, quick and clever.
Low and deep — the **bass**, Beethoven's marmot.

Every voice is a colour. Composers paint with them.
