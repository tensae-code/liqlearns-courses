# Hands-on — Singing Voices: Soprano to Bass

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## High, middle, low
Sing one short phrase four ways: as high and bright as you can (soprano side), in the middle, and as low and deep as you can (bass side). Say which colour fits a lullaby, and which fits a giant.

## Voice detective
Listen to any four singers — in class recordings or at home — and sort them: highest, two middles, lowest. Match them to the book's four voices.
