# Singing Voices: Soprano to Bass

## Do it first

Before you read: say or sketch what you already know about this lesson's subject — the singing voices met one by one — soprano, baritone, tenor and bass — how each voice's colour differs and what it can paint. Start there; the lesson builds on it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Four voices, four colours

The Grade 2 book meets the singing voices one by one, each inside a famous piece:

- **Tenor** — in Schubert's "The Trout".
- **Bass** — in Beethoven's "The Marmot".
- **Soprano** — in Mother Goat's lullaby (Koval's opera "The Wolf and the Seven Little Goats").
- **Baritone** — in Figaro's cavatina (Mozart).

Each voice has its own **colour**: the soprano high and bright, the bass low and deep, the tenor and baritone between them. A composer chooses the voice the way a painter chooses a colour — for what it can paint.
