# Music for the Stage: 'Defying Gravity' — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Wicked was written by…
a) Stephen Schwartz  b) John Williams  c) Baden Powell
**Answer: a) Stephen Schwartz**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Wicked first appeared in…
a) 2003  b) 1903  c) 2023
**Answer: a) 2003**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** 'Defying Gravity' ends…
a) Act 1  b) the overture  c) Act 5
**Answer: a) Act 1**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The song is a duet for Elphaba and…
a) Glinda  b) the Wizard  c) the ensemble only
**Answer: a) Glinda**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** In the song, Elphaba resolves to…
a) fight the Wizard and live without limits  b) go home quietly  c) give up flying
**Answer: a) fight the Wizard and live without limits**
