# Music for the Stage: 'Defying Gravity' — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** 'Defying Gravity' is the Act 1 finale of Wicked.
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Only one singer sings in 'Defying Gravity'.
**Answer: False — it is a duet, with the ensemble joining at the end.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Wicked retells the Oz story from the witches' point of view.
**Answer: True.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Elphaba decides to obey the Wizard's limits.
**Answer: False — she resolves to live without limits.**
