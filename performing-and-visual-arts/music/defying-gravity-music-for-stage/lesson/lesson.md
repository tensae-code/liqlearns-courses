# Music for the Stage: 'Defying Gravity'

## Do it first

Quick game: before you read on, say what you already know about this lesson's work — its name, its sound, its story. Then read and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — Wicked and its Act 1 finale

**Stephen Schwartz's Wicked (2003)** retells the Oz story from the witches' point of view. Its **Act 1 finale, 'Defying Gravity'**, is a **duet for Elphaba and Glinda**, with the **ensemble joining at the end**.

## Sub lesson 2 — Elphaba's resolve

In the song **Elphaba resolves to fight the Wizard and live without limits** — the decision that ends Act 1 and changes everything that follows.
