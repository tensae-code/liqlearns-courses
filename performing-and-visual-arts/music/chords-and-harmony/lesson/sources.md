Аккордтар мен аккорд символдары (Chords and chord symbols) — what it teaches: a chord (аккорд) is a combination of three or more musical sounds taken together, of different pitch; a chord of three sounds is called a triad; a chord of four sounds — квартты аккорд (quartal chord); chords' most complex type is the септаккорд (seventh chord); classical composers use three-sound chords in traditional compositions; in classical music the most used chords are built from the scale's degrees: тоника (T) — I degree, субдоминанта (S) — IV degree, доминанта (D) — V degree; research: any chords can be gathered into major or minor chords; usually major chords sound bright and joyful, minor chords gloomy and sad; research the concept «аккорд» and write its definition; what types of chord are there?; where are chords often used?; how can chords be invented?; draw the conclusion of your research: what do you know about chords?; play varied chords on a musical instrument; perform the chords you invented: invent several chords — the C major scale consists of seven sounds — C, D, E, F, G, A, B; music born from the C major scale starts from the note C; 1. choose the note C and varied types (for example tonic, subdominant, dominant), try to take out chords; 2. write the chords in notation; 3. play these chords on the synthesizer — what do you hear?; describe the invented chords' peculiarities in writing; 4. understand the concept «рок аккорды» (rock chords) and answer the next questions: why is this chord called a «rock chord»?; in which musical genres is this chord used?;
