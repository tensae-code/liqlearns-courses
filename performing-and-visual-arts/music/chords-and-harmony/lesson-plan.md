# Lesson plan — Chords and Harmony

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Music · **Lesson:** Chords and Harmony
**Place in the curriculum:** Music (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Chords, triads and chord symbols — how harmony is built from primary chords and how melody and harmony fit together.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the course's earlier music lessons — recommended first, never forced.
