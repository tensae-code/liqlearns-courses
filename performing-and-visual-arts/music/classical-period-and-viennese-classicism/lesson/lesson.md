# Classical Period and Viennese Classicism

## Do it first

Name one thing that belongs in this lesson — Viennese Classicism — Haydn, Mozart and Beethoven — music of balance, clarity and public concerts, and the lives of its great composers.. Say what it is in your own words. If you can name one, you are already inside the lesson.

## Sub lesson 1 — Lesson 16 "The Miracle Child"

expressing the story of the music, then learning the life of the musician Mozart, the child prodigy, filling the pencil-icon blanks of his story.

## Sub lesson 2 — Lesson 18 "Remnants of War"

listening to the music called "Fate Knocks at the Door" (Beethoven's Fifth).

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson holds, part by part, in your own words. Companion checks: did every part get named, with its Syrian name kept where the book gives one?

## Do It Yourself

1. Choose one named part of this lesson and find out one more true thing about it — from a person who knows it, or from the book your part came from.
2. Make or perform a small piece in the way of that part: a rhythm, a step, a drawing, a shaped piece of clay.
3. Write one sentence: what does this part show about Syria's own way of teaching its arts?
