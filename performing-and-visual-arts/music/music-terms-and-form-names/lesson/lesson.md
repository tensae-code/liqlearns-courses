## Sub lesson 1 — China HS Music Theory

The working vocabulary of the concert hall in three shelves — the expression words (some eighty Italian terms from Accarezzante to Zeffiroso, each with its Chinese meaning: tender, agitated, singing, sorrowful, majestic, playful…); the performance and voice-part words (Solo, Divisi, Unison, Arco, Pizzicato, Saltando, Staccato, and the voice family Soprano to Bass with coloratura and mezzo between); and the names of musical forms — Aria, Cadenza, Ballade, Coda, Concerto, Fantasia, Fugue, Intermezzo, Invention, Menuet, Overture, Prelude, Rhapsody, Romance, Rondo, Scherzo, Serenata, Sonata, Sonatina, Symphony, Tango, Toccata, Variation, Waltz
