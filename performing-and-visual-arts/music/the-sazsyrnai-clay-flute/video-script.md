This lesson is The Sazsyrnai Clay Flute.

The Sazsyrnai Clay Flute — Kazakhstan's sazsyrnai — a small clay wind instrument. — as Kazakhstan's school books teach it: the sazsyrnai, the ancient Kazakh clay wind instrument, an embodiment of the four elements; compared with the Russian svistulka and the Italian ocarina; clay as a raw material.

Watch, listen — then say it back in your own words.
