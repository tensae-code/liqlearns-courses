# Renaissance Music — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson teaches...
a) another country's arts only  b) Renaissance music — balance, clarity and equal-voice polyphony, the mass, motet and madrigal, and Palestrina its master  c) nothing — it is just names
**Answer: b)** — that is the lesson's one thing.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The first sub lesson is...
a) the Renaissance  b) an examination  c) a song from another continent
**Answer: a) the Renaissance.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** North Macedonia's own forms in this lesson are...
a) dropped and replaced  b) kept as named parts  c) hidden
**Answer: b) kept as named parts.**

**4. (Sub lesson 5)** The last sub lesson is...
a) "Audite nova!"  b) the bibliography  c) a review box
**Answer: a) "Audite nova!".**

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** Songs in this course are placed...
a) with full lyrics copied  b) by title and theme only, never transcribed  c) not at all
