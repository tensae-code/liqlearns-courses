Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson teaches...
a) another country's arts only  b) Lullabies — Armenia's own teaching of this topic, kept as named parts: Lullabies (Օրորներ): "Oror" (folk, words N. Mikhayelyan), "Buri" (folk) and Ohanyan's "Qir, qir" — the cradle-song kind: slow, soft, rocking a child to sleep  c) nothing — it is just names

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The first sub lesson is...

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Armenia's own forms in this lesson are...

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** The last sub lesson is...
