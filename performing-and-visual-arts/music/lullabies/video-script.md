# Video script — Lullabies

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This is Armenia's own lesson.

**Lullabies (Օրորներ).** Lullabies (Օրորներ): "Oror" (folk, words N. Mikhayelyan), "Buri" (folk) and Ohanyan's "Qir, qir" — the cradle-song kind: slow, soft, rocking a child to sleep.

That is Lullabies — Armenia's way, kept by name.
