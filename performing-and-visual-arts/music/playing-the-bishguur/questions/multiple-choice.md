# Playing the Bishguur — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** The bishguur is Mongolia's own…
a) end-blown flute  b) drum  c) string instrument
**Answer: a) end-blown flute**

**2.** Children begin playing the bishguur in…
a) Grade 3  b) Grade 8  c) Grade 1 only, never again
**Answer: a) Grade 3**
