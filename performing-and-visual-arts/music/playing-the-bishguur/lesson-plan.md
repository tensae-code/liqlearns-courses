# Lesson plan — Playing the Bishguur

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Music · **Lesson:** Playing the Bishguur
**Place in the curriculum:** Music (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** how to play music on the bishguur — Mongolia's own end-blown flute, begun in Grade 3.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the learner's earlier art and music lessons. Recommended, never forced.
