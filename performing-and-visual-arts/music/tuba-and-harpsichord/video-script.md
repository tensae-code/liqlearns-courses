This lesson is The Tuba and the Harpsichord.

The tuba, largest and lowest of the brass, its mouthpiece, coiled tubes, valves and great bell labelled, its sound made by buzzing lips; and the harpsichord, the piano's ancestor of the 15th–18th centuries, whose strings are plucked by a plectrum when a key is pressed — the instrument of Bach and Vivaldi.

Watch, listen — then say it back in your own words.
