# Video script — Anthems and Songs That Teach

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Our school has a song. Our region has an anthem. Our country has an anthem.
Stand straight for the anthem. It sings of honour, freedom, equality.
Elders were yesterday's teachers. Honour them and listen.
Plant the seedling. Water it. The forest song is a task, not only a tune.
