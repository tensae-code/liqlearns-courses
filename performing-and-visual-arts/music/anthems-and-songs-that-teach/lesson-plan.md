# Lesson plan — Anthems and Songs That Teach

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Music · **Lesson:** Anthems and Songs That Teach
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 3 — Music (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the songs a community sings about itself — our school song, the heritage song, the Amhara regional anthem and the Ethiopian national anthem, the elders' counsel, songs for professionals and for the forests, the festival songs of the year, and the songs that teach ethics, cleanliness and care for the earth.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Songs of Our Country and Traditional and Festival Songs — the learner already sings of country, flag and festivals; this lesson adds the anthems themselves and the teaching songs of the Grade 4–5 books. Songs are named and their messages taught; no song or anthem text is transcribed.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved (the gate: README, "Units, Lessons and Sub Lessons (ruled 2026-11-08)"):

1. **Our School and Protect Our Heritage** — "ትምህርት ቤታችን" (Our School) praises the school compound and teaches caring for it; the heritage song guards the nation's wealth and identity marks, naming Aksum, Lalibela, Sof Omar, Jegol and the Fasil castles (Grade 4, printed pp. 8–9 and 49).
2. **Our Region's Anthem and Our Country's Anthem** — the Amhara regional anthem "የታታሪ ህዝቦች" (The Diligent Peoples); the Ethiopian national anthem — citizenship's honour, peoples' freedom and equality (Grade 4, printed pp. 55 and 61–62).
3. **Honour the Elders: The Elders' Counsel** — "የአረጋውያን መዝሙር" (The Elders' Counsel) — a praise song with azmari refrains: elders' counsel is sweet as honey; honour them and listen (Grade 4, printed pp. 60–61).
4. **There Are Professionals; Protect the Forests** — the professionals song — every kebele and woreda served by its makers, all professionals equal in their work; the forest song — plant seedlings everywhere and make planting a culture (Grade 4, printed pp. 74–75 and 90–91).
5. **Festival songs of the year** — the year's festival music — New Year Enqutatash, the Gurage Meskel with its 'eyohe', Genna with its sticks, and Eid al-Fitr's sharing and menzuma (Grade 5, printed pp. 99–101).

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Songs of Our Country and Traditional and Festival Songs. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say what the school song praises and asks.
2. Name the heritage places the heritage song guards: Aksum, Lalibela, Sof Omar, Jegol, the Fasil castles.
3. Say what a regional anthem and a national anthem are, and how the national anthem is honoured standing.
4. Summarise the elders' counsel: what elders were, and why they are honoured.
5. Name the teaching songs: professionals, forests, festival songs of the year, ethics and cleanliness.

## What you need
Your voice and standing posture for the anthems; for the forest song's work, a seedling if the school is planting.

## If the learner is stuck
- Mixes the two anthems → the regional anthem is the Amhara region's ('The Diligent Peoples'); the national anthem is Ethiopia's — citizenship's honour, peoples' freedom and equality.
- Forgets the heritage places → sing them as a list to the heritage song's rhythm: Aksum, Lalibela, Sof Omar, Jegol, Fasil.
