# Anthems and Songs That Teach — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The heritage song names the Sof Omar caves among the places to guard.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The national anthem sings of peoples' freedom and equality.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Elders were yesterday's teachers and boundary-keepers.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** The forest song commands making planting a culture.
**Answer: True**

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** Enqutatash singers announce the year wishing peace and love.
**Answer: True**
