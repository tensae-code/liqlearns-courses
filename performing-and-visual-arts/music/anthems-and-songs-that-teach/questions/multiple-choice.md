# Anthems and Songs That Teach — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The school song teaches caring for the compound by…
  a) Watering plants, working together, keeping it beautiful b) Fencing it and keeping pupils out c) Painting it once and never again
**Answer: a) Watering plants, working together, keeping it beautiful** — watering plants, working together, keeping it beautiful for the next generation.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** "The Diligent Peoples" (የታታሪ ህዝቦች) is…
  a) The Amhara regional anthem b) The national anthem c) A wedding song
**Answer: a) The Amhara regional anthem** — it is the Amhara regional state's people's anthem.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Elders' counsel is compared to…
  a) Honey — it is sweet b) A stone c) A drum
**Answer: a) Honey — it is sweet** — the song says elders' counsel is sweet as honey.

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Which of these does the professionals song name?
  a) The weaver's gabi and netela b) The pilot's aeroplane c) The surgeon's hospital
**Answer: a) The weaver's gabi and netela** — carpenter, weaver, blacksmith and builder are the song's makers.

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** At Genna, the young men hold…
  a) Bent gena sticks and hold contests b) Umbrellas c) Paint brushes
**Answer: a) Bent gena sticks and hold contests** — Genna is played and celebrated with bent gena sticks and contests.

**6. (Sub lesson 6)** “Greetings — The Music Man” is…
  a) The book's one English song, printed as a score b) An Amharic anthem c) A counting song
**Answer: a) The book's one English song, printed as a score** — Grade 5, p. 123 — English, with its music, as a score image.

**7. (Sub lesson 6)** Its purpose in the book is…
  a) A language link — music carrying English greeting practice b) Replacing Amharic lessons c) Testing handwriting
**Answer: a) A language link — music carrying English greeting practice** — greetings sung are greetings remembered.
