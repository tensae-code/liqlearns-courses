# Anthems and Songs That Teach — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Heritages are called the nation's what?
**Answer: Wealth and identity marks.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How is the national anthem sung at school?
**Answer: Standing — with the honoured posture, from its first note to its last.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the song command us to do about elders?
**Answer: Honour them and listen, to be fruitful.**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** What does the Grade 5 forest song call the forest?
**Answer: Medicine for hunger — and the base of building, tools and soil protection.**

**5. (Sub lesson 6)** How is this song placed in the lesson, and why?
**Answer:** By name and purpose only — songs are never transcribed in this course.
