# Hands-on — Anthems and Songs That Teach

The lesson with your hands, your feet and your voice together, not only your eyes.

## Standing for the anthem
Practise the anthem posture the video script names: stand, still, facing the flag, from the first note to the last — when and how the anthem is sung at school.

## The forest song at work
Sing the forest song while planting: plant seedlings, water and tend them, as the song commands — make planting a culture, the song's own words for its work.
