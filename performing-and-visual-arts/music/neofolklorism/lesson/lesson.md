## Sub lesson 1 — Neofolklorism and Béla Bartók (1881–1945): folk music reworked, not quoted

Neofolklorism and Béla Bartók (1881–1945): folk music reworked, not quoted — the composer-ethnomusicologist who travelled the villages of Hungary, Romania and the Balkans recording peasants' songs on the phonograph, and built modern music on their asymmetric rhythms (5/8, 7/8, 9/8) and modal scales — "Mikrokosmos", "Allegro barbaro", the Romanian Folk Dances.
