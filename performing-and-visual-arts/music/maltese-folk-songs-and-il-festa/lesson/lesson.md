# Maltese Folk Songs and Il-Festa

## Do it first

Quick start: say one thing you already know — a sound, a song, a name — that belongs to this lesson's world. Keep it in mind; the parts will meet it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Il-Festa — the Joseph Vella song (Year 3)

Malta's own festa song in Maltese, **'Il-Festa'** (music **Joseph Vella**, words **Brother Henry**, from *Tfal Maltin Inkantaw*), tells the village feast day through its **three sounds**: **church bells, fireworks and the marching band** — each verse imitates its sound. Charles Camilleri's *Kanti Popolari* is named as further Maltese song repertoire. The song is placed here by summary only.

## Sub lesson 2 — Folk songs and Camilleri's Il-Festa (Year 6)

A **folk song** is a people's traditions and customs in song — its rhythm and melody give away its country — passed down the generations, or composed in folk colours, as **Charles Camilleri** did. His **'Il-Festa'** (words **Joe Friggieri**) paints the Maltese village festa: flags and banners, fireworks, Maltese food and ice-cream, the brass band, and families meeting. It is a **different 'Il-Festa'** from the Joseph Vella song above. Placed by summary only.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the parts in order, one sentence each. Companion checks: did every part get its sentence, and did the order hold?
