# Video script — Maltese Folk Songs and Il-Festa

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

It is the village **festa**!

First — the **church bells**. Then — **fireworks**! Then — the **marching band**!

Malta sings its feast day in its own language, in its own songs.
That is **Il-Festa** — the song of the Maltese village feast.
