# Maltese Folk Songs and Il-Festa — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The three sounds of the festa in the Joseph Vella song are…
a) bells, fireworks and the marching band  b) rain, wind and sea  c) trains, bells and drums
**Answer: a) bells, fireworks and the marching band.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Who wrote the music of the Year 3 'Il-Festa'?
a) Joseph Vella  b) Charles Camilleri  c) Joe Tanti
**Answer: a) Joseph Vella (words by Brother Henry).**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A folk song's rhythm and melody can give away…
a) its price  b) its country  c) its singer's age
**Answer: b) its country.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Camilleri's 'Il-Festa' paints the festa with…
a) flags, fireworks, food, the brass band and families meeting  b) only church bells  c) a train journey
**Answer: a) flags, fireworks, food, the brass band and families meeting.**
