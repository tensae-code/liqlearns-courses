# Fukera and Shilela: Boast and Answer — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A fukera performer moves…
  a) Side to side as he boasts b) Seated and still c) Backwards out of the circle
**Answer: a) Side to side as he boasts** — the performer moves side to side, holding weapons, sticks, farm tools or flags.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Qererito is…
  a) The companions' answering chant to the fukera b) A solo drum piece c) A kind of painting
**Answer: a) The companions' answering chant to the fukera** — shilela/qererito answers the boast while the fukera performs.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Zefen's melody and poem are…
  a) The community's own b) Always imported c) Written by one named poet only
**Answer: a) The community's own** — zefen is song whose melody and poem are the community's own.

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Qererito's verses are built in…
  a) Long lines, with pauses to reflect b) Short hurried lines c) No lines at all
**Answer: a) Long lines, with pauses to reflect** — qererito builds long lines; fukera is shorter and more hurried.
