# Fukera and Shilela: Boast and Answer — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Fukera gives strength to heroes and conscience to the fearful.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The companions' chant means 'we are with you, you are not alone'.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The book uses fukera and qererito to teach patience when people clash.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** The book gives both a qererito and a fukera for Lt. General Haylu Kebede.
**Answer: True**
