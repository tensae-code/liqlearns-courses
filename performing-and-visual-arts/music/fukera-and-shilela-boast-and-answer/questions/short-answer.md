# Fukera and Shilela: Boast and Answer — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What may a fukera performer hold?
**Answer: War weapons, sticks, farm tools, flags and the like.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name two qualities fukera and qererito carve into hearts.
**Answer: Any two of: boldness, resolution, confidence, heroism.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What is the first classroom use the book names?
**Answer: Guarding the country in unity — moving forward in unity, not back.**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Dejazmach Belay Zeleke is praised under what lion name?
**Answer: 'The Lion of Gojjam'.**
