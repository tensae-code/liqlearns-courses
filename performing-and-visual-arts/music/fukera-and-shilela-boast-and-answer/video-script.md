# Video script — Fukera and Shilela: Boast and Answer

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The hero boasts. Short lines, fast, shield up.
The companions answer. Long lines, slow, full of praise.
We are with you — you are not alone.
Fukera, shilela, zefen: poetry that makes heroes — and makes the fearful brave.
