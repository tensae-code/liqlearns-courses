# Hands-on — Fukera and Shilela: Boast and Answer

The lesson with your hands, your feet and your voice together, not only your eyes.

## Boast and answer circle
Stand in a circle. One pupil steps in as the fokari with an imaginary shield and gives one short boast line for a hero — a parent, a teacher, a patriot. The circle answers with a long praising line. Keep the book's rule: praise true deeds.

## Themes, not texts
Choose one exemplar hero. On a card, write the hero's name and the themes of their praise — 'the Lion of Gojjam' — and pin the cards in order: Tewodros, Belay Zeleke, Haylu Kebede.
