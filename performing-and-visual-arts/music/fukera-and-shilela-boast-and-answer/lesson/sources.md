**Acceptance count: taught in 1 country (Ethiopia).**

No fukera, shilela or qererito text is transcribed in this lesson. The two cries the Grade 5 placement itself quotes — the performer's "aki zeraf zeraf" movement-cry and the companions' "ere goraw, ere jegnaw" ("we are with you, you are not alone") — are quoted only as the placement quotes them. The Grade 6 exemplars are placed by hero and themes only, exactly as their placements require.
