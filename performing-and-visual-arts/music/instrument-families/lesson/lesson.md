# Instrument Families

## Do it first

Quick start: from the title alone, guess what this lesson's work is. Then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — Families and the woodwinds

The appreciation strand opens with **instrument families and classification** — the **woodwind family (bois)** first, with the **history of the recorder**, listening to **Baroque pieces for recorder** and the era's leading instruments: **Bach's Triple Concerto BWV 1044** and **Telemann's concerto for recorder, horn and continuo**.

## Sub lesson 2 — The woodwinds, one by one

Séance by séance the woodwinds come **one by one, each with its history and its concertos to hear**: the **transverse flute** (Vivaldi, Mozart), the **oboe** (Vivaldi, Bach, Haydn, Mozart), the **clarinet** (Mozart's K 622), the **bassoon** (Vivaldi RV 484, Mozart K 191) and the **cor anglais** (Fiala).

## Sub lesson 3 — The transverse flute and Vivaldi's recorder concerto

The EEA3 sequence returns to **instrument families and classification** — the woodwinds, the **history of the transverse flute**, and listening to **Vivaldi's recorder concerto RV 443**.
