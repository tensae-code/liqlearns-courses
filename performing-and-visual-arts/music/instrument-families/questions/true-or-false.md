# Instrument Families — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Instruments are sorted into families by classification.
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The cor anglais is a brass instrument.
**Answer: False — it is met among the woodwinds.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Each woodwind is learned with its history and concertos.
**Answer: True.**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Telemann wrote a concerto for recorder, horn and continuo named in the sequence.
**Answer: True.**
