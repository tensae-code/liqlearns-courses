# Instrument Families — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the family the sequence studies first.
**Answer: The woodwind family (bois).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the five woodwinds met one by one.
**Answer: The transverse flute, the oboe, the clarinet, the bassoon and the cor anglais.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Whose history is told in the EEA3 appreciation séances?
**Answer: The transverse flute's history.**
