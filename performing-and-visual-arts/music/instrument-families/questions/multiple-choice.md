# Instrument Families — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The recorder belongs to which family?
a) the woodwinds (bois)  b) the brass  c) the strings
**Answer: a) the woodwinds (bois)** — the woodwind family (bois).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Bach's concerto named for recorder listening is…
a) the Triple Concerto BWV 1044  b) K 622  c) RV 484
**Answer: a) the Triple Concerto BWV 1044** — the Triple Concerto BWV 1044.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Mozart's K 622 is the concerto for…
a) the clarinet  b) the bassoon  c) the oboe
**Answer: a) the clarinet** — the clarinet.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The bassoon's named concertos are by…
a) Vivaldi (RV 484) and Mozart (K 191)  b) Fiala only  c) Telemann only
**Answer: a) Vivaldi (RV 484) and Mozart (K 191)** — Vivaldi RV 484 and Mozart K 191.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Vivaldi's recorder concerto named in EEA3 is…
a) RV 443  b) RV 484  c) BWV 1044
**Answer: a) RV 443** — RV 443.
