# Hands-on — Pisiit: Inuit Personal Songs

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Write, then sing
Write one short line of a song of thanks in your own language, then sing it together — the pisiit way: written first, sung by all.

## The gathering
In a circle, the class sings one known song together, as a community gathers — then says what the gathering itself gives.
