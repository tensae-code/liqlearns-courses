# Pisiit: Inuit Personal Songs

## Do it first

Quick game: before you read on, say what you already know about this lesson's work — its name, its look, its story. Then read and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — ᐱᓰᑦ — written to be sung

The teachers who came from far away taught the faith, and many Inuit **learned to write pisiit in Inuktitut** — to sing them, a singer **had to be able to read**.

In your mind's eye: a Nunavut community gathered on a Sunday, singing pisiit together.

## Sub lesson 2 — The Sunday gathering

Communities **gather every Sunday to sing pisiit together** — a gathering that **matters greatly to the community**.
