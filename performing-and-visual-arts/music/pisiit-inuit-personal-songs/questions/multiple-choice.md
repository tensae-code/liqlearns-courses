# Pisiit: Inuit Personal Songs — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Pisiit were written in…
a) English only  b) Inuktitut  c) French only
**Answer: b) Inuktitut** — many Inuit learned to write pisiit in Inuktitut.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** To sing pisiit, a singer had to be able to…
a) read  b) dance  c) carve
**Answer: a) read** — read — the songs were written down.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Who taught the faith from which pisiit grew?
a) the whalers  b) the teachers who came from far away  c) the drum dancers
**Answer: b) the teachers who came from far away** — the teachers who came from far away.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The community gathers to sing pisiit…
a) every Sunday  b) once a year  c) only at weddings
**Answer: a) every Sunday** — every Sunday.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The Sunday gathering…
a) matters little  b) matters greatly to the community  c) is for singers only
**Answer: b) matters greatly to the community** — it matters greatly to the community.
