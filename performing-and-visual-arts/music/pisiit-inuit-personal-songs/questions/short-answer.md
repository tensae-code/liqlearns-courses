# Pisiit: Inuit Personal Songs — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What language were pisiit written in?
**Answer: Inuktitut.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How often does the community gather to sing pisiit?
**Answer: Every Sunday.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Why did a pisiit singer need to read?
**Answer: Because the pisiit were written songs — to sing them, a singer had to be able to read them.**
