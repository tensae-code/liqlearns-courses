# Video script — Pisiit: Inuit Personal Songs

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Teachers came from far away.

Inuit wrote songs in Inuktitut — pisiit — and learned to read to sing them.

Every Sunday, the community gathers and sings. That is pisiit.
