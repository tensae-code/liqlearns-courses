# Maltese Children's Songs — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** 'Il-Vapur ta' l-Art' — the 'boat on land' — is really…
a) a ferry  b) the Malta Railway train  c) a bus
**Answer: b) the Malta Railway train (1883–1931).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The train song's chorus is made of…
a) train sounds — onomatopoeia  b) church bells  c) sea sounds
**Answer: a) train sounds — onomatopoeia.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Maestro Indri Borg founded Malta's first brass band in…
a) 1860, in Żebbuġ  b) 1931, in Valletta  c) 1975, in Rabat
**Answer: a) 1860, in Żebbuġ.**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Christopher the sacristan…
a) drives the train  b) readies the church and rings the bells  c) leads the brass band
**Answer: b) readies the church and rings the bells.**
