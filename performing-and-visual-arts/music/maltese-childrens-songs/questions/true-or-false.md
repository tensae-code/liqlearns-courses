# Maltese Children's Songs — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Malta Railway ran from 1883 to 1931.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Indri Borg could see well.
**Answer: False — he was blind.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** 'Is-Sagristan' leaves space for body movements and group choreography.
**Answer: True**
