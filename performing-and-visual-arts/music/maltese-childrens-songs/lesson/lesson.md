# Maltese Children's Songs

## Do it first

Quick start: say one thing you already know — a sound, a song, a name — that belongs to this lesson's world. Keep it in mind; the parts will meet it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Il-Vapur ta' l-Art — the train song

**'Il-Vapur ta' l-Art'** (music **Joe Tanti**, performed by Greenfields 1973; words **Mikiel Spiteri 'Kilin'**) sings Malta's train: the 'boat on land' is the **Malta Railway** train that ran **Valletta–Rabat/Mtarfa from 1883 to 1931**. Its chorus is pure **train onomatopoeia** for children to reproduce. Placed by summary only.

## Sub lesson 2 — Il-Banda t' Indri — the first brass band

**Joe Tanti's** song (Greenfields, 1975) honours **Maestro Indri Borg**, the blind bandmaster who founded **Malta's first brass band in 1860 in Żebbuġ** with sixteen musicians — the band that played festas and every village celebration. Its 't'Indri' refrain is built for all abilities to join. Placed by summary only.

## Sub lesson 3 — Is-Sagristan — Christopher the sacristan

**Victor Cutajar's** jolly song tells of **Christopher the village sacristan** — the man who readies the church for every wedding, funeral and baptism and rings the bells for mass and feasts, happy to serve everyone. The song leaves space for **body movements and group choreography**. Placed by summary only.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the parts in order, one sentence each. Companion checks: did every part get its sentence, and did the order hold?

## Do It Yourself

1. Write the parts' names from memory, in order.
2. Do the hands-on activity once more, teaching it to someone else.
3. Write one sentence: what is the one thing this lesson teaches?
