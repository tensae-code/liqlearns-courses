# Video script — Maltese Children's Songs

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Malta sings its own stories.

A **train** that children called a boat on land!
A **blind bandmaster** who started Malta's first brass band!
And **Christopher**, who rings the church bells for everyone!

These are **Malta's children's songs** — in Maltese.
