# Hands-on — Dominican Minor Percussion

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Three ways
Take one small percussion instrument (or a kitchen stand-in): look at it closely, listen to its sound, then play a short pattern — the book's three ways.

## Draw the güira
Draw the güira as the book pictures it, played by a girl, and label its parts as far as you can see them.
