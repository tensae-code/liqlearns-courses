# Dominican Minor Percussion — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does “minor” percussion mean here?
**Answer: The small (hand) percussion instruments.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the three ways of learning the instruments in the book.
**Answer: Watching, hearing and playing.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Name one minor percussion instrument of the country.
**Answer: The güira.**
