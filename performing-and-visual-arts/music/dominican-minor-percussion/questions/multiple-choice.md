# Dominican Minor Percussion — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Minor percussion instruments are…
a) the country's small hand percussion  b) the biggest drums  c) string instruments
**Answer: a) the country's small hand percussion**.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** One pictured instrument is…
a) the güira  b) the piano  c) the violin
**Answer: a) the güira**.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The instruments are learned by…
a) watching, hearing and playing them  b) reading only  c) drawing only
**Answer: a) watching, hearing and playing them**.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The güira in the book is played by…
a) a girl  b) a machine  c) nobody
**Answer: a) a girl**.

**5. (Sub lesson 1)** These instruments belong to…
a) the Dominican Republic's folk music  b) another continent only  c) the orchestra's strings
**Answer: a) the Dominican Republic's folk music**.
