# Dominican Minor Percussion

## Do it first

Quick start: Small instruments, in small hands — scrape, shake, strike. Then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — The minor percussion instruments

The Grade 2 book's unit *Nuestros instrumentos* — our instruments — presents the **minor and folk percussion instruments of the country**: the small hand percussion that carries the folk rhythms. Among them is the **güira**, pictured **played by a girl**.

## Sub lesson 2 — Watch, hear and play

The instruments are learned **three ways: watched, heard and played**. You look at the instrument, you listen to its sound, and then your own hands play it — that is how the book's Grade 2 learners come to know their country's percussion.
