# Hands-on — Inuit Music and the Coming of the Newcomers

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Two musics meet
List two columns: the old forms (katajjaq, drum dancing) and the new kinds from this lesson (pisiit, dance music). Say what each side brought.

## Teach a song
One learner teaches the group a short song from their own home; the group learns it — the same meeting of musics, in your own room.
