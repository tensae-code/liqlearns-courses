# Inuit Music and the Coming of the Newcomers — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The newcomers kept their songs to themselves.
**Answer: False — they taught them to the people already there.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Inuit music grew new kinds after the newcomers came.
**Answer: True.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Pisiit came to Inuit music with the newcomers' teaching.
**Answer: True.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Dance music from that time is no longer used.
**Answer: False — it is still used today.**
