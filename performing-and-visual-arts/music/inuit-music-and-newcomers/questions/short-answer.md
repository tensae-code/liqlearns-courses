# Inuit Music and the Coming of the Newcomers — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Who taught the newcomers' songs to the people already in the Arctic?
**Answer: The Qallunaat (newcomers) themselves.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the two new kinds the book names.
**Answer: Pisiit and dance music.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Are pisiit and dance music still used?
**Answer: Yes — they are still used today.**
