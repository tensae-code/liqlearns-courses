# Inuit Music and the Coming of the Newcomers — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Qallunaat means…
a) the elders  b) the newcomers who arrived in the Arctic  c) the drum makers
**Answer: b) the newcomers who arrived in the Arctic** — Qallunaat — the newcomers.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The newcomers brought…
a) their own songs from their own lands  b) silence  c) only tools, no music
**Answer: a) their own songs from their own lands** — their own songs, and taught them to the people already there.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Among the new kinds Inuit music grew were…
a) opera and ballet  b) pisiit and dance music  c) rock bands
**Answer: b) pisiit and dance music** — pisiit and dance music.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Those new kinds are…
a) forgotten today  b) still used today  c) used only once
**Answer: b) still used today** — still used today.

**5. (Sub lesson 1)** When the newcomers arrived, music…
a) did not change at all  b) changed, as much else changed  c) stopped
**Answer: b) changed, as much else changed** — much changed, music included.
