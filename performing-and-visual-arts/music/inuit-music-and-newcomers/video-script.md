# Video script — Inuit Music and the Coming of the Newcomers

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

New people came to the Arctic.

They brought songs from their own lands — and taught them.

New kinds grew: pisiit, dance music — still sung today.
