# Hands-on — Sargam: Hindi Solfa

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Climb the sargam ladder
Sing sa–re–ga–ma–pa–dha–ni–sa up, then back down, with a hand action for each step.

## Name that pitch
A partner sings one sargam syllable; name it and sing the ladder from sa to it.
