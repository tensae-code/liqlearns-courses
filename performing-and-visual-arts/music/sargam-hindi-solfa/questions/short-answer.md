# Sargam: Hindi Solfa — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Write the eight sargam syllables.
**Answer: Sa, re, ga, ma, pa, dha, ni, sa.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What system is taught beside sargam in Fiji?
**Answer: Western tonic solfa (do–re–mi).**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** How is sargam sung in practice?
**Answer: Ascending and descending, with actions and pitch-recitation.**
