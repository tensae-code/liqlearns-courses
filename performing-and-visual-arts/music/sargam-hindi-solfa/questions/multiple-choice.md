# Sargam: Hindi Solfa — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Sargam is the solfa of…
a) Hindi music  b) Fijian meke  c) brass bands
**Answer: a) Hindi music.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The first sargam syllable is…
a) sa  b) do  c) ti
**Answer: a) sa.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The sargam syllables run…
a) sa re ga ma pa dha ni sa  b) do re mi only  c) C D E only
**Answer: a) sa re ga ma pa dha ni sa.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Sargam is practised sung…
a) ascending and descending  b) only descending  c) whispered
**Answer: a) ascending and descending.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Sargam practice includes…
a) actions and pitch-recitation  b) only writing  c) only listening
**Answer: a) actions and pitch-recitation.**
