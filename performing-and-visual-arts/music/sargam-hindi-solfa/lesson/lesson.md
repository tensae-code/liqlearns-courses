## Sub lesson 1 — The sargam syllables

**Sargam** is the **Hindi music notation system**. Its syllables are **sa, re, ga, ma, pa, dha, ni, sa** — the Hindi solfa, taught in Fiji beside the Western tonic solfa (do–re–mi).

## Sub lesson 2 — Singing sargam up and down

Sargam is **sung ascending and descending**, **with actions**, and practised by **pitch-recitation** — saying and singing each pitch in order and out of order.
