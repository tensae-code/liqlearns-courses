## Sub lesson 2 — Theme 1

Theme 1: The sources of Armenian sacred music — SHARAKANS, TAGHS, GANDZES: the syllabus table sets Komitas Aghtsetsi's arrangement, Grigor Narekatsi's hymns, Khachatur Taronatsi, Nerses Shnorhali's "Aravot luso", Makar Yekmalyan and Komitas's liturgy settings side by side.

## Sub lesson 3 — The development of Armenian music, 8th-15th centuries

The development of Armenian music, 8th-15th centuries — formed in the 4th-5th centuries with Christianity's adoption (301) and the alphabet (405): the first hymn-singers (կցուրդներ) arose in the 5th century, their beginnings tied to Mesrop Mashtots, Sahak Partev, Movses Khorenatsi and Hovhannes Mandakuni; Sahak A Partev (348-439).

## Sub lesson 4 — The hymn-writers

The hymn-writers — in the 7th-8th centuries new sharakan collections: Komitas A Aghtsetsi, Anania Shirakatsi (610-685, who wrote sharakans for the feasts), Sahak G Dzoroporetsi, Stepanos Syunetsi; the sharakan is sung to this day.
