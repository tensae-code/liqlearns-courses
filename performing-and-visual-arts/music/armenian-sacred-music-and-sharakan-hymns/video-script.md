This is Armenia's own lesson.

**Armenian medieval music.** Armenian medieval music: the first spiritual songs; the authors of the sharakans — Armenia's own hymn tradition and its named hymn-writers.

**Theme 1.** Theme 1: The sources of Armenian sacred music — SHARAKANS, TAGHS, GANDZES: the syllabus table sets Komitas Aghtsetsi's arrangement, Grigor Narekatsi's hymns, Khachatur Taronatsi, Nerses Shnorhali's "Aravot luso", Makar Yekmalyan and Komitas's liturgy settings side by side.

**The development of Armenian music, 8th-15th centuries.** The development of Armenian music, 8th-15th centuries — formed in the 4th-5th centuries with Christianity's adoption (301) and the alphabet (405): the first hymn-singers (կցուրդներ) arose in the 5th century, their beginnings tied to Mesrop Mashtots, Sahak Partev, Movses Khorenatsi and Hovhannes Mandakuni; Sahak A Partev (348-439).

**The hymn-writers.** The hymn-writers — in the 7th-8th centuries new sharakan collections: Komitas A Aghtsetsi, Anania Shirakatsi (610-685, who wrote sharakans for the feasts), Sahak G Dzoroporetsi, Stepanos Syunetsi; the sharakan is sung to this day.

**Chapter 1.** Chapter 1: ARMENIAN SACRED MUSIC (Հայ հոգևոր երաժշտություն) (teacher-guide evidence) — the grade-10 course opens with Armenia's own sacred music before turning to Europe.

That is Armenian Sacred Music and Sharakan Hymns — Armenia's way, kept by name.
