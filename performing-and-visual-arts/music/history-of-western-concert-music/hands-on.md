# Hands-on — History of Western Concert Music

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Music's beginnings
Do the practice this part names, with real materials and a partner: music's beginnings: sound as a link to the gods, the shaman as first musician, work songs (Brazil's washerwomen, US cotton pickers) feeding blues and jazz

## The medieval church
Do the practice this part names, with real materials and a partner: the medieval church: Gregorian chant (monodic, in unison) against the troubadours' secular folk music, from which polyphony begins

## Baroque and Classical
Do the practice this part names, with real materials and a partner: Baroque and Classical: luthiers and Stradivari, Bach, the sonata form's musical discourse, Mozart and Beethoven

## Romantic and modern
Do the practice this part names, with real materials and a partner: Romantic and modern: Verdi and Brazil's Carlos Gomes (O Guarani); Debussy, Stravinsky, Villa-Lobos' Bachianas Brasileiras, Schoenberg's twelve-tone system, Cage's chance sounds, Stockhausen's electronic studio
