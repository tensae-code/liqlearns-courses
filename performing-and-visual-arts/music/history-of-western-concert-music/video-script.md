# Video script — History of Western Concert Music

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

**Music's beginnings.** Music's beginnings: sound as a link to the gods, the shaman as first musician, work songs (Brazil's washerwomen, US cotton pickers) feeding blues and jazz

**The medieval church.** The medieval church: Gregorian chant (monodic, in unison) against the troubadours' secular folk music, from which polyphony begins

**Baroque and Classical.** Baroque and Classical: luthiers and Stradivari, Bach, the sonata form's musical discourse, Mozart and Beethoven

**Romantic and modern.** Romantic and modern: Verdi and Brazil's Carlos Gomes (O Guarani); Debussy, Stravinsky, Villa-Lobos' Bachianas Brasileiras, Schoenberg's twelve-tone system, Cage's chance sounds, Stockhausen's electronic studio
