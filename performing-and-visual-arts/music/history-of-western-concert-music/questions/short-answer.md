# History of Western Concert Music — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In your own words, what does this part teach?
**Answer: Music's beginnings: sound as a link to the gods, the shaman as first musician, work songs (Brazil's washerwomen, US cotton pickers) feeding blues and jazz**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In your own words, what does this part teach?
**Answer: The medieval church: Gregorian chant (monodic, in unison) against the troubadours' secular folk music, from which polyphony begins**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In your own words, what does this part teach?
**Answer: Baroque and Classical: luthiers and Stradivari, Bach, the sonata form's musical discourse, Mozart and Beethoven**
