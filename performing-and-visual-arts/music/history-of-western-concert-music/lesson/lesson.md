# History of Western Concert Music

## Do it first

Look at the sub lesson list. Pick the part you already know something about, and say what you know. Then start at Sub lesson 1 — every part builds on the one before it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Music's beginnings

Music's beginnings: sound as a link to the gods, the shaman as first musician, work songs (Brazil's washerwomen, US cotton pickers) feeding blues and jazz (Folha 16 "Música e músicas", Brazil's *ARTE — Ensino Médio*, printed p. 258).

## Sub lesson 2 — The medieval church

The medieval church: Gregorian chant (monodic, in unison) against the troubadours' secular folk music, from which polyphony begins (Folha 16, Brazil's *ARTE — Ensino Médio*, printed p. 263).

## Sub lesson 3 — Baroque and Classical

Baroque and Classical: luthiers and Stradivari, Bach, the sonata form's musical discourse, Mozart and Beethoven (Folha 16, Brazil's *ARTE — Ensino Médio*, printed p. 266).

## Sub lesson 4 — Romantic and modern

Romantic and modern: Verdi and Brazil's Carlos Gomes (O Guarani); Debussy, Stravinsky, Villa-Lobos' Bachianas Brasileiras, Schoenberg's twelve-tone system, Cage's chance sounds, Stockhausen's electronic studio (Folha 16, Brazil's *ARTE — Ensino Médio*, printed p. 267).
