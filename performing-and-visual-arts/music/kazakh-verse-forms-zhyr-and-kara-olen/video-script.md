This lesson is Kazakh Verse Forms: Zhyr and Kara Olen.

Kazakh Verse Forms: Zhyr and Kara Olen — Music and poetry in Kazakh — the zhyr and kara olen sung verse forms. — as Kazakhstan's school books teach it: what it teaches: poetry and music as one whole — өлең is both poem and song, the akyn is a poet; syllabic verse — the 7–8-syllable жыр line against the 11-syllable қара өлең; how song forms grew from ritual saryn and everyday songs to the sal/seri self-presentation songs; Baluan Sholak, singer and wrestler.
