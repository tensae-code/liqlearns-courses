# PNG Traditional and Contemporary Music

## Do it first

Look at the sub lesson list. Pick the part you already know something about, and say what you know. Then start at Sub lesson 1 — every part builds on the one before it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Traditional music by region

Traditional music is investigated by geographical setting, ethnic affiliation, history and function, with its costume and language, drawn from Papua New Guinea's four regions.

## Sub lesson 2 — Religious music of PNG

PNG-oriented religious music is studied with its denominational influences, region by region.

## Sub lesson 3 — Traditional contemporary music

Traditional contemporary music is the creative integration of traditional and modern instruments and concepts — the guide names Sanguma, tribal chants and Tambaran Culture — studied, arranged and performed.
