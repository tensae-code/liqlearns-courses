# PNG Traditional and Contemporary Music — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Traditional music is investigated by…
a) region, ethnic affiliation, history and function  b) price and sales
**Answer: a) region, ethnic affiliation, history and function.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Sanguma is named as…
a) traditional contemporary music  b) a dance costume
**Answer: a) traditional contemporary music.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Religious music studies include…
a) denominational influences by region  b) no regions
**Answer: a) denominational influences by region.**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Traditional contemporary music integrates…
a) traditional and modern instruments and concepts  b) only electronics
**Answer: a) traditional and modern instruments and concepts.**
