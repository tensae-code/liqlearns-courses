# PNG Traditional and Contemporary Music — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Costume and language are part of studying traditional music.
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Traditional contemporary music rejects traditional instruments.
**Answer: False — it integrates traditional and modern instruments.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** PNG's traditional music comes from its four regions.
**Answer: True.**
