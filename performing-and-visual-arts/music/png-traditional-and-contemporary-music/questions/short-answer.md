# PNG Traditional and Contemporary Music — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What four lenses does the guide investigate traditional music through?
**Answer: Geographical setting, ethnic affiliation, history and function (with costume and language).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name two named examples of traditional contemporary music.
**Answer: Sanguma, tribal chants and Tambaran Culture (any two).**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** How is religious music organised in the study?
**Answer: By region, with its denominational influences.**
