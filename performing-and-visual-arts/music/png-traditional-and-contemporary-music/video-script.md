# Video script — PNG Traditional and Contemporary Music

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

**Traditional music by region.** Traditional music is investigated by geographical setting, ethnic affiliation, history and function, with its costume and language, drawn from Papua New Guinea's four regions.

**Religious music of PNG.** PNG-oriented religious music is studied with its denominational influences, region by region.

**Traditional contemporary music.** Traditional contemporary music is the creative integration of traditional and modern instruments and concepts — the guide names Sanguma, tribal chants and Tambaran Culture — studied, arranged and performed.
