# Hands-on — PNG Traditional and Contemporary Music

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Traditional music by region
Do the practice this part names, with real materials and a partner: Traditional music is investigated by geographical setting, ethnic affiliation, history and function, with its costume and language, drawn from Papua New Guinea's four regions.

## Religious music of PNG
Do the practice this part names, with real materials and a partner: PNG-oriented religious music is studied with its denominational influences, region by region.

## Traditional contemporary music
Do the practice this part names, with real materials and a partner: Traditional contemporary music is the creative integration of traditional and modern instruments and concepts — the guide names Sanguma, tribal chants and Tambaran Culture — studied, arranged and performed.
