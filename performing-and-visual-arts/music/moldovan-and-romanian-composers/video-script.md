# Video script — Moldovan and Romanian Composers

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This is Moldova's own lesson.

**Alexandru Flechtenmacher.** Unit 3, Alexandru Flechtenmacher — the overture "Moldova" and "Hora Unirii" with Vasile Alecsandri; he founded the Conservatory.

**Gavriil Musicescu.** Unit 3, Gavriil Musicescu — the choral master: his two Choral Concertos, folk songs arranged for choir, and a rich repertoire for school choirs ("Ca o zi de primăvară").

**Ciprian Porumbescu.** Unit 3, Ciprian Porumbescu — the "Ballad for Violin and Piano" and the patriotic songs, among them "Trei culori".

**Ștefan Neaga.** Unit 3, Ștefan Neaga — the symphonic poem "Nistru", the first Moldovan symphonic work in the golden fund, and the five-generation Neaga dynasty of lăutari and composers.

**George Enescu.** Unit 3, George Enescu — the Romanian Rhapsodies Nos. 1 and 2, built on folk motives heard from lăutari in childhood.

**Eugen Doga.** Unit 3, Eugen Doga — the instrumental piece "Sonet" and the song "Codrii mei frumoși": a composer of symphonic, ballet, film and children's music.

**Muzica pentru copii.** Unit 4, Muzica pentru copii — Eugen Doga's music for children: the film songs of "Maria, Mirabela" on Grigore Vieru's verses.

**Constantin Rusnac.** Unit 1, Constantin Rusnac — the concert piece "Sărbătoreasca" ("Poem about Moldova") for folk orchestra: sârba-dance outer parts around a tender middle.

**Muzica și teatrul.** Unit 1, Muzica și teatrul — Eugen Doga's ballet "Luceafărul", on Eminescu's poem, libretto by Emil Loteanu, in two acts and seven scenes.

**Piesa "La joc".** Unit 3, Piesa "La joc" — Mihail Jora's orchestral suite "Priveliști moldovenești" (Moldavian Landscapes, 1924), built on a folk melody, and its second piece "La joc" (At the Dance), where trumpet calls summon the village to the dance.

That is Moldovan and Romanian Composers — Moldova's way, kept by name.
