# Lesson plan — Moldovan and Romanian Composers

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Music · **Lesson:** Moldovan and Romanian Composers
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 3 — Music (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Moldova and Romania's composers — Flechtenmacher, Musicescu and their school.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the course's earlier lessons in its title; recommended first, never forced.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of ..." in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Alexandru Flechtenmacher** — Unit 3, Alexandru Flechtenmacher — the overture "Moldova" and "Hora Unirii" with Vasile Alecsandri; he founded the Conservatory.
2. **Gavriil Musicescu** — Unit 3, Gavriil Musicescu — the choral master: his two Choral Concertos, folk songs arranged for choir, and a rich repertoire for school choirs ("Ca o zi de primăvară").
3. **Ciprian Porumbescu** — Unit 3, Ciprian Porumbescu — the "Ballad for Violin and Piano" and the patriotic songs, among them "Trei culori".
4. **Ștefan Neaga** — Unit 3, Ștefan Neaga — the symphonic poem "Nistru", the first Moldovan symphonic work in the golden fund, and the five-generation Neaga dynasty of lăutari and composers.
5. **George Enescu** — Unit 3, George Enescu — the Romanian Rhapsodies Nos. 1 and 2, built on folk motives heard from lăutari in childhood.
6. **Eugen Doga** — Unit 3, Eugen Doga — the instrumental piece "Sonet" and the song "Codrii mei frumoși": a composer of symphonic, ballet, film and children's music.
7. **Muzica pentru copii** — Unit 4, Muzica pentru copii — Eugen Doga's music for children: the film songs of "Maria, Mirabela" on Grigore Vieru's verses.
8. **Constantin Rusnac** — Unit 1, Constantin Rusnac — the concert piece "Sărbătoreasca" ("Poem about Moldova") for folk orchestra: sârba-dance outer parts around a tender middle.
9. **Muzica și teatrul** — Unit 1, Muzica și teatrul — Eugen Doga's ballet "Luceafărul", on Eminescu's poem, libretto by Emil Loteanu, in two acts and seven scenes.
10. **Piesa "La joc"** — Unit 3, Piesa "La joc" — Mihail Jora's orchestral suite "Priveliști moldovenești" (Moldavian Landscapes, 1924), built on a folk melody, and its second piece "La joc" (At the Dance), where trumpet calls summon the village to the dance.
