# Moldovan and Romanian Composers — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — Alexandru Flechtenmacher
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Unit 3, Alexandru Flechtenmacher — the overture "Moldova" and "Hora Unirii" with Vasile Alecsandri; he founded the Conservatory.

## Sub lesson 2 — Gavriil Musicescu
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Unit 3, Gavriil Musicescu — the choral master: his two Choral Concertos, folk songs arranged for choir, and a rich repertoire for school choirs ("Ca o zi de primăvară").

## Sub lesson 3 — Ciprian Porumbescu
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Unit 3, Ciprian Porumbescu — the "Ballad for Violin and Piano" and the patriotic songs, among them "Trei culori".

## Sub lesson 4 — Ștefan Neaga
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Unit 3, Ștefan Neaga — the symphonic poem "Nistru", the first Moldovan symphonic work in the golden fund, and the five-generation Neaga dynasty of lăutari and composers.

## Sub lesson 5 — George Enescu
Sub lesson 5 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Unit 3, George Enescu — the Romanian Rhapsodies Nos. 1 and 2, built on folk motives heard from lăutari in childhood.

## Sub lesson 6 — Eugen Doga
Sub lesson 6 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Unit 3, Eugen Doga — the instrumental piece "Sonet" and the song "Codrii mei frumoși": a composer of symphonic, ballet, film and children's music.

## Sub lesson 7 — Muzica pentru copii
Sub lesson 7 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Unit 4, Muzica pentru copii — Eugen Doga's music for children: the film songs of "Maria, Mirabela" on Grigore Vieru's verses.

## Sub lesson 8 — Constantin Rusnac
Sub lesson 8 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Unit 1, Constantin Rusnac — the concert piece "Sărbătoreasca" ("Poem about Moldova") for folk orchestra: sârba-dance outer parts around a tender middle.

## Sub lesson 9 — Muzica și teatrul
Sub lesson 9 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Unit 1, Muzica și teatrul — Eugen Doga's ballet "Luceafărul", on Eminescu's poem, libretto by Emil Loteanu, in two acts and seven scenes.

## Sub lesson 10 — Piesa "La joc"
Sub lesson 10 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Unit 3, Piesa "La joc" — Mihail Jora's orchestral suite "Priveliști moldovenești" (Moldavian Landscapes, 1924), built on a folk melody, and its second piece "La joc" (At the Dance), where trumpet calls summon the village to the dance.
