# Video script — Byzantine Notation

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is Byzantine Notation.

Byzantine chant notation — the signs — as Greece's school books teach it: the chant notes and their base and time; the martyriai (testimony signs) Pa-Vou-Ga-Di; the quantity characters ison, oligon, apostrophos; parallage (solmisation) and melos.

Watch, listen — then say it back in your own words.
