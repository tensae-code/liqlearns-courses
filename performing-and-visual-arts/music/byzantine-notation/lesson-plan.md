# Lesson plan — Byzantine Notation

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Music · **Lesson:** Byzantine Notation
**Place in the curriculum:** Music (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Byzantine chant notation — the signs
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this course in the same title.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Α΄ Γυμνασίου Ενότητες 2-3: Σημειογραφία — φθόγγοι, βάση, χρόνος; μαρτυρίες Πα-Βου-Γα-Δι (Byzantine notation I)** — the chant notes and their base and time; the martyriai (testimony signs) Pa-Vou-Ga-Di; the quantity characters ison, oligon, apostrophos; parallage (solmisation) and melos
2. **Α΄ Γυμνασίου Ενότητες 4-9: χαρακτήρες ποιότητος και χρόνου (Byzantine notation II)** — quality characters and the hyphen; time characters — klasma, apli, stavros, dipli, tripli, koronis and the rests; the martyriai Ke-Zo-Ni and kentimata; petasti, varia, psifiston; hyphesis and diesis, hyporoi; the gorgon
3. **Α΄ Γυμνασίου Ενότητες 10-18: συνδυασμοί και υπερβατές αναβάσεις (Byzantine notation III)** — combining oligon and kentimata with gorgon and klasma; the continuous elaphron and the omalon; leaping ascents and descents of two to seven voices; the high and low signs, antikenoma and syndesmos
4. **Β΄ Γυμνασίου Ενότητες 1-2: φθορές και παραχορδή (Phthorai and parachordi)** — the phthorai — signs that alter a note's genus — and the parachordi
5. **Α΄ Λυκείου Μάθ. 2: Το Σημειογραφικό σύστημα (The notation system)** — the neume system of Byzantine chant
6. **Α΄ Λυκείου Μάθ. 5–10: Χαρακτήρες ποσότητας Α–ΣΤ (The quantity characters, in six lessons)** — the quantity signs that raise and lower the chant step by step
7. **Α΄ Gymnasium Lessons 2–3: the notation's foundations — phthongoi (notes), base and time; the martyriai (signatures) Pa–Vou–Ga–Di; the quantity characters ison, oligon, apostrophos; parallage and melos** — Byzantine chant notation — the signs
8. **Α΄ Gymnasium Lessons 4–5: quality characters, the yphen, time characters, klasma, apli, stavros; dipli, tripli, coronis and rests** — Byzantine chant notation — the signs
9. **Α΄ Gymnasium Lessons 6–18: the remaining signs (kentimata, petasti, vareia, psifiston, yphesis/diesis, hyporroe, gorgon and its combinations, syneches elaphron, omalon), leaps of two to seven voices with supports, and interval, tone and the polychord** — Byzantine chant notation — the signs
10. **Α΄ Lyceum Lesson 2: the notation system (σημειογραφικό σύστημα) as a whole** — Byzantine chant notation — the signs
11. **Α΄ Lyceum Lessons 5–10: the quantity characters in six graded sets (Α–ΣΤ)** — Byzantine chant notation — the signs
12. **Chapter 14: Το βυζαντινό μουσικό αλφαβητάρι (the Byzantine musical alphabet — Greece's own chant notation, in a primary classroom)** — Byzantine chant notation — the signs
