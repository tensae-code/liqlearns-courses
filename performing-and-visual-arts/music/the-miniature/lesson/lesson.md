# The Musical Miniature

## Do it first

Name one thing that belongs in this lesson — The miniature in music — a small, complete musical work. Say what it is in your own words. If you can name one, you are already inside the lesson.

## Sub lesson 1 — Miniatura muzicală

Unit 4, Miniatura muzicală — the musical miniature: a small piece that says its ideas and feelings concisely, born in the Baroque for harpsichord and raised by Schubert, Mendelssohn, Schumann and Chopin — through Chopin's Prelude No. 4 in E minor, its ticking accompaniment and slow swell from piano to forte.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson holds, part by part, in your own words. Companion checks: did every part get named, with its Moldovan name kept where the book gives one?

## Do It Yourself

1. Choose one named part of this lesson and find out one more true thing about it — from a person who knows it, or from the book your part came from.
2. Make or perform a small piece in the way of that part: a rhythm, a step, a drawing, a shaped piece of clay.
3. Write one sentence: what does this part show about Moldova's own way of teaching its arts?
