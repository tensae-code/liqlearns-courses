This is Moldova's own lesson.

**Miniatura muzicală.** Unit 4, Miniatura muzicală — the musical miniature: a small piece that says its ideas and feelings concisely, born in the Baroque for harpsichord and raised by Schubert, Mendelssohn, Schumann and Chopin — through Chopin's Prelude No. 4 in E minor, its ticking accompaniment and slow swell from piano to forte.

That is The Musical Miniature — Moldova's way, kept by name.
