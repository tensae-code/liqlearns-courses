# Indigenous Music and Culture — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.


**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Indigenous music lives inside…
  a) Occasions — birth, wedding, worship, work, festival b) Glass cases only c) Examination halls
**Answer: a) Occasions — birth, wedding, worship, work, festival** — the music belongs to the people's occasions.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A wedding song sung in its wedding is…
  a) A working part of the people's life b) A specimen c) Out of place
**Answer: a) A working part of the people's life** — in its occasion, the song does its cultural work.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Culture gives music…
  a) Its occasions and meanings b) Nothing at all c) Only costumes
**Answer: a) Its occasions and meanings** — which song, for which feast, praising which value.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Music gives culture…
  a) Its memory and feeling b) Only noise c) Only rules
**Answer: a) Its memory and feeling** — names, histories and praises carried in song.
