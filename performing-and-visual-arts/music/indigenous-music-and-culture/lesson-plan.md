# Lesson plan — Indigenous Music and Culture

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Music · **Lesson:** Indigenous Music and Culture
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 3 — Music (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** indigenous music inside its culture — the occasions, dress, dance and values the music lives with, as the Grade 7 teacher guide carries them (evidence; EVIDENCE-LIMITED where outline-only).
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Appreciating Indigenous Knowledge in Music; this lesson is teacher-guide evidence and says so.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Music inside its occasions (teacher guide — evidence)** — the guide's framing: indigenous music lives inside birth, wedding, worship, work and festival — never apart from them.
2. **How music and culture hold each other (teacher guide — evidence)** — culture gives music its occasions and meanings; music gives culture its memory and feeling — each holds the other.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Appreciating Indigenous Knowledge in Music. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name three cultural settings indigenous music lives inside.
2. Say how music and culture hold each other, in the guide's framing.

## What you need
Only attention to the occasions around you — weddings, feasts, worship and work.
