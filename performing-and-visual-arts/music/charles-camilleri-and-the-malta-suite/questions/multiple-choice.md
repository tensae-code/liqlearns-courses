# Charles Camilleri and the Malta Suite — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Charles Camilleri wrote the Malta Suite…
a) in Valletta at forty  b) on Gozo at fifteen  c) in London
**Answer: b) on Gozo at fifteen.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The Malta Suite is built from…
a) Italian opera tunes  b) Maltese musical fragments  c) army marches
**Answer: b) Maltese musical fragments.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** One movement pupils match by ear is…
a) Village Festa  b) the train  c) a sea storm
**Answer: a) Village Festa.**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Camilleri wrote how many works?
a) about thirty  b) more than 300  c) twelve
**Answer: b) more than 300.**
