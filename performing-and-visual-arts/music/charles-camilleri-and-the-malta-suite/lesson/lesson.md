# Charles Camilleri and the Malta Suite

## Do it first

Quick start: say one thing you already know — a sound, a song, a name — that belongs to this lesson's world. Keep it in mind; the parts will meet it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Our roots — Charles Camilleri

Why **roots and tradition** matter in music is taught through Malta's pioneer of a classical tradition: **Charles Camilleri** (300+ works) wrote the **Malta Suite** on Gozo **at fifteen**, from **Maltese musical fragments** — 'the music of the people to my people'. Pupils match its heard excerpts to **Village Festa, a quiet night, happy dancing in the countryside and a traditional waltz**, describe the music in their own words, draw the festa from the Village Festa movement, and research the composer's life and works into a short project.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the parts in order, one sentence each. Companion checks: did every part get its sentence, and did the order hold?
