# Macedonian Performers and Conductors

## Do it first

Name one thing that belongs in this lesson — North Macedonia's known performers and conductors — the interpreters who carry its music to the stage. Say what it is in your own words. If you can name one, you are already inside the lesson.

## Sub lesson 1 — Македонски диригенти

Македонски диригенти — Macedonia's conductors: Fimcho Muratovski, Tomislav Shopov, Sasha Nikolovski-Gjumar, Bisera Chadlovska and Borjan Canev.

## Sub lesson 2 — Познати македонски интерпретатори

Познати македонски интерпретатори — famous Macedonian interpreters: the pianist Simon Trpchevski and the soprano Ana Durlovski.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson holds, part by part, in your own words. Companion checks: did every part get named, with its Macedonian name kept where the book gives one?

## Do It Yourself

1. Choose one named part of this lesson and find out one more true thing about it — from a person who knows it, or from the book your part came from.
2. Make or perform a small piece in the way of that part: a rhythm, a step, a drawing, a shaped piece of clay.
3. Write one sentence: what does this part show about North Macedonia's own way of teaching its arts?
