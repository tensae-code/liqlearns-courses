# Samoan Rhythms in Performance — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Samoa's guide has performers experiment with…
a) traditional Sāmoan rhythms  b) silence  c) only European rhythms
**Answer: a) traditional Sāmoan rhythms.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** To experiment with a rhythm means to…
a) copy it once and stop  b) try it in changed ways on purpose  c) forget it
**Answer: b) try it in changed ways on purpose.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The rhythm experiment is part of…
a) instrumental performance study  b) a spelling lesson  c) gardening
**Answer: a) instrumental performance study.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Performance study in the same section includes…
a) score reading and presenting a performance  b) only listening  c) no instruments
**Answer: a) score reading and presenting a performance.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The rhythms experimented with come from…
a) Samoa's own tradition  b) a computer game  c) nowhere — they are invented in class
**Answer: a) Samoa's own tradition.**
