# Samoan Rhythms in Performance

## Do it first

Play a rhythm you know. Now change one thing — hold one beat longer. Same hands, new feeling. That changing-on-purpose is experimenting, and Samoa's senior guide gives it a named place.

## Sub lesson 1 — Traditional Sāmoan rhythms

In the Performance section of Samoa's Years 11–12 Music Teacher Guide, pupils **experiment with traditional Sāmoan rhythms** — Samoa's own rhythm patterns are the material of the experiment, not an imported exercise.

## Sub lesson 2 — Rhythms inside performance study

The experiment is **part of instrumental performance study**: the same section's in-depth study of an instrument — its performance techniques, score reading, notation, scales and exercises — ending in preparing and presenting an instrumental performance. Samoa's rhythms are worked with the performer's full craft, inside the performance itself.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion: what do performers experiment with, and what study does the experiment belong to? Companion checks: traditional Sāmoan rhythms? Instrumental performance study?
