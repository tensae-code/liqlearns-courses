# Hands-on — Samoan Rhythms in Performance

## Try the rhythm three ways
Clap or tap one short traditional-style rhythm pattern of your own making. Play it three ways: steady, then with one beat held longer, then with the pattern starting on a different beat. Your partner says which version changed the feeling most.

## Into the piece
Take a short tune or rhythm you can already play. Add your experimented rhythm under it for eight beats. Present the two versions — plain, then with the Sāmoan-rhythm experiment — to a partner.
