# Video script — Percussion Instruments of India

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

From India — percussion with a hundred voices.

The **Tabla** — two drums, talking together.
The **Mridangam** — one head singing metallic.
And bowls of **water** that play gentle tunes — the **Jal Tarang**.
