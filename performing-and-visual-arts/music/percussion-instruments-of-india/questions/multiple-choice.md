# Percussion Instruments of India — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Tabla is…
a) one big drum  b) a pair — Dayan and Bayan  c) a water bowl
**Answer: b) a pair — wooden Dayan and clay/metal Bayan.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The Mridangam's black paste spot makes one head sound…
a) silent  b) metallic  c) lower only
**Answer: b) metallic.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The Jal Tarang is played by striking…
a) tuned clay bowls of water  b) metal bells  c) a clay pot skin
**Answer: a) tuned clay bowls of water.**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** The Tumbaknaer is held…
a) on a stand  b) between the legs or under the arm  c) overhead
**Answer: b) between the legs or under the arm.**
