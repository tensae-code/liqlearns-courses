# The Umuduri Musical Bow — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** The umuduri is…
a) a short string musical bow with a calabash voice box  b) a drum  c) a woven hat
**Answer: a) a short string musical bow with a calabash voice box**

**2.** The calabash on the umuduri is its…
a) voice box (resonator)  b) case  c) decoration only
**Answer: a) voice box (resonator)**

**3.** A learner plays a new umuduri pattern…
a) slowly first, then faster with practice  b) as fast as possible at once  c) only once
**Answer: a) slowly first, then faster with practice**

**4.** Before playing, the player learns the instrument's…
a) tuning and fingering  b) price  c) colour
**Answer: a) tuning and fingering**
