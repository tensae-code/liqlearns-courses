# The Umuduri Musical Bow — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1.** Which way must the voice box face, and why?
**Answer: The way that gives the loudest sound — the book teaches learners to find and use that facing.**

**2.** Name one teaching song the umuduri accompanies in the book's unit.
**Answer: Any one of: the drug-abuse song, the HIV/AIDS song, the malaria songs.**
