# The Umuduri Musical Bow

## Do it first

Start with the first part below — do it or make it before you read any explanation.

## Sub lesson 1 — The umuduri and its parts

The **umuduri** is Rwanda's **short string musical bow** with a **calabash voice box (resonator)**. Learners identify **its parts and the materials of each part**, say **which way the voice box faces for the loudest sound**, and learn **how a player holds it**.

## Sub lesson 2 — Playing the umuduri

Playing is learned by **tuning and fingering**: learn the tuning, practise the fingering, and **play slowly first, then faster with practice**. In the same unit the umuduri accompanies the book's teaching songs — on drug abuse, HIV/AIDS and malaria (those songs are stocked in their own lessons; the umuduri is their instrument).
