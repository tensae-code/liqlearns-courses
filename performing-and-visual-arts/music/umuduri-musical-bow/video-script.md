# Video script — The Umuduri Musical Bow

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The umuduri! Rwanda's musical bow.

A short bow, one string — and a calabash voice box.

Face the voice box right, and the sound stands up loud.

Tune it. Finger it. Slowly first — then faster!
