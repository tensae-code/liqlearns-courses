# Hands-on — Music Production and the Recording Studio

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Sound production and recording
Do the practice this part names, with real materials and a partner: Recording terminology; setting up a sound system for recording; recording on analogue equipment — handheld recorders, 4–8 track machines — and on digital equipment — computers; producing music for commercial purposes, advertisements and promos.

## Mixing principles
Do the practice this part names, with real materials and a partner: Signal flow; mono, two-track, four-track, eight-track and multi-track recording; and mixing, controlling and balancing sound on PA systems.
