# Music Production and the Recording Studio

## Do it first

Look at the sub lesson list. Pick the part you already know something about, and say what you know. Then start at Sub lesson 1 — every part builds on the one before it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Sound production and recording

Recording terminology; setting up a sound system for recording; recording on analogue equipment — handheld recorders, 4–8 track machines — and on digital equipment — computers; producing music for commercial purposes, advertisements and promos.

## Sub lesson 2 — Mixing principles

Signal flow; mono, two-track, four-track, eight-track and multi-track recording; and mixing, controlling and balancing sound on PA systems.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion this lesson's one thing in your own words, naming each sub lesson as you go. Companion checks: did every part get named?

## Do It Yourself

1. Do each sub lesson's practice once more, on your own.
2. Teach one part to someone else, the way this lesson taught it to you.
3. Write one sentence: what is this lesson's one thing?
