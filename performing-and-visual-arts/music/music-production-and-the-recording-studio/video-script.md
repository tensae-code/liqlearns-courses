# Video script — Music Production and the Recording Studio

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

**Sound production and recording.** Recording terminology; setting up a sound system for recording; recording on analogue equipment — handheld recorders, 4–8 track machines — and on digital equipment — computers; producing music for commercial purposes, advertisements and promos.

**Mixing principles.** Signal flow; mono, two-track, four-track, eight-track and multi-track recording; and mixing, controlling and balancing sound on PA systems.
