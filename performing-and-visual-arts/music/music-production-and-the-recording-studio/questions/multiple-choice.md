# Music Production and the Recording Studio — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Analogue recording equipment named includes…
a) handheld recorders and 4–8 track machines  b) only computers
**Answer: a) handheld recorders and 4–8 track machines.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Signal flow belongs to…
a) mixing principles  b) costume design
**Answer: a) mixing principles.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Commercial production includes…
a) advertisements and promos  b) silence
**Answer: a) advertisements and promos.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Balancing sound is done on…
a) PA systems  b) paper
**Answer: a) PA systems.**
