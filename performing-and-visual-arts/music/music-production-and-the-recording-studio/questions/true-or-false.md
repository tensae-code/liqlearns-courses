# Music Production and the Recording Studio — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Digital recording uses computers.
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Mixing never involves balancing sound.
**Answer: False — mixing, controlling and balancing sound on PA systems is the closing study.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Recording terminology is learned before recording begins.
**Answer: True.**
