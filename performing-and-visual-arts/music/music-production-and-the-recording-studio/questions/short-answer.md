# Music Production and the Recording Studio — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the track formats in the mixing study.
**Answer: Mono, two-track, four-track, eight-track and multi-track.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What two equipment families are recorded on?
**Answer: Analogue equipment and digital equipment (computers).**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** What must be set up before recording?
**Answer: A sound system for recording.**
