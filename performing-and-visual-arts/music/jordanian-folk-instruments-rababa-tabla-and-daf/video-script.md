This lesson is Jordanian Folk Instruments: Rababa, Tabla and Daf.

Jordan's folk instruments heard and named — the rababa, a string instrument, and the tabla and daf (riq), two rhythm instruments — used in folk music and at Bedouin celebrations; "an artist from my country": the rababa player Abdo Musa.

Watch, listen — then say it back in your own words.
