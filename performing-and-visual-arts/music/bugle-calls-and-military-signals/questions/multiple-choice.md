# Bugle Calls and Military Signals — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Before wireless, battlefield messages travelled by…
a) trumpets and drums  b) telephone  c) radio
**Answer: a) trumpets and drums**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Each unit had…
a) its own call  b) the same call as all others  c) no call
**Answer: a) its own call**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** A call is built from…
a) a few natural tones  b) fifty tones  c) spoken words
**Answer: a) a few natural tones**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** A call begins with…
a) an attention signal  b) the retreat  c) silence
**Answer: a) an attention signal**

**5. (Sub lesson 1)** The second part of a call gives…
a) the order itself  b) a song verse  c) a dance
**Answer: a) the order itself**
