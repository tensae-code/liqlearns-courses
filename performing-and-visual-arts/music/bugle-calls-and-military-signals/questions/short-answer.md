# Bugle Calls and Military Signals — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name two orders a call could give.
**Answer: Any two, e.g. charge and retreat.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Why did each unit need its own call?
**Answer: So its soldiers knew the order was for them.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** What two parts does a call have?
**Answer: An attention signal, then the order itself.**
