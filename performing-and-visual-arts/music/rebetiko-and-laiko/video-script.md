This lesson is Rebetiko and Laiko.

Greece's rebetiko and laiko — the urban popular song traditions of the Greek city. — as Greece's school books teach it: two rebetiko melodies written on the staff, compared for rhythm and melodic movement — their common elements; hearing and singing Manolis Chiotis' «Πολλές φορές».

Watch, listen — then say it back in your own words.
