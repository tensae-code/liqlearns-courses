# Video script — Latin American and Caribbean Music

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

One region — many musics!

Latin America's **genres** — each with its own sound. We listen. We value them. We appreciate them.

And its **instruments** — known not only by name, but by **how they are played**.

A whole region, singing. Listen with respect.
