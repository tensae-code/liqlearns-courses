# Latin American and Caribbean Music

## Do it first

Think of two songs from Latin America that sound different from each other. If one region can hold both, then Latin America does not have one music — it has many. This lesson meets them the way Honduras's Grade 8 book does.

## Sub lesson 1 — The genres of Latin America

The Grade 8 book's sequence **Valorando y apreciando la música latinoamericana — valuing and appreciating Latin American music** studies **the musical genres of Latin America**: the region's kinds of music, **valued and appreciated** — listened to with respect for what each genre is and where it lives.

## Sub lesson 2 — Instruments of Latin America

The same block returns to instrumental music, widened to the region: **Instrumentos de Latinoamérica — the musical instruments of Latin America and how they are played**. An instrument is known by its sound and by the playing that makes it — name and playing together.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion: how does the Grade 8 book study Latin America's music? Companion checks: its genres, valued and appreciated? Its instruments, with how they are played?

## Do It Yourself

1. Name two Latin American genres or songs you know and write one way they differ.
2. Choose one instrument of Latin America and write how it is played.
3. Write one sentence: what does it mean to value and appreciate a region's music?
