# Latin American and Caribbean Music — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How does the Grade 8 book organise Latin American music?
**Answer: By its musical genres, valued and appreciated.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Which country's Grade 8 book teaches this widening to Latin America?
