# Latin American and Caribbean Music — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Latin American music is studied by its genres.
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Valuing and appreciating a region's music means listening to it with respect.
**Answer: True.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The book teaches Latin America's instruments together with how they are played.
**Answer: True.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Naming an instrument is enough — how it is played does not matter.
**Answer: False.** — the playing is taught with the name.
