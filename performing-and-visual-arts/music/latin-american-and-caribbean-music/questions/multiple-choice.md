# Latin American and Caribbean Music — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Grade 8 book studies Latin American music through…
a) its musical genres  b) one single song  c) silence
**Answer: a) its musical genres**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** 'Valorando y apreciando' means the music is…
a) ignored  b) valued and appreciated  c) forbidden
**Answer: b) valued and appreciated**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The instruments sequence teaches the instruments of Latin America…
a) by name only  b) together with how they are played  c) without sound
**Answer: b) together with how they are played** — name and playing together.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** An instrument is known by…
a) its price only  b) its sound and how it is played  c) its colour only
**Answer: b) its sound and how it is played**

**5. (Sub lesson 1)** Latin America, in this lesson, has…
a) one single music  b) many genres  c) no music
**Answer: b) many genres**
