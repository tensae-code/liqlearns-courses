# Hands-on — Latin American and Caribbean Music

The lesson with your ears and hands, not only your eyes.

## Listen and value
Listen to two different pieces of Latin American music (or sing two songs of the region that your group knows). Say one way they differ — that difference is the region's genres at work.

## An instrument and its playing
Choose one instrument of Latin America that you know. Show or describe how it is played — not only its name. A partner repeats the playing back.
