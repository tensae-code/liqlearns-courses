# Video script — Ugandan Folk Songs

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Every people has its song.

Bunyoro sings — Kuhugura. Buganda sings — eggono.

Five tones carry the melody. Instruments answer the voice.

The song is old — grandmother to mother to you. Sing it on.

That is Uganda's folk songs.
