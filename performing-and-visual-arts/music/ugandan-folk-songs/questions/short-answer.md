# Ugandan Folk Songs — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** What is Buganda's vocal tone quality called?
**Answer: Eggono.**

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** What accompanies the vocal music?
**Answer: Traditional instruments.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** How were the folk songs handed down?
**Answer: Orally — transmitted by hearing and singing for centuries.**
