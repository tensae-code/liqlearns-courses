# Ugandan Folk Songs — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Ugandan folk traditions were transmitted…
a) orally, for centuries  b) by printed books only  c) by radio only
**Answer: a) orally, for centuries.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** How many musical traditions does Uganda hold?
a) one for the whole country  b) each ethnic group has its own  c) none
**Answer: b) each ethnic group has its own.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Kuhugura is the vocal tone quality of…
a) Buganda  b) Bunyoro  c) neither — it is an instrument
**Answer: b) Bunyoro.**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Ugandan music usually follows the…
a) pentatonic scale  b) twelve-tone row  c) major scale only
**Answer: a) pentatonic scale** — a few groups use hexatonic.

**5. (Sub lesson 4)** A folk-song performance should depict…
a) a foreign city  b) your natural environment  c) an examination hall
**Answer: b) your natural environment** — with appropriate vocal characteristics and instrument accompaniment.
