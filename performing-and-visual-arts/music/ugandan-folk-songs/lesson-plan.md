# Lesson plan — Ugandan Folk Songs

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Music · **Lesson:** Ugandan Folk Songs
**Place in the curriculum:** Music (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Uganda's folk songs — each ethnic group's own tradition, orally transmitted for centuries, with its own vocal tones, usually on the pentatonic scale, sung with traditional instruments.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this course in the same title.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Every group its own tradition, handed down by mouth** — Every ethnic group has its own musical tradition, orally transmitted for centuries.
2. **Vocal tones: Kuhugura and eggono** — Groups use different vocal tones — in Bunyoro the tone quality is called Kuhugura and in Buganda eggono.
3. **Pentatonic songs with traditional instruments** — Ugandan music usually follows the pentatonic scale (a few groups use hexatonic), and vocal music is accompanied by traditional instruments.
4. **Perform a folk song of your environment** — Identify and perform a folk song depicting your natural environment, with appropriate vocal characteristics and instrument accompaniment.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
