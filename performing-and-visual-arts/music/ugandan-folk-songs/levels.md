# Ugandan Folk Songs — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — Every group its own tradition, handed down by mouth
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Every ethnic group has its own musical tradition, orally transmitted for centuries.

## Sub lesson 2 — Vocal tones: Kuhugura and eggono
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Groups use different vocal tones — in Bunyoro the tone quality is called Kuhugura and in Buganda eggono.

## Sub lesson 3 — Pentatonic songs with traditional instruments
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Ugandan music usually follows the pentatonic scale (a few groups use hexatonic), and vocal music is accompanied by traditional instruments.

## Sub lesson 4 — Perform a folk song of your environment
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Identify and perform a folk song depicting your natural environment, with appropriate vocal characteristics and instrument accompaniment.
