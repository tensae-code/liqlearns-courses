# Hands-on — Ugandan Folk Songs

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Hear the tone
In groups from different peoples, sing one line of a folk song each. The class names what changes — the vocal tone — and hears Kuhugura and eggono as different qualities.

## Sing your environment
Perform your group's folk song depicting your natural environment, with its proper vocal characteristics and instrument accompaniment.
