# Hands-on — Friendship Songs

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Learn it in sections
In a group, learn a short friendship song one section at a time. Keep the rhythm with light clapping and tapping while you learn.

## Four formations
Perform your song four ways: solo, in a pair, in a small group, and all together — adding simple body movements for the full-class performance.
