# Video script — Friendship Songs

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A song for a friend!

A child is alone. Then — a friend comes! And the child is happy.
That is the song: Be My Friend.

We learn it piece by piece. Clap, tap — keep the rhythm!
Sing it alone. Sing it in two. Sing it in a group. Sing it — everybody!

A friendship song — sung together.
