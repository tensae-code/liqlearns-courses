# Friendship Songs — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** From which album does "Be My Friend" come?
**Answer: "Sing with Friends".**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** How is the song's rhythm kept while learning it?
**Answer: By light clapping and tapping.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the four ways the song is performed.
**Answer: Solo, in pairs, in groups and as a whole class.**
