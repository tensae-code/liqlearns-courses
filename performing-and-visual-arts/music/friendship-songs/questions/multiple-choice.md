# Friendship Songs — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The song "Be My Friend" is about…
a) a lonely child who becomes happy when he gains a friend  b) a journey  c) the sea
**Answer: a) a lonely child who becomes happy when he gains a friend.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The song is learned…
a) all at once from a book  b) section by section by rote  c) never sung
**Answer: b) section by section by rote.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Its rhythm is kept by…
a) silence  b) light clapping and tapping  c) stamping only
**Answer: b) light clapping and tapping.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The song can be performed…
a) only solo  b) only by the teacher  c) solo, in pairs, in groups and as a whole class
**Answer: c) solo, in pairs, in groups and as a whole class.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The performance adds…
a) body movements  b) costumes only  c) nothing
**Answer: a) body movements.**
