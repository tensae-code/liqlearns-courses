# Friendship Songs — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The friendship song tells of a child who is happy before he has a friend.
**Answer: False** — he is lonely first, and becomes happy when he gains a friend.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The song is learned section by section.
**Answer: True.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The whole class can perform the song together with body movements.
**Answer: True.**
