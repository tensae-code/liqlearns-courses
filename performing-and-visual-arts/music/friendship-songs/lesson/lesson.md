# Friendship Songs

## Do it first

Think of a friend. Now hum a song you would sing to them. A song made for friendship is this lesson.

## Sub lesson 1 — Learning the friendship song

Morocco's Grade 3 guide teaches the friendship song **"Be My Friend" (كن صديقي)**, from the album "Sing with Friends": a lonely child becomes happy when he gains a friend (summary only — the song's words are not written here). It is learned **section by section by rote**, its **rhythm kept by light clapping and tapping**.

## Sub lesson 2 — Performing it together

The song is performed **solo, in pairs, in groups and as a whole class**, with **body movements** — the same song, carried by one voice, two voices, and everyone's voices together.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion: what is the friendship song about, and in how many ways can it be performed? Companion checks: the lonely child who gains a friend? Solo, pairs, groups, whole class?

## Do It Yourself

1. Learn a short friendship song section by section, keeping its rhythm by clapping.
2. Perform it in two formations — for example in a pair, then as a whole class with movements.
3. Write one sentence: what does a friend change for a lonely child, in the song?
