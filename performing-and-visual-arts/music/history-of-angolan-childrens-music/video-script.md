# Video script — History of Angolan Children's Music

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

**The radio and television programmes.** Angola's children's-music history begins with the 1980s radio and TV programmes — Caluanda Pió, Carrossel, O Sol, and the Pió-Pió street shows — which carried children's songs into homes across the country.

**The child stars and the songwriters who taught them.** The programmes launched child stars — Mamborró, Maya Cool, Yuri da Cunha, Gingas do Maculusso — and behind them stood the songwriters who taught them: a generation of Angolan children learned music from other children, guided by the songwriters.

That is History of Angolan Children's Music — Angola's own books teach it this way.
