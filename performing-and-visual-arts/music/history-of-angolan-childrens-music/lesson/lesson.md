# History of Angolan Children's Music

## Do it first

## Sub lesson 1 — The radio and television programmes

Angola's children's-music history begins with the 1980s radio and TV programmes — Caluanda Pió, Carrossel, O Sol, and the Pió-Pió street shows — which carried children's songs into homes across the country.

## Sub lesson 2 — The child stars and the songwriters who taught them

The programmes launched child stars — Mamborró, Maya Cool, Yuri da Cunha, Gingas do Maculusso — and behind them stood the songwriters who taught them: a generation of Angolan children learned music from other children, guided by the songwriters.
