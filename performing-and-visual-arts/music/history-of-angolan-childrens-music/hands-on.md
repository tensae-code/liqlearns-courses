# Hands-on — History of Angolan Children's Music

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The radio and television programmes
Work through this part with real materials or your own body, the way the book sets it: angola's children's-music history begins with the 1980s radio and TV programmes — Caluanda Pió, Carrossel, O Sol, and the Pió-Pió street shows — which carried children's songs into homes across the country. Show a companion what you made or did, and say what the part teaches.

## The child stars and the songwriters who taught them
Work through this part with real materials or your own body, the way the book sets it: the programmes launched child stars — Mamborró, Maya Cool, Yuri da Cunha, Gingas do Maculusso — and behind them stood the songwriters who taught them: a generation of Angolan children learned music from other children, guided by the songwriters. Show a companion what you made or did, and say what the part teaches.
