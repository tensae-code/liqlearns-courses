This lesson is Timbre: Tone Colour.

Timbre — the nature of the sounds around us differs with the bodies they come from: the rough sound of a wood saw against the smooth sound of a water spring, and a cat's smooth sound sorted against a saw's rough one.

Watch, listen — then say it back in your own words.
