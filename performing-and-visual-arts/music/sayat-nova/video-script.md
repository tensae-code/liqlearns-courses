This is Armenia's own lesson.

**SAYAT-NOVA (1712-1795).** SAYAT-NOVA (1712-1795) — the most famed representative of Armenian ashugh music: invited by Prince Erekle to be court singer-musician, he learned the ashugh measures and to play qyamancha and violin; hear "Without you what shall I do" sung by Ruben Matevosyan, and Sheram's "Pnchlik-mnchlik".

That is Sayat-Nova — Armenia's way, kept by name.
