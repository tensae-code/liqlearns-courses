Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson teaches...
a) another country's arts only  b) Sayat-Nova — Armenia's own teaching of this topic, kept as named parts: SAYAT-NOVA (1712-1795) — the most famed representative of Armenian ashugh music: invited by Prince Erekle to be court singer-musician, he learned the ashugh measures and to play qyamancha and violin; hear "Without you what shall I do" sung by Ruben Matevosyan, and Sheram's "Pnchlik-mnchlik"  c) nothing — it is just names
**Answer: b)** — that is the lesson's one thing.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The first sub lesson is...
a) SAYAT-NOVA (1712-1795)  b) an examination  c) a song from another continent
**Answer: a) SAYAT-NOVA (1712-1795).**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Armenia's own forms in this lesson are...

**Answer: b) kept as named parts.**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** The last sub lesson is...
a) SAYAT-NOVA (1712-1795)  b) the bibliography  c) a review box
