# Sayat-Nova

## Do it first

Name one thing that belongs in this lesson — Sayat-Nova — Armenia's own teaching of this topic, kept as named parts: SAYAT-NOVA (1712-1795) — the most famed representative of Armenian ashugh music: invited by Prince Erekle to be court singer-musician, he learned the ashugh measures and to play qyamancha and violin; hear "Without you what shall I do" sung by Ruben Matevosyan, and Sheram's "Pnchlik-mnchlik". Say what it is in your own words. If you can name one, you are already inside the lesson.

## Sub lesson 1 — SAYAT-NOVA (1712-1795)

SAYAT-NOVA (1712-1795) — the most famed representative of Armenian ashugh music: invited by Prince Erekle to be court singer-musician, he learned the ashugh measures and to play qyamancha and violin; hear "Without you what shall I do" sung by Ruben Matevosyan, and Sheram's "Pnchlik-mnchlik".

## Do It Yourself

1. Choose one named part of this lesson and find out one more true thing about it — from a person who knows it, or from the book your part came from.
2. Make or perform a small piece in the way of that part: a rhythm, a step, a drawing, a shaped piece of clay.
3. Write one sentence: what does this part show about Armenia's own way of teaching its arts?
