# Lesson plan — Singing Our First Songs

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Music · **Lesson:** Singing Our First Songs
**Place in the curriculum:** Music (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** singing Our First Songs — its forms, history and cultural meaning, taught as one topic lesson.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this course in the same title.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **China Grade 6 Music Vol.1** — A song of loving the Milky Way, its melody built on leaps like stars darting — mind the pitch of every leap; an optimistic song of the blue sky calling — sung with pride; the song from the German film The Young Boy — mastering its dotted rhythms and exact note values; a cheerful, vigorous song for sunshine sports; an optional song of spring — Dili Dili
2. **China Grade 6 Music Vol.2** — A song of the grateful heart — quiet telling in the first half, an outcry in the second; the extension teaches signing the song (signs for confidence, fate, thanks); a tender song of growing up in a world of love — a sun and moon in the heart, warm hands and a guiding voice; a unison song of a sky full of loving stars — counting its sections and comparing its shape with the earlier song; a song of the Yangtze as home and mother — its ties, dotted notes and syncopations sung true; a farewell song — Fly Without Worry — schoolmates parting for their own worlds, promising not to cry this time; an optional song — Song and Smile, asking you to take the song home and leave your smile behind
