This lesson is Singing Our First Songs.

Singing Our First Songs — its forms, history and cultural meaning, taught as one topic lesson. — as China's school books teach it: A song of loving the Milky Way, its melody built on leaps like stars darting — mind the pitch of every leap; an optimistic song of the blue sky calling — sung with pride; the song from the German film The Young Boy — mastering its dotted rhythms and exact note values; a cheerful, vigorous song for sunshine sports; an optional song of spring — Dili Dili.

Watch, listen — then say it back in your own words.
