# Singing Our First Songs

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about singing our first songs. Then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — China Grade 6 Music Vol.1

A song of loving the Milky Way, its melody built on leaps like stars darting — mind the pitch of every leap; an optimistic song of the blue sky calling — sung with pride; the song from the German film The Young Boy — mastering its dotted rhythms and exact note values; a cheerful, vigorous song for sunshine sports; an optional song of spring — Dili Dili

## Sub lesson 2 — China Grade 6 Music Vol.2

A song of the grateful heart — quiet telling in the first half, an outcry in the second; the extension teaches signing the song (signs for confidence, fate, thanks); a tender song of growing up in a world of love — a sun and moon in the heart, warm hands and a guiding voice; a unison song of a sky full of loving stars — counting its sections and comparing its shape with the earlier song; a song of the Yangtze as home and mother — its ties, dotted notes and syncopations sung true; a farewell song — Fly Without Worry — schoolmates parting for their own worlds, promising not to cry this time; an optional song — Song and Smile, asking you to take the song home and leave your smile behind
