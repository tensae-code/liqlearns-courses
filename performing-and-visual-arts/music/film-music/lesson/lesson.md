## Sub lesson 1 — John Williams, the cue and the leitmotif

**John Williams** writes film scores for a **large symphony orchestra**. A **cue** is one piece of music for one part of a film; **underscoring** is music under the action and dialogue. Film music gives **atmosphere**, sets **time and place**, **moves the action**, **describes character** and makes **dramatic impact**. A **leitmotif** is a **recurring theme tied to a character or idea** — a device **pioneered by Wagner**.

## Sub lesson 2 — 'Main Title/Rebel Blockade Runner' follows the film

The cue follows the film moment by moment: a **heroic brass fanfare** and the **main theme — Luke's leitmotif** — then **contrasting strings**, a **shimmering vista**, a **piccolo solo**, a **pedal note** and **dissonant brass** building foreboding. It is read from a **three-stave orchestral reduction (short score)**. Wider listening: Williams' **Harry Potter** leitmotifs and Howard Shore's **Lord of the Rings** score.
