# Film Music — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is underscoring?
**Answer: Music played under the action and dialogue.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name two jobs film music does.
**Answer: Any two of: atmosphere; time and place; moving the action; describing character; dramatic impact.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Who wrote the Star Wars score?
**Answer: John Williams.**
