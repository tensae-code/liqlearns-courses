# Film Music — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Film music can describe a character before they speak.
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A leitmotif is heard only once in a whole film.
**Answer: False — it recurs, tied to its character or idea.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Dissonant brass can build a feeling of foreboding.
**Answer: True.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** John Williams wrote for a small folk band only.
**Answer: False — for a large symphony orchestra.**
