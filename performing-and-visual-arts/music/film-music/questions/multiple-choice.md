# Film Music — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A cue is…
a) one piece of music for one part of a film  b) the film's poster  c) the ticket price
**Answer: a) one piece of music for one part of a film**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A leitmotif is a recurring theme tied to…
a) a character or idea  b) the cinema building  c) the popcorn
**Answer: a) a character or idea**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The leitmotif was pioneered by…
a) Wagner  b) Mercury  c) Purcell
**Answer: a) Wagner**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The Star Wars main theme is the leitmotif of…
a) Luke  b) the piccolo  c) the audience
**Answer: a) Luke**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The cue is read from a…
a) three-stave orchestral reduction  b) one-line chant  c) guitar tab only
**Answer: a) three-stave orchestral reduction**
