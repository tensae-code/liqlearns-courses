# Lesson plan — Family Songs

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Music · **Lesson:** Family Songs
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 3 — Music (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** songs that honour the family one by one — mother, father, sister, brother — and then the whole family together, sung in a group and, at the deeper level, sung from written notes.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the learner's own family, and on Animal Songs — the learner can already sing a short song through with its class.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved (the gate: README, "Units, Lessons and Sub Lessons (ruled 2026-11-08)"):

1. **Mother and father songs** — "እናቴ" (My Mother), who thinks of her child morning and evening, and "አባቴ" (My Father), support and shelter who works to provide — the two honouring songs of Grade 1.
2. **Sister and brother songs** — "እህቴ" (My Sister), the playmate — playing, jumping and being happy together — and "ወንድሜ" (My Brother), who advises and cares.
3. **Our families together** — "ቤተሰብ" (Family) and "ቤተሰቦቻችን" (Our Families) — the whole family sung together in groups, and the Grade 2 step: singing the family song from written notes.
4. **My Childhood** — "ልጅነቴ" (My Childhood), source ህይወት ማሞ, a traditional melody — the child's own song inside the family: it celebrates childhood play and Ethiopia as an eternal home, standing in its book just before the photograph work as a sung picture of childhood. (Added by the supplementary build, Group G, from the Grade 2 placement the first groups left unstocked.)

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Animal Songs — singing a short song through. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Sing the mother song and say what it honours: a mother who thinks of her child morning and evening.
2. Sing the father song and say what it honours: support and shelter, working to provide.
3. Sing the sister song (the playmate) and the brother song (who advises and cares).
4. Sing the family songs together in a group, as the teacher intones them.
5. Explain what singing from notes means, in simple words.
6. Sing the My Childhood song and say what it celebrates: childhood play, and Ethiopia as an eternal home.

## What you need
Your voice, and your class to sing with. For the deepest part, a family song written in notes, as in the book.

## If the learner is stuck
- Loses the words → go back to the call-and-answer shape: the teacher (or the audio) sings a short piece, the learner answers with the same piece. Short pieces first, whole song after.
- Loses the tune → hum the song together before singing words; the tune first, the words ride on it.
- Sings but cannot say what the song teaches → ask the lesson's own question: what is this song about, and what does it ask us to do? Every song in this unit teaches something that can be said in one sentence.
