# Hands-on — Family Songs

The lesson with your hands, your feet and your voice together, not only your eyes.

## A song for each chair
Put four chairs in a row for mother, father, sister and brother. A child stands behind each chair and the class sings that person's song. Then everyone sings the whole-family song together.

## Sing it at home
Choose the song of the person who cared for you most this week. Sing it to them at home, all the way through. Tell the class tomorrow what happened.
