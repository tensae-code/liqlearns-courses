# Video script — Family Songs

The super-simple voice, for the sing-along video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The songs are named and summarised, never transcribed; the script sings the learner TO the song, not the song itself.

---

Mother thinks of you, morning and evening. Sing to her.
Father is support and shelter. He works for you. Sing to him.
Sister is your playmate — play, jump, be happy! Sing to her.
Brother advises and cares. Sing to him.

Then sing them all together — our families!
Long life to them!

And childhood itself has a song — "ልጅነቴ", My Childhood: play, and Ethiopia, our eternal home. Sing it.
