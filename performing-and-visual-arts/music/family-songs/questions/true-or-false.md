# Family Songs — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The mother and father songs are thank-you songs, sung with respect.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The book asks you to sing a family song you know and explain its message.
**Answer: True** — Exercise 7, beside the brother song.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The family songs are only ever sung by one person alone.
**Answer: False** — They are sung in groups, as the teacher intones.
