# Family Songs — matching

Match each one on the left with its pair on the right.

1. እናቴ — My Mother — a
2. አባቴ — My Father — b
3. እህቴ — My Sister — c
4. ወንድሜ — My Brother — d

The pairs on the right:
a. thinks of her child morning and evening
b. support and shelter, works to provide
c. the playmate
d. advises and cares

**Answer key:** 1 — a; 2 — b; 3 — c; 4 — d.
