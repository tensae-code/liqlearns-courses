# Family Songs — short answer

Answer in a few words, out loud or in writing.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the four Grade 1 family songs in the book's order.
**Answer:** "እናቴ" (My Mother), "አባቴ" (My Father), "እህቴ" (My Sister), "ወንድሜ" (My Brother).

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** What wish does the Our Families song sing back for mother, father, brother and sister?
**Answer:** Long life to them.
