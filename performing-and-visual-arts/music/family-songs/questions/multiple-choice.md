# Family Songs — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The mother song honours a mother who —
a) cooks only  b) thinks of her child morning and evening  c) sings in the morning only
**Answer: b) thinks of her child morning and evening**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In the father song, father is —
a) a visitor  b) support and shelter who works to provide  c) a playmate
**Answer: b) support and shelter who works to provide**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The sister in her song is —
a) a playmate — playing, jumping, being happy together  b) a teacher  c) a worker in the field
**Answer: a) a playmate — playing, jumping, being happy together**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The brother in his song —
a) sleeps all day  b) advises and cares  c) sings alone
**Answer: b) advises and cares**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** "ቤተሰቦቻችን" (Our Families) is sung —
a) by one child alone  b) in groups, as the teacher intones  c) without singing, only read
**Answer: b) in groups, as the teacher intones**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** Singing from notes means —
a) singing the loudest  b) following the written music notes as you sing  c) singing with no book at all
**Answer: b) following the written music notes as you sing**
