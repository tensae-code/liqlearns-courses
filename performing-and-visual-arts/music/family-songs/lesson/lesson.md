# Family Songs

## Do it first

Say the names of your family out loud: mother, father, sister, brother. Every person you just named has a song in this lesson. By the end you will sing to each of them.

## Sub lesson 1 — Mother and father songs

The first song is **"እናቴ"** — *My Mother*. It is a song of love and lifelong honour for a mother who thinks of her child morning and evening — the first thought of her day and the last of her night.

The second song is **"አባቴ"** — *My Father*. It honours a father as support and shelter: he works to provide for the family, and he worries for his child.

Sing both songs slowly and with respect. These are not shouting songs. They are thank-you songs.

## Sub lesson 2 — Sister and brother songs

The third song is **"እህቴ"** — *My Sister*. A sister in this song is a playmate: playing together, jumping together, being happy together.

The fourth song is **"ወንድሜ"** — *My Brother*. A brother in this song advises and cares — the older voice that guides the younger.

And here the book asks something of you: sing a song you know about your family, and explain its message. Your family's own song belongs in this lesson too.

## Sub lesson 3 — Our families together

Now the family is sung whole. The song **"ቤተሰብ"** — *Family* — honours mother and father as raisers, carers and supporters, to love, honour, obey and care for, wishing them long life. And **"ቤተሰቦቻችን"** — *Our Families* — names them all: mother, father, brother, sister, our carers and support, with the wish of long life sung back like a chorus.

These songs are sung **in groups, as the teacher intones** — the teacher starts the line, the groups sing it back.

And there is one deeper step. In Grade 2 the family song is **sung from notes**: the song is written in music notes, and the singers follow the written notes as they sing — the picture of the family sits beside the written song. Singing from notes means the page tells your voice where to go.

## Sub lesson 4 — My Childhood

One more song belongs to this family — the song of the child himself. In Grade 2 the book gives **"ልጅነቴ"** — *My Childhood*. Its source is named in the book: **ህይወት ማሞ** (Hiwot Mamo), and its melody is **traditional** — a tune carried by the people themselves.

The song celebrates **childhood play** — the playing years themselves, sung with joy — and it sings of **Ethiopia as an eternal home**: the country the child plays in is the home that lasts forever.

In the book the song stands immediately before the photograph work — a **sung picture of childhood**, just before the children meet pictured ones. Sing it as that: your own childhood, sung.

## Say it to your companion

Sing the mother song to your companion. Then say, for each of the four songs, one thing the song honours: mother, father, sister, brother. Finish with the message of a family song you know yourself.

## Do It Yourself

1. Sing all four Grade 1 songs in order: mother, father, sister, brother.
2. Sing a song you know about your family, and tell someone its message in one sentence.
3. At home, sing the mother song or the father song to your family. Watch their faces.
