# Video script — Composing and Producing Music with Computers

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This is Malaysia's own lesson.

**Creating rhythm patterns and melodies in notation software (Sibelius.** Units 1–7 Ekspresi Kreatif — creating rhythm patterns and melodies in notation software (Sibelius named), improvising, composing a cheerful song and a sad slow song from a stimulus, changing tempo, and saving work as MIDI.

**The production strand whole.** Units 1–6 — the production strand whole: MIDI in the music world, MIDI in composition, connecting MIDI and audio interfaces to a computer, processing MIDI files into a DAW, a DAW folio, exploring and editing MIDI files and scores, Virtual Studio Technology (VST) exploration, producing music with a DAW, and editing MIDI and audio data.

That is Composing and Producing Music with Computers — Malaysia's way, kept by name.
