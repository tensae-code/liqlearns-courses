# Composing and Producing Music with Computers

## Do it first

Name one thing that belongs in this lesson — Making music with software — composing on a computer, MIDI, DAW and VST production. Say what it is in your own words. If you can name one, you are already inside the lesson.

## Sub lesson 1 — Creating rhythm patterns and melodies in notation software (Sibelius

Units 1–7 Ekspresi Kreatif — creating rhythm patterns and melodies in notation software (Sibelius named), improvising, composing a cheerful song and a sad slow song from a stimulus, changing tempo, and saving work as MIDI.

## Sub lesson 2 — The production strand whole

Units 1–6 — the production strand whole: MIDI in the music world, MIDI in composition, connecting MIDI and audio interfaces to a computer, processing MIDI files into a DAW, a DAW folio, exploring and editing MIDI files and scores, Virtual Studio Technology (VST) exploration, producing music with a DAW, and editing MIDI and audio data.
