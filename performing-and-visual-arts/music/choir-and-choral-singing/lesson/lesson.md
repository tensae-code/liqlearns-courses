# Choir and Choral Singing

## Do it first

Quick start: say one thing you already know — a tune, a dance, a name — that belongs to this lesson's world. Keep it in mind; the parts will meet it.

## Sub lesson 1 — The M.A. Quartet

Back to Europe: the vocal groups. The **M.A. Quartet**, **Iceland's famous male quartet**, and the song **Laugardagskvöld** — a Swedish song in Magnús Ásgeirsson's translation, sung by the whole class.

## Sub lesson 2 — The Comedian Harmonists and the other vocal groups

Berlin's **Comedian Harmonists**; Iceland's **Öskubuskur** (five schoolgirls, then a famous duo) and **Tónasystur**; America's **Andrews Sisters** — close-harmony groups whose voices lock together like instruments.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the parts in order, one sentence each. Companion checks: did every part get its sentence, and did the order hold?

## Do It Yourself

1. Write the parts' names from memory, in order.
2. Do the hands-on activity once more, teaching it to someone else.
3. Write one sentence: what is the one thing this lesson teaches?
