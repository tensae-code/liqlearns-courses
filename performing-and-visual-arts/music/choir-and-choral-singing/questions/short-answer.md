# Choir and Choral Singing — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name two Icelandic vocal groups in this lesson.
**Answer: Any two of: M.A. Quartet, Öskubuskur, Tónasystur.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is close harmony?
**Answer: Voices singing tightly locked together, chord against chord.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Who were the Tónasystur's fellow Icelanders in the second part?
**Answer: Öskubuskur (and the M.A. Quartet in the first part).**
