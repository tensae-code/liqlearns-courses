# Choir and Choral Singing — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The M.A. Quartet is…
a) Iceland's famous male quartet  b) a Berlin group  c) a brass band
**Answer: a) Iceland's famous male quartet**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Laugardagskvöld is a Swedish song in translation by…
a) Magnús Ásgeirsson  b) Kálmán  c) Weill
**Answer: a) Magnús Ásgeirsson**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The Comedian Harmonists were from…
a) Berlin  b) Havana  c) Kansas City
**Answer: a) Berlin**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Öskubuskur began as…
a) five schoolgirls  b) a male quartet  c) a dance band
**Answer: a) five schoolgirls**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The Andrews Sisters were from…
a) America  b) Iceland  c) Germany
**Answer: a) America**
