# Hands-on — Indian Classical Music and Karnatak

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Indian classical music
Work the part with your hands, voice or body as its own craft directs: make it, perform it, or shape it — then show a companion and name the part aloud.

## Karnatak (South Indian classical music)
Work the part with your hands, voice or body as its own craft directs: make it, perform it, or shape it — then show a companion and name the part aloud.
