# Indian Classical Music and Karnatak

## Do it first

Name one thing that belongs in this lesson — India's classical music, with Malaysia's Karnatak. Say what it is in your own words. If you can name one, you are already inside the lesson.

## Sub lesson 1 — Indian classical music

Unit Bumiku Tercinta Apresiasi: Muzik Klasikal India — Indian classical music: appreciating its sound world, instruments and performance practice as one of Malaysia's classical traditions.

## Sub lesson 2 — Karnatak (South Indian classical music)

Units 1–5 Karnatak — Karnatak (South Indian classical music): its study (kajian), its sound and structures, and a closing dialogue comparing Sewang and Karnatak.
