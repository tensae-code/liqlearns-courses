# Hands-on — Sounds Around Us

The lesson with your hands, your feet and your voice together, not only your eyes.

## The one-minute listening game
Sit outside or by an open door. Everyone closes their eyes for one minute. Nobody talks. When the minute ends, each person names one sound they heard and what made it. The one who names a sound nobody else heard wins the round.

## Sound walk
Walk slowly from home to school — or around the schoolyard — with a partner. At every new sound, stop, point, and whisper the maker's name. Count how many different makers you find.

## Guess my sound
In a small group, one child imitates an animal, a bird or an instrument sound. The others guess the maker. The guesser who is right imitates next.
