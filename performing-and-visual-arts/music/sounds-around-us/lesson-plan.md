# Lesson plan — Sounds Around Us

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Music · **Lesson:** Sounds Around Us
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 3 — Music (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** listening comes first: the learner hears the sounds of home, the road and the schoolyard, names what makes each sound, and then makes the sounds back — sound is anything heard by the ear.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on nothing but the child's own ears — the sounds heard on the way to school and inside the home are the first music lesson.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved (the gate: README, "Units, Lessons and Sub Lessons (ruled 2026-11-08)"):

1. **Sounds around us** — the opening listening work: what sounds did you hear on the way to school, and what sounds do you know inside your home? Listening is naming real sounds.
2. **Sound and imitating sounds** — sound is anything heard by the ear; around us are many different sounds — birds, domestic and wild animals, music instruments — and the learner imitates the sounds of animals and birds they know.

3. **Kinds of music and sound in the Grade 2 guide (teacher guide — evidence)** — the G2 guide's Chapter 1 introduction: PVA inside children's daily life; children distinguish kinds of music and sound (guide, section level).

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: nothing — this is the first lesson of the Music unit. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name sounds heard on the way to school and inside the home, and say what makes each sound.
2. Say what sound is: anything heard by the ear.
3. Sort sound-makers into birds, domestic and wild animals, and music instruments.
4. Imitate the sounds of animals and birds you know.
5. Listen to a simple song carefully and tell what you heard in it.

## What you need
Your ears. A quiet minute. Paper and a pencil for the sound list.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — Close your eyes for one minute and just listen. Do not talk. When the minute ends, name every sound you heard — a voice, a door, a bird, a car, your own breathing. That listening is where this whole Music course begins: before we sing anything, we hear. (lesson/lesson.md).

## If the learner is stuck
- Loses the words → go back to the call-and-answer shape: the teacher (or the audio) sings a short piece, the learner answers with the same piece. Short pieces first, whole song after.
- Loses the tune → hum the song together before singing words; the tune first, the words ride on it.
- Sings but cannot say what the song teaches → ask the lesson's own question: what is this song about, and what does it ask us to do? Every song in this unit teaches something that can be said in one sentence.
