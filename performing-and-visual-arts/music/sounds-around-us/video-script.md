# Video script — Sounds Around Us

The super-simple voice, for the sing-along video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The songs are named and summarised, never transcribed; the script sings the learner TO the song, not the song itself.

---

Close your eyes. Listen.

A car. A cow. A bird in the tree.
You heard them on your way to school.

What is a sound?
A sound is anything heard by the ear.

Birds sing. Animals call. Instruments play.
Three makers of sound.

Now you make one back.
Imitate a bird. Imitate a cow.
Your friend guesses the maker.

Listen first. Name it. Make it back.
That is where music begins.
