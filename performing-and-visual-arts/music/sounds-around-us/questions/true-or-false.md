# Sounds Around Us — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Every sound around us is made by something.
**Answer: True** — A person, an animal, a bird, a machine, the wind or the rain.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** We should sing a song before we have listened to it carefully.
**Answer: False** — Listen first, then sing.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A cow and a goat are domestic animals.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** An instrument's sound is made by the wind only.
**Answer: False** — Instruments are sounds people make on purpose.
