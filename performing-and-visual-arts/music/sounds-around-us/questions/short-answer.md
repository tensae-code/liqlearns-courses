# Sounds Around Us — short answer

Answer in a few words, out loud or in writing.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name two sounds you can hear inside a home.
**Answer:** Any two from the lesson, e.g. water pouring; a pot on the fire; a baby crying; a radio playing; feet on the floor.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name one domestic animal and the sound group it belongs to.
**Answer:** E.g. the cow — the animals group (domestic animals).

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name the two kinds the guide's opening has children distinguish.
**Answer:** Kinds of music and kinds of sound.
