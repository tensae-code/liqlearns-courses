# Sounds Around Us — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is listening, in this lesson?
a) hearing a sound and naming the thing that makes it  b) singing a song loudly  c) drawing a picture of a sound
**Answer: a) hearing a sound and naming the thing that makes it** — Listening is naming real sounds and their makers.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which of these is a sound you might hear on the way to school?
a) a car passing  b) the colour blue  c) a quiet drawing
**Answer: a) a car passing**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Sound is —
a) anything seen by the eye  b) anything heard by the ear  c) anything held in the hand
**Answer: b) anything heard by the ear** — The book's own definition.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which list names the three big groups of sound-makers?
a) birds, animals, instruments  b) cars, doors, rain  c) songs, dances, drawings
**Answer: a) birds, animals, instruments**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Imitating a sound means —
a) writing it down  b) making the sound back with your voice  c) forgetting it
**Answer: b) making the sound back with your voice**
