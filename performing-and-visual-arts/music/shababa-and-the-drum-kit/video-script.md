# Video script — The Shababa and the Drum Kit

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is The Shababa and the Drum Kit.

The shababa, a folk wind instrument widespread in the Arab countries that makes long and short melodic sounds, set against the drum kit of Western and Arab countries, a group of instruments that makes only short beat sounds.

Watch, listen — then say it back in your own words.
