# Hands-on — Music in the Media and on Radio

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Work the lesson's own way
Work through the sub lessons in order — The kinds of sound media; Radio as an art medium; Programming for the listening audience — doing each part the way Cuba's books direct: by making, moving, singing or looking closely, as the part asks. Show your companion what you made or did, and name the part it belongs to.
