# Video script — Music in Public Life

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is Music in Public Life.

Music out in the world — in advertising, in public spaces and soundscapes, and the social and emotional air it makes. — as Greece's school books teach it: logging the music of five radio or TV advertisements and analysing what each tune is made to do.

Watch, listen — then say it back in your own words.
