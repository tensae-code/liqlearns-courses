# Moving in Note Values — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.


**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In the rows activity, pupils move…
  a) While sounding the note values b) In silence, watching the teacher c) Only after the lesson ends
**Answer: a) While sounding the note values** — §1.1.1 — movement and sounding happen together.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A whole note's movement lasts…
  a) Four beats b) One beat c) Two beats
**Answer: a) Four beats** — the whole note is the longest value: 4 beats.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** In the circles, the pupils' arms are…
  a) Raised above the head b) Folded behind the back c) Holding books
**Answer: a) Raised above the head** — the book's formation: three circles, arms raised.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** There are how many circles?
  a) Three b) One c) Seven
**Answer: a) Three** — the activity forms three circles.
