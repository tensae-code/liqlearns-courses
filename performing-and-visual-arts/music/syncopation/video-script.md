This is Moldova's own lesson.

**Sincopa muzicală.** Unit 2, Sincopa muzicală — syncopation: the accent shifted from the strong beat to the weak one before it, a favourite of dance music; signs that lengthen a note (tie and dot).

That is Syncopation — Moldova's way, kept by name.
