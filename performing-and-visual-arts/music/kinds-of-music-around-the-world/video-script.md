# Video script — Kinds of Music Around the World

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Music travels the whole world — and every kind has a home.

**Reggae** — Jamaica. The 1960s. Bob Marley made it famous.
**Jazz** — the USA. The early 1900s. Louis Armstrong made it famous.
**Gospel** — Christian music. Sung in churches and concerts — in the USA, Ireland and Britain.

Ask two questions, and you can tell any kind:
Where does it come from? Who made it famous?

That is how you read the world's music table.
