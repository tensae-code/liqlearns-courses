# Lesson plan — Kinds of Music Around the World

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Music · **Lesson:** Kinds of Music Around the World
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 3 — Music (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** kinds of music around the world can be told by where each kind comes from and who made it famous — reggae, jazz and gospel, as Cyprus's Year 6 reading table sets them out (teacher unit material — evidence).
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the songs lessons; this lesson is labelled teacher unit material — evidence throughout, because its Cyprus source is a teacher's unit plan, not a student textbook.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Reggae** — Jamaica, 1960s, Bob Marley.
2. **Jazz** — USA, early 20th century, Louis Armstrong.
3. **Gospel** — Christian music of churches and concerts, USA/Ireland/Britain.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Kinds of Traditional Music. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name three kinds of music from around the world.
2. For each kind, say where it comes from and who made it famous, or where it is sung.

## What you need
Your voice and a friend to test you.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — name a kind of music and the place it comes from (lesson/lesson.md).
2. **The explanation** — reggae, jazz and gospel, in order (lesson/lesson.md).
   - ADD IMAGE: a picture of a world map with Jamaica, the USA, Ireland and Britain marked — the student points to each kind's home.
3. **Say it to your companion** — tell each kind by its place and its famous name (lesson/lesson.md).
   - ADD VIDEO: the script in video-script.md, filmed — made later, when videos are made.
4. **Questions** — from questions/: multiple-choice, true-or-false, short-answer (levels.md).
5. **Hands-on** — hands-on.md.
6. **Do It Yourself** — build your own three-row table of world music kinds (lesson/lesson.md).

## Add-ons for this lesson (the full list)
- IMAGE (described only) — a world map marking Jamaica, the USA, Ireland and Britain → the student points to each kind's home.
- VIDEO — the video-script filmed (made later) → the student listens and answers.

## If the learner is stuck
- Mixes up the kinds → use the table's own way: ask first *where does it come from?* and then *who made it famous?* The two answers separate reggae, jazz and gospel cleanly.
