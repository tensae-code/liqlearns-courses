# Hands-on — Kinds of Music Around the World

The lesson with your hands and your voice, not only your eyes.

## Point to the home
On a map — drawn, printed or imagined — one player names a kind (reggae, jazz, gospel) and the other points to its home and names who made it famous, or where it is sung. Three kinds minimum, then swap.

## Build the table
Draw a table with three rows and three columns: kind, where it comes from, who made it famous / where it is sung. Fill it in from memory, then check it against the lesson.
