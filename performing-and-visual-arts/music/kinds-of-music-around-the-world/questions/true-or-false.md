# Kinds of Music Around the World — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Reggae began in Jamaica in the 1960s.
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Louis Armstrong is the name the table gives for reggae.
**Answer: False** — Louis Armstrong is given for jazz; Bob Marley is given for reggae.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Gospel is Christian music sung in churches and concerts.
**Answer: True.**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** The Cyprus table tells each kind by where it comes from and who made it famous.
**Answer: True.**
