# Kinds of Music Around the World — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Reggae comes from…
a) Jamaica  b) Ireland  c) Britain
**Answer: a) Jamaica.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Who made reggae famous, in the Cyprus table?
a) Louis Armstrong  b) Bob Marley  c) no one is named
**Answer: b) Bob Marley.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Jazz began in…
a) the 1960s in Jamaica  b) the early 20th century in the USA  c) churches in Ireland
**Answer: b) the early 20th century in the USA.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Who made jazz famous, in the Cyprus table?
a) Bob Marley  b) Louis Armstrong  c) no one is named
**Answer: b) Louis Armstrong.**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Gospel is…
a) a work song  b) Christian music of churches and concerts  c) traditional Irish music
**Answer: b) Christian music of churches and concerts.**
