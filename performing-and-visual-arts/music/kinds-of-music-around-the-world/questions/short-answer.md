# Kinds of Music Around the World — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** When did reggae begin, and where?
**Answer: In Jamaica, in the 1960s.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Tell jazz in the table's way: where, when, and who made it famous?
**Answer: The USA, the early 20th century, Louis Armstrong.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In which places does the Cyprus table place gospel?
**Answer: The USA, Ireland and Britain.**
