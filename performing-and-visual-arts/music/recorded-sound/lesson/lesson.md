# Recorded Sound

## Do it first

Name one thing that belongs in this lesson — Recording sound — from Edison's phonograph (1877) down the line of sound carriers. Say what it is in your own words. If you can name one, you are already inside the lesson.

## Sub lesson 1 — Edison's phonograph (1877), the first machine to record and play back sound

Edison's phonograph (1877), the first machine to record and play back sound; from it the whole line of sound carriers — gramophone, tape recorder, cassette, CD, mp3 and streaming — music no longer heard only in the moment of performance.
