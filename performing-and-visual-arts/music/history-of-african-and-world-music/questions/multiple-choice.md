# History of African and World Music — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.


**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The guide's first history lesson covers…
  a) The history of African music b) Only Ethiopian chant c) Only European opera
**Answer: a) The history of African music** — Lessons 22–23, first part — African music's history.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The guide pictures this history as…
  a) A timeline of African instruments and styles across centuries b) A single portrait c) A map with no dates
**Answer: a) A timeline of African instruments and styles across centuries** — the placement's own picture slot for the lesson.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The guide's widening order runs…
  a) Ethiopian → African → world history b) World → Ethiopian only c) A single country's history alone
**Answer: a) Ethiopian → African → world history** — Grade 9's Ethiopian history widens to Africa, then the world.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Africa's place in world music history, in this teaching, is…
  a) A foundation, not an appendix b) An appendix c) Absent
**Answer: a) A foundation, not an appendix** — the world's oldest instruments stand on African ground.
