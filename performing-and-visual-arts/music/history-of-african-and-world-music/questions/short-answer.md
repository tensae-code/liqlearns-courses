# History of African and World Music — short answer

Answer in a short sentence or a short list.


**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why is this lesson evidence only?
**Answer:** Grade 10 has no student book — its content stands on the teacher guide alone, and is labelled so.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name two oldest-music witnesses this course has already met.
**Answer:** The Hurrian Hymn No. 6 (earliest melody) and the Seikilos Epitaph (oldest complete surviving composition).
