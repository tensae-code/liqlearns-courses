# Lesson plan — History of African and World Music

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Music · **Lesson:** History of African and World Music
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 3 — Music (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the history of African music and the history of world music, as the Grade 10 teacher guide's history unit sets them (Lessons 22–23) — evidence only, for Grade 10 has no student book.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the whole course's Ethiopian histories; this lesson is teacher-guide evidence throughout and says so.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The history of African music (teacher guide — evidence)** — Guide Lessons 22–23, first half: African music's history — its instruments and styles across centuries, pictured as a timeline.
2. **The history of world music (teacher guide — evidence)** — Lessons 22–23, second half: world music's history — the widening from continent to world, with Ethiopia inside it.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Ethiopian Traditional Music and Its History; History of Indigenous Music. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Tell what the guide's two history lessons cover: African music's history; world music's history.
2. Place Ethiopia's music inside both histories in two sentences.
3. Say why this lesson is evidence only.

## What you need
Only the timeline habit — centuries laid in a row, Africa first, as the guide orders it.
