# The Russian National School

## Do it first

Name one thing that belongs in this lesson — The Russian national school — Glinka, father of Russian classical music, and the Five: Mussorgsky, Rimsky-Korsakov, Borodin and their companions. Say what it is in your own words. If you can name one, you are already inside the lesson.

## Sub lesson 1 — Mikhail Glinka (1804–1857), father of Russian classical music

Mikhail Glinka (1804–1857), father of Russian classical music: "Ivan Susanin" founding the heroic-historical opera and "Ruslan and Lyudmila" the fairy-tale opera, Western forms filled with Russian folk melody and patriotic chorus.

## Sub lesson 2 — the Russian Five

the Russian Five: Balakirev, Cui, Mussorgsky ("Boris Godunov", "Pictures at an Exhibition"), Rimsky-Korsakov ("Scheherazade") and Borodin ("Prince Igor") — mostly self-taught, building a Russian national style on folk music and history; the national symphony defined.
