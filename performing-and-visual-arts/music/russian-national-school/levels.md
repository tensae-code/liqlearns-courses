## Sub lesson 1 — Mikhail Glinka (1804–1857), father of Russian classical music
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Mikhail Glinka (1804–1857), father of Russian classical music: "Ivan Susanin" founding the heroic-historical opera and "Ruslan and Lyudmila" the fairy-tale opera, Western forms filled with Russian folk melody and patriotic chorus.

## Sub lesson 2 — the Russian Five
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: the Russian Five: Balakirev, Cui, Mussorgsky ("Boris Godunov", "Pictures at an Exhibition"), Rimsky-Korsakov ("Scheherazade") and Borodin ("Prince Igor") — mostly self-taught, building a Russian national style on folk music and history; the national symphony defined.
