# Lesson plan — Turkmen National Instruments

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Music · **Lesson:** Turkmen National Instruments
**Place in the curriculum:** Music (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Turkmenistan's national musical instruments — the dutar, gyjak, gargy tüýdük and dilli tüýdük, with the gopuz of the Russian-track book — pictured, named and heard as the country's own family.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this course.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The four national instruments** — dutar, gyjak, gargy tüýdük and dilli tüýdük, pictured and named; the "Dutar" movement from Halmämmedow's "Türkmenistan" to listen to.
2. **The instruments in the Russian-track book** — the gopuz, dutar and gijak (gyjak) pictured and named; the words dutarist and pianist explained.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier Music lessons of this course. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name the four instruments.
2. Name the gopuz and say what a dutarist is.
## What you need
Your ears, and a picture of each instrument to point to.
