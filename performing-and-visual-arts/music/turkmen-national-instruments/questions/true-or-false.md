# Turkmen National Instruments — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The gyjak is one of Turkmenistan's national instruments.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The national instruments appear only after the world instruments in the book.
**Answer: False** — they are pictured before any world instrument appears.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Gijak and gyjak name the same instrument in the two tracks.
**Answer: True**
