# Turkmen National Instruments — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the four national instruments of the Turkmen-track book.
**Answer: Dutar, gyjak, gargy tüýdük and dilli tüýdük.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which movement of Halmämmedow's "Türkmenistan" does the book set for listening?
**Answer: The "Dutar" movement.**
