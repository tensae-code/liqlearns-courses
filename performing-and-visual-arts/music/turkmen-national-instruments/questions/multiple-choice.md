# Turkmen National Instruments — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which is a Turkmen national instrument?
a) the dutar  b) the clarinet  c) the accordion
**Answer: a) the dutar**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The dutar is made of…
a) glass  b) mulberry wood, with two strings  c) metal
**Answer: b) mulberry wood, with two strings**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The gopuz appears in…
a) the Russian-track book's instruments chapter  b) a maths book  c) nowhere
**Answer: a) the Russian-track book's instruments chapter**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A dutarist is…
a) a dutar maker  b) a person who plays the dutar  c) a song
**Answer: b) a person who plays the dutar**
