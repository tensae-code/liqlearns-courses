# Hands-on — Turkmen National Instruments

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Name that instrument
One learner mimes playing an instrument — two strings plucked, a bow drawn, a flute held to the lips. The others name it in Turkmen: dutar, gyjak, gargy tüýdük, dilli tüýdük, gopuz. Then draw the dutar and label its two strings and mulberry-wood body.
