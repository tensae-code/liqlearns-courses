# Video script — Arranging and Interpreting Music

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

This is Indonesia's own lesson.

**Bab IV, Kegiatan 2–3.** developing rhythm patterns and creating one's own music (musik kreasi) from note and rhythm patterns.

**Bab 3 Membuat Kreasi Musik, Kegiatan 1 Musikku, Variasiku.** making a variation of a children's song by changing its rhythm pattern, tempo, dynamics and notes.

That is Arranging and Interpreting Music — Indonesia's way, kept by name.
