# Arranging and Interpreting Music

## Do it first

Name one thing that belongs in this lesson — How music is arranged and re-dressed for new voices and instruments, varied, and interpreted differently by each performer. Say what it is in your own words. If you can name one, you are already inside the lesson.

## Sub lesson 1 — Bab IV, Kegiatan 2–3

developing rhythm patterns and creating one's own music (musik kreasi) from note and rhythm patterns.

## Sub lesson 2 — Bab 3 Membuat Kreasi Musik, Kegiatan 1 Musikku, Variasiku

making a variation of a children's song by changing its rhythm pattern, tempo, dynamics and notes.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson holds, part by part, in your own words. Companion checks: did every part get named, with its Indonesian name kept where the book gives one?

## Do It Yourself

1. Choose one named part of this lesson and find out one more true thing about it — from a person who knows it, or from the book your part came from.
2. Make or perform a small piece in the way of that part: a rhythm, a step, a drawing, a shaped piece of clay.
3. Write one sentence: what does this part show about Indonesia's own way of teaching its arts?
