# Musical Avant-Gardes and Electronic Music — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In which century did the musical avant-gardes arise?
**Answer: The 20th century.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the avant-garde the book names among them.
**Answer: Electronic music.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Do the avant-gardes end in the 20th century?
**Answer: No — they run from the 20th century to our days.**
