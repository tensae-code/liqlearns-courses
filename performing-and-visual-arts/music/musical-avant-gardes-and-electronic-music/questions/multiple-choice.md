# Musical Avant-Gardes and Electronic Music — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The avant-gardes arose…
a) from the 20th century to our days  b) in the Middle Ages  c) only last year
**Answer: a) from the 20th century to our days**.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** An avant-garde in music means…
a) a new movement that breaks with what came before  b) old music repeated  c) silence
**Answer: a) a new movement that breaks with what came before**.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Electronic music is…
a) one of the avant-gardes  b) older than all the others  c) not music
**Answer: a) one of the avant-gardes**.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Electronic music is made with…
a) electronic means  b) only voices  c) only drums
**Answer: a) electronic means**.

**5. (Sub lesson 1)** The lesson studies the avant-gardes…
a) as a line from the 20th century to today  b) in one year only  c) backwards only
**Answer: a) as a line from the 20th century to today**.
