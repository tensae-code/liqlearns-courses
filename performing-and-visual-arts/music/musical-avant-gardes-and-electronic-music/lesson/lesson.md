# Musical Avant-Gardes and Electronic Music

## Do it first

Quick start: A hundred years of new sounds — each generation breaking the rules. Then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — The musical avant-gardes of the 20th century

The **musical avant-gardes** are the **new directions that arose in music from the 20th century to our days** — movement after movement breaking with what came before and remaking what music could be. The Grade 11 book studies them as one line: *Vanguardias del Siglo XX*.

## Sub lesson 2 — Electronic music

Among the avant-gardes the book names **electronic music** — music made with **electronic means**, the sound of machines turned into an art of its own, standing in the same line as the century's other new directions.
