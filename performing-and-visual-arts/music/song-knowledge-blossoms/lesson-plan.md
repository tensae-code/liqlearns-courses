# Lesson plan — Song: Knowledge Blossoms

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Music · **Lesson:** Song: Knowledge Blossoms
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 3 — Music (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the chant እውቀት ያብብ (Knowledge Blossoms) — knowledge and craft blooming across the country's professions, each named and honoured; placed by title and themes only.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Anthems and Songs That Teach — the learner knows songs carry a country's values; this chant carries its workforce.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Before this lesson
- Anthems and Songs That Teach. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name the chant and translate its title.
2. List the professions the chant names as knowledge.
3. Say the chant's whole teaching in two sentences.

## What you need
Only voices — the chant is placed by title and themes only.
