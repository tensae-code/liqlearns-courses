# Song: Knowledge Blossoms — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.


**1. (Sub lesson 1)** እውቀት ያብብ means…
  a) Knowledge Blossoms b) The Careful Student c) We Also Have Music
**Answer: a) Knowledge Blossoms** — the chant's title, as the book prints it.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which pair stands in the chant as knowledge at work?
  a) The builder builds, the farmer ploughs b) The singer sleeps, the dancer rests c) The driver waits, the shop closes
**Answer: a) The builder builds, the farmer ploughs** — the chant's own profession pairs.
