# Song: Knowledge Blossoms

## Do it first

On your road today you passed knowledge at work: a teacher teaching, a farmer ploughing, a builder building. The chant in this lesson gathers them all into one garden and says: knowledge blossoms. Meet it by its name and its themes — its words are the book's to sing, not ours to copy.

## Sub lesson 1 — እውቀት ያብብ — Knowledge Blossoms

The Grade 6 book prints the chant **እውቀት ያብብ (Knowledge Blossoms)** (§5.3.1, printed p. 110): **knowledge and craft bloom in our country, for our people's peace and development** — the **teacher teaches, the student learns; the builder builds the house, the farmer ploughs; the writer writes, the musician plays, the painter paints, the doctor heals**. After singing, learners tell **which professions the chant names as knowledge** — that naming is the lesson's test. The chant is placed by title and themes only; its words are not transcribed.

## Say it to your companion

Name the chant and its professions for your companion; let them add any profession the chant's garden is missing and say its work.

## Do It Yourself

1. Write the chant's profession list from memory and check it.
2. Add one verse-theme of your own: one more profession, named and honoured the same way.
