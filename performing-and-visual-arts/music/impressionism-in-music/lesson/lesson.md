# Impressionism in Music

## Do it first

Quick start: say one thing you already know — a tune, a dance, a name — that belongs to this lesson's world. Keep it in mind; the parts will meet it.

## Sub lesson 1 — La Belle Époque and Debussy

In **La Belle Époque**, **Debussy** painted with sound. His **"La fille aux cheveux de lin"** (The Girl with the Flaxen Hair) is heard on **an unbroken thread from Chopin to Debussy** — and the pianist's touch on the keys should be **as soft as Monet's brushstrokes** on canvas. The lesson asks every player: how hard or soft is your own touch, and can it change from song to song?

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the parts in order, one sentence each. Companion checks: did every part get its sentence, and did the order hold?

## Do It Yourself

1. Write the parts' names from memory, in order.
2. Do the hands-on activity once more, teaching it to someone else.
3. Write one sentence: what is the one thing this lesson teaches?
