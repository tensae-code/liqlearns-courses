# Impressionism in Music — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Impressionist music paints atmosphere and colour.
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Debussy's touch should be harsh and heavy.
**Answer: False.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** There is a thread from Chopin to Debussy.
**Answer: True.**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** A player's touch must stay the same for every song.
**Answer: False.**
