# Impressionism in Music — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Debussy's piece in this lesson is…
a) La fille aux cheveux de lin  b) the Blue Danube  c) Tiger Rag
**Answer: a) La fille aux cheveux de lin**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The thread runs unbroken from…
a) Chopin to Debussy  b) Sousa to Joplin  c) no one to no one
**Answer: a) Chopin to Debussy**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The pianist's touch should be as soft as…
a) Monet's brushstrokes  b) a drum beat  c) a march step
**Answer: a) Monet's brushstrokes**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Monet was a…
a) painter  b) bandleader  c) sailor
**Answer: a) painter**

**5. (Sub lesson 1)** Your touch on the keys can…
a) change from song to song  b) never change  c) only get harder
**Answer: a) change from song to song**
