# Music Technology and Electronic Instruments — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson teaches mainly about…
a) the weather only  b) music technology and electronic instruments  c) none of these
**Answer: b) music technology and electronic instruments.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The first part of the lesson is…
a) the last part of another lesson  b) Topic 36 area / listening 54–55: electric and electronic musical instruments and how music is amplified (озвучаване)  c) none of these
**Answer: b) Topic 36 area / listening 54–55: electric and electronic musical instruments and how music is amplified (озвучаване).**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Bulgaria's books teach this lesson as…
a) a secret kept from pupils  b) a named part of the school art course  c) none of these
**Answer: b) a named part of the school art course.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Another part of the lesson is…
a) a part of a different course  b) Topics 31–32: Кийборд (keyboard) and Електрическа китара (electric guitar)  c) none of these
**Answer: b) Topics 31–32: Кийборд (keyboard) and Електрическа китара (electric guitar).**

**5. (Sub lesson 1)** Every part of this lesson comes from…
a) a guess with no book behind it  b) a Bulgarian school book, with its printed pages named in the sources  c) none of these
**Answer: b) a Bulgarian school book, with its printed pages named in the sources.**
