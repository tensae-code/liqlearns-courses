This lesson is Performance Forms: Vocal and Instrumental.

The forms of performance — solo, ensemble and orchestra for instruments, and solo, part-singing and chorus for voices. — as Vietnam's school books teach it: The forms of singing performance — solo (đơn ca, one singer), duet (song ca, two), trio (tam ca, three), group song (tốp ca) and choir (đồng ca, many) — and choosing a form to perform the wind-chime song.

Watch, listen — then say it back in your own words.
