# The Orchestra and Its Instruments — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Malta meets each orchestral instrument first by…
a) its price  b) its recorded sound and timbre  c) its colour
**Answer: b) its recorded sound and timbre — ears first.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The four orchestral families are…
a) Strings, Woodwinds, Brass, Percussion  b) Strings, Drums, Bells, Keys  c) Wood, Metal, Skin, Air
**Answer: a) Strings, Woodwinds, Brass, Percussion.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In Grieg's story, the sneaking is painted by…
a) the whole orchestra  b) pizzicato violins  c) the timpani alone
**Answer: b) pizzicato violins.**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Brass instruments are known by…
a) reeds  b) cup mouthpieces and bells  c) four strings
**Answer: b) cup mouthpieces and bells.**

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** Which woodwind has a double reed?
a) flute  b) clarinet  c) oboe
**Answer: c) oboe (bassoon too; clarinet is single reed, flute none).**
