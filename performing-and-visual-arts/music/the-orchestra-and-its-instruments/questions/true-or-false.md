# The Orchestra and Its Instruments — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** An instrument is recognised by its timbre — its own sound quality.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The piccolo is the flute's 'little brother'.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The harp has four strings.
**Answer: False — it has forty-seven.**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Percussion instruments are hit, shaken or scraped.
**Answer: True**
