# The Orchestra and Its Instruments — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the first four instruments met in Year 1.
**Answer: Violin, trumpet, flute and drums.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which string instrument sounds deepest?
**Answer: The double bass.**

**3. (Sub lesson 5)** How does the trombone differ from the other brass?
**Answer: It uses a slide instead of valves.**
