# Video script — The Orchestra and Its Instruments

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Close your eyes. **Listen.**

That bright sound — a **violin**. That golden call — a **trumpet**.

Every instrument has its own voice — its **timbre**.
Sort them: **Strings, Woodwinds, Brass, Percussion**.
That is the **orchestra** — met ears-first.
