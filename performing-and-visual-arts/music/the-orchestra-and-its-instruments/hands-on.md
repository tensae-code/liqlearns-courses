# Hands-on — The Orchestra and Its Instruments

The lesson with your hands and your body, not only your eyes.

## Family sorting
Name sixteen instruments. Sort each into Strings, Woodwinds, Brass or Percussion, and say the mark that placed it — strings, reeds, cup mouthpiece and bell, or hit/shaken/scraped.

## Story painting
Tell a six-step story. Choose an instrument to paint each step, the way Grieg's orchestra paints Peer's steps.
