This is Malaysia's own lesson.

**Accompanying weddings, nasyid and marhaban.** Unit 5 Apresiasi: Kompang — the kompang frame drum (membranophone, brought by Arab traders): ensemble of 10–20 players seated, standing or processing; its three strokes — melalu (basic beat), menyilang (off-beat) and meningkah (ornament) — accompanying weddings, nasyid and marhaban.

That is Kompang — Malaysia's way, kept by name.
