# Kompang

## Do it first

Name one thing that belongs in this lesson — Kompang — Malaysia's frame-drum ensemble music for celebrations. Say what it is in your own words. If you can name one, you are already inside the lesson.

## Sub lesson 1 — Accompanying weddings, nasyid and marhaban

Unit 5 Apresiasi: Kompang — the kompang frame drum (membranophone, brought by Arab traders): ensemble of 10–20 players seated, standing or processing; its three strokes — melalu (basic beat), menyilang (off-beat) and meningkah (ornament) — accompanying weddings, nasyid and marhaban.
