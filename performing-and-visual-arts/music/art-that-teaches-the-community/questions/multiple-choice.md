# Art That Teaches the Community — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.


**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which is one cause-group of traffic accidents in the book?
  a) Drivers' carelessness and lack of skill b) Singing too loudly c) Rain in the rainy season
**Answer: a) Drivers' carelessness and lack of skill** — driver faults, vehicle faults, road-building and pedestrian road use are the book's four groups.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** “The Careful Student” (Grade 6 reprint) is credited…
  a) Words Solomon Gela, tune Ezra Abate b) Words Ezra Abate, tune Solomon Gela c) No credit is printed
**Answer: a) Words Solomon Gela, tune Ezra Abate** — the Grade 6 book prints that credit line.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** In the book's teaching, HIV/AIDS weakens…
  a) The white blood cells that defend the body b) The bones of the legs c) The voice for singing
**Answer: a) The white blood cells that defend the body** — the body's defenders are weakened until it cannot resist disease.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** HIV/AIDS is known for certain only by…
  a) A blood test b) Looking at a person c) Asking neighbours
**Answer: a) A blood test** — the book teaches it is known only by blood test.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Stimulant drugs in doping are…
  a) Banned substances that artificially excite muscle growth and performance b) Ordinary foods for athletes c) Medicines every athlete must take
**Answer: a) Banned substances that artificially excite muscle growth and performance** — the book's own definition.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** The punishment for doping runs…
  a) From suspension to lifetime expulsion b) A small fine only c) Nothing at all
**Answer: a) From suspension to lifetime expulsion** — the book's range: suspension up to lifetime expulsion.

**7. (Sub lesson 4)** The book's tasks are done…
  a) In pairs or groups, then discussed b) Alone and never shown c) Only by the teacher
**Answer: a) In pairs or groups, then discussed** — compose and perform in pairs/groups, then discuss how the message landed.

**8. (Sub lesson 4)** A teaching song has succeeded when…
  a) Listeners can repeat its lesson after one hearing b) It is the longest song c) Only singers understand it
**Answer: a) Listeners can repeat its lesson after one hearing** — the message landing is the test.
