# Art That Teaches the Community — short answer

Answer in a short sentence or a short list.


**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the careful student's three habits at a crossing.
**Answer:** Gathers his thoughts crossing the road, knows the traffic lights and laws, and listens to elders.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is the purpose of the song “So AIDS Does Not Catch Us”?
**Answer:** Prevention — to teach pupils to be careful of the disease, which has spread worldwide and has no cure.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Whom does doping harm besides the user, and how?
**Answer:** Clean athletes — they lose honestly-earned places and honours to artificially improved performance.

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Name the four topics the short theatre pieces may take.
**Answer:** Traffic, HIV, harmful practices and trade tax.
