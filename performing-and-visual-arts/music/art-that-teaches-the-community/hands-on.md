# Hands-on — Art That Teaches the Community

The lesson with your hands, your feet and your voice together, not only your eyes.

## Careful crossing drill
With a teacher at a quiet crossing, practise the careful student's habits: stop, gather thoughts, look, listen, cross when clear.

## One-hearing test
Perform your teaching song once for another group. They must repeat its lesson. If they cannot, shorten and sharpen it.
