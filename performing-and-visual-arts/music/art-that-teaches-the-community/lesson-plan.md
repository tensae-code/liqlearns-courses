# Lesson plan — Art That Teaches the Community

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Music · **Lesson:** Art That Teaches the Community
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 3 — Music (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** songs and theatre built to teach the whole community — road safety, HIV/AIDS prevention and the doping warning — each carried by a named song, exactly as the books teach them.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Anthems and Songs That Teach — songs already carry lessons there; here the lessons are the community's current dangers, taught in the books' own plain framing.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Traffic safety and “The Careful Student”** — the causes of traffic accidents; the road-safety song named with its teaching; the Grade 6 reprint carries its credit line.
2. **HIV/AIDS and the song “So AIDS Does Not Catch Us”** — the book's plain definition of HIV/AIDS, its red-ribbon figure, and the prevention song named by purpose.
3. **Doping: the stimulant-drugs warning** — stimulant drugs defined as the book defines them; doping tests; punishment from suspension to lifetime expulsion; the harm to clean athletes.
4. **Make your own teaching art** — the book's classroom tasks: compose and perform songs on HIV/AIDS and traffic in pairs/groups; prepare short theatres on traffic, HIV, harmful practices and trade tax.

5. **The Grade 5 chapter opens — songs and dances against today's dangers (chapter opening — Grade 5)** — the Grade 5 book's Chapter 5 opener and aims: link music with other subjects; use song for traffic-accident dangers and prevention; use song and dance for HIV/AIDS, traffic accidents and stimulant drugs; present air-pollution prevention combining song, dance and theatre; make and appreciate emblems and posters (Grade 5, printed p. 118). Includes the book's self-check mechanic (G5S-02).

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Anthems and Songs That Teach (songs that carry lessons). Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. List the causes of traffic accidents the book names and the careful student's habits.
2. State the book's plain teaching on HIV/AIDS and name its prevention song.
3. Define doping in the book's words and state its punishment.
4. Compose and perform a short teaching song or theatre piece of your own.

## What you need
Voices; a road-crossing place to observe safely with a teacher; paper for posters.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — cross a quiet road the careful way — stop, gather your thoughts, look, listen to elders' guidance — before this lesson explains why. (lesson/lesson.md).

   - ADD AUDIO: “The Careful Student” sung — the student names its three habits after listening.

## If the learner is stuck
If a teaching song's lesson is unclear, cut it to one danger and one safe action, repeated — plain beats clever when lives are taught.
