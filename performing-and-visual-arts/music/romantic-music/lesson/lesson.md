# Romantic Music

## Do it first

Quick start: say one thing you already know — a tune, a dance, a name — that belongs to this lesson's world. Keep it in mind; the parts will meet it.

## Sub lesson 1 — The Romantic movement

"Romantic" in art history does not mean love stories. It names a movement that prized **imagination and feeling** over the grey everyday. The guide hears **Chopin's Nocturne Op. 9 No. 2** as an escape from the everyday into an imagined world, and sets two pulls against each other: the **Apollonian** — order, light, form — and the **Dionysian** — wild feeling and release.

## Say it to your companion
