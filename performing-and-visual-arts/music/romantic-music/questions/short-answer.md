# Romantic Music — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does "Romantic" mean in art history?
**Answer: A movement that prized imagination and feeling over the everyday.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which Chopin piece does the guide use?
**Answer: The Nocturne Op. 9 No. 2.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the two pulls set against each other.
**Answer: The Apollonian and the Dionysian.**
