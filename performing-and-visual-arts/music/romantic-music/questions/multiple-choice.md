# Romantic Music — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** "Romantic" in art history means…
a) a movement prizing imagination and feeling  b) only love songs  c) music with no feeling
**Answer: a) a movement prizing imagination and feeling**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Chopin's Nocturne Op. 9 No. 2 is heard as…
a) an escape into an imagined world  b) a marching order  c) a dance lesson
**Answer: a) an escape into an imagined world**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The Apollonian stands for…
a) order, light and form  b) wild release only  c) silence
**Answer: a) order, light and form**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** The Dionysian stands for…
a) wild feeling and release  b) strict form only  c) counting beats
**Answer: a) wild feeling and release**

**5. (Sub lesson 1)** The Nocturne escapes from…
a) the grey everyday  b) the piano  c) the audience
**Answer: a) the grey everyday**
