# Story Songs

## Do it first

Name one thing that belongs in this lesson — Story Songs — Armenia's own teaching of this topic, kept as named parts: The SONG — a poet and a composer make a song together; folk song "Who talks a lot" and "Our first song" (Muradyan). Say what it is in your own words. If you can name one, you are already inside the lesson.

## Sub lesson 1 — The SONG

The SONG — a poet and a composer make a song together; folk song "Who talks a lot" and "Our first song" (Muradyan).

## Sub lesson 2 — Intonation in speech and music

Intonation in speech and music — an exclamation mark and an accent change how words sound; musical intonation does the same with sounds (Petrosyan's "The fly in the butter").

## Sub lesson 3 — Are there special performers among the people

Are there special performers among the people — the folk songs "Chem krna khagha" and "Naz mi ani": the people's own singers.

## Do It Yourself

1. Choose one named part of this lesson and find out one more true thing about it — from a person who knows it, or from the book your part came from.
2. Make or perform a small piece in the way of that part: a rhythm, a step, a drawing, a shaped piece of clay.
3. Write one sentence: what does this part show about Armenia's own way of teaching its arts?
