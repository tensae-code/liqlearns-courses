# Lesson plan — Story Songs

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Music · **Lesson:** Story Songs
**Place in the curriculum:** Music (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Story Songs — Armenia's own teaching of this topic, kept as named parts: The SONG — a poet and a composer make a song together; folk song "Who talks a lot" and "Our first song" (Muradyan)
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the course's earlier lessons in its title; recommended first, never forced.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of ..." in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The SONG** — The SONG — a poet and a composer make a song together; folk song "Who talks a lot" and "Our first song" (Muradyan)
2. **Intonation in speech and music** — Intonation in speech and music — an exclamation mark and an accent change how words sound; musical intonation does the same with sounds (Petrosyan's "The fly in the butter")
3. **Are there special performers among the people** — Are there special performers among the people — the folk songs "Chem krna khagha" and "Naz mi ani": the people's own singers

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
