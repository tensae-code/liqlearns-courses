# Video script — Story Songs

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This is Armenia's own lesson.

**The SONG.** The SONG — a poet and a composer make a song together; folk song "Who talks a lot" and "Our first song" (Muradyan).

**Intonation in speech and music.** Intonation in speech and music — an exclamation mark and an accent change how words sound; musical intonation does the same with sounds (Petrosyan's "The fly in the butter").

**Are there special performers among the people.** Are there special performers among the people — the folk songs "Chem krna khagha" and "Naz mi ani": the people's own singers.

That is Story Songs — Armenia's way, kept by name.
