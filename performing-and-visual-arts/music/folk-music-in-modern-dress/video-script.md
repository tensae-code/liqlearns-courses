# Video script — Folk Music in Modern Dress

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Old music, new clothes! Africa meets Ireland — over electronic beats!

Talking drum, djembe — bodhrán! Pipes, kora, hurdy gurdy!

Loops drop in, loops drop out — building, breaking, fading.

That is **fusion** — folk music in modern dress.
