## Sub lesson 1 — Afro Celt Sound System and fusion

**Fusion** joins musical traditions into a new whole. **Afro Celt Sound System (formed 1995, WOMAD performers)** fuse **African, Celtic and electronic dance music**: **talking drum and djembe** interlock with the Celtic **bodhrán** and **programmed digital beats**. **'Release' (1999)** is sung mainly by **Sinéad O'Connor**.

## Sub lesson 2 — The fusion instruments and the loop structure

The instrumentarium mixes **uilleann pipes and low whistle (Ireland)**, the **kora (long-necked African lute)** and the **hurdy gurdy (crank-turned wheel on strings)**. The pop form is built from **numbered loops dropping in and out of the mix**: **intro drone, verses, solos, drum break and build, fade-out outro**. Wider listening: Capercaillie's Gaelic folk-rock and Paul Simon's 'Homeless' with Ladysmith Black Mambazo.
