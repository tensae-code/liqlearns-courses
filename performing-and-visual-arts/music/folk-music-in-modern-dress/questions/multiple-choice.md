# Folk Music in Modern Dress — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Afro Celt Sound System fuse African, Celtic and…
a) electronic dance music  b) only silence  c) opera only
**Answer: a) electronic dance music**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** 'Release' dates from…
a) 1999  b) 1969  c) 2019
**Answer: a) 1999**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The kora is…
a) a long-necked African lute  b) an Irish drum  c) a keyboard
**Answer: a) a long-necked African lute**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The hurdy gurdy makes sound with…
a) a crank-turned wheel on strings  b) blown air only  c) hit bells
**Answer: a) a crank-turned wheel on strings**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The form is built from numbered…
a) loops  b) chapters  c) verses only
**Answer: a) loops**
