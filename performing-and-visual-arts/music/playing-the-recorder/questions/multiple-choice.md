# Playing the Recorder — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which is part of the recorder's basic technique?
a) tonguing (coup de langue)  b) bowing  c) pedalling
**Answer: a) tonguing (coup de langue)** — tonguing — beside posture, breath, attack and breathing.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The first recorder notes learned are…
a) sol, la, si  b) do, mi, sol  c) fa, si, ré
**Answer: a) sol, la, si** — sol–la–si.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The full octave learned runs…
a) do–ré–mi–fa–sol–la–si–do  b) sol–la–si only  c) do–mi–sol
**Answer: a) do–ré–mi–fa–sol–la–si–do** — do to do — the full octave.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** The programme's three practice instruments are…
a) recorder, voice and percussion  b) violin, cello and harp  c) trumpet, drum and piano
**Answer: a) recorder, voice and percussion** — recorder (flûte à bec), voice and percussion.

**5. (Sub lesson 4)** The new note learned in EEA3 is…
a) B-flat (Si bémol)  b) F-sharp  c) high C only
**Answer: a) B-flat (Si bémol)** — B-flat (Si bémol).
