# Playing the Recorder — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Posture comes before the notes in the initiation.
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Learners play little pieces containing the known notes by heart.
**Answer: True.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Recorder practice is always solo.
**Answer: False — the programme plays in small and large groups, with auditions and recitals.**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** B-flat is learned in the EEA3 sequence's recorder strand.
**Answer: True.**
