# Playing the Recorder — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name three parts of the recorder's basic technique.
**Answer: Posture, breath (souffle), tonguing (coup de langue), attack (attaque) and breathing (respiration) — any three.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How are the recorder's notes shown, beside their fingerings?
**Answer: In tablature.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In what two sizes of group does the programme's practice play together?
**Answer: Small groups and large groups.**
