# Choral Singing — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Choral singing begins with…
a) singing louder than everyone  b) harmonising the voice so the group sings together  c) singing alone
**Answer: b) harmonising the voice so the group sings together** — the voices are tuned to each other.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In the Honduras book, the choir sings…
a) only foreign songs  b) the country's own music together  c) nothing in particular
**Answer: b) the country's own music together** — 'tuning the voice to sing our music'.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Dirección coral means…
a) choral direction — how a choir is directed  b) a kind of drum  c) a written song
**Answer: a) choral direction — how a choir is directed**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The director's work is to…
a) sing instead of the choir  b) hold the choir's singing together  c) stop all singing
**Answer: b) hold the choir's singing together**

**5. (Sub lesson 1)** A choir is…
a) one singer  b) many unconnected singers  c) voices harmonised into one singing
**Answer: c) voices harmonised into one singing**
