# Choral Singing — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What must singers do to their voices to sing as a choir?
**Answer: Harmonise them — tune them to each other so the group sings together.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is dirección coral?
**Answer: Choral direction — how a choir is directed.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** What music does the Honduras Grade 7 sequence tune the voice to sing?
**Answer: Honduras's own music — 'nuestra música'.**
