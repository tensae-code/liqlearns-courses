# Hands-on — Choral Singing

The lesson with your voice, not only your eyes.

## Sing as one
In a group, sing a short song everyone knows. First sing it any way. Then sing it again listening to each other, matching your voices so it sounds like one singing. Say what changed.

## Lead and follow
One learner stands in front and gives the start and the stop of the song with a clear gesture. Swap leaders. Say what the leader's direction did for the group.
