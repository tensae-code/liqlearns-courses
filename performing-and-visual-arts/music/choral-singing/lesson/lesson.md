# Choral Singing

## Do it first

Sing a song you know — alone. Now sing it with friends, all at the same time. Was it one song, or many songs fighting? Making it one song is this lesson.

## Sub lesson 1 — Tuning the voice to sing together

Honduras's Grade 7 book gives a whole music sequence to it: **Afinando la voz para cantar nuestra música — tuning the voice to sing our music**. Choral singing begins with **harmonising the voice**: the singers tune their voices to each other so that the group sings the country's music **together**, as one choir.

## Sub lesson 2 — Directing the choir — dirección coral

A choir does not hold together by itself. The same sequence teaches **dirección coral — choral direction**: how a choir is **directed**, the leadership that starts the singing, holds it together and carries it to its end.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion: what makes many singers into a choir? Companion checks: voices harmonised to sing together? A direction that holds them?

## Do It Yourself

1. Sing a short song in a group twice — first without listening, then harmonising your voices. Write what changed.
2. Take turns directing your group: give the start and the stop clearly.
3. Write one sentence: why does a choir need direction?
