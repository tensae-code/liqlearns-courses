# Video script — Choral Singing

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

One voice sings. Many voices sing — together!

But together is work. The voices must be **tuned to each other** — harmonised — so the song is one song.

And someone leads: the **choral direction** — *dirección coral* — holds the choir together.

Many voices. One song. That is a choir.
