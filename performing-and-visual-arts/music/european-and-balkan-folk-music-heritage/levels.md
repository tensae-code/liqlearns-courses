# European and Balkan Folk Music Heritage — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — musical heritage in the region I
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: musical heritage in the region I — Bulgaria: open-throat diaphonic women's singing, the great kaba gaida, gadulka and kaval, the fast rachenitsa (7/16) and pajduško dances, and the kukeri, masked men dancing with heavy bells to drive out evil; Greece: klephtic songs of the fight against the Ottomans, the bouzouki and Cretan lyra, sirtaki, the solo zeibekiko and Crete's pentozali.

## Sub lesson 2 — musical heritage in the region II
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: musical heritage in the region II — Albania: northern Geg epic songs against southern Tosk iso-polyphony over a drone (UNESCO), çifteli and lahuta; Kosovo's shota dance; Serbia: the kolo, gusle epics, frula and the Guča trumpet; Montenegro: epic gusle recitation and the oro danced as a two-storey human tower; Croatia: UNESCO-listed klapa singing, bećarac wit, tamburica and the Dubrovnik linđo; Bosnia: the sevdalinka love song with its oriental melismas, played on saz and šargija.

## Sub lesson 3 — Europe's folk heritage, West and Centre
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Europe's folk heritage, West and Centre: French regional songs with bagpipe and fiddle; British and Irish ballads, reels and jigs, the Scottish bagpipe and Irish bodhrán; German and Austrian Lied, yodelling, ländler and waltz; Czech, Slovak and Slovenian polkas; Polish and Hungarian csárdás.

## Sub lesson 4 — Europe's folk heritage, East, North and South
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Europe's folk heritage, East, North and South: Romania's acrobatic călușari and the hora, with the pan flute and cimbalom; Russia's balalaika, domra and garmon; Ukraine's hopak and bandura; the North's hardanger fiddle, Finland's kantele and Kalevala epic singing; Italy's tarantella, Spain's flamenco and Portugal's fado.
