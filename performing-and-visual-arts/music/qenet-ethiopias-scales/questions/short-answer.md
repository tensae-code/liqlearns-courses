# Qenet: Ethiopia's Scales — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the four main qenet.
**Answer: Tizta, Bati, Anchi Hoye Lene and Ambassel.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name one Tizta practice melody by its title.
**Answer: "አንች ልጅ" (Anch Lij) or "እኛም አለን ሙዚቃ" (We Also Have Music).**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In the number-pyramid exercise, what comes after 121?
**Answer: 12321 — the pyramid grows up the scale and comes back down.**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** What are the two ways to count 6/8?
**Answer: Six eighth-note beats, or two dotted-quarter beats.**

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** Which time signatures does this practice use?
**Answer: The simple ones — 2/4, 3/4 and 4/4.**
