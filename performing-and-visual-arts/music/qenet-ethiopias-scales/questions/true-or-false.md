# Qenet: Ethiopia's Scales — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Ethiopian music is exhausted by the four main qenet.
**Answer: False**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Bati major omits the 2nd and 6th sounds.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Solfeggio's syllables trace to Guido d'Arezzo.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** The Kodály sign for Do stands at belly-button level.
**Answer: True**

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** In rhythmic dictation you write only the rhythm of the sounds played.
**Answer: True**
