# Qenet: Ethiopia's Scales — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Qenet are…
  a) Pentatonic — five sounds b) Always eight sounds c) Only for drums
**Answer: a) Pentatonic — five sounds** — the main qenet are pentatonic, with five sounds.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Tizta major is built by omitting…
  a) The 4th and 7th sounds b) The 2nd and 6th sounds c) The 1st and 8th sounds
**Answer: a) The 4th and 7th sounds** — Tizta omits 4 and 7; Bati omits 2 and 6.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The strong/weak pattern of 3/4 is…
  a) S-W-W b) S-W-M-W c) W-W-W
**Answer: a) S-W-W** — 3/4 runs strong-weak-weak; 4/4 runs S-W-M-W.

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** In 2/2 (alla breve), one beat is…
  a) A half note b) A quarter note c) A whole note
**Answer: a) A half note** — in 2/2 the half note gets one beat.

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** Dictation means…
  a) Writing down music you hear b) Singing music you have never seen c) Copying a printed page by hand
**Answer: a) Writing down music you hear** — dictation writes what is heard; sight reading performs what is seen, unseen before.
