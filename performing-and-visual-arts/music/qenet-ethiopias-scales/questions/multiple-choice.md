# Qenet: Ethiopia's Scales — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Qenet are…
  a) Pentatonic — five sounds b) Always eight sounds c) Only for drums
**Answer: a) Pentatonic — five sounds** — the main qenet are pentatonic, with five sounds.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Tizta major is built by omitting…
  a) The 4th and 7th sounds b) The 2nd and 6th sounds c) The 1st and 8th sounds
**Answer: a) The 4th and 7th sounds** — Tizta omits 4 and 7; Bati omits 2 and 6.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The strong/weak pattern of 3/4 is…
  a) S-W-W b) S-W-M-W c) W-W-W
**Answer: a) S-W-W** — 3/4 runs strong-weak-weak; 4/4 runs S-W-M-W.

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** In 2/2 (alla breve), one beat is…
  a) A half note b) A quarter note c) A whole note
**Answer: a) A half note** — in 2/2 the half note gets one beat.

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** Dictation means…
  a) Writing down music you hear b) Singing music you have never seen c) Copying a printed page by hand
**Answer: a) Writing down music you hear** — dictation writes what is heard; sight reading performs what is seen, unseen before.

**6. (Sub lesson 6)** An interval is…
  a) The vibration-measure difference between two sounds b) The loudest sound in a chant c) A kind of drum
**Answer: a) The vibration-measure difference between two sounds** — §1.2.3 — the building law of qenet.

**7. (Sub lesson 6)** In the book's example, the distance between 220 Hz and 240 Hz is taken as…
  a) 20 Hz b) 240 Hz c) 220 Hz
**Answer: a) 20 Hz** — 240 − 220 = 20 Hz — the difference is the distance.

**8. (Sub lesson 7)** Tizta minor and Bati minor are formed by…
  a) Their interval patterns from the base sound b) Copying the majors exactly c) Dropping the base sound
**Answer: a) Their interval patterns from the base sound** — the interval law forms majors and minors alike.

**9. (Sub lesson 7)** Bati (major and minor) is the family that omits…
  a) The 2nd and 6th sounds b) The 4th and 7th sounds c) The 1st and 5th sounds
**Answer: a) The 2nd and 6th sounds** — Bati omits 2 and 6; Tizta omits 4 and 7.

**10. (Sub lesson 8)** The four main qenet are completed by…
  a) Anchi Hoye and Ambassel b) Two more Tiztas c) The diatonic scale
**Answer: a) Anchi Hoye and Ambassel** — Tizta, Bati, Anchi Hoye, Ambassel — the four.

**11. (Sub lesson 8)** Activity 2.2 has a partner…
  a) Check your intervals b) Sing instead of you c) Erase your work
**Answer: a) Check your intervals** — build, sing, and be checked — the forming is proved socially.

**12. (Sub lesson 9)** Sound dictation means…
  a) Hearing sounds and writing them down b) Copying a neighbour's page c) Singing without listening
**Answer: a) Hearing sounds and writing them down** — proof by ear — the dictation half of reading.

**13. (Sub lesson 9)** Activity 2.5's melodies prove reading by…
  a) Performing them at sight, in their qenet b) Memorising them by their words c) Humming them approximately
**Answer: a) Performing them at sight, in their qenet** — sight performance with intervals true.

**14. (Sub lesson 10)** ቃና (qana) are…
  a) Sound-structures that enable melody-making, whose orderings form the qenet b) A kind of drum c) Printed songbooks
**Answer: a) Sound-structures that enable melody-making, whose orderings form the qenet** — §1.2.2 — qana first, then qenet, then melody.

**15. (Sub lesson 10)** Ezra Abate's qenet areas are…
  a) North & Central; East; South & West b) Only Addis Ababa c) Only the North
**Answer: a) North & Central; East; South & West** — Tigray/Amhara/Shewa; Harar/Dire Dawa/Somali; South Ethiopia/Gambela/Benishangul.
