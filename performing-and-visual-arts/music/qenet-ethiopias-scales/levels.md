# Qenet: Ethiopia's Scales — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure — README, "Units, Lessons and Sub Lessons"). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number, as before. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. The sub lessons climb inside this one lesson, from the youngest books to the oldest.

## Sub lesson 1 — Qenet: Ethiopia's scale system
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: define qenet and name the four main modes.

## Sub lesson 2 — Building Tizta major and Bati major
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: build Tizta and Bati from C major and say which sounds each leaves out.

## Sub lesson 3 — Solfeggio and pitch names (teacher guide — evidence)
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: sing the syllables up and down and clap the strong/weak patterns of 4/4 and 3/4.

## Sub lesson 4 — Kodály hand signs and music time (teacher guide — evidence)
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: sign the full ladder Do to high Do and count a bar of 6/8 both ways.

## Sub lesson 5 — Sight reading and dictation
Sub lesson 5 questions from the type files. Pass mark: sight-read one printed line and take one bar of rhythmic dictation correctly.

## Sub lesson 6 — Intervals — the building law of qenet
Sub lesson 6 questions from the type files. Pass mark: define interval, work the book's 220/240 Hz example, and name the five interval kinds.

## Sub lesson 7 — Tizta minor and Bati minor, formed
Sub lesson 7 questions from the type files. Pass mark: form Tizta minor and Bati minor from a given base by their interval patterns and sing each once.

## Sub lesson 8 — Anchi Hoye and Ambassel, formed — with Activity 2.2
Sub lesson 8 questions from the type files. Pass mark: form Anchi Hoye and Ambassel from a given base and pass a partner's interval check (Activity 2.2).

## Sub lesson 9 — Dictation and melodies at Grade 8 depth
Sub lesson 9 questions from the type files. Pass mark: take a four-sound dictation without a miss and perform one Activity 2.5 melody at sight in its qenet.

## Sub lesson 10 — Qana, Ezra Abate's areas, and the guides' qenet evidence
Sub lesson 10 questions from the type files. Pass mark: define qana, name Ezra Abate's three areas with their regions, and label the guides' parts as evidence.
