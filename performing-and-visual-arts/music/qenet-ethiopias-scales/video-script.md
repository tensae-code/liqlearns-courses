# Video script — Qenet: Ethiopia's Scales

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Ethiopia has its own scales: the qenet. Five sounds carry the song.
Tizta leaves out four and seven. Bati leaves out two and six.
Do at the belly, Re above, Mi at the heart — hands can sing.
Read it at sight. Write it from hearing. Two proofs of reading.
