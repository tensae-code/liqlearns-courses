# Hands-on — Qenet: Ethiopia's Scales

The lesson with your hands, your feet and your voice together, not only your eyes.

## Hand-sign ladder
Sing the scale slowly with the Kodály signs: Do (belly-button), Re (slightly higher), Mi (near heart), Fa (thumb-down turn), Sol (chin), La (arched hand, fingers down), Ti (eye level, pointing up), high Do (forehead).

## Six-eight two ways
Clap 6/8 first as six quick eighth beats, then as two big dotted-quarter beats — the guide's own two countings. Feel the same bar wearing two gaits.
