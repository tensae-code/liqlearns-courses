# Folk Songs of Mongolia's Peoples — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** "Agsal" is a folk song of the…
a) Torguud people  b) Zakhchin people  c) Kazakh people
**Answer: a) Torguud people**

**2.** The Zakhchin folk song heard is a…
a) lullaby (Бүүвэйн дуу)  b) march  c) waltz
**Answer: a) lullaby (Бүүвэйн дуу)**

**3.** "Agsal" is in three shares and is…
a) heroic and aspiring — a song-dance  b) calm and sleepy  c) a counting song
**Answer: a) heroic and aspiring — a song-dance**
