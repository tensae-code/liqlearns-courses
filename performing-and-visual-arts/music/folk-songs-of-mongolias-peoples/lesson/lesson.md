# Folk Songs of Mongolia's Peoples

## Do it first

Start with the first part below — do it, sing it or make it before you read any explanation.

## Sub lesson 1 — Сонсъё: "Агсал", a Torguud folk song, and "Бүүвэйн дуу", a Zakhchin folk song

Сонсъё: "Агсал", a Torguud folk song, and "Бүүвэйн дуу", a Zakhchin folk song — Mongolia's own peoples' songs heard and judged by their melodic character: Agsal in three shares, heroic and aspiring, a song-dance; the Zakhchin lullaby in three shares, calm and peaceful, a song — played along with in rhythm
