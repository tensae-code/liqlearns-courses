This is Armenia's own lesson.

**The Armenian new notation (Հայկական նոր նոտագրություն).** The Armenian new notation (Հայկական նոր նոտագրություն) — the khaz neume system Armenia wrote its chants with.

**Notation and the collections.** Notation and the collections — the medieval period developed sharakan, gandz, tagh singing; the collections Gandzaran, Manrusum and Tagharan gathered them; Nerses Shnorhali (1102-1173) and Grigor Narekatsi (950-1003) stand in the line; in 1813-15 Hambardzum Limonjian (1768-1839) created the new Armenian notation.

That is Armenian Khaz Notation — Armenia's way, kept by name.
