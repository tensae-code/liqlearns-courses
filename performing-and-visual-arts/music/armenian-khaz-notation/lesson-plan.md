## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The Armenian new notation (Հայկական նոր նոտագրություն)** — The Armenian new notation (Հայկական նոր նոտագրություն) — the khaz neume system Armenia wrote its chants with
2. **Notation and the collections** — Notation and the collections — the medieval period developed sharakan, gandz, tagh singing; the collections Gandzaran, Manrusum and Tagharan gathered them; Nerses Shnorhali (1102-1173) and Grigor Narekatsi (950-1003) stand in the line; in 1813-15 Hambardzum Limonjian (1768-1839) created the new Armenian notation
