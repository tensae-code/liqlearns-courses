# Video script — Caring for Musical Instruments

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This is Indonesia's own lesson.

**Bab III.** knowing the kinds of instruments, how each is played, and how to clean and care for it.

**Bab 2, Kegiatan 3 Belajar Merawat.** caring for instruments according to their material.

**Bab II.** learning the techniques of pianika and recorder — and learning to clean the pianika and recorder.

That is Caring for Musical Instruments — Indonesia's way, kept by name.
