# Caring for Musical Instruments

## Do it first

Name one thing that belongs in this lesson — How to look after instruments — cleaning, storing and handling them so they last and keep their sound. Say what it is in your own words. If you can name one, you are already inside the lesson.

## Sub lesson 1 — Bab III

knowing the kinds of instruments, how each is played, and how to clean and care for it.

## Sub lesson 2 — Bab 2, Kegiatan 3 Belajar Merawat

caring for instruments according to their material.

## Sub lesson 3 — Bab II

learning the techniques of pianika and recorder — and learning to clean the pianika and recorder.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson holds, part by part, in your own words. Companion checks: did every part get named, with its Indonesian name kept where the book gives one?

## Do It Yourself

1. Choose one named part of this lesson and find out one more true thing about it — from a person who knows it, or from the book your part came from.
2. Make or perform a small piece in the way of that part: a rhythm, a step, a drawing, a shaped piece of clay.
3. Write one sentence: what does this part show about Indonesia's own way of teaching its arts?
