## Sub lesson 1 — The Tuned Percussion Instruments

Some percussion instruments, like the tabla, cannot produce tunes, but some produce beautiful tunes — the xylophone, made of metal bars, and the marimba, made of wooden bars; both are played using two mallets or four, and their sounds differ because the material each is made of differs; the artist Mohamed Taha, a professional Jordanian marimba player who plays most percussion instruments and has taken part in many local and Arab festivals.
