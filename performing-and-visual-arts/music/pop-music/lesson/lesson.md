## Sub lesson 1 — Queen and 'Killer Queen'

**Queen** were a **1970s rock band** blending **rock with music hall, pseudo-classical arrangement and mock-operatic vocals**. **'Killer Queen' (1974, from Sheer Heart Attack)** was their **first chart single**, sung by **Freddie Mercury**, a **wide-range vocalist-pianist**.

## Sub lesson 2 — Verse-chorus structure and studio craft

The song is built in **verse-and-chorus structure** — **verses, choruses, an instrumental, a guitar solo and an outro**. It uses an unusual **compound 12/8 time signature** against pop's simple **4/4**, a **syncopated, wide-leaping vocal line**, and studio **multi-tracking and overdubbing** of guitars and backing vocals.
