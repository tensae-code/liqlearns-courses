# Video script — Pop Music

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

1974! A band called **Queen** — rock meets music hall!

**'Killer Queen'** — their first chart single. Freddie Mercury sings — high, low, leaping!

Verse, chorus, verse, chorus — and count it: not plain 4/4, but **12/8**!

In the studio, guitars and voices are layered — track upon track.
