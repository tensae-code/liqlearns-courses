# Pop Music — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Queen blended rock with music hall and mock-operatic vocals.
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** 'Killer Queen' is in simple 4/4 like most pop songs.
**Answer: False — it is in compound 12/8.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Freddie Mercury sang and played piano.
**Answer: True.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Overdubbing means singing without any recording.
**Answer: False — it is layering recordings in the studio.**
