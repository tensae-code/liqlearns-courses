# Pop Music — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** 'Killer Queen' was released in…
a) 1974  b) 1954  c) 2004
**Answer: a) 1974**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Queen's singer-pianist was…
a) Freddie Mercury  b) John Williams  c) Henry Purcell
**Answer: a) Freddie Mercury**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** 'Killer Queen' was Queen's first…
a) chart single  b) opera  c) film
**Answer: a) chart single**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The song's unusual time signature is…
a) 12/8  b) 3/4  c) 2/2
**Answer: a) 12/8**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Multi-tracking and overdubbing happen in…
a) the recording studio  b) the concert hall only  c) the printing press
**Answer: a) the recording studio**
