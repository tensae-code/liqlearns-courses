# Hands-on — Singing Technique and Voice Care

The lesson with your body, not only your eyes.

## Balloon breath
With a chimbomba (balloon) if you have one, or miming one if you do not: inhale as if filling it, hold the air while you count silently, then exhale slowly and steadily as if letting it empty without a rush. Repeat three times.

## Three actions in pairs
In pairs, one partner performs the three actions — inhaling, holding, exhaling — and the other names each action as it happens. Then swap.
