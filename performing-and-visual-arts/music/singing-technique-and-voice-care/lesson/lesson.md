# Singing Technique and Voice Care

## Do it first

Try to sing one long note. It stops when your breath stops. Singing is breath, shaped — and the breath can be practised. Nicaragua's Grade 2 guide practises it with balloons.

## Sub lesson 1 — Breathing in and holding the air

The guide's lesson is **Breathing for singing**: breathing practised with **chimbombas (balloons)**. The first actions are **inhaling** — taking the air in — and **holding the air** before it is used. The balloon makes the breath visible: it fills as you inhale, and stays filled while you hold.

## Sub lesson 2 — Breathing out for singing

The third action is **exhaling** — letting the held air out. A song needs the air let out steadily, not all at once: the exhale is the stream the voice rides on. Inhale, hold, exhale — the three actions together are the guide's breathing practice for singing.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the three breathing actions in order, and what tool the guide practises them with. Companion checks: inhaling, holding, exhaling? Chimbombas (balloons)?

## Do It Yourself

1. Practise the three actions three times: inhale, hold, exhale slowly.
2. Sing one note on a single exhale, and notice how the steady breath carries it.
3. Write one sentence: why does a singer practise breathing before singing?
