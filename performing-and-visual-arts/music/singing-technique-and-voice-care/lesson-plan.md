# Lesson plan — Singing Technique and Voice Care

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Music · **Lesson:** Singing Technique and Voice Care
**Place in the curriculum:** Music (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** how to sing well begins with the breath — breathing for singing, practised with chimbombas (balloons): inhaling, exhaling and holding the air.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of its title — recommended first, never forced.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Breathing in and holding the air** — inhaling and holding the air, practised with a chimbomba (balloon).
2. **Breathing out for singing** — exhaling the held air steadily, the breath a song rides on.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the songs lessons of the Music title. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Show the three breathing actions the Nicaragua Grade 2 guide practises: inhaling, holding the air, and exhaling.
2. Say why the practice uses chimbombas (balloons): so the learner can feel and see the breath being taken in, held and let out.

## What you need
Your voice, and a chimbomba (balloon) if one is at hand — the breath actions can be mimed without one.

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks singing breath is just breathing fast → the guide practises it in three named actions: inhale, hold, exhale.
- Thinks the balloon is a toy → it is the practice tool: it makes the invisible breath visible.
