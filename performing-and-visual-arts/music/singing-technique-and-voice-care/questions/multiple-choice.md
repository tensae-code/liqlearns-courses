# Singing Technique and Voice Care — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Grade 2 guide practises breathing for singing with…
a) chimbombas (balloons)  b) drums  c) pencils
**Answer: a) chimbombas (balloons)**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The first breathing action is…
a) exhaling  b) inhaling — taking the air in  c) shouting
**Answer: b) inhaling — taking the air in**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** After inhaling, the learner…
a) holds the air  b) runs away  c) closes the book
**Answer: a) holds the air**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The third breathing action is…
a) holding again  b) sleeping  c) exhaling — letting the air out
**Answer: c) exhaling — letting the air out**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** For singing, the exhale should be…
a) all at once in a rush  b) steady  c) never done
**Answer: b) steady** — the voice rides on the steady stream of air.
