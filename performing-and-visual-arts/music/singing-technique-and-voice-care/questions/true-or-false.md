# Singing Technique and Voice Care — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Breathing for singing is practised with chimbombas (balloons).
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Holding the air is one of the three breathing actions.
**Answer: True.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The guide teaches singers to let all their air out at once.
**Answer: False** — the exhale is steady.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Inhaling, holding and exhaling are the guide's three actions.
**Answer: True.**
