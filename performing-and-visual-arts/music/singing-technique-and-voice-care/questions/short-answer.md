# Singing Technique and Voice Care — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the three breathing actions in order.
**Answer: Inhaling, holding the air, and exhaling.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the guide use to practise breathing for singing?
**Answer: Chimbombas — balloons.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Why must the exhale be steady when singing?
**Answer: Because the voice rides on the stream of air — a steady exhale carries the song.**
