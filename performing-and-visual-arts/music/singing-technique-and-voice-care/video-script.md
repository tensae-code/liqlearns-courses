# Video script — Singing Technique and Voice Care

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A song rides on breath.

Take the breath **in**. Hold it. Let it out — slow, steady.

The balloon shows it: fill — hold — empty.

**Inhale. Hold. Exhale.**

That is breathing for singing.
