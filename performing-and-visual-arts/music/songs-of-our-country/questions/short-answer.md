# Songs of Our Country — short answer

Answer in a few words, out loud or in writing.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name two things the waterfalls give, in the tizta song's picture of the country.
**Answer:** Their air refreshes the mind (with the hills, rivers and gorges).

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** How does music reach us, in the Music song?
**Answer:** It travels through the air into the ear.
