# Songs of Our Country — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In Our Country, Ethiopia is sung as —
a) our mother, our green country  b) a far country  c) a market town
**Answer: a) our mother, our green country**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The flag's colours, in the song's chorus order, are —
a) red, yellow, green  b) green, yellow, red  c) yellow, green, red
**Answer: b) green, yellow, red**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The tizta song says love of country is —
a) a heavy load  b) an unforgettable memory  c) a school rule
**Answer: b) an unforgettable memory**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Our Arts praises —
a) mother's woven netela and the home's pottery  b) the school bell  c) the flag only
**Answer: a) mother's woven netela and the home's pottery**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Work-time songs are sung —
a) to stir the workers themselves  b) only for visitors  c) after work ends
**Answer: a) to stir the workers themselves**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** The three farm works with their own melodies are —
a) weaving, pottery, building  b) threshing, reaping, ploughing  c) planting, weeding, harvesting only
**Answer: b) threshing, reaping, ploughing**

**7. (Sub lesson 3)** "ሙዚቃ" (Music) praises music as —
a) a loud noise  b) food for the mind and satisfaction for the spirit  c) a school subject
**Answer: b) food for the mind and satisfaction for the spirit**

**8. (Sub lesson 3)** In the song, music refreshes —
a) the tired mind  b) the body only  c) nothing
**Answer: a) the tired mind** — Travelling through the air into the ear.
