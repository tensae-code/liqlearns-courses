# Songs of Our Country — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The tizta song loves the country's hills, rivers, gorges and waterfalls.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Our Arts ends with the wish to make the crafts too.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The threshing melody is sung standing still in a classroom only.
**Answer: False** — It belongs to circling the oxen on the threshing floor.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** The appreciation activity is to sing a song you know in class.
**Answer: True**
