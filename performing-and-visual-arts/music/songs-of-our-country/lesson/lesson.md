# Songs of Our Country

## Do it first

Say the colours of our flag, top to bottom. Now think of the land under that flag — hills, rivers, a waterfall's cool air. And think of your mother's weaving, your home's clay pots, the sound of singing from a threshing floor. This lesson sings all of it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Our country, its flag, and love of country

The first song is **"ሀገራችን"** — *Our Country*. Ethiopia is sung as **our mother**, our **green, lush country**, and its chorus carries the flag: **green, yellow, red**, with the wish that evil never touch it.

[Picture 9462 — the flag of Ethiopia flying over green hills and a waterfall]

The second song sings the same love in another manner: **"የሀገር ፍቅር ትዝታው"** — *Love of Country — the Tizta Memory*. Love of country, it says, is an unforgettable memory for one who knows it: the country's **hills, rivers and gorges**, and its **waterfalls' air**, refresh the mind. Its choruses come back like memory itself.

Sing the first song proudly, standing straight. Sing the tizta song slowly, like remembering.

## Sub lesson 2 — Praising our crafts and work songs

The crafts song is **"የኛ ጥበባት"** — *Our Arts*. It admires **mother's woven netela** and its coloured **tibeb** edging, and **praises the maker**. It admires the home's **pottery works** as the country's artworks — and it ends wanting: *I want to make them too.*

Then the songs of work itself. Work-time songs are sung by people at work **to stir themselves** — at farming, reaping, threshing and the like. Three are printed as repertoire, one for each work:

- The **threshing** melody — circling the oxen on the threshing floor, separating produce from chaff.
- The **reaping** melody — the brave reaper and the sickle's work.
- The **ploughing** melody — urging the ploughing oxen and the farmer.

In groups, your own area's farm-work songs belong here too: perform the songs sung at farm work where you live.

## Sub lesson 3 — A song about music itself

The last song sings about singing: **"ሙዚቃ"** — *Music*.

It praises music as **food for the mind** and **satisfaction for the spirit**. Music travels **through the air into the ear** and **refreshes the tired mind** — and the song's chorus repeats, as if the mind is being fed again and again.

And it sets you a task of appreciation: sing a song you know, in class, to show your appreciation of music. This whole unit has been that task, song by song.

## Say it to your companion

Sing the flag chorus of Our Country to your companion and name the colours in order. Name two things the tizta song loves, the crafts the Our Arts song praises, and the three works that have melodies. Finish with the Music song's best line: music is food for the mind.

## Do It Yourself

1. Draw the flag carefully — green, yellow, red — and sing Our Country over your drawing.
2. Hold a woven cloth or a clay pot from home. Sing Our Arts to the person who made it, or who uses it.
3. Next time you hear singing from work — a field, a threshing floor — stand and listen. Which work is it? What is the singing doing for the workers?
