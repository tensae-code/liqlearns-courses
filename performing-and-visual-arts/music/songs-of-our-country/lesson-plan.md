# Lesson plan — Songs of Our Country

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Music · **Lesson:** Songs of Our Country
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 3 — Music (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** songs of love for Ethiopia — the green country and its green-yellow-red flag, the tizta love of country, praise for our crafts, the songs sung at farm work, and a song that praises music itself.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the learner's own country, home crafts and farm work — everything sung here is already around the child.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved (the gate: README, "Units, Lessons and Sub Lessons (ruled 2026-11-08)"):

1. **Our country, its flag, and love of country** — "ሀገራችን" (Our Country) — Ethiopia our mother, our green country, the green-yellow-red flag chorus — and "የሀገር ፍቅር ትዝታው" (Love of Country — the Tizta Memory) — hills, rivers, gorges and waterfalls that refresh the mind.
2. **Praising our crafts and work songs** — "የኛ ጥበባት" (Our Arts) — mother's woven netela and the home's pottery praised, wanting to make them too — and the work songs of threshing, reaping and ploughing that stir people at farm work.
3. **A song about music itself** — "ሙዚቃ" (Music) — music praised as food for the mind and satisfaction for the spirit, travelling through the air into the ear, refreshing the tired mind.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Traditional and Festival Songs — the people's songs. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Sing "ሀገራችን" (Our Country) and name the flag's colours in order: green, yellow, red.
2. Sing the Tizta love-of-country song and name what it loves: hills, rivers, gorges, waterfalls.
3. Sing "የኛ ጥበባት" (Our Arts) and name the crafts it praises: the woven netela with its tibeb, the home's pottery.
4. Name the three farm-work melodies: threshing, reaping, ploughing — and say why work has songs.
5. Sing "ሙዚቃ" (Music) and say what music is, in the song's words.

## What you need
Your voice. For the crafts part, a real netela or a clay pot from home to hold while singing, if one can be borrowed.

## If the learner is stuck
- Loses the words → go back to the call-and-answer shape: the teacher (or the audio) sings a short piece, the learner answers with the same piece. Short pieces first, whole song after.
- Loses the tune → hum the song together before singing words; the tune first, the words ride on it.
- Sings but cannot say what the song teaches → ask the lesson's own question: what is this song about, and what does it ask us to do? Every song in this unit teaches something that can be said in one sentence.
