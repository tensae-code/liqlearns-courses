# Hands-on — Songs of Our Country

The lesson with your hands, your feet and your voice together, not only your eyes.

## Hold the craft, sing the praise
Borrow a netela, a woven tray or a clay pot. The class sings Our Arts while the craft is carried slowly around the circle, so everyone sees the tibeb and the clay work the song praises.

## Work-song rhythms
In three groups, make the rhythm of one work each: circling steps for threshing, swinging arms for the sickle's reaping strokes, steady trudging for the plough. Sing a work melody over your group's rhythm.
