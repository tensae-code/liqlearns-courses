# Video script — Songs of Our Country

The super-simple voice, for the sing-along video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The songs are named and summarised, never transcribed; the script sings the learner TO the song, not the song itself.

---

Ethiopia, our mother, our green country!
Green, yellow, red — sing the flag!
Hills and rivers, gorges and waterfalls —
love of country is a memory that never fades.

Mother's netela, bright with tibeb — praise the maker!
Songs on the threshing floor, songs at the reaping, songs behind the plough.

And music itself — food for the mind,
rest for the spirit. Sing!
