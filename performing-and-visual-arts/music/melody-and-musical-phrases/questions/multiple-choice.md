# Melody and Musical Phrases — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson teaches...
a) another country's arts only  b) Melody, the soul of music — its contour and structure, and question-and-answer phrases in musical dialogue and antiphony  c) nothing — it is just names

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The first sub lesson is...
a) Melodic question and answer  b) an examination  c) a song from another continent

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Malaysia's own forms in this lesson are...

**Answer: b) kept as named parts.**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** The last sub lesson is...
a) Melody craft  b) the bibliography  c) a review box
**Answer: a) Melody craft.**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Songs in this course are placed...
