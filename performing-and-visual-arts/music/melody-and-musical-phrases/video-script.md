# Video script — Melody and Musical Phrases

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This is Malaysia's own lesson.

**Melodic question and answer.** Unit 8 Kamu Tanya, Saya Jawab — melodic question and answer: hearing a question melody and inventing a fitting answer melody, then the group melody-creation game with notation cards.

**Creating question phrases and answer phrases (frasa soalan dan frasa.** Unit 3 — creating question phrases and answer phrases (frasa soalan dan frasa jawapan) with dotted-minim, crotchet and tied patterns in software.

**Melody craft.** Unit 4 Penghasilan Muzik — melody craft: melodic flow (aliran melodi), developing a melody from a motif, and noble values in producing a work.

That is Melody and Musical Phrases — Malaysia's way, kept by name.
