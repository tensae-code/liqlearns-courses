# Lesson plan — Melody and Musical Phrases

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Music · **Lesson:** Melody and Musical Phrases
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 3 — Music (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Melody, the soul of music — its contour and structure, and question-and-answer phrases in musical dialogue and antiphony.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the course's earlier lessons in its title; recommended first, never forced.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of ..." in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Melodic question and answer** — Unit 8 Kamu Tanya, Saya Jawab — melodic question and answer: hearing a question melody and inventing a fitting answer melody, then the group melody-creation game with notation cards.
2. **Creating question phrases and answer phrases (frasa soalan dan frasa** — Unit 3 — creating question phrases and answer phrases (frasa soalan dan frasa jawapan) with dotted-minim, crotchet and tied patterns in software.
3. **Melody craft** — Unit 4 Penghasilan Muzik — melody craft: melodic flow (aliran melodi), developing a melody from a motif, and noble values in producing a work.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
