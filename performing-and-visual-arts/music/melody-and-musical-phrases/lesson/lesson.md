# Melody and Musical Phrases

## Do it first

Name one thing that belongs in this lesson — Melody, the soul of music — its contour and structure, and question-and-answer phrases in musical dialogue and antiphony. Say what it is in your own words. If you can name one, you are already inside the lesson.

## Sub lesson 1 — Melodic question and answer

Unit 8 Kamu Tanya, Saya Jawab — melodic question and answer: hearing a question melody and inventing a fitting answer melody, then the group melody-creation game with notation cards.

## Sub lesson 2 — Creating question phrases and answer phrases (frasa soalan dan frasa

Unit 3 — creating question phrases and answer phrases (frasa soalan dan frasa jawapan) with dotted-minim, crotchet and tied patterns in software.

## Sub lesson 3 — Melody craft

Unit 4 Penghasilan Muzik — melody craft: melodic flow (aliran melodi), developing a melody from a motif, and noble values in producing a work.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson holds, part by part, in your own words. Companion checks: did every part get named, with its Malaysian name kept where the book gives one?

## Do It Yourself

1. Choose one named part of this lesson and find out one more true thing about it — from a person who knows it, or from the book your part came from.
2. Make or perform a small piece in the way of that part: a rhythm, a step, a drawing, a shaped piece of clay.
3. Write one sentence: what does this part show about Malaysia's own way of teaching its arts?
