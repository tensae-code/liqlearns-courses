## Sub lesson 1 — String-band music from the four regions
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: String-band music is studied from Papua New Guinea's four regions: its history, its performance, and original composition in the style.

## Sub lesson 2 — Pawa-band music
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Pawa-bands are met in person: interviewing band members, producing band profiles, performing cover versions, and recording a pawa-band style song.

## Sub lesson 3 — Oom-Cha and contemporary music in PNG
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Oom-Cha fuses PNG traditional, string-band and Western forms, characterised by a dominant upbeat in the bass complementing the drums, and is compared against traditional contemporary music.
