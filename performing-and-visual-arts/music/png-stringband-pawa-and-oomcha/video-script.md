# Video script — PNG Stringband: Pawa and Oomcha

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

**String-band music from the four regions.** String-band music is studied from Papua New Guinea's four regions: its history, its performance, and original composition in the style.

**Pawa-band music.** Pawa-bands are met in person: interviewing band members, producing band profiles, performing cover versions, and recording a pawa-band style song.

**Oom-Cha and contemporary music in PNG.** Oom-Cha fuses PNG traditional, string-band and Western forms, characterised by a dominant upbeat in the bass complementing the drums, and is compared against traditional contemporary music. Contemporary PNG also holds choirs, peroveta and ute styles, and PNG artists performing imported styles such as reggae, rap and pop.
