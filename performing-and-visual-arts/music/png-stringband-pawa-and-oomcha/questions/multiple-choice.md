# PNG Stringband: Pawa and Oomcha — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Oom-Cha is characterised by…
a) a dominant upbeat in the bass complementing the drums  b) total silence
**Answer: a) a dominant upbeat in the bass complementing the drums.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Pawa-band study includes…
a) interviewing band members and producing band profiles  b) never meeting a band
**Answer: a) interviewing band members and producing band profiles.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** String-band music is studied from…
a) PNG's four regions  b) one village only
**Answer: a) PNG's four regions.**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Peroveta and ute are…
a) contemporary PNG styles  b) European dances
**Answer: a) contemporary PNG styles.**
