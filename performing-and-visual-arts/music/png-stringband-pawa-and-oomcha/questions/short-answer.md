# PNG Stringband: Pawa and Oomcha — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What three forms does Oom-Cha fuse?
**Answer: PNG traditional, string-band and Western forms.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What four things do learners do with a pawa-band?
**Answer: Interview its members, produce a band profile, perform cover versions, and record a pawa-band style song.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name two foreign-imported styles PNG artists perform.
**Answer: Reggae, rap and pop (any two).**
