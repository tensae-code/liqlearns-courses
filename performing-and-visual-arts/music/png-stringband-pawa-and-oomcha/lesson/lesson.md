# PNG Stringband: Pawa and Oomcha

## Do it first

Look at the sub lesson list. Pick the part you already know something about, and say what you know. Then start at Sub lesson 1 — every part builds on the one before it.

## Sub lesson 1 — String-band music from the four regions

String-band music is studied from Papua New Guinea's four regions: its history, its performance, and original composition in the style.

## Sub lesson 2 — Pawa-band music

Pawa-bands are met in person: interviewing band members, producing band profiles, performing cover versions, and recording a pawa-band style song.

## Sub lesson 3 — Oom-Cha and contemporary music in PNG

Oom-Cha fuses PNG traditional, string-band and Western forms, characterised by a dominant upbeat in the bass complementing the drums, and is compared against traditional contemporary music. Contemporary PNG also holds choirs, peroveta and ute styles, and PNG artists performing imported styles such as reggae, rap and pop.
