# Hands-on — PNG Stringband: Pawa and Oomcha

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## String-band music from the four regions
Do the practice this part names, with real materials and a partner: String-band music is studied from Papua New Guinea's four regions: its history, its performance, and original composition in the style.

## Pawa-band music
Do the practice this part names, with real materials and a partner: Pawa-bands are met in person: interviewing band members, producing band profiles, performing cover versions, and recording a pawa-band style song.

## Oom-Cha and contemporary music in PNG
Do the practice this part names, with real materials and a partner: Oom-Cha fuses PNG traditional, string-band and Western forms, characterised by a dominant upbeat in the bass complementing the drums, and is compared against traditional contemporary music. Contemporary PNG also holds choirs, peroveta and ute styles, and PNG artists performing imported styles such as reggae, rap and pop.
