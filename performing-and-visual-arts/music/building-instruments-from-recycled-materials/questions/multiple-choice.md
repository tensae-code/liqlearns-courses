# Building Instruments from Recycled and Found Materials — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which of these does the syllabus name as a found material for music?
a) bottles  b) a piano  c) a trumpet
**Answer: a) bottles** — bottles, saw and comb are named.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The saw and the comb appear in the syllabus as…
a) tools for woodwork only  b) indigenous found materials for making music  c) drawing tools
**Answer: b) indigenous found materials for making music**.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Maracas and bamboo are named as…
a) foods  b) materials for making music  c) costumes
**Answer: b) materials for making music**.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** "Indigenous found materials" means…
a) materials imported for the lesson  b) materials found in the children's own place  c) materials drawn on paper
**Answer: b) materials found in the children's own place**.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Making music with found materials is set for…
a) Class 4 only  b) Classes 1–4 attainment  c) teachers only
**Answer: b) Classes 1–4 attainment**.
