# Building Instruments from Recycled and Found Materials

## Do it first

Quick start: say in one sentence what you think this lesson is about — music does not wait for a shop-bought instrument. Then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — Found materials that sound

The syllabus's indigenous-instruments work names the materials plainly: **bottles, saw and comb** — everyday things whose sound a child can draw out and use in music.

## Sub lesson 2 — Maracas and bamboo

The list continues with **maracas and bamboo** — shaken and struck sounds from the materials at hand in Barbados.

## Sub lesson 3 — Making music with what you find

Across Classes 1–4 the attainment is the same act: **making music with indigenous found materials** — choosing, sounding and playing together with what the place itself provides.
