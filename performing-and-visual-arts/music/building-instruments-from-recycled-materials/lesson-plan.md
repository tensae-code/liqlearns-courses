# Lesson plan — Building Instruments from Recycled and Found Materials

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Music · **Lesson:** Building Instruments from Recycled and Found Materials
**Place in the curriculum:** Music (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** music does not wait for a shop-bought instrument: Barbados's syllabus sets children making music with indigenous found materials — bottles, saw, comb, maracas and bamboo.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this course in the same title.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio.
