# Hands-on — Building Instruments from Recycled and Found Materials

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Sound hunt
Find three safe objects that make different sounds. Name each sound — shake, strike, scrape or blow — and play a short pattern with each.

## Found-material band
In a group, each player brings one found material. Build a piece together: one keeps the pulse, the others answer.
