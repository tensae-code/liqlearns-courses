# Lesson plan — Raga, Tala and Sargam

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Music · **Lesson:** Raga, Tala and Sargam
**Place in the curriculum:** Music (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The raga and tala of Indian and Oriental music — sargam swara singing, and the Himpalai and Saman ragas practised in song.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this course in the same title.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Let's sing swara exercises in the Himpalai raga** — the raga system from inside — a raga's swara set sung up and down (Arohana/Avarohana) in sargam notation, its phrases and character learned by singing them, with short swara patterns (Palta) drilled in tala
2. **Let's sing songs in the Saman raga** — a second raga whole — the Saman raga's swara set, its ascending/descending shapes and its songs sung from sargam, so the voice learns the raga as a place, not a scale
3. **Let's find how ragas differ** — raga identification — the Himpalai and Saman swara sets set side by side (sets A, B, C), their differing notes picked out and their songs told apart by ear and by notation
4. **Let's play the song 'Ma Na Shruti Karagena' on instruments** — raga playing — the sung raga material transferred to instruments, with thaat (parent-scale) charts for Bilawal and Kafi types and the song played from its sargam
5. **–05: the Bilawal raga** — Bilawal as the foundation raga — its swara name, its Madhyalaya (medium-tempo) song sung from sargam, and its short swara-pattern (කෙටි ස්වර බණ්ඩ) singing
6. **–19: the Thumali raga** — the Thumali (Thumri) raga's swara identity — five swaras in its body — its sargam patterns built note by note in the annex diagrams, and its character sung
7. **–39: the Kafi raga** — Kafi's Madhyalaya song in Keherwa tala (ස්ථායි/අන්තරා set out in sargam) and its short swara patterns, the raga's mood carried by its flattened notes
8. **raga songs with short swara compositions** — singing whole raga songs (Melaya, raga, Jathika Vadya, Sangeethi, Anugami, Chithrangi and Varnam swara types named) in Bilawal, Thumali and Kafi, each song's identity heard against its raga
