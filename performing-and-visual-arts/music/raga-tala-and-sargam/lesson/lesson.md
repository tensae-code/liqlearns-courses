# Raga, Tala and Sargam

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about raga, tala and sargam. Then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — Let's sing swara exercises in the Himpalai raga

The raga system from inside — a raga's swara set sung up and down (Arohana/Avarohana) in sargam notation, its phrases and character learned by singing them, with short swara patterns (Palta) drilled in tala

## Sub lesson 2 — Let's sing songs in the Saman raga

A second raga whole — the Saman raga's swara set, its ascending/descending shapes and its songs sung from sargam, so the voice learns the raga as a place, not a scale

## Sub lesson 3 — Let's find how ragas differ

Raga identification — the Himpalai and Saman swara sets set side by side (sets A, B, C), their differing notes picked out and their songs told apart by ear and by notation

## Sub lesson 4 — Let's play the song 'Ma Na Shruti Karagena' on instruments

Raga playing — the sung raga material transferred to instruments, with thaat (parent-scale) charts for Bilawal and Kafi types and the song played from its sargam

## Sub lesson 5 — –05: the Bilawal raga

Bilawal as the foundation raga — its swara name, its Madhyalaya (medium-tempo) song sung from sargam, and its short swara-pattern (කෙටි ස්වර බණ්ඩ) singing

## Sub lesson 6 — –19: the Thumali raga

The Thumali (Thumri) raga's swara identity — five swaras in its body — its sargam patterns built note by note in the annex diagrams, and its character sung

## Sub lesson 7 — –39: the Kafi raga

Kafi's Madhyalaya song in Keherwa tala (ස්ථායි/අන්තරා set out in sargam) and its short swara patterns, the raga's mood carried by its flattened notes

## Sub lesson 8 — raga songs with short swara compositions

Singing whole raga songs (Melaya, raga, Jathika Vadya, Sangeethi, Anugami, Chithrangi and Varnam swara types named) in Bilawal, Thumali and Kafi, each song's identity heard against its raga
