This lesson is Arab Rhythmic Cycles.

Arab music holds many rhythms differing in their beats; the Haja' rhythm (dum–tak–tak in two) is used mostly in religious chants, and the Sama'i Darij rhythm (dum–tak–tak–tak in three) in instrumental and sung pieces — both played from the pictured beat charts by clapping the dum and patting the thighs for the tak.

Watch, listen — then say it back in your own words.
