# Video script — Abai Kunanbaev

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is Abai Kunanbaev.

Abai Kunanbaev — Abai, Kazakhstan's great poet-composer, and the songs through which the Kazakh people remember him. — as Kazakhstan's school books teach it: Abai Kunanbaev, poet and composer, founder of Kazakh written literature; his songs kept by the people as treasure; singing a cappella, without accompaniment; his song «Қаранғы түнде тау қалғып» learned from notation — a night song whose words travelled from Goethe through Lermontov to Abai.

Watch, listen — then say it back in your own words.
