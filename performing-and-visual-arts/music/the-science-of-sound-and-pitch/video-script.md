# Video script — The Science of Sound and Pitch

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

What **is** sound?

**Vibration!** Something shakes — the ear hears it.
Big shakes — **loud**. Fast shakes — **high**.

A short tube sings high. A long tube sings low.
That is the **science of sound and pitch**.
