# The Science of Sound and Pitch — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Sound is…
a) light  b) vibration picked up by the ear  c) a kind of colour
**Answer: b) vibration picked up by the ear.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Sound cannot travel through…
a) water  b) stone  c) a vacuum
**Answer: c) a vacuum.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** A louder sound comes from…
a) smaller vibrations  b) bigger vibrations  c) no vibrations
**Answer: b) bigger vibrations.**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Frequency counts…
a) vibrations per second  b) instruments in an orchestra  c) beats in a bar
**Answer: a) vibrations per second.**

**5. (Sub lesson 1)** Which woodwind tube is longest and lowest?
a) piccolo  b) clarinet  c) bassoon
**Answer: c) bassoon.**
