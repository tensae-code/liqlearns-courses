# The Science of Sound and Pitch

## Do it first

Quick start: say one thing you already know — a sound, a song, a name — that belongs to this lesson's world. Keep it in mind; the parts will meet it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Science and sounds

**Sound is vibration** picked up by the ear. It travels through **air, stone, water, glass and brick — but not a vacuum**. **Bigger vibrations make a louder sound.** **Pitch** is how high or low a sound is, raised by **shorter or tighter** vibrating objects. **Frequency** counts **vibrations per second**. Sound-absorbing materials make ear protectors. And a woodwind's **tube length sets its pitch**: the piccolo is short and high, the clarinet medium, the bassoon long and low.
