# Hands-on — Musika Samoa: Samoa's Music History

## Three times, three moods
In a small group, choose one of the three times — the German Occupancy, the World Wars, the Mau. Talk together: what mood would a song of that time carry, and what words would give it that mood? Share your time and your mood with the class.

## Words steer the song
Say one short line of words in a proud voice, then in a sorrowful voice. The words did not change — the mood did. Say in one sentence how lyrics control a song's mood.
