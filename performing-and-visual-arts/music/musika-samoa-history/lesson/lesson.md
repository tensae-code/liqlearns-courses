# Musika Samoa: Samoa's Music History

## Do it first

Can a song remember history? Samoa's senior music guide says yes — and builds its history units on Samoa's own music. Which times in Samoa's past do you think its songs remember?

## Sub lesson 1 — Musika Sāmoa before independence

The Years 11–12 Music Teacher Guide's History and Research units study **Musika Sāmoa of pre-independence times**: the music of the **German Occupancy**, of the **two World Wars**, and of the **Mau**. The study asks after the music's **origins** and **the factors that changed it** — Samoa's music is explored from Samoa's own experience first, as the foundation for exploring the music of other traditions.

## Sub lesson 2 — Lyrics that lead the music

The guide gives the **lyrics** a leading place: pupils study **the importance of lyrics** in Musika Sāmoa and **how lyrics control the moods of songs**. In this music, the words are not an extra — they steer what the song does to the people who hear it.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion: name the three times whose music this lesson studies, and say what the lyrics do in Musika Sāmoa. Companion checks: German Occupancy, World Wars, Mau? Lyrics control the mood?
