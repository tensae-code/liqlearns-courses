# Musika Samoa: Samoa's Music History — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The guide's Musika Sāmoa history covers times before Samoa's independence.
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The Mau is one of the times whose music is studied.
**Answer: True.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** In Musika Sāmoa the melody matters but the lyrics have no effect on mood.
**Answer: False** — the lyrics control the moods of songs.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The importance of lyrics is part of the guide's history study.
**Answer: True.**
