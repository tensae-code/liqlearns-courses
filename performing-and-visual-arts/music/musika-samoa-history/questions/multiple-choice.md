# Musika Samoa: Samoa's Music History — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Musika Sāmoa in this lesson means the music of…
a) Samoa before independence  b) Europe in the Middle Ages  c) modern pop only
**Answer: a) Samoa before independence.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which three times does the history study?
a) the German Occupancy, the two World Wars and the Mau  b) three school terms  c) three festivals this year
**Answer: a) the German Occupancy, the two World Wars and the Mau.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The history study asks about the music's origins and…
a) its price  b) the factors that changed it  c) its colour
**Answer: b) the factors that changed it.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In Musika Sāmoa, the lyrics…
a) do not matter  b) control the moods of songs  c) are always in German
**Answer: b) control the moods of songs.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Samoa's guide uses Sāmoan music as…
a) the foundation for exploring other traditions' music  b) a reason to skip history  c) a warm-up only
**Answer: a) the foundation for exploring other traditions' music.**
