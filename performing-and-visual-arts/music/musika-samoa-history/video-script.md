# Video script — Musika Samoa: Samoa's Music History

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Before Samoa was independent, its music was already telling its story.

The years of the **German Occupancy**. The years of **two World Wars**. The years of the **Mau**.

What made this music? What changed it? And listen to the words — in **Musika Sāmoa**, the **lyrics control the mood** of the song.

That is Samoa's music history.
