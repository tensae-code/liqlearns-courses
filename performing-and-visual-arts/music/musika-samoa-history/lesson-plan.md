# Lesson plan — Musika Samoa: Samoa's Music History

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Music · **Lesson:** Musika Samoa: Samoa's Music History
**Place in the curriculum:** Music (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Musika Sāmoa of pre-independence times — the music of the German Occupancy, the two World Wars and the Mau — and how its lyrics carry its history and control the moods of its songs.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of its title — recommended first, never forced.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Musika Sāmoa before independence** — the music of the German Occupancy, the two World Wars and the Mau — its origins and the factors that changed it.
2. **Lyrics that lead the music** — the importance of lyrics in Musika Sāmoa, and how lyrics control the moods of songs.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of the Music title. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name the periods of Musika Sāmoa this lesson studies.
2. Say what changed Samoa's music and why its lyrics matter.

## What you need
Your ears and a companion to talk history with.

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks Samoa's music history starts at independence → the guide's history units study the times BEFORE independence: the German Occupancy, the World Wars and the Mau.
- Thinks lyrics are decoration → in Musika Sāmoa the lyrics are central: they control the moods of the songs.
