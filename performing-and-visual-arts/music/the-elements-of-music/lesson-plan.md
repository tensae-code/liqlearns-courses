# Lesson plan — The Elements of Music

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Music · **Lesson:** The Elements of Music
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 3 — Music (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** music's elements named as one set — the pupil book's six (beat, melody, harmony, structure, dynamics, tone colour) and the teacher guide's eight (rhythm, dynamics, tempo, pitch, melody, harmony, timbre, duration).
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Notes and Rests and Faster Notes, Dynamics and Tempo — the learner has met beat, dynamics and tempo one by one; here they stand together as a set.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The six elements (pupil book)** — §1.2 names music's elements: beat, melody, harmony, structure, dynamics/alteration signs, tone colour — with Grade 5's scope set inside the set.
2. **The eight elements (teacher guide — evidence)** — the guide's set: rhythm, dynamics, tempo, pitch, melody, harmony, timbre and duration — taught as labelled guide evidence.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Notes and Rests; Faster Notes, Dynamics and Tempo. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. List the pupil book's six elements of music.
2. List the guide's eight elements and say how the two lists agree.
3. Say what Grade 5's scope takes from the set: notes and rests and the sounded values.

## What you need
Voices, and any song the class knows well.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — sing one line of a known song four ways — steady, then loud-soft, then fast-slow, then high-low — and name which element you changed each time. (lesson/lesson.md).
2. **The explanation** — the sub lessons in order (lesson/lesson.md).
   - ADD IMAGE [Picture 14600]: a wheel of the six elements around a song — the student names each element in a line they sing.
3. **Say it to your companion** — teach the lesson back in your own words (lesson/lesson.md).
   - ADD VIDEO: the script in video-script.md, filmed — made later, when videos are made.
4. **Questions** — from questions/: multiple-choice, short-answer (levels.md).
5. **Hands-on** — hands-on.md.
6. **Do It Yourself** — the doing block at the end of lesson/lesson.md.

## If the learner is stuck
If the lists tangle, anchor on melody: the tune is melody; what carries it (beat/rhythm), colours it (timbre), lifts it (pitch) and powers it (dynamics) fall into place around it.
