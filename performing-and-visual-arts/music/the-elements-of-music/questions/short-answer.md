# The Elements of Music — short answer

Answer in a short sentence or a short list.


**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is Grade 5's scope inside the element set?
**Answer:** Notes and rests and their sounded values — the element-work this grade performs.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the guide's eight elements.
**Answer:** Rhythm, dynamics, tempo, pitch, melody, harmony, timbre and duration.
