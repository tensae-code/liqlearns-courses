# The Elements of Music — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.


**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which list is the pupil book's element set?
  a) Beat, melody, harmony, structure, dynamics, tone colour b) Only beat and rest c) Paper, pencil, ruler, song
**Answer: a) Beat, melody, harmony, structure, dynamics, tone colour** — §1.2 names exactly these six.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Tone colour means…
  a) The character of a sound — which voice or instrument makes it b) The colour of the printed page c) How loud a song is
**Answer: a) The character of a sound — which voice or instrument makes it** — tone colour (timbre) tells one sound-maker from another.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The guide's word for tone colour is…
  a) Timbre b) Tempo c) Tonic
**Answer: a) Timbre** — timbre and tone colour name the same element.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** ኃይለ ድምፅ (dynamics) is…
  a) The loud/soft power of sound b) The speed of the beat c) The height of a pitch
**Answer: a) The loud/soft power of sound** — dynamics = the power of sound, loud to soft.
