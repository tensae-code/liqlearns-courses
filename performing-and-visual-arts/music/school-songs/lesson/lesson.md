# School Songs

## Do it first

Sing the first letters you know: A, B, C. Now sing ሀ, ለ, ሐ. School has its own songs — songs for its letters, songs for its mornings, and a song for its teacher. All of them live in this lesson.

## Sub lesson 1 — The ABC song and English songs in class

The **ABC song** prints the Latin alphabet **A–Z** as its singing chart: the letters themselves are the song. Sing it pointing at each letter as it comes — the chart on the page, in varied writing styles, is there to be sung from.

The Grade 3 book prints **"Twinkle Twinkle Little Star" in English** — the same little star you sang in Amharic in Songs of Nature, now in the other language. And its activity opens the door wider: **collect different English songs and sing them in class.** English songs are classroom repertoire: find them, bring them, sing them together.

## Sub lesson 2 — The Fidel song and Let's Go to School

The **Fidel song** — **"ፊደል ከ ሀ እስከ ፐ"**, *The Fidel from Ha to Pe* — runs the whole fidel order from **ሀ** to **ፐ** in chart order, as a singing chart. The chart itself is the content: sing it pointing at each fidel family as its turn comes.

[Picture 9458 — two fidel orders: ሀ ሁ ሂ ሃ ሄ ህ ሆ and በ ቡ ቢ ባ ቤ ብ ቦ]

The same fidel lives inside a school-going song: **"እንሂድ ትምህርት ቤት"** — *Let's Go to School*. Its middle verses sing the fidel orders on the way: **ሀ ሁ ሂ ሃ ሄ ህ ሆ**, then **በ ቡ ቢ ባ ቤ ብ ቦ**, and a mixed line — አ ቡ ጊ ዳ ሄ ው ዞ — the letters dancing out of order. Sing the orders slowly; they are the staircase of reading.

## Sub lesson 3 — Morning songs

Two songs sing the school morning. **"ወንድሜ ያዕቆብ"** — *My Brother Yacob* — sings it as a story: **the bell rings** — get up! — wash face and hands, brush teeth, comb hair, go to school and gather good knowledge. The song carries the whole morning sequence in order, and its sing-along script acts each step as it is named.

The second is **"ጠዋት ተነስተን"** — *We Get Up in the Morning* (the Grade 2 morning song): get up, dress, wash faces, eat breakfast, take exercise books, go to school.

Sing them at the pace of a real morning: brisk, bright, no dawdling. The songs are clocks you can sing.

## Sub lesson 4 — Praising the teacher

The last school song turns to the person at the front of the class: **"ኑር መምህሬ"** — *Long Live, My Teacher*.

It is a long praise song. It honours the teacher as **the peak of professions** and **the child's pride**, and its choruses (አዝማች) come back again and again, the way thanks does. The class sings this one standing, to the teacher, as a gift.

The song came to the book from Ethiopian Children's TV — a song of the screen become a song of the classroom.

## Say it to your companion

Sing the ሀ order and the በ order to your companion from the chart. Then tell the morning order from My Brother Yacob: bell, get up, wash, brush, comb, school. Finish by singing the teacher song's chorus together.

## Do It Yourself

1. Sing the ABC song all the way to Z, pointing at each letter. Then sing the Fidel song's first two orders from the chart.
2. Tomorrow morning, do your morning in the song's order: get up, wash, brush, comb, dress, breakfast, books, school. Sing as you go.
3. Collect one English song — from a book, a screen, a visitor — and teach its chorus to the class.
