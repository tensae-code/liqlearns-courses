# Lesson plan — School Songs

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Music · **Lesson:** School Songs
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 3 — Music (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the songs of the classroom itself: the ABC song and Twinkle in English, the Fidel song from ሀ to ፐ, the morning songs that sing the day's routine, and the song that praises the teacher.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the learner's own school day — letters, mornings, and the teacher are already known; the songs carry all three.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the
exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an

shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved (the gate: README, "Units, Lessons and Sub Lessons (ruled 2026-11-08)"):

1. **The ABC song and English songs in class** — the Latin alphabet A–Z sung as its chart, and "Twinkle Twinkle Little Star" printed in English — sing English in class, and collect more English songs.
2. **The Fidel song and Let's Go to School** — "ፊደል ከ ሀ እስከ ፐ" — the whole fidel order sung as a singing chart — inside "እንሂድ ትምህርት ቤት" (Let's Go to School), whose verses sing the fidel orders on the way to school.
3. **Morning songs** — "ወንድሜ ያዕቆብ" (My Brother Yacob) — the bell rings, get up, wash, brush, comb, go to school — and "ጠዋት ተነስተን" (We Get Up in the Morning) — dress, wash faces, breakfast, books, school.
4. **Praising the teacher** — "ኑር መምህሬ" (Long Live, My Teacher) — honouring the teacher as the peak of professions and the child's pride, with its repeated choruses.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Songs of Nature — the little star song in Amharic lives there; here it returns in English. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Sing the ABC song, the Latin alphabet A–Z as its singing chart.
2. Sing "Twinkle Twinkle Little Star" in English in class, and know the activity: collect English songs and sing them.
3. Sing the Fidel song — the fidel order from ሀ (ha) to ፐ (pe) as a singing chart, pointing at each family.
4. Sing "እንሂድ ትምህርት ቤት" (Let's Go to School) with its fidel verses (ሀ and በ orders).
5. Sing both morning songs and tell the morning order each one sings.
6. Sing "ኑር መምህሬ" (Long Live, My Teacher) and say what it honours.

## What you need
Your voice; a fidel chart on the wall helps for the Fidel song; the class itself is the choir.

## If the learner is stuck
- Loses the words → go back to the call-and-answer shape: the teacher (or the audio) sings a short piece, the learner answers with the same piece. Short pieces first, whole song after.
- Loses the tune → hum the song together before singing words; the tune first, the words ride on it.
- Sings but cannot say what the song teaches → ask the lesson's own question: what is this song about, and what does it ask us to do? Every song in this unit teaches something that can be said in one sentence.
