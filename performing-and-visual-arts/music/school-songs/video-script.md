# Video script — School Songs

The super-simple voice, for the sing-along video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The songs are named and summarised, never transcribed; the script sings the learner TO the song, not the song itself.

---

A, B, C — sing the letters to Z!
ሀ ሁ ሂ ሃ ሄ ህ ሆ — sing the fidel, family by family,
from ሀ all the way to ፐ.

The bell rings! Get up! Wash, brush, comb —
take your books, let's go to school!

And one song stands tall for the teacher:
Long live, my teacher — the peak of professions,
our pride. Sing the chorus again!
