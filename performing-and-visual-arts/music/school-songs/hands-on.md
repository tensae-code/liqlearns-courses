# Hands-on — School Songs

The lesson with your hands, your feet and your voice together, not only your eyes.

## Point and sing the chart
Put a big fidel chart and an alphabet strip on the wall. Two leaders point; the class sings what is pointed at. Swap the pointer between the fidel chart and the ABC strip — the class must switch songs with the chart.

## Act the morning
In a line, mime the morning song step by step as you sing My Brother Yacob: sleeping, the bell, washing, brushing, combing, shouldering the bag, walking to school. Freeze at the school door on the last line.
