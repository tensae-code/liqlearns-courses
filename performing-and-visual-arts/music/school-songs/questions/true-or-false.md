# School Songs — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Grade 3 activity asks the class to collect different English songs and sing them in class.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The Fidel song is a singing chart: the chart itself is the content.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In My Brother Yacob you comb your hair before the bell rings.
**Answer: False** — The bell rings first — then get up, wash, brush, comb.

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** The teacher song's choruses come back again and again.
**Answer: True** — Choruses marked አዝማች.
