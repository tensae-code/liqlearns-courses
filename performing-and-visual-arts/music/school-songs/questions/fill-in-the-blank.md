# School Songs — fill in the blank

Say or write the missing word.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** The ሀ order is: ሀ ሁ ሂ ___ ሄ ህ ሆ.
**Answer:** ሃ

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The በ order is: በ ቡ ቢ ባ ___ ብ ቦ.
**Answer:** ቤ

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In We Get Up in the Morning: get up, dress, wash faces, eat ___, take exercise books, go to school.
