# School Songs — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The ABC song sings —
a) the fidel order  b) the Latin alphabet A–Z as its chart  c) the morning routine
**Answer: b) the Latin alphabet A–Z as its chart**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The Grade 3 book's English Twinkle song is the same star song as —
a) the Buhe song  b) the Amharic little-star song in Songs of Nature  c) the ABC song
**Answer: b) the Amharic little-star song in Songs of Nature**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The Fidel song runs from —
a) ሀ to ፐ  b) A to Z  c) በ to ሀ
**Answer: a) ሀ to ፐ**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In Let's Go to School, the verses sing —
a) the colours  b) the fidel orders  c) the numbers
**Answer: b) the fidel orders**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** My Brother Yacob's morning begins with —
a) breakfast  b) the bell rings — get up!  c) combing hair
**Answer: b) the bell rings — get up!**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** We Get Up in the Morning includes —
a) washing faces, breakfast, taking exercise books  b) only singing  c) going to the market
**Answer: a) washing faces, breakfast, taking exercise books**

**7. (Sub lesson 4)** "ኑር መምህሬ" honours the teacher as —
a) the tallest person  b) the peak of professions and the child's pride  c) the school's singer
**Answer: b) the peak of professions and the child's pride**

**8. (Sub lesson 4)** The teacher song came to the book from —
a) a wedding  b) Ethiopian Children's TV  c) the Grade 1 book
**Answer: b) Ethiopian Children's TV**
