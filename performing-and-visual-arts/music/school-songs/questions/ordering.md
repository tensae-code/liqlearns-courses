# School Songs — ordering

Put the things in the right order.

**1. (Sub lesson 3)** Put My Brother Yacob's morning in the song's order.
Here they are, mixed up: go to school; comb hair; brush teeth; wash face and hands; the bell rings — get up.
**Answer (right order):** the bell rings — get up → wash face and hands → brush teeth → comb hair → go to school
