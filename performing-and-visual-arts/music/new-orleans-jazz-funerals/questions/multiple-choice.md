# New Orleans Jazz Funerals — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** On the way to the cemetery the band plays…
a) slow hymns  b) ragtime  c) waltzes
**Answer: a) slow hymns**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** On the way back the band plays…
a) ragtime  b) hymns only  c) nothing
**Answer: a) ragtime**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The "second line" is…
a) the dancing crowd following the band  b) the second coffin  c) the choir
**Answer: a) the dancing crowd following the band**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** The account quoted is trumpeter…
a) Bunk Johnson's  b) Sousa's  c) Joplin's
**Answer: a) Bunk Johnson's**

**5. (Sub lesson 1)** The funeral music changes at…
a) the turn for home  b) the church door only  c) midnight
**Answer: a) the turn for home**
