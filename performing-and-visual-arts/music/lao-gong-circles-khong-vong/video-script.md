This lesson is Khong Vong: Lao Gong Circles.

Laos's khong vong gong circles — tuned gongs set in a ring and played seated. — as Laos's school books teach it: the khong vong noi (small gong circle, 18–21 tuned gongs in a rattan ring) and khong vong yai (large gong circle, 14–16 gongs), shown in photographs, their place in the Lao ensemble

Watch, listen — then say it back in your own words.
