# Video script — Sacred and Religious Songs

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is Sacred and Religious Songs.

"Forsaking the Idols", a chant of the Prophet, Gabriel's coming and the first revealed word "Read" (printed p. 8); "To the Generous", a chant of praise, prostration and the declaration of faith (printed p. 9); "Ablution", the chant of performing wudu whose water is light on the face (printed p. 9); "Like the Bird", the narrative chant of the Prophet's life — the cave of Hira, Gabriel and the call to Islam (printed p. 12); "Son of the Believers", the five-year-old in kindergarten learning his religion — cleanliness is of faith and prayer the pillar of religion (printed p. 13); "My Lord, My Lord", the child's prayer — an Arab Muslim child serving his homeland with effort and resolve (printed p. 13)

Watch, listen — then say it back in your own words.
