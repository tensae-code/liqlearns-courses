**Book 2:** *Музичка уметност за IV година гимназиско образование (изборен предмет) (Music Art for Year IV of gymnasium education, elective subject)* (student book, Macedonian) — North Macedonia, Министерство за образование и наука на Република Македонија (Ministry of Education and Science), Skopje; year 2010 (CIP), copy in hand is the 2015 printing; ISBN 978-608-226-254-3; author Suzana Tnokova Kuzmanovska; Ministry approval decision no. 22-4028/1 of 29.06.2010 Grade: Grade 13 (gymnasium Year IV, elective) (student/teacher: STUDENT). PDF pages: 96 (96 printed pages per CIP including covers; image-only scan read page by page from rendered sheets; read in place from phase3-staging, never deleted). Verdict: COMPLETE. North Macedonia's own way: the year's theory is carried on Macedonian backs — Teshkoto opens the book (in Blazhe Koneski's poem), Shuplevski, Skalovski and Prokopiev are the composer portraits, the folk songs are Bitola's, Christmas and Easter are told in Macedonian custom, and the ballet whose story is retold in full is Prokopiev's "Labin i Dojrana". Excluded per standing rules: the quotations page (printed p. 3) and the orchestra photograph (p. 4) are front matter; pure practice pages are not placed — the children's-instrument playing exercises (p. 20), the melodic exercises (pp. 38, 44), the syncopation/contratan/triplet drill examples (p. 53) and the recorder exercises (p. 65); the jazz (p. 60) and rock (p. 61) spreads are photo-and-listening pages whose teachable text duplicates Book 7's jazz and rock chapters; printed pp. 92–96 are a blank endpaper and blank staff paper. The Johann Strauss chapter (p. 72) repeats Book 7's Strauss biography and is placed here as printed in this book. Song lyrics are summarised, never transcribed.
