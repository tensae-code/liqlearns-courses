# Electronic Music and Techno

## Do it first

Name one thing that belongs in this lesson — Music made by electronic devices — the synthesizer, electronic music's story, and techno, the Detroit-born electronic dance music. Say what it is in your own words. If you can name one, you are already inside the lesson.

## Sub lesson 1 — electronic music: sound made, processed and reproduced by electronic devices

electronic music: sound made, processed and reproduced by electronic devices — synthesizers, oscillators, tape, computers and samplers; echo, speed change and transformation as composition; Stockhausen's spatial sound and Varèse.

## Sub lesson 2 — techno

techno: electronic dance music born in 1980s Detroit (Juan Atkins, Derrick May, Kevin Saunderson) — repeating rhythm, synthetic sounds and bass lines, atmosphere over melody, made for clubs.

## Sub lesson 3 — Техно музика

Техно музика — techno: born in mid-1980s Detroit (Kevin Saunderson, Juan Atkins, Derrick May) from house, disco, Afrika Bambaataa and Kraftwerk; underground in the USA, mainstream in Britain by the late 80s; its styles — hardcore techno at the edge of human endurance in BPM, ambient techno for the chill-out room, jungle's breakbeats and reggae, acid techno (Plastikman, Dave Clarke) and progressive techno.

## Sub lesson 4 — Најпознати претставници

Најпознати претставници — techno's best-known representatives in portrait: Carl Cox, Derrick May, Plastikman, Juan Atkins, Kevin Saunderson, Sven Väth, Umek, Jeff Mills, Marco Bailey and Dave Clarke.

## Sub lesson 5 — Синтисајзер

Синтисајзер — the synthesizer: electronic instruments can replace most instruments; from the rhythm machines came the electric keyboards — the synthesizer, analogue and digital, producing the most various sounds by synthetic means.

## Sub lesson 6 — Познати изведувачи

Познати изведувачи — the keyboard and synthesizer performers in portrait: Joe Zawinul, Jean-Michel Jarre, Peter Gabriel, Stevie Wonder, Herbie Hancock, Chick Corea and Bernie Worrell.
