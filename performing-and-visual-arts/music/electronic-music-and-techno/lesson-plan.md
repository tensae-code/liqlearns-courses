# Lesson plan — Electronic Music and Techno

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Music · **Lesson:** Electronic Music and Techno
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 3 — Music (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Music made by electronic devices — the synthesizer, electronic music's story, and techno, the Detroit-born electronic dance music.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the course's earlier lessons in its title; recommended first, never forced.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of ..." in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **electronic music: sound made, processed and reproduced by electronic devices** — electronic music: sound made, processed and reproduced by electronic devices — synthesizers, oscillators, tape, computers and samplers; echo, speed change and transformation as composition; Stockhausen's spatial sound and Varèse.
2. **techno** — techno: electronic dance music born in 1980s Detroit (Juan Atkins, Derrick May, Kevin Saunderson) — repeating rhythm, synthetic sounds and bass lines, atmosphere over melody, made for clubs.
3. **Техно музика** — Техно музика — techno: born in mid-1980s Detroit (Kevin Saunderson, Juan Atkins, Derrick May) from house, disco, Afrika Bambaataa and Kraftwerk; underground in the USA, mainstream in Britain by the late 80s; its styles — hardcore techno at the edge of human endurance in BPM, ambient techno for the chill-out room, jungle's breakbeats and reggae, acid techno (Plastikman, Dave Clarke) and progressive techno.
4. **Најпознати претставници** — Најпознати претставници — techno's best-known representatives in portrait: Carl Cox, Derrick May, Plastikman, Juan Atkins, Kevin Saunderson, Sven Väth, Umek, Jeff Mills, Marco Bailey and Dave Clarke.
5. **Синтисајзер** — Синтисајзер — the synthesizer: electronic instruments can replace most instruments; from the rhythm machines came the electric keyboards — the synthesizer, analogue and digital, producing the most various sounds by synthetic means.
6. **Познати изведувачи** — Познати изведувачи — the keyboard and synthesizer performers in portrait: Joe Zawinul, Jean-Michel Jarre, Peter Gabriel, Stevie Wonder, Herbie Hancock, Chick Corea and Bernie Worrell.
