# Electronic Music and Techno — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — electronic music: sound made, processed and reproduced by electronic devices
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: electronic music: sound made, processed and reproduced by electronic devices — synthesizers, oscillators, tape, computers and samplers; echo, speed change and transformation as composition; Stockhausen's spatial sound and Varèse.

## Sub lesson 2 — techno
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: techno: electronic dance music born in 1980s Detroit (Juan Atkins, Derrick May, Kevin Saunderson) — repeating rhythm, synthetic sounds and bass lines, atmosphere over melody, made for clubs.

## Sub lesson 3 — Техно музика
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Техно музика — techno: born in mid-1980s Detroit (Kevin Saunderson, Juan Atkins, Derrick May) from house, disco, Afrika Bambaataa and Kraftwerk; underground in the USA, mainstream in Britain by the late 80s; its styles — hardcore techno at the edge of human endurance in BPM, ambient techno for the chill-out room, jungle's breakbeats and reggae, acid techno (Plastikman, Dave Clarke) and progressive techno.

Sub lesson 5 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Синтисајзер — the synthesizer: electronic instruments can replace most instruments; from the rhythm machines came the electric keyboards — the synthesizer, analogue and digital, producing the most various sounds by synthetic means.
