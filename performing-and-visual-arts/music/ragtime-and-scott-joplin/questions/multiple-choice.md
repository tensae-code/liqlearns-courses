# Ragtime and Scott Joplin — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Ragtime was a craze of…
a) 1900–1914 America  b) 1700s Vienna  c) modern Iceland only
**Answer: a) 1900–1914 America**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Ragtime's king is…
a) Scott Joplin  b) Sousa  c) Debussy
**Answer: a) Scott Joplin**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The Maple Leaf Rag's form is…
a) A A B B A C C D D  b) AABA only  c) one section repeated
**Answer: a) A A B B A C C D D**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Syncopation rules which section?
a) the B section  b) the introduction  c) the ending only
**Answer: a) the B section**

**5. (Sub lesson 1)** The class hears Joplin from…
a) his own piano roll  b) a live orchestra  c) a radio play
**Answer: a) his own piano roll**
