# Ragtime and Scott Joplin — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Ragtime is syncopated piano music.
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Joplin never recorded his playing in any form.
**Answer: False.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The Maple Leaf Rag has lettered sections.
**Answer: True.**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Ragtime was a European court dance.
**Answer: False.**
