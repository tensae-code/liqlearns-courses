This lesson is Flamenco and Spanish Music.

Spain's flamenco and the wider Spanish music of the Mediterranean world. — as Greece's school books teach it: flamenco — Paco de Lucía's guitar in "Una gitana morena" and its steady pulse; de Falla's Ritual Fire Dance from El amor brujo.

Watch, listen — then say it back in your own words.
