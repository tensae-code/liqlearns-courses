## Sub lesson 1 — Vietnam Grade 9 Âm nhạc 9

The đàn đáy — the đàn đáy is a folk instrument of the Vietnamese people; the instrument's body is made of wood, in a trapezoidal shape, the large base lying above and the small base lying below; the đàn đáy has three strings of soft silk, and the player uses a bamboo stick or a plastic piece to pluck the strings to create the sound; the đàn đáy has a warm, slightly opaque timbre, and is often combined with the phách (clappers) and the trống chầu (praise drum) to accompany hát ca trù (ca trù singing) or the reciting of poetry; today the đàn đáy has also been brought into ensemble with the folk orchestra
