# Lesson plan — Performing Kinds of Music

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Music · **Lesson:** Performing Kinds of Music
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 3 — Music (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the kinds of music the class already knows by ear, now sung and performed — music learned by doing it, in two answering groups (teacher guide — evidence).
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Sounds Around Us and Kinds of Traditional Music; this lesson is labelled teacher-guide evidence throughout — the Grade 2 guide is a scan with no text layer, cited at section level only.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The kinds of music, performed (teacher guide — evidence)** — the music types distinguished in the guide's Chapter 1 are now sung and performed, not only recognised.
2. **Answering songs: two groups, one beat (teacher guide — evidence)** — the class sings in two groups answering each other — the guide's pictured way of performing the kinds.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Sounds Around Us (listening first). Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name the kinds of music the guide's Chapter 1 distinguished, and perform one of them.
2. Sing in two groups that answer each other, keeping the beat together.

## What you need
Only voices and a steady beat — hands or a drum.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — stand in two groups and answer each other's sung line, call and answer, keeping one shared beat. (lesson/lesson.md).
2. **The explanation** — the sub lessons in order (lesson/lesson.md).
   - ADD AUDIO: two groups answering each other in song — the student sings an answering line in time.
3. **Say it to your companion** — teach the lesson back in your own words (lesson/lesson.md).
   - ADD VIDEO: the script in video-script.md, filmed — made later, when videos are made.
4. **Questions** — from questions/: multiple-choice, short-answer (levels.md).
5. **Hands-on** — hands-on.md.
6. **Do It Yourself** — the doing block at the end of lesson/lesson.md.

## Add-ons for this lesson (the full list)
- VIDEO — the video-script filmed (made later).
- AUDIO (described only) — two groups answering in song; a song being sung is not drawn or synthesised in this build.

## If the learner is stuck
If the answer group loses the beat, slow the whole song down and clap the beat aloud until both groups share it.
