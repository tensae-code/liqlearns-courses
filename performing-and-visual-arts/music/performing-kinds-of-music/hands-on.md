# Hands-on — Performing Kinds of Music

The lesson with your hands, your feet and your voice together, not only your eyes.

## Answer me
Split into two groups. Group One sings any short line it knows; Group Two answers. Swap roles three times, keeping the beat with claps.

## Kinds by doing
Pick one kind of music the class knows. Perform a minute of it together, then name what made it that kind.
