# Performing Kinds of Music — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.


**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In this lesson, a kind of music is fully learned when you can…
  a) Perform it — sing it in time b) Only spell its name c) Draw it silently
**Answer: a) Perform it — sing it in time** — the guide's §2.1 turns the Chapter 1 kinds into singing and performing.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's source is…
  a) A teacher guide, cited at section level b) A student textbook chapter c) A song printed in full
**Answer: a) A teacher guide, cited at section level** — the Grade 2 guide is a scan with no text layer — section-level citation only.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** In the two-group singing, what must never break?
  a) The shared beat b) The loudest voice c) The standing order
**Answer: a) The shared beat** — the answer changes; the beat carries both groups.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Group Two's job in an answering song is to…
  a) Answer Group One's line in time b) Sing a different song over it c) Stay silent to the end
**Answer: a) Answer Group One's line in time** — call and answer — each group performs its part.
