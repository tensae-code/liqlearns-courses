# Animal Songs — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The lamb in the song is given by —
a) mother  b) father  c) the teacher
**Answer: b) father**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The lamb song ends with the singers —
a) barking  b) bleating  c) chirping
**Answer: b) bleating** — Imitating the lamb's bleating sound.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The cat's sound in its song is —
a) miyaw  b) a bark  c) a crow
**Answer: a) miyaw**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Who gives the cat in the song?
a) father  b) mother  c) a neighbour
**Answer: b) mother**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** The puppy in the dog song is —
a) angry  b) asleep  c) happy to see the child
**Answer: c) happy to see the child**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** The puppy answers the child with —
a) its barking sound  b) a song of its own  c) silence
**Answer: a) its barking sound**

**7. (Sub lesson 4)** The bird song praises the bird's —
a) nest and eggs  b) colour and morning chirping  c) long legs
**Answer: b) colour and morning chirping**

**8. (Sub lesson 4)** The bird in the song belongs to —
a) father  b) mother  c) the morning — no one gives it
**Answer: c) the morning — no one gives it**
