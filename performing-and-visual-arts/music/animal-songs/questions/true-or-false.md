# Animal Songs — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The lamb song is sung like a call and an answer.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A cat's miyaw should be made loud like a shout.
**Answer: False** — Small and soft — a cat does not shout.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The puppy's bark in the song is a happy greeting.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** The bird song is about a bird given by father.
**Answer: False** — The bird belongs to the morning.
