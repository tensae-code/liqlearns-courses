# Animal Songs

## Do it first

Make the sound of a lamb. Now a cat. Now a dog. Now a bird. You already know the endings of all four songs in this lesson — every song finishes with the animal's own sound, made by you.

## Sub lesson 1 — The lamb song

The first song is **"ትንሽ በግ አለኝ"** — *I Have a Little Lamb*.

A father gives his child a little lamb. The song goes back and forth like a call and an answer — one voice calls, the others answer. And at the end, the singers do what the lamb does: they bleat.

Sing it slowly first. When the lamb's turn comes, make the bleating sound soft and shaky, the way a real lamb calls for its mother.

## Sub lesson 2 — The cat song

The second song is **"ትንሽ ድመት አለችኝ"** — *I Have a Little Cat*.

This time it is mother who gives the animal: a little cat. The song is a loving song — the child loves the cat, holds it gently, and imitates its sound: **miyaw**.

Make the miyaw small and soft. A cat does not shout. Stroke the air gently as you sing, the way you would stroke a real cat.

## Sub lesson 3 — The dog song

The third song is **"ትንሽዬ ውሻ አለኝ"** — *I Have a Little Dog*.

Father gives this one too: a little puppy. The puppy in the song is happy — happy to see the child coming — and it answers the child with its barking sound.

Make the bark bright and friendly, two short happy barks. This puppy is not angry; it is greeting its friend.

## Sub lesson 4 — The bird song

The last song is **"ወፏን እወዳታለሁ"** — *I Love the Bird*.

No one gives this animal. The bird belongs to the morning. The song praises the bird's beautiful colour and its chirping sound — the first sound of the day — and the learners imitate the chirping.

Lift your head like a bird on a branch and make the chirping light and quick, again and again, the way the morning fills with it.

## Say it to your companion

Sing your favourite of the four songs to your companion, with its animal sound at the end. Then tell your companion: who gives the lamb? Who gives the cat? Who gives the puppy? And who does the bird belong to?

## Do It Yourself

1. Sing all four songs in a row, making each animal's sound at its place: bleat, miyaw, bark, chirp.
2. Draw the four animals on one page. Under each one, write the name of its song.
3. Teach the cat song to someone at home, with the miyaw at its place.
