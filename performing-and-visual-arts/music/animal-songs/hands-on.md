# Hands-on — Animal Songs

The lesson with your hands, your feet and your voice together, not only your eyes.

## Song circle
Stand in a circle. The teacher names an animal — lamb, cat, dog, bird — and the circle sings that animal's song together, making the sound at its place. Change animals quickly and keep the circle singing.

## Which animal is missing?
Sing all four sound-endings one after another — bleat, miyaw, bark, chirp — but leave one out. A partner names the missing animal. Then swap.
