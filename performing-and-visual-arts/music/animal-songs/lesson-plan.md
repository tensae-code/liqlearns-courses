# Lesson plan — Animal Songs

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Music · **Lesson:** Animal Songs
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 3 — Music (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** four little songs, one for each animal — the lamb, the cat, the dog and the bird — and every song ends the same way: you make the animal's own sound.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Sounds Around Us and Name That Animal Sound — the learner can hear, name and imitate animal sounds; now the sounds live inside songs.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the
exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an
image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture
of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does
with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and
while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is
dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the
student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph,
or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be
shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved (the gate: README, "Units, Lessons and Sub Lessons (ruled 2026-11-08)"):

1. **The lamb song** — "ትንሽ በግ አለኝ" (I Have a Little Lamb) — a call-and-response song about a lamb given by father, ending in imitating the lamb's bleating sound.
2. **The cat song** — "ትንሽ ድመት አለችኝ" (I Have a Little Cat) — a song about a cat given by mother; learners love the cat and imitate its "miyaw" sound.
3. **The dog song** — "ትንሽዬ ውሻ አለኝ" (I Have a Little Dog) — a song about a puppy given by father, happy to see the child, answering with its barking sound.
4. **The bird song** — "ወፏን እወዳታለሁ" (I Love the Bird) — a song praising a bird's colour and its morning chirping sound, which learners imitate.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Name That Animal Sound — the four animals and their sounds. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Sing the lamb song and finish with the lamb's bleating sound.
2. Sing the cat song and make the cat's miyaw sound.
3. Sing the dog song and answer with the puppy's bark.
4. Sing the bird song and imitate the morning chirping.
5. Say who gives each animal in the songs: father gives the lamb and the puppy; mother gives the cat.

## What you need
Your voice. Room to stand in a circle and sing together.

## If the learner is stuck
- Loses the words → go back to the call-and-answer shape: the teacher (or the audio) sings a short piece, the learner answers with the same piece. Short pieces first, whole song after.
- Loses the tune → hum the song together before singing words; the tune first, the words ride on it.
- Sings but cannot say what the song teaches → ask the lesson's own question: what is this song about, and what does it ask us to do? Every song in this unit teaches something that can be said in one sentence.
