# Modern Instruments

## Do it first

An azmari's masenqo melody, carried for centuries. Now a trumpet lifts it, a saxophone answers it, drums and keyboard hold its floor. The melody is the same Ethiopian melody — wearing modern company. This lesson names that company, instrument by instrument.

## Sub lesson 1 — The modern band instruments

The Grade 8 book treats the instrument families **exhaustively, traditional AND modern**: beside washint and embilta stand the modern winds — **trumpet and saxophone**; beside kebero and atamo, the **modern drum kit**; and the **keyboard** joins the band as harmony-maker. Each modern instrument takes a sound-role the traditional house already knew: trumpet lifts and proclaims (the meleket's old office), saxophone sings the melodic line, drums keep the beat family's time, keyboard fills the harmony the lyres implied.

## Sub lesson 2 — Modern and traditional, sounding together

The book's families meet in the **modern Ethiopian band**: **traditional melodies — built on the qenet — are carried by modern instruments**, masenqo and krar often sounding beside trumpet and keyboard in the same piece. The Grade 7 book sets the same contrast from its side (its modern-music opening against the traditional craft). The lesson's judgement, in the books' own spirit: **an instrument is modern by its making, but the music stays Ethiopian by its melody, its qenet and its occasions** — judge the music, not the metal.
