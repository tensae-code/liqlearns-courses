# Modern Instruments — short answer

Answer in a short sentence or a short list.


**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the four modern instruments this lesson treats.
**Answer:** Trumpet, saxophone, modern drum kit and keyboard.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Contrast the saxophone with its nearest traditional partner.
**Answer:** The saxophone is a modern metal wind singing the melodic line; its nearest traditional partner is the washint, the bamboo flute — same singing office, different making.
