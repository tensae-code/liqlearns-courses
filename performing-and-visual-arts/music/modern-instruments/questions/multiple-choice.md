# Modern Instruments — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.


**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The trumpet's modern role continues the old office of…
  a) Proclaiming — the meleket's office b) Keeping funeral silence c) Weaving cloth
**Answer: a) Proclaiming — the meleket's office** — lift and proclamation — the long trumpet's ancient job in new metal.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In the modern band, harmony is filled by…
  a) The keyboard b) Only the kebero c) The audience
**Answer: a) The keyboard** — keyboard as the band's harmony-maker.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Music stays Ethiopian, in this teaching, by…
  a) Its melody, its qenet and its occasions b) Only its oldest instruments c) Never changing at all
**Answer: a) Its melody, its qenet and its occasions** — judge the music, not the metal.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In the modern band, traditional and modern instruments…
  a) Sound together in the same piece b) Are never allowed to mix c) Play in different countries
**Answer: a) Sound together in the same piece** — masenqo beside trumpet — one piece, two ages of instruments.
