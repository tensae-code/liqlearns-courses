# Hands-on — Modern Instruments

The lesson with your hands, your feet and your voice together, not only your eyes.

## Two ages, one line
Sing a traditional melody. Half the class answers each line with a 'trumpet' voice (bright, proclaiming), half with a 'washint' voice (soft, breathing). Compare the offices.

## Band chart
Draw the modern band: four modern instruments placed beside their traditional partners.
