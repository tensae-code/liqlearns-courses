# Lesson plan — Modern Instruments

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Music · **Lesson:** Modern Instruments
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 3 — Music (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the modern instruments that joined Ethiopia's music — the band instruments heard beside the traditional families — and how modern and traditional sound together in the modern Ethiopian band.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Ethiopia's Instrument Families — the traditional house complete; here are its newer rooms.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The modern band instruments** — the Grade 8 book's modern set treated beside its traditional families: trumpet and saxophone among the winds, keyboard and modern drums in the band.
2. **Modern and traditional, sounding together** — the modern Ethiopian band: traditional melodies and qenet carried by modern instruments — the contrast the Grade 7 book also sets.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Ethiopia's Instrument Families; Amhara Music History (the road to the bands). Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name the modern band instruments the book treats and their sound-roles.
2. Tell how modern instruments joined the traditional music without ending it.
3. Contrast one modern instrument with its nearest traditional partner.

## What you need
Listening memory of any modern Ethiopian band recording the class knows.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — hum a traditional melody, then imagine a trumpet and a saxophone joining it — the melody did not change; its company did. (lesson/lesson.md).

3. **Say it to your companion** — teach the lesson back in your own words (lesson/lesson.md).

## If the learner is stuck
Anchor each modern instrument to its office: trumpet proclaims, saxophone sings, drums time, keyboard harmonises — the offices are older than the instruments.
