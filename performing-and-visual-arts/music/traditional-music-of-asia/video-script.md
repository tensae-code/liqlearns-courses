# Video script — Traditional Music of Asia

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This is Indonesia's own lesson.

**Bab III Apresiasi Keragaman Musik Asia.** appreciating the diversity of Asian music — the traditional instruments of Southeast Asian countries, and traditional songs of Asian countries together with the stories behind each work: the history, customs and nature of its country (summaries only, no lyrics).

That is Traditional Music of Asia — Indonesia's way, kept by name.
