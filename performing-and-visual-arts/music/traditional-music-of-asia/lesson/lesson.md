# Traditional Music of Asia

## Do it first

Name one thing that belongs in this lesson — Asia's traditional musics in their diversity, appreciated across the continent. Say what it is in your own words. If you can name one, you are already inside the lesson.

## Sub lesson 1 — Bab III Apresiasi Keragaman Musik Asia

appreciating the diversity of Asian music — the traditional instruments of Southeast Asian countries, and traditional songs of Asian countries together with the stories behind each work: the history, customs and nature of its country (summaries only, no lyrics).

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson holds, part by part, in your own words. Companion checks: did every part get named, with its Indonesian name kept where the book gives one?

## Do It Yourself

1. Choose one named part of this lesson and find out one more true thing about it — from a person who knows it, or from the book your part came from.
2. Make or perform a small piece in the way of that part: a rhythm, a step, a drawing, a shaped piece of clay.
3. Write one sentence: what does this part show about Indonesia's own way of teaching its arts?
