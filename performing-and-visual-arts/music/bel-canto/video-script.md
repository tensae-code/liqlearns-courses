This is North Macedonia's own lesson.

**Italian opera and bel canto, "beautiful singing".** Italian opera and bel canto, "beautiful singing": long expressive vocal lines prizing beauty, purity and virtuosity while the orchestra accompanies — Rossini, who raised comic opera to art ("The Barber of Seville") and wrote the heroic "William Tell"; Donizetti ("L'elisir d'amore"); Bellini ("Norma").

That is Bel Canto — North Macedonia's way, kept by name.
