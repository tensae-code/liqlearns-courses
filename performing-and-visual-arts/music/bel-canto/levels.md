## Sub lesson 1 — Italian opera and bel canto, "beautiful singing"
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Italian opera and bel canto, "beautiful singing": long expressive vocal lines prizing beauty, purity and virtuosity while the orchestra accompanies — Rossini, who raised comic opera to art ("The Barber of Seville") and wrote the heroic "William Tell"; Donizetti ("L'elisir d'amore"); Bellini ("Norma").
