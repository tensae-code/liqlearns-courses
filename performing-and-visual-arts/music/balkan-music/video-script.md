# Video script — Balkan Music

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is Balkan Music.

The musics of the Balkans — their rhythms, instruments and songs across the peninsula's peoples. — as Greece's school books teach it: singing «Βενζινάδικο» (Kustino Oro, a traditional Romani song); comparing it with the Gipsy Kings' "Mosaïque"; clapping the 7/8 metre with its accents.

Watch, listen — then say it back in your own words.
