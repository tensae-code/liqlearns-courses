# History of Indigenous Music — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.


**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's standing is…
  a) Teacher-guide evidence, labelled b) Pupil-book teaching c) A transcribed chant
**Answer: a) Teacher-guide evidence, labelled** — guide material is evidence and is named so.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The guide's history runs from chant systems through…
  a) Azmari and regional traditions to today's keepers b) Only foreign orchestras c) A single decade
**Answer: a) Azmari and regional traditions to today's keepers** — the long line of the community's own music.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Units 4–5 of the Grade 7 guide are labelled…
  a) EVIDENCE-LIMITED b) Fully verified c) Pupil teaching
**Answer: a) EVIDENCE-LIMITED** — placed from contents and syllabus only — and labelled so.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The guide's body was read to printed page…
  a) 109 b) 200 c) 12
**Answer: a) 109** — body printed pp. 1–109 only.
