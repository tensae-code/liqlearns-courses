# History of Indigenous Music — short answer

Answer in a short sentence or a short list.


**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does 'indigenous' add to the word 'music' here?
**Answer:** That the music is the community's own — built, carried and proved by the community itself across generations.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why label evidence-limited parts instead of dropping them?
**Answer:** So the learner knows exactly how far the source was read and how much weight each part can bear.
