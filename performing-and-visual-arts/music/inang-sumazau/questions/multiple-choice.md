# Inang and Sumazau Songs — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson teaches...
a) another country's arts only  b) The Inang Sumazau song of Sabah, Malaysia — the song that carries the Sumazau dance  c) nothing — it is just names
**Answer: b)** — that is the lesson's one thing.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The first sub lesson is...
a) A song that joins the Malay inang dance-song with Sabah's sumazau  b) an examination  c) a song from another continent
**Answer: a) A song that joins the Malay inang dance-song with Sabah's sumazau.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Malaysia's own forms in this lesson are...
a) dropped and replaced  b) kept as named parts  c) hidden
**Answer: b) kept as named parts.**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** The last sub lesson is...
a) A song that joins the Malay inang dance-song with Sabah's sumazau  b) the bibliography  c) a review box
**Answer: a) A song that joins the Malay inang dance-song with Sabah's sumazau.**
