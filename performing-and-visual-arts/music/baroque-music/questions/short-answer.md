# Baroque Music — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** For which play was 'Music for a While' written?
**Answer: Dryden and Lee's Oedipus.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What happens in the music when the snakes drop?
**Answer: A falling figure sounds — word-painting.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What stays the same while the melody changes?
**Answer: The ground bass pattern repeats.**
