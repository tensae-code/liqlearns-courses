# Baroque Music — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Purcell was the leading Baroque composer of…
a) England  b) France  c) Brazil
**Answer: a) England**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** 'Music for a While' was written in…
a) 1692  b) 1492  c) 1992
**Answer: a) 1692**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The song is for voice with…
a) harpsichord and bass viol  b) a rock band  c) a choir of fifty
**Answer: a) harpsichord and bass viol**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A ground bass is…
a) a repeating bass pattern  b) a very loud drum  c) the first singer
**Answer: a) a repeating bass pattern**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Word-painting means the music…
a) pictures the words  b) is painted on paper  c) has no words
**Answer: a) pictures the words**
