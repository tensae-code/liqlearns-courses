# Baroque Music — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** 'Music for a While' calms the Fury Alecto.
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In a ground bass, the bass pattern changes every bar and never repeats.
**Answer: False — it repeats under the changing melody.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Purcell wrote for the stage.
**Answer: True.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Word-painting means painting a picture of the composer.
**Answer: False — the music pictures the words.**
