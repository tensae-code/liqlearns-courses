## Sub lesson 1 — Purcell and 'Music for a While'

**Henry Purcell** was the **leading English Baroque composer**. His **1692** stage song **'Music for a While'** was written for Dryden and Lee's play **Oedipus**. It is a song for **voice and continuo — harpsichord and bass viol** — and in the play its music **calms the Fury Alecto**.

## Sub lesson 2 — The ground bass and word-painting

The song is built on a **ground bass** — a **repeating bass pattern** under a **changing melody**; the listener can **count the ground bass's returns**. Purcell also uses **word-painting**: the music **pictures the words** — a **falling figure** sounds as the **snakes drop** from Alecto's head.
