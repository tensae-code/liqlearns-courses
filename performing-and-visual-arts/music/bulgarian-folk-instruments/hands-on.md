# Hands-on — Bulgarian Folk Instruments

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the lesson's thing
Work through the lesson's first part yourself: Topics 14–15 and 17: Гайда (the gaida bagpipe), Тъпан (the tapan drum) and Народни инструменти (folk instruments as a family). Show a companion what you made or did, and say what the book calls it.

## Show and tell
Each learner shows their work and names the part of the lesson it comes from. The companion checks the name against the lesson.
