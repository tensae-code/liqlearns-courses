# Bulgarian Folk Instruments — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part is "Topics 14–15 and 17: Гайда (the gaida bagpipe), Тъпан (the tapan drum) and Народни инструменти (folk instruments as a family)".
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson was invented with no school book behind it.
**Answer: False** — it is stocked from Bulgarian school books, with printed pages named in the sources.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** "Topics 7, 9, 10: Кавал (the kaval end-blown flute), Гъдулка (the gadulka bowed lute) and Тамбура (the tambura long-necked lute)" is a part of this lesson.
**Answer: True.**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Songs and stories in this course are transcribed in full.
**Answer: False** — they are placed by summary only.
