# Bulgarian Folk Instruments — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson teaches mainly about…
a) the weather only  b) bulgarian folk instruments  c) none of these
**Answer: b) bulgarian folk instruments.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The first part of the lesson is…
a) the last part of another lesson  b) Topics 14–15 and 17: Гайда (the gaida bagpipe), Тъпан (the tapan drum) and Народни инструменти (folk instruments as a family)  c) none of these
**Answer: b) Topics 14–15 and 17: Гайда (the gaida bagpipe), Тъпан (the tapan drum) and Народни инструменти (folk instruments as a family).**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Bulgaria's books teach this lesson as…
a) a secret kept from pupils  b) a named part of the school art course  c) none of these
**Answer: b) a named part of the school art course.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Another part of the lesson is…
a) a part of a different course  b) Topics 7, 9, 10: Кавал (the kaval end-blown flute), Гъдулка (the gadulka bowed lute) and Тамбура (the tambura long-necked lute)  c) none of these
**Answer: b) Topics 7, 9, 10: Кавал (the kaval end-blown flute), Гъдулка (the gadulka bowed lute) and Тамбура (the tambura long-necked lute).**

**5. (Sub lesson 1)** Every part of this lesson comes from…
a) a guess with no book behind it  b) a Bulgarian school book, with its printed pages named in the sources  c) none of these
**Answer: b) a Bulgarian school book, with its printed pages named in the sources.**
