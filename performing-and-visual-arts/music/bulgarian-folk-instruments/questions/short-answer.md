# Bulgarian Folk Instruments — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the first part of this lesson.
**Answer: Topics 14–15 and 17: Гайда (the gaida bagpipe), Тъпан (the tapan drum) and Народни инструменти (folk instruments as a family).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which country's school books teach this lesson here?
**Answer: Bulgaria's.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Say one thing the last part of the lesson teaches.
**Answer: Topics 7, 9, 10: Кавал (the kaval end-blown flute), Гъдулка (the gadulka bowed lute) and Тамбура (the tambura long-necked lute) — as the book teaches it.**
