# Hands-on — Samoan Instruments: Pate, Lali and Selo

## Hear the staccato
Tap a short, sharp rhythm on a table, a wood block or two coconut shells — short sounds, cleanly cut off, the way the programme describes the staccato of pātē and lali. Your partner copies it exactly.

## Make one from the house
Choose one household thing — a tin, a shell, a piece of fala — and find two different sounds it can make. Name your instrument and play a four-beat pattern on it for the class.
