# Video script — Samoan Instruments: Pate, Lali and Selo

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Tap-tap-tap! Sharp and quick — that is the **staccato of the pātē and lali**, Samoa's own slit drums.

Children in Samoa play them and hear them. And listen — a horn made from PVC pipe calls the families together to worship!

No drum at home? No problem. A **selo**, a wood block, coconut shells — even fala and tins from the house become instruments.

These are Samoa's instruments.
