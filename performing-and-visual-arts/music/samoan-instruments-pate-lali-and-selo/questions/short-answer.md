# Samoan Instruments: Pate, Lali and Selo — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name Samoa's two slit drums.
**Answer: The pātē and the lali.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the selo's companions: two other homemade or gathered instruments the programme names.
**Answer: The wood block and coconut shells (homemade instruments from fala and tins are also named).**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** What does "staccato" tell you about the pātē and lali sound?
**Answer: It is short, sharp and cut off.**
