# Samoan Instruments: Pate, Lali and Selo — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The pātē and lali are Samoa's own instruments, played and heard by Samoan children.
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** PVC horns in Samoa's programme are toys with no real use.
**Answer: False** — they call families together to worship.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Samoa's programme says instruments must all be bought from a shop.
**Answer: False** — it names homemade instruments from household things like fala and tins.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The selo is named among Samoa's classroom instruments.
**Answer: True.**
