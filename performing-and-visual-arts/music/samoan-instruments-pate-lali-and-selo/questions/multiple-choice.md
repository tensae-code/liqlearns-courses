# Samoan Instruments: Pate, Lali and Selo — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The pātē and lali are…
a) string instruments  b) Samoa's slit drums  c) wind instruments from Europe
**Answer: b) Samoa's slit drums** — named among the instruments Samoan children play and hear.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The programme describes the sound of pātē and lali as…
a) staccato — short and sharp  b) a long hum  c) silent
**Answer: a) staccato — short and sharp.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The PVC-made horns are used to…
a) carry water  b) call families together to worship  c) mark the school fence
**Answer: b) call families together to worship.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which of these is named as a homemade instrument material in Samoa's programme?
a) glass bottles only  b) fala and tins from the household  c) plastic keyboards
**Answer: b) fala and tins from the household.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** In the senior years, which instrument joins the Samoan classroom?
a) the ukulele  b) the violin  c) the trumpet
**Answer: a) the ukulele.**
