# Musicians of the Amhara Region — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.


**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Lema Gebre Hiwot's childhood arts included…
  a) Mesenqo, washint, shilela and fukera b) Only the piano c) Only dance
**Answer: a) Mesenqo, washint, shilela and fukera** — an artist from age 7, on the region's own instruments and chants.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** During the Italian invasion, Lema…
  a) Roused patriots by shilela and fukera b) Left the country c) Stopped singing
**Answer: a) Roused patriots by shilela and fukera** — chant as patriotic duty — the Adwa line alive in his generation.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Aster Aweke was born at…
  a) Hamusit, South Gondar b) Koremash, North Shewa c) Addis Ababa
**Answer: a) Hamusit, South Gondar** — the book's opening fact of her life.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In America, Aster studied…
  a) Computer science b) Medicine c) Law
**Answer: a) Computer science** — at a community college in Northern Virginia, after travelling in 1981 E.C.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** The six-name roll of this lesson holds…
  a) Lema, Asefa, Aster, Bahru, Maritu, Asefu b) Only Aster Aweke c) Six foreign singers
**Answer: a) Lema, Asefa, Aster, Bahru, Maritu, Asefu** — the book's §3.1.1 company, kept whole.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** Maritu Legesse and Asefu Debalqe are placed here as…
  a) Named musicians of the Amhara region b) Invented examples c) Instrument makers only
**Answer: a) Named musicians of the Amhara region** — named in the book's lives — never embellished beyond it.
