# Musicians of the Amhara Region — short answer

Answer in a short sentence or a short list.


**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Where was Lema born, and where did his stage road lead?
**Answer:** Born Koremash, North Shewa; the road led to the Addis Ababa municipality stage (1944 E.C.) and the National Theatre.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is new in Aster Aweke's style?
**Answer:** She blends Ethiopian musical style with Western method into a new style, holding international festival awards.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Why keep the six names as one roll?
**Answer:** Because the book keeps the region's history as a company of named lives; the roll, kept whole, is the history.
