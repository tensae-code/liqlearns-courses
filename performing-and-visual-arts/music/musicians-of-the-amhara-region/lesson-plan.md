# Lesson plan — Musicians of the Amhara Region

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Music · **Lesson:** Musicians of the Amhara Region
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 3 — Music (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** six named lives from the Grade 7 book — Lema Gebre Hiwot, Asefa Abate, Aster Aweke, Bahru Kanie, Maritu Legesse and Asefu Debalqe — the region's music told through its musicians.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Amhara Music History — the road; here are the people who walked it.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Lema Gebre Hiwot and Asefa Abate** — Lema (1918–1987 E.C.): Koremash child artist, shilela/fukera patriot-rouser, bodyguard cavalryman, National Theatre; Asefa Abate, the named azmari.
2. **Aster Aweke and Bahru Kanie** — Aster (b. 1959 E.C.): Hamusit, South Gondar; Hager Fikir Theatre; America and computer science; a blended new style with international awards. Bahru Kanie in the azmari roll.
3. **Maritu Legesse and Asefu Debalqe** — the roll completed: Maritu Legesse and Asefu Debalqe, named musicians of the region in the book's §3.1.1 lives.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Amhara Music History. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Tell Lema Gebre Hiwot's life-line: child artist, patriot-rouser, bodyguard, national stage.
2. Tell Aster Aweke's road: Gondar childhood, Hager Fikir, America, a new blended style.
3. Name Bahru Kanie, Maritu Legesse and Asefu Debalqe with one true fact each.

## What you need
Only memory and respect — lives are told, never embellished beyond the book.
