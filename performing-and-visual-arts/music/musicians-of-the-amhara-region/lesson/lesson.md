# Musicians of the Amhara Region

## Do it first

History in the last lesson was venues. Here it is faces. A child who roused patriots with chant; a girl from Gondar who carried the region's voice across an ocean; singers whose names whole towns still know. Say their names first. Then learn their roads.

## Sub lesson 1 — Lema Gebre Hiwot and Asefa Abate

**Lema Gebre Hiwot (1918–1987 E.C.)** was born at **Koremash, North Shewa**, an artist from age 7 — **mesenqo, washint, shilela and fukera as a child**. During the Italian invasion he **roused patriots by shilela and fukera**; he served as a child soldier in the British Army, then **five years in the Imperial Bodyguard cavalry, never parting from chant and mesenqo**. His “አካሌ ውቤ” (Akale Wube) reached the Addis Ababa municipality stage in 1944 E.C., and the **National Theatre** followed. Beside him the book sets **Asefa Abate**, named first in its azmari roll — the tej-house master's name heading the trade's honour list.

## Sub lesson 2 — Aster Aweke and Bahru Kanie

**Aster Aweke (born 1959 E.C.)** was born at **Hamusit, South Gondar** — a bright student with many prizes who **entered music humming as a child**. Her first employment was at **Hager Fikir Theatre**, then private bands; after travelling in 1981 E.C. she **studied computer science at a community college in Northern Virginia, USA**. Her art **blends Ethiopian musical style with Western method into a new style**, and holds **international festival awards**. Beside her stands **Bahru Kanie**, named in the book's azmari roll — the traditional master's name on the same honour list as the modern star's: the book's point exactly.

## Sub lesson 3 — Maritu Legesse and Asefu Debalqe

The book's lives close with **Maritu Legesse** and **Asefu Debalqe** — named musicians of the Amhara region in the same §3.1.1 honour roll, their names carried with the singers and azmari above so the region's music is remembered as a company, not a solo. Learn the six names as one roll — **Lema Gebre Hiwot, Asefa Abate, Aster Aweke, Bahru Kanie, Maritu Legesse, Asefu Debalqe** — for that roll, kept whole, is how the book itself keeps the region's history.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion Lema's life in five beats; let them answer with Aster's road in five beats.

## Do It Yourself

1. Write the six-name roll from memory, twice.
2. Choose one musician and write their road as five short lines.
