# Fiji's Lali and Derua — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The lali is…
a) a wooden slit drum  b) a bamboo tube  c) a string instrument
**Answer: a) a wooden slit drum — Fiji's slit drum.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The derua is made of…
a) bamboo  b) brass  c) clay
**Answer: a) bamboo — a bamboo stamping tube.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The derua makes its sound by…
a) stamping  b) bowing  c) blowing
**Answer: a) stamping — stamped against the ground.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The lali and derua belong to which family?
a) percussion  b) woodwind  c) brass
**Answer: a) percussion.**

**5. (Sub lesson 1)** Fiji's instruments are recognised by…
a) picture and sound  b) price  c) colour only
**Answer: a) picture and sound.**
