# Fiji's Lali and Derua — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the lali?
**Answer: Fiji's wooden slit drum.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How does the derua make its beat?
**Answer: Bamboo tubes are stamped against the ground.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Name Fiji's two instruments in this lesson.
**Answer: The lali and the derua.**
