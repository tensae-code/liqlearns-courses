# Fiji's Lali and Derua — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The lali is a slit drum made of wood.
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The derua is a brass instrument.
**Answer: False — it is a bamboo stamping tube.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Both instruments are learned by picture and sound.
**Answer: True.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The derua is played with a bow.
**Answer: False — it is stamped.**
