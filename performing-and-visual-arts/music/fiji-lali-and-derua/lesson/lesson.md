# Fiji's Lali and Derua

## Do it first

Quick start: from the title alone, guess what this lesson's work is. Then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — The lali — Fiji's slit drum

The **lali** is Fiji's wooden **slit drum**. In the Year 1 percussion family it is met by **picture and by sound** — you learn to recognise the lali when you see it and when you hear it.

## Sub lesson 2 — The derua — bamboo stamping tubes

The **derua** is Fiji's **bamboo stamping tube** — bamboo stamped against the ground to make its beat. Like the lali, it is learned by picture and by sound, inside the percussion family.
