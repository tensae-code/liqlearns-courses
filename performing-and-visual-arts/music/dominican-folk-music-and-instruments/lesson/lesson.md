# Dominican Folk Music and Instruments

## Do it first

Quick start: Scrape the güira. Beat the tambora. Squeeze the acordeón. Then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — The Dominican folk instruments

The **principal Dominican instruments** are the **güira, the tambora and the acordeón**, used in **bachata, merengue, mangulina and Perico Ripiao**. The Grade 10 book researches the instruments of Dominican rhythms and their characteristic sounds, and meets the **Dominican percussionists** — **Guarionex Aquino, David Almengod, Fellé Vega, Isidro Bobadilla and Dante Cucurullo** — with their **experimental exploration of new instruments and sonorities**; then the learners **fabricate Dominican instruments** (the tambora among them) and film themselves playing them.

## Sub lesson 2 — The folk rhythms and their inventory

**Folk music** is **traditional music, passed from generation to generation orally, with no known authors**, born of the island's **Taíno, Spanish and African mix**. The Grade 6 book makes an **inventory and classification of Dominican folk rhythms** — **merengue, mangulina, carabiné, palos, yuca, bambulá, bachata, perico ripiao, pambiche, atabales** — listening to and identifying each, because **knowing the creole rhythms strengthens Dominican identity**. Grade 10 takes the same music as **cultural ancestry**: knowing the traditions and dances of my region, recognising and valuing them.

## Sub lesson 3 — Playing the rhythms

Folk music is learned **by playing it**. The Grade 6 book researches **how the güira and tambora are played** in traditional and folk merengue and **practises their rhythmic patterns until the rhythm is not lost**; the Grade 7 book plays **merengue's rhythmic patterns with two pencil-shaped sticks — palillos** — first following videos and clapping, then reproducing the patterns and inventing variants. Grade 10 **assembles a percussion set to play at bachata's rhythm**, learning the **bongó and güira patterns** from reference videos.

## Sub lesson 4 — Merengue as composition

Merengue is also **composition**. The Grade 10 book studies **Dominican merengue as a musical composition** through the **experts' lists of the best merengue singers** — the genre's canon, the singers by whom the composed tradition is known.
