# Dominican Folk Music and Instruments — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** From which three peoples' mix was the island's folk music born?
**Answer: Taíno, Spanish and African.**

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Which two instruments' patterns are practised for bachata?
**Answer: The bongó and the güira.**

**3. (Sub lesson 4)** How is the merengue canon studied in Grade 10?
**Answer: Through the experts' lists of the best merengue singers.**
