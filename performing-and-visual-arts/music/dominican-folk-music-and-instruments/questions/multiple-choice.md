# Dominican Folk Music and Instruments — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The principal Dominican instruments named first are…
a) güira, tambora and acordeón  b) piano, violin and flute  c) guitar only
**Answer: a) güira, tambora and acordeón**.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** One named Dominican percussionist is…
a) Fellé Vega  b) a European composer  c) a dancer
**Answer: a) Fellé Vega**.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Folk music is…
a) traditional music passed from generation to generation, with no known authors  b) music written last year  c) music only for radio
**Answer: a) traditional music passed from generation to generation, with no known authors**.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The inventory names…
a) merengue, mangulina, carabiné, palos, bachata and more  b) only merengue  c) only bachata
**Answer: a) merengue, mangulina, carabiné, palos, bachata and more**.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Merengue's patterns can be practised with…
a) two pencil-shaped sticks (palillos)  b) a piano only  c) silence
**Answer: a) two pencil-shaped sticks (palillos)**.
