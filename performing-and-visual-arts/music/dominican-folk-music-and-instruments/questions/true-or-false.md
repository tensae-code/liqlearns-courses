# Dominican Folk Music and Instruments — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The güira and tambora are played in traditional merengue.
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Folk rhythms are classified in an inventory the learners make.
**Answer: True.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The tambora's patterns must be practised until the rhythm is not lost.
**Answer: True.**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Merengue exists only as dance, never as composition.
**Answer: False.**
