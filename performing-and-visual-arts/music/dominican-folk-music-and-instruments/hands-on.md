# Hands-on — Dominican Folk Music and Instruments

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Palillos patterns
With two pencil-shaped sticks on a table, follow a merengue pattern: copy it, repeat it, then invent one variant, as the Grade 7 book does.

## Rhythm inventory
Write the inventory: merengue, mangulina, carabiné, palos, yuca, bambulá, bachata, perico ripiao, pambiche, atabales — and tick the ones you have heard.
