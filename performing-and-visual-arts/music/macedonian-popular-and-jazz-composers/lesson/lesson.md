# Macedonian Popular and Jazz Composers

## Do it first

Name one thing that belongs in this lesson — North Macedonia's popular and jazz composers — Špato, Brandjolica, Džambazov, Kostov, Pejovski and Glišić. Say what it is in your own words. If you can name one, you are already inside the lesson.

## Sub lesson 1 — the parallel line of popular and jazz composers

the parallel line of popular and jazz composers: Dragan Ǵakonovski-Špato, Ljubomir Brandjolica, Aleksandar Džambazov, Kire Kostov, Ilija Pejovski and Živojin Glišić — big bands, the MRT Dance Orchestra, festival songs and hundreds of arrangements joining Macedonian folklore to jazz harmony and improvisation.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson holds, part by part, in your own words. Companion checks: did every part get named, with its Macedonian name kept where the book gives one?

## Do It Yourself

1. Choose one named part of this lesson and find out one more true thing about it — from a person who knows it, or from the book your part came from.
2. Make or perform a small piece in the way of that part: a rhythm, a step, a drawing, a shaped piece of clay.
3. Write one sentence: what does this part show about North Macedonia's own way of teaching its arts?
