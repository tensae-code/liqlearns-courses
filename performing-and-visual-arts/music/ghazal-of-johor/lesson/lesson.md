# Ghazal of Johor

## Do it first

Name one thing that belongs in this lesson — Johor's ghazal — the Malay sung-poetry tradition of Johor, heard and appreciated as a regional art. Say what it is in your own words. If you can name one, you are already inside the lesson.

## Sub lesson 1 — Malay ghazal of Johor

Unit 3 Apresiasi: Ghazal — Malay ghazal of Johor: a syncretic ensemble of harmonium, tabla, gambus, guitar, violin, maracas and tambourine; songs of love, advice and community; played at official ceremonies and weddings.

## Do It Yourself

1. Choose one named part of this lesson and find out one more true thing about it — from a person who knows it, or from the book your part came from.
2. Make or perform a small piece in the way of that part: a rhythm, a step, a drawing, a shaped piece of clay.
3. Write one sentence: what does this part show about Malaysia's own way of teaching its arts?
