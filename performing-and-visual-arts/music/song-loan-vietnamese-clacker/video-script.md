This lesson is Song Loan: The Vietnamese Clacker.

The song loan — Vietnam's clacker percussion instrument that keeps the beat. — as Vietnam's school books teach it: The song loan — Vietnam's wooden percussion, a round flat wooden body whose stem the hand works to knock out a crisp, far-carrying sound; striking rhythm patterns in two answering groups and accompanying the magpie-robin song.

Watch, listen — then say it back in your own words.
