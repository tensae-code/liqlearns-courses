This lesson is Zhyr: The Kazakh Heroic Epic.

Zhyr: The Kazakh Heroic Epic — The zhyr — Kazakhstan's heroic epic, 'the pearl left from the ancestors', sung by the zhyrau. — as Kazakhstan's school books teach it: zhyr, the heroic epic sung to dombra or kobyz (Greeks say "epos", Kyrgyz "yr"): the batyrs Alpamys, Kobylandy, Er Targyn, Kambar; an excerpt of «Алпамыс батыр» heard and its 7–8-syllable zhyr measure found.

Watch, listen — then say it back in your own words.
