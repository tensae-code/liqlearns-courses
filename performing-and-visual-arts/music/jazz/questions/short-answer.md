# Jazz — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name two musics born where cultures mixed in New Orleans' world.
**Answer: Any two of: blues, New Orleans jazz, tango, rumba, samba.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name an all-women big band.
**Answer: Ina Ray Hutton's Melodears, or the International Sweethearts of Rhythm.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which instrument carries the bass in the Dixieland example?
**Answer: The tuba.**
