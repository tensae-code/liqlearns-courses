# Jazz — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Jazz was born in…
a) multicultural New Orleans  b) Vienna  c) Reykjavík only
**Answer: a) multicultural New Orleans**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Dixieland is…
a) the white musicians' version of New Orleans jazz  b) a sea song  c) a waltz
**Answer: a) the white musicians' version of New Orleans jazz**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** In Sing On you hear the clarinet and the…
a) tuba bass  b) harp only  c) organ only
**Answer: a) tuba bass**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Tiger Rag is by the…
a) Original Dixieland Jazz Band  b) Comedian Harmonists  c) M.A. Quartet
**Answer: a) Original Dixieland Jazz Band**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Mary Lou Williams was a…
a) pianist, arranger and composer  b) sailor  c) painter
**Answer: a) pianist, arranger and composer**
