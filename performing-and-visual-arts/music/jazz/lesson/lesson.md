# Jazz

## Do it first

Quick start: say one thing you already know — a tune, a dance, a name — that belongs to this lesson's world. Keep it in mind; the parts will meet it.

## Sub lesson 1 — The birth of jazz in New Orleans

Jazz could **only be born in multicultural New Orleans**: where unlike instruments and musics were mixed in new ways came the **blues, New Orleans jazz**, the Argentine **tango**, Havana's **rumba** and Rio's **samba**.

## Sub lesson 2 — Dixieland

**Dixieland** is the white musicians' version of New Orleans jazz. Hear the **clarinet's role** and the **tuba bass** in Sing On, and the **banjo** in the Original Dixieland Jazz Band's **Tiger Rag**.

## Sub lesson 3 — Women in jazz

Women stood at jazz's centre: the **all-women big bands** — Ina Ray Hutton's Melodears, the International Sweethearts of Rhythm and others — and **Mary Lou Williams**, pianist, arranger and composer.
