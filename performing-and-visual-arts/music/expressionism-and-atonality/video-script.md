**Expressionism and Arnold Schoenberg (1874–1951).** Expressionism and Arnold Schoenberg (1874–1951): music of inner tension that abandons tonality — atonality, the twelve-tone technique (dodecaphony) treating all twelve semitones equally, serialism, and Sprechgesang, speech-song ("Pierrot Lunaire"); the Second Viennese School with Berg and Webern.

That is Expressionism and Atonality — North Macedonia's way, kept by name.
