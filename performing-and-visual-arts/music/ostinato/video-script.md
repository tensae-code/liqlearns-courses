# Video script — Ostinato

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

This lesson is Ostinato.

The ostinato — the pattern that persists, repeating under the music while everything else changes. — as Greece's school books teach it: composing melodic and rhythmic ostinati over given melodies and performing them in layers; recognising ostinato by ear and by eye.

Watch, listen — then say it back in your own words.
