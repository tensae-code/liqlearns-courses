This lesson is Estrada: Pop Stage Music.

Estrada: Pop Stage Music — Estrada pop music — Kazakhstan's estrada and its stars, and estrada around the world. — as Kazakhstan's school books teach it: what it teaches: estrada and pop — «Подмосковные вечера», in the Guinness book as the most performed song; American pop and soul (Sinatra, Houston, Madonna); French chanson (Piaf, Aznavour, Brassens, Brel); disco (ABBA, Boney M, Bee Gees); rock basics; Michael Jackson and «We Are the World», the song that united the world to help the hungry.

Watch, listen — then say it back in your own words.
