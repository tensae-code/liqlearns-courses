## Sub lesson 1 — Kazakhstan Grade 6 Music (Musakhajaeva)

what it teaches: estrada and pop — «Подмосковные вечера», in the Guinness book as the most performed song; American pop and soul (Sinatra, Houston, Madonna); French chanson (Piaf, Aznavour, Brassens, Brel); disco (ABBA, Boney M, Bee Gees); rock basics; Michael Jackson and «We Are the World», the song that united the world to help the hungry; what it teaches: Kazakh estrada from satirical aitys artists to Gulzhikhan Galieva's 1965 estrada-circus studio; its composers (Baikadamov, Espaev, Kaldayakov, Tlendiev, Khasangaliev); Roza Baglanova, who sang the songs of many peoples and sang at the front in the war; Roza Rymbaeva, Golden Orpheus winner 1977; Q-pop and hip-hop
