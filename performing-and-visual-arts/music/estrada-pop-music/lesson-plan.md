# Lesson plan — Estrada: Pop Stage Music

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Music · **Lesson:** Estrada: Pop Stage Music
**Place in the curriculum:** Music (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Estrada pop music — Kazakhstan's estrada and its stars, and estrada around the world..
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this course in the same title.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Kazakhstan Grade 6 Music (Musakhajaeva)** — what it teaches: estrada and pop — «Подмосковные вечера», in the Guinness book as the most performed song; American pop and soul (Sinatra, Houston, Madonna); French chanson (Piaf, Aznavour, Brassens, Brel); disco (ABBA, Boney M, Bee Gees); rock basics; Michael Jackson and «We Are the World», the song that united the world to help the hungry; what it teaches: Kazakh estrada from satirical aitys artists to Gulzhikhan Galieva's 1965 estrada-circus studio; its composers (Baikadamov, Espaev, Kaldayakov, Tlendiev, Khasangaliev); Roza Baglanova, who sang the songs of many peoples and sang at the front in the war; Roza Rymbaeva, Golden Orpheus winner 1977; Q-pop and hip-hop
