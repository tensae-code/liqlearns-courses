## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Carl Orff (1895–1982)** — Carl Orff (1895–1982): the Orff-Schulwerk, an instrumentarium of simple melodic and percussion instruments joining music, word and movement in children's music-making, used in schools worldwide — and the scenic cantata "Carmina Burana" (1936) on medieval texts, all pounding rhythm and choral power.
2. **Карл Орф (1895–1982)** — Карл Орф (1895–1982) — Carl Orff: the Munich boy who staged puppet plays with thunder from the kitchen stove; "Carmina Burana", first of the Trionfi trilogy on Latin texts; and the Orff Schulwerk, his work with children — music and movement joined, awakening the child's own music-making with simple string instruments.
3. **Карл Орф** — Карл Орф — Orff's elemental music: with Mary Wigman and, from 1924, Dorothee Günther's school for gymnastics, dance and music — music, movement and speech are not divided but form one whole; the child, like early man, makes music naively from natural rhythm and movement.
4. **Поделба на детски музички инструменти** — Поделба на детски музички инструменти — the children's instruments, the "Orff instrumentarium": the rhythmic set (rattles, triangle, cymbals, sticks, dajre, little drum) and the melodic set (xylophone, metallophone, bells, block flute) — mostly percussion, so they sound best wedded to singing.
5. **Свечен марш / Детска игра** — Свечен марш / Детска игра — playing the children's instrumentarium: the "Solemn March" (unknown author) and Orff's own "Children's Play" in full score for the class ensemble.
6. **Блок флејта** — Блок флејта — the block flute (recorder): a wooden woodwind in two or three parts, six or seven holes in front and the thumb hole behind; the medieval flute the classical flute displaced, revived about 1910 for playing old music on its own instruments, and taken into schools across England, Germany and Switzerland — played in ensembles with the Orff instrumentarium.
7. **Блок флејта** — Блок флејта — the recorder's fingerings, hole by hole: close all seven holes and the eighth below for DO, then lift finger by finger — RE, MI, FA, SOL, LA, SI and the high DO2.
