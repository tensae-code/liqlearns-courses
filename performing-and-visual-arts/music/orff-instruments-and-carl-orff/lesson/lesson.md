# Orff Instruments and Carl Orff

## Do it first

Name one thing that belongs in this lesson — Carl Orff and his Schulwerk — the children's melodic and percussion instrumentarium joining music, word and movement, and Carmina Burana. Say what it is in your own words. If you can name one, you are already inside the lesson.

## Sub lesson 1 — Carl Orff (1895–1982)

Carl Orff (1895–1982): the Orff-Schulwerk, an instrumentarium of simple melodic and percussion instruments joining music, word and movement in children's music-making, used in schools worldwide — and the scenic cantata "Carmina Burana" (1936) on medieval texts, all pounding rhythm and choral power.

## Sub lesson 2 — Карл Орф (1895–1982)

Карл Орф (1895–1982) — Carl Orff: the Munich boy who staged puppet plays with thunder from the kitchen stove; "Carmina Burana", first of the Trionfi trilogy on Latin texts; and the Orff Schulwerk, his work with children — music and movement joined, awakening the child's own music-making with simple string instruments.

## Sub lesson 3 — Карл Орф

Карл Орф — Orff's elemental music: with Mary Wigman and, from 1924, Dorothee Günther's school for gymnastics, dance and music — music, movement and speech are not divided but form one whole; the child, like early man, makes music naively from natural rhythm and movement.

## Sub lesson 4 — Поделба на детски музички инструменти

Поделба на детски музички инструменти — the children's instruments, the "Orff instrumentarium": the rhythmic set (rattles, triangle, cymbals, sticks, dajre, little drum) and the melodic set (xylophone, metallophone, bells, block flute) — mostly percussion, so they sound best wedded to singing.

## Sub lesson 5 — Свечен марш / Детска игра

Свечен марш / Детска игра — playing the children's instrumentarium: the "Solemn March" (unknown author) and Orff's own "Children's Play" in full score for the class ensemble.

## Sub lesson 6 — Блок флејта

Блок флејта — the block flute (recorder): a wooden woodwind in two or three parts, six or seven holes in front and the thumb hole behind; the medieval flute the classical flute displaced, revived about 1910 for playing old music on its own instruments, and taken into schools across England, Germany and Switzerland — played in ensembles with the Orff instrumentarium.

## Sub lesson 7 — Блок флејта

Блок флејта — the recorder's fingerings, hole by hole: close all seven holes and the eighth below for DO, then lift finger by finger — RE, MI, FA, SOL, LA, SI and the high DO2.
