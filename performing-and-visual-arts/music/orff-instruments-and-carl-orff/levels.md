# Orff Instruments and Carl Orff — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — Carl Orff (1895–1982)
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Carl Orff (1895–1982): the Orff-Schulwerk, an instrumentarium of simple melodic and percussion instruments joining music, word and movement in children's music-making, used in schools worldwide — and the scenic cantata "Carmina Burana" (1936) on medieval texts, all pounding rhythm and choral power.

## Sub lesson 2 — Карл Орф (1895–1982)
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Карл Орф (1895–1982) — Carl Orff: the Munich boy who staged puppet plays with thunder from the kitchen stove; "Carmina Burana", first of the Trionfi trilogy on Latin texts; and the Orff Schulwerk, his work with children — music and movement joined, awakening the child's own music-making with simple string instruments.

## Sub lesson 3 — Карл Орф
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Карл Орф — Orff's elemental music: with Mary Wigman and, from 1924, Dorothee Günther's school for gymnastics, dance and music — music, movement and speech are not divided but form one whole; the child, like early man, makes music naively from natural rhythm and movement.

## Sub lesson 4 — Поделба на детски музички инструменти
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Поделба на детски музички инструменти — the children's instruments, the "Orff instrumentarium": the rhythmic set (rattles, triangle, cymbals, sticks, dajre, little drum) and the melodic set (xylophone, metallophone, bells, block flute) — mostly percussion, so they sound best wedded to singing.

## Sub lesson 5 — Свечен марш / Детска игра
Sub lesson 5 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Свечен марш / Детска игра — playing the children's instrumentarium: the "Solemn March" (unknown author) and Orff's own "Children's Play" in full score for the class ensemble.

## Sub lesson 6 — Блок флејта
Sub lesson 6 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Блок флејта — the block flute (recorder): a wooden woodwind in two or three parts, six or seven holes in front and the thumb hole behind; the medieval flute the classical flute displaced, revived about 1910 for playing old music on its own instruments, and taken into schools across England, Germany and Switzerland — played in ensembles with the Orff instrumentarium.

## Sub lesson 7 — Блок флејта
Sub lesson 7 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Блок флејта — the recorder's fingerings, hole by hole: close all seven holes and the eighth below for DO, then lift finger by finger — RE, MI, FA, SOL, LA, SI and the high DO2.
