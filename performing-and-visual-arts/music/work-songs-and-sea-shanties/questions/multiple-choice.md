# Work Songs and Sea Shanties — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A work song is…
a) a song sung by people while they work  b) a song only sung in concerts  c) a song with no words
**Answer: a) a song sung by people while they work.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Work songs sung by sailors at sea are called…
a) gospel  b) sea shanties  c) reggae
**Answer: b) sea shanties.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** "Day-O" is a work song from…
a) Ireland  b) Jamaica  c) the USA
**Answer: b) Jamaica.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** "Day-O" was sung by…
a) night-shift banana loaders  b) church choirs  c) jazz bands
**Answer: a) night-shift banana loaders.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** "Day-O" is sung in…
a) Jamaican Creole  b) French  c) Greek
**Answer: a) Jamaican Creole.**
