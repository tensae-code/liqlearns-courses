# Work Songs and Sea Shanties — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A work song is sung while the work is being done.
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** "Day-O" is a Jamaican work song.
**Answer: True.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** "Day-O" was sung by day-shift office workers.
**Answer: False** — it was sung by night-shift banana loaders.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson writes out the lyrics of "Day-O".
**Answer: False** — the song is filed by summary only; no lyrics are transcribed.
