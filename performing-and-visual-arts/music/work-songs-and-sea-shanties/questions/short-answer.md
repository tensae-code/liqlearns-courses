# Work Songs and Sea Shanties — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is a work song?
**Answer: A song sung by people while they work.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What work was "Day-O" sung at?
**Answer: Loading bananas at night — it is the banana-boat song of the night-shift banana loaders.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the Cyprus class do with "Day-O"'s language?
**Answer: It decodes it — works out what the Jamaican Creole words mean.**
