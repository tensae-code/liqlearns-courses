# Lesson plan — Work Songs and Sea Shanties

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Music · **Lesson:** Work Songs and Sea Shanties
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 3 — Music (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** a work song is a song sung by people while they work — taught through "Day-O", the Jamaican banana-boat song of the night-shift banana loaders, as Cyprus's Year 6 unit files it (teacher unit material — evidence; song filed by summary only, no lyrics).
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the songs lessons; this lesson is labelled teacher unit material — evidence throughout, because its Cyprus source is a teacher's unit plan, not a student textbook.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Work songs: singing to carry the work** — songs people sing while they work; work songs sung at sea are the sea shanties of this lesson's title.
2. **"Day-O", the banana-boat song** — a Jamaican work song sung by night-shift banana loaders, in Jamaican Creole, with the class decoding its language (song filed by summary only, no lyrics).

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Kinds of Traditional Music (its work songs). Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say what a work song is.
2. Tell what "Day-O" is: whose song it is, what work it was sung at, and what language it is sung in.

## What you need
Your voice and a friend to answer you.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — hum a steady rhythm as if carrying heavy work, and feel how the song helps the work (lesson/lesson.md).
2. **The explanation** — work songs, then "Day-O" (lesson/lesson.md).
   - ADD IMAGE: a picture of night-shift workers loading bananas onto a boat — the student says what work the song was sung at.
3. **Say it to your companion** — tell "Day-O" by its workers, its work and its language (lesson/lesson.md).
   - ADD VIDEO: the script in video-script.md, filmed — made later, when videos are made.
4. **Questions** — from questions/: multiple-choice, true-or-false, short-answer (levels.md).
5. **Hands-on** — hands-on.md.
6. **Do It Yourself** — name a work song from your own place (lesson/lesson.md).

## Add-ons for this lesson (the full list)
