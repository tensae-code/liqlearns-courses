# Video script — Work Songs and Sea Shanties

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

People sing while they work. The song carries the work.
That is a **work song**.

Here is a famous one: **"Day-O" — the banana-boat song.**
Jamaica. Night time. Workers loading bananas onto a boat — singing as they work.
The song is in **Jamaican Creole** — and the class works out what its words mean.

Work has a song. Listen for it.
