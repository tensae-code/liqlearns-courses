# Hands-on — Work Songs and Sea Shanties

The lesson with your hands and your voice, not only your eyes.

## A rhythm for the work
Pick a pretend job — carrying, sweeping, rowing. Move in a steady rhythm for that job and hum or chant a short line of your own making that fits the movement. The song must help the movement stay steady. Then swap jobs.

## Decode a line
With a companion, take one line of a song in a language variety you do not speak every day — "Day-O" is in Jamaican Creole — and work out together what it must mean from the words you can recognise and the work it was sung at. No lyrics of "Day-O" are written here: the song is filed by summary only.
