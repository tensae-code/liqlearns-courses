This lesson is Sáo Trúc: The Vietnamese Bamboo Flute.

The sáo trúc, Vietnam's transverse bamboo flute. — as Vietnam's school books teach it: The sáo trúc (bamboo flute) — a breath-blown instrument very common in daily life, its image always tied to the peaceful Vietnamese village; two kinds, the end-blown (sáo dọc) and the transverse (sáo ngang), made from bamboo or cane tubes; the transverse flute has one end stopped by a bamboo node beside the blow hole and many finger holes, a clear, bright sound able to express many musical characters; it performs solo and in ensembles and accompanies singing and poetry reciting.

Watch, listen — then say it back in your own words.
