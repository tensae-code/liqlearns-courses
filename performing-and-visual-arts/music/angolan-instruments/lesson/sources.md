Angola's own way: music starts from the voice and the body, names Angola's own instruments in their own languages (ngoma, dikanza, kisanji, hungu), and teaches the history of Angolan children's music itself. Grades 3 and 4 (student/teacher: STUDENT) — one combined file covering the grade-03 and grade-04 records, MD5-verified identical across its two grade records and placed once, both editions named. PDF pages: 56 (cover/title page verified against the record title). Verdict: COMPLETE (Grade 3 part printed pp. 6–33; Grade 4 part printed pp. 37–53). Angola's own way: Angola's traditional instruments are classified in the Hornbostel way (aerophones, membranophones, idiophones) and each is named in its own language with its ceremonial use — mpungi trumpets for enthroning chiefs, bavugu rattles tied on dancers' legs, the puíta friction drum.
