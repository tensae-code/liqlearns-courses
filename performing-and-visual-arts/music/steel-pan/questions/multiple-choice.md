# The Steel Pan — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** On the steel pan, pupils first play…
a) simple tunes  b) concertos  c) nothing — listening only
**Answer: a) simple tunes** — playing simple tunes is the set performance work.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A steel band group is…
a) one pupil alone  b) pupils playing together as a band  c) a listening club
**Answer: b) pupils playing together as a band**.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Your part in the band should be…
a) played only at the start  b) kept steady inside the group's playing  c) louder than everyone
**Answer: b) kept steady inside the group's playing**.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** The steel pan work stands in the syllabus as…
a) performance  b) drawing  c) story time
**Answer: a) performance** — Scope & Sequence performance, and Classes 3–4 groups.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Which classes' groups does the placement name for steel band work?
a) Classes 3–4  b) Class 1 only  c) no classes
**Answer: a) Classes 3–4**.
