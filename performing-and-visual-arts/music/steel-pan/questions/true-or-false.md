# The Steel Pan — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Pupils play the steel pan themselves in the Barbados syllabus.
**Answer: True.** — printed pp. 2, 16, 21.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Steel pan playing is always solo.
**Answer: False.** — pupils take part in a steel band group.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** First pan work is simple tunes.
**Answer: True.**.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The band group work is named for Classes 3–4.
**Answer: True.**.
