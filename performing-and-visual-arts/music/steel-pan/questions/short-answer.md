# The Steel Pan — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What do pupils play on the steel pan first?
**Answer: Simple tunes.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What group does the pan player take part in?
**Answer: A steel band group.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Is the pan taught by listening alone in this syllabus?
**Answer: No — pupils play simple tunes on it themselves.**
