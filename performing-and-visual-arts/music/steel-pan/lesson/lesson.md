## Sub lesson 1 — First tunes on the steel pan

The syllabus's performance work sets **playing simple tunes on the steel pan** — first tunes, played for real, as the pupil's own performance.

## Sub lesson 2 — Playing in a steel band group

The pan is played together: pupils **take part in a steel band group** — your tune inside the band's tune, in the Classes 3–4 groups.
