# Video script — Graphic Notation

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Music does not have to start with round notes.

A **symbol** can stand for a **sound**. A blank square — silence.
Read the grid. Now fill your own — and play your friend's!

A rising line — the sound rises. Dots — short sounds, high and low.

Clap, snap, stamp, slap — your body is a score too.
That is **graphic notation** — writing music in pictures.
