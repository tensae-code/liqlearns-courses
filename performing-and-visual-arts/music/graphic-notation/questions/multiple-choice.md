# Graphic Notation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In a symbol grid, a blank square means…
a) a louder sound  b) silence — a rest  c) a faster tempo
**Answer: b) silence — a rest — blanks are rests in the grid.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A rising drawn line as a pitch score means…
a) the sound stays flat  b) the sound falls  c) a continuous sound rising
**Answer: c) a continuous sound rising.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** In the body-percussion chart, the numbers 1–4 stand for…
a) clap, snap, stamp, slap  b) four instruments  c) four songs
**Answer: a) clap, snap, stamp, slap.**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** "Lub-Dab-Rest" is built on the pattern…
a) clap-clap-rest (ta-ta-sa)  b) stamp-stamp-stamp  c) one long note
**Answer: a) clap-clap-rest (ta-ta-sa) — the heartbeat pattern.**

**5. (Sub lesson 4)** In a box score, a blank box means…
a) play louder  b) rest — do not play  c) change instrument
**Answer: b) rest — do not play that beat.**
