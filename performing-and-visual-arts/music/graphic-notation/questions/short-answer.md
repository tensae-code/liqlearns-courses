# Graphic Notation — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What do pupils do after filling a blank symbol grid with their own symbols?
**Answer: They play each other's compositions.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What happens to tempo and pitch in story improvisation?
**Answer: They are varied to depict each character or situation.**

**3. (Sub lesson 4)** How are the instruments sorted for the Percussion Jam Band?
**Answer: Into high, medium and low pitched sounds.**
