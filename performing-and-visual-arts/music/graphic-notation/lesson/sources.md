Невмалық нота (Neumatic notation) — what it teaches: in the Middle Ages a new type of musical notation called «невмалық нота» (neumatic notation) appeared; the word «невма» comes from the Greek word «пневманың» — neumes are written together with the song's words (лирикалық композиция): these symbols show the melody's going up and down, the sounds' pitch and length; neumatic notation was widely used in Europe; all musical notation was so; in neumatic notation the musical composition's peculiarities were shown with signs and symbols showing the sounds' pitch and duration; so neumes' development brought modern musical symbols' appearance; analyse: comparing the neumatic notation's parts, understand the lyric composition's parts; listen and perform: listen to one of the Kazakh people's lyric songs — from the song find the sounds' duration and pitch, define your own notation, draw the neumatic notation; the modern type of musical notation (музыкалық нотаның қазіргі түрдегі ноталық стан) — the ноталық стан (staff) is a set of five lines placed above or beside each other; notes are written on this staff in parallel; the notes' heads are written in oval form, and according to their placement and order they are read in this order; repeat the material about musical notation from previous lessons (student book; original target `music/graphic-notation-and-music-writing`) | Book 8, printed p. 51 |
