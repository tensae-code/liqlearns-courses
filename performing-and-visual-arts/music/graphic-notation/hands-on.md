# Hands-on — Graphic Notation

The lesson with your hands and your body, not only your eyes.

## Your own symbol grid
Draw a 4×4 grid. Fill it with your own symbols for sounds — claps, stamps, shakers, voice sounds — leaving some squares blank for rests. Swap grids with a partner and play each other's compositions.

## Box-score jam
Sort the instruments you have into high, medium and low. Write an eight-box score for each group — coloured box = play, blank = rest — and layer the parts together.
