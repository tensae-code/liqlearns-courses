# Hands-on — Dikir Barat

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Watch for the marks
Watch a dikir barat performance. Count the moments the hands move as one, and raise your own hand each time you hear the shouted "Hah!".

## Create and perform
In a group, invent hand movements for a folksong you know, seated in dikir barat's way. Practise until the hands move as one, then perform for another group.
