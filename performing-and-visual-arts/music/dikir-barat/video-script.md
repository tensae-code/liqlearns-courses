# Video script — Dikir Barat

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Sit together. Sing together. Hands move — as one!

Drums beat. Hands fly together. Hah!

That is dikir barat — Singapore's Malay seated group singing.

Now make your OWN hand movements — and perform them together.
