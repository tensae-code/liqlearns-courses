# Dikir Barat

## Do it first

Quick start: from the title alone, guess what this lesson's work is. Then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — Studying a dikir barat performance

The guide's worked example studies **a video performance of dikir barat, Singapore's Malay seated group singing**, for **its drums and its synchronised hand actions and shouted "Hah!"** — a seated choir whose hands move as one and whose shout punctuates the song.

## Sub lesson 2 — Your own hand movements, performed

Then **groups create their own hand movements to the folksong "Geylang, Sipaku Geylang" and perform them** (the song is filed by name and summary only) — the learners take dikir barat's way — seated, together, hands as one — and make it their own.
