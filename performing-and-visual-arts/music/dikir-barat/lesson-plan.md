# Lesson plan — Dikir Barat

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Music · **Lesson:** Dikir Barat
**Place in the curriculum:** Music (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** dikir barat — Singapore's Malay seated group singing — studied from a video performance for its drums and its synchronised hand actions and shouted "Hah!", then groups creating their own hand movements to a folksong and performing them.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this course in the same title.

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks dikir barat is solo singing → it is group singing, seated, with synchronised hand actions — the togetherness is the art.
- Misses the shout → the performance is punctuated by a shouted "Hah!" — watch and listen for it in the video study.
