# Dikir Barat — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What two things is the video performance studied for?
**Answer: Its drums and its synchronised hand actions (and the shouted "Hah!").**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What do the groups create and perform?
**Answer: Their own hand movements to the folksong "Geylang, Sipaku Geylang".**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** How do dikir barat performers sit and sing?
**Answer: Seated, as a group, with hands moving in synchrony.**
