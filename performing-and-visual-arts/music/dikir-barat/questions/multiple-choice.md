# Dikir Barat — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Dikir barat is…
a) seated group singing  b) solo singing standing  c) a drum alone
**Answer: a) seated group singing** — Singapore's Malay seated group singing.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The video performance is studied for its…
a) drums and synchronised hand actions  b) costumes only  c) stage lights
**Answer: a) drums and synchronised hand actions** — and its shouted "Hah!".

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The performers punctuate the song with a shouted…
a) "Hah!"  b) "Hey!"  c) whistle
**Answer: a) "Hah!"**.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Groups then create their own…
a) hand movements to a folksong  b) drums  c) costumes
**Answer: a) hand movements to a folksong** — to "Geylang, Sipaku Geylang", filed by summary only.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Dikir barat performers perform…
a) seated, as a group  b) standing alone  c) marching
**Answer: a) seated, as a group**.
