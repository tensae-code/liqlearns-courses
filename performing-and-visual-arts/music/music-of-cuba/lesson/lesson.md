## Sub lesson 1 — Where Cuban music comes from

The history of Cuba's music and its sung repertoire, from the indigenous areito and its Hispanic and African roots to its concert music, learned through the songs Cuba's books mount in class — the Himno Invasor, the tonada, the guajira and the sones, placed by title and purpose only.
