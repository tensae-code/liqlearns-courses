# Lesson plan — Movement Songs

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Music · **Lesson:** Movement Songs
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 3 — Music (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** songs you sing with your whole body: clapping in time, walking through fields, and riding — song and dance combined in one moving line.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the beat work the learner's body already knows from playing; no reading is needed — only a steady beat and room to move.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved (the gate: README, "Units, Lessons and Sub Lessons (ruled 2026-11-08)"):

1. **Clapping songs** — "እናጨበጭባለን" (We Clap) — hand in hand, steps forward, let go and clap; steps back, let go and clap — a movement song built on a repeated simple beat.
2. **Walking songs** — "ሽርሽር እንሂድ" (Let's Go for a Walk) — rising in the morning, walking on foot, crossing a river and fields, passing among flowers, home before dark.
3. **Riding songs** — "ቼ ቼ ፈረሴ" (Che Che, My Horse) — riding a white horse, a black horse, a brown horse, singing "che che" as one rides; song combined with dance.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: any song lesson — sing a short song through first. The beat work of the instruments lessons helps too. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Sing and move "እናጨበጭባለን" (We Clap): forward, stop, clap; back, stop, clap, in time.
2. Sing "ሽርሽር እንሂድ" (Let's Go for a Walk) and tell its journey in order.
3. Sing "ቼ ቼ ፈረሴ" (Che Che, My Horse) with the riding movement, naming the three horses.
4. Keep a repeated simple beat with hands and feet while singing.
5. Combine a song with dance movements in a group performance.

## What you need
Room to move: a line, a circle, an open floor. No instruments needed — hands and feet make the beat.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — Clap four times, slowly, evenly: one, two, three, four. Again. That steady clap is the floor every song in this lesson stands on. Now keep clapping and take two steps forward, stop, clap; two steps back, stop, clap. You have already begun the first song. (lesson/lesson.md).

## If the learner is stuck
- Loses the words → go back to the call-and-answer shape: the teacher (or the audio) sings a short piece, the learner answers with the same piece. Short pieces first, whole song after.
- Loses the tune → hum the song together before singing words; the tune first, the words ride on it.
- Sings but cannot say what the song teaches → ask the lesson's own question: what is this song about, and what does it ask us to do? Every song in this unit teaches something that can be said in one sentence.
