# Hands-on — Movement Songs

The lesson with your hands, your feet and your voice together, not only your eyes.

## The clapping line
Make a line holding hands. A leader claps the beat slowly. The line steps forward, lets go, claps; steps back, lets go, claps, singing all the way. If the beat breaks, stop, clap it steady, begin again.

## Walk the song
Lay a scarf or a rope line on the ground for the river. Walk the line singing the walking song; step over the river carefully on its verse, and turn for home at the last verse.
