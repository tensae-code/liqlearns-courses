# Movement Songs

## Do it first

Clap four times, slowly, evenly: one, two, three, four. Again. That steady clap is the floor every song in this lesson stands on. Now keep clapping and take two steps forward, stop, clap; two steps back, stop, clap. You have already begun the first song.

## Sub lesson 1 — Clapping songs

The clapping song is **"እናጨበጭባለን"** — *We Clap*.

Here is its movement, exactly as the book teaches it. Hold hands in a line. **Step forward** together. **Let go and stand. Clap.** Then **step back**. **Let go and stand. Clap.** Again and again, on a repeated simple beat.

The beat never speeds up and never slows down. If the clap and the step stop matching, stop the line, clap the beat alone until it is steady, and start again.

## Sub lesson 2 — Walking songs

The walking song is **"ሽርሽር እንሂድ"** — *Let's Go for a Walk*.

Its journey goes like this: rise in the morning. Walk on foot. Cross a river. Walk through fields. Pass among flowers, enjoying the fresh air. And return before dark.

Sing it walking in a line, slowly, the way a real walk goes. When the song crosses the river, step carefully, stone by stone. When it passes among the flowers, breathe in the fresh air. The song is a walk, and your feet take it.

## Sub lesson 3 — Riding songs

The riding song is **"ቼ ቼ ፈረሴ"** — *Che Che, My Horse*.

It rides three horses in turn — a **white** horse, a **black** horse, a **brown** horse — and the riders sing **"che che"** as they ride. The song is not sung standing still: it **combines song with dance**, the trotting rhythm carried in the body.

Ride in place with a gentle trot — knees soft, body bobbing lightly on the beat — and sing "che che" on the trot. In groups, you can present the songs of your own area the same way: sung and danced together.

## Say it to your companion

Do the clapping pattern with your companion — forward, stop, clap; back, stop, clap — while you both sing. Then tell the journey of the walking song in order, and name the riding song's three horses.

## Do It Yourself

1. Teach the clapping pattern to a friend: forward, stop, clap; back, stop, clap. Keep the beat steady for eight rounds.
2. Walk a real short walk and sing the walking song as you go. Come back before the song's journey ends — return before dark!
3. Trot in place to the riding song, and change your horse's colour each time the song does: white, black, brown.
