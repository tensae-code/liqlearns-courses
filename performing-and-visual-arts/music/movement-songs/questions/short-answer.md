# Movement Songs — short answer

Answer in a few words, out loud or in writing.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the walking song's journey in order.
**Answer:** Rise in the morning; walk on foot; cross a river; walk through fields; pass among flowers enjoying the fresh air; return before dark.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** What do the riders sing as they ride?
**Answer:** "Che che."
