# Movement Songs — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** If the clap and the step stop matching, you stop, clap the beat steady, and start again.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In the walking song you pass among flowers and enjoy the fresh air.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The riding song is sung standing perfectly still.
**Answer: False** — It combines song with dance — a trotting movement.
