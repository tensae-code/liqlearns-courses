# Movement Songs — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In "እናጨበጭባለን" (We Clap), the movement pattern is —
a) forward, stop, clap; back, stop, clap  b) jump, sit, clap  c) run in a circle only
**Answer: a) forward, stop, clap; back, stop, clap**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The clapping song is built on —
a) a fast beat  b) a repeated simple beat  c) no beat
**Answer: b) a repeated simple beat**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The walking song's journey ends —
a) at the river  b) at school  c) returning before dark
**Answer: c) returning before dark**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which comes first in the walking song?
a) crossing the river  b) rising in the morning  c) passing among flowers
**Answer: b) rising in the morning**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** The riding song's horses, in order, are —
a) brown, white, black  b) white, black, brown  c) black, brown, white
**Answer: b) white, black, brown**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** The riding song combines song with —
a) drawing  b) dance  c) reading
**Answer: b) dance** — Song combined with dance; "che che" sung as one rides.
