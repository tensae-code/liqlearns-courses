# Lesson plan — Lao Folk Instruments

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Music · **Lesson:** Lao Folk Instruments
**Place in the curriculum:** Music (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Laos' folk instruments — grajabpi, wooden clappers, the kong pao drum and the su string instruments — by name and sound.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this course in the same title.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Music G6 teacher guide — lao traditional string instruments** — three string instruments of the Tai peoples of Laos, each shown in a photograph — the spike fiddle su i, the su u, and the long su bang (about 60–70 cm) — how each is built and held
2. **Music G3 teacher guide, 2021 edition — Lao Folk Instruments** — the five folk instruments of the Lao ensemble, each in a full-page photograph — the ranat ek and ranat thum xylophones, the kong that and tap hon drums, and the wooden clappers
3. **Arts & Crafts + Music G3 teacher guide, 2009 edition — Lao Folk Instruments** — three folk instruments in drawings — the grajabpi four-string lute, the paired wooden clappers (ໄມ້ຮັບແງບ) and the kong pao drum that accompanies lam singing — and the sound of each; the two gong instruments in drawings — the khong vong, a circle of 16 tuned gongs, and the khong aew, three hanging gongs on a frame — and how each is played
4. **Arts & Crafts and Music G5 teacher guide, 2009 combined guide — Lao Folk Instruments** — the saen, the Lao free-reed mouth organ — its bamboo pipes and reed, and its sizes (saen 7, 8 and 9), with a labelled drawing
5. **Music G1 teacher guide, 2020 edition — Lao Folk Instruments** — meeting the instruments in big labelled drawings — the grajabpi lute, the su i and su u fiddles, the khaen, the khim dulcimer and the saen
6. **Music G8 teacher guide, 2012 — lao traditional string instruments** — the families of instruments, and the grajabpi lute in depth — its body, strings and sound (photograph)
7. **Music G2 teacher guide, 2020 edition — Lao Folk Instruments** — meeting the folk instruments of Laos and how each one sounds
8. **Arts & Crafts and Music G4 teacher guide, 2009 combined guide — Lao Folk Instruments** — the folk instruments of Laos — the khui flute, the khaen and the su fiddles among them
9. **Arts & Crafts and Music G1 teacher guides, original editions — Lao Folk Instruments** — the folk instruments of Laos — the drums, the khaen mouth organ, the sing cymbals and the saeng
10. **Arts & Crafts and Music G2 teacher guides, original editions — Lao Folk Instruments** — two folk instruments of Laos — the sanad xylophone and the drum with two faces; three more folk instruments of Laos — the ui, the su fiddle and the khim dulcimer
