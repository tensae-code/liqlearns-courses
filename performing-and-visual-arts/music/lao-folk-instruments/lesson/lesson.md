# Lao Folk Instruments

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about lao folk instruments. Then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — Music G6 teacher guide — lao traditional string instruments

Three string instruments of the Tai peoples of Laos, each shown in a photograph — the spike fiddle su i, the su u, and the long su bang (about 60–70 cm) — how each is built and held

## Sub lesson 2 — Music G3 teacher guide, 2021 edition — Lao Folk Instruments

The five folk instruments of the Lao ensemble, each in a full-page photograph — the ranat ek and ranat thum xylophones, the kong that and tap hon drums, and the wooden clappers

## Sub lesson 3 — Arts & Crafts + Music G3 teacher guide, 2009 edition — Lao Folk Instruments

Three folk instruments in drawings — the grajabpi four-string lute, the paired wooden clappers (ໄມ້ຮັບແງບ) and the kong pao drum that accompanies lam singing — and the sound of each; the two gong instruments in drawings — the khong vong, a circle of 16 tuned gongs, and the khong aew, three hanging gongs on a frame — and how each is played

## Sub lesson 4 — Arts & Crafts and Music G5 teacher guide, 2009 combined guide — Lao Folk Instruments

The saen, the Lao free-reed mouth organ — its bamboo pipes and reed, and its sizes (saen 7, 8 and 9), with a labelled drawing

## Sub lesson 5 — Music G1 teacher guide, 2020 edition — Lao Folk Instruments

Meeting the instruments in big labelled drawings — the grajabpi lute, the su i and su u fiddles, the khaen, the khim dulcimer and the saen

## Sub lesson 6 — Music G8 teacher guide, 2012 — lao traditional string instruments

The families of instruments, and the grajabpi lute in depth — its body, strings and sound (photograph)

## Sub lesson 7 — Music G2 teacher guide, 2020 edition — Lao Folk Instruments

Meeting the folk instruments of Laos and how each one sounds

## Sub lesson 8 — Arts & Crafts and Music G4 teacher guide, 2009 combined guide — Lao Folk Instruments

The folk instruments of Laos — the khui flute, the khaen and the su fiddles among them

## Sub lesson 9 — Arts & Crafts and Music G1 teacher guides, original editions — Lao Folk Instruments

The folk instruments of Laos — the drums, the khaen mouth organ, the sing cymbals and the saeng

## Sub lesson 10 — Arts & Crafts and Music G2 teacher guides, original editions — Lao Folk Instruments

Two folk instruments of Laos — the sanad xylophone and the drum with two faces; three more folk instruments of Laos — the ui, the su fiddle and the khim dulcimer
