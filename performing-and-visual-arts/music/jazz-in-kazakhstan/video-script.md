This lesson is Jazz in Kazakhstan.

Jazz in Kazakhstan — Jazz as Kazakhstan took it up and made it its own. — as Kazakhstan's school books teach it: what it teaches: jazz in Kazakhstan — Soviet jazz founders Tsfasman, Utyosov and Lundstrem; oriental jazz built on the multinational heritage; the Bumerang ensemble under Takhir Ibragimov, in the USSR's top five in the 1970s–80s; Zhanna Sattarova, «queen of jazz».

Watch, listen — then say it back in your own words.
