## Sub lesson 1 — Folk Singing

Folk singing is part of any state's culture, linked to different environments and occasions, like work singing and the songs of social occasions; the folk song's marks — wide spread, simple words and tune that can be memorised easily, and easy performance in group form; the folk song "Mahla al-Dar wa-l-Dira" (How Lovely the Home and the Homeland), a song of the village, its weddings, dabka dancing and olive trees, read, listened to and sung (summary only); the artist Tawfiq Al-Nimri, the Jordanian composer and singer born in Al-Husn in Irbid governorate, famous for Jordanian heritage folk songs, with 750 artistic works and songs written for famous Arab singers.
