# Hands-on — Rap and Hip Hop

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Work the lesson's own way
Work through the sub lessons in order — Rap: recitation that is not sung; Cuba's rap movement; Creating your own rap — doing each part the way Cuba's books direct: by making, moving, singing or looking closely, as the part asks. Show your companion what you made or did, and name the part it belongs to.
