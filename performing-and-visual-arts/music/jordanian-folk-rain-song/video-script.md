# Video script — The Jordanian Folk Rain Song

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is The Jordanian Folk Rain Song.

The Jordanian folk rain song "Ya Allah al-Ghaith ya Rabbi", a communal prayer-song asking God for rain to water the western and the standing crops, read and sung along with its recording.

Watch, listen — then say it back in your own words.
