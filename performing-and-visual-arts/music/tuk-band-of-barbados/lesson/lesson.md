# The Tuk Band of Barbados

## Do it first

Quick start: say in one sentence what you think this lesson is about — Barbados's own tuk band — the syllabus's attainment targets ask every child to name and recognise its instruments, and to write about the instruments used in a tuk band and how they are played.. Then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — Naming and recognising the tuk band's instruments

The Classes 1–4 attainment targets name the tuk band as Barbados's own music to know: pupils **name and recognise the instruments of the tuk band** — the band is recognised by the instruments that make its sound.

## Sub lesson 2 — Writing about how they are played

Knowing goes further than naming: the targets ask pupils to **write about the instruments used in a tuk band and how they are played** — each instrument, and the player's way with it.
