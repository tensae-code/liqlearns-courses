# The Tuk Band of Barbados — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The tuk band targets run across Classes 1–4.
**Answer: True.** — they are Classes 1–4 attainment targets.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Naming the instruments is enough; writing about them is not asked.
**Answer: False.** — the targets ask for writing about the instruments and how they are played.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The tuk band is Barbados's own music in the syllabus.
**Answer: True.**.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Pupils write about how the instruments are played, not only what they are called.
**Answer: True.**.
