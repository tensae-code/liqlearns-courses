# The Tuk Band of Barbados — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which band does the Barbados syllabus name as its own indigenous band?
**Answer: The tuk band.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the two things pupils write about for the tuk band.
**Answer: The instruments used in a tuk band, and how they are played.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** For which classes do these attainment targets run?
**Answer: Classes 1–4.**
