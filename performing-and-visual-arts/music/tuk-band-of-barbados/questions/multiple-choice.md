# The Tuk Band of Barbados — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Barbados syllabus's attainment targets ask pupils to do what with the tuk band's instruments?
a) name and recognise them  b) build them  c) ignore them
**Answer: a) name and recognise them** — naming and recognising are the named targets.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The tuk band is presented in the syllabus as…
a) a foreign import  b) Barbados's own indigenous music  c) a kind of recorder
**Answer: b) Barbados's own indigenous music** — it stands under the syllabus's indigenous music.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Besides naming the instruments, pupils must also…
a) write about the instruments and how they are played  b) march with the band  c) sew its costumes
**Answer: a) write about the instruments and how they are played** — writing about them is the second target.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** "How they are played" asks about…
a) the player's way with each instrument  b) the band's fees  c) the weather
**Answer: a) the player's way with each instrument**.

**5. (Sub lesson 1)** Recognising an instrument means…
a) knowing it when you see or hear it  b) owning it  c) painting it
**Answer: a) knowing it when you see or hear it**.
