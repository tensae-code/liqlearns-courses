This lesson is Wannam Praise Songs of Sri Lanka.

The Wannam — Sri Lanka's praise-songs that name and honour (animals, places, virtues) in set melodic shapes — and the Hatan Gee war songs, sung with their cultural stories

Watch, listen — then say it back in your own words.
