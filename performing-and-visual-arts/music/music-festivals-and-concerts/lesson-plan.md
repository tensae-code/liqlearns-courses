# Lesson plan — Music Festivals and Concerts

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Music · **Lesson:** Music Festivals and Concerts
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 3 — Music (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** music Festivals and Concerts — its forms, history and cultural meaning, taught as one topic lesson.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the course's earlier lessons in its title; recommended first, never forced.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of ..." in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **festivals and concerts of popular music** — festivals and concerts of popular music: live meetings of musicians and audience that launch styles and careers — Glastonbury, Coachella, Tomorrowland, Montreux Jazz and Eurovision in the world; Makfest, the Skopje Jazz Festival, Jazz Fest Tetovo and Ohrid Summer at home.
2. **Значajни културни настани во Р. Македонија** — Значajни културни настани во Р. Македонија — the country's great cultural events: the Ohrid Choir Festival and the May Opera Evenings.
3. **Виенскиот новогодишен концерт** — Виенскиот новогодишен концерт — Vienna's New Year's Concert: the traditional classical concert given every 1 January in Vienna's Golden Hall, waltzes of Johann Strauss at its heart.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
