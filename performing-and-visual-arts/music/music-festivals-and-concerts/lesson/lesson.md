# Music Festivals and Concerts

## Do it first

Name one thing that belongs in this lesson — music Festivals and Concerts — its forms, history and cultural meaning, taught as one topic lesson. Say what it is in your own words. If you can name one, you are already inside the lesson.

## Sub lesson 1 — festivals and concerts of popular music

festivals and concerts of popular music: live meetings of musicians and audience that launch styles and careers — Glastonbury, Coachella, Tomorrowland, Montreux Jazz and Eurovision in the world; Makfest, the Skopje Jazz Festival, Jazz Fest Tetovo and Ohrid Summer at home.

## Sub lesson 2 — Значajни културни настани во Р. Македонија

Значajни културни настани во Р. Македонија — the country's great cultural events: the Ohrid Choir Festival and the May Opera Evenings.

## Sub lesson 3 — Виенскиот новогодишен концерт

Виенскиот новогодишен концерт — Vienna's New Year's Concert: the traditional classical concert given every 1 January in Vienna's Golden Hall, waltzes of Johann Strauss at its heart.
