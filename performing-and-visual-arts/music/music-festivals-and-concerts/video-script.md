The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

This is North Macedonia's own lesson.

**festivals and concerts of popular music.** festivals and concerts of popular music: live meetings of musicians and audience that launch styles and careers — Glastonbury, Coachella, Tomorrowland, Montreux Jazz and Eurovision in the world; Makfest, the Skopje Jazz Festival, Jazz Fest Tetovo and Ohrid Summer at home.

**Значajни културни настани во Р. Македонија.** Значajни културни настани во Р. Македонија — the country's great cultural events: the Ohrid Choir Festival and the May Opera Evenings.

**Виенскиот новогодишен концерт.** Виенскиот новогодишен концерт — Vienna's New Year's Concert: the traditional classical concert given every 1 January in Vienna's Golden Hall, waltzes of Johann Strauss at its heart.

That is Music Festivals and Concerts — North Macedonia's way, kept by name.
