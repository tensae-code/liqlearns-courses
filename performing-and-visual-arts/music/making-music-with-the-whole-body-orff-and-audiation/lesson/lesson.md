# Making Music with the Whole Body: Orff and Audiation

## Do it first

Quick start: from the title alone, guess what this lesson's work is. Then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — Orff — the whole body as an instrument

Trinidad and Tobago's Music guide builds its teaching on the great methods. **Orff**: the **whole body as a percussive instrument**, built on **innate rhythm and melody**, with **improvisation** — music begins in the body everyone already has.

## Sub lesson 2 — Suzuki — the mother-tongue way

**Suzuki**: the **mother-tongue environment** — music surrounded by **listening, rote and repetition**, the way a child learns their first language by hearing it all around them.

## Sub lesson 3 — Gordon — audiation

**Gordon**: **audiation** — **thinking music in the mind with understanding**. Audiation is the aim of **tonal and rhythm learning**: not just copying sounds, but hearing them inside, with meaning.
