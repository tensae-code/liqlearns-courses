# Making Music with the Whole Body: Orff and Audiation — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does Orff treat the whole body as?
**Answer: A percussive instrument.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Music in Suzuki's method is learned the way what is learned?
**Answer: A mother tongue — a first language, by listening, rote and repetition.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Whose method names audiation, and what is it the aim of?
**Answer: Gordon's; it is the aim of tonal and rhythm learning.**
