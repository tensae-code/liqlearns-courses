# Making Music with the Whole Body: Orff and Audiation — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Improvisation belongs in Orff's method.
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Suzuki's method avoids listening.
**Answer: False.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Audiation is thinking music in the mind with understanding.
**Answer: True.**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Audiation has nothing to do with tonal and rhythm learning.
**Answer: False.**
