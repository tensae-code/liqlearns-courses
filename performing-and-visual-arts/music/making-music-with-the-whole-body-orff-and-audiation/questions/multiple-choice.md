# Making Music with the Whole Body: Orff and Audiation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In Orff's method, the first instrument is…
a) the whole body, used percussively  b) the piano  c) the violin only
**Answer: a) the whole body, used percussively**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Orff's work is built on…
a) innate rhythm and melody, with improvisation  b) reading scores silently  c) buying instruments
**Answer: a) innate rhythm and melody, with improvisation**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Suzuki's environment is called…
a) the mother-tongue environment  b) the examination environment  c) the concert-hall environment
**Answer: a) the mother-tongue environment**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Suzuki learning works through…
a) listening, rote and repetition  b) only written theory  c) never listening
**Answer: a) listening, rote and repetition**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Audiation means…
a) thinking music in the mind with understanding  b) playing louder  c) singing without thinking
**Answer: a) thinking music in the mind with understanding**
