# Hands-on — Making Music with the Whole Body: Orff and Audiation

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Body percussion, Orff's way
Build a short rhythm piece from body sounds only — clap, stamp, pat, click — then improvise a variation in a small group.

## Listen, repeat, think
Listen to a short melody three times (Suzuki's rote way), sing it back, then close your eyes and hear it inside without singing — that inner hearing is audiation.
