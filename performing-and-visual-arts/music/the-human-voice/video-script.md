# Video script — The Human Voice

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

You carry an instrument everywhere. Your **voice**!

How is it made? By the **phonation apparatus** — *aparato fonador* — the body's voice-making apparatus.

And voices are not all the same. They are **classified** — sorted into kinds.

Your voice: an instrument you are born with.
