# The Human Voice

## Do it first

Sing one note. Now sing a higher one, and a lower one. Same voice — different sounds. Where does the sound come from, and why is your voice not the same as your friend's? That is this lesson.

## Sub lesson 1 — How the voice is produced — the phonation apparatus

Honduras's Grade 8 book gives a music sequence to **La entonación musical — musical intonation: the human voice**. It studies the **aparato fonador — the phonation apparatus**: the apparatus by which **the voice is produced**. Singing in tune begins with knowing the instrument that makes the sound — your own body.

## Sub lesson 2 — The classification of the human voice

The same sequence teaches the **classification of the human voice**: voices are not all alike, and they are **sorted into kinds**. To sing with others — in a choir, in a class — a singer first knows what kind of voice they carry.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion: what produces the voice, and what does it mean to classify voices? Companion checks: the phonation apparatus named? Voices sorted into kinds?

## Do It Yourself

1. Sing a high note and a low note and write one sentence about what your voice did.
2. Listen to three voices in your group and write one way they differ.
3. Write one sentence: why does a singer study the phonation apparatus?
