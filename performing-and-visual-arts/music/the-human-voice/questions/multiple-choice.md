# The Human Voice — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The voice is produced by…
a) the phonation apparatus (aparato fonador)  b) the hands  c) a drum
**Answer: a) the phonation apparatus (aparato fonador)**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The aparato fonador is…
a) a written song  b) the apparatus that produces the voice  c) a stage
**Answer: b) the apparatus that produces the voice**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The classification of the human voice means…
a) all voices are identical  b) voices are sorted into kinds  c) voices cannot be studied
**Answer: b) voices are sorted into kinds**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A singer studies their own voice in order to…
a) sing in tune and sing with others  b) never sing  c) hide their voice
**Answer: a) sing in tune and sing with others** — musical intonation is the sequence's aim.

**5. (Sub lesson 1)** Musical intonation, in the Honduras book, is taught through…
a) the human voice  b) the guitar only  c) silence
**Answer: a) the human voice**
