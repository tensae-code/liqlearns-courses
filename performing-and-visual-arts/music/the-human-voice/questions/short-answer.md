# The Human Voice — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the aparato fonador?
**Answer: The phonation apparatus — the apparatus by which the voice is produced.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the classification of the human voice do?
**Answer: It sorts voices into kinds.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Which book teaches the human voice, and in which sequence?
**Answer: The Honduras Grade 8 Educación Artística book, in its musical intonation sequence (Block II S3).**
