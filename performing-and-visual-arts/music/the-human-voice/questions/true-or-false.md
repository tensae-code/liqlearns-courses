# The Human Voice — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The human voice is produced by the phonation apparatus.
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** All human voices are exactly the same.
**Answer: False.** — voices are classified into kinds.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The voice can be studied like an instrument.
**Answer: True.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Classification of the voice means giving every singer the same part regardless of voice.
**Answer: False.** — it sorts voices into their kinds.
