# Hands-on — The Human Voice

The lesson with your own voice, not only your eyes.

## Feel the voice
Sing a steady note and feel where the voice works in your body. Sing a high note, then a low note. Say what changed.

## Sort the voices
Listen to the voices in your group speaking and singing. Sort them as the book does: voices differ, and the human voice is classified into kinds. Say one way your voices differ.
