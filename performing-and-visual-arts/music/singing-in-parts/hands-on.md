# Hands-on — Singing in Parts

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## The voice, warmed up
Do the practice this part names, with real materials and a partner: The voice is the most accessible instrument: warm-up breathing and loosening, scales and broken chords prepare it.

## Singing technique
Do the practice this part names, with real materials and a partner: Posture and breathing, diction and tone colour, accurate intervals and rhythm, expressive tone, tempo, dynamics, style, phrasing, harmonic balance and voice blending.

## Harmonising, and composing for voices
Do the practice this part names, with real materials and a partner: Singing in two-part, three-part and four-part harmony using solfa, numbers and letters — up to composing and performing original choral pieces and rearranging the school song or anthem.
