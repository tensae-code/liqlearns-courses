# Singing in Parts — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — The voice, warmed up
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: The voice is the most accessible instrument: warm-up breathing and loosening, scales and broken chords prepare it.

## Sub lesson 2 — Singing technique
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Posture and breathing, diction and tone colour, accurate intervals and rhythm, expressive tone, tempo, dynamics, style, phrasing, harmonic balance and voice blending.

## Sub lesson 3 — Harmonising, and composing for voices
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Singing in two-part, three-part and four-part harmony using solfa, numbers and letters — up to composing and performing original choral pieces and rearranging the school song or anthem.
