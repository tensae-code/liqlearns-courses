# Video script — Singing in Parts

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

**The voice, warmed up.** The voice is the most accessible instrument: warm-up breathing and loosening, scales and broken chords prepare it.

**Singing technique.** Posture and breathing, diction and tone colour, accurate intervals and rhythm, expressive tone, tempo, dynamics, style, phrasing, harmonic balance and voice blending.

**Harmonising, and composing for voices.** Singing in two-part, three-part and four-part harmony using solfa, numbers and letters — up to composing and performing original choral pieces and rearranging the school song or anthem.
