# Singing in Parts — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name four parts of singing technique.
**Answer: Posture, breathing, diction, tone colour, accurate intervals and rhythm, expressive tone, tempo, dynamics, style, phrasing, harmonic balance, voice blending (any four).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What three systems name the notes in harmony work?
**Answer: Solfa, numbers and letters.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What two existing songs may be rearranged?
**Answer: The school song and the anthem.**
