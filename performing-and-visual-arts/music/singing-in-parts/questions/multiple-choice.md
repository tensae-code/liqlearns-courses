# Singing in Parts — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Warm-ups include…
a) breathing, loosening, scales and broken chords  b) running laps only
**Answer: a) breathing, loosening, scales and broken chords.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Voice blending is part of…
a) singing technique  b) costume design
**Answer: a) singing technique.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Harmony is sung in…
a) two, three and four parts  b) never more than one part
**Answer: a) two, three and four parts.**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Original choral pieces are…
a) composed and performed by learners  b) forbidden
**Answer: a) composed and performed by learners.**
