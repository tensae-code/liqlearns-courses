# Singing in Parts

## Do it first

Look at the sub lesson list. Pick the part you already know something about, and say what you know. Then start at Sub lesson 1 — every part builds on the one before it.

## Sub lesson 1 — The voice, warmed up

The voice is the most accessible instrument: warm-up breathing and loosening, scales and broken chords prepare it.

## Sub lesson 2 — Singing technique

Posture and breathing, diction and tone colour, accurate intervals and rhythm, expressive tone, tempo, dynamics, style, phrasing, harmonic balance and voice blending.

## Sub lesson 3 — Harmonising, and composing for voices

Singing in two-part, three-part and four-part harmony using solfa, numbers and letters — up to composing and performing original choral pieces and rearranging the school song or anthem.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion this lesson's one thing in your own words, naming each sub lesson as you go. Companion checks: did every part get named?

## Do It Yourself

1. Do each sub lesson's practice once more, on your own.
2. Teach one part to someone else, the way this lesson taught it to you.
3. Write one sentence: what is this lesson's one thing?
