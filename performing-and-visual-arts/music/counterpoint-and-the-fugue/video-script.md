The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

**Contrapunto y fuga.** Counterpoint — two or more melodic lines sounding at once (polyphony) — and the fugue, imitative work built from one given theme.

That is Counterpoint, Polyphony and the Fugue — Venezuela's own books teach it this way.
