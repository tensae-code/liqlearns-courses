# Hands-on — Counterpoint, Polyphony and the Fugue

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Contrapunto y fuga
Work through this part with real materials or your own body, the way the book sets it: counterpoint — two or more melodic lines sounding at once (polyphony) — and the fugue, imitative work built from one given theme. Show a companion what you made or did, and say what the part teaches.
