This lesson is Kunqu Music.

Kunqu — China's classical opera music, the mother of its theatre forms. — as China's school books teach it: Kunqu music — Kunqu and its music, its singing and accompaniment, and its gongche notation and singing theory, through 《牡丹亭·游园》 and 《玉簪记·琴挑》.

Watch, listen — then say it back in your own words.
