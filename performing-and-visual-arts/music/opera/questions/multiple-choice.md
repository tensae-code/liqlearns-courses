# Opera — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Operetta is…
a) opera's lighter stage sister  b) a marching band  c) a silent film
**Answer: a) opera's lighter stage sister**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Operetta mixes songs with…
a) spoken words  b) only dancing  c) only drums
**Answer: a) spoken words**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The guide's operetta example is by…
a) Kálmán  b) Sousa  c) Joplin
**Answer: a) Kálmán**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Das Veilchen von Montmartre is…
a) an operetta  b) a sea shanty  c) a tango
**Answer: a) an operetta**

**5. (Sub lesson 1)** Operetta songs are…
a) tunes everyone ends up knowing  b) never sung again  c) always silent
**Answer: a) tunes everyone ends up knowing**
