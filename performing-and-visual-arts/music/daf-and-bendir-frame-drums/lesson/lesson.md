# Daf and Bendir: Frame Drums

## Do it first

## Sub lesson 1 — The daf (الدف) — Libya’s frame drum

A wooden frame with skin stretched over it and a thumb-hole in the frame; held up in the left hand at chest level and tapped with the right hand’s fingers, and it comes in two kinds.

## Sub lesson 2 — The bendir — the big frame drum of the zawaya

The large frame drum of the Sufi corners (zawaya), its skin stretched over a wooden frame with a snare string across the inside that buzzes as it is struck — Libya’s big frame drum beside the daf.
