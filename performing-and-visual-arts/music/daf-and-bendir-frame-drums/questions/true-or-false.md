# Daf and Bendir: Frame Drums — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The daf is tapped with the fingers of the right hand.
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The bendir’s skin is stretched over a wooden frame.
**Answer: True.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The daf is a wind instrument.
**Answer: False — it is a frame drum, a beat instrument.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The bendir is played in the Sufi corners (zawaya).
**Answer: True.**
