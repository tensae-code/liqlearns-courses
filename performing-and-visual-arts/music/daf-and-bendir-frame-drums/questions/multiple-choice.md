# Daf and Bendir: Frame Drums — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The daf is…
a) a wooden frame with skin stretched over it  b) a metal tube  c) a stringed box
**Answer: a) a wooden frame with skin stretched over it**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How is the daf held and played?
a) on the floor with sticks  b) held up in the left hand at chest level and tapped with the right hand’s fingers  c) blown like a flute
**Answer: b) held up in the left hand at chest level and tapped with the right hand's fingers**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The daf’s frame has…
a) a thumb-hole  b) six finger holes  c) tuning levers
**Answer: a) a thumb-hole**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The bendir is the large frame drum of…
a) the school band only  b) the Sufi corners (zawaya)  c) the orchestra’s strings
**Answer: b) the Sufi corners (zawaya)**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** What buzzes inside the bendir when it is struck?
a) a bell  b) loose stones  c) a snare string across the inside
**Answer: c) a snare string across the inside**
