# Daf and Bendir: Frame Drums — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How many kinds of daf does the Grade 1 guide name?
**Answer: Two kinds.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which hand holds the daf up, and at what level?
**Answer: The left hand, at chest level.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** How does the bendir differ from the daf in build?
**Answer: It is larger, and it carries a snare string across the inside of its frame that buzzes as it is struck.**
