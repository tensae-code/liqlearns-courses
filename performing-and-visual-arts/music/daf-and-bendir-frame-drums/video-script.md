# Video script — Daf and Bendir: Frame Drums

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Two drums, one family — the frame drums.
The daf: a wooden frame, skin stretched over it, a thumb-hole in the frame.
Up in the left hand, at chest level — tap, tap, with the right hand's fingers.
The bendir is bigger: skin over a wooden frame, a snare string inside that buzzes when it is struck — the drum of the zawaya.
