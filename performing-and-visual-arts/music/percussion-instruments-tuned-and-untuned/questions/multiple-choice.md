# Percussion Instruments: Tuned and Untuned — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Percussion sounds are made by…
a) bowing  b) striking, scraping or shaking  c) blowing only
**Answer: b) striking, scraping or shaking.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A tuned percussion instrument…
a) plays singable set pitches  b) has no pitch at all  c) is always a drum
**Answer: a) plays singable set pitches.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Which of these is untuned?
a) xylophone  b) glockenspiel  c) cymbals
**Answer: c) cymbals.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which keyboard instruments are counted in the percussion family here?
a) piano and celesta  b) organ and harpsichord  c) accordion only
**Answer: a) piano and celesta.**
