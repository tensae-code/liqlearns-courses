# Percussion Instruments: Tuned and Untuned — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Timpani are tuned, even though they are drums.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A wind machine is counted among percussion in this lesson.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Castanets are tuned instruments.
**Answer: False — they are untuned.**
