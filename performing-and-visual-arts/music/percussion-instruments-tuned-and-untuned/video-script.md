# Video script — Percussion Instruments: Tuned and Untuned

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

**Bang! Shake! Scrape!** That is percussion — the orchestra's heartbeat.

Some percussion can **sing a tune** — xylophone, glockenspiel. Those are **tuned**.
Some only **speak in rhythm** — cymbals, cowbell. Those are **untuned**.

Two families inside one family!
