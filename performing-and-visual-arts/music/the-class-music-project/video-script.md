# Video script — The Class Music Project

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This is North Macedonia's own lesson.

**Проект во наставата.** Проект во наставата — the class music project: groups of five or six research a composer, a performer or an element of folklore, form a performing group, and present — the time shared out: theme 1 part, research half, presentation-making and rehearsal a quarter each.

That is The Class Music Project — North Macedonia's way, kept by name.
