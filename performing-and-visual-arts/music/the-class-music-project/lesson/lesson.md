# The Class Music Project

## Do it first

Name one thing that belongs in this lesson — Doing a class music project — researching musical giftedness and presenting a musical work. Say what it is in your own words. If you can name one, you are already inside the lesson.

## Sub lesson 1 — Проект во наставата

Проект во наставата — the class music project: groups of five or six research a composer, a performer or an element of folklore, form a performing group, and present — the time shared out: theme 1 part, research half, presentation-making and rehearsal a quarter each.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson holds, part by part, in your own words. Companion checks: did every part get named, with its Macedonian name kept where the book gives one?

## Do It Yourself

1. Choose one named part of this lesson and find out one more true thing about it — from a person who knows it, or from the book your part came from.
2. Make or perform a small piece in the way of that part: a rhythm, a step, a drawing, a shaped piece of clay.
3. Write one sentence: what does this part show about North Macedonia's own way of teaching its arts?
