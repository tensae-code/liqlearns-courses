# Armenian National Instruments — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — Armenia's national instruments
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Armenia's national instruments — strings: qamani and kamancha; winds: sring, shvi, DUDUK, blul, zurna; and tar, kamancha, dhol in Movsisyan's song

## Sub lesson 2 — INSTRUMENTS
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: INSTRUMENTS: the BLUL — instruments serve to perform music; they divide into folk (born in national music) and classical; hear the blul, kin to the srings found all over the world; the blul-like pipe in Mozart's "Magic Flute"

## Sub lesson 3 — INSTRUMENTS
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: INSTRUMENTS: DHOL and DRUM (դհոլ և թմբուկ) — percussion sounds by hand or stick and carries the rhythm; the Indian dhol, the eastern dhak and the Japanese taiko compared

## Sub lesson 4 — INSTRUMENTS
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: INSTRUMENTS: the STRINGED — strings sounded by bow or by plucking fingers; the kamancha was played by the ashughs, among them the famed SAYAT-NOVA, who sang their own composed songs
