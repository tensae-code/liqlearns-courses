## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Armenia's national instruments** — Armenia's national instruments — strings: qamani and kamancha; winds: sring, shvi, DUDUK, blul, zurna; and tar, kamancha, dhol in Movsisyan's song
2. **INSTRUMENTS** — INSTRUMENTS: the BLUL — instruments serve to perform music; they divide into folk (born in national music) and classical; hear the blul, kin to the srings found all over the world; the blul-like pipe in Mozart's "Magic Flute"
3. **INSTRUMENTS** — INSTRUMENTS: DHOL and DRUM (դհոլ և թմբուկ) — percussion sounds by hand or stick and carries the rhythm; the Indian dhol, the eastern dhak and the Japanese taiko compared
4. **INSTRUMENTS** — INSTRUMENTS: the STRINGED — strings sounded by bow or by plucking fingers; the kamancha was played by the ashughs, among them the famed SAYAT-NOVA, who sang their own composed songs
5. **Folk works arranged, in the yearly plan (teacher-guide evidence)** — Folk works arranged, in the yearly plan (teacher-guide evidence) — the arrangement hours name Jivan Gasparyan (the duduk master) and Georgi Minasyan as the listening
