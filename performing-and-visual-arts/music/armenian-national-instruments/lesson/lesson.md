## Sub lesson 1 — Armenia's national instruments

Armenia's national instruments — strings: qamani and kamancha; winds: sring, shvi, DUDUK, blul, zurna; and tar, kamancha, dhol in Movsisyan's song.

## Sub lesson 2 — INSTRUMENTS

INSTRUMENTS: the BLUL — instruments serve to perform music; they divide into folk (born in national music) and classical; hear the blul, kin to the srings found all over the world; the blul-like pipe in Mozart's "Magic Flute".

## Sub lesson 3 — INSTRUMENTS

INSTRUMENTS: DHOL and DRUM (դհոլ և թմբուկ) — percussion sounds by hand or stick and carries the rhythm; the Indian dhol, the eastern dhak and the Japanese taiko compared.

## Sub lesson 4 — INSTRUMENTS

INSTRUMENTS: the STRINGED — strings sounded by bow or by plucking fingers; the kamancha was played by the ashughs, among them the famed SAYAT-NOVA, who sang their own composed songs.
