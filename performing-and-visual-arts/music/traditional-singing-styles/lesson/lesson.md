# Traditional Singing Styles of North Macedonia

## Do it first

Name one thing that belongs in this lesson — How North Macedonia's peoples sing traditionally — one-part and two-part singing. Say what it is in your own words. If you can name one, you are already inside the lesson.

## Sub lesson 1 — how the peoples of the country sing: Macedonian one-part and two-part singing

how the peoples of the country sing: Macedonian one-part and two-part singing — glasoečko echo singing, ukano, and bordun (drone) singing, the second voice at the upper third or fifth, three-part singing in places, ornaments throughout; Albanian throat singing, rhythm, polyphony and heterophony, men's epic and women's lyric songs; Turkish melismatic singing on modal makam scales; Roma singing of rhythm, virtuosity and free melody; Vlach melismatic singing with its leading line.

## Do It Yourself

1. Choose one named part of this lesson and find out one more true thing about it — from a person who knows it, or from the book your part came from.
2. Make or perform a small piece in the way of that part: a rhythm, a step, a drawing, a shaped piece of clay.
3. Write one sentence: what does this part show about North Macedonia's own way of teaching its arts?
