# Video script — Traditional Singing Styles of North Macedonia

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This is North Macedonia's own lesson.

**how the peoples of the country sing: Macedonian one-part and two-part singing.** how the peoples of the country sing: Macedonian one-part and two-part singing — glasoečko echo singing, ukano, and bordun (drone) singing, the second voice at the upper third or fifth, three-part singing in places, ornaments throughout; Albanian throat singing, rhythm, polyphony and heterophony, men's epic and women's lyric songs; Turkish melismatic singing on modal makam scales; Roma singing of rhythm, virtuosity and free melody; Vlach melismatic singing with its leading line.

That is Traditional Singing Styles of North Macedonia — North Macedonia's way, kept by name.
