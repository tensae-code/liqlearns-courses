## Sub lesson 1 — how the peoples of the country sing: Macedonian one-part and two-part singing
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: how the peoples of the country sing: Macedonian one-part and two-part singing — glasoečko echo singing, ukano, and bordun (drone) singing, the second voice at the upper third or fifth, three-part singing in places, ornaments throughout; Albanian throat singing, rhythm, polyphony and heterophony, men's epic and women's lyric songs; Turkish melismatic singing on modal makam scales; Roma singing of rhythm, virtuosity and free melody; Vlach melismatic singing with its leading line.
