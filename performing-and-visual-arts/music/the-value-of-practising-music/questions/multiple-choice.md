# The Value of Practising Music — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.


**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Practice turns knowledge of a sign into…
  a) The ability to perform it b) A longer school day c) A new instrument
**Answer: a) The ability to perform it** — §4.1 — knowing becomes doing through practice.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which is a gain of practice?
  a) Steadiness of beat b) Forgetting the rests c) Singing only once
**Answer: a) Steadiness of beat** — practice steadies the beat, sharpens accuracy and builds confidence.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Practised musicians give the community…
  a) Music ready for its occasions b) Silence at festivals c) Fewer songs
**Answer: a) Music ready for its occasions** — festivals, worship and teaching all call for ready music.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A teaching song helps the community by…
  a) Carrying lessons to its children b) Replacing all schools c) Ending celebrations
**Answer: a) Carrying lessons to its children** — songs teach — practised singers carry them.
