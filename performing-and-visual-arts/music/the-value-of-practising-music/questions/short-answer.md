# The Value of Practising Music — short answer

Answer in a short sentence or a short list.


**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name two things the practising learner gains.
**Answer:** Any two of: a steady beat, accuracy for the full value of each sound, confidence to perform before others.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name two community occasions that need practised music.
**Answer:** Any two of: festivals and celebrations, worship and chant, teaching songs for children, weddings and gatherings.
