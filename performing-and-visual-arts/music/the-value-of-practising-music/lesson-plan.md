# Lesson plan — The Value of Practising Music

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Music · **Lesson:** The Value of Practising Music
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 3 — Music (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** what practising music gives — to the learner who practises and to the community that hears the result (teacher guide — evidence).
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Playing Notes and Rests; this lesson is labelled teacher-guide evidence — it carries the Grade 4 guide's Chapter 4 opening teaching (§4.1).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **What practice gives the learner (teacher guide — evidence)** — the guide's §4.1 teaching: practice turns knowing a sign into being able to perform it.
2. **What practice gives the community (teacher guide — evidence)** — practised music serves the community: songs ready for festivals, worship and teaching.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Playing Notes and Rests (playing the signs). Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say what regular practice gives the learner.
2. Say what a practising musician gives the community.

## What you need
An instrument or the voice, and a regular practice time.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — play or sing one short line badly, then again after three careful repetitions — and feel what practice just did. (lesson/lesson.md).
2. **The explanation** — the sub lessons in order (lesson/lesson.md).

3. **Say it to your companion** — teach the lesson back in your own words (lesson/lesson.md).
   - ADD VIDEO: the script in video-script.md, filmed — made later, when videos are made.
4. **Questions** — from questions/: multiple-choice, short-answer (levels.md).
5. **Hands-on** — hands-on.md.
6. **Do It Yourself** — the doing block at the end of lesson/lesson.md.

## If the learner is stuck
If practice feels useless, shrink the target: one line, one corner, three slow repetitions — then judge again.
