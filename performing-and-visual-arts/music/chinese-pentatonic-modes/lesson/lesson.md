## Sub lesson 1 — China HS Music Theory

China's own modal system — the five tones generated by rising fifths, gong, shang, jue, zhi, yu, which become five pentatonic modes according to which of them is the final: Gong, Shang, Jue, Zhi and Yu modes; the same-gong family, all five modes sharing one gong tone and one set of fixed relations to it; the six-tone modes, a pentatonic scale plus one added tone — biangong below the gong or qingjue above the jue — making ten; and the seven-tone modes in their three historical scale forms: Qingyue (adding qingjue and biangong), Yayue (adding biangong and bianzheng) and Yanyue (adding qingjue and run), fifteen modes in the C-gong family alone
