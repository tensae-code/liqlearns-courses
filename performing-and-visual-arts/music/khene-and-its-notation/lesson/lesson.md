# The Khene and Its Notation

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about the khene and its notation. Then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — Music G9 teacher guide — khene notation reading

Reading the F major scale, its reverse and broken forms, then a first notated khene melody in 4/4; reading a second F major khene melody in 4/4, alone and together; reading a khene melody in 2/4 in F major, naming the signs used and the highest and lowest sounds; reading a khene melody in 2/4 in F major with correct rhythm; reading a khene melody in 3/4 in F major; reading a khene melody in D minor in 2/4; reading a khene melody in D minor in 3/4; reading a khene melody in D minor in 4/4; reading a khene melody in D minor in 4/4, the last of the reading series

## Sub lesson 2 — Music G9 teacher guide — khene notation dictation

Hearing the F major scale and writing down dictated notes (sets 1A and 1B) in 4/4; writing dictated F major notes in 3/4 and checking a partner's writing; writing dictated F major notes (sets 3A and 3B) in 2/4; writing dictated notes in D minor in 4/4; writing dictated notes in D minor in 2/4

## Sub lesson 3 — Music G6 teacher guide — khene notation reading

The C major scale up, down and broken, then a first melody read in 2/4; reading a C major melody in 2/4, naming its signs and intervals; reading a C major melody in 2/4 with rests; reading a C major melody in 2/4; reading a C major melody in 2/4 with dotted rhythms; reading a C major melody in 2/4; reading a C major melody in 2/4; reading a C major melody in 2/4; reading a C major melody in 2/4, the last of the first series

## Sub lesson 4 — Music G11 teacher guide — khene notation reading

The purpose and two-step method of reading khene notation (first read the scale notes, then read the melody), with the D major scale as the worked example; reading the D major scale up, down and broken, then a melody in 4/4; reading a D major melody in 4/4; reading a D major melody in 4/4; reading a D major melody in 3/4; reading a D major melody in 3/4, the last of the D series; reading the B minor scale up, down and broken, then a melody in 4/4; reading a B minor melody with repeated-note figures; reading a B minor melody with first and second endings; reading a B minor melody; reading a B minor melody; reading a B minor melody, the last of the Bm series

## Sub lesson 5 — Music G11 teacher guide — khene notation dictation

The three steps of dictation (memorise the scale, name the notes heard, write them), worked on D major melodies 1 and 2; writing dictated D major notes, naming each note heard before writing; writing dictated D major notes, the last of the dictation series; the reading method restated and three full dictation melodies in D major written from hearing

## Sub lesson 6 — Music G7 teacher guide — khene notation reading

Reading the C major scale, then a first melody in 4/4; reading a C major melody in 4/4; reading a C major melody in 2/4; reading a C major melody; reading the A minor scale, then a melody in 2/4; reading an A minor melody in 4/4; reading an A minor melody in 3/4; reading an A minor melody, the last of the series

## Sub lesson 7 — Music G7 teacher guide — khene notation dictation

The two preparation steps of dictation (memorise the scale; name the note heard) and writing a first dictated C major melody; writing dictated C major notes in 4/4; writing dictated C major notes in 3/4; writing dictated A minor notes in 4/4; writing dictated A minor notes in 4/4, the last of the series

## Sub lesson 8 — Music G8 teacher guide, 2012 — khene notation reading

Reading a khene piece in F major from notation and playing it; reading a khene piece in F major from notation and playing it; reading a khene piece in F major from notation and playing it; reading a khene piece in F major from notation and playing it; reading a khene piece in F major from notation and playing it; reading a khene piece in F major from notation, now with loud and soft marks; reading a khene piece in C major from notation and playing it; reading a khene piece in C major from notation and playing it

## Sub lesson 9 — Music G8 teacher guide, 2012 — khene notation dictation

Hearing a khene melody and writing it down; hearing a khene melody and writing it down; hearing a khene melody and writing it down; hearing a khene melody and writing it down; hearing a khene melody and writing it down
