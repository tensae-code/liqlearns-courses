# Lesson plan — The Khene and Its Notation

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Music · **Lesson:** The Khene and Its Notation
**Place in the curriculum:** Music (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Laos' khene mouth organ and its own notation — reading khene notation in exercises and writing it from dictation.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this course in the same title.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Music G9 teacher guide — khene notation reading** — reading the F major scale, its reverse and broken forms, then a first notated khene melody in 4/4; reading a second F major khene melody in 4/4, alone and together; reading a khene melody in 2/4 in F major, naming the signs used and the highest and lowest sounds; reading a khene melody in 2/4 in F major with correct rhythm; reading a khene melody in 3/4 in F major; reading a khene melody in D minor in 2/4; reading a khene melody in D minor in 3/4; reading a khene melody in D minor in 4/4; reading a khene melody in D minor in 4/4, the last of the reading series
2. **Music G9 teacher guide — khene notation dictation** — hearing the F major scale and writing down dictated notes (sets 1A and 1B) in 4/4; writing dictated F major notes in 3/4 and checking a partner's writing; writing dictated F major notes (sets 3A and 3B) in 2/4; writing dictated notes in D minor in 4/4; writing dictated notes in D minor in 2/4
3. **Music G6 teacher guide — khene notation reading** — the C major scale up, down and broken, then a first melody read in 2/4; reading a C major melody in 2/4, naming its signs and intervals; reading a C major melody in 2/4 with rests; reading a C major melody in 2/4; reading a C major melody in 2/4 with dotted rhythms; reading a C major melody in 2/4; reading a C major melody in 2/4; reading a C major melody in 2/4; reading a C major melody in 2/4, the last of the first series
4. **Music G11 teacher guide — khene notation reading** — the purpose and two-step method of reading khene notation (first read the scale notes, then read the melody), with the D major scale as the worked example; reading the D major scale up, down and broken, then a melody in 4/4; reading a D major melody in 4/4; reading a D major melody in 4/4; reading a D major melody in 3/4; reading a D major melody in 3/4, the last of the D series; reading the B minor scale up, down and broken, then a melody in 4/4; reading a B minor melody with repeated-note figures; reading a B minor melody with first and second endings; reading a B minor melody; reading a B minor melody; reading a B minor melody, the last of the Bm series
5. **Music G11 teacher guide — khene notation dictation** — the three steps of dictation (memorise the scale, name the notes heard, write them), worked on D major melodies 1 and 2; writing dictated D major notes, naming each note heard before writing; writing dictated D major notes, the last of the dictation series; the reading method restated and three full dictation melodies in D major written from hearing
6. **Music G7 teacher guide — khene notation reading** — reading the C major scale, then a first melody in 4/4; reading a C major melody in 4/4; reading a C major melody in 2/4; reading a C major melody; reading the A minor scale, then a melody in 2/4; reading an A minor melody in 4/4; reading an A minor melody in 3/4; reading an A minor melody, the last of the series
7. **Music G7 teacher guide — khene notation dictation** — the two preparation steps of dictation (memorise the scale; name the note heard) and writing a first dictated C major melody; writing dictated C major notes in 4/4; writing dictated C major notes in 3/4; writing dictated A minor notes in 4/4; writing dictated A minor notes in 4/4, the last of the series
8. **Music G8 teacher guide, 2012 — khene notation reading** — reading a khene piece in F major from notation and playing it; reading a khene piece in F major from notation and playing it; reading a khene piece in F major from notation and playing it; reading a khene piece in F major from notation and playing it; reading a khene piece in F major from notation and playing it; reading a khene piece in F major from notation, now with loud and soft marks; reading a khene piece in C major from notation and playing it; reading a khene piece in C major from notation and playing it
9. **Music G8 teacher guide, 2012 — khene notation dictation** — hearing a khene melody and writing it down; hearing a khene melody and writing it down; hearing a khene melody and writing it down; hearing a khene melody and writing it down; hearing a khene melody and writing it down
