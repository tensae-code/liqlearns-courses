# Video script — The Khene and Its Notation

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is The Khene and Its Notation.

Laos' khene mouth organ and its own notation — reading khene notation in exercises and writing it from dictation. — as Laos's school books teach it: reading the F major scale, its reverse and broken forms, then a first notated khene melody in 4/4; reading a second F major khene melody in 4/4, alone and together; reading a khene melody in 2/4 in F major, naming the signs used and the highest and lowest sounds; reading a khene melody in 2/4 in F major with correct rhythm; reading a khene melody in 3/4 in F major; reading a khene melody in D minor in 2/4; reading a khene melody in D minor in 3/4; reading a khene melody in D minor in 4/4; reading a khene melody in D minor in 4/4, the last of the reading series

Watch, listen — then say it back in your own words.
