# Hands-on — Songs of Nature

The lesson with your hands, your feet and your voice together, not only your eyes.

## Sing to a tree
Take the class outside to a tree. Sing "ወፎች ዘመሩ" (The Birds Sang) under it, gently. Then stand silent for ten breaths and listen for a real bird answering.

## A plant of my own
Plant a seedling or a flower at school or at home, as the planting song says. Water it every day for a week. Sing the planting song while you water.
