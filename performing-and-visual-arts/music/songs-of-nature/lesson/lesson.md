# Songs of Nature

## Do it first

Go to a door or a window. Look out. Name everything alive that you can see — trees, grass, birds, flowers. All of it is sung in this lesson. Nature is not just looked at here. It is sung.

## Sub lesson 1 — Our surroundings and the birds sang

The opening song is **"አካባቢያችን"** — *Our Surroundings*. It is a group song, and it presents our surroundings as **home and joy**: living together with neighbours, with animals and with birds, helping each other. This is the song that opens all the listening work of the course.

Then comes **"ወፎች ዘመሩ"** — *The Birds Sang*. A whole morning in one song: the wind rises, the trees sway, the leaves fall, and the birds sing in the morning.

Sing the first song standing together, shoulder to shoulder. Sing the second one gently, like the morning it pictures.

## Sub lesson 2 — Birds in flight and the little star

The song **"አዕዋፍ"** — *Birds* — follows the birds through their day: flying, building nests in the grass, spreading their wings and roaming the air, flying in procession, and waking to sing so the whole world delights in their tune. It is sung in groups, as the teacher intones it.

[GIF 9454 — birds flying across the sky, wings rising and falling]

And when the day ends, the **little star** song: **"ጭል ጭል ትንሿ ኮከብ"** — *Twinkle, Twinkle, Little Star*. The little star hangs above the world like a diamond in the sky, shining when the sun's heat fades. The Grade 3 book also prints this same song in English — you will meet that in School Songs.

## Sub lesson 3 — I plant plants and our green land

The song **"እፅዋት እተክላለሁ"** — *I Plant Plants* — is a working song: in my compound and my neighbourhood I plant plants beautifully — green plants, yellow colours, red flowers bright for beauty. My choice beautifies my compound; my flower brightens my neighbourhood; and there is joy when I water what I planted.

[Picture 9465 — our green land: hills, a river, trees and flowers]

The last song is **"የመልከዓ ምድር ልምላሜ"** — *The Greenness of the Land*. Our land is nature's gift and very attractive: the fresh air, the fields and gorges, the river running down and the mountain, the forest dressed in green — the animals' home. And the song ends with a promise we sing together: **let us all care for it together.**

## Say it to your companion

Sing "ወፎች ዘመሩ" (The Birds Sang) to your companion and picture its morning out loud: wind, trees, leaves, birds. Then sing the last promise of the green-land song together: let us all care for it together.

## Do It Yourself

1. Plant something — a flower, a seedling, a bean in wet cloth. Water it, the way the planting song sings.
2. Stand outside in the morning and listen for the birds. Which nature song does the morning sound like?
3. Draw your green land: hills, a river, trees, one animal's home. Sing the green-land song over your drawing.
