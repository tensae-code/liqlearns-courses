# Video script — Songs of Nature

The super-simple voice, for the sing-along video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The songs are named and summarised, never transcribed; the script sings the learner TO the song, not the song itself.

---

Our surroundings are home and joy — sing it together!
The wind rises. The trees sway. The birds sing in the morning.

Birds fly. Birds nest in the grass. Wings spread wide!
And at night — a little star, like a diamond in the sky.

I plant plants. I water them with joy.
Our land is green and beautiful.
Let us all care for it — together!
