# Lesson plan — Songs of Nature

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Music · **Lesson:** Songs of Nature
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 3 — Music (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** songs that sing the world outside — our surroundings as home, birds in flight, the little star, planting plants, and our green land that we must care for together.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Sounds Around Us — the learner knows the sounds of birds and the world outside; now those sounds grow into songs about nature itself.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the
exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an
image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture
of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does
with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and
while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is
dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the
student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph,
or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be
shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved (the gate: README, "Units, Lessons and Sub Lessons (ruled 2026-11-08)"):

1. **Our surroundings and the birds sang** — "አካባቢያችን" (Our Surroundings) — home and joy, living together with neighbours, animals and birds — and "ወፎች ዘመሩ" (The Birds Sang) — wind, swaying trees, falling leaves, morning birdsong.
2. **Birds in flight and the little star** — "አዕዋፍ" (Birds) — flying, nesting in the grass, spreading wings — and "ጭል ጭል ትንሿ ኮከብ" (Twinkle, Twinkle, Little Star) — the little star shining like a diamond when the sun's heat fades.
3. **I plant plants and our green land** — "እፅዋት እተክላለሁ" (I Plant Plants) — planting and watering to beautify home and neighbourhood — and "የመልከዓ ምድር ልምላሜ" (The Greenness of the Land) — our beautiful land, which we all care for together.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Sounds Around Us — the sounds of the world outside. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Sing "አካባቢያችን" (Our Surroundings) and say what it teaches: surroundings are home and joy.
2. Sing "ወፎች ዘመሩ" (The Birds Sang) and picture its morning: wind, trees, leaves, birds.
3. Sing "አዕዋፍ" (Birds) and name what the birds do: fly, nest in the grass, spread their wings, sing.
4. Sing the little-star song and say when the star shines.
5. Sing the planting song and the green-land song, and say how we care for the land.

## What you need
Your voice. If you can, sing these songs outside, under a real tree.

## If the learner is stuck
- Loses the words → go back to the call-and-answer shape: the teacher (or the audio) sings a short piece, the learner answers with the same piece. Short pieces first, whole song after.
- Loses the tune → hum the song together before singing words; the tune first, the words ride on it.
- Sings but cannot say what the song teaches → ask the lesson's own question: what is this song about, and what does it ask us to do? Every song in this unit teaches something that can be said in one sentence.
