# Songs of Nature — short answer

Answer in a few words, out loud or in writing.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the four things in the morning picture of The Birds Sang.
**Answer:** Wind rising, trees swaying, leaves falling, birds singing.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Why does the singer of I Plant Plants feel joy?
**Answer:** Joy when watering the plants they planted — the compound and neighbourhood are made beautiful.
