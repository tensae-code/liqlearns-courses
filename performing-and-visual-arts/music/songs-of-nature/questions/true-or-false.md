# Songs of Nature — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Our Surroundings is a group song about living together with neighbours, animals and birds.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The birds song is sung in groups, as the teacher intones it.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The little star is compared to a diamond in the sky.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** The green-land song says the land is only for animals, not for us to care for.
**Answer: False** — It is the animals' home — and we all care for it together.
