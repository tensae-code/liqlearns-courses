# Songs of Nature — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** "አካባቢያችን" (Our Surroundings) presents our surroundings as —
a) a danger  b) home and joy  c) a market
**Answer: b) home and joy**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In "ወፎች ዘመሩ" (The Birds Sang), what happens first in the picture?
a) the rain falls  b) the wind rises and the trees sway  c) the stars come out
**Answer: b) the wind rises and the trees sway**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** In "አዕዋፍ" (Birds), the birds build their nests —
a) on the roof  b) in the grass  c) in the river
**Answer: b) in the grass**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The little star shines —
a) at midday  b) when the sun's heat fades  c) only in winter
**Answer: b) when the sun's heat fades**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** The planting song plants —
a) only trees  b) green plants, yellow colours and red flowers  c) stones
**Answer: b) green plants, yellow colours and red flowers**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** The green-land song ends with the promise —
a) to sell the land  b) to care for it together, all of us  c) to fence it shut
**Answer: b) to care for it together, all of us**
