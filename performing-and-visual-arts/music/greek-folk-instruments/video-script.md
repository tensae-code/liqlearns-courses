This lesson is Greek Folk Instruments.

Greece's folk instruments — violin, santouri and their companions — met by sound and name. — as Greece's school books teach it: the instruments of Greek demotic (folk) music — most regions of Greece have their own folk music and their own instruments.

Watch, listen — then say it back in your own words.
