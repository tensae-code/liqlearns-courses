## Do it first

## Sub lesson 1 — 22. Γνωρίζοντας τα ελληνικά λαϊκά μουσικά όργανα (Meeting the Greek folk instruments)

the instruments of Greek demotic (folk) music — most regions of Greece have their own folk music and their own instruments

## Sub lesson 2 — Με βιολί σαντουροβιόλι (With violin and santouro-violin)

naming Greek folk instruments by ear alone — gaida, tsambouna, Cretan lyra and the Pontic lyra; then santouri, kanonaki, zournas, clarino, violin and daouli; accompanying the tsamikos song «Ο Όρλιγκας» with the printed rhythm pattern

## Sub lesson 3 — Chapter 22: Γνωρίζοντας τα ελληνικά λαϊκά μουσικά όργανα (getting to know the Greek folk musical instruments)

Greece's folk instruments — violin, santouri and their companions — met by sound and name.
