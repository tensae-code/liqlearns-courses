# Hands-on — Greek Folk Instruments

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the lesson's thing
Work through the lesson's first part yourself: 22. Γνωρίζοντας τα ελληνικά λαϊκά μουσικά όργανα (Meeting the Greek folk instruments). Show a companion what you made or did, and say what the book calls it.

## Show and tell
Each learner shows their work and names the part of the lesson it comes from. The companion checks the name against the lesson.
