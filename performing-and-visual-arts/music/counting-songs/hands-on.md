# Hands-on — Counting Songs

The lesson with your hands, your feet and your voice together, not only your eyes.

## Five stones row
Put five stones in a row. Sing Five Green Bottles; at each verse lay one stone gently on its side and shout the new count. Reset the row standing and sing again, faster.

## Duck fingers
Sing Five Little Ducks holding up the right number of fingers for the ducks who went out; at the mother's call, fold the missing duck's finger down and hold up only those who came back.
