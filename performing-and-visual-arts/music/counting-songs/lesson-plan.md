# Lesson plan — Counting Songs

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Music · **Lesson:** Counting Songs
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 3 — Music (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** maths sung backwards: five little ducks who come back one fewer each verse, and five green bottles that fall one by one — counting back from five to zero by song.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on counting forwards, which the learner already owns from the Maths course; these songs turn the count around and walk it backwards.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved (the gate: README, "Units, Lessons and Sub Lessons (ruled 2026-11-08)"):

1. **Five Little Ducks** — "አምስት ዳክየዎች" (Five Little Ducks) — five ducks play following their mother; she calls quack quack; one fewer comes back each verse, down through four, three, two, one.
2. **Five Green Bottles** — "አምስት አረንጓዴ ጠርሙሶች" (Five Green Bottles) — if one falls and breaks, how many remain? Sung down through four, three, two, one, to zero.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: counting forwards to five and beyond, in the Maths course. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Sing Five Little Ducks and count the returning ducks back: 4, 3, 2, 1.
2. Sing Five Green Bottles and answer 'how many remain?' down to zero.
3. Say what 'counting back' means: each verse, one fewer.
4. Count back from 5 to 0 without the song.

## What you need
Your voice, and five fingers — the class's counting tool. A row of five safe objects (stones, cups) helps the bottles verse; no real glass.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — Hold up five fingers. Now sing any tune and fold one finger down: four. Fold another: three. You are counting back. Both songs in this lesson do exactly that — one folds ducks, the other folds bottles. (lesson/lesson.md).

## If the learner is stuck
- Loses the words → go back to the call-and-answer shape: the teacher (or the audio) sings a short piece, the learner answers with the same piece. Short pieces first, whole song after.
- Loses the tune → hum the song together before singing words; the tune first, the words ride on it.
- Sings but cannot say what the song teaches → ask the lesson's own question: what is this song about, and what does it ask us to do? Every song in this unit teaches something that can be said in one sentence.
