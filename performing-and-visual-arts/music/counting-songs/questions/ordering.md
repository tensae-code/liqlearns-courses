# Counting Songs — ordering

Put the things in the right order.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Put the bottles count in the song's order.
Here they are, mixed up: 0 remain; 1 remains; 2 remain; 3 remain; 4 remain; 5 remain.
**Answer (right order):** 5 remain → 4 remain → 3 remain → 2 remain → 1 remains → 0 remain
