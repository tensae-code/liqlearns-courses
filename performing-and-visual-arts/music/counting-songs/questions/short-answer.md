# Counting Songs — short answer

Answer in a few words, out loud or in writing.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What sound does the mother duck make when she calls her ducks back?
**Answer:** Quack, quack.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Sing-count the bottles row from the start to the end.
**Answer:** 5, 4, 3, 2, 1, 0.
