# Counting Songs

## Do it first

Hold up five fingers. Now sing any tune and fold one finger down: four. Fold another: three. You are counting back. Both songs in this lesson do exactly that — one folds ducks, the other folds bottles.

## Sub lesson 1 — Five Little Ducks

The first song is **"አምስት ዳክየዎች"** — *Five Little Ducks*.

Five little ducks go out to play, following their mother. She calls — **quack, quack** — and the ducks come back. But not all of them: **only four come back. One remains behind.**

[Picture 9460 — a mother duck and her five little ducks on the water]

Sing it again: four ducks go out, the mother calls, only three come back. Then three go out — two come back. Then two — one comes back. Each verse, the line of ducks is one shorter. Your fingers can count the story as you sing: five, four, three, two, one.

## Sub lesson 2 — Five Green Bottles

The second song is **"አምስት አረንጓዴ ጠርሙሶች"** — *Five Green Bottles*.

Five green bottles stand in a row. The song asks its question every verse: **if one falls and breaks, how many remain?** Sing the answer: four remain. Then one more falls — three remain. Then two remain. Then one. Then the last one falls, and the row is empty: **zero**.

[GIF 9459 — five green bottles in a row; one falls, and the count drops from 5 to 4]

Watch the fall and count with the song: 5, 4, 3, 2, 1, 0. Counting back always ends at zero — the empty row, the folded-down last finger.

## Say it to your companion

Sing Five Little Ducks to your companion, counting the returning ducks on your fingers. Then ask the bottles question — if one falls, how many remain? — down the whole row to zero, and let your companion answer each verse.

## Do It Yourself

1. Sing Five Green Bottles with five cups or stones in a row. At each verse, lay one gently down and say how many remain.
2. Count back from 5 to 0 out loud, folding one finger at a time. Then do it from 10.
3. Teach Five Little Ducks to a younger child, with the finger counting.
