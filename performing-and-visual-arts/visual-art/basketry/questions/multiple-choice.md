# Basketry — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Basketry is…
a) a fibre art  b) a metal craft  c) a painting style
**Answer: a) a fibre art.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which list names the book's four weaving techniques?
a) coiling, plaiting, wicker and twining  b) cutting, gluing, folding and tearing  c) casting, firing, glazing and polishing
**Answer: a) coiling, plaiting, wicker and twining.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Plaiting works by…
a) crossing flat strands over and under  b) melting fibres together  c) carving a solid block
**Answer: a) crossing flat strands over and under.**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Patterned baskets are made…
a) to serve purposes in the cultural setting  b) only to be thrown away  c) only for examinations
**Answer: a) to serve purposes in the cultural setting.**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** A basket maker must maintain…
a) a continuous supply of materials  b) a shop in the capital  c) a written licence
**Answer: a) a continuous supply of materials.**
