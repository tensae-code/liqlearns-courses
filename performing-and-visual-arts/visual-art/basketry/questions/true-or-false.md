# Basketry — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Basketry is taught as a craft of the natural environment.
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Twining is one of the four techniques the book names.
**Answer: True.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Once a basket is made, materials never need gathering again.
**Answer: False — the maker maintains a continuous supply of materials.**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Baskets in this lesson are made only as decoration, never for use.
**Answer: False — they serve purposes in the cultural setting, for use and for sale.**
