# Basketry — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the four weaving techniques.
**Answer: Coiling, plaiting, wicker and twining.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Where do basketry's materials come from?
**Answer: From the natural environment — the maker's own surroundings.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** For what two ends are the woven articles made?
**Answer: For use and for sale, serving purposes in the cultural setting.**
