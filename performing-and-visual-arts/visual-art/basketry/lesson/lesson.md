## Sub lesson 1 — Fibre arts from the natural environment

Uganda's Senior One book teaches **basketry** inside **Crafts from the Natural Environment**: basketry is a **fibre art** — woven articles made from the materials of your own surroundings, in your own cultural setting.

In your mind's eye: baskets of different weaves, with the grasses and reeds they were made from.

[Picture 39029 — baskets and what they are made]

## Sub lesson 2 — Four weaving techniques: coiling, plaiting, wicker and twining

The book names and pictures four weaving techniques: **coiling, plaiting, wicker and twining**. Each builds the basket a different way — a coil wound and stitched round, flat strands crossed over and under, stiff rods woven, or two weavers twisted around uprights.

## Sub lesson 3 — Baskets for use and for sale — and keeping the supply

The woven articles — **patterned baskets** — are made **to serve purposes in the cultural setting**, for use and for sale. And the craft keeps going only if the maker **maintains a continuous supply of materials** — gathering and keeping fibres is part of the art.
