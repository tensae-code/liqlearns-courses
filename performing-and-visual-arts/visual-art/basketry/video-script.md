# Video script — Basketry

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Grass in your hands. Coil it round. Plait it flat. Weave it strong.

Four ways — coiling, plaiting, wicker, twining.

A basket grows — patterned, useful, made to carry and to sell.

That is basketry.
