# Hands-on — Basketry

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Try two techniques
With grass, reeds or paper strips, start one piece by coiling and one by plaiting. Say how the two ways feel different in your hands.

## A basket with a job
Plan a patterned basket for one real purpose in your home or school. Name the technique you would use and where its materials come from.
