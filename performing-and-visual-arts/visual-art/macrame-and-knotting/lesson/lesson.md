# Macramé and Knotting

## Do it first

## Sub lesson 1 — Braiding with three, four and five strips

Braiding (trançagem) with three, four and five strips, step by step: fix the strips' top ends, pass the left strip over the centre, then the right, in order, and finish by cutting, folding back and tucking in the ends.
