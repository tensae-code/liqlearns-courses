# Video script — Macramé and Knotting

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

**Braiding with three, four and five strips.** Braiding (trançagem) with three, four and five strips, step by step: fix the strips' top ends, pass the left strip over the centre, then the right, in order, and finish by cutting, folding back and tucking in the ends.

That is Macramé and Knotting — Angola's own books teach it this way.
