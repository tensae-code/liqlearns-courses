# Hands-on — Macramé and Knotting

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Braiding with three, four and five strips
Work through this part with real materials or your own body, the way the book sets it: braiding (trançagem) with three, four and five strips, step by step: fix the strips' top ends, pass the left strip over the centre, then the right, in order, and finish by cutting, folding back and tucking in the ends. Show a companion what you made or did, and say what the part teaches.
