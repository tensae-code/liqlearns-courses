This lesson is Encaustic Wax Painting.

Encaustic Wax Painting — Encaustic — painting with hot wax, an ancient technique still practised today. — as Kazakhstan's school books teach it: what it teaches: Энкаустика is a method of drawing and fixing a picture by melting paints mixed with wax; when working by the encaustic method, specially prepared tempera (балауыз бен араластырылған — mixed with wax) is used, distinguished by its peculiarity; encaustic's most widespread kind is considered the mummy portraits (Фаюмдік портреттер — Fayum portraits) painted on wooden boards after the deceased's death; materials: paper, an iron (үтік), wax pencils (балауыз қарындаштар); technology: take a definite-coloured or varied-coloured wax pencils, heat the iron's sole (жылы үтіктің табанын жай немесе нүктелер, өрнектер түсіру керек), then drive the heated iron on the paper face or press it; in the work's course for doing big works use the iron's sole, for small works use the iron's tip (үштігін) — likewise the methods of driving (жүргізу), pressing-taking (басып алу), drawing with the tip (ұшымен жүргізу) are used; another interesting method is pictured: dripping candle wax on thick white paper or cardboard and melting crayons with a hairdryer (фен) so the pencils melt and the wax flows downward (materials: қалың ақ картон қағаз, желім, фен; technology: glue the pencils in a row at the paper's top edge, hold them so they do not separate from each other; when the glue dries turn the top upward, add glue, then with the hairdryer melt the pencils from above downward — the pencils melt and the wax begins to flow downward slowly).
