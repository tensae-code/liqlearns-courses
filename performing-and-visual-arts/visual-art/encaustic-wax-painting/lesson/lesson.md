# Encaustic Wax Painting

## Sub lesson 1 — Kazakhstan Grade 9 NIS Art (Akhmetkalieva)

what it teaches: Энкаустика is a method of drawing and fixing a picture by melting paints mixed with wax; when working by the encaustic method, specially prepared tempera (балауыз бен араластырылған — mixed with wax) is used, distinguished by its peculiarity; encaustic's most widespread kind is considered the mummy portraits (Фаюмдік портреттер — Fayum portraits) painted on wooden boards after the deceased's death; materials: paper, an iron (үтік), wax pencils (балауыз қарындаштар); technology: take a definite-coloured or varied-coloured wax pencils, heat the iron's sole (жылы үтіктің табанын жай немесе нүктелер, өрнектер түсіру керек), then drive the heated iron on the paper face or press it; in the work's course for doing big works use the iron's sole, for small works use the iron's tip (үштігін) — likewise the methods of driving (жүргізу), pressing-taking (басып алу), drawing with the tip (ұшымен жүргізу) are used; another interesting method is pictured: dripping candle wax on thick white paper or cardboard and melting crayons with a hairdryer (фен) so the pencils melt and the wax flows downward (materials: қалың ақ картон қағаз, желім, фен; technology: glue the pencils in a row at the paper's top edge, hold them so they do not separate from each other; when the glue dries turn the top upward, add glue, then with the hairdryer melt the pencils from above downward — the pencils melt and the wax begins to flow downward slowly); what it teaches: in encaustic there are several methods of working with an iron (үтікпен жұмыс істеудің бірнеше тәсілі бар): Үтіктеу (ironing) — this is a method of drawing a picture with a heated iron on cardboard's face; for it first join the iron, put its heat regulator to the lowest temperature; holding the iron's sole upward, apply the wax pencil (балауыз қарындашты жақ); the pencil melts, when it begins to run from the iron's sole, begin drawing the picture; the iron must be driven on the paper's face slowly and evenly; Баспа-таңба (a print-stamp) — a method of drawing a picture by the iron's sole quickly pressing-taking on paper: for it put the iron's sole on the cardboard's face, then lift it quickly; between the iron and the cardboard air enters, and on the paper's face a relief picture arises; Үтік қырымен жұмыс істеу әдісі (working with the iron's edge) — through it varied lines are drawn, branches and leaves are divided; for drawing first apply the wax paint to the cardboard; then drive the heated iron's edge along it — so varied lines can be drawn; Үтік ұшымен жұмыс істеу арқылы (through working with the iron's tip) small, thin details (flowers, small animals) can be drawn; the safety rules for working with an iron are given
