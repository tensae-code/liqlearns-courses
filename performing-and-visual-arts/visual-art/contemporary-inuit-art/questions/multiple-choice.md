# Contemporary Inuit Art — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Inuit art today…
a) has stopped growing  b) keeps growing into new forms alongside the old  c) has replaced every old form
**Answer: b) keeps growing into new forms alongside the old** — new forms grow alongside the old.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The book's spread of today's art shows…
a) only oil paintings  b) beaded sealskin bags, crocheted work, prints and silver  c) only photographs
**Answer: b) beaded sealskin bags, crocheted work, prints and silver** — beaded sealskin bags, crocheted work, prints and silver in one spread.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Inuit artists' work today is…
a) known only in Nunavut  b) known around the world  c) no longer made
**Answer: b) known around the world** — known around the world.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Crocheted work appears in the book as…
a) not art at all  b) one of today's Inuit art forms  c) a southern import with no place
**Answer: b) one of today's Inuit art forms** — crocheted work stands in the spread of today's art.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The new forms grow…
a) instead of the old forms, which are dropped  b) alongside the old forms  c) only in museums
**Answer: b) alongside the old forms** — alongside the old — the old forms continue.
