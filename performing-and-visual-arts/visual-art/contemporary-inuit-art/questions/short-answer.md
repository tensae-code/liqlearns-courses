# Contemporary Inuit Art — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name two new-form works in the book's spread of today's art.
**Answer: Any two of: beaded sealskin bags, crocheted work, prints, silver work.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How far does Inuit art reach today?
**Answer: It is known around the world.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** What Inuktitut heading does the book give today's art?
**Answer: ᑕᑯᒥᓇᖅᓴᐃᑦ — today's art.**
