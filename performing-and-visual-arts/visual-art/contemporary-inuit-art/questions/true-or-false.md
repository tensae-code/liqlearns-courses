# Contemporary Inuit Art — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Today's Inuit art includes crocheted work and beaded sealskin bags.
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** When new forms arrive, the old Inuit forms are finished.
**Answer: False — the new forms grow alongside the old.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Inuit artists' work is known around the world.
**Answer: True.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Prints belong only to the old art, never to today's.
**Answer: False — prints stand in the book's spread of today's art too.**
