# Hands-on — Contemporary Inuit Art

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Old beside new
Lay out two columns: an old form from the Inuit Arts and Crafts lesson and a new form from this one. Say what carries across — the subjects and the ways.

## Your new form
Make a small piece that joins an old Inuit subject (an Arctic animal or tool shape) with a material or form new to you.
