# Hands-on — Painting Still Life

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Arrange and look
Arrange a pot, a cup and a fruit on a table. Walk around the table and look from three sides. Choose the side where the group looks best — that composed arrangement is your model. Say out loud what stands in front, what behind, and where the light falls.

## Draw it lightly
On thick paper on a support board, draw the arrangement lightly in pencil — big shapes first, details last. If a line is wrong, erase and draw again; the drawing is the map, not the picture.

## Paint, lightest first
Pour the colours you need onto a palette. Paint the lightest colour first, then the next, waiting for drying as you go. Keep clean water and a cloth beside you. Finish the painting and let it dry flat.
