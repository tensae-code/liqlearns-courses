# Video script — Painting Still Life


The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.


---


Arrange three things on a table. Move them… until the group pleases your eye.

That is a **still life** — simple objects, composed, studied, painted.

But where do the colours come from? From **light**!

Light through a prism breaks into seven colours: red, orange, yellow, green, blue, indigo, violet. The rainbow is the same spectrum in the sky.

Now paint. Draw the arrangement **lightly** first.

Pour your colours on the palette. Start from the **lightest** colour. Wait for drying as you go.

Arrange… draw… paint… finish. Four stages. One painting. Yours.
