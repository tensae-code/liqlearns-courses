# Painting Still Life — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In a still life, the objects are arranged by the painter before the painting begins.
**Answer: True** — a composed arrangement with composed design — arranging is part of the work.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Without light, colours still show clearly.
**Answer: False** — without light all is dark; colour's source is light.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The pencil drawing for a still life should be drawn heavily and darkly.
**Answer: False** — draw the arrangement LIGHTLY in pencil on thick paper.

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** In the practice set you may skip the light drawing and begin with the darkest colour.
**Answer: False** — the stages stand: draw lightly, paint from the lightest colour.

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** In the guide's account, painting is pigment + medium.
**Answer: True** — pigment + medium — with canvas, brushes, palette, sponge and palette knives as equipment.
