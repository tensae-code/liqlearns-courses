# Painting Still Life — short answer

Answer in a sentence or a short list.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name two simple objects from your own home that could stand in a still-life arrangement.
**Answer:** Any two simple objects found around us — e.g. a pot and a cup (the book's point: the varied simple objects found around us).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the seven spectrum colours in order.
**Answer:** Red, orange, yellow, green, blue, indigo (ሰማያዊ ወይነጠጅ), violet.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name four tools a still-life painter needs.
**Answer:** Any four of: thick drawing paper, watercolour, palette, clean water, cloth piece, pencil, eraser, sharpener, support board.

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** After finishing a painting, what two things do you compare, and why?
**Answer:** The painting and its model arrangement — to see what the painting caught and what it missed, so the next painting does better.

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** Name two painting media from the guide's equipment list that the Grade 6 book did not name.
**Answer:** Any two of: acrylic, gouache, tempera, oil, poster colour.
