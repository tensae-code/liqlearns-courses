# Patterns and Texture — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** A khee pattern is composed from…
a) line arrangements — straight, zigzag, arc and curved lines  b) random scribbles only  c) numbers
**Answer: a) line arrangements — straight, zigzag, arc and curved lines**

**2.** Wave and zigzag bands are drawn across a strip of…
a) 5×10 cm, the spaces between coloured differently  b) 1×1 m  c) a whole wall
**Answer: a) 5×10 cm, the spaces between coloured differently**
