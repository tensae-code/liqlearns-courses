# Lesson plan — Patterns and Texture

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Visual Art · **Lesson:** Patterns and Texture
**Place in the curriculum:** Visual Art (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** patterns and repeating shapes — composing a khee pattern with lines, and building designs from repeated shapes.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the learner's earlier art and music lessons. Recommended, never forced.
