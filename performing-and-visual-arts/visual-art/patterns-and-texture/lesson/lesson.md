Part I: Дүрс олшруулан будах (Colouring multiplied shapes) — folding the paper to multiply squares and triangles into a grid, then colouring every cell a different colour

## Sub lesson 2 — Part I: Зураасаар хээ зохиох (Composing a khee pattern with lines)

Part I: Зураасаар хээ зохиох (Composing a khee pattern with lines) — deciding from examples which line arrangements make a pattern: straight, zigzag, arc and curved line patterns (and which crossings fail); wave and zigzag bands drawn across a 5×10 cm strip and the spaces between coloured differently
