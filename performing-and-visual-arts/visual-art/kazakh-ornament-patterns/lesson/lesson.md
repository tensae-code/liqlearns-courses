# Kazakh Ornament Patterns

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about kazakh ornament patterns. Then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — Kazakhstan Grade 1 Visual Art Guide

Kazakh national decorative art; rhythm and symmetry; drawing a simple ornament in a strip with the brush; cutting a symmetrical shape from coloured paper and felt; the ram's-horn motif of Kazakh ornament

## Sub lesson 2 — Kazakhstan Grade 1 Labour Guide

a first Kazakh ornament made by hand, national-culture elements named in the making; travelling through the ornament patterns and naming their national elements

## Sub lesson 3 — Kazakhstan Grade 1 Labour Guide RU

composing a template of the Kazakh national ornament; cutting ornaments from paper; composing a Kazakh ornament; making the geometric "star" ornament; Kazakh ornament runs through the Russian-medium year as template, cutting, composition and star

## Sub lesson 4 — Kazakhstan Grade 1 Ermilova Art Labour

the kinds of Kazakh ornament (animal-like, plant-like, geometric, cosmogonic), ornament as rhythm and symmetry, and making an ayakkap (decorated felt/leather bag) with a cut symmetrical ornament; cutting a geometric/plant ornament and decorating your instrument with it; then mounting an exhibition with labels (zatbelgi) for each instrument

## Sub lesson 5 — Kazakhstan Grade 1 Raupova Art Labour

Kazakh ornament read on real craft objects; ornament as the mark of the people's crafts

## Sub lesson 6 — Kazakhstan Grade 2 Visual Art Guide, plan pp. 7-13

"Ornament in a circle": Kazakh ornament fitted inside a round shape; "Ornament in a triangle": Kazakh ornament fitted inside a triangular shape; "A blanket with ornament": a patchwork blanket carrying Kazakh ornament

## Sub lesson 7 — Kazakhstan Grade 2 Labour Guide KZ

"Ornament": the Kazakh ornament drawn

## Sub lesson 8 — Kazakhstan Grade 2 Labour Guide RU

ornament; ornament on a template; decorating an object; a decorative plate

## Sub lesson 9 — Kazakhstan Grade 2 Korolkova Art Labour

a Kazakh chest (sandyk) decorated and embroidered with ornament; ornament carried across wood, felt and thread

## Sub lesson 10 — Kazakhstan Grade 2 Raupova Art Labour, Term 3 lessons

Kazakh ornament in a strip and in a circle, on crafts

## Sub lesson 11 — Kazakhstan Grade 2 Ermilova Art Labour (Uyghur), contents pp. 4-5

the magic trunk decorated and embroidered; the shanyrak

## Sub lesson 12 — Kazakhstan Grade 3 Muratkyzy Labour

what oyu-ornek is, its use on the yurt and household things; the qoshqarmuiz (ram's horn) ornament drawn and cut step by step

## Sub lesson 13 — Kazakhstan Grade 3 Tolebiev Visual Art

the four families of Kazakh ornament (zoomorphic, plant, geometric, cosmogonic) with their named motifs charted

## Sub lesson 14 — Kazakhstan Grade 3 Zhanaev Visual Art

composition and the ornament families (plant, zoomorphic, geometric, cosmogonic) with an oyu-ornek composition painted

## Sub lesson 15 — Kazakhstan Grade 3 Syzdykov Labour

Kazakhstan", Topic 5 "Baskur" — the yurt's woven baskur band and its ornaments (qoshqarmuiz, gul, synykmuiz) and their meanings; an oyu-ornek cut from folded paper

## Sub lesson 16 — Kazakhstan Grade 3 Rabilova Visual Art

my homeland", Lessons 5-6 "Kazakh national ornament in a band" and "in a circle" — symmetry and rhythm in the band; the ornament kinds charted (astronomical, plant, zoomorphic, geometric) with named motifs; a plate decorated with a circular ornament; snowflake geometry; dot painting (toчечная роспись) — the cosmogonic ornament (circle-sun, star, crescent) dotted onto mittens; the basis of the language of painting", "Material for painting — gouache. A woven band", "Plant and animal elements of the national ornament. Tabak" — geometric ornament drawn with colour shades; a baskur band painted in gouache; a tabak plate drawn and decorated in a circle

## Sub lesson 17 — Kazakhstan Grade 3 Raupova Art Labour

the source of life": Water in the national oyu-ornek (the su örkesh wave pattern); the marina genre (Aivazovsky, Turner); how the sea is depicted (Van Gogh); the sea in pointillism, and a "sea in a bottle" made

## Sub lesson 18 — Kazakhstan Grade 4 Mamyrova Visual Art

decorative-applied art: form, colour, decor; national dishes (Kazakh, Chinese, Greek) compared; a teapot of one's own shape designed and ornamented

## Sub lesson 19 — Kazakhstan Grade 4 Ormanbekova Labour

the yurt interior and its objects; a syrmak made in the mosaic technique (the "Zhuldizsha" cosmogonic and "Botagoz" geometric ornaments)
