# Lesson plan — Kazakh Ornament Patterns

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Visual Art · **Lesson:** Kazakh Ornament Patterns
**Place in the curriculum:** Visual Art (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Kazakh ornament — national patterns such as the qoshqarmuiz ram's-horn, worked in strips, gouache and felt..
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this course in the same title.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Kazakhstan Grade 1 Visual Art Guide** — Kazakh national decorative art; rhythm and symmetry; drawing a simple ornament in a strip with the brush; cutting a symmetrical shape from coloured paper and felt; the ram's-horn motif of Kazakh ornament
2. **Kazakhstan Grade 1 Labour Guide** — a first Kazakh ornament made by hand, national-culture elements named in the making; travelling through the ornament patterns and naming their national elements
3. **Kazakhstan Grade 1 Labour Guide RU** — composing a template of the Kazakh national ornament; cutting ornaments from paper; composing a Kazakh ornament; making the geometric "star" ornament; Kazakh ornament runs through the Russian-medium year as template, cutting, composition and star
4. **Kazakhstan Grade 1 Ermilova Art Labour** — the kinds of Kazakh ornament (animal-like, plant-like, geometric, cosmogonic), ornament as rhythm and symmetry, and making an ayakkap (decorated felt/leather bag) with a cut symmetrical ornament; cutting a geometric/plant ornament and decorating your instrument with it; then mounting an exhibition with labels (zatbelgi) for each instrument
5. **Kazakhstan Grade 1 Raupova Art Labour** — Kazakh ornament read on real craft objects; ornament as the mark of the people's crafts
6. **Kazakhstan Grade 2 Visual Art Guide, plan pp. 7-13** — "Ornament in a circle": Kazakh ornament fitted inside a round shape; "Ornament in a triangle": Kazakh ornament fitted inside a triangular shape; "A blanket with ornament": a patchwork blanket carrying Kazakh ornament
7. **Kazakhstan Grade 2 Labour Guide KZ** — "Ornament": the Kazakh ornament drawn
8. **Kazakhstan Grade 2 Labour Guide RU** — ornament; ornament on a template; decorating an object; a decorative plate
9. **Kazakhstan Grade 2 Korolkova Art Labour** — a Kazakh chest (sandyk) decorated and embroidered with ornament; ornament carried across wood, felt and thread
10. **Kazakhstan Grade 2 Raupova Art Labour, Term 3 lessons** — Kazakh ornament in a strip and in a circle, on crafts
11. **Kazakhstan Grade 2 Ermilova Art Labour (Uyghur), contents pp. 4-5** — the magic trunk decorated and embroidered; the shanyrak
12. **Kazakhstan Grade 3 Muratkyzy Labour** — what oyu-ornek is, its use on the yurt and household things; the qoshqarmuiz (ram's horn) ornament drawn and cut step by step
13. **Kazakhstan Grade 3 Tolebiev Visual Art** — the four families of Kazakh ornament (zoomorphic, plant, geometric, cosmogonic) with their named motifs charted
14. **Kazakhstan Grade 3 Zhanaev Visual Art** — composition and the ornament families (plant, zoomorphic, geometric, cosmogonic) with an oyu-ornek composition painted
15. **Kazakhstan Grade 3 Syzdykov Labour** — Kazakhstan", Topic 5 "Baskur" — the yurt's woven baskur band and its ornaments (qoshqarmuiz, gul, synykmuiz) and their meanings; an oyu-ornek cut from folded paper
16. **Kazakhstan Grade 3 Rabilova Visual Art** — my homeland", Lessons 5-6 "Kazakh national ornament in a band" and "in a circle" — symmetry and rhythm in the band; the ornament kinds charted (astronomical, plant, zoomorphic, geometric) with named motifs; a plate decorated with a circular ornament; snowflake geometry; dot painting (toчечная роспись) — the cosmogonic ornament (circle-sun, star, crescent) dotted onto mittens; the basis of the language of painting", "Material for painting — gouache. A woven band", "Plant and animal elements of the national ornament. Tabak" — geometric ornament drawn with colour shades; a baskur band painted in gouache; a tabak plate drawn and decorated in a circle
17. **Kazakhstan Grade 3 Raupova Art Labour** — the source of life": Water in the national oyu-ornek (the su örkesh wave pattern); the marina genre (Aivazovsky, Turner); how the sea is depicted (Van Gogh); the sea in pointillism, and a "sea in a bottle" made
18. **Kazakhstan Grade 4 Mamyrova Visual Art** — decorative-applied art: form, colour, decor; national dishes (Kazakh, Chinese, Greek) compared; a teapot of one's own shape designed and ornamented
19. **Kazakhstan Grade 4 Ormanbekova Labour** — the yurt interior and its objects; a syrmak made in the mosaic technique (the "Zhuldizsha" cosmogonic and "Botagoz" geometric ornaments)
