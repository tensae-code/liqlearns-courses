# Video script — The Photographic Darkroom

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This is North Macedonia's own lesson.

**Развивање.** Развивање — developing the film: developer and fixer at exactly 20 °C, washing and drying — how the invisible picture becomes a negative.

**Темна комора.** Темна комора — the darkroom: printing the positive with the enlarger under red light, and the test strip that finds the right exposure.

That is The Photographic Darkroom — North Macedonia's way, kept by name.
