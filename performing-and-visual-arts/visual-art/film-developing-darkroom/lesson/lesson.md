# The Photographic Darkroom

## Do it first

Name one thing that belongs in this lesson — The darkroom — developing film, controlling the negative, and printing photographs in the darkroom. Say what it is in your own words. If you can name one, you are already inside the lesson.

## Sub lesson 1 — Развивање

Развивање — developing the film: developer and fixer at exactly 20 °C, washing and drying — how the invisible picture becomes a negative.

## Sub lesson 2 — Темна комора

Темна комора — the darkroom: printing the positive with the enlarger under red light, and the test strip that finds the right exposure.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson holds, part by part, in your own words. Companion checks: did every part get named, with its Macedonian name kept where the book gives one?

## Do It Yourself

1. Choose one named part of this lesson and find out one more true thing about it — from a person who knows it, or from the book your part came from.
2. Make or perform a small piece in the way of that part: a rhythm, a step, a drawing, a shaped piece of clay.
3. Write one sentence: what does this part show about North Macedonia's own way of teaching its arts?
