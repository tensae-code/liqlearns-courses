# Video script — Public Art and Murals

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Art is leaving the building.

Once, art stayed in the **private rooms of kings and collectors**. Now — **el arte sale a la calle** — art goes out into the **street**, into the open air, where everyone who passes is its public.

A **painted skate bowl** in **Medellín's Parque del Río**. Another in **Ipanema**.

Your turn: **intervene the leisure spaces near where you live**.
