# Public Art and Murals — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Who is the public of open-air art?
**Answer: Everyone who passes — it stands in the open air, not in private rooms.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the two painted skate bowls the guide gives.
**Answer: Parque del Río (Medellín) and Ipanema (Brazil).**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What does 'intervening' a leisure space mean here?
**Answer: Making art in/on it — changing the space with art, as the painted bowls do.**
