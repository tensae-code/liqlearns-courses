# Public Art and Murals — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In the guide, art leaves the private rooms of…
a) kings and collectors  b) teachers  c) farmers only
**Answer: a) kings and collectors**.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** "El arte sale a la calle" means art goes out into…
a) the street  b) the kiln  c) the staff
**Answer: a) the street**.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Parque del Río, with its painted skate bowl, is in…
a) Medellín  b) Bogotá's Forbidden City  c) Ipanema
**Answer: a) Medellín**.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The guide's task is to intervene…
a) the leisure spaces near where you live  b) a king's palace  c) an examination hall
**Answer: a) the leisure spaces near where you live**.
