# Lesson plan — Public Art and Murals

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Visual Art · **Lesson:** Public Art and Murals
**Place in the curriculum:** Visual Art (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** art in public space: how art left the private rooms of kings and collectors for the open air — painted skate bowls and the leisure spaces near where you live.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this course in the same title.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Art leaves the private rooms** — "El arte sale a la calle" — **art goes out into the street**: art leaves **the private rooms of kings and collectors for the open air**, where everyone who passes is its public.
2. **Painted skate bowls and intervening your own spaces** — The guide's examples are places of play: **painted skate bowls — Parque del Río, Medellín, and Ipanema, Brazil** — and the task it sets: **intervening the leisure spaces near where you live**.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of this course in the same title. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say what art leaves the private rooms teaches, in the book's own way.
2. Say what painted skate bowls and intervening your own spaces teaches, in the book's own way.
## What you need
Paper and pencil, and one public leisure space near your home or school in mind.
