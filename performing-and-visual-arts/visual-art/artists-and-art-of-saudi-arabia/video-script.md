# Video script — Artists and Art of Saudi Arabia

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Every country has its artists. Saudi Arabia's books name theirs with pride.

Bashrahil's dough fish. Al-Salim's golden desert. Radwi, Mosly, Al-Mis'ari.

They paint the land they live in — desert, mountain, sea and terrace.

And they paint the homeland's story — its founding, its growth, its National Day.

Meet your own country's artists. One day, a book may name you.
