# Hands-on — Cave Art and Church Painting

The lesson with your hands, your feet and your voice together, not only your eyes.

## Earth-colour palette
Collect the cave painter's colours from around you: charcoal black, red soil, yellow earth, white stone dust. Paint with a finger or a chewed stick, as the first painters did.

## Frontal faces
Draw three faces as the church painters did: a saint full-face, a righteous person two-thirds, a sinner in profile with one eye. Feel how the face itself tells the story.
