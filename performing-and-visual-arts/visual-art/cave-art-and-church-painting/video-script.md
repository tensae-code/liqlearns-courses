# Video script — Cave Art and Church Painting

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Before writing existed, people painted.
On cave walls: horses, deer, buffalo — about 40,000 years ago.
Ethiopia has its own caves: Porc-Epic and Lega Oda, near Dire Dawa.
Later, church walls carried the painting: few colours, no depth, faces looking straight at you.
