# Cave Art and Church Painting — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** About how old are the Lascaux and Altamira paintings, as the book estimates?
  a) About 40,000 years b) About 400 years c) About 40 years
**Answer: a) About 40,000 years** — the book estimates them at around 40,000 years.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How many painted signs does Lega Oda hold?
  a) More than 600 b) About 60 c) Exactly 6
**Answer: a) More than 600** — Lega Oda holds more than 600 painted signs and writings.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which of these is a mark of Ethiopian church painting?
  a) No depth and no background b) Deep perspective like a photograph c) Hundreds of mixed colours
**Answer: a) No depth and no background** — the book's second mark is no depth and no background — two-dimensional only.

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Which is NOT one of the guide's four types of Ethiopian painting?
  a) Photography b) Illuminated manuscripts c) Body painting
**Answer: a) Photography** — the four are illuminated manuscripts, mural painting, modern portable painting and body painting.
