# Cave Art and Church Painting — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** It is certainly known why cave people painted.
**Answer: False**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The coloured stones used to make the paints were found at Porc-Epic.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In church painting, sinners are shown full-face with both eyes.
**Answer: False**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** The guide gives Dire Dawa as the home example of cave paintings.
**Answer: True**
