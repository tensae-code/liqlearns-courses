# Cave Art and Church Painting — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What three reasons do scholars suggest for cave painting?
**Answer: Communication, worship and gatherings.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In which two colours are Lega Oda's paintings mostly smeared?
**Answer: Red and black.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In whose reign was the Book of Paintings written?
**Answer: Emperor Lebna Dengel's reign.**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Which peoples does the guide name for body painting?
**Answer: The Omotic peoples of southern Ethiopia.**

**5. (Sub lesson 7)** Name two heritage aims of Grade 5 Chapter 3.
**Answer:** Any two of: know how traditional crafts are made and used; know the country's cultural heritages; know cave art's history and beginnings.
