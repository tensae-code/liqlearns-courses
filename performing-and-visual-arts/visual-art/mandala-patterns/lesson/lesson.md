# Mandala Patterns

## Do it first

Name one thing that belongs in this lesson — Mandala patterns — circular, balanced pattern designs from Indian art, built around a centre.. Say what it is in your own words. If you can name one, you are already inside the lesson.

## Sub lesson 1 — Lesson 13 "Indian Art"

Indian mandala art — circular patterned designs built from plant and geometric ornaments, drawn and coloured.

## Sub lesson 2 — "Various Techniques: Coloured Formations"

drawing a geometric or plant ornament in colour and joining your classmates' squares into one shared wall formation.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson holds, part by part, in your own words. Companion checks: did every part get named, with its Syrian name kept where the book gives one?

## Do It Yourself

1. Choose one named part of this lesson and find out one more true thing about it — from a person who knows it, or from the book your part came from.
2. Make or perform a small piece in the way of that part: a rhythm, a step, a drawing, a shaped piece of clay.
3. Write one sentence: what does this part show about Syria's own way of teaching its arts?
