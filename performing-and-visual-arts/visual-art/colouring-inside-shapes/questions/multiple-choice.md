# Colouring Inside Shapes — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Careful colouring means —
a) colouring as fast as possible  b) the colour fills the shape and stays inside the outline  c) using every colour at once
**Answer: b) the colour fills the shape and stays inside the outline.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The careful order is —
a) middle first, edge never  b) edge first, then fill the middle  c) outside first
**Answer: b) edge first, then fill the middle, strokes one way.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The Grade 2 fruit in this lesson is —
a) a banana  b) an orange  c) a mango
**Answer: b) an orange — drawn round, with its leaf, and coloured evenly.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The book's orange activity sends you to —
a) the market  b) your exercise book, to colour the orange pictures  c) the board
**Answer: b) your exercise book — colour the orange pictures there.**
