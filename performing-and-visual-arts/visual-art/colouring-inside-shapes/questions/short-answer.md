# Colouring Inside Shapes — short answer

Answer in a short sentence, or by doing what the question asks.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Do it: colour one outline shape now. How will you stop the colour crossing the border?
**Answer: Colour the edge band first with short strokes, then fill the middle (any careful method that guards the edge).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the two steps for the orange, in order.
**Answer: Draw the round shape (with its leaf) first, then colour it carefully and evenly.**
