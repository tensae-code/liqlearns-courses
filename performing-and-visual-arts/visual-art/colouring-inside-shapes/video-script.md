# Video script — Colouring Inside Shapes

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Colour has a home.
The outline is its fence.

Edge first — slowly, slowly.
Now fill — one way, one way.
No white gaps. Nothing outside.

Carefully — draw it carefully.
Carefully — colour it carefully.

A round orange, full and even.
Beautiful!
