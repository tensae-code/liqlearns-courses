# Hands-on — Colouring Inside Shapes


The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The border guard game
Draw a big outline shape. Colour ONLY the border band — a thick careful frame just inside the line — then swap papers with a partner and fill each other's middle. The band makes finishing easy and tidy.

## Fruit from the kitchen
Look at a real orange or another fruit: its shape, where the leaf sits, how even its colour is. Then draw and colour it from looking, not from memory.
