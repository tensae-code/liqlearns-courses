# Hands-on — Houses of Our Country

The lesson with your hands, your feet and your voice together, not only your eyes.

## The school exhibition
Gather pupils' works, make frames from paper or wood, and hang them in a suitable hall or classroom wall, as the book directs. Walk the wall slowly in pairs.

## Draw a household treasure
Choose one traditional household object — a chair, a door, a shield, a pot. Draw it in line, colour and shape, then prepare a poem or drama about traditional household goods, as the book's activity asks.
