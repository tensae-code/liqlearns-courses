# Houses of Our Country — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Appreciation builds knowledge and is enjoyable.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Mosques built in North Shewa and South Wollo are among the book's medieval architecture plates.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Spears and a shield are pictured as craft.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Works are appreciated by content, form, colour and composition.
**Answer: True**

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** What can be appreciated, in the Grade 4 Chapter 4 summary, is only man-made things.
**Answer: False** — the chapter appreciates the beauty and movement of man-made and natural things.
