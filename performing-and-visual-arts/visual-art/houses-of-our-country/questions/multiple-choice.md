# Houses of Our Country — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Appreciation can happen…
  a) In museums and anywhere, including school b) Only in museums abroad c) Only for paintings
**Answer: a) In museums and anywhere, including school** — the book places appreciation in museums and anywhere; movable works can be shown at school.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The book compares traditional house building of which two parts of the country?
  a) The highlands and the lowlands b) The north and the south coasts c) Two foreign countries
**Answer: a) The highlands and the lowlands** — highland against lowland house building.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which wooden goods does the book picture?
  a) A chair and a table b) A boat and a cart c) A plough and a harrow
**Answer: a) A chair and a table** — Fig. 4.5 shows a wooden chair and table.

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Frames for the school exhibition are made from…
  a) Paper or wood b) Glass and steel only c) Clay
**Answer: a) Paper or wood** — the book directs frames of paper or wood.
