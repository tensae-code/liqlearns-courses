# Houses of Our Country — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name three kinds of presentable works from the book's list.
**Answer: Any three of: clay, dress, weapons and shields, painting, drawing, sculpture, architecture, photography, parchment writings, religious paintings, music, drama, film.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which pictured building is in Gojjam?
**Answer: Merto Lemariam.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In the book's activity, how is a favourite home object drawn?
**Answer: In line, colour and shape.**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Name one song type the appreciation activity asks you to perform.
**Answer: Shilela, engurguro, or fukera.**
