# Lesson plan — Houses of Our Country

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Visual Art · **Lesson:** Houses of Our Country
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 2 — Visual Art (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** learning to appreciate art by looking closely — at artworks in an exhibition, and at Ethiopia's own houses, home ornaments, wooden goods, doors and craft weapons, highland and lowland.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Houses Then and Now and Appreciating Drawing — the learner can already compare old and new houses and show their own work; this lesson teaches the appreciation itself, the Grade 4 way.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved (the gate: README, "Units, Lessons and Sub Lessons (ruled 2026-11-08)"):

1. **Learning to appreciate art** — appreciation judges natural or man-made things by criteria of better art use and result; it happens in museums and anywhere, and at school exhibitions (Grade 4, printed pp. 65–66).
2. **Houses of the highlands and the lowlands** — traditional house building of the highland and lowland parts; Merto Lemariam in Gojjam; medieval architecture — Tedbabe Mariam and the mosques of North Shewa and South Wollo (Grade 4, printed pp. 66–67).
3. **Home ornaments, wooden goods, doors — and weapons as craft** — home decoration ornaments; wooden chair and table; door designs; spears and shield as craft (Grade 4, printed pp. 68–69).
4. **Exhibiting paintings and appreciating the other arts** — hold a school exhibition — gather, frame, hang; appreciate by content, form, colour and composition; players, dancers and instruments appreciated in pictures (Grade 4, printed pp. 72–78).

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Houses Then and Now and Appreciating Drawing. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say what appreciation is and where it can happen.
2. Tell highland house building from lowland house building.
3. Name the historic buildings the book shows: Merto Lemariam, Tedbabe Mariam and the mosques of North Shewa and South Wollo.
4. Hold a school exhibition: gather, frame, hang, and appreciate by content, form, colour and composition.

## What you need
Eyes and time — and, for the exhibition part, the class's own drawings, paper or wood for frames, and a wall.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — Look at the house you are in, or the nearest house you can see. Look slowly for one full minute: roof, walls, door, the things made by hand. Appreciating begins with slow looking — this lesson practises it. (lesson/lesson.md).
