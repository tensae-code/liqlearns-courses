# Hands-on — Photographs

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The twenty-count look
Find a real photograph where you live or learn. Look at it for a slow count of twenty without speaking. Then tell a partner three things in it — and one thing about where the camera must have stood.

## Sort the sets
On paper, make four boxes: PLACE — PERSON — ANIMAL — WORK. Write or draw each photograph from the lesson into its box: the falls, the dam and the cave are places; Abebe Bikila is a person; the walia is an animal; the market and the harvest are work. Which box is fullest?

## A visit without travelling
Choose one historic-place photograph — Bete Giyorgis, Axum, Fasilides' castle or Sof Omar. "Visit" it with your eyes for one full minute. Then describe it to someone who has not seen it, so exactly that they could draw its main shape from your words alone.
