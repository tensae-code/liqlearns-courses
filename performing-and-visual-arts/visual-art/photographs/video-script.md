# Video script — Photographs

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This is not a drawing. A **camera** caught this.
A photograph is an image a photo camera gathers — a person, a place, a thing, as it truly was.

Look. A wide waterfall — the **Tis Abay**, the Blue Nile Falls.
Look. A runner on the track — **Abebe Bikila**, racing.
A great dam wall, water pouring through.
A **walia**, standing on the rocks, against the sky.

A market, full of traders. A golden field — harvest time.
A church cut down into the rock — **Bete Giyorgis**.
A tall stone among ruins — **Axum**. A castle — **Fasilides'**, at Gondar.
A cave mouth in the rock — **Sof Omar**.

Every one was really there, in real light, at one real moment.
Now you — find a photograph, look slowly, and tell what you understood.
