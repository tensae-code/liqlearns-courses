# Photographs — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number, as before. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. The sub lessons climb from the definition of a photograph through the books' own photograph sets — famous first, then work and land, then historic places — and end in appreciation.

## Sub lesson 1 — Looking at photographs
Pass mark: define a photograph (an image of a person, landscape or object obtained using a photo camera) and tell what you understood from one photograph.

## Sub lesson 2 — Famous places, people and animals
Pass mark: name the Tis Abay Falls, Abebe Bikila running, the Abbay Dam and the walia ibex, each as its photograph shows it.

## Sub lesson 3 — Work, seasons, land and sky
Pass mark: tell what the market and harvest photographs show (traders with their goods; a golden field with haystacks) and name the landscape (መልክዓምድር) and aeroplane photographs.

## Sub lesson 4 — Historic places in photographs
Pass mark: name Bete Giyorgis, the Axum obelisk among ruins, Fasilides' castle at Gondar and the Sof Omar cave entrance — and say which photograph pleased you.

## Sub lesson 5 — Appreciating photographs
Pass mark: appreciate one photograph from the set aloud — what you understood from it and what you feel — remembering everything in it was really there.
