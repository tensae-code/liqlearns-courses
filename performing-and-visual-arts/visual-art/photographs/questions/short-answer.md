# Photographs — short answer

Answer in a word or a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What three kinds of things can a photograph be an image of? **Answer: a person, a landscape, or an object.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Where does the walia ibex stand in its photograph, and against what? **Answer: standing on rocks, against the sky.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What is the Amharic word the book gives for the landscape photograph? **Answer: መልክዓምድር.**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** What does the Sof Omar photograph show? **Answer: a cave entrance in rock.**

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** Name two things in the appreciating set of photographs besides the jebena pots. **Answer: any two of — girls in white festival dress in a group photo; a large festival crowd in a town square; a road through a gorge; a rock-hewn church landscape.**
