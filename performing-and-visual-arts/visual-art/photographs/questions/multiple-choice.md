# Photographs — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A photograph is —
a) a drawing made by hand  b) the image of a person, landscape or object obtained using a photo camera  c) a story told aloud
**Answer: b)** — the camera gathers the image.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The athlete photographed running on a stadium track in a race is —
a) Abebe Bikila  b) a walia ibex  c) Emperor Fasilides
**Answer: a) Abebe Bikila.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The harvest photograph shows —
a) a dam wall  b) a golden field with haystacks  c) an aeroplane
**Answer: b) a golden field with haystacks.**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Bete Giyorgis is —
a) a waterfall  b) the rock-hewn church of St George at Lalibela  c) a cave entrance
**Answer: b)** — a church cut into the rock.

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** Appreciating a photograph, you say —
a) only its price  b) what you understood from it and what you feel  c) nothing — photographs cannot be appreciated
**Answer: b)** — look slowly, say what you see, say what you feel.
