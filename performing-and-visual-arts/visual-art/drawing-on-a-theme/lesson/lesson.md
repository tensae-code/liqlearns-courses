# Drawing on a Theme

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about drawing on a theme. Then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — Arts & Crafts G11 teacher guide — Drawing on a Theme

Choosing one impressive event (a festival, a sports day, work), composing it from a thumbnail, adding light-and-shade, enlarging and colouring so the content of the theme reads clearly; drawing people in motion from a school activity (photographs of a football match and pupils standing in rows as starting points), from thumbnail composition to shaded, coloured figures; drawing a shared family activity (example paintings: a mother washing jars with her children, a family group indoors), composing the scene from a thumbnail and colouring it; what cultural heritage and antiques are (with paintings of That Ing Hang, the Plain of Jars stone ruins and a Lao village with That Luang), then drawing a heritage place of your own province

## Sub lesson 2 — Arts & Crafts G10 teacher guide — Drawing on a Theme

Drawing a scene of community life (example paintings: a woman carrying offerings to a festival, the Boun Pi Mai crowd), from choosing the content and composing to colouring in matching tones; drawing a scene of helping at home (the example drawing shows children helping their mother prepare food), choosing foreground and background parts and colouring in harmonious tones; drawing your own village's landscape (example paintings and drawings of stilt houses, fields and a river), choosing the viewpoint, and using warm and cool colour tones to give the feeling of the place

## Sub lesson 3 — Arts & Crafts G9 teacher guide — Drawing on a Theme

Drawing the Boun Pi Mai festival from its real activities (drawings of people pouring water, making sand stupas and celebrating at the temple), choosing the content, composing and colouring to match the festival air; drawing your own pet (drawings of a cat with a ball, a cat eating, a girl feeding her cat, children playing with a cat at home), from observing the animal's parts to a finished coloured scene; drawing your family (the example drawings: eating rice together with mother, going to the fields with father), choosing a family moment, composing it and colouring; drawing a famous sacred place of the country from photographs — That Ing Hang, the Wat Sisaket cloister, Ho Phra Keo, That Luang of Vientiane and Wat Phou — setting the building in its ground and colouring

## Sub lesson 4 — Arts & Crafts + Music G3 teacher guide, 2009 edition — Drawing on a Theme

Drawing the National Day celebration at school (the example drawing: pupils presenting flowers to their teacher), composing the scene from the day's real activities; drawing the story of the red rooster (drawings of the rooster and the hen in the yard), building a chicken from an egg shape in three steps; drawing a child helping mother (the example drawings: children gardening with mother, a boy sweeping the house), choosing the helping moment and composing it; drawing a school (the example: the school building with its flag, fence and football field), from looking at the real school and choosing what to show

## Sub lesson 5 — Arts & Crafts G6 teacher guide — Drawing on a Theme

How a theme drawing is built — choosing the content, dividing the picture into its main part and its supporting parts, fitting the movement of people and animals to the theme, and colouring (a classroom drawing as the worked example); drawing the class cleaning its room (the example drawing: pupils sweeping, dusting and wiping the board), choosing the moment and arranging the figures; drawing a day of helping at home (the example drawings: playing with the baby sibling, eating together as a family), choosing which activities to show

## Sub lesson 6 — Arts & Crafts G3 teacher guide, 2021 edition — Drawing on a Theme

Drawing sugarcane from a photograph of the real plant, in six steps — the frame, the stalks, the nodes, the leaves, and the colouring; drawing your school (the example painting: the school building, the flag, the pond and the pupils in the yard), choosing what of the school to show

## Sub lesson 7 — Arts & Crafts G7 teacher guide — Drawing on a Theme

Drawing your mother at her work (the example drawings: mother selling at the market, mother teaching her class), choosing the moment that shows her; drawing your village (the example drawings: farmers transplanting rice, a mother and child looking over the fields), choosing the view that tells the village's life; drawing a village tradition (the example drawings: a boun festival meal, the Pi Mai celebration, boat racing, a village gathering), choosing the custom and its crowd; drawing the rooster as the theme subject — its comb, beak, feathers and stance (the example: a fully shaded rooster on a branch)

## Sub lesson 8 — Arts & Crafts and Music G5 teacher guide, 2009 combined guide — Drawing on a Theme

Drawing one of the pupils' own activities (the example: a football match, with the players' movement); drawing the work you hope to do when you grow up (the examples: a nurse at a bedside, a forest guard on patrol); drawing the market — the stalls, the sellers and the buyers (the example: a coloured market scene); drawing an occasion that stayed in your mind (the example: a sepak takraw match with its crowd); drawing Children's Day (the example: children dancing together outdoors)

## Sub lesson 9 — Arts & Crafts and Music G4 teacher guide, 2009 combined guide — Drawing on a Theme

Drawing the set theme of pupils choosing and doing work at school; drawing the set theme of a family's traditional celebration day; drawing the set theme of a sight that impressed you; drawing the set theme of your village's nature (the lesson works from a model painting of birds over the rice fields)

## Sub lesson 10 — Arts & Crafts G8 teacher guide — Drawing on a Theme

Drawing the set theme of your own village and its surroundings; drawing the set theme of a national celebration day; drawing the set theme of your friend; drawing the set theme of your dream for the future

## Sub lesson 11 — Arts & Crafts and Music G1 teacher guides, original editions — Drawing on a Theme

Drawing the set theme of children with flowers; drawing the set theme of children with their toys; drawing the set theme of a hen with her chicks

## Sub lesson 12 — Arts & Crafts and Music G2 teacher guides, original editions — Drawing on a Theme

Drawing the set theme of planting trees; drawing the set theme of your own little group (title as printed); drawing the set theme of your favourite sport; drawing the set theme of a duck with her ducklings
