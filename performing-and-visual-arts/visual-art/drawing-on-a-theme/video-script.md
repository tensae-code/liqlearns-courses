# Video script — Drawing on a Theme

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is Drawing on a Theme.

Drawing on a set theme — an event that impressed you, movement in a school activity, and other given subjects drawn with intent. — as Laos's school books teach it: choosing one impressive event (a festival, a sports day, work), composing it from a thumbnail, adding light-and-shade, enlarging and colouring so the content of the theme reads clearly; drawing people in motion from a school activity (photographs of a football match and pupils standing in rows as starting points), from thumbnail composition to shaded, coloured figures; drawing a shared family activity (example paintings: a mother washing jars with her children, a family group indoors), composing the scene from a thumbnail and colouring it; what cultural heritage and antiques are (with paintings of That Ing Hang, the Plain of Jars stone ruins and a Lao village with That Luang), then drawing a heritage place of your own province

Watch, listen — then say it back in your own words.
