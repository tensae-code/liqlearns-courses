This lesson is Shevchenko and His Aral Paintings.

Shevchenko and His Aral Paintings — Taras Shevchenko's Aral paintings — the works he made on the Aral expedition, read in Kazakhstan's curriculum. — as Kazakhstan's school books teach it: Taras Shevchenko (1814–1861), poet and artist, the first to paint the Aral: on the 1848 expedition he made over 350 landscapes and portraits; his «Қосаралдағы орыс кемелері» and «Қосаралдағы айлы түн» read by tempo and dynamics — fast/slow, forte/piano, crescendo.

Watch, listen — then say it back in your own words.
