## Sub lesson 1 — Artistic crafts — useful and beautiful

**Artistic crafts are diverse and are highly valued today too**, although industrial mass production exists. **The products of artistic crafts are at the same time useful and beautiful.** Artistic crafts **developed from old traditional crafts**, and today people practise them not only in the village but also in the city. The book pictures a traditional Japanese doll, hats, **Serbian earrings**, the **14th-century Radul ring** and a **Pirot kilim**.

## Sub lesson 2 — Serbia's pottery — Zlakusa and folk ceramics

**It matters to preserve the old traditional crafts.** The **pottery of baked-clay vessels is preserved at Užice, where the Zlakusa art colony makes and fires clay vessels in the way it was done in the Stone Age**. **Pottery-making is one of the oldest crafts** — vessels of baked clay of various forms, used for keeping and preparing food and drink and also specially decorated — and it is often called **folk ceramics**. **The potter's craft has existed in Serbia since ancient times.** Also pictured: a vase by **Dušan Janković**, a carved wooden chair and a mid-19th-century **Kosovo dress**.

## Sub lesson 3 — Make and decorate a vessel

The making that belongs with the crafts: **make a vessel of clay or plasticine** — first decide **what it will serve for**, keeping food or drink, then what form suits that; shape the vessel and **decorate it by pressing a little stick, a pin, crumpled paper or buttons into the wet clay** to make a pattern; on another clay piece, **draw a pattern or form by cutting (урезивање)**.
