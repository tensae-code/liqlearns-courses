# Lesson plan — Serbian Traditional Crafts

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Visual Art · **Lesson:** Serbian Traditional Crafts
**Place in the curriculum:** Visual Art (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Serbia's artistic crafts grew from its old traditional crafts — their products are at the same time useful and beautiful — above all its pottery: the Zlakusa potters near Užice still make and fire clay vessels in the way it was done in the Stone Age, and the potter's craft has existed in Serbia since ancient times.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this course in the same title.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Artistic crafts — useful and beautiful** — Artistic crafts are diverse and are highly valued today too, although industrial mass production exists.
2. **Serbia's pottery — Zlakusa and folk ceramics** — It matters to preserve the old traditional crafts.
3. **Make and decorate a vessel** — The making that belongs with the crafts: make a vessel of clay or plasticine — first decide what it will serve for, keeping food or drink, then what form suits that; shape the vessel and decorate it by pressing a little stick, a pin, crumpled paper or buttons into the wet clay to make a pattern; on another clay piece, draw a pattern or form by cutting (урезивање)..

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
