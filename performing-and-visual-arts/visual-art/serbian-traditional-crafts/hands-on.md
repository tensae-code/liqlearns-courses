# Hands-on — Serbian Traditional Crafts

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## A vessel with a job
Decide: is your vessel for food or for drink? Shape it in clay or plasticine to fit that job. Press a pattern into the wet clay with a stick, a pin or buttons.

## Cut a pattern
On a flat clay piece, draw a pattern by cutting (урезивање). Compare a pressed pattern and a cut pattern side by side.
