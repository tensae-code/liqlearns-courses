## Sub lesson 3 — Make and decorate a vessel
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: say what this part teaches, in the book's own way — The making that belongs with the crafts: make a vessel of clay or plasticine — first decide what it will serve for, keeping food or drink, then what form suits that; shape the vessel and decorate it by pressing a little stick, a pin, crumpled paper or buttons into the wet clay to make a pattern; on another clay piece, draw a pattern or form by cutting (урезивање).
