# Serbian Traditional Crafts — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Industrial mass production ended the value of artistic crafts.
**Answer: False** — they are highly valued today too.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Artistic crafts are practised only in villages now.
**Answer: False** — today people practise them in the village and also in the city.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The Pirot kilim is pictured among the crafts.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** A vessel's decoration can be pressed into wet clay with a pin or buttons.
**Answer: True**
