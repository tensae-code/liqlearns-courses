# Serbian Traditional Crafts — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name two pictured products of artistic crafts.
**Answer: Any two of: a traditional Japanese doll, hats, Serbian earrings, the 14th-century Radul ring, a Pirot kilim.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Where is Serbia's old pottery preserved, and by whom?
**Answer: At Užice, by the Zlakusa art colony.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Since when has the potter's craft existed in Serbia?
**Answer: Since ancient times.**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Before shaping a vessel, what two things must you decide?
**Answer: What it will serve for (keeping food or drink), and what form would be suitable for that.**
