# Serbian Traditional Crafts — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The products of artistic crafts are at the same time…
a) useful and beautiful  b) only useful, never beautiful  c) only for museums
**Answer: a) useful and beautiful**.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Artistic crafts developed from…
a) old traditional crafts  b) factories  c) computers
**Answer: a) old traditional crafts**.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The Zlakusa potters fire their vessels…
a) in the way it was done in the Stone Age  b) in electric kilns only  c) they do not fire them
**Answer: a) in the way it was done in the Stone Age**.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Pottery of baked clay is often called…
a) folk ceramics  b) folk calligraphy  c) folk weaving
**Answer: a) folk ceramics**.
