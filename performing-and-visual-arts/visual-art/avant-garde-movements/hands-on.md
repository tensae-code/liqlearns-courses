# Hands-on — Avant-Garde Movements

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Expresionismo
Work through this part with real materials or your own body, the way the book sets it: Expressionism — reaction against naturalism, realism and impressionism: dramatic, gestural expression in free strokes and strong colour contrasts, on themes of society's decadence and the artists' emotional conflicts (Munch's Scream, Nolde). Show a companion what you made or did, and say what the part teaches.

## Fauvismo
Work through this part with real materials or your own body, the way the book sets it: Fauvism — brilliant, intense, complementary colours and impulsive deformation; the critics named its painters fauves, wild beasts. Show a companion what you made or did, and say what the part teaches.

## Cubismo
Work through this part with real materials or your own body, the way the book sets it: Cubism — objects fragmented into geometric surfaces and shown whole on one plane, with collage (Picasso's Guernica, Braque, Juan Gris). Show a companion what you made or did, and say what the part teaches.

## Futurismo
Work through this part with real materials or your own body, the way the book sets it: Futurism — representing movement and speed, fragmenting form and colour (Boccioni's Unique Forms of Continuity in Space). Show a companion what you made or did, and say what the part teaches.

## Dadaísmo y el objeto encontrado
Work through this part with real materials or your own body, the way the book sets it: Dada — the radical movement questioning Western art and its museums, proposing chance and improvisation; and Duchamp's ready-made: an everyday industrial object (the Fountain urinal) placed in a new context as a work of art. Show a companion what you made or did, and say what the part teaches.

## Surrealismo
Work through this part with real materials or your own body, the way the book sets it: Surrealism — expressing the world of the unconscious, dreams and imagination (Dalí's Premonition of Civil War). Show a companion what you made or did, and say what the part teaches.

## Abstracción lírica y abstracción geométrica
Work through this part with real materials or your own body, the way the book sets it: the two abstractions — lyrical abstraction, where the object is secondary to chromatic richness and simplification (Kandinsky), and geometric abstraction, simple geometric forms combined, reacting against the purely emotional (Mondrian). Show a companion what you made or did, and say what the part teaches.

## Pop art
Work through this part with real materials or your own body, the way the book sets it: Pop art — appropriating advertising images, fashion, comics and design into mass-produced art, against the consumer society it depicts: Andy Warhol's serigraphed Mona Lisa; Venezuela's Marisol Escobar. Show a companion what you made or did, and say what the part teaches.
