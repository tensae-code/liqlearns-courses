# Lesson plan — Moldovan Art History

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Visual Art · **Lesson:** Moldovan Art History
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 2 — Visual Art (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The art of Bessarabia and the Republic of Moldova — Moldova's own art history.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the course's earlier lessons in its title; recommended first, never forced.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of ..." in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Arta Basarabiei și a Republicii Moldova** — Unit IV §20, Arta Basarabiei și a Republicii Moldova — the art of Bessarabia and Moldova in five periods: icon painting continuing the Middle Ages (1812–1887); art schools opened and every branch constituted (1887–1918); the modern apogee under Romania (1918–1940); socialist realism under censorship (1945–late 1980s) — only permitted subjects, party leaders, pioneers, field and factory scenes, many artists exiled to Siberia; and liberation into contemporary experiment from the late 1980s.
2. **The lost Pinacoteca** — Unit IV §20 — the lost Pinacoteca: the Bessarabian artists' works and the Art School archive gathered to found Chișinău's picture gallery in 1939, lost entirely in the war of 1941–1945.
