# Moldovan Art History — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — Arta Basarabiei și a Republicii Moldova
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Unit IV §20, Arta Basarabiei și a Republicii Moldova — the art of Bessarabia and Moldova in five periods: icon painting continuing the Middle Ages (1812–1887); art schools opened and every branch constituted (1887–1918); the modern apogee under Romania (1918–1940); socialist realism under censorship (1945–late 1980s) — only permitted subjects, party leaders, pioneers, field and factory scenes, many artists exiled to Siberia; and liberation into contemporary experiment from the late 1980s.

## Sub lesson 2 — The lost Pinacoteca
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Unit IV §20 — the lost Pinacoteca: the Bessarabian artists' works and the Art School archive gathered to found Chișinău's picture gallery in 1939, lost entirely in the war of 1941–1945.
