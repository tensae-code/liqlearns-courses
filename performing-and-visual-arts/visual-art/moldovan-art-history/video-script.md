This is Moldova's own lesson.

**Arta Basarabiei și a Republicii Moldova.** Unit IV §20, Arta Basarabiei și a Republicii Moldova — the art of Bessarabia and Moldova in five periods: icon painting continuing the Middle Ages (1812–1887); art schools opened and every branch constituted (1887–1918); the modern apogee under Romania (1918–1940); socialist realism under censorship (1945–late 1980s) — only permitted subjects, party leaders, pioneers, field and factory scenes, many artists exiled to Siberia; and liberation into contemporary experiment from the late 1980s.

**The lost Pinacoteca.** Unit IV §20 — the lost Pinacoteca: the Bessarabian artists' works and the Art School archive gathered to found Chișinău's picture gallery in 1939, lost entirely in the war of 1941–1945.

That is Moldovan Art History — Moldova's way, kept by name.
