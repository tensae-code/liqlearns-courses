# Hands-on — Scratch Techniques

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Work the lesson's own way
Work through the sub lessons in order — Laying the colour layers; Scratching the picture out; Areas made by line — doing each part the way Cuba's books direct: by making, moving, singing or looking closely, as the part asks. Show your companion what you made or did, and name the part it belongs to.
