Интерьер заттары және жиһаздардың өндірілуі (The production of interior items and furniture) — what it teaches: since many centuries a person's life has been connected with the things and items used in everyday use, and furniture also enters among such things: making furniture of varied forms is a definite art, connected with the need of the person using that furniture and with aesthetic taste; the furniture and interior items' production field, in which technical progress, art and culture play an important role in contemporary human life, develops together with them; in our fast-developing country construction grows year by year, and with its growth the demand for furniture and interior items grows and the need for their production arises; furniture is classified by varied signs — functional, use, construction peculiarities, making way, the kinds of materials used and other descriptions distinguish them; by use purpose furniture divides into тұрмыстық (household — intended for dwellings), әкімшілік-қоғамдық (administrative-public), қоғамдық (public) and жеке көліктерге арналған (intended for private transport); the pictured furniture: корпустық жиһаз (corpus/cabinet furniture) and өзгертілетін жиһаз (transformable furniture) (student book; original target `visual-art/furniture-kinds-and-production`) | Book 6, printed p. 44 |
| Kazakhstan — § 12 (continued). Жиһаз түрлері және өндіріс түрлері (Furniture kinds and production kinds) — what it teaches: by construction peculiarities furniture divides into секциялық (sectional), жиналатын (folding/assembled) and жиналмайтын (non-folding), кіріктірілген (built-in), өзгертілетін (transformable), корпустық (corpus) and other kinds, and by functional purpose into furniture intended for sleeping, storing things, eating and other works; contemporary production is a complex process turning raw material, materials and other labour objects into a ready product, and the industry labour tools and people's action directed at preparing a definite product is called «өндіріс үдерісі» (the production process) — in producing interior items and furniture, labour (people's work action), labour tools (production's material-technical basis, instruments), and labour things (the material needed for preparing the product) are the production process's elements, and the technological process is connected with the processed material's volume, kind and properties' change; in designing furniture, one of its stages is preparing its макеттік үлгісі (model sample) — the item's model or its part is made reduced at a definite scale, and with the model sample's help the future item's varied characteristic signs can be researched and the technical and artistic solutions' rightness checked; by the produced product's number and output character, production divides within itself into жекешеленген (individual), топтық (batch) and жаппай (mass): Жекешеленген өндіріс (individual production) is the production kind outputting one kind of product in one or a few counted copies (an individual copy) — furniture is usually prepared by a definite person's individual order or for an individual-use sample; Топтық өндіріс (batch production) is the production kind outputting an item in big parties (groups) systematically — this production kind is often used in furniture-preparing enterprises; Жаппай өндіріс (mass production) is the production kind constantly outputting uniform items in a definite measure by construction, service and technology kind; to define which furniture and interior items will be in demand, the market must be researched — the specialist who defines consumers' needs and taste by researching the market is called «маркетолог» (marketer): he analyses the market state, forecasts how it will develop, thinks how to advertise goods to interest consumers, thinks how the product will be sold and considers how it will be prepared; various profession owners work in furniture and interior items' production: the дизайнер (designer) prepares interior items and furniture — a designer must be a person different from everyone, able to think creatively, with high aesthetic taste; the конструктор (constructor) joins the item's parts and carries out its drawing — this profession requires knowing technical-economic calculations and design methods; the ағаш шебері (wood master) makes furniture parts from prepared material and joins them; the жинаушы (assembler) occupies himself with assembling furniture from parts ready from sections; the pictured furniture: кіріктірілген жиһаз (built-in furniture), секциялық жиһаз (sectional furniture), жиналатын жиһаз (folding furniture) and a chair's model sample (Креслоның макеттік үлгісі) (student book; original target `visual-art/furniture-kinds-and-production`) | Book 6, printed p. 45 |

Choosing materials) — Құрылым (Конструкция / Design) — what it teaches: furniture enterprises produce furniture's varied kinds, and furniture is made quality, artistic and strong, suiting accepted standards, and furniture's volume also suits standards — however, for varied reasons the sold furniture does not always suit consumers' demands: sometimes furniture made by standard does not suit the place given for it, sometimes it does not harmonise with the interior or does not suit a person's taste, so a row of furniture can be learned to be made with one's own hands; when preparing furniture, first it must be defined what it will be used for — according to use, furniture's construction (құрылымы) can also be varied, and when choosing furniture's construction, factors such as беріктік (strength), сенімділік (reliability — the item's property of carrying out its function exactly) and технологиялылық (manufacturability) must be regarded, and an item that does not break in the use process is counted as having the strength property; a technological item's description suits the economic technology of making it; furniture is first depicted in sketch (нобай) form, and then on its basis drawings are drawn and calculated — the scheme depicted in drawing form must be further completed, and the construction's separate parts drawn; the pictured design ideas for preparing furniture: a folding garden chair set, red modular seating that joins into a ball-like form, a chair-ladder, and bright children's furniture (student book; original target `visual-art/designing-furniture-idea-and-materials`) | Book 6, printed p. 48 |
| Kazakhstan — § 13 (continued). Жиһаз материалдары мен білдектер (Furniture materials and machines) — what it teaches: the future item's quality in many cases depends on the right choice of the material needed for making it — the main criteria when choosing a material must be its strength, ecologicalness and safety; nowadays varied materials are used for preparing furniture: in furniture making mainly ағаш сүрегі (wood timber) is used, and there is also furniture made from metal, glass and plastic, and made using them all together; materials got through processing wood are also used in furniture making, among them: АЖТ (ағаш жоңқалы тақта — chipboard), ЛАЖТ (ламинирленген ағаш жоңқалы тақта — laminated chipboard), АТТ (ағаш талшықты тақта — fibreboard), МДФ (орташа тығыздықты ағаш талшықты тақта — medium-density fibreboard, MDF), шпон (veneer) and фанер (plywood), used often — these materials are similar in their qualities, but each has its own peculiarity, and according to it their processing methods, use and price come varied; for preparing furniture, varied hand equipment and mechanical instruments are used, and using machines (білдектер) the furniture-preparing work can be somewhat lightened and sped up: the Токарлық білдек (lathe) is used in making varied items by taking chips from blanks prepared from wood — it is used for making decorative items, corpus and support parts; the Бұрғылау білдегі (drilling machine) is used for drilling holes in preparation — it gives the possibility to process the preparation with quality and high exactness; the Жону білдегі (milling machine) is used in making parts of varied complexity — with its help holes of varied form can be drilled or holes made, and during work the machine's working part, the фреза (milling cutter), must be lowered to the needed level, and it mills one part of the material from the preparation's face (student book; original target `visual-art/designing-furniture-idea-and-materials`) | Book 6, printed p. 50 |
