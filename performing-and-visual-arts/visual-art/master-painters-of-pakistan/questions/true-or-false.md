# Master Painters of Pakistan — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Shakir Ali is named both among the master painters and among the master calligraphers.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Gulgee is one of the seven master painters.
**Answer: False** — Gulgee is named among the master calligraphers, not in this list.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The master painters are honoured by name in Pakistan's own Class 9 book.
**Answer: True**
