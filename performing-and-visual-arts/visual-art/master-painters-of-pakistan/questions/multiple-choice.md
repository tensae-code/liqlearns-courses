# Master Painters of Pakistan — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which of these is named among Pakistan's master painters?
a) Abdul Rahman Chughtai  b) Gulgee  c) Dr. Sadia Murtaza
**Answer: a) Abdul Rahman Chughtai**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Sadequain also appears in the book as…
a) a master calligrapher  b) an architect  c) a weaver
**Answer: a) a master calligrapher**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The list of master painters holds…
a) five names  b) seven names  c) nine names
**Answer: b) seven names**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Anna Molka Ahmad is named as…
a) a master painter of Pakistan  b) a calligrapher only  c) a building
**Answer: a) a master painter of Pakistan**
