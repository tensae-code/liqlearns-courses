# Master Painters of Pakistan

## Do it first

Quick game: say what you already know about this lesson's subject — one thing, out loud. This lesson names it properly.

## Sub lesson 1 — The earlier masters

Pakistan's Class 9 book honours its master painters **by name**. The list opens with:

- **Haji Muhammad Sharif**
- **Abdul Rahman Chughtai**
- **Allah Baksh**

## Sub lesson 2 — Shakir Ali and Sadequain

- **Shakir Ali**
- **Sadequain**

These two names appear twice in the book: here among the master painters, and again in the chapter of **master calligraphers of Pakistan** — masters in two arts.

## Sub lesson 3 — Zahoor-Ul-Akhlaq and Anna Molka Ahmad

- **Zahoor-Ul-Akhlaq**
- **Anna Molka Ahmad**

Seven masters in all — the country's own painters, named in the country's own book, inside its history of painting in South Asia.
