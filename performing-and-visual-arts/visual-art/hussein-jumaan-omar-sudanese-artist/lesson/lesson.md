## Sub lesson 1 — Artistic Taste Topic 2 "Works of a Sudanese Plastic Artist"

the late Professor Hussein Jumaan Omar, pioneer of modern plastic art in Sudan — trained in design at the College of Fine Arts with a Master's in London and a doctorate from Juba University, dean and department head — who dug into folk-art sources for a style of his own: the Sudanese environment and old Nubian details and symbols across his panels, the Kingdom of Kush as his inspiration, and a spontaneity of line close to children's drawing, a green horse and a face of four eyes among its freedoms; winner of Japan's Noma Prize for children's book illustration 1999, his works pictured in colour
