# Hussein Jumaan Omar, Sudanese Artist — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Where did Omar take his Master's degree?
**Answer: In London (he trained in design at the College of Fine Arts, with a Master's in London and a doctorate from Juba University).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which university gave him his doctorate?
**Answer: Juba University.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Name one freedom of line named in his works.
**Answer: A green horse, or a face of four eyes.**
