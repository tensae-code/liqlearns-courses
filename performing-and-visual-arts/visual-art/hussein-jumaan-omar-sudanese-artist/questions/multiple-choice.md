# Hussein Jumaan Omar, Sudanese Artist — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Hussein Jumaan Omar is called a pioneer of…
a) modern plastic art in Sudan  b) photography  c) opera
**Answer: a) modern plastic art in Sudan**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** His inspiration, dug from folk-art sources, was…
a) the Kingdom of Kush  b) the sea  c) the railway
**Answer: a) the Kingdom of Kush** — the Sudanese environment and old Nubian details and symbols across his panels.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** He won Japan's Noma Prize in 1999 for…
a) children's book illustration  b) sculpture  c) calligraphy
**Answer: a) children's book illustration**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** His line is described as spontaneous, close to…
a) children's drawing  b) engineering drawing  c) maps
**Answer: a) children's drawing** — a green horse and a face of four eyes among its freedoms.
