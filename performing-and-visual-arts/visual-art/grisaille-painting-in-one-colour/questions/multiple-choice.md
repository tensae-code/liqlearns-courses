# Grisaille: Painting in One Colour — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Grisaille is painting built on…
a) every colour at once  b) the tones of a single colour  c) black only, no tones
**Answer: b) the tones of a single colour** — .

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In grisaille, light and shadow are made by…
a) changing colour  b) lightening and darkening the one colour  c) drawing lines
**Answer: b) lightening and darkening the one colour** — .

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The Grade 5 book teaches grisaille in…
a) Section 22  b) Section 4  c) Section 28
**Answer: a) Section 22** — .

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** A grisaille painting's colours number…
a) one  b) five  c) twelve
**Answer: a) one** — with all its tones.

**5. (Sub lesson 1)** Before grisaille, the book paints inside…
