# Grisaille: Painting in One Colour — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is grisaille?
**Answer: Painting built on the tones of a single colour.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** How does a grisaille painter show a bright highlight?
**Answer: With the lightest tone of the one colour.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** What work must the tones do in grisaille?
**Answer: The work of light, shadow, volume and distance — everything a full palette usually does.**
