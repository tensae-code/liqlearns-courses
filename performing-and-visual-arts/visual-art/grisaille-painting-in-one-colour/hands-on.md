# Hands-on — Grisaille: Painting in One Colour

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## One colour, five tones
Choose one colour. Mix five tones of it, from lightest to darkest, in a row. Then paint a simple object — a jug, an apple — using only those five tones: lightest for the highlight, darkest for the deep shadow.

## Tone detective
Look at your painting from a distance, squinting. If the object still stands out round and solid, your tones are working — that is grisaille's test.
