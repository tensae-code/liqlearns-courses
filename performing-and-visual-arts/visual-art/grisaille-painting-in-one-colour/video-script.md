# Video script — Grisaille: Painting in One Colour

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

One colour. Only one.

Lighten it — light. Darken it — shadow. Every tone between does the work of a whole palette.

That is **grisaille**: painting in the tones of a single colour.

Pick your colour. Paint the jug. One colour is enough.
