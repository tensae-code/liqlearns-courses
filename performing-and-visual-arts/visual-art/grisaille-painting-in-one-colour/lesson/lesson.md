# Grisaille: Painting in One Colour

## Do it first

Before you read: say or sketch what you already know about this lesson's subject — grisaille — painting built on the tones of a single colour. Start there; the lesson builds on it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Painting in one colour's tones

**Grisaille** is painting built on the **tones of a single colour** — one colour, lightened and darkened, does all the work that a whole palette usually does: light, shadow, volume and distance.

The Grade 5 book teaches it as its own section (Section 22), after the close-colour-range painting of the section before: first a family of close colours, then the discipline of one colour alone and all its tones.
