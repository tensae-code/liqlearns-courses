# Chinese Folk Paper and Festival Art

## Do it first

## Sub lesson 1 — China Grade 1 Art Vol.1

Chinese New Year arts — cutting paper window flowers (剪窗花) and making festival goods and decorations for the New Year

## Sub lesson 2 — China Grade 5 Art Vol.2

The twelve zodiac animals of one's birth year — Tang-dynasty pottery zodiac figures, zodiac stamps and Yangzhou folk paper-cuts; making your family's zodiac animals in coloured clay, paper cutting or collage, with personification and exaggeration
