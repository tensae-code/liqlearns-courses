# Hands-on — Assemblage

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Bead jewellery
Arrange natural and man-made beads into a piece of jewellery. Change the order once before you fix it — and say why the final order is better.

## Balanced object
Build a 3D object from found materials that stands in balance and shows its space. Then design a wooden toy on paper before you make anything.
