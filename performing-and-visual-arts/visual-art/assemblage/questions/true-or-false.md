# Assemblage — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Bead jewellery is part of Class 4 assemblage.
**Answer: True.**.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A 3D assemblage object need not balance.
**Answer: False.** — the objects show space and balance.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Toys from wood are designed before they are made.
**Answer: True.**.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Assemblage here means arranging and building with materials on purpose.
**Answer: True.**.
