# Assemblage

## Do it first

Quick start: say in one sentence what you think this lesson is about — assemblage the Barbados Class 4 way. Then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — Bead jewellery from natural and man-made beads

Class 4 assemblage begins small and wearable: **arranging natural and man-made beads into jewellery** — pieces chosen and ordered by the maker.

## Sub lesson 2 — 3D objects showing space and balance

The work stands up: **creating 3D objects showing space and balance** — a made thing that holds its space and does not fall, arranged on purpose.

## Sub lesson 3 — Toys from wood: design and make

Assemblage ends in play: **designing and making toys from wood** — a toy first designed, then made by the pupil's own hands.
