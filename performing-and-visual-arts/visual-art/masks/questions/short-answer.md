# Masks — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name three materials a mask can be assembled from.
**Answer: Card, coloured paper, ribbons, paper plates, elastic cord and glue — any three.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which three spaces are cut out of the mask?
**Answer: The eye, nose and mouth spaces.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** What holds the finished mask on the face?
**Answer: The elastic cord.**
