# Masks — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A mask is drawn and coloured before its spaces are cut out.
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Paper plates can be used to make a mask.
**Answer: True.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The eye spaces of a mask are left closed.
**Answer: False — the eye, nose and mouth spaces are cut out.**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Ribbons can decorate a mask.
**Answer: True.**
