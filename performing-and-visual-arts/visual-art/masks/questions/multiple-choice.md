# Masks — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A mask in this lesson is made by…
a) drawing and colouring a mask shape, then cutting out the spaces  b) buying a mask  c) folding paper only
**Answer: a) drawing and colouring a mask shape, then cutting out the spaces**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which spaces are cut out of the mask?
a) only the ears  b) the eye, nose and mouth spaces  c) the whole middle
**Answer: b) the eye, nose and mouth spaces**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Which of these can a mask be assembled from?
a) glass and metal  b) card, coloured paper, ribbons, paper plates, elastic cord and glue  c) clay only
**Answer: b) card, coloured paper, ribbons, paper plates, elastic cord and glue**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** The elastic cord on a mask is for…
a) holding the mask on  b) colouring it  c) cutting it
**Answer: a) holding the mask on**

**5. (Sub lesson 1)** Before cutting, the pupil first…
a) throws the card away  b) learns the mask shapes and draws the mask  c) glues everything shut
**Answer: b) learns the mask shapes and draws the mask**
