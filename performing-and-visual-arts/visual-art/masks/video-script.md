# Video script — Masks

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A flat shape on the table — card, coloured paper, a paper plate.
Draw the mask. Colour it.
Cut the eyes. Cut the nose. Cut the mouth.
Ribbons on. Elastic cord on.
Now the mask is ready — a new face, made by your own hands.
