# Cubism

## Do it first

Quick start: from the title alone, guess what this lesson's work is. Then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — Geometric forms and natural forms

The programme's Unité 2 teaches the learner to **tell geometric forms from natural or biomorphic forms**, and to **decompose an object or picture into geometric forms** — seeing the shapes a thing is built from.

## Sub lesson 2 — Cubism and the Vèvès

For that form work the programme names **its own paired references: cubism and the Vèvès of Haiti (Haitian sacred art)** — the European movement that broke things into geometric faces, set beside Haiti's own sacred drawn signs, each a road into form.
