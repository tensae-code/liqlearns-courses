# Cubism — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the two kinds of form the unit tells apart.
**Answer: Geometric forms and natural (biomorphic) forms.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does cubism do to the things it shows?
**Answer: It decomposes them into geometric forms.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** From which country are the Vèvès?
**Answer: Haiti.**
