# Cubism — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Decomposing a picture means…
a) breaking it into geometric forms  b) colouring it red  c) tearing it up
**Answer: a) breaking it into geometric forms** — breaking it into its geometric forms.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A leaf's outline is an example of…
a) a natural (biomorphic) form  b) a geometric form  c) a sound
**Answer: a) a natural (biomorphic) form** — a natural or biomorphic form.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Cubism is paired in the programme with…
a) the Vèvès of Haiti  b) pointillism  c) photography
**Answer: a) the Vèvès of Haiti** — the Vèvès — Haitian sacred art.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The Vèvès are…
a) Haitian sacred art  b) a French dance  c) a kind of clay
**Answer: a) Haitian sacred art** — Haitian sacred art, named as the programme's own reference.

**5. (Sub lesson 1)** Which is a geometric form?
a) a triangle  b) a cloud's edge  c) a leaf
**Answer: a) a triangle** — a triangle.
