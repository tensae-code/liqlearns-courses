## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **China Grade 1 Art Vol.1** — China's traditional folk toys — Shandong wooden drum-and-cymbal carts and twin-bird carts, Henan wooden painted eggs
2. **China Grade 1 Art Vol.2** — China's rocking folk toys — Shandong clay-and-paper tumblers (不倒翁), a Beijing hemp-stalk balance bird, wooden rocking horses
3. **China Grade 2 Art Vol.1** — China's folk clay and dough figures — Tianjin "Clay Figure Zhang" (泥人张) Sun Wukong and a Hebei flour-dough child riding a tiger; China's traditional toys across history — Shang-dynasty pottery xun whistles, Huaiyang clay dogs, Shanxi cloth tigers, rattle drums, Taian bamboo dragons, Beijing sand-swallow kites, diabolos, bristle figures and tumblers — plus world toys and a tangram extension
4. **China Grade 3 Art Vol.1** — China's sounding folk toys — rattle drums, Henan clay whistles and clay tigers, Guizhou pottery whistles, painted egg shakers — and the three sound principles: striking, friction and air through holes
5. **China Grade 4 Art Vol.1** — China's moving folk toys — Quanzhou (Fujian) string puppets and Beijing bristle figures; toys move by jumping, pulling or stored power
6. **China HS Crafts** — The Luban lock — six notched bars that lock into a solid ball of joinery with no nail or glue, from the mortise-and-tenon family (dovetail, shouldered, wedge-pinned and a dozen more); plane the bars true, mark the centre lines and the waste, saw inside the line and chisel the slots; carving the wooden mooncake mould — the cake's face is the mould's hollow, carved layer by layer from the centre out (longevity peach, double-happiness, grass patterns — the folk language of blessing); soak the finished mould in oil two days so cakes release; press, knock out, bake at 160 °C, and rest the cakes 3–5 days for the oil to return
