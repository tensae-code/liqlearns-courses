# Elements and Principles of Design — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number, as before. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — What design is
Pass mark: define design as size, shape, colour, form and texture beautifully and repeatedly organised, and say the chapter's order: the elements of design first, then its principles.

## Sub lesson 2 — The elements, one by one
Pass mark: name the six elements as the book's §1.1.1 walks them — line, colour, texture, pattern-shape, space, form — and say each one's mark: line's kinds and expressive roles; warm colour cheering and cool calming; texture as surface quality; pattern-shape as repeated shape; space as the area a work organises; form as three-dimensional, shown by light and shade.

## Sub lesson 3 — The principles, one by one
Pass mark: name the principles as the book lists them — pattern, contrast, emphasis, balance, scale, unity/harmony (ውህደት), rhythm and repetition, oneness (አንድነት) and variety — and point out at least three of them at work in a real design.

## Sub lesson 4 — Teacher-guide evidence — the elements at work in 3D art
Pass mark: say, as teacher-guide evidence, that the Grade 9 guide reviews the elements of art as design tools and applies them to three-dimensional work (Lessons 12–13), and name one way an element's job changes when the work has depth.
