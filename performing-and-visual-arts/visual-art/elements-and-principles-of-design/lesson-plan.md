# Lesson plan — Elements and Principles of Design

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Visual Art · **Lesson:** Elements and Principles of Design
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 2 — Visual Art (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** design as a system: what design is, its elements one by one — line, colour, texture, pattern-shape, space, form — and its principles one by one — the rules by which the elements are organised beautifully and repeatedly.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Elements of Visual Art, where the learner met the visual elements from the Grade 5 book. This lesson carries the Grade 7 book's own system: design defined, elements first, principles second — and a teacher-guide evidence part that applies the elements to three-dimensional art.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
1. **What design is** — the Grade 7 definition: size, shape, colour, form and texture, beautifully and repeatedly organised.
2. **The elements, one by one** — line, colour, texture, pattern-shape, space, form — each with its own work.
3. **The principles, one by one** — pattern, contrast, emphasis, balance, scale, unity/harmony, rhythm and repetition, oneness and variety.
4. **Teacher-guide evidence — the elements at work in 3D art** — labelled evidence from the Grade 9 guide: the elements reviewed as design tools, applied to three-dimensional work.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

- Recommended first: Elements of Visual Art (Visual Art) — the elements met first from the Grade 5 book. Recommended, never forced.
- Recommended first: Shapes, Forms and Patterns in Colour (Visual Art) — pattern and shape at work. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Define design in the Grade 7 book's words.
2. Name the elements of design and say what each one does.
3. Name the principles of design and say how they organise the elements.
4. Say, from the Grade 9 guide's evidence, how the elements serve as design tools in three-dimensional work.

## What you need
Paper, pencil, colours — and designed things to look at: a woven cloth, a pot, a printed page, a chair.

## If the learner is stuck
- Elements and principles blur together → elements are the things; principles are what you DO with the things. Line is an element; repetition is a principle working on it.
- A design looks messy → find its principle fault first: is it balance? unity? One named fault is fixable.
