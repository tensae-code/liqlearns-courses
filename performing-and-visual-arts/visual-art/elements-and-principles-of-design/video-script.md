# Video script — Elements and Principles of Design


The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.


---


What is **design**? Size, shape, colour, form and texture — **beautifully and repeatedly organised**.

First the **elements** — the things themselves: line… colour… texture… pattern-shape… space… form.

Then the **principles** — what you do with the things: pattern, contrast, emphasis, balance, scale, unity, rhythm and repetition, variety.

Elements are the bricks. Principles are the building.

Look at any made thing now — you can name how it was designed.
