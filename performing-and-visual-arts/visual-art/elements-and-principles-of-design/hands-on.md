# Hands-on — Elements and Principles of Design

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Label a designed thing
Take a designed object — a woven cloth, a pot, a chair. On paper, list its elements one by one: its lines, colours, texture, pattern-shape, space and form. Beside each, write where you see it.

## One element, three principles
Draw one simple shape. Organise it three ways: by repetition (the shape again and again), by contrast (one shape different from the rest), and by balance (the shapes resting evenly). Label each drawing with its principle.

## Judge a design
Find a design you think works and one you think fails. For each, name the element or principle responsible. That naming — not 'I like it' — is design judgement.
