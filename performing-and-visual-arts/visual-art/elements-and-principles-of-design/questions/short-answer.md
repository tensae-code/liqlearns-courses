# Elements and Principles of Design — short answer

Answer in a sentence or a short list.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the five things the definition says are organised in a design.
**Answer:** Size, shape, colour, form and texture.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Form is shown to be three-dimensional by what two things?
**Answer:** Light and shade — they predict and show the form (the book's Fig. 6, "the function of form").

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name four principles of design from the book's list.
**Answer:** Any four of: pattern, contrast, emphasis, balance, scale, unity/harmony, rhythm and repetition, oneness, variety.

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** How does space's job change when a work is three-dimensional?
**Answer:** The work stands in real space and holds space inside it — space is no longer only the area organised inside a flat frame.
