# Elements and Principles of Design — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In the definition, the organising of a design is both beautiful and repeated.
**Answer: True** — "beautifully and repeatedly organised".

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Pattern-shape (ዝርግ ቅርፅ) — repeated shape — stands as an element of design in its own right.
**Answer: True** — Fig. 4's plate: repeated shape as an element.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Unity/harmony (ውህደት) and oneness (አንድነት) are both named among the book's principles.
**Answer: True** — both stand in the §1.1.2 roll.

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** This sub lesson's source is a teacher's guide, not a student textbook.
**Answer: True** — Grade 9 has no student book in hand; the part is labelled evidence.
