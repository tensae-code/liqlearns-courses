# Elements and Principles of Design — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Design, in the Grade 7 book's definition, is —
a) any drawing that looks nice  b) size, shape, colour, form and texture beautifully and repeatedly organised  c) the price of a well-made thing
**Answer: b) size, shape, colour, form and texture beautifully and repeatedly organised** — named things, organised, beautiful, repeated.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The chapter treats first —
a) the principles of design  b) the elements of design  c) the history of design
**Answer: b) the elements of design** — elements first, then principles.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Which element is walked first in the book's list?
a) colour  b) line  c) form
**Answer: b) line** — line — the first element, its kinds and expressive roles.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Warm colours, in the book's teaching —
a) calm the viewer  b) catch the eye and cheer  c) hide the design
**Answer: b) catch the eye and cheer** — warm cheers and catches the eye; cool colours calm.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** A principle of design is —
a) a material like clay  b) a way the elements are organised  c) a kind of pencil
**Answer: b) a way the elements are organised** — principles organise the elements.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** The principle that makes one part stand first before the eye is —
a) balance  b) scale  c) emphasis
**Answer: c) emphasis** — emphasis — the eye is led to one part first.

**7. (Sub lesson 4)** The Grade 9 guide (evidence) applies the elements of art to —
a) two-dimensional posters only  b) three-dimensional work  c) music notation
**Answer: b) three-dimensional work** — Lessons 12–13: elements reviewed as design tools, applied to 3D work.
