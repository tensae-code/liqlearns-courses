# Video script — Woodworking

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Under the bark of every tree trunk lies wood.

The Sumerians made the plough and the wheel from it.

Cut the trunks. Carry them. Strip the bark. Slice, stack, dry.

Hard wood: small fibres, heavy. Soft wood: large fibres, light.

Wood bends to the hand and the machine — that is woodworking.
