## Sub lesson 1 — What wood is

Wood is a solid, rigid material found under the bark of tree trunks. Primitive man built his house from what he gathered of the trees scattered in the forests, then thought of benefiting from tree wood itself. The Sumerians of Mesopotamia were among the first makers of the plough and the wheel from wood — the plough easing farming, the wheel easing movement and travel.

## Sub lesson 2 — Preparing wood, stage by stage

The basic stages of preparing woods: cutting the tree trunks with big mechanical cutters; transporting the trunks by big carriers; extracting the bark layer with specialised devices; cutting the trunks into blocks and slices of different shapes and sizes; stacking the blocks and slices to dry them; and forming and manufacturing them according to life's requirements — boats, houses, carts and more.

## Sub lesson 3 — Hard wood and soft wood

Wood is of two kinds: hard wood, whose fibres are small and cohesive and which is heavy; and soft wood, whose fibres are large and cohesive and which is somewhat light. The wood material is among the most formable materials, mechanically and by hand — its features are the variety of its types, shapes and sizes and the ease of cutting and forming it as the need requires.
