# Hands-on — Graphic Design

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Design a cover
Design the cover of a magazine about your community's culture: a title, one drawing or photograph, and the names of two things inside. Arrange them so the cover speaks at a glance.

## Lay out one page
Lay out one page with an article, an interview extract and a picture. Show a partner how your layout guides their eye.
