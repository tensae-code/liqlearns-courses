# Lesson plan — Graphic Design

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Visual Art · **Lesson:** Graphic Design
**Place in the curriculum:** Visual Art (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** producing a magazine of your community's culture — designing its cover and laying out its articles, interviews, drawings and photographs for print, digital or radio.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this course in the same title.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Publicarte: a magazine of your community's culture** — Guatemala's Grade 6 experience “Publicarte” sets the class to **produce a magazine of their community's culture** — its **dances, folk music, crafts, typical food, celebrations, Maya languages and stories**.
2. **Designing and presenting the magazine** — The magazine is made **in print, digital or radio-magazine form**, with a **designed cover, articles, interviews, drawings and photographs**, and it is **presented to the community**.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of this course in the same title. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name what a community-culture magazine carries — dances, folk music, crafts, typical food, celebrations, Maya languages and stories.
2. Design a cover and lay out a page with articles, interviews, drawings and photographs, for print, digital or radio form.

## What you need
Paper and colours; a camera or phone if you have one.
