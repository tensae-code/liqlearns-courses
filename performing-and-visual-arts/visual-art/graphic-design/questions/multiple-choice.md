# Graphic Design — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Publicarte magazine is about…
a) the community's own culture  b) foreign celebrities only  c) sports results
**Answer: a) the community's own culture.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which of these belongs in the magazine?
a) dances, folk music and crafts  b) only advertisements  c) only timetables
**Answer: a) dances, folk music and crafts.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The magazine also carries the community's…
a) Maya languages and stories  b) tax forms  c) exam answers
**Answer: a) Maya languages and stories.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The magazine can be made in…
a) print, digital or radio-magazine form  b) only stone carving  c) only handwriting
**Answer: a) print, digital or radio-magazine form.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** A designed cover must…
a) speak at a glance  b) hide the title  c) be blank
**Answer: a) speak at a glance.**
