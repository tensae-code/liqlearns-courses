# Light Painting

## Do it first

Dark-room game: a grown-up waves a torch in the air. Watch the trail your eyes keep for a moment — now imagine the camera keeping the whole trail. That kept trail is a light painting.

## Sub lesson 1 — Drawing with light

**Light painting** draws with light itself. The Finnish sheet teaches it step by step:

1. **Tape colour film over a torch.**
2. **Set a phone camera to a long exposure — 10 to 30 seconds — on self-timer**, standing on a flat surface in a **dark room**.
3. **Move the torch** — slowly, fast, held still, switched off for a moment, or in **two colours** — to **draw shapes or write words** in the photograph.

The cover's own pictures show hearts and lettering drawn in the air with a coloured torch. The camera craft inside the activity — exposure time, self-timer, steady surface — is stocked in the Photography lesson.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson taught, part by part. Companion checks each part against the sub lessons above.

## Do It Yourself

1. Make your own piece using this lesson's way, from start to finish.
2. Show it to the class and say what you made, how, and what you learnt.
3. Write one sentence: what will you do differently next time?
