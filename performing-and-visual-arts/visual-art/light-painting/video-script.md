# Video script — Light Painting

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Dark room. Torch ready. Film on the torch — colour!

Camera waits… ten, twenty, thirty seconds open.

Move slow — a line. Move fast — a ribbon. Stop — a star. Two colours — a rainbow heart!

The light paints. The camera keeps the painting.
