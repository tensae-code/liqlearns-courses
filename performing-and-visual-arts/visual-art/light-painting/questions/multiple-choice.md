# Light Painting — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In light painting, the picture is drawn with…
a) a pencil  b) a moving torch in front of a camera  c) chalk
**Answer: b) a moving torch in front of a camera**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The camera's exposure is set to…
a) 10–30 seconds  b) one second  c) one hour
**Answer: a) 10–30 seconds**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Before drawing, you tape over the torch…
a) paper  b) colour film  c) a cloth
**Answer: b) colour film**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** The camera stands…
a) in your hand, shaking  b) on a flat surface, on self-timer, in a dark room  c) outside in sunshine
**Answer: b) on a flat surface, on self-timer, in a dark room**
