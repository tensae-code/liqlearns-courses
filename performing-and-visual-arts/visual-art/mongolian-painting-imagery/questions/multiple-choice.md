# Mongolian Painting Imagery — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** The Grade 8 lesson names Mongolian painting's…
a) imagery and depiction features (дүр, дүрслэлийн онцлог)  b) frame sizes  c) prices
**Answer: a) imagery and depiction features (дүр, дүрслэлийн онцлог)**

**2.** Grade 5 Mongolian painting is drawn…
a) with space — and used for decorating  b) flat only, never with space  c) only in black
**Answer: a) with space — and used for decorating**
