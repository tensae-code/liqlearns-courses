# Art for Social Change

## Do it first

Name one thing that belongs in this lesson — Making art to change society and protect the environment — art created for a cause, not only for display. Say what it is in your own words. If you can name one, you are already inside the lesson.

## Sub lesson 1 — Bab IV Berkarya Seni untuk Perubahan

the Design for Change way of working — feel (map the problem in the school environment, form a group identity), imagine (alternative solutions), do (make the artwork as the solution), share — including a social-campaign poster and a short campaign video; environmental art (seni lingkungan).

## Sub lesson 2 — Bab IV Berkarya Seni Rupa untuk Lingkungan

art that answers a problem — identifying environmental problems nearby, forming the big idea, and making art that responds: a poster about environmental cleanliness carrying a moral message.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson holds, part by part, in your own words. Companion checks: did every part get named, with its Indonesian name kept where the book gives one?

## Do It Yourself

1. Choose one named part of this lesson and find out one more true thing about it — from a person who knows it, or from the book your part came from.
2. Make or perform a small piece in the way of that part: a rhythm, a step, a drawing, a shaped piece of clay.
3. Write one sentence: what does this part show about Indonesia's own way of teaching its arts?
