# Video script — Art for Social Change

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This is Indonesia's own lesson.

**Bab IV Berkarya Seni untuk Perubahan.** the Design for Change way of working — feel (map the problem in the school environment, form a group identity), imagine (alternative solutions), do (make the artwork as the solution), share — including a social-campaign poster and a short campaign video; environmental art (seni lingkungan).

**Bab IV Berkarya Seni Rupa untuk Lingkungan.** art that answers a problem — identifying environmental problems nearby, forming the big idea, and making art that responds: a poster about environmental cleanliness carrying a moral message.

That is Art for Social Change — Indonesia's way, kept by name.
