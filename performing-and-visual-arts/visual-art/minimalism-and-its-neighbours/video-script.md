This is North Macedonia's own lesson.

**the movements of the late 1960s–70s, first group.** the movements of the late 1960s–70s, first group: Minimalism's simple geometry (Judd, Stella, Flavin); Photorealism painting reality like a photograph (Chuck Close, Estes, Flack); Arte Povera — Italy's "poor art" of simple everyday materials against consumer society (Pistoletto's "Venus of the Rags", Kounellis, Merz); Feminist art answering gender inequality (Judy Chicago, Barbara Kruger, Cindy Sherman).

**Минимализам.** Минимализам — Minimalism: strict geometry and repeated identical forms that make the viewer study what little is there — Judd, Andre, Morris.

**Хиперреализам.** Хиперреализам — Hyperrealism: the everyday painted and sculpted more real than real — shoppers and streets at life size, in real clothes — Hanson.

That is Minimalism and Its Neighbours — North Macedonia's way, kept by name.
