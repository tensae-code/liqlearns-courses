## Sub lesson 1 — the movements of the late 1960s–70s, first group
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: the movements of the late 1960s–70s, first group: Minimalism's simple geometry (Judd, Stella, Flavin); Photorealism painting reality like a photograph (Chuck Close, Estes, Flack); Arte Povera — Italy's "poor art" of simple everyday materials against consumer society (Pistoletto's "Venus of the Rags", Kounellis, Merz); Feminist art answering gender inequality (Judy Chicago, Barbara Kruger, Cindy Sherman).

## Sub lesson 2 — Минимализам
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Минимализам — Minimalism: strict geometry and repeated identical forms that make the viewer study what little is there — Judd, Andre, Morris.

## Sub lesson 3 — Хиперреализам
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Хиперреализам — Hyperrealism: the everyday painted and sculpted more real than real — shoppers and streets at life size, in real clothes — Hanson.
