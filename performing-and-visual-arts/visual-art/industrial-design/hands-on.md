# Hands-on — Industrial Design

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Book 3, Design, "Industrial Design"
Do the thing this part teaches, with real materials where the book names them: industrial design as the science of treating three-dimensional products so they perform soundly and give the user physical and aesthetic comfort — kitchen tools, electrical devices, furniture, hospital equipment, farm machines, toys, aircraft and car shapes — renewing every product in wood, iron or plastic as human circumstances change, and the designer as the engine linking art, production and the public in a country's industrial renaissance
