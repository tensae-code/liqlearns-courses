# Industrial Design — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is industrial design, in the book's words?
**Answer: The science of treating three-dimensional products so they perform soundly and give the user physical and aesthetic comfort.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Why must products be renewed?
**Answer: Because human circumstances change.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Name two products the book names.
**Answer: Any two of: kitchen tools, electrical devices, furniture, hospital equipment, farm machines, toys, aircraft, car shapes.**
