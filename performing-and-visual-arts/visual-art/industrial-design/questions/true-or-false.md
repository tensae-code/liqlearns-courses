# Industrial Design — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Industrial design is a science of three-dimensional products.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The designer links art, production and the public.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Industrial design never changes its products.
**Answer: False** — It renews every product as human circumstances change.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Furniture and hospital equipment fall under industrial design.
**Answer: True**
