# Industrial Design — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Industrial design treats products so they give the user…
a) physical and aesthetic comfort  b) only cheapness  c) only speed
**Answer: a) physical and aesthetic comfort** — products that perform soundly and give physical and aesthetic comfort.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The designer is described as the engine linking…
a) art, production and the public  b) school and home  c) past and future
**Answer: a) art, production and the public** — in a country's industrial renaissance.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Industrial design renews products in…
a) wood, iron or plastic  b) only gold  c) only cloth
**Answer: a) wood, iron or plastic** — as human circumstances change.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Which of these is named as industrial design's field?
a) kitchen tools and farm machines  b) poems  c) songs
**Answer: a) kitchen tools and farm machines** — kitchen tools, electrical devices, furniture, hospital equipment, farm machines, toys, aircraft and car shapes.
