| Vietnam — Bài 1 Did-You-Know — design defined (the creative work of design artists on the basis of the visual art language — dot, line, area, mass, colour, material; "design" means sketching, drawing a model, making a model and joining the mass-production process, the fine-art and technical elements unified on one product) and the branch's history — from ancient times applied-product design was attached to handicraft workers and artisans who thought up the model, manufactured and completed the product in a closed process with rudimentary tools, singly, practical and eye-pleasing; when the market needed products both convenient and aesthetic beyond the aristocratic class and industrial production developed, production professionalised into a technical part (answering use function) and a fine-art part (answering aesthetic taste), and the Industrial Design branch appeared with a rather independent position; today products must also be convenient, attractive and safe, with machinery calculated for human stature, physical strength and labour psychology and for human abilities and limits, so the branch's contributions show a humanistic character, not only aesthetic value (industrial robot shown) (student book) | Book 13, printed p. 8 |
