# Fauvism — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What must the viewer forget in a Fauve panel?
**Answer: What the panel represents — shapes are simplified until the viewer must forget the subject.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What carries feeling in Fauvism?
**Answer: Colour's heat.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Who is named as the school's nearest Arab follower?
**Answer: The Arab artist Mohamed Moussa Naji of Alexandria.**
