# Fauvism — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Fauvism was named from the 1905 exhibition's…
a) 'cage of wild beasts'  b) quiet room  c) blue hall
**Answer: a) 'cage of wild beasts'**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The Fauves painted in…
a) pure colours at utmost contrast  b) grey only  c) black and white
**Answer: a) pure colours at utmost contrast** — red, blue, green, violet, orange.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** In Fauvism, perspective is…
a) little regarded  b) the whole point  c) measured exactly
**Answer: a) little regarded** — and feeling is carried by colour's heat.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Which painter is named among the Fauves?
a) Matisse  b) Monet  c) Rembrandt
**Answer: a) Matisse** — Matisse, Derain, Vlaminck, Dufy and Marquet.
