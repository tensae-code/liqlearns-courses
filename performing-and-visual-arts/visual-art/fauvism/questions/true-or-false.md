# Fauvism — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Fauvism means 'wild beasts' painting.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Fauve colours are pale and mixed to grey.
**Answer: False** — They are pure colours at the utmost contrast and harmony.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The arabesque's interlacing was borrowed by the Fauves.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Fauvism began with the 1905 exhibition.
**Answer: True**
