# Hands-on — Fauvism

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Book 1, Artistic Culture, "The Fauvist School"
Do the thing this part teaches, with real materials where the book names them: Fauvism — the "cage of wild beasts" of the 1905 exhibition: Matisse, Derain, Vlaminck, Dufy and Marquet painting in pure colours (red, blue, green, violet, orange) at the utmost contrast and harmony, shapes simplified until the viewer must forget what the panel represents, perspective little regarded, the arabesque's interlacing borrowed, and feeling carried by colour's heat — with the Arab artist Mohamed Moussa Naji of Alexandria, formed in Florence and Paris, named as the school's nearest Arab follower
