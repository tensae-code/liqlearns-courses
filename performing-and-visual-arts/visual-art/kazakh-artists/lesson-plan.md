# Lesson plan — Kazakh Artists

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Visual Art · **Lesson:** Kazakh Artists
**Place in the curriculum:** Visual Art (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Kazakhstan's famous artists and people of art, met through the country's own lessons..
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this course in the same title.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Kazakhstan Grade 1 Visual Art Guide** — the year's last drawing and modelling subjects from the home land
2. **Kazakhstan Grade 3 Muratkyzy Labour** — the wood master (agash sheberi) and his carved works; the painter Abilkhan Kasteev and his portraits, with a 3D picture made in his honour
3. **Kazakhstan Grade 3 Tolebiev Visual Art** — portrait and its kinds (Mona Lisa; Kasteev's "Zhas Abai"; group and ceremonial portraits; chamber portrait); self-portrait (Dürer's first self-portrait, Raphael, Ismailova) drawn; "I too will be a batyr": the Raiymbek and Aliya-Manshuk monuments and a batyr relief made
4. **Kazakhstan Grade 3 Zhanaev Visual Art** — portrait and self-portrait; Kasteev's self-portrait and Ualikhanov's portrait compared; a portrait drawn by the face-proportion scheme
5. **Kazakhstan Grade 3 Adamkulov Labour** — a portrait frame (zhaktau) made; the first Kazakh architect Toleu Basenov and a building modelled; sculptor Khasanzhan Nauryzbaev and a wood sculpture; the famous jeweller Aman Mukazhan and bead jewellery made; the sewing craft: a dress sewn to a pattern
6. **Kazakhstan Grade 3 Rabilova Visual Art** — Lessons 24-26 "How natural forms become decorative" (Kasteev at the State Museum; a decorative landscape written), "Architectural monuments of Kazakhstan. Designing buildings" (the Abai monuments, Yasawi and Aisha Bibi mausoleums; a museum/theatre project drawn), "Nauryz. A festive card" (Saki Romanov; a tulip card)
7. **Kazakhstan Grade 3 Tulebiev Visual Art RU** — the colours of New Year and of Nauryz read as emotions (a colour-emotion table: red energy, yellow sun, green renewal)
8. **Kazakhstan Grade 3 Raupova Art Labour** — Abai in painting and sculpture, Kasteev's Abai, Shokan Ualikhanov's portrait; portrait kinds (ceremonial, chamber, group, family); a frame made for a famous person's portrait
9. **Kazakhstan Grade 4 Mamyrova Visual Art** — the artistic image across plane and volume; Sidorkin's graphics for the Kazakh epics (Kozy Korpesh, Kambar Batyr); Iliyev's monument sculpture
