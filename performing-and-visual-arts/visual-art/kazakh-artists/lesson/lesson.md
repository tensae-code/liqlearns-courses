# Kazakh Artists

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about kazakh artists. Then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — Kazakhstan Grade 1 Visual Art Guide

the year's last drawing and modelling subjects from the home land

## Sub lesson 2 — Kazakhstan Grade 3 Muratkyzy Labour

the wood master (agash sheberi) and his carved works; the painter Abilkhan Kasteev and his portraits, with a 3D picture made in his honour

## Sub lesson 3 — Kazakhstan Grade 3 Tolebiev Visual Art

portrait and its kinds (Mona Lisa; Kasteev's "Zhas Abai"; group and ceremonial portraits; chamber portrait); self-portrait (Dürer's first self-portrait, Raphael, Ismailova) drawn; "I too will be a batyr": the Raiymbek and Aliya-Manshuk monuments and a batyr relief made

## Sub lesson 4 — Kazakhstan Grade 3 Zhanaev Visual Art

portrait and self-portrait; Kasteev's self-portrait and Ualikhanov's portrait compared; a portrait drawn by the face-proportion scheme

## Sub lesson 5 — Kazakhstan Grade 3 Adamkulov Labour

a portrait frame (zhaktau) made; the first Kazakh architect Toleu Basenov and a building modelled; sculptor Khasanzhan Nauryzbaev and a wood sculpture; the famous jeweller Aman Mukazhan and bead jewellery made; the sewing craft: a dress sewn to a pattern

## Sub lesson 6 — Kazakhstan Grade 3 Rabilova Visual Art

Lessons 24-26 "How natural forms become decorative" (Kasteev at the State Museum; a decorative landscape written), "Architectural monuments of Kazakhstan. Designing buildings" (the Abai monuments, Yasawi and Aisha Bibi mausoleums; a museum/theatre project drawn), "Nauryz. A festive card" (Saki Romanov; a tulip card)

## Sub lesson 7 — Kazakhstan Grade 3 Tulebiev Visual Art RU

the colours of New Year and of Nauryz read as emotions (a colour-emotion table: red energy, yellow sun, green renewal)

## Sub lesson 8 — Kazakhstan Grade 3 Raupova Art Labour

Abai in painting and sculpture, Kasteev's Abai, Shokan Ualikhanov's portrait; portrait kinds (ceremonial, chamber, group, family); a frame made for a famous person's portrait

## Sub lesson 9 — Kazakhstan Grade 4 Mamyrova Visual Art

the artistic image across plane and volume; Sidorkin's graphics for the Kazakh epics (Kozy Korpesh, Kambar Batyr); Iliyev's monument sculpture

Take one part of this lesson and do it again in your own way — then say which book part yours follows.
