# Lesson plan — Baker Lake Wall Hangings

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Visual Art · **Lesson:** Baker Lake Wall Hangings
**Place in the curriculum:** Visual Art (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the woven wall hangings of Qamani'tuaq (Baker Lake) — cloth worked into Arctic designs of animals and people, the art Baker Lake artists are known for.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this course in the same title.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **ᓄᕕᖅᓴᖅᓯᒪᔪᑦ ᓂᕕᖓᑖᑦ — hangings woven for the wall** — **Wall hangings** are woven and embroidered from strips and cloth, made to hang on the wall.
2. **Southern wool, Arctic designs** — The hangings work **southern wool** into **Arctic designs of animals and people**.

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks a wall hanging is a painting → it is woven and embroidered cloth — textile art made for the wall.
- Mixes Baker Lake with Pangnirtung → Baker Lake (Qamani'tuaq) is the wall-hanging place in this book; Pangnirtung is named for its print centre.
