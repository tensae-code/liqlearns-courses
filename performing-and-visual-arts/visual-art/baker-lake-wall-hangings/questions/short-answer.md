# Baker Lake Wall Hangings — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In Inuktitut syllabics, how does the book head this part?
**Answer: ᓄᕕᖅᓴᖅᓯᒪᔪᑦ ᓂᕕᖓᑖᑦ — the woven wall hangings.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** From what are the hangings woven or embroidered?
**Answer: From strips and cloth.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What place name does the book give Baker Lake in Inuktitut?
**Answer: Qamani'tuaq.**
