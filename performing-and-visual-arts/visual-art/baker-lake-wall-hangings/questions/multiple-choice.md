# Baker Lake Wall Hangings — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Baker Lake artists are known for…
a) stone carving  b) woven wall hangings  c) silver jewellery
**Answer: b) woven wall hangings** — wall hangings woven/embroidered from strips and cloth.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A wall hanging is made to…
a) be worn as clothing  b) hang on the wall  c) cover the floor
**Answer: b) hang on the wall** — it is textile art made for the wall.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The designs worked into the hangings show…
a) southern cities  b) Arctic animals and people  c) letters and numbers
**Answer: b) Arctic animals and people** — Arctic designs of animals and people.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The wool worked into the hangings comes from…
a) the south  b) the Arctic Ocean  c) caribou
**Answer: a) the south** — southern wool worked into Arctic designs.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The book shows Baker Lake works from…
a) the McCord Museum  b) the Jessie Oonark Centre  c) Dorset Fine Arts
**Answer: b) the Jessie Oonark Centre** — works shown are Jessie Oonark Centre works.
