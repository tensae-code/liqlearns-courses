# Baker Lake Wall Hangings — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Wall hangings are a textile art — worked in cloth, not carved in stone.
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Baker Lake is known above all for its silver jewellery.
**Answer: False — Baker Lake artists are known for woven wall hangings.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The hangings join southern material with Arctic designs.
**Answer: True — southern wool, Arctic designs of animals and people.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The Jessie Oonark Centre is in Pangnirtung.
**Answer: False — the Jessie Oonark Centre is in Qamani'tuaq (Baker Lake).**
