# Hands-on — Baker Lake Wall Hangings

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Design a hanging
On paper or cloth, draw a strip design of Arctic animals and people the way a Baker Lake hanging carries them. Say where your hanging would hang.

## Wool and design
With wool, thread or strips of cloth, work one small repeated motif — an animal or a person — and mount it flat, ready for a wall.
