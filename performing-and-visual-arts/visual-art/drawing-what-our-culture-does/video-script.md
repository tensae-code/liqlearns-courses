# Video script — Drawing What Our Culture Does

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

What does our culture DO?
It works. It plays. It celebrates.

Oxen threshing, round the stone ring.
Children dancing together.
Genna — sticks and a ball, run!
Coffee roasting — smell the smoke.

Choose one. Draw it big.
Who is there? What are they doing?
Put your feeling in the picture.
That is drawing what our culture does.
