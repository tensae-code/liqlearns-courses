# Hands-on — Drawing What Our Culture Does


The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Watch, then draw
If you can, watch a real practice first — coffee being roasted, grain being worked, a game being played — for five quiet minutes. Then draw it the same day, while your eyes still remember.

## The practice parade
Pin the class's culture drawings in a row: threshing, dancing, genna, coffee, and everyone's own-area practices. Walk the row together and let each drawer say one sentence about their practice.
