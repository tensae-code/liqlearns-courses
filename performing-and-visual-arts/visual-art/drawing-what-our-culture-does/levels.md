# Drawing What Our Culture Does — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: choose one of the four pictured practices (or the same kind from your own area), draw it with its people in action in their place, and say what feeling the drawing expresses.
Practice deepens inside the one skill: more items to work with, less help from the models, and harder corners — working faster without losing care, and doing the same skill on new things the learner chooses.
