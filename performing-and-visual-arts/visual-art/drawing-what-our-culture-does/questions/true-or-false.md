# Drawing What Our Culture Does — true or false

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1.** The book asks learners only to copy the practice pictures exactly.
**Answer: False — learners draw cultural performances and express their feelings in the drawing.**

**2.** You may draw a cultural practice from your own area, not only the book's four.
**Answer: True — the lesson's own work sends you to the practices of your home and street.**
