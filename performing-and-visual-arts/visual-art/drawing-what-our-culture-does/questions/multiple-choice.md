# Drawing What Our Culture Does — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which four practices do the book's drawing pages show?
a) threshing, dancing, the genna game, coffee roasting  b) reading, writing, counting, singing  c) building, driving, cooking rice, sleeping
**Answer: a) threshing, dancing, the genna game, coffee roasting.**

**2.** The genna game is played with —
a) ropes  b) curved sticks and a ball  c) stones only
**Answer: b) curved sticks and a ball — a hockey-like game.**

**3.** At threshing, the oxen walk —
a) in a line to market  b) round and round inside a stone-ringed floor  c) up a hill
**Answer: b) round and round inside a stone-ringed threshing floor, treading the grain out.**

**4.** A feeling can be put into a drawing by —
a) drawing the practice big, bright and with its details  b) leaving the page empty  c) hiding the picture
**Answer: a) drawing it big, bright and detailed — joy, pride or love of home, on purpose.**

**5.** The coffee picture shows —
a) a pan, rising smoke and cups waiting  b) a tea pot only  c) coffee trees
**Answer: a) a pan, rising smoke and cups waiting.**
