# Video script — Chiaroscuro: Light and Shadow

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A circle on paper is flat. Light and shadow make it round.

See the fruit: bright where the light lands, dark where it turns away.

Cézanne showed it. Zahra Al-Ghamdi paints it too.

Draw your fruit. Grade the pastel from light to dark.

That grading — that is chiaroscuro.
