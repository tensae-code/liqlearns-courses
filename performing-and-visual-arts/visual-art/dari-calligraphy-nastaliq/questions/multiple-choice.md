# Dari Calligraphy: Nastaliq — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Nastaliq letters are written on…
a) the baseline (khat-e korsi)  b) a spiral  c) the margin only
**Answer: a) the baseline (khat-e korsi)**

**2.** The dot measure of a letter is called…
a) nuqta-guzari (نقطه‌گذاری)  b) tazhib  c) sarmashq
**Answer: a) nuqta-guzari (نقطه‌گذاری)**

**3.** Chalipa is…
a) a panel composed in a crossed diagonal form  b) a kind of ink  c) a reed pen
**Answer: a) a panel composed in a crossed diagonal form**

**4.** Siyah-mashq means…
a) the 'black practice' — repeated words written dense and dark until the page becomes a picture  b) writing with no ink  c) erasing a page
**Answer: a) the 'black practice' — repeated words written dense and dark until the page becomes a picture**
