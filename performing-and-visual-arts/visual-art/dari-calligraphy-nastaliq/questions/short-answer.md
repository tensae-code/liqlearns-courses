# Dari Calligraphy: Nastaliq — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1.** Which two ways of writing س/ص does the Grade 4 book teach?
**Answer: Toothed (dandana-dar) and stretched (kashish-dar).**

**2.** Name three of the Grade 4 whole words and their meanings.
**Answer: Any three of: adab (manners), rasti (truthfulness), solh (peace), qanun (law), kitab (book), madar (mother), nan (bread), honar (art).**
