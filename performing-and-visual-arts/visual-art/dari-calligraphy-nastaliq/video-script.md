# Video script — Dari Calligraphy: Nastaliq

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Now the hand slopes — Nastaliq.

Each letter has its movements. Count them. Count its dots.
A word alone — kitab, book. Madar, mother. Honar, art.
Then the word inside a sentence, flowing on the baseline.
And at the end: chalipa, the crossed panel — and siyah-mashq, the black page that becomes a picture.
