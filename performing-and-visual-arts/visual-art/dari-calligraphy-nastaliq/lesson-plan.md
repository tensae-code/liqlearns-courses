# Lesson plan — Dari Calligraphy: Nastaliq

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Visual Art · **Lesson:** Dari Calligraphy: Nastaliq
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 2 — Visual Art (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** writing Dari in Nastaliq (خط نستعلیق), the sloping Persian calligraphic hand — each letter learned by its movements (حرکت‌ها) and its dot measure (نقطه‌گذاری) on the baseline (خط کرسی), with the reed pen, from single letters to whole words, to sentences, to the composed arts of chalipa (چلیپا) and siyah-mashq (سیاه مشق).
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the learner's earlier visual-art lessons. Recommended, never forced.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Single letters, their movements and their dot measure** — Grade 4 changes the hand from Naskh to Nastaliq. Each letter — آ، ب، ج، د، ر، س، ص، ط، ع، ف، ق، ک، گ، ل، م، ن، و، ه، ی —…
2. **Whole words in Nastaliq** — Then whole chosen words are written with the reed pen on the baseline — آدم (human), ادب (manners), راستی (truthfulness)…
3. **The words inside sentences** — Each practised word is then written correctly inside a full sentence in Nastaliq on the baseline, with all its letter mo…
4. **Combining letters — and the composed page: chalipa and siyah-mashq** — Grade 6 combines the Nastaliq letters in every position of a word, then crowns the subject with two composition arts: ch…
