# Hands-on — Dari Calligraphy: Nastaliq

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Do it the book's way
Write one word — کتاب (book) or هنر (art) — on a baseline, counting its dots aloud as you place each part. Then write it inside a short sentence. Last, make a small siyah-mashq corner: repeat your word, overlapping, darker each time, until the corner of the page turns into a pattern.
