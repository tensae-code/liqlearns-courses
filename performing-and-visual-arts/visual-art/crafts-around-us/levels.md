# Crafts Around Us — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number, as before. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. The sub lessons climb from the three crafts a young child meets first (metal, pottery, weaving) to the whole handicraft family, the three pottery families told apart by heat, and how crafts survive.

## Sub lesson 1 — Metal work
Pass mark: name the curved knife, sickle, pointed tool and hoe blade, and say what each is for.

## Sub lesson 2 — Pottery
Pass mark: tell a ድስት (dist) from a ጀበና (jebena) and say what each pot does.

## Sub lesson 3 — Weaving
Pass mark: explain that the mesob and sefed patterns are made by the weaving itself, strip over strip.

## Sub lesson 4 — Handicrafts old and new
Pass mark: list the craft families — pottery, weaving, wood, jewellery, basketry/sewing, metal — with one product each.

## Sub lesson 5 — Three families of pottery
Pass mark: order earthenware, stoneware and ceramic ware by firing heat, and match each to its strength and use.

## Sub lesson 6 — Crafts kept alive
Pass mark: say how pottery skills pass from mother to daughter, how makers organise in associations and specialise, and name the collectable crafts.
