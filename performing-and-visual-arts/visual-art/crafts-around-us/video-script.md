# Video script — Crafts Around Us

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Look at this table. Everything on it was made by hand.

A **knife**. A **sickle**. A pointed tool. A **hoe blade**.
Metal work made them. Each shape fits its job.

This black pot is a **ድስት** — a dist. It cooks the food.
This round pot is a **ጀበና** — a jebena. It makes the coffee.
Clay, water, hands, fire. That is pottery.

This basket is a **mesob**. It has a lid. Food is served on it.
The colours are not painted. The weaving makes the pattern.

Necklaces. Stools. Baskets. Pots. Tools. Clothes.
All of these are **እደ ጥበብ** — handicrafts, made by hand.

And pottery has three families.
Earthen — soft-fired, weakest. Stone — hard like stone. Ceramic — fired hottest, shining.

Hands teach hands, and the crafts stay alive.
Now you: find one handmade thing near you. What craft made it?
