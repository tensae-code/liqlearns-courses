# Crafts Around Us — true or false

Say if each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A sickle's curved shape helps it gather and cut stems. **Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The dist is a woven basket for serving food. **Answer: False** — the dist is a black clay cooking pot; the mesob is the woven serving basket.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A mesob has a lid. **Answer: True.**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Handicrafts are made by hand or with simple materials. **Answer: True.**

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** Earthenware is the strongest pottery family because it is fired hottest. **Answer: False** — earthenware is fired at low heat and is the weakest; ceramic ware is fired hottest.

**6. (Sub lesson 6)** When makers specialise — one in figurines, one in pots, one in plates — it helps the craft survive. **Answer: True.**
