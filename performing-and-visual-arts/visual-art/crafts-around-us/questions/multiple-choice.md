# Crafts Around Us — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which of these is a metal-work product?
a) a woven sefed  b) a sickle  c) a clay dist
**Answer: b) a sickle** — it is shaped from metal for cutting.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A ጀበና (jebena) is used for —
a) cooking stew  b) brewing and pouring coffee  c) carrying water to the field
**Answer: b) brewing and pouring coffee.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The pattern on a mesob comes from —
a) paint brushed on afterwards  b) the weaving itself, strip over strip  c) stickers stuck on the basket
**Answer: b) the weaving itself.**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Which list is all handicraft families?
a) pottery, weaving, wood, jewellery, basketry, metal  b) singing, dancing, acting  c) reading, writing, counting
**Answer: a)** — those are the እደ ጥበብ families.

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** The pottery family fired at the highest heat, with a shining finish, is —
a) earthenware  b) stoneware  c) ceramic ware
**Answer: c) ceramic ware.**

**6. (Sub lesson 6)** Pottery skills are most often kept alive by —
a) machines in big factories  b) women who inherit their mothers' skills and organise in associations  c) visitors from other countries
**Answer: b)** — the craft passes from hand to hand, mother to daughter.

**7.** (Sub lesson 7) Ancient handcraft differs from other artworks because it is made by hand, intended for use, and —
a) made only by kings  b) makeable by any skilled person  c) never sold
**Answer: b) makeable by any skilled person.**

**8.** (Sub lesson 8) Which is a weaving product?
a) jebena  b) aklil (crown)  c) stone mill
**Answer: b) aklil (crown).**

**9.** (Sub lesson 9) History says pottery in Ethiopia began in —
a) the Axumite era  b) the last century  c) the Zagwe era only
**Answer: a) the Axumite era.**

**10.** (Sub lesson 10) The stone mill (የድንጋይ ወፍጮ) belongs with —
a) weaving  b) ancient stone tools  c) jewellery
**Answer: b) ancient stone tools.**
