# Hands-on — Crafts Around Us

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The craft census
Walk through your home slowly. Count every handmade thing — pots, baskets, stools, tools, cloth, jewellery. Write each one with its craft family: pottery, weaving, wood, jewellery, basketry/sewing, or metal. Which family wins in your home?

## Sort the stall
Draw a market stall table. On it, draw one thing from each craft family and colour it with the material it is made of — grey for metal, clay-brown for pottery, stripes for weaving, gold and silver for jewellery.

## Ask the maker
Find one person near you who makes things by hand — a potter, a weaver, a basket maker, a smith. Ask: what do you make? What is it made of? Who taught you? Thank them, and tell the class one thing you learned.
