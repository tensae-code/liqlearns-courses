# Moldovan Folk Ornaments — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — Motive decorative în arta populară; Arta populară
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: G4 Ch. 3/4, Motive decorative în arta populară; Arta populară — Moldova's folk ornament and folk art: the sun, the human figure, the tree of life, the flower vase, the horse and birds, circle, rhombus and square on national carpets, peasant towels, folk costume, carved gates, veranda posts and painted Easter eggs, and the master folk craftsman (meșter popular) who joins the useful to the beautiful.

## Sub lesson 2 — Arta populară
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Unit 4, Arta populară — Moldova's craft map: pottery centres at Cinișeuți, Iurceni and Țigănești, winter carpet weaving in homes and monastery workshops, the embroidered ie and the folk costume for work and for feasts.

## Sub lesson 3 — Folk creators enrich the ornaments of ceramics
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Unit II §2 — folk creators enrich the ornaments of ceramics, woven fabrics and the national costume with line and point together.

## Sub lesson 4 — The rosette called mandala in the East
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Unit III §15 — the rosette called mandala in the East: the circular decorative motif symbolising light, peace, spiritual purity and perfection, whose minute drawing is used in art therapy.

## Sub lesson 5 — The ornament
Sub lesson 5 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Unit III §16 — the ornament: decorative motifs arranged by repetition, in bands or in networks; the traditional Moldovan motifs a folk master must keep — the tree of life, ram's horns, the rhombus, the spiral, the grape — in the natural colours of the land's plants and soils.

## Sub lesson 6 — The semantics of the motifs
Sub lesson 6 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Unit III §16 — the semantics of the motifs: reading the hidden messages — straight and wavy lines and hatching for water and abundance; the circle for the sun and endless renewal; the rhombus for the sun-cult and the sown earth; ram's horns for masculine strength; the wheat ear for agriculture, bread and peace; the spiral for energy and growth; the column for eternity; the rooster who wakes the sun; the tree of life for man's path and the generations' treasure — motifs shared, with shifting senses, across Romanian, Gagauz, Ukrainian, Bulgarian and Russian ornament.
