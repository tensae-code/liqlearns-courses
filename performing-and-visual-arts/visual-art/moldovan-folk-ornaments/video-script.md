This is Moldova's own lesson.

**Motive decorative în arta populară; Arta populară.** G4 Ch. 3/4, Motive decorative în arta populară; Arta populară — Moldova's folk ornament and folk art: the sun, the human figure, the tree of life, the flower vase, the horse and birds, circle, rhombus and square on national carpets, peasant towels, folk costume, carved gates, veranda posts and painted Easter eggs, and the master folk craftsman (meșter popular) who joins the useful to the beautiful.

**Arta populară.** Unit 4, Arta populară — Moldova's craft map: pottery centres at Cinișeuți, Iurceni and Țigănești, winter carpet weaving in homes and monastery workshops, the embroidered ie and the folk costume for work and for feasts.

**Folk creators enrich the ornaments of ceramics.** Unit II §2 — folk creators enrich the ornaments of ceramics, woven fabrics and the national costume with line and point together.

**The rosette called mandala in the East.** Unit III §15 — the rosette called mandala in the East: the circular decorative motif symbolising light, peace, spiritual purity and perfection, whose minute drawing is used in art therapy.

**The ornament.** Unit III §16 — the ornament: decorative motifs arranged by repetition, in bands or in networks; the traditional Moldovan motifs a folk master must keep — the tree of life, ram's horns, the rhombus, the spiral, the grape — in the natural colours of the land's plants and soils.

**The semantics of the motifs.** Unit III §16 — the semantics of the motifs: reading the hidden messages — straight and wavy lines and hatching for water and abundance; the circle for the sun and endless renewal; the rhombus for the sun-cult and the sown earth; ram's horns for masculine strength; the wheat ear for agriculture, bread and peace; the spiral for energy and growth; the column for eternity; the rooster who wakes the sun; the tree of life for man's path and the generations' treasure — motifs shared, with shifting senses, across Romanian, Gagauz, Ukrainian, Bulgarian and Russian ornament.

That is Moldovan Folk Ornaments — Moldova's way, kept by name.
