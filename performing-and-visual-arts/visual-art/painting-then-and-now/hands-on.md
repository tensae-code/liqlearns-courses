# Hands-on — Painting Then and Now

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## One subject, two ways
Paint one subject — a person, a house, a flower field — twice on two papers. First in the old way: flat, ordered, earth colours. Then in your way of today: any colours, any style. Lay them side by side and show a friend both.

## The slow fifty
Choose a painting or coloured picture where you live or learn. Stand before it for a slow count of fifty without speaking. Then list three things you noticed only after the first look.

## Hang it
Pick the painting you made that you like best. Make it a paper frame, hang it where people pass, and watch what they say when they stop to look.
