# Painting Then and Now — true or false

Say if each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The old style of Ethiopian painting is a mistake that should be forgotten. **Answer: False** — it carries the community's memory; today's style grew from it.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A companion looking at the same painting may notice a thing you missed. **Answer: True.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Harag works are among the main exhibits of indigenous artistic knowledge. **Answer: True.**

**4.** (Sub lesson 6) One practical stages the traditional reconciliation ceremony. **Answer: True.**
