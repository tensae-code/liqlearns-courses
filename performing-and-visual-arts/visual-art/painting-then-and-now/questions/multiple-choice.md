# Painting Then and Now — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Ethiopia's colour-painting style —
a) began only last year  b) is its own old style that has changed through time to the present  c) was copied last year from abroad
**Answer: b)** — the book compares the ancient painting with today's.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The modern painter the book names as today's example is —
a) Afework Tekle  b) the learner's teacher  c) no one is named
**Answer: a) Afework Tekle.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The old painting and today's painting are —
a) enemies — one must be thrown away  b) both Ethiopian painting; one grew into the other  c) the same picture
**Answer: b)** — a country's painting grows; it does not throw its past away.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** To appreciate a painting, first —
a) guess its price  b) stand before it and look slowly  c) copy it quickly
**Answer: b) stand before it and look slowly.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** One of the book's two appreciating works shows —
a) a portrait of a woman in a headscarf  b) a steam train  c) a football match
**Answer: a) a portrait of a woman in a headscarf** — the other shows a field of colourful flowers.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** When you appreciate a painting, you say —
a) only the one right answer  b) honestly what you see and feel — there is no single right answer  c) nothing at all
**Answer: b)** — appreciation is your own honest looking, said aloud.
