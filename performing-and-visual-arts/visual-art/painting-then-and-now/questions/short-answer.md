# Painting Then and Now — short answer

Answer in a word or a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the book's Figure 5 compare? **Answer: ancient painting and today's painting.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In one phrase, what happened to Ethiopia's painting style through time? **Answer: it changed (grew) through time to the present.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the second of the book's two appreciating works (not the portrait). **Answer: a field of colourful flowers.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Give one question you ask a painting when you appreciate it. **Answer: any of — What do I see first? Which colours carry it? How does it make me feel?**
