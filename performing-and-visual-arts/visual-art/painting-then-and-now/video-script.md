# Video script — Painting Then and Now

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Long ago, painters here painted like this.
Flat. Ordered. Gold and earth colours. Stories you can read.

And today, painters paint like this.
Bright. Free. Anything under the sun.

Both are **Ethiopian painting**. One grew into the other.
The book names a master of today's painting: **Afework Tekle**.

Now — stand here with me. Look at this painting. Slowly.
What do you see **first**?
Which **colours** do the work?
How does it make you **feel**?

That is appreciating. Looking until the painting speaks.
Now you — find a painting, and listen to it.
