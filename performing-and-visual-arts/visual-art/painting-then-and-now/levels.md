# Painting Then and Now — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number, as before. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. Two parts only: the history in one view, then the skill of appreciating.

## Sub lesson 1 — Ethiopian painting then and now
Pass mark: say that Ethiopia's own colour-painting style has changed through time to the present, and name Afework Tekle as the modern example the book names.

## Sub lesson 2 — Appreciating paintings
Pass mark: before one painting, say what you see first, which colours carry it, and how it makes you feel — and accept that a companion may see a thing you missed.

## Sub lesson 3 — Indigenous knowledge in visual art: the heritage roll (Grade 6)
Pass mark: recite the heritage roll (architecture, jewellery, pottery, cave paintings, Lalibela, Gondar, Jegol, Tiya) and say church painting has passed down since the 4th century.

## Sub lesson 4 — Indigenous knowledge on stage, film and photograph (Grade 6)
Pass mark: name the fields of indigenous knowledge (writing-craft to astronomy) and the three house kinds of Fig. 54 (stone, bamboo/reed, Konso).

## Sub lesson 5 — Showing appreciation: documentaries, exhibitions, stamps (Grade 6)
Pass mark: give the appreciation routes — staged stories, documentary films, a photo exhibition, photos on stamps/posters/flyers — and name Fig. 55's subjects.

## Sub lesson 6 — Heritage practicals (Grade 6)
Pass mark: carry out the four practicals — interview theatre circles, stage justice and reconciliation, discuss preservation, explain tourism-office photographs.

## Sub lesson 7 — Visual art IS our heritage (Grade 6)
Pass mark: recite the §5.1.1 heritage roll and name the visual ways heritage is protected — posters, stamps, drawing, sculpture, painting, jewellery.
