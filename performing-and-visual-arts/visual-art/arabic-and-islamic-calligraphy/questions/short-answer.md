# Arabic and Islamic Calligraphy — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is done to each piece first, before inking?
**Answer: It is traced in dotted outline.**

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Name the four caliphs composed together in the semicircle.
**Answer: Abu Bakr, Umar, Usman and Ali.**
