# Arabic and Islamic Calligraphy — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Calligraphy in this book is taught as…
a) writing freehand inside a geometric frame, then coloured  b) plain copying of letters  c) printing with a machine
**Answer: a) writing freehand inside a geometric frame, then coloured**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The name Allah is written inside a…
a) rectangle  b) semicircle  c) circle
**Answer: a) rectangle**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The name Muhammad is written inside a…
a) rectangle  b) semicircle  c) triangle
**Answer: b) semicircle**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** The four caliphs composed together are…
a) Abu Bakr, Umar, Usman and Ali  b) four painters of Pakistan  c) the four primary colours
**Answer: a) Abu Bakr, Umar, Usman and Ali**
