# Lesson plan — Arabic and Islamic Calligraphy

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Visual Art · **Lesson:** Arabic and Islamic Calligraphy
**Place in the curriculum:** Visual Art (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** calligraphy as design — writing freehand inside a geometric frame and colouring it: the name Allah in large letters inside a rectangle, the name Muhammad inside a semicircle, and the names of the four caliphs composed together inside a semicircle, each first traced in dotted outline, then inked and coloured.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this course.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Calligraphy as design** — writing freehand inside a geometric frame, then colouring the writing
2. **Allah in a rectangle; Muhammad in a semicircle** — the two names, each in its own frame
3. **The four caliphs in a semicircle** — Abu Bakr, Umar, Usman and Ali composed together; traced in dotted outline first, then inked and coloured

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier Visual Art lessons of this course. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say what calligraphy-as-design is: freehand writing inside a geometric frame, coloured.
2. Name the frame for each piece: Allah in a rectangle, Muhammad in a semicircle, the four caliphs together in a semicircle.
3. Say the working order: traced in dotted outline first, then inked and coloured.

## What you need
Paper, a pencil for the dotted outline, ink and colours, and a ruler for the frames.
