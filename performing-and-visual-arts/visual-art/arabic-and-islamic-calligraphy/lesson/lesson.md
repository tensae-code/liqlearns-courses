# Arabic and Islamic Calligraphy

## Do it first

Quick game: say what you already know about this lesson's subject — one thing, out loud. This lesson names it properly.

## Sub lesson 1 — Calligraphy as design

In the Class 7 book's Design chapter, **calligraphy is taught as design**: you **write freehand inside a geometric frame** and then **colour the writing**, the way you colour any other design in the chapter.

## Sub lesson 2 — Allah in a rectangle; Muhammad in a semicircle

**Problem 7:** the name **Allah** in large letters inside a **rectangle**.
**Problem 8:** the name **Muhammad** inside a **semicircle**.

## Sub lesson 3 — The four caliphs in a semicircle

**Problem 9:** the names of the **four caliphs — Abu Bakr, Umar, Usman and Ali** — composed together inside a **semicircle**.

Every piece is worked the same way: **first traced in dotted outline, then inked and coloured**.
