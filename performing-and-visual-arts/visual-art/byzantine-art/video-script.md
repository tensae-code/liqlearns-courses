This lesson is Byzantine Art.

Byzantine art as the spiritual heir of Roman and Greek art across a thousand years — church architecture with Corinthian capitals and marble revetment, sculpture of religious symbolism in relief and ivory, mosaic as its signature technique with the Madaba Mosaic Map, the oldest known map of the Holy Land, on the floor of St George's church in Madaba, and applied arts in gold, silver, glass and ivory.

Watch, listen — then say it back in your own words.
