## Sub lesson 1 — Weaving from natural materials

The Classes 3–4 weaving work sets **weaving various items from natural materials** — the island's own fibres and materials, woven into real items.

## Sub lesson 2 — Complex and mixed-media weaving

The work grows: **complex weaving and mixed-media weaving**, made **for decorative and useful objects** — weaving that is both beautiful and used.
