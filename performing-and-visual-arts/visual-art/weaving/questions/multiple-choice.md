# Weaving — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Classes 3–4 weaving uses, first of all…
a) natural materials  b) plastic only  c) paint
**Answer: a) natural materials** — weaving various items from natural materials.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Mixed-media weaving means…
a) weaving with mixed materials/media  b) weaving while watching media  c) one thread only
**Answer: a) weaving with mixed materials/media**.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The complex weaving is made for objects that are…
a) decorative and useful  b) invisible  c) thrown away at once
**Answer: a) decorative and useful**.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** "Various items" tells us pupils weave…
a) one fixed object only  b) different kinds of items  c) nothing finished
**Answer: b) different kinds of items**.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Complex weaving comes…
a) before any simple weaving  b) as the work grows in Classes 3–4  c) never
**Answer: b) as the work grows in Classes 3–4**.
