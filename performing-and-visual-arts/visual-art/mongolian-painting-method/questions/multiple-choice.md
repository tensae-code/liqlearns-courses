# Mongolian Painting Method — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Монгол зургийн аргаар зурах means…
a) drawing by the Mongolian painting method — the country's own traditional way  b) drawing by numbers  c) tracing a photograph
**Answer: a) drawing by the Mongolian painting method — the country's own traditional way**

**2.** The method is taught in…
a) Grade 4 as its own lesson  b) only at university  c) never in school
**Answer: a) Grade 4 as its own lesson**
