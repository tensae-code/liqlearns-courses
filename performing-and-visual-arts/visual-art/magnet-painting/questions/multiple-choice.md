# Magnet Painting — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In magnet painting, the paint is moved by…
a) a brush  b) a metal object moved by a magnet under the plate  c) blowing
**Answer: b) a metal object moved by a magnet under the plate**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Paint is added…
a) all at once  b) a little at a time, in many colours  c) never
**Answer: b) a little at a time, in many colours**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** In the box variant, the paper is cut to fit…
a) the lid  b) the bottom of a box or split cereal packet  c) a round plate only
**Answer: b) the bottom of a box or split cereal packet**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The raised edges of the box…
a) hide the picture  b) help guide the magnet painting  c) dry the paint
**Answer: b) help guide the magnet painting**
