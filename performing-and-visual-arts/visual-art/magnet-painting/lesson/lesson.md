# Magnet Painting

## Do it first

Hold a magnet under the table and slide a paperclip across the top — it follows! Now put that clip in paint on a plate. The magnet is about to become your paintbrush.

## Sub lesson 1 — Magnet painting — the object paints

Put **paint or strong watercolour** and a **metal object** on a **paper plate**. Lift the plate and move the object with a **magnet under the plate** — **the object itself paints**. Add paint **a little at a time** and use **many colours**. A science tool becomes the paintbrush: the magnet paints, moving the metal through the paint from under the plate; the sheet's four step photographs carry the teaching.

## Sub lesson 2 — Other ways — the box variant

For the **box variant**, **cut paper to fit the bottom of a box or split cereal packet** — the **raised edges help guide** the magnet painting.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson taught, part by part. Companion checks each part against the sub lessons above.

## Do It Yourself

1. Make your own piece using this lesson's way, from start to finish.
2. Show it to the class and say what you made, how, and what you learnt.
3. Write one sentence: what will you do differently next time?
