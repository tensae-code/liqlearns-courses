The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

**Cokwe art and Angolan sculpture.** Angola's own heritage sculpture, pictured and named: the Cokwe "O Pensador" (The Thinker) and Mwana Pwo figure, Benguela dolls, the Palanca Negra carved in wood, a hand-made batuque drum, Mayembe's sculpture and Etona's wood sculptures — set beside Neves e Sousa's painting of the same subject.

**Angolan traditional basketry.** Angolan traditional basketry (cestaria) — one of humanity's oldest arts, still practised across Angola's regions; artisanal production handed down generation to generation, every object bearing its maker's mark and never exactly alike. Basketry is kin to pottery (clay bowls were once formed over esparto baskets). The making steps: gather junco, palm branches, corn cane or mateba palm, dry four to five days, scrape the material into thread, then build the object's base. Its products run from a simple hat to furniture.

That is Angolan Crafts and Sculpture — Angola's own books teach it this way.
