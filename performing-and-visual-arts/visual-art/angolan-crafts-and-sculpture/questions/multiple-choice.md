# Angolan Crafts and Sculpture — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

a) Cokwe art and Angolan sculpture  b) an examination  c) nothing at all
**Answer: a) Cokwe art and Angolan sculpture.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The Angola book teaches this lesson as…
a) Angola's own heritage sculpture and traditional basketry  b) a sports rule  c) a cooking recipe
**Answer: a) Angola's own heritage sculpture and traditional basketry.**

**3.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) Angolan traditional basketry  b) long division  c) map coordinates
**Answer: a) Angolan traditional basketry.**

**4.** The parts of this lesson come from…
a) Angola's own Educação Manual e Plástica / Educação Musical books  b) a newspaper  c) a song chorus
**Answer: a) Angola's own books.**

**5.** "Cokwe art and Angolan sculpture" is taught in this lesson as…
