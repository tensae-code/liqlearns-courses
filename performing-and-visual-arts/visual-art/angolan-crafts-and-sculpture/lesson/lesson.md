## Sub lesson 1 — Cokwe art and Angolan sculpture

Angola's own heritage sculpture, pictured and named: the Cokwe "O Pensador" (The Thinker) and Mwana Pwo figure, Benguela dolls, the Palanca Negra carved in wood, a hand-made batuque drum, Mayembe's sculpture and Etona's wood sculptures — set beside Neves e Sousa's painting of the same subject.

## Sub lesson 2 — Angolan traditional basketry

Angolan traditional basketry (cestaria) — one of humanity's oldest arts, still practised across Angola's regions; artisanal production handed down generation to generation, every object bearing its maker's mark and never exactly alike. Basketry is kin to pottery (clay bowls were once formed over esparto baskets). The making steps: gather junco, palm branches, corn cane or mateba palm, dry four to five days, scrape the material into thread, then build the object's base. Its products run from a simple hat to furniture.
