# Hands-on — Colours Around Us


The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Colour sources hunt
With an adult, find the book's colour sources in real life: berbere and red soil (red), turmeric — እርድ — and a yellow flower (yellow), ink and a blue flower (blue). Line them up and name each colour's makers.

## Sunrise watch
Watch a sunrise or sunset sky and name its colours in order as they change. Red is mostly seen there — the book's words, watched with your own eyes.
