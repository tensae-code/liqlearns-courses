# Lesson plan — Colours Around Us

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Visual Art · **Lesson:** Colours Around Us
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 2 — Visual Art (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the colours of the world around the child — yellow and red in flowers, bananas and roses; what red and yellow mean; blue of sky and water; and naming the colours in real pictures, flag and sunrise included.
**This lesson's own level:** it is the course's first colour lesson by depth: Grade 1 meets the colours in photographs of the surroundings, and Grade 3 returns to red, yellow and blue one by one — where each lives, what it means, what it can be made from. The teacher guides' colours-around-us teaching is carried as evidence.

## Sub lessons
1. **Yellow and red around us** — the Grade 1 photographs — a yellow adey flower and bananas (yellow), and red roses (red).
2. **Red: where it lives and what it means** — Grade 3's red — seen mostly at sunrise and sunset; a sign of martyrs and sacrifice, often on flags; prepared from berbere, the tsigereda flower and red soil (Activities: draw a picture using red).
3. **Yellow: sun, hope and happiness** — Grade 3's yellow — connected with the sun, heat and happiness; seeing it improves brightness, hope and memory; prepared from turmeric (እርድ) and yellow flowers.
4. **Blue: sky, water, calm** — Grade 3's blue — it resembles the sky and watery bodies; painters use it to create cold and peaceful places; prepared from blue flowers, carbon and ink.
5. **Blue in Grade 1, and naming colours in pictures** — the Grade 1 sky-blue (ሰማያዊ) page, and Exercise 10 — tell the class the colours of pictured scenes: the Ethiopian flag, a sunrise over a river, a crowd in colourful traditional wraps.
6. **Colours around us in the teacher guides (teacher guide — evidence)** — the Grade 1 teacher guide's colours-around-us lesson (§3.4) and the Grade 2 teacher guide's colour-as-culture teaching (§3.4, cited at section level — that guide is a scan with no text layer) — cited as teacher-guide evidence.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: nothing in this unit — this is a first door into colour. The Primary Colours lesson sorts these same colours later. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name yellow things (adey flower, bananas) and red things (roses) in the surroundings.
2. Say where red is mostly seen (sunrise and sunset), what it is a sign of (martyrs and sacrifice, on flags), and what red can be prepared from (berbere, the tsigereda flower, red soil).
3. Say what yellow is connected with (the sun, heat, happiness; brightness, hope and memory) and what it can be prepared from (turmeric — እርድ — and yellow flowers).
4. Say what blue resembles (sky and watery bodies), what painters use it for (cold and peaceful places), and what it can be prepared from (blue flowers, carbon, ink).
5. Name the colours in real pictures: the Ethiopian flag (green-yellow-red), a sunrise over a river, people in colourful traditional wraps.

## What you need
Eyes and the world outside. Colours for drawing the sky and water in blue. A real banana, flower or flag nearby helps.

## If the learner is stuck
- Names only the biggest colour in a scene → ask "and what else?" three times; small colours count, and judges name them all.
- Thinks colours come only from shops → show the sources the book names: berbere and red soil for red, turmeric for yellow, ink and blue flowers for blue.
