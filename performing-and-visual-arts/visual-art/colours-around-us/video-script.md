# Video script — Colours Around Us

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Colours live around us.

Yellow — adey flower! Bananas!
Yellow is sun, hope, happiness.
It is made from **እርድ** — turmeric!

Red — sunrise! Sunset! Flags!
Red is a sign of sacrifice.
Made from berbere, from red soil!

Blue — **ሰማያዊ**!
Sky above. Water below.
Cold and peaceful.

Name the colours. You are the judge!
