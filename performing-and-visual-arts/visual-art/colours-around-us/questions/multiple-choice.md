# Colours Around Us — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The book's yellow photographs show —
a) an adey flower and bananas  b) the sky and sea  c) yellow clothes only
**Answer: a) a yellow adey flower and bananas.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The book's red photographs show —
a) red soil  b) red roses  c) a flag
**Answer: b) red roses.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Red is mostly seen at —
a) midday  b) sunrise and sunset  c) night
**Answer: b) sunrise and sunset.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Red is a sign of —
a) sleep  b) martyrs and sacrifice  c) rain
**Answer: b) martyrs and sacrifice — and it is often seen on flags.**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Yellow is connected with —
a) the sun, heat and happiness  b) night and cold  c) sadness
**Answer: a) the sun, heat and happiness — seeing it improves brightness, hope and memory.**

**6. (Sub lesson 4)** Blue resembles —
a) fire  b) the sky and watery bodies  c) soil
**Answer: b) the sky and watery bodies.**

**7. (Sub lesson 4)** Painters use blue to create —
a) hot and noisy places  b) cold and peaceful places  c) dark caves
**Answer: b) cold and peaceful places.**

**8. (Sub lesson 5)** The Ethiopian flag's colours, in order from the top, are —
a) red, yellow, green  b) green, yellow, red  c) blue, green, red
**Answer: b) green, yellow, red.**

**9. (Sub lesson 6)** The Grade 1 teacher guide's lesson for this work is called —
a) the colours around us  b) mixing colours  c) the flag lesson
**Answer: a) the colours around us — noticing and naming colours in the child's environment.**

**10. (Sub lesson 3)** Seeing yellow mostly improves —
a) brightness, hope and memory  b) sleep  c) hearing
**Answer: a) brightness, hope and memory.**

**11. (Sub lesson 5)** Exercise 10 asks you to —
a) tell the class what colours the pictures have  b) draw the flag from memory only  c) close the book
**Answer: a) tell the class what colours the pictured scenes have — flag, sunrise over a river, crowd in traditional wraps.**

**12. (Sub lesson 6)** In the Grade 2 guide, the colour of the flag is taught as colour's —
a) cultural face  b) price  c) weight
**Answer: a) cultural face — colour as culture, including what colours mean.**
