This lesson is Chinese Woodblock New Year Pictures.

China's woodblock New Year pictures — the folk prints, such as Yangjiabu's, made to celebrate the new year. — as China's school books teach it: Appreciating a Yangjiabu woodblock New Year picture of a door god — one of China's three great New Year picture centres (Tianjin Yangliuqing, Suzhou Taohuawu, Weifang Yangjiabu): woodblock outlines with hand-coloured fills in red, green, yellow and purple — bold northern strength with southern softness.

Watch, listen — then say it back in your own words.
