# Hands-on — Emblems and Posters That Speak

The lesson with your hands, your feet and your voice together, not only your eyes.

## Shrink test
Draw your emblem big. Then draw it as small as a coin. If it still speaks, it passes the book's rule: clear shrunk or enlarged.

## The school wall
With the teacher, paste the class's best posters on the school's notice boards, as the book directs — posters finish their work out in the world, not in a drawer.
