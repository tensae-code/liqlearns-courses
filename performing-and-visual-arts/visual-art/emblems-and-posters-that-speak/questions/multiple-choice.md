# Emblems and Posters That Speak — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** An emblem identifies its owner…
  a) Immediately b) Only after a long explanation c) Never
**Answer: a) Immediately** — an emblem is identified immediately — that is its job.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The Apple mark, as the book classes it, is…
  a) Picture-only b) Text-only c) Not an emblem
**Answer: a) Picture-only** — the book's picture-only plate shows Apple, Nike and Pepsi.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** When are a poster's letters written?
  a) Last, after the paint dries b) First, before the picture c) Never — posters have no letters
**Answer: a) Last, after the paint dries** — the steps end with writing the letters after the paint dries.

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Reading a sign with an artist's eye means looking at…
  a) Its mark, picture and few words — and whether they work b) Only its price c) Only who printed it
**Answer: a) Its mark, picture and few words — and whether they work** — mark, picture and words, judged by the design rules.

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** A tea-leaf package's picture does two jobs at once — it…
  a) Packages the tea and advertises it b) Cooks and serves it c) Hides and forgets it
**Answer: a) Packages the tea and advertises it** — the Grade 6 book's own example of a visual work doing two jobs.
