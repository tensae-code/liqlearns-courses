# Emblems and Posters That Speak — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Another body may copy a protected emblem and use it directly.
**Answer: False**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** An emblem's message must stay clear whether it is shrunk or enlarged.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A good poster uses very many words in small letters.
**Answer: False**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Direction signs are a kind of emblem at work in the locality.
**Answer: True**
