# Emblems and Posters That Speak — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is an emblem made of?
**Answer: A picture, letters, or both together.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is the first design step?
**Answer: Understand the message and value the emblem must express.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name the three kinds of poster.
**Answer: Commercial advertising, health, and campaign posters.**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** What is a posted notice called in the guide's Amharic?
**Answer: የተለጠፉ ማስታወቂያ — posted notices/advertisements.**
