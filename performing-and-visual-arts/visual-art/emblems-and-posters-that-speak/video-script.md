# Video script — Emblems and Posters That Speak

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

An emblem is a face a thing wears. You know it in one second.
Letters alone. A picture alone. Or both together.
Few colours. Few words. Big letters.
A poster must speak from far away.
