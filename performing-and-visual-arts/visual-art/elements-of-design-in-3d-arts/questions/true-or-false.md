# Elements of Design in 3D Arts — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Ergonomics is the fit of a made thing to the human body that uses it.
**Answer: True** — the handle shaped to the hand — the body is one of the measures.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Time and motion belong to 3D design because the viewer meets the work by moving around it.
**Answer: True** — the maker composes for a viewer who will not stand still.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** On a 3D work, a line can activate the surrounding space.
**Answer: True** — the guide's own word for line's work in space.
