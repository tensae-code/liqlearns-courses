# Elements of Design in 3D Arts — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A three-dimensional work adds, beyond length and width —
a) colour  b) depth  c) a title
**Answer: b) depth** — it stands in real space and holds space inside itself.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A pot's space is —
a) only the room it stands in  b) the space it takes in the room AND the space it holds inside itself  c) nothing — pots have no space
**Answer: b) the space it takes in the room AND the space it holds inside itself** — mass & space: the clay, and the emptiness it carries.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In the guide's account, line is —
a) made of colour patches  b) made of points — the first element  c) only found in drawing
**Answer: b) made of points — the first element** — line, made of points, dynamic, expressing gesture/direction.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Shape, in the guide's words, is —
a) a 2D enclosed area, geometric or organic  b) any solid object  c) the shadow of a form
**Answer: a) a 2D enclosed area, geometric or organic** — the flat face the solid shows from one side.
