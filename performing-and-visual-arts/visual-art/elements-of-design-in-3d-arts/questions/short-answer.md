# Elements of Design in 3D Arts — short answer

Answer in a sentence or a short list.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What do form and function mean together in 3D art?
**Answer:** The form (shape) of a made thing answers to its function (its job — holding, bearing, working).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Point at a vessel and name its mass and its two spaces.
**Answer:** Mass: the clay itself. Spaces: the room-space it occupies, and the inside space it holds.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What two kinds can a shape be, in the guide's account?
**Answer:** Geometric or organic.
