# Hands-on — Elements of Design in 3D Arts

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Turn the vessel
Take a clay vessel. Turn it slowly through a full turn, looking: where is its mass? What space does it hold inside? What space does it take in the room? Say each answer aloud as you point.

## Form follows the hand
Find one made thing whose shape fits its job and your hand — a handle, a grip, a spout. Draw it and label: form here, function here, the hand's comfort here.
