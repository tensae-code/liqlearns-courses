# Elements of Design in 3D Arts — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number, as before. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — What 3D means — form and function, ergonomics (teacher guide — evidence)
Pass mark: say, as teacher-guide evidence, what dimension/3D means (the work has depth and stands in real space), and explain form and function and ergonomics as the guide names them among the 3D topics.

## Sub lesson 2 — Mass and space, time and motion (teacher guide — evidence)
Pass mark: say, as teacher-guide evidence, that the guide names mass & space and time and motion among the 3D design topics, and explain each on a real vessel: its mass, the space inside and around it, and the time-and-motion of meeting it from all sides.

## Sub lesson 3 — Line, shape, and the rest — in three dimensions (teacher guide — evidence)
Pass mark: say, as teacher-guide evidence, how the guide presents line (the first element, made of points; dynamic, expressing gesture/direction, activating surrounding space) and shape (2D enclosed area, geometric or organic), and that the remaining elements are likewise applied to three-dimensional work.
