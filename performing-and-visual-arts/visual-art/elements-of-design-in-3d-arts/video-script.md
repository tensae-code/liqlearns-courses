*Evidence lesson — the teacher guide's 3D section, in simple voice.*

A drawing is flat. A pot is **not**.

Three dimensions: height, width — and **depth**.

Form has a **function**. A vessel is made to hold, and to be held — that is **ergonomics**, the fit to the hand.

**Mass** and **space** — the clay, and the emptiness it carries.

Turn it in your hands. Design you can walk around — that is 3D art.
