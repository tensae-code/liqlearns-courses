This lesson is Chinese Folk Clay and Dough Figures.

China's folk modelling — clay sculpture and dough figures made by folk artists. — as China's school books teach it: China's folk clay map — Wuxi Huishan's Big A-Fu (the god-child who wards off evil and brings blessing), Xunxian's whistling nigugu toys (two tail holes blow "gu-gu"), Fengxiang's painted clay (toys, hanging masks, standing figures), Chaozhou's Dawu clay figures — each rooted in its local customs.

Watch, listen — then say it back in your own words.
