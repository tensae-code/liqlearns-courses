## Sub lesson 1 — China HS Sculpture

China's folk clay map — Wuxi Huishan's Big A-Fu (the god-child who wards off evil and brings blessing), Xunxian's whistling nigugu toys (two tail holes blow "gu-gu"), Fengxiang's painted clay (toys, hanging masks, standing figures), Chaozhou's Dawu clay figures — each rooted in its local customs
