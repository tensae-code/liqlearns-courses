# Hands-on — Pakistani Textiles: Ajrak and Ralli

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Order check
Write the four arts in the chapter's order with their section numbers: carpet 8.3, ajrak 8.5, embroidery 8.6, ralli 8.7.

## Cloth hunt
Look at patterned cloth near you — a quilt, a printed scarf, an embroidered piece. Say which of the four arts it is nearest to, and why.
