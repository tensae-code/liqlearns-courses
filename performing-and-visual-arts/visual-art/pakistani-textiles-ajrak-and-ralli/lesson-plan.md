# Lesson plan — Pakistani Textiles: Ajrak and Ralli

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Visual Art · **Lesson:** Pakistani Textiles: Ajrak and Ralli
**Place in the curriculum:** Visual Art (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Pakistan's own textile arts, taught as a full part of its Class 9 textiles chapter: carpet, ajrak, embroidery and ralli work — the cloth arts the country makes its own.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this course.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Carpet and ajrak** — the chapter's carpet section (8.3) and ajrak (8.5) — ajrak made with its blocks
2. **Embroidery** — the chapter's embroidery section (8.6)
3. **Ralli work** — ralli work (8.7) — Pakistan's quilts, named as the chapter's closing textile art

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier Visual Art lessons of this course. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name the four textile arts of this lesson in the chapter's order.
2. Tell where they stand: in the Class 9 textiles chapter, beside weaving, hand loom and printing.
3. Say what ajrak and ralli are, in the book's own framing: ajrak made with blocks; ralli the country's quilt work.

## What you need
Your eyes — and any patterned cloth near you to compare.
