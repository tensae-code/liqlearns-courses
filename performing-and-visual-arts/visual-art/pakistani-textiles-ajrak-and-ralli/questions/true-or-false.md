# Pakistani Textiles: Ajrak and Ralli — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Ajrak and ralli are taught as Pakistan's own textile arts.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Embroidery is not part of the textiles chapter.
**Answer: False** — embroidery is section 8.6 of the chapter, and the chapter itself is titled Textiles: Weaving, Printing and Embroidery.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Ralli work opens the chapter's own-textiles sections.
**Answer: False** — it closes them, at section 8.7; carpet opens them at 8.3.
