# Pakistani Textiles: Ajrak and Ralli

## Do it first

Quick game: say what you already know about this lesson's subject — one thing, out loud. This lesson names it properly.

## Sub lesson 1 — Carpet and ajrak

The Class 9 book's textiles chapter — **Textiles: Weaving, Printing and Embroidery** — gives Pakistan's own cloth arts their own sections:

- **8.3 Carpet** — the carpet as a Pakistani textile art.
- **8.5 Ajrak** — ajrak, made with its **blocks** (the book's own drawing chapter names ajrak blocks among the things of Pakistan's culture).

## Sub lesson 2 — Embroidery

- **8.6 Embroidery** — embroidery as one of Pakistan's own textile arts, in the same chapter that teaches embroidery as a textile process.

## Sub lesson 3 — Ralli work

- **8.7 Ralli Work** — **ralli**, Pakistan's **quilt work**, closes the chapter's own-textiles sections.

Carpet, ajrak, embroidery and ralli: four cloth arts the book teaches as **Pakistan's own**.
