## Sub lesson 1 — Movements that move and shrink

Andorra's latest-trends block brings the newest art close to young people's reality. **Op and kinetic art** — art that seems to move, or moves — **Vasarely, Riley, Sempere, Calder, Ferrant**. Then **minimal art**, **Supports/Surfaces**, **arte povera** (poor art, made from humble materials), **hyperrealism** (painting sharper than a photograph) and the **Cobra movement**.

## Sub lesson 2 — Actions, ideas and returns to painting

The block continues where art leaves the frame: **happenings and Fluxus** — art as an event; **environments** the viewer enters; **performance and body art** — **Beuys**; **conceptual art** — **Kosuth** — where the idea is the work; **land art** made in the landscape; and painting's return in **neo-expressionism** — **Baselitz, Barceló** — and the Italian **transavanguardia**.
