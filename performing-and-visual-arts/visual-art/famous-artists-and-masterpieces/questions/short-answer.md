# Famous Artists and Masterpieces — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name one artist of op or kinetic art from the programme.
**Answer: Any one of Vasarely, Riley, Sempere, Calder and Ferrant.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which artist is named for conceptual art?
**Answer: Kosuth.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Name one neo-expressionist from the programme.
**Answer: Baselitz or Barceló.**
