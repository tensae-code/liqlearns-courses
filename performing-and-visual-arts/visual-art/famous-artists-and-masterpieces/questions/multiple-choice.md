# Famous Artists and Masterpieces — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Op and kinetic art are about…
**Answer: a) movement — seen or real  b) gold frames  c) portraits only**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Arte povera means art made from…
**Answer: a) humble, poor materials  b) jewels  c) marble only**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Calder is named with…
**Answer: a) kinetic art  b) hyperrealism  c) Cobra**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Conceptual art makes… the work.
**Answer: a) the idea  b) the frame  c) the price**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Beuys is named for…
**Answer: a) performance and body art  b) minimal art  c) op art**
