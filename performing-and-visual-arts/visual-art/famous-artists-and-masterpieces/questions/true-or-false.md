# Famous Artists and Masterpieces — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Hyperrealism paints sharper than a photograph.
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Minimal art fills every space with detail.
**Answer: False — it reduces art to the minimum.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Land art is made in the landscape.
**Answer: True.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Happenings are paintings hung in a row.
**Answer: False — happenings are art as an event.**
