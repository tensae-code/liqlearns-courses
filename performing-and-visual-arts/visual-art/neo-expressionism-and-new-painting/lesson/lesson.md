# Neo-Expressionism and New Painting

## Do it first

Name one thing that belongs in this lesson — Neo-expressionism and the new painting — the return of bold, energetic figurative painting with neo-abstraction beside it. Say what it is in your own words. If you can name one, you are already inside the lesson.

## Sub lesson 1 — return of expression

return of expression: neo-abstraction (Sean Scully), neo-expressionism's bold colour and energy (Basquiat, Kiefer, Baselitz), the new European painting joining abstraction and figuration (Richter, Tuymans, Rauch); the local schools — CoBrA, the School of London (Bacon, Freud), the Young British Artists (Hirst's shark in formaldehyde, Emin's "My Bed"); and artivism — art as a tool for social change (Ai Weiwei, Guerrilla Girls).

## Sub lesson 2 — Неоекспресионизам

Неоекспресионизам — Neo-Expressionism: strong feeling in rough, aggressive strokes — Baselitz's upside-down figures, Kiefer's landscapes laid with real straw.
