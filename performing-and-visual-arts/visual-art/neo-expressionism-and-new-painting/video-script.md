# Video script — Neo-Expressionism and New Painting

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This is North Macedonia's own lesson.

**return of expression.** return of expression: neo-abstraction (Sean Scully), neo-expressionism's bold colour and energy (Basquiat, Kiefer, Baselitz), the new European painting joining abstraction and figuration (Richter, Tuymans, Rauch); the local schools — CoBrA, the School of London (Bacon, Freud), the Young British Artists (Hirst's shark in formaldehyde, Emin's "My Bed"); and artivism — art as a tool for social change (Ai Weiwei, Guerrilla Girls).

**Неоекспресионизам.** Неоекспресионизам — Neo-Expressionism: strong feeling in rough, aggressive strokes — Baselitz's upside-down figures, Kiefer's landscapes laid with real straw.

That is Neo-Expressionism and New Painting — North Macedonia's way, kept by name.
