# Video script — Street Art and Graffiti

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

A wall in the city. Letters grow on it — big, coloured, composed.

That is **graffiti**: painting for the street — **urban painting**.

It does not hide in a gallery. It stands outside, where everyone walks past.

Lettering. Wall work. Art for the whole street.
