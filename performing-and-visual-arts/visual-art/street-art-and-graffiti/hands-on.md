# Hands-on — Street Art and Graffiti

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Design the lettering
On paper, draw your own name or one word as graffiti lettering: big letters, composed to fill the page the way wall lettering fills a wall. Colour it.

## Read a wall
Look at a public wall picture or lettering near you (or recall one). Say: what is lettered or pictured, and who is it for? Answer in one sentence: everyone who passes.
