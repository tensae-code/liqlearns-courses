# Street Art and Graffiti — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is graffiti?
**Answer: Urban painting — lettering and wall work.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why is graffiti called a public art form?
**Answer: Because it stands on walls in public space, where everyone passing can see it.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** What two things make up graffiti's work?
**Answer: Lettering and wall work.**
