# Street Art and Graffiti — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Graffiti, as the Albania guide teaches it, is…
a) urban painting  b) a kind of sculpture  c) a photograph
**Answer: a) urban painting.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Graffiti's two named works are…
a) lettering and wall work  b) clay and casting  c) printing and collage
**Answer: a) lettering and wall work.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Graffiti is a public art form because…
a) it is hidden indoors  b) it stands on walls in public space, seen by everyone passing  c) only one person may see it
**Answer: b) it stands on walls in public space, seen by everyone passing.**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Graffiti lettering is…
a) composed — sized, shaped and coloured for its wall  b) always accidental  c) never coloured
**Answer: a) composed — sized, shaped and coloured for its wall.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The audience of a street work is…
a) only its maker  b) everyone who passes  c) no one
**Answer: b) everyone who passes.**
