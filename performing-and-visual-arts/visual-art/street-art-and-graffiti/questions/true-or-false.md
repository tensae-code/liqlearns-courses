# Street Art and Graffiti — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Graffiti is urban painting.
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Graffiti lettering is never composed or coloured.
**Answer: False** — it is composed and coloured for its wall.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A graffiti wall work is public art, seen by everyone who passes.
**Answer: True.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Graffiti is made to be hidden from the street.
**Answer: False** — its place is the public wall.
