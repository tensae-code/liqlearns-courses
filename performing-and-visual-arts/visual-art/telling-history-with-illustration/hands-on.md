# Hands-on — Telling History with Illustration

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Divide a history into pictures
Choose a short history — a patriot's story the teacher or an elder tells briefly. Divide it into its main parts, one part per picture. Draw the picture-narrative design for each part in pencil.

## Paint the narrative
Beautify your design with watercolour or pen, giving light and shade. Then write the history beside the picture narrative in your most beautiful handwriting.

## Read an old photograph
Bring an old photograph — from home, a book, or a newspaper. Tell a companion what you understand from it: what its time was like, and how life in the photograph differs from our present life.
