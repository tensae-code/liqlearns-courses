# Video script — Telling History with Illustration


The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.


---


A country must remember. **Pictures help it remember.**

A painting, a sculpture, a monument — each one carries history to the generations.

An **illustration** takes a written history and draws it: choose the part, divide it, draw it, give it light and shade, and write the history beside the pictures.

A **photograph** is an era's witness — it binds a thing to its moment.

And on the **stage**, history walks and speaks — and the audience never forgets.

Your turn: take one history. Give it pictures.
