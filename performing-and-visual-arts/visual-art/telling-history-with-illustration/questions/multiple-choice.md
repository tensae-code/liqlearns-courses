# Telling History with Illustration — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The visual arts help a country to —
a) forget old events  b) remember and teach its history and pass it to generations  c) replace all its books
**Answer: b) remember and teach its history and pass it to generations** — remembering, teaching, passing to generations.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Illustration is —
a) photographing a written page  b) expressing in drawing a history or tale presented in writing  c) writing a history in beautiful letters only
**Answer: b) expressing in drawing a history or tale presented in writing** — a descriptive drawing made from a written work, to narrate and express it.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The last step of illustrating is —
a) divide the history into main parts  b) draw the design  c) write the history beside the picture narrative in beautiful handwriting
**Answer: c) write the history beside the picture narrative in beautiful handwriting** — words and pictures stand together at the end.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Photography began —
a) in the time of the ancient kings  b) at the end of the 19th century  c) in our own century
**Answer: b) at the end of the 19th century** — the end of the 19th century.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** A photograph is called —
a) an era's witness, memorial and sign  b) a drawing's copy  c) a painter's sketch
**Answer: a) an era's witness, memorial and sign** — it binds a thing to its moment and condition.

**6. (Sub lesson 4)** Theatre tells history by —
a) printing old photographs  b) presenting the history, its setting and its characters on stage  c) building monuments
**Answer: b) presenting the history, its setting and its characters on stage** — on stage, unforgettably, before the audience.

**7. (Sub lesson 4)** Which of these is a history play the book names?
a) "ጣይቱ" (Taytu)  b) a play about a football match  c) no plays are named
**Answer: a) "ጣይቱ" (Taytu)** — Hindeke, The Vision of Tewodros, Taytu, Qaqe Wirdiwet.
