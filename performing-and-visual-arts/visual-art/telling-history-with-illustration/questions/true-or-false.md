# Telling History with Illustration — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Painting, illustration and sculpture are among the methods that carry history.
**Answer: True** — the book names painting, illustration, sculpture and other methods.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** An illustration's design is beautified with watercolour or pen, giving light and shade.
**Answer: True** — light and shade are part of the beautifying step.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The Menelik-era photographs show beginnings such as the telephone, the car and the railway.
**Answer: True** — telephone, car, railway, school, architecture and medicine.

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** The named history plays were presented at the National Theatre and the Hager Fikir Theatre, among other venues.
**Answer: True** — named venues in the book's account.
