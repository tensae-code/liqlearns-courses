# Telling History with Illustration — short answer

Answer in a sentence or a short list.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name two visual-art methods that tell history.
**Answer:** Any two of: painting, illustration, sculpture (and other visual methods).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** List the illustration steps in order.
**Answer:** Identify the history part; divide the history into main parts; draw the picture-narrative design; beautify with watercolour or pen, giving light and shade; write the history beside the picture narrative in beautiful handwriting.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does a photograph bind a thing to?
**Answer:** Its moment and its condition — every photograph records its time.

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Name two of the history plays the book names.
**Answer:** Any two of: ህንደኬ (Hindeke), የቴዎድሮስ ራዕይ (The Vision of Tewodros), ጣይቱ (Taytu), የቃቄ ውርደወት (Qaqe Wirdiwet).
