# Telling History with Illustration — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number, as before. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — History in visual art
Pass mark: say that the visual arts — painting, illustration, sculpture and other methods — have a big role in remembering and teaching the country's history and passing it to generations.

## Sub lesson 2 — Illustration — drawing a written history
Pass mark: define illustration (expressing in drawing a history, composition, report or tale presented in writing) and work its steps: identify the part, divide into main parts, draw the design, beautify with watercolour or pen with light and shade, write the history beside the picture narrative in beautiful handwriting.

## Sub lesson 3 — History in photographs — an era's witness
Pass mark: say that photography began at the end of the 19th century, that it binds a thing to its moment and condition, and that a photograph is an era's witness, memorial and sign; read an old photograph for the difference between its time and our present life.

## Sub lesson 4 — History on the stage — theatre tells it
Pass mark: say how theatre tells history (presenting the country's history, its setting and its characters on stage, narrating unforgettably) and name the history plays and venues the book names.
