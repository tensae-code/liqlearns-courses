# Hands-on — Clay Toys of Belarus

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Model a clay toy
Roll a clay (or plasticine) log. Cut and join pieces to build a small animal — a goat, a horse, a bird — the way the Grade 1 sequences build animals from a log. Look at the shape of a real folk toy first: simple, strong, made from few pieces.

## Three places
On a map or a list, mark Nemanitsa, Zembin and Logoisk. Beside each, write its master's name. Your toy joins their line: sign it with your place's name.
