# Clay Toys of Belarus — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the three places whose clay toys the book shows.
**Answer: Nemanitsa, Zembin and Logoisk.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Who made the "Goat and Billy-Goat" clay toy, and where?
**Answer: Strelchanka, of Logoisk (2010).**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Why does the book show children the masters' clay toys?
**Answer: So the child has real Belarusian masters to model after while learning to model from clay.**
