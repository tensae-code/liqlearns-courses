# Clay Toys of Belarus — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Each place's clay toys are modelled by hand by its own folk masters.
**Answer: True**.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The tsatski are made in one big factory for the whole country.
**Answer: False** — each place's toys are handmade by its own masters.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Shmatko is the clay-toy master of Zembin.
**Answer: True**.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** The book names clay toys from Nemanitsa, Zembin and Logoisk.
**Answer: True**.
