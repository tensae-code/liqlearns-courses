# Hands-on — Making with Paper and Pieces

The lesson with your hands, your feet and your voice together, not only your eyes.

## Crafts from what is nearby
Gather what your locality gives freely — paper, grain seeds, plants, cloth pieces, leaves. Sort them by colour and roughness. Everything in this lesson is made from a spread like this one.

## Texture touch-test
With eyes closed, touch a bark piece, a stone, a glass and a leaf from your collage materials. Name rough or smooth for each before you open your eyes.
