# Making with Paper and Pieces — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** How do we tell roughness from smoothness?
**Answer: By looking and by touching.**

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** What is drawn on the board before the pieces go down?
**Answer: The design, with its dark and light degrees marked.**

**3. (Sub lesson 4)** What is carved to make the guide's printing stamp?
**Answer: A potato (ህትመት በድንች ማተም — printing by potato).**

**4. (Sub lesson 5)** Why is the boiling step a grown-up's job in our hands-on work?
**Answer: It uses a pot of boiling dyed water — hot water is handled by a grown-up.**

**5. (Sub lesson 6)** What materials does the decorating work use?
**Answer: Materials available nearby — what is at hand.**
