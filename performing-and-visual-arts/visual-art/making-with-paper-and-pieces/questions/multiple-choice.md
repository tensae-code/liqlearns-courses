# Making with Paper and Pieces — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In the mould method, the wet paper is layered…
  a) Over a chosen object, copying its shape b) Flat on the floor only c) Inside a fired clay pot
**Answer: a) Over a chosen object, copying its shape** — the layers go over the object so the work copies its shape.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the pulp modelled over?
  a) A tied wire-and-wood armature b) A fired pot c) A colour wheel
**Answer: a) A tied wire-and-wood armature** — the pulp goes over a wire-and-wood armature.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Collage is made by…
  a) Gluing things onto one surface b) Carving wood c) Mixing paints only
**Answer: a) Gluing things onto one surface** — collage glues natural and man-made things onto a surface.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** What is sorted before gluing a mosaic?
  a) The pieces, by light and shade b) The brushes, by size c) The papers, by age
**Answer: a) The pieces, by light and shade** — sort the cut pieces by light and shade.

**5. (Sub lesson 4)** The last step of a paper mosaic is…
  a) Dry it in the sun b) Fire it in a kiln c) Soak it in water
**Answer: a) Dry it in the sun** — the book's fifth step dries the picture in the sun.

**6. (Sub lesson 5)** What decides a tie-dye design?
  a) The folding, twisting and tying b) The size of the pot only c) The colour of the string only
**Answer: a) The folding, twisting and tying** — designs vary with the folding, twisting and tying.

**7. (Sub lesson 6)** The Grade 2 guide's making strand joins which two things?
  a) Decorating with materials, and drawing b) Singing, and dancing c) Filming, and printing
**Answer: a) Decorating with materials, and drawing** — §2.4 joins decorating/beautifying with materials and drawing (መሳል).
