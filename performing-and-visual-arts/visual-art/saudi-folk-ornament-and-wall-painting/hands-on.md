# Hands-on — Saudi Folk Ornament and Wall Painting

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Work the lesson's own way

Draw one folk ornament unit — a triangle, circle or star — and repeat it across a strip of paper as a wall band, in two of the folk colours named in this lesson. Then tell your companion where in a Saudi house your band could live: a wall, a door, or a window.
