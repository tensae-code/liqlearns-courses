# Video script — Saudi Folk Ornament and Wall Painting

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

On the walls of Asir, the folk artist painted his world.

Mountains, sun, crescent and stars — that is Al-Qatt Al-Asiri.

Gypsum engraved. Doors turned into masterpieces.

Circle, triangle, square, rhombus — repeated, and repeated again.

That repetition is Saudi folk ornament.
