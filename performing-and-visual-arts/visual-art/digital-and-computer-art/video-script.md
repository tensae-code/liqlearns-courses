# Video script — Digital and Computer Art

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Flat shapes of colour — no blending!

That is **posterisation**: number the tones, lightest to darkest, and paint each shape flat.

On screen, build a character shape by shape — fill, group, send to back, rotate.

Finish it as a poster with a message.
