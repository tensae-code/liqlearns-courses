# Digital and Computer Art

## Do it first

Quick game: look at a face and squint. The light parts and dark parts separate into shapes. This lesson paints exactly those shapes.

## Sub lesson 1 — Posterisation — separate tones

**Posterisation** uses colours and tones **not blended, but as separate shapes of flat colour**. To posterise a portrait, **number its tones from lightest to darkest**, mix **tints and shades of one colour**, and paint each numbered shape flat. The book's example honours **Shaheed Hussain Adam**, the Maldivian soldier killed defending the MNDF headquarters in 1988.

## Sub lesson 2 — Designing a character on screen

The book's Unit 11 digital making builds the character **shape by shape with a word processor's shapes**: **fill colour**, **group the parts**, **send shapes to the back**, **rotate** them — and finish the character as **a poster with a message**.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson taught, part by part. Companion checks each part against the sub lessons above.

## Do It Yourself

1. Make your own piece using this lesson's way, from start to finish.
2. Show it to the class and say, in the book's own closing words, talk about your art: what you made, how, and what you learnt.
3. Write one sentence: what will you do differently next time?
