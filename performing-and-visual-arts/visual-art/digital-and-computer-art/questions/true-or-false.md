# Digital and Computer Art — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In posterisation the tones are blended until they disappear.
**Answer: False — they are kept as separate flat shapes.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A posterised portrait can be planned by numbering tones lightest to darkest.
**Answer: True.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Shapes can be grouped so they move together.
**Answer: True.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A computer character cannot be turned into a poster.
**Answer: False — the book finishes it as a poster with a message.**
