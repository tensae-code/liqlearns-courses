# Digital and Computer Art — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is posterisation?
**Answer: Using colours and tones as separate shapes of flat colour, not blended.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name two things you can do to shapes on screen when building a character.
**Answer: Any two of: fill colour, group parts, send shapes to the back, rotate.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** How is the finished digital character presented?
**Answer: As a poster with a message.**
