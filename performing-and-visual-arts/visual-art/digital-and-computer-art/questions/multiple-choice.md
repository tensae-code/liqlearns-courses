# Digital and Computer Art — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Posterisation uses tones…
a) blended smoothly  b) as separate shapes of flat colour  c) not at all
**Answer: b) as separate shapes of flat colour**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Before painting, a posterised portrait's tones are…
a) numbered from lightest to darkest  b) hidden  c) mixed into one colour
**Answer: a) numbered from lightest to darkest**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Tints and shades are mixed from…
a) many colours at random  b) one colour  c) no colour
**Answer: b) one colour**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The digital character is built…
a) shape by shape with a word processor's shapes  b) only by hand in clay  c) by photographing a real person
**Answer: a) shape by shape with a word processor's shapes**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** "Send to back" is used to…
a) delete the character  b) place a shape behind the others  c) print the poster
**Answer: b) place a shape behind the others**
