# Video script — Greek and Roman Art

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is Greek and Roman Art.

Roman art's three fields — architecture of grandeur and function (concrete, arches and domes; Hadrian's Arch at Jerash; the Doric, Composite and Tuscan column orders), realistic sculpture that glorifies emperors such as Trajan and Tiberius down to the wrinkles, and mosaic and wall painting of daily life; pupils draw a Roman-style gate step by step and build a model Roman amphitheatre from card and recycled materials.

Watch, listen — then say it back in your own words.
