# Batik

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about batik. Then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — Naphthol dyeing

Batik chemistry in three baths — the naphthol bath, the salt bath and the diazo bath with exact spoon measures, 15–20 minute immersions, then the wax boiled out with soap and soda so the pattern stands

## Sub lesson 2 — Procion and Vat dyeing

Two more dye systems with their recipes — Procion M colours dosed by shade depth with salt, soda and resist salt, and the easier Vat dye method (Vat dye 200 g, caustic soda, hydrosulphite) with its preparation order
