# Video script — Art History of Afghanistan

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Afghanistan's art is very, very old.

Stone tools a hundred thousand years old. Lines in caves.
Painted pots at Mundigak. The Buddha world of Gandhara.
Then Ghazna and Herat — mosques, minarets, Timur's flowering.
And today the museum in Kabul keeps it all — the House of Wonders.
