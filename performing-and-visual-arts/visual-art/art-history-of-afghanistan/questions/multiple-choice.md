# Art History of Afghanistan — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** The oldest tools in Afghanistan, from the Nawur plain, are about…
a) 100,000 years old  b) 100 years old  c) 1,000 years old
**Answer: a) 100,000 years old**

**2.** Mundigak is remembered for…
a) clay figurines and pottery painted in regular geometric designs  b) oil painting on canvas  c) photography
**Answer: a) clay figurines and pottery painted in regular geometric designs**

**3.** Herat flowered under which rulers in the Islamic period?
a) the Ghaznavids, Ghurids and Timurids  b) the Romans  c) the Vikings
**Answer: a) the Ghaznavids, Ghurids and Timurids**

**4.** The National Museum was first founded as…
a) the 'House of Wonders' (عجایب‌خانه)  b) a school  c) a market
**Answer: a) the 'House of Wonders' (عجایب‌خانه)**
