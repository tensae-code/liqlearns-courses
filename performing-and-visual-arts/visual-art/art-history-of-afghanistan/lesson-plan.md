# Lesson plan — Art History of Afghanistan

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Visual Art · **Lesson:** Art History of Afghanistan
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 2 — Visual Art (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Afghanistan's art through time — the 100,000-year-old stone tools of the Nawur plain and the cave lines of Aq Kupruk and Hazar Sum, Mundigak and Gandhara before Islam; the Ghaznavid and Timurid flowering of the Islamic centuries; the contemporary masters and institutions of today; and the National Museum that keeps it all.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the learner's earlier visual-art lessons. Recommended, never forced.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The art of Afghanistan before Islam** — The oldest human tools in Afghanistan, of obsidian and flint from the Nawur plain of Ghazni, are about 100,000 years old…
2. **The Islamic period** — The Islamic centuries: the Ghaznavids of Sultan Mahmud and Masud in Ghazna and Herat; the great congregational mosque of…
3. **Contemporary art and the country's art institutions** — The contemporary period, met through pictured works by its masters — Abdul Wase Rahro Omarzad, Qamaruddin Chishti, Sher …
4. **The National Museum of Afghanistan** — A museum is where a country's historical, cultural and artistic works are kept. The National Museum was founded in 1297 …
