# Video script — Mural Painting

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

See that wall? Imagine your art covering it — huge!

That is a **mural** — art on walls, in public, for everyone.

Paint it straight on, or fix tiles together.

Add textures that rise up — now it is a **relief mural**, like Kanduras, full of sea life.
