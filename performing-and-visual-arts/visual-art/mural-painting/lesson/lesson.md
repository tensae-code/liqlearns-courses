# Mural Painting

## Do it first

Quick game: point to the biggest wall near you. Imagine your drawing covering all of it, for everyone who passes to see. That is the size this lesson thinks in.

## Sub lesson 1 — Murals — art on walls

A **mural** is a **huge artwork displayed on a wall in a public space**. A mural can be **painted directly** onto the wall, or **made of tiled artworks fixed together** into one great work.

## Sub lesson 2 — Relief murals

A **relief mural** adds or carves **textures and forms** so the wall work becomes **three-dimensional** — it rises from the wall. The book's example is Maldivian artist **Ahmed Ihsaan's *Kanduras***, a relief mural of **Maldivian marine life** — the sea creatures of the islands, raised on a wall.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson taught, part by part. Companion checks each part against the sub lessons above.

## Do It Yourself

1. Make your own piece using this lesson's way, from start to finish.
2. Show it to the class and say, in the book's own closing words, talk about your art: what you made, how, and what you learnt.
3. Write one sentence: what will you do differently next time?
