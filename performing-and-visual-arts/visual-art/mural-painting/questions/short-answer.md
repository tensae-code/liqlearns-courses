# Mural Painting — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Where is a mural displayed?
**Answer: On a wall in a public space.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is added or carved in a relief mural?
**Answer: Textures and forms, making the work three-dimensional.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Whose relief mural does the book study?
**Answer: Ahmed Ihsaan's Kanduras.**
