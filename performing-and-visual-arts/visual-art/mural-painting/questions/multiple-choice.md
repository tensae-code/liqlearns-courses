# Mural Painting — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A mural is…
a) a small sketch in a notebook  b) a huge artwork displayed on a wall in a public space  c) a song
**Answer: b) a huge artwork displayed on a wall in a public space**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A mural can be made by…
a) painting directly, or fixing tiled artworks together  b) folding paper only  c) singing
**Answer: a) painting directly, or fixing tiled artworks together**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A relief mural is three-dimensional because…
a) it is very old  b) textures and forms are added or carved  c) it uses only blue paint
**Answer: b) textures and forms are added or carved**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Ahmed Ihsaan's Kanduras shows…
a) mountains  b) Maldivian marine life  c) city traffic
**Answer: b) Maldivian marine life**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** A mural is displayed…
a) in public spaces, on walls  b) hidden in a drawer  c) under water only
**Answer: a) in public spaces, on walls**
