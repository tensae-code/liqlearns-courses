# Mural Painting — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Murals are huge artworks.
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A mural must always be painted directly on the wall.
**Answer: False — it can also be made of tiled artworks fixed together.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A relief mural rises from the wall in three dimensions.
**Answer: True.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Kanduras is a mural of desert animals.
**Answer: False — it shows Maldivian marine life.**
