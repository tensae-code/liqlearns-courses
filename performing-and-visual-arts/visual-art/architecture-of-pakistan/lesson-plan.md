# Lesson plan — Architecture of Pakistan

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Visual Art · **Lesson:** Architecture of Pakistan
**Place in the curriculum:** Visual Art (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Pakistan's architectural history, building by building: the Great Bath at Mohenjo-daro and the Julian Monastery from the ancient past, Lahore Fort and the Badshahi Mosque from the Mughal era, and the Mausoleum of Muhammad Ali Jinnah and Minar-e-Pakistan from the nation's own time.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this course.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The ancient buildings** — the Great Bath at Mohenjo-daro; the Julian Monastery
2. **The Mughal buildings** — Lahore Fort in the Mughal era; the Badshahi Mosque (1673–74)
3. **The nation's monuments** — the Mausoleum of Muhammad Ali Jinnah; Minar-e-Pakistan

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier Visual Art lessons of this course. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name Pakistan's six buildings of this lesson in time order.
2. Tell which two belong to the Mughal era, and the Badshahi Mosque's dates.
3. Tell which two are monuments of Pakistan's own time.

## What you need
Your eyes and this lesson's list — six buildings, in time order.
