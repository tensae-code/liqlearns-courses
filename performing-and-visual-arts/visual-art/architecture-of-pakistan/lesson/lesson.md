# Architecture of Pakistan

## Do it first

Quick game: say what you already know about this lesson's subject — one thing, out loud. This lesson names it properly.

## Sub lesson 1 — The ancient buildings

The Class 9 book's history of architecture in Pakistan begins in the deep past:

- **The Great Bath at Mohenjo-daro** — the ancient city's great water building.
- **The Julian Monastery** — an ancient monastery, studied as a made building.

## Sub lesson 2 — The Mughal buildings

- **Lahore Fort** — the fort of Lahore in the **Mughal era**.
- **The Badshahi Mosque (1673–74)** — the great Mughal mosque of Lahore, built in 1673–74.

## Sub lesson 3 — The nation's monuments

- **The Mausoleum of Muhammad Ali Jinnah** — the tomb of the nation's founder.
- **Minar-e-Pakistan** — the tower that marks Pakistan's own story.

Six buildings carry the whole arc: from Mohenjo-daro to Minar-e-Pakistan.
