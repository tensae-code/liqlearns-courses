# Hands-on — Architecture of Pakistan

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Time-line check
Write the six buildings on a line in time order: ancient pair, Mughal pair, nation's pair. Say each pair's era aloud.

## Draw one building
Choose one of the six and draw its front as simply as you can — a fort wall, a mosque with domes, or a tower. Label it with its name and era.
