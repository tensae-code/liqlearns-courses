# Architecture of Pakistan — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Great Bath stands at…
a) Lahore  b) Mohenjo-daro  c) Karachi
**Answer: b) Mohenjo-daro**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The Badshahi Mosque was built in…
a) 1673–74  b) 121–140  c) 1973–74
**Answer: a) 1673–74**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Lahore Fort belongs to…
a) the ancient era  b) the Mughal era  c) Pakistan's own time
**Answer: b) the Mughal era**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Minar-e-Pakistan is…
a) a Mughal mosque  b) an ancient bath  c) a monument of Pakistan's own time
**Answer: c) a monument of Pakistan's own time**
