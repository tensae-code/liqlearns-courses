# Architecture of Pakistan — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Julian Monastery is a Mughal building.
**Answer: False** — it stands with the ancient buildings, before the Mughal pair.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The Badshahi Mosque and Lahore Fort are both Mughal-era buildings of Lahore.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The lesson's arc runs from Minar-e-Pakistan back to Mohenjo-daro.
**Answer: False** — it runs from Mohenjo-daro forward to Minar-e-Pakistan.
