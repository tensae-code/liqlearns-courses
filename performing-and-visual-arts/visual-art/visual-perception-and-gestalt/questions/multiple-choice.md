# Visual Perception and Gestalt — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Ibn Al-Haytham overturned the Greek idea by showing light…
a) leaves the seen body and falls on the eye  b) leaves the eye  c) stays in the sky
**Answer: a) leaves the seen body and falls on the eye**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Newton split sunlight with…
a) a prism  b) a mirror only  c) a lens of water
**Answer: a) a prism** — into the seven spectrum colours.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The cones of the retina answer to…
a) red, blue and green light waves  b) sound waves  c) heat only
**Answer: a) red, blue and green light waves**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Colour fatigue comes from…
a) bright reds and blues  b) darkness  c) reading
**Answer: a) bright reds and blues**
