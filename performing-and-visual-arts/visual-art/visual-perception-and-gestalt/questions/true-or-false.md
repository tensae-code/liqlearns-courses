# Visual Perception and Gestalt — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The eye's retina holds cones that answer to coloured light.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Newton's prism joins colours into white only.
**Answer: False** — The prism splits sunlight into the seven spectrum colours.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Colour blindness has a cone explanation.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** The Greeks' idea of seeing stood unchallenged.
**Answer: False** — Ibn Al-Haytham overturned it: light leaves the seen body and falls on the eye.
