# Hands-on — Visual Perception and Gestalt

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Book 4, Colouring, "How Do We See Colours?"
Do the thing this part teaches, with real materials where the book names them: the science of colour vision — Ibn Al-Haytham's overturning of the Greek idea, light as something that leaves the seen body and falls on the eye; Newton's prism splitting sunlight into the seven spectrum colours; the eye and its retina, the cones that answer to red, blue and green light waves; colour blindness and its cone explanation, colour fatigue from bright reds and blues, and why the sky is blue
