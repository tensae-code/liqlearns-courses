# New Figuration and New Realism — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson teaches...
a) another country's arts only  b) New figuration and new realism — the movements that brought the figure and real objects back into art  c) nothing — it is just names
**Answer: b)** — that is the lesson's one thing.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The first sub lesson is...
a) Нова фигурација  b) an examination  c) a song from another continent
**Answer: a) Нова фигурација.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** North Macedonia's own forms in this lesson are...
a) dropped and replaced  b) kept as named parts  c) hidden
**Answer: b) kept as named parts.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The last sub lesson is...
a) Нов реализам  b) the bibliography  c) a review box
**Answer: a) Нов реализам.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Songs in this course are placed...
