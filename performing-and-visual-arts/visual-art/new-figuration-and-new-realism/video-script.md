# Video script — New Figuration and New Realism

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This is North Macedonia's own lesson.

**Нова фигурација.** Нова фигурација — New Figuration and art brut: the deformed, expressive figure and raw, untrained making as social criticism — Dubuffet, Bacon.

**Нов реализам.** Нов реализам — Nouveau Réalisme: redefining art with real things — Klein's body prints and monochromes, Tinguely's scrap machines, Arman's accumulations.

That is New Figuration and New Realism — North Macedonia's way, kept by name.
