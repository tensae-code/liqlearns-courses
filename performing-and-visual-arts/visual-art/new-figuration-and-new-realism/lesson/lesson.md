# New Figuration and New Realism

## Do it first

Name one thing that belongs in this lesson — New figuration and new realism — the movements that brought the figure and real objects back into art. Say what it is in your own words. If you can name one, you are already inside the lesson.

## Sub lesson 1 — Нова фигурација

Нова фигурација — New Figuration and art brut: the deformed, expressive figure and raw, untrained making as social criticism — Dubuffet, Bacon.

## Sub lesson 2 — Нов реализам

Нов реализам — Nouveau Réalisme: redefining art with real things — Klein's body prints and monochromes, Tinguely's scrap machines, Arman's accumulations.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson holds, part by part, in your own words. Companion checks: did every part get named, with its Macedonian name kept where the book gives one?

## Do It Yourself

1. Choose one named part of this lesson and find out one more true thing about it — from a person who knows it, or from the book your part came from.
2. Make or perform a small piece in the way of that part: a rhythm, a step, a drawing, a shaped piece of clay.
3. Write one sentence: what does this part show about North Macedonia's own way of teaching its arts?
