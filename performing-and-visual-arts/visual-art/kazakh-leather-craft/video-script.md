This lesson is Kazakh Leather Craft.

Kazakh Leather Craft — kazakh Leather Craft — its forms, history and cultural meaning, taught as one topic lesson. — as Kazakhstan's school books teach it: the kamchi, the Kazakh whip: symbol of power, wisdom and luck, still honouring the home; traditionally braided from five to forty leather cords with a tavolga-wood handle dressed in metal, leather and fringe; the class braids a round kamchi from cellophane-bag strips.

Watch, listen — then say it back in your own words.
