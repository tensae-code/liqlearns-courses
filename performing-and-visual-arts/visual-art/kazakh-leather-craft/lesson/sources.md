| Kazakhstan — § 8. Қазақ халқының тұрмыстық заттарының орындалу техникасы мен материалдарын (торсық, дөңгелек үстел) зерделеу (Studying the making technique and materials of Kazakh household items — torsyk, round table) — what it teaches: in Kazakh craft leather was widely used, and items made from it took a notable place in the nomadic people's life; vessels prepared from processed leather are varied in kind and volume and are used for varied purposes; from old times travellers and herdsmen took a торсық when going on the road — a торсық is a vessel for pouring a drink, made of leather; the торсық was tied to the saddle or the cart's shady side; as it moved, the drink inside the торсық shook and its taste entered; when the kymys or airag in the торсық ran out, a қатық formed at its neck side; water was added to it and a шалап was made and drunk; the торсық was made from soaked (жідитілген) leather — cut by putting it on a mould, then sewn with a strap; later two wooden boards were put between and, by pressing, an ornament was imprinted; wood was also widely used in Kazakh craft — for nomadic and semi-nomadic life to be convenient, ancient furniture and dishes and other household things were prepared light, small in volume and convenient to carry; items made of wood were decorated with ornament and inlaid (бедерленген) with bone and silver; the дөңгелек үстел (round table) is a low table joined together from wooden boards — around it көрпешелер (cushions) are laid and бестек (?) spread, and people sitting around the round table see each other freely; the round table is kept and carried conveniently — this was very convenient for nomadic life; by the tradition formed from old times, Kazakhs brought a guest up to the төр (place of honour) and gave food — a stranger who came to the house was received with open arms without asking where he came from or who he was, and seen off without giving food (?) (student book; original target `culture-and-values/kazakh-leather-craft`) | Book 5, printed p. 26 |
| Kazakhstan — § 10. Материалды өңдеу. Бұйымды дайындау (Processing the material. Making the product) — what it teaches: wood timber is used in varied production fields and in life; making a product from wood timber begins from choosing the preparation (blank) needed for the future product's parts — that is, the material (board, plywood and so on); its base — that is, the flattest face — is chosen, and on the preparation, lines and points are drawn showing the places to be processed; then sawing is carried out; the sawn face of the preparation can be rough — an allowance is left for processing it; planing (сүргілеу) the timber to smooth the preparation and bring it to the needed form and measurement — that is, the preparation's thin layers are cut off in the form of shavings; for changing the preparation's form and measurements varied hand tools and machines are used: a drill (бұрғы), a jointer (жонғыш), a thicknesser (рейсмус), a milling machine (фрезер) and so on; alongside wood timber, leather is counted a very strong and valuable material; before making a product from leather the pattern (пішім) must be prepared; when cutting paired pieces, take into account that leather stretches well crosswise, and cut in one direction; for sewing a leather product a strong kapron thread is used, and a decorative seam is run on its top; the strongest thread used in sewing leather is called ыңыршақ жіп (sinew thread) — it was first used in making the saddle (ер-тоқым) and horse gear (атәбзел); today the sinew thread is used in sewing leather products; for putting a seam mark on the leather's face a белгітүсіргіш (roller) is used; with a біз (awl) the seam's holes are pierced; the sewn leather pieces' cut places are smoothed with a борд tool; the leather souvenir (былғары кәдесый), variant 1 — a торсық: made from leather scraps (қиықтарынан); the soaked leather, taken from the wooden mould, was put into dye, boiled and dried; then the product was cut and the ornament sewn; by the sketch drawn on the торсық with a stylus (батырғы), hot sand was poured in (at work the leather was damp); after it fully dried the sand inside was poured out and the торсық dried in the wind — through that the торсық's form is preserved (student book; original target `culture-and-values/kazakh-leather-craft`) | Book 5, printed p. 30 |
| Kazakhstan — § 10 (continued). Былғары кәдесый (2-нұсқа) (The leather souvenir, variant 2) — what it teaches: the souvenir's material can be old leather scraps — before use they must be cleaned with a brush; to tan the leather it must be put into a solution of table salt and vinegar acid (500 ml water, 25 g table salt, 10 ml of 9% vinegar acid); variant 2 in twenty-six step photographs: the sketch drawn, a paper template cut, the ornament-shaped leather piece cut out, the ornament tooled in with stamps and cutting tools, the piece dyed, glued to its backing, the edges finished and a cord attached — the finished торсық-shaped leather pendant (student book; original target `culture-and-values/kazakh-leather-craft`) | Book 5, printed p. 34 |
