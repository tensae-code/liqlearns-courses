# Lesson plan — Shikasteh Calligraphy

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Visual Art · **Lesson:** Shikasteh Calligraphy
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 2 — Visual Art (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the shikasteh (شکسته, 'broken') Nastaliq — the flowing, joined-up hand of Dari and Persian calligraphy: first the dotting of its single letters, then combining its letters with the others in pairs and groups.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the learner's earlier visual-art lessons. Recommended, never forced.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**.

## Sub lessons

2. **Combining the Shikasteh letters** — Then its letters are combined with the others in pairs and groups — ا و ب؛ ج، د؛ ر، س؛ ص، ط؛ ع، ف؛ ق، ک؛ ل، م، ن؛ و، ه؛ …
