# Video script — Shikasteh Calligraphy

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

One more hand — the broken hand, shikasteh.

Its letters flow and run together.
First, each letter alone, counted in dots.
Then letters in pairs, in groups — a river of writing.
Shikasteh: the hand that does not stop.
