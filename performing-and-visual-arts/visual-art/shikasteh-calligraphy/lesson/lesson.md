# Shikasteh Calligraphy

## Do it first

Start with the first part below — do it or make it before you read any explanation.

## Sub lesson 1 — The single letters of Shikasteh and their dotting

Grade 6's second chapter teaches the 'broken' flowing hand: first the dotting (طرز نقطه‌گذاری) of its single letters — the measure of each letter counted in dots, as in Nastaliq, but in the shikasteh forms.

## Sub lesson 2 — Combining the Shikasteh letters

Then its letters are combined with the others in pairs and groups — ا و ب؛ ج، د؛ ر، س؛ ص، ط؛ ع، ف؛ ق، ک؛ ل، م، ن؛ و، ه؛ ی — the letters running into one another in the hand's flowing way.
