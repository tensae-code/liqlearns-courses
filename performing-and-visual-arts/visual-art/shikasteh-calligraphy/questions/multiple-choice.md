# Shikasteh Calligraphy — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Shikasteh (شکسته) means…
a) 'broken' — the flowing broken hand  b) 'golden'  c) 'printed'
**Answer: a) 'broken' — the flowing broken hand**

**2.** Shikasteh is a form of…
a) Nastaliq  b) Naskh only  c) Latin printing
**Answer: a) Nastaliq**

**3.** The chapter begins with…
a) the dotting of the single letters  b) whole essays  c) painting pictures
**Answer: a) the dotting of the single letters**

**4.** After the single letters, pupils learn…
a) combining the letters in pairs and groups  b) to erase letters  c) music notation
**Answer: a) combining the letters in pairs and groups**
