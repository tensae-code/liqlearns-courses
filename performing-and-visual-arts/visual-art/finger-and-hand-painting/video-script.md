# Video script — Finger and Hand Painting

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

**The whole hand as a stamp.** Dip the whole hand in paint and press-print it, filling a card with handprints — the hand itself is the stamp.

**Finger printing.** Dip the fingers in paint and press-print them on coloured card — each finger leaves its own small print.

**Fingers, fist and open hand together.** Print with the fingers, the closed fist and the open hand on one sheet, and compare the marks each part of the hand makes.

**Drawing with the fingers in paint.** Draw with the fingers in paint — a plant, the sun, a flower, a house, a person — the finger is the pencil.

That is Finger and Hand Painting — Angola's own books teach it this way.
