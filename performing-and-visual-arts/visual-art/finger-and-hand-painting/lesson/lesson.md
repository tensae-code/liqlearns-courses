# Finger and Hand Painting

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about this lesson's subject — painting directly with fingers and hands instead of a brush. Then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — The whole hand as a stamp

Dip the whole hand in paint and press-print it, filling a card with handprints — the hand itself is the stamp.

## Sub lesson 2 — Finger printing

Dip the fingers in paint and press-print them on coloured card — each finger leaves its own small print.

## Sub lesson 3 — Fingers, fist and open hand together

Print with the fingers, the closed fist and the open hand on one sheet, and compare the marks each part of the hand makes.

## Sub lesson 4 — Drawing with the fingers in paint

Draw with the fingers in paint — a plant, the sun, a flower, a house, a person — the finger is the pencil.
