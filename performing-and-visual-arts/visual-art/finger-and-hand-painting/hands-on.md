# Hands-on — Finger and Hand Painting

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The whole hand as a stamp
Work through this part with real materials or your own body, the way the book sets it: dip the whole hand in paint and press-print it, filling a card with handprints — the hand itself is the stamp. Show a companion what you made or did, and say what the part teaches.

## Finger printing
Work through this part with real materials or your own body, the way the book sets it: dip the fingers in paint and press-print them on coloured card — each finger leaves its own small print. Show a companion what you made or did, and say what the part teaches.

## Fingers, fist and open hand together
Work through this part with real materials or your own body, the way the book sets it: print with the fingers, the closed fist and the open hand on one sheet, and compare the marks each part of the hand makes. Show a companion what you made or did, and say what the part teaches.

## Drawing with the fingers in paint
Work through this part with real materials or your own body, the way the book sets it: draw with the fingers in paint — a plant, the sun, a flower, a house, a person — the finger is the pencil. Show a companion what you made or did, and say what the part teaches.
