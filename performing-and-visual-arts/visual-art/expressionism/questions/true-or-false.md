# Expressionism — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Expressionist colours can scream and shapes distort.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Die Brücke and Der Blaue Reiter are the same group.
**Answer: False** — Die Brücke is Dresden (1905); Der Blaue Reiter is Munich.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The Expressionist panel is the artist's own face and conscience.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Expressionism forbids feeling in painting.
**Answer: False** — Feeling — the artist's psychological states — is its very content.
