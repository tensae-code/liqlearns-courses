# Expressionism — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Expressionism is described as more than a style — it is…
a) a whole concept of life and world  b) a framing method  c) a kind of paper
**Answer: a) a whole concept of life and world** — man's projection onto nature.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Die Brücke was founded in…
a) Dresden (1905)  b) Munich  c) Paris
**Answer: a) Dresden (1905)** — with screaming colours and distorted shapes.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Der Blaue Reiter belonged to…
a) Munich  b) Dresden  c) Milan
**Answer: a) Munich** — Kandinsky, Franz Marc and Paul Klee.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Chagall said: other artists stand before nature, I stand before…
a) myself  b) the king  c) the sea
**Answer: a) myself** — the panel as the artist's own face and conscience.
