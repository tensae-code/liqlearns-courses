# Hands-on — Expressionism

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Book 1, Artistic Culture, "The Expressionist School"
Do the thing this part teaches, with real materials where the book names them: Expressionism — more than a style, a whole concept of life and world, and man's projection onto nature: Van Gogh, Munch and Ensor as pioneers; Die Brücke in Dresden (1905) with its screaming colours and distorted shapes; Der Blaue Reiter in Munich — Kandinsky, Franz Marc, Paul Klee — and the Bauhaus gathering of 1919; the panel as the artist's own face and conscience (Chagall: other artists stand before nature, I stand before myself), its colours the artist's psychological states, and its offshoots — committed art and magical realism
