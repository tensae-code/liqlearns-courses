# Muñecas Limé: Faceless Dolls of the Dominican Republic

## Do it first

Quick start: A doll with no face — and a whole country in it. Then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — The faceless dolls and what they stand for

The **Faceless Dolls** — *Muñecas sin Rostro* — were originally **Muñecas Limé**, or dolls **of El Higuerito**, created by the sculptor **Liliana Mera Limé**. They are a **Dominican craft emblem of the Afro-Antillean-European cultural mix**: no single face, because the doll stands for the whole mixed people.

## Sub lesson 2 — Making my own faceless doll

The book then **makes the doll**: in **papier-mâché**, step by step as pictured — a **balloon or bottle base**, the body of **crumpled paper**, strips pasted with **glue-and-water paste**, **layer upon dried layer**, until the faceless doll stands.
