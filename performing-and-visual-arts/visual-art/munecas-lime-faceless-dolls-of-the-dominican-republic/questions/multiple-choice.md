# Muñecas Limé: Faceless Dolls of the Dominican Republic — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The faceless dolls were created by…
a) the sculptor Liliana Mera Limé  b) a factory  c) an unknown child
**Answer: a) the sculptor Liliana Mera Limé**.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** They were originally called…
a) Muñecas Limé, or dolls of El Higuerito  b) caretas  c) güiras
**Answer: a) Muñecas Limé, or dolls of El Higuerito**.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The dolls are an emblem of…
a) the Afro-Antillean-European cultural mix  b) one single culture  c) a festival only
**Answer: a) the Afro-Antillean-European cultural mix**.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The doll is made in…
a) papier-mâché  b) clay only  c) wood only
**Answer: a) papier-mâché**.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The body is built over…
a) a balloon or bottle base with crumpled paper  b) a stone  c) a box only
**Answer: a) a balloon or bottle base with crumpled paper**.
