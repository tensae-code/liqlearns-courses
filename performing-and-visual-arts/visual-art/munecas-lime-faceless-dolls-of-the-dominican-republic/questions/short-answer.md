# Muñecas Limé: Faceless Dolls of the Dominican Republic — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why do the dolls have no face?
**Answer: They stand for the whole mixed people — the Afro-Antillean-European mix — not one single face.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What paste holds the strips?
**Answer: Glue-and-water paste, laid layer upon dried layer.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the doll in Spanish.
**Answer: Muñeca Limé — also Muñeca sin Rostro, the faceless doll.**
