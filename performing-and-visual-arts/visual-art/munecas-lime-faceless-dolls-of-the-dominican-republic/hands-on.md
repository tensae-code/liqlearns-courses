# Hands-on — Muñecas Limé: Faceless Dolls of the Dominican Republic

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## First layers
Begin a doll: choose a balloon or bottle base, shape the crumpled-paper body, and lay the first glue-and-water paste strips.

## Emblem card
Write the doll's card: its name (Muñeca Limé / Muñeca sin Rostro), its creator, its home, and the mix it stands for.
