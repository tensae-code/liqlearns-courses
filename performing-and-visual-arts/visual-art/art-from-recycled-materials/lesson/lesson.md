# Art from Recycled Materials

## Do it first

Quick start: look at an empty plastic container and a cardboard box. Toy shop or rubbish bin? Then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — The Toy Shop: a robot and an eco butterfly

**The Toy Shop** makes toys from **recyclable materials**: the **Little Robot (Roboțel)**, built from **boxes and packaging**, and the **Eco Butterfly**, made in **four steps** from a plastic container — **draw and colour the butterfly's wings and antennae; peel the container's label; glue the wings under the label; put the antennae into the bottle's mouth**.

## Sub lesson 2 — Invent your own toy

**Portfolio**: make toys from recyclable materials and **organise an exhibition**. **Sketch and build a toy you invent yourself — one with a practical function** — and make a toy from **whatever materials you have at hand in class**.
