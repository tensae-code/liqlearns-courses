# Video script — Art from Recycled Materials

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Do not throw it away — make a toy!

Boxes and packaging — a little robot! **Roboțel!**

A plastic container — wings on, antennae in — a butterfly!

Now invent your own toy. It must be useful.

That is art from recycled materials.
