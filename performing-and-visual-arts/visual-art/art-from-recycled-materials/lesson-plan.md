# Lesson plan — Art from Recycled Materials

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Visual Art · **Lesson:** Art from Recycled Materials
**Place in the curriculum:** Visual Art (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** toys from recyclable materials — the Little Robot from boxes and packaging, the Eco Butterfly from a plastic container in four steps, and inventing your own useful toy for the class exhibition.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this course in the same title.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The Toy Shop: a robot and an eco butterfly** — toys from recyclable materials — the Little Robot (Roboțel) built from boxes and packaging, and the Eco Butterfly made in four steps from a plastic container: draw and colour the wings and antennae, peel the container's label, glue the wings under the label, put the antennae into the bottle's mouth.
2. **Invent your own toy** — make toys from recyclable materials and organise an exhibition; sketch and build a toy you invent yourself, one with a practical function; and make a toy from whatever materials you have at hand in class.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
