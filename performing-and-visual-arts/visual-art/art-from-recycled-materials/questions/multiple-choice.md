# Art from Recycled Materials — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Little Robot (Roboțel) is built from…
a) boxes and packaging  b) clay  c) cloth
**Answer: a) boxes and packaging**.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The Eco Butterfly is made from…
a) a plastic container  b) a leaf  c) paper only
**Answer: a) a plastic container**.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The butterfly's wings are glued…
a) under the container's label  b) on the table  c) inside the bottle
**Answer: a) under the container's label**.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** The antennae are put into…
a) the bottle's mouth  b) the wings  c) the label
**Answer: a) the bottle's mouth**.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** A toy you invent yourself must have…
a) a practical function  b) a price tag  c) batteries
**Answer: a) a practical function**.
