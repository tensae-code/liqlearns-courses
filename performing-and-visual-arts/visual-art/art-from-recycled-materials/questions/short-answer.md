# Art from Recycled Materials — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the Eco Butterfly's four steps in order.
**Answer: Draw and colour the wings and antennae; peel the container's label; glue the wings under the label; put the antennae into the bottle's mouth.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How are the class's recycled toys shown?
**Answer: In an exhibition the class organises.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Before building your invented toy, what do you do first?
**Answer: Sketch it.**
