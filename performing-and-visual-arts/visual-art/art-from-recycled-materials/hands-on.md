# Hands-on — Art from Recycled Materials

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Eco Butterfly
Make the Eco Butterfly in its four steps: draw and colour the wings and antennae, peel the container's label, glue the wings under the label, put the antennae into the bottle's mouth.

## Invent and exhibit
Sketch a toy you invent yourself — one with a practical function — build it from recyclable materials you have at hand, and show it in a class exhibition.
