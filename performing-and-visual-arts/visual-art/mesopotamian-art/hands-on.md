# Hands-on — Mesopotamian Art

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Arquitectura mesopotámica
Work through this part with real materials or your own body, the way the book sets it: Mesopotamia, land between the Tigris and Euphrates — advances in the round arch, vault and dome; temples and palaces raised on terraces against the floods, in clay adobe; the ziggurat of Ur-Nammu, the Ishtar Gate and the palace of Sargon II. Show a companion what you made or did, and say what the part teaches.

## Escultura y pintura mesopotámicas
Work through this part with real materials or your own body, the way the book sets it: Mesopotamian sculpture — narrative reliefs of wars and hunts with realism in the animals, statues ranked by size (god greater than ruler, ruler greater than subject); painting in fresco murals and mosaics, flat colour within dark contour lines — the Standard of Ur. Show a companion what you made or did, and say what the part teaches.
