# Mesopotamian Art — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson — Mesopotamian Art — opens with…

**Answer: a) Arquitectura mesopotámica.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The Venezuela book teaches “Arquitectura mesopotámica” as…
a) a part of mesopotamian art  b) a sports rule  c) a cooking recipe
**Answer: a) a part of mesopotamian art.**

**3.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) Escultura y pintura mesopotámicas  b) long division  c) map coordinates
**Answer: a) Escultura y pintura mesopotámicas.**

**4.** The parts of this lesson come from…
a) Venezuela's own Educación Artística books  b) a newspaper  c) a song chorus
