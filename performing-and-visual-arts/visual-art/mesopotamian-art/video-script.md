# Video script — Mesopotamian Art

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

**Arquitectura mesopotámica.** Mesopotamia, land between the Tigris and Euphrates — advances in the round arch, vault and dome; temples and palaces raised on terraces against the floods, in clay adobe; the ziggurat of Ur-Nammu, the Ishtar Gate and the palace of Sargon II.

**Escultura y pintura mesopotámicas.** Mesopotamian sculpture — narrative reliefs of wars and hunts with realism in the animals, statues ranked by size (god greater than ruler, ruler greater than subject); painting in fresco murals and mosaics, flat colour within dark contour lines — the Standard of Ur.

That is Mesopotamian Art — Venezuela's own books teach it this way.
