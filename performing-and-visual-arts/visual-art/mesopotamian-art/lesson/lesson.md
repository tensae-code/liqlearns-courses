## Sub lesson 1 — Arquitectura mesopotámica

Mesopotamia, land between the Tigris and Euphrates — advances in the round arch, vault and dome; temples and palaces raised on terraces against the floods, in clay adobe; the ziggurat of Ur-Nammu, the Ishtar Gate and the palace of Sargon II.

## Sub lesson 2 — Escultura y pintura mesopotámicas

Mesopotamian sculpture — narrative reliefs of wars and hunts with realism in the animals, statues ranked by size (god greater than ruler, ruler greater than subject); painting in fresco murals and mosaics, flat colour within dark contour lines — the Standard of Ur.
