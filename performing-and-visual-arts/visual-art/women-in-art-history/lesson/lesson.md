## Sub lesson 1 — Women as model, patron and artist — the painters

Andorra's batxillerat history of art is taught through questions, and one whole block is **art and women**: women **as model, as patron and as artist**. In painting: **Cassatt, Morisot and O'Keeffe** with **Impressionism**; **Kahlo and Carrington** with **Surrealism**; **Frankenthaler and Krasner** with **abstract expressionism**.

## Sub lesson 2 — Women in sculpture and architecture

The same block continues into the other two arts. In **sculpture**: **Claudel, Bourgeois, Kusama, Iglesias, Graves and Solano**. In **architecture**: **Reich, Morgan, Hadid and Sejima**. Counted in, women change the story of all three arts — painting, sculpture and architecture.
