# Women in Art History — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the three roles of women in the art-and-women block.
**Answer: Model, patron and artist.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name two women architects from the programme.
**Answer: Any two of Reich, Morgan, Hadid and Sejima.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Name two women sculptors from the programme.
**Answer: Any two of Claudel, Bourgeois, Kusama, Iglesias, Graves and Solano.**
