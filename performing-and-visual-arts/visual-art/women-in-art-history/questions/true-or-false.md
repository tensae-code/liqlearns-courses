# Women in Art History — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Cassatt and Morisot are placed with Impressionism.
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The block treats women only as models.
**Answer: False — as model, patron and artist.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Zaha Hadid is named among the architects.
**Answer: True.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** No women are named in sculpture.
**Answer: False — six are named, including Claudel and Bourgeois.**
