This lesson is Chinese Gongbi Painting.

Gongbi — China's meticulous, fine-line detailed painting, taught through flowers and birds. — as China's school books teach it: Meticulous (gongbi) flowers — line neat and fine, colour true to the thing's own colour; its kinds (ink-outline, light colour, heavy colour) and its craft: the fine "clothing-line" and "leaf-vein" brushes, wrist for short strokes and shoulder for long, colour laid with two brushes at once, one carrying colour and one water.

Watch, listen — then say it back in your own words.
