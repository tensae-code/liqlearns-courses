# Chinese Gongbi Painting

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about chinese gongbi painting. Then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — China Grade 6 Art Vol.2

Meticulous (gongbi) flowers — line neat and fine, colour true to the thing's own colour; its kinds (ink-outline, light colour, heavy colour) and its craft: the fine "clothing-line" and "leaf-vein" brushes, wrist for short strokes and shoulder for long, colour laid with two brushes at once, one carrying colour and one water

## Sub lesson 2 — China HS Chinese Painting & Calligraphy

Meticulous flower-and-bird painting by the classical copy methods — lin (copy beside), mo (trace over), fang (recompose in the master's manner); copying the Song album leaf Lotus from Water in four steps: draft and fine outline, layered shading washes (fenran), colouring water-colours before mineral colours, and unifying glazes
