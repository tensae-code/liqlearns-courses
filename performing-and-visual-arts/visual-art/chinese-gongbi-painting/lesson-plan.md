# Lesson plan — Chinese Gongbi Painting

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Visual Art · **Lesson:** Chinese Gongbi Painting
**Place in the curriculum:** Visual Art (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Gongbi — China's meticulous, fine-line detailed painting, taught through flowers and birds.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this course in the same title.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **China Grade 6 Art Vol.2** — Meticulous (gongbi) flowers — line neat and fine, colour true to the thing's own colour; its kinds (ink-outline, light colour, heavy colour) and its craft: the fine "clothing-line" and "leaf-vein" brushes, wrist for short strokes and shoulder for long, colour laid with two brushes at once, one carrying colour and one water
2. **China HS Chinese Painting & Calligraphy** — Meticulous flower-and-bird painting by the classical copy methods — lin (copy beside), mo (trace over), fang (recompose in the master's manner); copying the Song album leaf Lotus from Water in four steps: draft and fine outline, layered shading washes (fenran), colouring water-colours before mineral colours, and unifying glazes

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
