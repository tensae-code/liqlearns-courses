# Drawing: The Foundation Skill — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does repeated study of a thing do to your drawing of it?
**Answer: It brings a corrected and better representation.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why does the way you hold the pencil matter?
**Answer: The right grip gives free hand movement and lets you rule lines freely.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does a gesture drawing catch?
**Answer: The figure's movement, in simple lines.**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Why train the hand before drawing?
**Answer: So mind and hand move together and the hand obeys like the heart.**

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** What is imitation drawing (አስመስሎ መስራት)?
**Answer: Drawing a thing on paper while looking at it — or at its picture, poster or photograph.**

**6. (Sub lesson 6)** What two sources can a drawing come from, in the guide's teaching?
**Answer: Imagination/memory, and observation of what you see.**

**7. (Sub lesson 8)** From the Grade 6 exercise: what is drawing's nature, and what are its tools?
**Answer:** Drawing is the basic skill of visual art — the foundation work in line; its tools are the drawing instruments this lesson names (pencils and their kin, charcoal, chalk, on paper).

**8. (Sub lesson 8)** From the Grade 6 exercise: what is drawing's use for painting and for sculpture?
**Answer:** Drawing underlies both — a painting is planned and built on drawing, and a sculpture's form is first found in drawing; drawing study serves every visual art.
