# Drawing: The Foundation Skill — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** An engineer may begin a building by drawing its design.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Pencils come in hard, medium and soft grades.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A gesture study tries to catch every small detail of clothing.
**Answer: False**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Line drills include quarter-circles, half-circles and full circles.
**Answer: True**

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** Drawing what you see means drawing from memory without looking.
**Answer: False**

**6. (Sub lesson 6)** Model drawing means drawing from observation of a subject.
**Answer: True**
