# Drawing: The Foundation Skill — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Drawing is the basis of which work?
  a) All visual-art work b) Only music c) Only dance
**Answer: a) All visual-art work** — the Grade 6 book calls drawing the basis of all visual-art work.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which tool holds the paper steady while you draw?
  a) The easel b) The eraser c) The sharpener
**Answer: a) The easel** — the easel is the fixed drawing support.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In shading, the tones should…
  a) Walk gradually from light to dark b) Jump straight from white to black c) Stay all the same grey
**Answer: a) Walk gradually from light to dark** — keep the gradation from light to dark.

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** How should line drills be practised?
  a) Freely, without a ruler b) Only with a ruler c) Once, very slowly, then never again
**Answer: a) Freely, without a ruler** — the book says practise lines freely without a ruler.

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** On a grid, how do you copy the picture?
  a) One square at a time b) All of it at once from memory c) Only the outline of the biggest square
**Answer: a) One square at a time** — copy cell by cell, checking neighbours.

**6. (Sub lesson 6)** Which list names the guide's five basic skills of drawing?
  a) Edges, proportion, perspective, colour schemes, putting the thought together b) Singing, dancing, acting, filming, printing c) Cutting, gluing, folding, tying, weaving
**Answer: a) Edges, proportion, perspective, colour schemes, putting the thought together** — the guide names those five skills.
