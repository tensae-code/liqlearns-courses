# Hands-on — Drawing: The Foundation Skill

The lesson with your hands, your feet and your voice together, not only your eyes.

## Study one thing three times
Choose one object. Draw it three times in a row, looking more each time. The book's way: repeated study of a thing brings a corrected and better representation.

## Gesture lines
Watch a person or animal move. Draw the movement in a few simple lines — no detail, only the action. Show it to a friend and let them guess the movement.
