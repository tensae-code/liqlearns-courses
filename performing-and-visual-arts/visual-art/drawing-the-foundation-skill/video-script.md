The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Drawing is the root of every visual art.
Pencil, paper, eraser, sharpener — and a stand for the paper.
Light walks into dark, step by step. That is shading.
Free your hand first. Then draw what your eyes truly see.
