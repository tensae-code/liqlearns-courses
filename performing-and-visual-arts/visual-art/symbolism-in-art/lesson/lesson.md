# Symbolism in Art

## Do it first

Quick game: draw one simple picture that stands for an idea — a heart for love, a dove for peace — without writing any words. If your companion can read it, you have made a symbol.

## Sub lesson 1 — Symbols in art

A **symbol** in art is a picture that **conveys an idea, a feeling or a message** — it stands for something beyond itself, and the viewer reads it the way they read a word.

## Sub lesson 2 — A symbol of yourself

The Maldives book's Unit 2, *Me-Symbol*, sets the making: draw **a symbol of yourself** from **simplified shapes**, give it **thick black outlines** and **bright flat colours**, and add **comic-style action lines** to show energy and movement. Your symbol says who you are without a single written word.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson taught, part by part. Companion checks each part against the sub lessons above.

## Do It Yourself

1. Make your own piece using this lesson's way, from start to finish.
2. Show it to the class and say, in the book's own closing words, talk about your art: what you made, how, and what you learnt.
3. Write one sentence: what will you do differently next time?
