# Symbolism in Art — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What three things can a symbol in art convey?
**Answer: An idea, a feeling or a message.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What colours does the Me-Symbol use?
**Answer: Bright flat colours.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Whose symbol do you draw in this lesson?
**Answer: A symbol of yourself.**
