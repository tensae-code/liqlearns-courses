# Symbolism in Art — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A symbol in art is a picture that…
a) only copies what the eye sees  b) conveys an idea, feeling or message  c) is always a photograph
**Answer: b) conveys an idea, feeling or message**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A symbol stands for…
a) nothing at all  b) something beyond itself  c) only its own colour
**Answer: b) something beyond itself**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The Me-Symbol is drawn from…
a) detailed realistic shading  b) simplified shapes  c) traced photographs
**Answer: b) simplified shapes**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The outlines of the Me-Symbol are…
a) thin and pale  b) thick and black  c) invisible
**Answer: b) thick and black**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Comic-style action lines in the symbol show…
a) energy and movement  b) the artist's name  c) the price
**Answer: a) energy and movement**
