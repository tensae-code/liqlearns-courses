# Symbolism in Art — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A symbol in art can carry a message without words.
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A symbol is only decoration and means nothing.
**Answer: False — a symbol conveys an idea, feeling or message.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The Me-Symbol uses thick black outlines.
**Answer: True.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The Me-Symbol must be a realistic portrait photograph.
**Answer: False — it is drawn from simplified shapes.**
