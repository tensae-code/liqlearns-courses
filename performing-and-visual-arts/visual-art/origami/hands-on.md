# Hands-on — Origami

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## First folds
Choose the right paper. Fold one traditional model — a boat or a frog — following the steps in order, and press every crease flat and smooth with your thumbnail.

## Crease check
Fold the same model a second time. Compare the two: where a crease was pressed true, the fold sits true. Say what changed.
