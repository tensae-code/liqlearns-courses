# Origami — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the four traditional origami models.
**Answer: Cranes, storks, frogs and boats.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What do you press each crease with?
**Answer: The thumbnail — every crease is pressed flat and smooth with it.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the three parts of the folding discipline.
**Answer: Follow the steps in order, choose the right paper, and press every crease flat and smooth with the thumbnail.**
