# Origami — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Origami models are made by folding alone, without cutting or gluing.
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Origami began in Japan.
**Answer: True.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Any paper at all will do for origami — there is no need to choose.
**Answer: False** — the discipline begins with choosing the right paper.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A crease should be left soft and round.
**Answer: False** — every crease is pressed flat and smooth with the thumbnail.
