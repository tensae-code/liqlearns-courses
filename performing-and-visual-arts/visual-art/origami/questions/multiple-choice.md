# Origami — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Origami is…
a) Japan's paper-folding art  b) cutting paper with scissors  c) painting on paper
**Answer: a) Japan's paper-folding art** — over 1,000 years old.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which is a traditional origami model?
a) a crane  b) a castle  c) a car
**Answer: a) a crane** — the traditional models are cranes, storks, frogs and boats.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** How old is the art of origami?
a) over 1,000 years  b) 10 years  c) 100 years
**Answer: a) over 1,000 years**.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Every crease is pressed…
a) flat and smooth with the thumbnail  b) with a hammer  c) not at all
**Answer: a) flat and smooth with the thumbnail**.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The folding steps are followed…
a) in any order  b) in order  c) backwards
**Answer: b) in order**.
