# Video script — Origami

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

One sheet of paper. No scissors. No glue.

Fold — in order. Press the crease flat — smooth, with your thumbnail.

A crane! A stork! A frog! A boat!

That is **origami** — Japan's folding art, over a thousand years old.
