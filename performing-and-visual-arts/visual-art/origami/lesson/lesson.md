# Origami

## Do it first

Quick start: take one sheet of paper. Without scissors and without glue, what could you turn it into, by folding alone? Then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — The art of folding paper

**Origami** is Japan's paper-folding art, over **1,000 years old**. From one flat sheet, folding alone makes the traditional models: **cranes, storks, frogs and boats**. Nothing is cut and nothing is glued — the fold does all the work.

## Sub lesson 2 — The folding discipline

Origami has a discipline, and the book names it plainly: **follow the steps in order**, **choose the right paper**, and **press every crease flat and smooth with the thumbnail**. A crease pressed true is what lets the next fold sit true.
