# Space in Art

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about space in art. Then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — Kazakhstan Grade 4 Artistic Labour

the starry sky: constellations and their hero names, Ptolemy's list, Dürer's printed star maps, Ulugbek's catalogue; making a "Starry Sky" lamp: a dark-paper cylinder pierced and drawn with a chosen constellation, foil stars glued on, a lamp inside — step photos; cosmonaut-artists: Alexei Leonov's space paintings, Vladimir Dzhanibekov, astronaut Alan Bean's moon landscapes; how people of the past imagined the future: the 1911 French postcard series "In the Year 2000" and which inventions came true

## Sub lesson 2 — Kazakhstan Grade 4 Labour Training (Ormanbekova)

magnets and weightlessness: an experiment on what magnets attract; a round space construction in which a cosmonaut floats, held off the base by repelling magnets

Take one part of this lesson and do it again in your own way — then say which book part yours follows.
