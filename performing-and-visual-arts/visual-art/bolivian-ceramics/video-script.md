This is Bolivia's own lesson.

**Ceramics as Bolivia's cultural identity.** Bolivian ceramics rooted in the origin arts — a fusion of precolonial indigenous traditions and Spanish elements, studied in the Quechua, Aymara and Guarani pottery, with the country's clay deposits named department by department.

**Preparing the clay.** The potter's earth prepared step by step: grind, sift, soak to a milky consistency, rest until it holds like plasticine; and clay itself — kaolin, chamote, barbotina, engobe, and phasa, the edible clay (teacher compendium — evidence).

That is Bolivian Ceramics — Bolivia's way, kept by name.
