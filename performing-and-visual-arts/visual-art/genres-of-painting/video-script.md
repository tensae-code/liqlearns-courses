# Video script — Genres of Painting

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

This is Moldova's own lesson.

**Genuri ale picturii.** G4 Ch. 4, Genuri ale picturii — the genres of painting: landscape, portrait (and self-portrait), still life, and composition — genre scenes, historical, religious and mythological compositions, each with its masters.

**categories of composition.** Unit 3, categories of composition — the genre composition of everyday life, the mythological composition (Prometheus; Dubinovschi's "Strâmbă-Lemne"), and the symbolic sculptural composition.

**The everyday-life composition.** Unit III §1 — the everyday-life composition: Dutch masters Vermeer ("The Milkmaid", its diagonal balancing the empty wall) and Pieter de Hooch; in Romanian art Grigorescu and Aman; in the 20th century Corneliu Baba, and in Bessarabia Igor Vieru, Rusu-Ciobanu, Glebus Sainciuc, Romanescu.

**The historical genre of composition.** Unit III §1 — the historical genre of composition: great persons and memorable events, battle and war scenes with clear schemas that spotlight the idea — Velázquez's "Surrender of Breda", Aman's "Turkish Envoys at Vlad Țepeș".

That is Genres of Painting — Moldova's way, kept by name.
