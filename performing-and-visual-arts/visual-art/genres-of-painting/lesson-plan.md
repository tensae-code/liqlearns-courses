# Lesson plan — Genres of Painting

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Visual Art · **Lesson:** Genres of Painting
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 2 — Visual Art (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The genres of painting — landscape, portrait, still life, battle and everyday-life (genre) painting, and how each is recognised.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the course's earlier lessons in its title; recommended first, never forced.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of ..." in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Genuri ale picturii** — G4 Ch. 4, Genuri ale picturii — the genres of painting: landscape, portrait (and self-portrait), still life, and composition — genre scenes, historical, religious and mythological compositions, each with its masters.
2. **categories of composition** — Unit 3, categories of composition — the genre composition of everyday life, the mythological composition (Prometheus; Dubinovschi's "Strâmbă-Lemne"), and the symbolic sculptural composition.
3. **The everyday-life composition** — Unit III §1 — the everyday-life composition: Dutch masters Vermeer ("The Milkmaid", its diagonal balancing the empty wall) and Pieter de Hooch; in Romanian art Grigorescu and Aman; in the 20th century Corneliu Baba, and in Bessarabia Igor Vieru, Rusu-Ciobanu, Glebus Sainciuc, Romanescu.
4. **The historical genre of composition** — Unit III §1 — the historical genre of composition: great persons and memorable events, battle and war scenes with clear schemas that spotlight the idea — Velázquez's "Surrender of Breda", Aman's "Turkish Envoys at Vlad Țepeș".

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
