# Lesson plan — Artists of Central America

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Visual Art · **Lesson:** Artists of Central America
**Place in the curriculum:** Visual Art (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the artists of Honduras and of Central America — Honduras's first artists and its current artists, and the artists of the Central American countries, known and valued by name and by their works.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of its title — recommended first, never forced.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Artists of Honduras** — Honduras's first artists and its current artists, known and valued by name.
2. **Artists of Central America** — the artists of the Central American countries, known by their works.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the painters lessons of the Visual Art title. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name the two groups the Grade 7 book meets: Honduras's first artists and its current artists.
2. Say that the Grade 8 book widens the meeting to the artists of all Central America, known by their works.

## What you need
Your eyes, and a picture by an artist of your own country if you can find one.

- Thinks a country's art has no names → both books name their artists: Honduras's first and current artists, then the region's.
