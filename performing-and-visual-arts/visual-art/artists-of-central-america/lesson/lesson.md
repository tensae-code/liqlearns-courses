# Artists of Central America

## Do it first

Look at a picture you like. Someone made it — a person with a name and a place. A country's art is not faceless: it has artists. This lesson meets them the way Honduras's books do — Honduras first, then the whole region.

## Sub lesson 1 — Artists of Honduras

The Grade 7 book's sequence **Conociendo y valorando lo nuestro — knowing and valuing our own** meets the **artists of Honduras**: the country's **first artists** and its **current artists**, **known and valued by name**. First and current together — a country's art has a beginning and a living present.

## Sub lesson 2 — Artists of Central America

The Grade 8 book widens the same meeting to the region: **Artistas centroamericanos — Central American artists**, the artists of the **Central American countries**, known **by their works**. The artist is met through the work: what they made is how we know them.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion: which artists do the two books meet? Companion checks: Honduras's first and current artists? The artists of all Central America, by their works?

## Do It Yourself

1. Name one artist of your own country and one work of theirs, if you know one.
2. Write two headings — "first artists" and "current artists" — and say why a book teaches both.
3. Write one sentence: why are artists known by their works?
