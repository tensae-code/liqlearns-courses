# Hands-on — Artists of Central America

The lesson with your eyes, not only your ears.

## Meet an artist
Find one artwork by an artist of your own country (a picture in a book, a wall, a public place). Say the artist's name if it is known, and one thing the work shows.

## First and now
In pairs, sort what you found into two groups as the Honduras book does: the first artists of a place, and its current artists. Say one sentence about each group.
