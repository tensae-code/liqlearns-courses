# Artists of Central America — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Grade 7 book teaches Honduras's first artists and its current artists.
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Only past artists matter — current artists are not taught.
**Answer: False.** — current artists are taught too.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The Grade 8 book covers artists of all Central America.
**Answer: True.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Artists are known without any works.
**Answer: False.** — they are known by their works.
