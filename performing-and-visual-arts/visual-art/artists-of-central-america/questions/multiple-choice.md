# Artists of Central America — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Grade 7 book meets Honduras's artists in two groups…
a) first artists and current artists  b) painters and drummers only  c) teachers and pupils
**Answer: a) first artists and current artists**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Honduras's artists are to be…
a) forgotten  b) known and valued by name  c) hidden
**Answer: b) known and valued by name** — 'conociendo y valorando lo nuestro'.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The Grade 8 book widens the meeting to…
a) one village  b) the artists of all Central America  c) artists of another continent
**Answer: b) the artists of all Central America**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Central America's artists are known…
a) by their works  b) by their prices  c) not at all
**Answer: a) by their works**

**5. (Sub lesson 1)** A country's art, in these books, is…
a) faceless  b) carried by named artists, first and current  c) only old
**Answer: b) carried by named artists, first and current**
