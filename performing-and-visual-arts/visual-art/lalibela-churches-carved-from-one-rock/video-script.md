# Video script — Lalibela: Churches Carved from One Rock


The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.


---


A town in North Wollo — **Lalibela**.

Its churches were not built up. They were **carved down** — from the roof, into one whole rock.

Why? To last forever. To honour God with an astonishing work. Perhaps — to hide from an enemy's sight.

Eleven churches. One stone. A second Jerusalem in Ethiopian rock.

Stand before Bete Giyorgis, the cross cut into the earth, and ask the visitor's question: how was this made — and by whom — and why?
