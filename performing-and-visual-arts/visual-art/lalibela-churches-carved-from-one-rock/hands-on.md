# Hands-on — Lalibela: Churches Carved from One Rock

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Carve downward
Take a lump of clay or a bar of soft soap. Do NOT build up. Cut a small doorway and a window DOWNWARD and inward, freeing a tiny room from the lump — sculpture's way, Lalibela's way. Notice: every cut removes; nothing is added.

## The trench walk
On paper, draw a square rock from above. Inside it draw the trench, and inside the trench the cross of Bete Giyorgis. Label the three: the rock, the trench, the church — one stone throughout.

## Name them in order
Write the eleven churches' names in order on a strip of paper, from Bete Medhanialem to Bete Giyorgis. Fold the strip and test yourself until the order holds.
