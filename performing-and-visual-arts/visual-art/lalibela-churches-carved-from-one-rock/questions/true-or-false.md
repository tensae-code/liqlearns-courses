# Lalibela: Churches Carved from One Rock — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** One of the study's questions is whether similar works exist elsewhere in the world.
**Answer: True** — held for the lesson's last sub lesson.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Francisco Alvarez came in the 1520s, stayed six years, and described each building.
**Answer: True** — the Portuguese priest's account, paraphrased in the book.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The local stone hindered the work because it was too hard to carve at all.
**Answer: False** — the stone suited the work — softening and hardening in the right measure.

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Rock-hewn churches older than Lalibela exist in Lasta and northern Ethiopia.
**Answer: True** — the craft is indigenous, practised for many centuries before Lalibela.

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** Bete Gebriel we Rufael is one name — the tenth — not two churches.
**Answer: True** — the tenth name joins Gebriel and Rufael in one church.

**6. (Sub lesson 6)** Indigenous artistic works express the community, culture, belief and civilisation of their place.
**Answer: True** — the book's closing principle for the comparison.
