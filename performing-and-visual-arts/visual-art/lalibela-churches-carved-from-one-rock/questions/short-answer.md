# Lalibela: Churches Carved from One Rock — short answer

Answer in a sentence or a short list.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name two things, besides the building way, that the study asks you to appreciate.
**Answer:** The story, and the ancients' thinking and civilisation history (with the churches' artistic history and benefit).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What did King Lalibela intend to build in Lasta?
**Answer:** A second Jerusalem.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What three marks of special craft set Lalibela's buildings apart?
**Answer:** The quality of the work, the buildings' size, and the cleverness of their decoration.

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Give the book's second reason in your own words.
**Answer:** To do an astonishing thing — showing honour and greatness to their God; people do the impossible for their faith.

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** Name the last three churches in the book's order.
**Answer:** Bete Merqorios (9), Bete Gebriel we Rufael (10), Bete Giyorgis (11).

**6. (Sub lesson 6)** In what four things do indigenous works differ from place to place?
**Answer:** Purpose, input, method and era.
