# Lalibela: Churches Carved from One Rock — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Lalibela study asks you to explain the churches' —
a) price and present owners  b) artistic history and benefit, and their building way/process/inputs/technique  c) distance from Addis Ababa
**Answer: b) artistic history and benefit, and their building way/process/inputs/technique** — history, benefit, and the building way in full.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Lalibela is a town in —
a) North Wollo Zone, Amhara Region  b) the Southern Nations region  c) Harari Region
**Answer: a) North Wollo Zone, Amhara Region** — North Wollo Zone, Amhara Region.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The first written information on the churches' history is —
a) Alvarez's letters  b) the Gedle Lalibela, the king's life-book  c) a Portuguese map
**Answer: b) the Gedle Lalibela, the king's life-book** — Ethiopian writers since at least the 15th century; the Gedle Lalibela first.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** The Lalibela churches were made by —
a) stacking cut stones from a foundation upward  b) carving downward from the roof, from one whole great rock  c) enlarging natural caves only
**Answer: b) carving downward from the roof, from one whole great rock** — cut from the roof downward — sculpture's method, not building's.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** The carvers' iron tools were made for —
a) hewing, prising and cutting  b) ploughing and harvesting  c) writing and painting
**Answer: a) hewing, prising and cutting** — varied iron tools for the three works of the rock.

**6. (Sub lesson 4)** The book's first reason for carving into the ground is —
a) to save building materials  b) to make the buildings eternal  c) to keep the churches cool
**Answer: b) to make the buildings eternal** — eternity — and centuries later we still admire them.

**7. (Sub lesson 4)** A church below ground, in an era of frequent wars, would be —
a) easier for invaders to find  b) not easily found by an invader  c) useless for worship
**Answer: b) not easily found by an invader** — the possible hiding reason.

**8. (Sub lesson 5)** How many churches does the book name at Lalibela?
a) ten  b) eleven  c) twelve
**Answer: b) eleven** — eleven — Bete Medhanialem to Bete Giyorgis.

**9. (Sub lesson 5)** The first church in the book's order is —
a) Bete Giyorgis  b) Bete Mariam  c) Bete Medhanialem
**Answer: c) Bete Medhanialem** — Bete Medhanialem stands first.

**10. (Sub lesson 6)** The book's Egyptian comparison is —
a) a pyramid of stacked stone  b) the tomb cut from one rock for Queen Hatshepsut  c) a painted tomb chamber
**Answer: b) the tomb cut from one rock for Queen Hatshepsut** — cut from one rock — Lalibela's own method.

**11. (Sub lesson 6)** The Khazneh temple stands in —
a) India  b) Egypt  c) Jordan
**Answer: c) Jordan** — Jordan; India's is the Kailasa temple.
