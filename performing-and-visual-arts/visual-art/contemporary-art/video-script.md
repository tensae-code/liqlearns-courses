The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

First the horse pulled the cart. Then came the bicycle.

Then — the car, the train, the aeroplane, the rocket!

The world began to move fast, and artists felt it.

Russolo and Balla painted speed itself.

That is one door into the art of our own century.
