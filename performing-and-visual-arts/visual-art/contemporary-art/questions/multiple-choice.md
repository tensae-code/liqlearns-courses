# Contemporary Art — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which machines came in the 20th century, much faster than before?
a) The car, the train, the aeroplane and the rocket  b) Only the bicycle  c) Only horse carts
**Answer: a) The car, the train, the aeroplane and the rocket**.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Before the bicycle, land machines were…
a) carts drawn by horses and sleds by dogs  b) electric cars  c) aeroplanes
**Answer: a) carts drawn by horses and sleds by dogs**.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The idea that especially interested 20th-century artists was…
a) speed  b) stillness  c) silence
**Answer: a) speed**.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Russolo and Balla were…
a) Futurist painters  b) calligraphers  c) potters
**Answer: a) Futurist painters**.
