# Contemporary Art — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Artists of the 20th century ignored the new machines completely.
**Answer: False** — they took up new ways of working, and speed especially interested them.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The rocket is one of the fast 20th-century machines the book names.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Futurism is the movement of speed in art.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Russolo and Balla painted only quiet, unmoving scenes.
**Answer: False** — their pictured Futurist paintings are about speed.
