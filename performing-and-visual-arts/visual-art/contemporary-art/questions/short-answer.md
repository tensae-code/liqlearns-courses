# Contemporary Art — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the three realms machines move in.
**Answer: On land, on water and through the air.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What came between the horse cart and the car in the book's telling?
**Answer: The bicycle.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Why did speed interest artists so much in the 20th century?
**Answer: Because people's machines had become much faster — speed was the new experience of the age.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** What did the Futurists make their subject?
**Answer: Speed and the movement of the modern world.**
