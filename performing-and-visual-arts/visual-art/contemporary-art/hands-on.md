# Hands-on — Contemporary Art

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Draw speed
Draw a bicycle, then a train, using quick repeated lines to show how fast each moves. Say which drawing feels faster and why.

## Machines in order
Draw the book's line of machines in order — horse cart, sled, bicycle, car, train, aeroplane, rocket — and label each with where it moves: land, water or air.
