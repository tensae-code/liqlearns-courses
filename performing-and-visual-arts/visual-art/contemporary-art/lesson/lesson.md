## Sub lesson 1 — A century of faster machines

People made machines that move **on land, on water and through the air** — first **carts drawn by horses and sleds by dogs**, then the **bicycle**, and in the **20th century** much faster machines: **the car, the train, the aeroplane and the rocket**. Art did not stand aside from this change: during the 20th century artists took up **new ways of artistic work**.

## Sub lesson 2 — Futurism — the art of speed

**That is why the idea of speed especially interested artists during the 20th century** — as in the pictured **Futurist paintings of Russolo and Balla**. The Futurists painted the modern world's movement itself: speed became the subject of the picture.
