| Vietnam — Lesson 3 — three representative works — Michael Pinsky's installation work "Pollution Pods" (Miền ô nhiễm, 2018), in which the artist performs an installation showing five dome tents joined together, simulating the air presence of the big cities and a place on the coast, the viewers walking through the cells with degrees of air pollution from dry and cold places to hot and warm, the work conveying the message of the air quality not being uniform between the rich and poor cities in the world; Marina Abramovic's performance work "The Artist is Present" (Nghệ sĩ của thực tại, 2010), in which the artist performing sits on a chair, facing an empty chair, and during her performance the artist interacts with the audience as they come to sit on the chair, both keeping silent and experiencing the moment of the present right at the time and place of the performance — this helps the viewers come closer to the artist and brings different experiences and feelings; and Nam June Paik's video-art work "Electronic Superhighway: Continental U.S., Alaska, Hawaii" (Siêu xa lộ điện tử, 1995), in which the artist installs 51 channels of electronic equipment from televisions, neon lights, steel and coloured wood, sound and more, to create the feeling of the images seen from a car running across the regions, with the message showing the picture of the countryside of the United States (Hoa Kỳ) always influenced by cinema and television; the themes of contemporary art works usually reflect the current-affairs problems of contemporary society in different ways, with the purpose of helping the viewers be conscious of the world around them according to the viewpoint the artist wants to convey (student book; original target `visual-art/contemporary-art-currents`) | Book 5, printed p. 14 |
