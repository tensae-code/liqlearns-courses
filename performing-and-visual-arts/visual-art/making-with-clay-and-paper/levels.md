# Making with Clay and Paper — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number, as before. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. The sub lessons climb from the first modelling steps to the full finishing chain, then across to paper, to the learner's own area, and to making for giving and for letters.

## Sub lesson 1 — Clay modelling
Pass mark: give the modelling steps in order — plan on paper, knead clean until soft, roll and shape by adding and taking away — and the ready-clay test (it does not stick to the hands).

## Sub lesson 2 — Drying, firing and decorating clay
Pass mark: put the finishing chain in order — windless shade to dry, smooth and line, fire with dung and chaff, brush on plant colours, gum for shine.

## Sub lesson 3 — Paper pictures: cut, fold and paste
Pass mark: make or describe a picture built only from cut, folded and pasted paper, following a model.

## Sub lesson 4 — Houses and pots of our area
Pass mark: name architecture (ኪነ ህንፃ) as house building, and say how a known pot is imitated from local soil.

## Sub lesson 5 — A New Year gift card
Pass mark: describe a New Year gift card made by colour-painting, made by hand to give away.

## Sub lesson 6 — Shaping letters from materials
Pass mark: shape or name three Amharic letters built from materials such as clay, sticks, stones or string.

## Sub lesson 7 — Papier-mâché: paper kneaded like clay (Grade 8)
Pass mark: give the papier-mâché process in order (soak ~24 hours, knead until it binds, flour paste with sugar, mix and knead, paste on or form like clay) and carry out the waste-basket activity.
