# Hands-on — Making with Clay and Paper

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Model an animal
Plan an animal you enjoy on paper. Knead clay until it does not stick to your hands. Roll and shape it, adding and taking away. If it must stand tall, build a thin stick frame inside first. Cover it with plastic while it dries in the shade.

## The finishing chain
With an adult's help where fire is used: let a small clay piece dry in a windless, shaded place, smooth it, and brush on colour with a simple brush. Say the chain out loud as you finish each step: dry — smooth — colour — shine.

## A picture with no pencil
Take coloured papers, or plain paper you colour yourself. Cut shapes, fold some of them, and paste them into a picture of your home or your street. No drawn lines allowed — the shapes must do all the work.

## Letters on the ground
Outside, shape three Amharic letters big on the ground from stones, sticks or string. Walk around each letter and read it aloud. Photograph or draw them before you sweep them away.
