# Video script — Making with Clay and Paper

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Here is a lump of clay. Squeeze it. It keeps the shape. Good.

First — **plan**. Draw what you want to make.
Now **knead**. Push, fold, push, fold, until the clay is soft.
Does it stick to your hands? Not anymore. It is ready.

**Roll** it. **Shape** it. Add a little. Take a little away.
Look — an animal! A pot! A letter!

Now it must rest. In the **shade**, where no wind blows.
Dry. Smooth. Then **fire** — chaff and dung packed around it.
Fire makes it hard. Fire makes it last.

Last — **colour**. Plant colours, brushed on softly.
A little warm gum, and it shines.

Clay done. Now paper!
**Cut** it. **Fold** it. **Paste** it. A picture grows — no pencil at all.

Your turn. Make one thing. Slowly. With your own hands.
