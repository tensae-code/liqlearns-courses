# Making with Clay and Paper — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the first step of clay modelling?
a) firing the clay  b) planning the thing on paper  c) painting the clay
**Answer: b) planning the thing on paper** — plan first, then knead, then shape.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A wet finished clay work should dry —
a) in strong wind  b) in a windless, shaded place  c) in direct hot sun
**Answer: b) in a windless, shaded place.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A paper picture is made by —
a) drawing only  b) cutting, folding/joining and pasting paper  c) printing photographs
**Answer: b) cutting, folding/joining and pasting paper.**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Architecture (ኪነ ህንፃ) means —
a) house building  b) coffee brewing  c) letter writing
**Answer: a) house building.**

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** A New Year gift card is made by —
a) buying a printed card  b) colour-painting a card by hand  c) weaving a card from strips
**Answer: b) colour-painting a card by hand.**

**6. (Sub lesson 6)** Letters can be shaped from —
a) only a pencil  b) different materials like clay, sticks and stones  c) nothing — letters cannot be shaped
**Answer: b) different materials like clay, sticks and stones.**
