# Making with Clay and Paper — ordering

Put each set in the right order.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The modelling steps: shape the planned thing — plan it on paper — knead the clay until it softens.
**Answer: plan it on paper → knead the clay until it softens → shape the planned thing.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The finishing chain: brush on plant colours — dry in a windless shaded place — fire with dung and chaff packed around — smooth and line the dry work.
**Answer: dry in a windless shaded place → smooth and line the dry work → fire with dung and chaff packed around → brush on plant colours.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Making a paper picture: paste the pieces down — cut the paper shapes — choose and fold the pieces.
**Answer: choose and fold the pieces → cut the paper shapes → paste the pieces down** (cut and fold in the order your picture needs; pasting is last).

**4.** (Sub lesson 7) Put the papier-mâché process in order: knead the soaked paper until it binds / soak the cut paper / mix with paste and form the thing / cut the fallen paper. **Answer: cut the fallen paper → soak the cut paper → knead the soaked paper until it binds → mix with paste and form the thing.**
