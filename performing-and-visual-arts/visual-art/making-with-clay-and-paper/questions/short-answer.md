# Making with Clay and Paper — short answer

Answer in a word or a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How do you know clay is ready to work? **Answer: it does not stick to your hands when you work it.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is packed inside and around a clay work to fire it? **Answer: dung and chaff.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In a paper picture, what holds the cut and folded pieces together? **Answer: paste (sticking/glue).**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Name two home utensils made of fired clay soil. **Answer: any two of — ጀበና (jebena), ድስት (dist), insira, gan, mitad.**

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** Who is a hand-made gift card for? **Answer: for giving to someone — a gift made by your own hands.**

**6. (Sub lesson 6)** Name one material you can shape an Amharic letter from besides clay. **Answer: sticks, stones, string or cut paper (any one).**
