# Making with Clay and Paper — true or false

Say if each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 4)** The houses of your own area can be designed and shown as architecture work. **Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 5)** A New Year gift card should be bought printed, never made by hand. **Answer: False** — the activity is to make it yourself by colour-painting.

**3. (Sub lesson 6)** Shaping letters from materials helps you learn the letters' shapes. **Answer: True.**
