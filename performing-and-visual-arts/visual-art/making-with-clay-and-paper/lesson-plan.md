# Lesson plan — Making with Clay and Paper

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Visual Art · **Lesson:** Making with Clay and Paper
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 2 — Visual Art (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** making real things with your hands — modelling clay from plan to fired, decorated work, and building pictures from cut, folded, pasted paper.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Crafts Around Us — the learner has already met the potter's and weaver's products; now their own hands do the making. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
1. **Clay modelling** — plan, knead, roll and shape; tools, the ready-clay test, and a figure on a wire frame.
2. **Drying, firing and decorating clay** — the finishing chain: shade-dry, smooth, dung-and-chaff fire, plant colours and gum shine.
3. **Paper pictures: cut, fold and paste** — pictures built from paper shapes alone.
4. **Houses and pots of our area** — architecture (ኪነ ህንፃ) and pottery in imitation, from the learner's own surroundings.
5. **A New Year gift card** — a card made by colour-painting, to give away.
6. **Shaping letters from materials** — Amharic letters built from clay, sticks, stones and string.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Crafts Around Us — the learner knows the dist and jebena as products before making clay work. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Model a planned thing in clay, step by step, and test when the clay is ready.
2. Carry a clay work through drying, firing and decorating in the right order.
3. Make a picture by cutting, folding and pasting paper.
4. Design a house of their area and imitate a known pot in clay.
5. Make and paint a New Year gift card.
6. Shape Amharic letters from found materials.

## What you need
Clay or soft earth, water, a clean flat surface, paper, scissors, paste or glue, colours, and found materials (sticks, stones, string).

## If the learner is stuck
- Clay cracks while working → it is too dry, or it dried in wind: knead in a little water, work in shade, and cover a finished piece with plastic.
- Clay sticks to everything → it is too wet: knead it longer; ready clay does not stick to the hands.
- The paper picture falls apart → paste the biggest shapes first, press each piece flat, and let it dry before adding the next.
