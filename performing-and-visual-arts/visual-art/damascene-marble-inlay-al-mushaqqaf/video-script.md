**"Art and Human Heritage: Al-Mushaqqaf".** how Damascenes decorated their houses from inside — marble in colours and varied ornaments in a single material clad the whole house, around the courtyard fountain (al-bahra) — this is al-Mushaqqaf; designing a bahra and decorating it with fitting geometric ornaments.

That is Damascene Marble Inlay: Al-Mushaqqaf — Syria's way, kept by name.
