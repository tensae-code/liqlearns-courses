# Lesson plan — Damascene Marble Inlay: Al-Mushaqqaf

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Visual Art · **Lesson:** Damascene Marble Inlay: Al-Mushaqqaf
**Place in the curriculum:** Visual Art (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Syria's al-mushaqqaf — Damascene marble inlay work, a heritage decorative art of patterned stone.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the course's earlier lessons in its title; recommended first, never forced.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of ..." in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **"Art and Human Heritage: Al-Mushaqqaf"** — how Damascenes decorated their houses from inside — marble in colours and varied ornaments in a single material clad the whole house, around the courtyard fountain (al-bahra) — this is al-Mushaqqaf; designing a bahra and decorating it with fitting geometric ornaments
