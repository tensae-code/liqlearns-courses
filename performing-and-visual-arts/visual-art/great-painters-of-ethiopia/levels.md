# Great Painters of Ethiopia — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure — README, "Units, Lessons and Sub Lessons"). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number, as before. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. The sub lessons climb inside this one lesson, from the youngest books to the oldest.

## Sub lesson 1 — Belachew Yimer
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: say where Belachew Yimer was born and name two kinds of his works.

## Sub lesson 2 — Laureate Afewerk Tekle
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: say Afewerk Tekle's title, birthplace and the three works the book names.

## Sub lesson 3 — More master painters (teacher guide — evidence)
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: name three of these painters and one gift each brought to Ethiopian art.

## Sub lesson 4 — Ethiopian manuscripts (teacher guide — evidence)
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: say how a codex is put together: folded leaves, gatherings, sewing, cover.

## Sub lesson 5 — The roll of great painters (Grade 7)
Pass mark: recite the roll's names, including Agegnehu Engida, Shanbel Lema Guya and Eskinder Bogossian.

## Sub lesson 6 — Agegnehu Engida (1905–1950 E.C.)
Pass mark: tell Agegnehu Engida's marks: Gondar birth, Paris study, the birr-note designs (the only painter on a birr note), Holy Trinity Cathedral painting, 'fully Ethiopian' modern work.

## Sub lesson 7 — Shanbel Lema Guya (1921–2013 E.C.)
Pass mark: tell Shanbel Lema Guya's road — clay with his mother, the wooden aeroplane and the Air Force, Asmara study, naturalistic paintings of the peoples' dress and life — and quote his saying.

## Sub lesson 8 — Afewerk's London road; Gebre Kristos the poet-painter (Grade 7)
Pass mark: tell Afewerk's London road (chosen for engineering, the emperor's advice, art school, the regions studied, the declined minister post) and Gebre Kristos's marks (Harar, abstract manner, the 1958 E.C. award, twelve years teaching, exile and death in Oklahoma).
