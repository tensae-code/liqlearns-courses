# Lesson plan — Great Painters of Ethiopia

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Visual Art · **Lesson:** Great Painters of Ethiopia
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 2 — Visual Art (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the artists Ethiopia honours — Belachew Yimer of Debre Markos, Laureate Afewerk Tekle, and the master painters and manuscripts the Grade 7 guide adds — met by their birthplaces, their works and what each one gave the country's art.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Cave Art and Church Painting — the learner knows the old painting tradition; this lesson meets the named artists who carried it into modern times.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved (the gate: README, "Units, Lessons and Sub Lessons (ruled 2026-11-08)"):

1. **Belachew Yimer** — born in Debre Markos, Gojjam; traditional-style paintings including "The Wedding of the Mouse and the Cat" and farming works (Grade 4, printed p. 70).
2. **Laureate Afewerk Tekle** — the honoured World Laureate Master Artist, born in Ankober, Shewa, in 1927 E.C.; "From Non-cooperation to Unity", "African Union" and "Mother Ethiopia" (Grade 4, printed p. 71).
3. **More master painters (teacher guide — evidence)** — Afewerk Tekle's stained glass, Alle Felege Selam and the first art school, Gebre Kristos Desta and abstract art, Zerihun Yetmgeta, Mezgebu Tesema, Tadesse Mesfin (Grade 7 teacher guide, printed pp. 100–106).
4. **Ethiopian manuscripts (teacher guide — evidence)** — Ethiopia's Ge'ez written tradition; the codex — folded parchment leaves in gatherings, sewn and covered (Grade 7 teacher guide, printed pp. 106–107).

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Cave Art and Church Painting. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Tell who Belachew Yimer was and name his best-known traditional-style works.
2. Tell who Afewerk Tekle was, when and where he was born, and name three of his works.
3. Name the master painters the Grade 7 guide adds and one gift each brought.
4. Say how an Ethiopian manuscript book (a codex) is made.

## What you need
Paper and colours, and a wall or board where the class's own 'artists we admire' can be pinned.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — Think of the best drawing or painting you have ever seen in real life. Who made it? If you do not know their name, that is normal — in church painting the painters' names rarely appear. Today you learn names worth knowing. (lesson/lesson.md).
