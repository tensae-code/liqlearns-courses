# Great Painters of Ethiopia — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Belachew Yimer was born in…
  a) Debre Markos, Gojjam b) Ankober, Shewa c) Harar
**Answer: a) Debre Markos, Gojjam** — the book gives Debre Markos, Gojjam.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What title does the book give Afewerk Tekle?
  a) World Laureate Master Artist b) Court musician c) Master potter
**Answer: a) World Laureate Master Artist** — the book honours him as World Laureate Master Artist.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Who founded the Addis Ababa School of Art in 1957?
  a) Alle Felege Selam Heruy b) Belachew Yimer c) Saint Yared
**Answer: a) Alle Felege Selam Heruy** — Alle Felege Selam founded it — the first formal art school in Ethiopia.

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** A codex (mäshaf) is made of…
  a) Folded parchment leaves, sewn and covered b) Printed paper from a factory c) Carved stone tablets only
**Answer: a) Folded parchment leaves, sewn and covered** — folded parchment leaves in gatherings, sewn, covered.

**5.** (Sub lesson 5) Which name belongs on the book's roll of great painters?
a) Eskinder Bogossian  b) Solomon Wagaye  c) Elisabet W/Giorgis
**Answer: a) Eskinder Bogossian.**

**6.** (Sub lesson 6) Agegnehu Engida is the only Ethiopian painter whose work appears on —
a) a postage stamp  b) a birr note  c) a church door
**Answer: b) a birr note.**

**7.** (Sub lesson 6) Agegnehu Engida studied painting for about eight years in —
a) London  b) Paris  c) Rome
**Answer: b) Paris.**

**8.** (Sub lesson 7) Shanbel Lema Guya's turning point was —
a) a clay pot  b) a wooden aeroplane model shown to the emperor  c) a portrait of his mother
**Answer: b) a wooden aeroplane model shown to the emperor.**

**9.** (Sub lesson 7) Shanbel's famous saying is —
a) 'Art is long'  b) 'This is my country; this is Ethiopia'  c) 'Paint what you see'
**Answer: b) 'This is my country; this is Ethiopia.'**

**10.** (Sub lesson 8) Afewerk Tekle was first sent to London to study —
a) engineering  b) medicine  c) law
**Answer: a) engineering** — his teachers then recognised the art gift.

**11.** (Sub lesson 8) Gebre Kristos Desta widely introduced which manner in Ethiopia?
a) cave painting  b) the abstract (ረቂቅ) modern manner  c) stencil printing
**Answer: b) the abstract (ረቂቅ) modern manner.**
