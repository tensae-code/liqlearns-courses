# Great Painters of Ethiopia — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** "The Wedding of the Mouse and the Cat" is one of Belachew Yimer's traditional-style paintings.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** "Mother Ethiopia" is one of Afewerk Tekle's works.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Gebre Kristos Desta brought abstract expressionism to Ethiopia.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Manuscript sizes run from pocket books to volumes over 45 cm.
**Answer: True**

**5.** (Sub lesson 5) Shanbel Lema Guya's name is on the Grade 7 roll. **Answer: True.**

**6.** (Sub lesson 8) Afewerk accepted the minister post offered on his return. **Answer: False** — he declined it to focus on visual art.
