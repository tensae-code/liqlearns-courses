# Great Painters of Ethiopia — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What subjects do his farming works show?
**Answer: Farming life, painted in the traditional style.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Where was Afewerk Tekle born, and in what year as the book gives it?
**Answer: Ankober, Shewa, in 1927 E.C.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** How big is Afewerk Tekle's Africa Hall stained glass, as the guide gives it?
**Answer: 150 square metres.**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** What is branna?
**Answer: Parchment — the material of the manuscript leaves.**
