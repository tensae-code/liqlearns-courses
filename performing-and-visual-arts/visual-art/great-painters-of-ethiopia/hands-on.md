# Hands-on — Great Painters of Ethiopia

The lesson with your hands, your feet and your voice together, not only your eyes.

## An exhibition of two
Pin up two large sheets: one titled with Belachew Yimer's name, one with Afewerk Tekle's. Under each, the class pins drawings 'in the manner of' that painter — traditional farming scenes under the first, bold mother-and-country pictures under the second.

## Make a codex cover
Fold sheets in gatherings, sew them through the fold with thread, and cover them with card — the manuscript maker's first steps, in paper.
