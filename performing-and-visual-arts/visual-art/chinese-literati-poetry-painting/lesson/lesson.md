# Chinese Literati Poetry-Painting

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about chinese literati poetry-painting. Then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — China Grade 5 Art Vol.1

Painting a poem — literati painting joins poetry and image: understand the poem's feeling, then paint the scene it carries (pupils illustrate Li Bai, Cao Zhi's Seven-Step Poem, Jia Dao and others)

## Sub lesson 2 — China Grade 6 Art Vol.1

Poem, calligraphy, painting and seal fused in one work — Zheng Banqiao's orchid-and-rock and Shitao's poem-painting album show how the four arts widen a picture's world

## Sub lesson 3 — China HS Chinese Painting & Calligraphy

The four-in-one work — poem, calligraphy, painting and seal composed together (a union Western painting does not have): Wang Mian's ink plum with his own poem of integrity, Zheng Banqiao hearing the people's suffering in bamboo, Li Keran's long prose inscription on his ox; the literati-painting tradition from Wang Wei through Su Shi and Zhao Mengfu; where inscriptions and seals sit so they complete the composition
