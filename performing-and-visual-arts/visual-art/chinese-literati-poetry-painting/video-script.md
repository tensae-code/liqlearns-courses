# Video script — Chinese Literati Poetry-Painting

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is Chinese Literati Poetry-Painting.

The literati unity of poetry and painting — poems inside pictures and pictures inside poems in Chinese art. — as China's school books teach it: Painting a poem — literati painting joins poetry and image: understand the poem's feeling, then paint the scene it carries (pupils illustrate Li Bai, Cao Zhi's Seven-Step Poem, Jia Dao and others).

Watch, listen — then say it back in your own words.
