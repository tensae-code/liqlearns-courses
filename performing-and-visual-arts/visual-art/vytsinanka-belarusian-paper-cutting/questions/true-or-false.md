# Vytsinanka: Belarusian Paper Cutting — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A vytsinanka border looks lace-like because its shapes repeat.
**Answer: True**.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Vytsinanka is cut from clay.
**Answer: False** — it is cut from folded paper.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** You must draw every repeated shape separately by hand.
**Answer: False** — the fold repeats the cut for you.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Trees and leaves can be vytsinanka motifs.
**Answer: True**.
