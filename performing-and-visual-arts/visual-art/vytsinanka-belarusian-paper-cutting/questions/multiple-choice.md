# Vytsinanka: Belarusian Paper Cutting — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Vytsinanka is the art of…
a) cutting folded paper into patterns  b) painting on glass  c) weaving straw
**Answer: a) cutting folded paper into patterns** — cutting folded paper into repeating lace-like patterns.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The first step in making a vytsinanka is to…
a) paint the paper  b) fold the paper  c) glue the paper
**Answer: b) fold the paper** — the fold makes the cuts repeat.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The Grade 1 book's vytsinanka patterns are built from…
a) letters and numbers  b) trees, arches and leaves  c) circles only
**Answer: b) trees, arches and leaves** — the book builds its patterns from trees, arches and leaves.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** When you open a folded, cut paper, the cuts…
a) disappear  b) repeat into a border or pattern  c) turn into a drawing
**Answer: b) repeat into a border or pattern** — repeat — one cut becomes many matching shapes.

**5. (Sub lesson 1)** Vytsinanka belongs to…
a) Belarus  b) Japan  c) Mexico
**Answer: a) Belarus** — it is Belarus's own paper-cutting art.
