# Vytsinanka: Belarusian Paper Cutting — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Tell the three steps of making a vytsinanka.
**Answer: Fold the paper, cut it, open it to reveal the repeating pattern.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Why does one cut appear many times in the finished piece?
**Answer: Because the paper was folded — the cut passes through every layer at once.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Name three shapes the book builds vytsinanka patterns from.
**Answer: Trees, arches and leaves.**
