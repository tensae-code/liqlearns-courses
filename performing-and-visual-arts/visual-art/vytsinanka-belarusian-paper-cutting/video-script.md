# Video script — Vytsinanka: Belarusian Paper Cutting

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Fold the paper. Cut the paper. Open the paper — lace!

That is **vytsinanka**, Belarus's paper-cutting art.

One cut, folded — and trees, arches and leaves repeat down the border like lace.

Fold. Cut. Open. Yours.
