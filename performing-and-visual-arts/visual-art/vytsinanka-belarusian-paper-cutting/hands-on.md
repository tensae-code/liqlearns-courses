# Hands-on — Vytsinanka: Belarusian Paper Cutting

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Fold, cut, open
Fold a strip of paper in halves, then in halves again. Cut small notches, leaf shapes and an arch along the folds. Open it: a lace-like border. Try a second strip with tree shapes.

## A vytsinanka card
Glue your border onto a dark card so the white lace stands out, the way the book's examples are shown.
