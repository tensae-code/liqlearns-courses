## Sub lesson 1 — Cutting vytsinanka

**Vytsinanka** is Belarus's own art of cutting paper. You **fold** the paper, then **cut** it — and when you open it, the cuts repeat into a **lace-like border or pattern**.

The Grade 1 book teaches it with worked examples: repeating borders, and patterns built from **trees, arches and leaves**. Because the paper was folded, one cut becomes many matching shapes — that is how the lace is made.
