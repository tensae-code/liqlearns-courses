# Video script — Siapo: Samoan Tapa Cloth

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Cloth made from bark, painted with Samoa's own designs — that is **siapo**, Samoa's tapa.

How is it made? What is it made from? And look closely at the patterns — their symbols come from **nature** itself!

Draw the patterns — straight lines, curved lines. Then make your own painting, from siapo and from legend.

That is siapo.
