# Siapo: Samoan Tapa Cloth — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name two of the four things pupils analyse in making Samoan tapa.
**Answer: Techniques, materials, design elements and processes (any two).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Where do tapa symbols come from?
**Answer: From natural sources that inspire the designs.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the learner design at the end of the siapo study?
**Answer: A painting or print based on Samoan siapo and legend.**
