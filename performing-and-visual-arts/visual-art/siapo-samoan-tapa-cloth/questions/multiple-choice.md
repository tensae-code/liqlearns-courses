# Siapo: Samoan Tapa Cloth — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Siapo is…
a) Samoa's tapa cloth  b) a kind of drum  c) a dance
**Answer: a) Samoa's tapa cloth.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Pupils analyse siapo's techniques, materials, design elements and…
a) prices  b) processes  c) owners
**Answer: b) processes.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Tapa symbols come from…
a) machines  b) natural sources that inspire the designs  c) foreign catalogues
**Answer: b) natural sources that inspire the designs.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Traditional Samoan patterns are drawn with…
a) linear and curved lines  b) numbers only  c) letters only
**Answer: a) linear and curved lines.**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** The learner's own design work is a painting or print based on…
a) siapo and legend  b) a photograph  c) nothing
**Answer: a) siapo and legend.**
