# Siapo: Samoan Tapa Cloth — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Siapo is Samoan tapa cloth.
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Tapa designs have no connection to nature.
**Answer: False** — their symbols come from natural sources.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Traditional Samoan patterns use both linear and curved lines.
**Answer: True.**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Pupils only look at siapo; they never design from it.
**Answer: False** — they design a painting or print based on siapo and legend.
