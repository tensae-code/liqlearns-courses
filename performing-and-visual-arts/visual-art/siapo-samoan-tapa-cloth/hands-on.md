# Hands-on — Siapo: Samoan Tapa Cloth

## Lines from nature
Choose one natural thing — a leaf, a shell, a wave. Draw its shape twice: once built from straight (linear) lines only, once from curved lines only, the way Samoa's programme has pupils draw traditional Samoan patterns.

## A siapo panel of your own
On one sheet, arrange your two patterns into a bordered panel, as a siapo design. Under it, write the natural source of each symbol.
