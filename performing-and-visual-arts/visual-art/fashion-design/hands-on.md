# Hands-on — Fashion Design

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Pattern the cloth
Draw a square of cloth on paper and fill it with a repeating pattern of your own design — the same motif, again and again, the way a textile pattern runs across cloth.

## Design the dress
Draw one garment — a dress or a shirt — and show on it where your cloth pattern would fall. Say which part is textile design and which part is fashion design.
