# Video script — Fashion Design

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Look at a cloth. Its pattern did not happen by itself — someone **designed** it.

That is **textile design**: pattern, designed for cloth.

And the dress made from the cloth? Someone designed that too.

That is **fashion design**: dress, designed as art you can wear.
