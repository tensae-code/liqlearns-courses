# Fashion Design — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Textile design is designing pattern for cloth.
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A textile pattern's motif is designed to repeat across the cloth.
**Answer: True.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Fashion design treats dress as applied visual art.
**Answer: True.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Textile design and fashion design are exactly the same work.
**Answer: False** — one patterns the cloth; the other designs the dress.
