# Fashion Design — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Textile design is…
a) designing pattern for cloth  b) carving wood  c) taking photographs
**Answer: a) designing pattern for cloth.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A textile pattern runs across cloth by…
a) appearing once only  b) repeating its motif  c) hiding its colours
**Answer: b) repeating its motif.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Fashion design is…
a) designing dress as applied visual art  b) a kind of wall painting  c) singing in parts
**Answer: a) designing dress as applied visual art.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In fashion design, the garment's design covers…
a) only its price  b) its shape and lines, and how the patterned cloth is used in it  c) nothing — garments are not designed
**Answer: b) its shape and lines, and how the patterned cloth is used in it.**

**5. (Sub lesson 1)** Textile design and fashion design are…
a) the same thing  b) two different designs — pattern for cloth, and the dress designed from it  c) both kinds of music
**Answer: b) two different designs — pattern for cloth, and the dress designed from it.**
