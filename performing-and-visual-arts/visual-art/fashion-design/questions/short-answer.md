# Fashion Design — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does a textile designer design?
**Answer: Pattern for cloth.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does a fashion designer design?
**Answer: Dress — the garment, as applied visual art.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Name one thing a fashion design decides about a garment.
**Answer: Its shape (or its lines, or how the patterned cloth is used in it).**
