# Fashion Design

## Do it first

Look at the cloth of a shirt or dress near you. Two designs are hiding in it: the pattern ON the cloth, and the shape OF the garment. Two different designs — this lesson separates them and names both.

## Sub lesson 1 — Textile design

Albania's Grade 9 visual-art guide teaches **textile design** as its own topic: **designing pattern for cloth**. The motif, its repeat and its colours are designed first, so the cloth itself carries the design wherever it is used.

## Sub lesson 2 — Fashion design

Beside it stands **fashion design**: **designing dress as applied visual art**. The garment — its shape, its lines, and how the patterned cloth is used in it — is designed as a work of visual art that a person wears.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion: what is textile design, and what is fashion design? Companion checks: pattern designed for cloth? Dress designed as applied visual art?

## Do It Yourself

1. Draw a repeating pattern for a square of cloth.
2. Draw a garment that uses your patterned cloth, and label the textile design and the fashion design.
3. Write one sentence: why does the cloth's design come before the dress is made?
