# Hands-on — Painting with Blown, Poured and Pressed Colour

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## String-paint an aurora
Dip a string in thin green acrylic and lay it waving across black paper; lift it away. Repeat in red or blue. Spatter white stars with a brush, and write the O2 and N2 signs on your finished aurora.
