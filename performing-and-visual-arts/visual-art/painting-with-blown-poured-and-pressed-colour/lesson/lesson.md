# Painting with Blown, Poured and Pressed Colour

## Do it first

The sky over Sápmi is dancing green and red. No brush can paint that dance — but a string can. Dip it, lay it waving, lift it: the aurora stays on the paper.

## Sub lesson 1 — String painting — the aurora

The Kahdeksan unit teaches **string painting (lankamaalaus)** step by step:

1. Use **thin green, red and blue acrylic paint**.
2. **Dip a string in the paint.**
3. **Lay it waving across black paper and lift it away** — it leaves an **aurora**.
4. Make **at least two colours** — the **oxygen and nitrogen colours** of the real aurora.
5. **Spatter white stars** with a brush.
6. **Add the O2 and N2 signs**, so the picture itself records what auroras are.

The colour is moved without a brush — pulled by the string — and the finished picture teaches the science it pictures. The unit's fox origami is glued onto this dried string-painted aurora picture (stocked in the Origami lesson).
