# Painting with Blown, Poured and Pressed Colour — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In string painting, the paint is carried by…
a) a brush  b) a string dipped in paint  c) a sponge only
**Answer: b) a string dipped in paint**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The string is laid on the paper…
a) straight and still  b) waving, then lifted away  c) in a ball
**Answer: b) waving, then lifted away**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The picture is painted on…
a) white paper  b) black paper  c) cloth
**Answer: b) black paper**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** The two aurora colours stand for…
a) oxygen and nitrogen  b) water and air  c) day and night
**Answer: a) oxygen and nitrogen**
