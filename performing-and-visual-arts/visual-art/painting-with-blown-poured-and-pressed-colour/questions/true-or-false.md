# Painting with Blown, Poured and Pressed Colour — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** String painting moves colour without a brush doing the painting.
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Only one colour of aurora is made.
**Answer: False** — at least two colours are made, the oxygen and nitrogen colours.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The aurora is painted on black paper.
**Answer: True.**
