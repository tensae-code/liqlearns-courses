# Photography — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A photograph is made when —
a) light passes through a camera lens, forms an image on film, and is printed on prepared papers  b) a painter copies a scene  c) a story is told twice
**Answer: a)** — that is the book's account of the photographic art.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A vertical (ቋሚ) photograph —
a) lies wider than it is tall  b) stands taller than it is wide  c) is always black and white
**Answer: b) stands taller than it is wide.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The message photograph of children reading together shows —
a) a rare animal  b) reading together, passing the message of learning shared  c) a historic place
**Answer: b)** — its pair, Figure 33, shows equality of work.

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Video passes a message by combining —
a) smell and taste  b) sound and moving image  c) paper and pencil
**Answer: b) sound and moving image.**
