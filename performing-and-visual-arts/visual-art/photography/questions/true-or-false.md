# Photography — true or false

Say if each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Choosing the colour behind your subjects is part of the guidance for taking a photograph. **Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** A photograph cannot pass a message, because it has no words. **Answer: False** — photographs pass history, culture, ways of life and more; the reading-together and equal-work photographs pass theirs without words.
