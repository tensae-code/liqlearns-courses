# Photography — short answer

Answer in a word or a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What must you take care about the set frame when taking a photograph? **Answer: that the image does not go out of the set frame.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What two names do the teacher guides give the two arrangements? **Answer: portrait and landscape photography (ቁምና ዝርግ ፎቶግራፍ).**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name two cultures-of-life things photographs can show. **Answer: dress culture and food culture (also: ways of life, culture itself).**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** After watching a video, what two things does the activity ask you to produce? **Answer: the message you understood, presented in writing; and a drawing of the message you saw.**
