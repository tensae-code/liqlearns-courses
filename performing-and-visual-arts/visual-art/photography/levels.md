# Photography — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number, as before. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. The sub lessons climb from the camera's own working, through the frame's two shapes, to photographs as message-carriers, and finally to video — the moving message.

## Sub lesson 1 — Simple ways of taking a photograph
Pass mark: say how a photograph is made (light through a lens, image on film, printed on prepared papers) and give the five guidance points in order.

## Sub lesson 2 — Vertical and horizontal pictures
Pass mark: tell vertical (ቋሚ) from horizontal (አግዳሚ) arrangement, name them portrait and landscape, and choose the frame by whether the subject stands or spreads.

## Sub lesson 3 — Passing a message by photograph
Pass mark: list at least four of the book's benefits of photographs to a community/country, and tell the message of the reading-together and equal-work photographs.

## Sub lesson 4 — Passing a message by video
Pass mark: define video (a message combining sound and moving image), name what is presented by video, and give the two after-watching tasks — present the message in writing, and draw it.
