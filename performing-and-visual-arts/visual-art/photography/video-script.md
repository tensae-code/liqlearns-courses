# Video script — Photography

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Hold the camera steady. Look through it.
Light comes in… the picture forms… and later, it is printed on paper.
That is how a photograph is born.

Before you press — five things!
**One:** decide what to photograph.
**Two:** choose the colour behind your subject.
**Three:** mind the light.
**Four:** keep everything inside the frame!
**Five:** standing, or wide?

**ቋሚ** — vertical, standing tall. For a person, a tower.
**አግዳሚ** — horizontal, lying wide. For a land, a market.
Turn the frame in your mind… then press.

And remember — a photograph **speaks**.
History. Culture. Dress. Food. Famous people. Rare animals.
Take a photograph, and you pass a message to people not yet born.

Now you — decide your message first. Then take the photograph that carries it.
