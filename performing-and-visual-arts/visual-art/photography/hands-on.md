# Hands-on — Photography

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The paper frame
Cut a rectangle window from a piece of card. Hold it up and "take photographs" by framing real scenes: a standing person in the tall frame (ቋሚ), a wide view in the lying frame (አግዳሚ). For each framing, say the five guidance points as you check them — decide, colour behind, light, inside the frame, arrangement.

## One subject, two frames
Choose one subject — a tree, a door, a friend (with their permission). Frame it both ways, tall and wide. If a camera is available, take both photographs. Compare: which frame serves the subject better, and why?

## The message photograph
Decide a message first — "we read together", "we share the work", "our place is beautiful". Arrange or find the scene that carries it, check the colour behind and the light, and take the photograph (or draw its frame-plan if no camera is at hand). Show it to someone and ask what message they understood — without telling them first.
