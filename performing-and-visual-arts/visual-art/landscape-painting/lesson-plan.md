# Lesson plan — Landscape Painting

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Visual Art · **Lesson:** Landscape Painting
**Place in the curriculum:** Visual Art (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** painting nature and the seasons as landscapes: autumn's carpet of leaves with near trees bigger and far trees smaller, leafless trees and winter in gouache, and spring's rainbow over green fields — Turkmenistan's season pictures.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this course.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Autumn: the carpet of leaves** — the ground lies like a carpet (haly) of fallen leaves; observe nature well; make near trees bigger and far ones a little smaller — a first step into depth.
2. **Winter: leafless trees and snow in gouache** — every tree has its own structure, some thin and tall, some spreading wide; draw leafless trees in coloured pencils; winter nature in gouache — white snow, fields and high mountains wrapped in white, every branch standing out.
3. **Spring: the rainbow** — after rain a rainbow (älemgoşar) appears in the sky, and green grass and beautiful flowers decorate the fields.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier Visual Art lessons of this course. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say how near and far trees are drawn.
2. Say how winter is painted and what medium carries it.
3. Say what appears after rain and what decorates the spring fields.
## What you need
Coloured pencils, gouache, paper, and a window or a walk to observe the season first.
