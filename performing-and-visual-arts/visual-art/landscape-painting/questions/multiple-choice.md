# Landscape Painting — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In the autumn picture, the ground lies like…
a) a road  b) a carpet (haly) of fallen leaves  c) a river
**Answer: b) a carpet (haly) of fallen leaves**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Near trees are drawn… and far trees…
a) smaller; bigger  b) bigger; a little smaller  c) the same size
**Answer: b) bigger; a little smaller**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Winter nature is drawn in…
a) gouache  b) ink only  c) chalk
**Answer: a) gouache**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** After rain, spring's sky shows…
a) snow  b) a rainbow (älemgoşar)  c) dust
**Answer: b) a rainbow (älemgoşar)**
