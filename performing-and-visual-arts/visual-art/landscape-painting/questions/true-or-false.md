# Landscape Painting — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Making far trees smaller gives a picture depth.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In the winter gouache, the branches disappear into the white.
**Answer: False** — every branch stands out against the white.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The rainbow appears before the rain.
**Answer: False** — it appears after rain.
