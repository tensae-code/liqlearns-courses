# Video script — Landscape Painting

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Three seasons, three pictures!

**Autumn** — a carpet of leaves. Near trees big, far trees small — depth!
**Winter** — bare branches, white gouache snow, mountains wrapped in white.
**Spring** — rain passes… a **rainbow**! Green grass, flowers everywhere.
