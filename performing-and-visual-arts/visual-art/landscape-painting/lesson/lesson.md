# Landscape Painting

## Do it first

Say what you already know about this lesson's subject — one thing you have seen, heard or done — before you read on.

## Sub lesson 1 — Autumn: the carpet of leaves

**"Güýzüň keşbi" — the image of autumn.** The ground lies **like a carpet (haly) of fallen leaves**.

**Observe the nature around you well** first. Then, in the drawing, **make the near trees bigger and the far ones a little smaller** — that size change is a first step into depth: the picture gains distance.

## Sub lesson 2 — Winter: leafless trees and snow in gouache

**"Ýapraksyz agaçlar" — leafless trees.** Every tree has **its own structure**: some thin and tall, some spreading their branches wide. Draw them in **coloured pencils**, branches bare.

Then **winter in gouache**: **white snow, fields and high mountains wrapped in white**, and **every branch of the trees standing out** against the white.

## Sub lesson 3 — Spring: the rainbow

**"Ýaz paslynyň gözel keşbi" — the beautiful image of spring.**

**After rain, a rainbow (älemgoşar) appears in the sky.** Below it, **green grass and beautiful flowers decorate the fields** — spring's landscape is sky, rainbow, grass and flowers together.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson taught, part by part. Companion checks each part against the sub lessons above.

## Do It Yourself

1. Make your own piece using this lesson's way, from start to finish.
2. Show it to the class and say what you made, how, and what you learnt.
3. Write one sentence: what will you do differently next time?
