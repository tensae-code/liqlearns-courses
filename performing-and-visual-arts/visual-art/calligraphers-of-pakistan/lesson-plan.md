# Lesson plan — Calligraphers of Pakistan

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Visual Art · **Lesson:** Calligraphers of Pakistan
**Place in the curriculum:** Visual Art (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Pakistan's master calligraphers, named in the country's own Class 9 book: Sadequain, Shakir Ali, Khurshid Alam Gohar Qalam and Gulgee.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this course.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The master calligraphists by name** — Sadequain; Shakir Ali; Khurshid Alam Gohar Qalam; Gulgee
2. **Sadequain and Shakir Ali — masters in two arts** — both names also stand among the master painters of Pakistan
3. **Gohar Qalam and Gulgee** — the list completed — four calligraphers in all

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier Visual Art lessons of this course. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name all four master calligraphers of Pakistan from the book's list.
2. Name the two who are also master painters.
3. Tell where the list stands: its own chapter, after the painting chapters.
