# Calligraphers of Pakistan

## Do it first

Quick game: say what you already know about this lesson's subject — one thing, out loud. This lesson names it properly.

## Sub lesson 1 — The master calligraphists by name

After its painting chapters, Pakistan's Class 9 book gives **calligraphy its own chapter of masters**:

- **Sadequain**
- **Shakir Ali**
- **Khurshid Alam Gohar Qalam**
- **Gulgee**

## Sub lesson 2 — Sadequain and Shakir Ali — masters in two arts

**Sadequain** and **Shakir Ali** stand in both of the book's master lists: among the **master painters of Pakistan** (Chapter 6.3) and here among the **master calligraphists** (Chapter 7).

## Sub lesson 3 — Gohar Qalam and Gulgee

**Khurshid Alam Gohar Qalam** and **Gulgee** complete the list — four master calligraphers of Pakistan, named in the country's own book.
