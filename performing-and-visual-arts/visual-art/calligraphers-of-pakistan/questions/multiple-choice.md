# Calligraphers of Pakistan — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How many master calligraphers does the book name?
a) four  b) seven  c) two
**Answer: a) four**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which of these is a master calligrapher of Pakistan?
a) Gulgee  b) Allah Baksh  c) Anna Molka Ahmad
**Answer: a) Gulgee**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The two masters named in both the painters' and calligraphers' lists are…
a) Sadequain and Shakir Ali  b) Gulgee and Gohar Qalam  c) Chughtai and Allah Baksh
**Answer: a) Sadequain and Shakir Ali**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Khurshid Alam's full name in the list is…
a) Khurshid Alam Gohar Qalam  b) Khurshid Alam Ahmad  c) Gohar Qalam Gulgee
**Answer: a) Khurshid Alam Gohar Qalam**
