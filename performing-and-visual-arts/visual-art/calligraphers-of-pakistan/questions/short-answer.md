# Calligraphers of Pakistan — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name all four master calligraphers.
**Answer: Sadequain, Shakir Ali, Khurshid Alam Gohar Qalam and Gulgee.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which two calligraphers are also named among the master painters?
**Answer: Sadequain and Shakir Ali.**
