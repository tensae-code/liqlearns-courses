# Calligraphers of Pakistan — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Calligraphy has its own chapter of masters in the Class 9 book.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Gulgee is named among the master painters.
**Answer: False** — Gulgee is named among the master calligraphers.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Anna Molka Ahmad is one of the four calligraphers.
**Answer: False** — she is named among the master painters, not here.
