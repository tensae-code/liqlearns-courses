# History of Photography

## Do it first

Name one thing that belongs in this lesson — The history of photography — its chronology and development, photographic technique, and the decisive moment. Say what it is in your own words. If you can name one, you are already inside the lesson.

## Sub lesson 1 — Хронологија и развој

Хронологија и развој — the history of photography: the camera obscura (1545), Daguerre and Niépce and the daguerreotype, Talbot's negative that could multiply, and reportage in the illustrated press.

## Sub lesson 2 — Хронологија и развој

Хронологија и развој — Milton Manaki of Bitola shot the first film material in Macedonia at the start of the 20th century.

## Do It Yourself

1. Choose one named part of this lesson and find out one more true thing about it — from a person who knows it, or from the book your part came from.
2. Make or perform a small piece in the way of that part: a rhythm, a step, a drawing, a shaped piece of clay.
