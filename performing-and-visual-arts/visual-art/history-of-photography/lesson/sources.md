| Kazakhstan — Бейнелеу өнері 3-тарау. Фотография. Жеке объект (Visual Art ch. 3. Photography. A single object) — what it teaches: in this chapter you will get acquainted with photography's wonders — photography is both a science and an art; a photograph depicts an object with light (жарық); material is gathered by a special device; in science, production, information devices photography is widely used; a photograph is connected with a person's whole life, from a person's birth a photo album gathers and leaves a trace in history; in this chapter you will get acquainted with photography's types and famous photographers' lives and works; today photography is not unfamiliar to anyone — mobile phones' possibilities have reached taking photos; the last-model phones take their own photos, even through Internet pages their system works; so in present days photography has entered our life widely — how to improve photo quality in connection with its wide spreading is important; you will get a certain skill in this chapter, through it you will form a direction and learn to take photos in your field; you will use the knowledge and skills taken in the «Портрет» (Portrait) chapter; photography appeared in the 19th century — but who invented it is difficult to say; the possibility of showing an object on a screen was known to people several centuries before; only at the achievement of physics and chemistry could an image be put on paper through light-sensitive (жарық сезгіш) chemical substances; the simplest camera — камера-обскура (camera obscura) — a dark room with a small hole letting light fall and an opposite wall where the image appears; a small camera (кішкентай камера) also works in the same way: light passing through a small hole, the camera's back wall's picture is formed; the photo apparatus's main work is so; in 1826 Joseph Niépce took the first photograph through a window with sunlight — this first photo taken on a tin plate was called «Вид из окна в Ле Гра» (View from the Window at Le Gras); Henri Cartier-Bresson — the famous French photographer of the mid-20th century; he is considered the founder of photojournalism (фотожурналистиканың негізін салушы) (student book; original target `visual-art/photography-history-and-the-decisive-moment`) | Book 2, printed p. 59 |
| Kazakhstan — Бейнелеу өнері 3-тарау (continued). Фототехника (Photographic technique) — what it teaches: sometimes a picture can be taken without a camera — such a photograph is called a фотограмма (photogram); for this a фотоқағаз (photo paper) is needed; any object placed on the photo paper casts a shadow; the fallen image's shadow depicts the object; such fallen shadows' picture is called a photogram; Man Ray used the photogram method and made abstract art works from his photograms; he called his works «рэйографтар» (rayographs); to make an abstract image the usual method can be used — for this varied household things and sculptures made of paper are used; such a composition is placed on light-sensitive (жарық сезгіш) material; a complex composition can be made by joining and re-gluing; think about the picture's last version carefully; the photographed object is at one distance from a defined point — that point clearly shows, and together with it the far point also goes into focus (фокусқа); this is called the object's sharpness depth (объектив тереңдігі — depth of field); photographers sometimes blur the object around on purpose to show the photo clearly — a clear, blurred picture's background is called боке (bokeh); this effect is often used in artistic depiction; it is used for showing models in advertising photographs; a photograph's quality is connected with light and shadow (жарық пен көлеңке); photographers define how light must fall at picture-taking and from which side, which side is light, which is much, which is less; if the light falls from above, texture and relief (бедер) are well seen; if the light falls from below, the shadow is strong and the texture's relief also looks gloomy; if the light falls from the side, texture and relief are seen clearly; do an experiment and discuss the result: to show light and shadow take several pictures in strong light; then try taking pictures so the shadow decreases; analyse the taken photographs together with classmates, pay attention to the successful moment and its cause; write the moments you keep in memory at picture-taking in the research album (student book; original target `visual-art/photography-history-and-the-decisive-moment`) | Book 2, printed p. 65 |
