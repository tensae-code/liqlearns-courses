**Хронологија и развој.** Хронологија и развој — the history of photography: the camera obscura (1545), Daguerre and Niépce and the daguerreotype, Talbot's negative that could multiply, and reportage in the illustrated press.

**Хронологија и развој.** Хронологија и развој — Milton Manaki of Bitola shot the first film material in Macedonia at the start of the 20th century.

That is History of Photography — North Macedonia's way, kept by name.
