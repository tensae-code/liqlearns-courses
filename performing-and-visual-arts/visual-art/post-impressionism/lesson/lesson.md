# Post-Impressionism

## Do it first

Name one thing that belongs in this lesson — Post-Impressionism — the painters who pushed past Impressionism toward structure, symbol and expression. Say what it is in your own words. If you can name one, you are already inside the lesson.

## Sub lesson 1 — Постимпресионизам

Постимпресионизам — two roads beyond Impressionism: Cézanne and Seurat toward form, structure and pointillism; Gauguin and Van Gogh toward emotion and spirit in expressive colour; Rousseau's clear-contoured fantasies; Toulouse-Lautrec's Moulin Rouge posters; Symbolism's inner world (Moreau, Redon, Munch's "The Scream") and Art Nouveau's organic decoration (Klimt's "The Kiss").

## Sub lesson 2 — Постимпресионизам

Постимпресионизам — Post-Impressionism: Cézanne building nature from sphere, cylinder and cone; Gauguin's flat, symbolic colour; Van Gogh's personal, swirling expression.
