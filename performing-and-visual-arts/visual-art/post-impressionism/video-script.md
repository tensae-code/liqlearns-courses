This is North Macedonia's own lesson.

**Постимпресионизам.** Постимпресионизам — two roads beyond Impressionism: Cézanne and Seurat toward form, structure and pointillism; Gauguin and Van Gogh toward emotion and spirit in expressive colour; Rousseau's clear-contoured fantasies; Toulouse-Lautrec's Moulin Rouge posters; Symbolism's inner world (Moreau, Redon, Munch's "The Scream") and Art Nouveau's organic decoration (Klimt's "The Kiss").

**Постимпресионизам.** Постимпресионизам — Post-Impressionism: Cézanne building nature from sphere, cylinder and cone; Gauguin's flat, symbolic colour; Van Gogh's personal, swirling expression.

That is Post-Impressionism — North Macedonia's way, kept by name.
