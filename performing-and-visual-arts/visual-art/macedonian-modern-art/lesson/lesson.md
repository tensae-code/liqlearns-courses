# Macedonian Modern Art

## Do it first

Name one thing that belongs in this lesson — North Macedonia's modern art — from its beginnings after icon and fresco painting to the generations after the Second World War. Say what it is in your own words. If you can name one, you are already inside the lesson.

## Sub lesson 1 — Модерната уметност во РС Македонија, beginnings

Модерната уметност во РС Македонија, beginnings: art bound to icon and fresco painting until the last zographs — Papradiški, Vangelović, Zografski — begin to paint space, volume and everyday scenes; the first academically trained moderns — Pandilov's poetic landscapes, Martinoski's emotional social scenes, Ličenoski's strong colour; Dimo Todorovski founding modern sculpture, Borka Avramova the first woman sculptor (exhibition 1954).

## Sub lesson 2 — Macedonian modern art after the Second World War

Macedonian modern art after the Second World War: art enters public space with architecture (Vladimirski, Kodžoman, Lazeski's murals); the 1950s–60s generation (Kondovski, Korubin, Mazev; sculptors Mitriќeski and Hadži Boškov); the Albanian painter Adem Kastrati's social and ethno-folklore motifs; the groups DENES and MUGRI; abstraction (Mazev, Kondovski) and Macedonian informel — rough matter and the strong gesture (Anastasov, Petlevski); figuration returning in the 1970s as symbol (Lulovski, Frangovski, Temkova, Omer Kaleši's portraits); Perčinkov's geometry tied to architecture; the postmodern new wave (Svetieva, Vangeli, Šumkovski, Pavleski, group Zero); Albanian artists Memeti, Bejtuli-Kica, Beќiri, Ameti.
