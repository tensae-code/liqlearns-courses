This is North Macedonia's own lesson.

**Модерната уметност во РС Македонија, beginnings.** Модерната уметност во РС Македонија, beginnings: art bound to icon and fresco painting until the last zographs — Papradiški, Vangelović, Zografski — begin to paint space, volume and everyday scenes; the first academically trained moderns — Pandilov's poetic landscapes, Martinoski's emotional social scenes, Ličenoski's strong colour; Dimo Todorovski founding modern sculpture, Borka Avramova the first woman sculptor (exhibition 1954).

**Macedonian modern art after the Second World War.** Macedonian modern art after the Second World War: art enters public space with architecture (Vladimirski, Kodžoman, Lazeski's murals); the 1950s–60s generation (Kondovski, Korubin, Mazev; sculptors Mitriќeski and Hadži Boškov); the Albanian painter Adem Kastrati's social and ethno-folklore motifs; the groups DENES and MUGRI; abstraction (Mazev, Kondovski) and Macedonian informel — rough matter and the strong gesture (Anastasov, Petlevski); figuration returning in the 1970s as symbol (Lulovski, Frangovski, Temkova, Omer Kaleši's portraits); Perčinkov's geometry tied to architecture; the postmodern new wave (Svetieva, Vangeli, Šumkovski, Pavleski, group Zero); Albanian artists Memeti, Bejtuli-Kica, Beќiri, Ameti.

That is Macedonian Modern Art — North Macedonia's way, kept by name.
