# Video script — Installation Art

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

**Instalaciones.** The installation — an idea that integrates drawing, music, painting, sculpture, graphics, architecture, video and body action in a real space, including streets and plazas, where the spectator can enter and take part.

**Ambientaciones.** Environments (ambientaciones) — transforming a whole place around one idea so all the senses carry the public into a different reality, as in Cruz-Diez's Chromosaturation chamber.

**Intervenciones.** Interventions — introducing material elements or actions that alter a given context, whether a conventional art space or an outdoor one.

That is Installation Art — Venezuela's own books teach it this way.
