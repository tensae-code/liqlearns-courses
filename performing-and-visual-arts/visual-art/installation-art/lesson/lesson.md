# Installation Art

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about this lesson's subject — installation art. Then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — Instalaciones

The installation — an idea that integrates drawing, music, painting, sculpture, graphics, architecture, video and body action in a real space, including streets and plazas, where the spectator can enter and take part.

## Sub lesson 2 — Ambientaciones

Environments (ambientaciones) — transforming a whole place around one idea so all the senses carry the public into a different reality, as in Cruz-Diez's Chromosaturation chamber.

## Sub lesson 3 — Intervenciones

Interventions — introducing material elements or actions that alter a given context, whether a conventional art space or an outdoor one.
