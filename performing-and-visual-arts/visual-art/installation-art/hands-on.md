# Hands-on — Installation Art

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Instalaciones
Work through this part with real materials or your own body, the way the book sets it: the installation — an idea that integrates drawing, music, painting, sculpture, graphics, architecture, video and body action in a real space, including streets and plazas, where the spectator can enter and take part. Show a companion what you made or did, and say what the part teaches.

## Ambientaciones
Work through this part with real materials or your own body, the way the book sets it: environments (ambientaciones) — transforming a whole place around one idea so all the senses carry the public into a different reality, as in Cruz-Diez's Chromosaturation chamber. Show a companion what you made or did, and say what the part teaches.

## Intervenciones
Work through this part with real materials or your own body, the way the book sets it: interventions — introducing material elements or actions that alter a given context, whether a conventional art space or an outdoor one. Show a companion what you made or did, and say what the part teaches.
