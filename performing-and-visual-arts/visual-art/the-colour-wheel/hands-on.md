# Hands-on — The Colour Wheel

The lesson with your hands, your feet and your voice together, not only your eyes.

## Build the wheel by mixing
Paint three big dots — red, yellow, blue — equally spaced on a circle. Mix neighbours pairwise and paint the secondary between them. Then mix each primary with its neighbouring secondary and paint the six tertiaries.

## Opposites hunt
Lay coloured papers or clothes in a circle like the wheel. Pick one, walk straight across the circle, and name the colour you reach.
