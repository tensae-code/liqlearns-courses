# The Colour Wheel — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A colour grows dull as it mixes with its opposite.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The colour wheel is divided into twelve parts.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Tertiary colours are made by mixing two secondary colours together.
**Answer: False**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Black and white are warm colours on the wheel.
**Answer: False**

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** A colour's opposite sits right beside it on the wheel.
**Answer: False**

**6.** (Sub lesson 6) Warm colours relate to fire. **Answer: True** — the Grade 6 book's colour character.
