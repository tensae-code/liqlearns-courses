# The Colour Wheel — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What three things are needed to see colour?
  a) Light, a reflecting surface, and the eye b) Paint, a brush, and paper c) Red, yellow, and blue
**Answer: a) Light, a reflecting surface, and the eye** — the book names light, a reflecting surface and the eye.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Red and yellow mixed in equal amounts make…
  a) Orange b) Green c) Violet
**Answer: a) Orange** — red + yellow = orange.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Blue + green, in equal amounts, makes…
  a) Blue-green b) Orange c) Magenta
**Answer: a) Blue-green** — a primary plus its neighbouring secondary makes the tertiary named from both.

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Which of these is a cool colour?
  a) Blue-green b) Red-orange c) Orange
**Answer: a) Blue-green** — blue-green sits in the cool half; the others are warm.

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** What is the opposite of blue on the wheel?
  a) Orange b) Green c) Red
**Answer: a) Orange** — the book's pairs put blue across from orange.
