# The Colour Wheel — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What are a colour's three properties?
**Answer: Its name, its purity (dullness), and its value.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which secondary colour sits between yellow and blue?
**Answer: Green.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the book's colour value chart show?
**Answer: One colour (blue) lightening as white is added and darkening as black is added.**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** What do black and white do in a drawing?
**Answer: They make shade and light — they are neutrals, neither warm nor cool.**

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** Name the opposite pairs of red and of yellow.
**Answer: Red–green and yellow–violet.**
