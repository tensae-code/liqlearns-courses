# Video script — The Colour Wheel

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Three colours make all the others: red, yellow, blue.
Mix two — a new colour is born between them.
Twelve colours stand in a circle.
Warm colours feel like sun and fire. Cool colours feel like water and air.
Across the circle sits the opposite. Red faces green.
