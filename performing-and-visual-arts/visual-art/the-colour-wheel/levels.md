# The Colour Wheel — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure — README, "Units, Lessons and Sub Lessons"). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number, as before. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. The sub lessons climb inside this one lesson, from the youngest books to the oldest.

## Sub lesson 1 — Colour basics: light, surface, eye
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: name the three things needed to see colour and the three properties of a colour.

## Sub lesson 2 — The wheel and the secondary colours
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: place the three primaries equally on a circle and mix the three secondaries between them.

## Sub lesson 3 — Tertiary colours and the colour value chart
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: recite the six tertiary formulas and make one tint row and one shade row of a single colour.

## Sub lesson 4 — Warm and cool colours, and the neutrals
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: sort the twelve wheel colours into their warm and cool halves and say where black and white stand.

## Sub lesson 5 — Opposite colours
Sub lesson 5 questions from the type files. Pass mark: find any colour's opposite by a line through the wheel's centre and name the three primary pairs.

## Sub lesson 6 — One colour, and neighbours: monochromatic and analogous (Grade 6)
Pass mark: define monochromatic (one colour in its tints and shades) and analogous (neighbouring wheel colours) and tell both from complementary opposites.
