# Beautiful Writing — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Calligraphy (ኪነ-ፅህፈት) is —
a) typing letters on a machine  b) the study of improving the shape and look of letters, writing them beautifully  c) spelling words correctly
**Answer: b)** — letters' shapes put down beautifully, decorated and adorned.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The line kind called ወጣ ገባ is —
a) curved  b) zigzag / uneven  c) horizontal
**Answer: b) zigzag / uneven.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Double writing (ድርብ) means the letter is —
a) written twice on the page  b) outlined — drawn as a shape with an inside and an outside  c) written very small
**Answer: b) outlined.**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** English capitals in single-line writing —
a) lean at different angles  b) sit evenly on the line, with equal side-thickness  c) never touch the line
**Answer: b) sit evenly on the line.**

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** The artist known for varied decorated letters beyond normal writing is —
a) Yigezu Bisrat  b) Afework Tekle  c) no artist is named
**Answer: a) Yigezu Bisrat** — his decorated letters are used in harag works.
