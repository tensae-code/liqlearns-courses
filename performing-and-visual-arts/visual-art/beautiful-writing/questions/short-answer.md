# Beautiful Writing — short answer

Answer in a word or a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name two places where beautiful writing earns its place. **Answer: any two of — graphics, commercial drawing, advertising, posters, display and title writing.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which two tools or ways are used to practise lines until length and size come out true? **Answer: freehand, and with a ruler.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What three things must you remember about Amharic letters when you practise them? **Answer: their own shape, size and writing order.**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** After writing English letters in double writing, what does the activity ask you to do to them? **Answer: colour them in an enjoyable colour.**

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** How can two writing tools make a double letter? **Answer: tie or double two writing tools together and write with them as one — the twin lines draw the outline.**
