# Beautiful Writing — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number, as before. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. The sub lessons climb from the idea of calligraphy, through its foundation (lines), to the two alphabets in single and double writing, and finally to decoration as art.

## Sub lesson 1 — Beautiful letter forms
Pass mark: define calligraphy (ኪነ-ፅህፈት) and name its methods — single, double, shade and light, ornament and pattern — and where it is used.

## Sub lesson 2 — Lines first
Pass mark: name and draw the line kinds — vertical (ቋሚ), horizontal (አግድም), diagonal (ሠያፍ), curved (ጎባጣ), zigzag (ወጣ ገባ), long and short — freehand and with a ruler.

## Sub lesson 3 — Amharic fidel, single and double
Pass mark: write fidel in single (ነጠላ) writing and in double (ድርብ, outlined) writing from a model, remembering each letter's shape, size and writing order.

## Sub lesson 4 — English letters, single and double
Pass mark: write capitals (even on the line, equal side-thickness) and small letters (rising heads, descending feet) in single and double writing; numerals have their own writing order.

## Sub lesson 5 — Shade, ornament and your name
Pass mark: produce one decorated letter (shade, ornament or leaves), a name in varied decorated letters, and ሰላም written large; name Yigezu Bisrat as the decorated-letters artist.
