# Lesson plan — Beautiful Writing

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Visual Art · **Lesson:** Beautiful Writing
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 2 — Visual Art (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** calligraphy (ኪነ-ፅህፈት) — writing letters beautifully: lines first, then Amharic fidel and English letters in single and double writing, up to shade, ornament and a decorated name.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the learner's ordinary handwriting — every learner arrives already able to write the letters; this lesson teaches them to beautify what they write. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
1. **Beautiful letter forms** — what calligraphy is, its methods, and where beautiful writing earns its place.
2. **Lines first** — the named line kinds, practised freehand and with a ruler.
3. **Amharic fidel, single and double** — ነጠላ and ድርብ, from the model, in varied line styles.
4. **English letters, single and double** — capitals even on the line, small letters' heads and feet, numerals too.
5. **Shade, ornament and your name** — block letters, light and shade, leaf decoration, Yigezu Bisrat, and the practice set ending in ሰላም written large.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the Amharic fidel lessons (Language and Literature) and Drawing's line work (Visual Art, Group B). Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Define calligraphy and name its methods: single, double, shade and light, ornament.
2. Name and draw the line kinds — ቋሚ, አግድም, ሠያፍ, ጎባጣ, ወጣ ገባ, long and short.
3. Write Amharic fidel in single and in double (outlined) writing.
4. Write English capitals and small letters single and double, and say the capital/small rules.
5. Decorate letters with shade and ornament, write their name beautifully, and write ሰላም large.

## What you need
Pencil, paper (a notebook for the practice set), a ruler, colours, and two pencils that can be tied together for the double-tools practice.

## If the learner is stuck
- Double letters wobble apart → write the letter single first, lightly, then walk the outline around it at one steady distance.
- Capitals lean unevenly → sit them on the line like chairs on a floor: even, with equal thickness on both sides.
- Shade looks like dirt → shade one side only, the same side on every letter, so the light seems to come from one place.
