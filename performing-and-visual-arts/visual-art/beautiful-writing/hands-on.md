# Hands-on — Beautiful Writing

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The line page
Fill one page with the line kinds: a row of vertical (ቋሚ), a row of horizontal (አግድም), diagonal (ሠያፍ), curved (ጎባጣ), zigzag (ወጣ ገባ), then long and short. Freehand first. Do the page again with a ruler where a ruler helps, until length and size come out true.

## Single, then double
Choose three Amharic fidel. Write each in single writing. Then write each in double writing — walk the outline around it — and colour the double letters in an enjoyable colour. Do the same for three English capitals.

## Two pencils, one letter
Tie two pencils together side by side (or hold two as one) and write a big letter: the twin lines draw a double letter by themselves. Try your first letter of your name this way.

## The name card
On a card, write your name in varied decorated letters — shade one side of every letter the same way, add leaves or ornaments in the spaces. Under it, write ሰላም large. Give the card to someone, or keep it over your desk.
