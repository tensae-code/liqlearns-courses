# Video script — Beautiful Writing

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Write your name. Fast! Messy.
Now write it again. Slow… careful… beautiful.
Same letters. New writing. That is **ኪነ-ፅህፈት** — calligraphy.

But first — lines!
**ቋሚ** — standing straight.
**አግድም** — lying flat.
**ሠያፍ** — slanting. **ጎባጣ** — curving. **ወጣ ገባ** — zigzag!
Every letter is hiding inside these lines.

Now a fidel. First **ነጠላ** — single, one clean line.
Now **ድርብ** — double! Walk around it. An outline. Fill it with colour!

English letters too. Capitals sit even, like chairs on a floor.
Small letters — heads rising, feet descending.

Shade one side. Add leaves. Add ornaments.
Now the best part… write **your name** like art.
And under it, big and peaceful: **ሰላም**.

Your turn. Slow pencil. Beautiful letters.
