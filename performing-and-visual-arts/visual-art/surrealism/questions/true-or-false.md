# Surrealism — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Surrealism turns to imagination and the dream.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Surrealism and Dada are enemies with nothing shared.
**Answer: False** — Surrealism filled the void Dada left.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Dalí's melting clocks project illusion on reality.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Surrealist dreams kept no known symbols.
**Answer: False** — The Surrealists' dreams kept known symbols — the book contrasts this with abstract art.
