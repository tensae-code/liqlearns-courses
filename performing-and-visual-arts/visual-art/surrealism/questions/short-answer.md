# Surrealism — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What did Surrealism declare war on?
**Answer: The laws and conventions that stifle thought — freedom from the mind's control and every moral and religious order.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What are Surrealism's two kinds of work?
**Answer: Unreal subjects and abstract forms.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Between which years did Surrealism spread to every art field?
**Answer: 1924–1939.**
