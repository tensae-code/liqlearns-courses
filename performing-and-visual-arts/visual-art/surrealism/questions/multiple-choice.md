# Surrealism — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Surrealism searched the void Dada left through…
a) science and intuition  b) armies  c) markets
**Answer: a) science and intuition** — searching phenomena through science and intuition.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The manifesto of Surrealism was…
a) Breton's  b) Marinetti's  c) Monet's
**Answer: a) Breton's** — Apollinaire gave the name; Breton wrote the manifesto.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Dalí projected illusion on reality — his famous image is…
a) melting clocks  b) a racing car  c) a blue horse
**Answer: a) melting clocks**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Max Ernst laid paper on wood and…
a) coloured the imprint through  b) burned it  c) framed it unpainted
**Answer: a) coloured the imprint through**
