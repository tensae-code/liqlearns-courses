## Book 1, Artistic Culture, "The Surrealist School"
Do the thing this part teaches, with real materials where the book names them: Surrealism — filling the void Dada left by searching phenomena through science and intuition: Apollinaire's name, Breton's manifesto, war declared on the laws and conventions that stifle thought, freedom from the mind's control and every moral and religious order, and the turn to imagination's ambiguity and the distant dream; Klee, Picasso and above all Dalí, projecting illusion on reality (his melting clocks), Max Ernst laying paper on wood and colouring the imprint through; its two kinds of work, unreal subjects and abstract forms, and its spread to every art field 1924–1939 beside the unconscious
