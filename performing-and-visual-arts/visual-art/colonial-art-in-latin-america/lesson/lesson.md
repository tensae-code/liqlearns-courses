## Sub lesson 1 — Arquitectura y escultura coloniales en América

Colonial art across Central and South America — coastal fortresses; churches of cruciform or rectangular plans loaded inside with gilded retablos, reliefs and images; wooden religious sculpture from the Quito and Lima schools, articulated, painted flesh-true, with glass eyes and natural hair, dressed in embroidered cloth for Holy Week processions.

## Sub lesson 2 — Pintura colonial en América

Colonial painting in America — local schools beside imported works; the privileged portrait, its sitters shown with authority and majesty in baroque half-light; religious canvases (Immaculate Conceptions, crucifixions, the Holy Family) on nebulous gold grounds, in tempera and oil — the Cuzco school among them.
