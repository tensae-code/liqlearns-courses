# Hands-on — Colonial Art in Latin America

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Arquitectura y escultura coloniales en América
Work through this part with real materials or your own body, the way the book sets it: colonial art across Central and South America — coastal fortresses; churches of cruciform or rectangular plans loaded inside with gilded retablos, reliefs and images; wooden religious sculpture from the Quito and Lima schools, articulated, painted flesh-true, with glass eyes and natural hair, dressed in embroidered cloth for Holy Week processions. Show a companion what you made or did, and say what the part teaches.

## Pintura colonial en América
Work through this part with real materials or your own body, the way the book sets it: colonial painting in America — local schools beside imported works; the privileged portrait, its sitters shown with authority and majesty in baroque half-light; religious canvases (Immaculate Conceptions, crucifixions, the Holy Family) on nebulous gold grounds, in tempera and oil — the Cuzco school among them. Show a companion what you made or did, and say what the part teaches.
