# Video script — Colonial Art in Latin America

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

**Arquitectura y escultura coloniales en América.** Colonial art across Central and South America — coastal fortresses; churches of cruciform or rectangular plans loaded inside with gilded retablos, reliefs and images; wooden religious sculpture from the Quito and Lima schools, articulated, painted flesh-true, with glass eyes and natural hair, dressed in embroidered cloth for Holy Week processions.

**Pintura colonial en América.** Colonial painting in America — local schools beside imported works; the privileged portrait, its sitters shown with authority and majesty in baroque half-light; religious canvases (Immaculate Conceptions, crucifixions, the Holy Family) on nebulous gold grounds, in tempera and oil — the Cuzco school among them.

That is Colonial Art in Latin America — Venezuela's own books teach it this way.
