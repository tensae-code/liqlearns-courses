# Samoan Tattoo Designs

## Do it first

Draw a small pattern of repeated marks. Now ask: what if every mark MEANT something — and everyone who saw it could read it? In Samoa, tattooing designs work exactly like that, and the school programme teaches them as design.

## Sub lesson 1 — Design principles in tattooing designs

Samoa's primary Expressive Arts programme has pupils **develop skills in demonstrating design principles through tattooing designs**: Samoa's tattooing is a school of design, where the principles are shown inside real, traditional designs. The classroom work is study and drawing — designs on paper, never tattooing on skin.

## Sub lesson 2 — Symbols, meanings and today's tattooing

Pupils **document tattooing symbols and their meanings** — the design is recorded together with what it carries — and **study contemporary Samoan tattooing and ritual attire**: the living art of today standing beside the customary attire it belongs with.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion: how are design principles learned in this lesson, and what do pupils document? Companion checks: demonstrated through tattooing designs? Symbols and their meanings?

## Do It Yourself

1. Write one sentence: what makes Samoan tattooing a study of design, not only decoration?
2. Draw one documented symbol on paper and write its documented meaning beside it.
3. Write one sentence on contemporary Samoan tattooing and ritual attire: what would you look for first?
