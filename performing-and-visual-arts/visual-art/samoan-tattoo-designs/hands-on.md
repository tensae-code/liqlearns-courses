# Hands-on — Samoan Tattoo Designs

## Document a symbol
Choose one documented tattooing symbol from the lesson's study (or a traditional pattern from your own community, with permission). Draw it large on paper and write beside it: its meaning, as documented. This is documentation work — paper only.

## Principles in the pattern
Under your drawing, name one design principle the pattern shows — repetition, balance or contrast — and point to where it shows it.
