# Lesson plan — Samoan Tattoo Designs

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Visual Art · **Lesson:** Samoan Tattoo Designs
**Place in the curriculum:** Visual Art (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Samoan tattooing as design — demonstrating design principles through tattooing designs, documenting tattooing symbols and their meanings, and studying contemporary Samoan tattooing and ritual attire.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of its title — recommended first, never forced.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Design principles in tattooing designs** — develop skills in demonstrating design principles through tattooing designs.
2. **Symbols, meanings and today's tattooing** — document tattooing symbols and their meanings, and study contemporary Samoan tattooing and ritual attire.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of the Visual Art title. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say how Samoan tattooing teaches design principles.
2. Say what pupils document about tattooing symbols, and what contemporary study completes the lesson.

## What you need
Paper and drawing tools — designs are studied and drawn on paper, never on skin.
