# Samoan Tattoo Designs — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In Samoa's programme, tattooing designs are used to demonstrate…
a) design principles  b) cooking methods  c) map reading
**Answer: a) design principles.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The classroom work on tattooing is…
a) tattooing on skin  b) study and drawing of designs  c) selling designs
**Answer: b) study and drawing of designs.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Pupils document tattooing symbols together with…
a) their meanings  b) their prices  c) their owners' addresses
**Answer: a) their meanings.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The present-day study in this lesson covers…
a) contemporary Samoan tattooing and ritual attire  b) European fashion only  c) nothing modern
**Answer: a) contemporary Samoan tattooing and ritual attire.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** A documented symbol is recorded with…
a) its design and what it carries — its meaning  b) a guess  c) no writing
**Answer: a) its design and what it carries — its meaning.**
