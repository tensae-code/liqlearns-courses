# Samoan Tattoo Designs — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Samoan tattooing is taught in the programme as a design art.
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Pupils practise tattooing on each other's skin in class.
**Answer: False** — the work is study and drawing on paper.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Tattooing symbols are documented without their meanings.
**Answer: False** — symbols and their meanings are documented together.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Contemporary Samoan tattooing is part of the study.
**Answer: True.**
