# Video script — Samoan Tattoo Designs

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Every line means something.

In Samoa, **tattooing is design**. Its symbols carry **meanings** — and pupils document them, one by one.

Learn the design principles inside the tattooing designs. Then look at Samoa's tattooing **today**, and the ritual attire it stands with.

That is Samoan tattoo design.
