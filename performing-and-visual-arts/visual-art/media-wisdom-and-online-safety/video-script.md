# Video script — Media Wisdom and Staying Safe Online

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Old media: newspaper, radio, television, the book.

New media: screens, social media, the internet.

Media wisdom: critical. Conscious. Creative. Active.

Beware: fake news, fake accounts, the hunger for likes.

And images speak — beeldtaal. They can inform… or mislead.

Be media-wise.
