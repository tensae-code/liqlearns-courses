# Media Wisdom and Staying Safe Online — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name three dangers of media named in the book.
**Answer: Any three of: fake news, fake accounts, addiction, mean messages spread at scale, everything filmed and posted, the hunger for likes.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which social media does the book name above all in Suriname?
**Answer: Facebook, WhatsApp and Instagram.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is beeldtaal?
**Answer: Image language: with moving images you can make a viewer feel and experience something — inform, seduce, move or mislead them.**
