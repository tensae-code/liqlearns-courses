# Media Wisdom and Staying Safe Online — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Mass media is when very many people are reached.
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Sharing your password widely is part of media wisdom.
**Answer: False — who may know your password is one of the debated dangers.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Moving images can mislead a viewer.
**Answer: True.**
