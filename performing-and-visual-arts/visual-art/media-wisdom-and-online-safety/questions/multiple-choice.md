# Media Wisdom and Staying Safe Online — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Old media are…
a) social media and apps  b) newspaper, radio, television and the printed book  c) only the internet
**Answer: b) newspaper, radio, television and the printed book**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Media wisdom means using media…
a) as much as possible  b) critically, consciously, creatively and actively  c) without thinking
**Answer: b) critically, consciously, creatively and actively**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Beeldtaal (image language) can make a viewer feel something in order to…
a) only inform  b) inform, seduce, move or mislead  c) do nothing
**Answer: b) inform, seduce, move or mislead**
