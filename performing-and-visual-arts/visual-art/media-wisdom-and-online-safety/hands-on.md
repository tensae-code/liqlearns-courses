# Hands-on — Media Wisdom and Staying Safe Online

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Debate the statements
Debate in class: a false age on your profile — allowed or not? Another's photo as your profile photo? Who may know your password? Defend your answer.

## Five sentences, one danger
In a group, write a five-sentence account of one internet activity — chatting, selfies, comments — meeting one danger — bullying, viruses, hackers — and how to solve or prevent it.

## Read the image language
Watch a short moving image. Say what it wants you to feel, and whether it informs, seduces, moves or misleads you. Say what told you.
