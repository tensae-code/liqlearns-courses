# Media Wisdom and Staying Safe Online

## Do it first

Quick start: from the title alone, guess what this lesson's work is. Then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — What media are — and what media wisdom is

**Media** come as **old media** — newspaper, radio, television, the printed book — and **new media** — screens, social media, internet — and as **mass media** when very many people are reached. In Suriname social media means above all **Facebook, WhatsApp and Instagram**. **Media wisdom** is using media **critically, consciously, creatively and actively**, knowing the dangers: **fake news and fake accounts, addiction, mean messages spread at scale, everything filmed and posted, the hunger for likes**. The first lesson debates statements — **a false age or another's photo on your profile, who may see your profile, filming and posting anything, who may know your password** — and sets groups to write five-sentence accounts of internet activities (chatting, apps, e-mail, selfies, comments, video) meeting their dangers (**blocking, viruses, bullying, privacy, hackers, quarrels**) and how to solve or prevent them.

## Sub lesson 2 — Image language — beeldtaal

In the deepening year media wisdom is more than technique — the **knowledge, skills and attitude** to move critically, consciously, creatively and actively in the media world. And **beeldtaal, image language**: with moving images you can make a viewer **feel and experience something** — **inform them, seduce them, move them, or mislead them** — which is why the year's one-minute film must visibly show what its makers learned about making film.
