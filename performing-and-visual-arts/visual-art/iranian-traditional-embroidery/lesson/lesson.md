# Iranian Traditional Embroidery

## Do it first

Before you read anything: try the first part below with your hands or your voice. What you manage — and where you stop — is exactly where this lesson starts.

## Sub lesson 1 — Needlework L1, Darvish embroidery

Iranian traditional embroidery — درویش دوزی: the Darvish needlework of Iran.

## Sub lesson 2 — Needlework L2, Zartoshti embroidery

زرتشتی دوزی: the Zoroastrian community's embroidery tradition.

## Sub lesson 3 — Needlework L3, Pateh embroidery

پته دوزی: pateh, the woollen embroidery of Kerman.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what each sub lesson taught, in order. Companion checks: did every part get its book example?

## Do It Yourself

1. Make or name one more example of your own for the first sub lesson.
2. Make or name one more example of your own for the last sub lesson.
3. Write one sentence: what is Iran's own way in this lesson?
