The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Today we learn **Iranian Traditional Embroidery**.

Step 1 — **Needlework L1, Darvish embroidery**. Iranian traditional embroidery — درویش دوزی: the Darvish needlework of Iran.

Step 2 — **Needlework L2, Zartoshti embroidery**. زرتشتی دوزی: the Zoroastrian community's embroidery tradition.

Step 3 — **Needlework L3, Pateh embroidery**. پته دوزی: pateh, the woollen embroidery of Kerman.

That is Iranian Traditional Embroidery — Iran's own way, step by step.
