# Lesson plan — Iranian Traditional Embroidery

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Visual Art · **Lesson:** Iranian Traditional Embroidery
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 2 — Visual Art (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Iran's traditional needlework — the Darvish embroidery, the Zartoshti (Zoroastrian community) embroidery, and pateh, the woollen embroidery of Kerman.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the learner's earlier art lessons. Recommended, never forced.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Needlework L1, Darvish embroidery** — Iranian traditional embroidery — درویش دوزی: the Darvish needlework of Iran.
2. **Needlework L2, Zartoshti embroidery** — زرتشتی دوزی: the Zoroastrian community's embroidery tradition.
3. **Needlework L3, Pateh embroidery** — پته دوزی: pateh, the woollen embroidery of Kerman.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier Visual Art lessons. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say and show what needlework l1, darvish embroidery teaches.
2. Say and show what needlework l2, zartoshti embroidery teaches.
3. Say and show what needlework l3, pateh embroidery teaches.

## What you need
Cloth, a needle, embroidery thread and wool for pateh.
