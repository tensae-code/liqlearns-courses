# Iranian Traditional Embroidery — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Pateh (پته دوزی) is…
a) the woollen embroidery of Kerman  b) a paper craft  c) a kind of lacquer
**Answer: a) the woollen embroidery of Kerman**

**2.** Zartoshti embroidery belongs to…
a) the Zoroastrian community's tradition  b) sailors  c) potters
**Answer: a) the Zoroastrian community's tradition**

**3.** Darvish embroidery is…
a) an Iranian traditional needlework  b) a dance  c) a script
**Answer: a) an Iranian traditional needlework**
