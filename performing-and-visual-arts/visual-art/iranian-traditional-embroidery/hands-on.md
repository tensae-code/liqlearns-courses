# Hands-on — Iranian Traditional Embroidery

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Do the sub lessons
- **Needlework L1, Darvish embroidery** — do the book's own task for this part: Iranian traditional embroidery — درویش دوزی: the Darvish needlework of Iran. Show your companion what you made or named.

- **Needlework L2, Zartoshti embroidery** — do the book's own task for this part: زرتشتی دوزی: the Zoroastrian community's embroidery tradition. Show your companion what you made or named.

- **Needlework L3, Pateh embroidery** — do the book's own task for this part: پته دوزی: pateh, the woollen embroidery of Kerman. Show your companion what you made or named.
