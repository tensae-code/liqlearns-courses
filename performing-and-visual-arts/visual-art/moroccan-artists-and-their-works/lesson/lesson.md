# Moroccan Artists and Their Works

## Do it first

Name one artist from your own country — any art. Morocco's workbook introduces its artists one page at a time, like meeting them.

## Sub lesson 1 — Meeting the artists, unit by unit

The Grade 4 workbook carries an **art-culture page (الثقافة الفنية)** in every unit: **identity cards of Moroccan artists with a photo, a sample of the work and QR links, for home self-learning**:

- Unit 1: the calligraphers **Mohamed Qarmad, Hamid El Kharbouchi and Abdessalam El Fallous**.
- Unit 2: the woman painter **Meriem Amziane**.
- Unit 3: the singer **Samira Ben Said**.

## Sub lesson 2 — Theatre pioneers and musicians

- Unit 4: pioneers of Moroccan theatre — **Amina Rachid, Ahmed Tayeb Laalej and Abdeljabbar Louzir**.
- Unit 5: musicians — **Saïd Choraybi, Habiba Raihani and Amina Rizq Allah**.

## Sub lesson 3 — The creators of a homeland song

- Unit 6: the creators of **"Live, My Country!"** — **Ali El Haddani** (words), **Mohamed Ben Abdessalam** (music) and **Mahmoud El Idrissi** (singing) — a song the class has sung, now met through the three people who made it.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion one artist and their art, from two different units. Companion checks: the art matches the artist? Two units covered?

## Do It Yourself

1. Make an identity card for one artist in this lesson: name, art, and one work or fact.
2. Make the same kind of card for an artist of your own place.
3. Write one sentence: why does the workbook put an artist in every unit?
