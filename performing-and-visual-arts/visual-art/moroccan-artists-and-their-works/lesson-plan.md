# Lesson plan — Moroccan Artists and Their Works

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Visual Art · **Lesson:** Moroccan Artists and Their Works
**Place in the curriculum:** Visual Art (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Morocco's artists met one by one — the workbook's art-culture pages give identity cards of Moroccan artists with a photo, a sample of the work and QR links, for home self-learning: calligraphers, a woman painter, a singer, theatre pioneers, musicians, and the creators of "Live, My Country!"
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this course in the same title.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Meeting the artists, unit by unit** — the art-culture identity cards — calligraphers Mohamed Qarmad, Hamid El Kharbouchi and Abdessalam El Fallous; the woman painter Meriem Amziane; the singer Samira Ben Said.
2. **Theatre pioneers and musicians** — pioneers of Moroccan theatre Amina Rachid, Ahmed Tayeb Laalej and Abdeljabbar Louzir; musicians Saïd Choraybi, Habiba Raihani and Amina Rizq Allah.
3. **The creators of a homeland song** — the creators of "Live, My Country!" — Ali El Haddani (words), Mohamed Ben Abdessalam (music) and Mahmoud El Idrissi (singing).

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of this title. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. name artists from at least two units and the art each one carries
2. name the theatre pioneers and the musicians the pages present
3. name who wrote the words, who made the music and who sang "Live, My Country!"
