# Moroccan Artists and Their Works — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the three calligraphers of Unit 1.
**Answer: Mohamed Qarmad, Hamid El Kharbouchi and Abdessalam El Fallous.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the three musicians of Unit 5.
**Answer: Saïd Choraybi, Habiba Raihani and Amina Rizq Allah.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Who wrote the words and who made the music of "Live, My Country!"?
**Answer: Ali El Haddani wrote the words; Mohamed Ben Abdessalam made the music.**
