# Moroccan Artists and Their Works — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The art-culture pages give each artist…
a) an identity card with a photo, a sample of the work and QR links  b) only a name  c) a test
**Answer: a) an identity card with a photo, a sample of the work and QR links.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Meriem Amziane is…
a) a singer  b) a woman painter  c) a calligrapher
**Answer: b) a woman painter.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Samira Ben Said is…
a) a singer  b) a theatre pioneer  c) a painter
**Answer: a) a singer.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Amina Rachid, Ahmed Tayeb Laalej and Abdeljabbar Louzir are…
a) musicians  b) pioneers of Moroccan theatre  c) calligraphers
**Answer: b) pioneers of Moroccan theatre.**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** "Live, My Country!" was sung by…
a) Mahmoud El Idrissi  b) Ali El Haddani  c) Mohamed Ben Abdessalam
**Answer: a) Mahmoud El Idrissi** — El Haddani wrote the words and Ben Abdessalam the music.**
