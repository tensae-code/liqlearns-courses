# Mixed Techniques

## Do it first

Quick game: take one drawing you have. What could you add to it — a glued piece, a printed leaf, a stamp? The moment you combine them, you are in this lesson.

## Sub lesson 1 — What a mixed technique is

A **mixed technique** is **one work made by combining several techniques and materials** — not one way of making, but several, working together in a single piece.

## Sub lesson 2 — The materials and the work

The book's combinations: **drawing materials with cut-out and glued materials, collage, cutting, direct printing of elements and stamping**, strengthened with **natural and recycled materials**.

Mixed techniques are used to **reproduce and improve the surrounding environment** in **compositions and panels**.

Tell your companion what this lesson taught, part by part. Companion checks each part against the sub lessons above.

## Do It Yourself

1. Make your own piece using this lesson's way, from start to finish.
2. Show it to a companion and talk about your art: what you made, how, and what you learnt.
3. Write one sentence: what will you do differently next time?
