# Hands-on — Mixed Techniques

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Combine three
Make one small composition that combines at least three: drawing, a cut-and-glued piece, and a printed or stamped element. Say which techniques you combined.

## Panel for your place
In a group, make a panel of your surrounding environment — its houses, trees and paths — in mixed techniques, with natural and recycled materials strengthening it.
