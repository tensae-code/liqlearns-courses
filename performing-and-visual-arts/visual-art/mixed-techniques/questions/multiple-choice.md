# Mixed Techniques — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A mixed technique is…
a) one work combining several techniques and materials  b) one technique used many times  c) painting with one colour
**Answer: a) one work combining several techniques and materials**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which belongs in mixed-technique work?
a) collage and stamping  b) only charcoal  c) only watercolour
**Answer: a) collage and stamping**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Mixed-technique work can be strengthened with…
a) natural and recycled materials  b) nothing  c) glass only
**Answer: a) natural and recycled materials**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The book uses mixed techniques to…
a) copy a photograph exactly  b) reproduce and improve the surrounding environment in compositions and panels  c) make posters only
**Answer: b) reproduce and improve the surrounding environment in compositions and panels**
