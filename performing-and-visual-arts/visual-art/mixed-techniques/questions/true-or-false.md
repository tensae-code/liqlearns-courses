# Mixed Techniques — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A drawing alone, with nothing combined, is a mixed technique.
**Answer: False** — a mixed technique combines several techniques and materials in one work.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Cutting and gluing materials can be part of a mixed technique.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Recycled materials have no place in mixed-technique work.
**Answer: False** — natural and recycled materials strengthen it.
