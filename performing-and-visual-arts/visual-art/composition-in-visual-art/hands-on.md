# Hands-on — Composition in Visual Art

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Badly, then well
Arrange five objects badly — crowded, scattered, falling off the edge. Draw the pile in two minutes. Now arrange the same five well, and draw again. Write under the second drawing what you moved.

## Compose a flat picture
On paper, compose a picture of three shapes: place them, move them, until the picture rests — nothing crowding, nothing lost. That resting-place of every element is composition in 2D.
