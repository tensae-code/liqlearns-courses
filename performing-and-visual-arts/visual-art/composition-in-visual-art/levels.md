# Composition in Visual Art — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number, as before. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — What composition is (teacher guide — evidence)
Pass mark: say, as teacher-guide evidence, that the guide teaches composition as its own concept in visual art (Lessons 7–9) — the arranging of a work's elements into the whole the viewer meets.

## Sub lesson 2 — Why composition matters (teacher guide — evidence)
Pass mark: say, as teacher-guide evidence, that the guide teaches the importance/purposes of composition as its own topic, and give composition's work: it makes a work hold together and carry its idea to the viewer in order.

## Sub lesson 3 — Composition and design — in two and three dimensions (teacher guide — evidence)
Pass mark: say, as teacher-guide evidence, that the guide joins composition with design — arranging a work in two dimensions and in three — and describe the difference: the flat work is arranged inside its edges; the three-dimensional work is arranged in space, to be met from more than one side.
