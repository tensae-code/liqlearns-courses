# Composition in Visual Art — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Composition in visual art is —
a) the price of the materials  b) the arranging of a work's elements into the whole  c) a kind of frame
**Answer: b) the arranging of a work's elements into the whole** — where each element stands, and what the eye meets in what order.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A work is first met by the viewer —
a) as separate elements, one per day  b) as a whole, at a glance  c) only after reading its title
**Answer: b) as a whole, at a glance** — the whole arrives before the parts are studied.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In three dimensions, a composed work must hold —
a) only from the front  b) from more than one side — it is met by walking around it  c) only in photographs
**Answer: b) from more than one side — it is met by walking around it** — 3D composition arranges in space.
