# Composition in Visual Art — short answer

Answer in a sentence or a short list.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why does the guide teach composition as a concept first?
**Answer:** Because a learner who can see composition can judge any work — beginning with their own.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** State composition's purpose in one sentence.
**Answer:** The whole holds together, and the work's idea arrives at the viewer — carried by the arrangement.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What boundary does two-dimensional composition arrange inside?
**Answer:** The picture's edges — every element's place is a place on the flat surface.
