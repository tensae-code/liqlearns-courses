*Evidence lesson — the teacher guide's composition lessons, in simple voice.*

Objects on a table — a pile. Move them… one by one… until the group looks **right**.

That moving-until-right is **composition**.

Composition is how a work is **arranged** — in two dimensions on paper, in three dimensions in space.

A composed work holds together. An uncomposed work falls apart before your eyes.

Arrange. Look. Move. Look again. That is the composer's work.
