# Video script — First Marks and Scribbles

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Take your pencil.

Do not draw a house.
Do not draw a person.
Just move.

Round and round.
Up and down.
Left and right.

This is **ሞነጫጨር** — scribbling.
The first drawing of all.

Your hand gets loose.
Your hand gets ready.
Now your hand can draw anything.
