# Hands-on — First Marks and Scribbles


The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The big-air scribble
Before paper, scribble in the air with your whole arm — big loops, big waves. Then sit down and let the pencil do on paper what your arm just did in the air. Feel how much freer the marks are.

## Two-hand pages
Fill one page with your drawing hand. Then try a few marks with your other hand. Laugh at the wobbly ones together — wobbly is allowed here; this lesson is about moving, not matching.

## Scribble, then look
Scribble a full page, then hold it up and look: can you find a shape hiding in your scribble — a hill, a snake, a cloud? Outline the shape you found with a darker line.
