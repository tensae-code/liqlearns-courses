# First Marks and Scribbles — short answer

Answer in a short sentence, or by doing what the question asks.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Show it: fill a small space with free marks in at least three directions, and say the Amharic word for scribbling.
**Answer: ሞነጫጨር — free marks going in different directions, with nothing copied.**

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Say it: name two things scribbling practice builds.
**Answer: Handwriting skill and drawing skill (it also teaches the shapes and colours of things).**
