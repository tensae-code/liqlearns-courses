# First Marks and Scribbles — true or false

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In scribbling, a mark can be wrong.
**Answer: False — there is nothing to get wrong; every free mark is a mark you chose to make.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In the Grade 2 activity, you must copy the pictured lines so they match exactly.
**Answer: False — you draw different lines of your own, following the pictured lines.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The Grade 1 review asks you to draw a line and an oval shape and colour it.
**Answer: True — that is the chapter review task.**
