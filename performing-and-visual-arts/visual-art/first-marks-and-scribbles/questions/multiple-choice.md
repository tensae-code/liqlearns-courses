# First Marks and Scribbles — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Scribbling — ሞነጫጨር — is best described as —
a) copying a picture exactly  b) moving the pencil or crayon freely on paper  c) colouring inside a shape
**Answer: b) moving the pencil or crayon freely on paper**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** On the book's first drawing page, the free scribble is —
a) blue  b) red  c) black
**Answer: b) red — a full page of free red scribble, above coloured vertical strokes.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The Grade 2 book defines scribbling as —
a) the last drawing method  b) the first-level drawing method  c) a colouring method
**Answer: b) the first-level drawing method — moving the hand left, right, up and down with pencil or crayon on paper.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Scribbling before drawing helps because it —
a) loosens the hand  b) sharpens the pencil  c) cleans the paper
**Answer: a) loosens the hand.**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** The Grade 1 summary says scribbling and line practice build —
a) only drawing skill  b) handwriting and drawing skill  c) singing skill
**Answer: b) handwriting and drawing skill — and they teach the shapes and colours of things.**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** The professional known for drawing is called —
a) a singer  b) an artist  c) a builder
**Answer: b) an artist (the Grade 2 closing exercise's own item).**
