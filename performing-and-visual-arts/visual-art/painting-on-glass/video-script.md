This lesson is Painting on Glass.

Painting on glass in the Byzantine manner, pictured step by step — prepare the outlining pen, glass colours and brushes, draw the design on the glass surface with the outliner, then colour the areas with glass paints to a finished painted vase.

Watch, listen — then say it back in your own words.
