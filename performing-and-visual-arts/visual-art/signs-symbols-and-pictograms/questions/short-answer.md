# Signs, Symbols and Pictograms — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What exercise does the Grade 2 book set with subject pictures?
**Answer: Think up and make three small pictures for three different school subjects, so classmates can recognise which subject each is for.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What two sign systems for deaf and blind people does the book name?
**Answer: The hand alphabet of sign language, and Braille's raised-dot letters.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What do traffic signs serve?
**Answer: Understanding in traffic.**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** What two questions should we ask when we see a picture-message?
**Answer: What does this mean? And: what message does this carry to us?**
