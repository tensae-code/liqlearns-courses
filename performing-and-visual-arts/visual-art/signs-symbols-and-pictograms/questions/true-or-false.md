# Signs, Symbols and Pictograms — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A sign for a subject should show something typical of that subject.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A badly designed, unclear sign is understood by more people.
**Answer: False** — the more clearly a sign is designed, the more people understand it.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Seals, coats of arms and flags are all signs people have used through history.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Picture-messages are rare; we almost never meet them.
**Answer: False** — we meet them constantly in everyday surroundings.
