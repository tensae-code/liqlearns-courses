# Signs, Symbols and Pictograms — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A good subject picture is recognised by…
a) what is typical for that subject  b) its price  c) its size only
**Answer: a) what is typical for that subject**.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Braille's letters are…
a) raised dots read by touch  b) pictures on a wall  c) hand movements
**Answer: a) raised dots read by touch**.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The more clearly a sign is designed…
a) the more people understand it  b) the fewer people see it  c) the faster it fades
**Answer: a) the more people understand it**.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Which flags does the Grade 3 book picture?
a) Serbia's flags of 1804, of the King's Guard 1904, and of the Republic of Serbia  b) only traffic flags  c) only school flags
**Answer: a) Serbia's flags of 1804, of the King's Guard 1904, and of the Republic of Serbia**.
