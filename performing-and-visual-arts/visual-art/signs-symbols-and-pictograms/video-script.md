# Video script — Signs, Symbols and Pictograms

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

No words — yet everybody understands.

A hand that speaks. Dots that fingers read. A sign that stops a car.

That is the power of a clear sign.

Seals, coats of arms, flags — signs have carried messages for thousands of years.

When you see one, ask: what does this mean?
