# Design Thinking and Design Technology — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The designer in the global age serves his own community.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Globalisation removed the designer's duties.
**Answer: False** — His counter-duty is health and consumer awareness and defending national production.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Television and packaging carry foreign cultures into the home.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** A logo is designed before a project is founded.
**Answer: True**
