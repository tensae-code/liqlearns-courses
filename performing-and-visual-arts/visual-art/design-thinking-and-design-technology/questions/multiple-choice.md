# Design Thinking and Design Technology — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Advertisement is called the age of globalisation's…
a) language  b) enemy  c) secret
**Answer: a) language** — crossing every barrier by visual communication.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The designer's counter-duty puts at its core…
a) health and consumer awareness  b) imported goods  c) fashion only
**Answer: a) health and consumer awareness** — knowledge shared with his own community, and national production defended.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Logos in the global age are designed…
a) before projects are founded  b) after failure  c) never
**Answer: a) before projects are founded**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Imported goods, the book says, bewitched…
a) the Arab consumer  b) the designer  c) no one
**Answer: a) the Arab consumer** — packaging and television carrying foreign cultures into the home.
