# Colouring Your Surroundings Beautiful

## Do it first

Name one thing that belongs in this lesson — Colouring Your Surroundings Beautiful — Syria's own teaching of this topic, kept as named parts: the world's painted towns — can informal settlements be turned into coloured cities? Would you colour your own neighbourhood, and which do you prefer, drawings on walls or coloured surfaces?. Say what it is in your own words. If you can name one, you are already inside the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Choose one named part of this lesson and find out one more true thing about it — from a person who knows it, or from the book your part came from.
2. Make or perform a small piece in the way of that part: a rhythm, a step, a drawing, a shaped piece of clay.
3. Write one sentence: what does this part show about Syria's own way of teaching its arts?
