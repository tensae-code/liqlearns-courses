# Caricature

## Do it first

Quick start: from the title alone, guess what this lesson's work is. Then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — Cartoon faces from the teacher's examples

A **cartoon face** is a face drawn with its features exaggerated on purpose — the comic distortion is the art. Bhutan's Class IV workbook teaches it from the board: the **teacher's examples** go up first, and from them the children **make different cartoon faces** — not one face copied, but many faces, each different.

## Sub lesson 2 — Colour your cartoon faces

Then **colour them** — the colouring finishes the faces the drawing invented.
