# Lesson plan — Caricature

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Visual Art · **Lesson:** Caricature
**Place in the curriculum:** Visual Art (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** caricature through cartoon faces — exaggerating a face on purpose for comic effect: Bhutan's Class IV workbook teaches it by making different cartoon faces from the teacher's examples on the board, and colouring them.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this course in the same title.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Cartoon faces from the teacher's examples** — From the teacher's examples on the board, make different cartoon faces — faces stretched and exaggerated on purpose, each one different.
2. **Colour your cartoon faces** — Colour the cartoon faces you have made.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of this course in the same title. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say what cartoon faces from the teacher's examples teaches, in the book's own way.
2. Say what colour your cartoon faces teaches, in the book's own way.

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks a cartoon face must look exactly like a real face → a cartoon face exaggerates on purpose: bigger, smaller, longer — that is the fun and the point.
- Thinks one face is enough → the workbook says different cartoon faces: make several, each changed in its own way.
