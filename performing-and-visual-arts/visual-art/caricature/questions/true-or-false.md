# Caricature — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Cartoon faces must look exactly like real faces.
**Answer: False.** — they exaggerate features on purpose.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The teacher shows examples on the board first.
**Answer: True.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The workbook asks for different cartoon faces, not just one.
**Answer: True.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The faces are left uncoloured.
**Answer: False.** — the workbook says to colour them.
