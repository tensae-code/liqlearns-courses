# Caricature — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The cartoon faces are made from…
a) the teacher's examples on the board  b) a photograph  c) a mirror only
**Answer: a) the teacher's examples on the board**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A cartoon face…
a) copies a face exactly  b) exaggerates features on purpose  c) has no features
**Answer: b) exaggerates features on purpose**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** How many cartoon faces does the workbook ask for?
a) one only  b) different cartoon faces — several  c) none
**Answer: b) different cartoon faces — several**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** After making the faces, you…
a) erase them  b) colour them  c) hide them
**Answer: b) colour them**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Colouring comes…
a) before the drawing  b) after the faces are made  c) never
**Answer: b) after the faces are made**
