# Lesson plan — Haitian Naïve and Saint Soleil Painting

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Visual Art · **Lesson:** Haitian Naïve and Saint Soleil Painting
**Place in the curriculum:** Visual Art (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Haiti's own painting — the naïve painters' vivid colours and the Saint-Soleil movement's way of composing with point and line, worked in copies and variants from Haitian heritage.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this course in the same title.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks Saint-Soleil is copying only → copying or imitating a Saint-Soleil work is the start — the learner then creates a variant of the copy.
- Thinks naïve painting means careless painting → the naïve painters are Haiti's honoured popular painters, known for their vivid colours — Casimir Laurent and Préfète Duffaut are named.
