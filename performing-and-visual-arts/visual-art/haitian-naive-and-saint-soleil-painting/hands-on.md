# Hands-on — Haitian Naïve and Saint Soleil Painting

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Copy and variant
Copy or imitate a Saint-Soleil-style composition of points and lines. Then make a variant: change the theme, keep the manner.

## Heritage colours
Where you can, work one part of your picture with a natural colour — coffee, roucou or red earth — and say what changed.
