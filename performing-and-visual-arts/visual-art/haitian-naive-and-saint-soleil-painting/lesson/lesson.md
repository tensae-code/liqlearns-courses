# Haitian Naïve and Saint Soleil Painting

## Do it first

Quick start: from the title alone, guess what this lesson's work is. Then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — Composing in the Saint-Soleil manner

In the atelier Saint-Soleil, composition is built from **point and line in space (mise en page)**, in the manner of Haiti's **Saint-Soleil movement**: copy or imitate a Saint-Soleil work, then **create a variant of the copy** — working where possible with **natural Haitian artisanal colours and dyes: campêche, café, roucou, terre rouge**.

## Sub lesson 2 — African art, Saint-Soleil and heritage

The sequence teaches **African art as a source** of artistic production in point, line and form — and its appropriation by **Pablo Picasso** — then Haiti's **Saint-Soleil movement as TIGA perceived it**: its foundations and characteristics, with point and line worked through **Haitian heritage (patrimoine haïtien)** in copies and variants.

## Sub lesson 3 — The naïve painters and Haitian craft

The programme sets learners to **reinterpret the Haitian « naïve » painters and their vivid colours** — **Casimir Laurent** and **Préfète Duffaut** are named — to analyse the **Saint-Soleil technique** and how it appropriates forms, lines and points, and to meet **points, lines and forms in Haitian craft (artisanat haïtien)**, where artisans build graphic constructions.
