# Haitian Naïve and Saint Soleil Painting — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A Saint-Soleil composition is built from…
a) point and line in space (mise en page)  b) only photographs  c) written words only
**Answer: a) point and line in space (mise en page)** — point and line in space (mise en page) — composing in the movement's manner.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which is one of the natural Haitian dyes named?
a) roucou  b) plastic paint  c) ink from a pen
**Answer: a) roucou** — roucou — beside campêche, café and terre rouge.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** African art in point, line and form was appropriated by…
a) Pablo Picasso  b) Mozart  c) Seurat
**Answer: a) Pablo Picasso** — Pablo Picasso.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Which pair are the named Haitian naïve painters?
a) Casimir Laurent and Préfète Duffaut  b) Bach and Telemann  c) Vivaldi and Mozart
**Answer: a) Casimir Laurent and Préfète Duffaut** — Casimir Laurent and Préfète Duffaut.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** In Haitian craft, artisans build…
a) graphic constructions from points, lines and forms  b) only machines  c) nothing decorated
**Answer: a) graphic constructions from points, lines and forms** — graphic constructions — points, lines and forms at work in artisanat.
