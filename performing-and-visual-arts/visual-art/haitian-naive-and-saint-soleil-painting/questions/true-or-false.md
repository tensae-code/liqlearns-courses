# Haitian Naïve and Saint Soleil Painting — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A Saint-Soleil work can be worked with natural Haitian colours and dyes.
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Saint-Soleil has nothing to do with Haitian heritage.
**Answer: False — point and line are worked through patrimoine haïtien in copies and variants.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The naïve painters are honoured popular painters of Haiti.
**Answer: True.**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** The atelier forbids copying a Saint-Soleil work.
**Answer: False — copying or imitating is the start; a variant follows.**
