| Vietnam — Bài 2, Did you know — the process of beeswax drawing and indigo dyeing creating the patterns of the H'Mông ethnic compatriots — the process of creating patterns on the traditional outfit of the H'Mông ethnic compatriots is recognised as a special folk art form with the stages: weaving fabric, drawing patterns in beeswax (vẽ hoa văn bằng sáp ong), dyeing and embroidering; this process is done meticulously by the manual method, and in that the technique of beeswax drawing and indigo dyeing (nhuộm chàm) on fabric is an extremely unique stage carrying a strong own cultural identity — Step 1: to perform, the H'Mông women must prepare the beeswax by cooking it melting on a small fire stove and keeping it hot continuously throughout the drawing process until it is complete; Step 2: to create the patterns, the H'Mông women use a special pen type with the pen nib made of copper and the pen handle made of bamboo, to dip the hot melting beeswax and draw the patterns on linen fabric (vải lanh); Step 3: when the drawing process is complete, the fabric sheet is taken to be dyed indigo many times until the desired colour is obtained; Step 4: after that, to melt the beeswax away, the fabric is dipped into hot water then taken to be dried in the sun, revealing the white patterns on the indigo background (student book; original target `culture-and-values/ethnic-costume-patterns-of-vietnam`) | Book 3, printed p. 25 |
