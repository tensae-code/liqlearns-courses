This lesson is Ethnic Costume Patterns of Vietnam.

Ethnic Costume Patterns of Vietnam — its forms, history and cultural meaning, taught as one topic lesson. — as Vietnam's school books teach it: The dress of Vietnam's Mông, Ê-đê and Chăm peoples — pleated skirts, shirts, sashes and hats whose woven patterns stylise flowers, leaves and animals into simple lines and shapes; each people's costume carries its own identity.
