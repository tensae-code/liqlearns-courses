# Lesson plan — Women Artists Around the World

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Visual Art · **Lesson:** Women Artists Around the World
**Place in the curriculum:** Visual Art (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Women Artists Around the World — Syria's own teaching of this topic, kept as named parts: Shalabiya Ibrahim (b. 1944), Egyptian woman painter of the naive style who lived in Syria — nature drawn from rural life with its human, animal and plant elements; proposing a name for her painting and drawing a person, birds and nature in her spirit
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the course's earlier lessons in its title; recommended first, never forced.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of ..." in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Lesson 9 "The Artist Shalabiya Ibrahim"** — Shalabiya Ibrahim (b. 1944), Egyptian woman painter of the naive style who lived in Syria — nature drawn from rural life with its human, animal and plant elements; proposing a name for her painting and drawing a person, birds and nature in her spirit
2. **Lesson 2 (the woman artist)** — a Syrian woman artist's paintings studied by the whole class, her homeland creativity honoured
3. **Lesson 22 (the woman artist)** — a young Syrian woman artist's panels studied by the whole class
4. **Lesson 28 (the woman artist)** — a world woman artist's panels — their subjects, the style she adopted (reality — imagination), the visual balance in them — studied by the whole class

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
