# Video script — Women Artists Around the World

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This is Syria's own lesson.

**Lesson 9 "The Artist Shalabiya Ibrahim".** Shalabiya Ibrahim (b. 1944), Egyptian woman painter of the naive style who lived in Syria — nature drawn from rural life with its human, animal and plant elements; proposing a name for her painting and drawing a person, birds and nature in her spirit.

**Lesson 2 (the woman artist).** a Syrian woman artist's paintings studied by the whole class, her homeland creativity honoured.

**Lesson 22 (the woman artist).** a young Syrian woman artist's panels studied by the whole class.

**Lesson 28 (the woman artist).** a world woman artist's panels — their subjects, the style she adopted (reality — imagination), the visual balance in them — studied by the whole class.

That is Women Artists Around the World — Syria's way, kept by name.
