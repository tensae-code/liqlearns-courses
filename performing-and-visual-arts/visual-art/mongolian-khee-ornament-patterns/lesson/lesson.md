# Mongolian Khee Ornament Patterns

## Do it first

Start with the first part below — do it, sing it or make it before you read any explanation.

## Sub lesson 1 — Part II: Чагтан хээг баримлын шавраар хийх (Making the chagtan khee in modelling clay)

Part II: Чагтан хээг баримлын шавраар хийх (Making the chagtan khee in modelling clay) — the chagtan (cross) pattern, one of Mongolia's traditional khee ornaments: shown repeated in a row, worked into a felt rug (shirmel shirdeg) and cut in coloured paper, then modelled in clay and used to decorate a copied dress and vases

## Sub lesson 2 — Part I: Эвэр угалз зурах (Drawing the ever ugalz)

Part I: Эвэр угалз зурах (Drawing the ever ugalz) — the horn-scroll pattern: drawn in white against a coloured ground with gradation; beside a photograph of argali sheep the book tells that ancient people, observing animals' horns, created the ever ugalz — it has a history; digits curled like it; a rich ugalz border whose pattern kinds are named; a 15 cm square drawn and a shape decorated with ever ugalz, and how three horn scrolls sit beautifully together
