# Mongolian Khee Ornament Patterns — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** The chagtan khee is the…
a) cross pattern  b) spiral pattern  c) wave pattern
**Answer: a) cross pattern**

**2.** The ever ugalz is the…
a) horn-scroll pattern, created by ancient people observing animals' horns  b) knot of thread  c) star pattern
**Answer: a) horn-scroll pattern, created by ancient people observing animals' horns**

**3.** In Grade 1 the chagtan is modelled in…
a) modelling clay, and used to decorate a dress and vases  b) wood only  c) glass
**Answer: a) modelling clay, and used to decorate a dress and vases**
