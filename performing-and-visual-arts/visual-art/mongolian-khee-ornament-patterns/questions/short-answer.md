# Mongolian Khee Ornament Patterns — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1.** Where does the ever ugalz come from?
**Answer: Ancient people, observing animals' horns, created it — it has a history.**

**2.** Name two places the chagtan khee appears.
**Answer: Any two of: repeated in a row; worked into a felt rug; cut in coloured paper; modelled in clay; decorating a dress and vases.**
