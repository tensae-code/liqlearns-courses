---

**Picotagem — pierced paper.** Picotagem: draw the figure on the back of coloured paper, lay it on a soft, padded surface, prick holes along the drawing's lines with a pin or needle (thread wrapped at the eye for safety), then tear or cut the figure out along the dotted line.

That is Paper Cutting and Kirigami — Angola's own books teach it this way.
