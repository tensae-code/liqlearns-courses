# Hands-on — Paper Cutting and Kirigami

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Picotagem — pierced paper
Work through this part with real materials or your own body, the way the book sets it: picotagem: draw the figure on the back of coloured paper, lay it on a soft, padded surface, prick holes along the drawing's lines with a pin or needle (thread wrapped at the eye for safety), then tear or cut the figure out along the dotted line. Show a companion what you made or did, and say what the part teaches.
