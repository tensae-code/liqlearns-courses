# Paper Cutting and Kirigami

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about this lesson's subject — cutting and piercing paper into art. Then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — Picotagem — pierced paper

Picotagem: draw the figure on the back of coloured paper, lay it on a soft, padded surface, prick holes along the drawing's lines with a pin or needle (thread wrapped at the eye for safety), then tear or cut the figure out along the dotted line.
