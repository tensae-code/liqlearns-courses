# Paper Cutting and Kirigami — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson — Paper Cutting and Kirigami — opens with…

**Answer: a) Picotagem — pierced paper.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The Angola book teaches this lesson as…
a) cutting and piercing paper into art  b) a sports rule  c) a cooking recipe
**Answer: a) cutting and piercing paper into art.**

**3.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) Picotagem — pierced paper  b) long division  c) map coordinates
**Answer: a) Picotagem — pierced paper.**

**4.** The parts of this lesson come from…
a) Angola's own Educação Manual e Plástica / Educação Musical books  b) a newspaper  c) a song chorus
**Answer: a) Angola's own books.**

**5.** "Picotagem — pierced paper" is taught in this lesson as…
