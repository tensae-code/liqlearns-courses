# Hands-on — Design and Perspective: Near and Far

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The road drawing
Draw a straight road going away from you: two edges narrowing to one vanishing point. Place three trees along it — the nearest large and dark, the farthest small and light.

## Shrink and lighten
Draw the same cup three times in a row, as if walking away from you: each time smaller, each time lighter in shade. Label them near, middle, far.
