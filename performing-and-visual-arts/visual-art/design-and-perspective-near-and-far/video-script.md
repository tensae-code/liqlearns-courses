# Video script — Design and Perspective: Near and Far


The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.


---


*Evidence lesson — the teacher guide's design section, in simple voice.*

Near things are **large and dark**. Far things are **small and light**.

A road runs away — and narrows… and narrows… to **one point**.

That is **perspective** — የርቀትና ቅርበት — the view of distance.

Distance changes light. Distance changes shade.

Draw the distance, and your picture gains a world behind it.
