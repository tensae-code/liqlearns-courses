# Design and Perspective: Near and Far — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Perspective, in the guide's design section, is —
a) the view of distance — near and far  b) a kind of paint  c) the frame around a picture
**Answer: a) the view of distance — near and far** — የርቀትና ቅርበት / ዕርዮተ አድማስ.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In the guide's picture, the road —
a) widens as it goes away  b) narrows to a vanishing point  c) ends at the first tree
**Answer: b) narrows to a vanishing point** — the edges run together to one point.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** As a thing goes farther away in a design, its shade —
a) darkens  b) lightens  c) turns red
**Answer: b) lightens** — distance changes light and shade — the far is lighter.
