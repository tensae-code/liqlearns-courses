# Design and Perspective: Near and Far — short answer

Answer in a sentence or a short list.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Give the two Amharic names the guide uses for perspective.
**Answer:** የርቀትና ቅርበት and ዕርዮተ አድማስ (the view of distance).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** State the pair of changes that show a thing going away from the viewer.
**Answer:** It shrinks (smaller size) and it lightens (lighter shade).
