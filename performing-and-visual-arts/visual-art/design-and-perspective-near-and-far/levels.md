# Design and Perspective: Near and Far — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number, as before. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — Near and far — perspective (teacher guide — evidence)
Pass mark: say, as teacher-guide evidence, that the guide's design section teaches perspective — የርቀትና ቅርበት / ዕርዮተ አድማስ, the view of distance — and describe its picture: a road narrowing to a vanishing point, nearer trees darker and larger.

## Sub lesson 2 — Near and far in light and shadow (teacher guide — evidence)
Pass mark: say, as teacher-guide evidence, that the guide's section also teaches near-and-far in light and shadow — how distance changes light and shade — and show it in a drawing: the near object carrying the darkest shade, the far the lightest.
