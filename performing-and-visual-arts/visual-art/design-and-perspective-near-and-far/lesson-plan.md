# Lesson plan — Design and Perspective: Near and Far

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Visual Art · **Lesson:** Design and Perspective: Near and Far
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 2 — Visual Art (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** perspective — the view of distance — in design for painting and printing: how near and far are shown, and how distance changes light and shadow. Teacher-guide evidence throughout, labelled.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the drawing lessons, where the learner shaded near objects. This lesson is the teacher guide's own design section carried as evidence: it names the principle the drawing hand was already using.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
1. **Near and far — perspective (teacher guide — evidence)** — the view of distance: the road, the vanishing point, nearer trees darker and larger.
2. **Near and far in light and shadow (teacher guide — evidence)** — how distance changes light and shade.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Drawing: The Foundation Skill (Visual Art) — shading and distance in drawing. Recommended, never forced.
- Recommended first: Painting Still Life (Visual Art) — the arranged scene this principle deepens. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say, from the guide's evidence, what perspective (የርቀትና ቅርበት / ዕርዮተ አድማስ) is: the view of distance in design.
2. Describe how near and far show themselves in a design: the picture slot's road narrowing to a vanishing point, nearer trees darker and larger.
3. Say how distance changes light and shade, from the guide's evidence.

## What you need
Paper, pencil — and a road, a line of trees, or a row of houses to look at.
