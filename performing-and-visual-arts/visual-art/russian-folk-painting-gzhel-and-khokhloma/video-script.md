This lesson is Russian Folk Painting: Gzhel and Khokhloma.

Russian Folk Painting: Gzhel and Khokhloma — Russia's named folk painting — gzhel's blue-on-white and khokhloma's gold-and-red painting, their elements and techniques, with cold batik painting beside them. — as Kazakhstan's school books teach it: what it teaches: художественная роспись — the art of decorating any surface with paints and a brush, and its many kinds; the three the course studies — batik on fabric, gzhel on ceramics and khokhloma on wood — compared by material, colour, subject and character; how painting differs from easel painting, and what makes a folk master different from a modern applied artist.

Watch, listen — then say it back in your own words.
