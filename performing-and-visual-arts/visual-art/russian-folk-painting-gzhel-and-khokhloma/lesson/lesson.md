# Russian Folk Painting: Gzhel and Khokhloma

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about russian folk painting: gzhel and khokhloma. Then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — Kazakhstan Grade 7 Artistic Labour (girls, Alimsaeva)

what it teaches: художественная роспись — the art of decorating any surface with paints and a brush, and its many kinds; the three the course studies — batik on fabric, gzhel on ceramics and khokhloma on wood — compared by material, colour, subject and character; how painting differs from easel painting, and what makes a folk master different from a modern applied artist; what it teaches: khokhloma — decorative painting on wood, its ornament plant-based (wild strawberries, rowan, flowering branches, rarely birds and fish), painted freehand with a soft brush and no pencil underdrawing, fine lines laid straight from the brush tip; its element alphabet, pictured stroke by stroke — осочки, травинки, капельки, усики, завитки, кустики and ягодки (berries dabbed with a rolled paper stamp, then «enlivened» with yellow); the founding legend of the icon-painter who fled to the forest; the three ways of holding the brush; what it teaches: gzhel — the blue-on-white painting of ceramics and porcelain from the village of Gzhel on the Gzhelka river (the name from жечь, to fire; clay dug there since the mid-17th century); born many-coloured, it settled into cobalt blue; its pictured vocabulary — nets and dots, the flower built from comma strokes, and the borders; what it teaches: three complete makes — cold batik in six steps (A4 sketch, transfer to fabric, marker contours, stretch on frame, water-based resist, paint light to dark, dry, decorate and mount); a khokhloma piece (invent the form, prime the ground black or yellow, lay the stem in one smooth thin line, set berries and flowers rhythmically along it, add the curly grass, watch the kolorit); a gzhel piece (mould the form in plasticine, paint it, understate spout and handles in dark blue, then the flower painted from its middle with a one-sided loaded brush, stroke over stroke, finished with leaves, curls and dots)

## Sub lesson 2 — Kazakhstan Grade 7 Artistic Labour 2025 (girls, Alimsaeva)

what it teaches: khokhloma, decorative painting on wood — its traditional ornament is vegetable: berries of strawberry and rowan, flowering branches, more rarely birds and fish; the khokhloma artist never draws clear contours in the patterns beforehand — even the fine drawing of small details, where a very thin line-stroke is needed, is done at once with a soft brush without a preliminary pencil drawing; its elements, each with its stroke — «осочки» (a light movement of the brush tip from top down), «травинки» (strokes with a small smooth thickening), «капельки» (drawn by applying the brush to the surface), «усики» (unbroken lines of one thickness curled into a spiral), «завитки» (with a light press in the element's middle), «кустики» (built of the simpler symmetric elements) and «ягодки» (cowberry, currant and rowan berries drawn with a печатка — a strip of paper rolled into a tight tube — gooseberry and strawberry with a brush, the dried berries «enlivened» with yellow); gzhel, the Russian craft of painting on ceramics — at first dishes and figures were painted yellow, red and green, later the blue drawing appeared, done in cobalt, a paint taking a saturated blue colour after firing in the kiln; its typical patterns are flowers, berries, birds and nets; lines-and-dots, flower and border pattern charts and the correct holds of the brush pictured for practice
