| Kazakhstan — § 5 (continued). Хохлома и гжель — what it teaches: khokhloma, decorative painting on wood — its traditional ornament is vegetable: berries of strawberry and rowan, flowering branches, more rarely birds and fish; the khokhloma artist never draws clear contours in the patterns beforehand — even the fine drawing of small details, where a very thin line-stroke is needed, is done at once with a soft brush without a preliminary pencil drawing; its elements, each with its stroke — «осочки» (a light movement of the brush tip from top down), «травинки» (strokes with a small smooth thickening), «капельки» (drawn by applying the brush to the surface), «усики» (unbroken lines of one thickness curled into a spiral), «завитки» (with a light press in the element's middle), «кустики» (built of the simpler symmetric elements) and «ягодки» (cowberry, currant and rowan berries drawn with a печатка — a strip of paper rolled into a tight tube — gooseberry and strawberry with a brush, the dried berries «enlivened» with yellow); gzhel, the Russian craft of painting on ceramics — at first dishes and figures were painted yellow, red and green, later the blue drawing appeared, done in cobalt, a paint taking a saturated blue colour after firing in the kiln; its typical patterns are flowers, berries, birds and nets; lines-and-dots, flower and border pattern charts and the correct holds of the brush pictured for practice (student book; original target `visual-art/khokhloma-and-gzhel-painting`) | Book 2, printed p. 30 |
