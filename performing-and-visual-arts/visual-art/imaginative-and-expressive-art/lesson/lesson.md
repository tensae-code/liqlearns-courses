# Imaginative and Expressive Art

## Do it first

Quick game: look at a cloud and say what else it could be — a ship, a fish, a balloon. You have started a fantasy landscape.

## Sub lesson 1 — Fantasy landscapes — a dream-like scene

A **fantasy landscape** **re-creates an object to resemble something else**, **changes sizes and positions**, and **combines objects into a dream-like scene**. The book's example: **Kush's clouds moulded into a hot-air balloon** — a cloud that is also a balloon.

## Sub lesson 2 — Tones with lines and dots

Tones in the fantasy landscape are added **with lines or dots** — drawn **closer together for darker**, further apart for lighter. The dream scene still needs light and dark to stand up.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson taught, part by part. Companion checks each part against the sub lessons above.

## Do It Yourself

1. Make your own piece using this lesson's way, from start to finish.
2. Show it to the class and say, in the book's own closing words, talk about your art: what you made, how, and what you learnt.
3. Write one sentence: what will you do differently next time?
