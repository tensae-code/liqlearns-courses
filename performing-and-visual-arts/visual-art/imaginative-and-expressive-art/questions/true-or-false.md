# Imaginative and Expressive Art — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A fantasy landscape may change an object's size.
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A fantasy landscape must copy the real scene exactly.
**Answer: False — it re-creates and combines objects into a dream-like scene.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Dots drawn closer together make a darker tone.
**Answer: True.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Lines cannot be used to add tone.
**Answer: False — tones are added with lines or dots.**
