# Imaginative and Expressive Art — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What scene does a fantasy landscape make?
**Answer: A dream-like scene.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How do dots make a darker tone?
**Answer: Drawn closer together.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Whose cloud-balloon does the book show?
**Answer: Kush's — clouds moulded into a hot-air balloon.**
