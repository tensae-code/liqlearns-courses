# Imaginative and Expressive Art — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A fantasy landscape combines objects into…
a) a photograph  b) a dream-like scene  c) a shopping list
**Answer: b) a dream-like scene**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In the book's example, Kush's clouds are moulded into…
a) a house  b) a hot-air balloon  c) a boat
**Answer: b) a hot-air balloon**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Fantasy landscapes can change objects'…
a) sizes and positions  b) prices  c) names only
**Answer: a) sizes and positions**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Darker tones are made with lines or dots drawn…
a) further apart  b) closer together  c) not at all
**Answer: b) closer together**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Re-creating an object to resemble something else is…
a) copying it exactly  b) a fantasy-landscape move  c) forbidden
**Answer: b) a fantasy-landscape move**
