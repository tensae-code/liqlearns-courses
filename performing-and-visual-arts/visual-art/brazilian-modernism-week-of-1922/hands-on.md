# Hands-on — Brazilian Modernism and the Week of 1922

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## The Week of Modern Art (São Paulo
Do the practice this part names, with real materials and a partner: the Week of Modern Art (São Paulo, 1922) and Anita Malfatti; the Pau-Brasil and Antropofágico manifestos; Tarsila's Abaporu and the releitura (re-reading a work in your own style); writing your own manifesto and remaking Abaporu

## Tarsila's Operários
Do the practice this part names, with real materials and a partner: Tarsila's Operários: the pyramid crowd of Brazil's many peoples; improvising a crowd scene from the painting
