# Brazilian Modernism and the Week of 1922

## Do it first

Look at the sub lesson list. Pick the part you already know something about, and say what you know. Then start at Sub lesson 1 — every part builds on the one before it.

## Sub lesson 1 — The Week of Modern Art (São Paulo

The Week of Modern Art (São Paulo, 1922) and Anita Malfatti; the Pau-Brasil and Antropofágico manifestos; Tarsila's Abaporu and the releitura (re-reading a work in your own style); writing your own manifesto and remaking Abaporu (Folha 14, Brazil's *ARTE — Ensino Médio*, printed p. 221).

## Sub lesson 2 — Tarsila's Operários

Tarsila's Operários: the pyramid crowd of Brazil's many peoples; improvising a crowd scene from the painting (Folha 14, Brazil's *ARTE — Ensino Médio*, printed p. 227).
