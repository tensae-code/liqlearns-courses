# Video script — Vernacular Architecture of the Dominican Republic

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Old houses are documents. Read them like a reporter.

Then build them again — small, in card and glue.

That is vernacular architecture, Dominican style.

That is the **maqueta of our identity**.
