# Vernacular Architecture of the Dominican Republic — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Vernacular architecture reflects…
a) a region's culture, history and ideology  b) only new buildings  c) only churches
**Answer: a) a region's culture, history and ideology**.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The learner documents heritage houses working as…
a) a reporter  b) a tourist  c) a seller
**Answer: a) a reporter**.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** One named example is…
a) the Victorian house of Puerto Plata  b) a skyscraper  c) a castle in Europe
**Answer: a) the Victorian house of Puerto Plata**.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A maqueta is…
a) a scale model of the documented house  b) a painting  c) a photograph
**Answer: a) a scale model of the documented house**.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** The Colonial City models are built from…
a) waste materials, with walls, doors and windows drawn, cut, folded and glued  b) bought kits  c) clay only
**Answer: a) waste materials, with walls, doors and windows drawn, cut, folded and glued**.
