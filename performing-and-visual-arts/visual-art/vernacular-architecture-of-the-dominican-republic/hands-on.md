# Hands-on — Vernacular Architecture of the Dominican Republic

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Reporter's page
Document one heritage house near you as the book's reporter does: draw it, name its style and elements, and write what epoch it speaks of.

## Maqueta start
Build the documented house's base and first walls on cardboard, from used boxes — walls with tabs, ready to fold and glue.
