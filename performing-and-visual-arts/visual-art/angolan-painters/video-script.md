# Video script — Angolan Painters

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

**Tomás Ana Etona and Neves e Sousa — warm and cold colours.** Appreciating named painters' works: Angolan painter Tomás Ana Etona's "Paragem do Zamba 1" and Neves e Sousa's "Tocador de quissange" (kisanji player), studied for warm and cold colours, beside Mondrian's primary-colour composition.

**Learning from known painters.** Studying known artists' paintings to identify their materials and techniques (oil, gouache, watercolour) — Ouattara (Côte d'Ivoire), Álvaro Macieira (Angola) and Albert Lubaki (Zaire) pictured — then copying a favourite painting as closely as you can.

That is Angolan Painters — Angola's own books teach it this way.
