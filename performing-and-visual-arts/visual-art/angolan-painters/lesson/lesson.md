# Angolan Painters

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about this lesson's subject — appreciating paintings by Angola's own painters. Then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — Tomás Ana Etona and Neves e Sousa — warm and cold colours

Appreciating named painters' works: Angolan painter Tomás Ana Etona's "Paragem do Zamba 1" and Neves e Sousa's "Tocador de quissange" (kisanji player), studied for warm and cold colours, beside Mondrian's primary-colour composition.

## Sub lesson 2 — Learning from known painters

Studying known artists' paintings to identify their materials and techniques (oil, gouache, watercolour) — Ouattara (Côte d'Ivoire), Álvaro Macieira (Angola) and Albert Lubaki (Zaire) pictured — then copying a favourite painting as closely as you can.
