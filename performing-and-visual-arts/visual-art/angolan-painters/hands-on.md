# Hands-on — Angolan Painters

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Tomás Ana Etona and Neves e Sousa — warm and cold colours
Work through this part with real materials or your own body, the way the book sets it: appreciating named painters' works: Angolan painter Tomás Ana Etona's "Paragem do Zamba 1" and Neves e Sousa's "Tocador de quissange" (kisanji player), studied for warm and cold colours, beside Mondrian's primary-colour composition. Show a companion what you made or did, and say what the part teaches.

## Learning from known painters
Work through this part with real materials or your own body, the way the book sets it: studying known artists' paintings to identify their materials and techniques (oil, gouache, watercolour) — Ouattara (Côte d'Ivoire), Álvaro Macieira (Angola) and Albert Lubaki (Zaire) pictured — then copying a favourite painting as closely as you can. Show a companion what you made or did, and say what the part teaches.
