# Stylisation and Decorative Motifs — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — Compoziții decorative
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: G4 Ch. 3, Compoziții decorative — decorative composition: stylisation (simplifying a natural form by geometrising it, step by step), the decorative motif (geometric, vegetal, zoomorphic), and the two ordering principles rhythm (repetition) and symmetry.

## Sub lesson 2 — Principii de construire a compozițiilor decorative
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Unit 3, Principii de construire a compozițiilor decorative — stylisation: simplifying a natural form by geometrising it into a decorative motif (geometric, vegetal, zoomorphic), built by rhythm and symmetry, as on Moldovan carpets and towels.

## Sub lesson 3 — Transformarea formelor din natură
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Unit I §3, Transformarea formelor din natură — nature's structures (animal, vegetal, mineral) turned into art: Egyptians simplified the lotus flower into jewellery and lotus-shaped columns.

## Sub lesson 4 — Tratarea decorativă
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Unit II §7, Tratarea decorativă — decorative treatment against pictorial: colour laid uniformly, surfaces with precise contours, no volume and no aerial perspective, motifs geometrised and simplified; stylisation "warm" with wavy cornerless lines, "cold" with straight angular ones.

## Sub lesson 5 — Crearea motivului decorativ
Sub lesson 5 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Unit II §7.2, Crearea motivului decorativ — making a decorative motif step by step: examine a tulip, draw it true, stylise it, then compose with it; the motif families — geometric, vegetal, zoomorphic, anthropomorphic (sun, man, birds, flowers, little horses).

## Sub lesson 6 — De la structura naturală spre structura plastică
Sub lesson 6 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Unit I §1, De la structura naturală spre structura plastică — turning a natural structure into a plastic one by the artist's procedures (study, construction, overlapping, sectioning, combining, stylisation, modelling, modulation); art may imitate nature faithfully or transform it into new decorative forms — Escher's "Lizards".

## Sub lesson 7 — Stilizarea ca procedeu artistic
Sub lesson 7 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Unit III §15, Stilizarea ca procedeu artistic — stylisation's three operations (simplify the form, geometrise it, substitute its colours) and the stylised form at work everywhere: coats of arms (Gheorghe Vrabie's State Coat of Arms of Moldova), pictograms, posters, packaging, fabrics — Klimt's "Tree of Life" against Aurel David's unstylised "Noon".
