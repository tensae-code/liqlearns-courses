# Barbadian Crafts and Makers

## Do it first

Quick start: say in one sentence what you think this lesson is about — Barbados's own makers and their work. Then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — Museums and local artifacts

Cultural Awareness in Classes 2–4 appreciates local culture through **museum visits — the Historic and Marine museums** — and through **collecting and drawing local artifacts**: the island's made things, looked at closely and drawn.

## Sub lesson 2 — Barbadian makers: textile artists, sculptors and potters

The appreciation names the makers: **Barbadian textile artists, local sculptors and potters** — studied as the island's own artists and craftspeople.

## Sub lesson 3 — Basket weavers, heard in person

The makers are met, not only read about: pupils **hear local basket weavers** — the weaver's own account of the craft, in the weaver's own presence.
