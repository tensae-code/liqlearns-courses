# Barbadian Crafts and Makers — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the two museums named in the guide.
**Answer: The Historic museum and the Marine museum.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name three kinds of Barbadian makers studied.
**Answer: Textile artists, sculptors and potters.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** How are basket weavers learned from?
**Answer: By hearing them — the weavers speak about their craft.**
