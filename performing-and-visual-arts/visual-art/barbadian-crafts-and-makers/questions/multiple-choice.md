# Barbadian Crafts and Makers — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The guide's museum visits are to…
a) the Historic and Marine museums  b) a cinema  c) a stadium
**Answer: a) the Historic and Marine museums**.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** With local artifacts, pupils…
a) collect and draw them  b) sell them  c) hide them
**Answer: a) collect and draw them**.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Which makers does the guide name for study?
a) Barbadian textile artists, local sculptors and potters  b) foreign painters only  c) architects only
**Answer: a) Barbadian textile artists, local sculptors and potters**.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Basket weavers are met by…
a) hearing them in person  b) reading a label only  c) guessing
**Answer: a) hearing them in person** — pupils hear local basket weavers.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** This appreciation work runs through…
a) Classes 2–4  b) Class 1 only  c) no classes
**Answer: a) Classes 2–4**.
