## Sub lesson 6 — Kazakhstan Grade 7 Artistic Labour (girls, Alimsaeva)

what it teaches: Askhat Akhmedyarov's installation «Әженің көзі» («Grandmother's Eye») — 32 cauldrons (казан) joined into two spheres, a necklace: in Kazakh, әженің көзі is the keepsake a grandmother passes to her granddaughters — the cauldron of the family hearth read as a bead of that necklace, a lifelong amulet like a pearl or coral bead; what it teaches: Kazakhstan's art since independence in three directions — the symbolic method, conceptual devices and realism — a refusal of stereotypes and a search for new readings of Kazakh life, nature and history; Duzelkhanov's realistic gallery of khans and heroes («Томирис»); Burdesbekov's «Сыргалы қыз», a steppe madonna in jewellery, built of abstract colour and light; Akanaev's psychological canvases («Саукеле», «Кочевые»); the new generation (Madanova, Khairullina, Balieva, Kazgulova), Mergenov, sculptor Dalbaev; the practical — contour cut-outs of Kazakh symbols composed into abstract vertical and horizontal compositions; what it teaches: the A. Kasteev State Museum of Arts in Almaty — over 20,000 originals of modern and ancient applied art, twelve halls, six of painting, Kasteev's own works in a separate hall; the ancient-art hall opening the display — rock engravings and petroglyphs, applied art, monumental and small plastic of the Palaeolithic, Bronze and early Iron ages, medieval architectural decor, and the museum's unique collection of Kazakh jewellery, carved and inlaid wood and bone; the world's great galleries named beside it

## Sub lesson 9 — Kazakhstan Grade 7 Artistic Labour 2025 (boys, Chukalin)

what it teaches: Kazakhstan's contemporary art is presented in the country's leading museums — in the National Museum of Arts of the Republic of Kazakhstan named after A. Kasteev in Almaty one can see many works of both classics and contemporary artists; the museum's collection is distinguished by diversity, artistic value and depth, letting one feel the distinctness and many-sidedness of the national artistic tradition; the Hall of Contemporary Art in the National Museum of the Republic of Kazakhstan in Astana serves as an important ground for presenting the achievements of Kazakhstani art of the last decades, and the space is equipped with contemporary multimedia technologies, which makes acquaintance with the exposition richer and more interactive; each of these museums demonstrates the wealth and diversity of Kazakhstan's contemporary art, opening for visitors the possibility to get acquainted with the creativity of the country's talented artists; the research work: using different sources, independently get acquainted with the creativity of contemporary Kazakhstani artists (of your choice) — define the genres and directions in which they work, carry out a comparative analysis of their works, and pick out similarities and differences; the practical work: create your picture using characteristic elements of the chosen artist's style (for example his technique or colour palette) — use national motifs in the work; explain why you chose exactly this artist; analyse how the artist's style was reflected in your work: which elements you adopted, and with the help of which you added your work's uniqueness; estimate how successfully you managed to depict the artist's style in your work
