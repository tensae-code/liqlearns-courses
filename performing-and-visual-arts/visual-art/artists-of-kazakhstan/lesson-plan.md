# Lesson plan — Artists of Kazakhstan

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Visual Art · **Lesson:** Artists of Kazakhstan
**Place in the curriculum:** Visual Art (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** artists of Kazakhstan — its forms, history and cultural meaning, taught as one topic lesson..
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this course in the same title.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Kazakhstan Grade 4 Artistic Labour** — Kazakhstani painters (Yskakov, Kenbaev, Kaspak, Volkova) and how each depicts the homeland's nature
2. **Kazakhstan Grade 5 Artistic Labour (girls, Alimsaeva)** — what it teaches: the Kasteev State Museum of Arts — twelve halls, a separate Kasteev hall, and an ancient-art hall of petroglyphs and bronze-age applied art; what it teaches: a gallery of Kazakhstani painting — Tansykbaev's «Кочевники», D. Kasteev's «Утро перед битвой», Galimbaeva's «Апорт» apples, Kim's «Первенец»
3. **Kazakhstan Grade 5 Artistic Labour (boys, Chukalin)** — what it teaches: the Kasteev State Museum of Arts — twelve halls, a separate Kasteev hall, and an ancient-art hall of petroglyphs and bronze-age applied art; what it teaches: a gallery of Kazakhstani painting — Tansykbaev's «Кочевники», D. Kasteev's «Утро перед битвой», Galimbaeva's «Апорт» apples, Kim's «Первенец»
4. **Kazakhstan Grade 6 Artistic Labour (girls, Alimsaeva)** — what it teaches: Kazakhstan's portrait masters — Kasteev, whose portrait of Amangeldy Imanov stands in the hero's native landscape, told by the artist's daughter; Togyzbaev's gallery of national figures (Tole bi, Abay); Ismailova's portraits of theatre and cinema people; Akiev's emotional watercolour portraits; Sariev's national style («Кыз Жибек»); Abuov's portraits of cultural figures and of Tlendiev's folk-orchestra musicians; what it teaches: a portrait gallery closing the book — Ismailova's Kulyash Baiseitova as Kyz Zhibek and her portrait of Tulegenova, Krutilnikov's fisherman, Sidorkin's «Алпамыс Батыр», Rusheva's lyceum students and Goncharova, Klementyev's and Abishev's portraits, Renoir's actress Samary beside Cherkassky's «Жамбыл Жабаев и Дина Нурпеисова», and Ordabekov's engraving «Бухар жырау»
5. **Kazakhstan Grade 6 Artistic Labour (boys, Chukalin)** — what it teaches: Kazakhstan's portrait masters — Kasteev, whose portrait of Amangeldy Imanov stands in the hero's native landscape, told by the artist's daughter; Togyzbaev's gallery of national figures (Tole bi, Abay); Ismailova's portraits of theatre and cinema people; Akiev's emotional watercolour portraits; Sariev's national style («Кыз Жибек»); Abuov's portraits of cultural figures and of Tlendiev's folk-orchestra musicians; what it teaches: the portrait gallery closing the book — the girls' edition's plates (Ismailova, Krutilnikov, Sidorkin, Rusheva, Abishev, Renoir, Cherkassky, Ordabekov) plus Duzelkhanov's three great biys — Tole bi, Kazybek bi, Aiteke bi — Kasteev's young Abay and «Портрет матери», and Ismailova's portraits of Auezov and of Eralieva as Karakoz
6. **Kazakhstan Grade 7 Artistic Labour (girls, Alimsaeva)** — what it teaches: Askhat Akhmedyarov's installation «Әженің көзі» («Grandmother's Eye») — 32 cauldrons (казан) joined into two spheres, a necklace: in Kazakh, әженің көзі is the keepsake a grandmother passes to her granddaughters — the cauldron of the family hearth read as a bead of that necklace, a lifelong amulet like a pearl or coral bead; what it teaches: Kazakhstan's art since independence in three directions — the symbolic method, conceptual devices and realism — a refusal of stereotypes and a search for new readings of Kazakh life, nature and history; Duzelkhanov's realistic gallery of khans and heroes («Томирис»); Burdesbekov's «Сыргалы қыз», a steppe madonna in jewellery, built of abstract colour and light; Akanaev's psychological canvases («Саукеле», «Кочевые»); the new generation (Madanova, Khairullina, Balieva, Kazgulova), Mergenov, sculptor Dalbaev; the practical — contour cut-outs of Kazakh symbols composed into abstract vertical and horizontal compositions; what it teaches: the A. Kasteev State Museum of Arts in Almaty — over 20,000 originals of modern and ancient applied art, twelve halls, six of painting, Kasteev's own works in a separate hall; the ancient-art hall opening the display — rock engravings and petroglyphs, applied art, monumental and small plastic of the Palaeolithic, Bronze and early Iron ages, medieval architectural decor, and the museum's unique collection of Kazakh jewellery, carved and inlaid wood and bone; the world's great galleries named beside it
7. **Kazakhstan Grade 7 Artistic Labour (boys, Chukalin)** — what it teaches: Askhat Akhmedyarov's installation «Әженің көзі» («Grandmother's Eye») — 32 cauldrons (казан) joined into two spheres, a necklace: in Kazakh, әженің көзі is the keepsake a grandmother passes to her granddaughters — the cauldron of the family hearth read as a bead of that necklace, a lifelong amulet like a pearl or coral bead; what it teaches: Kazakhstan's art since independence in three directions — the symbolic method, conceptual devices and realism — a refusal of stereotypes and a search for new readings of Kazakh life, nature and history; Duzelkhanov's realistic gallery of khans and heroes («Томирис»); Burdesbekov's «Сыргалы қыз», a steppe madonna in jewellery, built of abstract colour and light; Akanaev's psychological canvases («Саукеле», «Кочевые»); the new generation (Madanova, Khairullina, Balieva, Kazgulova), Mergenov, sculptor Dalbaev; the practical — contour cut-outs of Kazakh symbols composed into abstract vertical and horizontal compositions; what it teaches: the A. Kasteev State Museum of Arts in Almaty — over 20,000 originals of modern and ancient applied art, twelve halls, six of painting, Kasteev's own works in a separate hall; the ancient-art hall opening the display — rock engravings and petroglyphs, applied art, monumental and small plastic of the Palaeolithic, Bronze and early Iron ages, medieval architectural decor, and the museum's unique collection of Kazakh jewellery, carved and inlaid wood and bone; the world's great galleries named beside it
8. **Kazakhstan Grade 7 Artistic Labour 2025 (girls, Alimsaeva)** — what it teaches: contemporary art of Kazakhstan is presented in the country's leading museums — at the National Museum of Arts of the Republic of Kazakhstan named after A. Kasteev in Almaty many works of classics and of contemporary artists can be seen, the collection marked by variety, artistic value and depth, letting one feel the distinctness and many-sidedness of the national artistic tradition; the contemporary-art hall of the National Museum of the Republic of Kazakhstan in Astana serves as an important ground for showing the achievements of Kazakhstani art of the last decades, its space equipped with contemporary multimedia technologies, making acquaintance with the exposition fuller and interactive
9. **Kazakhstan Grade 7 Artistic Labour 2025 (boys, Chukalin)** — what it teaches: Kazakhstan's contemporary art is presented in the country's leading museums — in the National Museum of Arts of the Republic of Kazakhstan named after A. Kasteev in Almaty one can see many works of both classics and contemporary artists; the museum's collection is distinguished by diversity, artistic value and depth, letting one feel the distinctness and many-sidedness of the national artistic tradition; the Hall of Contemporary Art in the National Museum of the Republic of Kazakhstan in Astana serves as an important ground for presenting the achievements of Kazakhstani art of the last decades, and the space is equipped with contemporary multimedia technologies, which makes acquaintance with the exposition richer and more interactive; each of these museums demonstrates the wealth and diversity of Kazakhstan's contemporary art, opening for visitors the possibility to get acquainted with the creativity of the country's talented artists; the research work: using different sources, independently get acquainted with the creativity of contemporary Kazakhstani artists (of your choice) — define the genres and directions in which they work, carry out a comparative analysis of their works, and pick out similarities and differences; the practical work: create your picture using characteristic elements of the chosen artist's style (for example his technique or colour palette) — use national motifs in the work; explain why you chose exactly this artist; analyse how the artist's style was reflected in your work: which elements you adopted, and with the help of which you added your work's uniqueness; estimate how successfully you managed to depict the artist's style in your work
10. **Kazakhstan Grade 9 NIS Art (Akhmetkalieva)** — what it teaches: Karipbek Kuyukov is a special artist — he gives heat, he paints pictures holding the brush in his mouth, working with his head's... (he paints with the brush held in his teeth/mouth); the artist, himself a victim of nuclear tests and thinking of the future beforehand, to draw the viewer's attention chooses clear, bright, saturated colours; in his «Жарылыс» (Explosion) picture the light... the Creator is shown as a hand stretched from the sky saving the world from a nuclear explosion, calling to peace; after this picture's... at the 2016 summit on nuclear safety held in the USA (?) the work was gifted to Barack Obama by Nursultan Nazarbayev; his bright sun-ray pictures are pictured
