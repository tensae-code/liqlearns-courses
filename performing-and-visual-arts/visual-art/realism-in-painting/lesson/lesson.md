# Realism in Painting

## Do it first

Look at the sub lesson list. Pick the part you already know something about, and say what you know. Then start at Sub lesson 1 — every part builds on the one before it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Realism

Realism: Courbet painting workers' unadorned reality as social protest (Folha 4, Brazil's *ARTE — Ensino Médio*, printed p. 70).
