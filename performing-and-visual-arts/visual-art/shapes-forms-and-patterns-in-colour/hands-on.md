# Hands-on — Shapes, Forms and Patterns in Colour

The lesson with your hands, your feet and your voice together, not only your eyes.

## Pattern building
With pebbles, caps or cut papers, build the book's kinds of patterns: a shape-and-colour sequence, a two-colour repeat, a pattern from repeated shapes. Photograph or draw each before you sweep it away.

## Practising in our locality
Go outside, as the Grade 2 guide's locality practice directs: look at the shapes, forms and colours of your own place — roofs, doors, fields, clothes — and draw three of them where you stand.
