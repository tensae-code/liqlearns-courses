# Shapes, Forms and Patterns in Colour — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A shape is…
  a) The closed outline of a thing, made by joined lines b) Any sound a thing makes c) A colour's name
**Answer: a) The closed outline of a thing, made by joined lines** — shape is the closed outline joined lines make.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which list names geometric forms?
  a) Sphere, cylinder, cone, cube b) Circle, square, line, dot c) Red, green, blue, yellow
**Answer: a) Sphere, cylinder, cone, cube** — the solid geometric forms are sphere, cylinder, cone and cube.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A pattern is made by…
  a) Shapes and colours arranged in order b) One shape drawn once c) Colours mixed at random
**Answer: a) Shapes and colours arranged in order** — patterns arrange flat shapes and colours in order.

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** In the activity, after stating the pictures' message, you express it in…
  a) Drama and drawing b) Song only c) Numbers only
**Answer: a) Drama and drawing** — state the message, then express it in drama and drawing.

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** The locality practice happens…
  a) Outdoors, in the children's own locality b) Only inside a museum c) Only from photographs
**Answer: a) Outdoors, in the children's own locality** — the practices are carried on in the locality, outdoors.

**6.** (Sub lesson 6) Fig. 5.13 shows —
a) a line-and-colour veranda  b) a mountain  c) a portrait
**Answer: a) a line-and-colour veranda.**

**7.** (Sub lesson 6) In Fig. 5.14, the colour-painting composition appears when —
a) the page is folded  b) corridors are placed  c) the lights go out
**Answer: b) corridors are placed.**

**8. (Sub lesson 6)** Which of these is a shape, not a form?
  a) A flat triangle drawn on paper b) A wooden box c) A clay pot
**Answer: a) A flat triangle drawn on paper** — the exercise's shape vs form contrast — the flat triangle is the shape.
