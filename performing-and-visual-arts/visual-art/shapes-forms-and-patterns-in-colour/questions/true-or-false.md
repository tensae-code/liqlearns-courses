# Shapes, Forms and Patterns in Colour — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Natural shapes can be read from silhouettes of trees, animals and birds.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A form has width only, like a shape.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A divided circle can teach fractions.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Letter-writing forms in English and Amharic are shown together in this part.
**Answer: True**

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** This part is cited at section level because the guide is a scan with no text layer.
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 6)** In the Grade 4 book's exercise, shapes and forms can be distinguished by colour.
**Answer: True** — distinguishing shapes and forms by colour is a Chapter 1 aim the exercise checks.
