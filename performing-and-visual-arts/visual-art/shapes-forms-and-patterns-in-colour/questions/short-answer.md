# Shapes, Forms and Patterns in Colour — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name two geometric shapes the book names.
**Answer: Any two of: triangle, rectangle, rhombus, parallelogram.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How are household utensils' shapes shown in the activity?
**Answer: In different colour grades.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The circle is divided into 4 equal parts and 1 part is blue. What fraction is blue?
**Answer: One quarter — 1 of the 4 equal parts.**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** What is a pictorial composition?
**Answer: A story or essay told in pictures.**

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** What two things does the chapter join?
**Answer: Perceiving (looking and noticing) and practising (doing it in your locality).**
