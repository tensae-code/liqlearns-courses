## Sub lesson 1 — Vietnam Grade 4 Mĩ thuật 4

The đình honours the village's Thành Hoàng (tutelary god) and its heroes; its carved decoration pictures the life the villagers knew — bathing at the lotus pond, tug of war, boat racing, cockfighting, wrestling, chess, singing — familiar animals, and sacred creatures: dragons, phoenixes, a mother dragon entwined with her young, fairies riding dragons; named carvings include "Fairies Bathing at the Lotus Pond" (Đồng Viên đình, Hà Nội), "Elephant Driving off the Tiger" (Chảy đình, Ninh Bình) and the stone dog of the Địch Vĩ đình–pagoda complex

## Sub lesson 2 — Vietnam Grade 11 Mĩ thuật 11 — Kiến trúc

If fine art plays the foundation role for interior's development, interior in turn creates the spaces to display fine-art products
