This lesson is Dinh Lang Communal House Carving.

Dinh Lang Communal House Carving — its forms, history and cultural meaning, taught as one topic lesson. — as Vietnam's school books teach it: The đình honours the village's Thành Hoàng (tutelary god) and its heroes; its carved decoration pictures the life the villagers knew — bathing at the lotus pond, tug of war, boat racing, cockfighting, wrestling, chess, singing — familiar animals, and sacred creatures: dragons, phoenixes, a mother dragon entwined with her young, fairies riding dragons; named carvings include "Fairies Bathing at the Lotus Pond" (Đồng Viên đình, Hà Nội), "Elephant Driving off the Tiger" (Chảy đình, Ninh Bình) and the stone dog of the Địch Vĩ đình–pagoda complex.

Watch, listen — then say it back in your own words.
