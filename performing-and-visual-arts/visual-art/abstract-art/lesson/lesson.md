# Abstract Art

## Do it first

Quick start: from the title alone, guess what this lesson's work is. Then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — Create your own abstract art

Abstract art does not copy a visible thing. In Bhutan's Class III workbook the lesson is plain: **create your own abstract art** — the picture comes from you, not from an object placed in front of you.

## Sub lesson 2 — Let it dry and talk about your art

When the art is made, **let it dry**. Then **talk about your art to the class** — the making and the telling are both parts of the lesson.
