# Abstract Art — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Abstract art copies the object in front of you.
**Answer: False.** — it is your own creation, not a copy of a visible thing.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The workbook lets children create their own abstract art.
**Answer: True.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The artwork should be talked about while it is still wet.
**Answer: False.** — let it dry first, then talk about it.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Talking about your art is part of the lesson.
**Answer: True.**
