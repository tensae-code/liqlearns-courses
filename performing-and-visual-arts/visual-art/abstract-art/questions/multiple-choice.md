# Abstract Art — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Abstract art in the workbook lesson is…
a) a picture you create yourself, not a copy of a visible thing  b) a copy of an object on the table  c) a photograph
**Answer: a) a picture you create yourself, not a copy of a visible thing**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Who invents the abstract picture?
a) the teacher  b) the child who makes it  c) nobody — it is traced
**Answer: b) the child who makes it**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** After creating the abstract art, you first…
a) throw it away  b) let it dry  c) colour over it
**Answer: b) let it dry**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The lesson ends with…
a) a test  b) talking about your art to the class  c) packing the paints
**Answer: b) talking about your art to the class**

**5. (Sub lesson 1)** Abstract art is composed of…
a) shapes and colours for their own sake  b) only straight lines  c) only words
**Answer: a) shapes and colours for their own sake**
