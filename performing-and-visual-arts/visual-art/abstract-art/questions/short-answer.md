# Abstract Art — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the child create in Lesson 10 of the Bhutan Class III workbook?
**Answer: Their own abstract art.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What two things happen after the art is created?
**Answer: It is let to dry, and the child talks about it to the class.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** To whom do you talk about your art?
**Answer: To the class.**
