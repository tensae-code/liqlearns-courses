# Lines in Every Direction — short answer

Answer in a short sentence, or by doing what the question asks.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Do it: draw a row of U shapes — down, round the bottom, up — without stopping between them.
**Answer: A row of joined or repeated U cups, each in one stroke.**

**2. (Sub lesson 4)** Answer the teacher guide's question in your own words: what is a line?
**Answer: A line is the path the pencil travels (any answer that says the pencil's journey/path is right).**
