# Lines in Every Direction — true or false

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A vertical stroke stands up and down.
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A U shape is drawn in one flowing journey of the pencil.
**Answer: True.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In the pattern skill, you lift the pencil after every tooth of the zigzag.
**Answer: False — the pencil stays on the paper through the whole zigzag and wave.**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** The teacher guide asks "What is a line?" at the start of its line lesson.
**Answer: True — and its order is: warm up the hand, draw lines, colour.**
