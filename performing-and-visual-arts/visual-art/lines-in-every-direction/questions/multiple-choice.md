# Lines in Every Direction — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A line that runs across the page, flat, is —
a) vertical  b) horizontal  c) diagonal
**Answer: b) horizontal.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A slanting line, like a hillside, is —
a) diagonal  b) vertical  c) curved
**Answer: a) diagonal.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A curved line is a line that —
a) snaps at a corner  b) bends and flows  c) stands still
**Answer: b) bends and flows — like a smile, or the letter U.**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Sharp teeth, up and down, make the pattern called —
a) wave  b) zigzag  c) loops
**Answer: b) zigzag.**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Rows of overlapping oval shapes make —
a) loop patterns  b) straight lines  c) squares
**Answer: a) loop patterns.**

**6. (Sub lesson 4)** The teacher guide's order for line work is —
a) colour, line, warm up  b) warm up the hand, draw lines, colour  c) colour only
**Answer: b) warm up the hand, draw lines, colour.**
