# Video script — Lines in Every Direction

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A line is a journey.
Your pencil takes the trip.

Across — like a road.
Down — like rain.
Slanting — like a hill.
Curved — like a smile.

Again! Zigzag — teeth!
Again! Wave — water!
Loops — a row of eggs!

Do not lift the pencil.
The boat does not fly.
