# Hands-on — Lines in Every Direction


The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Lines on the ground
Outside, draw the directions BIG: drag a stick in dust or use chalk on a wall or floor — a zigzag path to walk along, a wave path to tiptoe. The feet learn the directions the hand is learning.

## The direction game
One person calls a direction — across! down! slanting! curved! — the other draws it at once, big and fast. Five calls each, then swap. Speed is fine here; the models are for the slow pages.

## Pattern borders
Decorate the edge of a drawing or a card with a pattern border: zigzag on one side, wave on the next, loops along the bottom. Patterns are how lines become decoration.
