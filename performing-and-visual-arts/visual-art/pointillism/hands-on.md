# Hands-on — Pointillism

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Dot patch
Draw a small square. Fill half with dots pressed close, half with dots spread wide. Step back: which half looks darker? That is tone from fusion.

## A pointillist fruit
Draw a fruit lightly in graphite. Colour it only with dots — no lines, no rubbing — until its light and shadow appear.
