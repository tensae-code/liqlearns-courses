# Pointillism — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Pointillism begins with…
a) a light graphite drawing  b) oil paint  c) a photograph
**Answer: a) a light graphite drawing**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In pointillism, colour and tone come from…
a) rubbing with cotton  b) the fusion of the dots  c) charcoal
**Answer: b) the fusion of the dots**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Pointillism asks for…
a) speed  b) patience and observation  c) a big brush
**Answer: b) patience and observation**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** The drawing underneath is made…
a) freehand  b) with a ruler only  c) by tracing a screen
**Answer: a) freehand**
