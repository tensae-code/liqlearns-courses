# Pointillism — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** In pointillism, shadow can be made by pressing dots closer together.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Pointillism colours the drawing with big flat areas of paint.
**Answer: False** — it colours with dots or small marks.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Pointillism is also called pontilhismo in the book's Portuguese.
**Answer: True**
