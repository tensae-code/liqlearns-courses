# Pointillism

## Do it first

Quick game: look at a photograph in a newspaper up close — it is made of tiny dots! Pointillism does the same thing by hand. Try three dots close together, then look from far away.

## Sub lesson 1 — Drawing first, in light graphite

**Pointillism (pontilhismo)** begins plainly: **draw freehand in light graphite on paper** — the drawing underneath stays light, because the dots will do the strong work.

## Sub lesson 2 — Colouring with dots

Then **colour the drawing with dots or small marks all around it**, so that **colour and tone — light and shadow — come from the fusion of the dots**.

It is a technique that asks for **patience and observation**: dot by dot, the picture gathers itself.
