# Video script — Pointillism

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Dots! Dots! Dots!

First — a light drawing. Pencil, soft, freehand.
Then — colour it with dots. Small marks, all around.
Step back! The dots fuse. Light, shadow, colour — from dots alone.

Pointillism. Patience wins.
