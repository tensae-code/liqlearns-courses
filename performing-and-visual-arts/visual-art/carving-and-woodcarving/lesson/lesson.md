## Sub lesson 1 — Технологии в декоративно-приложните изкуства, Карвинг (Carving) — part one

carving (from "curve" — to cut): carving, a carved ornament or a cut figure — an ancient art of artistic cutting on the most varied materials by kind and structure (wood, stone, ice, soap), and the interesting carving on fruits and vegetables that turns them into true works of art; arising in Southeast Asia some 700 years ago (the Thai legend of the palace servant who carved fruits and vegetables into flowers for the Festival of Floating Lanterns, then birds and a lotus composition that won first prize), it spread through Europe in the 20th century, each country using its characteristic techniques and thematic ornaments;

## Sub lesson 2 — Технологии в декоративно-приложните изкуства, Карвинг (Carving) — part two

suitable are all fruits and vegetables with a smooth, fresh surface (apples, citrus, melons, potatoes), combined with flowers and leafy-decorative plants, toys, chocolate figures, seeds and grains; the cutting is done with different instruments — most widespread the thin, narrow "Thai" knife, together with cutters of different forms (V-shaped, square, round, wide and narrow knives), the thin edges shaped with scissors, round forms cut with small spoons, and grooves on the surface made with an engraving knife, details joined with toothpicks and other auxiliary materials; the art of carving demands patience, concentration of attention, precision and a skilful hand — shown in a carving lesson at a Sofia school and in the pupils' own food-art works
