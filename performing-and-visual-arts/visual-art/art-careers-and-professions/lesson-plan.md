# Lesson plan — Art Careers and Professions

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Visual Art · **Lesson:** Art Careers and Professions
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 2 — Visual Art (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The visual-art industry in real life — its professions, businesses, production processes and the work artists actually do.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the course's earlier lessons in its title; recommended first, never forced.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of ..." in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Bab 1 Wawasan Industri Bidang Seni Rupa** — insight into the visual-art industry — its sectors, workplaces and how art is produced and sold as an industry
2. **Bab 2 Peluang Bisnis dan Profesi Bidang Seni Rupa** — business opportunities and professions in visual art — the named jobs and enterprises an art graduate can enter
3. **Bab 3 Proses Produksi Bidang Seni Rupa** — the production process in visual art — from idea and materials through making to a finished, presentable product
4. **Bab I Wawasan Industri Seni Rupa** — the visual-art creative industry, the development of digital technology in it, and the kinds of intellectual property (kekayaan intelektual)
5. **Bab II Peluang Bisnis dan Profesi Bidang Seni Rupa** — creativity and innovation, creativity-based business, startups, art-business management, and the professions of the visual-art field
6. **Bab III Proses Produksi Bidang Seni Rupa** — how artworks are produced — the production processes of painting, sculpture and illustration, each step by step

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
