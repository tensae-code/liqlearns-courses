# Video script — Art Careers and Professions

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This is Indonesia's own lesson.

**Bab 1 Wawasan Industri Bidang Seni Rupa.** insight into the visual-art industry — its sectors, workplaces and how art is produced and sold as an industry.

**Bab 2 Peluang Bisnis dan Profesi Bidang Seni Rupa.** business opportunities and professions in visual art — the named jobs and enterprises an art graduate can enter.

**Bab 3 Proses Produksi Bidang Seni Rupa.** the production process in visual art — from idea and materials through making to a finished, presentable product.

**Bab I Wawasan Industri Seni Rupa.** the visual-art creative industry, the development of digital technology in it, and the kinds of intellectual property (kekayaan intelektual).

**Bab II Peluang Bisnis dan Profesi Bidang Seni Rupa.** creativity and innovation, creativity-based business, startups, art-business management, and the professions of the visual-art field.

**Bab III Proses Produksi Bidang Seni Rupa.** how artworks are produced — the production processes of painting, sculpture and illustration, each step by step.

That is Art Careers and Professions — Indonesia's way, kept by name.
