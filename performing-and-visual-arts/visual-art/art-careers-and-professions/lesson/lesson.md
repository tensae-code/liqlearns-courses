# Art Careers and Professions

## Do it first

Name one thing that belongs in this lesson — The visual-art industry in real life — its professions, businesses, production processes and the work artists actually do. Say what it is in your own words. If you can name one, you are already inside the lesson.

## Sub lesson 1 — Bab 1 Wawasan Industri Bidang Seni Rupa

insight into the visual-art industry — its sectors, workplaces and how art is produced and sold as an industry.

## Sub lesson 2 — Bab 2 Peluang Bisnis dan Profesi Bidang Seni Rupa

business opportunities and professions in visual art — the named jobs and enterprises an art graduate can enter.

## Sub lesson 3 — Bab 3 Proses Produksi Bidang Seni Rupa

the production process in visual art — from idea and materials through making to a finished, presentable product.

## Sub lesson 4 — Bab I Wawasan Industri Seni Rupa

the visual-art creative industry, the development of digital technology in it, and the kinds of intellectual property (kekayaan intelektual).

## Sub lesson 5 — Bab II Peluang Bisnis dan Profesi Bidang Seni Rupa

creativity and innovation, creativity-based business, startups, art-business management, and the professions of the visual-art field.

## Sub lesson 6 — Bab III Proses Produksi Bidang Seni Rupa

how artworks are produced — the production processes of painting, sculpture and illustration, each step by step.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson holds, part by part, in your own words. Companion checks: did every part get named, with its Indonesian name kept where the book gives one?

## Do It Yourself

1. Choose one named part of this lesson and find out one more true thing about it — from a person who knows it, or from the book your part came from.
2. Make or perform a small piece in the way of that part: a rhythm, a step, a drawing, a shaped piece of clay.
3. Write one sentence: what does this part show about Indonesia's own way of teaching its arts?
