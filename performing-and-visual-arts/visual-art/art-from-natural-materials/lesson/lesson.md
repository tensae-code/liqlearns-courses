# Art from Natural Materials

## Do it first

Start with the first part below — do it, sing it or make it before you read any explanation.

## Sub lesson 1 — Part II: Навчаар урлах (Crafting with leaves)

Part II: Навчаар урлах (Crafting with leaves) — a fish, a rabbit and flowers assembled from dried leaves, a poplar tree leafed with real leaves, and talking about why a leaf's colour changes

## Sub lesson 2 — Part II: Өвс, ургамлаар бүтээл хийх (Creating with grass and plants)

Part II: Өвс, ургамлаар бүтээл хийх (Creating with grass and plants) — a spider with a clay body and twig legs (a small spider's legs from grass), and a crane built from leaves and grass — a leaf body, a trimmed head and neck, grass legs and feathers — the appliqué leaves sealed with tape before they dry

## Sub lesson 3 — Part II: Навч, өвсөн наамал (Leaf and grass appliqué)

Part II: Навч, өвсөн наамал (Leaf and grass appliqué) — gathering and cleaning leaves and pressing them flat between book pages; a mouse glued from whole and halved leaves, with ears, whiskers and eyes; scissor safety rules pictured
