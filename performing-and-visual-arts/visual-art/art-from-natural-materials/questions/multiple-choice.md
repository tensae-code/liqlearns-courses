# Art from Natural Materials — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Leaf crafts in the Grade 1 book build…
a) a fish, a rabbit and flowers from dried leaves  b) a house from bricks  c) a car from metal
**Answer: a) a fish, a rabbit and flowers from dried leaves**

**2.** Leaves for appliqué are first…
a) gathered, cleaned and pressed flat between book pages  b) painted silver  c) burned
**Answer: a) gathered, cleaned and pressed flat between book pages**

**3.** The crane in Grade 2 is built from…
a) leaves and grass — a leaf body, grass legs and feathers  b) plastic  c) wool only
**Answer: a) leaves and grass — a leaf body, grass legs and feathers**
