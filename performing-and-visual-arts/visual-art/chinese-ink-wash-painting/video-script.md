This lesson is Chinese Ink Wash Painting.

Chinese ink wash painting — playing and working in ink and coloured ink, the water-and-ink art of China. — as China's school books teach it: Ink-wash play — the tools (ink stick, xuan paper, brush, inkstone, seal) and ink's tones from burnt to clear; painting with dark/light and wet/dry ink changes, after Cui Zifan and Zhang Daqian's splashed-ink landscape.

Watch, listen — then say it back in your own words.
