Тенерифе тәсілімен тоқылған кілем (A carpet woven with the Tenerife method) — what it teaches: this method was invented by the Spanish in the XVI century (бұл тәсілді XVI ғасырда испандықтар ойлап тапқан), and in South America it is called «бразилиялық шілтер» (Brazilian lace); a hexagonal motif is woven on a hexagonal wooden frame in eighteen photographed steps: the design diagram (a hexagonal snowflake-like motif of dots) and its coloured version (red and green) are shown; green threads are stretched across the frame's pegs in three directions, making a triangular grid over the whole hexagon; a red thread is then woven round and round the centre, building a star medallion, the crossings are secured, the finished hexagon is cut from the frame and its edges finished — a red-and-green hexagonal woven medallion (student book) | Book 1, printed p. 24 |
