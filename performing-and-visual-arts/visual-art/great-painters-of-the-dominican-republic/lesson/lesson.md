# Great Painters of the Dominican Republic

## Do it first

Quick start: Open the book. Before any lesson — a master is waiting. Then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — The great painters, met by name

The Grade 4 book honours **Dominican painting**: **Celeste Woss y Gil** and **Elsa Núñez** are named as **the country's great painters** — with Miró and Kandinsky beside them — and their works are **read for primary, secondary and complementary colours**. The book itself opens with **Leopoldo Pérez's “Paisaje de montaña” (1986)** as its frontispiece artwork.

## Sub lesson 2 — The books' opening artworks

Every year's book opens with a master. **Clara Ledesma's** works open the Grade 5 book; **Roberto Flores's “Violoncelista”** (oil on canvas, 1992) opens Grade 6; **Francisco Santos's “Palomas”** (1997) opens Grade 7, and his **untitled work (1988, Banco Central collection)** opens Grade 9; **Jaime Colson's** works open Grade 11 — the master whose “Merengue” was studied at Grade 7; and **Elsa Núñez's** works open Grade 12, closing the series with one of its two named great painters.
