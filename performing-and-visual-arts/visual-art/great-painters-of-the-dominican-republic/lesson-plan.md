# Lesson plan — Great Painters of the Dominican Republic

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Visual Art · **Lesson:** Great Painters of the Dominican Republic
**Place in the curriculum:** Visual Art (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the Dominican Republic's great painters and paintresses, met through their works — Celeste Woss y Gil and Elsa Núñez honoured by name, and every Libro Abierto art book opening with a Dominican master's artwork: Pérez, Ledesma, Flores, Santos, Colson, Núñez.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this course in the same title.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The great painters, met by name** — Dominican painting honoured: Celeste Woss y Gil and Elsa Núñez named as the country's great painters (with Miró and Kandinsky beside them), their works read for primary, secondary and complementary colours; the book opens with Leopoldo Pérez's “Paisaje de montaña” (1986) as its frontispiece artwork.
2. **The books' opening artworks** — the frontispieces: Clara Ledesma's works open the Grade 5 book; Roberto Flores's “Violoncelista” (1992) the Grade 6; Francisco Santos's “Palomas” (1997) the Grade 7 and his untitled work (1988) the Grade 9; Jaime Colson's works the Grade 11; Elsa Núñez's works the Grade 12.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
