# Great Painters of the Dominican Republic — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Elsa Núñez is a Dominican painter.
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The Libro Abierto art books open with a Dominican artwork.
**Answer: True.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The “Violoncelista” is a photograph.
**Answer: False.**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Woss y Gil and Núñez are honoured as great painters of the country.
**Answer: True.**
