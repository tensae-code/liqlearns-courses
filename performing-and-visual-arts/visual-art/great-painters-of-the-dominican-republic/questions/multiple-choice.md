# Great Painters of the Dominican Republic — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Two painters named as the country's great painters are…
a) Celeste Woss y Gil and Elsa Núñez  b) Miró and Kandinsky  c) two sculptors
**Answer: a) Celeste Woss y Gil and Elsa Núñez**.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Their works are read for…
a) primary, secondary and complementary colours  b) prices  c) sizes only
**Answer: a) primary, secondary and complementary colours**.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The Grade 4 book opens with…
a) Leopoldo Pérez's “Paisaje de montaña” (1986)  b) a photograph  c) a map
**Answer: a) Leopoldo Pérez's “Paisaje de montaña” (1986)**.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The Grade 6 book's opening artwork is…
a) Roberto Flores's “Violoncelista”  b) a still life by Colson  c) a mural
**Answer: a) Roberto Flores's “Violoncelista”**.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Francisco Santos's “Palomas” (1997) opens…
a) the Grade 7 book  b) the Grade 2 book  c) the Grade 12 book
**Answer: a) the Grade 7 book**.
