# Hands-on — Great Painters of the Dominican Republic

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Read a painting for colour
Take one Dominican painting: list the primary, secondary and complementary colours you can find in it, the way the Grade 4 book reads the masters.

## Frontispiece gallery
Make a gallery strip: one small copy or drawing for each frontispiece — Pérez, Ledesma, Flores, Santos (twice), Colson, Núñez — labelled with its book's grade.
