# Lesson plan — Geometric and Technical Drawing

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Visual Art · **Lesson:** Geometric and Technical Drawing
**Place in the curriculum:** Visual Art (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the curved constructions of geometric drawing, traced step by step with ruler and compass: spirals of two, three and four centres; the óvulo and the oval; and the conic curves — the ellipse by the foci method, the parabola and the hyperbola by intersecting arcs — with compositions built from them for carpentry, metalwork, textiles and ornament.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this course.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Tracing spirals** — spirals of two, three and four centres
2. **The óvulo and the oval** — the egg-shaped óvulo and the oval from its major axis
3. **Conic curves and the ellipse** — conics from cutting a cone; the ellipse by the foci method
4. **The parabola and the hyperbola** — parabola and hyperbola by intersecting arcs

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier Visual Art lessons of this course. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Trace spirals of two, three and four centres, and the óvulo and oval.
2. Construct an ellipse by the foci method, and a parabola and hyperbola by intersecting arcs.

## What you need
Ruler, compass, pencil and paper — and patience for freehand joining.
