# Hands-on — Geometric and Technical Drawing

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Spiral of two centres
Trace a two-centre spiral step by step, alternating the compass point between the centres. Then try three centres.

## Ellipse by foci
Pin two foci, loop a string around them, and draw the ellipse the gardener's way; then repeat with the book's foci method and join the points freehand.

## Composition
Build one decorative composition from a spiral and an óvulo — the kind used in carpentry, metalwork or textiles.
