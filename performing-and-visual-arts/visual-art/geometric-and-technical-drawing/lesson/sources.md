| Mozambique — Lesson 23, "Tracing Conic Curves": the conics and the ellipse — conic curves as the figures made by cutting a cone with a plane: the ellipse (oblique cut), the parabola, the hyperbola, and the circle itself as a conic; the ellipse defined and its elements named (major and minor axes, vertices, foci, focal distance, centre), an ellipse being what a circle looks like in perspective, as in plates, basins and pots; the ellipse drawn by the foci method, step by step (the gardener's, rectangle and circumference methods named), the points joined freehand (student book; original target `visual-art/conic-curves-and-the-ellipse`) | Book 1, printed pp. 68–74 |
| Mozambique — Lesson 24, "Conic Curves (continued)": the parabola and the hyperbola — the parabola defined, its elements (directrix, axis, focus, parameter, vertex) and its construction by intersecting arcs from the focus onto lines parallel to the directrix, the points joined freehand (the satellite dish as its everyday form); the hyperbola defined as an open two-branch curve, its elements (axis, vertices, foci) and its construction by the same intersecting-arc method (pots and hourglasses as its everyday forms); compositions from the three conics (student book; original target `visual-art/parabola-and-hyperbola`) | Book 1, printed pp. 76–83 |
