# Geometric and Technical Drawing — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A spiral can be traced with two, three or four centres.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The oval is traced from its given major axis.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The circle is not a conic curve.
**Answer: False** — the circle itself is a conic — a cut of the cone.
