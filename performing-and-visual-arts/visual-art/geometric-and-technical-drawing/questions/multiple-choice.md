# Geometric and Technical Drawing — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A spiral is a curve that…
a) winds around a point, moving further from it at every turn  b) is a closed egg shape  c) never moves
**Answer: a) winds around a point, moving further from it at every turn**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The óvulo is traced from…
a) its major axis  b) a given span, with four centres  c) two foci
**Answer: b) a given span, with four centres**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Conic curves are made by…
a) cutting a cone with a plane  b) folding paper  c) rotating a module
**Answer: a) cutting a cone with a plane**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** The hyperbola is…
a) a closed egg curve  b) an open two-branch curve  c) a spiral
**Answer: b) an open two-branch curve**
