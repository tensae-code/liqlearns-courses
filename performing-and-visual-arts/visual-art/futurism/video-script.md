**Book 1, Artistic Culture, "The Futurist School".** Futurism — born in Milan in 1909, a political, social and artistic movement against the past, its manifesto praising war and calling to demolish the old cities; Marinetti's racing car declared more beautiful than the Nike of Samothrace; its two kinetic bases, bodies moving in space and spirit moving in body, learned from photography and cinema; Boccioni's three new elements; and, in its wake, De Chirico's Metaphysical painting — still nature estranged in wide empty space, turned to mysterious symbols
