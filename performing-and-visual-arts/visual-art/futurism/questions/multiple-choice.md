# Futurism — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Futurism was born in…
a) Milan in 1909  b) Paris in 1500  c) Cairo in 1800
**Answer: a) Milan in 1909** — a political, social and artistic movement against the past.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Marinetti declared a racing car more beautiful than…
a) the Nike of Samothrace  b) a horse  c) a cathedral
**Answer: a) the Nike of Samothrace**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Futurism's kinetic bases were learned from…
a) photography and cinema  b) weaving  c) pottery
**Answer: a) photography and cinema** — bodies moving in space and spirit moving in body.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** De Chirico's Metaphysical painting shows…
a) still nature estranged in wide empty space  b) busy markets  c) portraits only
**Answer: a) still nature estranged in wide empty space** — turned to mysterious symbols.
