# Futurism — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What did the Futurist manifesto praise and call for?
**Answer: It praised war and called to demolish the old cities.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the Futurist sculptor whose three new elements the book names.
**Answer: Boccioni.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** What are Futurism's two kinetic bases?
**Answer: Bodies moving in space, and spirit moving in body.**
