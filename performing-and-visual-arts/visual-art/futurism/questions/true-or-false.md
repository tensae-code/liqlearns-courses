# Futurism — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Futurism was a movement against the past.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Futurism worshipped speed, machines and movement.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Marinetti praised the old cities and asked to keep them.
**Answer: False** — The manifesto called to demolish the old cities.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** De Chirico painted in Futurism's wake.
**Answer: True**
