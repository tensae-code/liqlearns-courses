# Lesson plan — Maldivian Crafts and Dress

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Visual Art · **Lesson:** Maldivian Crafts and Dress
**Place in the curriculum:** Visual Art (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the Maldives' own crafts and dress — coconut-leaf weaving for useful and festive things, and the traditional dress with its kasabu-woven libaas and striped feyli.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier making and drawing lessons of this course.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Coconut-leaf weaving** — leaves trimmed, rolled, folded, plaited and woven into useful and decorative things — fences (thoshali), mats, baskets, fans, balls, birds and fish, and the koadi and bodumas made to celebrate Eid; weaving a fish from two leaf strips in the over-and-under pattern.
2. **Maldivian traditional dress** — the traditional dress studied in Aishath Shirhan's fabric-and-fibre doll — the libaas with its hand-woven kasabu threads, the black-and-white striped feyli skirt, and the hair bun worn on the right side of the head.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier drawing and making lessons of this course. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name things coconut leaves are woven into, and the pattern used for the woven fish.
2. Name the parts of the traditional dress the doll shows.

## What you need
Two strips of coconut leaf or paper, scissors.
