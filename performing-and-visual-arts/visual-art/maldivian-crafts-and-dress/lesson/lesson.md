# Maldivian Crafts and Dress

## Do it first

Quick game: with two strips of paper, go over, under, over, under. You are already weaving the way this lesson weaves a leaf.

## Sub lesson 1 — Coconut-leaf weaving

The Maldives' own **coconut-leaf weaving** trims, rolls, folds, plaits and weaves leaves into **useful and decorative things**: **fences (thoshali), mats, baskets, fans, balls, birds and fish** — and the **koadi and bodumas made to celebrate Eid**. The book teaches the making step by step: **weaving a fish from two leaf strips in the over-and-under pattern**.

## Sub lesson 2 — Maldivian traditional dress

The book studies the **Maldivian traditional dress** in **Aishath Shirhan's fabric-and-fibre doll**: the **libaas** with its **hand-woven kasabu threads**, the **black-and-white striped feyli skirt**, and the **hair bun worn on the right side of the head**. Dress, made and worn the Maldivian way, is art you can hold.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson taught, part by part. Companion checks each part against the sub lessons above.

## Do It Yourself

1. Make your own piece using this lesson's way, from start to finish.
2. Show it to the class and say, in the book's own closing words, talk about your art: what you made, how, and what you learnt.
3. Write one sentence: what will you do differently next time?
