# Maldivian Crafts and Dress — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** What threads decorate the libaas?
**Answer: Hand-woven kasabu threads.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** On which side is the hair bun worn?
**Answer: On the right side of the head.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Name three things woven from coconut leaves.
**Answer: Any three of: fences (thoshali), mats, baskets, fans, balls, birds, fish.**
