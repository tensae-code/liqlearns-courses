# Maldivian Crafts and Dress — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Coconut leaves are woven by…
a) trimming, rolling, folding, plaiting and weaving  b) melting them  c) painting only
**Answer: a) trimming, rolling, folding, plaiting and weaving**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A thoshali is a woven…
a) hat  b) fence  c) drum
**Answer: b) fence**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Koadi and bodumas are made to celebrate…
a) Eid  b) the new school year  c) harvest only
**Answer: a) Eid**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** The woven fish is made from two leaf strips in…
a) a knot-only pattern  b) the over-and-under pattern  c) a spiral
**Answer: b) the over-and-under pattern**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The feyli skirt is…
a) plain red  b) black-and-white striped  c) gold
**Answer: b) black-and-white striped**
