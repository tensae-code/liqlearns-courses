# Maldivian Crafts and Dress — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Coconut leaves can be woven into baskets and fans.
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The woven fish uses the over-and-under pattern.
**Answer: True.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The feyli skirt is black-and-white striped.
**Answer: True.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The hair bun is worn on the left side of the head.
**Answer: False — it is worn on the right side.**
