The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

This is Bolivia's own lesson.

**Origin architecture and Tiwanaku sculpture.** Bolivia's first building and its stone sculpture: the Ponce and Bennett monoliths, the Puerta del Sol, the Kalasasaya temple; Tiwanaku, flourishing between the 5th and 11th centuries; Andean architecture today; and adobe, the building material of the west (teacher compendium — evidence).

**The aguayo, featherwork and basketry.** The aguayo, one of the most used textiles of the culture, drawn and painted figure by figure; featherwork (plumarios), a ceremonial art bound to sacred activities; and palm-leaf basketry (cesteria) of the lake, river, rice and coffee communities.

**Pigments, rock art and the first cultures.** Pigments obtained from nature — eucalyptus, walnut, cochineal, coloured clays; rock art in Bolivia, more than 400 registered SIARB sites; the pre-Columbian cultures Wancarani, Chiripa, Tiwanaku and Uru Chipaya; and appreciating the art of the origin peoples.

That is Bolivian Origin Arts — Bolivia's way, kept by name.
