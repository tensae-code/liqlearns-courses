# Bolivian Origin Arts — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — Origin architecture and Tiwanaku sculpture
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Bolivia's first building and its stone sculpture: the Ponce and Bennett monoliths, the Puerta del Sol, the Kalasasaya temple; Tiwanaku, flourishing between the 5th and 11th centuries; Andean architecture today; and adobe, the building material of the west (teacher compendium — evidence).

## Sub lesson 2 — The aguayo, featherwork and basketry
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: The aguayo, one of the most used textiles of the culture, drawn and painted figure by figure; featherwork (plumarios), a ceremonial art bound to sacred activities; and palm-leaf basketry (cesteria) of the lake, river, rice and coffee communities.

## Sub lesson 3 — Pigments, rock art and the first cultures
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Pigments obtained from nature — eucalyptus, walnut, cochineal, coloured clays; rock art in Bolivia, more than 400 registered SIARB sites; the pre-Columbian cultures Wancarani, Chiripa, Tiwanaku and Uru Chipaya; and appreciating the art of the origin peoples.
