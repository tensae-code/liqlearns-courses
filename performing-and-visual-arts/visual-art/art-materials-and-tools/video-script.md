# Video script — Art Materials and Tools

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This is Moldova's own lesson.

**Instrumente.** G3 Ch. 1, Instrumente — the painter's tools: soft and stiff brushes and what each is for, the palette, modelling tools (eboșoare), the spatula and the eraser, and choosing the right paper for each material.

**Materiale și instrumente.** Unit 2, Materiale și instrumente — the eboșoar, the sculptor's modelling tool for small details, and the water-based felt-tip pen (carioca).

**Materiale și instrumente (2).** Unit 2, Materiale și instrumente — watercolour pencils, drawn dry then washed with a brush, and the water brush whose handle is a water reservoir.

**Paleta.** Unit 3, Paleta — the palette: oval, with a thumb hole, held in the hand or laid on the easel; white plastic palettes with wells for watercolour and gouache.

**Working with gouache three ways.** Unit I §1.1 — working with gouache three ways: dry brush (the support's texture shows through), painting on a dark ground for an oil-like effect, and the palette knife — flat, tip and even handle — for bold relief strokes.

**Subject, materials and technique must fit.** Unit III §4 — subject, materials, technique: the three must fit — pen and ink for small forms, fur and birds' plumage; gouache for layered cold chromatics; sometimes the material dictates the work — Munch's "The Scream" carried in both lithograph and painting.

That is Art Materials and Tools — Moldova's way, kept by name.
