# Art Materials and Tools — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — Instrumente
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: G3 Ch. 1, Instrumente — the painter's tools: soft and stiff brushes and what each is for, the palette, modelling tools (eboșoare), the spatula and the eraser, and choosing the right paper for each material.

## Sub lesson 2 — Materiale și instrumente
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Unit 2, Materiale și instrumente — the eboșoar, the sculptor's modelling tool for small details, and the water-based felt-tip pen (carioca).

## Sub lesson 3 — Materiale și instrumente (2)
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Unit 2, Materiale și instrumente — watercolour pencils, drawn dry then washed with a brush, and the water brush whose handle is a water reservoir.

## Sub lesson 4 — Paleta
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Unit 3, Paleta — the palette: oval, with a thumb hole, held in the hand or laid on the easel; white plastic palettes with wells for watercolour and gouache.

## Sub lesson 5 — Working with gouache three ways
Sub lesson 5 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Unit I §1.1 — working with gouache three ways: dry brush (the support's texture shows through), painting on a dark ground for an oil-like effect, and the palette knife — flat, tip and even handle — for bold relief strokes.

## Sub lesson 6 — Subject, materials and technique must fit
Sub lesson 6 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Unit III §4 — subject, materials, technique: the three must fit — pen and ink for small forms, fur and birds' plumage; gouache for layered cold chromatics; sometimes the material dictates the work — Munch's "The Scream" carried in both lithograph and painting.
