# Art Materials and Tools

## Do it first

Name one thing that belongs in this lesson — The artist's materials and tools — what each is, its character, and how testing and exploring them changes the work. Say what it is in your own words. If you can name one, you are already inside the lesson.

## Sub lesson 1 — Instrumente

G3 Ch. 1, Instrumente — the painter's tools: soft and stiff brushes and what each is for, the palette, modelling tools (eboșoare), the spatula and the eraser, and choosing the right paper for each material.

## Sub lesson 2 — Materiale și instrumente

Unit 2, Materiale și instrumente — the eboșoar, the sculptor's modelling tool for small details, and the water-based felt-tip pen (carioca).

## Sub lesson 3 — Materiale și instrumente (2)

Unit 2, Materiale și instrumente — watercolour pencils, drawn dry then washed with a brush, and the water brush whose handle is a water reservoir.

## Sub lesson 4 — Paleta

Unit 3, Paleta — the palette: oval, with a thumb hole, held in the hand or laid on the easel; white plastic palettes with wells for watercolour and gouache.

## Sub lesson 5 — Working with gouache three ways

Unit I §1.1 — working with gouache three ways: dry brush (the support's texture shows through), painting on a dark ground for an oil-like effect, and the palette knife — flat, tip and even handle — for bold relief strokes.

## Sub lesson 6 — Subject, materials and technique must fit

Unit III §4 — subject, materials, technique: the three must fit — pen and ink for small forms, fur and birds' plumage; gouache for layered cold chromatics; sometimes the material dictates the work — Munch's "The Scream" carried in both lithograph and painting.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson holds, part by part, in your own words. Companion checks: did every part get named, with its Moldovan name kept where the book gives one?

## Do It Yourself

1. Choose one named part of this lesson and find out one more true thing about it — from a person who knows it, or from the book your part came from.
2. Make or perform a small piece in the way of that part: a rhythm, a step, a drawing, a shaped piece of clay.
3. Write one sentence: what does this part show about Moldova's own way of teaching its arts?
