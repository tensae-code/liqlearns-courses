# Lesson plan — Stained Glass

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Visual Art · **Lesson:** Stained Glass
**Place in the curriculum:** Visual Art (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Stained glass (vitrazh) — composing pictures in coloured glass held by lead, made to shine with light.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the course's earlier lessons in its title; recommended first, never forced.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of ..." in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **"Art and Human Heritage: Stained Glass"** — stained glass goes back to the Phoenicians on the Mediterranean's eastern coasts — gypsum, wood or metal frames hold the coloured pieces in windows to let coloured light in; in Islamic art it rests on geometric and plant ornament, in Christian art it tells the stories of Christ, Mary and the saints; designing a stained-glass window with the hollowing method and coloured papers
