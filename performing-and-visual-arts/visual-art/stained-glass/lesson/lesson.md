# Stained Glass

## Do it first

Name one thing that belongs in this lesson — Stained glass (vitrazh) — composing pictures in coloured glass held by lead, made to shine with light.. Say what it is in your own words. If you can name one, you are already inside the lesson.

## Sub lesson 1 — "Art and Human Heritage: Stained Glass"

stained glass goes back to the Phoenicians on the Mediterranean's eastern coasts — gypsum, wood or metal frames hold the coloured pieces in windows to let coloured light in; in Islamic art it rests on geometric and plant ornament, in Christian art it tells the stories of Christ, Mary and the saints; designing a stained-glass window with the hollowing method and coloured papers.
