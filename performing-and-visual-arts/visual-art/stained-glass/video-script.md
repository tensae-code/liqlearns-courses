**"Art and Human Heritage: Stained Glass".** stained glass goes back to the Phoenicians on the Mediterranean's eastern coasts — gypsum, wood or metal frames hold the coloured pieces in windows to let coloured light in; in Islamic art it rests on geometric and plant ornament, in Christian art it tells the stories of Christ, Mary and the saints; designing a stained-glass window with the hollowing method and coloured papers.
