# Mixing Colours — short answer

Answer in a short sentence, or by doing what the question asks.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why must the measure be equal? What happens if one parent colour is much stronger?
**Answer: Equal measure gives the true child colour; a stronger parent makes the mix lean toward that parent.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A friend says purple is a primary colour. Correct them kindly, with the proof.
**Answer: Purple is secondary: it is made by mixing red and blue in equal measure — primaries are found pure and unmixed.**
