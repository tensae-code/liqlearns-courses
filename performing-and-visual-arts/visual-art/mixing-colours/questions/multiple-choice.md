# Mixing Colours — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Blue mixed with yellow in equal measure makes —
a) orange  b) green  c) purple
**Answer: b) green.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Red mixed with blue in equal measure makes —
a) purple (ወይንጠጅ)  b) green  c) orange
**Answer: a) purple — ወይንጠጅ.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Red mixed with yellow makes —
a) green  b) orange  c) blue
**Answer: b) orange.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The teacher guide's three colour levels are —
a) primary, secondary, neutral  b) dark, light, bright  c) warm, cold, hot
**Answer: a) primary (first-level), secondary (second-level) and neutral colours.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Green, orange and purple belong to which level?
a) primary  b) secondary  c) neutral
**Answer: b) secondary — the second level, born from mixing.**
