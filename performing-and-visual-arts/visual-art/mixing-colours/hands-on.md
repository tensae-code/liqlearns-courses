# Hands-on — Mixing Colours


The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The mixing plate
On one plate, make three puddles — green, orange, purple — from primaries only. Keep the parents beside the children in a row, so the whole family portrait sits on one plate for the teacher to see.

## Flower garden of mixtures
Draw a row of flower outlines. Colour each flower with a different mixed colour, and under each flower write its parents (for example: red + blue). A garden that teaches.
