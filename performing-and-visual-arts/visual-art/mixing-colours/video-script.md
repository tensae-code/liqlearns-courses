# Video script — Mixing Colours

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Take two pure colours.
Mix them — equal, equal.
A new colour is born!

Blue and yellow … **green**!
Red and yellow … **orange**!
Red and blue … **purple — ወይንጠጅ**!

These are the second-level colours.
The children of the primaries.
Now mix — and paint flowers
with colours YOU made.
