# Shapes You Can Draw — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. The sub lessons climb from the youngest books to the oldest inside this one lesson.

## Sub lesson 1 — Round and oval shapes
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: draw ovals of different sizes in one stroke each, ends meeting.

## Sub lesson 2 — Shapes made of lines
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: build a triangle and a square from single lines with closed corners.

## Sub lesson 3 — Regular shapes
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: copy the pictured regular shapes in your own way, even and closed, ready to show.

## Sub lesson 4 — Geometric shapes with a ruler
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: name the four geometric shapes and draw square and rectangle with a ruler, lines joined.

## Sub lesson 5 — Open shapes from nature
Sub lesson 5 questions from the type files. Pass mark: name three natural open shapes and join open shapes into one enjoyable picture.

## Sub lesson 6 — Forms: cylinder, cube and cone
Sub lesson 6 questions from the type files. Pass mark: name cylinder, cube and cone, draw each in pencil, and shade one so it stands up.

## Sub lesson 7 — Joining and colouring geometric shapes
Sub lesson 7 questions from the type files. Pass mark: join at least three geometric shapes into a design, repeat a shape in it, and colour the joined work.

## Sub lesson 8 — Building a house from shapes
Sub lesson 8 questions from the type files. Pass mark: build a house from at least three named shapes and colour it.

## Sub lesson 9 — Building a car from shapes
Sub lesson 9 questions from the type files. Pass mark: build a car from named shapes — body, cabin, two circle wheels — and colour it.
