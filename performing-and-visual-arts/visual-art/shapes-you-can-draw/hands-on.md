# Hands-on — Shapes You Can Draw


The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Shape hunt at home
Walk your home and yard naming shapes and forms: the cup is a cylinder, the box is a cube, the roof is a triangle, the wheel is a circle. Write or draw five finds — shapes live in real things, not only in books.

## Leaf and flower tracing
Lay a leaf on paper and draw round its edge — nature's own open shape, drawn by your hand. Do the same with a flower or a stone. Then join your nature shapes into one picture.

## Build it twice
Build the house from shapes twice: once small and careful with a ruler, once big and free without one. Say which shapes you used in each, and what changed.
