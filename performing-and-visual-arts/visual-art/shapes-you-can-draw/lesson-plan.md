# Lesson plan — Shapes You Can Draw

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Visual Art · **Lesson:** Shapes You Can Draw
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 2 — Visual Art (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** lines join and close into shapes — round and oval shapes, regular shapes, ruler-measured geometric shapes, shapes from nature, and the solid forms cylinder, cube and cone — and joined shapes build pictures: designs, a house, a car.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Lines in Every Direction — the learner steers lines in every direction; now the lines meet and close. Content climbs from Grade 1 ovals through Grade 2 line-built and regular shapes to Grade 3 geometric shapes, forms and joined designs.

## Sub lessons
1. **Round and oval shapes** — the Grade 1 book's coloured filled ovals and ellipses, scattered and grouped — the first closed-shape family (ዝርግ ቅርፅ pages).
2. **Shapes made of lines** — the Grade 2 book's line-built shape models (Figure 2.4.3) — a shape is lines that join up and close.
3. **Regular shapes** — the Grade 2 book's regular (even) shapes, with its activity — copy the pictured shapes in your own way and show the teacher.
4. **Geometric shapes with a ruler** — Grade 3's open shape (ዝርግ ቅርፅ) — a picture made by joining lines — and its geometric kind: measured shapes made with a ruler — pyramid, circle, square, rectangle.
5. **Open shapes from nature** — Grade 3's natural open shapes — the shapes of flowers, animals, leaves, people and clouds.
6. **Forms: cylinder, cube and cone** — Grade 3's form (ፎርም) — drawing the true appearance of things — and its three basic forms, drawn in pencil, shaded, then coloured.
7. **Joining and colouring geometric shapes** — Grade 3's joined geometric designs — joining and repeating basic geometric open shapes, then colouring the joined work in a colour you enjoy.
8. **Building a house from shapes** — the Grade 2 book's joined regular shapes making houses — design regular houses from the pictures, then colour them.
9. **Building a car from shapes** — the Grade 2 book's car made by joining shapes, and colouring it — draw the car design and colour it.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Lines in Every Direction. Recommended, never forced.
- Next door: Colouring Inside Shapes, where closed shapes get filled with care.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Draw an oval in one stroke, and draw shapes by joining lines until they close.
2. Copy regular (even) shapes and show them to the teacher, as the Grade 2 activity asks.
3. Name and draw the geometric shapes — pyramid, circle, square, rectangle — measured shapes made with a ruler.
4. Find open shapes in nature — flowers, leaves, clouds, people — and join open shapes into enjoyable pictures.
5. Name the three basic forms — cylinder, cube, cone — draw them in pencil and colour them.
6. Join and colour geometric shapes into designs, and build a house and a car from shapes.

## What you need
Paper, a pencil, colours, and a ruler for the geometric shapes. Real things to look at help: a cup (cylinder), a box (cube), a flower and a leaf.

## If the learner is stuck
- Oval ends do not meet → start at the top, go slowly round, and aim the pencil back at its own starting dot; a faint dot marks home.
- Corners leak → draw the last side to TOUCH the first line, even cross it a little; a closed corner matters more than a tidy one.
- Shading looks flat → shade one side only and leave the other side white; light and dark side by side is what lifts the form.
