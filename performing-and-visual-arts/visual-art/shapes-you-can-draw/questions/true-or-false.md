# Shapes You Can Draw — true or false

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** An oval is a circle that stretched.
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** If a shape's lines do not close, colour would leak out of it.
**Answer: True — the shape must close.**

**3. (Sub lesson 5)** A cloud's shape can be an open shape.
**Answer: True — natural open shapes include clouds.**

**4. (Sub lesson 7)** Colouring comes after joining in the joined-design work.
**Answer: True — join the shapes, then colour your joined work in a colour you enjoy.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** A triangle built from lines has three sides that must meet at three corners.
**Answer: True — each line must meet the next, or the shape leaks.**

**6. (Sub lesson 7)** In the joined-design work, you repeat a basic geometric shape to build the pattern.
**Answer: True — joining and repeating basic geometric open shapes makes the design.**

**7. (Sub lesson 9)** A car built from shapes needs two circle wheels to roll.
**Answer: True.**
