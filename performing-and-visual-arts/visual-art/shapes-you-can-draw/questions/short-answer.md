# Shapes You Can Draw — short answer

Answer in a short sentence, or by doing what the question asks.

**1. (Sub lesson 3)** Do the book's activity: copy one pictured regular shape in your own way. Which shape did you copy, and is it closed?
**Answer: The shape is named and its corners close.**

**2. (Sub lesson 4)** Name the four geometric shapes the Grade 3 book names.
**Answer: Pyramid, circle, square, rectangle.**

**3. (Sub lesson 8)** Name every shape you used in your house, part by part.
**Answer: Walls (square/rectangle), roof (triangle), door and windows (rectangles) — each part named.**

**4. (Sub lesson 9)** Say the car's parts in building order: body first, then what?
**Answer: Body (long rectangle), cabin/windows on top, then two circle wheels.**
