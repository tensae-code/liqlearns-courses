# Shapes You Can Draw — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** An oval is best drawn —
a) in many small lifts  b) in one stroke, coming home to the start  c) with a ruler
**Answer: b) in one stroke, coming home to the start.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A shape is made when lines —
a) run away from each other  b) join up and close  c) are coloured
**Answer: b) join up and close.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A regular shape is —
a) any wobbly shape  b) an even shape whose sides and corners match in an orderly way  c) a shape from nature
**Answer: b) an even shape — like the square with four equal sides.**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Geometric shapes are —
a) shapes found only in nature  b) measured shapes made with a ruler  c) shapes made of curves only
**Answer: b) measured shapes made with a ruler — pyramid, circle, square, rectangle.**

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** Which list is all natural open shapes?
a) flowers, leaves, clouds  b) squares, rulers, cubes  c) pyramids, rectangles, circles
**Answer: a) flowers, leaves, clouds — and animals and people too.**

**6. (Sub lesson 6)** The three basic forms in drawing are —
a) line, dot, wave  b) cylinder, cube, cone  c) oval, zigzag, loop
**Answer: b) cylinder, cube and cone.**

**7. (Sub lesson 6)** Shading a form — light on one side, dark on the other — makes it —
a) disappear  b) stand up, with body  c) flat
**Answer: b) stand up — a form (ፎርም) shows the true appearance of a thing.**

**8. (Sub lesson 7)** A design in this lesson is made by —
a) joining and repeating geometric shapes, then colouring  b) tearing paper  c) drawing one line only
**Answer: a) joining and repeating geometric shapes, then colouring the joined work.**

**9. (Sub lesson 8)** A house from shapes uses —
a) only circles  b) a square or rectangle for walls, a triangle for the roof, rectangles for door and windows  c) one oval
**Answer: b) walls, triangle roof, door and window rectangles.**

**10. (Sub lesson 9)** A car's wheels are drawn as —
a) squares  b) triangles  c) circles
**Answer: c) circles — two of them, so the car can roll.**

**11. (Sub lesson 1)** The Grade 1 ovals in the book are shown —
a) only as empty outlines  b) filled with colours, scattered and grouped  c) as photographs
**Answer: b) filled with colours, scattered and grouped.**

**12. (Sub lesson 1)** The Grade 1 ovals in the book are shown —
a) only as empty outlines  b) filled with colours, scattered and grouped  c) as photographs
**Answer: b) filled with colours, scattered and grouped.**

**13. (Sub lesson 3)** Which shape has four equal sides?
a) the square  b) the rectangle  c) the oval
**Answer: a) the square — the even, regular four-sided shape.**

**14. (Sub lesson 5)** A person is one of the book's natural open shapes.
a) true — people are named among nature's shapes  b) false — only machines are  c) false — only squares are
**Answer: a) true — flowers, animals, leaves, people and clouds are named.**

**15. (Sub lesson 8)** The roof of the shape-house is —
a) a circle  b) a triangle  c) an oval
**Answer: b) a triangle.**
