# Video script — Shapes You Can Draw

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Lines join. Lines close.
Now you have a **shape**.

An oval — one stroke, round and home.
A square — four lines, four corners.

With a ruler: pyramid, circle, square, rectangle.
From nature: flower, leaf, cloud.

With body, a shape is a **form (ፎርም)**:
cylinder, cube, cone.
Shade it — light here, dark there — it stands up!

Join the shapes.
A house! A car!
You built them from lines.
