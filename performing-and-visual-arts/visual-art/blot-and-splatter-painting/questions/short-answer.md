# Blot and Splatter Painting — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name two tools the "Splat!" example uses to throw paint.
**Answer: Spray and squirt bottles (balloons and sponges are also named).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Whose splatter paintings do the children look at first?
**Answer: Jackson Pollock's.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** How do you steer blown paint?
**Answer: With the straw and your breath — blow harder, softer, or in a new direction.**
