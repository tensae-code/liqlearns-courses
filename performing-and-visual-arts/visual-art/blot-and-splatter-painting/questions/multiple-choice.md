# Blot and Splatter Painting — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The "Splat!" art work happens…
a) outdoors on a big sheet  b) indoors on a tiny card  c) only in a book
**Answer: a) outdoors on a big sheet** — with spray and squirt bottles, balloons and sponges.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In splatter painting, the paint is…
a) thrown, sprayed and splatted on purpose  b) always kept inside lines  c) never allowed to land
**Answer: a) thrown, sprayed and splatted on purpose**.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Before the blow art, the children look at…
a) Jackson Pollock's splatter paintings  b) a colour wheel  c) a photograph
**Answer: a) Jackson Pollock's splatter paintings**.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Blow art moves the paint with…
a) a straw and your breath  b) a brush  c) a ruler
**Answer: a) a straw and your breath** — drops of paint blown to make crazy hair.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The crazy hair is blown onto…
a) a self-portrait  b) a window  c) the floor
**Answer: a) a self-portrait**.
