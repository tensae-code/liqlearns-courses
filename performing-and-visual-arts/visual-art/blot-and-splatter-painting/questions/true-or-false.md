# Blot and Splatter Painting — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Splatter painting is real art work in the guide, not just a mess.
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In blow art, a brush moves the paint.
**Answer: False** — drops of paint are blown through a straw.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The "Splat!" example is done outdoors on a big sheet.
**Answer: True.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Pollock's paintings are looked at after the children blow their paint.
**Answer: False** — the children look at Pollock first, then make the blow art.
