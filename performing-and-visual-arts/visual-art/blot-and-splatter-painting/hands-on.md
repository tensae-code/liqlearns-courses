# Hands-on — Blot and Splatter Painting

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Splat sheet
Spread a big sheet outdoors. Take turns with spray bottles, squirt bottles, balloons and sponges — throw, spray and splat the paint on purpose. Stand back together and name what the splats made.

## Blow-paint hair
Draw your face. Put drops of paint at the top of the head and blow through a straw — harder, softer, in new directions — until the crazy hair is done.
