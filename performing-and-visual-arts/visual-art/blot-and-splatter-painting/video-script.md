# Video script — Blot and Splatter Painting

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Big sheet on the ground. Spray! Squirt! Splat!

The paint flies — and lands. That landing is the picture.

Jackson Pollock painted like this. So can you.

Now — a drop of paint, a straw, a breath… blow!

Crazy hair grows — this way, that way, every way!

That is blot and splatter painting: paint set free, on purpose.
