# Lesson plan — Blot and Splatter Painting

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Visual Art · **Lesson:** Blot and Splatter Painting
**Place in the curriculum:** Visual Art (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** painting from splats and blows — paint thrown, sprayed, splatted and blown on purpose, then looked at and finished, the way Singapore's early-years guide teaches it through Jackson Pollock and the "Splat!" and "Crazy Hair" examples.

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks splattering is just making a mess → in this lesson the splat IS the painting: Pollock made whole paintings this way, and the guide sets it as real art work, outdoors on a big sheet.
- Thinks blown paint cannot be steered → the straw and the breath steer it: blow harder, softer, this way, that way, and the hair grows where you aim.
