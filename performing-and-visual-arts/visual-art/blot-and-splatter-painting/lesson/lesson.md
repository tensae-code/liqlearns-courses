# Blot and Splatter Painting

## Do it first

Quick start: from the title alone, guess what this lesson's work is. Then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — Splat! — paint thrown on purpose

Outdoors, on a **big sheet**, young children make art with **spray and squirt bottles, balloons and sponges**: paint is **thrown, sprayed and splatted on purpose**. The mess is the method — the splats land where they land, and the picture is what the splats make together.

## Sub lesson 2 — Crazy hair — blow art after Pollock

First the children **look at Jackson Pollock's splatter paintings**. Then comes **blow art**: **drops of paint blown through a straw** to make **crazy hair on a self-portrait** — the breath steers the paint, and every hair goes its own way.
