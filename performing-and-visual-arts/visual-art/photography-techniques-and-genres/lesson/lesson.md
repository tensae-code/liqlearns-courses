# Photography Techniques and Genres

## Do it first

Name one thing that belongs in this lesson — photography beyond the first snapshot — colour photography and filters, photographic film, printing and enlarging, studio lighting and flash, fine-art photography and the genres of photography. Say what it is in your own words. If you can name one, you are already inside the lesson.

## Sub lesson 1 — Fine-art photography as a project

Bab 1 Projek 2: Fotografi Seni Halus — fine-art photography as a project: the photograph made as a fine-art work.

## Sub lesson 2 — Photography genres

Tajuk 9 Seni Foto (Genre Fotografi) — photography genres: their definitions, kinds and functions, visual communication within genres, and the variety of genres.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson holds, part by part, in your own words. Companion checks: did every part get named, with its Malaysian name kept where the book gives one?

## Do It Yourself

1. Choose one named part of this lesson and find out one more true thing about it — from a person who knows it, or from the book your part came from.
2. Make or perform a small piece in the way of that part: a rhythm, a step, a drawing, a shaped piece of clay.
3. Write one sentence: what does this part show about Malaysia's own way of teaching its arts?
