# Lesson plan — Sewing and Garment Making

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Visual Art · **Lesson:** Sewing and Garment Making
**Place in the curriculum:** Visual Art (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** sewing clothes and textile things — hand and machine stitches, lockstitch quality, measuring, pattern drafting, cutting, assembling and finishing garments, toys and dolls..
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this course in the same title.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Kazakhstan Grade 4 Artistic Labour** — making an ayakkap, the felt bag for carrying dishes: sketch, symmetric ornament cut from folded felt, appliqué, fringe and strap, step photos throughout
2. **Kazakhstan Grade 4 Labour Training (Ormanbekova)** — making a kamzol: what a pattern (выкройка) is, tracing it onto felt, cutting, joining the shoulders and decorating with cut-paper ornament; the finished kamzol dresses a doll
3. **Kazakhstan Grade 4 Labour Training (Muratkыzy)** — sewing a torsyk from felt: trace the template, cut two pieces, sew the sides, stuff, close, and glue on an oracal ornament
4. **Kazakhstan Grade 5 Artistic Labour (girls, Alimsaeva)** — what it teaches: hand stitches and their jobs — straight basting, tacking and copy stitches for temporary joining, buttonhole and overcast stitches against fraying, cross stitch for finishing — with the rules for good hand work; what it teaches: the sewing machine — household, industrial and computer embroidery machines; threading the upper and bobbin threads in sequence; machine seams (stitched, overlay, faced, hem); pressing work and iron temperatures for each fabric; what it teaches: how clothing is classified (use, assortment, age, season, class) and the parts of a garment — front, back, sleeve, collar, skirt panels, trouser halves — plus the ways of joining parts: thread, glue, welding, combined, rivets; what it teaches: clothing design — from idea through sketch, pattern and mock-up to the product; the two pattern methods (calculation-graphic and draping on the figure); taking body measurements in order with a tape (Сш, Сг, Ст, Сб, Др…) and what each one decides; what it teaches: creating a costume on a mini-mannequin or doll three ways — draping one piece of fabric with pins and folds; wrapping the doll in foil, drawing the bodice lines, removing and cutting a true pattern; building a paper mock-up from measured modules
5. **Kazakhstan Grade 6 Artistic Labour (girls, Alimsaeva)** — what it teaches: the author's doll — a one-of-a-kind handmade doll, fruit of long careful work; textile dolls of thread and fabric, with or without a frame, and the sculptural-textile technique that gives a doll a living face; the kinds of textile dolls — teapot doll, pocket doll, pillow doll, souvenir doll, pyjama-bag doll, interior doll; developmental toys (cubes, panels, little books, rugs, clocks) of many textures with cords, ribbons, buttons and fasteners; the project begins from a worked-out sketch; what it teaches: three cloth families — woven (cotton prints and satin, silk for appliqué), non-woven (felt and voylok, edges that never fray, best for small details) and knitted (fleece, velour — elastic, edges that curl); stuffings — cotton wool, holofiber, foam, sintepon, plastic granulate, even groats, dry herbs and fur scraps; matching hand and machine needle numbers to fabric weight from the table; practising machine stitches and adjusting upper and lower thread tension; the first dolls were Greek, of clay, wax and ivory; what it teaches: the textile toy's road in a diagram — sketch, pattern preparation, cutting, shaping the parts, processing, assembly, finishing; drafting the pattern on a 2.5 cm squared grid from the sketch; economical layout on the fabric, big parts first; cutting rules — thin fabric cut from the wrong side, seam allowances added, fur cut with small scissors so the pile survives; one-piece dolls (a single edge seam) against separate-part dolls; what it teaches: joining parts by thread, the universal way; the processing order — fold right sides in, baste, stitch, trim the excess, notch the curved edges, remove the basting, turn right side out, press; edges of woven and knitted fabrics must be overcast against fraying, by loop stitch or overlock; stuffing the finished parts; what it teaches: finishing gives the doll its character — appliqué, beads, bugles, sequins and bright buttons; the face three ways — embroidered, cut from felt and glued, or combined (eyes glued, nose and mouth embroidered); hair, mane or tail wound from wool or silk thread on a rectangular frame — the more turns, the fuller the hank — cut at the middle, marked out on the head and sewn on; what it teaches: before cutting — check the fabric, press it, find length, width, right side and warp direction; the maker's stages (pattern, fabric preparation, cutting, edge and part processing, joining, pressing); the apron's own history — born on men in ancient Egypt, later a festive, lace-trimmed part of women's dress, only lately a work protector; the four measurements for a sewn product, taken in pairs and charted — bib length (Дн), lower-part length (Днч), half waist (Ст), half hips (Сб); what it teaches: drafting the one-piece apron's base in twelve construction steps, plus the belt and strap drafts; modelling — reshaping the base into your design, keeping every part's shape in agreement (a round hem wants a round pocket and bib); laying the pattern on 90 cm and 80 cm fabric; the cutting sequence — fold on the warp, pin, chalk, add allowances, cut, check; what it teaches: processing the apron's parts from diagrams with millimetre measures — the patch pocket (top hem folded, sides pressed under, corners mitred), the belt and strap (stitched, turned with a pencil, topstitched), the double bib joined to its strap, the bib's side edges, and the lower part's side and bottom hems with closed corners; Milan's monument «Needle, Thread and Knot» by Oldenburg and van Bruggen
6. **Kazakhstan Grade 7 Artistic Labour (girls, Alimsaeva)** — what it teaches: transformer things — products that change their look or function, practical and new every time; the eight transformation techniques (stretch–compress, detach–attach, adjust–fix, fold–unfold, disappear–appear, substitute, combine, rearrange); remaking as a kind of transformation — old jeans reborn as breeches, capris, shorts, an apron, a skirt, rugs, cushion covers, patchwork squares, pocket organisers, a hammock, a many-fastened bag, bracelets and hair clips; decorating a plain garment into an exclusive — logos, appliqué, beads and bugles, buttons, rhinestones, sequins, painted elements, ribbons, braid, lace, hand embroidery; what it teaches: preparing to sew — natural fibres and why they crease, synthetics blended in; the sewing machine's family (household, industrial, sewing-embroidery; hand, treadle and electric drives; overlocks); how the lockstitch forms in five pictured stages; stitch defects and their diagnosis — a correct stitch against looping above, looping below, too tight and too loose; adjusting the upper thread tension on scraps in six steps; the machine's decorative stitches as a way of decorating clothes; what it teaches: the комплект — an outfit of two or more pieces, accessories included; preparing fabric (find the right side, check for flaws, decatise, find pile and pattern direction) and pattern (model the drawing to your sketch, name and count the parts, mark grain lines and seam allowances); two layouts for a long circle skirt on 150 cm fabric; the skirt's hem processed in six tabled steps and its waistband joined in four; the product's cost calculated in a table; the finished set presented at a fashion show with invitations, music and audience interviews
7. **Kazakhstan Grade 7 Artistic Labour 2025 (girls, Alimsaeva)** — what it teaches: preparing materials and equipment — modern equipment for sewing: sewing machines, embroidery equipment, overlocks for neatening fabric edges, ironing equipment; sewing machines are universal and special, with hand, treadle and electric drives, household (electromechanical, computerised, sewing-embroidery, overlocks) and industrial; by stitch formation they divide into chain-stitch and lockstitch (челночный) machines, all household machines being lockstitch — straight-stitch and zigzag; the lockstitch line's formation in five pictured stages; wrong adjustment of the upper and lower threads' tension leads to defects of the machine line — the correct line, looping from above and looping from below pictured; to regulate the upper thread's tension, turn the tension regulator one division and make a test line, changing the value until the defect is gone, and regulate the lower thread only if the upper's regulation did not affect the line's quality
8. **Kazakhstan Grade 8 Artistic Labour (girls, Alimsaeva)** — what it teaches: the measurements for drafting the kamzol base (half neck, half chest, half waist, half hip circumferences, shoulder width, back length to waist, product length) and the full construction — the grid table, the back draft table and the front draft table with calculation formulas for every segment; making the kamzol in two variants, each in step photographs: cut the pieces from the pattern, draw the ornament, cut the openwork ornament through, sew the pieces on the machine, join and finish — the finished red openwork kamzol on a mannequin; the one-piece sleeve) — what it teaches: a garment's cut (киім пішімі) is the form given to a product by a defined cutting method; modern designers and fashion designers, when preparing a clothing model, rely on the national basis and use the folk cut; in a shoulder garment's cut the sleeve is joined with the ойынды (armhole) — that is, a sleeve cut; a sleeve (жең) is the part of clothing fully covering the arm or covering one part, and sleeves are various by length and cut; a қондырмалы жең is a sleeve sewn into the armhole passing to the hand from the shoulder; a реглан (raglan) is a sleeve whose shoulder part is cut together with the back and front — the cut's main peculiarity is that the ойынды begins from the neckline line; a тұтаспішімді жең (one-piece sleeve) is a sleeve cut in one piece with the product's back and front parts, the shoulder line lying flat; the one-piece sleeve is considered the folk cut's peculiarity — garments cut in one piece are met in many peoples' national clothes, and in Kazakh national clothes too they are frequent; national clothes mostly come straight, trapezoid and flared; in this cut garments can be voluminous, loose, long, short and so on
9. **Kazakhstan Grade 9 Artistic Labour (girls, Alimsaeva)** — Өндіріс (Производство / Production) — what it teaches: nowadays the clothes and accessories production (қазіргі уақытта киім мен аксессуарлар өндірісі) has turned into an industry giving much hope from the future (болашағынан көп үміт күттіретін индустрияға айналды); using quality materials (сапалы материалдарды пайдаланып) and perfecting their processing methods (оларды өңдеу әдіс-тәсілдерін қолдану арқылы), clothes suiting contemporary fashion's demands (заманауи сәннің талаптарына), production-effective (өндірістің тиімді), economical (үнемді болуын ескеріп), wear-enduring (тозуға төзімді) and beautiful (әрі әдемі киімдер дайындауға болады) can be prepared; using new technologies, automating production and knowing how to use tool-equipment effectively (жаңа технологияларды пайдалану, өндірісті автоматтандыру, құрал-жабдықты тиімді қолдана білу нәтижесінде), the time spent for preparing clothes and accessories shortened considerably (киім мен аксессуарлар дайындауға жұмсалатын уақыт біршама қысқарып) and the product's quality improved considerably (өнімнің сапасы едәуір жақсарды); clothes and accessories are prepared by two methods (киім мен аксессуарлар екі түрлі тәсілмен дайындалады) — that is, clothes are either mass produced (киім жаппай өндіріледі) or prepared individually (немесе нақты тапсырыс берушінің талап-тілегін ескеріліп жекелей дайындалады) reckoning an exact order-giver's demand-wish; nowadays in preparing clothes there is its own way (қазіргі кезде киім дайындаудың өзіндік жолы бар): 1. Үлгі дайындау, құрылымдап, технологиялық құжатын даярлау (preparing the sample, constructing it, preparing its technological document); 2. Материалды пішуге дайындау және пішу (preparing the material for cutting, and cutting); 3. Киімді тігіп, өңдеу және сапасын тексеру (sewing the clothes, processing it and checking its quality); in factories, by constructors' prepared drawings (құрастырушылар дайындаған сызбаүлгілер бойынша), in sewing factories all parts are cut too (тігін фабрикаларында барлық бөлшектер пішіледі де), and joined with a special device suiting the technological description and sewn (технологиялық сипаттамаға сәйкес арнайы құрылғымен біріктіріліп, тігіледі) — thus prepared, quality-high clothes go to sale (осылайша дайындалған, сапасы жоғары киім сатылымға түседі); when individually prepared (жекелей дайындалған кезде), for an exact order-giver the clothes are sewn in only one sample (нақты тапсырыс беруші үшін киім бір ғана үлгіде тігіледі) — individual order-givers' clothes are prepared in sewing ateliers made for individual order-givers (жекелей тапсырыс берушілерге арналған киімдер тігін ательелерінде дайындалады), and an artistic council (көркемдік кеңес) consisting of artists, fashion designers, the clothes' constructor and the enterprise's representatives looks at them and confirms them (суретші, модельер суретші, киімнің құрастырушысы және кәсіпорыннан келген өкілдерден құралған көркемдік кеңес қарастырып, бекітеді); they also reckon the designed samples' marketing value (олар өздері жобалаған үлгілердің маркетингтік құндылығын ескереді); for putting into a sample (үлгіге салу үшін), first the sample's sketch must be prepared (алдымен үлгінің нобайын дайындап алу қажет) — in this case the main and decorative elements' general composition, colour harmony, fabric's peculiarity and other questions are reckoned (бұл ретте негізгі және сәндік элементтерінің жалпы композициясы, түс үйлесімділігі, маталардың ерекшелігі және т.б. мәселелер ескеріледі); after the sketches are checked by an artistic leader (нобайларды көркемдік жетекші тексергеннен кейін), the samples' cutting and preparation processes are realised (үлгілерді пішу және дайындау үдерістері жүзеге асырылады); the pictured production: mass production of clothes (Киімді жаппай өндіру — a sewing factory floor), individual preparation of clothes (Киімді жекелей дайындау — a designer fitting a client), and preparing accessories (Аксессуарларды дайындау — a belt being sewn, a bag being decorated)
