# Video script — Light and Shadow in Art

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Light travels. A body stands in its way. Three areas appear.

The side facing the light — lit. The side turned away — the real shadow.

And on the table beneath — the cast shadow.

Strong light, strong shadow. Weak light, light shadow.

Black drinks the light. White throws it back. That is light and shadow in art.
