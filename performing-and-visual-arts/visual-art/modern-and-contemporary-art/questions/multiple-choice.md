# Modern and Contemporary Art — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Modern art's history begins in…
a) the 19th century  b) the Stone Age  c) the 21st century
**Answer: a) the 19th century** — when the schools broke from literal depiction.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Matisse's word was that depiction no longer cares for…
a) literal detail  b) colour  c) heritage
**Answer: a) literal detail**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** In the new art, subjects are taken from…
a) the artist's feeling and thought  b) only the thing shown  c) old rules
**Answer: a) the artist's feeling and thought** — rather than from the thing shown.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Heritage in modern art is kept as…
a) the starting point  b) a burden to drop  c) a secret
**Answer: a) the starting point**
