# Modern and Contemporary Art — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What are modern art's roots, in the book?
**Answer: Roots thousands of years old, but a history beginning in the 19th century.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Name two marks of the new art.
**Answer: Any two of: freedom from detail and old tradition; the artist's own vision unbarred; heritage kept as the starting point; subjects from feeling and thought.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** What did sunlight rendering make possible?
**Answer: It could carry the fastest transfer (of impressions).**
