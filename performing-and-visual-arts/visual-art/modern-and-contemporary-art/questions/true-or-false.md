# Modern and Contemporary Art — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Modern art broke from literal depiction.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Modern art throws heritage away entirely.
**Answer: False** — Heritage is kept as the starting point.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The artist's own vision is barred in modern art.
**Answer: False** — The artist's own vision is unbarred.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Modern art's history begins in the 19th century.
**Answer: True**
