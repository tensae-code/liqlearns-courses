# Hands-on — Modern and Contemporary Art

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Book 1, Artistic Culture, "Modern Plastic Art"
Do the thing this part teaches, with real materials where the book names them: what modern art is — roots thousands of years old but a history beginning in the 19th century, when the schools broke from literal depiction: Matisse's word that depiction no longer cares for literal detail, sunlight rendering able to carry the fastest transfer, and the new art's marks — freedom from detail and old tradition, the artist's own vision unbarred, heritage kept as the starting point, and subjects taken from the artist's feeling and thought rather than from the thing shown
