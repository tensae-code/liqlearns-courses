# Renaissance Art

## Do it first

Quick game: have you heard the name Leonardo da Vinci? Michelangelo? They stand at the centre of this lesson — the age when painting and sculpture took the lead.

## Sub lesson 1 — The Renaissance: art's greatest impact

The **Renaissance** is the **transition from the medieval to the contemporary period**. In it, **art gained its greatest impact** — and this time **painting and sculpture led**, where the medieval age had been led by architecture.

## Sub lesson 2 — The great masters

The Renaissance is known by its **great masters**:

- **Leonardo da Vinci** — *The Last Supper*.
- **Michelangelo** — *Pietà*.
- **Rafael Sanzio**.
- **Donatello**.

Learn each name with a work, the way the book teaches them.
