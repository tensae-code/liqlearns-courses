# Renaissance Art — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Renaissance is the transition from…
a) ancient to medieval  b) medieval to contemporary  c) contemporary to future
**Answer: b) medieval to contemporary**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In the Renaissance, the leading arts were…
a) architecture only  b) painting and sculpture  c) ceramics
**Answer: b) painting and sculpture**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The Last Supper is by…
a) Michelangelo  b) Donatello  c) Leonardo da Vinci
**Answer: c) Leonardo da Vinci**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The Pietà is by…
a) Michelangelo  b) Rafael Sanzio  c) Leonardo da Vinci
**Answer: a) Michelangelo**
