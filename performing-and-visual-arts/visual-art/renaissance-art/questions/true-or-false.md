# Renaissance Art — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In the Renaissance, architecture still led the arts.
**Answer: False** — painting and sculpture led.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Rafael Sanzio is one of the Renaissance masters.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Donatello is named among the great masters.
**Answer: True**
