# Hands-on — Renaissance Art

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Masters' cards
Make four small cards, one per master. On each, write the name and — for da Vinci and Michelangelo — the work the book names. Practise matching name to work with a partner.

## Transition line
Draw a timeline: medieval — Renaissance — contemporary. Mark which art led each age: architecture, then painting and sculpture.
