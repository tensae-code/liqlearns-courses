# Lesson plan — Animals in Art

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Visual Art · **Lesson:** Animals in Art
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 2 — Visual Art (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** drawing and painting animals — the animalistic genre from the ancient world to the expressive horses of the 19th century, and an animal's proportions and movement.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the course's earlier visual art lessons — recommended first, never forced.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. Media slots in this lesson are described in words only.
