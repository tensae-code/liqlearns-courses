# Hands-on — Animals in Art

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## From general to particular
Draw an animal in three passes: (1) the big simple shape only; (2) the main parts in proportion; (3) the particulars — ear, eye, tail. Stop after each pass and check, the book's law in your hand.

## Movement sketches
Watch an animal move (a pet, birds outside, or a partner miming one). Make five quick sketches, one minute each — catch the movement, not the details.
