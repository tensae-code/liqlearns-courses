# Animals in Art

## Do it first

Before you read: say or sketch what you already know about this lesson's subject — drawing and painting animals — the animalistic genre from the ancient world to the expressive horses of the 19th century, and an animal's proportions and movement. Start there; the lesson builds on it.

## Sub lesson 1 — The animalistic genre through time

The Grade 5 book's Section 9 teaches the **animalistic genre**: from the generalised animal images of the ancient world — flying fish to the bull, the bull and lion figures of ancient Mesopotamia — to the **expressive horses of the 19th century** and the Russian animalist artists such as **Vatagin**.

## Sub lesson 2 — Proportions and movement

Section 19 returns to the animal as drawing practice: drawing an animal obeys the law **from simple to complex, from general to particular**; and the learner makes **sketches of an animal in motion** — the body caught moving, not standing stiff.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the whole lesson back in your own words — every sub lesson, in order. Companion checks: did the main facts of each sub lesson arrive?
