# Video script — Animals in Art

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Animals have their own genre — the **animalistic genre**.

Ancient Mesopotamia carved its bulls and lions. The 19th century painted horses full of feeling. Vatagin drew animals all his life.

And the law of drawing them: **simple to complex, general to particular** — then sketch them moving!
