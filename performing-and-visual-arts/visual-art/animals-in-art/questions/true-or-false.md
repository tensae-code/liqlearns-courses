# Animals in Art — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The 19th century's animal art is known for expressive horses.
**Answer: True**.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The animalistic genre begins in the 19th century.
**Answer: False** — its images reach back to the ancient world.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Movement sketches catch the animal standing stiff.
**Answer: False** — they catch the body moving.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Flying fish appear among the ancient animal images named.
**Answer: True**.
