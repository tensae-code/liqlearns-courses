# Animals in Art — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Trace the animalistic genre's road in the book.
**Answer: From the ancient world's generalised images (Mesopotamia's bull and lion) to the 19th century's expressive horses and animalists such as Vatagin.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** State the law for drawing an animal.
**Answer: From simple to complex, from general to particular.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** What do the Section 19 sketches practise?
**Answer: An animal's body proportions and its movement.**
