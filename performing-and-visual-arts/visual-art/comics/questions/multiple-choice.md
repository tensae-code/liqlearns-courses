# Comics — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Character design creates a cartoon character's…
a) look and personality  b) price  c) frame
**Answer: a) look and personality**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Characters can be designed for…
a) a show, comic, mascot, book or poster  b) only exam papers  c) nothing
**Answer: a) a show, comic, mascot, book or poster**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Composite shapes are made by…
a) combining basic shapes  b) erasing all shapes  c) using one shape only
**Answer: a) combining basic shapes**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Facial expressions show the character's…
a) feelings  b) height only  c) maker's name
**Answer: a) feelings**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Kyle and Lulu is…
a) a Dhivehi animated series by Hussain Ihwan  b) a French comic  c) a radio play
**Answer: a) a Dhivehi animated series by Hussain Ihwan**
