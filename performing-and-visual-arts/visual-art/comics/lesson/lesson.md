# Comics

## Do it first

Quick game: draw a hero from three plain shapes — a circle, a triangle, a square. Already the shapes are telling us who this character is.

## Sub lesson 1 — Character design — look and personality

**Character design** creates **the look and personality of a cartoon character** — for **a show, a comic, a mascot, a book or a poster**. The designer **balances and combines basic shapes into composite shapes** until the character stands there, recognisable at a glance.

## Sub lesson 2 — Expressions — and Kyle and Lulu

A character comes alive when **facial expressions** are added — the same face can laugh, frown or wonder. The book's own example is Maldivian: **Kyle and Lulu, the Dhivehi animated series by Maldivian artist Hussain Ihwan** — characters designed in the Maldives, speaking Dhivehi.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson taught, part by part. Companion checks each part against the sub lessons above.

## Do It Yourself

1. Make your own piece using this lesson's way, from start to finish.
2. Show it to the class and say, in the book's own closing words, talk about your art: what you made, how, and what you learnt.
3. Write one sentence: what will you do differently next time?
