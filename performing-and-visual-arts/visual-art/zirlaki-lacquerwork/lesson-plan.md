# Lesson plan — Zirlaki Lacquerwork

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Visual Art · **Lesson:** Zirlaki Lacquerwork
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 2 — Visual Art (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Iran's zir-laki lacquerwork — layered paper built up and lacquered, from traditional bookbinding to lacquered boxes and wooden objects.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the learner's earlier art lessons. Recommended, never forced.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Lacquerwork L1, Traditional bookbinding** — Zirlaki lacquerwork — جلدسازی سنتی: traditional bookbinding by the zir-laki method of layered paper.
2. **Lacquerwork L2, Lacquered boxes** — جعبه های روغنی: making zir-laki boxes by the paper-strip method.
3. **Lacquerwork L3, Lacquered objects on wood** — اشیای روغنی: zir-laki lacquerwork on wooden objects.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier Visual Art lessons. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say and show what lacquerwork l1, traditional bookbinding teaches.
2. Say and show what lacquerwork l2, lacquered boxes teaches.
3. Say and show what lacquerwork l3, lacquered objects on wood teaches.

## What you need
Paper strips, glue, a small box or wooden object, and varnish handled with a teacher.
