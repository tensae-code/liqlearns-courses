# Hands-on — Zirlaki Lacquerwork

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Do the sub lessons
- **Lacquerwork L1, Traditional bookbinding** — do the book's own task for this part: Zirlaki lacquerwork — جلدسازی سنتی: traditional bookbinding by the zir-laki method of layered paper. Show your companion what you made or named.

- **Lacquerwork L2, Lacquered boxes** — do the book's own task for this part: جعبه های روغنی: making zir-laki boxes by the paper-strip method. Show your companion what you made or named.

- **Lacquerwork L3, Lacquered objects on wood** — do the book's own task for this part: اشیای روغنی: zir-laki lacquerwork on wooden objects. Show your companion what you made or named.
