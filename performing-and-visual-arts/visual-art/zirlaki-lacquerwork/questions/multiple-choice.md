# Zirlaki Lacquerwork — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Zir-laki work is built from…
a) layers of paper  b) carved stone  c) woven wool
**Answer: a) layers of paper**

**2.** Traditional bookbinding by the zir-laki method uses…
a) layered paper  b) glass  c) metal sheets
**Answer: a) layered paper**

**3.** Lacquered boxes (جعبه های روغنی) are made by…
a) the paper-strip method  b) pinching clay  c) tying knots
**Answer: a) the paper-strip method**
