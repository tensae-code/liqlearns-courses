# Video script — Zellige Mosaic Art

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

See the fountain — shining with small tiles!

This is zellige — Morocco's mosaic. Piece by piece, colour by colour.
One colour, then another — they take turns! Light against dark!
And look — the left half is the mirror of the right half!

Repetition. Alternation. Opposition.
That is how zellige sings.
