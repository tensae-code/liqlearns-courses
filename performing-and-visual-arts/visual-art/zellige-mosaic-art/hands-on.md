# Hands-on — Zellige Mosaic Art

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Mirror halves
Fold a paper in half. Draw half a fountain on the fold, cut or trace it open — a mirrored front. Colour the tile pieces so the two halves match.

## Three rhythms
Make three paper tile strips: one by repetition (same colour again and again), one by alternation (two colours taking turns), one by opposition (light against dark).
