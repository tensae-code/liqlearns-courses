# Zellige Mosaic Art — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A saqaya is…
a) a public fountain  b) a boat  c) a song
**Answer: a) a public fountain.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The saqaya is decorated with…
a) plain paint only  b) zellige mosaic  c) paper
**Answer: b) zellige mosaic.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** A ruler down the fountain's middle shows…
a) the two halves mirror each other  b) nothing  c) the halves are random
**Answer: a) the two halves mirror each other.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Pupils colour zellige pieces working by…
a) chance  b) repetition, alternation and opposition  c) covering them up
**Answer: b) repetition, alternation and opposition.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** In the Moroccan house, zellige is found with…
a) carved wood  b) plastic  c) glass only
**Answer: a) carved wood.**
