# Zellige Mosaic Art — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What rhythm do the fountain's mosaic colours follow?
**Answer: Alternation — the pieces alternate in colour (with opposition).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What does mirror symmetry mean on the fountain?
**Answer: The two halves of the fountain match each other in shape across the middle line.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the three working rhythms for colouring zellige.
**Answer: Repetition, alternation and opposition.**
