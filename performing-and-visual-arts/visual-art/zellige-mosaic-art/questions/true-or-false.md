# Zellige Mosaic Art — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Zellige is a mosaic art of Morocco.
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The two halves of the saqaya are deliberately different in shape.
**Answer: False** — they mirror each other.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Zellige pieces can be coloured by repetition, alternation and opposition.
**Answer: True.**
