# History of Architecture

## Do it first

Name one thing that belongs in this lesson — the history of architecture — how building design has changed across the ages and styles. Say what it is in your own words. If you can name one, you are already inside the lesson.

## Sub lesson 1 — Архитектура

Архитектура — the history of architecture: Egypt's pyramids and columns, Greece's three orders, Rome's arch and Vitruvius, Hagia Sophia, Romanesque mass, the Gothic pointed arch, Renaissance measure, Baroque movement, and Paxton's Crystal Palace of iron and glass.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson holds, part by part, in your own words. Companion checks: did every part get named, with its Macedonian name kept where the book gives one?

## Do It Yourself

1. Choose one named part of this lesson and find out one more true thing about it — from a person who knows it, or from the book your part came from.
2. Make or perform a small piece in the way of that part: a rhythm, a step, a drawing, a shaped piece of clay.
3. Write one sentence: what does this part show about North Macedonia's own way of teaching its arts?
