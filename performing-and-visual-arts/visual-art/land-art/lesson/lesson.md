# Land Art

## Do it first

Name one thing that belongs in this lesson — Land art — art that remakes the landscape itself as its material and gallery.. Say what it is in your own words. If you can name one, you are already inside the lesson.

## Sub lesson 1 — "Art Around Us: Art and Gardens"

gardens composed as artworks — topiary, flower carpets, a Mondrian garden — designing a garden and discussing whether it can be built in your school or neighbourhood.

## Sub lesson 2 — "Creative Creatures"

birds build their nests to fit their surroundings and protect their young, and the spider engineers her silk web with high skill — discussing with teacher, classmates and family how to benefit from the spider's creativity in making artworks.

## Sub lesson 3 — "I Contemplate Art in Nature"

contemplating the shape of the clouds, the sand dunes and mountains in the desert, natural erosion forming spontaneous artworks, and the caves in the mountains.

## Sub lesson 4 — Companion activities

creating an artwork from your imagination using environment waste during a trip you take.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson holds, part by part, in your own words. Companion checks: did every part get named, with its Syrian name kept where the book gives one?

## Do It Yourself

1. Choose one named part of this lesson and find out one more true thing about it — from a person who knows it, or from the book your part came from.
2. Make or perform a small piece in the way of that part: a rhythm, a step, a drawing, a shaped piece of clay.
3. Write one sentence: what does this part show about Syria's own way of teaching its arts?
