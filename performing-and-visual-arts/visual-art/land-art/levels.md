# Land Art — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — "Art Around Us: Art and Gardens"
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: gardens composed as artworks — topiary, flower carpets, a Mondrian garden — designing a garden and discussing whether it can be built in your school or neighbourhood

## Sub lesson 2 — "Creative Creatures"
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: birds build their nests to fit their surroundings and protect their young, and the spider engineers her silk web with high skill — discussing with teacher, classmates and family how to benefit from the spider's creativity in making artworks

## Sub lesson 3 — "I Contemplate Art in Nature"
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: contemplating the shape of the clouds, the sand dunes and mountains in the desert, natural erosion forming spontaneous artworks, and the caves in the mountains

## Sub lesson 4 — Companion activities
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: creating an artwork from your imagination using environment waste during a trip you take
