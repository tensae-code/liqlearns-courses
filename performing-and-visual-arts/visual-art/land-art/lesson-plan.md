# Lesson plan — Land Art

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Visual Art · **Lesson:** Land Art
**Place in the curriculum:** Visual Art (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Land art — art that remakes the landscape itself as its material and gallery.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the course's earlier lessons in its title; recommended first, never forced.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of ..." in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **"Art Around Us: Art and Gardens"** — gardens composed as artworks — topiary, flower carpets, a Mondrian garden — designing a garden and discussing whether it can be built in your school or neighbourhood
2. **"Creative Creatures"** — birds build their nests to fit their surroundings and protect their young, and the spider engineers her silk web with high skill — discussing with teacher, classmates and family how to benefit from the spider's creativity in making artworks
3. **"I Contemplate Art in Nature"** — contemplating the shape of the clouds, the sand dunes and mountains in the desert, natural erosion forming spontaneous artworks, and the caves in the mountains
4. **Companion activities** — creating an artwork from your imagination using environment waste during a trip you take

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
