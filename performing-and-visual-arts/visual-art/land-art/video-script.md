# Video script — Land Art

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This is Syria's own lesson.

**"Art Around Us: Art and Gardens".** gardens composed as artworks — topiary, flower carpets, a Mondrian garden — designing a garden and discussing whether it can be built in your school or neighbourhood.

**"Creative Creatures".** birds build their nests to fit their surroundings and protect their young, and the spider engineers her silk web with high skill — discussing with teacher, classmates and family how to benefit from the spider's creativity in making artworks.

**"I Contemplate Art in Nature".** contemplating the shape of the clouds, the sand dunes and mountains in the desert, natural erosion forming spontaneous artworks, and the caves in the mountains.

**Companion activities.** creating an artwork from your imagination using environment waste during a trip you take.

That is Land Art — Syria's way, kept by name.
