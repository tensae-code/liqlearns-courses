# Hands-on — Sumdang: Traditional Painting of Bhutan

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The Sumdang sequence
Paint a small traditional picture following the full sequence in order: basic colour inside the outline; basic colour mixed with white at the border; boundary lines in white; black to the inner part.

## Check the order
Show a partner your painting and name the steps in order; your partner checks no step was skipped or swapped.
