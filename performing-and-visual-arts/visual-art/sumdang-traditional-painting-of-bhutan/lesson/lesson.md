# Sumdang: Traditional Painting of Bhutan

## Do it first

Quick start: from the title alone, guess what this lesson's work is. Then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — Draw and lay the basic colour

Sumdang is Bhutan's traditional painting method, and the Class IV workbook teaches it **step by step**. First: **draw a traditional picture**, then **apply the basic colour inside the outline**.

## Sub lesson 2 — White at the border, boundary lines in white

Next: **mix the basic colour with white and apply it at the border line**, and **draw the boundary lines with white**. Last: **apply black to the inner part**. The order is the method — each step waits for the one before it.
