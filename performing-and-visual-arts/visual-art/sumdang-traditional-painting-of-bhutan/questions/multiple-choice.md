# Sumdang: Traditional Painting of Bhutan — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Sumdang is…
a) Bhutan's traditional painting method  b) a paper craft  c) a kind of printing
**Answer: a) Bhutan's traditional painting method**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The first colour step is…
a) apply the basic colour inside the outline  b) apply black  c) draw white boundary lines
**Answer: a) apply the basic colour inside the outline**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** At the border line you apply…
a) pure black  b) the basic colour mixed with white  c) no colour
**Answer: b) the basic colour mixed with white**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The boundary lines are drawn with…
a) white  b) black  c) the basic colour
**Answer: a) white**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** The last step applies black…
a) to the border  b) to the inner part  c) over the whole picture
**Answer: b) to the inner part**
