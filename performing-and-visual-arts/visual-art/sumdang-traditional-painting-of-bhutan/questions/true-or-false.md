# Sumdang: Traditional Painting of Bhutan — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Sumdang's steps follow a set order.
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Black is applied first in Sumdang.
**Answer: False.** — black is the last step, to the inner part.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** White is used both mixed at the border and pure for boundary lines.
**Answer: True.**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** The basic colour is applied outside the outline.
**Answer: False.** — it is applied inside the outline.
