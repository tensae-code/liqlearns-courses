This lesson is Designing Board Games.

Designing Board Games — Designing board games — their history and features, and researching, analysing and creating your own game. — as Kazakhstan's school books teach it: what it teaches: in this section you will get acquainted with board games' many types, their emergence and what material each game is made of; board games' design's development directly depends on the games' history; by researching the section's themes you will learn to choose board games, their structure and materials; at researching, board games' preparation's additional parts being a board game's preparation, understand what an additional part (қосымша бөліктері) is; a game (ойын) is a type of activity thought up, in it the result is not the goal, the process itself is its main value; «Ойын» ("Game") is also called objects specially for such actions or programmes' collection; games are divided into board (үстелдік), moving (қозғалмалы), sport, role (рөлдік), gambling (құмар), children's, computer, psychological, educational (құрастырмалы) games; a board game (үстел ойыны) is a game played on any convenient flat surface by placing special elements in advance; interesting data — board games' history (үстел ойындарының даму тарихы): board games' history counts at least 5500 years; Kazakh researchers say board games' development history is common; the author of the "Oxford history of board games" David Parlett also says modern board games' even oldest prototypes have a common history; the oldest games are considered games of the dice (дойбы) type; Senet (Сенет) is known as the world's oldest board game — it arose in Ancient Egypt (in our era before IV); Nard (Нард) game — arose in the East about 3000 years ago and is preserved in a changed form to this day; Go and Go-moku (Го және Го-моку) — ancient Chinese games; arose in the 1st millennium BC and preserved almost unchanged to this day; chess-type games (шахмат түріндегі ойындар) arose in the 5th century; Chaturanga (Чатуранга) — the first known chess-type game arose in India; Shatranj (Шартрандж) — a (Central Asian, Persian, Arab) and xiangqi (сянци) (Chinese chess) VI–VII centuries appeared; in the 2nd millennium in Korea chanchi (чанги) and in Japan shogi (сёги) games' first variants appeared; in present time in Europe chess's (шахматтың) modern rules appeared, the game spread to all European countries; from the 12th century game cards (ойын карталары) began to appear; in game theory (ойын теориясында) the player's strategy (ойыншының стратегиясы) is a full plan of the player's possible actions in all situations that may arise; strategy is defined in the game's process by the player's action; combinations (комбинациялары) — the player's varied actions' goal-directed joining; it is considered a chess term; systematic exercises develop them; the World Intellectual Games or Intellympics (Дүниежүзілік зияткерлік ойындар немесе Интеллимпиада (World Mind Sports Games, WMSG)) — held every four years, a sport intellectual games' competition; intellectual games' basis is the game types chess, draughts (дойбы), go, bridge, and also in the programme are included xiangqi (I Intellympics), mahjong (III Intellympics); the actor, teacher and writer Vadim Demchog's words quoted: «Ойын – дүниені танудың табиғи жолы» ("Game is the natural way of knowing the world").
