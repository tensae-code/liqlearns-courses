# Decorating Home and Environment — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number, as before. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — Decorating from ancient times to today
Pass mark: tell the practice's age (from ancient times; cave-dwellers beautified with pictures, writings and utensils); describe the local way — festivals and rains, varied soils, cattle products and ash as paint, inside and outside; paths beautified with stones, soil, sand and gravel; and today's form — scientific knowledge and skill, the architecture and gardening professions, wide manpower and high income.

## Sub lesson 2 — Decorating with ceramics and pottery — benefits and the task
Pass mark: give the three benefits of decorating with ceramics and pottery (a comfortable, pleasant home and environment; a better idea and motivation for the next generation; wide job opportunity for professionals) and carry out the book's task on a chosen site.
