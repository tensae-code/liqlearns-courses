# Lesson plan — Decorating Home and Environment

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Visual Art · **Lesson:** Decorating Home and Environment
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 2 — Visual Art (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** beautifying the home and its surroundings — the ancient practice, the Ethiopian way with soils, ash, stones and paths, and the special work of ceramics and pottery.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Crafts Around Us and Making with Clay and Paper: the learner arrives knowing pots are made. This lesson turns the made things outward — onto the home they beautify.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
1. **Decorating from ancient times to today** — cave-dwellers to professions; the Ethiopian way — soils, ash, and the beautified paths.
2. **Decorating with ceramics and pottery — benefits and the task** — the three benefits; the group task on a real site.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Crafts Around Us (Visual Art) — pottery among the crafts. Recommended, never forced.
- Recommended first: Making with Clay and Paper (Visual Art) — the clay hand that makes the pots. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Tell the history the book gives: decoration from the cave-dwellers to today's professions.
2. Describe the Ethiopian beautifying way: festival and rains, soils and ash inside and out, stone-edged paths.
3. Give the three benefits of decorating with ceramics and pottery.
4. Beautify a real site in a small group, using easily available local inputs.

## What you need
What your place offers: varied soils, ash, stones, sand and gravel; pots and ceramic pieces; a site at home or school to beautify in a small group.

## If the learner is stuck
- Nothing to decorate with → look again: the book's inputs are soils, ash, stones, sand, gravel and the pots already in the house. Decoration begins with what the place gives.
- The work looks scattered → choose one path or one wall and finish it fully; a finished corner teaches more than a touched whole yard.
