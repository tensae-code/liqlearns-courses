# Hands-on — Decorating Home and Environment

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Beautify a path
Choose one path — from the gate or the house door toward the kitchen or the garden. Edge it with stones, soil, sand or gravel of varied content and size, in the book's way. Finish the whole path, however short.

## A ceramic corner
Take the pots and ceramic pieces your home already has. Arrange them to beautify one corner or shelf: comfortable to live with, pleasant to look at. Change one thing at a time until the corner rests.

## The group task
The book's own task: in small groups, choose a site at home or school and beautify it using easily available local inputs, applying the theory learned in class. Plan it, do it, and walk a visitor through it.
