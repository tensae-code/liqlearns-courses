# Decorating Home and Environment — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The cave-dwellers beautified their caves with —
a) ceramic tiles  b) pictures, writings and utensils placed, pasted and hung  c) electric lights
**Answer: b) pictures, writings and utensils placed, pasted and hung** — the practice is ancient, in the world and in Ethiopia.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** At festivals and the rains, homes here are beautified using —
a) varied soils, cattle products and ash as "paint"  b) imported wallpaper  c) plastic flowers only
**Answer: a) varied soils, cattle products and ash as "paint"** — the local inputs the place gives.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Decorating with ceramics and pottery makes the home —
a) harder to clean only  b) comfortable and pleasant for our living  c) smaller
**Answer: b) comfortable and pleasant for our living** — the first of the book's three benefits.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The book's closing task is to —
a) write an essay about decoration  b) beautify a chosen site at home or school in small groups, using easily available local inputs  c) buy ceramic products
**Answer: b) beautify a chosen site at home or school in small groups, using easily available local inputs** — applying the theory learned in class.
