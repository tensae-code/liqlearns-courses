# Video script — Decorating Home and Environment


The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.


---


People have **always** beautified their homes — even the cave-dwellers painted their walls.

In Ethiopia, when the festivals come, the home is beautified inside and out — with soils, with ash, with care.

Outside: the **paths** — edged with stones, sand and gravel.

Inside: **pots and ceramics**, making the home comfortable and pleasant.

Your turn: choose one site. Beautify it with what your place gives.
