# Miniature Painting — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** A miniature is…
a) a tiny, fine painting, made above all to illustrate books  b) a very large wall painting  c) a photograph
**Answer: a) a tiny, fine painting, made above all to illustrate books**

**2.** The book calls the miniature a painting of…
a) the mind (نقاشی ذهنی)  b) the camera  c) chance
**Answer: a) the mind (نقاشی ذهنی)**

**3.** The pictured Khamsa of Nizami miniature comes from…
a) Herat, 900 AH  b) Spain  c) China
**Answer: a) Herat, 900 AH**

**4.** In the Eid al-Fitr miniature, pupils hunt for…
a) the spiral movement of the composition  b) a hidden signature  c) the frame
**Answer: a) the spiral movement of the composition**
