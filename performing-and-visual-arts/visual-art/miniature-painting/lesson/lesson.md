# Miniature Painting

## Do it first

Start with the first part below — do it or make it before you read any explanation.

## Sub lesson 1 — Knowing the miniature

Grade 8 asks why Islamic miniatures are world-famous: they are the art of illustrating books among Muslims — a painting of the mind (نقاشی ذهنی). Pictured works include a Khamsa of Nizami (Herat, 900 AH) and the Eid al-Fitr festival miniature, in which pupils hunt for the spiral movement of the composition.

## Sub lesson 2 — Copying a simple miniature

Grade 9 takes the next step: copying a simple miniature painting by hand — slowly, part by part, the way the old illustrators worked.
