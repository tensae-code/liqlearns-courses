# Dari Calligraphy: Naskh

## Do it first

Start with the first part below — do it or make it before you read any explanation.

## Sub lesson 1 — The alphabet letters in Naskh

Afghanistan's Grade 2 Beautiful Writing (حسن خط) year opens with the whole Dari alphabet written in the Naskh hand. Pupils look at each letter carefully — and count how many letters carry dots — then practise the letters with the reed pen or pencil.

## Sub lesson 2 — Joining the letters, family by family

Then each letter is joined to every other letter in turn: ب (b) first, then ج and its like-shaped family, س, ص, ط, ع, ف, ک, ل, م and ه — each in four ranges of partners. The method never changes: observe the model combinations carefully, then practise them with the reed pen in the notebook.

## Sub lesson 3 — Naskh letters in every position of a word

In Grade 6 the hand is completed: the movements of the Naskh letters in the different positions of words — beginning, middle and end — and each letter's combinations with the others, written by the rules of the script.
