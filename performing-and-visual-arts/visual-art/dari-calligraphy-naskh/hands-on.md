# Hands-on — Dari Calligraphy: Naskh

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Do it the book's way
Set a model line at the top of your page. Look at one letter for a full minute — its dots, its teeth, its tail — then write it ten times below, slowly, with pencil or a cut reed pen. Then join it to the next letter and do the same.
