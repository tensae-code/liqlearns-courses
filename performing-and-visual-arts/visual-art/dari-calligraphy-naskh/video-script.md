# Video script — Dari Calligraphy: Naskh

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Beautiful writing — husn-e khatt.

This is Naskh — the clear hand of Dari.
First, the letters alone. Look well. Count the dots.
Then letter joins letter — b to all, j to all, s to all.
Look at the model line. Then practise. Look — then practise.
That is how the hand is learned.
