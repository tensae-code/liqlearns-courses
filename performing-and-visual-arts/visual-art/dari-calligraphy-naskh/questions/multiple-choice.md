# Dari Calligraphy: Naskh — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Calligraphy in Afghanistan's schools is practised with…
a) a reed pen (qalam-e ney)  b) a paint roller  c) a computer mouse
**Answer: a) a reed pen (qalam-e ney)**

**2.** The model line the pupil copies is called…
a) sarmashq (سرمشق)  b) jantri  c) chalipa
**Answer: a) sarmashq (سرمشق)**

**3.** The first step of the method is…
a) to look at the model line carefully, then practise it  b) to write as fast as possible  c) to close your eyes
**Answer: a) to look at the model line carefully, then practise it**

**4.** In Naskh, a letter's shape…
a) can change with its position in the word  b) never changes at all  c) is drawn upside down
**Answer: a) can change with its position in the word**
