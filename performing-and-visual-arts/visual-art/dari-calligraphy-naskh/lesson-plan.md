# Lesson plan — Dari Calligraphy: Naskh

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Visual Art · **Lesson:** Dari Calligraphy: Naskh
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 2 — Visual Art (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** writing Dari in the Naskh script — first the alphabet letters on their own, then each letter joined to every other letter, always by looking carefully at the model line (سرمشق, sarmashq) and then practising it (مشق) with the reed pen (قلم نی).
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the learner's earlier visual-art lessons. Recommended, never forced.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The alphabet letters in Naskh** — Afghanistan's Grade 2 Beautiful Writing (حسن خط) year opens with the whole Dari alphabet written in the Naskh hand. Pupi…
2. **Joining the letters, family by family** — Then each letter is joined to every other letter in turn: ب (b) first, then ج and its like-shaped family, س, ص, ط, ع, ف,…
3. **Naskh letters in every position of a word** — In Grade 6 the hand is completed: the movements of the Naskh letters in the different positions of words — beginning, mi…

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
