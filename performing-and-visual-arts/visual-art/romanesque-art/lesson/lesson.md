# Romanesque Art

## Do it first

Name one thing that belongs in this lesson — Romanesque art — the round-arched art of medieval Europe before the Gothic. Say what it is in your own words. If you can name one, you are already inside the lesson.

## Sub lesson 1 — Arta romanică

Unit IV §1.2, Arta romanică — Romanesque art (10th–12th centuries): fortress architecture — castles, fortified monasteries like Cluny, the Alba Iulia cathedral; sculpture serving the church — portal reliefs, schematic figures, fantastic animals; mural fresco; the illuminated miniature; the Bayeux tapestry.
