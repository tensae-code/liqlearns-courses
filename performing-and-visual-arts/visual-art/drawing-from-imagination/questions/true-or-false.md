# Drawing from Imagination — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In free-theme drawing the learner chooses the theme.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A free theme must be copied from the book's pictures.
**Answer: False** — it comes from your own daily life.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Your own daily life can give you drawing themes.
**Answer: True**
