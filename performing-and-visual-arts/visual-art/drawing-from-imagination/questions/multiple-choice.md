# Drawing from Imagination — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In free-theme drawing, the theme is…
a) set by the teacher  b) chosen by you, from your own daily life  c) copied from a book
**Answer: b) chosen by you, from your own daily life**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A good free theme is one that…
a) interests you  b) bores you  c) someone else picked
**Answer: a) interests you**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Free-theme drawing draws…
a) only what is in front of you  b) what you choose and imagine from your life  c) nothing
**Answer: b) what you choose and imagine from your life**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** The Turkmen title "Erkin temada surat çekmek" means…
a) drawing on a free theme  b) drawing a house  c) copying lines
**Answer: a) drawing on a free theme**
