# Hands-on — Inuit Arts and Crafts

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Name the form
Show a picture or a real object — a beaded piece, a sewn piece, a print, a carving, a garment, a tool, a jewel. Your partner names the form and one thing that makes it art.

## Make one form yours
Choose one form from the lesson and make a small piece in it — a paper-bead collar pattern, a stitched square, a drawn print design. Say which Inuit form you followed.
