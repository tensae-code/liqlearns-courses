# Inuit Arts and Crafts — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 5)** What hard materials do Inuit carvers turn into animals full of life?
**Answer: Stone and ivory.**

**2. (Sub lesson 6)** Name two pieces of clothing the book teaches as designed artworks.
**Answer: The sealskin parka and the kamik with embroidered, beaded tops.**

**3. (Sub lesson 7)** What is the ulu, and why does the book call it art?
**Answer: The ulu is the women's knife, made as a beautiful object as well as a working tool.**
