# Inuit Arts and Crafts — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Inuit artworks come in…
a) only one form — carving  b) many forms — beadwork, sewing, weaving, drawing, carving, clothing and tools  c) only forms copied from elsewhere
**Answer: b) many forms — beadwork, sewing, weaving, drawing, carving, clothing and tools** — many forms, and each is real art.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Beadwork is…
a) quick work finished in minutes  b) a form Inuit learned from other cultures and made their own  c) never worn
**Answer: b) a form Inuit learned from other cultures and made their own** — learned from other cultures, made their own; its collars and necklaces are beautiful.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Sewn works are made from…
a) plastic only  b) cloth, sealskin and caribou skin  c) paper
**Answer: b) cloth, sealskin and caribou skin** — cloth, sealskin and caribou skin — dolls, kamik tops and hanging pieces.

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** "The Enchanted Owl" is…
a) a stone carving  b) Kenojuak Ashevak's 1960 stonecut print  c) a woven hanging
**Answer: b) Kenojuak Ashevak's 1960 stonecut print** — a 1960 stonecut by Kenojuak Ashevak (Dorset Fine Arts).

**5. (Sub lesson 8)** Inuit jewellery is worked in…
a) silver, into ulu-shaped and antler-form pieces  b) clay  c) glass beads only
**Answer: a) silver, into ulu-shaped and antler-form pieces** — silver worked at the bench into ulu-shaped and antler-form earrings and pendants.
