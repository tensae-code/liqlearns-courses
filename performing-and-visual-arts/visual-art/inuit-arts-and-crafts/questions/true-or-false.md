# Inuit Arts and Crafts — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Every form in this lesson — beadwork, sewing, carving, clothing, tools — is taught as real art.
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Beadwork pieces such as collars and necklaces are finished quickly.
**Answer: False — beadwork is slow, long work.**

**3. (Sub lesson 4)** Drawings and prints are made and sold at print centres such as Pangnirtung.
**Answer: True.**

**4. (Sub lesson 6)** Clothing cannot be art, because it is worn.
**Answer: False — the parka and the kamik are designed artworks.**
