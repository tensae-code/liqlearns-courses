# Video script — Inuit Arts and Crafts

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Many hands, many arts.

Beads — slow, beautiful. Cloth and skin — sewn small and fine.

A print from stone. A carving full of life.

A parka. A kamik. A knife made beautiful. Silver shining.

That is Inuit art — every form of it.
