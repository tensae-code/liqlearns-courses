# Typography — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Serif letters have…
a) small tails/lines extended on them  b) no tails at all  c) pictures inside them
**Answer: a) small tails/lines extended on them**

**2.** Sans-serif letters are letters…
a) without tails  b) written in clay  c) always three-dimensional
**Answer: a) without tails**

**3.** Which is one of Primary 4's named letter styles?
a) Gothic  b) Coiled  c) Stamped
**Answer: a) Gothic**

**4.** Three-dimensional letters are…
a) letters given depth, standing out from the page  b) letters sung aloud  c) letters woven in fibre
**Answer: a) letters given depth, standing out from the page**
