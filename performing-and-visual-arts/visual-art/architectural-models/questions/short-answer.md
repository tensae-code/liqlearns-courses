# Architectural Models — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Name three parts added to the card house.
**Answer: Roof tiles, shutters and a door.**

**2. (Sub lesson 4)** What are the street model's boxes decorated with?
**Answer: Windows and signs cut from card or stickers — town hall, hotel, museum.**

**3. (Sub lesson 6)** Name the fairy-tale castle example and where it stands.
**Answer: Neuschwanstein, on a rocky terrace in the Bavarian Alps.**
