# Architectural Models — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Architecture is the art of…
a) designing buildings  b) folding paper  c) weaving cloth
**Answer: a) designing buildings**.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** A macheta is…
a) a scale model that keeps the proportion among its elements  b) a full-size building  c) a drawing with no proportion
**Answer: a) a scale model that keeps the proportion among its elements**.

**3. (Sub lesson 5)** The first castles were built of…
a) wood, later stone  b) glass  c) plastic
**Answer: a) wood, later stone**.

**4. (Sub lesson 7)** Gaudí's forms are inspired by…
a) nature  b) machines  c) letters
**Answer: a) nature** — compared to a fantasy world of dragons and sandcastles.

**5. (Sub lesson 8)** The School Street project begins by…
a) walking the street and noting what you observe  b) buying a model  c) painting a castle
**Answer: a) walking the street and noting what you observe**.
