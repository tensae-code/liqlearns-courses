# Video script — Architectural Models

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A building begins as an idea — then as a model.

Fold, cut, glue — a card house!

Paint the boxes — a whole street!

Towers and walls — a castle!

Small, but in true proportion. That is the **macheta** — the architect's model.
