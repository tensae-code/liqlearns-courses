# Hands-on — Architectural Models

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Card house
Fold, cut and glue a card house with roof tiles, shutters and a door, following pictured steps you draw for yourself first.

## Our street in miniature
With your team, paint cardboard boxes, add windows and signs (town hall, hotel, museum), and glue them onto a big piece of card as a model street. Check: is the proportion among the buildings kept?
