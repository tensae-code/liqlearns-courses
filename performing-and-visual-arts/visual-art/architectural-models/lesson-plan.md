# Lesson plan — Architectural Models

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Visual Art · **Lesson:** Architectural Models
**Place in the curriculum:** Visual Art (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** architecture and its models — what architecture is and Ion Mincu's Romanian style, building card houses and castle models, the macheta (scale model), modelling a town street and your school street, and Gaudí's nature-inspired buildings.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this course in the same title.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Architecture and Ion Mincu** — architecture is the art of designing buildings — the architect always reckons a building's function, its placing in space and the balance of proportions among its elements; Ion Mincu (1852–1912) founded a new architectural style inspired by specifically Romanian elements.
2. **Build a house** — follow the pictured steps to fold, cut and glue a card house with roof tiles, shutters and a door.
3. **Villages, towns and the macheta** — the space built by people — houses, village, district, town, motorways, bridges, dams — is urban or rural; and the macheta: architects make scale models of streets, districts and whole towns before building them, keeping the proportion among the elements.
4. **Model a town street** — paint cardboard boxes first, decorate them with windows and signs cut from card or stickers (town hall, hotel, museum), and glue the boxes onto a big piece of card — the model is ready.
5. **Storybook castles** — a castle is an imposing construction that was both dwelling and shelter against enemies for noble families, first built of wood, later of stone; Romania's castles in pictures — Hunedoara, Bran and Peleș — and landscape architecture, the art of planning the green space around a house, garden or road.
6. **My castle** — build a castle model with your team, following the given model, decorated with crepe paper, polystyrene pieces and buttons; Neuschwanstein, the 'Sleeping Beauty castle' on a rocky terrace in the Bavarian Alps, is the fairy-tale example.
7. **Antoni Gaudí** — Spain's most original modern architect (1852–1926): forms inspired by nature, buildings covered with very colourful mosaics of tile, glass or stone; the Sagrada Familia, begun over 100 years ago and still unfinished; La Pedrera, its stone blocks in wavy lines like the sea's waves, with wrought-iron lace balconies.
8. **Project: the School Street** — walk your school's street and note what you observe; draw a sketch keeping its essential elements; then build a team model: 'The School Street in the Future'.
