Архитектуралық нысандар жасау (Making architectural objects — digital architecture and the process) — what it teaches: one of architecture's important peculiarities is reckoning the coming time, calculating it beforehand: it is the reflection of time's, society development's, a person's dream and striving; wrap the model's side edges going round with paper strips and glue them to the base; choose the model's colour in accordance with your idea and sketch (student book; original target `visual-art/making-an-architectural-model`) | Book 4, printed p. 144 |

Сәулеттік нысанның макетін орындауға қажетті материалдарды анықтау және таңдау (Defining and choosing the materials needed for carrying out an architectural object's model) — what it teaches: Сәулеттік макет (an architectural model) is a volumetric-spatial image of a designed or existing building made in reduced volume, and usually models are made at 1:100 or 1:200 масштаб (scale — Масштаб / Масштаб / Scale: the ratio of two numbers showing how many times a thing's real measurements (height, length, width and the like) are reduced or enlarged, written as a fraction whose numerator is one and whose denominator is the reduction degree — for example, if an object's real length is 12 metres, at 1:100 scale this measurement will be 120 mm, and each measurement must be calculated like this); the model consists of a тұғыр (base/stand), the building and the construction's form, and additionally planning elements, greening and decorating can be planned as an addition; the materials used in making a model divide into two — негізгі (main: intended for preparing the model base and the architectural object's parts) and қосымша (additional: intended for joining construction parts, painting and decorating); as the main material for preparing a model, paper is used — paper is convenient for building geometric forms exactly and for drying varied textures — and together with it қатырма қағаз (cardboard), ермексаз (plasticine), ағаш сүрегі (wood), гипс (gypsum), қаппарқатырма, көбікті қатырма (foam board), ПВХ (PVC — a thin synthetic material made from hard foam, enduring the environment's influence), органикалық шыны (organic glass — a very enduring hard material made from organic polymers; for a model, 0.1–0.5 cm thick organic glass is used) and other materials are used; көбікті қатырма (foam board) is a board kind in the form of a plate made from foam polyurethane glued on two sides — a light, thin material which, when bent, does not break and preserves its form well (student book; | Kazakhstan — § 10. Шығармашылық жұмыс. Сәулеттік нысан макеті (Creative work. An architectural object's model) — what it teaches: two architectural models are built step by step in photographs: the first variant — a stepped tower of nested square frames rising to a spire, cut from paper and card, assembled and crowned with a coloured geometric solid; the second variant — a round pavilion with columns, a dome and a flag, its parts cut, rolled and glued; for glueing parts, special glues used in modelling can be used, and if the bottle has no applicator the glue should be poured into a medical syringe with a needle — then the glue can be carefully applied; for making any curved form, the paper must be passed over a cylindrical thing (for example, a pencil or a pen); if the place where paper or cardboard folds is scored (кертін қойса), its edges do not break, do not bend and stay uniform; finished things made of paper cannot be painted, because when painted their kind changes — paper must be processed before painting: it must be painted in two directions, vertically and horizontally, and then it paints uniformly; newly painted paper swells and changes, and to prevent that the paper must be fixed to a flat place — when dried it stretches and straightens, and then it can be used in the model; for painting paper, watercolour, ink and even nitrate paints can be used, but for that an airbrush or a sprayer must be used (student book; original target `visual-art/making-an-architectural-model`) | Book 5, printed p. 38 |
