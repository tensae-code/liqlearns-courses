# Architectural Models

## Do it first

Quick start: look at your classroom. If you had to build it small enough to stand on this table — what would you keep, and what would you leave out? Then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — Architecture and Ion Mincu

**Architecture** is the **art of designing buildings**. The architect designs a building always reckoning **its function, its placing in space and the balance of proportions among its elements**. **Ion Mincu (1852–1912)** founded a **new architectural style inspired by specifically Romanian elements** — the Lahovari House, the Vernescu House, the Central School for Girls, the Kiseleff Buffet and the Robescu Villa.

## Sub lesson 2 — Build a house

Now build: follow the pictured steps to **fold, cut and glue a card house** — with **roof tiles, shutters and a door**. The model house is architecture made small enough to hold.

## Sub lesson 3 — Villages, towns and the macheta

The **space built by people** — houses, village, district, town, motorways, bridges, dams — is **urban or rural**. Before building, architects make a **macheta**: a **scale model** of streets, districts and whole towns. A model **reproduces the work at a smaller scale while keeping the proportion among its elements**.

## Sub lesson 4 — Model a town street

**On the Main Street**: model a town street — **paint cardboard boxes** first with beautiful coloured paint, **decorate them with windows and signs** cut from card or stickers (**town hall, hotel, museum**), and **glue the boxes onto a big piece of card** — the model is ready.

## Sub lesson 5 — Storybook castles

A **castle** is an imposing construction that was both **dwelling and shelter against enemies** for noble families — **first built of wood, later of stone**. Romania's castles in pictures: **Hunedoara, Bran and Peleș**. Beside them stands **landscape architecture** — the art of planning the green space around a house, garden or road.

## Sub lesson 6 — My castle

**My Castle!** Build a **castle model with your team**, following the given model, and decorate it with **crepe paper, polystyrene pieces and buttons**. The fairy-tale example is **Neuschwanstein**, the 'Sleeping Beauty castle' on a rocky terrace in the Bavarian Alps.

## Sub lesson 7 — Antoni Gaudí

**Antoni Gaudí (1852–1926)** is Spain's most original modern architect. His **forms are inspired by nature** — his architecture is compared to a children's fantasy world of dragons and sandcastles — and most of his buildings are covered with **very colourful mosaics of tile, glass or stone** in abstract forms. The **Sagrada Familia** was begun over 100 years ago and is **still unfinished**; **La Pedrera** sets its great stone blocks in **wavy lines like the sea's waves**, with **wrought-iron lace balconies**.

## Sub lesson 8 — Project: the School Street

**Project: The School Street.** **Walk your school's street** with the class and note what you observe — houses, blocks, administrative buildings, the children's play space, what the buildings share and how they differ. **Draw a sketch** of the street keeping its essential elements. Then build a **team model: 'The School Street in the Future'**.
