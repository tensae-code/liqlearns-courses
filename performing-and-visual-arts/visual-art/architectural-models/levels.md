# Architectural Models — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — Architecture and Ion Mincu
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: say what architecture is and the three things the architect always reckons — function, placing in space, balance of proportions — and name Ion Mincu and his Romanian-inspired style.

## Sub lesson 2 — Build a house
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: give the house build in order — fold, cut and glue the card house, with roof tiles, shutters and a door.

## Sub lesson 3 — Villages, towns and the macheta
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: tell urban from rural built space, and say what a macheta is — a scale model that reproduces the work smaller while keeping the proportion among its elements.

## Sub lesson 4 — Model a town street
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: give the street model's steps in order — paint the boxes, decorate with windows and signs, glue onto a big piece of card.

## Sub lesson 5 — Storybook castles
Sub lesson 5 questions from the type files. Pass mark: say what a castle is — dwelling and shelter, first wood then stone — and name Romania's three pictured castles: Hunedoara, Bran and Peleș.

## Sub lesson 6 — My castle
Sub lesson 6 questions from the type files. Pass mark: say how the team castle is built and decorated — following the given model, with crepe paper, polystyrene pieces and buttons — and name the fairy-tale example, Neuschwanstein.

## Sub lesson 7 — Antoni Gaudí
Sub lesson 7 questions from the type files. Pass mark: say what Gaudí's forms are inspired by — nature — and name his two works in the book: the Sagrada Familia (still unfinished) and La Pedrera (wavy stone, iron lace balconies).

## Sub lesson 8 — Project: the School Street
Sub lesson 8 questions from the type files. Pass mark: give the project's three steps in order — walk and observe the street, draw a sketch of its essential elements, build the team model 'The School Street in the Future'.
