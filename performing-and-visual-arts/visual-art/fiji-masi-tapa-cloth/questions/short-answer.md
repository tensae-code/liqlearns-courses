# Fiji Masi: Tapa Cloth — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What joins the overlapped bark pieces into larger cloth?
**Answer: The bark's own resin, beaten in with the ike mallet (edges stuck with yabia arrowroot starch).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How do Southern Lau designs differ from Vatulele's?
**Answer: Southern Lau is very detailed and intricate; Vatulele is big and bold.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name the traditional stencil material.
**Answer: Banana or pandanus leaves.**
