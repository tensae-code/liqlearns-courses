# Fiji Masi: Tapa Cloth — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Masi is made from the bark of…
a) the paper mulberry  b) the coconut palm  c) the mango tree
**Answer: a) the paper mulberry.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The bark is beaten with…
a) the ike mallet  b) a hammer of iron only  c) a rolling pin
**Answer: a) the ike mallet.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Vatulele's masi designs are…
a) big and bold  b) tiny and faint  c) plain white
**Answer: a) big and bold.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A printed masi is built in the order…
a) borders, semi-borders, centre piece  b) centre, borders, semi-borders  c) any order
**Answer: a) borders, semi-borders, centre piece.**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Modern masi stencils are cut on…
a) used x-ray film  b) glass  c) tin
**Answer: a) used x-ray film.**
