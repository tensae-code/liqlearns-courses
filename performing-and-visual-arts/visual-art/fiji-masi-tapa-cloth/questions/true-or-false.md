# Fiji Masi: Tapa Cloth — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Masi Kuvui, smoked brown, denotes chiefly status.
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Masi is worn as meke costume.
**Answer: True.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The centre piece is printed first.
**Answer: False — borders first, centre last.**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Stencils are thrown away after one use.
**Answer: False — they are reusable.**
