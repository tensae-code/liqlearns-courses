# Hands-on — Fiji Masi: Tapa Cloth

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Beat the bark (model)
With a soft mallet on layered cloth or paper, feel how beating joins layers. Say the real steps in order: strip, scrape, steep, separate, beat.

## Border first
Cut a simple triangle stencil and dab a border around a sheet, corners first — no excess paint.
