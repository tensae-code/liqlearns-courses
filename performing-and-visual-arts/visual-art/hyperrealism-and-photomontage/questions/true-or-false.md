# Hyperrealism and Photomontage — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A hyperrealist work is a photograph, not a painting.
**Answer: False** — it is painting seeking photography's register.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Photomontage joins photographs into a new picture.
**Answer: True** — that is its making.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Hyperrealism exists only outside Colombia, in this guide.
**Answer: False** — the guide names its exponents in Colombia.
