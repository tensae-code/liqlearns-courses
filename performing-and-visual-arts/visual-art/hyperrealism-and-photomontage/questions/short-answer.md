# Hyperrealism and Photomontage — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does a hyperrealist painting try to hold, in the guide's words?
**Answer: The register of photography — what a camera would hold.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How is a photomontage different from a single photograph?
**Answer: It is built by joining photographs into one picture.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Why is "Lavabo y espejo" a good hyperrealist example?
**Answer: An ordinary bathroom painted until it registers like a photograph.**
