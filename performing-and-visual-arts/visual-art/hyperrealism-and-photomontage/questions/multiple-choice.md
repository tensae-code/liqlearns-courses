# Hyperrealism and Photomontage — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Hyperrealism is painting that seeks the register of…
a) photography  b) cave art  c) weaving
**Answer: a) photography**.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** "Lavabo y espejo" is by…
a) Antonio López  b) Miró  c) Calatrava
**Answer: a) Antonio López**.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A photomontage is built by…
a) joining photographs  b) firing clay  c) carving stone
**Answer: a) joining photographs**.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The guide names hyperrealism's exponents in…
a) Colombia  b) only one foreign city  c) no country
**Answer: a) Colombia**.
