## Sub lesson 1 — Hyperrealism — painting that seeks photography's register

**Hyperrealism: painting that seeks the register of photography** — the guide's example is **Antonio López's "Lavabo y espejo" (Washbasin and mirror)**, a bathroom painted until it holds what a camera would hold.

## Sub lesson 2 — Photomontage — and Colombia's own hyperrealists

Beside the painting stands **photomontage** — a picture built by joining photographs — and the movement comes home: the guide names **hyperrealism's own exponents in Colombia**.
