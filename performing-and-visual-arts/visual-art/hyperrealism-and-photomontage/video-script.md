# Video script — Hyperrealism and Photomontage

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Is it a photo? No — it is paint.

**Hyperrealism**: painting that seeks the **register of photography**. **Antonio López** painted a washbasin and a mirror — "Lavabo y espejo" — until the paint held what a camera holds.

And **photomontage**: pictures built by **joining photographs**.

Colombia has its own hyperrealists. The guide names them.
