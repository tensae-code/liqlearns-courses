# Video script — Craft Materials and How They Behave

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Stone. Wood. Bone. Hard — and they last.

Clay. Plaster. Plasticine. Soft — mix them with water.

Add a little. Take a little away. The shape grows in your hands.

Every material behaves in its own way.

Learn its way — and it will work with you.
