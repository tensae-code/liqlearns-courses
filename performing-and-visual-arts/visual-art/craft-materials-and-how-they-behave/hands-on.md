# Hands-on — Craft Materials and How They Behave

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Press a lid picture
Wash and dry a yoghurt-cup lid. Set it on folded newspaper and press a picture into it with a blunt pencil or little stick — a work made by pressure alone.

## Add and take away
Take a lump of clay or plasticine. Build a small animal by adding mass, then change it by taking mass away. Say which change was easier, and why.
