# Craft Materials and How They Behave — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Stone resists weather conditions.
**Answer: True** — that is one of the hard materials' properties.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Clay is a hard material.
**Answer: False** — clay is a soft material.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Plasticine is soft, elastic and fragile.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Soft materials are shaped without ever adding or removing mass.
**Answer: False** — shaping them is adding and taking away mass.
