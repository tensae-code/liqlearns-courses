# Craft Materials and How They Behave — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Into which two groups do materials divide by their basic properties?
**Answer: Hard materials and soft materials.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the three properties of soft materials.
**Answer: Softness, elasticity and fragility.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Name three tools for shaping soft materials.
**Answer: Any three of: little sticks, feathers, wooden sticks, plastic sticks and wires.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** What do you press your work into in the book's exercise?
**Answer: A prepared yoghurt-cup lid, set on several layers of newspaper, pressed with a blunt pencil or little stick.**
