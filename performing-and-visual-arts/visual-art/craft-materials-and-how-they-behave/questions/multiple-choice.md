# Craft Materials and How They Behave — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Hard materials for shaping are…
a) stone, wood and bone  b) clay and plasticine  c) paper and wool
**Answer: a) stone, wood and bone**.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The properties of hard materials are…
a) hardness, permanence and resistance to weather  b) softness and fragility  c) elasticity only
**Answer: a) hardness, permanence and resistance to weather**.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Soft materials are mixed with…
a) water, so they can be shaped  b) sand, so they harden  c) paint, so they shine
**Answer: a) water, so they can be shaped**.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Shaping soft material works by…
a) adding and taking away mass  b) only cutting  c) only painting
**Answer: a) adding and taking away mass**.
