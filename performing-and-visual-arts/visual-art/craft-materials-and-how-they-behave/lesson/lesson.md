# Craft Materials and How They Behave

## Sub lesson 1 — Hard materials

There are many kinds of materials for artistic shaping, but by their basic properties they divide into **two groups — hard and soft**. **Hard materials are stone, wood, bone and the like**, and their properties are **hardness, permanence and resistance to weather conditions**. The book pictures **worked and rough stone, worked and rough wood**.

## Sub lesson 2 — Soft materials

**Soft materials are clay, plaster and plasticine and the like**, and their properties are **softness, elasticity and fragility**. Soft materials are **mixed with water** so that they can be shaped, and work with them is **shaping by adding and taking away mass**, for which **little sticks, feathers, wooden or plastic sticks and wires** can be used as tools. The exercise: wash, dry and prepare several **yoghurt-cup lids**, set one on a ground of several layers of newspaper, and **with a blunt pencil or little stick, by pressing, make a work** on a theme of your free choice.
