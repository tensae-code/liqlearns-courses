# Hands-on — Shadow Art

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Shadow hunt
Go outdoors and find five different shadows. Stand in the sun and make three shadow figures with your body — a friend names each one.

## Shadow drawing
Lay a leaf, twig or pebble so its shadow falls on your paper. Trace the shadow, then turn the traced shape into a new picture of your own.
