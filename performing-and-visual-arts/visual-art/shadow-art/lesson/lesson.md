# Shadow Art

## Do it first

Quick start: from the title alone, guess what this lesson's work is. Then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — Hunting shadows and making them

The **"Shadow Art" example (Kindergarten 2)** starts outdoors: **hunting shadows**, then **making shadow figures with the body** — hands, arms and the whole body cast the shapes. Then children **build shadow artworks from leaves, twigs, pebbles and feathers** — things whose shadows join the work.

## Sub lesson 2 — A new picture from a cast shadow

The last step turns a shadow into a drawing: take **an object's cast shadow** and **draw a new picture out of it** — the shadow gives the shape, the child gives the meaning.
