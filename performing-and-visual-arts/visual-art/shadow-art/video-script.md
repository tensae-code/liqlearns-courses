# Video script — Shadow Art

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Go outside. Hunt a shadow!

Your body makes one — hands up! A shadow figure!

Leaves, twigs, pebbles, feathers — their shadows join the art.

Now draw: a shadow on the paper… and a new picture grows out of it.

That is shadow art — pictures made of light and dark.
