# Lesson plan — Shadow Art

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Visual Art · **Lesson:** Shadow Art
**Place in the curriculum:** Visual Art (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** art made from shadows — hunting shadows outdoors, making shadow figures with the body, building shadow artworks from found things, and drawing a new picture out of an object's cast shadow.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this course in the same title.

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks a shadow is nothing → a shadow has a shape, and shapes are art material: bodies make them, found things build with them, and a cast shadow can start a drawing.
- Cannot find shadows on a dull day → shadow art needs light: hunt where the sun falls, and use the body to cast figures on a lit wall or ground.
