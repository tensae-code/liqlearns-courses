# Shadow Art — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name two found things used to build shadow artworks.
**Answer: Leaves and twigs (pebbles and feathers are also named).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the child's body make in this lesson?
**Answer: Shadow figures.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What starts the last drawing?
**Answer: An object's cast shadow.**
