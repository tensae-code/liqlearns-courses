# Shadow Art — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Shadow art begins with…
a) hunting shadows outdoors  b) mixing paint  c) cutting paper
**Answer: a) hunting shadows outdoors**.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Shadow figures are made with…
a) the body  b) a camera  c) clay
**Answer: a) the body** — hands, arms and the whole body cast the shapes.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Shadow artworks are built from…
a) leaves, twigs, pebbles and feathers  b) plastic toys  c) paint alone
**Answer: a) leaves, twigs, pebbles and feathers**.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A new picture is drawn out of…
a) an object's cast shadow  b) a photograph  c) a story
**Answer: a) an object's cast shadow** — the shadow gives the shape.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The "Shadow Art" example is set for…
a) Kindergarten 2  b) Nursery 2  c) Grade 6
**Answer: a) Kindergarten 2**.
