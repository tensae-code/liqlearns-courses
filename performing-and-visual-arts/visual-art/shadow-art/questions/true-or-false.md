# Shadow Art — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Children hunt shadows outdoors in this lesson.
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Shadow artworks use only bought materials.
**Answer: False** — they are built from leaves, twigs, pebbles and feathers.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A cast shadow can be turned into a new picture.
**Answer: True.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Shadow figures are made with a camera.
**Answer: False** — they are made with the body.
