# Drawing the Human Figure

## Do it first

Quick start: say in one sentence what you think this lesson is about — drawing the human body by measure: Vitruvian proportion, measuring a partner in head-units with a pencil at arm's length, and the canons of beauty artists built from such measures. Then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — Vitruvian proportion and measuring in head-units

The module opens with **Da Vinci and the Vitruvian figure**: **palm = four fingers, forearm = six palms, height = four forearms**. Then the class measures for itself: **measuring a partner in head-units with a pencil at arm's length and drawing them on the marked vertical**.

## Sub lesson 2 — Canons of beauty

From such measures artists built **canons of beauty** — agreed proportions for the ideal figure. A canon is a measuring rule a whole school shares, not a law of nature: the next lesson of the same guide shows every culture judging beauty differently.
