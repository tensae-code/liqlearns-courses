# Drawing the Human Figure — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In the Vitruvian measures, a palm equals…
a) four fingers  b) six palms  c) four forearms
**Answer: a) four fingers**.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Height, in the same measures, equals…
a) four forearms  b) four fingers  c) six palms
**Answer: a) four forearms**.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** A partner is measured in…
a) head-units, with a pencil at arm's length  b) kilograms  c) song verses
**Answer: a) head-units, with a pencil at arm's length**.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A canon of beauty is…
a) a shared measuring rule for the ideal figure  b) a kind of pencil  c) a museum
**Answer: a) a shared measuring rule for the ideal figure**.
