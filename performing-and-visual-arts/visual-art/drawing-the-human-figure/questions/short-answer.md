# Drawing the Human Figure — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How many palms make a forearm, in the guide's measures?
**Answer: Six palms.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Where do you draw your partner after measuring?
**Answer: On the marked vertical, in the head-units you measured.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** From what did artists build their canons of beauty?
**Answer: From measures of the body, such as the Vitruvian proportions.**
