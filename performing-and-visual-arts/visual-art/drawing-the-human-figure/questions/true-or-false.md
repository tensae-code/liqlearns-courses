# Drawing the Human Figure — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The forearm equals six palms in the Vitruvian scheme.
**Answer: True** — palm = four fingers, forearm = six palms, height = four forearms.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** You measure a partner by walking up and using a tape on their skin.
**Answer: False** — with a pencil held at arm's length, in head-units.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A canon of beauty is a law of nature that never changes.
**Answer: False** — it is a rule a school shares; cultures judge beauty differently.
