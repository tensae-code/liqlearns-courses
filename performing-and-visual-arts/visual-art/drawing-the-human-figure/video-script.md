# Video script — Drawing the Human Figure

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Draw a person — by measure.

**Da Vinci's** figure gives the measures: a **palm is four fingers**. A **forearm is six palms**. **Height is four forearms**.

Now measure your partner: pencil at **arm's length**, count in **head-units**, draw them on the **marked vertical**.

From measures like these, artists built their **canons of beauty** — shared rules for the ideal figure.
