# Hands-on — Drawing the Human Figure

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Measure a partner
Hold a pencil at arm's length and measure your partner in head-units, as the guide directs. Mark the vertical, then draw them to your measure.

## Check the canon
Compare your drawing's proportions with the Vitruvian measures: palm, forearm, height. Where does a real body leave the canon? Note one place.
