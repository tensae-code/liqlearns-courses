# Lesson plan — Kazakh Silver Jewellery

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Visual Art · **Lesson:** Kazakh Silver Jewellery
**Place in the curriculum:** Visual Art (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** kazakh Silver Jewellery — its forms, history and cultural meaning, taught as one topic lesson..
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this course in the same title.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. ## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Kazakhstan Grade 4 Artistic Labour** — Kazakh national jewellery and the zerger (jeweller): alka and onirzhiek necklaces, bilezik bracelets, syrga earrings, sholpy hair pendants, tumar amulets — silver engraved, granulated and set with stones
2. **Kazakhstan Grade 4 Labour Training (Ormanbekova)** — the zerger, master of artistic metalwork, and the "Golden Man" of the Issyk kurgan, whose costume the ancient zergers covered with gold plaques of birds and beasts
3. **Kazakhstan Grade 5 Artistic Labour (boys, Chukalin)** — what it teaches: the zerger jeweller's silver — bracelets (білезік), rings (жүзік, сақина), earrings (сырға) and sholpy hair pendants, silver being the Kazakh jeweller's chief metal
4. **Kazakhstan Grade 6 Artistic Labour (boys, Chukalin)** — what it teaches: the zergers — Kazakh jewellers who worked, alone among the crafts, at a professional level; their pieces were never only decoration — material, form and ornament carried protective, magical meaning; the main kinds pictured and named — clasps (қапсырма) joined by hinge or loop, bracelets (білезік) forged hot or cold, cut from silver plate or plaited from wire and set with coloured stones, signet rings (жүзік) and rings (сақина) worn daily and at feasts, inlaid with semi-precious stones, enamel and glass; women's earrings — the moon earrings айсырға, stone-set тасты сырға, and the two-tiered салпыншақ and шашақ сырға; what it teaches: jewellery that guards its wearer — the breast amulet-cases: triangular тұмарша (the triangle for the three levels of the world), rectangular қолтықша, tubular бойтұмар, and the festive өңіржиек of plaited plates and chains; hair ornaments — шолпы, coin-strung шаштеңге and the long silver chain шашбау; silver itself, the zergers' main metal, held to purify and protect, and the stones with their powers — carnelian for wellbeing, coral against the evil eye, turquoise for happiness, darkening when its owner falls ill; the modern master Berik Alibai, holder of the Carl Fabergé order
