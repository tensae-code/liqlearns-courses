## Sub lesson 1 — Design answering today's needs, responsibly

The project "Nature, art and design" sets design its task: **art and design answering today's needs — inhabiting the planet comfortably, functionally and responsibly** — and building **an environmental conscience through making**.

## Sub lesson 2 — Recycled and found materials

The designer's stock can be what the world throws away: **recycled and found materials** — as in **Miró's sculpture built from things the tide left on the beach**. What is found is not rubbish first; it is material with a shape already in it.
