# Design — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What needs must art and design answer, in the guide's words?
**Answer: Today's needs — inhabiting the planet comfortably, functionally and responsibly.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Give one example of a found material you could design with.
**Answer: Any honest found/recycled material from the learner's surroundings.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Why does the project join nature, art and design in one title?
**Answer: Because design answers today's needs on the planet we inhabit, with an environmental conscience.**
