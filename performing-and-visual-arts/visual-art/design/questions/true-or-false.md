# Design — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Design in this lesson is only about how things look.
**Answer: False** — it must answer needs functionally and responsibly.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A sculpture can be built from things the tide left on a beach.
**Answer: True** — Miró's is the guide's example.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Found materials have no shape or use until a designer sees one in them.
**Answer: False** — the found thing is material with a shape already in it.
