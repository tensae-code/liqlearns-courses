# Design — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The guide's design must inhabit the planet…
a) comfortably, functionally and responsibly  b) quickly and cheaply only  c) in gold and marble
**Answer: a) comfortably, functionally and responsibly**.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Making, in this project, builds…
a) an environmental conscience  b) only a grade  c) a wall
**Answer: a) an environmental conscience**.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Miró's beach sculpture was built from…
a) things the tide left on the beach  b) new marble  c) plastic kits
**Answer: a) things the tide left on the beach**.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Recycled and found materials are, for a designer…
a) material to design with  b) always rubbish  c) forbidden
**Answer: a) material to design with**.
