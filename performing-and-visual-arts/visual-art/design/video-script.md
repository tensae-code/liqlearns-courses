Design is an answer to a need.

Today's need: inhabit this planet **comfortably, functionally, responsibly**. Making things can build an **environmental conscience**.

And the materials? They can be **found**. **Miró** built a sculpture from **things the tide left on the beach**.

Look at what the world throws away. A designer sees material.
