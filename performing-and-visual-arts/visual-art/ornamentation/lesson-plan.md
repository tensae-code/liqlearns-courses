# Lesson plan — Ornamentation

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Visual Art · **Lesson:** Ornamentation
**Place in the curriculum:** Visual Art (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** an ornament is a decoration in the form of a pattern — made from geometric or natural forms repeated in a definite rhythm — with which people have decorated vessels, clothes, covers, furniture, buildings and everyday objects from ancient times to today.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this course in the same title.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **What an ornament is** — People have always had the need to decorate the objects they use, and the spaces they stay in, such as the facades of buildings.
2. **Ornament through the ages — and today** — From ancient times people decorated with ornament, besides vessels, also clothes, covers, bed linen and furniture — and modern man decorates everyday objects too.
3. **Make your own ornament** — The book's three makings: using squared or millimetre paper, make an ornament for your favourite T-shirt; draw and colour an ornament for your coverlet or bed linen; and paint an ornament in the spirit of your own region's heritage on a sheet cut to your notebook's measure, glue it on the cover, and write in it the impressions that matter to you..
