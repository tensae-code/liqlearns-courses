# Video script — Ornamentation

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

One flower is a flower. Repeat it in rhythm — it becomes an ornament.

People have patterned their vessels, clothes and covers for thousands of years.

A mosaic in Ravenna. A hanging from Iran. A dress from 1927.

Take squared paper. Repeat your form. Keep the rhythm.

Now your T-shirt, your coverlet, your notebook can carry it.
