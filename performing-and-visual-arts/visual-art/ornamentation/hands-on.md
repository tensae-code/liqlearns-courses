# Hands-on — Ornamentation

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Rhythm on squared paper
On squared paper, draw one small geometric form. Repeat it across a strip in a steady rhythm, then colour alternate repeats differently. Your strip is an ornament for a T-shirt.

## Heritage cover
Paint an ornament in the spirit of your region's heritage on a sheet cut to your notebook's measure. Glue it on the cover and write inside the impressions that matter to you.
