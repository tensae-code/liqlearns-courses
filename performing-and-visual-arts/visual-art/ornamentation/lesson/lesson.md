# Ornamentation

## Do it first

Quick start: say in one sentence what you think this lesson is about — an ornament is a decoration in the form of a pattern. Then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — What an ornament is

People have always had the need to **decorate the objects they use**, and the spaces they stay in, such as the facades of buildings. **An ornament is a decoration in the form of a pattern.** An ornament or pattern can be made from **geometric or natural forms — a plant or an animal** — and to make an ornament, those forms need to be **repeated in a definite rhythm**. An ornament can be **drawn, painted or shaped** on a sculpture or a building. The book pictures a **Byzantine mosaic from Ravenna**.

## Sub lesson 2 — Ornament through the ages — and today

From ancient times people decorated with ornament, besides vessels, also **clothes, covers, bed linen and furniture** — and **modern man decorates everyday objects too**. Pictured: an **Iranian wall hanging**, a **Sarajevo Quran**, and **Dušan Janković's dress, jacket and shawl of 1927**.

## Sub lesson 3 — Make your own ornament

The book's three makings: **using squared or millimetre paper, make an ornament for your favourite T-shirt**; **draw and colour an ornament for your coverlet or bed linen**; and **paint an ornament in the spirit of your own region's heritage** on a sheet cut to your notebook's measure, glue it on the cover, and write in it the impressions that matter to you.
