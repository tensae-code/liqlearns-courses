# Ornamentation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** An ornament is…
a) a decoration in the form of a pattern  b) a kind of vessel  c) a stage light
**Answer: a) a decoration in the form of a pattern**.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** To make an ornament, forms must be…
a) repeated in a definite rhythm  b) used once only  c) hidden
**Answer: a) repeated in a definite rhythm**.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** From ancient times, people decorated with ornament…
a) vessels, clothes, covers, bed linen and furniture  b) only aeroplanes  c) only books
**Answer: a) vessels, clothes, covers, bed linen and furniture**.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** The T-shirt ornament is made on…
a) squared or millimetre paper  b) a wall  c) a lid
**Answer: a) squared or millimetre paper**.
