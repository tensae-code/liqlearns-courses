# Ornamentation — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** From what two kinds of forms can an ornament be made?
**Answer: Geometric forms, or natural forms — a plant or an animal.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In what three ways can an ornament be made on things?
**Answer: It can be drawn, painted or shaped.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Name two pictured ornaments or ornamented things.
**Answer: Any two of: the Byzantine mosaic from Ravenna, the Iranian wall hanging, the Sarajevo Quran, Dušan Janković's dress, jacket and shawl of 1927.**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** What spirit should the notebook-cover ornament carry?
**Answer: The spirit of your own region's heritage.**
