# Hands-on — Colours on Clothes


The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The clothes audit
At home, audit one shelf or line of family clothes by colour: count garments per colour and find the family's most-worn colour. Report it with the exercise's answers.

## Everyday and festival
Draw the same person twice: in everyday clothes and in festival dress, coloured truly. The pair of drawings shows how one person's colours change with the day.
