# Colours on Clothes — true or false

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Naming a garment's colour precisely — like "green-and-yellow" — is part of this lesson's skill.
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The same person's everyday colours and festival colours are always the same.
**Answer: False — everyday and festival colours are often different.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Traditional dress colours are chosen by accident, with no meaning.
**Answer: False — its colours express cultural values, as the pictures' caption teaches.**
