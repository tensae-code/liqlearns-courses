# Colours on Clothes — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The book's garment photographs show —
a) a red T-shirt, a green-and-yellow sports shirt, a blue jacket  b) three white dresses  c) hats only
**Answer: a) a red T-shirt, a green-and-yellow sports shirt and a blue jacket.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The sports shirt's colours are —
a) red and blue  b) green and yellow  c) black and white
**Answer: b) green and yellow — a partnership of two colours.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Exercise 3.1.4 asks —
a) what colours of clothes your father and mother wear, and your classmates' colours  b) the price of clothes  c) how clothes are sewn
**Answer: a) what colours of clothes your father and mother wear — and what colours your classmates' clothes are.**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** The dress pictures' caption teaches that traditional dress expresses —
a) the weather  b) cultural values in the colours of dress  c) nothing
**Answer: b) cultural values in the colours of dress.**

**5. (Sub lesson 4)** The Grade 1 summary says colours —
a) distinguish our clothes and beautify the surroundings  b) hide our clothes  c) matter only in books
**Answer: a) distinguish our clothes and beautify the surroundings — plants green, flowers red and yellow.**
