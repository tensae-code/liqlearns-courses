# Hands-on — Textile Design and Surface Pattern

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Book 3, Design, "Textile Design and Printing"
Do the thing this part teaches, with real materials where the book names them: designing for cloth — putting the designs made for fabrics, preparing and printing them to the consumer's need: designs fitted to a child's taste, a woman's, a man's, and cloth and wallpaper fitted to offices, study rooms and homes, for clothes, furnishings and carpets; the design summed in the repeated decorative unit, composed from nature, folk arts or historical legacies through the designer's stored shapes, colours and lines
