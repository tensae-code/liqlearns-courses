# Textile Design and Surface Pattern — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Where do textile designs come from, in the book?
**Answer: From nature, folk arts or historical legacies, through the designer's stored shapes, colours and lines.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the repeated unit called?
**Answer: The repeated decorative unit.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Name two uses of designed cloth.
**Answer: Any two of: clothes, furnishings, carpets (and wallpaper for rooms).**
