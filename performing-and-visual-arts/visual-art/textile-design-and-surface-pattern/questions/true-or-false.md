# Textile Design and Surface Pattern — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Textile design is printing designs to the consumer's need.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** One design fits every wearer and every room.
**Answer: False** — Designs are fitted to a child's taste, a woman's, a man's, and rooms by their use.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The repeated decorative unit sums up the design.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Folk heritage is one source of textile designs.
**Answer: True**
