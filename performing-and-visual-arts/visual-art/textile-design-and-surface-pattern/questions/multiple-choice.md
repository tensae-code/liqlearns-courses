# Textile Design and Surface Pattern — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A textile design is summed in…
a) the repeated decorative unit  b) one single dot  c) a written essay
**Answer: a) the repeated decorative unit** — composed from nature, folk arts or historical legacies.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Designs are fitted to…
a) a child's taste, a woman's, a man's  b) one taste only  c) no one
**Answer: a) a child's taste, a woman's, a man's** — and cloth and wallpaper fitted to offices, study rooms and homes.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The designer's stored materials are…
a) shapes, colours and lines  b) only numbers  c) only words
**Answer: a) shapes, colours and lines**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Textile designs serve…
a) clothes, furnishings and carpets  b) only flags  c) only books
**Answer: a) clothes, furnishings and carpets**
