# Gothic Art

## Do it first

Name one thing that belongs in this lesson — Gothic art — the art of the Gothic cathedral age in medieval Europe. Say what it is in your own words. If you can name one, you are already inside the lesson.

## Sub lesson 1 — Arta gotică

Unit IV §1.3, Arta gotică — Gothic art: the cathedral rises in the city's centre — pointed (ogival) vaults, wide windows, the rose window and stained glass; Notre Dame, Chartres, Brașov's Black Church; statue-columns whose bodies regain nature's true proportions; panel painting (the Trebon Altarpiece).

## Do It Yourself
