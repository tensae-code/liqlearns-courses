This is Moldova's own lesson.

**Arta gotică.** Unit IV §1.3, Arta gotică — Gothic art: the cathedral rises in the city's centre — pointed (ogival) vaults, wide windows, the rose window and stained glass; Notre Dame, Chartres, Brașov's Black Church; statue-columns whose bodies regain nature's true proportions; panel painting (the Trebon Altarpiece).

That is Gothic Art — Moldova's way, kept by name.
