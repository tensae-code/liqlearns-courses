# Hands-on — Decorative-Applied Art of Belarus

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## A useful beautiful thing
Take a plain useful thing — a cup, a spoon, a box. Decorate it the way a folk master would: a band of pattern that fits its shape and its use. Say which Belarusian craft yours stands beside, and why.

## Craft map
List the four crafts with their places. Add one useful decorated thing from your own home and say what place's hand it carries.
