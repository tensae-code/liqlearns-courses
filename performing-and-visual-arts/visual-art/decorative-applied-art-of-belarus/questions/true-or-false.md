# Decorative-Applied Art of Belarus — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Every Belarusian craft in the book is named by its place.
**Answer: True**.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The koushyk ladle comes from Pruzhany.
**Answer: False** — it is of the Minsk region.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Ivenets ceramics is an old folk craft.
**Answer: True**.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Haradnaya is known for weaving.
**Answer: False** — Haradnaya is known for pottery.
