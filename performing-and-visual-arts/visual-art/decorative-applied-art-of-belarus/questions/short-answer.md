# Decorative-Applied Art of Belarus — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the four Belarusian crafts of this lesson, with their places.
**Answer: The koushyk ladle (Minsk region), Haradnaya pottery, Ivenets ceramics, Pruzhany pottery.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What do Ivenets masters make?
**Answer: Whole table services and water sets.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** What is decorative-applied art?
**Answer: Beauty made in useful things.**
