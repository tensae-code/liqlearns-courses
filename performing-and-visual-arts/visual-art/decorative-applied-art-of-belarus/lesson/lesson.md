# Decorative-Applied Art of Belarus

## Do it first

Before you read: say or sketch what you already know about this lesson's subject — Belarus's own decorative-applied arts — the koushyk ladle, Haradnaya and Pruzhany pottery, and Ivenets ceramics. Start there; the lesson builds on it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Belarus's own crafts

After learning what decorative-applied art is — beauty made in useful things — the Grade 5 book turns to **Belarus's own**:

- The **koushyk ladle** of the Minsk region.
- The **pottery of Haradnaya**.
- **Ivenets ceramics** — the old folk craft whose masters make whole table services and water sets.
- **Pruzhany pottery**.

Each craft is named by its place: the thing, the town, and the masters who keep it.
