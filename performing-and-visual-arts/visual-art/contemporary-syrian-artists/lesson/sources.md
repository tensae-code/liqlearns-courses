| Syria — Lesson 26 "The Contemporary Syrian Artist Ahmad al-Ahmad" — Ahmad al-Ahmad (b. 1946), Syrian sculptor, graduate of Damascus University, master's in sculpture from the Academy of Fine Arts in Warsaw, Poland, and a doctorate in the philosophy of human sciences in art history — his research subject the memorial monuments — from Warsaw University; he won several Arab prizes and his works are spread in some museums; abstract art is the departure from reality and the imitation of imagination with the artist's thought (mass — void); design in pencil a sculptural composition from the spirit of Ahmad al-Ahmad and try to execute it in the available materials (clay — soap — carton remains) (student book; original target `visual-art/contemporary-syrian-artists`) | Book 7, printed p. 52 |
