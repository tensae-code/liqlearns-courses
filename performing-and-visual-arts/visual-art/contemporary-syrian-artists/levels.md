## Sub lesson 24 — Lesson 5 "Said Makhlouf, Pioneer of Syrian Art"
Sub lesson 24 questions from the type files. Pass mark: the Syrian sculptor Said Makhlouf (1925-2000), who treated the hard tree trunks spontaneously and consciously, supervised the sculpture workshop at the Damascus International Fair; among his most famous works "The Winged Lion" at Damascus International Airport; he won the Syrian Order of Merit and took part in Arab exhibitions — describe what you see in the artwork: what material executed it, and did the artist adopt the realistic style?

## Sub lesson 25 — Lesson 7 "The Contemporary Syrian Artist Saad Yakan"
Sub lesson 25 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Saad Yakan (b. 1950), among the most prominent painters of the contemporary and modern Syrian (and Arab) art movement, who adopted drawing the human in his panels in an expressive way, his works spread across the world; contemplating his artwork for movement and composition, balance (Balance) of the panel's elements, their distribution, and the colour harmony (Harmony) between them; executing an artwork inspired by his panels and colouring it in fitting colours

## Sub lesson 26 — Lesson 15 "The Syrian Artist Nizar Ali Badr"
Sub lesson 26 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Nizar Ali Badr (b. 1964) invented a distinctive style reflecting varied techniques that imitate reality; the Syrian sculptor gathered from Jabal Safon (al-Jabal al-Aqra') on our coast small and medium stones to form artworks telling the Syrians' pain through the war on their homeland — Safon in the old Syrian language means the god of storms; contemplating his panels: the material used, whether he used colours, the elements in the panel; try making an artwork from the stones and pebbles in your environment, or from a journey you took, inspired by his works

## Sub lesson 27 — Lesson 23 "The Syrian Artist Michel Karsheh"
Sub lesson 27 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Michel Karsheh (1900-1973), Syrian artist, graduate of the National Higher School in Paris, counted among the pioneers who drew the natural scenes and old quarters in the impressionist style and drew faces in a realistic style; he won many Arab and world prizes; reading his panels — their subjects, the visual balance, the colours' relation; draw an artwork inspired by Karsheh's works in which perspective appears for some buildings, and colour it

## Sub lesson 28 — Lesson 26 "The Contemporary Syrian Artist Ahmad al-Ahmad"
Sub lesson 28 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Ahmad al-Ahmad (b. 1946), Syrian sculptor, graduate of Damascus University, master's in sculpture from the Academy of Fine Arts in Warsaw, Poland, and a doctorate in the philosophy of human sciences in art history — his research subject the memorial monuments — from Warsaw University; he won several Arab prizes and his works are spread in some museums; abstract art is the departure from reality and the imitation of imagination with the artist's thought (mass — void); design in pencil a sculptural composition from the spirit of Ahmad al-Ahmad and try to execute it in the available materials (clay — soap — carton remains)
