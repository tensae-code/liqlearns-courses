# Chinese Pictographs and Character Art

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about chinese pictographs and character art. Then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — China Grade 1 Art Vol.2

Chinese pictographs — how sun, moon, mountain, river, tree, water, fire, goat, horse, fish, deer, cart, hand and child were pictured in oracle-bone, bronze and seal scripts — and composing pictures from pictographs

## Sub lesson 2 — China HS Painting

Oracle-bone script and painting are blood kin — the Shang-dynasty characters carved on tortoise shell are pictures that became writing; every continent's pictographs ask why the drawn image was mankind's first tool for recording meaning

## Sub lesson 3 — China HS Chinese Painting & Calligraphy

Characters began as pictures — the Dawenkou pottery sign for "dawn" (sun over cloud over five peaks, 4–5,000 years old), oracle-bone script, bronze inscriptions, and the long evolution through seal, clerical, cursive, running and regular scripts; Feng Zikai: Chinese characters are the most artistic writing in the world
