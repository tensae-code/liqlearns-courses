# Transforming and Upcycling Clothes

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about transforming and upcycling clothes. Then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — Kazakhstan Grade 7 NIS Girls Artistic Labour teacher guide

transforming clothing: redesigning a garment into something new; generating and choosing ideas for a clothing transformation project

## Sub lesson 2 — Kazakhstan Grade 7 NIS Girls Artistic Labour student book

what it teaches: transforming clothing — changing a garment's form into another form; clothes can be transformed many ways — joining or detaching parts (modules), changing a product's size or volume, fastening and tying; one part of a garment can serve another, through buttons, loops, press-studs and ties; the 1980s British designer Lydia Silvestra's transformer dress that showed more than a hundred ways of wearing, later known as the Infinity Dress; what it teaches: ideas for transforming clothes — recycling (рециклинг): re-using textile materials as raw material for new sewn products, unwearable clothes and carpets; the transformation methods — cutting and re-joining parts, shrinking the product, changing parts, joining parts of other products, adding accessories; lace and decorative materials renew a garment; choose your garment, draw the change in the research album first, then carry it out

## Sub lesson 3 — Kazakhstan Grade 7 Artistic Labour 2025 (girls, Alimsaeva)

what it teaches: transforming and decorating sewn products — transformer things change their outward look or function, convenient, practical and multifunctional; transformation is achieved by detachable parts, adjustable ties, zips and buttons letting you change a product's length, style or volume, and modular elements combine parts of clothes into new images; the transformation devices named — «растяжение–сжатие» (stretching–squeezing), «отделение–присоединение» (detaching–attaching), «регулирование–фиксация» (regulating–fixing the size, volume and form), «свертывание–развертывание» (folding–unfolding), «исчезновение–появление» (disappearing–appearing of the whole product's volume), «замещение» (replacing parts with other parts), «совмещение–вкладывание» (combining–inserting) and «перестановка» (rearranging parts or elements); one kind of transformation is the remaking of old things — from old jeans sew a bag, apron, organiser or decorative cushions, shirts remake into a stylish top, skirt or home clothes, fabric remnants serve for plaids, covers and rugs in patchwork technique, and old things also make accessories — bracelets, earrings, rims and hair bands; transforming old things saves resources and supports an ecological way of life; decorating sewn products by hand — glued or sewn appliqué, many-coloured beads, bugle beads and glass beads, all kinds of buttons, rhinestones, sequins of varied shapes, painted elements laid on with felt pens and paints, many-coloured ribbons, braid and lace, hand embroidery and decorative stitches

## Sub lesson 4 — Kazakhstan Grade 7 NIS Girls Artistic Labour 2025 student book

what it teaches: transforming clothes — transformation is the turning of one form into another; transforming clothes can be done by different ways, and one kind of clothing transformation is the different ways of wearing clothes of one kind (one knitted top worn six ways pictured); the ways of transformation — 1. adding details: attachable collars, patch pockets, a hood; 2. unfastening elements: sleeves, the lower parts of skirts and trousers on zips, press-studs, buttons; 3. changing the length: adjustable turn-ups, gathering the fabric on a drawstring (кулиска); 4. using zips and buttons: for changing the style, dividing or joining parts; 5. using modular elements (модульные элементы): detachable inserts for widening or decor; 6. using reversible models: clothes with a two-sided design; 7. using replaceable accessories: detachable straps, belts, bags, decor on hook-and-loop tape; in the 1980s the British journalist Lydia Silvestra invented a transformer dress — more than a hundred variants of one dress were shown at the presentation, and the dress was named Infinity Dress — «бесконечное платье»; carry out an experiment with ways of wearing clothes and fixing them on a person's figure, choosing one kind of clothing and fixing the results in the research journal; what it teaches: ideas for transforming clothes — апсайклинг (upcycling) is the repeated use of things with giving them new functionality; there is also ресайклинг (recycling) — the full reprocessing of industrial and household waste, for example plastic bottles; in its essence upcycling is the reworking, remaking and turning of materials and things that have served their age into practically new ones; the upcycle is a feasible solution for each of us to the problem of overproduction and the load on the environment — the era of weekly renewals is going into the past; remaking clothes can be done by correcting the style (reducing, increasing or shortening the length, adding accents — using straps, patch pockets and so on), by simple re-sewing (remaking one part of the clothes), or by changing the fabric's structure (replacing the fabric on parts with other materials); remaking old clothes is a chance to renew the wardrobe, making it unique and fitting your figure; think what variants of clothing transformation you could propose — first choose a garment and look at two or three variants of its remaking or renewal, consider the possibility of decorating the products with ornament, define what materials you will need to realise your idea, then present the process of change in graphic form and describe the transformation process in the research album; try to remake a garment — pick or invent an unusual variant of its transformation, and before starting work ask adults' permission to remake the chosen clothing

## Sub lesson 5 — Kazakhstan Grade 7 NIS Girls Artistic Labour 2025 teacher guide

what it teaches: the guide's factual material — the transformation of clothes supposes the creation of clothes by simple means, the solution of the tasks of clothes' comfort, its variability, multifunctionality and universality; for this the combinatorial, modular methods, deconstruction and others are applied; combinatorics is characterised by using turns, permutations of parts of clothing, insects [inserts] and animals [as printed]; one of the combinatorial methods is the creation of dimensionless clothing — varied clothing is created by wrapping fabric round the body in different ways; Lydia Silvestra, the British journalist, invented and patented the transformer dress Infinite Dress («бесконечное платье»), which has more than 100 variants — Lydia by the kind of her activity had to travel much in different countries, so she needed functional clothing that does not take much place in luggage; such a dress has only one seam in its construction; in modular clothing, variety of assortment is achieved by set-formation, joining ready elements with the help of hooks, buttons, knots, cords and so on — for example, from a long product a short one can be made, from a simple form of a product compose a complex form (fasten a hood or a collar); transformation is defined as transformation, change of appearance or form of a product, and модификация (modification) is improvement, bringing in changes, adding new properties or signs; in history there are many examples of clothing transformation — in the Kazakh men's winter headdress «тымак» (tymak), in cold weather two side details covered the ears, the front part was let down on the forehead, and the occipital, largest detail covered the neck and part of the back; in warm weather these details were turned up to the headdress's crown; what it teaches: the guide's factual material — remaking clothes is considered as transformation, the turning of one or several kinds of clothes into other products; to remake clothes means to transform it into another kind of clothes or into accessories (headwear, bags, belts, textile costume jewellery and so on), change its external look, purpose; to renew clothes means to better it, improve its external look and purpose; the construction and sizes of a product, the colour and the material's composition define the content of the remaking and renewal process; the ways of remaking and renewing clothes are varied: re-cutting and joining of parts; joining the cut-off parts of one kind of clothes with other elements of clothes; replacing parts of clothes with inserts from knitted cloths, kerchiefs, materials of another colour and texture, laces and so on; carrying out parallel and figured cuts on the product's parts; cutting the lower parts of T-shirts or tops into vertical strips and tying them into knots; decorating with finishing materials, fittings, prints, appliqué and others; remaking can be done by the way of re-cutting the style, joining details, reducing the products in size, replacing parts, adding accessories to the clothes, and for remaking clothes appliqué, lace and other materials are applied
