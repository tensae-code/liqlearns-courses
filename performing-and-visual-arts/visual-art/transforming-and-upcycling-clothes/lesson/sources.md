| Kazakhstan — 3.2. Идеи для трансформации одежды — what it teaches: ideas for transforming clothes — апсайклинг (upcycling) is the repeated use of things with giving them new functionality; there is also ресайклинг (recycling) — the full reprocessing of industrial and household waste, for example plastic bottles; in its essence upcycling is the reworking, remaking and turning of materials and things that have served their age into practically new ones; the upcycle is a feasible solution for each of us to the problem of overproduction and the load on the environment — the era of weekly renewals is going into the past; remaking clothes can be done by correcting the style (reducing, increasing or shortening the length, adding accents — using straps, patch pockets and so on), by simple re-sewing (remaking one part of the clothes), or by changing the fabric's structure (replacing the fabric on parts with other materials); remaking old clothes is a chance to renew the wardrobe, making it unique and fitting your figure; think what variants of clothing transformation you could propose — first choose a garment and look at two or three variants of its remaking or renewal, consider the possibility of decorating the products with ornament, define what materials you will need to realise your idea, then present the process of change in graphic form and describe the transformation process in the research album; try to remake a garment — pick or invent an unusual variant of its transformation, and before starting work ask adults' permission to remake the chosen clothing (student book) | Book 4, printed p. 57 |

Преобразование одежды (трансформация) (teacher-guide evidence) — what it teaches: the guide's factual material — the transformation of clothes supposes the creation of clothes by simple means, the solution of the tasks of clothes' comfort, its variability, multifunctionality and universality; for this the combinatorial, modular methods, deconstruction and others are applied; combinatorics is characterised by using turns, permutations of parts of clothing, insects [inserts] and animals [as printed]; one of the combinatorial methods is the creation of dimensionless clothing — varied clothing is created by wrapping fabric round the body in different ways; Lydia Silvestra, the British journalist, invented and patented the transformer dress Infinite Dress («бесконечное платье»), which has more than 100 variants — Lydia by the kind of her activity had to travel much in different countries, so she needed functional clothing that does not take much place in luggage; such a dress has only one seam in its construction; in modular clothing, variety of assortment is achieved by set-formation, joining ready elements with the help of hooks, buttons, knots, cords and so on — for example, from a long product a short one can be made, from a simple form of a product compose a complex form (fasten a hood or a collar); transformation is defined as transformation, change of appearance or form of a product, and модификация (modification) is improvement, bringing in changes, adding new properties or signs; in history there are many examples of clothing transformation — in the Kazakh men's winter headdress «тымак» (tymak), in cold weather two side details covered the ears, the front part was let down on the forehead, and the occipital, largest detail covered the neck and part of the back; in warm weather these details were turned up to the headdress's crown (teacher-guide evidence) | Book 5, printed p. 42 |
| Kazakhstan — 3.2. Идеи для трансформации одежды (teacher-guide evidence) — what it teaches: the guide's factual material — remaking clothes is considered as transformation, the turning of one or several kinds of clothes into other products; to remake clothes means to transform it into another kind of clothes or into accessories (headwear, bags, belts, textile costume jewellery and so on), change its external look, purpose; to renew clothes means to better it, improve its external look and purpose; the construction and sizes of a product, the colour and the material's composition define the content of the remaking and renewal process; the ways of remaking and renewing clothes are varied: re-cutting and joining of parts; joining the cut-off parts of one kind of clothes with other elements of clothes; replacing parts of clothes with inserts from knitted cloths, kerchiefs, materials of another colour and texture, laces and so on; carrying out parallel and figured cuts on the product's parts; cutting the lower parts of T-shirts or tops into vertical strips and tying them into knots; decorating with finishing materials, fittings, prints, appliqué and others; remaking can be done by the way of re-cutting the style, joining details, reducing the products in size, replacing parts, adding accessories to the clothes, and for remaking clothes appliqué, lace and other materials are applied (teacher-guide evidence) | Book 5, printed p. 44 |
