# Hands-on — Turkmen Nagysh Ornament

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Dress the model
Draw the outline of a national dress. Study a pictured pattern, copy its flower-or-plant shape, repeat it in a band, and decorate the dress model with your band. Name your pattern, as every nagyş has a name. Then decorate a paper tree-toy shape the same way.
