# Turkmen Nagysh Ornament — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Nagyş are…
a) songs  b) national ornament patterns on traditional clothes  c) instruments
**Answer: b) national ornament patterns on traditional clothes**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Every nagyş pattern has…
a) its own name  b) no meaning  c) a number only
**Answer: a) its own name**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The patterns recall…
a) cars  b) flowers and plants  c) letters
**Answer: b) flowers and plants**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The book decorates New Year tree toys with…
a) plain colour only  b) national patterns, to make them more beautiful  c) nothing
**Answer: b) national patterns, to make them more beautiful**
