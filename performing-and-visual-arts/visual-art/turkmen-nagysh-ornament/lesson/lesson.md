# Turkmen Nagysh Ornament

## Do it first

Say what you already know about this lesson's subject — one thing you have seen, heard or done — before you read on.

## Sub lesson 1 — Patterns with names

Turkmen national clothes are decorated with **nagyş (ornament patterns)**.

**Every pattern has its own name** — a pattern is not just pretty, it is known. And the patterns **recall flowers and plants**: the plant world, carried onto cloth.

The book's way: **study the patterns on real national clothes** first, looking closely, before you draw any of your own. The year's final question asks it back: *which of our national ornaments do you know?*

## Sub lesson 2 — Decorating with nagyş

With the patterns learned, the book decorates:

1. **A model of national dress** — decorate it with nagyş, as the real clothes are decorated.
2. **New Year tree toys** — drawn from observation, then **decorated with national patterns to make them more beautiful**.

Ornament is not an extra here: it is how a made thing becomes beautiful, and becomes Turkmen.
