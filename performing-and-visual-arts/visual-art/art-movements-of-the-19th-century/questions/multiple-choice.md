# Art Movements of the 19th Century — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson — Art Movements of the 19th Century — opens with…
a) Neoclasicismo  b) an examination  c) nothing at all
**Answer: a) Neoclasicismo.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The Venezuela book teaches “Neoclasicismo” as…
a) a part of art movements of the 19th century  b) a sports rule  c) a cooking recipe
**Answer: a) a part of art movements of the 19th century.**

**3.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) Romanticismo  b) long division  c) map coordinates
**Answer: a) Romanticismo.**

**4.** The parts of this lesson come from…
a) Venezuela's own Educación Artística books  b) a newspaper  c) a song chorus
**Answer: a) Venezuela's own Educación Artística books.**

**5.** “Realismo” is taught in this lesson as…
a) one of its named parts  b) an unrelated game  c) a spelling list
