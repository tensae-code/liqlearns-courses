# Hands-on — Body Painting

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Organic colours from nature
Do the practice this part names, with real materials and a partner: Papua New Guinea's Grade 3 guide sets environmentally friendly organic colours — made from flowers, leaves, clay, soil and charcoal — beside modern paints, and asks which kind a painting needs. With them learners paint in geometric arrangement and make narrative painting about their own life, and meet a local painter and their work.

## Making traditional paint
Do the practice this part names, with real materials and a partner: The Grade 4 guide teaches plants used to make paint and the making of traditional paint itself, step by step, with the steps of making coconut oil — the binder and carrier of the tradition — taught as part of the same craft.

## Traditional designs, their meanings, and body painting
Do the practice this part names, with real materials and a partner: Traditional designs are not decoration only: the guides teach their meanings, and body painting using the traditional organic medium — the same colours, worn on the skin for dance and ceremony.
