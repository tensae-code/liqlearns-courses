# Body Painting — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Papua New Guinea's organic colours are made from…
a) flowers, leaves, clay, soil and charcoal  b) plastic tubes
**Answer: a) flowers, leaves, clay, soil and charcoal.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Traditional designs in the guides carry…
a) meanings, not decoration only  b) no meaning at all
**Answer: a) meanings, not decoration only.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Coconut oil is taught in the paint lessons because…
a) it is part of making and using traditional paint  b) it is a food only
**Answer: a) it is part of making and using traditional paint.**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Body painting in these guides uses…
a) the traditional organic medium  b) spray cans
**Answer: a) the traditional organic medium.**
