# Body Painting — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name three things organic colours are made from.
**Answer: Flowers, leaves, clay, soil and charcoal (any three).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What two kinds of painting does the Grade 3 guide set beside the organic colours?
**Answer: Painting in geometric arrangement and narrative painting about your own life.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the Grade 4 guide teach about traditional designs?
**Answer: Their meanings — the designs mean something, and the learner learns what.**
