# Video script — Body Painting

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

**Organic colours from nature.** Papua New Guinea's Grade 3 guide sets environmentally friendly organic colours — made from flowers, leaves, clay, soil and charcoal — beside modern paints, and asks which kind a painting needs. With them learners paint in geometric arrangement and make narrative painting about their own life, and meet a local painter and their work.

**Making traditional paint.** The Grade 4 guide teaches plants used to make paint and the making of traditional paint itself, step by step, with the steps of making coconut oil — the binder and carrier of the tradition — taught as part of the same craft.

**Traditional designs, their meanings, and body painting.** Traditional designs are not decoration only: the guides teach their meanings, and body painting using the traditional organic medium — the same colours, worn on the skin for dance and ceremony.
