# Body Painting — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — Organic colours from nature
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Papua New Guinea's Grade 3 guide sets environmentally friendly organic colours — made from flowers, leaves, clay, soil and charcoal — beside modern paints, and asks which kind a painting needs.

## Sub lesson 2 — Making traditional paint
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: The Grade 4 guide teaches plants used to make paint and the making of traditional paint itself, step by step, with the steps of making coconut oil — the binder and carrier of the tradition — taught as part of the same craft.

## Sub lesson 3 — Traditional designs, their meanings, and body painting
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Traditional designs are not decoration only: the guides teach their meanings, and body painting using the traditional organic medium — the same colours, worn on the skin for dance and ceremony.
