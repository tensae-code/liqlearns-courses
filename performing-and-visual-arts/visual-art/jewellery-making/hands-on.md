# Hands-on — Jewellery Making

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Draw a people, make its set
Draw the name of one of Suriname's peoples by lot. With your group, make that people's jewellery set — traditional or modern — from rest and natural materials.

## Use three techniques
In your set, use at least three of the material techniques: rolling, gluing, cutting, tearing, threading, painting, twisting, covering. Name them when you present the set.
