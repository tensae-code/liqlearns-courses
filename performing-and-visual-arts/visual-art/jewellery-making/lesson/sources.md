Suriname's own way: one integrated arts course — Kunst- en cultuureducatie (KCE) — in six domains (Music, Dance, Visual Arts, Cultural Heritage, Theatre, Media), each domain a series of one-hour lessons built on the same frame: the IKA lesson shape (Introductie–Kern–Afsluiting, the core in three phases — warm-up, technique, own work and showing moment) and the Big Idea design process in four phases (orient, research, experiment, reflect), every series answering one research question and ending in a presentation; each domain is deliberately fused with another school subject (music with arithmetic, dance with geography, visual arts with history, theatre with language); the course's heart is Suriname itself — its many peoples' dances, dress, jewellery, heritage and theatre forms — held together by the Alakondre ideal, "Alakondre na wan, wan na Alakondre" (unity in diversity); assessment is by evaluation card and portfolio, process over product. Named, not placed, as filed: each domain's begrippenlijst (glossary — Music printed p. 46, Dance pp. 84–86, Visual Arts pp. 123–124, Heritage pp. 150–151, Theatre p. 180, Media pp. 209–211) — inventory only; each domain's evaluatiekaart (evaluation card — Music pp. 47–49, Dance pp. 87–89, Visual Arts pp. 125–127, Heritage pp. 152–154, Theatre pp. 183–185, Media pp. 212–214) — assessment apparatus, not teaching content; the closing appendices (the KCE lesson form, printed p. 215; deepening questions for preparing a lesson, p. 216; the general KCE evaluation card, p. 217) — teacher apparatus; the Bronnenlijst (sources list, printed pp. 218–220) — inventory only.
