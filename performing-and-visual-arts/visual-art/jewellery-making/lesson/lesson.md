## Sub lesson 1 — Make your own jewellery — Mek mi eygi pranpran

The making lesson is **"Mek mi eygi pranpran" (Make My Own Jewellery)**: making a **3-D jewellery set from rest and natural materials — clay, seeds, pits, bottle caps, buttons, crown caps, paper, old telephone wire, iron wire** — with the material techniques of **rolling, gluing, cutting and tearing, threading (rijgen), painting, twisting and covering**. Each group **draws the name of one of Suriname's peoples by lot** and makes **that people's jewellery set, traditional or modern**. The book notes a **koto cannot be worn without jewellery**: it is part of a tradition that carries a Surinamese identity. The next year's work builds on the set: a **pattern border** gathered from nature is fastened onto the jewellery, headdress or footwear made here, so firmly that it stays on while the piece is worn.
