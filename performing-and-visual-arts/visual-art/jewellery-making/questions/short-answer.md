# Jewellery Making — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name four materials for the jewellery set.
**Answer: Any four of: clay, seeds, pits, bottle caps, buttons, crown caps, paper, old telephone wire, iron wire.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Name four material techniques.
**Answer: Any four of: rolling, gluing, cutting, tearing, threading, painting, twisting, covering.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the book say about the koto and jewellery?
**Answer: A koto cannot be worn without jewellery — it is part of a tradition that carries a Surinamese identity.**
