# Jewellery Making — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The jewellery set is made from…
a) gold and diamonds only  b) rest and natural materials — clay, seeds, pits, caps, buttons, paper and wire  c) plastic kits
**Answer: b) rest and natural materials — clay, seeds, pits, caps, buttons, paper and wire**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Each group makes the jewellery of…
a) its own choice, always the same people  b) one of Suriname's peoples, drawn by lot  c) no people in particular
**Answer: b) one of Suriname's peoples, drawn by lot**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Threading in Dutch is…
a) rijgen  b) plakken  c) draaien
**Answer: a) rijgen**
