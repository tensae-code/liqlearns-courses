# Jewellery Making — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The jewellery set is three-dimensional (3-D).
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Only precious metals may be used.
**Answer: False — rest and natural materials are the material.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** A group's set may be traditional or modern.
**Answer: True.**
