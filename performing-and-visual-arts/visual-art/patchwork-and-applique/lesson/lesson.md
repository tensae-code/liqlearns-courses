# Patchwork and Appliqué

## Do it first

Say what you already know about this lesson's subject — one thing you have seen, heard or done — before you read on.

## Sub lesson 1 — A flowering branch in appliqué

**Appliqué** builds a picture from **pieces**: cut the shapes from **coloured paper**, arrange them, and glue them into place.

Turkmenistan's lesson makes **"Gülleýän bag şahasy" — a flowering tree branch**: the branch's shape and its flowers are not drawn first and coloured after — they are **made**, piece by coloured piece, until the branch blooms on the page.
