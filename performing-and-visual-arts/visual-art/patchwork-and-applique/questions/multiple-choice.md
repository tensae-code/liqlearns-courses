# Patchwork and Appliqué — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Appliqué makes a picture from…
a) cut pieces of coloured paper, glued in place  b) one pencil line  c) water only
**Answer: a) cut pieces of coloured paper, glued in place**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The Turkmen lesson's appliqué subject is…
a) a car  b) a flowering tree branch  c) a house
**Answer: b) a flowering tree branch**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** In appliqué, the flowers are…
a) made as paper pieces  b) only imagined  c) painted with a brush
**Answer: a) made as paper pieces**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** The pieces are fixed with…
a) thread only  b) glue  c) pins forever
**Answer: b) glue**
