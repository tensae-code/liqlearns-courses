# Patchwork and Appliqué — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Appliqué pictures are built from pieces.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In the Turkmen lesson the branch is drawn with a pencil only.
**Answer: False** — its shape is made from coloured paper.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Arranging the pieces comes before gluing them.
**Answer: True**
