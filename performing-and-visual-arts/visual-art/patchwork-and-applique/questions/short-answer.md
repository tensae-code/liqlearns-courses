# Patchwork and Appliqué — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the Turkmen title of the appliqué lesson, and what does it mean?
**Answer: "Gülleýän bag şahasy" — a flowering tree branch.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the appliqué steps in order.
**Answer: Cut the shapes from coloured paper, arrange them into the picture, and glue them in place.**
