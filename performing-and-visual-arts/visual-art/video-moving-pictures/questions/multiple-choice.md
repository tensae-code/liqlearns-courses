# Video: Moving Pictures — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A video is…
  a) A moving image with its own duration b) A single still photograph c) A song without words
**Answer: a) A moving image with its own duration** — video is a moving image with its own duration, with or without sound.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why can a wedding not all be shot?
  a) You must pick the important moments — the whole event is too long b) Weddings may never be filmed c) The camera would melt
**Answer: a) You must pick the important moments — the whole event is too long** — plan the start and end and pick the important moments.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The close-up view in Amharic is…
  a) የቅርብ እይታ ቀረጻ b) የሩቅ እይታ ቀረጻ c) የመካከለኛ እይታ
**Answer: a) የቅርብ እይታ ቀረጻ** — የቅርብ እይታ ቀረጻ is the close-up (near) view.

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Bokeh is…
  a) Glowing unfocused light orbs in a photo b) A kind of clay c) A dance step
**Answer: a) Glowing unfocused light orbs in a photo** — bokeh is the glowing unfocused light orbs technique.

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** A sequence is made of…
  a) Scenes joined together b) One single shot only c) Only sounds
**Answer: a) Scenes joined together** — shots make scenes, scenes make sequences, sequences make the whole film.
