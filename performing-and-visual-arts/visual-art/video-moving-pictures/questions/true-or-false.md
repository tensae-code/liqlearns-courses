# Video: Moving Pictures — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A video can be watched on a mobile phone.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is behind the subjects matters when shooting.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A long view shows the whole place.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Motion blur can be made by a long exposure or by panning.
**Answer: True**

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** A shot is a fragment of picture and sound.
**Answer: True**
