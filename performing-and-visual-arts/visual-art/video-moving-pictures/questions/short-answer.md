# Video: Moving Pictures — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What new material does video add to the arts?
**Answer: Time — the moving image has its own duration.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the first two of the six things.
**Answer: Prepare the equipment properly; plan what to shoot and where it starts and ends.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which shot size shows a face's feelings best?
**Answer: The close-up view.**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** What does macro photography do?
**Answer: It makes the very small appear large.**

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** What does the guide call the film's soul?
**Answer: Its meaning — what is shown and what is hinted beneath (subtext).**
