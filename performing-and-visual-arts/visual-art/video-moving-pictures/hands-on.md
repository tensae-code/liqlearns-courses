# Hands-on — Video: Moving Pictures

The lesson with your hands, your feet and your voice together, not only your eyes.

## The six-things shoot
Choose one enjoyable thing to film. Before pressing record, say the six things aloud and check each. Film, watch with friends, and name the one thing you would fix first.

## Motion blur by hand
Photograph a moving hand or a spinning top with the longest exposure your camera allows; then photograph it frozen. Compare: blur tells speed, freezing tells shape.
