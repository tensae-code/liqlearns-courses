# Pottery Making

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about pottery making. Then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — Let's identify clay; let's create

Pottery as one of humanity's first crafts — ancient clay vessels pictured (fig. 5.1.1) and the grain-store jar (fig. 5.1.2), clay craft running from prehistory to the village potter

## Sub lesson 2 — clay types and materials

Knowing the earths — Kaolin white clay (the Boralesgamuwa and Meeyankodai deposits), ball clay, fire clay and red clay, then the potter's other materials: feldspar, silica sand, dolomite and talc, each pictured and placed

## Sub lesson 3 — Let's prepare moulds for making clay vessels

Mould-making whole — the mould materials (wood, clay, metal, cement, fibre, rubber, plaster of Paris) and a two-piece plaster mould built step by step around a bottle, soaped, poured 1½–2 inches thick, opened and cleaned
