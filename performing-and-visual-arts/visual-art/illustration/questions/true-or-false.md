# Illustration — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Each image in the portfolio may be on a different theme.
**Answer: False — the mini-portfolio holds one theme.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A portfolio's story can be told from your own heritage.
**Answer: True.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The story in images is only copied from a book.
**Answer: False — the learner creates it from history or heritage.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Finding references is part of the project.
**Answer: True.**
