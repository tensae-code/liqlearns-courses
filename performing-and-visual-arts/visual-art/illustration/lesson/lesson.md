# Illustration

## Do it first

Quick start: from the title alone, guess what this lesson's work is. Then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — The portfolio's guiding line

A **portfolio is a story told in images**. The EEA1 sequence teaches the learner to find a work's **ligne directrice (guiding line)** and to build a **mini-portfolio of 10–12 images on one theme**, with its **verbalisation** (saying what the images do) and its planned **making-steps (gestes à mobiliser)** — telling your own story from your own heritage.

## Sub lesson 2 — A story in images from history and heritage

The 8e sequence's final artistic project: create **a small story in images based on history or on heritage** — choose an element of **heritage and/or environment** for a mini-portfolio, find its **aesthetic and heritage references**, and structure everything gathered to **tell your story in images** — then analyse your own work against your environment and heritage.
