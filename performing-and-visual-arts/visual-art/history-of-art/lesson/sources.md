| Vietnam — Bài 3 — the outline for analysing a fine-art work, in four moves — description — when writing an analysis essay, first feel the overall impression through the image elements (line, shape, colour, composition) and analyse objectively what is seen (shape-mass, colour, how the form-creation elements combine, whether they fit the theme, the technique and the way the artist shows the work), asking whether the theme and content are reflected through the expression form, what technique and material the artist used, and whether the details fit and harmonise in the work's whole (Louise Bourgeois' multi-material spider statue "Maman", 1999, at the National Gallery of Canada); learning the information related to the author and the work — searching digitised data at libraries and on the internet, cross-checking with the research of museums, research institutes and magazines, and finding the stories of the creative process in dictionaries, books, newspapers and fine-art textbooks, verifying through the indirect channels before judging the work's value (Trịnh Thiệp's wood-engraving "Hà Nội giải phóng" — Hanoi Liberated, 1976);
