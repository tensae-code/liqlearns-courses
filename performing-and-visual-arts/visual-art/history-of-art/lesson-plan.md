# Lesson plan — History of Art

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Visual Art · **Lesson:** History of Art
**Place in the curriculum:** Visual Art (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the history of art as one story, read through Andorra's cross-cutting questions — art and power, art and mentality, and art and conflicts.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this course in the same title.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Art and power** — Baroque painting as an instrument consolidating political and religious power (Caravaggio, Rubens, Vermeer and Rembrandt, Velázquez); Romanesque and Gothic architecture as symbols of power, including the Romanesque of Andorra; classical Greek and Roman sculpture and the Xi'an warriors.
2. **Art and mentality** — deducing an epoch's mentality from its art: Renaissance painting and humanism; Catalan modernisme architecture and the Catalan bourgeoisie; late-20th-century sculpture as the mentality of our own time.
3. **Art and conflicts** — how conflicts have been transmitted through art: Goya and Picasso as witnesses (Guernica); Banksy's graffiti; Gargallo and Julio González; Le Corbusier's functional and Wright's organicist architecture around the Second World War.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say how Baroque painting and Romanesque and Gothic building each served power.
2. Say what mentality Renaissance painting and Catalan modernisme each show.
3. Name two artist-witnesses of conflict and the work that symbolises the Spanish Civil War.
