# History of Art — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name one kind of building that stood as a symbol of power.
**Answer: Romanesque and Gothic churches and monasteries, or Gothic cathedrals.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What mentality does Catalan modernisme show?
**Answer: The mentality of the Catalan bourgeoisie.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Who made graffiti criticising today's conflicts, in the programme?
