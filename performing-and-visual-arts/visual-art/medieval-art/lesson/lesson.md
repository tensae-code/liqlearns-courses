# Medieval Art

## Do it first

Quick game: draw two churches from memory — one low and wide, one tall and thin. You have just drawn this lesson: Romanesque and Gothic.

## Sub lesson 1 — Romanesque art

Medieval art (5th–15th centuries) was a **wholly religious art**, and **architecture led** it — painting and sculpture lived inside the architecture.

**Romanesque art (11th–13th centuries)**: **horizontal religious temples with cross-shaped plans**, built under Roman influence — solid, low and wide, like fortresses of faith.

## Sub lesson 2 — Gothic art

**Gothic art (late 12th–15th centuries)** turned upward: **very tall vertical temples reaching toward heaven**, with **many coloured mosaic windows** pouring coloured light inside.

In both styles, painting and sculpture did not stand alone — they **lived inside the architecture**.
