# Medieval Art — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Romanesque art belongs to the 11th–13th centuries.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Gothic temples are low and horizontal.
**Answer: False** — Gothic temples are very tall and vertical.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Medieval art spans the 5th–15th centuries.
**Answer: True**
