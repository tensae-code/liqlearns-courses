# Medieval Art — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Medieval art was…
a) wholly religious  b) only about markets  c) abstract
**Answer: a) wholly religious**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Romanesque temples are…
a) very tall and vertical  b) horizontal, with cross-shaped plans  c) made of glass
**Answer: b) horizontal, with cross-shaped plans**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Gothic temples reach…
a) toward the sea  b) toward heaven — very tall and vertical  c) underground
**Answer: b) toward heaven — very tall and vertical**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In medieval art, painting and sculpture…
a) lived inside the architecture  b) did not exist  c) replaced architecture
**Answer: a) lived inside the architecture**
