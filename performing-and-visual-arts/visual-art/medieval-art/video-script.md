Medieval art — the age of temples.

First Romanesque. Low. Wide. Horizontal. A cross for a plan. Rome's shadow over it.

Then Gothic. Up! Tall, taller — reaching for heaven. Windows of coloured glass, light like jewels.

Painting and sculpture lived inside these buildings. Architecture led.
