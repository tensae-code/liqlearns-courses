# Lesson plan — Vietnamese National Heroes in Art

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Visual Art · **Lesson:** Vietnamese National Heroes in Art
**Place in the curriculum:** Visual Art (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** vietnamese National Heroes in Art — its forms, history and cultural meaning, taught as one topic lesson.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this course in the same title.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. ## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Vietnam Grade 5 Mĩ thuật 5** — The heroes the nation honours, and how each art form shows them — Nguyễn Tư Nghiêm's lacquer "Gióng" (1990); Phạm Văn Đôn's woodcut "Spring Victory" (1978), the heroine riding her elephant under the banner; Diệp Minh Châu's bronze statue "Võ Thị Sáu" (1956); Trần Văn Lãm's bronze bust "Lý Tự Trọng" (1955); and Nguyễn Hồng Phong's green-stone relief "General Võ Nguyên Giáp with the Ethnic Peoples of Thái Nguyên Province" (2020) — learning to tell a painting, a print, a statue and a relief apart
2. **Vietnam Grade 11 Mĩ thuật 11 — Lí luận và lịch sử mĩ thuật** — Historical value — it records truthfully the image of a Party admission ceremony at Điện Biên Phủ, reflecting the heroic, resolute spirit of the nation's historic battle in the resistance against the French, and contributes an important part to researching the historical values that led to the Điện Biên Phủ campaign's resounding victory; aesthetic value — it records a typical style of a master painter of Vietnam's fine art, inheriting Western art's form-creation way with modern technique but carrying the strong spirit and Vietnamese colour, basic proof of Vietnamese lacquer art's diverse expressive ability besides the decorative depiction way in traditional fine art; and cultural value — it strongly influenced society, motivating the people's army spirit in the anti-American resistance period when the painting was born, and affected the painter generations after Nguyễn Sáng, an epic song (bản hùng ca) of the Vietnamese people's patriotism and revolutionary spirit
