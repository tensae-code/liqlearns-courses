The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

This lesson is Vietnamese National Heroes in Art.

Vietnamese National Heroes in Art — its forms, history and cultural meaning, taught as one topic lesson. — as Vietnam's school books teach it: The heroes the nation honours, and how each art form shows them — Nguyễn Tư Nghiêm's lacquer "Gióng" (1990); Phạm Văn Đôn's woodcut "Spring Victory" (1978), the heroine riding her elephant under the banner; Diệp Minh Châu's bronze statue "Võ Thị Sáu" (1956); Trần Văn Lãm's bronze bust "Lý Tự Trọng" (1955); and Nguyễn Hồng Phong's green-stone relief "General Võ Nguyên Giáp with the Ethnic Peoples of Thái Nguyên Province" (2020) — learning to tell a painting, a print, a statue and a relief apart.

Watch, listen — then say it back in your own words.
