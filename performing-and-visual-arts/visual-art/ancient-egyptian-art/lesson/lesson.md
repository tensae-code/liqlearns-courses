# Ancient Egyptian Art

## Do it first

Quick game: before you read on, say what you already know about this lesson's work — its name, its look, its story. Then read and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — Egypt's buildings: pyramids and the sphinx

Uganda's Senior One book closes its art history with **ancient Egypt**: examine the art forms of ancient Egypt and **evaluate their elements and principles**. First, the architecture — the **pyramids** — and the **sphinx**, the great carved guardian.

In your mind's eye: the pyramids and the sphinx, side by side.

## Sub lesson 2 — Egyptian paintings

Then the **Egyptian paintings** — wall pictures that carried the people's life in line and colour, judged by the same elements and principles you learned in this course.

## Sub lesson 3 — What the art shows: social life and the Nile

The art is also a record: it reflects Egypt's **social life** and its **technological advancement** — **boats on the Nile** among the clearest signs, the river craft that moved a civilisation.
