# Ancient Egyptian Art — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The pyramids are a form of Egyptian architecture.
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The book asks learners only to look at Egyptian paintings, never to evaluate them.
**Answer: False — learners examine the art forms and evaluate their elements and principles.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Egyptian art reflects the social life of its people.
**Answer: True.**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** The Nile boats in the art are a sign of technological advancement.
**Answer: True.**
