# Ancient Egyptian Art — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The pyramids are studied in this lesson as…
a) Egyptian architecture — an art form  b) natural hills  c) modern buildings
**Answer: a) Egyptian architecture — an art form.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The sphinx is…
a) a river boat  b) a great carved guardian figure  c) a painting tool
**Answer: b) a great carved guardian figure.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Egyptian paintings are evaluated by…
a) their price  b) their elements and principles  c) their weight
**Answer: b) their elements and principles.**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Boats on the Nile in the art show…
a) technological advancement  b) nothing at all  c) European life
**Answer: a) technological advancement.**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Egyptian art reflects Egypt's…
a) social life  b) examination results  c) weather forecasts
**Answer: a) social life.**
