# Ancient Egyptian Art — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name two Egyptian art forms the chapter examines.
**Answer: The pyramids (architecture) and the sphinx — paintings are the third.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** By what two things does the book evaluate the Egyptian art forms?
**Answer: Their elements and their principles.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name one sign of technological advancement the art reflects.
**Answer: Boats on the Nile.**
