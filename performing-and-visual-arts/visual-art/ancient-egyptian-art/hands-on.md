# Hands-on — Ancient Egyptian Art

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Read a picture
Show a picture of a pyramid, the sphinx or an Egyptian painting. Your partner names one element or principle in it — line, shape, colour, balance — and one thing it shows about Egyptian life.

## Draw like Egypt
Draw a Nile boat scene in profile, the Egyptian way: figures flat, important things big. Say one social-life detail your drawing carries.
