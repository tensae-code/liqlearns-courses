# Recycled and Sustainable Crafting

## Do it first

Follow one made thing through its whole life: the first sketch, the making, the mending years later, and the day its material becomes something new. Craft does not end at 'finished'.

## Sub lesson 1 — The craft process whole — life-cycle thinking

The Finnish craft-school project teaches the **craft process whole**:

- **Design** — sketches, trials, prototypes, and the **need** the thing answers.
- **Material and technique** — chosen with **responsibility, economy and durability** in mind, with the right **tools** and **safety**.
- **Making** — the work itself.
- **Care and repair** — **cleaning, mending, replacing parts**, so the thing lives long.
- **Recycling** — **sorting** what is left and **reusing leftover material**.

Craft is taught as a whole life-cycle — design, responsible material choice, making, care and repair, and recycling.
