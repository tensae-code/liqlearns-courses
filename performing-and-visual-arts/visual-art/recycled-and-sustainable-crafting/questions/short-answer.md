# Recycled and Sustainable Crafting — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the five stages of the craft life-cycle.
**Answer: Design; material and technique; making; care and repair; recycling.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Why does the framework put care and repair inside craft itself?
**Answer: Because a made thing's life continues after making — cleaning, mending and replacing parts keep it in use.**
