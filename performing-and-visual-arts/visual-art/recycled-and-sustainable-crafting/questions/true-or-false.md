# Recycled and Sustainable Crafting — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The craft process ends at the moment of making.
**Answer: False** — care, repair and recycling follow.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Safety is part of choosing material and technique.
**Answer: True.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Leftover material should be sorted and reused.
**Answer: True.**
