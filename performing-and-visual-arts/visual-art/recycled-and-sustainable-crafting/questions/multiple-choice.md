# Recycled and Sustainable Crafting — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The craft process begins with…
a) recycling  b) design — sketches, trials, prototypes, need  c) throwing away
**Answer: b) design — sketches, trials, prototypes, need**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Materials are chosen for…
a) responsibility, economy and durability  b) brightness only  c) weight only
**Answer: a) responsibility, economy and durability**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Care and repair include…
a) cleaning, mending and replacing parts  b) hiding the thing  c) buying a new one at once
**Answer: a) cleaning, mending and replacing parts**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Recycling in the craft process means…
a) burning leftovers  b) sorting and reusing leftover material  c) mixing everything together
**Answer: b) sorting and reusing leftover material**
