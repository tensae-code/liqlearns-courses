# Hands-on — Art of Ancient America

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Cultura olmeca
Work through this part with real materials or your own body, the way the book sets it: the Olmec, mother culture of Mesoamerica — ceremonial centres on artificial plains, the first stepped pyramids (La Venta, Cuicuilco), the planned city of Monte Albán with its ball court and observatory; monumental stone sculpture, modelled ceramics and jade, obsidian and serpentine jewellery. Show a companion what you made or did, and say what the part teaches.

## Cultura de Teotihuacán
Work through this part with real materials or your own body, the way the book sets it: Teotihuacán, the first planned city of Mesoamerica — the Avenue of the Dead, the Pyramids of the Sun and Moon and the Temple of Quetzalcóatl; stone sculpture and masks, tripod vessels painted in fresco, and fresco murals of gods and daily life in red and green. Show a companion what you made or did, and say what the part teaches.

## Civilización maya
Work through this part with real materials or your own body, the way the book sets it: the Maya — stepped pyramids and palaces in limestone with corbelled vaults (Temple of the Inscriptions at Palenque, Pyramid of Kukulkán), ball courts, polychromed relief sculpture and jade masks, the Bonampak frescoes, and the hieroglyphic codices on vegetable-fibre paper, the Dresden Codex among them. Show a companion what you made or did, and say what the part teaches.

## Civilización azteca
Work through this part with real materials or your own body, the way the book sets it: the Aztec — Tenochtitlán built on its lake with canals and causeways under a great twin pyramid; stone sculpture of the gods, the Aztec Calendar and the monumental Coatlicue; ceremonial objects and Moctezuma's quetzal-feather headdress, taken to Vienna. Show a companion what you made or did, and say what the part teaches.

## Culturas chavín y mochica
Work through this part with real materials or your own body, the way the book sets it: the Andean cultures — Chavín's ceremonial centre of tapia and carved stone with its Lanzón monolith of jaguar and condor gods; Mochica's adobe pyramids Huaca del Sol and Huaca de la Luna and its red-on-white painted ceramics of human and animal forms. Show a companion what you made or did, and say what the part teaches.

## Cultura nazca y tiahuanaco
Work through this part with real materials or your own body, the way the book sets it: Nazca's adobe architecture and weaving beside its ground drawings; Tiahuanaco's great fitted-stone walled cities entered by monolithic gates (the Puerta del Sol) and its characteristic cotton weaving. Show a companion what you made or did, and say what the part teaches.

## Civilización inca
Work through this part with real materials or your own body, the way the book sets it: the Inca — masters of construction above all: polygonal fitted stonework, walls, roads and bridges across the Andes, terraced cities such as Cuzco and Machu Picchu fitted to the mountain; also ceramics, weaving and goldwork. Show a companion what you made or did, and say what the part teaches.
