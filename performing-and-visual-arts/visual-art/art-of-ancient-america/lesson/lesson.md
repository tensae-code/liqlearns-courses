# Art of Ancient America

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about this lesson's subject — art of ancient america. Then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — Cultura olmeca

The Olmec, mother culture of Mesoamerica — ceremonial centres on artificial plains, the first stepped pyramids (La Venta, Cuicuilco), the planned city of Monte Albán with its ball court and observatory; monumental stone sculpture, modelled ceramics and jade, obsidian and serpentine jewellery.

## Sub lesson 2 — Cultura de Teotihuacán

Teotihuacán, the first planned city of Mesoamerica — the Avenue of the Dead, the Pyramids of the Sun and Moon and the Temple of Quetzalcóatl; stone sculpture and masks, tripod vessels painted in fresco, and fresco murals of gods and daily life in red and green.

## Sub lesson 3 — Civilización maya

The Maya — stepped pyramids and palaces in limestone with corbelled vaults (Temple of the Inscriptions at Palenque, Pyramid of Kukulkán), ball courts, polychromed relief sculpture and jade masks, the Bonampak frescoes, and the hieroglyphic codices on vegetable-fibre paper, the Dresden Codex among them.

## Sub lesson 4 — Civilización azteca

The Aztec — Tenochtitlán built on its lake with canals and causeways under a great twin pyramid; stone sculpture of the gods, the Aztec Calendar and the monumental Coatlicue; ceremonial objects and Moctezuma's quetzal-feather headdress, taken to Vienna.

## Sub lesson 5 — Culturas chavín y mochica

The Andean cultures — Chavín's ceremonial centre of tapia and carved stone with its Lanzón monolith of jaguar and condor gods; Mochica's adobe pyramids Huaca del Sol and Huaca de la Luna and its red-on-white painted ceramics of human and animal forms.

## Sub lesson 6 — Cultura nazca y tiahuanaco

Nazca's adobe architecture and weaving beside its ground drawings; Tiahuanaco's great fitted-stone walled cities entered by monolithic gates (the Puerta del Sol) and its characteristic cotton weaving.

## Sub lesson 7 — Civilización inca

The Inca — masters of construction above all: polygonal fitted stonework, walls, roads and bridges across the Andes, terraced cities such as Cuzco and Machu Picchu fitted to the mountain; also ceramics, weaving and goldwork.
