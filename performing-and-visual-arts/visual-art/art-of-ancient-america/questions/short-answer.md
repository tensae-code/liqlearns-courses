# Art of Ancient America — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the first part of this lesson called?
**Answer: Cultura olmeca.**

**2.** Name one more part of this lesson.
**Answer: Cultura de Teotihuacán (any one named part is right).**

**3.** Which country's books teach this lesson's parts?
**Answer: Venezuela's — Educación Artística 1 (Grade 7) and Recorriendo el Arte 2 (Grade 8), Colección Bicentenario.**
