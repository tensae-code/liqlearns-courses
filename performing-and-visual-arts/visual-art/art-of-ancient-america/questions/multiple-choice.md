# Art of Ancient America — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson — Art of Ancient America — opens with…
a) Cultura olmeca  b) an examination  c) nothing at all
**Answer: a) Cultura olmeca.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The Venezuela book teaches “Cultura olmeca” as…
a) a part of art of ancient america  b) a sports rule  c) a cooking recipe
**Answer: a) a part of art of ancient america.**

**3.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) Cultura de Teotihuacán  b) long division  c) map coordinates
**Answer: a) Cultura de Teotihuacán.**

**4.** The parts of this lesson come from…
a) Venezuela's own Educación Artística books  b) a newspaper  c) a song chorus
**Answer: a) Venezuela's own Educación Artística books.**

**5.** “Civilización maya” is taught in this lesson as…
a) one of its named parts  b) an unrelated game  c) a spelling list
**Answer: a) one of its named parts.**
