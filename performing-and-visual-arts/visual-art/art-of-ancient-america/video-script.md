# Video script — Art of Ancient America

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

**Cultura olmeca.** The Olmec, mother culture of Mesoamerica — ceremonial centres on artificial plains, the first stepped pyramids (La Venta, Cuicuilco), the planned city of Monte Albán with its ball court and observatory; monumental stone sculpture, modelled ceramics and jade, obsidian and serpentine jewellery.

**Cultura de Teotihuacán.** Teotihuacán, the first planned city of Mesoamerica — the Avenue of the Dead, the Pyramids of the Sun and Moon and the Temple of Quetzalcóatl; stone sculpture and masks, tripod vessels painted in fresco, and fresco murals of gods and daily life in red and green.

**Civilización maya.** The Maya — stepped pyramids and palaces in limestone with corbelled vaults (Temple of the Inscriptions at Palenque, Pyramid of Kukulkán), ball courts, polychromed relief sculpture and jade masks, the Bonampak frescoes, and the hieroglyphic codices on vegetable-fibre paper, the Dresden Codex among them.

**Civilización azteca.** The Aztec — Tenochtitlán built on its lake with canals and causeways under a great twin pyramid; stone sculpture of the gods, the Aztec Calendar and the monumental Coatlicue; ceremonial objects and Moctezuma's quetzal-feather headdress, taken to Vienna.

**Culturas chavín y mochica.** The Andean cultures — Chavín's ceremonial centre of tapia and carved stone with its Lanzón monolith of jaguar and condor gods; Mochica's adobe pyramids Huaca del Sol and Huaca de la Luna and its red-on-white painted ceramics of human and animal forms.

**Cultura nazca y tiahuanaco.** Nazca's adobe architecture and weaving beside its ground drawings; Tiahuanaco's great fitted-stone walled cities entered by monolithic gates (the Puerta del Sol) and its characteristic cotton weaving.

**Civilización inca.** The Inca — masters of construction above all: polygonal fitted stonework, walls, roads and bridges across the Andes, terraced cities such as Cuzco and Machu Picchu fitted to the mountain; also ceramics, weaving and goldwork.

That is Art of Ancient America — Venezuela's own books teach it this way.
