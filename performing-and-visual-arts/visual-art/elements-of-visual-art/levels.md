# The Elements of Visual Art — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure — README, "Units, Lessons and Sub Lessons"). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number, as before. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. The sub lessons climb inside this one lesson, from the youngest books to the oldest.

## Sub lesson 1 — Visual art is a language
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: name the seven elements and say, in one sentence, what visual art is for.

## Sub lesson 2 — Lines and their feelings
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: draw the five line kinds and say the feeling of each; tell a geometric shape from a natural one.

## Sub lesson 3 — Form, and positive and negative space
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: name the four basic forms and point out positive and negative space in one picture.

## Sub lesson 4 — Value: light and dark steps
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: make a nine-step value scale in pencil with even steps.

## Sub lesson 5 — The principles of design
Sub lesson 5 questions from the type files. Pass mark: name at least five principles and point to one of them working in a real picture.

## Sub lesson 6 — Composition: subject, form and content (Grade 6)
Pass mark: define composition and name its three parts (subject, form, content), its two kinds (pictured, abstract) and its three zones (foreground, middleground, background).

## Sub lesson 7 — Reading a real painting: Agegnehu Engida's self-portrait (Grade 8)
Pass mark: name the book's worked example (Agegnehu Engida's self-portrait) and find colour, space, texture, shape and line at work in a portrait, answering what the character looks like, his movement and form, and his feeling.

## Sub lesson 8 — Shape, form and space pictured — the painters' words (Grade 8)
Pass mark: match shape, form and space to their plates (Figs. 5–7), call the elements 'the painters' words', and carry out Activity 1.1 in pairs on a local picture.

## Sub lesson 9 — Balance and its three types (Grade 8)
Pass mark: define balance as equal visual weight and tell the three types apart — symmetrical, asymmetrical, radial.

## Sub lesson 10 — Proportion, rhythm, emphasis, movement, simplicity (Grade 8)
Pass mark: define proportion by fittingness (person and warka tree), rhythm by repeated order, emphasis by enlarging/multiplying, movement as the eye's path, and simplicity as limited elements.

## Sub lesson 11 — Unity and harmony — the Mother example (Grade 8)
Pass mark: define unity (the oneness of the combined elements) and harmony (ውህደት — varied elements combined so they agree), with the Mother (1963 E.C.) example.

## Sub lesson 12 — Activity 1.2: six tasks on a pictured example (Grade 8)
Pass mark: carry out the six Activity 1.2 tasks on one pictured example.

## Sub lesson 13 — Design: the foundation under painting (teacher guide — evidence)
Pass mark: say, as guide evidence, that the Grade 6 guide makes design (ንድፍ/ዲዛይን) the visual foundation of its painting chapter (§1.4.1).

## Sub lesson 14 — Strokes, pressure and texture's classes (teacher guide — evidence)
Pass mark: name, as guide evidence, pressure and the stroke sets (individual, continuous, M/W strokes) and texture's divisions — tactile/visual; actual, simulated, invented.
