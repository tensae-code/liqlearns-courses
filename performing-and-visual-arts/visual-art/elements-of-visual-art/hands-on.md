# Hands-on — The Elements of Visual Art

The lesson with your hands, your feet and your voice together, not only your eyes.

## Seven-element hunt
Take one picture — a book cover, a poster, a photograph. Find and point to one line, one shape, one form, one patch of space, one texture, one light place and one dark place.

## Value steps by hand
With one pencil, shade nine boxes from almost white to almost black. Press a little harder each time. Keep the steps even.
