# Video script — The Elements of Visual Art

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A picture is made of seven things.
Line. Shape. Form. Space. Texture. Value. Colour.
A straight-up line feels strong. A lying-down line feels at rest.
Light steps and dark steps — that is value.
Find them all in one picture. Now you can read any picture.
