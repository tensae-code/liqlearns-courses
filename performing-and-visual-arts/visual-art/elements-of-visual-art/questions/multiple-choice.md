# The Elements of Visual Art — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What are the elements of visual art?
  a) Line, shape, form, texture, space, value and colour b) Only red, yellow and blue c) Songs, dances and plays
**Answer: a) Line, shape, form, texture, space, value and colour** — the Grade 5 book names these seven as the backbone of a work's message.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which line feels like rest, peace and quiet?
  a) A horizontal line b) A zigzag line c) A diagonal line
**Answer: a) A horizontal line** — the book gives rest, peace and quiet to the horizontal line.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which of these is a form, not a flat shape?
  a) A sphere b) A circle drawn on paper c) A square outline
**Answer: a) A sphere** — a sphere has depth; the others are flat shapes.

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** You add white to a colour. What have you made?
  a) A tint b) A shade c) A zigzag
**Answer: a) A tint** — adding white makes a tint; adding black makes a shade.

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** What does emphasis do in a work?
  a) It makes one part catch the eye first b) It removes all colour c) It makes every part the same size
**Answer: a) It makes one part catch the eye first** — emphasis makes one part stand out first.

**6.** (Sub lesson 6) A composition's three parts are —
a) paper, pencil, paint  b) subject, form, content  c) top, middle, bottom
**Answer: b) subject, form, content.**

**7.** (Sub lesson 6) An abstract (ምስል አልባ) composition —
a) copies a thing exactly  b) conveys only the idea  c) has no elements
**Answer: b) conveys only the idea.**

**8.** (Sub lesson 7) The Grade 8 book's worked example painting is —
a) a landscape by an unnamed painter  b) Agegnehu Engida's self-portrait  c) a cave painting
**Answer: b) Agegnehu Engida's self-portrait.**

**9.** (Sub lesson 8) The book calls line, colour, shape, space, texture and form —
a) the painters' tools  b) the painters' words  c) the painters' rules
**Answer: b) the painters' words.**

**10.** (Sub lesson 9) Radial balance is —
a) two equal halves  b) equal visual weight radiating from a centre  c) no balance at all
**Answer: b) equal visual weight radiating from a centre.**

**11.** (Sub lesson 10) Proportion is shown by —
a) fittingness and relative size  b) the brightest colour  c) the frame
**Answer: a) fittingness and relative size** — the person and the warka tree.

**12.** (Sub lesson 11) Unity in a work is —
a) using one colour only  b) the oneness the combined elements create  c) a heavy frame
**Answer: b) the oneness the combined elements create.**

**13.** (Sub lesson 12) Activity 1.2's last task is to —
a) sell the picture  b) ask local art professionals about the elements' use  c) frame the picture
**Answer: b) ask local art professionals about the elements' use.**

**14.** (Sub lesson 13) The Grade 6 guide makes what the visual foundation of its painting chapter?
a) colour  b) design (ንድፍ/ዲዛይን)  c) clay
**Answer: b) design (ንድፍ/ዲዛይን).**

**15.** (Sub lesson 14) Simulated texture is —
a) really there to touch  b) copied in drawing  c) the paper itself
**Answer: b) copied in drawing.**

**16. (Sub lesson 15)** The first aim of Grade 5 Chapter 1 is to…
  a) Tell the elements apart b) Memorise a song c) Build a stage
**Answer: a) Tell the elements apart** — tell the elements apart — the chapter is The Elements of Art.

**17. (Sub lesson 15)** With visual elements, the chapter's learners will…
  a) Express ideas in pictures b) Hide their pictures c) Copy prices
**Answer: a) Express ideas in pictures** — express ideas in pictures with visual elements.

**18. (Sub lesson 16)** Grade 7 Chapter 1 is called…
  a) Artistic Awareness b) Expressing Creativity c) Current Issues
**Answer: a) Artistic Awareness** — Ch. 1 is 'Artistic Awareness' — knowing the arts as systems.

**19. (Sub lesson 16)** Which pair belongs to Chapter 1's aims?
  a) The elements and types of theatre; the elements and types of film b) Only painting techniques c) Only dance steps
**Answer: a) The elements and types of theatre; the elements and types of film** — the aims name theatre's and film's elements and types alongside design and traditional music/dance.

**20. (Sub lesson 18)** Grade 8 Chapter 1 asks learners not only to use the elements and principles in reading artworks, but to…
  a) Make works with them b) Only name them c) Sell them
**Answer: a) Make works with them** — use in artworks AND making works with them — the opener's double aim.

**21. (Sub lesson 18)** The basic qenet, in Chapter 1's aims, is built…
  a) In varied keys, and its written sounds and chants read b) In one key only c) Without ever being read
**Answer: a) In varied keys, and its written sounds and chants read** — building the basic qenet in varied keys; reading its sounds and chants.

**22. (Sub lesson 19)** The summary calls the elements and principles…
  a) The basic picture language b) Optional decorations c) A modern invention
**Answer: a) The basic picture language** — elements and principles as the basic picture language.

**23. (Sub lesson 19)** Ethiopian music is founded on…
  a) The Ethiopian qenet b) Foreign scales only c) Written notation alone
**Answer: a) The Ethiopian qenet** — founded on Ethiopian qenet — the summary's ground.

**24. (Sub lesson 19)** Theatre and film, in the summary, are arts reflecting life whose subject is always…
  a) Human beings b) Landscapes only c) Instruments
**Answer: a) Human beings** — their subject is always human beings.
