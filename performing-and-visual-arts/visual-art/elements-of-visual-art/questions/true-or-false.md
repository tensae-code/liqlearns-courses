# The Elements of Visual Art — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Visual art can pass history from one generation to the next.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A zigzag line feels calm and slow.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Negative space is the space the things in a picture occupy.
**Answer: False**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Value means how light or dark a work is.
**Answer: True**

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** Pattern means a shape or colour is repeated.
**Answer: True**

**6.** (Sub lesson 6) The subject sits in the middleground of a composition. **Answer: True.**

**7.** (Sub lesson 9) Asymmetrical balance places unequal things so they receive equal view. **Answer: True.**

**8.** (Sub lesson 10) Simplicity (ቅለት) means using limited visual elements. **Answer: True.**

**9.** (Sub lesson 14) Pressure is the hand's force creating a line's value. **Answer: True.**

**10. (Sub lesson 15)** In the Grade 5 book's self-check, a ✗ means 'I did not achieve it' — and the crossed item is left alone.
**Answer: False** — a cross means re-study that item until it can honestly be ticked.

**11. (Sub lesson 15)** The Grade 5 self-check checklist is printed once only, at the front of the book.
**Answer: False** — it is first printed at pp. 38–39 and repeated after every chapter.

**12. (Sub lesson 17)** A work of design can be read by its elements and principles.
**Answer: True** — that naming is the chapter's first aim and its summary's ground.

**13. (Sub lesson 17)** Theatre and film have no types — every play and film is the same kind.
**Answer: False** — the chapter teaches the elements AND types of both.

**14. (Sub lesson 17)** Ethiopian traditional music and dances belong to this chapter's awareness, not only to performance.
**Answer: True** — they stand in the aims as things to know, not only to do.

**15. (Sub lesson 19)** Traditional instruments, in the summary, are material heritage bearing the community's knowledge and skill.
**Answer: True** — the summary's own words for the instruments.
