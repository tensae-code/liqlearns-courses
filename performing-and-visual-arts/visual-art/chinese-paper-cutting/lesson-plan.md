# Lesson plan — Chinese Paper Cutting

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Visual Art · **Lesson:** Chinese Paper Cutting
**Place in the curriculum:** Visual Art (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Chinese paper cutting — folding and cutting paper into balanced cut-out designs.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this course in the same title.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **China Grade 2 Art Vol.1** — Appreciating a modern Shanxi Lüliang paper-cut — a figure with twin fish and lotus at her feet and swallows circling, a symbol of spring; fold-and-cut paper cutting — fold, draw half a shape, cut, unfold into a symmetrical figure; works by Ku Shulan and Andersen shown; cut shapes can be拼贴 composed into a picture
2. **China Grade 2 Art Vol.2** — Making decorative borders — one motif repeated to both sides; fold the paper, draw the pattern, cut away the hatched parts
3. **China Grade 3 Art Vol.2** — Designing a window — China's classical lattice windows; draw the window's structure on card, add details, cut along the lines, remove the inner parts, mount and decorate with a cut pattern
4. **China Grade 4 Art Vol.2** — The beauty of symmetry — fold paper, draw the shape keeping its joins on the fold side, cut the big form then the details, unfold; a Fuling County (Shaanxi) auspicious paper-cut shown
5. **China HS Crafts** — Cutting a window flower — coloured Xuan paper; fold, draft half the symmetrical design, staple the layers together; cut from the centre outward, small before large, fine before coarse, details (eyes, petals) before the outline; unfold and tease the layers apart; paper-cut subjects speak in symbols, metaphors and homophones; the northern schools (Yuxian, Shanxi, Shaanxi) against the southern (Foshan, Fujian, Yangzhou)
