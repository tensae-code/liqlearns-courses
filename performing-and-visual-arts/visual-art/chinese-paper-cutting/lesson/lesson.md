# Chinese Paper Cutting

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about chinese paper cutting. Then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — China Grade 2 Art Vol.1

Appreciating a modern Shanxi Lüliang paper-cut — a figure with twin fish and lotus at her feet and swallows circling, a symbol of spring; fold-and-cut paper cutting — fold, draw half a shape, cut, unfold into a symmetrical figure; works by Ku Shulan and Andersen shown; cut shapes can be拼贴 composed into a picture

## Sub lesson 2 — China Grade 2 Art Vol.2

Making decorative borders — one motif repeated to both sides; fold the paper, draw the pattern, cut away the hatched parts

## Sub lesson 3 — China Grade 3 Art Vol.2

Designing a window — China's classical lattice windows; draw the window's structure on card, add details, cut along the lines, remove the inner parts, mount and decorate with a cut pattern

## Sub lesson 4 — China Grade 4 Art Vol.2

The beauty of symmetry — fold paper, draw the shape keeping its joins on the fold side, cut the big form then the details, unfold; a Fuling County (Shaanxi) auspicious paper-cut shown

## Sub lesson 5 — China HS Crafts

Cutting a window flower — coloured Xuan paper; fold, draft half the symmetrical design, staple the layers together; cut from the centre outward, small before large, fine before coarse, details (eyes, petals) before the outline; unfold and tease the layers apart; paper-cut subjects speak in symbols, metaphors and homophones; the northern schools (Yuxian, Shanxi, Shaanxi) against the southern (Foshan, Fujian, Yangzhou)
