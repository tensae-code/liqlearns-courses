# Gold-Thread Embroidery — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which threads make embroidery more precious?
a) gold and silver threads (and silk)  b) iron wire  c) plastic string
**Answer: a) gold and silver threads (and silk)**

**2.** The quality of embroidery is judged by…
a) its design, pattern, colouring, delicacy and precision  b) its weight only  c) how fast it was made
**Answer: a) its design, pattern, colouring, delicacy and precision**

**3.** Embroidery motifs fall into which two families?
a) flowers, plants and living beings — and geometric shapes  b) letters only — and numbers only  c) animals only — and houses only
**Answer: a) flowers, plants and living beings — and geometric shapes**

**4.** Counted-thread work is called…
a) graf-duzi (گراف‌دوزی)  b) siyah-mashq  c) chalipa
**Answer: a) graf-duzi (گراف‌دوزی)**
