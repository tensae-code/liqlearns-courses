# Video script — Gold-Thread Embroidery

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Threads of gold, threads of silver.

On velvet, on taffeta — embroidery becomes precious.
Flowers and living beings, or shapes of geometry — and sometimes writing itself.
Good embroidery is delicate. Good embroidery is precise.
Now work a simple piece yourself.
