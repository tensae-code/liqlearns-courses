## Sub lesson 1 — Knowing the kinds of embroidery

Embroidery's fabrics and threads: silk, gold and silver threads make the work more precious — silver-thread work on velvet and gold-thread work on taffeta are pictured, and counted-thread work (گراف‌دوزی) is named. The quality of embroidery is its design, pattern, colouring, delicacy and precision. Motifs fall into two families — flowers, plants and living beings, and geometric shapes — and writings are sometimes embroidered too; a bride's shawl of the 12th century AH is pictured.

## Sub lesson 2 — Doing simple embroidery

Then the pupil carries out a simple piece of embroidery themselves — a small motif worked by hand, judged by the lesson's own measures: design, pattern, colouring, delicacy and precision.
