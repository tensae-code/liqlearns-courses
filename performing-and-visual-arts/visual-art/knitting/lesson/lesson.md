# Knitting

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about knitting. Then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — Kazakhstan Grade 6 Artistic Labour (girls, Alimsaeva)

what it teaches: a knitted product begins with a sketch in which every detail, stitch and element is drawn — the sketch fixes proportions and pattern placement before a loop is cast; yarns — wool, linen, cotton, synthetic — chosen by the product's purpose, judging thickness, elasticity and dye fastness; what it teaches: knitting needles by material (steel, bone, aluminium, plastic, wood, bamboo) and by form (straight, circular, stocking, auxiliary); the needle 1.5–2 times thicker than the yarn; casting on the first row in pictured steps; the two stitches everything is built from — knit and purl — edge stitches, and binding off the last row; what it teaches: composing your own pattern from knit and purl combinations and drawing it as a chart; chart symbols and how a chart is read — bottom up, snaking right-to-left then left-to-right; the makes — a fringed scarf in four pictured steps (fringe cut 20–25 cm, folded, hooked through the edge loops), a kerchief, a hot pad and a phone case, each with its chart
