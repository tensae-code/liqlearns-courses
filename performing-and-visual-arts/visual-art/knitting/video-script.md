This lesson is Knitting.

Knitting — knitting from the first stitches — needles and their kinds, knitting from a chart, and designing and making knitted products such as scarves. — as Kazakhstan's school books teach it: what it teaches: a knitted product begins with a sketch in which every detail, stitch and element is drawn — the sketch fixes proportions and pattern placement before a loop is cast; yarns — wool, linen, cotton, synthetic — chosen by the product's purpose, judging thickness, elasticity and dye fastness.

Watch, listen — then say it back in your own words.
