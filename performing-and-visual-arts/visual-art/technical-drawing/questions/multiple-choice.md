# Technical Drawing — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Standard lettering on a technical drawing is…
a) inclined at 75° or vertical at 90°  b) always slanted at 45°  c) written any way
**Answer: a) inclined at 75° or vertical at 90°**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The basket arch (abatido) is made of…
a) one semicircle  b) three concordant arcs  c) straight lines
**Answer: b) three concordant arcs**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A point's frontal projection A2 carries its…
a) afastamento  b) cota (height)  c) name
**Answer: b) cota (height)**

**4. (Sub lesson 5)** In isometric axonometry, X and Y are both at…
a) 45°  b) 7° and 42°  c) 30°
**Answer: c) 30°**
