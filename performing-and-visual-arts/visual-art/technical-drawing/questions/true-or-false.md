# Technical Drawing — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The title block (legenda) holds name, class, school, theme and drawing number.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 5)** In cavaleira axonometry, depths are kept true.
**Answer: False** — depths are halved; only isometric keeps all dimensions true.

**3. (Sub lesson 6)** Dimension lines may touch the figure's vertices.
**Answer: False** — they must stay at least 0.4 cm clear.
