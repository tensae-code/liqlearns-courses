# Lesson plan — Technical Drawing

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Visual Art · **Lesson:** Technical Drawing
**Place in the curriculum:** Visual Art (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** technical drawing as one precise graphic language: geometric drawing standardised (lettering, framing, title block), architectural arches, orthogonal projections in two and three views, forms in axonometry (isometric, dimetric and cavaleira), and dimensioning (cotagem) — every construction step by step, tied to carpentry, metalwork and building work.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this course.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Geometric drawing and standardisation** — ruler and compass, standardised so anyone can read it
2. **Architectural arches** — span and rise; Roman, ogive, basket and ogee arches
3. **Orthogonal projections: two views** — front and top views on two planes
4. **The third view** — European and American arrangements of the side view
5. **Forms in axonometry** — isometric, dimetric and cavaleira — raising solids from their base view
6. **Dimensioning a drawing** — cotas in millimetres, R and Ø, base plus height

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier Visual Art lessons of this course. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Frame and standardise a drawing, and divide a segment by the mediatriz method.
2. Trace the four arches, project a solid in two and three views, raise it in the three axonometries, and dimension it.

## What you need
Ruler, compass, set square, protractor, pencil and A4 paper.
