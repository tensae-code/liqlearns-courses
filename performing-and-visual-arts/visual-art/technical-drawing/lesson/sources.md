Technical specification) — what it teaches: the graphic and text document containing information about an item's construction is called құрылымдық құжаттама (construction documentation); in construction documentation there are: the item's construction, the joining parts' how they are joined and mutually act, shown in the жалпы түрінің сызбасы (general appearance drawing); the құрастырма сызбасы (assembly drawing) — the drawing joining the parts, needed for joining and collecting; the бөлшек сызбасы (part drawing) — the part's drawing containing the kind of parts needed in production and other information; the сипаттама (спецификация — specification), the document showing the thing's composition; the схема (scheme), showing the joining parts with conditional signs; and the нұсқаулық (instruction) containing instructions and rules relating to making and joining the item; construction documentation can also be prepared with the help of special programmes («AutoCad», «КОМПАС-3D» and others); the pictured documents: a school desk (Парта) as the item, its general appearance drawing (the desk base's frame) and a part drawing with a specification table (Тетіктің сызбасы — Тығын. Тетік, 1-реттік сан) (student book; original target `visual-art/sketch-technical-drawing-and-blueprint`) | Book 9, printed p. 52 |

Технологиялық құжаттама (Technological documentation) — what it teaches: the graphic and text document describing an item's preparation technological process belongs to технологиялық құжаттама (technological documentation); the main technological documents are the технологиялық карта (technological card) and the жұмыс картасы (work card) and cards like them: a Технологиялық карта (technological card) is a document fully describing the technological method, the material used, the construction document, the tool-equipment and instruments shown, and the item and parts' processing process; a Жұмыс картасы (work card) is a document showing the time spent for carrying out a definite work or task (student book; original target `visual-art/sketch-technical-drawing-and-blueprint`) | Book 9, printed p. 53 |
