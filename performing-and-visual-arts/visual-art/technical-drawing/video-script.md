# Video script — Technical Drawing

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

One language, read by everyone — technical drawing!

Ruler and compass. Letters at 75 degrees. The title block.
Arches — Roman, ogive, basket, ogee.
Views — front, top, and the third view.
Axonometry — isometric, dimetric, cavaleira.
And numbers on everything — cotagem!

Step by step. Measure twice. Draw once.
