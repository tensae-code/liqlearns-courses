# Hands-on — Technical Drawing

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Frame and divide
Frame an A4 sheet (2.5 cm left, 0.5 cm elsewhere), fill in a title block, then divide a segment into two and into four equal parts by the mediatriz method.

## Three views of a box
Take a small box. Draw its front view, top view and side view — European arrangement — transferring depths with compass arcs.

## Raise it
Draw the same box in isometric axonometry, then dimension it in millimetres, keeping dimension lines clear of the vertices.
