# Video script — Paint and Colour Crafts

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This is Syria's own lesson.

**Lesson 16 "Creating with Hand Movements".** how we benefit from the hand's movements and shapes in drawing — using your hand to draw and execute new artistic forms and adding decorative elements of your own invention.

**Lesson 19 (technique).** pastel colours — a technique of depiction in kinds: oil pastel and chalk pastel.

**Lesson 22 "Drawing on Stones".** contemplating the stones and their colouring — with what did they suggest to you, and were you visually affected by them? What kind of colours are used in executing the works? Agree with your companions on some smooth stones and colour them in an artistic way using the fitting colours — oil colours, Chinese ink and gouache colours can be used in drawing on stones.

**Lesson 25 "Drawing on Wood".** discussing the materials available in our environment and how to employ them in making artworks — painted wooden fences and articulated snakes; let us create together by carving the wooden pieces in the shape of an animal and colouring them.

That is Paint and Colour Crafts — Syria's way, kept by name.
