# Lesson plan — Paint and Colour Crafts

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Visual Art · **Lesson:** Paint and Colour Crafts
**Place in the curriculum:** Visual Art (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Paint and Colour Crafts — Syria's own teaching of this topic, kept as named parts: how we benefit from the hand's movements and shapes in drawing — using your hand to draw and execute new artistic forms and adding decorative elements of your own invention
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the course's earlier lessons in its title; recommended first, never forced.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of ..." in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Lesson 16 "Creating with Hand Movements"** — how we benefit from the hand's movements and shapes in drawing — using your hand to draw and execute new artistic forms and adding decorative elements of your own invention
2. **Lesson 19 (technique)** — pastel colours — a technique of depiction in kinds: oil pastel and chalk pastel
3. **Lesson 22 "Drawing on Stones"** — contemplating the stones and their colouring — with what did they suggest to you, and were you visually affected by them? What kind of colours are used in executing the works? Agree with your companions on some smooth stones and colour them in an artistic way using the fitting colours — oil colours, Chinese ink and gouache colours can be used in drawing on stones
4. **Lesson 25 "Drawing on Wood"** — discussing the materials available in our environment and how to employ them in making artworks — painted wooden fences and articulated snakes; let us create together by carving the wooden pieces in the shape of an animal and colouring them

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
