# Paint and Colour Crafts

## Do it first

Name one thing that belongs in this lesson — Paint and Colour Crafts — Syria's own teaching of this topic, kept as named parts: how we benefit from the hand's movements and shapes in drawing — using your hand to draw and execute new artistic forms and adding decorative elements of your own invention. Say what it is in your own words. If you can name one, you are already inside the lesson.

## Sub lesson 1 — Lesson 16 "Creating with Hand Movements"

how we benefit from the hand's movements and shapes in drawing — using your hand to draw and execute new artistic forms and adding decorative elements of your own invention.

## Sub lesson 2 — Lesson 19 (technique)

pastel colours — a technique of depiction in kinds: oil pastel and chalk pastel.

## Sub lesson 3 — Lesson 22 "Drawing on Stones"

contemplating the stones and their colouring — with what did they suggest to you, and were you visually affected by them? What kind of colours are used in executing the works? Agree with your companions on some smooth stones and colour them in an artistic way using the fitting colours — oil colours, Chinese ink and gouache colours can be used in drawing on stones.

## Sub lesson 4 — Lesson 25 "Drawing on Wood"

discussing the materials available in our environment and how to employ them in making artworks — painted wooden fences and articulated snakes; let us create together by carving the wooden pieces in the shape of an animal and colouring them.

## Do It Yourself

1. Choose one named part of this lesson and find out one more true thing about it — from a person who knows it, or from the book your part came from.
2. Make or perform a small piece in the way of that part: a rhythm, a step, a drawing, a shaped piece of clay.
3. Write one sentence: what does this part show about Syria's own way of teaching its arts?
