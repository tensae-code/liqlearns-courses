# Hands-on — Colour Psychology and Symbolism

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Book 4, Colouring, "The Psychology of Colours"
Do the thing this part teaches, with real materials where the book names them: colours' proven effect on the human psyche, mood, health and productive power, and their symbolism read colour by colour: white for purity, light, joy and peace; black for darkness and grief, the Abbasids' colour of mourning; yellow for light, sun and gold, much used in mosque and church ornament; red for raging feeling, strength, revolution and danger, the traffic signal's colour; blue for friendship, wisdom and eternity; violet, love and wisdom mixed, for calm sadness; green for growth, hope, life and peace — and the warm colours (red, orange, yellow) against the cool (blue, green, violet)
