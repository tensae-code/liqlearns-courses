# Colour Psychology and Symbolism — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What do colours affect, on the book's evidence?
**Answer: The human psyche, mood, health and productive power.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What does green stand for?
**Answer: Growth, hope, life and peace.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** What does yellow stand for?
**Answer: Light, sun and gold — much used in mosque and church ornament.**
