# Colour Psychology and Symbolism — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** White symbolises…
a) purity, light, joy and peace  b) grief  c) danger
**Answer: a) purity, light, joy and peace**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Red stands for…
a) raging feeling, strength, revolution and danger  b) calm sadness  c) eternity
**Answer: a) raging feeling, strength, revolution and danger** — it is the traffic signal's colour.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Blue stands for…
a) friendship, wisdom and eternity  b) mourning  c) gold
**Answer: a) friendship, wisdom and eternity**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** The warm colours are…
a) red, orange and yellow  b) blue, green and violet  c) black and white
**Answer: a) red, orange and yellow** — against the cool colours: blue, green and violet.
