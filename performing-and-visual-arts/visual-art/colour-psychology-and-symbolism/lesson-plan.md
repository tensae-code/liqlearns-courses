# Lesson plan — Colour Psychology and Symbolism

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Visual Art · **Lesson:** Colour Psychology and Symbolism
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 2 — Visual Art (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** what colours do to people — their effect on mood and health, and their symbolism colour by colour.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the visual-art and culture lessons before it in the ladder — recommended first, never forced.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Book 4, Colouring, "The Psychology of Colours"** — colours' proven effect on the human psyche, mood, health and productive power, and their symbolism read colour by colour: white for purity, light, joy and peace; black for darkness and grief, the Abbasids' colour of mourning; yellow for light, sun and gold, much used in mosque and church ornament; red for raging feeling, strength, revolution and danger, the traffic signal's colour; blue for friendship, wisdom and eternity; violet, love and wisdom mixed, for calm sadness; green for growth, hope, life and peace — and the warm colours (red, orange, yellow) against the cool (blue, green, violet)
