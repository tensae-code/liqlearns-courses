# Kite Making — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Kites in this lesson are designed, not only made.
**Answer: True.**.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Only natural materials may be woven in this work.
**Answer: False.** — a variety of natural and man-made materials.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The pupil makes the kite they designed.
**Answer: True.**.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** A kite's theme is like a picture's subject.
**Answer: True.**.
