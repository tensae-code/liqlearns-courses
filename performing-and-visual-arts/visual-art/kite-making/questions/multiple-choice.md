# Kite Making — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Barbados's kites are designed and made to…
a) depict given themes  b) hide their designs  c) be thrown away
**Answer: a) depict given themes**.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A theme on a kite works like…
a) a subject in a picture  b) a price tag  c) a knot
**Answer: a) a subject in a picture**.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** After designing, the pupil…
a) makes the kite  b) only talks about it  c) paints a wall
**Answer: a) makes the kite**.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** The weaving in this craft work uses…
a) natural materials only  b) man-made materials only  c) a variety of natural and man-made materials
**Answer: c) a variety of natural and man-made materials**.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** This work stands in the guide under…
a) Exploring Technology / crafts  b) Printing  c) Picture Composition
**Answer: a) Exploring Technology / crafts** — Class 2, printed p. 8.
