# Kite Making

## Do it first

Quick start: say in one sentence what you think this lesson is about — kite making. Then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — A kite that depicts a theme

Barbados's Class 2 craft work sets **designing and making kites to depict given themes** — the kite is not plain: its design carries a theme, the way a picture carries a subject.

## Sub lesson 2 — Making the kite

The design is built: the kite is **made** by the pupil's own hands, from plan to finished kite.

## Sub lesson 3 — Weaving with natural and man-made materials

The same Exploring Technology work sets **weaving with a variety of natural and man-made materials** — the maker's two material families, worked together.
