# Mongolian Crafts — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Uran darkhan is the…
a) master craftsman  b) school teacher  c) singer
**Answer: a) master craftsman**

**2.** Mongolia's felt lessons teach felting with…
a) one colour, then two, then three colours  b) ten colours at once  c) no colours
**Answer: a) one colour, then two, then three colours**

**3.** The Grade 8 lesson meets which crafts?
a) master craftsman's metalwork, and leather and felt art  b) glass blowing only  c) plastic moulding
**Answer: a) master craftsman's metalwork, and leather and felt art**
