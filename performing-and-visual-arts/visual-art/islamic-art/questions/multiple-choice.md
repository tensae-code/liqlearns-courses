# Islamic Art — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Islamic art was born in…
a) the 1st Hijri century  b) the 19th century  c) the Stone Age
**Answer: a) the 1st Hijri century** — gathered first by the Umayyads, then by Abbasid Baghdad.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Its two great sources were…
a) Byzantine and Sasanian  b) Greek and Roman only  c) Chinese and Indian only
**Answer: a) Byzantine and Sasanian**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Mosul inlay work is called…
a) al-takfit  b) qashani  c) lusterware
**Answer: a) al-takfit** — metalwork from Sasanian and Coptic inheritances to Mosul inlay (al-takfit).

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** The Umayyad style belongs to…
a) Damascus  b) Cairo  c) Andalusia
**Answer: a) Damascus** — the Umayyad style of Damascus; the Fatimid style is Cairo's.
