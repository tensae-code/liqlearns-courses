# Islamic Art — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How far did Islamic art spread?
**Answer: From India to Andalusia.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why did the regional schools keep converging?
**Answer: Princes moving artists and merchants moving objects kept the schools converging.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What stayed free of living-creature images?
**Answer: Religious buildings.**
