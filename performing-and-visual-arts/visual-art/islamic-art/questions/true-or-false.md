# Islamic Art — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Islamic art peaked in the 7th–8th Hijri centuries.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Qashani tiles are an applied art of the Islamic world.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The Safavid style belongs to Egypt.
**Answer: False** — The Seljuk and then Safavid styles belong to Iran.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Islamic objects travelled with merchants as far as Sudan.
**Answer: True**
