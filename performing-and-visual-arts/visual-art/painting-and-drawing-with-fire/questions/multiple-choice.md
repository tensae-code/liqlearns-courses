# Painting and Drawing with Fire — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Gourd ornament in Sudan is burned in with…
a) the electric cautery  b) a brush  c) a knife only
**Answer: a) the electric cautery** — ornaments are burned in by fire with the electric cautery.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The gourd used for this ornament is called…
a) al-qar'  b) jebena  c) birsh
**Answer: a) al-qar'**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The basis of gourd ornament lines is…
a) horizontal, vertical and slanting lines  b) only spirals  c) only dots
**Answer: a) horizontal, vertical and slanting lines** — in a single colour.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** On pottery, the pair of colours used is…
a) red and yellow  b) blue and green  c) black and white
**Answer: a) red and yellow**
