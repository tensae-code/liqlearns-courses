# Painting and Drawing with Fire — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Gourd ornaments are burned in by fire.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Gourd ornament uses many colours at once.
**Answer: False** — Its basis is lines in a single colour.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The cautery is an electric tool.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Red and yellow are the pair used on pottery.
**Answer: True**
