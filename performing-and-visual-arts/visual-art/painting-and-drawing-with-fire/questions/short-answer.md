# Painting and Drawing with Fire — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the pupil make in imitation of gourd ornament?
**Answer: A design imitating gourd ornament in black only.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What other decorative unit does the lesson set?
**Answer: A geometric decorative unit of intersecting horizontal and vertical lines.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Which Sudanese utensils does the wider topic ornament?
**Answer: Palm-frond and clay work, and above all the gourd (al-qar').**
