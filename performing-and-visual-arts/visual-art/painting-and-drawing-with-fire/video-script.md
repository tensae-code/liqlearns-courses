**Design & Ornament Topic "Heritage Ornament Colours on Utensils".** how Sudan's local utensils are ornamented — palm-frond and clay work and above all the gourd (al-qar'), whose ornaments are burned in by fire with the electric cautery, their basis horizontal, vertical and slanting lines in a single colour, red and yellow the pair used on pottery; the pupil makes a design imitating gourd ornament in black only, and a geometric decorative unit of intersecting horizontal and vertical lines
