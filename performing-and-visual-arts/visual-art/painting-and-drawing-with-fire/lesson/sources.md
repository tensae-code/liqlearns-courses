| Kazakhstan — §5 «Знакомство с искусством пирографии» — what it teaches: pyrography — «drawing with fire» from the Greek — a drawing burned into wood (also leather, fabric, gourd) with a red-hot needle; it decorates caskets, wooden dishes, hangers, statuettes, panels and pictures, and its subjects run from signs and pictograms to ornament, lettering, portraits and caricatures; the other burnings — by acid, friction, open flame, hot sand, and a magnifier in the sun; its history — centuries old, named in England, once done with a heated poker or an iron nail, then Paquelin's surgical apparatus on spirit or gas, today the electric burner with changeable nibs and the laser machine; the woods that take it best — birch, aspen, poplar, linden — sanded smooth, the design transferred through carbon paper; Rembrandt, Picasso and Hugo all burned (student book; original target `visual-art/pyrography-drawing-with-fire`) | Book 2, printed p. 22 |

| Kazakhstan — 2.5–2.6 (continued). Фьюмаж және отпен сурет салу (Fumage and painting with fire) — what it teaches: Фьюмаж (fr. fumage — smoking, sooting) is one of surrealism's techniques; its peculiarity is taking an image by holding a candle's or kerosene lamp's flame under paper or canvas; this technique was thought up by Wolfgang Paalen (Вольфганг Паален); later Salvador Dalí used fumage; today the Turkish-American artist Burhan Dogançay (Бұрхан Доғанчай) uses fumage; fire can be used not only for drawing a picture but as a picture-drawing tool: the Canadian artist Steven Spazuk (Стивен Спазук) uses a candle's flame instead of a brush and pencil for drawing pictures — he gives the possibility to draw pictures with soot using the fumage (фьюмаж) technique: the artist brings the candle's flame close to the paper's face and with smoke draws abstract forms and empty spaces; then with a special brush's help he turns them into real pictures (his portrait and tiger works are pictured); «Помпейдің соңғы күні» (The Last Day of Pompeii) — Karl Bryullov's (Карл Брюллов) answer: the composition's basis is made by light-colour effects; two kinds of light mutually act in the picture: the lightning's flashed light-yellow cold colour and the hot volcano crater's dark-red colour; the lightning lights the picture's front side, the volcano's red colour rising brings the burning city's image before the eyes; the volcano's dark-red colour passes to the people's clothes' red colour and to the dark-brown colour; red colour dominates in the picture: the figures' clothes, the red-coloured background — everything is red; only in the place the lightning's light fell, in the centre, blue, light green and yellow colours are noticed (student book; original target `visual-art/fumage-smoke-painting`) | Book 3, printed p. 57 |
