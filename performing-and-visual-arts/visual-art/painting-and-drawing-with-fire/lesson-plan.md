# Lesson plan — Painting and Drawing with Fire

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Visual Art · **Lesson:** Painting and Drawing with Fire
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 2 — Visual Art (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** making pictures with fire — Sudan's gourd ornament burned in with the electric cautery.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the visual-art and culture lessons before it in the ladder — recommended first, never forced.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Design & Ornament Topic "Heritage Ornament Colours on Utensils"** — how Sudan's local utensils are ornamented — palm-frond and clay work and above all the gourd (al-qar'), whose ornaments are burned in by fire with the electric cautery, their basis horizontal, vertical and slanting lines in a single colour, red and yellow the pair used on pottery; the pupil makes a design imitating gourd ornament in black only, and a geometric decorative unit of intersecting horizontal and vertical lines

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
