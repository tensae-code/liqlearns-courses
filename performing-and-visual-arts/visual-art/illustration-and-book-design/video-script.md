# Video script — Illustration and Book Design

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Too many details? A book cannot carry them all.

**Stylise!** Drop the small things. Keep the shape — a tree becomes triangles and a trunk, a flower becomes pure form.

In colour. In black-and-white. Clear at a glance.

That is the illustrator's move.
