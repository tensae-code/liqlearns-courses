## Do it first

## Sub lesson 1 — Stylisation: simplifying for illustration

The Grade 5 book's Section 25, "Stylisation", teaches the illustrator's key move: **simplifying an image down to geometric forms** — a tree, an architectural shape — and **stylised flower images in colour and in black-and-white variants**. A stylised image drops the small details and keeps the clear shape, so it can sit inside a book, repeat as a pattern, and read at a glance. (This lesson's Belarus stock is the stylisation section, as filed; the sources note says so.)
