# Lesson plan — Illustration and Book Design

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Visual Art · **Lesson:** Illustration and Book Design
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 2 — Visual Art (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** illustrating and designing books — here stocked from Belarus's stylisation teaching: simplifying an image to geometric forms for illustration, in colour and in black-and-white.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the course's earlier visual art lessons — recommended first, never forced.
