# Illustration and Book Design — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is stylisation?
**Answer: Simplifying an image down to geometric forms, keeping the clear shape.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the book's three stylisation examples.
**Answer: A tree, an architectural shape, and stylised flower images (in colour and in black-and-white).**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Why do books use stylised images?
**Answer: They read at a glance, sit well inside a book, and can repeat as pattern.**
