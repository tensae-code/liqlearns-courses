# Illustration and Book Design — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Stylisation simplifies an image down to…
a) photographic detail  b) geometric forms  c) letters
**Answer: b) geometric forms** — .

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The book's stylised examples include…
a) a tree and an architectural shape  b) a portrait and a battle  c) a map
**Answer: a) a tree and an architectural shape** — .

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Stylised flowers are made in…
a) colour only  b) black-and-white only  c) colour and black-and-white variants
**Answer: c) colour and black-and-white variants** — colour and in black-and-white variants.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** A stylised image keeps…
a) every small detail  b) the clear shape  c) only the outline's colour
**Answer: b) the clear shape** — small details are dropped.

**5. (Sub lesson 1)** Stylised images suit books because they…
a) are expensive  b) read at a glance and can repeat as pattern  c) hide the subject
**Answer: b) read at a glance and can repeat as pattern** — read at a glance and can repeat as a pattern.
