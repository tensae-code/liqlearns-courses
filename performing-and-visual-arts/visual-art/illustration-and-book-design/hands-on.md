# Hands-on — Illustration and Book Design

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Stylise it
Draw a tree as it looks. Then draw it again in geometric forms only — triangle or circle crown, rectangle trunk. Then a flower, twice: once in colour, once in black-and-white, the book's two variants.

## A book corner
Design a small corner ornament for a book page from your stylised flower — repeat it along the page edge and see the pattern read at a glance.
