| Kazakhstan — 3.9. Отделка и декорирование (teacher-guide evidence) — what it teaches: by the finishing of wooden art products is understood the creation on the wood's surface of protective-decorative coatings with the help of lacquer-paint and film materials; отделка (finishing) is the completing stage of making a product, it is at the same time one of the means of its artistic expressiveness; begin the lesson with a short conversation about the decorative-applied art of the Kazakh people — at studying the «Декоративно-прикладное искусство» section the learners already learned about the artistic devices of speaking (выговаривания) and the kinds of decorating; ask to recall what they know about decorating products from varied materials, about the artistic processing of wood by Kazakh craftsmen — what crafts of Kazakh masters are known, whether they can name national household objects, furniture, kitchen utensils, musical instruments; remind the learners of the multitude of Kazakh ornament, with which were painted, burnt and decorated many products — at the explanation demonstrate samples of national household objects, kitchen utensils, musical instruments decorated by varied ways; explain to the learners that after work's completion, depending on the product's purpose, a decorative-protective finishing of the product with lacquer is carried out; finishing is begun after sanding the product, singeing and cleaning its ends; in the majority of cases the surface of turned products of toys and souvenirs before covering with lacquer is first covered with a protective layer of colourless морилка (stain), then an acrylic primer — the primer is needed for filling the wood's pores and an even laying of paint; when the surface dries, a toy can be painted with gouache or tempera — for painting other kinds of wood морилка is used, a special material, usually liquid; in the process of work морилка is laid on the processed wood for giving it the needed colour; at painting with морилка the wood's natural drawing — текстура (texture) — in distinction from painting with other dyers does not get covered, on the contrary becomes more contrast, more juicy; морилки on a water base practically always protect the wood (except water ones); there exist colourless морилки possessing exclusively protective properties and coloured ones; liquid морилка is laid with a roller or a specially prepared tampon, if the laying area is not large than a brush; this way provides a more even laying than laying with a brush and lets one avoid smudges; the tampon is prepared by hand: cotton wool is wrapped in a clean rag; патина (patina) is a thin налёт (film) formed on the surface of copper, iron, brass, plaster, wood; it is applied at the artificial ageing of products by the technology of artificial ageing — the formed natural or artificial налёт lets one give furniture and other products an antique, antiquarian character; вощение (waxing) is the laying of mastic made of wax dissolved in turpentine or petrol on products from hard kinds of wood or marble, with further rubbing of it to a semi-matte shine; браширование (brushing), or текстурирование (texturing) of wood, is the stage supposing the laying on the surface of a relief characteristic for old wood — the brushed board looks realistically like old wood and has an original textural texture (teacher-guide evidence;
