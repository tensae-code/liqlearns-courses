# Woodwork and Wood Carving

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about woodwork and wood carving. Then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — A flat aesthetic composition in wood

The flat composition has only two dimensions, length and width. Choose suitable wood pieces, use the file to tidy the irregular edges, and add the adhesive to gather the pieces together in an innovative style. A flat work executed with matchsticks shows how small the pieces can be — and ice-cream sticks invent still more shapes.

## Sub lesson 2 — A solid composition from offcuts

When woods are cut for forming and manufacture, small pieces result — wood remains (بقايا الأخشاب), regular and irregular, of different shapes and sizes, that factories and workshops usually throw away. The three-dimensional composition gathers a quantity of them: file the irregular edges tidy, then glue the pieces together in an innovative style. Even a broken wooden clothes hanger is material.

## Sub lesson 3 — The bases of a wood structure

A wooden artistic structure stands on artistic bases: each structure has a focal point attracting the viewer's attention; the elements' distribution is balanced, whether organised symmetrically or asymmetrically; and there is unity of material, while the shapes, colours and sizes may differ or resemble one another.
