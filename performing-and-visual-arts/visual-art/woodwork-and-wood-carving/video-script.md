# Video script — Woodwork and Wood Carving

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Wood that factories throw away can become art.

Flat work first: pieces filed smooth, glued into a picture with length and width.

Then standing work: offcuts gathered into three dimensions.

Every structure needs a focal point, balance, and one material's unity.

That is woodwork — building beauty from pieces.
