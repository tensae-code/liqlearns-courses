# Mongolian Painters and Their Works — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Reading a Mongolian painter's artwork means…
a) looking closely at what the painting shows and tells  b) copying it without looking  c) naming its price
**Answer: a) looking closely at what the painting shows and tells**

**2.** The Grade 8 closing lesson meets…
a) the representatives of Mongolian fine art and their works  b) only foreign painters  c) photographers only
**Answer: a) the representatives of Mongolian fine art and their works**
