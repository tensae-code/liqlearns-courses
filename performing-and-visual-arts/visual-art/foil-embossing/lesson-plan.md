# Lesson plan — Foil Embossing

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Visual Art · **Lesson:** Foil Embossing
**Place in the curriculum:** Visual Art (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Foil embossing — pressing designs into foil, and making national-style jewellery by embossing..
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this course in the same title.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. ## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Kazakhstan Grade 4 Artistic Labour** — making a national-style jewellery piece from thick foil: transfer the ornament, emboss it with a blunt tool, add fine detail — step photos
2. **Kazakhstan Grade 4 Labour Training (Ormanbekova)** — chasing (чеканка) and embossing (тиснение): pressing a relief design into foil on a soft mat, alternating raised and sunken parts, to make a "Golden Man" ornament
3. **Kazakhstan Grade 7 Artistic Labour (boys, Chukalin)** — what it teaches: embossing foil — foil is sheet metal up to 0.3 mm, soft and plastic, so a relief image can be pressed into it quickly with the simplest tools; the embossing stylus (давильник) with its rounded working part, or plasticine tools, worked on a rubber- or linoleum-covered board; reliefs from foil decorate photo albums, photograph frames, caskets and turned dishes, and become masks and decorative plates on folk-tale and epic motifs or compositions of architectural monuments; a panel made in five steps — sketch, transfer, work the contours, colour with acrylic (excess blotted so the side lines stay visible), blacken, wipe and clean the contours with solvent for volume
4. **Kazakhstan Grade 9 NIS Art (Akhmetkalieva)** — what it teaches: зергерлік is one of the art kinds come from old times and not losing its value — gold and silver decorations come from Kazakh zergers' hands, and their forms and patterns are the Kazakh people's history and culture, its worldview; every man's ornament has its own meaning; the practical work — making foil decorations: materials: foil (фольга — the lids of dairy products in plastic packs, e.g. curd-mass packs, can be used), after gathering the needed foil wash it clean and dry it; the picture's face must be flat; cardboard (картон) 0.5 mm thick, two-sided sticky tape (скотч), small shiny beads, thin copper and aluminium wire, decorative cord; technology: first prepare the made item's sketch — draw it on thick paper, cut it out, then copy the sketch to the foil and glue it; cut the sketch leaving 1 cm around, cut the corners and fold the foil's excess back; then decorate the item's second face the same way; if making earrings, pierce a hole in the needed place, fix a hook (ілмек тағатын орнын белгілеп, ілмекті бекіт); the item can be decorated with beads, decorative cord ornaments, and сәнди шиюқималар (decorative ?) can also be used — the finished foil earrings and pendants are pictured
