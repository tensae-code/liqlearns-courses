This lesson is Garment Pattern Drafting.

Garment Pattern Drafting — Drafting garment patterns — drawing and cutting the pattern for one-piece-sleeve garments, ponchos and capes. — as Kazakhstan's school books teach it: what it teaches: to prepare the product the cut's draft must be constructed; the draft's correct construction depends on the product's fitting the body and the measurements' correct taking; the measurements table for the one-piece-sleeve shoulder garment (half neck, half chest, half hip circumferences, shoulder circumference, back length to waist, product length); in the draft the one-piece sleeve is drawn together with the front and back — that is, the back and front parts are taken together with the sleeve; to draft a shoulder garment first take the draft's basic grid (базистік тор) — by the grid's vertical and horizontal lines the back's and front's width and length are measured; the пішім (construction) lines form the main contour of the cut draft; the one-piece-sleeve garment's cut draft consists of the back and front parts — their form and measurement are the same, only the neckline line's form differs, so the back and front are drawn on one basic grid; the ease allowances — to half chest circumference (ҚЖҚа) 6–8 cm, to shoulder circumference (ҚИа) 5–7 cm, to neckline width (ҚЖМойа) 1.0 cm; the construction tables in full — the grid, the neckline, the sleeve, the side and hem lines, the waist line — with a formula for every segment; calculating the fabric amount: at width 140–150 cm — Бұ + 20 cm (for processing the hem and cutting the facings); at width 80–100 cm — 2 Бұ + 20 cm.
