# Video script — Exhibiting Your Artwork

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Made the work? Now show it — properly.

Theme. Slogan. Title.

Choose the works — at least one of every maker.

Captions: name, title, technique, material.

Hang at eye level. Hang it straight.

Then stand by your work — and present it.
