# Exhibiting Your Artwork

## Do it first

Quick start: from the title alone, guess what this lesson's work is. Then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — Setting up the exhibition

An exhibition is made by a **step plan**: **theme, slogan and title**; **selecting the works — at least one of every maker**; **writing the captions with maker's name, title, techniques and material**; **placing the furniture**; **laying out the room and hanging or laying the works**. A **poster carries the slogan**, and works are **hung at eye level and straight**.

## Sub lesson 2 — Presenting the exhibition

Then the exhibition is **presented to its public**: **each maker explains their work and the origin of its inspiration**. In the heritage series the same craft presents **heritage objects and pictures in the classroom**: your story to a known audience in **one to two minutes** — the **original background, function and meaning** of your object or tradition, **how it changed in your new application**, your **sources of inspiration**, and **why this heritage must be kept for the future** — with a **"tip and a top" of feedback** after each presentation.
