# Exhibiting Your Artwork — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does a caption carry?
**Answer: The maker's name, the title, the techniques and the material.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the step plan's parts in order.
**Answer: Theme, slogan and title; selecting the works; writing the captions; placing the furniture; laying out the room and hanging or laying the works.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What feedback follows each heritage presentation?
**Answer: A "tip and a top" — one tip and one top.**
