# Exhibiting Your Artwork — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The poster carries the exhibition's slogan.
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In the presentation, each maker explains their work and the origin of its inspiration.
**Answer: True.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Works may be hung crooked if the maker likes it.
**Answer: False — works are hung at eye level and straight.**
