# Exhibiting Your Artwork — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The selection rule for the exhibition is…
a) only the best maker's works  b) at least one work of every maker  c) works chosen by lot
**Answer: b) at least one work of every maker**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Works are hung…
a) near the floor  b) at eye level and straight  c) any way at all
**Answer: b) at eye level and straight**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The heritage presentation lasts…
a) one to two minutes  b) an hour  c) ten seconds
**Answer: a) one to two minutes**
