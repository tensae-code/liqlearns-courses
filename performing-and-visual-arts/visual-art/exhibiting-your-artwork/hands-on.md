# Hands-on — Exhibiting Your Artwork

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Run the step plan
In groups, set up a small exhibition of class work by the step plan: theme, slogan and title, selection (at least one work of every maker), captions, furniture, layout — works at eye level and straight.

## Make the poster
Design the exhibition poster carrying its slogan, so a visitor knows the theme before entering.

## Present in two minutes
Present one work or heritage object in one to two minutes: background, function and meaning, your new application, and why it must be kept. Take one tip and one top of feedback.
