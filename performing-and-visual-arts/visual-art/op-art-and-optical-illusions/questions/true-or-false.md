# Op Art and Optical Illusions — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Wavy lines drawn on flat paper can seem to stir.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Lines that look different in length are always different — testing is pointless.
**Answer: False** — the book sets the test because the eye can be tricked.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Vasarely is one of the artists whose works the book pictures.
**Answer: True** — four of his works, beside Riley's Fragment 5.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Optical illusions can be achieved especially easily with a computer today.
**Answer: True**
