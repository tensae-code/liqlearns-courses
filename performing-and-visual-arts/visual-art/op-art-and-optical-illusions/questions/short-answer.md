# Op Art and Optical Illusions — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What are optical illusions also called in the book?
**Answer: Optical tricks.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What question does the book ask about the blue and red circles?
**Answer: Do they seem to rest or to move?**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** How do you make your work following Vasarely?
**Answer: First draw it, then colour the work.**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Where else should you try to spot optical tricks?
**Answer: In nature and around you.**
