# Op Art and Optical Illusions — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** An optical illusion on paper looks as if it is…
a) moving, though it is only lines or forms on a flat ground  b) wet  c) singing
**Answer: a) moving, though it is only lines or forms on a flat ground**.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Densely arranged wavy lines seem to…
a) stir  b) disappear  c) turn into letters
**Answer: a) stir**.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The book asks you to test the dotted and arrowed lines for…
a) sameness of length  b) their colour  c) their price
**Answer: a) sameness of length**.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Fragment 5 is a work by…
a) Bridget Riley  b) Victor Vasarely  c) Rembrandt
**Answer: a) Bridget Riley**.
