# Video script — Op Art and Optical Illusions

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

These lines are not moving. Or are they?

Packed tight and wavy, they seem to stir on the flat page.

That is an optical illusion — a trick for the eye.

Riley and Vasarely built whole pictures from the trick.

Draw yours. Colour it. Watch it move.
