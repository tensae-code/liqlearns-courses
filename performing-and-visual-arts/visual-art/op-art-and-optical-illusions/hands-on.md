# Hands-on — Op Art and Optical Illusions

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Wavy lines that stir
Fill a page with densely arranged wavy lines, evenly spaced. Step back and look — do they seem to stir? Show a companion.

## Follow Vasarely
Choose a simple pattern of repeated forms, draw it carefully in a grid, then colour it in two strong colours. Look for the moment the flat pattern begins to pulse.
