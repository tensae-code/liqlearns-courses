# Video script — Armenian Miniature Painting

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This is Armenia's own lesson.

**Toros Roslin named among the masters.** Toros Roslin named among the masters — the great miniature painter stands in the grade-5 gallery beside Sargis Baghdasaryan's "Davit Bek" sculpture.

**Armenian manuscripts and miniature schools.** Armenian manuscripts and miniature schools — the "Msho Charentir" of 660 leaves, each from one calf's skin; scribes copied the text, painters the pictures; the Etchmiadzin Gospel and Hetum II's Lectionary; Sargis Pitsak's Gospel (1336) and Toros Roslin's Malatia Gospel (1268) in the Matenadaran.

**Surenyants the muralist.** Surenyants the muralist — Vardges Surenyants made many copies of manuscript miniatures; his sketches for the Yalta Armenian church's murals — "Intercession", the Holy Mother of God — and the brightest image of the Armenian Mother of God, rich in Armenian ornaments.

**Armenian medieval miniature painting (Հայկական միջնադարյան մանրանկարչություն).** Armenian medieval miniature painting (Հայկական միջնադարյան մանրանկարչություն) — the painted manuscript art of medieval Armenia.

That is Armenian Miniature Painting — Armenia's way, kept by name.
