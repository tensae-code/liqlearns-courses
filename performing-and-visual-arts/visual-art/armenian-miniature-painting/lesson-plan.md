# Lesson plan — Armenian Miniature Painting

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Visual Art · **Lesson:** Armenian Miniature Painting
**Place in the curriculum:** Visual Art (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Armenian Miniature Painting — Armenia's own teaching of this topic, kept as named parts: Toros Roslin named among the masters — the great miniature painter stands in the grade-5 gallery beside Sargis Baghdasaryan's "Davit Bek" sculpture
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the course's earlier lessons in its title; recommended first, never forced.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of ..." in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Toros Roslin named among the masters** — Toros Roslin named among the masters — the great miniature painter stands in the grade-5 gallery beside Sargis Baghdasaryan's "Davit Bek" sculpture
2. **Armenian manuscripts and miniature schools** — Armenian manuscripts and miniature schools — the "Msho Charentir" of 660 leaves, each from one calf's skin; scribes copied the text, painters the pictures; the Etchmiadzin Gospel and Hetum II's Lectionary; Sargis Pitsak's Gospel (1336) and Toros Roslin's Malatia Gospel (1268) in the Matenadaran
3. **Surenyants the muralist** — Surenyants the muralist — Vardges Surenyants made many copies of manuscript miniatures; his sketches for the Yalta Armenian church's murals — "Intercession", the Holy Mother of God — and the brightest image of the Armenian Mother of God, rich in Armenian ornaments
4. **Armenian medieval miniature painting (Հայկական միջնադարյան մանրանկարչություն)** — Armenian medieval miniature painting (Հայկական միջնադարյան մանրանկարչություն) — the painted manuscript art of medieval Armenia
