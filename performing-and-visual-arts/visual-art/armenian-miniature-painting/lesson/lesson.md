# Armenian Miniature Painting

## Do it first

Name one thing that belongs in this lesson — Armenian Miniature Painting — Armenia's own teaching of this topic, kept as named parts: Toros Roslin named among the masters — the great miniature painter stands in the grade-5 gallery beside Sargis Baghdasaryan's "Davit Bek" sculpture. Say what it is in your own words. If you can name one, you are already inside the lesson.

## Sub lesson 1 — Toros Roslin named among the masters

Toros Roslin named among the masters — the great miniature painter stands in the grade-5 gallery beside Sargis Baghdasaryan's "Davit Bek" sculpture.

## Sub lesson 2 — Armenian manuscripts and miniature schools

Armenian manuscripts and miniature schools — the "Msho Charentir" of 660 leaves, each from one calf's skin; scribes copied the text, painters the pictures; the Etchmiadzin Gospel and Hetum II's Lectionary; Sargis Pitsak's Gospel (1336) and Toros Roslin's Malatia Gospel (1268) in the Matenadaran.

## Sub lesson 3 — Surenyants the muralist

Surenyants the muralist — Vardges Surenyants made many copies of manuscript miniatures; his sketches for the Yalta Armenian church's murals — "Intercession", the Holy Mother of God — and the brightest image of the Armenian Mother of God, rich in Armenian ornaments.

## Sub lesson 4 — Armenian medieval miniature painting (Հայկական միջնադարյան մանրանկարչություն)

Armenian medieval miniature painting (Հայկական միջնադարյան մանրանկարչություն) — the painted manuscript art of medieval Armenia.
