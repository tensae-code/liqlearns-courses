# Video script — Papua New Guinea Carving and Storyboards

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

**Carved storyboards.** The Grade 3 guide's craft background: carved storyboards depict everyday life and legends — a story carried in wood. Environmental print belongs to the same unit: the printed and carved signs of the learner's own place.

**Soft carving and miniature caves.** Soft carving and foam carving come first, then miniature caves modelled in soap, foam and clay — small worlds built by cutting and shaping soft materials.

**Carving and modelling, safely.** The Grade 4 guide teaches safety when handling carving tools, what can be carved, and the process of making a carving; then clay models, and models built from wood, sticks, shells, grass chunks, soap and useful materials found in the environment.
