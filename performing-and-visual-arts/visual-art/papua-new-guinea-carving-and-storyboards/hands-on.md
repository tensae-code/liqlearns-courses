# Hands-on — Papua New Guinea Carving and Storyboards

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Carved storyboards
Do the practice this part names, with real materials and a partner: The Grade 3 guide's craft background: carved storyboards depict everyday life and legends — a story carried in wood. Environmental print belongs to the same unit: the printed and carved signs of the learner's own place.

## Soft carving and miniature caves
Do the practice this part names, with real materials and a partner: Soft carving and foam carving come first, then miniature caves modelled in soap, foam and clay — small worlds built by cutting and shaping soft materials.

## Carving and modelling, safely
Do the practice this part names, with real materials and a partner: The Grade 4 guide teaches safety when handling carving tools, what can be carved, and the process of making a carving; then clay models, and models built from wood, sticks, shells, grass chunks, soap and useful materials found in the environment.
