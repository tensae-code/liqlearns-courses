# Papua New Guinea Carving and Storyboards — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Storyboards in PNG carry stories in wood.
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The guides say any material at all can be carved the same way.
**Answer: False — the guide teaches what can be carved, material by material.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Clay models are part of the Grade 4 modelling lessons.
**Answer: True.**
