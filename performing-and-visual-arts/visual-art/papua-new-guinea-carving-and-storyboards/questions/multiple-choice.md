# Papua New Guinea Carving and Storyboards — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A Papua New Guinea storyboard carving depicts…
a) everyday life and legends  b) only numbers
**Answer: a) everyday life and legends.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Soft carving practice in the guide uses…
a) foam, soap and clay  b) steel plate
**Answer: a) foam, soap and clay.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Before carving, the Grade 4 guide teaches…
a) safety when handling carving tools  b) speed above all
**Answer: a) safety when handling carving tools.**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Models in the guide are built from…
a) wood, sticks, shells, grass, soap and found materials  b) imported kits only
**Answer: a) wood, sticks, shells, grass, soap and found materials.**
