# Papua New Guinea Carving and Storyboards — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What two subjects do carved storyboards depict?
**Answer: Everyday life and legends.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name two soft materials for miniature caves.
**Answer: Soap, foam and clay (any two).**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What must be learned before handling carving tools?
**Answer: Safety — how to handle the tools safely.**
