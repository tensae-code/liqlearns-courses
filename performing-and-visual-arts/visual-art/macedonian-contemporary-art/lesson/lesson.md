# Macedonian Contemporary Art

## Do it first

Name one thing that belongs in this lesson — contemporary art in North Macedonia since independence in 1991 — its artists and its many styles. Say what it is in your own words. If you can name one, you are already inside the lesson.

## Sub lesson 1 — Современата уметност во РС Македонија since independence (1991): no common style

Современата уметност во РС Македонија since independence (1991): no common style — installation, video, photography, performance and work in space; the Soros Center for Contemporary Art – Skopje (founded 1993) opening the scene to the world; the themes — identity, memory, body and gender, social and political change, the local against the global; the artists — Janešlieva, Musoviќ, Jankuloski, Moteska, Čalovski and Ivanoska working with archives and text, Toševski, Prlja, the OPA group, Hadži-Vasileva's works of organic waste.

## Do It Yourself

1. Choose one named part of this lesson and find out one more true thing about it — from a person who knows it, or from the book your part came from.
2. Make or perform a small piece in the way of that part: a rhythm, a step, a drawing, a shaped piece of clay.
3. Write one sentence: what does this part show about North Macedonia's own way of teaching its arts?
