# Macedonian Contemporary Art — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson teaches...
a) another country's arts only  b) contemporary art in North Macedonia since independence in 1991 — its artists and its many styles  c) nothing — it is just names
**Answer: b)** — that is the lesson's one thing.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The first sub lesson is...
a) Современата уметност во РС Македонија since independence (1991): no common style  b) an examination  c) a song from another continent
**Answer: a) Современата уметност во РС Македонија since independence (1991): no common style.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** North Macedonia's own forms in this lesson are...
a) dropped and replaced  b) kept as named parts  c) hidden
**Answer: b) kept as named parts.**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** The last sub lesson is...
a) Современата уметност во РС Македонија since independence (1991): no common style  b) the bibliography  c) a review box
**Answer: a) Современата уметност во РС Македонија since independence (1991): no common style.**

**5. (Sub lesson 1)** Songs in this course are placed...
a) with full lyrics copied  b) by title and theme only, never transcribed  c) not at all
