# Lesson plan — Macedonian Contemporary Art

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Visual Art · **Lesson:** Macedonian Contemporary Art
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 2 — Visual Art (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** contemporary art in North Macedonia since independence in 1991 — its artists and its many styles.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the course's earlier lessons in its title; recommended first, never forced.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of ..." in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Современата уметност во РС Македонија since independence (1991): no common style** — Современата уметност во РС Македонија since independence (1991): no common style — installation, video, photography, performance and work in space; the Soros Center for Contemporary Art – Skopje (founded 1993) opening the scene to the world; the themes — identity, memory, body and gender, social and political change, the local against the global; the artists — Janešlieva, Musoviќ, Jankuloski, Moteska, Čalovski and Ivanoska working with archives and text, Toševski, Prlja, the OPA group, Hadži-Vasileva's works of organic waste.

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks North Macedonia's arts are copies of other countries' → they are the country's own: named forms, kept in the country's own books, taught in the country's own way.
