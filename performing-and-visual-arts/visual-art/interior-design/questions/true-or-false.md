# Interior Design — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The purpose of a space does not affect how it is shaped.
**Answer: False** — spaces are shaped according to their purpose.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Light and colour help make a space's ambience.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** An artwork is well placed when it is hidden where nobody approaches it.
**Answer: False** — it belongs on a visible spot that can be approached.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The same artwork can come to expression better in one space than in another.
**Answer: True** — that is what the grounds exercise tests.
