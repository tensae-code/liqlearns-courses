# Interior Design — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A room or classroom is shaped according to…
a) its purpose  b) its price  c) the weather outside
**Answer: a) its purpose**.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** An ambience is made by…
a) the light, the colours and the room between objects  b) the size of the door only  c) the number of windows only
**Answer: a) the light, the colours and the room between objects**.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Indoor artworks need not be made of durable material because…
a) they are sheltered from rain, snow, wind and sun  b) nobody looks at them  c) they are always small
**Answer: a) they are sheltered from rain, snow, wind and sun**.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** An artwork in a closed space should be placed…
a) on a visible spot that can be approached  b) behind the furniture  c) on the floor in a corner
**Answer: a) on a visible spot that can be approached**.
