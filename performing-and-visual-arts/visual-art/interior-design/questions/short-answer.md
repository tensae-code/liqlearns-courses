# Interior Design — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name three things that make a room's ambience.
**Answer: The amount of light, the colours present, and the room left between objects.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the Grade 2 book ask you to draw?
**Answer: The room of your dreams, where you will feel comfortable — and your own room re-designed with a few details and mood-lifting colours.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Why can an indoor sculpture be made of a material that is not durable?
**Answer: Because indoors it is sheltered from rain, snow, wind and sun.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In the Grade 3 exercise, over which three grounds do you move the sculpture to find its best place?
**Answer: The gallery, the classroom and the office grounds.**
