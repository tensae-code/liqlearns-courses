# Video script — Interior Design

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Look at the room you are in.

It was shaped — for living, for learning, for work, for rest.

Its light, its colours, the room between its things — that is its ambience.

An artwork indoors does not fight the rain and the wind.

But it must stand where eyes can reach it.

Place it well — and the room begins to speak.
