Заманауи интерьердегі сәндік бұйымдар (Decorative items in the contemporary interior) — what it teaches: интерьер (interior) is a dwelling's inner side, which provides the dwelling's being beautiful and comfortable — not only furniture and processing materials, but a definite atmosphere, a mood-forming tool; стиль (style) is the unity of artistic methods and tools showing a definite direction in creativity — art's main category, the unity of content and form and depiction methods, recognising a whole epoch's creative idea through one person's thought; an interior is built in a definite style, or several styles can be held together at one time; interior styles divide into three main groups — этникалық (ethnic), тарихи (historical) and заманауи (contemporary); the pictured interiors: Mediterranean peoples' style, Лофт (loft), Классицизм (classicism), Псевдоафрикандық стиль (pseudo-African), Барокко (baroque), Поп-арт (pop-art), Хай-тек (high-tech) and ethnic style; contemporary interior styles come simple, with little decoration — кантри (country), минимализм (minimalism), модернизм (modernism), поп-арт, экостиль (eco-style), хай-тек, авангард (avant-garde), лофт — their common signs are straight lines' abundance, lattice colours' mutual harmony and furniture's exact form; decorative-applied art items connect the interior's separate parts with each other, fill empty places, give the room a definite character, create its own air and define the style direction — the decorative items used are dishes, furniture, woven or sculptural items, lighting devices and flower vases; when choosing a decorative item the interior's style must be reckoned, the things used for beautifying must harmonise with the room's general style and not narrow the space too much (student book; original target `visual-art/interior-design-styles`) | Book 5, printed p. 8 |
| Kazakhstan — § 19. Интерьердегі жарықтандыру. Табиғи және жасанды жарықтандыру (Lighting in the interior. Natural and artificial lighting) — Табиғи жарықтандыру (Естественное освещение / Daylight) — what it teaches: light fills the interior, enters its beauty, and also creates a condition for a person to feel themselves comfortable — rightly designed and suitably chosen light increases a person's work ability and does not negatively affect health; by the light source's fall, lighting kinds are Табиғи (natural), Жасанды (artificial) and Аралас (mixed); by quality, lighting's next kinds exist: бағытталған (directed — light rays directed at an exact object or plane), шағылысқан (reflected — light rays reflected from varied planes) and шашыраған (diffused — light rays divided uniformly over the dwelling's whole territory perimeter); artificial light, by its service, divides into four: Жалпы жарық (general light) is the universal kind uniformly dropping light to the surroundings; Нысанды (көмескі) жарық (object (auxiliary) light) is directed at the interior's separate parts, singling out a definite zone in the space; Жұмысқа ыңғайлы жарық (work-comfortable light) is directed at a definite zone for carrying out exactly-placed tasks needing weight dropped on the eye (for example, for carrying out a task given at home, drawing a picture and the like); Сәндік жарық (decorative light) is used only for decorating and beautifying; in a contemporary interior several kinds of light can be used together — it is called біріктірілген жарық (combined light) (student book; original target `visual-art/architectural-lighting`) | Book 5, printed p. 70 |
| Kazakhstan — § 20. Интерьерді жарықтандырудың жобасын даярлау (Preparing the interior's lighting project) — Функционалды аймақ (Функциональные зоны / Functional zones) — what it teaches: by what it is used for, space (кеңістік) divides into тұрғынжайға арналған (intended for dwelling), қоғамдық және өндірістік (public and production): dwelling space must be comfortable and cosy for a person's life — to it belong the тұрғын үй (dwelling house), the dwelling house's room, the пәтер (flat), the flat's room and the room; | Kazakhstan — § 21. Интерьерді аудандарға бөлу және жоспарлау (Dividing the interior into zones and planning) — Аудандарға бөлу (Зонирование / Zoning) — what it teaches: Аудандарға бөлу (zoning) is a decoration method showing a room divided into several zones without harming the space's common stylistics, and Интерьерді жобалау (designing an interior) is dividing any space into functional zones; in interior designing, the room's construction peculiarities and, together with it, what it is intended for must be reckoned, and placing all the parts rightly is important; in zoning, several purposes can be intended: in a wide, voluminous room, for a person to feel themselves comfortable, a row of zones must be delimited — in a small room, for the volume to fit, each inhabitant must have their own space, so the zones must stand mutually divided; space can be divided into zones variously — for it, mostly, additional architectural constructions are used, or recourse is had to the help of furniture and decorative қалқалар (screens/partitions); when dividing into zones, the inhabitants' everyday life and demand must be reckoned and the functional zones exactly taken, and when divided, attention must be paid to the space's being conveniently used and the interior's being harmonious and comfortable; the pictured zoning: dividing a room into zones through lighting (Жарықтандыру арқылы бөлмені аудандарға бөлу), dividing a room with furniture's help (Бөлмені жиһаздың көмегімен аудандарға бөлу) and dividing a room through decorative partitions (Бөлмені сәндік қалқалар арқылы аудандарға бөлу), with sketches of zoning projects (Аудандарға бөлу жобаларының нобайлары) (student book; original target `visual-art/zoning-an-interior`) | Book 5, printed p. 74 |
