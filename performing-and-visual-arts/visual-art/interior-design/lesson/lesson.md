# Interior Design

Quick start: say in one sentence what you think this lesson is about — the spaces we live, learn, work and rest in are shaped according to their purpose. Then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — Rooms shaped for their purpose

The spaces where people **live, learn, work and rest** are **shaped according to their purpose**, and everything found in a space makes its **ambience**. What makes an ambience pleasant? **The amount of light, the colours present, and the room left between objects.** Look at an **old classroom against a classroom of today** — the same purpose, shaped differently. The Grade 2 book sets the work: **draw the room of your dreams** where you will feel comfortable, and **re-design your own room** with a few details and mood-lifting colours.

## Sub lesson 2 — Artworks in closed spaces

In **closed spaces** we live, work and socialise **surrounded by artworks** — drawings, paintings, sculptures. Indoor works **need not be of durable material**, since they are sheltered from rain, snow, wind and sun. But it is very important that an artwork be **well placed in the space — on a visible spot that can be approached** — so that it is well seen, as in homes, classrooms, offices and concert halls. The Grade 3 exercise: move a sculpture over the **gallery, classroom and office** grounds until you find its **best place**, and arrange painted scenes on the same grounds, noticing in which space the works come to expression best and what influences that.
