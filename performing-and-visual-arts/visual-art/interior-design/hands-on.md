# Hands-on — Interior Design

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Re-design a room
Draw your own room as it is. Then draw it again with a few details changed and mood-lifting colours added. Say what changed the ambience most.

## Find the best place
Draw one artwork. Cut it out and try it in three drawn rooms — a gallery, a classroom, an office. Keep it where it comes to expression best, and say why.
