# Hands-on — Traditional Geometric Motifs

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Do the sub lessons
- **Traditional design L1, Geometric motifs in grids** — do the book's own task for this part: Traditional geometric motifs — drawing geometric motifs inside square grids, the foundation of Iran's traditional design. Show your companion what you made or named.

- **Traditional design L2, The drum-in-drum motif** — do the book's own task for this part: drawing the طبل در طبل (drum-in-drum) motif on a diagonal grid and extending it into new motifs, as used in the tilework of Yazd's Jameh Mosque. Show your companion what you made or named.

- **Traditional design L3, The wave motif** — do the book's own task for this part: drawing the نقش موج (wave motif) across a diagonal-square grid until it covers a whole ground, then transferring and colouring it, as in Isfahan's Chaharbagh tilework. Show your companion what you made or named.

- **Decorative motifs L1, Chaharlangeh** — do the book's own task for this part: چهارلنگه: drawing the four-part chaharlangeh motif of Iran's decorative design. Show your companion what you made or named.

- **Decorative motifs L2, The eight-knot and chaharlangeh** — do the book's own task for this part: گره هشت و چهارلنگه: constructing the eight-knot (gereh) geometry with the chaharlangeh. Show your companion what you made or named.

- **Traditional design L1, Traditional design** — do the book's own task for this part: طراحی سنتی: what traditional design is — nature's forms simplified into motifs, and the family of traditional arts those designs serve. Show your companion what you made or named.

- **Traditional design L2, Geometric motifs** — do the book's own task for this part: آشنایی با نقش‌های هندسی: getting to know the geometric motifs of Iran's traditional arts, drawn with geometric shapes and lines on squared paper. Show your companion what you made or named.

- **Traditional design L3, Drawing the gereh** — do the book's own task for this part: طراحی گره: drawing the gereh (knot) — a square and a diamond set on squared paper, built into the knot patterns of traditional design. Show your companion what you made or named.
