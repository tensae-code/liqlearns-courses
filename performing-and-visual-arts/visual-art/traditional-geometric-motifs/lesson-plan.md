# Lesson plan — Traditional Geometric Motifs

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Visual Art · **Lesson:** Traditional Geometric Motifs
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 2 — Visual Art (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Iran's traditional geometric motifs — nature's forms simplified into motifs, drawn in grids and gereh knot geometry, and extended until they cover a whole ground, as in the tilework of Yazd and Isfahan.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the learner's earlier art lessons. Recommended, never forced.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Traditional design L1, Geometric motifs in grids** — Traditional geometric motifs — drawing geometric motifs inside square grids, the foundation of Iran's traditional design.
2. **Traditional design L2, The drum-in-drum motif** — drawing the طبل در طبل (drum-in-drum) motif on a diagonal grid and extending it into new motifs, as used in the tilework of Yazd's Jameh Mosque.
3. **Traditional design L3, The wave motif** — drawing the نقش موج (wave motif) across a diagonal-square grid until it covers a whole ground, then transferring and colouring it, as in Isfahan's Chaharbagh tilework.
4. **Decorative motifs L1, Chaharlangeh** — چهارلنگه: drawing the four-part chaharlangeh motif of Iran's decorative design.
5. **Decorative motifs L2, The eight-knot and chaharlangeh** — گره هشت و چهارلنگه: constructing the eight-knot (gereh) geometry with the chaharlangeh.
6. **Traditional design L1, Traditional design** — طراحی سنتی: what traditional design is — nature's forms simplified into motifs, and the family of traditional arts those designs serve.
7. **Traditional design L2, Geometric motifs** — آشنایی با نقش‌های هندسی: getting to know the geometric motifs of Iran's traditional arts, drawn with geometric shapes and lines on squared paper.
8. **Traditional design L3, Drawing the gereh** — طراحی گره: drawing the gereh (knot) — a square and a diamond set on squared paper, built into the knot patterns of traditional design.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
