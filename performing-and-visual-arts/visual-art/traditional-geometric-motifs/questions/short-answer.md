# Traditional Geometric Motifs — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1.** What paper helps you draw geometric motifs true?
**Answer: Squared (grid) paper.**

**2.** Name the motif drawn on a diagonal grid and extended into new motifs.
**Answer: The drum-in-drum (طبل در طبل) motif.**

**3.** What is the four-part motif of Iran's decorative design called?
**Answer: Chaharlangeh (چهارلنگه).**
