# Traditional Geometric Motifs — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Traditional design begins by…
a) simplifying nature's forms into motifs  b) copying a photograph exactly  c) writing letters
**Answer: a) simplifying nature's forms into motifs**

**2.** A gereh is…
a) a knot pattern built from a square and a diamond on squared paper  b) a kind of paint  c) a song form
**Answer: a) a knot pattern built from a square and a diamond on squared paper**

**3.** The drum-in-drum motif is drawn on…
a) a diagonal grid  b) a blank page with no guide  c) a circle only
**Answer: a) a diagonal grid**

**4.** The wave motif is finished when it…
a) covers a whole ground, then is transferred and coloured  b) stays a single wave  c) is erased
**Answer: a) covers a whole ground, then is transferred and coloured**
