# Video script — Traditional Geometric Motifs

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today we learn **Traditional Geometric Motifs**.

Step 1 — **Traditional design L1, Geometric motifs in grids**. Traditional geometric motifs — drawing geometric motifs inside square grids, the foundation of Iran's traditional design.

Step 2 — **Traditional design L2, The drum-in-drum motif**. drawing the طبل در طبل (drum-in-drum) motif on a diagonal grid and extending it into new motifs, as used in the tilework of Yazd's Jameh Mosque.

Step 3 — **Traditional design L3, The wave motif**. drawing the نقش موج (wave motif) across a diagonal-square grid until it covers a whole ground, then transferring and colouring it, as in Isfahan's Chaharbagh tilework.

Step 4 — **Decorative motifs L1, Chaharlangeh**. چهارلنگه: drawing the four-part chaharlangeh motif of Iran's decorative design.

Step 5 — **Decorative motifs L2, The eight-knot and chaharlangeh**. گره هشت و چهارلنگه: constructing the eight-knot (gereh) geometry with the chaharlangeh.

Step 6 — **Traditional design L1, Traditional design**. طراحی سنتی: what traditional design is — nature's forms simplified into motifs, and the family of traditional arts those designs serve.

Step 7 — **Traditional design L2, Geometric motifs**. آشنایی با نقش‌های هندسی: getting to know the geometric motifs of Iran's traditional arts, drawn with geometric shapes and lines on squared paper.

Step 8 — **Traditional design L3, Drawing the gereh**. طراحی گره: drawing the gereh (knot) — a square and a diamond set on squared paper, built into the knot patterns of traditional design.

That is Traditional Geometric Motifs — Iran's own way, step by step.
