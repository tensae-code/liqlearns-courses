# Hands-on — Paints and How They Work

The lesson with your hands, your feet and your voice together, not only your eyes.

## Thin and thick
With one colour, paint one stripe thinned with much water and one stripe thick. Watch which dries first and how each covers the paper.

## Rubbing tone
With a crayon or a stick of charcoal, rub a strip from light to dark by pressing harder and layering — the rubbing paints' own technique.
