# Video script — Paints and How They Work

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Every paint has its own thinner and its own speed.
Watercolour flows with water, light first, dark after.
Oil is slow — days to dry — and thick as butter.
Acrylic is fast. Gouache covers flat. Tempera is ancient.
Crayon, pastel, charcoal: sticks that rub colour down.
