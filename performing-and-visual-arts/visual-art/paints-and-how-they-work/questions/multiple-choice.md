# Paints and How They Work — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Watercolour is worked…
  a) From light to shade, thinly b) Only from dark to light, thickly c) With no water at all
**Answer: a) From light to shade, thinly** — watercolour is thinned with water and worked light to shade.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which paint dries very fast?
  a) Acrylic b) Oil c) Tempera
**Answer: a) Acrylic** — the Grade 6 book calls acrylic very fast-drying; oil does not dry quickly.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** How long can oil paint take to dry?
  a) 3–4 days, sometimes a week b) One minute c) It never dries at all
**Answer: a) 3–4 days, sometimes a week** — oil dries in 3–4 days, sometimes a week.

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Which people first used tempera?
  a) The ancient Egyptians b) The ancient Greeks c) Modern factories
**Answer: a) The ancient Egyptians** — tempera was first used by the ancient Egyptians.
