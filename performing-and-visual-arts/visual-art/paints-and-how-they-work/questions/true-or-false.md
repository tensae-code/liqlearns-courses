# Paints and How They Work — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Oil colour can be worked thick or thin.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Pastel is paint in stick form.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The watercolour technique of painting light to dark slowly is called flow.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Crayon, pastel and charcoal are all applied by rubbing.
**Answer: True**
