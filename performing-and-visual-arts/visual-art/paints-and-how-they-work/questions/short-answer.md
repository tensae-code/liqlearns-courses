# Paints and How They Work — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What kind of brush does watercolour want?
**Answer: Soft brushes that can hold water.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is a palette for?
**Answer: It is the plate for mixing colours.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Acrylic can be used on which surfaces?
**Answer: Card, wood, metal and ceramics.**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Charcoal was first recorded in painting use where, and when?
**Answer: In England, in 1606 AD.**

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** Name three Chapter 2 aims kept by lessons other than this one.
**Answer:** Any three of: reading music; performing traditional dance; creating theatre/film ideas; singing in Bati and Tizta qenet.

**6. (Sub lesson 6)** The exercise's two fill-ins are about which two things in a work?
**Answer:** Characters and theme.
