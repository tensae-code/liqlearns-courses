# Video script — Impressionism

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Light changes colour — look, and look again!

**Impressionism** paints that moment, quickly, outdoors. Monet's *Impression, Sunrise* named it.

Now lay the paint thick — dab, dab! That is **impasto**.

Dab an ocean wave, and let the dabs be the water.
