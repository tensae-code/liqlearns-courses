# Impressionism

## Do it first

Quick game: look out of the window, then look again a minute later. The light has already changed the colours. Catching that change is this lesson.

## Sub lesson 1 — Impressionism — light and the moment

**Impressionism** observes **the changing effect of light on colour** and **paints quickly outdoors to capture a moment**. The movement took its name from **Monet's *Impression, Sunrise***.

## Sub lesson 2 — Impasto — thick dabs of paint

**Impasto** lays paint in **thick dabs** — with a **brush**, a **painting knife**, or **straight from the tube**. The book paints **an ocean wave** in this style: thick dabs for the moving water of the Maldives' own sea.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson taught, part by part. Companion checks each part against the sub lessons above.

## Do It Yourself

1. Make your own piece using this lesson's way, from start to finish.
2. Show it to the class and say, in the book's own closing words, talk about your art: what you made, how, and what you learnt.
3. Write one sentence: what will you do differently next time?
