# Impressionism — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Impressionists painted quickly outdoors.
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Impressionism ignores light.
**Answer: False — it observes the changing effect of light on colour.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Impasto paint is laid thin and smooth.
**Answer: False — it is laid in thick dabs.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A painting knife can be used for impasto.
**Answer: True.**
