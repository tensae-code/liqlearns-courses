# Impressionism — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Impressionism observes…
a) only indoor still life  b) the changing effect of light on colour  c) black outlines
**Answer: b) the changing effect of light on colour**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Impressionist painters painted…
a) slowly, only from memory  b) quickly outdoors to capture a moment  c) only at night
**Answer: b) quickly outdoors to capture a moment**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The movement was named from Monet's…
a) Water Lilies  b) Impression, Sunrise  c) Sunflowers
**Answer: b) Impression, Sunrise**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Impasto means laying paint…
a) very thin  b) in thick dabs  c) under the paper
**Answer: b) in thick dabs**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Impasto can be laid with…
a) a brush, a painting knife, or straight from the tube  b) only fingers  c) a ruler
**Answer: a) a brush, a painting knife, or straight from the tube**
