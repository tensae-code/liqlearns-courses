## Sub lesson 1 — Kazakhstan Grade 9 NIS Art (Akhmetkalieva)

what it teaches: in the same methods table: Стринг-арт (string art) — the art of drawing pictures with thread and nails (шегелер мен жіптерді қолданып картиналар салу өнері); one side of it is that in the work not a pre-drawn picture but... (бұл әдістің ерекшелігі — табиғи материалдың (ағаш, ұсақ тас, құм, қағаз, картон, тері, мата) бетіне қолданбалы өнер мен көркем графикада қолданылатын негізгі... ) — string art is counted a graphic art kind; what it teaches: to use the ideas, familiar methods can be used, new methods can also be used; 1-нұсқа — Стринг-арт (String Art) method: define the picture's density and take a thick cardboard, foam plastic, foam board (пеноплекс) as the base; put the sketch on the prepared base's face; mark the nail places 5–7 mm apart; drive the nails into the marked places; take the paper templates off the nails; the paper's leftovers must be cleaned; make a knot tying the thread's end at the top, wind the second end criss-cross (қиғаштап) tying to each nail; so a line is made; and wind the thread round each nail once each time the direction changes; after all the nails are wound with thread, tie the thread's end in a knot and cut it carefully (the pictured string-art geometric schemes and the making photos)
