# Lesson plan — String Art

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Visual Art · **Lesson:** String Art
**Place in the curriculum:** Visual Art (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** String art — winding thread between pins to build geometric pictures and patterns..
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this course in the same title.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Kazakhstan Grade 9 NIS Art (Akhmetkalieva)** — what it teaches: in the same methods table: Стринг-арт (string art) — the art of drawing pictures with thread and nails (шегелер мен жіптерді қолданып картиналар салу өнері); one side of it is that in the work not a pre-drawn picture but... (бұл әдістің ерекшелігі — табиғи материалдың (ағаш, ұсақ тас, құм, қағаз, картон, тері, мата) бетіне қолданбалы өнер мен көркем графикада қолданылатын негізгі... ) — string art is counted a graphic art kind; what it teaches: to use the ideas, familiar methods can be used, new methods can also be used; 1-нұсқа — Стринг-арт (String Art) method: define the picture's density and take a thick cardboard, foam plastic, foam board (пеноплекс) as the base; put the sketch on the prepared base's face; mark the nail places 5–7 mm apart; drive the nails into the marked places; take the paper templates off the nails; the paper's leftovers must be cleaned; make a knot tying the thread's end at the top, wind the second end criss-cross (қиғаштап) tying to each nail; so a line is made; and wind the thread round each nail once each time the direction changes; after all the nails are wound with thread, tie the thread's end in a knot and cut it carefully (the pictured string-art geometric schemes and the making photos)
