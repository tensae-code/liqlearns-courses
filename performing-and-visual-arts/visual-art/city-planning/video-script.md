This lesson is City Planning.

City Planning — planning a city — its functional zones, streets and districts, and how a planned city is drawn and modelled. — as Kazakhstan's school books teach it: what it teaches: people began building big settlements with a definite plan already at the beginning of the second millennium BC — Greece's, Rome's and Egypt's ancient cities' plans come down to today as proof; simple water channels and aqueducts were first made in Ancient Rome; at that time, when building a city, cities began to be divided into districts according to people's life state and profession; қаланы жоспарлау (city planning) is pre-determining all the constructions built inside a city, defining what purpose each will be used for, and placing them in mutual harmony in a definite order; in planning, all the difficulties that may arise in the future are thoroughly reckoned, and thoughtful decisions are sought; because the first plan influences the city's later life mandatorily; the main қалақұрушы факторлар (city-forming factors) are natural (жер бедері — the land relief), demographic (including the population number) and industrial factors; the pictured city plan schemes — 1-сурет: radial and ring (радиалды және шеңберлі), 2-сурет: systematic or chessboard (жүйелі немесе тақталы), 3-сурет: systematic or free (жүйелі немесе еркін) — and the coloured plans of Stockholm, Moscow and Nur-Sultan; if a city is planned rightly from the very beginning, it is convenient for the city inhabitants' everyday life-work and for guests coming to the city to go and come; at city planning's first stage the city's functional zones' (функционалдық аймақтарының) scheme is made, and in accordance with it their places are defined: each district is used for a definite purpose — for example, the қоныстану аймағы (settlement zone) is for dwelling houses and public places, the ландшафтық-демалыс аймағы (landscape-recreation zone) is for parks, squares and boulevards (саябақтар мен гүлзарлар); and the industrial zone is for energy-producing places and production places; to preserve the city from pollution the industrial zone is placed at the wind's lower side, rather far from the settlement zone, and divided from it by a belt (ағаш, орман, тоғай — trees, forest, grove); when dividing a city into districts, first the district's place and volume are marked, at the next stage the streets and roads, public places and other constructions begin to be built; the бас жоспар (master plan) — every settlement's plan, after at least 20 years, how it changed and developed is reckoned and a private plan is made; it is called the бас жоспар (master plan), that is, the city construction's most main document; the master plan's top part shows, at a definite scale on paper, the settlement's or construction buildings' existing and newly-planned buildings' view from above; such a plan can be made for a private district or a small district, and also for a whole city district or the whole city; in necessary cases changes can be entered into the master plan; Nur-Sultan's 2017 population table is given (972,692 at the year start; 1,026,181 by 1 November 2017).

Watch, listen — then say it back in your own words.
