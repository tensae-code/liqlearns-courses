# Video script — Latin American Art

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

**El arte del siglo XX en América Latina.** Latin American art between the local and the universal — Mexican muralism painting the struggles of peasants, indigenous and mestizos on public walls (Rivera, Siqueiros, Orozco); Brazilian modernismo centred on the autochthonous (Portinari, Tarsila, Malfatti); Torres García's inverted America — "our north is the south"; Figari, Peláez, Lam, Kahlo, Varo.

**Arte contemporáneo en América Latina.** Contemporary Latin American art — fusions of the traditional, modern, postmodern, popular and cultured; since the 1980s–90s the will to keep an own identity, art reaching every social group and often denouncing dictatorship (Guayasamín's Pinochet, the Mothers of Plaza de Mayo) (slug proposed above).

That is Latin American Art — Venezuela's own books teach it this way.
