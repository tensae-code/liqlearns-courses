# Latin American Art

## Sub lesson 1 — El arte del siglo XX en América Latina

Latin American art between the local and the universal — Mexican muralism painting the struggles of peasants, indigenous and mestizos on public walls (Rivera, Siqueiros, Orozco); Brazilian modernismo centred on the autochthonous (Portinari, Tarsila, Malfatti); Torres García's inverted America — "our north is the south"; Figari, Peláez, Lam, Kahlo, Varo.

## Sub lesson 2 — Arte contemporáneo en América Latina

Contemporary Latin American art — fusions of the traditional, modern, postmodern, popular and cultured; since the 1980s–90s the will to keep an own identity, art reaching every social group and often denouncing dictatorship (Guayasamín's Pinochet, the Mothers of Plaza de Mayo) (slug proposed above).
