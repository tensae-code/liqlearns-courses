# Hands-on — Latin American Art

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## El arte del siglo XX en América Latina
Work through this part with real materials or your own body, the way the book sets it: Latin American art between the local and the universal — Mexican muralism painting the struggles of peasants, indigenous and mestizos on public walls (Rivera, Siqueiros, Orozco); Brazilian modernismo centred on the autochthonous (Portinari, Tarsila, Malfatti); Torres García's inverted America — "our north is the south"; Figari, Peláez, Lam, Kahlo, Varo. Show a companion what you made or did, and say what the part teaches.

## Arte contemporáneo en América Latina
Work through this part with real materials or your own body, the way the book sets it: contemporary Latin American art — fusions of the traditional, modern, postmodern, popular and cultured; since the 1980s–90s the will to keep an own identity, art reaching every social group and often denouncing dictatorship (Guayasamín's Pinochet, the Mothers of Plaza de Mayo) (slug proposed above). Show a companion what you made or did, and say what the part teaches.
