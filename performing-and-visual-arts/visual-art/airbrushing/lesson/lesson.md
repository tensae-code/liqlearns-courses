# Airbrushing

## Sub lesson 1 — The airbrush and its tools

Airbrushing **sprays pigment with air** — its line runs back to pigment sprayed on cave walls. The tools are **single-action and double-action airbrushes**, their **nozzle and needles**, and an **air source delivering about 100 PSI**.

## Sub lesson 2 — Preparing, testing and cleaning

Paint is prepared to an **inky consistency** and chosen for the surface — **flexible for textiles, harder for metal**. Work is **tested on scratch paper at about 20 cm**, atomisation controlled by pressure, paint and needle (**15–40 PSI for precision**). The brush is **cleaned so colours do not mix**, and washed at once after work.

## Sub lesson 3 — Painting with the airbrush, step by step

**Sketch lightly**, paint **one colour at a time from light to dark**, **mask with frisket or tape** for sharp edges, **finish details with a fine needle**, and **seal with fixative** in horizontal then vertical coats.
