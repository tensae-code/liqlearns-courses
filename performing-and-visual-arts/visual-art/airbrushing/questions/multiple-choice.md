# Airbrushing — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** An airbrush sprays pigment with…
a) air  b) water only  c) sand
**Answer: a) air.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The two airbrush kinds are…
a) single-action and double-action  b) wet and dry  c) big and small only
**Answer: a) single-action and double-action.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Paint for airbrushing is prepared to…
a) an inky consistency  b) a thick paste  c) dry powder
**Answer: a) an inky consistency.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Testing is done on scratch paper at about…
a) 20 cm  b) 2 m  c) 1 cm
**Answer: a) 20 cm.**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Colours are painted…
a) one at a time, light to dark  b) all at once  c) dark to light only
**Answer: a) one at a time, from light to dark.**
