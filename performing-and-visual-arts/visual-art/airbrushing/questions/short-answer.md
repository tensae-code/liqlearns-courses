# Airbrushing — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What delivers the air?
**Answer: An air source delivering about 100 PSI.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why is the airbrush cleaned and washed at once?
**Answer: So colours do not mix (and the brush stays working).**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** How is the finished work sealed?
**Answer: With fixative, in horizontal then vertical coats.**
