# Airbrushing — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Masking with frisket or tape gives sharp edges.
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Textiles need a harder paint than metal.
**Answer: False — flexible paint for textiles, harder for metal.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Details are finished with a fine needle.
**Answer: True.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Precision work uses about 15–40 PSI.
**Answer: True.**
