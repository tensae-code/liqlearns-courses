# Hands-on — Papua New Guinea Painting

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Traditional painting in PNG societies
Do the practice this part names, with real materials and a partner: The Grade 8 guide studies traditional paintings in Papua New Guinea societies and their styles: describing the types, and collecting photographic samples of them.

## Contemporary painting, and telling the two apart
Do the practice this part names, with real materials and a partner: Contemporary painting in PNG and the world is set against the traditional: analysing colour mode and composition, and distinguishing a traditional painting from a contemporary one with reasons.
