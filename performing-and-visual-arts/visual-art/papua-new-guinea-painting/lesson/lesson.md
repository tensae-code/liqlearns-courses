# Papua New Guinea Painting

## Do it first

Look at the sub lesson list. Pick the part you already know something about, and say what you know. Then start at Sub lesson 1 — every part builds on the one before it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Traditional painting in PNG societies

The Grade 8 guide studies traditional paintings in Papua New Guinea societies and their styles: describing the types, and collecting photographic samples of them.

## Sub lesson 2 — Contemporary painting, and telling the two apart

Contemporary painting in PNG and the world is set against the traditional: analysing colour mode and composition, and distinguishing a traditional painting from a contemporary one with reasons.

## Say it to your companion
