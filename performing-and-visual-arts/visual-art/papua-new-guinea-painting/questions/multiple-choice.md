# Papua New Guinea Painting — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The guide compares traditional painting with…
a) contemporary painting in PNG and the world  b) no painting at all
**Answer: a) contemporary painting in PNG and the world.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A traditional painting is told from a contemporary one by…
a) its type, colour mode and composition  b) its price
**Answer: a) its type, colour mode and composition.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Learners collect…
a) photographic samples of traditional paintings  b) stamps
**Answer: a) photographic samples of traditional paintings.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Traditional painting is studied in…
a) PNG societies and their styles  b) one city only
**Answer: a) PNG societies and their styles.**
