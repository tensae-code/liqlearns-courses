# Video script — Papua New Guinea Painting

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

**Traditional painting in PNG societies.** The Grade 8 guide studies traditional paintings in Papua New Guinea societies and their styles: describing the types, and collecting photographic samples of them.

**Contemporary painting, and telling the two apart.** Contemporary painting in PNG and the world is set against the traditional: analysing colour mode and composition, and distinguishing a traditional painting from a contemporary one with reasons.
