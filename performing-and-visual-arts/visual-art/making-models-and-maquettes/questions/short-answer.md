# Making Models and Maquettes — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What place does the Bhutan workbook model?
**Answer: The children's own school.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name three materials the model is built from.
**Answer: Clay/mud, cartons, sticks, stones and paper (any three).**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** When is the model painted?
**Answer: When it is dry.**
