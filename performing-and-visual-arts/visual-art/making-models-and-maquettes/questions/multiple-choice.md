# Making Models and Maquettes — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The first step in making the school model is…
a) painting it  b) drawing a map of the school  c) buying materials
**Answer: b) drawing a map of the school**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The model is built…
a) alone  b) in big groups  c) by the teacher only
**Answer: b) in big groups**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Which materials does the workbook name?
a) glass and metal  b) clay/mud, cartons, sticks, stones and paper  c) only plastic
**Answer: b) clay/mud, cartons, sticks, stones and paper**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** The model is painted…
a) before building  b) when it is dry  c) never
**Answer: b) when it is dry**

**5. (Sub lesson 1)** The map serves as…
a) the plan the model follows  b) decoration  c) a test
**Answer: a) the plan the model follows**
