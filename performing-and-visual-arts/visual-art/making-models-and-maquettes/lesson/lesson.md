# Making Models and Maquettes

## Do it first

Quick start: from the title alone, guess what this lesson's work is. Then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — Draw a map of your school

A model is a real place made small. Bhutan's Class III workbook chooses the place the children know best — **their school**. The work starts on paper: **draw a map of your school** first.

## Sub lesson 2 — Build the school model in a big group

Then, **in big groups**, build the model from what is at hand — **clay or mud, cartons, sticks, stones and paper**. When the model is dry, **paint it**. Map, build, paint: that is the order.

## Sub lesson 3 — Paint the model when dry

When the model is dry, **paint it**. Map, build, paint: that is the order.
