# Body Marks Across Cultures

## Do it first

Look at the sub lesson list. Pick the part you already know something about, and say what you know. Then start at Sub lesson 1 — every part builds on the one before it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Piercing and tattoo across 5,000 years and many peoples

Piercing and tattoo across 5,000 years and many peoples — pharaohs, Maori, henna brides, and Tupinambá warriors tattooed as prizes for capturing enemies (the skin-health pages are science cross-content, named here, not placed) (Folha 3, Brazil's *ARTE — Ensino Médio*, printed p. 56).

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion this lesson's one thing in your own words, naming each sub lesson as you go. Companion checks: did every part get named?
