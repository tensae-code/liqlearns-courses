# Moroccan Calligraphy and Tifinagh Script

## Do it first

Write your name as beautifully as you can. Now imagine a whole country with its own way of shaping letters — two of them. That is this lesson.

## Sub lesson 1 — Morocco's own scripts

Inside its calligraphy lesson, Morocco's Grade 4 guide names the country's own lettering arts: the **Moroccan script (الخط المغربي المبسوط)** and the **Amazigh Tifinagh script** — shown in calligraphy panels by named Moroccan calligraphers, **Abderrahim Coulin** and **Hamid El Kharbouchi**.

## Sub lesson 2 — Making a panel

Pupils **write their own name** and **make a panel in Tifinagh** — the country's script art practised with the learner's own name, the first word every calligrapher owns.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the two scripts and one calligrapher who shows them. Companion checks: Moroccan script? Tifinagh? Coulin or El Kharbouchi?

## Do It Yourself

1. Write your name carefully in your best lettering.
2. Copy a short Tifinagh panel letter by letter, keeping each letter's shape true.
3. Write one sentence: why is a name a good first panel?
