# Hands-on — Moroccan Calligraphy and Tifinagh Script

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Your name, two ways
Write your name in your everyday letters, slowly and beautifully. Then copy it — or a short word — into a Tifinagh panel, letter by letter, checking each shape.

## Panel making
Mount your best lettering on card as a panel, the way the calligraphers' panels are shown. Sign it.
