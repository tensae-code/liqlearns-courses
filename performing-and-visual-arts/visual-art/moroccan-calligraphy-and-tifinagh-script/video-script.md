# Video script — Moroccan Calligraphy and Tifinagh Script

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Letters can be pictures!

Morocco writes beautifully — in its own Moroccan script, letters joined and flowing.
And the Amazigh people write in Tifinagh — letters like small signs, old and strong.

Great calligraphers make panels of them.
And you? Start with the best word of all — your name!
