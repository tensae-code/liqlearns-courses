# Moroccan Calligraphy and Tifinagh Script — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name Morocco's two scripts taught in this lesson.
**Answer: The Moroccan script (الخط المغربي المبسوط) and the Amazigh Tifinagh script.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Name one calligrapher whose panels show the scripts.
**Answer: Abderrahim Coulin (or Hamid El Kharbouchi).**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What do pupils make in Tifinagh?
**Answer: A panel of their own name.**
