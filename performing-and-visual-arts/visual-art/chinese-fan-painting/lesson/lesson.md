# Chinese Fan Painting

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about chinese fan painting. Then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — China Grade 6 Art Vol.2

Painting on fans — round fans and folding fans, the literati habit of painting and writing on them (a Song-dynasty fan of autumn sunflowers; modern fans by Tang Yun, Guan Shanyue, Huang Zhou, Lai Shaoqi); folding your own paper fan and painting landscape or flower-and-bird fitted to its shape

## Sub lesson 2 — China HS Crafts

Making a folding fan — the ribs counted in dang (9 to 40; 16 dang, about 30 cm, opening near 140°, is the favourite); wire the ribs, fold the 2:1 Xuan face by the diagonal method, paint the face and write on its back, glue ribs to face, hang the pendant — the fan's name card
