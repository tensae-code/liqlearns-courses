This lesson is Chinese Fan Painting.

Painting on fans — Chinese fan painting and the folding-fan craft. — as China's school books teach it: Painting on fans — round fans and folding fans, the literati habit of painting and writing on them (a Song-dynasty fan of autumn sunflowers; modern fans by Tang Yun, Guan Shanyue, Huang Zhou, Lai Shaoqi); folding your own paper fan and painting landscape or flower-and-bird fitted to its shape.

Watch, listen — then say it back in your own words.
