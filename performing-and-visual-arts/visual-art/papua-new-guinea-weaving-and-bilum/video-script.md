# Video script — Papua New Guinea Weaving and Bilum

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

**Weaving a mat from coconut fronds.** The Grade 3 guide begins with what is made locally — the fibre and fabric crafts of the learner's own community, their twines and materials — then weaves a simple mat from coconut fronds, step by step.

**Preparing the fibre.** The Grade 8 guide gives the full preparation: gathering suitable fibres, dyeing and colouring all fibres ready for weaving, and sun-drying them before the weaving starts.

**Weaving bags, bilums, baskets, belts and mats.** The prepared fibre is woven into the products of daily life: bags, bilums — Papua New Guinea's string bags — baskets, belts and mats.
