# Papua New Guinea Weaving and Bilum — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What three steps prepare fibre for weaving?
**Answer: Gathering suitable fibres, dyeing and colouring them, and sun-drying them.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the Grade 3 guide ask first about local crafts?
**Answer: What is made locally — the fibre and fabric crafts, twines and materials of the community.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name four woven products from the Grade 8 guide.
**Answer: Bags, bilums, baskets, belts and mats (any four).**
