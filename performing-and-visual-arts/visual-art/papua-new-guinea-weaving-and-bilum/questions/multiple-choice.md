# Papua New Guinea Weaving and Bilum — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Grade 3 guide weaves a simple mat from…
a) coconut fronds  b) plastic rope
**Answer: a) coconut fronds.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Before weaving, fibres are…
a) dyed, coloured and sun-dried  b) burned
**Answer: a) dyed, coloured and sun-dried.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A bilum is…
a) Papua New Guinea's woven string bag  b) a drum
**Answer: a) Papua New Guinea's woven string bag.**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** The fibre products named include…
a) bags, baskets, belts and mats  b) cars and boats
**Answer: a) bags, baskets, belts and mats.**
