# Papua New Guinea Weaving and Bilum

## Do it first

Look at the sub lesson list. Pick the part you already know something about, and say what you know. Then start at Sub lesson 1 — every part builds on the one before it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Weaving a mat from coconut fronds

The Grade 3 guide begins with what is made locally — the fibre and fabric crafts of the learner's own community, their twines and materials — then weaves a simple mat from coconut fronds, step by step.

## Sub lesson 2 — Preparing the fibre

The Grade 8 guide gives the full preparation: gathering suitable fibres, dyeing and colouring all fibres ready for weaving, and sun-drying them before the weaving starts.

## Sub lesson 3 — Weaving bags, bilums, baskets, belts and mats

The prepared fibre is woven into the products of daily life: bags, bilums — Papua New Guinea's string bags — baskets, belts and mats.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion this lesson's one thing in your own words, naming each sub lesson as you go. Companion checks: did every part get named?

## Do It Yourself

1. Do each sub lesson's practice once more, on your own.
2. Teach one part to someone else, the way this lesson taught it to you.
3. Write one sentence: what is this lesson's one thing?
