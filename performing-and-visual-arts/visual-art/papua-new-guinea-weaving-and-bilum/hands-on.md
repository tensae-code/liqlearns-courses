# Hands-on — Papua New Guinea Weaving and Bilum

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Weaving a mat from coconut fronds
Do the practice this part names, with real materials and a partner: The Grade 3 guide begins with what is made locally — the fibre and fabric crafts of the learner's own community, their twines and materials — then weaves a simple mat from coconut fronds, step by step.

## Preparing the fibre
Do the practice this part names, with real materials and a partner: The Grade 8 guide gives the full preparation: gathering suitable fibres, dyeing and colouring all fibres ready for weaving, and sun-drying them before the weaving starts.

## Weaving bags, bilums, baskets, belts and mats
Do the practice this part names, with real materials and a partner: The prepared fibre is woven into the products of daily life: bags, bilums — Papua New Guinea's string bags — baskets, belts and mats.
