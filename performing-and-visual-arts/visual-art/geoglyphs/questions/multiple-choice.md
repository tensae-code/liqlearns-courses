# Geoglyphs — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson — Geoglyphs — opens with…
a) Geoglifos  b) an examination  c) nothing at all
**Answer: a) Geoglifos.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The Venezuela book teaches “Geoglifos” as…
a) a part of geoglyphs  b) a sports rule  c) a cooking recipe
**Answer: a) a part of geoglyphs.**

**3.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) Las líneas de Nazca  b) long division  c) map coordinates
**Answer: a) Las líneas de Nazca.**

**4.** The parts of this lesson come from…
a) Venezuela's own Educación Artística books  b) a newspaper  c) a song chorus
**Answer: a) Venezuela's own Educación Artística books.**

**5.** “Las líneas de Nazca” is taught in this lesson as…
