# Hands-on — Geoglyphs

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Geoglifos
Work through this part with real materials or your own body, the way the book sets it: geoglyphs — rock art engraved on the earth to be seen from afar; Venezuela's La rueda del indio in Carabobo, a labyrinth trench 32 metres long, declared heritage in 1998. Show a companion what you made or did, and say what the part teaches.

## Las líneas de Nazca
Work through this part with real materials or your own body, the way the book sets it: the Nazca lines — great drawings made by clearing stones from the desert ground: animals, birds and mythical figures, probably ritual paths bound to agriculture, life and fertility (slug proposed above for Venezuela's geoglyph). Show a companion what you made or did, and say what the part teaches.
