## Sub lesson 1 — Geoglifos

Geoglyphs — rock art engraved on the earth to be seen from afar; Venezuela's La rueda del indio in Carabobo, a labyrinth trench 32 metres long, declared heritage in 1998.

## Sub lesson 2 — Las líneas de Nazca

The Nazca lines — great drawings made by clearing stones from the desert ground: animals, birds and mythical figures, probably ritual paths bound to agriculture, life and fertility (slug proposed above for Venezuela's geoglyph).
