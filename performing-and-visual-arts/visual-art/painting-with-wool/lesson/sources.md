Жүнмен сурет салу (Painting a picture with wool) — what it teaches: drawing an ordinary picture with wool — making a defined image from varied-coloured wool fibres; materials: a frame with glass, varied-coloured wool tufts, fabric — flizelin or flannel, a ready picture; technology: open the frame, take the glass out, stretch the fabric on the frame's back board and fix it; on the not-smooth white fabric lay white wool as the background; then with combing, stretching and rolling methods (тарақпен түту, созу, жұлмалау әдістері — pictured) lay coloured wool and make the picture's sketch; after the picture is finished the wool fibres must be pressed with glass so they do not move from their places when the wool layers up (student book) | Book 1, printed p. 24 |
