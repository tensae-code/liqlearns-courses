# Visual Communication

## Do it first

Quick game: point at something that speaks to you without words — a sign, a poster, a picture on a packet. Who sent that message? You are already reading visual communication.

## Sub lesson 1 — Communication and its four types

**Communication** is the **transmission of messages in a language**. It comes in **four types**: **verbal, non-verbal, interpersonal and visual** — and **visual communication's code is the image**.

Read it in examples: a mask is obligatory; no noise; more children than adults — pictures speaking without words.

## Sub lesson 2 — Agents, importance and media

Every visual message has its **agents**: **sender, receiver, message and channel/support** — find them in a health poster.

Its **importance** is wide, and so are its **media**: traffic signs, smoke, magazines, books, newspapers, packaging, posters, pictures, objects, computers, mobile phones, television and clothing.
