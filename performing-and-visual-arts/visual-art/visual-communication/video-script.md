Pictures speak!

Communication sends messages. Four types — verbal, non-verbal, interpersonal, visual.
Visual speaks in images. A sign. A poster. A red ribbon.

Sender. Receiver. Message. Channel.
Look around — signs, packaging, screens, clothes. Everything is talking.
