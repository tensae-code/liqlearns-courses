# Architecture

## Do it first

Quick start: say in one sentence what you think this lesson is about — architecture as structures fitted to human needs across time — caves, tipis, stilt houses and bridges — some built to last, some for a limited time. Then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — Structures fitted to human needs across time

The design project reads building as fitting structure to need, across the world and across time: **caves, the North American tipi, the palafitos (stilt houses) of Chiloé, a house in Togo, Calatrava's bridge in Jerusalem, and Beijing's Forbidden City**.

## Sub lesson 2 — Built to last — and built for a time

The same lesson draws the contrast plainly: **some structures were built to last, some for a limited time** — and both are architecture, judged by how well the structure answers its need and its time.
