# Architecture — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The palafitos of Chiloé are…
a) stilt houses  b) caves  c) bridges
**Answer: a) stilt houses**.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Calatrava's work in the guide is a…
a) bridge in Jerusalem  b) tipi  c) forbidden city
**Answer: a) bridge in Jerusalem**.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Some structures in the guide were built…
a) for a limited time  b) only of gold  c) under water
**Answer: a) for a limited time**.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Every structure in the lesson is fitted to…
a) human needs  b) one king  c) examinations
**Answer: a) human needs**.
