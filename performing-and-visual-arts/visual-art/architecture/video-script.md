# Video script — Architecture

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A building is an answer.

A **cave** answers shelter. A **tipi** answers a moving life. The **palafitos of Chiloé** stand on stilts over the water. A **house in Togo**, **Calatrava's bridge in Jerusalem**, **Beijing's Forbidden City** — every structure fitted to **human needs**.

Some were built **to last**. Some for **a limited time**. Both are architecture.
