## Sub lesson 1 — Модерно изкуство (Modern Art), §1 Modernism

modern art begins in the second half of the 19th century, independent of religious and political institutions, marked by experiment, provocation, new forms and materials: Symbolism (Gustave Moreau), Impressionism (Manet, Monet's Impression, Sunrise, Renoir, Degas), Post-Impressionism and Pointillism/Neo-Impressionism (Cézanne, Van Gogh's Starry Night, Gauguin, Seurat), Fauvism (Matisse), Cubism (Picasso, Juan Gris), Expressionism (Munch's The Scream, Käthe Kollwitz), Futurism (Severini, Boccioni), Abstractionism (Kandinsky, Malevich, Mondrian), Surrealism (Dalí, Magritte, Miró, De Chirico's metaphysical painting), Secession (Alphonse Mucha), Constructivism (Pevsner) and Brâncuși's avant-garde sculpture — each work captioned with its movement for the class gallery analysis

## Sub lesson 2 — Творци на модерното и на съвременното изкуство (Creators of Modern and Contemporary Art)

modern art, born in the second half of the 19th century and lasting to the 1950s, and its movements (Impressionism, Post-Impressionism, Art Nouveau, Fauvism, Futurism, Expressionism, Cubism, Constructivism, Symbolism, Abstractionism, Dada, Surrealism) with their named creators (Monet, Cézanne, Van Gogh, Braque, Matisse, Picasso, Munch, Kandinsky, Malevich, Klimt, Mondrian, Magritte, Duchamp, Dalí) and Bulgaria's modern artists (Nikola Petrov, Ivan Milev, Elena Karamihaylova, Boris Georgiev); contemporary art from the 1960s to today, distinguished by the underlined search for non-traditional, alternative visual messages — the individual style (the author's creative approach, subject content, expressive means, techniques and materials) made visible by technology's new possibilities: neo-avant-garde and postmodernism (Abstract Expressionism, Pop Art, Op Art, Minimalism), the installation forms (installations, constructions, package art, land art), the specific artistic expressions (performance, happening, artistic actions, body art, street art) and, after the 1990s, the digital arts (video art, computer animation, multimedia art, net art, interactive art); the named contemporary creators of the world (Allan Kaprow, Jackson Pollock, Victor Vasarely, Andy Warhol, Joan Miró, Jean Tinguely, Donald Judd, Frank Stella, Joseph Beuys, Alberto Giacometti, Marina Abramović, Nam June Paik, Jasper Johns, Damien Hirst, Georg Baselitz, Christo, Yayoi Kusama, Gerhard and Daniel Richter, David Hockney) — Lorenzo Quinn's "Giant Hands" rising from the Grand Canal at the 2017 Venice Biennale, Christo's "Floating Piers" on Lake Iseo (2016), and the graffiti artist David Choe, paid in Facebook shares for painting the company's walls — and of Bulgaria (Detsko Uzunov, Bencho Obreshkov, Iliya Petrov, Alexander Petrov, Svetlin Rusev, Georgi Bozhilov-Slona, Ioan Leviev, Encho Pironkov, Georgi Baev, Nedko Solakov, Lyuben Dimanov, Stoimen Stoilov, Stoyan Tsanev, Emil Popov, Georgi Chapkanov, Pavel Koychev, Vezhdi Rashidov, Stanislav Pamukchiev) — the same NEW lesson as proposed from the Grade 8 book (Modern Art Movements), here stocked creator by creator
