# Maya Art and Techniques — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Guatemala's Grade 4 art guide names as a learning theme…
a) the artistic techniques of the Maya people  b) only European painting  c) machine drawing
**Answer: a) the artistic techniques of the Maya people.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The Maya techniques are carried as…
a) a people's heritage  b) a new invention of the guide  c) a sport
**Answer: a) a people's heritage.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's evidence for the theme is…
a) theme level — the activity pages are not in the PARTIAL file  b) the full activity pages  c) a video
**Answer: a) theme level — the activity pages are not in the PARTIAL file.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** “Instrumental handling” means…
a) knowing the instruments a technique uses  b) playing in an orchestra only  c) drawing with a ruler
**Answer: a) knowing the instruments a technique uses.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** “Procedural handling” means…
a) following the technique's own order of working  b) working as fast as possible  c) skipping steps
**Answer: a) following the technique's own order of working.**
