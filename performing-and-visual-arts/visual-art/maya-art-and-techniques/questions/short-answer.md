# Maya Art and Techniques — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Whose artistic techniques does the guide name?
**Answer: The Maya people's own artistic techniques.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the two kinds of handling the guide names.
**Answer: Instrumental handling and procedural handling.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** How should a people's technique be handled?
**Answer: Carefully and respectfully, the way its own makers do.**
