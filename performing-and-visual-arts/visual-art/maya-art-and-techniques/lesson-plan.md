# Lesson plan — Maya Art and Techniques

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Visual Art · **Lesson:** Maya Art and Techniques
**Place in the curriculum:** Visual Art (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the Maya people's own artistic techniques, handled with their own instruments and procedures — taught at theme level, because the Grade 4 activity pages are not in the PARTIAL file.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this course in the same title.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The Maya people's own techniques** — Guatemala's Grade 4 Expresión Artística names, as one of its learning themes, the **artistic techniques of the Maya people** — a people's own ways of making art, carried as heritage.
2. **Handling instruments and procedures** — The theme is named in the guide's own terms: the **instrumental and procedural handling** of the Maya techniques — that is, knowing the instruments a technique uses, and following its procedure, its own order of working.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of this course in the same title. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say that the Maya people's own artistic techniques are a named learning theme in Guatemala's Grade 4 art guide.
2. Say what “instrumental and procedural handling” means — knowing a technique's instruments and following its procedure.
