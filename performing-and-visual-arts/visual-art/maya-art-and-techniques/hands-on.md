# Hands-on — Maya Art and Techniques

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Naming a technique of your own people
Name one artistic technique of your own community. Say what instruments it uses and what its procedure — its order of working — is, as far as you know it.

## Careful handling
Practise the first careful step of that technique with its real instrument or a safe stand-in, slowly, the way its makers would — handling first, speed later.
