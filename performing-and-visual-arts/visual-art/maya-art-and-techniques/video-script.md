# Video script — Maya Art and Techniques

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Every people has its own ways of making art.

Guatemala's guide names the artistic techniques of the Maya people as a thing to learn — their instruments, and their procedures, the order of the work.

Handle the instruments carefully. Follow the procedure respectfully. That is how a technique stays alive.
