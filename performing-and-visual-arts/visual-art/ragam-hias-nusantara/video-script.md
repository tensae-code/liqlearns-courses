# Video script — Ragam Hias Nusantara: Ornament and Crafts of the Indonesian Archipelago

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This is Indonesia's own lesson.

**Unit 1, Pertemuan 3.** the functions and benefits of textile craft and named examples of textile crafts across Nusantara (the archipelago), including their place in the global market.

**Unit 2, Pertemuan 7.** treating a textile surface, drawing motifs with their tools and materials, and the cultural value carried inside a motif.

**Unit 1 Ragam Hias Nusantara pada Produk Fesyen.** observing Nusantara fashion products and their ornamental motifs (ragam hias) — the archipelago's own decorative patterns and where they live.

**Unit 1 Analisa Ragam Kerajinan Nusantara.** analysing the variety (ragam) of Nusantara crafts — their kinds, materials, regions and characters.

**BAB II Seni Rupa Tradisi Dua Dimensi.** traditional two-dimensional art — copying ragam hias ornamental motifs onto cloth, motif by motif.

That is Ragam Hias Nusantara: Ornament and Crafts of the Indonesian Archipelago — Indonesia's way, kept by name.
