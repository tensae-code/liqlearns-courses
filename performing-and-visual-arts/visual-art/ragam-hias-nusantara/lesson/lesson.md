# Ragam Hias Nusantara: Ornament and Crafts of the Indonesian Archipelago

## Do it first

Name one thing that belongs in this lesson — Ragam Hias Nusantara: Ornament and Crafts of the Indonesian Archipelago — taught in Indonesia's own way, with the country's own named forms kept as parts. Say what it is in your own words. If you can name one, you are already inside the lesson.

## Sub lesson 1 — Unit 1, Pertemuan 3

the functions and benefits of textile craft and named examples of textile crafts across Nusantara (the archipelago), including their place in the global market.

## Sub lesson 2 — Unit 2, Pertemuan 7

treating a textile surface, drawing motifs with their tools and materials, and the cultural value carried inside a motif.

## Sub lesson 3 — Unit 1 Ragam Hias Nusantara pada Produk Fesyen

observing Nusantara fashion products and their ornamental motifs (ragam hias) — the archipelago's own decorative patterns and where they live.

## Sub lesson 4 — Unit 1 Analisa Ragam Kerajinan Nusantara

analysing the variety (ragam) of Nusantara crafts — their kinds, materials, regions and characters.

## Sub lesson 5 — BAB II Seni Rupa Tradisi Dua Dimensi

traditional two-dimensional art — copying ragam hias ornamental motifs onto cloth, motif by motif.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson holds, part by part, in your own words. Companion checks: did every part get named, with its Indonesian name kept where the book gives one?

## Do It Yourself

1. Choose one named part of this lesson and find out one more true thing about it — from a person who knows it, or from the book your part came from.
2. Make or perform a small piece in the way of that part: a rhythm, a step, a drawing, a shaped piece of clay.
3. Write one sentence: what does this part show about Indonesia's own way of teaching its arts?
