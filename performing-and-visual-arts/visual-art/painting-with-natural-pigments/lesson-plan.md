# Lesson plan — Painting with Natural Pigments

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Visual Art · **Lesson:** Painting with Natural Pigments
**Place in the curriculum:** Visual Art (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** painting with natural pigments — colours made from nature itself: red-cabbage juice that changes colour where lemon, vinegar or soda lands, and blueberries used as the art medium.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier making and drawing lessons of this course.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Painting with red-cabbage juice** — tape watercolour paper down, wash it all over with cabbage juice, let it dry, then paint patterns with lemon juice, vinegar and baking-soda solution, each with its own tool, and watch the colours change.
2. **Making the natural paint** — the teacher guide's making: chop and boil red cabbage for a strong juice paint, keep it about a week refrigerated or frozen in ice trays; blueberry juice also works as a colour-changing paint.
3. **Blueberry magic** — making art with blueberries through chemical reaction — nature's own materials as the art medium, observed with all senses (goals and framing as printed; the step sheet lives on the LUMA Lapland site).

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier drawing and making lessons of this course. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say how a red-cabbage painting is made, step by step.
2. Say how the cabbage-juice paint is made and kept.
3. Say what blueberry magic uses as its art medium.

## What you need
Watercolour paper and tape, red cabbage, lemon juice, vinegar, baking-soda solution, a tool for each substance; blueberries for the third part.
