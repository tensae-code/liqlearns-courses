# Painting with Natural Pigments — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The painting ground is washed all over with…
a) milk  b) red-cabbage juice  c) ink
**Answer: b) red-cabbage juice**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Patterns are painted with lemon juice, vinegar and…
a) baking-soda solution  b) sand  c) glue
**Answer: a) baking-soda solution**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The cabbage paint is made by…
a) freezing leaves whole  b) chopping and boiling red cabbage  c) drying cabbage in the sun
**Answer: b) chopping and boiling red cabbage**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Blueberry magic makes art with blueberries through…
a) printing  b) chemical reaction  c) weaving
**Answer: b) chemical reaction**
