# Natural Dyes and Dyeing

## Do it first

This cloth is white. These plants are colour. Put them together — and the cloth comes out wearing the plant's colour, ready to become a shirt.

## Sub lesson 1 — From plant-dyed material to a product

The Finnish craft-school pilot **"From plant-dyed material to a product"** teaches the whole path: **dye yarns and fabrics with plant dyes** — nature's own colours entering the fibre — and then **sew the dyed fabric into a shirt**. The pupils in the pilot were **11–15 years old**. Natural dyeing is a living school craft in Finland: the colour is made, not bought, and the dyed material is carried through to a finished, wearable product.
