# Natural Dyes and Dyeing — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Plant dyes are used to colour…
a) yarns and fabrics  b) glass  c) stone
**Answer: a) yarns and fabrics**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** After dyeing, the fabric is…
a) thrown away  b) sewn into a shirt  c) painted over
**Answer: b) sewn into a shirt**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The pilot's pupils were aged…
a) 5–7  b) 11–15  c) adults only
**Answer: b) 11–15**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** The colours in this lesson come from…
a) plants  b) plastic  c) chalk
**Answer: a) plants**
