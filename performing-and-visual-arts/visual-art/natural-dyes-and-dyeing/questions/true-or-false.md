# Natural Dyes and Dyeing — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The dyed fabric is carried through to a finished product.
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Plant dyes can colour yarn as well as fabric.
**Answer: True.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** In the pilot, dyeing and sewing were taught in different schools with no connection.
**Answer: False** — one pilot runs from plant-dyed material to a sewn product.
