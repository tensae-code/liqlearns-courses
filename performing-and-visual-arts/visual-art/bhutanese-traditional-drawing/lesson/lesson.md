# Bhutanese Traditional Drawing

## Do it first

Quick start: from the title alone, guess what this lesson's work is. Then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — The Do-Yen-Na-Nga drawings

Bhutan has its own disciplined traditional drawing. Its subject here is the **Do-Yen-Na-Nga — the Five Senses offering** of Bhutanese traditional art. The **teacher displays the drawings** of the Do-Yen-Na-Nga for the class to study.

## Sub lesson 2 — Copy the traditional forms

Then the **students copy the drawings**. That is how the traditional forms are learned — by drawing them, carefully, yourself.
