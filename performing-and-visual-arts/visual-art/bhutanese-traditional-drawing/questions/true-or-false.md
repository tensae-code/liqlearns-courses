# Bhutanese Traditional Drawing — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Do-Yen-Na-Nga is the Five Senses offering.
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The students display the drawings and the teacher copies them.
**Answer: False.** — the teacher displays; the students copy.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The traditional forms are learned by drawing them.
**Answer: True.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In this tradition, copying the forms is not allowed.
**Answer: False.** — copying is the way they are learned.
