# Bhutanese Traditional Drawing — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the Do-Yen-Na-Nga?
**Answer: The Five Senses offering of Bhutanese traditional art.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How do the students learn the traditional forms?
**Answer: By copying the drawings the teacher displays.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the teacher do first?
**Answer: Displays the drawings of the Do-Yen-Na-Nga.**
