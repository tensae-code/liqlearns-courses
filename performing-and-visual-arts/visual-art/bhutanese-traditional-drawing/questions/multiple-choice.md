# Bhutanese Traditional Drawing — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Do-Yen-Na-Nga is…
a) the Five Senses offering of Bhutanese traditional art  b) a kind of paper folding  c) a musical instrument
**Answer: a) the Five Senses offering of Bhutanese traditional art**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Who displays the traditional drawings?
a) the students  b) the teacher  c) nobody
**Answer: b) the teacher**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The students learn the forms by…
a) copying the drawings  b) describing them in words only  c) photographing them
**Answer: a) copying the drawings**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Copying in this lesson is…
a) the way the traditional forms are learned  b) forbidden  c) a test
**Answer: a) the way the traditional forms are learned**

**5. (Sub lesson 1)** This traditional drawing belongs to…
a) Bhutan  b) every country equally  c) no country
**Answer: a) Bhutan**
