# Video script — Animation

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

One picture. Another picture. Another — faster, faster!

Suddenly the drawn hero moves.

Puppets can move a film too — and today, computers help.

Invent two heroes. Give them a story. Give the film a name.

Fifteen scenes in a row — and a whole class has made a film.
