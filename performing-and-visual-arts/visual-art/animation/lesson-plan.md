# Lesson plan — Animation

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Visual Art · **Lesson:** Animation
**Place in the curriculum:** Visual Art (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** an animated film is built from little pictures that change quickly, so it looks as if the drawn heroes are moving — or from puppets that are moved — and a class can make its own: heroes invented, a story divided into scenes, every scene drawn, and the works exhibited in order so the story can be read.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this course in the same title.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **How animated films move** — A cartoon film is made of little pictures that change quickly, so that it looks as if the drawn heroes are moving.
2. **Invent heroes for an animated film** — The Grade 2 task: invent two heroes for an animated film, write a short story about them, then draw them, and at the end give your film a name..
3. **The class film — a story in fifteen scenes** — The Grade 3 class project makes a "sketch" for a joint animated film from the story "Јежић" (The Little Hedgehog), printed in the book.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
