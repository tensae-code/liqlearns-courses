# Animation — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is an animated film made of, in the book's other way?
**Answer: Puppets that are moved.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What must you invent first for your own animated film?
**Answer: Two heroes — then write a short story about them and draw them.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name the characters of "Ježić" the class lists.
**Answer: The hedgehog, the old squirrel, the little squirrel, and the magpie with the wounded wing.**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** How does the class check its film sketch at the end?
**Answer: By exhibiting the scenes in order from 1 to 15 and discussing whether the story can be clearly "read" from the pictures.**
