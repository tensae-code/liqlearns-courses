# Animation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A cartoon film looks alive because…
a) its little pictures change quickly  b) it has only one picture  c) the heroes are real
**Answer: a) its little pictures change quickly**.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Today, animated and cartoon films are made…
a) on the computer  b) only with puppets  c) only by hand, never on computer
**Answer: a) on the computer**.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The Grade 2 task ends by…
a) giving your film a name  b) selling the film  c) hiding the heroes
**Answer: a) giving your film a name**.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** The class divides the hedgehog story into…
a) 15 parts  b) 3 parts  c) 100 parts
**Answer: a) 15 parts**.
