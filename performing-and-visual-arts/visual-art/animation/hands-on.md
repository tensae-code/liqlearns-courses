# Hands-on — Animation

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Two heroes
Invent two heroes. Draw each one three times — standing, walking, waving — so the little pictures change a little each time, the way a cartoon film begins.

## One scene of fifteen
With a partner, take one scene number from a story your class has divided into 15 parts. Draw and paint that scene so someone who sees all fifteen in order can read the story.
