# Animation

## Do it first

Quick start: say in one sentence what you think this lesson is about — an animated film is built from little pictures that change quickly, so it looks as if the drawn heroes are moving. Then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — How animated films move

A **cartoon film is made of little pictures that change quickly, so that it looks as if the drawn heroes are moving**. An **animated film is made of puppets that are moved**. **Today both animated and cartoon films are made on the computer.**

## Sub lesson 2 — Invent heroes for an animated film

The Grade 2 task: **invent two heroes for an animated film, write a short story about them, then draw them, and at the end give your film a name.**

## Sub lesson 3 — The class film — a story in fifteen scenes

The Grade 3 class project makes a **"sketch" for a joint animated film** from the story **"Јежић" (The Little Hedgehog)**, printed in the book. Read the story aloud, **divide it together into 15 parts** and write what happens in each; list **all the characters** (the hedgehog, the old squirrel, the little squirrel, the magpie with the wounded wing); several pupils first **paint all the characters**; then, **in groups of two, each group draws and paints one scene, from 1 to 15**, conveying the story's message. At the end, **make an exhibition arranging the works from 1 to 15** and discuss whether the story can be clearly "read" from the pictures; **each group proposes a name for the film**, and the class jointly chooses the best one.
