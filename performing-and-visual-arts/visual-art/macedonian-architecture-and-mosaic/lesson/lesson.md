# Macedonian Architecture and Mosaic

## Do it first

## Sub lesson 1 — architecture and mosaic in 20th-century Macedonia

architecture and mosaic in 20th-century Macedonia: Skopje from early modernist and neoclassical landmarks (Officers' Home, Ristiḱ Palace, the National Theatre) through socialist modernism to the bold brutalism after the 1963 earthquake, rebuilt on Kenzo Tange's master plan (City Gate, City Wall, the Railway Station) by Konstantinovski, Konstantinov (the Main Post Office) and Čipan; mosaic from the ancient floors of Heraclea and Stobi to today's monumental mosaics — Čemerksi's Freedom Monument in Kočani and Gazanfer Bajram.
