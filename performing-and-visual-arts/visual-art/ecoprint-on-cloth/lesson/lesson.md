# Ecoprint on Cloth

## Do it first

Name one thing that belongs in this lesson — Ecoprint — printing leaves and plants directly onto cloth with their own colours and shapes. Say what it is in your own words. If you can name one, you are already inside the lesson.

## Sub lesson 1 — Bab II

making ecoprint on a cloth medium — printing from natural materials onto fabric.

## Sub lesson 2 — Unit 2, Pertemuan 8–11

ecoprint by pounding — transferring leaf shapes and colours onto a tote bag by hammering real leaves into the cloth.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson holds, part by part, in your own words. Companion checks: did every part get named, with its Indonesian name kept where the book gives one?

## Do It Yourself

1. Choose one named part of this lesson and find out one more true thing about it — from a person who knows it, or from the book your part came from.
2. Make or perform a small piece in the way of that part: a rhythm, a step, a drawing, a shaped piece of clay.
3. Write one sentence: what does this part show about Indonesia's own way of teaching its arts?
