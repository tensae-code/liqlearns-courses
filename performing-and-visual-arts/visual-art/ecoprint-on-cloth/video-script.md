The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

This is Indonesia's own lesson.

**Bab II.** making ecoprint on a cloth medium — printing from natural materials onto fabric.

**Unit 2, Pertemuan 8–11.** ecoprint by pounding — transferring leaf shapes and colours onto a tote bag by hammering real leaves into the cloth.

That is Ecoprint on Cloth — Indonesia's way, kept by name.
