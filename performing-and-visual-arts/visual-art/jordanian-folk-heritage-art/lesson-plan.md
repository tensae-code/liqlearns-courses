# Lesson plan — Jordanian Folk Heritage Art

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Visual Art · **Lesson:** Jordanian Folk Heritage Art
**Place in the curriculum:** Visual Art (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Jordan's folk heritage in art — folk dress and dancers in traditional dress, a woven straw plate coloured by its pattern, a Jordanian heritage rug (bisat) pattern, and the Badia's coloured clothing, homes and camel and horse coverings.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this course in the same title.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Art and Folk Heritage** — Jordan's folk heritage is full of colours and art shapes used in designing folk clothes and tools; dancers in traditional dress, completing the colouring of a woven straw plate by its pattern, and colouring a Jordanian heritage rug (bisat) pattern
2. **Drawings from the Badia** — the Badia (Jordan's desert) is beautiful, and many scenes expressing the life in it can be drawn — a camel resting in a decorated saddle blanket under a palm at sunset; the Badia's people used many colours and artistic shapes in their clothing and their homes, and in covering their camels and horses; colouring the Badia scene (tent, palm, camel, coffee dallah and woven seating) in wood colours, and why the Badia's people use the colour red so much in their weavings

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
