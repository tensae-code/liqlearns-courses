# Jordanian Folk Heritage Art

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about jordanian folk heritage art. Then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — Art and Folk Heritage

Jordan's folk heritage is full of colours and art shapes used in designing folk clothes and tools; dancers in traditional dress, completing the colouring of a woven straw plate by its pattern, and colouring a Jordanian heritage rug (bisat) pattern.

## Sub lesson 2 — Drawings from the Badia

The Badia (Jordan's desert) is beautiful, and many scenes expressing the life in it can be drawn — a camel resting in a decorated saddle blanket under a palm at sunset; the Badia's people used many colours and artistic shapes in their clothing and their homes, and in covering their camels and horses; colouring the Badia scene (tent, palm, camel, coffee dallah and woven seating) in wood colours, and why the Badia's people use the colour red so much in their weavings.
