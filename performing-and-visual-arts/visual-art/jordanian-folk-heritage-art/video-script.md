# Video script — Jordanian Folk Heritage Art

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is Jordanian Folk Heritage Art.

Jordan's folk heritage is full of colours and art shapes used in designing folk clothes and tools; dancers in traditional dress, completing the colouring of a woven straw plate by its pattern, and colouring a Jordanian heritage rug (bisat) pattern.

Watch, listen — then say it back in your own words.
