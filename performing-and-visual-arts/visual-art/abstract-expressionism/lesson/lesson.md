# Abstract Expressionism

## Do it first

Name one thing that belongs in this lesson — The abstract-expressionist movement — large, gestural painting driven by action and feeling rather than depiction. Say what it is in your own words. If you can name one, you are already inside the lesson.

## Sub lesson 1 — Апстрактен експресионизам

Апстрактен експресионизам — New York after the Second World War becomes art's new centre: gestural action painting — Pollock dripping and pouring paint over canvases laid on the floor, Kline, de Kooning — against colour-field painting — Rothko's and Newman's great flat fields that work on the viewer's feelings; its sculpture in David Smith, Calder and Noguchi.

## Sub lesson 2 — Апстрактен експресионизам

Апстрактен експресионизам — Abstract Expressionism: the huge canvas and the act of painting itself — Pollock's action painting and the colour-field painters.
