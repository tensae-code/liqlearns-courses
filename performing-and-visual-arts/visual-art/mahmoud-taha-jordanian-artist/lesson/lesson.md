## Sub lesson 1 — An Artist from My Country

The Jordanian artist and potter Mahmoud Taha (1942–2017), who studied ceramics in Baghdad and Cardiff and Arabic calligraphy under Mohamed Hashim Al-Baghdadi, and made calligraphy — Kufic and Thuluth script — a basic element of his ceramic murals and sculptural pieces, blending Islamic ceramic heritage with contemporary art on national subjects drawn from his own environment.
