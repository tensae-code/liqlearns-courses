# Video script — Art of the First Peoples

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

**Escultura de bulto y relieves de la época ágrafa.** The small sculptures of the unwritten age — voluminous female figures in ivory, stone and bone (the Venuses of Willendorf and Lespugue) exalting fecundity, and animals carved in bone.

That is Art of the First Peoples — Venezuela's own books teach it this way.
