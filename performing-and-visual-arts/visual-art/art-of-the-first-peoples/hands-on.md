# Hands-on — Art of the First Peoples

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Escultura de bulto y relieves de la época ágrafa
Work through this part with real materials or your own body, the way the book sets it: the small sculptures of the unwritten age — voluminous female figures in ivory, stone and bone (the Venuses of Willendorf and Lespugue) exalting fecundity, and animals carved in bone. Show a companion what you made or did, and say what the part teaches.
