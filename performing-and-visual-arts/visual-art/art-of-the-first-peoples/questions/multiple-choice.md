# Art of the First Peoples — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson — Art of the First Peoples — opens with…
a) Escultura de bulto y relieves de la época ágrafa  b) an examination  c) nothing at all
**Answer: a) Escultura de bulto y relieves de la época ágrafa.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The Venezuela book teaches “Escultura de bulto y relieves de la época ágrafa” as…
a) a part of art of the first peoples  b) a sports rule  c) a cooking recipe
**Answer: a) a part of art of the first peoples.**

**3.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) Escultura de bulto y relieves de la época ágrafa  b) long division  c) map coordinates
**Answer: a) Escultura de bulto y relieves de la época ágrafa.**

**4.** The parts of this lesson come from…
a) Venezuela's own Educación Artística books  b) a newspaper  c) a song chorus
**Answer: a) Venezuela's own Educación Artística books.**

**5.** “Escultura de bulto y relieves de la época ágrafa” is taught in this lesson as…
