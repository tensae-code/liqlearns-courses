# Embroidery

## Do it first

Quick game: look at a towel or cloth near you. Is any pattern stitched onto it? That stitching is embroidery — this lesson names its kinds.

## Sub lesson 1 — Embroidery and its three elements

**Embroidery (bordado)** is **creating, by hand or machine, ornamental designs or figures on fabric**. It needs **three elements: fabric, thread and needle**.

The lesson names its types: **cross stitch, vagonite, Russian stitch** — and **ribbon embroidery (bordado com fitas)**, named in the lesson's list but given no section of its own.

## Sub lesson 2 — Cross stitch and vagonite

**Cross stitch (ponto cruz)**: tight, delicate, precise stitches for everything from towels to baby sheets.

**Vagonite**: the beginner's stitch — symmetric, geometric designs and fine finishing on bath towels, tablecloths and clothes.

## Sub lesson 3 — Russian stitch

**Russian stitch (ponto russo)**: worked with a **special needle** from the **fabric's reverse side**, leaving a **3D effect** — for rugs and cushion covers.
