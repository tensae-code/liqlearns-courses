# Hands-on — Embroidery

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Three-element check
Lay out fabric, thread and needle. Say what each element does in embroidery before you begin.

## Stitch sampler (drawing first)
On paper, draw a sampler of the three stitches: a row of small crosses, a symmetric geometric vagonite band, and a raised Russian-stitch shape shaded to show its 3D effect. Label each.
