# Embroidery — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Embroidery needs three elements:
a) fabric, thread and needle  b) paper, glue and scissors  c) wood, nail and hammer
**Answer: a) fabric, thread and needle**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The beginner's stitch is…
a) cross stitch  b) vagonite  c) Russian stitch
**Answer: b) vagonite**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Cross stitch is…
a) loose and rough  b) tight, delicate and precise  c) worked from the reverse side
**Answer: b) tight, delicate and precise**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Russian stitch leaves…
a) a flat shine  b) a 3D effect  c) holes in the fabric
**Answer: b) a 3D effect**
