# Embroidery — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Embroidery can be done by hand or by machine.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Vagonite designs are symmetric and geometric.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Ribbon embroidery is given a full section of its own in the lesson.
**Answer: False** — it is named in the lesson's list but given no section of its own.
