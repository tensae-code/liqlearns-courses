This lesson is Herbarium and Dried-Flower Art.

Herbarium and Dried-Flower Art — herbarium art — pressing and arranging dried plants and flowers into panels and pictures. — as Kazakhstan's school books teach it: natural materials: how to gather and dry them without harming nature; a herbarium is a collection of dried plants, a panel (панно) is a framed composition.
