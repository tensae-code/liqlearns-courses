This is Armenia's own lesson.

**KHACHKARS (խաչքար).** KHACHKARS (խաչքար) — winged stone khachkars appear with the faith's spread; khachkar-making took a new stage in the 10th-11th centuries; after Julfa's destruction the richest complex is Noratus village cemetery; patrons' and princes' portraits on some stones; Poghos the master's Goshavank khachkar (1291) beside an Irish stone cross.

**Master Momik's khachkar.** Master Momik's khachkar — the "Tamta Khatun" khachkar (1308), among the finest examples: the carved cross at the centre wholly surrounded by ornament.

That is Khachkar Carving — Armenia's way, kept by name.
