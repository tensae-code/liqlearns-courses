# Khachkar Carving

## Sub lesson 1 — KHACHKARS (խաչքար)

KHACHKARS (խաչքար) — winged stone khachkars appear with the faith's spread; khachkar-making took a new stage in the 10th-11th centuries; after Julfa's destruction the richest complex is Noratus village cemetery; patrons' and princes' portraits on some stones; Poghos the master's Goshavank khachkar (1291) beside an Irish stone cross.

## Sub lesson 2 — Master Momik's khachkar

Master Momik's khachkar — the "Tamta Khatun" khachkar (1308), among the finest examples: the carved cross at the centre wholly surrounded by ornament.
