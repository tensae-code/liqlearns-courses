# Khachkar Carving — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer: Khachkar Carving — Armenia's own teaching of this topic, kept as named parts: KHACHKARS (խաչքար) — winged stone khachkars appear with the faith's spread; khachkar-making took a new stage in the 10th-11th centuries; after Julfa's destruction the richest complex is Noratus village cemetery; patrons' and princes' portraits on some stones; Poghos the master's Goshavank khachkar (1291) beside an Irish stone cross.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the first sub lesson.
**Answer: KHACHKARS (խաչքար).**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the last sub lesson.
**Answer: Master Momik's khachkar.**
