# Afghan Artists

## Sub lesson 1 — The founders of contemporary Afghan art

Professor Gholam Mohammad Maimanagi of Faryab — court painter as a boy, founder of the Kabul School of Industries in 1302 SH, master of painting, music, architecture and poetry, his works in the National Gallery. Abdul Ghafoor Breshna — trained at Habibia and at the West German academy of fine arts, who brought the life of the lowest classes into contemporary art in the purest Afghan colours, free of European influence. And Ghausuddin Ghausi — among the first forty pupils of the Kabul fine-arts school, designer of many Afghan postage stamps, painter of twenty historical works in Kandahar, head of the Afghan Artists' Union, with some 2,000–2,500 works.

## Sub lesson 2 — Masters of miniature and calligraphy

Haji Mohammad Saeed 'Mashal' of Ghor (born 1295 SH) — raised in an art-loving family, he strove to revive the school of Master Kamaluddin Behzad; among the finest masters of miniature and of illumination (تذهیب), with his own style; his works are kept in the National Gallery in Kabul. And Azizuddin Wakili Popalzai (born 1297 SH in Kabul) — famed as the 'calligrapher of the seven pens' (خطاط هفت قلمی), who joined calligraphy to painting in inventive ways.

## Sub lesson 3 — Realists and teachers

Karim Shah Khan (born 1298 SH in Kabul) — learned drawing at the Kabul School of Industries and taught the art of drawing for forty years; a realist, with a special gift for sketching animals and birds; his book 'Sketching Domestic and Wild Animals' was printed by the Ministry in 1332–33 SH. And Gholam Mohiuddin 'Shabnam' (born 1314 SH in Ghazni) — took his art degree in the Philippines and taught art at Kabul's teacher-training institute for thirty-nine years; author of the 'Guide to Teaching Painting for Children and Adolescents' and of 'Contemporary Painters of Afghanistan'.
