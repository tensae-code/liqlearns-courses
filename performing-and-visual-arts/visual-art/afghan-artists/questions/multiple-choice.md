# Afghan Artists — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Who founded the Kabul School of Industries?
a) Gholam Mohammad Maimanagi  b) Abdul Ghafoor Breshna  c) Karim Shah Khan
**Answer: a) Gholam Mohammad Maimanagi**

**2.** Breshna brought what into contemporary Afghan art?
a) the life of the lowest classes, in the purest Afghan colours  b) European court scenes  c) sea landscapes only
**Answer: a) the life of the lowest classes, in the purest Afghan colours**

**3.** 'Mashal' strove to revive the school of…
a) Master Kamaluddin Behzad  b) Leonardo da Vinci  c) Saint Yared
**Answer: a) Master Kamaluddin Behzad**

**4.** Popalzai was famed as…
a) the 'calligrapher of the seven pens'  b) the stamp designer  c) the animal sketcher
**Answer: a) the 'calligrapher of the seven pens'**
