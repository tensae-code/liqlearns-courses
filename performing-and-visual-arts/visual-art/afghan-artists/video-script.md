# Video script — Afghan Artists

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Meet Afghanistan's artists.

Maimanagi, who founded the school. Breshna, who painted the poorest people's lives in pure Afghan colours.
Mashal, master of the miniature. Popalzai, calligrapher of the seven pens.
Karim Shah Khan, who sketched every animal and bird — and taught for forty years.
These are the country's own masters.
