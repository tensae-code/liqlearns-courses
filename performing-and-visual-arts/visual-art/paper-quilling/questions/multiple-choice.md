# Paper Quilling — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In quilling, strips of paper are…
a) rolled on a tool and their ends glued  b) folded in half once  c) torn into pieces
**Answer: a) rolled on a tool and their ends glued**.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which is one of the basic quilling forms?
a) a teardrop  b) a cube  c) a knot
**Answer: a) a teardrop** — circle, petal, eye, teardrop, half-moon, heart, square, triangle, rectangle.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Quilled forms can be composed into…
a) cards, flowers and photo frames  b) clay pots  c) woven baskets
**Answer: a) cards, flowers and photo frames** — and little pictures, mărțișoare and gift-box ornaments.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The strips for the photo frame are cut…
a) 1 cm wide  b) 10 cm wide  c) in circles
**Answer: a) 1 cm wide**.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Leaf strips are rolled…
a) all the way  b) only halfway, and glued three together  c) not at all
**Answer: b) only halfway, and glued three together**.
