# Paper Quilling — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The ends of a rolled strip are glued to hold the coil.
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A mărțișor can be made by quilling.
**Answer: True.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Frame strips are rolled on a needle or a brush.
**Answer: True.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Leaves for the frame are rolled all the way to the end.
**Answer: False** — leaf strips are rolled only halfway and glued three together.
