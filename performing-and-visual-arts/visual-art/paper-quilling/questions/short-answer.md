# Paper Quilling — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name five basic quilling forms.
**Answer: Any five of: circle, petal, eye, teardrop, half-moon, heart, square, triangle, rectangle.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What tool can the strips be rolled on?
**Answer: A tool like a stick — for the frame, a needle or a brush.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** How are the petals and leaves fixed to the frame?
**Answer: They are glued around the frame, with the photo space cut out.**
