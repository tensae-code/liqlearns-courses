# Video script — Paper Quilling

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Thin strips of paper. Roll them on a tool. Glue the end.

Pinch — a petal! Pinch — an eye! A teardrop! A heart!

Put the shapes together — a flower, a card, a frame.

That is **quilling** — pictures made of rolled paper.
