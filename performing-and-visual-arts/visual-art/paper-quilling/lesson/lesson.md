# Paper Quilling

## Do it first

Quick start: take a thin strip of paper and roll it tightly around a pencil. Let go gently. What shape is left in your hand? Then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — The art of rolling paper

**Quilling** is the art of rolling paper. You **roll strips of paper on a tool** (like a stick), **glue the ends**, and shape the coils into the basic forms: **circle, petal, eye, teardrop, half-moon, heart, square, triangle and rectangle**. From those forms you compose **cards, little pictures, mărțișoare, flowers, photo frames and gift-box ornaments**.

## Sub lesson 2 — A quilled photo frame

The book builds a **quilled photo frame** step by step: cut **1 cm card strips**; **roll each on a needle or brush**; **pinch it into the petal shape** you want; for leaves, **roll the strips only halfway and glue three together**; then **glue petals and leaves around a frame** with the photo space cut out.
