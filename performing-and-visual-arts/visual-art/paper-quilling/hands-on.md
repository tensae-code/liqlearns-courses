# Hands-on — Paper Quilling

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Basic forms
Roll strips on a tool, glue the ends, and shape one of each basic form you can: circle, petal, eye, teardrop, half-moon, heart, square, triangle, rectangle. Lay them in a row and name them.

## Frame the photo
Cut 1 cm card strips. Make petals, and leaves rolled halfway and glued in threes. Glue them around a frame with the photo space cut out.
