# Telling History in Words and Pictures — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The teacher guide treats narrating a story/history as —
a) a gift that cannot be learned  b) a technique — a learnable craft with its own section  c) work only for book writers
**Answer: b) a technique — a learnable craft with its own section** — ታሪክ እንዴት ይተረካል — how a history/story is told, §3.3.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The guide's §3.4 teaches explaining written histories —
a) by memorising them word for word  b) by means of visual art — carrying them across into pictures  c) by translating them into songs only
**Answer: b) by means of visual art — carrying them across into pictures** — የተጻፈ ታሪኮችን በዕይታ ጥበባት ማብራራት.
