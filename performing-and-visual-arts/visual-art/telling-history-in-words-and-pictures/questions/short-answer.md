# Telling History in Words and Pictures — short answer

Answer in a sentence or a short list.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the Amharic title of the guide's narration section, and what does it mean?
**Answer:** ታሪክ እንዴት ይተረካል — "how a history/story is told".

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name two things a picture can make clear in a written history.
**Answer:** The place where it happened and the look of the moment (the people, the scene) — what words carry badly, the eye meets in the picture.
