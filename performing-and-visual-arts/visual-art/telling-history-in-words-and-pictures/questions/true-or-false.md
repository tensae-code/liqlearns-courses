# Telling History in Words and Pictures — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A good telling carries the listener through the events in their order.
**Answer: True** — the listener who was not there must be able to follow.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In this method, the picture replaces the written history completely.
**Answer: False** — the picture explains the written history; the two stand together.
