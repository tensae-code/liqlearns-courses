# Video script — Telling History in Words and Pictures


The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.


---


*Evidence lesson — the teacher guide's craft, in simple voice.*

A history is not only facts. It is a **telling**.

Tell it so the listener travels with you — event by event, in order.

And when words are not enough, **draw**.

A written history, explained by pictures, enters the eye — and stays.

Words carry the meaning. Pictures carry the moment. Together, they carry the past.
