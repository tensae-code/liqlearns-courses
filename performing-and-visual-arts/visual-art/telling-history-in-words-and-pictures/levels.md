# Telling History in Words and Pictures — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number, as before. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — How a history or story is told (teacher guide — evidence)
Pass mark: say, as teacher-guide evidence, that the guide teaches a technique of narrating a story/history (ታሪክ እንዴት ይተረካል) — the telling is a craft with its own section — and demonstrate a short telling that carries a listener through the events in their order.

## Sub lesson 2 — Written histories explained through visual art (teacher guide — evidence)
Pass mark: say, as teacher-guide evidence, that the guide teaches explaining written histories by means of visual art (የተጻፈ ታሪኮችን በዕይታ ጥበባት ማብራራት) — story and history carried across into pictures — and pair a written history's main parts with the pictures that explain them.
