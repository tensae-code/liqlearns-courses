# Lesson plan — Telling History in Words and Pictures

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Visual Art · **Lesson:** Telling History in Words and Pictures
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 2 — Visual Art (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the teacher guide's craft of narration: how a history or story is told — and how written histories are explained through visual art, so the words and the pictures carry the past together.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Telling History with Illustration, where the learner drew a written history. This lesson stands beside it as the teacher guide's own account of the telling craft — evidence throughout, labelled as such.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
1. **How a history or story is told (teacher guide — evidence)** — the guide's narration technique: the telling itself as a craft.
2. **Written histories explained through visual art (teacher guide — evidence)** — the guide's second move: a written history, explained by means of pictures.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Telling History with Illustration (Visual Art) — the pupil lesson this guide evidence stands beside. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say, from the guide's evidence, what the technique of narrating a story or history involves: a telling that carries the listener through the events.
2. Explain, from the guide's evidence, how a written history is explained by means of visual art.
3. Plan a short history told in words, with the pictures that will explain it.

## What you need
A history or story to work on — written, told, or remembered — plus pencil and paper for the picture plan.

## If the learner is stuck
- The telling rambles → return to the events' order; narration carries the listener through what happened, in its order.
- The pictures repeat the words → a picture should explain what words carry badly: the place, the faces, the moment's look.
