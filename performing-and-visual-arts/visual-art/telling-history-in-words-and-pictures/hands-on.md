# Hands-on — Telling History in Words and Pictures

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Tell it in order
Choose a short history or story. Write its events in order, one line each. Tell it aloud from your lines, carrying your listener through the events without jumping back.

## Picture the written history
Take one written history — from a book or a told account written down. Beside each main part, sketch the picture that explains it: the place, the people, the moment. Show a companion the words and pictures together.
