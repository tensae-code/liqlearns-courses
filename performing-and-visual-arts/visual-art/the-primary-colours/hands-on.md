# Hands-on — The Primary Colours


The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The primary hunt
Walk home or school with three colour cards — red, yellow, blue. Every time you find a pure primary colour in a real thing, hold up its card and name the thing. Count your finds per colour.

## One-colour page
Choose one primary and fill a whole drawing using ONLY it — light pressure and hard pressure give you its light and dark family. Then do the festival page: all three primaries together.
