# The Primary Colours — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Primary colours (መስራች) are colours that are —
a) mixed from two other colours  b) found pure in nature without mixing  c) found only in shops
**Answer: b) found pure in nature without mixing with other colours.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The three primary colours are —
a) red, green, blue  b) red, yellow, blue  c) yellow, green, orange
**Answer: b) red, yellow and blue.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The book pictures the primaries as —
a) balloons and glasses of coloured liquid  b) houses and cars  c) flags only
**Answer: a) balloons (yellow, blue, red) and glasses of coloured liquid (red, yellow, blue).**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The song "መስራች ቀለማት" calls the primaries —
a) the children of colours  b) the mother and father of colours  c) the kings of the sky
**Answer: b) the mother and father of colours — all other colours are born from them.**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** From a set — red, green, blue, orange, yellow, purple — the primaries are —
a) green, orange, purple  b) red, blue, yellow  c) red, green, yellow
**Answer: b) red, blue and yellow; the others are mixtures.**

**6. (Sub lesson 4)** Activity 14 asks you to —
a) make different drawings in the primary colours  b) wash the paint pots  c) name colours in English
**Answer: a) make different drawings in the primary colours.**

**7. (Sub lesson 5)** The teacher guides call the primaries by their other name —
a) the bright colours  b) the basic colours  c) the festival colours
**Answer: b) the basic colours.**

**8. (Sub lesson 5)** The guides' three colour levels are —
a) primary, secondary, neutral  b) light, dark, bright  c) first, second, third paint
**Answer: a) primary (first-level), secondary (second-level) and neutral colours.**
