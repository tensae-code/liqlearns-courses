# The Primary Colours — short answer

Answer in a short sentence, or by doing what the question asks.

**1. (Sub lesson 4)** Describe a one-colour study drawing in blue: name three things in it that must all find their blue.
**Answer: Any three drawn things (e.g. sky, water, flower) all coloured in blue.**

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** You are sorting clothes: a red dress, a green scarf, a blue hat, an orange belt. Which do you keep as primaries, and why?
**Answer: Keep the red dress and the blue hat — red and blue are primary; green and orange are mixtures, set aside.**

**3. (Sub lesson 5)** Name the three colour levels in the teacher guide's framework, in order.
**Answer: First-level (primary), second-level (secondary), and neutral colours.**
