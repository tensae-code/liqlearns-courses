# Video script — The Primary Colours

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Three colours are first.
Three colours are pure.
Nobody mixed them.

**Red. Yellow. Blue.**
**መስራች** — the primary colours.

The song calls them mother and father.
Why? Every other colour is born from them.

Find them! In balloons. In glasses.
In clothes. In your home.
Red. Yellow. Blue.
