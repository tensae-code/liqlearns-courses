# Handmade Cards and Gifts

## Do it first

Start with the first part below — do it, sing it or make it before you read any explanation.

## Sub lesson 1 — Part II: Мэндчилгээний загвар хийх (Making greeting cards)

Part II: Мэндчилгээний загвар хийх (Making greeting cards) — a folded card with a flower and leaf cut on the fold and glued inside, "I love my mother" written for mother, father, siblings or friends; a birthday card for a friend; round New Year greeting cards decorated inside and tied with ribbon

## Sub lesson 2 — Part II: Төрөл бүрийн материал ашиглах (Using all kinds of materials)

Part II: Төрөл бүрийн материал ашиглах (Using all kinds of materials) — bookmarks: a folded oblong bookmark decorated with shiny beads and a ribbon, glued at the book's top; a fancy bookmark made as a keepsake for a friend; bookmarks for the class library's books; and paperclip bookmarks tied with ribbon bows and beaded
