# Lesson plan — Handmade Cards and Gifts

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Visual Art · **Lesson:** Handmade Cards and Gifts
**Place in the curriculum:** Visual Art (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** making greeting cards and keepsakes by hand — folded cards for mother, father, siblings and friends, birthday cards, New Year cards and bookmarks made as gifts.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the learner's earlier art and music lessons. Recommended, never forced.
