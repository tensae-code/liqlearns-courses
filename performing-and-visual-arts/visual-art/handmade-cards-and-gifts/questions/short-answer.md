# Handmade Cards and Gifts — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1.** For whom can the folded card be made?
**Answer: Mother, father, siblings or friends.**

**2.** Name two bookmark kinds from Grade 2.
**Answer: Any two of: a folded oblong bookmark with beads and ribbon; a fancy keepsake bookmark; bookmarks for the class library; paperclip bookmarks with ribbon bows and beads.**
