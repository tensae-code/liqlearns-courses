# Handmade Cards and Gifts — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** The Grade 1 greeting card carries a flower and leaf…
a) cut on the fold and glued inside the folded card  b) bought from a shop  c) printed by a machine
**Answer: a) cut on the fold and glued inside the folded card**

**2.** The card's words in the book are…
a) "I love my mother" — written for mother, father, siblings or friends  b) a shopping list  c) a timetable
**Answer: a) "I love my mother" — written for mother, father, siblings or friends**

**3.** Bookmarks in Grade 2 are made as…
a) keepsakes for a friend, and for the class library's books  b) hats  c) kites
**Answer: a) keepsakes for a friend, and for the class library's books**
