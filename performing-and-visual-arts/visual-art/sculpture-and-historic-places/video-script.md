# Video script — Sculpture and Historic Places

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Walk around this figure. Keep walking… all the way around.
You can. It stands free. That is sculpture **in the round**.

Now this one. You cannot walk around it — it lives on a flat wall.
Raised here, lowered there. That is **relief**.

Four ways make sculpture.
**Modelling** — adding clay.
**Carving** — cutting away.
**Assembling** — joining things together.
**Casting** — pouring into a mould.

Watch the candle. Melt it in the cup. Let it set.
Lift it out — the candle holds the cup's shape!
Pour, set, lift. One form… many copies. That is casting.

Stone towers. Rock churches. Castles.
Our historic places stand where we can walk around them too.
Now you — find one sculpture near you, and walk around it slowly.
