# Sculpture and Historic Places — true or false

Say if each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Sculpture in the round has height, length and depth, and is viewed from all sides. **Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Assembling can give used-up materials a new message as one unified work. **Answer: True.**

**3. (Sub lesson 6)** Coins, tools, weapons and jewellery can all be artifacts that give clues about past civilisations. **Answer: True.**

**4.** (Sub lesson 7) The Yekatit 12 martyrs' monument stands at Sidist Kilo. **Answer: True.**

**5.** (Sub lesson 10) Axum coins carry the king's portrait ringed by ears of barley or wheat. **Answer: True.**

**6.** (Sub lesson 11) The Menelik monument at Piassa is made of bronze. **Answer: True.**
