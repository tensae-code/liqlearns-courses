# Sculpture and Historic Places — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Appreciating a sculpture, the book asks you to —
a) say what made you happy looking at the work  b) guess its weight  c) never walk around it
**Answer: a) say what made you happy looking at the work.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A relief sculpture is seen —
a) from all sides  b) from the front only — raised and lowered shapes on a flat surface  c) only from above
**Answer: b) from the front only.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which is NOT one of sculpture's four techniques?
a) modelling  b) assembling  c) photographing
**Answer: c) photographing** — the four are modelling, carving, assembling and casting.

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** In casting, the mould (ሞልድ) holds the form's —
a) positive image  b) negative image  c) photograph
**Answer: b) negative image** — the cast lifted from it is the positive.

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** The biggest papier-mâché mistake is —
a) tearing the strip edges  b) painting before it is dry all the way through  c) using newspaper
**Answer: b) painting too soon** — sealed-in moisture grows mould.

**6. (Sub lesson 6)** A monument is —
a) any old stone  b) a structure explicitly created to commemorate a person or event  c) a kind of coin
**Answer: b)** — the Tiya and Aksum stelae are examples.
