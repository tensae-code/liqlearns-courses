# Sculpture and Historic Places — short answer

Answer in a word or a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In the book's activity, how do you record a historic place you know? **Answer: draw it in pencil and colour it (and show it).**

**2. (Sub lesson 4)** In the book's simple example, a melted candle set in a cup comes out holding what? **Answer: the cup's form (shape).**

**3. (Sub lesson 5)** Give the sculptor's road in the teacher guides, in order. **Answer: sketch → clay maquette → making a mould → casting.**
