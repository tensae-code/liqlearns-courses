# Sculpture and Historic Places — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number, as before. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. The sub lessons climb from appreciating, through the two kinds and the four techniques, to casting in full — then two evidence parts (papier-mâché; monuments and artifacts) that widen the learner's map of sculpture.

## Sub lesson 1 — Appreciating sculpture and historic places
Pass mark: say what made you happy in a sculpture work, and draw a historic place you know in pencil and colour.

## Sub lesson 2 — Round and relief
Pass mark: tell ዙረያ ገባ (in the round — walked around, all sides) from ሪሊፍ (relief — front only, raised/lowered on a flat surface), and name sculpture materials.

## Sub lesson 3 — Four ways to make sculpture
Pass mark: name modelling, carving, assembling and casting, and give assembling's process (decide, join in order, keep with care).

## Sub lesson 4 — Casting: one form, many copies
Pass mark: give the gypsum sequence in order and use the words mould (ሞልድ), negative and positive correctly.

## Sub lesson 5 — Sculpture with clay and papier-mâché (teacher-guide evidence)
Pass mark: give the five steps — armature, make the material, apply, dry all the way through, paint and seal — and name the too-soon-painting mistake.

## Sub lesson 6 — Monuments and artifacts (teacher-guide evidence)
Pass mark: define artifact (any item made or given shape) and monument (built to commemorate a person or event), with the Tiya and Aksum stelae as examples.
