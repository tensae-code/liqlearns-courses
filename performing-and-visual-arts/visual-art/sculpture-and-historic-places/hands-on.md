# Hands-on — Sculpture and Historic Places

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Walk-around looking
Find any free-standing made thing — a pot, a stool, a figure. Walk slowly all the way around it and name one thing you can only see from the back. Then hold a flat picture of it and say what the walking gave you that the picture cannot.

## Assemble an animal
Gather wire, reeds, carton, coloured paper, cloth and string. Decide your animal first. Join the materials in the wanted order until it stands as one work. Keep it somewhere safe, with care — and be ready to say how each material earns its place.

## The candle cast (with an adult)
An adult melts a candle in a cup. Let it set fully. Lift the set candle out and hold the cup beside it: the candle holds the cup's form. Say the casting words as you point: the cup was the mould; the candle is the cast.

## Draw a historic place
Choose a historic or old place you know. Draw it in pencil first, then colour it. Write under it — in your head or on the back — what made you happy looking at it.
