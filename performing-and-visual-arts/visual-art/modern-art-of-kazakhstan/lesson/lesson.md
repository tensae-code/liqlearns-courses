## Sub lesson 6 — Kazakhstan Grade 7 NIS Girls Artistic Labour 2025 student book

what it teaches: modern movements in Kazakhstan's fine art — under Abai Kunanbaev's words «Искусство правдиво тогда, когда оно слито с правдой жизни» («Art is truthful when it is merged with the truth of life»); in a short historical span a whole pleiad of artists appeared working in different directions, turning to cultural memory and the values of traditional culture, at whose base lie the Saka and Turkic ideas of the world's unity; contemporary art of Kazakhstan is a unique phenomenon joining traditions and innovations — the mixing of local culture and history with global tendencies creates a unique artistic language, and the art not only reflects the country's cultural heritage but reacts to society's social and political processes; its many-sidedness runs from painting and sculpture to video-art and street art (Suleimenova's «Келин и женге», Akanaev's «Саукеле», Asylgazina's «Аударыспақ» IV, Turgynbai's «Бабалар» pictured); «Человек, который любит жить» — Karipbek Kuyukov, the famous Kazakhstani artist who draws holding the brush in his mouth and with his toes, a victim of the nuclear tests at the Semipalatinsk polygon and an activist of the International Movement for closing it, whose works were exhibited in Kazakhstan, the USA, Japan, Germany, Czechia, Switzerland and elsewhere, and whose «Взрыв» President Nursultan Nazarbayev presented to Barack Obama at the 2016 Washington nuclear-security summit; Kuanysh Bazargaliev, the contemporary Kazakhstani artist whose work concentrates around the search for national identity, showing its visual lexicon through paintings and mixed media productions — read his «Қошқармүйіз» composition, its known symbols and signs, its Kazakh ornament and its colours

## Sub lesson 7 — Kazakhstan Grade 7 NIS Girls Artistic Labour 2025 teacher guide

what it teaches: the guide's factual material — with Kazakhstan's gaining sovereignty its fine art entered a new stage of development: new forms began to develop — painting, graphics, sculpture and decorative-applied art; Kazakhstani contemporary art became very diverse and at times ambiguous, and in the last time it became recognisable abroad too; domestic artists demonstrate the boundless possibilities of artistic materials, techniques and technologies, using complex combinations of materials, engaging in the search for new paths in one of the classical kinds of art: in painting A.
