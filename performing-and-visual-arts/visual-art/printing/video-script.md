# Video script — Printing

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Press your finger here. Lift it. Look — your print!
Print again. And again. Many pictures from one thing. That is **printing**.

First, your palm. **Choose** a colour.
**Paint** your whole palm. Cover it well.
**Press** it flat. Hold it… **lift straight up!**
There it is — your hand, on the paper.
Again! Another colour! A circle of hands! A heart of hands!

Now a leaf. A fresh, juicy leaf.
Paint its face. Lay it down. **Press** — carefully, all over.
Lift… the veins came too! The leaf printed its bones.

A potato can print. Cut it. Draw on the flat face.
Carve away everything that is NOT your picture.
Careful — carve it **backwards**! The print will turn it around.

And a stencil: cut a hole, dab the colour through, lift — clean!

Paint it. Press it. Lift it. Again!
Now you — what will you print first?
