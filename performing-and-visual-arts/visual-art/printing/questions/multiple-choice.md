# Printing — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Printing means —
a) drawing one picture once  b) repeating a thing's image many times  c) colouring inside lines
**Answer: b) repeating a thing's image many times.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** After painting your palm, you —
a) wave it dry  b) press it flat on the paper and lift straight up  c) rub it on the paper side to side
**Answer: b) press it flat and lift straight up** — sliding smears the print.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** For leaf printing, choose leaves that are —
a) dry and curled  b) fresh and juicy, of different shapes  c) torn in half
**Answer: b) fresh and juicy, of different shapes.**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Which of these can print as a found thing?
a) a bottle cap  b) a sound  c) a smell
**Answer: a) a bottle cap** — flowers, hands, fingers and shoes print too.

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** In block printing, the part that prints is —
a) gouged away  b) left raised  c) painted last
**Answer: b) left raised** — the unwanted part is gouged away; the seal/stamp works the same way.

**6. (Sub lesson 6)** In stencil printing, the colour passes —
a) over the whole sheet evenly  b) through the cut-out holes, dabbed with sponge or cloth  c) under the paper
**Answer: b) through the cut-out holes.**

**7.** (Sub lesson 7) The book's sharp-tool rules say you must never —
a) sharpen a tool  b) share or pass sharp tools  c) carve wood
**Answer: b) share or pass sharp tools.**
