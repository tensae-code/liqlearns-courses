# Printing — matching

Match each printing way to its key move or rule.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** direct printing — **paint the thing's surface and press it straight on**
**2. (Sub lesson 2)** palm printing — **paint the palm, press flat, lift straight up**
**3. (Sub lesson 3)** leaf printing — **press the painted leaf carefully, then lift — veins print too**
**4. (Sub lesson 4)** found-thing printing — **dip or paint a found material and print its own mark**
**5. (Sub lesson 5)** block printing — **carve reversed: raised prints, gouged rests**
**6. (Sub lesson 6)** silk-screen — **colour pushed through silk on a frame, holes blocked opposite the design**
