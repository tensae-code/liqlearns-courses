# Printing — short answer

Answer in a word or a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Direct printing can be done onto paper and what else? **Answer: a wall (paper, wall and so on; also cloth and wood).**

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** What must you check about a leaf before using it for printing? **Answer: that it is even (and fresh and juicy).**

**3. (Sub lesson 4)** Name two simple tools of printing work besides the thing that prints. **Answer: any two of — flat brush, thick colour, water for thinning, flat table, mixing board, hand cloth or sponge, paper.**
