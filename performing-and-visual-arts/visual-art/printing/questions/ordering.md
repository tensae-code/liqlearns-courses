# Printing — ordering

Put each set in the right order.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Palm printing: press the painted palm flat — choose the colours — lift straight up — paint your palm.
**Answer: choose the colours → paint your palm → press the painted palm flat → lift straight up.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Making designs comes: after single palm prints — before ever touching paint — instead of printing.
**Answer: after single palm prints** — first print single palms, then arrange prints into circle, square and heart designs.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Repeat printing: print in one colour — lift the print to check it — paint the palm in a different colour — print again beside the first.
**Answer: print in one colour → lift the print to check it → paint the palm in a different colour → print again beside the first.**
