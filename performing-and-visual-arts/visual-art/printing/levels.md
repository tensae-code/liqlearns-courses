# Printing — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number, as before. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. The sub lessons climb from the definition of printing through the body's own block (the palm), nature's block (the leaf), found blocks, carved blocks with the mirror rule, and stencils up to batik and silk-screen.

## Sub lesson 1 — Direct printing
Pass mark: define printing and direct printing, and name things that print directly (leaves, hand, foot, potato, tyre, wood, paper).

## Sub lesson 2 — Palm printing step by step
Pass mark: give the steps in order — choose colours, paint the palm, press flat, lift straight up, repeat in different colours — and name the circle, square and heart designs prints can form.

## Sub lesson 3 — Leaf printing
Pass mark: give the leaf steps — fresh juicy leaves, check even, paint the surface, lay, press carefully, lift — and say the veins print too.

## Sub lesson 4 — Printing with found things
Pass mark: name found things that print (flowers, bottle caps, hands and fingers, shoes) and the simple tools of the work.

## Sub lesson 5 — Block printing: carve it backwards
Pass mark: state the mirror rule and the raised-prints rule, and give the potato-printing steps in order.

## Sub lesson 6 — Stencil printing, and printing grows up
Pass mark: give the stencil steps, say how three-colour stencilling works, and explain batik (wax resists dye) and silk-screen (colour through blocked silk) in one sentence each.
