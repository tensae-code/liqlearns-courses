# Hands-on — Printing

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Palm-print wreath
Paint your palm, press it flat on paper, lift straight up. Turn the paper a little and print again, in a new colour, until your prints make a circle wreath. Then try a square and a heart the same way.

## Leaf-print cloth
Collect fresh leaves of different shapes. Paint one leaf's surface, lay it on paper or cloth, press carefully all over, and lift. Repeat with the same leaf to make a decoration or a gift card. Compare: which leaf prints its veins best?

## Found-thing pattern
Gather a bottle cap, a flower, a piece of tyre or wood, a shoe. Paint each one and print its mark in a row. Under each print, write or say the thing's name — every surface signs its own name.

## Potato stamp (with an adult)
An adult helps with the cutting. Cut a potato in half, draw a simple design on the cut face — drawn backwards — gouge away everything outside it, paint the raised design, and stamp a repeating pattern on paper or cloth.
