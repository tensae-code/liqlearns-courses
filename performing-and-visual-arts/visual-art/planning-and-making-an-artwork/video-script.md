# Video script — Planning and Making an Artwork

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This is Indonesia's own lesson.

**Bab V Berkarya Seni Dua Dimensi dan Tiga Dimensi.** the logical, ordered steps of making a work — decide the work, choose technique, tool and material, then make it — for two-dimensional and three-dimensional art.

**Units I–IV, Kegiatan 2–3.** the maker cycle used in every unit — design the craft product first (Desain Produk Kerajinan), then make it (Membuat Kerajinan).

That is Planning and Making an Artwork — Indonesia's way, kept by name.
