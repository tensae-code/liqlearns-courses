# Planning and Making an Artwork

## Do it first

Name one thing that belongs in this lesson — Planning an artwork step by step — the art project from first idea through its stages to a finished 2D or 3D work. Say what it is in your own words. If you can name one, you are already inside the lesson.

## Sub lesson 1 — Bab V Berkarya Seni Dua Dimensi dan Tiga Dimensi

the logical, ordered steps of making a work — decide the work, choose technique, tool and material, then make it — for two-dimensional and three-dimensional art.

## Sub lesson 2 — Units I–IV, Kegiatan 2–3

the maker cycle used in every unit — design the craft product first (Desain Produk Kerajinan), then make it (Membuat Kerajinan).

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson holds, part by part, in your own words. Companion checks: did every part get named, with its Indonesian name kept where the book gives one?

## Do It Yourself

1. Choose one named part of this lesson and find out one more true thing about it — from a person who knows it, or from the book your part came from.
2. Make or perform a small piece in the way of that part: a rhythm, a step, a drawing, a shaped piece of clay.
3. Write one sentence: what does this part show about Indonesia's own way of teaching its arts?
