# Hands-on — Nature in Art

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## One meeting, one picture
Draw one meeting of a person and nature: a farmer and the field, a fisher and the river, a child and a tree. Show both sides of the relationship in the one picture.

## Say the relationship
Tell a partner, in one sentence, what your picture says about how the human being and nature belong to each other.
