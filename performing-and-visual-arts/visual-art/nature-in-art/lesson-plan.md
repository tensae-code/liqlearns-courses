# Lesson plan — Nature in Art

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Visual Art · **Lesson:** Nature in Art
**Place in the curriculum:** Visual Art (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** recreating the relationship between the human being and nature through artworks — taught at theme level, because the Grade 4 activity pages are not in the PARTIAL file.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this course in the same title.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Humans and nature, together** — Guatemala's Grade 4 experience “Las manos creadoras” (The Creator Hands) sets this theme: **the relationship between the human being and nature**, recreated **through artworks**.
2. **Recreating the relationship in an artwork** — To **recreate** the relationship is to make it again in a work: draw, paint or build a scene where the human being and nature meet — hands working the land, a community under its trees, a river and the people it carries.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of this course in the same title. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say the theme the Grade 4 experience sets — the relationship between the human being and nature, recreated through artworks.
2. Make a picture that shows a human being and nature meeting, and say the relationship it carries.

## What you need
Paper and colours.
