# Nature in Art — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The theme is taught in Guatemala's Grade 4 Expresión Artística.
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The theme asks for pictures of nature with the human being left out.
