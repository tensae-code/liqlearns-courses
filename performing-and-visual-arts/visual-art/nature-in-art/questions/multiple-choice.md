# Nature in Art — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Grade 4 theme joins…
a) the human being and nature  b) only machines  c) only cities
**Answer: a) the human being and nature.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The experience that sets the theme is called…
a) Las manos creadoras — The Creator Hands  b) Publicarte  c) Mi cuerpo habla
**Answer: a) Las manos creadoras — The Creator Hands.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The relationship is recreated through…
a) artworks  b) tests  c) speeches only
**Answer: a) artworks.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** To recreate the relationship in a work means…
a) to make it again in a drawing, painting or built scene  b) to copy a photograph exactly  c) to write an essay only
**Answer: a) to make it again in a drawing, painting or built scene.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** A picture that fits the theme shows…
a) a human being meeting nature  b) nature with no people and no meeting  c) a blank page
**Answer: a) a human being meeting nature.**
