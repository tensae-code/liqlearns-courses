# Nature in Art — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What relationship does the theme name?
**Answer: The relationship between the human being and nature.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Give one example scene that recreates the relationship.
**Answer: Any scene where a person and nature meet — e.g. a farmer and the field, a fisher and the river, a child and a tree.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Why is this lesson labelled theme level?
**Answer: Because the PARTIAL file holds the experience's opening page naming the theme; its activity pages are not in the file.**
