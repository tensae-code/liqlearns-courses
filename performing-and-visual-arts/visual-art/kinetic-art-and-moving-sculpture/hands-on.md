# Hands-on — Kinetic Art and Moving Sculpture

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## A fluttering animal
Bend wire into an animal's body. Tape on pieces of coloured collage paper as wings, ears or a tail, loosely, so they can move. Hold it in a current of air and watch which parts flutter.

## Hanging or standing?
Make your sculpture twice in drawing: once hanging, once standing, like Calder's two pictured kinds. Say which one moves more freely.
