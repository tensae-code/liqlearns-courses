# Kinetic Art and Moving Sculpture — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A moving sculpture is a sculpture whose parts…
a) move at the slightest breath of wind  b) are glued fast forever  c) are hidden in a box
**Answer: a) move at the slightest breath of wind**.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Calder's pictured moving sculptures are called…
a) mobiles  b) initials  c) ex libris
**Answer: a) mobiles**.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The book's animal sculpture is made from…
a) wire, sticky tape and coloured collage paper  b) stone and bronze only  c) watercolour on paper
**Answer: a) wire, sticky tape and coloured collage paper**.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The animal's parts must be able to…
a) flutter in a current of air  b) sing  c) light up by themselves
**Answer: a) flutter in a current of air**.
