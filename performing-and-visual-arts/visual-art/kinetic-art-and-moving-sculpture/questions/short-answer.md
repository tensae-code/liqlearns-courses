# Kinetic Art and Moving Sculpture — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In which century did artists take up this new kind of work?
**Answer: In the 20th century.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What two forms of Calder's mobiles are pictured?
**Answer: Hanging and standing mobiles.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What moves the parts of a moving sculpture?
**Answer: The air — the slightest breath of wind, a current of air.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** What animal will your moving sculpture show?
**Answer: An animal of your own choice — planned so its parts can flutter.**
