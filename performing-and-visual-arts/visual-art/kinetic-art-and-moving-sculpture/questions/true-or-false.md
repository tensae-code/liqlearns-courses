# Kinetic Art and Moving Sculpture — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A moving sculpture's parts never move.
**Answer: False** — they move at the slightest breath of wind.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Calder made mobiles.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The fluttering movement is planned into the sculpture from the start.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Moving sculptures can only be made of stone.
**Answer: False** — the book's own is made of wire, tape and collage paper.
