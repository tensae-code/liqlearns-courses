## Sub lesson 1 — Sculptures that move

During the 20th century artists took up new ways of artistic work. One new kind of artwork is **sculptures of different materials that move — their parts move at the slightest breath of wind** — and such works are called **moving sculptures**. The book pictures **Calder's mobiles, hanging and standing**.

## Sub lesson 2 — Make a sculpture that flutters

The book's making task: **using wire, sticky tape and pieces of coloured collage paper, make a sculpture of an animal of your choice — but such that its parts can flutter in a current of air.** The movement is not added afterwards; the sculpture is planned so the air itself can move it.
