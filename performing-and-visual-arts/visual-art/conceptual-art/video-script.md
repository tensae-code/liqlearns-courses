This is North Macedonia's own lesson.

**Концептуална уметност.** Концептуална уметност — the art of ideas (1960s): the concept before the object's look and material, against institutions and art's commercialisation — Kosuth's "One and Three Chairs" (object, photograph, dictionary definition), LeWitt's wall drawings made by others from his instructions, Weiner's text itself as the artwork.

**Концептуална уметност.** Концептуална уметност — Conceptual art: the idea is the work — the classic art object dematerialised into action, photograph and text — Kosuth, Beuys.

That is Conceptual Art — North Macedonia's way, kept by name.
