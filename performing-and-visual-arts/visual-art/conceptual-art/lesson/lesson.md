# Conceptual Art

## Do it first

Name one thing that belongs in this lesson — Conceptual art — art where the idea matters more than the object made. Say what it is in your own words. If you can name one, you are already inside the lesson.

## Sub lesson 1 — Концептуална уметност

Концептуална уметност — the art of ideas (1960s): the concept before the object's look and material, against institutions and art's commercialisation — Kosuth's "One and Three Chairs" (object, photograph, dictionary definition), LeWitt's wall drawings made by others from his instructions, Weiner's text itself as the artwork.

## Sub lesson 2 — Концептуална уметност

Концептуална уметност — Conceptual art: the idea is the work — the classic art object dematerialised into action, photograph and text — Kosuth, Beuys.

## Say it to your companion

## Do It Yourself

1. Choose one named part of this lesson and find out one more true thing about it — from a person who knows it, or from the book your part came from.
2. Make or perform a small piece in the way of that part: a rhythm, a step, a drawing, a shaped piece of clay.
3. Write one sentence: what does this part show about North Macedonia's own way of teaching its arts?
