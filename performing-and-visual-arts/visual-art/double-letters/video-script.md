# Video script — Double Letters


The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.


---


One letter. One line. Now — a **second** line, walking around it. That is **double writing**.

It wobbles? The book gives two helps.

First: **squared paper**. The little squares measure every letter.

Second: **two guide lines**. The letter rests between them — not too high, not too low.

Write slowly. Exchange. Correct freely. That is how double letters grow true.
