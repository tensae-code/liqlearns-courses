# Double Letters — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number, as before. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — Double writing on squared paper
Pass mark: say that squared paper suits double-letter practice best, produce double letters on squared paper, and take part in the exchange — commenting on a companion's work, separating strong and weak sides, correcting faults freely.

## Sub lesson 2 — Double writing between guide lines
Pass mark: rule two parallel guide lines and write letters and numerals between them, kept from sitting too high or too low; look at finished double writing and point out letters' differing sizes, widths and structures.
