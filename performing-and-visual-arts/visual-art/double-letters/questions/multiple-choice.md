# Double Letters — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** For double-letter practice, the book says the best paper is —
a) plain paper  b) squared paper  c) parchment
**Answer: b) squared paper** — squared paper suits double-letter practice best (§2.2.3.1).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** After working on squared paper, pupils —
a) throw the work away  b) exchange comments, separating strong and weak sides and correcting faults freely  c) copy the teacher's page
**Answer: b) exchange comments, separating strong and weak sides and correcting faults freely** — the peer exchange is part of the method.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Two parallel guide lines keep letters and numerals —
a) from being coloured in  b) from sitting too high or too low  c) from being read aloud
**Answer: b) from sitting too high or too low** — head and foot rest in their places between the lines.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The book's guide-lines plate shows letters' differing —
a) colours only  b) sizes, widths and structures  c) prices
**Answer: b) sizes, widths and structures** — each letter has its own size, width and structure.
