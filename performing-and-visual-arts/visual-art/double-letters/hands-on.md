# Hands-on — Double Letters

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Squared-paper doubles
On squared paper, write three letters and two numerals in double writing. Let the squares measure you: count the squares each letter spans, and keep twin lines one steady distance apart.

## Between the guide lines
Rule two parallel guide lines on plain paper. Write letters and numerals resting between them — head and foot in their places, nothing too high, nothing too low. This is the book's Exercise 8 ground: do it until the row sits even.

## The exchange
Swap work with a companion. Comment freely: separate the strong sides from the weak sides, then each of you corrects one fault on your own page.
