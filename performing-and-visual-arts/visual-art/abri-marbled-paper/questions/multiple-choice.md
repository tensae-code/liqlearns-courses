# Abri Marbled Paper — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Abri is…
a) a traditional marbled paper  b) a kind of calligraphy  c) a woven cloth
**Answer: a) a traditional marbled paper**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The oil colours are thinned with…
a) water  b) oil  c) glue
**Answer: b) oil**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The colour drops float on…
a) the paper  b) water in a wide tray  c) a brush
**Answer: b) water in a wide tray**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** The pattern reaches the paper when you…
a) paint it on with a brush  b) lay the paper on the water and lift it off  c) press the tray onto the paper
**Answer: b) lay the paper on the water and lift it off**
