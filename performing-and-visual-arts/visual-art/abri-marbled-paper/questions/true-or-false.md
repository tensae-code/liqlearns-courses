# Abri Marbled Paper — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Only one colour is used in abri.
**Answer: False** — drops of two or three colours are floated on the water.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The colours are floated with a brush.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The paper is laid under the water.
**Answer: False** — it is laid on the water's surface and lifted off with the pattern on it.
