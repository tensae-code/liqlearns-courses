# Hands-on — Abri Marbled Paper

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Tray check
Look at a wide tray of water. Say where the colour will float, and why the tray must be wide.

## Paper abri (drawing first)
On paper, draw a sheet covered in a swirling marble pattern of two or three colours, the way a finished abri looks. Label the steps under it: float, lay, lift, dry.
