# Video script — Abri Marbled Paper

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Colour — on water!

Thin the oil colours. Drop them on the water — two or three colours, floating.
Lay the paper down. Lift it up — the marble is on the sheet!

Abri — the paper that wears the water's pattern.
