# Abri Marbled Paper

## Do it first

Quick game: say what you already know about this lesson's subject — one thing, out loud. This lesson names it properly.

## Sub lesson 1 — What abri is

**Abri** is the **traditional marbled paper**. The finished sheet is a **swirling, many-coloured marble** — the pattern is not drawn on the paper; it is made on water and lifted onto the paper.

## Sub lesson 2 — Floating the colour

**Thin the oil colours with oil.** With a **brush**, float **drops of two or three colours** on **water in a wide tray**. The colours stay on the surface, spreading into one another.

## Sub lesson 3 — Laying, lifting and drying

**Lay the paper on the water's surface.** **Lift it off** — the colour pattern is on the paper now. **Dry the sheet.** That is the finished abri: a swirling many-coloured marble.
