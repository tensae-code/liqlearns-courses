# Lesson plan — Abri Marbled Paper

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Visual Art · **Lesson:** Abri Marbled Paper
**Place in the curriculum:** Visual Art (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** abri — the traditional marbled paper: oil colours thinned with oil are floated as drops on water in a wide tray, paper is laid on the water's surface and lifted off with the colour pattern on it, and the finished sheet is a swirling many-coloured marble.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this course.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **What abri is** — the traditional marbled paper — a swirling, many-coloured marble pattern on a sheet
2. **Floating the colour** — oil colours thinned with oil; drops of two or three colours floated on water in a wide tray with a brush
3. **Laying, lifting and drying** — the paper laid on the water's surface, lifted off with the pattern on it, and dried

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier Visual Art lessons of this course. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say what abri is and what the finished sheet looks like.
2. Tell how the colour is prepared and floated: thinned with oil, dropped on water with a brush.
3. Say the finishing steps in order: lay the paper on the surface, lift it off with the pattern, dry it.

## What you need
A wide tray of water, oil colours, oil to thin them, a brush, and paper.
