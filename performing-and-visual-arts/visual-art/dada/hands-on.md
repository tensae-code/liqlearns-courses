# Hands-on — Dada

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Book 1, Artistic Culture, "The Dada School"
Do the thing this part teaches, with real materials where the book names them: Dada — born 1917 from the First World War's tragedies, youth under a nonsense name found by chance in a dictionary, the wooden hobby-horse, as a sign of the void they lived: nothing has meaning, not even art; agreement against ethics, religion, philosophy and every corrupt value that disables freedom; depiction freed from all restriction, beauty arriving spontaneously — Duchamp's word that the age of depicting things themselves is gone, and his turn from Cubism to anti-art, with collage among its methods
