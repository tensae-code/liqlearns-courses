# Dada — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Dada was born in 1917 from…
a) the First World War's tragedies  b) a royal decree  c) a new pigment
**Answer: a) the First World War's tragedies**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The name Dada was found…
a) by chance in a dictionary  b) in a king's name  c) on a map
**Answer: a) by chance in a dictionary** — a nonsense name — the wooden hobby-horse.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Dada agreed against…
a) ethics, religion, philosophy and every corrupt value  b) colour  c) drawing
**Answer: a) ethics, religion, philosophy and every corrupt value** — every corrupt value that disables freedom.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Duchamp turned from Cubism to…
a) anti-art  b) academic art  c) portraiture
**Answer: a) anti-art** — his word: the age of depicting things themselves is gone.
