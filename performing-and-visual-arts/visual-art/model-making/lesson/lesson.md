# Model Making

## Do it first

Name a star picture in the sky — now imagine throwing YOUR OWN star picture onto your wall. You can build the machine that does it, from a milk carton.

## Sub lesson 1 — Building a star projector

The Finnish teacher guide builds the projector in steps 1–4:

1. **Cut both ends off a milk carton.**
2. **Draw your own or a known constellation on black card**, marking the stars with **pencil dots**.
3. **Prick small holes at the dots** with a compass point or needle.
4. **Tape the card over the carton end** so **no light leaks at the edges**.

The child is the star artist: each child draws their own constellation and builds the projector that throws it on the wall.

## Sub lesson 2 — Projecting and experimenting

**Shine a phone torch into the open end** in a **dim room** — your constellation appears **on wall or ceiling**. Then **experiment**: moving **nearer or farther** changes the picture's **size and sharpness**, and **new constellation cards** can be swapped in.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson taught, part by part. Companion checks each part against the sub lessons above.

## Do It Yourself

1. Make your own piece using this lesson's way, from start to finish.
2. Show it to the class and say what you made, how, and what you learnt.
3. Write one sentence: what will you do differently next time?
