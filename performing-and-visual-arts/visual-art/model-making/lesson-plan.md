# Lesson plan — Model Making

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Visual Art · **Lesson:** Model Making
**Place in the curriculum:** Visual Art (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** model making — building a working model from everyday materials: here, a star projector built from a milk carton that throws your own constellation on the wall.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier making and drawing lessons of this course.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Building a star projector** — cut both ends off a milk carton, draw your own or a known constellation on black card marking the stars with pencil dots, prick small holes at the dots, and tape the card over the carton end so no light leaks.
2. **Projecting and experimenting** — shine a phone torch into the open end in a dim room so your constellation appears on wall or ceiling, and test how moving nearer or farther changes the picture's size and sharpness, swapping in new constellation cards.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier drawing and making lessons of this course. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say how the star projector is built, step by step.
2. Say what moving nearer or farther does to the projected picture.

## What you need
A milk carton, black card, pencil, a compass point or needle (grown-up's job), tape, a phone torch and a dim room.
