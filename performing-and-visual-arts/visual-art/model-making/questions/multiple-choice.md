# Model Making — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The projector body is made from…
a) a milk carton with both ends cut off  b) a shoe  c) a bottle only
**Answer: a) a milk carton with both ends cut off**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The stars are marked on black card with…
a) paint  b) pencil dots, then pricked as small holes  c) stickers only
**Answer: b) pencil dots, then pricked as small holes**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The card is taped so that…
a) it falls off  b) no light leaks at the edges  c) it spins
**Answer: b) no light leaks at the edges**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Moving nearer or farther changes the picture's…
a) colour only  b) size and sharpness  c) smell
**Answer: b) size and sharpness**
