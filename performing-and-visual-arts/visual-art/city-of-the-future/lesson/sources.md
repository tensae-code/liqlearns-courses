| Kazakhstan — 4.1–4.2. Болашақтың қаласының тұжырымдамасы (The city of the future's concept) — what it teaches: the idea about the future's flawless city (мінсіз қала) has worried people for years; attempts to build a flawless city were made many times: in the XVIII century in France Claude Nicolas Ledoux (Клод Николя Леду) would build a garden-city (бақша-қала), in the XX century the famous architect Le Corbusier (Ле Корбюзье) came with the flawless city concept intended for a flawless society; but they did not reach their purpose; meanwhile, as time passed, the question of developing the city further became more topical: the land face's change, the population number's growth, people's moving to the city, production processes' complication, ecology and transport problems — these showed that today's cities cannot satisfy human needs; creating new principles of settling people, the idea of designing city space arose from itself: the future city's concepts — «Жасыл қала» тұжырымдамасы (the «Green City» concept): in such projects attention is paid that the city takes all the energy it needs from renewable sources; «Қала-үй» тұжырымдамасы (the «City-House» concept): in one building all the conditions needed for human life are characteristic; from the XX century's 90s the Smart City тұжырымдамасы (concept) became famous and began to be realised; China's Yinchuan city (Инчуань қаласы) is a city where you can live without holding money in your hand — this only city is the world's most «smart» city: in this city a system is placed paying only by a person's face image, recognised by a special apparatus (Азия... through mobile additions purchases can be made); local administration buildings' roofs... holograms work instead of secretaries (?); Japan's Fujisawa city (Фудзисава қаласы) — from 2016 all the houses use the sun's power, people ride only electric cars and bicycles; in the city sensor systems are placed, so the street light turns on only when a person walks; the 2018 list of the world's smartest cities: Copenhagen, Singapore, Stockholm, Zurich, Boston, Tokyo, San Francisco, Amsterdam, Geneva, Melbourne entered the world's «ең ақылды қалалары» (smartest cities) count; Kazakhstan's capital Nur-Sultan also began entering the «Ақылды қала» (Smart City) concept; such cities' evaluation's main criteria: 4G mobile connection; free Wi-Fi points' spreading; universal smartphones managed by sensors; smart vehicle stops (ақылды көлік тұрағы); vehicle sharing services (каршеринг); the transport movement's optimal system; state services' offer in an online system; waste's recycling (қоқыс қалдықтарын қайта өңдеу); city inhabitants' active citizen position; ecologically clean energy sources (student book; original target `visual-art/the-city-of-the-future-concept`) | Book 3, printed p. 126 |
| Kazakhstan — 4.3. Болашақтың қаласы. Жобалармен танысу (The city of the future. Meeting the projects) — what it teaches: on our planet cities with million inhabitants named «City of the Future» («болашақтың қаласы») increase every year; what must «the city of the future» be? Specialists of varied fields constantly seek an answer to this question; in connection with technology's development and solving ecological problems, taking social questions in the future into account beforehand, in the architecture field conceptual projects (тұжырымдамалық жобалар) are often made in various competitions; in such projects the city's topical problems' most good innovative ideas find appearance; future-city projects can be divided into several kinds by their placement in the city's space: Көк тіреген қалалар (sky-propping cities) — the main idea is to free land by building many-storey towers (көпқабатты зәулім үйлер) through vertical building, and to use the freed land place for agriculture and greening (such projects are characteristic of the Netherlands and Japan); Жерасты қалалары (underground cities) — a new phenomenon in architecture history; as its example the underground cities preserved in Cappadocia (Каппадокия, Turkey) can be named; Су бетіндегі қалалар (cities on the water's face) — the global warming problem and useful food shortage bring the idea that the city must be built on water's face; Әуеде қалқыған қалалар (cities floating in the air) — great discoveries and new searches in mastering air space propose, and show that human possibility and thought-fantasy are boundless; Ғарыштағы қалалар (cities in space); the concepts' main directions can be shown so: 1. Технологиялық бағыт (the technological direction) — using engineering, materials and science's newest achievements; 2. Ақпараттық бағыт (the informational direction) — introducing Smart City («ақылды қала») technology into city infrastructure; 3. Экологиялық бағыт (the ecological direction) — creating the possibility to live in a maximally convenient state not polluting the atmosphere and human life; likewise: Тау-қала (a mountain-city), Форма-қала (?), Гаджет-қала (a gadget-city — this is Smart City), Биорганикалық-қала (a bio-organic city — a city designed by bionics from ecologically clean natural materials); Vincent Callebaut (Винсент Каллебаут) is a Belgian architect; his creative ideas are directed to solving the problems standing before humanity; V. Callebaut's famous out-of-the-ordinary project is «Lilypad» («Су бетіндегі қала» — the City on the Water's Face): its outer form resembles a water-lily's leaf (су лалагүлінің жапырағына ұқсайды); the first Smart City projects arose in the XX century's 90s in connection with IT technology's development: Yinchuan (China) — the world's road... a city not needing money and bank cards: instead of them a person's face image is suitable; a special apparatus distinguishes the face image and takes money from the account in the face's (?) place; Fujisawa (Japan) — all the buildings use only the sun's power, together with vehicles only electric cars and bicycles are used; Milton Keynes (Great Britain) — a city living by the Catapult Transport Systems programme; Masdar (UAE) — a city working with the sun's energy sources (student book; original target `visual-art/the-city-of-the-future-concept`) | Book 3, printed p. 130 |
| Kazakhstan — 4.12. Болашақтың қаласы. Архитектуралық нысандар жасау (Jacque Fresco and the City in the Sky) — what it teaches: futuristic projects, by introducing science and technology's last achievements into all fields of social life, using power sources fully, preserving the environment and improving life, are directed to making life better; the engineer, production designer, futurologist Jacque Fresco's (Жак Фреско (Jacque Fresco)) offered «Шолпан жобасы» («The Venus Project») aims to provide all the necessities for human life without remainder; by Fresco's thought, the project's concept's main principle is not only futuristic buildings' construction but also society's new design, solving ecological problems, introducing a new method; this is humanity's bright future image that can provide today's and the future's demands; the project calls to rebuild society in a goal-directed way, rooting out war, hunger and poverty spread in the world; «Шолпан жобасының» (the Venus Project's) main condition is that all the natural resources on Earth are all humanity's common wealth; the British architect Tsvetan Toshkov's (Цветан Тошковтың) futuristic project made specially for megacities is called «Аспандағы қала» («City in the Sky»): the project author offers to build towers above the city; by his thought, in the city, in the daytime the sun's... towers in the form of a lotus flower (лотос гүлі түріндегі мұнаралар) standing on a high... must have the property of cleaning air and water and taking a rest-giving garden — the project idea is that the lotus flowers' gardens must be a place separated from the noisy city, giving the possibility to solve the city territory's problem truly; China's Songjiang InterContinental hotel (Қытайдағы Songjiang InterContinental қонақүйі) is one of the world's famous buildings: its main part is placed under the ground; it was built in a wide quarry near Shanghai city; instead of drying the water at the quarry's bottom, they decided to make it an artificial lake; the architects tried to turn this hotel into China's greenest building («ең жасыл» ғимаратқа айналдыруға тырысты); it was designed reckoning ecology, natural resources' use and protection fields' last novelties; the building was planned to use the sun's power and geothermal power; street furniture made by free design is pictured — Pablo Reinoso's (Пабло Рейносо) «Spaghetti» bench (author — artist-designer Pablo Reinoso), the bamboo-made bench (New York, USA; authors — designers Elena Goray, Kristof Tenes (?)), the «Жақ» round bench (Мехико, Мексика); the associative thinking method (ассоциативті ойлау тәсілі) in designing architectural objects: architect Emad Zand (Эмад Занд) holds the idea that a building must be designed by reckoning... — he enters the similar (animal, plant and other) forms' images into his similar buildings' design; in designing such a method is called associative thinking (ассоциативті ойлау деп аталады) (student book; original target `visual-art/the-city-of-the-future-concept`) | Book 3, printed p. 150 |
| Kazakhstan — 4.13. Болашақтың қаласы.
