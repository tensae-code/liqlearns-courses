# Video script — Saudi Folk Crafts

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The Kingdom's crafts grow out of its own ground.

Palm fronds become mats and baskets. Wool becomes the tent and the shamla.

Clay becomes pottery — and every region signs its own shapes.

Gold becomes the Door of the Kaaba, made by Saudi hands.

These are the Saudi folk crafts — old, useful, and beautiful.
