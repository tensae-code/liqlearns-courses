# Hands-on — Ornament and Decorative Pattern

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Design Lesson 4 "Ornament"
Do the thing this part teaches, with real materials where the book names them: ornament, whose beauty rests on symmetry — the matching repetition of units — in its two kinds: geometric ornament, built from triangles, squares, pentagons, hexagons and stars interlaced and repeated into bands and panels, and written ornament, built from Arabic letters, a Naskh letter chosen and repeated into a decorative shape
