# Ornament and Decorative Pattern — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Ornament's beauty rests on…
a) symmetry  b) speed  c) loudness
**Answer: a) symmetry** — symmetry — the matching repetition of units.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Geometric ornament is built from…
a) triangles, squares, pentagons, hexagons and stars  b) only circles  c) letters only
**Answer: a) triangles, squares, pentagons, hexagons and stars** — geometric shapes interlaced and repeated into bands and panels.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Written ornament is built from…
a) Arabic letters  b) numbers  c) animal tracks
**Answer: a) Arabic letters** — a Naskh letter chosen and repeated into a decorative shape.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Ornament units are repeated into…
a) bands and panels  b) songs  c) machines
**Answer: a) bands and panels**
