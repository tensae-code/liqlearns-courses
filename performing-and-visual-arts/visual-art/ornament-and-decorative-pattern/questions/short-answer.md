# Ornament and Decorative Pattern — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is symmetry in ornament?
**Answer: The matching repetition of units.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which script's letter does the book choose for written ornament?
**Answer: A Naskh letter.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Name two shapes used in geometric ornament.
**Answer: Any two of: triangles, squares, pentagons, hexagons, stars.**
