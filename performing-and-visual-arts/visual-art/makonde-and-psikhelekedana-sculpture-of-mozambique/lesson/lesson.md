# Makonde and Psikhelekedana Sculpture of Mozambique

## Do it first

Quick game: imagine one piece of wood carved two ways — one piece full of many people working together, one piece a single strange interlaced figure. Both are Mozambican. This lesson names them.

## Sub lesson 1 — Makonde sculpture

The **Makonde carving tradition** of **Cabo Delgado in the north** has spread across the whole country. Its carvers show **great mastery of wood**, in **pau-preto and pau-rosa**, carving animals and people in daily-life activities.

Its **three styles**: **Shetani** — stylised, interlaced humanoid and animal figures of great abstraction; **Ujamaa** — many people, their work tools and domestic animals artistically united in one piece; and **Figurative** — human and animal images from tradition.

And the **Mapiko mask**, used in the final public ceremonies of initiation rites.

## Sub lesson 2 — Psikhelekedana sculpture

**Psikhelekedana** is the sculptural art of the **south (Maputo and Gaza provinces)** — **decorative carving in mafurreira wood** showing **rural scenes, the great cities, historical events and political happenings** of the country.

Its named artist is **Dino Jethá**.
