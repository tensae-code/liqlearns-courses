# Hands-on — Makonde and Psikhelekedana Sculpture of Mozambique

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Style detective
Sketch three small figures: one interlaced and abstract (Shetani), one group of people with tools joined in one piece (Ujamaa), one plain traditional figure (Figurative). Label each style.

## Scene in wood (on paper)
Draw a Psikhelekedana-style scene — a rural scene or a city event — as if it were carved in mafurreira wood: bold shapes, many small figures.
