# Makonde and Psikhelekedana Sculpture of Mozambique — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Shetani figures are stylised and interlaced, of great abstraction.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Psikhelekedana comes from Cabo Delgado in the north.
**Answer: False** — it is the art of the south — Maputo and Gaza provinces.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Psikhelekedana shows rural scenes, cities and historical events.
**Answer: True**
