# Makonde and Psikhelekedana Sculpture of Mozambique — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Makonde tradition comes from…
a) Cabo Delgado in the north  b) Maputo in the south  c) Tete
**Answer: a) Cabo Delgado in the north**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The Ujamaa style shows…
a) one strange interlaced figure  b) many people, their tools and animals united in one piece  c) only masks
**Answer: b) many people, their tools and animals united in one piece**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The Mapiko mask is used in…
a) the final public ceremonies of initiation rites  b) markets  c) fishing
**Answer: a) the final public ceremonies of initiation rites**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Psikhelekedana carves in…
a) pau-preto  b) mafurreira wood  c) ivory
**Answer: b) mafurreira wood**
