# Video script — Makonde and Psikhelekedana Sculpture of Mozambique

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Two carvings, one country.

In the north — Makonde. Pau-preto, pau-rosa. Shetani, strange and interlaced. Ujamaa, many people in one piece. Figurative, from tradition. And the Mapiko mask.

In the south — Psikhelekedana. Mafurreira wood. Villages, cities, history — carved. Dino Jethá.

Learn the names. This is Mozambique carving.
