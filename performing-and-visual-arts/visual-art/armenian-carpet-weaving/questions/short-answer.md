# Armenian Carpet Weaving — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer: Armenian Carpet Weaving — Armenia's own teaching of this topic, kept as named parts: CARPETS (գորգ) — the Pazyryk carpet (5th-4th c. BC, Hermitage); mineral and plant dyes (the Megerian Carpet company's museum-showroom in Yerevan); the spinning tool (ճախարակ); ornament kinds — floral, rhombic, cross-shaped, star; the most widespread Armenian kind is perhaps the VISHAPAGORG (dragon carpet), which presents our national value system; Panos Terlemezyan's "Carpet-weaver woman" (1913).**
