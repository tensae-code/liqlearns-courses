This is Armenia's own lesson.

**CARPETS (գորգ).** CARPETS (գորգ) — the Pazyryk carpet (5th-4th c. BC, Hermitage); mineral and plant dyes (the Megerian Carpet company's museum-showroom in Yerevan); the spinning tool (ճախարակ); ornament kinds — floral, rhombic, cross-shaped, star; the most widespread Armenian kind is perhaps the VISHAPAGORG (dragon carpet), which presents our national value system; Panos Terlemezyan's "Carpet-weaver woman" (1913).

That is Armenian Carpet Weaving — Armenia's way, kept by name.
