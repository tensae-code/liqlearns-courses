**Конструктивизам.** Конструктивизам — Constructivism: the artist as engineer of space and time — Tatlin, Rodchenko, Gabo.

**Супрематизам.** Супрематизам — Suprematism: Malevich's non-objective world of pure geometric forms and pure feeling.

That is Constructivism and Suprematism — North Macedonia's way, kept by name.
