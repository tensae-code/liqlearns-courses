This is Syria's own lesson.

**Lesson 30 "The Damascene Arabic Thread (al-Khayt al-Arabi al-Dimashqi)".** al-Khayt al-Arabi is one of the professional Eastern arts used in making doors, ceilings and Eastern houses, and it relies on ornamental drawing and straight lines — a thin wooden thread by which the ornament is executed through its repetition; among the countries that care for it are Syria and Egypt; design a door and decorate it in the way of al-Khayt al-Arabi — did this kind of profession have a spirituality in the place you saw it, and what is the difference between it and contemporary art?.

That is Damascene Arabic Thread Ornament — Syria's way, kept by name.
