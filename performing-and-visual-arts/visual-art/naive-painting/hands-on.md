# Hands-on — Naïve Painting

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Pintura espontánea
Work through this part with real materials or your own body, the way the book sets it: spontaneous (naive) painting in Venezuela — untrained painters of their own lived world: Feliciano Carvallo's vibrant jungles and feasts, the worker Bárbaro Rivas painting Petare's daily and religious life, Salvador Valero, Víctor Millán, Elsa Morales — bearers of the collective culture, long underestimated. Show a companion what you made or did, and say what the part teaches.
