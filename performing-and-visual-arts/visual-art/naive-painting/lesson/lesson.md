## Sub lesson 1 — Pintura espontánea

Spontaneous (naive) painting in Venezuela — untrained painters of their own lived world: Feliciano Carvallo's vibrant jungles and feasts, the worker Bárbaro Rivas painting Petare's daily and religious life, Salvador Valero, Víctor Millán, Elsa Morales — bearers of the collective culture, long underestimated.
