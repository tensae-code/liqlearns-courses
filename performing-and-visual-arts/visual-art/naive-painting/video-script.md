# Video script — Naïve Painting

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

**Pintura espontánea.** Spontaneous (naive) painting in Venezuela — untrained painters of their own lived world: Feliciano Carvallo's vibrant jungles and feasts, the worker Bárbaro Rivas painting Petare's daily and religious life, Salvador Valero, Víctor Millán, Elsa Morales — bearers of the collective culture, long underestimated.

That is Naïve Painting — Venezuela's own books teach it this way.
