## Sub lesson 1 — China Grade 2 Art Vol.2

Borders in China's ethnic dress — Yao girls' headdress and clothing borders and a Tu people's embroidered waistband

## Sub lesson 2 — China Grade 3 Art Vol.1

China's traditional footwear — children's tiger-head shoes, Mongolian boots, Shui people's embroidered shoes and straw sandals

## Sub lesson 3 — China HS Crafts

Embroidery — the basic stitches worked on a framed ground, a leaf shaded by graded colour so it stands in relief, a 7 mm edge left for cutting out and mounting; embroidery today from portraiture to fashion
