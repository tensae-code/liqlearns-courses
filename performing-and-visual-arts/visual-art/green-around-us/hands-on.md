# Hands-on — Green Around Us


The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The green walk
Walk your street or compound on a green hunt: touch and name ten green things. At the end, sort them in your head — plant greens, thing greens, clothes greens. Which family is biggest?

## Caller practice
Practise being the game's caller: call slowly, then mix the order — green, red, green, yellow! A good caller is fair and surprising; a good player listens with the whole body.
