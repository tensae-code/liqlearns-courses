# Lesson plan — Green Around Us

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Visual Art · **Lesson:** Green Around Us
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 2 — Visual Art (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** green is the colour of the living surroundings — grass, leaves and the maize field — and it is also a command in a game: at "green" you run, at "yellow" you stop, at "red" you sit.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the Grade 1 colour work — naming colours in the surroundings (Colours Around Us in this unit) — and gives green its own deep look, plus the colour game that teaches with the whole body.

## Sub lessons
1. **Green in grass, leaves and maize** — the Grade 1 book's green page — a photo of green grass, then leaves and a maize field, all captioned as green colour.
2. **The Green-Yellow-Red game** — the listening-and-moving game "አረንጓዴ አረንጓዴ" (Green, Green) — run in a circle at green, stop at yellow, sit at red; whoever errs is out.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Colours Around Us in this unit, and Mixing Colours — green is the first colour your hands made. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name green things around you: grass, leaves, a maize field — all captioned as green colour in the book.
2. Play the colour game "አረንጓዴ አረንጓዴ" (Green, Green): run in a circle at green, stop at yellow, sit at red.
3. Say the game's rule of winning: whoever does it wrong is out; the one who never errs to the end wins.

## What you need
Open space for the game — a yard or cleared classroom floor. Nothing else; the greens are found outside.

## If the learner is stuck
- Sits at yellow, runs at red → slow the calls right down and add the action words beside the colours (green-run, yellow-stop, red-sit) until the body learns; then drop the help.
- Cannot tell leaves' greens apart → hold two leaves side by side against the same light; difference in green is easiest leaf-to-leaf.
