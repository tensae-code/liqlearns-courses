# Green Around Us — short answer

Answer in a short sentence, or by doing what the question asks.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name three green things you can find near your home or school, and say which is the deepest green.
**Answer: Any three real green finds (grass, leaves, maize, etc.) with a comparison.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** You are the caller. Call four colours in a surprising order and say what the players must do each time.
**Answer: Four calls with run (green), stop (yellow), sit (red) matched correctly.**
