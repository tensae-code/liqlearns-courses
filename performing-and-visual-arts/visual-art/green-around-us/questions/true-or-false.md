# Green Around Us — true or false

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Green is the colour plants wear while they are alive and growing.
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Whoever does the wrong action in the game keeps playing.
**Answer: False — whoever does it wrong is out; the one who never errs to the end wins.**
