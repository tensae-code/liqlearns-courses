# Green Around Us — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The book's green page shows —
a) grass, leaves and a maize field  b) the sea and sky  c) green clothes only
**Answer: a) grass, leaves and a maize field — all captioned as green colour.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In the game አረንጓዴ አረንጓዴ, at "green" you —
a) sit  b) stop  c) run in a circle
**Answer: c) run in a circle.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** At "yellow" you — and at "red" you —
a) run; sit  b) stop; sit  c) sit; run
**Answer: b) stop at yellow; sit at red.**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Which green is usually the biggest area of green?
a) one leaf  b) a maize field  c) a blade of grass
**Answer: b) a maize field — green as far as you can see.**
