# Video script — The Human Figure in Art

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A body is parts — and the parts measure each other.

Head against height. Legs against the whole. That is **proportion**.

Then wire for bones — an **armature** — and clay over it, keeping the proportions true.

Measure first. Model true.
