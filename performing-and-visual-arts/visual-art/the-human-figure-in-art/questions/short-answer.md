# The Human Figure in Art — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How does the book study the human body?
**Answer: Part by part — its proportions, head, legs and the rest measured against each other.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is an armature for?
**Answer: To hold the modelled figure up while it is built in plasticine or clay.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** What must the modelling keep?
**Answer: Real proportions and forms.**
