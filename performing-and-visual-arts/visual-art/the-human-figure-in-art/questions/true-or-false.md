# The Human Figure in Art — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** An armature holds a modelled figure up while it is built.
**Answer: True**.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Proportions do not matter if the figure looks friendly.
**Answer: False** — keeping real proportions is the section's rule.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The head and legs are among the parts measured.
**Answer: True**.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** The book models the figure in stone.
**Answer: False** — in plasticine or clay.
