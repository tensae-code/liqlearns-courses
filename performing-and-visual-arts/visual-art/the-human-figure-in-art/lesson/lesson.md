# The Human Figure in Art

## Do it first

Before you read: say or sketch what you already know about this lesson's subject — the human figure in art — body proportions part by part, and modelling the figure on an armature true to life. Start there; the lesson builds on it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Proportions of the human body

The Grade 5 book's Section 20 teaches the **proportions of the human body, part by part** — head, legs, and the rest measured against each other — and then **modelling the human figure on an armature** in plasticine or clay, **keeping real proportions and forms**. The wire armature holds the figure up; the proportions keep it true.
