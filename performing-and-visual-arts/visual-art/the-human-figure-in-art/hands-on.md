# Hands-on — The Human Figure in Art

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Measure yourself
With a partner, measure: how many head-lengths tall are you? How long are your legs against your whole height? Write the proportions down — the book's part-by-part study on a real body.

## Armature figure
Twist a simple wire figure (head loop, spine, arms, legs). Build plasticine or clay over it in the proportions you measured, and pose it standing, walking, reaching.
