# Hands-on — Portrait Drawing and Painting

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Study three faces
Look at the self-portraits of Georgette Chen, Vincent van Gogh and Yayoi Kusama. For each, say one thing the portrait tells you about its maker.

## My self-portrait
Paint your own self-portrait. Then, if clay is at hand, model your face in 3-D and compare the two.
