# Lesson plan — Portrait Drawing and Painting

**Course:** Performing and Visual Arts · **Title:** Visual Art · **Lesson:** Portrait Drawing and Painting
**Place in the curriculum:** Visual Art (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the portrait — in the Singapore guide, the self-portraits of Georgette Chen, Vincent van Gogh and Yayoi Kusama: what a self-portrait says about its maker, then children painting their own, even in 3-D with clay.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this course in the same title.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks a self-portrait is only a face-copy → the guide asks what the portrait SAYS about its maker: Chen, van Gogh and Kusama each show themselves in their own way.
- Thinks a portrait must be flat → the guide's own step makes the self-portrait in 3-D with clay as well as in paint.
