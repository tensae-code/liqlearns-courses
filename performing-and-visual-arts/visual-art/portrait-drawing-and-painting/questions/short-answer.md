# Portrait Drawing and Painting — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the three artists whose self-portraits the guide studies.
**Answer: Georgette Chen, Vincent van Gogh and Yayoi Kusama.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What question does the guide ask about a self-portrait?
