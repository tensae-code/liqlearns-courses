# Portrait Drawing and Painting — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Georgette Chen was a Singapore pioneer artist.
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A self-portrait says nothing about its maker.
**Answer: False** — the guide asks what it says about the maker.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A self-portrait can be modelled in clay.
**Answer: True.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Only van Gogh's self-portraits are studied.
**Answer: False** — Chen's and Kusama's are studied beside his.
