# Portrait Drawing and Painting — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A self-portrait is…
a) a portrait the artist makes of themselves  b) a portrait of a king only  c) a landscape
**Answer: a) a portrait the artist makes of themselves**.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Georgette Chen is…
a) Singapore's own pioneer artist  b) a Dutch painter  c) a Japanese sculptor
**Answer: a) Singapore's own pioneer artist** — her self-portraits are studied in the guide.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The guide asks what a self-portrait…
a) says about its maker  b) costs  c) hides in a museum
**Answer: a) says about its maker**.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Children then make self-portraits…
a) in paint, and even in 3-D with clay  b) only in pencil  c) never at all
**Answer: a) in paint, and even in 3-D with clay**.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Van Gogh and Kusama are studied for their…
a) self-portraits  b) landscapes  c) instruments
**Answer: a) self-portraits**.
