# Portrait Drawing and Painting

## Do it first

Quick start: from the title alone, guess what this lesson's work is. Then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — Self-portraits that speak of their makers

The guide's artwork strategy studies **the self-portraits of Georgette Chen (Singapore's own pioneer artist), Vincent van Gogh and Yayoi Kusama** — and asks **what a self-portrait says about its maker**: the face the artist chose to show is a statement about the artist.

## Sub lesson 2 — Paint your own — even in 3-D

Then the children **paint their own self-portraits** — and the guide goes further: a self-portrait can be made **even in 3-D with clay**, a face modelled, not only drawn.
