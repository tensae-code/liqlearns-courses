Көз, мұрын мен ерінді бейнелеу (Depicting the eyes, nose and lips) — what it teaches: Van Gogh was a very poor man — he never sold his art for money in his life; when he saw his surroundings he drew them; every day, later he drew pictures of streets and cafés seen in daylight, people and streets; the pictures' dense colours and clearly seen brush strokes, the colours' special brightness distinguish them; he drew with emotional and expressive means; his contemporaries did not value his works in his lifetime; now only one of his pictures is sold — «Арльдағы қызыл жүзімдіктер» (The Red Vineyard at Arles); analyse and answer: looking at Van Gogh's portrait («Жалғыз көзді адамның портреті», Vincent van Gogh, 1889, canvas, oil), how did he use colour and texture in this portrait, and what kind of person was the one-eyed man in real life?; artists keep form and proportion in drawing — for example a person's face has many moving parts, so depicting it is complex; keeping the basic rules helps depict the face rightly: for example eyes, nose and lips must be drawn together, not one by one; looking from the front and from the side, the face's structure is shown very exactly; so, learning to draw the eyes, nose and lips rightly, one can depict the face; draw your friend's eye picture, then compare it with the picture you drew of your friend; how to depict the nose and lips: in a picture the nose and lips are shown in the simplest ways; for depicting a complex form a cone, cube or pyramid-like simple form can be used; this method does not change the depicted part's peculiarities; learn by looking at the drawn nose and lips album and doing the research; how tone (рең) is made: on a face with a light source, take white paper, cut it in half from the middle or fold it; the bent part will be light or half-light; use a clean instrument on the prepared photo paper and stick it on the required place at the right time; for showing the face's front use dark tone, for the side light (тон) — the face's proportions (адам бетінің пропорциясы): Albrecht Dürer (1471–1528) was a famous artist of the Northern Renaissance who preserved classical sculpture in Italy; especially, he researched much mathematically the proportions of the human face and body and wrote several books of his conclusions about it; later artists were surprised by his research and called him a master; Dürer's self-portrait drawn with a silver pencil (күміс қарындашпен) on specially tinted paper could not be erased if drawn wrongly; so Dürer's many portraits and self-portraits later became masterpieces;
