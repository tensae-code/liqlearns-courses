# Hands-on — Appreciating Drawing


The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The first exhibition
Hold the classroom exhibition the book describes: draw as the teacher shows, arrange everyone's drawings in order on the wall, and walk the row together in silence first — then appreciations out loud.

## Materials of our area
Collect drawing materials your area gives: charcoal from a cooled fire, a stick for dust drawing, coloured earth or flower colour beside the pencil and pen. Draw the same small thing with three materials and compare.
