# Appreciating Drawing — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: say the book's definition of drawing, name two drawing materials found in your area, and arrange a row of drawings for others, saying one true thing you appreciate about each.
Practice deepens inside the one skill: more items to work with, less help from the models, and harder corners — working faster without losing care, and doing the same skill on new things the learner chooses.
