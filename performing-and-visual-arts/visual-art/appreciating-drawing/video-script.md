# Video script — Appreciating Drawing

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

What is drawing?
A thought — put down.
On paper. On canvas. On a wall.
With pencil, with colour, with pen, with charcoal.

How do we appreciate it?
We draw.
And we show.

Pin your drawings in a row.
Walk slowly.
Say one true thing you like — for every drawing.
That is appreciation.
