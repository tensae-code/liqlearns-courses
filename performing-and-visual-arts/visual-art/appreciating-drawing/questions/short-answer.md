# Appreciating Drawing — short answer

Answer in a short sentence, or by doing what the question asks.

**1.** Name two drawing materials that can be found in your own area.
**Answer: Any two of: pencil, colours, pen, charcoal (and local equivalents).**

**2.** Say the book's definition of drawing in full.
**Answer: Putting a thought down on paper, canvas or wall using pencil, colour, pen, charcoal and the like.**
