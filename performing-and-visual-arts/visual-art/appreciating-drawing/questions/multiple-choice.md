# Appreciating Drawing — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Drawing is —
a) putting a thought down on paper, canvas or wall  b) singing a song from memory  c) building with stones
**Answer: a) putting a thought down on paper, canvas or wall.**

**2.** Which list is all drawing materials named by the book?
a) pencil, colour, pen, charcoal  b) drum, flute, lyre  c) needle, thread, cloth
**Answer: a) pencil, colour, pen, charcoal and the like.**

**3.** Activity 4.2 asks you to arrange your drawings —
a) in a pile under the desk  b) in order, so friends can appreciate them  c) by size in a box
**Answer: b) in order, so friends can appreciate them — a first classroom exhibition.**

**4.** Appreciating a friend's drawing means —
a) saying one true thing you like about it  b) saying it is all perfect  c) looking for one second
**Answer: a) saying one true thing you like — the careful colour, the closed shape, the feeling in it.**

**5.** A thought in a drawing travels —
a) from inside one person to the eyes of another  b) nowhere  c) only to the teacher
**Answer: a) from inside one person to the eyes of another — that is why drawing matters.**
