# Making Soft Toys

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about making soft toys. Then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — Making weightless toy animals

Soft-toy making whole — choosing safe cloth for children, cutting by block patterns, sewing, stuffing and finishing, with the full-size dog pattern (pieces counted by colour) worked step by step

## Sub lesson 2 — the cloth doll

A doll made whole — face drawn from the book's diagram (eyes, nose, stitched mouth), body sewn and stuffed through the 4 cm opening, felt shoes, jumbo-yarn hair and button eyes (joins the Grade 10 soft-toys placement)
