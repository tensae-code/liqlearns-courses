# Sewing and Tailoring — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The tailor measures you with…
a) the tailor's centimetre  b) a ruler for drawing  c) a piece of string only
**Answer: a) the tailor's centimetre**.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The tipar is…
a) the pattern — the model by which material is cut  b) a kind of button  c) the finished garment
**Answer: a) the pattern — the model by which material is cut**.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Festoon-stitching the edges stops the material from…
a) fraying  b) shining  c) shrinking
**Answer: a) fraying**.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The fish pencil-case is made from…
a) two cloths  b) paper  c) clay
**Answer: a) two cloths**.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** The buttons are sewn one beside another in the shape of…
a) a heart  b) a star  c) a fish
**Answer: a) a heart**.
