# Sewing and Tailoring — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does a însăila mean?
**Answer: To baste — sewing with sparse stitches.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What does a retușa mean?
**Answer: To correct (to retouch the work).**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** How is the fish pencil-case finished?
**Answer: With a drawn eye and a cord.**
