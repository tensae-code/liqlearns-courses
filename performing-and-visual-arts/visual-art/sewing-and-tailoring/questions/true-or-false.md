# Sewing and Tailoring — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The tailor cuts the material following the pattern.
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The fish shape is turned and stuffed.
**Answer: True.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The buttons come from new clothes bought for the purpose.
**Answer: False** — they are gathered from clothes that no longer fit.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** A croi means to cut to a drawn outline.
**Answer: True.**
