# Video script — Sewing and Tailoring

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

First — measure! The tailor's centimetre.

Cut — following the pattern, the **tipar**.

Baste — big, sparse stitches. Festoon the edge — no fraying!

Sew a fish! Sew buttons — one, two, three — a heart!

That is the little tailor's craft.
