# Sewing and Tailoring

## Do it first

Quick start: a button has come off your shirt. Before the needle — what must the tailor know first: your size, or the cloth's colour? Then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — The little tailor

**The Little Tailor** works in order: he **measures you with the tailor's centimetre**; he **cuts the material following the pattern (tipar** — the model by which material is cut); he **bastes the pieces (a însăila** — sewing with sparse stitches); and he **festoon-stitches the edges (a festona)** so the material **does not fray**. The craft's own words: **a croi** — to cut to a drawn outline; **a retușa** — to correct.

## Sub lesson 2 — The little fish pencil-case

**Portfolio: The Little Fish Pencil-Case.** Sew a **fish-shaped pencil-case from two cloths**: **draw the fish on the cloth, cut it out, turn and stuff the shape**, and finish the fish with a **drawn eye and a cord**.

## Sub lesson 3 — Buttons sewn as a heart

**The Fantasy Workshop**: decorate your **headband or bag** — gather **coloured buttons from clothes that no longer fit** and **sew them one beside another in the shape of a heart** on a bag.
