# Hands-on — Sewing and Tailoring

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Fish pencil-case
Draw a fish on cloth, cut out two fish cloths, sew, turn and stuff the shape, and finish it with a drawn eye and a cord.

## Button heart
Gather coloured buttons from clothes that no longer fit. Sew them one beside another in the shape of a heart on a bag or headband.
