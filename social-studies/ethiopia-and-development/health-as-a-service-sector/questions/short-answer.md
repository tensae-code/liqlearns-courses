# Health as a service sector — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name two focuses of the post-1991 health rebuild.
**Answer: Any two of: decentralisation; preventive, promotional and curative care together; access for all; private and NGO participation.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name three measures of health performance.
**Answer: Any three of: IMR, child and maternal mortality, life expectancy at birth, fertility, crude birth/death rates, access to safe water.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name two keyed problems of the health sector.
**Answer: Any two of: limited/uneven physical access; inadequate budgetary allocation; low management quality; low resource-utilisation capacity.**
