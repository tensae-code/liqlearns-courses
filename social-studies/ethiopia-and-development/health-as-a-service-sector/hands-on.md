# Hands-on — Health as a service sector

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Distance rings
Draw your nearest health post at the centre and rings for 1, 5 and 10 km. Mark who lives beyond the outer ring and what the keyed remedies would do for them.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the health-post map from memory and label every part. Then open the lesson, compare, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
