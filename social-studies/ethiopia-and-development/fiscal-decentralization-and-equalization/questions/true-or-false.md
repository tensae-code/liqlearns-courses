# Fiscal decentralization and equalization — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Fiscal decentralization devolves taxing and spending powers to regional and local governments.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Fiscal equalization lifts poorer regions' capacity so services are similar at similar tax burdens.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Ethiopia directed Birr 7 billion to regional states in 2017/18 for education, health, water and sanitation projects.
**Answer: True**
