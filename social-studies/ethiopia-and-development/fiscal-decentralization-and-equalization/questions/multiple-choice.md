# Fiscal decentralization and equalization — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Power devolved"?
a) Fiscal decentralization devolves taxing and spending powers to regional and local governments  b) Fiscal equalization lifts poorer regions' capacity so services are similar at similar tax burdens  c) Ethiopia directed Birr 7 billion to regional states in 2017/18 for education, health, water and sanitation projects
**Answer: a) Fiscal decentralization devolves taxing and spending powers to regional and local governments**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Equalization — levelling the capacity"?
a) Fiscal equalization lifts poorer regions' capacity so services are similar at similar tax burdens  b) Ethiopia directed Birr 7 billion to regional states in 2017/18 for education, health, water and sanitation projects  c) Fiscal decentralization devolves taxing and spending powers to regional and local governments
**Answer: a) Fiscal equalization lifts poorer regions' capacity so services are similar at similar tax burdens**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Ethiopia's 7 billion birr"?
a) Ethiopia directed Birr 7 billion to regional states in 2017/18 for education, health, water and sanitation projects  b) Fiscal decentralization devolves taxing and spending powers to regional and local governments  c) Fiscal equalization lifts poorer regions' capacity so services are similar at similar tax burdens
**Answer: a) Ethiopia directed Birr 7 billion to regional states in 2017/18 for education, health, water and sanitation projects**
