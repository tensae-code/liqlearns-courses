# Fiscal decentralization and equalization — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Power devolved" in your own words.
**Answer:** Fiscal decentralization devolves taxing and spending powers to regional and local governments.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Equalization — levelling the capacity" in your own words.
**Answer:** Fiscal equalization lifts poorer regions' capacity so services are similar at similar tax burdens.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Ethiopia's 7 billion birr" in your own words.
**Answer:** Ethiopia directed Birr 7 billion to regional states in 2017/18 for education, health, water and sanitation projects.
