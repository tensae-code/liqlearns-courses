# Hands-on — Fiscal decentralization and equalization

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Divide the stream
Draw a central treasury dividing a budget stream among regional treasuries of different sizes, the smallest topped up by equalization. Label the 2017/18 figure and its four sectors.

## Ratio reading
A federation's central direct spending is 60 of 100 total (excluding transfers). Compute the centralization ratio (0.6) and say whether the system leans centralised or decentralised, and what single change would move it.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
