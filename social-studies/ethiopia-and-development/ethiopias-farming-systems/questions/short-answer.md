# Ethiopia's farming systems — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Smallholders — the dominant producer" in your own words.
**Answer:** Smallholders produce over 90% of output on about 95% of cropped area.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Pastoralism — herding the dry half" in your own words.
**Answer:** Pastoralists herd across about 40% of the land, in lowlands below 1,500 metres.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Commercial and state farms" in your own words.
**Answer:** Only 13 state farms remain, on 156,040 hectares.
