# Ethiopia's farming systems — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Smallholders produce over _____ of Ethiopia's farm output.
**Answer: 90%**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Pastoral herding ranges over about _____ of Ethiopia's land — the dry lowlands.
**Answer: 40%**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Only _____ state farms remain, on 156,040 hectares — the system's shrunken core.
**Answer: 13**
