# Ethiopia's farming systems — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Smallholders — the dominant producer"?
a) over 90% of production from some 10.58 million small households  b) herding over 40% of the land below 1,500 m, with 73% of the goats and a million camels  c) large farms pampered but inefficient, shrunk to about 5% of cropland; 13 state farms remain
**Answer: a) over 90% of production from some 10.58 million small households**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Pastoralism — herding the dry half"?
a) over 90% of production from some 10.58 million small households  b) large farms pampered but inefficient, shrunk to about 5% of cropland; 13 state farms remain  c) herding over 40% of the land below 1,500 m, with 73% of the goats and a million camels
**Answer: c) herding over 40% of the land below 1,500 m, with 73% of the goats and a million camels**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Commercial and state farms"?
a) herding over 40% of the land below 1,500 m, with 73% of the goats and a million camels  b) large farms pampered but inefficient, shrunk to about 5% of cropland; 13 state farms remain  c) over 90% of production from some 10.58 million small households
**Answer: b) large farms pampered but inefficient, shrunk to about 5% of cropland; 13 state farms remain**
