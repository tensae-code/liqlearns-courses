# Ethiopia's farming systems — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Smallholders produce over 90% of output on about 95% of cropped area.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Pastoralists herd across about 40% of the land, in lowlands below 1,500 metres.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Only 13 state farms remain, on 156,040 hectares.
**Answer: True**
