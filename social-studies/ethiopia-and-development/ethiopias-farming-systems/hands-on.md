# Hands-on — Ethiopia's farming systems

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Scale it on the floor: 95 paper squares are cropped land; learners colour 90 of the harvest cards as smallholder-grown. Then a camel-herd path is walked across the room's dry corner — 40% of the land, a fraction of the people.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — a family mixed farm under two hectares beside a large mechanised Awash farm, to scale, with a camel herd crossing dry lowland — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
