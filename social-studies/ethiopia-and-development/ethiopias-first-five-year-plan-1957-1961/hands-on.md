# Hands-on — Ethiopia's first five-year plan, 1957–1961

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Plan document
Draft the first plan's one-page summary: three priorities, each with the problem it answers, in the order the book gives them.

## Check the gap
Compute the plan's shortfalls: growth (3.7 − 3.2) and the export-import growth gap (6.4 − 3.5). Write one sentence on what the uncured deficit stored up for later plans.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
