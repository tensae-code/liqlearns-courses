# Ethiopia's first five-year plan, 1957–1961 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Planning begins" in your own words.
**Answer:** Ethiopia's first five-year plan ran 1957–1961 under Haile Selassie.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "The priorities" in your own words.
**Answer:** The first plan prioritised infrastructure, skilled workers for processing industries, and commercial agriculture.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "The scoreboard" in your own words.
**Answer:** GNP grew 3.2% against a 3.7% target, and coffee supplied 70% of foreign-exchange earnings.
