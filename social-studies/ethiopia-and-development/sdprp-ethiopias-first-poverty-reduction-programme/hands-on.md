# Hands-on — SDPRP — Ethiopia's first poverty reduction programme

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Average autopsy
Recompute the average: (−3.6 + 11.3 + 8.8) / 3 = 5.5. Then write why reporting only the average misleads, in two sentences a journalist should print.

## Pillars list
List SDPRP's building blocks from the lesson and mark which came from ADLI, which from governance reform, and which from the consultation process.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
