# SDPRP — Ethiopia's first poverty reduction programme — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Built on ADLI, consulted in 2001" in your own words.
**Answer:** SDPRP (2002/03–2004/05) built on ADLI, food security, decentralisation and capacity building, after 2001 consultations.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "The drought year and the average" in your own words.
**Answer:** The first-year drought cut GDP 3.6%; growth of 11.3% and 8.8% followed, averaging 5.5%.
