# SDPRP — Ethiopia's first poverty reduction programme — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** SDPRP (2002/03–2004/05) built on ADLI, food security, decentralisation and capacity building, after 2001 consultations.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The first-year drought cut GDP 3.6%; growth of 11.3% and 8.8% followed, averaging 5.5%.
**Answer: True**
