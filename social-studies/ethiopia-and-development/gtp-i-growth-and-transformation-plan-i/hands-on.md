# Hands-on — GTP I — Growth and Transformation Plan I

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Banner check
Draw a small workshop sewing garments beside a high-value crop field, both under an '11%' banner. Under each scene, write the GTP I promise that scene must keep.

## Objective sort
Write GTP I's four objectives on cards. For each, name one measurable sign by 2014/15 that it was being met — the habit the performance lessons will use.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
