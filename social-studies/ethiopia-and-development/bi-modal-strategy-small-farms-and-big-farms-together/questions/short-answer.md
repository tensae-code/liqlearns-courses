# Bi-modal strategy — small farms and big farms together — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "The dual track" in your own words.
**Answer:** Bi-modal runs two tracks: small farms intensified and a commercial sector built, as in Mexico and Colombia.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "What the second track adds" in your own words.
**Answer:** The commercial track adds accumulated land, capital, technology and large-scale diversification.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Its costs — the mirror of uni-modal's gains" in your own words.
**Answer:** Bi-modal costs: differentiation into capitalists and landless labourers, eviction, land loss for the majority, few off-farm jobs.
