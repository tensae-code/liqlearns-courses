# Bi-modal strategy — small farms and big farms together — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Bi-modal strategy abolishes commercial farming.
**Answer: False — It BUILDS a commercial sector beside the small farms — that second track is the 'bi' in bi-modal.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The commercial track adds nothing the small track lacks.
**Answer: False — It adds exactly the large-scale inputs — land accumulation, capital and technology — that tiny holdings cannot take.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Bi-modal strategy guarantees every peasant keeps their land.
**Answer: False — Land loss for the majority and eviction of poor peasants are its named costs — the mirror image of uni-modal's protections.**
