# Bi-modal strategy — small farms and big farms together — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "The dual track"?
a) Bi-modal runs two tracks: small farms intensified and a commercial sector built, as in Mexico and Colombia.  b) The commercial track adds accumulated land, capital, technology and large-scale diversification.  c) Bi-modal costs: differentiation into capitalists and landless labourers, eviction, land loss for the majority, few off-farm jobs.
**Answer: a) Bi-modal runs two tracks: small farms intensified and a commercial sector built, as in Mexico and Colombia.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "What the second track adds"?
a) Bi-modal costs: differentiation into capitalists and landless labourers, eviction, land loss for the majority, few off-farm jobs.  b) Bi-modal runs two tracks: small farms intensified and a commercial sector built, as in Mexico and Colombia.  c) The commercial track adds accumulated land, capital, technology and large-scale diversification.
**Answer: c) The commercial track adds accumulated land, capital, technology and large-scale diversification.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Its costs — the mirror of uni-modal's gains"?
a) The commercial track adds accumulated land, capital, technology and large-scale diversification.  b) Bi-modal costs: differentiation into capitalists and landless labourers, eviction, land loss for the majority, few off-farm jobs.  c) Bi-modal runs two tracks: small farms intensified and a commercial sector built, as in Mexico and Colombia.
**Answer: b) Bi-modal costs: differentiation into capitalists and landless labourers, eviction, land loss for the majority, few off-farm jobs.**
