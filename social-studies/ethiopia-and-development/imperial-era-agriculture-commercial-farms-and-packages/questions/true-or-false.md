# Imperial-era agriculture — commercial farms and packages — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Imperial plan carried out full land reform first.
**Answer: False — The reform was promised and IGNORED — the book blames the plan's failure on exactly that.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Commercial farms produced most of Ethiopia's farm output.
**Answer: False — They reached only about 5% of agricultural output before nationalisation in 1975.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Package projects lowered costs and protected tenants.
**Answer: False — They raised incomes but at HIGH cost and with INCREASED tenant evictions — the opposite pairing.**
