# Imperial-era agriculture — commercial farms and packages — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Targets without land reform" in your own words.
**Answer:** Imperial farm policy set output targets but ignored the promised land reform, which the book blames for the plan's failure.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Large commercial farms" in your own words.
**Answer:** Commercial farms won credit, five-year tax holidays above 200,000 birr, cheap land and duty-free machinery, yet reached only about 5% of output.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Package projects — and the cheaper sequel" in your own words.
**Answer:** Package projects (CADU, WADU, Adaa) raised incomes at high cost and with increased tenant evictions; the minimum package followed, cheaper and thinner.
