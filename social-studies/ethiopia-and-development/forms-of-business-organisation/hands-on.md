# Hands-on — Forms of business organisation

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Three ownership circles drawn on the floor — one chair, two chairs, a ring of shareholder chairs around an empty 'company person' chair. Learners stand in each form as a debt collector arrives, and the class rules what can be taken in each circle.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — one shopkeeper who is owner, manager and till in one, two partners signing one agreement, and a company standing apart from its shareholders — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
