# Forms of business organisation — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The sole owner's risk is _____ — creditors can reach personal property.
**Answer: unlimited liability**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Partners carry unlimited _____ liability — each answers for the whole firm's debts.
**Answer: joint**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Stockholders' liability in a corporation is _____ — capped at their shares.
**Answer: limited**
