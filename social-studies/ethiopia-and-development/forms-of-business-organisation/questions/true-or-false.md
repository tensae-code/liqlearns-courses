# Forms of business organisation — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A sole proprietor has unlimited liability reaching their personal property.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Partnership liability is unlimited and joint — each partner answers for the firm.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A corporation is a separate legal personality from its owners, with stockholders' liability limited.
**Answer: True**
