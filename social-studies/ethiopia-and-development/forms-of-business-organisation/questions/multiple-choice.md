# Forms of business organisation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Sole proprietorship — one person, all risk"?
a) one owner with unlimited liability — easiest to start, riskiest to hold  b) partners sharing profit and loss with unlimited joint liability, capped at 10 or 20  c) a separate legal person owned in shares, with limited liability and a board in control
**Answer: a) one owner with unlimited liability — easiest to start, riskiest to hold**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Partnership — shared and joint"?
a) one owner with unlimited liability — easiest to start, riskiest to hold  b) a separate legal person owned in shares, with limited liability and a board in control  c) partners sharing profit and loss with unlimited joint liability, capped at 10 or 20
**Answer: c) partners sharing profit and loss with unlimited joint liability, capped at 10 or 20**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Corporations — a separate legal person"?
a) partners sharing profit and loss with unlimited joint liability, capped at 10 or 20  b) a separate legal person owned in shares, with limited liability and a board in control  c) one owner with unlimited liability — easiest to start, riskiest to hold
**Answer: b) a separate legal person owned in shares, with limited liability and a board in control**
