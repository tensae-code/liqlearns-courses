# Forms of business organisation — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Sole proprietorship — one person, all risk" in your own words.
**Answer:** A sole proprietor has unlimited liability reaching their personal property.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Partnership — shared and joint" in your own words.
**Answer:** Partnership liability is unlimited and joint — each partner answers for the firm.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Corporations — a separate legal person" in your own words.
**Answer:** A corporation is a separate legal personality from its owners, with stockholders' liability limited.
