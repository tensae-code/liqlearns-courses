# ADLI — agriculture leading industrialization — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Western paradigm treated agriculture as passive and gave industry priority.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Since the 1970s, agriculture has been recast as a leading, dynamic sector.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** ADLI stands for Agricultural Development-Led Industrialization.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** ADLI was adopted in 1974.
**Answer: False — ADLI was adopted in 1994, after the 1991 liberalisation — the Imperial and Derg strategies came before it.**

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** PASDEP removed export crops from the strategy.
**Answer: False — PASDEP ADDED export-crop packages — spices, cut flowers, fruit and vegetables — to the ADLI line.**

**6. (Sub lesson 6)** Allocative efficiency means adding new capital and technology.
**Answer: False — That is TECHNICAL efficiency. Allocative efficiency is using existing land, labour and capital BETTER, through improved practices.**


**7. (Sub lesson 7)** The teacher guide's key instructs teachers to conclude that ADLI 'is not a success' — an evaluative judgement attributed to the guide.
**Answer: True**
