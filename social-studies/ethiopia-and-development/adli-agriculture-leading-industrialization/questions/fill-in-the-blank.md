# ADLI — agriculture leading industrialization — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The old paradigm treated agriculture as _____ — industry took priority.
**Answer: passive**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Modern economists recast agriculture as a _____, dynamic sector — not a passive one.
**Answer: leading**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** ADLI stands for _____ — Ethiopia's chosen development strategy.
**Answer: Agricultural Development-Led Industrialization**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** PADETES delivered fertiliser, seed and ____ packages from 1994/95.
**Answer: credit**

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** PASDEP added export-crop packages for spices, cut flowers, fruit and ____.
**Answer: vegetables**

**6. (Sub lesson 6)** Using existing resources better is ____ efficiency.
**Answer: allocative**


**7. (Sub lesson 7)** The verdict that ADLI 'is not a success' is the _____ 's own judgement, taught attributed — not a finding of the student book.
**Answer: teacher guide**
