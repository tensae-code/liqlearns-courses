# ADLI — agriculture leading industrialization — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "The old paradigm — industry first" in your own words.
**Answer:** The Western paradigm treated agriculture as passive and gave industry priority.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "The recasting — agriculture as leading sector" in your own words.
**Answer:** Since the 1970s, agriculture has been recast as a leading, dynamic sector.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Ethiopia's choice — ADLI" in your own words.
**Answer:** ADLI stands for Agricultural Development-Led Industrialization.

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Explain "ADLI in the Grade 11 book — the strategy stated" in your own words.
**Answer:** ADLI (1994): raise smallholder productivity and industrialise on domestic raw materials with labour-intensive technology, through forward and backward linkages.

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** Explain "After ADLI — SDPRP and PASDEP" in your own words.
**Answer:** SDPRP added training centres, water harvesting and micro-finance; PASDEP added roads, dams and export-crop packages.

**6. (Sub lesson 6)** Explain "How ADLI works — two efficiencies (teacher guide — evidence)" in your own words.
**Answer:** ADLI works through allocative efficiency (using existing resources better) and technical efficiency (adding capital and technology) — teacher guide evidence.


**7. (Sub lesson 7)** Explain "The guide's verdict on ADLI — attributed teacher evidence (teacher guide)" in your own words.
**Answer:** The teacher guide's key instructs teachers to conclude that ADLI 'is not a success' — an evaluative judgement attributed to the guide.
