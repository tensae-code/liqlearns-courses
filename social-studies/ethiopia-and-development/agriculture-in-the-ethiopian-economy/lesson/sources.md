Mixed-vintage figures dated, never blended, as the placements direct: the NBE 2019/20 shares show agriculture falling from 40% to 33% of GDP across 2014/15–2019/20 (industry 21% to 29%, services about 40%), while the book's features list still quotes the older picture — about 45% of GDP, over 80% of exports, about 85% of employment, about 70% of industry's raw materials — from 2006-era sources. Both sets are taught with their dates attached.
