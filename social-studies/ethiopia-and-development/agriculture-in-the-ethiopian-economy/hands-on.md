# Hands-on — Agriculture in the Ethiopian economy

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
A farm-to-factory rope runs across the room: learners pass raw-material cards forward (forward linkage) and tool-and-fertiliser cards back (backward linkage), narrating agriculture's four roles as the cards move.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — a dated pair of sector-share charts — 2006 picture versus 2019/20 — and a 65/25/10 split bar — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
