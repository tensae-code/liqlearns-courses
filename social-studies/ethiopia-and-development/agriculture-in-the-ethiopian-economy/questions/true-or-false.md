# Agriculture in the Ethiopian economy — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** On NBE 2019/20 shares, agriculture fell from 40% to 33% of GDP across 2014/15–2019/20.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Agriculture supplies industry through backward and forward linkages.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Crops make up about 65% of agricultural GDP, livestock about 25% and forestry and fishery about 10%.
**Answer: True**
