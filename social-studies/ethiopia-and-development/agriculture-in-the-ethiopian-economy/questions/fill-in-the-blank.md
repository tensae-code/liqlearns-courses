# Agriculture in the Ethiopian economy — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Agriculture's GDP share fell to _____ by 2019/20, from 40% in 2014/15.
**Answer: 33%**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Farm and factory are tied both ways by _____ — backward to inputs, forward to raw materials.
**Answer: linkages**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Crops account for about _____ of agricultural GDP — livestock 25%, forestry and fishery 10%.
**Answer: 65%**
