# Agriculture in the Ethiopian economy — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "The headline position — two dated pictures" in your own words.
**Answer:** On NBE 2019/20 shares, agriculture fell from 40% to 33% of GDP across 2014/15–2019/20.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Problems and roles" in your own words.
**Answer:** Agriculture supplies industry through backward and forward linkages.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "The internal structure — 65/25/10" in your own words.
**Answer:** Crops make up about 65% of agricultural GDP, livestock about 25% and forestry and fishery about 10%.
