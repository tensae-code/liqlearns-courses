# Unimodal strategy — betting on the smallholder — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the whole bet in the unimodal strategy?
**Answer: The smallholder farm, improved with modern inputs.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What replaced the unimodal approach under the Derg?
**Answer: Villagisation and collectivisation.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which two countries does the guide give as unimodal exemplars?
**Answer: China and Thailand.**
