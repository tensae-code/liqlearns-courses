# Video script — Unimodal strategy — betting on the smallholder

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The unimodal strategy concentrates agricultural transformation on the smallholder: improve small farms by supplying modern inputs — better seed, fertiliser, tools and know-how — and let millions of small improvements add up to a national transformation. There is no second, commercial mode beside it; the small farm is the whole bet.

The unimodal approach prevailed in Ethiopia even in the early Derg years, before villagisation and collectivisation replaced it. That matters for reading the later strategies: the smallholder-first idea is older than any one government — each regime inherited it, changed it, or abandoned it.

The guide's exemplar countries for the unimodal strategy are China and Thailand — countries that raised farm output by intensifying small peasant farms rather than by replacing them with estates. When a strategy question names China or Thailand in this course, it is pointing at this smallholder road.
