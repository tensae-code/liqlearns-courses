# Hands-on — Unimodal strategy — betting on the smallholder

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## One stream, many farms
Draw ten small farm squares. Draw one shared stream of seed and fertiliser reaching all ten. Now erase the stream's source and write what could cut it off — price, roads, rain — and one fix for each.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the picture from memory and label every part. Then open the lesson, compare, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
