# Video script — Education as a service sector

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Education in Ethiopia dates to the fourth century and was church-controlled for some 1,500 years before state-led change from the 19th century. The section marches policy through three regimes — pre-1974, post-1974 and post-1991 — and performance through primary, secondary and higher levels: one service, repeatedly rebuilt by each order.

Performance is read off two measures: the Gross Enrollment Rate (GER) and the Pupil–Teacher Ratio (PTR). Between 2003/04 and 2007/08, GER rose by 47.7% in the primary first cycle (grades 1–4) and by 27.8% in the second cycle (grades 5–8) — access grew fast; the ratio gauge tells you whether quality kept up.

The keyed problems are relevance, accessibility and equity, and poor quality and commitment of leadership at all levels. The remedies run through people, not only buildings: parent, teacher and community participation in policy, textbooks and facilities, and narrowing regional disparities in teacher quality.
