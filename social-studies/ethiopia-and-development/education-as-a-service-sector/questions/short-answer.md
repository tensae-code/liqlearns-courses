# Education as a service sector — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** For how long was education church-controlled?
**Answer: For some 1,500 years, before state-led change from the 19th century.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What are the two measures of education performance?
**Answer: The Gross Enrollment Rate and the Pupil–Teacher Ratio.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name two remedies the key gives.
**Answer: Any two of: parent, teacher and community participation in policy, textbooks and facilities; narrowing regional disparities in teacher quality.**
