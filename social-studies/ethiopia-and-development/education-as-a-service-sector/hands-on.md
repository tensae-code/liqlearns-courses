# Hands-on — Education as a service sector

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Measure your school
For a school you know, estimate its enrollment trend and its pupil–teacher ratio. Say which of the two gauges worries you more, and which keyed remedy fits it.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the two gauges from memory and label every part. Then open the lesson, compare, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
