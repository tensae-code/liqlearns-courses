# Hands-on — Services in the Ethiopian economy

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
A town street is drawn with ten service shops. Learners tag each shop three times with coloured strings — distributive or other, consumer or business or mixed, physical or intellectual or aesthetic — and defend one hard case aloud.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — an Ethiopian Airlines plane as the flagship service exporter, beside a three-way sorting diagram of one town's services — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
