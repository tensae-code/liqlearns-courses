# Services in the Ethiopian economy — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Only about _____ of the population works in services, though they produce about 45% of GDP.
**Answer: 10%**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Service exports reached nearly _____ in 2008/09 — more than goods earned.
**Answer: $2 billion**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Trade, transport, banking and public administration together are the _____ services.
**Answer: distributive**
