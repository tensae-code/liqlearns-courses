# Services in the Ethiopian economy — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Services produced 45.1% of GDP in 2008/09 with only about 10% of the population engaged.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Service exports reached nearly $2 billion in 2008/09 — more than the $1.5 billion from goods.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Distributive services include trade, transport and communication, banking and insurance, and public administration.
**Answer: True**
