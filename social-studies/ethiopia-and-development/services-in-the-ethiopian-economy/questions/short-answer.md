# Services in the Ethiopian economy — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Features — big share, few hands" in your own words.
**Answer:** Services produced 45.1% of GDP in 2008/09 with only about 10% of the population engaged.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Three contributions" in your own words.
**Answer:** Service exports reached nearly $2 billion in 2008/09 — more than the $1.5 billion from goods.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Components, classified three ways" in your own words.
**Answer:** Distributive services include trade, transport and communication, banking and insurance, and public administration.
