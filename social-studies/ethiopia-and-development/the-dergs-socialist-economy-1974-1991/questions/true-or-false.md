# The Derg's socialist economy, 1974–1991 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Derg nationalised land, abolished tenancy, and fixed grain prices below market with compulsory quotas.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** 1974–78 dislocation gave GDP +0.4% a year; the 1978–80 Zemecha recovery gave +5.7%.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The ten-year perspective plan (1984–94) leaned on cooperatives and state farms, GDP +5%.
**Answer: True**
