# The Derg's socialist economy, 1974–1991 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "The socialist turn"?
a) The Derg nationalised land, abolished tenancy, and fixed grain prices below market with compulsory quotas  b) 1974–78 dislocation gave GDP +0.4% a year; the 1978–80 Zemecha recovery gave +5.7%  c) The ten-year perspective plan (1984–94) leaned on cooperatives and state farms, GDP +5%

**Answer: a) 1974–78 dislocation gave GDP +0.4% a year; the 1978–80 Zemecha recovery gave +5.7%**
