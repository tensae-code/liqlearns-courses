# The Derg's socialist economy, 1974–1991 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "The socialist turn" in your own words.
**Answer:** The Derg nationalised land, abolished tenancy, and fixed grain prices below market with compulsory quotas.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Phases one and two — dislocation, then recovery" in your own words.
**Answer:** 1974–78 dislocation gave GDP +0.4% a year; the 1978–80 Zemecha recovery gave +5.7%.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Phases three and four — setback and the ten-year plan" in your own words.
**Answer:** The ten-year perspective plan (1984–94) leaned on cooperatives and state farms, GDP +5%.
