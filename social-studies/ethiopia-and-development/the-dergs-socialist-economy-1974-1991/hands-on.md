# Hands-on — The Derg's socialist economy, 1974–1991

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Four-phase strip
Draw the dated strip of four phases under a state farm and a grain-quota collection point. On each phase, write its GDP figure and its one-word character.

## Price below market
Grain's official price sits below the market price, with a compulsory quota. Trace a farmer's three possible responses and what each does to food supply in the towns.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
