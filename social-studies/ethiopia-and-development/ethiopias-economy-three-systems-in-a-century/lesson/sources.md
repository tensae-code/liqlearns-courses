Grade 10 teacher-guide framing divergence (placement 21, teacher guide — evidence): the guide introduces the unit as moving 'from feudal agricultural economic system during the Hailesellassie (1931-1974) to the developmental state economic system of the FDRE (1991-2021)' — naming Haile Selassie's reign dates and closing the FDRE period at 2021, where the student textbook speaks of the feudal monarchy to 1974 and the EPRDF from 1991 without an end date. Both framings are recorded here; the lesson text keeps the student book's wording. Source: *Economics, Teacher Guide Book, Grade 10*, printed p. 85.
