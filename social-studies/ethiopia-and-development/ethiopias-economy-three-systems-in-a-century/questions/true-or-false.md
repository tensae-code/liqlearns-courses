# Ethiopia's economy — three systems in a century — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Before 1974, Ethiopia ran a market economy under the feudal monarchy.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Under the Derg, the state owned industries, banks and insurance in a command economy.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The market-oriented economy dates from 28 May 1991, with privatisation and deregulation.
**Answer: True**
