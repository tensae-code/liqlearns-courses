# Ethiopia's economy — three systems in a century — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Markets under the monarchy"?
a) a market economy inside the feudal monarchy, to 1974  b) state ownership of industries, banks and insurance, 1974–91 — and it failed  c) privatisation, agricultural exports and deregulation from 28 May 1991
**Answer: a) a market economy inside the feudal monarchy, to 1974**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "The Derg's command economy"?
a) a market economy inside the feudal monarchy, to 1974  b) privatisation, agricultural exports and deregulation from 28 May 1991  c) state ownership of industries, banks and insurance, 1974–91 — and it failed
**Answer: c) state ownership of industries, banks and insurance, 1974–91 — and it failed**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Market-oriented from 1991"?
a) state ownership of industries, banks and insurance, 1974–91 — and it failed  b) privatisation, agricultural exports and deregulation from 28 May 1991  c) a market economy inside the feudal monarchy, to 1974
**Answer: b) privatisation, agricultural exports and deregulation from 28 May 1991**
