# Ethiopia's economy — three systems in a century — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Under the feudal monarchy to 1974, Ethiopia ran a _____ economy.
**Answer: market**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The Derg ran a _____ economy from 1974 to 1991, with the state owning industries and banks.
**Answer: command**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Ethiopia's market-oriented economy begins on _____ — with privatisation and deregulation.
**Answer: 28 May 1991**
