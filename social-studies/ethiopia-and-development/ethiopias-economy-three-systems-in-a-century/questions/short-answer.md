# Ethiopia's economy — three systems in a century — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Markets under the monarchy" in your own words.
**Answer:** Before 1974, Ethiopia ran a market economy under the feudal monarchy.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "The Derg's command economy" in your own words.
**Answer:** Under the Derg, the state owned industries, banks and insurance in a command economy.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Market-oriented from 1991" in your own words.
**Answer:** The market-oriented economy dates from 28 May 1991, with privatisation and deregulation.
