# Hands-on — Ethiopia's economy — three systems in a century

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Three stations around the room — monarchy market, Derg command, post-1991 market-oriented. A farmer character walks the stations; at each, the class calls out who owns the farm's buyers, bank and insurer.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — a three-panel timeline — monarchy, Derg, post-1991 — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
