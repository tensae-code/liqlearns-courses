# Bimodal strategy — smallholders and commercial farms together — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What are the two modes of the bimodal strategy?
**Answer: Modernised smallholder farms and large-scale commercial farming.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Who runs the commercial farms in the bimodal strategy?
**Answer: Private entrepreneurs.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** State the unimodal–bimodal contrast in one sentence.
**Answer: Unimodal intensifies small peasant farms alone; bimodal intensifies both small and commercial farms.**
