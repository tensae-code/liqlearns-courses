# Bimodal strategy — smallholders and commercial farms together — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Two modes at once"?
a) bimodal: modernise smallholders and promote large commercial farming at the same time  b) in bimodal, the commercial sector is strong and principal  c) unimodal = small farms alone; bimodal = small + commercial; exemplars Mexico and Ecuador
**Answer: a) bimodal: modernise smallholders and promote large commercial farming at the same time**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "The commercial sector is principal"?
a) in bimodal, the commercial sector is strong and principal  b) unimodal = small farms alone; bimodal = small + commercial; exemplars Mexico and Ecuador  c) bimodal: modernise smallholders and promote large commercial farming at the same time
**Answer: a) in bimodal, the commercial sector is strong and principal**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "The clean contrast, and the exemplars"?
a) unimodal = small farms alone; bimodal = small + commercial; exemplars Mexico and Ecuador  b) bimodal: modernise smallholders and promote large commercial farming at the same time  c) in bimodal, the commercial sector is strong and principal
**Answer: a) unimodal = small farms alone; bimodal = small + commercial; exemplars Mexico and Ecuador**
