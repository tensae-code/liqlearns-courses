# Bimodal strategy — smallholders and commercial farms together — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The bimodal strategy abandons smallholder farms.
**Answer: False**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In bimodal strategy the smallholder sector is the principal engine.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Bimodal intensifies small farms alone, with no commercial sector.
**Answer: False**
