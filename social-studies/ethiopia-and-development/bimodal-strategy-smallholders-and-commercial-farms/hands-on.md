# Hands-on — Bimodal strategy — smallholders and commercial farms together

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Two-mode map
Draw one valley: small plots on one side, one mechanised estate on the other, one policy banner over both. Label which mode is principal, then check against the lesson.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the picture from memory and label every part. Then open the lesson, compare, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
