# Hands-on — Ethiopia's resource base

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Count your area's resources
List the resources around your home — land, animals, water, trees, sun. Mark each one: used well, used badly, or not used yet. Say which single change would help most.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's resource map from memory and label every part. Then open the lesson, compare, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
