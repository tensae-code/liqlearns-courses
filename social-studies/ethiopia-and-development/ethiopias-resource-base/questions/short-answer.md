# Ethiopia's resource base — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name two costs of rapid population growth.
**Answer: Any two of: fewer jobs, falling wages, pressure on housing, health and services.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why is energy called the most promising resource?
**Answer: Because of the large hydroelectric potential (about 30,000 MW) and high solar potential.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Why can unmeasured minerals not yet be planned on?
**Answer: Because their quantity and quality are still poorly known.**
