# Ethiopia's resource base — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "People and land"?
a) population second in Africa, 84–89% rural, 35% of land arable  b) largest herd in Africa; forest 30% down to about 3%; hydro potential about 30,000 MW  c) minerals poorly known; exploration under way in fuels, gold and precious stones
**Answer: a) population second in Africa, 84–89% rural, 35% of land arable**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Animals, forests and energy"?
a) largest herd in Africa; forest 30% down to about 3%; hydro potential about 30,000 MW  b) minerals poorly known; exploration under way in fuels, gold and precious stones  c) population second in Africa, 84–89% rural, 35% of land arable
**Answer: a) largest herd in Africa; forest 30% down to about 3%; hydro potential about 30,000 MW**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Minerals — the unknown resource"?
a) minerals poorly known; exploration under way in fuels, gold and precious stones  b) population second in Africa, 84–89% rural, 35% of land arable  c) largest herd in Africa; forest 30% down to about 3%; hydro potential about 30,000 MW
