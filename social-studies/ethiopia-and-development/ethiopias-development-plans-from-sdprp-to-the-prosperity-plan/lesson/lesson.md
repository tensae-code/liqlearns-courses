# Ethiopia's development plans — from SDPRP to the Prosperity Plan

## Do it first

Global goals only matter when a country writes them into its own plans. Ethiopia ran the MDGs and then the SDGs through a chain of national plans — guess the chain's links before the timeline is laid out.

## Sub lesson 1 — Mainstreaming the MDGs

Ethiopia mainstreamed the MDGs into successive national plans — the SDPRP, then GTP I — and achieved six of the eight goals, with significant progress on the remaining two: gender equality and maternal health. A Climate Resilient Green Economy strategy accompanied the effort, with a vision of middle-income, carbon-neutral status by 2025.

## Sub lesson 2 — Aligning the SDGs

The SDGs were aligned into GTP II (2015/16–2019/20) and then into the ten-year Prosperity Plan (2019/20–2029/30) — each new global agenda folded into the next national plan rather than run beside it.

## Sub lesson 3 — Priorities — and the standing challenges

The Prosperity Plan's listed priorities keep agriculture as the mainstay, transform manufacturing, raise competitiveness and productivity, build the construction industry, develop human resources and a climate-resilient green economy, and eliminate rent-seeking. The book names low implementation capacity and scarce finance as the standing challenges to all of it.
