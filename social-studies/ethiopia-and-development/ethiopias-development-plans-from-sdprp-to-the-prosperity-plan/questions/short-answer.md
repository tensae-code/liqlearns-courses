# Ethiopia's development plans — from SDPRP to the Prosperity Plan — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Mainstreaming the MDGs" in your own words.
**Answer:** Ethiopia achieved six of the eight MDGs, with significant progress on gender equality and maternal health.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Aligning the SDGs" in your own words.
**Answer:** The SDGs were aligned into GTP II and the ten-year Prosperity Plan (2019/20–2029/30).

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Priorities — and the standing challenges" in your own words.
**Answer:** Prosperity Plan priorities keep agriculture as mainstay and target manufacturing, productivity, construction, human resources and a green economy; challenges are low capacity and scarce finance.
