# Ethiopia's development plans — from SDPRP to the Prosperity Plan — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Mainstreaming the MDGs"?
a) Ethiopia achieved six of the eight MDGs, with significant progress on gender equality and maternal health.  b) The SDGs were aligned into GTP II and the ten-year Prosperity Plan (2019/20–2029/30).  c) Prosperity Plan priorities keep agriculture as mainstay and target manufacturing, productivity, construction, human resources and a green economy; challenges are low capacity and scarce finance.
**Answer: a) Ethiopia achieved six of the eight MDGs, with significant progress on gender equality and maternal health.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Aligning the SDGs"?
a) Prosperity Plan priorities keep agriculture as mainstay and target manufacturing, productivity, construction, human resources and a green economy; challenges are low capacity and scarce finance.  b) Ethiopia achieved six of the eight MDGs, with significant progress on gender equality and maternal health.  c) The SDGs were aligned into GTP II and the ten-year Prosperity Plan (2019/20–2029/30).
**Answer: c) The SDGs were aligned into GTP II and the ten-year Prosperity Plan (2019/20–2029/30).**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Priorities — and the standing challenges"?
a) The SDGs were aligned into GTP II and the ten-year Prosperity Plan (2019/20–2029/30).  b) Prosperity Plan priorities keep agriculture as mainstay and target manufacturing, productivity, construction, human resources and a green economy; challenges are low capacity and scarce finance.  c) Ethiopia achieved six of the eight MDGs, with significant progress on gender equality and maternal health.
**Answer: b) Prosperity Plan priorities keep agriculture as mainstay and target manufacturing, productivity, construction, human resources and a green economy; challenges are low capacity and scarce finance.**
