# Ethiopia's development plans — from SDPRP to the Prosperity Plan — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Ethiopia achieved none of the MDGs.
**Answer: False — It achieved six of the eight, with significant progress on the other two — gender equality and maternal health.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The Prosperity Plan runs for two years.
**Answer: False — It is a TEN-year plan, 2019/20–2029/30, succeeding GTP II.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The book names no challenges to the plans.
**Answer: False — It names two standing challenges: low implementation capacity and scarce finance.**
