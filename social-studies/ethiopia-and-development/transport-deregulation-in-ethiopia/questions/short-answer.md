# Transport deregulation in Ethiopia — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Under the Derg — total state control" in your own words.
**Answer:** Proclamation No. 107/1976 put the whole commercial truck and passenger system under strict state control.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "1992 — the gate opens" in your own words.
**Answer:** Proclamation No. 14/1992 deregulated freight transport for efficiency and equitable distribution.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Free entry" in your own words.
**Answer:** Operators may freely join existing associations or obtain licences to form new ones.
