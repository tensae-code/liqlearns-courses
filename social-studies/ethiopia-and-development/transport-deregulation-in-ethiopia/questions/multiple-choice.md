# Transport deregulation in Ethiopia — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Under the Derg — total state control"?
a) Proclamation No. 107/1976 put the whole commercial truck and passenger system under strict state control.  b) Proclamation No. 14/1992 deregulated freight transport for efficiency and equitable distribution.  c) Operators may freely join existing associations or obtain licences to form new ones.
**Answer: a) Proclamation No. 107/1976 put the whole commercial truck and passenger system under strict state control.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "1992 — the gate opens"?
a) Operators may freely join existing associations or obtain licences to form new ones.  b) Proclamation No. 107/1976 put the whole commercial truck and passenger system under strict state control.  c) Proclamation No. 14/1992 deregulated freight transport for efficiency and equitable distribution.
**Answer: c) Proclamation No. 14/1992 deregulated freight transport for efficiency and equitable distribution.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Free entry"?
a) Proclamation No. 14/1992 deregulated freight transport for efficiency and equitable distribution.  b) Operators may freely join existing associations or obtain licences to form new ones.  c) Proclamation No. 107/1976 put the whole commercial truck and passenger system under strict state control.
**Answer: b) Operators may freely join existing associations or obtain licences to form new ones.**
