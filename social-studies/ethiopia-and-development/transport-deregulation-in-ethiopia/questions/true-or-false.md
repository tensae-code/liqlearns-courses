# Transport deregulation in Ethiopia — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Under the Derg, freight transport was freely competitive.
**Answer: False — It sat under STRICT STATE CONTROL — deregulation is the post-1991 story.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Proclamation 14/1992 tightened state control of freight.
**Answer: False — It DEREGULATED freight transport — private operators left the state corporation to form their own associations.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** After 1992 only the state corporation could carry freight.
**Answer: False — Private operators formed independent associations, with free entry and licences for new ones.**
