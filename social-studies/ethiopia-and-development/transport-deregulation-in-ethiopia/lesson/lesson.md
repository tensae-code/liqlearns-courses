# Transport deregulation in Ethiopia

## Do it first

In 1976 every commercial truck and bus answers to the state. Sixteen years later the gate swings open. What exactly changed in 1992 — who may carry freight now, and on what terms? Predict before the two proclamations are compared.

## Sub lesson 1 — Under the Derg — total state control

Under the Derg the whole commercial truck and passenger system sat under strict state control, set by Proclamation No. 107/1976: one state system decided who carried what, where, and on what terms.

[Picture 24955 — a state-controlled depot gate in 1976 opening into many independent truck associations in 1992]

## Sub lesson 2 — 1992 — the gate opens

Proclamation No. 14/1992 deregulated freight transport in the name of efficiency and equitable distribution. Many private operators then left the state corporation to form independent associations.

## Sub lesson 3 — Free entry

Entry is genuinely open: free entry into existing associations, and licences available to form new ones — an operator may join the carriers already organised or start an association of their own.
