# Communication — the village telephone effect — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What three services make up the communication sector?
**Answer: Telecommunications, postal services and media.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name three things a village telephone improves or facilitates.
**Answer: Any three of: price information, local administration, trade and small business, health services, education, agricultural extension.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** How is telecommunications performance counted?
**Answer: In fixed telephone lines, digital lines, mobile subscribers and internet subscribers.**
