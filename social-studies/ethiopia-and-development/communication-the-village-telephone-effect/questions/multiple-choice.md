# Communication — the village telephone effect — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Communication as development infrastructure"?
a) communication = telecommunications, postal services and media; treated as development infrastructure  b) a village phone: prices, administration, trade, health, education and extension services  c) performance: fixed lines, digital lines, mobile and internet subscribers; parcels and EMS
**Answer: a) communication = telecommunications, postal services and media; treated as development infrastructure**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "What one village telephone changes"?
a) a village phone: prices, administration, trade, health, education and extension services  b) performance: fixed lines, digital lines, mobile and internet subscribers; parcels and EMS  c) communication = telecommunications, postal services and media; treated as development infrastructure
**Answer: a) a village phone: prices, administration, trade, health, education and extension services**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Counting communication performance"?
a) performance: fixed lines, digital lines, mobile and internet subscribers; parcels and EMS  b) communication = telecommunications, postal services and media; treated as development infrastructure  c) a village phone: prices, administration, trade, health, education and extension services
