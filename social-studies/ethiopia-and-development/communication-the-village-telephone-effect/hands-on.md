# Hands-on — Communication — the village telephone effect

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The one-call test
Think of one decision your family made without price information. Write what one phone call would have changed, then check your list against the lesson's effects list and add what you missed.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the farmer-and-price-board picture from memory and label every part. Then open the lesson, compare, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
