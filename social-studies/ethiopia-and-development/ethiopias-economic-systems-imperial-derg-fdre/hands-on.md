# Hands-on — Ethiopia's economic systems — Imperial, Derg, FDRE

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Three corners of the room are three regimes; learners carrying enterprise cards must stand in the regime where their enterprise's ownership fits, and defend the stand.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — a three-regime timeline with the system name under each — Imperial capitalist, Derg command, FDRE mixed — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
