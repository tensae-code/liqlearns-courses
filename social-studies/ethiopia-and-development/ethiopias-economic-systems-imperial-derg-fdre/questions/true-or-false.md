# Ethiopia's economic systems — Imperial, Derg, FDRE — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Under the Imperial regime a capitalist system prevailed, with means of production privately owned and production for profit.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Under the Imperial regime a capitalist system prevailed, with means of production privately owned and production for profit.
**Answer: False** — that statement belongs to sub lesson 1 (Imperial — capitalist); sub lesson 2 says: Under the Derg regime a command system prevailed, with society owning the means of production and no right to private property.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Under the FDRE a mixed system prevails, because private and public sectors coexist and are growing together.
**Answer: True**
