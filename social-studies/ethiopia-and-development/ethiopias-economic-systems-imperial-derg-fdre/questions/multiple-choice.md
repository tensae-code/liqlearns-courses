# Ethiopia's economic systems — Imperial, Derg, FDRE — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Imperial — capitalist"?
a) privately owned means of production, production for profit  b) society owning the means of production, no right to private property  c) private and public sectors coexisting and growing together
**Answer: a) privately owned means of production, production for profit**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Derg — command"?
a) privately owned means of production, production for profit  b) private and public sectors coexisting and growing together  c) society owning the means of production, no right to private property
**Answer: c) society owning the means of production, no right to private property**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "FDRE — mixed"?
a) society owning the means of production, no right to private property  b) private and public sectors coexisting and growing together  c) privately owned means of production, production for profit
**Answer: b) private and public sectors coexisting and growing together**
