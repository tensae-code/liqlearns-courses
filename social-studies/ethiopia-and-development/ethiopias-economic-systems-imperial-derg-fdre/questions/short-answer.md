# Ethiopia's economic systems — Imperial, Derg, FDRE — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Imperial — capitalist" in your own words.
**Answer:** Under the Imperial regime a capitalist system prevailed, with means of production privately owned and production for profit.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Derg — command" in your own words.
**Answer:** Under the Derg regime a command system prevailed, with society owning the means of production and no right to private property.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "FDRE — mixed" in your own words.
**Answer:** Under the FDRE a mixed system prevails, because private and public sectors coexist and are growing together.
