# Ethiopia's economic systems — Imperial, Derg, FDRE — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Under the Imperial regime a _____ system prevailed.
**Answer: capitalist**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Under the Derg regime a _____ system prevailed.
**Answer: command**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Under the FDRE a _____ economic system prevails, because both sectors coexist.
**Answer: mixed**
