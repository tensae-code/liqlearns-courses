# Video script — Ethiopia's economic systems — Imperial, Derg, FDRE

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Under the Imperial regime a capitalist system prevailed (teacher guide — evidence): the means of production were privately owned and production was undertaken at private entrepreneurs' initiative, for profit.

Under the Military (Derg) regime a command system prevailed (teacher guide — evidence): society owned the means of production, with no right to private property, under the state's direction.

Under the FDRE a mixed economic system prevails (teacher guide — evidence), because private and public sectors coexist — and the guide's activity answer adds that both sectors are currently growing together in Ethiopia, which is why the mixed system is the relevant one today.
