# Hands-on — Home-grown economic reform — the macroeconomic front

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Pass the baton
Draw the baton passing from a public-investment crane to a private factory. Along the track, place the fiscal, financial and debt reforms as hurdles the baton must clear.

## Front sort
Sort ten reform items (tax audit, capital markets, contraband action, concessional loans, remittance incentives, project appraisal...) under fiscal, financial or external — and defend the two that sit on borders.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
