# Home-grown economic reform — the macroeconomic front — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Why reform, and whose"?
a) The HGER shifts the engine from public to private and from demand to supply  b) Fiscal reform modernises tax administration and disciplines debt; financial reform moves toward market-based rates and capital markets  c) External-debt policy: fewer commercial loans, more concessional borrowing, rescheduling
**Answer: a) The HGER shifts the engine from public to private and from demand to supply**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Fiscal and financial fronts"?
a) Fiscal reform modernises tax administration and disciplines debt; financial reform moves toward market-based rates and capital markets  b) External-debt policy: fewer commercial loans, more concessional borrowing, rescheduling  c) The HGER shifts the engine from public to private and from demand to supply
**Answer: a) Fiscal reform modernises tax administration and disciplines debt; financial reform moves toward market-based rates and capital markets**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Exports, remittances and debt"?
a) External-debt policy: fewer commercial loans, more concessional borrowing, rescheduling  b) The HGER shifts the engine from public to private and from demand to supply  c) Fiscal reform modernises tax administration and disciplines debt; financial reform moves toward market-based rates and capital markets
**Answer: a) External-debt policy: fewer commercial loans, more concessional borrowing, rescheduling**
