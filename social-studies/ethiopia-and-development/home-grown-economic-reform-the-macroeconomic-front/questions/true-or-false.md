# Home-grown economic reform — the macroeconomic front — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The HGER shifts the engine from public to private and from demand to supply.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Fiscal reform modernises tax administration and disciplines debt; financial reform moves toward market-based rates and capital markets.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** External-debt policy: fewer commercial loans, more concessional borrowing, rescheduling.
**Answer: True**
