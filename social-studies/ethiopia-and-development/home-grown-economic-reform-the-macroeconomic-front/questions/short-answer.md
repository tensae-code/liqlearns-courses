# Home-grown economic reform — the macroeconomic front — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Why reform, and whose" in your own words.
**Answer:** The HGER shifts the engine from public to private and from demand to supply.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Fiscal and financial fronts" in your own words.
**Answer:** Fiscal reform modernises tax administration and disciplines debt; financial reform moves toward market-based rates and capital markets.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Exports, remittances and debt" in your own words.
**Answer:** External-debt policy: fewer commercial loans, more concessional borrowing, rescheduling.
