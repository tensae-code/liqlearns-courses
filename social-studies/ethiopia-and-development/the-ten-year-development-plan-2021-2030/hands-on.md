# Hands-on — The ten-year development plan, 2021–2030

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Lighthouse drawing
Draw the lighthouse labelled 'prosperity' over ten pillar stones, naming at least six pillars correctly. Under it, list the six objectives in short form.

## Wider than income
Write the plan's prosperity definition as a checklist for one citizen's life, then score two households against it — one income-rich but service-poor, one the reverse.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
