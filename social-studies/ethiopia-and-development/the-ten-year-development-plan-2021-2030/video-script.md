# Video script — The ten-year development plan, 2021–2030

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The ten-year plan, 2021–2030 — 'Ethiopia: An African Beacon of Prosperity' — defines prosperity wider than income: basic needs met, services accessible to every citizen regardless of status, an enabling and just environment, and dignity, equality and free participation.

Its six objectives run from a pragmatic market-based system with a stronger private sector through macroeconomic stability, structural transformation, quality basic services and infrastructure, and a competent civil service, to strong inclusive institutions and the rule of law. Ten strategic pillars — including quality growth and shared prosperity, technological capability and the digital economy, resilient green economy, gender and social inclusion, access to justice, and regional peace and integration — focus into six priority areas.

The six priority areas include diversified growth sources, inclusive finance, the demographic dividend and sustainable urbanisation. A lighthouse labelled 'prosperity' standing over ten pillar stones is the plan's own picture of itself.
