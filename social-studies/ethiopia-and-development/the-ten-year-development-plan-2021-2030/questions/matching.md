# The ten-year development plan, 2021–2030 — matching

Match each part to its right statement.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Prosperity defined widely — c
**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Six objectives, ten pillars — a

Statements:
a) Six objectives and ten pillars, from private-sector strength to regional peace and integration
