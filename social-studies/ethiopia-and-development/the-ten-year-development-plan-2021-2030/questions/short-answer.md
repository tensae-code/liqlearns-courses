# The ten-year development plan, 2021–2030 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Prosperity defined widely" in your own words.
**Answer:** The ten-year plan defines prosperity as needs met, services for all, justice, dignity, equality and participation.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Six objectives, ten pillars" in your own words.
**Answer:** Six objectives and ten pillars, from private-sector strength to regional peace and integration.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Six priority areas" in your own words.
**Answer:** The priority areas include diversified growth, inclusive finance, the demographic dividend and sustainable urbanisation.
