# The ten-year development plan, 2021–2030 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Prosperity defined widely"?
a) The ten-year plan defines prosperity as needs met, services for all, justice, dignity, equality and participation  b) Six objectives and ten pillars, from private-sector strength to regional peace and integration  c) The priority areas include diversified growth, inclusive finance, the demographic dividend and sustainable urbanisation
