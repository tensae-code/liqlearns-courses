# The ten-year development plan, 2021–2030 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The ten-year plan defines prosperity as needs met, services for all, justice, dignity, equality and participation.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Six objectives and ten pillars, from private-sector strength to regional peace and integration.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The priority areas include diversified growth, inclusive finance, the demographic dividend and sustainable urbanisation.
**Answer: True**
