# Sustainable development — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "The definition — present and future" in your own words.
**Answer:** Sustainable development meets present needs without compromising future generations' ability to meet theirs.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Three pillars" in your own words.
**Answer:** The three pillars are economic growth, environmental stewardship and social inclusion.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Applied everywhere" in your own words.
**Answer:** Sustainability applies across cities, agriculture, infrastructure, energy, water and transport.
