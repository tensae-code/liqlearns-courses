# Sustainable development — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Sustainable development means stopping all growth.
**Answer: False — It means growth of the right kind — present needs met while the future's ability is preserved.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Profit is one of the three pillars.
**Answer: False — The pillars are economic growth, environmental STEWARDSHIP and social inclusion — care for the environment, not profit.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Sustainable development concerns only farming.
**Answer: False — The lesson applies it across six fields — cities, agriculture, infrastructure, energy, water and transport.**
