# Sustainable development — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "The definition — present and future"?
a) Sustainable development meets present needs without compromising future generations' ability to meet theirs.  b) The three pillars are economic growth, environmental stewardship and social inclusion.  c) Sustainability applies across cities, agriculture, infrastructure, energy, water and transport.
**Answer: a) Sustainable development meets present needs without compromising future generations' ability to meet theirs.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Three pillars"?
a) Sustainability applies across cities, agriculture, infrastructure, energy, water and transport.  b) Sustainable development meets present needs without compromising future generations' ability to meet theirs.  c) The three pillars are economic growth, environmental stewardship and social inclusion.
**Answer: c) The three pillars are economic growth, environmental stewardship and social inclusion.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Applied everywhere"?
a) The three pillars are economic growth, environmental stewardship and social inclusion.  b) Sustainability applies across cities, agriculture, infrastructure, energy, water and transport.  c) Sustainable development meets present needs without compromising future generations' ability to meet theirs.
**Answer: b) Sustainability applies across cities, agriculture, infrastructure, energy, water and transport.**
