# Sustainable development

## Do it first

A town doubles its income by draining the lake its grandchildren would have fished. It has grown — but has it developed in a way that can last? Build the test a lasting development must pass.

## Sub lesson 1 — The definition — present and future

Sustainable development meets the needs of the present without compromising future generations' ability to meet theirs. Growth that spends the future's means fails the test, however large it looks today.

[Picture 24938 — a roof held by three pillars — economy, environment, society — with a child of the next generation under it]

## Sub lesson 2 — Three pillars

Growth must be inclusive and environmentally sound, resting on three pillars: economic growth, environmental stewardship and social inclusion. Remove any pillar and the structure — development that lasts — falls.

## Sub lesson 3 — Applied everywhere

The pillars apply across cities, agriculture, infrastructure, energy, water and transport — every sector where today's building decides what tomorrow inherits.
