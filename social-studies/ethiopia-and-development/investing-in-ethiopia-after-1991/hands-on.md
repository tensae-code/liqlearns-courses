# Hands-on — Investing in Ethiopia after 1991 — proclamations and thresholds

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Gate maths
An investor has $120,000. Work out: could he enter before the threshold cut? After it? In a consultancy project under the 2003 rules, how much must he commit? Show each answer with its proclamation.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the widening gate from memory and label every part. Then open the lesson, compare, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
