# Investing in Ethiopia after 1991 — proclamations and thresholds — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name two areas reserved from private investors.
**Answer: Any two of: defence, electrical engineering works, air and marine transportation, postal and telecommunications.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** By how much did the foreign minimum capital fall?
**Answer: From US$500,000 to US$100,000 — a cut of US$400,000.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What two ownership forms does the 2003 proclamation allow a foreign investor?
**Answer: Sole proprietor with full equity, or jointly with domestic investors or the government.**
