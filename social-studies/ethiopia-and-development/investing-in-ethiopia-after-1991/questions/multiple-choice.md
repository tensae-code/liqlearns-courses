# Investing in Ethiopia after 1991 — proclamations and thresholds — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "The first measure — Proclamation No. 15/1992"?
a) Investment Proclamation No. 15/1992: incentives, with some areas reserved from private investors  b) foreign minimum capital cut from US$500,000 to US$100,000; 25% cash deposit lifted  c) Proclamation 375/2003: full equity or joint operation; $100,000 per project, $50,000 in named consultancy fields
