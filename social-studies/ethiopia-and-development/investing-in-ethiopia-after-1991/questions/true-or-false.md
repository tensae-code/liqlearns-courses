# Investing in Ethiopia after 1991 — proclamations and thresholds — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Postal and telecommunications were opened to all private investors in 1992.
**Answer: False**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The 25% cash-deposit requirement was raised by the reform.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Under the 2003 proclamation a foreign investor must always partner with the government.
**Answer: False**
