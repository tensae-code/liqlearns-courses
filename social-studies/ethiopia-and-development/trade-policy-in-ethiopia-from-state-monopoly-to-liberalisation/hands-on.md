# Hands-on — Trade policy in Ethiopia — from state monopoly to liberalisation

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Two trader diaries
Write two short diary entries for the same trader: one in 1985, one in 1995. Each entry must use at least three facts from the lesson about what he may do, must do, or is freed from.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the two-sided picture from memory and label every part. Then open the lesson, compare, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
