# Trade policy in Ethiopia — from state monopoly to liberalisation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Trade as a state system"?
a) Derg trade: AMC (agricultural) and EDDC (industrial), quotas, rationing, surrender of purchases  b) administered prices; fixed exchange rate; exporters surrendered 100% of earnings  c) from 1992: quotas abolished, export taxes abolished except coffee, managed float, max import duty 280% → 80% → 50%
**Answer: a) Derg trade: AMC (agricultural) and EDDC (industrial), quotas, rationing, surrender of purchases**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Administered prices and rationed foreign exchange"?
a) administered prices; fixed exchange rate; exporters surrendered 100% of earnings  b) from 1992: quotas abolished, export taxes abolished except coffee, managed float, max import duty 280% → 80% → 50%  c) Derg trade: AMC (agricultural) and EDDC (industrial), quotas, rationing, surrender of purchases
**Answer: a) administered prices; fixed exchange rate; exporters surrendered 100% of earnings**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "The reversal from 1992"?
a) from 1992: quotas abolished, export taxes abolished except coffee, managed float, max import duty 280% → 80% → 50%  b) Derg trade: AMC (agricultural) and EDDC (industrial), quotas, rationing, surrender of purchases  c) administered prices; fixed exchange rate; exporters surrendered 100% of earnings
