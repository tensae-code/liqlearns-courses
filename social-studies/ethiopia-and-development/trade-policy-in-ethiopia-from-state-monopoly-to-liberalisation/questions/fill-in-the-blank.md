# Trade policy in Ethiopia — from state monopoly to liberalisation — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word or words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The AMC handled ____ goods under the Derg.
**Answer: agricultural**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Exporters had to surrender ____ of their foreign-exchange earnings.
**Answer: 100%**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Maximum import duties were cut from 280% to 80% and then to ____.
