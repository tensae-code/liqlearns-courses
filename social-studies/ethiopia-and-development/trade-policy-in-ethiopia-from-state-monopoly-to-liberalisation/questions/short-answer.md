# Trade policy in Ethiopia — from state monopoly to liberalisation — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What were the AMC and EDDC?
**Answer: State corporations: the AMC for agricultural goods and the EDDC for industrial goods.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Who set farm prices, and from what?
**Answer: The Ministry of Agriculture, from cost-of-production studies.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name three liberalisation measures from 1992.
**Answer: Any three of: grain quota abolished; movement controls removed; export taxes abolished except coffee; managed float; duty drawback; duty-free inputs for exporters; lower coffee licence fees; import-duty cuts.**
