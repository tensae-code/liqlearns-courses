# Trade policy in Ethiopia — from state monopoly to liberalisation — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Under the Derg, private wholesalers kept all of their purchases.
**Answer: False**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Farm prices under the Derg were set by open bargaining in markets.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** After 1992, export taxes were abolished except on coffee.
**Answer: True**
