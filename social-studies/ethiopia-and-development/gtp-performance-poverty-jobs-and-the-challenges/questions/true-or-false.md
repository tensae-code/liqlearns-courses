# GTP performance — poverty, jobs and the challenges — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** GDP per capita rose from $389 to $1,080 and the poverty headcount fell from 29.6% to 23.5%.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The Gini edged up from 0.30 to 0.33 while urban unemployment rose to 18.7%, youth 25.7%.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The named challenges: too few jobs, debt distress, uncompetitive concentrated exports, and mismanagement and corruption.
**Answer: True**
