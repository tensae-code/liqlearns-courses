# Hands-on — GTP performance — poverty, jobs and the challenges

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Two lines crossing
Draw two lines — poverty falling (29.6 to 23.5) and the Gini inching up (0.30 to 0.33) — on one chart, with the unemployment figure standing apart. Write the one-sentence story the chart tells.

## Challenge brief
Take one named challenge and write a five-line brief: the evidence figure, who it hurts most, and the first policy that answers it — drawing the policy only from lessons in this course.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
