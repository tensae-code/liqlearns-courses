# What the HDI leaves out — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Equal weights for unlike things"?
a) The HDI weights its components equally, implying a year of life's value varies with national income.  b) The HDI ignores inequality, poverty and gender disparity, and its components overlap.  c) Scores capped at 1 leave rich countries almost no room to register further income gains.
**Answer: a) The HDI weights its components equally, implying a year of life's value varies with national income.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Overlap — and what is ignored"?
a) Scores capped at 1 leave rich countries almost no room to register further income gains.  b) The HDI weights its components equally, implying a year of life's value varies with national income.  c) The HDI ignores inequality, poverty and gender disparity, and its components overlap.
**Answer: c) The HDI ignores inequality, poverty and gender disparity, and its components overlap.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "The ceiling at 1"?
a) The HDI ignores inequality, poverty and gender disparity, and its components overlap.  b) Scores capped at 1 leave rich countries almost no room to register further income gains.  c) The HDI weights its components equally, implying a year of life's value varies with national income.
**Answer: b) Scores capped at 1 leave rich countries almost no room to register further income gains.**
