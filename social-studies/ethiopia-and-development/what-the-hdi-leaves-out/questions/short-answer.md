# What the HDI leaves out — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Equal weights for unlike things" in your own words.
**Answer:** The HDI weights its components equally, implying a year of life's value varies with national income.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Overlap — and what is ignored" in your own words.
**Answer:** The HDI ignores inequality, poverty and gender disparity, and its components overlap.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "The ceiling at 1" in your own words.
**Answer:** Scores capped at 1 leave rich countries almost no room to register further income gains.
