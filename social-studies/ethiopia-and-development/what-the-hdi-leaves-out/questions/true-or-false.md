# What the HDI leaves out — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The HDI weights income ten times more than health.
**Answer: False — All three components carry equal weight — that equal weighting is itself the first criticism.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The HDI fully adjusts for inequality inside each country.
**Answer: False — Adjusting for inequality is the separate inequality-adjusted HDI's job — the plain HDI ignores it.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Rich countries can raise their HDI without limit.
**Answer: False — The scale stops at 1 — near it, further income gains register almost nothing.**
