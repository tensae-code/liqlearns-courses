# Imperial-era industry — import substitution behind tariffs — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "The strategy — light industry behind protection" in your own words.
**Answer:** Imperial industry policy promoted import-substituting light industry, financed by expected foreign investment and shielded by tariffs, bans, incentives and credit.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "The state's own investments" in your own words.
**Answer:** The state invested directly in oil refinery, cement, sugar and textiles.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "The result — a weak, dual base" in your own words.
**Answer:** The result was a weak, dual base: handicraft and modern halves each contributing about half of manufacturing value added, the modern half foreign-dominated.
