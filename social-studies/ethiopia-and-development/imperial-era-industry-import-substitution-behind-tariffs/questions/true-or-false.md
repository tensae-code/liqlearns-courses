# Imperial-era industry — import substitution behind tariffs — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Imperial state exported heavy machinery as its industrial strategy.
**Answer: False — The strategy was import-substituting LIGHT industry for the home market, behind protective tariffs.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The state invested in nothing, leaving all industry to foreigners.
**Answer: False — It invested directly in the capital-heavy sectors — refinery, cement, sugar, textiles.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The modern sub-sector was mostly Ethiopian-owned.
**Answer: False — The modern half was predominantly FOREIGN-owned — and capital-intensive, in a labour-rich economy.**
