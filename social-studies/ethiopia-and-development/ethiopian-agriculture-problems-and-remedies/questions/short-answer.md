# Ethiopian agriculture — problems and remedies — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Nature's problem — drought" in your own words.
**Answer:** The core natural problem is recurrent drought in rain-dependent highland agriculture.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Human-made problems" in your own words.
**Answer:** Human-made problems include fragmentation, poor infrastructure (75% of households far from transport), no credit, insecure land rights, erosion and weak markets.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Remedies" in your own words.
**Answer:** Remedies include irrigation, afforestation, drought-resistant crops, secure land rights, stronger extension, farmer literacy and rural infrastructure.
