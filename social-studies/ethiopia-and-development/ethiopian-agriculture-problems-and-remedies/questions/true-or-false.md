# Ethiopian agriculture — problems and remedies — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Ethiopian agriculture is mostly irrigated, so drought barely matters.
**Answer: False — It is RAIN-DEPENDENT — that dependence is exactly why recurrent drought is the core natural problem.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Only 5% of farm households are far from transport lines.
**Answer: False — The figure is about 75% — three quarters of farm households far from transport and communication.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The lesson offers no remedies for the problems.
**Answer: False — Seven remedies are listed, each answering a named problem — irrigation first among them.**
