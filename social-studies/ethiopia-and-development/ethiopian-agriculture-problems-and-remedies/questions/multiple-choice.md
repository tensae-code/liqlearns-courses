# Ethiopian agriculture — problems and remedies — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Nature's problem — drought"?
a) The core natural problem is recurrent drought in rain-dependent highland agriculture.  b) Human-made problems include fragmentation, poor infrastructure (75% of households far from transport), no credit, insecure land rights, erosion and weak markets.  c) Remedies include irrigation, afforestation, drought-resistant crops, secure land rights, stronger extension, farmer literacy and rural infrastructure.
**Answer: a) The core natural problem is recurrent drought in rain-dependent highland agriculture.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Human-made problems"?
a) Remedies include irrigation, afforestation, drought-resistant crops, secure land rights, stronger extension, farmer literacy and rural infrastructure.  b) The core natural problem is recurrent drought in rain-dependent highland agriculture.  c) Human-made problems include fragmentation, poor infrastructure (75% of households far from transport), no credit, insecure land rights, erosion and weak markets.
**Answer: c) Human-made problems include fragmentation, poor infrastructure (75% of households far from transport), no credit, insecure land rights, erosion and weak markets.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Remedies"?
