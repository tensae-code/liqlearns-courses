# Creativity → innovation → entrepreneurship — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Creativity develops new ideas and new ways of seeing problems and opportunities.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Creativity develops new ideas and new ways of seeing problems and opportunities.
**Answer: False** — that statement belongs to sub lesson 1 (Creativity develops the ideas); sub lesson 2 says: Innovation applies creative solutions to problems and opportunities to improve lives and enrich society.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Creativity and innovation together open new niches and efficiencies that give the entrepreneur a competitive advantage.
**Answer: True**
