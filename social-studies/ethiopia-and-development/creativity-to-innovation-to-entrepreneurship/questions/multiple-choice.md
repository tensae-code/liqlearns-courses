# Creativity → innovation → entrepreneurship — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Creativity develops the ideas"?
a) new ideas and new ways of seeing problems and opportunities  b) creative solutions applied to problems and opportunities  c) new niches and efficiencies giving competitive advantage
**Answer: a) new ideas and new ways of seeing problems and opportunities**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Innovation applies them"?
a) new ideas and new ways of seeing problems and opportunities  b) new niches and efficiencies giving competitive advantage  c) creative solutions applied to problems and opportunities
**Answer: c) creative solutions applied to problems and opportunities**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Advantage — and national growth"?
a) creative solutions applied to problems and opportunities  b) new niches and efficiencies giving competitive advantage  c) new ideas and new ways of seeing problems and opportunities
**Answer: b) new niches and efficiencies giving competitive advantage**
