# Creativity → innovation → entrepreneurship — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Creativity develops the ideas" in your own words.
**Answer:** Creativity develops new ideas and new ways of seeing problems and opportunities.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Innovation applies them" in your own words.
**Answer:** Innovation applies creative solutions to problems and opportunities to improve lives and enrich society.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Advantage — and national growth" in your own words.
**Answer:** Creativity and innovation together open new niches and efficiencies that give the entrepreneur a competitive advantage.
