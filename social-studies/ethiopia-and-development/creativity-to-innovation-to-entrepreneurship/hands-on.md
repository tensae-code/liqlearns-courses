# Hands-on — Creativity → innovation → entrepreneurship

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Staircase relay: three learners stand on three levels — idea, applied idea, business — and pass an object up, each narrating their step's work before passing.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — a three-step staircase — idea, applied idea, business — with competitive advantage at the top — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
