# Creativity → innovation → entrepreneurship

## Do it first

An idea in a notebook, the same idea built and sold, and a business thriving on it: three stages of one journey. Which stage creates the idea, which applies it, which profits from it?

## Sub lesson 1 — Creativity develops the ideas

The chain begins: creativity develops new ideas and new ways of seeing problems and opportunities. Without this source, there is nothing for the later steps to work on.

[Picture 24084 — a three-step staircase — idea, applied idea, business — with competitive advantage at the top]

## Sub lesson 2 — Innovation applies them

Innovation applies creative solutions to those problems and opportunities to improve lives and enrich society. The applied step is where value first appears outside the inventor's notebook.

## Sub lesson 3 — Advantage — and national growth

Together they open new niches and efficiencies that give the entrepreneur a competitive advantage — which is why the three are called important contributors to a nation's economic growth. A firm's edge, summed across firms, becomes a country's growth.
