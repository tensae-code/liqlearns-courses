# Decentralization — the advantages — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Services tailored to local tastes" in your own words.
**Answer:** Local government tailors services to local tastes and responds to actual preferences.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Competition between jurisdictions" in your own words.
**Answer:** Mobility creates intergovernmental competition — citizens lost to better-run neighbours discipline managers.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Many experiments" in your own words.
**Answer:** Local experiments innovate, and successes are copyable elsewhere.
