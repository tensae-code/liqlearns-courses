# Decentralization — the advantages — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Local government tailors services to local tastes and responds to actual preferences.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Mobility creates intergovernmental competition — citizens lost to better-run neighbours discipline managers.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Local experiments innovate, and successes are copyable elsewhere.
**Answer: True**
