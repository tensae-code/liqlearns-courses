# Hands-on — Decentralization — the advantages

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Three towns
Draw three neighbouring towns offering three different service bundles, with citizens choosing between them. Label the tailoring, the competition and one experiment a town is running.

## Weigh the unit
Set this lesson against the last: build a two-column verdict on decentralization for one named service (primary schools), with the strongest item from each side, and rule — with your reason in one line.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
