Grade 10 teacher-guide note (placement 18): the naming of the three approaches comes from *Economics, Teacher Guide Book, Grade 10*, printed p. 78 (teacher guide — evidence). The Grade 10 student textbook teaches only the expenditure side (its Unit 7) and never names the other two approaches; the Grade 11 student textbook (Unit 3) later teaches all three in full, and this course's macroeconomics lessons build them out one by one.

**Acceptance count: taught in 1 country (Ethiopia).**

Notation error carried from the placements, taught corrected and named: §7.1 writes net exports as Export(X) − Import(I), using I for imports where I already means investment. This lesson writes net exports NX = X − M, keeping I for investment alone, and names the book's notation clash here only.
