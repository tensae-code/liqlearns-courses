# GDP and its components — the Ethiopian case — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A newly built house belongs to _____ in GDP — not to consumption.
**Answer: investment**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Social security and benefits — _____ — are excluded from G, since they buy no output.
**Answer: transfer payments**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** NX removes the foreign-made part so GDP counts only _____.
**Answer: domestic production**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** The three GDP approaches are income, expenditure and product — the _____ approach.
**Answer: value-added**
