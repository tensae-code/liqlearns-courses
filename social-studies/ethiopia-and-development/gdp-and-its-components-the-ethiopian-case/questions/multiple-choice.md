# GDP and its components — the Ethiopian case — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Consumption and investment"?
a) C: durables, nondurables, services; I: equipment, inventories, structures  b) state purchases of goods and services count; transfer payments do not  c) imports subtracted because they already sit inside C, I and G
**Answer: a) C: durables, nondurables, services; I: equipment, inventories, structures**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Government purchases — and what is excluded"?
a) C: durables, nondurables, services; I: equipment, inventories, structures  b) imports subtracted because they already sit inside C, I and G  c) state purchases of goods and services count; transfer payments do not
**Answer: c) state purchases of goods and services count; transfer payments do not**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Net exports — why imports leave"?
a) state purchases of goods and services count; transfer payments do not  b) imports subtracted because they already sit inside C, I and G  c) C: durables, nondurables, services; I: equipment, inventories, structures
**Answer: b) imports subtracted because they already sit inside C, I and G**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Which statement best matches "GDP has three measurement approaches (teacher guide)"?
a) income, expenditure and product/value-added — three approaches to one GDP  b) a different part of this lesson, as stated in the earlier sub lessons  c) the opposite claim to the one this sub lesson teaches
**Answer: a) income, expenditure and product/value-added — three approaches to one GDP**
