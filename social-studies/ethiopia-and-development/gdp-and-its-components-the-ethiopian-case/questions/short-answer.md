# GDP and its components — the Ethiopian case — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Consumption and investment" in your own words.
**Answer:** New housing counts as investment, not consumption, in GDP.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Government purchases — and what is excluded" in your own words.
**Answer:** Transfer payments are excluded from government purchases because they buy no output.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Net exports — why imports leave" in your own words.
**Answer:** Imports are subtracted so GDP counts only domestic production, since imports already sit inside C, I and G.

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Explain "GDP has three measurement approaches (teacher guide)" in your own words.
**Answer:** GDP can be measured by the income, expenditure and product (value-added) approaches.
