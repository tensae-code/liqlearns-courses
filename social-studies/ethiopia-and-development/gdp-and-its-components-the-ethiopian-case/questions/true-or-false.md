# GDP and its components — the Ethiopian case — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** New housing counts as investment, not consumption, in GDP.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Transfer payments are excluded from government purchases because they buy no output.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Imports are subtracted so GDP counts only domestic production, since imports already sit inside C, I and G.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** GDP can be measured by the income, expenditure and product (value-added) approaches.
**Answer: True**
