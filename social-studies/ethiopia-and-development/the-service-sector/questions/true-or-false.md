# The service sector — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Banking belongs to the manufacturing sector.
**Answer: False — Banking and insurance are services — listed inside the service sector's span.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** All services serve consumers only.
**Answer: False — Some serve businesses, some consumers, and some — like banking — both: the mixed kind.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Teaching is a physical service activity.
**Answer: False — Education and training are INTELLECTUAL activities. Physical ones act on things — repairs, hairdressing, cooking.**
