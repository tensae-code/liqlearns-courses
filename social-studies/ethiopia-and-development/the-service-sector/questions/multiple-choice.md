# The service sector — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "What the sector contains"?
a) The service sector spans trade, hotels, transport, education, banking, public administration, health and more.  b) Service industries are consumer-oriented, business-oriented or mixed.  c) Service activities divide into physical, intellectual and aesthetic.
**Answer: a) The service sector spans trade, hotels, transport, education, banking, public administration, health and more.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Whom services serve"?
a) Service activities divide into physical, intellectual and aesthetic.  b) The service sector spans trade, hotels, transport, education, banking, public administration, health and more.  c) Service industries are consumer-oriented, business-oriented or mixed.
**Answer: c) Service industries are consumer-oriented, business-oriented or mixed.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Three kinds of activity"?
a) Service industries are consumer-oriented, business-oriented or mixed.  b) Service activities divide into physical, intellectual and aesthetic.  c) The service sector spans trade, hotels, transport, education, banking, public administration, health and more.
**Answer: b) Service activities divide into physical, intellectual and aesthetic.**
