# The service sector — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "What the sector contains" in your own words.
**Answer:** The service sector spans trade, hotels, transport, education, banking, public administration, health and more.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Whom services serve" in your own words.
**Answer:** Service industries are consumer-oriented, business-oriented or mixed.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Three kinds of activity" in your own words.
**Answer:** Service activities divide into physical, intellectual and aesthetic.
