# The service sector — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or figure from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Hotels and restaurants belong to the ____ sector.
**Answer: service**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A bank, serving households and firms alike, is a ____ service.
**Answer: mixed**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Museums, theatre and music are ____ services.
**Answer: aesthetic**
