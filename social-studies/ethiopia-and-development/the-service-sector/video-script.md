# Video script — The service sector

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The service sector spans trade, hotels and restaurants, transport and communication, education, banking and insurance, public administration and defence, health and other services — everything produced that is not a farmed or manufactured thing.

Its industries are consumer-oriented, business-oriented or mixed: a haircut serves a consumer, freight serves a business, and a bank serves both at once.

Its activities divide into physical (repairing cars, hairdressing, cooking), intellectual (education and training) and aesthetic (museums, theatre, music) — services done to things, to minds, and to the senses.
