# Hands-on — Structural adjustment — Ethiopia's 1993–1996 program

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Three levers
Draw the console with its three levers. Under each lever write one real measure from the lesson it stands for. Then say which lever you would pull first in 1993, and why.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the console from memory and label every part. Then open the lesson, compare, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
