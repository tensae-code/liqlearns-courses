# Structural adjustment — Ethiopia's 1993–1996 program — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name two supporters of Ethiopia's SAP.
**Answer: Any two of: the IMF, the World Bank, the African Development Fund, donors.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** State the program's objectives.
**Answer: Revive growth, reduce macroeconomic distortion, improve efficiency and resource allocation, expand productive capacity.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name the three components of the SAP.
**Answer: Expenditure-reducing policies, expenditure-switching policies and institutional policy reform.**
