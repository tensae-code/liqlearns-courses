# Startups vs small businesses — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A startup is a temporary business model built for rapid growth and the largest possible market share.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A small business is content to serve a small portion of a large market steadily.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Angel investors typically take a 5–10% share of a startup for their seed money.
**Answer: True**
