# Startups vs small businesses — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A startup is built for _____ and the largest possible market share.
**Answer: rapid growth**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A small business serves a small share of a large market _____ — by design, not by failure.
**Answer: steadily**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Angels typically take a _____ share of the business for their seed money.
**Answer: 5–10%**
