# Startups vs small businesses — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "The startup — built for rapid growth"?
a) a temporary model built for rapid growth and the largest possible market share  b) content with a small, steady share of a large market  c) seed funding from angels (typically 5–10% stakes) and venture-capital firms
**Answer: a) a temporary model built for rapid growth and the largest possible market share**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "The small business — steady by design"?
a) a temporary model built for rapid growth and the largest possible market share  b) seed funding from angels (typically 5–10% stakes) and venture-capital firms  c) content with a small, steady share of a large market
**Answer: c) content with a small, steady share of a large market**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "How startups are funded"?
a) content with a small, steady share of a large market  b) seed funding from angels (typically 5–10% stakes) and venture-capital firms  c) a temporary model built for rapid growth and the largest possible market share
**Answer: b) seed funding from angels (typically 5–10% stakes) and venture-capital firms**
