# Startups vs small businesses — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "The startup — built for rapid growth" in your own words.
**Answer:** A startup is a temporary business model built for rapid growth and the largest possible market share.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "The small business — steady by design" in your own words.
**Answer:** A small business is content to serve a small portion of a large market steadily.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "How startups are funded" in your own words.
**Answer:** Angel investors typically take a 5–10% share of a startup for their seed money.
