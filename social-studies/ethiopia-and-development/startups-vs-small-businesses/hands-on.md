# Hands-on — Startups vs small businesses

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
A rocket card and a shop card. Learners pitch each business to the class in one minute — the app team pitching growth and market share, the baker pitching steady service — then angel learners offer seed money for stakes of 5–10% and defend their price.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — a rocket beside a steady shop — the startup and the small business — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
