# Finding the window — opportunity and creative destruction — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Ideas are not opportunities" in your own words.
**Answer:** An entrepreneurial opportunity exists where identifiable consumer demand meets the possibility of satisfying it.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Judging the window" in your own words.
**Answer:** Market size and the length of the window of opportunity are the primary factors determining risk and reward.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Creative destruction — Schumpeter's five routes" in your own words.
**Answer:** Schumpeter called entrepreneurial innovation 'creative destruction' — the disruptive force that sustains growth while destroying established firms.
