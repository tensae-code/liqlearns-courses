# Finding the window — opportunity and creative destruction — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** An opportunity exists where identifiable consumer _____ meets the possibility of satisfying it.
**Answer: demand**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** _____ and the length of the window are the primary factors determining risk and reward.
**Answer: market size**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Schumpeter's name for innovation's disruptive force is _____.
**Answer: creative destruction**
