# Finding the window — opportunity and creative destruction — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Ideas are not opportunities"?
a) identifiable demand that can be satisfied; alertness, consumers and associates as sources  b) market size, window length, capital and personal fit as the judging gauges  c) innovation as creative destruction, with five routes to new opportunity
**Answer: a) identifiable demand that can be satisfied; alertness, consumers and associates as sources**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Judging the window"?
a) identifiable demand that can be satisfied; alertness, consumers and associates as sources  b) innovation as creative destruction, with five routes to new opportunity  c) market size, window length, capital and personal fit as the judging gauges
**Answer: c) market size, window length, capital and personal fit as the judging gauges**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Creative destruction — Schumpeter's five routes"?
a) market size, window length, capital and personal fit as the judging gauges  b) innovation as creative destruction, with five routes to new opportunity  c) identifiable demand that can be satisfied; alertness, consumers and associates as sources
**Answer: b) innovation as creative destruction, with five routes to new opportunity**
