# Finding the window — opportunity and creative destruction — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** An entrepreneurial opportunity exists where identifiable consumer demand meets the possibility of satisfying it.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** An entrepreneurial opportunity exists where identifiable consumer demand meets the possibility of satisfying it.
**Answer: False** — that statement belongs to sub lesson 1 (Ideas are not opportunities); sub lesson 2 says: Market size and the length of the window of opportunity are the primary factors determining risk and reward.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Schumpeter called entrepreneurial innovation 'creative destruction' — the disruptive force that sustains growth while destroying established firms.
**Answer: True**
