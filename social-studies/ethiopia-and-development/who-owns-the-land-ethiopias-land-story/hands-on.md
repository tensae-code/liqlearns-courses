# Hands-on — Who owns the land? Ethiopia's land story

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Timeline walk: three stations (feudal estate, 1975 poster, today's certificate). At each, a learner states the farmer's rights there in one sentence; the class names what changed and what did not.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — a three-panel timeline — feudal estate, 1975 reform poster, today's holding-right certificate and lease — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
