# Lesson plan — Who owns the land? Ethiopia's land story

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Ethiopia and Development · **Lesson:** Who owns the land? Ethiopia's land story
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 3 — Economic Resources and Markets of the Grade 9 Economics course (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Three regimes: feudal holdings before 1974; 'Land to the Tiller' in 1975 bringing state ownership; and from 1991 continued state ownership with lifetime holding rights for rural peasants and pastoralists and 99-year urban leases.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the lessons before it in Ethiopia and Development, and on the earlier-grade lessons of this course.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of ..." in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.
