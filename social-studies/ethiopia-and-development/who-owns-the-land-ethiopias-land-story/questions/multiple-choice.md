# Who owns the land? Ethiopia's land story — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Before 1974 — feudal holdings"?
a) inequitable feudal holdings — tillers holding little or none  b) the feudal system removed, but the outcome was state ownership, not tiller ownership  c) lifetime holding rights except sale and mortgage; 99-year urban leases; access and compensation criticised
**Answer: a) inequitable feudal holdings — tillers holding little or none**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "1975 — Land to the Tiller"?
a) inequitable feudal holdings — tillers holding little or none  b) lifetime holding rights except sale and mortgage; 99-year urban leases; access and compensation criticised  c) the feudal system removed, but the outcome was state ownership, not tiller ownership
**Answer: c) the feudal system removed, but the outcome was state ownership, not tiller ownership**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "From 1991 — holding rights and leases"?
a) the feudal system removed, but the outcome was state ownership, not tiller ownership  b) lifetime holding rights except sale and mortgage; 99-year urban leases; access and compensation criticised  c) inequitable feudal holdings — tillers holding little or none
**Answer: b) lifetime holding rights except sale and mortgage; 99-year urban leases; access and compensation criticised**
