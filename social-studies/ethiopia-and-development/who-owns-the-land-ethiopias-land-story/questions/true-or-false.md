# Who owns the land? Ethiopia's land story — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Before 1974, Ethiopia's land was held in inequitable feudal holdings.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Before 1974, Ethiopia's land was held in inequitable feudal holdings.
**Answer: False** — that statement belongs to sub lesson 1 (Before 1974 — feudal holdings); sub lesson 2 says: In 1975 the feudal system was removed under 'Land to the Tiller', but the outcome was state ownership rather than ownership by the tillers.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** From 1991, rural peasants and pastoralists hold lifetime holding rights covering everything except sale and mortgage, and urban land is held on 99-year leases.
**Answer: True**
