# Who owns the land? Ethiopia's land story — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Before 1974 — feudal holdings" in your own words.
**Answer:** Before 1974, Ethiopia's land was held in inequitable feudal holdings.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "1975 — Land to the Tiller" in your own words.
**Answer:** In 1975 the feudal system was removed under 'Land to the Tiller', but the outcome was state ownership rather than ownership by the tillers.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "From 1991 — holding rights and leases" in your own words.
**Answer:** From 1991, rural peasants and pastoralists hold lifetime holding rights covering everything except sale and mortgage, and urban land is held on 99-year leases.
