# Who owns the land? Ethiopia's land story — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Before 1974, land sat in inequitable _____ holdings.
**Answer: feudal**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** After 1975 the outcome was _____ ownership rather than ownership by the tillers.
**Answer: state**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Holding rights cover everything except sale and _____.
**Answer: mortgage**
