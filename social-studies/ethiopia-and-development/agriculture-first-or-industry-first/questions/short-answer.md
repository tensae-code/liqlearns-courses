# Agriculture first or industry first — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What did Western economists see in industry?
**Answer: More opportunities to lead development than in other sectors.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why does farm productivity move the whole economy in Ethiopia?
**Answer: Because most people farm, most income is farm income and most exports are farm goods.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** On what does the key rest its verdict?
**Answer: On Ethiopia's objective realities — agriculture's dominance of output, exports and employment.**
