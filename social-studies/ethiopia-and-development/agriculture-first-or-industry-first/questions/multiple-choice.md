# Agriculture first or industry first — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "The industry-first case"?
a) Western economists long prioritised industry as the sector with more opportunities to lead  b) industry-first cannot succeed where agriculture dominates output, exports and employment  c) Ethiopia prioritised agriculture; ADLI is its functional strategy
**Answer: a) Western economists long prioritised industry as the sector with more opportunities to lead**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Why that strategy fails where farming dominates"?
a) industry-first cannot succeed where agriculture dominates output, exports and employment  b) Ethiopia prioritised agriculture; ADLI is its functional strategy  c) Western economists long prioritised industry as the sector with more opportunities to lead
**Answer: a) industry-first cannot succeed where agriculture dominates output, exports and employment**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Ethiopia's settlement — ADLI"?
a) Ethiopia prioritised agriculture; ADLI is its functional strategy  b) Western economists long prioritised industry as the sector with more opportunities to lead  c) industry-first cannot succeed where agriculture dominates output, exports and employment
**Answer: a) Ethiopia prioritised agriculture; ADLI is its functional strategy**
