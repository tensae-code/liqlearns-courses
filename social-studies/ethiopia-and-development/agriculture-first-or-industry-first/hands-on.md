# Hands-on — Agriculture first or industry first

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Argue both sides
In pairs: one argues industry first, one agriculture first, for three minutes each. Then swap sides and argue the opposite case. Finish by saying which argument survived the swap.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the balance from memory — plough and factory — and label which way it tips for Ethiopia and why. Then open the lesson and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
