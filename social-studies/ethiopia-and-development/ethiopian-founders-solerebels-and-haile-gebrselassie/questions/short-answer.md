# Ethiopian founders — SoleRebels and Haile Gebrselassie — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Bethlehem Tilahun Alemu — SoleRebels" in your own words.
**Answer:** Bethlehem Tilahun Alemu founded SoleRebels at 25, making shoes from recycled tyres, and Forbes named it the fastest-growing brand in Africa in 2014.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Haile Gebrselassie — from track to business" in your own words.
**Answer:** Haile Gebrselassie's Marathon Motors assembles Hyundai vehicles and rolled out its first electric car.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "What the two cases teach" in your own words.
**Answer:** Haile Gebrselassie's investments across hospitality, entertainment, real estate, agriculture and technology employ over 3,000 people.
