# Ethiopian founders — SoleRebels and Haile Gebrselassie — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Bethlehem Tilahun Alemu founded SoleRebels at 25, making shoes from recycled tyres, and Forbes named it the fastest-growing brand in Africa in 2014.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Bethlehem Tilahun Alemu founded SoleRebels at 25, making shoes from recycled tyres, and Forbes named it the fastest-growing brand in Africa in 2014.
**Answer: False** — that statement belongs to sub lesson 1 (Bethlehem Tilahun Alemu — SoleRebels); sub lesson 2 says: Haile Gebrselassie's Marathon Motors assembles Hyundai vehicles and rolled out its first electric car.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Haile Gebrselassie's investments across hospitality, entertainment, real estate, agriculture and technology employ over 3,000 people.
**Answer: True**
