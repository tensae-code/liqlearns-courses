# Hands-on — Ethiopian founders — SoleRebels and Haile Gebrselassie

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Gallery walk: two 'founder stations' with object cards (a tyre shoe, a car, a hotel key). Learners visit each, then retell the case to a partner without notes.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — a recycled-tyre shoe beside a locally assembled car, each with its founder's name — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
