# Land as a resource in Ethiopia — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Ethiopia's total area is 1,106,000 km², with about 35 percent suitable for agriculture.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Ethiopia's total area is 1,106,000 km², with about 35 percent suitable for agriculture.
**Answer: False** — that statement belongs to sub lesson 1 (Area and the farmable share); sub lesson 2 says: The highlands' two main suitable soils are red-to-reddish-brown soils and brownish-grey-to-black high-clay soils.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Altitude creates varied agro-ecological zones, so many crops can grow, including export crops earning foreign currency.
**Answer: True**
