# Land as a resource in Ethiopia — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Area and the farmable share" in your own words.
**Answer:** Ethiopia's total area is 1,106,000 km², with about 35 percent suitable for agriculture.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "The two highland soils" in your own words.
**Answer:** The highlands' two main suitable soils are red-to-reddish-brown soils and brownish-grey-to-black high-clay soils.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Altitude zones — and land as status" in your own words.
**Answer:** Altitude creates varied agro-ecological zones, so many crops can grow, including export crops earning foreign currency.
