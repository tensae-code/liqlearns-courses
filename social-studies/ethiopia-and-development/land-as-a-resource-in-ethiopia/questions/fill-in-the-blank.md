# Land as a resource in Ethiopia — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** About _____ of Ethiopia's 1,106,000 km² is suitable for agriculture.
**Answer: 35 percent**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** One highland soil type is _____-to-reddish-brown — mineral-rich, well drained and friable.
**Answer: red**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** _____ creates varied agro-ecological zones, so many different crops can grow.
**Answer: altitude**
