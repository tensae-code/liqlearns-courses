# Hands-on — Land as a resource in Ethiopia

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Map game: learners colour a rough Ethiopia outline — farming-suitable areas one colour, the rest another — then place crop cards where altitude zones allow them.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — a highland map with the two soil types coloured and altitude zones stacked beside it — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
