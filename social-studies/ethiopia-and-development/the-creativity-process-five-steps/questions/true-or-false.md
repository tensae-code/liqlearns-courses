# The creativity process — five steps from problem to proven idea — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The first steps are opportunity or problem recognition, then immersion: collecting information and dreaming up alternatives without yet evaluating them.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The first steps are opportunity or problem recognition, then immersion: collecting information and dreaming up alternatives without yet evaluating them.
**Answer: False** — that statement belongs to sub lesson 1 (Steps 1–2 — recognition and immersion); sub lesson 2 says: Incubation lets the subconscious arrange the information into new patterns, and insight is the solution flashing into mind at an unexpected moment.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Verification and application prove the solution has merit through supporting evidence, logical persuasion and experiment.
**Answer: True**
