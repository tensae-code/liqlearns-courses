# The creativity process — five steps from problem to proven idea — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Steps 1–2 — recognition and immersion" in your own words.
**Answer:** The first steps are opportunity or problem recognition, then immersion: collecting information and dreaming up alternatives without yet evaluating them.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Steps 3–4 — incubation and insight" in your own words.
**Answer:** Incubation lets the subconscious arrange the information into new patterns, and insight is the solution flashing into mind at an unexpected moment.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Step 5 — verification and application" in your own words.
**Answer:** Verification and application prove the solution has merit through supporting evidence, logical persuasion and experiment.
