# The creativity process — five steps from problem to proven idea — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Steps 1–2 — recognition and immersion"?
a) recognising the problem, then immersing — collecting information and alternatives without evaluating  b) incubation — the subconscious rearranging in the background; insight — the flash at an unexpected moment  c) proving the solution's merit through evidence, logical persuasion and experiment

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Step 5 — verification and application"?
