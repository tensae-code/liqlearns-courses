# Video script — The creativity process — five steps from problem to proven idea

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Step 1, opportunity or problem recognition: discovering a new opportunity or a problem needing resolution. Step 2, immersion: concentrating on the problem, recalling and collecting relevant information and dreaming up alternatives without yet evaluating them (teacher guide — evidence).

Step 3, incubation: the assembled information is held in mind while the subconscious keeps arranging it into new patterns, with no active work apparent. Step 4, insight: the solution flashing into mind at an unexpected moment — on the verge of sleep, in the shower, while running (teacher guide — evidence).

Step 5, verification and application: proving the solution has merit through supporting evidence, logical persuasion and experiment (teacher guide — evidence). The flash is not the finish — a creative idea earns its name only when it survives its test.
