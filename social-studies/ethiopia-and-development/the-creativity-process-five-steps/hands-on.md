# Hands-on — The creativity process — five steps from problem to proven idea

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Act the five steps in a relay: spot a problem card, immerse (scribble ideas for one minute), incubate (walk silently once round the room), insight (call it out), verify (test it against the group's questions).

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — a five-step path — spotting, diving in, letting it simmer, the flash, the test — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
