# Ethiopian industry — problems and remedies — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Finance and marketing problems"?
a) Finance problems: little farm surplus, import dependence, low savings, high collateral. Marketing: weak demand, missing information, cheap imports, bias against local goods.  b) Technology problems include capital-intensive methods ill-suited to a labour-surplus economy; inputs are blocked by scarce foreign exchange.  c) Remedies include stability, a regulated financial sector, infrastructure, skilled people, efficient civil service and law, and full-infrastructure zones.
