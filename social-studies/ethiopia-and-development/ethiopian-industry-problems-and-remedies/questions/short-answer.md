# Ethiopian industry — problems and remedies — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Finance and marketing problems" in your own words.
**Answer:** Finance problems: little farm surplus, import dependence, low savings, high collateral. Marketing: weak demand, missing information, cheap imports, bias against local goods.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Technology and inputs problems" in your own words.
**Answer:** Technology problems include capital-intensive methods ill-suited to a labour-surplus economy; inputs are blocked by scarce foreign exchange.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Remedies" in your own words.
**Answer:** Remedies include stability, a regulated financial sector, infrastructure, skilled people, efficient civil service and law, and full-infrastructure zones.
