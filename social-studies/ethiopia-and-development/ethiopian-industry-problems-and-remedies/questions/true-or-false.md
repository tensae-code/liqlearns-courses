# Ethiopian industry — problems and remedies — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Local products face no bias from Ethiopian consumers.
**Answer: False — Consumer bias against local products is one of the named marketing problems.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Capital-intensive methods suit Ethiopia's labour surplus perfectly.
**Answer: False — They are ill-suited: a labour-surplus economy needs methods that use its abundant labour, not scarce capital.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The lesson's remedy is to raise tariffs and nothing else.
**Answer: False — Six remedies are listed, and tariff protection is not among them — stability, finance, infrastructure, skills, administration and zones are.**
