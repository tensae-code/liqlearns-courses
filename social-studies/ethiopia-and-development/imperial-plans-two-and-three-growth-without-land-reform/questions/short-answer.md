# Imperial plans two and three — growth without land reform — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Plan two — agro-industry first" in your own words.
**Answer:** The second plan pushed large commercial farms over the smallholders who grew about 80% of cereals.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Plan three — the swing to the peasant" in your own words.
**Answer:** The third plan swung to agriculture and peasant farming with integrated rural development.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Growth without land reform" in your own words.
**Answer:** Growth did not improve most lives because feudal land tenure denied peasants rights over land.
