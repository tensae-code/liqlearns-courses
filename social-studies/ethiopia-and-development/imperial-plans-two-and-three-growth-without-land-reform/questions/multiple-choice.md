# Imperial plans two and three — growth without land reform — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Plan two — agro-industry first"?
a) The second plan pushed large commercial farms over the smallholders who grew about 80% of cereals  b) The third plan swung to agriculture and peasant farming with integrated rural development  c) Growth did not improve most lives because feudal land tenure denied peasants rights over land
**Answer: a) The second plan pushed large commercial farms over the smallholders who grew about 80% of cereals**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Plan three — the swing to the peasant"?
a) The third plan swung to agriculture and peasant farming with integrated rural development  b) Growth did not improve most lives because feudal land tenure denied peasants rights over land  c) The second plan pushed large commercial farms over the smallholders who grew about 80% of cereals
**Answer: a) The third plan swung to agriculture and peasant farming with integrated rural development**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Growth without land reform"?
a) Growth did not improve most lives because feudal land tenure denied peasants rights over land  b) The second plan pushed large commercial farms over the smallholders who grew about 80% of cereals  c) The third plan swung to agriculture and peasant farming with integrated rural development
**Answer: a) Growth did not improve most lives because feudal land tenure denied peasants rights over land**
