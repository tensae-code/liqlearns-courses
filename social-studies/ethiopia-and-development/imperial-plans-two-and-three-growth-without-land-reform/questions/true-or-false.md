# Imperial plans two and three — growth without land reform — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The second plan pushed large commercial farms over the smallholders who grew about 80% of cereals.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The third plan swung to agriculture and peasant farming with integrated rural development.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Growth did not improve most lives because feudal land tenure denied peasants rights over land.
**Answer: True**
