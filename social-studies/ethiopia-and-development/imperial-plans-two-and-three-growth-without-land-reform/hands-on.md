# Hands-on — Imperial plans two and three — growth without land reform

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Estate and plot
Draw a commercial sugar estate beside a tiny peasant plot, with a land deed locked away from the peasant. Annotate which plan favoured which side of the picture.

## Verdict paragraph
Write the era's verdict in five lines: the growth achieved, the evaluation missing, the lives unchanged, the cause named by the book, and the consequence of 1974.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
