# Video script — The Millennium Development Goals

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The Millennium Development Goals unified the UN development agenda into eight goals with targets for 2015: eradicate extreme poverty and hunger; universal primary education; gender equality and women's empowerment; reduce child mortality; improve maternal health; combat HIV/AIDS, malaria and other diseases; ensure environmental sustainability; and a global partnership for development.

The book credits them with lifting over a billion people from extreme poverty and expanding girls' schooling — the first time the whole development agenda moved under one set of targets, measurable progress followed on both.

Progress was uneven and bypassed the poorest, women, and people disadvantaged by age, disability or ethnicity — the averages improved while those at the very bottom were reached last, or not at all.
