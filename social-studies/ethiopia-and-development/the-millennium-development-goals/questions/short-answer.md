# The Millennium Development Goals — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Eight goals, one agenda" in your own words.
**Answer:** The MDGs set eight goals for 2015, from eradicating extreme poverty and hunger to a global partnership.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "What they achieved" in your own words.
**Answer:** The MDGs are credited with lifting over a billion people from extreme poverty and expanding girls' schooling.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Whom progress bypassed" in your own words.
**Answer:** MDG progress was uneven, bypassing the poorest, women, and people disadvantaged by age, disability or ethnicity.
