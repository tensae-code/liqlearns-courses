# The Millennium Development Goals — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Eight goals, one agenda"?
a) The MDGs set eight goals for 2015, from eradicating extreme poverty and hunger to a global partnership.  b) The MDGs are credited with lifting over a billion people from extreme poverty and expanding girls' schooling.  c) MDG progress was uneven, bypassing the poorest, women, and people disadvantaged by age, disability or ethnicity.
**Answer: a) The MDGs set eight goals for 2015, from eradicating extreme poverty and hunger to a global partnership.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "What they achieved"?
a) MDG progress was uneven, bypassing the poorest, women, and people disadvantaged by age, disability or ethnicity.  b) The MDGs set eight goals for 2015, from eradicating extreme poverty and hunger to a global partnership.  c) The MDGs are credited with lifting over a billion people from extreme poverty and expanding girls' schooling.
**Answer: c) The MDGs are credited with lifting over a billion people from extreme poverty and expanding girls' schooling.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Whom progress bypassed"?
a) The MDGs are credited with lifting over a billion people from extreme poverty and expanding girls' schooling.  b) MDG progress was uneven, bypassing the poorest, women, and people disadvantaged by age, disability or ethnicity.  c) The MDGs set eight goals for 2015, from eradicating extreme poverty and hunger to a global partnership.
**Answer: b) MDG progress was uneven, bypassing the poorest, women, and people disadvantaged by age, disability or ethnicity.**
