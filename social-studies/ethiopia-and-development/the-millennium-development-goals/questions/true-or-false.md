# The Millennium Development Goals — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The MDGs set seventeen goals for 2030.
**Answer: False — Seventeen goals for 2030 are the SDGs. The MDGs were EIGHT goals with a 2015 deadline.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The MDGs achieved none of their goals.
**Answer: False — The book credits real achievements — over a billion lifted from extreme poverty, girls' schooling expanded.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** MDG progress reached everyone equally.
**Answer: False — It was uneven — the poorest, women and those disadvantaged by age, disability or ethnicity were bypassed.**
