# Video script — Creativity — new ideas from old pieces

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Creativity is generating ideas, alternatives or possibilities useful for solving problems and communicating — seeing problems and opportunities in new ways. Its raw move: recombining elements already known but wrongly assumed unrelated.

Three marks define it: creativity is an ongoing process, not a single flash; it produces ideas with potential value; and it works by recombining. Anything missing one mark — a one-off accident, a useless novelty, a pure copy — is not quite creativity.

In organisational terms, creativity is the development of original, potentially useful ideas about products, practices, services or procedures. Firms schedule it, staff it and harvest it — the next lesson gives its five steps.
