# Hands-on — Creativity — new ideas from old pieces

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Recombination game: draw three object cards at random; in two minutes, the group must pitch a useful product combining all three. Repeat — creativity as a trainable move, not a gift.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — familiar objects being rearranged into a new useful combination — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
