# Creativity — new ideas from old pieces — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Seeing in new ways" in your own words.
**Answer:** Creativity generates useful ideas by seeing problems in new ways and recombining elements already known.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Three key marks" in your own words.
**Answer:** Creativity is an ongoing process that produces ideas with potential value by recombining.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Creativity in organisations" in your own words.
**Answer:** In organisations, creativity is the development of original, potentially useful ideas about products, practices, services or procedures.
