# Creativity — new ideas from old pieces — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Creativity generates useful ideas by seeing problems in new ways and recombining elements already known.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Creativity generates useful ideas by seeing problems in new ways and recombining elements already known.
**Answer: False** — that statement belongs to sub lesson 1 (Seeing in new ways); sub lesson 2 says: Creativity is an ongoing process that produces ideas with potential value by recombining.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In organisations, creativity is the development of original, potentially useful ideas about products, practices, services or procedures.
**Answer: True**
