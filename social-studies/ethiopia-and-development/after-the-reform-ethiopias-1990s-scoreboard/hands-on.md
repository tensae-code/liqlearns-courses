# Hands-on — After the reform — Ethiopia's 1990s scoreboard

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Read the scoreboard
Draw the falling deficit line with its one spike, and the rising approvals bar. Label the spike's cause from the lesson, and write one sentence on why approvals and commencements are different lines.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the scoreboard from memory and label every part. Then open the lesson, compare, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
