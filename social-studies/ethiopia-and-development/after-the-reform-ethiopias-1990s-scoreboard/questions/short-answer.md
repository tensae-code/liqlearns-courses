# After the reform — Ethiopia's 1990s scoreboard — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What three causes does the guide give for dismal 1960–1992 growth?
**Answer: Rigid macroeconomic policies, protracted war and recurrent drought.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How many projects actually commenced, and for how much?
**Answer: 1,491 projects, for 8.8 billion birr.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Why is 1998/99 an exception on the deficit line?
**Answer: It was the Ethio-Eritrea war year, with the highest recurrent expenditure.**
