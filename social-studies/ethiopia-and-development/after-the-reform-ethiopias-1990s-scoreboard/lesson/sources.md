Edition note: this guide is the 2011 GEQIP edition and is NOT the companion to the 2023 Grade 12 student textbook — a different author team, publisher, ISBN and curriculum (its eight units teach the Ethiopian economy sector by sector). It is used here as older-vintage Ethiopian-economy evidence only, never as an answer key for the 2023 student book.

Honesty: Two guide errors are corrected here and named: (1) the Content Check 8.2 key expands GDS as 'Gross Domestic Service' — Gross Domestic Saving is meant, and the corrected expansion is taught; (2) section 8.2's overview garbles its investment passage with an unexplained '50/50' token ('A total of 50/50 projects was approved…') — no project count is taught from that token; only the guide's coherent figures (41.5 billion birr approved; 1,491 projects commenced for 8.8 billion birr) are taught.
