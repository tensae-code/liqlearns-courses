# The capability approach — freedom as development — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Functionings — what people actually do" in your own words.
**Answer:** Functionings are what a person actually does and is, with the means at hand.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Capabilities — the real freedoms" in your own words.
**Answer:** Capabilities are the real freedoms to achieve valued functionings, chosen or not.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Conversion factors — and the fasting contrast" in your own words.
**Answer:** Personal, social and environmental conversion factors decide whether means become capabilities; the fasting rich man and the hungry farm-worker eat alike but differ in capability.
