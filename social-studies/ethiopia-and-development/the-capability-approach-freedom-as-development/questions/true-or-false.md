# The capability approach — freedom as development — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Functionings are the goods a person owns.
**Answer: False — Goods are means. Functionings are the achieved doings and beings — nourished, educated, participating.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A capability is a functioning a person has already achieved.
**Answer: False — Achieved functionings are functionings. Capabilities are the freedoms — including ones not yet, or never, exercised.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The fasting man and the farm-worker have equal capability to be nourished.
**Answer: False — They eat the same little, but his fast is chosen and hers is forced — his capability to be nourished is far greater.**
