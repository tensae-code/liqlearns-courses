# The capability approach — freedom as development — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Functionings — what people actually do"?
a) Functionings are what a person actually does and is, with the means at hand.  b) Capabilities are the real freedoms to achieve valued functionings, chosen or not.  c) Personal, social and environmental conversion factors decide whether means become capabilities; the fasting rich man and the hungry farm-worker eat alike but differ in capability.
**Answer: a) Functionings are what a person actually does and is, with the means at hand.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Capabilities — the real freedoms"?

**Answer: c) Capabilities are the real freedoms to achieve valued functionings, chosen or not.**
