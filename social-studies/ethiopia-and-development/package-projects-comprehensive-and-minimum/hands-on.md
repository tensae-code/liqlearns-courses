# Hands-on — Package projects — comprehensive and minimum

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Coverage maths
A budget can fully serve 2,000 farmers or minimally serve 200,000. Write what each group gets and loses, then say which you would choose for a country of smallholders — and what would change your mind.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the map from memory — four glowing zones, a dotted whole country — and label every part. Then open the lesson and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
