# Hands-on — Enterprise, entrepreneur, entrepreneurship

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Three learners wear the three words; the class calls situations and each learner steps forward only when their word is the actor. Mismatches restart the round with discussion.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — three nested panels — the process, the person, the legal business — with the five elements as a checklist — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
