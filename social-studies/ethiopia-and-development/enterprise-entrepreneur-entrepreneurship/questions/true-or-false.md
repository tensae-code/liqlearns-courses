# Enterprise, entrepreneur, entrepreneurship — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** An enterprise is a legal entity able to conduct business in its own right.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** An enterprise is a legal entity able to conduct business in its own right.
**Answer: False** — that statement belongs to sub lesson 1 (Enterprise — the legal entity); sub lesson 2 says: An entrepreneur is a person who creates and develops a business idea and takes the risk of setting up an enterprise.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Entrepreneurship is the process of spotting market opportunities and exploiting them for long-term gain, shifting people from job seekers to job creators.
**Answer: True**
