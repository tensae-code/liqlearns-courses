# Enterprise, entrepreneur, entrepreneurship — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Enterprise — the legal entity"?
a) a legal entity that can contract, own property, incur liabilities and hold bank accounts  b) the person who creates the business idea and takes the risk of setting it up  c) spotting opportunities and exploiting them for long-term gain; five named elements
**Answer: a) a legal entity that can contract, own property, incur liabilities and hold bank accounts**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Entrepreneur — the person"?
a) a legal entity that can contract, own property, incur liabilities and hold bank accounts  b) spotting opportunities and exploiting them for long-term gain; five named elements  c) the person who creates the business idea and takes the risk of setting it up
**Answer: c) the person who creates the business idea and takes the risk of setting it up**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Entrepreneurship — the process, five elements"?
a) the person who creates the business idea and takes the risk of setting it up  b) spotting opportunities and exploiting them for long-term gain; five named elements  c) a legal entity that can contract, own property, incur liabilities and hold bank accounts
**Answer: b) spotting opportunities and exploiting them for long-term gain; five named elements**
