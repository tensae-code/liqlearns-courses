# Enterprise, entrepreneur, entrepreneurship — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** An enterprise is a _____ able to conduct business in its own right.
**Answer: legal entity**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The entrepreneur creates the business idea and takes the _____ of setting it up.
**Answer: risk**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Entrepreneurship shifts people from job seekers to _____.
**Answer: job creators**
