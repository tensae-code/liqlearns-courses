# Enterprise, entrepreneur, entrepreneurship — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Enterprise — the legal entity" in your own words.
**Answer:** An enterprise is a legal entity able to conduct business in its own right.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Entrepreneur — the person" in your own words.
**Answer:** An entrepreneur is a person who creates and develops a business idea and takes the risk of setting up an enterprise.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Entrepreneurship — the process, five elements" in your own words.
**Answer:** Entrepreneurship is the process of spotting market opportunities and exploiting them for long-term gain, shifting people from job seekers to job creators.
