# Video script — Enterprise, entrepreneur, entrepreneurship

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

An enterprise is a legal entity able to conduct business in its own right: contract, own property, incur liabilities, hold bank accounts. The business itself — not its owner — signs, owns and owes.

An entrepreneur is a person who creates and develops a business idea and takes the risk of setting up an enterprise to produce something satisfying customer needs. The risk is personal: the idea fails, the entrepreneur carries it.

Entrepreneurship is the process of spotting market opportunities, arranging and investing resources to exploit them for long-term gain — shifting people from job seekers to job creators, critical in a high-unemployment country like Ethiopia. Its five elements: spotting a profitable opportunity, commercialising it (innovation), pursuing it long-term, pursuing it methodically, and accepting failure as a possibility.
