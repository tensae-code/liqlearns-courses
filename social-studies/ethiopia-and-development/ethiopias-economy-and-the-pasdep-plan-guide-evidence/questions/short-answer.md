# Ethiopia's economy and the PASDEP plan (guide evidence) — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** State the guide's sector order and agriculture's share.
**Answer:** Agriculture first — almost half of GDP — then services, then industry.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Distinguish GDP from GNP as the guide's keys do.
**Answer:** GDP is the total value produced within the country (GNP counts nationals' production wherever it occurs — the guide's key fixes the GDP side of the distinction).

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What was PASDEP, and when did it run?
**Answer:** The Plan for Accelerated and Sustained Development to End Poverty, Ethiopia's guiding development document of its period, 2005/06–2009/10.
