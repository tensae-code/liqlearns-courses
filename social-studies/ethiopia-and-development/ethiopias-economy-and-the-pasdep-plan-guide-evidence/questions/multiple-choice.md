# Ethiopia's economy and the PASDEP plan (guide evidence) — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In the guide, agriculture accounts for…
a) almost half of GDP  b) a tenth of GDP  c) all of GDP
**Answer: a) almost half of GDP**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The sector order by GDP is…
a) industry, services, agriculture  b) agriculture, services, industry  c) services, agriculture, industry
**Answer: b) agriculture, services, industry**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The lion's share of the labour force works in…
a) industry  b) services  c) agriculture
**Answer: c) agriculture**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** GDP, in the guide's answer keys, is…
a) value produced within the country  b) value produced by citizens abroad only  c) government spending
**Answer: a) value produced within the country**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** PASDEP stands for…
a) Plan for Accelerated and Sustained Development to End Poverty  b) Programme for African Development  c) Public Agricultural Scheme
**Answer: a) Plan for Accelerated and Sustained Development to End Poverty**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** PASDEP ran…
a) 2005/06–2009/10  b) 2015–2020  c) it still runs today
**Answer: a) 2005/06–2009/10**
