# Video script — Ethiopia's economy and the PASDEP plan (guide evidence)

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The 2010–11 guide reports an economy where agriculture made almost half of GDP and employed the lion's share of workers, guided in its era by PASDEP — the Plan for Accelerated and Sustained Development to End Poverty. (Teacher-guide evidence, PASDEP era.) First, the economy as the guide reports it (teacher guide — evidence): This lesson stands on the 2010–11 teacher's guide alone, era-labelled as PASDEP-era evidence. Then, pasdep — the era's guiding plan (teacher guide — evidence): PASDEP — the Plan for Accelerated and Sustained Development to End Poverty — is presented in the guide as the guiding document of Ethiopia's development for its period, with increasing GDP per capita among its tracked goals. Hold those two parts together and you hold the lesson.
