## Sub lesson 1 — The economy as the guide reports it (teacher guide — evidence)

This lesson stands on the 2010–11 teacher's guide alone, era-labelled as PASDEP-era evidence. The guide states: agriculture accounts for almost half of GDP, followed by services, then industry — and agriculture employs the lion's share of the labour force.

Ethiopia's GDP per capita is among the world's lowest but has been increasing, the guide states, with faster growth recorded across the three sectors from the 2005/06 base year. Its answer keys distinguish GDP from GNP: GDP is the total value produced within the country.

## Sub lesson 2 — PASDEP — the era's guiding plan (teacher guide — evidence)

PASDEP — the Plan for Accelerated and Sustained Development to End Poverty — is presented in the guide as the guiding document of Ethiopia's development for its period, with increasing GDP per capita among its tracked goals.

PASDEP ran 2005/06–2009/10 and has been superseded as policy. It stands here as historical and teacher evidence only: this is how the old Grade 12 course described Ethiopia's economy and its plan, in that plan's own years.
