# Hands-on — Five sources of start-up finance

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Staircase game: a founder climbs five steps, at each naming the source, what it gives and what it takes (risk shares, honesty, a partnership, equity, high-return growth demands).

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — a five-step funding staircase from own savings up to venture capital — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
