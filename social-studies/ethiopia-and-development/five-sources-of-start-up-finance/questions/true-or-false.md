# Five sources of start-up finance — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Personal savings come first, with at least half the start-up funds from your own pocket by the rule of thumb.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Personal savings come first, with at least half the start-up funds from your own pocket by the rule of thumb.
**Answer: False** — that statement belongs to sub lesson 1 (Your own pocket first); sub lesson 2 says: Friends and relatives come next, told honestly about opportunities and risks, then partners taken on to expand capital.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Angels are wealthy private investors backing start-ups for a share of the risk, and venture capital firms invest for high return and high growth potential.
**Answer: True**
