# Five sources of start-up finance — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Your own pocket first" in your own words.
**Answer:** Personal savings come first, with at least half the start-up funds from your own pocket by the rule of thumb.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Friends, relatives, partners" in your own words.
**Answer:** Friends and relatives come next, told honestly about opportunities and risks, then partners taken on to expand capital.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Angels and venture capital" in your own words.
**Answer:** Angels are wealthy private investors backing start-ups for a share of the risk, and venture capital firms invest for high return and high growth potential.
