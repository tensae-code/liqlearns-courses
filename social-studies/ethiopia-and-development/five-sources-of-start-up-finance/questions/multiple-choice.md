# Five sources of start-up finance — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Your own pocket first"?
a) personal savings first, at least half the start-up funds by the rule of thumb  b) friends and relatives told honestly; partners taken on to expand capital  c) angels — wealthy private investors; venture firms — for high return and high growth
**Answer: a) personal savings first, at least half the start-up funds by the rule of thumb**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Friends, relatives, partners"?
a) personal savings first, at least half the start-up funds by the rule of thumb  b) angels — wealthy private investors; venture firms — for high return and high growth  c) friends and relatives told honestly; partners taken on to expand capital
**Answer: c) friends and relatives told honestly; partners taken on to expand capital**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Angels and venture capital"?
a) friends and relatives told honestly; partners taken on to expand capital  b) angels — wealthy private investors; venture firms — for high return and high growth  c) personal savings first, at least half the start-up funds by the rule of thumb
**Answer: b) angels — wealthy private investors; venture firms — for high return and high growth**
