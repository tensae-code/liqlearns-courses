# Hands-on — Decentralization — the disadvantages

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Welcome sign
Draw a generous town's welcome sign as poor families stream in and a tax-paying firm packs to leave. Caption it with the mechanism in one sentence, using the word redistribution.

## National view
Take the under-taxed capital case. Write the town mayor's defence in two lines, then the finance minister's reply in two lines, each correct from its own level's view.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
