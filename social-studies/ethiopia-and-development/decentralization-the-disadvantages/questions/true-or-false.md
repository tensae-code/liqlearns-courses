# Decentralization — the disadvantages — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Local decisions spill over borders, and small local provision loses scale economies.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Capital is elastic locally, so localities under-tax it to attract investment.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Local redistribution attracts the poor and repels the rich who fund it, until it collapses.
**Answer: True**
