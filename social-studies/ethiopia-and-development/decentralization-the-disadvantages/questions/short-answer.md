# Decentralization — the disadvantages — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Spillovers and lost scale" in your own words.
**Answer:** Local decisions spill over borders, and small local provision loses scale economies.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Mispriced tax bases" in your own words.
**Answer:** Capital is elastic locally, so localities under-tax it to attract investment.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Redistribution that self-destructs" in your own words.
**Answer:** Local redistribution attracts the poor and repels the rich who fund it, until it collapses.
