# Decentralization — the disadvantages — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Spillovers and lost scale"?
a) Local decisions spill over borders, and small local provision loses scale economies  b) Capital is elastic locally, so localities under-tax it to attract investment  c) Local redistribution attracts the poor and repels the rich who fund it, until it collapses
**Answer: a) Local decisions spill over borders, and small local provision loses scale economies**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Mispriced tax bases"?
a) Capital is elastic locally, so localities under-tax it to attract investment  b) Local redistribution attracts the poor and repels the rich who fund it, until it collapses  c) Local decisions spill over borders, and small local provision loses scale economies
**Answer: a) Capital is elastic locally, so localities under-tax it to attract investment**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Redistribution that self-destructs"?
a) Local redistribution attracts the poor and repels the rich who fund it, until it collapses  b) Local decisions spill over borders, and small local provision loses scale economies  c) Capital is elastic locally, so localities under-tax it to attract investment
**Answer: a) Local redistribution attracts the poor and repels the rich who fund it, until it collapses**
