# Decentralization — the disadvantages — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or figure from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Costs spilling across a border between jurisdictions are ____.
**Answer: externalities**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Locally, capital is ____ — it can move away.
**Answer: elastic**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Most economists agree ____ belongs to the centre.
**Answer: stabilisation**
