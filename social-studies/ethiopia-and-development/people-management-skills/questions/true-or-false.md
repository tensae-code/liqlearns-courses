# People management skills — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Communication skills mean expressing ideas orally and in writing, and leadership skills mean inspiring people to work as needed.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Communication skills mean expressing ideas orally and in writing, and leadership skills mean inspiring people to work as needed.
**Answer: False** — that statement belongs to sub lesson 1 (Communication and leadership); sub lesson 2 says: Motivational skills mean understanding what drives people, and delegation means allocating tasks by each person's skills.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Performance is the combination of business knowledge, both skill families and personal motivation.
**Answer: True**
