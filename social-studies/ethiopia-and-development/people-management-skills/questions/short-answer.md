# People management skills — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Communication and leadership" in your own words.
**Answer:** Communication skills mean expressing ideas orally and in writing, and leadership skills mean inspiring people to work as needed.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Motivation and delegation" in your own words.
**Answer:** Motivational skills mean understanding what drives people, and delegation means allocating tasks by each person's skills.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Negotiation — and the performance formula" in your own words.
**Answer:** Performance is the combination of business knowledge, both skill families and personal motivation.
