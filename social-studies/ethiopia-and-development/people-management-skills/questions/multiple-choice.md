# People management skills — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Communication and leadership"?
a) expressing ideas orally and in writing; inspiring people to work as needed  b) knowing what drives people; allocating tasks by each person's skills  c) reading a situation to maximise outcomes for all parties; performance = knowledge + both skill families + motivation
