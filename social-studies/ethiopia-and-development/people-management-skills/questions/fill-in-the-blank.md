# People management skills — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** _____ skills mean expressing ideas orally and in writing.
**Answer: communication**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** _____ skills mean allocating tasks with an understanding of each person's skills.
**Answer: delegation**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Performance combines business knowledge, both skill families and personal _____.
**Answer: motivation**
