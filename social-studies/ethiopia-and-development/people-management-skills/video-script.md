# Video script — People management skills

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

People skills serve committed insiders and supporting outsiders — customers, suppliers, investors. Communication skills: expressing ideas orally and in writing. Leadership skills: inspiring people to work as needed.

Motivational skills: understanding what drives people and what they expect from their jobs. Delegation skills: allocating tasks with an understanding of each person's skills — the right task to the right hands, by knowledge, not by habit.

Negotiation skills: reading a situation and maximising outcomes for all parties. Performance itself is the combination of business knowledge, both skill families — general and people management — and personal motivation; entrepreneurs should keep auditing their own strengths and weaknesses against it.
