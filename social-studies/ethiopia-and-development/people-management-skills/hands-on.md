# Hands-on — People management skills

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Conversation stations: five short role-plays (team, customer, supplier, investor, partner), each testing one named skill; the class calls the skill before the scene ends.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — one founder in the middle of five labelled conversations — team, customer, supplier, investor, partner — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
