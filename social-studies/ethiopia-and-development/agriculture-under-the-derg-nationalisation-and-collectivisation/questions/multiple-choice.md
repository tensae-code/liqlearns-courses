# Agriculture under the Derg — nationalisation and collectivisation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Land and farms under state control"?
a) Under the Derg, rural land was nationalised, tenancy abolished and private farm investment prohibited.  b) The Derg imposed involuntary collectivisation, forced villagisation, state market control and grain quotas at low fixed prices.  c) The book judges the Derg's interventions the primary reason agriculture failed to develop.
**Answer: a) Under the Derg, rural land was nationalised, tenancy abolished and private farm investment prohibited.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Collectives, quotas and controls"?

**Answer: c) The Derg imposed involuntary collectivisation, forced villagisation, state market control and grain quotas at low fixed prices.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "The verdict — and the projects' path"?
