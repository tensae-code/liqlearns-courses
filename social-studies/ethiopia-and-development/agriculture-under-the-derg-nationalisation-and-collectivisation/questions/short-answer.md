# Agriculture under the Derg — nationalisation and collectivisation — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Land and farms under state control" in your own words.
**Answer:** Under the Derg, rural land was nationalised, tenancy abolished and private farm investment prohibited.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Collectives, quotas and controls" in your own words.
**Answer:** The Derg imposed involuntary collectivisation, forced villagisation, state market control and grain quotas at low fixed prices.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "The verdict — and the projects' path" in your own words.
**Answer:** The book judges the Derg's interventions the primary reason agriculture failed to develop.
