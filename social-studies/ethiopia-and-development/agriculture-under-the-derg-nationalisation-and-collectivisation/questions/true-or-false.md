# Agriculture under the Derg — nationalisation and collectivisation — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Derg encouraged private investment in agriculture.
**Answer: False — Private investment in agriculture was PROHIBITED — the state took the sector under central planning.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Peasants joined collectives voluntarily at market prices.
**Answer: False — Collectivisation was involuntary, villagisation forced, and grain quotas were delivered at LOW FIXED prices.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The book praises the Derg's farm interventions as the sector's success.
**Answer: False — Its verdict is the reverse: the interventions were the PRIMARY REASON the sector failed to develop.**
