# Growth versus development — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Growth — more output, in percent" in your own words.
**Answer:** Growth is the percentage increase in real GDP; the worked example gives 2.27%.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Development — wider than output" in your own words.
**Answer:** Development adds changed institutions, rising living standards, dignity, freedom, less inequality and eradicated poverty to more output.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Richer without developing" in your own words.
**Answer:** A country can grow richer without developing.
