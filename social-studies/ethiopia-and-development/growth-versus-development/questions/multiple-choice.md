# Growth versus development — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Growth — more output, in percent"?
a) Growth is the percentage increase in real GDP; the worked example gives 2.27%.  b) Development adds changed institutions, rising living standards, dignity, freedom, less inequality and eradicated poverty to more output.  c) A country can grow richer without developing.
**Answer: a) Growth is the percentage increase in real GDP; the worked example gives 2.27%.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Development — wider than output"?
a) A country can grow richer without developing.  b) Growth is the percentage increase in real GDP; the worked example gives 2.27%.  c) Development adds changed institutions, rising living standards, dignity, freedom, less inequality and eradicated poverty to more output.
**Answer: c) Development adds changed institutions, rising living standards, dignity, freedom, less inequality and eradicated poverty to more output.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Richer without developing"?
a) Development adds changed institutions, rising living standards, dignity, freedom, less inequality and eradicated poverty to more output.  b) A country can grow richer without developing.  c) Growth is the percentage increase in real GDP; the worked example gives 2.27%.
**Answer: b) A country can grow richer without developing.**
