# Growth versus development — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The worked example's growth rate is 22.7%.
**Answer: False — 384,656 ÷ 16,920,328 ≈ 0.0227 — that is 2.27%, not 22.7%.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Development means exactly the same as growth.
**Answer: False — Growth is one number; development is the wider change — institutions, standards, freedom and equality.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** If GDP grows, development has automatically happened.
**Answer: False — Output can rise while institutions, equality and poverty do not improve — growth without development.**
