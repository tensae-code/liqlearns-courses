# Video script — Growth versus development

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Economic growth is the quantitative percentage increase in GDP between periods, conventionally computed on real (inflation-adjusted) GDP and often per capita, to remove population change. The worked example computes growth of 2.27%: real GDP rising from 16,920,328 to 17,304,984 dollars — a rise of 384,656, which is 2.27% of the starting figure.

Development is wider: more output plus changed technical and institutional arrangements — rising living standards, self-esteem, dignity, freedom of choice and control over one's life, with reduced inequality and eradicated poverty.

A country can grow richer without developing: output can rise while institutions stagnate, inequality widens and poverty persists. That gap between the two words is why this unit needs both.
