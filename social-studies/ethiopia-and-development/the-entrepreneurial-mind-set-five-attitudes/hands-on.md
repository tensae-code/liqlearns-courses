# Hands-on — The entrepreneurial mind-set — five attitudes

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Badge game: attitude cards are pinned on learners as scenarios are read ('route blocked', 'supplier cheated', '14-hour day') — each pinned learner must respond in character.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — five badges — heart, shield, bending road, clock, handshake — one per attitude — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
