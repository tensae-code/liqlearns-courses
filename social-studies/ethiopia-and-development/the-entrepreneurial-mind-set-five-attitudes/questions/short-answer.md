# The entrepreneurial mind-set — five attitudes — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Mind-set, passion, bravery" in your own words.
**Answer:** Passion is the drive to succeed, and bravery uses fear of failure as fuel to work harder, not as a brake.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Flexibility and work ethic" in your own words.
**Answer:** Flexibility means changing the route when obstacles block it while keeping the goal.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Integrity" in your own words.
**Answer:** Integrity is being demonstrably trustworthy and honest toward suppliers, customers, colleagues and investors.
