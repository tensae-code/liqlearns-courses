# The entrepreneurial mind-set — five attitudes — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Passion is the drive to succeed, and bravery uses fear of failure as fuel to work harder, not as a brake.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Passion is the drive to succeed, and bravery uses fear of failure as fuel to work harder, not as a brake.
**Answer: False** — that statement belongs to sub lesson 1 (Mind-set, passion, bravery); sub lesson 2 says: Flexibility means changing the route when obstacles block it while keeping the goal.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Integrity is being demonstrably trustworthy and honest toward suppliers, customers, colleagues and investors.
**Answer: True**
