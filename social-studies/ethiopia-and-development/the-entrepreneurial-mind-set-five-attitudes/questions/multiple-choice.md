# The entrepreneurial mind-set — five attitudes — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Mind-set, passion, bravery"?
a) passion as the drive; bravery as fear used as fuel, not a brake  b) changing the route while keeping the goal; the long hours of building from the ground  c) demonstrable trustworthiness and honesty to suppliers, customers, colleagues and investors
**Answer: a) passion as the drive; bravery as fear used as fuel, not a brake**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Flexibility and work ethic"?
a) passion as the drive; bravery as fear used as fuel, not a brake  b) demonstrable trustworthiness and honesty to suppliers, customers, colleagues and investors  c) changing the route while keeping the goal; the long hours of building from the ground
**Answer: c) changing the route while keeping the goal; the long hours of building from the ground**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Integrity"?
a) changing the route while keeping the goal; the long hours of building from the ground  b) demonstrable trustworthiness and honesty to suppliers, customers, colleagues and investors  c) passion as the drive; bravery as fear used as fuel, not a brake
**Answer: b) demonstrable trustworthiness and honesty to suppliers, customers, colleagues and investors**
