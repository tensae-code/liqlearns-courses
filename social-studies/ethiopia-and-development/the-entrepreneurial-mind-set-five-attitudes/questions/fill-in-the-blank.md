# The entrepreneurial mind-set — five attitudes — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** _____ uses fear of failure as fuel to work harder and correct mistakes.
**Answer: bravery**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Flexibility changes the _____ when obstacles block it, while keeping the goal.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** _____ is being demonstrably trustworthy and honest toward suppliers, customers, colleagues and investors.
