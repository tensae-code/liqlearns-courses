# Video script — The entrepreneurial mind-set — five attitudes

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A mind-set is the state of mind that shapes how goals are pursued; successful entrepreneurs expect to fail at least once and learn from it. Passion — for ideas, goals and the company — is the drive to succeed. Bravery is fear of failure used as fuel to work harder and correct mistakes, not as a brake.

Flexibility is changing the route when obstacles block it while keeping the goal. Strong work ethic is the long hours of building from the ground up — the unglamorous arithmetic of starting.

Integrity is being demonstrably trustworthy and honest toward suppliers, customers, colleagues and investors. It is the attitude the other four need: passion without integrity burns the very people a business must keep.
