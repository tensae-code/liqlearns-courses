# Video script — Productivity — more from what you have

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Productivity is the ratio of output volume to input volume — labour and capital used efficiently. It is most commonly measured as GDP per hour worked: how much output each hour of work produces.

Productivity can be raised three ways: more output from the same input, the same output from less input, or a large output rise from a small input rise. Each way grows the ratio — the numerator, the denominator, or both at once.

Productivity is presented as a key source of growth and competitiveness: an economy that gets more from what it has can raise output without endlessly adding land, labour and machines.
