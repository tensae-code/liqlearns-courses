# Productivity — more from what you have — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Output per unit of input" in your own words.
**Answer:** Productivity is output per unit of input, commonly GDP per hour worked.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Three ways to raise it" in your own words.
**Answer:** Productivity rises via more output from the same input, the same output from less input, or a large output rise from a small input rise.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Why productivity matters" in your own words.
**Answer:** Productivity is a key source of growth and competitiveness.
