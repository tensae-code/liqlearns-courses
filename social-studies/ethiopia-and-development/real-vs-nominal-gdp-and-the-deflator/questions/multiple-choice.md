# Real vs nominal GDP and the deflator — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Nominal counts prices, real counts quantities"?
a) nominal at current prices; real at base-year prices; equal in the base year  b) deflator = nominal ÷ real × 100; inflation from its percentage change  c) nominal 27,500 / 62,000 / 100,000; real 27,500 / 47,500 / 67,500; deflators 100 / 131 / 148
**Answer: a) nominal at current prices; real at base-year prices; equal in the base year**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "The deflator and inflation"?
a) nominal at current prices; real at base-year prices; equal in the base year  b) nominal 27,500 / 62,000 / 100,000; real 27,500 / 47,500 / 67,500; deflators 100 / 131 / 148  c) deflator = nominal ÷ real × 100; inflation from its percentage change
**Answer: c) deflator = nominal ÷ real × 100; inflation from its percentage change**
