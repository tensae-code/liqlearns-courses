# Real vs nominal GDP and the deflator — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Nominal counts prices, real counts quantities" in your own words.
**Answer:** Real GDP values output at base-year prices, so only quantity changes move it.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "The deflator and inflation" in your own words.
**Answer:** The GDP deflator equals nominal GDP divided by real GDP, times 100.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "The kitfo-and-chumbo worked example" in your own words.
**Answer:** Table 7.2's deflators run 100, 131 and 148 across 2010–2012.
