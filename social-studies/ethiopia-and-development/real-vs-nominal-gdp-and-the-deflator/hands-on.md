# Hands-on — Real vs nominal GDP and the deflator

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Two price-tag sets for one basket — base-year tags and new-year tags. Learners total the basket both ways with quantities held fixed, and read the deflator off the two totals as a class.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — the same basket priced in two different years, and the two-dish table of kitfo and chumbo — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
