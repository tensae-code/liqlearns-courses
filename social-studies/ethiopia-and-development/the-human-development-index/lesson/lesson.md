# The Human Development Index

## Do it first

## Sub lesson 1 — Why a wider measure was needed

Because GDP misses development's multidimensional nature, the UNDP's Human Development Index composites three dimensions into one score. It was created by Mahbub ul Haq and Amartya Sen and has been published in the Human Development Report since 1989.

[Picture 24934 — three pillars — a heartbeat, a schoolbook, a coin — holding up one HDI score]

## Sub lesson 2 — The three dimensions

The HDI composites three indices: life expectancy at birth; educational attainment (adult literacy rate plus combined gross enrolment ratio); and standard of living (real GDP per capita). Health, knowledge and means — one index each.

## Sub lesson 3 — The sibling measures

The UNDP also publishes sibling measures that adjust or extend the HDI: the inequality-adjusted HDI, the Gender Inequality Index, the Multidimensional Poverty Index and the Gender Empowerment Measure.
