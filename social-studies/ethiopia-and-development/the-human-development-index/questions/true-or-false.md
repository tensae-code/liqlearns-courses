# The Human Development Index — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The HDI was created by the World Bank in 2010.
**Answer: False — Its creators are Mahbub ul Haq and Amartya Sen, for the UNDP, published in the Human Development Report since 1989.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Military spending is one of the HDI's three dimensions.
**Answer: False — The three are life expectancy, education (literacy plus enrolment) and standard of living (real GDP per capita).**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The HDI is the UNDP's only development measure.
**Answer: False — Four siblings are named — inequality-adjusted HDI, GII, MPI and GEM.**
