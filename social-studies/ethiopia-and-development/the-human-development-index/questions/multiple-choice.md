# The Human Development Index — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Why a wider measure was needed"?
a) The HDI was created by Mahbub ul Haq and Amartya Sen and published since 1989.  b) The HDI's three dimensions are life expectancy, educational attainment and standard of living.  c) The UNDP's siblings include the inequality-adjusted HDI, Gender Inequality Index, Multidimensional Poverty Index and Gender Empowerment Measure.
**Answer: a) The HDI was created by Mahbub ul Haq and Amartya Sen and published since 1989.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "The three dimensions"?
a) The UNDP's siblings include the inequality-adjusted HDI, Gender Inequality Index, Multidimensional Poverty Index and Gender Empowerment Measure.  b) The HDI was created by Mahbub ul Haq and Amartya Sen and published since 1989.  c) The HDI's three dimensions are life expectancy, educational attainment and standard of living.
**Answer: c) The HDI's three dimensions are life expectancy, educational attainment and standard of living.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "The sibling measures"?
a) The HDI's three dimensions are life expectancy, educational attainment and standard of living.  b) The UNDP's siblings include the inequality-adjusted HDI, Gender Inequality Index, Multidimensional Poverty Index and Gender Empowerment Measure.  c) The HDI was created by Mahbub ul Haq and Amartya Sen and published since 1989.
**Answer: b) The UNDP's siblings include the inequality-adjusted HDI, Gender Inequality Index, Multidimensional Poverty Index and Gender Empowerment Measure.**
