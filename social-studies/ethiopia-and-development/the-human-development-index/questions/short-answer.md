# The Human Development Index — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Why a wider measure was needed" in your own words.
**Answer:** The HDI was created by Mahbub ul Haq and Amartya Sen and published since 1989.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "The three dimensions" in your own words.
**Answer:** The HDI's three dimensions are life expectancy, educational attainment and standard of living.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "The sibling measures" in your own words.
**Answer:** The UNDP's siblings include the inequality-adjusted HDI, Gender Inequality Index, Multidimensional Poverty Index and Gender Empowerment Measure.
