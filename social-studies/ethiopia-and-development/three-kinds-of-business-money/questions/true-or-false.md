# Three kinds of business money — permanent, working, asset finance — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Permanent capital funds start-up costs and major developments and is repaid only when the firm can afford it.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Permanent capital funds start-up costs and major developments and is repaid only when the firm can afford it.
**Answer: False** — that statement belongs to sub lesson 1 (Permanent capital); sub lesson 2 says: Working capital is short-term finance for raw materials and running costs.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Asset finance is loans of typically 3 to 10 years or more, matched to the asset's useful life, for plant, machinery, vehicles and buildings.
**Answer: True**
