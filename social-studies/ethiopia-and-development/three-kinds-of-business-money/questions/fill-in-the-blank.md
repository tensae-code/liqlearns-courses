# Three kinds of business money — permanent, working, asset finance — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** _____ capital funds start-up costs and major developments.
**Answer: permanent**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** _____ capital is short-term finance for raw materials and running costs.
**Answer: working**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Asset finance is matched to the asset's _____.
**Answer: useful life**
