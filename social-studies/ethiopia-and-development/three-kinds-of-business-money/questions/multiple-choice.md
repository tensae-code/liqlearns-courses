# Three kinds of business money — permanent, working, asset finance — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Permanent capital"?
a) share investment or partners' loans for start-up and major developments, repaid when the firm can afford it  b) short-term finance for raw materials and running costs  c) loans of 3–10 years or more matched to the asset's useful life
**Answer: a) share investment or partners' loans for start-up and major developments, repaid when the firm can afford it**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Working capital"?
a) share investment or partners' loans for start-up and major developments, repaid when the firm can afford it  b) loans of 3–10 years or more matched to the asset's useful life  c) short-term finance for raw materials and running costs
**Answer: c) short-term finance for raw materials and running costs**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Asset finance — and the matching test"?
a) short-term finance for raw materials and running costs  b) loans of 3–10 years or more matched to the asset's useful life  c) share investment or partners' loans for start-up and major developments, repaid when the firm can afford it
**Answer: b) loans of 3–10 years or more matched to the asset's useful life**
