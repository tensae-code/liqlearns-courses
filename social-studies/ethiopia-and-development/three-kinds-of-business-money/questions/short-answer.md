# Three kinds of business money — permanent, working, asset finance — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Permanent capital" in your own words.
**Answer:** Permanent capital funds start-up costs and major developments and is repaid only when the firm can afford it.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Working capital" in your own words.
**Answer:** Working capital is short-term finance for raw materials and running costs.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Asset finance — and the matching test" in your own words.
**Answer:** Asset finance is loans of typically 3 to 10 years or more, matched to the asset's useful life, for plant, machinery, vehicles and buildings.
