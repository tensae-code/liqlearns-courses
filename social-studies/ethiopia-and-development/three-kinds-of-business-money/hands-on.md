# Hands-on — Three kinds of business money — permanent, working, asset finance

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Three pots on the table — building, stock, machine. Expense cards are dealt; learners drop each into its pot and must name the finance kind and its time-shape before the drop counts.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — three pots of different sizes labelled with what each pays for — the building, the stock, the machine — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
