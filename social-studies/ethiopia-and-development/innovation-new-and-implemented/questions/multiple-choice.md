# Innovation — new and implemented — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "The definition — creativity plus implementation"?
a) a new or improved product or process, made available or brought into use  b) organisational, process, product and marketing innovation with the book's examples  c) innovation cutting environmental impact across the whole life cycle, toward the 2030 goals
**Answer: a) a new or improved product or process, made available or brought into use**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "The four Oslo types"?
a) a new or improved product or process, made available or brought into use  b) innovation cutting environmental impact across the whole life cycle, toward the 2030 goals  c) organisational, process, product and marketing innovation with the book's examples
**Answer: c) organisational, process, product and marketing innovation with the book's examples**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Eco-innovation — the fifth type"?
a) organisational, process, product and marketing innovation with the book's examples  b) innovation cutting environmental impact across the whole life cycle, toward the 2030 goals  c) a new or improved product or process, made available or brought into use
**Answer: b) innovation cutting environmental impact across the whole life cycle, toward the 2030 goals**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Which statement best matches "Ethiopian innovation examples — BPR, KAIZEN, the drone (teacher guide)"?
a) BPR and KAIZEN as process innovations; the drone as a product innovation  b) a different part of this lesson, as stated in the earlier sub lessons  c) the opposite claim to the one this sub lesson teaches
**Answer: a) BPR and KAIZEN as process innovations; the drone as a product innovation**
