# Innovation — new and implemented — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Innovation means a new or significantly improved product or process actually made available or brought into use.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The four Oslo types are organisational, process, product and marketing innovation.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Eco-innovation drives progress toward the UN's 2030 sustainable-development goals.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** The guide classifies BPR and KAIZEN as process innovations and the drone as a product innovation.
**Answer: True**
