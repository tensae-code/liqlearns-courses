# Innovation — new and implemented — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Innovation is creativity plus _____ — an unbuilt idea is not yet innovation.
**Answer: implementation**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** New design, packaging, pricing or promotion is _____ innovation — the fourth Oslo type.
**Answer: marketing**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Innovation aimed at the 2030 sustainable-development goals is called _____.
**Answer: eco-innovation**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** BPR and KAIZEN — Ethiopia's own improvement programmes — are classified as _____.
**Answer: process innovations**
