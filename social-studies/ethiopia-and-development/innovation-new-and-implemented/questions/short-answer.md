# Innovation — new and implemented — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "The definition — creativity plus implementation" in your own words.
**Answer:** Innovation means a new or significantly improved product or process actually made available or brought into use.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "The four Oslo types" in your own words.
**Answer:** The four Oslo types are organisational, process, product and marketing innovation.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Eco-innovation — the fifth type" in your own words.
**Answer:** Eco-innovation drives progress toward the UN's 2030 sustainable-development goals.

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Explain "Ethiopian innovation examples — BPR, KAIZEN, the drone (teacher guide)" in your own words.
**Answer:** The guide classifies BPR and KAIZEN as process innovations and the drone as a product innovation.
