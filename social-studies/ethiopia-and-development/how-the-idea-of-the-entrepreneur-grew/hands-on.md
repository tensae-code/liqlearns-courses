# Hands-on — How the idea of the entrepreneur grew

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Five learners stand as the five centuries; each states their definition in character, and the class orders them by applause when the sequence stands correct.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — a five-step timeline from project manager to innovator — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
