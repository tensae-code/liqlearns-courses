# How the idea of the entrepreneur grew — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Manager and contractor"?
a) a manager of large projects; then a government contractor at a fixed price  b) buying at known prices to sell at unknown prices; risk separated from capital  c) an organiser for personal gain; then an innovator and inventor
**Answer: a) a manager of large projects; then a government contractor at a fixed price**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Cantillon's risk-taker"?
a) a manager of large projects; then a government contractor at a fixed price  b) an organiser for personal gain; then an innovator and inventor  c) buying at known prices to sell at unknown prices; risk separated from capital
**Answer: c) buying at known prices to sell at unknown prices; risk separated from capital**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Organiser, then innovator"?
a) buying at known prices to sell at unknown prices; risk separated from capital  b) an organiser for personal gain; then an innovator and inventor  c) a manager of large projects; then a government contractor at a fixed price
**Answer: b) an organiser for personal gain; then an innovator and inventor**
