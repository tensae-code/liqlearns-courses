# How the idea of the entrepreneur grew — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In the ancient period the entrepreneur was a manager of large projects, and in the 17th century a contractor supplying government at a fixed price.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In the ancient period the entrepreneur was a manager of large projects, and in the 17th century a contractor supplying government at a fixed price.
**Answer: False** — that statement belongs to sub lesson 1 (Manager and contractor); sub lesson 2 says: Cantillon's entrepreneur is a risk-taker who buys at known prices to sell at unknown prices.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** By the mid-20th century the entrepreneur is understood as an innovator and inventor.
**Answer: True**
