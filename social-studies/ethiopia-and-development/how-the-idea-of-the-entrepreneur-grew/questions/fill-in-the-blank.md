# How the idea of the entrepreneur grew — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In the 17th century the entrepreneur was a _____ supplying the government at a fixed price.
**Answer: contractor**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Cantillon's risk-taker buys at known prices to sell at _____ prices.
**Answer: unknown**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** By the mid-20th century the entrepreneur is an _____ and inventor.
**Answer: innovator**
