# How the idea of the entrepreneur grew — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Manager and contractor" in your own words.
**Answer:** In the ancient period the entrepreneur was a manager of large projects, and in the 17th century a contractor supplying government at a fixed price.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Cantillon's risk-taker" in your own words.
**Answer:** Cantillon's entrepreneur is a risk-taker who buys at known prices to sell at unknown prices.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Organiser, then innovator" in your own words.
**Answer:** By the mid-20th century the entrepreneur is understood as an innovator and inventor.
