# Video script — Health policy in Ethiopia — the 1993 policy and HSDP

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Definite health policy began only in 1963. After 1974 a centralised primary-health-care approach was declared in the 1978 ten-year plan — care organised from the centre, aimed first at the basics every community needs.

The post-1991 settlement is the 1993 National Health Policy and the 20-year Health Sector Development Plan from 1997, now in its third phase. Its goals: decentralisation to districts on block funding, preventive alongside curative care, access for all population segments, and private-sector and NGO participation — aimed at communicable diseases, malnutrition and maternal and child health.

The problems: limited and unevenly distributed facilities, inadequate budgets and management, low facility quality, high maternal and child mortality, and shortages of trained personnel, drugs and maintained equipment. Remedies include expanding programmes, family-planning services at every level, and expanded training of health personnel.
