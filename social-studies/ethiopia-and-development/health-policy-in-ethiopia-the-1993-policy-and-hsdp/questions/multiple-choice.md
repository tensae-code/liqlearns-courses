# Health policy in Ethiopia — the 1993 policy and HSDP — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "The road to a health policy"?
a) Definite health policy began in 1963; the 1978 ten-year plan declared a centralised primary-health-care approach.  b) The 1993 policy and HSDP pursue decentralisation on block funding, preventive alongside curative care, access for all, and private/NGO participation.  c) Health problems include uneven facilities, weak budgets, high maternal and child mortality and staff/drug shortages; remedies include family planning at every level and more training.
