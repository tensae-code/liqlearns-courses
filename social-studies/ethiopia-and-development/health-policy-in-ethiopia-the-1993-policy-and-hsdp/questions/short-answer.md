# Health policy in Ethiopia — the 1993 policy and HSDP — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "The road to a health policy" in your own words.
**Answer:** Definite health policy began in 1963; the 1978 ten-year plan declared a centralised primary-health-care approach.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "The 1993 policy and the HSDP" in your own words.
**Answer:** The 1993 policy and HSDP pursue decentralisation on block funding, preventive alongside curative care, access for all, and private/NGO participation.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Problems and remedies" in your own words.
**Answer:** Health problems include uneven facilities, weak budgets, high maternal and child mortality and staff/drug shortages; remedies include family planning at every level and more training.
