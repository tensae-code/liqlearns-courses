# Health policy in Ethiopia — the 1993 policy and HSDP — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Ethiopia had a definite health policy from 1900.
**Answer: False — Definite health policy began only in 1963 — and the primary-care declaration came in the 1978 plan.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The 1993 policy centralised all health funding at the ministry.
**Answer: False — Its first goal is the opposite: DECENTRALISATION to districts, funded by block grants.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Maternal and child mortality are low and need no remedy.
**Answer: False — HIGH maternal and child mortality is one of the named problems the policy aims at.**
