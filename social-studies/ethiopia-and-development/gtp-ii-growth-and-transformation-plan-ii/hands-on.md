# Hands-on — GTP II — Growth and Transformation Plan II

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Nine pillars again

## Gap watch
The plan names the saving–investment gap and the trade deficit as targets. From the earlier lessons, pull the figures each gap will be judged by in the performance scoreboard.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
