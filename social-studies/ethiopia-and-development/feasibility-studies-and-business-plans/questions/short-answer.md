# Feasibility studies and business plans — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Feasibility before plan — always" in your own words.
**Answer:** A feasibility study normally costs under 20% of what a business plan costs.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Contents and the five steps" in your own words.
**Answer:** The feasibility study must answer whether the idea will work and whether it is profitable.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "The function — a reality check" in your own words.
**Answer:** The feasibility plan is the first reality check, testing the idea before money is committed.
