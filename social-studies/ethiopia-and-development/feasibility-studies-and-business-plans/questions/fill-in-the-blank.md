# Feasibility studies and business plans — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A feasibility study normally costs under _____ of a business plan's cost.
**Answer: 20%**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The study's first of five questions is: _____ — before profit, cost or worth.
**Answer: will it work**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The feasibility plan is the entrepreneur's and investors' first _____.
**Answer: reality check**
