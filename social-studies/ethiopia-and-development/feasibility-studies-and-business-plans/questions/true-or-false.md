# Feasibility studies and business plans — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A feasibility study normally costs under 20% of what a business plan costs.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The feasibility study must answer whether the idea will work and whether it is profitable.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The feasibility plan is the first reality check, testing the idea before money is committed.
**Answer: True**
