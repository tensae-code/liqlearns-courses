# Hands-on — Feasibility studies and business plans

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
A five-tab study booklet is assembled by five teams, one tab each — idea, people, objectives, market, site. The booklet is then cross-examined by an investor team asking only the study's five questions.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — a small gate before a long road — feasibility before the plan — with the five questions as a checklist and a five-tab study booklet — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
