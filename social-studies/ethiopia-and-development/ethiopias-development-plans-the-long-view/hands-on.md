# Hands-on — Ethiopia's development plans — the long view

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Build the timeline
On one long strip of paper, mark 1957, 1974, 1991, 2002 and 2005/06. Write each plan's name and its one big idea under its years. Check every date against the lesson.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the three-regime timeline from memory and label every part. Then open the lesson, compare, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
