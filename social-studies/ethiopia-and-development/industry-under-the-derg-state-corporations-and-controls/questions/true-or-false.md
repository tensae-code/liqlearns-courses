# Industry under the Derg — state corporations and controls — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Private investors could run unlimited ventures under the Derg.
**Answer: False — Private investment was capped at half a million birr AND limited to one venture per entrepreneur.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Manufacturing grew steadily throughout the Derg period.
**Answer: False — It declined sharply at first; the partial recovery came via the 1977/78 zemecha campaign.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The mixed-economy turn happened in 1975.
**Answer: False — The turn came in March 1990 — fifteen years in, and too late before the May 1991 regime change.**
