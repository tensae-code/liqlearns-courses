# Industry under the Derg — state corporations and controls — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Nationalisation and the cap"?
a) The Derg nationalised most medium/large firms into state corporations and capped private investment at 500,000 birr, one venture per entrepreneur.  b) Manufacturing declined sharply, then partly recovered through the 1977/78 zemecha production campaign.  c) The mid-1980s brought the first real industrial policy; the March 1990 mixed-economy turn came too late before May 1991.
**Answer: a) The Derg nationalised most medium/large firms into state corporations and capped private investment at 500,000 birr, one venture per entrepreneur.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Controls — and the decline"?
a) The mid-1980s brought the first real industrial policy; the March 1990 mixed-economy turn came too late before May 1991.  b) The Derg nationalised most medium/large firms into state corporations and capped private investment at 500,000 birr, one venture per entrepreneur.  c) Manufacturing declined sharply, then partly recovered through the 1977/78 zemecha production campaign.
**Answer: c) Manufacturing declined sharply, then partly recovered through the 1977/78 zemecha production campaign.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "A late policy, a late turn"?
a) Manufacturing declined sharply, then partly recovered through the 1977/78 zemecha production campaign.  b) The mid-1980s brought the first real industrial policy; the March 1990 mixed-economy turn came too late before May 1991.  c) The Derg nationalised most medium/large firms into state corporations and capped private investment at 500,000 birr, one venture per entrepreneur.
**Answer: b) The mid-1980s brought the first real industrial policy; the March 1990 mixed-economy turn came too late before May 1991.**
