# Industry under the Derg — state corporations and controls — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Nationalisation and the cap" in your own words.
**Answer:** The Derg nationalised most medium/large firms into state corporations and capped private investment at 500,000 birr, one venture per entrepreneur.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Controls — and the decline" in your own words.
**Answer:** Manufacturing declined sharply, then partly recovered through the 1977/78 zemecha production campaign.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "A late policy, a late turn" in your own words.
**Answer:** The mid-1980s brought the first real industrial policy; the March 1990 mixed-economy turn came too late before May 1991.
