# Video script — Industry under the Derg — state corporations and controls

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The military government nationalised most medium and large-scale enterprises into state corporations under a declared socialist economic policy. Private investment was capped at half a million birr, with one venture per entrepreneur.

Wide price controls, a regulated labour market and quantitative import restrictions with higher tariffs governed what industry remained. Manufacturing declined sharply at first, then partly recovered through the 1977/78 'zemecha' production campaign.

Industry had no real industrial policy until the mid-1980s focus on import-substituting, labour-intensive industry through public investment. The March 1990 turn to a mixed economy came too late to bear fruit before the May 1991 regime change.
