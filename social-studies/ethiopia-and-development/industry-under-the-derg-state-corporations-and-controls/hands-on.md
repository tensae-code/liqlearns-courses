# Hands-on — Industry under the Derg — state corporations and controls

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Use stones, cups or coins as the lesson's quantities — birr, units, or index points, as the lesson uses them. Act out the lesson's central worked example or comparison with a partner, step by step, saying each step aloud as you place the objects.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — a nationalised factory under a state-corporation sign, a price-control board, and a birr-500,000 cap stamped on a private investor's plan — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
