# Uni-modal strategy — betting on the smallholder — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Uni-modal strategy moves peasants off their land into large estates.
**Answer: False — Keeping peasants ON their land with family labour is the strategy's core — eviction is what it protects against.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Uni-modal strategy increases out-migration from villages.
**Answer: False — It REDUCES out-migration — peasants with viable small farms have reason to stay.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Tiny holdings can use every large-scale input easily.
**Answer: False — Tiny holdings CANNOT take large-scale inputs — that limit is one of the strategy's named weaknesses.**
