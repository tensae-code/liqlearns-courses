# Uni-modal strategy — betting on the smallholder — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "The strategy — intensify the small farm" in your own words.
**Answer:** Uni-modal strategy intensifies small peasant farms in place, with divisible innovations, revolving credit and a food-crop focus.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Its strengths" in your own words.
**Answer:** Uni-modal is pro-poor, blocks eviction, and reduces rural poverty and out-migration.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Its weaknesses" in your own words.
**Answer:** Its weaknesses: high-value crops, market/weather information and infrastructure neglected; tiny holdings cannot take large-scale inputs.
