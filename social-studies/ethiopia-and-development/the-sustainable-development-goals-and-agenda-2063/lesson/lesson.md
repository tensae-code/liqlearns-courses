# The Sustainable Development Goals — and Africa's Agenda 2063

## Do it first

The MDGs ended in 2015. Their successor list is longer, binds rich countries too, and runs to 2030 — while Africa wrote a fifty-year plan of its own. Hold both in mind before the lesson sets them side by side.

## Sub lesson 1 — Seventeen goals for 2030

The 2030 Agenda, adopted by all UN member states in 2015, sets 17 Sustainable Development Goals — broader and more universal than the MDGs, binding developed and developing countries alike, and integrating the social, economic and environmental dimensions.

[Picture 24940 — the 17-goal wheel beside a map of Africa marked 2013 to 2063]

## Sub lesson 2 — Broader than the MDGs

Where the MDGs targeted the poorest countries' basics, the SDGs integrate the social, economic and environmental dimensions into one agenda for everyone — development as a single fabric, not eight separate campaigns.

## Sub lesson 3 — Agenda 2063 — Africa's blueprint

Alongside the global goals stands Agenda 2063: the African Union's 50-year blueprint (2013–2063) for an integrated, prosperous and peaceful Africa, born at the OAU/AU golden jubilee.
