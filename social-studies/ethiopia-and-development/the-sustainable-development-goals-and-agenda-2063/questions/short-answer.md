# The Sustainable Development Goals — and Africa's Agenda 2063 — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Seventeen goals for 2030" in your own words.
**Answer:** The SDGs are 17 goals for 2030, adopted by all UN member states in 2015 and binding rich and poor countries alike.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Broader than the MDGs" in your own words.
**Answer:** The SDGs integrate social, economic and environmental dimensions for all countries.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Agenda 2063 — Africa's blueprint" in your own words.
**Answer:** Agenda 2063 is the AU's 50-year blueprint (2013–2063) for an integrated, prosperous and peaceful Africa.
