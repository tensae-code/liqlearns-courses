# The Sustainable Development Goals — and Africa's Agenda 2063 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The SDGs bind developing countries only.
**Answer: False — Unlike the MDGs, the SDGs bind developed AND developing countries — that universality is a headline difference.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The SDGs dropped the environmental dimension.
**Answer: False — Environment is one of the three integrated dimensions — social, economic and environmental.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Agenda 2063 is a UN programme for Asia.
**Answer: False — It is the African Union's own blueprint for Africa, born at the OAU/AU golden jubilee, spanning 2013–2063.**
