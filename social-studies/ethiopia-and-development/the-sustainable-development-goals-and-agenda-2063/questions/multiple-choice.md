# The Sustainable Development Goals — and Africa's Agenda 2063 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Seventeen goals for 2030"?
a) The SDGs are 17 goals for 2030, adopted by all UN member states in 2015 and binding rich and poor countries alike.  b) The SDGs integrate social, economic and environmental dimensions for all countries.  c) Agenda 2063 is the AU's 50-year blueprint (2013–2063) for an integrated, prosperous and peaceful Africa.
**Answer: a) The SDGs are 17 goals for 2030, adopted by all UN member states in 2015 and binding rich and poor countries alike.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Broader than the MDGs"?
a) Agenda 2063 is the AU's 50-year blueprint (2013–2063) for an integrated, prosperous and peaceful Africa.  b) The SDGs are 17 goals for 2030, adopted by all UN member states in 2015 and binding rich and poor countries alike.  c) The SDGs integrate social, economic and environmental dimensions for all countries.
**Answer: c) The SDGs integrate social, economic and environmental dimensions for all countries.**
