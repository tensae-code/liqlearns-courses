# Industrial parks — Ethiopia's factory zones — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Industrial parks scatter production factors across the country.
**Answer: False — They CONCENTRATE factors in one serviced place — that concentration is the whole instrument.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Ethiopia's parks are sited far from transport and workers.
**Answer: False — They are sited along corridors, linked to ports by road and electric railway, near large labour pools.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Zero liquid discharge is not part of the parks' sustainability anchor.
**Answer: False — It is — sustainability is anchored on renewable energy and zero liquid discharge.**
