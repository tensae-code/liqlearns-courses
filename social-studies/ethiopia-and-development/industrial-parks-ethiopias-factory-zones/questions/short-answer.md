# Industrial parks — Ethiopia's factory zones — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Why parks — factors in one place" in your own words.
**Answer:** Parks concentrate scarce production factors to draw investment, create jobs, raise skills and join global value chains.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "What Ethiopia's parks are like" in your own words.
**Answer:** Ethiopia's parks sit along corridors linked to ports by road and electric railway, near large labour pools.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Anchors and credited gains" in your own words.
**Answer:** Park anchors: specialisation/clustering, export promotion, sustainability, vertical integration, skills; gains include jobs, exports and forex.
