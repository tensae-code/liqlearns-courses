# Industrial parks — Ethiopia's factory zones

## Do it first

Factories scattered one by one each need their own power, water, road and customs. What changes when a state builds one serviced zone and invites them all inside the fence? List the gains before the parks are described.

## Sub lesson 1 — Why parks — factors in one place

Industrial parks concentrate scarce production factors in one place to draw labour- and capital-intensive domestic and foreign investment, create jobs, raise skills and plug the economy into global value chains.

[Picture 24950 — an aerial view of a specialised industrial park beside a railway line running toward a port]

## Sub lesson 2 — What Ethiopia's parks are like

Ethiopia's parks are open to domestic and foreign investors, state-of-the-art, and sited along economic corridors linked to ports by road and electric railway, near large labour pools — placed where workers are and where exports can leave.

## Sub lesson 3 — Anchors and credited gains

The parks are anchored on specialisation and clustering, export promotion, sustainability (renewable energy, zero liquid discharge), vertical integration, and skills development — and are credited with employment, revenue, exports, product diversification and foreign-exchange earnings.
