# Video script — General management skills

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

General management skills organise the firm's physical and financial resources. Strategy skills: seeing the whole business, its market fit and its edge over competitors. Planning skills: preparing now for what the future may bring.

Marketing skills: seeing how offerings satisfy customer needs. Financial skills: managing money, tracking expenses and cash flow, judging investments and their risks. One skill finds the customers; the other keeps the firm alive to serve them.

Project management skills: objectives, timelines, and resources in the right place at the right time. Time management skills: prioritising and finishing on time. Together they are the difference between a business that intends and a business that delivers.
