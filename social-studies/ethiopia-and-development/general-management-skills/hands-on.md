# Hands-on — General management skills

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Dial game: scenario cards are read ('a big order lands', 'cash runs short', 'a rival opens') — learners point to the skill-dial each scenario tests and say the action it demands.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — a six-dial control panel, one dial per skill, over a small business — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
