# General management skills — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Strategy and planning" in your own words.
**Answer:** Strategy skills mean seeing the whole business, its market fit and its edge over competitors; planning skills mean preparing now for the future.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Marketing and financial skills" in your own words.
**Answer:** Marketing skills mean seeing how offerings satisfy customer needs, and financial skills mean managing money and tracking expenses and cash flow.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Project and time management" in your own words.
**Answer:** Project management puts objectives, timelines and resources in the right place at the right time, and time management means prioritising and finishing on time.
