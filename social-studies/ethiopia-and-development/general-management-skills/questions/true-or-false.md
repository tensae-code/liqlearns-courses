# General management skills — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Strategy skills mean seeing the whole business, its market fit and its edge over competitors; planning skills mean preparing now for the future.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Strategy skills mean seeing the whole business, its market fit and its edge over competitors; planning skills mean preparing now for the future.
**Answer: False** — that statement belongs to sub lesson 1 (Strategy and planning); sub lesson 2 says: Marketing skills mean seeing how offerings satisfy customer needs, and financial skills mean managing money and tracking expenses and cash flow.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Project management puts objectives, timelines and resources in the right place at the right time, and time management means prioritising and finishing on time.
**Answer: True**
