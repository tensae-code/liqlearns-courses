# General management skills — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Strategy and planning"?
a) seeing the whole business and its edge; preparing now for the future  b) seeing how offerings satisfy needs; managing money, expenses, cash flow and investment risk  c) objectives, timelines and resources in place; prioritising and finishing on time
**Answer: a) seeing the whole business and its edge; preparing now for the future**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Marketing and financial skills"?
a) seeing the whole business and its edge; preparing now for the future  b) objectives, timelines and resources in place; prioritising and finishing on time  c) seeing how offerings satisfy needs; managing money, expenses, cash flow and investment risk
**Answer: c) seeing how offerings satisfy needs; managing money, expenses, cash flow and investment risk**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Project and time management"?
a) seeing how offerings satisfy needs; managing money, expenses, cash flow and investment risk  b) objectives, timelines and resources in place; prioritising and finishing on time  c) seeing the whole business and its edge; preparing now for the future
**Answer: b) objectives, timelines and resources in place; prioritising and finishing on time**
