# Hands-on — ADLI — Agricultural Development-Led Industrialization

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Engine drawing
Draw an extension worker handing seed and fertiliser to a smallholder, with a factory on the horizon the harvest is meant to feed. Label the arrow 'agriculture leads' and say what travels along it.

## Three thrusts check
Write ADLI's three thrusts from memory, then sort six programme items (a rural road, a seed scheme, a weaving cooperative, a fertiliser depot, a dam, a TVET college) under the thrust each serves — some serve the support ring instead; say which.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
