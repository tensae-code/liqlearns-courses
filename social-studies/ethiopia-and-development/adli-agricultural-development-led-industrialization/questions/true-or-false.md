# ADLI — Agricultural Development-Led Industrialization — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** ADLI (1994) made agriculture the engine of industrialization.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** ADLI's three thrusts: agricultural technologies, infrastructure and inputs, rural non-farm sectors.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The PADETES extension system carried fertiliser, seed, credit and advice to smallholders.
**Answer: True**
