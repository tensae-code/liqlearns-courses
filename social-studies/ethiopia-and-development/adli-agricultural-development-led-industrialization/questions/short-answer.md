# ADLI — Agricultural Development-Led Industrialization — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "The reversal of 1991–94" in your own words.
**Answer:** ADLI (1994) made agriculture the engine of industrialization.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Food self-sufficiency through productivity" in your own words.
**Answer:** ADLI's three thrusts: agricultural technologies, infrastructure and inputs, rural non-farm sectors.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "The supporting system" in your own words.
**Answer:** The PADETES extension system carried fertiliser, seed, credit and advice to smallholders.
