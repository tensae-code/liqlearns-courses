Grade 10 teacher-guide note (placement 22): the factory names come from the Activity 7.4 key in *Economics, Teacher Guide Book, Grade 10*, printed p. 100 (teacher guide — evidence) — the concrete Ethiopian examples the student textbook's industry section lacks.

**Acceptance count: taught in 1 country (Ethiopia).**

As printed, §7.4's sub-sector share sentence garbles the electricity share twice; the composition taught here uses the lesson's printed contribution figures (large and medium manufacturing 38.4%, small-scale and handicrafts 18.7%, mining 3.9%, quarrying 15.8%, electricity 23.2% for 1991/92–97/98 — summing to 100) and names the garbled sentence here only. Manufacturing's food-and-beverages share is printed as about 48% of gross output in 2007/08 and elsewhere as 50.7%; the lesson teaches 'about 48%' from the fuller passage and records the second figure here.
