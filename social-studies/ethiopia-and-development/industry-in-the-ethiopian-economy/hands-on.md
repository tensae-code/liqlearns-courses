# Hands-on — Industry in the Ethiopian economy

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
An assembly line of learners passes product cards; a 'missing spare part' card stops the line cold. The class names the shortage problem, then restarts the line with export cards — textiles, food, leather — flowing to a forex till.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — a beverage plant as the leading manufacturer and a textile bale stamped for export under AGOA, beside a factory line idle for want of a spare part — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.

## Grade 11 deepening from the Grade 10 teacher guide — say it with things at home
Take the newest part of this lesson and act it out with things at home — objects, counters, or a partner — saying each key term aloud as you use it. Then teach the part to your partner without the lesson open.
