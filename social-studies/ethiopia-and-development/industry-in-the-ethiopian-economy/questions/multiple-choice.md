# Industry in the Ethiopian economy — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Small size, clear features"?
a) about 13% of GDP, held back by shortages, low productivity and underused capacity  b) manufacturing led by food and beverages at about 48% of gross output  c) output, employment and forex — textiles 41.5% of sector forex, AGOA opening the US market
**Answer: a) about 13% of GDP, held back by shortages, low productivity and underused capacity**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Composition and manufacturing"?
a) about 13% of GDP, held back by shortages, low productivity and underused capacity  b) output, employment and forex — textiles 41.5% of sector forex, AGOA opening the US market  c) manufacturing led by food and beverages at about 48% of gross output
**Answer: c) manufacturing led by food and beverages at about 48% of gross output**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Three contributions"?
a) manufacturing led by food and beverages at about 48% of gross output  b) output, employment and forex — textiles 41.5% of sector forex, AGOA opening the US market  c) about 13% of GDP, held back by shortages, low productivity and underused capacity
**Answer: b) output, employment and forex — textiles 41.5% of sector forex, AGOA opening the US market**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Which statement best matches "Ethiopia's named factories (teacher guide)"?
a) cement from Mugher, Dangote, Mosobo, Capital and Habesha; sugar and textiles from their named factories  b) a different part of this lesson, as stated in the earlier sub lessons  c) the opposite claim to the one this sub lesson teaches
