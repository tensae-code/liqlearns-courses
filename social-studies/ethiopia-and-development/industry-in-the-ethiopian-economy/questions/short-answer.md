# Industry in the Ethiopian economy — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Small size, clear features" in your own words.
**Answer:** Industry makes up about 13% of GDP and 9.5% of employment.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Composition and manufacturing" in your own words.
**Answer:** Manufacturing is dominated by food and beverages at about 48% of gross output in 2007/08.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Three contributions" in your own words.
**Answer:** Textiles earn 41.5% of industry's foreign exchange, ahead of food and beverages at 32.9%.

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Explain "Ethiopia's named factories (teacher guide)" in your own words.
**Answer:** Ethiopia's cement factories named in the guide include Mugher, Dangote, Mosobo, Capital and Habesha.
