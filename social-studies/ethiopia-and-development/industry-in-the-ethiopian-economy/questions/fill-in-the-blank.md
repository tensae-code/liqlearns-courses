# Industry in the Ethiopian economy — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Industry accounts for about _____ of GDP — small beside agriculture and services.
**Answer: 13%**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Manufacturing is dominated by _____ at about 48% of gross output.
**Answer: food and beverages**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** _____ earn 41.5% of industry's foreign exchange — with AGOA opening the US market.
**Answer: textiles**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Ethiopia's named cement producers include _____, Dangote, Mosobo, Capital and Habesha.
**Answer: Mugher**
