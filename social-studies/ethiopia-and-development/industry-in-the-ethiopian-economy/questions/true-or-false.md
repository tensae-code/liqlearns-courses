# Industry in the Ethiopian economy — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Industry makes up about 13% of GDP and 9.5% of employment.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Manufacturing is dominated by food and beverages at about 48% of gross output in 2007/08.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Textiles earn 41.5% of industry's foreign exchange, ahead of food and beverages at 32.9%.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Ethiopia's cement factories named in the guide include Mugher, Dangote, Mosobo, Capital and Habesha.
**Answer: True**
