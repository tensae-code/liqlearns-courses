# Creative destruction — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Schumpeter's argument" in your own words.
**Answer:** Schumpeter argued capitalism develops through creative destruction — imbalance, not equilibrium.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "The fallen leaders" in your own words.
**Answer:** Nokia and Kodak were leaders who failed to see their industries change.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "The gallery of innovators" in your own words.
**Answer:** The gallery names eleven innovators, from Leonardo da Vinci to Steve Jobs and Marie Curie.
