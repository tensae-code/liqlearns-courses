# Creative destruction — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** For Schumpeter, capitalism develops through _____ — new ideas destroying the old.
**Answer: imbalance**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** _____ and Nokia, once leaders, failed to see their industries change — the fallen pair.
**Answer: Kodak**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The book's gallery of famous innovators names _____ people, each tied to a field.
