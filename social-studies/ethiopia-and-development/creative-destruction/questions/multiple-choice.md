# Creative destruction — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Schumpeter's argument"?
a) development through imbalance — new ideas destroying the old to make room for better  b) Nokia and Kodak — leaders who failed to see their industries change  c) eleven named innovators, each tied to their field
**Answer: a) development through imbalance — new ideas destroying the old to make room for better**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "The fallen leaders"?
a) development through imbalance — new ideas destroying the old to make room for better  b) eleven named innovators, each tied to their field  c) Nokia and Kodak — leaders who failed to see their industries change
**Answer: c) Nokia and Kodak — leaders who failed to see their industries change**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "The gallery of innovators"?
a) Nokia and Kodak — leaders who failed to see their industries change  b) eleven named innovators, each tied to their field  c) development through imbalance — new ideas destroying the old to make room for better
**Answer: b) eleven named innovators, each tied to their field**
