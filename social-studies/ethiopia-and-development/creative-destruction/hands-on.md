# Hands-on — Creative destruction

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
A portrait wall is built on the classroom wall: learners pin innovator cards (Jobs, Curie, Musk, Kiessling, Tesla, Jones, Edison, Hopper, da Vinci, Cochrane, Bell) with their fields, then pin the film camera and the phone camera at the end as before-and-after.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — a film camera and a phone camera, before and after, beside a portrait wall of eleven innovators — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
