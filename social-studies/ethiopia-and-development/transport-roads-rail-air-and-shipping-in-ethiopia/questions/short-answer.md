# Transport — roads, rail, air and shipping in Ethiopia — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name two ways road performance is judged.
**Answer: Any two of: network stock in km; road density per 1,000 km²; share of land more than 5 km from an all-weather road; average time to reach one.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** When was Ethiopian Airlines founded, and with whose assistance?
**Answer: On 29 December 1945, with TWA's assistance.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name three measures of the shipping line's performance.
**Answer: Any three of: cargo lifted, manpower, revenue, profit, dry-cargo market share.**
