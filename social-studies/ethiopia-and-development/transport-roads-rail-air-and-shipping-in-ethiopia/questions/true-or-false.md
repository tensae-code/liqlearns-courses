# Transport — roads, rail, air and shipping in Ethiopia — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Road performance is judged only by the number of vehicles registered.
**Answer: False**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Ethiopian Airlines' first flights used five Douglas DC-3s.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Ethiopia has no shipping line because it is landlocked.
**Answer: False**
