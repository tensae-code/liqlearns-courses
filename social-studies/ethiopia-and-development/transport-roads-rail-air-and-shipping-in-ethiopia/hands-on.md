# Hands-on — Transport — roads, rail, air and shipping in Ethiopia

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Mode audit
For one journey you know well, list which mode carries each leg and estimate each mode's share. Then say where the over-90% road dependence helps and where it hurts.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the four-mode map from memory and label every part. Then open the lesson, compare, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
