# Teamwork and diversity make businesses succeed — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Cooperation and broader perspectives" in your own words.
**Answer:** Diverse teams are cited as more creative and performing up to 35 percent better.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Productivity and learning" in your own words.
**Answer:** Teamwork raises productivity through shared workload, combined skills and brainstorming that builds on others' ideas.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "The culture it builds" in your own words.
**Answer:** Teamwork builds a culture of friendship, loyalty, trust and belonging that raises morale, reliability and effectiveness.
