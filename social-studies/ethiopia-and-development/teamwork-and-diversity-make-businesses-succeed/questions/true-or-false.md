# Teamwork and diversity make businesses succeed — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Diverse teams are cited as more creative and performing up to 35 percent better.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Diverse teams are cited as more creative and performing up to 35 percent better.
**Answer: False** — that statement belongs to sub lesson 1 (Cooperation and broader perspectives); sub lesson 2 says: Teamwork raises productivity through shared workload, combined skills and brainstorming that builds on others' ideas.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Teamwork builds a culture of friendship, loyalty, trust and belonging that raises morale, reliability and effectiveness.
**Answer: True**
