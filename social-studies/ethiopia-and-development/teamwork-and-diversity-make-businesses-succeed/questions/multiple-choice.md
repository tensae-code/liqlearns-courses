# Teamwork and diversity make businesses succeed — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Cooperation and broader perspectives"?
a) cooperation toward a common goal; diverse teams cited up to 35 percent better  b) shared workload, combined skills and brainstorming; learning through exchanged knowledge  c) friendship, loyalty, trust and belonging raising morale, reliability and effectiveness

**Answer: c) shared workload, combined skills and brainstorming; learning through exchanged knowledge**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "The culture it builds"?
