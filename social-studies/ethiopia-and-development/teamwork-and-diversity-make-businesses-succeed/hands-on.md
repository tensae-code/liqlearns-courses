# Hands-on — Teamwork and diversity make businesses succeed

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Passing drill as metaphor: a ball (or a task card) must reach a goal through every team member's hands; then once through only the 'stars'. Compare speed and count the lesson's benefits in each run.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — a diverse team passing a task along like a ball toward one goal — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
