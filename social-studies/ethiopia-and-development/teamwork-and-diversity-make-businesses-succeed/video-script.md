# Video script — Teamwork and diversity make businesses succeed

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Teamwork's first benefits: cooperation toward a common goal — the football team that passes to the better-placed player wins more. And broader perspectives: diverse teams (gender, age, ethnicity) are cited as more creative and performing up to 35 percent better.

Higher productivity follows through shared workload, combined skills and brainstorming that builds on others' ideas. And learning flows through exchanging knowledge — a team teaches its members as a by-product of working.

Teamwork builds a culture of friendship, loyalty, trust and belonging that raises morale, reliability and effectiveness. Against the book's target — the 'few star employees' idea — the team's culture is itself a business asset no star can carry alone.
