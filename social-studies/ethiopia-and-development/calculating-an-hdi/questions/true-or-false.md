# Calculating an HDI — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Income enters the HDI at its full face value.
**Answer: False — Income enters logarithmically — extra income's utility diminishes, so its index grows more slowly.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The HDI is the largest of the three component indices.
**Answer: False — It is the simple AVERAGE of the three — no component, however strong, decides it alone.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Ethiopia's 2019 HDI placed it in the high human development category.
**Answer: False — 0.485 sits in the LOW human development category — though it climbed 66.1% from 2000.**
