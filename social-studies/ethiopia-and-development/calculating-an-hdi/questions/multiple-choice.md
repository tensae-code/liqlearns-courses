# Calculating an HDI — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Goalposts — turning measures into indices"?
a) Each index = (actual − minimum) ÷ (maximum − minimum); income enters logarithmically.  b) Life expectancy 65 on goalposts 20–80 gives an index of 0.75, and the HDI is the simple average of the three indices.  c) Ethiopia's 2019 HDI was 0.485, low human development, 173rd of 189, up 66.1% since 2000.
