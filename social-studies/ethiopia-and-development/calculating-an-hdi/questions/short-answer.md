# Calculating an HDI — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Goalposts — turning measures into indices" in your own words.
**Answer:** Each index = (actual − minimum) ÷ (maximum − minimum); income enters logarithmically.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "The worked example — 0.75" in your own words.
**Answer:** Life expectancy 65 on goalposts 20–80 gives an index of 0.75, and the HDI is the simple average of the three indices.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Ethiopia's score" in your own words.
**Answer:** Ethiopia's 2019 HDI was 0.485, low human development, 173rd of 189, up 66.1% since 2000.
