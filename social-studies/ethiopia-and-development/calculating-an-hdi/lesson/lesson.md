# Calculating an HDI

## Do it first

A country scores 0.9 on income but 0.4 on life expectancy. Before any formula, guess: will its HDI sit near the top score, the bottom one, or between them — and what rule decides?

## Sub lesson 1 — Goalposts — turning measures into indices

Each component becomes an index between fixed minimum and maximum goalposts: index = (actual − minimum) ÷ (maximum − minimum). Income enters logarithmically, because extra income's utility diminishes — a rich country's extra birr counts for less than a poor country's.

## Sub lesson 2 — The worked example — 0.75

The book works life expectancy of 65 years against goalposts of 20 and 80: index = (65 − 20) ÷ (80 − 20) = 45 ÷ 60 = 0.75. The HDI itself is then the simple average of the three component indices — life, education and living standard added and divided by three.

## Sub lesson 3 — Ethiopia's score

Ethiopia's HDI for 2019 was 0.485, in the low human development category, ranked 173rd of 189 countries. That is up 66.1% from 0.292 in 2000, with life expectancy up 19.5 years since 1990 — a low score, but a fast-climbing one.
