BOOK INCONSISTENCY TAUGHT CORRECTED: the student book's §6.3 gives two inconsistent life-expectancy scalings — its formula box uses minimum 25 / maximum 85, while its worked example uses minimum 20 / maximum 80. This lesson follows the WORKED EXAMPLE (20/80), which produces the book's stated index of 0.75 for 65 years: (65 − 20) ÷ (80 − 20) = 0.75. On the formula box's 25/85 posts the same life expectancy would give (65 − 25) ÷ 60 ≈ 0.667, not 0.75 — so the two cannot both be right, and the worked example is the one carried here.
