# Cost-benefit analysis — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Cost-benefit analysis includes dollar values for intangibles and opportunity cost.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Step 2 of CBA gives every cost and benefit a monetary value.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Running the b/c ratio across rival options lets them be compared side by side.
**Answer: True**
