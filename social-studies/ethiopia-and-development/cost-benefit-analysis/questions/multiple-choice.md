# Cost-benefit analysis — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Weighing benefits against costs"?
a) summed benefits against summed costs, intangibles and opportunity cost priced in  b) list everything, price everything, then compare by the b/c ratio  c) the same b/c equation across rivals, compared side by side
**Answer: a) summed benefits against summed costs, intangibles and opportunity cost priced in**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "The three-step procedure"?
a) summed benefits against summed costs, intangibles and opportunity cost priced in  b) the same b/c equation across rivals, compared side by side  c) list everything, price everything, then compare by the b/c ratio
**Answer: c) list everything, price everything, then compare by the b/c ratio**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Comparing rival options"?
a) list everything, price everything, then compare by the b/c ratio  b) the same b/c equation across rivals, compared side by side  c) summed benefits against summed costs, intangibles and opportunity cost priced in
**Answer: b) the same b/c equation across rivals, compared side by side**
