# Cost-benefit analysis — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Weighing benefits against costs" in your own words.
**Answer:** Cost-benefit analysis includes dollar values for intangibles and opportunity cost.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "The three-step procedure" in your own words.
**Answer:** Step 2 of CBA gives every cost and benefit a monetary value.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Comparing rival options" in your own words.
**Answer:** Running the b/c ratio across rival options lets them be compared side by side.
