# Hands-on — Cost-benefit analysis

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
A balance scale drawn large: learners place birr-note cards for benefits on one pan and costs on the other — software price, lost hours priced, time saved priced — and read the b/c verdict off the tilt.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — a balance scale with birr benefits on one side and birr costs on the other, and two piles of birr-notes compared — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
