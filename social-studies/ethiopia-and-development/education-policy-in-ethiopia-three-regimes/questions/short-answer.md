# Education policy in Ethiopia — three regimes — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Before 1974 — a review too late" in your own words.
**Answer:** The Imperial Education Sector Review recommended universal primary education and ruralised curricula, published February 1974.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "The Derg — schooling for socialism" in your own words.
**Answer:** The Derg sent some 60,000 students and teachers to the rural zemecha campaign and aimed schooling at socialist consciousness, production and research.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "After 1991 — the ESDP and its problems" in your own words.
**Answer:** The ESDP's six components run from primary education to institutional capacity building; remedies centre on participation, materials, equality and better teachers.
