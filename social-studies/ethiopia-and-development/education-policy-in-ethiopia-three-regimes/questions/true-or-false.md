# Education policy in Ethiopia — three regimes — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Imperial government ran a full education strategy from 1950.
**Answer: False — It adopted NO real education strategy; its review appeared only in February 1974.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The Derg kept private schools fully independent.
**Answer: False — Private schools were NATIONALISED, and the university itself was closed for the campaign.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The ESDP had two components.
**Answer: False — Six components are listed — primary, secondary, TVET, teacher training, tertiary, and institutional capacity.**
