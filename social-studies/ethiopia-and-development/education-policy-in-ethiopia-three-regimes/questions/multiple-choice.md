# Education policy in Ethiopia — three regimes — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Before 1974 — a review too late"?
a) The Imperial Education Sector Review recommended universal primary education and ruralised curricula, published February 1974.  b) The Derg sent some 60,000 students and teachers to the rural zemecha campaign and aimed schooling at socialist consciousness, production and research.  c) The ESDP's six components run from primary education to institutional capacity building; remedies centre on participation, materials, equality and better teachers.
