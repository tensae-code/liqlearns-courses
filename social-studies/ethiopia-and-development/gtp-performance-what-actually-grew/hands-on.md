# Hands-on — GTP performance — what actually grew

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Scoreboard bars
Draw the scoreboard: target 11% versus achieved 9.2% as paired bars, with construction, crops and trade as the three tallest sector bars. Label every bar with its figure.

## Find the deficit
Recompute the resource deficit from 25.9 and 10.1. Then write two sentences: why a deficit that size can finance growth for a while, and why it cannot do so forever.

## Teach it on the spot
