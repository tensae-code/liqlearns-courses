# GTP performance — what actually grew — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Real GDP grew 9.2% a year against the 11% target, from Birr 828 billion to Birr 1.99 trillion.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Industry grew 17.2% and construction supplied 77.1% of industry's contribution to growth.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Exports were 10.1% of GDP against imports of 25.9% — a resource deficit of 15.8%.
**Answer: True**
