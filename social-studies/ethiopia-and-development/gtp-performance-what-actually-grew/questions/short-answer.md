# GTP performance — what actually grew — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "The headline scoreboard" in your own words.
**Answer:** Real GDP grew 9.2% a year against the 11% target, from Birr 828 billion to Birr 1.99 trillion.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Which sectors pulled" in your own words.
**Answer:** Industry grew 17.2% and construction supplied 77.1% of industry's contribution to growth.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "The demand side's weak point" in your own words.
**Answer:** Exports were 10.1% of GDP against imports of 25.9% — a resource deficit of 15.8%.
