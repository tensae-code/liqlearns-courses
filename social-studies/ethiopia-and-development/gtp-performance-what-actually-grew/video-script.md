# Video script — GTP performance — what actually grew

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Measured at constant 2015/16 prices, real GDP grew 9.2% a year on average — 1.8 points short of the 11% target — rising from Birr 828 billion (2009/10) to Birr 1.99 trillion (2019/20). Nominal GDP rose from Birr 395.9 billion to Birr 3.37 trillion.

Sector growth: agriculture 5.3%, industry 17.2%, services 9.7% — contributing 24%, 37.9% and 40.8% of growth respectively. The real engines inside them: construction (77.1% of industry's contribution), crops (72.7% of agriculture's) and wholesale/retail trade (37.8% of services').

From the demand side, final consumption was 79% of GDP (over three-quarters of it private), investment averaged 35.1%, exports only 10.1% against imports of 25.9% — an average resource deficit of 15.8% a year (25.9 − 10.1). That gap was the plans' structural weak point.
