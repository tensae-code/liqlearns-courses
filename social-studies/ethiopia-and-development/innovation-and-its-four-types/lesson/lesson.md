# Innovation — and its four types

## Do it first

Four moves: build the first-ever solar lamp; add a bigger battery to an existing lamp; copy a proven lamp exactly for a new town; merge a lamp with a phone charger. Which is which type of innovation? Sort before reading.

## Sub lesson 1 — Innovation — the idea implemented

Innovation is at the heart of the entrepreneurial process: implementing a new idea — renewing a product, service or process by new processes, techniques or successful ideas to create new value. It is the means by which opportunity is exploited: creativity proposes, innovation delivers.

## Sub lesson 2 — Invention and extension

Four distinct types. Invention: creation of a new product, service or process. Extension (the summary and review also call it expansion): growth of an existing one — the same thing, developed further.

## Sub lesson 3 — Duplication and synthesis

Duplication: replication of an existing product, service or process — copying what works, into a new place or firm. Synthesis: combining existing concepts into a new formulation — two known things becoming a third thing that is neither.
