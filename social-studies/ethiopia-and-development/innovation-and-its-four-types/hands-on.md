# Hands-on — Innovation — and its four types

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Card sort: deal eight innovation story cards; groups sort them into the four types and must read the deciding phrase aloud ('brand-new', 'growth of existing', 'exact copy', 'combination').

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — four panels — a brand-new object, an existing one growing, an exact copy, two ideas merging into one — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
