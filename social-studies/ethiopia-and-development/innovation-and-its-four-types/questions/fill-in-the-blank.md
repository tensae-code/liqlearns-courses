# Innovation — and its four types — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Innovation is _____ a new idea to create new value.
**Answer: implementing**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** _____ is the creation of a new product, service or process.
**Answer: invention**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** _____ combines existing concepts into a new formulation.
**Answer: synthesis**
