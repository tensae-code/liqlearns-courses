# Innovation — and its four types — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Innovation — the idea implemented" in your own words.
**Answer:** Innovation is implementing a new idea to create new value, and it is the means by which opportunity is exploited.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Invention and extension" in your own words.
**Answer:** Invention is the creation of a new product, service or process, and extension is the growth of an existing one.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Duplication and synthesis" in your own words.
**Answer:** Duplication is replication of an existing product, service or process, and synthesis is combining existing concepts into a new formulation.
