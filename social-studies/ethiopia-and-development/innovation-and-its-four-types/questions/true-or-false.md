# Innovation — and its four types — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Innovation is implementing a new idea to create new value, and it is the means by which opportunity is exploited.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Innovation is implementing a new idea to create new value, and it is the means by which opportunity is exploited.
**Answer: False** — that statement belongs to sub lesson 1 (Innovation — the idea implemented); sub lesson 2 says: Invention is the creation of a new product, service or process, and extension is the growth of an existing one.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Duplication is replication of an existing product, service or process, and synthesis is combining existing concepts into a new formulation.
**Answer: True**
