# PASDEP — the second poverty reduction strategy — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Scaling up to the MDGs" in your own words.
**Answer:** PASDEP aimed at the MDGs by 2015 and middle-income status by 2025.
