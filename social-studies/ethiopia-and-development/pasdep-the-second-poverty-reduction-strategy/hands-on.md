# Hands-on — PASDEP — the second poverty reduction strategy

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Eight pillars
Draw eight pillars holding a lintel labelled 'middle income by 2025', each pillar named. Under the drawing, write which pillar you judge most loaded, and why.

## Two futures
Write the two scenarios as two one-paragraph futures for a farming family — at 7% and at 10% — using only differences the growth gap could plausibly make.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
