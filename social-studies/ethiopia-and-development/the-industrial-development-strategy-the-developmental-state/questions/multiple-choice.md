# The Industrial Development Strategy — the developmental state — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "The road to the strategy"?
a) The comprehensive Industrial Development Strategy of 2002/03, built on ADLI, emphasised export-led, labour-intensive industrialisation.  b) The strategy names the private sector as engine and government as leading manager — a developmental state.  c) The strategy curbs rent-seeking capitalists, promotes developmental ones, and prioritises textiles, meat, leather, agro-processing, construction and small enterprises.
**Answer: a) The comprehensive Industrial Development Strategy of 2002/03, built on ADLI, emphasised export-led, labour-intensive industrialisation.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Engine and driver"?
a) The strategy curbs rent-seeking capitalists, promotes developmental ones, and prioritises textiles, meat, leather, agro-processing, construction and small enterprises.  b) The comprehensive Industrial Development Strategy of 2002/03, built on ADLI, emphasised export-led, labour-intensive industrialisation.  c) The strategy names the private sector as engine and government as leading manager — a developmental state.
**Answer: c) The strategy names the private sector as engine and government as leading manager — a developmental state.**
