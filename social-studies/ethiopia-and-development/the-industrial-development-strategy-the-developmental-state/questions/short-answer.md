# The Industrial Development Strategy — the developmental state — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "The road to the strategy" in your own words.
**Answer:** The comprehensive Industrial Development Strategy of 2002/03, built on ADLI, emphasised export-led, labour-intensive industrialisation.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Engine and driver" in your own words.
**Answer:** The strategy names the private sector as engine and government as leading manager — a developmental state.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Two capitalists — and the priority sectors" in your own words.
**Answer:** The strategy curbs rent-seeking capitalists, promotes developmental ones, and prioritises textiles, meat, leather, agro-processing, construction and small enterprises.
