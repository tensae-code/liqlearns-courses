# The Industrial Development Strategy — the developmental state — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The 2002/03 strategy emphasised capital-intensive heavy industry.
**Answer: False — It emphasised EXPORT-LED, LABOUR-INTENSIVE industrialisation, with small enterprises for mass job creation.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The strategy makes government the engine of industrialisation.
**Answer: False — Government is the leading MANAGER; the private sector is the ENGINE. The developmental state steers, it does not replace.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Rent-seeking capitalists are the strategy's favoured group.
**Answer: False — They are to be CURBED. The promoted group is the 'developmental' capitalists.**
