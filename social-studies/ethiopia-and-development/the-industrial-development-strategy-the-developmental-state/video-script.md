# Video script — The Industrial Development Strategy — the developmental state

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

An export promotion strategy for high-value agriculture and labour-intensive manufactures (clothing, textiles, leather) was adopted in 1998. The comprehensive Industrial Development Strategy followed in 2002/03, built on ADLI, emphasising export-led, labour-intensive industrialisation, supporting infrastructure, and small enterprises for mass job creation.

Its principles name the private sector as the engine of industrialisation, and the government as leading manager rather than mere facilitator — a self-described developmental state on the East Asian model, steering the engine it does not itself provide.

The strategy sharply distinguishes 'rent-seeking' capitalists, to be curbed, from 'developmental' capitalists, to be promoted. Priority sectors for direct support: textiles and garments, meat, leather and leather products, agro-processing including sugar, construction, and micro and small enterprises.
