# Hands-on — Home-grown economic reform — sectoral and structural fronts

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Four icons
Draw four sector icons — field, mine, hotel, satellite dish — above a row of structural levers, and label two levers from the list under the icons they most affect.

## Crowding-out test
A ministry proposes running a business itself. Apply the structural rules as a three-question test (proven failure? capacity? could it be handed over?) and write the verdict for one real-style case.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
