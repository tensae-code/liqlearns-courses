# Home-grown economic reform — sectoral and structural fronts — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Sectoral reforms — four fronts"?
a) Sectoral reforms target agriculture, mining, tourism and ICT  b) Government leads but must not crowd out: intervention only where failure is proven, support by merit  c) The structural list runs from dry ports and import substitution to WTO accession and AfCFTA ratification
**Answer: a) Sectoral reforms target agriculture, mining, tourism and ICT**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Structural rules — lead without crowding out"?
a) Government leads but must not crowd out: intervention only where failure is proven, support by merit  b) The structural list runs from dry ports and import substitution to WTO accession and AfCFTA ratification  c) Sectoral reforms target agriculture, mining, tourism and ICT
**Answer: a) Government leads but must not crowd out: intervention only where failure is proven, support by merit**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "The long structural list"?
a) The structural list runs from dry ports and import substitution to WTO accession and AfCFTA ratification  b) Sectoral reforms target agriculture, mining, tourism and ICT  c) Government leads but must not crowd out: intervention only where failure is proven, support by merit
**Answer: a) The structural list runs from dry ports and import substitution to WTO accession and AfCFTA ratification**
