# Home-grown economic reform — sectoral and structural fronts — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Sectoral reforms target agriculture, mining, tourism and ICT.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Government leads but must not crowd out: intervention only where failure is proven, support by merit.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The structural list runs from dry ports and import substitution to WTO accession and AfCFTA ratification.
**Answer: True**
