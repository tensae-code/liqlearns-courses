# Home-grown economic reform — sectoral and structural fronts — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Sectoral reforms — four fronts" in your own words.
**Answer:** Sectoral reforms target agriculture, mining, tourism and ICT.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Structural rules — lead without crowding out" in your own words.
**Answer:** Government leads but must not crowd out: intervention only where failure is proven, support by merit.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "The long structural list" in your own words.
**Answer:** The structural list runs from dry ports and import substitution to WTO accession and AfCFTA ratification.
