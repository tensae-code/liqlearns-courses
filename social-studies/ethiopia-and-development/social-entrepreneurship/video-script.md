# Video script — Social entrepreneurship — business for social change

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Social entrepreneurship is entrepreneurship aimed at social change: individuals and start-ups developing solutions that directly address social issues. The market tool is kept; the target is a problem in people's lives.

Social entrepreneurs explore business opportunities with a positive impact on their community, society or the world. Profit keeps the enterprise alive; impact is what the enterprise is for.

The book is candid that the term has grown so broad and popular that its definition is unsettled — it now houses all manner of socially beneficial activity — while its promise of lasting, transformational benefit is clear. A wide word, an exacting promise.
