# Social entrepreneurship — business for social change — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Business aimed at change"?
a) solutions that directly address social issues, aimed at social change  b) business opportunities with a positive impact on community, society or the world  c) the term is broad and unsettled, though its transformational promise is clear
**Answer: a) solutions that directly address social issues, aimed at social change**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Impact as the measure"?
a) solutions that directly address social issues, aimed at social change  b) the term is broad and unsettled, though its transformational promise is clear  c) business opportunities with a positive impact on community, society or the world
**Answer: c) business opportunities with a positive impact on community, society or the world**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "A term honestly unsettled"?
a) business opportunities with a positive impact on community, society or the world  b) the term is broad and unsettled, though its transformational promise is clear  c) solutions that directly address social issues, aimed at social change
**Answer: b) the term is broad and unsettled, though its transformational promise is clear**
