# Social entrepreneurship — business for social change — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Business aimed at change" in your own words.
**Answer:** Social entrepreneurship develops business solutions that directly address social issues, aiming at social change.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Impact as the measure" in your own words.
**Answer:** Social entrepreneurship explores business opportunities with a positive impact on community, society or the world.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "A term honestly unsettled" in your own words.
**Answer:** The book says the term social entrepreneurship has grown broad and its definition is unsettled, while its promise of lasting transformational benefit is clear.
