# Social entrepreneurship — business for social change — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Social entrepreneurship develops business solutions that directly address social issues, aiming at social change.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Social entrepreneurship develops business solutions that directly address social issues, aiming at social change.
**Answer: False** — that statement belongs to sub lesson 1 (Business aimed at change); sub lesson 2 says: Social entrepreneurship explores business opportunities with a positive impact on community, society or the world.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The book says the term social entrepreneurship has grown broad and its definition is unsettled, while its promise of lasting transformational benefit is clear.
**Answer: True**
