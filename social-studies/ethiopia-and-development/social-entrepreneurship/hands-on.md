# Hands-on — Social entrepreneurship — business for social change

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Pitch circle: each learner pitches a social enterprise in three sentences; listeners must name the social issue and the business engine before applauding.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — a business whose profit arrow loops back into a community project — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
