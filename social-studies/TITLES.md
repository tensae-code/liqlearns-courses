# Social Studies course — titles, FROM THE BOTTOM

Social Studies is one world course: the Earth, its peoples and
their past are the same everywhere; only the way of teaching
differs, and the way shows inside as examples. The first stocked
part is built from Ethiopia's Social Studies books for Grades 7
and 8. The path climbs from the learner's own ground outward —
the planet under their feet, the people on it, the past behind
them, and the world they are growing into.

**First Steps → Earth and Space → Population, Transport and Maps
→ People and Resources → Ethiopia: Ancient and Medieval → Africa
to 1500 → The Ancient World → The Land and Air → Culture and
Work → Resources and Conflict → Modern Ethiopia → Africa after
1500 → The Modern World → Global Issues**

That ladder IS the path order (recommended, never forced — a
learner can jump in higher; the "start here instead" path points
down this ladder). Inside the course the organisation is by
**levels, not grades**: both school years' lessons sit inside
the one title at rising depth wherever a title spans both books
(as in Global Issues).

## The titles

- `first-steps/` — what Social Studies is, and how this course
  works (the books' own way: geography → history → today,
  activity first).
- `earth-and-space/` — the universe, the solar system, the
  Earth: shape, size, movements, seasons, time.
- `population-transport-and-maps/` — people on the Earth:
  distribution, movement, transport, and reading maps.
- `people-and-resources/` — settlement, natural resources,
  economic activities, and looking after the land.
- `ethiopia-ancient-and-medieval/` — Ethiopia from the earliest
  times through the medieval kingdoms and sultanates.
- `africa-to-1500/` — Africa's peoples, states and trade to
  about 1500.
- `ancient-world/` — the ancient civilisations and the medieval
  world beyond Africa.
- `the-land-and-air/` — continents and oceans, the forces that
  shape the land, the atmosphere, weather and climate, map work.
- `culture-and-work/` — culture, economic activities, trade,
  and tax.
- `resources-and-conflict/` — natural resources, their care,
  and conflict over resources.
- `modern-ethiopia/` — Ethiopia from 1855 to today. (Student
  book alone — no teacher evidence yet; the Grade 8 teacher
  guide's Units 4–7 are still owed.)
- `africa-after-1500/` — Africa from 1500: trade, colonisation,
  resistance, independence. (No teacher evidence yet.)
- `modern-world/` — the early-modern and modern world. (No
  teacher evidence yet.)
- `global-issues/` — today's shared problems: population
  pressure, HIV/AIDS, climate, water, forests — and protecting
  the environment where you live. (Grade 8 part: no teacher
  evidence yet.)
