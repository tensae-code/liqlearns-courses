# Social Studies course — titles, FROM THE BOTTOM

Social Studies is one world course: the Earth, its peoples and
their past are the same everywhere; only the way of teaching
differs, and the way shows inside as examples. The first stocked
part is built from Ethiopia's Social Studies books for Grades 7
and 8. The path climbs from the learner's own ground outward —
the planet under their feet, the people on it, the past behind
them, and the world they are growing into.

**First Steps → Earth and Space → Population, Transport and Maps
→ People and Resources → Ethiopia: Ancient and Medieval → Africa
to 1500 → The Ancient World → The Land and Air → Culture and
Work → Resources and Conflict → Modern Ethiopia → Africa after
1500 → The Modern World → Global Issues → Microeconomics →
Market and Money → Macroeconomics → Ethiopia and Development**

That ladder IS the path order (recommended, never forced — a
learner can jump in higher; the "start here instead" path points
down this ladder). Inside the course the organisation is by
**levels, not grades**: both school years' lessons sit inside
the one title at rising depth wherever a title spans both books
(as in Global Issues).

## The titles

- `first-steps/` — what Social Studies is, our neighbourhood,
  my house, my home, my town, my district and my village,
  Ibn Battuta — traveller and explorer, teaching Geography
  to beginning readers, and how this course
  works (the books' own way: geography →
  history → today, activity first).
- `earth-and-space/` — the universe, the solar system, the
  Earth: shape, size, movements, seasons, time (including
  the Moon and its phases).
- `population-transport-and-maps/` — people on the Earth:
  distribution, movement, transport, and reading maps
  (including colouring Suriname's map and flag, Argentina's
  population today, thematic maps of Mercosur integration, a
  day on the border, Iceland's administrative districts and
  voyaging and settling the Pacific islands, movement
  — people, goods and ideas, and Morocco's map, borders and
  cities, drawing a relief map of Morocco, Morocco's
  population distribution and Morocco's transport networks,
  no entry — borders and excluded spaces, stop dreaming
  of a car — energy and transport, Sweden on the map,
  living in Sweden — town and country, and kinds of houses).
- `people-and-resources/` — settlement, natural resources,
  economic activities, and looking after the land (including
  quality of life — Argentina and Latin America compared,
  Mercosur economic integration, kinds of integration, the
  South American gas pipeline database, India's growing
  economy, Moroccan agriculture and livestock, Morocco's two
  sea fronts and ports, Morocco's minerals and the
  phosphate industry, Brazil could be different — territory,
  and regional blocs — does union make strength?).
- `ethiopia-ancient-and-medieval/` — Ethiopia from the earliest
  times through the medieval kingdoms and sultanates.
- `africa-to-1500/` — Africa's peoples, states and trade to
  about 1500.
- `ancient-world/` — the ancient civilisations and the medieval
  world beyond Africa.
- `the-land-and-air/` — continents and oceans, the forces that
  shape the land, the atmosphere, weather and climate, map work
  (including the UAE's location and emirates map and desert
  wildlife of the UAE, Zambia's agro-ecological regions and
  climate and agro-ecological regions, reading a district
  boundary map of Iceland, India — landscape and monsoon
  climate, hot-spot islands, how coral atolls form and high
  islands and low islands of Micronesia, place — what
  makes a place, the Arabian Peninsula — location and
  land, Morocco's relief — mountains, plateaus and plains,
  Morocco's climate — temperature and rainfall, Morocco's
  rivers and dams, producing and consuming space, Sweden's
  nature, naming Sweden's plants and animals, warm and
  cold climates, and Sweden's climate — a long country).
- `culture-and-work/` — culture, economic activities, trade,
  and tax (including agriculture in Zambia's agro-ecological
  regions, festivals of the world's religions, sacred texts,
  food and clothing of religions, religion and caste in
  India, is industry over?, and shopping malls and
  consumption space).
- `resources-and-conflict/` — natural resources, their care,
  and conflict over resources (including environmental hazards
  and disasters, catastrophes — avoidable or inevitable?,
  agrochemicals — do you take poison?, Ukraine on the map,
  Russia on the map, and NATO countries in Europe 2022).
- `modern-ethiopia/` — Ethiopia from 1855 to today. (Student
  book alone — no teacher evidence yet; the Grade 8 teacher
  guide's Units 4–7 are still owed.)
- `africa-after-1500/` — Africa from 1500: trade, colonisation,
  resistance, independence. (No teacher evidence yet.)
- `modern-world/` — the early-modern and modern world. (No
  teacher evidence yet.)
- `global-issues/` — today's shared problems: population
  pressure, HIV/AIDS, climate, water, forests — and protecting
  the environment where you live (including India's
  environmental problems, environmental problems in
  Morocco, networks — we of the network, are humans
  rational — and the environment?, sorting and recycling
  waste, and choices that help the climate). (Grade 8 part: no teacher
  evidence yet.)
