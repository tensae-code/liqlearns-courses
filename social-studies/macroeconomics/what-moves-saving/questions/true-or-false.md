# What moves saving? — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Greater income inequality lowers total saving.
**Answer: False — The rich save a larger share, so concentrating income raises the total saved.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Direct taxes have no effect on saving.
**Answer: False — Taxes decide how much income remains — and saving comes out of what remains.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Two people on identical incomes always save identical amounts.
**Answer: False — Their own natures differ: the book's miser and spendthrift save oppositely on the same income.**
