# What moves saving? — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Income — level and distribution" in your own words.
**Answer:** Higher income raises saving, and greater inequality raises total saving because the rich save more.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Expectations, interest, wealth and tax" in your own words.
**Answer:** Expectations, the interest rate, wealth and direct taxes all steer saving.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "The saver's own nature" in your own words.
**Answer:** A person's own nature — miser against spendthrift — is itself a determinant of saving.
