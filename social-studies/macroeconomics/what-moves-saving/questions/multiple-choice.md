# What moves saving? — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Income — level and distribution"?
a) Higher income raises saving, and greater inequality raises total saving because the rich save more.  b) Expectations, the interest rate, wealth and direct taxes all steer saving.  c) A person's own nature — miser against spendthrift — is itself a determinant of saving.
**Answer: a) Higher income raises saving, and greater inequality raises total saving because the rich save more.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Expectations, interest, wealth and tax"?
a) A person's own nature — miser against spendthrift — is itself a determinant of saving.  b) Higher income raises saving, and greater inequality raises total saving because the rich save more.  c) Expectations, the interest rate, wealth and direct taxes all steer saving.
**Answer: c) Expectations, the interest rate, wealth and direct taxes all steer saving.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "The saver's own nature"?
a) Expectations, the interest rate, wealth and direct taxes all steer saving.  b) A person's own nature — miser against spendthrift — is itself a determinant of saving.  c) Higher income raises saving, and greater inequality raises total saving because the rich save more.
**Answer: b) A person's own nature — miser against spendthrift — is itself a determinant of saving.**
