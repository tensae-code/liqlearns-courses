# Video script — Waqf — the charitable endowment

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Iran's Grade 8 Social Studies book, in Lesson 2 "Cooperation (2)", teaches **waqf (وقف)** — an **old Iranian institution of giving**. Its **benefactors** built for everyone to use: **schools, bridges, caravanserais, water reservoirs and public baths**. The gift is not money handed out once; it is a thing built, or an asset set aside, whose use or income keeps serving people. The book gives the second form: a benefactor **directed the income of an asset — farmland, a production workshop — to the needy**. And it states the rule that makes waqf last: **endowed assets (موقوفات) may never be appropriated and must stay as dedicated forever**. Once a thing is made waqf, no one may take it back or turn it to private use; the dedication is permanent. The book brings the institution into the present. Today **more than 2,500 hospitals and clinics in Iran are waqf-funded**, and **university research is financed from waqf capital**. The centuries-old form still pays for care and knowledge: the endowment's income, not the endowment itself, is what gets spent.
