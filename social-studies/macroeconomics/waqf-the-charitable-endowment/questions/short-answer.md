# Waqf — the charitable endowment — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What did waqf benefactors build, and for whom?
**Answer:** Schools, bridges, caravanserais, water reservoirs and public baths — built for all to use.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What rule makes waqf last forever?
**Answer:** Endowed assets may never be appropriated and must stay as dedicated forever — no one may take a waqf asset back or turn it to private use.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** How does waqf pay for hospitals and research today — from the endowment itself, or from its income?
**Answer:** From its income. The endowed assets stay dedicated forever and are never spent or appropriated; their income funds more than 2,500 hospitals and clinics and university research.
