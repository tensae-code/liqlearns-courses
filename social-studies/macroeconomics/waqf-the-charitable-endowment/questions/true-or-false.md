# Waqf — the charitable endowment — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Benefactors under waqf built schools, bridges, caravanserais, water reservoirs and public baths for all to use.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** An endowed asset may be taken back by the benefactor's family when they need it.
**Answer: False — Endowed assets (موقوفات) may never be appropriated and must stay as dedicated forever.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Today more than 2,500 hospitals and clinics in Iran are waqf-funded.
**Answer: True**
