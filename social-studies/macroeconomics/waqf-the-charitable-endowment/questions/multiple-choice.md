# Waqf — the charitable endowment — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Waqf is —
a) a tax paid to the state  b) an old Iranian institution of giving, where an asset is dedicated to serve people forever  c) a loan from a bank
**Answer: b) an old Iranian institution of giving, where an asset is dedicated to serve people forever**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Under waqf, a benefactor could direct to the needy —
a) the income of an asset such as farmland or a production workshop  b) a tax bill  c) a bank loan
**Answer: b) the income of an asset such as farmland or a production workshop**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Waqf today in Iran funds —
a) only caravanserais  b) more than 2,500 hospitals and clinics, and university research  c) nothing — it ended centuries ago
**Answer: b) more than 2,500 hospitals and clinics, and university research**
