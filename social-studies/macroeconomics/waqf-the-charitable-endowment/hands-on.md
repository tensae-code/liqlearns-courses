# Hands-on — Waqf — the charitable endowment

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Design an endowment
Choose one asset — a field, a workshop, a building. Write its waqf deed in three lines: the asset, who its income serves, and the rule that it may never be taken back.

## Find the waqf around you
Name one school, clinic, bath, reservoir or place of worship near you that people use free. Say whether it could be a waqf, and what would prove it.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach sub lesson 2 aloud — income to the needy, assets dedicated forever — as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
