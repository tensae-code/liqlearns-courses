# The costs of the food security system

## Do it first

## Sub lesson 1 — Overflowing granaries and rotting grain

Granaries **overflow past buffer norms** and grain **rots** in storage — more stock than the system was built to hold.

## Sub lesson 2 — High carrying costs; MSP pulls land and water to rice and wheat

Holding the stock costs heavily — **high carrying costs** — and a **rising MSP pulls land and water into rice and wheat**, away from other crops.

## Sub lesson 3 — Ration-shop malpractices

At the last link, **ration-shop malpractices** — grain diverted or sold unfairly — keep part of the food from reaching the people it was bought for.
