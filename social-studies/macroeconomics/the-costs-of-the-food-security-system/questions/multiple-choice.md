# The costs of the food security system — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Overflowing stock leads to —
a) grain rotting in granaries  b) lower costs  c) empty stores
**Answer: a) grain rotting in granaries**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A rising MSP pulls land and water into —
a) rice and wheat  b) only vegetables  c) no crops
**Answer: a) rice and wheat**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Malpractices happen at —
a) ration shops  b) the FCI's farms  c) foreign ports
**Answer: a) ration shops**
