# The costs of the food security system — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Extra stock costs nothing to keep.
**Answer: False — carrying costs are high and grain rots.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Rising MSP spreads land evenly across all crops.
**Answer: False — it pulls land and water into rice and wheat.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Every ration shop always delivers grain exactly as intended.
**Answer: False — malpractices at ration shops are one of the system's strains.**
