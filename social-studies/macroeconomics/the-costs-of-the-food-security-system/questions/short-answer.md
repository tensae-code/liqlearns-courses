# The costs of the food security system — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What happens when stocks pass buffer norms?
**Answer:** Granaries overflow and grain rots.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name two strains of the system beside rotting grain.
**Answer:** High carrying costs, and rising MSP pulling land and water into rice and wheat.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What malpractice strains the system?
**Answer:** Ration-shop malpractices that divert grain.
