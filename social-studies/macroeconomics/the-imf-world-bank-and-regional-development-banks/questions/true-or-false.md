# The IMF, the World Bank and the regional development banks — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Nicaragua's Grade 10 book sets the state facing the international financial institutions one by one, starting with the IMF and the World Bank.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Next stands the Inter-American Development Bank (BID), the regional bank for the Americas, in the state's relations with the international organisms.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Closest to home stands something else entirely, Central America's own integration bank.
**Answer: False** — Closest to home stands the Central American Bank for Economic Integration (BCIE), Central America's own integration bank.
