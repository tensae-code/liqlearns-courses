# The IMF, the World Bank and the regional development banks — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Nicaragua's Grade 10 book sets the state facing the international financial institutions one by one, starting with _____.
**Answer: the IMF and the World Bank**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Next stands _____, the regional bank for the Americas, in the state's relations with the international organisms.
**Answer: the Inter-American Development Bank (BID)**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Closest to home stands _____, Central America's own integration bank.
**Answer: the Central American Bank for Economic Integration (BCIE)**
