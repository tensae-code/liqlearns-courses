# The IMF, the World Bank and the regional development banks — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the book teach about the imf and the world bank?
**Answer:** Nicaragua's Grade 10 book sets the state facing the international financial institutions one by one, starting with the IMF and the World Bank.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the book teach about the inter-american development bank (bid)?
**Answer:** Next stands the Inter-American Development Bank (BID), the regional bank for the Americas, in the state's relations with the international organisms.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the book teach about the central american bank (bcie)?
**Answer:** Closest to home stands the Central American Bank for Economic Integration (BCIE), Central America's own integration bank.
