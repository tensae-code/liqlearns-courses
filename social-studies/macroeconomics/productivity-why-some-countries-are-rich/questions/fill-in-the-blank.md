# Productivity — why some countries are rich — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The one-word explanation of living-standard gaps is _____ — output per worker.
**Answer: productivity**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Japan grew rich despite few natural resources, leaning on trade and _____ — the fourth determinant.
**Answer: technological knowledge**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Kuznets lists structural transformation with a rising _____ class among growth's five characteristics.
**Answer: entrepreneurial**
