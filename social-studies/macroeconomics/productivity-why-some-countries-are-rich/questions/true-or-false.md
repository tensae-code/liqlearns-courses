# Productivity — why some countries are rich — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Productivity — output per worker — explains why living standards differ so widely between countries.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The four determinants of productivity are physical capital, human capital, natural resources and technological knowledge, each per worker.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Kuznets's characteristics include structural transformation with a rising entrepreneurial class.
**Answer: True**
