# Productivity — why some countries are rich — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Productivity — the one-word explanation"?
a) output per worker explaining gaps of over ten times between countries  b) physical capital, human capital, natural resources and technological knowledge per worker  c) five characteristics from rising income to applied science and structural transformation
**Answer: a) output per worker explaining gaps of over ten times between countries**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "The four determinants"?
a) output per worker explaining gaps of over ten times between countries  b) five characteristics from rising income to applied science and structural transformation  c) physical capital, human capital, natural resources and technological knowledge per worker
**Answer: c) physical capital, human capital, natural resources and technological knowledge per worker**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Kuznets's five characteristics"?
a) physical capital, human capital, natural resources and technological knowledge per worker  b) five characteristics from rising income to applied science and structural transformation  c) output per worker explaining gaps of over ten times between countries
**Answer: b) five characteristics from rising income to applied science and structural transformation**
