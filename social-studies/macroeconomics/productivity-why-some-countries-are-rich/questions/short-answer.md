# Productivity — why some countries are rich — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Productivity — the one-word explanation" in your own words.
**Answer:** Productivity — output per worker — explains why living standards differ so widely between countries.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "The four determinants" in your own words.
**Answer:** The four determinants of productivity are physical capital, human capital, natural resources and technological knowledge, each per worker.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Kuznets's five characteristics" in your own words.
**Answer:** Kuznets's characteristics include structural transformation with a rising entrepreneurial class.
