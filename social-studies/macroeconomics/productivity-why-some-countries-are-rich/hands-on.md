# Hands-on — Productivity — why some countries are rich

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Two fishing stations: hands only, then a string net. Time each for one minute counting paper fish, compute both productivities, and name which determinant of productivity the net represents.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — Crusoe with a net versus Crusoe with a line, and four pillars holding up a living-standards roof — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
