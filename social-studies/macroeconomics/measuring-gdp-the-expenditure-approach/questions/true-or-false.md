# Measuring GDP — the expenditure approach — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Counting both an intermediate good and the final good made from it would double count its value.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Household consumption includes durables lasting over three years, non-durables and services; business investment includes inventory change.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Net exports are exports minus imports, and GDP by expenditure is C + I + G + NX.
**Answer: True**
