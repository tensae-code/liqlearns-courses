# Measuring GDP — the expenditure approach — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Final goods only — the double-counting warning" in your own words.
**Answer:** Counting both an intermediate good and the final good made from it would double count its value.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Consumption and investment" in your own words.
**Answer:** Household consumption includes durables lasting over three years, non-durables and services; business investment includes inventory change.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Government purchases and net exports" in your own words.
**Answer:** Net exports are exports minus imports, and GDP by expenditure is C + I + G + NX.
