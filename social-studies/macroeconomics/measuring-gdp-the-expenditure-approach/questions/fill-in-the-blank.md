# Measuring GDP — the expenditure approach — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Counting flour and the bread made from it counts the flour twice — that error is _____.
**Answer: double counting**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Business investment includes the change in _____ — goods produced but not yet sold.
**Answer: inventories**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** GDP by the expenditure approach is C + I + G + _____, where the last term is exports minus imports.
**Answer: net exports**
