# Hands-on — Measuring GDP — the expenditure approach

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Four learners hold stream cards C, I, G and NX, pouring counters into one 'GDP' bowl. A fifth holds 'intermediate goods' counters that the class must refuse — with the double-counting reason spoken aloud.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — four labelled streams — households, firms, government, foreigners — flowing into one GDP tank — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
