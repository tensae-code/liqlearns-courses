# The GDP deflator — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The GDP deflator is nominal GDP divided by real GDP, times 100.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A deflator of about 127 means prices are some 27% above the base year's level.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Dividing nominal GDP by the deflator converts it back into real GDP.
**Answer: True**
