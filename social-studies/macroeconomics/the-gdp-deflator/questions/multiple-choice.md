# The GDP deflator — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "The formula"?
a) deflator = nominal GDP / real GDP x 100; 100 in the base year  b) 950 / 750 x 100 = about 127 — prices about 27% above the base year  c) real GDP = nominal GDP divided by the deflator, times 100
**Answer: a) deflator = nominal GDP / real GDP x 100; 100 in the base year**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "The worked example — 127"?
a) deflator = nominal GDP / real GDP x 100; 100 in the base year  b) real GDP = nominal GDP divided by the deflator, times 100  c) 950 / 750 x 100 = about 127 — prices about 27% above the base year
**Answer: c) 950 / 750 x 100 = about 127 — prices about 27% above the base year**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Converting back to real"?
a) 950 / 750 x 100 = about 127 — prices about 27% above the base year  b) real GDP = nominal GDP divided by the deflator, times 100  c) deflator = nominal GDP / real GDP x 100; 100 in the base year
**Answer: b) real GDP = nominal GDP divided by the deflator, times 100**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Nominal GDP is 950 million and real GDP is 750 million. What is the GDP deflator (to the nearest whole number)?
a) 127  b) 79  c) 100
**Answer: a) 127** — Deflator = 950 / 750 × 100 = 126.7, about 127.
