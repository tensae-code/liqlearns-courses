# The GDP deflator — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The deflator is nominal GDP divided by _____, times 100.
**Answer: real GDP**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Nominal 950 over real 750 gives a deflator of about _____ — prices some 27% up on the base year.
**Answer: 127**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** To strip price rises out of a nominal total, divide it by the _____.
**Answer: deflator**
