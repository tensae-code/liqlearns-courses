# The GDP deflator — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "The formula" in your own words.
**Answer:** The GDP deflator is nominal GDP divided by real GDP, times 100.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "The worked example — 127" in your own words.
**Answer:** A deflator of about 127 means prices are some 27% above the base year's level.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Converting back to real" in your own words.
**Answer:** Dividing nominal GDP by the deflator converts it back into real GDP.
