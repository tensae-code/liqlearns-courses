# Hands-on — The GDP deflator

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Stack 950 counters labelled 'at today's prices' beside 750 labelled 'at base prices'. The extra 200 counters are pure price rise — divide and announce the deflator to the class.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — a fraction bar — nominal GDP over real GDP — with the 127 worked result beside it — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
