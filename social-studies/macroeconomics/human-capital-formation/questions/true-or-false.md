# Human capital formation — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Grade 6 book treats trained people as a resource the country holds.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Human capital returns the same whether trained people find matching work or not.
**Answer: False — training returns most when skills meet the jobs that need them; left unused it returns nothing.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Minimising risk is one of the spread's two rules for human capital.
**Answer: True**
