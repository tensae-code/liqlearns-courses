# Human capital formation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Human capital is —
a) money held in a bank  b) the trained capability carried in a country's people  c) a kind of container ship
**Answer: b) the trained capability carried in a country's people**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The spread's first rule is to — the investment from human capital.
a) maximise  b) hide  c) halve
**Answer: a) maximise**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The spread's second rule is to — the economic and social risk.
a) increase  b) ignore  c) minimise
**Answer: c) minimise**
