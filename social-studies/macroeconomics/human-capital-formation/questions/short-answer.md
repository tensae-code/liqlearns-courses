# Human capital formation — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is human capital, and why is it called an investment?
**Answer:** Human capital is the capability carried in people — what trained workers know and can do. It is an investment because it costs time and teaching to form and returns work and income for years after.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** State the spread's two rules for human capital.
**Answer:** Maximise the investment from human capital, and minimise the economic and social risk around it.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Give one economic risk and one social risk of wasted human capital.
**Answer:** Economically, training that finds no matching work is an investment lost. Socially, people whose skills cannot find a place carry the loss into their households and communities.
