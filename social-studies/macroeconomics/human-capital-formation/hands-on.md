# Hands-on — Human capital formation

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Take two piles of counters. In the first pile, put one counter for each skill someone in your home has been trained in. Say out loud what the family gets from that pile — that is the investment human capital returns.

## Draw it and check it
Draw a balance: on one side write "investment from human capital — maximised", on the other "economic and social risk — minimised". Under each side write one example. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
