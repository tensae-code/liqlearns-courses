# Video script — Human capital formation

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The Bahamas' Grade 6 book files people under conservation, next to the natural resources. That is the first idea of this lesson: trained people are a stock the country holds — human capital.

The book's rule for that stock has two halves. Maximise the investment you get from human capital — let trained people do the most their training makes possible. And minimise the economic and social risk around it — the losses that come when training is wasted or workers cannot find the work their skills match. Form the capital, use it fully, guard it carefully.
