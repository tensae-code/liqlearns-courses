# The informal economy and types of employment

## Do it first

Think of this topic the way the Nicaragua book opens it — The informal economy and types of employment. What do you already know about it before you read on?

## Sub lesson 1 — Full-time and seasonal farm work

Nicaragua's Grade 7 book teaches kinds of employment, starting with full-time work and seasonal farm work — the coffee pickers who work only in the harvest season.

## Sub lesson 2 — Wage work — state and private

Other Nicaraguans work for wages, in state and private-sector wage work — a salary paid by an employer, public or private.

## Sub lesson 3 — The informal sector

Much of the population works in the informal sector — own-account street vendors and services, working for themselves outside formal wage employment.
