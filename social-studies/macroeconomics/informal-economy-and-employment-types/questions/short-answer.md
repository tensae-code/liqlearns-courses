# The informal economy and types of employment — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the book teach about full-time and seasonal farm work?
**Answer:** Nicaragua's Grade 7 book teaches kinds of employment, starting with full-time work and seasonal farm work — the coffee pickers who work only in the harvest season.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the book teach about wage work — state and private?
**Answer:** Other Nicaraguans work for wages, in state and private-sector wage work — a salary paid by an employer, public or private.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the book teach about the informal sector?
**Answer:** Much of the population works in the informal sector — own-account street vendors and services, working for themselves outside formal wage employment.
