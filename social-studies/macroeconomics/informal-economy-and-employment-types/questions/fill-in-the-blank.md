# The informal economy and types of employment — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Nicaragua's Grade 7 book teaches kinds of employment, starting with full-time work and _____ — the coffee pickers who work only in the harvest season.
**Answer: seasonal farm work**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Other Nicaraguans work for wages, in _____ — a salary paid by an employer, public or private.
**Answer: state and private-sector wage work**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Much of the population works in _____ — own-account street vendors and services, working for themselves outside formal wage employment.
**Answer: the informal sector**
