# The informal economy and types of employment — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Nicaragua's Grade 7 book teaches kinds of employment, starting with full-time work and seasonal farm work — the coffee pickers who work only in the harvest season.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Other Nicaraguans work for wages, in state and private-sector wage work — a salary paid by an employer, public or private.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Much of the population works in something else entirely — own-account street vendors and services, working for themselves outside formal wage employment.
**Answer: False** — Much of the population works in the informal sector — own-account street vendors and services, working for themselves outside formal wage employment.
