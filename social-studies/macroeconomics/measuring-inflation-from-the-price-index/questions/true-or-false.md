# Measuring inflation from the price index — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The inflation rate is calculated from the consumer price index (IHK): how much the index has risen over a period, as a percentage.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** To measure inflation, compare this period's index with the earlier period's index — the rise divided by the earlier index, times 100.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The result is the inflation rate for that period — the something else entirely by which the general price level rose.
**Answer: False** — The result is the inflation rate for that period — the percentage by which the general price level rose.
