# Measuring inflation from the price index — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the book teach about from index to rate?
**Answer:** The inflation rate is calculated from the consumer price index (IHK): how much the index has risen over a period, as a percentage.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the book teach about the calculation?
**Answer:** To measure inflation, compare this period's index with the earlier period's index — the rise divided by the earlier index, times 100.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the book teach about reading the result?
**Answer:** The result is the inflation rate for that period — the percentage by which the general price level rose.
