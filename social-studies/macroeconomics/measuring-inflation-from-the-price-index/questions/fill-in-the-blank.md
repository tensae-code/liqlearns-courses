# Measuring inflation from the price index — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The inflation rate is calculated from the _____: how much the index has risen over a period, as a percentage.
**Answer: consumer price index (IHK)**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** To measure inflation, compare this period's index with the earlier period's index — the rise divided by the earlier index, _____.
**Answer: times 100**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The result is the inflation rate for that period — the _____ by which the general price level rose.
**Answer: percentage**
