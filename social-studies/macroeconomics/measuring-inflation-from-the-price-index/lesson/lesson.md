# Measuring inflation from the price index

## Do it first

Think of this topic the way the Indonesia book opens it — Measuring inflation from the price index. What do you already know about it before you read on?

## Sub lesson 1 — From index to rate

The inflation rate is calculated from the consumer price index (IHK): how much the index has risen over a period, as a percentage.

## Sub lesson 2 — The calculation

To measure inflation, compare this period's index with the earlier period's index — the rise divided by the earlier index, times 100.

## Sub lesson 3 — Reading the result

The result is the inflation rate for that period — the percentage by which the general price level rose.
