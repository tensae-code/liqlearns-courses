# Regional integration — gains and costs — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "What integration is" in your own words.
**Answer:** Regional integration is countries cooperating by cutting trade barriers and coordinating policies.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Three gains" in your own words.
**Answer:** Integration's gains: trade benefits, employment benefits and political cooperation.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Three costs" in your own words.
**Answer:** The three costs are trade diversion, job losses from relocation, and erosion of sovereignty.
