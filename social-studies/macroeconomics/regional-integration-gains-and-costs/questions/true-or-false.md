# Regional integration — gains and costs — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Regional integration means one country ruling its neighbours.
**Answer: False — It is an agreement to cooperate — cutting barriers and coordinating policies for peace, stability and wealth.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Political cooperation is listed as a cost of integration.
**Answer: False — Cooperation is the third GAIN. The costs are diversion, job losses and lost sovereignty.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Trade diversion means buying from a cheaper outsider.
**Answer: False — Diversion is the reverse: buying from a DEARER member because the outsider now faces the barrier.**
