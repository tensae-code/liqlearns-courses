# Regional integration — gains and costs — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "What integration is"?
a) Regional integration is countries cooperating by cutting trade barriers and coordinating policies.  b) Integration's gains: trade benefits, employment benefits and political cooperation.  c) The three costs are trade diversion, job losses from relocation, and erosion of sovereignty.
**Answer: a) Regional integration is countries cooperating by cutting trade barriers and coordinating policies.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Three gains"?
a) The three costs are trade diversion, job losses from relocation, and erosion of sovereignty.  b) Regional integration is countries cooperating by cutting trade barriers and coordinating policies.  c) Integration's gains: trade benefits, employment benefits and political cooperation.
**Answer: c) Integration's gains: trade benefits, employment benefits and political cooperation.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Three costs"?
a) Integration's gains: trade benefits, employment benefits and political cooperation.  b) The three costs are trade diversion, job losses from relocation, and erosion of sovereignty.  c) Regional integration is countries cooperating by cutting trade barriers and coordinating policies.
**Answer: b) The three costs are trade diversion, job losses from relocation, and erosion of sovereignty.**
