# Regional integration — gains and costs

## Do it first

Neighbouring countries agree to trade freely and coordinate their policies. List what each side gains and what each side risks before the lesson counts three of each.

## Sub lesson 1 — What integration is

Regional (economic) integration is an agreement among countries to cooperate for peace, stability and wealth by cutting trade barriers and coordinating policies — going further together than any member goes alone.

[Picture 24929 — neighbouring countries lowering the fences between them, three benefit suns and three cost clouds above]

## Sub lesson 2 — Three gains

Its three broad gains are trade benefits (cheaper trade, wider availability, stronger purchasing power), employment benefits (market expansion, technology sharing, cross-border investment), and political cooperation between the members.

## Sub lesson 3 — Three costs

Its three costs: trade diversion — buying from a dearer member instead of a cheaper outsider; job losses as firms relocate to cheaper members; and erosion of national sovereignty, as members accept the union's common rules.
