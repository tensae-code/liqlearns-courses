# Problems of banking in Nigeria — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What do the problems of banking in Nigeria limit?
**Answer:** Banks in Nigeria face problems that limit how well they can serve savers and borrowers.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What holds Nigerian banking back?
**Answer:** Low banking habits, cash held outside banks and sparse branches in rural areas hold Nigerian banking back.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Why do banking problems matter?
**Answer:** When banking is weak, savings are not gathered and credit does not reach the firms that need it.
