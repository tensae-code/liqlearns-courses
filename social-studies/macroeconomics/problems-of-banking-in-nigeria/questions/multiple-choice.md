# Problems of banking in Nigeria — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** About the problems banks face in nigeria — the lesson teaches that —
a) banks in Nigeria face problems that limit how well they can serve savers and borrowers.  b) low banking habits, cash held outside banks and sparse branches in rural areas hold Nigerian banking back.  c) when banking is weak, savings are not gathered and credit does not reach the firms that need it.
