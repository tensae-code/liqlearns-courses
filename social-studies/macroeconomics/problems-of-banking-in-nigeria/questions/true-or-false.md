# Problems of banking in Nigeria — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Banks in Nigeria face no problems at all.
**Answer: False** — Banks in Nigeria face problems that limit how well they can serve savers and borrowers.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Most Nigerian savings are already held inside banks.
**Answer: False** — Low banking habits, cash held outside banks and sparse branches in rural areas hold Nigerian banking back.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Weak banking makes credit reach every firm.
**Answer: False** — When banking is weak, savings are not gathered and credit does not reach the firms that need it.
