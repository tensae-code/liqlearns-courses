# Problems of banking in Nigeria

## Do it first

## Sub lesson 1 — The problems banks face in Nigeria

Banks in Nigeria face problems that limit how well they can serve savers and borrowers.

## Sub lesson 2 — Problems of trust, cash and reach

Low banking habits, cash held outside banks and sparse branches in rural areas hold Nigerian banking back.

## Sub lesson 3 — Why the problems matter

When banking is weak, savings are not gathered and credit does not reach the firms that need it.
