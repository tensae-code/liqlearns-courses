# Oligopolies and other reasons prices rise — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Prices also rise because companies that control markets — called oligopolies — fix prices solely by their profit interest
**Answer: True** — this is what Colombia's book teaches in this part.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Sub lesson 2 is mainly about prices also rise because companies that control markets.
**Answer: False** — sub lesson 2 is about and for political reasons when the state raises; prices also rise because companies that control markets is sub lesson 1.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** And for political reasons when the state raises the price of a product it controls
**Answer: True** — this is what Colombia's book teaches in this part.
