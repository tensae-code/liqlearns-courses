# Oligopolies and other reasons prices rise — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the lesson teach about prices also rise because companies that control markets?
**Answer:** Prices also rise because companies that control markets — called oligopolies — fix prices solely by their profit interest

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the lesson teach about and for political reasons when the state raises?
**Answer:** And for political reasons when the state raises the price of a product it controls

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the lesson teach about and for political reasons when the state raises — more?
**Answer:** And for political reasons when the state raises the price of a product it controls
