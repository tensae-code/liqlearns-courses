# How economic policies work together — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why can no policy be judged alone?
**Answer: Because a move in one policy changes what the others do.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the lesson trace, policy by policy?
**Answer: The relations between the policies — the wires, not only the switches.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What must pupils see before coordination makes sense?
**Answer: The relations between the policies.**
