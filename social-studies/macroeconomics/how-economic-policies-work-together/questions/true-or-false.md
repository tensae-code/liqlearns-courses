# How economic policies work together — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The policies touch and change each other.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** State spending, in this lesson, affects nothing outside the budget book.
**Answer: False — State spending does affect money and credit — the policies are wired together.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A policy breaking another's work has not succeeded, however well it hit its own target.
**Answer: True**
