# How economic policies work together — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The lesson's central claim is —
a) each policy works alone with no effects on others  b) the separate policies touch and change each other  c) a country needs only one policy ever
**Answer: b) the separate policies touch and change each other**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** State spending, in this lesson, affects —
a) the money and credit the central bank manages  b) only the spending ministry's own office  c) nothing outside the budget book
**Answer: a) the money and credit the central bank manages**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A policy that hits its own target but breaks another policy's work —
a) cannot be judged at all  b) has succeeded completely  c) has not succeeded
**Answer: c) has not succeeded**
