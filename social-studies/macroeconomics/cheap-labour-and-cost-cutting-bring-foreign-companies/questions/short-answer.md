# Cheap labour and cost-cutting bring foreign companies — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why do foreign companies come?
**Answer:** For cheap labour, longer hours and lower safety spending — costs fall and profits rise.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why do workers accept unsafe work?
**Answer:** Heavy unemployment makes them easily replaced.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which case teaches the human price of cost-cutting?
**Answer:** Union Carbide at Bhopal.
