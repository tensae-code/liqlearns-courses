# Cheap labour and cost-cutting bring foreign companies — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Foreign companies come to pay the highest wages.
**Answer: False — they come for cheap labour and lower costs.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Workers can easily refuse unsafe work when jobs are scarce.
**Answer: False — they accept it because they are easily replaced.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Bhopal is presented as a success of cost-cutting.
**Answer: False — it shows the human price of cutting safety costs.**
