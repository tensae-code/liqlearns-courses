# Cheap labour and cost-cutting bring foreign companies — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Foreign companies are drawn by —
a) cheap labour, longer hours and lower safety spending  b) high wages  c) strict safety everywhere
**Answer: a) cheap labour, longer hours and lower safety spending**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Heavy unemployment means a worker is —
a) easily replaced  b) impossible to replace  c) always promoted
**Answer: a) easily replaced**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The Bhopal case involves —
a) Union Carbide  b) a textile co-operative  c) the FCI
**Answer: a) Union Carbide**
