# Video script — Cheap labour and cost-cutting bring foreign companies

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Cheap labour and cost-cutting bring foreign companies — as India's book teaches it, says the narrator: foreign companies come for cheap labour, longer hours and lower safety spending, so costs fall and profits rise; with heavy unemployment a worker is easily replaced and accepts unsafe work — taught through Union Carbide at Bhopal.
