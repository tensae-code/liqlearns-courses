# Cheap labour and cost-cutting bring foreign companies

## Do it first

Before you read on, write what you already think this lesson's title means — cheap labour and cost-cutting bring foreign companies — in one sentence of your own. Keep it; you will check it against the lesson at the end.

## Sub lesson 1 — Foreign companies come for cheap labour

Foreign companies come where **labour is cheap, hours are longer and safety spending is lower** — so **costs fall and profits rise**.

## Sub lesson 2 — Heavy unemployment makes workers easily replaced

With **heavy unemployment**, a worker is **easily replaced** and so **accepts unsafe work** rather than lose the job.

## Sub lesson 3 — Bhopal — Union Carbide and the price of cheapness

The lesson is taught through **Union Carbide at Bhopal** — the disaster that showed the human price when safety is cut to keep costs low.
