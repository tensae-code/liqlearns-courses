# Hands-on — Inflation — a general, lasting rise in prices

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Price-line drawing: learners plot a jagged rising line across a wall chart, marking a one-day bump and crossing it out as 'not inflation', then name three causes from the lesson that could push the whole line.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — a price-level line climbing steadily, with a small one-day bump crossed out as not inflation — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
