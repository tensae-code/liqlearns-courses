# Inflation — a general, lasting rise in prices — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "The definition, word by word" in your own words.
**Answer:** Inflation is a sustained, persistent and appreciable increase in the general price level, measured by a price index.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Deflation — the opposite" in your own words.
**Answer:** Deflation is a general fall in prices.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "What causes it" in your own words.
**Answer:** Causes of inflation include growth of the money supply, rising input costs, imported inflation, a weaker exchange rate and declining productivity.
