# Inflation — a general, lasting rise in prices — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "The definition, word by word"?
a) a sustained, persistent, appreciable increase in the general price level  b) a general fall in prices  c) money-supply growth, input costs, imported inflation, weaker exchange rate, declining productivity
**Answer: a) a sustained, persistent, appreciable increase in the general price level**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Deflation — the opposite"?
a) a sustained, persistent, appreciable increase in the general price level  b) money-supply growth, input costs, imported inflation, weaker exchange rate, declining productivity  c) a general fall in prices
**Answer: c) a general fall in prices**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "What causes it"?
a) a general fall in prices  b) money-supply growth, input costs, imported inflation, weaker exchange rate, declining productivity  c) a sustained, persistent, appreciable increase in the general price level
**Answer: b) money-supply growth, input costs, imported inflation, weaker exchange rate, declining productivity**
