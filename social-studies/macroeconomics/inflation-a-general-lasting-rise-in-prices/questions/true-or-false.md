# Inflation — a general, lasting rise in prices — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Inflation is a sustained, persistent and appreciable increase in the general price level, measured by a price index.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Inflation is a sustained, persistent and appreciable increase in the general price level, measured by a price index.
**Answer: False** — that statement belongs to sub lesson 1 (The definition, word by word); sub lesson 2 says: Deflation is a general fall in prices.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Causes of inflation include growth of the money supply, rising input costs, imported inflation, a weaker exchange rate and declining productivity.
**Answer: True**
