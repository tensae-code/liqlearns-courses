# Inflation — a general, lasting rise in prices — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Inflation is a sustained increase in the _____, measured by a price index.
**Answer: general price level**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Deflation is a general _____ in prices.
**Answer: fall**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Listed causes of inflation include growth of the _____ and rising input costs.
**Answer: money supply**
