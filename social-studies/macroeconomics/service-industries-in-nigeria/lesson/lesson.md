## Sub lesson 1 — The service industry and its types

Service industries provide services rather than goods — trade, transport, banking, education, health and more.

## Sub lesson 2 — Services in Nigeria

Nigeria's services range from markets and transport to banks and telecommunications.

## Sub lesson 3 — Their contribution to development

Services contribute a large and growing share of Nigeria's output and employment.
