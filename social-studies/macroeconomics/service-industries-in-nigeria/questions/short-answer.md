# Service industries in Nigeria — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What do service industries provide?
**Answer:** Service industries provide services rather than goods — trade, transport, banking, education, health and more.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What services does Nigeria have?
**Answer:** Nigeria's services range from markets and transport to banks and telecommunications.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What do services contribute to Nigeria?
**Answer:** Services contribute a large and growing share of Nigeria's output and employment.
