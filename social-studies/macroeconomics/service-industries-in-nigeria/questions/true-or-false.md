# Service industries in Nigeria — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Service industries provide goods, never services.
**Answer: False** — Service industries provide services rather than goods — trade, transport, banking, education, health and more.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Nigeria has no banking or telecommunications services.
**Answer: False** — Nigeria's services range from markets and transport to banks and telecommunications.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Services contribute a shrinking share of Nigeria's output.
**Answer: False** — Services contribute a large and growing share of Nigeria's output and employment.
