# Service industries in Nigeria — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Service industries provide _____ rather than goods.
**Answer: services**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Services contribute a large and growing share of output and _____.
