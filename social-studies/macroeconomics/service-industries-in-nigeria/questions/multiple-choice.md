# Service industries in Nigeria — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** About the service industry and its types — the lesson teaches that —
a) service industries provide services rather than goods — trade, transport, banking, education, health and more.  b) nigeria's services range from markets and transport to banks and telecommunications.  c) services contribute a large and growing share of Nigeria's output and employment.
