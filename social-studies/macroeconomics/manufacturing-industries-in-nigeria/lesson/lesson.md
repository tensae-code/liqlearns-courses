## Sub lesson 1 — The nature of Nigeria's manufacturing

Manufacturing turns raw materials into finished goods, and Nigeria's manufacturing industries have their own nature and make-up.

## Sub lesson 2 — The structure of manufacturing in Nigeria

Nigeria's manufacturing runs from small workshops to large factories making food, textiles, cement and more.

## Sub lesson 3 — Manufacturing's place in the economy

Manufacturing adds value, jobs and goods that would otherwise have to be imported.
