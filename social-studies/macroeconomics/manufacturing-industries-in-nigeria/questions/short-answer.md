# Manufacturing industries in Nigeria — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does manufacturing do?
**Answer:** Manufacturing turns raw materials into finished goods, and Nigeria's manufacturing industries have their own nature and make-up.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How is Nigeria's manufacturing structured?
**Answer:** Nigeria's manufacturing runs from small workshops to large factories making food, textiles, cement and more.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does manufacturing add to the economy?
**Answer:** Manufacturing adds value, jobs and goods that would otherwise have to be imported.
