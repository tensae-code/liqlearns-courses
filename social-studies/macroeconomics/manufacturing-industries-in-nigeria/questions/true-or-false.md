# Manufacturing industries in Nigeria — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Manufacturing turns finished goods back into raw materials.
**Answer: False** — Manufacturing turns raw materials into finished goods, and Nigeria's manufacturing industries have their own nature and make-up.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Nigeria has no factories at all.
**Answer: False** — Nigeria's manufacturing runs from small workshops to large factories making food, textiles, cement and more.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Manufacturing removes jobs from the economy.
**Answer: False** — Manufacturing adds value, jobs and goods that would otherwise have to be imported.
