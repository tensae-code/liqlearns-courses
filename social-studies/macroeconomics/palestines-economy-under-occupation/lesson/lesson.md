# Palestine's economy under occupation

## Do it first

Before you read on, write down what you already think this lesson's question is — how, since 1967, the occupation has weakened the palestinian economy and kept it dependent — in one sentence of your own. Keep it; you will check it at the end.

## Sub lesson 1 — Land seized, resources plundered

Palestine's Grade 9 books end the colonial-economics lesson **inside Palestine**. Since **1967** the occupation has weakened the Palestinian economy, first on the ground itself: **seizing land — the main means of production** — and **plundering natural resources**. An economy whose main means of production is taken from it starts every other question weakened.

## Sub lesson 2 — Ties severed — borders, crossings, customs and flooded markets

Next, its ties to the world are cut: the occupation **severs Palestine's external economic ties through control of borders, crossings and customs**, and **floods its markets with goods**. What may enter, what may leave, and on what terms, are decided outside the economy itself — the same dependence the colonial lesson names, applied at home.

## Sub lesson 3 — Utilities, industry and the Gaza blockade

The Grade 9 Part 2 lesson, asking why Palestine's economic activities are weak, completes the list: the occupation **controls water, electricity, energy and communications**, **confiscates farmland and water sources, blocks raw-material entry, obstructs industrial exports, bombs industrial plants, cuts electricity, blockades Gaza's coast, and dominates the international crossings**, hindering imports and exports. Weakness here is taught not as a natural fact but as the result of named controls.
