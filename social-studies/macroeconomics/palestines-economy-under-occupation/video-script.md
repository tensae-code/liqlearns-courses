# Video script — Palestine's economy under occupation

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Why is Palestine's economy weak? Its own Grade 9 books answer with named controls, not fate. Since 1967: land — the main means of production — seized, natural resources plundered. Borders, crossings and customs controlled, markets flooded. Water, electricity, energy and communications controlled, raw materials blocked, industrial exports obstructed, Gaza's coast blockaded. Dependence, taught at home.
