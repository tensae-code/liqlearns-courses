# Hands-on — Palestine's economy under occupation

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Draw a small farm, a factory and a port on paper. For each control in the lesson — land seized, crossings controlled, water and electricity controlled, raw materials blocked — lay a strip of paper across the drawing it stops, and say its name aloud.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the three sub lessons as three rings around a map of Palestine — land, gateways, utilities — with every named control inside its ring. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare, and fix every control you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
