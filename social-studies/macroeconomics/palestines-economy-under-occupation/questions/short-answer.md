# Palestine's economy under occupation — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why does seizing land weaken the whole economy?
**Answer:** Because land is the main means of production — taking it weakens every activity that rests on it.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What three gateways does the occupation control?
**Answer:** Borders, crossings and customs.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Give two ways industry itself is obstructed.
**Answer:** Blocking raw-material entry and obstructing industrial exports (also: bombing industrial plants, cutting electricity).
