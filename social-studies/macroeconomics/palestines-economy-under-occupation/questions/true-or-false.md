# Palestine's economy under occupation — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Land is named as a minor detail, not the main means of production.
**Answer: False — land is named as the main means of production, and it is seized**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Palestine controls its own borders, crossings and customs freely.
**Answer: False — the occupation controls them, severing its external economic ties**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The blockade of Gaza's coast is named in the lesson.
**Answer: True**
