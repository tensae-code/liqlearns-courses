# Palestine's economy under occupation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Since when, and by seizing what, has the occupation weakened the economy?
a) since 1967, by seizing land — the main means of production  b) since 2000, by seizing libraries  c) it has not seized land
**Answer: a) since 1967, by seizing land — the main means of production**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How are Palestine's external ties severed?
a) through control of borders, crossings and customs, while its markets are flooded with goods  b) by free-trade agreements  c) they are not severed
**Answer: a) through control of borders, crossings and customs, while its markets are flooded with goods**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which utilities and pressures complete the Part 2 list?
a) control of water, electricity, energy and communications; blocked raw materials and exports; the Gaza coast blockade  b) none  c) only postal services
**Answer: a) control of water, electricity, energy and communications; blocked raw materials and exports; the Gaza coast blockade**
