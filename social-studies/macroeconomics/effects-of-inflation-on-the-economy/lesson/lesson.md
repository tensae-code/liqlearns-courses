# Effects of inflation on the economy

## Do it first

Think of this topic the way the Uzbekistan Grade 9 book opens it — Topic 23, Consequences of Inflation. What do you already know about it before you read on?

## Sub lesson 1 — Who inflation hurts — budget-paid employees, pensioners and savers

The book names who inflation hurts: **budget-paid employees, pensioners and savers** — people whose money incomes and savings lose value as prices rise.

## Sub lesson 2 — Four citizens and 1,000,000 sum — judged against the real chart

Four citizens each put **1,000,000 sum** into a **bank deposit at 40%**, **jewellery**, **foreign currency** or **brick-factory shares**. Their results are judged against Uzbekistan's real inflation chart **1995–2004 (371% falling to 3.7%)**.

## Sub lesson 3 — The extreme cases — Hungary 1946 and Germany 1923

As the extreme cases of what inflation can do, the book names **Hungary 1946** and **Germany 1923**.
