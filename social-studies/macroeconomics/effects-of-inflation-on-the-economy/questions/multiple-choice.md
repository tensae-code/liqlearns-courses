# Effects of inflation on the economy — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Inflation hurts —
a) budget-paid employees, pensioners and savers  b) only shopkeepers  c) no one
**Answer: a) budget-paid employees, pensioners and savers**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Each of the four citizens put in —
a) 1,000,000 sum  b) 100,000 sum  c) 10,000 sum
**Answer: a) 1,000,000 sum**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Uzbekistan's inflation fell from 371% to —
a) 3.7%  b) 40%  c) 100%
**Answer: a) 3.7%**
