# Effects of inflation on the economy — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the three groups the book says inflation hurts.
**Answer:** Budget-paid employees, pensioners and savers.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What four things did the citizens put their money into?
**Answer:** A bank deposit at 40%, jewellery, foreign currency and brick-factory shares.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which two extreme cases does the book name?
**Answer:** Hungary 1946 and Germany 1923.
