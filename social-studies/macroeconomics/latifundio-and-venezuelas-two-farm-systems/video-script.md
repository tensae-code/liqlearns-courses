# Video script — Latifundio and Venezuela's two farm systems

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

In the 1920s, when the oil economy took over from the agricultural economy.

The traditional system (the colonisers' latifundio and the indigenous conuco) and the more productive modern system.

Any three of: traditional tools; large extensions of unused land; soil-exhausting monoculture; land concentrated in few hands; landless peasants on low wages handing half the harvest to the owner.
