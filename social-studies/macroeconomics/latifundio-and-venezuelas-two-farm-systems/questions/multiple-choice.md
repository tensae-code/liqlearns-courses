# Latifundio and Venezuela's two farm systems — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Agricultural production in the Grade 5 book covers —
a) farming, livestock, fishing and timber  b) only oil drilling  c) only factory work
**Answer: a) farming, livestock, fishing and timber**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Venezuela's traditional farm system joins —
a) the colonisers' latifundio and the indigenous conuco  b) only state farms  c) only city gardens
**Answer: a) the colonisers' latifundio and the indigenous conuco**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The book accuses the latifundio of —
a) stagnating production with unused land and soil-exhausting monoculture  b) producing too much food  c) paying peasants too well
**Answer: a) stagnating production with unused land and soil-exhausting monoculture**
