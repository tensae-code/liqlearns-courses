# Latifundio and Venezuela's two farm systems — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Agricultural production includes fishing and timber, not only growing crops.
**Answer: True** — the book lists farming, livestock, fishing and timber.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The conuco was brought by the colonisers.
**Answer: False** — the conuco is the indigenous inheritance; the latifundio is the colonisers'.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** On the latifundio, land was spread evenly among many small owners.
**Answer: False** — land was concentrated in few hands, with landless peasants working it.
