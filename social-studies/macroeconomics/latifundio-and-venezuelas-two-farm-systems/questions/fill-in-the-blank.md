# Latifundio and Venezuela's two farm systems — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Venezuela had an agricultural economy until the _____ economy took over in the 1920s.
**Answer: oil**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The _____ system is the more productive of Venezuela's two farm systems.
**Answer: modern**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** On the latifundio, landless peasants had to hand _____ of the harvest to the owner.
**Answer: half**
