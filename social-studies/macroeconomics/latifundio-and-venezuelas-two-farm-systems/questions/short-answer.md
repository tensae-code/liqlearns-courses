# Latifundio and Venezuela's two farm systems — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** When did Venezuela's economy stop being mainly agricultural, and what took over?
**Answer:** In the 1920s, when the oil economy took over from the agricultural economy.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name Venezuela's two farm systems and the two inheritances inside the traditional one.
**Answer:** The traditional system (the colonisers' latifundio and the indigenous conuco) and the more productive modern system.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Give three charges the book lays against the latifundio.
**Answer:** Any three of: traditional tools; large extensions of unused land; soil-exhausting monoculture; land concentrated in few hands; landless peasants on low wages handing half the harvest to the owner.
