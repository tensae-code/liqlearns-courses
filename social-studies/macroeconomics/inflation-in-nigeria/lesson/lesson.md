# Inflation in Nigeria

## Do it first

Before you read on, write what you already think this lesson's title means — inflation in nigeria — in one sentence of your own. Keep it; you will check it against the lesson at the end.

## Sub lesson 1 — Inflation as experienced in Nigeria

Inflation in Nigeria is felt in the prices of food, transport and everyday goods.

## Sub lesson 2 — Nigeria's inflation story

Nigeria's inflation has risen and fallen with oil income, money creation and the exchange rate.

## Sub lesson 3 — What inflation does in Nigeria

Inflation in Nigeria cuts what wages buy, hurts savers and makes planning hard for households and firms.
