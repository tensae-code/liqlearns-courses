# Inflation in Nigeria — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Inflation in Nigeria is felt in the prices of food, transport and everyday _____.
**Answer: goods**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Nigeria's inflation moves with oil income, money creation and the _____ rate.
**Answer: exchange**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Inflation cuts what _____ buy and hurts savers.
**Answer: wages**
