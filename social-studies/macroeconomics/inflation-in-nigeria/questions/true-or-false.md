# Inflation in Nigeria — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Inflation is never felt in everyday prices in Nigeria.
**Answer: False** — Inflation in Nigeria is felt in the prices of food, transport and everyday goods.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Nigeria's inflation has never changed.
**Answer: False** — Nigeria's inflation has risen and fallen with oil income, money creation and the exchange rate.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Inflation helps savers by raising what their savings buy.
**Answer: False** — Inflation in Nigeria cuts what wages buy, hurts savers and makes planning hard for households and firms.
