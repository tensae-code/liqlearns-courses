# Inflation in Nigeria — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Where is inflation felt in Nigeria?
**Answer:** Inflation in Nigeria is felt in the prices of food, transport and everyday goods.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What has Nigeria's inflation moved with?
**Answer:** Nigeria's inflation has risen and fallen with oil income, money creation and the exchange rate.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does inflation do in Nigeria?
**Answer:** Inflation in Nigeria cuts what wages buy, hurts savers and makes planning hard for households and firms.
