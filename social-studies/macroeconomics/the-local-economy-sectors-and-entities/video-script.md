# Video script — The local economy — sectors and entities

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

How do you read a local economy? Mongolia's Grade 8 book answers in 3.1, says the narrator: population, sectors and organisations. The sectors are industry and construction, agriculture, and trade and services — worked through the 2015 Övörkhangai aimag statistics. The organisations are state, non-state and profit-seeking enterprise. And pupils prove it with a street-map survey of their own district.
