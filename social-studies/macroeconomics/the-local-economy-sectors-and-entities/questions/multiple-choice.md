# The local economy — sectors and entities — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A local economy, in the book, is read through —
a) prices only  b) population, sectors and organisations  c) one big factory
**Answer: b) population, sectors and organisations**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which set names the book's sectors?
a) industry and construction, agriculture, trade and services  b) mining, fishing, banking  c) schools, hospitals, courts
**Answer: a) industry and construction, agriculture, trade and services**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The three kinds of organisation are —
a) big, medium and small  b) old, new and foreign  c) state, non-state and profit-seeking enterprise
**Answer: c) state, non-state and profit-seeking enterprise**
