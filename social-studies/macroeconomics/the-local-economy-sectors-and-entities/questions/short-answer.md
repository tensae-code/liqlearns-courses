# The local economy — sectors and entities — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the three parts the book uses to read a local economy.
**Answer:** Its population, its sectors, and its organisations.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the book's three sectors.
**Answer:** Industry and construction, agriculture, and trade and services.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** How do pupils study the organisations of their own local economy?
**Answer:** By a street-map survey of their own district — mapping the enterprises around their school and sorting each into state, non-state or profit-seeking enterprise.
