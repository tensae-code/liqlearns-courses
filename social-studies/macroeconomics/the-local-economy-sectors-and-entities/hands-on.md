# Hands-on — The local economy — sectors and entities

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Map your own district
Draw a simple street map of the area around your home or school. Mark every enterprise and organisation you know, then sort each into state, non-state or profit-seeking enterprise.

## Say it with things at home
Put three bowls on the table — industry and construction, agriculture, trade and services. Name work done near you and drop a stone in the right bowl for each.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 3 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
