# The local economy — sectors and entities

## Do it first

Picture the streets around your school. Before you read on, name the enterprises and organisations you would pass on the way there, and guess what kinds they are.

## Sub lesson 1 — What a local economy is made of — population, sectors and organisations

Mongolia's Grade 8 book opens its economy chapter by asking, in 3.1, what we know about the **structure of the local economy**. Its answer has three parts: the local economy is its **population**, its **sectors** of work, and the **organisations** that do that work. To know a local economy, the book teaches, you read all three together.

## Sub lesson 2 — The sectors — industry and construction, agriculture, trade and services

The sectors the book names are **industry and construction**, **agriculture**, and **trade and services**. It works them through real numbers — the **2015 Övörkhangai aimag statistics** — so a province's economy is read sector by sector: how much of its work and output sits in each part.

## Sub lesson 3 — Three kinds of organisation — and surveying your own district

The organisations come in three kinds: **state**, **non-state**, and **profit-seeking enterprise**. The book does not leave this as a list — pupils carry out a **street-map survey of their own district**, mapping the enterprises around their own school and sorting each one into its kind. The local economy is learned by walking it.

## Say it to your companion
