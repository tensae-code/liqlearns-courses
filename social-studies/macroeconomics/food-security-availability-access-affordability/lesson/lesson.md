## Sub lesson 1 — Availability, accessibility, affordability

**Food security** means food is **available, accessible and affordable** — grown or stocked, reachable, and priced so people can buy it.

## Sub lesson 2 — Sen's entitlements and the Bengal famine of 1943

**Amartya Sen** deepened the idea with **entitlements** — what people can command; the **Bengal famine of 1943** shows people starving not only when food is absent, but when they cannot reach or buy it.

## Sub lesson 3 — Who is food-insecure; seasonal and chronic hunger

The **food-insecure** are those who cannot reliably get enough food; hunger comes as **seasonal hunger** (at lean times of the year) and **chronic hunger** (always too little).
