# Food security — availability, access, affordability — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Food security needs food to be —
a) available, accessible and affordable  b) available only  c) exported only
**Answer: a) available, accessible and affordable**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The entitlements idea comes from —
a) Amartya Sen  b) the FCI  c) the WTO
**Answer: a) Amartya Sen**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Hunger that returns at lean times of year is —
a) seasonal hunger  b) chronic hunger  c) no hunger
**Answer: a) seasonal hunger**
