# Food security — availability, access, affordability — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Food security means only that food exists somewhere in the country.
**Answer: False — it must also be accessible and affordable.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In a famine, food shortage is always the only cause of starvation.
**Answer: False — people may starve because they cannot access or afford food.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Seasonal hunger means being hungry all year round.
**Answer: False — seasonal hunger comes at lean times; chronic hunger is always present.**
