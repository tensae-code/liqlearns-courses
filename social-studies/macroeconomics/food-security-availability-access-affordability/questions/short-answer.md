# Food security — availability, access, affordability — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the three parts of food security.
**Answer:** Availability, accessibility and affordability.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Whose entitlements idea deepens food security, and which famine illustrates it?
**Answer:** Amartya Sen's; the Bengal famine of 1943.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What are the two kinds of hunger named?
**Answer:** Seasonal hunger and chronic hunger.
