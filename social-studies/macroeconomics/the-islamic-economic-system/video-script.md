# Video script — The Islamic economic system

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The Islamic economic system — as Pakistan's book teaches it, says the narrator: the system's features include private ownership, no storing of wealth, equitable distribution and interest-free banking; in Pakistan the system runs in practice through partnership and free bank services; the Islamic system is compared with capitalism and socialism on production, equality and welfare.
