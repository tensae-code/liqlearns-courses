# The Islamic economic system — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name three salient features of the Islamic economic system.
**Answer:** Any three of: right of private ownership, prohibition of storing wealth, equitable distribution of wealth, the price mechanism, interest-free banking.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How is interest-free banking shown in Pakistan?
**Answer:** In practice — the bank's business in partnership services and free services.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** How is the Islamic economic system compared at the book's close?
**Answer:** Against capitalism and socialism, on means of production, economic equality and collective welfare.
