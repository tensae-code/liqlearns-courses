# The Islamic economic system — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Islamic economic system bans all private ownership.
**Answer: False** — the right of private ownership is its first named feature.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Interest-free banking is never practised in Pakistan in the book's account.
**Answer: False** — the book teaches it in practice, in Pakistan.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The book compares the Islamic system with no other system.
**Answer: False** — capitalism and socialism are its named comparisons.
