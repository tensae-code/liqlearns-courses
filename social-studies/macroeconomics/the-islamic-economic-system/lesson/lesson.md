## Sub lesson 1 — Salient features and basic values

The Class 12 book closes with a whole system: the **Islamic economic system**. Its **salient features** — **right of private ownership, prohibition of storing wealth, equitable distribution of wealth, the price mechanism, and interest-free banking** — rest on **basic values**, **equality** among them; the chapter also treats the implications of orientation and hoarding.

## Sub lesson 2 — Interest-free banking in Pakistan in practice

The system is shown working: **interest-free banking in Pakistan in practice** — the business of the bank in **partnership services** and **free services**, banking carried on without interest inside a real country's system.

## Sub lesson 3 — Compared with capitalism and socialism

The book closes by **comparing the Islamic economic system with capitalism and with socialism** — on **means of production, economic equality and collective welfare**. Three systems, marked on the same three tests.
