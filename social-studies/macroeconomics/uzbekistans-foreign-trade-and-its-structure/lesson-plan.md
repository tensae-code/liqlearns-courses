# Lesson plan — Uzbekistan's foreign trade and its structure

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Macroeconomics · **Lesson:** Uzbekistan's foreign trade and its structure
**Place in the curriculum:** Macroeconomics (CURRICULUM.md) — built in the Economics expansion (Uzbekistan, 2026-11-16).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The country's own trade story from its real 2004 tables (exports $4,853.0m led by cotton fibre $878.4m; imports $3,816.0m led by machines and equipment $1,755.4m) and the policy conclusion — export finished goods, not raw materials, and make import-replacing goods at home.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the Uzbekistan Grade 9 book's own teaching of this topic — Topic 27, Uzbekistan's Foreign Trade — worked in sum on Uzbek cases.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of ..." in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.
