# Uzbekistan's foreign trade and its structure — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What led Uzbekistan's 2004 exports, and at what value?
**Answer:** Cotton fibre, $878.4m.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What led Uzbekistan's 2004 imports, and at what value?
**Answer:** Machines and equipment, $1,755.4m.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** State the book's policy conclusion in full.
**Answer:** Export finished goods, not raw materials, and make import-replacing goods at home.
