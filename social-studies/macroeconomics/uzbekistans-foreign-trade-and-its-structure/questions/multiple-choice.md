# Uzbekistan's foreign trade and its structure — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Uzbekistan's 2004 exports were —
a) $4,853.0m  b) $3,816.0m  c) $878.4m
**Answer: a) $4,853.0m**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Uzbekistan's 2004 imports were led by —
a) cotton fibre  b) machines and equipment  c) food
**Answer: b) machines and equipment**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The book's policy conclusion is —
a) export raw materials only  b) export finished goods, not raw materials, and make import-replacing goods at home  c) stop trading
**Answer: b) export finished goods, not raw materials, and make import-replacing goods at home**
