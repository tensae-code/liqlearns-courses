# Government policies to reduce income inequality — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The book presents —
a) arguments both for and against intervention  b) only one side  c) no arguments
**Answer: a) arguments both for and against intervention**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A beneficial inequality policy —
a) lifts the bottom without badly damaging the economy  b) destroys all incentives  c) helps only the top
**Answer: a) lifts the bottom without badly damaging the economy**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The central tradeoff is between —
a) incentives and income equality  b) exports and imports  c) price and quantity
**Answer: a) incentives and income equality**
