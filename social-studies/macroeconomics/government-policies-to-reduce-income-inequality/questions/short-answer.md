# Government policies to reduce income inequality — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the book do first on inequality policy?
**Answer:** It sets out the arguments for and against government intervention.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What makes a way of reducing inequality 'beneficial'?
**Answer:** It lifts the bottom without badly damaging how the economy works.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** State the tradeoff.
**Answer:** Pushing income equality harder can blunt incentives to work, save and build; policy must balance the two.
