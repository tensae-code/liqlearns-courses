# Government policies to reduce income inequality — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** There are no arguments against intervening on inequality.
**Answer: False — the book sets out arguments on both sides.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Every way of reducing inequality harms the economy equally.
**Answer: False — some ways are beneficial: they lift the bottom without badly damaging the economy.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** More equality never affects incentives in any way.
**Answer: False — the tradeoff is real: pushing equality can blunt incentives, so policy balances the two.**
