# Why manufacturing is the backbone of development — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Manufacturing has no link to agriculture.
**Answer: False — it modernises agriculture.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Raw materials are worth more than manufactured goods.
**Answer: False — manufacturing turns them into higher-value goods.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** India's manufacturing share matches East Asia's.
**Answer: False — India's ~17% lags East Asia's 25–35%.**
