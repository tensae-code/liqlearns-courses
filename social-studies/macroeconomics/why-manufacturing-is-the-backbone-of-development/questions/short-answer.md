# Why manufacturing is the backbone of development — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name two things manufacturing does for development.
**Answer:** It modernises agriculture and creates non-farm jobs (also: earns foreign exchange, adds value).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How does manufacturing earn and add value?
**Answer:** Exports earn foreign exchange; raw materials become higher-value goods.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Compare India's manufacturing share with East Asia's.
**Answer:** India is stuck near 17% of GDP; East Asia reaches 25–35%.
