# Why manufacturing is the backbone of development — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Manufacturing creates —
a) non-farm jobs  b) only farm jobs  c) no jobs
**Answer: a) non-farm jobs**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Manufacturing earns foreign exchange through —
a) exports  b) imports  c) barter only
**Answer: a) exports**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** India's manufacturing share is near —
a) 17% of GDP  b) 35% of GDP  c) 50% of GDP
**Answer: a) 17% of GDP**
