# Video script — Why manufacturing is the backbone of development

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Why manufacturing is the backbone of development — as India's book teaches it, says the narrator: manufacturing modernises agriculture, creates non-farm jobs, earns foreign exchange through exports and turns raw materials into higher-value goods — while India's manufacturing share has stagnated near 17% of GDP against 25–35% in East Asian economies.
