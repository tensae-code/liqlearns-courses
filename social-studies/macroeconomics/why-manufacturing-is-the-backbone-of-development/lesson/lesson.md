# Why manufacturing is the backbone of development

## Do it first

Before you read on, write what you already think this lesson's title means — why manufacturing is the backbone of development — in one sentence of your own. Keep it; you will check it against the lesson at the end.

## Sub lesson 1 — Manufacturing modernises agriculture and creates jobs

**Manufacturing modernises agriculture** (machines, fertiliser, processing) and **creates non-farm jobs** for people leaving the land.

## Sub lesson 2 — Exports earn foreign exchange; raw materials gain value

Manufactured goods **earn foreign exchange through exports**, and manufacturing **turns raw materials into higher-value goods**.

## Sub lesson 3 — India's share stuck near 17% against East Asia's 25–35%

India's **manufacturing share has stagnated near 17% of GDP**, against **25–35% in East Asian economies** — the gap the lesson asks India to close.
