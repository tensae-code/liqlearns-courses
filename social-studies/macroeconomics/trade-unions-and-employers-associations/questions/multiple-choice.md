# Trade unions and employers' associations — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The book's diagram joins —
a) state, employers, workers and unions  b) banks and shops  c) exports and imports
**Answer: a) state, employers, workers and unions**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Trade unions are —
a) state bodies  b) non-state organisations defending workers' interests  c) employers' clubs
**Answer: b) non-state organisations defending workers' interests**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Full employment means only —
a) frictional and structural unemployment  b) no unemployment of any kind  c) cyclical unemployment
**Answer: a) frictional and structural unemployment**
