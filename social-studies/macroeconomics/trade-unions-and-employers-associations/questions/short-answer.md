# Trade unions and employers' associations — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which four sides does the book's diagram join?
**Answer:** State, employers, workers and unions.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What are trade unions, in the book's words?
**Answer:** Non-state organisations that defend workers' interests before employers and the state.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** How does the book define full employment?
**Answer:** Only frictional and structural unemployment remains — a natural rate of 4–5%.
