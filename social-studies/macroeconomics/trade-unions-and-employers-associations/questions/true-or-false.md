# Trade unions and employers' associations — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Employers' and workers' claims clash and must be balanced.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Unions defend workers' interests before employers and the state.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The natural rate of unemployment in the book is 4–5%.
**Answer: True**
