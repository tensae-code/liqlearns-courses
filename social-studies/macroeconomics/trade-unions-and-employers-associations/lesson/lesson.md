# Trade unions and employers' associations

## Do it first

Think of this topic the way the Uzbekistan Grade 9 book opens it — Topic 25, Trade Unions. What do you already know about it before you read on?

## Sub lesson 1 — The clash and balance — employers' and workers' claims

Employers and hired workers make opposite claims on each other. The book draws the balance as a diagram: **state – employers – workers – unions**.

## Sub lesson 2 — Trade unions — defending workers' interests

**Trade unions** are the **non-state organisations** that **defend workers' interests before employers and the state**.

## Sub lesson 3 — Full employment — only frictional and structural unemployment

**Full employment** is defined as only **frictional and structural** unemployment remaining — a **natural rate of 4–5%**.
