The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Employers and hired workers make opposite claims on each other.

Trade unions are the non-state organisations that defend workers' interests before employers and the state.

Full employment is defined as only frictional and structural unemployment remaining — a natural rate of 4–5%.
