# Investment — gross and net — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Investment adds to the capital stock" in your own words.
**Answer:** Investment is spending that adds to the stock of capital for future income.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Gross and replacement investment" in your own words.
**Answer:** Gross investment is total capital spending before depreciation; replacement investment only offsets wear.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Net investment grows capacity" in your own words.
**Answer:** Net investment = gross minus depreciation, and only positive net investment expands productive capacity.
