# Investment — gross and net — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Investment means buying goods for immediate consumption.
**Answer: False — Goods for immediate use are consumption. Investment accumulates capital to produce future income.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Replacement investment expands the capital stock.
**Answer: False — Replacement only offsets capital worn out — it keeps the stock level, it does not expand it.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Net investment is gross investment plus depreciation.
**Answer: False — Net is gross MINUS depreciation — what is left after making good the wear.**
