# Investment — gross and net — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or figure from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Investment adds to the stock of ____.
**Answer: capital**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Spending that merely offsets worn-out capital is ____ investment.
**Answer: replacement**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Net investment equals gross investment minus ____.
**Answer: depreciation**
