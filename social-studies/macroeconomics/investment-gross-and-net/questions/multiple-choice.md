# Investment — gross and net — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Investment adds to the capital stock"?
a) Investment is spending that adds to the stock of capital for future income.  b) Gross investment is total capital spending before depreciation; replacement investment only offsets wear.  c) Net investment = gross minus depreciation, and only positive net investment expands productive capacity.
**Answer: a) Investment is spending that adds to the stock of capital for future income.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Gross and replacement investment"?
a) Net investment = gross minus depreciation, and only positive net investment expands productive capacity.  b) Investment is spending that adds to the stock of capital for future income.  c) Gross investment is total capital spending before depreciation; replacement investment only offsets wear.
**Answer: c) Gross investment is total capital spending before depreciation; replacement investment only offsets wear.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Net investment grows capacity"?
a) Gross investment is total capital spending before depreciation; replacement investment only offsets wear.  b) Net investment = gross minus depreciation, and only positive net investment expands productive capacity.  c) Investment is spending that adds to the stock of capital for future income.
**Answer: b) Net investment = gross minus depreciation, and only positive net investment expands productive capacity.**
