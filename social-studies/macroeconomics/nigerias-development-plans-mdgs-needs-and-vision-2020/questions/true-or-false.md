# Nigeria's development plans — MDGs, NEEDS and Vision 2020 — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The MDGs set Nigeria no targets of any kind.
**Answer: False** — The Millennium Development Goals set Nigeria targets on poverty, health and education to be reached by 2015.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** NEEDS was a foreign country's plan imposed without Nigeria's part.
**Answer: False** — NEEDS — the National Economic Empowerment and Development Strategy — was Nigeria's own reform and poverty-reduction plan.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Vision 2020 aimed to make Nigeria the world's smallest economy.
**Answer: False** — Vision 2020 aimed to make Nigeria one of the world's twenty largest economies by the year 2020.
