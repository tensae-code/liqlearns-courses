# Nigeria's development plans — MDGs, NEEDS and Vision 2020 — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What did the MDGs set Nigeria?
**Answer:** The Millennium Development Goals set Nigeria targets on poverty, health and education to be reached by 2015.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What was NEEDS?
**Answer:** NEEDS — the National Economic Empowerment and Development Strategy — was Nigeria's own reform and poverty-reduction plan.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What did Vision 2020 aim at?
**Answer:** Vision 2020 aimed to make Nigeria one of the world's twenty largest economies by the year 2020.
