## Sub lesson 1 — The MDGs in Nigeria

The Millennium Development Goals set Nigeria targets on poverty, health and education to be reached by 2015.

## Sub lesson 2 — NEEDS

NEEDS — the National Economic Empowerment and Development Strategy — was Nigeria's own reform and poverty-reduction plan.

## Sub lesson 3 — Vision 2020

Vision 2020 aimed to make Nigeria one of the world's twenty largest economies by the year 2020.
