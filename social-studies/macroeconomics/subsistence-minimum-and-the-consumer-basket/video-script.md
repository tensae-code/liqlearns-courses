# Video script — The subsistence minimum and the consumer basket

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Consumption is using goods and services to meet needs. Its level is set by personal income.

The consumer basket is the set of typical goods and services a person consumes. The subsistence minimum is the minimum level a person needs to live on, measured through that basket.

Together they are the yardstick of living standards across countries — and from the subsistence minimum, the state sets the minimum wage.
