Belarus's own way: economics arrives inside a philosophy-and-civics course as the ethics of work — profit defined on one diagram, then wages, the subsistence minimum and the consumer basket as measures of a decent life. This lesson keeps that way: a decent life is measured, not just described — the basket is priced, and the minimum is read off it. The lesson teaches the placement's content in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
