# The subsistence minimum and the consumer basket — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is consumption, and what sets its level?
**Answer:** Consumption is using goods and services to meet needs. Its level is set by personal income.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is the consumer basket?
**Answer:** The consumer basket is the set of typical goods and services a person consumes — the standard list of what a life ordinarily uses.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** How are the subsistence minimum and the consumer basket used?
**Answer:** They are the yardstick of living standards across countries, and from the subsistence minimum the state sets the minimum wage.
