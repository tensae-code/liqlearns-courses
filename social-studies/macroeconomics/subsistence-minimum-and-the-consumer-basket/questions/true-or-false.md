# The subsistence minimum and the consumer basket — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Consumption is using goods and services to meet needs.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The consumer basket holds only luxury goods that nobody needs.
**Answer: False** — the consumer basket is the set of typical goods and services a person consumes.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The subsistence minimum and the consumer basket are a yardstick of living standards across countries.
**Answer: True**
