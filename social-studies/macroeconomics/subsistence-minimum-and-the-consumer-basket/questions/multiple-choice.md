# The subsistence minimum and the consumer basket — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The level of a person's consumption is set by —
a) the weather  b) personal income  c) the number of shops
**Answer: b) personal income**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The "consumer basket" is —
a) a basket a shopper carries  b) the set of typical goods and services a person consumes  c) a tax on goods
**Answer: b) the set of typical goods and services a person consumes**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** From the subsistence minimum, the state sets —
a) the maximum wage  b) the price of bread  c) the minimum wage
**Answer: c) the minimum wage**
