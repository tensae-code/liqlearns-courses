# The subsistence minimum and the consumer basket — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Consumption is using goods and services to meet _____.
**Answer: needs**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The consumer basket (спажывецкі кошык) is the set of _____ goods and services a person consumes.
**Answer: typical**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The _____ minimum (пражытачны мінімум) is the minimum level a person needs to live on.
**Answer: subsistence**
