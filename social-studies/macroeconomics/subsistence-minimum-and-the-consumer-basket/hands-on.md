# Hands-on — The subsistence minimum and the consumer basket

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Put real things — or cards naming them — into a basket: the typical goods and services one person in your home uses in a week. Say out loud why each one belongs in the basket, and what leaving it out would do to the measure.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's basket and its yardstick from memory — the basket of typical goods and services, and the subsistence minimum line read off it — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the basket and the minimum there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
