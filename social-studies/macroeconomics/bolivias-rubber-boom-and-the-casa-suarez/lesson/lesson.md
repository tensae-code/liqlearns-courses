## Sub lesson 1 — Tyre rubber — a boom of prices

European and US demand for tyre rubber made prices — more than volumes — boom in the Amazon. The world's new bicycles and cars ran, through Bolivia's forests, on wild rubber.

## Sub lesson 2 — Estradas and the Casa Suárez — 60% of production

Concessions were demarcated in estradas of about 150 rubber trees each, and one house rose above all: the Casa Suárez y Hnos. came to control 60% of Bolivia's rubber production.

## Sub lesson 3 — Enganche — and the end of the boom

The section's own heading names the boom's human price: the enslavement of the indigenous population through the enganche, the debt-hook that bound tappers to the estates. The boom ended when rubber was found in Asia and prices fell.
