# Video script — Bolivia's rubber boom and the Casa Suárez

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Bolivia's rubber boom and the Casa Suárez — as Bolivia's book teaches it, says the narrator: European and US demand for tyre rubber made prices boom in the Amazon; the Casa Suárez came to control 60% of Bolivia's rubber production, in estradas of about 150 trees; the boom rested on the enganche's enslavement of indigenous tappers, and ended when rubber was found in Asia and prices fell.
