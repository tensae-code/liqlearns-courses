# Bolivia's rubber boom and the Casa Suárez — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The boom came from Bolivian demand for tyres.
**Answer: False** — it was European and US demand for tyre rubber.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Rubber production stayed split among thousands of equal small firms.
**Answer: False** — one house, the Casa Suárez, controlled 60% of it.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The boom ended because demand for tyres disappeared.
**Answer: False** — it ended when Asian rubber appeared and prices fell.
