# Bolivia's rubber boom and the Casa Suárez — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the lesson teach about tyre rubber — a boom of prices?
**Answer:** European and US demand for tyre rubber made prices boom in the Amazon.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the lesson teach about estradas and the Casa Suárez — 60% of production?
**Answer:** The Casa Suárez came to control 60% of Bolivia's rubber production, in estradas of about 150 trees.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the lesson teach about enganche — and the end of the boom?
**Answer:** The boom rested on the enganche's enslavement of indigenous tappers, and ended when rubber was found in Asia and prices fell.
