# Bolivia's rubber boom and the Casa Suárez — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** About tyre rubber — a boom of prices — the lesson teaches that —
a) European and US demand for tyre rubber made prices boom in the Amazon  b) the Casa Suárez came to control 60% of Bolivia's rubber production, in estradas of about 150 trees  c) the boom rested on the enganche's enslavement of indigenous tappers, and ended when rubber was found in Asia and prices fell
