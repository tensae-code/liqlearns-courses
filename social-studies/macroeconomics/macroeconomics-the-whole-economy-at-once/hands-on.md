# Hands-on — Macroeconomics — the whole economy at once

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Tile game: each learner holds one household or firm card; the cards are stacked into one big panel while the class reads the aggregate names the stack creates — output, employment, price level.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — the two-column comparison with the teff-price and Ethiopia-GDP examples marked; many small household and firm tiles adding up into one whole-economy panel — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.

## The Grade 12 layer — Keynes and the Depression
On one page, write the classical claim ('the economy heals itself') and the Depression fact that broke it, then the Keynesian answer in one line. Underneath, list the eight Grade 12 units in order from the road-map picture and mark the two you most want to reach.
