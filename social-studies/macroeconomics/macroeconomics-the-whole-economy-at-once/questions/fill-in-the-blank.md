# Macroeconomics — the whole economy at once — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Macroeconomics studies the economy as a whole and its _____ — national income, total employment, the general price level.
**Answer: aggregates**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The guide's scope of aggregative analysis has _____ factors: national income, employment, general price level, growth.
**Answer: four**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Micro and macro are _____, not rivals — one economy at two heights of view.
**Answer: complementary**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Macroeconomics was born with Keynes's ____ Theory of 1936.
**Answer: General**
