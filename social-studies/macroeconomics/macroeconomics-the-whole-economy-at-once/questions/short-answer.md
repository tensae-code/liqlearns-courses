# Macroeconomics — the whole economy at once — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "The whole-economy branch" in your own words.
**Answer:** Macroeconomics studies the economy as a whole and its aggregates: national income and output, total employment and the general price level.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Adding up — and the scope" in your own words.
**Answer:** Macroeconomics adds up the activity of all households and businesses, and its scope is national income, employment, the general price level and economic growth.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Micro and macro compared — in Ethiopian examples" in your own words.
**Answer:** The price of teff is micro, while the GDP of Ethiopia is macro; the two branches are complementary, not rivals.

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Explain "Born from the Depression — Keynes, 1936" in your own words.
**Answer:** Macroeconomics studies the economy as a whole and was born with Keynes's 1936 General Theory, after the Depression broke classical self-correction.
