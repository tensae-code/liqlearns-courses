# Macroeconomics — the whole economy at once — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "The whole-economy branch"?
a) the economy as a whole — national income, total employment, the general price level  b) adding up all households and businesses; scope — national income, employment, price level, growth  c) teff price and one person's saving are micro; Ethiopia's GDP and price level are macro
**Answer: a) the economy as a whole — national income, total employment, the general price level**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Adding up — and the scope"?
a) the economy as a whole — national income, total employment, the general price level  b) teff price and one person's saving are micro; Ethiopia's GDP and price level are macro  c) adding up all households and businesses; scope — national income, employment, price level, growth
**Answer: c) adding up all households and businesses; scope — national income, employment, price level, growth**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Micro and macro compared — in Ethiopian examples"?
a) adding up all households and businesses; scope — national income, employment, price level, growth  b) teff price and one person's saving are micro; Ethiopia's GDP and price level are macro  c) the economy as a whole — national income, total employment, the general price level
**Answer: b) teff price and one person's saving are micro; Ethiopia's GDP and price level are macro**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Which statement best matches "Born from the Depression — Keynes, 1936"?
a) Macroeconomics studies one firm's pricing decision in a single market  b) Macroeconomics studies the economy as a whole and was born with Keynes's 1936 General Theory, after the Depression broke classical self-correction  c) Macroeconomics counts only the exports and imports between two countries
**Answer: b) Macroeconomics studies the economy as a whole and was born with Keynes's 1936 General Theory, after the Depression broke classical self-correction**
