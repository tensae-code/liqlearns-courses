# Macroeconomics — the whole economy at once — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Macroeconomics studies the economy as a whole and its aggregates: national income and output, total employment and the general price level.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Macroeconomics studies the economy as a whole and its aggregates: national income and output, total employment and the general price level.
**Answer: False** — that statement belongs to sub lesson 1 (The whole-economy branch); sub lesson 2 says: Macroeconomics adds up the activity of all households and businesses, and its scope is national income, employment, the general price level and economic growth.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The price of teff is micro, while the GDP of Ethiopia is macro; the two branches are complementary, not rivals.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Macroeconomics studies the economy as a whole and was born with Keynes's 1936 General Theory, after the Depression broke classical self-correction.
**Answer: True**
