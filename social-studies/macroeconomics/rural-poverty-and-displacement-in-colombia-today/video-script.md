Rural poverty and displacement in colombia today — as Colombia's book teaches it, says the narrator: Over 68% of Colombia's rural population is poor and 27.5% of campesinos live in misery; More than 30% of the rural population was expelled from the countryside by violence in 15 years And together with the search for better economic opportunities this leaves the rural sector in crisis, linked to city unemployment through migration
