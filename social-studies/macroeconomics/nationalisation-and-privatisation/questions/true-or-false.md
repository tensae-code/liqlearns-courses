# Nationalisation and privatisation — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Nationalisation is the state taking an enterprise into state ownership.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Privatisation means the state takes an enterprise over.
**Answer: False — Privatisation is selling back into private ownership — the opposite of taking over.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Each move is judged by what it does, not by its name.
**Answer: True**
