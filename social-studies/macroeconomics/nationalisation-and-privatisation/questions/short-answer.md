# Nationalisation and privatisation — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What three things change when an enterprise is nationalised?
**Answer: Who owns it, who decides, and who receives what it earns.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Give one reason a country privatises.
**Answer: To bring in private capital and management (or because the enterprise is judged better run outside the state's hands).**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What moves from one side to the other in both moves?
**Answer: Ownership, decisions, who gains and who carries losses.**
