# Nationalisation and privatisation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Nationalisation means —
a) a family sells its shop to a neighbour  b) the state takes an enterprise into its own ownership  c) an enterprise opens a second branch
**Answer: b) the state takes an enterprise into its own ownership**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Privatisation means —
a) the state sells an enterprise back into private ownership  b) an enterprise closes forever  c) the state takes an enterprise over
**Answer: a) the state sells an enterprise back into private ownership**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** How should each move be judged, in the book's habit?
a) never — judgement is impossible  b) by its name alone  c) by what it does to the enterprise and the economy, not by its name
**Answer: c) by what it does to the enterprise and the economy, not by its name**
