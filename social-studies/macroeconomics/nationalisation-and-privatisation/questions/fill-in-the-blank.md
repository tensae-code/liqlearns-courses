# Nationalisation and privatisation — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** _____ is the state taking an enterprise into its own ownership.
**Answer: Nationalisation**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Privatisation is the state _____ an enterprise back into private ownership.
**Answer: selling**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Nationalisation and privatisation are _____ images of each other.
**Answer: mirror**
