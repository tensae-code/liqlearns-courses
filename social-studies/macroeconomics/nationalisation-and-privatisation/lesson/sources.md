Cambodia's own way (Cambodia Grade 11 Economics): a full academic course built around Cambodia's own transition — the market system is introduced, judged, and then located in Cambodia itself (its market-economy foundations, its national economy, its reform programme), and the book closes with the history of economic thought from the first schools to Keynes. This lesson is built from that book's placement: Ch. 2, Lesson 8: Nationalisation and privatisation — teaches: taking enterprises into state ownership and selling them back, and what each move changes. The lesson teaches the placement's content in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

Egypt's own way (Secondary 12 Statistics and Economics): a full school Economics course in six chapters, ending at home — the last chapter brings globalisation back to Egypt and Africa, and the money chapter tells Egypt's own story (the National Bank's 1898 notes, the Central Bank of Egypt from 1961).
