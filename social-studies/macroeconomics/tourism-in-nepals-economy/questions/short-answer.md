# Tourism in Nepal's economy — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the book teach about tourism and tourists?
**Answer:** Tourism is a service business built around the tourist — guiding, food, lodging, travel agencies and adventure sports — and its tourists are internal and external tourists: internal tourists travel inside their own country, and external tourists come from abroad.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the book teach about what tourism does for nepal?
**Answer:** Tourism creates employment, earns foreign currency, raises government revenue and develops linked handicraft industries, trade and infrastructure.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the book teach about nepal's tourism possibilities and promotion?
**Answer:** Nepal's possibilities include the Great Himalaya Trail, natural beauty, religious sites, heritage, adventure, climate and culture, and promotion means developing the sites, services and infrastructure tourists use.
