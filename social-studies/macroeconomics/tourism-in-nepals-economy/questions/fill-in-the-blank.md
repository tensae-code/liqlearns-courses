# Tourism in Nepal's economy — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Tourism is a service business built around the tourist — guiding, food, lodging, travel agencies and adventure sports — and its tourists are _____: internal tourists travel inside their own country, and external tourists come from abroad.
**Answer: internal and external tourists**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Tourism creates employment, _____, raises government revenue and develops linked handicraft industries, trade and infrastructure.
**Answer: earns foreign currency**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Nepal's possibilities include _____, natural beauty, religious sites, heritage, adventure, climate and culture, and promotion means developing the sites, services and infrastructure tourists use.
**Answer: the Great Himalaya Trail**
