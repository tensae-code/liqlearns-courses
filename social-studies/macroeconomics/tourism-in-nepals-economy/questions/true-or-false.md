# Tourism in Nepal's economy — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Tourism is a service business built around the tourist — guiding, food, lodging, travel agencies and adventure sports — and its tourists are internal and external tourists: internal tourists travel inside their own country, and external tourists come from abroad.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Tourism creates employment, earns foreign currency, raises government revenue and develops linked handicraft industries, trade and infrastructure.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Nepal's possibilities include something else entirely, natural beauty, religious sites, heritage, adventure, climate and culture, and promotion means developing the sites, services and infrastructure tourists use.
**Answer: False** — Nepal's possibilities include the Great Himalaya Trail, natural beauty, religious sites, heritage, adventure, climate and culture, and promotion means developing the sites, services and infrastructure tourists use.
