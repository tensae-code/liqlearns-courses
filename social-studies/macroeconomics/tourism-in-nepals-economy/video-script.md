# Video script — Tourism in Nepal's economy

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Tourism is a service business that creates employment and earns Nepal foreign currency.

Tourism is a service business built around the tourist — guiding, food, lodging, travel agencies and adventure sports — and its tourists are internal and external tourists: internal tourists travel inside their own country, and external tourists come from abroad.

Tourism creates employment, earns foreign currency, raises government revenue and develops linked handicraft industries, trade and infrastructure.

Nepal's possibilities include the Great Himalaya Trail, natural beauty, religious sites, heritage, adventure, climate and culture, and promotion means developing the sites, services and infrastructure tourists use.
