# Zakat and Ushr — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** About the system of zakat — the lesson teaches that —
a) Zakat is a set share of wealth, given through a system, in the Islamic fiscal order  b) Ushr is the share taken from agricultural produce  c) Zakat and Ushr fairly distribute wealth, alleviate poverty and expand the economy
**Answer: a) Zakat is a set share of wealth, given through a system, in the Islamic fiscal order**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** About the system of ushr; charity beside them — the lesson teaches that —
a) Zakat is a set share of wealth, given through a system, in the Islamic fiscal order  b) Ushr is the share taken from agricultural produce  c) Zakat and Ushr fairly distribute wealth, alleviate poverty and expand the economy
**Answer: b) Ushr is the share taken from agricultural produce**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** About what zakat and ushr do — the lesson teaches that —
a) Zakat is a set share of wealth, given through a system, in the Islamic fiscal order  b) Ushr is the share taken from agricultural produce  c) Zakat and Ushr fairly distribute wealth, alleviate poverty and expand the economy
**Answer: c) Zakat and Ushr fairly distribute wealth, alleviate poverty and expand the economy**
