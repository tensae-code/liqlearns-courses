# Zakat and Ushr — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is Zakat, as the book teaches it?
**Answer:** A set share of wealth given by those who have it, through a system — a fiscal institution of the Islamic economic order.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is Ushr?
**Answer:** Ushr is the share taken from agricultural produce, standing beside Zakat, with charity beside both.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name three effects of Zakat and Ushr from the book.
**Answer:** Any three of: fair distribution of wealth, poverty alleviation, solution of unemployment, increase in circulation of capital, expansion of the economy.
