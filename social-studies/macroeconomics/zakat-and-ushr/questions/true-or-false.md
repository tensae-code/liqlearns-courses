# Zakat and Ushr — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Zakat is a voluntary gift with no set share or system.
**Answer: False** — the book teaches it as a system with a set share.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Ushr is a tax on factory machinery.
**Answer: False** — Ushr falls on agricultural produce.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Zakat and Ushr concentrate wealth in fewer hands.
**Answer: False** — their named effects are the opposite — fair distribution and poverty alleviation.
