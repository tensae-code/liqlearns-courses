# Zakat and Ushr — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The set share of wealth in the Islamic fiscal system is _____.
**Answer: Zakat**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The share taken from agricultural produce is _____.
**Answer: Ushr**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Poverty _____ is a named effect of Zakat and Ushr.
**Answer: alleviation**
