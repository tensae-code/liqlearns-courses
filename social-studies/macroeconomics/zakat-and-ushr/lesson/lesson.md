## Sub lesson 1 — The system of Zakat

The Class 12 book teaches **Zakat** as a working fiscal institution: a set share of wealth given by those who have it, collected and distributed by a system — a tax-like pillar of the Islamic economic order, not private charity alone.

## Sub lesson 2 — The system of Ushr; charity beside them

Beside Zakat stands **Ushr** — the share taken from agricultural produce — and **charity** beside both. Together they are the book's redistributive machinery on land and wealth alike.

## Sub lesson 3 — What Zakat and Ushr do

The book names their effects: **fair distribution of wealth, poverty alleviation, solution of unemployment, increase in circulation of capital, and expansion of the economy**. Redistribution is taught as economic policy with measurable results.
