# Video script — Zakat and Ushr

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Zakat and Ushr — as Pakistan's book teaches it, says the narrator: Zakat is a set share of wealth, given through a system, in the Islamic fiscal order; Ushr is the share taken from agricultural produce; Zakat and Ushr fairly distribute wealth, alleviate poverty and expand the economy.
