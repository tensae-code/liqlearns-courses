# The digital economy — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The digital economy is economic activity carried through _____ — buying, selling and paying online.
**Answer: digital technology**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** E-commerce contributes a growing share of trade, and digital finance includes crowdfunding, microfinancing and _____.
**Answer: peer-to-peer lending**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The teacher guide adds financial-literacy teaching on _____: borrowing by phone is easy, so its costs and risks must be understood first.
**Answer: online loans (pinjol)**
