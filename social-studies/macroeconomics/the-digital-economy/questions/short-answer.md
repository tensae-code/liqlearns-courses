# The digital economy — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the book teach about the digitalisation of the economy?
**Answer:** The digital economy is economic activity carried through digital technology — buying, selling and paying online.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the book teach about e-commerce and digital finance?
**Answer:** E-commerce contributes a growing share of trade, and digital finance includes crowdfunding, microfinancing and peer-to-peer lending.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the book teach about online loans (pinjol) — financial literacy?
**Answer:** The teacher guide adds financial-literacy teaching on online loans (pinjol): borrowing by phone is easy, so its costs and risks must be understood first.
