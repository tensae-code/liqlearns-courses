# The digital economy — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The digital economy is economic activity carried through digital technology — buying, selling and paying online.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** E-commerce contributes a growing share of trade, and digital finance includes crowdfunding, microfinancing and peer-to-peer lending.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The teacher guide adds financial-literacy teaching on something else entirely: borrowing by phone is easy, so its costs and risks must be understood first.
**Answer: False** — The teacher guide adds financial-literacy teaching on online loans (pinjol): borrowing by phone is easy, so its costs and risks must be understood first.
