# The digital economy

## Do it first

Think of this topic the way the Indonesia book opens it — The digital economy. What do you already know about it before you read on?

## Sub lesson 1 — The digitalisation of the economy

The digital economy is economic activity carried through digital technology — buying, selling and paying online.

## Sub lesson 2 — E-commerce and digital finance

E-commerce contributes a growing share of trade, and digital finance includes crowdfunding, microfinancing and peer-to-peer lending.

## Sub lesson 3 — Online loans (pinjol) — financial literacy

The teacher guide adds financial-literacy teaching on online loans (pinjol): borrowing by phone is easy, so its costs and risks must be understood first.
