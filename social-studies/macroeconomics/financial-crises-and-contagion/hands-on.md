# Hands-on — Financial crises and contagion

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Trace the routes
Draw three arrows from a crashing country to a neighbour, labelled trade, capital flight and confidence, with one concrete example on each arrow.

## Domino order
Put the four crises in date order from memory — Mexico, Asia, Russia, Argentina — then attach one transmission route to each step.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
