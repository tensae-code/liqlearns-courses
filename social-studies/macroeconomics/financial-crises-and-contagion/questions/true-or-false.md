# Financial crises and contagion — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Crises spread through trade, capital flight and confidence.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Mexico's 1995 collapse (GDP −8.1%) began a chain running through Asia 1997–98 to Russia and Argentina.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Policy watches other countries because contagion carries their crises home.
**Answer: True**
