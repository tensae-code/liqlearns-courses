# Financial crises and contagion — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Crises are contagious" in your own words.
**Answer:** Crises spread through trade, capital flight and confidence.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "The chain — Mexico, Asia, Russia, Argentina" in your own words.
**Answer:** Mexico's 1995 collapse (GDP −8.1%) began a chain running through Asia 1997–98 to Russia and Argentina.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Why policy watches abroad" in your own words.
**Answer:** Policy watches other countries because contagion carries their crises home.
