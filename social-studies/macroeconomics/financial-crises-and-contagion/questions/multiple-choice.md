# Financial crises and contagion — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Crises are contagious"?
a) Crises spread through trade, capital flight and confidence  b) Mexico's 1995 collapse (GDP −8.1%) began a chain running through Asia 1997–98 to Russia and Argentina  c) Policy watches other countries because contagion carries their crises home
**Answer: a) Crises spread through trade, capital flight and confidence**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "The chain — Mexico, Asia, Russia, Argentina"?
a) Mexico's 1995 collapse (GDP −8.1%) began a chain running through Asia 1997–98 to Russia and Argentina  b) Policy watches other countries because contagion carries their crises home  c) Crises spread through trade, capital flight and confidence
**Answer: a) Mexico's 1995 collapse (GDP −8.1%) began a chain running through Asia 1997–98 to Russia and Argentina**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Why policy watches abroad"?
a) Policy watches other countries because contagion carries their crises home  b) Crises spread through trade, capital flight and confidence  c) Mexico's 1995 collapse (GDP −8.1%) began a chain running through Asia 1997–98 to Russia and Argentina
**Answer: a) Policy watches other countries because contagion carries their crises home**
