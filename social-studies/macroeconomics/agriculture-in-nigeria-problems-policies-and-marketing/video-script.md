Agriculture in Nigeria — problems, policies and marketing — as Nigeria's book teaches it, says the narrator: nigerian agriculture combines crops and livestock, and faces problems from methods, pests, finance and land.; nigeria's agricultural policies try to raise production and support farmers.; agricultural marketing moves produce to buyers through boards and markets, and the sector's prospects rest on solving its problems..
