# Agriculture in Nigeria — problems, policies and marketing — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What problems does Nigerian agriculture face?
**Answer:** Nigerian agriculture combines crops and livestock, and faces problems from methods, pests, finance and land.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What do Nigeria's agricultural policies try to do?
**Answer:** Nigeria's agricultural policies try to raise production and support farmers.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** How does agricultural marketing work?
**Answer:** Agricultural marketing moves produce to buyers through boards and markets, and the sector's prospects rest on solving its problems.
