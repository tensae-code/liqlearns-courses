# Agriculture in Nigeria — problems, policies and marketing — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Nigerian agriculture faces no problems.
**Answer: False** — Nigerian agriculture combines crops and livestock, and faces problems from methods, pests, finance and land.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Nigeria has never had an agricultural policy.
**Answer: False** — Nigeria's agricultural policies try to raise production and support farmers.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Agricultural marketing keeps produce away from buyers.
**Answer: False** — Agricultural marketing moves produce to buyers through boards and markets, and the sector's prospects rest on solving its problems.
