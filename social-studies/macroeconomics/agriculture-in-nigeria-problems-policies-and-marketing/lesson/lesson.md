## Sub lesson 1 — Components and problems of agriculture

Nigerian agriculture combines crops and livestock, and faces problems from methods, pests, finance and land.

## Sub lesson 2 — Nigerian agricultural policies

Nigeria's agricultural policies try to raise production and support farmers.

## Sub lesson 3 — Marketing and prospects

Agricultural marketing moves produce to buyers through boards and markets, and the sector's prospects rest on solving its problems.
