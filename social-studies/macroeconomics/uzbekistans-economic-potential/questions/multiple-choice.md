# Uzbekistan's economic potential — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Uzbekistan's gold reserves rank —
a) first in the world  b) 4th in the world  c) tenth
**Answer: b) 4th in the world**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The Asaka plant makes —
a) Nexia, Matiz and Tico cars  b) Otoyol minibuses  c) tractors
**Answer: a) Nexia, Matiz and Tico cars**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Cotton-led agriculture stands at —
a) 28% of GDP  b) 4% of GDP  c) 90% of GDP
**Answer: a) 28% of GDP**
