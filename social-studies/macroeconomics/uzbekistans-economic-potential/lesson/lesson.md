# Uzbekistan's economic potential

## Do it first

Think of this topic the way the Uzbekistan Grade 9 book opens it — Topic 21, The Economic Potential of Uzbekistan. What do you already know about it before you read on?

## Sub lesson 1 — Territory, population and natural wealth

The book tells the country's own national-economy story: its **territory and population**, and its **natural wealth** — **gold reserves 4th in the world**, gas, oil and uranium.

## Sub lesson 2 — Industry — the Asaka cars and Samarkand's minibuses

Uzbekistan's industry is shown through its own plants: the **Asaka car plant's Nexia, Matiz and Tico**, and **Samarkand's Otoyol minibuses**.

## Sub lesson 3 — Cotton-led agriculture — and the data tables

**Cotton-led agriculture** stands at **28% of GDP**. The topic closes with data tables: **GDP 1991–2004** and GDP growth across the CIS.
