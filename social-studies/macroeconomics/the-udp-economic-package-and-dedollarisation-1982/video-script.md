# Video script — The UDP economic package and dedollarisation, 1982

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The UDP economic package and dedollarisation, 1982 — as Bolivia's book teaches it, says the narrator: the returning UDP government's package acted on the exchange rate, the currency, prices and the external debt; the package restored a fixed exchange rate and prohibited dollar transactions — dedollarisation; the package raised prices to fund salaries and suspended external-debt payment for three months.
