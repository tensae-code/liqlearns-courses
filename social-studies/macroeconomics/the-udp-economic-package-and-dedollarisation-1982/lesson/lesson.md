# The UDP economic package and dedollarisation, 1982

## Do it first

## Sub lesson 1 — Democracy returns — the package

The returning democratic government — the UDP — answered the crisis it inherited with an economic package: a set of emergency measures on the exchange rate, the currency, prices and the external debt.

## Sub lesson 2 — Fixed rate restored — and the economy dedollarised

Two of its measures: restore a fixed exchange rate against the dollar, and dedollarise the economy — dollar transactions prohibited, the boliviano made the only money of contracts again.

## Sub lesson 3 — Prices raised, debt payment suspended

The package also raised the prices of goods and services to fund salary payments, and suspended external-debt payment for three months — buying time with the creditors while paying wages at home.
