# The UDP economic package and dedollarisation, 1982 — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the lesson teach about democracy returns — the package?
**Answer:** The returning UDP government's package acted on the exchange rate, the currency, prices and the external debt.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the lesson teach about fixed rate restored — and the economy dedollarised?
**Answer:** The package restored a fixed exchange rate and prohibited dollar transactions — dedollarisation.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the lesson teach about prices raised, debt payment suspended?
**Answer:** The package raised prices to fund salaries and suspended external-debt payment for three months.
