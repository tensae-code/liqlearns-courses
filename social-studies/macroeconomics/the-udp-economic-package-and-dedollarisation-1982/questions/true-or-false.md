# The UDP economic package and dedollarisation, 1982 — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The package was decreed by a military junta in 1970.
**Answer: False** — it was the returning democratic UDP government's package.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The package made the dollar the official currency.
**Answer: False** — it prohibited dollar transactions — dedollarisation.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The package repaid the whole external debt at once.
**Answer: False** — it suspended payment for three months.
