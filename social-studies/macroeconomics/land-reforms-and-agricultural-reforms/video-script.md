# Video script — Land reforms and agricultural reforms

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Land reforms and agricultural reforms — as India's book teaches it, says the narrator: how India reformed its farm sector — institutional reforms after Independence (collectivisation, consolidation, cooperation, abolition of zamindari, First Plan land reform), the Green and White Revolutions, crop insurance, Grameen banks, Kisan Credit Cards, support prices, and Bhoodan–Gramdan.
