# Land reforms and agricultural reforms

## Sub lesson 1 — Institutional reforms after Independence

After Independence came **institutional reforms: collectivisation, consolidation of holdings, cooperation and abolition of zamindari**, with **land reform in the First Five Year Plan**.

## Sub lesson 2 — Green and White Revolutions; insurance, banks, cards, support prices

Then came the **Green and White Revolutions**, plus **crop insurance, Grameen banks and cooperative credit, Kisan Credit Cards and announced support prices** for farmers.

## Sub lesson 3 — Bhoodan–Gramdan — land given voluntarily

The **Bhoodan–Gramdan movement** was a **voluntary land gift** — landowners gave land to be shared with the landless.
