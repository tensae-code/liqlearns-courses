# Land reforms and agricultural reforms — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name two institutional reforms after Independence.
**Answer:** Any two of: collectivisation, consolidation of holdings, cooperation, abolition of zamindari.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name three supports named for farmers.
**Answer:** Any three of: crop insurance, Grameen banks/cooperative credit, Kisan Credit Cards, support prices.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What was Bhoodan–Gramdan?
**Answer:** A movement of voluntary land gifts to the landless.
