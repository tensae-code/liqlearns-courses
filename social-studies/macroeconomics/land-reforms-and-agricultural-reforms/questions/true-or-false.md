# Land reforms and agricultural reforms — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Zamindari was created after Independence.
**Answer: False — it was abolished as a reform.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The White Revolution is one of the reforms named.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Bhoodan seized land by force.
**Answer: False — it was a voluntary land gift.**
