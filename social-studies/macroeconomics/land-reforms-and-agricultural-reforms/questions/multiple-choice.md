# Land reforms and agricultural reforms — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Abolition of zamindari was —
a) an institutional reform after Independence  b) a Green Revolution input  c) a kind of seed
**Answer: a) an institutional reform after Independence**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Kisan Credit Cards give farmers —
a) credit  b) land titles abroad  c) free tractors
**Answer: a) credit**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Bhoodan–Gramdan was —
a) a voluntary land gift movement  b) a tax  c) a bank
**Answer: a) a voluntary land gift movement**
