# Exhaustion of Costa Rica's welfare-state model — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** By the mid-1970s the model weakened — its Central American Common Market was too small and was hit by the region's guerrilla _____, and the state had taken on regulator, benefactor and entrepreneur roles it could no longer sustain.
**Answer: violence**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This sub lesson is about _____.
**Answer: Agotamiento del modelo económico del Estado Benefactor (1950–1980)**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Imported second-hand machinery and imported raw materials and technology, external debt taken for roads, schools and ports, unprofitable CODESA firms _____ to keep than they earned, a large fiscal deficit covered by international loans —…
**Answer: costlier**
