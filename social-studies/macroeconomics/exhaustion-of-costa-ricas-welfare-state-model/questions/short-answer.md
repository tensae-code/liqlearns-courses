# Exhaustion of Costa Rica's welfare-state model — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the lesson teach about agotamiento del estado benefactor?
**Answer:** By the mid-1970s the model weakened — its Central American Common Market was too small and was hit by the region's guerrilla violence, and the state had taken on regulator, benefactor and entrepreneur roles it could no longer sustain.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the lesson teach about agotamiento del modelo económico del estado benefactor (1950–1980)?
**Answer:** The model ran out.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the lesson teach about agotamiento del modelo económico del estado benefactor (1950–1980) — more?
**Answer:** Imported second-hand machinery and imported raw materials and technology, external debt taken for roads, schools and ports, unprofitable CODESA firms costlier to keep than they earned, a large fiscal deficit covered by international loans — then OPEC raised crude from US$3 to US$9 a barrel, unemployment rose from 5% (1976) to 9.2% (1982), lenders stopped lending, and the colón collapsed from 8.6 to 45 per dollar in under six months.
