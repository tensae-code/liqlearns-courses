# The accelerator — how demand growth pulls investment — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Investment's double role"?
a) Investment is both part of aggregate demand and the grower of the capital stock, shifting the PPC outward.  b) Investment = accelerator coefficient × change in GDP; demand growth pulls a larger rise in capital-goods demand.  c) Investment swings harder than demand because it answers to demand's change, not its level.
**Answer: a) Investment is both part of aggregate demand and the grower of the capital stock, shifting the PPC outward.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "The accelerator principle"?
a) Investment swings harder than demand because it answers to demand's change, not its level.  b) Investment is both part of aggregate demand and the grower of the capital stock, shifting the PPC outward.  c) Investment = accelerator coefficient × change in GDP; demand growth pulls a larger rise in capital-goods demand.
**Answer: c) Investment = accelerator coefficient × change in GDP; demand growth pulls a larger rise in capital-goods demand.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Why investment swings"?
a) Investment = accelerator coefficient × change in GDP; demand growth pulls a larger rise in capital-goods demand.  b) Investment swings harder than demand because it answers to demand's change, not its level.  c) Investment is both part of aggregate demand and the grower of the capital stock, shifting the PPC outward.
