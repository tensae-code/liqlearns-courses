# The accelerator — how demand growth pulls investment — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Investment spending pays back fully within the same year.
**Answer: False — Revenue from capital investment arrives only over years — that delay is part of the point.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The accelerator says investment depends on the level of GDP, not its change.
**Answer: False — It depends on the CHANGE in output — that is why investment can fall while GDP is still high but no longer rising.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Investment is steadier than consumer demand.
**Answer: False — The accelerator makes investment MORE volatile: it follows the change in output, amplified by the coefficient.**
