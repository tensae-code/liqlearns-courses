# The accelerator — how demand growth pulls investment — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Investment's double role" in your own words.
**Answer:** Investment is both part of aggregate demand and the grower of the capital stock, shifting the PPC outward.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "The accelerator principle" in your own words.
**Answer:** Investment = accelerator coefficient × change in GDP; demand growth pulls a larger rise in capital-goods demand.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Why investment swings" in your own words.
**Answer:** Investment swings harder than demand because it answers to demand's change, not its level.
