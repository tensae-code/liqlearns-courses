# Lesson plan — Impacts of economic ideologies

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Macroeconomics · **Lesson:** Impacts of economic ideologies
**Place in the curriculum:** Macroeconomics (CURRICULUM.md) — built in the Economics expansion (Papua New Guinea, 2026-11-16).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Researching how conservatism, protectionism, liberalism and the free market each affect production, distribution and consumption, and world trade.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the Papua New Guinea Grade 11 Economics TG book's own treatment — Unit 3, Topics 4–5: Impacts of economic ideologies on production, distribution and consumption; Impacts of economic ideologies on world trade (printed p. 71). Teacher guide — evidence only.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of ..." in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.
