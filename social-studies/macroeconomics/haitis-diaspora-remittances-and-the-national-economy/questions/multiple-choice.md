# Haiti's diaspora remittances and the national economy — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In the Haitian book, the work on the diaspora begins by —
a) graphing prices  b) mapping where Haitians abroad live  c) reading a bank statement
**Answer: b) mapping where Haitians abroad live**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The diaspora's money transfers are studied —
a) region by region, from statistics  b) family by family, from letters  c) only in one city
**Answer: a) region by region, from statistics**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Press analysis read in the book calls the diaspora —
a) a burden on the economy  b) the Haitian economy's lifeline  c) a tourist attraction
**Answer: b) the Haitian economy's lifeline**
