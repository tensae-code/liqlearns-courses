# Haiti's diaspora remittances and the national economy — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The diaspora means Haitians living outside Haiti.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The foreign currency from diaspora transfers carries no weight in the country's accounts.
**Answer: False** — the book weighs the foreign currency from transfers in the country's accounts; the transfers are a pillar of the national economy.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Pupils build a graph of the diaspora's contributions to the national economy.
**Answer: True**
