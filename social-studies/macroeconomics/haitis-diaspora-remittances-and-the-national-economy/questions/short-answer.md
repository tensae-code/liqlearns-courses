# Haiti's diaspora remittances and the national economy — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why does the book map the diaspora before weighing its money?
**Answer:** Because the money follows the people — you cannot weigh what the diaspora sends home until you know where Haitians abroad live.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How are the transfers studied, and what is weighed in the country's accounts?
**Answer:** The transfers are studied from statistics, region by region, and the foreign currency they bring in is weighed in the country's accounts.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What do pupils build, and what claim does the press analysis make?
**Answer:** Pupils build a graph of the diaspora's contributions to the national economy, and the press analysis calls the diaspora the Haitian economy's lifeline.
