# Haiti's diaspora remittances and the national economy — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Haitians living abroad are called the _____ — Les Haïtiens de l'extérieur.
**Answer: diaspora**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Money sent home by Haitians abroad is studied as money _____.
**Answer: transfers**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Pupils build a _____ of the diaspora's contributions to the national economy.
**Answer: graph**
