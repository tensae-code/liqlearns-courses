Haiti's own way: the national economy is taught through the diaspora — pupils map where Haitians abroad live, then work from statistics on their money transfers, weigh the foreign currency they bring into the country's accounts, and graph the diaspora's contribution to the national economy. This lesson keeps that way: Haiti's own story of remittances as a pillar of the national economy. The lesson teaches the placement's content in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
