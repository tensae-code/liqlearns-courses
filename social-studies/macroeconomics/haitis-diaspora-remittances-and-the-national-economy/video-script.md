# Video script — Haiti's diaspora remittances and the national economy

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Haitians live and work all over the world. That is the diaspora — Les Haïtiens de l'extérieur, the Haitians outside. The Haitian book starts by mapping where they live, because the money follows the people.

Then it weighs the money: the transfers the diaspora sends home, studied region by region, and the foreign currency those transfers bring into the country's accounts. Weighed properly, the transfers are a pillar of the national economy.

Last, the pupils graph the diaspora's contribution — and read the press calling the diaspora Haiti's economic lifeline. Map the people, weigh the money, graph the contribution.
