# Hands-on — Haiti's diaspora remittances and the national economy

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Map it first
Draw a rough map and mark the places where Haitians abroad live, the book's way — the people first, because the money follows the people. Draw arrows from those places back to Haiti and label each arrow "transfers".

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's graph from memory — the diaspora's contribution to the national economy as one line — and label what the line shows. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
