# Hands-on — Disguised unemployment — employed, but adding nothing

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Team task with six learners on a job sized for three: finish it, then remove three members and redo it in the same time. The class applies the lesson's test to the result.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — six workers on a plot whose harvest stays the same when three step away — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
