# Disguised unemployment — employed, but adding nothing — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Employed, but redundant" in your own words.
**Answer:** Disguised unemployment is workers in redundant work that adds minimal or no productivity.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "The removal test" in your own words.
**Answer:** In disguised unemployment, removing some workers would not reduce output.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Hidden, and common where labour is surplus" in your own words.
**Answer:** Disguised unemployment does not show up as open joblessness and is very common in developing countries such as Ethiopia.
