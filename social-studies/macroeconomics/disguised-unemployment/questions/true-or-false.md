# Disguised unemployment — employed, but adding nothing — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Disguised unemployment is workers in redundant work that adds minimal or no productivity.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Disguised unemployment is workers in redundant work that adds minimal or no productivity.
**Answer: False** — that statement belongs to sub lesson 1 (Employed, but redundant); sub lesson 2 says: In disguised unemployment, removing some workers would not reduce output.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Disguised unemployment does not show up as open joblessness and is very common in developing countries such as Ethiopia.
**Answer: True**
