# Contractionary fiscal policy — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Demand above full-employment output creates an inflationary gap.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Cut public-works spending and raise taxes, especially on the rich.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** AD shifts back to potential GDP without damaging production and saving.
**Answer: True**
