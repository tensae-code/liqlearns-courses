# Contractionary fiscal policy — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "The diagnosis — an inflationary gap" in your own words.
**Answer:** Demand above full-employment output creates an inflationary gap.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "The withdrawal moves" in your own words.
**Answer:** Cut public-works spending and raise taxes, especially on the rich.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Landing on potential — carefully" in your own words.
**Answer:** AD shifts back to potential GDP without damaging production and saving.
