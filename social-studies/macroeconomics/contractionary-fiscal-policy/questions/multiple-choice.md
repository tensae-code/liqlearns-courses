# Contractionary fiscal policy — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "The diagnosis — an inflationary gap"?
a) Demand above full-employment output creates an inflationary gap  b) Cut public-works spending and raise taxes, especially on the rich  c) AD shifts back to potential GDP without damaging production and saving
**Answer: a) Demand above full-employment output creates an inflationary gap**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "The withdrawal moves"?
a) Cut public-works spending and raise taxes, especially on the rich  b) AD shifts back to potential GDP without damaging production and saving  c) Demand above full-employment output creates an inflationary gap
**Answer: a) Cut public-works spending and raise taxes, especially on the rich**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Landing on potential — carefully"?
a) AD shifts back to potential GDP without damaging production and saving  b) Demand above full-employment output creates an inflationary gap  c) Cut public-works spending and raise taxes, especially on the rich
**Answer: a) AD shifts back to potential GDP without damaging production and saving**
