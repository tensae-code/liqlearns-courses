# Hands-on — Contractionary fiscal policy

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Withdraw wisely
Choose a tax rise and a spending cut to close an inflationary gap. For each, name who loses purchasing power and argue the choice does not damage production or saving.

## Mirror drawing
Draw the inflationary gap (AD right of LRAS) and the contractionary shift back. Place it beside the expansionary drawing and write one sentence on what makes them mirrors.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
