# Contractionary fiscal policy

## Do it first

Demand is running ahead of what the economy can produce and prices are climbing. What does the government withdraw — and from whom?

## Sub lesson 1 — The diagnosis — an inflationary gap

When aggregate demand exceeds full-employment output, the result is an inflationary gap: too much spending chasing the economy's maximum real output, so the excess comes out as rising prices.

## Sub lesson 2 — The withdrawal moves

The government cuts expenditure on public works and raises taxes — especially on the rich — withdrawing purchasing power from the economy. Each birr withdrawn is a birr that cannot bid prices up.

## Sub lesson 3 — Landing on potential — carefully

AD shifts back to potential GDP, taking care the measures do not damage production and saving. Cool the demand, not the economy's capacity to produce — that care is the difference between a landing and a crash.
