---

Why do some countries grow rich while others stall? The Armenian-born economist Daron Acemoglu answers with a picture: a narrow corridor. On one side, a state too weak to keep order — that way lies anarchy, where no rule holds and nothing can be built. On the other side, a state too strong and unchecked — the authoritarian Leviathan, which can take and silence as it pleases.

Prosperity lives inside the corridor, where the state is strong enough to act and society is strong enough to balance it. That balance — not money, not machines — is what lets people invest, work and build.
