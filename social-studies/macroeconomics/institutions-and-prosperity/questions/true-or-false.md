# Institutions and prosperity — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Narrow Corridor is the balance between a state strong enough to act and a society strong enough to hold it in check.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A state that is too weak helps prosperity, because people are completely free.
**Answer: False — a state too weak to hold order brings anarchy, and prosperity cannot be built without working rules**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A very strong state always guarantees prosperity, however unchecked it is.
**Answer: False — a state too strong and unchecked becomes the authoritarian Leviathan, under which initiative and investment wither**
