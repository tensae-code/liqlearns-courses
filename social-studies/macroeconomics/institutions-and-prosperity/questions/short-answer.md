# Institutions and prosperity — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Whose frame is the Narrow Corridor, and what does it say prosperity depends on?
**Answer:** Daron Acemoglu's; prosperity depends on the balance between state and society.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What goes wrong when the state is too weak?
**Answer:** It cannot enforce laws, protect property or keep the peace — anarchy — so contracts mean little and investment stays away.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What goes wrong when the state is too strong?
**Answer:** Unchecked by society it becomes the authoritarian Leviathan, able to take and silence as it pleases, and people's initiative and investment wither.
