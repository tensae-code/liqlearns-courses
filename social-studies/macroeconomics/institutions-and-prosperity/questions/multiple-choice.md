# Institutions and prosperity — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In Acemoglu's "Narrow Corridor", as Armenia's book teaches it, prosperity depends on —
a) the balance between state and society  b) having no state at all  c) the size of the army
**Answer: a) the balance between state and society**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A state too weak to enforce laws or keep order brings —
a) faster growth  b) anarchy  c) the authoritarian Leviathan
**Answer: b) anarchy**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The authoritarian Leviathan is —
a) a state too strong and unchecked by society  b) a weak state  c) a trade agreement
**Answer: a) a state too strong and unchecked by society**
