# Hands-on — Institutions and prosperity

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Lay two books on the floor as the corridor's two walls — anarchy on the left, the authoritarian Leviathan on the right. Walk the corridor between them and say aloud, at each wall, what goes wrong when a country falls against it.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the corridor with its two cliffs labelled and a town inside it. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
