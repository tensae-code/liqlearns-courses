# Hands-on — How Colombia's land was organised by region

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Lay it out on the table
On paper, draw the lesson in three boxes — one for each sub lesson: A regional table of farm exploitation in early-20th-century; Great coffee haciendas consolidated after 1870 in cundinamarca; And the other regions' specific exploitations. Write one line of the lesson in each box, from memory, then open lesson/lesson.md, compare, and fix anything missing.

## Say it with things at home
Put three stones on the table — one for each sub lesson — and say what each stone stands for, in order, without looking. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 3 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes.
