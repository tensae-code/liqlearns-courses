# Early economic thought — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Early economic thought comes from a time when —
a) nobody had ever thought about wealth  b) economics had no name yet, but people already asked about wealth and trade  c) trade did not exist anywhere
**Answer: b) economics had no name yet, but people already asked about wealth and trade**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A school of thought is —
a) a group of thinkers sharing a way of explaining the economy  b) a tax office  c) a building where merchants meet
**Answer: a) a group of thinkers sharing a way of explaining the economy**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Why does the chapter start with the earliest schools?
a) later schools have no connection to earlier ones  b) the earliest schools are the only ones that matter  c) every later school answers a question an earlier one raised
**Answer: c) every later school answers a question an earlier one raised**
