# Early economic thought — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Early thought predates the name 'economics' but already asked about wealth and trade.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A school of thought is a building where merchants meet.
**Answer: False — A school of thought is a group of thinkers, not a building.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Later schools answer questions raised by earlier ones.
**Answer: True**
