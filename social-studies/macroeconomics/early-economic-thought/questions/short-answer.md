# Early economic thought — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What two questions did the earliest thinkers ask?
**Answer: Where a country's wealth comes from, and how trade should be run.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How did economics grow, according to this lesson?
**Answer: By argument — later schools answering and arguing against earlier ones.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name one later school this lesson prepares for.
**Answer: Classical (or Marxist, neoclassical, or Keynesian).**
