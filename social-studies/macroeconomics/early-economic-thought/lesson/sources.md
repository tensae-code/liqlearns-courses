**Acceptance count: taught in 1 country (Cambodia).**

Cambodia's own way (Cambodia Grade 11 Economics): a full academic course built around Cambodia's own transition — the market system is introduced, judged, and then located in Cambodia itself (its market-economy foundations, its national economy, its reform programme), and the book closes with the history of economic thought from the first schools to Keynes. This lesson is built from that book's placement: Ch. 3, Lesson 1: The first schools of thought — teaches: the earliest economic thinking, before economics had a name, and the first attempts to explain wealth and trade. The lesson teaches the placement's content in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
