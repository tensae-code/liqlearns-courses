# Video script — Early economic thought

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

People thought about wealth, trade and fair exchange long before anyone called the subject economics. The earliest thinkers asked where a country's wealth comes from and how trade should be run. Their answers were the first schools of thought — the ground the whole history chapter grows from.

A school of thought is a group of thinkers who share a way of explaining the economy. The first schools gave the answers of their age — wealth tied to treasure and trade balances in the earliest accounts — and later schools argued against them. Economics grew by this argument, school answering school.

The history chapter starts at the beginning for a reason: every later system — classical, Marxist, neoclassical, Keynesian — is an answer to a question an earlier school raised. A pupil who knows the first questions can hear what each later school is answering.
