# Macroeconomic goals revisited — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Inflation driven by rising production costs is _____ inflation; driven by demand, demand-pull.
**Answer: cost-push**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The drought-time idleness of farmers is _____ unemployment — its work vanishes with the rains.
**Answer: seasonal**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Optimisation is getting the best _____ outcome — not a perfect one.
**Answer: attainable**
