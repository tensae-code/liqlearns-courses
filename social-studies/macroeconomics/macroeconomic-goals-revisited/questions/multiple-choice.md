# Macroeconomic goals revisited — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Trade and prices — two goals"?
a) current-account surplus desirable; inflation as cost-push or demand-pull  b) growth desirable; unemployment in types including seasonal  c) optimisation as the best attainable outcome among competing goals
**Answer: a) current-account surplus desirable; inflation as cost-push or demand-pull**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Growth and jobs — two more"?
a) current-account surplus desirable; inflation as cost-push or demand-pull  b) optimisation as the best attainable outcome among competing goals  c) growth desirable; unemployment in types including seasonal
**Answer: c) growth desirable; unemployment in types including seasonal**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Optimisation — the best attainable"?
a) growth desirable; unemployment in types including seasonal  b) optimisation as the best attainable outcome among competing goals  c) current-account surplus desirable; inflation as cost-push or demand-pull
**Answer: b) optimisation as the best attainable outcome among competing goals**
