# Hands-on — Macroeconomic goals revisited

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Four dial cards on the wall — trade, prices, growth, jobs. Learners take turns moving one dial up and narrating what happens to the other three, until the class has told the drought-season story through all four dials.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — four goal dials — trade, prices, growth, jobs — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
