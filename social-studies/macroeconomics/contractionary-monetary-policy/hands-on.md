# Hands-on — Contractionary monetary policy

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Mirror the chain
Write the contractionary chain as six arrows, placed line by line under the expansionary chain from the previous lesson's work, each arrow the exact reverse.

## Overshoot guard
Output starts at 750, potential at 700. Argue in three sentences why tightening must stop at potential — and what the economy looks like if it does not.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
