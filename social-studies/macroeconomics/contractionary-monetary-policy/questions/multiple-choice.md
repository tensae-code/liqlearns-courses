# Contractionary monetary policy — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "All three levers toward tight"?
a) Raise the discount rate, sell securities, raise the reserve ratio — money supply falls  b) Dear money: borrowing costs rise and investment and consumption fall  c) The book's figure brings output from 750 down to the potential 700
**Answer: a) Raise the discount rate, sell securities, raise the reserve ratio — money supply falls**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Dear money"?
a) Dear money: borrowing costs rise and investment and consumption fall  b) The book's figure brings output from 750 down to the potential 700  c) Raise the discount rate, sell securities, raise the reserve ratio — money supply falls
**Answer: a) Dear money: borrowing costs rise and investment and consumption fall**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Output down to potential"?
a) The book's figure brings output from 750 down to the potential 700  b) Raise the discount rate, sell securities, raise the reserve ratio — money supply falls  c) Dear money: borrowing costs rise and investment and consumption fall
**Answer: a) The book's figure brings output from 750 down to the potential 700**
