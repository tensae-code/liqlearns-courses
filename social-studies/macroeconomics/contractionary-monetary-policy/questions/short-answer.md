# Contractionary monetary policy — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "All three levers toward tight" in your own words.
**Answer:** Raise the discount rate, sell securities, raise the reserve ratio — money supply falls.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Dear money" in your own words.
**Answer:** Dear money: borrowing costs rise and investment and consumption fall.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Output down to potential" in your own words.
**Answer:** The book's figure brings output from 750 down to the potential 700.
