# Contractionary monetary policy — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Raise the discount rate, sell securities, raise the reserve ratio — money supply falls.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Dear money: borrowing costs rise and investment and consumption fall.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The book's figure brings output from 750 down to the potential 700.
**Answer: True**
