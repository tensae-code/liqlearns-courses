# Economic planning in Pakistan

## Sub lesson 1 — Planning, the Planning Commission, objectives and strategy

**Economic planning** is the state deciding in advance what the economy should achieve and how. In Pakistan the **Planning Commission** carries it: the chapter teaches planning's meaning, the Commission, the **objectives of planning in Pakistan**, and its **strategy and implementation**.

## Sub lesson 2 — Problems of agriculture and industry — with solutions

The chapter then takes the **problems of the agricultural and industrial sectors of Pakistan**, each **with their solutions**: planning judged by whether it answers the real troubles of the farm and the factory.

## Sub lesson 3 — Development of industries

Planning's industrial arm is the **development of industries**: building the factory sector the plans aim at, as the chapter's closing movement from objectives to results.
