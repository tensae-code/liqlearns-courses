# Economic planning in Pakistan — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is economic planning, and who carries it in Pakistan?
**Answer:** The state deciding in advance what the economy should achieve and how; in Pakistan, the Planning Commission.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How does the chapter treat the sectors' problems?
**Answer:** It names the problems of Pakistan's agricultural and industrial sectors together with their solutions.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What is the chapter's closing industrial theme?
**Answer:** The development of industries — building the factory sector the plans aim at.
