# Economic planning in Pakistan — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Pakistan has no planning body.
**Answer: False** — the Planning Commission is named as its carrier.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The chapter names problems but offers no solutions.
**Answer: False** — solutions are taught beside the problems.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The plans aim to close Pakistan's industries.
**Answer: False** — they aim at industries' development.
