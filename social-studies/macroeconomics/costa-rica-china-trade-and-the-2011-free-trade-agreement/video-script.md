# Video script — Costa Rica–China trade and the 2011 free trade agreement

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

After breaking with Taiwan in 2007 Costa Rica's exports to China passed 15% of all goods exports, concentrated in integrated circuits and electronics;.

The 2011 Free Trade Agreement promotes tilapia, shrimp, flowers, cacao, chocolate and rum out, and textile inputs, technology and footwear in.

China also donated the National Stadium.
