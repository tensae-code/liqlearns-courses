# Unequal land distribution — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How many of Palampur's families own no land?
**Answer:** 150 of the 450 families are landless.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How many families cultivate under 2 hectares, and who holds most land?
**Answer:** 240 families cultivate under 2 hectares; 60 medium and large farmers hold most of the land.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What happened to Gobind's 2.25 hectares?
**Answer:** It split into 0.75-hectare plots for his three sons — fragmentation.
