# Unequal land distribution — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** No family in Palampur is landless.
**Answer: False — 150 of 450 families are landless.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The 240 small families hold most of the land.
**Answer: False — the 60 medium and large farmers hold most of it.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Gobind's sons each received 2.25 hectares.
**Answer: False — each received a 0.75-hectare plot.**
