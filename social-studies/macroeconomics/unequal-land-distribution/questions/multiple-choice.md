# Unequal land distribution — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Of Palampur's 450 families, how many are landless?
a) 150  b) 60  c) 240
**Answer: a) 150**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Most of Palampur's land is held by —
a) 60 medium and large farmers  b) the 150 landless families  c) no one
**Answer: a) 60 medium and large farmers**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Gobind's 2.25 hectares split into plots of —
a) 0.75 hectares for each of three sons  b) 2 hectares each  c) 10 hectares each
**Answer: a) 0.75 hectares for each of three sons**
