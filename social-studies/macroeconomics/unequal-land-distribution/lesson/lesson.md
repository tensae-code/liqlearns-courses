# Unequal land distribution

## Do it first

Before you read on, write what you already think this lesson's title means — unequal land distribution — in one sentence of your own. Keep it; you will check it against the lesson at the end.

## Sub lesson 1 — 150 of 450 families are landless

In Palampur, of **450 families, 150 are landless** — they own no land at all.

## Sub lesson 2 — 240 families cultivate under 2 hectares; 60 hold most land

**240 families cultivate under 2 hectares** each, while **60 medium and large farmers hold most of the land**.

## Sub lesson 3 — Fragmentation — Gobind's plots split among three sons

When land passes down it **fragments**: Gobind's **2.25 hectares split into 0.75-hectare plots for his three sons**.

## Say it to your companion

Explain unequal land distribution to your companion in your own words, part by part, from the 3 sub lessons. Your companion asks you one hard question about it — answer from the lesson.
