# Global interdependence and the world trade chain — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** About intercambio de materias primas y manufacturas a escala global — the lesson teaches that —
a) World trade divides functions between countries.
b) Raw-material providers, transporters, transformers, consumers and logistics.
c) "interdependencia mundial": if Brazil cannot sell wood, Europe cannot build the furniture it sells to China, and a whole chain of countries loses.
**Answer: a) World trade divides functions between countries.**
