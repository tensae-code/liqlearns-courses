# Global interdependence and the world trade chain — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** World trade divides _____ between countries.
**Answer: functions**

**Answer: transformers**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** "interdependencia mundial": if Brazil cannot sell wood, Europe cannot build the _____ it sells to China, and a whole chain of countries loses.
