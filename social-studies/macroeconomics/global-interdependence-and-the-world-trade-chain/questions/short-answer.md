# Global interdependence and the world trade chain — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the lesson teach about intercambio de materias primas y manufacturas a escala global?
**Answer:** World trade divides functions between countries.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the lesson teach about intercambio de materias primas y manufacturas a escala global — more?
**Answer:** Raw-material providers, transporters, transformers, consumers and logistics.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the lesson teach about intercambio de materias primas y manufacturas a escala global — more — more?
**Answer:** "interdependencia mundial": if Brazil cannot sell wood, Europe cannot build the furniture it sells to China, and a whole chain of countries loses.
