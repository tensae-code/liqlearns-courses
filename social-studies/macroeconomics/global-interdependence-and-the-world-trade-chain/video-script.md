# Video script — Global interdependence and the world trade chain

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

World trade divides functions between countries.

Raw-material providers, transporters, transformers, consumers and logistics.

"interdependencia mundial": if Brazil cannot sell wood, Europe cannot build the furniture it sells to China, and a whole chain of countries loses.
