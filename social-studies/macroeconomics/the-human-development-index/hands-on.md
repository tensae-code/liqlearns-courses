# Hands-on — The Human Development Index

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Score your street
Score your own neighbourhood 0 to 1 on health, schooling and income, from what you can see. Then say which of the three drags the combined score down most, and why.

## Draw it and check it
Draw three dials — health, education, income — feeding one gauge marked HDI. Label each dial, then check your drawing against the lesson.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
