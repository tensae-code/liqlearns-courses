# The Human Development Index — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Three parts to a good life" in your own words.
**Answer:** The HDI combines health, education and income into one quality-of-life index.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Why income alone is not enough" in your own words.
**Answer:** The HDI exists so development is not read off GDP alone.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Reading the index" in your own words.
**Answer:** Ethiopia's 2019 HDI was 0.485, ranked 173 of 189.
