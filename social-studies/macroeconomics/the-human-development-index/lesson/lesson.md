# The Human Development Index

## Do it first

Two countries have the same income per person. In one, children finish school and people live long; in the other, many leave school early and die young. Are the two countries equally developed? Say your answer first.

## Sub lesson 1 — Three parts to a good life

The Human Development Index, the HDI, measures quality of life, not income alone. It combines three parts: health, education and income. A country can only score high when all three move together.

[Picture 25201 — three dials feeding one HDI gauge]

## Sub lesson 2 — Why income alone is not enough

GDP per person says nothing about how long people live or what they learn. The HDI was built so a country's development is not read off GDP alone: a rich country with sick, unschooled people is not fully developed.

## Sub lesson 3 — Reading the index

The HDI gives one number between 0 and 1. Higher means people live longer, learn more and earn more together. Ethiopia's 2019 value, 0.485, ranked 173 of 189 — low human development, a figure the poverty unit returns to.
