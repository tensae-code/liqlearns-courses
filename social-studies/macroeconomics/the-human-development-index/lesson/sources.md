Nepal's own way (Nepal Grade 12 Social Studies & Life Skills): Grade 12 economics is the development unit with real tables — sector shares of GDP, the trade growth and direction tables, Nepal's growth rates through the COVID year, the HDI bands, and Nurkse's vicious circle drawn as two loops, supply side and demand side.

Armenia's own way (Armenia Grade 11 Social Science student): Grade 11 macroeconomics is anchored in Armenian official statistics — learners compute with the Statistical Committee's data, meet the 425-item consumer basket Armenia uses for inflation, the Central Bank's 3% ±1 inflation target, and the Armenian-born Nobel laureate Daron Acemoglu's "Narrow Corridor" on institutions and prosperity.

Vietnam's own way (Vietnam Grade 12 Economic & Legal Education, Study Topics): integration is taught as national history with milestones (Đổi mới 1986, ASEAN 1995, APEC 1998, WTO 2007) and the CPTPP as the worked case, and development is measured with Vietnam's own HDI, income and Gini figures.

Vietnam's own way (Vietnam Grade 12 Economic & Legal Education): the macro course is built on Vietnam's national accounts — the expenditure formula GDP = C + I + G + (X − M), GDP/GNI tables against China and Singapore, the 8.12% growth of 2022, sector-share charts 2018 vs 2022, and the 50/30/20-style family budget rule (50% essentials / 20% non-essentials / 30% saving, investment and reserve).

Dominica's own way: Economics lives inside the CSEC Principles of Business course — money, markets, banks, taxes, GDP and Caribbean problems (unemployment, migration, debt) are taught as the economics core of a business textbook, always through Caribbean cases (ECCB, CARICOM/CSME, sou-sou savings, the Barbados and Dominica sugar taxes). This lesson teaches the placement's content in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

Venezuela's own way (Venezuela Grade 8 Venezuela G8 Nuestra Historia Republicana SB Spanish): republican history is taught as an economic balance sheet — trade balances by partner, the debt share in state income, and wages paid in tokens redeemable only at the landlord's store. This lesson teaches the placements' content in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

Venezuela's own way (Venezuela Grade 11 Venezuela G11 Geografía de Venezuela SB Spanish): the senior geography book is an economic survey of the whole country — land, oil, industry by ownership form, commerce under a fair-prices law, and integration as the Bolivarian alternative to free trade.
