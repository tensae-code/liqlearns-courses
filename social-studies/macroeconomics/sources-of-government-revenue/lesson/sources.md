**Acceptance count: taught in 1 country (Uganda).**

Uganda's own way (Uganda Primary 5 Excel Standard Social Studies): a commercial cram-style textbook that teaches real public finance at Primary Five — PAYE, VAT, customs and excise, the Uganda Revenue Authority, smuggling, and surplus/balanced/deficit budgets — alongside barter, cowrie shells and the shilling; government revenue is taught there as the government's income, with taxation named its main source.

Uganda's own way (Uganda Primary 5 Home-Study Merged Package): the home-study version teaches the same Primary Five Economics core — where the government's money comes from and goes — but in guided self-study steps with an adult at home, starting from the work parents do to get money and the things families spend it on.

Uganda's own way (Uganda Senior 1 Entrepreneurship Textbook (NCDC Prototype)): an Entrepreneurship textbook that ends by teaching public finance — the Senior One learner meets government revenue, taxation and toll roads (the Entebbe Express Highway) as the last chapter of learning how businesses and the state fit together; revenue there belongs to the government and the local authorities together, and its sources are split into tax sources and non-tax sources.
