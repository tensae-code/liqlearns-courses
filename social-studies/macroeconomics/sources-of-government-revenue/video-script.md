# Video script — Sources of government revenue

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Where does a government's money come from? Uganda's books answer at Primary Five and again at Senior One. The money a government gets is its revenue; the money it spends is its expenditure — on social services, salaries and pensions, infrastructure, disaster relief and repaying its debts.

The main source is taxation — income tax, VAT, customs duty, excise duty. Around it stands the non-tax list: grants that are not paid back, loans that are, donations, fines, licence fees, tourism, selling minerals and electricity, earnings from government enterprises, the national lottery, rent and rates, and the tolls on a road like the Entebbe Express Highway.

One main source, and many smaller ones around it — that is the shape of government revenue.
