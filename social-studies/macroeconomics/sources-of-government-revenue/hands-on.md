# Hands-on — Sources of government revenue

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Tell the story of a government's money out loud, the family way the home-study book starts from: first where the money comes in — name the main source and at least five non-tax sources — then what it is spent on. Finish with the lesson's shape: one main source, many smaller ones around it.

## Draw it and check it
Draw two columns, REVENUE IN and EXPENDITURE OUT, and fill each side from memory. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare, and add every source or use you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
