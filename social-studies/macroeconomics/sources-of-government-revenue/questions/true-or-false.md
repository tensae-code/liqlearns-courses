# Sources of government revenue — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Expenditure is the money the government spends, as revenue is the money it gets or earns.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Taxes are a small, unimportant source that a government cannot plan on.
**Answer: False** — Uganda's books name taxation the main, most important source, charged on the country's own people and trade year after year.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Road and bridge tolls, the national lottery and earnings from government enterprises are all non-tax sources of revenue.
**Answer: True**
