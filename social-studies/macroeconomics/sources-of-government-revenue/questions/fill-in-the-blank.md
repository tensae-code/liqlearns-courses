# Sources of government revenue — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Government _____ is the government's income; government _____ is the money it spends.
**Answer: revenue; expenditure**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Among Uganda's tax sources are income tax, Value Added Tax (VAT), customs duty and _____ duty.
**Answer: excise**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A _____ is money paid as a punishment for breaking a rule or a law, and a _____ is a permission to do something, paid for.
**Answer: fine; licence**
