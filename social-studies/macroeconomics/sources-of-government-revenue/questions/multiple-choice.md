# Sources of government revenue — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Government revenue is —
a) the money a government spends  b) the government's income — all the money a government and local authorities receive to finance the country's sectors  c) one family's wages
**Answer: b) the government's income — all the money a government and local authorities receive to finance the country's sectors**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The main source of government revenue in Uganda's books is —
a) the national lottery  b) tourism  c) taxation
**Answer: c) taxation**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A grant differs from a loan because a grant —
a) is money given to help the government, not to be paid back, while a loan is borrowed and repaid  b) is paid by drivers on a toll road  c) is a punishment for breaking a law
**Answer: a) is money given to help the government, not to be paid back, while a loan is borrowed and repaid**
