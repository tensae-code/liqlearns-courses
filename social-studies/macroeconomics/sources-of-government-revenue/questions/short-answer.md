# Sources of government revenue — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name three things Uganda's Senior One book says government revenue is used for.
**Answer:** Any three of: social services; public administration salaries and pensions; infrastructure; disaster relief; development schemes such as SACCO financing; modernising agriculture; repaying government debt.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why can a government plan on taxes more than on the other sources?
**Answer:** Because taxes are charged on the country's own people and trade, year after year — they keep coming, while the other sources come and go.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name five non-tax sources of government revenue from Uganda's books.
**Answer:** Any five of: grants, loans, donations and gifts, fines and penalties (including court fines), licences, fees, tourism, sale of minerals and electricity, agricultural exports, sale of parastatal bodies, earnings from government enterprises, the national lottery, rent and rates, road and bridge tolls.
