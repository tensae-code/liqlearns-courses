# Stages of economic integration — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Free trade area and customs union"?
a) A free trade area drops internal barriers; a customs union adds a common external trade policy.  b) A common market frees movement of labour and capital; workers need no visa inside it.  c) An economic union adopts common economic policies outright, as the European Union does.
**Answer: a) A free trade area drops internal barriers; a customs union adds a common external trade policy.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "The common market"?
a) An economic union adopts common economic policies outright, as the European Union does.  b) A free trade area drops internal barriers; a customs union adds a common external trade policy.  c) A common market frees movement of labour and capital; workers need no visa inside it.
**Answer: c) A common market frees movement of labour and capital; workers need no visa inside it.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "The economic union"?
a) A common market frees movement of labour and capital; workers need no visa inside it.  b) An economic union adopts common economic policies outright, as the European Union does.  c) A free trade area drops internal barriers; a customs union adds a common external trade policy.
**Answer: b) An economic union adopts common economic policies outright, as the European Union does.**
