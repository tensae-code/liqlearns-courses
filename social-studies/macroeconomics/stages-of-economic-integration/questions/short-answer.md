# Stages of economic integration — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Free trade area and customs union" in your own words.
**Answer:** A free trade area drops internal barriers; a customs union adds a common external trade policy.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "The common market" in your own words.
**Answer:** A common market frees movement of labour and capital; workers need no visa inside it.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "The economic union" in your own words.
**Answer:** An economic union adopts common economic policies outright, as the European Union does.
