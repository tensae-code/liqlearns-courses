# Stages of economic integration — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In a free trade area, members share one policy toward outsiders.
**Answer: False — That shared external policy is what makes a CUSTOMS union. Free-trade members keep their own.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In a common market, workers still need visas to work in member countries.
**Answer: False — Freedom of movement is the stage's defining addition — no visa or permit is needed inside it.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** An economic union is the loosest stage of integration.
**Answer: False — It is the DEEPEST stage — common economic policies outright, beyond even a common market.**
