# Video script — Stages of economic integration

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

From loosest to deepest: a free trade area removes barriers among members, who keep their own policies toward outsiders (NAFTA is the example). A customs union adds a common external trade policy — one shared face to the outside world.

A common market additionally frees movement of labour and capital, so workers need no visa or permit inside it. COMESA is the lesson's example of this stage.

Deepest of all, an economic union adopts common economic policies outright — the European Union is the example. Each step up the staircase sheds one more barrier and pools one more piece of national policy.
