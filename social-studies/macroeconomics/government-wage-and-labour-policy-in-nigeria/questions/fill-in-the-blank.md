# Government wage and labour policy in Nigeria — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Nigeria's government sets wage policy, including _____ wages.
**Answer: minimum**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Labour policy governs how workers are employed, protected and _____.
**Answer: organised**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Wage and labour policy shapes workers' _____ and industrial peace.
**Answer: incomes**
