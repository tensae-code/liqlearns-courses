# Government wage and labour policy in Nigeria — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** About nigeria's wage policy — the lesson teaches that —
a) the Nigerian government sets wage policy, including minimum wages, for its workers and the wider economy.  b) labour policy governs how workers in Nigeria are employed, protected and organised.  c) wage and labour policy shapes workers' incomes and industrial peace across Nigeria.
**Answer: a) the Nigerian government sets wage policy, including minimum wages, for its workers and the wider economy.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** About nigeria's labour policy — the lesson teaches that —
a) the Nigerian government sets wage policy, including minimum wages, for its workers and the wider economy.  b) labour policy governs how workers in Nigeria are employed, protected and organised.  c) wage and labour policy shapes workers' incomes and industrial peace across Nigeria.
**Answer: b) labour policy governs how workers in Nigeria are employed, protected and organised.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** About policy in practice — the lesson teaches that —
a) the Nigerian government sets wage policy, including minimum wages, for its workers and the wider economy.  b) labour policy governs how workers in Nigeria are employed, protected and organised.  c) wage and labour policy shapes workers' incomes and industrial peace across Nigeria.
**Answer: c) wage and labour policy shapes workers' incomes and industrial peace across Nigeria.**
