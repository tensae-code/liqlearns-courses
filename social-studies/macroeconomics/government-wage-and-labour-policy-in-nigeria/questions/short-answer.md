# Government wage and labour policy in Nigeria — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does Nigeria's wage policy include?
**Answer:** The Nigerian government sets wage policy, including minimum wages, for its workers and the wider economy.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does labour policy govern?
**Answer:** Labour policy governs how workers in Nigeria are employed, protected and organised.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does wage and labour policy shape in practice?
**Answer:** Wage and labour policy shapes workers' incomes and industrial peace across Nigeria.
