# Government wage and labour policy in Nigeria — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Nigeria's government never sets any wage policy.
**Answer: False** — The Nigerian government sets wage policy, including minimum wages, for its workers and the wider economy.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Labour policy governs only employers, never workers.
**Answer: False** — Labour policy governs how workers in Nigeria are employed, protected and organised.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Wage policy has no effect on workers' incomes.
**Answer: False** — Wage and labour policy shapes workers' incomes and industrial peace across Nigeria.
