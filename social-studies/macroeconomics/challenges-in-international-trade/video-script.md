# Video script — Challenges in international trade

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The types of challenges encountered in international trade and how a trading country works around them, assessed through the challenges faced in exports and imports.

Papua New Guinea's own way of teaching this, from the Papua New Guinea Grade 8 Social Science TG: Grade 8 Trade is entirely international trade — PNG's raw-material exports (gold, copper, gas, oil, copra, cocoa, coffee, palm oil) exchanged for Japanese machinery and vehicles, the challenges of trading, and the PNG authorities (IPA, Customs, NAQIA) that police the trade procedures.

Putting it together: the types of challenges encountered in international trade and how a trading country works around them, assessed through the challenges faced in exports and imports — taught in Economic Activities unit, Lesson 131 "Challenges in International Trade" (KSAV) (Papua New Guinea Grade 8 Social Science TG, printed p. 87).
