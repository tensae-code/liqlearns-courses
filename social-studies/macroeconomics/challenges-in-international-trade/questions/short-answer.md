# Challenges in international trade — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the lesson teach in "The types of challenges encountered in international trade"?
**Answer:** The types of challenges encountered in international trade and how a trading country works around them, assessed through the challenges faced in exports and imports.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the lesson teach in "Papua New Guinea's own way of teaching this"?
**Answer:** Papua New Guinea's own way of teaching this, from the Papua New Guinea Grade 8 Social Science TG: Grade 8 Trade is entirely international trade — PNG's raw-material exports (gold, copper, gas, oil, copra, cocoa, coffee, palm oil) exchanged for Japanese machinery and vehicles, the challenges of trading, and the PNG authorities (IPA, Customs, NAQIA) that police the trade procedures.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the lesson teach in "Putting it together: the types of challenges encountered"?
**Answer:** Putting it together: the types of challenges encountered in international trade and how a trading country works around them, assessed through the challenges faced in exports and imports — taught in Economic Activities unit, Lesson 131 "Challenges in International Trade" (KSAV) (Papua New Guinea Grade 8 Social Science TG, printed p. 87).
