# International cooperation and development finance — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which three organisms does the Grade 7 book name as lenders to Honduras?
a) IDB, World Bank and IMF  b) MCCA, CARICOM and CELAC  c) SAR, INE and BCH
**Answer: a) IDB, World Bank and IMF**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** IMF loans come with —
a) no conditions at all  b) supervision of the country's economic policy  c) a ban on all trade
**Answer: b) supervision of the country's economic policy**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Debt forgiveness (condonación) means a country is —
a) forced to borrow more  b) exempted from repaying a debt, in whole or in part  c) given a new currency
**Answer: b) exempted from repaying a debt, in whole or in part**
