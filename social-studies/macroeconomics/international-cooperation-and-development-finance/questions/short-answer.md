# International cooperation and development finance — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Who lends to Honduras, as the books teach it?
**Answer:** The international cooperation and financing organisms — the IDB, the World Bank and the IMF; the Grade 9 book adds that these organisms, directed by the rich countries, were created to finance underdeveloped countries.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What comes with IMF loans?
**Answer:** Supervision of the country's economic policy — and, since the 1990s, structural-adjustment plans (PAE) shaping the economy.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What two ways does outside money reach a country besides ordinary loans?
**Answer:** Debt forgiveness (condonación), which exempts a country from repaying in whole or part, and foreign investment eased by laws removing tariff barriers on imports.
