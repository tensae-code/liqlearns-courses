# International cooperation and development finance — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The World Bank lends only to rich countries.
**Answer: False** — The books teach that these organisms were created to finance underdeveloped countries, and the World Bank lends to countries with balance-of-payments problems.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** IMF loans come with no supervision of economic policy.
**Answer: False** — The Grade 7 book teaches that IMF loans come with supervision of economic policy.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Condonación means a debt must be repaid twice.
**Answer: False** — It exempts a country from repaying, in whole or in part.
