**Acceptance count: taught in 1 country (Honduras).**

Honduras's own way (Honduras Grade 7 G7 Student CCSS): Grade 7 carries the set's first real Economics block — it starts from the community economy and the Banco Central's own activity classification, then builds Honduras's national story: sectors, formal/informal work (70.8% informal per INE), indicators, poverty, globalisation and the foreign lenders behind structural adjustment.

Honduras's own way (Honduras Grade 8 G8 Student CCSS): Grade 8 teaches Latin America's development problem head-on — development vs underdevelopment, the integration ladder, and the debt lesson built around Honduras's own fiscal-deficit chart and the structural-adjustment era.

Honduras's own way (Honduras Grade 9 G9 Student CCSS): Grade 9 closes the cycle with the world economy and Honduras inside it — work formal and informal, the lempira's devaluation named the country's biggest problem, and remittances weighed honestly: a strong boost to the economy that also demotivates production and drains the labour force. This lesson is built from these books' placements: Grade 7 Lesson 38 (printed p. 236), Grade 8 Lesson 36 (printed p. 222) and Grade 9 Lesson 35 (printed p. 220). The lesson teaches the placements' content in the course's own words — the books' texts themselves are never transcribed. Checked first: no existing lesson covers this thing — the lessons on foreign investment and on the balance of payments teach single flows, not the lending organisms, their supervision and structural adjustment, and debt forgiveness as one system, so the filed NEW slug is built as filed.
