# Video script — International cooperation and development finance

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Some money crosses borders as loans to whole countries. Honduras's books name the lenders: the IDB, the World Bank and the IMF.

Their loans come with conditions — the IMF supervises economic policy, and structural-adjustment plans have shaped Honduras since the 1990s.

There is also a release valve — debt forgiveness, condonación — and governments open a second door, passing laws that ease foreign investment.
