## Sub lesson 1 — Food security and water security

The lesson names **food security** and **water security** among the Gulf's economic challenges — a state must be able to feed its people and secure its water.

## Sub lesson 2 — Green energy — and the risk of depending on oil

Against the challenges the book sets **green energy**, and names **the risk of depending on oil as the main income source** — the same dependence the energy lesson flagged as the basic problem.

## Sub lesson 3 — The small-trader / shareholder project

The lesson closes with a project: the **small-trader / shareholder project**, where the learner takes the trader's and shareholder's place in the economy the book has described.
