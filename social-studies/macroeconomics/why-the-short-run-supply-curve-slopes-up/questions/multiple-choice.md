# Why the short-run supply curve slopes up — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Sticky wages — the book's numbers"?
a) With the wage stuck at $30, a price fall from 1.50 to 1.25 raises the real wage from $20 to $24 and employment falls from 1,000 to 800  b) Workers may misperceive a nominal wage rise as a real one and supply more labour  c) In the long run supply is vertical at potential output because wages and expectations fully adjust
**Answer: a) With the wage stuck at $30, a price fall from 1.50 to 1.25 raises the real wage from $20 to $24 and employment falls from 1,000 to 800**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Worker misperception"?
a) Workers may misperceive a nominal wage rise as a real one and supply more labour  b) In the long run supply is vertical at potential output because wages and expectations fully adjust  c) With the wage stuck at $30, a price fall from 1.50 to 1.25 raises the real wage from $20 to $24 and employment falls from 1,000 to 800
**Answer: a) Workers may misperceive a nominal wage rise as a real one and supply more labour**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Why it ends — the vertical long run"?
a) In the long run supply is vertical at potential output because wages and expectations fully adjust  b) With the wage stuck at $30, a price fall from 1.50 to 1.25 raises the real wage from $20 to $24 and employment falls from 1,000 to 800  c) Workers may misperceive a nominal wage rise as a real one and supply more labour
**Answer: a) In the long run supply is vertical at potential output because wages and expectations fully adjust**
