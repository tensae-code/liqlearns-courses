# Why the short-run supply curve slopes up — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Sticky wages — the book's numbers" in your own words.
**Answer:** With the wage stuck at $30, a price fall from 1.50 to 1.25 raises the real wage from $20 to $24 and employment falls from 1,000 to 800.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Worker misperception" in your own words.
**Answer:** Workers may misperceive a nominal wage rise as a real one and supply more labour.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Why it ends — the vertical long run" in your own words.
**Answer:** In the long run supply is vertical at potential output because wages and expectations fully adjust.
