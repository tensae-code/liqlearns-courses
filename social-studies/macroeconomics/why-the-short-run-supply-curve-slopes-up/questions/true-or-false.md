# Why the short-run supply curve slopes up — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** With the wage stuck at $30, a price fall from 1.50 to 1.25 raises the real wage from $20 to $24 and employment falls from 1,000 to 800.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Workers may misperceive a nominal wage rise as a real one and supply more labour.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In the long run supply is vertical at potential output because wages and expectations fully adjust.
**Answer: True**
