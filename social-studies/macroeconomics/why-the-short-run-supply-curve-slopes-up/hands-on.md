# Hands-on — Why the short-run supply curve slopes up

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Recompute the example
Redo the book's arithmetic yourself: real wage at price index 1.50 and at 1.25 with a $30 wage. Then run it backwards — prices rising to 1.80 — and say what happens to the real wage and employment.

## Two stories, one slope
Explain the same upward slope twice: once with sticky wages, once with misperception, in three sentences each, without mixing the stories.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
