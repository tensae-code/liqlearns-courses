# Hands-on — Cambodia's market economy — foundations

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Work it with real things
Take paper and a pencil and work through sub lesson 1 (A market economy founded in Cambodia) with a real Cambodian example you can point to — a family business, a market stall, or a price you paid this week. Write the example down in the lesson's own terms.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson as three boxes, one for each sub lesson — A market economy founded in Cambodia; What the foundations are; Why the foundations matter for Cambodia — with one picture or example in each box. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare, and fix every box you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 2 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
