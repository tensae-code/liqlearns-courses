# Video script — Cambodia's market economy — foundations

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The market system in Cambodia is not presented as an abstract idea from abroad. The book teaches how Cambodia's own market economy was founded — the country turned to a system where goods are produced and exchanged through markets, prices and private business, and the lesson tells that founding as Cambodia's own story.

A market economy rests on foundations: people may own property, start businesses, and buy and sell freely; prices are formed in markets; and buyers and sellers meet and compete. Where these foundations stand, the market system can work; where one is missing, the whole system leans. Cambodia's lesson names the foundations its own economy rests on.

Knowing the foundations lets a pupil judge Cambodia's economy part by part: is ownership secure, can a business start, do prices move freely? The rest of the Grade 11 book builds on this lesson — private ownership and freedom of choice come next, then Cambodia's economy as a whole and its reform programme.
