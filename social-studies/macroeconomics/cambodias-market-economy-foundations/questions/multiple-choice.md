# Cambodia's market economy — foundations — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How does the book present Cambodia's market economy?
a) as an idea that never reached Cambodia  b) as Cambodia's own founded system, told as the country's story  c) as a system with no history at all
**Answer: b) as Cambodia's own founded system, told as the country's story**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does a market economy rest on?
a) private ownership, free buying and selling, and prices formed in markets  b) one fixed price for every good forever  c) a ban on private business
**Answer: a) private ownership, free buying and selling, and prices formed in markets**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What can a pupil judge once the foundations are known?
a) only other countries' economies  b) nothing — the foundations are only history  c) Cambodia's economy part by part — ownership, starting a business, free prices
**Answer: c) Cambodia's economy part by part — ownership, starting a business, free prices**
