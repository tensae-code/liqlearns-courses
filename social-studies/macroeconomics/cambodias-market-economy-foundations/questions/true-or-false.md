# Cambodia's market economy — foundations — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The book tells the founding of the market economy as Cambodia's own story.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A market economy rests on a ban on private business.
**Answer: False — A market economy does not ban private business — private business is one of its foundations.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The foundations let pupils judge Cambodia's own economy part by part.
**Answer: True**
