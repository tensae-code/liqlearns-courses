# Cambodia's market economy — foundations — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is a market economy, in the terms of this lesson?
**Answer: A system where goods are produced and exchanged through markets, prices and private business.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What happens where one foundation is missing?
**Answer: The whole system leans — the market cannot work as it should.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name one foundation question a pupil can ask about Cambodia's economy.
**Answer: Is ownership secure? (Or: can a business start? Or: do prices move freely?)**
