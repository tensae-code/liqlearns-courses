# Tax revenue and non-tax revenue — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Government revenue (rajaswa) splits in two: tax revenue, and non-tax revenue from _____, fees, fines and penalties, heirless property and public assets.
**Answer: gifts and grants**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Value added tax is charged at _____ on the value added to goods and services, and excise (antahshulka) is charged on goods produced or traded inside the country.
**Answer: 13 percent**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Customs duty (bhansar mahasul) is _____ under law and is an important source of revenue, and the federal, provincial and local governments are each empowered to levy their own taxes.
**Answer: collected at the border point**
