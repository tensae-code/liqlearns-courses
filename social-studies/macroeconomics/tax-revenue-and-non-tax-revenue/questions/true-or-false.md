# Tax revenue and non-tax revenue — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Government revenue (rajaswa) splits in two: tax revenue, and non-tax revenue from gifts and grants, fees, fines and penalties, heirless property and public assets.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Value added tax is charged at 13 percent on the value added to goods and services, and excise (antahshulka) is charged on goods produced or traded inside the country.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Customs duty (bhansar mahasul) is something else entirely under law and is an important source of revenue, and the federal, provincial and local governments are each empowered to levy their own taxes.
**Answer: False** — Customs duty (bhansar mahasul) is collected at the border point under law and is an important source of revenue, and the federal, provincial and local governments are each empowered to levy their own taxes.
