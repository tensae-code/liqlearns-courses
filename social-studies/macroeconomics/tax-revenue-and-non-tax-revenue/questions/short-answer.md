# Tax revenue and non-tax revenue — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the book teach about revenue split in two?
**Answer:** Government revenue (rajaswa) splits in two: tax revenue, and non-tax revenue from gifts and grants, fees, fines and penalties, heirless property and public assets.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the book teach about vat and excise?
**Answer:** Value added tax is charged at 13 percent on the value added to goods and services, and excise (antahshulka) is charged on goods produced or traded inside the country.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the book teach about customs duty and who may tax?
**Answer:** Customs duty (bhansar mahasul) is collected at the border point under law and is an important source of revenue, and the federal, provincial and local governments are each empowered to levy their own taxes.
