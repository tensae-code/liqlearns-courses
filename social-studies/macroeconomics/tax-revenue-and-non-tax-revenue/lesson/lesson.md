# Tax revenue and non-tax revenue

## Do it first

Think of this topic the way the Nepal book opens it — Tax revenue and non-tax revenue. What do you already know about it before you read on?

## Sub lesson 1 — Revenue split in two

Government revenue (rajaswa) splits in two: tax revenue, and non-tax revenue from gifts and grants, fees, fines and penalties, heirless property and public assets.

## Sub lesson 2 — VAT and excise

Value added tax is charged at 13 percent on the value added to goods and services, and excise (antahshulka) is charged on goods produced or traded inside the country.

## Sub lesson 3 — Customs duty and who may tax

Customs duty (bhansar mahasul) is collected at the border point under law and is an important source of revenue, and the federal, provincial and local governments are each empowered to levy their own taxes.
