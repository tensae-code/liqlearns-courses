The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

To control cheap raw materials and win juicy super-profits in colonial and semicolonial areas.

Recover the capital they had invested in about fourteen years, from the guaranteed interest alone.

Because the guaranteed 7% interest was calculated on the stated capital — the increase was also covered by the guarantee, so a bigger figure meant bigger guaranteed payments.
