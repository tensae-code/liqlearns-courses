# Foreign railway capital and the guaranteed seven percent — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In the imperialism phase the great powers exported —
a) their capital surpluses to colonial and semicolonial areas  b) their railways' workers  c) their farm surpluses only
**Answer: a) their capital surpluses to colonial and semicolonial areas**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The State guaranteed foreign railway companies —
a) 7% annual interest on the capital invested  b) 1% annual interest  c) no return at all
**Answer: a) 7% annual interest on the capital invested**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Railway companies frequently —
a) inflated the initial capital, because the increase was also covered by the guaranteed interest  b) understated their capital to pay less tax  c) refused the guarantee
**Answer: a) inflated the initial capital, because the increase was also covered by the guaranteed interest**
