# Foreign railway capital and the guaranteed seven percent — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Foreign railway capital came to Venezuela in the last quarter of the 19th century.
**Answer: True** — in capitalism's economic-imperialism phase.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The guaranteed interest was paid on the capital the companies invested.
**Answer: True** — 7% annually, guaranteed by the State.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Inflating the initial capital raised the guaranteed interest a company received.
**Answer: True** — the guarantee was a percentage of the stated capital, increase included.
