# Foreign railway capital and the guaranteed seven percent — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why did the great powers export their capital surpluses?
**Answer:** To control cheap raw materials and win juicy super-profits in colonial and semicolonial areas.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What did the 7% guarantee let the railway companies do, and in how long?
**Answer:** Recover the capital they had invested in about fourteen years, from the guaranteed interest alone.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Why did companies inflate their initial capital?
**Answer:** Because the guaranteed 7% interest was calculated on the stated capital — the increase was also covered by the guarantee, so a bigger figure meant bigger guaranteed payments.
