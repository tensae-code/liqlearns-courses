# The 1929 crisis in Latin America and Bolivia — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Latin America was untouched by the 1929 crisis.
**Answer: False** — the book names capital flight and falling export prices as its arrival.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Bolivia's mineral exports rose through the crisis.
**Answer: False** — falling imports abroad depressed mineral consumption and prices.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Recovery came only after governments did nothing for a decade.
**Answer: False** — the book credits proactive public policies.
