# The 1929 crisis in Latin America and Bolivia — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the lesson teach about latin America — capital flight and collapsing export prices?
**Answer:** In Latin America the 1929 crisis brought capital flight and an abrupt fall in export prices.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the lesson teach about bolivia — minerals unsold, credit suspended?
**Answer:** In Bolivia mineral demand and prices fell, external credit was suspended, and capital left to service the external debt.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the lesson teach about a relatively quick recovery — public policies?
**Answer:** Latin America's recovery was relatively quick thanks to proactive public policies.
