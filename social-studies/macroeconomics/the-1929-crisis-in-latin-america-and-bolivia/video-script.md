# Video script — The 1929 crisis in Latin America and Bolivia

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The 1929 crisis in Latin America and Bolivia — as Bolivia's book teaches it, says the narrator: in Latin America the 1929 crisis brought capital flight and an abrupt fall in export prices; in Bolivia mineral demand and prices fell, external credit was suspended, and capital left to service the external debt; Latin America's recovery was relatively quick thanks to proactive public policies.
