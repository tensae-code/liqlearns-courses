# Video script — Cambodia's economy

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Most lessons take one part of an economy. This one takes Cambodia's economy whole: what the country produces, sector by sector, and how the parts fit into one national story. A pupil who has met sectors, markets and national product separately now meets them together, on home ground.

The story runs through Cambodia's sectors — the farming that employs and feeds the country, the industry of its factories, and its services — and then judges the economy's condition: where it is strong, where it is weak, and what its people earn and lack. Condition is read from the real economy, not wished into being.

A whole-economy story ends facing forward: what direction is Cambodia's economy moving in, and what would move it faster or hold it back? The lesson's direction part prepares the book's later lessons — the state's role, national income, and Cambodia's own reform and development programme.
