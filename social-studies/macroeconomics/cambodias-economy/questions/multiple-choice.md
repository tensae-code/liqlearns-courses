# Cambodia's economy — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson take as its subject?
a) one single shop in one market  b) Cambodia's national economy as a whole  c) another country's economy only
**Answer: b) Cambodia's national economy as a whole**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The lesson judges the economy's condition from —
a) the real economy — its sectors, earnings and lacks  b) foreign newspapers alone  c) what people wish were true
**Answer: a) the real economy — its sectors, earnings and lacks**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the lesson's last part ask about Cambodia's economy?
a) nothing about the future at all  b) only what happened a century ago  c) what direction it is moving in, and what would speed or hold it
**Answer: c) what direction it is moving in, and what would speed or hold it**
