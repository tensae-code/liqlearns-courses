# Cambodia's economy — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why study the economy whole after studying its parts?
**Answer: So the parts — sectors, markets, national product — can be seen fitting together on home ground.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the three broad sectors the story runs through.
**Answer: Farming (agriculture), industry, and services.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which later lesson does the direction part prepare for?
**Answer: Cambodia's reform and development programme (and the lessons on the state's role and national income).**
