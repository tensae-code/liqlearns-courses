# The role of the state in the economy — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The state makes the laws business runs under, provides public services, collects _____ and spends.
**Answer: taxes**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The state's role is needed because the market does not do _____.
**Answer: everything**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The market organises most production and exchange; the state sets the _____.
**Answer: frame**
