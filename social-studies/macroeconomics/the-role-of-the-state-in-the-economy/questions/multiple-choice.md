# The role of the state in the economy — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which of these is part of the state's role in the economy?
a) selling every good in every shop itself  b) making laws, providing public services, collecting taxes and spending  c) banning all private business
**Answer: b) making laws, providing public services, collecting taxes and spending**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why is the state's role needed alongside the market?
a) the market does not do everything — some needs it leaves unmet  b) the state has no real jobs to do  c) the market can do literally everything alone
**Answer: a) the market does not do everything — some needs it leaves unmet**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The lesson's picture of state and market is —
a) the market replacing the state completely  b) the state replacing the market completely  c) the two together, each doing what it does best
**Answer: c) the two together, each doing what it does best**
