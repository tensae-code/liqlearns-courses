# The role of the state in the economy — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The state's role includes laws, public services, taxes and spending.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The state's role is needed because the market can do literally everything alone.
**Answer: False — The market cannot do everything alone — that is why the state's role is needed.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The picture is state and market together.
**Answer: True**
