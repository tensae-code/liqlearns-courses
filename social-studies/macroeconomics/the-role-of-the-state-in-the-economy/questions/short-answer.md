# The role of the state in the economy — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name two jobs the state does in the economy.
**Answer: Any two of: makes the laws business runs under; provides public services and works; collects taxes and spends; steps in where the market alone fails.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Give one thing the market alone will not provide.
**Answer: Goods everyone needs that cannot be sold one by one (public goods), or protection where market failures leave people unprotected.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Who answers for the whole economy's direction in this picture?
**Answer: The state.**
