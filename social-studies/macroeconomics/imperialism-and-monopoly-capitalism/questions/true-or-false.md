# Imperialism and monopoly capitalism — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Imperialism was driven by industrial powers' need for cheap raw materials and investment outlets.
**Answer: True** — that is the book's account of its rise.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Neocolonial pressure is imperialism without colonies, working through favourable laws.
**Answer: True** — the book names it alongside colonies as a way of guaranteeing supply.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** One businessman alone usually had enough capital for imperial expansion.
**Answer: False** — expansion needed more capital than one businessman has — hence fusing capital.
