# Imperialism and monopoly capitalism — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Imperialism arose at the end of the 19th century when industrial powers needed —
a) cheap raw materials abroad and places to invest large capitals  b) new sports and tourism  c) nothing in particular
**Answer: a) cheap raw materials abroad and places to invest large capitals**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Supply was guaranteed by exploiting Asia and Africa through —
a) colonies, or neocolonial pressure for favourable laws  b) fair trade agreements between equals  c) charity
**Answer: a) colonies, or neocolonial pressure for favourable laws**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Monopoly capitalism solves the capital problem by —
a) fusing the capital of several businessmen to finance transnational firms  b) borrowing from serfs  c) printing money
**Answer: a) fusing the capital of several businessmen to finance transnational firms**
