# Imperialism and monopoly capitalism — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Imperialism arose at the end of the _____ century.
**Answer: 19th**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Pressure for laws favouring monopolist firms, without formal rule, is called _____ pressure.
**Answer: neocolonial**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Economic imperialism is also called _____ capitalism.
**Answer: monopoly**
