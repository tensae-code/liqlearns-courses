# Imperialism and monopoly capitalism — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What two needs drove the rise of imperialism?
**Answer:** Cheap raw materials abroad, and places to invest large capitals.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the two ways the powers guaranteed their supply from Asia and Africa.
**Answer:** By colonies, or by neocolonial pressure for laws favouring their monopolist firms.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Why is economic imperialism also called monopoly capitalism?
**Answer:** Because expansion needs more capital than one businessman has, solved by fusing several businessmen's capital to finance transnational firms.
