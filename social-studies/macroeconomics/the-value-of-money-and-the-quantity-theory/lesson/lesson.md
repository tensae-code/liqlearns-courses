# The value of money and the quantity theory

## Do it first

## Sub lesson 1 — The value of money

The value of money is what money will buy — its purchasing power over goods and services.

## Sub lesson 2 — What changes the value of money

The value of money falls as prices rise and rises as prices fall — it moves opposite to the price level.

## Sub lesson 3 — The quantity theory

The quantity theory links the amount of money to the price level: more money chasing the same goods raises prices.
