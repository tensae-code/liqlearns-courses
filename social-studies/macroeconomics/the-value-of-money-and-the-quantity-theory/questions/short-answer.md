# The value of money and the quantity theory — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the value of money?
**Answer:** The value of money is what money will buy — its purchasing power over goods and services.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How does the value of money move with prices?
**Answer:** The value of money falls as prices rise and rises as prices fall — it moves opposite to the price level.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the quantity theory say?
**Answer:** The quantity theory links the amount of money to the price level: more money chasing the same goods raises prices.
