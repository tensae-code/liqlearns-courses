# The value of money and the quantity theory — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** About the value of money — the lesson teaches that —
a) the value of money is what money will buy — its purchasing power over goods and services.  b) the value of money falls as prices rise and rises as prices fall — it moves opposite to the price level.  c) the quantity theory links the amount of money to the price level: more money chasing the same goods raises prices.
