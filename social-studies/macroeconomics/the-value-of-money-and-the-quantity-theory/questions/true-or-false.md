# The value of money and the quantity theory — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The value of money is the metal a coin is made of.
**Answer: False** — The value of money is what money will buy — its purchasing power over goods and services.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The value of money rises whenever prices rise.
**Answer: False** — The value of money falls as prices rise and rises as prices fall — it moves opposite to the price level.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The quantity theory says the amount of money never affects prices.
**Answer: False** — The quantity theory links the amount of money to the price level: more money chasing the same goods raises prices.
