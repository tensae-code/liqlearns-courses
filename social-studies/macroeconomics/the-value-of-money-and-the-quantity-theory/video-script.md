The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

The value of money and the quantity theory — as Nigeria's book teaches it, says the narrator: the value of money is what money will buy — its purchasing power over goods and services.; the value of money falls as prices rise and rises as prices fall — it moves opposite to the price level.; the quantity theory links the amount of money to the price level: more money chasing the same goods raises prices..
