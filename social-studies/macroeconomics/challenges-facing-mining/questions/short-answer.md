# Challenges facing mining — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In your own words, what does sub lesson 1 teach about accidents, capital and skills?
**Answer:** Mining faces accidents and poor working conditions, inadequate capital to prospect and mine, and lack of skilled people — dependence on other countries for skills.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In your own words, what does sub lesson 2 teach about transport, power and land conflicts?
**Answer:** Mining is held back by poor transport, insufficient power and land conflicts.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In your own words, what does sub lesson 3 teach about over-reliance and the remedies?
**Answer:** Over-reliance on mining by government neglects agriculture and leaves the economy exposed when world mineral prices fluctuate. Remedies include training miners, building technical schools and diversifying into agriculture and tourism.
