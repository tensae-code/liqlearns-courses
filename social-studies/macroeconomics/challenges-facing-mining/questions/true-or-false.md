# Challenges facing mining — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Mining faces accidents and poor working conditions, inadequate capital to prospect and mine, and lack of skilled people — dependence on other countries for skills.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Mining is held back by poor transport, insufficient power and land conflicts.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Over-reliance on mining by government neglects agriculture and leaves the economy exposed when world mineral prices fluctuate. Remedies include training miners, building technical schools and diversifying into agriculture and tourism.
**Answer: True**
