# Challenges facing mining

## Do it first

## Sub lesson 1 — Accidents, capital and skills

Mining faces **accidents and poor working conditions**, **inadequate capital** to prospect and mine, and **lack of skilled people** — dependence on other countries for skills.

## Sub lesson 2 — Transport, power and land conflicts

Mining is held back by **poor transport**, **insufficient power** and **land conflicts**.

## Sub lesson 3 — Over-reliance and the remedies

**Over-reliance on mining** by government **neglects agriculture** and leaves the economy **exposed when world mineral prices fluctuate**. Remedies include **training miners**, **building technical schools** and **diversifying into agriculture and tourism**.
