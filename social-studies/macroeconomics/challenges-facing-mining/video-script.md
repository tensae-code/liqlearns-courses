# Video script — Challenges facing mining

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Challenges facing mining — as South Sudan's book teaches it, says the narrator: Mining in South Sudan faces accidents and poor working conditions, inadequate capital to prospect and mine, lack of skilled people (dependence on other countries for skills), poor transport and insufficient power, and land conflicts. Government over-reliance on mining neglects agriculture and leaves the economy exposed when world mineral prices fluctuate. Remedies include training miners, building technical schools and diversifying into agriculture and tourism.
