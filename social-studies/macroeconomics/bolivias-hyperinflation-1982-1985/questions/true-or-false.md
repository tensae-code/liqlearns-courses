# Bolivia's hyperinflation, 1982–1985 — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Inflation fell steadily from 1982 to 1985.
**Answer: False** — it rose from 123% to 8,767% — the worst in Bolivia's history.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The peso strengthened against the dollar through 1985.
**Answer: False** — a dollar went from 200 pesos to over a million.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The IMF increased its support throughout the hyperinflation.
**Answer: False** — the book says the IMF and World Bank withdrew support while demanding adjustment.
