# Bolivia's hyperinflation, 1982–1985 — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the lesson teach about from 123% to 8,767%?
**Answer:** Inflation rose from 123% in 1982 to 8,767% in 1985 — Bolivia's worst hyperinflation.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the lesson teach about the dollar — 200 pesos to over a million?
**Answer:** The dollar went from 200 pesos in 1982 to over a million pesos on the black market in 1985.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the lesson teach about drought, speculation, debt — and withdrawn support?
**Answer:** Drought cutting farm output 25%, speculation, external debt, and the IMF and World Bank withdrawing support fed the hyperinflation.
