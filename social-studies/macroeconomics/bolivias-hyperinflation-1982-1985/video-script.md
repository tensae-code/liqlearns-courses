# Video script — Bolivia's hyperinflation, 1982–1985

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Bolivia's hyperinflation, 1982–1985 — as Bolivia's book teaches it, says the narrator: inflation rose from 123% in 1982 to 8,767% in 1985 — Bolivia's worst hyperinflation; the dollar went from 200 pesos in 1982 to over a million pesos on the black market in 1985; drought cutting farm output 25%, speculation, external debt, and the IMF and World Bank withdrawing support fed the hyperinflation.
