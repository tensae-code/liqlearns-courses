## Sub lesson 1 — From 123% to 8,767%

The worst hyperinflation in Bolivia's history: inflation rose from 123% in 1982 to 8,767% in 1985. Prices did not climb — they galloped, and money died in people's hands.

## Sub lesson 2 — The dollar — 200 pesos to over a million

The exchange rate tells the same death: the dollar went from 200 pesos in 1982 to over a million pesos on the black market in 1985. A currency's value, counted in what a dollar costs, fell five-thousandfold.

## Sub lesson 3 — Drought, speculation, debt — and withdrawn support

The feeds of the fire: a drought that cut farm production 25%, rampant speculation in food and basics, the weight of the external debt — and the IMF and World Bank withdrawing support while demanding adjustment policies. In that storm Decree 21060 was born.
