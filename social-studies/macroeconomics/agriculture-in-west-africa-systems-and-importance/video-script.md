# Video script — Agriculture in West Africa — systems and importance

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Agriculture in West Africa — systems and importance — as Nigeria's book teaches it, says the narrator: agriculture in West Africa combines crop farming and livestock under systems running from subsistence plots to plantations.; plantation and settlement schemes organise farming on a large scale, settling farmers on planned land with support.; large-scale farming produces for the market, and agriculture as a whole feeds the region, employs most of its people and earns exports..
