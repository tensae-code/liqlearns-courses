## Sub lesson 1 — Components and systems of agriculture

Agriculture in West Africa combines crop farming and livestock under systems running from subsistence plots to plantations.

## Sub lesson 2 — Plantation and settlement schemes

Plantation and settlement schemes organise farming on a large scale, settling farmers on planned land with support.

## Sub lesson 3 — Large-scale farming and agriculture's importance

Large-scale farming produces for the market, and agriculture as a whole feeds the region, employs most of its people and earns exports.
