# Agriculture in West Africa — systems and importance — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does agriculture in West Africa combine?
**Answer:** Agriculture in West Africa combines crop farming and livestock under systems running from subsistence plots to plantations.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What do plantation and settlement schemes do?
**Answer:** Plantation and settlement schemes organise farming on a large scale, settling farmers on planned land with support.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Why is agriculture important in West Africa?
**Answer:** Large-scale farming produces for the market, and agriculture as a whole feeds the region, employs most of its people and earns exports.
