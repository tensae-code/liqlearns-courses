# Agriculture in West Africa — systems and importance — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** West African agriculture is plantations only.
**Answer: False** — Agriculture in West Africa combines crop farming and livestock under systems running from subsistence plots to plantations.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Settlement schemes move farmers off the land entirely.
**Answer: False** — Plantation and settlement schemes organise farming on a large scale, settling farmers on planned land with support.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Agriculture employs almost nobody in West Africa.
**Answer: False** — Large-scale farming produces for the market, and agriculture as a whole feeds the region, employs most of its people and earns exports.
