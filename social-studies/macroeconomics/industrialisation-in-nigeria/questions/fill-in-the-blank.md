# Industrialisation in Nigeria — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Industry covers activities that process materials and make _____.
**Answer: goods**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** _____ is the growth of industry until it transforms the economy.
**Answer: Industrialisation**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Nigeria's industrialisation is the story of its factories and _____.
