# Industrialisation in Nigeria — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does industry cover?
**Answer:** Industry covers the activities that process materials and make goods, classed by what they produce.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is industrialisation?
**Answer:** Industrialisation is the growth of industry until it transforms the whole economy.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What is Nigeria's industrialisation the story of?
**Answer:** Nigeria's industrialisation is the story of its factories, its policies and the place of manufacturing in its development.
