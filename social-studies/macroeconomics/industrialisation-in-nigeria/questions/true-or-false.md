# Industrialisation in Nigeria — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Industry covers farming only.
**Answer: False** — Industry covers the activities that process materials and make goods, classed by what they produce.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Industrialisation is the shrinking of industry.
**Answer: False** — Industrialisation is the growth of industry until it transforms the whole economy.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Nigeria has no industrialisation story to tell.
**Answer: False** — Nigeria's industrialisation is the story of its factories, its policies and the place of manufacturing in its development.
