## Sub lesson 1 — Economic planning

Economic planning sets goals for the economy and organises resources to reach them over a set period.

## Sub lesson 2 — Nigeria's planning experience

Nigeria has planned its economy through successive national development plans since independence.

## Sub lesson 3 — Planning's problems and lessons

Nigeria's plans have met problems of finance, data and execution, teaching lessons for later plans.
