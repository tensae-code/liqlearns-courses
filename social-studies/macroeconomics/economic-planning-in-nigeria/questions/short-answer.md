# Economic planning in Nigeria — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is economic planning?
**Answer:** Economic planning sets goals for the economy and organises resources to reach them over a set period.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How has Nigeria planned?
**Answer:** Nigeria has planned its economy through successive national development plans since independence.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What problems have Nigeria's plans met?
**Answer:** Nigeria's plans have met problems of finance, data and execution, teaching lessons for later plans.
