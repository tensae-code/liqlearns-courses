# Economic planning in Nigeria — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Economic planning means leaving the economy with no goals at all.
**Answer: False** — Economic planning sets goals for the economy and organises resources to reach them over a set period.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Nigeria has never had a national development plan.
**Answer: False** — Nigeria has planned its economy through successive national development plans since independence.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Nigeria's plans never met any problem.
**Answer: False** — Nigeria's plans have met problems of finance, data and execution, teaching lessons for later plans.
