# Video script — What else moves consumption?

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Beyond income itself, the distribution of income moves total consumption: the poor consume a higher share of their income than the rich, so the same national income produces more total consumption when it is spread toward poorer households.

The level of direct taxes cuts the disposable income households can spend from. Expectations work the other way into behaviour: households expecting higher future income or higher future prices tend to spend more now.

The rate of interest steers spending too: higher rates discourage spending and encourage saving. And the level of wealth a household already holds supports higher consumption — a household with assets behind it can spend more freely.
