# What else moves consumption? — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Total consumption depends only on total income, never on who receives it.
**Answer: False — Distribution matters: income in poorer hands is consumed in a higher share, raising total consumption.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Expecting prices to rise makes households delay all spending.
**Answer: False — Expecting higher prices usually pulls spending forward — buying now before the rise.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Higher interest rates encourage households to spend more and save less.
**Answer: False — It is the reverse: higher rates reward saving and make borrowing dearer, so spending falls.**
