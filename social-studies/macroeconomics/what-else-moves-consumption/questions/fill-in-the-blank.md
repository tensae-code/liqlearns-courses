# What else moves consumption? — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or figure from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Because the poor consume a higher share, income ____ affects total consumption.
**Answer: distribution**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Households spend from income left after ____ taxes.
**Answer: direct**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A higher rate of ____ encourages saving over spending.
**Answer: interest**
