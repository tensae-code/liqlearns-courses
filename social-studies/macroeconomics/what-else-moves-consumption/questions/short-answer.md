# What else moves consumption? — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Income — and how it is shared out" in your own words.
**Answer:** How income is distributed moves total consumption, because the poor consume a higher share.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Taxes and expectations" in your own words.
**Answer:** Direct taxes reduce spendable income; expectations of higher income or prices raise spending now.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Interest rates and wealth" in your own words.
**Answer:** Higher interest rates discourage spending and encourage saving; greater wealth supports more consumption.
