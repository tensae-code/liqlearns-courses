# What else moves consumption? — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Income — and how it is shared out"?
a) How income is distributed moves total consumption, because the poor consume a higher share.  b) Direct taxes reduce spendable income; expectations of higher income or prices raise spending now.  c) Higher interest rates discourage spending and encourage saving; greater wealth supports more consumption.
**Answer: a) How income is distributed moves total consumption, because the poor consume a higher share.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Taxes and expectations"?
a) Higher interest rates discourage spending and encourage saving; greater wealth supports more consumption.  b) How income is distributed moves total consumption, because the poor consume a higher share.  c) Direct taxes reduce spendable income; expectations of higher income or prices raise spending now.
**Answer: c) Direct taxes reduce spendable income; expectations of higher income or prices raise spending now.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Interest rates and wealth"?
a) Direct taxes reduce spendable income; expectations of higher income or prices raise spending now.  b) Higher interest rates discourage spending and encourage saving; greater wealth supports more consumption.  c) How income is distributed moves total consumption, because the poor consume a higher share.
**Answer: b) Higher interest rates discourage spending and encourage saving; greater wealth supports more consumption.**
