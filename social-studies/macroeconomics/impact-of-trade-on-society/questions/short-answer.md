# Impact of trade on society — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the lesson teach in "The economic, social, cultural and political changes trade"?
**Answer:** The economic, social, cultural and political changes trade brings to a society, through the case study of the Tami Islanders of Morobe Province, whose specialised wooden bowls made them the heart of a regional sea-trading network.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the lesson teach in "Investigating what trade does to a nation's economy"?
**Answer:** Investigating what trade does to a nation's economy.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the lesson teach in "Papua New Guinea's own way of teaching this"?
**Answer:** Papua New Guinea's own way of teaching this, from the Papua New Guinea Grade 9 Social Science TG: secondary Social Science carries a full Economics strand — PNG's own tax system (about 75% of the budget from tax, collected by the IRC), the Bank of Papua New Guinea's direct and indirect monetary instruments, a public-service wage bill of over K200 million a fortnight, the Hiri-Motu traditional trade, and development judged against PNG's Five National Goals and Directive Principles.
