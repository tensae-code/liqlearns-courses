# Video script — Impact of trade on society

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The economic, social, cultural and political changes trade brings to a society, through the case study of the Tami Islanders of Morobe Province, whose specialised wooden bowls made them the heart of a regional sea-trading network.

Investigating what trade does to a nation's economy.

Papua New Guinea's own way of teaching this, from the Papua New Guinea Grade 9 Social Science TG: secondary Social Science carries a full Economics strand — PNG's own tax system (about 75% of the budget from tax, collected by the IRC), the Bank of Papua New Guinea's direct and indirect monetary instruments, a public-service wage bill of over K200 million a fortnight, the Hiri-Motu traditional trade, and development judged against PNG's Five National Goals and Directive Principles.
