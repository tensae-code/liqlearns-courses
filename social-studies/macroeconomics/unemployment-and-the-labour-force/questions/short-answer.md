# Unemployment and the labour force — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Who counts as unemployed" in your own words.
**Answer:** To count as unemployed, a person must be without a job, able to work, and wanting work at the going wage.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "The labour force" in your own words.
**Answer:** The labour force is everyone fit, willing and available to work, in practice ages 15 to 64, employed plus unemployed.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "The rate — and the edges" in your own words.
**Answer:** The unemployment rate is the unemployed divided by the labour force.
