# Unemployment and the labour force — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** An unemployed person wants work at the _____.
**Answer: going wage**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The labour force is in practice the _____ age bracket, employed plus unemployed.
**Answer: 15–64**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The unemployment rate is the unemployed divided by the _____.
**Answer: labour force**
