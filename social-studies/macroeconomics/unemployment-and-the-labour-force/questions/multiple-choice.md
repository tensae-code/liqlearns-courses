# Unemployment and the labour force — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Who counts as unemployed"?
a) without a job, able to work, and wanting work at the going wage  b) the 15–64 employed plus unemployed — fit, willing and available  c) unemployed divided by the labour force; marginally attached and discouraged workers at the edges
**Answer: a) without a job, able to work, and wanting work at the going wage**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "The labour force"?
a) without a job, able to work, and wanting work at the going wage  b) unemployed divided by the labour force; marginally attached and discouraged workers at the edges  c) the 15–64 employed plus unemployed — fit, willing and available
**Answer: c) the 15–64 employed plus unemployed — fit, willing and available**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "The rate — and the edges"?
a) the 15–64 employed plus unemployed — fit, willing and available  b) unemployed divided by the labour force; marginally attached and discouraged workers at the edges  c) without a job, able to work, and wanting work at the going wage
**Answer: b) unemployed divided by the labour force; marginally attached and discouraged workers at the edges**
