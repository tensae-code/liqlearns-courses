# Unemployment and the labour force — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** To count as unemployed, a person must be without a job, able to work, and wanting work at the going wage.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** To count as unemployed, a person must be without a job, able to work, and wanting work at the going wage.
**Answer: False** — that statement belongs to sub lesson 1 (Who counts as unemployed); sub lesson 2 says: The labour force is everyone fit, willing and available to work, in practice ages 15 to 64, employed plus unemployed.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The unemployment rate is the unemployed divided by the labour force.
**Answer: True**
