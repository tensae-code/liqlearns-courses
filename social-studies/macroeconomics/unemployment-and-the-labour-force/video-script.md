# Video script — Unemployment and the labour force

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Unemployment is people who are employable and actively seeking work but unable to find it. To count as unemployed a person must meet all three tests: without a job, able to work, and wanting work at the going wage.

The labour force is everyone fit, willing and available to work — in practice the 15–64 age bracket, employed plus unemployed. It is found by stripping children, the old and those unable to work from the population, then those unwilling or unavailable.

The unemployment rate is the unemployed divided by the labour force. At the edges stand the marginally attached — not searched in the last four weeks but searched within twelve months — and discouraged workers, who have given up searching and so slip outside the count.
