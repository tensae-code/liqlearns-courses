# Video script — Privatisation of communal land and the hacienda regime

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Privatisation of communal land and the hacienda regime — as Bolivia's book teaches it, says the narrator: communal land was privatised through encomienda, repartimiento and reducciones; the Republic continued the expropriation by auctions and appropriations, passing indigenous lands to oligarchic sectors to pay its debts; under the hacienda regime most people could reach resources only through servile labour.
