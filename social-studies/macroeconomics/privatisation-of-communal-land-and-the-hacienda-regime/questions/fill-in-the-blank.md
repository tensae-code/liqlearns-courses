# Privatisation of communal land and the hacienda regime — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Communal land was privatised through the encomienda, the repartimiento and the _____.
**Answer: reducciones**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The Republic passed indigenous lands to oligarchic sectors to pay its _____.
**Answer: debts**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Under the hacienda regime, most people reached resources only through _____ labour.
**Answer: servile**
