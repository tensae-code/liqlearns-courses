# Privatisation of communal land and the hacienda regime — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** About the colonial steps — encomienda, repartimiento, reducciones — the lesson teaches that —
a) communal land was privatised through encomienda, repartimiento and reducciones  b) the Republic continued the expropriation by auctions and appropriations, passing indigenous lands to oligarchic sectors to pay its debts  c) under the hacienda regime most people could reach resources only through servile labour
**Answer: a) communal land was privatised through encomienda, repartimiento and reducciones**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** About the Republic continues the expropriation — the lesson teaches that —
a) communal land was privatised through encomienda, repartimiento and reducciones  b) the Republic continued the expropriation by auctions and appropriations, passing indigenous lands to oligarchic sectors to pay its debts  c) under the hacienda regime most people could reach resources only through servile labour
**Answer: b) the Republic continued the expropriation by auctions and appropriations, passing indigenous lands to oligarchic sectors to pay its debts**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** About the hacienda regime — resources through servile labour — the lesson teaches that —
a) communal land was privatised through encomienda, repartimiento and reducciones  b) the Republic continued the expropriation by auctions and appropriations, passing indigenous lands to oligarchic sectors to pay its debts  c) under the hacienda regime most people could reach resources only through servile labour
**Answer: c) under the hacienda regime most people could reach resources only through servile labour**
