# Privatisation of communal land and the hacienda regime — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Colonial rule strengthened communal land ownership.
**Answer: False** — the book lists the three institutions as the steps of privatisation.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The Republic returned all indigenous lands to the communities.
**Answer: False** — it continued the expropriation to pay the new Republic's debts.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The hacienda regime gave everyone free access to land.
**Answer: False** — access ran through servile labour on the estates.
