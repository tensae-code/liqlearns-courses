# Privatisation of communal land and the hacienda regime — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the lesson teach about the colonial steps — encomienda, repartimiento, reducciones?
**Answer:** Communal land was privatised through encomienda, repartimiento and reducciones.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the lesson teach about the Republic continues the expropriation?
**Answer:** The Republic continued the expropriation by auctions and appropriations, passing indigenous lands to oligarchic sectors to pay its debts.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the lesson teach about the hacienda regime — resources through servile labour?
**Answer:** Under the hacienda regime most people could reach resources only through servile labour.
