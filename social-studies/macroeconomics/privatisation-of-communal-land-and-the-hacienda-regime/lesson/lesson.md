# Privatisation of communal land and the hacienda regime

## Sub lesson 1 — The colonial steps — encomienda, repartimiento, reducciones

Communal land ownership was privatised step by colonial step: through the encomienda, the repartimiento and the reducciones, indigenous communities' common holding was broken into private hands.

## Sub lesson 2 — The Republic continues the expropriation

Independence did not stop it: the Republic continued the expropriation through public auctions and state appropriations — indigenous lands passing to oligarchic sectors to pay the new Republic's debts.

## Sub lesson 3 — The hacienda regime — resources through servile labour

The end of the road was the hacienda regime and its latifundios, which dominated the countryside — until most people could only reach resources through servile labour on the great estates.
