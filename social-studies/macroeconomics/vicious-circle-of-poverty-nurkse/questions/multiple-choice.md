# The vicious circle of poverty — Nurkse — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In this lesson, “A country is poor because it is poor” turns on —
a) poor because it is poor  b) low saving, low investment  c) raising investment
**Answer: a) poor because it is poor**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In this lesson, “The supply-side and demand-side loops” turns on —
a) low saving, low investment  b) raising investment  c) poor because it is poor
**Answer: a) low saving, low investment**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In this lesson, “Breaking the circle” turns on —
a) raising investment  b) poor because it is poor  c) low saving, low investment
**Answer: a) raising investment**
