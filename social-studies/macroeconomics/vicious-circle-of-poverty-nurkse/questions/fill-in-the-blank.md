# The vicious circle of poverty — Nurkse — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Ragnar Nurkse's vicious circle says a country is _____: poverty runs in a loop and returns to poverty.
**Answer: poor because it is poor**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** On the supply side the loop runs low income, _____ and low production; on the demand side it runs low income, low demand, low investment and low production.
**Answer: low saving, low investment**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The circle is broken by _____, using natural resources to create employment, targeted-group programmes, people's participation, modernising agriculture, and education and training.
**Answer: raising investment**
