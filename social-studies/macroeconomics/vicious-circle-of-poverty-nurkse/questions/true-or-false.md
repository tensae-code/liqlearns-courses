# The vicious circle of poverty — Nurkse — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Ragnar Nurkse's vicious circle says a country is poor because it is poor: poverty runs in a loop and returns to poverty.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** On the supply side the loop runs low income, low saving, low investment and low production; on the demand side it runs low income, low demand, low investment and low production.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The circle is broken by something else entirely, using natural resources to create employment, targeted-group programmes, people's participation, modernising agriculture, and education and training.
**Answer: False** — The circle is broken by raising investment, using natural resources to create employment, targeted-group programmes, people's participation, modernising agriculture, and education and training.
