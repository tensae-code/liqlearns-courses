# The vicious circle of poverty — Nurkse — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the book teach about a country is poor because it is poor?
**Answer:** Ragnar Nurkse's vicious circle says a country is poor because it is poor: poverty runs in a loop and returns to poverty.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the book teach about the supply-side and demand-side loops?
**Answer:** On the supply side the loop runs low income, low saving, low investment and low production; on the demand side it runs low income, low demand, low investment and low production.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the book teach about breaking the circle?
**Answer:** The circle is broken by raising investment, using natural resources to create employment, targeted-group programmes, people's participation, modernising agriculture, and education and training.
