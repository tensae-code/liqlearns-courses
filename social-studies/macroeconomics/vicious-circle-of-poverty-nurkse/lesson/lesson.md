## Sub lesson 1 — A country is poor because it is poor

Ragnar Nurkse's vicious circle says a country is poor because it is poor: poverty runs in a loop and returns to poverty.

## Sub lesson 2 — The supply-side and demand-side loops

On the supply side the loop runs low income, low saving, low investment and low production; on the demand side it runs low income, low demand, low investment and low production.

## Sub lesson 3 — Breaking the circle

The circle is broken by raising investment, using natural resources to create employment, targeted-group programmes, people's participation, modernising agriculture, and education and training.
