# The quinto real — colonial mining tax

## Do it first

Before you read on, write what you already think this lesson's title means — the quinto real — colonial mining tax — in one sentence of your own. Keep it; you will check it against the lesson at the end.

## Sub lesson 1 — La industria minera colonial

Whoever found a mine owed the Crown the quinto real, a fifth of the gold, silver or mineral won.

## Sub lesson 2 — La industria minera colonial — more

Cut to an eighth.

## Sub lesson 3 — La industria minera colonial — more — more

Tenth or twelfth in places to stimulate mining, watched by the Casa de Contratación.

## Say it to your companion
