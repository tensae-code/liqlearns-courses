# Video script — The quinto real — colonial mining tax

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Whoever found a mine owed the Crown the quinto real, a fifth of the gold, silver or mineral won.

Cut to an eighth.

Tenth or twelfth in places to stimulate mining, watched by the Casa de Contratación.
