# The quinto real — colonial mining tax — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the lesson teach about la industria minera colonial?
**Answer:** Whoever found a mine owed the Crown the quinto real, a fifth of the gold, silver or mineral won.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the lesson teach about la industria minera colonial — more?
**Answer:** Cut to an eighth.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the lesson teach about la industria minera colonial — more — more?
**Answer:** Tenth or twelfth in places to stimulate mining, watched by the Casa de Contratación.
