# The quinto real — colonial mining tax — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Whoever found a mine owed the Crown the quinto real, a fifth of the gold, silver or mineral won.
**Answer: True** — this is what Costa Rica's book teaches in this part.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Sub lesson 2 is mainly about la industria minera colonial.
**Answer: False** — sub lesson 2 is about la industria minera colonial — more.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Tenth or twelfth in places to stimulate mining, watched by the Casa de Contratación.
**Answer: True** — this is what Costa Rica's book teaches in this part.
