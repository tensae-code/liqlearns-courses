# The quinto real — colonial mining tax — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** About la industria minera colonial — the lesson teaches that —
a) Whoever found a mine owed the Crown the quinto real, a fifth of the gold, silver or mineral won.
b) Cut to an eighth.
c) Tenth or twelfth in places to stimulate mining, watched by the Casa de Contratación.
**Answer: a) Whoever found a mine owed the Crown the quinto real, a fifth of the gold, silver or mineral won.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** About la industria minera colonial — more — the lesson teaches that —
a) Tenth or twelfth in places to stimulate mining, watched by the Casa de Contratación.
b) Cut to an eighth.
c) Whoever found a mine owed the Crown the quinto real, a fifth of the gold, silver or mineral won.
**Answer: b) Cut to an eighth.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** About la industria minera colonial — more — more — the lesson teaches that —
a) Whoever found a mine owed the Crown the quinto real, a fifth of the gold, silver or mineral won.
b) Cut to an eighth.
c) Tenth or twelfth in places to stimulate mining, watched by the Casa de Contratación.
**Answer: c) Tenth or twelfth in places to stimulate mining, watched by the Casa de Contratación.**
