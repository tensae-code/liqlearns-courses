# Small and large farmers of India — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What share of rural families are labourers?
**Answer:** Two in five rural families are labourers.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Are most Indian farmers large or small?
**Answer:** Most are small — 80% of farmers are small farmers.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name two things large farmers diversify into, beside farming.
**Answer:** Any two of: mills, shops and moneylending.
