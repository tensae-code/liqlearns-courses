# Small and large farmers of India — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Two in five rural families own large farms.
**Answer: False — two in five are labourers, not large farmers.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Most of India's farmers are large farmers.
**Answer: False — 80% are small farmers.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Large farmers sell only a small part of the produce.
**Answer: False — the few large farmers sell most of the produce.**
