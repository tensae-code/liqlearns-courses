# Small and large farmers of India — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Two in five rural families in India are —
a) labourers who work on others' land  b) large farmers  c) mill owners
**Answer: a) labourers who work on others' land**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What share of India's farmers are small farmers?
a) 80%  b) 20%  c) half
**Answer: a) 80%**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The few large farmers —
a) sell most produce and diversify into mills, shops and moneylending  b) sell nothing  c) work as labourers on others' fields
**Answer: a) sell most produce and diversify into mills, shops and moneylending**
