# Small and large farmers of India

## Do it first

## Sub lesson 1 — Two in five rural families are labourers

In India's villages, **two in five rural families are labourers** — families who own no land of their own and work on other people's fields.

## Sub lesson 2 — 80% of farmers are small farmers

**80% of India's farmers are small farmers**, farming little land; only a few farmers are large.

## Sub lesson 3 — The few large farmers sell most produce and diversify

The **few large farmers sell most of the produce** and **diversify** beyond farming — into **mills, shops and moneylending**.
