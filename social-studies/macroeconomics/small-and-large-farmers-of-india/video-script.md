# Video script — Small and large farmers of India

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Small and large farmers of India — as India's book teaches it, says the narrator: India's farm structure — two in five rural families are labourers, 80% of farmers are small, and the few large farmers sell most of the produce and diversify into mills, shops and moneylending.
