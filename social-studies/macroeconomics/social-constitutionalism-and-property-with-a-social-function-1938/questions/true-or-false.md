# Social constitutionalism and property with a social function, 1938 — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The 1938 Constitution strengthened the non-intervening liberal state.
**Answer: False** — it abandoned liberal constitutionalism for the social kind.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** 1938 made property an absolute right with no conditions.
**Answer: False** — property was retained only while it served a social function.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The Convention declared land irrelevant to indigenous poverty.
**Answer: False** — its own debate sentence ties the two together.
