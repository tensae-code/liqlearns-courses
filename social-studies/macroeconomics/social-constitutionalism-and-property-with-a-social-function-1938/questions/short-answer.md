# Social constitutionalism and property with a social function, 1938 — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the lesson teach about 1938 — from liberal to social constitutionalism?
**Answer:** The 1938 Constitution replaced liberal constitutionalism with social constitutionalism — the state must watch over its members' wellbeing.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the lesson teach about property — a right with a social function?
**Answer:** Property is retained only while it fulfils a legitimate social function.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the lesson teach about 'The problem of the Indian is the problem of the land'?
**Answer:** The 1938 debate named it: the problem of the Indian is the problem of the land.
