# Cambodia's reform and development programme — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A reform and development programme is the state's plan for the economy.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Reform, in a programme, means only building more factories.
**Answer: False — Reform is about changing how things are run, not only building more.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Citizens read the programme and check results against its promises.
**Answer: True**
