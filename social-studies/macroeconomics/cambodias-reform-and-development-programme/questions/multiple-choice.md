# Cambodia's reform and development programme — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is a reform and development programme?
a) a single family's shopping list  b) the state's plan for changing and growing the economy  c) a shop's price list for one week
**Answer: b) the state's plan for changing and growing the economy**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Reform, in a programme, means —
a) changing how things are run — rules, institutions, the state's working  b) ignoring how things are run  c) only building more factories
**Answer: a) changing how things are run — rules, institutions, the state's working**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** How should citizens treat a national programme, on this lesson?
a) accept every promise without checking  b) never read it — plans are not for citizens  c) read it, list its promises, and check its results against them
**Answer: c) read it, list its promises, and check its results against them**
