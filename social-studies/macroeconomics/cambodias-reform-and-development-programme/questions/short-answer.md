# Cambodia's reform and development programme — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which country's programme does the lesson teach?
**Answer: Cambodia's own programme.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why must reform and development go together?
**Answer: Growth without reform can stall, and reform without growth leaves people waiting.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Why does the book place the programme last in the chapter?
**Answer: So pupils finish the state-and-economy chapter judging their own country's actual plan.**
