# The reserve requirement — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "A fraction locked away"?
a) The reserve requirement is the fraction of deposits banks must hold as reserves  b) In January 2012 the NBE cut the deposit reserve from 15% to 10% and the liquidity ratio from 25% to 20%  c) At 10% instead of 15%, each 1,000 birr of deposits frees 50 birr more for lending
**Answer: a) The reserve requirement is the fraction of deposits banks must hold as reserves**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "The NBE's January 2012 cut"?
a) In January 2012 the NBE cut the deposit reserve from 15% to 10% and the liquidity ratio from 25% to 20%  b) At 10% instead of 15%, each 1,000 birr of deposits frees 50 birr more for lending  c) The reserve requirement is the fraction of deposits banks must hold as reserves
**Answer: a) In January 2012 the NBE cut the deposit reserve from 15% to 10% and the liquidity ratio from 25% to 20%**
