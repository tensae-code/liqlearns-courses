# The reserve requirement — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The reserve requirement is the fraction of deposits banks must hold as reserves.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In January 2012 the NBE cut the deposit reserve from 15% to 10% and the liquidity ratio from 25% to 20%.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** At 10% instead of 15%, each 1,000 birr of deposits frees 50 birr more for lending.
**Answer: True**
