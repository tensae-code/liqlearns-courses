# The reserve requirement — matching

Match each part to its right statement.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A fraction locked away — c
**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The NBE's January 2012 cut — a
**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Work the numbers — b

Statements:
a) In January 2012 the NBE cut the deposit reserve from 15% to 10% and the liquidity ratio from 25% to 20%
b) At 10% instead of 15%, each 1,000 birr of deposits frees 50 birr more for lending
c) The reserve requirement is the fraction of deposits banks must hold as reserves

**Answer key:** 1 — c; 2 — a; 3 — b
