# The reserve requirement — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "A fraction locked away" in your own words.
**Answer:** The reserve requirement is the fraction of deposits banks must hold as reserves.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "The NBE's January 2012 cut" in your own words.
**Answer:** In January 2012 the NBE cut the deposit reserve from 15% to 10% and the liquidity ratio from 25% to 20%.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Work the numbers" in your own words.
**Answer:** At 10% instead of 15%, each 1,000 birr of deposits frees 50 birr more for lending.
