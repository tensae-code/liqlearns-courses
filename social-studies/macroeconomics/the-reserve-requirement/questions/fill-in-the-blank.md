# The reserve requirement — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or figure from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The fraction of deposits banks must hold back is the ____ requirement.
**Answer: reserve**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The NBE cut the minimum deposit reserve in 2012 from 15% to ____ percent.
**Answer: 10**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** On 1,000 birr of deposits at a 10% reserve, ____ birr is locked as reserves.
**Answer: 100**
