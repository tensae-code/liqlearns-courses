# Hands-on — The reserve requirement

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Vault arithmetic
Compute reserves locked and lending freed on 10,000 birr of deposits at 15%, 10% and 20%. Then say which direction the NBE chose in 2012 and what it wanted to happen.

## Why exporters
Write two sentences on why a reserve cut aimed 'particularly at the export sector' helps the trade balance lessons you have already met.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
