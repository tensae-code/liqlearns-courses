# Two kinds of inflation — demand-pull and cost-push — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Demand-pull — too much money chasing too few goods" in your own words.
**Answer:** Demand-pull inflation is aggregate demand growing faster than productive capacity — too much money chasing too few goods.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Cost-push — supply shrinking under costs" in your own words.
**Answer:** Cost-push inflation is aggregate supply continuously falling, so less output faces the same demand and prices rise.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Telling them apart" in your own words.
**Answer:** Demand-pull starts with demand growing; cost-push starts with supply falling.
