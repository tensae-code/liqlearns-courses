# Two kinds of inflation — demand-pull and cost-push — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Demand-pull — too much money chasing too few goods"?
a) aggregate demand growing faster than productive capacity near full employment  b) aggregate supply falling under bad weather, wage and input rises, shortages, breakdowns, strikes  c) whichever moved first — demand surging or supply shrinking
**Answer: a) aggregate demand growing faster than productive capacity near full employment**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Cost-push — supply shrinking under costs"?
a) aggregate demand growing faster than productive capacity near full employment  b) whichever moved first — demand surging or supply shrinking  c) aggregate supply falling under bad weather, wage and input rises, shortages, breakdowns, strikes
**Answer: c) aggregate supply falling under bad weather, wage and input rises, shortages, breakdowns, strikes**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Telling them apart"?
a) aggregate supply falling under bad weather, wage and input rises, shortages, breakdowns, strikes  b) whichever moved first — demand surging or supply shrinking  c) aggregate demand growing faster than productive capacity near full employment
**Answer: b) whichever moved first — demand surging or supply shrinking**
