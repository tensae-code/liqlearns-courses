# Two kinds of inflation — demand-pull and cost-push — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Demand-pull inflation is aggregate demand growing faster than productive capacity — too much money chasing too few goods.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Demand-pull inflation is aggregate demand growing faster than productive capacity — too much money chasing too few goods.
**Answer: False** — that statement belongs to sub lesson 1 (Demand-pull — too much money chasing too few goods); sub lesson 2 says: Cost-push inflation is aggregate supply continuously falling, so less output faces the same demand and prices rise.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Demand-pull starts with demand growing; cost-push starts with supply falling.
**Answer: True**
