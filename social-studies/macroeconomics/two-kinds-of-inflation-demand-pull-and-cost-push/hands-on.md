# Hands-on — Two kinds of inflation — demand-pull and cost-push

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Two-panel drama: Panel A, buyers crowd a fixed stall waving more money; Panel B, the stall's supply shrinks as 'cost' cards strike the producer. The class names each panel's inflation.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — two panels — a crowd chasing a small pile of goods; the same pile shrinking because the factory behind it has stalled — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
