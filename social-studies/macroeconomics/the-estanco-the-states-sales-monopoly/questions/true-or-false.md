# The estanco the states sales monopoly — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The estanco prohibited private manufacture and free sale of goods
**Answer: True** — this is what Colombia's book teaches in this part.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Sub lesson 2 is mainly about the estanco prohibited private manufacture and free sale.
**Answer: False** — sub lesson 2 is about the government reserving the sales right or licensing; the estanco prohibited private manufacture and free sale is sub lesson 1.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Playing cards, sealed paper, salt, cochineal and silk-worm raising, and the trade in enslaved Africans
**Answer: True** — this is what Colombia's book teaches in this part.
