# Decree 21060 and Bolivia's neoliberal turn — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Decree 21060 nationalised Bolivia's private firms.
**Answer: False** — it opened the neoliberal era of privatisation and trade opening.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Article 55 guaranteed every miner's job for life.
**Answer: False** — it imposed free hiring and firing — 23,000 miners were relocalised.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Capitalisation kept the state firms fully state-owned.
**Answer: False** — the book calls it a process of privatisation of public enterprises.
