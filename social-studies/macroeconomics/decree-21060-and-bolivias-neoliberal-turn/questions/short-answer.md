# Decree 21060 and Bolivia's neoliberal turn — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the lesson teach about 29 August 1985 — 'Bolivia se nos muere'?
**Answer:** Decree 21060 of 29 August 1985 opened Bolivia's neoliberal era.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the lesson teach about article 55 — free hiring and the relocalisation of 23,000 miners?
**Answer:** Article 55 imposed free hiring and firing, relocalising 23,000 of COMIBOL's 30,000 miners.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the lesson teach about capitalisation — ENDE, ENTEL, ENFE, YPFB and LAB?
**Answer:** Capitalisation privatised the great state firms — ENDE, ENTEL, ENFE, YPFB and LAB.
