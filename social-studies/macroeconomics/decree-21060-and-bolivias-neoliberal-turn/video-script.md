Decree 21060 and Bolivia's neoliberal turn — as Bolivia's book teaches it, says the narrator: Decree 21060 of 29 August 1985 opened Bolivia's neoliberal era; Article 55 imposed free hiring and firing, relocalising 23,000 of COMIBOL's 30,000 miners; capitalisation privatised the great state firms — ENDE, ENTEL, ENFE, YPFB and LAB.
