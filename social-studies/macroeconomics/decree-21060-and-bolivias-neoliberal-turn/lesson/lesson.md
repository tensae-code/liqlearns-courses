# Decree 21060 and Bolivia's neoliberal turn

## Sub lesson 1 — 29 August 1985 — 'Bolivia se nos muere'

On 29 August 1985 Paz Estenssoro promulgated the privatisation law known as Decree 21060 — 'Bolivia se nos muere', Bolivia is dying for us — opening the neoliberal era. The Grade 12 book names it, with the public-enterprise privatisation programme, as the twin instruments of Bolivia's neoliberal transformation: privatisation of public firms, trade opening, sale of state assets, reduced state participation and attraction of foreign investment.

## Sub lesson 2 — Article 55 — free hiring and the relocalisation of 23,000 miners

Article 55 imposed free hiring and firing — 'relocalising' 23,000 of COMIBOL's 30,000 miners. The miners answered with the 1986 Marcha por la Vida, the March for Life.

## Sub lesson 3 — Capitalisation — ENDE, ENTEL, ENFE, YPFB and LAB

Sánchez de Lozada deepened the system from 1993 with the 'capitalisation' of the great state firms — ENDE (electricity), ENTEL (telecoms), ENFE (railways), YPFB (oil and gas) and the LAB airline — carried out, the student book says plainly, as a process of privatisation of public enterprises.
