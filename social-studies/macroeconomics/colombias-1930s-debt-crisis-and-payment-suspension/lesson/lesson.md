Colombia borrowed heavily in 1926–28 for public works;

## Sub lesson 2 — When us external credit was cut in late

When US external credit was cut in late 1928, public works were suspended (unemployment), workers dismissed en masse, prices fell and commercial and mortgage banks entered depression;

## Sub lesson 3 — In 1931 with exchange control colombia postponed external-debt

In 1931, with exchange control, Colombia postponed external-debt payment, in 1933 (war with Peru) suspended all payments, and in 1939 agreed to cut interest from 6% to 3% and fund annual repayment through open-market purchases
