# Video script — Colombia's 1930s debt crisis and payment suspension

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Colombia's 1930s debt crisis and payment suspension — as Colombia's book teaches it, says the narrator: Colombia borrowed heavily in 1926–28 for public works; When US external credit was cut in late 1928, public works were suspended (unemployment), workers dismissed en masse, prices fell and commercial and mortgage banks entered depression; In 1931, with exchange control, Colombia postponed external-debt payment, in 1933 (war with Peru) suspended all payments, and in 1939 agreed to cut interest from 6% to 3% and fund annual repayment through open-market purchases
