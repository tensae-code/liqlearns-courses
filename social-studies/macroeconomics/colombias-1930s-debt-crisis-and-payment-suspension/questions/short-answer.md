# Colombia's 1930s debt crisis and payment suspension — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**Answer:** Colombia borrowed heavily in 1926–28 for public works;

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the lesson teach about when us external credit was cut in late?
**Answer:** When US external credit was cut in late 1928, public works were suspended (unemployment), workers dismissed en masse, prices fell and commercial and mortgage banks entered depression;

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the lesson teach about in 1931 with exchange control colombia postponed external-debt?
