# Colombia's 1930s debt crisis and payment suspension — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Colombia borrowed heavily in _____ for public works;
**Answer: 1926–28**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** When US external credit was cut in late _____ public works were suspended (unemployment), workers dismissed en masse, prices fell and commercial and mortgage banks entered depression;
**Answer: 1928**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In _____ with exchange control, Colombia postponed external-debt payment, in 1933 (war with Peru) suspended all payments, and in 1939 agreed to cut interest from 6% to 3% and fund annual repayment through open-market purchases
**Answer: 1931**
