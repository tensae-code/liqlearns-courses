# Video script — Globalization and economic interdependence

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Globalisation's benefits and challenges from a PNG small-island developing-economy perspective, including foreign-investor dependence, the Look North policy and what a national development policy must do.

How trade between nations creates interdependence, and the role of international corporations and bodies such as APEC in creating it.

Papua New Guinea's own way of teaching this, from the Papua New Guinea Grade 9 Social Science TG: secondary Social Science carries a full Economics strand — PNG's own tax system (about 75% of the budget from tax, collected by the IRC), the Bank of Papua New Guinea's direct and indirect monetary instruments, a public-service wage bill of over K200 million a fortnight, the Hiri-Motu traditional trade, and development judged against PNG's Five National Goals and Directive Principles.
