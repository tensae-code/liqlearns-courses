# Globalization and economic interdependence — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the lesson teach in "Globalisation's benefits and challenges from a PNG small-island"?
**Answer:** Globalisation's benefits and challenges from a PNG small-island developing-economy perspective, including foreign-investor dependence, the Look North policy and what a national development policy must do.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the lesson teach in "How trade between nations creates interdependence, and the"?
**Answer:** How trade between nations creates interdependence, and the role of international corporations and bodies such as APEC in creating it.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the lesson teach in "Papua New Guinea's own way of teaching this"?
**Answer:** Papua New Guinea's own way of teaching this, from the Papua New Guinea Grade 9 Social Science TG: secondary Social Science carries a full Economics strand — PNG's own tax system (about 75% of the budget from tax, collected by the IRC), the Bank of Papua New Guinea's direct and indirect monetary instruments, a public-service wage bill of over K200 million a fortnight, the Hiri-Motu traditional trade, and development judged against PNG's Five National Goals and Directive Principles.
