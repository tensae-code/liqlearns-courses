# Anti-corruption and standards agencies in the Nigerian economy — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What do the EFCC and ICPC do?
**Answer:** The EFCC and the ICPC fight economic and financial crimes and corruption in Nigeria.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What do NAFDAC and SON guard?
**Answer:** NAFDAC guards the safety of food and drugs, and SON — the Standards Organisation of Nigeria — guards the quality of goods.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What do these agencies protect?
**Answer:** These agencies protect consumers, honest businesses and Nigeria's trading name.
