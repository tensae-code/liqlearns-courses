# Anti-corruption and standards agencies in the Nigerian economy — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The EFCC and ICPC exist to protect corruption.
**Answer: False** — The EFCC and the ICPC fight economic and financial crimes and corruption in Nigeria.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** NAFDAC and SON guard nothing in the economy.
**Answer: False** — NAFDAC guards the safety of food and drugs, and SON — the Standards Organisation of Nigeria — guards the quality of goods.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** These agencies exist to harm honest businesses.
**Answer: False** — These agencies protect consumers, honest businesses and Nigeria's trading name.
