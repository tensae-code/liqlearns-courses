## Sub lesson 1 — EFCC and ICPC

The EFCC and the ICPC fight economic and financial crimes and corruption in Nigeria.

## Sub lesson 2 — NAFDAC and SON

NAFDAC guards the safety of food and drugs, and SON — the Standards Organisation of Nigeria — guards the quality of goods.

## Sub lesson 3 — Their role in the economy

These agencies protect consumers, honest businesses and Nigeria's trading name.
