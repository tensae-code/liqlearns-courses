# Hands-on — The minimum wage

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Stand on the floor
Draw a wage ladder with a floor drawn under it and a worker standing safely on it. Mark two jobs below the floor that the law lifts, and say who pays for the lift.

## Argue the floor
Write the strongest three-line case for a minimum wage from the lesson's purposes, then the first question a critic would ask — and what evidence would answer it.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
