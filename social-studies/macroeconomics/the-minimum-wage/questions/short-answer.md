# The minimum wage — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "A legal floor under pay" in your own words.
**Answer:** A minimum wage is a legal price floor on hourly pay that no agreement may undercut.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Why the floor exists" in your own words.
**Answer:** The minimum wage stops unduly low pay, guarantees a living wage, and cuts inequality.
