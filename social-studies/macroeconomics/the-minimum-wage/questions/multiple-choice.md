# The minimum wage — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "A legal floor under pay"?
a) A minimum wage is a legal price floor on hourly pay that no agreement may undercut  b) The minimum wage stops unduly low pay, guarantees a living wage, and cuts inequality
**Answer: a) A minimum wage is a legal price floor on hourly pay that no agreement may undercut**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Why the floor exists"?
a) The minimum wage stops unduly low pay, guarantees a living wage, and cuts inequality  b) A minimum wage is a legal price floor on hourly pay that no agreement may undercut
**Answer: a) The minimum wage stops unduly low pay, guarantees a living wage, and cuts inequality**
