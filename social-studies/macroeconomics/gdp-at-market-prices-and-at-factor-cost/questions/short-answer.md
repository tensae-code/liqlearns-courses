# GDP at market prices and at factor cost — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What differs between the two valuations?
**Answer:** The price tag: buyers' prices versus the amounts the factors of production receive.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** State the bridge between the two measures.
**Answer:** GDP at factor cost = GDP at market prices − indirect taxes + subsidies.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** When is factor cost the better measure?
**Answer:** When the question is about incomes earned by the factors of production, stripped of indirect taxes and subsidies.
