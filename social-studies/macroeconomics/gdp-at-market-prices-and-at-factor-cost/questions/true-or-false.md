# GDP at market prices and at factor cost — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The two valuations measure the same product with different price tags.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A subsidy makes buyers pay less than factors receive.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A tax rise can lift GDP at market prices while factor incomes stand still.
**Answer: True**
