# GDP at market prices and at factor cost — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** GDP valued at what buyers pay is GDP at _____ prices.
**Answer: market**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The wedge between the two valuations is indirect taxes and _____.
**Answer: subsidies**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Factor cost shows what production earned for labour, land and _____.
**Answer: capital**
