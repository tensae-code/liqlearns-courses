# Video script — Globalisation in India — winners and losers

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Globalisation in India — winners and losers — as India's book teaches it, says the narrator: globalisation's impact in India — urban consumers gain choice, quality and lower prices; MNC investment creates jobs and prosperous local suppliers; top Indian companies raise standards and some (Tata Motors, Infosys, Ranbaxy) become multinationals; IT services are exported worldwide.
