# Globalisation in India — winners and losers — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Urban consumers gained —
a) choice, quality and lower prices  b) fewer goods  c) higher prices only
**Answer: a) choice, quality and lower prices**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** MNC investment created —
a) jobs and prosperous local suppliers  b) no jobs  c) only imports
**Answer: a) jobs and prosperous local suppliers**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which Indian companies became multinationals?
a) Tata Motors, Infosys and Ranbaxy  b) none  c) only foreign firms
**Answer: a) Tata Motors, Infosys and Ranbaxy**
