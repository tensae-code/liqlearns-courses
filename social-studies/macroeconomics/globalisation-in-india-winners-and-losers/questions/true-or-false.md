# Globalisation in India — winners and losers — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Consumers got less choice after globalisation.
**Answer: False — they gained choice, quality and lower prices.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** MNC investment created no local benefits.
**Answer: False — it created jobs and prosperous suppliers.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** No Indian company became a multinational.
**Answer: False — Tata Motors, Infosys and Ranbaxy did.**
