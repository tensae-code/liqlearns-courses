# Globalisation in India — winners and losers

## Sub lesson 1 — Urban consumers gain choice, quality, lower prices

In India, **urban consumers gained choice, quality and lower prices** as global brands and competition arrived.

## Sub lesson 2 — MNC investment — jobs and prosperous suppliers

**MNC investment created jobs** and made some **local suppliers prosperous**, selling components and services to the foreign firms.

## Sub lesson 3 — Indian companies become multinationals; IT exported

Top Indian companies **raised their standards**, some — **Tata Motors, Infosys, Ranbaxy** — **became multinationals** themselves, and **IT services are exported worldwide**.
