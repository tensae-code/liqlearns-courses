# Costa Rica's services economy and call centres — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Costa Rica shifted from a mainly agricultural economy to selling _____ (computing, support, telecoms, tourism).
**Answer: services**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** 46% of earnings in 2015 (US$6,160 million) and 44% of all _____, the region's highest share.
**Answer: exports**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Earned with low-cost qualified labour for firms like Amazon, HP and Pfizer, and needing qualified labour, stable exchange rate, _____ and infrastructure, with weaknesses in bureaucracy, credit access and the fiscal deficit.
**Answer: internet**
