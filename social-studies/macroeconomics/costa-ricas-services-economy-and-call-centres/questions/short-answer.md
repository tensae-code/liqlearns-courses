# Costa Rica's services economy and call centres — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the lesson teach about posición geo-estratégica de costa rica?
**Answer:** Costa Rica shifted from a mainly agricultural economy to selling services (computing, support, telecoms, tourism).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the lesson teach about posición geo-estratégica de costa rica — more?
**Answer:** 46% of earnings in 2015 (US$6,160 million) and 44% of all exports, the region's highest share.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the lesson teach about posición geo-estratégica de costa rica — more — more?
**Answer:** Earned with low-cost qualified labour for firms like Amazon, HP and Pfizer, and needing qualified labour, stable exchange rate, internet and infrastructure, with weaknesses in bureaucracy, credit access and the fiscal deficit.
