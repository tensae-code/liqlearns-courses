## Sub lesson 1 — Posición geo-estratégica de Costa Rica

Costa Rica shifted from a mainly agricultural economy to selling services (computing, support, telecoms, tourism).

46% of earnings in 2015 (US$6,160 million) and 44% of all exports, the region's highest share.

## Sub lesson 3 — Posición geo-estratégica de Costa Rica — more — more

Earned with low-cost qualified labour for firms like Amazon, HP and Pfizer, and needing qualified labour, stable exchange rate, internet and infrastructure, with weaknesses in bureaucracy, credit access and the fiscal deficit.
