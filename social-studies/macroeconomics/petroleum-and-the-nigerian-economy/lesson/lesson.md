## Sub lesson 1 — The petroleum industry in Nigeria

Petroleum is the backbone of Nigeria's exports and government revenue, with the NNPC at the centre of the industry.

## Sub lesson 2 — NNPC and OPEC

The NNPC runs the state's part in the oil industry, and Nigeria acts with other oil producers inside OPEC.

## Sub lesson 3 — The Petroleum Industry Bill

The Petroleum Industry Bill sought to reform how Nigeria's oil industry is governed and how its income is shared.
