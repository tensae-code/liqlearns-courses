# Petroleum and the Nigerian economy — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Petroleum plays almost no part in Nigeria's exports.
**Answer: False** — Petroleum is the backbone of Nigeria's exports and government revenue, with the NNPC at the centre of the industry.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Nigeria acts entirely alone in world oil, outside any grouping.
**Answer: False** — The NNPC runs the state's part in the oil industry, and Nigeria acts with other oil producers inside OPEC.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The Petroleum Industry Bill abolished the oil industry.
**Answer: False** — The Petroleum Industry Bill sought to reform how Nigeria's oil industry is governed and how its income is shared.
