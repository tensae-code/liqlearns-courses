# Petroleum and the Nigerian economy — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What part does petroleum play in Nigeria's economy?
**Answer:** Petroleum is the backbone of Nigeria's exports and government revenue, with the NNPC at the centre of the industry.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What are the NNPC and OPEC in Nigeria's oil story?
**Answer:** The NNPC runs the state's part in the oil industry, and Nigeria acts with other oil producers inside OPEC.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What did the Petroleum Industry Bill seek to do?
**Answer:** The Petroleum Industry Bill sought to reform how Nigeria's oil industry is governed and how its income is shared.
