# Kuwait — resources and national wealth

## Do it first

Before you read on, write what you already think this lesson's title means — kuwait — resources and national wealth — in one sentence of your own. Keep it; you will check it against the lesson at the end.

## Sub lesson 1 — Kuwait's own resource base

After the whole Arab homeland's resources, the book turns home: **the resources and wealth of the State of Kuwait** — Kuwait's own resource base, the wealth the country itself stands on.

## Sub lesson 2 — The Gulf-coast location — a major income source

Inside that resource base the book names Kuwait's place itself: **how its Gulf-coast location provides a major income source** — the coast is part of the country's wealth.

## Sub lesson 3 — Resources and wealth — one national story

The lesson joins the two: a **resource base** in the land and sea, and a **location** on the Gulf coast that brings income — together they are Kuwait's national wealth as the book presents it.
