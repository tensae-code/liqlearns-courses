# Kuwait — resources and national wealth — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The lesson teaches Kuwait's own resource base.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Kuwait's Gulf-coast location is taught as a burden, not an income source.
**Answer: False — the location provides a major income source.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Resources and location together form the national wealth story.
**Answer: True**
