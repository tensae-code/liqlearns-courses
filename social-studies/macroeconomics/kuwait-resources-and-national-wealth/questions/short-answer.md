# Kuwait — resources and national wealth — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Whose resources and wealth does the lesson teach?
**Answer:** The State of Kuwait's.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How does the Gulf coast provide income?
**Answer:** Kuwait's Gulf-coast location itself provides a major income source, as the book teaches it.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What two things join into the national wealth story?
**Answer:** Kuwait's own resource base and its Gulf-coast location.
