# Video script — Pakistan's balance of payments and trade problems

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Pakistan's balance of payments and trade problems — as Pakistan's book teaches it, says the narrator: the balance of payments is Pakistan's full account with the rest of the world; exports are pressed by low prices, quotas and unfavourable terms of trade; Pakistan trades through economic cooperation organisations as well as on its own.
