## Sub lesson 1 — The balance of payments of Pakistan

The Class 12 chapter reads **the balance of payments of Pakistan**: the country's full account with the rest of the world — what comes in and what goes out, set against the trade basket taught just before.

## Sub lesson 2 — The export problem and the causes of decrease in exports

Pakistan's export problem has a **nature**: **low price of the products, quota restrictions, unfavourable terms of trade**. And exports decrease for named causes: **increase in cost of production, increase in invisible imports, decrease of foreign demand, and the demonstration effect**.

## Sub lesson 3 — Economic cooperation organisations

The chapter closes with the **economic cooperation organisations** Pakistan trades through — the groupings that frame its external trade, cooperation standing beside competition in the country's trade story.
