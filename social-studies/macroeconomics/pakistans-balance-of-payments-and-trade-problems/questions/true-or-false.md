# Pakistan's balance of payments and trade problems — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The balance of payments covers only one shop's sales.
**Answer: False** — it is the whole country's external account.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Pakistan's exports face no problems in the book's account.
**Answer: False** — their problems and the causes of decrease are named in detail.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Pakistan belongs to no trade cooperation body in the book's account.
**Answer: False** — the organisations it trades through close the chapter.
