# Pakistan's balance of payments and trade problems — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** About the balance of payments of pakistan — the lesson teaches that —
a) the balance of payments is Pakistan's full account with the rest of the world  b) exports are pressed by low prices, quotas and unfavourable terms of trade  c) Pakistan trades through economic cooperation organisations as well as on its own
