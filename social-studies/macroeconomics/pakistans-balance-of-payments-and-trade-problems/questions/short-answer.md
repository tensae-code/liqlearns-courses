# Pakistan's balance of payments and trade problems — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is Pakistan's balance of payments?
**Answer:** The country's full account with the rest of the world — all that comes in and goes out.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the causes of decrease in Pakistan's exports.
**Answer:** Increase in cost of production, increase in invisible imports, decrease of foreign demand, and the demonstration effect.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Through what does Pakistan frame part of its trade?
**Answer:** Through economic cooperation organisations — the groupings named at the chapter's close.
