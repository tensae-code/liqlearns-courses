# Tax rate, base, impact, incidence, effect — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or figure from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The quantity of activity taxed is the tax ____.
**Answer: base**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Where the tax burden finally rests is its ____.
**Answer: incidence**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The whole economy's response to a tax is its ____.
**Answer: effect**
