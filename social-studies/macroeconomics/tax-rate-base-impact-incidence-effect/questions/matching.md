# Tax rate, base, impact, incidence, effect — matching

Match each part to its right statement.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Rate and base — c
**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Impact and incidence — a
**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Effect — the whole response — b

Statements:
a) Impact is who hands the money over; incidence is where the burden finally rests
b) The effect is the whole economy's response in production, saving, investment and inequality
c) The tax rate is the charge; the tax base is the quantity of activity taxed, and high rates shrink it

**Answer key:** 1 — c; 2 — a; 3 — b
