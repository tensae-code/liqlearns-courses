# Tax rate, base, impact, incidence, effect — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Rate and base"?
a) The tax rate is the charge; the tax base is the quantity of activity taxed, and high rates shrink it  b) Impact is who hands the money over; incidence is where the burden finally rests  c) The effect is the whole economy's response in production, saving, investment and inequality
**Answer: a) The tax rate is the charge; the tax base is the quantity of activity taxed, and high rates shrink it**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Impact and incidence"?
a) Impact is who hands the money over; incidence is where the burden finally rests  b) The effect is the whole economy's response in production, saving, investment and inequality  c) The tax rate is the charge; the tax base is the quantity of activity taxed, and high rates shrink it
**Answer: a) Impact is who hands the money over; incidence is where the burden finally rests**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Effect — the whole response"?
a) The effect is the whole economy's response in production, saving, investment and inequality  b) The tax rate is the charge; the tax base is the quantity of activity taxed, and high rates shrink it  c) Impact is who hands the money over; incidence is where the burden finally rests
**Answer: a) The effect is the whole economy's response in production, saving, investment and inequality**
