# Tax rate, base, impact, incidence, effect — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The tax rate is the charge; the tax base is the quantity of activity taxed, and high rates shrink it.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Impact is who hands the money over; incidence is where the burden finally rests.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The effect is the whole economy's response in production, saving, investment and inequality.
**Answer: True**
