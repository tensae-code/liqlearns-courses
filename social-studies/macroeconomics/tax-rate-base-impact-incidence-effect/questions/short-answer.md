# Tax rate, base, impact, incidence, effect — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Rate and base" in your own words.
**Answer:** The tax rate is the charge; the tax base is the quantity of activity taxed, and high rates shrink it.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Impact and incidence" in your own words.
**Answer:** Impact is who hands the money over; incidence is where the burden finally rests.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Effect — the whole response" in your own words.
**Answer:** The effect is the whole economy's response in production, saving, investment and inequality.
