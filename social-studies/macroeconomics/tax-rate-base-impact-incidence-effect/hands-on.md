# Hands-on — Tax rate, base, impact, incidence, effect

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Pass the burden
Act a relay: a tax is handed from firm to customer in prices. Narrate the baton's journey, naming impact at the start and incidence where it stops.

## Five questions
Write the five words down a page. Beside each, write the question it answers about one real tax you know, and a one-line answer.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
