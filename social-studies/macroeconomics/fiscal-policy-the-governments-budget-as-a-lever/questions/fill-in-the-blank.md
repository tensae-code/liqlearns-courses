# Fiscal policy — the government's budget as a lever — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or figure from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Expenditure-and-revenue policy is ____ policy.
**Answer: fiscal**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Payments with nothing bought in return are ____ payments.
**Answer: transfer**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Taxes move ____ income, and so consumption.
**Answer: disposable**
