# Fiscal policy — the government's budget as a lever — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Fiscal policy is the government's expenditure-and-revenue policy.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Spending comprises purchases, transfer payments, grants in aid and net interest on debt.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Revenue comes from personal taxes, social-insurance contributions, taxes on production and imports, and corporate taxes.
**Answer: True**
