# Fiscal policy — the government's budget as a lever — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "The budget as a policy tool" in your own words.
**Answer:** Fiscal policy is the government's expenditure-and-revenue policy.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Four kinds of spending" in your own words.
**Answer:** Spending comprises purchases, transfer payments, grants in aid and net interest on debt.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Where the revenue comes from" in your own words.
**Answer:** Revenue comes from personal taxes, social-insurance contributions, taxes on production and imports, and corporate taxes.
