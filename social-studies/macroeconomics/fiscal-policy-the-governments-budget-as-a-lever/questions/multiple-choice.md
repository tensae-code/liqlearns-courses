# Fiscal policy — the government's budget as a lever — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "The budget as a policy tool"?
a) Fiscal policy is the government's expenditure-and-revenue policy  b) Spending comprises purchases, transfer payments, grants in aid and net interest on debt  c) Revenue comes from personal taxes, social-insurance contributions, taxes on production and imports, and corporate taxes
**Answer: a) Fiscal policy is the government's expenditure-and-revenue policy**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Four kinds of spending"?
a) Spending comprises purchases, transfer payments, grants in aid and net interest on debt  b) Revenue comes from personal taxes, social-insurance contributions, taxes on production and imports, and corporate taxes  c) Fiscal policy is the government's expenditure-and-revenue policy
**Answer: a) Spending comprises purchases, transfer payments, grants in aid and net interest on debt**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Where the revenue comes from"?
a) Revenue comes from personal taxes, social-insurance contributions, taxes on production and imports, and corporate taxes  b) Fiscal policy is the government's expenditure-and-revenue policy  c) Spending comprises purchases, transfer payments, grants in aid and net interest on debt
**Answer: a) Revenue comes from personal taxes, social-insurance contributions, taxes on production and imports, and corporate taxes**
