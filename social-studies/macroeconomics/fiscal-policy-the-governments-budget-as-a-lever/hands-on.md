# Hands-on — Fiscal policy — the government's budget as a lever

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Build a budget
Invent a small government budget with all four spending kinds and all four revenue kinds, with figures that balance. Label which lines buy output and which only transfer it.

## Pull the lever
From your budget, choose one spending rise and one tax cut. Trace each through disposable income or demand to real GDP, in arrows.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
