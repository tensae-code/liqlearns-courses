**Acceptance count: taught in 4 countries (Ethiopia, Dominican Republic, Colombia, Costa Rica).**

Costa Rica's own way (Estudios Sociales CONED 11): the full national economic story in one book — agro-export dependence, the first state-intervention reforms of 1914, import substitution under the welfare state, then the 1980s crisis and the PAE adjustment that opened the economy and courted Intel and the free zones.

Costa Rica's own way (Estudios Sociales 11, Porras y Gamboa): a commercial textbook built on the same national programme as the CONED anthology but with far more economic detail — full PAE I/II/III measure lists, the CAT export-certificate fraud, state banking's six effects, and Costa Rica's services/call-centre economy measured against the region.

Colombia's own way (Colombia Grade 9 Secundaria Activa G9): Grade 9 is economic history with the numbers kept — hectares, factory counts, the US$25 million Panama indemnity, the 6%-to-3% debt deal, and the Banco de la República's founding functions.

Dominican Republic's own way (Dominican Republic Grade 9 Ciencias Sociales 3° Secundaria): economic geography is taught continent-first and then brought home — North America's industrial rise, Latin America's import-substitution strategy, and finally the Dominican Republic's own industrialisation, told through Trujillo's monopoly factories and the wartime switch from rice importer to rice exporter.
