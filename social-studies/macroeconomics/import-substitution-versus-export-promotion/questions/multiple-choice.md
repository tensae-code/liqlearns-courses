# Import substitution versus export promotion — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Import substitution — make it at home"?
a) Import substitution replaces imports with home production behind protective support.  b) Import substitution dominated developing-country strategy from the early 1900s to the late 1970s, born of import shortages and scarce foreign currency.  c) Export promotion gives the export sector preferential incentives to earn foreign currency.
**Answer: a) Import substitution replaces imports with home production behind protective support.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Where the strategy came from"?
a) Export promotion gives the export sector preferential incentives to earn foreign currency.  b) Import substitution replaces imports with home production behind protective support.  c) Import substitution dominated developing-country strategy from the early 1900s to the late 1970s, born of import shortages and scarce foreign currency.
**Answer: c) Import substitution dominated developing-country strategy from the early 1900s to the late 1970s, born of import shortages and scarce foreign currency.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Export promotion — sell to the world"?
a) Import substitution dominated developing-country strategy from the early 1900s to the late 1970s, born of import shortages and scarce foreign currency.  b) Export promotion gives the export sector preferential incentives to earn foreign currency.  c) Import substitution replaces imports with home production behind protective support.
**Answer: b) Export promotion gives the export sector preferential incentives to earn foreign currency.**
