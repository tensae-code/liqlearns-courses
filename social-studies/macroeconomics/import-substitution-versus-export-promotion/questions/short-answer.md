# Import substitution versus export promotion — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Import substitution — make it at home" in your own words.
**Answer:** Import substitution replaces imports with home production behind protective support.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Where the strategy came from" in your own words.
**Answer:** Import substitution dominated developing-country strategy from the early 1900s to the late 1970s, born of import shortages and scarce foreign currency.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Export promotion — sell to the world" in your own words.
**Answer:** Export promotion gives the export sector preferential incentives to earn foreign currency.
