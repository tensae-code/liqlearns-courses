# Import substitution versus export promotion — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Import substitution means importing more and producing less at home.
**Answer: False — It means the opposite: producing at home what was imported, behind protection.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Import substitution became popular only in the 2000s.
**Answer: False — Its era runs from the early 1900s to the late 1970s — after which export promotion rose.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Export promotion restricts exports to keep goods at home.
**Answer: False — It promotes exports — incentives for the export sector, the mirror image of substitution.**
