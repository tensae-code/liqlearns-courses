# Video script — Import substitution versus export promotion

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Import substitution replaces imports with domestic production behind protective support. It shields infant industries while they grow and earns the government tariff revenue along the way.

Import substitution was the popular developing-country strategy from the early 1900s to the late 1970s, born of import shortages and scarce foreign currency: when imports cannot be had or cannot be paid for, making the goods at home is the fallback.

Export promotion does the opposite: instead of walling imports out, it gives the export sector preferential incentives to earn foreign currency by selling more abroad.
