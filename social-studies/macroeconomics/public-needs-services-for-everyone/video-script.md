# Video script — Public needs — services for everyone

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Some needs no one can meet alone — defence, safety, education, healthcare, transport. Those are public needs. Most are met by services the state or municipality offers — army, police, fire service, street lighting, parks — paid for from everyone's taxes. The rest are met by private firms — a private school, a cinema, a museum — and those you pay for only when you use them.
