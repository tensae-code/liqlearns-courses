# Public needs — services for everyone — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name five public needs from the book's list.
**Answer:** Any five of: national defence; security and safety on the roads, in the street and at home; education; healthcare; transport.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name five services the state or municipality offers in the book.
**Answer:** Any five of: the army, police, fire service, street signs, marking and lighting, parks and playgrounds.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What is the difference in paying between state services and private firms' services for public needs?
**Answer:** State and municipal services are paid for from taxes, which citizens and firms must set aside whether or not they used the services; private firms' services — private schools, hospitals, cinemas, theatres, museums, transport — are paid for by citizens only when they use them.
