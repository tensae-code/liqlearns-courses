# Public needs — services for everyone — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** _____ needs are people's needs that cannot be satisfied by the efforts of one single person.
**Answer: Public**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Most public needs are satisfied through services the _____ or the municipality offers.
**Answer: state**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The money for these services is provided in the form of _____.
**Answer: taxes**
