# Public needs — services for everyone — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** National defence, education, healthcare and transport are public needs in the book's list.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The money for state and municipal services comes from taxes citizens and firms set aside from income and profit.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A citizen pays for a private cinema only through taxes, whether or not they ever go.
**Answer: False — private firms' services are paid for only when citizens use them.**
