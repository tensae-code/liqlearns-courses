# Public needs — services for everyone — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Public needs are needs that —
a) one person can satisfy alone  b) cannot be satisfied by the efforts of one single person  c) only rich people have
**Answer: b) cannot be satisfied by the efforts of one single person**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Most public needs are satisfied through services offered by —
a) the state or the municipality  b) foreign countries  c) single families
**Answer: a) the state or the municipality**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Private services for public needs, like a private school or a cinema, are paid for —
a) through taxes by everyone  b) only when citizens use them  c) never
**Answer: b) only when citizens use them**
