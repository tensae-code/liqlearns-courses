# Hands-on — Public needs — services for everyone

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Map your street
Walk your street or draw it from memory. Mark everything that answers a public need — lighting, signs, the road, a park — and say who offers each service, the state/municipality or a private firm.

## Sort the services
Write these on slips: army, private school, police, cinema, fire service, museum, playground, hospital. Sort them into two piles — paid from taxes, or paid when used. Then open the lesson and fix every slip in the wrong pile.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach sub lesson 1 aloud — what makes a need public — as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
