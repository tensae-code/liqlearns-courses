Agrarian reform land must serve a social function — as Colombia's book teaches it, says the narrator: Colombia's agrarian-reform laws — Ley 200 de 1936 gave land to those cultivating land that was not theirs, established that private property must fulfil a social function, banned evictions of campesinos, let the state expropriate, and extinguished ownership of land left unexploited for 10 years; The 1991 Constitution (art. 64) makes it the state's duty to promote agrarian workers' progressive access to land, credit, product commercialisation and technical assistance
