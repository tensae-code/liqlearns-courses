# Arguments for restricting imports — infant industry, dumping and national interest

## Do it first

## Sub lesson 1 — Infant industry and anti-dumping

The **infant industry** argument shields a **young industry** until it can stand; **anti-dumping** answers **dumping** — selling abroad **below cost or below the home price** to capture a market.

## Sub lesson 2 — Environment and unsafe products

Restrictions are also argued for **environmental protection** and against **unsafe consumer products** — keeping out goods that would harm nature or buyers.

## Sub lesson 3 — National interest and the race to the bottom

The **national interest** argument protects industries a country deems vital; the chapter also defines the **race to the bottom** — countries **undercutting each other's standards** to attract business.
