# Arguments for restricting imports — infant industry, dumping and national interest — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** An infant industry is an old, dominant industry.
**Answer: False — an infant industry is young and not yet able to stand against rivals.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Unsafe products must always be admitted freely.
**Answer: False — keeping out unsafe consumer products is one of the arguments examined.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** National interest can never be argued for any industry.
**Answer: False — the national interest argument protects industries deemed vital.**
