# Arguments for restricting imports — infant industry, dumping and national interest — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** State the infant industry argument.
**Answer:** A young industry should be shielded until it grows strong enough to stand against foreign rivals.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the environment-related argument.
**Answer:** Restricting imports for environmental protection.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Define the race to the bottom.
**Answer:** Countries undercutting each other's standards — on labour or environment — to attract business.
