# Arguments for restricting imports — infant industry, dumping and national interest — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Dumping is —
a) selling abroad below cost or below the home price  b) any exporting  c) importing too much
**Answer: a) selling abroad below cost or below the home price**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Two further arguments named are —
a) environmental protection and unsafe consumer products  b) lower prices and more choice  c) none
**Answer: a) environmental protection and unsafe consumer products**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The race to the bottom is —
a) countries undercutting each other's standards to attract business  b) prices racing upward  c) a running race for workers
**Answer: a) countries undercutting each other's standards to attract business**
