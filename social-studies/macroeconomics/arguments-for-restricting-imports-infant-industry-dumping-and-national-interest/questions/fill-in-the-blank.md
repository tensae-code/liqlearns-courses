# Arguments for restricting imports — infant industry, dumping and national interest — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Selling abroad below cost to capture a market is _____.
**Answer: dumping**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Restrictions are argued against _____ consumer products.
**Answer: unsafe**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Countries undercutting each other's standards is the race to the _____.
**Answer: bottom**
