# Video script — Welfare economy and the welfare state

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

In a **welfare economy**, the state guarantees quality basic needs for everyone, and endless employment opportunity.

The welfare state runs **social safety nets** and **unemployment allowances**: people who lose work or fall into difficulty are supported, so that losing a job does not mean losing the basic needs of life..

The money comes from a **progressive tax on the rich**, redistributed to essential sectors.
