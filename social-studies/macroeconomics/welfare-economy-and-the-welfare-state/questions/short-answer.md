# Welfare economy and the welfare state — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the state guarantee in a welfare economy?
**Answer:** Quality basic needs for everyone, and endless employment opportunity.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name two supports the welfare state runs.
**Answer:** Social safety nets and unemployment allowances.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** How is the welfare system paid for, and which countries are the example?
**Answer:** By a progressive tax on the rich, redistributed to essential sectors; the Scandinavian countries are the example.
