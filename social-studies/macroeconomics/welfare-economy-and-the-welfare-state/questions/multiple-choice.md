# Welfare economy and the welfare state — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In a welfare economy, quality basic needs are guaranteed by —
a) nobody  b) the state  c) foreign countries
**Answer: b) the state**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A person who loses their job in a welfare state is supported by —
a) social safety nets and unemployment allowances  b) nothing  c) higher prices
**Answer: a) social safety nets and unemployment allowances**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The welfare system is paid for by —
a) a progressive tax on the rich, redistributed to essential sectors  b) taxing the poor most  c) printing money
**Answer: a) a progressive tax on the rich, redistributed to essential sectors**
