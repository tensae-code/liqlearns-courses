# Welfare economy and the welfare state — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Scandinavian countries are the book's example of the welfare economy.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In a welfare economy, employment opportunity is left entirely to chance.
**Answer: False** — the state guarantees endless employment opportunity.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A progressive tax takes a higher rate from the rich.
**Answer: True**
