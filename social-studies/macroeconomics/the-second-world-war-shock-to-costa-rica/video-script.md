# Video script — The Second World War shock to Costa Rica

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The Second World War repeated the trap.

80% of Costa Rica's exports rested on just two products (coffee and banana) bought by Europe and the US.

So the colón fell, unemployment and external debt grew, contraband spread, the fiscal deficit widened, imports grew scarce and exports fell.
