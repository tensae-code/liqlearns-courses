# The Second World War shock to Costa Rica — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Second World War _____ the trap.
**Answer: repeated**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** 80% of Costa Rica's exports rested on just two _____ (coffee and banana) bought by Europe and the US.
**Answer: products**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** So the colón fell, unemployment and external debt grew, contraband spread, the fiscal _____ widened, imports grew scarce and exports fell.
**Answer: deficit**
