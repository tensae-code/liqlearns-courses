# The Second World War shock to Costa Rica — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** About factores externos que influyeron en los conflictos de la década de 1940 — the lesson teaches that —
a) The Second World War repeated the trap.
b) 80% of Costa Rica's exports rested on just two products (coffee and banana) bought by Europe and the US.
