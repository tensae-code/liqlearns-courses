# Video script — The optimal investment of natural resources

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A natural resource is not best used by using it up. Jordan's Grade 6 book teaches the optimal investment of natural resources: use them for the maximum benefit, without harming them and without stealing from future generations.

For water, that means smart irrigation and water harvesting — catching and keeping the rain a dry country gets. For land, careful farm use. For materials, recycling projects. For energy, the sun and the wind.

And one resource stands behind all the others: people. Investing in human capital — teaching and training people — is part of investing a country's resources well.
