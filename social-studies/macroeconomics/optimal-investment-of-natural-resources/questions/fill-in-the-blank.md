# The optimal investment of natural resources — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Using resources for the maximum benefit without harming them is called the _____ investment of natural resources.
**Answer: optimal**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Putting water where the plants need it, instead of flooding the whole field, is called smart _____.
**Answer: irrigation**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Teaching and training people is investing in _____ capital.
**Answer: human**
