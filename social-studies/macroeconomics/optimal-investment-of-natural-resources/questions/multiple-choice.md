# The optimal investment of natural resources — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The optimal investment of natural resources means —
a) using resources up as fast as possible  b) using resources for the maximum benefit without harming them or future generations  c) never using any resource at all
**Answer: b) using resources for the maximum benefit without harming them or future generations**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Water harvesting is —
a) catching and storing the rain that falls instead of letting it run away  b) buying water from another country  c) watering fields at midday so the sun dries them
**Answer: a) catching and storing the rain that falls instead of letting it run away**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Investing in human capital means —
a) buying more machines  b) saving money in a bank  c) teaching, training and keeping people healthy, so people can use resources better
**Answer: c) teaching, training and keeping people healthy, so people can use resources better**
