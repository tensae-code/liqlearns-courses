# The optimal investment of natural resources — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A resource invested well keeps giving — it serves people today and the generations after them.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Renewable energy from the sun and the wind is one of Jordan's ways of investing its resources well.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Recycling harms a resource, because the material is used twice.
**Answer: False — recycling is one of the ways: used materials are turned back into useful things, so a resource gives its benefit twice instead of once.**
