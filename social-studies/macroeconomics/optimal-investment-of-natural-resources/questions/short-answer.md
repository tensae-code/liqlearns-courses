# The optimal investment of natural resources — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What two harms must the optimal investment of a resource avoid?
**Answer:** It must not harm the resource itself, and it must not harm the future generations who will need the resource.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name two Jordanian ways of investing water well.
**Answer:** Smart irrigation — putting water where the plants need it — and water harvesting — catching and storing the rain that falls.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Why does investing in people count as investing a country's resources?
**Answer:** Because people do all the other investing: a country whose people know more can farm, build and save better with the same land and water.
