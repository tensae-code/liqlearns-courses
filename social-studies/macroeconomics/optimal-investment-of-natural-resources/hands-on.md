# Hands-on — The optimal investment of natural resources

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Audit one resource at home
Pick one resource your home uses — water, electricity or food. For one day, write down every use of it. Mark each use: needed, or wasteful. Then write one change that would invest that resource better, the Jordanian way — using it for the greatest benefit without harming it.

## Draw it and check it
Draw Jordan's investment ways from the lesson — water harvesting, smart irrigation, renewable energy, recycling — as four boxes with one picture each. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare, and fix every box you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
