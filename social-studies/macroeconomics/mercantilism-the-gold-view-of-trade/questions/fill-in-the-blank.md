# Mercantilism — the gold view of trade — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or figure from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Mercantilists measured wealth in gold and ____.
**Answer: silver**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** On the mercantilist view, trade is ____-sum: one nation's gain is another's loss.
**Answer: zero**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** For the classical writers, wealth is productive ____, not metal.
**Answer: capacity**
