# Mercantilism — the gold view of trade — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Wealth as gold and silver" in your own words.
**Answer:** Mercantilists held that national wealth and power consist of gold and silver holdings.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Export more, import less" in your own words.
**Answer:** Mercantilism prescribes exporting more than importing, suppressing imports, and treats trade as zero-sum.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "The refutation and the echoes" in your own words.
**Answer:** The classical refutation says wealth is productive capacity, not metal; mercantilism echoes in import suspicion and regulation faith.
