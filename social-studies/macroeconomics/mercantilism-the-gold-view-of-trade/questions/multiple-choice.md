# Mercantilism — the gold view of trade — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Wealth as gold and silver"?
a) Mercantilists held that national wealth and power consist of gold and silver holdings.  b) Mercantilism prescribes exporting more than importing, suppressing imports, and treats trade as zero-sum.  c) The classical refutation says wealth is productive capacity, not metal; mercantilism echoes in import suspicion and regulation faith.
**Answer: a) Mercantilists held that national wealth and power consist of gold and silver holdings.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Export more, import less"?
a) The classical refutation says wealth is productive capacity, not metal; mercantilism echoes in import suspicion and regulation faith.  b) Mercantilists held that national wealth and power consist of gold and silver holdings.  c) Mercantilism prescribes exporting more than importing, suppressing imports, and treats trade as zero-sum.
**Answer: c) Mercantilism prescribes exporting more than importing, suppressing imports, and treats trade as zero-sum.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "The refutation and the echoes"?
a) Mercantilism prescribes exporting more than importing, suppressing imports, and treats trade as zero-sum.  b) The classical refutation says wealth is productive capacity, not metal; mercantilism echoes in import suspicion and regulation faith.  c) Mercantilists held that national wealth and power consist of gold and silver holdings.
**Answer: b) The classical refutation says wealth is productive capacity, not metal; mercantilism echoes in import suspicion and regulation faith.**
