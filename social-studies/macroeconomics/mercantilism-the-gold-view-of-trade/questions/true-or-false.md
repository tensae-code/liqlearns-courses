# Mercantilism — the gold view of trade — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Mercantilists measured wealth by a country's factories and farms.
**Answer: False — That is the classical refutation's measure — productive capacity. Mercantilists measured wealth in precious metal.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Mercantilists wanted imports freed and exports taxed.
**Answer: False — The recipe is the reverse: encourage exports, suppress imports with tariffs and quotas.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The classical economists agreed wealth is gold.
**Answer: False — They refuted it: wealth is productive capacity, not holdings of metal.**
