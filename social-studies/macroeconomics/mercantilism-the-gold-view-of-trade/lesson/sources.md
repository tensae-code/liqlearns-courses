Haiti's own way: the plantation economy is reconstructed from Haiti's own ground — pupils inventory the vestiges, maps and plans of actual colonial habitations (sugar, coffee and indigo estates) near their school, recording each estate's planter, size and workforce, before the system is generalised. This Haiti material stands on the teacher guide as evidence only.
