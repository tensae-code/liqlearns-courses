# Food security and food sovereignty — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The idea of food and nutrition security was born in the 1980s around —
a) production and availability  b) restaurant menus  c) food advertising
**Answer: a) production and availability**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The FAO's definition comes from —
a) the 1996 World Food Summit in Rome  b) a school textbook of 1950  c) the INCAP offices
**Answer: a) the 1996 World Food Summit in Rome**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In the experience, food sovereignty is —
a) never mentioned  b) the experience's second activity, after security  c) the name of a market stall
**Answer: b) the experience's second activity, after security**
