# Food security and food sovereignty — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A place can grow plenty of food and still fail food security, because access, safety, culture and right are part of the idea.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** INCAP's definition is about physical, economic and social access to the food people need, in quantity and quality.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Food sovereignty is taught before the security concept in the experience.
**Answer: False** — security comes first (its history and definitions); food sovereignty is the experience's second activity.
