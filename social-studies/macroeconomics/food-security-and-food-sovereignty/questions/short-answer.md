# Food security and food sovereignty — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Tell the history of the security idea in its three steps.
**Answer:** Born in the 1980s around production and availability; widened to physical and economic access; completed in the 1990s with food safety, cultural preferences and its standing as a human right.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** State the FAO's 1996 definition.
**Answer:** All people, at all times, with physical and economic access to sufficient, safe and nutritious food for an active and healthy life.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** How is the experience organised, and why does its order matter?
**Answer:** Security first — the concept, its history and the INCAP and FAO definitions in full — and food sovereignty second, as its own activity; the order teaches that sovereignty is taken up separately, in its own right, after security is understood.
