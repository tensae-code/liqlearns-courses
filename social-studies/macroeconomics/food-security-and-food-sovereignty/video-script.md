# Video script — Food security and food sovereignty

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Food can exist in a place while people there still go hungry. Food security is bigger than food existing.

Guatemala's book teaches the idea's history: born in the 1980s around production and availability, widened to access, and completed in the 1990s with safety, cultural preferences and its standing as a human right.

Then it gives two definitions in full — INCAP's, and the FAO's from the 1996 World Food Summit — before turning to food sovereignty.
