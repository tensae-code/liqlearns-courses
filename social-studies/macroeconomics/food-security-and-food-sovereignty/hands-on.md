# Hands-on — Food security and food sovereignty

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Test a place against the idea
Take your own town or street. On paper, write the completed idea's parts in a column — production and availability; physical access; economic access; safety; cultural preferences; human right — and beside each, one line on how your place stands.

## Definitions from memory
Write INCAP's definition and the FAO's 1996 definition from memory, side by side. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare word by word, and fix every part you missed or blurred.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
