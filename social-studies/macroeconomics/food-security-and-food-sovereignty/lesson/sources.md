Costa Rica's own way (Estudios Sociales CONED 7): Economics is taught inside history and geography — the Neolithic surplus as the first economy, pre-Columbian markets and tribute, then Costa Rica's own ecological footprint/ecological debt (Estado de la Nación 2016 data) and its world-famous payment-for-environmental-services (PSA/Fonafifo) forest scheme as the national case studies.

Guatemala's own way: food security is taught as a human right with the region's own definitions — INCAP's and the FAO's 1996 Rome wording given in full — inside an experience titled for both security and sovereignty, security first and sovereignty as its second activity. The lesson teaches the placement's content in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
