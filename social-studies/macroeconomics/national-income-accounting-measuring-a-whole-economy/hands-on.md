# Hands-on — National income accounting — measuring a whole economy

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Hold up four cards, one per use of national income accounting. A partner reads out minister's questions; raise the right card for each and defend the match.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — a national dashboard with output, trend arrow, policy lever and two flags compared — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
