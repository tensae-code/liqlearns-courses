# National income accounting — measuring a whole economy — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** National income accounting is the official system for measuring an economy's aggregate activity.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** National income accounts show the economy's output level and its long-run trend.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** National income accounting gives evidence for policy formulation and makes cross-country comparison possible.
**Answer: True**
