# National income accounting — measuring a whole economy — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** National income accounting measures an economy's _____ activity — the whole country's production, income and spending.
**Answer: aggregate**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Watching output across many years shows the economy's long-run _____.
**Answer: trend**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Because all countries count the same way, the accounts make cross-country _____ possible.
**Answer: comparison**
