# National income accounting — measuring a whole economy — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "The official measuring system"?
a) the official system measuring aggregate activity, summarised by GDP or GNP  b) used to know the output level and observe its long-run trend  c) evidence for policy and the basis for cross-country comparison
**Answer: a) the official system measuring aggregate activity, summarised by GDP or GNP**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Knowing output and its trend"?
a) the official system measuring aggregate activity, summarised by GDP or GNP  b) evidence for policy and the basis for cross-country comparison  c) used to know the output level and observe its long-run trend
**Answer: c) used to know the output level and observe its long-run trend**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Policy evidence and country comparison"?
a) used to know the output level and observe its long-run trend  b) evidence for policy and the basis for cross-country comparison  c) the official system measuring aggregate activity, summarised by GDP or GNP
**Answer: b) evidence for policy and the basis for cross-country comparison**
