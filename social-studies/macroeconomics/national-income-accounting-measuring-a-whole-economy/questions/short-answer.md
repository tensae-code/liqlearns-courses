# National income accounting — measuring a whole economy — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "The official measuring system" in your own words.
**Answer:** National income accounting is the official system for measuring an economy's aggregate activity.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Knowing output and its trend" in your own words.
**Answer:** National income accounts show the economy's output level and its long-run trend.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Policy evidence and country comparison" in your own words.
**Answer:** National income accounting gives evidence for policy formulation and makes cross-country comparison possible.
