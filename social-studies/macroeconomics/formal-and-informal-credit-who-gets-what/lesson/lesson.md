# Formal and informal credit — who gets what

## Do it first

## Sub lesson 1 — Formal sources meet only about half of rural needs

**Formal sources — banks and cooperatives, supervised by the Reserve Bank of India — meet only about half of rural credit needs.**

## Sub lesson 2 — Moneylenders supply a third, at high rates

**Moneylenders alone supply a third of rural household credit**, at **high rates** of interest.

## Sub lesson 3 — Poor borrow informally; rich borrow formally and cheaply

**Poor urban households borrow mostly informally**, while **rich households borrow formally and cheaply** — the same credit market, two different worlds.
