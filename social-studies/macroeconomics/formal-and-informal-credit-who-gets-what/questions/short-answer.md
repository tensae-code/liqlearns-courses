# Formal and informal credit — who gets what — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Who supervises formal credit, and how much of rural need does it meet?
**Answer:** The Reserve Bank of India supervises it; it meets about half of rural needs.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How much do moneylenders supply, and at what price?
**Answer:** A third of rural household credit, at high rates.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Contrast poor and rich households' borrowing.
**Answer:** The poor borrow mostly informally; the rich borrow formally and cheaply.
