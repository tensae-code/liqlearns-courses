# Formal and informal credit — who gets what — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Formal credit is supervised by —
a) the Reserve Bank of India  b) moneylenders  c) the WTO
**Answer: a) the Reserve Bank of India**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Moneylenders supply what share of rural household credit?
a) a third  b) a tenth  c) all of it
**Answer: a) a third**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Rich households borrow —
a) formally and cheaply  b) informally at high rates  c) never
**Answer: a) formally and cheaply**
