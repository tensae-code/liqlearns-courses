# Formal and informal credit — who gets what — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Formal sources meet about _____ of rural credit needs.
**Answer: half**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Moneylenders alone supply _____ of rural household credit.
**Answer: a third**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Poor households borrow mostly _____.
**Answer: informally**
