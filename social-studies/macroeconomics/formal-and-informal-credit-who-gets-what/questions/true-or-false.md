# Formal and informal credit — who gets what — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Formal sources meet all rural credit needs.
**Answer: False — they meet only about half.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Moneylenders charge the lowest rates.
**Answer: False — they charge high rates.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Poor and rich households borrow on the same terms.
**Answer: False — the poor borrow informally, the rich formally and cheaply.**
