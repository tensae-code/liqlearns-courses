# Video script — Formal and informal credit — who gets what

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Formal and informal credit — who gets what — as India's book teaches it, says the narrator: formal sources (banks, cooperatives, supervised by the Reserve Bank of India) meet only about half of rural credit needs; moneylenders alone supply a third of rural household credit at high rates, and poor households borrow mostly informally while rich households borrow formally and cheaply.
