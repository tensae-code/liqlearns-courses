# Nicaragua's economy — sectors and trade — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the book teach about agriculture first?
**Answer:** Nicaragua's economy is taught sector by sector, with agriculture as the main activity and about 60% of exports, on 3.6 million fertile hectares of agroindustry.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the book teach about industry, services and the free zones?
**Answer:** Commerce and services raise their share of GDP, tourism and telecoms grow, mining and renewable energy expand, the free-zone regime (58% garments) works with its CAFTA textile preference, and MIPYMEs as over 90% of all firms carry the country's business.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the book teach about exports and imports?
**Answer:** Nicaragua's trade is read through its own products: fishing and aquaculture exports (over 80% to the US), its main exports and imports, organic production and forestry.
