# Nicaragua's economy — sectors and trade — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Nicaragua's economy is taught sector by sector, with _____ and about 60% of exports, on 3.6 million fertile hectares of agroindustry.
**Answer: agriculture as the main activity**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Commerce and services raise their share of GDP, tourism and telecoms grow, mining and renewable energy expand, the free-zone regime (58% garments) works with its CAFTA textile preference, and _____ carry the country's business.
**Answer: MIPYMEs as over 90% of all firms**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Nicaragua's trade is read through its own products: _____ (over 80% to the US), its main exports and imports, organic production and forestry.
**Answer: fishing and aquaculture exports**
