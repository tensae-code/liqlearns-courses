## Sub lesson 1 — Agriculture first

Nicaragua's economy is taught sector by sector, with agriculture as the main activity and about 60% of exports, on 3.6 million fertile hectares of agroindustry.

## Sub lesson 2 — Industry, services and the free zones

Commerce and services raise their share of GDP, tourism and telecoms grow, mining and renewable energy expand, the free-zone regime (58% garments) works with its CAFTA textile preference, and MIPYMEs as over 90% of all firms carry the country's business.

## Sub lesson 3 — Exports and imports

Nicaragua's trade is read through its own products: fishing and aquaculture exports (over 80% to the US), its main exports and imports, organic production and forestry.
