The different ways natural and capital resources can be boosted, and the role of resources in transforming communities and nations.

Papua New Guinea's own way of teaching this, from the Papua New Guinea Grade 12 Economics TG: Grade 12 Economics turns analytical — demand and supply with graphs, elasticity coefficients, the incidence of a sales tax drawn between producer and consumer, market structures illustrated with PNG firms, and a full unit on trade relations and foreign aid, the economy PNG actually lives in.

Putting it together: the different ways natural and capital resources can be boosted, and the role of resources in transforming communities and nations — taught in Unit 1, Topics 5–6: Boosting capital and natural resources; Transformation of communities and nations (Papua New Guinea Grade 12 Economics TG, printed p. 48).
