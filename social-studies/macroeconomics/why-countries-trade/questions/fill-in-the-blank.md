# Why countries trade — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or figure from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Ethiopia imports computers because making them at home would cost ____ than the world price.
**Answer: more**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Different costs rest on unequal resource ____, technology and know-how.
**Answer: endowments**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Wider markets let producers gain economies of ____.
**Answer: scale**
