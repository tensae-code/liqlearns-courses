# Why countries trade — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Price differences — the immediate cause"?
a) The immediate cause of trade is price differences between nations.  b) Price differences rest on unequal resource endowments, technology and know-how.  c) Trade's gains include specialisation, wider variety, scale economies, technology learning and cultural exchange.
**Answer: a) The immediate cause of trade is price differences between nations.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Unequal endowments underneath"?
a) Trade's gains include specialisation, wider variety, scale economies, technology learning and cultural exchange.  b) The immediate cause of trade is price differences between nations.  c) Price differences rest on unequal resource endowments, technology and know-how.
**Answer: c) Price differences rest on unequal resource endowments, technology and know-how.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "The gains from trading"?
a) Price differences rest on unequal resource endowments, technology and know-how.  b) Trade's gains include specialisation, wider variety, scale economies, technology learning and cultural exchange.  c) The immediate cause of trade is price differences between nations.
**Answer: b) Trade's gains include specialisation, wider variety, scale economies, technology learning and cultural exchange.**
