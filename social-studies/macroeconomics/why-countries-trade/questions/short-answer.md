# Why countries trade — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Price differences — the immediate cause" in your own words.
**Answer:** The immediate cause of trade is price differences between nations.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Unequal endowments underneath" in your own words.
**Answer:** Price differences rest on unequal resource endowments, technology and know-how.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "The gains from trading" in your own words.
**Answer:** Trade's gains include specialisation, wider variety, scale economies, technology learning and cultural exchange.
