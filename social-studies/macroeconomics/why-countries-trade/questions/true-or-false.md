# Why countries trade — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Countries trade mainly to get rid of goods nobody wants.
**Answer: False — Trade follows price differences: goods flow from cheap sources to dear markets.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** All countries have identical resources and technology.
**Answer: False — If they did, prices would not differ — unequal endowments and know-how are the root cause.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Trade benefits only the exporting country.
**Answer: False — The gains list runs to both sides — specialisation and variety raise what each country can consume.**
