| Saudi Arabia — Trade exchange (Lesson 16): why countries depend on trade with each other — to obtain products hard to find at home, to use other countries' industries (electronics, cars), and to sell national production such as oil, its derivatives and dates | Saudi Arabia, *Social Studies, Grade 4 Primary*, Unit 4 — Economics, Lesson 16 Trade exchange — printed p. 101 |

Saudi Arabia's own way: a whole Economics unit in Grade 4, anchored in the Kingdom's own production — dates, milk and oil — and ending with a buy-Saudi-made activity at the supermarket. This placement keeps that way: countries trade because the Kingdom sells its oil, its derivatives and its dates to the world and brings in the electronics and cars other countries' industries make.
