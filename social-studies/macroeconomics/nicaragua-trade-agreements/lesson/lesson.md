# Nicaragua's trade agreements

## Do it first

Think of this topic the way the Nicaragua book opens it — Nicaragua's trade agreements. What do you already know about it before you read on?

## Sub lesson 1 — Treaties that strengthen the national economy

Nicaragua's Grade 3 book teaches its free-trade treaties as a way to strengthen the national economy — agreements signed with other countries so trade can grow.

## Sub lesson 2 — The treaties Nicaragua has signed

The treaties Nicaragua has signed are named one by one: Mexico, Dominican Republic, Colombia and Panama, among others.

## Sub lesson 3 — The Central American Common Market

Among Nicaragua's agreements stands the Central American Common Market, the shared market of the Central American countries themselves.
