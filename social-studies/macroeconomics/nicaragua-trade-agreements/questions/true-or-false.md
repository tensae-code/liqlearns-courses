# Nicaragua's trade agreements — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Nicaragua's Grade 3 book teaches its free-trade treaties as a way to strengthen the national economy — agreements signed with other countries so trade can grow.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The treaties Nicaragua has signed are named one by one: Mexico, Dominican Republic, Colombia and Panama, among others.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Among Nicaragua's agreements stands something else entirely, the shared market of the Central American countries themselves.
**Answer: False** — Among Nicaragua's agreements stands the Central American Common Market, the shared market of the Central American countries themselves.
