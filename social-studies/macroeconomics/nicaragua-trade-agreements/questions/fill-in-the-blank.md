# Nicaragua's trade agreements — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Nicaragua's Grade 3 book teaches its free-trade treaties as a way to _____ — agreements signed with other countries so trade can grow.
**Answer: strengthen the national economy**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The treaties Nicaragua has signed are named one by one: _____, among others.
**Answer: Mexico, Dominican Republic, Colombia and Panama**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Among Nicaragua's agreements stands _____, the shared market of the Central American countries themselves.
**Answer: the Central American Common Market**
