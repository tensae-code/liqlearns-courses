# Nicaragua's trade agreements — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the book teach about treaties that strengthen the national economy?
**Answer:** Nicaragua's Grade 3 book teaches its free-trade treaties as a way to strengthen the national economy — agreements signed with other countries so trade can grow.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the book teach about the treaties nicaragua has signed?
**Answer:** The treaties Nicaragua has signed are named one by one: Mexico, Dominican Republic, Colombia and Panama, among others.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the book teach about the central american common market?
**Answer:** Among Nicaragua's agreements stands the Central American Common Market, the shared market of the Central American countries themselves.
