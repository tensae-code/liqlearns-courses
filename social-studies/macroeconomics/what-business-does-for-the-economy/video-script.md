# Video script — What business does for the economy

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

What does a business do for the whole economy? The Laos Grade 10 book lists it plainly. Business creates jobs and starts private enterprise. It promotes competition and spreads development. And it creates income — for people, and for the state. Five roles, and every business around you is doing them.
