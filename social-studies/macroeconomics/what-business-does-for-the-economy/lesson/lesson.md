# What business does for the economy

## Do it first

Look at the businesses nearest your home. Before you read on, list what each one gives to the people around it — beyond the goods it sells.

## Sub lesson 1 — Business creates jobs and starts private enterprise

Laos' Grade 10 Business book teaches the roles of doing business in Ch. 2 §§1.2–1.3. The first roles are about work itself: **business creates jobs** — a business that opens and grows takes people into work — and **business starts private enterprise**, people setting up businesses of their own.

## Sub lesson 2 — Business promotes competition and spreads development

The same sections name two wider roles. **Business promotes competition**: where businesses stand beside each other, each must win its customers. And **business spreads development** — the activity a business brings reaches beyond its own walls into the place around it.

## Sub lesson 3 — Business creates income for people and the state

The last role is income. **Business creates income for people and the state**: the people who work in and own businesses receive income from them, and the state receives income from business activity too. Jobs, enterprise, competition, development and income — that is the book's whole list of what business does for the economy.

## Say it to your companion

Explain what business does for the economy to your companion in your own words, part by part, from the 3 sub lessons. Your companion asks you one hard question about it — answer from the lesson.
