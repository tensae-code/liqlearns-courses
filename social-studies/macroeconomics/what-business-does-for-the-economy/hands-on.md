# Hands-on — What business does for the economy

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Count the roles around you
Pick one business you know. Write the lesson's five roles in a column — jobs, private enterprise, competition, development, income — and beside each one write how that business does it. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare, and fix every role you missed.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the business in the middle of a page and five arrows out of it, one for each role, with the person or place each arrow reaches. Compare with the lesson and fix every label.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
