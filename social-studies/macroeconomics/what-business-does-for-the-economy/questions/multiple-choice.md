# What business does for the economy — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Business creates —
a) jobs  b) laws  c) weather
**Answer: a) jobs**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Where businesses stand beside each other, business promotes —
a) silence  b) competition  c) closure
**Answer: b) competition**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Business creates income for —
a) people only  b) the state only  c) people and the state
**Answer: c) people and the state**
