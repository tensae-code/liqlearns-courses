# What business does for the economy — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Starting private enterprise is one of the roles of business.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The book says business holds development back.
**Answer: False — business spreads development.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Only the state receives income from business, never the people who work in it.
**Answer: False — business creates income for people and the state.**
