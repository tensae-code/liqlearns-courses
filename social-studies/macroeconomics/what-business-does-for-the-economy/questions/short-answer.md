# What business does for the economy — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the two roles of business that are about work and ownership.
**Answer:** Business creates jobs, and business starts private enterprise — people setting up businesses of their own.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What two wider roles does the book name?
**Answer:** Business promotes competition, where businesses stand beside each other, and business spreads development into the place around it.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** For whom does business create income?
**Answer:** For people — those who work in and own businesses — and for the state.
