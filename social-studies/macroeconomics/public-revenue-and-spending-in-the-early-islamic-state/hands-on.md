# Hands-on — Public revenue and spending in the early Islamic state

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Label seven small piles with the seven revenue names, gather them all into one box labelled Bayt al-Mal, then pay out three labelled shares — soldiers and officials, the poor, public services — saying each name aloud as it moves.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the chain: revenues → Bayt al-Mal → spending, with every name from the lesson on it. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare, and fix every name you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
