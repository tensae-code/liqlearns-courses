# Video script — Public revenue and spending in the early Islamic state

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

How did the early Islamic state pay for itself? Palestine's Grade 7 book lists the revenues by name: zakat, the kharaj land tax, the fifth of spoils, fay', jizya, the ushr tithe on wheat and olives, and rikaz on minerals. All of it gathered in one treasury — the Bayt al-Mal. And out of it: pay for soldiers and officials, care of the poor, and public services. Revenue, treasury, spending — one chain.
