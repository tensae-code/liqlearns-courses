# Public revenue and spending in the early Islamic state — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The _____ is the tithe on crops such as wheat and olives.
**Answer: ushr**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The revenues were gathered in the _____, the state treasury.
**Answer: Bayt al-Mal**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Spending covered soldiers' and officials' pay, care of the _____ and public services.
**Answer: poor**
