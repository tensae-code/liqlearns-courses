# Public revenue and spending in the early Islamic state — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which list names revenues of the early Islamic state?
a) zakat, kharaj, the fifth of spoils, fay', jizya, ushr and rikaz  b) customs, VAT and income tax only  c) bank loans
**Answer: a) zakat, kharaj, the fifth of spoils, fay', jizya, ushr and rikaz**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Where were the revenues gathered?
a) in the Bayt al-Mal, the state treasury  b) in each soldier's home  c) in foreign banks
**Answer: a) in the Bayt al-Mal, the state treasury**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What did the state spend on?
a) pay of soldiers and officials, care of the poor and public services  b) only palaces  c) nothing — the money was never spent
**Answer: a) pay of soldiers and officials, care of the poor and public services**
