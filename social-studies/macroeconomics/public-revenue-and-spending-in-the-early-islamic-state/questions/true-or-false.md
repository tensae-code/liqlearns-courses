# Public revenue and spending in the early Islamic state — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Jizya was a tax on crops such as wheat and olives.
**Answer: False — jizya was paid by protected non-Muslims; the ushr was the tithe on crops**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The Bayt al-Mal was a private merchant's shop.
**Answer: False — it was the state's treasury, where the revenues gathered**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Care of the poor was one of the heads of spending.
**Answer: True**
