# Public revenue and spending in the early Islamic state — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What was kharaj?
**Answer:** The land tax — one of the named revenues of the early Islamic state.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the Bayt al-Mal do in the lesson's chain?
**Answer:** It gathers the named revenues into one public store, from which the state spends.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name the three spending heads the book gives.
**Answer:** Pay of soldiers and officials, care of the poor, and public services.
