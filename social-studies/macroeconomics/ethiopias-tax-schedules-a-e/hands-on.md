# Hands-on — Ethiopia's tax schedules A–E

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Five folders
Label five folders A–E on a desk. File twelve income slips (salary, building rent, shop profit, dividend, pension contribution, hardship allowance...) into the right folders, and name the proclamation that set the filing system.

## Withholding in action
An employer pays a salary under Schedule A. Write the employer's three steps (compute, withhold, remit) and say why the tax counts as final for the employee.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
