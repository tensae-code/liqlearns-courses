# Ethiopia's tax schedules A–E — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Schedule A — employment income" in your own words.
**Answer:** Schedule A taxes employment income at 10–35%, withheld by the employer as a final tax.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Schedules B, C and D" in your own words.
**Answer:** B is rental income, C is business income, D is other income such as royalties and dividends.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Schedule E — exempt income" in your own words.
**Answer:** Schedule E lists exempt income, from medical costs and hardship allowances to pension contributions.
