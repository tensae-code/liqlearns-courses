# Ethiopia's tax schedules A–E — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Schedule A — employment income"?
a) Schedule A taxes employment income at 10–35%, withheld by the employer as a final tax  b) B is rental income, C is business income, D is other income such as royalties and dividends  c) Schedule E lists exempt income, from medical costs and hardship allowances to pension contributions
**Answer: a) Schedule A taxes employment income at 10–35%, withheld by the employer as a final tax**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Schedules B, C and D"?
a) B is rental income, C is business income, D is other income such as royalties and dividends  b) Schedule E lists exempt income, from medical costs and hardship allowances to pension contributions  c) Schedule A taxes employment income at 10–35%, withheld by the employer as a final tax
**Answer: a) B is rental income, C is business income, D is other income such as royalties and dividends**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Schedule E — exempt income"?
a) Schedule E lists exempt income, from medical costs and hardship allowances to pension contributions  b) Schedule A taxes employment income at 10–35%, withheld by the employer as a final tax  c) B is rental income, C is business income, D is other income such as royalties and dividends
**Answer: a) Schedule E lists exempt income, from medical costs and hardship allowances to pension contributions**
