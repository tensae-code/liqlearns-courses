# Ethiopia's tax schedules A–E — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Schedule A taxes employment income at 10–35%, withheld by the employer as a final tax.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** B is rental income, C is business income, D is other income such as royalties and dividends.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Schedule E lists exempt income, from medical costs and hardship allowances to pension contributions.
**Answer: True**
