# Ethiopia's tax schedules A–E — matching

Match each part to its right statement.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Schedule A — employment income — c
**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Schedules B, C and D — a
**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Schedule E — exempt income — b

Statements:
a) B is rental income, C is business income, D is other income such as royalties and dividends
b) Schedule E lists exempt income, from medical costs and hardship allowances to pension contributions
c) Schedule A taxes employment income at 10–35%, withheld by the employer as a final tax

**Answer key:** 1 — c; 2 — a; 3 — b
