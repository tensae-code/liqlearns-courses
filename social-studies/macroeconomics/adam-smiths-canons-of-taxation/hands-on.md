# Hands-on — Adam Smith's canons of taxation

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## 1776 beside today
Draw Smith's open 1776 book beside a modern checklist poster. Tick the same four principles on both, then add the modern-only items in a second colour.

## Canon court
Invent a bad tax (a levy whose amount the collector decides on the spot). Prosecute it under three canons, naming the canon broken in each charge.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
