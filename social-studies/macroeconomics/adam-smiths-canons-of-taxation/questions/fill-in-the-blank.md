# Adam Smith's canons of taxation — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or figure from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Smith's canon that time, manner and amount must be clear is ____.
**Answer: certainty**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A tax should be collected at the time most ____ to pay.
**Answer: convenient**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A tax yield that grows with the economy shows ____.
**Answer: buoyancy**
