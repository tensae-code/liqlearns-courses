# Adam Smith's canons of taxation — matching

Match each part to its right statement.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Ability and certainty — c
**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Convenience and economy — a
**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The modern restatement — b

Statements:
a) Levy taxes conveniently, and take as little as possible beyond what reaches the treasury
b) The modern list demands equity, certainty, convenience, economy, flexibility, simplicity, diversity and buoyancy
c) Smith: pay in proportion to ability, and the tax must be certain, not arbitrary
