# Adam Smith's canons of taxation — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Smith: pay in proportion to ability, and the tax must be certain, not arbitrary.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Levy taxes conveniently, and take as little as possible beyond what reaches the treasury.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The modern list demands equity, certainty, convenience, economy, flexibility, simplicity, diversity and buoyancy.
**Answer: True**
