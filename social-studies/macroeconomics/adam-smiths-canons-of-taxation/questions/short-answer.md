# Adam Smith's canons of taxation — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Ability and certainty" in your own words.
**Answer:** Smith: pay in proportion to ability, and the tax must be certain, not arbitrary.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Convenience and economy" in your own words.
**Answer:** Levy taxes conveniently, and take as little as possible beyond what reaches the treasury.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "The modern restatement" in your own words.
**Answer:** The modern list demands equity, certainty, convenience, economy, flexibility, simplicity, diversity and buoyancy.
