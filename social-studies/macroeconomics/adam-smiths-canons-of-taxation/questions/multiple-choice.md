# Adam Smith's canons of taxation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Ability and certainty"?
a) Smith: pay in proportion to ability, and the tax must be certain, not arbitrary  b) Levy taxes conveniently, and take as little as possible beyond what reaches the treasury  c) The modern list demands equity, certainty, convenience, economy, flexibility, simplicity, diversity and buoyancy
**Answer: a) Smith: pay in proportion to ability, and the tax must be certain, not arbitrary**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Convenience and economy"?
a) Levy taxes conveniently, and take as little as possible beyond what reaches the treasury  b) The modern list demands equity, certainty, convenience, economy, flexibility, simplicity, diversity and buoyancy  c) Smith: pay in proportion to ability, and the tax must be certain, not arbitrary
**Answer: a) Levy taxes conveniently, and take as little as possible beyond what reaches the treasury**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "The modern restatement"?
a) The modern list demands equity, certainty, convenience, economy, flexibility, simplicity, diversity and buoyancy  b) Smith: pay in proportion to ability, and the tax must be certain, not arbitrary  c) Levy taxes conveniently, and take as little as possible beyond what reaches the treasury
**Answer: a) The modern list demands equity, certainty, convenience, economy, flexibility, simplicity, diversity and buoyancy**
