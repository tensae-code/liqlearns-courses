# Hands-on — Monetary policy — the central bank's lever

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Two institutions
Write the two central banks and their countries. Then trace one rate decision from the central bank's desk to a shopkeeper's loan in four steps.

## Ease or tighten
For four situations (recession, inflation, both together, neither), state whether monetary policy should ease, tighten or hold, with the overreaction danger named for your choice.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
