# Monetary policy — the central bank's lever — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or figure from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Ethiopia's central bank is the ____ Bank of Ethiopia.
**Answer: National**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Monetary policy works ____, through the monetary base and the banks.
**Answer: indirectly**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Policy loosened in recession and tightened against inflation is ____.
**Answer: countercyclical**
