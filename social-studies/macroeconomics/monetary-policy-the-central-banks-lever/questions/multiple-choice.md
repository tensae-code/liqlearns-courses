# Monetary policy — the central bank's lever — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "The central bank's management"?
a) Monetary policy is the central bank's management of the money supply and interest rates; in Ethiopia, the National Bank of Ethiopia  b) Monetary policy works indirectly through the monetary base and banks' reserve behaviour  c) Monetary policy should be countercyclical, and overreaction either way is a danger
**Answer: a) Monetary policy is the central bank's management of the money supply and interest rates; in Ethiopia, the National Bank of Ethiopia**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Indirect, through the banks"?
a) Monetary policy works indirectly through the monetary base and banks' reserve behaviour  b) Monetary policy should be countercyclical, and overreaction either way is a danger  c) Monetary policy is the central bank's management of the money supply and interest rates; in Ethiopia, the National Bank of Ethiopia
**Answer: a) Monetary policy works indirectly through the monetary base and banks' reserve behaviour**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Countercyclical — and the overreaction danger"?
a) Monetary policy should be countercyclical, and overreaction either way is a danger  b) Monetary policy is the central bank's management of the money supply and interest rates; in Ethiopia, the National Bank of Ethiopia  c) Monetary policy works indirectly through the monetary base and banks' reserve behaviour
**Answer: a) Monetary policy should be countercyclical, and overreaction either way is a danger**
