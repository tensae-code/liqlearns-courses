# Monetary policy — the central bank's lever — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "The central bank's management" in your own words.
**Answer:** Monetary policy is the central bank's management of the money supply and interest rates; in Ethiopia, the National Bank of Ethiopia.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Indirect, through the banks" in your own words.
**Answer:** Monetary policy works indirectly through the monetary base and banks' reserve behaviour.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Countercyclical — and the overreaction danger" in your own words.
**Answer:** Monetary policy should be countercyclical, and overreaction either way is a danger.
