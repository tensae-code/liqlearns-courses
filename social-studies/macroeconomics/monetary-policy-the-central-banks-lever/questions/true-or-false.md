# Monetary policy — the central bank's lever — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Monetary policy is the central bank's management of the money supply and interest rates; in Ethiopia, the National Bank of Ethiopia.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Monetary policy works indirectly through the monetary base and banks' reserve behaviour.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Monetary policy should be countercyclical, and overreaction either way is a danger.
**Answer: True**
