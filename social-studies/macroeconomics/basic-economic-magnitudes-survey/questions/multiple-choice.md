# Basic economic magnitudes — a survey — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The chapter teaches the magnitudes —
a) one by one in isolation
b) together as a set
c) as history
**Answer: b) together as a set**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Unemployment answers the question of —
a) prices
b) how much of the labour force wanting work has none
c) total production
**Answer: b) how much of the labour force wanting work has none**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The survey's magnitudes are best read —
a) alone, one at a time
b) together, correcting each other
c) only as history
**Answer: b) together, correcting each other**
