# Basic economic magnitudes — a survey — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the five magnitudes in the survey.
**Answer:** GDP, per-capita income, the standard of living, unemployment and inflation.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Match GDP and per-capita income to their questions.
**Answer:** GDP — how much the economy produces in total; per-capita income — the average share per person.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Why teach the magnitudes as one chapter?
**Answer:** Because read together they correct each other — any one alone can flatter or frighten falsely.
