# Challenges facing farming — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In your own words, what does sub lesson 1 teach about money, technology and inputs?
**Answer:** Farming is held back by inadequate financial services, low use of improved technology and inadequate inputs — seed, fertiliser and improved breeds.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In your own words, what does sub lesson 2 teach about roads, water, skills and pests?
**Answer:** Poor rural infrastructure prevents access to markets. Farmers also face low literacy, weak research and extension, lack of water and pasture in the dry season, pests and diseases and inadequate skilled labour.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In your own words, what does sub lesson 3 teach about relief syndrome?
**Answer:** Relief syndrome holds farming back: communities rely on relief food and neglect to farm for themselves.
