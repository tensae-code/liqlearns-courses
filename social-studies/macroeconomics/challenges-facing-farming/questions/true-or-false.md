# Challenges facing farming — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Farming is held back by inadequate financial services, low use of improved technology and inadequate inputs — seed, fertiliser and improved breeds.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Poor rural infrastructure prevents access to markets. Farmers also face low literacy, weak research and extension, lack of water and pasture in the dry season, pests and diseases and inadequate skilled labour.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Relief syndrome holds farming back: communities rely on relief food and neglect to farm for themselves.
**Answer: True**
