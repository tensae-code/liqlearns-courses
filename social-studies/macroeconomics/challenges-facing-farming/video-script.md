# Video script — Challenges facing farming

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Challenges facing farming — as South Sudan's book teaches it, says the narrator: Farming in South Sudan is held back by inadequate financial services, low use of improved technology, poor rural infrastructure that prevents access to markets, inadequate inputs (seed, fertiliser, improved breeds), low literacy, weak research and extension, lack of water and pasture in the dry season, pests and diseases, inadequate skilled labour, and relief syndrome — communities relying on relief food and neglecting to farm for themselves.
