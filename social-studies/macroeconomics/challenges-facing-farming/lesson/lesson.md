# Challenges facing farming

## Do it first

## Sub lesson 1 — Money, technology and inputs

Farming is held back by **inadequate financial services**, **low use of improved technology** and **inadequate inputs** — seed, fertiliser and improved breeds.

## Sub lesson 2 — Roads, water, skills and pests

**Poor rural infrastructure prevents access to markets**. Farmers also face **low literacy**, **weak research and extension**, **lack of water and pasture in the dry season**, **pests and diseases** and **inadequate skilled labour**.

## Sub lesson 3 — Relief syndrome

**Relief syndrome** holds farming back: communities **rely on relief food** and **neglect to farm for themselves**.
