# Colombia's regions and what they produce — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the lesson teach about each natural region produces from its own resources?
**Answer:** Each natural region produces from its own resources — Amazonía: indigenous yuca/fariña, hunting and fishing;

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the lesson teach about andina coffee eje cafetero rice and other crops?
**Answer:** Andina: coffee (Eje Cafetero), rice and other crops, sheep/cattle/pig raising whose meat supplies markets and the food industry, emeralds and salt mining;

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the lesson teach about caribe cattle and pig raising for markets?
**Answer:** Caribe: cattle and pig raising for markets
