# Video script — Colombia's regions and what they produce

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Colombia's regions and what they produce — as Colombia's book teaches it, says the narrator: Each natural region produces from its own resources — Amazonía: indigenous yuca/fariña, hunting and fishing; Andina: coffee (Eje Cafetero), rice and other crops, sheep/cattle/pig raising whose meat supplies markets and the food industry, emeralds and salt mining; Caribe: cattle and pig raising for markets
