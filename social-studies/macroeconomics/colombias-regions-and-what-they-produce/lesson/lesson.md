## Sub lesson 1 — Each natural region produces from its own resources

Each natural region produces from its own resources — Amazonía: indigenous yuca/fariña, hunting and fishing;

## Sub lesson 2 — Andina coffee eje cafetero rice and other crops

Andina: coffee (Eje Cafetero), rice and other crops, sheep/cattle/pig raising whose meat supplies markets and the food industry, emeralds and salt mining;

## Sub lesson 3 — Caribe cattle and pig raising for markets

Caribe: cattle and pig raising for markets
