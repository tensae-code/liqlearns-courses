# What drives and blocks international trade — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the book teach about what drives trade?
**Answer:** Trade is driven by differences between countries — in resources, climate, technology and what each can make cheaply — and by the gains exchange brings.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the book teach about what blocks trade?
**Answer:** Trade is blocked by barriers and costs that keep goods from moving freely between countries.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the book teach about indonesia's nickel-export case?
**Answer:** The book teaches driving and blocking factors through Indonesia's nickel-export case — a resource Indonesia sells to the world.
