# What drives and blocks international trade — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Trade is driven by differences between countries — in resources, climate, technology and what each can make cheaply — and by the gains exchange brings.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Trade is blocked by barriers and costs that keep goods from moving freely between countries.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The book teaches driving and blocking factors through Indonesia's something else entirely case — a resource Indonesia sells to the world.
**Answer: False** — The book teaches driving and blocking factors through Indonesia's nickel-export case — a resource Indonesia sells to the world.
