# What drives and blocks international trade — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Trade is driven by _____ — in resources, climate, technology and what each can make cheaply — and by the gains exchange brings.
**Answer: differences between countries**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Trade is blocked by _____ that keep goods from moving freely between countries.
**Answer: barriers and costs**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The book teaches driving and blocking factors through Indonesia's _____ case — a resource Indonesia sells to the world.
**Answer: nickel-export**
