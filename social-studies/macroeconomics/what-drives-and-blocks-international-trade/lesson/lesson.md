# What drives and blocks international trade

## Do it first

Think of this topic the way the Indonesia book opens it — What drives and blocks international trade. What do you already know about it before you read on?

## Sub lesson 1 — What drives trade

Trade is driven by differences between countries — in resources, climate, technology and what each can make cheaply — and by the gains exchange brings.

## Sub lesson 2 — What blocks trade

Trade is blocked by barriers and costs that keep goods from moving freely between countries.

## Sub lesson 3 — Indonesia's nickel-export case

The book teaches driving and blocking factors through Indonesia's nickel-export case — a resource Indonesia sells to the world.
