# Keynesian economic thought — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Keynes wrote in the shadow of the 1930s slump.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In Keynes's idea, only the amount of gold in the treasury drives the economy.
**Answer: False — Keynes's driver is demand — spending — not gold stocks.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Keynesian policy has the state take up the slack when private spending falls.
**Answer: True**
