# Keynesian economic thought — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What did Keynes ask that the old schools had not answered?
**Answer: Why the economy had not righted itself in the slump.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In a slump, what is missing in Keynes's account?
**Answer: Demand — spending by households and firms.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Why does Keynesian thought close the history chapter?
**Answer: It supplies the argument behind the fiscal and monetary policies pupils studied earlier in the book.**
