# Keynesian economic thought — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Keynes wrote in the shadow of —
a) a time when no economy had ever slumped  b) the 1930s slump, with idle factories and mass unemployment  c) his own career as a factory owner
**Answer: b) the 1930s slump, with idle factories and mass unemployment**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In Keynes's idea, what drives the economy?
a) spending — total demand  b) the weather alone  c) only the amount of gold in the treasury
**Answer: a) spending — total demand**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** On Keynes's idea, when private spending falls the state should —
a) ban private spending  b) do nothing in every case, forever  c) use public spending and policy to take up the slack
**Answer: c) use public spending and policy to take up the slack**
