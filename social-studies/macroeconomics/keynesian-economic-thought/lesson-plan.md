# Lesson plan — Keynesian economic thought

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Macroeconomics · **Lesson:** Keynesian economic thought
**Place in the curriculum:** Macroeconomics (CURRICULUM.md) — built in the Economics expansion (Cambodia, 2026-11-16).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Keynes (1883–1946), the slump of the 1930s, and the new idea that demand drives the economy and the state must manage it.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Cambodia's Grade 12 Economics/Social Studies book — Ch. 3, Lesson 5: Keynes and Keynesian thought (printed p. 192).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of ..." in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.
