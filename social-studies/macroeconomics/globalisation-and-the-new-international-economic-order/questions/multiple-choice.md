# Globalisation and the New International Economic Order — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** About the new international economic order — the lesson teaches that —
a) the New International Economic Order was the developing countries' demand for fairer rules in the world economy.  b) globalisation ties countries together through trade, investment, technology and the movement of people.  c) globalisation brings wider markets and investment, and strains when its gains are unevenly shared.
