# Globalisation and the New International Economic Order — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What was the New International Economic Order?
**Answer:** The New International Economic Order was the developing countries' demand for fairer rules in the world economy.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does globalisation tie countries together through?
**Answer:** Globalisation ties countries together through trade, investment, technology and the movement of people.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does globalisation bring, and what strains?
**Answer:** Globalisation brings wider markets and investment, and strains when its gains are unevenly shared.
