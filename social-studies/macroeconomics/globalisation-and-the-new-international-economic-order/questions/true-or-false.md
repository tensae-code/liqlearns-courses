# Globalisation and the New International Economic Order — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The New International Economic Order demanded less fair world rules.
**Answer: False** — The New International Economic Order was the developing countries' demand for fairer rules in the world economy.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Globalisation cuts countries off from each other.
**Answer: False** — Globalisation ties countries together through trade, investment, technology and the movement of people.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Globalisation's gains are always shared perfectly evenly.
**Answer: False** — Globalisation brings wider markets and investment, and strains when its gains are unevenly shared.
