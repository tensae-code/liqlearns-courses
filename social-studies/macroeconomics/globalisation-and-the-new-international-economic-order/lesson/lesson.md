# Globalisation and the New International Economic Order

## Do it first

## Sub lesson 1 — The New International Economic Order

The New International Economic Order was the developing countries' demand for fairer rules in the world economy.

## Sub lesson 2 — Globalisation

Globalisation ties countries together through trade, investment, technology and the movement of people.

## Sub lesson 3 — Gains and strains

Globalisation brings wider markets and investment, and strains when its gains are unevenly shared.
