# Hands-on — Inflation, deflation and disinflation

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Classify three years
Invent three years of CPI values: one deflation year, one disinflation year, one high-inflation year. Exchange with a partner and classify each other's years, showing the percentage working.

## Headline check
Find or invent a headline that confuses deflation with disinflation. Rewrite it correctly in one sentence.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
