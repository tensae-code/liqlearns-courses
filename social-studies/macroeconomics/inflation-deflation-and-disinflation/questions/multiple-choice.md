# Inflation, deflation and disinflation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Reading the change off the CPI"?
a) Inflation is the percentage change in the CPI; 100 to 140 is +40% and 140 to 120 is −14.3%  b) A falling price level is deflation; CPI 120 to 200 is +66.6%  c) Disinflation is a falling but still positive inflation rate
**Answer: a) Inflation is the percentage change in the CPI; 100 to 140 is +40% and 140 to 120 is −14.3%**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Deflation — the level itself falls"?
a) A falling price level is deflation; CPI 120 to 200 is +66.6%  b) Disinflation is a falling but still positive inflation rate  c) Inflation is the percentage change in the CPI; 100 to 140 is +40% and 140 to 120 is −14.3%
**Answer: a) A falling price level is deflation; CPI 120 to 200 is +66.6%**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Disinflation — rising more slowly"?
a) Disinflation is a falling but still positive inflation rate  b) Inflation is the percentage change in the CPI; 100 to 140 is +40% and 140 to 120 is −14.3%  c) A falling price level is deflation; CPI 120 to 200 is +66.6%
**Answer: a) Disinflation is a falling but still positive inflation rate**
