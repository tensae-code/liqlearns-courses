# Inflation, deflation and disinflation — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Inflation is the percentage change in the CPI; 100 to 140 is +40% and 140 to 120 is −14.3%.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A falling price level is deflation; CPI 120 to 200 is +66.6%.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Disinflation is a falling but still positive inflation rate.
**Answer: True**
