# Inflation, deflation and disinflation — matching

Match each part to its right statement.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Reading the change off the CPI — c
**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Deflation — the level itself falls — a
**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Disinflation — rising more slowly — b

Statements:
a) A falling price level is deflation; CPI 120 to 200 is +66.6%
b) Disinflation is a falling but still positive inflation rate
c) Inflation is the percentage change in the CPI; 100 to 140 is +40% and 140 to 120 is −14.3%

**Answer key:** 1 — c; 2 — a; 3 — b
