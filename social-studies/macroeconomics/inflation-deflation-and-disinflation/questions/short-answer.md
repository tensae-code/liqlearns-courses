# Inflation, deflation and disinflation — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Reading the change off the CPI" in your own words.
**Answer:** Inflation is the percentage change in the CPI; 100 to 140 is +40% and 140 to 120 is −14.3%.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Deflation — the level itself falls" in your own words.
**Answer:** A falling price level is deflation; CPI 120 to 200 is +66.6%.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Disinflation — rising more slowly" in your own words.
**Answer:** Disinflation is a falling but still positive inflation rate.
