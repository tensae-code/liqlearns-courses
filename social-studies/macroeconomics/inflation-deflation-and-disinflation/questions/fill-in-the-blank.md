# Inflation, deflation and disinflation — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or figure from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** CPI moving from 140 to 120 is a change of ____ percent.
**Answer: −14.3**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A falling general price level is called ____.
**Answer: deflation**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Prices rising more slowly than before is ____, not deflation.
**Answer: disinflation**
