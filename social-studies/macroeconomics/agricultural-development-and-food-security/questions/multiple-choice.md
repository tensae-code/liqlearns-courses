# Agricultural development and food security — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Agricultural development raises production through —
a) area, irrigation, fertilisers, HYV seed and mechanisation  b) importing all food  c) closing farms
**Answer: a) area, irrigation, fertilisers, HYV seed and mechanisation**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The small Indian farm sells at —
a) the mandi  b) a foreign stock exchange  c) no market
**Answer: a) the mandi**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The US case farm is —
a) large and mechanised  b) small and hand-worked  c) landless
**Answer: a) large and mechanised**
