# Agricultural development and food security — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name three ways farm production is raised.
**Answer:** Any three of: more area, irrigation, fertilisers, HYV seed, mechanisation.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How does the small Indian farm finance and sell?
**Answer:** It uses cooperative credit and sells at the mandi.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** How does the US case differ from the Indian case?
**Answer:** It is a large mechanised farm, against India's small farm.
