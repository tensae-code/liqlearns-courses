# Agricultural development and food security

## Do it first

## Sub lesson 1 — Raising production — area, irrigation, fertilisers, HYV, machines

**Agricultural development** raises farm production through **more area, irrigation, fertilisers, HYV seed and mechanisation** — so the country has food security.

## Sub lesson 2 — The small Indian farm — cooperative credit and the mandi

The book's Indian case is a **small farm** that borrows through **cooperative credit** and sells its crop at the **mandi** (the regulated market).

## Sub lesson 3 — The large mechanised US farm

Against it stands a **large mechanised US farm** — big area, machines doing the work — the developed-country case of agricultural development.
