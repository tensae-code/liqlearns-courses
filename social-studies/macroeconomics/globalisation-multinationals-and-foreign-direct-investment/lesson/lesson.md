# Globalisation, multinationals and foreign direct investment

## Do it first

## Sub lesson 1 — Globalisation — economies joined together

**Globalisation** is the joining of the world's economies, and the book sets out the **factors driving it**.

## Sub lesson 2 — Multinational companies in Malaysia

**Multinational companies** are firms that **operate across borders** — the book studies them **operating in Malaysia**.

## Sub lesson 3 — Foreign direct investment

**Foreign direct investment (pelaburan langsung asing)** is investment coming into the country from abroad — money arriving to set up and run production in Malaysia.
