# Globalisation, multinationals and foreign direct investment — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In your own words, what does sub lesson 1 teach about globalisation — economies joined together?
**Answer:** Globalisation is the joining of the world's economies, and the book sets out the factors driving it.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In your own words, what does sub lesson 2 teach about multinational companies in malaysia?
**Answer:** Multinational companies are firms that operate across borders — the book studies them operating in Malaysia.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In your own words, what does sub lesson 3 teach about foreign direct investment?
**Answer:** Foreign direct investment (pelaburan langsung asing) is investment coming into the country from abroad — money arriving to set up and run production in Malaysia.
