# Globalisation, multinationals and foreign direct investment — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Globalisation is the joining of the world's economies, and the book sets out the factors driving it.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Multinational companies are firms that operate across borders — the book studies them operating in Malaysia.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Foreign direct investment (pelaburan langsung asing) is investment coming into the country from abroad — money arriving to set up and run production in Malaysia.
**Answer: True**
