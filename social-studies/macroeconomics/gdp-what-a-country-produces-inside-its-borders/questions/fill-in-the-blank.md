# GDP — what a country produces inside its borders — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** GDP counts currently produced _____ goods and services within the country's boundary.
**Answer: final**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Intermediate goods are excluded from GDP to avoid _____.
**Answer: double counting**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** GDP is valued at _____ — GDP = Σ Pi × Qi over final outputs.
**Answer: market prices**
