# GDP — what a country produces inside its borders — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "The definition" in your own words.
**Answer:** GDP is the total value of currently produced final goods and services produced within a country's boundary in a given period.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "The rules that protect the count" in your own words.
**Answer:** GDP counts current production only and final goods only, excluding intermediate goods to avoid double counting.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Valued at market prices" in your own words.
**Answer:** GDP is valued at market prices as the sum of price times quantity over final outputs.
