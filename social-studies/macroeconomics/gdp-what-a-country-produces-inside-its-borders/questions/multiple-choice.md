# GDP — what a country produces inside its borders — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "The definition"?
a) the total value of currently produced final goods and services within the boundary in a period  b) current production only; final goods only, to avoid double counting; location decides  c) GDP = Σ Pi × Qi over final outputs, at market prices
**Answer: a) the total value of currently produced final goods and services within the boundary in a period**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "The rules that protect the count"?
a) the total value of currently produced final goods and services within the boundary in a period  b) GDP = Σ Pi × Qi over final outputs, at market prices  c) current production only; final goods only, to avoid double counting; location decides
**Answer: c) current production only; final goods only, to avoid double counting; location decides**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Valued at market prices"?
a) current production only; final goods only, to avoid double counting; location decides  b) GDP = Σ Pi × Qi over final outputs, at market prices  c) the total value of currently produced final goods and services within the boundary in a period
**Answer: b) GDP = Σ Pi × Qi over final outputs, at market prices**
