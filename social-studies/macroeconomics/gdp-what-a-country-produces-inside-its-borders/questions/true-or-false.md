# GDP — what a country produces inside its borders — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** GDP is the total value of currently produced final goods and services produced within a country's boundary in a given period.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** GDP is the total value of currently produced final goods and services produced within a country's boundary in a given period.
**Answer: False** — that statement belongs to sub lesson 1 (The definition); sub lesson 2 says: GDP counts current production only and final goods only, excluding intermediate goods to avoid double counting.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** GDP is valued at market prices as the sum of price times quantity over final outputs.
**Answer: True**
