# Foreign trade policy — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Foreign trade policy is —
a) a single merchant's buying list  b) the state's choices about trade across its borders  c) a ban on all trade with everyone
**Answer: b) the state's choices about trade across its borders**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The policy encourages —
a) exports that earn and imports the economy needs  b) only goods the country cannot use  c) nothing at all — encouragement is impossible
**Answer: a) exports that earn and imports the economy needs**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A state steers trade in order to —
a) make its currency worthless  b) stop its people ever trading  c) protect growing producers, earn foreign currency and secure needed goods
**Answer: c) protect growing producers, earn foreign currency and secure needed goods**
