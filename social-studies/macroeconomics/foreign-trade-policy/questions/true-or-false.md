# Foreign trade policy — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Foreign trade policy is the state's set of choices about cross-border trade.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The policy encourages nothing at all — encouragement is impossible.
**Answer: False — The policy does encourage — exports that earn and imports the economy needs.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** States steer trade to protect, earn and secure.
**Answer: True**
