# Foreign trade policy — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Does every trading country have a foreign trade policy?
**Answer: Yes — written as a plan or grown from its laws and taxes at the border.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the lesson ask for behind each encouragement or restriction?
**Answer: The reason for the choice.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name two reasons a state steers trade.
**Answer: Any two of: protect producers while they grow; earn foreign currency; secure needed goods; bargain with partners from strength.**
