# Manufacturing and construction industries in Nigeria

## Sub lesson 1 — Nigeria's manufacturing industries

Nigeria's manufacturing makes goods from food and drink to cement and textiles for its huge home market.

## Sub lesson 2 — Nigeria's construction industries

Construction builds Nigeria's roads, houses and factories, employing many workers.

## Sub lesson 3 — Their contribution to development

Manufacturing and construction together contribute output, jobs and the buildings that development stands on.
