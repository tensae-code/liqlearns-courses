# Manufacturing and construction industries in Nigeria — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does Nigeria's manufacturing make?
**Answer:** Nigeria's manufacturing makes goods from food and drink to cement and textiles for its huge home market.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does construction build in Nigeria?
**Answer:** Construction builds Nigeria's roads, houses and factories, employing many workers.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What do manufacturing and construction contribute?
**Answer:** Manufacturing and construction together contribute output, jobs and the buildings that development stands on.
