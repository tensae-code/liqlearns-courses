# Manufacturing and construction industries in Nigeria — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Nigeria's manufacturing makes nothing for its home market.
**Answer: False** — Nigeria's manufacturing makes goods from food and drink to cement and textiles for its huge home market.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Construction employs almost nobody in Nigeria.
**Answer: False** — Construction builds Nigeria's roads, houses and factories, employing many workers.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Manufacturing and construction contribute nothing to development.
**Answer: False** — Manufacturing and construction together contribute output, jobs and the buildings that development stands on.
