# Manufacturing and construction industries in Nigeria — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** About nigeria's manufacturing industries — the lesson teaches that —
a) nigeria's manufacturing makes goods from food and drink to cement and textiles for its huge home market.  b) construction builds Nigeria's roads, houses and factories, employing many workers.  c) manufacturing and construction together contribute output, jobs and the buildings that development stands on.
**Answer: a) nigeria's manufacturing makes goods from food and drink to cement and textiles for its huge home market.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** About nigeria's construction industries — the lesson teaches that —
a) nigeria's manufacturing makes goods from food and drink to cement and textiles for its huge home market.  b) construction builds Nigeria's roads, houses and factories, employing many workers.  c) manufacturing and construction together contribute output, jobs and the buildings that development stands on.
**Answer: b) construction builds Nigeria's roads, houses and factories, employing many workers.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** About their contribution to development — the lesson teaches that —
a) nigeria's manufacturing makes goods from food and drink to cement and textiles for its huge home market.  b) construction builds Nigeria's roads, houses and factories, employing many workers.  c) manufacturing and construction together contribute output, jobs and the buildings that development stands on.
**Answer: c) manufacturing and construction together contribute output, jobs and the buildings that development stands on.**
