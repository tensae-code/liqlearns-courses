# Manufacturing and construction industries in Nigeria — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Nigeria's manufacturing makes goods from food and drink to cement and _____.
**Answer: textiles**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Construction builds roads, houses and _____ in Nigeria.
**Answer: factories**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Together they contribute output, jobs and the _____ development stands on.
**Answer: buildings**
