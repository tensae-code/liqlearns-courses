# Labour, employment and entrepreneurship — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In this lesson, “Labour” turns on —
a) for economic gain  b) skill and capacity  c) bearing risk to earn profit
**Answer: a) for economic gain**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In this lesson, “Employment and unemployment” turns on —
a) skill and capacity  b) bearing risk to earn profit  c) for economic gain
**Answer: a) skill and capacity**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In this lesson, “Entrepreneurship” turns on —
a) bearing risk to earn profit  b) for economic gain  c) skill and capacity
