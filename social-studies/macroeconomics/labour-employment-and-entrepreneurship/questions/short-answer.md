# Labour, employment and entrepreneurship — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the book teach about labour?
**Answer:** Labour is the active means that sets idle capital and resources moving, and in economics a person who works physically or mentally for economic gain is a labourer, while work done unpaid for one's own pleasure is not labour.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the book teach about employment and unemployment?
**Answer:** Employment is earning income by using one's own labour, skill and capacity; one of working age who has the capacity and skill, has searched and not found paid work, is unemployed.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the book teach about entrepreneurship?
**Answer:** An entrepreneur runs a business bearing risk to earn profit, and entrepreneurship is the effort and innovation that makes it succeed, as with the mushroom farmer who borrowed from his cooperative and employed fifteen people.
