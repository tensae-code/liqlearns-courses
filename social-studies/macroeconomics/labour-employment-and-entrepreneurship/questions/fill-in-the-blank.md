# Labour, employment and entrepreneurship — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Labour is the active means that sets idle capital and resources moving, and in economics a person who works physically or mentally _____ is a labourer, while work done unpaid for one's own pleasure is not labour.
**Answer: for economic gain**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Employment is earning income by using one's own labour, _____; one of working age who has the capacity and skill, has searched and not found paid work, is unemployed.
**Answer: skill and capacity**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** An entrepreneur runs a business _____, and entrepreneurship is the effort and innovation that makes it succeed, as with the mushroom farmer who borrowed from his cooperative and employed fifteen people.
**Answer: bearing risk to earn profit**
