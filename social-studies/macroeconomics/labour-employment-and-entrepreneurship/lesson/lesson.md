# Labour, employment and entrepreneurship

## Sub lesson 1 — Labour

Labour is the active means that sets idle capital and resources moving, and in economics a person who works physically or mentally for economic gain is a labourer, while work done unpaid for one's own pleasure is not labour.

## Sub lesson 2 — Employment and unemployment

Employment is earning income by using one's own labour, skill and capacity; one of working age who has the capacity and skill, has searched and not found paid work, is unemployed.

## Sub lesson 3 — Entrepreneurship

An entrepreneur runs a business bearing risk to earn profit, and entrepreneurship is the effort and innovation that makes it succeed, as with the mushroom farmer who borrowed from his cooperative and employed fifteen people.
