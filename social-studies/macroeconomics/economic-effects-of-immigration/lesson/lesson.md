# Economic effects of immigration

## Do it first

## Sub lesson 1 — More producers and consumers

Immigrants are both **producers and consumers** — immigration grows **production and consumption** together, expanding the whole economy.

## Sub lesson 2 — Effects on wage levels

Immigration's **effects on wage levels** depend on whose work the newcomers **compete with or complement** — wages of close substitutes face pressure, others may gain.

## Sub lesson 3 — Taxes, spending and the history since 1900

Immigrants also change **government taxes and spending** — paying taxes and using services; the book reads all this on **US immigration history since 1900**.
