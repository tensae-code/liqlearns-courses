# Economic effects of immigration — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Immigrants add to —
a) both production and consumption  b) consumption only  c) neither
**Answer: a) both production and consumption**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Wage pressure falls most on —
a) workers whose work the newcomers closely substitute  b) all workers equally  c) no workers
**Answer: a) workers whose work the newcomers closely substitute**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The book's history runs from —
a) 1900  b) 1492  c) 2000
**Answer: a) 1900**
