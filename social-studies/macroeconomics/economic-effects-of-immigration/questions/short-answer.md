# Economic effects of immigration — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What happens to production and consumption when immigration rises?
**Answer:** Both grow, expanding the whole economy.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Whose wages face the most pressure?
**Answer:** Workers whose work the newcomers most closely substitute.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name the government channel in this lesson.
**Answer:** Government taxes and spending — immigrants pay taxes and use services.
