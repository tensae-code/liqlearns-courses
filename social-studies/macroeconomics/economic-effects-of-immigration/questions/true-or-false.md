# Economic effects of immigration — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Immigrants only consume and never produce.
**Answer: False — immigrants are producers as well as consumers.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Immigration lowers every wage by the same amount.
**Answer: False — effects differ: close substitutes face pressure, complements may gain.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Immigrants pay no taxes and use no services.
**Answer: False — immigrants both pay taxes and use services; the net effect is the question.**
