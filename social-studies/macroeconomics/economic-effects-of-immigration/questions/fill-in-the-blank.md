# Economic effects of immigration — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Immigrants are both producers and _____.
**Answer: consumers**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Wage effects depend on whether newcomers' work competes with or _____ local work.
**Answer: complements**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The book reads the effects on US immigration history since _____.
**Answer: 1900**
