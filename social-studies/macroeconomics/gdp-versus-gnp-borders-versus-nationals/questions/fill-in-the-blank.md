# GDP versus GNP — borders versus nationals — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** GNP counts production by a country's _____, wherever in the world they are.
**Answer: nationals**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** GNP = GDP + _____ — what citizens earn abroad minus what foreigners earn at home.
**Answer: net foreign factor income**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** GDP is a _____ — measured per unit of time, not at a point in time.
**Answer: flow**
