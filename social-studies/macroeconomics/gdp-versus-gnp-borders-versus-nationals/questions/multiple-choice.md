# GDP versus GNP — borders versus nationals — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "GNP — the nationals' total"?
a) GNP counts production by the country's nationals, wherever they are  b) GNP = GDP + NFFI; NFFI can be negative  c) a flow is per unit of time, a stock at a point in time; GDP is a flow
**Answer: a) GNP counts production by the country's nationals, wherever they are**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "The bridge — net foreign factor income"?
a) GNP counts production by the country's nationals, wherever they are  b) a flow is per unit of time, a stock at a point in time; GDP is a flow  c) GNP = GDP + NFFI; NFFI can be negative
**Answer: c) GNP = GDP + NFFI; NFFI can be negative**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Stocks versus flows"?
a) GNP = GDP + NFFI; NFFI can be negative  b) a flow is per unit of time, a stock at a point in time; GDP is a flow  c) GNP counts production by the country's nationals, wherever they are
**Answer: b) a flow is per unit of time, a stock at a point in time; GDP is a flow**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** A country has GDP of 500 billion birr and net foreign factor income of −20 billion birr. What is its GNP?
a) 480 billion birr  b) 520 billion birr  c) 500 billion birr
**Answer: a) 480 billion birr** — GNP = GDP + NFFI = 500 + (−20) = 480 billion birr.
