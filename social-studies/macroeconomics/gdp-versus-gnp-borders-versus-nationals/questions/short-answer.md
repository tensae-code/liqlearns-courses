# GDP versus GNP — borders versus nationals — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "GNP — the nationals' total" in your own words.
**Answer:** GNP counts what a country's nationals produce wherever they are in the world.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "The bridge — net foreign factor income" in your own words.
**Answer:** GNP equals GDP plus net foreign factor income.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Stocks versus flows" in your own words.
**Answer:** GDP is a flow, measured per unit of time; a stock is measured at a point in time.
