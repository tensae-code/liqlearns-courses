# GDP versus GNP — borders versus nationals — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** GNP counts what a country's nationals produce wherever they are in the world.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** GNP equals GDP plus net foreign factor income.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** GDP is a flow, measured per unit of time; a stock is measured at a point in time.
**Answer: True**
