# Hands-on — GDP versus GNP — borders versus nationals

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Two overlapping hoops on the floor: 'produced inside Ethiopia' and 'produced by Ethiopians'. Place cards — a New York apartment's rent, a factory in Addis, an Ethiopian pilot working abroad — in the right hoop or the overlap.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — two overlapping circles — produced inside Ethiopia and produced by Ethiopians — with the New York apartment in the second only — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
