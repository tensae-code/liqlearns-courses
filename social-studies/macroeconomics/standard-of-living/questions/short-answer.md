# Standard of living — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name three non-income parts of the standard of living.
**Answer:** Health, education and living conditions.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why does income alone mislead as a welfare measure?
**Answer:** Because health, services, environment and conditions shape real life without passing through money income.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** State the lesson's method for judging living standards.
**Answer:** Read income together with health, education and living conditions — no single number settles it.
