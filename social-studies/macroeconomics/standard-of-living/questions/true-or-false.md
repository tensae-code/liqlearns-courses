# Standard of living — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Two places with equal income per head can have different standards of living.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Public services enter living standards without passing through a wage.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The workbook's next step shows GDP per head alone does not settle how well people live.
**Answer: True**
