# Standard of living — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The standard of living includes, besides income —
a) only savings
b) health, education and living conditions
c) exports
**Answer: b) health, education and living conditions**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Per-capita income is best read as —
a) the whole story of welfare
b) what could be bought — the standard of living asks what life is like
c) a measure of health
**Answer: b) what could be bought — the standard of living asks what life is like**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Living standards should be judged by —
a) GDP per head alone
b) several measures together
c) exports per head
**Answer: b) several measures together**
