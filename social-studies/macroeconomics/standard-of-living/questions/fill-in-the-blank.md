# Standard of living — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The standard of living is more than _____: it includes health, education and living conditions.
**Answer: income**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The standard of living is taught as the necessary _____ of per-capita income.
**Answer: correction**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Judge living standards by several _____ together, never one number.
**Answer: measures**
