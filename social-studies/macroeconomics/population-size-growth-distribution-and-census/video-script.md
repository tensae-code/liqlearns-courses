Population — size, growth, distribution and the census — as Nigeria's book teaches it, says the narrator: a population's size and growth are set by births, deaths and migration, and carry wide implications for the economy.; population is distributed unevenly across places, and its age and sex structure shapes the labour force and dependency.; a census counts and describes the whole population, giving the facts that planning depends on..
