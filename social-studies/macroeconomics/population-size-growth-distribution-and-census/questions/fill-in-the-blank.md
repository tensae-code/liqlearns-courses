# Population — size, growth, distribution and the census — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Population size and growth are set by births, deaths and _____.
**Answer: migration**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The age and sex _____ of a population shapes its labour force.
**Answer: structure**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A _____ counts and describes the whole population.
