# Population — size, growth, distribution and the census — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** About size and growth of population — the lesson teaches that —
a) a population's size and growth are set by births, deaths and migration, and carry wide implications for the economy.  b) population is distributed unevenly across places, and its age and sex structure shapes the labour force and dependency.  c) a census counts and describes the whole population, giving the facts that planning depends on.
