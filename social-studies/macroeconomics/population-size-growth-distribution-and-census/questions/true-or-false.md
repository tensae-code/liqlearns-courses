# Population — size, growth, distribution and the census — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Population growth is set by births alone.
**Answer: False** — A population's size and growth are set by births, deaths and migration, and carry wide implications for the economy.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Population is always distributed evenly across places.
**Answer: False** — Population is distributed unevenly across places, and its age and sex structure shapes the labour force and dependency.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A census counts only the people in one town.
**Answer: False** — A census counts and describes the whole population, giving the facts that planning depends on.
