# Population — size, growth, distribution and the census — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What sets a population's size and growth?
**Answer:** A population's size and growth are set by births, deaths and migration, and carry wide implications for the economy.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does a population's age and sex structure shape?
**Answer:** Population is distributed unevenly across places, and its age and sex structure shapes the labour force and dependency.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What is a census?
**Answer:** A census counts and describes the whole population, giving the facts that planning depends on.
