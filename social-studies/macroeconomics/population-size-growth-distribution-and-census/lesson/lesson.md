# Population — size, growth, distribution and the census

## Do it first

Before you read on, write what you already think this lesson's title means — population — size, growth, distribution and the census — in one sentence of your own. Keep it; you will check it against the lesson at the end.

## Sub lesson 1 — Size and growth of population

A population's size and growth are set by births, deaths and migration, and carry wide implications for the economy.

## Sub lesson 2 — Distribution and structure

Population is distributed unevenly across places, and its age and sex structure shapes the labour force and dependency.

## Sub lesson 3 — The census

A census counts and describes the whole population, giving the facts that planning depends on.
