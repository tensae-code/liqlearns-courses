# Video script — Development means different things to different people

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Development means different things to different people — as India's book teaches it, says the narrator: developmental goals differ and can conflict — a dam means electricity to industrialists and displacement to tribals — and people seek non-material goals (equality, freedom, security, respect) alongside income.
