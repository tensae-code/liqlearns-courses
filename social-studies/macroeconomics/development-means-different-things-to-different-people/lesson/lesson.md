# Development means different things to different people

## Do it first

Before you read on, write what you already think this lesson's title means — development means different things to different people — in one sentence of your own. Keep it; you will check it against the lesson at the end.

## Sub lesson 1 — Goals differ from person to person

What people want from development **differs**: each person or group has its own **developmental goals**, and the goals can **conflict**.

## Sub lesson 2 — The dam — electricity to some, displacement to others

A **dam means electricity to industrialists** but **displacement to tribals** whose land it floods — one project, two opposite meanings.

## Sub lesson 3 — Non-material goals beside income

People also seek **non-material goals — equality, freedom, security and respect** — alongside income; development is not money alone.
