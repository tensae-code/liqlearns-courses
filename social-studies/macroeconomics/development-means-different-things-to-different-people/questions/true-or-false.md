# Development means different things to different people — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Everyone wants exactly the same things from development.
**Answer: False — goals differ and can conflict.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A dam means the same thing to industrialists and tribals.
**Answer: False — electricity to one, displacement to the other.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Development goals are only about income.
**Answer: False — people also seek equality, freedom, security and respect.**
