# Development means different things to different people — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** People's developmental goals differ and can _____.
**Answer: conflict**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A dam means _____ to industrialists, displacement to tribals.
**Answer: electricity**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Beside income, people seek equality, freedom, security and _____.
**Answer: respect**
