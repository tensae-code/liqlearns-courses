# Development means different things to different people — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why can't one development plan please everyone?
**Answer:** Because goals differ and can conflict.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Give the book's example of conflicting goals.
**Answer:** A dam: electricity for industrialists, displacement for tribals.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name two non-material goals.
**Answer:** Any two of: equality, freedom, security, respect.
