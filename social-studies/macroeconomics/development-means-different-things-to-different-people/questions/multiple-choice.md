# Development means different things to different people — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Developmental goals —
a) differ and can conflict  b) are the same for everyone  c) are only about dams
**Answer: a) differ and can conflict**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** To tribals, a dam can mean —
a) displacement  b) electricity profits  c) nothing
**Answer: a) displacement**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Non-material goals include —
a) equality, freedom, security and respect  b) only higher wages  c) only more goods
**Answer: a) equality, freedom, security and respect**
