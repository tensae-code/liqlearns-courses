# Intra-industry trade and economies of scale — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Intra-industry trade means a country —
a) exports and imports goods of the same industry  b) trades only raw materials  c) never imports
**Answer: a) exports and imports goods of the same industry**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Intra-industry trade runs mainly between —
a) similar economies  b) a rich and a poor economy only  c) no economies
**Answer: a) similar economies**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Economies of scale in this lesson mean —
a) unit costs fall as a producer grows large  b) small is always cheaper  c) variety disappears
**Answer: a) unit costs fall as a producer grows large**
