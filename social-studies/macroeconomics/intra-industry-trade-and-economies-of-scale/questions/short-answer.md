# Intra-industry trade and economies of scale — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is intra-industry trade?
**Answer:** A country exporting and importing goods of the same industry at once.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why is this trade a puzzle for the older theories?
**Answer:** Absolute and comparative advantage explain trade between different economies, not two-way trade in like goods between similar ones.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** How do economies of scale explain intra-industry trade?
**Answer:** Each country specialises narrowly, produces at large scale and low unit cost, and imports the other varieties.
