# Intra-industry trade and economies of scale — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Intra-industry trade is trade between different industries only.
**Answer: False — it is trade within the same industry, both ways.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Comparative advantage fully explains intra-industry trade.
**Answer: False — it goes beyond what absolute and comparative advantage explain.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Specialisation under scale economies removes all variety for buyers.
**Answer: False — buyers keep variety through imports of the same industry's other varieties.**
