# Intra-industry trade and economies of scale — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Trade within the same industry, both ways, is _____-industry trade.
**Answer: intra**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** It runs between _____ economies.
**Answer: similar**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Specialising narrowly lets producers grow and cut unit _____ through economies of scale.
**Answer: costs**
