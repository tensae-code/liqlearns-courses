# Intra-industry trade and economies of scale

## Sub lesson 1 — Trade within one industry

**Intra-industry trade** is trade **within the same industry** — a country both **exports and imports** cars, or machines, at once.

## Sub lesson 2 — Between similar economies

This trade runs **between similar economies** — rich countries trading like goods with each other — which **absolute and comparative advantage do not explain** on their own.

## Sub lesson 3 — Its link to economies of scale

The link is **economies of scale**: specialising in a narrow range lets each producer **grow large and cut unit costs**, while buyers still get **variety** from imports.
