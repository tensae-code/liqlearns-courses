# Lesson plan — Marginal propensity to consume

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Macroeconomics · **Lesson:** Marginal propensity to consume
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 4 — Consumption, Saving and Investment of the Grade 11 Economics course (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** MPC is the change in consumption per unit change in income, between 0 and 1; 600 of 1,000 gives 0.6.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the lessons before it in Macroeconomics, and on the earlier-grade lessons of this course. Grade 11 stacks on the Grade 10 Economics foundations in the same titles.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of ..." in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.
