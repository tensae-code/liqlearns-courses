# Marginal propensity to consume — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "The share of each extra birr that is spent"?
a) MPC is the change in consumption per unit change in income, between 0 and 1; 600 of 1,000 gives 0.6.  b) MPC falls as income rises, so the poor have a higher MPC than the rich.  c) Consumption of 640 birr = autonomous 80 + induced 560 (0.7 of 800).
**Answer: a) MPC is the change in consumption per unit change in income, between 0 and 1; 600 of 1,000 gives 0.6.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "MPC is higher for the poor"?

**Answer: c) MPC falls as income rises, so the poor have a higher MPC than the rich.**
