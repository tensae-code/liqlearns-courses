# Marginal propensity to consume — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "The share of each extra birr that is spent" in your own words.
**Answer:** MPC is the change in consumption per unit change in income, between 0 and 1; 600 of 1,000 gives 0.6.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "MPC is higher for the poor" in your own words.
**Answer:** MPC falls as income rises, so the poor have a higher MPC than the rich.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Building consumption from the two parts" in your own words.
**Answer:** Consumption of 640 birr = autonomous 80 + induced 560 (0.7 of 800).
