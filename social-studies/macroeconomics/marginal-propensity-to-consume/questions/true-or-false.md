# Marginal propensity to consume — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** An MPC of 0.6 means 60% of total income is consumed.
**Answer: False — MPC is about the change: 60% of each extra birr is consumed — it says nothing directly about the share of total income.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The rich have a higher MPC than the poor because they spend more overall.
**Answer: False — Spending more overall is not the test — of each extra birr, the rich spend a smaller share, so their MPC is lower.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In the 640-birr example, the 560 birr is the autonomous part.
**Answer: False — The 560 is the induced part — 0.7 of the 800 income. The 80 is autonomous.**
