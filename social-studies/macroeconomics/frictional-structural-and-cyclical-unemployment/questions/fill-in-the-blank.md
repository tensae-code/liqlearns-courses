# Frictional, structural and cyclical unemployment — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Frictional unemployment is brief spells _____ jobs.
**Answer: between**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Structural unemployment is a _____ between seekers' skills or locations and vacancies.
**Answer: mismatch**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Cyclical unemployment follows the downswing of the _____.
**Answer: trade cycle**
