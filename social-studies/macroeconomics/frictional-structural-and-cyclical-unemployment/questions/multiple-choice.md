# Frictional, structural and cyclical unemployment — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Frictional — between jobs"?
a) brief spells between jobs — seasonal ends, job switches, new entrants  b) skills or locations mismatched to vacancies as production's structure changes  c) deficient overall demand in the trade cycle's downswing — vacancies themselves missing
**Answer: a) brief spells between jobs — seasonal ends, job switches, new entrants**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Structural — the mismatch"?
a) brief spells between jobs — seasonal ends, job switches, new entrants  b) deficient overall demand in the trade cycle's downswing — vacancies themselves missing  c) skills or locations mismatched to vacancies as production's structure changes
**Answer: c) skills or locations mismatched to vacancies as production's structure changes**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Cyclical — the missing vacancies"?
a) skills or locations mismatched to vacancies as production's structure changes  b) deficient overall demand in the trade cycle's downswing — vacancies themselves missing  c) brief spells between jobs — seasonal ends, job switches, new entrants
**Answer: b) deficient overall demand in the trade cycle's downswing — vacancies themselves missing**
