# Frictional, structural and cyclical unemployment — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Frictional unemployment is brief spells between jobs, like seasonal work ending or switching jobs for a better one.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Frictional unemployment is brief spells between jobs, like seasonal work ending or switching jobs for a better one.
**Answer: False** — that statement belongs to sub lesson 1 (Frictional — between jobs); sub lesson 2 says: Structural unemployment is a mismatch between seekers' skills or locations and the vacancies' requirements or locations.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Cyclical unemployment comes from deficient overall demand in the downswing of the trade cycle.
**Answer: True**
