# Frictional, structural and cyclical unemployment — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Frictional — between jobs" in your own words.
**Answer:** Frictional unemployment is brief spells between jobs, like seasonal work ending or switching jobs for a better one.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Structural — the mismatch" in your own words.
**Answer:** Structural unemployment is a mismatch between seekers' skills or locations and the vacancies' requirements or locations.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Cyclical — the missing vacancies" in your own words.
**Answer:** Cyclical unemployment comes from deficient overall demand in the downswing of the trade cycle.
