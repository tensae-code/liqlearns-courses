# Hands-on — Frictional, structural and cyclical unemployment

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Vacancy board game: post vacancy cards; frictional players wait one round then match, structural players' skill cards never match until 'retrained', cyclical players face an empty board until 'recovery' cards arrive.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — three workers — one between two jobs, one whose skills fit no vacancy board, one facing a board with no vacancies at all — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
