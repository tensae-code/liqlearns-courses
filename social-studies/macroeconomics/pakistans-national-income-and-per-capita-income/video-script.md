# Video script — Pakistan's national income and per capita income

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Pakistan's national income and per capita income — as Pakistan's book teaches it, says the narrator: Pakistan's income is tabled as GNP at market price and national income at current and constant prices; measuring Pakistan's income is hindered by missing cottage-industry data and non-monetary production; per capita income is low in Pakistan, and the book gives its causes and the measures to raise it.
