## Sub lesson 1 — Pakistan's national income in the book's tables

The Class 12 book measures Pakistan itself: **GNP at market price**, and **national income at current prices and at constant prices**, in the book's own tables. Constant prices strip out inflation so years can be honestly compared.

## Sub lesson 2 — Difficulties of measuring it in Pakistan

Measurement in Pakistan has named difficulties: **lack of data on the cottage industry**, **unresponsible staff** (as the book puts it) and **non-monetary production** — output that never passes through a market and so escapes the counters.

## Sub lesson 3 — Per capita income — low, and how to raise it

The chapter closes with **per capita income in Pakistan**: its level, the **causes of low per capita income**, and the **measures to increase it**. The average share is low because income is divided among a fast-growing population.
