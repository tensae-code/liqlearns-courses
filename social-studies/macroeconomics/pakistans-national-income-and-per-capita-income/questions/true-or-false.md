# Pakistan's national income and per capita income — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** National income at constant prices rises with inflation alone.
**Answer: False** — constant prices strip inflation out so years can be compared.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Pakistan's income is perfectly easy to measure, with no difficulties.
**Answer: False** — the book names three difficulties.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Per capita income cannot be raised by any measure.
**Answer: False** — the book names measures to increase it.
