# Pakistan's national income and per capita income — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** National income measured with inflation stripped out is at _____ prices.
**Answer: constant**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Output that never passes through a market is _____ production.
**Answer: non-monetary**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Income divided by population is _____ income.
**Answer: per capita**
