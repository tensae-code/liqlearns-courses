# Pakistan's national income and per capita income — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How does the book table Pakistan's national income?
**Answer:** As GNP at market price, and national income at current prices and at constant prices.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the difficulties of measuring Pakistan's national income.
**Answer:** Lack of data on the cottage industry, unresponsible staff, and non-monetary production.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the book teach about per capita income in Pakistan?
**Answer:** Its level, the causes of its being low, and the measures to increase it.
