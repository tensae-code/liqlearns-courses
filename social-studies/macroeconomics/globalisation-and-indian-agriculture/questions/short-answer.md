# Globalisation and Indian agriculture — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What did fertiliser subsidy cuts do?
**Answer:** They raised farmers' production costs.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How did lower import duties affect farmers?
**Answer:** They hurt farm incomes by exposing them to cheap imports.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What did farmers do under the double pressure?
**Answer:** They withdrew investment.
