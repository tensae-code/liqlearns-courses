# Globalisation and Indian agriculture — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Subsidy cuts made fertiliser cheaper.
**Answer: False — cuts raised production costs.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Lower import duties always help farm incomes.
**Answer: False — in this case they hurt farm incomes.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Farmers increased investment as costs rose and incomes fell.
**Answer: False — they withdrew investment.**
