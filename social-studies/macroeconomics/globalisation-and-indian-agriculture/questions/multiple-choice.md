# Globalisation and Indian agriculture — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Fertiliser subsidy cuts —
a) raised production costs  b) lowered all costs  c) ended farming
**Answer: a) raised production costs**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Reduced import duties —
a) hurt farm incomes  b) raised all farm incomes  c) stopped imports
**Answer: a) hurt farm incomes**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Squeezed farmers responded by —
a) withdrawing investment  b) doubling investment  c) exporting everything
**Answer: a) withdrawing investment**
