# Globalisation and Indian agriculture

## Do it first

## Sub lesson 1 — Fertiliser subsidy cuts raise production costs

Under globalisation, **fertiliser subsidy cuts raised farmers' production costs** — inputs cost more than before.

## Sub lesson 2 — Reduced import duties hurt farm incomes

**Reduced import duties** let cheap imports in and **hurt Indian farm incomes** — local produce must compete with the world's prices.

## Sub lesson 3 — Farmers withdraw investment

Squeezed both ways, **farmers withdrew investment** from their farms — globalisation reaching all the way to the field.
