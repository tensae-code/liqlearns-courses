# International economic organisations

## Do it first

## Sub lesson 1 — The world bodies

The IMF and the World Bank (IBRD) stand at the centre of the world's economic organisations, lending and setting rules.

## Sub lesson 2 — Regional and trade groupings

Regional bodies such as the ADB and ECA, and groupings such as the EEC, join countries together for development and trade.

## Sub lesson 3 — Trade and development bodies

UNCTAD, GATT and groupings such as WACH work on trade and development between nations.
