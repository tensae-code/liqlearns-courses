# International economic organisations — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which bodies stand at the centre of the world's economic organisations?
**Answer:** The IMF and the World Bank (IBRD) stand at the centre of the world's economic organisations, lending and setting rules.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What do regional economic bodies join countries for?
**Answer:** Regional bodies such as the ADB and ECA, and groupings such as the EEC, join countries together for development and trade.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What do UNCTAD and GATT work on?
**Answer:** UNCTAD, GATT and groupings such as WACH work on trade and development between nations.
