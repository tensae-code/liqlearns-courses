# International economic organisations — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** About the world bodies — the lesson teaches that —
a) the IMF and the World Bank (IBRD) stand at the centre of the world's economic organisations, lending and setting rules.  b) regional bodies such as the ADB and ECA, and groupings such as the EEC, join countries together for development and trade.  c) uNCTAD, GATT and groupings such as WACH work on trade and development between nations.
**Answer: a) the IMF and the World Bank (IBRD) stand at the centre of the world's economic organisations, lending and setting rules.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** About regional and trade groupings — the lesson teaches that —
a) the IMF and the World Bank (IBRD) stand at the centre of the world's economic organisations, lending and setting rules.  b) regional bodies such as the ADB and ECA, and groupings such as the EEC, join countries together for development and trade.  c) uNCTAD, GATT and groupings such as WACH work on trade and development between nations.
**Answer: b) regional bodies such as the ADB and ECA, and groupings such as the EEC, join countries together for development and trade.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** About trade and development bodies — the lesson teaches that —
