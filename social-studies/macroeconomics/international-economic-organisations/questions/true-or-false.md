# International economic organisations — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The IMF and World Bank lend nothing and set no rules.
**Answer: False** — The IMF and the World Bank (IBRD) stand at the centre of the world's economic organisations, lending and setting rules.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Regional economic bodies keep countries apart.
**Answer: False** — Regional bodies such as the ADB and ECA, and groupings such as the EEC, join countries together for development and trade.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** UNCTAD and GATT work against all trade between nations.
**Answer: False** — UNCTAD, GATT and groupings such as WACH work on trade and development between nations.
