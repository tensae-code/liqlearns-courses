# International economic organisations — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The IMF and the _____ Bank stand at the centre of the world's economic organisations.
**Answer: World**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Regional bodies join countries for development and _____.
**Answer: trade**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** UNCTAD and GATT work on trade and _____ between nations.
**Answer: development**
