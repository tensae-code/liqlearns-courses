# Health and the quality of population — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The quality of population is measured by —
a) literacy, life expectancy and skill formation  b) only the number of people  c) only farm output
**Answer: a) literacy, life expectancy and skill formation**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Education spending is tracked at near —
a) 3% of GDP  b) 30% of GDP  c) 0% of GDP
**Answer: a) 3% of GDP**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Beside education spending, the tables track —
a) the growth of health infrastructure  b) only exports  c) only railways
**Answer: a) the growth of health infrastructure**
