# Health and the quality of population — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Population quality has nothing to do with the growth rate.
**Answer: False — the quality of population decides the growth rate.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Education spending is near 30% of GDP.
**Answer: False — it is near 3% of GDP.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Health infrastructure is not part of population quality.
**Answer: False — health, with education, forms the quality of population.**
