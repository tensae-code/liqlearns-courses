# Health and the quality of population — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What three things make up the quality of population?
**Answer:** Literacy, life expectancy and skill formation.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How much of GDP goes to education, as the book tracks it?
**Answer:** Near 3% of GDP.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What health-side growth do the tables track?
**Answer:** The growth of health infrastructure.
