# The objectives of taxation — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "More than revenue" in your own words.
**Answer:** Taxation also aims to reduce inequality and stabilise the economy.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Steering behaviour and growth" in your own words.
**Answer:** Taxes discourage harmful products, incentivise capital formation and steer resources to essentials.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Places, living standards and jobs" in your own words.
**Answer:** Taxation reduces regional imbalance, raises living standards and cuts unemployment while encouraging exports.
