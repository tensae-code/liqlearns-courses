# The objectives of taxation — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Taxation also aims to reduce inequality and stabilise the economy.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Taxes discourage harmful products, incentivise capital formation and steer resources to essentials.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Taxation reduces regional imbalance, raises living standards and cuts unemployment while encouraging exports.
**Answer: True**
