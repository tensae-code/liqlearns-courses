# The objectives of taxation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "More than revenue"?
a) Taxation also aims to reduce inequality and stabilise the economy  b) Taxes discourage harmful products, incentivise capital formation and steer resources to essentials  c) Taxation reduces regional imbalance, raises living standards and cuts unemployment while encouraging exports
**Answer: a) Taxation also aims to reduce inequality and stabilise the economy**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Steering behaviour and growth"?
a) Taxes discourage harmful products, incentivise capital formation and steer resources to essentials  b) Taxation reduces regional imbalance, raises living standards and cuts unemployment while encouraging exports  c) Taxation also aims to reduce inequality and stabilise the economy
**Answer: a) Taxes discourage harmful products, incentivise capital formation and steer resources to essentials**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Places, living standards and jobs"?
a) Taxation reduces regional imbalance, raises living standards and cuts unemployment while encouraging exports  b) Taxation also aims to reduce inequality and stabilise the economy  c) Taxes discourage harmful products, incentivise capital formation and steer resources to essentials
**Answer: a) Taxation reduces regional imbalance, raises living standards and cuts unemployment while encouraging exports**
