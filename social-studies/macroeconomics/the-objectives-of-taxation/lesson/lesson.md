# The objectives of taxation

## Do it first

Revenue is the obvious purpose of tax. Name three more things a government might be trying to do with the same tax — before the book's eight are listed.

## Sub lesson 1 — More than revenue

Beyond revenue, taxation aims to reduce income and wealth inequality, and to stabilise the economy. A tax system only chasing revenue misses the two purposes that justify its shape.


[Picture 25265 — one tax birr splitting into eight streams]
## Sub lesson 2 — Steering behaviour and growth

Taxes discourage harmful products, incentivise private capital formation, and steer scarce resources toward essential goods. The tax is a steering wheel as well as a bucket.

## Sub lesson 3 — Places, living standards and jobs

Taxation also reduces regional imbalance, raises living standards, and cuts unemployment while encouraging exports. Eight objectives in all — one tax birr splitting into eight labelled streams.
