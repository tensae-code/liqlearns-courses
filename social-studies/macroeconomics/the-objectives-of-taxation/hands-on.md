# Hands-on — The objectives of taxation

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Eight streams
Draw one tax birr splitting into eight labelled streams, one per objective. Beside each stream, write one real tax or relief that serves it.

## Purpose audit
Take one tax you know well. Score it against all eight objectives: serving, neutral, or working against. Defend your two hardest scores.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
