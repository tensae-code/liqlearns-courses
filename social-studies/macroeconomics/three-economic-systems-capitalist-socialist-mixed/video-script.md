# Video script — Three economic systems — capitalist, socialist, mixed

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The world's economies run in three systems: capitalist, socialist and mixed — Nepal follows the mixed system.

The economy is the integrated structure of the interrelated activities of production, consumption, exchange, distribution and revenue, and the world has three economic systems.

In the capitalist system price is fixed by the interplay of demand and supply and the private sector leads; the socialist system is wholly state-controlled and directed, a centrally planned economy.

The mixed system combines the good sides of both, and it is the system Nepal and India follow.
