# Three economic systems — capitalist, socialist, mixed — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The economy is the integrated structure of the interrelated activities of production, consumption, exchange, distribution and revenue, and the world has _____.
**Answer: three economic systems**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In the capitalist system price is fixed by the interplay of demand and supply and the private sector leads; the socialist system is wholly state-controlled and directed, _____.
**Answer: a centrally planned economy**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The mixed system combines the good sides of both, and it is the system _____.
**Answer: Nepal and India follow**
