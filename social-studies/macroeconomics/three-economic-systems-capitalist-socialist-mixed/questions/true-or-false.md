# Three economic systems — capitalist, socialist, mixed — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The economy is the integrated structure of the interrelated activities of production, consumption, exchange, distribution and revenue, and the world has three economic systems.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In the capitalist system price is fixed by the interplay of demand and supply and the private sector leads; the socialist system is wholly state-controlled and directed, a centrally planned economy.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The mixed system combines the good sides of both, and it is the system something else entirely.
**Answer: False** — The mixed system combines the good sides of both, and it is the system Nepal and India follow.
