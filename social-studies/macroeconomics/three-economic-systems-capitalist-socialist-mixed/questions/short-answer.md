# Three economic systems — capitalist, socialist, mixed — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the book teach about the economy and its systems?
**Answer:** The economy is the integrated structure of the interrelated activities of production, consumption, exchange, distribution and revenue, and the world has three economic systems.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the book teach about capitalist and socialist systems?
**Answer:** In the capitalist system price is fixed by the interplay of demand and supply and the private sector leads; the socialist system is wholly state-controlled and directed, a centrally planned economy.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the book teach about the mixed system?
**Answer:** The mixed system combines the good sides of both, and it is the system Nepal and India follow.
