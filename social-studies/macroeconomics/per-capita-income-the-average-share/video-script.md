# Video script — Per capita income — the average share

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Per capita income is GDP divided by total population. Two numbers you already have — the country's whole product and its whole people — divided, give the average share.

Per capita income is used to judge the population's standard of living: not how much the economy makes, but how much there is per person living from it. Countries compare themselves, and years compare themselves, through this average.

An average share is not everyone's share: per capita income says nothing about how income is distributed. Read it as the size of the slice if the cake were shared equally — a measure of the cake and the crowd, not of the sharing.
