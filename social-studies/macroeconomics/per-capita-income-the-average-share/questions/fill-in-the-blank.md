# Per capita income — the average share — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Per capita income is GDP _____ by total population.
**Answer: divided**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Per capita income is used to judge the population's _____.
**Answer: standard of living**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Per capita income is an _____ share, not everyone's actual share.
**Answer: average**
