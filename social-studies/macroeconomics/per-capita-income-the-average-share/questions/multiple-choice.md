# Per capita income — the average share — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "The definition"?
a) GDP divided by total population  b) the average used to judge the standard of living  c) an average that hides distribution — equal-share fiction
**Answer: a) GDP divided by total population**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "What it is used for"?
a) GDP divided by total population  b) an average that hides distribution — equal-share fiction  c) the average used to judge the standard of living
**Answer: c) the average used to judge the standard of living**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "An average, with an average's limits"?
a) the average used to judge the standard of living  b) an average that hides distribution — equal-share fiction  c) GDP divided by total population
**Answer: b) an average that hides distribution — equal-share fiction**
