# Per capita income — the average share — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Per capita income is GDP divided by total population.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Per capita income is GDP divided by total population.
**Answer: False** — that statement belongs to sub lesson 1 (The definition); sub lesson 2 says: Per capita income is used to judge the population's standard of living.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Per capita income is an average share; it does not show how income is actually distributed.
**Answer: True**
