# Per capita income — the average share — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "The definition" in your own words.
**Answer:** Per capita income is GDP divided by total population.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "What it is used for" in your own words.
**Answer:** Per capita income is used to judge the population's standard of living.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "An average, with an average's limits" in your own words.
**Answer:** Per capita income is an average share; it does not show how income is actually distributed.
