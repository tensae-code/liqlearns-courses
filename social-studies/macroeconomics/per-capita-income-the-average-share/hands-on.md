# Hands-on — Per capita income — the average share

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Share a pile of 100 counters (income) among 10 learners, then among 20 — the class computes the per-person share both times and names the measure.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — one GDP cake being divided equally among a crowd, one slice labelled per person — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
