# Lesson plan — Fishing and salt in Eritrea's economy

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Macroeconomics · **Lesson:** Fishing and salt in Eritrea's economy
**Place in the curriculum:** Macroeconomics (CURRICULUM.md) — built in the Economics expansion (Eritrea, 2026-11-16).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Red Sea fishing and salt as national-economy earners in Eritrea's Grade 8 telling: fish as food, as industrial inputs and as an export bringing foreign exchange from Europe, the Middle East and the Far East, and sea salt and rock salt mining beside it.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the Grade 8 book's farming lesson — Eritrea's main economic activity — and turns to the sea: the same national survey, one section on, where the country's second great natural earner is taught.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of ..." in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.
