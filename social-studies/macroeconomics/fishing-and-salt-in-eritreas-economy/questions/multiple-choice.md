# Fishing and salt in Eritrea's economy — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In Eritrea's Grade 8 book, the first use of the Red Sea catch is —
a) fish as food  b) fish as money  c) fish as building material
**Answer: a) fish as food**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Eritrea's fish exports go to —
a) only its neighbours  b) Europe, the Middle East and the Far East  c) the Americas only
**Answer: b) Europe, the Middle East and the Far East**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The two kinds of salt the book names for Eritrea are —
a) sea salt and rock salt  b) table salt and bath salt  c) river salt and lake salt
**Answer: a) sea salt and rock salt**
