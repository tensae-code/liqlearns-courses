# Fishing and salt in Eritrea's economy — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Fishing in Eritrea is done in the _____ Sea.
**Answer: Red**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Fish exports bring _____ _____ into the country.
**Answer: foreign exchange**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Eritrea mines _____ salt from the sea and _____ salt from the ground.
**Answer: sea; rock**
