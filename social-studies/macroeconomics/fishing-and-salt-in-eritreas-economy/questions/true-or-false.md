# Fishing and salt in Eritrea's economy — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In the book, fish are only ever food — they are never used by industry or exported.
**Answer: False** — fish are food, industrial inputs, and an export that brings foreign exchange from Europe, the Middle East and the Far East.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Fish exports bring foreign exchange into Eritrea.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Eritrea's salt is all of one kind, taken only from the sea.
**Answer: False** — the book names two kinds: sea salt and rock salt.
