# Fishing and salt in Eritrea's economy — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the three uses of fish in Eritrea's economy, in the book's order.
**Answer:** Fish as food; fish as industrial inputs; fish as an export.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Where do Eritrea's fish exports go, and what do they bring the country?
**Answer:** To Europe, the Middle East and the Far East; they bring foreign exchange.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What two kinds of salt does Eritrea mine, and where does each come from?
**Answer:** Sea salt, taken from the sea along the coast; and rock salt, mined from the ground.
