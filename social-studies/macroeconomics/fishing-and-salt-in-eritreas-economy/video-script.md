# Video script — Fishing and salt in Eritrea's economy

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Farming feeds Eritrea — but Eritrea also has a sea. Its Grade 8 book turns from the fields to the Red Sea coast, and teaches fishing and salt as earners for the whole national economy.

Fish first: food on Eritrean tables. Then industry: fish as inputs for other production. Then export: Eritrean fish sold to Europe, the Middle East and the Far East, bringing foreign exchange home. And beside the fishing, salt — sea salt and rock salt, mined as the coast's second treasure.

Food, industry, export — and salt from sea and rock. That is the Red Sea's place in Eritrea's economy.
