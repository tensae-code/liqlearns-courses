# Hands-on — Fishing and salt in Eritrea's economy

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Tell the Red Sea's story out loud: a fish landed at the coast three ways — eaten as food, used by industry, exported for foreign exchange — then the salt: where each kind comes from.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the coast with three arrows from the fishing boats — FOOD, INDUSTRY, EXPORT — and a salt pan beside them. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare, and add anything you missed, including where the exports go.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 2 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
