# Free trade agreements and CAFTA in Costa Rica

## Sub lesson 1 — Tratados de libre comercio

The free-trade treaties, above all CAFTA with Central America, the Dominican Republic and the United States.

## Sub lesson 2 — Tratados de libre comercio — more

Approved in Costa Rica by referendum — whose problem was that the state stopped collecting import and export taxes on the agreed products, losing an important revenue source;.

## Sub lesson 3 — Tratados de libre comercio — more

The advantages listed (more sales options, cheaper foreign products, new jobs, forced modernisation) and Costa Rica's other treaties: Colombia, Canada, Chile, China, Mexico, Panama, Peru and Singapore.
