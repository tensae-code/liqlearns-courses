# Free trade agreements and CAFTA in Costa Rica — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the lesson teach about tratados de libre comercio?
**Answer:** The free-trade treaties, above all CAFTA with Central America, the Dominican Republic and the United States.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the lesson teach about tratados de libre comercio — more?
**Answer:** Approved in Costa Rica by referendum — whose problem was that the state stopped collecting import and export taxes on the agreed products, losing an important revenue source;.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the lesson teach about tratados de libre comercio — more?
**Answer:** The advantages listed (more sales options, cheaper foreign products, new jobs, forced modernisation) and Costa Rica's other treaties: Colombia, Canada, Chile, China, Mexico, Panama, Peru and Singapore.
