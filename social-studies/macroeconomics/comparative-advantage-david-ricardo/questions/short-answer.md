# Comparative advantage — David Ricardo — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Trade survives one country being better at both" in your own words.
**Answer:** Comparative advantage is judged by opportunity cost: specialise where your advantage is greatest or disadvantage smallest.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Ethiopia and Germany — cars and wine" in your own words.
**Answer:** Germany's opportunity cost is lower in automobiles, Ethiopia's in wine, so Ethiopia exports wine and Germany automobiles.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "The guide's worked numbers — and the criticisms" in your own words.
**Answer:** In the guide's worked case, specialisation gains the world 30 units of coffee and 20 computers (teacher guide — evidence).
