# Video script — Venezuela's monoexport economy — cacao, coffee, oil

Each concentrated population where it grew — cacao in the colonial Costa-Montaña, coffee in its valleys and ports, oil in its oil regions.

It gave jobs in the valleys where it grew and in the export ports that shipped it.

Whether an economy should depend on a single product.
