# Hands-on — Venezuela's monoexport economy — cacao, coffee, oil

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Work the Venezuelan case
Take the lesson's case on paper: cacao — the colonial export. Write its three parts in your own words, one line each, and check them against lesson/lesson.md. Fix any line the lesson does not support.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the chain from memory: Cacao — the colonial export → Coffee — the 19th-century export → Oil — one product still carries the income. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare, and fix what you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
