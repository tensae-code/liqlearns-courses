# Venezuela's monoexport economy — cacao, coffee, oil — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Cacao concentrated population in the colonial _____.
**Answer: Costa-Montaña**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Coffee gave jobs in the valleys and in the export _____.
**Answer: ports**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** An economy depending on one export product is called a _____ economy.
**Answer: monoexport**
