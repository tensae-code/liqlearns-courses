# Venezuela's monoexport economy — cacao, coffee, oil — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What did each of Venezuela's export crops do to its population?
**Answer:** Each concentrated population where it grew — cacao in the colonial Costa-Montaña, coffee in its valleys and ports, oil in its oil regions.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How did coffee employ people, according to the book?
**Answer:** It gave jobs in the valleys where it grew and in the export ports that shipped it.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What question does the book ask about the monoexport economy?
**Answer:** Whether an economy should depend on a single product.
