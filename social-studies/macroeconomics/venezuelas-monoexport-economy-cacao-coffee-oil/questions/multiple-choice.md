# Venezuela's monoexport economy — cacao, coffee, oil — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Venezuela's first great export, in colonial times, was —
a) cacao  b) oil  c) coffee
**Answer: a) cacao**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In the 19th century Venezuela's leading export was —
a) coffee  b) cacao  c) oil
**Answer: a) coffee**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Oil exports displaced coffee from first place from —
a) 1926  b) 1830  c) 1950
**Answer: a) 1926**
