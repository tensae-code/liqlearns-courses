# Venezuela's monoexport economy — cacao, coffee, oil — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Each export crop concentrated population where it grew.
**Answer: True** — that is the book's pattern for cacao, coffee and oil alike.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** When coffee replaced cacao, Venezuela stopped depending on a single export.
**Answer: False** — the product changed but the single-export pattern continued.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Since 1926, one single product — oil — has generated most of Venezuela's national income.
**Answer: True** — that is the book's statement of the monoexport economy.
