## Sub lesson 1 — The factors — economic, political, socio-cultural

Development's factors are sorted into three families: **economic** factors, **political** factors and **socio-cultural** factors. No single family explains a country's rise; the book teaches them together.

## Sub lesson 2 — Sector shares in national income

The chapter weighs the **proportional importance of the sectors in national income** — how much agriculture, industry and services each contribute — because a developing economy's structure itself shifts as it develops.

## Sub lesson 3 — Quality of life in Pakistan

The factors are brought home to **quality of life in Pakistan**: **per capita income, educational backwardness, lack of residential facilities, income disparities** and **increase in inflation** — development judged by how people actually live.
