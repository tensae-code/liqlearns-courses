# Factors of economic development — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What are the families of development factors?
**Answer:** Economic, political and socio-cultural factors.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the chapter weigh about the sectors?
**Answer:** Their proportional importance — their shares — in national income.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** How is quality of life in Pakistan judged in the book?
**Answer:** By per capita income, educational backwardness, lack of residential facilities, income disparities and increase in inflation.
