# Factors of economic development — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Only economic factors matter for development.
**Answer: False** — the book sorts them into three families, all taught together.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Sector shares never change as an economy develops.
**Answer: False** — they shift — that shift is part of development itself.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Quality of life is judged only by the number of factories.
**Answer: False** — the book judges it by how people actually live — five named measures.
