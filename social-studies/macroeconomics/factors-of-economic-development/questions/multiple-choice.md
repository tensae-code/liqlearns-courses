# Factors of economic development — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** About the factors — economic, political, socio-cultural — the lesson teaches that —
a) the factors of development are economic, political and socio-cultural  b) the sectors' proportional shares in national income show the economy's structure  c) quality of life in Pakistan is judged by income, education, housing, disparities and inflation
