# Video script — How tourism earns a country money

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

How tourism earns a country money — as South Sudan's book teaches it, says the narrator: A tourist pays foreign currency for services, which government can use to build schools, roads and hospitals. Tourism earns foreign exchange, government revenue from taxes and licence fees paid by tourist businesses, and employment (tour guides, drivers). It brings infrastructure development around sites and a market for agriculture and local industry (carvings, baskets sold to tourists). People who are employed are paid, and people who sell things get money.
