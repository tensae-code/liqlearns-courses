# How tourism earns a country money

## Do it first

Before you read on, write what you already think this lesson's title means — how tourism earns a country money — in one sentence of your own. Keep it; you will check it against the lesson at the end.

## Sub lesson 1 — Foreign currency and foreign exchange

A **tourist pays foreign currency** for services, which **government can use to build schools, roads and hospitals**. Tourism earns **foreign exchange**.

## Sub lesson 2 — Revenue and employment

Tourism brings **government revenue from taxes and licence fees** paid by tourist businesses, and **employment** — tour guides, drivers. People who are employed are paid.

## Sub lesson 3 — Infrastructure and a market for local goods

Tourism brings **infrastructure development around sites** and a **market for agriculture and local industry** — carvings and baskets sold to tourists. People who sell things get money.
