# How tourism earns a country money — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In your own words, what does sub lesson 1 teach about foreign currency and foreign exchange?
**Answer:** A tourist pays foreign currency for services, which government can use to build schools, roads and hospitals. Tourism earns foreign exchange.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In your own words, what does sub lesson 2 teach about revenue and employment?
**Answer:** Tourism brings government revenue from taxes and licence fees paid by tourist businesses, and employment — tour guides, drivers. People who are employed are paid.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In your own words, what does sub lesson 3 teach about infrastructure and a market for local goods?
**Answer:** Tourism brings infrastructure development around sites and a market for agriculture and local industry — carvings and baskets sold to tourists. People who sell things get money.
