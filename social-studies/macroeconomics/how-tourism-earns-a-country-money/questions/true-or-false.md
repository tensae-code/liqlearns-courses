# How tourism earns a country money — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A tourist pays foreign currency for services, which government can use to build schools, roads and hospitals. Tourism earns foreign exchange.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Tourism brings government revenue from taxes and licence fees paid by tourist businesses, and employment — tour guides, drivers. People who are employed are paid.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Tourism brings infrastructure development around sites and a market for agriculture and local industry — carvings and baskets sold to tourists. People who sell things get money.
**Answer: True**
