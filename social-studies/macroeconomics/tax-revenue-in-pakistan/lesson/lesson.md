## Sub lesson 1 — Pakistan's tax take read as data

The Class 12 public finance chapter turns to Pakistan itself: the **ratio of tax revenue** — how much of the country's income the state actually collects as tax. Read as data, the ratio is the first verdict on the system.

## Sub lesson 2 — An ineffective tax system

The book's verdict is plain: Pakistan's tax system is **ineffective** — it does not collect what the country's needs require, and its weight falls in a **regressive-leaning** pattern, pressing harder on those least able to pay.

## Sub lesson 3 — Examined against the canons

The chapter examines the system **against the canons of taxation** — the tests a good tax must pass. Pakistan's own revenue is marked, canon by canon, against the standard the book taught just before.
