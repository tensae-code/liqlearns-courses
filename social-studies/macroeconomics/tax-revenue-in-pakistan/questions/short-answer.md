# Tax revenue in Pakistan — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How does the chapter first judge Pakistan's tax system?
**Answer:** By the ratio of tax revenue — how much of national income is actually collected as tax.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is the book's verdict on Pakistan's tax system?
**Answer:** It is ineffective and regressive-leaning — it under-collects and presses harder on those least able to pay.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Against what standard is Pakistan's tax system examined?
**Answer:** Against the canons of taxation — the tests a good tax must satisfy.
