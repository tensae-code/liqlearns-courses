# Tax revenue in Pakistan — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Tax revenue read against national income is its _____.
**Answer: ratio**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A tax pattern pressing harder on those least able to pay leans _____.
**Answer: regressive**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The standard Pakistan's taxes are marked against is the _____ of taxation.
**Answer: canons**
