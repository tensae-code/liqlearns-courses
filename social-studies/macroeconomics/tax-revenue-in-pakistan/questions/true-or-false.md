# Tax revenue in Pakistan — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Pakistan collects all of its national income as tax.
**Answer: False** — the ratio is a share — and reading it is the chapter's first step.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The book finds Pakistan's tax system fully effective.
**Answer: False** — its verdict is ineffective, with a regressive lean.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The chapter judges Pakistan's taxes with no standard at all.
**Answer: False** — it marks them against the canons taught just before.
