# Land concentration haciendas and minifundios — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** About most campesinos live on small mountain farms minifundios — the lesson teaches that —
a) Most campesinos live on small mountain farms (minifundios)  b) While in flat territories land ownership and agroindustry concentrate in a few hands — the cattle  c) Sugar and coffee haciendas
**Answer: a) Most campesinos live on small mountain farms (minifundios)**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** About while in flat territories land ownership and agroindustry — the lesson teaches that —
a) Most campesinos live on small mountain farms (minifundios)  b) While in flat territories land ownership and agroindustry concentrate in a few hands — the cattle  c) Sugar and coffee haciendas
**Answer: b) While in flat territories land ownership and agroindustry concentrate in a few hands — the cattle**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** About sugar and coffee haciendas — the lesson teaches that —
a) Most campesinos live on small mountain farms (minifundios)  b) While in flat territories land ownership and agroindustry concentrate in a few hands — the cattle  c) Sugar and coffee haciendas
**Answer: c) Sugar and coffee haciendas**
