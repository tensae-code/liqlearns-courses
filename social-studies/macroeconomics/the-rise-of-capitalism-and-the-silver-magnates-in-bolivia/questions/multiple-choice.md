# The rise of capitalism and the silver magnates in Bolivia — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** About mining revives after 1850 — the lesson teaches that —
a) cheaper Californian mercury, steam machinery and international capital revived Bolivian mining from about 1850  b) Arce, Aramayo and Pacheco bought mines already rehabilitated by ruined predecessors  c) the silver magnates formed a new pressure group for free trade

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** About a new pressure group for free trade — the lesson teaches that —
