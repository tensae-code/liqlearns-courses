# The rise of capitalism and the silver magnates in Bolivia — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the lesson teach about mining revives after 1850?
**Answer:** Cheaper Californian mercury, steam machinery and international capital revived Bolivian mining from about 1850.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the lesson teach about the silver magnates buy the rehabilitated mines?
**Answer:** Arce, Aramayo and Pacheco bought mines already rehabilitated by ruined predecessors.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the lesson teach about a new pressure group for free trade?
**Answer:** The silver magnates formed a new pressure group for free trade.
