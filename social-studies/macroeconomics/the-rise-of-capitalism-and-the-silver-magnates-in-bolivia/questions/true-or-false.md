# The rise of capitalism and the silver magnates in Bolivia — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Mining revived after 1850 with no foreign capital involved.
**Answer: False** — international capital is one of the three revivers the book names.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The magnates built all their mines from nothing.
**Answer: False** — they bought mines already rehabilitated by ruined predecessors.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The magnates pressed for higher export taxes on silver.
**Answer: False** — their pressure group stood for free trade.
