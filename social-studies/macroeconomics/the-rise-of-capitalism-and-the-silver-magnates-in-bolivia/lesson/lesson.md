# The rise of capitalism and the silver magnates in Bolivia

## Sub lesson 1 — Mining revives after 1850

From about 1850, cheaper Californian mercury, steam machinery and international capital revived Bolivia's mining. The industry that had slept since independence woke with new inputs, new machines and foreign money.

## Sub lesson 2 — The silver magnates buy the rehabilitated mines

The future silver magnates — Arce at Huanchaca, Aramayo, Pacheco — bought mines already rehabilitated by ruined predecessors. A new mining capitalism stood on the shoulders of the men it replaced.

## Sub lesson 3 — A new pressure group for free trade

The magnates formed a new pressure group in Bolivian politics — and its programme was free trade: open exports, low barriers, the state's hands off the silver trade their fortunes rode on.
