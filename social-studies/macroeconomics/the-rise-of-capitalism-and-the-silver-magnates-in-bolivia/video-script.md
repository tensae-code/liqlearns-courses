The rise of capitalism and the silver magnates in Bolivia — as Bolivia's book teaches it, says the narrator: cheaper Californian mercury, steam machinery and international capital revived Bolivian mining from about 1850; Arce, Aramayo and Pacheco bought mines already rehabilitated by ruined predecessors; the silver magnates formed a new pressure group for free trade.
