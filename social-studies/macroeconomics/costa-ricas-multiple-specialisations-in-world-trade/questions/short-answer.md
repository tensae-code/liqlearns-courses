# Costa Rica's multiple specialisations in world trade — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the lesson teach about la especialización de los países?
**Answer:** Countries specialise as industrialised.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the lesson teach about la especialización de los países — more?
**Answer:** Raw-material producers or service sellers, but Costa Rica holds several at once.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the lesson teach about la especialización de los países — more?
**Answer:** Coffee and banana, Intel industry, Dos Pinos agroindustry, and services such as tourism, health and call centres.
