# Japan industrialises by importing raw materials and learning technology — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Japan, agricultural at 1900 with only 20% cultivable land, industrialised vertiginously after the Second World War;
**Answer: True** — this is what Colombia's book teaches in this part.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Sub lesson 2 is mainly about japan agricultural at 1900 with only 20 cultivable.
**Answer: False** — sub lesson 2 is about lacking natural resources and energy sources it imported; japan agricultural at 1900 with only 20 cultivable is sub lesson 1.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Lacking natural resources and energy sources it imported raw materials (copper from Peru and Chile) and sent people to Europe and North America to learn their technology
**Answer: True** — this is what Colombia's book teaches in this part.
