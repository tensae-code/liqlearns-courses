# Video script — The economy of the Islamic civilisation

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The economy of the Islamic civilisation — as Kuwait's book teaches it, says the narrator: the economic manifestations of Islamic civilisation as Kuwait's Grade 8 book teaches them: the Islamic state's farming push (irrigation canals, dams as water stores), industries such as cheap paper after Samarkand and wood crafts, trade centres reaching China, India and South-East Asia, and market supervision (the muhtasib).
