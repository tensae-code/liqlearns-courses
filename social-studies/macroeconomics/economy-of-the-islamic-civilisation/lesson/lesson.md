# The economy of the Islamic civilisation

## Do it first

Before you read on, write what you already think this lesson's title means — the economy of the islamic civilisation — in one sentence of your own. Keep it; you will check it against the lesson at the end.

## Sub lesson 1 — The farming push — irrigation canals and dams

The Islamic state pushed farming forward: **irrigation canals** were dug and **dams built as water stores**, so water reached the fields the civilisation fed itself from.

## Sub lesson 2 — Industries — cheap paper and wood crafts

Its industries grew with its farming: **cheap paper after Samarkand** — paper-making learned and spread — and **wood crafts** stand among the industries the book names.

## Sub lesson 3 — Trade centres and the market inspector

Its **trade centres reached China, India and South-East Asia**, and its markets were supervised: the **muhtasib**, the market inspector, watched over the market's life.
