# The economy of the Islamic civilisation — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How did the Islamic state push farming?
**Answer:** With irrigation canals and dams as water stores.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name two industries of the civilisation.
**Answer:** Cheap paper (after Samarkand) and wood crafts.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Who supervised the markets?
**Answer:** The muhtasib, the market inspector.
