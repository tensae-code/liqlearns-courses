The Structural Adjustment Programme in Nigeria — as Nigeria's book teaches it, says the narrator: the Structural Adjustment Programme was Nigeria's programme of economic reform begun in 1986 to restructure the economy.; sAP's measures included devaluing the naira, removing subsidies and opening trade and prices to the market.; sAP is debated to this day: its supporters credit it with correcting distortions, its critics with hardship and deindustrialisation..
