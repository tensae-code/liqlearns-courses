## Sub lesson 1 — What SAP was

The Structural Adjustment Programme was Nigeria's programme of economic reform begun in 1986 to restructure the economy.

## Sub lesson 2 — SAP's measures

SAP's measures included devaluing the naira, removing subsidies and opening trade and prices to the market.

## Sub lesson 3 — SAP's effects and debates

SAP is debated to this day: its supporters credit it with correcting distortions, its critics with hardship and deindustrialisation.
