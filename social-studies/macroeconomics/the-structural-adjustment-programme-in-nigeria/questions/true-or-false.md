# The Structural Adjustment Programme in Nigeria — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** SAP was a programme to keep Nigeria's economy exactly unchanged.
**Answer: False** — The Structural Adjustment Programme was Nigeria's programme of economic reform begun in 1986 to restructure the economy.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** SAP's measures included fixing all prices forever.
**Answer: False** — SAP's measures included devaluing the naira, removing subsidies and opening trade and prices to the market.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Everyone agrees SAP had only good effects.
**Answer: False** — SAP is debated to this day: its supporters credit it with correcting distortions, its critics with hardship and deindustrialisation.
