# The Structural Adjustment Programme in Nigeria — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What was the Structural Adjustment Programme?
**Answer:** The Structural Adjustment Programme was Nigeria's programme of economic reform begun in 1986 to restructure the economy.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What measures did SAP include?
**Answer:** SAP's measures included devaluing the naira, removing subsidies and opening trade and prices to the market.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** How is SAP debated?
**Answer:** SAP is debated to this day: its supporters credit it with correcting distortions, its critics with hardship and deindustrialisation.
