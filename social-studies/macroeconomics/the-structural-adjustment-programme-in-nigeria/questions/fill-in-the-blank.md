# The Structural Adjustment Programme in Nigeria — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** SAP was Nigeria's programme of economic _____ begun in 1986.
**Answer: reform**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** SAP included devaluing the _____ and removing subsidies.
**Answer: naira**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** SAP's critics charge it with hardship and _____.
**Answer: deindustrialisation**
