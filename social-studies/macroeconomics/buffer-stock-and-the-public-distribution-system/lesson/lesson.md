## Sub lesson 1 — Buffer stock bought by the FCI at MSP

The government buys grain through the **FCI** at the **Minimum Support Price (MSP)** and holds it as a **buffer stock** against bad years.

## Sub lesson 2 — The PDS — from universal to targeted

The **Public Distribution System (PDS)** sells grain cheaply through ration shops; it evolved from **universal** coverage to **revamped** and then **targeted (TPDS)** coverage, with **Antyodaya** for the poorest.

## Sub lesson 3 — The National Food Security Act 2013 and the subsidy

The **National Food Security Act 2013** made food a legal right for much of the population; the gap between the cost of grain and its cheap **issue price** is the **subsidy** the government pays.
