# Buffer stock and the Public Distribution System — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Who procures the buffer stock, and at what price?
**Answer:** The FCI, at the Minimum Support Price (MSP).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How did the PDS evolve?
**Answer:** From universal to revamped, then targeted (TPDS), with Antyodaya for the poorest.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What did the 2013 Act do, and what is the subsidy?
**Answer:** It made food a legal right; the subsidy is the gap between grain's cost and its issue price.
