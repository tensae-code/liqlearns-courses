# Buffer stock and the Public Distribution System — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Buffer stock is bought at whatever price the market reaches.
**Answer: False — it is procured at the Minimum Support Price.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The PDS stayed universal forever.
**Answer: False — it became targeted, with Antyodaya for the poorest.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Issue prices are set above the full cost of grain.
**Answer: False — issue prices are below cost; the gap is the subsidy.**
