# Buffer stock and the Public Distribution System — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Buffer stock is procured by the FCI at —
a) the Minimum Support Price  b) any price farmers ask  c) import prices
**Answer: a) the Minimum Support Price**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Antyodaya is the PDS part for —
a) the poorest  b) exporters  c) large farmers only
**Answer: a) the poorest**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The National Food Security Act is from —
a) 2013  b) 1943  c) 1991
**Answer: a) 2013**
