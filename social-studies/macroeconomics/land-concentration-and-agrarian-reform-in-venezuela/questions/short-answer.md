# Land concentration and agrarian reform in Venezuela — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How was land held before the conquest, and how did the conquerors work it after?
**Answer:** Indigenous families worked it communally and no one owned it; the conquerors seized it for export production under mercantilism, through encomiendas and enslaved Africans.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Describe Venezuela's land tenure through the 19th and 20th centuries as the book gives it.
**Answer:** Unjust tenure continued: land concentrated in few hands, fertile extensions unused, and peasants staying poor.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What do agrarian reforms and censuses aim at, and what provides most of Venezuela's income now?
**Answer:** They aim to put land in peasants' hands and guarantee food sovereignty; oil now provides the country's biggest share of income.
