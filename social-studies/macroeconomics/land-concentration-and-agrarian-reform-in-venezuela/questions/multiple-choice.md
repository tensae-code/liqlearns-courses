# Land concentration and agrarian reform in Venezuela — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Before the conquest, indigenous families —
a) worked the land communally and no one owned it  b) sold land on a market  c) worked as wage labourers
**Answer: a) worked the land communally and no one owned it**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Through the 19th and 20th centuries, Venezuelan land —
a) stayed concentrated in few hands, with fertile extensions unused while peasants stayed poor  b) was evenly divided among peasants  c) was mostly given to cooperatives
**Answer: a) stayed concentrated in few hands, with fertile extensions unused while peasants stayed poor**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Agrarian reforms and agricultural censuses aim to —
a) put land in peasants' hands and guarantee food sovereignty  b) concentrate land further  c) end farming in Venezuela
**Answer: a) put land in peasants' hands and guarantee food sovereignty**
