# Land concentration and agrarian reform in Venezuela — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The conquerors seized the land for export production under mercantilism.
**Answer: True** — that is the chapter's account of the seizure.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Land concentration in Venezuela ended with independence.
**Answer: False** — unjust land tenure continued through the 19th and 20th centuries.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Oil now provides Venezuela's biggest share of income, as the chapter states.
**Answer: True** — stated alongside the case for agrarian reform.
