# Hands-on — Land concentration and agrarian reform in Venezuela

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Work the Venezuelan case
Take the lesson's case on paper: communal land, then seizure — encomiendas and enslaved labour. Write its three parts in your own words, one line each, and check them against lesson/lesson.md. Fix any line the lesson does not support.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the chain from memory: Communal land, then seizure — encomiendas and enslaved labour → Concentration through two centuries → Agrarian reform, censuses and food sovereignty. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare, and fix what you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
