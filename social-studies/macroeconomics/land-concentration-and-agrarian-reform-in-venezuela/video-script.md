# Video script — Land concentration and agrarian reform in Venezuela

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Indigenous families worked it communally and no one owned it; the conquerors seized it for export production under mercantilism, through encomiendas and enslaved Africans.

Unjust tenure continued: land concentrated in few hands, fertile extensions unused, and peasants staying poor.

They aim to put land in peasants' hands and guarantee food sovereignty; oil now provides the country's biggest share of income.
