# The Cochabamba Water War and water privatisation — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the lesson teach about law 2029 and the concession to Aguas del Tunari?
**Answer:** Law 2029 (1999) gave Cochabamba's water concession to Aguas del Tunari, with tariffs indexed to the US dollar.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the lesson teach about blockades and the April 2000 strike?
**Answer:** Blockades and the April 2000 indefinite strike forced Aguas del Tunari to withdraw and the law to change.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the lesson teach about water in the Constitution — a fundamental right for life?
**Answer:** The Constitution now makes water a fundamental right for life.
