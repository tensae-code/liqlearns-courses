# The Cochabamba Water War and water privatisation — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Law 2029 made water free for all Cochabamba households.
**Answer: False** — it let authorised companies supply water and set prices, indexed to the dollar.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Aguas del Tunari stayed and raised its tariffs after 2000.
**Answer: False** — after the strike the company withdrew from Bolivia and the law changed.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The Constitution declares water a commodity like any other.
**Answer: False** — it makes water a fundamental right for life.
