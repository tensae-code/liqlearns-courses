# The Cochabamba Water War and water privatisation

## Sub lesson 1 — Law 2029 and the concession to Aguas del Tunari

Law 2029 (1999) let only authorised companies supply drinking water and set prices; tariffs were to be indexed to the US dollar, and the concession for Cochabamba's water went to Aguas del Tunari. Water — the book's Year 4 unit asks whether it is a commodity, a human right or a force of life — was made a business.

## Sub lesson 2 — Blockades and the April 2000 strike

The city answered: blockades, and in April 2000 an indefinite strike. Under that pressure the company withdrew from Bolivia and the law was changed — the Water War won.

## Sub lesson 3 — Water in the Constitution — a fundamental right for life

The settlement outlived the war: Bolivia's Constitution (CPE) now makes water a fundamental right for life — the humanist and indigenous visions of the Year 4 unit written into the state's founding text, against the neoliberal vision of water as merchandise.
