The Cochabamba Water War and water privatisation — as Bolivia's book teaches it, says the narrator: Law 2029 (1999) gave Cochabamba's water concession to Aguas del Tunari, with tariffs indexed to the US dollar; blockades and the April 2000 indefinite strike forced Aguas del Tunari to withdraw and the law to change; the Constitution now makes water a fundamental right for life.
