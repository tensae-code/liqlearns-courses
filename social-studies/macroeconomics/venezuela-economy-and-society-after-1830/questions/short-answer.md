# Venezuela — economy and society after 1830 — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What two things could the scarce treasury not do after independence?
**Answer:** It could not drive the economy, and could not pay Venezuela's share of Gran Colombia's external debt.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the book's export table compare?
**Answer:** Export weights in 1830–31 against 1845–46 for indigo, cacao, coffee and hides.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Why did coffee producers borrow from moneylenders?
**Answer:** Because world coffee prices decided bonanza or crisis, and in the bad times producers borrowed to keep going — until the 1834 Law of Freedom of Contracts.
