# Venezuela — economy and society after 1830 — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** After independence Venezuela was rich enough to drive its economy easily.
**Answer: False** — it faced poverty and a scarce national treasury.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Coffee became Venezuela's main export in this period.
**Answer: True** — the book's export table (1830–31 vs 1845–46) shows coffee's rise.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** World coffee prices had little effect on Venezuela's economy in this period.
**Answer: False** — they decided bonanza or crisis.
