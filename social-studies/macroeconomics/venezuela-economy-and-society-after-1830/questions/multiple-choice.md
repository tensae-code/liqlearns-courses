# Venezuela — economy and society after 1830 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** After independence Venezuela's national treasury was —
a) scarce — too little to drive the economy or pay its debt share  b) overflowing with coffee money  c) exactly enough for every need
**Answer: a) scarce — too little to drive the economy or pay its debt share**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Free-trade laws after 1830 favoured —
a) merchants and export landowners, while others stayed poor  b) landless peasants above all  c) nobody in particular
**Answer: a) merchants and export landowners, while others stayed poor**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In the coffee economy, bonanza or crisis was decided by —
a) world coffee prices  b) the weather in Europe only  c) the treasury's savings
**Answer: a) world coffee prices**
