# Migration — causes, types and effects

## Do it first

## Sub lesson 1 — Types of migration

Migration is the movement of people to live elsewhere — within a country or across borders, voluntary or forced.

## Sub lesson 2 — Causes of migration

People migrate for work, income, services and safety — pushed by hardship at home and pulled by opportunity elsewhere.

## Sub lesson 3 — Effects of migration

Migration changes both the place left and the place arrived at — labour, incomes, remittances and pressure on services.
