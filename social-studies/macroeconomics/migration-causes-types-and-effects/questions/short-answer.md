# Migration — causes, types and effects — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is migration?
**Answer:** Migration is the movement of people to live elsewhere — within a country or across borders, voluntary or forced.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why do people migrate?
**Answer:** People migrate for work, income, services and safety — pushed by hardship at home and pulled by opportunity elsewhere.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does migration change?
**Answer:** Migration changes both the place left and the place arrived at — labour, incomes, remittances and pressure on services.
