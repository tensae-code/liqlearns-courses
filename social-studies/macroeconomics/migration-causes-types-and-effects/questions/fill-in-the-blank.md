# Migration — causes, types and effects — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Migration is movement to live elsewhere, within a country or across _____.
**Answer: borders**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** People are pushed by hardship and _____ by opportunity elsewhere.
**Answer: pulled**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Migration changes both the place left and the place _____ at.
**Answer: arrived**
