# Migration — causes, types and effects — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** About types of migration — the lesson teaches that —
a) migration is the movement of people to live elsewhere — within a country or across borders, voluntary or forced.  b) people migrate for work, income, services and safety — pushed by hardship at home and pulled by opportunity elsewhere.  c) migration changes both the place left and the place arrived at — labour, incomes, remittances and pressure on services.
