# Migration — causes, types and effects — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Migration only ever happens across international borders.
**Answer: False** — Migration is the movement of people to live elsewhere — within a country or across borders, voluntary or forced.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** People migrate only for holidays.
**Answer: False** — People migrate for work, income, services and safety — pushed by hardship at home and pulled by opportunity elsewhere.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Migration changes only the place people leave.
**Answer: False** — Migration changes both the place left and the place arrived at — labour, incomes, remittances and pressure on services.
