# Video script — Migration — causes, types and effects

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Migration — causes, types and effects — as Nigeria's book teaches it, says the narrator: migration is the movement of people to live elsewhere — within a country or across borders, voluntary or forced.; people migrate for work, income, services and safety — pushed by hardship at home and pulled by opportunity elsewhere.; migration changes both the place left and the place arrived at — labour, incomes, remittances and pressure on services..
