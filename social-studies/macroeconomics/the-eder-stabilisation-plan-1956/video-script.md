The Eder stabilisation plan, 1956 — as Bolivia's book teaches it, says the narrator: with inflation reaching 900%, the Eder Plan's decree of 15 December 1956 set out to stop the hyperinflation; the Eder Plan unified the exchange rate, ended price controls and subsidies, and held bank credit rigidly tight; the Eder Plan was a condition for a US$25 million US loan, and its tariff cuts bankrupted small national industries.
