# The Eder stabilisation plan, 1956 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** About inflation at 900% — the plan arrives — the lesson teaches that —
a) with inflation reaching 900%, the Eder Plan's decree of 15 December 1956 set out to stop the hyperinflation  b) the Eder Plan unified the exchange rate, ended price controls and subsidies, and held bank credit rigidly tight  c) the Eder Plan was a condition for a US$25 million US loan, and its tariff cuts bankrupted small national industries
