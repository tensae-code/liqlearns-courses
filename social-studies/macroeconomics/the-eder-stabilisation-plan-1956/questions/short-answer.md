# The Eder stabilisation plan, 1956 — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the lesson teach about inflation at 900% — the plan arrives?
**Answer:** With inflation reaching 900%, the Eder Plan's decree of 15 December 1956 set out to stop the hyperinflation.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the lesson teach about the measures — one exchange rate, no controls, credit held tight?
**Answer:** The Eder Plan unified the exchange rate, ended price controls and subsidies, and held bank credit rigidly tight.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the lesson teach about a relative success — bought with austerity, conditioned by a loan?
**Answer:** The Eder Plan was a condition for a US$25 million US loan, and its tariff cuts bankrupted small national industries.
