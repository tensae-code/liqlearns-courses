# The Eder stabilisation plan, 1956 — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Inflation in 1956 was under 5%.
**Answer: False** — the student book puts it at 900% — that is why the plan came.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The plan multiplied the exchange rates and raised subsidies.
**Answer: False** — it unified the rate and eliminated controls and subsidies.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The plan cost Bolivia nothing and hurt no one.
**Answer: False** — the book names the bill: austerity, bankrupt small industries, wages behind prices.
