# Consumption drives production and growth — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Consumption is the purpose and driving force of production.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Rising consumption can create jobs and increase state budget revenue.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Spending far beyond income on luxury is praised in the lesson.
**Answer: False — the lesson calls luxury consumption far beyond income waste.**
