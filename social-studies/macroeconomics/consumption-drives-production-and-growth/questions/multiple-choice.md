# Consumption drives production and growth — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Consumption is likened to production's —
a) "order" from society  b) tax bill  c) raw material
**Answer: a) "order" from society**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** As incomes rise, high-end consumption —
a) grows and stimulates new market segments  b) disappears  c) is forbidden
**Answer: a) grows and stimulates new market segments**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Luxury consumption far beyond income is —
a) investment  b) waste  c) saving
**Answer: b) waste**
