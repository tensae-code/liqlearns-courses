# Consumption drives production and growth — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why is consumption called production's driving force?
**Answer:** Because it is production's purpose — what people consume orders what production makes, and positive changes in consumption push production to develop.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name three effects of growing consumption on the economy.
**Answer:** Any three of: new market segments stimulated, market structure diversified, total demand raised, market's attractiveness to investment raised, jobs created, state budget revenue increased, living standards raised.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Where does the lesson draw the line on consumption?
**Answer:** At luxury consumption far beyond income, which it calls waste.
