# Video script — Consumption drives production and growth

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Vietnam's Grade 11 book turns consumption from an ending into a beginning: consumption is society's order to production.

It is production's purpose and its driving force. When incomes rise and consumption grows, new market segments open, demand rises, investment follows, jobs appear and the state budget gains.

One warning closes the lesson: luxury consumption far beyond income is not development — it is waste.
