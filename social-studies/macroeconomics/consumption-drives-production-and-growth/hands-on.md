# Hands-on — Consumption drives production and growth

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the lesson's own activity
Trace one growing consumption habit around you — a new drink, a delivery app, a style of shoe. Follow the order it placed: what production answered it, what new segment or jobs appeared, and whether the spending sits within people's incomes or beyond them. Write the chain and your verdict on its last link.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main sorting or chain from memory and label its parts with the lesson's key words. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare, and fix every label you placed wrong.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
