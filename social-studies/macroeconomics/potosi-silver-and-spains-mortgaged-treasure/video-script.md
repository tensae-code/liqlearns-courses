Potosí silver and Spain's mortgaged treasure — as Bolivia's book teaches it, says the narrator: silver made up 99% of Spanish America's exports and stimulated Europe's economy; the silver stimulated Europe's economy but not Spain's — Spain had the cow, others drank the milk; the Crown granted whole silver shipments in advance to foreign bankers, and taxes paid debt annuities — only a minimal part of the silver entered Spain's economy.
