# Potosí silver and Spain's mortgaged treasure — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Silver was a minor part of Spanish America's exports.
**Answer: False** — 99% — nearly the whole of them, in the book's figure.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Spain kept and used all of Potosí's silver at home.
**Answer: False** — the heading says others drank the milk — the silver passed through.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The Crown owed nothing to any banker.
**Answer: False** — it mortgaged shipments in advance to German, Genoese, Flemish and Spanish bankers.
