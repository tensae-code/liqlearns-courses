# Potosí silver and Spain's mortgaged treasure — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the lesson teach about silver — 99% of Spanish America's exports?
**Answer:** Silver made up 99% of Spanish America's exports and stimulated Europe's economy.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the lesson teach about 'Spain had the cow, but others drank the milk'?
**Answer:** The silver stimulated Europe's economy but not Spain's — Spain had the cow, others drank the milk.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the lesson teach about mortgaged shipments and debt annuities?
**Answer:** The Crown granted whole silver shipments in advance to foreign bankers, and taxes paid debt annuities — only a minimal part of the silver entered Spain's economy.
