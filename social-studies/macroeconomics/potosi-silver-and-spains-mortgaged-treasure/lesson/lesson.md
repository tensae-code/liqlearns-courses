## Sub lesson 1 — Silver — 99% of Spanish America's exports

Silver made up 99% of Spanish America's exports, and Potosí's mountain stood behind most of it. The metal stimulated Europe's economy — trade, prices, industry felt its flood.

## Sub lesson 2 — 'Spain had the cow, but others drank the milk'

The section's own heading gives the lesson its sting: España tenía la vaca, pero otros tomaban la leche — Spain had the cow, but others drank the milk. The silver stimulated Europe's economy, but not Spain's.

## Sub lesson 3 — Mortgaged shipments and debt annuities

Why? The Crown mortgaged itself: it granted whole silver shipments in advance to German, Genoese, Flemish and Spanish bankers, and the taxes collected went to paying the annuities of its debt titles — so only a minimal part of the silver ever entered the Spanish economy.
