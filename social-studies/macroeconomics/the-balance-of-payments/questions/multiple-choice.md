# The balance of payments — a country's account with the world — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "The full record"?
a) the record, usually for a year, of all transactions between residents and the rest of the world  b) exports and imports, borrowing or lending, and reserve movements  c) debit and credit not matching in practice — deficit or surplus; a trade deficit feeds in
**Answer: a) the record, usually for a year, of all transactions between residents and the rest of the world**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "What it shows"?
a) the record, usually for a year, of all transactions between residents and the rest of the world  b) debit and credit not matching in practice — deficit or surplus; a trade deficit feeds in  c) exports and imports, borrowing or lending, and reserve movements
**Answer: c) exports and imports, borrowing or lending, and reserve movements**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Disequilibrium"?
a) exports and imports, borrowing or lending, and reserve movements  b) debit and credit not matching in practice — deficit or surplus; a trade deficit feeds in  c) the record, usually for a year, of all transactions between residents and the rest of the world
**Answer: b) debit and credit not matching in practice — deficit or surplus; a trade deficit feeds in**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Which statement best matches "The nation's ledger — credits and debits"?
a) The balance of payments is a double-entry record of all transactions with the rest of the world; credits bring payment in, debits send it out.  b) The current account sums merchandise trade, net services and net transfers.  c) Overall balance = current + capital; an uncovered deficit is paid from official reserves.
**Answer: a) The balance of payments is a double-entry record of all transactions with the rest of the world; credits bring payment in, debits send it out.**
