# The balance of payments — a country's account with the world — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The balance of payments records all economic transactions between a country's residents and the rest of the world, usually for a year.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The balance of payments records all economic transactions between a country's residents and the rest of the world, usually for a year.
**Answer: False** — that statement belongs to sub lesson 1 (The full record); sub lesson 2 says: The balance of payments shows exports and imports, borrowing from or lending to the world, and whether the central bank is adding to or drawing down reserves.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** When debit and credit do not match in practice, the imbalance is disequilibrium, appearing as deficit or surplus.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Imports are credits in the balance of payments.
**Answer: False — Imports are payments OUTWARD — debits. Credits are what brings payment in: exports, inflows, receipts.**

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** The current account includes purchases of foreign shares.
**Answer: False — Share purchases are capital-account movements. The current account covers goods, services and transfers.**

**6. (Sub lesson 6)** A current-account deficit can be ignored indefinitely.
**Answer: False — It must be covered by a capital surplus or from reserves — ignoring it drains the reserves that settle it.**
