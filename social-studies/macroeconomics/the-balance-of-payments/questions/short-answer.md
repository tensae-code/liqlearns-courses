# The balance of payments — a country's account with the world — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "The full record" in your own words.
**Answer:** The balance of payments records all economic transactions between a country's residents and the rest of the world, usually for a year.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "What it shows" in your own words.
**Answer:** The balance of payments shows exports and imports, borrowing from or lending to the world, and whether the central bank is adding to or drawing down reserves.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Disequilibrium" in your own words.
**Answer:** When debit and credit do not match in practice, the imbalance is disequilibrium, appearing as deficit or surplus.

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Explain "The nation's ledger — credits and debits" in your own words.
**Answer:** The balance of payments is a double-entry record of all transactions with the rest of the world; credits bring payment in, debits send it out.

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** Explain "The current account at Grade 11 depth" in your own words.
**Answer:** The current account sums merchandise trade, net services and net transfers.

**6. (Sub lesson 6)** Explain "How deficits get settled at Grade 11 depth" in your own words.
**Answer:** Overall balance = current + capital; an uncovered deficit is paid from official reserves.
