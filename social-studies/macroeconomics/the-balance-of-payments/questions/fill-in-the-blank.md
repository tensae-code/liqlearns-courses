# The balance of payments — a country's account with the world — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The balance of payments records transactions between a country's _____ and the rest of the world.
**Answer: residents**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** It shows whether the central bank is adding to or drawing down foreign-currency _____.
**Answer: reserves**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Debit and credit not matching in practice is called _____, appearing as deficit or surplus.
**Answer: disequilibrium**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Exports are ____ in the balance of payments.
**Answer: credits**

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** Net ____ — gifts, donations and remittances — are the current account's third balance.
**Answer: transfers**

**6. (Sub lesson 6)** An uncovered deficit is paid from official foreign-exchange ____.
**Answer: reserves**
