# Hands-on — The balance of payments — a country's account with the world

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Ledger game: two-column board, the world on the other side; transaction cards are posted as money in or out while a 'reserve jar' of tokens rises and falls to make the columns meet.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — a two-column ledger, the whole world on the other side, with a reserve jar rising and falling at the bottom — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
