## Sub lesson 1 — Dalence's 1851 statistics

The Grade 10 book grounds its account of 'acumulación originaria' — primitive, original accumulation — in Dalence's 1851 statistics on land distribution: numbers first, then the verdict.

## Sub lesson 2 — 106,132 community properties against 5,135 haciendas

The figures: 106,132 community properties shared among 478,084 people, against 5,135 haciendas enjoyed by only some 5,000 owners. Count the people per property on each side and the concentration stands bare.

## Sub lesson 3 — Accumulation through the hacienda

That concentration is the accumulation: wealth — land, and the labour and product land commands — gathered into few hands through the latifundio hacienda, the statistical base for calling it primitive accumulation.
