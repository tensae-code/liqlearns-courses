# Primitive accumulation through the latifundio hacienda — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The book argues from theory with no figures at all.
**Answer: False** — it grounds the account in Dalence's 1851 statistics.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The haciendas served more people than the community properties.
**Answer: False** — 478,084 people shared the community properties; some 5,000 owners held the haciendas.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Primitive accumulation here means wealth spread evenly by trade.
**Answer: False** — the book's case is concentration through the hacienda, proved by the statistics.
