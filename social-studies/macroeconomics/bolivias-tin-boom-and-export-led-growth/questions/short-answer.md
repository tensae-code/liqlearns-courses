# Bolivia's tin boom and export-led growth — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the lesson teach about tin exports quintuple, 1900–1929?
**Answer:** Tin exports quintupled 1900–1929 and Bolivia supplied about a quarter of world production.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the lesson teach about three barons — 70% of exports, 3% for the country?
**Answer:** The three tin barons exported 70% of all exports while the country received only 3% of the gains.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the lesson teach about profits abroad — Patiño's loan and the low taxes?
**Answer:** Patiño's £600,000 loan froze tax rises for five years, and the tin profits went abroad instead of being reinvested in Bolivia.
