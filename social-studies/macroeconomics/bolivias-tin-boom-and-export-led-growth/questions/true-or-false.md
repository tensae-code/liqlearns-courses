# Bolivia's tin boom and export-led growth — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Bolivia supplied almost all of the world's tin.
**Answer: False** — about a quarter of world production — the book's figure.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The tin gains were shared equally across Bolivia.
**Answer: False** — the barons exported 70% of exports; the country received 3% of the gains.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The tin barons reinvested all their profits inside Bolivia.
**Answer: False** — the book says the profits were not reinvested in Bolivia but went abroad.
