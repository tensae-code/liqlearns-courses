Bolivia's tin boom and export-led growth — as Bolivia's book teaches it, says the narrator: tin exports quintupled 1900–1929 and Bolivia supplied about a quarter of world production; the three tin barons exported 70% of all exports while the country received only 3% of the gains; Patiño's £600,000 loan froze tax rises for five years, and the tin profits went abroad instead of being reinvested in Bolivia.
