# Video script — Balance of payments disequilibrium and foreign reserves

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Balance of payments disequilibrium and foreign reserves — as Nigeria's book teaches it, says the narrator: a balance of payments is in disequilibrium when payments abroad persistently exceed receipts from abroad, or the reverse.; a country corrects disequilibrium by adjusting its spending, its exchange rate and its trade.; foreign reserves are a country's holdings of foreign currency and gold, kept to settle deficits and steady its currency; Nigeria's reserves rise and fall with oil..
