## Sub lesson 1 — Balance of payments disequilibrium

A balance of payments is in disequilibrium when payments abroad persistently exceed receipts from abroad, or the reverse.

## Sub lesson 2 — Correcting disequilibrium

A country corrects disequilibrium by adjusting its spending, its exchange rate and its trade.

## Sub lesson 3 — Foreign reserves

Foreign reserves are a country's holdings of foreign currency and gold, kept to settle deficits and steady its currency; Nigeria's reserves rise and fall with oil.
