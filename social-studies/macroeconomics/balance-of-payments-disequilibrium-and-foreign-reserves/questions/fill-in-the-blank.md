# Balance of payments disequilibrium and foreign reserves — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Disequilibrium is when payments abroad persistently _____ receipts.
**Answer: exceed**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A country corrects disequilibrium by adjusting spending, the exchange rate and _____.
**Answer: trade**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Foreign reserves are holdings of foreign currency and _____.
**Answer: gold**
