# Balance of payments disequilibrium and foreign reserves — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is balance of payments disequilibrium?
**Answer:** A balance of payments is in disequilibrium when payments abroad persistently exceed receipts from abroad, or the reverse.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How is disequilibrium corrected?
**Answer:** A country corrects disequilibrium by adjusting its spending, its exchange rate and its trade.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What are foreign reserves?
**Answer:** Foreign reserves are a country's holdings of foreign currency and gold, kept to settle deficits and steady its currency; Nigeria's reserves rise and fall with oil.
