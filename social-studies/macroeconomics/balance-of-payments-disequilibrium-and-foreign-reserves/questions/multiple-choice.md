# Balance of payments disequilibrium and foreign reserves — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** About balance of payments disequilibrium — the lesson teaches that —
a) a balance of payments is in disequilibrium when payments abroad persistently exceed receipts from abroad, or the reverse.  b) a country corrects disequilibrium by adjusting its spending, its exchange rate and its trade.  c) foreign reserves are a country's holdings of foreign currency and gold, kept to settle deficits and steady its currency; Nigeria's reserves rise and fall with oil.
**Answer: a) a balance of payments is in disequilibrium when payments abroad persistently exceed receipts from abroad, or the reverse.**
