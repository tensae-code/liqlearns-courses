# Balance of payments disequilibrium and foreign reserves — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Disequilibrium means payments and receipts always balance exactly.
**Answer: False** — A balance of payments is in disequilibrium when payments abroad persistently exceed receipts from abroad, or the reverse.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Disequilibrium can never be corrected by any policy.
**Answer: False** — A country corrects disequilibrium by adjusting its spending, its exchange rate and its trade.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Foreign reserves are a country's debts to foreigners.
**Answer: False** — Foreign reserves are a country's holdings of foreign currency and gold, kept to settle deficits and steady its currency; Nigeria's reserves rise and fall with oil.
