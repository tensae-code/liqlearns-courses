# Video script — Nationalisation of the mines, 1952

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Nationalisation of the mines, 1952 — as Bolivia's book teaches it, says the narrator: Paz Estenssoro signed the mine nationalisation decree at Catavi on 31 October 1952 under FSTMB and COB pressure; COMIBOL administered 163 mines in 13 companies with 29,000 workers producing 27,000 tonnes of tin; worker control gave union representatives on COMIBOL's directorates the right of veto — a duality of power between COB and MNR.
