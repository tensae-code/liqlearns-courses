# Nationalisation of the mines, 1952 — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the lesson teach about catavi, 31 October 1952 — the decree?
**Answer:** Paz Estenssoro signed the mine nationalisation decree at Catavi on 31 October 1952 under FSTMB and COB pressure.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the lesson teach about cOMIBOL — 163 mines, 29,000 workers?
**Answer:** COMIBOL administered 163 mines in 13 companies with 29,000 workers producing 27,000 tonnes of tin.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the lesson teach about worker control with right of veto?
**Answer:** Worker control gave union representatives on COMIBOL's directorates the right of veto — a duality of power between COB and MNR.
