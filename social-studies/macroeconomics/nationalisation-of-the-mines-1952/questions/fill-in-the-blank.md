# Nationalisation of the mines, 1952 — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The nationalisation decree was signed at _____ on 31 October 1952.
**Answer: Catavi**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** COMIBOL administered _____ mines in 13 companies.
**Answer: 163**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Worker control gave the miners' representatives the right of _____ in COMIBOL's directorates.
**Answer: veto**
