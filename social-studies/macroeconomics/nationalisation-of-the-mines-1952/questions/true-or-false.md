# Nationalisation of the mines, 1952 — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The mines were nationalised by the tin barons themselves.
**Answer: False** — the decree was the Revolution's answer to the barons' Super Estado Minero.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** COMIBOL was a small private mining firm.
**Answer: False** — it was the state corporation running 163 mines with 29,000 workers.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Worker control was a title with no powers attached.
**Answer: False** — it carried a real right of veto over the directorates' decisions.
