# Average propensity to consume — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "The average share of income consumed"?
a) APC is total consumption divided by total disposable income; 3,000 of 4,000 birr gives 0.75.  b) Keynes held that APC falls as income rises.  c) At very low incomes APC can exceed 1, because necessities force spending beyond income.
**Answer: a) APC is total consumption divided by total disposable income; 3,000 of 4,000 birr gives 0.75.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "APC falls as income rises"?
a) At very low incomes APC can exceed 1, because necessities force spending beyond income.  b) APC is total consumption divided by total disposable income; 3,000 of 4,000 birr gives 0.75.  c) Keynes held that APC falls as income rises.
**Answer: c) Keynes held that APC falls as income rises.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "When APC passes 1 — spending beyond income"?
a) Keynes held that APC falls as income rises.  b) At very low incomes APC can exceed 1, because necessities force spending beyond income.  c) APC is total consumption divided by total disposable income; 3,000 of 4,000 birr gives 0.75.
**Answer: b) At very low incomes APC can exceed 1, because necessities force spending beyond income.**
