# Average propensity to consume — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** An APC of 0.75 means the household saves 75% of its income.
**Answer: False — APC 0.75 means it consumes 75% — it saves the other 25%.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Keynes held that richer households consume a larger share of their income than poorer ones.
**Answer: False — It is the other way round: as income rises, basic needs take a smaller share, so APC falls.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** An APC above 1 means the household is saving heavily.
**Answer: False — APC above 1 means consumption is larger than income — the household is dissaving, not saving.**
