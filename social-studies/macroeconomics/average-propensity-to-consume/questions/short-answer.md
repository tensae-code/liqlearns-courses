# Average propensity to consume — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "The average share of income consumed" in your own words.
**Answer:** APC is total consumption divided by total disposable income; 3,000 of 4,000 birr gives 0.75.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "APC falls as income rises" in your own words.
**Answer:** Keynes held that APC falls as income rises.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "When APC passes 1 — spending beyond income" in your own words.
**Answer:** At very low incomes APC can exceed 1, because necessities force spending beyond income.
