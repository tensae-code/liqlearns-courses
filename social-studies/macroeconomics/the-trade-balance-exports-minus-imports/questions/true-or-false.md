# The trade balance — exports minus imports — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The trade balance is receipts for exported goods minus payments for imported goods.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The trade balance is receipts for exported goods minus payments for imported goods.
**Answer: False** — that statement belongs to sub lesson 1 (The visible balance); sub lesson 2 says: A trade surplus means export receipts exceed import payments; a deficit means the reverse.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Ethiopia is the book's example of a trade deficit, its export receipts falling short of import expenditure.
**Answer: True**
