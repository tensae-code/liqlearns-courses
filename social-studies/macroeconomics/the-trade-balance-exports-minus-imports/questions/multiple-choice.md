# The trade balance — exports minus imports — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "The visible balance"?
a) receipts for exported goods minus payments for imported goods  b) surplus — exports exceed imports; deficit — the reverse  c) Ethiopia's export receipts fall short of its import expenditure — a trade deficit
**Answer: a) receipts for exported goods minus payments for imported goods**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Surplus and deficit"?
a) receipts for exported goods minus payments for imported goods  b) Ethiopia's export receipts fall short of its import expenditure — a trade deficit  c) surplus — exports exceed imports; deficit — the reverse
**Answer: c) surplus — exports exceed imports; deficit — the reverse**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Ethiopia's standing example"?
a) surplus — exports exceed imports; deficit — the reverse  b) Ethiopia's export receipts fall short of its import expenditure — a trade deficit  c) receipts for exported goods minus payments for imported goods
**Answer: b) Ethiopia's export receipts fall short of its import expenditure — a trade deficit**
