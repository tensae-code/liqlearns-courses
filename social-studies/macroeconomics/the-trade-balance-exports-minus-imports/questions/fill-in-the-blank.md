# The trade balance — exports minus imports — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The trade balance is receipts for _____ goods minus payments for imported goods.
**Answer: exported**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A surplus means export receipts _____ import payments.
**Answer: exceed**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Ethiopia is given as an economy experiencing a trade _____.
**Answer: deficit**
