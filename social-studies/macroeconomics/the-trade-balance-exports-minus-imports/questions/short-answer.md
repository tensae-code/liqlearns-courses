# The trade balance — exports minus imports — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "The visible balance" in your own words.
**Answer:** The trade balance is receipts for exported goods minus payments for imported goods.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Surplus and deficit" in your own words.
**Answer:** A trade surplus means export receipts exceed import payments; a deficit means the reverse.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Ethiopia's standing example" in your own words.
**Answer:** Ethiopia is the book's example of a trade deficit, its export receipts falling short of import expenditure.
