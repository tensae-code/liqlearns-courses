# Hands-on — The trade balance — exports minus imports

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Border-post game: goods cards cross a table line both ways with price tags; the class treasurer nets the two money piles and announces surplus or deficit and its size.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — trucks of goods leaving and entering a border post, with two money totals compared underneath — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
