# Fishing industry — growth and jobs — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Fishing provides employment and income for the people who fish and trade in fish.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Fish is a primary source of protein, and its productivity reduces hunger and poverty.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Fishing has enabled the growth of fishery industries and of South Sudan's economy.
**Answer: True**
