# Fishing industry — growth and jobs — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In your own words, what does sub lesson 1 teach about employment and income from fishing?
**Answer:** Fishing provides employment and income for the people who fish and trade in fish.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In your own words, what does sub lesson 2 teach about fish, protein, hunger and poverty?
**Answer:** Fish is a primary source of protein, and its productivity reduces hunger and poverty.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In your own words, what does sub lesson 3 teach about fishing grows industries and the economy?
**Answer:** Fishing has enabled the growth of fishery industries and of South Sudan's economy.
