# Fishing industry — growth and jobs

## Do it first

Before you read on, write what you already think this lesson's title means — fishing industry — growth and jobs — in one sentence of your own. Keep it; you will check it against the lesson at the end.

## Sub lesson 1 — Employment and income from fishing

**Fishing provides employment and income** for the people who fish and trade in fish.

## Sub lesson 2 — Fish, protein, hunger and poverty

**Fish is a primary source of protein**, and its productivity **reduces hunger and poverty**.

## Sub lesson 3 — Fishing grows industries and the economy

Fishing has enabled the **growth of fishery industries** and of **South Sudan's economy**.

## Say it to your companion
