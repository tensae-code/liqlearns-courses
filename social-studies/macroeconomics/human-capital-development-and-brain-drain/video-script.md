# Video script — Human capital development and brain drain

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Human capital development and brain drain — as Nigeria's book teaches it, says the narrator: human capital is the skill, knowledge and health embodied in people, built by education and training.; human capital works alongside physical capital, and its efficiency shows in how much skilled people can produce.; brain drain is the loss of trained people who emigrate, taking their human capital to other countries..
