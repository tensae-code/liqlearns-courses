# Human capital development and brain drain — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Human capital is skill, knowledge and health built by education and _____.
**Answer: training**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Human capital works alongside _____ capital.
**Answer: physical**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Brain drain is the loss of trained people who _____.
