# Human capital development and brain drain — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** About human capital — the lesson teaches that —
a) human capital is the skill, knowledge and health embodied in people, built by education and training.  b) human capital works alongside physical capital, and its efficiency shows in how much skilled people can produce.  c) brain drain is the loss of trained people who emigrate, taking their human capital to other countries.
**Answer: a) human capital is the skill, knowledge and health embodied in people, built by education and training.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** About human versus physical capital and efficiency — the lesson teaches that —
a) human capital is the skill, knowledge and health embodied in people, built by education and training.  b) human capital works alongside physical capital, and its efficiency shows in how much skilled people can produce.  c) brain drain is the loss of trained people who emigrate, taking their human capital to other countries.
**Answer: b) human capital works alongside physical capital, and its efficiency shows in how much skilled people can produce.**
