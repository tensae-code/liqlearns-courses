# Human capital development and brain drain — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is human capital?
**Answer:** Human capital is the skill, knowledge and health embodied in people, built by education and training.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does human capital work alongside?
**Answer:** Human capital works alongside physical capital, and its efficiency shows in how much skilled people can produce.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What is brain drain?
**Answer:** Brain drain is the loss of trained people who emigrate, taking their human capital to other countries.
