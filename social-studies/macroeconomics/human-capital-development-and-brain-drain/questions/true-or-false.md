# Human capital development and brain drain — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Human capital is a kind of factory building.
**Answer: False** — Human capital is the skill, knowledge and health embodied in people, built by education and training.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Human capital and physical capital are exactly the same thing.
**Answer: False** — Human capital works alongside physical capital, and its efficiency shows in how much skilled people can produce.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Brain drain is the arrival of trained people from abroad.
**Answer: False** — Brain drain is the loss of trained people who emigrate, taking their human capital to other countries.
