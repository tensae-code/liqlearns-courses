## Sub lesson 1 — Human capital

Human capital is the skill, knowledge and health embodied in people, built by education and training.

## Sub lesson 2 — Human versus physical capital and efficiency

Human capital works alongside physical capital, and its efficiency shows in how much skilled people can produce.

## Sub lesson 3 — Brain drain

Brain drain is the loss of trained people who emigrate, taking their human capital to other countries.
