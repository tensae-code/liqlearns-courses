# Video script — Forced labour systems of the colonial world

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Forced labour systems of the colonial world — as Bolivia's book teaches it, says the narrator: the encomienda let an entrusted conqueror collect tribute and personal service from indigenous people at the rates of the Tasa; Toledo turned the Inca mita into an obligatory mining draft for Potosí, worked in turns by a seventh part of the men aged 18 to 50; the obrajes were pre-industrial textile workshops run on the cheapest possible indigenous labour — a semi-slave system.
