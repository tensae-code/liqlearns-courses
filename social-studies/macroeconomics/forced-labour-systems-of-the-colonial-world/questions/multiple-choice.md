# Forced labour systems of the colonial world — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** About the encomienda — tribute and personal service — the lesson teaches that —
a) the encomienda let an entrusted conqueror collect tribute and personal service from indigenous people at the rates of the Tasa  b) Toledo turned the Inca mita into an obligatory mining draft for Potosí, worked in turns by a seventh part of the men aged 18 to 50  c) the obrajes were pre-industrial textile workshops run on the cheapest possible indigenous labour — a semi-slave system
