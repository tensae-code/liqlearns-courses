# Forced labour systems of the colonial world — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the lesson teach about the encomienda — tribute and personal service?
**Answer:** The encomienda let an entrusted conqueror collect tribute and personal service from indigenous people at the rates of the Tasa.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the lesson teach about the mita distorted — Toledo's mining draft for Potosí?
**Answer:** Toledo turned the Inca mita into an obligatory mining draft for Potosí, worked in turns by a seventh part of the men aged 18 to 50.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the lesson teach about the obrajes — semi-slave textile workshops?
**Answer:** The obrajes were pre-industrial textile workshops run on the cheapest possible indigenous labour — a semi-slave system.
