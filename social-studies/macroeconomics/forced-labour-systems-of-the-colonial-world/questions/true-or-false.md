# Forced labour systems of the colonial world — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The New Laws of 1542 ended the encomienda's abuses in practice.
**Answer: False** — the book says they regulated it on paper without stopping the abuse.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Mitayos were free wage workers who could leave at will.
**Answer: False** — the mita was an obligatory draft — men fled or paid bribes to escape it.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The obrajes paid the highest wages in the colony.
**Answer: False** — they ran on the cheapest possible indigenous labour.
