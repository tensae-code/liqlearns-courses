# Forced labour systems of the colonial world — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The encomienda's tribute rates were fixed in the _____.
**Answer: Tasa**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Inside the mine, mitayos worked up to _____ hours for 4 reales a day.
**Answer: 36**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The obrajes were forbidden to compete with _____ textiles, so they made cheaper, lower-quality cloth.
**Answer: Spain's**
