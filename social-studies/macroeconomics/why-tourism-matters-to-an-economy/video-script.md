# Video script — Why tourism matters to an economy

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Why does a country care about tourists? Because a visitor does not spend in one place only. The tourist sleeps in a hotel, eats in a restaurant, buys food and gifts — thousands of visitors set many fields of work moving at once.

And tourism is not luck. It is built: on natural beauty — the sea coast, the mountains, the forests, the desert; on the sites of history, like Palmyra; on the roads and routes that bring visitors in; and on the institutions and hospitality that receive them. That is why Syria's Grade 4 book counts tourism among the pillars of the economy.
