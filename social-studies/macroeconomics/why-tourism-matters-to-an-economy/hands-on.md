# Hands-on — Why tourism matters to an economy

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Act out one tourist's day in a town: where does the visitor sleep, eat, and buy? At each stop, say out loud which field of work the visitor has just set moving — hotel, restaurant, food seller, gift shop.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the four foundations of tourism as four pillars holding one roof labelled TOURISM: natural beauty, historic sites, transport routes, tourist institutions and hospitality. Under the first two, write Syria's own examples from the lesson. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
