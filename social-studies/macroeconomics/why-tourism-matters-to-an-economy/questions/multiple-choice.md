# Why tourism matters to an economy — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** When thousands of tourists visit, they set moving —
a) only the hotels  b) many fields at once — hotels, restaurants, food and gift buying  c) nothing outside the airport
**Answer: b) many fields at once — hotels, restaurants, food and gift buying**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which of these is one of the book's foundations of tourism?
a) natural beauty — coast, mountains, forests, desert  b) heavy industry  c) the army
**Answer: a) natural beauty — coast, mountains, forests, desert**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The human foundation tourism stands on is —
a) tourist institutions and hospitality  b) the price of oil  c) the school timetable
**Answer: a) tourist institutions and hospitality**
