# Why tourism matters to an economy — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In Syria's Grade 4 book, tourism is one of the pillars of the economy.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Palmyra is named in the book as a natural landscape.
**Answer: False** — Palmyra is an archaeological and historical site; the natural foundations are the coast, mountains, forests and desert.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Tourism needs nothing but beautiful places — routes and hospitality do not matter.
**Answer: False** — the book names transport routes and tourist institutions and hospitality among the things tourism is built on.
