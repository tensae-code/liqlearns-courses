# Why tourism matters to an economy — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why does a tourist's visit matter to more than one business?
**Answer:** One visitor's spending spreads across many fields at once — hotels, restaurants, and food and gift buying — so thousands of tourists set many people's work moving.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the natural and historical foundations of Syria's tourism as the book gives them.
**Answer:** Natural beauty — the Mediterranean coast, mountains, forests and desert — and archaeological and historical sites such as Palmyra.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What two working foundations does the book add to nature and history?
**Answer:** Transport routes that bring visitors in, and tourist institutions and hospitality that receive them.
