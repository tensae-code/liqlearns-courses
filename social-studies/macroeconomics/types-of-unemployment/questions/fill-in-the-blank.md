# Types of unemployment — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or figure from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The short gap between jobs is ____ unemployment.
**Answer: frictional**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Unemployment that rises when demand falls is ____ unemployment.
**Answer: cyclical**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Unemployment from a mismatch of skills or place is ____ unemployment.
**Answer: structural**
