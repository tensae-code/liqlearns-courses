# Types of unemployment — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Frictional unemployment is the short gap between jobs.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Cyclical unemployment rises when demand falls, as when overseas coffee demand drops.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Structural unemployment is a mismatch of skills or place; disguised unemployment is put at 30–35% in underdeveloped economies.
**Answer: True**
