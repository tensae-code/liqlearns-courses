# Types of unemployment — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Frictional — the gap between jobs"?
a) Frictional unemployment is the short gap between jobs  b) Cyclical unemployment rises when demand falls, as when overseas coffee demand drops  c) Structural unemployment is a mismatch of skills or place; disguised unemployment is put at 30–35% in underdeveloped economies
**Answer: a) Frictional unemployment is the short gap between jobs**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Cyclical — when demand falls"?
a) Cyclical unemployment rises when demand falls, as when overseas coffee demand drops  b) Structural unemployment is a mismatch of skills or place; disguised unemployment is put at 30–35% in underdeveloped economies  c) Frictional unemployment is the short gap between jobs
**Answer: a) Cyclical unemployment rises when demand falls, as when overseas coffee demand drops**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Structural — and the hidden kinds"?
a) Structural unemployment is a mismatch of skills or place; disguised unemployment is put at 30–35% in underdeveloped economies  b) Frictional unemployment is the short gap between jobs  c) Cyclical unemployment rises when demand falls, as when overseas coffee demand drops
**Answer: a) Structural unemployment is a mismatch of skills or place; disguised unemployment is put at 30–35% in underdeveloped economies**
