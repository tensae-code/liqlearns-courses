# Types of unemployment — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Frictional — the gap between jobs" in your own words.
**Answer:** Frictional unemployment is the short gap between jobs.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Cyclical — when demand falls" in your own words.
**Answer:** Cyclical unemployment rises when demand falls, as when overseas coffee demand drops.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Structural — and the hidden kinds" in your own words.
**Answer:** Structural unemployment is a mismatch of skills or place; disguised unemployment is put at 30–35% in underdeveloped economies.
