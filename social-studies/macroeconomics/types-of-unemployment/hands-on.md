# Hands-on — Types of unemployment

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Sort the cases
Write six short job-loss stories, two of each main type, shuffle them, and sort a partner's stories by type with a one-line reason for each.

## Match the fix
For each type, name the fix that fits — time, recovery of demand, or retraining — and one fix that would not work, saying why.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
