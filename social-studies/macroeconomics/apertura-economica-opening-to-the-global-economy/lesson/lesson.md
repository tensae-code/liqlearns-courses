# Apertura económica opening to the global economy

## Do it first

## Sub lesson 1 — In small towns trade happens on one market

In small towns trade happens on one market day a week, in big cities permanently in shopping centres;

## Sub lesson 2 — Nations regulate production and commercialisation with taxes controls

Nations regulate production and commercialisation with taxes, controls and institutions;

## Sub lesson 3 — When a country reduces controls on goods entering

When a country reduces controls on goods entering and leaving and specialises in one product to join the global economy, that is apertura económica — it seeks competitiveness and wider markets, but small and medium merchants say they cannot compete globally while big business gains
