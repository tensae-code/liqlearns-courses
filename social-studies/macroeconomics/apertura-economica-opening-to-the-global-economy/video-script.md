# Video script — Apertura económica opening to the global economy

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Apertura económica opening to the global economy — as Colombia's book teaches it, says the narrator: In small towns trade happens on one market day a week, in big cities permanently in shopping centres; Nations regulate production and commercialisation with taxes, controls and institutions; When a country reduces controls on goods entering and leaving and specialises in one product to join the global economy, that is apertura económica — it seeks competitiveness and wider markets, but small and medium merchants say they cannot compete globally while big business gains
