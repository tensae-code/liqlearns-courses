# Apertura económica opening to the global economy — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In small towns trade happens on one market day a week, in big cities _____ in shopping centres;
**Answer: permanently**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Nations regulate production and _____ with taxes, controls and institutions;
**Answer: commercialisation**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** When a country reduces controls on goods entering and leaving and specialises in one product to join the global economy, that is apertura económica — it seeks _____ and wider markets, but small and medium merchants say they cannot compete globally while big business gains
**Answer: competitiveness**
