# Apertura económica opening to the global economy — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the lesson teach about in small towns trade happens on one market?
**Answer:** In small towns trade happens on one market day a week, in big cities permanently in shopping centres;

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the lesson teach about nations regulate production and commercialisation with taxes controls?
**Answer:** Nations regulate production and commercialisation with taxes, controls and institutions;

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the lesson teach about when a country reduces controls on goods entering?
**Answer:** When a country reduces controls on goods entering and leaving and specialises in one product to join the global economy, that is apertura económica — it seeks competitiveness and wider markets, but small and medium merchants say they cannot compete globally while big business gains
