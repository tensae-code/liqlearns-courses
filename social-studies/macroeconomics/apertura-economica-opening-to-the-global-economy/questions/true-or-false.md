# Apertura económica opening to the global economy — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In small towns trade happens on one market day a week, in big cities permanently in shopping centres;
**Answer: True** — this is what Colombia's book teaches in this part.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Sub lesson 2 is mainly about in small towns trade happens on one market.
**Answer: False** — sub lesson 2 is about nations regulate production and commercialisation with taxes controls; in small towns trade happens on one market is sub lesson 1.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** When a country reduces controls on goods entering and leaving and specialises in one product to join the global economy, that is apertura económica — it seeks competitiveness and wider markets, but small and medium merchants say they cannot compete globally while big business gains
**Answer: True** — this is what Colombia's book teaches in this part.
