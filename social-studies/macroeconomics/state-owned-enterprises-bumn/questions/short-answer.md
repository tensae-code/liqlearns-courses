# State-owned enterprises — BUMN — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the book teach about the two forms — persero and perum?
**Answer:** Indonesia's state-owned enterprises (BUMN) take two forms: the Persero, a limited company owned by the state, and the Perum, a public corporation serving the public.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the book teach about their role in the economy?
**Answer:** BUMN play a leading role in the Indonesian economy, running vital branches of production and services for the public.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the book teach about strengths and weaknesses?
**Answer:** The book weighs BUMN's strengths — state capital and a public-service mandate — against weaknesses such as inefficiency and slow decision-making.
