# State-owned enterprises — BUMN — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Indonesia's state-owned enterprises (BUMN) take two forms: the Persero, a limited company owned by the state, and the Perum, a public corporation serving the public.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** BUMN play a leading role in the Indonesian economy, running vital branches of production and services for the public.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The book weighs BUMN's strengths — state capital and a public-service mandate — against weaknesses such as something else entirely and slow decision-making.
**Answer: False** — The book weighs BUMN's strengths — state capital and a public-service mandate — against weaknesses such as inefficiency and slow decision-making.
