# State-owned enterprises — BUMN — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Indonesia's state-owned enterprises (BUMN) take two forms: the _____, a limited company owned by the state, and the Perum, a public corporation serving the public.
**Answer: Persero**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** BUMN play a leading role in the Indonesian economy, running _____ of production and services for the public.
**Answer: vital branches**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The book weighs BUMN's strengths — state capital and a public-service mandate — against weaknesses such as _____ and slow decision-making.
**Answer: inefficiency**
