# Video script — State-owned enterprises — BUMN

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Indonesia's state-owned enterprises (BUMN) take two forms: the Persero, a limited company owned by the state, and the Perum, a public corporation serving the public.

BUMN play a leading role in the Indonesian economy, running vital branches of production and services for the public.

The book weighs BUMN's strengths — state capital and a public-service mandate — against weaknesses such as inefficiency and slow decision-making.
