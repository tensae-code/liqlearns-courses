# Employment discrimination and earnings gaps — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Earnings gaps are measured —
a) by race and gender  b) by height  c) by hometown only
**Answer: a) by race and gender**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A discriminating firm in a competitive market —
a) raises its own costs by refusing able workers  b) always profits most  c) faces no pressure
**Answer: a) raises its own costs by refusing able workers**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** US policies here aim to —
a) reduce employment discrimination  b) widen earnings gaps  c) ban competition
**Answer: a) reduce employment discrimination**
