# Employment discrimination and earnings gaps — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** No earnings gaps exist in the data.
**Answer: False — the data show systematic gaps by race and gender.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Competition makes discrimination costless to the discriminator.
**Answer: False — refusing able workers raises the firm's own costs.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** No US public policy addresses employment discrimination.
**Answer: False — US public policies are designed to reduce it.**
