# Employment discrimination and earnings gaps — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What do the earnings data show?
**Answer:** Systematic pay gaps by race and gender.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does discrimination do inside a competitive market?
**Answer:** It raises the discriminator's own costs — yet gaps persist, which needs explaining.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What do US policies in this lesson aim at?
**Answer:** Reducing employment discrimination and its earnings gaps.
