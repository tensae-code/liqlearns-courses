## Sub lesson 1 — Earnings gaps by race and gender

The data show **earnings gaps by race and gender** — groups with similar work histories earning **systematically different pay**.

## Sub lesson 2 — Discrimination inside a competitive market

**Discrimination** inside a **competitive market** is costly to the discriminator — a firm that refuses able workers **raises its own costs**, yet gaps persist, which the book examines.

## Sub lesson 3 — US policies against it

**US public policies designed to reduce discrimination** — equal-opportunity law and its enforcement — are the book's closing part.
