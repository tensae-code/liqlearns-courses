# Hands-on — Schools of macroeconomic thought

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Six signposts
Draw one road with six signposts, one per school, each carrying its one-line claim. Check every claim against the lesson.

## Stage the argument
Two voices: a classical adviser and a Keynesian adviser face the same crash. Write three lines for each, then a monetarist interrupting with the money-growth rule.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
