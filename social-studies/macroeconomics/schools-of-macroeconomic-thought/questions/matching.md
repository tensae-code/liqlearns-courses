# Schools of macroeconomic thought — matching

Match each part to its right statement.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Classical and neoclassical — trust self-correction — c
**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Keynesian and monetarist — manage demand, or rule the money — a
**3. (Sub lesson 3)** New classical and new Keynesian — the modern argument — b

Statements:
a) Keynesians manage demand; monetarists prescribe a constant money-growth rule
b) New classicals claim anticipated policy cannot move real output; new Keynesians answer with nominal rigidities
