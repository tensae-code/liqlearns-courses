# Schools of macroeconomic thought — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Classical and neoclassical — trust self-correction" in your own words.
**Answer:** The classical school trusts self-correction, and its supply-side branch draws the Laffer curve.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Keynesian and monetarist — manage demand, or rule the money" in your own words.
**Answer:** Keynesians manage demand; monetarists prescribe a constant money-growth rule.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "New classical and new Keynesian — the modern argument" in your own words.
**Answer:** New classicals claim anticipated policy cannot move real output; new Keynesians answer with nominal rigidities.
