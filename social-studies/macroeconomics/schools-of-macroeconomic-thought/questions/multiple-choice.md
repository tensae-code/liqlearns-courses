# Schools of macroeconomic thought — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Classical and neoclassical — trust self-correction"?
a) The classical school trusts self-correction, and its supply-side branch draws the Laffer curve  b) Keynesians manage demand; monetarists prescribe a constant money-growth rule  c) New classicals claim anticipated policy cannot move real output; new Keynesians answer with nominal rigidities
**Answer: a) The classical school trusts self-correction, and its supply-side branch draws the Laffer curve**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Keynesian and monetarist — manage demand, or rule the money"?
a) Keynesians manage demand; monetarists prescribe a constant money-growth rule  b) New classicals claim anticipated policy cannot move real output; new Keynesians answer with nominal rigidities  c) The classical school trusts self-correction, and its supply-side branch draws the Laffer curve
**Answer: a) Keynesians manage demand; monetarists prescribe a constant money-growth rule**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "New classical and new Keynesian — the modern argument"?
a) New classicals claim anticipated policy cannot move real output; new Keynesians answer with nominal rigidities  b) The classical school trusts self-correction, and its supply-side branch draws the Laffer curve  c) Keynesians manage demand; monetarists prescribe a constant money-growth rule
**Answer: a) New classicals claim anticipated policy cannot move real output; new Keynesians answer with nominal rigidities**
