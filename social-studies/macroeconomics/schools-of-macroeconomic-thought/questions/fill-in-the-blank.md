# Schools of macroeconomic thought — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or figure from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The school that trusts markets to self-correct is the ____ school.
**Answer: classical**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The school prescribing a constant money-growth rule is the ____ school.
**Answer: monetarist**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Sticky prices and wages are called nominal ____.
**Answer: rigidities**
