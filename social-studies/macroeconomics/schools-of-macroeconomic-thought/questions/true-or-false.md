# Schools of macroeconomic thought — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The classical school trusts self-correction, and its supply-side branch draws the Laffer curve.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Keynesians manage demand; monetarists prescribe a constant money-growth rule.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** New classicals claim anticipated policy cannot move real output; new Keynesians answer with nominal rigidities.
**Answer: True**
