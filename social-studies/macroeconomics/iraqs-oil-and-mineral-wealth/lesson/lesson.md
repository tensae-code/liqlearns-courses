# Iraq's oil and mineral wealth

## Do it first

Before you read on, write what you already think this lesson's title means — iraq's oil and mineral wealth — in one sentence of your own. Keep it; you will check it against the lesson at the end.

## Sub lesson 1 — Mineral wealth — a major part of Iraq's economy

Iraq's Grade 9 book gives **mineral wealth** a major role in the Iraqi economy, and puts **oil and natural gas first** among the country's minerals.

## Sub lesson 2 — Oil and gas — fields in three regions, oil moved by ship, gas the cleanest fuel

Iraq's **oil fields** lie in **three regions — north, centre and south (Basra)** — and its oil is **moved by ship**. **Natural gas** is named the **cleanest fuel**.

## Sub lesson 3 — Sulfur and phosphates — Akashat in Anbar

Beside oil and gas, Iraq's mineral wealth includes **sulfur** and **phosphates**, the phosphates lying at **Akashat in Anbar**.
