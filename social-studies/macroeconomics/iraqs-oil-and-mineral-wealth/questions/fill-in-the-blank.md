# Iraq's oil and mineral wealth — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Iraq's oil fields lie in three regions: north, centre and _____.
**Answer: south (Basra)**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** _____ is named the cleanest fuel.
**Answer: Natural gas**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The phosphates lie at _____ in Anbar.
**Answer: Akashat**
