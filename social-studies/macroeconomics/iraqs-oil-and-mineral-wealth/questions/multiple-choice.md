# Iraq's oil and mineral wealth — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Iraq's book puts which minerals first in the country's mineral wealth?
a) oil and natural gas  b) sulfur only  c) phosphates only
**Answer: a) oil and natural gas**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Iraq's oil fields lie in how many regions?
a) three — north, centre and south  b) one — Basra only  c) two — north and south
**Answer: a) three — north, centre and south**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The phosphates of Iraq lie at —
a) Akashat in Anbar  b) Basra in the south  c) Kirkuk in the north
**Answer: a) Akashat in Anbar**
