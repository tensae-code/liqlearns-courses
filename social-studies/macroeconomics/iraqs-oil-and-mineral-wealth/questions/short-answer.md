# Iraq's oil and mineral wealth — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What role does mineral wealth play in Iraq's economy?
**Answer:** It has a major role, with oil and natural gas first.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How is Iraq's oil moved, and where do its fields lie?
**Answer:** The oil is moved by ship; the fields lie in three regions — north, centre and south (Basra).

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name two of Iraq's minerals beside oil and gas.
**Answer:** Sulfur and phosphates (the phosphates at Akashat in Anbar).
