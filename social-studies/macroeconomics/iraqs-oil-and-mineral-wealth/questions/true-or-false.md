# Iraq's oil and mineral wealth — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Mineral wealth has a major role in the Iraqi economy.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Natural gas is named the dirtiest fuel.
**Answer: False — gas is named the cleanest fuel.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Iraq's oil is moved by ship.
**Answer: True**
