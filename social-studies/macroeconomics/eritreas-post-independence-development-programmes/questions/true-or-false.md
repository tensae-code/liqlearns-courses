# Eritrea's post-independence development programmes — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Under the 1995 land reform, only men may hold use rights to land.
**Answer: False** — use rights are equal for men and women; all land is state property, and investment in the land one uses is permitted.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** People's participation — the people themselves taking part in the building — is one of the National Charter goals.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The Warsai-Yikaalo campaign was a food-self-sufficiency drive with no building work in it.
**Answer: False** — it was the 2002 post-war recovery campaign; infrastructure building and food self-sufficiency are the programme's drives, and the campaign was financed from internal resources and loans.
