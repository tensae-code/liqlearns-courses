# Eritrea's post-independence development programmes — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the three National Charter goals the book gives for Eritrea's development.
**Answer:** Self-reliance; fair distribution of wealth; people's participation.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** State the three rules of the 1995 land reform.
**Answer:** All land is state property; use rights are equal for men and women; investment in the land one uses is permitted.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What was the Warsai-Yikaalo campaign, and how was it financed?
**Answer:** It was the 2002 post-war recovery campaign. It was financed from internal resources and loans.
