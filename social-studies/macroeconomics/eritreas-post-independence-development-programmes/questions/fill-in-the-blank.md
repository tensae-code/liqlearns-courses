# Eritrea's post-independence development programmes — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The three National Charter goals are _____, fair distribution of wealth, and people's _____.
**Answer: self-reliance; participation**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The _____ land reform made all land _____ property, with equal use rights for men and women.
**Answer: 1995; state**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The _____ recovery campaign of 2002 was financed from _____ resources and loans.
**Answer: Warsai-Yikaalo; internal**
