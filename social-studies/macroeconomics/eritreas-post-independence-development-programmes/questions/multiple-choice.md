# Eritrea's post-independence development programmes — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The National Charter goals in Eritrea's Grade 8 book are —
a) self-reliance, fair distribution of wealth, and people's participation  b) exports, imports and tourism  c) taxation, banking and trade
**Answer: a) self-reliance, fair distribution of wealth, and people's participation**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Under the 1995 land reform, land in Eritrea is —
a) owned privately by the farmers who work it  b) state property, with equal use rights for men and women and investment permitted  c) owned by foreign companies
**Answer: b) state property, with equal use rights for men and women and investment permitted**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The 2002 Warsai-Yikaalo recovery campaign was financed —
a) only by foreign grants  b) from internal resources and loans  c) by selling the country's land
**Answer: b) from internal resources and loans**
