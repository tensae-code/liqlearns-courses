# Hands-on — Eritrea's post-independence development programmes

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Tell Eritrea's programme out loud in its own order: the Charter's three goals first, then the 1995 land reform and its three rules, then the drives, then the Warsai-Yikaalo campaign and where its money came from.

## Draw it and check it
Draw three boxes — GOALS, LAND REFORM, CAMPAIGN — and fill each from memory. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare, and add anything you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
