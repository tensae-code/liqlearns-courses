# Lesson plan — Eritrea's post-independence development programmes

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Macroeconomics · **Lesson:** Eritrea's post-independence development programmes
**Place in the curriculum:** Macroeconomics (CURRICULUM.md) — built in the Economics expansion (Eritrea, 2026-11-16).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Eritrea's own development model as its Grade 8 book teaches it: the National Charter goals (self-reliance, fair distribution of wealth, people's participation), the 1995 land reform (all land state property, equal use rights for men and women, investment permitted), the infrastructure and food-self-sufficiency drives, and the 2002 Warsai-Yikaalo recovery campaign financed from internal resources and loans.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on what the learner already knows about development and about farming's place in Eritrea's economy, and narrows to one country's own programme: this is a country's national-economy story, told by its own schoolbook.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of ..." in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.
