# Video script — Eritrea's post-independence development programmes

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Most development lessons teach a model. Eritrea's Grade 8 book teaches Eritrea — its own development programmes, as national homework.

First, the goals, from the National Charter: self-reliance, a fair distribution of wealth, and the people's participation. Then the 1995 land reform: all land is state property, use rights are equal for men and women, and investment is permitted. Then the drives — infrastructure, and food self-sufficiency. And in 2002, the Warsai-Yikaalo recovery campaign, financed from internal resources and loans.

Goals, land, building, and a campaign paid for at home — that is Eritrea's programme, in its own book's words.
