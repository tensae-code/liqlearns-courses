# Industrialisation in the Dominican Republic — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What were the first factories of the import-substitution process, and when did they open?
**Answer:** Shoe, beer and food factories, opening from 1937, as a timid import-substitution process began under Trujillo with the Second World War economic boom.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What two changes did the monopoly factories bring to the country?
**Answer:** They created an important urban working mass and strengthened public finances — even though the firms were monopolies owned by Trujillo himself.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** How did wartime agriculture change, in the book's figures?
**Answer:** The country turned from a rice importer into an exporter of 630,000 quintales, as irrigated land rose 91.8% between 1936 and 1944.
