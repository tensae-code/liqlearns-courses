# Industrialisation in the Dominican Republic — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Under Trujillo, a timid _____ process began with the Second World War economic boom.
**Answer: import-substitution**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The firms were _____ owned by Trujillo himself.
**Answer: monopolies**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Wartime agriculture turned the country into a rice exporter of _____ quintales.
**Answer: 630,000**
