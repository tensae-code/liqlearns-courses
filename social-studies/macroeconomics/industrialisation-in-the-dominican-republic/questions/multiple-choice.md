# Industrialisation in the Dominican Republic — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** When did the Dominican Republic's first import-substitution factories open, and what were they?
a) from 1937 — shoe, beer and food factories  b) in 1961 — car factories  c) in 1844 — sugar mills only
**Answer: a) from 1937 — shoe, beer and food factories**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Who owned the new industrial firms?
a) Trujillo himself — they were his monopolies  b) foreign companies  c) workers' cooperatives
**Answer: a) Trujillo himself — they were his monopolies**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In the war years, the country switched from rice importer to —
a) rice exporter of 630,000 quintales  b) rice importer of twice as much  c) wheat exporter
**Answer: a) rice exporter of 630,000 quintales**
