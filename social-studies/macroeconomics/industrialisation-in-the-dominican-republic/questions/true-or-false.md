# Industrialisation in the Dominican Republic — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The book calls the import-substitution process under Trujillo a large and complete one.
**Answer: False** — the book calls it a timid import-substitution process, begun with the Second World War economic boom

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The cement factory and the industrial slaughterhouse were among the factories that followed the first shoe, beer and food factories.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Irrigated land fell between 1936 and 1944.
**Answer: False** — irrigated land rose 91.8% between 1936 and 1944
