# Hands-on — Industrialisation in the Dominican Republic

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Set out three small factories on a table — a shoe, a bottle, a loaf. Say for each one: what it replaces that used to be imported, then gather all three under one owner's name and say what changes when one man owns every factory in town.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the timeline 1936 → 1937 → 1944: the first factories in 1937, the later sack, cement and slaughterhouse factories, and irrigated land rising 91.8% by 1944 with rice exports of 630,000 quintales. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare, and fix every date and figure you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
