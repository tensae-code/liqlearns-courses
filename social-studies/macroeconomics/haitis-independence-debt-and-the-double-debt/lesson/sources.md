Haiti's own way: public debt is taught entirely through Haiti's own case — pupils run a year-long enquiry from President Aristide's 2003 restitution speech and current press investigations, building the chronology of the 1825 indemnity and the loans that followed, and end by arguing the restitution/reparation debate themselves. This lesson keeps that way: the chronology first — ordinance, treaties, loans — in the course's own words; the book's text itself is never transcribed.
