# Hands-on — Haiti's independence debt and the double debt

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Build the chronology
Write the lesson's three stops on pieces of paper — 1825 ordinance, 1838 treaties, loans from French banks — lay them in order, and say out loud what each one did to the debt, the way the Haitian pupils build the chronology.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the double debt from memory — the indemnity on one side, the loans taken to pay it on the other, both owed at once — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
