# Lesson plan — Haiti's independence debt and the double debt

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Macroeconomics · **Lesson:** Haiti's independence debt and the double debt
**Place in the curriculum:** Macroeconomics (CURRICULUM.md) — built in the Economics expansion (Haiti, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The 1825 ordinance imposing an indemnity on Haiti in exchange for recognition, the 1838 treaties, and how Haiti then had to borrow from French banks to pay the indemnity — the "double debt" of debt plus loans, with successive national and international borrowing.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the book's own chronology — ordinance, treaties, loans — built as an enquiry, the way the Haitian pupils build it.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of ..." in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.
