# Haiti's independence debt and the double debt — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The indemnity was the price set on recognition of Haiti's independence.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** After the 1838 treaties, Haiti owed nothing more.
**Answer: False** — the treaties renegotiated the debt; Haiti went on owing and paying.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Haiti borrowed from French banks to pay the indemnity.
**Answer: True**
