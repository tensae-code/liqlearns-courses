# Haiti's independence debt and the double debt — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The 1825 ordinance imposed on Haiti —
a) a trade embargo  b) an indemnity in exchange for recognition of its independence  c) a new tax on coffee
**Answer: b) an indemnity in exchange for recognition of its independence**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The 1838 treaties —
a) cancelled the debt  b) renegotiated the debt — its terms changed, the debt did not end  c) created the indemnity
**Answer: b) renegotiated the debt — its terms changed, the debt did not end**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The "double debt" is —
a) two indemnities  b) the indemnity plus the loans Haiti took from French banks to pay it  c) a debt owed by two countries
**Answer: b) the indemnity plus the loans Haiti took from French banks to pay it**
