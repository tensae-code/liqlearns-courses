# Haiti's independence debt and the double debt — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The 1825 ordinance imposed an _____ on Haiti in exchange for recognition.
**Answer: indemnity**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The debt was renegotiated by the _____ treaties.
**Answer: 1838**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Debt plus the loans taken to pay it is called the _____ debt.
**Answer: double**
