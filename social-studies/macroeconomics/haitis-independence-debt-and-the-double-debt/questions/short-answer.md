# Haiti's independence debt and the double debt — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What did the 1825 ordinance do?
**Answer:** It imposed an indemnity on Haiti — a sum to pay in exchange for recognition of Haiti's independence; the debt was negotiated and imposed, not borrowed for a project.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What did the 1838 treaties change, and what did they not change?
**Answer:** They renegotiated the debt's terms; they did not end the debt — Haiti went on owing and paying.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Why is the debt called "double"?
**Answer:** Because Haiti had to borrow from French banks to pay the indemnity, so it owed the indemnity and the loans together — debt plus loans, with successive national and international borrowing after.
