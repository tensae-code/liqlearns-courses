# Video script — Haiti's independence debt and the double debt

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

In 1825, an ordinance imposed an indemnity on Haiti — a sum to pay France in exchange for recognition of Haiti's independence. The debt was the price of recognition itself.

In 1838, treaties renegotiated the debt. The terms changed; the debt did not end.

And to pay the indemnity, Haiti had to borrow from French banks. Debt plus the loans taken to pay it — that is the double debt, followed by successive national and international borrowing.
