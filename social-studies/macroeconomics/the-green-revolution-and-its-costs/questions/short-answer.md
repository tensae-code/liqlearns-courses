# The Green Revolution and its costs — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the modern inputs the Green Revolution combined.
**Answer:** HYV seeds, irrigation, chemical fertiliser and pesticides.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How much did wheat yield rise by?
**Answer:** From 1300 to 3200 kg per hectare.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What are the two environmental costs named?
**Answer:** Lost soil fertility and a lowered water table.
