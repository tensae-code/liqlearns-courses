# The Green Revolution and its costs — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Green Revolution's modern farming methods use —
a) HYV seeds with irrigation, chemical fertiliser and pesticides  b) only hand tools  c) no water at all
**Answer: a) HYV seeds with irrigation, chemical fertiliser and pesticides**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Wheat yield rose from 1300 to —
a) 3200 kg per hectare  b) 2000 kg per hectare  c) 1300 kg per hectare
**Answer: a) 3200 kg per hectare**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Overuse in the Green Revolution has cost —
a) soil fertility and the water table  b) nothing at all  c) only the price of seed
**Answer: a) soil fertility and the water table**
