# The Green Revolution and its costs — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Green Revolution used no fertiliser.
**Answer: False — it used chemical fertiliser and pesticides.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Wheat yield fell under the Green Revolution.
**Answer: False — it rose from 1300 to 3200 kg per hectare.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Overuse of fertiliser improved the water table.
**Answer: False — overuse lowered the water table.**
