# The Nigerian economy — structure, sectors and potentials

## Do it first

## Sub lesson 1 — The structure of Nigeria's economy

Nigeria's economy is built of its sectors — agriculture, industry, services and petroleum — each contributing its share.

## Sub lesson 2 — The contributions of the sectors

Each sector contributes output, jobs and exports, and the balance between the sectors shows how the economy stands.

## Sub lesson 3 — Nigeria's potentials

Nigeria's land, people and resources give the economy great potential that fuller development can unlock.
