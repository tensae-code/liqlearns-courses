# The Nigerian economy — structure, sectors and potentials — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the structure of Nigeria's economy?
**Answer:** Nigeria's economy is built of its sectors — agriculture, industry, services and petroleum — each contributing its share.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does each sector contribute?
**Answer:** Each sector contributes output, jobs and exports, and the balance between the sectors shows how the economy stands.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What gives Nigeria's economy its potential?
**Answer:** Nigeria's land, people and resources give the economy great potential that fuller development can unlock.
