# The Nigerian economy — structure, sectors and potentials — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Nigeria's economy has only one sector.
**Answer: False** — Nigeria's economy is built of its sectors — agriculture, industry, services and petroleum — each contributing its share.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The sectors contribute nothing to output or jobs.
**Answer: False** — Each sector contributes output, jobs and exports, and the balance between the sectors shows how the economy stands.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Nigeria's economy has no potential to develop further.
**Answer: False** — Nigeria's land, people and resources give the economy great potential that fuller development can unlock.
