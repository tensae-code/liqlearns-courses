# Steam and rail the transport revolution — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the lesson teach about canals linked england's cities but the true revolution?
**Answer:** Canals linked England's cities, but the true revolution came with the steamship and the railway;

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the lesson teach about alongside it the text argues the bourgeois earning?
**Answer:** Alongside it, the text argues the bourgeois, earning ever more, contrives to pay workers ever lower wages

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the lesson teach about leaving them in complete misery some economists said?
**Answer:** Leaving them in complete misery — some economists said workers' conditions were worse than those of slaves, serfs and artisans of earlier periods
