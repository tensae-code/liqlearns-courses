# Remittances and migration — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Remittances support the families migrants leave at home.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Remittances bring Fiji less foreign exchange than sugar.
**Answer: False — the book teaches that remittances bring Fiji more foreign exchange than sugar.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Brain drain means the home country loses workers it trained.
**Answer: True**
