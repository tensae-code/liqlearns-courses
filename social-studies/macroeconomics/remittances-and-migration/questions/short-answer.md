# Remittances and migration — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why do Pacific people migrate overseas?
**Answer:** They migrate for work — the job and its pay are overseas.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What do remittances do for families and for Fiji?
**Answer:** They support families at home, and they bring Fiji more foreign exchange than sugar.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name the two costs of migration the lesson weighs.
**Answer:** Brain drain — losing trained workers — and poor economic growth at home.
