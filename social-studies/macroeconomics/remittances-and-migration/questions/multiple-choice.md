# Remittances and migration — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Pacific people in the lesson migrate overseas mainly —
a) for work  b) for holidays  c) to study the weather
**Answer: a) for work**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Remittances are —
a) taxes on exports  b) money migrants send home to their families  c) loans from a bank
**Answer: b) money migrants send home to their families**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** One cost of migration named in the lesson is —
a) brain drain  b) higher sugar prices  c) too much rain
**Answer: a) brain drain**
