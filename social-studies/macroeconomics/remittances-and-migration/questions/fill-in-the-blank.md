# Remittances and migration — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Money sent home by migrants is called _____.
**Answer: remittances**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Pacific people migrate overseas for _____.
**Answer: work**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The loss of trained workers who leave is called _____ drain.
**Answer: brain**
