# Video script — Remittances and migration

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

In Fiji's Year 7 Social Science book, Strand 4.2 — People and Work — turns to migration overseas: Pacific people leave their home islands to work in other countries.

Remittances are the money migrants send back to their families at home. In the book's teaching, remittances support families at home — the money earned overseas feeds, houses and schools the family that stayed.

The book weighs migration honestly, advantages against disadvantages. On the cost side stand brain drain — the home country loses the very workers it trained, as they leave to work overseas — and poor economic growth at home.
