# Hands-on — Remittances and migration

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Follow one remittance
Draw the lesson's journey: a worker overseas, the money sent home, the family it supports, and the foreign exchange it brings the country. Label each step, then add the two costs — brain drain and poor growth — where they belong on the drawing.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's picture from memory — a Pacific worker overseas sending money home to a family in Fiji, with an arrow of money flowing back across the ocean to the family and the village — and label its parts with the lesson's key words. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare, and fix every label you placed wrong.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
