# Federal and provincial revenue and expenditure in Pakistan — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** About federal revenue sources — the lesson teaches that —
a) federal revenue comes from customs duty, income and corporation tax, central excise and sales tax  b) federal spending heads are defence, debt servicing, administration, subsidies, services and development  c) provinces have their own revenue, a share in federal taxes, and their own expenditure heads
**Answer: a) federal revenue comes from customs duty, income and corporation tax, central excise and sales tax**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** About federal expenditure heads — the lesson teaches that —
a) federal revenue comes from customs duty, income and corporation tax, central excise and sales tax  b) federal spending heads are defence, debt servicing, administration, subsidies, services and development  c) provinces have their own revenue, a share in federal taxes, and their own expenditure heads
**Answer: b) federal spending heads are defence, debt servicing, administration, subsidies, services and development**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** About provincial governments' finances — the lesson teaches that —
a) federal revenue comes from customs duty, income and corporation tax, central excise and sales tax  b) federal spending heads are defence, debt servicing, administration, subsidies, services and development  c) provinces have their own revenue, a share in federal taxes, and their own expenditure heads
**Answer: c) provinces have their own revenue, a share in federal taxes, and their own expenditure heads**
