# Federal and provincial revenue and expenditure in Pakistan — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The federal government has no tax revenue.
**Answer: False** — four tax sources are named for it.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Debt servicing is not a federal spending head.
**Answer: False** — it is named second in the book's list.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Provinces receive no share of federal taxes.
**Answer: False** — a share in federal taxes is named as theirs.
