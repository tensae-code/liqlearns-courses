# Federal and provincial revenue and expenditure in Pakistan — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the federal government's revenue sources.
**Answer:** Customs duty, income tax and corporation tax, central excise duty, and sales tax.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the heads of federal expenditure.
**Answer:** Defence, debt servicing, administration, subsidies, social and economic services, and development expenditure.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** How are provincial finances arranged?
**Answer:** Provinces have their own revenue sources, a share in federal taxes, and their own expenditure heads.
