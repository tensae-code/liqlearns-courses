# Federal and provincial revenue and expenditure in Pakistan — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Federal revenue from imports and exports is _____ duty.
**Answer: customs**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Paying the state's debts is debt _____.
**Answer: servicing**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Provinces receive a share in _____ taxes.
**Answer: federal**
