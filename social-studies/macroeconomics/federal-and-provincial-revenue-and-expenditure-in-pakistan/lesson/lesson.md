# Federal and provincial revenue and expenditure in Pakistan

## Do it first

Before you read on, write what you already think this lesson's title means — federal and provincial revenue and expenditure in pakistan — in one sentence of your own. Keep it; you will check it against the lesson at the end.

## Sub lesson 1 — Federal revenue sources

The Class 12 government-finance chapter lists the **sources of revenue of the federal government**: **customs duty** on exports and imports, **income tax and corporation tax**, **central excise duty**, and **sales tax**.

## Sub lesson 2 — Federal expenditure heads

Against the revenue stand the **heads of federal expenditure**: **defence, debt servicing, administration, subsidies, social and economic services, and development expenditure**. The list itself shows what the Pakistani state spends on first.

## Sub lesson 3 — Provincial governments' finances

The **provincial governments** have their **own revenue sources**, a **share in federal taxes**, and their **own expenditure heads** — the federation's money divided between centre and provinces, each with its part.
