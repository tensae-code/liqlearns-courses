# Video script — Federal and provincial revenue and expenditure in Pakistan

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Federal and provincial revenue and expenditure in Pakistan — as Pakistan's book teaches it, says the narrator: federal revenue comes from customs duty, income and corporation tax, central excise and sales tax; federal spending heads are defence, debt servicing, administration, subsidies, services and development; provinces have their own revenue, a share in federal taxes, and their own expenditure heads.
