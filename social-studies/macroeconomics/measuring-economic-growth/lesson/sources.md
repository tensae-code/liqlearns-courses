Grade 10 teacher-guide note (placement 19): the data snapshot comes from the activity keys in *Economics, Teacher Guide Book, Grade 10*, printed pp. 82–83 (teacher guide — evidence), and is dated: growth averaging 9.4% a year from 2010/11 to 2019/20, slowing to 6.1% in 2019/20 under COVID-19, with per-capita income of $850 and the national aim of lower-middle-income status by 2025. The guide's worked exercise (real GDP $95.91bn in 2019, $96.61bn in 2020, $110.00bn in 2021, population 110 million) prints its own uncertainties ('14% ??', '0.007% ??') and its 2020 per-capita growth figure is arithmetically wrong as printed: (96.61 − 95.91) / 95.91 = 0.73%, not 0.007% — the key appears to print the decimal fraction as a percentage. The added sub lesson teaches the corrected 0.73%; the guide's 2021 total growth of about 14% (13.86% exactly) checks out and is taught as computed.
