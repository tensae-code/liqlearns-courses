# Measuring economic growth — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Long-run growth's first mark is a sustained rise in _____ real income.
**Answer: per-capita**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Growth is necessary for development but not _____ — output alone does not improve life.
**Answer: sufficient**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In the 2008 World Bank bands, low income runs up to _____ of GNI per capita.
**Answer: $975**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Ethiopia's growth averaged _____ a year from 2010/11 to 2019/20, by the guide's dated snapshot.
**Answer: 9.4%**

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** From $95.91bn to $96.61bn, real GDP growth is (96.61 − 95.91) ÷ 95.91 = _____.
**Answer: 0.73%**
