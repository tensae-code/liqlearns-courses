# Measuring economic growth — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Long-run growth means a sustained rise in per-capita real income together with structural change.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Growth is necessary for development but not sufficient — development is wider.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The World Bank bands sort countries by GNI per capita, from low income up to $975 (2008 thresholds).
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** The guide's snapshot gives Ethiopian growth averaging 9.4% a year from 2010/11 to 2019/20, slowing to 6.1% in 2019/20.
**Answer: True**

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** Real GDP growth from $95.91bn to $96.61bn is (96.61 − 95.91) / 95.91 = 0.73%.
**Answer: True**
