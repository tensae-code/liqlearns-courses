# Measuring economic growth — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Short-run and long-run growth" in your own words.
**Answer:** Long-run growth means a sustained rise in per-capita real income together with structural change.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Growth inside development" in your own words.
**Answer:** Growth is necessary for development but not sufficient — development is wider.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Countries classified by income" in your own words.
**Answer:** The World Bank bands sort countries by GNI per capita, from low income up to $975 (2008 thresholds).

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Explain "Ethiopia's growth snapshot, 2010/11–2019/20 (teacher guide)" in your own words.
**Answer:** The guide's snapshot gives Ethiopian growth averaging 9.4% a year from 2010/11 to 2019/20, slowing to 6.1% in 2019/20.

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** Explain "Working a growth rate — and checking it (teacher guide, corrected)" in your own words.
**Answer:** Real GDP growth from $95.91bn to $96.61bn is (96.61 − 95.91) / 95.91 = 0.73%.
