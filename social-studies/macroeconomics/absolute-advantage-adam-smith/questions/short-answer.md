# Absolute advantage — Adam Smith — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Smith's argument — specialise where you are best" in your own words.
**Answer:** Smith: specialise where your cost is absolutely lower, import where it is higher; trade is positive-sum.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "The model's assumptions" in your own words.
**Answer:** The model assumes two countries, two goods, labour as the only (homogeneous, immobile) factor, fixed technology and no transport costs.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "The Ethiopia–China worked example" in your own words.
**Answer:** Ethiopia holds absolute advantage in coffee (4 vs 1 kg), China in cloth (5 vs 2 yards); specialisation raises joint output to 8 kg and 10 yards.
