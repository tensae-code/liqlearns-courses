# Absolute advantage — Adam Smith — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Smith held that trade is zero-sum, like the mercantilists.
**Answer: False — Smith's break with mercantilism is exactly this: trade is positive-sum when each specialises at its absolute advantage.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Smith's model assumes many factors of production including mobile capital.
**Answer: False — Labour is the ONLY factor in the model, and it cannot move between countries — goods move instead.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** After specialisation the pair produce less than before trade.
**Answer: False — Joint output RISES — from 5 kg of coffee to 8, and from 7 yards of cloth to 10. That rise is the gain.**
