# Absolute advantage — Adam Smith — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Smith's argument — specialise where you are best"?
a) Smith: specialise where your cost is absolutely lower, import where it is higher; trade is positive-sum.  b) The model assumes two countries, two goods, labour as the only (homogeneous, immobile) factor, fixed technology and no transport costs.  c) Ethiopia holds absolute advantage in coffee (4 vs 1 kg), China in cloth (5 vs 2 yards); specialisation raises joint output to 8 kg and 10 yards.
