# Video script — Government aid to local producers

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Government aid to local producers through infrastructure and government services — the SMEC helping people start and run businesses, agricultural extension officers, district support funds, and SMEC loans through the National Development Bank with their success stories.

How government and the economy promote enterprise — start-up programmes and business schools, funds through the National Development Bank, SME incubation centres, private ownership and contract law, macroeconomic stability through monetary and fiscal policy, and protection of infant industries.

Papua New Guinea's own way of teaching this, from the Papua New Guinea Grade 7 Social Science TG: Grade 7 Trade profiles the economic activities that contribute to PNG's national development — primary, secondary and tertiary industries, the banks, transport and communication firms that aid business, development projects, and direct government aid to local producers including SMEC loans through the National Development Bank.
