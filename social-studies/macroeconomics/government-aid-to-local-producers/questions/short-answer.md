# Government aid to local producers — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the lesson teach in "Government aid to local producers through infrastructure and"?
**Answer:** Government aid to local producers through infrastructure and government services — the SMEC helping people start and run businesses, agricultural extension officers, district support funds, and SMEC loans through the National Development Bank with their success stories.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the lesson teach in "How government and the economy promote enterprise —"?
**Answer:** How government and the economy promote enterprise — start-up programmes and business schools, funds through the National Development Bank, SME incubation centres, private ownership and contract law, macroeconomic stability through monetary and fiscal policy, and protection of infant industries.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the lesson teach in "Papua New Guinea's own way of teaching this"?
**Answer:** Papua New Guinea's own way of teaching this, from the Papua New Guinea Grade 7 Social Science TG: Grade 7 Trade profiles the economic activities that contribute to PNG's national development — primary, secondary and tertiary industries, the banks, transport and communication firms that aid business, development projects, and direct government aid to local producers including SMEC loans through the National Development Bank.
