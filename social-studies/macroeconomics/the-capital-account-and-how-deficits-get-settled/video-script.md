# Video script — The capital account — and how deficits get settled

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The capital (financial) account records movements of financial capital: inflows — borrowing from abroad, foreigners buying domestic assets — are credits; outflows — lending abroad, buying assets abroad — are debits.

The overall balance of payments is the current account plus the capital account. The two accounts are not independent stories: together they state whether the country, on balance, gained or used foreign exchange over the period.

A current-account deficit must be covered by a capital-account surplus or, failing that, paid out of official foreign-exchange reserves. That is why countries come under pressure to restrict trade: reserves are finite, and a deficit that outruns both cover routes drains them.
