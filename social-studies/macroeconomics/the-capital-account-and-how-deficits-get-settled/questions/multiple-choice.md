# The capital account — and how deficits get settled — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Capital movements, in and out"?
a) Capital inflows are credits and outflows are debits in the capital account.  b) The overall balance = current account + capital account.  c) A current-account deficit is covered by a capital surplus or out of official reserves.
**Answer: a) Capital inflows are credits and outflows are debits in the capital account.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "The overall balance"?
a) A current-account deficit is covered by a capital surplus or out of official reserves.  b) Capital inflows are credits and outflows are debits in the capital account.  c) The overall balance = current account + capital account.
**Answer: c) The overall balance = current account + capital account.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "How a deficit gets settled"?
a) The overall balance = current account + capital account.  b) A current-account deficit is covered by a capital surplus or out of official reserves.  c) Capital inflows are credits and outflows are debits in the capital account.
**Answer: b) A current-account deficit is covered by a capital surplus or out of official reserves.**
