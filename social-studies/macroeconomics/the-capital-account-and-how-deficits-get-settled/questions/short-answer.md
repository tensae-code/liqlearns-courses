# The capital account — and how deficits get settled — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Capital movements, in and out" in your own words.
**Answer:** Capital inflows are credits and outflows are debits in the capital account.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "The overall balance" in your own words.
**Answer:** The overall balance = current account + capital account.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "How a deficit gets settled" in your own words.
**Answer:** A current-account deficit is covered by a capital surplus or out of official reserves.
