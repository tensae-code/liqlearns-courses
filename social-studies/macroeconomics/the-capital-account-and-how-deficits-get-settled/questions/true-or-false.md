# The capital account — and how deficits get settled — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Foreigners buying domestic assets is a capital outflow.
**Answer: False — Money coming IN for domestic assets is an inflow — a credit. Outflows are lending and buying assets abroad.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The overall balance is the current account alone.
**Answer: False — Both accounts count: overall = current + capital.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A current-account deficit never needs covering.
**Answer: False — It must be covered — by capital inflows or from reserves. Uncovered, the payments cannot settle.**
