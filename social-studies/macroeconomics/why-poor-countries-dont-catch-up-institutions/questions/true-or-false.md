# Why poor countries don't catch up — institutions — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Neoclassical theory predicted poor countries would grow faster through technology transfer and diminishing returns to capital in rich countries.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Contrary to the neoclassical prediction, poor countries did not on the whole catch up with rich ones.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Keefer and Knack explain the failure to catch up by deficient institutions: weak rule of law, corruption, and expropriation and contract-repudiation risks.
**Answer: True**
