# Why poor countries don't catch up — institutions — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "The prediction — poor countries should catch up"?
a) technology transfer plus diminishing returns to capital should let poor countries grow faster  b) poor countries did not catch up as predicted — the reverse happened  c) weak rule of law, corruption, expropriation and contract-repudiation risks keep capital away
**Answer: a) technology transfer plus diminishing returns to capital should let poor countries grow faster**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "What actually happened — the reverse"?
a) technology transfer plus diminishing returns to capital should let poor countries grow faster  b) weak rule of law, corruption, expropriation and contract-repudiation risks keep capital away  c) poor countries did not catch up as predicted — the reverse happened
**Answer: c) poor countries did not catch up as predicted — the reverse happened**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "The institutional explanation — Keefer and Knack"?
a) poor countries did not catch up as predicted — the reverse happened  b) weak rule of law, corruption, expropriation and contract-repudiation risks keep capital away  c) technology transfer plus diminishing returns to capital should let poor countries grow faster
**Answer: b) weak rule of law, corruption, expropriation and contract-repudiation risks keep capital away**
