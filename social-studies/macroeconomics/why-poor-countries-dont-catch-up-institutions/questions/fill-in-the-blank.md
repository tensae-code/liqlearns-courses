# Why poor countries don't catch up — institutions — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Poor countries were predicted to catch up partly through _____ — adopting the rich world's existing technology.
**Answer: technology transfer**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Instead of catching up as predicted, the _____ happened — poor countries largely failed to close the gap.
**Answer: reverse**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Keefer and Knack blame the failed catch-up on deficient _____ — weak rule of law, corruption and insecure contracts.
**Answer: institutions**
