# Why poor countries don't catch up — institutions — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "The prediction — poor countries should catch up" in your own words.
**Answer:** Neoclassical theory predicted poor countries would grow faster through technology transfer and diminishing returns to capital in rich countries.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "What actually happened — the reverse" in your own words.
**Answer:** Contrary to the neoclassical prediction, poor countries did not on the whole catch up with rich ones.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "The institutional explanation — Keefer and Knack" in your own words.
**Answer:** Keefer and Knack explain the failure to catch up by deficient institutions: weak rule of law, corruption, and expropriation and contract-repudiation risks.
