# Hands-on — Why poor countries don't catch up — institutions

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Act the two runners: one runs a clear lane, the other must stop at three 'official' desks that stamp, delay and confiscate. The class names each desk from the lesson, then removes them one by one and re-runs.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — two runners where the behind runner's track is blocked by broken-rules signage — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
