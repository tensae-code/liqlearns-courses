# Public finance — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Public finance studies —
a) how a family saves for a festival  b) how the state raises, holds and spends public money  c) how a shop prices its goods
**Answer: b) how the state raises, holds and spends public money**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The state raises public money chiefly through —
a) taxes and other public revenues  b) money printed for officials to keep  c) officials' private savings
**Answer: a) taxes and other public revenues**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Public money is spent through —
a) nothing — raised money is never spent  b) officials' private choices with no budget  c) the budget, on the public's business
**Answer: c) the budget, on the public's business**
