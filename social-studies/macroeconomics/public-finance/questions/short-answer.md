# Public finance — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How is the state's money different from a family's?
**Answer: It is raised by law, held for the public, and spent on the public's business.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Where is public money held?
**Answer: In the public treasury and budget.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name the three parts public finance joins into one discipline.
**Answer: Raising public money, holding it, and spending it.**
