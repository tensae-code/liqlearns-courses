# Public finance — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Public finance is about the state's money.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The state raises public money chiefly through officials' private savings.
**Answer: False — Public money is raised by taxes and revenues under law — it is never officials' private money.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Public money is spent through the budget on the public's business.
**Answer: True**
