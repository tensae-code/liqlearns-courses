# Video script — Public finance

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Public finance studies the state's money as a subject of its own: where public money comes from, how it is held, and where it goes. It is not the finance of a family or a firm — the state's money is raised by law, held for the public, and spent on the public's business.

The state raises public money chiefly through taxes and other public revenues, and holds it in the public treasury and budget. Every riel raised is raised under a law and owed an account: public money is never the private money of the officials who touch it.

Public money is spent through the budget on what the state exists to do — services, works, administration, defence and the rest of the public's business. Public finance joins the three parts into one discipline: raise honestly, hold accountably, spend on the public's needs — the ground the foreign-trade and state-and-private-sector lessons then build on.
