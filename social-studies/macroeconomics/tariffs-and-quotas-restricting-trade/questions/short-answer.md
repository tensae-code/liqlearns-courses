# Tariffs and quotas — restricting trade — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Trade policy, for and against" in your own words.
**Answer:** Restrictions come in price form (tariffs) and quantity form (quotas).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "The tariff — a tax at the border" in your own words.
**Answer:** A tariff raises the import's domestic price, cuts its demand, and raises government revenue.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "The quota — and why governments restrict" in your own words.
**Answer:** A quota fixes a maximum import volume; reasons to restrict include protecting jobs and infant industries and national security.
