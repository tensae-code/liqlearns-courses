# Tariffs and quotas — restricting trade — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** All trade restrictions work by banning imports completely.
**Answer: False — Neither tool here is a ban: a tariff prices imports up; a quota caps their volume.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A tariff lowers the price of imports.
**Answer: False — A tariff is a duty added at the border — it raises the domestic price, which is how it cuts demand.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Protecting infant industries is never a stated reason for restricting trade.
**Answer: False — It is one of the six stated reasons — a new industry shielded until it can stand against imports.**
