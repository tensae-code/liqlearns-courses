# Tariffs and quotas — restricting trade — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or figure from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A tariff restricts through ____; a quota through quantity.
**Answer: price**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A tariff is a customs or import ____.
**Answer: duty**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A quota fixes the maximum ____ of imports.
**Answer: volume**
