# Tariffs and quotas — restricting trade — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Trade policy, for and against"?
a) Restrictions come in price form (tariffs) and quantity form (quotas).  b) A tariff raises the import's domestic price, cuts its demand, and raises government revenue.  c) A quota fixes a maximum import volume; reasons to restrict include protecting jobs and infant industries and national security.
**Answer: a) Restrictions come in price form (tariffs) and quantity form (quotas).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "The tariff — a tax at the border"?
a) A quota fixes a maximum import volume; reasons to restrict include protecting jobs and infant industries and national security.  b) Restrictions come in price form (tariffs) and quantity form (quotas).  c) A tariff raises the import's domestic price, cuts its demand, and raises government revenue.
**Answer: c) A tariff raises the import's domestic price, cuts its demand, and raises government revenue.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "The quota — and why governments restrict"?
a) A tariff raises the import's domestic price, cuts its demand, and raises government revenue.  b) A quota fixes a maximum import volume; reasons to restrict include protecting jobs and infant industries and national security.  c) Restrictions come in price form (tariffs) and quantity form (quotas).
**Answer: b) A quota fixes a maximum import volume; reasons to restrict include protecting jobs and infant industries and national security.**
