# Video script — Consequences of the 1980s crisis and adjustment in Costa Rica

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The social bill of the new model in the book's own tables.

The state stopped being the main employer (4,650 public posts cut in the 1995 adjustment), real wages fell and families slid from poverty into extreme poverty, subsidies were eliminated, basic-service tariffs rose.

The informal sector grew (street selling), housing credit grew dearer, and attempts were made to sell state assets such as the ICE, the INS and the CCSS.
