# Consequences of the 1980s crisis and adjustment in Costa Rica — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the lesson teach about consecuencias de la crisis económica de los años 80?
**Answer:** The social bill of the new model in the book's own tables.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the lesson teach about consecuencias de la crisis económica de los años 80 — more?
**Answer:** The state stopped being the main employer (4,650 public posts cut in the 1995 adjustment), real wages fell and families slid from poverty into extreme poverty, subsidies were eliminated, basic-service tariffs rose.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the lesson teach about consecuencias de la crisis económica de los años 80 — more — more?
**Answer:** The informal sector grew (street selling), housing credit grew dearer, and attempts were made to sell state assets such as the ICE, the INS and the CCSS.
