# Free-trade treaties in the Americas — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Americas are organised into five integration blocs, and Nicaragua's books read the continent's free-trade treaties through those blocs and their schemes since 1960 — ALADI, MCCA, CAN, SELA, MERCOSUR, NAFTA, DR-CAFTA, ALCA, ALBA and CELAC.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** CAFTA is read critically as an investment treaty: the maquila model, foreign capital, and why a small economy's treaty with a giant is debated, because powerful nations have imposed terms in the history of these agreements.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Trade agreements should something else entirely under fair competition — that is what they regulate and what they are for, as Nicaragua's Grade 8 book lists them.
**Answer: False** — Trade agreements should remove obstacles and widen markets under fair competition — that is what they regulate and what they are for, as Nicaragua's Grade 8 book lists them.
