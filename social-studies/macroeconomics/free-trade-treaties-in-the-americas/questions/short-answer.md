# Free-trade treaties in the Americas — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the book teach about the continent's integration blocs?
**Answer:** The Americas are organised into five integration blocs, and Nicaragua's books read the continent's free-trade treaties through those blocs and their schemes since 1960 — ALADI, MCCA, CAN, SELA, MERCOSUR, NAFTA, DR-CAFTA, ALCA, ALBA and CELAC.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the book teach about cafta read critically — the maquila model?
**Answer:** CAFTA is read critically as an investment treaty: the maquila model, foreign capital, and why a small economy's treaty with a giant is debated, because powerful nations have imposed terms in the history of these agreements.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the book teach about what trade agreements should do?
**Answer:** Trade agreements should remove obstacles and widen markets under fair competition — that is what they regulate and what they are for, as Nicaragua's Grade 8 book lists them.
