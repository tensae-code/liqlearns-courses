# Free-trade treaties in the Americas — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Americas are organised into _____, and Nicaragua's books read the continent's free-trade treaties through those blocs and their schemes since 1960 — ALADI, MCCA, CAN, SELA, MERCOSUR, NAFTA, DR-CAFTA, ALCA, ALBA and CELAC.
**Answer: five integration blocs**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** CAFTA is read critically as an investment treaty: _____, foreign capital, and why a small economy's treaty with a giant is debated, because powerful nations have imposed terms in the history of these agreements.
**Answer: the maquila model**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Trade agreements should _____ under fair competition — that is what they regulate and what they are for, as Nicaragua's Grade 8 book lists them.
**Answer: remove obstacles and widen markets**
