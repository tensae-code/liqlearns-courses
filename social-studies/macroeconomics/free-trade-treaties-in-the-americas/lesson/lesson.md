# Free-trade treaties in the Americas

## Do it first

Think of this topic the way the Nicaragua book opens it — Free-trade treaties in the Americas. What do you already know about it before you read on?

## Sub lesson 1 — The continent's integration blocs

The Americas are organised into five integration blocs, and Nicaragua's books read the continent's free-trade treaties through those blocs and their schemes since 1960 — ALADI, MCCA, CAN, SELA, MERCOSUR, NAFTA, DR-CAFTA, ALCA, ALBA and CELAC.

## Sub lesson 2 — CAFTA read critically — the maquila model

CAFTA is read critically as an investment treaty: the maquila model, foreign capital, and why a small economy's treaty with a giant is debated, because powerful nations have imposed terms in the history of these agreements.

## Sub lesson 3 — What trade agreements should do

Trade agreements should remove obstacles and widen markets under fair competition — that is what they regulate and what they are for, as Nicaragua's Grade 8 book lists them.
