# Theories of economic growth — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Classical growth theory ties an economy's growth to its _____.
**Answer: capital, labour and land**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Harrod-Domar makes growth depend on _____, while Schumpeter makes the innovating entrepreneur the engine of growth.
**Answer: saving and investment**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Endogenous growth theory finds the engine inside the economy itself — _____ built up by investment.
**Answer: knowledge, skills and ideas**
