# Theories of economic growth — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the book teach about classical growth theory?
**Answer:** Classical growth theory ties an economy's growth to its capital, labour and land.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the book teach about harrod-domar and schumpeter?
**Answer:** Harrod-Domar makes growth depend on saving and investment, while Schumpeter makes the innovating entrepreneur the engine of growth.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the book teach about endogenous growth?
**Answer:** Endogenous growth theory finds the engine inside the economy itself — knowledge, skills and ideas built up by investment.
