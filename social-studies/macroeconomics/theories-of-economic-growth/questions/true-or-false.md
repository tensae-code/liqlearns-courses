# Theories of economic growth — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Classical growth theory ties an economy's growth to its capital, labour and land.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Harrod-Domar makes growth depend on saving and investment, while Schumpeter makes the innovating entrepreneur the engine of growth.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Endogenous growth theory finds the engine inside the economy itself — something else entirely built up by investment.
**Answer: False** — Endogenous growth theory finds the engine inside the economy itself — knowledge, skills and ideas built up by investment.
