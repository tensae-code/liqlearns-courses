# Theories of economic growth — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In this lesson, “Classical growth theory” turns on —
a) capital, labour and land  b) saving and investment  c) knowledge, skills and ideas
**Answer: a) capital, labour and land**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In this lesson, “Harrod-Domar and Schumpeter” turns on —
a) saving and investment  b) capital, labour and land  c) knowledge, skills and ideas
**Answer: a) saving and investment**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In this lesson, “Endogenous growth” turns on —
a) knowledge, skills and ideas  b) capital, labour and land  c) saving and investment
**Answer: a) knowledge, skills and ideas**
