# Video script — Theories of economic growth

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Classical growth theory ties an economy's growth to its capital, labour and land.

Harrod-Domar makes growth depend on saving and investment, while Schumpeter makes the innovating entrepreneur the engine of growth.

Endogenous growth theory finds the engine inside the economy itself — knowledge, skills and ideas built up by investment.
