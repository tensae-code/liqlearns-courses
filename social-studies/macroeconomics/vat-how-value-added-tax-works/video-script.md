# Video script — VAT — how value added tax works

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The value added tax, in force in Ethiopia from 1 January 2003 at a standard 15%, is charged on the value added at each stage of production and trade. Each registered vendor charges VAT in the selling price and credits the VAT already paid on inputs — so the slices stack to 15% of the final price, no more.

VAT is a destination-based consumption tax: it falls on final consumption, where the good is used, not where it was made. Businesses along the chain are collectors; the final consumer is the bearer — an indirect tax, as the earlier lesson classified it.

Exempt supplies flip the burden: the supplier charges no output VAT on its sales, but bears unrecoverable input VAT on its own purchases. Exemption is not escape — the input tax sticks in the exempt supplier's costs.
