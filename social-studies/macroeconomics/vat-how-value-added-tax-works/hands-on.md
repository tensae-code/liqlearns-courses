# Hands-on — VAT — how value added tax works

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Stack the slices
A good is made for 100, sold on at 160, then retailed at 240 (values before VAT). At 15%, compute each stage's value added and VAT slice, and show the slices total 15% of 240 = 36.

## Animate it on paper
Draw the product moving through three hands, a small tax slice added at each stage, the slices stacking into one 15% column over the final price. Label the input credit at each hand.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
