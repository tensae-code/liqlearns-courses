# The neoliberal model in Latin America — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the lesson teach about the free, unregulated market allocates best?
**Answer:** Neoliberalism holds the free, unregulated market to be the most efficient allocator of resources.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the lesson teach about a smaller state — privatisation, deregulation, austerity?
**Answer:** Neoliberalism minimises state intervention — privatisation, deregulation, financial liberalisation — with fiscal austerity.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the lesson teach about free trade, tax cuts and flexible labour?
**Answer:** Neoliberalism adds free trade and FDI, tax cuts for firms and high incomes, and labour deregulation.
