# The neoliberal model in Latin America — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Neoliberalism holds state planning to be the best allocator.
**Answer: False** — its first characteristic is the unregulated market as allocator.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Neoliberalism expands state intervention in every market.
**Answer: False** — minimising state intervention is its named second characteristic.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Neoliberalism raises taxes especially on high incomes.
**Answer: False** — its tax characteristic is reductions, especially for firms and high incomes.
