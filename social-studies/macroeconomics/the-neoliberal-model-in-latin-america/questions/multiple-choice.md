# The neoliberal model in Latin America — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** About the free, unregulated market allocates best — the lesson teaches that —
a) neoliberalism holds the free, unregulated market to be the most efficient allocator of resources  b) neoliberalism minimises state intervention — privatisation, deregulation, financial liberalisation — with fiscal austerity  c) neoliberalism adds free trade and FDI, tax cuts for firms and high incomes, and labour deregulation
**Answer: a) neoliberalism holds the free, unregulated market to be the most efficient allocator of resources**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** About a smaller state — privatisation, deregulation, austerity — the lesson teaches that —
a) neoliberalism holds the free, unregulated market to be the most efficient allocator of resources  b) neoliberalism minimises state intervention — privatisation, deregulation, financial liberalisation — with fiscal austerity  c) neoliberalism adds free trade and FDI, tax cuts for firms and high incomes, and labour deregulation
**Answer: b) neoliberalism minimises state intervention — privatisation, deregulation, financial liberalisation — with fiscal austerity**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** About free trade, tax cuts and flexible labour — the lesson teaches that —
a) neoliberalism holds the free, unregulated market to be the most efficient allocator of resources  b) neoliberalism minimises state intervention — privatisation, deregulation, financial liberalisation — with fiscal austerity  c) neoliberalism adds free trade and FDI, tax cuts for firms and high incomes, and labour deregulation
**Answer: c) neoliberalism adds free trade and FDI, tax cuts for firms and high incomes, and labour deregulation**
