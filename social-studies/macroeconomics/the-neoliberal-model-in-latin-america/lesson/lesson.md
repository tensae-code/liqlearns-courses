# The neoliberal model in Latin America

## Do it first

Before you read on, write what you already think this lesson's title means — the neoliberal model in Latin America — in one sentence of your own. Keep it; you will check it against the lesson at the end.

## Sub lesson 1 — The free, unregulated market allocates best

The Grade 12 book sets out neoliberalism's characteristics one by one. First: the free, unregulated market as the most efficient allocator of resources — prices and competition, left alone, assign everything best.

## Sub lesson 2 — A smaller state — privatisation, deregulation, austerity

Second, the state steps back: minimising state intervention through privatisation, deregulation and financial liberalisation, with fiscal austerity as its budget rule.

## Sub lesson 3 — Free trade, tax cuts and flexible labour

The rest of the list: free trade and foreign direct investment; private property and individualism; tax reductions, especially for firms and high incomes; and labour deregulation — flexible contracts and reduced workers' rights.
