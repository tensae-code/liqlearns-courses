# Video script — The neoliberal model in Latin America

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The neoliberal model in Latin America — as Bolivia's book teaches it, says the narrator: neoliberalism holds the free, unregulated market to be the most efficient allocator of resources; neoliberalism minimises state intervention — privatisation, deregulation, financial liberalisation — with fiscal austerity; neoliberalism adds free trade and FDI, tax cuts for firms and high incomes, and labour deregulation.
