# Who gains from the market

## Do it first

Before you read on, write what you already think this lesson's title means — who gains from the market — in one sentence of your own. Keep it; you will check it against the lesson at the end.

## Sub lesson 1 — The foreign businessperson gains most

In the shirt's market chain, the **foreign businessperson gains most** from the market.

## Sub lesson 2 — The exporter gains moderately

The **exporter gains moderately** — more than the producers, far less than the foreign buyer at the top of the chain.

## Sub lesson 3 — Those who toiled longest gain least — dependence explains it

The **cotton farmer, weavers and factory workers who toiled longest gain least**, because **the poor depend on the rich for loans, materials, marketing and jobs**.
