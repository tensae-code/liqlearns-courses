# Video script — Who gains from the market

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Who gains from the market — as India's book teaches it, says the narrator: the market's gains are unequal — the foreign businessperson gains most, the exporter moderately, while the cotton farmer, weavers and factory workers who toiled longest gain least, because the poor depend on the rich for loans, materials, marketing and jobs.
