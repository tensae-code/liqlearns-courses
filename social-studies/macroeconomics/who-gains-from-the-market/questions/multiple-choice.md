# Who gains from the market — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Who gains most from the market chain?
a) the foreign businessperson  b) the cotton farmer  c) the weavers
**Answer: a) the foreign businessperson**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The exporter gains —
a) moderately  b) the most  c) nothing
**Answer: a) moderately**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Those who gain least are —
a) the farmer, weavers and factory workers  b) the foreign buyers  c) the exporters
**Answer: a) the farmer, weavers and factory workers**
