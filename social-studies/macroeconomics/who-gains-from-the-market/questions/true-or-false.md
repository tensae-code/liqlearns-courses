# Who gains from the market — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The cotton farmer gains most from the chain.
**Answer: False — the foreign businessperson gains most.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The exporter gains least of all.
**Answer: False — the farmer, weavers and workers gain least.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The poor gain least because they depend on nobody.
**Answer: False — they depend on the rich for loans, materials, marketing and jobs.**
