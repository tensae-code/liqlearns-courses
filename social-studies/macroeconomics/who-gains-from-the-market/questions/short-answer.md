# Who gains from the market — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Who gains most, and who moderately?
**Answer:** The foreign businessperson gains most; the exporter gains moderately.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How much does the exporter gain?
**Answer:** Moderately — between the foreign buyer and the producers.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Why do the poor gain least?
**Answer:** They depend on the rich for loans, materials, marketing and jobs.
