# Video script — Regionally-owned enterprises — BUMD

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A BUMD is a business entity owned by a regional government in Indonesia.

BUMD take forms such as regional companies and regional public companies, and their role is to serve the region and add to regional income.

The book weighs BUMD's strengths in serving local needs against weaknesses such as limited capital and management.
