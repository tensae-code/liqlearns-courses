# Regionally-owned enterprises — BUMD — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A BUMD is a business entity owned by a _____ in Indonesia.
**Answer: regional government**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** BUMD take forms such as regional companies and regional public companies, and their role is to serve the region and add to _____.
**Answer: regional income**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The book weighs BUMD's strengths in serving local needs against weaknesses such as _____ and management.
**Answer: limited capital**
