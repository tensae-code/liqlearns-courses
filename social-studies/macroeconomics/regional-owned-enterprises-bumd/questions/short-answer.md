# Regionally-owned enterprises — BUMD — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the book teach about what a bumd is?
**Answer:** A BUMD is a business entity owned by a regional government in Indonesia.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the book teach about their types and role?
**Answer:** BUMD take forms such as regional companies and regional public companies, and their role is to serve the region and add to regional income.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the book teach about strengths and weaknesses?
**Answer:** The book weighs BUMD's strengths in serving local needs against weaknesses such as limited capital and management.
