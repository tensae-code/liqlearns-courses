# Regionally-owned enterprises — BUMD — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A BUMD is a business entity owned by a regional government in Indonesia.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** BUMD take forms such as regional companies and regional public companies, and their role is to serve the region and add to regional income.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The book weighs BUMD's strengths in serving local needs against weaknesses such as something else entirely and management.
**Answer: False** — The book weighs BUMD's strengths in serving local needs against weaknesses such as limited capital and management.
