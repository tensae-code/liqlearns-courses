## Sub lesson 1 — Average gains from trade

Trade brings **average gains** — taken across the whole economy, **gains exceed losses**, which is the case for open trade.

## Sub lesson 2 — Disruptive market change

Against the average stands **disruptive market change** — the **workers and firms displaced** when imports shift an industry bear **concentrated, real losses**.

## Sub lesson 3 — The tradeoff policy must face

Trade policy faces the **tradeoff whole**: keep the **average gains** while answering the **disruption** — the complexity of international trade is that **both are true at once**.
