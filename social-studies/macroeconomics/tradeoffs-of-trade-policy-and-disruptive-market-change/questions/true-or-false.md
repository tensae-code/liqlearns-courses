# Tradeoffs of trade policy and disruptive market change — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Average gains means nobody loses from trade.
**Answer: False — averages hide real losses; gains exceed losses overall, not for everyone.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Displaced workers move costlessly to new jobs at once.
**Answer: False — displacement is disruptive: real, concentrated losses for the workers and firms hit.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Policy can have the gains with no disruption to answer.
**Answer: False — both are true at once; that is the tradeoff.**
