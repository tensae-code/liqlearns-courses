# Tradeoffs of trade policy and disruptive market change — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Average gains means —
a) across the whole economy, trade's gains exceed its losses  b) every single person gains  c) there are no losses
**Answer: a) across the whole economy, trade's gains exceed its losses**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Disruptive market change falls on —
a) the workers and firms displaced by shifting trade  b) no one  c) only consumers
**Answer: a) the workers and firms displaced by shifting trade**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The tradeoff is between —
a) average gains and disruptive losses for the displaced  b) exports and imports only  c) price and quantity
**Answer: a) average gains and disruptive losses for the displaced**
