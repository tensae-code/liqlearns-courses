# Tradeoffs of trade policy and disruptive market change — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What are trade's average gains?
**Answer:** Across the whole economy, the gains from trade exceed the losses.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Who bears trade's disruptive change?
**Answer:** The workers and firms displaced when trade shifts an industry.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** State the tradeoff of trade policy.
**Answer:** Trade gains on average, yet displaces particular workers and firms; policy must hold both truths at once.
