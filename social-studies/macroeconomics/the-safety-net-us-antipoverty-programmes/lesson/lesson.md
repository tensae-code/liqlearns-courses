# The safety net — US antipoverty programmes

## Do it first

## Sub lesson 1 — Who the safety net catches

The **safety net** is the set of **antipoverty programmes** for people **below and just above the poverty line** — a floor under incomes.

## Sub lesson 2 — Goals and history

The programmes carry **goals** — relieving poverty and cushioning shocks — and a **history**: built up over decades of US policy.

## Sub lesson 3 — Why it is controversial

The safety net is **controversial** — arguments run over its **cost, its effects on work incentives, and who should receive help**.
