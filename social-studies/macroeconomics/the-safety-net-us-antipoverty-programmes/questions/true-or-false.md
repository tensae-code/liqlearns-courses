# The safety net — US antipoverty programmes — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The safety net covers only people far above the poverty line.
**Answer: False — it is for those below and just above the poverty line.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The safety net has no stated goals.
**Answer: False — its programmes carry goals: relieving poverty and cushioning shocks.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Everyone agrees on every part of the safety net.
**Answer: False — it is controversial, over cost, incentives and eligibility.**
