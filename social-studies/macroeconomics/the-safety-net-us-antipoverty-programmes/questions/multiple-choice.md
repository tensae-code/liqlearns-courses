# The safety net — US antipoverty programmes — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The safety net serves people —
a) below and just above the poverty line  b) only the rich  c) only firms
**Answer: a) below and just above the poverty line**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The safety net was —
a) built up over decades of US policy  b) created in a single year with no history  c) imported unchanged
**Answer: a) built up over decades of US policy**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The safety net is controversial over —
a) cost, work incentives and who should receive help  b) nothing at all  c) its name only
**Answer: a) cost, work incentives and who should receive help**
