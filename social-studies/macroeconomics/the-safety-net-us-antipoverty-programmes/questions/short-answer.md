# The safety net — US antipoverty programmes — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Who is the safety net for?
**Answer:** People below and just above the poverty line.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What are the safety net's goals?
**Answer:** Relieving poverty and cushioning shocks for those near the poverty line.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name one controversy over the safety net.
**Answer:** Its cost, its effect on work incentives, or who should receive help (any one).
