# Costa Rica's coffee agro-export model — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** From the 1830s coffee inserted Costa Rica into the world market — a coffee oligarchy arose, smallholders became wage labourers, women and children earned _____ income, artisans lost to imports, and the national economy came to depend on…
**Answer: complementary**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The model defined (an economic system based on producing and _____ one or two farm products, with monoculture) and its working.
**Answer: exporting**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Banana in foreign hands (United Fruit Company) on the Caribbean coast while coffee stayed Costa Rican and sold to Europe and the US, industrial countries controlling raw-material _____ through capital in railways, ports and finance, and…
**Answer: producers**
