# Costa Rica's coffee agro-export model — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the lesson teach about el café como motor del cambio?
**Answer:** From the 1830s coffee inserted Costa Rica into the world market — a coffee oligarchy arose, smallholders became wage labourers, women and children earned complementary income, artisans lost to imports, and the national economy came to depend on one export (first shipped via Chile, direct to London from 1845, the US taking about 25% from 1860).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the lesson teach about la crisis del modelo agroexportador: antecedentes?
**Answer:** The model defined (an economic system based on producing and exporting one or two farm products, with monoculture) and its working.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the lesson teach about la crisis del modelo agroexportador: antecedentes — more?
**Answer:** Banana in foreign hands (United Fruit Company) on the Caribbean coast while coffee stayed Costa Rican and sold to Europe and the US, industrial countries controlling raw-material producers through capital in railways, ports and finance, and export profits funding the Liberal era's infrastructure.
