# The current account — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Three balances in one account"?
a) The current account sums the trade balance, the services balance and net transfers.  b) The trade balance is in surplus when exports exceed imports, deficit when they fall short.  c) Transfers are unrequited: gifts, donations and remittances, with nothing owed in return.
**Answer: a) The current account sums the trade balance, the services balance and net transfers.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "The trade balance in goods"?
a) Transfers are unrequited: gifts, donations and remittances, with nothing owed in return.  b) The current account sums the trade balance, the services balance and net transfers.  c) The trade balance is in surplus when exports exceed imports, deficit when they fall short.
**Answer: c) The trade balance is in surplus when exports exceed imports, deficit when they fall short.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Services and transfers"?
a) The trade balance is in surplus when exports exceed imports, deficit when they fall short.  b) Transfers are unrequited: gifts, donations and remittances, with nothing owed in return.  c) The current account sums the trade balance, the services balance and net transfers.
**Answer: b) Transfers are unrequited: gifts, donations and remittances, with nothing owed in return.**
