# The current account — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Three balances in one account" in your own words.
**Answer:** The current account sums the trade balance, the services balance and net transfers.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "The trade balance in goods" in your own words.
**Answer:** The trade balance is in surplus when exports exceed imports, deficit when they fall short.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Services and transfers" in your own words.
**Answer:** Transfers are unrequited: gifts, donations and remittances, with nothing owed in return.
