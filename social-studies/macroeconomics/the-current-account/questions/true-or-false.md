# The current account — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The current account records purchases of foreign factories.
**Answer: False — Buying assets abroad belongs to the capital account. The current account covers goods, services and transfers.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A trade deficit means imports are smaller than exports.
**Answer: False — Deficit means the opposite: imports exceed exports.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Remittances must be repaid with interest.
**Answer: False — Remittances are transfers — received 'for free', with nothing owed in return. Loans belong elsewhere in the accounts.**
