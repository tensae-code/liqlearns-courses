# Video script — The current account

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The current account sums three balances: merchandise trade, services, and transfers. It is the part of the balance of payments that records the economy's current, everyday exchange with the world.

The trade balance covers merchandise alone: it is in surplus when exports exceed imports and in deficit when they fall short. Learners are asked to judge Ethiopia's own position from its trade pattern.

Net services count shipping, insurance and banking earned minus bought. Net transfers are unrequited receipts and payments — gifts, donations and remittances — received 'for free', with nothing owed in return.
