# Seigniorage — the inflation tax — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or figure from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Revenue from issuing new money is called ____.
**Answer: seigniorage**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The hidden tax that money-printing lays on money holders is the ____ tax.
**Answer: inflation**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Printing money is a ____ of inflation, not a cure.
**Answer: cause**
