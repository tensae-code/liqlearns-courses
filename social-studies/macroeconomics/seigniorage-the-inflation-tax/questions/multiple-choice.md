# Seigniorage — the inflation tax — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Revenue from the printing press"?
a) Seigniorage is the revenue a government gains by issuing new money  b) Money-printing taxes money holders through the inflation it causes  c) Money-printing is a cause of inflation, not a cure for it
**Answer: a) Seigniorage is the revenue a government gains by issuing new money**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "The inflation tax"?
a) Money-printing taxes money holders through the inflation it causes  b) Money-printing is a cause of inflation, not a cure for it  c) Seigniorage is the revenue a government gains by issuing new money
**Answer: a) Money-printing taxes money holders through the inflation it causes**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "A cause, not a cure"?
a) Money-printing is a cause of inflation, not a cure for it  b) Seigniorage is the revenue a government gains by issuing new money  c) Money-printing taxes money holders through the inflation it causes
**Answer: a) Money-printing is a cause of inflation, not a cure for it**
