# Seigniorage — the inflation tax — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Revenue from the printing press" in your own words.
**Answer:** Seigniorage is the revenue a government gains by issuing new money.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "The inflation tax" in your own words.
**Answer:** Money-printing taxes money holders through the inflation it causes.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "A cause, not a cure" in your own words.
**Answer:** Money-printing is a cause of inflation, not a cure for it.
