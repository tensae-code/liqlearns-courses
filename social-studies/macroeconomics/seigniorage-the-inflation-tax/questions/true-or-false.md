# Seigniorage — the inflation tax — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Seigniorage is the revenue a government gains by issuing new money.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Money-printing taxes money holders through the inflation it causes.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Money-printing is a cause of inflation, not a cure for it.
**Answer: True**
