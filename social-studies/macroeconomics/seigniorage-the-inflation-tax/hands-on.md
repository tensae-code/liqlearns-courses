# Hands-on — Seigniorage — the inflation tax

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Shrink the loaf
Draw one banknote buying a full loaf, then half a loaf, then a quarter, as printed money doubles and doubles again. Label who paid for the government's spending in each frame.

## Argue both sides
In pairs: one argues a government in a cash crisis may print a little money; the other answers with the inflation tax. Both must use the word seigniorage correctly.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
