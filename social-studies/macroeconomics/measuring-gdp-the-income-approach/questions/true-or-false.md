# Measuring GDP — the income approach — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The income approach measures GDP from the incomes that production generates.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Wages, rent, interest and profits sum to national income.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** GDP equals national income plus indirect business taxes plus depreciation.
**Answer: True**
