# Measuring GDP — the income approach — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The income approach builds GDP from the _____ production generates.
**Answer: incomes**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Wages plus rent plus interest plus profits sum to _____.
**Answer: national income**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** National income plus indirect business taxes plus _____ — capital used up — equals GDP.
**Answer: depreciation**
