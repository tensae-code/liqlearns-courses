# Measuring GDP — the income approach — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Income is production, seen from the pocket"?
a) GDP measured by adding the incomes production generates  b) wages, rent, interest and profits sum to national income  c) GDP = national income + indirect business taxes + depreciation
**Answer: a) GDP measured by adding the incomes production generates**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "The four factor incomes"?
a) GDP measured by adding the incomes production generates  b) GDP = national income + indirect business taxes + depreciation  c) wages, rent, interest and profits sum to national income
**Answer: c) wages, rent, interest and profits sum to national income**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Adding back taxes and depreciation"?
a) wages, rent, interest and profits sum to national income  b) GDP = national income + indirect business taxes + depreciation  c) GDP measured by adding the incomes production generates
**Answer: b) GDP = national income + indirect business taxes + depreciation**
