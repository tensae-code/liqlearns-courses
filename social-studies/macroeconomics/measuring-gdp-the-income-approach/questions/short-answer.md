# Measuring GDP — the income approach — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Income is production, seen from the pocket" in your own words.
**Answer:** The income approach measures GDP from the incomes that production generates.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "The four factor incomes" in your own words.
**Answer:** Wages, rent, interest and profits sum to national income.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Adding back taxes and depreciation" in your own words.
**Answer:** GDP equals national income plus indirect business taxes plus depreciation.
