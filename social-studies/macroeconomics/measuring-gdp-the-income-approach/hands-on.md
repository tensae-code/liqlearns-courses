# Hands-on — Measuring GDP — the income approach

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
 Pour counters into four jars — wages, rent, interest, profit — as 'national income'; then add two more jars on top, taxes and depreciation, and lift the whole stack as 'GDP'.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — four factor-income coins — wages, rent, interest, profit — stacked, with tax and depreciation added on top — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
