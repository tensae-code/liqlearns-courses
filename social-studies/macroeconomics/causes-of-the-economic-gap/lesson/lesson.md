# Causes of the economic gap

## Do it first

Think of this topic the way the Indonesia book opens it — Causes of the economic gap. What do you already know about it before you read on?

## Sub lesson 1 — The gap between people and regions

The economic gap (kesenjangan ekonomi) is the uneven spread of income and wealth between people and between regions.

## Sub lesson 2 — Where the gap comes from

The book traces the gap to unequal chances — differences in education, jobs, capital and where a person lives.

## Sub lesson 3 — Why the causes matter

Knowing the causes matters because each cause points to a different way of closing the gap.
