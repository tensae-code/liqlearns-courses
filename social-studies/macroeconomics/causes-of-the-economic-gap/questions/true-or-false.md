# Causes of the economic gap — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The economic gap (kesenjangan ekonomi) is the uneven spread of income and wealth between people and between regions.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The book traces the gap to unequal chances — differences in education, jobs, capital and where a person lives.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Knowing the causes matters because each cause points to a different way of something else entirely.
**Answer: False** — Knowing the causes matters because each cause points to a different way of closing the gap.
