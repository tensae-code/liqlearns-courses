# Causes of the economic gap — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the book teach about the gap between people and regions?
**Answer:** The economic gap (kesenjangan ekonomi) is the uneven spread of income and wealth between people and between regions.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the book teach about where the gap comes from?
**Answer:** The book traces the gap to unequal chances — differences in education, jobs, capital and where a person lives.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the book teach about why the causes matter?
**Answer:** Knowing the causes matters because each cause points to a different way of closing the gap.
