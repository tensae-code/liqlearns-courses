# Causes of the economic gap — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The economic gap (kesenjangan ekonomi) is the uneven spread of income and wealth between people and _____.
**Answer: between regions**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The book traces the gap to _____ — differences in education, jobs, capital and where a person lives.
**Answer: unequal chances**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Knowing the causes matters because each cause points to a different way of _____.
**Answer: closing the gap**
