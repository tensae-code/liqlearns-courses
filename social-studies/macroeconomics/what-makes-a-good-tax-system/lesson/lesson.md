# What makes a good tax system

## Do it first

Design the perfect tax system on one poster: what must it be, to be called good? Write your poster's lines before the book's checklist is revealed.

## Sub lesson 1 — Simple, adequate, elastic

A good system is simple, financially adequate and elastic; broad-based across income, property and commodities; and administratively efficient. A system failing any of these fails in practice, however elegant its rates.

## Sub lesson 2 — Fair and steady

It should be balanced and harmonious, inequality-reducing, stability-promoting, and consistent with optimum resource allocation — equitable in burden and efficient in effect.

## Sub lesson 3 — Usable as an instrument

Above all it is understandable to the taxpayer and usable as a fiscal-policy instrument: growth- and living-standard-raising, socially advantageous. A tax people cannot understand cannot steer an economy.
