# What makes a good tax system — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Simple, adequate, elastic" in your own words.
**Answer:** A good tax system is simple, financially adequate, elastic and broad-based.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Fair and steady" in your own words.
**Answer:** A good system is inequality-reducing and stability-promoting.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Usable as an instrument" in your own words.
**Answer:** A good system is understandable to the taxpayer and usable as a fiscal-policy instrument.
