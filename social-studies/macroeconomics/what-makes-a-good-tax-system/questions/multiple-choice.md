# What makes a good tax system — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Simple, adequate, elastic"?
a) A good tax system is simple, financially adequate, elastic and broad-based  b) A good system is inequality-reducing and stability-promoting  c) A good system is understandable to the taxpayer and usable as a fiscal-policy instrument
**Answer: a) A good tax system is simple, financially adequate, elastic and broad-based**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Fair and steady"?
a) A good system is inequality-reducing and stability-promoting  b) A good system is understandable to the taxpayer and usable as a fiscal-policy instrument  c) A good tax system is simple, financially adequate, elastic and broad-based
**Answer: a) A good system is inequality-reducing and stability-promoting**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Usable as an instrument"?
a) A good system is understandable to the taxpayer and usable as a fiscal-policy instrument  b) A good tax system is simple, financially adequate, elastic and broad-based  c) A good system is inequality-reducing and stability-promoting
**Answer: a) A good system is understandable to the taxpayer and usable as a fiscal-policy instrument**
