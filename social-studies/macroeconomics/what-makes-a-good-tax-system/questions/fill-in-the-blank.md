# What makes a good tax system — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or figure from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A system spread across income, property and commodities is ____-based.
**Answer: broad**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A good system reduces ____ in its burden.
**Answer: inequality**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A good tax must be ____ to the taxpayer.
**Answer: understandable**
