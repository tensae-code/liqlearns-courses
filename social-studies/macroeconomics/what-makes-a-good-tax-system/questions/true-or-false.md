# What makes a good tax system — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A good tax system is simple, financially adequate, elastic and broad-based.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A good system is inequality-reducing and stability-promoting.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A good system is understandable to the taxpayer and usable as a fiscal-policy instrument.
**Answer: True**
