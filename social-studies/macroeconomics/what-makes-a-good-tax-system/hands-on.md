# Hands-on — What makes a good tax system

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Poster check
Compare your do-first poster with the lesson's checklist: tick what you had, add what you missed, and argue for one item of yours the book's list lacks — or concede it.

## Grade a system
Grade an invented tax system (narrow base, complex forms, high yield) against five of the lesson's characteristics, one line each, and give it a final mark with a reason.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
