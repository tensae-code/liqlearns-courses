# Costa Rica's 1980s economic crisis — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The second oil shock and the fall in coffee prices pushed GDP per capita down (negative growth from 1978–79); by 1982 inflation ran near 80% a year with consumer prices up 225%, _____ reserves were lost and intraregional trade fell by half.
**Answer: international**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The oil shock met a state that had stopped receiving tax income while spending far more than it _____ (health, education, a swollen state payroll).
**Answer: received**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** It borrowed from international banks until the external debt passed US$1,000 million and reached US$3,700 million by 1983, _____ climbed from 7.8% (1979) to 81% (1982), and President Carazo broke with the IMF and declared a moratorium on…
**Answer: inflation**
