# Costa Rica's 1980s economic crisis — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the lesson teach about crisis económica década de 1980?
**Answer:** The second oil shock and the fall in coffee prices pushed GDP per capita down (negative growth from 1978–79); by 1982 inflation ran near 80% a year with consumer prices up 225%, international reserves were lost and intraregional trade fell by half.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the lesson teach about factores externos e internos que propiciaron la crisis de la década de los 80?
**Answer:** The oil shock met a state that had stopped receiving tax income while spending far more than it received (health, education, a swollen state payroll).

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the lesson teach about factores externos e internos que propiciaron la crisis de la década de los 80 — more?
**Answer:** It borrowed from international banks until the external debt passed US$1,000 million and reached US$3,700 million by 1983, inflation climbed from 7.8% (1979) to 81% (1982), and President Carazo broke with the IMF and declared a moratorium on the external debt.
