# Hands-on — What a tax is

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Quid pro quo test
List five payments you know (bus fare, tax, a fine, a gift, rent). Mark which carry a direct return in proportion to payment, and place tax correctly — with its reason.

## No-tax morning
Describe one morning in a town where taxation vanished a year ago: what is missing by breakfast? Name at least four public things and who used to pay for them.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
