# What a tax is — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Compulsory and unrequited"?
a) Taxes are compulsory, unrequited payments to general government, with no quid pro quo  b) Taxation supplies 90% or more of government receipts in peacetime
