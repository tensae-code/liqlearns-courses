# What a tax is — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Compulsory and unrequited" in your own words.
**Answer:** Taxes are compulsory, unrequited payments to general government, with no quid pro quo.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "The government's main revenue" in your own words.
**Answer:** Taxation supplies 90% or more of government receipts in peacetime.
