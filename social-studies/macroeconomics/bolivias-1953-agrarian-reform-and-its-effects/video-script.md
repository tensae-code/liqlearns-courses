# Video script — Bolivia's 1953 agrarian reform and its effects

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Bolivia's 1953 agrarian reform and its effects — as Bolivia's book teaches it, says the narrator: in 1953 hacendados controlled 92% of cultivable land and 4.5% of rural owners held 70% of it; the reform made colonos and pongos small proprietors and brought almost 2,000,000 Bolivians into the national economy; the minifundio decayed into surcofundio, driving migration and an urban informal sector, while the east built agroindustry.
