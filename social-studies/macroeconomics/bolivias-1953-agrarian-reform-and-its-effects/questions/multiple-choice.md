Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** About 1953 — 4.5% of owners hold 70% of the land — the lesson teaches that —
a) in 1953 hacendados controlled 92% of cultivable land and 4.5% of rural owners held 70% of it  b) the reform made colonos and pongos small proprietors and brought almost 2,000,000 Bolivians into the national economy  c) the minifundio decayed into surcofundio, driving migration and an urban informal sector, while the east built agroindustry
