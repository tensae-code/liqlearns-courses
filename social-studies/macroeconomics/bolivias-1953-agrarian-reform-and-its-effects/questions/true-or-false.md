# Bolivia's 1953 agrarian reform and its effects — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Land in 1953 was shared almost equally among rural families.
**Answer: False** — 4.5% of owners held 70% — the inequality the reform struck at.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The reform kept the colonos as pongos on the haciendas.
**Answer: False** — it turned them into small proprietors — that was its point.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The reform stopped all migration to the cities.
**Answer: False** — surcofundio plots drove migration and the informal sector grew.
