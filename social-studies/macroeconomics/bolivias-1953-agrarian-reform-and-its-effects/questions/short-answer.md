# Bolivia's 1953 agrarian reform and its effects — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the lesson teach about 1953 — 4.5% of owners hold 70% of the land?
**Answer:** In 1953 hacendados controlled 92% of cultivable land and 4.5% of rural owners held 70% of it.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the lesson teach about 'The land is for who works it'?
**Answer:** The reform made colonos and pongos small proprietors and brought almost 2,000,000 Bolivians into the national economy.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the lesson teach about minifundio, surcofundio, and the road to the cities?
**Answer:** The minifundio decayed into surcofundio, driving migration and an urban informal sector, while the east built agroindustry.
