# Public debt and revenue allocation in Nigeria — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Public debt is money the government is owed by everyone.
**Answer: False** — Public debt is what a government owes, borrowed at home and abroad to cover spending beyond its revenue.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In Nigeria all revenue goes to one level of government only.
**Answer: False** — In Nigeria's federation, revenue is shared between federal, state and local governments by an agreed formula.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Revenue allocation decides nothing about government spending.
**Answer: False** — Revenue allocation decides what each level of government can spend, and is one of the federation's most argued questions.
