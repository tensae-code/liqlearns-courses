# Public debt and revenue allocation in Nigeria — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is public debt?
**Answer:** Public debt is what a government owes, borrowed at home and abroad to cover spending beyond its revenue.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How is revenue allocated in Nigeria's federation?
**Answer:** In Nigeria's federation, revenue is shared between federal, state and local governments by an agreed formula.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Why does revenue allocation matter?
**Answer:** Revenue allocation decides what each level of government can spend, and is one of the federation's most argued questions.
