## Sub lesson 1 — Public and national debt

Public debt is what a government owes, borrowed at home and abroad to cover spending beyond its revenue.

## Sub lesson 2 — Revenue allocation in the federation

In Nigeria's federation, revenue is shared between federal, state and local governments by an agreed formula.

## Sub lesson 3 — Why allocation matters

Revenue allocation decides what each level of government can spend, and is one of the federation's most argued questions.
