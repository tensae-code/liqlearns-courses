Public debt and revenue allocation in Nigeria — as Nigeria's book teaches it, says the narrator: public debt is what a government owes, borrowed at home and abroad to cover spending beyond its revenue.; in Nigeria's federation, revenue is shared between federal, state and local governments by an agreed formula.; revenue allocation decides what each level of government can spend, and is one of the federation's most argued questions..
