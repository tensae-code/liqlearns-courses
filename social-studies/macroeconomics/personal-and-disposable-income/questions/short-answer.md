# Personal and disposable income — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Earned but not received — take it out" in your own words.
**Answer:** Personal income subtracts income earned but not received: undistributed profits, social insurance taxes and corporate profit taxes.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Received but not earned — add it in" in your own words.
**Answer:** Transfer payments are added to personal income because they are received though not earned.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Disposable income — the spendable amount" in your own words.
**Answer:** Disposable income is personal income minus personal taxes.
