# Personal and disposable income — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Personal income subtracts income earned but not received: undistributed profits, social insurance taxes and corporate profit taxes.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Transfer payments are added to personal income because they are received though not earned.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Disposable income is personal income minus personal taxes.
**Answer: True**
