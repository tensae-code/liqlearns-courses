# Personal and disposable income — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Personal income subtracts income that was earned but _____ — it never reached households.
**Answer: not received**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** _____ — received but not earned, like pensions — are added in to reach personal income.
**Answer: transfer payments**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Disposable income is personal income minus _____ — the spendable, take-home amount.
**Answer: personal taxes**
