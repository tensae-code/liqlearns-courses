# Personal and disposable income — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Earned but not received — take it out"?
a) personal income subtracts undistributed profits, social insurance taxes and corporate profit taxes  b) transfer payments received but not earned are added to reach personal income  c) disposable income = personal income minus personal taxes — available for consumption or saving
**Answer: a) personal income subtracts undistributed profits, social insurance taxes and corporate profit taxes**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Received but not earned — add it in"?
a) personal income subtracts undistributed profits, social insurance taxes and corporate profit taxes  b) disposable income = personal income minus personal taxes — available for consumption or saving  c) transfer payments received but not earned are added to reach personal income
**Answer: c) transfer payments received but not earned are added to reach personal income**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Disposable income — the spendable amount"?
a) transfer payments received but not earned are added to reach personal income  b) disposable income = personal income minus personal taxes — available for consumption or saving  c) personal income subtracts undistributed profits, social insurance taxes and corporate profit taxes
**Answer: b) disposable income = personal income minus personal taxes — available for consumption or saving**
