# Hands-on — Personal and disposable income

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Pass income counters down a line of learners: three 'earned but not received' toll-keepers take their share out, one 'transfers' giver adds counters in, and a final 'personal tax' keeper takes the last cut — what remains is disposable.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — a waterfall from national income down through the three deductions and one addition to personal income, then one more step to disposable income — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
