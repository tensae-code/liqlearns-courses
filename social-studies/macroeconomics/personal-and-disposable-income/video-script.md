# Video script — Personal and disposable income

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

National income counts income as it is earned, but people do not receive all of it. Undistributed corporate profits stay in the company, social insurance taxes are withheld, and corporate profit taxes go to the government. Personal income starts by subtracting these three — income earned but not received.

In the opposite direction, households receive transfer payments — pensions, benefits, gifts from the state — that were not earned in current production. These are added to personal income: received, though not earned. What remains after both adjustments is what people actually receive.

One step remains: disposable income is personal income minus personal taxes. This is the take-home amount households can actually dispose of — the income available for consumption or saving, and the income concept the consumption lessons of the next unit are built on.
