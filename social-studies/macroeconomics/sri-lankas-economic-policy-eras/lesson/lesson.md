# Sri Lanka's Economic Policy Eras

## Do it first

Before you read on, think of one case of this lesson's subject from your own life or town and say what you already know about it. Hold your answer — the lesson takes its subject in three parts, in order.

In Sri Lanka's book (Grade 13 Economics Teacher Guide 2018), **Level 12.1 Sri Lanka's Policy Eras** — teaches: Sri Lanka's economic policy in four eras — 1948-1960 (partly open economy, extensive social welfare, settlement schemes), 1961-1977 (nationalisation, exchange control and the dual exchange rate, import substitution begins), 1978-1994 (export-oriented policy, unified then floating exchange rate, controls removed, investment promotion zones, privatisation), 1994-2015 (open economy strengthened, war spending, infrastructure, foreign debt focus) — with import-substitution and export-promotion industrial policies detailed This lesson teaches that placement in the course's own words.
