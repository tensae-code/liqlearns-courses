# Lesson plan — Sri Lanka's Economic Policy Eras

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Macroeconomics · **Lesson:** Sri Lanka's Economic Policy Eras
**Place in the curriculum:** Macroeconomics (CURRICULUM.md) — built in the Economics expansion (Sri Lanka, 2026-11-16).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** sri Lanka's economic policy in four eras — 1948-1960 (partly open economy, extensive social welfare, settlement schemes), 1961-1977 (nationalisation, exchange control and the dual exchange rate, import substitution begins), 1978-1994 (export-oriented policy, unified then floating exchange rate, controls removed, investment promotion zones, privatisation), 1994-2015 (open economy strengthened, war spending, infrastructure, foreign debt focus) — with import-substitution and export-promotion industrial policies detailed.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the Sri Lanka book's own treatment — Level 12.1 Sri Lanka's Policy Eras (Grade 13 Economics Teacher Guide 2018, printed p. 195 (TEACHER)).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of ..." in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.
