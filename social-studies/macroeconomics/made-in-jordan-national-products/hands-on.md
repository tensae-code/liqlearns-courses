# Hands-on — Made in Jordan — national products

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Hunt the label at home
Find five products at home and read each label: where was it made, and what is its production date? Mark each one J (made in Jordan) or F (made abroad). For every J, write which Jordanian industry made it, using the lesson's list.

## Draw it and check it
Draw Jordan's industry map from the lesson — food, clothing, medicines, Dead Sea products, fertilisers, phosphate, furniture, electrical goods — as eight boxes with one product picture each. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare, and fix every box you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 2 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
