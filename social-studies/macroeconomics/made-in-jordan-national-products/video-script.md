# Video script — Made in Jordan — national products

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Turn a juice pack around and read it: Made in Jordan — and the production date beside it. Two of Jordan's school books open the country's industries from that one label.

Jordan is known for food products, for textiles and clothing, for medicines and for Dead Sea products. The older children's book widens the list: chemical industries making fertilisers, mining industries digging phosphate — Jordan is among the world's biggest phosphate producers — medicines, food, wood and furniture, and engineering and electrical industries.

And factories matter for two plain reasons: they provide the goods and services people need, and they create job opportunities.
