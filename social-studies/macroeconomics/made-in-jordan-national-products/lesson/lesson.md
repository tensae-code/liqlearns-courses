# Made in Jordan — national products

## Do it first

Pick up any pack or bottle near you and turn it around. Before you read on, find two things on it: the words that say where it was made, and its production date. Say what country they name.

## Sub lesson 1 — Reading 'Made in Jordan'

Jordan's Grade 2 book opens its closing lesson with exactly that task: reading **"Made in Jordan"** and the **production date** on a juice pack. The label is a small geography and economics lesson by itself — it tells you the product is a **national product**, made in your own country, and the date tells you when its journey through the factory finished.

From that one pack, the book tours the industries Jordan is known for, following the **stages a Jordanian product passes through** from its materials to the shelf.

## Sub lesson 2 — The industries Jordan is known for

The Grade 2 tour names four: **food products**, **textiles and clothing**, **medicines**, and **Dead Sea products** — the salts and creams of the world's lowest shore, sold under Jordan's name everywhere.

The Grade 3 book widens the same tour: **chemical industries** (fertilisers), **mining industries** (phosphate — Jordan is among the world's biggest producers), **medicines**, **food**, **wood and furniture**, and **engineering and electrical industries**. Two books, one list growing with the child: what Jordan makes is what Jordan is known for.

## Sub lesson 3 — Why factories and national products matter

The Grade 3 book gives the reason plainly: **factories provide needed goods and services** — the food, medicine, clothing and goods people cannot do without — **and create job opportunities** for the people who work in them.

So a national product is never only a thing on a shelf. Behind the "Made in Jordan" label stands a chain: materials, a factory, workers with jobs, and a family that can buy what it needs made at home.

## Say it to your companion

Explain Made in Jordan to your companion in your own words: what the label and the production date tell you, three industries Jordan is known for, and the two reasons factories matter. Your companion asks you one hard question about it — answer from the lesson.
