Jordan's own way (Jordan Grade 2 Term 2): a whole early-years Economics unit built on a family shopping trip — a real receipt in Jordanian dinars, Jordanian coins and banknotes to order by value, the wheat-to-bread chain for producer and consumer, a child's own money box for saving, and a closing lesson that tours Jordan's own industries. This lesson is that closing lesson: 'Made in Jordan' read off a juice pack.

Jordan's own way (Jordan Grade 3 Term 2): trade is taught as a Jordanian family story that starts at the hundred-year-old Hejaz railway, runs from barter and camel peddlers to e-commerce, and lands on the child's own choices — what a family can and cannot do without, where income comes from, and what 2, 3, 5 or 12 dinars in hand can buy. Its industries lesson widens the Grade 2 tour and says why factories matter. The lesson teaches the placements' content in the course's own words — the books' text itself is never transcribed.
