# Made in Jordan — national products — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The two things the Grade 2 book reads on a juice pack are —
a) the price and the shop's name  b) 'Made in Jordan' and the production date  c) the farmer's name and address
**Answer: b) 'Made in Jordan' and the production date**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which of these is Jordan among the world's biggest producers of?
a) phosphate  b) rice  c) coffee
**Answer: a) phosphate**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Factories matter because they —
a) only make exports  b) close other workplaces  c) provide needed goods and services and create job opportunities
**Answer: c) provide needed goods and services and create job opportunities**
