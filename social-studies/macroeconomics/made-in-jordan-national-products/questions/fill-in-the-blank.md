# Made in Jordan — national products — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The words _____ _____ _____ on a pack tell you the product was made in your own country.
**Answer: Made in Jordan**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Jordan's chemical industries make _____.
**Answer: fertilisers**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Factories create job _____ for the people who work in them.
**Answer: opportunities**
