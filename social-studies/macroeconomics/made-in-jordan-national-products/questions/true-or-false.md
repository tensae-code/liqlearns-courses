# Made in Jordan — national products — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Dead Sea products are one of the industries Jordan is known for.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The Grade 3 book's industry list is shorter than the Grade 2 book's list.
**Answer: False — the Grade 3 list is wider: it adds chemical industries (fertilisers), mining industries (phosphate), wood and furniture, and engineering and electrical industries.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A national product is a product made in your own country.
**Answer: True**
