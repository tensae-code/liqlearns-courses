# Made in Jordan — national products — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name four industries Jordan is known for, from the Grade 2 tour.
**Answer:** Food products, textiles and clothing, medicines, and Dead Sea products.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name three more industries from the Grade 3 tour.
**Answer:** Any three of: chemical industries (fertilisers), mining industries (phosphate), wood and furniture, engineering and electrical industries (medicines and food are in both tours).

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What two reasons does the Grade 3 book give for why factories matter?
**Answer:** Factories provide the goods and services people need, and they create job opportunities.
