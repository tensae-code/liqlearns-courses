# Rural exodus to the oilfields — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Rural population in the book works mainly in industry.
**Answer: False** — rural population works in agricultural activities; industry is urban work.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Venezuela is still about 90% rural today.
**Answer: False** — it flipped — today about 90% are urban.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The book says the peasants who left for the oilfields always improved their living conditions.
**Answer: False** — it says their living conditions did not necessarily improve.
