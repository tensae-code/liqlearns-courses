# Rural exodus to the oilfields — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In the book, a rural place has under —
a) 2,500 inhabitants  b) 25,000 inhabitants  c) 250 inhabitants
**Answer: a) 2,500 inhabitants**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Venezuela went from about 90% rural in 1830 to about —
a) 90% urban today  b) 50% urban today  c) 10% urban today
**Answer: a) 90% urban today**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The rural exodus began when foreign oil companies in Zulia needed workers, from —
a) 1914  b) 1830  c) 2014
**Answer: a) 1914**
