# Rural exodus to the oilfields — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How does the book divide rural from urban population?
**Answer:** Rural: places under 2,500 inhabitants, working in agriculture. Urban: working in industry, commerce and services.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** State Venezuela's rural–urban flip in the book's two numbers.
**Answer:** About 90% rural in 1830; about 90% urban today.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What caused the rural exodus, and what warning does the book add?
**Answer:** From 1914 foreign oil companies in Zulia needed workers and poor peasants left the countryside seeking jobs — without their living conditions necessarily improving.
