# Rural exodus to the oilfields — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Urban population works in industry, commerce and _____.
**Answer: services**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In 1830 about _____ percent of Venezuelans were rural.
**Answer: 90**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The movement of poor peasants to the oilfields is called the rural _____ (éxodo campesino).
**Answer: exodus**
