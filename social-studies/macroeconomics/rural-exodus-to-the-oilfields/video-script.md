# Video script — Rural exodus to the oilfields

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Rural: places under 2,500 inhabitants, working in agriculture. Urban: working in industry, commerce and services.

About 90% rural in 1830; about 90% urban today.

From 1914 foreign oil companies in Zulia needed workers and poor peasants left the countryside seeking jobs — without their living conditions necessarily improving.
