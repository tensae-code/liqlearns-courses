# The Greek economy — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Where does the Greek economy sit in the book?
**Answer:** As the closing section of the final chapter, after international trade, the balance of payments and the European Union.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which questions does the lesson put to a national economy?
**Answer:** What it produces (sectors), whom it trades with and in what, and how it sits inside its integration bloc.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Why does the course end at home?
**Answer:** So students can analyse the economy they live in — the home economy is the final application of the whole course.
