# The Greek economy — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Grade 12 book closes by applying the course to —
a) an imaginary island
b) Greece's own economy
c) ancient Athens
**Answer: b) Greece's own economy**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Reading a national economy asks about its sectors, trade and —
a) its ancient history
b) its place in its integration bloc
c) its weather
**Answer: b) its place in its integration bloc**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The home-economy section works as —
a) an appendix to skip
b) the course's final application of itself
c) a history chapter
**Answer: b) the course's final application of itself**
