# Hands-on — Globalisation — trade and interdependence

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Map the web
Draw Fiji at the centre and draw the lesson's flows reaching it: trade, capital, migration and knowledge, carried by transport and telecommunications. Then mark where the regional forum and donor aid stand in the picture.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's picture from memory — the Pacific islands linked by trade ships, planes, cables and money flows to the wider world, with Fiji at the centre of the web — and label its parts with the lesson's key words. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare, and fix every label you placed wrong.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
