# Globalisation — trade and interdependence — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the four flows that integrate the world economy in the Year 8 book.
**Answer:** Trade and transactions, capital and investment movements, migration, and the spread of knowledge.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why do small Pacific economies feel globalisation sharply?
**Answer:** They are small and vulnerable — tied into world trade and investment with little cushion against the world's changes.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** How does the Pacific answer globalisation?
**Answer:** Together, through regional forum responses, and with donor aid for poverty reduction and sustainable development, including Australia's aid.
