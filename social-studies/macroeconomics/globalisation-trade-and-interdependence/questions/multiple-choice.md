# Globalisation — trade and interdependence — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Globalisation, in the lesson, is —
a) one island trading only with itself  b) international integration through trade, capital, migration and knowledge  c) a tax on imports
**Answer: b) international integration through trade, capital, migration and knowledge**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Pacific economies in the face of globalisation are described as —
a) large and sheltered  b) small and vulnerable  c) closed to trade
**Answer: b) small and vulnerable**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Donor aid to the Pacific in the lesson is aimed at —
a) poverty reduction and sustainable development  b) building armies  c) buying luxury goods
**Answer: a) poverty reduction and sustainable development**
