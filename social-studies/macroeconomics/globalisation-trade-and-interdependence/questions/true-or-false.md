# Globalisation — trade and interdependence — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Transport and telecommunications drive globalisation.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The Year 9 book names banking, multinational companies and tourism among globalisation's agents.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Each Pacific island answers globalisation entirely on its own, with no regional forum.
**Answer: False — the book teaches regional forum responses, the islands acting together.**
