# Video script — GNP — what a country's own people produce, anywhere

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Gross National Product is the total value produced by domestically owned factors of production in a period, wherever in the world they are located. GDP asks where; GNP asks whose.

GNP = GDP + NFI, where net factor income = factor income received from abroad by the country's citizens minus factor income paid to foreigners. NFI is the net of the two streams crossing the border.

If NFI is positive, GNP exceeds GDP; if negative, GNP falls short; and if zero — as in a closed economy with no dealings abroad — the two are equal. The sign of one number tells the whole story.
