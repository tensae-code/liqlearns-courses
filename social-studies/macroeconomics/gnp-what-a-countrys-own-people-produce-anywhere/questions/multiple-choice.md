# GNP — what a country's own people produce, anywhere — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "From place to people"?
a) production by domestically owned factors, wherever located  b) GNP = GDP + NFI; NFI = income received from abroad minus income paid to foreigners  c) NFI positive — GNP above GDP; negative — below; zero — equal
**Answer: a) production by domestically owned factors, wherever located**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "The bridge — net factor income"?
a) production by domestically owned factors, wherever located  b) NFI positive — GNP above GDP; negative — below; zero — equal  c) GNP = GDP + NFI; NFI = income received from abroad minus income paid to foreigners
**Answer: c) GNP = GDP + NFI; NFI = income received from abroad minus income paid to foreigners**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "When GNP passes or trails GDP"?
a) GNP = GDP + NFI; NFI = income received from abroad minus income paid to foreigners  b) NFI positive — GNP above GDP; negative — below; zero — equal  c) production by domestically owned factors, wherever located
**Answer: b) NFI positive — GNP above GDP; negative — below; zero — equal**
