# GNP — what a country's own people produce, anywhere — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** GNP counts production by _____ factors, wherever they are located.
**Answer: domestically owned**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Net factor income is income received from abroad _____ income paid to foreigners.
**Answer: minus**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In a closed economy NFI is _____, and GNP and GDP are equal.
**Answer: zero**
