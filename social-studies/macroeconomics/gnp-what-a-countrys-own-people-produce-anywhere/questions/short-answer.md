# GNP — what a country's own people produce, anywhere — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "From place to people" in your own words.
**Answer:** GNP is the total value produced by domestically owned factors of production, wherever in the world they are located.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "The bridge — net factor income" in your own words.
**Answer:** GNP equals GDP plus net factor income, which is factor income received from abroad minus factor income paid to foreigners.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "When GNP passes or trails GDP" in your own words.
**Answer:** If net factor income is positive GNP exceeds GDP, if negative it falls short, and if zero the two are equal.
