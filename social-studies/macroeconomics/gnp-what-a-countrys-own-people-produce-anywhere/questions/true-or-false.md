# GNP — what a country's own people produce, anywhere — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** GNP is the total value produced by domestically owned factors of production, wherever in the world they are located.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** GNP is the total value produced by domestically owned factors of production, wherever in the world they are located.
**Answer: False** — that statement belongs to sub lesson 1 (From place to people); sub lesson 2 says: GNP equals GDP plus net factor income, which is factor income received from abroad minus factor income paid to foreigners.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** If net factor income is positive GNP exceeds GDP, if negative it falls short, and if zero the two are equal.
**Answer: True**
