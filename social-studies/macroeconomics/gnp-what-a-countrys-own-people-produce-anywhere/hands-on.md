# Hands-on — GNP — what a country's own people produce, anywhere

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Two baskets: 'inside our borders' and 'by our people'. Deal earner cards (citizen abroad, foreign firm inside, citizen inside) into both baskets and read GNP off the second.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — the country outline plus its citizens working abroad, minus foreigners earning inside, as a three-term sum — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
