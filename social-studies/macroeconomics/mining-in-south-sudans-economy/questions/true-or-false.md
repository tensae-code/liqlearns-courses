# Mining in South Sudan's economy — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Oil is the chief mineral export and earns foreign exchange — gold is exported to Japan and India, and the exchange buys machinery and other goods. The teacher's guide adds that mining is the second economic activity in South Sudan and the main source of income to the country, and oil is the main mineral mined.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Mining employs people directly and indirectly and raises living standards. Minerals give raw materials to manufacturing — limestone to cement and lime factories.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Mining money builds roads, railways, schools and hospitals, and mining grows towns such as Juba, Faloj and Kapoeta. The teacher's guide adds that almost all development in South Sudan has been contributed by mining.
**Answer: True**
