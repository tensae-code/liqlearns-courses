# Video script — Mining in South Sudan's economy

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Mining in South Sudan's economy — as South Sudan's book teaches it, says the narrator: Oil is South Sudan's chief mineral export and earns foreign exchange (gold is exported to Japan and India; the exchange buys machinery and other goods). Mining employs people directly and indirectly and raises living standards. Minerals give raw materials to manufacturing (limestone to cement and lime factories). Mining money builds roads, railways, schools and hospitals, and mining grows towns such as Juba, Faloj and Kapoeta. The teacher's guide adds: mining is the second economic activity in South Sudan and the main source of income to the country, oil is the main mineral mined, and almost all development in South Sudan has been contributed by mining.
