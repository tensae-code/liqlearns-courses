# Mining in South Sudan's economy

## Sub lesson 1 — Oil, exports and foreign exchange

**Oil is the chief mineral export** and **earns foreign exchange** — gold is exported to Japan and India, and the exchange buys machinery and other goods. The teacher's guide adds that **mining is the second economic activity** in South Sudan and the **main source of income** to the country, and **oil is the main mineral mined**.

## Sub lesson 2 — Jobs, living standards and raw materials

Mining **employs people directly and indirectly** and **raises living standards**. Minerals give **raw materials to manufacturing** — limestone to cement and lime factories.

## Sub lesson 3 — Mining builds towns and services

**Mining money builds roads, railways, schools and hospitals**, and mining **grows towns such as Juba, Faloj and Kapoeta**. The teacher's guide adds that **almost all development in South Sudan has been contributed by mining**.
