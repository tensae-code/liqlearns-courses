# Trend and structure of Nigeria's external trade — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Nigeria's external trade has swung with the price of _____.
**Answer: oil**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Nigeria's exports are dominated by _____.
**Answer: petroleum**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Trade resting on one export leaves the economy _____ when its price falls.
**Answer: exposed**
