# Trend and structure of Nigeria's external trade — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What has Nigeria's external trade moved with?
**Answer:** Nigeria's external trade has grown and swung with the price of oil over the years.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What dominates Nigeria's exports?
**Answer:** Nigeria's exports are dominated by petroleum, while its imports are manufactures and food.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does a one-export trade structure mean?
**Answer:** A trade structure resting on one export leaves the economy exposed when that export's price falls.
