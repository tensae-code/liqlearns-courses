# Trend and structure of Nigeria's external trade — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Nigeria's external trade never changes.
**Answer: False** — Nigeria's external trade has grown and swung with the price of oil over the years.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Nigeria's exports contain no petroleum.
**Answer: False** — Nigeria's exports are dominated by petroleum, while its imports are manufactures and food.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Resting trade on one export makes an economy safest.
**Answer: False** — A trade structure resting on one export leaves the economy exposed when that export's price falls.
