## Sub lesson 1 — The trend of Nigeria's external trade

Nigeria's external trade has grown and swung with the price of oil over the years.

## Sub lesson 2 — The structure of Nigeria's external trade

Nigeria's exports are dominated by petroleum, while its imports are manufactures and food.

## Sub lesson 3 — What the structure means

A trade structure resting on one export leaves the economy exposed when that export's price falls.
