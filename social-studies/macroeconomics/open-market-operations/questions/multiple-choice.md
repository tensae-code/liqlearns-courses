# Open market operations — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Buying expands, selling drains"?
a) Buying bonds expands the monetary base and money supply; selling drains both  b) The NBE operates through the treasury-bill market, holding an allocation of government T-bills  c) Open market operations are the flexible daily tool for fine-tuning liquidity
**Answer: a) Buying bonds expands the monetary base and money supply; selling drains both**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "The NBE's treasury-bill market"?
a) The NBE operates through the treasury-bill market, holding an allocation of government T-bills  b) Open market operations are the flexible daily tool for fine-tuning liquidity  c) Buying bonds expands the monetary base and money supply; selling drains both
**Answer: a) The NBE operates through the treasury-bill market, holding an allocation of government T-bills**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Why this tool leads"?
a) Open market operations are the flexible daily tool for fine-tuning liquidity  b) Buying bonds expands the monetary base and money supply; selling drains both  c) The NBE operates through the treasury-bill market, holding an allocation of government T-bills
**Answer: a) Open market operations are the flexible daily tool for fine-tuning liquidity**
