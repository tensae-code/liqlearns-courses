# Open market operations — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Buying expands, selling drains" in your own words.
**Answer:** Buying bonds expands the monetary base and money supply; selling drains both.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "The NBE's treasury-bill market" in your own words.
**Answer:** The NBE operates through the treasury-bill market, holding an allocation of government T-bills.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Why this tool leads" in your own words.
**Answer:** Open market operations are the flexible daily tool for fine-tuning liquidity.
