# Open market operations — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Buying bonds expands the monetary base and money supply; selling drains both.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The NBE operates through the treasury-bill market, holding an allocation of government T-bills.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Open market operations are the flexible daily tool for fine-tuning liquidity.
**Answer: True**
