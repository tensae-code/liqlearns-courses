# Hands-on — Open market operations

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Follow the birr
Draw the two flows across the counter — bonds one way, birr the other — for a purchase and for a sale. Under each, write what happens to the money supply.

## Steer a week
Invent a five-day liquidity diary for a banking system (short on Monday, flooded on Wednesday). Write the NBE's buy or sell for each day and the size direction.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
