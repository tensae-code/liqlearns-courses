# Hands-on — Tax equity — the benefits approach

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Price the bundle
List the public services one household receives in a week. Mark each 'priceable' or 'unpriceable' under the benefits approach, and total only the priceable half — then state the approach's problem in one sentence.

## Defend and attack
Write two lines defending benefits taxation and two lines attacking it from measurement. End by naming which side the unpriceable shield (defence) supports.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
