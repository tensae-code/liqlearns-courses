# Tax equity — the benefits approach — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Pay for what you receive"?
a) The benefits approach taxes each person in line with the benefit received from public services  b) Roughly half of public spending, like defence and courts, cannot be assigned to beneficiaries
**Answer: a) The benefits approach taxes each person in line with the benefit received from public services**

a) Roughly half of public spending, like defence and courts, cannot be assigned to beneficiaries  b) The benefits approach taxes each person in line with the benefit received from public services
