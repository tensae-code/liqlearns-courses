# Tax equity — the benefits approach — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Pay for what you receive" in your own words.
**Answer:** The benefits approach taxes each person in line with the benefit received from public services.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "The measurement weakness" in your own words.
**Answer:** Roughly half of public spending, like defence and courts, cannot be assigned to beneficiaries.
