# The limits of averages — income distribution — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "An average is silent about the split" in your own words.
**Answer:** GDP and per capita GDP cannot show how income is distributed among the population.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Same average, different countries" in your own words.
**Answer:** Two economies with the same per capita GDP can distribute income very differently.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Why other measures now stand beside GDP" in your own words.
**Answer:** Inequality measures and poverty analysis now accompany GDP in policy analysis.
