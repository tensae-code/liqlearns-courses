# The limits of averages — income distribution — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "An average is silent about the split"?
a) averages report one number and cannot show who earns what  b) identical averages can hide opposite distributions of income  c) inequality and poverty measures now accompany GDP because averages are not enough
**Answer: a) averages report one number and cannot show who earns what**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Same average, different countries"?
a) averages report one number and cannot show who earns what  b) inequality and poverty measures now accompany GDP because averages are not enough  c) identical averages can hide opposite distributions of income
**Answer: c) identical averages can hide opposite distributions of income**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Why other measures now stand beside GDP"?
a) identical averages can hide opposite distributions of income  b) inequality and poverty measures now accompany GDP because averages are not enough  c) averages report one number and cannot show who earns what
**Answer: b) inequality and poverty measures now accompany GDP because averages are not enough**
