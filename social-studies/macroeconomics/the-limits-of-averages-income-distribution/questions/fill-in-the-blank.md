# The limits of averages — income distribution — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Averages cannot show the _____ of income — who earns what.
**Answer: distribution**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Two economies with identical averages can distribute income very _____.
**Answer: differently**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Because averages hide the split, _____ measures and poverty analysis now accompany GDP.
**Answer: inequality**
