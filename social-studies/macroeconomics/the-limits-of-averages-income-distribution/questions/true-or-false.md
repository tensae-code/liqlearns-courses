# The limits of averages — income distribution — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** GDP and per capita GDP cannot show how income is distributed among the population.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Two economies with the same per capita GDP can distribute income very differently.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Inequality measures and poverty analysis now accompany GDP in policy analysis.
**Answer: True**
