# Hands-on — The limits of averages — income distribution

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Share counters two ways among ten learners: equally, then almost all to two learners with crumbs for the rest. Both shares average the same — ask the class which distribution they would join, and why the average cannot answer.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — two countries with identical per-capita bars, one with equal slices, one with a few large and many tiny slices — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
