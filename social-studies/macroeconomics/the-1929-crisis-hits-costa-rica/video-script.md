# Video script — The 1929 crisis hits Costa Rica

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The US stopped buying coffee and banana.

Sales to the US fell from a US$24 million average in 1929 to under US$1 million in 1932.

With the book's full consequences table (exports and tax income down, colón and wages down, rents and import prices up, hunger) and the responses: higher indirect taxes, the Instituto de Defensa del Café, minimum wages and public-works jobs "just like the New Deal".
