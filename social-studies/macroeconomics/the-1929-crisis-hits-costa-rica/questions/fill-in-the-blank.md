# The 1929 crisis hits Costa Rica — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The US _____ buying coffee and banana.
**Answer: stopped**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Sales to the US fell from a US$24 million _____ in 1929 to under US$1 million in 1932.
**Answer: average**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** With the book's full consequences table (exports and tax income down, colón and wages down, rents and import prices up, hunger) and the responses: higher indirect taxes, the _____ de Defensa del Café, minimum wages and public-works jobs…
**Answer: Instituto**
