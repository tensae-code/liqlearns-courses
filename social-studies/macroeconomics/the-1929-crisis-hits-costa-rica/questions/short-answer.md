# The 1929 crisis hits Costa Rica — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the lesson teach about la crisis de 1929 en costa rica?
**Answer:** The US stopped buying coffee and banana.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the lesson teach about la crisis de 1929 en costa rica — more?
**Answer:** Sales to the US fell from a US$24 million average in 1929 to under US$1 million in 1932.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the lesson teach about la crisis de 1929 en costa rica — more — more?
**Answer:** With the book's full consequences table (exports and tax income down, colón and wages down, rents and import prices up, hunger) and the responses: higher indirect taxes, the Instituto de Defensa del Café, minimum wages and public-works jobs "just like the New Deal".
