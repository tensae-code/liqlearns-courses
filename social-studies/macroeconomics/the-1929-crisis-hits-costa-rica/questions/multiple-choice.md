# The 1929 crisis hits Costa Rica — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** About la crisis de 1929 en costa rica — the lesson teaches that —
a) The US stopped buying coffee and banana.
b) Sales to the US fell from a US$24 million average in 1929 to under US$1 million in 1932.
c) With the book's full consequences table (exports and tax income down, colón and wages down, rents and import prices up, hunger) and the responses:…
**Answer: a) The US stopped buying coffee and banana.**
