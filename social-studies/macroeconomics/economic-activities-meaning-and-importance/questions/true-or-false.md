# Economic activities — meaning and importance — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Economic activities are the means and sources of income, introduced through a Chepang beekeeper of Makwanpur who began with Rs. 1,000 and now sells 15–20 tons of honey a year, and Magar homestays whose community fund keeps 10% of income.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Their fields were traditionally farming, industry and jobs, and now also include services, tourism and foreign employment.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Economic activities matter because employment and income rise, daily needs are met, living standards improve, local resources are mobilised and something else entirely.
**Answer: False** — Economic activities matter because employment and income rise, daily needs are met, living standards improve, local resources are mobilised and national income grows.
