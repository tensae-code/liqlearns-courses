# Economic activities — meaning and importance — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the book teach about what economic activities are?
**Answer:** Economic activities are the means and sources of income, introduced through a Chepang beekeeper of Makwanpur who began with Rs. 1,000 and now sells 15–20 tons of honey a year, and Magar homestays whose community fund keeps 10% of income.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the book teach about the fields of economic activities?
**Answer:** Their fields were traditionally farming, industry and jobs, and now also include services, tourism and foreign employment.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the book teach about why economic activities matter?
**Answer:** Economic activities matter because employment and income rise, daily needs are met, living standards improve, local resources are mobilised and national income grows.
