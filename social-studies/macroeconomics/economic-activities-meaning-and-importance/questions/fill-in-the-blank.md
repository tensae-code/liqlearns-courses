# Economic activities — meaning and importance — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Economic activities are the _____, introduced through a Chepang beekeeper of Makwanpur who began with Rs. 1,000 and now sells 15–20 tons of honey a year, and Magar homestays whose community fund keeps 10% of income.
**Answer: means and sources of income**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Their fields were traditionally farming, industry and jobs, and now also include _____.
**Answer: services, tourism and foreign employment**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Economic activities matter because employment and income rise, daily needs are met, living standards improve, local resources are mobilised and _____.
**Answer: national income grows**
