## Sub lesson 1 — What economic activities are

Economic activities are the means and sources of income, introduced through a Chepang beekeeper of Makwanpur who began with Rs. 1,000 and now sells 15–20 tons of honey a year, and Magar homestays whose community fund keeps 10% of income.

## Sub lesson 2 — The fields of economic activities

Their fields were traditionally farming, industry and jobs, and now also include services, tourism and foreign employment.

## Sub lesson 3 — Why economic activities matter

Economic activities matter because employment and income rise, daily needs are met, living standards improve, local resources are mobilised and national income grows.
