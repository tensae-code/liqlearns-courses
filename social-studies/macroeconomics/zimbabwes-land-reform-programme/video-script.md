# Video script — Zimbabwe's land reform programme

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Zimbabwe's land reform redistributed land from white settlers to indigenous Zimbabweans — first slowly, by willing buyer–willing seller, then fast: the fast-track reform of 2000.

It came because of land shortage, unequal distribution, the aim of empowerment, and funding promises that were not met.

And it was meant to bring economic empowerment, ownership of the means of production, more food production, jobs, and less poverty.
