# Hands-on — Zimbabwe's land reform programme

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Build the timeline
Make a two-step timeline on paper — willing buyer–willing seller, then fast-track 2000 — and under each step write what changed. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare, and fix anything you missed.

## Draw it and check it
Draw two maps of the same farmland — before reform and after reform — showing who holds the land in each. Label each map with one cause and one benefit from the lesson. Then check your labels against lesson/lesson.md.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
