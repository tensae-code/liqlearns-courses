# Zimbabwe's land reform programme — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Before fast track, land was redistributed by willing buyer–willing seller.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Unequal distribution of land was one of the causes of the reform.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The unit lists increased food production among the causes of land reform.
**Answer: False — increased food production is one of its economic benefits; the causes are land shortage, unequal distribution, empowerment and unmet funding promises.**
