# Zimbabwe's land reform programme — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Zimbabwe's land reform was the redistribution of land —
a) from the state to foreign companies  b) from white settlers to indigenous Zimbabweans  c) from towns to cities
**Answer: b) from white settlers to indigenous Zimbabweans**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The fast-track reform came in —
a) 1980  b) 1990  c) 2000
**Answer: c) 2000**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which of these is an economic benefit of land reform in the unit?
a) unmet funding promises  b) land shortage  c) owning the means of production
**Answer: c) owning the means of production**
