# Zimbabwe's land reform programme — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What was Zimbabwe's land reform programme?
**Answer:** The redistribution of land from white settlers to indigenous Zimbabweans — first by willing buyer–willing seller, then through the fast-track reform of 2000.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the causes of the reform the unit lists.
**Answer:** Land shortage, unequal distribution of land, empowerment, and unmet funding promises.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name the economic benefits the unit lists.
**Answer:** Economic empowerment, owning the means of production, increased food production, job creation, and poverty reduction.
