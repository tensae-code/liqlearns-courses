# Education and health sectors of Pakistan — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How does the book treat education?
**Answer:** As an economic sector — counted, with its share of the economy measured and its shortfalls named.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How does the book treat health?
**Answer:** As an economic sector — employing, spending and serving like any other part of the economy.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What do the book's tables show for education and health?
**Answer:** Their shares of the economy and their shortfalls against Pakistan's needs.
