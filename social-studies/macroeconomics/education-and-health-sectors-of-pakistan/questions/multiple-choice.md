# Education and health sectors of Pakistan — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** About education read as an economic sector — the lesson teaches that —

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** About their shares and shortfalls tabled — the lesson teaches that —
