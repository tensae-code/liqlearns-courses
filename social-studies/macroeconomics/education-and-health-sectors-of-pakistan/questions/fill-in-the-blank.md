# Education and health sectors of Pakistan — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Pakistan's schools and teachers are counted in the _____ sector.
**Answer: education**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Hospitals and health spending form the _____ sector.
**Answer: health**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The sectors' gaps against Pakistan's needs are their _____.
**Answer: shortfalls**
