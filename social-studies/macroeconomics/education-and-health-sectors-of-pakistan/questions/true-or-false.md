# Education and health sectors of Pakistan — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The book treats education as outside the economy entirely.
**Answer: False** — it reads it as a sector, share and all.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Health spending is not economic activity in the book's account.
**Answer: False** — the book reads health as a sector, like education.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The book hides Pakistan's education and health shortfalls.
**Answer: False** — it tables them openly from the country's data.
