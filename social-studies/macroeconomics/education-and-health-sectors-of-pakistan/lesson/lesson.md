# Education and health sectors of Pakistan

## Do it first

Before you read on, write what you already think this lesson's title means — education and health sectors of pakistan — in one sentence of your own. Keep it; you will check it against the lesson at the end.

## Sub lesson 1 — Education read as an economic sector

The Class 12 book reads Pakistan's **education sector** as an economic sector: schools, teachers and students counted like any other part of the economy, with its share measured and its shortfalls named.

## Sub lesson 2 — Health read as an economic sector

The **health sector** gets the same treatment: hospitals, doctors and health spending read as economic activity — a sector that both serves welfare and employs and spends like any other.

## Sub lesson 3 — Their shares and shortfalls tabled

Both sectors are **tabled**: their shares set out in numbers, and their **shortfalls** — where Pakistan's education and health fall behind its needs — shown in the same tables. The learner reads the country's gaps from its own data.
