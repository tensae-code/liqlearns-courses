# Indonesia's employment problems

## Do it first

Think of this topic the way the Indonesia book opens it — Indonesia's employment problems. What do you already know about it before you read on?

## Sub lesson 1 — The problems named

Indonesia's employment problems include a workforce whose quality and skills lag behind the jobs on offer.

## Sub lesson 2 — Low workforce quality

Low workforce quality is named as a central problem: many workers have limited education and training for the work available.

## Sub lesson 3 — The migrant-worker (PMI) case

The book uses the case of Indonesian migrant workers (PMI) — Indonesians working abroad — as part of its picture of the employment problem.
