# Indonesia's employment problems — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Indonesia's employment problems include a workforce whose quality and _____ lag behind the jobs on offer.
**Answer: skills**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Low workforce quality is named as a central problem: many workers have limited _____ for the work available.
**Answer: education and training**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The book uses the case of Indonesian _____ — Indonesians working abroad — as part of its picture of the employment problem.
**Answer: migrant workers (PMI)**
