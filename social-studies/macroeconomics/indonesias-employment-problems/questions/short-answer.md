# Indonesia's employment problems — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the book teach about the problems named?
**Answer:** Indonesia's employment problems include a workforce whose quality and skills lag behind the jobs on offer.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the book teach about low workforce quality?
**Answer:** Low workforce quality is named as a central problem: many workers have limited education and training for the work available.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the book teach about the migrant-worker (pmi) case?
**Answer:** The book uses the case of Indonesian migrant workers (PMI) — Indonesians working abroad — as part of its picture of the employment problem.
