# Indonesia's employment problems — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Indonesia's employment problems include a workforce whose quality and skills lag behind the jobs on offer.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Low workforce quality is named as a central problem: many workers have limited education and training for the work available.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The book uses the case of Indonesian something else entirely — Indonesians working abroad — as part of its picture of the employment problem.
**Answer: False** — The book uses the case of Indonesian migrant workers (PMI) — Indonesians working abroad — as part of its picture of the employment problem.
