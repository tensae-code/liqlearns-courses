# Gulf energy resources and national income

## Do it first

Before you read on, write what you already think this lesson's title means — gulf energy resources and national income — in one sentence of your own. Keep it; you will check it against the lesson at the end.

## Sub lesson 1 — The concept of national income

Kuwait's Grade 6 Term 2 book teaches energy resources — oil and natural gas — through **the concept of national income**: what a country earns, with oil and gas standing at the centre of the Gulf states' income.

## Sub lesson 2 — Gulf oil and gas — production, reserves and the biggest fields

The lesson sets out **Gulf oil and gas production and reserve shares of world totals**, read from the book's **2023 charts**, and names **the biggest fields** of the region.

## Sub lesson 3 — The problem of relying on oil

The book names the problem from the start: **relying on oil as the basic income source**. The Gulf's energy wealth is great, and the lesson teaches that depending on it as the basic source is itself a problem.
