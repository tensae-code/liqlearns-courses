# Factors that drive development — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the book teach about economic factors?
**Answer:** The economic factors that drive development include capital, natural resources, labour and technology.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the book teach about non-economic factors?
**Answer:** The non-economic factors include education, health, institutions and stability — the conditions around the economy.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the book teach about working together?
**Answer:** Development moves when economic and non-economic factors work together; a shortage in either holds the whole back.
