# Factors that drive development — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The economic factors that drive development include capital, natural resources, labour and technology.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The non-economic factors include education, health, institutions and stability — the conditions around the economy.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Development moves when economic and non-economic factors something else entirely; a shortage in either holds the whole back.
**Answer: False** — Development moves when economic and non-economic factors work together; a shortage in either holds the whole back.
