# Factors that drive development — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The economic factors that drive development include capital, natural resources, labour and _____.
**Answer: technology**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The non-economic factors include education, health, _____ and stability — the conditions around the economy.
**Answer: institutions**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Development moves when economic and non-economic factors _____; a shortage in either holds the whole back.
**Answer: work together**
