# Video script — Factors that drive development

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The economic factors that drive development include capital, natural resources, labour and technology.

The non-economic factors include education, health, institutions and stability — the conditions around the economy.

Development moves when economic and non-economic factors work together; a shortage in either holds the whole back.
