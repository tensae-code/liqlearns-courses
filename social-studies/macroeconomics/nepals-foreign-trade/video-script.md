# Video script — Nepal's foreign trade

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Nepal's foreign trade is its buying and selling with other countries — mostly with India, and with imports larger than exports.

Foreign trade is Nepal's buying and selling with other countries, and its current state and importance are taught from the country's own trade figures.

By SITC group, food and live animals are the largest export group and mineral fuels the largest import, and the trade balance is negative and widening throughout the years tabled.

India takes roughly two-thirds of both Nepal's exports and imports, China is second, and all other countries together are the remainder.
