# Nepal's foreign trade — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the book teach about what foreign trade is for nepal?
**Answer:** Foreign trade is Nepal's buying and selling with other countries, and its current state and importance are taught from the country's own trade figures.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the book teach about what nepal trades?
**Answer:** By SITC group, food and live animals are the largest export group and mineral fuels the largest import, and the trade balance is negative and widening throughout the years tabled.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the book teach about where nepal trades?
**Answer:** India takes roughly two-thirds of both Nepal's exports and imports, China is second, and all other countries together are the remainder.
