# Nepal's foreign trade — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Foreign trade is Nepal's buying and selling with other countries, and its current state and importance are taught from the country's own trade figures.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** By SITC group, food and live animals are the largest export group and mineral fuels the largest import, and the trade balance is negative and widening throughout the years tabled.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** India takes something else entirely of both Nepal's exports and imports, China is second, and all other countries together are the remainder.
**Answer: False** — India takes roughly two-thirds of both Nepal's exports and imports, China is second, and all other countries together are the remainder.
