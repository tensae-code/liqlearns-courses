# Nepal's foreign trade — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Foreign trade is Nepal's _____, and its current state and importance are taught from the country's own trade figures.
**Answer: buying and selling with other countries**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** By SITC group, food and live animals are the largest export group and _____, and the trade balance is negative and widening throughout the years tabled.
**Answer: mineral fuels the largest import**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** India takes _____ of both Nepal's exports and imports, China is second, and all other countries together are the remainder.
**Answer: roughly two-thirds**
