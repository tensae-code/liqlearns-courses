# Hands-on — Measuring GDP — value added at every stage

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Pass a loaf card along four learners — farmer, miller, baker, shop. At each handover the holder adds only their own stage's counters to the bowl, never the full price, and says why.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — the four-stage bread chain with a value added tag growing at each handover — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
