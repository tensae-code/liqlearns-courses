# Measuring GDP — value added at every stage — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Production in stages" in your own words.
**Answer:** Adding full selling prices at every stage of the bread chain would count the wheat's value repeatedly.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Value added at each stage" in your own words.
**Answer:** Value added at a stage is its selling price minus the intermediate inputs it bought.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "All three approaches, one GDP" in your own words.
**Answer:** The sum of value added at every stage equals the GDP found by the expenditure and income approaches.
