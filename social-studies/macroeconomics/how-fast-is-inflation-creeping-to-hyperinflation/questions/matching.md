# How fast is inflation — creeping to hyperinflation — matching

Match each part to its right statement.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The slow speeds — creeping and walking — c
**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The fast speeds — running and galloping — a

a) Running inflation is 10–20% a year, and galloping inflation is faster still
