# How fast is inflation — creeping to hyperinflation — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Creeping inflation is up to about 3% a year and walking inflation is 3% to under 10%.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Running inflation is 10–20% a year, and galloping inflation is faster still.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Hyperinflation is over 50% a month; Zimbabwe's crisis ended in dollarisation.
**Answer: True**
