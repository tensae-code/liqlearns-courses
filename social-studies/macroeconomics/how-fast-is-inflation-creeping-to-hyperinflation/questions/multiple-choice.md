# How fast is inflation — creeping to hyperinflation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "The slow speeds — creeping and walking"?
a) Creeping inflation is up to about 3% a year and walking inflation is 3% to under 10%  b) Running inflation is 10–20% a year, and galloping inflation is faster still  c) Hyperinflation is over 50% a month; Zimbabwe's crisis ended in dollarisation
**Answer: a) Creeping inflation is up to about 3% a year and walking inflation is 3% to under 10%**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "The fast speeds — running and galloping"?
a) Running inflation is 10–20% a year, and galloping inflation is faster still  b) Hyperinflation is over 50% a month; Zimbabwe's crisis ended in dollarisation  c) Creeping inflation is up to about 3% a year and walking inflation is 3% to under 10%
**Answer: a) Running inflation is 10–20% a year, and galloping inflation is faster still**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Hyperinflation — money dies"?
a) Hyperinflation is over 50% a month; Zimbabwe's crisis ended in dollarisation  b) Creeping inflation is up to about 3% a year and walking inflation is 3% to under 10%  c) Running inflation is 10–20% a year, and galloping inflation is faster still
**Answer: a) Hyperinflation is over 50% a month; Zimbabwe's crisis ended in dollarisation**
