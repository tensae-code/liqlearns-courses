# How fast is inflation — creeping to hyperinflation — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or figure from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Inflation of 2% a year is called ____ inflation.
**Answer: creeping**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Inflation of 15% a year is ____ inflation.
**Answer: running**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Inflation over 50% a month is called ____.
**Answer: hyperinflation**
