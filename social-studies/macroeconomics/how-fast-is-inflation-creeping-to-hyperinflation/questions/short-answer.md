# How fast is inflation — creeping to hyperinflation — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "The slow speeds — creeping and walking" in your own words.
**Answer:** Creeping inflation is up to about 3% a year and walking inflation is 3% to under 10%.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "The fast speeds — running and galloping" in your own words.
**Answer:** Running inflation is 10–20% a year, and galloping inflation is faster still.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Hyperinflation — money dies" in your own words.
**Answer:** Hyperinflation is over 50% a month; Zimbabwe's crisis ended in dollarisation.
