# How fast is inflation — creeping to hyperinflation

## Do it first

Inflation of 2% a year and inflation of 50% a month are both called inflation. Is the same word hiding two very different dangers? Say what you think first.

## Sub lesson 1 — The slow speeds — creeping and walking

Inflation is classified by speed. Creeping inflation is up to about 3% a year — mild enough that people barely adjust. Walking inflation runs from 3% to under 10% a year: noticeable, and worth watching.

## Sub lesson 2 — The fast speeds — running and galloping

Running inflation is 10–20% a year: money visibly loses value and people rush to spend it. Beyond that comes galloping inflation, faster still, where holding money becomes costly by the week.

## Sub lesson 3 — Hyperinflation — money dies

Hyperinflation is over 50% a month. Hungary in July 1946 reached 41.9 quadrillion percent, with prices doubling every 15.3 hours. Zimbabwe in 2007–09 printed a $100-trillion note and ended by abandoning its currency — dollarisation.
