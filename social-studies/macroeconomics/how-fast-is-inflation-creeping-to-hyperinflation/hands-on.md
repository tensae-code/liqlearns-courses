# Hands-on — How fast is inflation — creeping to hyperinflation

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Place the speeds
Write the five speeds on a line from slowest to fastest with their rates. Then place three invented country years on the line and defend each placement.

## The doubling rule
At prices doubling every 15.3 hours, work out roughly how many times prices double in one week of the Hungarian crisis (7 × 24 / 15.3 ≈ 11 doublings). Feel what that does to a wage paid on Monday.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
