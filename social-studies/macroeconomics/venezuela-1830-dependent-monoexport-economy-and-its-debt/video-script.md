# Video script — Venezuela 1830 — a dependent monoexport economy and its debt

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Agriculture and livestock were devastated and exports scarce.

Politically independent but economically dependent — e.g. it imported all kinds of manufactures from England, with a negative trade balance there, while foreign houses controlled its exports and imports and invested very little in production.

Debt payments absorbed almost 40% (Venezuela had assumed 28.5% of Gran Colombia's debt) and military spending over 50% — leaving almost nothing to increase production.
