# Venezuela 1830 — a dependent monoexport economy and its debt — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In 1830 Venezuela's agriculture and livestock were —
a) devastated, with exports scarce  b) booming, with exports flooding out  c) already based on oil
**Answer: a) devastated, with exports scarce**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Venezuela's trade balance was negative with —
a) England, from which it imported all kinds of manufactures  b) the United States  c) Germany
**Answer: a) England, from which it imported all kinds of manufactures**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Paying the inherited external debt absorbed about —
a) 40% of state income  b) 4% of state income  c) nothing — the debt was forgiven
**Answer: a) 40% of state income**
