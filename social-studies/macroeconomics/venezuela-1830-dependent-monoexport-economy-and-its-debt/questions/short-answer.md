# Venezuela 1830 — a dependent monoexport economy and its debt — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What was the state of agriculture, livestock and exports in 1830?
**Answer:** Agriculture and livestock were devastated and exports scarce.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Give the book's verdict on Venezuela in 1830 in one phrase, and one fact behind it.
**Answer:** Politically independent but economically dependent — e.g. it imported all kinds of manufactures from England, with a negative trade balance there, while foreign houses controlled its exports and imports and invested very little in production.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What shares of state income went to debt and to the military, and what was left for production?
**Answer:** Debt payments absorbed almost 40% (Venezuela had assumed 28.5% of Gran Colombia's debt) and military spending over 50% — leaving almost nothing to increase production.
