# Venezuela 1830 — a dependent monoexport economy and its debt — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Venezuela supplied tropical products — mainly _____, also cacao and tobacco.
**Answer: coffee**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Foreign commercial houses controlled trade through La Guaira, Puerto Cabello and _____.
**Answer: Maracaibo**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Venezuela assumed _____ percent of Gran Colombia's debt.
**Answer: 28.5**
