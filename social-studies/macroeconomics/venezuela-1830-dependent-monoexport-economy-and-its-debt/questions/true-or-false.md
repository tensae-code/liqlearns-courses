# Venezuela 1830 — a dependent monoexport economy and its debt — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In 1830 Venezuela entered the world market under a new scheme unlike the colonial one.
**Answer: False** — it inserted itself under the colonial scheme, as supplier of tropical products.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The book calls 1830 Venezuela politically independent but economically dependent.
**Answer: True** — a backward monoproductora and monoexportadora economy — the mark of semicolonial countries.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Military spending took over 50% of state income, leaving almost nothing to increase production.
**Answer: True** — with debt payments near 40%, almost nothing remained.
