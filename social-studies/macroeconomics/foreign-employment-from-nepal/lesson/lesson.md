# Foreign employment from Nepal

## Do it first

## Sub lesson 1 — What foreign employment is

Foreign employment is working in another country, prepared for under the state's rules with a labour permit, a health examination, orientation training and insurance.

## Sub lesson 2 — Its gains and its costs

Foreign employment earns foreign currency for the country, set against family separation, disease carried home and workers drained from development work at home.

## Sub lesson 3 — The policy that governs it

The Foreign Employment Act 2064, second amendment 2075, requires licensed agencies, open competition without gender discrimination at eighteen, and insurance of at least five lakh rupees for every worker sent.
