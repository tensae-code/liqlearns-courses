# Foreign employment from Nepal — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Foreign employment is working in another country, prepared for under the state's rules with a labour permit, a health examination, orientation training and insurance.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Foreign employment earns foreign currency for the country, set against family separation, disease carried home and workers drained from development work at home.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The Foreign Employment Act 2064, second amendment 2075, requires licensed agencies, open competition without gender discrimination at eighteen, and insurance of at least something else entirely for every worker sent.
**Answer: False** — The Foreign Employment Act 2064, second amendment 2075, requires licensed agencies, open competition without gender discrimination at eighteen, and insurance of at least five lakh rupees for every worker sent.
