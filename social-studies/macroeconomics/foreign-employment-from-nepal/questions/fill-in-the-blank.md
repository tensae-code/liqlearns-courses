# Foreign employment from Nepal — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Foreign employment is working in another country, prepared for under the state's rules with _____, a health examination, orientation training and insurance.
**Answer: a labour permit**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Foreign employment earns foreign currency for the country, set against _____, disease carried home and workers drained from development work at home.
**Answer: family separation**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The Foreign Employment Act 2064, second amendment 2075, requires licensed agencies, open competition without gender discrimination at eighteen, and insurance of at least _____ for every worker sent.
**Answer: five lakh rupees**
