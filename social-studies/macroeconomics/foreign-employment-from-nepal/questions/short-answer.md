# Foreign employment from Nepal — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the book teach about what foreign employment is?
**Answer:** Foreign employment is working in another country, prepared for under the state's rules with a labour permit, a health examination, orientation training and insurance.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the book teach about its gains and its costs?
**Answer:** Foreign employment earns foreign currency for the country, set against family separation, disease carried home and workers drained from development work at home.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the book teach about the policy that governs it?
**Answer:** The Foreign Employment Act 2064, second amendment 2075, requires licensed agencies, open competition without gender discrimination at eighteen, and insurance of at least five lakh rupees for every worker sent.
