# Direct and indirect taxes — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Direct — impact and incidence together"?
a) A direct tax's impact and incidence fall on the same person  b) The direct families are income, transfer, property and capital-gains taxes  c) An indirect tax's impact and incidence fall on different people, as with VAT and excise
**Answer: a) A direct tax's impact and incidence fall on the same person**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "The direct families"?
a) The direct families are income, transfer, property and capital-gains taxes  b) An indirect tax's impact and incidence fall on different people, as with VAT and excise  c) A direct tax's impact and incidence fall on the same person
**Answer: a) The direct families are income, transfer, property and capital-gains taxes**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Indirect — impact here, incidence there"?
a) An indirect tax's impact and incidence fall on different people, as with VAT and excise  b) A direct tax's impact and incidence fall on the same person  c) The direct families are income, transfer, property and capital-gains taxes
**Answer: a) An indirect tax's impact and incidence fall on different people, as with VAT and excise**
