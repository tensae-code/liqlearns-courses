# Direct and indirect taxes — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or figure from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In a direct tax, impact and ____ fall on the same person.
**Answer: incidence**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** One direct family taxes ____ gains.
**Answer: capital**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** VAT is an ____ tax because seller and bearer differ.
**Answer: indirect**
