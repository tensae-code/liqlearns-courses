# Direct and indirect taxes — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Direct — impact and incidence together" in your own words.
**Answer:** A direct tax's impact and incidence fall on the same person.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "The direct families" in your own words.
**Answer:** The direct families are income, transfer, property and capital-gains taxes.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Indirect — impact here, incidence there" in your own words.
**Answer:** An indirect tax's impact and incidence fall on different people, as with VAT and excise.
