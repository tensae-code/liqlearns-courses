# Direct and indirect taxes — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A direct tax's impact and incidence fall on the same person.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The direct families are income, transfer, property and capital-gains taxes.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** An indirect tax's impact and incidence fall on different people, as with VAT and excise.
**Answer: True**
