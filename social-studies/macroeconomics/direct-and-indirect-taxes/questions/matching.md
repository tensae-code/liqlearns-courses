# Direct and indirect taxes — matching

Match each part to its right statement.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Direct — impact and incidence together — c
**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The direct families — a
**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Indirect — impact here, incidence there — b

Statements:
a) The direct families are income, transfer, property and capital-gains taxes
b) An indirect tax's impact and incidence fall on different people, as with VAT and excise
c) A direct tax's impact and incidence fall on the same person

**Answer key:** 1 — c; 2 — a; 3 — b
