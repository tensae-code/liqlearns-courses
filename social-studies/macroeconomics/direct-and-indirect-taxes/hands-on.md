# Hands-on — Direct and indirect taxes

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Two arrows
Draw the two arrows: one striking the earner directly, one bouncing from seller to buyer. Label impact and incidence at each arrow's ends.

## Sort the Ethiopian set
Sort ten named Ethiopian taxes into direct and indirect columns, using the impact-incidence test — not the tax's name — as your rule, and be ready to defend the two hardest.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
