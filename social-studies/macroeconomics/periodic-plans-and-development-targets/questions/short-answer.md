# Periodic plans and development targets — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the book teach about what a periodic plan is?
**Answer:** A periodic plan is a programme that uses available resources properly to reach set goals within a set period.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the book teach about nepal's planning history?
**Answer:** Nepal's planning runs from the first five-year plan of 2013 BS, with ten five-year and five three-year plans implemented since.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the book teach about the sixteenth plan's targets?
**Answer:** The Sixteenth Plan (2081/82–2085/86) targets economic growth from 3.5 to 7.3 percent, per-capita income from $1,456 to $2,351, poverty from 20.3 to 12.0 percent and unemployment from 11.4 to 5.0 percent.
