# Periodic plans and development targets — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A periodic plan is a programme that uses available resources properly to reach set goals _____.
**Answer: within a set period**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Nepal's planning runs from _____, with ten five-year and five three-year plans implemented since.
**Answer: the first five-year plan of 2013 BS**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The Sixteenth Plan (2081/82–2085/86) targets economic growth from 3.5 to 7.3 percent, per-capita income from _____, poverty from 20.3 to 12.0 percent and unemployment from 11.4 to 5.0 percent.
**Answer: $1,456 to $2,351**
