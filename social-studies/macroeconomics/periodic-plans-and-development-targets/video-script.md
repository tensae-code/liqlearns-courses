A periodic plan uses available resources to reach set goals within a set period — Nepal's Sixteenth Plan sets its targets in numbers.

A periodic plan is a programme that uses available resources properly to reach set goals within a set period.

Nepal's planning runs from the first five-year plan of 2013 BS, with ten five-year and five three-year plans implemented since.

The Sixteenth Plan (2081/82–2085/86) targets economic growth from 3.5 to 7.3 percent, per-capita income from $1,456 to $2,351, poverty from 20.3 to 12.0 percent and unemployment from 11.4 to 5.0 percent.
