# Lesson plan — Global economic problems

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Macroeconomics · **Lesson:** Global economic problems
**Place in the curriculum:** Macroeconomics (CURRICULUM.md) — built in the Economics expansion (Uzbekistan, 2026-11-16).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The problems no country can solve alone — war spending (3 billion dollars a day worldwide), environmental pollution, the coming shortage of raw materials and energy, uneven development (85% of wealth consumed by 17% of people) and food shortage and hunger, with Malthus as the sidebar portrait.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the Uzbekistan Grade 9 book's own teaching of this topic — Topic 30, Global Economic Problems — worked in sum on Uzbek cases.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of ..." in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.
