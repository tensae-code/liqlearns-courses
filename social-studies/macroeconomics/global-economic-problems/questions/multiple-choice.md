# Global economic problems — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** War spending worldwide is —
a) 3 billion dollars a day  b) 3 million a year  c) nothing
**Answer: a) 3 billion dollars a day**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Uneven development: 85% of wealth is consumed by —
a) 17% of people  b) 85% of people  c) half of people
**Answer: a) 17% of people**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The last global problem listed is —
a) food shortage and hunger  b) inflation  c) unemployment
**Answer: a) food shortage and hunger**
