# Global economic problems

## Do it first

Think of this topic the way the Uzbekistan Grade 9 book opens it — Topic 30, Global Economic Problems. What do you already know about it before you read on?

## Sub lesson 1 — War spending and environmental pollution

The book lists the problems **no country can solve alone**. First: **war spending — 3 billion dollars a day worldwide**; and **environmental pollution**.

## Sub lesson 2 — Coming shortages and uneven development

Next: the **coming shortage of raw materials and energy**, and **uneven development — 85% of wealth consumed by 17% of people**.

## Sub lesson 3 — Food shortage and hunger — and Malthus

Last: **food shortage and hunger**. The topic's sidebar portrait is **Malthus**, who tied population to food.
