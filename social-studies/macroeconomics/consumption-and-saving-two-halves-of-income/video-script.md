# Video script — Consumption and saving — two halves of income

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Because an extra birr can only be consumed or saved, MPC + MPS = 1. The averages obey the same lock: APC + APS = 1. The two pairs are not four free numbers — each pair is one split seen two ways.

Plotting consumption against the 45-degree line — the line where expenditure equals income — frames the whole picture: the consumption function is read against the line of spending exactly what you earn.

Where the consumption line crosses the 45-degree line is the break-even income: below it consumption exceeds income (dissaving), at the crossing saving is zero, and above it saving is positive.
