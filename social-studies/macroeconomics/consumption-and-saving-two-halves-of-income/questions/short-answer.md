# Consumption and saving — two halves of income — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "MPC + MPS = 1" in your own words.
**Answer:** MPC + MPS = 1 and APC + APS = 1, because income can only be consumed or saved.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "The 45-degree line" in your own words.
**Answer:** The 45-degree line is where expenditure equals income.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Break-even income" in your own words.
**Answer:** At break-even income saving is zero; below it households dissave, above it they save.
