# Consumption and saving — two halves of income — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** MPC + MPS can exceed 1 for high incomes.
**Answer: False — Each extra birr is consumed or saved — the two shares of it must total exactly 1.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The 45-degree line shows consumption at maximum.
**Answer: False — It shows every point where spending exactly equals income — the reference line, not a maximum.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Below break-even income, households save positively.
**Answer: False — Below the crossing consumption exceeds income — that is dissaving, not positive saving.**
