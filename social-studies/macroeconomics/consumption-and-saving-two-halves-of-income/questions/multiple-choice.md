# Consumption and saving — two halves of income — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "MPC + MPS = 1"?
a) MPC + MPS = 1 and APC + APS = 1, because income can only be consumed or saved.  b) The 45-degree line is where expenditure equals income.  c) At break-even income saving is zero; below it households dissave, above it they save.
**Answer: a) MPC + MPS = 1 and APC + APS = 1, because income can only be consumed or saved.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "The 45-degree line"?
a) At break-even income saving is zero; below it households dissave, above it they save.  b) MPC + MPS = 1 and APC + APS = 1, because income can only be consumed or saved.  c) The 45-degree line is where expenditure equals income.
**Answer: c) The 45-degree line is where expenditure equals income.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Break-even income"?
a) The 45-degree line is where expenditure equals income.  b) At break-even income saving is zero; below it households dissave, above it they save.  c) MPC + MPS = 1 and APC + APS = 1, because income can only be consumed or saved.
**Answer: b) At break-even income saving is zero; below it households dissave, above it they save.**
