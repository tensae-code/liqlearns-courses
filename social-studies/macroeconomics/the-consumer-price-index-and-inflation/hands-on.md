# Hands-on — The consumer price index and inflation

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Fill a real basket with the lesson's goods. Price it twice — with old price cards, then new ones — and have two learners announce the index and the inflation rate as a news bulletin.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — a shopping basket priced in two years, with a three-row deflator-versus-CPI comparison beneath — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.

## The Grade 12 layer — the Ethiopian Calendar basket
Recompute all four CPI values from the basket costs 200, 280, 240 and 400 birr, showing every division. Then recompute the 2003 EC value with the corrected 440 birr cost from the book's own price table, and write one sentence on which figure you would quote, and why.
