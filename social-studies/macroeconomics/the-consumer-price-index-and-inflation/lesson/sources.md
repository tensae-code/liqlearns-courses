Grade 12 extension added 2026-11-12 (Ethiopia Wave 4, Band E): sub lessons 4–5 and their questions rest on the Grade 12 student book named above. WORKED-ANSWER ERRORS CORRECTED: (1) the Grade 12 book's CPI introduction is garbled (a 'past price total $340' appears, then the worked formula uses 560 and 430) — those stray figures are not taught; (2) its worked example's price table prices 2003 EC edible oil at 24 birr, but the printed solution computes with 20 birr, giving a basket cost of 400 birr where the table implies 440 birr (CPI 220, not 200) — sub lesson 5 teaches the corrected figure.
