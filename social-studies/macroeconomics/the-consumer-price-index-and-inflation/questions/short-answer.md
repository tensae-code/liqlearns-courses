# The consumer price index and inflation — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Pricing a fixed basket" in your own words.
**Answer:** The CPI compares the cost of a fixed consumer basket with its cost in the base year.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Deflator versus CPI — three differences" in your own words.
**Answer:** Imports appear in the CPI but not in the GDP deflator.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Inflation — the CPI's growth" in your own words.
**Answer:** The inflation rate is the percentage growth of the CPI between two periods.

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Explain "The Ethiopian Calendar basket — the worked example" in your own words.
**Answer:** Basket costs of 200, 280, 240 and 400 birr in 2000–2003 EC give CPI values of 100, 140, 120 and 200.

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** Explain "The corrected oil price — 440, not 400" in your own words.
**Answer:** With the table's oil price of 24 birr, the 2003 basket costs 440 birr and its CPI is 220.
