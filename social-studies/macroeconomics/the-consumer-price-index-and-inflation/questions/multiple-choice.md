# The consumer price index and inflation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Pricing a fixed basket"?
a) the CPI prices a fixed typical basket against its base-year cost  b) coverage, imports, and the fixed-versus-moving basket distinguish CPI from deflator  c) inflation is the percentage growth of the CPI between two periods
**Answer: a) the CPI prices a fixed typical basket against its base-year cost**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Deflator versus CPI — three differences"?
a) the CPI prices a fixed typical basket against its base-year cost  b) inflation is the percentage growth of the CPI between two periods  c) coverage, imports, and the fixed-versus-moving basket distinguish CPI from deflator
**Answer: c) coverage, imports, and the fixed-versus-moving basket distinguish CPI from deflator**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Inflation — the CPI's growth"?
a) coverage, imports, and the fixed-versus-moving basket distinguish CPI from deflator  b) inflation is the percentage growth of the CPI between two periods  c) the CPI prices a fixed typical basket against its base-year cost
**Answer: b) inflation is the percentage growth of the CPI between two periods**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** The CPI rises from 100 in one year to 112 the next. What is the inflation rate?
a) 12%  b) 112%  c) 1.12%
**Answer: a) 12%** — Inflation = the CPI's percentage growth: (112 − 100) / 100 = 12%.

**5. (Sub lesson 4)** Which statement best matches "The Ethiopian Calendar basket — the worked example"?
a) Basket costs of 200, 280, 240 and 400 birr give CPI values of 100, 120, 140 and 200  b) Basket costs of 200, 280, 240 and 400 birr in 2000–2003 EC give CPI values of 100, 140, 120 and 200  c) The Ethiopian Calendar basket changes its contents every year to follow fashion
**Answer: b) Basket costs of 200, 280, 240 and 400 birr in 2000–2003 EC give CPI values of 100, 140, 120 and 200**

**6. (Sub lesson 5)** Which statement best matches "The corrected oil price — 440, not 400"?
a) With the table's oil price, the 2003 basket costs 400 birr and its CPI is 200  b) With the table's oil price of 24 birr, the 2003 basket costs 440 birr and its CPI is 220  c) A printed solution should never be checked against its own table
**Answer: b) With the table's oil price of 24 birr, the 2003 basket costs 440 birr and its CPI is 220**
