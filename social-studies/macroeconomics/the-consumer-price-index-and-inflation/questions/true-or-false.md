# The consumer price index and inflation — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The CPI compares the cost of a fixed consumer basket with its cost in the base year.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Imports appear in the CPI but not in the GDP deflator.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The inflation rate is the percentage growth of the CPI between two periods.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Basket costs of 200, 280, 240 and 400 birr in 2000–2003 EC give CPI values of 100, 140, 120 and 200.
**Answer: True**

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** With the table's oil price of 24 birr, the 2003 basket costs 440 birr and its CPI is 220.
**Answer: True**
