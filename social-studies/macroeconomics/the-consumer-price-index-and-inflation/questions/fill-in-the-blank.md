# The consumer price index and inflation — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The CPI prices a fixed _____ bought by a typical consumer, against its base-year cost.
**Answer: basket**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Imports appear in the _____ but not in the deflator, because the deflator covers only domestic production.
**Answer: CPI**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The inflation rate is the percentage growth of the _____ between two periods.
**Answer: CPI**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** A basket costing 280 birr against a 200 birr base year gives a CPI of ____.
**Answer: 140**

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** With edible oil at the table's 24 birr, the 2003 basket costs ____ birr.
**Answer: 440**
