# Video script — Maquilas cheap labour in a global economy

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Maquilas cheap labour in a global economy — as Colombia's book teaches it, says the narrator: Maquilas offer jobs to citizens of developing countries but make labour conditions more precarious — low pay, no promotion possibilities, great instability; Factory owners worldwide are restructuring production models and labour relations to cut labour costs Treating cheap labour as the only advantage in a global, highly competitive economy
