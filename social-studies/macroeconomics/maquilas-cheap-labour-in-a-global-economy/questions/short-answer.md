# Maquilas cheap labour in a global economy — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the lesson teach about maquilas offer jobs to citizens of developing countries?
**Answer:** Maquilas offer jobs to citizens of developing countries but make labour conditions more precarious — low pay, no promotion possibilities, great instability;

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the lesson teach about factory owners worldwide are restructuring production models and?
**Answer:** Factory owners worldwide are restructuring production models and labour relations to cut labour costs

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the lesson teach about treating cheap labour as the only advantage in?
**Answer:** Treating cheap labour as the only advantage in a global, highly competitive economy
