# Business tax and PAN in Nepal — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Business (corporate) tax is collected under the Income Tax Act 2058 on a self-assessment system: the taxpayer works out and pays his own tax, with interest, fines and even imprisonment for late payment.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** As printed, ordinary companies pay 25 percent, banks, insurance and telecom companies 30 percent, and listed public companies and production industries 20 percent.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The Permanent Account Number (PAN) is the unique, permanent something else entirely the Inland Revenue Department issues to every taxpayer, needed for business, employment and investment alike, with an online application route.
**Answer: False** — The Permanent Account Number (PAN) is the unique, permanent nine-digit number the Inland Revenue Department issues to every taxpayer, needed for business, employment and investment alike, with an online application route.
