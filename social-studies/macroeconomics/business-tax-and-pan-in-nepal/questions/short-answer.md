# Business tax and PAN in Nepal — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the book teach about business tax by self-assessment?
**Answer:** Business (corporate) tax is collected under the Income Tax Act 2058 on a self-assessment system: the taxpayer works out and pays his own tax, with interest, fines and even imprisonment for late payment.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the book teach about the rate table?
**Answer:** As printed, ordinary companies pay 25 percent, banks, insurance and telecom companies 30 percent, and listed public companies and production industries 20 percent.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the book teach about the permanent account number?
**Answer:** The Permanent Account Number (PAN) is the unique, permanent nine-digit number the Inland Revenue Department issues to every taxpayer, needed for business, employment and investment alike, with an online application route.
