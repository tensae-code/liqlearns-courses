# Business tax and PAN in Nepal — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Business (corporate) tax is collected under the Income Tax Act 2058 on a _____ system: the taxpayer works out and pays his own tax, with interest, fines and even imprisonment for late payment.
**Answer: self-assessment**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** As printed, ordinary companies pay _____, banks, insurance and telecom companies 30 percent, and listed public companies and production industries 20 percent.
**Answer: 25 percent**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The Permanent Account Number (PAN) is the unique, permanent _____ the Inland Revenue Department issues to every taxpayer, needed for business, employment and investment alike, with an online application route.
**Answer: nine-digit number**
