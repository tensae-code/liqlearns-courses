## Sub lesson 1 — Business tax by self-assessment

Business (corporate) tax is collected under the Income Tax Act 2058 on a self-assessment system: the taxpayer works out and pays his own tax, with interest, fines and even imprisonment for late payment.

## Sub lesson 2 — The rate table

As printed, ordinary companies pay 25 percent, banks, insurance and telecom companies 30 percent, and listed public companies and production industries 20 percent.

## Sub lesson 3 — The Permanent Account Number

The Permanent Account Number (PAN) is the unique, permanent nine-digit number the Inland Revenue Department issues to every taxpayer, needed for business, employment and investment alike, with an online application route.
