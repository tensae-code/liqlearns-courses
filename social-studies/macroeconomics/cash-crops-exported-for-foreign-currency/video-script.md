# Video script — Cash crops — exported for foreign currency

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Not every crop is grown to be eaten at home. Cash crops are grown to be sold — and Yemen's cash crops, coffee, cotton and sesame, are grown to be exported abroad, to buyers in other countries.

Exports earn foreign currency — the money of other countries, which the homeland needs to buy what it does not make. So the cash-crop fields support the whole economy: field, harbour, foreign currency, homeland.
