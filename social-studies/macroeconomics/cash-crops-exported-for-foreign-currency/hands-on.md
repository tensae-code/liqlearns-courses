# Hands-on — Cash crops — exported for foreign currency

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with the chain
Draw the chain in four boxes: cash crop — export harbour — foreign currency — the homeland's economy. Put coffee, cotton and sesame in the first box, and say the whole chain aloud.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's picture from memory — coffee, cotton and sesame being loaded at a harbour for export, with foreign currency flowing back to the homeland — and label its parts with the lesson's key words. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare, and fix every label you placed wrong.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
