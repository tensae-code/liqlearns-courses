# Cash crops — exported for foreign currency — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Cash crops are grown to be eaten at home.
**Answer: False — they are grown to be sold, and Yemen's are exported abroad.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Coffee, cotton and sesame are Yemen's cash crops in the book.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Foreign currency from exports supports the homeland's economy.
**Answer: True**
