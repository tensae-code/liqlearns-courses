# Cash crops — exported for foreign currency — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is a cash crop?
**Answer:** A crop grown to be sold, not eaten by the grower's own household.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name Yemen's three cash crops in the lesson.
**Answer:** Coffee, cotton and sesame.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** How do cash crops support the whole homeland's economy?
**Answer:** They are exported abroad and earn foreign currency, which the homeland needs to buy what it does not make.
