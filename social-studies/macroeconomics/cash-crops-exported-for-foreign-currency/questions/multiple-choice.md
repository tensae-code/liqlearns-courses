# Cash crops — exported for foreign currency — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A cash crop is a crop grown to be —
a) eaten by the grower's family only  b) sold  c) stored forever
**Answer: b) sold**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Yemen's cash crops in the lesson are —
a) coffee, cotton and sesame  b) wheat, dates and grapes  c) rice, tea and sugar
**Answer: a) coffee, cotton and sesame**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Exported cash crops earn the homeland —
a) foreign currency  b) rainfall  c) new farmland
**Answer: a) foreign currency**
