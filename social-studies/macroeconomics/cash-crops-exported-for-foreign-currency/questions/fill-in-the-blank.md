# Cash crops — exported for foreign currency — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Crops grown to be sold are called _____ crops.
**Answer: cash**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Yemen's cash crops — coffee, _____ and sesame — are exported abroad.
**Answer: cotton**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Exports earn _____ currency for the homeland.
**Answer: foreign**
