Yemen's own way (Grade 4 Al-Tarbiya al-Ijtima'iya): the economy is taught as the homeland's wealth — crops, herds, fish, workshops and ports — with the state, the agricultural associations and the export harbour all named as actors a nine-year-old can point to. This lesson is built from that book's placement: Unit 2, Lesson 1 — My homeland's agricultural resources (§cash crops) — teaches: Some crops are grown not to eat at home but to sell abroad: Yemen's cash crops — coffee, cotton and sesame — are exported to other countries, and the foreign currency they earn supports the homeland's economy. The lesson teaches the placement's content in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
