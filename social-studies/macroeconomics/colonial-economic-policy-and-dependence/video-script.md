# Video script — Colonial economic policy and dependence

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Colonialism, in Palestine's books, is an economic system first. The motive: resources — oil, gas, the water straits — new markets, and raw materials, like Egyptian cotton forced for British factories. The control: foreign banks, loans and debt, raw materials seized, cheap labour, the Suez revenues — and a currency pegged to sterling. The answer after independence: nationalisation, sanctions, development plans — and, from the people, boycott.
