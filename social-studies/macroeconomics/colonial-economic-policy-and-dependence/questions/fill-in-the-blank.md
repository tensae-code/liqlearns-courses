# Colonial economic policy and dependence — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Colonial policy made the Arab economy _____ to the foreign economy.
**Answer: subordinate**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Britain's control of the _____ Canal and its revenues is the books' example of economic control.
**Answer: Suez**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The popular reaction was _____ of colonial goods, companies and settlement products.
**Answer: boycotting (the boycott)**
