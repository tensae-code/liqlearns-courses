# Colonial economic policy and dependence — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What drove colonisation, in the books' economic account?
a) the economic motive — controlling resources such as oil, gas and water straits, markets and raw materials  b) tourism  c) sport
**Answer: a) the economic motive — controlling resources such as oil, gas and water straits, markets and raw materials**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which is economic control as a form of colonialism?
a) seizing raw materials, marketing industrial products, obtaining cheap labour and investing capital  b) building schools only  c) translating books
**Answer: a) seizing raw materials, marketing industrial products, obtaining cheap labour and investing capital**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** How did Arab states and peoples react after independence?
a) officially by nationalisation, sanctions and development plans; popularly by boycott  b) by doing nothing  c) by inviting more foreign banks
**Answer: a) officially by nationalisation, sanctions and development plans; popularly by boycott**
