# Colonial economic policy and dependence — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why did Britain force cotton growing on Egypt?
**Answer:** To feed British industry — Egypt colonised for the raw material its factories needed.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What dependence was left behind in money and resources?
**Answer:** The local currency tied to a foreign currency, and colonial control of the oil and natural-gas fields.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name two official reactions.
**Answer:** Nationalisation and economic sanctions (also: withdrawing investments, development plans and financing funds, cooperation with rising economies).
