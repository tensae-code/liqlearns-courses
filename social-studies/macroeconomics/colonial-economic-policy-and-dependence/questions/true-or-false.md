# Colonial economic policy and dependence — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Foreign banks helped the Arab economy stay independent of loans and debt.
**Answer: False — foreign banks drew in Arab deposits and drowned the region in loans and debt**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The Egyptian pound was pegged to British sterling — an example of currency dependence.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Boycotting colonial goods and settlement products was an official reaction.
**Answer: False — boycott was the popular reaction; nationalisation, sanctions and development plans were official**
