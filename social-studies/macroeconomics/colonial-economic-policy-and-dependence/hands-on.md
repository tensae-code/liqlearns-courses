# Hands-on — Colonial economic policy and dependence

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Set out a raw cotton ball, a finished shirt with a foreign label, and a coin. Say which country grows, which country manufactures and sells, and whose currency the coin follows — then move the shirt's making home and say what changed.

## Draw it and check it
Draw three boxes — motive, control, reaction — and fill each with the names from its sub lesson. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare, and fix every name you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach sub lesson 2 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
