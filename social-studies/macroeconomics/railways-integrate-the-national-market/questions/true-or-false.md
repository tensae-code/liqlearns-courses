# Railways integrate the national market — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Without railways and roads the internal economy suffered high freight costs, difficulty importing machinery
**Answer: True** — this is what Colombia's book teaches in this part.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Sub lesson 2 is mainly about without railways and roads the internal economy suffered.
**Answer: False** — sub lesson 2 is about few possibilities of bringing land into the productive; without railways and roads the internal economy suffered is sub lesson 1.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The 1920s railway build-out aimed first at integrating the national economy, then at routes for the external economy
**Answer: True** — this is what Colombia's book teaches in this part.
