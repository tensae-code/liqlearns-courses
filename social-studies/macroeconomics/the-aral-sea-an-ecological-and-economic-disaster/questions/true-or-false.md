# The Aral Sea — an ecological and economic disaster — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Aral Sea grew between 1960 and 1995.
**Answer: False** — It shrank, as the maps show.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A salt desert spreads where the sea was.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The disaster is presented as the direct result of economic activity.
**Answer: True**
