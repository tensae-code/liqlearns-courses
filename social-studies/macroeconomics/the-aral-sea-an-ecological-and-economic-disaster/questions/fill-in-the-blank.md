# The Aral Sea — an ecological and economic disaster — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The sea 'before and today' is shown on shoreline maps for 1960, 1980 and _____.
**Answer: 1995**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** With the sea went its fisheries and Muynak's _____; a salt desert spreads.
**Answer: cannery**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The disaster is to be priced as an _____ cost.
**Answer: alternative**
