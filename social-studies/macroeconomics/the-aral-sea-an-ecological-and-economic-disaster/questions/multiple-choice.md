# The Aral Sea — an ecological and economic disaster — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The book's Aral Sea maps show the shoreline in —
a) 1960, 1980 and 1995  b) 1900 and 2000 only  c) 2020
**Answer: a) 1960, 1980 and 1995**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Muynak lost its —
a) car plant  b) cannery  c) airport
**Answer: b) cannery**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Pupils are asked to price the disaster as —
a) a tax  b) an alternative cost  c) a dividend
**Answer: b) an alternative cost**
