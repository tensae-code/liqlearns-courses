# The Aral Sea — an ecological and economic disaster — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What do the three maps show?
**Answer:** The Aral Sea's shoreline shrinking from 1960 to 1980 to 1995.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name two things lost with the sea.
**Answer:** Its fisheries and Muynak's cannery (and the sea itself, replaced by salt desert).

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Why does the book call the Aral Sea an economic disaster as well as an ecological one?
**Answer:** It was the direct result of economic activity, and pupils must weigh what that activity gained against everything the sea's loss cost.
