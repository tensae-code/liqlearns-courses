# The Aral Sea — an ecological and economic disaster

## Do it first

Think of this topic the way the Uzbekistan Grade 9 book opens it — Topic 12, The Aral Sea. What do you already know about it before you read on?

## Sub lesson 1 — The sea before and today — the shrinking shoreline

The book sets Uzbekistan's own case: the **Aral Sea** 'before and today', on shoreline maps for **1960, 1980 and 1995** — the sea shrinking as the water that fed it was taken for economic activity.

## Sub lesson 2 — What was lost — fisheries, Muynak's cannery, and the salt desert

With the sea went its **fisheries** and **Muynak's cannery**, the fish-processing plant of the port town; in the sea's place a **salt desert** spreads.

## Sub lesson 3 — Pricing the disaster — an alternative cost

The book asks pupils to **price the disaster as an alternative cost**: what was gained by the economic activity must be weighed against everything the sea's loss cost.
