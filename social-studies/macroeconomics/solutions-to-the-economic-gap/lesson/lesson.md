# Solutions to the economic gap

## Do it first

Think of this topic the way the Indonesia book opens it — Solutions to the economic gap. What do you already know about it before you read on?

## Sub lesson 1 — Closing the gap

Solutions to the economic gap are the measures a country takes to spread income and opportunity more evenly.

## Sub lesson 2 — What the measures do

The Grade 11 book's solutions work on the gap's causes — widening access to work, education and support for those left behind.

## Sub lesson 3 — The gap as a national task

In Indonesia's book the economic gap is treated as a national task shared by government, business and communities.
