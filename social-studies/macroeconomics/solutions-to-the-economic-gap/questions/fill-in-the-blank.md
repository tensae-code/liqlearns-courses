# Solutions to the economic gap — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Solutions to the economic gap are the measures a country takes to spread income and opportunity _____.
**Answer: more evenly**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The Grade 11 book's solutions work on the gap's causes — widening _____, education and support for those left behind.
**Answer: access to work**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In Indonesia's book the economic gap is treated as a _____ shared by government, business and communities.
**Answer: national task**
