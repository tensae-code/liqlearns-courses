# Solutions to the economic gap — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Solutions to the economic gap are the measures a country takes to spread income and opportunity more evenly.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The Grade 11 book's solutions work on the gap's causes — widening access to work, education and support for those left behind.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In Indonesia's book the economic gap is treated as a something else entirely shared by government, business and communities.
**Answer: False** — In Indonesia's book the economic gap is treated as a national task shared by government, business and communities.
