# Solutions to the economic gap — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the book teach about closing the gap?
**Answer:** Solutions to the economic gap are the measures a country takes to spread income and opportunity more evenly.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the book teach about what the measures do?
**Answer:** The Grade 11 book's solutions work on the gap's causes — widening access to work, education and support for those left behind.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the book teach about the gap as a national task?
**Answer:** In Indonesia's book the economic gap is treated as a national task shared by government, business and communities.
