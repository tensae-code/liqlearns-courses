# Video script — Debt burden and debt relief

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Debt burden and debt relief — as Nigeria's book teaches it, says the narrator: a heavy debt burden drains a developing country's income into repayments instead of development.; debt burdens grow through borrowing, interest and falling export earnings.; debt relief cancels or eases a country's debts, freeing resources for development — as Nigeria won relief in 2005..
