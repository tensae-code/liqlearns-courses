# Debt burden and debt relief — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** About the debt burden — the lesson teaches that —
a) a heavy debt burden drains a developing country's income into repayments instead of development.  b) debt burdens grow through borrowing, interest and falling export earnings.  c) debt relief cancels or eases a country's debts, freeing resources for development — as Nigeria won relief in 2005.
**Answer: a) a heavy debt burden drains a developing country's income into repayments instead of development.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** About how the burden grows — the lesson teaches that —

**Answer: b) debt burdens grow through borrowing, interest and falling export earnings.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** About debt relief — the lesson teaches that —
a) a heavy debt burden drains a developing country's income into repayments instead of development.  b) debt burdens grow through borrowing, interest and falling export earnings.  c) debt relief cancels or eases a country's debts, freeing resources for development — as Nigeria won relief in 2005.
