# Debt burden and debt relief — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A heavy debt burden drains income into _____ instead of development.
**Answer: repayments**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Debt burdens grow through borrowing, interest and falling export _____.
**Answer: earnings**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Debt relief cancels or eases debts, freeing resources for _____.
**Answer: development**
