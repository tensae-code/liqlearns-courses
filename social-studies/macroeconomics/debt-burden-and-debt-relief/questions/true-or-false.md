# Debt burden and debt relief — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A heavy debt burden costs a country nothing.
**Answer: False** — A heavy debt burden drains a developing country's income into repayments instead of development.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Debt burdens shrink whenever a country borrows more.
**Answer: False** — Debt burdens grow through borrowing, interest and falling export earnings.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Debt relief increases a country's debts.
**Answer: False** — Debt relief cancels or eases a country's debts, freeing resources for development — as Nigeria won relief in 2005.
