# Debt burden and debt relief — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does a heavy debt burden do?
**Answer:** A heavy debt burden drains a developing country's income into repayments instead of development.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How do debt burdens grow?
**Answer:** Debt burdens grow through borrowing, interest and falling export earnings.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What is debt relief?
**Answer:** Debt relief cancels or eases a country's debts, freeing resources for development — as Nigeria won relief in 2005.
