# Debt burden and debt relief

## Do it first

Before you read on, write what you already think this lesson's title means — debt burden and debt relief — in one sentence of your own. Keep it; you will check it against the lesson at the end.

## Sub lesson 1 — The debt burden

A heavy debt burden drains a developing country's income into repayments instead of development.

## Sub lesson 2 — How the burden grows

Debt burdens grow through borrowing, interest and falling export earnings.

## Sub lesson 3 — Debt relief

Debt relief cancels or eases a country's debts, freeing resources for development — as Nigeria won relief in 2005.
