# Hands-on — Tourism in the Bahamian economy

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Lay out ten small stones for ten Bahamian workers. Move more than half of them to one side and say: these work in tourism, directly or indirectly. Say out loud what jobs the moved stones stand for.

## Draw it and check it
Draw two boxes — international tourism and domestic tourism. In each box, write who the visitor is and one thing that visitor pays for. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
