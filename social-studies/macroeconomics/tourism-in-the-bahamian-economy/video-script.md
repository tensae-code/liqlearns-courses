# Video script — Tourism in the Bahamian economy

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Tourism is The Bahamas' number one industry. Over half of the country's workforce works in it, directly or indirectly, and most of the country's revenue comes from it.

The Grade 5 book splits tourism in two: international tourism — visitors coming from abroad, to resorts on the scale of Atlantis — and domestic tourism, Bahamians travelling in their own islands. And the Grade 4 book names the man who made it a year-round business: Sir Stafford Sands. Tourism is not a season in The Bahamas — it is the economy's first pillar, all year round.
