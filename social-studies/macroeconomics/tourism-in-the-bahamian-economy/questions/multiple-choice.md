# Tourism in the Bahamian economy — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Tourism in The Bahamas is —
a) a small side activity  b) the country's number one industry  c) an industry that ended in the 1950s
**Answer: b) the country's number one industry**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Over half the Bahamian workforce is employed in tourism —
a) directly only  b) indirectly only  c) directly or indirectly
**Answer: c) directly or indirectly**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A Bahamian family holidaying on another Bahamian island is an example of —
a) international tourism  b) domestic tourism  c) transshipment
**Answer: b) domestic tourism**
