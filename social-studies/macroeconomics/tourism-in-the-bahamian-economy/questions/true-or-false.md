# Tourism in the Bahamian economy — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Tourism brings in most of The Bahamas' revenue.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Only workers who meet visitors face to face depend on tourism.
**Answer: False — over half the workforce is employed directly or indirectly; the indirect ring never meets a visitor.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Tourism in The Bahamas is only a winter season.
**Answer: False — Sir Stafford Sands made it a year-round business.**
