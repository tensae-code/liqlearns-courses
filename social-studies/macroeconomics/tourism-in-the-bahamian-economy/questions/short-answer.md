# Tourism in the Bahamian economy — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why does the Grade 4 book teach tourism first among the country's industries?
**Answer:** Tourism is The Bahamas' number one industry — most of the country's revenue comes from it, and over half the workforce is employed in it directly or indirectly.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is the difference between direct and indirect tourism employment?
**Answer:** Direct employment is the jobs a visitor sees — the people who work where visitors stay, eat, travel and are entertained. Indirect employment is the jobs behind them, supplying, building for, transporting or serving the tourism trade.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name the two kinds of tourism in the Grade 5 book, with the book's example of the first.
**Answer:** International tourism — visitors from abroad, such as Atlantis-scale resort tourism — and domestic tourism, Bahamians travelling inside their own country.
