# Government revenue and the national budget — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In your own words, what does sub lesson 1 teach about where government revenue comes from?
**Answer:** Before a government can spend, it must take in revenue — the money that flows in to the state, which the book sets out source by source.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In your own words, what does sub lesson 2 teach about what government spending pays for?
**Answer:** Government spending is the other side: the money flowing out, set out by what it is spent on.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In your own words, what does sub lesson 3 teach about the national budget — belanjawan negara?
**Answer:** The Belanjawan Negara, the national budget, is the yearly plan joining the two — revenue coming in and spending going out, planned together for the year.
