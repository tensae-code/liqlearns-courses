# Government revenue and the national budget — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which of these belongs to sub lesson 1 — Where government revenue comes from?
a) the money that flows in to the state  b) a thing from another lesson  c) none of these
**Answer: a) the money that flows in to the state**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which of these belongs to sub lesson 2 — What government spending pays for?
a) what the state's money is spent on  b) a thing from another lesson  c) none of these
**Answer: a) what the state's money is spent on**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which of these belongs to sub lesson 3 — The national budget — Belanjawan Negara?
a) the yearly plan joining revenue and spending  b) a thing from another lesson  c) none of these
**Answer: a) the yearly plan joining revenue and spending**
