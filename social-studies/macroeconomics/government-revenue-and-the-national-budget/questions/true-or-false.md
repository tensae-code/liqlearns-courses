# Government revenue and the national budget — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Before a government can spend, it must take in revenue — the money that flows in to the state, which the book sets out source by source.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Government spending is the other side: the money flowing out, set out by what it is spent on.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The Belanjawan Negara, the national budget, is the yearly plan joining the two — revenue coming in and spending going out, planned together for the year.
**Answer: True**
