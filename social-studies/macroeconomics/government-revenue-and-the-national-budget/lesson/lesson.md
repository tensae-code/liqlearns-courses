# Government revenue and the national budget

## Do it first

## Sub lesson 1 — Where government revenue comes from

Before a government can spend, it must take in **revenue** — the money that flows in to the state, which the book sets out source by source.

## Sub lesson 2 — What government spending pays for

**Government spending** is the other side: the money flowing out, set out by what it is spent on.

## Sub lesson 3 — The national budget — Belanjawan Negara

The **Belanjawan Negara**, the **national budget**, is the **yearly plan joining the two** — revenue coming in and spending going out, planned together for the year.
