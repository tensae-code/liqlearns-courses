# When production and employment move apart

## Do it first

Before you read on, write what you already think this lesson's title means — when production and employment move apart — in one sentence of your own. Keep it; you will check it against the lesson at the end.

## Sub lesson 1 — The tertiary sector came to produce the most

Over **forty years**, the **tertiary (service) sector came to produce the most** of India's output.

## Sub lesson 2 — The primary sector still employs the most

Yet the **primary sector still employs the most people** — production and employment no longer move together.

## Sub lesson 3 — Service production rose faster than service jobs

**Service production rose far faster than service jobs** — output without matching employment is the jobs gap at the heart of India's sector shift.
