# When production and employment move apart — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Over forty years, the sector producing most became —
a) the tertiary sector  b) the primary sector  c) no sector
**Answer: a) the tertiary sector**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The sector employing most people is still —
a) the primary sector  b) the tertiary sector  c) the secondary sector
**Answer: a) the primary sector**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Service production rose —
a) far faster than service jobs  b) slower than service jobs  c) exactly with service jobs
**Answer: a) far faster than service jobs**
