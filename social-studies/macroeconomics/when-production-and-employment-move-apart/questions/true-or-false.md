# When production and employment move apart — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The primary sector still produces the most output.
**Answer: False — the tertiary sector came to produce the most.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Most workers have moved into services.
**Answer: False — the primary sector still employs the most.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Service jobs grew as fast as service production.
**Answer: False — production rose far faster than jobs.**
