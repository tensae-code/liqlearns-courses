# When production and employment move apart — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which sector came to produce the most?
**Answer:** The tertiary (service) sector.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which sector employs the most, even now?
**Answer:** The primary sector.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What is the jobs gap?
**Answer:** Service production rose far faster than the service jobs created.
