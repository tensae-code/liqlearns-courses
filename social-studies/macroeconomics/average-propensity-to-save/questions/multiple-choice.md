# Average propensity to save — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "The average share of income saved"?
a) APS is total saving divided by total income; 500 of 2,000 birr gives 0.25.  b) APS = 1 − APC, because every birr is either consumed or saved.  c) Given either average propensity, the other follows at once: they are two readings of one split of income.
**Answer: a) APS is total saving divided by total income; 500 of 2,000 birr gives 0.25.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "APS = 1 − APC"?
a) Given either average propensity, the other follows at once: they are two readings of one split of income.  b) APS is total saving divided by total income; 500 of 2,000 birr gives 0.25.  c) APS = 1 − APC, because every birr is either consumed or saved.
**Answer: c) APS = 1 − APC, because every birr is either consumed or saved.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Reading the two together"?
a) APS = 1 − APC, because every birr is either consumed or saved.  b) Given either average propensity, the other follows at once: they are two readings of one split of income.  c) APS is total saving divided by total income; 500 of 2,000 birr gives 0.25.
**Answer: b) Given either average propensity, the other follows at once: they are two readings of one split of income.**
