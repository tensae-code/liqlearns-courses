# Average propensity to save — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "The average share of income saved" in your own words.
**Answer:** APS is total saving divided by total income; 500 of 2,000 birr gives 0.25.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "APS = 1 − APC" in your own words.
**Answer:** APS = 1 − APC, because every birr is either consumed or saved.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Reading the two together" in your own words.
**Answer:** Given either average propensity, the other follows at once: they are two readings of one split of income.
