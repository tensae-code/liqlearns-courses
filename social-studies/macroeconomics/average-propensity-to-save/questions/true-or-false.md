# Average propensity to save — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** An APS of 0.25 means the household saves 25 birr.
**Answer: False — APS is a share, not an amount: 0.25 means a quarter of income is saved.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** APC + APS can total more than 1 for a careful household.
**Answer: False — Impossible by construction: each birr is consumed or saved, so the two shares always total 1.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** You need a household's saving figure before you can find its APS if you know its APC.
**Answer: False — No extra figure is needed: APS = 1 − APC, so APC alone fixes APS.**
