# Aggregate demand — C + I + G + (X − M) — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "The four components"?
a) AD = C + I + G + (X − M): consumption, investment, government spending and net exports  b) The AD curve slopes downward against the price level, and a price change moves along it  c) Income and wealth shift C, interest rates and expectations shift I, policy shifts G, exchange rates and foreign income shift net exports
**Answer: a) AD = C + I + G + (X − M): consumption, investment, government spending and net exports**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "The downward slope"?
a) The AD curve slopes downward against the price level, and a price change moves along it  b) Income and wealth shift C, interest rates and expectations shift I, policy shifts G, exchange rates and foreign income shift net exports  c) AD = C + I + G + (X − M): consumption, investment, government spending and net exports
**Answer: a) The AD curve slopes downward against the price level, and a price change moves along it**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Shifters of each component"?
a) Income and wealth shift C, interest rates and expectations shift I, policy shifts G, exchange rates and foreign income shift net exports  b) AD = C + I + G + (X − M): consumption, investment, government spending and net exports  c) The AD curve slopes downward against the price level, and a price change moves along it
**Answer: a) Income and wealth shift C, interest rates and expectations shift I, policy shifts G, exchange rates and foreign income shift net exports**
