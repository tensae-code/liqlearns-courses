# Aggregate demand — C + I + G + (X − M) — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or figure from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In AD = C + I + G + (X − M), the letter G stands for ____ spending.
**Answer: government**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A change in the price level moves the economy ____ the AD curve, not shifting it.
**Answer: along**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Interest rates and expectations are shifters of ____.
**Answer: investment (I)**
