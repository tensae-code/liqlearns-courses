# Aggregate demand — C + I + G + (X − M) — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** AD = C + I + G + (X − M): consumption, investment, government spending and net exports.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The AD curve slopes downward against the price level, and a price change moves along it.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Income and wealth shift C, interest rates and expectations shift I, policy shifts G, exchange rates and foreign income shift net exports.
**Answer: True**
