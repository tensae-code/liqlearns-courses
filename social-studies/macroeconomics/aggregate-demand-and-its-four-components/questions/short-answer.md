# Aggregate demand — C + I + G + (X − M) — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "The four components" in your own words.
**Answer:** AD = C + I + G + (X − M): consumption, investment, government spending and net exports.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "The downward slope" in your own words.
**Answer:** The AD curve slopes downward against the price level, and a price change moves along it.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Shifters of each component" in your own words.
**Answer:** Income and wealth shift C, interest rates and expectations shift I, policy shifts G, exchange rates and foreign income shift net exports.
