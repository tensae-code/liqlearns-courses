# Hands-on — Aggregate demand — C + I + G + (X − M)

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Four tributaries
Draw four streams labelled C, I, G and (X − M) joining one river labelled AD. On each stream write its main shifter.

## Shift or slide
List five events; for each, say whether AD shifts or the economy slides along it, and through which component. Include one pure price-level change as the trap.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
