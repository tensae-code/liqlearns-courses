# Colombia's modernisation internal market and salaried work — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** By the late _____ Colombia entered accelerated economic development, especially of the modern industrial sector;
**Answer: 1920s**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A _____ internal market and a salaried labour market had been created, the working class and labour movements emerged, and the state for the first time had instruments to influence the economy and regulate labour conflicts;
**Answer: significant**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Political voices of the era backed cooperatives, _____ and unions as fairer ways to give workers their proportional share
