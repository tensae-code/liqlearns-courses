Colombia's modernisation internal market and salaried work — as Colombia's book teaches it, says the narrator: By the late 1920s Colombia entered accelerated economic development, especially of the modern industrial sector; A significant internal market and a salaried labour market had been created, the working class and labour movements emerged, and the state for the first time had instruments to influence the economy and regulate labour conflicts; Political voices of the era backed cooperatives, corporativism and unions as fairer ways to give workers their proportional share
