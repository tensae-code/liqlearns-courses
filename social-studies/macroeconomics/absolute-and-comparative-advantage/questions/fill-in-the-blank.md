# Absolute and comparative advantage — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The classical theory of trade is built on cost of _____.
**Answer: production**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Producing a good at lower cost than another country is _____ advantage.
**Answer: absolute**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Specialising where your disadvantage is least follows comparative _____.
**Answer: advantage**
