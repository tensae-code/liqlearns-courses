# Absolute and comparative advantage — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The classical theory says all countries produce everything at the same cost.
**Answer: False** — it is built on cost differences between countries.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Absolute advantage means producing everything at higher cost.
**Answer: False** — it means lower cost than the other country.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A country worse at everything can never gain from trade.
**Answer: False** — comparative advantage shows it gains by specialising where its disadvantage is least.
