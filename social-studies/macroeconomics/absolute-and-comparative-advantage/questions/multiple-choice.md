# Absolute and comparative advantage — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** About the classical theory — built on cost of production — the lesson teaches that —
a) the classical theory builds trade on differences in cost of production  b) absolute advantage is producing a good at lower cost than another country  c) comparative advantage — specialise where your disadvantage is least — brings gains from trade
**Answer: a) the classical theory builds trade on differences in cost of production**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** About absolute advantage — the lesson teaches that —
a) the classical theory builds trade on differences in cost of production  b) absolute advantage is producing a good at lower cost than another country  c) comparative advantage — specialise where your disadvantage is least — brings gains from trade
**Answer: b) absolute advantage is producing a good at lower cost than another country**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** About comparative advantage — and the gains from trade — the lesson teaches that —
a) the classical theory builds trade on differences in cost of production  b) absolute advantage is producing a good at lower cost than another country  c) comparative advantage — specialise where your disadvantage is least — brings gains from trade
**Answer: c) comparative advantage — specialise where your disadvantage is least — brings gains from trade**
