# Absolute and comparative advantage — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the classical theory of trade built on?
**Answer:** Differences in the cost of production between countries.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is absolute advantage?
**Answer:** Producing a good at lower cost than another country can.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What is comparative advantage?
**Answer:** A country gains by specialising where its cost disadvantage is least (or its advantage greatest), even if it is worse at everything.
