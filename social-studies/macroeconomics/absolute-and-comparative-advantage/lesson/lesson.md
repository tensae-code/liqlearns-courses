## Sub lesson 1 — The classical theory — built on cost of production

The Class 12 trade chapter builds the **classical theory of international trade** on **cost of production**: countries trade because the same good costs different amounts of labour and resources in different places.

## Sub lesson 2 — Absolute advantage

A country has an **absolute advantage** in a good when it can produce it at lower cost than another country. The book works the idea in **two-country tables**: each country specialises where its cost is absolutely lower, and both gain.

## Sub lesson 3 — Comparative advantage — and the gains from trade

The deeper law is **comparative advantage** (comparative cost): even a country worse at everything gains by specialising where its disadvantage is least. The book shows the **gains from international trade** in its tables and draws the **production possibility curve** behind them.
