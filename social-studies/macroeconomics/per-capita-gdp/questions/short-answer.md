# Per capita GDP — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Dividing by population" in your own words.
**Answer:** Per capita GDP is GDP divided by population.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "What the average is for" in your own words.
**Answer:** Per capita GDP allows countries of different sizes to be compared person against person.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Market price versus factor cost" in your own words.
**Answer:** GDP at market price equals factor cost plus net indirect taxes — indirect taxes minus subsidies.
