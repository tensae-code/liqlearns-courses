# Per capita GDP — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Dividing by population"?
a) per capita GDP = GDP / population, in nominal or real form  b) averages allow size-free comparison and track progress despite population growth  c) GDP at market price = factor cost + (indirect taxes − subsidies)
**Answer: a) per capita GDP = GDP / population, in nominal or real form**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "What the average is for"?
a) per capita GDP = GDP / population, in nominal or real form  b) GDP at market price = factor cost + (indirect taxes − subsidies)  c) averages allow size-free comparison and track progress despite population growth
**Answer: c) averages allow size-free comparison and track progress despite population growth**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Market price versus factor cost"?
a) averages allow size-free comparison and track progress despite population growth  b) GDP at market price = factor cost + (indirect taxes − subsidies)  c) per capita GDP = GDP / population, in nominal or real form
**Answer: b) GDP at market price = factor cost + (indirect taxes − subsidies)**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** GDP is 110 billion birr and population is 110 million. What is per capita GDP?
a) 1,000 birr  b) 110 birr  c) 1 birr
**Answer: a) 1,000 birr** — Per capita GDP = 110 billion / 110 million = 1,000 birr per person.
