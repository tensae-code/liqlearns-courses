# Per capita GDP — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Per capita GDP divides GDP by _____ to give the average income per person.
**Answer: population**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Per capita figures compare countries _____ against person, whatever their sizes.
**Answer: per person**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** GDP at market price equals factor cost plus _____ — indirect taxes minus subsidies.
**Answer: net indirect taxes**
