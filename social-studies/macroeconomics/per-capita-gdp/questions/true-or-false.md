# Per capita GDP — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Per capita GDP is GDP divided by population.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Per capita GDP allows countries of different sizes to be compared person against person.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** GDP at market price equals factor cost plus net indirect taxes — indirect taxes minus subsidies.
**Answer: True**
