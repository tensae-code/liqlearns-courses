# Hands-on — Per capita GDP

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Share one big pile of counters equally among a row of learners: that share is per capita. Add more learners without adding counters and reshare — everyone watches the average fall while the total stands still.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — one GDP total being shared out across a crowd of figures, one equal slice each — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
