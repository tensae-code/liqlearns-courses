# Hands-on — What GDP misses

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Deal $5,000,000 in paper notes two ways to ten learners — evenly, then all to one. The class votes which village it would rather join, then names what GDP could not see in the difference.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — two villages with the same GDP per person drawn very unequally — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.

## Grade 11 — say it with things at home
Take the newest part of this lesson and act it out with things at home — objects, counters, or a partner — saying each key term aloud as you use it. Then teach the part to your partner without the lesson open.

## The Grade 12 layer — the developing-country misses
List the five developing-country misses from sub lesson 4 down a page. Beside each, write one example from a real household or village you know, and mark which of the earlier sub lessons' misses it extends.
