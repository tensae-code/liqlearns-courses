# Lesson plan — What GDP misses

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Macroeconomics · **Lesson:** What GDP misses
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 6 — Economic Growth of the Grade 10 Economics course (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** GDP omits leisure, home production, volunteer work, environmental quality, distribution and black-market activity — good for most purposes, not all.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the lessons before it in Macroeconomics, and on the earlier-grade lessons of this course. Grade 10 stacks on the Grade 9 Economics foundations in the same titles.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of ..." in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.
