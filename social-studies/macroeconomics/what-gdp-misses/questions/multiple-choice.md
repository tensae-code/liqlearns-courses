# What GDP misses — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "The unpaid and the uncounted"?
a) leisure, home production and volunteer work excluded from GDP  b) environmental quality and black-market activity also missed  c) the same average from equal sharing or from 10 rich and 90 with nothing
**Answer: a) leisure, home production and volunteer work excluded from GDP**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Environment and the underground"?
a) leisure, home production and volunteer work excluded from GDP  b) the same average from equal sharing or from 10 rich and 90 with nothing  c) environmental quality and black-market activity also missed
**Answer: c) environmental quality and black-market activity also missed**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Distribution — the same average, opposite lives"?
a) environmental quality and black-market activity also missed  b) the same average from equal sharing or from 10 rich and 90 with nothing  c) leisure, home production and volunteer work excluded from GDP
**Answer: b) the same average from equal sharing or from 10 rich and 90 with nothing**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Which statement best matches "Grade 11 — the measurement problems in full"?
a) double counting of resales, the informal sector, transfers, untraded output and unmeasured quality  b) register used-item services only, exclude transfers, estimate informal and own-consumption activity in  c) the opposite claim to the one this sub lesson teaches
**Answer: a) double counting of resales, the informal sector, transfers, untraded output and unmeasured quality**

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** Which statement best matches "Grade 11 — the fixes the book prescribes"?
a) double counting of resales, the informal sector, transfers, untraded output and unmeasured quality  b) the opposite claim to the one this sub lesson teaches  c) register used-item services only, exclude transfers, estimate informal and own-consumption activity in
**Answer: c) register used-item services only, exclude transfers, estimate informal and own-consumption activity in**

**6. (Sub lesson 4)** Which statement best matches "Why GDP understates output in developing countries"?
a) GDP understates output in poor countries because they export too little  b) In developing countries GDP misses subsistence output, underreported production, the non-monetary sector, price distortion and pollution costs  c) GDP already deducts pollution costs in developing economies
**Answer: b) In developing countries GDP misses subsistence output, underreported production, the non-monetary sector, price distortion and pollution costs**
