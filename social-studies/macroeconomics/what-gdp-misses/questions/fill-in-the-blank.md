# What GDP misses — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The chef cooking at home adds nothing to GDP, because _____ is excluded.
**Answer: home production**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Output can rise while air and water worsen, because GDP misses _____.
**Answer: environmental quality**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Both villages in the lesson total _____ of income — the same, by two opposite distributions.
**Answer: $5,000,000**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Street trade, home repairs and unpaid family work belong to the _____ sector that GDP counting easily misses.
**Answer: informal**

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** One prescribed fix is _____ informal and own-consumption activity into GDP instead of leaving it at zero.
**Answer: estimating**

**6. (Sub lesson 6)** Producers may underreport output for fear of ____.
**Answer: tax**
