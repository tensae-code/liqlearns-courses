# What GDP misses — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** GDP excludes non-market home production — the chef cooking at home adds nothing to it.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** GDP misses environmental quality and black-market activity.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The same GDP per person can come from 100 people earning $50,000 or 10 earning $500,000 and 90 earning nothing.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** GDP measurement problems include the large informal sector of a developing country like Ethiopia — street trade, home repairs and unpaid family work.
**Answer: True**

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** The book's remedies include estimating informal and own-consumption activity into GDP.
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 4)** In developing countries GDP misses subsistence output, underreported production, the non-monetary sector, price distortion and pollution costs.
**Answer: True**
