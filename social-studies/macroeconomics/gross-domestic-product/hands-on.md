# Hands-on — Gross domestic product

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Mark a border line on the floor with string. Partners hold product cards; step inside the line if the production belongs in this country's GDP, outside if not — owner does not matter, place does.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — a map outline with factories, farms and services inside it all feeding one GDP total — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
