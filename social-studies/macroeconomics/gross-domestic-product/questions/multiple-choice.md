# Gross domestic product — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "The definition"?
a) the market value of all final goods and services produced within the borders in a year  b) place decides: foreign-owned production inside the border counts in GDP  c) GDP equally equals total income earned or total expenditure on output
**Answer: a) the market value of all final goods and services produced within the borders in a year**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Inside the border — whoever owns it"?
a) the market value of all final goods and services produced within the borders in a year  b) GDP equally equals total income earned or total expenditure on output  c) place decides: foreign-owned production inside the border counts in GDP
**Answer: c) place decides: foreign-owned production inside the border counts in GDP**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "One total, three faces"?
a) place decides: foreign-owned production inside the border counts in GDP  b) GDP equally equals total income earned or total expenditure on output  c) the market value of all final goods and services produced within the borders in a year
**Answer: b) GDP equally equals total income earned or total expenditure on output**
