# Gross domestic product — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "The definition" in your own words.
**Answer:** GDP is the market value of all final goods and services produced within a country's borders in a year.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Inside the border — whoever owns it" in your own words.
**Answer:** Foreign-owned production inside a country's borders counts in that country's GDP.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "One total, three faces" in your own words.
**Answer:** GDP equally equals the total income earned in the economy or the total expenditure on its output.
