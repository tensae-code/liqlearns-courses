# Gross domestic product — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** GDP counts only _____ goods and services, so intermediate stages are not counted twice.
**Answer: final**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** GDP counts production within a country's _____, whoever owns the resources.
**Answer: borders**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** GDP also equals the total _____ earned in the economy — every birr spent becomes someone's earnings.
**Answer: income**
