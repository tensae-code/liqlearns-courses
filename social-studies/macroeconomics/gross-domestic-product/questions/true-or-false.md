# Gross domestic product — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** GDP is the market value of all final goods and services produced within a country's borders in a year.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Foreign-owned production inside a country's borders counts in that country's GDP.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** GDP equally equals the total income earned in the economy or the total expenditure on its output.
**Answer: True**
