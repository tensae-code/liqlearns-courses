# Video script — Poverty in the community — causes, effects and ways out

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Poverty is not having enough money for food, clothes or school.

Its causes are unemployment, limited access to education, natural disasters and civil wars. Its effects are poor health and malnutrition, school dropouts, early marriages and crime.

And it can be reduced: by creating jobs, by income-generating projects, and by access to education — each way out answering one of the causes.
