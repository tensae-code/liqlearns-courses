# Lesson plan — Poverty in the community — causes, effects and ways out

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Macroeconomics · **Lesson:** Poverty in the community — causes, effects and ways out
**Place in the curriculum:** Macroeconomics (CURRICULUM.md) — built in the Economics expansion (Zimbabwe, 2026-11-16).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Poverty is not having enough money for food, clothes or school; it has named causes, it does named harm, and a community reduces it in named ways — jobs, income-generating projects, and access to education.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the learner's own community as Zimbabwe's Grade 5 guide reads it — the family without enough for food, clothes or school fees, and the projects and schooling that lift it out.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of ..." in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.
