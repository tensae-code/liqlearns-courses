# Hands-on — Poverty in the community — causes, effects and ways out

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Sort the lists
Write the unit's twelve items — the four causes, the four effects, the three ways out, and the definition — on slips. Sort them into three piles from memory, then check against lesson/lesson.md and fix any slip in the wrong pile.

## Draw it and check it
Draw poverty as a chain in three boxes — causes, poverty itself, effects — with an arrow out of the last box labelled with the three ways out. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare, and fix every item you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
