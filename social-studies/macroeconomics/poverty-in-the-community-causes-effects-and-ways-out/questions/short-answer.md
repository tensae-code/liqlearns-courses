# Poverty in the community — causes, effects and ways out — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How does the Grade 5 unit define poverty?
**Answer:** Poverty is not having enough money for food, clothes or school.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the four causes and the four effects of poverty the unit lists.
**Answer:** Causes: unemployment, limited access to education, natural disasters, civil wars. Effects: poor health and malnutrition, school dropouts, early marriages, crime.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** How does each way out answer a cause?
**Answer:** Creating jobs answers unemployment; income-generating projects give a family income where no job exists; access to education answers the limited access to education that helped cause the poverty.
