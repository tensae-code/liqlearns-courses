# Poverty in the community — causes, effects and ways out — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Limited access to education is one of poverty's causes.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Poor health and malnutrition are causes of poverty in the unit's list.
**Answer: False — they are effects of poverty; the causes are unemployment, limited access to education, natural disasters and civil wars.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** An income-generating project is one of the ways of reducing poverty.
**Answer: True**
