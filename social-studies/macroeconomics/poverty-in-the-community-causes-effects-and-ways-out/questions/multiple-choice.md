# Poverty in the community — causes, effects and ways out — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Poverty, as Grade 5 defines it, is —
a) having no money at all, ever  b) not having enough money for food, clothes or school  c) living in the countryside
**Answer: b) not having enough money for food, clothes or school**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which of these is a cause of poverty in the unit?
a) school dropouts  b) early marriages  c) unemployment
**Answer: c) unemployment**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which set names only ways of reducing poverty?
a) creating jobs, income-generating projects, access to education  b) crime, malnutrition, dropouts  c) natural disasters, civil wars, unemployment
**Answer: a) creating jobs, income-generating projects, access to education**
