# Poverty in the community — causes, effects and ways out — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Poverty is not having enough money for food, clothes or _____.
**Answer: school**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Two effects of poverty are poor health and _____, and school dropouts.
**Answer: malnutrition**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Poverty is reduced by creating jobs, income-generating projects, and access to _____.
**Answer: education**
