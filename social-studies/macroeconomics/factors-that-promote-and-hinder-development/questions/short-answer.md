# Factors that promote and hinder development — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In your own words, what does sub lesson 1 teach about development and what promotes it?
**Answer:** Development is improving people's lives and surroundings. Trade, agriculture and tourism promote it.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In your own words, what does sub lesson 2 teach about what hinders development — capital, transport, education and land?
**Answer:** Development is hindered by inadequate capital, poor transport networks, low education and inadequate land.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In your own words, what does sub lesson 3 teach about what hinders development — population growth and civil war?
**Answer:** Development is also hindered by high population growth and civil war.
