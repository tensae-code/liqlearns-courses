# Factors that promote and hinder development — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Development is improving people's lives and surroundings.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Development is hindered by inadequate capital, poor transport networks, low education and inadequate land.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Development is also hindered by high population growth and civil war.
**Answer: True**
