# Video script — Factors that promote and hinder development

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Factors that promote and hinder development — as Rwanda's book teaches it, says the narrator: Development is improving people's lives and surroundings. Trade, agriculture and tourism promote it. Development is hindered by inadequate capital, poor transport networks, low education and inadequate land. Development is also hindered by high population growth and civil war.
