# Factors that promote and hinder development

## Do it first

Before you read on, write what you already think this lesson's title means — factors that promote and hinder development — in one sentence of your own. Keep it; you will check it against the lesson at the end.

## Sub lesson 1 — Development and what promotes it

**Development** is **improving people's lives and surroundings**. **Trade, agriculture and tourism promote** it.

## Sub lesson 2 — What hinders development — capital, transport, education and land

Development is hindered by **inadequate capital**, **poor transport networks**, **low education** and **inadequate land**.

## Sub lesson 3 — What hinders development — population growth and civil war

Development is also hindered by **high population growth** and **civil war**.
