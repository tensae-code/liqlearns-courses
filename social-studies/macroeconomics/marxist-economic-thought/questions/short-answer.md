# Marxist economic thought — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Who are the school's named thinkers in this lesson?
**Answer: Marx, Engels and Lenin.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** From what split does the school explain capitalism's crises?
**Answer: The split between owners and workers, whose interests are opposed.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Why does Cambodia's book teach this school whole?
**Answer: Because it shaped the century Cambodia lived through — pupils need it whether they accept it or not.**
