# Marxist economic thought — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Marxist thought begins as —
a) a praise of every factory owner  b) a critique of the capitalist system  c) a theory with nothing to say about workers
**Answer: b) a critique of the capitalist system**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In the Marxist account, who holds the means of production?
a) the owners  b) nobody — they hold themselves  c) the workers, all of them equally
**Answer: a) the owners**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Marxist thought argues capitalism must change into a system where —
a) production stops altogether  b) one owner holds everything forever  c) the means of production are held in common
**Answer: c) the means of production are held in common**
