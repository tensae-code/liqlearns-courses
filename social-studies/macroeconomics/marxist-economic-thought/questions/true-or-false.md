# Marxist economic thought — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Marxist thought begins as a critique of capitalism.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In the Marxist account, the workers, all of them equally, hold the means of production.
**Answer: False — In the Marxist account the owners hold the means of production.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The school argues for the means of production held in common.
**Answer: True**
