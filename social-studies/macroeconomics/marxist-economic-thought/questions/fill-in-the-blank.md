# Marxist economic thought — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Marx and Engels argued capitalism's wealth rests on the _____ of workers.
**Answer: labour**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Workers sell their _____, and owners hold the means of production.
**Answer: labour**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** _____ carried the Marxist argument into the age of empires.
**Answer: Lenin**
