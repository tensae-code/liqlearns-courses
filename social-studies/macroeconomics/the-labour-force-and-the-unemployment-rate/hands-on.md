# Hands-on — The labour force and the unemployment rate

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Count your compound
List ten adults you know by category — working, seeking, studying, retired. Build the labour force and compute the unemployment rate for your small sample, showing the denominator you used.

## Spot the error
A newspaper divides the unemployed by the whole population. Explain in two sentences why its rate is wrong and what it should have divided by.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
