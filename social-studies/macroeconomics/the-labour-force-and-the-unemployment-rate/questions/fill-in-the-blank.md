# The labour force and the unemployment rate — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or figure from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The minimum age for formal employment in the book's frame is ____.
**Answer: 18**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The employed plus those seeking work make up the ____ force.
**Answer: labour**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The unemployment rate divides the unemployed by the ____.
**Answer: labour force**
