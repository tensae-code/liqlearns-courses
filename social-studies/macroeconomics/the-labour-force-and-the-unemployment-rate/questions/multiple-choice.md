# The labour force and the unemployment rate — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Who counts — the working-age frame"?
a) Ethiopia's working-age band is given as 14–60 years, with 18 the minimum age for formal employment  b) The labour force is the employed plus those seeking work  c) The unemployment rate is the unemployed divided by the labour force, times 100
**Answer: a) Ethiopia's working-age band is given as 14–60 years, with 18 the minimum age for formal employment**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "The labour force"?
a) The labour force is the employed plus those seeking work  b) The unemployment rate is the unemployed divided by the labour force, times 100  c) Ethiopia's working-age band is given as 14–60 years, with 18 the minimum age for formal employment
**Answer: a) The labour force is the employed plus those seeking work**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "The unemployment rate"?
a) The unemployment rate is the unemployed divided by the labour force, times 100  b) Ethiopia's working-age band is given as 14–60 years, with 18 the minimum age for formal employment  c) The labour force is the employed plus those seeking work
**Answer: a) The unemployment rate is the unemployed divided by the labour force, times 100**
