# The labour force and the unemployment rate — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Ethiopia's working-age band is given as 14–60 years, with 18 the minimum age for formal employment.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The labour force is the employed plus those seeking work.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The unemployment rate is the unemployed divided by the labour force, times 100.
**Answer: True**
