# The labour force and the unemployment rate — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Who counts — the working-age frame" in your own words.
**Answer:** Ethiopia's working-age band is given as 14–60 years, with 18 the minimum age for formal employment.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "The labour force" in your own words.
**Answer:** The labour force is the employed plus those seeking work.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "The unemployment rate" in your own words.
**Answer:** The unemployment rate is the unemployed divided by the labour force, times 100.
