# Hands-on — The transition to capitalism in the Dominican Republic

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Act the credit company: one person is the government needing money, one the port merchant. Agree a loan of 100 'pesos' at 30% for a year and count out what must be repaid; then say where the merchant's profit goes next — a cattle farm, cacao, a sugar mill.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the chain: port merchants → credit companies (24–36%) → government loans and profits → cattle farms, cacao and sugar mills → sugar in the capitalist sector, cacao and coffee for the peasantry. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare, and fix every link and figure you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
