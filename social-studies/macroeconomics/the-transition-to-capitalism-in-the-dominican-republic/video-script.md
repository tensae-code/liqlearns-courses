# Video script — The transition to capitalism in the Dominican Republic

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

How does capitalism arrive? From the late 1870s, the Dominican liberal experiment set out to develop the productive forces and sell more to the world through new export lines. The money came from merchants: governments borrowed instead of issuing notes, and port merchant elites formed credit companies charging 24 to 36 percent a year. Their profits went into cattle farms, cacao and sugar mills. Sugar stood out in the capitalist sector — and cacao and coffee carried the peasantry into the market economy.
