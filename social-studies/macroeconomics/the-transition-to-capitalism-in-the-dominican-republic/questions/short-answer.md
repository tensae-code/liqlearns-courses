# The transition to capitalism in the Dominican Republic — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What did the liberal experiment from the late 1870s rest on?
**Answer:** On developing the productive forces and inserting the country further into international transactions through new export lines.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How did the merchant credit companies work, and where did their profits go?
**Answer:** Governments borrowed from merchants instead of issuing notes; the port merchant elites formed credit companies (compañías de crédito) charging exorbitant interest of 24–36% a year, and their profits were invested in productive activities such as cattle farms, cacao and sugar mills.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which crops stood out in the capitalist sector, and which marked the peasantry's entry into the market economy?
**Answer:** Sugar production stood out in the capitalist sector; cacao and coffee marked the peasantry's entry into the market economy.
