# The transition to capitalism in the Dominican Republic — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** From the late 1870s, the liberal experiment rested on —
a) developing the productive forces and inserting the country further into international transactions through new export lines  b) closing the ports to foreign trade  c) issuing paper notes without limit
**Answer: a) developing the productive forces and inserting the country further into international transactions through new export lines**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The port merchant elites formed credit companies charging interest of —
a) 2–3% a year  b) 24–36% a year  c) no interest at all
**Answer: b) 24–36% a year**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Cacao and coffee marked —
a) the end of the peasantry  b) the peasantry's entry into the market economy  c) the sugar sector's decline
**Answer: b) the peasantry's entry into the market economy**
