# The transition to capitalism in the Dominican Republic — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Governments of the liberal experiment issued their own notes instead of borrowing.
**Answer: False** — governments borrowed from merchants instead of issuing notes

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The credit companies' profits were invested in productive activities such as cattle farms, cacao and sugar mills.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Sugar production stood out in the peasant sector.
**Answer: False** — sugar production stood out in the capitalist sector; cacao and coffee marked the peasantry's entry into the market economy
