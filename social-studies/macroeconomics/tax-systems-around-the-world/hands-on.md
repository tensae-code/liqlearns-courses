# Hands-on — Tax systems around the world

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Draw the US pie
Draw the US federal pie with its three given shares (41.6, 34.8, 15.1) and a remainder slice of 8.5% for all other revenue. Check the slices sum to 100.

## Compare a third pie
Sketch Ethiopia's likely pie from what you already know of Schedules and VAT in this unit, marking every slice 'estimate'. Beside it, list what evidence would turn estimates into facts.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
