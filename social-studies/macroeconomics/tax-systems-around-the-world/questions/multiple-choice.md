# Tax systems around the world — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "The United Kingdom's list"?
a) The UK list includes 20% basic-rate income tax, national insurance, VAT at 17.5% and sin taxes  b) US federal revenue: 41.6% individual income, 34.8% payroll, 15.1% corporate  c) Every state assembles the same tax families in its own proportions
