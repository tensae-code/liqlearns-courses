# Tax systems around the world — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The UK list includes 20% basic-rate income tax, national insurance, VAT at 17.5% and sin taxes.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** US federal revenue: 41.6% individual income, 34.8% payroll, 15.1% corporate.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Every state assembles the same tax families in its own proportions.
**Answer: True**
