# Tax systems around the world — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "The United Kingdom's list" in your own words.
**Answer:** The UK list includes 20% basic-rate income tax, national insurance, VAT at 17.5% and sin taxes.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "The United States' five types" in your own words.
**Answer:** US federal revenue: 41.6% individual income, 34.8% payroll, 15.1% corporate.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Two pies, one lesson" in your own words.
**Answer:** Every state assembles the same tax families in its own proportions.
