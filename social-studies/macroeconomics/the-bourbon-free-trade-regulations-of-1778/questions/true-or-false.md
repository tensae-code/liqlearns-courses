# The Bourbon free-trade regulations of 1778 — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Bourbon reforms began by cutting all colonial taxes.
**Answer: False** — they raised production taxes first — that rise fuelled the rebellions.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The 1778 Reglamento closed the American ports to all trade.
**Answer: False** — it opened 13 metropolitan and 22 American ports.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Register ships traded with no inspection at all.
**Answer: False** — they traded under the Casa de Contratación's inspection.
