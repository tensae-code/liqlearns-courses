# The Bourbon free-trade regulations of 1778 — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the lesson teach about first, reforms that raised the taxes?
**Answer:** The Bourbon reforms first raised production taxes, fuelling rebellions, before loosening the trade monopoly.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the lesson teach about the 1778 Reglamento — 13 ports and 22 ports?
**Answer:** The 1778 Reglamento de Comercio Libre opened 13 metropolitan and 22 American ports.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the lesson teach about register ships replace the fleet and galleons?
**Answer:** The fleet-and-galleon system was replaced by licensed register ships under the Casa de Contratación's inspection.
