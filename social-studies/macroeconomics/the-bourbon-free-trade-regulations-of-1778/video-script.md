The Bourbon free-trade regulations of 1778 — as Bolivia's book teaches it, says the narrator: the Bourbon reforms first raised production taxes, fuelling rebellions, before loosening the trade monopoly; the 1778 Reglamento de Comercio Libre opened 13 metropolitan and 22 American ports; the fleet-and-galleon system was replaced by licensed register ships under the Casa de Contratación's inspection.
