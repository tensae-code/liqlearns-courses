# Imported Inflation — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Sri Lanka's 1972–74 imported inflation.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Rice up from Rs 544 to Rs 2,685 a ton.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** This lesson teaches that its subject has nothing to do with imported Inflation.
**Answer: False — The lesson is built on Sri Lanka's book teaching and the Colombo index climbing from 138.2 to 185.8.**
