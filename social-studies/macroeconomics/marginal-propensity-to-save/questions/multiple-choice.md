# Marginal propensity to save — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "The share of each extra birr that is saved"?
a) MPS is the change in saving per unit change in income, between 0 and 1.  b) MPS = 1 − MPC, because each extra birr is either consumed or saved.  c) MPS rises with income — the rich save a larger share of each extra birr.
**Answer: a) MPS is the change in saving per unit change in income, between 0 and 1.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "MPS = 1 − MPC"?
a) MPS rises with income — the rich save a larger share of each extra birr.  b) MPS is the change in saving per unit change in income, between 0 and 1.  c) MPS = 1 − MPC, because each extra birr is either consumed or saved.
**Answer: c) MPS = 1 − MPC, because each extra birr is either consumed or saved.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "MPS rises with income"?
a) MPS = 1 − MPC, because each extra birr is either consumed or saved.  b) MPS rises with income — the rich save a larger share of each extra birr.  c) MPS is the change in saving per unit change in income, between 0 and 1.
**Answer: b) MPS rises with income — the rich save a larger share of each extra birr.**
