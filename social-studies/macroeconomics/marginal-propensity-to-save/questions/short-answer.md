# Marginal propensity to save — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "The share of each extra birr that is saved" in your own words.
**Answer:** MPS is the change in saving per unit change in income, between 0 and 1.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "MPS = 1 − MPC" in your own words.
**Answer:** MPS = 1 − MPC, because each extra birr is either consumed or saved.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "MPS rises with income" in your own words.
**Answer:** MPS rises with income — the rich save a larger share of each extra birr.
