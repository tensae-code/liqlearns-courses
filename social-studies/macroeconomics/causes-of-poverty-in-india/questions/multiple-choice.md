# Causes of poverty in India — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Colonial policies —
a) ruined the handicrafts  b) built the handicrafts  c) had no effect on crafts
**Answer: a) ruined the handicrafts**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Poverty persisted partly because of —
a) low growth beside high population growth  b) high growth beside falling population  c) no population growth at all
**Answer: a) low growth beside high population growth**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Indebtedness is —
a) both a cause and an effect of poverty  b) only an effect  c) unrelated to poverty
**Answer: a) both a cause and an effect of poverty**
