# Causes of poverty in India — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Colonial rule strengthened India's handicrafts.
**Answer: False — colonial policies ruined them.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** India's early growth was high and population growth low.
**Answer: False — growth was low and population growth high.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Indebtedness is only an effect of poverty, never a cause.
**Answer: False — it is both cause and effect.**
