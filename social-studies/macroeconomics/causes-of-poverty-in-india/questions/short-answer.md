# Causes of poverty in India — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What did colonial policies do to the handicrafts?
**Answer:** They ruined them.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How did growth and population combine to keep poverty?
**Answer:** Growth was low while population growth was high.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name two more causes of poverty in India.
**Answer:** Unequal distribution of land and resources, and indebtedness.
