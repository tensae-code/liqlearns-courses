The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Causes of poverty in India — as India's book teaches it, says the narrator: why poverty persisted in India — colonial policies that ruined handicrafts, decades of low growth beside high population growth, unequal distribution of land and resources, and indebtedness that is both cause and effect of poverty.
