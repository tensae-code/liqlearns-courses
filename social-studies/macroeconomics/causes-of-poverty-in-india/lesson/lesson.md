# Causes of poverty in India

## Do it first

## Sub lesson 1 — Colonial policies ruined the handicrafts

**Colonial policies ruined India's handicrafts** — the crafts that had employed many people were destroyed under colonial rule.

## Sub lesson 2 — Low growth beside high population growth

For decades India had **low economic growth beside high population growth** — output grew slowly while the number of people grew fast, so poverty persisted.

## Sub lesson 3 — Unequal land and resources; debt as cause and effect

Poverty also comes from the **unequal distribution of land and resources**, and from **indebtedness, which is both a cause and an effect of poverty** — the poor borrow because they are poor, and borrowing keeps them poor.
