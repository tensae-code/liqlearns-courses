# Public debt and the debt-to-GDP ratio — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Public debt is —
a) one year's budget deficit  b) the total a government owes from its borrowing  c) a tax paid at customs
**Answer: b) the total a government owes from its borrowing**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The debt-to-GDP ratio weighs a government's debt against —
a) its population  b) everything the economy produces in a year  c) its exports only
**Answer: b) everything the economy produces in a year**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Per-capita debt is the total debt divided by —
a) the number of taxpayers  b) the population  c) the number of banks
**Answer: b) the population**
