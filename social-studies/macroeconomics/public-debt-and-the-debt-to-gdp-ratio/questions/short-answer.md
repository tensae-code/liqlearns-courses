# Public debt and the debt-to-GDP ratio — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is public debt?
**Answer:** The total a government owes from its borrowing, read as one of the whole-economy indicators.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the debt-to-GDP ratio show?
**Answer:** How heavy the debt is, by weighing what the government owes against everything the economy produces in a year.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Why does the book's exercise use three measures together?
**Answer:** So countries of different sizes can be compared fairly — the total, the debt-to-GDP ratio and per-capita debt together show which debt really weighs heaviest.
