# Public debt and the debt-to-GDP ratio — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Public debt is the same thing as one year's budget deficit.
**Answer: False** — The deficit is one year's gap; public debt is the total owed from borrowing.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A debt total on its own says how heavy a country's debt is.
**Answer: False** — It must be weighed against the economy (the debt-to-GDP ratio) and the population (per-capita debt).

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In the book's figure, Honduras's debt was 40.16% of GDP in 2018.
**Answer: True**
