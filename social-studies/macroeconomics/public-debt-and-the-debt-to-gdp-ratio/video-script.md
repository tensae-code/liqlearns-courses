# Video script — Public debt and the debt-to-GDP ratio

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Public debt is the total a government owes — and a total alone does not tell you how heavy it is.

Weigh the debt against the whole economy and you get the debt-to-GDP ratio. Honduras's book gives its own figure: 40.16% of GDP in 2018.

Divide the debt by the people and you get per-capita debt. With the three measures together, countries of very different sizes can be compared fairly.
