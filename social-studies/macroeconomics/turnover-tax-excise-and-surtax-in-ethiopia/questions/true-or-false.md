# Turnover tax, excise and surtax in Ethiopia — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Turnover tax catches traders below the 500,000 birr VAT threshold, at 2% or 10%.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Excise covers 378 goods in 19 groups at rates from 5% to 500%.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Surtax adds 10% on imports, exempting fertilizers, petroleum, vehicles, aircraft and capital goods.
**Answer: True**
