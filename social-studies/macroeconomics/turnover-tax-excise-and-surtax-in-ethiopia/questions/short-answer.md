# Turnover tax, excise and surtax in Ethiopia — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "TOT — for those outside VAT" in your own words.
**Answer:** Turnover tax catches traders below the 500,000 birr VAT threshold, at 2% or 10%.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Excise — on luxuries and harms" in your own words.
**Answer:** Excise covers 378 goods in 19 groups at rates from 5% to 500%.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Surtax — 10% on imports" in your own words.
**Answer:** Surtax adds 10% on imports, exempting fertilizers, petroleum, vehicles, aircraft and capital goods.
