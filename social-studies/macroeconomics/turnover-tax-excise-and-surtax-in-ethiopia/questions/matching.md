# Turnover tax, excise and surtax in Ethiopia — matching

Match each part to its right statement.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** TOT — for those outside VAT — c
**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Excise — on luxuries and harms — a
**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Surtax — 10% on imports — b

Statements:
a) Excise covers 378 goods in 19 groups at rates from 5% to 500%
b) Surtax adds 10% on imports, exempting fertilizers, petroleum, vehicles, aircraft and capital goods
c) Turnover tax catches traders below the 500,000 birr VAT threshold, at 2% or 10%

**Answer key:** 1 — c; 2 — a; 3 — b
