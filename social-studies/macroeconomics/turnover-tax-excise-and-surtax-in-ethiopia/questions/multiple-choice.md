# Turnover tax, excise and surtax in Ethiopia — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "TOT — for those outside VAT"?
a) Turnover tax catches traders below the 500,000 birr VAT threshold, at 2% or 10%  b) Excise covers 378 goods in 19 groups at rates from 5% to 500%  c) Surtax adds 10% on imports, exempting fertilizers, petroleum, vehicles, aircraft and capital goods
**Answer: a) Turnover tax catches traders below the 500,000 birr VAT threshold, at 2% or 10%**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Excise — on luxuries and harms"?
a) Excise covers 378 goods in 19 groups at rates from 5% to 500%  b) Surtax adds 10% on imports, exempting fertilizers, petroleum, vehicles, aircraft and capital goods  c) Turnover tax catches traders below the 500,000 birr VAT threshold, at 2% or 10%
**Answer: a) Excise covers 378 goods in 19 groups at rates from 5% to 500%**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Surtax — 10% on imports"?
a) Surtax adds 10% on imports, exempting fertilizers, petroleum, vehicles, aircraft and capital goods  b) Turnover tax catches traders below the 500,000 birr VAT threshold, at 2% or 10%  c) Excise covers 378 goods in 19 groups at rates from 5% to 500%
**Answer: a) Surtax adds 10% on imports, exempting fertilizers, petroleum, vehicles, aircraft and capital goods**
