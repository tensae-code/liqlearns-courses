# Hands-on — Turnover tax, excise and surtax in Ethiopia

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Three stamps
Draw a crate of imports with three customs stamps — TOT 2%/10%, EXCISE, SURTAX 10% — and a note under each saying who pays it and when.

## Which tax catches it
Six cases (a small café's services, a big importer's cigarettes, imported fertilizer, a grain mill, a luxury car, a local tractor): name which of TOT, excise and surtax touch each case, or none, with rates where the lesson gives them.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
