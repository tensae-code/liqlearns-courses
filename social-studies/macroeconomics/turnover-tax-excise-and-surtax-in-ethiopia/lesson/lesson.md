# Turnover tax, excise and surtax in Ethiopia

## Do it first

VAT catches the big traders. Who catches the small ones — and what extra tax falls on a crate of imported cigarettes, beyond everything else?

## Sub lesson 1 — TOT — for those outside VAT

Turnover tax catches those outside VAT: registration for VAT is limited to traders above 500,000 birr of annual transactions. TOT runs at 2% for contractors, grain mills, tractors and combine harvesters, and 10% for other local services.

## Sub lesson 2 — Excise — on luxuries and harms

Excise falls on 19 groups covering 378 goods — luxuries and health-harming goods — at rates from 5% to 500%. It is payable at customs clearance for imports and within 30 days of production for local goods.

## Sub lesson 3 — Surtax — 10% on imports

Surtax adds 10% on imports. Exempt from it: fertilizers, petroleum and lubricants, freight and passenger vehicles, aircraft and capital goods — the inputs and machines the economy is trying to encourage, spared the extra tenth.
