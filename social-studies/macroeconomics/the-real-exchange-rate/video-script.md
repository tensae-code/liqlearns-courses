# Video script — The real exchange rate

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The real exchange rate converts the nominal rate by relative prices — nominal rate times foreign price over domestic price — to say how much foreign goods cost in domestic-goods terms. It answers the traveller's real question: what does the same thing cost, here against there?

Worked example: the dollar at 45 birr, a burger at 5 dollars in the USA and 60 birr in Ethiopia. Real rate = 45 × 5 ÷ 60 = 225 ÷ 60 = 3.75 Ethiopian burgers per US burger — so 5 dollars buys 3.75 times as much burger in Ethiopia.

If Ethiopian burger prices rise to 100 birr, the real rate falls to 45 × 5 ÷ 100 = 2.25: the same dollars now buy less extra burger in Ethiopia. A real rate of 1 means the good costs the same in both countries.
