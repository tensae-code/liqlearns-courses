# The real exchange rate — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "From nominal to real" in your own words.
**Answer:** Real exchange rate = nominal rate × foreign price ÷ domestic price.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "The burger calculation" in your own words.
**Answer:** 45 × 5 ÷ 60 = 3.75 Ethiopian burgers per US burger.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "When domestic prices rise" in your own words.
**Answer:** If the Ethiopian burger rises to 100 birr, the real rate falls to 2.25; a rate of 1 means equal cost in both countries.
