# The real exchange rate — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The real exchange rate ignores prices in both countries.
**Answer: False — Relative prices are its whole content: nominal rate times foreign price over domestic price.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The burger calculation gives a real rate of 37.5.
**Answer: False — 45 × 5 = 225, and 225 ÷ 60 = 3.75 — check the division before trusting the stack.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A real exchange rate of 1 means the foreign good is free.
**Answer: False — A rate of 1 means the good costs the SAME in both countries — neither cheaper nor dearer.**
