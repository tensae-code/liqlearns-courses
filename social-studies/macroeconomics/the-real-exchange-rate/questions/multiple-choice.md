# The real exchange rate — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "From nominal to real"?
a) Real exchange rate = nominal rate × foreign price ÷ domestic price.  b) 45 × 5 ÷ 60 = 3.75 Ethiopian burgers per US burger.  c) If the Ethiopian burger rises to 100 birr, the real rate falls to 2.25; a rate of 1 means equal cost in both countries.
**Answer: a) Real exchange rate = nominal rate × foreign price ÷ domestic price.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "The burger calculation"?
a) If the Ethiopian burger rises to 100 birr, the real rate falls to 2.25; a rate of 1 means equal cost in both countries.  b) Real exchange rate = nominal rate × foreign price ÷ domestic price.  c) 45 × 5 ÷ 60 = 3.75 Ethiopian burgers per US burger.
**Answer: c) 45 × 5 ÷ 60 = 3.75 Ethiopian burgers per US burger.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "When domestic prices rise"?
a) 45 × 5 ÷ 60 = 3.75 Ethiopian burgers per US burger.  b) If the Ethiopian burger rises to 100 birr, the real rate falls to 2.25; a rate of 1 means equal cost in both countries.  c) Real exchange rate = nominal rate × foreign price ÷ domestic price.
**Answer: b) If the Ethiopian burger rises to 100 birr, the real rate falls to 2.25; a rate of 1 means equal cost in both countries.**
