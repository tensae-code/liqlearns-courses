## Sub lesson 1 — The capitalist economy was the means by which

The capitalist economy was the means by which industrialised Western countries extended dominion worldwide;

## Sub lesson 2 — To guarantee production and cheapen costs they sought

To guarantee production and cheapen costs they sought new markets and raw materials in greater quantity at lower price — conquering peoples such as Africa's, with forced labour, political domination and exploitation of all natural resources (the partition of Africa) imperialism differs from colonialism (constant conquest and economic dominion, not only political control);

## Sub lesson 3 — Its economic causes nations dominate others through the

Its economic causes — nations dominate others through the expansion of industrial capitalism, and powers rank in a hierarchy by military/political force and by the amount of capital they possess (same NEW slug first proposed at Book 23)
