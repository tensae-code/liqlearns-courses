# Hands-on — Tax avoidance, evasion and Ethiopia's compliance problem

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Two doors
Draw two doors — 'avoidance: legal, costly to revenue' and a barred door, 'evasion: illegal'. Behind each door, write two example actions from the lesson and their consequences.

## Raise compliance
Pick three causes of non-compliance from the lesson and propose one realistic fix for each, addressed to the tax administration. Mark which fix costs money and which saves it.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
