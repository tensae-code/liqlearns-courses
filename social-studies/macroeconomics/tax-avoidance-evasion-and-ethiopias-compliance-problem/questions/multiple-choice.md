# Tax avoidance, evasion and Ethiopia's compliance problem — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Avoidance — legal, but erosive"?
a) Avoidance is legal arranging within the law, but it erodes revenue  b) Evasion is falsifying returns or not filing — a crime fed by the underground economy  c) Complexity, perceived corruption and unfairness, and low tax knowledge depress voluntary compliance
**Answer: a) Avoidance is legal arranging within the law, but it erodes revenue**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Evasion — the crime"?
a) Evasion is falsifying returns or not filing — a crime fed by the underground economy  b) Complexity, perceived corruption and unfairness, and low tax knowledge depress voluntary compliance  c) Avoidance is legal arranging within the law, but it erodes revenue
**Answer: a) Evasion is falsifying returns or not filing — a crime fed by the underground economy**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Why compliance fails"?
a) Complexity, perceived corruption and unfairness, and low tax knowledge depress voluntary compliance  b) Avoidance is legal arranging within the law, but it erodes revenue  c) Evasion is falsifying returns or not filing — a crime fed by the underground economy
**Answer: a) Complexity, perceived corruption and unfairness, and low tax knowledge depress voluntary compliance**
