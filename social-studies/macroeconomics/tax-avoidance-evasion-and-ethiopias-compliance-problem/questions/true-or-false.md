# Tax avoidance, evasion and Ethiopia's compliance problem — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Avoidance is legal arranging within the law, but it erodes revenue.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Evasion is falsifying returns or not filing — a crime fed by the underground economy.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Complexity, perceived corruption and unfairness, and low tax knowledge depress voluntary compliance.
**Answer: True**
