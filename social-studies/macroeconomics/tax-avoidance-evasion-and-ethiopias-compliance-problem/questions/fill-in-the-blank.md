# Tax avoidance, evasion and Ethiopia's compliance problem — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or figure from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Arranging affairs within the law to pay less tax is ____.
**Answer: avoidance**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Falsifying a tax return is ____.
**Answer: evasion**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The system's lifeblood is ____ compliance by taxpayers.
**Answer: voluntary**
