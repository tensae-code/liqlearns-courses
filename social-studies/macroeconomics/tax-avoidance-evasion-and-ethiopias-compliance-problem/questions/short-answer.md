# Tax avoidance, evasion and Ethiopia's compliance problem — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Avoidance — legal, but erosive" in your own words.
**Answer:** Avoidance is legal arranging within the law, but it erodes revenue.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Evasion — the crime" in your own words.
**Answer:** Evasion is falsifying returns or not filing — a crime fed by the underground economy.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Why compliance fails" in your own words.
**Answer:** Complexity, perceived corruption and unfairness, and low tax knowledge depress voluntary compliance.
