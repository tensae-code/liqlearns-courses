# Dependent capitalism — Venezuela and Latin America — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A dependent capitalist economy is one —
a) conditioned by the development interests of stronger capitalist countries  b) with no trade at all  c) run entirely by cooperatives
**Answer: a) conditioned by the development interests of stronger capitalist countries**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The dependent pattern in the book's table shows —
a) high population growth, low industrial development, high poverty, monoexport trade  b) low poverty and high industry  c) no foreign trade
**Answer: a) high population growth, low industrial development, high poverty, monoexport trade**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Development, redefined, is measured by —
a) gains in people's standard of living with the least environmental damage  b) exports alone  c) the size of the army
**Answer: a) gains in people's standard of living with the least environmental damage**
