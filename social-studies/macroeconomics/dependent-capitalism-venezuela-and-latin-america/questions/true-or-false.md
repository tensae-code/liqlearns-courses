# Dependent capitalism — Venezuela and Latin America — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Dependence means an economy's direction is conditioned by stronger countries' interests.
**Answer: True** — that is the book's definition.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Venezuelan industry makes all its own machinery and technology.
**Answer: False** — it depends on imported machinery and technology.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The book measures development only by how much a country exports.
**Answer: False** — it uses standard-of-living gains and least environmental damage (UN human development).
