# Dependent capitalism — Venezuela and Latin America — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Venezuela and Latin America have a _____ capitalist economy.
**Answer: dependent**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Venezuelan industry depends on imported machinery, foreign financial capital and imported _____.
**Answer: raw materials**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** _____ development with social property is presented as the road to economic independence.
**Answer: Endogenous**
