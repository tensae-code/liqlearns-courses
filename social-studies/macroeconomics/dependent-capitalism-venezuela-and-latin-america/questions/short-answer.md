# Dependent capitalism — Venezuela and Latin America — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Define a dependent capitalist economy in the book's words.
**Answer:** An economy conditioned by the development interests of stronger capitalist countries, leaving a weak, underdeveloped economy.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the three things Venezuelan industry depends on from abroad.
**Answer:** Imported machinery and technology, foreign financial capital, and imported raw materials.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** How does the book redefine development, and what road does it name to independence?
**Answer:** Development = gains in a people's standard of living with the least environmental damage; endogenous development with social property is the road to economic independence.
