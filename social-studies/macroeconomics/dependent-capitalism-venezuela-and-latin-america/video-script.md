# Video script — Dependent capitalism — Venezuela and Latin America

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

An economy conditioned by the development interests of stronger capitalist countries, leaving a weak, underdeveloped economy.

Imported machinery and technology, foreign financial capital, and imported raw materials.

Development = gains in a people's standard of living with the least environmental damage; endogenous development with social property is the road to economic independence.
