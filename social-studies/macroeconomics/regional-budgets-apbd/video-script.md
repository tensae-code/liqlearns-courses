# Video script — Regional budgets — APBD

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The APBD is the regional budget — the yearly plan of revenue and spending for a province, regency or city in Indonesia.

The APBD sets out what the region spends on and the steps by which the budget is drawn up and approved.

Village funds (Dana Desa) flow through the regional budget story to villages, reducing poverty in the countryside.
