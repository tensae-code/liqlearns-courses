# Regional budgets — APBD — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the book teach about what the apbd is?
**Answer:** The APBD is the regional budget — the yearly plan of revenue and spending for a province, regency or city in Indonesia.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the book teach about regional spending and how it is drawn up?
**Answer:** The APBD sets out what the region spends on and the steps by which the budget is drawn up and approved.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the book teach about village funds (dana desa)?
**Answer:** Village funds (Dana Desa) flow through the regional budget story to villages, reducing poverty in the countryside.
