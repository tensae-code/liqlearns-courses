# Regional budgets — APBD — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The APBD is the regional budget — the yearly plan of revenue and spending for a province, regency or city in Indonesia.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The APBD sets out what the region spends on and the steps by which the budget is drawn up and approved.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** something else entirely flow through the regional budget story to villages, reducing poverty in the countryside.
**Answer: False** — Village funds (Dana Desa) flow through the regional budget story to villages, reducing poverty in the countryside.
