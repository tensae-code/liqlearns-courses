# Regional budgets — APBD — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The APBD is the _____ — the yearly plan of revenue and spending for a province, regency or city in Indonesia.
**Answer: regional budget**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The APBD sets out what the region spends on and the steps by which the budget is drawn up and _____.
**Answer: approved**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** _____ flow through the regional budget story to villages, reducing poverty in the countryside.
**Answer: Village funds (Dana Desa)**
