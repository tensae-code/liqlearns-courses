# Causes of poverty — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which are household-level causes in the book's list?
a) low and irregular income, widespread unemployment, large families on limited income  b) only bad luck  c) none are named
**Answer: a) low and irregular income, widespread unemployment, large families on limited income**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which are state and regional causes?
a) low government spending on health and education under debt, fragmentation, dependence and corruption  b) weather only  c) none
**Answer: a) low government spending on health and education under debt, fragmentation, dependence and corruption**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** How does the occupation deepen poverty in Palestine?
a) through closures, siege, land confiscation and control of ports, crossings and trade policy  b) it does not  c) through tourism
**Answer: a) through closures, siege, land confiscation and control of ports, crossings and trade policy**
