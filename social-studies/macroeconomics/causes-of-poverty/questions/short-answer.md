# Causes of poverty — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why can a large family be poor on the same income as a small one?
**Answer:** Because the limited income must stretch across more people — large families on limited income are a named cause.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the regional causes.
**Answer:** The Arab world's fragmentation and lack of economic integration, and the Arab economy's dependence on the West with its markets flooded by foreign products.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name two occupation controls named as causes.
**Answer:** Closures and siege (also: land confiscation, control of ports, crossings and trade policy).
