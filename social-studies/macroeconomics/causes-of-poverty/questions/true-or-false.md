# Causes of poverty — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Seasonal and day labour are the book's example of low, regular income.
**Answer: False — they are its example of low and irregular income**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Corruption is named among the causes.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** About a third of Palestinians live under the national poverty line.
**Answer: True**
