# Hands-on — Causes of poverty

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Write each cause on a slip of paper and sort the slips into three piles — household, state and region, occupation — saying each cause aloud as it lands. Then shuffle and sort again without looking.

## Draw it and check it
Draw three nested boxes — household inside state and region inside Palestine under occupation — with every cause in its box. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare, and fix every cause you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
