# Video script — Causes of poverty

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

What causes poverty? Palestine's Grade 9 book sorts the causes by level. In the household: low and irregular income — seasonal, day labour — widespread unemployment, large families on limited income. In the state and region: low spending on health and education under debt, fragmentation, dependence, corruption. And in Palestine, over all of it: closures, siege, confiscated land, controlled ports and crossings — and about a third of Palestinians under the poverty line.
