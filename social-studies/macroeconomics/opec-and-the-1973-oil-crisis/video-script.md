OPEC and the 1973 oil crisis — as Bolivia's book teaches it, says the narrator: the Persian Gulf producers raised the oil price 70% on 16 October 1973; the Arab exporters imposed an embargo mainly on the US and Holland; by January 1974 OPEC had doubled the barrel price in three months, forcing the West to restructure.
