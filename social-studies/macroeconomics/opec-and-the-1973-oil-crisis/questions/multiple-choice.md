# OPEC and the 1973 oil crisis — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** About 16 October 1973 — the price rises 70% — the lesson teaches that —
a) the Persian Gulf producers raised the oil price 70% on 16 October 1973  b) the Arab exporters imposed an embargo mainly on the US and Holland  c) by January 1974 OPEC had doubled the barrel price in three months, forcing the West to restructure
**Answer: a) the Persian Gulf producers raised the oil price 70% on 16 October 1973**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** About the embargo — mainly on the US and Holland — the lesson teaches that —
a) the Persian Gulf producers raised the oil price 70% on 16 October 1973  b) the Arab exporters imposed an embargo mainly on the US and Holland  c) by January 1974 OPEC had doubled the barrel price in three months, forcing the West to restructure
**Answer: b) the Arab exporters imposed an embargo mainly on the US and Holland**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** About doubled in three months — the West restructures — the lesson teaches that —
a) the Persian Gulf producers raised the oil price 70% on 16 October 1973  b) the Arab exporters imposed an embargo mainly on the US and Holland  c) by January 1974 OPEC had doubled the barrel price in three months, forcing the West to restructure
**Answer: c) by January 1974 OPEC had doubled the barrel price in three months, forcing the West to restructure**
