# OPEC and the 1973 oil crisis — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the lesson teach about 16 October 1973 — the price rises 70%?
**Answer:** The Persian Gulf producers raised the oil price 70% on 16 October 1973.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the lesson teach about the embargo — mainly on the US and Holland?
**Answer:** The Arab exporters imposed an embargo mainly on the US and Holland.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the lesson teach about doubled in three months — the West restructures?
**Answer:** By January 1974 OPEC had doubled the barrel price in three months, forcing the West to restructure.
