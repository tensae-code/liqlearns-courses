# OPEC and the 1973 oil crisis — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The oil price fell in October 1973.
**Answer: False** — the Gulf producers raised it 70% during the fourth Arab-Israeli war.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The embargo covered every country equally.
**Answer: False** — it was aimed mainly at the United States and Holland.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The crisis left the western economies unchanged.
**Answer: False** — it forced them to restructure the capitalist system.
