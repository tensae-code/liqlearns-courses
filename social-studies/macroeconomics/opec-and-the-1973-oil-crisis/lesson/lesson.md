# OPEC and the 1973 oil crisis

## Do it first

## Sub lesson 1 — 16 October 1973 — the price rises 70%

During the fourth Arab-Israeli war, the Persian Gulf producers raised the oil price 70% on 16 October 1973. One decision, and the world's chief fuel cost nearly double at a stroke.

## Sub lesson 2 — The embargo — mainly on the US and Holland

The Arab exporters threatened production cuts and imposed an embargo mainly on the United States and Holland — oil used as a weapon, aimed at the war's backers.

## Sub lesson 3 — Doubled in three months — the West restructures

By January 1974 OPEC had doubled the barrel price in three months, triggering a severe crisis that forced the western countries to restructure the capitalist system itself.
