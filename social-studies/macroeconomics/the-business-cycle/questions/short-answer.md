# The business cycle — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Fluctuations around a trend" in your own words.
**Answer:** Business-cycle fluctuations are irregular and unpredictable — not truly cyclical despite the name.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Boom and recession" in your own words.
**Answer:** In the boom, spending and investment feed each other through the multiplier.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Depression and recovery" in your own words.
**Answer:** Confidence is at its lowest in the depression or trough phase.

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Explain "Expansion, peak, contraction, trough — the Grade 12 names" in your own words.
**Answer:** The Grade 12 phases are expansion, peak, contraction and trough around the trend, the frame countercyclical policy tries to smooth.
