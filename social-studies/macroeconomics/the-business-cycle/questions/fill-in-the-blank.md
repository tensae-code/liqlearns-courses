# The business cycle — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The cycle's swings are _____ and unpredictable — no timetable governs them.
**Answer: irregular**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Boom spending feeds investment through the _____ — each round fuelling the next.
**Answer: multiplier**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Confidence bottoms in the depression, also called the slump or _____.
**Answer: trough**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** The lowest point of the business cycle is the ____.
**Answer: trough**
