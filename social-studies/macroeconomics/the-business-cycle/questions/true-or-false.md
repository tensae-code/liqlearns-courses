# The business cycle — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Business-cycle fluctuations are irregular and unpredictable — not truly cyclical despite the name.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In the boom, spending and investment feed each other through the multiplier.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Confidence is at its lowest in the depression or trough phase.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** The Grade 12 phases are expansion, peak, contraction and trough around the trend, the frame countercyclical policy tries to smooth.
**Answer: True**
