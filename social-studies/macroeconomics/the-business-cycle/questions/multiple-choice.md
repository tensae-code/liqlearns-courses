# The business cycle — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Fluctuations around a trend"?
a) irregular, unpredictable swings of activity around the trend  b) boom fuelling itself by the multiplier; recession as caution cuts investment  c) the trough where confidence is lowest, then recovery back past the trend
**Answer: a) irregular, unpredictable swings of activity around the trend**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Boom and recession"?
a) irregular, unpredictable swings of activity around the trend  b) the trough where confidence is lowest, then recovery back past the trend  c) boom fuelling itself by the multiplier; recession as caution cuts investment
**Answer: c) boom fuelling itself by the multiplier; recession as caution cuts investment**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Depression and recovery"?
a) boom fuelling itself by the multiplier; recession as caution cuts investment  b) the trough where confidence is lowest, then recovery back past the trend  c) irregular, unpredictable swings of activity around the trend
**Answer: b) the trough where confidence is lowest, then recovery back past the trend**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Which statement best matches "Expansion, peak, contraction, trough — the Grade 12 names"?
a) The cycle runs trough, trough, peak, peak in a fixed four-year rhythm  b) The Grade 12 phases are expansion, peak, contraction and trough around the trend, the frame countercyclical policy tries to smooth  c) Countercyclical policy aims to make the wave taller
**Answer: b) The Grade 12 phases are expansion, peak, contraction and trough around the trend, the frame countercyclical policy tries to smooth**
