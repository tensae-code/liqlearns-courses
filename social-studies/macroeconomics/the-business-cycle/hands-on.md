# Hands-on — The business cycle

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
The class walks the wave: learners pace a rising line in boom posture (busy, spending), slow into recession caution, crouch at the trough, and rise into recovery — narrating investment and confidence at each phase.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — a wavy line crossing a straight trend line, labelled through its four phases — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.

## The Grade 12 layer — four phases, two namings
Draw the wave once more and label it twice: the Grade 10 names (boom, recession, depression, recovery) above the line and the Grade 12 names (expansion, peak, contraction, trough) below it. Then write one line on what countercyclical policy is trying to do to the wave.
