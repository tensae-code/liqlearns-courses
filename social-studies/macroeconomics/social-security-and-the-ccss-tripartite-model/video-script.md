# Video script — Social security and the CCSS tripartite model

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The CCSS (created 1 November 1941) is financed by a tripartite system.

State, employers and workers contribute.

Began with sickness and maternity cover, added invalidity, old age and death, was universalised in 1961, and also runs the non-contributory regime paying pensions to very poor elderly people who never contributed.
