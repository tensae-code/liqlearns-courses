# Social security and the CCSS tripartite model — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the lesson teach about caja costarricense de seguro social?
**Answer:** The CCSS (created 1 November 1941) is financed by a tripartite system.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the lesson teach about caja costarricense de seguro social — more?
**Answer:** State, employers and workers contribute.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the lesson teach about caja costarricense de seguro social — more — more?
**Answer:** Began with sickness and maternity cover, added invalidity, old age and death, was universalised in 1961, and also runs the non-contributory regime paying pensions to very poor elderly people who never contributed.
