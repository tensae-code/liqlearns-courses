# Social security and the CCSS tripartite model — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** About caja costarricense de seguro social — the lesson teaches that —
a) The CCSS (created 1 November 1941) is financed by a tripartite system.
b) State, employers and workers contribute.
c) Began with sickness and maternity cover, added invalidity, old age and death, was universalised in 1961, and also runs the non-contributory regime…
**Answer: a) The CCSS (created 1 November 1941) is financed by a tripartite system.**
