# Social security and the CCSS tripartite model — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The CCSS (created 1 November 1941) is _____ by a tripartite system.
**Answer: financed**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** State, employers and _____ contribute.
**Answer: workers**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Began with sickness and maternity cover, added invalidity, old age and death, was universalised in 1961, and also runs the _____ regime paying pensions to very poor elderly people who never contributed.
**Answer: non-contributory**
