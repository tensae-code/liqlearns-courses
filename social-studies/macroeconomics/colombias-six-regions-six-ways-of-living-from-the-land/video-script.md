Colombia's six regions six ways of living from the land — as Colombia's book teaches it, says the narrator: Colombia's six geographic regions (Amazonia, Andina, Caribe, Insular, Pacífica Orinoquia) have each related economically to their environment in their own way — subienda fishing, terrace cultivation The Eje Cafetero's coffee, emeralds, salt and other mining wealth — and students map their own region's geography onto its economic and cultural practices
