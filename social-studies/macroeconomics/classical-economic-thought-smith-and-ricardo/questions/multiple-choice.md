# Classical economic thought — Smith and Ricardo — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Who built the classical school?
a) only tax collectors of the old kingdoms  b) Adam Smith and David Ricardo and their fellows  c) thinkers who never wrote about production
**Answer: b) Adam Smith and David Ricardo and their fellows**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Smith's answer to what makes a nation wealthy is —
a) the work of its people, divided and specialised, producing for exchange  b) having no trade with anyone  c) the gold locked in its treasury
**Answer: a) the work of its people, divided and specialised, producing for exchange**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Ricardo showed that two countries both gain when —
a) both produce only the same single good  b) neither trades with the other at all  c) each specialises in what it does relatively best and they trade freely
**Answer: c) each specialises in what it does relatively best and they trade freely**
