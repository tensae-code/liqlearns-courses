# Classical economic thought — Smith and Ricardo — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Smith and Ricardo built the classical school.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Smith's answer to what makes a nation wealthy is the gold locked in its treasury.
**Answer: False — Smith tied wealth to people's work and production, not locked-up gold.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Ricardo showed both countries gain from specialisation and free trade.
**Answer: True**
