# Classical economic thought — Smith and Ricardo — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The classical school asked how nations grow rich through production, work and _____.
**Answer: exchange**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In Smith, people's own efforts are guided as if by an unseen _____ to serve others too.
**Answer: hand**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Ricardo argued about what gives goods their _____, and showed the gains of free trade.
**Answer: value**
