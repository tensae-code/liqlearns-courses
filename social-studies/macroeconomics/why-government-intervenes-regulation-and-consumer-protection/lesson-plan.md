# Lesson plan — Why government intervenes — regulation and consumer protection

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Macroeconomics · **Lesson:** Why government intervenes — regulation and consumer protection
**Place in the curriculum:** Macroeconomics (CURRICULUM.md) — built in the Economics expansion (Iran, 2026-11-16).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Iran's Grade 10 Economics book, in Lesson 6 "What is the government's role in the economy?", gives the case plainly.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Iran's Grade 10 Economics Lesson 6, in its regulation sections.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of ..." in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.
