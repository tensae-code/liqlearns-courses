# Hands-on — Why government intervenes — regulation and consumer protection

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Judge the case
Write the drug case as a short hearing: the honest producer speaks, the cheap firm speaks, a buyer speaks. Then give the state's ruling, from the lesson.

## Testable or not?
List six things sold near you. Mark each: can the buyer test its quality before buying, or not? The 'not' pile is where this lesson says supervision matters most.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach sub lesson 1 aloud — the drug case — as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
