# Why government intervenes — regulation and consumer protection — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Buyers in the drug case can easily see inside the medicine before buying.
**Answer: False — Buyers cannot see inside the medicine; they buy the cheaper one, and the honest maker loses the market — that is why supervision exists.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A weak or false medicine sold cheaply is a bargain for the consumer.
**Answer: False — It is a harm sold as a saving — consumers are endangered by goods they cannot test with their own eyes before buying.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The body of state laws exists to stop all exchange.
**Answer: False — The book says the body of state laws exists to facilitate and encourage exchanges that benefit buyers and sellers.**
