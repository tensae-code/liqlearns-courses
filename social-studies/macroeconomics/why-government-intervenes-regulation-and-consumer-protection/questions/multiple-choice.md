# Why government intervenes — regulation and consumer protection — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Without state supervision, the book's drug case shows —
a) the honest producer always winning  b) a low-quality, low-price drug firm driving out the honest producer  c) prices disappearing
**Answer: b) a low-quality, low-price drug firm driving out the honest producer**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Consumers are endangered in the drug case because —
a) medicines are too cheap  b) they cannot test the medicine's real quality with their own eyes before buying  c) there are too many producers
**Answer: b) they cannot test the medicine's real quality with their own eyes before buying**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The body of state laws exists, in the book's account, to —
a) facilitate and encourage exchanges that benefit buyers and sellers  b) stop all buying and selling  c) set every price itself
**Answer: b) facilitate and encourage exchanges that benefit buyers and sellers**
