# Why government intervenes — regulation and consumer protection — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Tell the book's drug case in your own words: who wins unwatched, and why?
**Answer:** The low-quality, low-price drug firm wins, because buyers cannot see inside the medicine and buy the cheaper one; the honest producer, whose real medicine costs more to make, is driven out.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why are consumers endangered in an unsupervised market for goods like medicine?
**Answer:** Because they cannot test the good's real quality with their own eyes before buying; a low-quality firm can undercut the honest producer and sell them a harm as if it were a saving.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What, in this lesson's account, is the body of state laws for?
**Answer:** To facilitate and encourage exchanges that benefit buyers and sellers — a frame that keeps exchange honest, so beneficial exchanges can go on and cheating ones cannot.
