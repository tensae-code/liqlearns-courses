# Why government intervenes — regulation and consumer protection — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Without state _____, a low-quality drug firm drives out the honest producer.
**Answer: supervision**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** State supervision protects _____ against dangers they cannot test before buying.
**Answer: consumers**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** State laws facilitate exchanges that benefit buyers and _____.
**Answer: sellers**
