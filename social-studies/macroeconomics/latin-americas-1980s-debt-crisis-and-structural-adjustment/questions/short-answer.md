# Latin America's 1980s debt crisis and structural adjustment — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the lesson teach about ajuste económico y crisis en américa latina?
**Answer:** Inconsistent policies built up heavy external debt through international loans while financial liberalisation kept interest rates high and starved investment;.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the lesson teach about ajuste económico y crisis en américa latina — more?
**Answer:** The Latin American adjustment answer was higher taxes and utility tariffs, privatisation of state firms, a smaller state and debt renegotiation.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the lesson teach about ajuste económico y crisis en américa latina — more — more?
**Answer:** And falling real wages.
