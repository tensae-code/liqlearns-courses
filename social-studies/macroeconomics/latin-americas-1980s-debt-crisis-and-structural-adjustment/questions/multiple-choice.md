# Latin America's 1980s debt crisis and structural adjustment — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** About ajuste económico y crisis en américa latina — the lesson teaches that —
a) Inconsistent policies built up heavy external debt through international loans while financial liberalisation kept interest rates high and starved…
