# Hands-on — Latin America's 1980s debt crisis and structural adjustment

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Lay it out on the table
On paper, draw the lesson in 3 boxes — one for each sub lesson: Ajuste económico y crisis en América Latina; Ajuste económico y crisis en América Latina — more; Ajuste económico y crisis en América Latina — more — more. Write one line of the lesson in each box, from memory, then open lesson/lesson.md, compare, and fix anything missing.

## Say it with things at home
Put 3 stones on the table — one for each sub lesson — and say what each stone stands for, in order, without looking. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 3 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes.
