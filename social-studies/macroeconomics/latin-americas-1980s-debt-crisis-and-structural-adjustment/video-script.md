# Video script — Latin America's 1980s debt crisis and structural adjustment

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Inconsistent policies built up heavy external debt through international loans while financial liberalisation kept interest rates high and starved investment;.

The Latin American adjustment answer was higher taxes and utility tariffs, privatisation of state firms, a smaller state and debt renegotiation.

And falling real wages.
