# Multinationals and foreign investment why they come — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Apertura adopted rules to attract foreign capital (creating industries, multinational headquarters, buying national companies' assets);
**Answer: True** — this is what Colombia's book teaches in this part.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Sub lesson 2 is mainly about apertura adopted rules to attract foreign capital creating.
**Answer: False** — sub lesson 2 is about multinationals are industrial financial or commercial firms established; apertura adopted rules to attract foreign capital creating is sub lesson 1.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** They come for abundant cheap labour, plentiful natural resources, the chance to monopolise markets, low production costs and taxes, and limited regulation — and Colombia has its own multinationals (Argos, Avianca, Grupo Nacional de Chocolates, Terpel, Éxito)
**Answer: True** — this is what Colombia's book teaches in this part.
