# Multinationals and foreign investment why they come — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the lesson teach about apertura adopted rules to attract foreign capital creating?
**Answer:** Apertura adopted rules to attract foreign capital (creating industries, multinational headquarters, buying national companies' assets);

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the lesson teach about multinationals are industrial financial or commercial firms established?
**Answer:** Multinationals are industrial, financial or commercial firms established in one country that set up branches in others to expand production and send profits home;

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the lesson teach about they come for abundant cheap labour plentiful natural?
**Answer:** They come for abundant cheap labour, plentiful natural resources, the chance to monopolise markets, low production costs and taxes, and limited regulation — and Colombia has its own multinationals (Argos, Avianca, Grupo Nacional de Chocolates, Terpel, Éxito)
