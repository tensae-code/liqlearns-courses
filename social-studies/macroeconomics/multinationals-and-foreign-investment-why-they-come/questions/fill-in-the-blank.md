# Multinationals and foreign investment why they come — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Apertura adopted rules to attract foreign capital (creating industries, _____ headquarters, buying national companies' assets);
**Answer: multinational**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** _____ are industrial, financial or commercial firms established in one country that set up branches in others to expand production and send profits home;
**Answer: Multinationals**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** They come for abundant cheap labour, plentiful natural resources, the chance to monopolise markets, low production costs and taxes, and limited regulation — and _____ has its own multinationals (Argos, Avianca, Grupo Nacional de Chocolates, Terpel, Éxito)
**Answer: Colombia**
