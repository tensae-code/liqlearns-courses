# Video script — Multinationals and foreign investment why they come

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Multinationals and foreign investment why they come — as Colombia's book teaches it, says the narrator: Apertura adopted rules to attract foreign capital (creating industries, multinational headquarters, buying national companies' assets); Multinationals are industrial, financial or commercial firms established in one country that set up branches in others to expand production and send profits home; They come for abundant cheap labour, plentiful natural resources, the chance to monopolise markets, low production costs and taxes, and limited regulation — and Colombia has its own multinationals (Argos, Avianca, Grupo Nacional de Chocolates, Terpel, Éxito)
