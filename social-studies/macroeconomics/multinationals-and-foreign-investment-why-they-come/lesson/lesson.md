# Multinationals and foreign investment why they come

## Do it first

Before you read on, write what you already think this lesson's title means — multinationals and foreign investment why they come — in one sentence of your own. Keep it; you will check it against the lesson at the end.

## Sub lesson 1 — Apertura adopted rules to attract foreign capital creating

Apertura adopted rules to attract foreign capital (creating industries, multinational headquarters, buying national companies' assets);

## Sub lesson 2 — Multinationals are industrial financial or commercial firms established

Multinationals are industrial, financial or commercial firms established in one country that set up branches in others to expand production and send profits home;

## Sub lesson 3 — They come for abundant cheap labour plentiful natural

They come for abundant cheap labour, plentiful natural resources, the chance to monopolise markets, low production costs and taxes, and limited regulation — and Colombia has its own multinationals (Argos, Avianca, Grupo Nacional de Chocolates, Terpel, Éxito)
