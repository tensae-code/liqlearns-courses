# Macroeconomic policy — objectives and instruments — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Why policy exists at all"?
a) Policy exists because markets cycle between recession and overheating, and self-correction failed in the 1930s  b) The six objectives are maximum output, high growth, full employment, price stability, equality and a healthy balance of payments  c) The four policy families are fiscal, monetary, income and foreign-exchange policy
**Answer: a) Policy exists because markets cycle between recession and overheating, and self-correction failed in the 1930s**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "The six objectives"?
a) The six objectives are maximum output, high growth, full employment, price stability, equality and a healthy balance of payments  b) The four policy families are fiscal, monetary, income and foreign-exchange policy  c) Policy exists because markets cycle between recession and overheating, and self-correction failed in the 1930s
**Answer: a) The six objectives are maximum output, high growth, full employment, price stability, equality and a healthy balance of payments**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Four families of instruments"?
a) The four policy families are fiscal, monetary, income and foreign-exchange policy  b) Policy exists because markets cycle between recession and overheating, and self-correction failed in the 1930s  c) The six objectives are maximum output, high growth, full employment, price stability, equality and a healthy balance of payments
