# Macroeconomic policy — objectives and instruments — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Policy exists because markets cycle between recession and overheating, and self-correction failed in the 1930s.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The six objectives are maximum output, high growth, full employment, price stability, equality and a healthy balance of payments.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The four policy families are fiscal, monetary, income and foreign-exchange policy.
**Answer: True**
