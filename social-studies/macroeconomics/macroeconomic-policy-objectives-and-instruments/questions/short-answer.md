# Macroeconomic policy — objectives and instruments — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Why policy exists at all" in your own words.
**Answer:** Policy exists because markets cycle between recession and overheating, and self-correction failed in the 1930s.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "The six objectives" in your own words.
**Answer:** The six objectives are maximum output, high growth, full employment, price stability, equality and a healthy balance of payments.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Four families of instruments" in your own words.
**Answer:** The four policy families are fiscal, monetary, income and foreign-exchange policy.
