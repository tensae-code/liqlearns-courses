# Hands-on — Macroeconomic policy — objectives and instruments

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Six gauges
Draw a control panel with six gauges, one per objective. Set each gauge for an economy you invent in recession, then again for the same economy overheating.

## Family match
Write one crisis per gauge. For each, name which policy family you would reach for first, and why that family fits that gauge.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
