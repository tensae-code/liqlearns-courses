## Sub lesson 1 — Why policy exists at all

Free markets cycle: recession brings idle capacity and rising unemployment; overheating brings inflation. The classical faith in automatic self-correction died in the 1930s, and Keynes made intervention the government's job.

## Sub lesson 2 — The six objectives

Every policy in this unit serves six objectives: maximum feasible output, high growth, full employment, price stability, equality in income and wealth distribution, and a healthy balance of payments. A policy is judged against all six, not one.

## Sub lesson 3 — Four families of instruments

Four policy families follow: fiscal, monetary, income and foreign-exchange policy. The rest of this unit takes them in turn — what each moves, and how hard it can push.
