Malaysia's own way (Malaysia Grade 11 Ekonomi Tingkatan 5): Form 5 turns to the state and the world — Bank Negara's policy tools, the national budget (Belanjawan), and Malaysia's own trade composition and direction as the data pupils analyse.

Cambodia's own way (Cambodia Grade 11 Economics): a full academic course built around Cambodia's own transition — the market system is introduced, judged, and then located in Cambodia itself (its market-economy foundations, its national economy, its reform programme), and the book closes with the history of economic thought from the first schools to Keynes.

Cambodia's own way (Cambodia Grade 12 Economics): the final-year course turns practical — the market is studied the way a Cambodian trader meets it (consumers, prices, banks, riel exchange), and macro policy is taught as coordination: monetary policy, exchange rates and public finance fitted together, closing with foreign trade and the world trading system.
