Dominican Republic's own way (Dominican Republic Grade 11 Ciencias Sociales 5° Secundaria): the final-year history course doubles as the national-economy story — a depressed post-independence economy of subsistence production and port-by-port trade gives way, through merchant credit companies and the sugar industry, to capitalism. This lesson teaches the placement's content in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
