# Hands-on — The Dominican economy after independence

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Put three bowls on the table — one with leaves for tobacco, one with a piece of wood, one empty for the cattle plains. For each bowl, say which region it is, what it sells, through which port, and to whom — then try to 'trade' between the bowls with no road between them and say what goes wrong.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the island with its three regions and their ports, each region's product, and a broken line where the unified market should be. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare, and fix every region, product and axis you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
