# The Dominican economy after independence — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why was the State's material support precarious in the first republican decades?
**Answer:** Because the country stayed depressed with weak insertion in the world market and most production destined for self-consumption, so the State could live only on meagre taxes on foreign trade.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How did the missing roads and railways shape the economy?
**Answer:** There was no unified market: each port exported its own separated region's products, so the regions faced outward separately instead of trading with each other.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name the three regional economies the book describes.
**Answer:** The Cibao's export tobacco, forming a peasantry and a merchant bourgeoisie around Santiago–Puerto Plata; the South cutting precious woods such as mahogany for England and France; and El Seibo keeping the declining colonial cattle hato.
