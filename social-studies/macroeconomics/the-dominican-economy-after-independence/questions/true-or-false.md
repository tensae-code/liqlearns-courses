# The Dominican economy after independence — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The State's material support after independence was strong, resting on high taxes of every kind.
**Answer: False** — it was precarious, limited to meagre taxes on foreign trade, because most production was for self-consumption and insertion in the world market was weak

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The South lived from cutting precious woods such as mahogany for England and France.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** El Seibo's cattle hato was a new and growing industry of the republican decades.
**Answer: False** — El Seibo kept the declining colonial cattle hato
