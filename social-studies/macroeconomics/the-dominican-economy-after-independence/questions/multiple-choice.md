# The Dominican economy after independence — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** After 1844, most Dominican production was destined for —
a) export to Europe  b) self-consumption  c) the factories of the capital
**Answer: b) self-consumption**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why was there no unified market?
a) there were no roads or railways, and each port exported its own separated region's products  b) the regions all produced the same crop  c) the State banned internal trade
**Answer: a) there were no roads or railways, and each port exported its own separated region's products**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The Cibao's export tobacco formed —
a) a peasantry and a merchant bourgeoisie around the Santiago–Puerto Plata axis  b) a class of factory owners  c) the colonial cattle hato
**Answer: a) a peasantry and a merchant bourgeoisie around the Santiago–Puerto Plata axis**
