# The Dominican economy after independence — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** After 1844 the country stayed depressed, with most production destined for _____.
**Answer: self-consumption**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** With no roads or railways there was no _____ market.
**Answer: unified**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The South lived from cutting precious woods such as _____ for England and France.
**Answer: mahogany**
