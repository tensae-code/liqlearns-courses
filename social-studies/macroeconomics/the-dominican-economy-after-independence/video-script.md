# Video script — The Dominican economy after independence

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Independence does not feed a treasury. After 1844 the Dominican Republic stayed depressed: most production was for self-consumption, its hold on the world market was weak, and the State lived precariously on meagre taxes on foreign trade. There were no roads and no railways, so there was no unified market — each port exported its own region's products. The Cibao grew export tobacco around Santiago and Puerto Plata, the South cut mahogany for England and France, and El Seibo kept its declining cattle hato.
