# Nicaragua's industry and tourism — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the book teach about from artisan workshops to industry?
**Answer:** Nicaragua's industry grew from artisan workshops through cotton gins, coffee mills and sugar ingenios — the book follows that road sector by sector.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the book teach about today's manufactures?
**Answer:** Today's manufactures are food, textile, free-zone and other industries, over 60% concentrated in Managua, the capital.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the book teach about tourism — a new industry?
**Answer:** Tourism is taught as a new industry for Nicaragua, growing about 10% a year.
