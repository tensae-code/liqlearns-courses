# Nicaragua's industry and tourism — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Nicaragua's industry grew from artisan workshops through cotton gins, coffee mills and sugar ingenios — the book follows that road sector by sector.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Today's manufactures are food, textile, free-zone and other industries, over 60% concentrated in Managua, the capital.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Tourism is taught as a new industry for Nicaragua, something else entirely.
**Answer: False** — Tourism is taught as a new industry for Nicaragua, growing about 10% a year.
