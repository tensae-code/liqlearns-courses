# Nicaragua's industry and tourism — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Nicaragua's industry grew from _____ through cotton gins, coffee mills and sugar ingenios — the book follows that road sector by sector.
**Answer: artisan workshops**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Today's manufactures are food, textile, free-zone and other industries, _____, the capital.
**Answer: over 60% concentrated in Managua**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Tourism is taught as a new industry for Nicaragua, _____.
**Answer: growing about 10% a year**
