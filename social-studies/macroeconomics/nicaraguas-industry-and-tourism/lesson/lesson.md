## Sub lesson 1 — From artisan workshops to industry

Nicaragua's industry grew from artisan workshops through cotton gins, coffee mills and sugar ingenios — the book follows that road sector by sector.

## Sub lesson 2 — Today's manufactures

Today's manufactures are food, textile, free-zone and other industries, over 60% concentrated in Managua, the capital.

## Sub lesson 3 — Tourism — a new industry

Tourism is taught as a new industry for Nicaragua, growing about 10% a year.
