# Costa Rica's entrepreneur state and CODESA — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the lesson teach about características del estado empresario en costa rica?
**Answer:** In the 1970s the state itself became an entrepreneur through CODESA.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the lesson teach about características del estado empresario en costa rica — more?
**Answer:** Creating firms such as Cementos del Pacífico, Algodones de Costa Rica.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the lesson teach about características del estado empresario en costa rica — more?
**Answer:** ALUMINASA, FERTICA, the CATSA sugar central, the RECOPE oil refinery and the INCOFER railways.
