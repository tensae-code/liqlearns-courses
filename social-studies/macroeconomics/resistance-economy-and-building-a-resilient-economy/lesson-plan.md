# Lesson plan — Resistance economy — building a resilient economy

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Macroeconomics · **Lesson:** Resistance economy — building a resilient economy
**Place in the curriculum:** Macroeconomics (CURRICULUM.md) — built in the Economics expansion (Iran, 2026-11-16).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Iran's books teach the setting first.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Iran's Grade 10 Economics Lesson 10 and Grade 9 Social Studies Lesson 24, in their resistance-economy sections — Iran's own national-economy doctrine, taught in Iran's own books.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of ..." in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Why resistance — sanctions set against a country's progress**
2. **The principles — least waste, own feet, national production**
3. **Using domestic capacity — making at home what was imported**

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
