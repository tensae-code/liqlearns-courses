# Video script — Resistance economy — building a resilient economy

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Iran's books teach the setting first. After the Islamic Revolution, **imposed war and economic sanctions were set against Iran's progress**. The Grade 10 Economics book works the pressure through its running family story: the workshop's **export orders from Oman and Iraq are blocked by US banking sanctions** — the goods are sold, but the money cannot be collected — and import costs multiply. An economy under that pressure needs a doctrine for standing, and Iran's answer is named in both books: the **resistance economy (اقتصاد مقاومتی)**. The Grade 9 book's box "Resistance economy" (اقتصاد مقاومتی) gives the policy's principles, adopted **on the Leader's guidance**: in hard conditions have the **least waste of cost**, **stand on your own feet**, **support national production**, **avoid extravagance** and **use resources correctly**. The Grade 10 book's Lesson 10, "Making the economy resilient" (مقاومسازی اقتصاد), has the students build the principles of a resistance economy themselves. The Grade 10 lesson names the working side: **using domestic capacity**. Where an input was imported, Iran's own **industrial universities** set out to produce it at home — the workshop's imported inputs are the book's small case of a national pattern the book names in **missiles, nuclear technology, laser and medicine**. Resilience, in this doctrine, is not only spending less; it is being able to make what the economy needs when the outside road is closed.
