# Hands-on — Resistance economy — building a resilient economy

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The blocked payment
Act the workshop's case in two voices: the seller in Iran, the buyer in Oman, and the bank between them refusing the transfer. Then list what the seller's country could build so the next sale does not depend on that bank.

## Five principles card
Write the five principles on a card from memory — least waste, own feet, national production, no extravagance, resources used correctly — and check it against the lesson.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach sub lesson 2 aloud — the principles — as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
