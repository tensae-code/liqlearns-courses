# Resistance economy — building a resilient economy — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The resistance economy is Iran's answer to —
a) a fall in tourism  b) imposed war and economic sanctions set against its progress  c) a shortage of shops
**Answer: b) imposed war and economic sanctions set against its progress**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The resistance economy's principles include —
a) maximum waste in hard conditions  b) least waste of cost, standing on your own feet and supporting national production  c) importing everything
**Answer: b) least waste of cost, standing on your own feet and supporting national production**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Domestic capacity is used, in this doctrine, by —
a) closing the universities  b) industrial universities producing at home what was imported  c) buying more imports
**Answer: b) industrial universities producing at home what was imported**
