# Resistance economy — building a resilient economy — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In the Grade 10 book's story, banking sanctions block the workshop's export money from being collected.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The resistance-economy principles include wasting resources freely in hard conditions.
**Answer: False — The principles are the opposite: least waste of cost, stand on your own feet, support national production, avoid extravagance and use resources correctly.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Resilience in this doctrine is only about spending less.
**Answer: False — It is also about capacity: using domestic capacity — industrial universities producing at home what was imported, as in missiles, nuclear technology, laser and medicine.**
