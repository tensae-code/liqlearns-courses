# Resistance economy — building a resilient economy — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What pressure does this lesson say the resistance economy answers?
**Answer:** Imposed war and economic sanctions set against Iran's progress — worked in the Grade 10 book as export orders from Oman and Iraq whose money banking sanctions stop from being collected, and multiplied import costs.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the resistance economy's principles as the Grade 9 book's box gives them.
**Answer:** In hard conditions: have the least waste of cost, stand on your own feet, support national production, avoid extravagance, and use resources correctly — a policy adopted on the Leader's guidance.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** How does using domestic capacity make an economy resilient, in this lesson's account?
**Answer:** When the outside road is closed by sanctions, an economy that can make its own inputs at home — through its industrial universities and national production — keeps producing instead of stopping; it stands on its own feet.
