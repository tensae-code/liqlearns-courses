# Video script — Investing in human capital

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Why do students study for years instead of earning at once? France's Seconde manual answers like an economist: studies are an investment — an investment in human capital. You pay now: the cost of the studies themselves, and the earnings you give up while you study. The manual compares what studies cost in France, in Europe and in the USA. And you collect later, for a whole working life: a higher salary, but also well-being and capabilities — the things a person becomes able to do.

Whether that investment is open to everyone depends on public education policy — on how much of the cost the public carries.
