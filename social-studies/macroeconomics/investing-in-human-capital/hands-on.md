# Hands-on — Investing in human capital

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Tell the story of one person's studies as an investment, out loud: what did the studies cost, in money and in time, and what did they return? Finish with the manual's three returns: salary, well-being and capabilities.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the investment as a timeline: the study years on the left with their costs, the working years on the right with their returns. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare, and add every cost or return you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
