# Investing in human capital — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why does the manual call studies an investment?
**Answer:** Because the student pays real costs now — the cost of the studies and the earnings given up — for returns collected later, like any other investment; the capital built is human capital.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What are the two parts of the cost of studies?
**Answer:** The direct cost of the studies themselves, and the earnings the student does not collect while studying.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What role does public education policy play in this lesson?
**Answer:** Policy decides how much of the cost of studies is carried publicly, and so decides for whom the investment in human capital is within reach.
