# Investing in human capital — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Human capital is the knowledge, skill and capability carried in the person.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The cost of studies is the same everywhere, so where a student studies does not matter.
**Answer: False** — the manual compares France, Europe and the USA precisely because the cost differs, and public policy decides how much of it falls on the student.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A few expensive study years can pay for themselves many times over, because the salary return is collected across a whole working life.
**Answer: True**
