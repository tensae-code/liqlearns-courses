# Investing in human capital — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Studies are an investment in _____ capital.
**Answer: human**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Beside the direct cost of studies, the student pays with the _____ given up during the study years.
**Answer: earnings**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The manual's returns of studies are salary, well-being and _____.
**Answer: capabilities**
