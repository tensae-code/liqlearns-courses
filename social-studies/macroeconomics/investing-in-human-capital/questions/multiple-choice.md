# Investing in human capital — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In France's manual, studies are treated as —
a) a pure expense with no return  b) an investment in human capital — a cost paid now for a return collected later  c) a tax
**Answer: b) an investment in human capital — a cost paid now for a return collected later**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The manual compares the cost of studies in —
a) France, Europe and the USA  b) three French towns  c) three centuries
**Answer: a) France, Europe and the USA**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Besides salary, the manual counts among the returns of studies —
a) only exam marks  b) well-being and capabilities  c) nothing else
**Answer: b) well-being and capabilities**
