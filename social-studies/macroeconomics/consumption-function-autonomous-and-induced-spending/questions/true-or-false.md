# The consumption function — autonomous and induced spending — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Disposable income is gross income before any tax is taken away.
**Answer: False — Disposable income is what is left after personal taxes — gross income minus personal taxes.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Autonomous consumption stops completely when income falls to zero.
**Answer: False — It continues at zero income — that is what 'autonomous' means here; it is financed from past savings or borrowing.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Illness raises consumption by moving the household along its consumption function.
**Answer: False — Illness is a non-income change: it shifts the whole function, it does not move the household along it.**
