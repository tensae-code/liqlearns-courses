# The consumption function — autonomous and induced spending — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Disposable income — what can be spent or saved" in your own words.
**Answer:** Consumption rises with disposable income, which is gross income minus personal taxes.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Autonomous consumption — spending at zero income" in your own words.
**Answer:** Autonomous consumption is the survival spending that continues even at zero income, paid from past savings or borrowing.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Induced consumption — the part income pulls up" in your own words.
**Answer:** Induced consumption is the fraction of each extra birr that is spent; non-income shocks like illness shift the whole function.
