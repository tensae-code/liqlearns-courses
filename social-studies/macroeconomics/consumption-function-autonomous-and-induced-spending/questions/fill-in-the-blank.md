# The consumption function — autonomous and induced spending — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or figure from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Consumption is built from ____ income — gross income minus personal taxes.
**Answer: disposable**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The spending that continues even at zero income is called ____ consumption.
**Answer: autonomous**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The part of consumption that rises with each extra birr is called ____ consumption.
**Answer: induced**
