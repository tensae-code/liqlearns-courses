# The consumption function — autonomous and induced spending — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Disposable income — what can be spent or saved"?
a) Consumption rises with disposable income, which is gross income minus personal taxes.  b) Autonomous consumption is the survival spending that continues even at zero income, paid from past savings or borrowing.  c) Induced consumption is the fraction of each extra birr that is spent; non-income shocks like illness shift the whole function.
**Answer: a) Consumption rises with disposable income, which is gross income minus personal taxes.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Autonomous consumption — spending at zero income"?
a) Induced consumption is the fraction of each extra birr that is spent; non-income shocks like illness shift the whole function.  b) Consumption rises with disposable income, which is gross income minus personal taxes.  c) Autonomous consumption is the survival spending that continues even at zero income, paid from past savings or borrowing.
**Answer: c) Autonomous consumption is the survival spending that continues even at zero income, paid from past savings or borrowing.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Induced consumption — the part income pulls up"?
a) Autonomous consumption is the survival spending that continues even at zero income, paid from past savings or borrowing.  b) Induced consumption is the fraction of each extra birr that is spent; non-income shocks like illness shift the whole function.  c) Consumption rises with disposable income, which is gross income minus personal taxes.
**Answer: b) Induced consumption is the fraction of each extra birr that is spent; non-income shocks like illness shift the whole function.**
