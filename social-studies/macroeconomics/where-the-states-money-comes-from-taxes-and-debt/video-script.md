The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Where the states money comes from taxes and debt — as Colombia's book teaches it, says the narrator: The Colombian state needs money for justice, security, education and health and for economic activities where private capital is scarce; Its income comes from two primary sources — the taxes citizens pay (obliged contributions) and borrowing; In the 1980s Latin American countries took large loans from international banks and bodies such as the IMF, the IDB and the World Bank to face their economic crises (recession is defined in the margin: a period of low production)
