# India's anti-poverty strategy — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** India's strategy rests on —
a) growth plus targeted programmes  b) growth alone  c) programmes alone
**Answer: a) growth plus targeted programmes**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** MGNREGA guarantees —
a) 100 days of wage employment  b) 10 days of work  c) a free house
**Answer: a) 100 days of wage employment**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Mixed results trace to —
a) implementation and targeting  b) the programmes' names  c) too much growth
**Answer: a) implementation and targeting**
