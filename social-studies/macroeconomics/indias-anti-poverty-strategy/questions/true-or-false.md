# India's anti-poverty strategy — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** India's strategy has only one plank.
**Answer: False — it has two: growth and targeted programmes.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** SGSY works through self-help groups with bank credit and subsidy.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The programmes all worked exactly as planned.
**Answer: False — results were mixed, through implementation and targeting.**
