# India's anti-poverty strategy — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the two planks of the strategy.
**Answer:** Promoting economic growth and targeted programmes.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name two targeted programmes and what each does.
**Answer:** Any two: MGNREGA (100 days of wage work), PMRY/REGP (self-employment), SGSY (self-help groups with bank credit and subsidy), PMGY (basic services).

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Why have results been mixed?
**Answer:** Because of implementation and targeting failures.
