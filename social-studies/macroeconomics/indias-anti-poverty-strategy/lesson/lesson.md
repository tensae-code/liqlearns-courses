# India's anti-poverty strategy

## Do it first

## Sub lesson 1 — Two planks — growth and targeted programmes

India's anti-poverty strategy has **two planks: promoting economic growth** and **targeted anti-poverty programmes**.

## Sub lesson 2 — The programmes — MGNREGA, PMRY, REGP, SGSY, PMGY

The targeted programmes include **MGNREGA's 100 days of wage employment**, **PMRY and REGP self-employment**, **SGSY self-help groups with bank credit and subsidy**, and **PMGY basic services**.

## Sub lesson 3 — Mixed results — implementation and targeting

The programmes' **mixed results trace to implementation and targeting** — help not always reaching the people it was meant for, in the way it was meant to arrive.
