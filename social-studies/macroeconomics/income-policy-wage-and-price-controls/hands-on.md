# Hands-on — Income policy — wage and price controls

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Guideline or freeze
Draft a one-page voluntary wage-price guideline for an invented economy, then rewrite its strongest clause as a mandatory freeze. Compare what each version does to a small shop's decisions.

## Productivity pay
A firm's output per worker rises 5%. Show a wage settlement that shares it so real wages grow with productivity, and one that breaks the rule — with the inflation consequence of each.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
