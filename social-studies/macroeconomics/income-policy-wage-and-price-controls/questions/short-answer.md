# Income policy — wage and price controls — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Controlling wages and prices directly" in your own words.
**Answer:** Income policies are economy-wide wage and price controls, from guidelines to freezes.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "When they are used" in your own words.
**Answer:** Income policy is used when fiscal and monetary restraint fails, or as their complement.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "The developing-country twist" in your own words.
**Answer:** In developing countries income policy can keep real wages growing with productivity to sustain domestic demand.
