# Income policy — wage and price controls — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Income policies are economy-wide wage and price controls, from guidelines to freezes.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Income policy is used when fiscal and monetary restraint fails, or as their complement.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In developing countries income policy can keep real wages growing with productivity to sustain domestic demand.
**Answer: True**
