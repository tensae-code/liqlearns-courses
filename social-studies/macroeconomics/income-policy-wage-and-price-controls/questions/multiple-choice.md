# Income policy — wage and price controls — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Controlling wages and prices directly"?
a) Income policies are economy-wide wage and price controls, from guidelines to freezes  b) Income policy is used when fiscal and monetary restraint fails, or as their complement  c) In developing countries income policy can keep real wages growing with productivity to sustain domestic demand
**Answer: a) Income policies are economy-wide wage and price controls, from guidelines to freezes**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "When they are used"?
a) Income policy is used when fiscal and monetary restraint fails, or as their complement  b) In developing countries income policy can keep real wages growing with productivity to sustain domestic demand  c) Income policies are economy-wide wage and price controls, from guidelines to freezes
**Answer: a) Income policy is used when fiscal and monetary restraint fails, or as their complement**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "The developing-country twist"?
a) In developing countries income policy can keep real wages growing with productivity to sustain domestic demand  b) Income policies are economy-wide wage and price controls, from guidelines to freezes  c) Income policy is used when fiscal and monetary restraint fails, or as their complement
**Answer: a) In developing countries income policy can keep real wages growing with productivity to sustain domestic demand**
