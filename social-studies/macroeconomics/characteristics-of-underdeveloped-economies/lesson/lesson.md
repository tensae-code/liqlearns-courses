# Characteristics of underdeveloped economies

## Do it first

## Sub lesson 1 — The marks of underdevelopment

Underdeveloped economies are marked by low income, dependence on agriculture and high population growth.

## Sub lesson 2 — More marks of underdevelopment

Weak industry, poor infrastructure and dependence on a few exports complete the picture of underdevelopment.

## Sub lesson 3 — Explanations of development

Explanations of development ask why some economies break out of underdevelopment while others stay trapped in it.
