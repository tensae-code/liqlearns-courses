# Characteristics of underdeveloped economies — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What marks an underdeveloped economy?
**Answer:** Underdeveloped economies are marked by low income, dependence on agriculture and high population growth.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What further marks complete the picture?
**Answer:** Weak industry, poor infrastructure and dependence on a few exports complete the picture of underdevelopment.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What do explanations of development ask?
**Answer:** Explanations of development ask why some economies break out of underdevelopment while others stay trapped in it.
