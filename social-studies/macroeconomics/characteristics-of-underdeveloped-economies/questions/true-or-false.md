# Characteristics of underdeveloped economies — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Underdeveloped economies are marked by the highest incomes.
**Answer: False** — Underdeveloped economies are marked by low income, dependence on agriculture and high population growth.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Strong industry is a mark of underdevelopment.
**Answer: False** — Weak industry, poor infrastructure and dependence on a few exports complete the picture of underdevelopment.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** No economy has ever broken out of underdevelopment.
**Answer: False** — Explanations of development ask why some economies break out of underdevelopment while others stay trapped in it.
