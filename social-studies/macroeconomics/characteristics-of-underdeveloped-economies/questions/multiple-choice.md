# Characteristics of underdeveloped economies — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** About the marks of underdevelopment — the lesson teaches that —
a) underdeveloped economies are marked by low income, dependence on agriculture and high population growth.  b) weak industry, poor infrastructure and dependence on a few exports complete the picture of underdevelopment.  c) explanations of development ask why some economies break out of underdevelopment while others stay trapped in it.
