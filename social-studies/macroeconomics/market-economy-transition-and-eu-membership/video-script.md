# Video script — Bulgaria — market-economy transition and EU membership

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Bulgaria's economy in four stages: craft towns and the first Sliven textile factory of 1834; a small farming country with its National Bank; the socialist model of 1946 to 1989 — nationalisation and cooperatives; then the hard transition after 1989 — privatisation, exchanges, land returned, foreign investment — and since 2007, the European Union: stability, European funds and free movement of people, goods, services and capital.
