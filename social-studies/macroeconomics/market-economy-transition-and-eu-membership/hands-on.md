# Hands-on — Bulgaria — market-economy transition and EU membership

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Build the timeline
Draw a line from 1834 to 2007. Place the first Sliven factory, the Liberation, the Bulgarian National Bank, 1946, 1989 and 2007 on it, with one line under each saying what changed in the economy.

## Sort the ownership
Write private, state, cooperative and mixed on four slips. For a school, a family shop, a farm cooperative and a partly state-owned firm, place the slip that fits — the transition's equal standing of ownership in your own settlement.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach sub lesson 2 aloud — the socialist model and the transition — as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
