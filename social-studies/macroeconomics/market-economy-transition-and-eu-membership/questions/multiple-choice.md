# Bulgaria — market-economy transition and EU membership — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Bulgaria's first textile factory was founded at Sliven in —
a) 1878  b) 1834  c) 1946
**Answer: b) 1834**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The socialist model of 1946–1989 rested on —
a) nationalisation of factories and mines and land united in cooperatives  b) private ownership of everything  c) EU membership
**Answer: a) nationalisation of factories and mines and land united in cooperatives**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Bulgaria has been a member of the European Union since —
a) 1989  b) 2007  c) 2017
**Answer: b) 2007**
