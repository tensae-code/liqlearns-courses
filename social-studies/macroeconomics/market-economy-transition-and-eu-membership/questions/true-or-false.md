# Bulgaria — market-economy transition and EU membership — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In the transition, the agrarian reform returned the land to its owners.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Privatisation after 1989 brought no unemployment — employment rose at once.
**Answer: False — as a consequence of privatisation unemployment was formed and the percentage of the employed and the quantity of production decreased, while the country lost many markets.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** EU membership gives free movement of labour resources, goods, services and capital.
**Answer: True**
