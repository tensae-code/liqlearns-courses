# Bulgaria — market-economy transition and EU membership — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Tell the four stages of Bulgaria's economy as the Grade 7 book gives them.
**Answer:** Craft centres and trade before 1878 (first textile factory at Sliven, 1834); a small agricultural country after the Liberation, with banking developing and the Bulgarian National Bank founded; the socialist model of 1946–1989, with nationalisation and land in cooperatives; and after 1989 the transition to a market economy, with EU membership from 2007.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name four changes of the post-1989 reform from the Grade 10 book.
**Answer:** Any four of: equal standing of private, state, cooperative and mixed ownership; privatisation with foreign capitals; commodity, labour and stock exchanges and privatisation funds formed; the agrarian reform returning land to its owners; restitution of large city property; denationalisation of large industrial enterprises; foreign investments attracted; price and quality becoming the basic requirements of the internal market.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does EU membership give Bulgaria's economy, in the book's words?
**Answer:** Stability and help through the European funds, free movement of labour resources, goods, services and capital, and travel in the EU countries with an identity card, without visas.
