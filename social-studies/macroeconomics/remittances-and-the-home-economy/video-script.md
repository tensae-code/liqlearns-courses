# Video script — Remittances and the home economy

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Remittances are money sent home by people working abroad — in Honduras's case, some 1.8 million Hondurans.

The book's first verdict: a strong boost to the national economy.

Its second verdict, taught together with the first: remittances demotivate production at home and drain the productive labour force.
