# Remittances and the home economy — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Some _____ million Hondurans abroad send remittances home.
**Answer: 1.8**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Remittances are a strong _____ to the national economy.
**Answer: boost**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Remittances demotivate _____ and drain the productive labour force.
**Answer: production**
