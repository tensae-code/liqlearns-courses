# Remittances and the home economy — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Remittances are —
a) taxes paid at customs  b) money sent home by Hondurans working abroad  c) loans from the IMF
**Answer: b) money sent home by Hondurans working abroad**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The book's first verdict on remittances is that they are —
a) a strong boost to the national economy  b) too small to matter  c) illegal
**Answer: a) a strong boost to the national economy**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The book's warning is that remittances also —
a) raise the lempira  b) demotivate production and drain the productive labour force  c) stop all migration
**Answer: b) demotivate production and drain the productive labour force**
