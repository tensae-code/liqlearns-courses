# Remittances and the home economy — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Remittances are sent by about 1.8 thousand Hondurans abroad.
**Answer: False** — The book says some 1.8 million Hondurans abroad.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The book teaches remittances are only a boost and never a cost.
**Answer: False** — It teaches both together: a strong boost that also demotivates production and drains the labour force.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The labour-force drain happens because the senders are workers the home economy has lost.
**Answer: True**
