# Remittances and the home economy — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What are remittances, in the book's teaching?
**Answer:** Money sent home by Hondurans working abroad — from some 1.8 million Hondurans abroad.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What good do remittances do, as the book teaches it?
**Answer:** They are a strong boost to the national economy — money earned abroad flows into Honduran homes and spending.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What harm does the book teach alongside the boost?
**Answer:** Remittances demotivate production at home and drain the productive labour force, because the senders are workers the home economy has lost.
