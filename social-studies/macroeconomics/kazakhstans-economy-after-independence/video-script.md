# Video script — Kazakhstan's economy after independence

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Kazakhstan's Grade 10 books open with the country's own story, says the narrator: after 1991 came the post-Soviet crisis, and markets had to be rebuilt. Small and medium business did much of the rebuilding, with the state supporting domestic business — and Schumpeter's entrepreneur stands in the book as the engine of economic development.
