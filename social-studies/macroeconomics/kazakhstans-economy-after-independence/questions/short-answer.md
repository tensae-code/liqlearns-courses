# Kazakhstan's economy after independence — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does Aganina's historical excursion teach about Kazakhstan after 1991?
**Answer:** The post-Soviet crisis and the rebuilding of markets after 1991.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What two supports for the rebuilding do the books name?
**Answer:** Small and medium business playing its role in the new economy, and the state's support for domestic business.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Who is Schumpeter's entrepreneur, in the book's teaching?
**Answer:** The engine of economic development — the figure who drives development forward.
