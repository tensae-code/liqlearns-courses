# Kazakhstan's economy after independence — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Kazakhstan's market economy was already fully built before 1991.
**Answer: False — the book teaches the post-Soviet crisis and the rebuilding of markets after 1991.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Duisenkhanov's Lesson 2 teaches the state's support for domestic business.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Schumpeter's entrepreneur is taught as a brake on economic development.
**Answer: False — as the engine of economic development.**
