# Kazakhstan's economy after independence — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** After 1991, Kazakhstan's economy went through —
a) an unbroken boom  b) the post-Soviet crisis and a rebuilding of markets  c) a return to barter only
**Answer: b) the post-Soviet crisis and a rebuilding of markets**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In the rebuilding, a central role is given to —
a) foreign armies  b) small and medium business  c) state shops only
**Answer: b) small and medium business**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In Schumpeter's account, as the book teaches it, the engine of economic development is —
a) the tax office  b) the entrepreneur  c) the customs post
**Answer: b) the entrepreneur**
