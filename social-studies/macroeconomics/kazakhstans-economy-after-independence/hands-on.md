# Hands-on — Kazakhstan's economy after independence

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Build the timeline
On paper, draw a line for Kazakhstan's economy: 1991 at the left, today at the right. Mark the post-Soviet crisis and the rebuilding of markets on it. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare, and fix anything missing.

## Say it with things at home
Put three stones on the table — one for the crisis, one for small and medium business, one for the state's support — and say what each stone did in the rebuilding. Then say which stone is Schumpeter's engine.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 3 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
