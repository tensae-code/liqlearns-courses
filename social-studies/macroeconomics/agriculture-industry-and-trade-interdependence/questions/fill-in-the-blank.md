# Agriculture, industry and trade — how they hold each other up — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Farms feed industry: _____ and produce sugar, so rising farm output feeds industry.
**Answer: sugar factories buy the farmers' sugarcane**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Fertiliser factories, _____, easier industry setup, loan concessions and market promotion support farming in return.
**Answer: crop insurance**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Investment in _____ grows with domestic industry and export prospects, so agriculture, industry and trade deepen each other's work.
**Answer: tea and cardamom**
