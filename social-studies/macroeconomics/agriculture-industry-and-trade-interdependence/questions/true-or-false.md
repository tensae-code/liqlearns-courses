# Agriculture, industry and trade — how they hold each other up — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Farms feed industry: sugar factories buy the farmers' sugarcane and produce sugar, so rising farm output feeds industry.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Fertiliser factories, crop insurance, easier industry setup, loan concessions and market promotion support farming in return.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Investment in something else entirely grows with domestic industry and export prospects, so agriculture, industry and trade deepen each other's work.
**Answer: False** — Investment in tea and cardamom grows with domestic industry and export prospects, so agriculture, industry and trade deepen each other's work.
