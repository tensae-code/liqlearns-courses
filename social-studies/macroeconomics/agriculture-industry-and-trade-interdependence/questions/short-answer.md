# Agriculture, industry and trade — how they hold each other up — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the book teach about farms feed industry?
**Answer:** Farms feed industry: sugar factories buy the farmers' sugarcane and produce sugar, so rising farm output feeds industry.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the book teach about industry and trade support farming?
**Answer:** Fertiliser factories, crop insurance, easier industry setup, loan concessions and market promotion support farming in return.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the book teach about trade carries both?
**Answer:** Investment in tea and cardamom grows with domestic industry and export prospects, so agriculture, industry and trade deepen each other's work.
