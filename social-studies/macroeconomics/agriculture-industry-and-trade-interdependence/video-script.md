# Video script — Agriculture, industry and trade — how they hold each other up

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Agriculture, industry and trade hold each other up: each one feeds the others.

Farms feed industry: sugar factories buy the farmers' sugarcane and produce sugar, so rising farm output feeds industry.

Fertiliser factories, crop insurance, easier industry setup, loan concessions and market promotion support farming in return.

Investment in tea and cardamom grows with domestic industry and export prospects, so agriculture, industry and trade deepen each other's work.
