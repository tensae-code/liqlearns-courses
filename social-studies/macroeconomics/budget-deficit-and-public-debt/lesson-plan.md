# Lesson plan — Budget deficit and public debt

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Macroeconomics · **Lesson:** Budget deficit and public debt
**Place in the curriculum:** Macroeconomics (CURRICULUM.md) — built in the Economics expansion (Albania, 2026-11-16).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** When a government spends more in a year than it takes in, the gap is the budget deficit; borrowing to fill the gap year after year piles up the public debt — and both the deficit and the debt carry gains, costs and hard choices about bringing them down.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the learner's own household picture — a family that spends more than it earns in a month borrows, and its borrowings add up.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of ..." in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.
