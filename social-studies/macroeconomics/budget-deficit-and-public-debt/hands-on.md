# Hands-on — Budget deficit and public debt

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Run a household budget
Take a family's month: income 100, spending 115. Work out the month's deficit, borrow it on paper, and repeat for six months, adding a small interest charge each month. Say when the debt starts to bite, and name one spending cut and one income rise that would stop it growing.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the chain from memory: deficit → borrowing → public debt → interest → next year's expenditure. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare, and fix what you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
