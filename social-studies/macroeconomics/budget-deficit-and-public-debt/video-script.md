# Video script — Budget deficit and public debt

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

When a government spends more in a year than it takes in, the gap is the budget deficit. The government borrows to fill it.

Year after year, those borrowings pile up into the public debt — and the interest on the debt becomes part of next year's spending.

A deficit can fund investment and support the economy, but it burdens the future. Reducing it means raising revenue, cutting spending, or growing the economy — every route a choice with winners and losers.
