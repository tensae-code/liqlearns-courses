# Budget deficit and public debt — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** When revenue is greater than expenditure, the budget has a _____.
**Answer: surplus**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** One year's deficit is a flow; the public debt is the _____ it flows into.
**Answer: stock**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Borrowing to fill a deficit can finance _____ whose returns come later, such as roads and schools.
**Answer: investment**
