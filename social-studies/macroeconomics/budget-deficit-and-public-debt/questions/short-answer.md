# Budget deficit and public debt — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the link between the budget deficit and the public debt?
**Answer:** A deficit must be covered by borrowing, and that borrowing adds to the public debt; the debt's interest then becomes part of future expenditure, making future deficits more likely.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Give one argument for a deficit and one against it.
**Answer:** For: it can support the economy in a downturn and fund investment whose returns come later. Against: it adds to the debt and its interest bill and leaves less room to act in an emergency (any one of each).

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name the three routes to bringing a deficit down.
**Answer:** Raise revenue, cut expenditure, or grow the economy so the same debt weighs less against a larger income.
