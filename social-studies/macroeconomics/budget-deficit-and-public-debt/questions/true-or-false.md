# Budget deficit and public debt — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A deficit adds to the public debt.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Interest on past borrowing is part of a government's expenditure.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A large public debt costs nothing, because the government never has to think about it again.
**Answer: False** — the debt carries an interest bill inside each year's expenditure, and a debt growing faster than the ability to pay burdens future taxpayers.
