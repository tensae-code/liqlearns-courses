# Budget deficit and public debt — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A budget deficit is —
a) the total a government owes  b) the gap in a year when expenditure is greater than revenue  c) a tax on imports
**Answer: b) the gap in a year when expenditure is greater than revenue**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The public debt is —
a) one year's deficit  b) the total the government owes from past borrowing not yet repaid  c) the interest rate
**Answer: b) the total the government owes from past borrowing not yet repaid**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A proposal to reduce a deficit could be —
a) collect taxes better or spend more efficiently  b) stop keeping a budget  c) print the budget on smaller paper
**Answer: a) collect taxes better or spend more efficiently**
