# Video script — The circular flow of income

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Production, income and expenditure circulate continuously around the economy: one agent's expenditure is another agent's income. Money paid out for goods returns as revenue; revenue is paid out as incomes; incomes are spent again. The flow has no natural starting point — it is a circle, not a line.

The book traces one extra phone sale: selling one more phone raises expenditure and income by the same amount. If no new worker was hired, the extra income lands as profit; if one was hired, it lands as wages. Either way the same payment is an expenditure on one ledger and an income on another.

The circle is the reason the expenditure and income approaches must arrive at the same GDP: they are measuring the same flow at two different points on its round. Where the value-added approach avoids counting the flow twice, the circle explains why counting it by spending or by receiving gives one answer, not two.
