# Hands-on — The circular flow of income

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Stand in a circle passing one coin token from hand to hand, each holder naming the payment it now is — wage, spending, revenue, rent. The coin never rests: that is the point.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — a circular diagram of households and firms with money flowing one way and goods the other — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
