# The circular flow of income — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Production, income and expenditure _____ continuously — one agent's spending is another's income.
**Answer: circulate**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** One more phone sold raises expenditure and income by the _____.
**Answer: same amount**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The expenditure and income approaches agree because both measure the same _____.
**Answer: circular flow**
