# The circular flow of income — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "One agent's spending is another's income" in your own words.
**Answer:** One agent's expenditure is another agent's income, so production, income and spending circulate continuously.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "The phone example" in your own words.
**Answer:** Selling one more phone raises expenditure and income by the same amount.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Why the approaches agree" in your own words.
**Answer:** The expenditure and income approaches agree because they measure the same circular flow at different points.
