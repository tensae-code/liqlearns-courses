# The circular flow of income — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** One agent's expenditure is another agent's income, so production, income and spending circulate continuously.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Selling one more phone raises expenditure and income by the same amount.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The expenditure and income approaches agree because they measure the same circular flow at different points.
**Answer: True**
