# The circular flow of income — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "One agent's spending is another's income"?
a) expenditure and income are the same payments seen from two sides, circulating endlessly  b) one more phone sold raises expenditure and income equally — as profit, or as wages if a worker was hired  c) the two approaches measure one circular flow at two points, so they agree
**Answer: a) expenditure and income are the same payments seen from two sides, circulating endlessly**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "The phone example"?
a) expenditure and income are the same payments seen from two sides, circulating endlessly  b) the two approaches measure one circular flow at two points, so they agree  c) one more phone sold raises expenditure and income equally — as profit, or as wages if a worker was hired
**Answer: c) one more phone sold raises expenditure and income equally — as profit, or as wages if a worker was hired**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Why the approaches agree"?
a) one more phone sold raises expenditure and income equally — as profit, or as wages if a worker was hired  b) the two approaches measure one circular flow at two points, so they agree  c) expenditure and income are the same payments seen from two sides, circulating endlessly
**Answer: b) the two approaches measure one circular flow at two points, so they agree**
