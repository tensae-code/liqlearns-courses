# The Triangular Plan and conditionality — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Triangular Plan was financed by Bolivia alone.
**Answer: False** — its credits were American, West German and IDB money.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The credits came with no conditions at all.
**Answer: False** — closures, dismissals and foreign oversight were the conditions.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Bolivia accepted the Soviet smelter and its credit.
**Answer: False** — abstaining from it was one of the plan's conditions.
