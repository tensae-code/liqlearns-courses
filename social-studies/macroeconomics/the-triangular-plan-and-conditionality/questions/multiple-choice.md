# The Triangular Plan and conditionality — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** About credits to rehabilitate the nationalised mines — the lesson teaches that —
a) US, West German and IDB credits under the Triangular Plan were to rehabilitate the nationalised mining industry  b) the conditions were closing deficit mines, dismissing a fifth of COMIBOL's workforce, and the financiers' advisers overseeing the plan  c) Bolivia had to refuse the Soviet offer of a tin smelter with a US$150 million credit
**Answer: a) US, West German and IDB credits under the Triangular Plan were to rehabilitate the nationalised mining industry**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** About the conditions — closures, dismissals, overseers — the lesson teaches that —
a) US, West German and IDB credits under the Triangular Plan were to rehabilitate the nationalised mining industry  b) the conditions were closing deficit mines, dismissing a fifth of COMIBOL's workforce, and the financiers' advisers overseeing the plan  c) Bolivia had to refuse the Soviet offer of a tin smelter with a US$150 million credit
**Answer: b) the conditions were closing deficit mines, dismissing a fifth of COMIBOL's workforce, and the financiers' advisers overseeing the plan**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** About the Soviet smelter offer Bolivia had to refuse — the lesson teaches that —
a) US, West German and IDB credits under the Triangular Plan were to rehabilitate the nationalised mining industry  b) the conditions were closing deficit mines, dismissing a fifth of COMIBOL's workforce, and the financiers' advisers overseeing the plan  c) Bolivia had to refuse the Soviet offer of a tin smelter with a US$150 million credit
**Answer: c) Bolivia had to refuse the Soviet offer of a tin smelter with a US$150 million credit**
