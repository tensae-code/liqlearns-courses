# The Triangular Plan and conditionality — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the lesson teach about credits to rehabilitate the nationalised mines?
**Answer:** US, West German and IDB credits under the Triangular Plan were to rehabilitate the nationalised mining industry.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the lesson teach about the conditions — closures, dismissals, overseers?
**Answer:** The conditions were closing deficit mines, dismissing a fifth of COMIBOL's workforce, and the financiers' advisers overseeing the plan.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the lesson teach about the Soviet smelter offer Bolivia had to refuse?
**Answer:** Bolivia had to refuse the Soviet offer of a tin smelter with a US$150 million credit.
