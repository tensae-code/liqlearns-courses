The Triangular Plan and conditionality — as Bolivia's book teaches it, says the narrator: US, West German and IDB credits under the Triangular Plan were to rehabilitate the nationalised mining industry; the conditions were closing deficit mines, dismissing a fifth of COMIBOL's workforce, and the financiers' advisers overseeing the plan; Bolivia had to refuse the Soviet offer of a tin smelter with a US$150 million credit.
