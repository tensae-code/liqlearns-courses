# Video script — Terms of trade

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Terms of trade — as Nigeria's book teaches it, says the narrator: the terms of trade compare export prices with import prices — how much import each unit of export buys.; terms of trade are favourable when export prices rise against import prices, so exports buy more imports.; terms of trade are unfavourable when export prices fall against import prices, so the country must export more to buy the same imports..
