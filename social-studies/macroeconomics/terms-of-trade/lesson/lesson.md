# Terms of trade

## Do it first

## Sub lesson 1 — What terms of trade means

The terms of trade compare export prices with import prices — how much import each unit of export buys.

## Sub lesson 2 — Favourable terms of trade

Terms of trade are favourable when export prices rise against import prices, so exports buy more imports.

## Sub lesson 3 — Unfavourable terms of trade

Terms of trade are unfavourable when export prices fall against import prices, so the country must export more to buy the same imports.
