# Terms of trade — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** About what terms of trade means — the lesson teaches that —
a) the terms of trade compare export prices with import prices — how much import each unit of export buys.  b) terms of trade are favourable when export prices rise against import prices, so exports buy more imports.  c) terms of trade are unfavourable when export prices fall against import prices, so the country must export more to buy the same imports.
**Answer: a) the terms of trade compare export prices with import prices — how much import each unit of export buys.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** About favourable terms of trade — the lesson teaches that —
a) the terms of trade compare export prices with import prices — how much import each unit of export buys.  b) terms of trade are favourable when export prices rise against import prices, so exports buy more imports.  c) terms of trade are unfavourable when export prices fall against import prices, so the country must export more to buy the same imports.
**Answer: b) terms of trade are favourable when export prices rise against import prices, so exports buy more imports.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** About unfavourable terms of trade — the lesson teaches that —
a) the terms of trade compare export prices with import prices — how much import each unit of export buys.  b) terms of trade are favourable when export prices rise against import prices, so exports buy more imports.  c) terms of trade are unfavourable when export prices fall against import prices, so the country must export more to buy the same imports.
**Answer: c) terms of trade are unfavourable when export prices fall against import prices, so the country must export more to buy the same imports.**
