# Terms of trade — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What do the terms of trade compare?
**Answer:** The terms of trade compare export prices with import prices — how much import each unit of export buys.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** When are terms of trade favourable?
**Answer:** Terms of trade are favourable when export prices rise against import prices, so exports buy more imports.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** When are terms of trade unfavourable?
**Answer:** Terms of trade are unfavourable when export prices fall against import prices, so the country must export more to buy the same imports.
