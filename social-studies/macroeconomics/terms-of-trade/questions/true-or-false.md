# Terms of trade — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Terms of trade compare a country's population with its land area.
**Answer: False** — The terms of trade compare export prices with import prices — how much import each unit of export buys.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Favourable terms of trade mean exports buy fewer imports.
**Answer: False** — Terms of trade are favourable when export prices rise against import prices, so exports buy more imports.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Unfavourable terms of trade mean imports become free.
**Answer: False** — Terms of trade are unfavourable when export prices fall against import prices, so the country must export more to buy the same imports.
