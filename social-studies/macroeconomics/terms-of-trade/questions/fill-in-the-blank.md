# Terms of trade — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Terms of trade compare export prices with _____ prices.
**Answer: import**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Terms of trade are favourable when exports buy _____ imports.
**Answer: more**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** When terms of trade are unfavourable, the country must export _____ to buy the same imports.
**Answer: more**
