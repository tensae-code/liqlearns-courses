Sri Lanka's own way (Grade 11 Social Education 1996): a whole Social Education year given to one subject — development — and Sri Lanka is the case study throughout: its own PQLI of 82 against a GNP of only $540, its plantation past, its dependency ratio worked out from the 1981 census, and its post-1977 turn told sector by sector.

Sri Lanka's own way (Grade 11 Social Education and History 2000): world trade taught off the Central Bank's exchange-rate board for 14 April 2000 and Sri Lanka's own trade balances for 1997–99; the population chapter reads Sri Lanka's whole census story 1871–1998, down to a map of how many people stand on each square kilometre.
