# Hands-on — Expansionary fiscal policy

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Spend a slump budget
You have a slump and a fixed extra budget. Allocate it among public works, tax cuts and transfers, and defend the split by which moves demand fastest.

## Draw the shift
Draw AD left of LRAS, then the expansionary shift back to potential. Label the price level's recovery and the deficit that paid for it.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
