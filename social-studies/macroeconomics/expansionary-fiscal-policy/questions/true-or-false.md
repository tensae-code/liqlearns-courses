# Expansionary fiscal policy — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Expansionary fiscal policy answers deficient demand, with equilibrium left of LRAS.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Spend more on public works, cut taxes, and expand pensions, allowances and subsidies.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** AD shifts right toward potential GDP, and the deficit is accepted deliberately.
**Answer: True**
