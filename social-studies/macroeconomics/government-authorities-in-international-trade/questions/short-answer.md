# Government authorities in international trade — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the lesson teach in "The government authorities that ensure international trade procedures"?
**Answer:** The government authorities that ensure international trade procedures are followed — the Investment Promotion Authority (IPA).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the lesson teach in "The Papua New Guinea Customs Authority"?
**Answer:** The Papua New Guinea Customs Authority.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the lesson teach in "The National Agriculture Quarantine and Inspection Authority (NAQIA"?
