# Video script — Poverty and the poverty line

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

BPS, Indonesia's statistics agency, measures poverty by the ability to meet basic needs.

The poverty line is set per capita: those whose spending per person falls below the line are counted as poor.

The teacher guide sets out the criteria for who counts as poor, so poverty is measured by a rule, not by impression.
