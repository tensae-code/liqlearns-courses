# Poverty and the poverty line — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the book teach about what poverty is, as bps measures it?
**Answer:** BPS, Indonesia's statistics agency, measures poverty by the ability to meet basic needs.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the book teach about the per-capita poverty line?
**Answer:** The poverty line is set per capita: those whose spending per person falls below the line are counted as poor.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the book teach about the criteria for the poor?
**Answer:** The teacher guide sets out the criteria for who counts as poor, so poverty is measured by a rule, not by impression.
