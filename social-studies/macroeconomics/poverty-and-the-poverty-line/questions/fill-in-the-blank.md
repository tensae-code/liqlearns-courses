# Poverty and the poverty line — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** BPS, Indonesia's statistics agency, measures poverty by the ability to meet _____.
**Answer: basic needs**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The poverty line is set _____: those whose spending per person falls below the line are counted as poor.
**Answer: per capita**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The teacher guide sets out the _____ for who counts as poor, so poverty is measured by a rule, not by impression.
**Answer: criteria**
