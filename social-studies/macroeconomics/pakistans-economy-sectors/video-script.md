# Video script — Pakistan's economy — sectors

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Pakistan's economy — sectors — as Pakistan's book teaches it, says the narrator: agriculture is the first sector in the book's account of Pakistan's economy; Pakistan's industry is taught in named branches — textile first, then automobile, cement, fertiliser and more; the sectors are compared by their shares of the economy.
