# Pakistan's economy — sectors — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The book opens Pakistan's economy with the stock exchange.
**Answer: False** — it opens sector by sector, with agriculture first.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The book names no branches of Pakistani industry.
**Answer: False** — it names them — textile, automobile, paint and varnish, cement, fertiliser, public-sector industries and SMEs.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The book gives no shares for the sectors.
**Answer: False** — shares are how it compares them.
