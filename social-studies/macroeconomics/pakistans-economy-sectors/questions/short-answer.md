# Pakistan's economy — sectors — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which sector does the book treat first for Pakistan?
**Answer:** Agriculture.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name four branches of Pakistan's industry from the book.
**Answer:** Any four of: textile, automobile, paint and varnish, cement, fertiliser, public-sector industries, small and medium enterprises.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** How are Pakistan's sectors compared in the book?
**Answer:** By their shares of the economy — agriculture, industry and the trade sector set side by side.
