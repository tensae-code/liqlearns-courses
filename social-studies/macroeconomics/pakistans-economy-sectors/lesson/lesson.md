## Sub lesson 1 — Agriculture

The Class 12 book's Pakistan chapters open sector by sector. First is **agriculture** — the sector the country's economy rests on, employing its people and feeding its industries with raw material.

## Sub lesson 2 — Industry — its named branches

Next, **industry** in its named branches: **textile, automobile, paint and varnish, cement, fertiliser**, the **public-sector industries**, and **small and medium enterprises**. Textiles lead the list, as they lead Pakistan's exports.

## Sub lesson 3 — The trade sector's share

The account closes with the **share of the trade sector** — how much of the economy's activity is buying, selling and moving goods, set beside the shares of agriculture and industry so the three can be compared.
