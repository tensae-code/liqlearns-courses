# Video script — Agriculture — the backbone of a country's economy

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Agriculture — the backbone of a country's economy — as South Sudan's book teaches it, says the narrator: 90% of South Sudan's land is suitable for farming and agriculture is the backbone of its economy, with forestry and fisheries also helping. Farming systems are developed through mechanisation, plantation farming, commercial farming and irrigation. Farming is done for subsistence or commercial purposes — traditional (rain-fed, pastoralism) against modern (irrigation, greenhouses, plantations) — and mixed farming saves costs because animals give manure to crops and crops give fodder to animals.
