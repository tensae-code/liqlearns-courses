# Agriculture — the backbone of a country's economy — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In your own words, what does sub lesson 1 teach about agriculture is the backbone?
**Answer:** 90% of South Sudan's land is suitable for farming and agriculture is the backbone of its economy, with forestry and fisheries also helping. About half the country's land is agricultural land and its soil, climate and water make it suitable for agriculture.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In your own words, what does sub lesson 2 teach about developing farming systems?
**Answer:** Farming systems are developed through mechanisation, plantation farming, commercial farming and irrigation.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In your own words, what does sub lesson 3 teach about subsistence, commercial and mixed farming?
**Answer:** Farming is done for subsistence or commercial purposes — traditional (rain-fed, pastoralism) against modern (irrigation, greenhouses, plantations) — and mixed farming saves costs because animals give manure to crops and crops give fodder to animals.
