# Agriculture — the backbone of a country's economy — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** 90% of South Sudan's land is suitable for farming and agriculture is the backbone of its economy, with forestry and fisheries also helping. About half the country's land is agricultural land and its soil, climate and water make it suitable for agriculture.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Farming systems are developed through mechanisation, plantation farming, commercial farming and irrigation.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Farming is done for subsistence or commercial purposes — traditional (rain-fed, pastoralism) against modern (irrigation, greenhouses, plantations) — and mixed farming saves costs because animals give manure to crops and crops give fodder to animals.
**Answer: True**
