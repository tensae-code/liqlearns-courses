# Agriculture — the backbone of a country's economy

## Sub lesson 1 — Agriculture is the backbone

**90% of South Sudan's land is suitable for farming** and **agriculture is the backbone of its economy**, with **forestry and fisheries also helping**. About half the country's land is agricultural land and its soil, climate and water make it suitable for agriculture.

## Sub lesson 2 — Developing farming systems

Farming systems are developed through **mechanisation**, **plantation farming**, **commercial farming** and **irrigation**.

## Sub lesson 3 — Subsistence, commercial and mixed farming

Farming is done for **subsistence or commercial purposes** — **traditional** (rain-fed, pastoralism) against **modern** (irrigation, greenhouses, plantations) — and **mixed farming saves costs** because animals give manure to crops and crops give fodder to animals.
