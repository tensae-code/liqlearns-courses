# Ethiopia's tax system — federal and regional — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "From the imperial tenth to the modern state" in your own words.
**Answer:** Income taxation arrived in 1944 with an imperial decree taking one-tenth of peasants' produce.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "A federal split of revenue sources" in your own words.
**Answer:** The 1995 Constitution splits revenue sources between federal government and regional states.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Eleven regions, two administrations" in your own words.
**Answer:** As of April 2022 Ethiopia had 11 regional states plus the Addis Ababa and Dire Dawa administrations.
