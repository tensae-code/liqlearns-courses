# Ethiopia's tax system — federal and regional — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Income taxation arrived in 1944 with an imperial decree taking one-tenth of peasants' produce.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The 1995 Constitution splits revenue sources between federal government and regional states.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** As of April 2022 Ethiopia had 11 regional states plus the Addis Ababa and Dire Dawa administrations.
**Answer: True**
