# Ethiopia's tax system — federal and regional — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "From the imperial tenth to the modern state"?
a) Income taxation arrived in 1944 with an imperial decree taking one-tenth of peasants' produce  b) The 1995 Constitution splits revenue sources between federal government and regional states  c) As of April 2022 Ethiopia had 11 regional states plus the Addis Ababa and Dire Dawa administrations
**Answer: a) Income taxation arrived in 1944 with an imperial decree taking one-tenth of peasants' produce**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "A federal split of revenue sources"?
a) The 1995 Constitution splits revenue sources between federal government and regional states  b) As of April 2022 Ethiopia had 11 regional states plus the Addis Ababa and Dire Dawa administrations  c) Income taxation arrived in 1944 with an imperial decree taking one-tenth of peasants' produce
**Answer: a) The 1995 Constitution splits revenue sources between federal government and regional states**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Eleven regions, two administrations"?
a) As of April 2022 Ethiopia had 11 regional states plus the Addis Ababa and Dire Dawa administrations  b) Income taxation arrived in 1944 with an imperial decree taking one-tenth of peasants' produce  c) The 1995 Constitution splits revenue sources between federal government and regional states
**Answer: a) As of April 2022 Ethiopia had 11 regional states plus the Addis Ababa and Dire Dawa administrations**
