# Hands-on — Ethiopia's tax system — federal and regional

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Two layers
Draw a map-style diagram of Ethiopia with two layers of revenue offices, federal and state, and one reserved tax source labelled on each layer from the lesson.

## Source sort
List six revenue sources from this unit so far. For each, argue whether it belongs to the federal level, the state level, or is shared — marking clearly which claims the lesson supports and which are your reasoning.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
