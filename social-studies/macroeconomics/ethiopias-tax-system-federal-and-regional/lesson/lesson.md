# Ethiopia's tax system — federal and regional

## Do it first

Income tax in Ethiopia began with a ruler taking one-tenth of peasants' produce. Which year, which ruler's era — and who writes the tax rules today? Guess, then read.

## Sub lesson 1 — From the imperial tenth to the modern state

Income taxation arrived in 1944 with an imperial decree taking one-tenth of peasants' produce. Today the federal Ministry of Revenues prepares the fiscal decrees and directives for parliament.

## Sub lesson 2 — A federal split of revenue sources

The framework rests on Proclamation No. 33/1992 and the 1995 FDRE Constitution, which split revenue sources between the federal government (Article 51(10)) and the regional states (Article 52(2)(e)). Each level taxes its reserved sources and draws up its own budget.

## Sub lesson 3 — Eleven regions, two administrations

Ethiopia as of April 2022 had 11 regional states plus the Addis Ababa and Dire Dawa administrations. The map matters: two layers of revenue offices, federal and state, work the same country.
