# Hands-on — Nominal and real GDP

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Hold the same basket of goods twice. First price it with this year's price cards (nominal), then with the base year's older cards (real). The goods never changed — only the price tags did.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — two bars for the same year — a tall nominal bar and a shorter real bar, the gap labelled price rises — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
