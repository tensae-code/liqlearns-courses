# Nominal and real GDP — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Nominal GDP values output at current prices, so nominal growth overstates performance under inflation.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Real GDP values output at constant base-year prices and rises only when quantities rise.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Real GDP growth is driven by more capital and labour, or by productivity gains.
**Answer: True**
