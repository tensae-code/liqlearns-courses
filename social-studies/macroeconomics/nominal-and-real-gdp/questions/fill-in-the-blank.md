# Nominal and real GDP — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Nominal GDP values output at _____ prices — the prices of the year itself.
**Answer: current**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Real GDP values every year at constant _____ prices, so only quantities can move it.
**Answer: base-year**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** With prices frozen, real growth comes from more capital and labour, or from _____ gains.
**Answer: productivity**
