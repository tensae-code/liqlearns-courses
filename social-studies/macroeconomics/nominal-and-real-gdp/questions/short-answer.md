# Nominal and real GDP — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Nominal GDP — current prices" in your own words.
**Answer:** Nominal GDP values output at current prices, so nominal growth overstates performance under inflation.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Real GDP — base-year prices" in your own words.
**Answer:** Real GDP values output at constant base-year prices and rises only when quantities rise.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Where real growth comes from" in your own words.
**Answer:** Real GDP growth is driven by more capital and labour, or by productivity gains.
