# Exchange rates — the price of a currency — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Exchange rates are set by printing more birr.
**Answer: False — Rates form in the market: demanders (tourists, importers, investors abroad) against suppliers (exporters, visitors, foreign investors).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** At going to press, 1 dollar cost about 4.5 birr.
**Answer: False — The figure is close to 45 birr per dollar (and 53 per euro) — at the time of going to press.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** 1 birr bought about 2.2 dollars at going to press.
**Answer: False — 1 ÷ 45 ≈ 0.022 — one birr bought about two US cents, not two dollars.**
