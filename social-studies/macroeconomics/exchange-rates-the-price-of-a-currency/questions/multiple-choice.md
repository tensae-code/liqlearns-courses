# Exchange rates — the price of a currency — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "The forex market"?
a) Exchange rates are set in the forex market by demand for and supply of foreign currency.  b) At going to press, the nominal rate was about 45 birr per dollar and 53 birr per euro.  c) Quoted in reverse, 1 birr ≈ 0.022 dollars — the same rate read from the other side.
**Answer: a) Exchange rates are set in the forex market by demand for and supply of foreign currency.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "The nominal rate — the price as quoted"?
a) Quoted in reverse, 1 birr ≈ 0.022 dollars — the same rate read from the other side.  b) Exchange rates are set in the forex market by demand for and supply of foreign currency.  c) At going to press, the nominal rate was about 45 birr per dollar and 53 birr per euro.
**Answer: c) At going to press, the nominal rate was about 45 birr per dollar and 53 birr per euro.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Quoting it the other way"?
a) At going to press, the nominal rate was about 45 birr per dollar and 53 birr per euro.  b) Quoted in reverse, 1 birr ≈ 0.022 dollars — the same rate read from the other side.  c) Exchange rates are set in the forex market by demand for and supply of foreign currency.
**Answer: b) Quoted in reverse, 1 birr ≈ 0.022 dollars — the same rate read from the other side.**
