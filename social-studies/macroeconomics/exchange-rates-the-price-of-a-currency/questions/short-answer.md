# Exchange rates — the price of a currency — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "The forex market" in your own words.
**Answer:** Exchange rates are set in the forex market by demand for and supply of foreign currency.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "The nominal rate — the price as quoted" in your own words.
**Answer:** At going to press, the nominal rate was about 45 birr per dollar and 53 birr per euro.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Quoting it the other way" in your own words.
**Answer:** Quoted in reverse, 1 birr ≈ 0.022 dollars — the same rate read from the other side.
