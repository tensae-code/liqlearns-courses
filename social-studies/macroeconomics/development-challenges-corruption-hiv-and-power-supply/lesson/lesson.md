## Sub lesson 1 — HIV/AIDS and the economy

HIV/AIDS strikes workers and families, cutting the labour force and raising health costs.

## Sub lesson 2 — Corruption

Corruption diverts public resources and discourages investment, holding development back.

## Sub lesson 3 — Power supply and resource control

Poor power supply raises every firm's costs, and control of resources is contested where the wealth is produced.
