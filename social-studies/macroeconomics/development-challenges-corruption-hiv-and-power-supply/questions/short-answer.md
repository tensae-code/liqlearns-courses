# Development challenges — corruption, HIV and power supply — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does HIV/AIDS do to the economy?
**Answer:** HIV/AIDS strikes workers and families, cutting the labour force and raising health costs.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does corruption do?
**Answer:** Corruption diverts public resources and discourages investment, holding development back.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What do poor power supply and resource control do?
**Answer:** Poor power supply raises every firm's costs, and control of resources is contested where the wealth is produced.
