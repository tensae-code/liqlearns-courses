# Development challenges — corruption, HIV and power supply — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** HIV/AIDS has no effect on the labour force.
**Answer: False** — HIV/AIDS strikes workers and families, cutting the labour force and raising health costs.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Corruption encourages investment and speeds development.
**Answer: False** — Corruption diverts public resources and discourages investment, holding development back.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Poor power supply lowers every firm's costs.
**Answer: False** — Poor power supply raises every firm's costs, and control of resources is contested where the wealth is produced.
