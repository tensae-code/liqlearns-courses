# Hands-on — From GDP to national income

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Lay GDP as a full row of counters. Remove the depreciation share and hold up what remains — NDP. Then sort the rest into five labelled piles, one per national-income component.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — a step diagram — GDP, minus depreciation to NDP, then the five national-income slices as a pie — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
