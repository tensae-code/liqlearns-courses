# From GDP to national income — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Net domestic product" in your own words.
**Answer:** Net domestic product is GDP minus the capital consumption allowance (depreciation).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "National income — payments to resource owners" in your own words.
**Answer:** National income is the sum of payments to resource owners, including compensation of employees and proprietors' income.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Corporate profits, rent and interest" in your own words.
**Answer:** Corporate profits split into dividends, retained earnings and corporate profit taxes.
