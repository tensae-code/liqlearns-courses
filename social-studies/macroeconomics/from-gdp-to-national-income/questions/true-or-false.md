# From GDP to national income — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Net domestic product is GDP minus the capital consumption allowance (depreciation).
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** National income is the sum of payments to resource owners, including compensation of employees and proprietors' income.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Corporate profits split into dividends, retained earnings and corporate profit taxes.
**Answer: True**
