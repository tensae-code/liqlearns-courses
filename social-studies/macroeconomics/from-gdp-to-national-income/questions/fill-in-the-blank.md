# From GDP to national income — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Net domestic product is GDP minus _____ — the capital used up in production.
**Answer: depreciation**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** National income is the sum of payments to the _____ who produced the output.
**Answer: resource owners**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Corporate profits enter national income as _____, retained earnings and profit taxes.
**Answer: dividends**
