# From GDP to national income — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Net domestic product"?
a) NDP = GDP minus the capital consumption allowance  b) national income sums payments to resource owners: compensation and proprietors' income first  c) corporate profits split into dividends, retained earnings and profit taxes; plus rental income and net interest
**Answer: a) NDP = GDP minus the capital consumption allowance**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "National income — payments to resource owners"?
a) NDP = GDP minus the capital consumption allowance  b) corporate profits split into dividends, retained earnings and profit taxes; plus rental income and net interest  c) national income sums payments to resource owners: compensation and proprietors' income first
**Answer: c) national income sums payments to resource owners: compensation and proprietors' income first**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Corporate profits, rent and interest"?
a) national income sums payments to resource owners: compensation and proprietors' income first  b) corporate profits split into dividends, retained earnings and profit taxes; plus rental income and net interest  c) NDP = GDP minus the capital consumption allowance
**Answer: b) corporate profits split into dividends, retained earnings and profit taxes; plus rental income and net interest**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** GDP is 900 billion birr and the capital consumption allowance is 90 billion. What is net domestic product?
a) 810 billion birr  b) 990 billion birr  c) 90 billion birr
**Answer: a) 810 billion birr** — NDP = GDP − depreciation = 900 − 90 = 810 billion birr.
