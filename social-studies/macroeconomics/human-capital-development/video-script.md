# Video script — Human capital development

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Human capital is the skill a country's people carry — and developing it means training people, and upgrading the skills of people already trained, for employment.

Zimbabwe names its builders: vocational training centres for practical skills, universities, nursing and teacher colleges — and ZIMDEF supporting the training.

Land is a family's asset. Trained skill is a person's.
