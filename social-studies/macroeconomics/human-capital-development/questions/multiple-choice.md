# Human capital development — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Human capital development is —
a) building more factories  b) the training and upgrading of skills for employment  c) saving money in a bank
**Answer: b) the training and upgrading of skills for employment**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Vocational training centres (VTCs) mainly build —
a) practical, hands-on skills for work  b) tourism sites  c) grain reserves
**Answer: a) practical, hands-on skills for work**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** ZIMDEF is named in the unit as —
a) a commercial bank  b) an institution of human capital development supporting training  c) a tourism board
**Answer: b) an institution of human capital development supporting training**
