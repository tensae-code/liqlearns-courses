# Human capital development — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is human capital development?
**Answer:** The training and upgrading of a people's skills for employment — growing the stock of skill a country's people carry.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the institutions the unit lists for building human capital.
**Answer:** Vocational training centres, universities, nursing and teacher colleges, and ZIMDEF.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Why does the guide place human capital among Zimbabwe's economic units?
**Answer:** Because skills are developed for employment: a trained person's skill is their economic asset, as land is a farming family's — it lets them earn, and lets the country's workforce do more and better work.
