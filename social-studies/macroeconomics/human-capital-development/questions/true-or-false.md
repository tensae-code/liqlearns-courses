# Human capital development — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Human capital means the skills a country's people carry.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Development of human capital stops when a person first qualifies.
**Answer: False — it includes upgrading skills: lifting skills already gained to a higher level.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Nursing and teacher colleges are among the institutions that build human capital in Zimbabwe.
**Answer: True**
