# Human capital development — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The training and upgrading of skills for employment is called _____ capital development.
**Answer: human**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Practical skills for work are trained at vocational training centres, shortened to _____.
**Answer: VTCs**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Skills are developed for _____: trained people carry what an employer needs, or a skill to employ themselves.
**Answer: employment**
