# Hands-on — Human capital development

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Match skill to institution
Write four skills — welding, nursing, teaching, engineering — on slips, and the four kinds of institution on others. Match each skill to the institution that trains it, then check against lesson/lesson.md.

## Draw it and check it
Draw a person at three stages — untrained, in training, upgraded — and label what grows at each stage. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
