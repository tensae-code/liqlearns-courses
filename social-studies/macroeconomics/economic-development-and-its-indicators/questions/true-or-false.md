# Economic development and its indicators — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Economic development is wider than growth: it is improvement in people's lives, not only a bigger national income.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Development is read through indicators such as income, life expectancy and literacy.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A country is developing when its something else entirely — income rising with longer lives and wider literacy, as the Grade 12 book and its teacher guide's worked answers show.
**Answer: False** — A country is developing when its indicators move together — income rising with longer lives and wider literacy, as the Grade 12 book and its teacher guide's worked answers show.
