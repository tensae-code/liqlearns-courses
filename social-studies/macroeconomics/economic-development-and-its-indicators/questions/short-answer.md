# Economic development and its indicators — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the book teach about development is more than growth?
**Answer:** Economic development is wider than growth: it is improvement in people's lives, not only a bigger national income.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the book teach about the indicators?
**Answer:** Development is read through indicators such as income, life expectancy and literacy.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the book teach about reading a country by its indicators?
**Answer:** A country is developing when its indicators move together — income rising with longer lives and wider literacy, as the Grade 12 book and its teacher guide's worked answers show.
