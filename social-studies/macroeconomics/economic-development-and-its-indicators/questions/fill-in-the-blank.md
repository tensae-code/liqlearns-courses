# Economic development and its indicators — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Economic development is wider than growth: it is improvement in _____, not only a bigger national income.
**Answer: people's lives**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Development is read through indicators such as income, _____.
**Answer: life expectancy and literacy**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A country is developing when its _____ — income rising with longer lives and wider literacy, as the Grade 12 book and its teacher guide's worked answers show.
**Answer: indicators move together**
