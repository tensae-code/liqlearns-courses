# Video script — Economic development and its indicators

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Economic development is wider than growth: it is improvement in people's lives, not only a bigger national income.

Development is read through indicators such as income, life expectancy and literacy.

A country is developing when its indicators move together — income rising with longer lives and wider literacy, as the Grade 12 book and its teacher guide's worked answers show.
