# The informal economy — street vendors and home work — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The buhoneros set up street-vendor _____ in the streets.
**Answer: stalls**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Watching and washing cars for income is part of the _____ economy.
**Answer: informal**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** New immigrants settle in _____ and join the informal economy.
**Answer: barrios**
