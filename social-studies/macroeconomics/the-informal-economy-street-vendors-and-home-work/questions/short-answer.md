# The informal economy — street vendors and home work — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Who are the buhoneros?
**Answer:** Street vendors — people selling anything in the streets from their own stalls, outside formal employment.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Give the book's three car-related examples of informal home work.
**Answer:** Watching cars, washing cars, and repairing them at homes.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Why does the book teach the informal economy inside the migration chapters?
**Answer:** Because most new immigrants from Latin America settle in barrios and join the informal economy.
