# The informal economy — street vendors and home work — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Street vendors in Venezuela are called —
a) buhoneros  b) aparceros  c) conuqueros
**Answer: a) buhoneros**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The book's home-work examples are —
a) watching, washing and repairing cars at homes  b) assembling cars in a factory  c) driving buses for a company
**Answer: a) watching, washing and repairing cars at homes**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Most new immigrants from Latin America, in the book, —
a) settle in barrios and join the informal economy  b) are hired at once by oil companies  c) return home within a week
**Answer: a) settle in barrios and join the informal economy**
