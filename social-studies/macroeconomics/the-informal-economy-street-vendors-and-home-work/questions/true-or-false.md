# The informal economy — street vendors and home work — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Buhoneros work as employees of large formal firms.
**Answer: False** — they sell in the streets on their own — informal work.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Informal work includes repairing cars at homes.
**Answer: True** — the book lists watching, washing and repairing cars at homes.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The informal economy has nothing to do with migration in the book.
**Answer: False** — the book teaches it inside the migration chapters — most new immigrants join it.
