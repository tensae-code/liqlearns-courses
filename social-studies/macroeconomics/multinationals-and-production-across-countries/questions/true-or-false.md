# Multinationals and production across countries — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** An MNC produces in only one country.
**Answer: False — it owns production in more than one country.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Ford in India sells only inside India.
**Answer: False — it exports and supplies components worldwide.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Foreign trade and foreign investment are the same thing.
**Answer: False — trade moves goods; investment owns production abroad.**
