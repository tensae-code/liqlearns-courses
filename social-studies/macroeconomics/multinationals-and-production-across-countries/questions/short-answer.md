# Multinationals and production across countries — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What makes a company multinational?
**Answer:** It owns production in more than one country and interlinks it.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What three things does Ford in India produce for?
**Answer:** Cars for home, cars for export, and components for its world factories.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Distinguish foreign trade from foreign investment.
**Answer:** Trade moves goods across borders; investment owns production in another country.
