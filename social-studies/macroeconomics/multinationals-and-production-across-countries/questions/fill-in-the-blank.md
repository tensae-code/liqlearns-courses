# Multinationals and production across countries — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** An MNC owns _____ in more than one country.
**Answer: production**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Ford in India also makes _____ for its world factories.
**Answer: components**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Goods crossing borders is foreign _____; owning production abroad is foreign investment.
**Answer: trade**
