# Multinationals and production across countries — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** An MNC —
a) owns and interlinks production in more than one country  b) trades in one village only  c) never invests abroad
**Answer: a) owns and interlinks production in more than one country**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Ford's Indian plant makes —
a) cars for home and export, and components for world factories  b) only components  c) only cars for India
**Answer: a) cars for home and export, and components for world factories**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Owning production abroad is —
a) foreign investment  b) foreign trade  c) barter
**Answer: a) foreign investment**
