# Multinationals and production across countries

## Do it first

## Sub lesson 1 — An MNC owns production in more than one country

A **multinational corporation (MNC)** **owns production in more than one country** and **interlinks** that production across borders.

## Sub lesson 2 — Ford in India — cars for home, export and components

**Ford in India** builds cars for the **home market**, for **export**, and **components for its world factories** — one plant serving the whole network.

## Sub lesson 3 — Foreign trade against foreign investment

Countries link by **foreign trade** (goods crossing borders) and by **foreign investment** (owning production abroad) — MNCs are the investment link made visible.
