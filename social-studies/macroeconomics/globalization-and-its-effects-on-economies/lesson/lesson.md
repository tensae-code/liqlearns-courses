## Sub lesson 1 — What globalization is

**Globalization** is the **growing interconnection of national economies** — goods, services, money and ideas cross borders more and more.

## Sub lesson 2 — Why globalization matters

Globalization **matters** because what happens in one economy now **spreads to others** — a shock or a boom abroad reaches home.

## Sub lesson 3 — Its effects on economies

Its **effects** run through trade, jobs and prices in **every connected economy** — the book weighs its importance for how economies are organised.
