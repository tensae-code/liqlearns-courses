# Globalization and its effects on economies — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Globalization is —
a) the growing interconnection of national economies  b) each economy closing itself off  c) one country ruling all trade
**Answer: a) the growing interconnection of national economies**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Globalization matters because —
a) events in one economy spread to others  b) borders stop all effects  c) only one country trades
**Answer: a) events in one economy spread to others**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Globalization's effects run through —
a) trade, jobs and prices  b) nothing at all  c) one village only
**Answer: a) trade, jobs and prices**
