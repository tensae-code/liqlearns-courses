# Globalization and its effects on economies — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** _____ is the growing interconnection of national economies.
**Answer: Globalization**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A boom or shock abroad reaches home because economies are _____.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Globalization's effects run through trade, _____ and prices.
