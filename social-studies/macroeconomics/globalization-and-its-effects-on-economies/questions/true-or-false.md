# Globalization and its effects on economies — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Globalization means economies grow more connected.
**Answer: True — globalization is the growing interconnection of national economies.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Globalization has no effects outside one country.
**Answer: False — its effects spread across connected economies.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Only exporters feel globalization's effects.
**Answer: False — its effects run through trade, jobs and prices in every connected economy.**
