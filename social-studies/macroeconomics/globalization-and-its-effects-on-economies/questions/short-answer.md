# Globalization and its effects on economies — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is globalization?
**Answer:** The growing interconnection of national economies.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why does a shock abroad reach home?
**Answer:** Because national economies are interconnected, so effects spread.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name two channels of globalization's effects.
**Answer:** Trade and jobs (and prices).
