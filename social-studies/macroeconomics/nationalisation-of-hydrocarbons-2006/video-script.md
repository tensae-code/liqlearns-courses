The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Nationalisation of hydrocarbons, 2006 — as Bolivia's book teaches it, says the narrator: the Gas War and the 2004 referendum expressed the people's decision to recover their natural resources; Decree 28701 'Héroes del Chaco' of 1 May 2006 nationalised the hydrocarbons, handing operations to YPFB; state income rose considerably and funded services and programmes, and began the industrialisation of hydrocarbon derivatives.
