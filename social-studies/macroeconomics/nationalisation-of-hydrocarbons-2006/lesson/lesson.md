## Sub lesson 1 — The Gas War and the 2004 referendum

After the Gas War and the 2004 referendum expressed the people's decision to recover their natural resources from the foreign firms that held them through capitalisation and concessions, the third great nationalisation came.

## Sub lesson 2 — Decree 28701 'Héroes del Chaco', 1 May 2006

Decree 28701 'Héroes del Chaco', of 1 May 2006, nationalised the hydrocarbons: the foreign companies had to hand their operations to YPFB, the state firm capitalisation had hollowed out, now refilled with the nation's gas.

## Sub lesson 3 — Income rises — services and industrialisation funded

State income rose considerably, and funded governorships, municipalities and health, education, housing and employment programmes — and began the industrialisation of hydrocarbon derivatives: gas not only sold raw, but processed at home.
