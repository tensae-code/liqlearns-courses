# Nationalisation of hydrocarbons, 2006 — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the lesson teach about the Gas War and the 2004 referendum?
**Answer:** The Gas War and the 2004 referendum expressed the people's decision to recover their natural resources.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the lesson teach about decree 28701 'Héroes del Chaco', 1 May 2006?
**Answer:** Decree 28701 'Héroes del Chaco' of 1 May 2006 nationalised the hydrocarbons, handing operations to YPFB.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the lesson teach about income rises — services and industrialisation funded?
**Answer:** State income rose considerably and funded services and programmes, and began the industrialisation of hydrocarbon derivatives.
