# Nationalisation of hydrocarbons, 2006 — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The 2004 referendum voted to keep the concessions as they were.
**Answer: False** — it expressed the decision to recover the resources.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The decree sold YPFB to foreign companies.
**Answer: False** — it nationalised the hydrocarbons and handed operations to YPFB.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** State income fell after the 2006 nationalisation.
**Answer: False** — the book says it rose considerably and funded programmes.
