# Hands-on — Tax equity — ability to pay

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Two earners, one richer
Draw two earners with identical pay handing over identical tax, and a third, richer earner handing over more. Label the first picture horizontal equity, the second vertical.

## Choose a base
Argue for one measure of ability (income, consumption or wealth) for a village tax, naming what it misses. A partner argues for a different base; the class votes on measurability.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
