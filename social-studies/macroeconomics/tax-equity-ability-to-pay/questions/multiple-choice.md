# Tax equity — ability to pay — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Tax by capacity"?
a) Ability-to-pay taxes each person according to capacity to pay  b) Equals pay equally (horizontal equity); unequals pay unequally (vertical equity)  c) Ability can be measured by income, consumption, wealth or leisure — none fully measurable
**Answer: a) Ability-to-pay taxes each person according to capacity to pay**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Horizontal and vertical equity"?
a) Equals pay equally (horizontal equity); unequals pay unequally (vertical equity)  b) Ability can be measured by income, consumption, wealth or leisure — none fully measurable  c) Ability-to-pay taxes each person according to capacity to pay
**Answer: a) Equals pay equally (horizontal equity); unequals pay unequally (vertical equity)**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "The hard questions"?
a) Ability can be measured by income, consumption, wealth or leisure — none fully measurable  b) Ability-to-pay taxes each person according to capacity to pay  c) Equals pay equally (horizontal equity); unequals pay unequally (vertical equity)
**Answer: a) Ability can be measured by income, consumption, wealth or leisure — none fully measurable**
