# Tax equity — ability to pay — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Tax by capacity" in your own words.
**Answer:** Ability-to-pay taxes each person according to capacity to pay.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Horizontal and vertical equity" in your own words.
**Answer:** Equals pay equally (horizontal equity); unequals pay unequally (vertical equity).

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "The hard questions" in your own words.
**Answer:** Ability can be measured by income, consumption, wealth or leisure — none fully measurable.
