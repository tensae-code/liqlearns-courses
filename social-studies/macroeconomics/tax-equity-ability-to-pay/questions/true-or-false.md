# Tax equity — ability to pay — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Ability-to-pay taxes each person according to capacity to pay.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Equals pay equally (horizontal equity); unequals pay unequally (vertical equity).
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Ability can be measured by income, consumption, wealth or leisure — none fully measurable.
**Answer: True**
