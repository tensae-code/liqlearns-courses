# Small projects in the national economy

## Do it first

Before you read on, write down what you already think this lesson's question is — why small projects matter to a whole economy — in one sentence of your own. Keep it; you will check it at the end.

## Sub lesson 1 — Income and gross domestic product

Palestine's Grade 11 Small Projects book opens by placing the small project inside the **national economy**. Small projects **raise local income and gross domestic product (GDP)**: each project's earnings are income where it stands, and added together across the country they are part of what the whole economy produces.

## Sub lesson 2 — Jobs, unemployment and poverty

Small projects **create jobs** — and jobs are the book's bridge to welfare: work created by small projects **cuts unemployment and poverty**. A lesson about one small firm is therefore also a lesson about the country's joblessness and its poor.

## Sub lesson 3 — Goods and services the community needs — and why states support them

Small projects **supply goods and services the community needs**. That is the third reason, and the book draws the conclusion itself: **which is why states support them**. Income, jobs and needed goods — the national case for the small project, taught before any project is planned.
