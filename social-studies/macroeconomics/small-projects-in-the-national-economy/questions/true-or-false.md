# Small projects in the national economy — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Small projects lower gross domestic product, in the book's account.
**Answer: False — they raise local income and gross domestic product**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Creating jobs is how small projects cut unemployment.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Small projects supply goods and services the community needs.
**Answer: True**
