# Small projects in the national economy — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why does one project's income matter nationally?
**Answer:** Added across the country, small projects' earnings are part of what the whole economy produces — its GDP.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the two social ills jobs from small projects cut.
**Answer:** Unemployment and poverty.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** State the book's conclusion in one sentence.
**Answer:** States support small projects because of their importance to income, jobs and the community's needs.
