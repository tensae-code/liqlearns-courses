# Small projects in the national economy — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What do small projects raise, at local and national level?
a) local income and gross domestic product  b) only prices  c) nothing measurable
**Answer: a) local income and gross domestic product**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What do small projects do to unemployment and poverty?
a) they create jobs, cutting unemployment and poverty  b) they raise both  c) they have no effect on jobs
**Answer: a) they create jobs, cutting unemployment and poverty**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Why do states support small projects?
a) because they raise income and GDP, create jobs and supply goods and services the community needs  b) for ceremony only  c) the book gives no reason
**Answer: a) because they raise income and GDP, create jobs and supply goods and services the community needs**
