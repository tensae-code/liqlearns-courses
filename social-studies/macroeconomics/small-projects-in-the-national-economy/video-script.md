# Video script — Small projects in the national economy

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Why does a whole country care about one small project? Palestine's Grade 11 book gives three reasons. Small projects raise local income and gross domestic product. They create jobs — and jobs cut unemployment and poverty. And they supply the goods and services a community needs. That is why states support them. The national economy, built one small project at a time.
