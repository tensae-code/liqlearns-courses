# Hands-on — Small projects in the national economy

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Set out three small shops made from boxes. For each, say one job it creates, one good or service the street needs from it, and the income it brings — then gather all three and say what the street's little economy has gained.

## Draw it and check it
Draw one small project in the centre and three arrows out — income/GDP, jobs, goods and services — labelled with the book's words. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare, and fix every arrow you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach sub lesson 2 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
