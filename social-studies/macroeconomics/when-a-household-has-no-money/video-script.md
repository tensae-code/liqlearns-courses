# Video script — When a household has no money

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

When a household has no money — as Rwanda's book teaches it, says the narrator: Some families cannot get money. The book names the barriers: unemployment, laziness, disasters, epidemics, many children, waste and conflict. Each barrier stops the family's money coming in or uses it up. When a household has no money, the book teaches, it leads to debt, theft, untreated illness and conflict at home. Lacking money is not only about coins: illness goes untreated, the family falls into debt, and conflict grows — so the barriers in sub lesson 1 harm health, honesty and peace together.
