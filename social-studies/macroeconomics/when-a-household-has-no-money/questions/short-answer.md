# When a household has no money — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In your own words, what does sub lesson 1 teach about barriers that stop a family getting money?
**Answer:** Some families cannot get money. The book names the barriers: unemployment, laziness, disasters, epidemics, many children, waste and conflict. Each barrier stops the family's money coming in or uses it up.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In your own words, what does sub lesson 2 teach about what lacking money leads to at home?
**Answer:** When a household has no money, the book teaches, it leads to debt, theft, untreated illness and conflict at home.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In your own words, what does sub lesson 3 teach about money, health and peace at home?
**Answer:** Lacking money is not only about coins: illness goes untreated, the family falls into debt, and conflict grows — so the barriers in sub lesson 1 harm health, honesty and peace together.
