# When a household has no money — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Some families cannot get money.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** When a household has no money, the book teaches, it leads to debt, theft, untreated illness and conflict at home.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Lacking money is not only about coins: illness goes untreated, the family falls into debt, and conflict grows — so the barriers in sub lesson 1 harm health, honesty and peace together.
**Answer: True**
