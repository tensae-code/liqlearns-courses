# Transport, communication and human resource development in Pakistan — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why do transport and communication matter economically?
**Answer:** They reduce the cost of production, expand trade, and link remote areas.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What means of transport and communication does the chapter survey?
**Answer:** The road network and Pakistan Railways with their problems; and modern communication — computer and IT, e-government.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What raises the productivity of labour, in the book?
**Answer:** Physical fitness, mental health, education and training — the factors of human resource development.
