# Video script — Transport, communication and human resource development in Pakistan

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Transport, communication and human resource development in Pakistan — as Pakistan's book teaches it, says the narrator: transport and communication cut production costs, expand trade and link remote areas; the means are roads and Pakistan Railways with their problems, plus IT and e-government; labour productivity is raised by physical fitness, mental health, education and training.
