## Sub lesson 1 — Why transport and communication matter

The Class 12 chapter opens with the **importance of communication and transportation**: **reduction in cost of production, expansion of trade, and linking remote areas**. Roads and messages are economic machinery, not background.

## Sub lesson 2 — Means of transport; modern communication

Pakistan's means are surveyed: the **road network** and **Pakistan Railways**, **with their problems**; then modern communication — **computer and information technology** and **e-government** — as the new means joining the old.

## Sub lesson 3 — Human resource development

The chapter closes with **human resource development**: **manpower objectives**, and the factors that raise the **productivity of labour** — **physical fitness, mental health, education and training**. People are a resource that can be developed like any other.
