# Perestroika — from central planning to a market economy — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the lesson teach about perestroika?
**Answer:** Gorbachev's restructuring aimed at a market economy in which production responds to demand and prices reflect the real costs.

**Answer:** With autonomous public, private or cooperative firms.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the lesson teach about perestroika — more?
