# Privatisation, commercialisation and deregulation in Nigeria — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is privatisation?
**Answer:** Privatisation sells state enterprises to private owners.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is commercialisation?
**Answer:** Commercialisation makes state enterprises run on commercial lines, earning their keep without a full sale.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What is deregulation?
**Answer:** Deregulation removes state controls so that prices and entry are set by the market; Nigeria applied all three reforms together.
