# Privatisation, commercialisation and deregulation in Nigeria — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** _____ sells state enterprises to private owners.
**Answer: Privatisation**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Commercialisation makes state enterprises run on _____ lines.
**Answer: commercial**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Deregulation removes state _____ so the market sets prices.
**Answer: controls**
