# Video script — Privatisation, commercialisation and deregulation in Nigeria

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Privatisation, commercialisation and deregulation in Nigeria — as Nigeria's book teaches it, says the narrator: privatisation sells state enterprises to private owners.; commercialisation makes state enterprises run on commercial lines, earning their keep without a full sale.; deregulation removes state controls so that prices and entry are set by the market; Nigeria applied all three reforms together..
