# Privatisation, commercialisation and deregulation in Nigeria

## Do it first

## Sub lesson 1 — Privatisation

Privatisation sells state enterprises to private owners.

## Sub lesson 2 — Commercialisation

Commercialisation makes state enterprises run on commercial lines, earning their keep without a full sale.

## Sub lesson 3 — Deregulation

Deregulation removes state controls so that prices and entry are set by the market; Nigeria applied all three reforms together.
