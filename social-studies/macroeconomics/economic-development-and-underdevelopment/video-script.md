# Video script — Economic development and underdevelopment

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Economic development and underdevelopment — as Pakistan's book teaches it, says the narrator: economic development is defined through Lewis and Williamson — capacity and welfare rising, not income alone; underdevelopment's problems are low per capita income, rapid population growth and unproductive resource use; development is measured by rises in national income, per capita income and capital goods.
