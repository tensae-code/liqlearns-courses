## Sub lesson 1 — Economic development defined

The Class 12 development chapter opens with definitions: **economic development** as **Professor Arthur Lewis** and **Williamson** define it — not mere growth of income, but the economy's capacity and people's welfare rising together.

## Sub lesson 2 — Problems of an underdeveloped economy

An **underdeveloped economy** has named problems: **low per capita income**, **rapid growth of population**, and **unproductive use of resources**. The three together are the trap the chapter sets out to explain.

## Sub lesson 3 — How development is measured

Development is **measured** three ways in the book: by **increase in national income**, by **increase in per capita income**, and by **increase in capital goods** — the stock of machines and equipment the economy works with.
