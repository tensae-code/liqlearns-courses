# Economic development and underdevelopment — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How is economic development defined in the book?
**Answer:** Through Lewis and Williamson: the economy's capacity and people's welfare rising together, not income alone.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the problems of an underdeveloped economy from the book.
**Answer:** Low per capita income, rapid growth of population, and unproductive use of resources.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** How is development measured, in the book?
**Answer:** By increase in national income, increase in per capita income, and increase in capital goods.
