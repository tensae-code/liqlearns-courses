# Economic development and underdevelopment — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Development is only a rise in prices.
**Answer: False** — the definitions tie it to capacity and welfare, not prices.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** An underdeveloped economy's problem is that its population grows too slowly.
**Answer: False** — rapid population growth is a named problem.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Development is measured by the number of holidays.
**Answer: False** — the book's measures are income, per capita income and capital goods.
