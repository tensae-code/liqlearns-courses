# Economic development and underdevelopment — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** About economic development defined — the lesson teaches that —
a) economic development is defined through Lewis and Williamson — capacity and welfare rising, not income alone  b) underdevelopment's problems are low per capita income, rapid population growth and unproductive resource use  c) development is measured by rises in national income, per capita income and capital goods
**Answer: a) economic development is defined through Lewis and Williamson — capacity and welfare rising, not income alone**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** About problems of an underdeveloped economy — the lesson teaches that —
a) economic development is defined through Lewis and Williamson — capacity and welfare rising, not income alone  b) underdevelopment's problems are low per capita income, rapid population growth and unproductive resource use  c) development is measured by rises in national income, per capita income and capital goods
**Answer: b) underdevelopment's problems are low per capita income, rapid population growth and unproductive resource use**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** About how development is measured — the lesson teaches that —
a) economic development is defined through Lewis and Williamson — capacity and welfare rising, not income alone  b) underdevelopment's problems are low per capita income, rapid population growth and unproductive resource use  c) development is measured by rises in national income, per capita income and capital goods
**Answer: c) development is measured by rises in national income, per capita income and capital goods**
