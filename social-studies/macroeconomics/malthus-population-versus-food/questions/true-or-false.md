# Malthus — population versus food — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Malthus held that the imbalance between population and food brings poverty and misery.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In Malthus's two lines, food pulls away from population after a few steps.
**Answer: False — population pulls away: it doubles (1, 2, 4, 8, 16) while food only adds one each step (1, 2, 3, 4, 5).**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Population growth pressures the natural resources available per person.
**Answer: True**
