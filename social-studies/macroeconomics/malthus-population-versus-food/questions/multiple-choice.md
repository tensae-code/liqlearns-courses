# Malthus — population versus food — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In Malthus's theory, population grows —
a) arithmetically — 1, 2, 3, 4, 5  b) geometrically — 1, 2, 4, 8, 16  c) not at all
**Answer: b) geometrically — 1, 2, 4, 8, 16**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Food production, in Malthus's theory, grows —
a) arithmetically — 1, 2, 3, 4, 5  b) geometrically — 1, 2, 4, 8, 16  c) faster than population
**Answer: a) arithmetically — 1, 2, 3, 4, 5**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** When population grows fast, the book says saving and investment rates —
a) rise  b) stay the same  c) fall, and economic development is delayed
**Answer: c) fall, and economic development is delayed**
