# Malthus — population versus food — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Write Malthus's two lines to five steps each.
**Answer:** Population: 1, 2, 4, 8, 16 (doubling). Food: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 (adding one).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the imbalance between the two lines bring, according to Malthus?
**Answer:** Poverty and misery — more people must share food and resources that grow only slowly, so there is less per person.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** How does fast population growth delay development, in the book's account?
**Answer:** Saving and investment rates fall, because families and the country have less left over to put aside and invest, so economic development is delayed.
