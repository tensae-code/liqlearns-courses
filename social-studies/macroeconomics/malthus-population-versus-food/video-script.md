# Video script — Malthus — population versus food

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Write two lines of numbers. People: 1, 2, 4, 8, 16 — doubling every step. Food: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 — adding one every step. Jordan's Grade 8 book teaches Thomas Malthus from exactly those two lines.

Malthus said population grows geometrically and food production grows arithmetically, so people must outrun food — and the imbalance brings poverty and misery.

The book carries the theory into development: more people press on the natural resources each person can claim, saving and investment rates fall, and economic development is delayed.
