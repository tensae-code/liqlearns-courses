# Hands-on — Malthus — population versus food

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Write the two lines
Write Malthus's two lines out to ten steps each: population doubling (1, 2, 4, 8, 16…) and food adding one (1, 2, 3, 4, 5…). At which step does population first pass double the food line? Check your lines against lesson/lesson.md.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the two staircases from the lesson plan's picture in words: the doubling staircase and the adding staircase. Label each step with its number. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare, and fix every step you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
