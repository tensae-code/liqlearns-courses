# Video script — Economic organisation in ancient Mesopotamia

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Economic organisation in ancient Mesopotamia — as Iraq's book teaches it, says the narrator: the first appearance of the state in history in Mesopotamia's city-states (Uruk, Ur, Nippur, Babylon, Hatra), its economic jobs — organising irrigation water and canals, protecting foreign trade routes, legislating, and shielding people from famine — and the forms of agricultural ownership that followed.
