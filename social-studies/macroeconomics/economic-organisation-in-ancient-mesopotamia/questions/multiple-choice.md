# Economic organisation in ancient Mesopotamia — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The state first appeared in history in —
a) the city-states of Mesopotamia  b) the Roman empire  c) feudal Europe
**Answer: a) the city-states of Mesopotamia**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which of these was one of the early state's economic jobs?
a) organising irrigation water and canals  b) printing money  c) building railways
**Answer: a) organising irrigation water and canals**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The lesson's third part treats —
a) forms of agricultural ownership  b) kinds of taxes today  c) types of banks
**Answer: a) forms of agricultural ownership**
