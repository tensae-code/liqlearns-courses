# Economic organisation in ancient Mesopotamia — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the city-states the lesson lists.
**Answer:** Uruk, Ur, Nippur, Babylon and Hatra.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the early state's four economic jobs.
**Answer:** Organising irrigation water and canals, protecting foreign trade routes, legislating, and shielding people from famine.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What followed on the land as the city-states rose?
**Answer:** Forms of agricultural ownership.
