# Economic organisation in ancient Mesopotamia — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Uruk, Ur, Nippur, Babylon and Hatra are named as city-states.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The early state left irrigation to chance.
**Answer: False — organising irrigation water and canals was one of its economic jobs.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Shielding people from famine was one of the state's jobs.
**Answer: True**
