## Sub lesson 1 — The first state — the city-states of Mesopotamia

The state **first appeared in history** in the **city-states** of Mesopotamia — **Uruk, Ur, Nippur, Babylon and Hatra**.

## Sub lesson 2 — The state's economic jobs — water, trade routes, laws and famine protection

The early state's **economic jobs** were **organising irrigation water and canals**, **protecting foreign trade routes**, **legislating**, and **shielding people from famine**.

## Sub lesson 3 — Forms of agricultural ownership

On the land, the new order brought **forms of agricultural ownership** that followed the rise of the city-states.
