# Video script — Internal and external public debt

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A country's total debt splits in two — by who lent the money.

Internal debt is owed to the country's own citizens. External debt is owed abroad — including bilateral debt from foreign governments and central banks.

Honduras's book has pupils read that split off the country's own fiscal deficit chart, 2017 to 2020.
