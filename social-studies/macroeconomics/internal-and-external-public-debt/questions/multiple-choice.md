# Internal and external public debt — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A country's total debt splits into —
a) taxes and spending  b) internal and external debt  c) exports and imports
**Answer: b) internal and external debt**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Internal debt is owed to —
a) foreign governments  b) the country's own citizens  c) the IMF only
**Answer: b) the country's own citizens**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Bilateral debt is part of external debt and is owed to —
a) foreign governments and central banks  b) local shops  c) the country's own citizens
**Answer: a) foreign governments and central banks**
