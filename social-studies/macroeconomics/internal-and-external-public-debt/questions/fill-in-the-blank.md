# Internal and external public debt — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The split of total debt is by _____, not by size or year.
**Answer: lender**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Debt owed to a country's own citizens is called _____ debt.
**Answer: internal**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Honduras's chart in the book is the central-government fiscal _____ chart, 2017–2020.
**Answer: deficit**
