# Internal and external public debt — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How does the book split a country's total debt?
**Answer:** Into internal debt and external debt — a split by lender, where the money was borrowed from.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is internal debt?
**Answer:** The part of the debt owed to the country's own citizens — borrowed at home, from its own people and institutions.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What is external debt, and how does the book show it?
**Answer:** The part owed abroad, including bilateral debt from foreign governments and central banks; the book reads it off Honduras's central-government fiscal deficit chart, 2017–2020.
