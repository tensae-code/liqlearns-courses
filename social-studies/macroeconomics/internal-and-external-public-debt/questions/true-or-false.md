# Internal and external public debt — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Internal and external debt are split by the size of each loan.
**Answer: False** — The split is by lender, not by size or year.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Internal debt is owed to foreign central banks.
**Answer: False** — Internal debt is owed to the country's own citizens; foreign governments and central banks are external, bilateral lenders.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The book's debt lesson is read off Honduras's own fiscal deficit chart.
**Answer: True**
