# The discount rate — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "The price of borrowing from the central bank" in your own words.
**Answer:** The discount rate is what the central bank charges banks that borrow from it.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "From the window to the street" in your own words.
**Answer:** Cheaper discount borrowing lets banks lend more.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Ethiopia's discount window — March 2001" in your own words.
**Answer:** Ethiopia's discount-window facility for commercial banks started in March 2001.
