# The discount rate — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The discount rate is what the central bank charges banks that borrow from it.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Cheaper discount borrowing lets banks lend more.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Ethiopia's discount-window facility for commercial banks started in March 2001.
**Answer: True**
