# The discount rate — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "The price of borrowing from the central bank"?
a) The discount rate is what the central bank charges banks that borrow from it  b) Cheaper discount borrowing lets banks lend more  c) Ethiopia's discount-window facility for commercial banks started in March 2001
**Answer: a) The discount rate is what the central bank charges banks that borrow from it**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "From the window to the street"?
a) Cheaper discount borrowing lets banks lend more  b) Ethiopia's discount-window facility for commercial banks started in March 2001  c) The discount rate is what the central bank charges banks that borrow from it
**Answer: a) Cheaper discount borrowing lets banks lend more**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Ethiopia's discount window — March 2001"?
a) Ethiopia's discount-window facility for commercial banks started in March 2001  b) The discount rate is what the central bank charges banks that borrow from it  c) Cheaper discount borrowing lets banks lend more
**Answer: a) Ethiopia's discount-window facility for commercial banks started in March 2001**
