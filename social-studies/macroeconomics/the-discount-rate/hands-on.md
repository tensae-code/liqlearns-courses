# Hands-on — The discount rate

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Signal reading
Write three headlines announcing discount-rate moves. For each, state what the central bank is signalling and what a borrower at a commercial bank should expect.

## Tool comparison
Set this tool beside open market operations: which moves daily, which signals, which would you use for a one-night shortage? Answer in a small table.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
