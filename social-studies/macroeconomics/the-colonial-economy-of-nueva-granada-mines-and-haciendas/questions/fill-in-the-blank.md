# The colonial economy of Nueva Granada mines and haciendas — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Nueva _____ exported plant products (maize, potato, cacao, tobacco, cotton, quinine…) to the whole world;
