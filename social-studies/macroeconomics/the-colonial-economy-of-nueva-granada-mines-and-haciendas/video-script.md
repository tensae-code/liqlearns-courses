# Video script — The colonial economy of Nueva Granada mines and haciendas

The colonial economy of Nueva Granada mines and haciendas — as Colombia's book teaches it, says the narrator: Nueva Granada exported plant products (maize, potato, cacao, tobacco, cotton, quinine…) to the whole world; The race for precious metals opened the gold mines of Antioquia Chocó and Popayán and changed the economy — but mining centres could not survive without food and transport, so haciendas and estancias grew around the mines
