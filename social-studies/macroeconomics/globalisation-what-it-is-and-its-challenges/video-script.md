# Video script — Globalisation — what it is and its challenges

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Globalisation is the world's economies knitting together into one system — goods, money, knowledge and information crossing borders ever faster.

Its realities today: knowledge and information are the main input, state rules over markets are loosened, and capital moves between countries in growing amounts.

For developing countries that brings chances and hard challenges at once — and the Egypt book ends by asking where Egypt and Africa stand inside it.
