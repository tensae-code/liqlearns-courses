# Globalisation — what it is and its challenges — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is globalisation?
**Answer:** The deepening knitting-together of the world's economies into one world economic system, where production, trade, money and information cross borders ever more freely.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name globalisation's three realities as the book teaches them.
**Answer:** Knowledge and information as the main input, deregulation loosening state rules over markets, and growing capital movements between countries.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Why is globalisation a challenge for developing countries?
**Answer:** They must compete in a system whose main input is knowledge they are still building, face capital that can arrive and leave quickly, and find a place in world trade from a weaker starting point.
