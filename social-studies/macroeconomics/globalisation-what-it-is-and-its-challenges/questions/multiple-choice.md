# Globalisation — what it is and its challenges — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Globalisation is —
a) one country trading alone  b) the world's economies knitting together into one world economic system  c) a tax on imports
**Answer: b) the world's economies knitting together into one world economic system**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In globalisation's realities, the main input of production is now —
a) raw material alone  b) knowledge and information  c) land
**Answer: b) knowledge and information**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The Egypt book closes its globalisation chapter by asking about —
a) Egypt and Africa's position inside globalisation  b) the price of wheat  c) the history of money
**Answer: a) Egypt and Africa's position inside globalisation**
