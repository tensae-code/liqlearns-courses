# Globalisation — what it is and its challenges — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Under globalisation, what happens in one country's economy can be felt in others.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Deregulation means the state adds more rules over markets and trade.
**Answer: False** — Deregulation loosens the state's rules over markets and trade.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** For developing countries, globalisation is only a chance and never a challenge.
**Answer: False** — The book teaches it as a challenge as much as a chance.
