# How inflation is fought — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The first anti-inflation lever —
a) shrinks the excess money mass  b) prints more money  c) raises all prices
**Answer: a) shrinks the excess money mass**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The second lever —
a) stimulates production  b) bans saving  c) cuts all credit
**Answer: a) stimulates production**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The aim of lever two is —
a) more goods chasing the money  b) fewer goods  c) higher prices
**Answer: a) more goods chasing the money**
