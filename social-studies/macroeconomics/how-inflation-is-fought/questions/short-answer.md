# How inflation is fought — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How does the first lever shrink the money mass?
**Answer:** By drawing savings into banks and state bonds.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the second lever use to stimulate production?
**Answer:** Soft credits and tax reliefs.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name the state's two anti-inflation levers.
**Answer:** Shrinking the excess money mass, and stimulating production.
