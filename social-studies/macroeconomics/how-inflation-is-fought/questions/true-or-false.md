# How inflation is fought — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Drawing savings into banks and state bonds shrinks the money chasing goods.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Soft credits and tax reliefs are part of the second lever.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The book gives the state three anti-inflation levers.
**Answer: False** — It gives two: shrinking the excess money mass, and stimulating production.
