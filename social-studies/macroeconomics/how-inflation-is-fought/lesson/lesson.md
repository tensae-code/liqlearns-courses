# How inflation is fought

## Do it first

Think of this topic the way the Uzbekistan Grade 9 book opens it — Topic 23, Measures to Prevent Inflation. What do you already know about it before you read on?

## Sub lesson 1 — The problem — excess money chasing goods

Inflation is fought by the state, against the cause the book has already shown: an **excess money mass** chasing the goods there are.

## Sub lesson 2 — Lever one — shrinking the excess money mass

The first lever **shrinks the excess money mass**: drawing savings **into banks and state bonds**, so the money is held instead of chasing goods.

## Sub lesson 3 — Lever two — stimulating production

The second lever **stimulates production**: **soft credits and tax reliefs** for producers, so **more goods chase the money**.
