# WTO and Pakistan

## Do it first

Before you read on, write what you already think this lesson's title means — wto and pakistan — in one sentence of your own. Keep it; you will check it against the lesson at the end.

## Sub lesson 1 — WTO objectives and agreements — for Pakistan

The Class 12 book returns to the WTO with Pakistan in view: its **objectives** and its **agreements** — the **Agreement on Agriculture** and **Trade-Related Intellectual Property Rights (TRIPs)** named for their weight on Pakistan.

## Sub lesson 2 — Impact on agriculture and industry

Then the impact, sector by sector: **on agriculture** and **on industry** — what WTO rules do to Pakistan's farms and factories, the two sectors the whole book has followed.

## Sub lesson 3 — Impact on the balance of payments; cultural and social impacts

The tracing closes with the impact **on the balance of payments**, and the **cultural and social impacts** — the WTO judged not only in trade numbers but in the country's life.
