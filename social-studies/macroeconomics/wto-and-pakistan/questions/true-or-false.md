# WTO and Pakistan — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The WTO has no agreement on agriculture.
**Answer: False** — the Agreement on Agriculture is named first for Pakistan.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The WTO affects neither farms nor factories in Pakistan.
**Answer: False** — its impact on both is the chapter's core.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The WTO's impact is judged in trade numbers only.
**Answer: False** — cultural and social impacts close the chapter.
