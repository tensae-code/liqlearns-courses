# WTO and Pakistan — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which WTO agreements does the book name for Pakistan?
**Answer:** The Agreement on Agriculture and Trade-Related Intellectual Property Rights (TRIPs).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** On which sectors is the WTO's impact traced first?
**Answer:** On agriculture and on industry.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What impacts close the chapter?
**Answer:** The impact on the balance of payments, and the cultural and social impacts.
