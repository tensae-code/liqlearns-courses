# Public debt and development — Haiti's case — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Séance 1 of the Haitian book asks pupils to analyse —
a) the effects of a country's debt and successive loans on its development  b) the price of coffee  c) the rules of elections
**Answer: a) the effects of a country's debt and successive loans on its development**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** "Neocolonialism by debt" argues that —
a) debt has no effects after independence  b) a formally independent country can still be bound by its debt — the indemnity and the loans taken to pay it  c) colonies should borrow more
**Answer: b) a formally independent country can still be bound by its debt — the indemnity and the loans taken to pay it**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The book's enquiry ends with pupils —
a) copying the chronology  b) arguing the restitution and reparations debate themselves  c) sitting a map test
**Answer: b) arguing the restitution and reparations debate themselves**
