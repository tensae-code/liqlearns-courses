# Public debt and development — Haiti's case — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The book teaches debt and development as a closed question with one settled answer.
**Answer: False** — Séance 1 opens it as a contemporary debate, and pupils analyse the effects of the debt and successive loans on development.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The book's title names the argument "neocolonialism by debt".
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The enquiry draws on President Aristide's 2003 restitution speech and current press investigations.
**Answer: True**
