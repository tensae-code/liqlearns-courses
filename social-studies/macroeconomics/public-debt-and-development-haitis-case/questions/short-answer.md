# Public debt and development — Haiti's case — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does Séance 1 set pupils to analyse?
**Answer:** The effects of a country's debt and its successive loans on its development — traced on Haiti itself, as a contemporary debate.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** State the neocolonialism-by-debt argument.
**Answer:** A country can be formally independent and still be bound by its debt — the indemnity imposed at recognition and the successive loans taken to pay it keep wealth flowing out and choices constrained long after independence.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** How does the book close the enquiry?
**Answer:** Openly — pupils argue the restitution and reparations debate themselves, working from President Aristide's 2003 restitution speech and current press investigations.
