# Hands-on — Public debt and development — Haiti's case

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Argue both sides
Write two short speeches on pieces of paper — one for restitution and reparations, one against — each using one effect of debt on development from the lesson. Read both aloud, the book's way: the debate is argued, not recited.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the neocolonialism-by-debt argument from memory — independence on one side, the indemnity and the successive loans still binding the country on the other — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
