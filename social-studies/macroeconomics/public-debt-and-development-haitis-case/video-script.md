# Video script — Public debt and development — Haiti's case

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

What does a country's debt do to its development? Haiti's Grade 9 book opens with exactly that question — and calls it a contemporary debate.

Pupils analyse the effects of the debt and the successive loans on Haiti's development. Then they weigh the book's own argument: neocolonialism by debt — a country formally independent, still bound by what it owes.

The book ends open: pupils argue the restitution and reparations debate themselves, from a 2003 restitution speech and today's press investigations.
