# Hands-on — Natural resources, exports and jobs

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Put three small piles on the table: one for food eaten at home, one for food exported, one for raw materials shipped abroad. Name a Bahamian example for each pile from the lesson.

## Draw it and check it
Draw two chains from a fishing boat: a short chain to the fisher's own income (direct), and a longer chain to the people whose work stands behind the boat (indirect). Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
