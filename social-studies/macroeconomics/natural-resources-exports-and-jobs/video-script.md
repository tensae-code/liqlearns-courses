# Video script — Natural resources, exports and jobs

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Before tourism, there were resources. The Bahamas' Grade 4 book teaches that natural resources built the Bahamian economy — sisal and sponging in the past, whole industries standing on what the islands grow and the sea provides.

The pattern still runs. Food is produced for local consumption and for export. Raw materials — cascarilla, aragonite, salt — are shipped abroad for manufacturing. And fishing and farming provide jobs and income, direct and indirect: the fisher and farmer themselves, and everyone whose work stands behind them.
