# Natural resources, exports and jobs — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The book says the Bahamian economy was built on —
a) natural resources  b) container ships  c) offshore banks
**Answer: a) natural resources**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Cascarilla, aragonite and salt are shipped abroad —
a) as finished products  b) for manufacturing  c) as tourists
**Answer: b) for manufacturing**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Indirect jobs in fishing and farming are —
a) the fisher and farmer themselves  b) no jobs at all  c) the work standing behind the fisher and farmer — supplying, carrying, selling, serving
**Answer: c) the work standing behind the fisher and farmer — supplying, carrying, selling, serving**
