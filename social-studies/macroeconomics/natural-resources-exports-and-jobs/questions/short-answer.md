# Natural resources, exports and jobs — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the spread claim natural resources did for The Bahamas?
**Answer:** Natural resources built the Bahamian economy — sisal and sponging in the past, and fishing and farming still providing jobs and income.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the three raw materials the book says are shipped abroad for manufacturing.
**Answer:** Cascarilla, aragonite and salt.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What is the difference between a direct and an indirect job in fishing?
**Answer:** A direct job is the fisher himself, earning from the catch. An indirect job is the work standing behind him — supplying, carrying, selling or serving the fishing trade.
