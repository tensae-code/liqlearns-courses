# Natural resources, exports and jobs — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Sisal and sponging are past Bahamian resource industries.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** All food produced in The Bahamas is eaten in The Bahamas.
**Answer: False — food is produced for local consumption and export.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Aragonite is shipped abroad as a finished product.
**Answer: False — aragonite is a raw material, shipped abroad for manufacturing.**
