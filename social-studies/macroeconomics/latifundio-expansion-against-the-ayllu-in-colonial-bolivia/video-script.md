# Video script — Latifundio expansion against the ayllu in colonial Bolivia

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Latifundio expansion against the ayllu in colonial Bolivia — as Bolivia's book teaches it, says the narrator: the hacienda and latifundio concentrated land in few hands, against the ayllu's common holding; the hacienda seized the ayllus' fertile land, and debt and Crown taxes forced families to sell or rent communal land to the creole aristocracy; auctions in 1866–69 and the 1880 law abolishing the communities cut the community to a third of its founding farm property by the 20th century.
