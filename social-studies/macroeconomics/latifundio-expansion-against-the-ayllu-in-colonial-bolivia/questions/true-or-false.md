# Latifundio expansion against the ayllu in colonial Bolivia — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The minifundio is the largest kind of estate.
**Answer: False** — the minifundio is the tiny plot; the latifundio is the concentrated great holding.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The ayllus gained the fertile land near the cities as haciendas grew.
**Answer: False** — the book teaches the reverse — Europeans seized the ayllus' most fertile lands.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** By the 20th century the communities held more land than at the Republic's founding.
**Answer: False** — they held only a third of the farm property they had held then.
