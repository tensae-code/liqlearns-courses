# Latifundio expansion against the ayllu in colonial Bolivia — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Andean community that held its land in common was the _____.
**Answer: ayllu**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Falling production drove indigenous families into _____, and many sold or rented communal land to pay the Crown's taxes.
**Answer: debt**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The _____ law granted absolute property and legally abolished the communities.
**Answer: 1880**
