# Latifundio expansion against the ayllu in colonial Bolivia — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the lesson teach about hacienda, latifundio, minifundio — the words first?
**Answer:** The hacienda and latifundio concentrated land in few hands, against the ayllu's common holding.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the lesson teach about the expansion — fertile land taken, families in debt?
**Answer:** The hacienda seized the ayllus' fertile land, and debt and Crown taxes forced families to sell or rent communal land to the creole aristocracy.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the lesson teach about auctions and laws — the community abolished on paper?
**Answer:** Auctions in 1866–69 and the 1880 law abolishing the communities cut the community to a third of its founding farm property by the 20th century.
