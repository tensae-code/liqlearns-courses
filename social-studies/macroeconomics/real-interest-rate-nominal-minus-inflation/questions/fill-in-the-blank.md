# Real interest rate — nominal minus inflation — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or figure from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The rate as quoted, before inflation, is the ____ rate.
**Answer: nominal**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Real rate equals nominal rate ____ inflation.
**Answer: minus**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** At 15% nominal and 8% inflation, the real return is ____%.
**Answer: 7**
