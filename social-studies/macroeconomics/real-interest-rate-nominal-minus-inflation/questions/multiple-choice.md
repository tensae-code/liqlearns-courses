# Real interest rate — nominal minus inflation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "The nominal rate — the rate as quoted"?
a) The nominal rate is the quoted rate before inflation.  b) The real rate = nominal rate minus inflation, and measures the true cost of borrowing and gain from saving.  c) 15% nominal with 8% inflation gives a 7% real return.
**Answer: a) The nominal rate is the quoted rate before inflation.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "The real rate — the true cost and gain"?
a) 15% nominal with 8% inflation gives a 7% real return.  b) The nominal rate is the quoted rate before inflation.  c) The real rate = nominal rate minus inflation, and measures the true cost of borrowing and gain from saving.
**Answer: c) The real rate = nominal rate minus inflation, and measures the true cost of borrowing and gain from saving.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "The worked example — 15, 8 and 7"?
a) The real rate = nominal rate minus inflation, and measures the true cost of borrowing and gain from saving.  b) 15% nominal with 8% inflation gives a 7% real return.  c) The nominal rate is the quoted rate before inflation.
**Answer: b) 15% nominal with 8% inflation gives a 7% real return.**
