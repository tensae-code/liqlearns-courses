# Real interest rate — nominal minus inflation — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "The nominal rate — the rate as quoted" in your own words.
**Answer:** The nominal rate is the quoted rate before inflation.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "The real rate — the true cost and gain" in your own words.
**Answer:** The real rate = nominal rate minus inflation, and measures the true cost of borrowing and gain from saving.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "The worked example — 15, 8 and 7" in your own words.
**Answer:** 15% nominal with 8% inflation gives a 7% real return.
