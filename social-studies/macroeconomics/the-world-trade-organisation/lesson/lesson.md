## Sub lesson 1 — The WTO — what it is

The Class 12 chapter introduces the **World Trade Organisation (WTO)**: the world body where countries agree the rules of trade between them, instead of each making its own rules against the others.

## Sub lesson 2 — Its objectives

The book sets out the WTO's **objectives**: freer and fairer trade between members, under agreed rules, with disputes settled by the organisation's procedures rather than by trade wars.

## Sub lesson 3 — Its agreements — GATT, GATS, TRIPs

The headwords of its agreements are named: **GATT** for trade in goods, **GATS** for trade in services, and **TRIPs** for trade-related intellectual property — the three pillars a learner must be able to name.
