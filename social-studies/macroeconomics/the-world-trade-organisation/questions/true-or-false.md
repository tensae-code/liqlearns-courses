# The world trade organisation — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The WTO is a bank that lends to traders.
**Answer: False** — it is the rule-making body for world trade, not a bank.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The WTO aims to stop all trade between countries.
**Answer: False** — it aims at freer, fairer trade — the opposite.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** GATS is the agreement on goods.
**Answer: False** — GATT covers goods; GATS covers services.
