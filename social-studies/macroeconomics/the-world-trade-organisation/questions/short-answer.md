# The world trade organisation — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the WTO?
**Answer:** The World Trade Organisation — the world body where countries agree the rules of trade between them.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What are the WTO's objectives as the book sets them out?
**Answer:** Freer and fairer trade between members under agreed rules, with disputes settled by procedure.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name the WTO agreements the book gives and what each covers.
**Answer:** GATT — goods; GATS — services; TRIPs — trade-related intellectual property.
