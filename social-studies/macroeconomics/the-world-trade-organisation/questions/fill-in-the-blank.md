# The world trade organisation — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The world body for the rules of trade is the World Trade _____.
**Answer: Organisation**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Freer and fairer trade under agreed rules are the WTO's _____.
**Answer: objectives**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The agreement on trade in services is _____.
**Answer: GATS**
