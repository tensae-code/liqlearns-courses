# The world trade organisation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** About the wto — what it is — the lesson teaches that —
a) the WTO is the world body where countries agree the rules of trade  b) the WTO's objectives are freer, fairer trade under agreed rules  c) GATT covers goods, GATS services, and TRIPs intellectual property
**Answer: a) the WTO is the world body where countries agree the rules of trade**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** About its objectives — the lesson teaches that —
a) the WTO is the world body where countries agree the rules of trade  b) the WTO's objectives are freer, fairer trade under agreed rules  c) GATT covers goods, GATS services, and TRIPs intellectual property
**Answer: b) the WTO's objectives are freer, fairer trade under agreed rules**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** About its agreements — gatt, gats, trips — the lesson teaches that —
a) the WTO is the world body where countries agree the rules of trade  b) the WTO's objectives are freer, fairer trade under agreed rules  c) GATT covers goods, GATS services, and TRIPs intellectual property
**Answer: c) GATT covers goods, GATS services, and TRIPs intellectual property**
