# Latin America and globalisation — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** When did economic globalisation take off, according to the book?
**Answer:** At the end of the 20th century.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Who holds most of the world's markets under globalisation, in the book's account?
**Answer:** A small group of powers.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the book say Latin American countries share, and what does it argue for?
**Answer:** They share economic and social problems, and the book makes the case for regional union.
