# Latin America and globalisation — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Globalisation means the whole world becoming one field of activity.
**Answer: True** — the book builds the meaning from the word 'globe'.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The book presents globalisation as a field where all countries hold equal market power.
**Answer: False** — a small group of powers holds most of the world's markets.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The book argues Latin American countries should unite regionally.
**Answer: True** — shared problems ground the case for regional union.
