# Latin America and globalisation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The word globalisation comes from —
a) globe  b) global markets were named after a bank  c) a Latin word for money
**Answer: a) globe**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In globalisation, most of the world's markets are held by —
a) a small group of powers  b) all countries equally  c) Latin America alone
**Answer: a) a small group of powers**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The book's answer to Latin America's shared problems is —
a) regional union  b) each country acting entirely alone  c) leaving the world market
**Answer: a) regional union**
