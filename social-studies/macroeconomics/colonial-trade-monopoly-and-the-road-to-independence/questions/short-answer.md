# Colonial trade monopoly and the road to independence — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the lesson teach about el monopolio comercial español?
**Answer:** Spain was the colonies' only buyer.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the lesson teach about el monopolio comercial español — more?
**Answer:** Fixed purchase and sale prices itself and charged excessive taxes, while inter-colonial and foreign trade was banned.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the lesson teach about el monopolio comercial español — more?
**Answer:** A core economic cause of the independence movements.
