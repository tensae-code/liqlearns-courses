## Sub lesson 1 — El monopolio comercial español

Spain was the colonies' only buyer.

## Sub lesson 2 — El monopolio comercial español — more

Fixed purchase and sale prices itself and charged excessive taxes, while inter-colonial and foreign trade was banned.

## Sub lesson 3 — El monopolio comercial español — more

A core economic cause of the independence movements.
