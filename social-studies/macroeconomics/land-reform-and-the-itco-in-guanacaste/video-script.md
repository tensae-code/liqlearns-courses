# Video script — Land reform and the ITCO in Guanacaste

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Latifundio and minifundio defined, and in Guanacaste only 22% of the farm population owned land to cultivate, much of it unproductive in few hands.

So the ITCO (created 1961.

Financed under the Alliance for Progress) bought land from the big owners and redistributed it to people with few resources, later organising peasant settlements in cooperatives.
