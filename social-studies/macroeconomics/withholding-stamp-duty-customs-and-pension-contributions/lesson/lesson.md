# Withholding, stamp duty, customs and pension contributions

## Do it first

On one payslip, two deductions build a pension: one from the worker, one from the boss. Guess the two percentages — they add to eighteen. Then read.

## Sub lesson 1 — Pension contributions — 11% and 7%

Pension contributions are salary-based: 11% from the employer and 7% from the employee, paid to the Private Organizations Pension Fund. On a 10,000 birr salary that is 1,100 birr from the employer and 700 from the employee — 1,800 birr a month toward old age.

## Sub lesson 2 — Withholding and stamp duty

Withholding tax takes 2% of domestic transaction values at source, remitted monthly — tax collected before the income fully settles. Stamp duty is paid by affixing government stamps to designated documents, taxing the document itself.

## Sub lesson 3 — Customs duty as a wall

Customs duty on imports and exports doubles as a trade barrier: set high — 100% or more — it can shut out imports and shelter infant domestic industries from subsidised foreign competition. A revenue tool and a protection tool in one schedule.
