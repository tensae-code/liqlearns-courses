# Hands-on — Withholding, stamp duty, customs and pension contributions

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Read a payslip
Build a payslip for a 10,000 birr salary showing the 11% and 7% pension lines computed (1,100 and 700). Label whose money each line is before it becomes pension.

## Wall or window
Argue both sides of a 100% customs duty on one imported good with a young Ethiopian rival industry: the shelter case and the shopper's case, two lines each.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
