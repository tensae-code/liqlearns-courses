# Withholding, stamp duty, customs and pension contributions — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Pension contributions — 11% and 7%" in your own words.
**Answer:** Pension contributions are 11% from the employer and 7% from the employee.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Withholding and stamp duty" in your own words.
**Answer:** Withholding tax takes 2% at source, remitted monthly; stamp duty is paid by affixing stamps to documents.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Customs duty as a wall" in your own words.
**Answer:** Customs duty at 100% or more can shut out imports and shelter infant industries.
