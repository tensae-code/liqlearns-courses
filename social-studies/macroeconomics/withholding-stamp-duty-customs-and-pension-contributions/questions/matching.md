# Withholding, stamp duty, customs and pension contributions — matching

Match each part to its right statement.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Pension contributions — 11% and 7% — c
**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Withholding and stamp duty — a
**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Customs duty as a wall — b

Statements:
a) Withholding tax takes 2% at source, remitted monthly; stamp duty is paid by affixing stamps to documents
b) Customs duty at 100% or more can shut out imports and shelter infant industries
c) Pension contributions are 11% from the employer and 7% from the employee

**Answer key:** 1 — c; 2 — a; 3 — b
