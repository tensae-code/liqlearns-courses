# Withholding, stamp duty, customs and pension contributions — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Pension contributions are 11% from the employer and 7% from the employee.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Withholding tax takes 2% at source, remitted monthly; stamp duty is paid by affixing stamps to documents.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Customs duty at 100% or more can shut out imports and shelter infant industries.
**Answer: True**
