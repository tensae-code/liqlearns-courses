# Withholding, stamp duty, customs and pension contributions — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Pension contributions — 11% and 7%"?
a) Pension contributions are 11% from the employer and 7% from the employee  b) Withholding tax takes 2% at source, remitted monthly; stamp duty is paid by affixing stamps to documents  c) Customs duty at 100% or more can shut out imports and shelter infant industries
**Answer: a) Pension contributions are 11% from the employer and 7% from the employee**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Withholding and stamp duty"?
a) Withholding tax takes 2% at source, remitted monthly; stamp duty is paid by affixing stamps to documents  b) Customs duty at 100% or more can shut out imports and shelter infant industries  c) Pension contributions are 11% from the employer and 7% from the employee
**Answer: a) Withholding tax takes 2% at source, remitted monthly; stamp duty is paid by affixing stamps to documents**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Customs duty as a wall"?
a) Customs duty at 100% or more can shut out imports and shelter infant industries  b) Pension contributions are 11% from the employer and 7% from the employee  c) Withholding tax takes 2% at source, remitted monthly; stamp duty is paid by affixing stamps to documents
**Answer: a) Customs duty at 100% or more can shut out imports and shelter infant industries**
