# Neoclassical economic thought — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The marginal revolution rebuilt economics around —
a) the total treasure of the king  b) the next one — the margin — not totals  c) ignoring individual choices completely
**Answer: b) the next one — the margin — not totals**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The Austrian wing of the school pressed —
a) individual choice, time and free markets  b) abolishing prices  c) planning the whole economy from above
**Answer: a) individual choice, time and free markets**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The neoclassical answer to why water is cheap though useful is —
a) diamonds are more useful than water  b) water has no value at all  c) price follows the value of the next unit, small for plentiful water
**Answer: c) price follows the value of the next unit, small for plentiful water**
