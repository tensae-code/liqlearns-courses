# Expansionary monetary policy — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "All three levers toward loose" in your own words.
**Answer:** Cut the discount rate, buy securities, cut the reserve ratio — money supply rises.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "The chain to demand" in your own words.
**Answer:** Money up, interest rates down, borrowing and spending up.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Output back to potential" in your own words.
**Answer:** The book's figure carries output from 600 up to the potential 700.
