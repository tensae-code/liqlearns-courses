# Expansionary monetary policy — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Cut the discount rate, buy securities, cut the reserve ratio — money supply rises.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Money up, interest rates down, borrowing and spending up.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The book's figure carries output from 600 up to the potential 700.
**Answer: True**
