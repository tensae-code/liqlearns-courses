# Expansionary monetary policy — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "All three levers toward loose"?
a) Cut the discount rate, buy securities, cut the reserve ratio — money supply rises  b) Money up, interest rates down, borrowing and spending up  c) The book's figure carries output from 600 up to the potential 700
**Answer: a) Cut the discount rate, buy securities, cut the reserve ratio — money supply rises**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "The chain to demand"?
a) Money up, interest rates down, borrowing and spending up  b) The book's figure carries output from 600 up to the potential 700  c) Cut the discount rate, buy securities, cut the reserve ratio — money supply rises
**Answer: a) Money up, interest rates down, borrowing and spending up**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Output back to potential"?
a) The book's figure carries output from 600 up to the potential 700  b) Cut the discount rate, buy securities, cut the reserve ratio — money supply rises  c) Money up, interest rates down, borrowing and spending up
**Answer: a) The book's figure carries output from 600 up to the potential 700**
