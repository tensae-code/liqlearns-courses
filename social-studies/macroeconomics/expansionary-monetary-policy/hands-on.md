# Hands-on — Expansionary monetary policy

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Chain the links
Write the expansionary chain as six arrows from 'reserve ratio cut' to 'output at potential'. Break one link deliberately and say where the policy stalls.

## Lever check
For each of the three tools from the last three lessons, state its expansionary direction in one line — then mix them up and re-sort a partner's lines.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
