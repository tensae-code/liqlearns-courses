# Private and public investment — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Private investment is spending by the state on infrastructure.
**Answer: False — State spending is public investment. Private investment belongs to firms and households.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Pensions are a kind of physical investment.
**Answer: False — Pensions and social security are consumption investment. Physical investment means infrastructure.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The book says all public investment must always be done by the state alone.
**Answer: False — It notes the opposite pressure — toward privatisation and public–private partnerships.**
