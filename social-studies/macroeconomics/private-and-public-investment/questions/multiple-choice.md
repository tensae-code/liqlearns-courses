# Private and public investment — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Private investment"?
a) Private investment is firms' and households' spending on machinery, factories and inventories.  b) Public investment divides into physical, human and consumption investment.  c) Modern practice pressures toward privatisation and public–private partnerships.
**Answer: a) Private investment is firms' and households' spending on machinery, factories and inventories.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Public investment — and its three kinds"?
a) Modern practice pressures toward privatisation and public–private partnerships.  b) Private investment is firms' and households' spending on machinery, factories and inventories.  c) Public investment divides into physical, human and consumption investment.
**Answer: c) Public investment divides into physical, human and consumption investment.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Sharing the work — partnerships"?
a) Public investment divides into physical, human and consumption investment.  b) Modern practice pressures toward privatisation and public–private partnerships.  c) Private investment is firms' and households' spending on machinery, factories and inventories.
**Answer: b) Modern practice pressures toward privatisation and public–private partnerships.**
