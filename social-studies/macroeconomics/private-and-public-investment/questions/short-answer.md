# Private and public investment — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Private investment" in your own words.
**Answer:** Private investment is firms' and households' spending on machinery, factories and inventories.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Public investment — and its three kinds" in your own words.
**Answer:** Public investment divides into physical, human and consumption investment.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Sharing the work — partnerships" in your own words.
**Answer:** Modern practice pressures toward privatisation and public–private partnerships.
