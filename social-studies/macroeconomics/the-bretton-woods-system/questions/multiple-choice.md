# The Bretton Woods system — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** About la conferencia de bretton woods — the lesson teaches that —
a) The 1944 system for a New International Economic Order.
b) A gold-exchange standard with the dollar at $35 an ounce.
c) Other currencies fixed to the dollar within a 1% band, and IMF loans to finance temporary balance-of-payments deficits in exchange for agreed…
**Answer: a) The 1944 system for a New International Economic Order.**
