# The Bretton Woods system — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The 1944 system for a New International _____ Order.
**Answer: Economic**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A gold-exchange _____ with the dollar at $35 an ounce.
**Answer: standard**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Other currencies fixed to the dollar within a 1% band, and IMF loans to finance temporary balance-of-payments _____ in exchange for agreed economic policies.
**Answer: deficits**
