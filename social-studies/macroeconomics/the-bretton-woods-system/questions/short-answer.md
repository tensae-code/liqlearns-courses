# The Bretton Woods system — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the lesson teach about la conferencia de bretton woods?
**Answer:** The 1944 system for a New International Economic Order.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the lesson teach about la conferencia de bretton woods — more?
**Answer:** A gold-exchange standard with the dollar at $35 an ounce.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the lesson teach about la conferencia de bretton woods — more — more?
**Answer:** Other currencies fixed to the dollar within a 1% band, and IMF loans to finance temporary balance-of-payments deficits in exchange for agreed economic policies.
