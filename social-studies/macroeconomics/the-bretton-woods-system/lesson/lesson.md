# The Bretton Woods system

## Do it first

## Sub lesson 1 — La Conferencia de Bretton Woods

The 1944 system for a New International Economic Order.

## Sub lesson 2 — La Conferencia de Bretton Woods — more

A gold-exchange standard with the dollar at $35 an ounce.

## Sub lesson 3 — La Conferencia de Bretton Woods — more — more

Other currencies fixed to the dollar within a 1% band, and IMF loans to finance temporary balance-of-payments deficits in exchange for agreed economic policies.
