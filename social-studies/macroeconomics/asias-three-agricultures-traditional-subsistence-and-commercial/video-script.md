# Video script — Asias three agricultures traditional subsistence and commercial

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Asias three agricultures traditional subsistence and commercial — as Colombia's book teaches it, says the narrator: Asia's agriculture of contrasts — traditional (manual, labour-intensive, e.g. China's rice), subsistence (peasants satisfy their own needs, only a minimum of the harvest goes to trade) And commercial (highly technified, high yields, surpluses for export)
