# What decides investment? — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Higher business taxes encourage firms to invest more.
**Answer: False — Taxes cut expected after-tax profit, so higher business taxes discourage investment.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Ethiopian firms report no significant challenges.
**Answer: False — The surveys list six challenge families — infrastructure heads the list.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Ethiopia ranked 19th of 190 on ease of doing business in 2019.
**Answer: False — The rank was 159th of 190 — near the bottom, which is why it prompted reforms.**
