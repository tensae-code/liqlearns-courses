# What decides investment? — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Interest, expectations and taxes" in your own words.
**Answer:** Investment falls as interest rates rise, rises with optimistic sales expectations, and falls when business taxes cut after-tax profit.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Ethiopia's firm challenges" in your own words.
**Answer:** Ethiopian firms' chief challenges include infrastructure, access to finance, regulations, raw materials, informal practices and markets.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "The ease-of-doing-business rank" in your own words.
**Answer:** Ethiopia ranked 159th of 190 on ease of doing business in 2019, prompting reforms.
