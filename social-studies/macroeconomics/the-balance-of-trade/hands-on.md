# Hands-on — The balance of trade

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Your household's trade balance
List what your household 'exports' (sells or earns from outside) and 'imports' (buys from outside) in a month. Compute its balance and say what finances any deficit.

## Two scales
Draw two balance scales, imports outweighing exports for Ethiopia 2020 and exports outweighing imports for China, with the two figures labelled. Check the direction of each tip.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
