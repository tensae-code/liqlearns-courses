# The balance of trade — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Exports minus imports of goods" in your own words.
**Answer:** The balance of trade is exports minus imports of goods: surplus or deficit.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Ethiopia's deficit, China's surplus" in your own words.
**Answer:** In 2020 Ethiopia ran a trade deficit of about US$10.7 billion against China's surplus of about US$535 billion.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Why the balance matters" in your own words.
**Answer:** A lasting trade deficit must be financed and is what foreign-exchange policy answers.
