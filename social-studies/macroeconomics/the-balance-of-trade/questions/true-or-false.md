# The balance of trade — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The balance of trade is exports minus imports of goods: surplus or deficit.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In 2020 Ethiopia ran a trade deficit of about US$10.7 billion against China's surplus of about US$535 billion.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A lasting trade deficit must be financed and is what foreign-exchange policy answers.
**Answer: True**
