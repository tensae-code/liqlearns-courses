# The balance of trade — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Exports minus imports of goods"?
a) The balance of trade is exports minus imports of goods: surplus or deficit  b) In 2020 Ethiopia ran a trade deficit of about US$10.7 billion against China's surplus of about US$535 billion  c) A lasting trade deficit must be financed and is what foreign-exchange policy answers
**Answer: a) The balance of trade is exports minus imports of goods: surplus or deficit**
