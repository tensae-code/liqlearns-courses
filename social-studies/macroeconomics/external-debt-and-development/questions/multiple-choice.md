# External debt and development — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** About la deuda externa como obstáculo al desarrollo — the lesson teaches that —
a) Loans from international financial institutions and rich countries carried high interest;.
b) States must devote a large share of their resources to repayment, blocking development, and defaulting means exclusion from future loans.
c) Alongside technological dependence that leaves poor countries uncompetitive.
**Answer: a) Loans from international financial institutions and rich countries carried high interest;.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** About la deuda externa como obstáculo al desarrollo — more — the lesson teaches that —
a) Alongside technological dependence that leaves poor countries uncompetitive.
b) States must devote a large share of their resources to repayment, blocking development, and defaulting means exclusion from future loans.
c) Loans from international financial institutions and rich countries carried high interest;.
**Answer: b) States must devote a large share of their resources to repayment, blocking development, and defaulting means exclusion from future loans.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** About la deuda externa como obstáculo al desarrollo — more — more — the lesson teaches that —
a) Loans from international financial institutions and rich countries carried high interest;.
b) States must devote a large share of their resources to repayment, blocking development, and defaulting means exclusion from future loans.
c) Alongside technological dependence that leaves poor countries uncompetitive.
**Answer: c) Alongside technological dependence that leaves poor countries uncompetitive.**
