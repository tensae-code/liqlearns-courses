# External debt and development — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the lesson teach about la deuda externa como obstáculo al desarrollo?
**Answer:** Loans from international financial institutions and rich countries carried high interest;.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the lesson teach about la deuda externa como obstáculo al desarrollo — more?
**Answer:** States must devote a large share of their resources to repayment, blocking development, and defaulting means exclusion from future loans.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the lesson teach about la deuda externa como obstáculo al desarrollo — more — more?
**Answer:** Alongside technological dependence that leaves poor countries uncompetitive.
