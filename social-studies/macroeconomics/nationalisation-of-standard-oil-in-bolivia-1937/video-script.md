Nationalisation of Standard Oil in Bolivia, 1937 — as Bolivia's book teaches it, says the narrator: Standard Oil exported oil to Argentina through a secret pipeline; in fact 1,300 barrels were exported without paying the 11% export tax; in 1937 the state nationalised Standard Oil's goods in Bolivia.
