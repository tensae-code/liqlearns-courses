# Nationalisation of Standard Oil in Bolivia, 1937 — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the lesson teach about the secret pipeline to Argentina?
**Answer:** Standard Oil exported oil to Argentina through a secret pipeline.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the lesson teach about 704 admitted, 1,300 in fact — the 11% tax unpaid?
**Answer:** In fact 1,300 barrels were exported without paying the 11% export tax.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the lesson teach about the state nationalises the company's goods?
**Answer:** In 1937 the state nationalised Standard Oil's goods in Bolivia.
