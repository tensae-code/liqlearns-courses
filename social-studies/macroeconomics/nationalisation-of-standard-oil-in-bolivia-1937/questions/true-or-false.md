# Nationalisation of Standard Oil in Bolivia, 1937 — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Standard Oil declared every barrel it exported.
**Answer: False** — it exported through a secret pipeline and under-declared the amount.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Standard Oil had paid all its export taxes in full.
**Answer: False** — the 11% export tax on the secret exports was never paid.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The state fined the company and let it continue as before.
**Answer: False** — the state nationalised its goods — the pattern of 1952 and 2006 begins here.
