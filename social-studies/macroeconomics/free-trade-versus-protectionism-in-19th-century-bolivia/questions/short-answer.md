# Free trade versus protectionism in 19th-century Bolivia — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the lesson teach about the free-traders' programme?
**Answer:** The free-traders declared raw-metal exports duty-free, cut tariffs on foreign cloth and ended the quina monopoly.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the lesson teach about the protectionists' programme?
**Answer:** The protectionists wanted state control of silver and quina exports to secure treasury income.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the lesson teach about santa Cruz the protectionist — and the fight's outcome?
**Answer:** Santa Cruz protected national industry by prohibiting competing imports such as tocuyo cloth, yet free trade won the century's fight.
