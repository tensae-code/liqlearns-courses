# Free trade versus protectionism in 19th-century Bolivia — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The free-traders declared raw-metal exports _____.
**Answer: duty-free**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The protectionists wanted state control of silver and _____ exports.
**Answer: quina**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Santa Cruz prohibited imports of _____ cloth to protect its local producers.
**Answer: tocuyo**
