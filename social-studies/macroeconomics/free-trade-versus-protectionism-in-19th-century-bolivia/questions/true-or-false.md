# Free trade versus protectionism in 19th-century Bolivia — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The free-traders raised tariffs on foreign cloth.
**Answer: False** — they cut tariffs on foreign cloth and ended the quina monopoly.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The protectionists wanted no state role in exports at all.
**Answer: False** — they wanted state control of silver and quina exports.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Santa Cruz was the leader of the free-traders.
**Answer: False** — the book calls him clearly mercantilist and highly protectionist.
