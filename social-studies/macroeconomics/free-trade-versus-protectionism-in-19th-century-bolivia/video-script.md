# Video script — Free trade versus protectionism in 19th-century Bolivia

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Free trade versus protectionism in 19th-century Bolivia — as Bolivia's book teaches it, says the narrator: the free-traders declared raw-metal exports duty-free, cut tariffs on foreign cloth and ended the quina monopoly; the protectionists wanted state control of silver and quina exports to secure treasury income; Santa Cruz protected national industry by prohibiting competing imports such as tocuyo cloth, yet free trade won the century's fight.
