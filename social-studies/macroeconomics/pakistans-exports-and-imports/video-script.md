# Video script — Pakistan's exports and imports

Pakistan's exports and imports — as Pakistan's book teaches it, says the narrator: Pakistan's exports are led by the cotton chain — cotton, yarn, hosiery and garments; Pakistan's major imports are petroleum products, chemicals and medicines, machinery and edible goods; the direction of imports shows which countries Pakistan buys from.
