# Pakistan's exports and imports

## Do it first

Before you read on, write what you already think this lesson's title means — pakistan's exports and imports — in one sentence of your own. Keep it; you will check it against the lesson at the end.

## Sub lesson 1 — Major exports

The Class 12 foreign-trade chapter lists Pakistan's **major exports**: **cotton, cotton yarn, hosiery and ready-made garments, leather and leather products, carpets, fish and fish products, vegetables and fruits, surgical instruments, and sports goods** — a basket led by the cotton chain.

## Sub lesson 2 — Major imports

Against them stand the **major imports**: **petroleum products, chemicals and medicines, machinery, and edible goods** — what Pakistan cannot supply itself and must buy, oil first.

## Sub lesson 3 — The direction of imports

The chapter also teaches the **direction of imports** — which countries Pakistan buys from. A trade basket has a geography as well as a list, and the direction shows the country's economic ties.
