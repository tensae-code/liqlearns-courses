# Pakistan's exports and imports — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name four of Pakistan's major exports.
**Answer:** Any four of: cotton, cotton yarn, hosiery and ready-made garments, leather goods, carpets, fish products, vegetables and fruits, surgical instruments, sports goods.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name Pakistan's major imports.
**Answer:** Petroleum products, chemicals and medicines, machinery, and edible goods.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What is the direction of imports?
**Answer:** Which countries Pakistan buys from — the geography of its import trade.
