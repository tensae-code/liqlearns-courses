# Pakistan's exports and imports — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** About major exports — the lesson teaches that —
a) Pakistan's exports are led by the cotton chain — cotton, yarn, hosiery and garments  b) Pakistan's major imports are petroleum products, chemicals and medicines, machinery and edible goods  c) the direction of imports shows which countries Pakistan buys from
