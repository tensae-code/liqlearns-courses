# Pakistan's exports and imports — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Pakistan's leading exports are cars and computers.
**Answer: False** — the list is led by cotton and its products.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Petroleum is a major Pakistani export in the book's list.
**Answer: False** — petroleum products head the imports list.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Imports have no direction in the book's account.
**Answer: False** — their direction is a named part of the chapter.
