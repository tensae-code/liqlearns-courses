# Hands-on — Progressive, proportional and regressive taxes

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Classify three schedules
Invent three small schedules (tax at three incomes each). Compute every average rate, classify each schedule, and swap with a partner to verify the divisions.

## Draw the three lines
Draw three average-rate lines against income — rising, flat, falling — and label each with its name and a one-line verdict on fairness under ability-to-pay.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
