# Progressive, proportional and regressive taxes — matching

Match each part to its right statement.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Three shapes of the average rate — c
**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The workout — check it — a
**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Ability-to-pay in a schedule — b
