# Progressive, proportional and regressive taxes — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or figure from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A tax taking the same share at every income is ____.
**Answer: proportional**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Tax of 300 birr on 4,000 birr is an average rate of ____ percent.
**Answer: 7.5**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Vertical equity normally argues for the ____ shape.
**Answer: progressive**
