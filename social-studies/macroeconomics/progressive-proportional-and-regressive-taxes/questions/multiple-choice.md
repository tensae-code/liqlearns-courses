# Progressive, proportional and regressive taxes — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Three shapes of the average rate"?
a) Progressive takes a rising share, proportional the same share, regressive a falling share  b) 300 on 4,000 is 7.5% and 1,400 on 20,000 is 7% — a falling share, so the schedule is regressive  c) The three shapes put ability-to-pay into a schedule; vertical equity argues for the progressive shape
