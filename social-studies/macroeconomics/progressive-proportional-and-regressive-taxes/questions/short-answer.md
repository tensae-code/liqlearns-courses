# Progressive, proportional and regressive taxes — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Three shapes of the average rate" in your own words.
**Answer:** Progressive takes a rising share, proportional the same share, regressive a falling share.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "The workout — check it" in your own words.
**Answer:** 300 on 4,000 is 7.5% and 1,400 on 20,000 is 7% — a falling share, so the schedule is regressive.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Ability-to-pay in a schedule" in your own words.
**Answer:** The three shapes put ability-to-pay into a schedule; vertical equity argues for the progressive shape.
