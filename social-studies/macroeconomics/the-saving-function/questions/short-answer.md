# The saving function — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Saving is income not consumed" in your own words.
**Answer:** Saving is disposable income not consumed, and the saving function comes from subtracting consumption from income.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Starting negative — dissaving" in your own words.
**Answer:** The saving function starts negative because autonomous consumption must be financed at low income.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "The slope, and saving versus savings" in your own words.
**Answer:** The saving function's slope is 1 − MPC; saving (a flow) differs from savings (the accumulated stock).
