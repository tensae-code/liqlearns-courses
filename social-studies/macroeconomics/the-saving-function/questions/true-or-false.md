# The saving function — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Saving is the money a household spends on durable goods.
**Answer: False — Spending on goods is consumption. Saving is the part of disposable income NOT consumed.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Saving can never be negative.
**Answer: False — It can: at low incomes households spend beyond income — negative saving, financed by borrowing or past wealth.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Saving and savings are the same thing.
**Answer: False — Saving is the act — a flow. Savings is the stock accumulated from past saving.**
