# Short-run aggregate supply — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "The upward slope" in your own words.
**Answer:** SRAS slopes upward because some costs lag behind prices in the short run.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "The shifters" in your own words.
**Answer:** SRAS shifts with input prices, productivity, production taxes and subsidies, and expectations.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Short run versus long run" in your own words.
**Answer:** In the long run supply stands vertical at potential output.
