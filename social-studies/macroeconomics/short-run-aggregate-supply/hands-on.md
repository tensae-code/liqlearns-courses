# Hands-on — Short-run aggregate supply

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Draw both shifts
Draw one SRAS curve, then two copies: one shifted left by an oil-price rise, one shifted right by a productivity gain. Label the cause on each arrow.

## Shifter sort
Sort eight events into 'shifts SRAS left', 'shifts SRAS right' and 'moves along SRAS' — remembering that a change in the general price level itself only moves along it.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
