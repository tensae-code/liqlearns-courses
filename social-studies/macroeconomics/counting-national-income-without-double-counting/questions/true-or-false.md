# Counting National Income Without Double Counting — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Only payments for this year's goods and services count.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Transfer payments and second-hand sales do not.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** This lesson teaches that its subject has nothing to do with counting National Income Without Double Counting.
**Answer: False — The lesson is built on Sri Lanka's book teaching intermediate goods excluded by the value-added method, worked from cotton.**
