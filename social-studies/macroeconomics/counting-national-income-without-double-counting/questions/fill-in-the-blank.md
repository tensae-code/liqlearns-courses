# Counting National Income Without Double Counting — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** _____ payments for this year's goods and services count.
**Answer: Only**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** _____ payments and second-hand sales do not.
**Answer: Transfer**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** _____ goods excluded by the value-added method, worked from cotton.
**Answer: Intermediate**
