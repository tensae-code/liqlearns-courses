# The plantation economy monoculture for export — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the lesson teach about foreign trade was characterised by monoculture sugar cotton?
**Answer:** Foreign trade was characterised by monoculture (sugar, cotton, coffee, vanilla, tobacco, indigo grown extensively);

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the lesson teach about plantation economies were subordinated to intensive exploitation of?
**Answer:** Plantation economies were subordinated to intensive exploitation of a single export product while market demand lasted, using enslaved, servile or cheap labour

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the lesson teach about with enormous gains for one group and profits?
**Answer:** With enormous gains for one group and profits swelling landowners' personal fortunes
