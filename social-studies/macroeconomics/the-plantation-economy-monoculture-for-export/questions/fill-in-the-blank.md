# The plantation economy monoculture for export — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Foreign trade was _____ by monoculture (sugar, cotton, coffee, vanilla, tobacco, indigo grown extensively);
**Answer: characterised**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Plantation economies were _____ to intensive exploitation of a single export product while market demand lasted, using enslaved, servile or cheap labour
**Answer: subordinated**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** With enormous gains for one group and profits swelling _____ personal fortunes
**Answer: landowners'**
