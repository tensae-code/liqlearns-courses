# Video script — State and private sector roles

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The economy's work is divided between two great actors. The private sector — families, farmers, traders and enterprises — produces and exchanges most goods and services. The state sets the frame, provides public goods and services, and answers for the whole. Cambodia's Grade 12 book closes with this division of work.

Private hands do the economy's daily producing: growing, making, carrying, selling, lending and serving, guided by prices and their own choices. Their strength is closeness and energy — millions of decisions made where the knowledge is. The book gives the private sector its full weight before it weighs the state's.

The state's work is the frame and the remainder: laws, money, public goods, protection, and steering the whole through its policies. The closing judgement of the book is balance: an economy where the division is well drawn lets each side do what it does best — the final form of the question the whole course has asked since the market system was first judged.
