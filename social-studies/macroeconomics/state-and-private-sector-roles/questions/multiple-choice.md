# State and private sector roles — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The private sector is made of —
a) only ministries and offices  b) families, farmers, traders and enterprises  c) nobody — it is an empty name
**Answer: b) families, farmers, traders and enterprises**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Private hands are guided by —
a) prices and their own choices  b) chance alone, with no signals  c) a daily order assigning every task
**Answer: a) prices and their own choices**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The state's work in the division is —
a) nothing — the state has no work  b) doing all the daily producing itself  c) the frame and the remainder — laws, money, public goods, protection, steering
**Answer: c) the frame and the remainder — laws, money, public goods, protection, steering**
