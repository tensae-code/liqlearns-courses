# State and private sector roles — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The private sector is families, farmers, traders and enterprises.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Private hands are guided by a daily order assigning every task.
**Answer: False — Private production is guided by prices and choices, not by daily assigned orders.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The state's work is the frame, public goods and steering.
**Answer: True**
