# Autonomous and induced investment — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Autonomous investment — the flat line"?
a) Autonomous investment ignores income, interest and profit, and is drawn as a horizontal line.  b) Induced investment is profit-motivated and rises with income.  c) The two kinds differ by motive: autonomous ignores income; induced answers to it.
**Answer: a) Autonomous investment ignores income, interest and profit, and is drawn as a horizontal line.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Induced investment — the rising line"?
a) The two kinds differ by motive: autonomous ignores income; induced answers to it.  b) Autonomous investment ignores income, interest and profit, and is drawn as a horizontal line.  c) Induced investment is profit-motivated and rises with income.
**Answer: c) Induced investment is profit-motivated and rises with income.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Two kinds, two motives"?
a) Induced investment is profit-motivated and rises with income.  b) The two kinds differ by motive: autonomous ignores income; induced answers to it.  c) Autonomous investment ignores income, interest and profit, and is drawn as a horizontal line.
**Answer: b) The two kinds differ by motive: autonomous ignores income; induced answers to it.**
