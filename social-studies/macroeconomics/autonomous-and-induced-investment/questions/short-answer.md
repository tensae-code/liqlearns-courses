# Autonomous and induced investment — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Autonomous investment — the flat line" in your own words.
**Answer:** Autonomous investment ignores income, interest and profit, and is drawn as a horizontal line.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Induced investment — the rising line" in your own words.
**Answer:** Induced investment is profit-motivated and rises with income.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Two kinds, two motives" in your own words.
**Answer:** The two kinds differ by motive: autonomous ignores income; induced answers to it.
