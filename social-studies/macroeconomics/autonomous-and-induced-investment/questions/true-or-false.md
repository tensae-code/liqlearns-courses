# Autonomous and induced investment — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Autonomous investment rises steeply with national income.
**Answer: False — That rising pattern is induced investment. Autonomous investment stays flat — typically government welfare spending.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Induced investment is done mainly for welfare reasons.
**Answer: False — Welfare-driven spending is the autonomous kind. Induced investment is profit-motivated.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Autonomous and induced investment are both driven by profit.
**Answer: False — Only induced investment is profit-driven; autonomous investment is typically welfare-driven government spending.**
