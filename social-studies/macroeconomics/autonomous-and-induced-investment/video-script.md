# Video script — Autonomous and induced investment

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Autonomous investment does not respond to income, interest or profit. It is typically government, welfare-driven spending, and it is drawn as a horizontal line — the same amount at every level of income.

Induced investment is profit-motivated and rises with income: higher national income raises demand, and higher demand makes more projects profitable, so firms invest more. It is drawn as an upward line.

The split is by motive: spending that goes ahead whatever the economy does, against spending the economy's own success pulls upward. Both are investment — both add to capital — but only the induced kind answers to income.
