# National strategic reserves — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Stockpiles of essential commodities kept by a government for emergencies are called strategic _____.
**Answer: reserves**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The _____ Marketing Board keeps Zimbabwe's grain reserve, for food security.
**Answer: Grain**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** ZINWA keeps Zimbabwe's _____ reserve.
**Answer: water**
