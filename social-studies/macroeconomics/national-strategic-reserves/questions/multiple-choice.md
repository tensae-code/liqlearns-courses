# National strategic reserves — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Strategic reserves are —
a) goods a shop keeps for its best customers  b) stockpiles of essential commodities a government keeps for emergencies  c) money a family saves in a piggy bank
**Answer: b) stockpiles of essential commodities a government keeps for emergencies**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In Zimbabwe, petroleum reserves are kept by —
a) the Grain Marketing Board  b) ZINWA  c) NOCZIM
**Answer: c) NOCZIM**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The reserve institutions keep economic stability by —
a) managing supply and demand of essentials  b) setting school fees  c) choosing what farmers plant
**Answer: a) managing supply and demand of essentials**
