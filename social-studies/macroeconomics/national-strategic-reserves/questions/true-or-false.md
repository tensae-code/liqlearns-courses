# National strategic reserves — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The emergencies strategic reserves are kept for include natural disasters, war and economic crises.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The Reserve Bank of Zimbabwe keeps the country's grain reserve.
**Answer: False — the Grain Marketing Board keeps grain, for food security; the Reserve Bank of Zimbabwe issues local currency to banks.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** ZETDC is Zimbabwe's electricity reserve institution.
**Answer: True**
