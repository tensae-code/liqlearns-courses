# National strategic reserves — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is a strategic reserve, and for what times is it kept?
**Answer:** A strategic reserve is a stockpile of essential commodities a government keeps for emergencies — natural disasters, war and economic crises — when the normal supply can fail.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name Zimbabwe's five reserve institutions and the essential each one keeps.
**Answer:** The Grain Marketing Board (grain and food security), NOCZIM (petroleum), ZINWA (water), ZETDC (electricity) and the Reserve Bank of Zimbabwe (issuing local currency to banks).

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** How do the reserve institutions keep economic stability?
**Answer:** They manage supply and demand of essentials — releasing stock when supply runs short and holding it when supply is plentiful — so essentials keep flowing steadily even in an emergency.
