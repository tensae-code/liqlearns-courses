# Hands-on — National strategic reserves

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Match the keepers
Write the five essentials — grain, petroleum, water, electricity, local currency — on five slips, and the five institutions on five more. Match each essential to its keeper from memory, then check against lesson/lesson.md and fix any pair you missed.

## Draw it and check it
Draw Zimbabwe's reserves as five stores — a silo, a fuel depot, a dam, a power station, a bank — and label each with its institution. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
