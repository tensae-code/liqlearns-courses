# Video script — National strategic reserves

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A strategic reserve is a stockpile of essentials a government keeps before an emergency — for a natural disaster, a war, or an economic crisis.

Zimbabwe names its keepers: the Grain Marketing Board for grain, NOCZIM for petroleum, ZINWA for water, ZETDC for electricity, and the Reserve Bank of Zimbabwe issuing local currency to banks.

The keepers manage supply and demand of the essentials, and that is how the reserves keep the whole economy stable.
