# Trend and structure of Nigeria's national income — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What has Nigeria's national income moved with?
**Answer:** Nigeria's national income has moved over time with oil, agriculture and the wider economy.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the structure of national income show?
**Answer:** The structure of national income shows which sectors the income comes from and how that make-up has changed.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What do trend and structure together show?
**Answer:** Trend and structure together show whether the economy is growing and what that growth rests on.
