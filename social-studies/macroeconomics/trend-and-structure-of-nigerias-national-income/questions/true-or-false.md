# Trend and structure of Nigeria's national income — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Nigeria's national income has never changed.
**Answer: False** — Nigeria's national income has moved over time with oil, agriculture and the wider economy.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The structure of national income shows nothing about sectors.
**Answer: False** — The structure of national income shows which sectors the income comes from and how that make-up has changed.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Trend and structure together hide whether the economy grows.
**Answer: False** — Trend and structure together show whether the economy is growing and what that growth rests on.
