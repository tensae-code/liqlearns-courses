# Economic lessons from the Asian Tigers and Japan — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How did the Asian Tigers grow rich?
**Answer:** The Asian Tigers grew rich fast through exports, industry, education and disciplined policy.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What was the Japanese Miracle?
**Answer:** Japan's miracle rebuilt a ruined economy into an industrial giant within a generation.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Why does Nigeria teach the Asian Tigers and Japan?
**Answer:** Nigeria teaches the Asian Tigers and Japan explicitly as lessons — what they did is held up for Nigeria to learn from.
