# Economic lessons from the Asian Tigers and Japan — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Asian Tigers grew rich by avoiding industry and exports.
**Answer: False** — The Asian Tigers grew rich fast through exports, industry, education and disciplined policy.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Japan's economy stayed ruined after the war.
**Answer: False** — Japan's miracle rebuilt a ruined economy into an industrial giant within a generation.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Nigeria's book says there is nothing to learn from the Tigers or Japan.
**Answer: False** — Nigeria teaches the Asian Tigers and Japan explicitly as lessons — what they did is held up for Nigeria to learn from.
