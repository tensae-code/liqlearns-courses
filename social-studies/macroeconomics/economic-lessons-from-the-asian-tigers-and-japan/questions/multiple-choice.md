# Economic lessons from the Asian Tigers and Japan — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** About the asian tigers — the lesson teaches that —
a) the Asian Tigers grew rich fast through exports, industry, education and disciplined policy.  b) japan's miracle rebuilt a ruined economy into an industrial giant within a generation.  c) nigeria teaches the Asian Tigers and Japan explicitly as lessons — what they did is held up for Nigeria to learn from.
