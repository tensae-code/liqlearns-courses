# Costa Rica's structural adjustment programmes (PAE) — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** From 1982 Costa Rica became a neoliberal-adjustment state — PAE II (Arias, 1986–1990) lists opening to the world market, tariff cuts to boost exports, shrinking the state and selling state institutions, financial reform…
**Answer: True** — this is what Costa Rica's book teaches in this part.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Sub lesson 2 is mainly about programas de ajuste estructural.
**Answer: False** — sub lesson 2 is about planes o programas de ajuste estructural (paes).

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** PAE I (Monge, 1982–86; US$80 million from the World Bank) in full — mini-devaluations of the colón, a farm-promotion law, import taxes cut or eliminated for non-traditionals, CENPRO created, CAT tax-credit certificates…
**Answer: True** — this is what Costa Rica's book teaches in this part.

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Sub lesson 4 is mainly about programas de ajuste estructural.
**Answer: False** — sub lesson 4 is about los pae ii y iii.
