# Costa Rica's structural adjustment programmes (PAE) — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** From 1982 Costa Rica became a neoliberal-adjustment state — PAE II (Arias, 1986–1990) lists opening to the world market, tariff cuts to boost exports, shrinking the state and selling state institutions, _____ reform opening private…
**Answer: financial**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The PAEs defined as sets of measures imposed in public administration to remove the obstacles to free trade and production — often the state itself and the unions — pushed by the World Bank and IMF, which lent money only if the measures…
**Answer: implemented**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** PAE I (Monge, 1982–86; US$80 million from the World Bank) in full — mini-devaluations of the colón, a farm-promotion law, import taxes cut or eliminated for non-traditionals, CENPRO created, CAT tax-credit certificates for exporters,…
**Answer: education**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** PAE II (Arias, signed 1987; US$200 million from the World Bank and Japan) — strengthen private banking, fix basic-grain prices, sell CODESA firms, restrict state-bank credit — and PAE III (Figueres Olsen, 1994; US$250 million from the…
**Answer: privatisation**
