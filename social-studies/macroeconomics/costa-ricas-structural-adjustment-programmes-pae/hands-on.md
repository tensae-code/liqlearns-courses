# Hands-on — Costa Rica's structural adjustment programmes (PAE)

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Lay it out on the table
On paper, draw the lesson in 4 boxes — one for each sub lesson: Programas de Ajuste Estructural; Planes o Programas de Ajuste Estructural (PAES); El PAE I y sus medidas; Los PAE II y III. Write one line of the lesson in each box, from memory, then open lesson/lesson.md, compare, and fix anything missing.

## Say it with things at home
Put 4 stones on the table — one for each sub lesson — and say what each stone stands for, in order, without looking. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 4 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes.
