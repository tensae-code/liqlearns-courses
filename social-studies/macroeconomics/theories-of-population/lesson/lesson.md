# Theories of population

## Do it first

## Sub lesson 1 — Population theories

Theories of population explain how population grows and what that growth does to living standards.

## Sub lesson 2 — Optimum population

A population can be under, over or at the optimum — the size that gives the highest living standard with the resources available.

## Sub lesson 3 — The theories against Nigeria's own population

Nigeria teaches its population theories against its own fast-growing population and what that growth means for the country.
