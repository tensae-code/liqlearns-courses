# Theories of population — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What do theories of population explain?
**Answer:** Theories of population explain how population grows and what that growth does to living standards.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is the optimum population?
**Answer:** A population can be under, over or at the optimum — the size that gives the highest living standard with the resources available.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** How does Nigeria teach population theories?
**Answer:** Nigeria teaches its population theories against its own fast-growing population and what that growth means for the country.
