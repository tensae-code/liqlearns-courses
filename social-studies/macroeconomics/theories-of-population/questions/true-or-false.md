# Theories of population — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Population theories say growth never affects living standards.
**Answer: False** — Theories of population explain how population grows and what that growth does to living standards.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The optimum population is always the largest possible population.
**Answer: False** — A population can be under, over or at the optimum — the size that gives the highest living standard with the resources available.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Nigeria's books never apply population theory to Nigeria itself.
**Answer: False** — Nigeria teaches its population theories against its own fast-growing population and what that growth means for the country.
