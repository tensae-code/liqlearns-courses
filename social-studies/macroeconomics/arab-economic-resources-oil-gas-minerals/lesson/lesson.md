# Arab economic resources — oil, gas and minerals

## Do it first

Before you read on, write what you already think this lesson's title means — arab economic resources — oil, gas and minerals — in one sentence of your own. Keep it; you will check it against the lesson at the end.

## Sub lesson 1 — Arab oil and gas — and their uses in daily life

The lesson teaches **Arab oil and gas** — the Arab homeland's great energy wealth — and **their uses in daily life**, the ways oil and gas reach everyday living.

## Sub lesson 2 — Mineral wealth

Beside energy stands **mineral wealth**: the minerals of the Arab homeland as a second pillar of its economic resources.

## Sub lesson 3 — Investing national wealth well

Resources alone are not the end of the lesson: it teaches **investing national wealth well** — putting the homeland's oil, gas and minerals to good use.
