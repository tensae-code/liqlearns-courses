# Video script — External and internal public debt in Costa Rica

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The book defines both debts — external debt as resources raised on capital markets abroad, usually in foreign currency, draining foreign exchange and interest until public investment had to be cut from the 1980s;.

Internal debt as the state's domestic credits.

The unit's own definitions — internal debt as the state's obligations from selling titles inside the country, to be repaid with interest, and external debt as the money the state owes to other countries.
