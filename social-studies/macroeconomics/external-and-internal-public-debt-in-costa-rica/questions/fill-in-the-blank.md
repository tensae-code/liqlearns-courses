# External and internal public debt in Costa Rica — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The book defines both debts — external debt as resources raised on capital markets abroad, usually in _____ currency, draining foreign exchange and interest until public investment had to be cut from the 1980s;.
**Answer: foreign**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Internal debt as the state's _____ credits.
**Answer: domestic**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The unit's own definitions — internal debt as the state's obligations from selling titles inside the _____, to be repaid with interest, and external debt as the money the state owes to other countries.
**Answer: country**
