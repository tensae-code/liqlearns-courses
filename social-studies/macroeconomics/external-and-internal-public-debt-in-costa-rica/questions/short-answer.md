# External and internal public debt in Costa Rica — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the lesson teach about deuda externa y deuda interna?
**Answer:** The book defines both debts — external debt as resources raised on capital markets abroad, usually in foreign currency, draining foreign exchange and interest until public investment had to be cut from the 1980s;.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the lesson teach about deuda externa y deuda interna — more?
**Answer:** Internal debt as the state's domestic credits.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the lesson teach about conceptos básicos: deuda interna y deuda externa?
**Answer:** The unit's own definitions — internal debt as the state's obligations from selling titles inside the country, to be repaid with interest, and external debt as the money the state owes to other countries.
