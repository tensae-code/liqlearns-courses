# The 1914 crisis of the agro-export model — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** About factores externos que llevaron a la crisis del modelo agroexportador — the lesson teaches that —
a) The First World War broke the model.
b) European buyers stopped consuming coffee, the price fell over 50%, the colón devalued fast.
c) Banks were impoverished, the fiscal deficit grew, unemployment and poverty rose, and the state lost the 78% of its revenue that came from import and…
