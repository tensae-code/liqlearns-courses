# The 1914 crisis of the agro-export model — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the lesson teach about factores externos que llevaron a la crisis del modelo agroexportador?
**Answer:** The First World War broke the model.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the lesson teach about factores externos que llevaron a la crisis del modelo agroexportador — more?
**Answer:** European buyers stopped consuming coffee, the price fell over 50%, the colón devalued fast.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the lesson teach about factores externos que llevaron a la crisis del modelo agroexportador — more — more?
**Answer:** Banks were impoverished, the fiscal deficit grew, unemployment and poverty rose, and the state lost the 78% of its revenue that came from import and export taxes.
