# Video script — The 1914 crisis of the agro-export model

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The First World War broke the model.

European buyers stopped consuming coffee, the price fell over 50%, the colón devalued fast.

Banks were impoverished, the fiscal deficit grew, unemployment and poverty rose, and the state lost the 78% of its revenue that came from import and export taxes.
