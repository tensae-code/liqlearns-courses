# Municipal finance — where a town's money comes from and goes — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the five revenue sources the book lists.
**Answer:** State money, municipal fees/taxes, municipal enterprises, donations, and renting municipal property.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why does the book call municipal spending 'public finance with a face'?
**Answer:** Because its results — the road, the square, the school roof — are visible in daily life.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What is the chapter's place in learning public finance?
**Answer:** It is the first budget: revenue and spending sides named and matched, at the scale of the town.
