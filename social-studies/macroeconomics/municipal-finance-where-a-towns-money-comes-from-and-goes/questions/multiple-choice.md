# Municipal finance — where a town's money comes from and goes — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Municipal revenue includes —
a) only state grants
b) state money, fees and taxes, enterprises, donations and rents
c) only donations
**Answer: b) state money, fees and taxes, enterprises, donations and rents**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The book's examples of municipal spending are —
a) roads and squares, events, school buildings
b) armies and navies
c) imports
**Answer: a) roads and squares, events, school buildings**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The municipality chapter teaches public finance at —
a) national scale
b) town scale
c) world scale
**Answer: b) town scale**
