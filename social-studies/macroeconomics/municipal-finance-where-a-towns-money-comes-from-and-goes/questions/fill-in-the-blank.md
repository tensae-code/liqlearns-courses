# Municipal finance — where a town's money comes from and goes — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A municipality is funded from several _____ at once.
**Answer: streams / sources**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Roads, squares, events and school _____ are where the money goes.
**Answer: buildings**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Money in from named sources, money out on shared purposes: the town is the first _____ lesson.
**Answer: budget**
