# Video script — Municipal finance — where a town's money comes from and goes

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Greece's Grade 6 book teaches public finance at the scale a child can walk around: the municipality. Its revenue comes from state money, municipal fees and taxes, municipal enterprises, donations, and renting out municipal property.

Against the revenue the book sets the spending a child can see: roads and squares, events, and school buildings — works and services shared by everyone in the town.

The chapter is Greece's first budget lesson: money in from named sources, money out on named shared purposes, and the two sides made to meet — the whole logic of public finance, at town scale.
