# Hands-on — Municipal finance — where a town's money comes from and goes

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with an example
Find or invent one real example of this lesson's subject — from your home, your street or your town. Say aloud which sub lesson it belongs to and why, using the lesson's own words for it.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's picture from memory — a town hall with arrows of money flowing in from five named sources and out to a road, a square and a school — and label its parts with the lesson's key words. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare, and fix every label you placed wrong.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
