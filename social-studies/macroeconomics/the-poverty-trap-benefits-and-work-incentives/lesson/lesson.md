# The poverty trap — benefits and work incentives

## Sub lesson 1 — Help that phases out

The safety net helps with **food, shelter, healthcare and income** — but the help **phases out (falls) as earnings rise**.

## Sub lesson 2 — The trap: work stops paying

If benefits fall almost as fast as earnings rise, **extra work barely raises total income** — the **incentive to work is cut**, and the person is caught in the **poverty trap**.

## Sub lesson 3 — Drawn as a budget constraint

The trap is **drawn as a budget constraint** — its flat stretch shows earnings rising while **total income (earnings plus benefits) hardly moves**.
