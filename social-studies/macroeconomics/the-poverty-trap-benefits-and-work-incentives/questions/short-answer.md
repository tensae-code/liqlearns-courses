# The poverty trap — benefits and work incentives — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name helps named in this lesson.
**Answer:** Food, shelter, healthcare and income help (any two).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why does the trap cut the incentive to work?
**Answer:** Because benefits fall almost as fast as earnings rise, so extra work barely raises total income.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the flat stretch show?
**Answer:** Earnings rising while total income — earnings plus remaining benefits — hardly moves.
