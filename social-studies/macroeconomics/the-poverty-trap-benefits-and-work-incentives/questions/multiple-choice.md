# The poverty trap — benefits and work incentives — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** As earnings rise, means-tested benefits —
a) phase out (fall)  b) rise equally  c) never change
**Answer: a) phase out (fall)**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The poverty trap is when —
a) benefits falling with earnings leave extra work barely paying  b) wages are very high  c) there are no benefits at all
**Answer: a) benefits falling with earnings leave extra work barely paying**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** On the budget-constraint drawing, the trap appears as —
a) a flat stretch where total income hardly rises with earnings  b) a vertical line  c) no line at all
**Answer: a) a flat stretch where total income hardly rises with earnings**
