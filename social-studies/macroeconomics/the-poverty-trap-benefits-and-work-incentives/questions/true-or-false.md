# The poverty trap — benefits and work incentives — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Benefits stay at their full level however much a person earns.
**Answer: False — the help phases out as earnings rise.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Phasing out benefits never affects the incentive to work.
**Answer: False — if benefits fall as fast as earnings rise, work barely pays, cutting the incentive.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The budget constraint drawing hides the trap.
**Answer: False — the drawing shows it as the flat stretch.**
