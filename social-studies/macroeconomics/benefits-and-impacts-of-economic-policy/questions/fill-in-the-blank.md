# Benefits and impacts of economic policy — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Good economic policy delivers stability, lower unemployment and _____.
**Answer: controlled inflation**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Every economic policy has impacts beyond its target, so the book weighs a policy's benefits against its effects on the _____.
**Answer: whole economy**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A policy is judged by whether its benefits — stability, jobs and controlled prices — outweigh its _____.
**Answer: unwanted impacts**
