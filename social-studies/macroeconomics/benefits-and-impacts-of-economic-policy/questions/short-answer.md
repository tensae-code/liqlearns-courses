# Benefits and impacts of economic policy — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the book teach about what good policy delivers?
**Answer:** Good economic policy delivers stability, lower unemployment and controlled inflation.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the book teach about the impacts, good and bad?
**Answer:** Every economic policy has impacts beyond its target, so the book weighs a policy's benefits against its effects on the whole economy.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the book teach about judging a policy?
**Answer:** A policy is judged by whether its benefits — stability, jobs and controlled prices — outweigh its unwanted impacts.
