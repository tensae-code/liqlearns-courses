# Benefits and impacts of economic policy — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Good economic policy delivers stability, lower unemployment and controlled inflation.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Every economic policy has impacts beyond its target, so the book weighs a policy's benefits against its effects on the whole economy.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A policy is judged by whether its benefits — stability, jobs and controlled prices — outweigh its something else entirely.
**Answer: False** — A policy is judged by whether its benefits — stability, jobs and controlled prices — outweigh its unwanted impacts.
