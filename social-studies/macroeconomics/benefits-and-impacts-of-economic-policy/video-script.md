# Video script — Benefits and impacts of economic policy

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Good economic policy delivers stability, lower unemployment and controlled inflation.

Every economic policy has impacts beyond its target, so the book weighs a policy's benefits against its effects on the whole economy.

A policy is judged by whether its benefits — stability, jobs and controlled prices — outweigh its unwanted impacts.
