The slave-trade triangle and the Industrial Revolution — as Bolivia's book teaches it, says the narrator: the slave trade in America, the supply of cotton to Europe and the textile industry formed one triangle; enslaved workers on American plantations grew the cotton that fed Europe's textile mills; industrial demand for cotton deepened the slave economies of the Americas.
