# The slave-trade triangle and the Industrial Revolution — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The slave trade had no connection with European industry.
**Answer: False** — the book draws it as one side of the triangle that powered the Industrial Revolution.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** European mills grew their own cotton in England.
**Answer: False** — the cotton came from plantations in America worked by enslaved people.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The Industrial Revolution quickly ended slavery in the Americas.
**Answer: False** — the book teaches that industrial demand for cotton deepened the slave economies.
