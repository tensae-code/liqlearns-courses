# The slave-trade triangle and the Industrial Revolution — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The triangle linked the slave trade, the supply of _____ to Europe, and the textile industry.
**Answer: cotton**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Europe's textile mills ran on cotton grown on American _____ by enslaved workers.
**Answer: plantations**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Industrial demand for cotton _____ the slave economies.
**Answer: deepened**
