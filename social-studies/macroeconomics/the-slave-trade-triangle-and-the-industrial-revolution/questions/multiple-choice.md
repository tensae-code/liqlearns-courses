# The slave-trade triangle and the Industrial Revolution — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** About three sides of one triangle — the lesson teaches that —
a) the slave trade in America, the supply of cotton to Europe and the textile industry formed one triangle  b) enslaved workers on American plantations grew the cotton that fed Europe's textile mills  c) industrial demand for cotton deepened the slave economies of the Americas
**Answer: a) the slave trade in America, the supply of cotton to Europe and the textile industry formed one triangle**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** About cotton — grown by enslaved hands, spun in mills — the lesson teaches that —
a) the slave trade in America, the supply of cotton to Europe and the textile industry formed one triangle  b) enslaved workers on American plantations grew the cotton that fed Europe's textile mills  c) industrial demand for cotton deepened the slave economies of the Americas
**Answer: b) enslaved workers on American plantations grew the cotton that fed Europe's textile mills**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** About industrial demand deepens the slave economies — the lesson teaches that —
a) the slave trade in America, the supply of cotton to Europe and the textile industry formed one triangle  b) enslaved workers on American plantations grew the cotton that fed Europe's textile mills  c) industrial demand for cotton deepened the slave economies of the Americas
**Answer: c) industrial demand for cotton deepened the slave economies of the Americas**
