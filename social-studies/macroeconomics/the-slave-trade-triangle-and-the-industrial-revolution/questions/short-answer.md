# The slave-trade triangle and the Industrial Revolution — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the lesson teach about three sides of one triangle?
**Answer:** The slave trade in America, the supply of cotton to Europe and the textile industry formed one triangle.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the lesson teach about cotton — grown by enslaved hands, spun in mills?
**Answer:** Enslaved workers on American plantations grew the cotton that fed Europe's textile mills.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the lesson teach about industrial demand deepens the slave economies?
**Answer:** Industrial demand for cotton deepened the slave economies of the Americas.
