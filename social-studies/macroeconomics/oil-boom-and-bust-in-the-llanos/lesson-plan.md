# Lesson plan — Oil boom and bust in the Llanos

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Macroeconomics · **Lesson:** Oil boom and bust in the Llanos
**Place in the curriculum:** Macroeconomics (CURRICULUM.md) — built in the Economics expansion (Colombia, 2026-11-16).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Discovering oil changed llanero life — while wells are exploited, work and income rise;
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Colombia's own book teaching — the learner meets the thing as Colombia's national course teaches it.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of ..." in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.
