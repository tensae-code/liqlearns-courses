# Credit and investment policy — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the book teach about credit policy — a named branch of economic policy?
**Answer:** Credit policy is one named branch of the state's economic policy in Nicaragua's Grade 10 book, standing alongside the fiscal, monetary and exchange branches.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the book teach about investment policy — the state's investment branch?
**Answer:** Investment policy is another named branch of the state's economic policy, named in the book together with credit policy.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the book teach about the branches work as one economic policy?
**Answer:** The book presents credit policy and investment policy as parts of one economic policy, next to the fiscal policy, the monetary policy and the exchange policy the state already uses.
