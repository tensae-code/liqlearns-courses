# Credit and investment policy — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Credit policy is one named branch of the state's economic policy in Nicaragua's Grade 10 book, standing alongside the fiscal, monetary and exchange branches.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Investment policy is another named branch of the state's economic policy, named in the book together with credit policy.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The book presents credit policy and investment policy as parts of something else entirely, next to the fiscal policy, the monetary policy and the exchange policy the state already uses.
**Answer: False** — The book presents credit policy and investment policy as parts of one economic policy, next to the fiscal policy, the monetary policy and the exchange policy the state already uses.
