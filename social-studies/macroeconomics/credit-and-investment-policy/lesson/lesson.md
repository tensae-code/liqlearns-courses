## Sub lesson 1 — Credit policy — a named branch of economic policy

Credit policy is one named branch of the state's economic policy in Nicaragua's Grade 10 book, standing alongside the fiscal, monetary and exchange branches.

## Sub lesson 2 — Investment policy — the state's investment branch

Investment policy is another named branch of the state's economic policy, named in the book together with credit policy.

## Sub lesson 3 — The branches work as one economic policy

The book presents credit policy and investment policy as parts of one economic policy, next to the fiscal policy, the monetary policy and the exchange policy the state already uses.
