# AD/AS equilibrium and shocks — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Where AD meets SRAS"?
a) Equilibrium sits where AD meets SRAS, fixing the price level and real output  b) A demand shock shifts AD and moves output and prices in the same direction  c) A supply shock shifting SRAS left raises prices while output falls — stagflation
**Answer: a) Equilibrium sits where AD meets SRAS, fixing the price level and real output**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Demand shocks"?
a) A demand shock shifts AD and moves output and prices in the same direction  b) A supply shock shifting SRAS left raises prices while output falls — stagflation  c) Equilibrium sits where AD meets SRAS, fixing the price level and real output
**Answer: a) A demand shock shifts AD and moves output and prices in the same direction**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Supply shocks and stagflation"?
a) A supply shock shifting SRAS left raises prices while output falls — stagflation  b) Equilibrium sits where AD meets SRAS, fixing the price level and real output  c) A demand shock shifts AD and moves output and prices in the same direction
**Answer: a) A supply shock shifting SRAS left raises prices while output falls — stagflation**
