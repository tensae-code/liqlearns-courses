# AD/AS equilibrium and shocks — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Where AD meets SRAS" in your own words.
**Answer:** Equilibrium sits where AD meets SRAS, fixing the price level and real output.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Demand shocks" in your own words.
**Answer:** A demand shock shifts AD and moves output and prices in the same direction.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Supply shocks and stagflation" in your own words.
**Answer:** A supply shock shifting SRAS left raises prices while output falls — stagflation.
