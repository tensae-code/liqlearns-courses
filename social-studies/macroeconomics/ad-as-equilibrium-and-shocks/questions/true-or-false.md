# AD/AS equilibrium and shocks — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Equilibrium sits where AD meets SRAS, fixing the price level and real output.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A demand shock shifts AD and moves output and prices in the same direction.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A supply shock shifting SRAS left raises prices while output falls — stagflation.
**Answer: True**
