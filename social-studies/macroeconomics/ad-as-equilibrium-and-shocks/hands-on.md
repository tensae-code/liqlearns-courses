# Hands-on — AD/AS equilibrium and shocks

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Animate it on paper
Draw AD and SRAS crossing, then draw the oil shock in a second panel: SRAS left, new crossing marked, arrows showing price up and output down. Label it stagflation.

## Name the shock
For six events (a tax cut, a drought, a confidence crash, an oil spike, a spending boom, a wage surge), name the curve that shifts, its direction, and the result for price and output.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
