# Video script — AD/AS equilibrium and shocks

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Short-run equilibrium sits where aggregate demand meets short-run aggregate supply: that crossing fixes both the price level and real output. The long-run resting point adds the vertical LRAS at potential output.

A demand shock — a spending surge or a collapse — shifts AD. Output and prices move together: up in a boom, down in a slump. Returning demand to potential is the demand manager's whole job in the Keynesian telling.

A supply shock shifts SRAS. An oil-price spike shifts it left: prices rise while output falls — stagflation, the combination demand policy handles worst, since fixing one half worsens the other. Over time wages and expectations adjust, SRAS returns, and in the long run only the price level stays changed.
