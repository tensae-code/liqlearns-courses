# Fixed exchange rates — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "The peg — and defending it" in your own words.
**Answer:** Under fixed rates the central bank defends the set rate by buying and selling foreign currency from reserves.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "The system's benefits" in your own words.
**Answer:** Fixed rates bring stability, planning certainty for traders, and help in holding inflation low.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Devaluation and revaluation" in your own words.
**Answer:** Devaluation officially raises foreign currency's price, making imports dearer and exports cheaper abroad.
