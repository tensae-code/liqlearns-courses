# Fixed exchange rates — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "The peg — and defending it"?
a) Under fixed rates the central bank defends the set rate by buying and selling foreign currency from reserves.  b) Fixed rates bring stability, planning certainty for traders, and help in holding inflation low.  c) Devaluation officially raises foreign currency's price, making imports dearer and exports cheaper abroad.
**Answer: a) Under fixed rates the central bank defends the set rate by buying and selling foreign currency from reserves.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "The system's benefits"?
a) Devaluation officially raises foreign currency's price, making imports dearer and exports cheaper abroad.  b) Under fixed rates the central bank defends the set rate by buying and selling foreign currency from reserves.  c) Fixed rates bring stability, planning certainty for traders, and help in holding inflation low.
**Answer: c) Fixed rates bring stability, planning certainty for traders, and help in holding inflation low.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Devaluation and revaluation"?
a) Fixed rates bring stability, planning certainty for traders, and help in holding inflation low.  b) Devaluation officially raises foreign currency's price, making imports dearer and exports cheaper abroad.  c) Under fixed rates the central bank defends the set rate by buying and selling foreign currency from reserves.
**Answer: b) Devaluation officially raises foreign currency's price, making imports dearer and exports cheaper abroad.**
