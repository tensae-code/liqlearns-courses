# Fixed exchange rates — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Under a fixed system the rate is never defended.
**Answer: False — Defence is the system's daily work — the bank trades its reserves to hold the promised rate.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Fixed rates make import prices unpredictable.
**Answer: False — Predictability is the point: a held rate holds import prices steady, helping keep inflation low.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Devaluation makes exports dearer abroad.
**Answer: False — Devaluation makes exports CHEAPER abroad (and imports dearer at home) — that is its usual purpose.**
