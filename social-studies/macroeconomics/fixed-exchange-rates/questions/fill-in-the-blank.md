# Fixed exchange rates — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or figure from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A fixed rate is defended from the central bank's ____.
**Answer: reserves**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Fixed rates give exporters and importers ____ about the rate.
**Answer: certainty**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Officially raising foreign currency's price is a ____.
**Answer: devaluation**
