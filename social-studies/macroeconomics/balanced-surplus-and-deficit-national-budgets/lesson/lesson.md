## Sub lesson 1 — Balanced and surplus national budgets

A **balanced budget** has **revenue equal to expenditure**. A **surplus budget** has **revenue more than expenditure**.

## Sub lesson 2 — The deficit national budget

A **deficit budget** has **expenditure more than revenue** — the government spends more than it takes in.

## Sub lesson 3 — Why the national budget matters

The national budget matters because it helps government **use public money well**, **allocate by people's needs** and promotes **transparency and accountability**.
