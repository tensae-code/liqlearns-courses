# Video script — Balanced, surplus and deficit national budgets

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Balanced, surplus and deficit national budgets — as Rwanda's book teaches it, says the narrator: A balanced budget has revenue equal to expenditure. A surplus budget has revenue more than expenditure. A deficit budget has expenditure more than revenue — the government spends more than it takes in. The national budget matters because it helps government use public money well, allocate by people's needs and promotes transparency and accountability.
