# Balanced, surplus and deficit national budgets — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A balanced budget has revenue equal to expenditure.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A deficit budget has expenditure more than revenue — the government spends more than it takes in.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The national budget matters because it helps government use public money well, allocate by people's needs and promotes transparency and accountability.
**Answer: True**
