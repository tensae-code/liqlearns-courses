# Balanced, surplus and deficit national budgets — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In your own words, what does sub lesson 1 teach about balanced and surplus national budgets?
**Answer:** A balanced budget has revenue equal to expenditure. A surplus budget has revenue more than expenditure.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In your own words, what does sub lesson 2 teach about the deficit national budget?
**Answer:** A deficit budget has expenditure more than revenue — the government spends more than it takes in.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In your own words, what does sub lesson 3 teach about why the national budget matters?
**Answer:** The national budget matters because it helps government use public money well, allocate by people's needs and promotes transparency and accountability.
