# What enabled globalisation

## Do it first

## Sub lesson 1 — Containers and cheaper air freight

**Container transport and cheaper air freight** made moving goods across the world fast and cheap — the transport enabler of globalisation.

## Sub lesson 2 — ICT spreads service production across countries

**Information and communication technology (ICT)** spreads **service production across countries** — work can be done wherever the skilled people are.

## Sub lesson 3 — A London magazine designed and printed in Delhi

The book's example: a **London magazine designed and printed in Delhi**, and **paid for by e-banking** — services, production and payment all crossing borders.
