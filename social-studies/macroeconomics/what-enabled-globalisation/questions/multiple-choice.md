# What enabled globalisation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Goods move cheaply worldwide thanks to —
a) containers and cheaper air freight  b) bullock carts  c) higher freight costs
**Answer: a) containers and cheaper air freight**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** ICT spreads which kind of production across countries?
a) service production  b) only farming  c) no production
**Answer: a) service production**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The London magazine in the example is designed and printed in —
a) Delhi  b) London  c) New York
**Answer: a) Delhi**
