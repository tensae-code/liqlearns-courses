# What enabled globalisation — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Transport got slower and dearer as globalisation grew.
**Answer: False — containers and cheaper air freight made it faster and cheaper.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Services must always be produced where they are used.
**Answer: False — ICT lets services be produced across countries.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The magazine example involves no technology.
**Answer: False — it runs on ICT and e-banking.**
