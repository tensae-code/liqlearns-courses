# What enabled globalisation — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the transport enablers.
**Answer:** Container transport and cheaper air freight.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does ICT do to service production?
**Answer:** It spreads it across countries.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Give the book's example of ICT-enabled globalisation.
**Answer:** A London magazine designed and printed in Delhi, paid by e-banking.
