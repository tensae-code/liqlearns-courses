# Video script — Tax accounting

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

All taxation runs on accounting: financial statements are what tax liabilities are calculated from. No records, no reliable liability — for the office or for the taxpayer.

For individuals the focus is income, qualifying deductions and investment gains or losses — and in Ethiopia most employees' income tax is simply paid over by their employers. Businesses must keep current financial records to determine what they owe.
