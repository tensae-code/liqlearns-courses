# Tax accounting — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Liabilities are calculated from statements"?
a) Tax liabilities are calculated from financial statements  b) Individuals focus on income, deductions and investment gains; businesses must keep current records
**Answer: a) Tax liabilities are calculated from financial statements**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Individuals and businesses"?
a) Individuals focus on income, deductions and investment gains; businesses must keep current records  b) Tax liabilities are calculated from financial statements
**Answer: a) Individuals focus on income, deductions and investment gains; businesses must keep current records**
