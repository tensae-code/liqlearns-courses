# Hands-on — Tax accounting

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Ledger to return
Open an imaginary ledger beside a tax return: draw three figures flowing from ledger to return (income, a deduction, a gain) and write the rule each figure obeys.

## The missing book
A shop kept no records for a year. Write what the tax office cannot know, what the shop cannot prove, and why both sides lose — in four lines.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
