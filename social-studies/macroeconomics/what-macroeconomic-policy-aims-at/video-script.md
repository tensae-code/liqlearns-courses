# Video script — What macroeconomic policy aims at

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Five general objectives head the list with economic growth — a consistent increase in national income — and full employment, the maximum possible use of factors of production. The guide's answers phrase the list with one addition (teacher guide — evidence): 'the maximum feasible output' heads the list, before growth and full employment.

A stable balance of payments in equilibrium with the rest of the world, and price stability — avoiding long inflation or deflation and sustaining the value of money, especially important for savers. Stability goals guard the measuring sticks themselves.

Fifth: fair distribution of income and wealth — the sharing goal standing among the production goals. Policies differ by country and by conditions: the five aims are general, the mix chosen to chase them is not.
