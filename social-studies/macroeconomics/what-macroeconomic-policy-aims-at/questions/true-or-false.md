# What macroeconomic policy aims at — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Economic growth is a consistent increase in national income, and full employment is the maximum possible use of factors of production.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Economic growth is a consistent increase in national income, and full employment is the maximum possible use of factors of production.
**Answer: False** — that statement belongs to sub lesson 1 (Growth and full employment); sub lesson 2 says: Price stability means avoiding long inflation or deflation and sustaining the value of money, especially important for savers.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The fifth goal is fair distribution of income and wealth, and macroeconomic policies differ by country and by conditions.
**Answer: True**
