# What macroeconomic policy aims at — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Growth and full employment"?
a) a consistent increase in national income; the maximum possible use of factors  b) balance of payments in equilibrium; avoiding long inflation or deflation  c) fair distribution of income and wealth; policies differ by country and conditions
**Answer: a) a consistent increase in national income; the maximum possible use of factors**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Stability — prices and payments"?
a) a consistent increase in national income; the maximum possible use of factors  b) fair distribution of income and wealth; policies differ by country and conditions  c) balance of payments in equilibrium; avoiding long inflation or deflation
**Answer: c) balance of payments in equilibrium; avoiding long inflation or deflation**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Fair distribution — and national differences"?
a) balance of payments in equilibrium; avoiding long inflation or deflation  b) fair distribution of income and wealth; policies differ by country and conditions  c) a consistent increase in national income; the maximum possible use of factors
**Answer: b) fair distribution of income and wealth; policies differ by country and conditions**
