# What macroeconomic policy aims at — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Growth and full employment" in your own words.
**Answer:** Economic growth is a consistent increase in national income, and full employment is the maximum possible use of factors of production.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Stability — prices and payments" in your own words.
**Answer:** Price stability means avoiding long inflation or deflation and sustaining the value of money, especially important for savers.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Fair distribution — and national differences" in your own words.
**Answer:** The fifth goal is fair distribution of income and wealth, and macroeconomic policies differ by country and by conditions.
