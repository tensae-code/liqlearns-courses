# What macroeconomic policy aims at — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** _____ is the maximum possible use of factors of production.
**Answer: full employment**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Price stability sustains the value of money, especially important for _____.
**Answer: savers**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The fifth goal is _____ of income and wealth.
**Answer: fair distribution**
