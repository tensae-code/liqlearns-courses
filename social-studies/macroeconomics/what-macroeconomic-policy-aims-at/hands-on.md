# Hands-on — What macroeconomic policy aims at

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Target boards: five learners hold goal cards while 'event' cards are read (prices jump, jobs open, exports fall). Holders say whether their target is hit or hurt by each event.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — five target boards — growth, jobs, stable prices, balanced trade, fair shares — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
