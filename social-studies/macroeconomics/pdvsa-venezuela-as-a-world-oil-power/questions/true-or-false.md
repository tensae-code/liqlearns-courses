# PDVSA — Venezuela as a world oil power — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Venezuela is one of the main oil exporters worldwide.
**Answer: True** — and the world's greatest holder of oil reserves.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Oil is used only as fuel, in the chapter's account.
**Answer: False** — most everyday products come from oil — plastics, nylon, paints and more.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** PDVSA is presented as an industry committed to the people.
**Answer: True** — that is the chapter's presentation of the state oil industry.
