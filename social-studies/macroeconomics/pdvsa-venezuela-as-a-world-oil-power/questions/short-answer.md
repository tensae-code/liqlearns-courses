# PDVSA — Venezuela as a world oil power — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** State Venezuela's double standing in the oil chapter.
**Answer:** The world's greatest holder of "black gold", with reserves for about 300 years, and one of the main oil exporters worldwide.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name three everyday products the chapter traces to oil.
**Answer:** Any three of: plastics (from polyethylene), fuels, nylon, paints.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What is PDVSA, and how does the chapter present it?
**Answer:** Petróleos de Venezuela S.A., the state oil industry — presented as an industry committed to the people.
