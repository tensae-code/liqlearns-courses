# PDVSA — Venezuela as a world oil power — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Venezuela's oil reserves are estimated to last about —
a) 300 years  b) 30 years  c) 3 years
**Answer: a) 300 years**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The chapter's everyday products from oil include —
a) plastics from polyethylene, fuels, nylon and paints  b) bread and cheese  c) cotton cloth only
**Answer: a) plastics from polyethylene, fuels, nylon and paints**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** PDVSA is —
a) the state oil industry, presented as an industry committed to the people  b) a foreign oil company  c) a private cooperative
**Answer: a) the state oil industry, presented as an industry committed to the people**
