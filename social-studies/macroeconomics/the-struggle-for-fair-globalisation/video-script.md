# Video script — The struggle for fair globalisation

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

The struggle for fair globalisation — as India's book teaches it, says the narrator: competition pushes employers to hire workers flexibly — temporary, long hours, low wages, no security — so globalisation must be made fair through government policy, enforced labour laws and people's organisations.
