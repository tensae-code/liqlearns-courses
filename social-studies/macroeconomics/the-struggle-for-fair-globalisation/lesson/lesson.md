# The struggle for fair globalisation

## Do it first

Before you read on, write what you already think this lesson's title means — the struggle for fair globalisation — in one sentence of your own. Keep it; you will check it against the lesson at the end.

## Sub lesson 1 — Flexible hiring — temporary, long hours, low pay, no security

Competition pushes employers to hire **flexibly: temporary work, long hours, low wages and no security** for the worker.

## Sub lesson 2 — Fair globalisation needs government policy

Making globalisation fair starts with **government policy** that protects those who lose from open competition.

## Sub lesson 3 — Enforced labour laws and people's organisations

It also needs **enforced labour laws** and **people's organisations** that press the workers' case — laws on paper are not enough.

## Say it to your companion
