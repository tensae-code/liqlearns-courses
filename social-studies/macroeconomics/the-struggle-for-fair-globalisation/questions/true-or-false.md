# The struggle for fair globalisation — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Flexible hiring gives workers permanent security.
**Answer: False — it gives temporary work with no security.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Governments have no part in making globalisation fair.
**Answer: False — government policy is named first.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Labour laws work even when nobody enforces them.
**Answer: False — they must be enforced.**
