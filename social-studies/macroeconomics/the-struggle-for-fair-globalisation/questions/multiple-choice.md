# The struggle for fair globalisation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Flexible hiring means —
a) temporary work, long hours, low wages, no security  b) permanent jobs with benefits  c) shorter hours
**Answer: a) temporary work, long hours, low wages, no security**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Fair globalisation needs —
a) government policy  b) no rules at all  c) only foreign pressure
**Answer: a) government policy**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Besides policy, fair globalisation needs —
a) enforced labour laws and people's organisations  b) lower standards  c) silence from workers
**Answer: a) enforced labour laws and people's organisations**
