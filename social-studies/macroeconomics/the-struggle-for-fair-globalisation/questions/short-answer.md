# The struggle for fair globalisation — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name two marks of flexible hiring.
**Answer:** Any two of: temporary, long hours, low wages, no security.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is the first tool named for fair globalisation?
**Answer:** Government policy.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name the two supports beside government policy.
**Answer:** Enforced labour laws and people's organisations.
