# Colonial taxes alcabala quinto real and the estanco — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The colonial tax system named and distinguished — alcabala (sales tax), almojarifazgo (customs duty)
**Answer: True** — this is what Colombia's book teaches in this part.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Sub lesson 2 is mainly about the colonial tax system named and distinguished alcabala.
**Answer: False** — sub lesson 2 is about quinto real mining tax diezmo church tax on; the colonial tax system named and distinguished alcabala is sub lesson 1.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Armada de Barlovento (tax on basic goods) and the estanco (state monopoly over aguardiente, salt and other goods)
**Answer: True** — this is what Colombia's book teaches in this part.
