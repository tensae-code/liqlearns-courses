# Colonial taxes alcabala quinto real and the estanco — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the lesson teach about the colonial tax system named and distinguished alcabala?
**Answer:** The colonial tax system named and distinguished — alcabala (sales tax), almojarifazgo (customs duty)

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the lesson teach about quinto real mining tax diezmo church tax on?
**Answer:** Quinto real (mining tax), diezmo (church tax on farm products)

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the lesson teach about armada de barlovento tax on basic goods and?
**Answer:** Armada de Barlovento (tax on basic goods) and the estanco (state monopoly over aguardiente, salt and other goods)
