# Colonial taxes alcabala quinto real and the estanco — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The colonial tax system named and _____ — alcabala (sales tax), almojarifazgo (customs duty)
**Answer: distinguished**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Quinto real (mining tax), diezmo (church tax on farm _____

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Armada de _____ (tax on basic goods) and the estanco (state monopoly over aguardiente, salt and other goods)
