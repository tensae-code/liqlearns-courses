Colonial taxes alcabala quinto real and the estanco — as Colombia's book teaches it, says the narrator: The colonial tax system named and distinguished — alcabala (sales tax), almojarifazgo (customs duty) Quinto real (mining tax), diezmo (church tax on farm products) Armada de Barlovento (tax on basic goods) and the estanco (state monopoly over aguardiente, salt and other goods)
