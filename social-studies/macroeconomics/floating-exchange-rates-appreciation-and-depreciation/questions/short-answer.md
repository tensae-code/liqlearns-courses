# Floating exchange rates — appreciation and depreciation — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "The market sets the rate" in your own words.
**Answer:** Most real floats are managed floats; the NBE follows the birr under managed floating.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Depreciation — the birr weakens" in your own words.
**Answer:** If the dollar rises from 45 to 50 birr, the birr has depreciated.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Appreciation — the birr strengthens" in your own words.
**Answer:** A fall in the dollar's price from 45 to 30 birr is an appreciation of the birr.
