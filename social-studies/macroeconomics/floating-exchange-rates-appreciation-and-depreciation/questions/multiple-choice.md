# Floating exchange rates — appreciation and depreciation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "The market sets the rate"?
a) Most real floats are managed floats; the NBE follows the birr under managed floating.  b) If the dollar rises from 45 to 50 birr, the birr has depreciated.  c) A fall in the dollar's price from 45 to 30 birr is an appreciation of the birr.
**Answer: a) Most real floats are managed floats; the NBE follows the birr under managed floating.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Depreciation — the birr weakens"?
a) A fall in the dollar's price from 45 to 30 birr is an appreciation of the birr.  b) Most real floats are managed floats; the NBE follows the birr under managed floating.  c) If the dollar rises from 45 to 50 birr, the birr has depreciated.
**Answer: c) If the dollar rises from 45 to 50 birr, the birr has depreciated.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Appreciation — the birr strengthens"?
a) If the dollar rises from 45 to 50 birr, the birr has depreciated.  b) A fall in the dollar's price from 45 to 30 birr is an appreciation of the birr.  c) Most real floats are managed floats; the NBE follows the birr under managed floating.
**Answer: b) A fall in the dollar's price from 45 to 30 birr is an appreciation of the birr.**
