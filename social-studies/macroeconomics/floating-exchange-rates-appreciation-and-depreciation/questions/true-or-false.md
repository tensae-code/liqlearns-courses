# Floating exchange rates — appreciation and depreciation — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Ethiopia's birr floats with no central-bank involvement at all.
**Answer: False — The book says the NBE follows the rate in line with MANAGED floating — the bank still steers.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** If the dollar rises from 45 to 50 birr, the birr has appreciated.
**Answer: False — A dearer dollar means a weaker birr — that is depreciation. Appreciation is the fall in the dollar's price.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Appreciation is an official act by the central bank.
**Answer: False — Official acts are devaluation and revaluation. Appreciation is a market-driven strengthening.**
