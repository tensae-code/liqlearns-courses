# The food basket as a cost-of-living measure — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The food basket is —
a) a statistical indicator (INE) giving the monthly cost of a set of foods  b) a basket sold in markets  c) a family's weekly shopping list
**Answer: a) a statistical indicator (INE) giving the monthly cost of a set of foods**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The basket's foods must cover —
a) all the energy and nutrient requirements of a typical Venezuelan family  b) only the cheapest foods in the shop  c) luxury foods for celebrations
**Answer: a) all the energy and nutrient requirements of a typical Venezuelan family**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The basket is built taking into account —
a) consumption habits, national production availability and the lowest possible cost  b) only imported foods  c) whatever foods are fashionable
**Answer: a) consumption habits, national production availability and the lowest possible cost**
