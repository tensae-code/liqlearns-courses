# The food basket as a cost-of-living measure — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The food basket determines the monthly cost in _____ of a set of foods.
**Answer: bolívares**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The basket covers the needs of a typical Venezuelan _____.
**Answer: family**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** One consideration in building the basket is the lowest possible _____.
**Answer: cost**
