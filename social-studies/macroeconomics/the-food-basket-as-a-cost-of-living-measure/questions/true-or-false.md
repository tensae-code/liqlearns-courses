# The food basket as a cost-of-living measure — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The food basket is published as a statistical indicator by the INE.
**Answer: True** — the book names the INE as its source.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The food basket only needs to cover half a family's nutrient requirements.
**Answer: False** — it must cover all the energy and nutrient requirements.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** National production availability is one of the basket's building considerations.
**Answer: True** — alongside consumption habits and lowest possible cost.
