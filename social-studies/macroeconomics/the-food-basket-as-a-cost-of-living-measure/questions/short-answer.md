# The food basket as a cost-of-living measure — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the food basket measure, and who publishes it?
**Answer:** The monthly cost in bolívares of a set of foods covering a typical family's needs — an indicator of the INE.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What standard must the basket's set of foods meet?
**Answer:** It must cover all the energy and nutrient requirements of a typical Venezuelan family.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name the three considerations that build the food basket.
**Answer:** Consumption habits, national production availability, and the lowest possible cost.
