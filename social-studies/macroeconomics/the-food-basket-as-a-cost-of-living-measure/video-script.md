# Video script — The food basket as a cost-of-living measure

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The monthly cost in bolívares of a set of foods covering a typical family's needs — an indicator of the INE.

It must cover all the energy and nutrient requirements of a typical Venezuelan family.

Consumption habits, national production availability, and the lowest possible cost.
