# Trade, jobs, wages and working conditions — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Trade's job effects are —
a) jobs grow in export industries and shrink under import competition  b) no jobs ever change  c) all jobs grow equally
**Answer: a) jobs grow in export industries and shrink under import competition**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Trade influences —
a) wages, labour standards and working conditions  b) wages only  c) nothing at work
**Answer: a) wages, labour standards and working conditions**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The opportunity cost of protectionism is —
a) the jobs, prices and choices lost elsewhere by shielding  b) zero  c) paid by foreigners only
**Answer: a) the jobs, prices and choices lost elsewhere by shielding**
