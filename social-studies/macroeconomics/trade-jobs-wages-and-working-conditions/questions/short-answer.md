# Trade, jobs, wages and working conditions — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How does trade reshape the job market?
**Answer:** Jobs grow in export industries and shrink where imports compete.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name two things trade influences at work besides jobs.
**Answer:** Wages and working conditions (or labour standards).

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What is protectionism's opportunity cost?
**Answer:** The jobs, higher prices and lost choices elsewhere that shielding causes.
