# Trade, jobs, wages and working conditions — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Trade never costs any worker a job.
**Answer: False — jobs shrink where imports compete, even as export jobs grow.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Trade can only ever raise working conditions, never press them.
**Answer: False — the book weighs both pressure and gains.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Protectionism protects jobs at no cost anywhere else.
**Answer: False — its opportunity cost is what is lost elsewhere: jobs, prices and choices.**
