# Trade, jobs, wages and working conditions — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Jobs grow in export industries and shrink where _____ compete.
**Answer: imports**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Trade influences wages, labour standards and working _____.
**Answer: conditions**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Shielding jobs costs jobs and choices elsewhere — protectionism's _____ cost.
**Answer: opportunity**
