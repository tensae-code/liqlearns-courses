# Trade, jobs, wages and working conditions

## Sub lesson 1 — Trade and the job market

International trade **reshapes the job market** — jobs grow in **export industries** and shrink where **imports compete**, so workers must move between them.

## Sub lesson 2 — Wages, standards and working conditions

Trade also influences **wages, labour standards and working conditions** — competition can press them, while richer trade can raise them; the book weighs both.

## Sub lesson 3 — The opportunity cost of protectionism

**Protectionism has an opportunity cost** — shielding one set of jobs **costs jobs, prices and choices elsewhere**, which must be counted against it.
