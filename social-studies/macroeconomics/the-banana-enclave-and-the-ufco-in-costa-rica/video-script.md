# Video script — The banana enclave and the UFCO in Costa Rica

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The Atlantic railway concession handed 323,752 hectares and import-tax exemptions to what became the UFCO banana enclave.

Exhausted soils were abandoned, workers migrated massively, and key economic decisions were taken in the United States, endangering sovereignty.

Inside the United Fruit enclave, with its own rules, workers were paid not in money but in a "boleta" voucher exchangeable only in the company's own stores, so they could never get hold of money — near-slavery conditions.
