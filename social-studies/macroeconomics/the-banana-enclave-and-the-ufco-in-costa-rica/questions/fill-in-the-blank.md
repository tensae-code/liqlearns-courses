# The banana enclave and the UFCO in Costa Rica — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Atlantic railway concession handed 323,752 _____ and import-tax exemptions to what became the UFCO banana enclave.
**Answer: hectares**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Exhausted soils were abandoned, workers migrated _____, and key economic decisions were taken in the United States, endangering sovereignty.
**Answer: massively**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Inside the United Fruit enclave, with its own rules, workers were paid not in money but in a "boleta" voucher _____ only in the company's own stores, so they could never get hold of money — near-slavery conditions.
**Answer: exchangeable**
