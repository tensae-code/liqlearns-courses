# The banana enclave and the UFCO in Costa Rica — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** About la producción bananera en el caribe — the lesson teaches that —
a) The Atlantic railway concession handed 323,752 hectares and import-tax exemptions to what became the UFCO banana enclave.
b) Exhausted soils were abandoned, workers migrated massively, and key economic decisions were taken in the United States, endangering sovereignty.
