# The banana enclave and the UFCO in Costa Rica — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the lesson teach about la producción bananera en el caribe?
**Answer:** The Atlantic railway concession handed 323,752 hectares and import-tax exemptions to what became the UFCO banana enclave.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the lesson teach about la producción bananera en el caribe — more?
**Answer:** Exhausted soils were abandoned, workers migrated massively, and key economic decisions were taken in the United States, endangering sovereignty.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the lesson teach about los trabajadores de las zonas bananeras?
**Answer:** Inside the United Fruit enclave, with its own rules, workers were paid not in money but in a "boleta" voucher exchangeable only in the company's own stores, so they could never get hold of money — near-slavery conditions.
