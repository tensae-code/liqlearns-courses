# Hands-on — Oil dependence and export diversification

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## One basket or many?
Write a pretend country's exports on slips — oil, oil, oil, dates, carpets, steel. First count the money resting on oil alone; then add the other slips and say what changed. That change is diversification.

## The grandchildren's share
Argue the book's debate in two voices: one voice says extract everything now, the other speaks for future generations. Write the strongest sentence of each side.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach sub lesson 1 aloud — oil as a heritage that is used up — as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
