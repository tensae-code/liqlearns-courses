# Oil dependence and export diversification — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What question does the Grade 6 book set the class to debate about oil?
**Answer:** Whether the present generation may extract all the oil and gas without thinking of future generations.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Who took the profit from Iran's oil for most of a century, and who took over after the Islamic Revolution?
**Answer:** Foreign oil companies extracted and refined the oil and took great profits under contracts. After the Islamic Revolution, Iranian specialists took over.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What is export diversification, and why does a country with oil need it?
**Answer:** It is spreading a country's export earnings across many goods instead of resting them on oil alone. A country needs it because oil is a heritage that is used up — when it finishes, or its price falls, an economy resting on it alone has nothing else to sell.
