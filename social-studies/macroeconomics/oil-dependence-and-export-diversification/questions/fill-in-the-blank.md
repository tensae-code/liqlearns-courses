# Oil dependence and export diversification — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Oil is a natural _____ that is used up — every barrel taken out is gone.
**Answer: heritage**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The countries of Southwest Asia depend on oil exports while importing machinery, food, _____ and consumer goods.
**Answer: medicine**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Iran tries not to depend on oil exports and to sell other _____ abroad.
**Answer: goods**
