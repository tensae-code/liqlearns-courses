# Oil dependence and export diversification — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Oil is described in the Grade 6 book as —
a) a heritage that renews itself every year  b) a natural heritage that is used up  c) a good with no value abroad
**Answer: b) a natural heritage that is used up**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** For most of a century, the profit from Iran's oil was taken by —
a) Iranian specialists  b) foreign oil companies under contracts  c) the country's farmers
**Answer: b) foreign oil companies under contracts**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Export diversification means —
a) selling oil only  b) spreading export earnings across many goods instead of depending on oil alone  c) importing more goods
**Answer: b) spreading export earnings across many goods instead of depending on oil alone**
