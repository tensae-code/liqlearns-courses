# Oil dependence and export diversification — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The first well in the book's story is the Masjid Soleiman well, about 100 years ago.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** After the Islamic Revolution, foreign oil companies kept extracting Iran's oil and taking the profits.
**Answer: False — For most of a century foreign companies extracted and refined Iran's oil and took great profits under contracts; after the Islamic Revolution Iranian specialists took over.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Export diversification means depending on oil exports alone.
**Answer: False — Export diversification is the opposite: spreading what a country earns across many exports — selling other goods abroad instead of depending on the one heritage that runs out.**
