Globalisation and multinational corporations — as Pakistan's book teaches it, says the narrator: globalisation is economies knitting together across borders into one world market; the book teaches globalisation's advantages — wider markets and cheaper, more varied goods; multinational and transnational corporations produce and sell in many countries at once.
