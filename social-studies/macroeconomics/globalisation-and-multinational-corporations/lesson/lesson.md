## Sub lesson 1 — Globalisation

The Class 12 trade chapter names **globalisation**: economies knitting together across borders — goods, money, firms and work moving between countries as if the world were one market.

## Sub lesson 2 — Its advantages

The book then sets out globalisation's **advantages**: wider markets, cheaper and more varied goods, and production placed where it is done best — the gains that pull countries into the world market.

## Sub lesson 3 — Multinational and transnational corporations

The companies that carry globalisation are named next: **multinational corporations** and **transnational corporations** — firms that produce and sell in many countries at once, organising production across borders.
