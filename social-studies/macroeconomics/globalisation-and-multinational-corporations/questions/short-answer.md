# Globalisation and multinational corporations — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is globalisation?
**Answer:** Economies knitting together across borders — goods, money, firms and work moving as in one world market.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What advantages of globalisation does the book teach?
**Answer:** Wider markets, cheaper and more varied goods, and production placed where it is done best.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which companies carry globalisation, in the book's account?
**Answer:** Multinational corporations and transnational corporations — firms producing and selling in many countries at once.
