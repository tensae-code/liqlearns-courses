# Globalisation and multinational corporations — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Globalisation means each country trading only with itself.
**Answer: False** — it means the opposite — knitting together across borders.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Globalisation has no advantages in the book's account.
**Answer: False** — its advantages are a named part of the chapter.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A multinational corporation works in one country only.
**Answer: False** — it works in many countries at once.
