# Globalisation and multinational corporations — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Economies knitting together across borders is called _____.
**Answer: globalisation**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Wider markets and cheaper goods are among globalisation's _____.
**Answer: advantages**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Firms producing and selling in many countries at once are _____ corporations.
**Answer: multinational**
