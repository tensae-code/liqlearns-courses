# The Grade 12 economics road map — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Units 1–3 — the whole economy, its balance, its failures" in your own words.
**Answer:** Unit 1 Fundamental Concepts of Macroeconomics tells how macroeconomics was born with Keynes, names its five recurring challenges — growth, inflation, unemployment, the business cycle and the trade balance — and sets out six schools of thought.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Units 4–6 — policy levers, taxes, poverty" in your own words.
**Answer:** Unit 4 Macroeconomic Policy Instruments works fiscal, monetary, income and foreign-exchange policy, each split expansionary and contractionary, and closes with the birr's exchange-rate history from 1945 to the 2017 devaluation.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Units 7–8 — Ethiopia's reforms, then the climate economy" in your own words.
**Answer:** Unit 7 Macroeconomic Reforms in Ethiopia runs the Imperial five-year plans, the Derg, ADLI, SDPRP, PASDEP, GTP I and II with their performance tables, the 2021–2030 ten-year plan, the home-grown reforms and fiscal decentralization.
