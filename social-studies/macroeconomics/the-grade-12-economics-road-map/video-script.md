# Video script — The Grade 12 economics road map

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Welcome. Grade 11 built the consumer and the firm; this course works at the scale of the whole economy, in eight stops. First, the fundamental concepts of macroeconomics. Second, aggregate demand and aggregate supply. Third, market failure and consumer protection. Fourth, the policy instruments. Fifth, tax theory and practice. Sixth, poverty and inequality. Seventh, Ethiopia's own reforms. Eighth, the economy, the environment and climate change.

Every unit defines its words, works its examples in birr, summarises, and closes with review questions. The second half is Ethiopia's own — its plans, its reforms, and its climate strategy.
