# The Gibe Oromo kingdoms — Jimma — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** True or false: The largest and richest Gibe kingdom — this is Jimma.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** True or false: in this lesson, Gibe valley means rule by a king — the form the Gibe states adopted.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** True or false: A second great export of the Gibe trade — this is ivory.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** True or false: in this lesson, coffee means the convoy linking Jimma to northern markets.
**Answer: False**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** True or false: Abba Jifar's court seat at Jimma — this is palace.
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** True or false: in this lesson, Abba Jifar means the example that shows a whole pattern, as Jimma shows the Gibe states.
