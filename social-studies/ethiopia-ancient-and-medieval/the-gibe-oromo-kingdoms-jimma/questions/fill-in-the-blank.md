# The Gibe Oromo kingdoms — Jimma — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own term.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The term for 'rule by a king — the form the Gibe states adopted' is ______.
**Answer: monarchy**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The term for 'the convoy linking Jimma to northern markets' is ______.
**Answer: caravan**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The term for 'the example that shows a whole pattern, as Jimma shows the Gibe states' is ______.
**Answer: type case**
