# Video script — The Gibe Oromo kingdoms — Jimma

Story-style script in the animated-map spirit: the map opens, peoples and armies move, routes light up. Super-simple voice; made later, when videos are made.

---

[Scene 1 — Oromo monarchies of the Gibe valley. The map and the scene build as the voice speaks.]
In the fertile Gibe valley and the Leqa lands, Oromo peoples who had lived by the gadaa system raised monarchies — kingdoms with courts, capitals and kings. Watch the names appear one by one: Gibe valley, Jimma, monarchy. This is the part of the story to hold.

[Scene 2 — Rich on coffee, ivory and trade. The map and the scene build as the voice speaks.]
Jimma grew rich on what its forests and farms produced — coffee above all — plus ivory and other goods carried north, and on the slave trade that stained the region's commerce.

Caravans linked Jimma to the northern markets; its king taxed, protected and profited from the flow.. Watch the names appear one by one: coffee, ivory, caravan. This is the part of the story to hold.

[Scene 3 — Abba Jifar's Jimma. The map and the scene build as the voice speaks.]
Under Abba Jifar, Jimma reached its height: a palace, a court, ordered markets and a king who dealt with the northern empire as a power in his own right.

His Jimma is this lesson's type case — the Gibe pattern at full stretch, just before the age of incorporation.. Watch the names appear one by one: Abba Jifar, palace, type case. This is the part of the story to hold.
