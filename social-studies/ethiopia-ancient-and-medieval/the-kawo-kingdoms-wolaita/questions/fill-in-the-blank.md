# The kawo kingdoms — Wolaita — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own term.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The term for 'the sacral royal title of Kafa, contrasted with kawo' is ______.
**Answer: tato**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The term for 'a rich kingdom watched hungrily by stronger neighbours' is ______.
**Answer: prize**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The term for 'the long self-rule Wolaita remembered' is ______.
**Answer: independence**
