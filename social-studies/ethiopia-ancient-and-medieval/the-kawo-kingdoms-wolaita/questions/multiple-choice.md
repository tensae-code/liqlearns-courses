# The kawo kingdoms — Wolaita — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is kawo?
a) the royal title of Wolaita and its neighbours
b) dynastic memory
c) tato
**Answer: a) the royal title of Wolaita and its neighbours**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is expansion?
a) prize
b) tribute
c) growth by conquest and tribute, as Wolaita's kings practised
**Answer: c) growth by conquest and tribute, as Wolaita's kings practised**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What is resistance?
a) Wolaita's defence of its independence against highland pressure
b) independence
c) incorporation
**Answer: a) Wolaita's defence of its independence against highland pressure**
