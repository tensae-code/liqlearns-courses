# Harar — walled city of trade and Islamic learning — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Harar's Adal sultanate was established about — by —.
a) 1270, Yekuno-Amlak  b) 1380, members of Ifat's ruling family  c) 1636, Fasiladas
**Answer: b) 1380, members of Ifat's ruling family** — After Ifat's 1332 defeat.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Harar controlled trade routes between —
a) the Nile and the Congo  b) the Gulf of Aden / Indian Ocean coast and the interior highlands  c) the Mediterranean and Lake Tana
**Answer: b) the Gulf of Aden / Indian Ocean coast and the interior highlands** — Coast to highlands.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Harar has been a seat of Islamic learning since —
a) the 14th century  b) the 19th century  c) 1974
**Answer: a) the 14th century** — Learning since the 1300s.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Its walled trading prime in this lesson runs —
a) the 9th–10th centuries  b) the late 16th to the 19th century  c) only the 20th century
**Answer: b) the late 16th to the 19th century** — Late 1500s to 1800s.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Harar's later Emirate was founded by —
a) Ali ibn Dawd  b) Emir Nur Mujahid  c) Abba Jifar I
**Answer: a) Ali ibn Dawd** — Ali ibn Dawd.

**6. (Sub lesson 1)** Ifat fell in —, setting the retreat to Harar in motion.
a) 1332  b) 1529  c) 1285
**Answer: a) 1332** — Defeated in 1332.
**7. (Sub lesson 3)** What is emirs?
a) a detail belonging to a different lesson in this course
b) the sea trade Harar linked to the interior
c) Harar's rulers, of its commercial and scholarly age
**Answer: c) Harar's rulers, of its commercial and scholarly age**
