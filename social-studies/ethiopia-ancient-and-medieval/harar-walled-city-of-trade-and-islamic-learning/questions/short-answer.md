# Harar — walled city of trade and Islamic learning — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why was Harar a strategic seat for Adal?
**Answer:** It commands the trade routes linking the Gulf of Aden and Indian Ocean coast with the interior highlands.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Give Harar's three roles across this lesson.
**Answer:** Political power (sultanate, then emirate), major coast–interior trade centre, and seat of Islamic learning.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Date the arc: Ifat's defeat, Adal at Harar, the walled prime.
**Answer:** 1332 — c.1380 — late 16th to 19th century.
