# Harar — walled city of trade and Islamic learning — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Sultanate of Adal at Harar was founded before Ifat's defeat.
**Answer: False** — It followed the defeat — c.1380 after 1332.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Harar's importance was only military.
**Answer: False** — Trade centre and seat of learning as well as a walled power.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Ali ibn Dawd founded Harar's later Emirate.
**Answer: True** — The city's second political life.
**4. (Sub lesson 3)** True or false: The sea trade Harar linked to the interior — this is Gulf of Aden trade.
**Answer: True**
