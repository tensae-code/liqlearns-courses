# Video script — Harar — walled city of trade and Islamic learning

The story-style script for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list), in the animated-map spirit: the map opens, peoples and armies move, capitals rise, routes light up. Super-simple voice. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Dusk. The map finds a walled town on the eastern hills. Gates swing shut.
We rewind: 1332, Ifat falls. A ruling family rides east — and at Harar, about 1380, raises a sultanate again.
Caravans pour through the gates: coast goods up, highland goods down. Harar takes its due.
Night falls. In the mosques, lamps — scholars, students, books. A city that trades by day and teaches by night.
Empires around it rise and fall. The walls stand. Harar endures.
