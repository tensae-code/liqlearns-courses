# Lesson plan — Harar — walled city of trade and Islamic learning

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Ethiopia Ancient and Medieval · **Lesson:** Harar — walled city of trade and Islamic learning
**Place in the curriculum:** Grade 9 History, Unit 8 — Political, Social and Economic Processes in Ethiopia, mid-16th–mid-19th C. (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked into this title in the Ethiopia Wave 4 build.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** After **Ifat's defeat in 1332**, members of its ruling family **retreated to Harar** and established the **Sultanate of Adal** there about **1380**.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the learner having met Ifat, Adal and the Zeila road — Harar is where that eastern story settled and endured.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.
