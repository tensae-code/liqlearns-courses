# Hands-on — Harar — walled city of trade and Islamic learning

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Gates of the city
Build Harar from boxes or stones: a wall ring with gates. Run a caravan (counters or beans) from 'coast' to 'highlands' through the gates, paying the city's due at each — then state Harar's geographic advantage in one sentence.

## Trade and learning timetable
Make the city's day as a timetable: dawn caravan, market hours, afternoon study, night gates closed. Defend each block from the lesson's three roles.

## Grade 11 depth — Harar in depth — the emirs' city

Re-draw this lesson's map or table with the Grade 11 part added in a second colour, then tell the new part's story aloud from the drawing alone. Where the drawing cannot carry the story, the story is not yet learned — re-read that part.
