# Ancient Ethiopia — Da'amat and Yeha — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word or number.

**1.** Da'amat's centre was the town of ____.
**Answer: Yeha**

**2.** A mukarrib was religious guardian, chief ____ and war commander.
**Answer: judge**

**3. (Grade 11 depth)** The term for 'the pre-Aksumite temple site witnessing the early highland world' is ______.
**Answer: Yeha**
