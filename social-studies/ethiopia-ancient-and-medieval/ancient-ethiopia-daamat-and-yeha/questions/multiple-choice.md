# Ancient Ethiopia — Da'amat and Yeha — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Da'amat was centred at —
a) Lalibela  b) Yeha  c) Gondar  d) Harar
**Answer: b) Yeha** — Yeha, south of today's Aksum, was its centre.

**2.** Yeha lies —
a) South of today's Aksum  b) On the Dahlak islands  c) In the Bale highlands  d) Beside Lake Tana
**Answer: a) South of today's Aksum** — The book places Yeha south of Aksum.

**3.** Mukarrib means a ruler who is —
a) Only a priest  b) Religious guardian, chief judge and war commander  c) A sea trader  d) A builder of churches
**Answer: b) Religious guardian, chief judge and war commander** — The South Arabian title carries all three jobs.

**4.** The title mukarrib is evidence of —
a) War with Egypt  b) Close political ties between Ethiopia and South Arabia  c) The coming of Christianity  d) Trade with Rome only
**Answer: b) Close political ties between Ethiopia and South Arabia** — Borrowed royal titles show close political ties.
