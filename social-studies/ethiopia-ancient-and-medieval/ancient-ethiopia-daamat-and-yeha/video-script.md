# Video script — Ancient Ethiopia — Da'amat and Yeha

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

North of Aksum stands Yeha, very old.
Here ruled Da'amat.
Its kings took a title from across the sea: mukarrib.
Guardian. Judge. Commander of war.
Yet they kept their own gods.
Two shores, closely tied — that is Da'amat.
