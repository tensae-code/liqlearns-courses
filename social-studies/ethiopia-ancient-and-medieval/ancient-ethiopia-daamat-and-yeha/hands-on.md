# Hands-on — Ancient Ethiopia — Da'amat and Yeha

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Temple stones
With stones or blocks, build a small square temple tower like Yeha's. Count how many stones you need for just one wall — then think about the real builders' work.

## Three hats of a king
Find three head-coverings or draw three hats: one for the religious guardian, one for the chief judge, one for the war commander. Say one duty for each hat.

## Six-name map (Grade 9)
Place the six named principalities on an outline map of the northern highlands and coast as far as the lesson allows, each with one line: what the Grade 9 book says of it. Where the book gives no location, write 'placed by region only' — the honesty is part of the exercise.
