# Ancient Ethiopia — Da'amat and Yeha — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson, with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can do the lesson's outcomes — Say Da'amat was a northern-Ethiopian state centred at Yeha, south of today's Aksum.; Give the title mukarrib and its three meanings: religious guardian, chief judge, war commander.; Explain why the title is evidence of close political ties with South Arabia. Practice deepens inside the whole lesson: more items per question set, then the harder corners — exact dates, exact names, and the cause-and-effect links between them — until the retelling in 'Say it to your companion' runs clean from memory.
