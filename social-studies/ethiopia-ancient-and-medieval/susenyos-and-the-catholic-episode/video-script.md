# Video script — Susenyos and the Catholic episode

Story-style script in the animated-map spirit: the map opens, peoples and armies move, routes light up. Super-simple voice; made later, when videos are made.

---

[Scene 1 — An emperor tries to recentralise. The map and the scene build as the voice speaks.]
Emperor Susenyos came to the throne through civil war and set out to recentralise a kingdom slipping toward its lords. Watch the names appear one by one: Susenyos, recentralise, Jesuits. This is the part of the story to hold.

[Scene 2 — Conversion and imposition. The map and the scene build as the voice speaks.]
Susenyos converted to Catholicism and, urged by the Jesuits, imposed it on an Orthodox kingdom — new rites, rebaptism, and penalties for refusal.

The kingdom answered with rebellion. Watch the names appear one by one: conversion, imposition, rebellion. This is the part of the story to hold.

[Scene 3 — Abdication, 1632. The map and the scene build as the voice speaks.]
In 1632, facing a kingdom in arms against his faith, Susenyos did the rare thing: he abdicated, restoring freedom of worship and handing the throne to his son Fasilides.

The episode closed Ethiopia to Catholic missions for centuries — and taught its rulers the price of imposing belief from the throne.. Watch the names appear one by one: abdication, Fasilides, freedom of worship. This is the part of the story to hold.
