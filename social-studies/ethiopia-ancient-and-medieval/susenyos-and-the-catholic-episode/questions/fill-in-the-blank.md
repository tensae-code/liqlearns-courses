# Susenyos and the Catholic episode — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own term.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The term for 'the Catholic missionaries at Susenyos's court' is ______.
**Answer: Jesuits**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The term for 'the provinces' rising for the Orthodox faith' is ______.
**Answer: rebellion**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The term for 'what Susenyos restored in abdicating' is ______.
**Answer: freedom of worship**
