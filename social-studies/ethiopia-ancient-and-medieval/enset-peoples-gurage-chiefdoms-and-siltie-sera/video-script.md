# Video script — Enset peoples — Gurage chiefdoms and Siltie Sera

The story-style script for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list), in the animated-map spirit: the map opens, peoples and armies move, capitals rise, routes light up. Super-simple voice. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The camera opens on green — enset leaves like sails, field after field of them.
People thick on the land. A market hums at a border: highland enset, lowland spice.
West of the lake, Gurage chiefdoms — written of since the 1300s, remembering Gura.
And a Siltie assembly gathers: YeMule Siltie Melcho. The Raga judges rise.
One plant. Many peoples. Their own law. That is the enset south.
