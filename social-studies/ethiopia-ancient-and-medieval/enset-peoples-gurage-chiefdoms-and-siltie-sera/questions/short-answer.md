# Enset peoples — Gurage chiefdoms and Siltie Sera — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why does a plant sit at the centre of this lesson's politics?
**Answer:** Enset feeds many people from little land, making dense settlement — and with it markets, assemblies and law — possible and necessary.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Give the Gurage chiefdoms' location and first written mention.
**Answer:** West of Lake Ziway; first mentioned in 14th-century written sources.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name Sera's three Siltie marks from this lesson.
**Answer:** Descent also through the mother line; the general assembly YeMule Siltie Melcho; the Raga judiciary.
