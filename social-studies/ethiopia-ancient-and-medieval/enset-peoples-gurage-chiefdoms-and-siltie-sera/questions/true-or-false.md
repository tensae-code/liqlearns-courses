# Enset peoples — Gurage chiefdoms and Siltie Sera — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Enset grows only in lowlands, in this account.
**Answer: False** — Enset is the highland crop; lowlands grew maize, wheat and spices.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Gurage oral tradition tells of descent from Christian settlers from Gura in Eritrea.
**Answer: True** — The book's report of the tradition.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** YeMule Siltie Melcho is the Siltie general assembly.
**Answer: True** — The assembly under Sera.
**4. (Sub lesson 4)** True or false: The Gurage systems named only in the teacher guide (evidence) — this is Yajoka Qicha and Gordanna Sera.
**Answer: True**
