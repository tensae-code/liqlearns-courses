# Enset peoples — Gurage chiefdoms and Siltie Sera — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The 'enset-culture complex' was shared by —
a) the northern highlands only  b) southern and south-western Ethiopia  c) the Afar lowlands
**Answer: b) southern and south-western Ethiopia** — Among the country's most densely populated areas.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In the enset lands, markets served as —
a) fortresses  b) communication across ethnic borders  c) tax offices
**Answer: b) communication across ethnic borders** — Meeting, trade and news across borders.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The Gurage chiefdoms lay —
a) east of Harar  b) west of Lake Ziway  c) on the Dahlak islands
**Answer: b) west of Lake Ziway** — West of the lake.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Gurage are first mentioned in written sources in —
a) the 4th century  b) the 14th century  c) the 19th century
**Answer: b) the 14th century** — 14th-century written sources.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Siltie descent was reckoned —
a) only through fathers  b) also through the mother line  c) by election
**Answer: b) also through the mother line** — The mother line counts too.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** The Siltie judiciary is called —
a) the Raga system  b) the Guurti  c) the chaffe
**Answer: a) the Raga system** — Raga judges; the general assembly is YeMule Siltie Melcho.
