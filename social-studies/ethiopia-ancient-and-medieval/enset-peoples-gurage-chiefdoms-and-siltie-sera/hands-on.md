# Hands-on — Enset peoples — Gurage chiefdoms and Siltie Sera

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Market day map
Draw a market at a border between two peoples: stalls, goods from highland (enset) and lowland (maize, wheat, spices). Label what besides goods crosses the border — news, marriages, languages.

## Sit in the Melcho
Hold a mock YeMule Siltie Melcho: an assembly must settle a stolen-goat case. Appoint Raga judges; the assembly debates; the judges rule. Afterwards, name which part was assembly and which was judiciary.
