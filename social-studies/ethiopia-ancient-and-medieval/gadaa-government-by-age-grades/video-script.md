# Video script — Gadaa — government by age grades and eight-year succession

The story-style script for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list), in the animated-map spirit: the map opens, peoples and armies move, capitals rise, routes light up. Super-simple voice. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The map opens on Oromo land. No palace rises. Instead — a great tree. The odaa.
Under it, the chaffe gathers. An old grade stands; a new grade steps forward. The Butta begins.
Eight years of power, handed over on schedule. The sceptre passes. The law stays.
Abbaa Sera. Abbaa Duulaa. Abbaa Chaffe. Named offices, divided powers, no crown anywhere.
A government that hands itself over, century after century. That is Gadaa.
