# Gadaa — government by age grades and eight-year succession — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A Gadaa leader rules for life once chosen.
**Answer: False** — Eight years, then the Butta hands power on.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Executive, legislative and judicial functions are divided in Gadaa.
**Answer: True** — Division of power is one of its credited marks.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** There is a testing period between election and investiture.
**Answer: True** — Leaders are tested before taking full office.
