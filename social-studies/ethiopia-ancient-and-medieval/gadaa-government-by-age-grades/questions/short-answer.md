# Gadaa — government by age grades and eight-year succession — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How does power change hands in Gadaa, and how often?
**Answer:** By age grade, every eight years, at the Butta ceremony at the chaffe assembly.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name four Abbaa offices with their meanings where the book gives them.
**Answer:** Abbaa Bokku (father of the sceptre), Abbaa Sera (father of law), Abbaa Duulaa (war leader), Abbaa Chaffe (head of assembly) — also Abbaa Sa'a and Abbaa Alanga.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** List four checks the book credits to Gadaa.
**Answer:** Any four of: eight-year succession; division of executive/legislative/judicial power; balanced opposition among five parties; power-sharing; laws above all; accountability with confession and impeachment (buqqisu); testing period before investiture.
