# Gadaa — government by age grades and eight-year succession — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Gadaa organises society in —
a) castes  b) age grades  c) guilds
**Answer: b) age grades** —  Grades through life, power by turn.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Power transfers every — years, at the — ceremony.
a) four, coronation  b) eight, Butta  c) twenty, harvest
**Answer: b) eight, Butta** — Eight years; the Butta at the chaffe.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Abbaa Sera is —
a) father of the sceptre  b) father of law  c) the war leader
**Answer: b) father of law** — Sera = law.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The war leader is —
a) Abbaa Duulaa  b) Abbaa Bokku  c) Abbaa Chaffe
**Answer: a) Abbaa Duulaa** — Duulaa leads in war; Bokku holds the sceptre.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Buqqisu is —
a) a tax  b) confession and impeachment — removing a leader  c) a grade name
**Answer: b) confession and impeachment — removing a leader** — Accountability with teeth.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** In Gadaa, what stands above all — even leaders?
a) the army  b) the laws  c) the treasury
**Answer: b) the laws** — Laws standing above all.
