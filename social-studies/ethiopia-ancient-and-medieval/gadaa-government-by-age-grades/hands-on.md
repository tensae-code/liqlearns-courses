# Hands-on — Gadaa — government by age grades and eight-year succession

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The eight-year class
Run the class as a Gadaa grade for a week: elect an Abbaa Sera for disputes and an Abbaa Chaffe to chair meetings, with a fixed hand-over date. At the Butta (a ten-minute ceremony), the outgoing leaders confess one mistake — buqqisu's spirit — and invest the next grade.

## Under the odaa
Hold a council outdoors or under a drawn tree: an agenda of three school questions, offices assigned from the lesson, laws written first and read aloud before any decision — laws above all, in practice.
