# Caravans to Zeila — the eastern trade — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Prophet is said to have exempted Ethiopia from jihad in return for the refuge given.
**Answer: True** — The book's account of the refuge.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Muslim states stood along the Zeila road only from the 15th century.
**Answer: False** — Communities and states along its branches from the 8th–9th centuries.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Hadya and Bali are named among the principalities.
**Answer: True** — Two of the chain of nine named.
