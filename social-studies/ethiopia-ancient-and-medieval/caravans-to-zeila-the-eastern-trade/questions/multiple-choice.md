# Caravans to Zeila — the eastern trade — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Islam first reached Ethiopia as —
a) conquest  b) refuge — early Muslims received by the Aksumite king  c) a Portuguese mission
**Answer: b) refuge — early Muslims received by the Aksumite king** — Refuge first.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Islam entered the interior mainly along trade routes from —
a) Massawa  b) Zeila  c) Adulis in its prime
**Answer: b) Zeila** — After Adulis declined, Zeila's road.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Zeila became chief port for — trade.
a) northern Red Sea  b) south-eastern Ethiopian  c) Mediterranean
**Answer: b) south-eastern Ethiopian** — And Islam's main gateway into the Horn.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The earliest sultanate on the road was —
a) Ifat  b) Adal  c) Shewa
**Answer: c) Shewa** — Shewa, first Makhzumite.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Shewa's Makhzumite dynasty was succeeded in 1285 by —
a) the Walasma dynasty of Ifat  b) the Zagwe  c) the Mamluks
**Answer: a) the Walasma dynasty of Ifat** — Walasma, 1285.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** The principalities' existence turned on —
a) controlling trade  b) naval power  c) mining
**Answer: a) controlling trade** — Like the Christian kingdom's, on trade.
**7. (Sub lesson 4)** What is Dahlak route?
a) the island-hopping Red Sea route of the eastern trade
b) the staged markets between highland and lowland
c) a detail belonging to a different lesson in this course
**Answer: a) the island-hopping Red Sea route of the eastern trade**
