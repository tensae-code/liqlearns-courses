# Caravans to Zeila — the eastern trade — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Trace Islam's two arrivals: refuge, then road — with the ports named.
**Answer:** First as refuge at the Aksumite court; then, after Adulis declined, inland along trade routes from Zeila.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why did states form along the road rather than away from it?
**Answer:** Because wealth moved along it — controlling the road meant taxing and guarding the caravans between port and rich interior.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name five principalities of the chain.
**Answer:** Any five of: Ifat, Fatigar, Dawaro, Dara, Bali, Hadya, Arababini, Sharka, Adal.
