# Hands-on — Caravans to Zeila — the eastern trade

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Beads on a string
Thread nine beads or buttons on a string, one per principality, with Zeila as the clasp. Each learner ties one bead and states its place in the chain and the road's purpose.

## Toll-gate debate
Two courts — Shewa and the Christian kingdom — each claim the road's tolls. Argue from trade alone: who needs the road, who guards it, who pays? The class names the shared fact both sides depend on.

## Grade 11 depth — Caravans to Zeila in depth — the eastern trade system

Re-draw this lesson's map or table with the Grade 11 part added in a second colour, then tell the new part's story aloud from the drawing alone. Where the drawing cannot carry the story, the story is not yet learned — re-read that part.
