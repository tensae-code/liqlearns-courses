# Hands-on — Aksum — contributions and decline

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Make a coin
Press a coin design into clay or thick foil: your initial on one side, a picture on the other, the way Ezana's coins carried inscriptions in two languages. Say what your two sides proclaim.

## Two-column debate
With a partner, argue: which hurt Aksum more — losing the sea trade, or the troubles inside? Each takes one side, using the lesson's facts.

## Grade 11 depth — The Aksumite Kingdom in depth — trade, coinage and the long decline

Re-draw this lesson's map or table with the Grade 11 part added in a second colour, then tell the new part's story aloud from the drawing alone. Where the drawing cannot carry the story, the story is not yet learned — re-read that part.
