# Aksum — contributions and decline — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Aksum gave Ethiopia a literary language.
**Answer: True** — Ge'ez became Ethiopia's literary language under Aksum.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Aksum fell only because of foreign enemies.
**Answer: False** — Inside troubles — revolt, struggles, weakening — worked with the outside blows.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The Aksumite state ended about 1150.
**Answer: True** — It ended about 1150, after the centre moved to Lasta.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** True or false: The Aksumite king whose expedition crossed to Himyar — this is Kaleb.
**Answer: True**
