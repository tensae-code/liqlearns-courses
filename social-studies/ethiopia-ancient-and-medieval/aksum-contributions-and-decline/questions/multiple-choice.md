# Aksum — contributions and decline — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Aksum's coins were made of —
a) Iron only  b) Gold, silver and bronze  c) Copper only  d) Clay
**Answer: b) Gold, silver and bronze** — Coinage in three metals, inscribed in Greek and Ge'ez.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Whose gold coin is pictured in the book?
a) Lalibela's  b) Ezana's  c) Amda Tsion's  d) Fasiledes'
**Answer: b) Ezana's** — Ezana's gold coin is the pictured example.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Adulis was taken in —
a) 615  b) 702  c) 1270  d) 1543
**Answer: b) 702** — Adulis taken in 702, as Arab control of Red Sea trade grew.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which was an INSIDE cause of Aksum's fall?
a) Arab control of the Red Sea  b) The Beja revolt and invasion  c) The loss of Adulis  d) Ottoman expansion
**Answer: b) The Beja revolt and invasion** — Beja revolt, power struggles and weakening were internal.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** After Aksum weakened, the centre moved to —
a) Gondar  b) Lasta  c) Harar  d) Yeha
**Answer: b) Lasta** — The centre moved to Lasta; the state ended about 1150.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** What is Adulis?
a) Aksum's Red Sea port, source of its trading wealth
b) the Aksumite king whose expedition crossed to Himyar
c) a detail belonging to a different lesson in this course
**Answer: a) Aksum's Red Sea port, source of its trading wealth**
