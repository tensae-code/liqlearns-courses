# Hands-on — Ethiopian chronicles and hagiographies as sources

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Praise and proof
In pairs, one writes a praise-poem of the other's week (hagiography style); the other extracts a factual timeline from it, marking every claim that needs an outside check. Swap roles.

## The testing table
Lay a chronicle-style text (teacher-made) beside a coin drawing, a king list and a map. Groups must confirm or reject three claims in the text using only the other sources, and report their verdicts.
