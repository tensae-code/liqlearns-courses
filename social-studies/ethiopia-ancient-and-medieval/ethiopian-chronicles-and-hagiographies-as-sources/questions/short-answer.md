# Ethiopian chronicles and hagiographies as sources — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What changed in the early 20th century, and what began in the 1960s?
**Answer:** The indigenous chronicle tradition changed in the early 20th century; Ethiopian academic history dates from the 1960s.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why is a hagiography harder to use than a king list, and how is it used well?
**Answer:** It was written to glorify its saint, mixing memory and miracle; used well, it is cross-tested against inscriptions, coins and other chronicles, and only what survives is written as history.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Name three other primary-source kinds the book lists beside chronicles.
**Answer:** Any three of: monuments, tools, coins, inscriptions, ruins, manuscripts, diaries, letters, law codes, court records, photographs, maps, audiovisual material.
