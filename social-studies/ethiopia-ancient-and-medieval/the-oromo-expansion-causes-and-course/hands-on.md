# Hands-on — The Oromo expansion — causes and course

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Arrows on the ground
Chalk a rough map outdoors: Bale at the south. Walk outward in five directions with a partner, naming the region you enter and the decade band you are in.

## Generations rope
Lay a rope marked 1522 — 1580 — 1604 — 1710. Stand at each mark and say what stage of the movement it names.
