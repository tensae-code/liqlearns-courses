# Hands-on — The Oromo expansion — causes and course

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Arrows on the ground
Chalk a rough map outdoors: Bale at the south. Walk outward in five directions with a partner, naming the region you enter and the decade band you are in.

## Generations rope
Lay a rope marked 1522 — 1580 — 1604 — 1710. Stand at each mark and say what stage of the movement it names.

## Grade 11 depth — The Oromo movement in depth — waves on the gadaa wheel

Re-draw this lesson's map or table with the Grade 11 part added in a second colour, then tell the new part's story aloud from the drawing alone. Where the drawing cannot carry the story, the story is not yet learned — re-read that part.
