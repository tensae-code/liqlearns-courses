# The Oromo expansion — causes and course — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson, with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can do the lesson's outcomes — Say the Oromo are Cushitic-speaking, homeland in the Bale highlands (Meda Welabu) by oral tradition.; Give the three causes: population growth, search for grazing land, the Gada system encouraging taking new land.; Give the course: began under Melbah Gada 1522, expansion 1550s–1580s in all directions and into Kenya and Somalia, slowed after 1604, campaigns to 1710 for Innarya. Practice deepens inside the whole lesson: more items per question set, then the harder corners — exact dates, exact names, and the cause-and-effect links between them — until the retelling in 'Say it to your companion' runs clean from memory.
