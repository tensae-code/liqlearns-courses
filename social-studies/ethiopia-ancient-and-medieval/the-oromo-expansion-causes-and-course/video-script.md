# Video script — The Oromo expansion — causes and course

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

From the Bale highlands, a people moves.
The Oromo — Cushitic-speaking herders and farmers.
More people. A need for grazing land.
And Gada, organising each generation.
1550s onward: north, east, west, south.
Into Kenya and Somalia too.
A movement of generations, not a single march.

[Grade 11 depth scene — The Oromo movement in depth — waves on the gadaa wheel. The animated map carries the story as the voice speaks.]
From the mid-16th century the Oromo expand northward in waves, and the Grade 11 book gives the engine its name: the gadaa generation system, cycling age-sets through rule every eight years, organising expansion as a people's whole undertaking. The names to hold: gadaa, age-set.
