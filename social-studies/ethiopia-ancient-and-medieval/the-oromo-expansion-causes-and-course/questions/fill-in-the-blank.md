# The Oromo expansion — causes and course — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word or number.

**1.** The Oromo homeland by tradition is ____ Welabu.
**Answer: Meda**

**2.** The movement began under the ____ Gada in 1522.
**Answer: Melbah**

**3. (Grade 11 depth)** The term for 'a generation group moving through gadaa grades together' is ______.
**Answer: age-set**
