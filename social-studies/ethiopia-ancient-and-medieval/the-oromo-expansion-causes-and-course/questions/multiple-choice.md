# The Oromo expansion — causes and course — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Oral tradition places the Oromo homeland at —
a) Meda Welabu in the Bale highlands  b) Yeha  c) The Dahlak islands  d) Lake Tana
**Answer: a) Meda Welabu in the Bale highlands** — Meda Welabu, Bale highlands.

**2.** The Oromo speak a language of which family?
a) Semitic  b) Cushitic  c) Nilo-Saharan  d) Omotic only
**Answer: b) Cushitic** — A Cushitic-speaking people.

**3.** Which is NOT one of the book's three causes?
a) Population growth  b) Search for grazing land  c) The Gada system  d) A Portuguese invitation
**Answer: d) A Portuguese invitation** — The three causes are growth, grazing land, and Gada.

**4.** The movement began under the Melbah Gada in —
a) 1380  b) 1522  c) 1636  d) 1769
**Answer: b) 1522** — Began under the Melbah Gada in 1522.

**5.** The expansion slowed after —
a) 1543  b) 1604  c) 1710  d) 1855
**Answer: b) 1604** — Slowed after 1604; campaigns ran to 1710 for Innarya.

**6. (Grade 11 depth)** What is gadaa?
a) a detail belonging to a different lesson in this course
b) the Oromo generation-grade system organising society and expansion
c) a generation group moving through gadaa grades together
**Answer: b) the Oromo generation-grade system organising society and expansion**
