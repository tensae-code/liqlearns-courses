# The Oromo expansion — causes and course — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** Name the three causes of the expansion.
**Answer:** Population growth, the search for grazing land, and a Gada system that encouraged taking new land.

**2.** To what year did the campaigns run, and for which land?
**Answer:** To 1710, for Innarya.
