# Video script — Aksum — the first Hijra

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

They were hunted for their faith.
So they crossed the sea to Aksum.
First, in 615, a small group — among them Ruqayya, the Prophet's daughter.
Then, in 616, about a hundred and nineteen, led by Ja'far.
King al-Ashama received them. 'Live in peace,' he said.
Most sailed home in 629. Some stayed for ever.
Their graves at Negash are honoured still.
Justice. Order. Welcome. That is the story's lesson.
