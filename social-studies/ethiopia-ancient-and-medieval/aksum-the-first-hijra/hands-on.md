# Hands-on — Aksum — the first Hijra

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Tell the story aloud
Tell the Hijra story to a family member or friend from memory, the way a storyteller would — beginning 'In the days when…' Correct yourself afterwards against the lesson.

## Graves of honour
At a local graveyard or memorial place, stand quietly for a minute and think: why do people honour the graves of guests who died far from home? Write two lines about it.
