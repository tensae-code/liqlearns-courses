# Aksum — the first Hijra — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1.** The refugees were forced to give up their faith in Aksum.
**Answer: False** — They lived in peace, free in their faith.

**2.** Most of the refugees returned to Arabia in 629.
**Answer: True** — Most returned in 629; some settled.

**3.** Those who died were buried without honour.
**Answer: False** — 15–20 who died were buried with honour at Negash.
