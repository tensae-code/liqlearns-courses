# Aksum — the first Hijra — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** The first Hijra to Aksum was in —
a) 616  b) 615  c) 629  d) 330
**Answer: b) 615** — The first crossing was in 615.

**2.** The first group numbered about —
a) 119  b) 15–18  c) 300  d) 40
**Answer: b) 15–18** — 15–18 people, named in the account.

**3.** Among the first group was Ruqayya, who was —
a) The king's wife  b) The Prophet's daughter  c) A merchant of Adulis  d) A Nine Saint
**Answer: b) The Prophet's daughter** — Ruqayya, the Prophet's daughter, is named.

**4.** The second Hijra (616) was led by —
a) Ezana  b) Ja'far ibn Abi Talib  c) Umar Walasma  d) Frumentius
**Answer: b) Ja'far ibn Abi Talib** — About 119 people under Ja'far ibn Abi Talib.

**5.** The king who received them was —
a) Zoscales  b) Al-Ashama (Armah)  c) Yekuno Amlak  d) Susenyos
**Answer: b) Al-Ashama (Armah)** — King al-Ashama (Armah, 615–631).
