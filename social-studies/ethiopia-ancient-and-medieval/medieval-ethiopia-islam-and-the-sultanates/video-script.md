# Video script — Medieval Ethiopia — Islam and the sultanates

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

No armies brought Islam to Ethiopia.
Traders did — through Dahlak, Zeila, Berbera.
East and south-east, the faith took root:
Harar, Wollo, Bale, Arsi.
Along the Zeila road, states rose: the sultanates.
Trade and faith, travelling together.

[Grade 11 depth scene — Ifat, Adal and the eastern sultanates in depth. The animated map carries the story as the voice speaks.]
The eastern sultanates receive their Grade 11 growth story: Ifat, Adal and their sisters rising on the trade routes to Zeila, their wealth in slaves, ivory and gold moving to the sea, their faith and scholarship deepening as their caravans lengthened. The names to hold: Ifat, Zeila.
