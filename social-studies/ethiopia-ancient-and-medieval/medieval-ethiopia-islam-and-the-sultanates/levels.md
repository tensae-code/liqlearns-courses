# Medieval Ethiopia — Islam and the sultanates — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson, with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can do the lesson's outcomes — Name the entry points Dahlak, Zeila and Berbera, and the carriers: Arab Muslim traders, peacefully.; Say Islam spread in the east and south-east (Harar, Wollo, Bale, Arsi) and stayed limited to the Red Sea shores in the Christian north.; Explain that the sultanates rose along the Zeila trade route. Practice deepens inside the whole lesson: more items per question set, then the harder corners — exact dates, exact names, and the cause-and-effect links between them — until the retelling in 'Say it to your companion' runs clean from memory.
