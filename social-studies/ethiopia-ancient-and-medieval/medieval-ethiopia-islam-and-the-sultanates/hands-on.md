# Hands-on — Medieval Ethiopia — Islam and the sultanates

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Caravan line
Line up as a caravan: each person carries one 'good' card and one 'news of the faith' card along a chalk route from 'coast' to 'interior'. At each stop, say what a trader leaves behind besides goods.

## Two gateways map
Draw the coast with Dahlak islands offshore and Zeila on the mainland. Make Zeila's arrow into the interior thickest — the guide calls it the main gateway.
