# Medieval Ethiopia — Islam and the sultanates — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1.** Islam entered Ethiopia peacefully.
**Answer: True** — Through traders and peaceful ties — the book's own word.

**2.** In the Christian north, Islam spread deep into the interior.
**Answer: False** — There it stayed limited to the Red Sea shores.

**3.** The teacher guide names Zeila the main gateway to the interior.
**Answer: True** — Guide: two gateways, Dahlak and Zeila, Zeila the main one (teacher guide — evidence).

**4. (Grade 11 depth)** True or false: The Red Sea port of the sultanates' trade — this is Zeila.
**Answer: True**
