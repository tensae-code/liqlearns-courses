# Medieval Ethiopia — Islam and the sultanates — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word or number.

**1.** Islam entered through Dahlak, ____ and Berbera.
**Answer: Zeila**

**2.** The sultanates rose along the ____ trade route.
**Answer: Zeila**

**3. (Grade 11 depth)** The term for 'the Red Sea port of the sultanates' trade' is ______.
**Answer: Zeila**
