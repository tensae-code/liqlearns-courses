# Medieval Ethiopia — Islam and the sultanates — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Islam entered Ethiopia through —
a) Gondar, Aksum and Lasta  b) Dahlak, Zeila and Berbera  c) The Nile valley only  d) Portuguese ships
**Answer: b) Dahlak, Zeila and Berbera** — The three named sea gates.

**2.** Islam was carried mainly by —
a) Invading armies  b) Arab Muslim traders  c) The Nine Saints  d) Ethiopian kings
**Answer: b) Arab Muslim traders** — Traders and peaceful ties carried it.

**3.** Islam spread most in —
a) The Christian highland interior  b) The east and south-east: Harar, Wollo, Bale, Arsi  c) Only the Dahlak islands  d) The Lake Tana area
**Answer: b) The east and south-east: Harar, Wollo, Bale, Arsi** — It spread where Christianity had not reached.

**4.** The sultanates rose along —
a) The Nile  b) The Zeila trade route  c) The Gondar road  d) The Abay river
**Answer: b) The Zeila trade route** — States grew along the trade route.
