# The Gondar period — city life — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** The royal compound held —
a) Only barracks  b) Palaces, the abun's and echage's residences, and churches  c) Only markets  d) A single castle
**Answer: b) Palaces, the abun's and echage's residences, and churches** — All stood inside the compound.

**2.** Churches outside the compound taught —
a) Tax law  b) Zema and qene  c) Navigation  d) Gun-making
**Answer: b) Zema and qene** — Sacred chant (zema) and sacred poetry (qene).

**3.** Gondar's two trade routes ran —
a) To Zeila and Berbera  b) North within Ethiopia, and west to Sudan  c) To Rome and India  d) Down the Nile only
**Answer: b) North within Ethiopia, and west to Sudan** — The two routes of the trade centre.

**4.** The Bete Israel worked as —
a) Sailors  b) Builders, potters, basket-makers and metalworkers  c) Only soldiers  d) Tax farmers
**Answer: b) Builders, potters, basket-makers and metalworkers** — The city's crafts are listed as theirs.

**5.** Yohannes I settled in quarters outside the city —
a) Only Armenians  b) Muslims, Bete Israel, Armenians, Indians and Greek merchants  c) Only Portuguese  d) The Nine Saints
**Answer: b) Muslims, Bete Israel, Armenians, Indians and Greek merchants** — Five groups named for the quarters.
