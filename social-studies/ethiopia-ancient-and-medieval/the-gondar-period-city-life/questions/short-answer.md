# The Gondar period — city life — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** What are zema and qene?
**Answer:** Zema is sacred chant; qene is sacred poetry — taught in the churches as religious learning.

**2.** Whose hands did much of Gondar's craft work, and in which crafts?
**Answer:** The Bete Israel — builders, potters, basket-makers and metalworkers.
