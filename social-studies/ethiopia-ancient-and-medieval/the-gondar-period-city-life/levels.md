# The Gondar period — city life — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson, with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can do the lesson's outcomes — Describe the royal compound (palaces, abun's and echage's residences, churches in Aksum/Zagwe-like tradition) and the learning churches (zema, qene).; Say Gondar traded on two routes (north Ethiopia; west to Sudan) with a daily market mixing foreign merchants, townspeople and rural folk.; Name the Bete Israel's crafts and the peoples Yohannes I (1667–1682) settled in quarters outside the city. Practice deepens inside the whole lesson: more items per question set, then the harder corners — exact dates, exact names, and the cause-and-effect links between them — until the retelling in 'Say it to your companion' runs clean from memory.
