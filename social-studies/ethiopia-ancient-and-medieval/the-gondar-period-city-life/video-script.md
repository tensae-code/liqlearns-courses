# Video script — The Gondar period — city life

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Inside the walls: palaces and churches of stone.
The abun and the echage live beside the kings.
Outside, churches teach zema and qene.
Every day, the market crowds in —
foreign merchants, townsfolk, farmers.
Bete Israel build, pot, weave and forge.
Many peoples, one city: Gondar.

[Grade 11 depth scene — The Gondarine Period in depth, 1636–1769. The animated map carries the story as the voice speaks.]
Gondar's century at Grade 11 depth: castle-building emperors raising stone palaces in sequence, a court culture of poetry, painting and church debate — and, underneath the splendour, the slow weakening, as regional lords gather the power the castles proclaim. The names to hold: Gondarine Period, regional lords.
