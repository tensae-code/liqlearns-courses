# Hands-on — The Gondar period — city life

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Plan your compound
Draw a plan of a small royal compound: palace, two churchmen's houses, a church. Then add outside it a learning church, a market square and three quarters. Label each in the lesson's words.

## Craft hands
Try one Bete Israel craft in miniature: coil a small clay pot, weave a paper basket strip, or shape a foil ornament. Say which Gondar craft you are honouring.
