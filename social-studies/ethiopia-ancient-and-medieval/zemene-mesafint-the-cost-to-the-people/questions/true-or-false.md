# Zemene Mesafint — the cost to the people — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1.** Harvests were looted by lords' armies.
**Answer: True** — One of the listed burdens.

**2.** National feeling grew stronger as lordships multiplied.
**Answer: False** — National feeling itself declined.

**3.** Culture and crafts flourished in the era's security.
**Answer: False** — Culture and crafts decayed.
