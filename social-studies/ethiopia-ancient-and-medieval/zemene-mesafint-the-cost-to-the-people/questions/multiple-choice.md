# Zemene Mesafint — the cost to the people — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** In the Zemene Mesafint, the cost fell heaviest on —
a) The emperors  b) The peasantry  c) Foreign merchants only  d) The church
**Answer: b) The peasantry** — The peasantry bore it.

**2.** Farmers were forced to —
a) Build churches  b) Join endless campaigns  c) Sail to Zeila  d) Learn qene
**Answer: b) Join endless campaigns** — Forced into lords' campaigns.

**3.** Shiftnet means —
a) A tax  b) Banditry  c) A market day  d) Army service
**Answer: b) Banditry** — Many farmers abandoned farming for shiftnet (banditry).

**4.** Merchants suffered because —
a) Salt ran out  b) They were robbed and taxed at each lord's checkpoint  c) Ships sank  d) Gondar banned markets
**Answer: b) They were robbed and taxed at each lord's checkpoint** — Repeated checkpoint taxes withered trade.

**5.** The book's verdict on the era is —
a) A golden age  b) All-round backwardness  c) Steady progress  d) Peaceful unity
**Answer: b) All-round backwardness** — The book's verdict, plainly given.
