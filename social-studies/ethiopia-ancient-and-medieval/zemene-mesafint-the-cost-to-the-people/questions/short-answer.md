# Zemene Mesafint — the cost to the people — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** Give three burdens the peasantry bore.
**Answer:** Any three: forced to join endless campaigns; harvests looted by lords' armies; fields turned battlefields; provisions owed to every lord.

**2.** Why did trade wither?
**Answer:** Merchants were robbed and taxed repeatedly at each lord's checkpoint, so carrying goods across the divided land stopped paying.
