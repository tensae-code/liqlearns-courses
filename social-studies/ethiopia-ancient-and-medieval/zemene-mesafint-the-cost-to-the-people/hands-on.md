# Hands-on — Zemene Mesafint — the cost to the people

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Five checkpoints
Walk a 'merchant' with a basket of goods past five checkpoint players; each takes one item as 'tax'. Count what arrives. That shrinking basket is why trade withered.

## The farmer's year
Draw a farmer's year as a circle of seasons. Mark where campaigns, looting and provisions strike it. Present your circle and end with the book's verdict.
