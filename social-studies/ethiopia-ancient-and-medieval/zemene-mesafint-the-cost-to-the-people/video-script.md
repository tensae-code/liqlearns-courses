# Video script — Zemene Mesafint — the cost to the people

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Who paid for the princes' wars? The farmer.
Taken to campaigns. Harvest looted.
His field, a battlefield. His grain, owed to every lord.
Some left the plough for banditry.
The merchant paid at every checkpoint — until trade died.
Crafts decayed. National feeling thinned.
The book's verdict: backwardness, all round.
