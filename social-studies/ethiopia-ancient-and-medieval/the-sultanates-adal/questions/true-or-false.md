# The sultanates — Adal — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1.** Adal's founders withdrew to the Harar highlands' snows.
**Answer: False** — They withdrew to the Harar LOWLANDS.

**2.** Adal and Ifat's rulers shared the Walasma line.
**Answer: True** — Both houses were Walasma.

**3.** The split happened before Ifat submitted to the highland kingdom.
**Answer: False** — The split followed Ifat's submission.
