# The sultanates — Adal — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Adal was founded in —
a) 1270  b) 1316  c) 1380  d) 1527
**Answer: c) 1380** — Founded 1380, at the end of the 14th century.

**2.** Adal was born from the split of —
a) The Zagwe dynasty  b) Ifat's ruling house  c) The Dahlak sultans  d) The Gondar court
**Answer: b) Ifat's ruling house** — Ifat's house split after submission.

**3.** The party that accepted highland overlordship was —
a) The farmers  b) The merchants  c) The soldiers  d) The priests
**Answer: b) The merchants** — The merchants accepted; the others withdrew.

**4.** Adal's centres, in order, were —
a) Harar then Dakar  b) Dakar then Harar  c) Zeila then Dakar  d) Dakar then Zeila
**Answer: b) Dakar then Harar** — First centre Dakar, later Harar.

**5.** Both houses of the split belonged to the —
a) Solomonic line  b) Walasma line  c) Makhzumite dynasty  d) Zagwe line
**Answer: b) Walasma line** — Both houses were of the Walasma line.
