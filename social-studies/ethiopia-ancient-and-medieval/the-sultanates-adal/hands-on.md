# Hands-on — The sultanates — Adal

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Fork in the road role-play
Two groups argue Ifat's choice in one sentence each — 'Pay and trade' against 'Refuse and leave'. Then the refusing group moves to a new 'centre' across the room and names it Dakar, then Harar.

## Family tree split
Draw the Walasma line as a tree trunk splitting into two branches: Ifat and Adal. Label the split year 1380 and the question that split it.
