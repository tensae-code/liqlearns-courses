# The sultanates — Adal

## Do it first

A ruling family argues over one question: submit to the highland kingdom, or resist it? The argument splits the family — and one half walks away to found a new state. That state is Adal, and its quarrel will shape two centuries of war.

## Born of a split, 1380

Adal was **founded at the end of the 14th century — in 1380** — when **Ifat's ruling house split** after Ifat's submission to the highland kingdom. One party, **the merchants, accepted the highland overlordship** — tribute was the price of peaceful trade. The other party refused: they **withdrew to the Harar lowlands and founded Adal**. Note the bloodline: **both houses were of the Walasma line** — Adal is Ifat's own family, divided against itself over the question of submission.

## Dakar, then Harar

Adal's **first centre was Dakar**; **later it moved to Harar** — the city that would become the sultanate's heart. Hold Adal's birth in mind as the wars begin: a state born from refusal to submit, planted in the Harar lowlands, facing the highland kingdom across the trade routes. The first phase of the Adal wars is the next lesson.
