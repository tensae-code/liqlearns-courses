# Movements of the Somali, Afar and Argoba — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is Somali?
a) Argoba
b) a Cushitic people of the eastern lowlands and coast
c) Afar
**Answer: b) a Cushitic people of the eastern lowlands and coast**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is pasture?
a) stages
b) grazing land, a first driver of herders' movement
c) clan section
**Answer: b) grazing land, a first driver of herders' movement**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What is corridor?
a) salt
b) a route-valley held and used by a people, as the Afar held the salt road
c) board
**Answer: b) a route-valley held and used by a people, as the Afar held the salt road**
