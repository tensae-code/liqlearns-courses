# Movements of the Somali, Afar and Argoba — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own term.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The term for 'a people of the escarpment settlements' is ______.
**Answer: Argoba**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The term for 'movement by steps over seasons, not single marches' is ______.
**Answer: stages**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The term for 'the settlement pattern later states inherited' is ______.
**Answer: board**
