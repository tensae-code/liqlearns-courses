# Video script — Movements of the Somali, Afar and Argoba

Story-style script in the animated-map spirit: the map opens, peoples and armies move, routes light up. Super-simple voice; made later, when videos are made.

---

[Scene 1 — Peoples of the eastern lowlands. The map and the scene build as the voice speaks.]
East of the highlands, Somali, Afar and Argoba peoples moved and settled across the lowlands and escarpment in this period — herding, trading and farming the corridors between the plateau and the sea.

Their movements redrew the region's human map as surely as any king's campaign.. Watch the names appear one by one: Somali, Afar, Argoba. This is the part of the story to hold.

[Scene 2 — Why peoples move. The map and the scene build as the voice speaks.]
Movement had drivers: pasture and water for herds, trade openings at the ports, pressure from wars, and the pull of kin already settled. Watch the names appear one by one: pasture, clan section, stages. This is the part of the story to hold.

[Scene 3 — Filling the corridors. The map and the scene build as the voice speaks.]
By the period's end, Somali settlement reached deep along the coast and inland valleys, Afar held the salt and caravan corridors, and Argoba communities sat in escarpment towns between the worlds.

The eastern map these movements made is the board on which Harar, the sultanates and later Ethiopia all played.. Watch the names appear one by one: corridor, salt, board. This is the part of the story to hold.
