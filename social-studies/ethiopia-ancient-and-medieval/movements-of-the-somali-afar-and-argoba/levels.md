# Movements of the Somali, Afar and Argoba — sub lessons and depth

This is the depth record of the lesson, re-expressed as sub lessons: the learner proves each sub lesson before the next opens, and practice deepens inside each part (more items, harder corners) without new rungs.

## Sub lesson 1 — Peoples of the eastern lowlands

Pass mark: the learner can retell this part in order and answer its gate-pool questions — the key term is **Somali** (a Cushitic people of the eastern lowlands and coast).

## Sub lesson 2 — Why peoples move

Pass mark: the learner can retell this part in order and answer its gate-pool questions — the key term is **pasture** (grazing land, a first driver of herders' movement).

## Sub lesson 3 — Filling the corridors

Pass mark: the learner can retell this part in order and answer its gate-pool questions — the key term is **corridor** (a route-valley held and used by a people, as the Afar held the salt road).
