# Video script — The sultanates — Dahlak

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Islands in the Red Sea: Dahlak.
Arabs settled them after Adulis fell in 702.
A sultanate rose in the 10th century, living by trade.
Before Zeila, all trade passed here.
Coveted by all, conquered by none — its sultans kept peace.
Until the Ottomans came, in the mid-1500s.
