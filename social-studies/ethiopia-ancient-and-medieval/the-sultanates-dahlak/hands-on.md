# Hands-on — The sultanates — Dahlak

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Hold the door
Play a doorway game: one 'sultan' must let traders (classmates with goods cards) pass a doorway, keeping everyone friendly and taking a small toll token from each. Peaceful toll-keeping is Dahlak's method.

## Island map
Draw a coast and a cluster of offshore islands. Put Dahlak on the biggest island, draw dhow routes to Arabia and to the mainland, and star the islands: 'the door before Zeila'.
