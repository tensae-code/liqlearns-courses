## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say Arabs settled Dahlak after taking Adulis in 702, and a sultanate formed in the 10th century on a trade economy.
2. Say foreign trade ran through Dahlak before Zeila rose.
3. Say the sultans kept independence by peaceful means until Ottoman Turks took Dahlak in the mid-16th century.
