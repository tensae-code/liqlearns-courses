# The sultanates — Dahlak — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** Why did everyone covet the Dahlak islands?
**Answer:** They held a door of the foreign sea trade — before Zeila rose, trade ran through Dahlak.

**2.** When did Dahlak's independence end, and to whom?
**Answer:** In the mid-16th century, to the Ottoman Turks.
