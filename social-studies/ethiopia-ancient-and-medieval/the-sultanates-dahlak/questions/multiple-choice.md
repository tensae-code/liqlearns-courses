# The sultanates — Dahlak — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Arabs settled Dahlak after taking —
a) Zeila in 615  b) Adulis in 702  c) Harar in 1380  d) Gondar in 1636
**Answer: b) Adulis in 702** — Settlement followed the taking of Adulis in 702.

**2.** The Dahlak sultanate formed in the —
a) 7th century  b) 10th century  c) 13th century  d) 16th century
**Answer: b) 10th century** — A Muslim sultanate formed in the 10th century.

**3.** Dahlak's economy rested on —
a) Irrigation farming  b) Trade  c) Iron mining  d) Cattle herding only
**Answer: b) Trade** — A trade economy on the sea door.

**4.** The Dahlak sultans kept independence by —
a) A great navy  b) Peaceful means  c) Mountain fortresses  d) Portuguese help
**Answer: b) Peaceful means** — They balanced their neighbours peacefully.

**5.** Dahlak was finally taken by —
a) Amda Tsion  b) Ottoman Turks  c) The Portuguese  d) Imam Ahmad
**Answer: b) Ottoman Turks** — Ottoman Turks took it in the mid-16th century.
