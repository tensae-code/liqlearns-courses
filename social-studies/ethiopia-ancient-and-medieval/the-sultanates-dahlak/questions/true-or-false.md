# The sultanates — Dahlak — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1.** Before Zeila rose, foreign trade ran through Dahlak.
**Answer: True** — Stated directly.

**2.** Dahlak's sultans fought constant wars to stay independent.
**Answer: False** — They kept independence by peaceful means.

**3.** Both Arab and Ethiopian states coveted Dahlak.
**Answer: True** — Its sea-door position made all sides want it.
