# Video script — Zagwe — the rock-hewn churches of Lalibela

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

At Lalibela, builders did not pile stones up.
They carved down, into one solid rock.
Bete Giyorgis: a cross sunk in stone.
Aksum carved stelae; Zagwe carved churches.
Farms and trade paid for the wonder.
Yet no rule for who rules next — and the dynasty fell.
