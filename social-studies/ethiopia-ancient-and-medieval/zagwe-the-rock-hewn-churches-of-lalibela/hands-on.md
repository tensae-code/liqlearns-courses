# Hands-on — Zagwe — the rock-hewn churches of Lalibela

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Carve down, not up
In a block of soap, a potato, or packed wet sand in a box, carve a small cross-shaped pit downward with a spoon handle. You are working the Lalibela way: removing, not adding.

## Succession rule draft
With family or friends, draft a fair 'succession rule' for a club or team captain: who follows, chosen how, so no struggle follows. The Zagwe fell for want of one.
