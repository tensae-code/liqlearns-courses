# Zagwe — the rock-hewn churches of Lalibela — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1.** The Zagwe spread Christianity by building churches and founding monasteries.
**Answer: True** — That is stated directly.

**2.** Bete Giyorgis was built up from the ground like a tower.
**Answer: False** — It was cut DOWN into the rock.

**3.** The guide says the Zagwe were opposed because of their religion.
**Answer: False** — The key stresses they were opposed as supposed usurpers, not for religion (teacher guide — evidence).
