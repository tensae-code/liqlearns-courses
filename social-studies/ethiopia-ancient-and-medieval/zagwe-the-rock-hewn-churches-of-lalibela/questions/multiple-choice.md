# Zagwe — the rock-hewn churches of Lalibela — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** The Lalibela churches were made by —
a) Piling stones course on course  b) Cutting them from single rock  c) Casting bronze  d) Weaving reeds
**Answer: b) Cutting them from single rock** — They are hewn down into living rock.

**2.** Bete Giyorgis is shaped, from above, like —
a) A circle  b) A cross  c) A star  d) A boat
**Answer: b) A cross** — Its cross shape is cut into the rock.

**3.** The rock churches continued a tradition of —
a) Harari wall building  b) Aksumite rock carving  c) Roman concrete work  d) Egyptian pyramid building
**Answer: b) Aksumite rock carving** — The Zagwe continued Aksum's rock-carving tradition.

**4.** The churches were funded by —
a) Portuguese gold  b) Farming and trade  c) Ottoman taxes  d) Pilgrim fees only
**Answer: b) Farming and trade** — Farming and trade funded the building.

**5.** The book says the Zagwe fell because of —
a) A foreign invasion only  b) No peaceful succession law, and power struggles  c) An earthquake  d) Losing Adulis
**Answer: b) No peaceful succession law, and power struggles** — Those are the book's two stated causes.
