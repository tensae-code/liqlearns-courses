# Zagwe — the rock-hewn churches of Lalibela — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** What does 'cut from single rock' mean for Bete Giyorgis?
**Answer:** The whole church was hewn out of one solid rock, carved downward, not built up from separate stones.

**2.** Which earlier kingdom's tradition did the rock churches continue?
**Answer:** The Aksumite rock-carving tradition.
