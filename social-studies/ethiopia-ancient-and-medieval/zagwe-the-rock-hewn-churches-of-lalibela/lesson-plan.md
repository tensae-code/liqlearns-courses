# Lesson plan — Zagwe — the rock-hewn churches of Lalibela

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Ethiopia: Ancient and Medieval · **Lesson:** Zagwe — the rock-hewn churches of Lalibela
**Place in the curriculum:** Title — Ethiopia: Ancient and Medieval (CURRICULUM.md path; TITLES.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The Zagwe kings **put their wealth and power into churches**.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the lessons before it in this title — the learner has met the periods of Ethiopian history and the states named so far in this title's order. It needs nothing else.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Describe the rock-hewn churches: cut from single rock, Bete Giyorgis cross-shaped, continuing an Aksumite tradition.
2. Say farming and trade funded the churches, and churches and monasteries spread Christianity.
3. Give the fall's causes: no peaceful succession law and power struggles.
