# Shewa grows in the south — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is Shewa?
a) dynasty
b) the southern highland kingdom that grew while the north fragmented
c) puppets
**Answer: b) the southern highland kingdom that grew while the north fragmented**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is Sahle Selassie?
a) expansion
b) the Shewan king who expanded and enriched the kingdom
c) granaries
**Answer: b) the Shewan king who expanded and enriched the kingdom**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What is Menilek?
a) fuse
b) reunify
c) the Shewan king who later reunified Ethiopia
**Answer: c) the Shewan king who later reunified Ethiopia**
