# Shewa grows in the south — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** True or false: Shewa's own ruling line — this is dynasty.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** True or false: in this lesson, Shewa means Gondar's emperors under the warlords, contrasted with Shewa's kings.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** True or false: Shewa's outward growth under its kings — this is expansion.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** True or false: in this lesson, Sahle Selassie means the stored surplus of Shewa's prosperity.
**Answer: False**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** True or false: To join the fragmented land again, as Shewa's line did — this is reunify.
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** True or false: in this lesson, Menilek means the long consequence this short lesson sets burning.
**Answer: False**
