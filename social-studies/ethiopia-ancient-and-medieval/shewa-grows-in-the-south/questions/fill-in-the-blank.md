# Shewa grows in the south — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own term.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The term for 'Gondar's emperors under the warlords, contrasted with Shewa's kings' is ______.
**Answer: puppets**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The term for 'the stored surplus of Shewa's prosperity' is ______.
**Answer: granaries**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The term for 'the long consequence this short lesson sets burning' is ______.
**Answer: fuse**
