# Shewa grows in the south — sub lessons and depth

This is the depth record of the lesson, re-expressed as sub lessons: the learner proves each sub lesson before the next opens, and practice deepens inside each part (more items, harder corners) without new rungs.

## Sub lesson 1 — A kingdom apart

Pass mark: the learner can retell this part in order and answer its gate-pool questions — the key term is **Shewa** (the southern highland kingdom that grew while the north fragmented).

## Sub lesson 2 — Sahle Selassie expands

Pass mark: the learner can retell this part in order and answer its gate-pool questions — the key term is **Sahle Selassie** (the Shewan king who expanded and enriched the kingdom).

## Sub lesson 3 — The kingdom that will reunify Ethiopia

Pass mark: the learner can retell this part in order and answer its gate-pool questions — the key term is **Menilek** (the Shewan king who later reunified Ethiopia).
