# Video script — Shewa grows in the south

Story-style script in the animated-map spirit: the map opens, peoples and armies move, routes light up. Super-simple voice; made later, when videos are made.

---

[Scene 1 — A kingdom apart. The map and the scene build as the voice speaks.]
While the north fragmented in the Era of the Princes, Shewa — the highland kingdom south of the Abbay bend — grew under its own dynasty, largely spared the warlord wars.

Shewa's kings kept a court, an army and a succession while Gondar's emperors were puppets. Watch the names appear one by one: Shewa, dynasty, puppets. This is the part of the story to hold.

[Scene 2 — Sahle Selassie expands. The map and the scene build as the voice speaks.]
Under Sahle Selassie, Shewa expanded and enriched itself: borders pushed outward, trade encouraged, and the kingdom's fields and markets prospered.

He ruled for decades — long reigns build what short ones cannot: roads of custom, full granaries, a name abroad.. Watch the names appear one by one: Sahle Selassie, expansion, granaries. This is the part of the story to hold.

[Scene 3 — The kingdom that will reunify Ethiopia. The map and the scene build as the voice speaks.]
Shewa's line did not stop: from this southern kingdom came Menilek, who would reunify Ethiopia and move its centre south for good.

So this short lesson carries a long fuse — the Era of the Princes ends, in the end, from Shewa.. Watch the names appear one by one: Menilek, reunify, fuse. This is the part of the story to hold.
