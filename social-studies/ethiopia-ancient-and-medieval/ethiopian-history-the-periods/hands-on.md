# Hands-on — Ethiopian history — the periods

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Paper timeline
Stretch a strip of paper or a string across the floor. Mark 1270 about two-thirds along and 1855 near the end. Stand at each mark and name what begins there.

## Name cards
Write each state or power on its own card. Shuffle, then lay the cards in time order without looking at the lesson. Check yourself against the timeline picture.
