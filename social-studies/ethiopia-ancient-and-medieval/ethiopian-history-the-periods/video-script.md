# Video script — Ethiopian history — the periods

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Ethiopia's early story has two great parts.
Ancient history runs to the year 1270.
Punt. Da'amat. Aksum. Zagwe.
Then, in 1270, the medieval age begins.
A restored kingdom. Sultanates. Wars. Gondar.
It ends in 1855.
Two dates — 1270 and 1855 — hold the whole map.
