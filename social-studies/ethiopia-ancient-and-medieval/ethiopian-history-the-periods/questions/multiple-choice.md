# Ethiopian history — the periods — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Ancient Ethiopian history runs up to which year?
a) 1270  b) 1855  c) 1636  d) 702
**Answer: a) 1270** — The ancient period ends and the medieval period begins in 1270.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which of these belongs to the ANCIENT period?
a) Gondar  b) Aksum  c) Zemene Mesafint  d) The Solomonic restoration
**Answer: b) Aksum** — Aksum stands before 1270; the others are medieval.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The medieval period runs from 1270 to —
a) 1299  b) 1543  c) 1855  d) 1706
**Answer: c) 1855** — Medieval history: 1270 to 1855.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** What opens the medieval period in 1270?
a) The founding of Gondar  b) The Solomonic restoration  c) The Oromo expansion  d) The fall of Aksum
**Answer: b) The Solomonic restoration** — Yekuno Amlak's victory in 1270 opens the medieval period.

**5. (Sub lesson 1)** The Zagwe dynasty is famous for —
a) Sea trade with Rome  b) Churches cut from solid rock  c) Founding Gondar  d) The first coins of Ethiopia
**Answer: b) Churches cut from solid rock** — The rock-hewn churches of Lalibela are Zagwe's signature.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** 'Christian highland kingdom' means —
a) A kingdom in the lowlands  b) The Christian kingdom centred in the highlands  c) A Muslim sultanate  d) A city on the coast
**Answer: b) The Christian kingdom centred in the highlands** — That is the term's own gloss.
