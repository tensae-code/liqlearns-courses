# Ethiopian history — the periods — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Punt is the oldest named state in this story.
**Answer: True** — Punt is presented first, as the oldest named state.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Gondar was founded in the ancient period.
**Answer: False** — Gondar was founded in 1636 — medieval.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Zemene Mesafint comes at the END of the medieval period.
**Answer: True** — It runs to 1855, the period's close.
