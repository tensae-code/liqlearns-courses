# Ethiopian history — the periods — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the four powers of the ancient period, in order.
**Answer:** Punt, Da'amat, Aksum, Zagwe.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name two Muslim sultanates of the medieval period.
**Answer:** Any two of: Dahlak, Ifat, Hadiya, Bale, Adal (Shewa is also named in the teacher guide).
