# Gumuz, Shinasha and the gold sheikhdoms of Benishangul — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Contrast the Gumuz and Shinasha land systems in two sentences.
**Answer:** The Gumuz shifted — clearing and cropping new ground as soils tired. The Shinasha settled around cattle and held reserved forests under a no-cutting law.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the three sheikhdoms and the two powers that targeted them, with decades.
**Answer:** Asosa, Benishangul and Khomosha; Egypt in the 1820s and Sudanese-Mahdist forces in the 1880s.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is 'silent commerce' as the book associates it with this region?
**Answer:** The old account of trade conducted without speech — goods left, gold taken — associated with this gold-producing border region since ancient times.
