# Gumuz, Shinasha and the gold sheikhdoms of Benishangul — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Shinasha law allowed anyone to cut the reserved forests.
**Answer: False** — Cutting their trees was forbidden.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The sheikhdoms surrendered their independence to Egypt in the 1820s.
**Answer: False** — They defended their independence against both Egypt and Mahdist penetration.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The region's gold fame reaches back to the days of Punt.
**Answer: True** — Known since Punt; 'silent commerce' is its old association.
