# Hands-on — Gumuz, Shinasha and the gold sheikhdoms of Benishangul

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Two systems, one rainfall
In pairs, defend one system — shifting fields or reserved forests — as the wiser answer to the same land. The class votes, but must cite the lesson for every reason.

## Gold draws maps
On an outline map, mark the gold region and draw the two incoming arrows (Egypt 1820s, Mahdists 1880s). Write under each: what they wanted, what happened.
