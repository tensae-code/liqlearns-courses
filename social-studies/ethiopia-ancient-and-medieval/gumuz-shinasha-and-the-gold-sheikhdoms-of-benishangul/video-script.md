# Video script — Gumuz, Shinasha and the gold sheikhdoms of Benishangul

The story-style script for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list), in the animated-map spirit: the map opens, peoples and armies move, capitals rise, routes light up. Super-simple voice. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The map slides west, down from the highlands into a green lowland of rivers.
Smoke rises from a fresh clearing — Gumuz fields, farmed by fire and movement.
Beside them, a forest stands untouched, guarded by law older than any ministry.
Gold glints in a river pan. Three sheikhdoms rise on the trade: Asosa, Benishangul, Khomosha.
Empires come for the gold — Egypt, then the Mahdists. The sheikhdoms hold. The map keeps their names.

[Grade 11 depth scene — Benishangul-Gumuz and Gambella in depth — the western borderlands. The animated map carries the story as the voice speaks.]
The western borderlands get their Grade 11 page in full: the sheikhdoms of Benishangul-Gumuz, tied by gold and trade to the Sudanese world across the border, and the local chiefs of the Gambella peoples on the rivers of the far west. The names to hold: sheikhdoms, Gambella chiefs.
