# Hands-on — Afar sultanates and Somali society

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Sit in Guurti
Hold a class Guurti on a real small dispute (a borrowed book, a broken rule). Elders are drawn by lot; every adult voice may speak once before the council decides. Afterwards, name the level your council acted at.

## Confederation string
Four groups are four sultanates, each ruling its own village-cards. A drought card strikes: negotiate shared wells as a confederation — no group may command another; agreement must be unanimous.

## Grade 11 depth — Afar sultanates and Somali society in depth

Re-draw this lesson's map or table with the Grade 11 part added in a second colour, then tell the new part's story aloud from the drawing alone. Where the drawing cannot carry the story, the story is not yet learned — re-read that part.
