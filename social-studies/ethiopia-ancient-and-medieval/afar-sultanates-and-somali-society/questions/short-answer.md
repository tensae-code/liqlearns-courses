# Afar sultanates and Somali society — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the four Afar sultanates the book names.
**Answer:** Aussa, Bidu, Tadjoura and Rahaito.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Describe the Guurti in three phrases.
**Answer:** A council of elders; deciding criminal and civil cases; highly democratic with equal adult male participation, at sub-clan, clan and inter-clan levels.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Why does the book call the Afar land strategically weighted and a cradle of humankind?
**Answer:** It sits at the Horn's Red Sea gateway with abundant resources — and its earth holds the early fossil record (the Afar finds of this course).
