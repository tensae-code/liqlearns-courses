# Afar sultanates and Somali society — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Afar Triangle spans —
a) Ethiopia, Eritrea and Djibouti  b) Ethiopia, Kenya and Somalia  c) Sudan, Egypt and Libya
**Answer: a) Ethiopia, Eritrea and Djibouti** — Three modern states share the Triangle.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The Afar sultanates named include —
a) Asosa and Khomosha  b) Aussa, Bidu, Tadjoura and Rahaito  c) Ifat and Dawaro
**Answer: b) Aussa, Bidu, Tadjoura and Rahaito** — A confederation, not one throne.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The Afar are presented as predominantly —
a) Muslim pastoralists  b) Christian farmers  c) city merchants
**Answer: a) Muslim pastoralists** — Pastoralists in sultanates.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The Guurti is —
a) a clan chief's palace guard  b) a council of elders deciding criminal and civil cases  c) a market tax
**Answer: b) a council of elders deciding criminal and civil cases** — Elders deciding cases.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** In the Guurti, participation was —
a) for sultans only  b) equal for all adult males  c) by lot
**Answer: b) equal for all adult males** — Highly democratic, in the book's words.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** The Guurti worked at — levels.
a) only village  b) sub-clan, clan and inter-clan  c) only international
**Answer: b) sub-clan, clan and inter-clan** — Three nested levels.
