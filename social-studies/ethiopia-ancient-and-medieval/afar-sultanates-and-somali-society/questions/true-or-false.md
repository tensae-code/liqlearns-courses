# Afar sultanates and Somali society — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** One sultan ruled the whole Afar Triangle from a capital.
**Answer: False** — A confederation of sultanates, each of several villages.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Somali society was only pastoralist.
**Answer: False** — It combined pastoralists with settled farming and trading communities.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The Guurti decided both criminal and civil cases.
**Answer: True** — Both kinds, at three levels.
