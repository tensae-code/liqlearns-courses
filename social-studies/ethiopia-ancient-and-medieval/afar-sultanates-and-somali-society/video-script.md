# Video script — Afar sultanates and Somali society

The story-style script for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list), in the animated-map spirit: the map opens, peoples and armies move, capitals rise, routes light up. Super-simple voice. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The map drops into the Afar Triangle — white salt, black lava, the hottest land on earth.
Tents and herds. Four names rise: Aussa, Bidu, Tadjoura, Rahaito. No capital. A confederation.
East, under a great tree, elders sit in a ring. The Guurti is in session.
A herder speaks. A farmer speaks. Every adult male has his say — then the council decides.
Two systems, one east: many thrones linked, and a council of equals.
