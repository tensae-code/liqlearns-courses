# Hands-on — The Adal wars — the first phase

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Timeline of blows
Make a washing-line timeline 1314–1527. Hang a card for each named fight in order. Step along it, saying winner and loser at each card.

## Two causes debate
Two speakers: one argues 'it was about trade wealth', the other 'it was about independence'. Each must use the book's causes — then agree both causes stood together.
