# The Adal wars — the first phase — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** The first phase of the Adal wars runs —
a) 1270–1314  b) 1314–1527  c) 1527–1543  d) 1380–1430
**Answer: b) 1314–1527** — First phase: 1314–1527.

**2.** One cause was control of —
a) The Nile  b) The Zeila trade route and its wealth  c) The Dahlak islands only  d) The Gondar market
**Answer: b) The Zeila trade route and its wealth** — The route's wealth is the first named cause.

**3.** Dawit defeated Sa'ad al-Din in —
a) 1387  b) 1403  c) 1430  d) 1527
**Answer: b) 1403** — 1387 Haqq al-Din II; 1403 Sa'ad al-Din; 1430 Ahmad.

**4.** Zara Yaqob fought —
a) Only Dahlak  b) Sultan Ahmad and Garad Mahiko of Hadiya  c) The Portuguese  d) The Beja
**Answer: b) Sultan Ahmad and Garad Mahiko of Hadiya** — Both are named against Zara Yaqob.

**5.** In the first phase, the upper hand lay with —
a) Adal  b) The highland kingdom  c) Ifat alone  d) The Ottomans
**Answer: b) The highland kingdom** — The highland kingdom was uppermost throughout phase one.
