# The Adal wars — the first phase — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** Name the two causes of the Adal wars.
**Answer:** Control of the Zeila trade route and its wealth; the highland kingdom's drive to subjugate the sultanates against their drive to recover land and independence.

**2.** Which kings carry the wars after Zara Yaqob in this phase?
**Answer:** Ba'eda Maryam, Eskinder and Na'od.
