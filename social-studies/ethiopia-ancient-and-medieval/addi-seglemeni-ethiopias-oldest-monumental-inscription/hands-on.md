# Hands-on — Addi-Seglemeni — Ethiopia's oldest monumental inscription

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Cut a message in 'stone'
On thick card or a flat stone, design a monumental inscription for your school: five words maximum, meant to be read in 500 years. Say what it proves about you to a future archaeologist — and what it hides.

## Ten-kilometre questions
On a local map, mark a point roughly 10 km from a known centre. List what a future digger there could and could not learn about the centre from that one site.
