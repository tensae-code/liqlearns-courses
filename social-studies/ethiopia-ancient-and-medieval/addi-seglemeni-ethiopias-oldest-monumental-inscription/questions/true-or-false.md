# Addi-Seglemeni — Ethiopia's oldest monumental inscription — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The slab at Addi-Seglemeni was a wooden board.
**Answer: False** — A stone slab.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Ethiopia's outside relations began only with the Aksumite kingdom.
**Answer: False** — The guide places them before Aksum.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's find rests on teacher-guide evidence.
**Answer: True** — Labelled as such in its sources.
