# Addi-Seglemeni — Ethiopia's oldest monumental inscription — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Where is Addi-Seglemeni, and what was found there?
**Answer:** 10 km south-west of Aksum; a stone slab bearing the oldest Ethiopian monumental inscription.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Who initiated these pre-Aksumite sites, and from where had they come?
**Answer:** The Habashat and Agazi tribes, who migrated into Ethiopia from Saudi Arabia (southern Arabia).

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What does a monumental inscription prove about the people who cut it?
**Answer:** They commanded writing, masons and public authority — an organised polity, not a passing camp.
