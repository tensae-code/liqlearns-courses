# Addi-Seglemeni — Ethiopia's oldest monumental inscription — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Addi-Seglemeni lies — of Aksum.
a) 10 km south-west  b) 100 km north  c) on the coast
**Answer: a) 10 km south-west** — The guide's answer.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What was discovered there?
a) a gold crown  b) the oldest Ethiopian monumental inscription  c) a Roman road
**Answer: b) the oldest Ethiopian monumental inscription** — Cut in a stone slab.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The sites were initiated by —
a) the Habashat and Agazi  b) the Zagwe  c) the Portuguese
**Answer: a) the Habashat and Agazi** — Migrants from southern Arabia.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The Habashat and Agazi migrated from —
a) Egypt  b) Saudi Arabia (southern Arabia)  c) Mesopotamia
**Answer: b) Saudi Arabia (southern Arabia)** — Across the Red Sea.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The guide says Ethiopia sought relations with the outside world —
a) only after Aksum fell  b) from ancient times, even before Aksum  c) only in the 19th century
**Answer: b) from ancient times, even before Aksum** — Centre of the Horn, active early.

**6. (Sub lesson 1)** A monumental inscription is —
a) a private diary  b) formal public writing cut to outlast its maker  c) a trade receipt
**Answer: b) formal public writing cut to outlast its maker** — Public and permanent by design.
