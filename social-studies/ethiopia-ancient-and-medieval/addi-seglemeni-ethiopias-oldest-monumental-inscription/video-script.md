# Video script — Addi-Seglemeni — Ethiopia's oldest monumental inscription

The story-style script for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list), in the animated-map spirit: the map opens, peoples and armies move, capitals rise, routes light up. Super-simple voice. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The map opens on Aksum. The camera slides — ten kilometres south-west. A pin drops: Addi-Seglemeni.
Hands brush soil from a stone slab. Letters appear, cut deep, older than the kingdom next door.
Across the Red Sea, the map draws its arrow: Habashat, Agazi — Arabia to the highlands.
Before Aksum, Ethiopia was already writing in public. The stone says so.
