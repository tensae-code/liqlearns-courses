# The sultanates — Ifat — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** Why did controlling the routes make Ifat rich and strong?
**Answer:** All trade between the Ethiopian interior and Zeila crossed Ifat, so Ifat controlled — and could tax — the region's trade.

**2.** In what year does the guide say Ifat was made tributary?
**Answer:** 1332 (teacher guide — evidence).
