# The sultanates — Ifat — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1.** Ifat was the weakest of the sultanates.
**Answer: False** — It was the strongest and foremost of them.

**2.** Ifat dominated its neighbours in the early 14th century.
**Answer: True** — Before Amda Tsion's campaigns.

**3.** The guide places Ifat toward the Awash valley.
**Answer: True** — North-eastern foothills of the Shewan plateau to the Awash valley (teacher guide — evidence).
