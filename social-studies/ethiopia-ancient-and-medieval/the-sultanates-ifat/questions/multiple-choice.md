# The sultanates — Ifat — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Ifat was founded by —
a) Sabr al-Din  b) Umar Walasma  c) Mera Tekle Haymanot  d) Imam Ahmad
**Answer: b) Umar Walasma** — Umar Walasma founded it in the mid-13th century.

**2.** Ifat was founded in the —
a) 10th century  b) Mid-13th century  c) 1380s  d) 1520s
**Answer: b) Mid-13th century** — Founded mid-13th century.

**3.** Ifat's strength came from —
a) Its navy  b) The interior–Zeila trade routes crossing its territory  c) Its rock churches  d) Portuguese guns
**Answer: b) The interior–Zeila trade routes crossing its territory** — Control of the routes meant control of the region's trade.

**4.** Ifat's supremacy was ended by —
a) The Zagwe  b) Amda Tsion's campaigns  c) The Ottomans  d) The Oromo expansion
**Answer: b) Amda Tsion's campaigns** — Amda Tsion ended its supremacy.

**5.** Sultan Sabr al-Din —
a) Founded Ifat  b) Fought for independence, was defeated and made tributary  c) Took Dahlak  d) Founded Adal
**Answer: b) Fought for independence, was defeated and made tributary** — That is his recorded fate.
