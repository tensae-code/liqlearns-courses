# Hands-on — The sultanates — Ifat

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Toll road game
Lay a rope 'road' across the room from 'interior' to 'Zeila'. Travellers carry goods cards along it; the 'Ifat' player collects one counter per load — then an 'Amda Tsion' player arrives and the toll changes hands. Discuss who grew rich and why.

## Foothills map
Draw a plateau edge and a river valley (the Awash). Place Ifat on the foothills between plateau and coast road, as the guide describes it.
