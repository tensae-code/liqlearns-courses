# Video script — The sultanates — Ifat

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Mid-1200s: Umar Walasma founds Ifat.
The road to Zeila runs through its land.
Every caravan pays. Ifat grows strongest.
Early 1300s: Ifat dominates its neighbours.
Then Amda Tsion comes.
Sabr al-Din fights, falls, pays tribute.
The road made Ifat — and the road undid it.
