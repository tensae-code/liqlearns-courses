# Ethiopia's language families — Cushitic, Semitic, Omotic — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** All Ethiopian languages belong to one family.
**Answer: False** — Two super-families; three Afro-Asiatic branches in this lesson.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Cushitic divides into North, Central and East.
**Answer: True** — The book's division.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Amharic is a northern Semitic language in this classification.
**Answer: False** — Amharic is listed among the southern Semitic languages.
**4. (Sub lesson 4)** True or false: The language family of the western lowlands — this is Nilo-Saharan.
**Answer: True**
