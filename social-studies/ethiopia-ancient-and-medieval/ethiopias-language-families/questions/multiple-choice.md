# Ethiopia's language families — Cushitic, Semitic, Omotic — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Ethiopia and the Horn hold over — languages.
a) 20  b) 80  c) 200
**Answer: b) 80** — Over 80.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Ethiopia's languages belong to the — and — super-families.
a) Indo-European, Sino-Tibetan  b) Afro-Asiatic, Nilo-Saharan  c) Bantu, Khoisan
**Answer: b) Afro-Asiatic, Nilo-Saharan** — The book's two.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The largest branch by area and speakers is —
a) Semitic  b) Omotic  c) Cushitic
**Answer: c) Cushitic** — North, Central and East Cushitic.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Ge'ez is —
a) an Omotic language  b) the church language, northern Semitic  c) extinct and forgotten
**Answer: b) the church language, northern Semitic** — Ethiopia's classical written tongue.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Gafat is the book's example of —
a) a language family  b) an extinct language  c) a trade language
**Answer: b) an extinct language** — Southern Semitic, no longer spoken.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** Omotic centres on —, with Shinasha far north.
a) the Omo basin  b) the Red Sea coast  c) Lake Tana
**Answer: a) the Omo basin** — The Omo basin; Shinasha the outlier.
