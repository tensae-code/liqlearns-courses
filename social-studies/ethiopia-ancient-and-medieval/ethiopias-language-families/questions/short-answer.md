# Ethiopia's language families — Cushitic, Semitic, Omotic — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the three Afro-Asiatic branches of Ethiopian languages.
**Answer:** Cushitic, Semitic and Omotic.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** List the northern and southern Semitic languages the book names.
**Answer:** Northern: Ge'ez, Tigre, Tigrinya. Southern: Amharic, Argobba, Gafat, Gurage, Silt'e, Harari, Zay.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What happens to a language when its speaker numbers fall far enough, and which language is the example?
**Answer:** It becomes endangered and may go extinct; Gafat is the worked example.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** How does the book rank the Cushitic branch, and into what three parts does it divide?
**Answer:** Cushitic is the largest branch by area and by speakers, divided into North, Central and East Cushitic.
