# Hands-on — Ethiopia's language families — Cushitic, Semitic, Omotic

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Language census
Survey the class or family: which languages are spoken, by whom, where learned? Tally them onto the family tree as living data.

## The Gafat warning
In groups, plan a rescue for an imaginary endangered language with twelve elderly speakers: what would you record first, and why? Connect each step to how Gafat was lost.

## Grade 11 depth — Language families in depth — the key to the whole region

Re-draw this lesson's map or table with the Grade 11 part added in a second colour, then tell the new part's story aloud from the drawing alone. Where the drawing cannot carry the story, the story is not yet learned — re-read that part.
