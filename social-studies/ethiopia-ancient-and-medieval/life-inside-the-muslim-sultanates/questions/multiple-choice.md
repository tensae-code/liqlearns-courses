# Life inside the Muslim sultanates — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is sultanate?
a) caravan calendar
b) council
c) a state ruled by a sultan, as Ifat and Adal were
**Answer: c) a state ruled by a sultan, as Ifat and Adal were**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is escarpment?
a) production
b) crafts
c) the steep edge between highland and lowland where much farming sat
**Answer: c) the steep edge between highland and lowland where much farming sat**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What is Islamic learning?
a) frontier
b) Harar
c) the mosque-school tradition of the sultanates
**Answer: c) the mosque-school tradition of the sultanates**
