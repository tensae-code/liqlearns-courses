# Life inside the Muslim sultanates — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** True or false: The elders and officials advising and checking the ruler — this is council.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** True or false: in this lesson, sultanate means the seasonal rhythm around which sultanate towns organised.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** True or false: in this lesson, escarpment means the farming and making on which the sultanates' wealth rested.
