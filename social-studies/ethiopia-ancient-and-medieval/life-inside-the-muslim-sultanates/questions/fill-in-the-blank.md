# Life inside the Muslim sultanates — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own term.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The term for 'the seasonal rhythm around which sultanate towns organised' is ______.
**Answer: caravan calendar**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The term for 'the farming and making on which the sultanates' wealth rested' is ______.
**Answer: production**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The term for 'the contested edge between sultanates and highland kingdom' is ______.
**Answer: frontier**
