# Video script — Life inside the Muslim sultanates

Story-style script in the animated-map spirit: the map opens, peoples and armies move, routes light up. Super-simple voice; made later, when videos are made.

---

[Scene 1 — Rulers, councils and towns. The map and the scene build as the voice speaks.]
Inside the eastern sultanates, sultans ruled with councils of elders and officials, and towns grew at the trade's nodes — market towns where caravans were taxed, warehoused and sent on.

These were working states, not camps: law, coin, markets and mosques, organised around the caravan calendar.. Watch the names appear one by one: sultanate, council, caravan calendar. This is the part of the story to hold.

[Scene 2 — Fields, herds and crafts. The map and the scene build as the voice speaks.]
Most people farmed and herded: grain on the escarpment fields, livestock in the lowlands, and crafts — weaving, metalwork, leather — in the towns.

The sultanates' wealth rested on this ordinary production as much as on the glamorous caravan trade it taxed.. Watch the names appear one by one: escarpment, crafts, production. This is the part of the story to hold.

[Scene 3 — Scholarly Islam. The map and the scene build as the voice speaks.]
The sultanates were centres of Islamic learning: mosques and schools taught the Qur'an and law, and scholars linked the region to Harar, Arabia and the wider Muslim world.

Faith here was government, education and trade-network at once — the same triple role Christianity played in the highland kingdom across the frontier.. Watch the names appear one by one: Islamic learning, Harar, frontier. This is the part of the story to hold.
