# How the Christian Highland Kingdom was governed — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own term.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The term for 'the court's camp-to-camp progress through the regions' is ______.
**Answer: moving capital**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The term for 'the payment in kind or labour owed upward through the system' is ______.
**Answer: tribute**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The term for 'the strain when gult-holders squeezed the peasantry too hard' is ______.
**Answer: friction**
