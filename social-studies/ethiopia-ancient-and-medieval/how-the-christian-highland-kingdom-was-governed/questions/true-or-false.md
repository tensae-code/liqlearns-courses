# How the Christian Highland Kingdom was governed — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** True or false: The king's travelling household, the state's centre — this is court.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** True or false: in this lesson, negus means the court's camp-to-camp progress through the regions.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** True or false: A royal grant of tribute-rights over a district — this is gult.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** True or false: in this lesson, rist means the payment in kind or labour owed upward through the system.
**Answer: False**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** True or false: To grant land and rights, as kings endowed churches — this is endow.
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** True or false: in this lesson, church–state partnership means the strain when gult-holders squeezed the peasantry too hard.
**Answer: False**
