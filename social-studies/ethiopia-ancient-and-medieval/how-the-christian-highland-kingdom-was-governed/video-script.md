# Video script — How the Christian Highland Kingdom was governed

Story-style script in the animated-map spirit: the map opens, peoples and armies move, routes light up. Super-simple voice; made later, when videos are made.

---

[Scene 1 — The negus and his court. The map and the scene build as the voice speaks.]
The restored kingdom was ruled by the negus — the king of kings — whose court was the state's centre: appointing governors, judging great cases, granting land, and leading the army in war.

For centuries the capital moved with the court, camp to camp, consuming each region's surplus in turn — government as a travelling city.. Watch the names appear one by one: negus, court, moving capital. This is the part of the story to hold.

[Scene 2 — Gult and rist — two kinds of land right. The map and the scene build as the voice speaks.]
Land held the system together. Watch the names appear one by one: rist, gult, tribute. This is the part of the story to hold.

[Scene 3 — Church and state, tribute and tax. The map and the scene build as the voice speaks.]
 Church and kingdom were partners: the church held great gult grants, kept learning and legitimacy, and crowned and counselled kings; the state protected and endowed the church.

Tribute flowed up from peasant fields through gult-holders to court and camp — the kingdom's fuel, and its friction when holders squeezed too hard.. Watch the names appear one by one: church–state partnership, endow, friction. This is the part of the story to hold.
