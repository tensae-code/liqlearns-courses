# Aksum — the stelae — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** The largest Aksum stela measured —
a) 22 m  b) 24 m  c) 33 m  d) 12 m
**Answer: c) 33 m** — 33 m — now fallen and broken.

**2.** The stela that STILL STANDS at Aksum is —
a) 33 m  b) 24 m  c) 22 m  d) 40 m
**Answer: b) 24 m** — The 24 m stela still stands.

**3.** The stela taken to Italy was returned in —
a) 1636  b) 1855  c) 2005  d) 1270
**Answer: c) 2005** — The 22 m stela came home in 2005.

**4.** The stelae show that Aksum had —
a) No craftsmen  b) Strong organisation, skill and wealth  c) Only farmers  d) No kings
**Answer: b) Strong organisation, skill and wealth** — Raising such stones takes all three.

**5.** Aksum emerged in —
a) the 1st century AD  b) the 4th century BC  c) 1270 AD
**Answer: a) the 1st century AD** — Its centre the city of Aksum.

**6.** Between the 4th and 6th centuries Aksum expanded —
a) only within Tigray  b) beyond the Red Sea into southern Arabia, west to the Abay, north to Eritrea's tip, south to northern Shewa  c) to Egypt and Persia
**Answer: b) beyond the Red Sea into southern Arabia, west to the Abay, north to Eritrea's tip, south to northern Shewa** — Four directions of reach.

**7.** Aksum's port was —
a) Zeila  b) Adulis  c) Massawa
**Answer: b) Adulis** — Red Sea trade through Adulis made it rich.

**8.** Its economy rested first on —
a) slave exports  b) plough agriculture and tribute  c) mining
**Answer: b) plough agriculture and tribute** — Then increasingly on trade.
