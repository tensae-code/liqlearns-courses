# Aksum — the stelae — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1.** The largest stela still stands today.
**Answer: False** — The largest (33 m) fell and broke; the 24 m one stands.

**2.** One Aksum stela was taken to Italy and later returned.
**Answer: True** — The 22 m stela, returned in 2005.

**3.** A stela of 24 m is about twelve people high.
**Answer: True** — A person is under 2 m; 24 m is more than twelve people.
