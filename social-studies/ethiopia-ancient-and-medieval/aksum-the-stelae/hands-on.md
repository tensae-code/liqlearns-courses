# Hands-on — Aksum — the stelae

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Twelve people high
Lie down head-to-toe with friends or measure with a rope: how long is a line of twelve people? That is about the height of the standing stela.

## Raise a mini-stela
Stand a tall book or carved stick upright in soft earth using only string and small sticks as props. Feel how hard 'just standing it up' is — then think of 24 metres of stone.

## Three legs of power (Grade 9)
Build a three-legged stool diagram for Aksum: plough agriculture, tribute, Adulis trade. Under each leg, write one fact from the Grade 9 part. Then answer: which leg grew stronger over time, and what does the book say it did to Aksum?
