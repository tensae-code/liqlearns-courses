# Aksum — the stelae — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson, with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can do the lesson's outcomes — Give the three stelae heights: 33 m (fallen and broken), 24 m (still standing), 22 m (returned from Italy in 2005).; Explain what the stelae show about Aksum's organisation, skill and wealth. Practice deepens inside the whole lesson: more items per question set, then the harder corners — exact dates, exact names, and the cause-and-effect links between them — until the retelling in 'Say it to your companion' runs clean from memory.
