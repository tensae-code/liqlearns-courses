# Video script — Aksum — the stelae

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

At Aksum, stones touch the sky.
The greatest, 33 metres, fell and broke.
The second, 24 metres, still stands.
The third, 22 metres, was taken to Italy —
and came home in 2005.
Carved power. That is Aksum.
