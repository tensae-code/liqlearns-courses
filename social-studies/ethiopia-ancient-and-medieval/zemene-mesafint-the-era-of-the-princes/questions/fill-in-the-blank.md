# Zemene Mesafint — the era of the princes — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word or number.

**1.** The era of the princes began in ____.
**Answer: 1769**

**2.** Dejazmach ____ of Qwara was among the defenders.
**Answer: Kassa Hailu**

**3. (Grade 11 depth)** The term for 'the dynasty of regional rulers who dominated the era's middle' is ______.
**Answer: Yejju lords**
**4. (Grade 9 depth)** The Yejju dynasty is also called the ____.
**Answer: Warra Sheh**
