# Zemene Mesafint — the era of the princes — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1.** Central power grew stronger during the Zemene Mesafint.
**Answer: False** — Central power withered; the land split.

**2.** Kassa Hailu of Qwara was among the local defenders.
**Answer: True** — Named with Wube and Kinfu.

**3.** The local defenders defeated Egypt cheaply and safely.
**Answer: False** — They paid with their lives.

**4. (Grade 11 depth)** True or false: The dynasty of regional rulers who dominated the era's middle — this is Yejju lords.
**Answer: True**
**5. (Grade 9 depth)** True or false: At its strongest under Ras Gugsa (1803–1825), the Yejju dynasty was the leading power of the Zemene-Mesafint.
**Answer: True**
