# Zemene Mesafint — the era of the princes — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** What does it mean that the rasoch were 'king-makers and king-breakers'?
**Answer:** They raised emperors up and cast them down — the lords, not the kings, held real power.

**2.** Why did defence fall on local lords?
**Answer:** There was no effective central power or army; Muhammad Ali's advance had to be met by the lords of each region.
