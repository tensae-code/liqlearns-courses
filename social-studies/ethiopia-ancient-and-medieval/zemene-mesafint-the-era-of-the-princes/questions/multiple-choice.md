# Zemene Mesafint — the era of the princes — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** The Zemene Mesafint ran —
a) 1636–1706  b) 1769–1855  c) 1527–1543  d) 1270–1299
**Answer: b) 1769–1855** — The era of the princes: 1769–1855.

**2.** In this era, real power lay with —
a) The emperors in Gondar  b) The rasoch, the regional lords  c) The Portuguese  d) The sultans of Adal
**Answer: b) The rasoch, the regional lords** — King-makers and king-breakers.

**3.** Some lords even conducted —
a) Their own foreign relations  b) Sea trade to China  c) Crusades  d) Coin reform
**Answer: a) Their own foreign relations** — Without Gondar's recognition.

**4.** Muhammad Ali of Egypt strengthened from —
a) 1706  b) 1821  c) 1855  d) 1769
**Answer: b) 1821** — Strengthening from 1821.

**5.** Muhammad Ali took —
a) Aksum and Yeha  b) Mitsiwa and Harar  c) Gondar and Lasta  d) Zeila and Berbera
**Answer: b) Mitsiwa and Harar** — He controlled the Red Sea route and took Mitsiwa and Harar.

**6. (Grade 11 depth)** What is Ras Mikael Sehul?
a) the first great warlord of the Era of the Princes
b) the dynasty of regional rulers who dominated the era's middle
c) a detail belonging to a different lesson in this course
**Answer: a) the first great warlord of the Era of the Princes**
