# Hands-on — Zemene Mesafint — the era of the princes

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Crown and power game
One player wears a paper crown but may not move; three 'lords' hold the string-tokens of army, tax and foreign letters. Discuss: who is really ruling? Then swap the tokens to the crown and feel the difference.

## Defenders' roll
Write the three defenders' names on a honour card with their deed in one line each. Read the roll aloud, slowly, the way names of the fallen are read.

## Grade 11 depth — Zemene Mesafint in depth, 1769–1855

Re-draw this lesson's map or table with the Grade 11 part added in a second colour, then tell the new part's story aloud from the drawing alone. Where the drawing cannot carry the story, the story is not yet learned — re-read that part.
