# Video script — Zemene Mesafint — the era of the princes

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

1769. A king is killed by the lord called to save him.
Now the lords rule: makers and breakers of kings.
The land splits into territories.
Egypt's Muhammad Ali seizes the sea road.
Local lords defend the land — and die for it.
This is Zemene Mesafint, to 1855.
