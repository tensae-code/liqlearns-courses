# Zemene Mesafint — the era of the princes — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson, with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can do the lesson's outcomes — Give the Zemene Mesafint span 1769–1855 and explain: central power withered, the land split, rasoch made and broke kings.; Say some lords ran their own foreign relations without Gondar's recognition.; Say Muhammad Ali of Egypt (from 1821) controlled the Red Sea route and took Mitsiwa and Harar; defence fell to local lords including Wube, Kinfu and Kassa Hailu, who paid with their lives. Practice deepens inside the whole lesson: more items per question set, then the harder corners — exact dates, exact names, and the cause-and-effect links between them — until the retelling in 'Say it to your companion' runs clean from memory.
