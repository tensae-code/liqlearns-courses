# Hands-on — The sultanates — Shewa, the oldest sultanate

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Elder's timeline
Add Shewa to your sultanate timeline at 897 — far left of Dahlak (10th c.) and Ifat (mid-13th c.). Feel how much older 'oldest' is.

## Gateway stones
Place two 'gateway' stones (Dahlak, Zeila) at a doorway and ten name cards behind them. Read the guide's list aloud as each card passes the gate.
