# The sultanates — Shewa, the oldest sultanate — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson, with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can do the lesson's outcomes — State the guide's Shewa entry: oldest sultanate, Makhzumite dynasty, founded about 897 AD, rulers titled Sultan.; Say Shewa related to Damot, fought Ifat as its worst enemy, and was defeated and ended by Ifat about 1280.; Name the guide's two gateways (Dahlak, Zeila — Zeila main) and its wider sultanate list. Practice deepens inside the whole lesson: more items per question set, then the harder corners — exact dates, exact names, and the cause-and-effect links between them — until the retelling in 'Say it to your companion' runs clean from memory.
