## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the guide's Shewa entry: oldest sultanate, Makhzumite dynasty, founded about 897 AD, rulers titled Sultan.
2. Say Shewa related to Damot, fought Ifat as its worst enemy, and was defeated and ended by Ifat about 1280.
3. Name the guide's two gateways (Dahlak, Zeila — Zeila main) and its wider sultanate list.
