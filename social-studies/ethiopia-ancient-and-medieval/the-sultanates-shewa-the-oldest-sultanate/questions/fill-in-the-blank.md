# The sultanates — Shewa, the oldest sultanate — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word or number.

**1.** Shewa was founded by the ____ dynasty.
**Answer: Makhzumite**

**2.** Shewa was ended by Ifat around the year ____.
**Answer: 1280**

**3. (Grade 11 depth)** The term for 'the sultanate that absorbed Shewa' is ______.
**Answer: Ifat**
