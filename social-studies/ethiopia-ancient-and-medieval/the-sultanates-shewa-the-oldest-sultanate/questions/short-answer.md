# The sultanates — Shewa, the oldest sultanate — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** With which kingdom did Shewa have a relationship?
**Answer:** The kingdom of Damot (teacher guide — evidence).

**2.** Name four sultanates from the guide's Zeila-route list besides Shewa.
**Answer:** Any four of: Ifat, Adal, Fatagar, Arababini (Arbabini), Dawaro, Dara, Sharka, Bali, Hadiya.
