# The sultanates — Shewa, the oldest sultanate — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Shewa was founded by which dynasty?
a) Walasma  b) Makhzumite  c) Solomonic  d) Zagwe
**Answer: b) Makhzumite** — Founded by the Makhzumite dynasty (teacher guide — evidence).

**2.** The guide dates Shewa's founding to about —
a) 702  b) 897 AD  c) 1150  d) 1380
**Answer: b) 897 AD** — Around 897 AD (teacher guide — evidence).

**3.** Shewa's rulers used the title —
a) Garad  b) Sultan  c) Mukarrib  d) Abba Gada
**Answer: b) Sultan** — Its rulers used the title Sultan.

**4.** Shewa's worst enemy was —
a) Damot  b) Ifat  c) Adal  d) Dahlak
**Answer: b) Ifat** — Ifat was its worst enemy — and its ender.

**5.** Shewa was defeated and ended by Ifat about —
a) 1150  b) 1280  c) 1332  d) 1527
**Answer: b) 1280** — Around 1280 (teacher guide — evidence).
