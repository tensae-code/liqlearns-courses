# Video script — The sultanates — Shewa, the oldest sultanate

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

One sultanate is older than all the rest: Shewa.
The guide keys it: Makhzumite dynasty, about 897.
Its rulers took the title Sultan.
Its friend: Damot. Its worst enemy: Ifat.
About 1280, Ifat ended it.
The oldest sultanate — known from the guide alone.

[Grade 11 depth scene — The Sultanate of Shewa in depth. The animated map carries the story as the voice speaks.]
The Grade 11 depth sets Shewa's sultanate in its full standing: from the 9th–10th century, the earliest known Muslim state in the region, ruled by its Makhzumite dynasty on the highland's eastern edge — proof that Islam's Ethiopian history begins in organised states, not only in trade. The names to hold: Makhzumite dynasty, Ifat.
