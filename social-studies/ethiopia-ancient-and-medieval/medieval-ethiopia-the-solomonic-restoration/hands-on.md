# Hands-on — Medieval Ethiopia — the Solomonic restoration

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Name as argument
Invent two names for the same school team — one plain, one that claims a glorious past ('The Restored Champions'). Discuss which name does more work, as 'Solomonic' did.

## Five in five game
Deal five cards as five 'kings'. Time one minute per card and try to complete a small task for each reign before the time runs out — feel why five reigns in five years meant chaos.
