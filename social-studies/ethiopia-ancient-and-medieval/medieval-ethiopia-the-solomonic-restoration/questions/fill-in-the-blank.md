# Medieval Ethiopia — the Solomonic restoration — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word or number.

**1.** Yekuno Amlak handed power to his son ____ Tsion.
**Answer: Yagbe'a**

**2.** The crisis of five reigns in five years ran 1294–____.
**Answer: 1299**

**3. (Grade 11 depth)** The term for 'the Glory of Kings — the dynasty's legitimating tradition' is ______.
**Answer: Kebra Nagast**
