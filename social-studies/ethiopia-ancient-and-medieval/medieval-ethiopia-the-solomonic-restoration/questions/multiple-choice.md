# Medieval Ethiopia — the Solomonic restoration — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Yekuno Amlak defeated the last Zagwe king in —
a) 1150  b) 1270  c) 1314  d) 1380
**Answer: b) 1270** — 1270 — the restoration year.

**2.** The last Zagwe king was —
a) Lalibela  b) Yetbarak  c) Mera Tekle Haymanot  d) Ezana
**Answer: b) Yetbarak** — Yetbarak was defeated in 1270.

**3.** The name 'Solomonic' was chosen to —
a) Honour a general  b) Claim legitimacy and cast the Zagwe as usurpers  c) Mark a new capital  d) Please Egypt
**Answer: b) Claim legitimacy and cast the Zagwe as usurpers** — That is the book's own explanation.

**4.** Yagbe'a Tsion reigned —
a) 1270–1285  b) 1285–1294  c) 1294–1299  d) 1314–1344
**Answer: b) 1285–1294** — Son and successor, 1285–1294.

**5.** To end the succession struggle, royal descendants were —
a) Sent to Jerusalem  b) Confined on Amba Geshen  c) Made governors  d) Sent to trade at Zeila
**Answer: b) Confined on Amba Geshen** — All but the reigning king were confined there under guard.

**6. ** In the guide's framing, the medieval kingdom was called the Christian Kingdom because —
a) It had no Muslims  b) The church–state relationship became very strong, almost reaching its peak, as Christianity expanded south  c) Its kings were priests  d) Rome ruled it
**Answer: b) The church–state relationship became very strong, almost reaching its peak, as Christianity expanded south** — guide evidence.

**7. ** The medieval Ethiopian kings ruled from —
a) Gondar  b) A moving court — no fixed capital city  c) Aksum  d) Harar
**Answer: b) A moving court — no fixed capital city** — the fact Fasilides' Gondar later overturns — guide evidence.

**8. ** The guide says the gult right lasted —
a) One reign  b) Until the 20th century  c) Until 1270  d) A single generation
**Answer: b) Until the 20th century** — the land right's extraordinary lifespan — guide evidence.
