# Medieval Ethiopia — the Solomonic restoration — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson, with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can do the lesson's outcomes — Say Yekuno Amlak defeated Yetbarak in 1270 and proclaimed the Solomonic dynasty restored.; Explain the name's purpose: claim legitimacy, cast the Zagwe as usurpers.; Give the reigns 1270–1285 and 1285–1294, the five-sons crisis 1294–1299, and the Amba Geshen confinement. Practice deepens inside the whole lesson: more items per question set, then the harder corners — exact dates, exact names, and the cause-and-effect links between them — until the retelling in 'Say it to your companion' runs clean from memory.
