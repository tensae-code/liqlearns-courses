# Video script — The Bete-Israel

Story-style script in the animated-map spirit: the map opens, peoples and armies move, routes light up. Super-simple voice; made later, when videos are made.

---

[Scene 1 — A Jewish people of the Ethiopian highlands. The map and the scene build as the voice speaks.]
The Bete-Israel — the House of Israel — are Ethiopia's Jewish people, living for centuries in the northern highlands. Watch the names appear one by one: Bete-Israel, origins debate, chronicle. This is the part of the story to hold.

[Scene 2 — Crafts, land and livelihood. The map and the scene build as the voice speaks.]
The Bete-Israel were renowned craftspeople — potters, weavers and smiths whose work highland society needed. Watch the names appear one by one: craftspeople, landholding, tenure. This is the part of the story to hold.

[Scene 3 — Neighbours of the Christian kingdom. The map and the scene build as the voice speaks.]
To the end of the 13th century, the Bete-Israel lived alongside — and in negotiation with — the Christian kingdom: sometimes integrated, sometimes set apart by faith, craft and land rules.

Their story belongs inside Ethiopian history, not beside it: the kingdom's religious map was never single-coloured.. Watch the names appear one by one: Christian kingdom, negotiation, religious map. This is the part of the story to hold.
