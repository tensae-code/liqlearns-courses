# The Bete-Israel — sub lessons and depth

This is the depth record of the lesson, re-expressed as sub lessons: the learner proves each sub lesson before the next opens, and practice deepens inside each part (more items, harder corners) without new rungs.

## Sub lesson 1 — A Jewish people of the Ethiopian highlands

Pass mark: the learner can retell this part in order and answer its gate-pool questions — the key term is **Bete-Israel** (the House of Israel — Ethiopia's Jewish people of the northern highlands).

## Sub lesson 2 — Crafts, land and livelihood

Pass mark: the learner can retell this part in order and answer its gate-pool questions — the key term is **craftspeople** (makers — potters, weavers, smiths — as many Bete-Israel were).

## Sub lesson 3 — Neighbours of the Christian kingdom

Pass mark: the learner can retell this part in order and answer its gate-pool questions — the key term is **Christian kingdom** (the highland kingdom alongside which the Bete-Israel lived).
