# The Bete-Israel — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is Bete-Israel?
a) chronicle
b) the House of Israel — Ethiopia's Jewish people of the northern highlands
c) origins debate
**Answer: b) the House of Israel — Ethiopia's Jewish people of the northern highlands**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is craftspeople?
a) landholding
b) tenure
c) makers — potters, weavers, smiths — as many Bete-Israel were
**Answer: c) makers — potters, weavers, smiths — as many Bete-Israel were**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What is Christian kingdom?
a) negotiation
b) religious map
c) the highland kingdom alongside which the Bete-Israel lived
**Answer: c) the highland kingdom alongside which the Bete-Israel lived**
