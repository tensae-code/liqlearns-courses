# The Bete-Israel — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own term.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The term for 'a written royal record, one witness in the origins debate' is ______.
**Answer: chronicle**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The term for 'the security with which land is held' is ______.
**Answer: tenure**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The term for 'the true pattern of faiths in the land — never a single colour' is ______.
**Answer: religious map**
