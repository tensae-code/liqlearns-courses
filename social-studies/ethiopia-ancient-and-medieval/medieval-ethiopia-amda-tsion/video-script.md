# Video script — Medieval Ethiopia — Amda Tsion

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

1314. Amda Tsion takes the throne.
Damot submits. Gojjam submits.
The Lake Tana lands join the kingdom.
East and south-east, the sultanates pay tribute.
Manpower. Wealth. A great power rises.
But tribute breeds resistance — remember that.

---

Grade 9 story addition (Ethiopia Wave 4):

The map draws a road — Zeila to the highlands — and two hands reach for it.

1332. Amda Seyon's armies take Ifat. The road changes masters.

For two hundred years, successors will march back down that road to win it again.
