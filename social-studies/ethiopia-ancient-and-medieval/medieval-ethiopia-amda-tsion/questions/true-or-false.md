# Medieval Ethiopia — Amda Tsion — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1.** The Lake Tana area came under Amda Tsion's campaigns.
**Answer: True** — Named among the lands brought in.

**2.** Amda Tsion's expansion weakened the kingdom's treasury.
**Answer: False** — It gained the king wealth.

**3.** The sultanates welcomed permanent tribute without resistance.
**Answer: False** — Tribute stored up resistance — Ifat fought repeatedly for independence.
