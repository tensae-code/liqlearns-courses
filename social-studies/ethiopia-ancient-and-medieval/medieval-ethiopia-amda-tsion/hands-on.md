# Hands-on — Medieval Ethiopia — Amda Tsion

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Grow the map
On a blank outline map (draw one roughly), shade a small 'kingdom' area, then add Damot, Gojjam and the Tana area in three steps, saying at each step what the kingdom gained.

## Tribute tally
With counters or stones, show tribute: each 'land' gives 2 stones a year to the centre. After five rounds, count the centre's pile — that growth is the wealth the book names.

## Whose road? (Grade 9)
Stage the claim: an Ifat merchant-prince and a kingdom official each argue for the Zeila tolls before a neutral caravan leader, using only trade facts from this lesson's parts. The caravan leader rules — and must say what both sides agree the road is worth.

## Grade 11 depth — Expansion in depth — Amda Seyon and the kingdom's medieval shape

Re-draw this lesson's map or table with the Grade 11 part added in a second colour, then tell the new part's story aloud from the drawing alone. Where the drawing cannot carry the story, the story is not yet learned — re-read that part.
