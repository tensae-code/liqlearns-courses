# Hands-on — The sultanates — Hadiya and Bale

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Ring map
Draw Bale as a centre circle and place Dawaro/Sharka above, Hadiya left, Adal right, using string or stones. Say each neighbour's direction aloud.

## Unknown birthday honesty
Write a card for a family object whose 'birthday' nobody knows (an old pot, a tree). List what you DO know about it without its date — the Hadiya-Bale method.
