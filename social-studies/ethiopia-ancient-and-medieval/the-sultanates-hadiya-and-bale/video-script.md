Two sultanates of the south: Hadiya and Bale.
No one recorded their founding days.
Hadiya: farms and trade, tributary from 1316.
Bale: ruled by garads, tributary for two centuries.
Around Bale, a ring of sultanates.
A whole southern system, side by side.
