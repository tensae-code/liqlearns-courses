# The sultanates — Hadiya and Bale — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Hadiya was made tributary by Amda Tsion in —
a) 1270  b) 1316  c) 1380  d) 1543
**Answer: b) 1316** — First known when made tributary in 1316.

**2.** Hadiya's economy rested on —
a) Fishing and pearls  b) Farming and trade  c) Iron smelting only  d) Sea trade
**Answer: b) Farming and trade** — Farming and trade, and supplying slaves to the trade.

**3.** Bale's rulers carried the title —
a) Sultan  b) Garad  c) Mukarrib  d) Ras
**Answer: b) Garad** — Its rulers were titled garad.

**4.** Bale was tributary to the highland kingdom —
a) Only in 1316  b) From the 14th to the 16th century  c) Never  d) From 897 to 1280
**Answer: b) From the 14th to the 16th century** — Two centuries of tribute.

**5.** Which sultanate lay EAST of Bale?
a) Hadiya  b) Adal  c) Dawaro  d) Sharka
**Answer: b) Adal** — Dawaro and Sharka north, Hadiya west, Adal east.
