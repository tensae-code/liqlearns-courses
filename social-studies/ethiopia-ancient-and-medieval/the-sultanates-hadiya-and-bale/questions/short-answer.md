# The sultanates — Hadiya and Bale — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** When is Hadiya first clearly known, and by what event?
**Answer:** In 1316, when Amda Tsion made it tributary.

**2.** Name Bale's neighbours by direction.
**Answer:** Dawaro and Sharka north, Hadiya west, Adal east.
