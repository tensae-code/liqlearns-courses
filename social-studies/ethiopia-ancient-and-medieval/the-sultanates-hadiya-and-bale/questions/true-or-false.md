# The sultanates — Hadiya and Bale — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1.** Both Hadiya's and Bale's founding dates are recorded exactly.
**Answer: False** — Both are unrecorded.

**2.** Hadiya was known for supplying slaves to the trade.
**Answer: True** — Stated in the record.

**3.** Dawaro and Sharka lay south of Bale.
**Answer: False** — They lay to the NORTH of Bale.
