# Hands-on — Damot, Kafa and the southern states

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## States on the string
Pin the nine state-cards along a string in the order the lesson meets them. Each learner must place one card and justify its position from the lesson's geography.

## The envoy's visit
Role-play an envoy from the Christian kingdom arriving at a southern court: the court asks three questions (faith? tribute? trade?) and the envoy answers — the class judges which state would accept which terms.
