# Damot, Kafa and the southern states — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why does Damot matter as a 'door' to the south?
**Answer:** Its ruler's conversion by Abune Tekle Haymanot tied it by faith toward the Christian kingdom, opening the south to the kingdom's faith, trade and pressure.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name six southern or central states or peoples of this lesson.
**Answer:** Any six of: Damot, Kafa, Ennarya, Bizamo, Walaita, Yem, Kambata, the Gurage chiefdoms, the Agaw kingdom of Gojjam.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What two lenses does the book apply to each southern state?
**Answer:** Its political order and its socio-economic order, as the Christian kingdom pressed to bring it under its rule.
