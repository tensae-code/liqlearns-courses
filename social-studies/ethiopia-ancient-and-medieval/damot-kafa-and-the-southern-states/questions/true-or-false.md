# Damot, Kafa and the southern states — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Motalami's conversion made Damot a province without its own throne at once.
**Answer: False** — Conversion tied it by faith; it kept its own ruler — the pressure was a process.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The southern states shared indigenous religion as their foundation.
**Answer: True** — The book's framing for the group.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Gojjam's Agaw kingdom is named among the states of this survey.
**Answer: True** — The Agaw kingdom of Gojjam.
**4. (Sub lesson 3)** True or false: The Blue Nile, Damot's northern boundary river — this is Abbay.
**Answer: True**

**5. (Sub lesson 4)** True or false: The western peoples bordering the highland kingdom — this is Gonga.
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 5)** True or false: The forest kingdom of coffee, type case of the tato states — this is Kafa.
**Answer: True**
