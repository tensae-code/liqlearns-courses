# Damot, Kafa and the southern states — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Damot lay —
a) north of Lake Tana  b) south of the Blue Nile  c) on the Red Sea coast
**Answer: b) south of the Blue Nile** — South of the Abay.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Damot's ruler Motalami was converted by —
a) Frumentius  b) Abune Tekle Haymanot  c) Amda Seyon
**Answer: b) Abune Tekle Haymanot** — In the late 13th century.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Damot is described as — of the southern states.
a) the richest  b) probably the earliest  c) the last to fall
**Answer: b) probably the earliest** — Probably the earliest.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which list names southern and central states of the period?
a) Ifat, Adal, Shewa  b) Kafa, Ennarya, Bizamo, Walaita, Yem, Kambata  c) Aussa, Bidu, Rahaito
**Answer: b) Kafa, Ennarya, Bizamo, Walaita, Yem, Kambata** — The indigenous-religion south.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The Gurage were organised as —
a) one kingdom  b) chiefdoms  c) a sultanate
**Answer: b) chiefdoms** — The Gurage chiefdoms.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** The Christian kingdom pressed these states to —
a) leave Ethiopia  b) come under its rule  c) stop trading
**Answer: b) come under its rule** — Each state is examined under that pressure.
**7. (Sub lesson 3)** What is Damot?
a) the wealthy kingdom south of the Abbay, rich in gold
b) the Blue Nile, Damot's northern boundary river
c) a detail belonging to a different lesson in this course
**Answer: a) the wealthy kingdom south of the Abbay, rich in gold**

**8. (Sub lesson 4)** What is Ennarya?
a) a detail belonging to a different lesson in this course
b) the western peoples bordering the highland kingdom
c) the wealthy southern state of the gold trade
**Answer: c) the wealthy southern state of the gold trade**

**9. (Sub lesson 5)** What is tato?
a) the forest kingdom of coffee, type case of the tato states
b) the sacral royal title and system of Kafa and neighbours
c) a detail belonging to a different lesson in this course
**Answer: b) the sacral royal title and system of Kafa and neighbours**
**10. (Sub lesson 6)** The king of Kafa bore the title —
a) Kawo  b) Tato  c) Moti  d) Woma
**Answer: b) Tato — Kafa's king was the Tato, with twelve insignia, many of gold.**

**11. (Sub lesson 6)** Kafa's capital was —
a) Harar  b) Anderecha  c) Bonga  d) Gondar
**Answer: c) Bonga — Bonga was the capital; Anderecha the second seat.**
