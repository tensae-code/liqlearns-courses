# Damot in depth — gold south of the Abbay — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own term.

**1. (Sub lesson 3)** The term for 'the Blue Nile, Damot's northern boundary river' is ______.
**Answer: Abbay**

**2. (Sub lesson 4)** The term for 'the western peoples bordering the highland kingdom' is ______.
**Answer: Gonga**

**3. (Sub lesson 5)** The term for 'the forest kingdom of coffee, type case of the tato states' is ______.
**Answer: Kafa**
