# Video script — Damot, Kafa and the southern states

The story-style script for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list), in the animated-map spirit: the map opens, peoples and armies move, capitals rise, routes light up. Super-simple voice. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The map opens on the Blue Nile. South of the river, lights kindle one by one.
Damot first — the earliest. A saint arrives; a king listens. Motalami is converted.
More lights: Kafa in its forests, Ennarya, Bizamo, Walaita, Yem, Kambata. Chiefdoms among the Gurage.
From the north, a slow pressure — faith, trade, tribute. Each light must answer.
This crowded south is the stage. The next act will redraw it.

[Grade 11 depth scene — Damot in depth — gold south of the Abbay. The animated map carries the story as the voice speaks.]
Damot, south of the Abbay, takes the Grade 11 depth on its own: a wealthy pagan kingdom whose gold and slaves drew highland trade southward across the great river, and whose dealings with the Christian kingdom — commerce first, pressure later — ended in absorption. The names to hold: Damot, Abbay.

[Grade 11 depth scene — The southern and western states in depth — Gojjam to the Gonga. The animated map carries the story as the voice speaks.]
The Grade 11 survey widens the ring around the highland kingdom: Gojjam across the Abbay; the small states of Bizamo, Wajj and Ganz; wealthy Ennarya of the gold and slave trades; and the Gonga peoples of the west. The names to hold: Ennarya, Gonga.

[Grade 11 depth scene — The tato kingdoms in depth — Kafa and the Omotic states. The animated map carries the story as the voice speaks.]
The Omotic kingdoms of the south-west receive their Grade 11 depth under their own design: the tato system, sacral kingship in which the king's person is hedged with ritual and the kingdom's health is read in his. The names to hold: tato, Kafa.

[Grade 9 depth: Kafa and Yem in depth — the forest kingdoms — added scene. The animated map carries the story as the voice speaks.]
