# Ancient Ethiopia — Punt — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** We know about Punt mainly from —
a) Punt's own royal books  b) Egyptian inscriptions, tomb records and archaeology  c) Roman histories  d) Arab travellers' tales
**Answer: b) Egyptian inscriptions, tomb records and archaeology** — No books of Punt survive; Egypt's records carry the evidence.

**2.** The famous expedition to Punt was sent by —
a) King Ezana  b) Queen Hatshepsut  c) Imam Ahmad  d) King Lalibela
**Answer: b) Queen Hatshepsut** — Hatshepsut (1490–1468, as printed) sent the celebrated voyage.

**3.** Which of these did the ships bring FROM Punt?
a) Glass and olive oil  b) Incense, myrrh and ivory  c) Cloth and wine  d) Paper and compasses
**Answer: b) Incense, myrrh and ivory** — Those are Punt's own products in the list.

**4.** The majority view places Punt —
a) Only in Yemen  b) Only in northern Somalia  c) Across today's Eritrea, Djibouti, Ethiopia, Somalia and Yemen  d) In Egypt itself
**Answer: c) Across today's Eritrea, Djibouti, Ethiopia, Somalia and Yemen** — That wide Red Sea land is the majority claim.
