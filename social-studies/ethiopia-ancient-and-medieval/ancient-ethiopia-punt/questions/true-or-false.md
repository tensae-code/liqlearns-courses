# Ancient Ethiopia — Punt — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1.** Punt is the oldest named state in this course's Ethiopian story.
**Answer: True** — It is presented first, before Da'amat and Aksum.

**2.** Gold, silver and copper were among the goods taken from Punt.
**Answer: True** — All three metals are in the list.

**3.** Scholars all agree exactly where Punt was.
**Answer: False** — Its location is debated; three claims are made.
