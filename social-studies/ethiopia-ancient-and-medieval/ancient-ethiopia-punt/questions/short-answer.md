# Ancient Ethiopia — Punt — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** Name four goods the Egyptian ships brought from Punt.
**Answer:** Any four of: incense, myrrh, cinnamon, ivory, rhino horn, leopard skins, fox, bear, giraffe, gold, silver, copper, ebony.

**2.** What three kinds of evidence tell us about Punt?
**Answer:** Hieroglyphic inscriptions, tomb records and archaeology.
