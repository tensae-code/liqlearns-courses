# Hands-on — Ancient Ethiopia — Punt

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Goods from home
List fragrant, beautiful or valuable things your own area produces — coffee, honey, hides, grain. Which of them would a far-away trader sail for, like the Egyptians sailed for Punt's goods?

## Weigh the claims
On three cards write the three location claims. Under each, write what evidence would support it. Lay the majority-view card in the middle and say why it stands there.
