# Video script — Ancient Ethiopia — Punt

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Long ago, ships sailed south from Egypt.
They came to a rich land called Punt.
Incense. Myrrh. Ivory. Gold.
Queen Hatshepsut sent the most famous voyage.
Where exactly was Punt? Scholars still debate it.
Most say: a wide land around the southern Red Sea.
