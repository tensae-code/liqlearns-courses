# Video script — Aksum — Christianity arrives

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A traveller from Syria came to Aksum.
His name was Frumentius — Abune Selama.
King Ezana believed, about the year 330.
Christianity became the state religion.
A century later, the Nine Saints walked the land.
The Bible was translated into Ge'ez.
The faith now spoke Ethiopia's own language.
