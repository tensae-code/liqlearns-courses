# Aksum — Christianity arrives — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Christianity was brought to Aksum by —
a) The Nine Saints  b) Frumentius  c) King Lalibela  d) Imam Ahmad
**Answer: b) Frumentius** — The Syrian Frumentius brought the faith.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** King Ezana reigned —
a) 615–631  b) 325–360  c) 1314–1344  d) 1607–1632
**Answer: b) 325–360** — Ezana's reign is given as 325–360.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Ezana proclaimed Christianity —
a) His private hobby  b) The state religion  c) Forbidden  d) Only for merchants
**Answer: b) The state religion** — He accepted it and proclaimed it the state religion, about 330.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The Nine Saints came in the —
a) 1st century  b) 3rd century  c) 5th century  d) 9th century
**Answer: c) 5th century** — In the 5th century they carried the faith widely.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The Nine Saints carried Christianity to —
a) Only the palace  b) The lower classes  c) South Arabia  d) Egypt
**Answer: b) The lower classes** — They took the faith to ordinary people.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** The Bible was translated from Greek into —
a) Amharic  b) Ge'ez  c) Arabic  d) Latin
**Answer: b) Ge'ez** — Translated into Ge'ez, Ethiopia's literary language.
