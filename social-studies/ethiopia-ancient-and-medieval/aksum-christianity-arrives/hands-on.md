# Hands-on — Aksum — Christianity arrives

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Two-step journey
Walk a path in two stages: first to a 'palace' point (one person waits there as king), then on to three 'village' points. Say at each stop what happened in Aksum's story.

## Translate one line
Take a short line you know in another language and write it in your own language underneath, as the translators put the Bible into Ge'ez. Say why your translation helps a friend who cannot read the first language.
