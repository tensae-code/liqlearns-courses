# Hands-on — Settlement of the peoples of Ethiopia and the Horn

One line of framing: this lesson is learned by doing — paper, talk and the learner's own ground.

## Map the story
On a blank page, draw the lesson's map from memory: its places in their true rough positions, its routes and movements as arrows. Then open the lesson and correct your map in a second colour — the corrections are the learning.

## Terms in the hand
Write each key term (settlement / oral tradition / archaeology, highlands / lowlands / way of life, board / alliance / summary) on its own slip. Shuffle, deal, and for each slip tell one true sentence of the story that uses it.

## Teach it onward
Teach the story to someone at home in five minutes, in order, without the book. Where you stall is where you re-read.
