# Settlement of the peoples of Ethiopia and the Horn — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own term.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The term for 'the study of the past through dug-up remains' is ______.
**Answer: archaeology**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The term for 'the fit between a people, its land and its livelihood' is ______.
**Answer: way of life**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The term for 'the map as a compressed record of countless unwritten decisions' is ______.
**Answer: summary**
