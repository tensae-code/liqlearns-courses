# Settlement of the peoples of Ethiopia and the Horn — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** True or false: A people's remembered accounts of its journeys and past — this is oral tradition.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** True or false: in this lesson, settlement means the study of the past through dug-up remains.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** True or false: The dry lands where herding peoples lived — this is lowlands.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** True or false: in this lesson, highlands means the fit between a people, its land and its livelihood.
**Answer: False**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** True or false: Peoples joining together, shaped by who settled where — this is alliance.
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** True or false: in this lesson, board means the map as a compressed record of countless unwritten decisions.
**Answer: False**
