# Video script — Settlement of the peoples of Ethiopia and the Horn

Story-style script in the animated-map spirit: the map opens, peoples and armies move, routes light up. Super-simple voice; made later, when videos are made.

---

[Scene 1 — Reading settlement from three kinds of evidence. The map and the scene build as the voice speaks.]
Who lives where in Ethiopia today is the end of a long story of movement and settling. Watch the names appear one by one: settlement, oral tradition, archaeology. This is the part of the story to hold.

[Scene 2 — Peopling a varied land. The map and the scene build as the voice speaks.]
Highlands, lowlands, Rift Valley lakes and Red Sea plains invited different lives: plough farming on the cool heights, herding in the dry lowlands, trade at the passes between. Watch the names appear one by one: highlands, lowlands, way of life. This is the part of the story to hold.

[Scene 3 — Why settlement history matters. The map and the scene build as the voice speaks.]
Every later conflict and alliance in this course — sultanate and kingdom, movement and state — plays out on this settlement board. Watch the names appear one by one: board, alliance, summary. This is the part of the story to hold.

[Grade 9 depth: Fission and fusion — how Ethiopia was peopled — added scene. The animated map carries the story as the voice speaks.]
Tell this part as story, in the lesson's own order, in the super-simple voice: who acted, what they wanted, and what changed because of it.
