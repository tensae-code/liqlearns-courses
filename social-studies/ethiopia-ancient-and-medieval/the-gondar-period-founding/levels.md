# The Gondar period — founding — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson, with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can do the lesson's outcomes — Say Susenyos (1607–1632) ruled from Denbiya decentralised, local rulers keeping defence, tax and army duties.; Say Fasiledes (1632–1667) expelled divisive foreigners in 1633 and founded Gondar in 1636 for national and religious unity.; Give Gondar's capital span: 1636–1855, and its golden age 1636–1706 (teacher guide — evidence). Practice deepens inside the whole lesson: more items per question set, then the harder corners — exact dates, exact names, and the cause-and-effect links between them — until the retelling in 'Say it to your companion' runs clean from memory.
