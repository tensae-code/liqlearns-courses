# Hands-on — The Gondar period — founding

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Found a capital
Choose a 'capital corner' for a pretend kingdom in your compound: it must have water, a road, and room for a market. Defend your choice in three reasons, as Fasiledes' choice of Gondar can be defended.

## Decentralised vs centred
Two rounds of a group task: first with each small group deciding alone (Susenyos' way), then with one centre deciding (Fasiledes' way). Which was faster? Which felt fairer?

## Grade 11 depth — Fasilides in depth — expulsion, restoration and a fixed capital

Re-draw this lesson's map or table with the Grade 11 part added in a second colour, then tell the new part's story aloud from the drawing alone. Where the drawing cannot carry the story, the story is not yet learned — re-read that part.
