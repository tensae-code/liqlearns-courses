# The Gondar period — founding — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1.** Fasiledes expelled the foreigners he judged divisive in 1633.
**Answer: True** — Expulsion 1633, founding 1636.

**2.** Fasiledes cut all ties with the coastal rulers.
**Answer: False** — He kept close ties with Suakin, Mitsiwa and the Turks.

**3.** Susenyos centralised all defence, tax and army duties at his court.
**Answer: False** — His order was decentralised; local rulers kept those duties.

**4. (Grade 11 depth)** True or false: The centuries of the travelling camp-capital, ended at Gondar — this is moving-court age.
**Answer: True**
