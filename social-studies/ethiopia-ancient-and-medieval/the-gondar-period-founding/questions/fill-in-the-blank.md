# The Gondar period — founding — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word or number.

**1.** Gondar was founded in the year ____.
**Answer: 1636**

**2.** Susenyos ruled from ____ with a decentralised order.
**Answer: Denbiya**

**3. (Grade 11 depth)** The term for 'the centuries of the travelling camp-capital, ended at Gondar' is ______.
**Answer: moving-court age**
