# The Gondar period — founding — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Susenyos ruled from —
a) Gondar  b) Denbiya  c) Harar  d) Roha
**Answer: b) Denbiya** — Susenyos ruled from Denbiya.

**2.** Under Susenyos, local rulers kept —
a) No duties  b) Defence, tax and army duties  c) Only church duties  d) Sea trade
**Answer: b) Defence, tax and army duties** — A decentralised order.

**3.** Fasiledes founded Gondar in —
a) 1607  b) 1633  c) 1636  d) 1667
**Answer: c) 1636** — Founded in 1636.

**4.** Gondar was founded as a centre for —
a) Sea trade  b) National and religious unity  c) The salt trade  d) A military prison
**Answer: b) National and religious unity** — That was its founding purpose.

**5.** Gondar's capital role ran —
a) 1636–1706  b) 1636–1855  c) 1607–1632  d) 1270–1855
**Answer: b) 1636–1855** — Capital 1636–1855 (golden age 1636–1706 per the guide).

**6. (Grade 11 depth)** What is Gondar, 1636?
a) a detail belonging to a different lesson in this course
b) the centuries of the travelling camp-capital, ended at Gondar
c) Fasilides' founding of the fixed capital
**Answer: c) Fasilides' founding of the fixed capital**
