# Video script — The Adal wars — Imam Ahmad

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

1527, Shimbra Kure: the Imam strikes.
Sultanates united under Ahmad al-Ghazi.
By 1535: Dawaro, Bale, Tigray — fallen.
The highlands call Portugal: 400 soldiers land in 1541.
The Imam fields 900 Turks.
1543, Weyna Dega: the Imam falls.
Both lands bleed. A new age walks in.

[Grade 11 depth scene — The wars of Imam Ahmad in depth — 1529 to Wayna Daga. The animated map carries the story as the voice speaks.]
The Grade 11 outline is stark: Imam Ahmad ibn Ibrahim al-Ghazi — Gragn — invades from Adal in 1529; campaign by campaign the highland kingdom shatters, its churches burned, its emperor a fugitive; then Portuguese musketeers land, and at Wayna Daga in 1543 the war turns and Gragn falls. The names to hold: Gragn, Wayna Daga.
