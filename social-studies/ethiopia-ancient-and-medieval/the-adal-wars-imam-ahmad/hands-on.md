# Hands-on — The Adal wars — Imam Ahmad

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Supremacy timeline
Draw a bar 1527–1543. Mark Shimbra Kure at the start, the conquests 1531–1535, the Portuguese landing 1541, Weyna Dega at the end. Walk the bar, narrating.

## Counting the cost
Two piles of stones: 'what the war destroyed' and 'what the war changed'. Place a stone for each result in the lesson and say which pile it belongs to — some belong to both.

## Grade 11 depth — The wars of Imam Ahmad in depth — 1529 to Wayna Daga

Re-draw this lesson's map or table with the Grade 11 part added in a second colour, then tell the new part's story aloud from the drawing alone. Where the drawing cannot carry the story, the story is not yet learned — re-read that part.
