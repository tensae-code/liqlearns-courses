# Hands-on — The Adal wars — Imam Ahmad

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Supremacy timeline
Draw a bar 1527–1543. Mark Shimbra Kure at the start, the conquests 1531–1535, the Portuguese landing 1541, Weyna Dega at the end. Walk the bar, narrating.

## Counting the cost
Two piles of stones: 'what the war destroyed' and 'what the war changed'. Place a stone for each result in the lesson and say which pile it belongs to — some belong to both.
