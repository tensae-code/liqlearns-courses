# The Adal wars — Imam Ahmad — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Imam Ahmad opened the war at —
a) Weyna Dega, 1543  b) Shimbra Kure, 1527  c) Dakar, 1380  d) Harar, 1430
**Answer: b) Shimbra Kure, 1527** — Shimbra Kure in 1527 opens phase two.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The Imam's supremacy lasted —
a) 1380–1430  b) 1527–1543  c) 1314–1527  d) 1541–1543
**Answer: b) 1527–1543** — Political supremacy 1527–1543.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Which lands did he take in 1531–1535?
a) Only Harar  b) Dawaro, Bale, Wej, Debre Berhan, Tigray and the Red Sea areas  c) Gojjam and Damot only  d) Gondar and Lasta
**Answer: b) Dawaro, Bale, Wej, Debre Berhan, Tigray and the Red Sea areas** — The named conquests of 1531–1535.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The Portuguese soldiers who came to help numbered —
a) 900  b) 400  c) 119  d) 40
**Answer: b) 400** — 400 Portuguese soldiers in 1541.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The Imam was defeated at —
a) Shimbra Kure  b) Weyna Dega, 1543  c) Adulis  d) Debre Berhan
**Answer: b) Weyna Dega, 1543** — Defeated at Weyna Dega in 1543.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** One result of the war was —
a) The founding of Gondar at once  b) Room opened for the Oromo expansion  c) Aksum restored  d) Dahlak freed
**Answer: b) Room opened for the Oromo expansion** — Among the book's listed results.

**7. (Sub lesson 3)** What is Gragn?
a) a detail belonging to a different lesson in this course
b) Imam Ahmad's byname — the left-handed
c) the 1543 battle where the war turned
**Answer: b) Imam Ahmad's byname — the left-handed**
