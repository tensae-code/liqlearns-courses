# The Adal wars — Imam Ahmad — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word or number.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Imam Ahmad ibn Ibrahim al-____ united the sultanates.
**Answer: Ghazi**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The decisive battle of 1543 was at Weyna ____.
**Answer: Dega**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The term for 'the 1543 battle where the war turned' is ______.
**Answer: Wayna Daga**
