# The Adal wars — Imam Ahmad — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Imam Ahmad united the sultanates before his campaigns.
**Answer: True** — Uniting them was his first achievement.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Lebna Dengel refused all foreign help.
**Answer: False** — He sought Portuguese help: Bermudez mission 1535, 400 soldiers 1541.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The war's results fell on the highland side only.
**Answer: False** — Deaths and destruction are listed on BOTH sides.
