# The Adal wars — Imam Ahmad — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Who sought Portuguese help, and what came in 1541?
**Answer:** Lebna Dengel; 400 Portuguese soldiers arrived in 1541 (after the Bermudez mission of 1535).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How many Turkish soldiers did the Imam have?
**Answer:** 900.
