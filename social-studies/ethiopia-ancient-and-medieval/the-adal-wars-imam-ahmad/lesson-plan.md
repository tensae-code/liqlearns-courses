# Lesson plan — The Adal wars — Imam Ahmad

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Ethiopia: Ancient and Medieval · **Lesson:** The Adal wars — Imam Ahmad
**Place in the curriculum:** Title — Ethiopia: Ancient and Medieval (CURRICULUM.md path; TITLES.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** **Imam Ahmad ibn Ibrahim al-Ghazi united the sultanates** and opened the war at **Shimbra Kure in 1527**.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the lessons before it in this title — the learner has met the periods of Ethiopian history and the states named so far in this title's order. It needs nothing else.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The Imam's supremacy, 1527–1543** — United sultanates, the victory at Shimbra Kure, and conquests to 1535.
2. **Foreign soldiers and the end at Weyna Dega** — Portuguese for the highlands, Turks for the Imam; defeat in 1543 and the war's results.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of this title, in the title's order — most nearest, the lesson just before this one. Recommended, never forced.
- A learner may jump straight in here: the lesson stands alone, and names are explained where they first appear.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say Imam Ahmad united the sultanates, opened the war at Shimbra Kure (1527) and held supremacy to 1543.
2. Name the lands taken 1531–1535: Dawaro, Bale, Wej, Debre Berhan, Tigray, the Red Sea areas.
3. Give the foreign help: Bermudez mission 1535 and 400 Portuguese (1541) against 900 Turkish soldiers; the Imam defeated at Weyna Dega in 1543.
4. List results: deaths and destruction both sides, best highland soldiers lost, displacement, communities living together, foreign intervention, room for the Oromo expansion.
