# Egalitarian systems — governing without kings — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** True or false: In the book's presentation, self-governance traditions were unique to the Oromo.
**Answer: False — They were widespread — Luwa, ballee, Konso and Burji are the evidence.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** True or false: Under Luwa the same leaders ruled for life.
**Answer: False — Power rotated every eight years.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** True or false: Konso had a single king ruling all its towns.
**Answer: False — Its towns governed themselves in a decentralised order.**
