# Egalitarian systems — governing without kings — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word or number.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Sidama age-grade system of rotating governance is ____.
**Answer: Luwa**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The Gedeo governed through the ____ system.
**Answer: ballee**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Burji's administration was ____ — officials were chosen, not born to office.
**Answer: elective**
