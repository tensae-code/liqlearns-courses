# Egalitarian systems — governing without kings — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In your own words: name the four systems in the book's survey and the people of each.
**Answer: Sidama — Luwa; Gedeo — ballee; Konso — decentralised town organisation; Burji — elective administration.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In your own words: what principle do rotation and election share?
**Answer: Power is not owned for life or by birth: it circulates among the governed, who can replace those who hold it.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In your own words: what is the book proving by closing the unit with these systems?
**Answer: That self-governance traditions were widespread among Ethiopia's peoples, not unique to one people — Gadaa had company.**
