# Medieval Ethiopia — trade routes and goods — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Mitsiwa stopped serving trade when Zeila rose.
**Answer: False** — Mitsiwa kept serving the north-east.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Slaves were sent in large numbers to Arabia, Persia and India.
**Answer: True** — Stated directly — the trade's grim side.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The guide calls trade a factor of peaceful interaction among peoples.
**Answer: True** — Peoples exchanged values, customs and ideas with goods (teacher guide — evidence).
