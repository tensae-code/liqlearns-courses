# Video script — Medieval Ethiopia — trade routes and goods

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The gate moves: from Adulis toward Zeila.
Routes cross the land: Harar, Dawaro, Fatagar.
Ivory. Gold. Civet — and slaves, in sorrowful numbers.
Money here is salt: amole bars, hand to hand.
Weekly markets feed the villages.
Caravans feed the kings — and pay their taxes.
Trade carries ideas, too.
