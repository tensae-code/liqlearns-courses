# Hands-on — Medieval Ethiopia — trade routes and goods

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Weekly market visit
Visit a real local market (or picture your own): list its merchants, its trade items and any tax or fee you can find — the guide's own market-visit project.

## Salt money
Weigh out three equal 'amole' bars of salt or sugar in cloth. Price three classroom objects in amole bars and 'buy' one. What makes a good money — and what makes salt awkward money?
