# European travellers in Ethiopia after 1805 — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own term.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The term for 'one who saw what they report — valuable and biased at once' is ______.
**Answer: eyewitness**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The term for 'to desire another's land, as powers came to covet Ethiopia' is ______.
**Answer: covet**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The term for 'testing travellers against local sources, and local sources against them' is ______.
**Answer: double reading**
