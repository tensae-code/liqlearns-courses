# Video script — European travellers in Ethiopia after 1805

Story-style script in the animated-map spirit: the map opens, peoples and armies move, routes light up. Super-simple voice; made later, when videos are made.

---

[Scene 1 — Travellers as sources. The map and the scene build as the voice speaks.]
From 1805 onward, European travellers — Salt among the first of the century, in the road Bruce had opened — crossed Ethiopia and published what they saw: courts, churches, wars, markets and roads.

Their books are sources like any other: priceless eyewitnesses, and biased ones — read them with the criticism Unit 1 taught.. Watch the names appear one by one: Salt, Bruce, eyewitness. This is the part of the story to hold.

[Scene 2 — Mixed motives. The map and the scene build as the voice speaks.]
The travellers came mixed: science and curiosity, trade and diplomacy, and — increasingly — the advance work of empire, mapping a land European powers were learning to covet.

Ethiopian rulers knew it and handled them accordingly: hosting, questioning, and using their visits for their own diplomacy.. Watch the names appear one by one: diplomacy, advance work, covet. This is the part of the story to hold.

[Scene 3 — Reading the travellers well. The map and the scene build as the voice speaks.]
Use the travellers for what only they give — dated, placed, observed detail — and check them against chronicles and oral memory for what they could not see: motives, meanings, the inside of things.

That double reading closes this unit's method where it began: every source tested, none worshipped.. Watch the names appear one by one: observed detail, chronicles, double reading. This is the part of the story to hold.
