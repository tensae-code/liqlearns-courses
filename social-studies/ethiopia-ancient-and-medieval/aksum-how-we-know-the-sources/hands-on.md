# Hands-on — Aksum — how we know: the sources

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Be the inscription
Write a short 'inscription' about your own school or family on a card, in formal words, as a stone would carry it. What will it prove to someone in 500 years?

## Two witnesses game
With a partner, describe the same event (a market day, a football game) separately, then compare: where do your two accounts agree? That agreement is what historians trust.
