# Aksum — how we know: the sources — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1.** The Periplus describes Aksum as weak and disorganised.
**Answer: False** — It shows a well-organised, strong state.

**2.** Stone inscriptions at Yeha are among the pictured sources.
**Answer: True** — Stone inscriptions at Yeha and a Sabaean bronze inscription are pictured.

**3.** Both great sources were written by Aksum's own kings.
**Answer: False** — The Periplus is a foreign sailor's handbook; only the inscription is the king's voice.
