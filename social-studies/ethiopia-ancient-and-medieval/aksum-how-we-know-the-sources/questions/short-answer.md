# Aksum — how we know: the sources — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** Which source lets the king speak for himself, and which is an outsider's account?
**Answer:** The Adulis inscription is the king's own account; the Periplus is a foreign visitor's account.

**2.** Name two places the evidence comes from besides the two great texts.
**Answer:** Stone inscriptions at Yeha and a Sabaean bronze inscription.
