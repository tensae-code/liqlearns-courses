# Aksum — how we know: the sources — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** The Periplus of the Eritrean Sea was written about —
a) 50 AD  b) 330 AD  c) 1270 AD  d) 1500 BC
**Answer: a) 50 AD** — About 50 AD — a 1st-century trading handbook.

**2.** The Periplus says King Zoscales —
a) Built the stelae  b) Spoke Greek well  c) Founded Adulis  d) Wrote the Bible in Ge'ez
**Answer: b) Spoke Greek well** — The writer noted the king's good Greek.

**3.** The Greek inscription of Adulis is preserved in —
a) The Kebra Nagast  b) Cosmas Indicopleustes' Christian Topography  c) A Roman law book  d) An Egyptian tomb
**Answer: b) Cosmas Indicopleustes' Christian Topography** — Cosmas preserved the inscription's text.

**4.** In the Adulis inscription the king recounts —
a) His sea trade prices  b) Campaigns north, west and east, across the Red Sea  c) Building churches  d) His family line
**Answer: b) Campaigns north, west and east, across the Red Sea** — The king tells of campaigns in all directions, even across the sea.
