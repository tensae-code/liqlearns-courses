How do we know about Aksum?
A sailor's handbook, the Periplus, written about 50 AD.
It names Adulis, and King Zoscales, who spoke Greek.
And a king's own words, carved in Greek at Adulis.
Campaigns north, west, east — across the sea.
Two witnesses. One strong kingdom.
