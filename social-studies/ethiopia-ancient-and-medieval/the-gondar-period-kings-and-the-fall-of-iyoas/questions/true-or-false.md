# The Gondar period — kings and the fall of Iyoas — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Iyasu I collected yearly tribute from Shewa's rulers.
**Answer: True** — He recognised them and collected yearly tribute.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Mentewab built her own palace.
**Answer: True** — Bakaffa's wife Mentewab built her own palace.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Mikael Sehul defended Iyoas to the end.
**Answer: False** — Called in as help, he killed Iyoas in 1769.
