# The Gondar period — kings and the fall of Iyoas — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name two foreign contacts of Iyasu I's reign.
**Answer:** Any two: treated by the French physician Jacques Poncet (1699–1700); received three missionaries; sent seven young Ethiopian Christians as envoys to the Pope in Rome.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Whom did Mikael enthrone after killing Iyoas?
**Answer:** Tekle Haymanot II.
