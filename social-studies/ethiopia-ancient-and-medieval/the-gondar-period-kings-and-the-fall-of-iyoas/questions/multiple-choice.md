# The Gondar period — kings and the fall of Iyoas — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Iyasu I revised which law book?
a) The Kebra Nagast  b) The Fetha Nagast  c) The Justinian Code  d) The Quran
**Answer: b) The Fetha Nagast** — He revised the Fetha Nagast.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The French physician who treated Iyasu I was —
a) Bermudez  b) Jacques Poncet  c) Cosmas  d) Poncet's king
**Answer: b) Jacques Poncet** — Jacques Poncet treated him in 1699–1700.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Iyasu I sent seven young Ethiopian Christians as envoys to —
a) The Ottoman sultan  b) The Pope in Rome  c) The king of Portugal  d) Harar
**Answer: b) The Pope in Rome** — Seven young envoys to the Pope.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Iyasu II ruled under the regency of —
a) Wubit  b) Mentewab  c) Ruqayya  d) The echage
**Answer: b) Mentewab** — His mother Mentewab was regent.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Under Iyoas, the palace guards were —
a) Portuguese  b) Oromo guards, with Oromigna spoken at court  c) Turkish janissaries  d) Beja herders
**Answer: b) Oromo guards, with Oromigna spoken at court** — Oromigna at court; Oromo guards at the palace.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Iyoas was killed in 1769 by —
a) Dejazmach Wube  b) Ras Mikael Sehul  c) Imam Ahmad  d) Bakaffa
**Answer: b) Ras Mikael Sehul** — Mikael Sehul killed Iyoas and enthroned Tekle Haymanot II.
