**Named correction — the Mentewab divergence (filed here by Wave 4 History Band E, 2026-11-12).** The Grade 9 History books tell Mentewab's regency two different ways, and this lesson does not merge them silently. The Grade 9 student textbook (printed pp. 181–183, placement 85) centres the Wollo party of Itege Wubit against the Qara party of her mother-in-law Itege Mentewab, regent in the name of her grandson Iyoas (r. 1755–1769). The Grade 9 teacher guide's answer (printed p. 159, teacher placements placement 17) instead makes Mentewab wife of Emperor Bakafa and regent for her young son Iyasu II, supported by her brother Welde Leul as Ras Bitweded, losing power in 1769 to Ras Michael Sehul — and its own dating is internally contradictory ("regent for her son Iyasu II (r.1730-55) until he died in 1768"). This lesson's telling — Mentewab as regent across Iyasu II's reign and grandmother-regent in Iyoas's, per its Book 1 above — stands as built; the guide's divergent version is recorded here as a named divergence, taught nowhere as fact, and the guide's contradictory dates are not taught at all.
