# Hands-on — The Gondar period — kings and the fall of Iyoas

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Envoy letters
Write a short formal letter as one of Iyasu II's court to a far ruler, in your best formal words, as the seven young envoys might have carried to Rome. Seal it with a foil stamp.

## Chain of people
Stand in a line as Bakaffa, Mentewab, Iyasu II, Wubit, Iyoas, Mikael. Each steps forward and says one line of their part in the chain, ending with 1769.
