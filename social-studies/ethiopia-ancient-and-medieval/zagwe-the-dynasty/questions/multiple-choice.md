# Zagwe — the dynasty — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** The Zagwe dynasty was founded by —
a) Yekuno Amlak  b) Mera Tekle Haymanot  c) Ezana  d) Menelik
**Answer: b) Mera Tekle Haymanot** — Mera Tekle Haymanot founded the dynasty.

**2.** The Zagwe centres, in order, were —
a) Aksum, Yeha, Adafa  b) Adafa, Roha, Lalibela  c) Harar, Dakar, Zeila  d) Gondar, Roha, Adafa
**Answer: b) Adafa, Roha, Lalibela** — First Adafa in Lasta, then Roha, finally Lalibela town.

**3.** In Jerusalem the Zagwe —
a) Fought in the Crusades  b) Held land through peaceful relations  c) Built a stela  d) Were expelled at once
**Answer: b) Held land through peaceful relations** — They held land in Jerusalem peacefully and did not fight in the Crusades.

**4.** Zagwe trade ran through —
a) Adulis only  b) Dahlak, Zeila and the Gulf of Aden  c) Mitsiwa only  d) The Nile
**Answer: b) Dahlak, Zeila and the Gulf of Aden** — Those were its sea outlets.
