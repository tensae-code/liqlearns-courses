# Zagwe — the dynasty — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1.** King Lalibela is tied to the dynasty's final centre.
**Answer: True** — The centre finally settled at Lalibela town under King Lalibela.

**2.** The Zagwe fought as Crusaders in Palestine.
**Answer: False** — They did not fight in the Crusades.

**3.** The Zagwe kept ties with Egypt and Palestine.
**Answer: True** — Strong ties with both are stated.

**4. (Grade 11 depth)** True or false: The Zagwe homeland and seat in the northern highlands — this is Lasta.
**Answer: True**
