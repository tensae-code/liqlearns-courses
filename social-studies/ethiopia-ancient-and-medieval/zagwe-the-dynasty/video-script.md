# Video script — Zagwe — the dynasty

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Aksum faded. Power moved to Lasta.
Mera Tekle Haymanot founded the Zagwe.
Adafa. Then Roha. Then Lalibela town.
They traded through Dahlak and Zeila.
They held land in Jerusalem — by peace, not war.
The mountain dynasty. That is Zagwe.
