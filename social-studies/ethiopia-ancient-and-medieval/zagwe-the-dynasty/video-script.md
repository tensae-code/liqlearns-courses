# Video script — Zagwe — the dynasty

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Aksum faded. Power moved to Lasta.
Mera Tekle Haymanot founded the Zagwe.
Adafa. Then Roha. Then Lalibela town.
They traded through Dahlak and Zeila.
They held land in Jerusalem — by peace, not war.
The mountain dynasty. That is Zagwe.

[Grade 11 depth scene — The Zagwe in depth — Agaw kings and a new capital. The animated map carries the story as the voice speaks.]
The Grade 11 telling restores the Zagwe to the main line: Agaw kings ruling from Lasta after Aksum's age ended, keeping the Christian kingdom alive through centuries the chronicles treat briefly — and building, under King Lalibela, the rock-hewn churches that still anchor Ethiopia's fame. The names to hold: Zagwe, Lasta.
