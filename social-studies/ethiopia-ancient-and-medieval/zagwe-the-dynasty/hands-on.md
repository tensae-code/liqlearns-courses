# Hands-on — Zagwe — the dynasty

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Three centres trail
Lay three stones in a line on the ground: Adafa, Roha, Lalibela. Walk the line, saying at each stone one fact about the Zagwe there.

## Balance diplomacy
Hold a tray level with both hands while a partner places 'Muslim states' and 'Christian states' cards on opposite sides. Keeping it level is the Zagwe way — peace with both.
