# Hands-on — Aksum — its economy

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Market day baskets
Set out two baskets: 'We sell' and 'We buy'. Put local things (or name cards) in each, the way Aksum sorted its goods. Say why each thing sits in its basket.

## Carry the water
Carry a small jerrycan of water to a plant or garden patch and water it carefully. That guiding of water to where crops grow is irrigation in your own hands.
