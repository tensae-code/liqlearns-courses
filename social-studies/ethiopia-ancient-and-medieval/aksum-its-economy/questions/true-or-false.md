# Aksum — its economy — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Aksum was strongest from the 4th to the 7th century.
**Answer: True** — Founded about the 1st century; strongest 4th–7th.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Rist-holders were foreign merchants.
**Answer: False** — They were peasant land-holders who paid tribute.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Aksum exported cloth and imported ivory.
**Answer: False** — It is the other way round: ivory out, cloth in.
