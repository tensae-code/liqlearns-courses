# Aksum — its economy — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Irrigation helped Aksum because it —
a) Raised farm output  b) Built the stelae  c) Brought Christianity  d) Ended all wars
**Answer: a) Raised farm output** — Guided water made the fields produce more.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Peasant rist-holders paid the kingdom —
a) Coins only  b) Tribute  c) Nothing at all  d) Glass and wine
**Answer: b) Tribute** — Rist-holders paid tribute from what they grew.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Aksum's Red Sea port was —
a) Zeila  b) Adulis  c) Berbera  d) Dahlak
**Answer: b) Adulis** — Foreign trade ran from Adulis inland.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which list is Aksum's EXPORTS?
a) Cloth, glass, olive oil, wine  b) Ivory, myrrh, incense, gold  c) Household goods and cloth  d) Wine and glass only
**Answer: b) Ivory, myrrh, incense, gold** — Exports were the riches of Aksum's own land.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Which of these was an IMPORT to Aksum?
a) Rhino horn  b) Hippo hide  c) Olive oil  d) Tortoise shell
**Answer: c) Olive oil** — Olive oil was brought in; the others were sent out.
