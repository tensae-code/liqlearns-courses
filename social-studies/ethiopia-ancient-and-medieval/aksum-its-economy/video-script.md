# Video script — Aksum — its economy

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Aksum stood on two strong legs.
First leg: farming. Water guided to the fields.
Peasants on their rist land paid tribute.
Second leg: trade, through the port of Adulis.
Out went ivory, gold and incense.
In came cloth, glass and wine.
Fed at home, rich abroad — that was Aksum.
