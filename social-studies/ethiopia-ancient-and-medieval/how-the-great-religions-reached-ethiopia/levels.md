# How the great religions reached Ethiopia — sub lessons and depth

This is the depth record of the lesson, re-expressed as sub lessons: the learner proves each sub lesson before the next opens, and practice deepens inside each part (more items, harder corners) without new rungs.

## Sub lesson 1 — The faiths that were already here

Pass mark: the learner can retell this part in order and answer its gate-pool questions — the key term is **indigenous beliefs** (the local faiths of the region before the world religions arrived).

## Sub lesson 2 — Judaism and Christianity at Aksum

Pass mark: the learner can retell this part in order and answer its gate-pool questions — the key term is **Frumentius** (the teacher whose work brought Christianity to the Aksumite court).

## Sub lesson 3 — Islam and the first hijra

Pass mark: the learner can retell this part in order and answer its gate-pool questions — the key term is **first hijra** (the flight of the Prophet's persecuted followers to refuge at Aksum).
