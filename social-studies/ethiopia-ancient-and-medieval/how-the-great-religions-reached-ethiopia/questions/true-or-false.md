# How the great religions reached Ethiopia — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** True or false: One sent to spread a faith into new lands — this is missionary.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** True or false: in this lesson, indigenous beliefs means a practice or belief handed down within a people.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** True or false: The Aksumite king who converted to Christianity in the 4th century — this is Ezana.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** True or false: in this lesson, Frumentius means the monks who carried Christianity deep into Ethiopia after Aksum's conversion.
**Answer: False**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** True or false: A migration for the faith's safety, here from Arabia to Aksum — this is hijra.
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** True or false: in this lesson, first hijra means the paths along which Islam spread into Ethiopia's east and south-east.
**Answer: False**
**7. (Sub lesson 4)** True or false: Ancestor reverence and sacrifice to spirits belonged to Ethiopia's indigenous religions.
**Answer: True**
