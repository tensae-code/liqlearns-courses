# How the great religions reached Ethiopia — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is indigenous beliefs?
a) the local faiths of the region before the world religions arrived
b) tradition
c) missionary
**Answer: a) the local faiths of the region before the world religions arrived**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is Frumentius?
a) Nine Saints
b) Ezana
c) the teacher whose work brought Christianity to the Aksumite court
**Answer: c) the teacher whose work brought Christianity to the Aksumite court**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What is first hijra?
a) trade routes
b) the flight of the Prophet's persecuted followers to refuge at Aksum
c) hijra
**Answer: b) the flight of the Prophet's persecuted followers to refuge at Aksum**
**4. (Sub lesson 4)** In the old indigenous religions, village priests were paid —
a) Nothing; they served free  b) Tribute for their services  c) Coins from Aksum only  d) Taxes by the king
**Answer: b) Tribute for their services — The communities paid tribute for the priests' prayers and services.**

**5. (Sub lesson 4)** The old priests prayed above all for —
a) Victory in foreign wars  b) Rain, thanksgiving and safety from natural hazards  c) Ships to arrive  d) A new calendar
**Answer: b) Rain, thanksgiving and safety from natural hazards — Their prayers served the farming village's survival.**
