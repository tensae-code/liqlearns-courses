# How the great religions reached Ethiopia — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is indigenous beliefs?
a) the local faiths of the region before the world religions arrived
b) tradition
c) missionary
**Answer: a) the local faiths of the region before the world religions arrived**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is Frumentius?
a) Nine Saints
b) Ezana
c) the teacher whose work brought Christianity to the Aksumite court
**Answer: c) the teacher whose work brought Christianity to the Aksumite court**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What is first hijra?
a) trade routes
b) the flight of the Prophet's persecuted followers to refuge at Aksum
c) hijra
**Answer: b) the flight of the Prophet's persecuted followers to refuge at Aksum**
