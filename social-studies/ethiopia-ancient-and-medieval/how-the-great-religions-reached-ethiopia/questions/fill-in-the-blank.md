# How the great religions reached Ethiopia — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own term.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The term for 'a practice or belief handed down within a people' is ______.
**Answer: tradition**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The term for 'the monks who carried Christianity deep into Ethiopia after Aksum's conversion' is ______.
**Answer: Nine Saints**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The term for 'the paths along which Islam spread into Ethiopia's east and south-east' is ______.
**Answer: trade routes**
