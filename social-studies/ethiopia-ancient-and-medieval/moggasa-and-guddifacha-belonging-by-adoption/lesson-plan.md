# Lesson plan — Moggasa and Guddifacha — belonging by adoption

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Ethiopia Ancient and Medieval · **Lesson:** Moggasa and Guddifacha — belonging by adoption
**Place in the curriculum:** Grade 9 History, Unit 7 — States, Principalities, Population Movements and Interactions in Ethiopia, 13th–mid-16th C. (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked into this title in the Ethiopia Wave 4 History fix build.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Peoples can grow by birth and by war — the Oromo also grew by adoption: Guddifacha adopted a child into a family with rights equal to a biological child's, and Moggasa adopted individuals and whole communities into a clan, sharing everything equally.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on this title's Gadaa and Oromo-expansion lessons. It needs nothing else.
