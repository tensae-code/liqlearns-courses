# Moggasa and Guddifacha — belonging by adoption — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In your own words: what is the difference between Guddifacha and Moggasa?
**Answer: Guddifacha adopts a child into a family; Moggasa adopts individuals or whole communities into a clan.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In your own words: give two results the book credits to the two adoption institutions.
**Answer: Any two: peoples mixing and living in peace; adopted populations joining the next campaigns; Gadaa and the Oromo language spreading; the Oromo adopting neighbours' cultures in return.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In your own words: why call adoption a way of growing a people, not just helping a person?
**Answer: Because whole communities could be taken in with equal rights and protection, enlarging the people and spreading its system and language without destroying the newcomers.**
