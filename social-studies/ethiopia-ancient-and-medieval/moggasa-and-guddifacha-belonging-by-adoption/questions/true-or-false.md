# Moggasa and Guddifacha — belonging by adoption — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** True or false: Guddifacha and Moggasa spread the Gadaa system and the Oromo language to neighbours.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** True or false: An adopted child had fewer rights than a biological child.
**Answer: False — The adopted child enjoyed rights equal to a biological child's.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** True or false: Moggasa could adopt an entire community into a clan.
**Answer: True**
