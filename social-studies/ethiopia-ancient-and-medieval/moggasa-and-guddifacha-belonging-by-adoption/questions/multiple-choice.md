# Moggasa and Guddifacha — belonging by adoption — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Under Guddifacha, an adopted child —
a) Served the family unpaid  b) Enjoyed rights equal to a biological child's  c) Was freed at adulthood only  d) Kept a separate name forever
**Answer: b) Enjoyed rights equal to a biological child's — Adoption in law, not charity.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Guddifacha is the adoption of —
a) A whole community  b) A child into a family  c) A clan chief  d) A trading partner
**Answer: b) A child into a family — Guddifacha works at family scale.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Moggasa adopted —
a) Only orphans  b) Individuals or whole communities into a clan  c) Only warriors  d) Priests only
**Answer: b) Individuals or whole communities into a clan — Moggasa works at clan scale.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Adopted members under Moggasa gained —
a) Taxes to pay  b) Protection and material benefit, sharing everything equally  c) A probationary century  d) Land outside the clan
**Answer: b) Protection and material benefit, sharing everything equally — Equal sharing is the institution's core.**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Adoption fed the Oromo expansion because —
a) Adopted populations joined the next campaigns  b) It frightened neighbours away  c) It ended all wars  d) It built roads
**Answer: a) Adopted populations joined the next campaigns — The movement grew by welcome as well as by force.**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** The adoption traffic ran both ways: the Oromo also adopted —
a) Roman law  b) Neighbours' cultures, including monarchical systems  c) Islam at once  d) The Ge'ez script
**Answer: b) Neighbours' cultures, including monarchical systems — Adopters were changed too.**
