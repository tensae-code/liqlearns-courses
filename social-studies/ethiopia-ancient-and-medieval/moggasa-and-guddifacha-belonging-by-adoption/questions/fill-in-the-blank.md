# Moggasa and Guddifacha — belonging by adoption — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word or number.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A family's adoption of a child is called ____.
**Answer: Guddifacha**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The adoption of individuals or whole communities into a clan is called ____.
**Answer: Moggasa**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Adopted clan members shared ____ equally with those born to the clan.
**Answer: everything**
