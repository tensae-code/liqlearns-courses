# The Oromo expansion — society and effects — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The two Oromo moieties are —
a) Mecha and Tulama  b) Borana and Barentu  c) Dawaro and Fatagar  d) Kereyu and Itu
**Answer: b) Borana and Barentu** — The two great halves: Borana and Barentu.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Waqa is —
a) A river god  b) The creator God of the sky  c) A war leader  d) A market town
**Answer: b) The creator God of the sky** — Waqa, creator God of the sky.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The Qallu were —
a) Tax collectors  b) Religious leaders believed possessed by the ayana  c) Portuguese missionaries  d) Harar's merchants
**Answer: b) Religious leaders believed possessed by the ayana** — The Qallu were believed ayana-possessed.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** After the expansion, the highland kingdom's centre was pushed —
a) South of the Abay  b) North of the Abay  c) To the coast  d) To Innarya
**Answer: b) North of the Abay** — Pushed north of the Abay.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Harar responded by —
a) Opening its gates  b) Walling itself — the Jugol — and becoming a city-state  c) Moving to Dakar  d) Joining Ifat
**Answer: b) Walling itself — the Jugol — and becoming a city-state** — Adal walled Harar (Jugol) and became a city-state.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Which pair KEPT their independence by hard fighting?
a) Ifat and Dawaro  b) Kefa and Wolayta  c) Bale and Hadiya  d) Damot and Gafat
**Answer: b) Kefa and Wolayta** — Kefa and Wolayta held out; the others named were destroyed.
