# Video script — The Oromo expansion — society and effects

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Two halves made one people: Borana and Barentu.
Above them, Waqa, creator of the sky.
His spirit — ayana — rests on the Qallu.
Then the land itself changed.
The kingdom pushed north of the Abay.
Harar raised its Jugol wall.
Some states fell. Kefa and Wolayta stood.
