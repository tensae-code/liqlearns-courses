# The Oromo expansion — society and effects

## Do it first

Before the expansion, who were the Oromo to themselves? They were organised in two great halves, and they prayed to a creator God of the sky. And after the expansion, the whole map of Ethiopia was redrawn. This lesson takes both: the society, then the effects.

## Sub lesson 1 — Two moieties, one faith of the sky

Before the expansion the Oromo stood in **two moieties — two great halves: Borana** (Mecha, Tulama, Guji, southern Borana and others) and **Barentu** (Kereyu, Itu, Raqqo, Akachu, Warantisha, Hambana and others). Their own religion centred on **Waqa, the creator God of the sky**, whose power shows in the world as **ayana — spirit**. Its religious leaders, the **Qallu**, were believed to be **possessed by the ayana**. The teacher guide adds the Gada vocabulary of government: the **Caffee (Chaffee)** is the **general assembly of the Abba Gada**, and **Gada** itself is the **socio-political structure by which Oromo public life was administered**. Note honestly that the guide's Barentu list differs in membership and spelling from the student book's (Karayu, Marawa, Ittu, Humbana, Akachu, Wollo, Arsi) — the pupil list above follows the student book.

## Sub lesson 2 — What the expansion changed

The effects reach across the whole land. The movement brought **wars and meetings with many peoples**. The **highland kingdom's centre was pushed north of the Abay** river. **Adal walled Harar — the Jugol wall — and became a city-state**, defending one city instead of ruling a wide sultanate. Some peoples held their land by hard fighting: **Kefa and Wolayta kept their independence**. Others were **destroyed outright: Damot, Gafat, Bizamo, Bale, Hadiya, Ifat, Dawaro and Fatagar**. Read that list against the earlier lessons: states you met as powers — Ifat, Hadiya, Bale — end here. The map of medieval Ethiopia was not merely adjusted by the expansion; it was remade.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the two moieties and two groups in each, who Waqa is and who the Qallu are, then three effects: the kingdom north of the Abay, Harar walled, and states destroyed against states that held out.
