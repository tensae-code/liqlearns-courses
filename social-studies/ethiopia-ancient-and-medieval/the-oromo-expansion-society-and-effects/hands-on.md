# Hands-on — The Oromo expansion — society and effects

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Moiety tree
Draw a family-tree style chart on a big sheet: Oromo at the top, Borana and Barentu branching, member groups as leaves. Present it and explain ayana and the Qallu from your chart.

## Wall the city
Build a low wall of stones or boxes around a 'city' of small houses (Jugol around Harar). Discuss: what changes when a wide sultanate shrinks to one defended city?
