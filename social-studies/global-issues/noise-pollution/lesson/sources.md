**Acceptance count: taught in 3 countries (Cambodia, Libya, Iraq).**

Cambodia's own way (Cambodia Grade 11 Geography): the standalone upper-secondary Geography book is a human-impact course — people reshaping the earth, people destroying natural resources, the sea, pollution of air/water/noise/waste, economic globalisation, city and countryside, and a world of uneven development.

Searchable tags: noise pollution, Cambodia, social studies, global-issues

Libya's own way (Libya Grade 12 LY G12 Environmental Geography): The geography course closes with a full environmental-geography book: the environment as a system of living and non-living parts, the world's great environments (water, then land biome by biome), the social environment and its balance with nature, the problems of settlements, the five pollutions (air, water, soil, food, noise) — and protection ending in sustainable development.

Iraq's own way (Iraq Grade 11 Earth Science): the scientific track's earth-science course — atmosphere, earthquakes and volcanoes, weathering and soil, environment and pollution, and fossils and geological time — bilingual with English technical terms throughout, and Iraqi touches kept (the air masses over Iraq, the yellow dust storms, Iraq's Natural History Museum).
