# Hands-on — The Oic and the Palestinian Cause

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The Oic and the Palestinian Cause
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Palestine and Jerusalem hold Islamic consensus in the organisation's resolutions, which demand that the international community, represented by the UN, end the occupation and free the Arab and Palestinian land occupied in 1967, Jerusalem at its head, and recognise the PLO as the sole legitimate representative of the Palestinian people, urging member states to open PLO representation offices in their capitals and backing Palestine's membership in international organisations and bodies; it organised political, material and economic support through the Islamic Solidarity Fund, asked every member state to issue a postage stamp bearing Palestine's name whose revenue goes to the PLO, and founded the Jerusalem Fund and the Jerusalem Committee under Morocco's supervision to gather money for Jerusalem's institutions and inhabitants and protect the holy places — and urged member states to teach Palestine's geography and history in all of them. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
