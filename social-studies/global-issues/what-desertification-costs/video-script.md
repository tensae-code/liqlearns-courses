# Video script — What desertification costs

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Degraded land loses resilience to natural climatic variation, so soil and production fail together: falling food yields, malnutrition and hunger follow, with added famine risk where poverty, civil disturbance or conflict already exist. The costs arrive as a household's empty store before they arrive as statistics. The further consequences: population migration as people leave land that cannot feed them, and wars and conflict over freshwater as the remaining resource is contested. The estimated annual income loss in affected regions runs to over 42 billion USD — every year, from land that once produced. Desertification is not an environmental line item; it is an economic haemorrhage and a driver of movement and conflict.
