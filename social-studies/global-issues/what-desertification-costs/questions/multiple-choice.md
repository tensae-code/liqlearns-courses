# What desertification costs — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Degraded land loses…
a) its colour  b) resilience to climatic variation  c) its name
**Answer: b) resilience to climatic variation**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Soil and production…
a) fail together  b) improve  c) alternate
**Answer: a) fail together**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Famine risk rises where poverty or… already exist
a) conflict/civil disturbance  b) wealth  c) rain
**Answer: a) conflict/civil disturbance**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** People respond to dead land by…
a) migrating  b) celebrating  c) planting more
**Answer: a) migrating**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Conflict grows over…
a) freshwater  b) sand  c) roads
**Answer: a) freshwater**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Estimated annual income loss is over…
a) 42 million USD  b) 42 billion USD  c) 4 billion USD
**Answer: b) 42 billion USD**
