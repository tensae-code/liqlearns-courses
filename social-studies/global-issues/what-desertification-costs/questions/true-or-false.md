# What desertification costs — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Malnutrition and hunger follow falling yields.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Degraded land forgives a bad year as healthy land does.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The income loss is a one-time cost.
**Answer: False**
