# What desertification costs — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the first cost named?
**Answer:** Loss of resilience — soil and production failing together under climatic variation.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name two social consequences.
**Answer:** Population migration and conflict over freshwater.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Where does famine risk rise most?
**Answer:** Where poverty, civil disturbance or conflict already exist.
