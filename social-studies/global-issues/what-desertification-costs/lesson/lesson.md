# What desertification costs

## Do it first

Healthy land forgives a bad year. Degraded land cannot — that lost forgiveness is the first cost.

## Sub lesson 1 — Land that cannot bounce back

Degraded land loses resilience to natural climatic variation, so soil and production fail together: falling food yields, malnutrition and hunger follow, with added famine risk where poverty, civil disturbance or conflict already exist. The costs arrive as a household's empty store before they arrive as statistics.

## Sub lesson 2 — People move, money vanishes

The further consequences: population migration as people leave land that cannot feed them, and wars and conflict over freshwater as the remaining resource is contested. The estimated annual income loss in affected regions runs to over 42 billion USD — every year, from land that once produced. Desertification is not an environmental line item; it is an economic haemorrhage and a driver of movement and conflict.
