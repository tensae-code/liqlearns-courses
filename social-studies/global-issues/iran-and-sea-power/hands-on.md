# Hands-on — Iran And Sea Power

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Lesson 11: Iran and sea power (ایران و قدرت دریایی) — Iran reaches the sea from north and south, with over 2,000 km of maritime border on its southern coasts; its two important ports on the Sea of Oman, Jask and the ocean port of Chabahar (Sistan and Baluchestan), developing Iran's sea transport and Central Asia's access. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
