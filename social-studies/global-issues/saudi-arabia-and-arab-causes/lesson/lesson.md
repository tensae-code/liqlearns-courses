# Saudi Arabia and Arab Causes

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: the Kingdom's initiatives to solve the Arab states' causes and help their peoples, from its care for the Arab world's security, stability and unity: King Abdulaziz striving for the Arab homeland's unity from the unification's proclamation in 1351H/1932 — sending Crown Prince Saud to Jerusalem in 1354H/1935 to support the Palestinians, his total rejection of Palestine's partition project and great efforts to prevent it, and charging Prince Faisal with forming popular committees to collect donations to save Palestine; the Kingdom's continuing support of the Palestinian cause in every age, the Lebanese cause and the Taif settlement, and the Jerusalem Summit — as Saudi Arabia's books teach it. ## Sub lesson 1 — Saudi Arabia and Arab Causes

Saudi Arabia Grade 10 Social Studies (Secondary Tracks, First Common Year) (printed pp. 23–37), teaches: The Kingdom's initiatives to solve the Arab states' causes and help their peoples, from its care for the Arab world's security, stability and unity: King Abdulaziz striving for the Arab homeland's unity from the unification's proclamation in 1351H/1932 — sending Crown Prince Saud to Jerusalem in 1354H/1935 to support the Palestinians, his total rejection of Palestine's partition project and great efforts to prevent it, and charging Prince Faisal with forming popular committees to collect donations to save Palestine; the Kingdom's continuing support of the Palestinian cause in every age, the Lebanese cause and the Taif settlement, and the Jerusalem Summit. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Saudi Arabia's way — the book's own teaching way

Saudi Arabia's own way: the Grade 10 Tracks book starts from the components that make a state strong and influential and Saudi Arabia's own foundations and components, then follows the Kingdom's work for Arab, Islamic and international causes, and surveys the organisations it belongs to — the European Union as a model of integration, the League of Arab States, the Muslim World League, the Organisation of Islamic Cooperation, OPEC, the United Nations and the G20. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
