# Hands-on — Saudi Arabia and Arab Causes

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: the Kingdom's initiatives to solve the Arab states' causes and help their peoples, from its care for the Arab world's security, stability and unity: King Abdulaziz striving for the Arab homeland's unity from the unification's proclamation in 1351H/1932 — sending Crown Prince Saud to Jerusalem in 1354H/1935 to support the Palestinians, his total rejection of Palestine's partition project and great efforts to prevent it, and charging Prince Faisal with forming popular committees to collect donations to save Palestine; the Kingdom's continuing support of the Palestinian cause in every age, the Lebanese cause and the Taif settlement, and the Jerusalem Summit. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Saudi Arabia's way
Say how Saudi Arabia's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
