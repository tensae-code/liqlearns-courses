# Hands-on — Pakistan On A Fault Line

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Major faults in Pakistan: the Eurasian and Indo-Australian plates collide; seismic zones map; the 1935 and 2005 earthquakes. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Pakistan's way
Say how Pakistan's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
