# Video script — The World's Natural Environments and Their Diversity

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Palestine's Grade 9 student book, the *Social Studies, Grade 9, Part Two* (printed p. 4), teaches: the concept of the natural environment; types of natural environments in the world; the effect of environmental diversity on economic activities.

Palestine's own way (Grade 9, PS G9P2): the course closes at world scale — the diversity of natural environments, peoples and economies, then the age's challenges (population explosion, famines, poverty) framed as matters for international cooperation.
