# Lesson plan — Forests — Shrinking and Care

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Global Issues · **Lesson:** Forests — Shrinking and Care
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 14 — Global Issues (CURRICULUM.md) — the fifteenth and last lesson of the title, after Ethiopia's Green Economy (TITLES.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** forests once covered wide parts of the world and now very little remains, felled by farm and grazing expansion, city growth and cutting for services — bringing climate disturbance, soil degradation, flooding, fuel-wood shortage and lost species — and forests answer care with a high power to replace themselves, through the book's seven practices.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the whole title: desertification's deforestation cause, overload's wood engine, and the green economy's fourth pillar all land here. Grade 8 depth throughout (the Grade 8 book's §§ on forests); no teacher evidence exists for Grade 8 Chapter 7 and none is claimed.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say what a forest is, in the book's words, and how the world's cover has changed.
2. Name the drivers of forest shrinking.
3. List the ecological results the book names.
4. Give the seven care practices and tell coppicing and reforestation apart.

## If the learner is stuck
- Puts 'groundwater increasing' among the results → the review stock keys that as NOT a result: when forests shrink, groundwater falls.
- Confuses coppicing with clear-cutting → coppicing cuts in a way that leaves the stump to sprout shoots; the tree is used AND returns.
