# Forests — Shrinking and Care — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Forests are —
a) any green field  b) gatherings of vegetation where trees abound  c) plantations only
**Answer: b) gatherings of vegetation where trees abound** — the book's definition.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which drives deforestation in developing countries, on the chapter's review stock?
a) City growth  b) Fast grazing expansion and farm-land hunger  c) All of these
**Answer: c) All of these** — the stock keys all of them.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Which is NOT a result of forest shrinking?
a) Groundwater increasing  b) Heavy flooding  c) Fuel-wood shortage
**Answer: a) Groundwater increasing** — groundwater falls when forests shrink — the stock's keyed odd one out.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Coppicing is —
a) burning stumps  b) cutting trees for use in a way that makes shoots sprout from the uncut stump  c) planting a new forest
**Answer: b) cutting trees for use in a way that makes shoots sprout from the uncut stump** — the stump is left to answer with shoots.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Which soil-care method does NOT belong with the others, on the review stock?
a) Planting  b) Replanting and contour ploughing  c) Farming the land bare
**Answer: c) Farming the land bare** — 'farming the land bare' is the keyed outsider.
