# Forests — Shrinking and Care — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Re-covering with forest a place that was forest and is now bare is ______.
**Answer: reforestation.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Controlling pests includes clearing the fallen trunks and leaf-litter that harbour organisms like the ______ as printed.
**Answer: qinqin.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Over all the practices, ______-use planning is applied.
**Answer: land.**
