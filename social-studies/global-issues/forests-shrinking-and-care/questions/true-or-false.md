# Forests — Shrinking and Care — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Very little of the world's former forest cover remains.
**Answer: True** — wide parts of four continents were covered; now very little remains.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Forests failing to remain breeding places of plant species is among the results.
**Answer: True** — it closes the book's results list.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Forests have a low capacity to replace themselves, so care rarely works.
**Answer: False** — the book gives forests a high capacity to reproduce and replace themselves.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Forest protection means wholly banning cutting where overuse threatens the ecological balance.
**Answer: True** — that is the practice as the book states it.
