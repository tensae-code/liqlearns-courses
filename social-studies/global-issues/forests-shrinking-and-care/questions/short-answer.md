# Forests — Shrinking and Care — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** List the results of forest shrinking the book names.
**Answer:** Climate disturbance; accelerated soil degradation; faster silt accumulation; heavy flooding; fuel-wood shortage; loss of individual plant species; forests failing to remain the breeding places of plant species.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the seven care practices.
**Answer:** Reforestation; coppicing; forest protection; using proper tree-cutting methods; controlling forest fires; controlling forest diseases; controlling pests — with land-use planning applied.
