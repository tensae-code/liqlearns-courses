# Video script — Forests — Shrinking and Care

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Once the world wore forests. Now very little remains.

Farms spread. Grazing spread. Cities grew. Axes fell.

And the bill: climate disturbed, soil washing, floods rising, fuel scarce, species gone.

But a forest wants to come back.

Replant the bare place. Cut so the stump sprouts. Guard what stands.

Fight its fires, its diseases, its pests. Plan the land.

Seven practices. One power: the forest replaces itself — if we let it.
