# Hands-on — Forests — Shrinking and Care

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Stump survey
Find a cut stump in your locality and look closely: are shoots sprouting from it? That is coppicing happening by itself. Record the stump, the shoots and whether people leave it room to grow — or clear it away.

## Seven-practice walk
Walk one grove or wooded slope with the seven practices as a checklist. For each, write: done here, partly done, or not done — with what you saw as the evidence. Bring the sheet back as your locality's forest report.
