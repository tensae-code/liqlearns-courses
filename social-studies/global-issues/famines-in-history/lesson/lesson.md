# Famines in history

## Do it first

Wherever chroniclers wrote, famine appears beside war and plague. Read its long record without flinching.

## Sub lesson 1 — Famine is as old as records

Famines recur through recorded history — chronicled in ancient India, Egypt, Western Asia, China, Greece and Rome. Medieval Europe suffered them constantly: 89 'general' famines in France alone across the 10th–18th centuries, plus hundreds of local ones. Famine was not an interruption of the old world; it was one of its regular visitors.

## Sub lesson 2 — The modern exceptions — and Ethiopia's

Agricultural innovation and the Industrial Revolution made Western European famine rare, with catastrophic exceptions: Ireland's 1846–51 Great Famine, and the Netherlands' 1944 hunger winter under military stalemate. Death tolls are notoriously disputed — famine counts its dead badly. In modern history, Ethiopia's 1984–85 famine stands as the most disastrous — the event against which this whole unit is quietly written.
