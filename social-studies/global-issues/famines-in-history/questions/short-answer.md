# Famines in history — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Why are death tolls disputed?
**Answer:** Famine counts its dead badly — records fail amid collapse (the book notes tolls are notoriously disputed).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the two catastrophic Western exceptions.
**Answer:** Ireland 1846–51 and the Netherlands 1944.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What ended famine's regularity in Western Europe?
**Answer:** Agricultural innovation and the Industrial Revolution.
