# Famines in history — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Famine death tolls are precisely known.
**Answer: False**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Medieval Europe suffered famines constantly.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The hunger winter happened under military stalemate.
**Answer: True**
