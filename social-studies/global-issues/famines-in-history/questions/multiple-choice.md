# Famines in history — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Medieval France suffered how many 'general' famines (10th–18th c.)?
a) 9  b) 89  c) 890
**Answer: b) 89**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Ancient famines are chronicled in…
a) India, Egypt, China, Greece and Rome  b) only Europe  c) only Africa
**Answer: a) India, Egypt, China, Greece and Rome**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What made Western European famine rare?
a) Agricultural innovation and the Industrial Revolution  b) Luck  c) Imports only
**Answer: a) Agricultural innovation and the Industrial Revolution**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Ireland's Great Famine ran…
a) 1846–51  b) 1900–05  c) 1789–99
**Answer: a) 1846–51**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The Netherlands' hunger winter was in…
a) 1816  b) 1944  c) 1918
**Answer: b) 1944**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Modern history's most disastrous famine, in the book, is Ethiopia's…
a) 1973  b) 1984–85  c) 1999
**Answer: b) 1984–85**
