# Video script — Famines in history

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Famines recur through recorded history — chronicled in ancient India, Egypt, Western Asia, China, Greece and Rome. Medieval Europe suffered them constantly: 89 'general' famines in France alone across the 10th–18th centuries, plus hundreds of local ones. Famine was not an interruption of the old world; it was one of its regular visitors. Agricultural innovation and the Industrial Revolution made Western European famine rare, with catastrophic exceptions: Ireland's 1846–51 Great Famine, and the Netherlands' 1944 hunger winter under military stalemate. Death tolls are notoriously disputed — famine counts its dead badly. In modern history, Ethiopia's 1984–85 famine stands as the most disastrous — the event against which this whole unit is quietly written.
