# Hands-on — Opec

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: the member states found they must cooperate and unite to coordinate their oil policies for the greatest return for their peoples, after the major oil companies had agreed among themselves to face the producing states with one policy; OPEC's goals — coordinating and unifying the member countries' oil policies, and setting ways to guarantee price stability in the world oil markets and protect the producers' interests; and oil prices' weight in the world economy — a fall in prices shrinks the revenues of states whose income is mainly oil, widens budget deficits, cuts spending, slows growth and raises unemployment, so price stability serves the growth of the producing states and of the whole world economy, while the great industrial states research energy alternatives with large budgets. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
