# Opec

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: the member states found they must cooperate and unite to coordinate their oil policies for the greatest return for their peoples, after the major oil companies had agreed among themselves to face the producing states with one policy; OPEC's goals — coordinating and unifying the member countries' oil policies, and setting ways to guarantee price stability in the world oil markets and protect the producers' interests; and oil prices' weight in the world economy — a fall in prices shrinks the revenues of states whose income is mainly oil, widens budget deficits, cuts spending, slows growth and raises unemployment, so price stability serves the growth of the producing states and of the whole world economy, while the great industrial states research energy alternatives with large budgets — as Saudi Arabia's books teach it. ## Sub lesson 2 — Saudi Arabia's way — the book's own teaching way

Saudi Arabia's own way: the Grade 10 Tracks book starts from the components that make a state strong and influential and Saudi Arabia's own foundations and components, then follows the Kingdom's work for Arab, Islamic and international causes, and surveys the organisations it belongs to — the European Union as a model of integration, the League of Arab States, the Muslim World League, the Organisation of Islamic Cooperation, OPEC, the United Nations and the G20.
