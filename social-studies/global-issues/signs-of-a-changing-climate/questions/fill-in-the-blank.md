# Signs of a changing climate — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or figure from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Rainfall that swings between too much and too little is ____ rainfall.
**Answer: erratic**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** There are fewer cold days and ____.
**Answer: frosts**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Malaria appears in formerly cold ____ as mosquitoes spread.
**Answer: highlands**
