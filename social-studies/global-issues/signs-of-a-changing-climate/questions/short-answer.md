# Signs of a changing climate — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Water and heat signs" in your own words.
**Answer:** Signs include erratic rainfall, higher air and ocean temperatures, floods and sea-level rise.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Ice, seasons and storms" in your own words.
**Answer:** Melting glaciers, fewer frosts, more heat waves, heavier storms in some regions and intensifying drought in others.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Diseases on the move" in your own words.
**Answer:** Malaria spreads into formerly cold highlands as mosquitoes move with the warming.
