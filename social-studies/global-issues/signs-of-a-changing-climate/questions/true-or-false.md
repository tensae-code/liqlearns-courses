# Signs of a changing climate — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Signs include erratic rainfall, higher air and ocean temperatures, floods and sea-level rise.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Melting glaciers, fewer frosts, more heat waves, heavier storms in some regions and intensifying drought in others.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Malaria spreads into formerly cold highlands as mosquitoes move with the warming.
**Answer: True**
