# Signs of a changing climate — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Water and heat signs"?
a) Signs include erratic rainfall, higher air and ocean temperatures, floods and sea-level rise  b) Melting glaciers, fewer frosts, more heat waves, heavier storms in some regions and intensifying drought in others  c) Malaria spreads into formerly cold highlands as mosquitoes move with the warming
**Answer: a) Signs include erratic rainfall, higher air and ocean temperatures, floods and sea-level rise**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Ice, seasons and storms"?
a) Melting glaciers, fewer frosts, more heat waves, heavier storms in some regions and intensifying drought in others  b) Malaria spreads into formerly cold highlands as mosquitoes move with the warming  c) Signs include erratic rainfall, higher air and ocean temperatures, floods and sea-level rise
**Answer: a) Melting glaciers, fewer frosts, more heat waves, heavier storms in some regions and intensifying drought in others**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Diseases on the move"?
a) Malaria spreads into formerly cold highlands as mosquitoes move with the warming  b) Signs include erratic rainfall, higher air and ocean temperatures, floods and sea-level rise  c) Melting glaciers, fewer frosts, more heat waves, heavier storms in some regions and intensifying drought in others
