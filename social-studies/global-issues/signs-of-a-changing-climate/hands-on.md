# Hands-on — Signs of a changing climate

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Wall of gauges
Draw a wall of gauges and photo-pairs — thermometer, rain gauge, glacier before/after, sea-level marker — each labelled with the sign it records from the lesson.

## Sign sort
Sort the lesson's signs into 'too much water', 'too little water', 'heat' and 'life responding'. One sign (erratic rainfall) belongs in two boxes; argue its double placing.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
