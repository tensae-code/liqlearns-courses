# Consequences of climate change — heat, rain, sea — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Heat waves raise deaths, partly offset by fewer…
a) flood deaths  b) cold-related illnesses  c) storms
**Answer: b) cold-related illnesses**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Lower latitudes face more…
a) snow  b) drought and unpredictable rainfall  c) steady rain
**Answer: b) drought and unpredictable rainfall**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The heaviest rain events are…
a) weakening  b) staying the same  c) intensifying
**Answer: c) intensifying**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Twentieth-century sea-level rise was about…
a) 1–2 cm  b) 17–19 cm  c) 1 metre
**Answer: b) 17–19 cm**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The two halves of sea-level rise are melting land ice and…
a) rain  b) thermal expansion  c) tides
**Answer: b) thermal expansion**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Salt water contaminating groundwater threatens…
a) mountain farms  b) coastal wells and farms  c) desert wells only
**Answer: b) coastal wells and farms**
