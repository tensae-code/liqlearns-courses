# Consequences of climate change — heat, rain, sea — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** How fast has sea level risen since 1993?
**Answer:** About 3.4 cm per decade — roughly twice the earlier rate.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Why is uneven rain a stress on agriculture?
**Answer:** Crops need rain that is enough and on time; heavy bursts plus faster drying between rains give neither.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Name two coastal harms of sea-level rise.
**Answer:** Any two of: higher storm surges, damaged reefs, salt-contaminated groundwater.
