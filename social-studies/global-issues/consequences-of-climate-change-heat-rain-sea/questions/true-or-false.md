# Consequences of climate change — heat, rain, sea — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Soils dry faster between rains even where total rain rises.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Sea level has risen more slowly since 1993.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Storm surges ride higher on a raised sea.
**Answer: True**
