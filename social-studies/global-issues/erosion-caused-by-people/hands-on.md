# Hands-on — Erosion Caused By People

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Human activities and erosion: deforestation, garden watering, agriculture shifting topsoil, mining, construction digging, and air pollution causing acid rain. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Pakistan's way
Say how Pakistan's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
