# Water — a Human Right or a Commodity? — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: Water: commodity, human right or force of life? The UN's 2010 recognition of water and sanitation as a human right (teacher guidance, Secundaria 4).
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson stands on a pupil's student book handed to the class.
**Answer: False — it stands on Bolivia's teacher compendium, cited as teacher-guide evidence only, counted nowhere.**
