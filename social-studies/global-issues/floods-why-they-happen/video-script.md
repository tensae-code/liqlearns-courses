# Video script — Floods Why They Happen

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Iran's book, the *Iran Grade 12 Geography (3)* (printed p. 89), teaches: Lesson 5: Floods (سیل) — a river's discharge (آبدهی / دبی) in cubic metres per second, changing through the year; the natural and human factors in a flood's happening (rainfall intensity and duration first); round basins whose streams arrive together overflowing, against elongated basins; the bridge destroyed by flood at Tang-e Kanesht in Kermanshah, and city rubbish and building waste blocking bridge mouths and worsening floods.

Iran's own way (Iran Grade 12 Geography (3)): 'applied' geography organised as three problems of Iranian life — settlements, transport, and natural hazards — each taken in two lessons (what it is, then how to manage it) with a geographical-skills section (GIS, time zones, remote sensing) closing each chapter.
