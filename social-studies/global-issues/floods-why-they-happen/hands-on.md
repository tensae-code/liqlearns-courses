# Hands-on — Floods Why They Happen

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Lesson 5: Floods (سیل) — a river's discharge (آبدهی / دبی) in cubic metres per second, changing through the year; the natural and human factors in a flood's happening (rainfall intensity and duration first); round basins whose streams arrive together overflowing, against elongated basins; the bridge destroyed by flood at Tang-e Kanesht in Kermanshah, and city rubbish and building waste blocking bridge mouths and worsening floods. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
