# Video script — Peace as a Human Right

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Morocco Grade 7 Fadaa Ijtiamiat 1AC, printed p. 181, teaches: peace as a human right and a condition for security between peoples, and the United Nations' role in world peace.

Morocco's own way: collège Social Studies carries a full Citizenship Education component built on ten value lessons in two axes — five personal-social values (dignity, freedom, equality, justice, solidarity), then tolerance, democracy, peace, the role of law, and a field visit to a human-rights institution — each lesson built from constitutional and international-charter texts.
