Turkey Grade 7 Sosyal Bilgiler 1. Kitap, printed p. 58, teaches: globalisation is the world becoming economically, culturally and politically more bound together as transport and communication speed up; it strengthens economic and cultural relations and also brings harms — local cultures weakening, epidemics spreading fast, environmental problems growing

Turkey's own way: the last middle-school year opens by widening the circle one final time — from how we talk to each other in a group, to the equality a constitution owes a child in a wheelchair, to the whole society closing ranks on a national issue, and finally to the country's own seat at the world's problem-solving table.
