# Hands-on — Humanitarian Work and Its Martyrs

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Martyrs of humanitarian work
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: humanitarian work (العمل الإنساني) defined as helping people whose lives are threatened — by killing, hunger, illness, displacement — an independent, neutral work directed to everyone without distinction of sex, colour, religion or political affiliation; World Humanitarian Day (19 August, set by the UN General Assembly); the Zayed Humanitarian Work Day on 19 Ramadan; and honouring those killed doing this work Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## United Arab Emirates' way — the book's own teaching way
Say how United Arab Emirates's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
