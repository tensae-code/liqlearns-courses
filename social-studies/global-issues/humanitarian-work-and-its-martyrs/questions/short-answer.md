# Humanitarian Work and Its Martyrs — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does part 1 of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Martyrs of humanitarian work — humanitarian work (العمل الإنساني) defined as helping people whose lives are threatened — by killing, hunger, illness, displacement — an independent, neutral work directed to everyone without distinction of sex, colour, religion or political affiliation; World Humanitarian Day (19 August, set by the UN General Assembly); the Zayed Humanitarian Work Day on 19 Ramadan; and honouring those killed doing this work (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does part 2 of this lesson teach?
**Answer: United Arab Emirates' way — the book's own teaching way — citizenship at Grade 6 is a whole unit of its own between History and Geography — its duties are split into what the law compels and what the citizen volunteers, its model citizens are the mothers of martyrs, and its proof of goodness is measured in tonnes of aid flown to a hundred countries. (any close wording).**
