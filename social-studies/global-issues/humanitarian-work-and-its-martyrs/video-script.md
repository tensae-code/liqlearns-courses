# Video script — Humanitarian Work and Its Martyrs

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

United Arab Emirates Social Studies & National Education, G6 Term 1, printed p. 90, teaches: humanitarian work (العمل الإنساني) defined as helping people whose lives are threatened — by killing, hunger, illness, displacement — an independent, neutral work directed to everyone without distinction of sex, colour, religion or political affiliation; World Humanitarian Day (19 August, set by the UN General Assembly); the Zayed Humanitarian Work Day on 19 Ramadan; and honouring those killed doing this work

United Arab Emirates' own way: citizenship at Grade 6 is a whole unit of its own between History and Geography — its duties are split into what the law compels and what the citizen volunteers, its model citizens are the mothers of martyrs, and its proof of goodness is measured in tonnes of aid flown to a hundred countries.
