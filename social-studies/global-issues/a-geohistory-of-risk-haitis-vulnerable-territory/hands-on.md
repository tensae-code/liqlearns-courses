# Hands-on — A Geohistory of Risk — Haiti's Vulnerable Territory

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Order the disasters
Put the lesson's three ages of the island — Quisqueya, Saint-Domingue, Haiti — in order, and say what kind of map the sequence builds from their disasters.

## One place, three names
Draw one outline of the island and write its three names on it in order. Tell your companion why one ground can carry three names.
