# Hands-on — Why Pakistan Is Underdeveloped

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Causes of underdevelopment in Pakistan: the energy crisis, wastage of natural resources, foreign debt, rapid population growth pressing on resources, and low literacy. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Pakistan's way
Say how Pakistan's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
