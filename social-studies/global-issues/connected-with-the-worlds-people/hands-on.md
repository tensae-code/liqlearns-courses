# Hands-on — Connected with the Worlds People

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: the 2020 Tokyo Olympics and Paralympics, where people from every country gather across their differences; things from other countries in our own daily life; and greetings that differ by country — Japan's bow, America's handshake, India's namaste, Mongolia's sain bainaa. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Japan's way
Say how Japan's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
