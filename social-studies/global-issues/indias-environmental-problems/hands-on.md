# Hands-on — India's Environmental Problems

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Map the pressure
Draw a fast-growing city. Around it write the four pressures from the lesson: air pollution, water pollution, deforestation, land degradation. Draw an arrow from the growing city to each one.

## Problems, then responses
Write the chapter's order in two lines: first the environmental problems India faces, then the turn to responses. Name the river the chapter names with the water pollution.
