# India's Environmental Problems — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the two pollutions the chapter names, and the river it names with one of them.
**Answer: Air pollution and water pollution — the Ganges is named with the water pollution (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What happens to India's forests and land in this chapter?
**Answer: Deforestation — forests cut down — and land degradation, land worn out and made worse (any close wording).**
