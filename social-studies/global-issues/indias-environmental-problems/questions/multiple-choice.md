# India's Environmental Problems — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Chapter 4's pressure begins with —
a) rapid population and city growth  b) falling population  c) Iceland's fishing
**Answer: a) rapid population and city growth**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The river named with India's water pollution is —
a) the Ganges  b) the Nile  c) the Amazon
**Answer: a) the Ganges**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Deforestation means —
a) forests being cut down  b) planting new forests  c) a kind of rain
**Answer: a) forests being cut down**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** After the problems, the chapter turns to —
a) the responses to them  b) India's religions  c) the three seasons
**Answer: a) the responses to them**
