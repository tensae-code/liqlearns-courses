# India's Environmental Problems

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: India is growing fast — more people, bigger cities — and that growth presses hard on its air, its water, its forests and its land. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Growth that presses on the land and water

Chapter 4 of Iceland's India guide, Umhverfismál — environmental matters — begins with the pressure: rapid population growth and rapid city growth. More people in fast-growing cities means more pressure on everything around them, and the chapter names the results: air pollution and water pollution — the Ganges, India's great river, named among the waters harmed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Pollution, forests and land — and responses

The pressure reaches the land as well: deforestation — forests cut down — and land degradation, land worn out and made worse. The chapter does not stop at the problems: it turns to the responses, what is done against them. Problems first, then responses — that is the chapter's order.
