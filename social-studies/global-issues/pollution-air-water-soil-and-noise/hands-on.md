# Hands-on — Pollution Air Water Soil And Noise

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Pollution and its kinds, each with causes and effects: air pollution, water pollution (most of Pakistan's water unfit to drink), soil pollution and noise pollution. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Pakistan's way
Say how Pakistan's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
