# Video script — What a Desert Is

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Picture a place where almost no rain falls.

Less than 250 millimetres in a whole year.

That is a desert. Not hot — dry.

Even ice can be a desert.

The two biggest deserts are the cold ones at the poles.

Then comes the Sahara, wide and hot.

Four kinds: polar, subtropical, cold-winter, cool coastal.

A desert is made by missing rain.
