# Hands-on — What a Desert Is

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Rain gauge of the mind
Find out, from an elder, a teacher or a weather record if one is near, roughly how much rain your place gets in a year. Compare it with 250 mm. Is your place near desert dryness, or far from it? Write the comparison.

## Four-type hunt
On any world map you can find, point to one place for each desert type: a polar desert, a subtropical desert, a cold-winter desert, a cool coastal desert. Write what helped you place each one.
