# What a Desert Is — sub lessons and depth

The three sub lessons follow the book's own order in §7.1: the definition first, then the types, then the greatest deserts. This is a Grade 7-depth lesson; the Grade 8 climb on deserts comes inside Desertification and the later climate lessons of this title.

## Sub lesson 1 — Rain, not heat
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: define a desert as under 250 mm of rain a year and state that about a third of the Earth's land is desert.

## Sub lesson 2 — The four types by location
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: name polar, subtropical, cold-winter and cool coastal deserts.

## Sub lesson 3 — The greatest deserts
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: rank Antarctic (14,200,000 km²), Arctic (13,900,000 km²) and Sahara (9,200,000 km²), and place the Great Basin and the Gobi as the book's table does.
