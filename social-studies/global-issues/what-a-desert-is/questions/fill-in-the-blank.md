# What a Desert Is — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A desert receives under ______ mm of rain a year.
**Answer: 250.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The four desert types are polar, subtropical, cold-winter and cool ______ deserts.
**Answer: coastal.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The second-largest desert is the ______ desert, at 13,900,000 km².
**Answer: Arctic.**
