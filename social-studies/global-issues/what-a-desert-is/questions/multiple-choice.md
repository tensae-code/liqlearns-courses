# What a Desert Is — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A desert is a place that receives —
a) more than 1,000 mm of rain a year  b) under 250 mm of rain a year  c) no rain at all, ever
**Answer: b) under 250 mm of rain a year** — the book's definition is rainfall under 250 mm a year.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** About how much of the Earth's land is desert?
a) About two-thirds  b) About one-tenth  c) About one-third
**Answer: c) About one-third** — the book's figure is about a third.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Which of these is NOT one of the four desert types by location?
a) Polar desert  b) Cool coastal desert  c) Mountain-top desert
**Answer: c) Mountain-top desert** — the four are polar, subtropical, cold-winter and cool coastal.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** The largest desert in the world is —
a) the Sahara  b) the Antarctic desert  c) the Gobi
**Answer: b) the Antarctic desert** — the Antarctic desert, 14,200,000 km², is the largest.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** The Sahara's area, as the book prints it, is —
a) 9,200,000 km²  b) 13,900,000 km²  c) 492,098 km²
**Answer: a) 9,200,000 km²** — the Sahara is third, at 9,200,000 km².
