# What a Desert Is — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the four types of desert by location.
**Answer:** Polar deserts, subtropical deserts, cold-winter deserts and cool coastal deserts.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** A classmate says the Sahara is the world's largest desert. Correct them, with figures.
**Answer:** The Sahara (9,200,000 km²) is only third; the cold polar deserts are larger — Antarctic 14,200,000 km² and Arctic 13,900,000 km².
