# What a Desert Is — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Heat is the defining mark of a desert.
**Answer: False** — the defining mark is low rainfall, not heat.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The Gobi is counted as a cold desert.
**Answer: True** — Asia's Gobi is counted cold in the book's table.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The Arctic desert is larger than the Sahara.
**Answer: True** — Arctic 13,900,000 km² against Sahara 9,200,000 km².

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Two-thirds of the world's land is desert.
**Answer: False** — about one-third of the Earth's land is desert.
