# Hands-on — The Organisation of Islamic Cooperation

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: The OIC's principles (the right of self-determination; strengthening human rights and basic freedoms, good governance and the rule of law), its political, economic, cultural and social aims, and its main organs. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
