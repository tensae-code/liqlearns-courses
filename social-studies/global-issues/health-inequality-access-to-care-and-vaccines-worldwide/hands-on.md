# Hands-on — Health Inequality — Access to Care and Vaccines Worldwide

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Map the gap
Draw a rough world map and shade it lightly where the lesson says care and vaccines are easy to reach, darkly where they are hard to reach. Add a legend.

## The home case
Say, from the lesson, which disease is Haiti's home case in this séance, and tell your companion one thing its map shows.
