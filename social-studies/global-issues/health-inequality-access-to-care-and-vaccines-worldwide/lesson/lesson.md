## Sub lesson 1 — Health inequality — access to care and vaccines worldwide

Haiti's Grade 8 sequence maps health inequality worldwide: the cartography of health resources, access to care and access to vaccines, with Covid-19 in Haiti as the home case — the gap between rich and poor countries is drawn on a map before it is argued in words.

## Sub lesson 2 — Haiti's way — map the care, then question the gap

This is a teacher sequence, written for the teacher, not handed to the pupil. In Haiti's Projet Nectar sequence world inequality is questioned through the words used for it and through Haiti's own case; the health séance follows the same way — authenticated statistics and maps, crossed from the local scale to the world scale.

Haiti's own way (teacher sequence — evidence): World inequality is questioned through the words used for it (Nord/Sud, développé/sous-développé) and through Haiti's own case — a rich-country/poor-country comparison (Canada or USA vs Haiti), the independence debt, food insecurity and Covid vaccination in Haiti — with every claim built from authenticated statistics, graphs and maps crossed from local to world scale. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
