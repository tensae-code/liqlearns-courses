# Video script — Health Inequality — Access to Care and Vaccines Worldwide

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Haiti's Grade 8 sequence maps health inequality worldwide: the cartography of health resources, access to care and access to vaccines, with Covid-19 in Haiti as the home case — the gap between rich and poor countries is drawn on a map before it is argued in words.

This is a teacher sequence, written for the teacher, not handed to the pupil. In Haiti's Projet Nectar sequence world inequality is questioned through the words used for it and through Haiti's own case; the health séance follows the same way — authenticated statistics and maps, crossed from the local scale to the world scale.
