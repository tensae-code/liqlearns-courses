Somalia's own way: by Grade 6 the subject is open political education — the enemies of unity are named in a row (colonialism, racism, clanism, Zionism), the world's organisation is studied, the state's doctrine is taught as science, and citizenship becomes fiscal: the book ends with taxes and with the newspapers that keep a citizen informed.
