# World Zionism as the Book Teaches It — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The part of this lesson called "Sahyuuniyadda Caalamiga (World Zionism)" is mainly about —
a) world Zionism presented in the state's own political-education framing as a global movement the pupil is taught to oppose — filed as Somalia's own way of teaching world politics  b) ancient kings only  c) the Moon's phases
