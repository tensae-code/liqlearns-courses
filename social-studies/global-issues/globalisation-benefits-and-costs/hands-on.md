# Hands-on — Globalisation Benefits and Costs

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Globalisation as the all-round drawing-together of countries into one system of technological, financial, economic, political and cultural ties on the newest communication technologies; its contradictory effects — the growth of transnational corporations and of the IMF, World Bank and WTO, pressure on national economies toward one model, the weakening of state sovereignty and the need for supranational institutions, with economic globalisation running far ahead of political. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
