# Video script — Human Trafficking

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then. This script is written in full — the placements name a full script need for this lesson.

---

A man comes with sweet words. Good work. Big pay. Come now, decide now.

Stop. Ask three questions.

Who exactly employs me? Where exactly will I sleep? Can my family visit and phone me?

Trafficking begins with a promise that cannot be checked.

People are moved by lies, hidden, and worked for another man's profit.

That is slavery with a new name.

Under eighteen, it is child trafficking — always a crime, never a job.

Check the promise. Tell your family. Travel in the open.
