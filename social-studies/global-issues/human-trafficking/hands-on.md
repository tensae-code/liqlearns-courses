# Hands-on — Human Trafficking

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The interview (the book's own task)
Interview someone who knows migration's horror — a returnee, a family member, a teacher — with permission, and write the one-page report the book asks for. Do not press for painful detail; record what they choose to tell.

## Poster against the poster
Make a warning poster for your school or kebele notice place: the broker's three sweetest promises on one side, the three checking questions on the other, and the under-18 line at the foot.
