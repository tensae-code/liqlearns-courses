# Human Trafficking — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Group the causes of trafficking as the book groups them, with one example each.
**Answer:** Social, economic and political — for example family breakup (social), poverty (economic), weak government or conflict and instability (political).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Answer the book's question in two sentences: if you were Ethiopia's leader, how would you solve migration?
**Answer:** The learner's own answer, judged for using the lesson: work and education at home, action against brokers, protection for migrants — the book's own leader task.
