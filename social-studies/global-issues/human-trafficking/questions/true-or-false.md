# Human Trafficking — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Victims' wages are paid fairly once they arrive.
**Answer: False** — wages are stolen — forced work for the trafficker's profit.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Some victims in Ethiopia kill themselves under the suffering.
**Answer: True** — the book states it plainly.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Lack of education and jobs are among the causes.
**Answer: True** — they close the book's cause list.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Poor sanitation and inadequate nutrition are part of what victims suffer, on the guide's account.
**Answer: True** — both are itemised (teacher guide — evidence).
