# Human Trafficking — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Brokers ______ rural youth to cities for labour exploitation.
**Answer: lure.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In the worst cases, organs such as the kidney and ______ are taken for sale.
**Answer: heart.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Child trafficking is the illegal recruiting or transporting of a person under ______ for exploitation.
**Answer: 18 (eighteen).**
