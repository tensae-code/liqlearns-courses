# Human Trafficking — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Trafficking moves people by —
a) free choice with full information  b) force or false promises  c) government resettlement
**Answer: b) force or false promises** — force or false promises is the definition's hinge.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Trafficking is called the symbol of —
a) modern slavery  b) modern trade  c) urban life
**Answer: a) modern slavery** — victims are hidden and worked for another's profit.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Children are trafficked into —
a) schools  b) begging  c) apprenticeships
**Answer: b) begging** — children into begging, youth into war, women into hidden domestic service and forced marriage.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In Ethiopia the main victims are —
a) retired farmers  b) poor youth migrating illegally  c) university lecturers
**Answer: b) poor youth migrating illegally** — poor youth migrating illegally are the book's named victims.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Child trafficking, in the guide's definition, concerns any person under —
a) 16  b) 21  c) 18
**Answer: c) 18** — under 18 — the guide's keyed line.
