# Human Trafficking — sub lessons and depth

Depth structure: sub lessons 1–2 are the student book's pupil depth (printed pp. 156–159). Sub lesson 3 climbs with the guide (printed pp. 114–116, teacher guide — evidence): the under-18 child definition and the health bill the student book does not itemise. The guide's keyed causes are shorter than the student book's; the student book's grouping is the one taught.

## Sub lesson 1 — The modern slavery
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: define trafficking, say why it is called the symbol of modern slavery, name who is hidden in what work, and group the causes.

## Sub lesson 2 — Ethiopia's own wound
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: name the main victims in Ethiopia and what they suffer, and carry the book's interview and leader tasks.

## Sub lesson 3 — Child trafficking and the health bill (teacher guide — evidence)
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: state the under-18 definition and itemise the victim's sufferings and health consequences.
