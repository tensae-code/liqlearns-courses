# Video script — Rapid Population Growth — Effects

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Growth outruns everything built for fewer people.

The family pays: food, clothes, school fees, strain.

The town pays: crowded schools, crowded clinics.

The country pays: money spent coping, not growing.

The forest pays: felled for fuel, homes and farms.

And the guide names the engine — overload.

More people, more wood cut, fewer trees standing.
