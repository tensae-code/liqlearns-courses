# Lesson plan — Rapid Population Growth — Effects

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Global Issues · **Lesson:** Rapid Population Growth — Effects
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 14 — Global Issues (CURRICULUM.md) — the sixth lesson of the title, after Rapid Population Growth — Causes (TITLES.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** rapid growth costs four ways at once — family, social, economic and environmental — and the teacher guide names the engine inside the environmental cost: demographic overload, where a wood-dependent population cuts trees in great quantity and deforestation follows.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Rapid Population Growth — Causes. Sub lessons 1–2 are the Grade 7 student book's fourfold cost; sub lesson 3 is the Addis Ababa guide's overload mechanism, folded in as labelled teacher evidence — the lesson still stands on the student book, so its acceptance count stays 1 country.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the
exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an
image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture
of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does
with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and
while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is
dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the
student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph,
or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be
shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Rapid Population Growth — Causes — the effects answer the drivers. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. List the four cost families: family, social, economic, environmental.
2. Give two costs inside each family.
3. Explain demographic overload (teacher guide — evidence): why rapid growth cuts trees in great quantity in Ethiopia.
4. Name the guide's keyed problem list that follows from overload.
