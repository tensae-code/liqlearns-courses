# Rapid Population Growth — Effects — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The four cost families are family, social, economic and ______.
**Answer: environmental.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Capital diverted to social services makes per-capita ______ fall.
**Answer: income (product and income).**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Demographic ______ is the guide's name for population pressure that cuts trees in great quantity.
**Answer: overload.**
