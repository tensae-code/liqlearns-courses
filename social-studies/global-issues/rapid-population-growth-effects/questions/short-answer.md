# Rapid Population Growth — Effects — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Trace the environmental cost chain from growth to air pollution.
**Answer:** Growth fells forest for building, fuel and farmland; the felling is followed by erosion and air pollution.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the guide say happens where population grows with no control mechanism?
**Answer:** Uncontrolled growth depletes and destroys the Earth's natural resources (teacher guide — evidence).
