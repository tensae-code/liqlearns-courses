# Rapid Population Growth — Effects — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Parental economic and psychological strain is one of the family costs.
**Answer: True** — it is on the family list.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Per-capita income rises when capital is diverted to coping services.
**Answer: False** — per-capita product and income fall.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Demographic overload causes deforestation, on the guide's teaching.
**Answer: True** — that is the guide's keyed mechanism.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** The guide's problem list from overload includes water pollution and the greenhouse effect.
**Answer: True** — both are on the keyed list.
