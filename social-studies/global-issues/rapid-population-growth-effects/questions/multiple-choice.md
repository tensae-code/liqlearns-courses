# Rapid Population Growth — Effects — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Pressure on schools, health, housing, water and electricity is which cost family?
a) Family  b) Social  c) Economic
**Answer: b) Social** — shared services stretched — a social cost.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Higher mother and child deaths are listed under —
a) family costs  b) environmental costs  c) political costs
**Answer: a) family costs** — the book lists them in the family costs.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Capital diverted from productive investment to social services causes —
a) per-capita product and income to fall  b) exports to rise  c) forests to regrow
**Answer: a) per-capita product and income to fall** — that diversion is the economic cost.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Forest felled for building, fuel and farmland is followed by —
a) rainfall and clean air  b) erosion and air pollution  c) cheaper food
**Answer: b) erosion and air pollution** — erosion and air pollution follow the felling.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** In the guide's overload mechanism, trees are cut in great quantity mainly because most people depend on wood for —
a) export  b) fuel, house construction and tools  c) ceremonies only
**Answer: b) fuel, house construction and tools** — fuel, building and tools — the guide's keyed reason.
