# Hands-on — Rapid Population Growth — Effects

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## One service under pressure
Choose one shared service near you — a school, a clinic, a water point. Ask an elder how it served people when they were young, and write what growth has changed: queues, crowding, cost. Present the service's story in the lesson's cost language.

## Wood-use diary
For one day, note every use of wood or charcoal in your home: cooking, heating, building, tools. Total them. This is the demand the guide's overload mechanism describes — your household's line in it.
