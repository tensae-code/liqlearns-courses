# Rapid Population Growth — Effects — sub lessons and depth

Depth structure: sub lessons 1–2 are the student book's fourfold cost (Grade 7 pupil depth). Sub lesson 3 climbs one step — the guide's demographic-overload mechanism that explains the environmental cost from inside (teacher guide — evidence, printed pp. 108–110). The lesson stands on the student book; the guide part is evidence folded in, not the lesson's base.

## Sub lesson 1 — The family and social costs
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: list family costs (food shortage, rising costs, strain, higher mother and child deaths) and social costs (pressure on schools, health, housing, water, electricity).

## Sub lesson 2 — The economic and environmental costs
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: state the capital-diversion cost and the falling per-capita product/income; state the forest-felling cost and its erosion and air-pollution sequel.

## Sub lesson 3 — Demographic overload — the engine (teacher guide — evidence)
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: explain overload: wood for fuel, building and tools drives great-quantity tree cutting, causing deforestation, and name the guide's keyed problem list.
