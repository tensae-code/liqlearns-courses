# Desertification — Causes and Defence — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** 850,000,000 people are affected, on the book's figures.
**Answer: True** — that is the book's printed figure.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** City growth is one of the causes the book lists.
**Answer: True** — city growth is on the causes list.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Desertification is caused only by the climate, never by people.
**Answer: False** — every cause the book lists is a human activity.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Population beyond what resources can carry is one of the causes.
**Answer: True** — it closes the book's causes list.
