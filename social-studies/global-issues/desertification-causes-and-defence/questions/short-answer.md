# Desertification — Causes and Defence — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** List four causes of desertification from the book.
**Answer:** Any four of: overgrazing beyond the land's capacity; deforestation; backward ploughing; unbounded chemical fertiliser and pesticide use; city growth; air and soil pollution; population beyond what resources can carry.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Name one desertifying activity in your own woreda and one remedy for it.
**Answer:** An activity from the learner's own place (for example overgrazing, tree-cutting for fuel, ploughing that loses soil) with a workable remedy (graze within capacity, replant and protect trees, plough to hold the soil).
