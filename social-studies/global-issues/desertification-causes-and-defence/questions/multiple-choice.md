# Desertification — Causes and Defence — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** On the book's UN (1991) figures, desertification threatens —
a) 35% of the world  b) 5% of the world  c) 90% of the world
**Answer: a) 35% of the world** — 35% of the world — 4.5 billion hectares.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The continent worst hit by desertification is —
a) Europe  b) Africa  c) Australia
**Answer: b) Africa** — Africa, two-thirds of whose land is desert, is worst hit.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Which is a cause of desertification in the book's list?
a) Overgrazing beyond the land's capacity  b) Planting trees  c) Terracing slopes
**Answer: a) Overgrazing beyond the land's capacity** — overgrazing beyond capacity is on the list.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which area does 4.5 billion hectares equal, in the book's comparison?
a) Africa alone  b) The two Americas  c) Asia
**Answer: b) The two Americas** — the book compares it to the two Americas.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** The book's own defence task asks pupils to —
a) memorise the world map of deserts  b) list their woreda's desertifying activities and propose remedies  c) write to the UN
**Answer: b) list their woreda's desertifying activities and propose remedies** — the defence begins with one's own woreda.
