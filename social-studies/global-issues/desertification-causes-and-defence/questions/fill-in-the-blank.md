# Desertification — Causes and Defence — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Desertification threatens ______ % of the world, on the book's UN (1991) figures.
**Answer: 35.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Grazing animals beyond the land's capacity is called ______.
**Answer: overgrazing.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Two-thirds of ______'s land is already desert, and it is the worst-hit continent.
**Answer: Africa.**
