# Video script — Desertification — Causes and Defence

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A desert can grow.

Grass goes. Trees go. The soil washes away.

That spreading is called desertification.

People cause it — too many animals, cut forests, careless ploughing.

Africa is hit the worst.

But what people cause, people can answer.

Hold the soil. Plant the trees. Graze with care.
