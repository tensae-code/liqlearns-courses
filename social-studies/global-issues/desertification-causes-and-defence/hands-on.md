# Hands-on — Desertification — Causes and Defence

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Slope watch
Choose one slope or bare patch near home or school. Draw it as it is today: what covers it, where water runs in rain. Under the drawing, write the three changes that would push it toward desert, and one change that would hold it.

## Woreda list, for real
With family or neighbours, list the desertifying activities in your woreda — the book's own task. Bring the list back and sort it: which cause from the lesson is each activity an example of?
