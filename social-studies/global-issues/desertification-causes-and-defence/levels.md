# Desertification — Causes and Defence — sub lessons and depth

The three sub lessons follow the placement's own run: the spreading and its scale, the causes, the defence the book sets as a local task. Grade 7 depth; the Grade 8 book revisits spreading deserts as a climate effect later in this title.

## Sub lesson 1 — Desert spreading
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: state the UN (1991) figures as printed and say Africa is worst hit, two-thirds of its land already desert.

## Sub lesson 2 — The causes
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: list the book's causes: overgrazing, deforestation, backward ploughing, unbounded fertiliser/pesticide use, city growth, air and soil pollution, population beyond resources.

## Sub lesson 3 — The defence
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: name a desertifying activity in one's own woreda and propose a remedy for it.
