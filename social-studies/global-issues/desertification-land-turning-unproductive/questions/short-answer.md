# Desertification — land turning unproductive — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Give the UNCCD definition.
**Answer:** Human-induced land degradation in dry and sub-humid regions.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How much land is at risk, in the book's accounting?
**Answer:** About 5.45 billion hectares — 47% of the land.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Why is vulnerability mapping hard?
**Answer:** The processes are multiple and complex.
