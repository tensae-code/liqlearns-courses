# Desertification — land turning unproductive — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** About a quarter of the world's land is already degraded.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Desertification is the Sahara marching south as an army of sand.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The Sahel drought of the early 1970s preceded the Nairobi conference.
**Answer: True**
