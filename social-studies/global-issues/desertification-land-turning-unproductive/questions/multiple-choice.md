# Desertification — land turning unproductive — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Desertification is…
a) natural deserts expanding  b) human-induced land degradation in dry regions  c) sandstorms
**Answer: b) human-induced land degradation in dry regions**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The definition used is the…
a) UNCCD's  b) World Bank's  c) one school's
**Answer: a) UNCCD's**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** It can only occur on land that is…
a) wet  b) vulnerable to it  c) mountainous
**Answer: b) vulnerable to it**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** The 1977 conference on it was held in…
a) Nairobi  b) Cairo  c) Addis Ababa
**Answer: a) Nairobi**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Drylands at risk are about… of the land
a) 5%  b) 47%  c) 90%
**Answer: b) 47%**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** People exposed number up to…
a) 3 million  b) 3 billion  c) 300,000
**Answer: b) 3 billion**
