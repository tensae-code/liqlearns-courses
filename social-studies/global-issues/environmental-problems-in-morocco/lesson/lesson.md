# Environmental Problems in Morocco

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: Morocco's Grade 5 teacher guide closes its Geography with the homeland's environmental problems — pollution and the water problem — and answers them with positive behaviours and the Green Generation 2020-2030 strategy. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Pollution and the water problem

The twelfth Geography lesson of Morocco's Grade 5 guide is Environmental problems in my homeland — desertification, pollution and the water problem. This lesson carries its pollution and water half: the pollution that harms the homeland, and the water problem — water scarce where it is needed — named as problems of Morocco itself. (The lesson's desertification part is stocked with the course's desertification lesson.)

## Sub lesson 2 — Positive behaviours and Green Generation 2020-2030

The guide does not leave the learner with problems only. It teaches positive behaviours — what the learner themselves does for the land and water — and sets them inside the country's own plan: the Green Generation 2020-2030 strategy.
