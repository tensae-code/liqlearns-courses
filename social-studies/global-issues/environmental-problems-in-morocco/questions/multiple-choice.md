# Environmental Problems in Morocco — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Morocco's Geography Lesson 12 teaches —
a) environmental problems in my homeland  b) only ancient history  c) only sea ports
**Answer: a) environmental problems in my homeland**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Besides desertification, the lesson names —
a) pollution and the water problem  b) only earthquakes  c) only snow
**Answer: a) pollution and the water problem**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The lesson answers the problems with —
a) positive behaviours  b) doing nothing  c) more pollution
**Answer: a) positive behaviours**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The country's strategy named in the lesson is —
a) Green Generation 2020-2030  b) a railway plan  c) a port plan
**Answer: a) Green Generation 2020-2030**
