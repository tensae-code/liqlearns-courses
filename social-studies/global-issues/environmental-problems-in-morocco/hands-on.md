# Hands-on — Environmental Problems in Morocco

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Name the problems near you
Write down one pollution problem and one water problem you have seen near your home, as the lesson names pollution and the water problem for Morocco.

## Practise a positive behaviour
Choose one positive behaviour from the lesson's spirit — one real thing that protects land or water — and do it this week. Write what you did.
