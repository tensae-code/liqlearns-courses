# Video script — Managing Hazards Before During After

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Iran's book, the *Iran Grade 12 Geography (3)* (printed p. 100), teaches: Lesson 6 Managing natural hazards (مدیریت مخاطرات طبیعی): hazard and crisis (مخاطره و بحران); the signs that may come before an earthquake — changes in the groundwater in wells as the rock layers are squeezed; preventing settlements, activity centres and cities from being built around active faults; relief and rescue centres and shelters with tents and emergency supplies ready; what to do during the quake — not using stairs or lifts, keeping away from tall buildings, lamp posts and shop signs in the street, stopping the car at the roadside.

Iran's own way (Iran Grade 12 Geography (3)): 'applied' geography organised as three problems of Iranian life — settlements, transport, and natural hazards — each taken in two lessons (what it is, then how to manage it) with a geographical-skills section (GIS, time zones, remote sensing) closing each chapter.
