# Hands-on — Managing Hazards Before During After

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Lesson 6 Managing natural hazards (مدیریت مخاطرات طبیعی): hazard and crisis (مخاطره و بحران); the signs that may come before an earthquake — changes in the groundwater in wells as the rock layers are squeezed; preventing settlements, activity centres and cities from being built around active faults; relief and rescue centres and shelters with tents and emergency supplies ready; what to do during the quake — not using stairs or lifts, keeping away from tall buildings, lamp posts and shop signs in the street, stopping the car at the roadside. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
