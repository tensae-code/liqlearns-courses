# Video script — Pandemics in history — from early plagues to Spanish Flu

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Pandemics became possible when farming, settlement and contact linked the world: from malaria, tuberculosis, leprosy, influenza and smallpox to the eleven accelerated pandemics crowned by the 1918 Spanish Flu. First, from old diseases to worldwide ones: A pandemic is the worst case in infectious disease: an outbreak gone worldwide. Then, eleven pandemics — and malaria's toll: About eleven accelerated pandemics are counted up to the most famous: the 1918 Spanish Flu, named from wire-service reports of a Madrid outbreak — the name records where the news came from, not necessarily where the flu began. Hold those two parts together and you hold the lesson.
