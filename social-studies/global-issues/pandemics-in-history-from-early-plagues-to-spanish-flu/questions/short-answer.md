# Pandemics in history — from early plagues to Spanish Flu — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What three changes made worldwide pandemics possible?
**Answer:** Farming, settlement and inter-regional contact.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why is the 1918 flu called 'Spanish'?
**Answer:** From wire-service reports of a Madrid outbreak — the name records the news source.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the five early-arrival diseases listed.
**Answer:** Malaria, tuberculosis, leprosy, influenza and smallpox.
