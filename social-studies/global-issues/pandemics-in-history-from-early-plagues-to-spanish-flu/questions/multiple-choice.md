# Pandemics in history — from early plagues to Spanish Flu — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A pandemic is…
a) a local outbreak  b) the worst case — infectious disease gone worldwide  c) a cure
**Answer: b) the worst case — infectious disease gone worldwide**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Communicable diseases date from…
a) 1918  b) hunter-gatherer life  c) the age of flight
**Answer: b) hunter-gatherer life**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Worldwide pandemics became possible with…
a) farming, settlement and inter-regional contact  b) antibiotics  c) maps
**Answer: a) farming, settlement and inter-regional contact**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Early travelling diseases include…
a) malaria, tuberculosis, leprosy, influenza, smallpox  b) COVID-19  c) Ebola only
**Answer: a) malaria, tuberculosis, leprosy, influenza, smallpox**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The Spanish Flu's name comes from…
a) its true origin, proven  b) wire-service reports of a Madrid outbreak  c) a Spanish doctor
**Answer: b) wire-service reports of a Madrid outbreak**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Malaria, as the book reports it, kills a child in Africa roughly every…
a) 30 seconds  b) 30 days  c) 30 years
**Answer: a) 30 seconds**
