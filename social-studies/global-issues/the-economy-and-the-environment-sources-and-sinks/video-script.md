# Video script — The economy and the environment — sources and sinks

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Economics allocates scarce resources — land, labour, capital, entrepreneurship, sometimes energy — among limitless wants, at an opportunity cost. Every allocation draws on somewhere and discards to somewhere; this unit names those two places.

The environment is where the resources come from and where the waste goes: a flow of raw materials and energy from planetary sources, through the economy, into planetary sinks. The economy sits inside the environment, not beside it.

Population and industrial growth press on both ends of the flow. Environmental resources, ultimately finite, grow scarcer as activity expands — the pressure that every later lesson in this unit measures.
