# The economy and the environment — sources and sinks — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Economics allocates scarce resources among limitless wants at an opportunity cost.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The environment supplies raw materials and energy (sources) and receives waste (sinks).
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Environmental resources are ultimately finite and grow scarcer as activity expands.
**Answer: True**
