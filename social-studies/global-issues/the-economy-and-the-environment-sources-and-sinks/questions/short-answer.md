# The economy and the environment — sources and sinks — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Allocation at an opportunity cost" in your own words.
**Answer:** Economics allocates scarce resources among limitless wants at an opportunity cost.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Sources and sinks" in your own words.
**Answer:** The environment supplies raw materials and energy (sources) and receives waste (sinks).

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Finite, and pressed" in your own words.
**Answer:** Environmental resources are ultimately finite and grow scarcer as activity expands.
