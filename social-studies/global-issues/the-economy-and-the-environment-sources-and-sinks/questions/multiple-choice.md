# The economy and the environment — sources and sinks — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Allocation at an opportunity cost"?
a) Economics allocates scarce resources among limitless wants at an opportunity cost  b) The environment supplies raw materials and energy (sources) and receives waste (sinks)  c) Environmental resources are ultimately finite and grow scarcer as activity expands
**Answer: a) Economics allocates scarce resources among limitless wants at an opportunity cost**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Sources and sinks"?
a) The environment supplies raw materials and energy (sources) and receives waste (sinks)  b) Environmental resources are ultimately finite and grow scarcer as activity expands  c) Economics allocates scarce resources among limitless wants at an opportunity cost
**Answer: a) The environment supplies raw materials and energy (sources) and receives waste (sinks)**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Finite, and pressed"?
a) Environmental resources are ultimately finite and grow scarcer as activity expands  b) Economics allocates scarce resources among limitless wants at an opportunity cost  c) The environment supplies raw materials and energy (sources) and receives waste (sinks)
**Answer: a) Environmental resources are ultimately finite and grow scarcer as activity expands**
