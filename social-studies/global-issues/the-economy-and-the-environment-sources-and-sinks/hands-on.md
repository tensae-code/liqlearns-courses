# Hands-on — The economy and the environment — sources and sinks

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Circle diagram
Draw the circular diagram: forest and mine feeding a factory town, smoke and waste flowing out to land and water. Label sources on the way in and sinks on the way out.

## Press both ends
Add one arrow of population growth pressing the source end and one of industrial growth pressing the sink end. Write what each pressure does to scarcity in one line.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
