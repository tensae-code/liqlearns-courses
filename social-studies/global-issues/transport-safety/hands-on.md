# Hands-on — Transport Safety

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Lesson 4: Safety (ایمنی) a chief matter in transport management — the safety of passengers and goods in movement; the reflection box: about 16,000 people killed on Iran's roads in 1396 (2017) alone, and the forensic medicine organisation confirming 315,000 road deaths in 14 years; activity: list the material and spiritual costs road accidents impose on society, and the most important work to prevent them. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
