# Video script — Transport Safety

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Iran's book, the *Iran Grade 12 Geography (3)* (printed p. 64), teaches: Lesson 4: Safety (ایمنی) a chief matter in transport management — the safety of passengers and goods in movement; the reflection box: about 16,000 people killed on Iran's roads in 1396 (2017) alone, and the forensic medicine organisation confirming 315,000 road deaths in 14 years; activity: list the material and spiritual costs road accidents impose on society, and the most important work to prevent them.

Iran's own way (Iran Grade 12 Geography (3)): 'applied' geography organised as three problems of Iranian life — settlements, transport, and natural hazards — each taken in two lessons (what it is, then how to manage it) with a geographical-skills section (GIS, time zones, remote sensing) closing each chapter.
