# Natural climate change — Milankovitch cycles — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Milankovitch theory ties ice ages to…
a) volcanoes  b) three slow orbital cycles  c) ocean currents
**Answer: b) three slow orbital cycles**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Eccentricity is the cycle of the orbit's…
a) shape  b) speed  c) colour
**Answer: a) shape**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Precession is the axis…
a) tilting  b) wobbling  c) stopping
**Answer: b) wobbling**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Obliquity is the axis…
a) tilt leaning more or less  b) wobble  c) orbit shape
**Answer: a) tilt leaning more or less**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** A feedback is…
a) a change in one element altering many others  b) a single cause with a single effect  c) a weather forecast
**Answer: a) a change in one element altering many others**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Today's forcing, the book notes, sits…
a) inside the natural cycle's range  b) outside the natural cycle's range  c) exactly on the cycle
**Answer: b) outside the natural cycle's range**
