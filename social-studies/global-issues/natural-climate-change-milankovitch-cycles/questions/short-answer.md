# Natural climate change — Milankovitch cycles — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the three Milankovitch cycles.
**Answer:** Eccentricity, precession and obliquity.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Give one feedback example.
**Answer:** Melting ice exposes darker ground, which absorbs more heat, which melts more ice (or the book's equivalent).

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** What do the cycles vary?
**Answer:** The solar energy the Earth receives.
