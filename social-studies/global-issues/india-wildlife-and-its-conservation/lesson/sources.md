**Acceptance count: taught in 1 country (India).**

India's own way (Grade 6 The Earth Our Habitat): The first Geography year goes planet → globe → motions → maps → Earth's domains → landforms, and only then turns home: the last two chapters are India itself (location, neighbours, physical divisions, monsoon climate, vegetation and wildlife), so world basics are immediately applied to the home country.

India's own way (Grade 9 Contemporary India-I): From Grade 9 the Geography book is India alone, in depth — Contemporary India-I is the physical book: size and location, physiography built from plate tectonics, drainage, the monsoon system, vegetation and wildlife, and population, each chapter working from India's own maps and census data.
