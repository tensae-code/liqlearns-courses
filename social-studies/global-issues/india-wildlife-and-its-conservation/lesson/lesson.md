## Sub lesson 1 — India — Wildlife and Its Conservation

India's Grade 6 The Earth Our Habitat book, the *India Grade 6 The Earth Our Habitat* (printed p. 60), teaches: Ch.8: India's wildlife (Gir lions, Assam rhinos, migratory birds), declining species, sanctuaries and Wildlife Week. India's Grade 9 Contemporary India-I book, the *India Grade 9 Contemporary India-I* (printed p. 48), teaches: Ch.5 (deeper Grade 9 treatment of the Grade 6 proposal): India's wildlife, national parks, wildlife sanctuaries and biosphere reserves. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — India's way — the book's own teaching way

India's own way (Grade 6 The Earth Our Habitat): The first Geography year goes planet → globe → motions → maps → Earth's domains → landforms, and only then turns home: the last two chapters are India itself (location, neighbours, physical divisions, monsoon climate, vegetation and wildlife), so world basics are immediately applied to the home country. India's own way (Grade 9 Contemporary India-I): From Grade 9 the Geography book is India alone, in depth — Contemporary India-I is the physical book: size and location, physiography built from plate tectonics, drainage, the monsoon system, vegetation and wildlife, and population, each chapter working from India's own maps and census data. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
