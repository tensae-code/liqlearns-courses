The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

India's Grade 6 The Earth Our Habitat book, the *India Grade 6 The Earth Our Habitat* (printed p. 60), teaches: Ch.8: India's wildlife (Gir lions, Assam rhinos, migratory birds), declining species, sanctuaries and Wildlife Week. India's Grade 9 Contemporary India-I book, the *India Grade 9 Contemporary India-I* (printed p. 48), teaches: Ch.5 (deeper Grade 9 treatment of the Grade 6 proposal): India's wildlife, national parks, wildlife sanctuaries and biosphere reserves.

India's own way (Grade 6 The Earth Our Habitat): The first Geography year goes planet → globe → motions → maps → Earth's domains → landforms, and only then turns home: the last two chapters are India itself (location, neighbours, physical divisions, monsoon climate, vegetation and wildlife), so world basics are immediately applied to the home country. India's own way (Grade 9 Contemporary India-I): From Grade 9 the Geography book is India alone, in depth — Contemporary India-I is the physical book: size and location, physiography built from plate tectonics, drainage, the monsoon system, vegetation and wildlife, and population, each chapter working from India's own maps and census data.
