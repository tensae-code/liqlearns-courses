# Human causes of climate change — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Sulfate and nitrate particles can form in the air from human emissions.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Land-use change has no effect on climate.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Human aerosols can reflect or absorb sunlight.
**Answer: True**
