# Human causes of climate change — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the three human levers on climate in this lesson.
**Answer:** Greenhouse gases, aerosols, and land-use change.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why does clearing forest reduce local rain?
**Answer:** It removes the evaporation/transpiration that returns about half the rainfall to the air.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Name three aerosol sources.
**Answer:** Any three of: factories, vehicles, aircraft, power plants, households.
