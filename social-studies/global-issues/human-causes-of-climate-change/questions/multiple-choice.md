# Human causes of climate change — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Burning fossil fuels injects…
a) greenhouse gases  b) only water  c) oxygen
**Answer: a) greenhouse gases**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Greenhouse gases enhance the…
a) ozone layer  b) greenhouse effect  c) rainfall
**Answer: b) greenhouse effect**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Which is a human aerosol source?
a) A volcano  b) A power plant  c) A dust storm
**Answer: b) A power plant**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Clearing rainforest removes evaporation that returns about how much of its rainfall?
a) A tenth  b) About half  c) All of it
**Answer: b) About half**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Albedo is…
a) how much sunlight a surface reflects  b) a kind of cloud  c) a greenhouse gas
**Answer: a) how much sunlight a surface reflects**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Overgrazing and excessive cultivation change climate by…
a) changing the land surface  b) cooling the oceans  c) stopping the wind
**Answer: a) changing the land surface**
