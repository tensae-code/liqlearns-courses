# Video script — Human causes of climate change

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Humans change the climate first through the air's composition. Burning fossil fuels and other activities inject greenhouse gases that enhance the greenhouse effect, trapping more heat. Human aerosols come from factories, vehicles, aircraft, power plants and households, plus sulfate and nitrate particles formed in the air itself; they reflect or absorb sunlight. The gases warm steadily and stay; the particles shift with the week's industry and weather. Land-use change warms directly. Clearing Amazon rainforest removes the evaporation and transpiration that return about half its rainfall to the air, and changes the surface albedo — how much sunlight the ground reflects. Overgrazing and excessive cultivation do the same work elsewhere: stripped, pale, dry ground heats differently from covered ground. So humans push the climate three ways at once — gases, particles and the land's own skin.
