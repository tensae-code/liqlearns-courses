## Sub lesson 1 — Political borders

Kuwait Grade 8 Term 2 (printed pp. 40–44), teaches: How drawn political borders became a standing source of dispute and tension between Islamic-world states. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Kuwait's way — the book's own teaching way

Kuwait's own way: Grade 8 Term 2 frames the whole Islamic world as one body of challenges and answers — wars, contested borders and besieged minorities on one side; on the other, an organisation with principles (self-determination, human rights, rule of law) and Kuwait itself cast as the helper-state — its Fund, its charities, its Red Crescent — while the values unit keeps the same virtues at home scale: unity, altruism, rushing to help, the mother's home clinic. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
