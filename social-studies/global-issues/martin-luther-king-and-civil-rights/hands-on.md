# Hands-on — Martin Luther King and Civil Rights

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Martin Luther King Jr.'s non-violent movement (learned from Gandhi) — Rosa Parks and the 381-day Montgomery bus boycott, the 1963 March on Washington and "I Have a Dream", the Civil Rights Act 1964 and Voting Rights Act 1965, and the Nobel Peace Prize. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
