# Video script — Ukraine on the Geopolitical Map

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Ukraine's Grade 10 book closes by turning to Ukraine itself, placing the country on the geopolitical map of the world in §37. Learners locate Ukraine among its neighbours and blocs and see what its position means — the home-country end of the book's world tour.

Ukraine learns this inside its "Geography: Regions and Countries" course: A "Geography: Regions and Countries" course — every region is done in the same order (position, nature/resources, population, primary/secondary/tertiary sectors) then typological country studies with rubrics (country visiting-card, capital, pride of the country, unique experience), ending with Ukraine's own place in the world; research tasks (Дослідження) embedded in chapters. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
