# Lesson plan — Irans Neighbours Azerbaijan And Armenia

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Global Issues · **Lesson:** Irans Neighbours Azerbaijan And Armenia
**Place in the curriculum:** Global Issues (CURRICULUM.md) — built in the Geography expansion (Iran, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Lesson 11 The neighbouring countries (1) (کشورهای همسایه ۱): as people in a city or village live in each other's neighbourhood, countries too have neighbours — in the neighbourhood of our vast country Iran live the people of other countries; the Republic of Azerbaijan in the north-west of Iran, made of two parts with Armenia standing between them; agriculture flourishes in Azerbaijan — its vineyards are famous, and cotton and tobacco fields have spread in its inner and foothill parts; and the Republic of Armenia, also in Iran's north-west, a mountainous country some of whose peaks are over 4,000 metres, small in extent and poor in mineral resources, with electrical, chemical and tyre industries whose raw materials it imports from abroad — as Iran's book teaches it (see lesson/sources.md for the book and printed page).
