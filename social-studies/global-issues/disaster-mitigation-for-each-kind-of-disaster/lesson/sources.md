**Acceptance count: taught in 2 countries (Indonesia, Dominican Republic).**

Indonesia's own way (Grade 11): the whole book is organised around Indonesia itself — its strategic position first, then its own biodiversity (Wallace/Wallacea), its own environment-and-population problems, and its own disaster map; every concept is taught through Indonesian examples, maps and infographics, with a chapter-end knowledge assessment (Asesmen) per chapter.

Indonesia's own way (Grade 11 teacher guide — evidence): a Kurikulum Merdeka teacher guide — a general part sets out the Fase F learning outcomes (Capaian Pembelajaran), Pancasila-student-profile targets per chapter, teaching strategies and assessment design; a special part then guides each student-book chapter activity by activity, with prerequisite concepts, pre-learning assessment, competence + character scoring rubrics for every activity, answer keys and reflection. Note: its Bab IV is retitled "Perubahan Iklim dan Kebencanaan" (climate change and disasters), widening the student book's disaster chapter.

Dominican Republic's own way (Grade 10): This is the physical-geography year: the universe and Earth's layers, continental drift with volcanoes and earthquakes — including earthquake-prevention measures as a group task — water and winds across the continents, and a closing unit on political and economic geography that treats borders, geopolitical order and international climate agreements as geography, with hurricane-season citizen responsibility as the water unit's group activity.
