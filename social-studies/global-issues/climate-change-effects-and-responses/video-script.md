# Video script — Climate Change — Effects and Responses

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The harm does not stay where the smoke rose.

Farms yield less, while more mouths ask for food.

Soil washes away — into other countries' rivers.

Sickness climbs: malaria reaches the highlands now.

Wells sink. Desert spreads.

The answers are plain: burn less. Plant trees. Guard the forests.

And welcome inventions that pull the gas back out of the air.
