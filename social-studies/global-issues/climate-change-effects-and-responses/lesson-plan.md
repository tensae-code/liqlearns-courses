# Lesson plan — Climate Change — Effects and Responses

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Global Issues · **Lesson:** Climate Change — Effects and Responses
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 14 — Global Issues (CURRICULUM.md) — the thirteenth lesson of the title, after Climate Change — Causes (TITLES.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the harms of climate change cross borders and strike four ways in Ethiopia — falling farm output, soil degradation, diseases moving uphill into the highlands, and shrinking surface and groundwater — and the book states the remedies: burn less, plant many trees, care for dense forests, and encourage inventions that draw carbon dioxide out of the air.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Climate Change — Causes. Grade 8 depth throughout; no teacher evidence exists for Grade 8 Chapter 7 and none is claimed.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say why the harms do not stay where they were caused.
2. Give the four effects: farm output, soil, health, water.
3. Name the diseases moving into the highlands: malaria and trypanosomiasis.
4. State the book's four remedies.

## If the learner is stuck
- Reads 'diseases moving uphill' as a figure of speech → the book means it plainly: malaria and trypanosomiasis, once lowland-confined, are reaching the highlands as the change grows.
- Lists remedies from general knowledge → hold to the book's four: burn less, plant many trees, care for dense forests, encourage carbon-drawing inventions.
