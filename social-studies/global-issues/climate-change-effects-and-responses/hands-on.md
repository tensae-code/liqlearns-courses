# Hands-on — Climate Change — Effects and Responses

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Highland health watch
Ask a health worker, teacher or elder whether illnesses once called 'lowland sickness' are now seen in your area. Write what you are told as a report, clearly marked as testimony, and set it beside the lesson's claim — agreement or difference both recorded honestly.

## Soil-loss walk
After rain, walk a slope and read the soil's travel: rills, exposed roots, muddy water. Draw where the soil went. This is sub lesson 1's mechanism, seen with your own eyes.
