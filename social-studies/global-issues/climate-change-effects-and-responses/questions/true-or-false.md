# Climate Change — Effects and Responses — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Climate change can worsen instability, migration, hunger and war, on the book's account.
**Answer: True** — the book says so beyond the four effects.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Surface water shrinks but groundwater is untouched.
**Answer: False** — spreading desert and repeated drought draw down both.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Caring for dense forests is one of the remedies.
**Answer: True** — with burning less, planting trees and carbon-drawing inventions.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Drought worsens and desert spreads under the change, in the soil account.
**Answer: True** — the change worsens drought, spreads desert and unbalances nature.
