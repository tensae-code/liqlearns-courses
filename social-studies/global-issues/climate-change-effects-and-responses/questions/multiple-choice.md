# Climate Change — Effects and Responses — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The harms of climate change —
a) stay in the country that caused them  b) cross borders  c) fall only on cities
**Answer: b) cross borders** — they do not stay where they were caused.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Falling farm output bites hardest because —
a) most Ethiopians live by farming while population grows fast  b) farming is new in Ethiopia  c) imports are banned
**Answer: a) most Ethiopians live by farming while population grows fast** — a farming majority plus fast growth plus falling productivity.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Soil washes into neighbouring countries driven by —
a) running water and, in dry hot areas, whirlwinds  b) trucks  c) earthquakes
**Answer: a) running water and, in dry hot areas, whirlwinds** — the book names both drivers.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The diseases named as moving into the highlands are —
a) cholera and flu  b) malaria and trypanosomiasis  c) measles and mumps
**Answer: b) malaria and trypanosomiasis** — malaria and trypanosomiasis (ገንዲ).

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Which is one of the book's stated remedies?
a) Plant many trees  b) Burn more brush  c) Drain the wetlands
**Answer: a) Plant many trees** — plant many trees is stated.
