# Climate Change — Effects and Responses — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Give the four effects of climate change the book teaches, in order.
**Answer:** (a) Falling farm output; (b) soil degradation; (c) harm to human and animal health; (d) shrinking surface and groundwater.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** State the book's four remedies.
**Answer:** Cut the things burned for various services; plant many trees; care for the dense forests; encourage inventions that can draw carbon dioxide out of the air.
