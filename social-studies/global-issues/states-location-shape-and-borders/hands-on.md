# Hands-on — States Location Shape And Borders

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Lesson 10 The country, a political region (کشور، یک ناحیه سیاسی): the country's three foundations, land first (سرزمین) — no country is thinkable without its land; the kinds of border: natural borders (drawn on natural features) and artificial borders (drawn by geographical coordinates, parallels and meridians, where nature gives no line), adapted borders fitted to cultural conditions parting groups different in ethnicity, language or religion (the India example), and maritime borders, fixed from the low-water line and hard to settle; the importance of borders, and Iran's more than 21 land border terminals trading with neighbours. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
