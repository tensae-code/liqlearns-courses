Iran's book, the *Iran Grade 11 Geography (2)* (printed p. 127), teaches: Lesson 10 The country, a political region (کشور، یک ناحیه سیاسی): the country's three foundations, land first (سرزمین) — no country is thinkable without its land; the kinds of border: natural borders (drawn on natural features) and artificial borders (drawn by geographical coordinates, parallels and meridians, where nature gives no line), adapted borders fitted to cultural conditions parting groups different in ethnicity, language or religion (the India example), and maritime borders, fixed from the low-water line and hard to settle; the importance of borders, and Iran's more than 21 land border terminals trading with neighbours.

Iran's own way (Iran Grade 11 Geography (2)): the whole book is built on one organising idea — the region (ناحیه). Four chapters divide the world into regions four ways: what a region is, natural regions (climate, landforms, living, coasts, mountains — with Nepal as the highland case study), human regions (cultural, economic), and political regions (the state, borders, geopolitics — with the Persian Gulf and the European Union as case studies).
