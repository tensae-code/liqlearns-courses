# Video script — Mapping corruption — and the remedies

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Mapped by Transparency International's scores, only a few countries are clean and most of the world sits at moderately high corruption — so the section closes with causes, consequences and remedies. First, the map of scores: Transparency International's scores let corruption be mapped. Then, causes, consequences, remedies: A map alone does not fix anything, so the section closes analytically: with the causes of corruption, with its consequences for the states it weakens, and with the remedial measures against it. Hold those two parts together and you hold the lesson.
