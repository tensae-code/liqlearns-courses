# Mapping corruption — and the remedies — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The scores used are…
a) Transparency International's  b) the World Bank's only  c) FIFA's
**Answer: a) Transparency International's**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Countries named as scoring clean include…
a) Canada, Singapore, Australia, New Zealand  b) Uzbekistan and Turkmenistan  c) none
**Answer: a) Canada, Singapore, Australia, New Zealand**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Most of the world sits at…
a) zero corruption  b) moderately high corruption  c) perfect scores
**Answer: b) moderately high corruption**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Worst scores concentrate in…
a) Scandinavia  b) parts of Africa and the Middle East, parts of Asia, totalitarian states  c) the Pacific islands
**Answer: b) parts of Africa and the Middle East, parts of Asia, totalitarian states**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Asian exceptions to low scores include…
a) Japan, South Korea, Singapore  b) none  c) Uzbekistan
**Answer: a) Japan, South Korea, Singapore**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Table 5.3 distributes world population by…
a) income  b) their state's corruption degree  c) age
**Answer: b) their state's corruption degree**
