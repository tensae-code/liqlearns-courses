# Hands-on — Air Pollution

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Smell walk
Walk a short route at a busy time and a quiet time. Note every smell and its likely source in each walk. Which hour's air is closer to the book's clean air? Write the evidence.

## Smoke census
From one spot, count the smoke sources you can see or smell in ten minutes — chimneys, vehicles, fires, waste heaps. Draw the spot at the centre and mark each source around it.
