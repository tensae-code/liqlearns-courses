# Video script — Air Pollution

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Breathe in. Clean air has no smell.

No taste. No colour. Just air.

Pollution is poison mixing into that air.

Smoke from factories. Smoke from cars. Smoke from homes.

Only one person in ten breathes clean air now.

Name the smoke near you. That is where the defence starts.
