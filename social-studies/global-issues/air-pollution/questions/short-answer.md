# Air Pollution — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Define air pollution in the book's terms.
**Answer:** Air pollution is when natural or human-made toxic substances mix into the air and change it against living things.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name two causes of air pollution near you and a defence for each.
**Answer:** Causes such as factory, vehicle or household smoke and burning waste, each with a source-level defence (controlling smoke, managing waste instead of burning it).

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Why does the book begin with the bad smell of wastes?
**Answer:** Because it is the everyday sign of air pollution that pupils can detect themselves — clean air has no smell, so a bad smell announces something toxic has mixed into the air.
