# Air Pollution — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Polluted air can come from natural substances as well as human-made ones.
**Answer: True** — the book says natural or human-made toxic substances.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Nine out of ten people breathe clean air, on the study the book cites.
**Answer: False** — one in ten breathes clean air — nine in ten do not.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Clean air has a pleasant smell.
**Answer: False** — clean air has no smell at all.
