# Air Pollution — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Clean air, by nature, is —
a) sweet-smelling  b) without smell, taste or colour  c) slightly blue
**Answer: b) without smell, taste or colour** — the book: air is without smell, taste or colour.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Air pollution happens when —
a) wind blows  b) toxic substances mix into the air and change it against living things  c) air gets warm
**Answer: b) toxic substances mix into the air and change it against living things** — that is the book's definition.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** On the WHO study the book cites, how many people breathe clean air?
a) One in two  b) Nine in ten  c) One in ten
**Answer: c) One in ten** — only one person in ten breathes clean air.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The everyday sign of air pollution the book gives is —
a) the bad smell of wastes  b) rainbow colours  c) cold wind
**Answer: a) the bad smell of wastes** — the bad smell of wastes is its everyday sign.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** 'Smog' is a blend of the words…
a) smoke and fog  b) smoke and gas  c) sun and fog
**Answer: a) smoke and fog**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** Ground-level ozone forms from…
a) NOx and VOCs  b) oxygen and water  c) CO₂ and methane
**Answer: a) NOx and VOCs**

**7. (Sub lesson 3)** VOCs both warm the climate and…
a) make rain  b) cook up ozone  c) clean the air
**Answer: b) cook up ozone**
