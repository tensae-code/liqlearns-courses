# Lesson plan — Protecting the Environment Where You Live

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Global Issues · **Lesson:** Protecting the Environment Where You Live
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 14 — Global Issues (CURRICULUM.md) — the eleventh lesson of the title, after Global Warming, Ozone, Waste and Biodiversity (TITLES.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** environmental protection is the practice of conserving the natural environment — conserving what exists, repairing damage and reversing trends — carried by individuals, organisations and governments, with the community's own forms named: afforestation, reforestation, educating people, and reducing, reusing and recycling. This lesson stands on teacher-guide evidence only.
**This lesson's own level:** this lesson stands on teacher-guide evidence only (Addis Ababa Grade 7 guide, §7.1.2) — the student book has no equivalent standalone protection lesson; its remedies sit inside its desertification lesson. ## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Define environmental protection in the guide's terms and its three values.
2. Say who carries protection: individuals, organisations, governments.
3. Name the community-participation forms.
4. Tell afforestation from reforestation precisely, as the guide keys them.

## If the learner is stuck
- Uses afforestation and reforestation as the same word → the guide's key is the test: NO previous tree cover = afforestation; destroyed or damaged forest replanted = reforestation.
- Thinks protection is the government's work alone → the guide names individuals and organisations alongside governments, and ends on the pupil's own role.
