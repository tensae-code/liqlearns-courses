# Video script — Protecting the Environment Where You Live

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Protection is a practice, not a wish.

Keep what stands. Repair what is damaged. Turn the trend around.

Governments do it. Groups do it. You do it.

Plant where no trees ever stood — that is afforestation.

Replant where forest was lost — reforestation.

Reduce. Reuse. Recycle. Teach your neighbour.

Your locality is your post. Stand at it.
