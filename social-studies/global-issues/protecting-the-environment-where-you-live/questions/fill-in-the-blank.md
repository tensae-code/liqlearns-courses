# Protecting the Environment Where You Live — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Protection is the practice of ______ the natural environment.
**Answer: conserving.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Regenerating or replanting forest areas destroyed or damaged is ______.
**Answer: reforestation.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The triad is reduce, ______ and recycle.
**Answer: reuse.**
