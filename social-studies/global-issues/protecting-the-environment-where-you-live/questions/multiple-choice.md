# Protecting the Environment Where You Live — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Environmental protection is valued for —
a) conserving what exists, repairing damage and reversing trends  b) making parks for tourists only  c) raising taxes
**Answer: a) conserving what exists, repairing damage and reversing trends** — the guide's three values.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Who is engaged in protection, on the guide's account?
a) Governments only  b) Individuals, organisations and governments  c) Foreign agencies only
**Answer: b) Individuals, organisations and governments** — all three are named.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Afforestation means —
a) replanting a damaged forest  b) establishing a forest where there was no previous tree cover  c) cutting with care
**Answer: b) establishing a forest where there was no previous tree cover** — the keyed distinction: no previous tree cover.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which is a community-participation form the guide names?
a) Educating people  b) Building fences only  c) Moving villages
**Answer: a) Educating people** — afforestation, reforestation, educating people, reduce/reuse/recycle.
