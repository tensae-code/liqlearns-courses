# Protecting the Environment Where You Live — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** State the guide's three values of environmental protection.
**Answer:** Conserving what exists, repairing damage, and reversing trends (teacher guide — evidence).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A hillside that never carried trees is planted; a felled wanza grove is replanted. Name each action and say why.
**Answer:** The never-covered hillside is afforestation — forest established where there was no previous tree cover. The replanted grove is reforestation — regenerating forest destroyed or damaged (teacher guide — evidence).

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Write the role line the guide asks of pupils, in your own case.
**Answer:** The learner's own reflection on their role and identity as an environmental actor in their locality, with a concrete action named (teacher guide — evidence).
