# Hands-on — Protecting the Environment Where You Live

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## One hillside, chosen
Choose the bare or damaged place from your Do-it-first. Decide honestly: is its planting afforestation or reforestation? Write the plan — what is planted, who waters it, who guards it — in the guide's community forms.

## Reduce, reuse, recycle at home
For three days, run the triad at home: refuse one needless thing (reduce), find a second life for one thing (reuse), sort one thing for recycling or compost. Keep the tally and bring it back.
