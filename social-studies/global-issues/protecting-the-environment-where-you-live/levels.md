# Protecting the Environment Where You Live — sub lessons and depth

Depth record for a guide-only lesson: both sub lessons are the guide's §7.1.2 depth (teacher guide — evidence, printed pp. 106–107). The student-book ground beneath them is Desertification — Causes and Defence, where remedies sit inside the problem lesson.

## Sub lesson 1 — What protection is, and who does it (teacher guide — evidence)
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: define protection, give its three values — conserving what exists, repairing damage, reversing trends — and name individuals, organisations and governments as its carriers.

## Sub lesson 2 — The community's forms, and two words kept apart (teacher guide — evidence)
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: name afforestation, reforestation, educating people and reduce/reuse/recycle, and tell afforestation (no previous cover) from reforestation (replanting damaged forest) exactly.
