# The sustainability challenge — energy, wealth and green design

## Do it first

Put two households side by side — one rich, one poor. The book's chart says the rich one burns far more energy per head. That gap is the challenge in one picture.

## Sub lesson 1 — Wealth burns energy

The sustainability challenges are structural: a population to manage, and the broad relation between wealth and energy consumption — the book's figure of global energy consumption per head (2021) shows richer societies burning far more per person. Any honest solution must therefore work on design and efficiency, not on asking the poor to stay poor: the energy problem is concentrated where the wealth is.

## Sub lesson 2 — Solutions by design

The solutions half answers with sustainable design. Its worked image is green buildings, with solar-chimney-type technologies that cut a building's energy appetite by design rather than by sacrifice — using sun and airflow, built into the walls and roof, instead of machines fighting the climate outside. Design is the cheapest energy source: the kilowatt a building never needs.
