# The sustainability challenge — energy, wealth and green design — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Energy consumption per head is far higher in…
a) poorer societies  b) richer societies  c) all societies equally
**Answer: b) richer societies**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The book's energy figure is for the year…
a) 2001  b) 2021  c) 1991
**Answer: b) 2021**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The challenge is described as…
a) structural  b) a matter of manners  c) solved
**Answer: a) structural**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Green buildings cut energy appetite by…
a) design  b) sacrifice  c) law only
**Answer: a) design**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** A solar chimney uses…
a) coal  b) sun and airflow  c) diesel
**Answer: b) sun and airflow**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** The cheapest energy is…
a) imported fuel  b) the energy a design never needs  c) nuclear only
**Answer: b) the energy a design never needs**
