# The sustainability challenge — energy, wealth and green design — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** State the wealth–energy relation.
**Answer:** Richer societies burn far more energy per person.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How does a solar-chimney building save energy?
**Answer:** By design — sun and airflow built into the structure replace machine heating/cooling.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Why must solutions not rest on the poor staying poor?
**Answer:** Because the energy problem is concentrated where the wealth is; design and efficiency are the honest levers.
