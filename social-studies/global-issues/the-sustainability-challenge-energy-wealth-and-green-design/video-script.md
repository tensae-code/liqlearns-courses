# Video script — The sustainability challenge — energy, wealth and green design

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The sustainability challenges are structural: a population to manage, and the broad relation between wealth and energy consumption — the book's figure of global energy consumption per head (2021) shows richer societies burning far more per person. Any honest solution must therefore work on design and efficiency, not on asking the poor to stay poor: the energy problem is concentrated where the wealth is. The solutions half answers with sustainable design. Its worked image is green buildings, with solar-chimney-type technologies that cut a building's energy appetite by design rather than by sacrifice — using sun and airflow, built into the walls and roof, instead of machines fighting the climate outside. Design is the cheapest energy source: the kilowatt a building never needs.
