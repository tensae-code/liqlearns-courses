# Video script — Human Impact on Climate

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Kazakhstan's book, the *Kazakhstan Grade 8 Sakhova NIS* (printed p. 58), teaches: 3.2.7 How is human activity changing the climate?.

Kazakhstan's own way (Kazakhstan Grade 8 Sakhova NIS): the NIS Grade 8 course phrases its lessons as questions and tasks — «Әр жерде неліктен уақыт әркелкі?» (Why is time different in different places?, 2.2), «Тау жыныстары қалай пайда болады?» (How do rocks form?, 3.1.5), «Өзеннің суы қалай өзгереді?» (How does a river's water change?, 3.3.4) — and adds a lesson on the culture of showing geographic names on the map (2.3).
