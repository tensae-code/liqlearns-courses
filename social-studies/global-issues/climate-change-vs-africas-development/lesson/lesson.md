# Climate change vs Africa's development

## Do it first

Africa did least to cause the warming, yet stands in its front line. The IPCC, as the book cites it, says why.

## Sub lesson 1 — The most vulnerable continent

Africa is among the most vulnerable continents to climate change. Two weaknesses meet there: a low adaptive capacity — less money, technology and institutional slack to adjust with — and a deep dependence on climate-sensitive livelihoods, above all rain-fed farming. When the rain shifts, the harvest, the income, the school fees and the clinic visit all shift with it.

## Sub lesson 2 — What is threatened

The threatened prize is Africa's development vision itself, and the MDG-era goals the section names: poverty and hunger reduction, health, education and livelihoods secured household by household. Climate change does not attack these goals one at a time; it presses on the rain and the seasons underneath all of them at once. That is why adaptation in Africa is not an environmental extra — it is development policy itself.
