# Video script — Climate change vs Africa's development

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Africa is among the most vulnerable continents to climate change. Two weaknesses meet there: a low adaptive capacity — less money, technology and institutional slack to adjust with — and a deep dependence on climate-sensitive livelihoods, above all rain-fed farming. When the rain shifts, the harvest, the income, the school fees and the clinic visit all shift with it. The threatened prize is Africa's development vision itself, and the MDG-era goals the section names: poverty and hunger reduction, health, education and livelihoods secured household by household. Climate change does not attack these goals one at a time; it presses on the rain and the seasons underneath all of them at once. That is why adaptation in Africa is not an environmental extra — it is development policy itself.
