# Climate change vs Africa's development — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name Africa's two weaknesses in the book's account.
**Answer:** Weak adaptive capacity, and dependence on climate-sensitive livelihoods.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why is adaptation development policy in Africa?
**Answer:** Because the goals themselves — food, health, schooling, income — rest on the rain and seasons that are shifting.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Whose assessment does the book cite for Africa's vulnerability?
**Answer:** The IPCC's, as cited in the book.
