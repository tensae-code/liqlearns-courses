# Climate change vs Africa's development — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Africa is among the… continents for climate vulnerability
a) least vulnerable  b) most vulnerable  c) unaffected
**Answer: b) most vulnerable**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Adaptive capacity in Africa is described as…
a) strong  b) weak  c) unlimited
**Answer: b) weak**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** African livelihoods depend heavily on…
a) climate-sensitive work such as rain-fed farming  b) indoor industry only  c) tourism only
**Answer: a) climate-sensitive work such as rain-fed farming**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** When the rain shifts, what shifts with it?
a) Only the harvest  b) Harvest, income, school fees and clinic visits  c) Nothing else
**Answer: b) Harvest, income, school fees and clinic visits**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The goals threatened are the…
a) MDG-era development goals  b) military goals  c) space goals
**Answer: a) MDG-era development goals**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** For Africa, adaptation is…
a) an environmental extra  b) development policy itself  c) unnecessary
**Answer: b) development policy itself**
