# Rural development and environmental damage — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or figure from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Rural development improves livelihoods through land-intensive agriculture and ____.
**Answer: forestry**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Farming the same land harder is ____.
**Answer: intensification**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Clearing new land to farm is ____.
**Answer: extensification**
