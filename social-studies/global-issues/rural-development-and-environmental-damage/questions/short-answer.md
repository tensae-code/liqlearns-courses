# Rural development and environmental damage — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Development's agricultural ledger" in your own words.
**Answer:** Rural development's environmental ledger: pesticides, fertility loss, erosion, desertification, water overuse.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Intensification — harder on the same land" in your own words.
**Answer:** Intensification brings erosion, nutrient imbalance, salination, waterlogging, pest resistance and biodiversity loss.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Extensification — and Ethiopia's cycle" in your own words.
**Answer:** Extensification clears new land; Ethiopia's cycle runs firewood-cutting, marginal farming, overgrazing, bare soil, erosion.
