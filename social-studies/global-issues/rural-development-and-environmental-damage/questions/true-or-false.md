# Rural development and environmental damage — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Rural development's environmental ledger: pesticides, fertility loss, erosion, desertification, water overuse.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Intensification brings erosion, nutrient imbalance, salination, waterlogging, pest resistance and biodiversity loss.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Extensification clears new land; Ethiopia's cycle runs firewood-cutting, marginal farming, overgrazing, bare soil, erosion.
**Answer: True**
