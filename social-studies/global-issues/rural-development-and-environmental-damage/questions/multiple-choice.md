# Rural development and environmental damage — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Development's agricultural ledger"?
a) Rural development's environmental ledger: pesticides, fertility loss, erosion, desertification, water overuse  b) Intensification brings erosion, nutrient imbalance, salination, waterlogging, pest resistance and biodiversity loss  c) Extensification clears new land; Ethiopia's cycle runs firewood-cutting, marginal farming, overgrazing, bare soil, erosion
**Answer: a) Rural development's environmental ledger: pesticides, fertility loss, erosion, desertification, water overuse**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Intensification — harder on the same land"?
a) Intensification brings erosion, nutrient imbalance, salination, waterlogging, pest resistance and biodiversity loss  b) Extensification clears new land; Ethiopia's cycle runs firewood-cutting, marginal farming, overgrazing, bare soil, erosion  c) Rural development's environmental ledger: pesticides, fertility loss, erosion, desertification, water overuse
**Answer: a) Intensification brings erosion, nutrient imbalance, salination, waterlogging, pest resistance and biodiversity loss**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Extensification — and Ethiopia's cycle"?
a) Extensification clears new land; Ethiopia's cycle runs firewood-cutting, marginal farming, overgrazing, bare soil, erosion  b) Rural development's environmental ledger: pesticides, fertility loss, erosion, desertification, water overuse  c) Intensification brings erosion, nutrient imbalance, salination, waterlogging, pest resistance and biodiversity loss
**Answer: a) Extensification clears new land; Ethiopia's cycle runs firewood-cutting, marginal farming, overgrazing, bare soil, erosion**
