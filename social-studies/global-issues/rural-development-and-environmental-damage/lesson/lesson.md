# Rural development and environmental damage

## Do it first

More people on the same land must eat. They can farm the same plots harder, or clear new land. Both roads damage something. Name the damage on each road before reading.

## Sub lesson 1 — Development's agricultural ledger

Rural development improves country people's livelihoods — electricity, clean water, roads, education — traditionally through land-intensive agriculture and forestry. Its environmental ledger is agricultural: pesticides, fertility loss, topsoil erosion, desertification, unsustainable water use.

## Sub lesson 2 — Intensification — harder on the same land

Population pressure works two damaging routes. Intensification — more inputs and cropping on the same land — brings erosion, nutrient imbalance, salination, waterlogging, pest resistance and biodiversity loss.

## Sub lesson 3 — Extensification — and Ethiopia's cycle

Extensification — clearing new land — brings deforestation and desertification. Ethiopia's cycle is spelled out: poor households cut trees for firewood and farm marginal land, overgrazing joins in, soil lies bare to flood and erosion, and rural poverty deepens.
