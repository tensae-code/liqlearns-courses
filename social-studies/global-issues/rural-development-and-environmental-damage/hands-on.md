# Hands-on — Rural development and environmental damage

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Two arrows
Draw two arrows from one crowded village — one to an over-cropped plot, one to a cleared hillside — and write the damages along each arrow from the lesson's lists.

## Break the cycle
On Ethiopia's cycle diagram, mark the link you judge cheapest to break (firewood, marginal farming, overgrazing, bare soil) and name the intervention that breaks it.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
