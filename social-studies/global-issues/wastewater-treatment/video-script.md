Jordan's book, the *Jordan G10 ES T2* (printed p. 75), teaches: Unit 5 Lesson 3 + Enrichment: Treating Wastewater; the Benefits of Sludge (معالجة المياه العادمة؛ فوائد الحمأة). Jordan's book, the *Jordan G10 ES T2 Workbook* (printed p. 29), teaches: Unit 5 Wastewater practicals: purifying water from pollutants, wastewater properties, measuring pollutants at a treatment plant, treatment types, Jordan's wastewater treatment plants, modelling a treatment plant. Jordan's book, the *Jordan G10 ES T2 Teacher Guide* (printed p. 60), teaches: Unit 5 Wastewater: teacher guidance for the wastewater concept, effects and treatment lessons.

Jordan's own way (Jordan G10 ES T2): Term 2 is weather watching (air masses, fronts, pressure systems, weather balloons), the oceans (water, waves, currents, satellite study), and wastewater — what it is, its harms, and how Jordan treats it.

Jordan's own way (Jordan G10 ES T2 Workbook): practicals for weather (air masses, pressure systems, cloud formation), oceans (temperature with depth, wave motion, density currents) and wastewater — including measuring pollutants at one of Jordan's treatment plants.

Jordan's own way (Jordan G10 ES T2 Teacher Guide): the Term 2 guide mirrors the student book — meteorology, oceans and wastewater — with teaching sequences, answers and the enrichment routes (weather balloons, satellite ocean study, sludge benefits).
