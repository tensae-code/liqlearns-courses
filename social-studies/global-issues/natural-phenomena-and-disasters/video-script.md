# Video script — Natural Phenomena and Disasters

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Grade 3 separates two ideas children often mix: a fenómeno natural is something nature does — rain, wind, a shaking earth, an erupting volcano; a desastre natural is what happens when that phenomenon strikes people's lives, homes and crops. The same phenomenon can pass harmlessly over empty land or become a disaster in a crowded valley, so the lesson teaches both words and the difference between them.

Ecuador learns this inside its own books' way: the older SM edition is a classic skills-first book — a whole opening unit on orientation, cardinal points, the compass rose and plans before any content; every unit runs the same skeleton (content lessons, “Ser Estudiante” test, Cultura del Buen Vivir, scientific work, digital skills, unit evaluation), and the country is learned parish → canton → province → nation. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
