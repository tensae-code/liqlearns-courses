The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Uganda Excel P6 SST (printed p. 16), teaches: The co-ordinating committee of permanent secretaries, the council of ministers, and the east african legislative assembly (eala) as the eac's parliament that approves its budget.

Uganda Shine P6 SST (printed p. 17), teaches: The secretariat at arusha, headed by the secretary general who runs the community's daily affairs and finances, and the east african court of justice, which promotes human rights and settles disputes among member states.

Uganda's own way (Uganda Excel P6 SST): P6 looks outward and inward at once — the East African Community's own organs and symbols beside the member states' flags, then the citizen: what citizenship is, the child's rights, what violating rights looks like (mob justice named plainly), and how elections actually run.
Uganda's own way (Uganda Shine P6 SST): a second publisher teaches the same P6 civics — the EAC's organs (Secretariat at Arusha, Court of Justice) and symbols, then each member state's flag and arms and why symbols matter. PARTIAL copy, as filed: only the held span (printed pp. 1–84) is read.
