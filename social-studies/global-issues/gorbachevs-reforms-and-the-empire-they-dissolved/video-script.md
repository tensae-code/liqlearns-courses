# Video script — Gorbachev's reforms — and the empire they dissolved

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style, in the animated-map spirit: the map opens, peoples and armies move, capitals rise, routes light up. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The map opens on the red expanse of the USSR, 1985. A new leader's portrait; two words appear over the map — glasnost, perestroika.
Windows open. Voices rise — first in the capital, then in the republics, then across the whole Eastern Bloc.
1989: the bloc's colours fall like dominoes, capital by capital, west to east in reverse. No tanks come.
The great red shape quietly separates into many flags. The Cold War's map dissolves — not in fire, but in ink.
