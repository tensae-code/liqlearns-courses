# Hands-on — Gorbachev's reforms — and the empire they dissolved

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Two windows
Draw a house with two windows labelled glasnost and perestroika. Through each window, write what rushed in once it opened. Say why the reformer did not expect the result.

## Count the successors
On a blank map of the former USSR, shade the union's outline, then draw internal lines dividing it into 'over a dozen' successor states. The exact borders are not the exercise — the fact of division is.
