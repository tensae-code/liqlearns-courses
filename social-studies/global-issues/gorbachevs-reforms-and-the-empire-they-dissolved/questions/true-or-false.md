# Gorbachev's reforms — and the empire they dissolved — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** Glasnost and perestroika were meant to strengthen the Soviet Union.
**Answer: True** — they licensed the demands that dissolved it instead.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** Moscow sent tanks to save the Eastern Bloc governments in 1989.
**Answer: False** — reformed and overstretched, it did not.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) ** The Cold War ended in a great war between the superpowers.
**Answer: False** — it ended by reform, protest and exhaustion.

**4. (Sub lesson 1) ** The reforms were meant to cure and strengthen the USSR, not dissolve it.
**Answer: True** — they licensed the demands that dissolved it instead.
