# Lesson plan — Gorbachev's reforms — and the empire they dissolved

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Global Issues · **Lesson:** Gorbachev's reforms — and the empire they dissolved
**Place in the curriculum:** Global Issues — the post-1945 thread's close; the Grade 10 book's Soviet collapse lesson (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Mikhail Gorbachev's glasnost and perestroika, meant to save the Soviet Union, released the demands that dissolved it: in 1989 the Eastern Bloc swept communism away, and the USSR itself broke into over a dozen independent nations, ending the Cold War.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the whole post-1945 thread — this lesson is where that thread ends. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Glasnost and perestroika** — Gorbachev from 1985: openness — transparency, freer speech and press; restructuring — some private ownership in a hybrid economy.
2. **1989 — and the union dissolves** — protests spread, anti-communist movements sweep the Eastern Bloc in 1989, the USSR cannot match US military spending, and the union breaks into over a dozen nations.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Define glasnost and perestroika, each in a phrase.
2. Tell the chain: reforms, demands, 1989, dissolution.
3. Give the two pressures the book names: military spending and bloc economics.
