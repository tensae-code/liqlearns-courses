## Sub lesson 1 — Al Ain's cultural sites on the UNESCO World Heritage List

United Arab Emirates Al Ain's Cultural Sites on the UNESCO World Heritage List, G11 teacher guide, volume 2, printed p. 8, teaches: what UNESCO is and its role in preserving the world's heritage; the archaeological discoveries in the Emirate of Abu Dhabi (from 4,000 years ago) as the story the sites tell; keeping the Emirati cultural heritage as part of the national identity; and, in the guide's own cross-links to Moral Education, the cultural and civilisational heritage of the UAE as a store of shared human values (القيم الحضارية والأخلاق الإنسانية المشتركة) This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — United Arab Emirates' way — the book's own teaching way

United Arab Emirates' own way: an eleventh-grade term is spent inside one city's antiquities — the state teaches its deepest past through the world list, tying the protection of Al Ain's tombs and oases to the pupils' own cultural identity and to the shared civilisational values of mankind. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
