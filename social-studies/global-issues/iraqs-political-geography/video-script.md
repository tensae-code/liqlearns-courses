Iraq's book, the *Iraq Grade 12 Human Geography* (printed p. 153), teaches: Ch. 6 applied study: The political geography of Iraq — the rise of the state in Iraq, its maritime outlook on the Arabian Gulf exporting oil to the world, the table of Iraq's border lengths with its neighbours, and the activity of measuring how near Iraq's shape is to the compact circle.

Iraq's own way (Iraq Grade 12 Human Geography): a human-geography course in six chapters — population, cities and countryside, agriculture, industry and mining, transport and trade, political geography and tourism — and every chapter closes with an applied study of Iraq itself, so the world pattern is always brought home.
