# Video script — Waste Disposal And Pollution At Home

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Sri Lanka's book, the *Sri Lanka Grade 6 G6 English* (printed p. 53), teaches: Ch. 3 Disposal of waste materials: improper disposal polluting air, water and land; sorting and composting. Sri Lanka's book, the *Sri Lanka Grade 6 G6 Tamil 2007* (printed p. 42), teaches: Ch. 4.2 கழிவுகள் (Wastes): agricultural waste, industrial waste and household waste and what to do with each.

Sri Lanka's own way (Sri Lanka Grade 6 G6 English): geography starts from the child's own school and home — location in the Grama Niladhari → Divisional Secretariat → District → Province hierarchy, a plan of the school garden, home-made weather instruments, and care of the home surroundings — before widening to Sri Lanka's location in the world in the final chapter.

Sri Lanka's own way (Sri Lanka Grade 6 G6 Tamil 2007): dialogue-driven lessons (teacher–pupil conversations) that open with Sri Lanka's location and a comparison with the world's other islands, then work through the home and its surroundings, caring for that environment, and finish with map-making and scale.
