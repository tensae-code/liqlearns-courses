# Regional Cooperation and Its Benefits — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This part teaches: regional cooperation is neighbouring states agreeing to upgrade relationships through common institutions and rules; it lowers trade barriers, builds trust, enhances cross-border cooperation and turns cultural differences into sources of diversity rather than conflict — the worked case is the Ethiopia–South Sudan agreements and MoU signed in Addis Ababa on 24 February 2017.
