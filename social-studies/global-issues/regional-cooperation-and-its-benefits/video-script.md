# Video script — Regional Cooperation and Its Benefits

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

South Sudan Primary 8 P8 Social Studies SB, printed p. 74, teaches: regional cooperation is neighbouring states agreeing to upgrade relationships through common institutions and rules; it lowers trade barriers, builds trust, enhances cross-border cooperation and turns cultural differences into sources of diversity rather than conflict — the worked case is the Ethiopia–South Sudan agreements and MoU signed in Addis Ababa on 24 February 2017.

South Sudan's own way: The final primary book is a civics of unity for a country at war with itself — power sharing is taught from the 2016 Transitional Government of National Unity cabinet photograph, the constitution's Section 33 equality clause is quoted in full, and Kenya's 2007–08 crisis and Kofi Annan's mediation are the worked example of how a neighbour's unity process shapes South Sudan's own.
