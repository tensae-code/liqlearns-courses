# Hands-on — Regional Cooperation and Its Benefits

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Activity 11 Laws and justice in relation to regional cooperation and national unity
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: regional cooperation is neighbouring states agreeing to upgrade relationships through common institutions and rules; it lowers trade barriers, builds trust, enhances cross-border cooperation and turns cultural differences into sources of diversity rather than conflict — the worked case is the Ethiopia–South Sudan agreements and MoU signed in Addis Ababa on 24 February 2017. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
