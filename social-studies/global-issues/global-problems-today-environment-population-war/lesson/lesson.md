## Sub lesson 1 — Global problems of our time (Глобальные проблемы современности) — Grade 11

Belarus Grade 11 Obshchestvovedenie (printed p. 207), teaches: Global problems as those touching all humanity — after the Cold War the arms-race problem receded and uneven development moved forward, with raw-material and energy strain, unemployment, food shortage, international terrorism and drug trafficking intertwined; the types of global problems, beginning with those between states and social communities (disarmament and war prevention). This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Belarus's way — the book's own teaching way

Belarus' own way: the whole Grade 11 course is the state describing itself — the 1994 Constitution and its 1996/2004 referendums, the President as guarantor of the Constitution, a two-chamber National Assembly and elected local Soviets of deputies under appointed executive committees, and two state languages — so its civics is placed as Belarus's own constitutional order, not as generic civics.. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
