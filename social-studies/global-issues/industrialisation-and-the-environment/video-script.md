# Video script — Industrialisation and the Environment

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

South Sudan's book, the *South Sudan Grade 7 P7 Social Studies SB* (printed p. 35), teaches: Effects of industrialisation on the environment: water pollution, spoiled scenic beauty, waste reducing soil productivity. South Sudan's book, the *South Sudan Grade 7 P7 Social Studies TG* (printed p. 45), teaches: Guide: effects of the development of industries on the environment; what can be done to prevent them. (teacher guide — evidence) South Sudan's book, the *South Sudan Grade 8 P8 Social Studies SB* (printed p. 36), teaches: Fieldwork: questionnaire on how a local industry controls emission of gases to the atmosphere.

South Sudan's own way (South Sudan Grade 7 P7 Social Studies SB): Everything is costed against home ground — Mount Imatong both blocks movement and feeds rivers and the Imatong forest industry; mining is debated head-on ("Mining is the backbone to the economy of South Sudan") with a field trip to a real mining sector.

South Sudan's own way (South Sudan Grade 7 P7 Social Studies TG) (teacher guide — evidence): The guide starts the land unit outdoors — learners go and look for physical features neighbouring their school, beyond the ones in their books — and ends it with the balancing question: principles and strategies to protect the environment while industry and agriculture develop. That book is a teacher guide, cited as evidence only; South Sudan is counted in this lesson for its student book(s) named here, not for this guide.

South Sudan's own way (South Sudan Grade 8 P8 Social Studies SB): The climate unit is two-way and practical — how climate shapes people's activities (poor climate ruins roads) and how people adapt: use scarce water efficiently, build flood defences and raise dykes, grow drought-tolerant crops, and make biogas.
