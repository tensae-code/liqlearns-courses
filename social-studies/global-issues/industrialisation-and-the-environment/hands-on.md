# Hands-on — Industrialisation and the Environment

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Effects of industrialisation on the environment: water pollution, spoiled scenic beauty, waste reducing soil productivity; Guide: effects of the development of industries on the environment; what can be done to prevent them; Fieldwork: questionnaire on how a local industry controls emission of gases to the atmosphere. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
