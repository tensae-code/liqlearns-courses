## Sub lesson 2 — Tonga's way — the book's own teaching way

Tonga's own way: land is ancestral and sacred — knowledge of it is an inheritance elders hand down, and using it well is a duty owed to grandchildren; and the guide's "Notes for teachers in Tonga" tie the regional readers to Tonga's own classrooms; its method is head, heart and hands — knowledge, values and action together.
