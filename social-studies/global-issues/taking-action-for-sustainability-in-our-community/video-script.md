# Video script — Taking Action for Sustainability in Our Community

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Tonga's Years 7–8 People and the Natural Environment (printed p. 19) and Tonga's ESD Teacher Guide (printed pp. 3–4), teaches: What can we do? and the action learning cycle — teaches: work as a team with elders, teachers, religious leaders, community members and family to name a local issue and act — fortnightly clean-ups, or a class letter to the government asking for recycling bins; and pupils identify a sustainability issue in their own community, explore the values in it, build a vision, choose an action with a decision-making grid (poster, beach clean-up, or lobbying the government to ban single-use plastics), act and reflect.

Tonga's own way: land is ancestral and sacred — knowledge of it is an inheritance elders hand down, and using it well is a duty owed to grandchildren; and the guide's "Notes for teachers in Tonga" tie the regional readers to Tonga's own classrooms; its method is head, heart and hands — knowledge, values and action together.
