# Lesson plan — Taking Action for Sustainability in Our Community

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Global Issues · **Lesson:** Taking Action for Sustainability in Our Community
**Place in the curriculum:** Global Issues (CURRICULUM.md) — built in the Social Studies remaining-strands expansion (Tonga, 2026-11-17).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** What can we do? and the action learning cycle — teaches: work as a team with elders, teachers, religious leaders, community members and family to name a local issue and act — fortnightly clean-ups, or a class letter to the government asking for recycling bins; and pupils identify a sustainability issue in their own community, explore the values in it, build a vision, choose an action with a decision-making grid (poster, beach clean-up, or lobbying the government to ban single-use plastics), act and reflect — as Tonga's books teach it (see lesson/sources.md for the book and printed page).
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on nothing but the lesson itself — working as a team to name a local sustainability issue and act on it, through the action learning cycle — identify, explore the values, build a vision, choose an action, act and reflect.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of ..." in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.
