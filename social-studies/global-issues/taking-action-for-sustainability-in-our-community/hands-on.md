# Hands-on — Taking Action for Sustainability in Our Community

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: What can we do? and the action learning cycle — teaches: work as a team with elders, teachers, religious leaders, community members and family to name a local issue and act — fortnightly clean-ups, or a class letter to the government asking for recycling bins; and pupils identify a sustainability issue in their own community, explore the values in it, build a vision, choose an action with a decision-making grid (poster, beach clean-up, or lobbying the government to ban single-use plastics), act and reflect. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
