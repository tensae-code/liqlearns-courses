The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Iran's book, the *Iran Grade 12 Geography (3)* (printed p. 81), teaches: Lesson 5 The features and kinds of natural hazards (ویژگی‌ها و انواع مخاطرات طبیعی): the chapter opens on the earthquake of Sunday evening 21 Aban 1396 (12 Nov 2017), magnitude 7.3, near Ezgeleh in Kermanshah province — hundreds killed, over ten thousand injured, thousands of homes destroyed — asking which of these hazards has struck where the pupil lives.

Iran's own way (Iran Grade 12 Geography (3)): 'applied' geography organised as three problems of Iranian life — settlements, transport, and natural hazards — each taken in two lessons (what it is, then how to manage it) with a geographical-skills section (GIS, time zones, remote sensing) closing each chapter.
