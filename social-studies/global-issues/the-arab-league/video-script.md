Saudi Arabia Grade 10 Social Studies (Secondary Tracks, First Common Year) (printed pp. 105–107), teaches: The idea of one Arab organisation ripened during the Second World War; Egypt took the initiative and a preparatory committee of Saudi Arabia, Syria, Lebanon, Jordan, Iraq, Egypt and Yemen settled on the name League of Arab States; its Charter's three main organs — the League Council, the highest authority, of all member states' representatives with one vote each whatever their number, the permanent committees for the fields of cooperation, and the General Secretariat; and the twenty-ninth Arab summit at Dhahran in 1439H/2018, which King Salman named the Jerusalem Summit in solidarity with the Palestinian cause, proposing the initiative of strengthening Arab national security against the common challenges and the development of the League and its system.

Saudi Arabia's own way: the Grade 10 Tracks book starts from the components that make a state strong and influential and Saudi Arabia's own foundations and components, then follows the Kingdom's work for Arab, Islamic and international causes, and surveys the organisations it belongs to — the European Union as a model of integration, the League of Arab States, the Muslim World League, the Organisation of Islamic Cooperation, OPEC, the United Nations and the G20.
