# Lesson plan — HIV and AIDS

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Global Issues · **Lesson:** HIV and AIDS
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 14 — Global Issues (CURRICULUM.md) — the ninth lesson of the title, after Human Trafficking (TITLES.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** HIV is the virus that weakens the body's defence, AIDS the condition that follows, stated in the book to have no cure found — so the chapter's weight falls on knowing the signs, the transmission routes and non-routes, and prevention.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on nothing earlier in the title. Grade 7 depth throughout; the placements record that the Addis Ababa guide has NO counterpart for HIV/AIDS, and none is claimed. This lesson carries a full video script — the prevention script the placement names.

## If the learner is stuck
- Believes HIV passes through shared eating → the chapter's review stock keys that false: shared eating is a non-route, and the lesson holds it firm.
- Hears 'no cure found' as 'nothing can be done' → the book's own answer is prevention: the routes are known, and guarding against them is the defence the chapter teaches.
