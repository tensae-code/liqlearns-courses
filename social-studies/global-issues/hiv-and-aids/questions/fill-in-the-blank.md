# HIV and AIDS — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** HIV weakens the body's ______.
**Answer: defence.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Fever and swollen ______ are among the six signs.
**Answer: glands.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Because no cure has been found, ______ is the chapter's defence.
**Answer: prevention.**
