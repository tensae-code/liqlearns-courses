# HIV and AIDS — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** HIV is —
a) a bacterium in water  b) the virus that weakens the body's defence  c) a kind of fever
**Answer: b) the virus that weakens the body's defence** — that is the book's naming of it.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which set is all signs from the book's list?
a) Night sweats, fever, swollen glands  b) Broken bones, toothache, hunger  c) Coughing only
**Answer: a) Night sweats, fever, swollen glands** — the book prints six signs; these three are among them.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** HIV passes to another person through —
a) shared eating  b) sharing a plate at a meal  c) neither of these — shared eating is a non-route
**Answer: c) neither of these — shared eating is a non-route** — the chapter's stock fixes shared eating as a non-route.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Why does prevention carry this chapter?
a) Because a cure is sold in every pharmacy  b) Because the book states no cure for AIDS has been found, so guarding the routes is the defence  c) Because the signs are invisible
**Answer: b) Because the book states no cure for AIDS has been found, so guarding the routes is the defence** — the book's framing puts the weight on prevention.
