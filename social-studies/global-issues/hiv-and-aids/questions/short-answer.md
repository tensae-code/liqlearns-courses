# HIV and AIDS — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the six signs the book prints.
**Answer:** Night sweats; mouth and throat sores; fatigue and anxiety; muscle pain and skin rashes; fever; swollen glands.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A friend refuses to eat with a neighbour who has HIV. Correct them from the lesson.
**Answer:** HIV does not pass through shared eating — the chapter's stock fixes shared eating as a non-route; the shared plate is safe, and the refusal rests on a myth.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** State the book's position on a cure, exactly as the lesson carries it.
**Answer:** The book states that no cure for AIDS has been found — the book's own dated framing — and therefore teaches prevention as the defence.
