# Hands-on — HIV and AIDS

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Signs card
Make a pocket card of the six signs, drawn as a body outline with markers. The card is for recognising, not diagnosing — write at its foot: 'Signs are a reason to test and to seek care, not a verdict.'

## Myth sorting
Collect sayings about HIV from daily talk — write each on a slip. Sort the slips into routes, non-routes and unsure, using the lesson. Bring the unsure slips back as questions; never leave a myth standing unmarked.
