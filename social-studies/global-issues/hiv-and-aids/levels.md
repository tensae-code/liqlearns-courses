# HIV and AIDS — sub lessons and depth

Two sub lessons at Grade 7 pupil depth: the virus and its signs, then routes, non-routes and prevention as the chapter frames them. There is no teacher-guide climb for this lesson — the guide has no HIV/AIDS counterpart.

## Sub lesson 1 — The virus and the signs
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: say HIV weakens the body's defence and name the six printed signs.

## Sub lesson 2 — Routes, non-routes and prevention
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: separate routes from non-routes — holding shared eating as a non-route — and state why prevention carries the chapter.
