# Video script — HIV and AIDS

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then. This script is written in full — the placements name a full script need for this lesson.

---

There is a virus that weakens the body's shield. Its name is HIV.

The sickness that follows is AIDS.

Know its signs: sweats at night, sores in the mouth, tiredness, pain, rashes, fever, swollen glands.

Know how it travels — and how it does not.

It does not travel through shared eating. Do not fear the shared plate.

No cure has been found. So prevention is the shield we have.

Know the routes. Guard against them. That is how we protect each other.
