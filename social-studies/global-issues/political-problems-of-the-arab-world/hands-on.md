# Hands-on — Political Problems of the Arab World

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Political stability as a pillar of any society's renaissance, and the three political problems worked through — foreign occupation (the Palestine question from 1948; Ceuta and Melilla), political separatist movements (the Sudan split), and nuclear risks (weapons, radiation leaks, nuclear waste). Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
