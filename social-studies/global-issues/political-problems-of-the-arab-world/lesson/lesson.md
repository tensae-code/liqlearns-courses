## Sub lesson 1 — Political problems facing the Arab world's states

Kuwait Grade 7 Term 2 (printed pp. 54–56), teaches: Political stability as a pillar of any society's renaissance, and the three political problems worked through — foreign occupation (the Palestine question from 1948; Ceuta and Melilla), political separatist movements (the Sudan split), and nuclear risks (weapons, radiation leaks, nuclear waste). This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Kuwait's way — the book's own teaching way

Kuwait's own way: Grade 7 Term 2 teaches the pupil to read their region's troubles whole — political (occupation, separatism, nuclear risk), social (addiction, rumours, bullying) — and then to answer them with plans: a national vision with traits and shared Arab goals, Kuwait's own 2035 vision, and Arab integration through named organisations; the values unit grounds it at home — the grandmother who runs the family's learning, and the neighbour who feeds the absent diver's family. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
