# Video script — Adaptation — living with climate change

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Adaptation is adjustment in ecological, social or economic systems to actual or expected climate stimuli — moderating damage or exploiting opportunities. It is the UNFCCC's second response strategy, long overshadowed by mitigation, and since the IPCC Third Assessment it has become an urgent priority: even perfect emission cuts tomorrow cannot call back the warming already in motion. Adaptation sorts by timing and motive: reactive (after impacts) against anticipatory (before them); private (households and companies) against public (government); planned (deliberate policy) against autonomous (systems adjusting on their own — the only kind unmanaged nature has). Ethiopia's common strategies are named: soil conservation, terracing, water harvesting, crop diversification, changed planting dates, tree planting and irrigation — a working set any learner can see in the countryside.
