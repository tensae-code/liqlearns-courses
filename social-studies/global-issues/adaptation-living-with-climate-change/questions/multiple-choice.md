# Adaptation — living with climate change — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Adaptation means…
a) stopping all emissions  b) adjusting systems to actual or expected climate stimuli  c) ignoring climate change
**Answer: b) adjusting systems to actual or expected climate stimuli**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Adaptation is the UNFCCC's…
a) only strategy  b) second response strategy  c) newest strategy
**Answer: b) second response strategy**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Planting after seeing the impact is…
a) anticipatory  b) reactive  c) autonomous
**Answer: b) reactive**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A household's own adjustment is…
a) public  b) planned  c) private
**Answer: c) private**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Unmanaged nature can only adapt…
a) by policy  b) autonomously  c) by planning
**Answer: b) autonomously**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Which is an Ethiopian adaptation strategy?
a) Terracing and water harvesting  b) Building sea walls only  c) Importing snow
**Answer: a) Terracing and water harvesting**
