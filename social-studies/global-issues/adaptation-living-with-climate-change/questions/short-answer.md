# Adaptation — living with climate change — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the three sorting pairs for adaptation.
**Answer:** Reactive/anticipatory; private/public; planned/autonomous.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Why did adaptation become urgent after the IPCC Third Assessment?
**Answer:** Because warming already in motion cannot be called back, so systems must adjust to it.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Name three Ethiopian adaptation strategies.
**Answer:** Any three of: soil conservation, terracing, water harvesting, crop diversification, changed planting dates, tree planting, irrigation.
