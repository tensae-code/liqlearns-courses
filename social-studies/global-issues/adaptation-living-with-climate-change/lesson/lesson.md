# Adaptation — living with climate change

## Do it first

Some change is already on its way whatever we do. Adaptation is how systems adjust to live with it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Adjusting to the change that comes

Adaptation is adjustment in ecological, social or economic systems to actual or expected climate stimuli — moderating damage or exploiting opportunities. It is the UNFCCC's second response strategy, long overshadowed by mitigation, and since the IPCC Third Assessment it has become an urgent priority: even perfect emission cuts tomorrow cannot call back the warming already in motion.

## Sub lesson 2 — Kinds of adaptation — and Ethiopia's set

Adaptation sorts by timing and motive: reactive (after impacts) against anticipatory (before them); private (households and companies) against public (government); planned (deliberate policy) against autonomous (systems adjusting on their own — the only kind unmanaged nature has). Ethiopia's common strategies are named: soil conservation, terracing, water harvesting, crop diversification, changed planting dates, tree planting and irrigation — a working set any learner can see in the countryside.
