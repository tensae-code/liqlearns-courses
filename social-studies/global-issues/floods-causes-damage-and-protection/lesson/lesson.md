# Floods — Causes, Damage and Protection

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: hazards associated with rainfall, as Jamaica's curriculum guide teaches it. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Hazards associated with rainfall

Jamaica's Grade 5 Exploratory Core guide (Social Studies Term 3 Unit 1, printed p. 345) teaches the hazards associated with rainfall, in the same unit as its types-of-rainfall topic. This lesson stands on that placement, under the title the placement gives it — floods, their causes, damage and protection: when rainfall becomes a hazard, water does the damage, and protection is part of the topic. This is a curriculum guide, written for the teacher, not handed to the pupil: it is cited here as curriculum-guide evidence.

## Sub lesson 2 — Jamaica's way — the book's own teaching way

Jamaica's own way (Grade 5 Exploratory Core, curriculum guide — evidence): Grade 5 Social Studies pairs a heritage year (Europeans and Africans, national heroes, Caribbean culture) with a physical-environment year — erosion, locating Jamaica and the Caribbean by compass and latitude/longitude, types of rainfall, the Solar System, plants and water pollution. That evidence is stocked here as the way Jamaica teaches this thing; it does not change the acceptance count above. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
