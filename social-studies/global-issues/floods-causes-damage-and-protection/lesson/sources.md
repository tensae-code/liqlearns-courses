Jamaica's own way (Grade 5 Exploratory Core, curriculum guide — evidence): Grade 5 Social Studies pairs a heritage year (Europeans and Africans, national heroes, Caribbean culture) with a physical-environment year — erosion, locating Jamaica and the Caribbean by compass and latitude/longitude, types of rainfall, the Solar System, plants and water pollution. This lesson stands on that curriculum guide as evidence: it teaches the guide's thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

Searchable tags: floods, rainfall hazards, damage, protection, Jamaica, curriculum guide evidence, social studies, global issues

Eritrea's own way (ER G6 Social Studies): one integrated Social Studies book per grade weaving History, Geography, Civics and Culture strands; Geography is taught through Eritrea itself first (Eritrea's location and size, Eritrean settlement, migration and disasters) with very practical, activity-led lessons.

Slovenia's own way (Slovenia Grade 10 Geografija 1 (gimnazija)): the gimnazija first year is systematic physical geography first (Earth's interior, rocks, landforms, weather and climate, soils, vegetation, waters), then human geography (population, settlements, farming, industry and energy, transport, tourism), closing on sustainable development and spatial planning — with Slovenian cases (earthquake risk in Slovenia, the Port of Koper) set inside the world topics.
