# Video script — Floods — Causes, Damage and Protection

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Jamaica's Grade 5 Exploratory Core guide (Social Studies Term 3 Unit 1, printed p. 345) teaches the hazards associated with rainfall, in the same unit as its types-of-rainfall topic. This lesson stands on that placement, under the title the placement gives it — floods, their causes, damage and protection: when rainfall becomes a hazard, water does the damage, and protection is part of the topic. This is a curriculum guide, written for the teacher, not handed to the pupil: it is cited here as curriculum-guide evidence.

Jamaica's own way (Grade 5 Exploratory Core, curriculum guide — evidence): Grade 5 Social Studies pairs a heritage year (Europeans and Africans, national heroes, Caribbean culture) with a physical-environment year — erosion, locating Jamaica and the Caribbean by compass and latitude/longitude, types of rainfall, the Solar System, plants and water pollution. That evidence is stocked here as the way Jamaica teaches this thing; it does not change the acceptance count above.
