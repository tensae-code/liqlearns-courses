The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Bolivia's Grade 7 student book, the *Texto de Aprendizaje — Ciencias Sociales, Secundaria 1* (printed p. 20), teaches: the importance of Mother Earth as a subject of rights; the human right to water and sanitation in Bolivia (Laws 071 and 300). Bolivia's teacher compendium, the *Compendio para Maestras y Maestros* (teacher guidance, printed p. 19), teaches: mother Earth as a subject of rights: Law 300, Framework Law of Mother Earth and Integral Development for Living Well (teacher guidance).

Bolivia's own way (Grade 7, Secundaria 1): The Bolivian Texto de Aprendizaje runs every unit through the same four moments — Práctica, Teoría, Valoración, Producción — and frames geography inside the plurinational, Madre Tierra worldview: continental drift and geological time sit beside the Laws of Mother Earth (071 and 300), the human right to water, and a closing general geography of Bolivia itself (regions, orography, political map, salt flats and lithium).
