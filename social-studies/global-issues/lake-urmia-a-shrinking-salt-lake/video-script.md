# Video script — Lake Urmia A Shrinking Salt Lake

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Iran's book, the *Iran Grade 10 Geography of Iran* (printed p. 60), teaches: Lake Urmia: the world's second salt lake, its ecology and its falling water level.

Iran's own way (Iran Grade 10 Geography of Iran): the whole of Grade 10 geography is one national book — Iran's own natural and human geography for every branch; nearly every lesson closes with a "know your own living place" activity tied to the separate provincial-studies book, so national content is always re-applied to the learner's own province.
