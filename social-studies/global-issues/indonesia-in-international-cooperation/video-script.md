# Video script — Indonesia in International Cooperation

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Indonesia's book teaches: Bab 3 C Kerja sama Indonesia dalam kancah internasional (ASEAN member map and other forums) (Geografi untuk SMA/MA Kelas XII, Grade 12, printed p. 175); Bab 4 Panduan Pembelajaran Bab 3 siswa: Dinamika Kerja Sama Antarnegara dan Ketahanan Wilayah Indonesia (teaching guide) (Buku Panduan Guru Geografi untuk SMA/MA Kelas XII, Grade 12, printed p. 137).

Indonesia's own way (Grade 12): Grade 12 is applied human geography built entirely on Indonesian cases — regional development and spatial planning (tata ruang) measured against a Happiness Index, Industry 4.0 / Society 5.0, and Indonesia's own geopolitics and ASEAN-era cooperation as pillars of national resilience. Indonesia's own way (Grade 12 teacher guide — evidence): Kurikulum Merdeka guide mirroring the Grade 12 student book: a general part (Fase F outcomes, how the student book works, general strategies) then a chapter guide for each student chapter, each with the teacher's role, alternative learning routes, named misconceptions to correct, handling of special-needs learners, parent interaction, assessment and answer keys.
