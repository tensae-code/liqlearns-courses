# Hands-on — Human Activities That Invite Disasters

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Human activities and natural disasters: deforestation (wildfires, lost biodiversity, more CO2, broken water cycle, floods, drought), urbanization and cemented surfaces, dam construction, mining, pollution and over-cultivation. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
