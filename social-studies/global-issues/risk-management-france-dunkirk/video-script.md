# Video script — Risk Management in France — Dunkirk

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Theme 1's France half studies the management of risks at Dunkerque (Dunkirk): natural risks and technological risks in one place.

The management tools are named: the DICRIM (the town's risk-information document) and the Seveso sites — industrial sites classed for the danger they hold.
