# Hands-on — Networks — We of the Network

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Draw your network
Draw or list one network you belong to — people and places joined together — as Folha 11 teaches: Nós da rede / We of the network.

## Follow one link
Pick one link in your network that reaches far away and write how it connects your place to the world, as the lesson teaches networks and globalisation.
