# Video script — Networks — We of the Network

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Folha 11 is called Nós da rede / We of the network. Its one thing is the network: people and places joined in networks, so that the learner is not outside the network looking in — we are of the network.

The Folha teaches networks and globalisation together: globalisation works through networks, and being joined in a network is how distant places affect each other and how the learner's own place is connected to the world.
