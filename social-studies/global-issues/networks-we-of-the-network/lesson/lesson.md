Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — We of the network — everyone connected

Folha 11 is called Nós da rede / We of the network. Its one thing is the network: people and places joined in networks, so that the learner is not outside the network looking in — we are of the network.

## Sub lesson 2 — Networks and globalisation

The Folha teaches networks and globalisation together: globalisation works through networks, and being joined in a network is how distant places affect each other and how the learner's own place is connected to the world.

Brazil's book has no chapters in the old style: it is 17 "Folhas" (sheets) like this one, each opening with a provocative question, grouped under four dimensions of geographical space (political, cultural-demographic, economic, socio-environmental) and taught from songs, news and the student's own place.
