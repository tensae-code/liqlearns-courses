## Sub lesson 2 — South Sudan's way — the book's own teaching way

South Sudan's own way (South Sudan Grade 9 S1 Geography SB): Geography is built around South Sudan itself — its own physical features, resources, floods/drought experience and the Nile — with paper-folding and paper-tearing practical experiments for folding, faulting and sea-floor spreading, and group discussion of local examples in nearly every topic.

South Sudan's own way (South Sudan Grade 5 P5 Social Studies TG) (teacher guide — evidence): The guide insists on both sides of every feature — positive and negative effects of physical features on human activities — and on human agency: dumping material in a valley can cause flooding, and climate dictates which farming system a region of Africa can practise. That book is a teacher guide, cited as evidence only; South Sudan is counted in this lesson for its student book(s) named here, not for this guide. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
