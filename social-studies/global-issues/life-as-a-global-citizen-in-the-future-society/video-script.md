The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

South Korea's Grade 10 Jihaksa Integrated Society 2 (2022), printed p. 146, teaches: 미래와 지속가능한 삶 3 미래 사회와 세계시민으로서의 삶 — teaches: life as a global citizen in the future society — the pupil's own role in the world community.

South Korea's own way: the 2022 curriculum splits Integrated Society into two volumes; volume 2 is the civics-heavy one — human rights and the constitution, justice and inequality, then globalisation and peace — and it closes by naming the pupil's own role outright: "life as a global citizen in the future society" (세계시민으로서의 삶). Its enrichment boxes (사회 고수 되기) add the proportionality principle in limiting basic rights, redistributive justice for the socially weak, and the death penalty debated as a human-rights question.
