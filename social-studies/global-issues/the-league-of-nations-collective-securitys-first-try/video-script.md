# Video script — The League of Nations — collective security's first try

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style, in the animated-map spirit: the map opens, peoples and armies move, capitals rise, routes light up. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The map opens on Geneva, 1919. Flags rise over a new hall — the first time the world has built a house for peace itself.
The idea draws itself: one member attacked, and every flag turns toward the attacker together. Collective security.
1935: the map shifts to the Horn. Invasion arrows cross Ethiopia's border. In Geneva the Emperor speaks; the hall applauds; the flags do not move.
The hall empties. 1939 — war again. When the lights come back on, a new building is rising on the League's foundations: the United Nations.
