# Hands-on — The League of Nations — collective security's first try

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The empty chair

## Design the stronger successor
List three weaknesses that let the League fail Ethiopia. Beside each, write the fix you would build into a successor organisation. Compare with the United Nations lesson that follows.
