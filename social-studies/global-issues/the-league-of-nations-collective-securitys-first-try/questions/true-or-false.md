# The League of Nations — collective security's first try — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** The League was founded in 1926.
**Answer: False** — it was founded in 1919 — the 1926 dating in one part of the book is an error, taught corrected.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) ** Collective security replaced the old alliance system as the League's peace idea.
**Answer: True** — all members defending any member attacked.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) ** The League stopped Fascist Italy's invasion of Ethiopia.
**Answer: False** — the plea was unanswered and the occupation proceeded.
