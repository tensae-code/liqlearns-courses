# Terrorism — sub lessons and depth

Depth structure: sub lesson 1 is the student book's pupil depth — definition, mark, and the September 11 case. Sub lesson 2 climbs with the guide's itemised bill (teacher guide — evidence, printed pp. 111–113, 117). The student book's September 11 case study has no guide counterpart, and none is claimed.

## Sub lesson 1 — The definition, its mark, and the September 11 case
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: define terrorism, state its mark against ordinary war, and give the September 11, 2001 case with about 3,000 dead.

## Sub lesson 2 — The bill, itemised (teacher guide — evidence)
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: list the guide's impacts across economic, social, cultural and political life, including fear, anxiety and depression.
