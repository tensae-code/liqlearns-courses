# Hands-on — Terrorism

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Civil places map
Draw your own neighbourhood's civil places: transport stops, schools, markets, offices. These are the places the definition names. Beside the map, write what each place means to ordinary days — that ordinariness is exactly what terrorism targets.

## Prevention discussion, written
The book sets pupils to discuss causes and prevention. Write your discussion as a short page: two causes, two preventions, and whether attacks have occurred in Ethiopia, as far as you know — marked clearly as what you know and what you were told.
