# Terrorism — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What separates terrorism from ordinary or guerrilla war?
**Answer:** Its mark: it strikes unarmed people and civil institutions — public transport, schools, markets — rather than armed forces.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name four impacts from the guide's inventory.
**Answer:** Any four of: loss of lives; social disruption; environmental loss; destruction of property; damage to infrastructure; loss of confidence; fear, anxiety and depression (teacher guide — evidence).
