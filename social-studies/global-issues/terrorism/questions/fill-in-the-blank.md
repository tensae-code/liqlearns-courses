# Terrorism — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Terrorism attacks life and property without ______ to create fear.
**Answer: distinction.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The twin towers destroyed in 2001 belonged to the ______ Trade Center.
**Answer: World.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Loss of lives, social disruption and destruction of ______ are on the guide's impact list.
**Answer: property.**
