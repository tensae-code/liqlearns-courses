# Terrorism — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Terrorism aims, above all, to —
a) win land in battle  b) create fear in society for a particular political aim  c) collect taxes
**Answer: b) create fear in society for a particular political aim** — fear for a political aim is its purpose.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Terrorism's distinguishing mark is that it strikes —
a) armies in the field  b) unarmed people and civil institutions  c) only soldiers' camps
**Answer: b) unarmed people and civil institutions** — public transport, schools and markets are its chosen targets.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The September 11 attack destroyed —
a) a military base in Europe  b) the World Trade Center's twin towers in New York  c) a parliament building
**Answer: b) the World Trade Center's twin towers in New York** — the book's central case, in 2001.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which is on the guide's impact list?
a) Loss of confidence  b) Cheaper transport  c) More tourists
**Answer: a) Loss of confidence** — loss of confidence is itemised in the guide.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The guide says terrorism touches —
a) only economic life  b) every aspect of economic, social, cultural and political life  c) only city life
**Answer: b) every aspect of economic, social, cultural and political life** — the guide's words cover all four.
