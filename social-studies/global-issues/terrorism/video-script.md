# Video script — Terrorism

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Some violence is aimed at armies. Terrorism is not.

It strikes people who carry no weapon.

A bus. A school. A market. That is its mark.

It plans fear, for a political aim.

On September 11, 2001, towers fell in New York. About three thousand died.

And the bill runs on: lives, property, trust — and fear that stays.
