This whole lesson is the Addis Ababa guide's §7.1.1 — four of its five problems (global warming, ozone layer depletion, waste disposal, loss of biodiversity) are topics the Amhara student book's Chapter 7 never names as taught problems, per the placements. The Addis Ababa Grade 7 teacher guide is NOT edition-matched to the Amhara student books (different bureau, different year, different language of instruction); guide material is used only as labelled teacher evidence, never as pupil content from the student book. The lesson therefore stands on teacher-guide evidence only, is labelled so in every row above, and counts in no country's pupil-taught acceptance. There is NO Grade 8 teacher-guide coverage for Chapter 7: the Grade 8 guide in hand holds only Units 1–3, so no teacher evidence is claimed for any Grade 8 part of this lesson. Bahamas note (merged 2026-11-14): the Bahamas Grade 6 student book now stocks this lesson as pupil content (Natural Disasters & Global Warming, PDF p. 10), so the lesson is pupil-taught in 1 country (Bahamas); the Ethiopia material above remains teacher-guide evidence only, exactly as labelled.
