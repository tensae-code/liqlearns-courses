# Lesson plan — Global Warming, Ozone, Waste and Biodiversity

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Global Issues · **Lesson:** Global Warming, Ozone, Waste and Biodiversity
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 14 — Global Issues (CURRICULUM.md) — the tenth lesson of the title, after HIV and AIDS (TITLES.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the environment's major problems, as the teacher guide names them, are five — pollution, global warming, ozone layer depletion, waste disposal and loss of biodiversity — each with its keyed definition, and the human impacts that trigger the damage. This lesson stands on teacher-guide evidence only.
**This lesson's own level:** this lesson stands on teacher-guide evidence only (Addis Ababa Grade 7 guide, §7.1.1) — it extends the student book's air- and water-pollution lessons with four problems the student book never names as taught problems. Its acceptance count is 0 countries, stated plainly in its sources.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Air Pollution and Water — Pollution and Shrinking Clean Water — the student book's pollution lessons this guide lesson widens. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. List the guide's five major environmental problems.
2. Give the keyed definition of each: pollution, global warming, waste disposal, biodiversity.
3. Name the keyed human impacts and what they trigger.
4. State the review key's effects: skin cancer, breathing problems, loss of acquired immunity, rising air temperature.
