# Global Warming, Ozone, Waste and Biodiversity — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Give the keyed definitions of waste disposal and biodiversity.
**Answer:** Waste disposal is the collection, processing, recycling or deposition of human society's waste. Biodiversity is the variety of life, ecosystems and living organisms (teacher guide — evidence).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the four keyed human impacts and two things they trigger.
**Answer:** Overpopulation, pollution, burning fossil fuels and deforestation — triggering, among others, climate change, soil erosion, poor air quality and undrinkable water (teacher guide — evidence).
