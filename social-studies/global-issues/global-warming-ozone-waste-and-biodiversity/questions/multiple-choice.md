# Global Warming, Ozone, Waste and Biodiversity — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which list is the guide's five major environmental problems?
a) Pollution, global warming, ozone depletion, waste disposal, biodiversity loss  b) Floods, earthquakes, volcanoes, storms, drought  c) Noise, light, traffic, crowds, prices
**Answer: a) Pollution, global warming, ozone depletion, waste disposal, biodiversity loss** — those are the guide's five.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Global warming, in the keyed definition, is primarily from —
a) volcanoes only  b) fossil-fuel burning raising heat-trapping greenhouse gases  c) the Moon's pull
**Answer: b) fossil-fuel burning raising heat-trapping greenhouse gases** — the keyed definition names fossil-fuel burning.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Biodiversity means —
a) only wild animals  b) the variety of life, ecosystems and living organisms  c) the number of farms
**Answer: b) the variety of life, ecosystems and living organisms** — the keyed definition is the wide one.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which is a keyed human impact?
a) Burning fossil fuels  b) Planting trees  c) Terracing
**Answer: a) Burning fossil fuels** — burning fossil fuels is keyed.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The review key's effects include —
a) skin cancer and breathing problems  b) better harvests  c) cooler air
**Answer: a) skin cancer and breathing problems** — skin cancer, breathing problems, loss of acquired immunity, rising air temperature.
