# Hands-on — Global Warming, Ozone, Waste and Biodiversity

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Five-tile survey
Walk your locality and find one visible sign of each of the five problems — smoke or fouled water, heat-trapping burning, waste heaped or dumped, a place where life's variety has thinned. Photograph in words: write what you saw and where, one line per tile.

## Waste audit
For one day, list your household's waste and where each item goes. Sort it by the guide's waste-disposal chain: what could be collected, processed, recycled — and what is simply deposited? Mark the recycled share you could raise.
