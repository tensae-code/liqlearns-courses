The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the

The guide widens the picture. Five problems, one environment.

Pollution — harmful things let into the world around us.

Global warming — the Earth heating, from burning fuels.

A thinning ozone layer. Waste with nowhere to go. Life's variety shrinking.

People drive all five. People can answer all five.
