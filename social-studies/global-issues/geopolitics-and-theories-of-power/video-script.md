Iran's book, the *Iran Grade 11 Geography (2)* (printed p. 142), teaches: Lesson 11 Geopolitics (ژئوپلیتیک): the word power (قدرت) — ability and capacity; the instruments of power (army, police, judiciary, intelligence) usable to hold power and even oppress; the pioneers of political geography — Ratzel concluding that in the struggle for power the great states win and annex the small; Alfred Mahan's sea-power theory, the seas as the effective besieger of land power.

Iran's own way (Iran Grade 11 Geography (2)): the whole book is built on one organising idea — the region (ناحیه). Four chapters divide the world into regions four ways: what a region is, natural regions (climate, landforms, living, coasts, mountains — with Nepal as the highland case study), human regions (cultural, economic), and political regions (the state, borders, geopolitics — with the Persian Gulf and the European Union as case studies).
