# Hands-on — Geopolitics And Theories Of Power

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Lesson 11 Geopolitics (ژئوپلیتیک): the word power (قدرت) — ability and capacity; the instruments of power (army, police, judiciary, intelligence) usable to hold power and even oppress; the pioneers of political geography — Ratzel concluding that in the struggle for power the great states win and annex the small; Alfred Mahan's sea-power theory, the seas as the effective besieger of land power. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
