# Hands-on — Strategies to Promote Peace

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Activity 4 Strategies to promote peace in South Sudan
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: at the individual level, improving education and peace education (war begins in the mind); democratisation as the most effective peace strategy; increasing prosperity with equal distribution of resources, since poverty and unjust distribution are major causes of war; and at the international system level, replacing the use of force with compromise and negotiations. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
