# Video script — Strategies to Promote Peace

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

South Sudan Primary 8 P8 Social Studies SB, printed p. 82, teaches: at the individual level, improving education and peace education (war begins in the mind); democratisation as the most effective peace strategy; increasing prosperity with equal distribution of resources, since poverty and unjust distribution are major causes of war; and at the international system level, replacing the use of force with compromise and negotiations.

South Sudan's own way: The final primary book is a civics of unity for a country at war with itself — power sharing is taught from the 2016 Transitional Government of National Unity cabinet photograph, the constitution's Section 33 equality clause is quoted in full, and Kenya's 2007–08 crisis and Kofi Annan's mediation are the worked example of how a neighbour's unity process shapes South Sudan's own.
