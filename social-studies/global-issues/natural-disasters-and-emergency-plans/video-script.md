# Video script — Natural Disasters and Emergency Plans

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

From Grade 2, Ecuador teaches natural disasters together with the answer to them: every school and family needs an emergency plan, and everyone must know the evacuation routes before anything happens. In a country of volcanoes, floods and landslides, the plan and the route are taught as part of the disaster topic itself — not as an extra.

Ecuador learns this inside its own books' way: Geography is taught inside three thematic sections that always start from the child's family, school and province and widen to Ecuador and South America; heavy use of hazard maps (floods, landslides, volcanoes) and emergency plans from Grade 2, reflecting Ecuador's volcanic / El Niño risk profile. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
