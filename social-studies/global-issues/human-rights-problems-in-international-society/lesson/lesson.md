## Sub lesson 1 — Human Rights Problems in International Society

South Korea's Grade 10 MiraeN Integrated Society, printed p. 116; Grade 10 Donga Integrated Society, printed p. 120, teaches: IV.주제6 국제 사회의 인권 문제, IV.06 지구촌 인권 문제의 해결 방안은? — teaches: human-rights problems in international society and the world's responses to them; solutions to the global village's human-rights problems. ## Sub lesson 2 — South Korea's way — the books' own teaching way

South Korea's own way: the high-school common subject 통합사회 (Integrated Society) is built around nine key questions asked from temporal, spatial, social and ethical perspectives at once; its civics core is Unit IV (human rights guaranteed by the constitution, law-abiding spirit and citizen participation) and Unit VI (justice and inequality), and its peace unit teaches the peaceful resolution of Korea's own division and of East Asia's history conflicts as the closing civics content. Donga writes every lesson as a question ("Why is civil disobedience ever needed?", "What does universal ethics do in understanding a culture?") and is the only one of the five publishers to teach civil disobedience (시민 불복종) beside the law-abiding spirit, and to split the conditions of happiness into two lessons — living environment/economic stability, then democracy's development and moral practice. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
