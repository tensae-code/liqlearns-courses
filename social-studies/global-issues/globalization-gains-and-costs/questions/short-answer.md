# Globalization — gains and costs — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Define globalization.
**Answer:** The deepening worldwide interconnection of production, trade, finance, culture and media.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name two gains and two costs from the weighing.
**Answer:** Gains — any two of: expanded production of goods and services; wider markets; cultural exchange. Costs — any two of: dependency; unequal gains; the charge of a new imperialism.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Who carries globalization, on the book's account?
**Answer:** Multinational corporations.
