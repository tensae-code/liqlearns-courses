# Globalization — gains and costs — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Globalization interconnects…
a) production, trade, finance, culture, media  b) villages only  c) oceans
**Answer: a) production, trade, finance, culture, media**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The process is…
a) brand new  b) centuries old but now accelerated  c) finished
**Answer: b) centuries old but now accelerated**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Its carriers are…
a) multinational corporations  b) village councils  c) rivers
**Answer: a) multinational corporations**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The political frame is…
a) East against West  b) Global North / Global South  c) town against country
**Answer: b) Global North / Global South**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Gains claimed include…
a) expanded production, markets, cultural exchange  b) isolation  c) falling trade
**Answer: a) expanded production, markets, cultural exchange**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Costs charged include…
a) dependency and unequal gains  b) too much equality  c) slow ships
**Answer: a) dependency and unequal gains**
