# Video script — Latin American Integration Blocs

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Venezuela's book, the *Venezuela Grade 6 G6 CS Venezuela y Su Gente* (printed p. 63), teaches: Nuevos organismos de unión latinoamericana y caribeña. Venezuela's book, the *Venezuela Grade 7 G7 Geography Humanidad* (printed p. 110), teaches: ¡Unidad, unidad, unidad!: ALBA, CELAC, UNASUR, Petrocaribe, MERCOSUR, OPEP and the multipolar world. Venezuela's book, the *Venezuela Grade 11 G11 CS Geografía de Venezuela* (printed p. 252), teaches: Unión de nuestra América para el fortalecimiento de los pueblos.

Venezuela's own way (Venezuela Grade 6 G6 CS Venezuela y Su Gente): the whole Geography block is one argument — "Nuestro espacio geográfico en tiempos del petróleo": how oil remade population (2011 census), cities (metropolisation), industry location, internal migration and landscapes; learners start by learning how to *look* at a piece of oil-era Venezuela.

Venezuela's own way (Venezuela Grade 7 G7 Geography Humanidad): a Geohistoria (Ramón Tovar) approach — geographic space is always explained through the historical conditions that built it; every chapter opens with a literary epigraph, works through "Entérate / Entretejiendo saberes / Indagando" boxes, and keeps returning to the learner's own community and to Caracas as the worked example.

Venezuela's own way (Venezuela Grade 11 G11 CS Geografía de Venezuela): a final-year economic geography of Venezuela alone — population past/present/future, human development in numbers, then resource by resource (minerals, land, fisheries, forests, oil, water/hydroelectricity) each read through sovereignty, collective participation and environmental conscience, closing on Latin American union.
