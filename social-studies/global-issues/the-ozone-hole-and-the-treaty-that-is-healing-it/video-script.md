# Video script — The ozone hole — and the treaty that is healing it

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The ozone (O₃) layer is a stratospheric barrier about 15–30 km up that absorbs harmful ultraviolet radiation. It is being thinned by ozone-depleting substances, above all chlorofluorocarbons (CFCs), everywhere except the tropics — letting through UV that raises skin cancers and cataracts. Note the place: this protective ozone sits high in the stratosphere; it is not the ground-level ozone of smog, which harms. The Montreal Protocol's phase-out of ozone-depleting substances protects the ozone layer and the climate together, since many of those substances are also powerful greenhouse gases. Replacement fluorinated gases (F-gases) serve as the substitute technology in cooling and industry. Unit 6 opens by framing the whole family of global environmental problems together — climate change, ozone depletion, biodiversity loss, air and water pollution, land degradation — and this lesson is its proof that a global problem can be met by a global agreement that works.
