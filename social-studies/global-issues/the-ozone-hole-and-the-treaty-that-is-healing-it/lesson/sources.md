Afghanistan's own way (Afghanistan Grade 12 Geography (Dari)): the final year is pure human geography — Afghanistan's borders side by side (north, north-east, east/south, west), its population and ethnic make-up, religion and language, transport and communications, then the same themes for the world: population, races, religions, languages, global communications, and global environmental problems to close the whole twelve-year course.

Pakistan's own way (Pakistan Grade 7 Geography 7): a dedicated English-medium Geography book built entirely on Pakistan's own physical and economic geography — the Indus plain and delta, the canal system, Rabi/Kharif crops, Sui gas, Thar coal, Khewra salt and Tarbela power — with world concepts (river courses, settlement hierarchy, greenhouse effect, mining types) taught through Pakistani examples first.

Mozambique's own way (Mozambique Grade 10 PESD II Mod 2): a full physical-climatology course — atmosphere structure to magnetosphere, heat balance, pressure/wind circulation, air masses and fronts, humidity and precipitation, the water cycle, then Köppen/Thornthwaite classification worked through climate tables with African station graphs (Arkassa, Tamanrasset, Algiers), closing on protection of bioclimatic resources.
