# The ozone hole — and the treaty that is healing it — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The ozone layer sits about… up
a) 1–2 km  b) 15–30 km  c) 100 km
**Answer: b) 15–30 km**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** It absorbs harmful…
a) infrared  b) ultraviolet radiation  c) rain
**Answer: b) ultraviolet radiation**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The chief ozone-depleting substances are…
a) CFCs  b) oxygen  c) nitrogen
**Answer: a) CFCs**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Extra UV raises…
a) skin cancers and cataracts  b) frostbite  c) malaria
**Answer: a) skin cancers and cataracts**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The treaty phasing out these substances is the…
a) Kyoto Protocol  b) Montreal Protocol  c) Paris Agreement
**Answer: b) Montreal Protocol**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** The substitute technology named is…
a) F-gases  b) more CFCs  c) coal
**Answer: a) F-gases**
