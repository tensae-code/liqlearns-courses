# The ozone hole — and the treaty that is healing it — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The ozone layer is thinned everywhere except the tropics, as the book reports.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Protecting the ozone layer also protects the climate.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Ground-level ozone and stratospheric ozone do the same job.
**Answer: False**
