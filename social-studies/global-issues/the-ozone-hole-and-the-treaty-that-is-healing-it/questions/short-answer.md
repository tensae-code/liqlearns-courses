# The ozone hole — and the treaty that is healing it — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Why is Montreal called a success story?
**Answer:** Its phase-out is working — protecting the ozone layer and the climate together.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Where exactly is the ozone layer?
**Answer:** In the stratosphere, about 15–30 km up.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Name three other problems in Unit 6's family.
**Answer:** Any three of: climate change, biodiversity loss, air pollution, water pollution, land degradation.
