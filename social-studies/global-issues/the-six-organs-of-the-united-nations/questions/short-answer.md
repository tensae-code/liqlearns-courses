# The Six Organs of the United Nations — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Ch. 2 The six principal organs — the UN drawn as a temple standing on six pillars — the General Assembly, the Security Council, the Economic and Social Council, the Trusteeship Council, the International Court of Justice and the Secretariat (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Ch. 2 The General Assembly — every member state inside it with one vote each; its work — debating the principles of peace and disarmament, taking up what the Security Council sends it, approving the budget and sharing out its costs, and electing the Secretary-General on the Council's recommendation; it sits each year from September (any close wording).**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Ch. 2 ECOSOC and the Trusteeship Council — the Economic and Social Council, 54 members serving three years in turns, steering the UN's economic and social work and guarding human rights; and the Trusteeship Council walking its eleven trust territories toward self-government until the last of them, Palau, stood free in 1994 (any close wording).**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Ch. 2 The Court and the Secretariat — the International Court of Justice at The Hague, the UN's own judicial organ, judging the disputes states bring before it and advising the Assembly and the Council; and the Secretariat under the Secretary-General, the organisation's chief administrative officer, chosen for five years, who carries threats to the peace before the Council (any close wording).**

(any close wording).**
