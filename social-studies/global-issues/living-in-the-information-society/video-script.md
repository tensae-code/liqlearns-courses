# Video script — Living in the Information Society

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Japan Grades 3–4 Watashitachi no Doutoku (printed p. 170), teaches: Information machines are convenient, but never give out friends' names and phone numbers to a stranger who calls asking.
Japan Grades 5–6 Watashitachi no Doutoku (printed p. 184), teaches: Using the internet well — too much gaming, personal information, and what cannot be said without meeting.

Japan's own way (Book 2): the middle pupils' reader widens in the same four steps as the other two Doutoku books — raise yourself, relate to people, feel life, relate to everyone — and plants the first civics inside moral teaching: society's rules are introduced through a banned swing the class wins back by making rules, and fairness is taught as never shutting anyone out.

Japan's own way (Book 3): the older pupils' reader moves from self-discipline to society in four widening units — polish yourself, connect with people, cherish life, connect with everyone — and teaches civics inside moral stories: rights are introduced as the other face of duties, the Diet appears as the place where rules are made, and fairness is taught through a story of a Vietnamese classmate facing discrimination.
