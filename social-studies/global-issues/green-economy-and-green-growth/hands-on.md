# Hands-on — Green economy and green growth

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Retire the smokestack
Draw a town powered by sun, wind and water icons, its smokestack retired. Under it, write the green economy's three adjectives and one town feature matching each.

## Catalyst argument
Write four lines arguing the book's claim — green shift as poverty catalyst, not luxury — for a developing country, using one renewable source and one poverty link per line pair.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
