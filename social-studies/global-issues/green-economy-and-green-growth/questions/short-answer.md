# Green economy and green growth — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "The green economy defined" in your own words.
**Answer:** A green economy is low-carbon, resource-efficient and socially inclusive, improving wellbeing while cutting environmental risk.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Investment as the driver" in your own words.
**Answer:** Green growth fosters growth while natural assets keep providing resources and services.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Renewable energy, and peak oil" in your own words.
**Answer:** The energy basis is renewable: solar, biomass, geothermal, hydro, tidal and wind.
