# Green economy and green growth — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A green economy is low-carbon, resource-efficient and socially inclusive, improving wellbeing while cutting environmental risk.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Green growth fosters growth while natural assets keep providing resources and services.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The energy basis is renewable: solar, biomass, geothermal, hydro, tidal and wind.
**Answer: True**
