# Green economy and green growth — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "The green economy defined"?
a) A green economy is low-carbon, resource-efficient and socially inclusive, improving wellbeing while cutting environmental risk  b) Green growth fosters growth while natural assets keep providing resources and services  c) The energy basis is renewable: solar, biomass, geothermal, hydro, tidal and wind
