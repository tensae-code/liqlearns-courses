# Hands-on — Irans Neighbours Pakistan And Turkmenistan

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Lesson 11 The neighbouring countries (2) (کشورهای همسایه ۲): the Islamic Republic of Pakistan in the east of Iran, with high mountains in its north and west; in the last thirty years Pakistan has progressed greatly in economy and industry — the port of Karachi among the world's most important centres of trade and export and import, with steel, cement, textile, telecommunications and aviation industries; and the Republic of Turkmenistan in Iran's north-east, where a great desert covers most of the country's area, and its people are mostly busy with farming and animal husbandry — cotton, fruit, grapes and wheat among its important farm products.
