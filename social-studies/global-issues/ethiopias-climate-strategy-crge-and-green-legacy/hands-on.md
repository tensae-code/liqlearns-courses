# Hands-on — Ethiopia's climate strategy — CRGE and Green Legacy

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Terraced hillside
Draw seedlings being planted on a terraced hillside under CRGE and Green Legacy banners. Annotate which of deforestation, flooding and food insecurity each terrace answers.

## Policy line
Draw Ethiopia's policy line as a timeline — NAPA 2007, CRGE 2011, Green Legacy 2020 — with the diagnosis under the first and the headline number under the last. Then name the responsible ministry correctly.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
