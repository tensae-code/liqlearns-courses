# Ethiopia's climate strategy — CRGE and Green Legacy — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "A vulnerable country diagnoses itself"?
a) NAPA (2007) diagnoses vulnerability as rain-fed agriculture, underdeveloped water resources and high population growth  b) The CRGE (2011) aims at carbon-neutral, energy-efficient growth and middle-income status by 2025  c) The Green Legacy (June 2020) was credited with an estimated 4.1 billion trees by August 2020
