# Ethiopia's climate strategy — CRGE and Green Legacy — matching

Match each part to its right statement.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A vulnerable country diagnoses itself — c
**2. (Sub lesson 2)** CRGE — carbon-neutral, resilient growth — a
**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Green Legacy — billions of trees — b

Statements:
a) The CRGE (2011) aims at carbon-neutral, energy-efficient growth and middle-income status by 2025
b) The Green Legacy (June 2020) was credited with an estimated 4.1 billion trees by August 2020
c) NAPA (2007) diagnoses vulnerability as rain-fed agriculture, underdeveloped water resources and high population growth

**Answer key:** 1 — c; 2 — a; 3 — b
