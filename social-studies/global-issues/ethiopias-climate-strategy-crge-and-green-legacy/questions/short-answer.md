# Ethiopia's climate strategy — CRGE and Green Legacy — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "A vulnerable country diagnoses itself" in your own words.
**Answer:** NAPA (2007) diagnoses vulnerability as rain-fed agriculture, underdeveloped water resources and high population growth.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "CRGE — carbon-neutral, resilient growth" in your own words.
**Answer:** The CRGE (2011) aims at carbon-neutral, energy-efficient growth and middle-income status by 2025.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Green Legacy — billions of trees" in your own words.
**Answer:** The Green Legacy (June 2020) was credited with an estimated 4.1 billion trees by August 2020.
