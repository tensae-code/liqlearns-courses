# Ethiopia's climate strategy — CRGE and Green Legacy — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** NAPA (2007) diagnoses vulnerability as rain-fed agriculture, underdeveloped water resources and high population growth.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The CRGE (2011) aims at carbon-neutral, energy-efficient growth and middle-income status by 2025.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The Green Legacy (June 2020) was credited with an estimated 4.1 billion trees by August 2020.
**Answer: True**
