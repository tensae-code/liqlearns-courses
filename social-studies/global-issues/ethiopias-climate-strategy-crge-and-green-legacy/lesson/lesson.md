# Ethiopia's climate strategy — CRGE and Green Legacy

## Do it first

One of the world's most climate-vulnerable countries answers with a strategy and a tree-planting campaign counted in billions. Guess the strategy's two adjectives — resilient and what?

## Sub lesson 1 — A vulnerable country diagnoses itself

Ethiopia is among the most climate-vulnerable countries: a century of recurrent drought and famine, plus soil erosion and land degradation from cultivation of marginal land and grazing pressure. Its National Adaptation Programme of Action (2007) and Nationally Appropriate Mitigation Actions (2011) diagnose vulnerability as rain-fed agriculture, underdeveloped water resources and high population growth, with agriculture, health, transport, natural resources, energy and industry the most exposed sectors.

## Sub lesson 2 — CRGE — carbon-neutral, resilient growth

The 2011 Climate Resilient Green Economy strategy aims at carbon-neutral, energy-efficient growth and middle-income status by 2025 with GDP per capita of $1,000. Resilience and greenness as one strategy, not two — the unit's mitigation and adaptation lessons fused into national policy.

## Sub lesson 3 — Green Legacy — billions of trees

The Green Legacy Initiative (June 2020) is a national tree-planting campaign against deforestation, flooding and food insecurity, credited with an estimated 4.1 billion trees planted by August 2020. The responsible agency is the Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change.
