# Hands-on — Gcc Challenges

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: the problems that came with the Gulf states' rapid development — expatriate labour (large remittances sent abroad, pressure on basic services, an unmixed social environment, some security problems) and the states' balancing of national against expatriate labour; unemployment (youth refusing vocational work, education and training not matching the labour market, employers preferring cheaper expatriates) and the programmes against it (directing the private sector to employ nationals, technical institutes, incentives for vocational work); environmental pollution of air, water and soil; and desertification (sand encroachment, removing vegetation, drought, overgrazing, cutting trees) and the fight against it (protected reserves, the unified wildlife-protection system, tree weeks, penalties and guards). Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
