The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Saudi Arabia Grade 8 Social Studies (printed pp. 192–197), teaches: The problems that came with the Gulf states' rapid development — expatriate labour (large remittances sent abroad, pressure on basic services, an unmixed social environment, some security problems) and the states' balancing of national against expatriate labour; unemployment (youth refusing vocational work, education and training not matching the labour market, employers preferring cheaper expatriates) and the programmes against it (directing the private sector to employ nationals, technical institutes, incentives for vocational work); environmental pollution of air, water and soil; and desertification (sand encroachment, removing vegetation, drought, overgrazing, cutting trees) and the fight against it (protected reserves, the unified wildlife-protection system, tree weeks, penalties and guards).

Saudi Arabia's own way: the Grade 8 book places Saudi Arabia inside the Gulf Cooperation Council — its organs and goals, its achievements and its challenges — then teaches systems and regulations (how a system is made, their kinds and their benefits), and ends with planning priorities, time management and evaluating plans, and hobbies including the old folk games.
