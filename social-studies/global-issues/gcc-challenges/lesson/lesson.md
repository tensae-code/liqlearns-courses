# Gcc Challenges

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: the problems that came with the Gulf states' rapid development — expatriate labour (large remittances sent abroad, pressure on basic services, an unmixed social environment, some security problems) and the states' balancing of national against expatriate labour; unemployment (youth refusing vocational work, education and training not matching the labour market, employers preferring cheaper expatriates) and the programmes against it (directing the private sector to employ nationals, technical institutes, incentives for vocational work); environmental pollution of air, water and soil; and desertification (sand encroachment, removing vegetation, drought, overgrazing, cutting trees) and the fight against it (protected reserves, the unified wildlife-protection system, tree weeks, penalties and guards) — as Saudi Arabia's books teach it. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Gcc Challenges

Saudi Arabia Grade 8 Social Studies (printed pp. 192–197), teaches: The problems that came with the Gulf states' rapid development — expatriate labour (large remittances sent abroad, pressure on basic services, an unmixed social environment, some security problems) and the states' balancing of national against expatriate labour; unemployment (youth refusing vocational work, education and training not matching the labour market, employers preferring cheaper expatriates) and the programmes against it (directing the private sector to employ nationals, technical institutes, incentives for vocational work); environmental pollution of air, water and soil; and desertification (sand encroachment, removing vegetation, drought, overgrazing, cutting trees) and the fight against it (protected reserves, the unified wildlife-protection system, tree weeks, penalties and guards). This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Saudi Arabia's way — the book's own teaching way

Saudi Arabia's own way: the Grade 8 book places Saudi Arabia inside the Gulf Cooperation Council — its organs and goals, its achievements and its challenges — then teaches systems and regulations (how a system is made, their kinds and their benefits), and ends with planning priorities, time management and evaluating plans, and hobbies including the old folk games. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
