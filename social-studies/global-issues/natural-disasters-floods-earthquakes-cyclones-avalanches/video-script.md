Pakistan's book, the *Pakistan Grade 4 SS4* (printed pp. 82–83), teaches: Natural disasters: floods, earthquakes (2005 Kashmir earthquake), tropical cyclones, avalanches. Pakistan's book, the *Pakistan Grade 6 Geography 6* (printed p. 59), teaches: What a natural disaster is and its kinds: earthquakes, volcanic eruptions, tsunami, landslides, floods, droughts, cyclones, desertification, forest fires (the lesson slug was proposed as NEW in Book 1 of this file; counted here as an existing target). Pakistan's book, the *Pakistan Grade 8 Geography 8* (printed p. 31), teaches: Natural disasters introduced: volcanism, landslides, desertification, floods, cyclones, earthquakes and forest fires (the lesson slug was proposed as NEW in Book 1 of this file; counted here as an existing target).

Pakistan's own way (Pakistan Grade 4 SS4): one combined Social Studies book in Urdu where the Geography chapter (Ch.5, printed pp. 61–93) works entirely through Pakistan's own map, landforms, weather and census, with BOLTS as the map-elements mnemonic and "I have learnt!" / "Work for me to do" boxes closing every topic.

Pakistan's own way (Pakistan Grade 6 Geography 6): world physical Geography taught through Pakistan's own land — its fault lines and 1935/2005 earthquakes, Khewra salt and Thar coal among the rocks, K2 and the Karakoram among the mountains, Pakistan's four climatic regions, its six forest types from mangrove to alpine, and the Indus flood as the country's defining disaster.

Pakistan's own way (Pakistan Grade 8 Geography 8): the Urdu-medium twin of the English Geography course — deep map-and-graph craft first (dot and choropleth maps of Pakistan itself), then landform agents, oceans, a full Pakistan-anchored disaster chapter, the world's natural regions, Pakistan's own climate, its four neighbouring regions and strategic place, underdevelopment with Pakistan's own causes, and modern techniques down to Pak Sat-1.
