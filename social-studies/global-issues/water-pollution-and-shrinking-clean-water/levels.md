# Water — Pollution and Shrinking Clean Water — sub lessons and depth

Depth structure (TITLES.md, titles spanning both books): sub lesson 1 is the Grade 7 treatment — water pollution, printed pp. 150–151 of the Grade 7 book. Sub lessons 2–3 are the Grade 8 climb — shrinking clean-water supply, printed pp. 188–189 of the Grade 8 book — same water, deeper question: not only 'is it spoiled?' but 'why is there not enough of it, and what are the ways out?'

## Sub lesson 1 — Water pollution (the Grade 7 depth)
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: define water pollution and state what polluted water loses: its nature changed, no self-renewal, physical quality, biological content and economic use lost.

## Sub lesson 2 — Why clean water runs short (the Grade 8 climb)
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: explain shortage from uneven spread, waste, climbing consumption and lost quality, against 71% cover and shortage in 80 countries; explain the Abay saying.

## Sub lesson 3 — Results, and the three ways out (the Grade 8 climb)
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: list the results and give the three solutions — saving water, clean-water storage, desalination — in the book's order.
