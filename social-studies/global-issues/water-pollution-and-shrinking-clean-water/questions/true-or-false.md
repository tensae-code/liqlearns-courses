# Water — Pollution and Shrinking Clean Water — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Polluted water can still renew itself as easily as clean water.
**Answer: False** — polluted water cannot renew itself.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** 71% of the Earth is covered by water.
**Answer: True** — that is the book's figure.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Water shortage happens only where there are no rivers.
**Answer: False** — it strikes some Ethiopian cities plainly, rivers notwithstanding — spread, waste and quality decide.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Stopping leaks is part of saving water.
**Answer: True** — reduce consumption, stop leaks, replace pipes in time, use water reasonably.
