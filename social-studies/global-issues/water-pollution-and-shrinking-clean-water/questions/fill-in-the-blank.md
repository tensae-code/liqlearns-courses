# Water — Pollution and Shrinking Clean Water — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Polluted water loses its physical quality, biological content and ______ use.
**Answer: economic.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Converting salt water into clean water is called ______.
**Answer: desalination.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The first solution — the one every person on Earth can do — is ______ water.
**Answer: saving.**
