# Water — Pollution and Shrinking Clean Water — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Give the four reasons clean water runs short, from the lesson.
**Answer:** The stores are unevenly spread; water is wasted; consumption climbs yearly; and quality is lost.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** List the results of water shortage the book names.
**Answer:** Falling crop products; rising death and migration of domestic and wild animals; vegetation shrinking and desert spreading; and rising human death and migration from drinking-water shortage.
