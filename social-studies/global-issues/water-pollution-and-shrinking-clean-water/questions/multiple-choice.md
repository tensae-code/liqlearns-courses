# Water — Pollution and Shrinking Clean Water — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Polluted water loses all of these EXCEPT —
a) its physical quality  b) its ability to renew itself  c) its wetness
**Answer: c) its wetness** — pollution takes quality, self-renewal, biological content and economic use — the water is still wet, and unusable.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Water pollution reaches which of these?
a) Only rivers  b) Lakes, rivers, seas, oceans and groundwater  c) Only wells
**Answer: b) Lakes, rivers, seas, oceans and groundwater** — the book names all five.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Clean-water shortage is recorded in how many of the world's countries?
a) 8  b) 80  c) 180
**Answer: b) 80** — shortage is recorded in 80 countries.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** 'The Abay's child dies of thirst' means —
a) the Abay river has dried up  b) great rivers flow near, yet clean drinking water is unreached  c) children should not live near rivers
**Answer: b) great rivers flow near, yet clean drinking water is unreached** — amount near at hand is not the same as clean supply piped and safe.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** The solution the book calls most promising is —
a) saving water  b) storing clean water  c) desalination — converting salt water into clean water
**Answer: c) desalination — converting salt water into clean water** — desalination is named most promising as printed.
