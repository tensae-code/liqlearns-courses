# Video script — Water — Pollution and Shrinking Clean Water

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Water can be spoiled. Waste flows in, and the river changes.

It cannot clean itself. It cannot be used.

And clean water is shrinking too.

The Earth is covered in water — but it is spread unevenly, wasted, lost.

Eighty countries record shortage.

Save water. Store it clean. Even the sea can be made fresh.

The Abay's child must not die of thirst.
