# Hands-on — Water — Pollution and Shrinking Clean Water

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The kebele water audit
Carry out the book's audit: visit or recall your kebele's rivers, ponds, wells and springs. For each, write what reaches it — waste, runoff, nothing — and how household waste is disposed of nearby.

## Leak hunt
At home or school, find every dripping tap, leaking pipe or open container. Estimate together how much a day of dripping wastes by catching it in a jug for one hour and multiplying. Fixing one leak is sub lesson 3 in your hands.
