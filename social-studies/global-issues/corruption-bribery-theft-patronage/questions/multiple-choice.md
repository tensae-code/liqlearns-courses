# Corruption — bribery, theft, patronage — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Corruption is…
a) abuse of public position for private gain  b) any crime  c) tax collection
**Answer: a) abuse of public position for private gain**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The North–South frame says corruption predominantly affects…
a) the North only  b) states, particularly in the Global South, implicating both  c) villages only
**Answer: b) states, particularly in the Global South, implicating both**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Bribery is…
a) the rarest type  b) the most familiar type  c) not corruption
**Answer: b) the most familiar type**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** State capture is a form of…
a) theft of public assets  b) patronage  c) voting
**Answer: a) theft of public assets**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Kick-backs are…
a) the bribery form of asset theft  b) salaries  c) fines
**Answer: a) the bribery form of asset theft**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Patronage distributes favours through…
a) examinations  b) loyalty networks  c) lotteries
**Answer: b) loyalty networks**
