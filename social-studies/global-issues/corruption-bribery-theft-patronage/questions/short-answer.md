# Corruption — bribery, theft, patronage — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the three types of corruption.
**Answer:** Bribery; theft of public assets; patronage.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is state capture?
**Answer:** Organised theft of public assets — the state itself taken over for private gain, in the book's typology.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Give the definition of corruption.
**Answer:** The abuse of public position for private gain.
