# Video script — Corruption — bribery, theft, patronage

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Corruption is the abuse of public position for private gain — in three types: bribery that sways decisions, theft of public assets including state capture, and patronage through loyalty networks. First, the concept: Corruption is the abuse of public position for private gain. Then, the three types — and the scales: First, bribery — the most familiar type: payments to sway administrative decisions, reaching across the whole scope of government. Hold those two parts together and you hold the lesson.
