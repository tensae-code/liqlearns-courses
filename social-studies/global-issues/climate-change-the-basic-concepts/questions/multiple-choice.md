# Climate change — the basic concepts — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the difference between weather and climate?
a) Weather is over many years; climate is today  b) Weather is today's condition; climate is the pattern over many years  c) They are the same thing
**Answer: b) Weather is today's condition; climate is the pattern over many years**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Gondar's cold winters, year after year, describe its…
a) weather  b) climate  c) storm season
**Answer: b) climate**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Global warming is…
a) the measured rise in Earth's average temperature  b) a single hot day  c) the changing of the seasons
**Answer: a) the measured rise in Earth's average temperature**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In the IPCC definition, climate change must persist for…
a) one week  b) one season  c) decades or longer
**Answer: c) decades or longer**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Climate change can come from…
a) natural variability or human activity  b) human activity only  c) volcanoes only
**Answer: a) natural variability or human activity**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** In the framework cycle, society's response…
a) ends the story  b) feeds back into the causes  c) does not matter
**Answer: b) feeds back into the causes**
