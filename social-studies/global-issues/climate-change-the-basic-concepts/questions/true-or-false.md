# Climate change — the basic concepts — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A very hot afternoon is, by itself, climate change.
**Answer: False**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Warmer air can warm oceans, and warmer oceans can feed stronger storms.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Climate change means a change in the mean and/or the variability of climate.
**Answer: True**
