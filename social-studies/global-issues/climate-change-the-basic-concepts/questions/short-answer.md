# Climate change — the basic concepts — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why does today's clothing depend on weather, not climate?
**Answer:** Because weather is today's condition; clothing answers the day, while climate is the long pattern.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Name two knock-on changes global warming drives.
**Answer:** Any two of: stronger hurricanes, melting glaciers, lost habitats.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What three parts does the framework cycle link?
**Answer:** Causes, impacts, and society's response.
