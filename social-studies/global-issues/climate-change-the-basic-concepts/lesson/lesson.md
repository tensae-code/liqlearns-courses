## Sub lesson 1 — Weather today, climate over years

Weather is the air's condition today and this week — it decides the clothing you choose this morning. Climate is the atmosphere's condition in a place over many years: Gondar's cold winters and Gambella's year-round humid warmth are climates, not weather. A region's climate scales up to the global climate. Global warming is the measured rise in the Earth's average temperature, and because air, water and land are interdependent — warmer air warms oceans, warmer oceans feed stronger storms — that one rise drives knock-on changes: stronger hurricanes, melting glaciers and lost habitats.

## Sub lesson 2 — What climate change means

The IPCC defines climate change as an identifiable change in the state of the climate — in its mean and/or its variability — that persists for decades or longer, whether it comes from natural variability or from human activity. The unit's framework figure links the whole story in one cycle: causes act on the climate, the climate's changes land as impacts on people and nature, and society responds — and the response feeds back into the causes. Keep that cycle in mind through this whole unit: nothing in climate change stands alone.
