## Sub lesson 2 — Palestine's way — the book's own teaching way

Palestine's own way (Grade 5, PS G5P1): Social Studies opens with a full Earth-science unit (shape, movements, surface, life components, disasters) taught through Palestinian examples — seasons read off Palestinian crops, surface forms read off Palestine's own map, which pupils draw with its borders. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
