# Hands-on — The Muslim World League

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: a world Islamic people's organisation headquartered in Makkah, clarifying the true Islamic call and building bridges of Islamic and humanitarian cooperation with all; founded by a resolution of the General Islamic Conference at Makkah in 1381H/1962; its goals — coordinating the efforts of those working for Islam worldwide, developing the methods of spreading the call by the Quran and Sunnah, raising the media, preaching, educational and cultural means, founding Islamic offices and centres, urgent relief for Muslims struck by wars and natural disasters, and activating mosques; its bodies — the General Islamic Conference, the Supreme Council, the World Supreme Council for Mosques, the International Organisation for Relief, Care and Development, the Islamic Fiqh Academy and the World Organisation for the Book and the Sunnah — with more than thirty-four offices and thirteen Islamic cultural centres across Europe, Africa, Australia and America. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
