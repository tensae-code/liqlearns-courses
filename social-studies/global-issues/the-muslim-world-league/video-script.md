Saudi Arabia Grade 10 Social Studies (Secondary Tracks, First Common Year) (printed pp. 108–110), teaches: A world Islamic people's organisation headquartered in Makkah, clarifying the true Islamic call and building bridges of Islamic and humanitarian cooperation with all; founded by a resolution of the General Islamic Conference at Makkah in 1381H/1962; its goals — coordinating the efforts of those working for Islam worldwide, developing the methods of spreading the call by the Quran and Sunnah, raising the media, preaching, educational and cultural means, founding Islamic offices and centres, urgent relief for Muslims struck by wars and natural disasters, and activating mosques; its bodies — the General Islamic Conference, the Supreme Council, the World Supreme Council for Mosques, the International Organisation for Relief, Care and Development, the Islamic Fiqh Academy and the World Organisation for the Book and the Sunnah — with more than thirty-four offices and thirteen Islamic cultural centres across Europe, Africa, Australia and America.

Saudi Arabia's own way: the Grade 10 Tracks book starts from the components that make a state strong and influential and Saudi Arabia's own foundations and components, then follows the Kingdom's work for Arab, Islamic and international causes, and surveys the organisations it belongs to — the European Union as a model of integration, the League of Arab States, the Muslim World League, the Organisation of Islamic Cooperation, OPEC, the United Nations and the G20.
