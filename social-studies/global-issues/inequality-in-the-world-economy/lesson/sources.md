**Acceptance count: taught in 4 countries (Iran, Bangladesh, Honduras, Costa Rica). Haiti is teacher-guide evidence only — named here, counted nowhere.**

Costa Rica's own way (Estudios Sociales CONED 11): the full national economic story in one book — agro-export dependence, the first state-intervention reforms of 1914, import substitution under the welfare state, then the 1980s crisis and the PAE adjustment that opened the economy and courted Intel and the free zones.

Costa Rica's own way (Estudios Sociales 11, Porras y Gamboa): a commercial textbook built on the same national programme as the CONED anthology but with far more economic detail — full PAE I/II/III measure lists, the CAT export-certificate fraud, state banking's six effects, and Costa Rica's services/call-centre economy measured against the region.

Iran's own way (Iran Grade 9 Social Studies): one combined Social Studies book of 24 lessons in 12 chapters, and here the Geography comes first: Ch. 1 Our planet, the Earth (Lessons 1–2, printed pp. 1–14), Ch. 2 Lithosphere, hydrosphere, atmosphere (Lessons 3–4, printed pp. 15–28), Ch. 3 Biosphere, an amazing diversity (Lessons 5–6, printed pp. 29–40) and Ch. 4 The inhabitants of planet Earth (Lessons 7–8, printed pp. 41–52). The other parts are named here, not placed: History — Ch. 5 The age of unity and flourishing (Lessons 9–10, printed pp. 53–66), Ch. 6 Iran from Nader Shah to Naser al-Din Shah (Lessons 11–12, printed pp. 67–80), Ch. 7 Iran in the constitutional era (Lessons 13–14, printed pp. 81–94), Ch. 8 The fall of the monarchy and the forming of the Islamic Republic (Lessons 15–16, printed pp. 95–116); Civics and culture — Ch. 9 Culture and identity (Lessons 17–18, printed pp. 117–130), Ch. 10 Family and society (Lessons 19–20, printed pp. 131–142), Ch. 11 Government and people (Lessons 21–22, printed pp. 143–156); Economics — Ch. 12 Productivity (Lessons 23–24, printed pp. 157–175).

Iran's own way (Iran Grade 11 Geography (2)): the whole book is built on one organising idea — the region (ناحیه). Four chapters divide the world into regions four ways: what a region is, natural regions (climate, landforms, living, coasts, mountains — with Nepal as the highland case study), human regions (cultural, economic), and political regions (the state, borders, geopolitics — with the Persian Gulf and the European Union as case studies).

Honduras's own way (Honduras Grade 6 G6 Student CCSS): Grade 6 goes global — world tourism, the maldistribution of world wealth, and integration blocs with the common market explained, while Honduras's own trade (US as main market) anchors it home.

Honduras's own way (Honduras Grade 9 G9 Student CCSS): Grade 9 closes the cycle with the world economy and Honduras inside it — work formal and informal, the lempira's devaluation named the country's biggest problem, and remittances weighed honestly: a strong boost to the economy that also demotivates production and drains the labour force.
