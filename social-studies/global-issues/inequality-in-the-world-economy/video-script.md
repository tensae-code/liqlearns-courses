The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Iran's book, the *Iran Grade 9 Social Studies* (printed p. 47), teaches: Lesson 8 Injustice and inequality in the world (بی‌عدالتی و نابرابری در جهان): the land surface is divided among more than 200 countries parted by borders; the Human Development Index in different countries in 2023, and Iran compared with its neighbours; an estimated 80 per cent of the world's income owned by 20 per cent of its people, most people in the high-income countries (North America and Western Europe) in welfare and a high level of life; the ranking of countries by gross domestic product (World Bank, 2021) and foreign debts; literacy and education as measures of a country's state; glossary: shanty settlement (زاغه‌نشینی / آلونک‌نشینی), settling without permission, illegally. Iran's book, the *Iran Grade 11 Geography (2)* (printed p. 102), teaches: Lesson 8: Inequality in the world economy (نابرابری در اقتصاد جهانی) — all countries must trade, but they do not stand equal in it; the causes holding back the less industrially developed countries, external (colonialism and the domination of the powerful) and internal; the multinational companies pictured; researchers' reports that the wealth–poverty gap widens daily, and inequality written on the face of the Earth because resources and services are not fairly spread.

Iran's own way (Iran Grade 9 Social Studies): one combined Social Studies book of 24 lessons in 12 chapters, and here the Geography comes first: Ch. 1 Our planet, the Earth (Lessons 1–2, printed pp. 1–14), Ch. 2 Lithosphere, hydrosphere, atmosphere (Lessons 3–4, printed pp. 15–28), Ch. 3 Biosphere, an amazing diversity (Lessons 5–6, printed pp. 29–40) and Ch. 4 The inhabitants of planet Earth (Lessons 7–8, printed pp. 41–52). The other parts are named here, not placed: History — Ch. 5 The age of unity and flourishing (Lessons 9–10, printed pp. 53–66), Ch. 6 Iran from Nader Shah to Naser al-Din Shah (Lessons 11–12, printed pp. 67–80), Ch. 7 Iran in the constitutional era (Lessons 13–14, printed pp. 81–94), Ch. 8 The fall of the monarchy and the forming of the Islamic Republic (Lessons 15–16, printed pp. 95–116); Civics and culture — Ch. 9 Culture and identity (Lessons 17–18, printed pp. 117–130), Ch. 10 Family and society (Lessons 19–20, printed pp. 131–142), Ch. 11 Government and people (Lessons 21–22, printed pp. 143–156); Economics — Ch. 12 Productivity (Lessons 23–24, printed pp. 157–175).

Iran's own way (Iran Grade 11 Geography (2)): the whole book is built on one organising idea — the region (ناحیه). Four chapters divide the world into regions four ways: what a region is, natural regions (climate, landforms, living, coasts, mountains — with Nepal as the highland case study), human regions (cultural, economic), and political regions (the state, borders, geopolitics — with the Persian Gulf and the European Union as case studies).
