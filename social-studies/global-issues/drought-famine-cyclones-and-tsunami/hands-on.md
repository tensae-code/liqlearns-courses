# Hands-on — Drought Famine Cyclones And Tsunami

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Famines and droughts (Balochistan and Sindh, Tharparkar worst hit), hurricanes and cyclones, and tsunami (the 2004 Indian Ocean tsunami, about 225,000 dead). Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
