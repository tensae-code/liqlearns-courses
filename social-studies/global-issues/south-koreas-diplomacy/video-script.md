# Video script — South Koreas Diplomacy

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

South Korea's Grade 8 Bisang Social ②, printed p. 114; Grade 8 Cheonjae Middle Social Studies ② (2015), printed pp. 116–124, teaches: VI.3 우리나라의 외교, Ⅵ 국제 사회와 국제 정치 (외교와 한반도 평화) — teaches: South Korea's own diplomacy — its aims and how Korea conducts foreign relations; foreign policy and settling peace on the Korean Peninsula — the unit wrap-up names 외교 정책 and the peninsula.

South Korea's own way: the middle-school Social Studies ② volume opens with civics, not geography — human rights and the Constitution first, then the three state institutions one by one, and the full text of the Constitution of the Republic of Korea printed as an appendix for pupils to read; it closes with global-citizenship units (global problems, reducing inter-regional inequality). this Cheonjae edition reorganises the same national frame into twelve units of its own — human rights get a whole unit that ends in remedies (국가인권위원회, 헌법 소원) and labour rights, and the book closes with 더불어 사는 세계 ("the world we live in together"): hunger, biodiversity, appropriate technology and fair trade as the pupil's own global agenda.
