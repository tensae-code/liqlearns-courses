# Video script — The linked crises — climate, desertification, drought, famine

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Climate change, desertification, drought and famine are interlinked contemporary issues — each feeding the others. Some regions already face more severe drought and rising wildfire risk under the changing climate; drought-stressed land degrades toward desertification; degraded land and failed rain together open the door to famine. The unit's next sections unpack the chain link by link: desertification first, then drought, then famine. Treating famine as only a food problem, or desertification as only a soil problem, misses the machinery connecting them: climate shifts the rain; rain failure stresses land and livelihoods; stressed land yields less; less yield, where poverty already stands, becomes hunger. The chain is the unit's map — every later lesson hangs on it.
