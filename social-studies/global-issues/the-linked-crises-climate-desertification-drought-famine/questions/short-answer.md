# The linked crises — climate, desertification, drought, famine — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Draw the chain in words.
**Answer:** Climate change → drought stress → land degradation/desertification → failed yields → famine (links feed each other).

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Which link does the unit teach first after this opening?
