# The linked crises — climate, desertification, drought, famine — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The four linked issues are climate change, desertification, drought and…
a) famine  b) flooding  c) earthquakes
**Answer: a) famine**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Some regions already face more severe…
a) drought and wildfire risk  b) snow  c) floods only
**Answer: a) drought and wildfire risk**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Drought-stressed land degrades toward…
a) forest  b) desertification  c) wetland
**Answer: b) desertification**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The chain's lesson is that responses must…
a) treat one link only  b) answer the connected machinery  c) wait
**Answer: b) answer the connected machinery**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Degraded land plus failed rain opens the door to…
a) famine  b) bumper harvests  c) tourism
**Answer: a) famine**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Unit 7 unpacks the chain in the order: desertification, drought, then…
a) famine  b) climate  c) oceans
**Answer: a) famine**
