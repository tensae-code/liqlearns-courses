# Climate Change — Causes — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Shrinking forests are one of the chapter's three issues.
**Answer: True** — with climate change and shrinking water supply.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The book stresses the human-made part as the danger.
**Answer: True** — nature contributes, but the danger stressed is the human-made part.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Greenhouse gases are harmful because there are any at all.
**Answer: False** — the cause is their accumulating excessively, disturbing the air's former natural state.
