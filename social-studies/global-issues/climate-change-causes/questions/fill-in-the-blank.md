# Climate Change — Causes — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Heat-trapping gases are also called ______ gases.
**Answer: greenhouse.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Coal, petroleum and natural gas burned widely release ______ dioxide.
**Answer: carbon.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Volcanoes and natural ______ also feed the change.
**Answer: wildfires.**
