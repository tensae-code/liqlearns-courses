# Climate Change — Causes — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Grade 8 chapter's three issues are —
a) climate change, shrinking water, shrinking forests  b) earthquakes, floods, storms  c) roads, schools, hospitals
**Answer: a) climate change, shrinking water, shrinking forests** — the chapter's own three.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Climate change is caused by heat-trapping gases —
a) disappearing  b) accumulating excessively in the atmosphere  c) turning into rain
**Answer: b) accumulating excessively in the atmosphere** — accumulating excessively is the book's mechanism.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The wide human use of coal, petroleum and natural gas releases —
a) oxygen  b) carbon dioxide  c) steam only
**Answer: b) carbon dioxide** — carbon dioxide, disturbing the air's former natural state.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Nature's contributions named in the book are —
a) volcanoes and natural wildfires  b) tides and moonlight  c) earthquakes
**Answer: a) volcanoes and natural wildfires** — volcanoes and natural wildfires also feed the change.
