# Climate Change — Causes — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** State the cause of climate change in the book's full sentence-form.
**Answer:** Climate change is caused by heat-trapping (greenhouse) gases accumulating excessively in the atmosphere, above all from the wide use of coal, petroleum and natural gas releasing carbon dioxide.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Where do this chapter's other two issues live in this title?
**Answer:** Shrinking water stands in the water lesson (Water — Pollution and Shrinking Clean Water) and shrinking forests in the closing lesson (Forests — Shrinking and Care) — the chapter's three issues are carried across the title.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Why does the book mention volcanoes at all, if the danger is human-made?
**Answer:** To be accurate about all the feeders of the change — nature contributes too — while stressing that the human-made part is the danger and the part that can be answered.
