# Hands-on — Climate Change — Causes

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## One-day emissions diary
From your Do-it-first list, keep a one-day diary of every fossil-fuel or wood burning your household does or uses — cooking, light, transport, work. Mark each: could it be done with less burning? Star the two biggest.

## Sky layer drawing
Draw the Earth's sky layer as the lesson pictures it: the air's former natural state as a clear band, then the same band thickened with what burning adds. Label what traps the heat.
