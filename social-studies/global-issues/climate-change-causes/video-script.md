# Video script — Climate Change — Causes

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The air had a balance. We are disturbing it.

Coal burns. Oil burns. Gas burns.

Out comes carbon dioxide — a gas that traps heat.

Too much of it gathers in the sky. That gathering is the cause.

Volcanoes add some. Wildfires add some.

But the wide burning is ours — and the book says: that is the danger.

Next: what the change does to crops, soil, health and water.
