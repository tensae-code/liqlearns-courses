# Climate Change — Causes

## Do it first

Count the fires and engines in one ordinary day of your household: cooking fires, vehicles, lamps, machines. Each one breathes something into the sky. Write the list before the book names what gathers up there.

## Sub lesson 1 — The chapter's three issues

The Grade 8 chapter takes up three contemporary issues: **climate change, shrinking water supply and shrinking forests**. The water and forest climbs stand in their own lessons of this title; this lesson and the next take the first issue.

## Sub lesson 2 — Greenhouse gases piled too high

**Climate change is caused by heat-trapping (greenhouse) gases accumulating excessively in the atmosphere.** Above all, this is human work: humankind's wide use of **coal, petroleum and natural gas** releases **carbon dioxide** and **disturbs the air's former natural state**. Nature contributes too — **volcanoes and natural wildfires** also feed the change. But the danger the book stresses is **the human-made part**, and the disasters that follow from it.

## Say it to your companion

Name the three chapter issues, then the three fuels. Your companion names the gas released and nature's two contributions; swap until both halves are firm.
