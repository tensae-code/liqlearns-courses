# Video script — Arab Solidarity

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

United Arab Emirates Social Studies & National Education, G9 teacher guide, volume 1, printed p. 11, teaches: the factors that bind the Arab peoples and the aims of their solidarity (التضامن العربي); its models in practice — the efforts of the UAE and Saudi Arabia to protect the Arab region, Arab support in the October 1973 war, and the Emirati–Saudi alliance in Decisive Storm — solidarity taught as a duty peoples owe each other, under the citizenship concepts of belonging, cooperation and social responsibility

United Arab Emirates' own way: the ninth-grade year opens with the state itself — its constitution first, then its place among peoples: Arab solidarity, and the Human Fraternity Document signed in its own capital, studied analytically and compared with the Prophet's Medina Charter.
