# Hands-on — Arab Solidarity

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Arab solidarity
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: the factors that bind the Arab peoples and the aims of their solidarity (التضامن العربي); its models in practice — the efforts of the UAE and Saudi Arabia to protect the Arab region, Arab support in the October 1973 war, and the Emirati–Saudi alliance in Decisive Storm — solidarity taught as a duty peoples owe each other, under the citizenship concepts of belonging, cooperation and social responsibility Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## United Arab Emirates' way — the book's own teaching way
Say how United Arab Emirates's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
