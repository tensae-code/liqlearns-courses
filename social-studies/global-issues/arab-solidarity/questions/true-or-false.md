# Arab Solidarity — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of the lesson teaches: the factors that bind the Arab peoples and the aims of their solidarity (التضامن العربي); its models in practice — the efforts of the UAE and Saudi Arabia to protect the Arab region, Arab support in the October 1973 war, and the Emirati–Saudi alliance in Decisive Storm — solidarity taught as a duty peoples owe each other, under the citizenship concepts of belonging, cooperation and social responsibility
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of the lesson teaches: the ninth-grade year opens with the state itself — its constitution first, then its place among peoples: Arab solidarity, and the Human Fraternity Document signed in its own capital, studied analytically and compared with the Prophet's Medina Charter.
**Answer: True**
