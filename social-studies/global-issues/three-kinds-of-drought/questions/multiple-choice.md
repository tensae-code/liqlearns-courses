# Three kinds of drought — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Meteorological drought is…
a) low reservoirs  b) rainfall well below the long-term average  c) dry soil
**Answer: b) rainfall well below the long-term average**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Hydrological drought is water falling short in…
a) streams, reservoirs and aquifers  b) clouds  c) leaves
**Answer: a) streams, reservoirs and aquifers**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Agricultural drought is scarcity of…
a) soil water for crops  b) river water  c) drinking water only
**Answer: a) soil water for crops**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Agricultural drought can strike even when rain statistics look…
a) terrible  b) less severe  c) perfect
**Answer: b) less severe**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The Ethiopian case of independent hydrological drought involves…
a) a cut-off aquifer  b) a volcano  c) a flood
**Answer: a) a cut-off aquifer**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Treating the three as synonyms is…
a) correct  b) usually done, but wrong  c) required
**Answer: b) usually done, but wrong**
