# Three kinds of drought — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the three kinds and their shortages.
**Answer:** Meteorological — rain; hydrological — stored water; agricultural — soil water for crops.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why does naming the kind matter?
**Answer:** The responses differ by kind.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What causes the book's independent hydrological case?
**Answer:** Geological change cutting off part of an aquifer.
