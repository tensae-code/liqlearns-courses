# Three kinds of drought — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Hydrological drought can arrive independently of meteorological drought.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The three kinds always arrive together.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Meteorological drought is measured against the long-term average.
**Answer: True**
