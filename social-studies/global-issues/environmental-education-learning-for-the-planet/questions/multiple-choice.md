# Environmental education — learning for the planet — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Environmental education is education…
a) in, about and for the environment  b) about exams  c) for teachers only
**Answer: a) in, about and for the environment**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** It builds ecological literacy, values and…
a) opportunities for action  b) test scores  c) uniforms
**Answer: a) opportunities for action**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Its forms include formal and…
a) secret  b) informal/non-formal  c) military
**Answer: b) informal/non-formal**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** It is lifelong: its audience includes…
a) only children  b) employees, clergy, activists and officials too  c) only officials
**Answer: b) employees, clergy, activists and officials too**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** At its core it is…
a) pure chemistry  b) social — behaviour, critical thinking, participation  c) pure biology
**Answer: b) social — behaviour, critical thinking, participation**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** The practical school outlet named is…
a) exams  b) environmental clubs  c) assemblies
**Answer: b) environmental clubs**
