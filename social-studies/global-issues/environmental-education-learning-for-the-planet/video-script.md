# Video script — Environmental education — learning for the planet

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Environmental education is most simply education in, about and for the environment: an approach engaging learners, communities and policy makers to build ecological literacy, values and opportunities for action. It runs formal (in school) and informal/non-formal (public settings, media, workplaces), and it also travels as tacit knowledge — what people absorb by living and working in a place. Its defining characteristics: it is lifelong — youth are a critical audience, but employees, clergy, activists and officials count too — and interdisciplinary: geography and ecology among the sciences, but social at its core, working on behaviour, critical thinking and citizen participation. Its roots are traced to the 18th century and Jean-Jacques Rousseau. The unit's practical outlet is close to home: school environmental clubs, where learning becomes planting, protecting and teaching others.
