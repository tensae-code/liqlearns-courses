# Lesson plan — Natural Hazards And Disaster Management In PNG And The Pacific

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Global Issues · **Lesson:** Natural Hazards And Disaster Management In PNG And The Pacific
**Place in the curriculum:** Global Issues (CURRICULUM.md) — built in the Geography expansion (Papua New Guinea, 2026-11-14).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Strand 1 Unit 5 Natural Hazards: Common natural hazards and warning signs; causes of natural hazards and disasters in Papua New Guinea, Lessons 41 and 45; Strand 1 Unit 5 Natural Hazards: Earthquakes (causes), safety guidelines, risk management and disaster planning, Lessons 41–52; Strand 1 Unit 5 Natural Hazards: Global hazards and risk management — plague, eruptions and mudslides, tornadoes, freezing and heat; rehabilitation programs, disaster resilience, relief assistance, Lessons 45–52; Strand 1 Unit 4, Topic 3: Natural disasters; Unit 11.2: comparative table of natural hazards and consequences, local hazard-risk mapping, disaster management strategies with emphasis on PNG and the Pacific — as Papua New Guinea's teacher guides teach it (see lesson/sources.md for the book and printed page).
