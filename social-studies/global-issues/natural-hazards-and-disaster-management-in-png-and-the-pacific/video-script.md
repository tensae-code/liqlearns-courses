Papua New Guinea's book, the *Papua New Guinea Grade 6 Social Science TG* (printed p. 53), teaches: Strand 1 Unit 5 Natural Hazards: Common natural hazards and warning signs; causes of natural hazards and disasters in Papua New Guinea, Lessons 41 and 45 (teacher guide — evidence). Papua New Guinea's book, the *Papua New Guinea Grade 7 Social Science TG* (printed p. 51), teaches: Strand 1 Unit 5 Natural Hazards: Earthquakes (causes), safety guidelines, risk management and disaster planning, Lessons 41–52 (teacher guide — evidence). Papua New Guinea's book, the *Papua New Guinea Grade 8 Social Science TG* (printed p. 48), teaches: Strand 1 Unit 5 Natural Hazards: Global hazards and risk management — plague, eruptions and mudslides, tornadoes, freezing and heat; rehabilitation programs, disaster resilience, relief assistance, Lessons 45–52 (teacher guide — evidence). Papua New Guinea's book, the *Papua New Guinea Grade 9 Social Science TG* (printed p. 76), teaches: Strand 1 Unit 4, Topic 3: Natural disasters (teacher guide — evidence). Papua New Guinea's book, the *Papua New Guinea Grade 11 Geography TG OBE* (printed p. 33), teaches: Unit 11.2: comparative table of natural hazards and consequences, local hazard-risk mapping, disaster management strategies with emphasis on PNG and the Pacific (teacher guide — evidence).

Papua New Guinea's own way (Papua New Guinea Grade 6 Social Science TG) (teacher guide — evidence): in Senior Primary the strands are re-ordered and Environment and Resources comes first and largest — a full local-environment course (physical/human environment, environmental change, human impacts, sustainability, natural hazards) taught through sample guided lessons with benchmarks, before Civics, Culture, History and Trade. That evidence is stocked here as the way Papua New Guinea teaches this thing; it is cited as evidence only, outside acceptance counts.

Papua New Guinea's own way (Papua New Guinea Grade 7 Social Science TG) (teacher guide — evidence): Environment and Resources now turns national — formation of PNG's mountains, islands and rivers; PNG's population and migration; its mineral, marine and agricultural resources and how extraction impacts are managed; conservation areas located on the map of PNG; hazard safety and risk management. That evidence is stocked here as the way Papua New Guinea teaches this thing; it is cited as evidence only, outside acceptance counts.

Papua New Guinea's own way (Papua New Guinea Grade 8 Social Science TG) (teacher guide — evidence): the local-to-global ladder completes — after province (G5), nation (G7), Grade 8's Environment and Resources strand is global: global climate, global settlement and population, global farming, urbanization and logging impacts, global hazards and disaster resilience. That evidence is stocked here as the way Papua New Guinea teaches this thing; it is cited as evidence only, outside acceptance counts.

Papua New Guinea's own way (Papua New Guinea Grade 9 Social Science TG) (teacher guide — evidence): at secondary level Social Science splits into five strands — Geography, History, Political Science, Economics, Environment. The Geography strand is a weather-and-climate skills course (instruments, data, graphs — with Port Moresby and Kokopo data) moving to world climate/vegetation, migration, and land use; the Environment strand carries Earth systems, resources and sustainability. That evidence is stocked here as the way Papua New Guinea teaches this thing; it is cited as evidence only, outside acceptance counts.

Papua New Guinea's own way (Papua New Guinea Grades 11–12 Geography TG OBE) (teacher guide — evidence): an outcomes-based guide — it prescribes no lesson scripts; for each syllabus unit it lists suggested learning activities and assessment tasks built around the local environment (soil profiles, local hazard sites, a PNG mine and logging case study, a transect along latitude 6°S), with fieldwork and geographical inquiry as the core skills. That evidence is stocked here as the way Papua New Guinea teaches this thing; it is cited as evidence only, outside acceptance counts.
