# Hands-on — Externalities — costs that fall on others

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Cost-arrow game: a 'factory' learner stacks profit blocks while cost cards are dealt onto bystander learners' desks. The class names the externality, then replays with the factory paying its own cost cards.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — a factory's smoke drifting over a neighbourhood, with a clinic bill arriving at a house that bought nothing; a cost arrow landing on the surrounding community — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
