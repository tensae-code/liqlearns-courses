# Externalities — costs that fall on others — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Air pollution is named a negative externality of a business organisation.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Air pollution is named a negative externality of a business organisation.
**Answer: False** — that statement belongs to sub lesson 1 (The word and its first appearance); sub lesson 2 says: An external cost is a cost not borne by the firm but incurred by other members of society.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Air is a free resource, but people may pay for treatment of the health harm caused by air pollution.
**Answer: True**
