# Externalities — costs that fall on others — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "The word and its first appearance"?
a) a negative externality — an effect landing on people outside the transaction  b) a cost not borne by the firm but incurred by other members of society  c) air is free, but treatment for pollution's health harm is paid for
**Answer: a) a negative externality — an effect landing on people outside the transaction**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "External cost defined"?
a) a negative externality — an effect landing on people outside the transaction  b) air is free, but treatment for pollution's health harm is paid for  c) a cost not borne by the firm but incurred by other members of society
**Answer: c) a cost not borne by the firm but incurred by other members of society**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Air stays free — you pay for the harm"?
a) a cost not borne by the firm but incurred by other members of society  b) air is free, but treatment for pollution's health harm is paid for  c) a negative externality — an effect landing on people outside the transaction
**Answer: b) air is free, but treatment for pollution's health harm is paid for**
