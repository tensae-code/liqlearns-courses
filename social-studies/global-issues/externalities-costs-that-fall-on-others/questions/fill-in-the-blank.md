# Externalities — costs that fall on others — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Air pollution is named a negative _____ of a business organisation.
**Answer: externality**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** An external cost is borne not by the firm but by other members of _____.
**Answer: society**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Air itself is unpaid, but people may pay for treatment of the _____ harm pollution causes.
**Answer: health**
