# Externalities — costs that fall on others — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "The word and its first appearance" in your own words.
**Answer:** Air pollution is named a negative externality of a business organisation.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "External cost defined" in your own words.
**Answer:** An external cost is a cost not borne by the firm but incurred by other members of society.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Air stays free — you pay for the harm" in your own words.
**Answer:** Air is a free resource, but people may pay for treatment of the health harm caused by air pollution.
