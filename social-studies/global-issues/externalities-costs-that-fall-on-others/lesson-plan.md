# Lesson plan — Externalities — costs that fall on others

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Global Issues · **Lesson:** Externalities — costs that fall on others
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 3 — Economic Resources and Markets / Unit 5 — Introduction to Production and Cost of the Grade 9 Economics course (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** An external cost is a cost not borne by the firm but incurred by other members of society — like air pollution, where the air is free but people pay for the health harm. (Teacher-guide evidence lesson.)
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the lessons before it in Global Issues, and on the earlier-grade lessons of this course.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of ..." in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.
