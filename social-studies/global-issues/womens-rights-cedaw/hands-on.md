# Hands-on — Womens Rights Cedaw

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: CEDAW, the Convention on the Elimination of All Forms of Discrimination Against Women (adopted 1979, in force 1981; Bangladesh among its 165 signatories by 1999) — ending gender disparity and securing women's equal place in every field. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Bangladesh's way
Say how Bangladesh's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
