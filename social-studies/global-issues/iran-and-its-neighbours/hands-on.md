# Hands-on — Iran And Its Neighbours

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Lesson 19 Our neighbours (همسایگان ما): what is a border (مرز چیست؟) — note: the border line is not only on the ground; the space above the land border is part of it too, as if an imaginary wall stood over it; we are parted from neighbouring countries not only by land borders — by sea too we are neighbours with some countries: name them; people of the neighbouring countries come to see Iran's historical works and shrines (the holy shrine of Imam Hussein and Hazrat Abbas in Karbala pictured the other way), and the shared festivals and feasts — Nowruz, one of the oldest feasts left from ancient Iran, and Eid al-Fitr in a neighbouring country. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Iran's way
Say how Iran's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
