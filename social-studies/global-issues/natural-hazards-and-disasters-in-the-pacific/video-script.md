# Video script — Natural Hazards and Disasters in the Pacific

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Fiji's book, the *Fiji Year 7 Social Science* (printed p. 55), teaches: 3.2 Hazard, disaster and its socio-economic and environmental impact (tropical cyclones, floods, earthquakes, tsunamis, drought; man-made deforestation and pollution; traditional warning signs and modern instruments). Fiji's book, the *Fiji Years 11–12 Physical Geography* (printed p. 106), teaches: Chapter 10 Natural Hazards – Fiji: hazard vs disaster; tectonic, atmospheric and cultural hazards; tropical cyclones — causes and formation over warm oceans.

Fiji's own way (Fiji Year 7 Social Science): Year 7 widens the lens to the whole South Pacific — island types, "islandness" and vulnerability, Pacific hazards, and resources owned by tribes and clans; Fiji is taught as the hub of the South West Pacific.

Fiji's own way (Fiji Years 11–12 Physical Geography): The physical volume is comparative — every theme (climate, relief, soil, vegetation, hazards) is taught twice, New Zealand first, then Fiji (Monsoon Asia for hazards), so Fiji's land is always read against a temperate and a monsoon case.
