# What causes desertification — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name four human causes.
**Answer:** Any four of: cultivating drought-risk land, firewood cutting/deforestation, fire for grazing, overgrazing/domestication pressure, poverty, population growth.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain albedo feedback in one line.
**Answer:** Stripped land reflects more, heats the air differently, and deepens desert conditions.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Why does poverty drive degradation?
**Answer:** It forces short-term survival over long-term care of the land.
