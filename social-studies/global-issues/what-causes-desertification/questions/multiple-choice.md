# What causes desertification — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A principal cause in tropical arid areas is…
a) firewood cutting  b) tourism  c) fishing
**Answer: a) firewood cutting**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Fire is used to…
a) improve grazing  b) make rain  c) build soil
**Answer: a) improve grazing**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Overgrazing presses hardest around…
a) water-scarce settlements  b) ports  c) capitals
**Answer: a) water-scarce settlements**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Poverty acts as a driver by forcing…
a) long-term care  b) short-term survival over long-term care  c) migration only
**Answer: b) short-term survival over long-term care**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Albedo feedback begins when stripped land…
a) turns dark  b) reflects more sunlight  c) grows forest
**Answer: b) reflects more sunlight**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** The feedback deepens…
a) rainfall  b) the desert conditions that caused it  c) rivers
**Answer: b) the desert conditions that caused it**
