# What causes desertification — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Cultivating drought-risk land is among the causes.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Deforestation can in turn drive desertification.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Albedo feedback cools and heals stripped land.
**Answer: False**
