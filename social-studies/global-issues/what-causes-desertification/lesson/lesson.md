# What causes desertification

## Do it first

List the ways people press on dry land: ploughing it, cutting it, burning it, grazing it. The book's causes are mostly hands, not sky.

## Sub lesson 1 — Human pressure leads

The causes are led by human pressure: cultivation of drought-risk land; firewood cutting — a principal cause in tropical arid areas, its deforestation in turn driving desertification; fire used to improve grazing; overgrazing and domestication pressure around water-scarce settlements; poverty itself as a driver, forcing short-term survival over long-term care; and population growth multiplying every other pressure.

## Sub lesson 2 — The albedo feedback

The causes interact with climate through albedo feedback: stripped land reflects more sunlight, heats the air differently, and deepens the desert conditions that stripped it. Cause becomes effect becomes cause — the same feedback shape you met in the climate unit, here working on land. Breaking the loop anywhere — cover, rest, alternative fuel — weakens the whole cycle.
