# Video script — What causes desertification

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The causes are led by human pressure: cultivation of drought-risk land; firewood cutting — a principal cause in tropical arid areas, its deforestation in turn driving desertification; fire used to improve grazing; overgrazing and domestication pressure around water-scarce settlements; poverty itself as a driver, forcing short-term survival over long-term care; and population growth multiplying every other pressure. The causes interact with climate through albedo feedback: stripped land reflects more sunlight, heats the air differently, and deepens the desert conditions that stripped it. Cause becomes effect becomes cause — the same feedback shape you met in the climate unit, here working on land. Breaking the loop anywhere — cover, rest, alternative fuel — weakens the whole cycle.
