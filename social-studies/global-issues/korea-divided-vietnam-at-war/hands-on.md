# Hands-on — Korea divided, Vietnam at war — the Cold War turns hot in Asia

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The divided family
Write a short letter, never sent, from a Korean elder in the North to a brother in the South in 1953, using only facts from this lesson. Read it aloud.

## Two acts programme
Design a theatre programme for 'Vietnam: a war in two acts' — Act I France 1946–54, Act II America 1957–73 — with Ho Chi Minh and the Viet Minh billed as the constant company.
