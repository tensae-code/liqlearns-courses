# Lesson plan — Korea divided, Vietnam at war — the Cold War turns hot in Asia

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Global Issues · **Lesson:** Korea divided, Vietnam at war — the Cold War turns hot in Asia
**Place in the curriculum:** Global Issues — the Cold War in Asia thread; the Grade 10 book's second Cold War Asia lesson (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** where the Cold War turned hot: Korea, divided between Kim Il-sung's communist North and Syngman Rhee's South, and Vietnam, where Ho Chi Minh's Viet Minh fought France (1946–54) and then America (to 1973).
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Japan rises again; China turns communist — China's shadow lies over both wars. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Korea — one people, two states** — the peninsula divided: Kim Il-sung's communist North against Syngman Rhee's South along the 38th parallel.
2. **Vietnam — two wars in one** — Ho Chi Minh and the Viet Minh: the 'dirty war' against France 1946–54, then American involvement 1957–1973 and Nixon's Vietnamization.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Japan rises again; China turns communist. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name Korea's two leaders and its dividing line.
2. Tell Vietnam's two acts: the French war and the American war, with dates.
3. Define Vietnamization and whose policy it was.

## What you need
