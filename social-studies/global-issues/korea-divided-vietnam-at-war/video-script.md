# Video script — Korea divided, Vietnam at war — the Cold War turns hot in Asia

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style, in the animated-map spirit: the map opens, peoples and armies move, capitals rise, routes light up. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The map opens on a peninsula. A line is drawn across its waist — the 38th parallel. Two flags rise, two capitals light up.
The line holds through war. The camera rests on it: one people, two states.
South to Vietnam. Jungle green; first French flags fade by 1954, then American aircraft shadow the canopy for two decades.
A word appears over the withdrawal: Vietnamization. The superpower's flag lowers; the war it leaves behind burns on.
