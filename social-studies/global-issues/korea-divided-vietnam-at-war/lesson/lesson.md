# Korea divided, Vietnam at war — the Cold War turns hot in Asia

## Do it first

The Cold War was cold only in Europe. In Asia it burned. One peninsula was cut in half by a line on a map; one jungle country fought two great powers in a row for nearly thirty years. This lesson walks both battlefields.

## Sub lesson 1 — Korea — one people, two states

Korea came out of the Second World War liberated from Japan — and divided by the Cold War that followed liberation. In the North stood a communist state under Kim Il-sung; in the South, a state under Syngman Rhee aligned with the United States. Between them ran the 38th parallel, a line drawn by the victors that hardened into a border between two worlds in one nation.

When war came to the peninsula it was the Cold War turned hot: the two Koreas, and behind them their great-power backers, fought to a standstill that left the division standing. Families divided by the parallel stayed divided. Korea remains the Cold War's clearest Asian scar: one people, two states, one line.

## Sub lesson 2 — Vietnam — two wars in one

Vietnam's wars run in two acts. The independence movement — Ho Chi Minh and the Viet Minh — first fought France, the returning colonial power, in the confrontation of 1946–54 that the book calls the dirty war. France's defeat ended the first act; it did not end the war, because the Cold War's logic drew the United States into the second: American involvement in the Second Indo-China War, whose presidents' policies from 1957 to 1973 the book sets as the study question.

The American war's late policy has its own name in the lesson: Vietnamization — President Nixon's policy of handing the fighting over to South Vietnamese forces while American troops withdrew. It names the superpower's dilemma exactly: how to leave a war it could not win. Vietnam's long resistance, against two great powers in succession, is Asia's other Cold War epic.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion: Korea — two leaders, one parallel; Vietnam — two wars, one independence movement. Define Vietnamization to finish.
