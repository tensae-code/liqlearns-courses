# Hands-on — Managing Floods And Landslides

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Lesson 6: Flood management (مدیریت سیل) — the retaining wall protecting the riverside settlements of Garmabdareh, and floodwater guided and diverted by side channels on the Zayandeh Rud at the Jajrud headwaters in Tehran province; structural and non-structural methods; after the flood stops: siting temporary shelter and raising the relief tents; landslide management — the slides' extent far smaller than earthquakes' (most under a kilometre), and during a slide: get out of its path and the mudflow's at once, inform the rescue services, help those who need it to leave. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
