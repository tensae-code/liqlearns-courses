# Video script — Managing Floods And Landslides

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Iran's book, the *Iran Grade 12 Geography (3)* (printed p. 104), teaches: Lesson 6: Flood management (مدیریت سیل) — the retaining wall protecting the riverside settlements of Garmabdareh, and floodwater guided and diverted by side channels on the Zayandeh Rud at the Jajrud headwaters in Tehran province; structural and non-structural methods; after the flood stops: siting temporary shelter and raising the relief tents; landslide management — the slides' extent far smaller than earthquakes' (most under a kilometre), and during a slide: get out of its path and the mudflow's at once, inform the rescue services, help those who need it to leave.

Iran's own way (Iran Grade 12 Geography (3)): 'applied' geography organised as three problems of Iranian life — settlements, transport, and natural hazards — each taken in two lessons (what it is, then how to manage it) with a geographical-skills section (GIS, time zones, remote sensing) closing each chapter.
