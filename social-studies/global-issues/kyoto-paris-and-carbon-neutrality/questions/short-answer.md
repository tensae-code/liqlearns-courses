# Kyoto, Paris and carbon neutrality — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Name two co-benefits of emission cuts.
**Answer:** Any two of: slowed greenhouse enhancement, less acid rain, less haze/photochemical smog, health gains.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name three renewable energy sources from the unit.
**Answer:** Any three of: sun, wind, rain (hydro), tides, waves, geothermal.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Which three countries are named for carbon-neutrality pledges?
**Answer:** Costa Rica, Iceland and Norway.
