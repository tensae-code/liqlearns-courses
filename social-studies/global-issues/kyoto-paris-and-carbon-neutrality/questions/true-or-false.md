# Kyoto, Paris and carbon neutrality — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Global emissions rose over 25% from 1990 to 2012.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** China carried a Kyoto reduction duty.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Tree planting can offset emissions toward neutrality.
**Answer: True**
