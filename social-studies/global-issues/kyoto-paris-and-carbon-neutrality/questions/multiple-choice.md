# Kyoto, Paris and carbon neutrality — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Kyoto's cuts were…
a) voluntary  b) mandatory for developed countries  c) for developing countries only
**Answer: b) mandatory for developed countries**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Kyoto required at least 5% below… levels
a) 2005  b) 1990  c) 2015
**Answer: b) 1990**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Kyoto entered into force on…
a) 16 February 2005  b) 1 January 1997  c) 12 December 2015
**Answer: a) 16 February 2005**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Why did global emissions still rise under Kyoto?
a) The USA never ratified and big developing emitters had no duty  b) Nobody cut anything  c) The treaty was secret
**Answer: a) The USA never ratified and big developing emitters had no duty**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Paris works by…
a) binding orders  b) voluntary national targets with regular reporting  c) fines
**Answer: b) voluntary national targets with regular reporting**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Carbon neutrality means emissions are…
a) ignored  b) fully offset  c) doubled
**Answer: b) fully offset**
