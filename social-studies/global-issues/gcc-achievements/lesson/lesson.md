## Sub lesson 1 — Gcc Achievements

Saudi Arabia Grade 8 Social Studies (printed pp. 189–190), teaches: The Council's achievements — political (Arab and Islamic solidarity, constant support of the Palestinian cause in all international forums, support of Iraq's and Yemen's unity and sovereignty), military and security (the unified Peninsula Shield force, the GCC joint defence agreement, cooperation against terrorism and drugs, criminal investigation and traffic), economic (free movement of national goods among the member states), and social (health-protection systems, treating GCC citizens like nationals in public hospitals and health centres, equal treatment of pupils in education, cooperation in media, awareness, youth and sport), with the Saudi–Emirati Coordination Council as a leading model of bilateral cooperation. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Saudi Arabia's way — the book's own teaching way

Saudi Arabia's own way: the Grade 8 book places Saudi Arabia inside the Gulf Cooperation Council — its organs and goals, its achievements and its challenges — then teaches systems and regulations (how a system is made, their kinds and their benefits), and ends with planning priorities, time management and evaluating plans, and hobbies including the old folk games. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
