# Hands-on — Gcc Achievements

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: the Council's achievements — political (Arab and Islamic solidarity, constant support of the Palestinian cause in all international forums, support of Iraq's and Yemen's unity and sovereignty), military and security (the unified Peninsula Shield force, the GCC joint defence agreement, cooperation against terrorism and drugs, criminal investigation and traffic), economic (free movement of national goods among the member states), and social (health-protection systems, treating GCC citizens like nationals in public hospitals and health centres, equal treatment of pupils in education, cooperation in media, awareness, youth and sport), with the Saudi–Emirati Coordination Council as a leading model of bilateral cooperation. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Saudi Arabia's way
Say how Saudi Arabia's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
