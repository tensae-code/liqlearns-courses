# Consequences — poles, ecosystems, people — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why do the poles matter to every coast?
**Answer:** Their ice holds sea level; melting it raises seas everywhere (and their white surface helps cool the planet).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Through what two channels do species changes hit human well-being?
**Answer:** Disease and food supply.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What happens in dry high-latitude forests?
**Answer:** Wildfires spread.
