# Consequences — poles, ecosystems, people — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Arctic sea ice hit record summer lows in…
a) 1990 and 1995  b) 2007 and 2012  c) 2020 and 2021
**Answer: b) 2007 and 2012**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Greenland's ice sheet is…
a) growing  b) shrinking  c) unchanged
**Answer: b) shrinking**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Extra CO₂ can let dominant species…
a) feed all others  b) eliminate others  c) stop growing
**Answer: b) eliminate others**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Crop yields may fall in…
a) tropical developing regions  b) high latitudes  c) nowhere
**Answer: a) tropical developing regions**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The ice-core CO₂ range over 800,000 years was…
a) 180–300 ppm  b) 400–500 ppm  c) 50–100 ppm
**Answer: a) 180–300 ppm**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** The overall verdict is…
a) more winners than losers  b) more losers than winners  c) no change
**Answer: b) more losers than winners**
