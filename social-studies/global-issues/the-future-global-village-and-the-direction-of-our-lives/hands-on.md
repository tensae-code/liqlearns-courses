# Hands-on — The Future Global Village and the Direction of Our Lives

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: IX.주제5 미래 지구촌과 삶의 방향, IX.03 미래 지구촌의 모습과 우리의 삶, IX.3 지구촌의 미래와 우리의 삶, IX.3 미래 지구촌의 모습과 내 삶의 방향, IX.05 미래의 지구촌 모습과 삶의 방향은? — teaches: the future global village and the direction of our lives in it; the face of the future global village and our lives in it; the future of the global village and the attitude we prepare for the future society; the face of the future global village and the direction of my own life; the future global village's face and the direction of our lives. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
