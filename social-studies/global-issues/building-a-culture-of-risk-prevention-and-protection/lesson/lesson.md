## Sub lesson 1 — Building a culture of risk — prevention and protection

Haiti's Grade 7 risk sequence closes by building a culture of risk: the national risk plan (PNGRD 2019-2030), the citizen gestures of prevention and protection, and posters and safety icons made by the pupils for their own school — knowing the risks ends in acting on them.

## Sub lesson 2 — Haiti's way — posters and safety icons for your own school

This is a teacher sequence, written for the teacher, not handed to the pupil. In Haiti's Projet Nectar sequence the last step of risk geography is public: what the class has surveyed, mapped and historicised is turned outward into prevention — a poster on a school wall is the sequence's final map.

Haiti's own way (teacher sequence — evidence): Risk is taught as geography first: pupils survey and map the hazards of their own locality, build Haiti's vulnerability factor by factor (anarchic urbanisation, deforestation, waste, poverty), read the country's disaster history from Quisqueya to the 2010 earthquake, and finish by making prevention posters and safety icons for their own school from the national risk plan (PNGRD 2019-2030). This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
