# Hands-on — Building a Culture of Risk — Prevention and Protection

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Make the poster
Design a prevention poster for your own school: one risk from the lesson, one clear picture, one short line telling people what to do.

## Teach the gesture
Pick one protection gesture from your poster and teach it to your companion, step by step, until they can do it back to you.
