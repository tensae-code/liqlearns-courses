# Video script — Building a Culture of Risk — Prevention and Protection

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Haiti's Grade 7 risk sequence closes by building a culture of risk: the national risk plan (PNGRD 2019-2030), the citizen gestures of prevention and protection, and posters and safety icons made by the pupils for their own school — knowing the risks ends in acting on them.

This is a teacher sequence, written for the teacher, not handed to the pupil. In Haiti's Projet Nectar sequence the last step of risk geography is public: what the class has surveyed, mapped and historicised is turned outward into prevention — a poster on a school wall is the sequence's final map.
