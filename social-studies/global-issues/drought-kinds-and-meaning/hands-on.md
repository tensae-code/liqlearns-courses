# Hands-on — Drought Kinds And Meaning

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Lesson 5: Drought (خشکسالی) — where 2,000 mm of rain is normal, 1,000 mm brings drought and some plant species dry out, while the same 1,000 mm is more than the usual rain of wet coasts; agricultural drought: the rain not below the region's average, yet not matching the different water needs of the plants.
