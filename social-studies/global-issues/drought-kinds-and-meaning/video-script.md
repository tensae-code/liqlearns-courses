Iran's book, the *Iran Grade 12 Geography (3)* (printed p. 98), teaches: Lesson 5: Drought (خشکسالی) — where 2,000 mm of rain is normal, 1,000 mm brings drought and some plant species dry out, while the same 1,000 mm is more than the usual rain of wet coasts; agricultural drought: the rain not below the region's average, yet not matching the different water needs of the plants.

Iran's own way (Iran Grade 12 Geography (3)): 'applied' geography organised as three problems of Iranian life — settlements, transport, and natural hazards — each taken in two lessons (what it is, then how to manage it) with a geographical-skills section (GIS, time zones, remote sensing) closing each chapter.
