# Video script — International Society What It is

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

South Korea's Grade 8 Bisang Social ②, printed p. 104, teaches: VI.1 국제 사회의 이해 — teaches: what international society is — its actors and why states must cooperate in it.

South Korea's own way: the middle-school Social Studies ② volume opens with civics, not geography — human rights and the Constitution first, then the three state institutions one by one, and the full text of the Constitution of the Republic of Korea printed as an appendix for pupils to read; it closes with global-citizenship units (global problems, reducing inter-regional inequality).
