# Video script — The Distribution of Disasters in Indonesia

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Indonesia's book teaches: Bab 4 I.D Persebaran bencana di Indonesia (earthquake, volcano, tsunami, flood, drought, landslide, forest fire, whirlwind) (Geografi untuk SMA Kelas XI, Grade 11, printed p. 186).

Indonesia's own way (Grade 11): the whole book is organised around Indonesia itself — its strategic position first, then its own biodiversity (Wallace/Wallacea), its own environment-and-population problems, and its own disaster map; every concept is taught through Indonesian examples, maps and infographics, with a chapter-end knowledge assessment (Asesmen) per chapter.
