# Video script — Caribbean Neighbours Working Together

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Jamaica's Grade 6 Exploratory Core guide (Social Studies Term 3 Unit 4, printed p. 218) closes the Grade 6 year on cooperation: how we benefit from cooperating with our Caribbean neighbours. Jamaica is taught as one country among Caribbean neighbours, gaining from working with them. This is a curriculum guide, written for the teacher, not handed to the pupil: it is cited here as curriculum-guide evidence.

Jamaica's own way (Grade 6 Exploratory Core, curriculum guide — evidence): the Grade 6 Social Studies year is built as three paired terms — culture and independence first, then the physical world (mountains, landmasses and water bodies), then climate, Earth movements and the atmosphere, closing on Caribbean cooperation. That evidence is stocked here as the way Jamaica teaches this thing; it does not change the acceptance count above.
