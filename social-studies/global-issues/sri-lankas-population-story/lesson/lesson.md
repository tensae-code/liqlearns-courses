# Sri Lanka's Population Story

## Do it first

Before you read on, think of one case of this lesson's subject from your own life or town and say what you already know about it. Hold your answer — the lesson takes its subject in three parts, in order.

In Sri Lanka's book (Grade 11 Social Education and History 2000), **Ch. 3 Sri Lanka's population** — teaches: Sri Lanka's censuses 1871–1998 (2.9 million to 18.7 million; birth rate 28.5 to 17.9 per thousand; death rate 20.5 to 6.1), density rising from 37 to 305 people per square kilometre, and the doubling time shrinking from 55 to 31 years This lesson teaches that placement in the course's own words.
