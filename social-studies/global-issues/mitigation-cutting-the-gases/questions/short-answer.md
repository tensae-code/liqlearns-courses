# Mitigation — cutting the gases — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** State the response logic in one line.
**Answer:** Adaptation handles impacts; mitigation handles causes; an effective regime needs both.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the three sinks.
**Answer:** Oceans, forests and soil.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Why is mitigation without adaptation not enough?
**Answer:** People would be abandoned to warming already locked in.
