# Mitigation — cutting the gases — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Mitigation works on…
a) impacts only  b) emissions and sinks  c) weather forecasts
**Answer: b) emissions and sinks**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The sinks that store carbon include…
a) oceans, forests and soil  b) cities and roads  c) clouds only
**Answer: a) oceans, forests and soil**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The largest emission source to cut is…
a) fossil fuels for electricity, heat and transport  b) cooking fires only  c) breathing
**Answer: a) fossil fuels for electricity, heat and transport**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Adaptation handles…
a) causes  b) impacts  c) neither
**Answer: b) impacts**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** An effective regime needs…
a) mitigation only  b) adaptation only  c) both
**Answer: c) both**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Growing sink capacity means…
a) burning more  b) storing more carbon in forests, soil and oceans  c) building reservoirs only
**Answer: b) storing more carbon in forests, soil and oceans**
