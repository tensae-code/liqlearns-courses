# Video script — Mitigation — cutting the gases

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Mitigation avoids and reduces emissions of heat-trapping gases, and enhances the sinks that store them — oceans, forests and soil. Its main front is cutting fossil-fuel burning for electricity, heat and transport, the largest source of the gases. Every tonne not emitted is warming that never happens; every hectare of forest kept or planted is carbon held out of the sky. The unit's response logic is simple and complete: adaptation handles impacts, mitigation handles causes, and an effective regime needs both. Mitigation without adaptation abandons people to the warming already locked in; adaptation without mitigation chases a target that keeps moving as emissions grow. The two strategies from the conventions — the pair you met in the adaptation lesson — are partners, not rivals.
