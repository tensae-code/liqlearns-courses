# Video script — Biodiversity — the variety of life, under threat

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Biodiversity embraces all variety of life — genes, species, organisms and communities, from the smallest ecosystem to the whole biosphere. The variety is not decoration: genes carry the options a species needs when conditions change, species hold ecosystems together, and communities are the living fabric whole regions depend on. Despite international agreements — the Biodiversity Convention among them — ecosystem degradation and species loss continue rapidly. The book attributes the failure to missing political will and financial commitment at the scale the problem needs. A signed agreement is a promise; biodiversity keeps the score of whether the promise was funded and enforced.
