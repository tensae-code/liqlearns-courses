# Biodiversity — the variety of life, under threat — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Biodiversity includes variety of…
a) genes, species, organisms and communities  b) rocks and soils  c) clouds
**Answer: a) genes, species, organisms and communities**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Its range runs from the smallest ecosystem to the…
a) village  b) biosphere  c) continent
**Answer: b) biosphere**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Genes matter because they carry…
a) options for when conditions change  b) colour only  c) nothing
**Answer: a) options for when conditions change**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Species loss today is…
a) stopped  b) continuing rapidly  c) reversing everywhere
**Answer: b) continuing rapidly**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The book blames the failure on missing…
a) science  b) political will and financial commitment  c) species
**Answer: b) political will and financial commitment**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** One agreement named is the…
a) Biodiversity Convention  b) Montreal Protocol  c) Kyoto Protocol
**Answer: a) Biodiversity Convention**
