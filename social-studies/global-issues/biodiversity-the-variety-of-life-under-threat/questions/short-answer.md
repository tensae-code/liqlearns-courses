# Biodiversity — the variety of life, under threat — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the four levels of variety.
**Answer:** Genes, species, organisms and communities.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why does loss continue despite agreements?
**Answer:** Political will and financial commitment are missing at the needed scale.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the largest scale biodiversity reaches?
**Answer:** The biosphere.
