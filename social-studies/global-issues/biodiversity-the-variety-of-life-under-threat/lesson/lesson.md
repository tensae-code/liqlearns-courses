# Biodiversity — the variety of life, under threat

## Do it first

Count the different living things you can see from one spot — then imagine the differences inside them that you cannot see. Both counts are biodiversity.

## Sub lesson 1 — Variety at every scale

Biodiversity embraces all variety of life — genes, species, organisms and communities, from the smallest ecosystem to the whole biosphere. The variety is not decoration: genes carry the options a species needs when conditions change, species hold ecosystems together, and communities are the living fabric whole regions depend on.

## Sub lesson 2 — Agreements without enough will

Despite international agreements — the Biodiversity Convention among them — ecosystem degradation and species loss continue rapidly. The book attributes the failure to missing political will and financial commitment at the scale the problem needs. A signed agreement is a promise; biodiversity keeps the score of whether the promise was funded and enforced.
