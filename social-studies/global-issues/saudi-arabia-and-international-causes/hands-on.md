# Hands-on — Saudi Arabia and International Causes

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: the Non-Aligned Movement's rise after the Second World War between the Warsaw and Atlantic pacts (Bandung 1374H/1955, Belgrade) as background to the Kingdom's international stance; the war on terrorism — Islam as the religion of moderation, neither excess leading to extremism nor neglect leading to dissolution, the extremist groups of past and present, and the Islamic Military Counter Terrorism Coalition announced by Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman in 1437H/2015 of forty-one states coordinating in the intellectual, media, counter-financing and military fields, with the Etidal centre (prevention, confrontation, awareness, partnership); and the King Salman Humanitarian Aid and Relief Centre, founded by the King's direction on 27 Rajab 1436H/2015 as an international centre for relief — aiding the needy and the stricken anywhere in the world through UN and non-profit organisations, across food security, camps, shelter, health, education, water and protection. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
