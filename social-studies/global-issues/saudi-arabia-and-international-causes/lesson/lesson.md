## Sub lesson 1 — Saudi Arabia and International Causes

Saudi Arabia Grade 10 Social Studies (Secondary Tracks, First Common Year) (printed pp. 44–49), teaches: The Non-Aligned Movement's rise after the Second World War between the Warsaw and Atlantic pacts (Bandung 1374H/1955, Belgrade) as background to the Kingdom's international stance; the war on terrorism — Islam as the religion of moderation, neither excess leading to extremism nor neglect leading to dissolution, the extremist groups of past and present, and the Islamic Military Counter Terrorism Coalition announced by Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman in 1437H/2015 of forty-one states coordinating in the intellectual, media, counter-financing and military fields, with the Etidal centre (prevention, confrontation, awareness, partnership); and the King Salman Humanitarian Aid and Relief Centre, founded by the King's direction on 27 Rajab 1436H/2015 as an international centre for relief — aiding the needy and the stricken anywhere in the world through UN and non-profit organisations, across food security, camps, shelter, health, education, water and protection. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Saudi Arabia's way — the book's own teaching way

Saudi Arabia's own way: the Grade 10 Tracks book starts from the components that make a state strong and influential and Saudi Arabia's own foundations and components, then follows the Kingdom's work for Arab, Islamic and international causes, and surveys the organisations it belongs to — the European Union as a model of integration, the League of Arab States, the Muslim World League, the Organisation of Islamic Cooperation, OPEC, the United Nations and the G20.
