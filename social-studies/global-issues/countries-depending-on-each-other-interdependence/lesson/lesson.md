## Sub lesson 2 — Trinidad and Tobago's way — the book's own teaching way

Trinidad and Tobago's own way: one "My Country" theme for the whole year — the multicultural society is taught as "Different But The Same": each ancestral people's contributions first, then the FUSION of food, music and dance into forms unique to Trinidad and Tobago, then the emblems, pledge and festival calendar that unite the same peoples.
