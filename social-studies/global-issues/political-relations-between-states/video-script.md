# Video script — Political Relations Between States

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Palestine Grade 5 Social Studies Part 2, printed p. 71, teaches: political relations are all forms of interaction between states to achieve common interests such as security, stability and preventing wars; their visible forms are signing agreements, political meetings and exchanging embassies between states.

Palestine's own way: civics is taught as relations — the child's social, cultural and political relations are four faces of one human-relations unit — and the constitution is not abstract: fifth-graders read three named articles of Palestine's own Basic Law and finish by drafting a constitution for their own classroom.
