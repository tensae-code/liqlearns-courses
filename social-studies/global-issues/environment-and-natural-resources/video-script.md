# Video script — Environment And Natural Resources

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Vietnam's book, the *Vietnam Grade 10 Địa lí 10* (printed pp. 108–110), teaches: Bài 39: Environment (natural, social and man-made) and natural resources — their meaning and role in human life and development.

Vietnam's own way (Vietnam Grade 10 Địa lí 10): a single Grade 10 course that runs the whole discipline — map craft, the Earth's spheres, then economic geography sector by sector — with Vietnam itself as the standing worked example (its power-plant and rice maps, the Red River's regime at Hanoi, Hanoi's climate chart, Vietnam's GDP structure, its internet and tourism figures) inside world-scale treatment.
