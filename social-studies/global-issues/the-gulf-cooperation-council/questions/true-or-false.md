# The Gulf Cooperation Council — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: The GCC — six member states, founded 25 May 1981 in Abu Dhabi, headquarters in Riyadh — and why the Gulf states cooperate.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson's second part teaches: The GCC states' joint security and defence cooperation, the Peninsula Shield force.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** This lesson's third part teaches: GCC cultural and educational cooperation — the Arab Bureau of Education for the Gulf States (Riyadh) and the Joint Programme Production Institution (Kuwait).
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** This lesson's fourth part teaches: Where the GCC states aspire to go, and Kuwait's own national vision, New Kuwait 2035.
**Answer: True**

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** This lesson stands on a teacher guide, with no student book.
**Answer: False — it stands on Kuwait's student book(s), named in lesson/sources.md.**
