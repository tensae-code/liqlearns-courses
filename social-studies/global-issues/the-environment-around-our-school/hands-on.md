# Hands-on — The Environment Around Our School

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Ibidukikije: the environment, the environment seen at school and in the area around the school (Unit 7); Ibidukikije biri mu gace ishuri riherereyemo: the environment in the area where the school is, incl. planting trees and flowers (Unit 7). Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Rwanda's way
Say how Rwanda's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
