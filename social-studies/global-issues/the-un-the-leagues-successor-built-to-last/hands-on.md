# Hands-on — The UN — the League's successor, built to last

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Build the chart
On a large sheet, draw the four organs as four rooms of one house. In each room write who sits there and what happens there. Hang it as the class's UN chart.

## The stronger house
Take your League weaknesses list from the earlier lesson. For each weakness, find the UN's answer in this lesson — or mark it honestly: still weak.
