# Video script — The UN — the League's successor, built to last

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style, in the animated-map spirit: the map opens, peoples and armies move, capitals rise, routes light up. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The map opens in 1945. Ruins smoke from Europe to the Pacific. Delegates gather; a Charter is signed.
A new building rises — this time with a strong room at its centre. Flags multiply at its door: 193 of them.
Inside, the camera visits four rooms: the great hall where all speak; the small council that can act; the secretariat's humming offices; the quiet court where states argue with documents, not divisions.
One flag is Ethiopia's — a founding member now, in the house its emperor once begged in vain.
