# The UN — the League's successor, built to last — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** The UN succeeded the League of Nations.
**Answer: True** — established 1945.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) ** The League of Nations was founded in 1926.
**Answer: False** — founded 1919 — the 1926 dating in §5.1 is the book's error, taught corrected.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) ** Every member state has one vote in the General Assembly.
**Answer: True** — the great hall of the house.
