# Lesson plan — The gap between rich and poor countries

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Global Issues · **Lesson:** The gap between rich and poor countries
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 5 — Issues in Sustainable Development III: Challenges of Economic Development (Grade 12 Geography) (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The rich–poor gap between countries stands at its highest in decades — and geography measures it with per-capita GDP, trade balances, the Gini coefficient and its Lorenz curve, and the McLoone Index.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the lessons before it in Global Issues, and on the earlier-grade lessons of this title.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of ..." in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.
