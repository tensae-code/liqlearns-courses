# The gap between rich and poor countries — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Through what two lenses is the gap conceptualised?
**Answer:** Per-capita GDP and trade balances.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the Lorenz curve show?
**Answer:** Actual income distribution bowing away from the line of perfect equality — the bow's size is the inequality.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the two measurement instruments.
**Answer:** The Gini coefficient (with the Lorenz curve) and the McLoone Index.
