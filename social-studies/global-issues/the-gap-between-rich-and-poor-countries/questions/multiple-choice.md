# The gap between rich and poor countries — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The rich–poor gap stands at…
a) its lowest ever  b) its highest in decades  c) zero
**Answer: b) its highest in decades**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The gap is conceptualised through…
a) per-capita GDP and trade balances  b) rainfall  c) population alone
**Answer: a) per-capita GDP and trade balances**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Trade imbalance's causes and effects are…
a) ignored  b) traced in the section  c) unknowable
**Answer: b) traced in the section**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The Gini coefficient pairs with the…
a) Lorenz curve  b) climate graph  c) population pyramid
**Answer: a) Lorenz curve**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** On the Lorenz diagram, a bigger bow means…
a) more equality  b) more inequality  c) more people
**Answer: b) more inequality**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** The second named index is the…
a) McLoone Index  b) Richter scale  c) HDI only
**Answer: a) McLoone Index**
