The rich–poor gap between countries stands at its highest in decades — and geography measures it with per-capita GDP, trade balances, the Gini coefficient and its Lorenz curve, and the McLoone Index. First, the gap and its causes: Economic inequality between countries and regions — the rich–poor gap — stands at its highest in decades. Then, measuring it — gini, lorenz, mcloone: Inequality has named instruments. Hold those two parts together and you hold the lesson.
