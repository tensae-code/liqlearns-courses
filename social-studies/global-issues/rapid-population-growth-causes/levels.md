# Rapid Population Growth — Causes — sub lessons and depth

The book's own run: the definition, Ethiopia's sorted drivers, then the world list. Grade 7 depth throughout this lesson.

## Sub lesson 1 — What rapid growth means
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: define rapid growth as population beyond what resources can carry and beyond the jobs the economy can create.

## Sub lesson 2 — Ethiopia's drivers, sorted
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: place the cultural drivers (honour, disdain, 'a child grows by its luck'), the economic drivers (income, old-age security) and the political driver (numbers as survival and supremacy).

## Sub lesson 3 — The world list
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: name the world drivers, including high child mortality and unexpanded birth-control services.
