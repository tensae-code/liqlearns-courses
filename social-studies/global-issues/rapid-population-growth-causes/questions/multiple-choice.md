# Rapid Population Growth — Causes — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Rapid population growth means population beyond —
a) one million people  b) what resources can carry and what jobs the economy can create  c) the size of the capital city
**Answer: b) what resources can carry and what jobs the economy can create** — that is the book's definition.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** 'A child grows by its luck' is which kind of driver?
a) Economic  b) Political  c) Cultural
**Answer: c) Cultural** — the book sorts the saying among the cultural drivers.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Children as old-age security is which kind of driver?
a) Economic  b) Cultural  c) Political
**Answer: a) Economic** — old-age security is about support — an economic driver.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Which is on the book's world list of drivers?
a) High child mortality  b) Cheap mobile phones  c) New highways
**Answer: a) High child mortality** — high child mortality is on the world list.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Numbers as a group's survival and supremacy guarantee is —
a) a cultural driver  b) a political driver  c) not a driver
**Answer: b) a political driver** — the book sorts it as political.
