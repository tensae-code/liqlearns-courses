# Rapid Population Growth — Causes — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Sort these three drivers: children as income; numbers as a group's supremacy guarantee; honour for mothers of many children.
**Answer:** Children as income — economic. Numbers as supremacy guarantee — political. Honour for mothers of many children — cultural.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain how high child mortality can drive rapid growth.
**Answer:** Where many children die young, families have more children so that some survive — the book lists high child mortality among the world drivers of rapid growth.
