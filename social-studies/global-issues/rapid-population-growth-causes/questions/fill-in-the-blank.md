# Rapid Population Growth — Causes — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Rapid growth is population beyond what natural resources can ______ and beyond the jobs the economy can create.
**Answer: carry.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Honour for mothers of many children is a ______ driver.
**Answer: cultural.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Women's unexpanded schooling and ______ chances are on the world list.
**Answer: work.**
