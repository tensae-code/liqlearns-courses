# Rapid Population Growth — Causes — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Rapid growth is a fruit of development, so it is not a problem.
**Answer: False** — the chapter's review stock keys this false — rapid growth is defined against resources and jobs.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Disdain for a mother of one or two children is a cultural driver in the book.
**Answer: True** — it is listed among the cultural drivers.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** High child mortality lowers population growth, so it cannot be a driver of rapid growth.
**Answer: False** — the book lists high child mortality among the world drivers — families have more children where many die.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Unexpanded birth-control services appear on the world list.
**Answer: True** — they do.
