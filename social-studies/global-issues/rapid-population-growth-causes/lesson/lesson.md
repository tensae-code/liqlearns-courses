# Rapid Population Growth — Causes

## Do it first

Ask at home, or recall: why do families in your place have the number of children they have? Write down every reason you hear — honour, help, security, custom — before the book sorts them.

## Sub lesson 1 — What rapid growth means

**Rapid population growth** is population **beyond what natural resources can carry and beyond the jobs the economy can create**. Growth itself is not the problem named here — growth outrunning resources and jobs is.

## Sub lesson 2 — Ethiopia's drivers, sorted

The book sorts Ethiopia's drivers three ways. **Cultural:** honour for mothers of many children, disdain for the childless or the one-or-two-child mother, and the saying **'a child grows by its luck'**. **Economic:** children as income, and children as old-age security. **Political:** numbers counted as a group's survival and supremacy guarantee.

## Sub lesson 3 — The world list

For the world the book lists: **low education, early marriage age, children as labour, no pension security, women's unexpanded schooling and work chances, religious and cultural attitudes, unexpanded birth-control services, and high child mortality**. Notice how many of the world's drivers are the Ethiopian drivers seen wider — and how high child mortality drives growth: where many children die, families have more.

## Say it to your companion

Sort six drivers your companion names into cultural, economic or political. Then say the definition of rapid growth together, word for word.
