# Video script — Rapid Population Growth — Causes

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A population grows fast — faster than the land can carry.

Faster than the jobs the economy can make.

Why? Honour for many children. Children as income.

Children as security for old age. Numbers as strength.

And in the world: early marriage, little schooling, many children dying young.

Name the driver, and you can answer it.
