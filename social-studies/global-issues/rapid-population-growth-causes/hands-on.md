# Hands-on — Rapid Population Growth — Causes

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Driver interviews
Ask two elders, separately, why families in their youth had many children. Write each reason down in their words, then sort every reason into the lesson's columns. Bring back one reason the book does not list, if you find one, marked as yours.

## Column building
On a big sheet, draw the cultural, economic and political columns. In a group, fill each column from the lesson first, then add examples from your own place under each — keeping the book's examples and yours in different colours or marks.
