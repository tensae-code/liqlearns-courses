# Hands-on — Mongolia And the Worlds Non State Actors

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Topic 6.2 "Mongolia and the non-state actors of international relations"
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Mongolia's own framework — since 1990 an open, peace-loving, independent and multi-pillared foreign policy; the Foreign Policy Concept's six pillar directions, diagrammed: balanced friendly relations with the two neighbours; active participation in Asia-Pacific political and economic integration; friendly relations with the developed countries of West and East; realistic cooperation with developing countries toward shared goals; cooperation with the UN and its specialised agencies, the IMF, the World Bank and the ADB; and friendly relations with the post-socialist and CIS countries — the fourth direction being the one that runs through non-state actors (APEC, ASEAN channels, the Non-Aligned Movement, the UN system); and the Concept's standing commitments — strengthening democracy; fighting terrorism and organised crime together with others; joining UN peacekeeping; adapting to climate change and guarding against disaster and epidemic — as the work a small state does inside the world's common institutions. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
