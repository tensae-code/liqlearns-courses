# Elections in the Municipality

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: how local elections work — voters, candidates, and choosing the mayor and council — as Greece's books teach it. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Elections in the municipality — Grade 6

Greece Grade 6 Koinoniki kai Politiki Agogi ST' Dimotikou (printed p. 33), teaches: How local elections work — voters, candidates, and choosing the mayor and council. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Elections in the municipality — Grade 3

Greece Grade 3 Meleti Perivallontos G' Dimotikou (printed p. 24), teaches: How the dimos elects its mayor and council. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 3 — Greece's way — the book's own teaching way

Greece's own way: two Aristophanes characters (Dikaiopolis and Eirini, "Justice-City" and "Peace") guide the pupil outward from the family to the state, the EU and the world; Greece's own municipalities, constitution and EU membership are the worked examples. Grade 3's opening unit is the primary civics core of the whole EVS series — living together needs rules, the community is cared for, and the child learns who decides by following municipal elections in their own dimos. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
