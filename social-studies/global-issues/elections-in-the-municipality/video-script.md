# Video script — Elections in the Municipality

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Greece Grade 6 Koinoniki kai Politiki Agogi ST' Dimotikou (printed p. 33), teaches: How local elections work — voters, candidates, and choosing the mayor and council.

Greece Grade 3 Meleti Perivallontos G' Dimotikou (printed p. 24), teaches: How the dimos elects its mayor and council.

Greece's own way: two Aristophanes characters (Dikaiopolis and Eirini, "Justice-City" and "Peace") guide the pupil outward from the family to the state, the EU and the world; Greece's own municipalities, constitution and EU membership are the worked examples. Grade 3's opening unit is the primary civics core of the whole EVS series — living together needs rules, the community is cared for, and the child learns who decides by following municipal elections in their own dimos.
