# Video script — The Paris Agreement How the World Decides on Climate

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Costa Rica Grade 8 CONED Antología 8 (printed p. 83), teaches: The paris agreement (2015) point by point — the 2 °c / 1.5 °c goal, voluntary national pledges from 186 countries, five-year review and verification, common-but-differentiated responsibilities, the green fund for the south, and the countries that did not sign.

Costa Rica's own way: a geography-and-climate anthology that closes by lifting the child's own country into world governance — international treaties are defined first, then the Paris Agreement is taught point by point (the 2 °C goal, national pledges, five-year review, the Green Fund) with Costa Rica's own payment-for-environmental-services programme as the home case.
