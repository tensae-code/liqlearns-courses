# Hands-on — The Paris Agreement How the World Decides on Climate

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: the Paris Agreement (2015) point by point — the 2 °C / 1.5 °C goal, voluntary national pledges from 186 countries, five-year review and verification, common-but-differentiated responsibilities, the Green Fund for the South, and the countries that did not sign. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
