## Sub lesson 1 — The Paris Agreement How the World Decides on Climate

Costa Rica Grade 8 CONED Antología 8 (printed p. 83), teaches: the Paris Agreement (2015) point by point — the 2 °C / 1.5 °C goal, voluntary national pledges from 186 countries, five-year review and verification, common-but-differentiated responsibilities, the Green Fund for the South, and the countries that did not sign. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Costa Rica's way — the book's own teaching way

Costa Rica's own way: a geography-and-climate anthology that closes by lifting the child's own country into world governance — international treaties are defined first, then the Paris Agreement is taught point by point (the 2 °C goal, national pledges, five-year review, the Green Fund) with Costa Rica's own payment-for-environmental-services programme as the home case. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
