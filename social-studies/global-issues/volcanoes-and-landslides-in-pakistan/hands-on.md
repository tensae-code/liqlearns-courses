# Hands-on — Volcanoes And Landslides In Pakistan

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Volcanism (and why Pakistan has no active volcano hazard) and landslides: causes, damage and protective measures; landslides blocking roads and rivers in Pakistan's northern mountains. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Pakistan's way
Say how Pakistan's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
