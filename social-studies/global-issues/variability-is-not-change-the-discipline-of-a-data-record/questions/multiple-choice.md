# Variability is not change — the discipline of a data record — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Variability is…
a) a permanent shift  b) the natural wobble around a level  c) a single hot day only
**Answer: b) the natural wobble around a level**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** One failed rainy season proves…
a) climate change  b) nothing by itself about climate  c) that rain has ended
**Answer: b) nothing by itself about climate**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Climate change is a shift that persists for…
a) a week  b) a season  c) decades
**Answer: c) decades**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The drivers of climate change can be…
a) natural and/or anthropogenic  b) anthropogenic only  c) imaginary
**Answer: a) natural and/or anthropogenic**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The discipline of a data record means…
a) using the longest record and testing persistence  b) using the latest year only  c) ignoring old data
**Answer: a) using the longest record and testing persistence**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** The Grade 12 record shows about how much warming in a century?
a) 0.8 °C  b) 8 °C  c) 0.08 °C
**Answer: a) 0.8 °C**
