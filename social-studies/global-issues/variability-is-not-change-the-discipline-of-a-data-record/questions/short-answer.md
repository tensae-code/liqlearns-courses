# Variability is not change — the discipline of a data record — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In one line, separate variability from change.
**Answer:** Variability is wobble around a level; change is a persistent shift of the level itself.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What two questions do you ask of any climate claim?
**Answer:** How long is the record, and does the shift persist?

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What does 'anthropogenic' mean here?
**Answer:** Human-caused — from human activity.
