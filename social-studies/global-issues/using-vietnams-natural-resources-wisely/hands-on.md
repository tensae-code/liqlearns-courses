# Hands-on — Using Vietnams Natural Resources Wisely

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Bài 5: Using Vietnam's natural resources rationally and protecting the environment — degrading land (of over 33.1 million ha in 2024, 84.5% is farmland), biological resources in decline (forest area 1943–2024: the total recovered to 14.9 million ha but natural forest shrank as planted forest grew), falling and polluted water, air pollution (the fine-dust table), and the remedies. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
