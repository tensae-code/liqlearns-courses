Vietnam's book, the *Vietnam Grade 12 Địa lí 12* (printed pp. 28–32), teaches: Bài 5: Using Vietnam's natural resources rationally and protecting the environment — degrading land (of over 33.1 million ha in 2024, 84.5% is farmland), biological resources in decline (forest area 1943–2024: the total recovered to 14.9 million ha but natural forest shrank as planted forest grew), falling and polluted water, air pollution (the fine-dust table), and the remedies.

Vietnam's own way (Vietnam Grade 12 Địa lí 12): the school course ends with a full portrait of Vietnam itself — its monsoon nature and how it varies, its people, each economic sector, each of its regions, and its seas and islands — all carried on the newest statistics (2024 yearbook figures, and the 34-province map in force from 2025).
