**Acceptance count: taught in 3 countries (Cuba, Colombia, Costa Rica).**

Costa Rica's own way (Estudios Sociales CONED 8): a climate-and-water book — Economics enters as resource scarcity: water rationing stopping factories and housing in Costa Rica, the country's 98% renewable electricity mix, and carbon compensation flowing from developed to developing countries.

Colombia's own way (Colombia Grade 2 Escuela Nueva G2 Cartilla 2): work is taught historically — children compare their grandparents' trades with today's to see how inventions change jobs.

Cuba's own way (Cuba Grade 7 Geografía Física G7): Physical geography is taught sphere by sphere (lithosphere → atmosphere → hydrosphere → biosphere) but every sphere chapter closes on rational use and sustainability — wind energy, rational water use, soil protection — and Cuban evidence runs through it (Cuba's forest cover fell from ~95 % at the Spanish arrival to 16 % by 1959 and was recovered by policy).
