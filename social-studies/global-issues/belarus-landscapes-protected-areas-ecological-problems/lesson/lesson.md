# Belarus Landscapes, Protected Areas and Ecological Problems

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: Belarus's geographical landscapes, the development of its territory, its recreational resources, its nature-protection territories, and human impact on its environment, as Belarus's Grade 10 book teaches them. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Geographical landscapes, development of the territory and recreational resources

Theme III of Belarus's Grade 10 book (§§22–24) studies the country's geographical landscapes, how its territory was developed, and its recreational resources — the landscapes people rest and travel in.

## Sub lesson 2 — Nature-protection territories and human impact on the environment

Theme III §§25–26 follows with nature-protection territories — the parts of Belarus set aside for protection — and human impact on the environment: what people's activity does to the country's nature, and the ecological problems that follow.

Belarus's own way: the whole course is one country studied whole — position and the history of its own geographical research first (including Belarusian place-names), then nature component by component, then its five natural provinces, population, the economy sector by sector, Belarus in the world, and finally each of its six regions in turn; every paragraph opens with a "recall" rubric and closes with questions and tasks.
