## Sub lesson 2 — Pakistan's way — the book's own teaching way

Pakistan's own way (Pakistan Grade 4 SS4): one combined Social Studies book in Urdu where the Geography chapter (Ch.5, printed pp. 61–93) works entirely through Pakistan's own map, landforms, weather and census, with BOLTS as the map-elements mnemonic and "I have learnt!" / "Work for me to do" boxes closing every topic.

Pakistan's own way (Pakistan Grade 5 SS5): the Class 5 Geography chapter (Ch.5, printed pp. 51–85) deepens the Class 4 spiral — same BOLTS, landforms, weather and population topics, but now with scale types, latitude/longitude and time-zone arithmetic, worked population-density calculations, and graph-drawing exercises from real Pakistani city data. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
