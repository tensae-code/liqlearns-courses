South Korea's Grade 10 MiraeN Integrated Society, printed p. 246, teaches: VIII.주제5 남북 분단의 평화적 해결 — teaches: the peaceful resolution of the North–South division of Korea.

South Korea's own way: the high-school common subject 통합사회 (Integrated Society) is built around nine key questions asked from temporal, spatial, social and ethical perspectives at once; its civics core is Unit IV (human rights guaranteed by the constitution, law-abiding spirit and citizen participation) and Unit VI (justice and inequality), and its peace unit teaches the peaceful resolution of Korea's own division and of East Asia's history conflicts as the closing civics content.
