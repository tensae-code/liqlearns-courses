**Acceptance count: taught in 1 country (Guyana).**

Guyana's own way: the newest ministry series teaches citizenship from the child's own roles outward — family duties, community roles, youth responsibilities at home and across the Caribbean, then power, privilege and human rights in plain language, with Guyanese and Caribbean case studies (Annette Arjoon-Martins, Mia Mottley) and a value component woven into every unit.

Guyana's own way: the Grade 2 book of the new series starts citizenship at the child's own doorstep — the many communities a child belongs to at once, the councils that serve them, the peoples and festivals that fill them, then decisions, conflict and rights, all before any map or market is taught.

Guyana's own way: the secondary book teaches the same citizenship at national scale — CARICOM as the region's own institution, the family as society's oldest institution, women's equality written into the 1980 Constitution and the Acts of the 1990s, and the state answering pollution through the Environmental Protection Agency.
