# Hands-on — What Disasters Do

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Impacts of natural disasters: floods (Pakistan 1947, 1988, 2010, 2022), landslides blocking roads, earthquakes (Quetta 1935, Balakot/Mansehra 2005) and the effects of volcanism. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Pakistan's way
Say how Pakistan's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
