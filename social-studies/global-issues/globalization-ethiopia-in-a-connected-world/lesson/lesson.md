# Globalization — Ethiopia in a connected world

## Do it first

Phones assembled from three continents, coffee flown out overnight, students learning from teachers an ocean away — what single word names the web tying all of it together, and what does Ethiopia gain and risk inside it?

## Sub lesson 1 — What globalization is

Globalization is the growing interdependence of economies, populations and cultures through cross-border trade, capital flows and rapid technology spread — the world knitting itself into one connected system.

[Picture 24931 — Addis Ababa's skyline with the AU headquarters, trade and data lines radiating to other continents]

## Sub lesson 2 — What it brings

It brings access to markets and technology, cheaper and more varied goods through competition, and — for a late starter — the chance to jump straight to newer technology instead of climbing every older step.

## Sub lesson 3 — Ethiopia in the web — and its downsides

Ethiopia is presented as having drawn world-class investors and technology, registered rapid growth, and seen Addis Ababa flourish as the African Union's headquarters city. The downsides named are loss of cultural identity and exploitation of employees in foreign countries — both noted as live issues in Ethiopia too.
