# Globalization — Ethiopia in a connected world — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "What globalization is" in your own words.
**Answer:** Globalization is growing interdependence through trade, capital flows and technology spread.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "What it brings" in your own words.
**Answer:** Globalization brings market and technology access, cheaper varied goods, and a late starter's leap to newer technology.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Ethiopia in the web — and its downsides" in your own words.
**Answer:** Ethiopia has drawn investors and technology and hosts the AU headquarters in Addis Ababa; downsides include cultural loss and worker exploitation.
