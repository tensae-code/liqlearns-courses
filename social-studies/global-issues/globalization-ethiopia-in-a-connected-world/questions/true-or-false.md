# Globalization — Ethiopia in a connected world — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Globalization means countries trading only with neighbours.
**Answer: False — It is worldwide interdependence — cross-border trade, capital and technology, not neighbourhood trade alone.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Globalization makes goods dearer and less varied.
**Answer: False — Competition under globalization makes goods cheaper and MORE varied — the lesson lists both as gains.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The book names no downsides of globalization for Ethiopia.
**Answer: False — It names two: loss of cultural identity, and exploitation of employees in foreign countries.**
