# Globalization — Ethiopia in a connected world — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "What globalization is"?
a) Globalization is growing interdependence through trade, capital flows and technology spread.  b) Globalization brings market and technology access, cheaper varied goods, and a late starter's leap to newer technology.  c) Ethiopia has drawn investors and technology and hosts the AU headquarters in Addis Ababa; downsides include cultural loss and worker exploitation.
