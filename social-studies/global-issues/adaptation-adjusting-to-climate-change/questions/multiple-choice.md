# Adaptation — adjusting to climate change — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Adjusting to what is coming"?
a) Adaptation adjusts systems to actual or expected climate effects, because some change is already inevitable  b) Planned adaptation is policy; autonomous adaptation is households, farms and firms adjusting themselves  c) Mobility across space, storage across time, diversification across assets, communal pooling across households
**Answer: a) Adaptation adjusts systems to actual or expected climate effects, because some change is already inevitable**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Planned and autonomous"?
a) Planned adaptation is policy; autonomous adaptation is households, farms and firms adjusting themselves  b) Mobility across space, storage across time, diversification across assets, communal pooling across households  c) Adaptation adjusts systems to actual or expected climate effects, because some change is already inevitable
**Answer: a) Planned adaptation is policy; autonomous adaptation is households, farms and firms adjusting themselves**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Four strategy classes"?
a) Mobility across space, storage across time, diversification across assets, communal pooling across households  b) Adaptation adjusts systems to actual or expected climate effects, because some change is already inevitable  c) Planned adaptation is policy; autonomous adaptation is households, farms and firms adjusting themselves
**Answer: a) Mobility across space, storage across time, diversification across assets, communal pooling across households**
