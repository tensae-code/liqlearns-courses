# Adaptation — adjusting to climate change — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Adjusting to what is coming" in your own words.
**Answer:** Adaptation adjusts systems to actual or expected climate effects, because some change is already inevitable.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Planned and autonomous" in your own words.
**Answer:** Planned adaptation is policy; autonomous adaptation is households, farms and firms adjusting themselves.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Four strategy classes" in your own words.
**Answer:** Mobility across space, storage across time, diversification across assets, communal pooling across households.
