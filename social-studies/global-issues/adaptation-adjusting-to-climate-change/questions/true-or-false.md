# Adaptation — adjusting to climate change — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Adaptation adjusts systems to actual or expected climate effects, because some change is already inevitable.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Planned adaptation is policy; autonomous adaptation is households, farms and firms adjusting themselves.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Mobility across space, storage across time, diversification across assets, communal pooling across households.
**Answer: True**
