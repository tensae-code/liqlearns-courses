# Hands-on — Adaptation — adjusting to climate change

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Four panels
Draw four small panels — a herder moving cattle, a grain store, mixed crops, a village sharing a well — and label each with its strategy class and its geometry (space, time, spread, share).

## Plan one household
Write one farming household's adaptation plan using all four classes, one move per class, each move realistic for a household with almost no cash.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
