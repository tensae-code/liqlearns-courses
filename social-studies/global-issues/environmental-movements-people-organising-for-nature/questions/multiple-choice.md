# Environmental movements — people organising for nature — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** An environmental movement is…
a) a lone protester  b) organised collective action for the environment  c) a government office
**Answer: b) organised collective action for the environment**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Movements range from local campaigns to…
a) global ones  b) none  c) household ones only
**Answer: a) global ones**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Bright greens seek sustainability through…
a) better design, new technology and social innovation  b) ending all industry  c) prayer
**Answer: a) better design, new technology and social innovation**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Dark greens see the problems as…
a) a design flaw  b) inherent to industrial capitalist civilisation  c) imaginary
**Answer: b) inherent to industrial capitalist civilisation**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Dark greens demand…
a) new gadgets  b) radical political change  c) nothing
**Answer: b) radical political change**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** The section closes with movements in…
a) Ethiopia  b) Brazil  c) Japan
**Answer: a) Ethiopia**
