# Video script — Environmental movements — people organising for nature

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Environmental movements are organised collective action for the environment — people joining, persisting and pressing together rather than acting alone. The section builds the concept and classifies movement types, from local campaigns over a single forest or river to national and global movements over the climate itself. At one pole stand the 'bright greens': sustainability through better design, new technology and social innovation — the problem is real, and better tools can solve it. At the other stand the 'dark greens': environmental problems are inherent to industrial capitalist civilisation itself, demanding radical political change, not better gadgets. The section closes with environmental movements in Ethiopia, where community and national action — including great tree-planting campaigns — show the spectrum at work at home.
