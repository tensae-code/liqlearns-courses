**Acceptance count: taught in 2 countries (Bolivia, Egypt).**

Bolivia's own way (Grade 12, Sexto Año): The final year opens with the census itself — the censista's work as civic-geographic practice — and later teaches geopolitics critically: Haushofer and geopolitical thought, geographic scales, and natural resources as a product of human knowledge (Sunkel), alongside Bolivia's own resource politics: the Gas War, hydrocarbon nationalisation and the urea plant that industrialises gas.

Searchable tags: geopolitics and geopolitical thought, Bolivia, Ciencias Sociales, Texto de Aprendizaje, social studies, global issues

Egypt's own way (Grade 12): a full political-geography course to close school geography — the state first (definition, unitary/federal/confederal types, capitals chosen for stated reasons, the natural then the human components of a state's power, electoral geography), then borders taught with the border/frontier distinction and the Great Wall of China as the historical case, then blocs and alliances, ending with international relations and the new world order; activities, exam models and a maps appendix fill the back half of the book.
