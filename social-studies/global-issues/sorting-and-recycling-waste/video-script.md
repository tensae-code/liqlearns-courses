# Video script — Sorting and Recycling Waste

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Sweden's Grade 1 book ends its nature teaching at the pupil's own home: waste is sorted at the recycling station. The teacher guide's notes for the textbook's pages 60–61 name the sorts: paper, food scraps, plastic, metal, glass and batteries.

Sorting is taught as saving nature's resources — and so is saving electricity. The lesson joins the two: what the household throws away, sorted rightly, and what it uses, used carefully.
