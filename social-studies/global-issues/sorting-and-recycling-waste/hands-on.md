# Hands-on — Sorting and Recycling Waste

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Sort a day's waste
List one day's waste from your home and sort each item the Swedish way: paper, food scraps, plastic, metal, glass, batteries — or none of these.

## Save electricity for a day
Name three moments in a day when your home uses electricity and one way to save at each moment, as the lesson teaches.
