Iran's book, the *Iran Grade 12 Geography (3)* (printed p. 109), teaches: Lesson 6: Drought management — the chief strategies: saving water and avoiding wasteful use of the surface and ground waters we have, and raising irrigation efficiency in farming with pressurised irrigation methods (activity: research them and report their uses and advantages).

Iran's own way (Iran Grade 12 Geography (3)): 'applied' geography organised as three problems of Iranian life — settlements, transport, and natural hazards — each taken in two lessons (what it is, then how to manage it) with a geographical-skills section (GIS, time zones, remote sensing) closing each chapter.
