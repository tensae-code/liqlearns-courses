## Sub lesson 1 — Companies across borders — and the cities that steer world trade

Costa Rica's Grade 11 Porras y Gamboa workbook gives globalisation its organisers. In Unit 1 B (cont.) it teaches transnational companies and their power — firms that produce and sell across many countries at once — and global cities, the few great cities where international trade and company decisions are steered. The topic is worked in the workbook's fixed cycle of problem question, basic concepts, the learner's previous ideas, widening knowledge, applying it and exam preparation, with heavy map-work.

## Sub lesson 2 — Costa Rica's way — the workbook cycle and Costa Rica's own data

Costa Rica learns this inside its own book's way: a commercial workbook twin of the CONED 11 anthology — same ministry programme, but every topic is a fixed cycle (problem question, basic concepts, my previous ideas, widening my knowledge, I apply my knowledge, problem-based learning, exam preparation) with heavy map-work and Costa Rica-specific data: ICE internet-coverage map, services-export league table, Costa Rica's geo-strategic bridge position between North/South America and the Caribbean. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
