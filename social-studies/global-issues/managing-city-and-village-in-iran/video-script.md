# Video script — Managing City And Village In Iran

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Iran's book, the *Iran Grade 12 Geography (3)* (printed p. 35), teaches: Lesson 2: The bodies that manage city and village in Iran — the Ministry of Roads and Urban Development, the municipalities and the Islamic city councils for the cities; the dehyaris (دهیاری‌ها) and Islamic village councils for the villages; spatial planning (آمایش سرزمین) — ordering geographical space and distributing population in a balanced, even and logical way; Iran's hierarchy of spatial development planning, the comprehensive plan of the land (طرح جامع سرزمین) at the top of the pyramid.

Iran's own way (Iran Grade 12 Geography (3)): 'applied' geography organised as three problems of Iranian life — settlements, transport, and natural hazards — each taken in two lessons (what it is, then how to manage it) with a geographical-skills section (GIS, time zones, remote sensing) closing each chapter.
