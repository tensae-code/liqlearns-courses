# Hands-on — Managing City And Village In Iran

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Lesson 2: The bodies that manage city and village in Iran — the Ministry of Roads and Urban Development, the municipalities and the Islamic city councils for the cities; the dehyaris (دهیاری‌ها) and Islamic village councils for the villages; spatial planning (آمایش سرزمین) — ordering geographical space and distributing population in a balanced, even and logical way; Iran's hierarchy of spatial development planning, the comprehensive plan of the land (طرح جامع سرزمین) at the top of the pyramid. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
