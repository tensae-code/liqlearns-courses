# Video script — Climate change — what it is and what causes it

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Climate change is a persistent, decades-long shift in the mean state of the climate, from anthropogenic — human-caused — emissions. It is distinct from weather, the day-to-day mix: weather is what you dress for; climate is the wardrobe you own.

Carbon dioxide causes about 77% of the greenhouse effect, methane 14% and nitrous oxide 8% (IPCC, 2007). The largest US source is fossil fuels burnt for electricity, heat and transport, while deforestation contributes over 20% of harmful emissions in developing countries.

The IPCC projection: without curbing fossil emissions, mean temperatures rise 1.5–4°C by 2099. One degree was already reached by 2016 — the lower end of the range is not a distant scenario but a partly-visited one.
