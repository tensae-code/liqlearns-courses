# Climate change — what it is and what causes it — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Climate change is a persistent decades-long shift in the climate's mean state, distinct from weather.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** CO2 causes about 77% of the greenhouse effect, methane 14% and nitrous oxide 8%.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Without curbing emissions, mean temperatures rise 1.5–4°C by 2099; 1°C was reached by 2016.
**Answer: True**
