# Climate change — what it is and what causes it — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "A persistent shift, not weather"?
a) Climate change is a persistent decades-long shift in the climate's mean state, distinct from weather  b) CO2 causes about 77% of the greenhouse effect, methane 14% and nitrous oxide 8%  c) Without curbing emissions, mean temperatures rise 1.5–4°C by 2099; 1°C was reached by 2016
**Answer: a) Climate change is a persistent decades-long shift in the climate's mean state, distinct from weather**
