# Climate change — what it is and what causes it — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "A persistent shift, not weather" in your own words.
**Answer:** Climate change is a persistent decades-long shift in the climate's mean state, distinct from weather.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "The shares — CO2 77%" in your own words.
**Answer:** CO2 causes about 77% of the greenhouse effect, methane 14% and nitrous oxide 8%.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "The projection — 1.5 to 4 degrees" in your own words.
**Answer:** Without curbing emissions, mean temperatures rise 1.5–4°C by 2099; 1°C was reached by 2016.
