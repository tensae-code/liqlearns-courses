# Hands-on — Climate change — what it is and what causes it

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Small pie
Draw the three gases' shares as a small pie (77, 14, 8, with 1% rounding) and label the source each slice mostly comes from in the lesson's account.

## Weather or climate
Write six statements, three weather and three climate. Swap with a partner and classify each other's, using 'persistent, decades-long' as the test phrase.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
