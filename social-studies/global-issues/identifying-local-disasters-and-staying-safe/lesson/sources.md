**Acceptance count: taught in 1 country (Nepal).**

Nepal's own way (Nepal Grade 5 TG np-g5tg) (teacher guide — evidence): Guide to the new-curriculum Grade 5 book (companion to the trial-edition line, not to the older English np-g5): its "Hamro Prithvi" unit teaches the solar system and Earth's structure, then Nepal's natural form, climate, vegetation, population and social life, local disasters, and map-making — learners draw their own province's map showing their district and local level and study the Nepal map. Economic unit works from local economic activities and locally produced goods (and the markets that sell them) up to the country's major economic activities across Himalayan, Hilly and Terai regions. That book is a teacher guide, cited as evidence only; Nepal is counted in this lesson for its student book(s) named here, not for this guide.

Nepal's own way (Nepal Grade 6 np-g6): Grade 6 has a full "Hamro Prithvi" (Our Earth) unit — Earth's introduction, latitude/longitude, outer-layer structure, then Nepal itself: geographical description, climate, rivers/glaciers/lakes, natural vegetation, natural resources and map study, closing with disaster management; a population unit follows (demography, the census). Economic activities are organised by region and resource: agriculture and industry, services and trade, tourism, and economic resources (land and water; forest, minerals and people).

Nepal's own way (Nepal Grade 7 np-g7): Grade 7 Earth science is motion-first — Earth's daily and annual motion, surface form and the factors that change it, surface form's relation to human life — then Nepal's climate, the seven provinces of federal Nepal, climate change, disasters and map work; population is taught as structure, change and management.

Nepal's own way (Nepal Grade 7 TG np-g7tg) (teacher guide — evidence): The guide teaches the same Grade 7 course as np-g7 with globe-and-ball demonstrations of rotation/revolution, chart work on Nepal's demographic structure (age, sex, religion, caste, language distributions), and discussion-led lessons; its geography content mirrors the student book lesson for lesson, so placements repeat with guide page references. That book is a teacher guide, cited as evidence only; Nepal is counted in this lesson for its student book(s) named here, not for this guide.

Nepal's own way (Nepal Grade 8 TG np-g8tg) (teacher guide — evidence): Grade 8 economics is monetary — monetary transactions, Nepal's financial institutions, tourism, foreign trade and foreign employment; the Earth unit pairs Earth's motions with weather/climate elements, climate change, Nepal's bordering neighbours and SAARC, disaster management (with the 2072 earthquake as case material) and a map-making practical (draw Nepal, fill in geographical facts). That book is a teacher guide, cited as evidence only; Nepal is counted in this lesson for its student book(s) named here, not for this guide.

Nepal's own way (Nepal Grade 9 np-g9): Grade 9 "Hamro Prithvi" widens from Nepal (latitude/longitude, surface form, climate, vegetation, minerals and water resources, map work) to whole continents — Asia, Europe and Australia, each in two lessons (geographical/natural environment, then economic activities and social life); the preface notes this is a model textbook tested in schools under the 2076 secondary curriculum.

Nepal's own way (Nepal Grade 12 np-g12): Grade 12 geography is federal and applied — each of Nepal's provinces studied four ways (administration, population, social-cultural life, economy) plus inter-provincial relations; disasters in Nepal with the national disaster-management system and plan-making; GIS/geo-information in social mapping; and a full urbanisation-and-migration unit (village–city relations, safe and dignified migration, migration effects).
