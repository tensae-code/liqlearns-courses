# Lesson plan — International Relations and Aid Partners

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Global Issues · **Lesson:** International Relations and Aid Partners
**Place in the curriculum:** Global Issues (CURRICULUM.md) — built in the Social Studies remaining-strands expansion (Papua New Guinea, 2026-11-17).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** How countries relate to each other — aid partners, the impacts of international relations, and the kinds of relationships countries have — as Papua New Guinea's books teach it (see lesson/sources.md for the book and printed page).
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on nothing but the lesson itself — how countries relate to each other — aid partners, the impacts of international relations, and the kinds of relationships countries have.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of ..." in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **International Aid Partners; Impacts of International Relations; Types of International Relationships**
2. **PNG's bilateral and multilateral relations; Purpose, impact and outcomes of international relations**
3. **PNG's international trade relations; PNG's international trade agreements**
4. **Relationships between different democratic countries; Nature of relations**
5. **The regional organisations Papua New Guinea belongs to or works with**
6. **The cultural, social, political and economic relations between Papua New Guinea and the global community**
7. **Papua New Guinea's way — the books' own teaching way**

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
