# International Relations and Aid Partners — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's part teaches: how countries relate to each other — aid partners, the impacts of international relations, and the kinds of relationships countries have.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson's part teaches: Papua New Guinea's one-to-one (bilateral) and many-country (multilateral) relationships with other democratic countries, and why PNG keeps them — their purpose, their impact, and what they achieve for national needs.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** This lesson's part teaches: the goods and services Papua New Guinea gains through its relationships with other nations, their importance and relevance, and the trade agreements PNG has made with other democratic nations.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** This lesson's part teaches: the different ways people from different democratic countries relate to each other, and the purpose, impact and outcomes of those relationships.
**Answer: True**

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** This lesson's part teaches: the regional organisations Papua New Guinea belongs to or works with — the South Pacific Forum and the Asia Pacific Economic Community (APEC) — and their main functions, such as discussing common interests like trade and security in the region.
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 6)** This lesson's part teaches: the cultural, social, political and economic relations between Papua New Guinea and the global community — treaties, trade agreements, cultural and sporting events — and PNG's increasing interdependence with other countries.
**Answer: True**

**7. (Sub lesson 7)** This lesson stands on a student book, with no teacher guide.
**Answer: False — it stands on Papua New Guinea's teacher guides only, named in lesson/sources.md, as teacher-guide evidence.**
