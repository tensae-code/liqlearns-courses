# Hands-on — International Relations and Aid Partners

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## International Aid Partners; Impacts of International Relations; Types of International Relationships
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: How countries relate to each other — aid partners, the impacts of international relations, and the kinds of relationships countries have. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## PNG's bilateral and multilateral relations; Purpose, impact and outcomes of international relations
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Papua New Guinea's one-to-one (bilateral) and many-country (multilateral) relationships with other democratic countries, and why PNG keeps them — their purpose, their impact, and what they achieve for national needs. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## PNG's international trade relations; PNG's international trade agreements
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: The goods and services Papua New Guinea gains through its relationships with other nations, their importance and relevance, and the trade agreements PNG has made with other democratic nations. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Relationships between different democratic countries; Nature of relations
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: The different ways people from different democratic countries relate to each other, and the purpose, impact and outcomes of those relationships. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## The regional organisations Papua New Guinea belongs to or works with
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: The regional organisations Papua New Guinea belongs to or works with — the South Pacific Forum and the Asia Pacific Economic Community (APEC) — and their main functions, such as discussing common interests like trade and security in the region. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## The cultural, social, political and economic relations between Papua New Guinea and the global community
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: The cultural, social, political and economic relations between Papua New Guinea and the global community — treaties, trade agreements, cultural and sporting events — and PNG's increasing interdependence with other countries. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
