# Hands-on — Protecting Irans Habitats

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Lesson 12 Protecting Iran's habitats (حفاظت از زیستگاه‌های ایران): Iran's geographical conditions give it a great diversity of plant and animal species; overhunting and overfishing have cut some animal species (onager and gazelle among those hunted in the plains); Iran's rare species in danger of extinction — the Iranian cheetah and the gando (Iranian crocodile); why biodiversity matters — all living things depend on each other in the food chain; the Environment Protection Organization (سازمان حفاظت محیط‌زیست), the protected areas (منطقۀ حفاظت‌شده) it declares, and Rangers' Day (8 Aban). Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Iran's way
Say how Iran's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
