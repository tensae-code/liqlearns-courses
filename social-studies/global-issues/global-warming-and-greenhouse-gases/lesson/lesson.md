## Sub lesson 1 — The greenhouse effect

Global warming is the rise in average air and ocean temperatures since the mid-twentieth century, attributed largely to human emissions of greenhouse gases — water vapour, carbon dioxide, nitrous oxide, methane and ozone — which absorb and re-emit the earth's infrared radiation. That absorption is the greenhouse effect.

## Sub lesson 2 — Emissions up 70%

Global greenhouse-gas emissions grew 70% between 1970 and 2004. The chain from economy to climate is explicit: economic activity determines energy and material flows, which determine emissions.

## Sub lesson 3 — Which activity, which gas

Fossil fuels give carbon dioxide; agriculture and waste give methane. The sources-and-sinks lesson returns as chemistry: what an economy burns and buries decides what its sky holds.
