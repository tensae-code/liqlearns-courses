WORKED-ANSWER / FACT ERROR CORRECTED: §8.2.1 of the book garbles the greenhouse-gas increases since pre-industrial times, naming nitrous oxide twice ('N2O increased by 150% and nitrous oxide by 20%'). The 150% figure belongs to methane: the corrected set is CO2 up about 40%, methane up about 150%, nitrous oxide up about 20%. Only the corrected set is taught in this unit.
