# Global warming and greenhouse gases — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Greenhouse gases absorb and re-emit the earth's infrared radiation — the greenhouse effect.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Global GHG emissions grew 70% between 1970 and 2004.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Fossil fuels give CO2; agriculture and waste give methane.
**Answer: True**
