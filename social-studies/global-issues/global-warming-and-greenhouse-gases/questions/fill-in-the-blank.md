# Global warming and greenhouse gases — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or figure from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Greenhouse gases absorb and re-emit ____ radiation.
**Answer: infrared**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Global GHG emissions grew ____ percent between 1970 and 2004.
**Answer: 70**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Agriculture and waste give chiefly ____.
