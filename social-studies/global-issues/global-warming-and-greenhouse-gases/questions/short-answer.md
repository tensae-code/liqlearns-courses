# Global warming and greenhouse gases — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "The greenhouse effect" in your own words.
**Answer:** Greenhouse gases absorb and re-emit the earth's infrared radiation — the greenhouse effect.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Emissions up 70%" in your own words.
**Answer:** Global GHG emissions grew 70% between 1970 and 2004.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Which activity, which gas" in your own words.
**Answer:** Fossil fuels give CO2; agriculture and waste give methane.
