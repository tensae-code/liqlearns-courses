# Global warming and greenhouse gases — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "The greenhouse effect"?
a) Greenhouse gases absorb and re-emit the earth's infrared radiation — the greenhouse effect  b) Global GHG emissions grew 70% between 1970 and 2004  c) Fossil fuels give CO2; agriculture and waste give methane
**Answer: a) Greenhouse gases absorb and re-emit the earth's infrared radiation — the greenhouse effect**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Emissions up 70%"?
a) Global GHG emissions grew 70% between 1970 and 2004  b) Fossil fuels give CO2; agriculture and waste give methane  c) Greenhouse gases absorb and re-emit the earth's infrared radiation — the greenhouse effect
**Answer: a) Global GHG emissions grew 70% between 1970 and 2004**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Which activity, which gas"?
a) Fossil fuels give CO2; agriculture and waste give methane  b) Greenhouse gases absorb and re-emit the earth's infrared radiation — the greenhouse effect  c) Global GHG emissions grew 70% between 1970 and 2004
**Answer: a) Fossil fuels give CO2; agriculture and waste give methane**
