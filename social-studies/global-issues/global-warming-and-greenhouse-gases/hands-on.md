# Hands-on — Global warming and greenhouse gases

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Greenhouse drawing
Draw sunlight entering a greenhouse while heat rays bounce inside it, then scale the same drawing up to the whole atmosphere with the gas layer labelled.

## Chain of three
Write the three-link chain (economic activity, energy and material flows, emissions) for one day in your town, with a real activity at the first link and its gas at the last.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
