# Land degradation and poisoned soils — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Land degradation is a negative trend from…
a) natural causes only  b) direct or indirect human causes  c) the moon
**Answer: b) direct or indirect human causes**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** It affects about… people
a) 3.2 million  b) 3.2 billion  c) 320,000
**Answer: b) 3.2 billion**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** It feeds climate change through…
a) released carbon  b) extra rain  c) cooling
**Answer: a) released carbon**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** LDN targets aim to…
a) degrade neutrally  b) lose no more good land than is restored  c) stop farming
**Answer: b) lose no more good land than is restored**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The most common soil contaminant named is…
a) plastic bags  b) discarded dry-cell batteries  c) glass
**Answer: b) discarded dry-cell batteries**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Susceptibility to soil contamination varies by…
a) age and general health  b) height  c) language
**Answer: a) age and general health**
