# Land degradation and poisoned soils — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does Land Degradation Neutrality mean?
**Answer:** Voluntary targets so that land lost is balanced by land restored — no net loss of good land.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** How does degraded land feed climate change?
**Answer:** It releases stored carbon.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Who is most susceptible to contaminated soil?
**Answer:** People by age and general health — the young and the unwell first.
