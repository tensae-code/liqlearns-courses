# The warming trend — what the record shows — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** About how much has global surface temperature risen in a century?
a) 0.08 °C  b) 0.8 °C  c) 8 °C
**Answer: b) 0.8 °C**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Instrumental temperature records began in…
a) 1861  b) 1901  c) 2001
**Answer: a) 1861**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The IPCC's 2001 report found the warmest decade was the…
a) 1960s  b) 1980s  c) 1990s
**Answer: c) 1990s**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Most warming of the past 50 years was found to be…
a) natural  b) human-caused  c) unexplained
**Answer: b) human-caused**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** If emissions keep rising, the projected rise by 2100 is…
a) +0.1 °C  b) +1.4 to 5.8 °C  c) a fall of 2 °C
**Answer: b) +1.4 to 5.8 °C**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Which of these is already shifting?
a) Sea level and snow cover  b) The Earth's orbit  c) The length of the day
**Answer: a) Sea level and snow cover**
