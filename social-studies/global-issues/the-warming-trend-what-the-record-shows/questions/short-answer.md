# The warming trend — what the record shows — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How much warming came in just three decades?
**Answer:** About 0.6 °C.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why does a record starting in 1861 matter?
**Answer:** It gives a long, measured baseline, so the trend rests on instruments, not memory.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What must keep rising for the high 2100 projection to happen?
**Answer:** Emissions of heat-trapping gases.
