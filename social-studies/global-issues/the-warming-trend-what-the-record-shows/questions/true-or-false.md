# The warming trend — what the record shows — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** 1998 was the warmest year since records began, in the 2001 report's finding.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The warming has slowed down in recent decades.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Rainfall patterns are already shifting.
**Answer: True**
